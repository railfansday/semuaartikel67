Ruben Amorim and Milan find themselves in need of points in the Europa League table and are preparing for a tough test away against the unbeaten Olympiacos in Athens on Thursday evening.

It kicks off at 17.45 GMT [18.45 CET].

The Rossoneri have won just one and lost three of their first four matches in the Europa League this season, and will be tasked with taking on Olympiacos, who have three wins and one draw on their record so far this term.

Milan were victorious on their return to action over the weekend following the recent international break, putting five past Frosinone in a dominant display that brought goals from Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic.

It has been revealed that they will be without nine players for Thursday’s clash with Olympiacos, though. Luka Modric, Strahinja Pavlovic, Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers, Samuel Chukwueze, Francesco Camarda, David Bartesaghi and Pierro Terracciano are all expected to be unavailable this evening.

According to recent reports, that means that Amorim is unable to make too many alterations to the side that beat Frosinone over the weekend. Permission Estupinan is set to replace Bartesaghi at left-back, Ardon Jashari could come into the starting XI and Omari Hutchinson could also be introduced to the attack.

Kick-off this evening is due at 17.45 GMT.

Olympiacos (4-2-3-1): Ortega; Saliakas, Retsos, Pirola, Bruno; Freuler Gustavo Sà; Gelson Martins, Fortounis, Jota Silva, Gonzalez.

Milan (4-4-1-1): Maignan, Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Estupinan; Hutchinson, Jashari, Rabiot, Moreira; Pulisic; Ramos.

According to recent reports, tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 4 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
