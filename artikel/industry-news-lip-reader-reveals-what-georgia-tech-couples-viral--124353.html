The Wake Forest-Georgia Tech game on November 21 may have been underwhelming, but it still got plenty of people talking, thanks to a viral moment captured between two students in the stands. Unbeknownst to Georgia Tech seniors Tallulah Wall and Braden Flournoy, their argument was being broadcast on ESPN and narrated by Bachelor host Jesse Palmer, who started off by quipping, “This playoff situation is intense right now. Maybe not as intense as this conversation, though.”.

The two appeared on Today three days later to address their viral fight, revealing that they’ve only been together for “two months and one week”...and most people didn’t even know they were dating until they appeared on ESPN together.

And while they didn’t reveal what exactly they were arguing about, Braden mentioned “it was completely insignificant” and “more miscommunication than anything.” Plus, they’re still together, so it couldn’t have been all that bad.

We may never know exactly what was noted during that tense exchange, but there are certainly theories. The New York Post even tapped Nicola Hickling, in partnership with LipReader, to give her expert take on the convo. According to her, the back-and-forth involved Flournoy seemingly telling Wall, “I know he’s a piece of shit, but I value human life,” to which she seemingly agreed, “Okay. So do I.”.

Per Hickling, Wall possibly went on to say, “Are you suggesting I would follow you to the end of the earth? Why the fuck would I do that? Do you remember that? Are you serious right now? Do you think I expected you to do that?”.

According to recent reports, the argument then seemingly concluded with Flournoy saying, “I am completely serious. No excuses, that’s it,” and Wall replying, “I believe you.”.
