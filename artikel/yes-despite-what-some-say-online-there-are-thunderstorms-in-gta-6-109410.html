This includes the game’s dynamic and realistic weather system, a feature Rockstar hyped up quite a bit before launch. However, some players online aren’t seeing much rain and are claiming Rockstar lied. The reality is that, yes, rainstorms happen, but you might not be seeing them very often for a few reasons.

Why are some people suggesting the weather system in GTA 6 isn’t working or was a lie, while others, like myself, are dealing with thunderstorms?

Recent findings suggest that first up is that it does seem like something was borked with GTA 6‘s dynamic weather system at launch. On Monday, the first post-launch patch for GTA 6 arrived and fixed an issue that, according to Rockstar’s patch notes, led to the weather not changing for extended periods of time. So yeah, one reason it might not have been raining in your game is because of a bug.

In a recent development, however, even after the patch, some people are still claiming it isn’t raining in GTA 6. And maybe something is still bugged. The more likely explanation is that people don’t realize just how dynamic and advanced the weather is in GTA 6.

In all previous Grand Theft Auto games and most other open-world games, when it rains in the game, it rains across the whole map. That’s not how weather in GTA 6 works. Instead, storms, fog, and other weather events can happen locally and in small parts of the map. At one point I was flying a plane and it was sunny, but ahead in the sky were massive dark clouds. As I reached them, I entered a rainstorm. As I left the clouds behind, the rain stopped. So it’s very possible that it is raining somewhere in your GTA 6 world; you just aren’t in the spot where the rain is falling.

According to recent reports, I’ve also seen some people suggesting the weather is locked to certain conditions earlier in the game, which seems plausible but hasn’t been confirmed by Rockstar yet. Once I reached chapter three of the game, I had a few in-game days when it rained a lot. I started to miss the sun.

The point is that, no, Rockstar didn’t lie. Grand Theft Auto 6‘s dynamic weather system is so good that I think some people are just dodging the rain without realizing it. The size of the map and the randomness at play in the system may mean that you go for a while without encountering rainy weather, but keep playing and sooner or later, the storms will come.
