I spent all night playing GTA 6 — and these 3 PS5 settings made it look and sound incredible

In a recent development, GTA 6 is finally here! It’s easily Rockstar Games’ most ambitious title ever, featuring dense environments and lush soundscapes. You get a rich visual and audio experience out of the box, but tweaking a few PS5 settings can enhance it.

We’re talking specifically about the PS5’s settings, since GTA 6 has no graphical settings beyond a motion blur slider. Even on a PS5 Pro, your options are limited. But with a few smart tweaks, you can make GTA 6 look and sound its best on either PS5 console.

Here are the 3 PS5 settings you need to check to enhance your GTA 6 experience.

It has been revealed that this one is for folks who own an OLED TV. In the Video Output tab in System Settings, scroll down to HDR. Click it, and you’ll be taken to another screen to calibrate the console’s HDR.

Sources indicate that you’ll adjust the brightness across three pages. On the first two pages, press up or down until the sun logo disappears. On the last page, dial it all the way down, since your OLED TV can display deep blacks.

GTA 6 also has in-game HDR calibration. Just follow the on-screen prompts to get your desired result, though I’ve found the default settings work well.

Recent findings suggest that after 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto VI has arrived. Set in the state of Leonida, with Vice City at its heart, you play Jason and Lucia, two criminals forced into an increasingly dangerous situation as they seek their fortune, discovering the darker side of the sunniest spot in America.

GTA 6 is undoubtedly a feast for the eyes, but you shouldn’t neglect the audio side of the equation.

Sources indicate that while the PS5 didn’t launch with Dolby Atmos, it now supports the feature. So if you have a soundbar or surround sound system that supports Dolby Atmos, you should definitely enable it. To do so, go to Sound in System Settings, click Sound Output, scroll down to Audio Format, and select Dolby Atmos.

Sources indicate that GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on all consoles. Rockstar Games even confirmed to Kotaku that the PS5 Pro won’t be patched to run at 40 or 60 fps. That remarked, we can’t completely rule it out.

If GTA 6 ever adds a performance-enhancing feature, you’ll want to set the PS5 Pro for that. In the Video Output tab of the Screen and Audio settings, make sure that VRR is set to Automatic for unsupported games and that 120Hz output is set to Automatic.

Again, this won’t make GTA 6 run at a higher frame rate, but it prepares your PS5 Pro if it ever does. As a rule of thumb, you should have these settings enabled anyway, since many PlayStation titles offer 40 fps modes.

Until we get the inevitable PC version of GTA 6, we'll have to make do with the experience on existing consoles, in this case, the PS5. That noted, the advice I've given you should help you get the most from the game. Have fun in Vice City!
