The midfielder was substituted on Sunday with what coach Sergej Jakirovic initially thought was a knee problem, but which Iroegbunam told him was a hamstring injury.

He had surgery on Tuesday, posting on Instagram: "I will be back stronger".

The former Aston Villa and Everton midfielder's Hull season had been a slow burner after joining with an abductor injury picked up in the summer which severely limited his pre-season, but he was just starting to find some form.

His four starts for the club have all come in the last four games.

Hull Live are reporting he could miss three months at the time of year when the Premier League calendar is at its most condensed, with UEFA's cup competitions set for a winter break. That period could include 16 of Hull's 38 league matches.

The Tigers have plenty of central midfield options, but lots are having their own fitness issues, and some are not eligible to play until the new year.

Lucas Gourna, Hidemasa Morita, Jens Hjerto-Dahl, Christos Mouzakitis were all signed in the summer along with Iroegbunam to compete with Regan Slater and Matt Crooks.

Dahl and Mouzakitis are yet to start in the Premier League, having played just 30 and 15 minutes (plus stoppage time) this season.

Gourna and Morita have adapted quickly but, hampered by minor injury issues, have only started three matches between them.

Darko Gyabi, Kieran Dowell and Oscar Zambrano were not registered to play in the first half of the campaign because Hull had more than the permitted 25 senior players, but can be added to the squad when the transfer window opens on Fresh Year's Day.

Hull have 21 points to play for before then.

None of the trio went out on loan, and have instead been working on their fitness playing for the club's under-21 team, which they are allowed to play for in the meantime.

Of the 18 players Hull signed in the summer, Nobel Mendy and Elliot Stroud have started eight out of 11 league matches each, but no one other than 23-year-old Iroegbunam has yet started more than three.
