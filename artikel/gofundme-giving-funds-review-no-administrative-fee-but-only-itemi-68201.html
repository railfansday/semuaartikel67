📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that open a GoFundMe Giving Fund if you itemize and plan to give cash, stocks, or exchange-traded funds over a few years, since it charges no administrative fee and lets you contribute or grant as little as $5. Skip it for crypto or private organization shares, and give straight to charities instead if you take the standard deduction.

It has been revealed that goFundMe Giving Funds is a donor-advised fund for givers who want a tax deduction now and time to choose charities later, run since June 2025 by GoFundMe Giving Fund, a 501(c)(3) public charity separate from GoFundMe. It charges no administrative fee, where DAFgiving360 charges 0.60% a year on the first $500,000, which works out to $60 a year on a $10,000 balance.

In a recent development, itemizers who give cash, stocks, or ETFs over a few years lose none of their balance to an administrative fee here. If you take the standard deduction, give straight to charities instead, because starting in 2026 up to $1,000 of cash gifts ($2,000 on a joint return) is deductible without itemizing, and contributions to a donor-advised fund don’t qualify.
