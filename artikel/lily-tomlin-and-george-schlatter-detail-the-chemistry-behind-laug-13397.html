You bet your sweet blippy Lily Tomlin and George Schlatter have only the best memories of Laugh-In.

The two comedy icons gave behind-the-scenes details of the very eenteresting time they shared on the sketch comedy series—which ran for six seasons from 1968 to 1973—ahead of the release of the documentary Sock It to Me: The Legend of George Schlatter, which will be available to rent or buy on all digital platforms beginning Dec. 8.

“A major, major reason for our success was that Miss Tomlin,” Schlatter exclusively shared with E! News’ Will Marfuggi. “God love her.”.

And Tomlin—who joined Laugh-In halfway through season three and was a regular performer until the show ended—was forever grateful that the flying fickle finger of fate led her to Schlatter.

Sources indicate that “When I walked into the room, he just threw his arms around me,” the 87-year-old told E! of her audition. “We’d never met. He said, ‘About time you got here, kid,’ or something like that. And he just made me feel so comfortable. I knew he got me. I knew he got me from the start.”.

The moment is one that Schlatter himself also fondly remembered.

“You just walked in the room and the room lit up,” the 96-year-old shared. “The room just elevated into another space where it stayed, and Lily became a major, major part of my life, my career, and my everything.”.

Tomlin—whose famous characters included the obnoxious telephone operator Ernestine—was just one of the stars to come out of Laugh-In, which was hosted by comedians Dan Rowan and Dick Martin and helped launch the careers of Goldie Hawn, Ruth Buzzi and more.

Looking back on it now, Schlatter is amazed by all the comedy they created for NBC in beautiful downtown Burbank—all of which is touched on in the latest documentary that includes interviews with Tomlin, Hawn, Tom Hanks, Billy Crystal, Martin Short and more.

“We came into a time period where there was nothing,” he mused. “They were getting no rating at all. And so we could arrive and I said, ‘I’ll give you a show ’til you get a real show ready.’”.

And when it comes to his memories of the award-winning series, finding Tomlin and still being able to give her a ringy-dingy all these years later is certainly one of his greatest achievements.

“There was no other Lily Tomlin,” Schlatter said. “Lily Tomlin was just a magical, magical personality that just happened and exploded. Timing was right for her, and it sure was right for me. Lily, we had a good time.”.

Of course, Tomlin agreed with one of her oldest friends, adding, “It's not enough to say we had a good time because it would open the door for me on everything.”.
