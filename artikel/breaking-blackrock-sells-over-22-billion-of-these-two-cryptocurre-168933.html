BlackRock’s investors have sold cryptocurrencies worth more than $22 billion so far in November 2026, despite cumulative positive fundamentals.

As of Friday, November 27, BlackRock had offloaded crypto assets worth approximately $22.36 billion, according to data Coinpedia fetched from Arkham Intelligence, a blockchain analytics platform.

As a result, the firm’s crypto portfolio had $48.81 billion, down from $71.17 billion recorded at the beginning of the month.

BlackRock investors sold Bitcoin (BTC) worth $19.33 billion over the past three weeks, down from $63.85 billion on November 1 to $44.51 billion at the time of reporting. Notably, BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) slushed its trove by 70,677 BTC so far this month, down from 825,657 coins to 744,981 BTC at the time of publication.

On the other hand, the firm’s clients have cut their Ethereum (ETH) holdings by $3.02 billion so far this month, down from $7.32 billion on November 1, to $4.3 billion at the time of writing. Meanwhile, the company’s iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA) saw its holdings fall by 641,592 ETH, down from 3.22 million coins to 2.578 million ETH.

Over the past month, Bitcoin’s price has dropped by nearly 26% to trade at about $59,850; as such, the flagship coin had a market capitalization of roughly $1.25 trillion.

During the last 24 hours, BTC’s average trading volume had spiked by 18.82% to $31.62 billion.

Recent findings suggest that as BlackRock accelerated its ETH selloff in November, Ethereum price capitulated by 28.76% to trade at $1,669.20. On Friday, the leading altcoin saw its average 24-hour trading volume drop by over 29% to $11.6 billion.

Consequently, a renewed demand for these cryptocurrencies by BlackRock’s investors could spur a potential rebound.
