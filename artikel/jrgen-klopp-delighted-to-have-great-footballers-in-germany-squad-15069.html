Germany booked their place in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals after a resounding 5-0 win against the Netherlands on Monday.

In a recent development, following the win, head coach Jürgen Klopp stated that the positive approach the team took was the reason for the win. “We took a positive approach and looked at what we have. Not what we don’t have. We knew what we wanted to do from day one,” he noted after the game.

Klopp further added his happiness for the fans of the national team who managed to witness a great win in a game that was labelled a ‘mini-final’ and Klopp’s first big test in charge of the national team, given the magnitude with qualification for the knockout stages on the line.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m absolutely delighted for the German football public that they’ve seen we have guys who can really play football. Great footballers! And the next generation is coming through with Mohya and co,” Klopp stated.

Germany’s head coach further noted that he ‘fell in love’ with the team from the moment he started. “If we can get these highly talented players firing, then we’re going to be a very difficult team to play against,” he added.
