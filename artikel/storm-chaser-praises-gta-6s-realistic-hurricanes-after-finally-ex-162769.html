A veteran storm chaser has weighed in on Grand Theft Auto 6’s hurricanes, saying they are “pretty realistic” compared to his usual chases.

Sources indicate that back before Grand Theft Auto 6 was released, fans eagerly anticipated what Rockstar Games had up their sleeves for different features. One of the most-talked-about features pre-launch was the weather.

With Florida – sorry, Leonida – having some pretty extreme weather shifts, replicating that in-game seemed like a significant task. Rockstar ultimately confirmed that the game would have the “most advanced” weather system to date. That means having torrential rainstorms, hurricanes, and massive waves hitting players.

Industry observers note that the hurricanes, though, don’t appear to unlock until you’ve completed a Strangers and Freaks mission in Lake Leonida with Freddie Gibbs, as he asks you to stay inside his doomsday bunker with him. It doesn’t have any bearing on the story, bar the new weather, which you can choose to deal with.

Veteran storm chaser Reed Timmer – who starred in the TV show Storm Chasers – weighed in on that exact part of the game.

In posting a video of the hurricane effects, Timmer noted that it was “pretty realistic for a hurricane chase,” and added that he had gone “chasing a hurricane.”.

Timmer’s video put a focus on how trees reacted to the intense wind gusts, as well as how much vehicles struggled to deal with it too. “You can really see the wind here,” he said at one point while driving an excavator. “Look at that!”.

As noted, you can opt to leave Freddie’s bunker and go out in the upcoming weather, just like Timmer did, or you can ride it out from inside.

Choosing the latter option will frustrate either Jason or Lucia, depending on who you complete the mission with, seeing as you won’t contact them for a few in-game days.

When you complete GTA 6’s story, you will unlock a cheat code that allows you to cycle through weather systems. Activating a cheat will remove your ability to unlock trophies and achievements, but it provides some fun.

Elsewhere, a birdwatching expert has weighed in on GTA 6’s wildlife, seeing as they finally found all the birds in the game.
