The final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday confirmed Steve Rogers's choice to go back in time to live with Peggy Carter in Avengers: Endgame kickstarts the plot of the new movie.

Industry observers note that but, there's one timeline question I can't figure out: what does that mean for Old Steve Rogers? At the end of Endgame, Sam Wilson is given the shield by an elderly Steve, who grew old in the past with Peggy. So, if he actually had to return to the present to help fight Doctor Doom, shouldn't the old Steve have already lived through Doomsday?

It's a debate Marvel fans are having in the wake of the trailer, and nobody seems to agree.

Sooooo Do we think this Steve went through the events of Doomsday or?November 22, 2026.

"I keep going back and forth about this but probably? The only way for Doomsday Steve to have any stakes is if it's the same one, rather than a new branch variant created by Doom's disruption," thinks one Marvel fan.

"When Loki comes back and talks to him in the past, he created another timeline. The old Steve Rogers is a different Steve from the Doomsday, although they had the same starting point," notes someone else.

Recent findings suggest that "I'd be a little disappointed if he didn't. Seems like there's a fantastic opportunity to make this hit harder upon a rewatch. I personally wouldn't pass up the opportunity. It even goes as far to present that Sam Wilson survives and leads the latest Avengers proudly," thinks another fan.

"My guess is No. Since Endgame explained that once something happened, it happened, and any change in the past will only create another timeline – meaning this old Steve gave Sam the shield, and only later they met in Doomsday – then this old Steve is but a variant of Doomsday Steve," is another person's theory.

"Ig yes he went through all the events of Doomsday and Secret Wars yet he remained mute because he knew you can't change the future as it will branch a new timeline so he calmly accepted the truth and relaxed," speculates another person.

That's not the only timeline question, either, since it looks like Steve and Peggy actually live across two different timelines in the Marvel Phase 6 movie – one where they're visited by Loki, and one where the Fantastic Four arrive to talk to them.

We don't have long to find out what's actually going on. Doomsday arrives this December 18, and it pits Earth's Mightiest Heroes against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom.
