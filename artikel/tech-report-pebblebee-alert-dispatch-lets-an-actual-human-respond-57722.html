While many services are turning to AI in place of an actual human being, Pebblebee’s new Alert Dispatch brings the help of a real person into an emergency situation, and it all starts with your Find Hub tracker.

Pebblebee released its Alert feature in 2025 as a way to use a Clip 5 tracker for Find Hub as a siren in an emergency. The tech giant later added an optional live location-sharing feature to this, and also released “Halo” as an emergency siren first, tracker second with a special design.

Now, “Alert Dispatch” takes the whole system up a notch.

It has been revealed that instead of relying solely on location sharing and the siren, Pebblebee’s Alert Dispatch assigns an actual human being to the situation, with a trained dispatch agent reaching out first over SMS or a call, then pulling in emergency responders if the situation calls for it. The feature works with Clip 4, Clip 5, and Halo.

Alert Dispatch connects Pebblebee owners with a trained dispatch agent when they trigger an Alert, starting with a text and escalating to an Emergency call if they don’t respond. With an attempt to send help when users cannot confirm they’re safe, plus live location sharing and private Check-In calls on Clip 5 and Halo, it adds another layer of support when calling for help isn’t an option.
