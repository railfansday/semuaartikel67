AC Milan resume their Serie A campaign after the final international break on 2026 by welcoming Frosinone to San Siro.

It has been revealed that ruben Amorim’s side are in need of a win to climb back into the top four after Como’s result on Saturday, while Roma and Lazio also picked up victories. They face a Frosinone team who will be full of confidence after an excellent start to their campaign, following promotion in 2025-26.

The latest team news suggests that Amorim will go with a 4-4-1-1 formation, though in his pre-match press conference he did not give too much away about his selection choices, especially in view of Thursday’s trip to Greece.

At the risk of sounding like a broken record, something simply has to click for Goncalo Ramos, and preferably soon. He has gone one month without a goal and his struggles to even generate shots followed him to Portugal on international duty. However, Frosinone are a side that score goals but also let them in too, so there should be spaces to exploit.

In a recent development, it will be an intriguing battle between the No.9 and Gabriele Calvani, while it could even be an audition of sorts for the centre-back. Milan have been linked with the Genoa loanee, who has made an impressive start to the campaign, so it will be an up-close look as he tries to pile the misery on Ramos.

With top scorer Antonio Raimondo out injured, Giorgi Kvernadze will be the main goal threat for the away side. The Georgian’s seven goal contributions are only bettered by the Italian striker, and he has started all 11 games under Alvini so far in Serie A.

Recent findings suggest that it seems that Mario Gila will be tasked with stopping Kvernadze. The Spaniard spent the break at Milanello after not being called up by Spain again, and he should slot in as the right-sided defender again next to Koni De Winter.

Moving into midfield, there will be a fascinating contest between Adrien Rabiot and Giacomo Calo. It starts with a very obvious point of comparison: both are known for their goal scoring prowess, with the Frenchman already on five for the season and the Italian having racked up the same number.

According to recent reports, the pair are likely to face each other head-to-head too. Whereas Rabiot has usually played deeper like Calo, it seems that the head coach is thinking of moving him further forward to play behind Ramos, so he will see a lot of Calo and also Patrizio Masini.

Alvini is expected to give the nod to Aleksa Terzic at left-back, which is notable because the Serbian has only started one game so far in 2026-27, with his other four appearances all coming off the bench. He does like to get forward in transition though, and has a goal to his name.

In a recent development, primarily, Terzic will be tasked with trying to limit Diego Moreira’s impact on the game. The Belgian – who scored for his national team for the first time in their rout of Italy – is expected to start on the right for the Rossoneri, and his seven goal contributions in seven games tell their own story.

Sources indicate that finally, we return to the middle of the park where Luka Modric and Romano Schmid will headline as series of interesting duels. Modric’s importance to Milan in all phases of the game have been written about at length, but particularly the build-up phase in games where his team are expected to dominate the ball.

Recent findings suggest that the Croatian is the metronome for the Rossoneri just as the summer signing Schmid is for the Canarini. The Austrian has been playing mostly as an attacking midfielder, with two goals and four assists to his name, and starts in all 11 rounds thus far.
