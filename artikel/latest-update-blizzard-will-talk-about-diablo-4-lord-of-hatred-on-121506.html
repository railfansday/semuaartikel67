Latest Update: Blizzard will talk about Diablo 4: Lord of Hatred one last time before the expansion's launch next week

🌐 Source: Vg247 • 📅 17 Apr 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (308 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • Recent findings suggest that next week, however, we’re getting an updated look at the new content as part of a livestream.
  • The next Developer Patch is set for Thursday, April 23.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Diablo 4 is mere days away from the launch of its biggest expansion yet, and Blizzard wants to make sure it got one last chance to tell you about it. Lord of Hatred is the follow-up to 2024’s Vessel of Hatred, and it’s set to arrive on April 28.

Recent findings suggest that next week, however, we’re getting an updated look at the new content as part of a livestream. This is the Developer Upgrade kind; the meaty, information-dense show anyone who follows Diablo’s livestreams should be familiar with.

The next Developer Patch is set for Thursday, April 23. It’ll kick off at 11am PT, 2pm ET, 6pm GMT. The show will, naturally, focus on Lord of Hatred. In particular, we’re getting another look at the expansion content that hasn’t been given the same level of coverage as the bigger features.

Everyone knows about the newly introduced Skovos region, the Warlock class (and the Paladin before that), but we haven’t seen much from the other gameplay-altering features that affect a lot of the core flow.

Industry observers note that in next week’s showcase, Blizzard will show off the updated and expanded skill trees - which come to all classes, the Talisman system, and the gear-altering Horadric Cube. War Intends, the new make-your-own-endgame feature will be discussed, too.

Sources indicate that more interestingly, we’re also going to hear about the upcoming season kicking off right alongside the launch of Lord of Hatred. A short, in-betweener season is presently live in Diablo 4, and it’s going to end right as the next one/the expansion launch.

And, of course, the show won’t end before the team takes a few questions from chat, as is tradition. If you want to catch it live, you’ll be able to do so on Diablo’s official YouTube, Twitch, Twitter, and TikTok channels.
