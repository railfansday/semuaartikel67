Industry observers note that "Ok now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally out I can share with you guys a secret, which is that I'm in it. I play Mandy, I do her voice over and I do her motion capture, and she's a lot of fun. So if you play the game, let me know if you find me." via Instagram.

You'd think that everyone's favourite character in GTA 6 would be either Jason or Lucia—one of the main characters—but no, another front-runner has emerged for fans: Mandy. She's so much of a favourite, in fact, that we've had to confirm that you can't cheat on Lucia with her.

Recent findings suggest that you meet Mandy early on in GTA 6 when Jason visits her home to collect the rent she owes to her landlord Brian Heder and also fix her blocked sink. It's pretty apparent that she has a thing for Jason, she is his ex after all, but the feeling isn't reciprocated, no matter how much players want it to be.

According to recent reports, things also get a little weird with her as she pushes just a little too far and suggests she's been stalking him, not a great look Mandy. But the weirdest thing of all is what Jason finds in her sink. As he's clearing out the blocked pipe he finds what looks like a severed finger. He only has it for a second before Mandy snatches it out of his hands and runs off with it, but players are pretty sure they saw what they saw.

Industry observers note that this has led some to believe that Mandy could be a serial killer. Obviously that's only one finger, and to be serial she'd need to have a whole hand at least (come on, do we not have standards anymore?), but her odd behaviour plus the severed finger means that something is more than just a bit off with her.

Sources indicate that this still isn't enough to dissuade interest in her for some, as one player proudly confirms: "I can fix her". Godspeed soldier, better you than me.
