Lazio and Lecce kick off the return of Serie A action at 14.00 GMT following the recent international break, and Gennaro Gattuso is expected to have Davide Frattesi available despite his withdrawal from the Italy national team squad last week.

Hosts Lazio sit at the top of the Serie A standings after 11 matches of the 2026-27 season with nine victories and two draws totalling 29 points thus far. They are one of two teams still unbeaten in the Italian top flight after 11 games, along with city rivals Roma.

It has been revealed that lecce find themselves in 14th place ahead of kick-off with three wins, one draw and seven defeats on their record thus far. They went into the international break off the back of a 1-1 draw away against Torino.

Industry observers note that gattuso is expected to keep his attacking and midfield line-up from their 1-1 draw away against Napoli prior to the international break. Frattesi is available despite missing Italy’s second match of the international break, and he is expected to join Kenneth Taylor and Reda Belahyane in the middle of the park.

There could be some changes at the back: Nuno Tavares is unavailable after picking up an injury with Portugal, and Sky Sport Italia suggests that Danilho Doekhi and Diogo Leite could start at centre-back.

Pre-match reports suggest that Willem Geubells is likely to start over Nikola Stulic at centre-forward for Lecce.

Lazio (4-3-3): Mandas; Floriani Mussolini, Leite, Doekhi, Pedraza; Frattesi, Belahyane, Taylor; Isaksen, Pinamonti, Noslin.

Lecce (4-3-3): Falcone; D.Veiga, Gaspar, T.Gabriel, Gallo; Coulibaly, Ngom, Maleh; N’Dri, Geubbels, Fatah.

Industry observers note that kicking off at 14.00 GMT, today’s game is unavailable to watch live in the UK. You can follow the pre-match build-up, in-game commentary and post-match reactions on the Football Italia Liveblog. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
