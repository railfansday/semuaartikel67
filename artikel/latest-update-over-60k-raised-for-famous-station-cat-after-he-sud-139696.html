Over $60,000 has been raised to help cover the vet bills of a beloved railway station cat in England after he suddenly fell ill.

On November 23, a 12-year-old ginger cat known for greeting passengers at a train station in the UK was taken to the vet after his family noticed he was eating less and sleeping more than usual.

While George’s family is still waiting to find out what made their beloved pet unwell, feline fans from across the globe have shown their support by raising thousands of dollars to help with his treatment.

“George has brought so much happiness, laughter and comfort to so many people over the years, and now we’re doing everything we can to get him back to his happy, healthy self,” reads a post on the station cat’s Facebook page.

According to recent reports, “We know times are difficult for so many, and your kind messages, love and good wishes mean more than you could possibly imagine…Thank you for loving our gorgeous ginger boy as much as we do. We’re keeping everything crossed for him.”.

In a post shared on George’s GoFundMe page, his family revealed that the cat’s medical bill has already reached $1,958 (£1,500), and mentioned that further investigations into the cat’s health could lead to further costs.

However, since posting the campaign, George has received an outpouring of donations to help cover the costs. As of November 25, the cute cat has raised $60,582 (£46,393) of his $78,000 (£60,000) goal, much to the shock of his owners.

“We are completely overwhelmed and incredibly humbled by the love, kindness and generosity that has been shown towards our little ginger boy,” said George’s social media manager Simone Carter.

“When we set up this fundraiser, we could never have imagined the response it would receive. From the bottom of our hearts, thank you to every single person who has donated, shared the appeal, or taken the time to send George their love and good wishes.”.

George’s family has pledged to use any money left over after his medical bills are paid to help other cats in need.

“George has spent so many years bringing a little happiness into people’s lives, and it is incredibly moving to see just how much love is being returned to him,” mentioned Carter.

“How wonderful that, through George, we will also be able to make a difference to the lives of other cats who need a little kindness and a helping hand.”.
