It has been revealed that the Governors Awards are back. The annual gala hosted by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences (who also hosts the Oscars) to honor lifetime achievement and extraordinary contributions to film has returned for its 17th edition—and the red carpet is brimming with star power.

While this gala itself is not aired live, like the main Oscars ceremony (which takes place in March every year), the awards given are considered genuine Oscars. Until 2009, the awards now given at the Governors Awards were presented live during the main Oscars telecast, but the Academy separated them into their own gala—both to shorten the main television broadcast and to give honorees more time to celebrate their wins and present their speeches.

Last year, Tom Cruise, Debbie Allen, and Wynn Thomas received Honorary Oscars, while Dolly Parton was given the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award. On the red carpet, we saw everyone, from Jennifer Lawrence to Anya Taylor-Joy, Cynthia Erivo, and Dakota Johnson.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, tonight’s honorees include Glenn Close, Ridley Scott, and Floyd Norman—who are all receiving Honorary Oscars—alongside producers Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler, who will receive the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.

But there are plenty of other guests in attendance this evening at the Ray Dolby Ballroom at Ovation Hollywood in Los Angeles—from Penélope Cruz in Chanel to Zendaya in Gilles Deacon, Charlize Theron in Tom Ford, Tessa Thompson in Schiaparelli, and Emma Stone in Louis Vuitton.
