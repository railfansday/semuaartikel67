Although XRP has achieved a sharp price rally after the severe price correction seen in the past months, its recent recovery has yet to influence the performance of its ETF market.

Latest data from SoSoValue shows that momentum across the XRP ETF market has remained slow as the funds have continued to post massive withdrawals during each trading session in recent days.

Data reveals that the XRP ETFs managed by Canary Capital and Grayscale recorded combined withdrawals of $6.14 million during their latest trading session on November 17.

This also represents the total daily net outflows recorded on that day as only the two funds were active while the rest of the XRP ETF products remained muted with zero participation from investors.

While this means that investors have continued to withdraw their funds despite XRP attempting to reclaim the $1 price level, Canary Capital's XRP ETF saw the highest withdrawal of the day.

Notably, the fund recorded over 5 million XRP, worth about $5.01 million, in daily outflows, while Grayscale's XRP ETF followed with just $1.12 million in outflows.

Although Bitwise did not record any inflow or outflow on this day, the firm still stands as XRP’s biggest ETF manager as it controls over $392.64 million in net assets as of this date.

Despite its dominance in the XRP ETF market, Bitwise has remained muted all week, showing zero daily participation since November 9, when it posted its highest-ever daily outflow of $9.40 million.
