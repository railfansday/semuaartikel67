In a recent development, grand Theft Auto 6 has officially introduced, and unsurprisingly, it's earning rave reviews. It's arriving hot on the heels of several critically acclaimed games this year, but does it have enough momentum to knock the current best-reviewed game of the year, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, off its throne? The answer is a resounding yes: not only is GTA 6 being reviewed higher than Nintendo's little hero in green, but it's also emerging as one of the highest-rated games of all time. When Ocarina of Time launched in early November, it promptly shot to the top of the Metacritic charts, earning a metascore of 96 from GameSpot's sister site. That score came from 128 reviews, and Metacritic rates it as "Universal Acclaim" on its scale. Historically, GTA games have always had some of the highest review aggregates, especially after the series moved to the third dimension. Starting with GTA 3, mainline entries have all earned metascores in the high 90s, with Grand Theft Auto 4 being the highest-reviewed game in the franchise until GTA 6 came along. The remake has sold more than 5 million copies globally in its first four days, and those numbers will keep growing as we enter the festive season. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick recently reacted to the game's release, saying it's "terrific" and that Nintendo has every right to be proud of it. "This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7," GameSpot editor-at-large Tamoor Hussain wrote in GameSpot's GTA 6 review.
