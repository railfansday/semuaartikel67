Former Roma and Paris Saint-Germain attacker Javier Pastore has shared his thoughts ahead of the meeting of his two former clubs in the Champions League on Wednesday evening, and has explained what makes the two teams so enjoyable to watch: ‘Gasperini has made his philosophy clear to the players, like Lucho at PSG’.

Roma face another tough test in the Champions League away against the reigning champions, PSG, on Wednesday night. The Giallorossi have four points from their opening four matches in the competition this season after tests away against Fenerbahce and Manchester United and at home against Slovan Bratislava and Real Madrid.

PSG, meanwhile, have had a perfect start and will be looking to make it five wins from five on Wednesday night. Luis Enrique’s side have beaten Slovan Bratislava, Manchester City, Barcelona and Villarreal already this season.

“Both play to impose themselves with great talents in attack,” said Pastore in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport. “They have coaches who work differently, but with the same attacking and counter-attacking principles. PSG will be more confident and have a perfect record, Roma need to pick up points. It will be an exciting game.”.

Pastore feels that the coaches might be both sides’ strongest assets.

“Both know how to get into their players’ heads. They are not just good tactically, but also at managing their teams, which is fundamental in modern football. I don’t know Gasperini very well, but he did great things at Atalanta and he’s brought an offensive, selfless style of play to Roma. They take risks and have lots of quality going forwards.

“He’s made his philosophy clear to the players, like Lucho at PSG, where he has changed the game concepts and the players’ mentality, who still follow him after winning two Champions Leagues.”.

Pastore pinpointed the players that Roma should be wary of.

“I’ve always liked Doue and Kvaratskhelia, who won’t be involved unfortunately, but also Mendes and Hakimi, the best full-backs in the world. I also like everything about Neves, a very special player who doesn’t always draw attention, but always helps his team in recovery, building from the back, creating space, looking for smart passes and arriving in front of goal. He’s a complete player.”.

According to recent reports, “For Roma, Dybala is a spectacle, dangerous anywhere. Malen is better than any other striker this year. And there’s also Soule, who brings something different, is technical and dribbles and scores well.”.

Alarmingly for Roma, Pastore states that there are no weaknesses in the PSG side.

It has been revealed that “I don’t see any, but maybe they will come out while the game is in progress. PSG are a united team built on the consistency of their play both in attack and defence. And Gasperini is following a similar path with Roma, always wanting to score one more than the opponent. You could even see it against Real Madrid, despite the result.”.

Still, Malen’s form is encouraging. The Dutchman has scored 16 goals from 15 games across all competitions and netted four goals during Roma’s 5-1 win over Parma on Sunday.

“With Malen in this type of form, pretty much any ball into the box ends with a goal and it’s hard to stop him. Dybala is dangerous in every situation: From free-kicks, from the edge of the area, with an assist or a shot into the top corner. These are the types of players who usually give you something extra in games like this.”.

Recent findings suggest that and lastly, when asked if he had to pick a side, Pastore mentioned: “Rome is beautiful with wonderful people, a place I lived for three great years. I’m in Paris more often because I got married there, and my children were born there. But I would take the Italian food anywhere.”.
