Your Capricorn Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

🌐 Source: Cosmopolitan • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read (857 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Industry observers note that if it’s not coming from your inner voice, it’s all just noise, Capricorn.
  • In a recent development, friendship is what’s keeping you afloat once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4.
  • Industry observers note that you’re making decisions based on your gut instincts once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that if it’s not coming from your inner voice, it’s all just noise, Capricorn. The month starts out with the Sun moving through Sagittarius, inviting you to reconnect with your intuition. You might notice certain themes, symbols, and patterns showing up in your dreams that offer insight into your waking life. You’re also more aware of certain habits in your own life that you’re ready to break. It’s a season of acceptance and change.

In a recent development, friendship is what’s keeping you afloat once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4. Spending time with groups of people who have similar hobbies or ideals as you brings a sense of belonging and grounding. This can be a fun time to get to know new people and friends, or to put yourself out there. You might also meet certain people who can help you pursue your goals. This is a season of support and feeling inspired to chase after your wildest dreams.

Industry observers note that you’re making decisions based on your gut instincts once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6. The symbols that show up in your dreams might seem more poignant and relevant to what you’re dealing with in your waking life. This can also be a time where you’re more caught up in your own head, fantasies, or the past. Certain self-defeating patterns and habits that keep you stagnant are made known, inviting you to make a change.

A Fresh Moon in Sagittarius helps you say goodbye and surrender to some changes in your life on December 8. It’s possible you’re moving through an ending and this can stir up feelings of grief. There may be a person, situation, or habit that you need to leave in the past once and for all. This is your season of realization and processing. Take your time to feel fully what comes up for you. Feeling is your way to healing and what will help you access new possibilities and timelines.

Sources indicate that there are challenges you need to confront in your personal life when Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. You might need to set a firm boundary with a family member or have a harsh conversation with a roommate. You might also create a more regimented routine to care for your own living space, recognizing how precious your own home is and that it deserves to be cared for in a more consistent way. This energy can feel jarring, asking you to confront the past, but it also helps you push past anything or anything that wants to keep you tethered to an old version of you that you’ve already outgrown.

In a recent development, let the helping begin once Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. Interactions with others are emotional and sentimental. This energy can help you access feelings you’ve avoided and bring about revelations in some of your private relationships. It might also test your ability to maintain your boundaries in your private life or face a reality check if there’s someone or something you’ve been putting on a pedestal.

You’re making an crucial financial decision when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You might get serious about paying off some debt, or invest in your future in some way. There might also be a change in an ongoing contract, job, or obligation in your personal life, leaving you to reflect on the give and take in this commitment. Does it feel fulfilling? You might also be wildly self-aware in a way that sparks a whole new healing journey. You’re ready to face your shadows.

It has been revealed that the Sun enters your sign and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, making you the main character! This is a season of new beginnings—whether it’s your relationships, setting a new personal goal, moving, or changing up your wardrobe and how you express yourself to the world. You’re in a more confident mood and this can put you front and center in many areas and aspects of your life. Take up space and see where the road takes you!

A Full Moon in Cancer highlights your close relationships on December 23. There may be significant developments in a key relationship—from lovers to family members to business partners and BFFs. Some might take the next step, solidifying their shared future while others could address issues or part ways. This can also reveal certain relationship dynamics that need to be adjusted. If you’ve been working toward a specific personal goal, this might also be a moment of reaching a milestone.

Industry observers note that the month wraps up with vital news coming your way once Mercury enters your sign on December 25. Conversations you have and information you learn might feel a bit more personal. You’re also thinking more about yourself and your goals. What are you committed to now and how are you going to bring those desires to life? This is your cosmic permission slip to focus on you and take necessary steps to make your manifestations a reality!
