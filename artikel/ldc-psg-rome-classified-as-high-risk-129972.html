Sources indicate that after the international break, Paris Saint-Germain will return to the Champions League with the reception of AS Roma at the Parc des Princes on November 25. A meeting placed at very high risk according to the French authorities, as indicated RMC Sport. If, usually, the visitor parking lot at the PSG grounds can accommodate between 2,000 and 2,300 supporters depending on the matches, the capacity of this area of ​​the Parc des Princes will be reduced to 1,500 spectators for the Italians, by order of the Paris Police Prefecture and the National Division for the Fight against Hooliganism (DNLH).

This measure is explained in particular by the close proximity between certain fervent supporters of Paris Saint-Germain and those of Napoli. This attachment could lead to a rise in tension around this meeting with the AS Roma ultras. UEFA must also rule on this situation, which requires 5% of the stadium to be reserved for visiting fans.
