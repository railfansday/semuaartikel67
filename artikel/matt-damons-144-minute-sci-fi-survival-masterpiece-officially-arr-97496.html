📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Matt Damon is riding high on the success of The Odyssey this year. After multiple collaborations with Christopher Nolan, Damon finally plays the leading man in his latest movie. The highly anticipated feature was not only technically perfect but also had compelling performances, yielding a billion-dollar gross at the box office, with fans rewatching the brilliant piece of cinema in theatres.

Damon has a versatile filmography; in the same year, he appeared in a streaming-only feature like The Rip and shouldered the big-budget R-rated feature. His filmography is full of brilliant titles, like playing the titular role in The Talented Mr. Ripley and in Steven Spielberg’s Saving Private Ryan. Playing real-life characters in Air, where he plays Nike executive Sonny Vaccaro, Carroll Shelby in Ford v Ferrari and Leslie Groves in Oppenheimer. But The Bourne franchise has a separate fan base and is probably the crown jewel of his filmography.

Among the long list of titles, there’s a feature which is thematically close to The Odyssey. He plays a resourceful hero stranded far from home and yearns to return. The feature in question is Ridley Scott’s The Martian. It casts Damon as an astronaut, Mark Watney, who strives to survive on Mars when a space mission goes wrong. While he undergoes different challenges attempting to stay alive on Mars, his colleagues at NASA exhaust all options to bring him home. The Martian is very close to The Odyssey with its “stranded hero” archetype, who fights overwhelming odds to return home. Like Odysseus, Watney too relies on his cleverness and strategy to combat an unforgiving environment. Perhaps the biggest similarity is the “Bring Matt Damon home” trope that fans have noted across many of his films. Now, after watching The Odyssey, fans are returning to movies that follow the trope.

For fans who want to see The Martian, they're in luck, as the movie just got more accessible. The Martian is coming to Hulu on December 1, as per ComicBook. The 91% Rotten Tomatoes-rated sci-fi survival depicts an astronaut's struggle to survive on Mars after being left behind and NASA's efforts to return him to Earth. Along with Damon, the feature also stars Jessica Chastain, Jeff Daniels, Kristen Wiig, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Sean Bean, Michael Peña, Kate Mara, Sebastian Stan, and Mackenzie Davis. With compelling performances and a gripping narrative, the film earned $653.6 million worldwide on a $108 million budget and is often considered one of Ridley Scott’s best movies.

Check it out on Hulu on December 1 and stay tuned to Collider for more such updates.
