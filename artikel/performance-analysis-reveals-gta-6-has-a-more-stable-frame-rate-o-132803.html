Performance analysis reveals GTA 6 has a more stable frame-rate on Xbox Series X compared to PlayStation 5

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, if you don't own a PlayStation 5 Pro, the best way to play Grand Theft Auto 6 is reportedly on Xbox Series X. That's according to newly introduced performance analysis, which highlights Microsoft's console is more consistent at maintaining the game's 30fps frame-rate target than the base PS5.

The findings come from the tech experts Digital Foundry, who published a significant report covering all the technical similarities and differences between Sony and Microsoft's consoles.

Unsurprisingly, PS5 Pro is in the lead with no competition, as Xbox elected not to release a mid-generation upgrade for the Series console family. Sony's more powerful console delivers "the definitive console GTA 6 experience," according to Digital Foundry. The PS5 Pro has the most stable 30fps performance of all four consoles, and benefits from a sharper presentation at 4K thanks to Sony's PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) image upscaler, which upscales the image from a 1440p base resolution.

Sources indicate that it's when comparing the base consoles - Xbox Series X and PS5 - that things get a little more interesting. Both target a 30fps frame-rate and often maintain that target, but certain sections trigger drops. According to the report, those include sequences with busy crowds and fast vehicle traversal. In some cases, the drops can be as low as mid-to-high 20fps.

With that in mind, PS5 has "substantially more frequent" frame-rate drops compared to Series X. The Xbox console is also aided by its support for 120Hz Variable Refresh Rate (VRR), which helps smooth out small dips in the frame-rate.

"Ultimately, Series X is meaningfully better off," Digital Foundry concluded.

Despite the studio's close marketing alignment with PlayStation historically - to the point Xbox can't even say GTA 6 in certain posts about GTA 6 - this is not the first time a Rockstar game has run better or looked better on an Xbox console.

It has been revealed that at Red Dead Redemption 2's launch in 2018, Digital Foundry hailed Xbox One X as the best place to play it, owing to - unsurprisingly - a more stable 30fps frame-rate and sharper visuals. Not much has changed in the past eight years on that front, then.

It has been revealed that given all the information we have today, including the lack of 60fps and 40fps modes on PS5 Pro, it's going to be really interesting to see the kind of PC specs required to achieve parity with console visuals at 60fps and beyond. If Rockstar doesn't get there first, modders are already working on porting GTA 6 to PC.
