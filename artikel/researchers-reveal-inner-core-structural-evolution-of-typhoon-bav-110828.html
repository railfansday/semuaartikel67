Researchers reveal inner-core structural evolution of Typhoon Bavi using multisensor satellite observations

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (51 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Tropical cyclones frequently undergo rapid, complex structural changes within their inner cores, such as eyewall replacement and wind-field expansion, that strongly influence typhoon intensity and coastal hazards. However, capturing these multilayered transformations continuously from the ocean surface to the upper cloud tops remains a major observational challenge.
