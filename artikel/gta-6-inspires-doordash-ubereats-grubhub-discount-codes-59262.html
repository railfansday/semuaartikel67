📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) was finally released into the wild on Nov. 19, unveiling the beloved video game franchise's first title in 13 years. The highly anticipated game even inspired eager fans to take time off work in favor of immersing themselves in GTA 6, reveling in everything from crashing planes to home renovation in-game.

According to recent reports, yet while our virtual avatars engage in miscellaneous violent crimes, our physical bodies still require sustenance. Food delivery apps such as Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Grubhub have thus jumped on the GTA 6 hype, offering discount codes to those too locked in to their marathon gaming session to cook.

DoorDash's GTA 6 discounts are the most limited in terms of food options, offering $6 off Cheesecake Factory orders of $20 or more. Customers just need to enter the discount code CAKEGTA6 when placing their DoorDash order, with the offer only available on Nov. 19.

The firm is also offering $15 off orders of $20 or more from Wendy's with codes given out on the Wendy's Twitch stream until 9 p.m. ET / 6 p.m. PT (check the scrolling bar at the top of the screen). However, these DoorDash codes are limited to the first 100 customers, and only valid for 30 minutes after they're released.

Industry observers note that uber Eats' discount code ONAMISSION will save users $10 off an order of $20 or more, and isn't restricted to a specific restaurant. Uber Eats has indicated this code is specifically for GTA 6's launch day, so if you're interested you should redeem it sooner rather than later.

According to recent reports, grubhub's discount code OUTOFOFFICE offered $10 off orders over $10, and was active from 9 a.m. ET / 6 a.m. PT on GTA 6 launch day. Unfortunately, it was limited to the first 1,000 customers, with some people reporting that they were unable to use the code within minutes of the promotion being posted. Less than an hour and a half later, Grubhub confirmed that its GTA 6-inspired discount code had been exhausted.

In a recent development, food delivery apps have apparently received an influx of orders surrounding GTA 6's launch. The Fresh York Times reports that DoorDash's energy drink sales spiked by over 20 percent on Wednesday, when many Grand Theft Auto fans stayed up for the game's midnight launch. DoorDash's Wednesday night sales were reportedly up by double digits in multiple U.S. states, while UberEats' overnight sales increased by 30 percent in Miami (the inspiration behind GTA 6's Vice City).

The next few days may be busy for food delivery workers, with gamers expected to spend hours exploring GTA 6. As such, remember to be patient and tip well. No doubt they'd rather be playing as well.
