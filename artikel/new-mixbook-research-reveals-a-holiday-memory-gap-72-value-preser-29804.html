📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Two 2026 studies find 62% of Americans plan to continue their most cherished childhood traditions, while 39% have too many holiday photos to sort through.

Industry observers note that REDWOOD CITY, Calif., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Most Americans plan to carry their favorite childhood holiday traditions into this season, and they want to hold on to the photos that capture them. According to the 2026 Holiday Traditions Survey from Mixbook, 62% of Americans who celebrate winter holidays plan to keep every tradition tied to their fondest childhood memories. In Mixbook's 2026 Holiday Photo Report, 72% say preserving holiday photos is very or extremely crucial.

Revisiting those photos is another story, and the result is a holiday memory gap. In the Traditions Survey, about 3 in 4 (76%) look at holiday photos or videos once a year or less. It isn't for lack of caring. In the Photo Report, 39% say they have too many holiday photos to sort through, and 30% say they don't have the time.

According to recent reports, "The holiday traditions that stay with us are often the simplest ones, like the same recipe, the same song, or gathering with the people we love," said Andrew Laffoon, CEO and Co-Founder of Mixbook. "What these studies show is that people care deeply about holding on to those moments, but saving a photo is only the beginning. The real value comes when we revisit those memories, share the stories behind them, and pass them on.".

"People aren't short on holiday photos. They're short on time and an easy way to do something meaningful with them," stated Leslie Albertson, Senior Director of Brand at Mixbook. "Hundreds of photos can feel overwhelming, so we're making it simple to find the ones you love and make something you'll actually enjoy and share.".

Industry observers note that the Mixbook app helps people rediscover the photos already on their phones and turn them into photo books, calendars, cards, and shareable Memory Art. With Story Mode, which launched earlier this year, people can start a photo book with a simple conversation about the memory instead of a blank page.

Full results, including state-by-state rankings, are available in the 2026 Holiday Traditions Survey and the 2026 Holiday Photo Report.

The 2026 Holiday Traditions Survey polled more than 2,300 U.S. adults across 46 states in September 2026. All respondents celebrate at least one winter holiday tradition. The 2026 Holiday Photo Report surveyed more than 2,200 U.S. adults across 43 states in October 2026. Respondents primarily use an iPhone and self-reported their 2025 and December 2025 photo counts from the Photos app.

Recent findings suggest that mixbook is a creative photo and memory platform whose photo books have been rated #1 by NYT Wirecutter from 2021 to 2026. Co-founded in 2006 by Andrew Laffoon and Aryk Grosz and based in Redwood City, California, Mixbook helps people turn the photos on their phones into photo books, cards, calendars, home decor, and digital Stories they can relive and share. Over 20 years, Mixbook has earned the trust of more than 6 million customers and over 36,000 five-star reviews.
