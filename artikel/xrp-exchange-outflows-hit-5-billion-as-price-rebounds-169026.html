XRP has seen traders withdraw large amounts of tokens out of major crypto exchanges, especially Binance, over the last 30 days even as the broad market volatility persists.

Despite the intense selling pressure XRP has witnessed over the past few months, data from the crypto analytics platform Cryptoquant indicates that XRP’s exchange outflow has reached 5 billion XRP over the last month.

While the massive 30-day exchange outflow of 5 billion XRP is a relief signal because it signals persistent demand for XRP even amid the intense volatility, further data reveals that the outflow is relatively low compared to levels seen in October.

Although the 5 billion XRP outflow is significant and November has yet to wrap up, the data reveals that it marks a decent decrease from October, when total exchange outflows for the month reached about 5.7 billion XRP.

Despite the 700,000 XRP decrease in the metric, the outflows registered in the last 30 days are still considered substantial and have drawn attention from market analysts as they signal strong investor conviction even as XRP suffered larger price losses this month compared with October.

Usually, increases in the exchange outflow of an asset suggest that investors are moving assets off exchanges into external wallets, most likely to hold them for longer periods.

Hence, this metric provides a bullish signal for XRP as it indicates strong demand among traders, putting Binance in the spotlight as over a billion XRP were withdrawn from the exchange during the period.

While OKX and Bybit saw traders withdraw 865.27 million XRP and 697.65 million XRP from their platforms, respectively, Binance registered the highest XRP withdrawals, with about 1.439 billion XRP leaving its exchange over the period.
