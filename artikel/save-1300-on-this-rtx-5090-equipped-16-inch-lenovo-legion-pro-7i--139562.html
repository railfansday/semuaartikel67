It has been revealed that the 16-inch Lenovo Legion Pro 7i gaming laptop comes with an Intel Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus CPU, RTX 5090 (175W TGP) GPU, 32GB of DDR5 memory, and 1TB of storage. The OLED display offers a 240Hz refresh rate and a 2560 x 1600 resolution.
