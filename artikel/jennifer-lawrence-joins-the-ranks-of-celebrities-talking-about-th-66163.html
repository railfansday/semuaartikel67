📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jennifer Lawrence has always leaned toward honesty in interviews, earning her a reputation as one of Hollywood’s most relatable celebrities. And as online chatter about plastic surgery—who did it, and what did they do—reaches a fever pitch, Lawrence is setting the record straight by openly discussing her “new” breast implants.

During an appearance on Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen, alongside her Hunger Games co-star Josh Hutcherson, Lawrence told Cohen offhandedly that she “has implants.” When Cohen asked, she said they’re new and credited Santa Monica–based plastic surgeon Steven Teitelbaum.

Lawrence went on to say she went with a full-C implant, restoring her breasts to the “same size they were”—meaning, before she had two kids. This is the second time in recent months that the Oscar winner has admitted to a postpartum plastic surgery procedure. In an interview with The Cut for her new haircare brand, Mes, Lawrence admitted to getting a breast lift following the birth of her second child. “Everything bounced back, pretty much, after the first one,” the actress told The New Yorker in October 2025. “Second one, nothing bounced back.”.

She took that sentiment even further on an October 2026 episode of Call Her Daddy, where she gave host Alex Cooper, who was then pregnant, a frank piece of motherly wisdom: “Your vagina will bounce back, your butthole won’t.”.

It has been revealed that lawrence is the latest in a string of A-list celebrities opening up about cosmetic treatments, like Kylie Jenner, who made waves last year when she admitted on TikTok to her exact implant size and surgeon. From Lawrence, the casual admission feels less like a calculated confession and more like advice from a trusted friend who doesn’t hold back about motherhood and pregnancy’s physical toll.
