Luke Roberts put Danny Hollands’ side ahead after eight minutes and Henry Mcdonald doubled the lead 17 minutes later.

Liam Ferdinand pulled one back for H&Y four minutes into the second half and that made the remainder of the game more tense for the home fans than it might have been. But the Rocks held on and remain third in the league, three points behind leaders Egham.

The Rocks visit second-placed Jersey Bulls – who won 6-0 at Littlehampton – next weekend.
