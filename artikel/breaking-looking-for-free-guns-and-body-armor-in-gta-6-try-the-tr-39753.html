Remember how stealing a cop car in the older GTA games used to get you a free shotgun? Well, turns out Vice City's police force is even better armed. If you need some extra guns quickly, particularly shotguns and assault rifles, filching them from the trunk of a police car is a great method, though obviously it comes with certain risks.

Recent findings suggest that you might have realised, but stealing cars in GTA 6 is significantly more complicated than previous games, especially if they're alarmed. Thankfully, this doesn't apply to any police pickup trucks like the Bravado Bison the cops sometimes use, which you'll spot occasionally both in rural areas and in the city.

With the police pickups, you can simply climb onto the bed at the back and open the storage crate, grabbing any weapons and ammo inside, including other potential items like Stun Guns, without setting off any alarms.

Sources indicate that for proper police cruisers, though, their trunks are alarmed along with the doors, meaning you'll need a tool to deal with them, the slim jim, which you get during the "Moses" mission in Chapter 2. If you slim jim the door of the cop car and get inside, the trunk will unlock, so you can head around the back, interact to open it, and steal whatever's in the boot without tackling the alarm, whether it be an assault rifle, shotgun, or even body armor.

If you don't have the slim jim yet, you can still open the trunk (you'll see your character use a screwdriver) but the alarm will go off.

Industry observers note that once you've collected the weapons, you can interact with your own car trunk, and you'll see a prompt to stow the weapons inside. You'll be rather conspicuous wandering around with an assault rifle in your hands or strapped to your back, after all.

Obviously, it's best not to try and steal cop cars with cops in them, but you can find them around, or parked at police stations, such as in Port Gellhorn or Vice City.

Sources indicate that more generally, it's definitely worth looting random car trunks to see what you can find. Sometimes they contain cash, drugs, handguns, or consumables—just make sure to scan them with the Wainkit App first (which you're introduced to in Chapter 1) to tell if they're alarmed or not, and whether you can break in without the slim jim.
