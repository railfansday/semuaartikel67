Heath were hoping to extend their good run of just one defeat in 12 matches, while the Us were hoping to get back to winning ways to aid their push for promotion.

Chris Simmons’ Heath took the lead on 12 minutes when Zac Young received the ball wide on the right some 40 yards from goal and the Heath talisman advanced towards the Hastings goal before striking a left-foot shot from distance that beat Us goalkeeper Harry Haines at the near post.

The visitors best chance of the half came five minutes before the break when Sam Pidgeon worked his way to the edge of the Heath penalty area and hit a left foot shot which deflected off the Heath goalkeeper Alfie Hadfield and flew narrowly wide of the post – but it remained 1-0 a the break.

The Us started the second half well, forcing Heath on to the backfoot but failing to create any clear-cut chances.

Their most dangerous tactic seemed to be launching long throws into the Bears penalty area where they met by a solid-looking Broadbridge Heath defence marshalled by Lewis Hyde and supported by his protégé Jay Richardson.

But the Bears doubled their lead on the hour mark when Young received the ball wide on the right of the Hastings penalty area and forced his way forward, where he was challenged by a defender – bringing the Heath player crashing to the ground.

The match referee had no doubt about it being a foul, pointing immediately to the penalty spot, and MoM Young picked himself up, dusted himself down and hammered the spot kick into the back of the net for his second of the afternoon, helping his side to a 2-0 win.

The victory saw Broadbridge Heath move into one of the play-off places in seventh – just one point behind Hastings United.

Fixtures continue to come thick and fast with the Bears travelling to second-placed Erith Town on Tuesday evening before returning home again on Saturday to face leaders Faversham Town.

BBH: Hadfield, Thiselton, Royall, Marino (Church 65), Hyde (Peters 70), Richardson, Connell, Bejashvili (Rawlins 71), Buchanan, Hards (Doughty 90), Young.
