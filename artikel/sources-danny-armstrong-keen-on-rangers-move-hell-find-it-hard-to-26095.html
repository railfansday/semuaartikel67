Dinamo Bucharest winger Danny Armstrong is keen to move to Rangers in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 29-year-old has emerged as a target for Derek McInnes' side, after the wideman previously played under the Gers boss at Kilmarnock.

McInnes "knows all about" Armstrong as a player, and is understood to be hopeful of reuniting with him in the upcoming transfer windows.

Sources say that the player is also keen to return to Scotland, and would find the chance to move to Ibrox "hard to turn down" this winter.

Armstrong has been away from Scottish football since 2025, but has since been flourishing in Romania.

The forward has already notched six goals and 10 assists from just 15 gams for Dinamo this term, making him the most productive player in Romania's top flight.

Armstrong was already at Kilmarnock when McInnes first took the reins there back in January 2022, but he promptly became a key figure for the boss.

He starred in his team's surprise fourth-placed finish in the Premiership back in 2023-24, notching 16 league goal involvements along the way.

McInnes was a big fan of the wideman, who made 40 or more appearances in all competitions in each of his three campaigns in charge.

The pair both left Rugby Park in 2025, but only after they led them to within one game of a place in the league phase of the UEFA Conference League.

McInnes and Armstrong enjoyed a hugely successful relationship during their time together, and have since both gone on to newly introduced heights in their careers.

With both parties open to a deal in the coming months, it would be no surprise to see Armstrong return to Scotland with Rangers.

Former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness recently told Football Insider that Vanja Dragojevic's recent struggles may be down to him "not settling" well in Glasgow.

The midfielder joined Rangers from Partizan Belgrade in the summer, but has been starved of game time over the last few months.

Wyness believes that moving to Glasgow may have been a big culture shock for the 20-year-old, and pointed out that several players have struggled to adapt over the years.

Dragojevic remains highly-rated at Rangers, but has played just seven minuts of football since the end of August.
