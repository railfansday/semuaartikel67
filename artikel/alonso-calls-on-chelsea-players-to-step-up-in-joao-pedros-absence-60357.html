Xabi Alonso has called on his Chelsea players to step up in front of goal in the absence of the injured Joao Pedro.

Joao Pedro has missed Chelsea's last four games across all competitions, including three in the Premier League, after suffering a thigh injury.

The Blues have failed to score in two of those three league games without the Brazilian, losing to Tottenham and Sunderland either side of a win over Manchester United.

Industry observers note that three of Chelsea’s four Premier League defeats this season have come to nil, and without Joao Pedro playing. The Blues average 1.8 goals per league game with him in the starting XI since the start of last season, compared to 0.9 goals without him starting.

Joao Pedro has scored five goals and provided three assists in six league games this season, more than any other Chelsea player.

Only Erling Haaland and Evanilson (both nine) have directly contributed to more goals than Joao Pedro in the English top-flight this season, but Alonso does not want to use his striker's injury as an excuse for his side.

"[We need] the whole team for that," Alonso noted. "It's not just the strikers that can score. We can score from set-pieces, from defenders, from midfielders.
