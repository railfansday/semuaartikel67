Missouri has opened an investigation into a photography business after allegedly leaving numerous customers without the pictures they paid for or a refund.

According to a report by First Alert 4, Leave It to Beaver Photography — a professional family, newborn and wedding photography business based in Wentzville, Missouri — announced it had closed this week after 13 years in business.

Sources indicate that however, numerous customers have contacted First Alert 4 with complaints about the company. Some allege they paid for photography services covering events such as weddings, births and anniversaries, but never received their photographs or a refund.

According to recent reports, kristen Moran alleges she hired Leave It to Beaver Photography to take family photographs during a Memorial Day weekend camping trip. She also contracted the firm for a maternity shoot in July.

Moran mentioned the photographs were supposed to be delivered within 20 days, but they did not arrive as promised.

Recent findings suggest that “It came to months and months of her saying oh I have them, don’t worry about it. Just kind of getting the runaround,” Moran tells the news outlet.

Moran noted the photographs eventually arrived in late October, but by then she had already given birth and no longer needed the maternity images.

It has been revealed that “Late October she did send me the photos. She sent me like 300 of them. I was like okay, great. They’re not really relevant anymore, I already had my baby,” Moran claims.

Industry observers note that in another case, Monica Dannegger alleges she hired Leave It to Beaver Photography to photograph her wedding on December 12, 2026. Dannegger notes she paid the business $1,500, representing half the cost of photographing her wedding and an engagement party. However, she alleges the photographer did not attend the engagement party and that she has received no communication about the wedding. Dannegger also says she has been unable to obtain a refund.

In a recent development, “It’s unfortunate that her end of the bargain isn’t being upheld,” Dannegger tells First Alert 4. “I would have been left high and dry.”.

In a recent development, the Missouri attorney general has reportedly opened an investigation into the photography business, which operates in St. Charles County. The office is encouraging customers who believe they have been affected to file a complaint through its website. Wentzville police have also advised customers who paid for services they did not receive to file a police report. Officers say they will investigate.

First Alert 4 notes it had contacted Leave It to Beaver Photography for comment but had been unable to reach the photographer.

The news comes a wedding photographer was ordered to pay $1.7 million by the North Carolina Attorney General after leaving hundreds of customers without the services they paid for.
