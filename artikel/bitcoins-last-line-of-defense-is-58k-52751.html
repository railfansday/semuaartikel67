She notes her longer-term indicators for Bitcoin are somewhat constructive, pointing to a major low in June and July near long-term support between about $58,000 and $60,000. Bitcoin is headed back toward that support on negative short-term momentum, and she says there are no signs yet that the retracement is done. As long as it holds that range, she says, it preserves the potential for a long-term basing phase, backed by indicators like the monthly MACD and monthly stochastic oscillator.
