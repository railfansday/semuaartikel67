Oceanwide Expeditions Lands on Snow Hill Island in Antarctica

Oceanwide Sail Expeditions has announced that it concluded an expedition that landed guests at the emperor penguin colony on Snow Hill Island.

Guests onboard the Ortelius experienced the northernmost emperor penguin colony on Earth, offering an opportunity to observe adults and chicks.

Following IAATO guidelines, guests observed the colony in an intimate setting.

“This successful landing is a testament to the expertise and dedication of our teams, as well as the remarkable capabilities of the Ortelius,” stated Remi Bouysset, CEO of Oceanwide Expeditions.

“We are proud to work with some of the very best specialists in the industry, whose experience and professionalism consistently enable us to push the boundaries of polar exploration.”.

The tech giant said in a press release that operating in Antarctica, particularly in the Weddell Sea, requires extensive experience, careful planning and the ability to adapt to constantly changing conditions.

To maximize the chances of reaching Snow Hill Island, Oceanwide incorporated operational flexibility into its expedition planning.

The Ortelius, Oceanwide Expeditions’ ice-strengthened expedition vessel, is purpose-built for exploring some of the most remote and challenging regions on Earth.

The company added that the ship’s capabilities, combined with the experience and dedication of its crew and expedition team, make it suited to expeditions into the Weddell Sea.

In 2025 and 2026, the ship set a new farthest south record for a non-icebreaker vessel in the Weddell Sea as part of the company’s “Remote Weddell Sea Explorer” itinerary, set to return for a final time later this Antarctic season.

Two further In Search of the Emperor Penguin Snow Hill Island itineraries are scheduled in the coming weeks, operated by Oceanwide, with additional landings anticipated later this month and in early December.

The itinerary is set to return in the manufacturer’s 2027-28 and 2028-29 seasons.
