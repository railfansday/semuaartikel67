Breaking: 350 years ago, Ole Rømer showed that light has a finite speed

🌐 Source: Flipso • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (42 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Ole Rømer used Io’s eclipses to show that light does not travel instantaneously. In 1676, he predicted a delayed appearance of Jupiter’s moon Io and was right, providing the first solid evidence that light takes time to travel.
