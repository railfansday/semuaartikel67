We may have a proper title race on our hands as City and Chelsea drop points and LCL strengthen their grip on third place.

Drew Spence rolled back the years with a vintage performance as her Tottenham side produced a shock 3-1 victory away against the defending champions, Manchester City. The 34-year-old marshalled the midfield and then had the vision to thread an outstanding through ball that split the City defence and found the run of Matilda Vinberg, who put Spurs 2-1 up. “She’s unbelievable. There are so many more gears in Drew and I wish I’d been able to work with her a bit earlier [in my career] because her leadership is second to none,” the Tottenham head coach, Martin Ho, said of the club captain. “Her performance levels, since I’ve been here, week on week have grown. She is covering every blade of grass and leads by example. For me, she’s one of the best midfielders in the league.” Tom Garry.
