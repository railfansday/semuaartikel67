Resy has just rolled out the Resy Rewards program, which lets American Express card members in the United States earn an extra 4x points per dollar spent at participating Resy restaurants, on top of their credit card rewards. As you’d expect, it’s not quite that straightforward, but I’d still consider this to be pretty exciting!

Industry observers note that american Express owns both Resy and Tock (which is now being integrated into Resy), as part of its larger “lifestyle” play for attracting card members. So along with that, Resy is leaning more into the rewards program side of things, with the new Resy Rewards program offering an extra 4x Amex points per dollar spent at eligible Resy restaurants.

Resy Rewards is first launching in New York City, where users will see a new “Rewards” tab within the Resy iOS app, where they can browse and book eligible restaurants. Restaurants participating in the Resy Rewards launch include Carne Mare, Le Crocodile, Hearth, Kiko, OPTO, Le Pavillon, and more. In 2027, Resy Rewards plans to expand to additional U.S. cities, and select Tock venues will become eligible to participate in the program.

According to recent reports, so, how exactly does Resy Rewards work? Card members need to enroll a US-based Membership Rewards program eligible card in Resy Rewards, then book a Resy Rewards reservation through the “Rewards” tab in the Resy iOS app, and then pay the bill for the dining experience with the enrolled card. So this requires a little more jumping through hoops than just using an Amex card Resy credit, for example.

Recent findings suggest that these rewards are in addition to whatever the standard rewards are on an Amex card. For example, the American Express® Gold Card (learn more) ordinarily earns 4x points at restaurants globally, so if you dined at a restaurant eligible for Resy Rewards, you could be earning 8x points per dollar spent, which is pretty awesome.

Here’s how Pablo Rivero, CEO of Resy and SVP of American Express Global Dining, describes this:.

“For the first time, we’re bringing American Express’s industry-leading Rewards program into the Resy iOS app and connecting two things our Card Members value deeply: great dining experiences and meaningful rewards. As we’ve seen with the success of the Resy Credit, rewarding diners while driving demand and revenue to Resy restaurants is a win on both sides of the table.”.

I’m delighted to see Amex introduce an opportunity to earn points for dining out, beyond the bonus categories already offered on some of the issuer’s most popular cards. For the time being, this is rather niche, given that it’s limited to select restaurants in Latest York City, but the expectation is that it will expand more broadly in 2027 (though it remains to be seen how broadly).

Essentially think of this as being similar to a dining rewards program, as the idea is that restaurants have to opt-in to participate in this, with the hope being that this drums up business for them (and presumably they pay Amex and Resy something extra in exchange for that).

Furthermore, you do have to specifically reserve your reservation through Resy to qualify for this, so if you don’t have a reservation made through the “Rewards” tab on Resy, then you won’t earn bonus points.

I’m happy to see this, as earning 8x points on dining when paying with the Amex Gold Card is quite a great deal. Then again, so much of my dining out is through InKind, which is my favorite dining rewards program, given the excellent selection of restaurants the program has in Miami, and it’s tough to compete with the rewards offered by the program.

Recent findings suggest that the new Resy Rewards program offers bonus Amex points when you make a reservation at an eligible restaurant and then pay with your eligible Amex card. The idea is that you can earn an extra 4x points per dollar spent on purchases at eligible restaurants, which is pretty awesome.

The catch is that this is initially launching in New York City, only “eligible” restaurants qualify, and you need to reserve through Resy and then pay with the same linked card in order to earn the bonus points. It’s nice to see this launch, and I look forward to it expanding to more markets.
