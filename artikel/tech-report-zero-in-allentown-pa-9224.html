I perfectly re-create last Sunday’s exact itinerary featuring coffee, reflective and comparative blog writing, pizza, drinks, and sports.

As my hike winds down, I can’t help but compare my endeavor to the F1 season that I have followed all year long. For many weeks, if not months, it feels like the championship winner has all but been decided. Ever since I finished the White Mountains in Upcoming Hampshire, I have felt that it was a matter of when, not if, I would finish my hike. As shared last Sunday, Kimi Antonelli got the job done and secured his crown. I have 2 weeks remaining, but there is little doubt I’ll complete my double hike of the AT by the end of the year. I expect to have the job done as planned the weekend before Thanksgiving.

F1 introduced completely new cars for this year, and I tried a new approach to the year as well. It began as an experiment. Could I spend nearly all day hiking for consecutive days? If I adjusted to that, then, could I adjust to sleeping in a cramped tent in the woods? Once I got comfortable with that, could I stay consistent and hike enough to complete the AT twice by the end of the year? I took it one week and even one day at a time and, as it turns out, it seems I can do all of the above. It wasn’t always smooth, but I proved that where there is a will there is a way if you are just willing to try something.

My Sunday was just about a carbon copy of last week, starting with an early arrival at the grocery store to pick out some breakfast before heading to the cafe for bottomless coffees. The Formula One Grand Prix in Brazil coverage began at 11:00AM. With the championship already decided it could have been a dull race, but a few passing showers turned up the pressure on the teams and drivers and gave viewers a few edge of their seat moments. I won’t be able to watch the next race live, it takes place in Las Vegas and starts around midnight eastern time. If all goes well, I should be on the trail attempting to finish off my double hike with the 4 state challenge.

After the race I took an afternoon lunch break followed by the chore of bagging up the week’s food. I returned to the cafe to watch the post race show and put the finishing touches on today’s blog post. With my itinerary already in good shape for the upcoming week I officially had down time to enjoy. All I wanted to do was listen to music and drink raspberry iced tea into the night. Another long week was in the books!

So far “Rocksylvania” has been good to me. I can’t complain about the weather or even the trail conditions. I’ve certainly been more frustrated on trail than I was at any point this week.

According to recent reports, I seem to have passed 4,000 miles hiked this year on Tuesday, simply incredible. True, there are some shortcuts not reflected there, but neither are the countless spur trails to shelters, overlooks, springs, and trailheads. Of those 4,000 miles, 3,000 have come with my backpack on. I seem to recall at some point it being crucial to me that I backpack at least the distance of the AT this year. Well, I’ve gone some 807.9 miles beyond that but who’s counting?

Looking ahead, there isn’t much to say about my next-to-last week on the trail except I might have some cold weather heading my way. I am determined to continue tenting, my setup is warm enough, but I am not much of a morning person when the temperature outside starts with a 3!

I’ll also be on the lookout for the 1,200 mile marker, the last of the 100 mile markers I need to complete my collection.

Edit: It looks like I may have just been assigned a mission for tomorrow. We shall…see.
