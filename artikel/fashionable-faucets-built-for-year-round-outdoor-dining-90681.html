Temps are continuing to cool down in many parts of the country, but that doesn’t mean the party has to move inside just yet. The COTO Series by California Faucets is designed to extend outdoor entertaining, with 316 marine-grade stainless steel engineered to resist corrosion in the face of damp weather and fluctuating temperatures.

Recent findings suggest that the durable finish is elevated by a sleek arcing design and customizable elements that allow homeowners to tailor the fixture to their needs. High- or low-arc spout options and an optional pull-down sprayer are joined with a range of complementary accessories featuring the same durable material as the faucets themselves.

Industry observers note that of the eight finish options available, five offer Physical Vapor Deposition coatings. Applied with a vacuum-based process that deposits vaporized material onto the surface, PVD coatings are thin, durable, and designed to provide a high level of protection against discoloration, scratches, and corrosion.

So come wind, rain, and changing temperatures, the only weather you’ll need to worry about is the forecast for your next dinner party. Visit calfaucets.com to find an online dealer or a showroom near you.
