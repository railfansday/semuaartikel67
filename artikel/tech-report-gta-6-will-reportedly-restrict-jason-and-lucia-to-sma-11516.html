GTA 6 will begin with a more gradual progression system for players, with Jason and Lucia unable to commit major crimes from the start of the game.

Grand Theft Auto VI is less than a week away, releasing worldwide on November 19. The soundtrack album was leaked on November 14, with 34-tracks across a four-side-long double album.

Now, recent news has revealed that deuteragonists Jason and Lucia will be limited at the start of the game, including the extent of the crimes that they are able to engage in.

According to The Guardian, Jason and Lucia will start out as small-time criminals, meaning players will have to work their way up before attempting larger heists.

This includes restrictions on stealing vehicles, with Jason and Lucia initially only able to hotwire lower-end cars. More expensive vehicles, including supercars, will remain inaccessible until players develop more advanced abilities.

The same progression system will reportedly apply to breaking into buildings, with more sophisticated infiltration methods becoming available later in the game.

Recent findings suggest that players will also have to be more strategic when planning robberies, rather than simply turning up at a bank and attempting to steal its contents. Instead, they’ll need to scope out targets and consider potential security measures, including surveillance cameras and alarms.

As per Forbes, Rockstar’s Rob Nelson explained that the changes are partly intended to support the game’s narrative, saying: “Narratively, it doesn’t make a lot of sense for them to go out and cause as much carnage and make as much noise as they can.”.
