Latest Update: The Guest in Residence x Moon Boot Collab Is Back for Round Two

Cashmere sweaters and leather jackets may be in full rotation right now, but with snow season just a whisper away, we’ll all be swapping for puffer coats and thick socks before we know it. Usually that also means pulling out chunky boots that do more for warmth than they do for an outfit. But Gigi Hadid’s Guest in Residence and Moon Boot are here to make finding non-hideous winter boots a lot easier. Launching November 17, the two brands will release a second collaboration of five special-edition boots, each one pairing Guest in Residence’s colorful hand-knit textures with Moon Boot’s cushy soles and weatherproof materials, fully built for any chilly-day adventure—in the city or the mountains.

Industry observers note that the two brands are putting forth the notion that a snow boot can look just as good as your favorite sweater, and this round further homes in on that idea. “Each silhouette tells a story that feels nostalgic and familiar yet entirely fresh at the same time,” Hadid shares. The new styles hark back to vintage themes—think alpine motifs and old cabin textiles. The Icon Knit Postcard features a custom knit landscape of snowy pines that looks like a walk through the woods and comes in an ice and red colorway or dark brown and sand. The Nolace Fair Isle is low-profile and pairs a classic Fair Isle pattern with a fuzzy fleece texture. For something more pared back, the Icon Low Signature comes in suede with pastel laces, blue on the pale pair and pink on the brown. The Ltrack Cable Knit adds wool and alpaca cable-knit. Every style has its own playful vibe.

Hadid started Guest in Residence in 2022 and it swiftly became the place to get really good colorful cashmere sweaters. She’s also been wearing Moon Boots for years, so teaming up with the brand was a no-brainer. “We’ve brought our vibrant palettes and retro influences to Moon Boot’s futuristic silhouettes, and it’s been fun to explore new expressions of our partnership,” Hadid claims.

Recent findings suggest that and no, you don’t need to be a prolific skier to put them to use. Built on Moon Boot’s signature soles, they’re as useful on a slick sidewalk as they are on the slopes. Moon Boot itself was never strictly for the snow either. It started in 1969 as an après-ski boot inspired by what the astronauts wore on the moon. If you are venturing out to the slopes this season, Guest in Residence just released an eight-piece ski capsule with Halfdays, the label cofounded by former Olympic freestyle skier Kiley McKinnon, so you can match your boots to your bib. The line is featured within the brands’ joint campaign, shot in faux airport terminals and cabins, with all manner of cable knits, cozy thermals, and necessary footwear for a trip to the mountains.

Sources indicate that whether you’re looking for a statement shoe to complete your ski suit or a low-profile ankle boot to polish off your winter wares, there’s a pair that fits the bill in the collaboration. Prices range from $395 to $495, and the collection is available starting November 17 at Moon Boot and Guest in Residence. Vogue readers can shop the drop exclusively 24 hours before the rest on Vogue Shopping.
