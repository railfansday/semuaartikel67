The fight over Heathrow's third runway has been about whether it fits net zero. Lawmakers have now added a more basic question: who pays, and how much ends up in fares? Until ministers can answer that, and price in the cost of cutting emissions, the initiative is stalled.

British lawmakers have called for aims for a third runway at Heathrow to be paused, saying ministers haven't shown what it will cost airlines and passengers or how it fits the UK’s climate targets.

The chair of the cross-party Transport Committee Ruth Cadbury remarked plans for an extra runway at the country's busiest airport, and a key global hub, “should be grounded for now,” and that the government should go back to the drawing board.
