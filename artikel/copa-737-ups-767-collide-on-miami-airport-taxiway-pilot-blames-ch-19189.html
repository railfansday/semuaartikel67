Copa 737 & UPS 767 Collide On Miami Airport Taxiway: Pilot Blames Checklist Distraction

In a recent development, last night, a Copa Boeing 737 and a UPS Boeing 767 collided on a taxiway at Miami International Airport (MIA). South Florida is known for its horrendous drivers, and I guess that trend is making its way to the taxiways at airports as well.

According to recent reports, this accident happened shortly after 9PM on Saturday, November 14, 2026. It involves a Copa Boeing 737-800 that had just arrived from Panama City (PTY) as flight CM427, and a UPS Boeing 767-300 that was getting ready to depart to Bogota (BOG) as flight 5X358.

Sources indicate that the UPS 767 had been advised to give way to the Copa 737 on the taxiway, but the pilots failed to do that. As a result, the wing of the Copa 737 hit the nose of the UPS 767. While a full investigation will of course be performed, the air traffic control audio gives us a good sense of what happened:.

Sources indicate that copa pilot: “427, somebody hit us.”ATC: “427, roger. UPS, you’re supposed to give way to the 73 there.”UPS pilot: “Roger, we running checklist, runway change.”ATC: “UPS 358, did you hit that aircraft?”UPS pilot: “Yeah, we went too far, and then he clipped us with the wing.”.

So as you can see, the UPS pilots were at fault here, and the excuse seems to be that they were distracted because they were going through checklists because of a runway change.

According to recent reports, fortunately there were no major injuries, but two people on the Copa flight did complain of minor back pain. The damage to the planes is extensive, though, including to the nose of the UPS 767 and the left wing of the Copa 737.
