If you’ve been thinking about buying an electric bike, scooter or electric unicycle, Black Friday and Cyber Monday are bringing some good opportunities to save. Segway, Apollo, Inmotion and several other manufacturers have marked down their rides, with discounts ranging from $100 to more than $1,000.

According to recent reports, I’ve had several of these models in-house for testing, from everyday commuters to bikes capable of carrying passengers and cargo. Some also offer features riders will appreciate, including automatic lighting, turn signals, Apple Find My support and rear-approach detection.

Saving money shouldn’t mean compromising on safety. That’s why I’m focusing on models with UL-certified electrical systems, specifically UL 2849 for e-bikes and UL 2272 for electric scooters and unicycles. You can also add the optional Rearview Radar for $100 to receive alerts about vehicles approaching from behind.

At $400 off, it’s worth considering if you’re looking for an everyday commuter with some useful technology built in.

The bike has automatic lighting, integrated turn signals and Apple Find My support, which can help locate the bike if it goes missing. It also includes the $100 Rearview Radar option.

The Muxi is an interesting alternative for errands and shorter trips where you might otherwise grab the car.

Segway has additional deals on the Xafari and Xyber, with advertised savings reaching $1,300.

It has been revealed that don’t get too caught up in the discount. Make sure the bike, scooter or unicycle fits your needs, particularly when it comes to rider weight, range, portability and storage. Manufacturers’ range estimates are generally based on favorable conditions, and your actual mileage will vary depending on weight, speed, hills and temperature.

Apple Find My and other tracking systems can help locate a missing ride, but they’re no substitute for a good physical lock. And when it comes to battery safety, look for the appropriate UL certification, use the manufacturer-approved charger and never charge your bike or scooter where it blocks your exit.
