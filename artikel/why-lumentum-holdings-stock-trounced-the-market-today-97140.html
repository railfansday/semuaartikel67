Monday was a banner day for investors in tech hardware companies, especially those that make crucial components for next-generation products. For proof, look no further than advanced optical components specialist Lumentum Holdings (LITE +18.20%), whose stock popped by more than 18% on a day when the S&P 500 index rose by under 5%. The company mainly has an crucial business partner to thank for that.

Lumentum's partner is none other than Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%), which dramatically brought back controversy-tinged Jensen Huang as its CEO shortly after he stepped down from the position.

Recent findings suggest that his return strongly indicates a sharp bounce-back to the status quo for the company, the world's leading purveyor of artificial intelligence (AI) chips. This is an encouraging development for the numerous component suppliers used by Nvidia; Lumentum, which focuses on highly specialized and sophisticated optical technology, is an crucial one.

It has been revealed that lumentum is currently being acquired by Marvell Technology (MRVL +18.37%), another AI chipmaker. Although Marvell was once considered a rival to Nvidia, the two companies have increased their collaboration; moreover, Nvidia holds a significant equity stake in Marvell.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

It has been revealed that it isn't easy or comfortable to switch suppliers, particularly in the high-demand, fast-paced world of AI hardware. I don't think Lumentum's position as an notable partner of Nvidia was in much jeopardy no matter who was running the latter company (or, for that matter, who was acquiring Lumentum). Still, we should never underestimate the power of a relief rally. Lumentum is a play more on the Marvell acquisition than anything else, however, so investors should consider it on that basis first and foremost.
