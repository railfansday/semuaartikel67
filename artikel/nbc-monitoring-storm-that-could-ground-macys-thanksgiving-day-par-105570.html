📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the 100th celebration of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City is finding itself at the mercy of the weather gods. It’s made it through rain and even snow, but this time it’s facing an incoming storm that is threatening to bring heavy winds. If things get too windy, the iconic balloons could be grounded.

According to recent reports, this would mark the first time since 1971 that the balloons would be grounded. Sources indicate that balloons are grounded when sustained winds reach 24 mph or wind gusts reach 35 mph. Any decisions regarding balloon flight will be made on parade morning and will be based on weather conditions.

NBC is closely monitoring the weather forecast, with safety being its top priority.

As previously reported, this year’s parade is titled “The Greatest Celebration,” in honor of its centennial anniversary with over 100 floats and 100 balloons.

Among the balloons that are set to take flight are Sorcerer’s Apprentice Mickey and Joe Cool.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s a massive deal for the folks at Disney,” Brendan Kennedy, director of creative production at Macy’s Studio told Variety. “It’s not an IP that generally comes out, except for a huge celebration. It’s our 100th parade, and they were really excited to join us. It was just a joy to get close to Mickey, but the Sorcerer’s Apprentice is so special. It’s that next level of elevated magic that blends really well with Macy’s.”.

Another highlight is Snoopy, who returns to the parade in a new role as Joe Cool. “Joe Cool is what happens when Snoopy is pretending to be a student at school. He’s never been in the parade before in this form, which makes it really fun,” Kennedy said. “We’re excited to bring him and Woodstock together. I was on the Parade Studio floor yesterday and saw an arm and an ear being worked on.”.
