📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that this evening, Taylor Swift returns to the CMA Awards stage for her first performance in 13 years. But first, there’s the red carpet, and the singer just made a stylish entrance. Swift arrived at the Nashville ceremony in a bright yellow Oscar de la Renta gown featuring a dramatic high-low hem and ruffled details from the brand’s spring/summer 2027 collection. She completed the look with dangling earrings and gold high-heeled sandals. The singer walked the red carpet solo.

Swift has one nomination tonight: Single of the Year for her Toy Story 5 song “I Knew It, I Knew You.”.

She last attended the ceremony in 2016, wearing a cutout dress by Julien Macdonald:.

In a recent development, last month, Swift was back in Nashville for her induction into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame. During her acceptance speech, she reflected on the city’s significance to her career.

According to recent reports, “I started writing songs when I was a little kid and I knew from the time I wrote my first song that even if it couldn’t be my career, it would be my passion,” she said. “It would be what I centered my entire life around. Nashville is the place that I went to in my middle school daydreams. It was the place where all the wisdom that I wished to acquire existed in one place. All the lessons that I wished to learn existed in this magical place for me. And it ended up being all of that and more. It ended up being everything.

“Fame, that’s sort of a byproduct of my love for music,” she continued. “It’s kind of like songwriter plus Nashville equals fame. It’s why a lot of people know the words to my music, which is awesome. It’s also why there’s sort of like these daily random doses of chaos that just pop up in my life every once in a while, every couple of hours. That can be kind of weird, but I have tools in place in my mind that help me navigate that and get back to true north when life feels so bizarre. Those tools are my memories and the memories that I visit most frequently when I need to return back to some semblance of remembering what normalcy is and why I do this.

Recent findings suggest that “I think back to when I first got to Nashville and I started writing on Music Row. I was lucky enough to get a publishing deal with Sony Publishing when I was 14, and I just hit the ground running. I started writing every single day. I learned so much. I was like a sponge and I have really curly hair and it was yellow, so I kind of looked like a sponge too. But I learned by repetition. I think you truly learn a craft when you do it over and over and over, and that is what the magnificent songwriters that work in this amazing town do. There is no music community like this music community. I know. I checked all the communities. There’s no one doing it.”.
