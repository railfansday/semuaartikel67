Arsenal head coach Slegers leaves by mutual consent

Slegers initially joined Arsenal in 2023 as an assistant coach under Jonas Eidevall.

Arsenal have parted company with head coach Renee Slegers by mutual consent after a disappointing start to the season.

Slegers, 37, was initially appointed on an interim basis in October 2024 before being handed the role permanently three months later.

The Dutchwoman led Arsenal to a second Women's Champions League title in May 2025 and the inaugural Fifa Women's Champions Cup in February 2026.

But she leaves with the team sixth in the Women's Super League after 11 matches - 12 points behind leaders Chelsea.

Assistant coach Aaron D'Antino has also left the club, with assistant Melissa Clarke-Reed placed in interim charge as they begin the search for a permanent replacement.

D'Antino had been part of Arsenal's coaching staff since August 2018.

Arsenal director of women's football Clare Wheatley said the decision had been "extremely difficult".

"In leading us to glory in the Champions League and the inaugural Fifa Champions Cup, Renee has made a significant and lasting contribution to the history of Arsenal Football Club, with close support from Aaron," she said.

"After careful reflection, it is clear that results have been below the expected level, and change is necessary.".

Chief executive Richard Garlick paid tribute to both departing coaches, describing Slegers as "a wonderful person" who had played "an integral role in some of the most historic achievements in the club's history".

"Our ambition is to compete for and win major trophies consistently, and our recent results have led us to conclude that a change is necessary to give us the best opportunity to achieve those goals," he added.
