Why mega-constellations have a hidden space junk blind spot

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (77 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

We are consistently launching more and more stuff into space. As of now, space agencies track more than 30,000 objects larger than a softball, with an expected 1 million more bullet-sized pieces of debris orbiting Earth that are too small to spot from the ground. Government agencies and private commercial entities are planning to have up to 100,000 active spacecraft in orbit by the early 2030s, turning some orbital shells into crowded highways.
