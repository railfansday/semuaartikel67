According to recent reports, chainLink's rally results from September are essentially gone after the price violated the $9 price threshold. Both short-term and long-term charts suggest a bearish outlook for the asset; the most likely scenario starting from here would be an aggravation.

According to recent reports, despite peaking at $15.5 in October, ChainLink followed the rest of the market due to the lack of foundational strength and tumbled rapidly towards the current price level. The occurrence of a death cross is yet another factor that is most likely going to make LINK's positions more complicated.

The control of bulls over the momentum is undisputed at this point in time. 200 and 50-day moving averages had a cross downwards, suggesting the beginning of a prolonged downtrend. Shorter term moving averages are also nowhere near looking upside.

The cross of longer-term moving averages confirms a downtrend, even though it doesn't itself provide any signals regarding future price movements of the cryptocurrency.

The first support to rely on for LINK would be the $8-$8.2 price area as it coincides with the lowest level it has reached in November, so traders should've accumulated enough buying orders along the way to provide some kind of support for the asset.

ChainLink's rebound on the short-term chart is not relevant, unfortunately, mostly due to the poor volume profiles that don't provide enough support here. With RSI changing direction it's almost paramount that the bullish momentum is short-lived here.

The price is trading below the moving average cluster on the 4HR chart, making the $8.9-$9.2 area the first resistance to overcome. Without any strong volume spikes, almost any recovery is speculative, rather than trend-defining.

It has been revealed that LINK remains below its moving averages, and the latest rebound has done little to change the overall picture. If the $8-$8.2 support area fails to hold, the next level to watch would be around $7.5.

Industry observers note that the broader market is not providing much support either. Most altcoins continue to follow the general bearish trend, while the lack of buying interest makes any meaningful recovery increasingly difficult. Unless market conditions improve, LINK is likely to remain under pressure.
