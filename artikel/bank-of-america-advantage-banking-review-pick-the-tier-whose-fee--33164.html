Keep savings in a high-yield account elsewhere, since only the top tier earns interest, at most 0.02% a year.

Advantage Banking is Bank of America’s checking account for people who want branches and ATMs, in three tiers you can switch between without changing account numbers.

Recent findings suggest that advantage Banking is worth opening if you’ll meet a waiver every cycle, as anyone under 25 or with a paycheck of $250 or more by direct deposit will. Savers should keep cash in a high-yield savings account and reach BofA Rewards’ $30,000 only with Merrill investments, and those who can’t meet a waiver should open a free checking account instead.
