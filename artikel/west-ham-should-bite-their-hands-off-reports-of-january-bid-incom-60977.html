The news that Turkish Superlig side Trabzonspor are interested in one of West Ham’s squad would not normally be welcomed with unbridled enthusiasm, particularly in January when there’s such a short window to bring in replacement players.

However when the report confirms that the Turkish title contenders are focussed on Pablo Felipe there’ll probably be universal approbation amongst supporters who have had to sit and endure – though gritted teeth – the Portuguese forward’s West Ham career fail to ignite.

One solitary league and one cup goal is the extent of his output which, for the 20 million plus that was inexplicably paid, is not nearly good enough. Almost, but not even, Niclas Fullkrug – like performance.

One Claret and Hugh reader labelled him ‘The striker that cannot shoot, head, trap or pass the ball’ : He has always looked like one with the demeanour of a spectator plucked from obscurity as a competition winner to represent the team he came to watch, rather than a €20 million signing.

“The world loves a trier” – West Ham fans would prefer a goal scorer.

So the news that there’s a side actually lining up a bid for the forward this winter can only be seen as a good thing for the club and probably for Pablo himself.

Which will add fuel to the fire of speculation that the Irons are indeed planning on a fresh striker acquisition this winter: Sadly, there’s no way the money spent on Pablo or for that matter the £27 million blown on Taty Castellanos will be fully recouped.

The hope can only be that with Nils Koppen running transfers this winer there may be slightly more productive arrivals.
