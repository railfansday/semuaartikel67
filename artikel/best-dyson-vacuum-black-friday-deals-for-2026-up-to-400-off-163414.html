The V15 was our top Dyson vacuum pick for a long time because it's more affordable than the Gen5Detect and V16, but still packs two cleaner heads (the Digital Motorbar for carpet and Fluffy Optic for hard floors) and has 240 air-watts of suction (almost 100 more than the V10 Konical and other cheaper Dyson vacuums). The PencilVac is incredibly handy and compact, and great if you're trying to do quick pickups daily on hard floors. You won't want it if you have any kind of rug or carpet, since it's only designed for hard flooring, but I did find it incredibly easy to grab and use for constant litter trails in my house. It's incredibly lightweight and a great fit for anyone with mobility or strength concerns. If these features speak to you, this is a great time to buy, as it's already on sale.

This wet floor cleaner is often on sale for around $400, so it's not a huge surprise to see this deal ahead of Black Friday. The Dyson Clean+Wash is primarily a wet floor cleaner, but it can do dry cleans, too, and does not use a filter you inevitably need to keep clean. WIRED reviewer Kat Merck noticed no weird smells in three months of use. It's similar to the Dyson WashG1, with a self-propelled design that makes it easy to push around the room, and it does a good job scrubbing floors clean. Keep in mind that it's not great for dry debris, since it’s just rolling, not sucking.
