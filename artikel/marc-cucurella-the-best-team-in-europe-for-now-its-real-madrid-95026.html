Sources indicate that since its defeat against Atlético de Madrid on September 20, Real Madrid have definitely taken off and have just scored 8 consecutive victories in all competitions, with a prestigious success during the Clasico and an explosive comeback against AS Roma in the Champions League. From then on, Marc Cucurella, questioned by the Cadena Copeassured that Real Madrid were the best team in Europe at the moment.

“We are on a very good run and the team is very focused”he replied. “Better than PSG? »asked the journalist again. “Honestly, I don’t watch their matches much. It’s true that they are very good, but for now, it’s Real Madrid.”concluded the 28-year-old left-back. Remember that Real Madrid is second in La Liga, behind FC Barcelona, ​​and second in the Champions League league phase, behind PSG.
