Lionel Messi’s mother told Nico Paz that the Como talent was ‘the successor’ to her son in the Argentina squad, it has been revealed, as he is the favourite to inherit the Number 10 jersey.

Messi hung up his international boots in a final friendly match against Benin on October 7, which was a very emotional occasion for fans and family.

At that moment, the race began to find a suitable heir, and the player who would take on the iconic Number 10 shirt for the Albiceleste.

According to Nico Paz’s uncle, Fabian Paz, the decision has already been made by Messi’s mother, Celia Cuccittini.

“Nico is a very calm person, very humble, he doesn’t get it into his head that he is the successor of Messi,” his uncle told Radio La Red in Argentina.

“It was already a huge thing just to get to know him, be his teammate and be close to him. However, after the final game for Messi, Nico ran into Messi’s mother, who was very emotional.

“Nico stated that she went to shake his hand and said: ‘You are the successor to my son’ in the Argentina side.”.

The 22-year-old is already the favourite to inherit the Number 10 jersey, and has scored six goals and assisted another two in 16 senior caps for his country.

Having flourished at Como, it is widely reported that Real Madrid intend to activate their €80m buy-back option for the player next summer.

So far this season, Nico Paz contributed six goals and eight assists in 14 games for Como between Serie A and the Champions League.
