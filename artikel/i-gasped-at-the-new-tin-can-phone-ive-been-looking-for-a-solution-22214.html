📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Yes, I genuinely gasped when the Tin Can press release landed in my inbox this morning, as it is exactly the product I've been searching for.

According to recent reports, while I'm committed to a smartphone-free childhood for my children, I empathise with my children's need to communicate with their friends outside of school. They're also a bit young for the best dumbphones, but I still gatekeep my phone, only allowing occasional texting to other parent's phones in order to reach their pals. And I suddenly realised how unfair this is.

When I was nine, as my daughter is now, I was using our landline to call friends every day, honing valuable communication skills, and the lack of a home phone in my house means that my daughter is unable to do the same. As a newly introduced product, it's filling a very real gap in family life – and it's beautifully designed as well.

Recent findings suggest that the Tin Can is already available in the US and Canada, and it's now making it to the UK. It runs on Wi-Fi, so no costly extra landline charges on your broadband (the route I was all set to go down), it looks simple to use, is crucially screen-free, and comes in a variety of delicious colours. It also has helpful parent controls, including restrictions like 'quiet time', you can only call other Tin Can handsets (for free), as well as parent-approved numbers (as part of a monthly subscription), and emergency numbers.

Of course, for best results, your children's friends also need to have a Tin Can – so I'm off to convince the parents I know to put it on their Christmas lists.

Pre orders are open for the UK, and it will arrive with you from 30 November.
