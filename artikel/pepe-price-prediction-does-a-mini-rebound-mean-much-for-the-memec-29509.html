PEPE price prediction — Does a mini-rebound mean much for the memecoin?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, straying from the broader market trend, PEPE climbed by 2.41% in 24 hours. With no clear catalyst driving the surge, the rally appeared to be specific to the memecoin rather than part of a wider market recovery.

It has been revealed that in fact, Bitcoin was down 0.25% over the same period, while the total crypto market capitalization edged up by just 0.22%. With PEPE still down 27.28% over the last 7 days and its 24-hour trading volume falling by 6.60%, the rebound could prove short-lived.

Following a steep retracement, PEPE was trading at $0.00000384 at press time, positioned between the 78.6% and 100% Fibonacci retracement levels. The price had also just tested the key $0.00000383-support zone (yellow), making its defence crucial for any recovery attempt.

However, indicators have so far suggested that holding this level would be difficult. The OBV had started ticking lower too, hinting at renewed selling pressure. The RSI at 37.14 revealed that bearish momentum was still going strong too.

Despite attempting to break above the 23.6% Fibonacci level at $0.00000391 previously, the bulls failed to establish it as support. PEPE has since slipped back below this level.

Recent findings suggest that the OBV remained in a broader downtrend despite a brief recovery, while the MACD showed improving short-term momentum. However, both lines remained below zero. This suggested that the recovery was yet to overturn the broader bearish bias.

It has been revealed that PEPE needs to defend $0.000003816 and reclaim $0.0000039703 to strengthen its recovery prospects. A break above the latter could open the way towards $0.000004038. On the other hand, losing $0.00000383 would expose the token’s $0.0000037207 support.
