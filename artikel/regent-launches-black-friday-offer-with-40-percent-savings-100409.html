Regent Launches Black Friday Offer with 40 Percent Savings

Regent Seven Seas Cruises has offered travel advisors in the US and Canada a $350 gift card for every eligible new booking made as part of The Black Friday Collection.

The offer also provides their US and Canadian clients with savings of up to 40 percent on select voyages and suite categories, plus a $500 Shipboard Credit per suite.

“The Black Friday offer presents an extraordinary opportunity for travelers to discover the world while enjoying the exceptional value of an all-inclusive Regent voyage,” stated Wes D’Silva, president of Regent Seven Seas Cruises.

“Our travel advisors play an integral role in introducing guests to the unrivaled Regent experience, and we are delighted to offer them additional opportunities to grow their business this Black Friday,” said Shawn Tubman, chief sales officer, Regent Seven Seas Cruises.

“With exceptional savings and added benefits for their clients, alongside a $350 gift card incentive for eligible bookings, The Black Friday Collection provides our valued partners with even more reasons to engage their clients and secure their next luxury voyage,” added Tubman.

Regent said in a press release that the sale is valid for new bookings through December 1, 2025; the limited-time offer features more than 60 itineraries.

The collection allows travel advisors to connect their clients with Regent’s signature all-inclusive, ultra-luxury offerings while taking advantage of exclusive booking incentives, the manufacturer added.

To further support travel advisors in promoting the offer, Regent is providing a sales toolkit featuring customizable flyers, social media posts, email templates and imagery.

According to the press release, guests sailing with Regent Seven Seas Cruises can enjoy an all-inclusive ultra-luxury experience featuring spacious accommodations, world-class cuisine in specialty restaurants and al fresco dining venues, fine wines and spirits, entertainment, unlimited complimentary shore excursions, Starlink Wi-Fi, valet laundry, gratuities and more.

The Shipboard Credit can be used to enhance their voyage with spa treatments, boutique shopping, Regent Choice Shore Excursions, connoisseur wines and enriching culinary experiences.
