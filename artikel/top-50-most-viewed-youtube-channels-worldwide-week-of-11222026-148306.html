According to recent reports, during this new media gold rush sparked by movies like Iron Lung, Obsession, and Backrooms, everyone’s been talking about Hollywood tapping creators to write, direct, and star in projects. But this isn’t just happening in the West.

Sources indicate that indru, one of the youngest members of the vlog 4u family, was plucked up to feature during childhood scenes in the action/crime drama film Bachelor Party D’eux. A Nov. 19 vlog from the family has them trucking to the theater to watch the film’s opening day debut, and talking about how proud they are to have Indru make his first silver screen appearance. His total runtime in the film is around five minutes, right at D’eux‘s beginning, but he was on set for 10 days, Indru’s father explains.

“It’s a massive dream come true,” he adds. “It’s a day we’ve been wishing for, waiting for, and dreaming about for a long time.”.

Industry observers note that the family’s vlogs about Indru’s appearance have brought around ~140,000 views apiece–so not as much as their Tom and Jerry skits, which tend to earn tens of millions of views. This view discrepancy is pretty normal: When a channel has built an audience on one type of content with one subject, and then switches abruptly to posting something different, the new content can be hit-or-miss with viewers.

But 140,000 views is nothing to sneeze at, and Indru’s movie vlogs getting that many eyes means at least a portion of the vlog 4u audience is interested in following his onscreen adventure (or adventures?).

Recent findings suggest that what’s intriguing about this story is the vlog 4u channel’s growth in 2026, which very likely played a part in Indru being selected for his role. At the beginning of this year, the channel was bringing around 330 million views per month (again, nothing to sneeze at). It started seriously climbing in March 2026 (~700 million), and by August had crossed over one billion views a month. Now it’s getting 400 million weekly–a level of traffic that’s keeping it firmly in our top 50.

Industry observers note that will this lead to Indru–or other vlog 4u members–starring in more films? Maybe! We’ve seen some YouTubers produce or star in megahits, while others’ attempts to theater-ize have not gone so well. Whichever way it goes for the vlog 4u family, they clearly won’t be hurting for audience engagement.
