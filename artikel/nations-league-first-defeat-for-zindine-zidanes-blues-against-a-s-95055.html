It has been revealed that A return to basics for Zinédine Zidane’s 5th match at the head of the France team, in a San Siro stadium which saw his debut as a player for Juventus Turin thirty years ago. But it was above all the desire to continue the work started during an attractive first gathering that motivated Zizou for this Italy-France, 5th day of the Nations League. Faced with an opponent who had drawn the match at the Stade de France, the Blues started the match very well, despite constant rain and a pitch in a deplorable state. With Barcola positioned at number 9 and a Da Cunha-Rabiot-Cherki trio in the midfield, they stifled the pale Italians and created the first chances.

According to recent reports, donnarumma worked on a header from Rabiot, from close range (9th), before sinking by sending a restart, at the foot, directly into those of Michael Olise, who took great pleasure in eliminating Bastoni before adjusting Donnarumma (0-1, 11th). Thanks to another very interesting pressing, Zidane’s men did not leave much to their hosts, who still ideally exploited a rare opportunity. Cambiaghi overwhelmed Gusto and crossed hard at the near post for Pio Esposito, more lively than the French defense to deceive Maignan (1-1, 26th). Which had the gift of waking up the players of Mancini and San Siro.

It has been revealed that barcola found the post, in the 35th minute, with a beautiful curling shot, but the French team returned to the locker room with the score 1-1. Returning from the locker room, Malo Gusto won his first duel of the evening against Cambiaghi but came close to being penalized for a shirt pulling in his area. The Blues accelerated again and, thanks to their pressing, offered themselves a superb opportunity which Cherki wasted by refusing to shoot rapidly (51st). The French midfielder started in the 52nd minute, but the right post repelled his attempt. Rabiot once again came up against Donnarumma (58th), before Gusto did the irreparable by irregularly stopping the very restless Cambiaghi in the area (58th). Fortunately, Sebastiano Esposito sent a shot off target (60th).

Recent findings suggest that the match was going completely crazy, Roberto Mancini took out Cambiaghi but his replacement Dimarco was decisive with a cross for Frattesi, who beat Jacquet to shoot Maignan (2-1, 63rd). And the French team, far from being sovereign on the defensive level, which again made mistakes. Truffert took too substantial a risk on a restart, Vergara appeared, pushing Upamecano to make a mistake. This time, it was Pio Esposito who took charge of the penalty and he made no mistake (3-1, 75th). Zidane then changed his two full-backs, with the entries of Diouf and Kumbedi, and launched Kroupi and Mayulu in place of Barcola and Doué. Then Camavinga replaced Cherki but that changed nothing in the scenario of the meeting. Already frustrated by Italy at the Stade de France, Zinedine Zidane experienced his first defeat against Squadra Azzurra, which even went ahead of France in the group 1 standings.
