Recent findings suggest that arthur Hayes has just set bullish price targets for Ethereum, Zcash, NEAR and Venice Token, less than a month after saying he closed Maelstrom’s directional trading book. His $1,800 Zcash target was 97.3% above the token’s Nov. 18.

Sources indicate that in a Nov. 17 post, Hayes called the tokens his “Four Horsemen.” He put ETH at $2,800, ZEC at $1,800, NEAR at $6 and VVV at $40. He described the prospective trade size as “small to medium,” expressed little conviction and expected a rally before the end of what he called the AI bubble.

The post gave no deadline for the targets. Hayes gave no details about purchases by him or Maelstrom, or about the fund’s current exposure.

On Oct. 24, CryptoSlate reported that Hayes remarked he had closed Maelstrom’s directional book and taken profits on ETH, Ethena, Uniswap and other tokens. The exit covered bets on which way prices would move. ETH was among the tokens he sold and one of the four he expects to rally.

Industry observers note that hayes set out his condition for staying on the sidelines in a Nov. 5 essay. He wrote that Maelstrom would wait unless the fiat liquidity picture changed dramatically. His concern was that a decline in AI stocks could make lenders demand higher yields from those companies. That would make debt harder to issue and, in his view, leave less dollar liquidity available to markets.

NEAR and ZEC were names Hayes had publicly walked away from before. In a June 8 essay, he wrote that he had sold NEAR the previous week and gave a separate reason for exiting ZEC, citing a bug in Zcash’s Orchard privacy pool. The two tokens are now back in his public outlook, and ZEC has the widest gap to its target.

Hayes described Maelstrom’s exit and its conditions for staying out. His later post forecast a rally in four tokens.

In a recent development, hayes’s public targets followed his account of Maelstrom’s exit. Any new fund exposure remains undisclosed.
