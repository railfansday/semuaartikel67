An alleged new video demonstrates Galaxy S27 dummy units that look like the latest leaks, and Samsung fans aren’t having it.

At this point, it’s anyone’s best guess as to what the Galaxy S27 series looks like, but it’s seeming surer and surer that Samsung will be giving the series a massive redesign this coming year. Chris Franklin, a writer at SammyGuru, posted an unboxing video of the Galaxy S27 on X, citing that there’s a possibility the unboxing is AI-created — there are some eyebrow-raising reflections here, but it’s hard to tell for certain. The design looks similar to the “confirmed” renders that leaked a few days ago, although there are some subtle differences.

I don't think much about the design of phones, they're essentially all the same, especially when considering you're looking at the front 90% of the time, but I've always found the Galaxy's vertical camera layout to be the least attractive of any of the major phones. I think this looks much better.

Unsurprisingly, another leak from earlier in the month has a similar look but, again, with subtle differences. So far, all the major leaks in the past few months are pointing to Samsung outfitting the Galaxy S27 series with a raised camera island that looks extremely similar to Apple’s design on the iPhone 17 Pro and 18 Pro series.

According to recent reports, samsung isn’t alone here. The Xiaomi 18 series and Honor Magic 9 Pro series both have eerily similar designs, and many other 2026 and 2027 phones have followed suit. The most obvious problem with Samsung’s potential design (and, by extension, Apple’s) is the raised camera lenses, which protrude above the raised camera island. Xiaomi’s island is flat, following Google’s idea that a camera bar should protect the lenses and also keep the phone from wobbling or feeling off-balance.

Drama has already taken off on X (formerly Twitter), where Noah Cat reposted the video, and it seems Samsung fans aren’t happy. Samsung has needed to change up the vertical camera bar for years, as it has regularly gotten in the way of Qi2 and MagSafe magnetic accessories. This upcoming design might help that, but it seems fans wanted Samsung to return to the Galaxy S10 horizontal camera bar days, not this new iPhone-esque raised island.
