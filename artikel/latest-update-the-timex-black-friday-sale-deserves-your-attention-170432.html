The Timex Black Friday sale is sort of redundant if you think about it. Like, the brand that's been hawking classic watches at affordable prices for generations is making stated affordably-priced classic watches even more affordable? Maybe that's just the writer in me talking. But I'm not one to complain. If anything, maybe the Timex Black Friday sale isn't redundant so much as it is an emphasis on the fact that its range of watches, which includes everything from automatic divers to art-deco-inspired tickers to marathon-crushing pacers, is just so much of a bargain, they had to say it twice.

Recent findings suggest that because while watch snobs might scoff at any timepiece less than four figures, practically every watch guru made their way through at least a couple of Timex watches in their ascendance up the horological ladder. Plus, the real watch lovers know it ain't about the price of the piece on your wrist. That's why Timex has kept ticking all these years—its watches are as easy on the eyes as they are on the wallet, and if 30% off for Black Friday keeps churning out watch appreciators such as this writer, well that's all good by me.

What are the best watches to buy from Timex?

In a recent development, as versatile as a Hanes white tee, as rugged as a pair of Levi’s jeans, and as classically stylish as a Bass Weejuns penny loafer, Timex watches have been an intrinsic part of the American style playbook for nearly a century. While you may associate Timex with best-selling quartz models like the Weekender and Expedition Scout, the brand has lately broadened its scope, introducing upcoming lines of mechanical watches and a ton of new and rebooted vintage designs.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 27, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.
