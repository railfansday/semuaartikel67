Justin Devenny’s superb strike saw Northern Ireland football earn promotion to the top flight of the Nations League with a hard-fought 1-1 draw away to closest challengers Hungary football in Budapest.

Barnabas Varga ended Northern Ireland’s proud record of not having conceded in this campaign when he fired the hosts in front after 35 minutes – a goal that also sent Hungary to the top of the group.

But moments after Isaac Price had hit the post for Michael O’Neill’s young side, Devenny smashed the ball into the top left-hand corner in the 65th minute to restore Northern Ireland’s one-point advantage as group leaders.

The visitors were on the back foot for much of the first half, with Hungary captain Dominik Szoboszlai hitting the post, but Michael O’Neill, serving a touchline ban in the stands as former captain Steven Davis led things from the touchline, orchestrated key substitutions.

Northern Ireland started with Patrick Kelly in place of the suspended Jamie Donley up front but brought on Josh Magennis at half-time before Kieran Morrison and Brodie Spencer soon followed to help the visitors become a much greater threat going forward.

Magennis’ physical presence and the extra pace paid dividends as, just two years after earning promotion from League C, Northern Ireland completed an unbeaten campaign in Group B2 to secure their place at the top table of European football.
