In a recent development, it might only be November, but the holidays are just around the corner, and with Hanukkah starting early this year on December 4, I’m already collecting gifts for everyone I love. Getting ahead of the curve has its perks: fewer frantic shipping fees, for one, and enough time to find something that feels genuinely special.

The easiest way to make a gift special? Get it personalized. Because who wants to tear through wrapping paper only to find the same thing everyone else is getting this season? Go the custom route, and even a classic gift feels like it was made just for them (because, well, it was).

Think engraved ID bracelets and signet rings for the guys, birthstone bracelets and sparkly initial pendants for the women in your life, and personalized sports merch for the fan whose team is a whole personality trait. Add sentimental photo albums full of family memories and a few delightfully quirky surprises they’ll never see coming, and you’ve got a list worth getting a head start on. Here are some of our favorite personalized gift ideas for the holidays this year.

According to recent reports, know someone who takes great pleasure in writing hand-written letters? Make their next correspondence even more exciting with personalized writing paper from Smythson. Customize everything from the size and color of the paper, the text and motif at the top of the paper, and the tissue lining and border on the envelopes.

Engraved jewelry is one of the classiest personalized gifts you can get for someone, especially when it’s simple and refined like this ID chain bracelet from Miansai. I got this bracelet for my boyfriend with his name engraved on it for the holidays last year, and he wears it pretty much every day.

Recent findings suggest that A personalized jersey is a no-brainer for anyone with a favorite sports team. The Fanatics custom shop has merch for NFL, MLB, NBA, WNBA, NHL, MLS, and even college teams, so it really has something for every kind of sports fan. And it’s not just jerseys—there’s also custom drinkware, bags, accessories, and more.

According to recent reports, even if your giftee isn’t super into astrology, they’ll appreciate a candle that’s just for them (or, well, anyone that shares their birthday). If they are into astrology, they’ll love all the fun facts about their birthday on the candle’s label, including their Zodiac sign, element, constellation, tarot card, and more. And all you have to know is their birthday, so it’s a pretty easy gift.

Speaking of birthdays, birthstone jewelry is another classic gift that’s personalized by design. And all you have to know is their birth month to get it right. Brilliant Earth has a great selection of birthstone jewelry, from pendant necklaces and colorful earrings to gemstone tennis bracelets and rings. You can even add multiple birthstones to this eternity lab diamond ring, so you can personalize it with the birthstones of everyone in your family.

For the one who spends their weekends on the golf course: They’ll truly impress their golf buddies with a box of personalized golf balls. And it comes with tees, a divot tool, and poker-chip ball markers so they have everything they need.

If you have the time and want to do something really special, order one of these luxury lighters from S.T. Dupont and get it engraved in person at a local engraving shop or jewelry repair store. The extra effort will show, and your giftee will have something to carry with them wherever they go for years to come.

You’ll have fun playing around with all the features in Ralph Lauren’s online custom hat maker—we certainly have. Choose the color and add a name, logo, or monogram, and the result will be a one-of-a-kind baseball cap your giftee will wear all the time.

Sources indicate that don’t just print out a photo and call it a day; get it printed on glass for something truly unique. Upload your photo to Fracture and personalize it even more with colorful borders, templates, frames, and stands.

Recent findings suggest that ever gone into a comic book store and seen the walls of Funko POP action figures from every franchise you can imagine? Having cute action figures of all your favorite Star Wars characters is great, but imagine how cool it would be to give someone an action figure of themself. With Funko POP, you can do just that—and you’ll have fun building it. You can even get a pair of Funko POP figures, so you can include yourself in it, too.

In a recent development, if you’re looking for a romantic personalized gift for your partner, surprise them with a watercolor painting of the place where you had your first date or the spot where you got engaged. Whether it was a local coffee shop, beloved movie theater, or beautiful outdoor space, it’ll look good painted above your names.

In a world where everything is online and in the cloud, a physical photo album is all the more sentimental and special. Artifact Uprising makes putting together a photo album easy, and the result is high quality and beautiful. It’s something they’ll want to display on their coffee table for everyone to see.

Recent findings suggest that gifts based on birthdays is a common theme in this gift guide, but what can we say? It’s an easy way to personalize stuff. If they’re a history buff (or just love reading the news), they’ll enjoy flipping through this customized book of newspaper headlines from their birthday through the years.

You know that person who is absolutely obsessed with their dog or cat? (Okay, it’s me.) They’ll obsess over any gift that has to do with their beloved pet, like this custom sweatshirt embroidered with the face of their dog or cat. Just upload a photo of their pet (you can probably find one on their Facebook or Instagram) and choose a sweater color and size.

Recent findings suggest that A customized L.L. Bean tote bag is something they’ll use all the time. It’s one of our favorite gifts of all time, and it’s an absolute classic.
