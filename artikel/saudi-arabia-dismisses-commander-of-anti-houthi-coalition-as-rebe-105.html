Saudi Arabia dismisses commander of anti-Houthi coalition as rebels advance

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (53 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Saudi Arabia has removed the commander of the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen, state media remarked Monday, as the Iran-backed rebels advanced on the temporary capital of Aden. The shake up comes after Houthi forces took territory on Yemen's southern coast and Saudi-backed forces fell back to defend the capital.
