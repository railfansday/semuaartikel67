Arsenal midfielder Bruno Guimaraes claimed yet another assist for Vinicius Junior, with the Real Madrid forward rediscovering his goalscoring touch.

Vinicius Jr scored a hat-trick as Brazil thumped Singapore 7-0 in a friendly on Tuesday morning, following up on his brace in the 3-2 defeat to Japan on Saturday.

An impressive tally of five goals in two games during a successful international break may prove a turning point in the season for the 26-year-old after admitting his form for Real Madrid had been impacted by summer links to Arsenal.

“I don’t think I prepared as well as I could have after the World Cup, because the impact of that tournament is so significant for all Brazilians,” Vinicius Jr stated.

It has been revealed that “Being knocked out so early was something we hadn’t expected. So, I took advantage of my holidays to enjoy myself, rest, relax, and spend more time with my family. I ended up training less than in previous years.

“That may have affected my start to the season. On top of that, regarding my contract renewal, with so many clubs showing interest last summer, I felt truly overwhelmed, as with the situation at Real Madrid. But it’s a very long season.”.

Interestingly, perhaps, of Vinicius Jr’s five goals across the two games, three of them were created by Bruno Guimaraes.

The Arsenal midfielder assisted both goals against Japan and then repeated the trick inside the opening 10 minutes against Singapore with a fine pass - though Vinicius’ first touch did make the opportunity even easier.

The connection between Guimaraes and Vinicius Jr in recent days has prompted chatter on social media among some Arsenal fans about revisiting a deal for the latter at some point in 2027.

That said, reports claim the Gunners still view the 26-year-old as their leading target to strengthen the left wing, with senior figures believing a deal could be possible as early as next summer.

Arsenal are stated to remain an attractive destination after the forward seriously considered their offer last summer. However, any move is unlikely before next summer, with the club prepared to be patient in their pursuit.
