According to recent reports, in unexpected news, Kensington Palace has just declared that Kate Middleton will accompany Prince William on his upcoming three-day official visit to Mumbai, India. The trip, which commences on Monday, will mark their first joint tour outside the United Kingdom in the past four years and represents a newly introduced milestone in Kate Middleton’s gradual return to public duties since she was diagnosed with cancer in early 2024 and began scaling back her official engagements.

Last May, the princess made a brief solo trip to Italy to learn about early childhood development, but this trip to India (a nine-hour flight from London) represents her first long-distance journey and her highest-profile diplomatic engagement since the Caribbean tour she undertook in 2022 and the Earthshot Awards gala she attended in Boston that same year.

"So proud, I'm very, very proud. She's been amazing. She's been through so much in the last couple of years, particularly," William shared on UK radio show Heart Breakfast in May, pleased to see that Kate was “gradually resuming” her travels. Still, the heir to the throne wanted to be cautious and warned that these visits are very taxing. "Away trips like that take a lot out of you," he explained. "So we have to balance that, make sure she's, she's OK and rested, but she's in good form.".

The royal couple at the 2022 Earthshot Prize ceremony in Boston, their most recent joint trip abroad to date.

The reason for the visit is the sixth edition of the Earthshot Prize, an initiative spearheaded by the heir to the British throne that each year awards one million pounds (about $1,323,249) to five innovative environmental projects and which, after making stops in other cities abroad such as Singapore, Cape Town, and Rio de Janeiro, is now taking place in the country that has produced the most finalists and winners in the competition’s history.

It has been revealed that standout finalists this year include Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, for its fleet of electric motorcycle taxis designed to reduce urban pollution, and Mahila Housing Trust, which installs reflective roofs capable of lowering the temperature inside homes by up to five degrees during extreme heat. The awards ceremony will be held this Tuesday evening at the Jio World Centre in Mumbai and will feature a performance by the band Take That and be attended by celebrities such as actor Rami Malek.

William and Kate during their 2016 visit to the Taj Mahal.

It has been revealed that hours before the main event, the Prince and Princess of Wales will also preside over the first edition of the Earthshot Children’s Awards, a competition in which 15,000 schools from 24 Indian states have participated with environmental proposals designed by young people aged 9 to 17. The itinerary also includes a solo appearance by Kate Middleton, meetings with the Bollywood film industry, cricket-related celebrations, and a possible meeting with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

In addition to being a positive sign regarding the princess’s health, this return to India holds strong symbolic significance for the couple, who last visited the country in April 2016. On that occasion, the then-Duke and Duchess of Cambridge visited the Taj Mahal and posed on the same white marble bench where Princess Diana was photographed alone in 1992—an image that has gone down in history as a reflection of the breakdown of her marriage to the now-King Charles III.
