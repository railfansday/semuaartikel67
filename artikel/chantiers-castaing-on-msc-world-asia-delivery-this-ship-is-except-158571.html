Chantiers’ Castaing on MSC World Asia Delivery: ‘This Ship Is Exceptional’

Speaking during the delivery of the MSC World Asia in Saint-Nazaire on Wednesday, Chantiers de l’Atlantique General Manager Laurent Castaing highlighted the vessel’s energy efficiency and celebrated the shipyard’s 25-year partnership with MSC Cruises.

The handover marks the 20th cruise ship built by the French yard for the company, adding a new vessel to the World Class series, which began in 2022 with the MSC World Europa.

It has been revealed that “This ship is exceptional,” Castaing said. “Following the wishes of her shipowner and thanks to our great flexibility, major new features for passengers have been added to this excellent World Class platform.”.

He also pointed to the sustainability features of the vessel, noting that its greenhouse gas emissions are 50 percent lower than the IMO reference baseline.

“What we are very proud of is the environmental performance of this ship. From a distance, she looks like others, but she is truly exceptional, and we have extensively modified the ship’s machinery,” Castaing noted.

“She is the best in energy efficiency across the entire cruise ship fleet, and by far. Few modes of transport can boast such a 50 percent reduction compared to the benchmarks. We are ahead of our competitors, and we will continue moving toward zero emissions.”.

Castaing mentioned the ship was the result of human effort, adding that thousands of people had worked on its construction since 2024.

He thanked MSC Cruises for the partnership with Chantiers de l’Atlantique, noting that the firm has now built 20 ships at the French yard over the past 25 years.

“It is exceptional. Our relationship has always been one of trust. We have not weathered any storms in our relations. Sometimes clouds have passed by, but honestly, I have never gone to Geneva with a knot in my stomach,” Castaing noted.

“Even if there were occasionally a few last-minute squabbles, our deliveries have always gone smoothly and have always been celebrated with dignity here in Saint-Nazaire, just like today, and we thank you for that,” he stated, addressing MSC Cruises Executive Chairman Pierfrancesco Vago.

Castaing remarked that MSC Cruises has brought more than 100 million hours of work to Chantiers de l’Atlantique over 25 years.

According to recent reports, “Building ships is not only about relationships between people, even though that matters enormously. There are also tens of billions of euros in orders, tens of billions of euros necessary for our trade balance,” he added.
