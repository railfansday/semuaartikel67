📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that love and Deepspace has kicked off its latest Memory Pair event today: Where Silverwings Rest, the new chapter for dragon boy - and now vampire boy - Sylus. There are new Memories to explore, a new Myth - and, of course, a new companion form for him.

This latest limited-time event is live now until Tuesday, December 16, making for a two-week runtime.

Industry observers note that the highlight in Where Silverwings Rest, of course, are the upcoming 5-Star Memories. There’s a new, fittingly themed tale about restlessness. Players are getting Sylus: Crimson Rapture, and Sylus: Crimson Departure, two 5-Star Solar-Slot Memory Pairs.

Recent findings suggest that you’ll find them in the Wish Pool throughout the event’s duration. You can do a maximum of 150 pulls before you’re guaranteed to unlock the two of them, and earning the Sylus: Silverwing Fiend Companion. That Companion Form is also latest for Sylus.

Sources indicate that if you’re a fan of reading, you’ll find the fresh Crimson Spirit Myth, which is very much a vampire tale about the tormenting reality of being an immortal being. Beyond all the lore and visuals, there’s also the Crimson Feast event that runs alongside it.
