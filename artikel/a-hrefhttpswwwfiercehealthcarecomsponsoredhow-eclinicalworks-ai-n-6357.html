According to recent reports, many existing inpatient systems require extensive implementation, requiring resources, large IT teams, and substantial ongoing maintenance costs, yet they often fall short in delivering the efficiency gains healthcare organizations need today. These healthcare systems were designed before modern artificial intelligence existed, so AI arrives as a separate vendor, a separate login, and a separate integration project. Unlike most hospital EHRs, the eClinicalWorks® AI-native inpatient hospital EHR was designed with a different approach: leveraging AI and automation to reduce administrative burden, streamline operational workflows, and improve efficiency without increasing technology complexity or cost.Reimagining the Hospital TechnologyThe eClinicalWorks AI-native inpatient hospital EHR supports hospital staff across every department, helping to streamline documentation, retrieve clinical records at the point of care, enhance billing, and automate routine tasks, all while keeping healthcare professionals in control of final decisions. It leverages the new eClinicalWorks Smart Canvas, a redesigned workspace where consumers can access relevant information, tasks, and actions through a more intuitive interface.Alabama-based Lakeland Community Hospital selected the eClinicalWorks inpatient hospital EHR. Wes Sigler, MPA, CFO/COO at the hospital, expressed that "It's going to not only improve our experience on the clinical side but also make our experience on the hospital side even more amazing." The new workspace helps clinicians and staff complete routine tasks and review relevant information seamlessly. Fully integrated AI capabilities across the unified platform further support hospital operations:.

Recent findings suggest that AI Agents as Force MultipliersThe next phase of healthcare focuses on creating operationally intelligent healthcare environments. The eClinicalWorks AI Workbench enables organizations to build, customize, and deploy AI agents that automate routine tasks, improve efficiency, and reduce administrative burdens for hospital staff and providers.Financial performance remains one of the largest challenges facing healthcare organizations. eClinicalWorks is extending AI-driven capabilities into areas such as autonomous revenue cycle management (RCM) and autonomous value-based care (VBC), helping organizations streamline financial, administrative, and care management processes while reducing manual effort.Extending Operational Intelligence to PharmacyModern pharmacy technology, such as the eClinicalWorks Pharmacy Management System, connects pharmacists, providers, and patients through a shared record, helping to eliminate communication silos and reduce duplicate work. This can help bring operational workflows into a single environment. Peyton Frye, COO at East Georgia Healthcare Center, collaborated closely with eClinicalWorks to develop the newly introduced pharmacy system. He shared how information such as patient contact details is now synchronized between the medical and pharmacy sides, eliminating the need to patch multiple systems. For hospitals, this creates a more connected experience across inpatient, ambulatory, and pharmacy workflows, helping teams access the information they need without having to navigate multiple systems or fragmented processes. When inpatient, ambulatory, and pharmacy workflows operate within a connected platform, hospitals can reduce duplication, eliminate unnecessary handoffs, and improve visibility across departments.Building the Future of Connected CareIn an era defined by mounting workforce challenges and financial pressures, the goal is not simply to add more technology. It is to build a smarter hospital environment where information, workflows, and AI work together to help organizations deliver high-quality care more efficiently.The next frontier is creating hospitals that can leverage AI to streamline operations, coordinate care, and support clinicians in real time.eClinicalWorks is advancing this vision with an AI-native hospital platform that connects inpatient, ambulatory, pharmacy, and revenue cycle workflows through a unified experience. By combining embedded AI, customizable agents, workflow automation, and a shared patient record, hospitals can reduce administrative burden while creating a more connected environment for both care teams and patients.
