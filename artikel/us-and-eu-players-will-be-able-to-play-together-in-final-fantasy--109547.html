US and EU players will be able to play together in Final Fantasy 14 soon, with Square Enix considering 'the possibility of permanently enabling travel' in the future

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

"To help alleviate server congestion following the release of Patch 8.0, we will be enabling cross-region Data Center Travel between the North American and European data centers during scheduled maintenance on Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

"… We will continue to take community feedback into account as we carefully evaluate the possibility of permanently enabling travel between the North American and European data centers, as well as opening travel between other physical data centers," via the Lodestone.

This is a significant deal and I'm very excited.

Industry observers note that I play Final Fantasy 14 on the US servers—which is a problem, because I live in the UK. I do so because, well, some of my closest friends are on those servers, too; but also because there's a slightly livelier roleplay community bustling on the US side of things, and I'm a roleplayer who likes squeezing my warrior of light like he's a stress toy.

As a result, I've often stared longingly at the people who play in the UK due to the crime of my time zone: Unable to play with my UK friends, unable to join a static raiding group that starts at a reasonable hour, and unable to play the game without terrible animation lock on my abilities. Unless I, say, broke ToS and used an addon that brought me on-par with people who have 30 ping, hypothetically. Which I don't do.

Square Enix has thrown open these doors, however, to mainly deal with congestion troubles:.

According to recent reports, "As peak play times differ between North America and Europe, another physical data center may be less crowded and serve as a viable option when login queues on your usual data center are long. While players connecting from geographically distant locations may experience increased latency, we encourage you to try playing on a different physical data center before the expansion releases.".

However, they know that people like me have expressed their yearning to hop between the good ol' USA and Europe: "However, we have received ongoing feedback from players wishing to travel between the North American and European data centers, and many players in both regions are able to communicate in English even when it is not their first language.".

According to recent reports, all in all, this'll be a huge quality of life improvement. There might be some difficulties, though: raiding culture can vary drastically even between logical datacenters like Aether and Crystal, let alone between continents. However, I'd be shocked if the differences between US and EU raiding cultures were insurmountable; JP and US? Might be a bigger deal.
