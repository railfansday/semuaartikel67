England U21s boss Lee Carsley has delivered his verdict on the young stars who are continuing to make waves in the Premier League.

The 52-year-old is actively preparing to defend England's crown at next summer's European Championship, which he is looking to win for the third time in his managerial career at youth level.

The Three Lions have the chance to pick up their third-consecutive victory, having also secured silverware in 2023 and 2025, and could become the first side to do so since Italy between 1992 and 1996.

According to recent reports, simply put, the talent in Carsley's hands is historic and he is well aware of that. The coach took time to praise several England stars ahead of the official draw, which saw the Three Lions placed in Group A with Poland, France, and co-hosts Serbia.

Carsley had the chance to speak highly of Archie Gray, who has a big decision to make about the future of his international career, and Arsenal's Max Dowman.

According to recent reports, the latter has become the centre of conversation in English football since he became the youngest goalscorer in Premier League history against Everton last season. Many believe he will be the future of Thomas Tuchel's side.

Carsley decided to join in with the praise on Thursday evening, telling GIVEMESPORT: "I think he showed last camp that he was more than capable of being there. He’s an outstanding talent on and off the pitch.

Sources indicate that "Great player to work with. Lovely kid as well also, which is really important. Settled into the group nicely. Max is a player that we rate highly.".

Having already secured a record in the Premier League, Dowman also still has the chance to become a history-maker at international level.

If the 16-year-old earns his first senior England cap in the next 12 months, then he will become the youngest player in the country's history to play for the Three Lions - beating Theo Walcott, who was 17 years and 75 days old when he debuted in 2006.

According to recent reports, if this current England generation is golden, then the next looks like it could be pure platinum. England's next best stars are promptly making a name for themselves around European and English football.

Luca Williams-Barnett is now well on his way to stardom at Tottenham. Rio Ngumoha is already arguably Liverpool's most dangerous wide man and is already a stat-leader in the Champions League - completing more take-ons than anyone else.

Recent findings suggest that then there's Dowman, who's just waiting to become Arsenal's difference-maker when the time is right. All three stars have received high praise, with former Premier League forward Jay Bothroyd going as far as dubbing Dowman "the future of England".

While there will be plenty of focus on the U21s Euros next summer, there are already several stars who are ready to make their mark for the senior side. And when the 2028 senior European Championship arrives, they may do exactly that.
