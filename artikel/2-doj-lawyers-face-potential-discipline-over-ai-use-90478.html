The U.S. Department of Justice’s Office of Professional Responsibility—its internal ethics watchdog—on Tuesday revealed that it has referred two of its attorneys for potential bar discipline over their use of artificial intelligence in doing their jobs.

The lawyers’ professional conduct complications included disclosing confidential documents to a public AI platform and ‌making misleading statements to a judge when discussing how AI-generated errors appeared in a court filing, according to a story by Reuters.

Neither lawyer is named in the report.

The lawyers have been referred to the DOJ’s Professional Misconduct Review Unit, which can then make recommendations to state bar authorities, according to a story by Bloomberg Law.
