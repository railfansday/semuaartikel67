Napoli have a growing injury crisis, but start Kevin De Bruyne, Scott McTominay and Rasmus Hojlund away to Gianluigi Donnarumma and Enzo Maresca’s Manchester City.

It kicks off at the Etihad in Manchester at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

Sources indicate that you can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Como vs. AEK Athens on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Partenopei are struggling badly this season, and confidence was only slightly boosted by a 1-0 win over Torino at the weekend, which left them eighth in the Serie A table.

They risk early European elimination for the second year running, having managed one win and three defeats so far.

Max Allegri’s injury crisis keeps getting worse, with Antonio Vergara, Benoit Badiashile, Alex Meret, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Alisson Santos, Alessandro Buongiorno and suspended Rafa Marin unavailable.

It means the 3-5-1-1 formation is used again, but Noa Lang is used in support of lone striker Hojlund, dropping both De Bruyne and McTominay into midfield, leaving Andre Frank Zambo Anguissa on the bench.

Matias Olivera has to turn into a makeshift central defender, with Matteo Politano and Leonardo Spinazzola out wide.

Manchester City are seventh in the Champions League table after winning three of the first four matches, losing 4-2 at home to trophy holders Paris Saint-Germain.

However, their recent Premier League form has been dismal, scraping one point from four rounds, and coach Maresca must keep an eye on the upcoming clash with Arsenal.

Matheus Nunes and Marc Guehi have shaken off knocks to be in the squad, meaning the only absentee is Antoine Semenyo.

Erling Haaland remains deadly in attack, with support from Juventus transfer target Rayan Cherki, but Ilman Ndiaye and Jeremy Doku are rested ahead of Arsenal, as is captain Ruben Dias.

Italy international Donnarumma has been facing criticism for his performances in goal.

Manchester City beat Napoli here 2-0 last season when Di Lorenzo was sent off after just 21 minutes, prompting Antonio Conte to substitute De Bruyne against his former club.

The Belgian veteran will be hoping to make more of an impact here this time around.

Manchester City: Donnarumma; Matheus Nunes, Guehi, Gvardiol, O’Reilly; Bouaddi, Anderson; Allan, Enzo Fernandez, Cherki; Haaland.

Man City bench: Rulli, Ruben Dias, Ndiaye, Kovacic, Doku, Ait-Nouri, Vitor Reis, Khusanov, Foden, McAidoo, Samba, Lewis.

Napoli: Milinkovic Savic; Beukema, Rrahmani, Olivera; Politano, De Bruyne, Gilmour, McTominay, Spinazzola; Lang, Hojlund.

Napoli bench: Contini, Spinelli, Neres, Lucca, Giovane, Chiummariello, Anguissa.
