West Ham United and Wales winger Ffion Morgan has had surgery on an anterior cruciate ligament injury and is now expected to sit out the rest of the 2026/27 campaign, a heavy blow for club and country with Wales less than a week away from a World Cup play-off final.

In a short injury patch published by West Ham, the club confirmed that the operation has already taken place and that the 26-year-old picked up the damage in the Women’s Super League home win over Charlton Athletic earlier this month. Morgan was helped off during that game, which Viviane Asseyi settled with two goals at the London Stadium.

The Hammers said Morgan will now start her rehabilitation under the supervision of their medical staff, adding: “Everyone at West Ham United wishes Ffion a full and timely recovery.”.

Sources indicate that the timing makes the news even harder to take. As BBC Sport Wales reports, Morgan had just come back into the West Ham side after another lengthy absence, and this is not the first time she has faced an ACL rehabilitation. She also suffered an ACL injury as a teenager while on duty with the Wales Under-19s.

Morgan moved to east London on a two-year contract in the summer of 2025 after her deal at Bristol City ran out. Before that she turned out for Crystal Palace, Coventry United and Cardiff City, and she is now a regular in Rhian Wilkinson’s Wales squads with 56 senior caps.

Industry observers note that morgan was already missing from the 26-player group named for the two-legged World Cup play-off final against Norway. The Football Association of Wales squad announcement brought Ceri Holland, Hayley Ladd, Ella Powell, Rhiannon Roberts and goalkeeper Olivia Clark back from injury and recalled Gwen Zimmerman, but there was no place for the West Ham winger.

In a recent development, norway visit Cardiff City Stadium for the first leg on Tuesday 1 December (7:15pm), before the return leg at the Ullevaal Stadion in Oslo on Saturday 5 December. Both games are live on BBC Wales.

It has been revealed that victory over two legs would not necessarily be the end of the journey. Only the seven best-ranked winners from this round go straight through to the 2027 finals in Brazil, while the eighth heads into the inter-confederation play-offs in February. Even if Wales do make it, Morgan’s recovery timeline leaves her a major doubt for next summer’s tournament.

Wilkinson will now have to carry on without one of her most experienced wide players, while West Ham plan for the rest of the season without a player who had only just fought her way back to fitness.
