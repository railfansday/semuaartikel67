Exoprimal, Capcom's Dinosaur Hero Shooter, Will Go Offline And Become Unplayable Next Year

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Capcom has been on a truly generational run in recent years, but even their legendary lineup of games was bound to have a few stumbling points. Exoprimal is a prime example – the dinosaur-themed hero shooter was not a terrible game by any means, but it requires online play and hasn't managed to maintain much of a player base. Today, Capcom unveiled prepares to sunset the game, rendering it unplayable by September of next year.

"Thank you so much for your continued use of Exoprimal," an announcement on the Exoprimal site reads. "We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027." The announcement goes on to specify that the game will "no longer be playable" after that date – Exoprimal is an online-only game which requires an internet connection, so once the servers are offline, that's it.

It has been revealed that between now and September 29, Capcom will bring Exoprimal down in stages. As of yesterday, players can no longer purchase a Survival Pass Premium Tier for seasons 1-4. On March 30, Exoprimal and all its DLC will be de-listed from stores, but software owners can still play it and/or re-download it. Finally, the game will fully shut down by September 29.

It has been revealed that any online-only game has a finite lifespan, but it's still sad to see them go. Many gamers are frustrated in recent years because it feels like they don't really own anything, whether it's a subscription-based product or a digital download that can be discontinued at a moment's notice. It's the primary cause of outrage behind PlayStation's decision to stop producing physical discs in 2028.

As for Exoprimal, our reviewer Marcus Stewart largely enjoyed his time with it, writing, "Exoprimal’s creative subversion of expectations impressed me in more ways than one, and its approach to telling a robust narrative within a multiplayer framework is an example I hope other titles study. I just hope it’s enough to keep the game from going extinct.".
