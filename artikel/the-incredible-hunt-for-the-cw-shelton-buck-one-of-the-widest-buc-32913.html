Kentucky controls its whitetail population by allowing hunting only in counties where the herds have built up to the carrying capacity of available deer range. Live-trapping crews thinned out the overpopulated sections and stocked the deer in other areas that had heavy browse conditions. As these herds build to huntable populations, additional counties are opened to hunting.
