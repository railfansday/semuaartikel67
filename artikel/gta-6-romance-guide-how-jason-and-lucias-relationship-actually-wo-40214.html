Industry observers note that in GTA 6 you've got a partner in crime, and it's quite a bit different than it was when you were pulling heists with Michael, Franklin, and Trevor in the last game. Jason and Lucia aren't just criminal associates, they're a couple, and their romantic relationship is a big part of the story.

It has been revealed that jason and Lucia can grow closer or more distant, depending on your actions, behavior, and choices. That goes for everything from story missions to random criminal acts to everyday interactions and activities. Here's how Jason and Lucia's romance works in GTA 6.

Industry observers note that according to Rockstar, if you want a strong relationship in GTA 6, you have to play both sides of that relationship. That means not just playing as Jason and doing nice things for Lucia, but also playing as Lucia and treating Jason well.

For my playthrough, where I reached the end of the game with a 4/4 "Hot Together" status, my stats show that I played nearly the exact same amount of time as Jason as I did Lucia.

Don't worry, this couple isn't joined at the hip. Both Lucia and Jason have missions, storylines, and activities they can (and are sometimes required to) complete alone. You can play plenty of GTA 6 as just a single character running around without the other, just remember to occasionally spend some time with your partner, respond to their texts, and switch to the other character from time to time.

But trust me, you'll want to spend lots of time together. Their relationship is fun to take part in, from both sides. Plus, doing crimes as a couple really pays off. It's helpful to have an extra set of hands to point guns at people, do crowd control during robberies, shoot out a cop's tires while driving, and carry off more loot than you could handle if you were rolling solo.

In a recent development, I'll be honest: I don't know how the game is different if you have a 4/4 relationship or a 1/4 relationship, because I've only played through the story once at this point, and I kept my relationship as strong as possible for nearly the entire game. We'll know more about the effects of a weak relationship as other players experiment with their relationship status, and we'll enhancement this guide with what we find out.
