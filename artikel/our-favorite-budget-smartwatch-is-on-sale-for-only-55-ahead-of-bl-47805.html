The Watch 3 Pro features a large AMOLED display and boasts an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance. In addition to the standard health features you'll see on most smartwatches these days — think heart-rate tracking, blood oxygen monitoring, and step counts — it supports workout tracking for over 130 sports, plus a running training feature. It can track your sleep quality, too, and Allain noted its metrics were just as detailed as the ones she got from a $3,300 smartbed.

Let's talk battery life. Allain managed to squeeze 16 days of regular use from her Watch 3 Pro, which is ridiculously impressive. For comparison's sake, I'm right now testing out the new Apple Watch Series 12, and it only lasts about a day and a half. (It charges a lot faster, though.).
