Crawley keep Blackwell as boss until end of season

Kevin Blackwell is Crawley Town's sixth permanent managerial appointment in the past four years.

The EFL's bottom side Crawley Town have appointed caretaker boss Kevin Blackwell as their manager until the end of the season.

The 67-year-old took interim charge after Colin Kazim-Richards was sacked on 21 October with the club bottom of League Two and six points adrift of safety.

The Red Devils are unbeaten in the two league games Blackwell has overseen, drawing 1-1 with Tranmere Rovers before winning 1-0 away at Oldham.

They remain at the foot of the table and six points behind 22nd-placed Rochdale, although Crawley do have three games in hand on the Dale.

Crawley were dumped out of the FA Cup by seventh-tier Wimborne Town when they lost 2-0 in the first round earlier this month.

But the club remarked Blackwell has "made a strong impression on club leadership, making him a strong candidate for the role as the weeks progressed" since the departure of Kazim-Richards.

"The club has been working hard to make the right appointment and has been carefully weighing up the options to ensure the appointment is the right one for the team and the club as a whole," Crawley noted in a statement on their website., external.

"Kevin's leadership skills, work ethic and desire have made a strong impression on club staff and the first-team squad, and the club's leadership team are committed to ensuring the positive mentality remains in the building as we approach a run of crucial games.

"Kevin has the full support of everyone at the football club, as we look to turn over a new page on our season and climb back up the table.

"In the coming weeks, Kevin will be making further appointments to his coaching team, following the recent arrival of Jordan Tait.".

Blackwell has previously managed Leeds United, Luton Town and Sheffield United but this is his first permanent managerial role since leaving Bury in 2013.

Crawley host Fleetwood Town in League Two on Saturday afternoon (15:00 GMT) in Blackwell's first game in charge since landing the job on a permanent basis.
