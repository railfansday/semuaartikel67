Arsenal spurned the chance to go eight points clear at the top of the Premier League table as they were forced to come from behind to draw 2-2 with Newcastle United at St James' Park.

Warned to expect a "hellish" fixture at a ground where they have not had much luck in recent years, the champions were in the ascendancy from the off and deservedly went into half time ahead courtesy of Eberechi Eze's first-time finish.

Matthias Jaissle's men were booed off at half time after generating just 0.08 Expected Goals during the opening period, but that number shot up just four minutes into the second half, when a gift from Martin Odegaard handed Harvey Barnes an equaliser against the run of play.

Barnes's strike triggered a magnificent Newcastle response, one that saw them rewarded with the game's third goal through Malick Thiaw just past the hour mark following a corner scramble.

However, the momentum pendulum swung back Arsenal's way shortly after, as the effervescent Noni Madueke drew the holders level to set up a grand-stand finish.

Nine minutes of added time was not enough for either team to snatch a winner, as Arsenal extended their lead over Manchester City and Brighton & Hove Albion to six points, while Newcastle remain 12th with 13 points.

For all of Arsenal's first-half dominance, the Gunners were guilty of a perpetual shortcoming in the Arteta era; not putting the game to bed before the break.

Staunch Newcastle defending and shoddy Arsenal finishing denied the visitors a healthier first-half advantage, and while the champions cut complacent figures at the start of the second half, Jaissle's half-time team talk worked a treat.

Odegaard's blunder helped the hosts on their way, and Arsenal found themselves unfamiliarly rattled for a good 30 minutes, until the increasingly impressive Madueke came to the fore again.

However, the winger himself admitted that his side should have been at least three to the good at half time, yet more evidence that a killer finisher is a matter of priority for the January transfer window.
