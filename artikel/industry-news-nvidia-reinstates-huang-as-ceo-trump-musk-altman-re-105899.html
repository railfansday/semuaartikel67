📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, nvidia has reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as CEO less than two weeks after he stepped down, and less than a week after the company's board said it had no intention of bringing him back. Reactions to Huang's return have been a mix of praise, criticism, and jokes, with several other tech executives rallying around him.

According to recent reports, nvidia unveiled Huang's resignation on Nov. 12, a day after the board accepted it with immediate effect. He stepped down amid reports that he'd had extramarital affairs with both an alleged Chinese spy and a Nvidia employee, with allegations of a third affair with another worker subsequently surfacing. His relationship with Alice Huang (no relation) drew particular scrutiny, as she was suspected of smuggling Nvidia chips. She previously worked as an executive at Megaspeed, a tech giant U.S. authorities have been investigating over concerns it smuggled advanced Nvidia chips to China in circumvention of U.S. restrictions.

Industry observers note that while Huang said that he would be "honored" to return as CEO if asked, the board said on Nov. 17 that it did not proposal to bring him back. Yet after reported pressure from President Donald Trump, the chipmaker has put Huang back in the top job after all. In a press release announcing Huang's reinstatement, Nvidia stated that it had based the decision on "input from senior levels of the United States government.".

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," remarked Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones, who last week said that the tech giant "does not intend to reinstate Jensen.".

In a recent development, trump has been characteristically celebratory about Nvidia's decision, making several posts on his social media platform Truth Social on the topic. This included an edited image based on a photo of Trump after a shooter attempted to assassinate him in 2024. Originally posted by X account @grandoldmemes, the picture has been edited to show Huang holding a red bra aloft as a Nvidia banner is displayed in the background.

Sources indicate that calling Huang's reinstatement an "absolutely GREAT decision" (emphasis original), Trump stated that having him installed as Nvidia's CEO is vital to the U.S. economy.

Recent findings suggest that "The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Tech giant, but the American Economy," wrote Trump. "The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to Nvidia poses any risk to National Security…. On a personal note, I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure.".

The U.S. president also spoke about Huang's reinstatement in a phone call to CNBC on Monday.

"I think he's gonna start off at an almost higher level," Trump stated. "He's apologised for [his] indiscretion, which was unfortunate. Probably that’s what brought it to such a level, because sex sells, fellas. Now [CNBC host Jim] Cramer would not know that because he’s a very, very, very committed person. But sex sells.".

Trump's on-and-off ally Elon Musk also celebrated Huang's return, reposting Nvidia's announcement on X and writing that "the mistake has been corrected." Last week, Musk stated that he was signing a petition to reinstate Nvidia's then-ousted CEO.

Industry observers note that "Everyone I know at Nvidia wanted Jensen back and it indicates," the Tesla and SpaceX CEO continued, sharing a post from investor Gavin Baker. In it, Baker shared images of people crowding around Huang and applauding him on his return to Nvidia's offices.

"Grateful to the President, everyone in the administration, everyone who signed the petition, everyone who supported Jensen publicly, everyone who called the board, everyone on the board who fought for him, everyone," Baker wrote. Recent findings suggest that musk himself was ousted as CEO of X.com in 2000 after it merged with Confinity, the company behind PayPal. He had reportedly wanted to phase out the PayPal name in favor of X.com, despite concerns about its explicit connotations.

Various other prominent figures in tech have also voiced support for Huang's return, several of whom have previously faced comparable turmoil at their own companies.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman appeared to express approval for Huang's reinstatement, publishing a post on X that simply read, "Jensen!" Altman went through a similar incident when OpenAI's board fired him in 2023, accusing him of lying, retaliating against critics, and creating a "toxic atmosphere." Despite these allegations, Altman was ultimately reinstated after days of outcry from supporters.

Meanwhile, Binance co-founder and former CEO Changpeng Zhao posted to X that he was "glad to see @JensenHuang back," resharing his previous reaction that it was "a mistake for Nvidia to let Jensen resign." Zhao resigned from his role as CEO of Binance and pleaded guilty to failing to maintain an effective anti-money laundering program in 2023, serving a four-month prison sentence. However, he remains Binance's majority shareholder and retains significant influence over the global cryptocurrency exchange.

Recent findings suggest that telegram CEO Pavel Durov also celebrated Huang's return, posting "Jensen's back" with a party popper emoji. He then joked about the situation, writing that "Nvidia's board finally accepted that a CEO has to evaluate Chinese models personally. You can't delegate that.".

Durov was ousted from his former role as CEO of Russian social media platform VK in 2014 after the board remarked it had accepted a resignation he'd submitted and failed to withdraw. Durov claimed the resignation had been an April Fools' joke and suggested that his firing was politically motivated.

Sources indicate that given their own personal experiences, it's no surprise that many former and current tech executives have supported Huang's return as Nvidia's CEO. Other reactions have been a mixed bag — some celebrating the news, some condemning it, and others simply enjoying the jokes and memes.

"Reinstating Huang is a disaster for corporate governance and even the rule of law, but it is objectively very very funny," author and tech reporter David Z. Morris posted to X.

Some have also noted the timing of Huang's temporary ousting, joking that he deliberately orchestrated several days away from work in order to play Grand Theft Auto VI.

It has been revealed that it's been a tumultuous few weeks for Nvidia, and it's unclear how the firm plans to move forward at this stage. However, whatever happens, the jokes will likely continue for a while.
