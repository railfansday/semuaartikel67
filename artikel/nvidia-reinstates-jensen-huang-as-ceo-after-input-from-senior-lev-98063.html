📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jensen Huang has been reinstated as Nvidia CEO, president, and board director by the company's board. The vote on Huang's return, which comes just weeks after his resignation amid a scandal over allegedly multiple affairs, was unanimous following unspecified but telling "input from senior levels of the United States government.".

According to recent reports, in a press release published today, Nvidia's board is suddenly flush with praise of Huang, the unfaithful man they only just fired out of a cannon. "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Nvidia indicates. "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Manufacturer.".

Huang himself, who recently said he would be more than happy to return to one of the highest-paying jobs imaginable, also commented on his reinstatement. "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," he insists.

Recent findings suggest that before anything, Huang notes, "I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as Chair, and to Colette Kress and our executive staff for leading NVIDIA during this period.".

In a recent development, huang adds, "I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can't wait to get back to work.”.

Industry observers note that A recent New York Times report details extensive allegations of abusive conduct against Huang, with multiple sources saying he regularly threatened employees with violence and used public humiliation to impose his will. His actions reportedly included threatening to tear one employee's arms off, threatening to crack another's skull open and urinate on their brain, mocking an employee for spending time with their family, and telling an elderly employee that people are hoping for their death.

Sources indicate that with Nvidia stock plunging in recent weeks and the firm at the center of much of the AI data center buildout propping up both the US economy's growth and the stock market's outsized tech sector, US President Donald Trump has repeatedly called for the return of Huang in television and social media statements. "I'd like to see Jensen make a big apology and go back," Trump mentioned just days ago.
