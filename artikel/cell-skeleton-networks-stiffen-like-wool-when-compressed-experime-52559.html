Cell skeleton networks stiffen like wool when compressed, experiments reveal

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (53 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

As they move and push against obstacles, cells generate forces. These forces originate in their internal skeleton, some parts of which behave like wool. This mechanical property has been revealed by scientists from the CNRS, Université Paris Cité and Université Paris-Saclay. This study is published in the journal Nature.
