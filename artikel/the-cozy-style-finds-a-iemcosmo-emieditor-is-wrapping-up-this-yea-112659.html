Sources indicate that not to brag, but here at Cosmo, we’re pretty talented at gift-giving. One of our present-scouring pros is assistant beauty editor Jasmine Hyman. “My personal gifting philosophy is knowing my giftee’s interests and combining them with new and trending products,” she states. “For nailing a style gift, I love getting someone a piece they’ll use or wear often, and something I think they would like but might not necessarily spring for on their own.”.

And because she knows that gifting isn’t one-size-fits-all, Jasmine relies on Target: “It’s my gift-shopping go-to because it truly has something for everyone—whether it’s my sports-enthusiast dad, fashion-loving mom, or fitness-obsessed boyfriend, there’s a variety of options with every aisle you stroll—or scroll—down.”.
