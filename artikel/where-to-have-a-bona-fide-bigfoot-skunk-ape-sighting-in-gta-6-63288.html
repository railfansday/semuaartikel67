In a recent development, one of GTA's many gags is back in GTA 6: the ol' bigfoot sighting. Well, technically I think it's a "skunk ape," real-life Florida's maybe real-life boogeyman "cousin" to Bigfoot. Don't get that cryptid family tree twisted. How do I know it's skunk ape? I saw the thing, I swear, and there was a cloud of green stink wafting off of it. Here's exactly where it's at if you want your own smelly primate encounter.

According to recent reports, the skunk ape sighting I've found so far is just across Gambit Bay from Jason's initial house. Hop in Jason's boat on the dock outside his place and set course for the dead end road on the inside of this peninsula. Before you even make land you'll get a prompt telling you to use Focus Mode to follow the source of the noises.

Sources indicate that hark! It's skunk ape! The creature hightails its stinky hide off into the mangrove-y woods and you'll need to keep using focus mode to chase after. You'll lose sight of it and instead find some creepy effigy thing standing beside a tree. Check it out, get stink-apacitated, and get your not so informative close-up sighting of bigfoot's substantial feet.

After your sighting, keep your eyes peeled for flyers about the skunk ape posted on storefronts and telephone poles around Leonida. They'll lead you to a mission where you can learn more about the mysterious beast.
