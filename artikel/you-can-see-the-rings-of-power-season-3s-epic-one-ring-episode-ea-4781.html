Good news Lord of the Rings fans, you can see the penultimate episode of The Rings of Power season 3 two days early, but you'll have to head to the movie theater.

On Monday, November 23, The Rings of Power season 3 episode 7 will screen in 662 selected theaters in nine countries, including the US and UK, according to The Lord of the Rings Fan Club. Check out the poster below.

It is not out of the ordinary for huge reveals to send anticipated episodes to the significant screen. On New Year's Eve 2025, Netflix demonstrated the final episode of Stranger Things season 5 across the US and overseas in over 600 locations. More recently, Prime Video screened The Boys series finale in cinemas in the US. However, you may be wondering why Amazon is choosing to screen the penultimate episode rather than the finale. Well, that is because the seventh episode finally shows the forging of the One Ring.

It has been revealed that 25 years after the first Lord of the Rings movie hit screens, fans will finally get to see The One Ring to Rule Them All being made. So, it's fair to say that it's a long time coming. The sequence is so important, as it is the catalyst that set the whole franchise in motion. We know that The Ring will eventually make its way to The Shire, where Frodo and the Fellowship set out to destroy it. Though the movies reference the sequence, we never actually see the full forging, showing Sauron binding himself to the ring inside the fires of Mount Doom.

Everything has been leading up to this. As we have seen in The Rings of Power so far, Sauron has been on a quest to forge the One Ring to unlock his ultimate power and rule over Middle-earth. However, we still have a way to go before the Dark Lord reaches Mount Doom. The Rings of Power returned to Prime this November with a stacked four-episode premiere. Episodes 5 and 6 are set to land on the streamer on Wednesday, November 18, which continues Sauron's hunt for the Elves and Dwarves.
