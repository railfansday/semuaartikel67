Industry News: Caedrel and Dan Gaskin win for Britain at Esports Awards as Counter-Strike 2 takes top game

Sources indicate that britain's Marc 'Caedrel' Lamont and Dan Gaskin were among the winners at the 2026 Esports Awards in Miami, where Counter-Strike 2 was named game of the year and Team Vitality and BLAST also took industry honours. The results crown a strong Counter-Strike year eight days before the next CS2 Major begins in Singapore.

According to recent reports, the 2026 Esports Awards honoured two British figures from the streaming and broadcast side of the scene. Caedrel is a League of Legends streamer and co-streamer. Gaskin works as a caster.

Sources indicate that the ceremony took place on the evening of 17 November at the Adrienne Arsht Center for the Performing Arts of Miami-Dade County. It is the scene’s main end-of-season awards show. The event began in London in 2016.

At Worlds 2026, Caedrel was the top co-streamer for both hours watched and peak viewers, according to Esports Charts.

It has been revealed that his co-stream success came in a softer year for the tournament overall. Worlds 2026 peaked at 3.98 million viewers, which was 41% lower than the previous year. Caedrel’s numbers show how much attention a single co-streamer can hold even when the event’s headline peak falls.

Gaskin was not the only caster honoured in Miami. The other broadcast honours went to Alex ‘Machine’ Richardson for Play-by-Play Caster of the Year, Iain Chambers for Host of the Year and Anthony ‘NAMELESS’ Wheeler for Analyst of the Year. iShowSpeed was named Streamer of the Year.

Sources indicate that counter-Strike featured prominently among the industry prizes. Vitality’s award as best organisation came alongside Esports PC Player of the Year for Mathieu ‘ZywOo’ Herbaut. OpTic Texas were named Esports Team of the Year.

BLAST, which stages many of its events in Europe, was named Esports Tournament Organiser of the Year.

Industry observers note that vitality’s results through the year back up their award. They beat Spirit to win IEM Rio 2026 on 19 April, giving them back-to-back ESL Grand Slam titles. ZywOo took his 31st career HLTV MVP award at that event.

Recent findings suggest that the team’s form held into the autumn. Vitality swept MOUZ 3-0 in the ESL Pro League Season 24 grand final on 11 October. That was their second title in a row after winning StarSeries Fall in September.

The PGL Major Singapore 2026 runs from 25 November to 13 December with 32 teams and a US$1,250,000 prize pool, The Kallang reported. The final stages will be played at the Singapore Indoor Stadium from 10 to 13 December.
