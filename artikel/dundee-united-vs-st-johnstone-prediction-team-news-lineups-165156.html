Hoping to create some separation in the top half of the Scottish Premiership table, Dundee United will welcome St Johnstone to Tannadice Park on Saturday.

The Tangerines could make it nine games unbeaten this weekend, while the Saints are keen to bounce back from defeat last time out.

Jim Goodwin's Dundee Utd have proven immensely resilient this term, and arrive full of confidence having lost just once on home turf in the league.

Adding to that feeling is the fact the Tangerines thrashed Hibernian 4-0 on the road last Saturday, thanks to a brace from Zachary Sapsford and second-half efforts from Lyall Cameron and Jesse Randall.

That victory has Goodwin's side fifth in the Scottish Premiership table with 20 points from their 13 matches, a tally that sees them three clear of city rivals Dundee in seventh having played one game fewer.

United will be aiming to distance themselves from the bottom half this weekend, though it is notable they have drawn as many matches as they have won in 2026-27, including three of their last five.

The hosts also kept just one clean sheet during that spell while conceding six goals in the process, letting in two in the 3-2 home win over Aberdeen, and two in the 2-2 stalemate at Celtic on October 28.

Meanwhile, Simo Valakari's St Johnstone are feeling the pressure from their rivals, and face a tough test to stay in the top half this weekend.

The Saints were downed 2-1 by Falkirk last Saturday, falling two goals behind shortly after the interval and only pulling one back late on through Josh Campbell.

That defeat brought a three-game unbeaten streak to an end, and means that Valakari's side arrive having triumphed in just one of their last six matches, beating Dundee 2-1 on November 7.

To make matters worse, St Johnstone failed to keep a clean sheet in any of those six clashes, conceding two or more on four separate occasions.

Currently sixth with 17 points after 14 games, the Saints will be looking over their shoulder given they are level with Dundee and just three points ahead of Aberdeen, Hibernian and 10th-placed St Mirren.

Knowing that they are at risk of dropping down into the bottom six, fans of the visitors will also be concerned after seeing their team score the fewest goals of any team in the top half (18).

Dundee Utd will be without attacker Neil Farrugia due to a groin injury, while versatile left-back Will Ferry is dealing with a muscle glitch.

In their absence, Jesse Randall and Daniel Bennie look set to support striker Zachary Sapsford up top on Saturday, with Joshua Rawlins and Kevin Ciubotaru offering further width from full-back.

As for St Johnstone, they are missing centre-back Morgan Boyes, who has a calf injury to contend with, though Danny Finlayson and Bozo Mikulic should be on hand to start at the heart of the visitors' defence.

At the opposite end of the pitch, winger Unax Alvarez has a calf injury of his own, and attacking midfielder Jamie Gullan is sidelined due to a groin problem.

With that in mind, Josh Fowler should lead the line this weekend, flanked by Taylor Steven and Kai Fotheringham.
