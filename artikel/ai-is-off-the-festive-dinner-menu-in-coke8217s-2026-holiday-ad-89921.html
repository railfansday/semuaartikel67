Coca-Cola’s 2026 holiday ad is just as notable for the warm-and-fuzzies it brings as for what it’s lacking: AI (as well as it’s signature polar bears).

Titled “Enjoy Joy” and created by WPP Open X, led by Ogilvy, the 30-second spot is pure Norman Rockwell’s America.

Friends and family toss a football around in the yard of a white country farmhouse as the men deep fry a turkey. In the cavernous dining room, it’s a potpourri of food, laughter, embraces, and two liter bottles of Coke.

Trimmable to 15- and 6-second formats, the spot has both English and Spanish versions.

Robert Klara is the senior editor of brands at Adweek, specializing in the evolution and impact of brands.
