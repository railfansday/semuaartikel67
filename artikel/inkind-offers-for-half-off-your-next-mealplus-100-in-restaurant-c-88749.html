Costco will sell $100 in inKind restaurant credit for $64.99 on Thanksgiving. There are also live offers for $50 off a $100 meal and $25 off a $50 meal, while inKind is sending some customers a 50% bonus offer for buying credit. The Costco sale actually gives you a bigger discount than that 50% bonus, before credit card rewards.

I’ve written about how inKind works and why I use it. They’ve signed up restaurants where I already eat: The Well, Peached Tortilla, Wu Chow, Lao’d Bar and Old Thousand in Austin. Unfortunately Waterloo Ice House, where the food isn’t very good but they have playgrounds for my kids, has left the program..

You ask for the check, select the restaurant in the app, enter the check number and pay with your phone. Tips go to your linked credit card. You don’t need to have booked your table through inKind.

The business behind it is restaurant financing: inKind buys future food and beverage credit, then sells or awards it to diners. Restaurants get money now and customers later.

Danny the Deal Guru flags the upcoming sale, and Costco’s Thanksgiving ad details: Pay $64.99 for $100 in inKind credit. Valid online November 26, 2026 only, while supplies last. Limit five. Buying all five gets you $500 in dining credit for $324.95. That’s a 35.01% discount. It’s the same price I covered in September, and it’s coming back for Thanksgiving rather than Black Friday. Here’s the Costco product page. It’s showing $74.99 as I write this, so wait for the sale. The credit covers food, beverages and applicable taxes at participating restaurants. Costco lists the item as nonrefundable, and says the redemption code normally arrives by email within 24 hours. Others report needing to purchase at least $500 to get the same percentage bonus. Check your email and the app for the offer attached to your account. Paying $100 for $150 means you’re paying 66⅔ cents per dollar of restaurant credit. Costco’s Thanksgiving sale charges 64.99 cents. For the same $300 in dining credit, that’s $200 through a 50% bonus offer versus $194.97 at Costco. The $5.03 difference is small enough that different credit card earnings could change which purchase is better. The amount you pay for inKind Cash doesn’t expire. The promotional portion has a three year expiration. Neither purchase earns another round of inKind rewards when you spend the prepaid balance.
