Sources indicate that the federal sterile cockpit rule specifically includes taxi among the critical phases of flight. It prohibits activities that could distract flight crew members from their duties.

A separate provision restricts the use of personal wireless communications devices at a flight crew duty station during flight, with exceptions for specified purposes under approved airline procedures. The FAA’s guidance makes clear that prohibited personal use includes telephone conversations, texting, and browsing the internet.

Sources indicate that pilots can use approved electronic flight bags (EFBs are the iPads that replace paper manuals and charts) and make necessary operational communications. Taking an ordinary personal call while taxiing is another matter.
