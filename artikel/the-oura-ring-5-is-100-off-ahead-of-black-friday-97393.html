The Oura Ring 5 is the world’s smallest smart ring and looks more like a piece of stylish jewelry than a fitness tracker. Though it’s rarely discounted, it’s presently seeing a record-low price of $299 (originally $399) in this early Black Friday deal.

In addition to being 40% smaller than its predecessor, the Oura Ring 5 also lasts up to nine days longer than the Oura Ring 4, and has upgraded sensors for better accuracy. It’s completely waterproof and more durable, with increased scratch resistance, but still has the same discreet aesthetic, with a titanium build. It earned an Editors’ Choice Award from PCMag and has new features like long-term breathing-pattern measurements and overnight blood-pressure checks, joining the suite of features that track sleep, health, and activity trends.

According to recent reports, it does charge an annual membership fee to access most of those features, so those looking for a basic fitness tracker might want to opt for a cheaper alternative like the Fitbit Air. However, if you’re shopping for a more robust screen-less health and fitness tracker with an exceptional, elegant design that feels lightweight and looks natural,  the Oura Ring 5 is a worthwhile option.
