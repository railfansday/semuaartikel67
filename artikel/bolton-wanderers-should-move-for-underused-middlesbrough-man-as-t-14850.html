Bolton Wanderers should move for underused Middlesbrough man as they look to avoid Championship relegation battle: Opinion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that bolton Wanderers have made a positive start to life in the Championship since winning promotion in the summer. Steven Schumacher‘s Trotters sit seven points above the bottom three after 15 games; a good position, but they will know there is still a lot of work to do.

Recent findings suggest that despite their good start, BWFC have found a number of problems that they will have to fix if they want to remain in the Championship. One of those issues has been depth in midfield, with Schumacher’s squad containing just five players they deem ready to play in the central areas.

Recent findings suggest that attacking midfield has been a particularly unsettled area, with Atsuke Ito and Ruben Rodrigues failing to lock down the position. One solution would be to make a loan move for Middlesbrough’s Leo Castledine.

According to recent reports, the 21-year-old has featured in just eight games for Boro, and with Kim Hellberg‘s side hunting promotion, it seems unlikely that he will force his way into the starting picture. It means the door is ajar for Bolton Wanderers to swoop in and pick up a player who could help them avoid relegation.

It has been revealed that for Schumacher and co, it is all about staying in the Championship this season. Depth in all areas will be key to do just that, and Bolton Wanderers need it in the attacking midfield role.

The Trotters play a 4-2-3-1 formation that utilises a single attacking midfielder. Ito and Rodrigues have been the main two players to play the role, with three goals coming from the pair. The problem is, both have spent time on the injury table, and neither player has hit the heights Schumacher needs.

Castledine, who showed just what he can do at the position with Huddersfield Town in 2025/26, would slot in not only as welcome depth, but as a player who knows the role inside out.

Castledine’s move from Chelsea to Middlesbrough has not yet worked out and Boro have other priorities this season. He has not found a regular role and with promotion at the forefront of Hellberg’s mind, moving the 21-year-old on to strengthen his squad and help the player develop suits all.

That opens the door for Bolton Wanderers to pick up a quality player in an area of need for a low-cost deal. It satisfies all parties, including Castledine’s need for regular football and Middlesbrough’s desire to add quality as they push for the Premier League.

Other clubs are sure to be lurking, but BWFC could be a great destination for Castledine as he looks for a chance to prove his ability in the Championship.
