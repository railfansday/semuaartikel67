VanEck ends Gemini deal for 3 crypto ETFs – What’s next for VSOL?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

VanEck has terminated Gemini as custodian and clearing agent for three U.S. crypto ETFs. In doing so, it has made Coinbase Custody its sole remaining safekeeper, thus putting into jeopardy the Solana fund’s staking future.

As per three 8-K filings from the 19th of November, VanEck Digital Assets delivered written termination notices to Gemini Trust Company on the 16th of November, with the contract set to officially end on 16th February.

The notice ended custody and clearing arrangements for the VanEck Bitcoin ETF (HODL) and the VanEck Ethereum ETF (ETHV).

In a recent development, the third ETF, the VanEck Solana ETF (VSOL), is the most affected one in this regard, because there is no news on who will take over staking services for it. Market participants are assuming it is Coinbase, but as of now, there is no confirmation yet.

As expected, the filings have opened a series of questions, as they have not established the allocation of assets between Gemini and Coinbase. This, in turn, has made it impossible to quantify precisely what Gemini is losing in dollar terms.

The filing also failed to explain why VanEck chose to terminate the agreements now, with roughly three months of notice before the effective date. Moreover, they did not even address the staking question that matters most for VSOL shareholders.

This comes as the crypto ETF landscape has been in a consolidation for a while now, with Coinbase already being a custodian for most U.S. Spot crypto ETFs.

All in all, VanEck’s current decision to put an end to its Gemini arrangements has left it with a single custodian across all three funds and a massive unresolved operational gap.

What’s left now is that by 16th February, 2027, the firm will have to find a new staking provider for VSOL.
