Liverpool are at the centre of an exciting move, and it could be for a bargain.

If there is one thing Andoni Iraola will be disappointed by during his start to life at Anfield it's the lack of creativity in his attack.

Industry observers note that the club's recruitment team has assembled an exciting cohort of attackers. Yet aside from Alexander Isak, none of them appear to be thriving too much in front of goal.

In fact, Liverpool could be a lot worse off if it was not for Isak's efficiency who is very much putting away most of his chances at the moment.

But statistically, Liverpool are struggling to create chances, and this is due to the accumulation of various factors.

It's something that has to be addressed in the market, but there could be internal solutions as well.

Recent findings suggest that why are Liverpool struggling to create chances?A lot of people have pinned the blame on Florian Wirtz. He is after all the chief architect at the club. But Liverpool's problems are not simply down to Wirtz not pulling his weight. If you're looking at Liverpool's attackers with the exception of Bradley Barcola, most of them are attackers who want to cut into the middle. And then behind them there is not enough offensive output from the full-backs on the wings. Milos Kerkez is not a super creative player neither is Ronald Araujo. Both have their uses for various other reasons. However, ultimately, Liverpool could be better off recruiting more technical full-backs who are capable of producing chances and playing passes promptly into their teammates. The same can be said for the midfield, where Liverpool are missing a more creative force.
