According to recent reports, bitcoin ETF outflows and nearly $3.02 billion in liquidations offer a measurable picture of the latest market damage. But whether the approaching Anthropic IPO has anything to do with it remains unproven.

Industry observers note that the Bitcoin price fell to $62,021 on November 15. Meanwhile, US spot funds recorded approximately $1.91 billion in net withdrawals across seven trading sessions from November 5 to November 13.

Sources indicate that blackRock’s IBIT accounted for roughly $1.42 billion of those withdrawals, around 74% of the total. November 13 alone saw approximately $185 million leave the funds.

Ethereum ETFs also recorded about $398 million in net withdrawals during the week ending November 13.

Recent findings suggest that the biggest long-liquidation days were November 13, at $692.16 million, and November 14, at $629.09 million. November 15 added another $165.13 million in wiped-out long positions.
