In a recent development, prince William and Kate Middleton’s visit to India for the Earthshot Prize ceremony marked their first joint overseas tour in four years, and the couple used it as an opportunity to demonstrate their star power. Through their action-packed itinerary, which took them from Mumbai to New Delhi, the Prince and Princess of Wales have paved the way for a new style of royal tour.

From Kate unexpectedly presenting an award and delivering a speech at the star-studded Earthshot Prize event to her and William playing cricket and traveling around Mumbai in an electric rickshaw, there have been plenty of photo opportunities and headlines around the world about the environmental initiative William is so passionate about.

According to recent reports, according to royals author Russell Myers, who followed the couple on tour, the speech Kate gave at the awards was kept top secret. “None of us knew she was presenting an award on the night, or that she was going to speak. The auditorium was packed. There were about 5,000 people there. It’s been the biggest Earthshot yet, and you could see Catherine was nervous, but she delivered an absolutely brilliant speech. I’d say her best yet.

“It’s been wonderful for her to be here, supporting William, and you can see they’ve really had fun. Because of ongoing terror threats, there have been less walkabouts, but it’s been a very successful and varied tour, and I think, being shorter than most tours, it sets a upcoming blueprint when it comes to royal tours for the couple in the future.”.

Prince William and Princess Kate arrive to attend the Earthshot Prize awards night at the Jio World Convention Centre on November 17, 2026 in Mumbai, India.

There are seemingly already plans for another royal tour, states Myers.

“William let slip that Australia is on the cards next, and if that’s the case, I imagine they will go as a family. They don’t want to be away from their children for too long, and what a way it would be to win support for the monarchy Down Under,” Myers claims.

According to recent reports, according to royals insiders, there had been some nervousness around the trip after the couple’s disastrous tour of the Caribbean in March 2022, when their visit was overshadowed by calls for the UK to apologize for slavery. Then some of their photo calls, including one that involved the couple riding in an open-top Land Rover, led to accusations of the royals appearing colonial and out of touch.

Sources indicate that “I think there has been a slight nervousness about how this tour would be received, but the reception has been really good,” Myers says. “The fact that William and Catherine have included a visit to Latest Delhi to see Prime Minister Modi is a clever move. While this is a private trip to promote Earthshot, the Foreign Office was keen for William to meet with the Indian prime minister, so they have packed that into the itinerary too.”.

Industry observers note that the visit to India has come at a time when the royals are on something of a charm offensive following a difficult year for the royal family, from Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor’s arrest to what one palace insider calls “the ongoing soap opera” with Prince Harry and Meghan Markle. (Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor has not been charged and has denied all allegations of wrongdoing.).

Sources indicate that william and Kate are keen to carve out their own initiatives and projects focusing on their major priorities, including the environment, children’s early years, and mental health. With the challenge of having Harry and Meghan carrying out their own engagements, the Prince and Princess of Wales have been careful to establish clear parameters that set them apart as working royals. In one telling moment while on an engagement in Mumbai, Kate was asked for an autograph. She politely declined, so as not to breach protocol, a clear contrast to the Duchess of Sussex, who has signed autographs in the past.

Recent findings suggest that “Catherine was asked to sign an autograph, but she was right not to. It wasn’t a snub; it was just adhering to protocol. An autograph could be sold for money,” notes Dickie Arbiter, former press secretary to Queen Elizabeth II.

According to recent reports, according to author Tom Bower, the royals are more relatable now than they were during the reign of Queen Elizabeth II, who famously never gave a press interview. In recent weeks, Prince William has appeared as a guest on The Graham Norton Show (to promote Earthshot), and he’s revealed how he drinks his cider and his love of wild swimming as part of his guest-editing stint for a recent concern of Country Life magazine.

It has been revealed that “The royals are more relatable, perhaps, than they ever have been, but they are not celebrities. There’s a danger of being too relatable, too much ‘one of the people,’ which means they can lose some of their mystery,” Bower says.

“William will have been advised to do TV appearances and take on media opportunities to bolster his public image. Of course, it’s charming to hear these nuggets and see this side of the prince, but I do think there’s a need to see and hear more from William about his broader prepares. Over the years, Charles has made huge efforts to appeal to all cultural aspects of society. I’m not sure William has done that. He’s got to find a way of connecting with everyone, not just the younger generation,” Bower adds.

The Earthshot Prize awards have been a powerful way of demonstrating that William doesn’t just talk the talk; he is insisting on action.

“The Earthshot is a master class in what needs to be done,” concludes Arbiter. “He’s pushing for environmental complications and climate change, and he’s getting people to do something about it. The fact that he is so well supported, and by so many high-profile people, indicates his pulling power and influence.”.
