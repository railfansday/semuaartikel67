According to recent reports, landed this summer at Galatasaray for a check for 25 million euros, Aleksey Batrakov (21 years old) particularly impresses. The Russian attacking midfielder has become essential with his team. He also has 6 goals and 4 assists in 13 matches with the Stambouliotes. Interesting figures for a player who had been targeted by Paris Saint-Germain.

Sources indicate that and in fact, two other substantial names are now interested in it. With his four goals in the Champions League, the former Lokomotiv Moscow player caught the eye of FC Barcelona and Bayern Munich according to Fanatik. Observers from both clubs will attend his match against Aston Villa on Tuesday, November 24. Galatasaray already prepares to increase its release clause from 55 to 100 million euros.
