A upcoming report from the AI evaluation nonprofit METR finds that some capabilities are improving exponentially—but models aren’t as good at tasks that require judgment as they are at coding.

Recent findings suggest that the report updates METR’s widely shared time horizon plot, which tested models on a large suite of tasks. The upcoming version adds more open-ended tasks, such as designing AI evaluations and scoring philosophical arguments.

Models struggled with these tasks, which could prove crucial to automating research and development.

Google, Microsoft, and others are investing hundreds of millions of dollars in startups spreading crushed rock across farms to capture carbon from the air.

The approach, known as enhanced rock weathering, could also improve soil quality and boost crop yields.

Recent findings suggest that but latest research raises serious questions about how much material can be added to farmland before it starts reducing yields, MIT Technology Review can exclusively report. The findings suggest the carbon removal potential could be far smaller than previously estimated.
