West Ham United manager Scott Parker has spoken positively about Joël Piroe ahead of Saturday’s Championship clash with Preston North End, raising an interesting question over his starting striker.

During his pre-match press conference, Parker acknowledged the Dutchman’s impressive goalscoring record and revealed that he was looking forward to spending more time working with him.

Recent findings suggest that “I’ve known about Joël, having played against his teams. His goalscoring record is really good,” said Parker.

“It’s a shame for us he’s been away, but he’s in good form, and I’m looking forward to working with him more.”.

Personally, I would 100% start Piroe ahead of Taty Castellanos, irrespective of how well the Argentine might have trained during the international break.

Piroe is a proven Championship goalscorer, and Parker knows all about him from his previous experience managing in this division.

I think he very much deserves a run of games in an attempt to fire at least one of our strikers into action.

The alternative is to continue persevering with Castellanos, who has been a considerable disappointment since West Ham’s relegation.

And there is another factor to consider.

As Claret & Hugh exclusively revealed, the club are exploring the possibility of selling Castellanos in January, with Nils Koppen already investigating potential replacements.

Further reports from Argentina have suggested the striker wants to leave, although no departure has been agreed.

Piroe is admired by Scott Parker.

I don’t think there’s any mystery surrounding what Piroe offers.

He knows the Championship, he knows how to score goals in it, and he’s already demonstrated that he can contribute to this West Ham team.

As we’ve previously argued when discussing Parker’s selection options, Piroe has a compelling case to start ahead of Castellanos.

Of course, the manager will have his own ideas about how he wants his centre-forward to play.

But given the lack of goals from our recognised strikers, I’d rather see Parker give the Dutchman a proper opportunity than continue waiting for Castellanos to discover some form.

For me, Piroe starts against Preston.
