Liverpool believe a 6ft5 star might actually grow even further. He's going to be a monster.

Liverpool have spent the last few years amassing as much elite talent as possible. That's mainly seen in the substantial-money signings like Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola.

According to recent reports, those three are all nine-figure signings and among the most expensive players in history. But it's not the only way Liverpool have gone about their plan.

They've also moved for young players. Those with elite-level talent and who require a push in the right direction to place them among the best in the world. Jeremy Jacquet might be the most obvious example in Liverpool's first-team right now.

According to recent reports, but there is another who we've been waiting to see. And Liverpool feel he hasn't even finished developing physically.

In a recent development, jacquet might be the star centre-back of the future right now but he wasn't a little over a year ago. Back then it was Giovanni Leoni, who arrived from Parma with incredible hype.

Sources indicate that however, Leoni would suffer a terrible knee injury on his debut, meaning he still hasn't actually completed 90 minutes for Liverpool. He hasn't played since, either, and only got back to training with teammates this week.

Recent findings suggest that liverpool are managing Leoni's comeback very, very carefully. That would be the case anyway, of course, but there's an extra reason to do so here as The Times reports that the club feel he might still be growing.

Recent findings suggest that that complicates his recovery process but it also slightly surreal to hear as Leoni is already 6ft5. He's a massive defender as it is but it appears the 19-year-old may get even more massive.

Sources indicate that growth like that can have negative effects. It generates extra pressure on joints, means players carry weight differently, and can lead to complications. Of course, it can also have positive effects like a player who towers over forwards and doesn't even need to jump particularly high to win headers.

We'll see how things land with that one.

Industry observers note that leoni is expected to feature at some point in the coming months. All signs suggest his rehabilitation has been good overall and Liverpool are reportedly excited about his return to the fold.
