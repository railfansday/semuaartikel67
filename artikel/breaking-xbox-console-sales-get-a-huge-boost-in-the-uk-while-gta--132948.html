We've got some new data coming out about the UK games market in November 2026 today, and unsurprisingly, it's a market that's been absolutely dominated by the launch of GTA 6 last week.

According to the folks at The Game Business, sales of the Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S rose by a whopping 75% during GTA 6's launch week, which represents the biggest jump for Xbox hardware since 2024.

The PS5 was close behind at 46%, and console sales were up 38% overall (after a 29% increase the week before).

Industry observers note that in terms of UK console sales, PS5 sales rose 46% during the week GTA 6 came out, while Xbox Series S and X sales jumped 75%. It’s the biggest jump for Xbox since 2024. Overall, UK console sales last week were up 38%, which comes on the back of a 29% increase the week prior.

Meanwhile, the controversial "physical" edition of GTA 6 broke numerous records in Europe, serving as the biggest physical launch in Spain's history according to their charts, and selling over 1m physical copies in France in three days.

Industry observers note that it was also the biggest physical launch in the UK since FIFA 20 in September 2019, and is the 17th biggest physical launch since records began there in 1995. This is according to data provided by UK data tracker Nielsen IQ.

"GTA 6 is the biggest physical game launch since 2019, reveals UK data tracker Nielsen IQ. It generated £32 million in revenue in just three days.".

It has been revealed that just last week, on the day of GTA 6's release, we mentioned how Xbox hardware spending in the U.S. was up 42% YoY in October, attributed to the likes of Gears of War: E-Day and GTA 6.

It's going to be fascinating to see how the rest of the world stacks up in terms of Xbox sales data from November (which should be revealed over the coming days and weeks), and indeed whether GTA 6's physical edition is also setting records in even more regions. It most likely is - this is a ridiculously popular game after all.
