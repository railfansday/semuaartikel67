Industry observers note that los Angeles FC defender Sergi Palencia believes his side can benefit from home advantage in Game 2 against St. Louis City SC in the MLS Cup playoffs.

It has been revealed that st. Louis won the opening matchup in the best-of-three Round One series 3-2 at Energizer Park, already putting LAFC in a must-win situation.

Palencia was one of the goalscorers for LAFC, while Denis Bouanga also netted from the penalty spot in the defeat.

The Spaniard has urged his team-mates to put the defeat behind them and instead embrace the home encounter on Saturday.

In a recent development, "It's one game, but it's at home," stated Palencia. "We have to be positive.

According to recent reports, "There's some positives [from Game 1], and there's some things that we have to learn, but we're sure, with our people, we can win the next game and then let's see. Our full focus has to be on that.

It has been revealed that "Saturday is a fresh start. They'll likely make some adjustments, and we will too. We'll also look to improve by analysing what didn't go quite right, with the goal of securing a win.".

Sources indicate that st. Louis' Game 1 win was their first-ever victory in MLS playoff history, and it was sealed by a brilliant goal from Simon Becher.

City boss Yoann Damet heaped praise on Becher, as well as his side's humility in key moments.

"He's had a great season in terms of how much he's grown and also his output," Damet noted about Becher.

"What's special in our locker room is there are a lot of [selfless guys], and you can see that Simon is coming in [and] having an impact.".

Becher added: "I think the group is really comfortable pushing each other.

"The guys that are in the 11, the guys that are on the bench, and the guys that aren't on the bench… knowing their roles and coming on to help however they can.".

Playing in his 16th career MLS Cup playoff match in Game 1, Bouanga scored his 11th career postseason goal.

The only players in MLS history with more playoff goals are Landon Donovan (25), Carlos Ruiz (16), Roy Lassiter (13) and Jaime Moreno (12).
