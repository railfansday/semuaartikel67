Breaking: Learning facts out of interest may take less mental effort than learning them for money

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, when completing tasks or acquiring information, humans can be driven by intrinsic and extrinsic motivations. The term intrinsic motivation entails doing something because it is interesting to us or we find it enjoyable, while extrinsic motivation means doing it to obtain a reward, such as money, praise or a good grade.
