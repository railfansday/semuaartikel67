Breaking: Why Man City can't wear their new kit in Premier League

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Manchester City will only be wearing their orange kit for selected Champions League games.

Manchester City sported a upcoming orange fourth kit during their Champions League draw with Napoli on Tuesday - but will not be able to wear it in the Premier League.

Top-flight clubs in England can only wear a maximum of three strips in a league campaign, meaning Enzo Maresca's side cannot play in their new kit.

However, there is no limit in the Champions League, meaning City can wear their newly introduced strip for the rest of their European campaign.

City can also wear the kit in the FA Cup, provided that an application is made to the Football Association, as well as a reason for its requested use.

That is why Arsenal were allowed to play in a white fourth strip in the 2025-26 FA Cup as part of their campaign to combat youth knife crime in London.

Fans could scan the badge on City's 2025-26 fourth kit to receive items in a video game.

City also released a green fourth kit in 2025-26 with a gaming-inspired theme.

The kit, debuted during their Champions League victory over Villarreal, featured an NFC chip in the club badge that fans could scan to unlock gaming rewards.

The club released another fourth kit in the 2024-25 season co-designed by Noel Gallagher in tribute to the 30th anniversary of Oasis' 1994 album 'Definitely Maybe'.

It was worn in the Champions League by the men's team against Inter Milan and Paris St-Germain, and the women's team against Paris FC and Barcelona.

A fifth kit was also used by the men's team during the 2025 Club World Cup.

During the 2023-24 season, the women's side also sported a Chinese Latest Year-inspired shirt in a League Cup victory against rivals Manchester United.

While the Premier League is more restrictive over the number of kits that can be released - the rules are more relaxed in other European leagues.

Napoli wore 13 strips during the 2021-22 season; including a Halloween kit, four different Diego Maradona tributes and four Europa League-specific kits.
