Josh Hutcherson said he gave up on superhero movies after losing the role of Spider-Man in Sony's 2012 reboot of the formerly Sam Raimi-led franchise.

"Since I didn't get cast in Spider-Man, I was like, 'I don't ever want to be a superhero. Like, it's fine. I never really wanted that,'" Hutcherson noted on the Happy Sad Confused podcast. "And then Mister Miracle came around, and I read, all I had was like a few of the scenes to do the audition, and it was such a cool character.".

In a recent development, hutcherson was up for the role of Peter Parker/Spider-Man alongside Jamie Bell, Logan Lerman, and Alden Ehrenreich. Luckily for Hutcherson, the loss of Spidey led him to get the role of Peeta Mellark in Francis Lawrence's blockbuster movie franchise based on the dystopian YA book series, Hunger Games.

According to recent reports, the animated Mister Miracle series was announced just a couple of months ago with Hutcherson as the lead, with the show being canon to the new DCU... meaning Hutcherson could then go on to reprise the role in a live-action project later on. Sadly, the fate of Mister Miracle is presently up in the air as we don't know what projects will be axed now that James Gunn and Peter Safran are no longer the co-CEOs of DC Studios. So far, it looks like Lanterns season 2 is up on the chopping block.

Recent findings suggest that next up for Marvel is Avengers: Doomsday hits theaters worldwide on December 18. For more, check out how to watch the Marvel movies in order.
