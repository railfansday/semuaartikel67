Now I know what it feels like to have the Chaos gods whispering temptations in my ear, because Joytoy Warhammer action figures (which look incredible, FYI) are right now sitting at a major discount as part of Black Friday.

You can find all of the latest offers at Amazon, but strap in - there are quite a few. More specifically, everything from Warhammer 40K Intercessors to Horus Heresy Primarchs has been included in the roundup, with smatterings of Chaos thrown in for good measure. No matter which Chapter you like the most, chances are you'll find something to suit you in this Warhammer Joytoy sale.

In a recent development, while most of these offers seem exclusive to Prime members, don't lose hope if you aren't part of that club; you could always grab a free trial and bail once your figure arrives. Because these are amongst the better Black Friday Warhammer deals I've seen so far, I'd say it's worth the effort.

Anyway, here are my favorites from the sale so far.

As the poster boys for Warhammer 40K, it's no surprise that the Ultramarines are well represented in the Joytoy range... but this elite trooper (pulled directly from Space Marine 2) with Relic Primaris Armour is a cut above the rest thanks to that regal getup and a ton more detail. The action figure has never been cheaper than it is here.

As a Dark Angels fan, this action figure version of Cypher - a fallen Space Marine whose loyalties are incredibly hard to pin down - caught my eye. Its design captures everything about the gothic side of 40K that I adore, from hooded cloaks that are perfect for brooding in to an overabundance of spike and skull iconography.

Recent findings suggest that although I've got a soft spot for the Imperial factions, my heart truly belongs to Chaos - and Nurgle has always been my favorite of the four main clans. Their putrid grossness has been recreated well with this toy, and I love the abundance of vials filled with questionable potions that make it look like this guy's running a one-man apothecary.

It has been revealed that as a 40K prequel, the Horus Heresy's Space Marines are a little more rudimentary - and this action figure from the Iron Warriors faction is a great example. We've never seen this one go for less. Want a more rank-and-file trooper? The Night Lords Mark III action figure is currently on offer at Amazon too.

You can't beat the classics, and Joytoy's standard Intercessors are all incredibly well done. Only the White Scars version is on offer right now, but that just gives me an excuse to praise a faction I think is critically underrated.

Elsewhere in the hobby, these new Bretonnia models imply just how successful Warhammer The Old World has become.
