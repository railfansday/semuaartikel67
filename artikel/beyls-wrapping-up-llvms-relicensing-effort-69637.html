Kristof Beyls has published a summary to the LLVM discussion forum to announce that the project is declaring success and bringing its relicensing effort to a close after eight years.

In a recent development, we've been asking past contributors to relicense their work since 2018. Upcoming contributions have come in under Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception since January 19, 2019. Code contributed before that date was under LLVM's earlier licenses (UIUC/NCSA, plus MIT for some runtime libraries), so we needed permission from its authors to move it over. [...].

Rather than trying to "chase the last tiny fraction forever" LLVM is winding up the effort. Any contributors with code contributed before January 19, 2019 who have not signed a relicensing agreement and do not want their contributions made available under Apache-2.0 with the LLVM-exception can still reach out to the project to ensure their code has the appropriate SPDX license identifier.
