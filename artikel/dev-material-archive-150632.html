A new resource resurrecting lost web-dev articles and posts:.

This is an archive of written articles about web development, web design, and web accessibility that once existed on the World Wide Web and which would otherwise be lost to time. Publications close, SEO strategies change, rights lapse, standards alter, content management systems are migrated between, acquisitions happen, websites get redesigned.

Whatever the reason may be, so much high-quality web writing has been lost through the years.

It has been revealed that I love the effort, which is ongoing. Link rot is a real thing. It’s links that point to either removed content or a page that’s changed URLs. Either way, it’s an awkward topic in our field. No one is obligated to archive and retain content, but doing so clearly benefits us all.

A big thanks to this site for helping preserve the things we care about. The same can said about Jay Hoffman and his long-running work on The History of the Web.
