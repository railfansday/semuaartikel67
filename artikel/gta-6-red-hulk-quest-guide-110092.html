GTA 6: Red Hulk Quest Guide

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

While many of Grand Theft Auto 6’s craziest moments happen out of the blue while freeroaming, others are rewards for helping out strangers with their extended questlines. These questlines can be picked up in a variety of ways, rewarding players for scrolling Snapmatic or keeping an open eye around the world.

One such questline centers around the strawberry farmer Kyle McKittrick, a man who needs a hand or two ensuring that his special growth formula wins the local strawberry competition. As is GTA tradition, this simple premise spirals quickly, giving a glimpse of Rockstar’s take on a Marvel legend, but only if you can find him.

Sources indicate that kyle’s questline can be started as early as Chapter 1, provided you spot his Snapmatic post while scrolling as Jason. Look for a post featuring a prop plane and open it to get the quest marker far to the north of the Keys. This is a major detour from any of the story content in Chapter 1, so don’t worry about waiting, Kyle isn’t going anywhere.

Recent findings suggest that when you have time, drive up to his farm, and he’ll ask whichever protagonist you’re playing to pilot his prop plane and douse his strawberry fields with his own blend of growth hormones. This is a super chill mission, all you need to do is dump the chemicals above where Kyle is parked, dousing both him and his strawberries on each run. He’ll pay you for your help and ask you to keep in touch for any updates.

Sources indicate that after around 24 hours in-game, Kyle will call Jason and Lucia, asking them to pick up a supply of chemicals from a local biker gang. Once you arrive at the marked location, you’ll call him again for some extra context. He explains that the bikers will be expecting payment, but he doesn’t care how you get the chems either way.

The goal is to leave the compound with the van filled with Kyle’s chemicals, all else is secondary. At first, the bikers will demand $5,000 for the van, but it can be easily bartered down to $3,000. Refusing their price will start a fight, and it can be difficult to get the van off the premises with a sea of angry bikers.

I’d recommend paying the bartered-down cost if you can afford it, allowing you to safely drive the van out and park it at Kyle’s to complete the quest.

Wait a bit longer for another message from Kyle, or keep an eye on his Snapmatic account for a video where he demonstrates off his insanely massive strawberries before running off camera. Return to Kyle’s farm one final time to find an armed outfit of government agents attempting to get him to come outside.

To sneak in, enter the chemical van parked nearby, where you can slip on a hazmat suit to blend into the crowd. Head behind the barn and two guards will escort you inside, revealing Kyle’s new, Hulkish form. He’ll throw open the doors of the barn, and the assembled agents will open fire.

In a recent development, if you do nothing, Kyle will die, so take out the two guards still beside you in the barn and assist Kyle with clearing the rest. Once you do, he’ll fall to his knees, where you can comfort him and give him a hug for a huge boost in Professional Criminal Profile points.

It has been revealed that after you calm him down, Kyle will leap off, surely to assist against a future alien invasion of some kind. Thus ends one of Grand Theft Auto 6’s wildest questlines, shifting from farming assistance to superhero origin story at the drop of a hat.
