According to recent reports, in February 2011, Newcastle United completed one of the most incredible comebacks in Premier League history to draw 4-4 with Arsenal at St James’ Park. We want to know how well you remember the game.

Newcastle, who had just sold Andy Carroll to Liverpool, got off to a dreadful start and Arsenal’s Theo Walcott scored after 44 seconds.

Johan Djourou doubled their lead a couple of minutes later before Robin van Persie netted a brace to make it 4-0 inside the opening 26 minutes.

Sources indicate that but Arsenal’s Abou Diaby received a red card at the start of the second half and the game completely changed. Alan Pardew’s side scored two penalties in the second half and their No.20 also found the back of the net from close range.

A midfielder then scored a stunning volley in the 87th minute to complete the comeback and rescue a point for Newcastle.

Recent findings suggest that “It was not how to approach a Premier League game,”  Newcastle manager Alan Pardew stated after the game. “You have to pressure people, tackle people, turn people around [and] force them into errors. You can force any team into errors.

“Part of my [half-time] message was that you cannot sulk in front of 52,000 people at St James’ Park. They were letting themselves down more than anyone else. They showed to their family and friends that they are better than that.”.
