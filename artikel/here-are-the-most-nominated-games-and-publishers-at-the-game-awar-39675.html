Sources indicate that the nominees for 2026's The Game Awards have been declared, but which games and publishers scored the most total nominations? The most-nominated game this year Grand Theft Auto 6, which picked up eight total nominations, including a nod for Game of the Year. The game being nominated at all surprised some people considering that it's not even out yet, but members of the voting jury had played it already. In terms of the most-nominated publishers, Nintendo led the way with a dozen, followed by Capcom with 11 and Annapurna and PlayStation with nine each. The Game Awards take place on Thursday, December 10. In addition to handing out awards, the show will include game announcements and new trailers. Specifically, Disney has teased some "major" announcements for the event.
