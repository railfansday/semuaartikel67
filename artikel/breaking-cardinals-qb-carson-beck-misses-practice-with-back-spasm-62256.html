📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Arizona Cardinals might be turning to their fourth quarterback of the season.

In a press conference, Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur mentioned Carson Beck is dealing with back spasms and did not practice Friday. As a result, the team is preparing for backup Josh Johnson to be the team's starting quarterback for Week 11.

"Not gonna shut the door on him [Beck], but we're gonna proceed as if Josh is gonna be starting on Sunday," LaFleur stated.

Beck, who is a rookie, was thrust into the role of starter after Gardner Minshew went on injured reserve with a torn UCL. The Cardinals' original starter, Jacoby Brissett, was traded to the Baltimore Ravens after being benched for Minshew mid-game the week before.

Beck was officially ruled as questionable in Friday's injury report.

Industry observers note that laFleur remarked in his press conference that the team will monitor how Beck responds to treatment and how he feels on Saturday before making any final decisions. The coach added that this is an matter that Beck has dealt with before, but wasn't able to provide a clear timeline for Beck's return.

In the meantime, Johnson, who was signed off the Cincinnati Bengals' practice squad after the Minshew injury, looks poised to be stepping up for Sunday's matchup against the Kansas City Chiefs.

In a recent development, the itinerant backup quarterback also has some history with LaFleur: Johnson and LaFleur's careers overlapped in 2021, when both were with the Fresh York Jets (LaFleur was an offensive coordinator at the time). LaFleur had high praise for Johnson, noting some of the reasons he's been able to keep his career going this long.

It has been revealed that "If it is Josh, I've called plays for him before, and had a really great experience calling plays for him," LaFleur stated. "He is incredible in the quarterback room, he's incredible for the starters. He's incredible for young quarterbacks, in terms of the way he sees the game, his energy, his positivity, how he keeps it real. He's mature. And he can still sling it. … If that's the route that ends up happening, let's go.".

Industry observers note that arizona is the 15th NFL franchise that Johnson has been a part of in his career, as the 40-year-old quarterback has bounced between half of the teams in the league. Across 16 seasons, he has only started 11 games in his NFL career.
