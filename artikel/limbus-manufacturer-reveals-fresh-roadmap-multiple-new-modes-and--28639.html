Sources indicate that every live service title needs a constant output of content to keep fans engaged, and some like to let players get a basic idea of how a season or a few months of content will look long before it begins. Typically called roadmaps, these announcement timelines let fans prepare for a series of content long before it begins, which is necessary for gacha and other currency-reliant games.

Coming off the hype of their fantastic 10th anniversary concert, Project Moon Director Kim Ji-hoon hosted a livestream to showcase the content coming to Limbus Company up until the first half of 2027. Fully revealed in a Twitter post soon after the stream ended, the roadmap teases fresh modes, multiple Walpurgisnacht banners, and all the upcoming story content coming to Limbus.

This article will avoid spoiling any content found in Canto X, aside from some character titles when discussing Meursault’s capstone Identity. Make sure you catch up!

Starting from the top, it seems like next week’s Wednesday upgrade will introduce the first new mode Limbus has gotten since the introduction of Reflectrials, titled Schemacraft. This new mode takes the form of the long-teased auto-battler, with players starting with small teams that slowly improve over time and combining them with EGOs to support the team mid-combat.

In a recent development, soon after, we’re getting the Bokgak rerun of the Spring Cultivation Intervallo, revising Xichun’s training for her Family Hierarch Evaluation with new voice acting, Identites, and a new announcer. This Bokgak should finally add Meursault’s Heishou slot, completing the team after 2 full seasons.

Right after revisiting Hongyuan, we’ll see the immediate consequences of Canto X’s climax, a dire omen for anyone who understands exactly what that means. We have no banner teases for that Intervallo, however, another Walpurgisnacht comes right after, this time themed after Lobotomy Corporation.

Industry observers note that both Walp banners teased during this window will be Lobotomy Corp-themed, a pivot from the back-to-back Ruina banners we got over the last two Walpurgisnachts. With the power level of the Sinners gradually increasing and their progress through the original facility extending, our next drop should be themed after the Extraction team headed by Binah, opening up some spicy ID options.

This upcoming mode takes the form of the long-teased auto-battler, with players starting with small teams that slowly improve over time.

Back to the direct timeline, smaller content via a Refraction Railway Line 3 Bokgak and a upcoming Reflectrial will give players revamped fights to chew on while waiting for one of the biggest drops of the season, Meursault’s Capstone Identity. Like other Capstone Identity drops, Meursault’s is themed around the worst possible ending of his story, placing him in the role of the villain.

Recent findings suggest that kim Ji-Hoon took the time to reveal that Meursault’s Capstone is based on the Chaplain, one of the final bosses of Canto X’s campaign and a major thematic parallel to Meursault himself. This ID will supposedly convert all Le Rouge allies into Le Noir, forcing them to align with Meursault’s vision and optimal team.

It has been revealed that immediately following Meursault’s Capstone, the second Intervallo of the season is seemingly themed after the Seven Association, a detective group we have several IDs for, but no direct story interaction. With their entire game plan focused on Rupture, the post-Canto season is looking up for Rupture players.

According to recent reports, after the Intervallo drops, we’ll have a few weeks of minor content, including a fresh Refraction Railway in Line 7, and another Reflectrial leading up to Outis’s Canto, The Unreturning. Not only is the rest of Season 8 set to give love to teams left out in the cold, but it could be offering the strongest units the game has seen yet!

Combine that with some seasonal events like a free Christmas EGO and some kind of teased New Year's event, and it’s all coming up on Project Moon.
