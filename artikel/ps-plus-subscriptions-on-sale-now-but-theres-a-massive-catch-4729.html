Original Story: Sony has revealed its PS Plus membership at all three tiers will be going on sale from tomorrow, 16th November, for two weeks.

However, if you've been paying attention to how Sony handles this promotion in the last few years, then you will already know what's coming: the discounted prices only apply to new sign-ups; current members won't have access to them.

"Players in participating countries who join PlayStation Plus during PlayStation’s Black Friday promotion can save substantial on select memberships," Sony clarifies in an updated PlayStation Blog post. As it states, you have to "join" PS Plus during the Black Friday offer period to qualify, which lasts from 16th November to 30th November.

The only promotion open to current PS Plus members is a subscription upgrade to PS Plus Premium. Anyone at the PS Plus Essential or PS Plus Extra tiers can upgrade to PS Plus Premium at a discounted rate.

This is a tactic Sony has utilised for years now — it has not let you extend a PS Plus membership at the same tier for a very long time. Instead, the promotion once again only applies to PS5, PS4 owners joining PS Plus.

In a recent development, in fact, this year, Sony hasn't even revealed what the discount will be yet. Normally, the accompanying graphic tells you the sort of offers coming (usually 33% off), but the image above has nothing on it. We'll have to wait until tomorrow to find out what the PS Plus deal for Black Friday 2026 is.
