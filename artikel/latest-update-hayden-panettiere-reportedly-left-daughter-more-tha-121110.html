📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hayden Panettiere reportedly left an estate worth more than $6 million to her 11-year-old daughter after her shocking and untimely death.

According to court documents obtained by Page Six on Tuesday, November 24, the late star made Kaya — whom Panettiere shared with former fiancé Wladimir Klitschko — as the “sole heir” to her estate, which allegedly included “$5,208,519 in personal property” and “$1,023,600 in real property,” totalling $6,232,119.

Us has reached out to a representative for Panettiere, who died at age 36 on August 16, for comment.

The outlet also reported that the Heroes alum died without a will and that Klitschko, 50, who had previously been appointed as the temporary legal guardian of the estate, “filed a petition to have Melissa Moskal Bencivenga appointed as the administrator of Panettiere’s estate” within the latest court documents.

Bencivenga is a tax and financial advisor based in Los Angeles, per her LinkedIn profile.

Industry observers note that last month, People reported that Kaya appeared alongside her father during a court hearing regarding Panettiere’s estate. The October 8 appearance was filmed remotely, with the child briefly seen on camera to confirm her identity to the judge when asked whether she was with Klitschko.

Industry observers note that the boxing world champion was granted temporary guardianship of his ex’s estate during the hearing after his attorney, Diane Y. Park, informed the judge that he requested additional powers to “request” and “authorize” law enforcement to investigate and aid in “recovery of property believed to be misappropriated” by a non-family member as Panettiere’s estate continues to settle.

The ruling came after the former athlete previously filed court documents, demanding answers from law enforcement about why personal items belonging to Panettiere were located in a pawn shop.

It has been revealed that panettiere’s shock death was ruled as an accident by the coroner’s office in Greenville, South Carolina, one month after she died. The cause of death was determined by the office as “toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, alprazolam, methocarbamol and quetiapine.”.

A rep for Panettiere told Us at the time that the investigation was “still ongoing” due to the case being “complex.”.
