A year after being announced, the Odyssey G6 is finally being released.

According to recent reports, after being teased in December 2025 and getting an arguably unnecessary pre-release spec bump in August, the Samsung Odyssey G6 G60H rolls out next month for $899.99. The 27-inch gaming monitor will have an absurd 1,100Hz refresh rate, narrowly edging out LG’s UltraGear 25G590B, which tops out at 1,000Hz.

Is there any value in these ultra-fast refresh rates? That’s up for debate. But whether or not you can actually see the difference, it does give the company bragging rights. So there is that, I guess.

The G60H will only be able ot hit 1,100Hz in 1080p. Bumping the resolution up to 1440p will drop the refresh rate to 600Hz, which is still absurdly fast. The Dual Mode IPS panel is also the first to feature Samsung’s new Odyssey Clear Motion tech, which reduces motion blur during games. Eventually, the company aims to bring Clear Motion to more gaming monitors. Black Equalizer also makes it easier to see details in dark scenes.

Beyond that, the G60H also supports HDR10+, Nvidia G-Sync, and AMD FreeSync Premium.
