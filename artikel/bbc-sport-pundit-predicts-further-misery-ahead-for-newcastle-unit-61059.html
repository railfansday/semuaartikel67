Interesting comments from this BBC Sport pundit on Newcastle United.

Chris Sutton speaking as United prepare to play their fourteenth match of the 2026-27 season.

Newcastle United up against Arsenal in the Premier League.

Matthias Jaissle hoping to see his team get a positive performance and result against league leaders Arsenal.

The BBC Sport pundit declaring ahead of Saturday: “I am covering this game and I think it is a huge one for Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle. The Magpies have picked up as many points – 12 – in their first 10 games under Jaissle as they did when Eddie Howe was in charge last season, but I still don’t think their fans are overly convinced by him.”.

According to recent reports, chris Sutton adding: “Some of their unease is because Newcastle don’t seem to have a defined style of play, and can be a bit kamikaze going forward. Because of that, I just think Arsenal will pick them off, and win comfortably.”.

The BBC Sport pundit predicting further misery for Newcastle United, forecasting a 2-0 away victory for Arsenal.

Sutton stating: “The Gunners have conceded only five league goals so far and have made an incredible start to their title defence with nine wins out of 10. The worry for their rivals is that it feels to me Mikel Arteta’s side have not even got going yet, but I still don’t see anyone being close to them.”.

When you consider Arsenal have won nine of ten Premier League matches AND three of the five goals they’ve conceded were in one match, their 3-0 defeat at Brighton, it sums up how huge a challenge this is.

It is over seven hours of Premier League minutes since Arsenal have conceded a goal.

Arsenal are ruthlessly efficient and whilst not great to watch, they tend to get the job done.

According to recent reports, they haven’t scored more than two goals in a Premier League match since the opening day of the season but with a negative set up and easily the best defence, they don’t need to score many goals. Apart from that Brighton blip, Arsenal have kept seven clean sheets and conceded one goal against both Chelsea and Leeds.

Newcastle United conceded 14 goals in their opening seven Premier League matches but only leaked two in their last three, that coinciding with the return of Dan Burn to full fitness and back in the starting eleven.

My feeling is that for Matthias Jaissle and his players to get any points on Saturday it will either be a goalless draw, or Newcastle United needing to score first, because once you get behind to this Arsenal side they are very difficult to play against and can punish you if you leave gaps when pushing forward. 🎙️ THE MAG PODCAST Ep 18: Newcastle United 2020-21 featuring Steve Bruce, Joe Willock and the Road to the takeover Listen to the latest episode of The Mag Pod!
