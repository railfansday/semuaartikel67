Recent findings suggest that it's official, and there's no escaping it — Black Friday is here. With it comes huge discounts on everything from Ninja appliances to Dyson vacuums to MacBooks and iPhones — and soundbars.

Recent findings suggest that upgrading your home cinema setup has never been cheaper (well, except maybe in like 2001 or something). Amazon has reduced the price of all its own-brand soundbars, including the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar for just $74, the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus for $139, the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar with Subwoofer for $229, and the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar with Subwoofer and Rears for $349.

I've reviewed both the $74 2.0-channel soundbar and the $139 3.1-channel soundbar with built-in subwoofer, and both really surprised me, especially the $74 model. This is by far the cheapest soundbar I've ever awarded a 4.5-star rating — I would snap this one up for an instant TV speaker upgrade.

The Amazon Fire TV Soundbar is the cheapest option on Amazon's home theater lineup, but it's the most surprising. With powerful bass and a fantastic dialogue mode, I didn't miss a thing during testing.

Recent findings suggest that the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus is extremely well-priced at its MSRP of $249, but $139 is almost incomprehensibly cheap. While the subwoofer can be a little boomy, if you mostly watch Netflix and YouTube, this won't be an glitch at all.

Industry observers note that the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus with Subwoofer is an excellent level up. If you value clear and controlled bass, this would be the option for you. This model has clearer dialogue than the built-in subwoofer version.

According to recent reports, for those who value true surround sound, the Amazon Fire TV Soundbar Plus with Subwoofer and Rears is your best bet. This is the only model capable of true Dolby Atmos, so movie fanatics need look no further.
