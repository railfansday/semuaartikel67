Sources indicate that there’s so much to love the holiday season: catching up with family and friends, sipping homemade hot chocolate, and making snow angels, to name a few. The not-quite-as-merry part? Finding the perfect gift for your impossible-to-please sister, your mom who already has everything, and that best friend who insists you don’t have to get her anything. You know who's much easier to shop for? Yourself. After all, nobody knows your taste better than you do—and you can skip the gift receipt altogether.

It has been revealed that this year, we're putting ourselves at the top of our shopping lists, starting with a few luxury makeup upgrades from Ulta Beauty. Think beautifully packaged palettes, lipsticks that make even your laziest outfit look put-together, and a couple of indulgences you might normally talk yourself out of buying. Consider these early presents to yourself—with plenty of opportunities to put them to use throughout the holiday season and long after the decorations come down. Some makeup is so pretty you almost feel guilty using it. (Almost.) The Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit Unlocked Palette is one of those treats, with six complexion powders—five of them brand-upcoming shades—that give skin the kind of flattering, softly diffused finish you'd normally need a ring light to pull off. Inside, you'll find a mix of finishing powders, blushes, a bronzer, and a highlighter, so you can add warmth, definition, and just the right amount of sheen. Created in collaboration with French jewelry designer Lauren Rubinski, the packaging features playful motifs meant to attract good fortune, including a four-leaf clover. Not that you’ll need luck; with this palette, your lit-from-within look is one thing you won't have to leave to chance.

Industry observers note that dare to shine! For a luminous finish with a little artistry, the about-face Ballet Beam Highlighting Pearls feature nine shimmering pearls you can swirl together for a multidimensional glow or use individually to spotlight your favorite features. Think lit-from-within radiance with a playful, editorial edge. Its light-reflecting pearls blend seamlessly into the skin for a radiant, multidimensional finish that’s perfect for making a statement with your holiday looks.

We'll spare you the "shine bright like a diamond" references (though Rihanna sure made it tempting with this one). Suffice it to say that Fenty Beauty's limited-edition Midnight Diamonds Diamond Bomb All-Over Diamond Veil makes a convincing case for going all out with the sparkle. The jelly-like powder melts into skin, leaving behind a prismatic, diamond-dusted effect that shifts as the light hits it, rather than looking like a flat layer of glitter. You can use it on both face and body, so go ahead and sweep a little across your collarbones and shoulders after slipping into that party dress. If there's ever a time of year to be extra, it's the holidays.

Make tarte’s Tartelette Tubing Mascara your go-to for holiday-ready lashes. Its tubing formula delivers length and definition while helping resist smudges and flakes, so your lashes stay party-ready from the first toast to the last dance. It also comes off easily with micellar water, so you can skip the scrubbing and get straight into bed on those nights when you’re just too tired to deal with your makeup. (No judgment.).

We've officially entered leggings-and-oversized-sweater season, a.k.a. our daily uniform until further notice. But somehow, one swipe of lipstick makes the whole look feel a lot more intentional. MAC's MACximal Silky Matte Lipstick is a fresh take on the brand's longtime matte formula, delivering more intense pigment with a creamier texture that won't leave lips feeling like sandpaper. Credit the combination of coconut oil, organic shea butter, and cocoa butter for making the finish feel so comfortable. The formula has serious staying power, too, so you won't have to worry about your color disappearing between your morning coffee run and after-work drinks.

It has been revealed that between holiday shopping and family gatherings, nobody has time for a 30-minute makeup routine. Treat yourself to the beloved Rare Beauty Soft Pinch Liquid Blush for a rose-gold lip gloss or a little shine. Plus, it’s versatile and easy to apply, saving you time when the holiday rush takes you from one event to the next.

Name a more iconic lip color than Clinique's Black Honey. (We'll wait.) For the somehow-still-uninitiated, don't let the almost-black berry color in the tube intimidate you: It goes on sheer, leaving behind a glossy, just-bitten tint that lets your natural lip color peek through. The formula falls somewhere between a lipstick and a balm, with castor seed oil and carnauba wax that help it glide on without any tugging or dragging.

DIBS Beauty’s Desert Island Duo pairs bronzer and blush in one double-ended stick, making it easy to add warmth and a pop of color in just a few swipes. The creamy formula blends easily into the skin, whether you’re going for a softly sculpted look or a more defined flush for holiday prepares. Best of all, it's practically impossible to mess up, even when you're applying it without a mirror.
