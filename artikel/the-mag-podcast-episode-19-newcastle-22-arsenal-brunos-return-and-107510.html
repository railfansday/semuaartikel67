The Mag Podcast Episode 19: Newcastle 2–2 Arsenal, Bruno’s return and the games we missed.

Published: 23/11/2026 | Duration: 59 mins | Hosts: Jamie Smith, Simon Cahill & David Hutchinson.

In Episode 19 of The Mag Pod, we find Jamie, Simon, and Hutch reflecting on Newcastle United matches they have individually missed. There is also of course a full post-mortem on the 2-2 Arsenal draw, including a certain Bruno Guimaraes. Plus a looking forward to chips and ice cream on Brighton seafront….

Listen to the latest episode of The Mag Pod! Join Jamie, Simon, and Hutch for unfiltered football debate.

Jamie Smith missed Newcastle United’s 2–2 draw with Arsenal for a day out with old friends, so he’s asked Simon Cahill and Hutch to tell him what he missed at St James’ Park.

Industry observers note that that turns out to include a second half Hutch reckons was Newcastle’s best of the season and a frosty reception for former captain Bruno Guimarães. It also leaves Jamie with a question for the other two. Which match do you still wish you’d been at?

First, Simon and Hutch try to account for the difference between the side that barely laid a glove on Arsenal before the break and the one that went 2–1 up. Simon picks out what changed down the right when Tino Livramento and Anthony Elanga got together. There’s also a second look at Arsenal’s equaliser and a difference of opinion over what VAR contributed to the afternoon.

Recent findings suggest that the lads can understand wanting to give an Arsenal player a hard time while he’s on the ball. Where it gets more complicated is what happens after the final whistle.

Does the manner of Bruno’s departure change where he stands in Newcastle’s history?

And is it fair to put him in the same bracket as Alexander Isak?

Jamie’s article for The Mag, Missing Home Matches, brings back memories of a 4–3 comeback against Norwich just days after his son was born.

Simon remembers catching Moussa Sissoko’s afternoon against Chelsea on a tiny telly in a Northumberland pub.

Hutch explains why he watched Newcastle beat Sunderland 3–0 alongside his daughter despite having a ticket for the away end.

Finally, Jamie and Hutch look ahead to their first visit to the Amex, with chips and ice cream on Brighton seafront promised whatever the weather.
