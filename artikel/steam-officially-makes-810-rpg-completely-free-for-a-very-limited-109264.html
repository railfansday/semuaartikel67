📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Steam can really sneak up on you and surprise you. Or, it can absolutely pelt you over the head with games until you're knocked out. It really depends on the week with the platform, which for many people is now synonymous with "PC." But the best things in life are often free, and Steam can really deliver on that front if it wants to.

Recent findings suggest that thanks to a latest promotion, there's a free game on Steam that you can grab now, and the reviews are overall positive. Just know that there's a ticking clock on this one, but it's a pretty interesting clock.

Live now on Steam from developer JackyGames is Auto Chess Souls, a "casual strategy" game that aims to mix the classic roguelike progression system with auto-chess. So far, it's been met with "positive" reviews on Steam, and a lot of that is due to the strategic elements included that elevate it over some other similar games. You can combine tools to form more effective items, as well as chess pieces.

Here's a breakdown from the studio:.

Sources indicate that "Auto Chess Souls is a casual strategy adventure game that combines auto-chess and roguelike elements. Through consecutive choices, your team grows increasingly stronger. Explore the unknown tower, collect powerful equipment, create unique powerful combinations. Embark on this exciting adventure now!".

Pretty much any PC can run it, and many reviews cite it as fun and simple to learn.

According to recent reports, that mentioned, note that this is only a limited-time offer, and the promotional period is a bit nebulous. Here's the full rundown:.

"The game is now live and presently free. After finishing the demo, I ran out of money and had to get a job, so I haven’tbeen able to enhancement the game’s content yet. Development is currently about 20% complete, and there’s still so much fun stuff I haven’t had time to add. I don’t feel right charging for it in its current state. Thank you all for supporting my game. If you download it during this free period, it’s yours to keep forever. I’ll finish the game as soon as I can. Once it’s complete, it will become a paid game again, but anyone who downloaded it during the free period won’t need to pay. Thank you!".

At this point, Steam developers often need to get creative to sell their game in an increasingly busy marketplace. A lot of games are straight-up provided for free, with no strings attached, in the effort to get the developer's name out there. Others are looking to stand out and get on the "best of free games" list.

With Auto Chess Souls, the studio is taking a decidedly different approach on Steam, telling people to pick up the game now and that, when it's done, it'll be sold to newcomers for a fee. This is pretty much the best of all worlds, as JackyGames will certainly want to make some cash from this to support future development, but you can get in on the ground floor now for free.
