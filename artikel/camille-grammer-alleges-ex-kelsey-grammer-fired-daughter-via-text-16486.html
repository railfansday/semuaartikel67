Camille Grammer is once again putting her ex-husband, Kelsey Grammer, on blast.

During a Monday, November 16, appearance on the inaugural episode of the “Everybody Sees the Villain. Nobody Sees Who Needed Her to Be One” podcast, Camille, 58, alleged that the Frasier star once fired their daughter, Mason, 25, from a job via text message.

The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills alum said that Mason, a film major from Emerson College, recently filmed a television project (believed to be a pilot for a Los Angeles-based spinoff of Bravo’s Next Gen NYC), but that her last name didn’t make breaking into Hollywood any easier.

“She needs something to open the door,” Camille said, alleging that Kelsey, 71, “doesn’t do anything for either one of us.”.

In a recent development, “He fired her through text… it was ridiculous,” she claimed. “She went from working for a year to over text, ‘Oh, I’m shutting down the office. Your employment’s up.’ That’s it.”.

Camille stated that Kelsey’s alleged text message firing was “like how he dumped me” and that he “said our marriage was over through text … what a coward.”.

Industry observers note that as for Mason, the Bravolebrity said that prior to filming her own project, “She worked with her dad. She did work in production for a while, she did script coverage and other things, but she’s like, ‘Where is the work?’”.

In a recent development, camille added, “She has to fight against the nepo baby [stigma]. Listen — nepo babies, significant, yes,” but conceded that while “you can get in the door as a nepo,” it isn’t as easy as it seems.

Us has reached out to Kelsey’s representative for comment.

Last month, Camille hit out at her ex, claiming that he never reached out to Mason on her 25th birthday.

“A daughter should never have to wonder whether her father remembers her birthday. Three days later, and still not a call or a text. 💔,” she wrote via Instagram at the time. “You can be a beloved actor to the world, but being a father is a role that requires showing up when the cameras are off. Fame, accolades, and public admiration mean very little if you neglect the people who should matter most. Some wounds come not from what is said, but from the silence.”.

In a recent development, “A mother never stops hurting when her child is hurt. 25 is a milestone birthday. Sometimes the silence is deafening, and the disappointment speaks volumes,” Camille wrote.

Recent findings suggest that she further claimed, “Mason told me she kept checking her phone all weekend, hoping her father had called, even from an unfamiliar number or that his message had somehow ended up in her spam folder. She even checked there, searching for any sign that he had reached out.”.

Kelsey did not publicly respond to Camille’s allegations at the time.

Camille and Kelsey were married from 1997 to 2011 and share daughter Mason and son Jude, 22. Kelsey has six other children, including the actresses Spencer, 43, whom he shares with first wife Doreen Alderman, and Greer, 34, whom he shares with ex-girlfriend Barrie Buckner. He and his current wife, Kayte Walsh, have four more children together.
