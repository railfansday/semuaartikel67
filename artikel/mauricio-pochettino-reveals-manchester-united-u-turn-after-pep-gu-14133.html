Mauricio Pochettino says he has a point to prove at Manchester United, after revealing his conversation with Pep Guardiola prior to sealing a return to club management.

The Argentine was appointed new Man United head coach last week, succeeding Michael Carrick who was sacked as a result of a disappointing start to the season with the side languishing in the Premier League relegation zone.

This will be the 54-year-old’s first job in club management since leaving Chelsea at the end of the 2023-24 season and fourth overall in England’s top-flight following spells at Southampton and Tottenham.

Despite the announcement, Pochettino does not officially start work at Man Utd until Wednesday as he has been granted permission to finish up with the United States Men’s National Team [USMNT] before reporting to Carrington.

6-0 up from the first-leg of the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final against Haiti, it is expected to be a routine exercise for USA in the early hours of Wednesday morning [UK time] for what is Pochettino’s farewell game.

Speaking with the Telegraph in preparation of saying goodbye to international management, Pochettino stated he admitted to Guardiola in September that he missed the day-to-day element of club management.

“I am ready [to return],” he said. “This is what I miss a little bit. I miss to be, every day, with something here.

“Guardiola asked me when I was at the game, he mentioned, ‘Oh, you don’t want to come back to the Premier League?’ I said, ‘No, I’m happy here.’.

“[He said] ‘Yeah, but you don’t miss, every day, to…’ I say, ‘A little bit, yes, because you think now today, and then we have December camp against Jamaica for the MLS [players in the pool].

“But after, you need to wait [until] March. What do you do? You need to watch games, you need to travel – administrative things. That is nice, it’s good.

Industry observers note that “But every day, to be involved: press conference, training, everything … this type of possibility, like with United, you say yes. I want that. I want to prove myself again.”.
