Kate Middleton is shedding light on one of her darkest chapters.

The Princess of Wales shared a vulnerable glimpse into her cancer journey in the trailer for her upcoming documentary Heal, detailing how her 2024 diagnosis fundamentally altered her life forever.

According to recent reports, “Cancer does change you,” Kate—who publicly shared her diagnosis in March 2024—remarked in the trailer released Nov. 21. “It changes your body. It changes you physically. But there's a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you.”.

It has been revealed that the documentary, available on NBC Nov. 30, follows Kate as she undertakes the National Three Peaks Challenge, climbing the highest mountains in Scotland, England and Wales within 24 hours in support of The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, which funds cancer treatment and research.

In one clip, Kate informed her brother James Middleton that she “didn’t ring the bell” when she learned she was cancer free in September 2024, to which he replied, “Maybe this is your ringing of the bell.” (Kate announced that she was in remission in January 2025.).

Throughout the physically demanding trek, which Kate completed this past June, the 44-year-old not only reflected on her own experience with the disease, but she also connected with others who are navigating their own health battles.

Sources indicate that “You know, healing is a journey,” she remarked. “How to reconnect with the things you once knew, and how to find a sense of hope and joy alongside uncertainty.”.

Recent findings suggest that “It's clear we're not meant to carry life's challenges alone,” she added. “Things can renew and grow. Often, it is through pain that we realize there's a greater beauty that lies beyond that.”.

And one of those greater beauties is experiencing special moments with her and Prince William’s kids Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 8, who she hugged tightly in the trailer after completing the challenge.

She also gave her husband a long embrace following the emotional milestone. And while having her loved ones by her side made the moment that much more special, Kate previously shared how the achievement was made even sweeter considering the hurdles she's jumped in her health battle.

“Lots of people have asked me why I’m doing this challenge and partly it’s personal,” Kate remarked in an Instagram video posted June 28. “I’m so grateful to be here, to be strong enough to walk these hills. But more importantly it’s to give something back.”.
