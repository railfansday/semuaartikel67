Jennifer Lawrence Goes Full Dior With a Plunging Tuxedo Top, Feathery Skirt, and Swan Clutch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, it has been a busy week for Jennifer Lawrence and the house of Dior. After attending Monday night’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere in a fur-trimmed floral-embroidered coatdress from Jonathan Anderson’s Pre-Fall 2026 collection for the house, the actor spent her Tuesday evening at the WWD Beauty Inc Awards—in another ensemble from the French label.

Sources indicate that for last night’s event, Lawrence swapped the long train of her Katniss Everdeen-approved premiere gown for something mini. She hit the red carpet in a plunging tuxedo top from the Dior Spring/Summer 2027 show, designed with a softly sculpted hourglass shape and long silk panels in place of lapels. On the runway, the ribbons were tied in a knot, creating organic gathers in the fabric, but here, the Academy Award-winning star left them down, pressed straight against the bodice.

Recent findings suggest that that remarked, Lawrence did take one styling cue from the runway—pairing the top with its ivory counterpart. Her miniskirt was trimmed with butter-yellow feathers, which peeked out from underneath the tuxedo jacket. For shoes, she traded the sandals seen during Paris Fashion Week for a pair of black peep-toe pumps, and for accessories, she carried one of the collection’s most talked-about pieces: the swan minaudière.

Industry observers note that the bird-shaped clutch featured a shiny white body with metallic silver “feathers” and a gold beak, reminiscent of the viral pigeon bag that Anderson designed for his own label, JW Anderson. It was a clear example of the creative director putting his own stamp on the heritage house.

To top it all off, Lawrence slicked her hair back, highlighting her antique diamond earrings from Fred Leighton, which dated back to the 1850s.
