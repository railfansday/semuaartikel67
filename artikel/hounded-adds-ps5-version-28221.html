Third-person story-driven adventure game Hounded will launch for PlayStation 5 alongside its previously announced PC (Steam) version in 2027, publisher Silver Lining Studios and developer IMPRNT STUDIOS announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Silver Lining Studios:.

Hounded is a third-person, story-driven adventure game set in a stunning Flintlock Fantasy world. You play as Duke, a determined young Border Collie on a perilous quest across the quaint countryside of Anglodia. You must find your missing master, Fletcher, the fugitive leader of the Rebellion against the usurper King Jaymes.

It has been revealed that however, a cunning antagonist, The Hunter, has been sent to kill your master and is tracking your every move. Armed with your canine instincts and the strange magic of The Tide, you must run, leap, and sniff your way through a fractured land to spark hope in the darkness.
