On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 16, 2026, H World Group (HTHT +8.53%) confirmed expanded asset-light operations and raised full-year revenue guidance. For long-term shareholders, the shift toward managed franchise hotels drives steady margin expansion while shielding capital. Three things management said on the call stand out.

It has been revealed that the company is shifting away from directly leased and owned (L&O) hotels to focus on manachise and franchise (M&F) properties, which require less capital and generate higher margins. As franchise revenue expands, profit growth outpaces overall revenue.

Lowering procurement costs for franchisees keeps hotel owners loyal and attracts new property partners across China. Management looks to keep procurement costs low while gradually opening its supply network to third parties.

In a recent development, the business is securing flagship properties in major metropolitan districts while expanding into smaller cities. Closing lower-quality locations over the next two years will elevate network performance across all brands.

Sources indicate that travel demand across Chinese hubs is tracking above prior expectations as business events and exhibitions recover. Executive leadership shows clear satisfaction with fourth-quarter momentum. Operational efficiency tools and membership scale underpin store productivity.

Strategic spending on technology and brand marketing supports long-term scale across domestic and international markets.
