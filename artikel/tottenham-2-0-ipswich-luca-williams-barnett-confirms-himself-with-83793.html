Luca Williams-Barnett lived up to the hype with a sparking display on his full debut for Tottenham to inspire a 2-0 win over Ipswich.

Roberto De Zerbi turned to the 18-year-old after two bright cameos and the highly-rated Spurs attacker delivered with aplomb, scoring on his first Premier League start after 15 minutes before a mazy run set up Dominic Solanke at the start of the second half.

After Williams-Barnett made his senior debut at the age of 16 last season, the clamour for the teenager to play has only increased during a difficult last 12 months and with injuries to Mathys Tel and Mykhailo Mudryk, the England Under-19 international grasped his chance to show why he has been hailed as a “special talent” by De Zerbi.

It was not a completely positive afternoon for De Zerbi with Savio forced off late on with a problem on his comeback after a seven-week lay-off.

However, this victory made it three home wins in a row for Tottenham in the Premier League for the first time in two years in further evidence a corner may have been turned in N17.
