While most game studios are carefully avoiding the Grand Theft Auto 6 behemoth, Devolver Digital has relentlessly and theatrically pursued it. In May 2025 it pledged to launch a game on May 26, head-to-head with GTA 6—and when GTA 6 was delayed, Devolver delayed too.

And now, with GTA 6 slowly rolling through its official launch on November 19, it's finally (almost) here: Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, "a spectacular remaster of the original remake of the unreleased action-platformer starring the most famous videogame mascot of all time," developed by Gunbrella/Gato Roboto studio Doinksoft.

So, first things first: As the word "unreleased" amidst that slurry of words from the Steam page suggests, this is not actually a remake of a popular game from the days of yore, although it certainly has the vibe, and actually looks pretty decent as these things go. And yes, Volvy is indeed Devolver's mascot—here's some massively overpriced merchandise, if you're into that, or just want evidence.

Sources indicate that "Adventure across four revamped worlds full of naughty baddies and menacing boss fights while collecting delicious onions to keep Volvy at extreme levels of awesome," Devolver said of the game. "There's some sort of story too. Basically some warlocks released some slime and Volvy is stopping them. Also, they kidnapped his friends.".

In a recent development, what really brings us here, though, is that dogged pursuit of GTA 6. It's obviously coattails marketing, but as PC Gamer's Shaun Prescott said in 2025, it's a pretty good bit—and Devolver is really sticking with it.

Yes indeed, the Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed launch trailer is a virtual shot-for-shot remake of the GTA 6 launch trailer, music and all.
