GTA 6: How to Find Tom Cruise from Collateral

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that grand Theft Auto 6 has a wide variety of encounters that can crop up across its central locales. While most are tied to a specific area or mission, some of the game’s most memorable are those that can occur anywhere within a given zone, adding tons of random madness to Leonida while free-roaming.

In a recent development, many of these quests are direct references to other pieces of crime fiction, from subtle hints to straight-up placing the protagonists in the situation from the films. One such quest sees either Jason or Lucia held at gunpoint by a stand-in for Tom Cruise’s character from Collateral. If you want to trigger this event and get out alive, make sure to read on!

This encounter is a standout, not appearing anywhere on the map and not being accessible until midway into Chapter 3. Once you’ve gained easy access to Vice City and start heading toward Vice Beach, sitting in any car alone for an extended period gives a suited stranger a chance to enter the back seat suddenly.

Industry observers note that he’ll proceed to stick up the protagonist, forcing them to start driving. From here on, failure to comply with his orders will lead to him shooting the player, not enough to kill outright, but enough to push you over the edge if you’re already wounded.

Your next task is to run a red light, any red light you can find. Thankfully, the agent is rather patient as long as he can tell you’re looking for a light or waiting for one to change. Once it’s clear you can follow instructions, he’ll mark an area near the park in Vice Beach for you to drive to, a trek depending on where you started the mission.

Make sure you don’t slow down too much or try to turn on the radio. Your passenger wants a nice, quiet ride to his destination. Once you arrive, things change fast, giving you a split-second decision.

Once you arrive at the location, the agent will explain that this entire charade was to put the protagonist’s face and car on a police suspect database for running that red light. Now that you’ve made it to his destination, we’re about to be in real hot water, as he guns down a nearby guard protecting a small drug lab.

Sources indicate that he insists you leave, but if you’re feeling spiteful or profit-focused, you can easily follow him inside. Without a gun to your head, he’s as easy to take down as any other enemy, plus he’ll have already cleared the guards inside and outside the lab.

Recent findings suggest that make sure to keep your car running by double-tapping your exit button, and promptly pocket everything he stood to gain before heading out. He wasn’t kidding, the cops are already on their way, so whether you’re willing to snag some for yourself or just want to hightail it, make sure you leave fast.

I’d fully recommend hitting the lab, as it’s easily a 10k payout for very little effort. There are also a few handy guns to pocket if you need the hardware. The Jeep parked outside sells for a pretty penny as well, giving you an excuse to swing by Randy and continue his questline.
