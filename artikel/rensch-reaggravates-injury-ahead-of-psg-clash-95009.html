After their 5-1 victory at Parma, Roma are starting to prepare for the trip to Paris to face Luis Enrique’s side.

The former Giallorossi coach will be without the long-term absentee Kvaratskhelia and the newly injured Doué.

These absences don’t make things any easier for the Roman side, as the French side are on a 12-match winning streak with 40 goals, including 20 in the Champions League against Barcelona, ​​Manchester City, Villareal, and Slovan Bratislava.

Gasp must monitor the condition of Leonardo Balerdi, who is struggling with an ankle problem, and Rensch, who is at serious risk of injury again.

Devyne Rensch has suffered another injury. As Il Romanista reports, the Dutch full-back has suffered another injury, this time to his left flexor. The 2003-born player is not in top form and risks missing the match against PSG.

It has been revealed that this season, the Dutch full-back has played 337 minutes, picking up two yellow cards, and has not scored or provided any assists. Rensch has already missed four games due to injury.
