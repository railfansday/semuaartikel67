Wayne Rooney has revealed he contemplated suicide while playing at Manchester United.

The ex-England striker, who remains United’s record goalscorer, has spoken publicly of his struggles with mental health and alcohol consumption in the past.

But he has now revealed in a candid interview that at his ‘lowest point’ he spent days at home drinking and considered taking his own life.

Speaking on the ‘Life At The Midpoint with Gabby Logan’ podcast, Rooney explained: “I can look back and say I was selfish, but I knew I was going through something which I was really struggling to deal with.”.

“Then I didn’t want (his eldest son) Kai seeing me like that, so that’s why I’d hide away as well.

According to recent reports, “I got to a place where you’re contemplating suicide. You just didn’t want to be around anyone. I didn’t want to be see people in the streets. I didn’t want to go into shops.

“I was always comfortable on a football pitch, but everything else, (I) really struggled with it.”.

Kai Rooney, the eldest of Wayne’s four children, is now 17 years old and impressing in United’s academy ranks.

Sources indicate that asked to remember the lowest point of his life, Rooney continued: "When I was drinking, I would lock myself in my house for two days and just drink. This is probably in my best part of my career playing wise.".

He added that he “struggled to deal with it all” and acknowledged he “needed help to get through it” after a conversation with his wife Coleen.
