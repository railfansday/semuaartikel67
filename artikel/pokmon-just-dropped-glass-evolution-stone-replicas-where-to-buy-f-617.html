Industry observers note that if you have ever wanted to own an actual Evolution Stone from the Pokémon games, Pokémon Center just made that possible. Although they can be scarce in some regions, these peculiar stones are essential items for Trainers looking to evolve certain species of Pokémon whenever they choose. Now, the official storefront has released faceted glass replicas of three classic stones from its Pokémon Trainer Essentials collection: the Fire Stone, Water Stone, and Thunder Stone.

These Pokémon Center Original collectibles are crafted from solid, faceted glass crystal built to sit on a shelf or complete a Pokémon Trainer cosplay. Each stone features its elemental icon suspended inside the glass — a flame inside the Fire Stone, a barrage of bubbles inside the Water Stone, and a bolt of lightning inside the Thunder Stone.

In a recent development, inside the box, you get the replica figure nested inside a protective display case with a clear, transparent lid to keep dust off the glass. Each package also includes a certificate of authenticity and an adhesive plaque featuring the stone's name and its exact description from the Pokémon video games, matching the text printed on the display box:.

Industry observers note that fire Stone — "A peculiar stone that can make certain species of Pokémon evolve. It has a fiery orange heart.".

In a recent development, water Stone — "A peculiar stone that can make certain species of Pokémon evolve. It is the clear blue of a deep pool.".

Thunder Stone — "A peculiar stone that can make certain species of Pokémon evolve. It has a distinct thunderbolt pattern.".

Because the plaque has an adhesive back, you can stick it onto the display case, a shelf, or your wall to build your own exhibit. Whether you pick up a single favorite or collect all three, Pokémon Center notes that, because they are made of real glass, these are adult display pieces rather than toys and should be kept out of children's reach.

All three Pokémon Trainer Essentials Evolution Stone replicas are available to order now on the Pokémon Center website.
