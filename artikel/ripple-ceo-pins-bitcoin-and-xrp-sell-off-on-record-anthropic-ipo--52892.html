Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said on Nov. 19 that crypto markets could rally after Anthropic's IPO, as they did after SpaceX went public. He spoke to CNBC on the day Anthropic's shares started trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

According to Garlinghouse, investors sold Bitcoin and XRP before the listing to free up cash for shares of the Claude developer.

Recent findings suggest that "We talked earlier about the most recent sell-off in the crypto markets — we also saw a sell-off very similar when SpaceX went public. What happens is you've got risk-on assets and people are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included — Bitcoin, XRP — and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Anthropic IPO. What we saw after the SpaceX IPO was a rally in the crypto markets. I think you'll see that happen here as well," the Ripple CEO said.

Sources indicate that anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the initial range of $70 to $78. The company and its seven co-founders sold 980 million shares, up from the 825 million shares they planned to sell earlier. The offering raised $81.3 billion, and a $26 billion private placement brought the total to $107.3 billion.

It has been revealed that at a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, Anthropic became the world's 12th-biggest organization, just behind Tesla. SpaceX raised $75 billion in its market debut in June.

According to recent reports, dan Ives of Yorkville Ives said that "Anthropic's IPO is a major near-term win for the tech sector." University of Florida finance professor Jay Ritter compared AI with the early internet. "Thirty years ago, the internet was the next big thing. It has changed our lives. Today, artificial intelligence is the next big thing. It will change our lives, too," Ritter said.

It has been revealed that asked about the listing itself, Garlinghouse said that Anthropic's shares would trade well. "I think it's gonna be extremely well received, and we obviously saw a price above the range they had. I think it's gonna trade very well," he said.

In the same interview, Garlinghouse called blockchain-based transactions "a wave of emerging technology." "I think 10 years from now you'll see blockchain implemented across our financial infrastructure," he said.

According to recent reports, after the SpaceX IPO in June, Bitcoin took almost three weeks to turn higher. It started to rise after the U.S. Treasury confirmed that it would buy back government bonds.
