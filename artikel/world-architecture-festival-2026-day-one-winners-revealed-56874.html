The first award winners of the 2026 World Architecture Festival (WAF) have been announced, following Day One of the world's largest international live-judged architectural event, being held at Broward County Convention Center, Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

In total, the three-day festival will see over 400 live pitches from the 2026 finalists in front of over 130 international judges. Today has seen shortlisted projects from around the world compete for 23 award categories within Completed Buildings, Future Projects and Interiors. Award winners include Studio Lotus, Foster + Partners, Warren and Mahoney, and Haptic Architects.
