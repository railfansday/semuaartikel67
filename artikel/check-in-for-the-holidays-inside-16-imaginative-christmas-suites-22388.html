Recent findings suggest that hotel lobbies may feature towering holiday trees and elaborate gingerbread displays, but some of the season’s most imaginative designs are happening behind closed doors. Creative teams are giving guest rooms full seasonal makeovers—blending thoughtful décor with interactive touches. From nostalgic throwbacks to festive retreats, these design-forward suites make the stay itself the destination. Ahead, explore some of the most imaginative holiday suites to check into this season.

In a recent development, the Watercolor Wonderland Suite creates a whimsical, storybook setting with pastel ornaments, pink-wrapped gifts, and a miniature holiday village. Photography courtesy of The Mills House.

Sources indicate that inspired by the Lowcountry’s pastel façades and historic architecture, the Watercolor Wonderland Suite at The Mills House in Charleston, South Carolina, was created in collaboration with local artist Rebecca Meixner. Original watercolor illustrations of Charleston landmarks, including Rainbow Row and the city’s steepled churches, appear throughout the suite, while pastel ornaments bring a soft seasonal touch to the Christmas tree.

In a recent development, in the Watercolor Wonderland Suite, pink holiday wreaths adorn the bathroom’s geometric tile walls, adding a playful contrast to the black-and-white palette. Photography courtesy of The Mills House.

The Watercolor Wonderland Suite is available to book from Dec.1, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at millshouse.com.

It has been revealed that the Evergreen Suite brings a woodland-inspired holiday look to the bedroom, with pinecone garlands, greenery, and deep navy accents. Photography courtesy of The Whitney Hotel.

In a recent development, the Evergreen Suite is available to book from Nov. 14, 2026, through Jan. 16, 2027, at whitneyhotelboston.com.

It has been revealed that the Festive Suite pairs deep green pillows, a playful “fa la la” cushion, and a plush throw with warm wood and gold finishes. Photography courtesy of Hamilton Princess & Beach Club.

The Festive Suite at Hamilton Princess & Beach Club in Bermuda pairs Scandinavian-inspired Christmas décor with waterfront views of Pitt’s Bay and Hamilton Harbour. The suite features festive green-and-gold furnishings alongside commissioned artwork by Bermudian artists Ewan David Eason and Guy Gee.

The Festive Suite features an oversized evergreen wreath, white reindeer figurines, candles, and green-and-white knit stockings. Photography courtesy of Hamilton Princess & Beach Club.

Industry observers note that the Festive Suite is available to book from Dec. 1, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at thehamiltonprincess.com.

It has been revealed that the Elf on the Shelf Caribbean Vacation Suite carries its Scout Elf theme into the bathroom, with custom character wallpaper, red-and-white towels, and festive holiday accents. Photography courtesy of Beaches.

The Elf on the Shelf Caribbean Vacation Suite at Beaches Turks & Caicos and Beaches Negril features Scout Elves trading the North Pole for a Caribbean getaway. Custom wallpaper depicts Scout Elves on vacation among palm trees, seashells, and coconuts, while themed bedding, festive collectibles, and a Christmas tree carry the holiday concept throughout.

In a recent development, A miniature Christmas tree, Scout Elf dolls, and festive pillows bring a tropical twist to the holidays in the Elf on the Shelf Caribbean Vacation Suite. Photography courtesy of Beaches.

The Elf on the Shelf Caribbean Vacation Suite is available to book from Nov. 25, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at beaches.com.

In a recent development, the adjoining children’s room at Amway Grand Plaza embraces the Grinch theme with character-printed bedding, bright green pillows, and playful holiday touches. Photography courtesy of Amway Grand Plaza.

The historic Amway Grand Plaza in downtown Grand Rapids, Michigan, offers families two connecting rooms with their own holiday themes: the Tower Luxury Suite takes a traditional approach, with decorated Christmas trees, seasonal artwork, and Santa-inspired details, while the adjoining Two Queen River View room features a colorful, Grinch-themed escape tailored for younger guests.

A red Santa mailbox, complete with a holiday wish list and letters addressed to the North Pole, gives young guests a festive way to share their Christmas wishes. Photography courtesy of Amway Grand Plaza.

Sources indicate that the Santa Suite is available to book from Nov. 20 through Dec. 31, 2026, at amwaygrand.com.

It has been revealed that the Holiday Rewind Suite recreates a nostalgic Christmas kitchen with retro floral wallpaper, red bows, vintage-inspired table settings, and festive nutcrackers. Photography courtesy of Club Wyndham.

At Club Wyndham Midtown 45 in New York City, the Holiday Rewind Suite turns back the clock to Christmas 1999. The living room features a Christmas tree, retro-patterned wallpaper, and vintage holiday décor, plus a library of classic VHS tapes. A rotary-style landline, vintage holiday cards, and era-defining toys reinforce the late-’90s nostalgia. The separate bedroom leans into a full-on Y2K teen dream, complete with glow-in-the-dark ceiling stars and a CD player loaded with late-’90s hits.

A decorated Christmas tree, red sofa, nostalgic games, and holiday memorabilia bring a throwback feel to the Holiday Rewind Suite’s living and dining areas. Photography courtesy of Club Wyndham.

The suite includes a kitchen stocked with hot chocolate and Rice Krispies Treats kits, as well as take-home keepsakes like a disposable camera and virtual pet keychain.

Recent findings suggest that the Holiday Rewind Suite is available to book from Nov. 6, 2026, through Jan. 6, 2027, at clubwyndham.wyndhamdestinations.com.

Evergreen garland draped across the mirrored headboard and teal holiday pillows bring seasonal touches to the Christmas Chapter suite, complementing its deep blue walls and eclectic artwork. Photography courtesy of The Untitled Nashville Hotel.

The Untitled Nashville Hotel takes a personal, music-inspired approach to the season with The Christmas Chapter, a seasonal suite decked out in teal, white, and gold, with natural greenery and festive accents. A decorated Holiday Tradition Tree serves as the room’s focal point, inviting guests to write a favorite family tradition on an ornament-shaped card and hang it on the tree. An in-suite record player stocked with holiday vinyl sets a nostalgic soundtrack, while seasonal books and personalized postcards, which the front desk will mail to loved ones, add thoughtful touches.

Industry observers note that the Christmas Chapter is available to book from Nov. 20, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at theuntitlednashvillehotel.com.

Industry observers note that deep green sofas, a decorated Christmas tree, and garlands draped along the ceiling create a festive setting in the Christmas Suite, along with nutcrackers and Santa figures. Photography courtesy of Westgate Las Vegas.

Christmas lasts all year long at Westgate Las Vegas, where the 1,500-square-foot Christmas Suite is styled like a holiday lodge in the desert. Three decorated trees, seasonal greenery, and an electric fireplace set the tone, while a festive in-suite bar and Elf on the Shelf details in the bathrooms add a playful note.

In a recent development, A decorated Christmas tree, plaid throw, and glowing “Ho ho ho!” neon sign bring holiday cheer to the Christmas Suite’s bedroom. Photography courtesy of Westgate Las Vegas.

Unlike seasonal holiday suites, this one stays up all year, so guests can proposal a winter getaway or celebrate Christmas in July.

The Christmas Suite is available to book year-round at westgateresorts.com.

Recent findings suggest that vintage Christmas books, brass reindeer, and colorful ornaments create a nostalgic holiday display in the Santa Suite. Photography courtesy of Manchester Grand Hyatt San Diego.

Sources indicate that the Santa Suite at Manchester Grand Hyatt San Diego brings a traditional North Pole aesthetic to the California coast, with rich crimson and gold setting the tone. A towering Christmas tree dressed in classic ornaments and velvet ribbon anchors the room, complemented by pine garland draped across the headboard and traditional tartan accent pillows. Under the tree, vintage sleds and toy trains add a nostalgic touch, while shelves display brass candlesticks and Santa figurines. Younger guests can post letters in an in-suite North Pole mailbox or browse a collection of holiday picture books.

It has been revealed that the Santa Suite is available to book from Nov. 23, 2026, through Jan. 5, 2027, at hyattexperiences.com.

A towering Christmas tree, wrapped gifts, red stockings, and woodland-inspired accents give the Après Ski suite a festive retreat. Photography courtesy of Lotte Newly introduced York Palace.

Holiday decorating takes center stage at the Lotte New York Palace, where four suites have been transformed in partnership with Balsam Hill, each showcasing a distinct seasonal personality.

According to recent reports, all four suites are available to book from Nov. 23, 2026, through Jan. 10, 2027, at lottenypalace.com.

According to recent reports, A decorated Christmas tree, glowing fireplace, and evergreen garland set a cozy holiday scene in a Christmas Cottage Suite, with red stockings and classic seasonal accents completing the look. Photography courtesy of Patrick O’Brien for Barnsley Resort.

Industry observers note that barnsley Resort draws inspiration from its North Georgia woodland setting for its Christmas Cottage Suites, where understated seasonal décor complements the natural surroundings. Long-needle pine garlands, cedar boughs, and native pinecones create a freshly gathered feel, while stockings hang from the mantel over a wood-burning fireplace, and velvet-wrapped ornaments add rich texture to the Christmas tree. Garland-lined porches and exterior lighting extend the warmth outside.

Sources indicate that the Christmas Cottage Suites are available to book from Nov. 18, 2026, through Jan. 4, 2027, at barnsleyresort.com.

A rendering of the upcoming Holiday Suite shows an elaborate canopy of greenery, oversized ornaments, and cascading ribbons transforming the bed into a holiday focal point, while a life-size nutcracker adds a traditional Christmas accent. Rendering courtesy of The Belle Noel.

The Santa Suite at Hilton Anatole in Dallas brings the magic of Christmas to a spacious family retreat. Designed to give parents privacy while keeping everyone under one festive roof, the suite pairs a king-size primary bedroom with a dedicated bunk-bed area for children. Traditional crimson and evergreen accents blend with warm neutrals and cozy textiles, while an illuminated Christmas tree serves as the focal point. The stay also includes four complimentary tickets to the hotel’s outdoor ice-skating rink.

The Santa Suite is available to book from Nov. 27, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at hilton.com.

A snowman-embroidered pillow and oversized paper snowflakes add playful seasonal details to the Holiday Suite bedroom. Photography courtesy of Canopy by Hilton Sioux Falls Downtown.

The Holiday Suite at Canopy by Hilton Sioux Falls Downtown offers a modern, seasonal retreat that blends natural evergreens, gold accents, and rich reds with the hotel’s contemporary architecture. Highlights include a 7.5-foot decorated Christmas tree beside floor-to-ceiling windows, snowflake-inspired pendant lighting, and a garland-trimmed private balcony with seasonal mugs. Guests receive a welcome delivery of local treats, including peanut brittle, gingersnaps, and snickerdoodles.

Industry observers note that the Holiday Suite is available to book from Nov. 20, 2026, through Jan. 3, 2027, at hilton.com.

A festive train filled with candy canes and ornaments, a whimsical reindeer, and a decorated Christmas tree bring classic Christmas charm to the Santa Suite’s living room. Photography courtesy of Fairmont Grand Del Mar.

Sources indicate that A decorated Christmas tree, vintage-style ornaments, plaid stockings, and a classic Radio Flyer wagon filled with presents bring a nostalgic holiday feel to the Rockin’ Retro Christmas Suite. Photography courtesy of The Fifty Sonesta.

Christmas gets a midcentury makeover at The Fifty Sonesta Hotel New York, where the Rockin’ Retro Christmas Suite takes inspiration from holiday traditions of the 1950s and ’60s. The space is a nostalgic time capsule, with vintage-inspired wallpaper, a chenille bedspread, and a living room Christmas tree decorated with Shiny Brite ornaments, glass bead garland, and silver tinsel. A retro turntable, faux fireplace, bedroom toys, and original Sears Wish Books evoke the Christmases of decades past. In the bedroom, a silver aluminum Christmas tree glows beneath a rotating color wheel that washes its branches in red, blue, green, and amber. Guests receive a welcome bag of nostalgic treats, including a miniature New York City snow globe, hot cocoa, holiday candy, a mini tinsel tree with its own rotating color wheel, and a custom ornament.

The Rockin’ Retro Christmas Suite is available to book from Nov. 22, 2026, through Jan. 6, 2027, at nyc.sonesta.com.
