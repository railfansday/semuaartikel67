Industry News: All 11 GTA 6 meditation spots and where to find them

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that there are 11 meditation spots in GTA 6 you can find and use to gain a boost to your max Focus. Confusingly, however, there's a trophy or achievement for meditating a total of 18 times in the game. That's because there are two tutorial spots that only Jason can access, and which one you get depends on what you do before the end of Chapter 1 in GTA 6. After that there are eight spots that both Jason and Lucia can both do separately, and then a final spot that unlocks at the end that they can only do together. Not the clearest system in terms of making 11 add up to 18 but if you just follow the map below you'll find them all. They're often highlighted or pinpointed as you play and explore, but if you want that sweet Focus boost sooner rather than later it's worth chasing, as well as looking into the other GTA 6 max stat increasing missions you can find.

Industry observers note that generally, meditation spots will be marked out with rocks and candles, with the spot itself a small circular mat placed on a rug. They should pop up when you get close enough, if they haven't been noted on the map already via texts and conversations.

Sources indicate that just remember that of the 11 up there, you'll only get one of the first two for Jason, then the next eight need to be used by both Jason and Lucia separately, to activate the final one that can then only be done together. For that reason alone you're best always visiting these spots as a couple and getting it ticked off for both of them to save you coming back later. © GamesRadar+.
