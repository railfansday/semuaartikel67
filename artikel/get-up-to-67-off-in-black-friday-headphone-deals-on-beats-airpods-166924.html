The massive day (Nov 27) has arrived and the Black Friday deals are rolling in on headphones from all of the major brands, including Apple, Bose, Sony and more.

The Skullcandy Crusher Evo literally have a bass slider in them to make your head wobble! So if it's bass you're after, and you fancy $120 off, this one's for you.

Great fit, finish, sound and noise cancelling — the JBL Live 780NC are some impressive mid-range cans. And this Black Friday deal makes them even better.

According to recent reports, the venerable Beats Studio Pro. but this time... $200 off MSRP! Solid ANC? Check. A cool, stylish look? Check. Good sound quality? This isn't a deal to pass up.

The Nothing Headphone (1) certainly look the part, and can help you live out your Cyberpunk dreams while offering solid customizable sound and a great control scheme.

It has been revealed that these may have been succeeded by the Momentum 5, but that doesn't make them any less impressive, especially given this massive $120 saving. The Sennheiser Momentum 4 are seriously good value at this price.

Sources indicate that these Sonos cans earned an impressive 4.5 stars in our Sonos Ace review. The sound and noise cancelling were on point, as was the battery life, the design and the integration with other Sonos gear. And now they're $120 off!

Recent findings suggest that the Beats 360 are the latest in the Beats range (seriously, they only launched like 2 months ago) and ideal for working out. They're water- and sweatproof, and they feature replaceable ear and head pads. Sony's XM6 are the latest flagships. They weigh very little and sound excellent — and now you can get them for $100 off. As you'll see in our Sony WH-1000XM6 review, they offer excellent noise cancellation, improved call quality over prior models, and an epic 30 hours of battery life. Flagship quality, and now mid-range price.
