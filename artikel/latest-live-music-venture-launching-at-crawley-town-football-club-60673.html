Redz Live will bring live music to the club's Redz Bar, Broadfield Stadium every Friday night starting from Friday, February 5.

The project is being organised by Phil Atkinson, who has been responsible for the music programme at the renowned Half Moon Putney since 2017 where he has established a regular programme of emerging artists and live music.

Recent findings suggest that phil also organises weekly live music events at Sutton United FC. He said: “I'm delighted to bring that experience to Crawley.

“The aim is to establish Redz Live as a regular destination for live entertainment in the town, bringing some of the UK's best tribute bands and live music acts to the area.”.

Phil has already got a fantastic programme of reveals confirmed, including tributes to David Bowie, Led Zeppelin, Bon Jovi, Fleetwood Mac, The Killers and Queen.

Industry observers note that “It's an exciting opportunity to bring something new to Crawley, make further use of the stadium outside matchdays and create a regular entertainment offering for the local community. The reveals will bring in revenue to the club which is becoming increasingly notable.”.

Industry observers note that and it was the community feel that attracted Phil to start the venture with the football club. “I was chatting to my colleague at The Half Moon Putney about football and he mentioned that his Mum, Rhona Kaye, has been running the bars at Crawley Town FC for years,” he said.

"We went down together to watch an evening match and I loved the atmosphere and the space is perfect for putting gigs on. It's a real community feel at the club and when I mentioned it could be a good thing to work together on some gigs. They are really enthusiastic and I'm excited to be building something special there.”.

And he added that football clubs are great for these kinds of gigs and that the Redz Bar is perfect for gigs. “They are often at the heart of communities with a broad range of people connected to the club. The venue itself is a perfect shape for live music and the infrastructure is all in place for busy matchdays,” remarked Phil. “There are great transport links back into town, a large free car park and a great bar that is used to pouring pints as promptly as possible!”.

You can find out more about the project, see the full programme and buy tickets at www.redzlive.com.
