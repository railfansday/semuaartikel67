It has been revealed that after more than 10 years of waiting, Rockstar's GTA 6 is finally available now (at least in Fresh Zealand and Australia). The anticipation has been sky-high for both consumers and investors alike, and Rockstar's owner, Take-Two, is seeing a huge uplift in its share price today after the game's overwhelmingly positive reviews landed. The US stock market doesn't officially open until 9:30 AM ET, but ahead of that, Take-Two shares are up close to 7% in pre-market trading. If this holds, it would boost Take-Two's total market cap by billions, and it could go even higher. Why? No one really knows, because the stock market is fickle. Take-Two's stock price got hammered earlier this year when Google confirmed new AI software to make games, presumably spooking investors. The stock has not recovered since. The 52-week high for Take-Two is $265.65, and the 52-week low is $183.86. Take-Two closed on November 17 at $203.93, but is up to $216 in pre-market trading today. As stated, the market opens at 9:30 AM ET today, so check back for more. GameSpot's GTA 6 review scored the game a rare 10/10. GTA 6 has now unseated the Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2 to become the highest-rated game of 2026 on GameSpot sister site, Metacritic, and is tied with the original Ocarina of Time for the highest aggregate review score ever.
