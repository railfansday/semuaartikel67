Globant (GLOB +4.13%) was one of the rare stocks to rise amid the sharp decline of the wider market on Friday. It acquired that port-in-a-storm quality on its latest set of quarterly results, which were good enough to draw investors to its equity. The digital transformation specialist's shares ended the day more than 4% higher.

Sources indicate that globant's third-quarter revenue was $611.1 million, down 1% year over year. Non-IFRS (adjusted) net income also declined, falling by almost 9% to nearly $63.8 million, or $1.48 per share.

That earnings per share (EPS) figure met analyst expectations while revenue notched a slight beat. The pundit top-line consensus for that line item was $610.9 million.

According to recent reports, like the more progressive legacy software companies, Globant -- which specializes in developing custom software for enterprises automating aspects of their business -- has embraced artificial intelligence (AI) in its solutions. It said in its earnings release that its recently introduced AI Pods subscription service had doubled its annualized run rate.

Globant quoted co-founder and CEO Martin Migoya as saying that "We are seeing growing enterprise adoption as clients shift from hourly billing toward outcome- and consumption-based delivery.".

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

In a recent development, it's one thing to embrace AI as either a tool for internal software development or to tack on some of its functionalities to an existing software suite. It's quite another to lead with it as a product offering, like Globant is doing with AI Pods.

According to recent reports, the service is clearly resonating with the market, and I think it might soon become a driver of growth for this company given its relatively high margins. This is starting to look something like a sleeper stock among the many software titles on the market, and as such, it's well worth watching.
