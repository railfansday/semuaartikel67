A large cluster of leveraged long positions around the $860 price level has traders on guard for a possible liquidation event that may result in increased volatility for Zcash (ZEC).

A liquidation cluster is an area where many leveraged positions could be liquidated if the price of the asset moves against the traders. CoinGlass data shows a long liquidation cluster worth $1.4 million is near $860.

Zcash is currently trading at $877, and a move lower to $860 could put long-position holders under liquidation pressure.

According to recent reports, if ZEC approaches this level, forced liquidations could add to the selling pressure and possibly accelerate a move to the downside. Liquidations occur when traders who have borrowed or leveraged money are unable to maintain margin on their positions.

According to recent reports, but the presence of a liquidation cluster does not mean that the price will fall to $860. It identifies a potential area of market vulnerability rather than a confirmed price target.

Zcash fell below the $1,000 mark amid a weeklong selloff further compounded by concerns over leadership turmoil at Nvidia Corp., which weighed on the artificial intelligence trade.

According to recent reports, A broader weakness in the crypto market coincided with strength in the U.S. dollar. On Friday, the dollar index reached its highest level since April 2025, following hotter-than-expected inflation earlier this week that increased expectations of further Fed tightening, pushing Treasury yields and the dollar higher while pulling down both cryptocurrencies and gold.

Sources indicate that zcash fell to a low of $861 early in Saturday's session, after marking seven consecutive days of losses since November 7, 2026. If today ends in a loss, it will mark the eighth consecutive day of losses. Zcash lost momentum after dropping below the 50 DMA, a support level that had buoyed its price surge in August 2026, and a return above it is crucial for its price recovery.

Large buy walls are present, according to on-chain data, at $845, $811, and $805, marking key support levels for Zcash that might halt the bears' rampage.
