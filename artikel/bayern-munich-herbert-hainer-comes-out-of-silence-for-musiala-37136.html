Recent findings suggest that jamal Musiala’s sporting future raises questions at Bayern Munich. Victim of two illnesses in three days during the Bavarian club’s summer preparation, the 23-year-old German attacking midfielder suffers from an incurable illness whose symptoms can nevertheless be controlled by treatment, according to Uli Hoeness. On Sunday, Bayern’s honorary president expressed his concerns about the player’s ability to return to his best level. “ It is impossible to know if he will ever return to his level of performance », he declared on the microphone of German radio BR24. Asked about these comments, in particular about the expectations surrounding one of the highest paid players in the squad, Bayern president Herbert Hainer preferred to defend his predecessor’s freedom of speech rather than contradict him. “ We are, of course, in regular contact with Uli Hoeness, and he always thinks about FC Bayern, and sometimes worries about the club. He also expresses these concerns. That’s how we know him, and it’s part of what makes him who he is “, he explained.

According to recent reports, herbert Hainer also wanted to recall Bayern Munich’s attachment to its German international, without commenting directly on his ability to return to his past performances. “ We all really appreciate Jamal “, simply added the Bavarian leader. For his part, Musiala had already spoken on Instagram after his two illnesses to reassure supporters. The player explained that he was aware of his health problems and assured that they did not present any additional risk, citing “ brief and temporary absences, easily treatable “. After several months of uncertainty, the attacking midfielder returned to training, an vital step in his return to competition. Uli Hoeness also assured that Bayern would continue to financially support his player, while recognizing that questions remained about the rest of his career at the highest level.
