Engineers reveal the interlayer elastic limits of van der Waals crystals

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that A research team led by Professor Yang Lu from the Department of Mechanical Engineering in the Faculty of Engineering at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), in collaboration with Tsinghua University, has measured the interlayer elastic limits of graphite and hexagonal boron nitride, two representative van der Waals crystals, and revealed the physical mechanism that sets them. The work is published in the journal Nature Communications.
