Google announced on November 21, 2026 that it is providing the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT Kharagpur) with $5 million to support advanced AI research, expand AI education and build AI capacity across the university’s campus.

According to recent reports, the funding is designed for university researchers and students across three pillars. The research component covers applied AI work in Indic languages, materials science, agriculture and educational technology. The education component supports AI training and curriculum development for students, faculty and teachers.

Sources indicate that the capacity-building component will support IIT Kharagpur’s AI frameworks and tooling to strengthen the technological environment across campus. This structure links the research agenda to curriculum development and the technical resources available to the wider university community.

Google said the fund is intended to support applied research and technological solutions benefiting communities across India and beyond, framing deep collaboration and strong academic leadership as necessary for building AI that works for everyone.

Sundar Pichai, CEO of Google and Alphabet, connected the collaboration to his own experience at the institute: “Looking forward to seeing this collaboration shape the path for the next generation of students, just as IIT Kharagpur shaped my own.”.

Pichai also described India as increasingly at the forefront of innovation and remarked programs like this would help implement Prime Minister Modi’s vision of AI benefits reaching everyone in India.

The funding follows an existing research relationship. On July 10, 2025, Google confirmed that it was collaborating with IIT Kharagpur researchers to bring the Google Research-led Amplify Initiative to India. The project was designed to give developers and researchers free access to structured, locally grounded datasets reflecting India’s linguistic and cultural diversity. Its aim is to address gaps in large language models resulting from the limited representation of languages, dialects and cultural context in existing AI training data.

Sources indicate that google Research’s original Amplify explanation, published May 2, 2025, described an open platform built around community data collection, with datasets suitable for model fine-tuning and evaluation. It named IIT Kharagpur as its Indian partner, with a remit to gather and validate local material that an AI model could not generate by itself.

Under the collection process, sector experts enter information about vital local issues through privacy-preserving Android and web apps. Regional partners and country research leads then translate the queries and assess their local relevance, coherence and fluency, as well as the collection’s coverage. The approach places local experts and partner institutions at the center of both data creation and review.

In a September 15, 2026 update, Google reported that Amplify involved more than 1,600 local experts and 20 universities across four continents, including IIT Kharagpur, contributing 15,000 multimodal data points. Those figures cover the full program, not IIT Kharagpur’s individual contribution.

The new commitment is broader than the Amplify collaboration: it pairs research in several fields with curriculum development and campus AI tooling. For IIT Kharagpur, that connects the questions being investigated with the training and technical resources needed to pursue them.
