The Virtual CFO Platform, built by AI software developers and tested by CPA firms, puts every client's financial picture at a firm's fingertips.

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AI can now do the analysis that once took a team of accountants, and CPA firms are wondering how to adapt. Today, Netsurit and Southwestern Talent announced the Virtual CFO Platform, an AI-powered advisory engine that turns raw client financial data into CFO-grade insight, so firms can build advisory practices without hiring a team of advisory specialists.

The question facing CPA firms has changed. It is no longer whether AI can do accounting analysis, but what firms do now that it can. In October 2026, Mercor published a study that put a frontier AI model head-to-head with 12 licensed U.S. CPAs on well defined accounting tasks. The CPAs working alone met an average of 37% of the grading criteria. The AI, working alone, met nearly all of them, in a fraction of the time and at a fraction of the cost.

Recent findings suggest that the work firms have long billed by the hour is the work AI now does best, while the pipeline of latest CPAs has thinned. Unique CPA exam candidates fell by about a third between 2016 and 2022, according to the AICPA's 2025 Trends Report, and are only now beginning to recover.

Industry observers note that many firm leaders see business advisory as the way forward. A full-time CFO costs $350,000 or more a year, and even a fractional CFO runs thousands of dollars a month, which leaves most small businesses without CFO-level guidance. AI makes it possible to offer that guidance at a price those clients will pay, with the CPA supplying the judgment and the trust.

According to recent reports, the obstacle is delivery. Advisory is often priced like compliance, built from scratch for each client, and dependent on one or two partners. Building a bench of specialists to change that is difficult for any firm, and harder when talent is this scarce.

Industry observers note that the Virtual CFO Platform was built to close that gap. A dedicated team of AI software developers built it for CPA firms, and practicing CPA firms and managing partners tested and validated each module before release.

According to recent reports, connected to a client's accounting system, the platform first checks that the books are ready, then scores the client across six dimensions of financial health: liquidity, solvency, profitability, efficiency, growth and risk. Every client rolls into a single dashboard that shows who needs a call this week and the advisory opportunity for each. A cash flow module runs thousands of simulations on receivables and payables to recommend when to pay and when to collect. The analysis, a written narrative and a ranked risk register arrive ready for the conversation, all inside the firm's own Microsoft Azure tenant, so client data stays in the firm's environment.

Sources indicate that "AI can now do the month-end analysis that once took a team of accountants," noted Dr. Alex Heublein, Chief Innovation & AI Officer at Netsurit. "That opens the door to advisory work. Most firms aren't short on demand for it. They're short on the people and the playbook to deliver it. The Virtual CFO Platform does the heavy analysis before the meeting, so the CPA can own the conversation.".

For firms that want an added layer of assurance, the platform is also available with human review: every output is checked by a trained CPA analyst through Southwestern Talent before it reaches the client. The roles are defined. The AI analyzes, reviewers check the work, and the firm's CPAs bring the context, judgment and accountability that clients pay for.

It has been revealed that "CPA firms have told us the same thing for years. The demand for advisory is there, but the people to deliver it are hard to find," noted Pieter Burger, Senior Partner at Southwestern Talent. "The Virtual CFO Platform changes what a firm has to staff for. The analysis arrives ready, and where a firm wants it, a trained CPA analyst reviews the work before it goes to the client. That frees a firm's own people to focus on the relationship, which is where advisory is won.".

In a recent development, with the Virtual CFO Platform, a firm can see which clients need attention the moment the books close, put a dollar figure on the advisory opportunity across its client base, and walk into each client conversation with the analysis already done. CPA firm leaders and CAS practice teams can see the platform in action and book a demo at go.netsurit.com/virtual-cfo-platform.
