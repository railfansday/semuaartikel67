Roma fans invaded the Tardini, with the away match having opened only a few days earlier.

At the stadium, everything went smoothly, with the Roman supporters enjoying a team capable of scoring five goals against Parma, thus overtaking Inter in the standings.

It has been revealed that before kick-off, around 2:00 PM, however, an unpleasant incident occurred, which is now circled in red. “We saw these black vans near Arezzo, the maroon banners: we slaughtered them, destroyed them.”.

One of the voice messages sent through the most internal chats among the Roma fans, reported by Repubblica.

The Giallorossi and Torino supporters clashed along the Autostrada del Sole, near the Arno Est service area.

The National Observatory for Sporting Events is now waiting for the investigators from the DIGOS (Italian Special Operations Unit) of Turin, Parma, Florence, and Rome to complete the reconstruction of the facts before taking any necessary disciplinary action.

Roma and Turin fans could face another suspension of away matches. This scenario cannot be ruled out, given what happened on the highway. Recalling Saturday’s events, approximately 140 Roma ultras attacked 70 members of the “Torino Hooligan” group, but when traffic police arrived on the scene, no one was left.

Industry observers note that now investigators from the Rome Digos, along with other Italian police stations, are trying to identify the individuals involved. One initial finding is already certain: all those involved in the clashes avoided entering the Tardini or Maradona stadiums. The final report will determine the Observatory’s decisions. For Roma and Turin fans, there is a real risk of another suspension of away matches.
