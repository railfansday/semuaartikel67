Latest York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn was hired by the team thanks to his excellence as a defensive coordinator. But after the team struggled on defense during Glenn's first year as head coach, he decided to take over defensive play-calling duties in 2026.

That change lasted just nine games. Glenn told reporters Monday he gave up defensive play-calling ahead of the team's 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills in Week 10, per ESPN.

Sources indicate that glenn said the move was made to help the team win games. He added that while Jets defensive coordinator Brian Duker called plays Sunday, Glenn was "very involved" in the team's scheme.

Sources indicate that "I've remarked this and I will continue to do this: Anything I can do as a head coach to help us win games, I'm willing to do it," Glenn said. "I'm not afraid to do that. So I gave him the lead, but I was very involved in what we ran.".

According to recent reports, the change appeared positive initially, as the Jets (2-8) gave up just 10 points in the first half Sunday. But Buffalo's offense got rolling the second half, allowing the team to pull away.

According to recent reports, the Jets ultimately gave up 31 points in a loss to the Bills. Though, in Duker's defense, facing Josh Allen in your first game as a defensive play-caller is among the toughest asks in the NFL. The Bills rank first in the NFL with 31.9 points scored per game. In that sense, Duker's defense actually held the team under its season-long average … barely. The Jets still allowed 31 points, so the team shouldn't start popping the champagne just yet.

Glenn was noncommittal on whether Duker would continue to call defensive plays for the Jets, per ESPN. It was the first time ever in Duker's career that he was tasked with calling plays in a game. The 37-year-old has spent time as an assistant and position coach during his career, but this is his first season as an NFL defensive coordinator.

The decision puts Glenn in a tough spot with seven games left in the regular season. Since being hired, Glenn is just 5-22 with the Jets. The team has shown some improvement on both offense and defense in 2026, but not enough to drastically alter its outlook. Unless that improvement starts leading to wins down the stretch, Glenn could be out of a job at the end of the year.
