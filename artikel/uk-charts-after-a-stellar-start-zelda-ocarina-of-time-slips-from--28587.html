Our latest look at the UK charts is in, and after Zelda: Ocarina of Time got off to a wonderful start last week (the second biggest UK physical launch in franchise history, and all), we expected it to enjoy a little longer in the top spot. We were wrong.

Link's latest has slipped to second this time, overtaken by the unstoppable force of EA Sports FC 27, no less. This pushes Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 down to third, while Mario Kart World and Nintendo Switch Sports Resort round out the top five.

Here's the full top 40, with platform breakdowns for games available on Switch, Switch 2, and other consoles:.

Hot Wheels Monster Trucks: Stunt Mayhem.

Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream.

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild.

Harry Potter: Quidditch Champions.

Resident Evil 7: Biohazard Gold Edition.

Assassin's Creed: Black Flag Resynced.

The Witcher III: Wild Hunt GOTY Edition.

That's it for this week's physical UK charts. Did you pick up anything new last week? Let us know in the comments.
