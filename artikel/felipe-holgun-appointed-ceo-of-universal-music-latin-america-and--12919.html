Felipe Holguín Appointed CEO of Universal Music Latin America and Iberian Peninsula

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Felipe Holguín, an entrepreneur and executive whose experience has straddled music, media, technology and finance across countries and languages, has been named CEO of Universal Music Latin America & Iberian Peninsula, effective immediately, the firm unveiled Tuesday (Nov. 17). He will be based in Miami, reporting directly to Sir Lucian Grainge, chairman and CEO of UMG.

Holguín takes over as the head of UMG’s Latin operations, including Spain, Portugal and Brazil, after longtime CEO Jesús López confirmed Monday (Nov. 16) that he was stepping down from that post after nearly 30 years. López has taken on a new role as chairman emeritus of Global Talent Services (GTS).

Holguín doesn’t come directly from the music industry and hasn’t worked inside a label, but he has vast experience in building creative businesses and leading digital organizations.

“Latin music has led the way in opening global audiences to artists around the world, showing how music can cross borders while staying rooted in the cultures that give it life,” Grainge said in a press release. “Felipe combines deep respect for artistry and the Latin creative ecosystem with expertise across business, technology, and data. I’m excited to see him work with our teams to invest in the extraordinary and the unexpected and to create more global opportunities for artists.”.

Holguin added: “Artists are the ultimate change agents. Our role is to bring together the ideas, platforms, and partnerships that help them reach fans and move culture forward. Jesús and the talented people across Latin America and Iberia have helped shape Latin music’s global rise. I’m excited to work with UMG’s teams across the globe to build our manufacturer around artists and help their music travel even further.”.

Industry observers note that “I’m proud to welcome Felipe to UMG,” said López. “He’s an accomplished leader with a clear vision for the future, and I’m confident he’ll build on the extraordinary foundation our teams have created.”.

In a recent development, while not a former label exec, Holguin is known to those in the industry and has close ties in many sectors, particularly in the U.S. Latin market.

Born in Cali, Colombia, Holguín has an MBA from Harvard and his previous experience includes serving as CEO of Fusion Media Group inside Univision, where he applied a multi-language, multicultural approach. He was also executive vp of digital, news and music at Univision, overseeing all operations, including mergers and acquisitions, music streaming and the network’s televised award demonstrates. Holguín later worked with Exile Content Studio on the launch, growth and sale of its music business to HYBE and its eventual acquisition by Candle Media. Most recently, he worked with HYBE Latin America on strategy and operations.

Holguín will begin his tenure at Universal’s Miami offices this week and will be working with both local and global teams at UMG to “help identify and develop the next generation of talent and support artists at every stage of their careers,” according to a press statement.
