📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that sony Pictures has won the services to one of the most in-demand commercial directors working today, Shawn Levy. Levy and his 21 Laps banner have signed a major first look overall film deal with Sony Pictures, with Sony beating out multiple studios in what’s mentioned to be a competitive process.

Recent findings suggest that levy will develop and produce theatrical films through the 21 Laps Entertainment banner as part of the deal, which begins fall 2027 and extends across all Sony Pictures labels, so presumably that could include labels like Screen Gems or even Sony Animation. It’s a producing deal, not for directing, but we wouldn’t be surprised if that ends up being the case with one that his team brings in. In a recent development, this film deal comes after Levy and 21 Laps as recently as September signed a latest overall TV deal with Disney’s 20th Television, with him exiting his Netflix deal. That pact doesn’t begin until October 2027.

Levy is reuniting with Sony’s leadership Tom Rothman and Sanford Panitch after he previously directed the “Night at the Museum” movies, “Cheaper by the Dozen,” and “Date Night” for them while they were all at Fox.

“I go ALL the way back with Shawn. We made a pile of hits together before he went on to far greater successes, none of which surprised me in the slightest,” Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group Chairman and CEO Tom Rothman said in a statement. “His talent as a producer, director and head of the coolest creative firm around, is rivaled only by his unmatched enthusiasm. I feel lucky as hell to reunite, and excited for everyone at Sony to discover what I have long known — Shawn Levy is the real deal.”.

Sources indicate that “Tom, Sanford and I have a long history together, and some of the most formative and rewarding experiences of my career came during the years we worked together at Fox,” Levy remarked in a statement. “Tom saw the future of my career before I did, and he bet on me early and with a bighearted (and loud!) conviction that is one of his trademarks, and for which I’ll be forever grateful. 21 Laps was built on empowering fellow filmmakers and helping them tell stories imbued with their unique voices and visions. Along with my entire 21 Laps team, I’m thrilled to begin this next chapter with the team at Sony, and am excited about the wide-open breadth of opportunity across Sony’s many labels. I deeply value their commitment to theatrical filmmaking.”.

It has been revealed that while just about everyone else in Hollywood has taken a victory lap with the success of “Backrooms,” you may have forgotten that Levy and 21 Laps were producers on the A24 horror movie, and he of course directed and produced Netflix’s “Stranger Things,” which concluded at the end of last year. Up next for Levy is “Star Wars: Starfighter,” which is a “Star Wars” spinoff starring Ryan Gosling and which opens May 28, 2027. And he’s also teaming with Gosling and Disney yet again on Marvel’s “Ghost Rider,” which opens July 28, 2028. His last feature he directed was “Deadpool & Wolverine,” which made $1.3 billion worldwide.

On the 21 Laps side, the banner is producing a rom-com with Emma Stone and Chris Pine set at Universal called “The Catch;” Netflix’s drama series “The Sticks,” starring Michelle Monaghan; Amazon MGM Studios’ supernatural horror film “Seasons;” Netflix’s feature comedy “One Attempt Remaining,” starring Jennifer Garner and John Cena; and an untitled Netflix spy feature starring Kevin Hart and Henry Cavill.

Shawn Levy and 21 Laps Entertainment are represented by WME and Wendy Kirk at JSSK.
