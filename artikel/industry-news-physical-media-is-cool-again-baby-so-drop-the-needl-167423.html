In a recent development, I am an absolute sucker for physical media—my bookshelves are adorned with generations of console games, I have a CD stash tucked away in my Mum's attic, and I still refuse to buy a Kindle in favour of good ol' fashioned paper and ink. And as we increasingly veer into a more digital age, I've noticed a huge uptick in people yearning to bring their hobbies and collections back into a more tangible form.

Music, especially, has seen a huge resurgence in physical popularity. Y'all, my best friend is buying CDs and ripping them onto his PC. Like the good old days! And we've also seen an increasing number of videogame soundtracks make their way onto CD and vinyl, which is something that I am a huge fan of. I love music. I love videogames. I love things I can hold in my hands. It's a triple win!

Unfortunately, this Black Friday hasn't brought about a ton of good deals on videogame vinyl. But that doesn't mean there aren't little offers to be found here and there.
