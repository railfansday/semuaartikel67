Industry News: Toyota Tundra Driver Hits 130 MPH in Police Chase, Then Claims He Was Asleep in the Back Seat: Cops

Police officers have heard all sorts of excuses from speeders, from trying to get to work on time to running late for a haircut. Chances are, whatever excuse you try, the police have heard it before. That mentioned, a Toyota Tundra driver that police say recently lead them on a 130-mile-per-hour chase in Florida may have tried the most unique excuse ever: according to Gulf Coast News, the man claimed he was sleeping in the back seat the whole time.

WINK News reports that a Collier County Sheriff's Office Corporal saw a beige Toyota Tundra at about 2:20 a.m. running over a curb while attempting to get to a gas station in Naples, Florida. The Toyota reportedly refused to stop for police; a chase ensued, and authorities claim the truck hit speeds of 130 mph in 45-mile-per-hour zones—impressive velocity, for a Tundra. Deputies reportedly deployed stop sticks, successfully puncturing the Tundra's tires, but that still didn’t end the chase; authorities say it took a PIT maneuver to finally bring the truck to a halt.

According to recent reports, then, it seems, things took an odd turn. According to the arrest report, a passenger told deputies that the driver, identified as Oswaldo Perez-Ramirez, jumped from the driver's seat into the back seat—while the truck was spinning after the PIT maneuver. When officers questioned Perez-Ramirez, he reportedly insisted he hadn't been driving at all; in fact, he allegedly claimed he'd been sleeping in the back seat.

However, that story fell apart when police reviewed the dashcam video, and it demonstrated the driver wearing a white shirt; Perez-Ramirez was also wearing a white shirt when officers removed him from the truck, police say. He was subsequently arrested and faces charges of fleeing and eluding, fleeing and eluding at a high rate of speed, driving without a valid license, failure to stop, and DUI.
