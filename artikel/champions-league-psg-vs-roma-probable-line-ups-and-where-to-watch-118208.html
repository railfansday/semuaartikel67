Roma will be hoping that Donyell Malen carries his goalscoring form into the Champions League when the Giallorossi take on the reigning champions PSG at the Parc des Princes on Wednesday evening.

It has been revealed that the Dutchman netted four goals in a 5-1 victory over Parma in Serie A over the weekend, which brings him up to 16 goals from 15 appearances across all competitions at club level so far this season. He has scored in each of his last three Champions League outings as well, including a brace against Real Madrid back in October.

Despite their 12-match unbeaten streak since the start of the Serie A season, Roma have found themselves under a bit of pressure in the Champions League, with four points from their first four matches in the competition, leaving them in 23rd place at the midway stage of the fifth round of league stage fixtures.

Hosts PSG have won all of their first four matches in the competition, with victories over Slovan Bratislava, Manchester City, Barcelona and Villarreal so far.

Luis Enrique does have a handful of decisions to make in attack following the long-term injuries to Desire Doue and former Napoli star Khvicha Kvaratskhelia. Ousmane Dembele is expected to start at centre-forward; Maghnes Akliouche and Ferran Torres are most likely to support him, but it would not be a surprise to see either Mika Godts or Senny Mayulu in attack either.

Otherwise, there are no major surprises expected.

As for Roma, Gaian Piero Gasperini is expected to field Malen, Paulo Dybala and Matias Soule in attack, with support from Wesley and Nahuel Molina at wing-back.

Manu Kone is expected to start in midfield in what has been described as an ‘audition’ by certain reports in the Italian press, given PSG’s supposed transfer interest in the Frenchman.

Device Rensch is Roma’s only major absence through injury.

Kick-off this evening is due at 20.00 GMT.

PSG (4-3-3): Safonov; Hakimi, Marquinhos, Paxton, N.Mendes; Neves, Vitinha, F.Ruiz; Akliouche, Dembele, T orres.

Roma (3-4-2-1): Svilar; Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso; Molina, Kone, Cristante, Wesley; Dybala, Soule; Malen.

In a recent development, tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 4, TNT Sports 10 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
