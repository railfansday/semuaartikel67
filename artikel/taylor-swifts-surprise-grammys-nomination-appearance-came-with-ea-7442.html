Taylor Swift’s Surprise Grammys Nomination Appearance Came With Easter Eggs

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Taylor Swift made Grammys history this morning, joining Kendrick Lamar as the only other artist to have five consecutive albums nominated for Album of the Year. But the singer also made a surprise appearance during the nomination announcement broadcast. In a video, she introduced the two biggest categories, Album of the Year and Song of the Year. She kept her look simple, wearing a black sweater. But fans homed in on her accessories, which included a large horseshoe necklace. Her second necklace continued the cowgirl theme with its horse charm.

Swift has worn the horseshoe necklace multiple times before, including at the Toy Story 5 premiere in June. The horseshoe seems to be an Easter egg for her Toy Story 5 song, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which is about the movie’s cowgirl character Jessie. The track was nominated for Song of the Year and Best Song Written for Visual Media this morning.

The second necklace, featuring a horse’s head in a curved, horseshoe-shaped frame, is another nod to Jessie, who rides toy horse Bullseye in the movie.

Initially, some fans thought the second necklace had a snake charm. The singer has drawn attention for wearing snake jewelry before. In March 2025, fans noticed one during her virtual appearance at the iHeartRadio Music Awards. Snakes have long been associated with Swift’s Reputation era, and Swifties have been waiting for years for her to release the rerecorded Taylor’s Version of that album.

When Swift bought back her first six albums’ masters in May 2025, she gave fans an update on that project in a public letter. “I know, I know. What about Rep TV?” she wrote. “Full transparency: I haven’t even rerecorded a quarter of it. The Reputation album was so specific to that time in my life, and I kept hitting a stopping point when I tried to remake it. All that defiance, that longing to be understood while feeling purposely misunderstood, that desperate hope, that shame-born snarl and mischief. To be perfectly honest, it’s the one album in those first six that I thought couldn’t be improved upon by redoing it. Not the music, or photos, or videos. So I kept putting it off. There will be a time (if you’re into the idea) for the unreleased Vault tracks from that album to hatch.”.

In her video today, Swift congratulated her fellow artists and playfully reflected on her own history with the two categories she presented. She noted, “Good morning, America, it’s Taylor. I’m very honored that the Grammys have asked me to be here to help reveal the last two categories for the Grammy nominations. And I first wanted to start off by saying congratulations to anyone who’s nominated for a Grammy this year in any category because what this means is that your peers—your fellow musicians, producers, engineers, artists, writers—they love what you made. And that’s the coolest feeling in the world. I hope you have a really wonderful day. The last two categories to be declared, one of them I’ve had a lot of luck in [Album of the Year, which she has won four times], and the other one I’m zero for eight. Here are the nominees for Album and Song of the Year.”.
