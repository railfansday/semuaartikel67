📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Although Shiba Inu has continued to struggle amid the persisting market volatility, showing substantial daily losses, the leading meme token has just reached a major milestone in its holder count.

The latest on-chain data from Etherscan highlights that Shiba Inu has seen a rapid surge in adoption, with its number of on-chain holders surpassing 1.7 million for the first time.

While this has been a long-anticipated milestone in the SHIB community, the data has drawn attention across the market as it shows that SHIB now has about 1,700,247 holders as of Tuesday, November 17.

Reaching a 1.7 million holder count at a time like this, when the market is facing extreme volatility, marks a major milestone for the SHIB ecosystem as it signals its growing appeal despite unfavorable market conditions.

Nonetheless, it is notable to note that the number of holders alone does not necessarily indicate an increase in unique individual investors, as existing investors could be opening upcoming accounts.

However, each additional wallet holding SHIB tends to bolster its growing on-chain presence, positioning the asset for potential demand that could eventually bolster its price movement.

Although increases in holder count do not automatically trigger a price rally for the concerned asset, market participants expect that the new holder milestone could further drive demand for SHIB and potentially drive a relief rally for the asset following the extreme volatility facing the market.

Shiba Inu’s 1.7 million holder milestone arrived when SHIB was trading at about $0.00000382, following a 0.78% decline over the last day.

With its price trading far below local support levels, its user activity has flashed a brief bullish signal that is expected to help the asset stabilize its price movement.
