📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that yesterday, Timothée Chalamet and Kylie Jenner both shared glimpses of their evening at the Governors Awards, though they avoided sharing photos of each other. Chalamet posed alone on the red carpet, and Jenner revealed her vintage Roberto Cavalli dress on Instagram. But the couple met up inside, and attendees posted footage of their affectionate but low-key date night.

A source told People that they were in good spirits: “They were sweet with each other, laughing and enjoying the night together.”.

According to recent reports, it’s not unusual for Jenner to join Chalamet inside industry events. At this year’s Oscars, Critics Choice Awards, and Golden Globes, she skipped the red carpet but sat with him for the ceremonies.

The pair has been together for more than three years. During Chalamet’s 2026 Critics Choice Awards acceptance speech for Best Actor, he gave his girlfriend a rare shout-out. “And lastly, I’ll just say thank you to my partner of three years,” he said. “Thank you for our foundation. I love you. I couldn’t do this without you. Thank you from the bottom of my heart.”.

In August, a source told People that they are taking their time with their milestones. “Kylie and Timmy are very much in love,” the insider said. “They don’t have plans to rush an engagement. They love how their relationship is.”.

Two months earlier, a source discussed why the couple works so well. On June 24, the insider told People that Chalamet and Jenner “both are in a great place, make each other laugh, and friends and family love them together. Kylie is so relaxed with him, he puts her at ease and she makes him feel so secure. Friends can see them in it for the long haul, but they aren’t rushing anything.”.
