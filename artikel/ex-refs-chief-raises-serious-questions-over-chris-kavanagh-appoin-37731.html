Chris Kavanagh is being stretched far too thin after being appointed to the game clash between Newcastle United and Arsenal.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that there is very little thought to Premier League referee appointments.

Kavanagh will be in charge of the game at St James' Park on Saturday, 21 November, a game which could already have major impacts on the title race.

Then, he must travel to Merseyside ahead of Liverpool's game against Manchester United on Sunday, 22 November.

While he will only be the fourth official at Anfield, with Sam Barrott in the middle, alarm bells are being set off in one expert's head.

Should Barrott pick up an injury, Kavanagh would be the one to replace him, and that would be quite a lot to handle for the official.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett has warned Howard Webb over how he is right now deciding referee appointments for matches.

"There appears to be very little thought applied to the appointment of match officials to Premier League games," he remarked.

"Referee Chris Kavanagh will be the man in the middle for the Newcastle United vs Arsenal on Saturday, 21 November, with a 17.30 kick off.

"He will be late leaving St James' Park, and then has a 185-mile, four-hour journey to arrive at his hotel on the outskirts of Liverpool. Yes, he will be 4th official for the game between Liverpool vs Man United with a 16:30 kick off.

"But if Samuel Barrott sustains an injury, then Kavanagh will take over from Barrott, who will be doing his third game in the middle of Anfield this season.

It has been revealed that "Let's hope that Kavanagh does not have to take over. The fact is we would not expect a professional football to be involved in two games over on weekend. Why are we asking our referees to do this?

Webb has plenty of talented officials at his disposal, but he clearly has his favourites in the Premier League this season.

As well as the Premier League, Kavanagh was in action during the international break, refereeing the game between France and Turkey.

Wanting to fix the problem, Hackett suggested: "ProRef need to take advantage of the increased pool of officials available to them after the summer merger of Premier League and Championship officials.".

Sources indicate that one referee who is only a fourth official this weekend is Farai Hallam. He is a favourite of Hackett's, and would undoubtedly do a good job in stressful circumstances.
