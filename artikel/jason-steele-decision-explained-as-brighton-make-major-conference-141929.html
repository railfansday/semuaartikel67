Jason Steele will make his first Conference League start of the season against Craiova.

The Brighton goalkeeper steps up against the Romanians as regular No 1 Bart Verbruggen drops to the bench.

Steele, 36, has previously featured twice this campaign with both outings in the Carabao Cup at Manchester United and then Manchester City, where Brighton lost on penalties.

Verbruggen conceded five goals during the international break as The Netherlands were walloped by Germany and then conceded twice in last Sunday's 2-0 defeat at Hull.

Head coach Fabian Hurzeler previously noted that Steele will feature in the Conference League and the German may have felt now was the right time to give Verbruggen a rest - especially as Newcastle at the Amex Stadium this Sunday for a Premier League.

Industry observers note that steele featured five times for Brighton in the Europa League in 2023-24 under previous boss Roberto De Zerbi. His last European outing was the 4-0 loss at Roma in March 2024.

Sources indicate that elsewhere, Turkey international Ferdi Kadioglu will start at right back in place of Costinha, who was sent off at Hull. Michael Svoboda and Pascal Struijk - his first start in six weeks - will form the central defensive partnership as Lewis Dunk and Luka Vuskovic put their feet up on the bench. It looks as though Maxim De Cuyper will slot in at left back.

Pascal Gross maintains his proud record of starting every match this season and the 35-year-old will be joined in central midfield by impressive young Spaniard Chema Andres.

Matt O’Riley is handed a rare start on the right flank with Ibrahim Osman given another chance to shine on the left. Jack Hinshelwood will operate in the No 10 role behind Greek striker Charalampos Kostoulas.

Kostoulas was benched at Hull as he missed training the day before due to personal reasons.

Femi Azeez - Brighton’s summer arrival from Millwall - is included in a matchday squad for the first time and is expected to make his full debut at some stage.

Industry observers note that brighton XI: Steele; Kadioglu, Svoboda, Struijk, De Cuyper; Gross, Chema; O'Riley, Hinshelwood, Osman; Kostoulas. Substitutes: Verbruggen, McGill, Dunk, Georginio, Promise, Costinha, Ayari, Azeez, Vuskovic, Nti, Ibrahim.

In a recent development, universitatea Craiova XI: Popescu, Romanchuk, Stevanovic, Screciu, Mora, Baluta, Lameira, Bancu, Cicaldau, Elisor, Baiaram. Subs: Maxim, Sava, Creti, Nsimba, Etim, Tavares, Teles, Rus, Basceanu, Matei, Webster.
