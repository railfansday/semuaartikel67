📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

“Heal,” a documentary from Princess Kate on her cancer diagnosis and living life in the face of serious illness, will debut in the U.K. on Nov. 29, with international premieres to follow.

Sources indicate that the documentary will premiere on the BBC in the U.K. on Nov. 29, via NBCUniversal in the United States on Nov. 30, Channel 9 in Australia on Dec. 1 and CTV in Canada on Dec. 7.

The Princess of Wales was diagnosed with cancer in early 2024 and revealed she was in remission in January 2025 after undergoing chemotherapy. Sources indicate that the documentary follows Princess Kate as she undertakes the National Three Peaks Challenge, climbing the highest mountains in Scotland, England and Wales within 24 hours in support of The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity. During her challenge attempt, she reflects on her experience alongside others navigating their health journeys, exploring the impact that illness has on a person and the people, places and experiences that can aid in healing.

The documentary shines a light on navigating diagnosis, recovery and life beyond cancer. Princess Kate aims to transform access to holistic cancer care that supports resilience and quality of life during and after diagnosis. All funds received during the documentary’s distribution have been directed to The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity to support those living with and beyond a cancer diagnosis.

In the U.S., “Heal” will be available on Nov. 30 at 8 p.m. ET/PT on NBC and at 11:30 p.m. ET/PT on Bravo. It will also be available to stream next day on Peacock.

“Heal” was produced by Detail Films and directed by Benji Jackson.
