As we head into the end of the year, now's the time for relaxation and recuperation — but between festive travel, deadlines and winter bugs, it could take more than a movie day on the sofa to undo the effects of stress.

It has been revealed that so isn't it lucky that you can aid your recovery at a fraction of the usual cost thanks to the Black Friday sales? Some of the best brands I've tested, including Hyperice and Bob and Brad, have had their prices slashed for the sales event, so you can give yourself the gift of relaxation.

I've put together a list of some of the best deals to build a wellness routine, so you don't have to search for them, from the best massage guns to plunge pools.

Recent findings suggest that the Bob and Brad ThermoRed Heating Pad is an effective post-workout recovery tool. Whether it's back pain from putting up the Christmas decorations or running off Thanksgiving dinner, it's a great solution. The combination of heat, vibration and red light therapy soothes muscles and reduces pain. It can be used anywhere on the body thanks to the adjustable strap and extender.

The Hyperice Hypervolt Go 2 is a premium massage gun that becomes far more affordable in the Black Friday sale. It has a runtime of 180 minutes, so you can cram in a few therapy sessions before needing to recharge. It comes in white or black with a flat and bullet attachment for all of your massage needs.

Industry observers note that though the weather is already cold, a cold plunge is one of the best ways to refresh yourself and get the blood pumping for fast muscle healing. This one from The Cold Pod is an 85-gallon round tub that comes with a cover, and the insulation keeps the tub cold for hours. It's easy to maintain and has dual drainage to make the process even easier.

Sources indicate that I recently tested the Hyperice Hypervolt Go 3 and loved it. It's a great value, entry-level massage gun that gives effective percussion therapy for tension release and recovery. It has an impressive four-hour battery life and useful app-guided treatment intends. It has two attachments, depending on the type of therapy you want.

Recent findings suggest that the Theragun is a Porsche among massage guns, and while it's not cheap, this Black Friday deal makes it an absolute steal. It has five massage speeds and two hours of battery life for uninterrupted recovery. It's also rugged, with protection for drops up to 10 feet, useful for when you're getting to hard to reach places.

The Hyperice Venom 2 Back is an excellent alternative to a massage gun if you suffer from back pain. It plays a key part in my recovery thanks to its quick heating and nine vibration settings. Plus, it's comfortable to wear and comes with an app that helps to visualize treatment.

It has been revealed that the Hyperice Hypervolt 3 Pro is the more advanced version of the aforementioned Hypervolt Go 3. It comes with two extra massage attachments, so you can further personalize your recovery. It has six speeds and powerful percussion to reach even the toughest muscle groups.
