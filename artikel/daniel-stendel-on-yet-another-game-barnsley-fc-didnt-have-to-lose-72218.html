For large parts of their South Yorkshire derby against Sheffield Wednesday, the Reds played very well.

But they gifted the Owls two cheap goals in the first 15 minutes and only took one of the chances they created, through Vimal Yoganathan.

It left coach Stendel downbeat when he addressed the media after the game.

When asked if he thought the first 15 minutes had decided the game, Stendel replied: "I would wouldn't say the first 15 minutes was two situations, and I know you would think, 'What you do in training?' but we talked exactly that about this, especially the second goal after last week.

"We prepared the team especially for a situation like this yesterday (Thursday). We do it in training.

"But yeah, when you do not defend the ball with the last desire, then you concede a goal like this, and this was hard.

"The support was really good but 2-0 down after 15 minutes in a game like this is hard against a team with so much quality.

"After the second goal, we have the feeling only we played in the game but we only scored one goal, and we talk again about the defeat in the game that we don't have to lose.".

Both Jamal Lowe's goals were very preventable from Barnsley perspective.

"It's not the quality from Sheffield, our defending in two situations decided the game," lamented Stendel.

"We all said we played a good game but we have to do it better.

"We talk a lot in the season about situations like that.

"Everybody can see that the players never give up, we really have a good character in the squad, a lot of power, and we can play at 2-0 down against one from the top teams in the league.

"We lose by only on one goal a lot of times.

"It demonstrated we have a good mentality in the group, but everybody - not only the players, not only supporters, everybody needs a positive result in the league.".
