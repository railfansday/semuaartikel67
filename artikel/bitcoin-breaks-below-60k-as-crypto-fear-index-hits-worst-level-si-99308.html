Bitcoin Breaks Below $60K as Crypto Fear Index Hits Worst Level Since February

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin broke below $60,000 on Friday, plunging through a major psychological price level as the selloff gathered pace after Anthropic’s stock market debut.

The daily chart reveals BTC at $59,703.72 , down 3.55% for the session after touching a low of $59,300.

The Fear and Greed Index chart dipped to 9, a reading classified as “extreme fear” and its lowest since February. For reference, the index was at 18 a week ago and a neutral 51 a month earlier.

The indicator tracks crypto market sentiment on a scale from zero, or maximum fear, to 100, or extreme greed, using market data rather than individual investor surveys. Its 2026 low was 5 on February 5, compared with a yearly high of 82 on August 26.

The selling went well beyond Bitcoin. Ethereum fell 4.24%, Solana lost 6.36%, BNB slid 4.38% and XRP declined 5.53% over the same period.

Sources indicate that forced selling added to the pressure. As Decrypt reported earlier Friday, roughly $727 million in leveraged crypto positions had been liquidated over 24 hours, about 86% of them bullish bets, while U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs lost $582.2 million on Thursday.

Spot fund withdrawals did not begin with Anthropic’s IPO. By Thursday, roughly $2.9 billion in U.S. Bitcoin ETF outflows for November, alongside higher Treasury yields and expectations of tighter Federal Reserve policy.

Sources indicate that anthropic made its Nasdaq debut Thursday at $111.50, up 34% from the $83 IPO price, before closing at $97.17. Friday brought another decline for the Claude developer, with the stock almost losing all the prime it gained after its IPO.

The listing raised $81.34 billion through the public offering, but enthusiasm for Anthropic shares did not carry over to the companies building the AI boom.

According to recent reports, friday’s market heatmap reveals tech stock suffering in the middle of mostly green pastures. Nvidia down 3.78%, Broadcom is down 5.65%, AMD declining 5.06% and memory-chip maker Micron dropping 6.51%. Meta fell 2.21%, adding to pressure on companies spending heavily on AI infrastructure.

Sources indicate that other major stocks were doing better. Alphabet rose 2.81%, Microsoft gained 0.50% and Apple edged up 0.25%, while healthcare stocks Eli Lilly and Johnson & Johnson rose 1.21% and 1.78%, respectively. Walmart added 2.91% and Visa climbed 1.34%.

Recent findings suggest that but it was not a uniform crash across the S&P 500. The tech-heavy Nasdaq was falling more sharply than the broader index, while pockets of healthcare, consumer, and financial shares remained green.

It has been revealed that bitcoin’s daily chart turned more decisively bearish after the break below $60,000. Its 50-day exponential moving average, or EMA, was below the 200-day EMA, a death-cross configuration, and spot prices were trading beneath both lines. That confirms sustained downside momentum rather than a brief intraday dip.

The relative strength index, or RSI, had fallen into the teens, far below the 30 threshold usually associated with oversold conditions. The average directional index, or ADX, climbed to 43.7, signaling a strong trend, with the chart’s directional readings favoring sellers. Oversold does not mean a bounce is guaranteed.

The longer-range chart puts the $60,000 breakdown in context. Bitcoin traded around this area several times in 2024 and returned to it during the mid-2026 retreat, making it a visible historical price zone as well as a round-number threshold.

With $60,000 giving way, the roughly $58,000–$59,000 support area near the mid-2026 lows comes into focus. If that zone fails, the next notable historical demand area is around $55,000–$56,000, followed by the $50,000 region marked by the deeper 2024 selloff.

Sources indicate that for bulls, $60,000 is now the first level to hold. The next hurdle is $62,000–$62,500, followed by $65,000 and heavier resistance near $68,000–$70,000. A brief bounce back above $60,000 would not, by itself, reverse the bearish trend.

In a recent development, as of right now, Bitcoin had touched $59,300.13, confirming that sellers had pushed below the $60,000 support zone. The next test is whether that level becomes resistance on a rebound.
