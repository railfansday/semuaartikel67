However, according to Decider, Quill attended Carlone University (locally referred to as Campus Carlone) in Nice. So if you’re looking to study abroad, that might be a good place to start.

This is a major change from the book series, however, which takes place in the United States at a fictional school called Sycamore University. But since Quill herself is French, and went to school in France, this doesn’t feel like that significant of a change.

The Prime Video series filmed on the French Riviera, a.k.a. the Côte d’Azur, in the South of France between Nice, Cannes, and Antibes. Lane’s apartment is filmed at the Marina Baie des Anges, a famous waterfront complex in Villeneuve-Loubet. And finally, Villa Eilenroc in Cap d’Antibes is where they filmed Belrose University.
