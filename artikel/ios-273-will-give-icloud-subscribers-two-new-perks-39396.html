When iOS 27.3 beta 2 debuted earlier this week, we detailed how the revision introduces AI usage limits for Siri AI and select other features.

In a recent development, for AI features that depend on server-based models, each device running iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, or macOS 27.3 will have limited daily access to advanced AI features. These include complex Siri AI requests, Image Playground, certain Photos app editing tools, and more.

The only iCloud+ tier that doesn’t include higher limits is the $0.99/month plan.

Industry observers note that similar to Apple’s other AI usage limits, your access to the Cloud Pro model in Shortcuts increases if you have a higher storage plan. Per this support document, the 200GB plan supports standard limits, the 2TB plan offers 5x access, followed by 10x on the 6TB plan and 20x on the 12TB plan.

iCloud+ already provided far more than just increased cloud storage, but with iOS 27.3 it’s set to be an asset for AI power owners too.
