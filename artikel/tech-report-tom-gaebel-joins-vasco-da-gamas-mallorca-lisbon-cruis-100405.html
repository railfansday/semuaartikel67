Nicko Cruises has announced that musician Tom Gaebel will be joining a cruise aboard the Vasco da Gama next fall.

From Oct 27 to Nov 4, 2027, the singer will provide musical highlights on a nine-day “Mediterranean treasures in Spain and Portugal” sailing from Mallorca to Lisbon.

Gaebel’s repertoire ranges from his own songs to the homage to Sinatra and License to Swing featuring music from the world of James Bond and other film classics.

Together with his saxophonist, he will perform at a concert evening at the ship’s Hollywood’s Show Theater.

Additionally, cruiseCLUB members have the opportunity to meet the artist in person during the club cocktail and an exclusive meet and greet event.

The nine-day cruise heads from Palma de Mallorca to Mahón on Menorca where guests can discover the original side of the Balearic island, taste regional wines and cheeses, or hike through the s’Albufera des Grau nature park.

The ship then docks in Ibiza, after which the route continues along the Spanish Mediterranean coast, calling at Alicante, Cartagena and Motril.

After a day at sea, the ship arrives in Lisbon for an overnight stay, allowing guests to explore the Portuguese capital.

The Vasco da Gama recently embarked on its 193-day world cruise from Hamburg. After visiting a wide range of destinations around the Atlantic, Pacific and Indian Oceans, the ship is set to return to the same port on May 5, 2027.
