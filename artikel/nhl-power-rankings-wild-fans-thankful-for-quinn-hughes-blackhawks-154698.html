According to recent reports, that's a question that will be asked at dinner tables around the country on Thursday. There will be some very thoughtful and earnest answers. There will be some not-so-thoughtful answers.

In a recent development, because it's Thanksgiving week, that seemed like a perfect exercise for the NHL Power Rankings. No matter where a team is in the NHL standings, it can give thanks for something. Well, most teams can be grateful for something.

For teams like the Minnesota Wild, it's easy. They've been near the top of the NHL standings all season, and they get to watch Quinn Hughes play hockey every night. Hughes is a perennial Norris Trophy contender in his prime, and he seems to create magic every time the puck touches his stick. Oh, he's also on pace for 119 points, which would be the first time a defenseman has hit those heights since Paul Coffey racked up 138 points in 1985-86.

It was a little tougher for teams like the Chicago Blackhawks, which is wallowing away at the bottom of the standings (again) while their star player recovers from surgery (again). If nothing else, they share fans with another exciting team in town that can distract from some of the pain.
