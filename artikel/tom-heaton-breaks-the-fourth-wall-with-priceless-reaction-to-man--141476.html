Tom Heaton’s smile mentioned a lot about Manchester United’s decision to do post-match weight training on the side of the pitch and in the view of the media.

Manchester United drew 2-2 away at Sporting in a game filled with incident. Three penalties, one red card, a goalkeeper howler and a horrendous pitch-enforced bobble means that Mauricio Pochettino will have to wait until his third game at least for his first win in charge.

A lot of the talk surrounding Pochettino’s arrival at Old Trafford is that the players better be ready to work hard and so what better way to demonstrate that work than by having the players perform a weight routine on the side of the pitch where all the cameras can see them.

The TNT cameras were on hand to capture the United players doing weight routines on the side of the pitch with Shea Lacey front and centre.

One player who looked slightly bemused as to why this was happening is third-choice goalkeeper Tom Heaton.

The 40-year-old, who has been around the block a few times, gave a cheeky look towards the cameras as if to say “yes, we are doing performative weight training on the side of the pitch.”.

All of this is part of Project Pochettino which aims to turn the unbothered United squad into one of the hardest-working in the Premier League.

But he will want to start winning games as soon as possible and suggested they should have on Wednesday night.

“I think we dominated and we deserved to win the game, but we didn’t for different circumstances,” he noted.

“All the teams make mistakes, but for these mistakes the punishment is too major in every single mistake. Many other clubs make mistakes or tilt in a different circumstance, in a more positive way. But that is in the way we are. We need to take responsibility.”.

Pochettino may also have a problem in goal after Senne Lammens, who at one point in his United career looked like a much-needed safe pair of hands, spilt an easy catch for Sporting’s second.

Pochettino, though, defended his player and remarked these things happen.

“This type of thing happens,” he remarked. “That is football. Football is about making mistakes.

“I always want to support my player. I think we need to talk about responsibility. In this type of action our defensive line needs to cover the keeper better because it was very difficult.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s not to blame the defensive line, but it’s two things that we need to improve, details that we need to work on. We need to manage this type of situation better.”.
