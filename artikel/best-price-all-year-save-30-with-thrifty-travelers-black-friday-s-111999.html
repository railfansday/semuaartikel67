Thrifty Traveler is one of our favorite ways to find deal alerts, and membership is currently on sale.

The best part of membership is you'll receive emails about cheap cash deals — and some incredible points and miles bargains — on airline tickets and/or hotel stays. This sale reduces the $129.99 price to $90.99 for the first year for flight deals, or from $189.99 to $132.99 for flight and hotel deals.

According to recent reports, this huge sale won’t last long: It ends at 11:59 p.m. EST on Wednesday, Dec. 2.

To take advantage of this deal, just enter "THEPOINTSGUY" in the promotional box or use our link to skip entering promo codes altogether.

"From dirt-cheap cash fares to ultra-rare award availability to fly first and business class with points and miles, Thrifty Traveler Premium finds deals from more than 200 airports in the U.S. and Canada — more than any other service," the tech giant remarked in a statement to TPG. "Members can tailor their alerts to get deals only from their home airport, all 200-plus cities, or anything in between.".

The service even lets you opt into instant text alerts for mistake fares and the rarest “Unicorn” deals.

I've personally used Thrifty Traveler Premium for years. In fact, it's how I scored one of the best points redemptions of my life: a lie-flat Delta One seat from Los Angeles to Brisbane, Australia, for just 73,100 Delta SkyMiles plus $120 in taxes and fees.

According to recent reports, again, this 30% off deal is a limited-time offer that's ending soon. After that, using the TPG code will continue to net you $20 off your purchase — but you should take the steeper discount while it's available.
