Industry observers note that arsenal Women vs Barcelona: Arsenal Women claimed a vital point at the Camp Nou. They drew 1-1 against Barcelona in the Champions League on Thursday night.

Sources indicate that frida Maanum gave the Gunners the lead in the first half. Caroline Graham Hansen scored to bring the game level after the break. Whilst Renee Slegers will be disappointed to miss out on all three points, her side played some encouraging football.

Despite needing to play differently, Arsenal had an even game against an opposition who would inevitably have long periods of the ball. The visitors played very well defensively and looked dangerous when they could attack.

Sources indicate that the result indicated that Slegers has a team that can adapt to fresh challenges. This could be key for Arsenal as they try to make a mark in Europe.

According to recent reports, one of the key decisions made by Slegers was to include Maanum. She had only recently returned from injury but made an impressive impact against Barcelona.

It has been revealed that the midfielder scored early on to start the game in the right direction. Slegers was very impressed with her play when not in possession. She was praised for her communication skills and assisting others.

Arsenal’s attacking play was mainly based on fast counter attacks. They knew they wouldn’t be able to play in the same style as Barcelona and therefore tried to take advantage of the space on offer.

However, there were chances created in both halves. Arsenal may have been more clinical with their chances, they were always a threat.

In a recent development, eventually, Barcelona scored their equaliser through Graham Hansen. Slegers noted that Arsenal lost their shape during the move, which gave Hansen too much space.

It was a reminder of how rapidly Barcelona can strike if you lose your concentration for a moment.

The Arsenal manager was pleased with the way her players implemented the game blueprint, showing tenacity, discipline, good communication and bravery.

Slegers admitted Arsenal did lose the ball too much in the second half, and by keeping it longer, they would have taken the pressure off defending against Barca’s dangerous attack.

Sources indicate that there was some frustration that Arsenal couldn’t hold onto their lead, and there were more chances created towards the end of the game. The visitors got into good positions to win corners but couldn’t get the winning goal.

Recent findings suggest that they will feel very positive from their display. They put themselves up against one of the biggest tests in the Camp Nou and proved that they can cause Barcelona problems.

There isn’t much time to really reflect on the result ahead of their game against the London City Lionesses on Sunday, but Slegers now has another example of what her side can do when they play with purpose and structure.
