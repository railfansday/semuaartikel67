Industry observers note that former Bundesliga striker Wout Weghorst has expressed his admiration for SC Freiburg’s remarkable development in recent years. The 34-year-old Dutch international, who previously represented VfL Wolfsburg and TSG Hoffenheim, returned to Germany with FC Twente for Thursday evening’s Conference League encounter at the Europa-Park Stadion.

Despite watching his side concede a dramatic stoppage-time winner in a 2-1 defeat, Weghorst was full of praise for the Bundesliga club’s progress.

Industry observers note that “Respect, just simply respect,” Weghorst noted when asked about Freiburg’s scintillating form by Kicker’s Carsten Schröter-Lorenz in the mixed zone afterwards. “I spoke of respect before the game as well. When I was here [in Germany] playing for Wolfsburg and then later at Hoffenheim they were more of a mid-table team. One has beheld a simply amazing development in the last years. Praise to them for that. They’re on a good track.”.

In a recent development, weghorst became familiar with Freiburg during his four seasons at Wolfsburg between 2018 and 2022 and his subsequent loan spell at Hoffenheim during the 2023/24 campaign. The Schwarzwaldverein were already establishing themselves as regular contenders for European qualification during that period, but their continued progress has since carried them to even greater heights.

Freiburg reached their first European final last season, eventually losing to Aston Villa in the Europa League showpiece in Istanbul. The club nevertheless secured another season of European football and entered the current campaign with a revamped squad.

As Get German Football News examined extensively in our 2026/27 Freiburg tactical preview, the club’s ability to navigate significant departures, reinvest intelligently, and maintain tactical continuity has remained central to its success.

Freiburg’s current European campaign has offered further evidence of that progress. Following November’s hard-fought Conference League victory over Trabzonspor, Schuster’s men have now extended their remarkable home-winning streak in European competition to 14 matches.

Thursday’s contest against Twente was hardly straightforward. The Dutch visitors enjoyed the better of the opening half despite conceding an early goal to Cyriaque Irié. Younes Taha’s superb second-half free kick eventually brought Twente level before substitute Niklas Beste struck in stoppage time to secure Freiburg’s sixth consecutive competitive victory.

The result also kept alive the European ambitions that Schuster and Vincenzo Grifo discussed following the club’s Conference League opener against FK Jablonec. Freiburg’s supporters have made no secret of their hopes for another deep continental run after last season’s historic adventure.

For Weghorst, the progress is unmistakable. The former Bundesliga striker may have departed Freiburg empty-handed on Thursday evening, but his assessment of the club’s remarkable transformation was unequivocally positive.
