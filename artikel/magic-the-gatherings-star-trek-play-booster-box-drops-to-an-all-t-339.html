TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Star Trek Play Booster Box is on sale for $104.99 at Amazon, down from its $132.95 list price.

Sources indicate that magic: The Gathering's Star Trek crossover only arrived on Nov. 13, but the price of its 30-pack Play Booster Box has had another cut in line with the current market price — perfect timing if you’ve been holding off on the trading card expansion in hopes of an early Black Friday deal.

As of Nov. 16, Magic: The Gathering’s 30-pack Star Trek Play Booster Box costs just $104.99 at Amazon, taking $27.96 off its $132.95 listing price. That's a 21% discount, which means you're paying roughly $3.50 per pack.

Recent findings suggest that the Star Trek Play Booster Box was selling for as low as $129.95 only 10 days ago. It's now $24.96 cheaper, and only 31 cents above its estimated $104.68 market price on TCGplayer. The box is sold and shipped directly by Amazon rather than a third-party seller — with delivery estimated between Nov. 27-29.

It has been revealed that inside, you'll find 30 Play Boosters containing 14 Magic cards, featuring captains, ships, and other familiar faces from Star Trek's 60-year history. There's a guaranteed Traditional Foil in every pack, with alternate-art and special-frame treatments to chase.
