Bayern Munich have revealed England captain Harry Kane will be out for a "few months" after he sustained an ankle injury in the 1-1 draw against FC Koln.

After netting in England's 3-2 win over Spain on Sunday, Kane returned to action for Bayern in Friday's Bundesliga meeting with Koln at the Allianz Arena.

Kane was named in the starting lineup as Vincent Kompany made his return to the touchline for the first time since he collapsed while watching Bayern's second team train.

As it proved, Bayern could not have got off to a worse start as Mikey Moore put Koln in front, shortly before Kane was forced off in the 20th minute.

The Ballon d'Or winner picked up the injury in the 15th minute when he attempted to reach Michael Olise's cross.

However, Koln full-back Joel Schmied slid in to win the ball while also making contact with Kane in the process.

The 33-year-old hobbled on for a few minutes before he eventually went to ground to receive treatment.

Unable to continue, Kane was replaced by Tom Bischof, who provided the assist for Nathaniel Brown to equalise with what proved to be the final goal of the game.

Bayern also lost Michael Olise to injury at the half-time interval, which compounded a disappointing evening for the reigning Bundesliga champions.

The Bavarians have since provided a swift but pessimistic update on Kane's injury status.

The statement read: "Harry Kane suffered a torn syndesmotic ligament in his left ankle during FC Bayern's Bundesliga home match against 1. FC Koln on Friday evening.

"This was confirmed by an examination conducted by FC Bayern's medical department. The 33-year-old will therefore be sidelined for the next few months.".

Bayern also revealed Olise will "receive treatment in the coming days" after sustaining a foot problem.

In line with Bayern's "few months" timeframe, Florian Plettenberg reports that Kane will be out for at least three months with the ankle problem.

However, there is an understandable caution in putting an exact timeframe on his recovery at this early stage.

Using the early three-month prediction as a guideline, Kane will miss Bayern's final four Champions League league phase matches against Lille, Slavia Prague, Manchester United and Real Betis.

Kane would also be a doubt to feature in the knockout phase playoffs if Bayern fail to secure a top-eight finish in the league phase.

In regard to the Bundesliga, he could miss the next 12 matchdays if he is out until at least February 20.

Meanwhile, England boss Thomas Tuchel will be nervously waiting to find out the exact timeframe for Kane's rehabilitation process.

If it is around three months, Kane would be back in time for England's UEFA Nations League quarter-final clash against Norway.

But if he needs longer to recover, England's greatest goalscorer would be in a race to prove his fitness in time for the two-legged clash.

The Three Lions will travel to Norway on March 27 before hosting the second leg a few days later.
