We are rapidly approaching the arrival of The Game Awards on 10th December, and today, Geoff Keighley has announced all of the nominees for this year's show.

Recent findings suggest that while the weight of GTA 6 cast its shadow over most categories (come on, it's not even out yet!), Nintendo picked up a good number of nominations itself, including a spot in the all-crucial GOTY top six for Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Heck, it's the most-nominated publisher this year with 12 nods all to itself — overtaking the likes of Capcom (11), Annapurna Interactive (9) and PlayStation (9).

Link's latest is joined by 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto 6, Phantom Blade 0 and Resident Evil Requiem in the running for this year's top prize.

On the Nintendo-related side of things, Switch 2 exclusive Orbitals picked up nominations in the Best Multiplayer Game and Best Debut Game categories, and The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is nominated for the Best Adaptation Award.

Sources indicate that you'll find the full list of this year's nominees on The Game Awards' website. The full awards show will be livestreamed on YouTube on 10th December.
