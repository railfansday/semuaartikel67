In a recent development, new blow for the end of Manuel Neuer’s career. The Bayern Munich goalkeeper suffered a “partial tear of a tendon in his right foot” against Mainz last weekend (2-1), when he was just returning from injury. He did not last a half and had to give way to Jonas Urbig. The goalkeeper will miss approximately two months of competition.

His physique raises questions, to the point that the subject of early retirement was suggested in the German media, a few months before the end of his contract (June 2027). This option should not be chosen according to Sky Sports. Neuer plans to finish the season and his career as initially planned. On the other hand, his club has indeed begun its search for a future number one goalkeeper (or number 2 depending on the desired hierarchy) and has targeted Oliver Baumann, Bernd Leno and Loris Karius, in addition to two other candidates.
