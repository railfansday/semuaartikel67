I’ll admit it. Being a fashion reporter can be a lot of fun. I get to sit across from some the most brilliant minds in the industry and peek at gorgeous collections seasons before they hit stores—long before they start shaping what we will wear. But what most people don’t realize is that the job also involves a lot of squinting at fiber-content labels. I’ve asked founders extremely nerdy questions about leather tanning methods. I’ve toured factories and tromped through cotton fields. Along the way, I come across many products. Over the course of a year, I wander through dozens of stores, wade through stacks of applications from brands hoping to land on our annual lists, and put bold latest product claims to the test. Most of it is forgettable. But every so often, I come across something that I love: a clever jacket that is also beautiful, heels you can actually walk in all day, a pair of sneakers with a story so moving I still think about it months later. This list is the best of what I found in a year of poking, prodding, and road testing. If you’re shopping for someone who cares about style and quality, these are good bets. [Source Image: Resolute RGL] Every Stitch Tells a StoryResolute RGL, Najla in Olive Green, $350 Of the many products I reviewed this year, this is the one that made a biggest impression. Each Resolute sneaker carries a hand-embroidered panel of tatreez, the Palestinian cross embroidery that has passed down through generations. Each panel is stitched by a woman who lives in the villages in the South Hebron Hills of the West Bank. She chooses the pattern and colors herself, and a panel takes 14 to 24 hours to complete. The cooperative co-owns the organization, and embroiderers are paid as soon as their panels are finished. The shoes themselves are lovely, with an olive suede upper, memory foam insoles, and recycled rubber soles. They’re made to order and take eight to 10 weeks to arrive, so strategy ahead. [Source Image: Cole Haan] Light as a Feather, Tough Enough for a Nor’easterCole Haan, Grand Flex Fenella Buckle Lace-Up Waterproof Booties, $240 Most winter boots are heavy, adding weight as you trudge through the snow. Cole Haan has engineered these boots to be lightweight, but still set on a chunky lug sole that provides traction on icy sidewalks. They’re made from waterproof leather with sealed seams and a foam footbed that keep them cushy through a full day of walking. The buckle and laces give them a little edge, and a hidden ankle zip means you don’t have to do the laces again every time you leave the house. [Source Image: Parker Thatch] Mary Poppins Would Be Into ThisParker Thatch, Jack Tote in Leopard, $1,250 I am a person who carries too much: laptop, a novel, a snack, and a backup snack for my kids. The Jack allows overpackers to carry what they need for the day without looking like a duffel bag. It’s made from leopard-print hair-on cowhide with black leather trim and handles, and it fits a laptop with room to spare. There’s an interior zip pocket, two open pockets so your phone and glasses don’t get lost, and a snap closure. It’s the ideal gift for a professional woman in the market for a power bag. [Source Image: Lake] In-Law-Approved LoungewearLake, DreamModal Kimono Three-Piece Pants Set in Velvet Green Pencil Stripe, $168 The holidays involve lots of overnight guests, which can pose a dilemma. You want to be cozy, but you don’t want your in-laws to see you in your slinky nightgown or threadbare college T-shirt. Lake has many lounge sets. This one includes a wrap top with bell sleeves, a scoop-neck tank, and wide-leg pants with an elastic waist. It’s all made from a silky modal fiber, so it feels like you’re wearing pajamas but it looks like you’re in an outfit. The green and white stripe is festive without being kitschy. It’s machine washable, too. [Source Image: Margaux] Yes, You Can Walk in These HeelsMargaux, The Paloma Platform in Gold Nappa, $395 For years, most women assumed that “comfortable heels” is an oxymoron. Margaux is working hard to prove them wrong. The Paloma shoe has a 3.5-inch block heel, with a 1-inch platform, allowing you get the height without feeling unstable. There is plush foam padding under the sole and the straps are positioned so they don’t pinch. They’re handmade of nappa leather in Spain. I like the gold ones because they go with every holiday outfit. [Source Image: Maison de Sabré] Honey, I Shrunk the HandbagMaison de Sabré, The Mini Palais in Candy Plum, $675 Some nights call for nothing more than a phone, a lipstick, and a credit card. The Mini Palais is just big enough for these things, and its adjustable strap lets you wear it crossbody, on the shoulder, or in hand. The bags are also sustainable. Maison de Sabré skips conventional tanning, which is heavy on chemicals, in favor of DriTan, a waterless tanning technology. Each bag carries a serial number that allows you to trace it back to tanneries and farms in the Netherlands. And you can dress the bag up with adorable charms made from scraps of colorful leather discarded on the factory floor. The matching pareo means you can wear the outfit to your hotel’s buffet breakfast before lounging poolside. (And if the whole family is coming along, the suit can be coordinated with matching styles for everybody.) [Source Image: Overland] A Functional, Beautiful JacketOverland, Kylie Reversible Sheepskin Jacket, $1,195 If you read my coats roundup this fall, you’ll recognize this jacket, made by Overland, which has been making sheepskin coats since 1973. If you flip it inside out, it transforms into a plush shearling jacket. The pockets work from either side. It’s built to last, too, since sheepskin can last a lifetime with proper care. It has teamed up with Assouline to produce a silk-covered, 280-page tome about the 25 most influential handbags of all time, from the Hermès Birkin to the Fendi Baguette and the Dior Saddle, plus newer icons from Bottega Veneta and Loewe. It has more than 200 illustrations that will be eye candy to fashionistas. [Source Image: ME+EM] Three Earrings in OneME+EM, Transformer Earrings, $495 This piece of jewelry magically transforms to suit your outfit. The earrings start as gold-plated studs for the office. Clip on the detachable pendant and they become drops for dinner. The pendant is double-sided, with black marbled resin on one side and gold on the other, so you can flip it to suit your mood or your outfit. They’re made in Italy, and they work as well with a turtleneck as with a cocktail dress.
