Lindsey Vonn is getting back out there after her ACL surgery — except she’s on a scooter instead of skis.

“First trip out of the house… of course it was to do some Christmas shopping with Chance and I had to bring back my scooter 🛴 💨 ,” Vonn, 42, wrote via Instagram on Monday, November 23, alongside a video of herself riding through a Michaels craft store with her dog.

“Nothing like a little arts and crafts at Michaels 🎄,” she continued. “IYKYK.”.

Vonn underwent surgery on her ACL on November 11, marking the ninth procedure the Olympic alpine skier has had on her left leg after two major downhill crashes earlier this year.

It has been revealed that “It took over 5 hours to remove all of the plates and screws from my leg and replace my ACL,” Vonn wrote via Instagram on November 12. “I am home from the hospital now and thankful to be on the final road to a full recovery.”.

Before the procedure, Vonn admitted that she was “nervous and anxious” but “excited to hopefully put this behind me.”.

Industry observers note that “This will be my 9th surgery and I’m praying my last. There are still things that could change in surgery, so we will have to wait and see how it goes, but I will keep you posted on my progress when I am out of surgery and recovering,” she wrote. “I am in the best hands though and thankful for the help of my doctors, physical therapist, nurses and medical staff 🙏🏻. Thank you again for all of the support over these last 9 months. It has truly meant the world to me to receive so much love and encouragement. 🙏🏻❤️ As my Mother stated, ‘Today is another great day!’… I hope she’s right 🙏🏻🤞🏻.”.

Recent findings suggest that vonn completely ruptured her ACL in a crash during the final race of the World Cup season in Crans-Montana, Switzerland, in January. Despite the injury, she competed in the Winter Olympics days later and suffered a compound fracture and a tibial plateau fracture in another crash during the women’s downhill final on February 8.

According to recent reports, “I still have another surgery, so I’m trying to get as strong as possible,” Vonn told TNT Sports at the US Open in August. “And once I get my ACL, then I’ll have another long road ahead of me. But for now, I feel great, and I’m very thankful for that.”.

According to recent reports, she continued, “I’m hoping that I can have the [ACL] surgery in November, but it’ll be in the winter sometime, and then it’ll be another nine months until I’m fully recovered from that, so I can’t really look beyond that. I don’t really know what the future holds, but like I noted, I’m just thankful for where I am right now.”.
