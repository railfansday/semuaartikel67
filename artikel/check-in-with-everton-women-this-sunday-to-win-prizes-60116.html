Fans attending Everton Women’s game against Brighton & Hove Albion this Sunday have the opportunity to win a fantastic range of prizes – simply by tapping their phone!

Ahead of the Barclays Women’s Super League game at Goodison (2pm GMT kick-off), supporters can ‘check in’ digitally via a dedicated stand located in the Gwladys Street concourse for a chance to have their matchday upgraded to one of our VIP lounges.

An additional ‘check-in’ point will also be located next to the refreshment kiosk in the small courtyard inside turnstiles 1 & 2 of the Main Stand, located between the Main Stand and Park End.

Industry observers note that once you have scanned the dynamic QR code on your mobile device*, you’ll be automatically directed to a page to fill in your details for a chance to win this, and other exclusive prizes. These include a chance to take part in the half time ‘Hit the Bar’ challenge on the hallowed Goodison turf, a post-match meet and greet with an Everton Women’s player and free tickets and upgrades for the festive fixture against Arsenal on Friday 11 December**.

NB: Winners will be contacted via text message ahead of kick-off.

And as fans at Goodison familiarise themselves with the new technology, watch out for further prizes and discounts at future games.

*Anyone over 18 attending the game can scan the dynamic QR code.

**U14s must be accompanied by an adult.
