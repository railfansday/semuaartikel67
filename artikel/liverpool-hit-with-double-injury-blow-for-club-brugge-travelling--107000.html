Andoni Iraola has named his travelling squad for Liverpool’s Champions League trip to Club Brugge, with one notable omission.

Currently in eighth place, the Reds are only ahead of ninth-place Brugge on goals scored and are seeking three points that would allow them to reinforce their position in automatic qualification places.

Key players such as captain Virgil Van Dijk and midfield maestro Dominik Szoboszlai are fit to travel after facing minor injury struggles this season, but one midfielder was not so lucky.

Ryan Gravenberch is not among the 22 who have travelled to Bruges for Wednesday’s fixture, as he continues his recovery from a heel fracture.

The midfielder was a notable absence for Liverpool’s triumphant 4-0 drubbing of rivals Manchester United last week, and remains sidelined while managing pain.

Gravenberch has enjoyed a prolific Reds career since joining in 2023, lifting both the Premier League title and Young Player of the Season award in 2023/24 and penning a latest six-year contract this year.

Before last weekend’s Premier League clash, Iraola confirmed that the Dutchman had returned to limited training.

The Basque manager said: “Ryan has been training but still is managing the pain.

“We are going day to day – some days he can. We have been waiting until the end but he was in pain yesterday. Let’s hope he can recover for the next one.”.

Netherlands teammate Cody Gakpo is also absent from the Champions League through a calf injury sustained during international duty, but has not trained.

The Dutch duo have not yet been ruled out of this Sunday’s Merseyside Derby against Everton, and could feature if able to return to fitness.

Liverpool travelling squad for Club Brugge: Alisson, Gomez, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Kerkez, Wirtz, Szoboszlai, Isak, Mac Allister, Tsimikas, Munoz, Mamardashvili, Woodman, Barcola, Frimpong, Araujo, Nyoni, Chambers, McConnell, Koumas, Ngumoha, Danns.
