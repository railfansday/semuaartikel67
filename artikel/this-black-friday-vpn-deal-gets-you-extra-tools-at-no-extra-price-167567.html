According to recent reports, most VPN companies offer advanced tiers, which get you extra tools and features compared to the basic VPN-only strategy. If you just want a way to change your location, you might not consider subscribing for these extras, but now you don't need to spend any more money.

It has been revealed that as part of its Black Friday sales, Surfshark has reduced the monthly cost of its middling Surfshark One strategy down to the price of its lowest Surfshark Starter. In effect, that lets you enjoy all the extra features like an antivirus, scam protection and data leak monitoring, without the extra cost.

The Surfshark Starter plan is also discounted by $0.50, which means you're paying $1.99 per month for the two year span. That tier gets you access to a VPN, data masking and an ad blocker, with tools like the antivirus, data leak monitoring and scam protection just n the One tier.

If you're signing up for a VPN just so you can check out what's on Netflix somewhere else, you might not think extra features are worth the extra $0.30 per month. And Surfshark Starter, with its ad blocker and data masking, already makes for a solid proposition.

But there are some tools worth paying for, and many would consider that data leak protection is one of them. This feature, included in the One blueprint, alerts you when your personal information is featured in a data leak from a major manufacturer. Many services exist just for this reason, like Incogni and Have I Been Pwned. They keep you off scammers' or phishers' lists.

If you're worried about data privacy, it's really handy, and would be well worth the small amount extra you're paying for Surfshark One. Though if this isn't a concern, perhaps the Surfshark Starter discount is better.
