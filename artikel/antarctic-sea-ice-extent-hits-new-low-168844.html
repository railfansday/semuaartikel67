Antarctic sea ice extent hits new low

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (46 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Satellite observations show Antarctic sea ice coverage dipped to record daily lows in October 2026, surpassing even the extremely low extent observed in October 2023—the year poor sea ice conditions led to the deaths of thousands of emperor penguin chicks in Antarctica.
