A man has been charged with felony retail theft after police say he posed as a Walmart delivery driver to steal Pokemon cards from a store in Dodgeville, Wisconsin.

Dodgeville police identified the man as Charles L. LaChapelle. According to WMTV 15 News, investigators allege he took merchandise from the store during pickups while posing as a Spark driver. Spark is Walmart’s app for independent drivers who pick up and deliver customer orders.

The cards were from Pokemon’s 30th Celebration expansion, which launched on September 16 with 30 different foil Pikachu cards, one in each booster pack. The set arrived weeks after Walmart began holding online drawings for some Pokemon card releases.

Recent findings suggest that officers responded to a report of a theft in progress at the Dodgeville Walmart Supercenter shortly after 11:15 a.m. local time on November 19. They pulled over a black Tesla with Florida plates and said a search turned up 125 packages of 30th Celebration boosters.

Police also recovered 112 packages of One Piece trading cards, 26 Pokemon anniversary tin sets, and a Rayquaza Lego set, along with other merchandise still in its packaging.

In a recent development, police mentioned the Tesla had traveled to several locations in Wisconsin and Minnesota, as well as Missouri. The department is asking other agencies to come forward if they can link any of the additional merchandise to thefts in their area.

In a separate case in September, an Ohio collector was sentenced to 90 days of GPS monitoring after putting a tracker on a van used to deliver Pokemon cards to Walmart.
