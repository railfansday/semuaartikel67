Give Your Budget a Fun-Money Line So One Splurge Doesn’t End It

Sources indicate that build a set amount of fun money into your budget each pay period, and spend it on anything you like with no tracking and no guilt. Fund savings, bills, and debt payments first, so a splurge never comes out of them. Room for small pleasures is what keeps you from quitting your budget.

According to recent reports, A budget that bans every treat works like a crash diet. It holds for a few weeks until one splurge makes the whole blueprint feel broken, and you quit.

It has been revealed that sort your budget’s lines, the categories you give money to, into two kinds. Firm lines are savings transfers, bills, and debt payments, and you never borrow from them. Flexible lines, such as groceries, dining out, and clothes, can change from month to month. Fun money is a flexible line too, and the only one with no rules about what it buys.

In a recent development, once treats have their own line, buying one stops feeling like a failure. A $12 lunch paid from the fun line is spending you planned, so there’s nothing to hide from your budget or make up for later.

Fund the firm lines first, then set the fun line at 5% to 10% of take-home pay. While you’re paying off a credit card or other high-interest debt, keep it closer to 5%. If you and a partner combine finances, set a separate amount for each of you, so neither has to justify a purchase.

Keep the fun money in a separate free checking account with its own debit card, so the balance is all you ever check. Say you’re paid twice a month and take home $3,500 a month. Moving $150 from each paycheck into that account gives you $300 a month to spend on anything, a little under 9% of take-home pay.

Sources indicate that cover any overspending that month by moving money from another flexible line, the same move a zero-based budget uses to keep every dollar assigned. Never take it from savings. If the same line runs over three months in a row, its amount is too low for how you live, so raise it and trim another flexible line by the same amount.

Choose your amount before your next payday, and set up an automatic transfer so it reaches the fun-money account the same day your pay arrives.
