It has been revealed that dungeon crawler HARK THE GHOUL will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam on January 21, 2027, publisher Kwalee and developer Deep Denizens revealed. The console versions are newly announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Kwalee:.

HARK THE GHOUL is a neo-gothic love letter to the graphics and games of a bygone era, complete with customizable visual filters and a 72-page in-game artbook with developer commentary.

The rain-lashed city of CLERGERAC languishes in the glare of the ASTRE. Its citizens, mad with desire, claw at the earth for shards of their beloved AMBER.

Sources indicate that beneath the cobbled streets a secret cocoon stirs. Deeper still, the darkness teems with unimaginable horrors. And in their midst, a glint draws the eye.
