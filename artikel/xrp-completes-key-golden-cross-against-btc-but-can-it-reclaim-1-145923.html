XRP has completed a short-term golden cross against Bitcoin, giving bulls a fresh technical signal as the cryptocurrency eyes a retest of $1.

Industry observers note that the 50 MA rose above the 200 MA on the 3-hour chart, completing a golden cross, as XRP saw relative outperformance against Bitcoin. The timing of the golden cross remains surprising given the current selling in the market.

At the time of writing, XRP was down 5.83% in the last 24 hours to $0.923, giving back most of a rally that sharply took it past $1 after Jensen Huang returned as CEO of Nvidia.

According to recent reports, on Monday, Nvidia shocked the markets with the surprise announcement of Jensen Huang returning as the CEO of the popular tech and AI company. For crypto traders, the initial response looked extremely encouraging as most tokens sharply rose. However, the rebound began fading, with most cryptocurrencies seeing visible losses in an ongoing selloff in the early Tuesday session.

Bitcoin was down 5.23% in the last 24 hours to $60,298, with total liquidations across the crypto market reaching $596 million.

The $1 price point remains significant for XRP as it is slightly above the lower boundary of the channel in which XRP traded in recent months.

XRP failed to break above the $1.55 resistance at the top of the channel and completed a full reversal toward the channel’s lower boundary at $0.99. XRP broke below this key support level in the past week, cascading to a low of $0.86, last seen in November 2024.

This drop also coincided with a death cross on the daily chart of its USD pairing, the first such occurrence in 2026. For the bearish outlook to weaken, XRP would need to reclaim $0.99 as support.

XRP's attempt to return above $1 has met a brick wall since mid-November. On November 18, XRP's price rise stopped exactly at $1, before it started to decline again.

On November 23, 2026, XRP tried to break above $1 again after reaching $1.02, but bulls could not hold the momentum. If the price goes above $1, XRP will target $1.21 and $1.40. On the downside, key support is expected near $0.80 and $0.64.
