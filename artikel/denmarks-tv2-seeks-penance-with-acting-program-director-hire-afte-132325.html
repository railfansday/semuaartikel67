📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Denmark’s TV2 has dipped into the independent production market for an Acting Program Director as it seeks penance for the scandal around Truman Show-like reality series Everybody Loves Rasmus.

Sune Roland will take up the position on December 1, and will lead the TV2 programming division during a period the network “is working to rebuild the Danish public’s trust.” Given his position is an acting role, it’s not certain he will be TV2’s long-term program chief just yet.

Recent findings suggest that the follows the departures of Content Director Ulla Pors and Program Director Dorthe Thirstrup following the disastrous launch of Alle Elsker Rasmus (Everybody Loves Rasmus). Recent findings suggest that the show faced immense public backlash for duping comedian Rasmus Wallbridge into believing his dream film concept was being produced. It was scrapped soon after launch, and TV2’s directors have introduced an investigation into how the show was greenlit in the first place. Their response has been fast and decisive during in an almost unprecedented situation for a major European broadcaster. Watch on Deadline.

Recent findings suggest that roland is a former TV2 exec, having risen as high as Head of Content, and returns after four years in the independent production sector. He had worked as Managing Director of Strong Productions, the Fremantle Denmark production house that merged with Blu last year. Following his exit there, he introduced his own company, Shift Studios.

Sources indicate that frederik Lauesen, who was installed as Acting Content Director at TV2 in the aftermath of the scandal, said: “I am very pleased that Sune Roland has agreed to take on the role of Acting Programme Director. Sune brings extensive experience; he is well-liked and respected within the industry and possesses a deep understanding of both TV 2 and the Danish production landscape. He is a highly skilled professional and an accomplished leader, and we will greatly benefit from his expertise in the time ahead.”.

Lauesen remarked TV2 was facing “a serious situation” and that rebuilding trust was paramount. “We must address this while continuing to manage a large and diverse portfolio of content – an effort we carry out in close collaboration with our talented colleagues in the programming department and the Danish production industry. Sune’s experience from both sides of the table will prove valuable in the time ahead.”.

In a recent development, roland acknowledged he was joining in “particularly challenging times,” but added: TV2 holds a special place for me, and I know how many talented and dedicated people work every day to create content that truly matters to the Danish public. I look forward to contributing my experience and collaborating with both colleagues and production companies.”.

Roland worked as TV2’s Head of Factual between 2007 and 2011, and then from 2012 until 2022 held roles such as Head of Programming, Channel Head, and Head of Content. He has also held senior leadership positions at Koncern TV and Metronome Productions.

TV2 will begin a recruitment process for a “future leadership team within the programming division” at a later date.
