Apple is rolling out the second developer beta of macOS 27.3 ahead of the official launch of the enhancement, which includes several notable additions. Here’s what’s coming.

Two weeks ago, Apple released macOS 27.3 developer beta 1, followed by the public beta a few days later, amid a slew of hardware releases that included fresh M6-powered iMacs and 14-inch MacBook Pro, and the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display.

According to recent reports, and contrary to macOS 27.2, the latest upgrade is shaping up to be pretty significant. In addition to expanding Siri AI support to four more languages, including Traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish, and Vietnamese, the system also expands Siri AI’s capabilities across Apple’s own apps, allowing people to perform more complex actions in apps like Messages, Music, Calendar, Photos, and Reminders, as well as manage files and folders.

macOS 27.3 beta 2 also introduces new privacy controls for Full Disk Access and automation tools, and you can read more about that here. This change comes in response to concerns about increasingly capable AI agents gaining broad access to users’ personal data and control over their Macs.

Apple is also introducing new tools for third-party developers to create advanced Siri AI integrations using Markdown-based instructions, allowing the assistant to coordinate multistep actions across different apps.

It has been revealed that macOS 27.3 also introduces Vertical Tabs and Adaptive Dark Mode in Safari, as well as native Live Activities support in the menu bar. Additionally, macOS 27.3 so far include native Markdown rendering in Quick Look, a new Pro Light screensaver, and additional checks for potentially harmful content shared through public iCloud links.

The build number for macOS 27.3 Golden Gate developer beta 2 is 26C5047e. We are digging through the system and will report back with what we find.
