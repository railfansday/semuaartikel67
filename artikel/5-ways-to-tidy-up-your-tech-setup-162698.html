The device organizer isn’t the only way I’ve been trying to tidy up my gadget situation. Here are a handful of other solutions I’ve accumulated over the years:.

I mentioned retractable charging cables back in my year-end list of favorite tech things. The Baseus charging station on my desk (seemingly discontinued, though I’m happy with it) has a built-in cable I can pull out when my phone needs a top-up, while a separate Anker charger in the living room has a pair of retractable cables for the kids’ iPads. Both have cut down on the number of cables constantly dangling about.

In a recent development, while I’d love it if all my devices charged via USB-C, I’ve got a few stragglers that depend on Apple’s Lightning connector or the aging Micro USB standard. Instead of keeping separate cables around for these devices, I’ve decluttered by using little charging adapters that snap onto any USB-C connector.

The key is to find ones with built-in lanyards, so they can hang onto to a USB-C cable even when they’re not plugged in. I use these Lightning-to-USB-C adapters for the aforementioned kids’ iPads, and have finally gotten around to buying a similar set for Micro USB.

I am forever hoarding the velcro ties that sometimes come with charging cables, so I can use them to bunch various charging and data cables together. Huge packs of them are also available for cheap. (Alternatively, you can install cable hiders to keep things extra clean, but I’m reluctant to head down that rabbit hole.).

In a recent development, perhaps you noticed the lack of an iPad Pro to go with the Magic Keyboard in my organizer. That’s because it has its own magnetic charging stand in front of my desk, where I often use the iPad to take interview notes.

Beyond that, I bought this cheap 3-in-1 Anker charging stand years ago, and it remains a nightstand fixture both at home and on the road. It works with any phone and earbuds with wireless charging, and you can snake a third charging cable through the USB-A port underneath. (Ostensibly, it’s for Apple Watches, but I’ve used it for other things as well.) I’ve also got a Steam Deck docking station that keeps it fully charged.

I’m not saying you need to go on a massive organizational spree right now, but over time these little improvements have brought some much-needed order to my device empire. Perhaps they’ll give you some ideas of your own.

Alternatively, you might just be thinking that I have too many gadgets sitting around to begin with. But that is a problem for another day.
