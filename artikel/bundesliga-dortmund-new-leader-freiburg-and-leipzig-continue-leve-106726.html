In a recent development, the Bundesliga multiplex this Saturday promised to be particularly lively, with five matches scheduled for 3:30 p.m. The most anticipated poster pitted Borussia Mönchengladbach, struggling in 18th place, against Borussia Dortmund, runner-up in the championship. A duel which finally turned to the advantage of the Marsupials thanks to a late achievement from Nmecha (80th). For its part, Bayer Leverkusen, fifth, hosted Paderborn. The locals did not tremble with a significant victory (4-0) and a double from the former Lyonnais, Afonso Moreira.

Recent findings suggest that the other matches pitted Eintracht Frankfurt and Hoffenheim, two teams close in the standings, and it was Frankfurt who came out on top with a goal from Burkardt in the first act. Finally, the promoted Elversberg fell (0-1) against Fribourg. In the standings, Dortmund takes the lead and is ahead of Bayern, Freiburg and Leipzig, who defeated Union Berlin (5-1).

Bayer Leverkusen 4-0 Paderborn: Moreira (14th, 47th), Schick (51st) and Fernandez (59th).
