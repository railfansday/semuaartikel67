Liverpool have been constantly upgrading their team over the last couple of years.

The Anfield outfit have spent well over £600 million on new players to try and improve their ranks.

And, while some of the deal that they've struck have worked out, some still need to prove their worth.

Indeed, the performances of Liverpool's squad as a whole have improved thanks to the appointment of Andoni Iraola but, it's clear that there are still some problems that need rectifying.

Whether the Reds need to sign back-ups or replacements for their starting XI, change is needed so that they don't fall too far behind their title rivals.

So, the perfect attacker now being found by Liverpool is rather exciting.

Yunus Akgun is wanted by Liverpool.

Liverpool's attack has been one of their main focuses when improving their squad.

The Anfield outfit have spent well over £300 million on attackers alone over the last couple of years but, it still seems like the club aren't fully happy with the options that they have at their disposal.

That is obviously a crazy statement to share as, just this summer, £123 million was spent on Bradley Barcola alone.

But, with how many players Premier League clubs buy every season, there's no wonder why clubs have to constantly spend to keep up with their rivals.

And now, thanks to an update from X account Mekteb-i Gala, who have 50,000 followers, it seems like another attacker has made his way onto Liverpool's shortlist.

"EXCLUSIVE | PSG, Manchester City, Liverpool, Atletico Madrid, Como and Juventus are monitoring Yunus Akgün in every match," they wrote.

"They are in the process of gathering information on the player.".

Yunus Akgun could be an unreal back-up option for Liverpool.

Akgun is almost certainly a player that most Liverpool fans won't have heard of.

The Turkiye international is a 26-year-old winger who contributed to 21 goals across all competitions for Fenerbahce last term.

He's also had previous experience of English football as he spent the 2023-24 campaign on loan to Leicester in the Championship.

Unfortunately, his time in England didn't pan out as well as he would have hoped as he only contributed to six goals in the 29 games that he featured in.

Obviously, since most of his career has been spent in Turkiye, he probably wouldn't be able to get straight into Iraola's starting XI but, since he can play as a number 10 or a winger on either side, he could be an extremely useful back-up option for Liverpool to have.

These links are all just rumours at the moment but, of all the players who have been recently linked with Liverpool, Akgun feels like a really interesting deal for the Reds to explore, especially since they still need a back-up number 10 for Florian Wirtz.
