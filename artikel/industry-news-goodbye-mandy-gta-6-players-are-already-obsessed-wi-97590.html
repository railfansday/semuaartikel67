As Grand Theft Auto 6 began releasing around the world last week, players rapidly became obsessed with Mandy. She's a neighbor of Jason's who tries to seduce the himbo while he fixes her sink. (And she is totally not a serial killer, no way, why would you ask?) Mandy reveals up early in GTA 6. But as more players advance in the game, they're becoming obsessed with a upcoming character: Vicki, a standout character from Chapter 2.

Industry observers note that [Ed. note: Light spoilers for the end of Chapter 1 and beginning of Chapter 2 follow. We'll keep them vague.].

According to recent reports, after Jason is injured at the end of Chapter 1, Lucia takes him to a run-down motel to lie low. She wants to find stronger painkillers for him, which leads her to Vicki. She's a goth exotic dancer who also moonlights as a small-time drug dealer, and immediately pulls one over on Lucia by selling her over-the-counter Zombix (GTA's brand of painkiller) for $50. Lucia then barges back into Vicki's room and demands the drugs she paid for, which takes the two on a trip around Port Gellhorn. They get Vicki's paycheck from Delights Cabaret and save her friend Dunk from a gang, all the while getting to know one another. They bond over the fact that they're both moms trying to support their kids by whatever means necessary.

Recent findings suggest that players have quickly fallen for Vicki. "Love her she's my favorite character so far," one player wrote on Reddit.

"Punk Ana de Armas," another mentioned, and, you know, I can kinda see it.

Vicki is voiced by Taryn Manning, who's best known for her role in Orange Is the New Black. She's also starred in 8 Mile, Hustle and Flow, and Hawaii Five-0.

Sources indicate that manning's work as Vicki is part of the reason why players are flocking to her. "Mandy didn't do it for me, but Vicki did because I recognized Taryn Manning’s voice immediately," one Redditor said.

"Vicki the baddie that made everyone move on from Mandy lmao," another remarked.

Sources indicate that basically, you can tell how far thirsty GTA 6 players are getting in the game based on who they're obsessively posting about over on the Grand Theft Auto subreddits. In a few days, as players continue to marathon Rockstar's hit, I'm sure they'll have a newly introduced favorite.
