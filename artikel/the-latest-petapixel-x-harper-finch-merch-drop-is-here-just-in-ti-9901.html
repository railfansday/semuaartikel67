It has been revealed that this past spring, PetaPixel partnered with photography t-shirt brand Harper Finch to bring you a limited-edition set of co-branded merch. It proved very popular, and we’re happy to announce that we’ve dropped round two! Okeh, let’s talk about bokeh!

In a recent development, as a recap, Harper Finch is a curator of some excellent photography-inspired shirts, which is what put them on PetaPixel‘s radar earlier this year. As viewers of the PetaPixel YouTube channel and The PetaPixel Podcast may have noticed, Chris Niccolls, Jordan Drake, and I have been sporting some of the brand’s designs for the past couple of months and even previewing some of the designs from this drop more recently.

This drop is headlined by bokeh! Fans of the show will most certainly recognize Chris’s phrase, “Okeh, let’s talk about bokeh,” from many of his lens reviews, and now you can wear those words on your chest in two designs! This drop also debuts a new color option: natural! All of the t-shirts in this drop are available in this new color option.

Both bokeh-themed designs are also available as white hoodies.

We also worked with the team at Harper Finch to create a series of camera team designs! Canon, Nikon, Sony, and Fujifilm are some of the most popular camera manufacturers, and now you can rep your favorite team: red, yellow, orange, and green! Chris likes to rock Team Orange, while Jordan and I rep Team Yellow and Team Green.

All of these designs are available in either white or natural t-shirt colors and as white hoodies.

Breaking from our last drop, some of these options will remain as permanent fixtures in our shop and all of them will be available through the holiday season. They make great gifts for the photographer in your life (or if you like to stuff your own stockings). Make sure to check the handy size chart, but Harper Finch also has a great 30-day return policy and support if something doesn’t fit.

Industry observers note that we are really excited about this drop, and we hope you like what we’ve created. But that’s not all: keep a close eye on the store during Black Friday weekend! While the merch already reflects the most discounted price we’ll ever offer it for, we do have some surprises in store for November 27 through 29.
