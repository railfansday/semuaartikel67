The H3 Podcast could be preparing to return without Ethan Klein, after his estranged wife Hila reunited with members of the show’s production team.

In a recent development, in October, Hila Klein accused husband Ethan Klein of having an affair lasting more than six months. As a result, members of the H3 team expressed their support for Hila, announcing that they would not work with Ethan and that the podcast was on “indefinite hiatus.”.

In a recent development, ethan later revealed that he would give up ownership of the podcast. Now, in the first post since their initial response, it appears the H3 team have reunited with Hila to continue work.

On Friday, November 13, the official H3 Instagram page shared a photo in which members of the crew, along with Hila, could each be seen posing thoughtfully with interlaced fingers.

The photo was not accompanied by a caption, but many have taken it to be evidence that the H3 team will continue work on a latest version of the podcast without Ethan, who was missing from the photo.

A post shared by The H3 Podcast (@h3_podcast).

Notably absent from the photograph is Harley Morenstein, best known as the founder of Epic Meal Time and a recurring personality in H3 productions, as well as Tamara Joseph, a key member of the production team.

Sources indicate that some fans on Reddit have speculated that Harley may have been frozen out due to his friendship with Ethan, while others have guessed that Tamara may be involved in some way. At the time of writing, no official information has yet been released about the reasons for their absence.
