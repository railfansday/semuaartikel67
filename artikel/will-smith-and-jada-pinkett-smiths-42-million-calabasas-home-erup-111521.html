Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith’s $42 Million Calabasas Home Erupts in Flames

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith's $42 million Los Angeles property went up in flames after their shed caught fire.

The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department and the Malibu/Lost Hills Sheriff’s Station responded to a 911 call at the property in the early hours of Nov. 23.

Officials told People that they arrived to discover a "structure fire" at the couple's Calabasas home. They added that no one was hurt.

“It appears a shed caught fire, and the cause is believed to be accidental,” the statement from the sheriff's department revealed.

In audio from the 911 call obtained from Page Six, firefighters can be heard saying, "We do have a shed on fire with no spread to the brush.".

An emergency personnel noted that they were already "on scene," and then another firefighter explained that the fire had been "downgraded to a structure.".

The couple's Calabasas property serves as their primary residence. Set on 150 acres, the estate—which they began working on in 1997, the same year they tied the knot—features high-end amenities, including a basketball court, a tennis court, and a massive swimming pool.

The property was designed by architect Stephen Samuelson, who teamed up with Smith and Pinkett Smith in 1997, after they were inspired by the work he had done on the home of Carol Burnett, according to Architectural Digest.

According to recent reports, however, it was to be a labor of love for the couple—who had to wait four years to secure the permits to begin construction on their dream home. It would sit at the center of an enormous property that they had amassed over time by buying up adjacent plots of land as they became available.

In the end, Smith and Pinkett Smith created an idyllic Mediterranean-inspired sanctuary for their family, boasting all manner of luxury from a meditation lounge and a recording studio to a sunken trampoline.

In a recent development, it was previously believed that Smith and Pinkett Smith were living apart, with the actress revealing during a previous "Today" interview that she had moved into a new abode in the Los Angeles area. She did not reveal any other details about the property.

The fire comes after Smith, 58, and Pinkett Smith, 55, were spotted in a rare outing alongside their children, Jaden, 28, and Willow, 26.

Last year, the couple put one of their many homes on the market for the relatively low price of $2 million—amid rampant speculation over the state of their marriage.

Many fans have been questioning the status of their relationship since 2022, when Smith infamously slapped Chris Rock across the face during the Oscars ceremony after the comedian made a joke about Pinkett Smith's hair.

Then, just over a year later, Pinkett Smith sensationally revealed during an interview on the "Today" show that she and Smith had been living "completely separate lives" since 2016, despite insisting that they had no looks to legally divorce.

In the two years since that candid confession, questions have continued to be swirl about the truth of their relationship, with Smith sending the rumor mill into overdrive again in February when he put on a very raunchy display with singer India Martínez during an on-stage performance of their song, "First Love.".

Following that public display, the "Men In Black" actor pushed forward a latest album, titled "Based on a True Story," marking the first time he has rolled out a new record in years.

According to Smith, the album is the result of "self-examination" that began after the Oscars incident, which saw the actor banned from all academy events for 10 years.

"I’ve taken the last couple of years to really do a deep dive on the parts of me that may or may not been in that level of certainty and asking those deep scary internal questions," he told the Associated Press.

In a recent development, "It really is the result of my initial self-examination. Every song is about some part of myself that I discovered or wanted to explore, something I wanted to share. It’s the most full musical offering that I’ve ever created.".
