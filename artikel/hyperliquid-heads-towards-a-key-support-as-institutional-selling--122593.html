Hyperliquid’s declining open interest, negative funding and weak CVD highlight bearish momentum, putting $50 in focus.

As the crypto market consolidates ahead of the US PCE rates, Hyperliquid and the other cryptos plunge below their respective support levels. HYPE price began the day’s trade below the $60 support level and is approaching a crucial price range after an extended decline. On the other hand, recent movements by some of the biggest players in the crypto space have drawn attention to HYPE’s weakening structure. Right now, the price is trading around $56.5, with a 10% decline in the past 24 hours exposing the rally towards the crucial support at $50.

In a recent development, hyperliquid is facing growing scrutiny as two major crypto market participants, Abraxas Capital and Wintermute, make significant moves involving HYPE tokens and derivatives positions. Abraxas Capital reportedly withdrew its remaining funds from Hyperliquid after generating $552.49 million in profits from $12.49 billion in perpetual futures trading volume, according to Lookonchain.

According to recent reports, before the withdrawal, the firm sold 219,081 HYPE tokens worth nearly $13.25 million and purchased 4,230 ETH valued at $7.09 million. Adding to concerns, Wintermute reportedly requested the instaking of 150,000 HYPE tokens worth approximately $9 million, with the tokens expected to become available on December 01.

The market maker also reportedly holds a $3.2 million short position in HYPE perpetual futures, which was already in profit at the press time. Together, these developments highlight changing positioning among major market participants as HYPE struggles to recover from its recent decline.

In a recent development, HYPE is showing a clear bearish structure on the daily chart, with the token trading at approximately $56.72 on November 25 after declining 5.28% during the session. The decline has pushed the price towards a key support region around 54–55, which previously acted as a consolidation zone between May and June. HYPE has been forming lower highs and lower lows since its October peak near $98. The token has also slipped below the $60 psychological level, which now acts as immediate resistance.

It has been revealed that the daily RSI stands at 27.40, entering oversold territory and reflecting intense selling momentum. However, this does not confirm a reversal, particularly as the broader price structure remains bearish. The volume profile also shows considerable historical trading activity around $60 to $76, making this an important overhead supply region during any recovery attempt. Meanwhile, the derivatives chart provides additional evidence of weakening market participation.

The combination of falling open interest, negative funding and declining spot and futures CVD suggests that bearish positioning and selling activity are dominating the current market structure.

The immediate support zone lies around $54 to $55. A sustained breakdown below this region could expose the Hyperliquid (HYPE) price to further losses towards the psychologically vital $50 level.

On the upside, buyers need to reclaim $60 to $61 to signal an initial recovery. A stronger move above this region could open the way towards $70, although the $76 to $77 region remains a significant overhead resistance zone.

Sources indicate that for now, the technical outlook remains bearish. While the oversold RSI leaves room for a relief bounce, the derivatives data suggests that buyers have yet to regain control.
