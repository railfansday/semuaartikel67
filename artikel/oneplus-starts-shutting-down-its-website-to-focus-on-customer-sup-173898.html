OnePlus starts shutting down its website to ‘focus on customer support’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After shutting down its US operations earlier this year, OnePlus has quietly closed its website in some regions as it shifts to “focus on customer support.”.

OnePlus revealed in July that it would end new product introduces in the US and Europe. Just weeks later, the OnePlus 15 and other products the brand was selling in the US were sold out for good and, more recently, OnePlus rolled out its Android 17 patch to transition devices to Oppo’s ColorOS instead of OxygenOS.

Now, OnePlus has fully pulled the plug on its US website, as well as some other regions.

oneplus.com/us has purged all product listings, including for accessories, with a new message saying that the brand is “streamlining” the site:.

Following our July 16, 2026 announcement, OnePlus is streamlining this website to focus on customer support for existing OnePlus devices. For warranty, repair, and other support, please visit our support site Click.

The same applies to the OnePlus website in the Canada, and other regions, though the UK and EU countries such as France still have an operational site that mostly push Oppo products over remaining OnePlus devices, a practice that started a few months ago prior to the shutdown. The OnePlus 15, for example, is no longer available to purchase in the UK at all. Presumably, though, the OnePlus website will shut down in these regions eventually as well.

The OnePlus website in India is unaffected and still selling devices.

According to recent reports, the warranty website OnePlus links to appears to be more than a bit out of date, with a focus on 2023’s OnePlus Open. The most recent device mentioned is the OnePlus 13, though the page has been updated based on what we’re able to see on the Wayback Machine.
