The American People Deserve a Better Deal.

Washington, DC — Today, the Senate passed the “Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026” following little debate and an insufficient amendment process. BAAJA will now go to the House of Representatives for consideration.

In response, Sierra Club Executive Director Loren Blackford released the following statement:.

Sources indicate that “While a rogue president is waging a war on clean energy, the Senate rushed a deeply flawed bill that furthers the buildout of fossil fuels, strips away power from communities and Tribes, threatens our clean water and public lands, and endangers wildlife. The House of Representatives has an equal voice in the legislative process. They must reject the Senate’s fast-tracked bill and demand debate and consideration to deliver for the people, not corporate polluters and Donald Trump. The American people made a clear call merely weeks ago: our elected leaders must address soaring costs and a corrupt system that prioritizes corporate polluter profits over our health, our wallets, and our future.

“The Sierra Club thanks the Senators, Tribes, and advocates across the country who raised their voices in the fight to fix this bill. Americans deserve a better deal.”.
