Arion Kurtaj, a member of the Lapsus$ cybercrime group who leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022 and hacked other major companies, pleaded guilty on Monday, according to the UK’s Crown Prosecution Service (CPS).

Kurtaj, who is now 21, hacked BT/EE sometime between August and November 2021, and starting in early November of that year, the manufacturer “noted a marked increase in unauthorised SIM swaps of their customers mobile phones.” He was charged for those offenses and moved to a hotel for his “own protection” in September 2022, but “breached his bail conditions to carry out further hacks on major companies using a mobile phone, the hotel TV’s Wi-Fi and a firestick,” including hacks on Rockstar Games, Uber, and Revolut, the CPS says.

He was sentenced to life in a secure hospital in December 2023 because of his autism, but in January, Kurtaj was deemed fit for trial and moved to prison, the BBC reports.

Kurtaj is set to be sentenced on November 23rd. GTA VI will be released on November 19th.
