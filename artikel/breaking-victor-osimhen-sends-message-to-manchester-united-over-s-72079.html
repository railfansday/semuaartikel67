Galatasaray striker Victor Osimhen would be open to joining Manchester United next summer, according to reports.

United are expected to be in the market to sign a upcoming striker within the next two transfer windows due to their shortage of options in attack.

Mauricio Pochettino arrived at United this week with Benjamin Sesko sidelined until next year due to a shin injury, while Joshua Zirkzee has scored just 11 goals in 85 appearances since his £36.5 million move to Old Trafford in 2024.

Osimhen, meanwhile, is also out of action after undergoing knee surgery earlier this month but Galatasaray are hoping that the Nigeria international will return before the end of December.

The 27-year-old is in his third season with Galatasaray after the Turkish club made his loan move permanent for €75m (£64.8m) last year.

Osimhen has thrived during his time in Turkey, scoring 69 goals in 84 appearances for Galatasaray.

According to The Sun, Osimhen is keen on playing in the Premier League before the end of his career and the striker would be open to joining United if the opportunity arises.

The report claims that Osimhen’s camp have been made aware of United’s renewed interest.

‘Of course the interest really flatters him but he’s a loyal guy so it will be up to United to move hard for him,’ a source close to Osimhen told the newspaper.

Osimhen also came close to joining Chelsea in the summer of 2024 but instead waited 12 months to sign Joao Pedro in a £60m deal from Brighton.
