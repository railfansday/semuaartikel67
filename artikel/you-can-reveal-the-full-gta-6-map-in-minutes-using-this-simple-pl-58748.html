Clear up that fog in no time at all, but be careful.

GTA 6 has a huge map, which is around twice as major as the one found with GTA 5. That's a whole lot of Florida-inspired strip malls and alligator-soaked swamps. However, fresh players start the game in a fairly remote region, based on the Florida Keys, and the vast majority of the map is unavailable for perusal.

Sources indicate that fog is obscuring every part of the map that you haven't explored yet. New regions get uncovered as you travel through them. You could do this the long way, by following the story. You could also do this the slightly-shorter way, by grabbing a car and just driving north until the wheels fall off. GTA 6 doesn't have a system in place that automatically ups the wanted level as you enter new areas, so that's good.

Sources indicate that however, the quickest way to unlock the full map is found in the skies, and not the roads. Long-time players know the drill. Jason needs to get an airplane.

In a recent development, luckily, there's a plane just a few miles north of Jason's first house in Key Lento. Take a break from making home renovations and head to Southpoint Airfield, as seen in the map above. It's basically just north of the starting area.

Sources indicate that drive the western bridge that runs over the Shoeless Keys and then head west a little bit onto a runway. It's hard to miss. The area is marked as restricted, but it's mostly abandoned so there shouldn't be any armed onlookers.

It has been revealed that once at the airfield, look for a duster plane just waiting to be flown. Hop in and do your thing. Planes move incredibly fast and will swiftly unlock all of those foggy areas. Just fly carefully and keep an eye on fuel.

According to recent reports, you probably won't be able to get the whole map done on a single fly through, due to that fuel, but planes automatically regenerate at airfields as soon as one leaves. Fly back to Southpoint and start again, this time heading in another direction. You'll have the whole map uncovered in minutes.

In a recent development, happy travels. The only real thing to worry about here is the Mile Zero Air Station at the bottom left of the map. Flying over that airspace will instantly generate a four-star wanted rating. This wanted level can bring police after you in helicopters, so airplanes aren't necessarily safe. Save this area for last and exit as soon as the fog lifts from the map. Mile Zero is home to some really fast aircraft, but it's likely not worth the trouble just yet.
