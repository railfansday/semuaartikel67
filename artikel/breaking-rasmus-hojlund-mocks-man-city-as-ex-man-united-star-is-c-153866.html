By LEWIS BROWNING, SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 17:58 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 19:15 EST, 24 November 2026.

Former Manchester United forward Rasmus Hojlund aimed a dig at Manchester City after scoring against them for Napoli in the Champions League.

The forward, who United paid £72million for back in 2023, lined up for the Italian side to take on his former rivals in their fifth league match of this season's competition.

He was yet to score in Europe this campaign for a Napoli side who had been struggling to even reach the play-off qualification spots, but that all changed on Tuesday night.

Hojlund opened the scoring after half-time of the 2-2 draw when he fired low past Gianluigi Donnarumma to condemn City to a 10th game in a row conceding a goal.

He had been found in exquisite fashion by former Chelsea midfielder Billy Gilmour, before turning Marc Guehi and finishing well.

And his celebration caught the eye, as he held up one finger and appeared to shout 'one team in Manchester' in the direction of the home fans.

Former Manchester United forward Rasmus Hojlund taunted Manchester City fans after scoring against them on Tuesday.

Hojlund did well to take on a pass from Billy Gilmour before firing low into the goal.

After the game, he confirmed he did indeed jibe at Enzo Maresca's side but insisted it was all in good faith.

'Yeah, it's good banter,' he said on TNT Sports. 'There's a rivalry between Manchester City and United and I have a past with United.

According to recent reports, 'That's my club. Nothing against Manchester City. It was good to score here.'.

The strike was Hojlund's third goal of the season in 11 games as he continues to try and find his feet again after a disastrous spell in England.

Having arrived from Atalanta as a 20-year-old three years ago, there were hopes he would be the man to lead the Red Devils' forward line for years to come.

In his first season, he scored 17 goals in all competitions, including 10 in the Premier League, but things went downhill rapidly.

He would only score another nine goals for the club before being shipped out on loan to Napoli halfway through last season. In total, he netted 26 times for United in 95 games.

In the summer, he joined Napoli permanently, with the Italians coughing up £38m - a £34m loss for United, who are 19th in the Premier League.

It has been revealed that it was another disappointing result for City overall, with Maresca admitting to TNT Sports at full-time: 'It is quite clear we are conceding too many goals. It is something we need to adjust, and we are able to adjust that.'.
