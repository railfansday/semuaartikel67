Bitcoin Price Prediction: Next Rally Timeline, Is $100K Coming or Is a Drop Next?

🌐 Source: Coinpedia • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (462 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Bitcoin posted a small bounce on Thursday, and the chart now hints at a move toward $66,000 next.
  • The bear market is not over yet, and no major low has been confirmed this year.
  • Bitcoin is deeply oversold, negative momentum on the MACD is fading, and a bullish MACD crossover could appear soon.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin posted a small bounce on Thursday, and the chart now hints at a move toward $66,000 next. US stock markets are shut for Thanksgiving, so trading volume could stay thin. In recent weeks, thin markets have produced some of Bitcoin’s wildest swings.

The Bigger Picture Is Still Bearish.

The bear market is not over yet, and no major low has been confirmed this year. The short-term picture has changed, though. For the first time since the decline began at the September high, there are signs that a local low may be in place. At every earlier stage of the sell-off, Bitcoin barely reacted and never tried to break resistance.

Selling pressure is also easing. Bitcoin is deeply oversold, negative momentum on the MACD is fading, and a bullish MACD crossover could appear soon. A similar setup in June did not mark the final bottom, but a local low did form there.

Sources indicate that in the bearish case, Bitcoin’s rebound has already peaked as a weak three-leg bounce, and a latest five-leg decline has started. The first leg down is done. A short relief bounce would come next, then a sharper third leg lower. That route points to roughly $40,000 or even lower.

Path 2: A Wider Bounce Toward $90,000.

It has been revealed that in the bullish case, Bitcoin stays in a broad range and the current bounce runs further than expected. Recovery moves like this are often messy and hard to read. If it extends, Bitcoin could climb toward a long-term trendline around $90,000 or higher.

The key decision zone sits between $66,000 and $79,358. How Bitcoin trades there will likely decide which path plays out.

What the Short-Term Chart Reveals.

From the November low, Bitcoin first rose in three legs, helped partly by a rally in Nvidia. It made a higher high and broke above about $62,100, then slipped back into its range in another three-leg move.

The latest climb looks stronger. It now resembles a clean five-leg advance with no sign of a top yet, much like the recent move in S&P 500 futures. If that holds, a short pullback could come over the next few sessions before the next push higher. A move like that could also give altcoins a short-term lift.

Recent findings suggest that not yet. Even the most bullish near-term path points to around $90,000, and that would still count as a bounce inside a bear market. Before a run toward $100,000 can come into view, Bitcoin first needs to hold a higher low, break $62,100 and then clear the $66,000 to $79,358 zone. There is no clear timeline for that. The next few sessions will show whether the bounce has real strength.
