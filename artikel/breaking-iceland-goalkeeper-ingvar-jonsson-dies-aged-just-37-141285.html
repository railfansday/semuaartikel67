Iceland goalkeeper Ingvar Jonsson, who was in the famous Euro 2016 squad, has passed away at the age of just 37.

Back in the summer of 2016, Iceland surprised the football world as they managed to reach the quarter-finals of Euro 2016, which was held in France.

In the round of 16, Iceland were up against an England team packed with quality players, but pulled off a famous 2-1 win, with Ragnar Sigurdsson and Kolbeinn Sigthorsson scoring to cancel out Wayne Rooney’s opener.

At the time, Jonsson was an unused substitute as Iceland were eventually knocked out 5-2 by France, who then fell to a 1-0 defeat in the final against Cristiano Ronaldo’s Portugal. During the tournament, Iceland were also remembered for their fans' spectacular thunder clap celebration at the end of matches.

According to The Sun, Jonsson was diagnosed with cancer earlier in the year and sadly lost the battle this week, leaving behind his wife and three children.

On Thursday evening, Jonsson’s club, Vikingur Reykjavik, confirmed the 37-year-old’s death in a statement.

“It is with great sadness and deep sorrow that we announce that our dear friend and teammate Ingvar Jonsson, Víkingur’s goalkeeper, passed away on Wednesday, November 25, surrounded by his loved ones.

According to recent reports, “The thoughts of the club and all Víkingur are with his family and loved ones. We send them our deepest condolences.”.

Born in Keflavik, Jonsson began his football career at Njarovík, a club based in the Reykjanesbaer region of Iceland.

In 2011, Jonsson signed for Stjarnan, going on to win the Icelandic title for the first time in the club’s history; they did so by going the entire season without tasting defeat.

During what was a brilliant title-winning season in 2014, Jonsson played a huge role and was eventually voted as the Players’ Player of the Year.

As a result of his domestic success, Jonsson has also played in the Europa League and the Europa Conference League, with Stjarnan beating the likes of Motherwell and Lech Poznan before losing to Inter Milan in 2014.

After leaving Stjarnan ahead of the 2015-2016 campaign, he went on to play for IK Start, Sandnes Ulf, Sandefjord and Viborg before rounding off his career at Vikingur Reykjavik.

It has been revealed that on the international stage, Jonsson earned eight caps for Iceland, having previously played for the U19s and the U21s. His senior team debut came in November 2014, when he replaced Ogmundur Kristinsson against Belgium.
