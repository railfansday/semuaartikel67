Johnstone Burgh won the Scottish Junior Cup, now the Scottish Communities Cup, two seasons ago.

Scottish fifth-tier club Johnstone Burgh will no longer be forced to play two cup ties on the same day after one of the fixtures was postponed.

Johnstone were scheduled to play away to Tayport in the Scottish Communities Cup third round at 14:30 GMT and host Dunipace 65 miles away in the Lowland League Cup first round at 15:00 on Saturday.

However, the tie with Tayport has been called off because of a frozen pitch.

The double engagement came about after neither of Johnstone's opponents, nor any other clubs they were scheduled to play on other weekends, were willing to shift from Saturday afternoon kick-off times.

Two cup ties half an hour apart for Johnstone Burgh.
