Mauricio Pochettino’s whirlwind arrival as Manchester United’s fresh head coach has left little time to work out where he will live – but a key factor in that decision will be his beloved dog Sansa.

Pochettino will leave the TQL Stadium in Cincinnati after the USA’s game against Haiti in the early hours of Wednesday morning and go straight to the airport to catch an eight-hour flight to Manchester on board a private jet paid for by United.

The club will also provide him with somewhere to live as he settles into the newly introduced job and finds a more permanent residence.

The 54-year-old, who also has a home in Barcelona, is noted to have lived just outside London when he was Tottenham manager and then spent the entirety of his 18-month spell as Paris Saint-Germain boss in a luxury hotel.

Pochettino lived in Knightsbridge while he was in charge of Chelsea, and revealed that he enjoyed getting feedback from fans while out on his daily 90-minute dog walks with Sansa.

He named the Rhodesian Ridgeback after a character from Game of Thrones and has even given the dog her own Instagram account.

Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of the US national team for the final time in the early hours of Wednesday morning, then head straight to Manchester on a private jet.

Pochettino (pictured with his son Maurizio) will have to find accommodation which suits his dog Sansa, a Rhodesian Ridgeback (Source: Instagram/pochettino).

Describing the difficulty of Spurs playing on a Saturday night in 2018, he noted: ‘At 7pm, I am normally on my sofa with my dog Sansa watching the Spanish league because they start at 7.45pm, or France because Ligue 1 starts at 8pm, or Italy, or different games.

Sources indicate that ‘It will be so strange for my dog. Not for me but my dog. “Where is my dad?” she is going to say. She is beautiful and I am in love with her.’.

The living habits of United bosses have varied over the years. Sir Alex Ferguson, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer and Ruben Amorim favoured Wilmslow and Alderley Edge, David Moyes remained further afield in Lytham, Louis van Gaal and Erik ten Hag chose Hale, and Jose Mourinho famously spent 895 days at the Lowry Hotel in Manchester (where dogs can stay for an additional £30).

United have no prepares to change the timings for Wednesday’s training session, even though Pochettino and four of his backroom team may not arrive at Carrington until the early afternoon.

The private jet carrying the Argentine, first-team coach Miguel D’Agostino, strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya and analyst Alec Scott should touch down from Cincinnati around noon.

An advance party of three more coaches – Pochettino’s assistant Jesus Perez, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez, and his son and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino – have been in Manchester since Sunday after they were released early by the US Soccer Federation as a gesture of goodwill following the 6-0 first-leg win over Haiti in the quarter-finals of the North American equivalent of the Nations League.

They took training on Tuesday as United’s players continue to report back from international duty, and will also oversee the session on Wednesday morning after United decided not to reschedule it so Pochettino could be present.

The Argentine will be firmly hands-on from Thursday, though, as he prepares for his first game against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

Pochettino, who will speak to club media shortly after his arrival before conducting his first press conference on Friday, has faced Liverpool 19 times in his managerial career, losing 11 games, drawing five and winning three.

Pochettino's assistant Jesus Perez (right) has been in Manchester since Sunday and took training on Tuesday.

Strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya - the first woman to be part of United's backroom staff - will travel with Pochettino to Manchester following the Haiti game.

Pochettino will inherit a team in the relegation zone – which could even be bottom of the Premier League by kick-off on Sunday – and it’s understood he will be given modest funds to improve his squad in the January transfer window.

United are in desperate need of a left back and a striker, having failed to sign cover in both positions in the summer.

It has been revealed that one potential target may be US centre forward Folarin Balogun who has prospered under Pochettino but only started once for Monaco this season after a summer move to Everton fell through. United sources say the new head coach will have input in the weeks ahead before a clearer proposal emerges.

United also want a centre back after Confidential revealed last week that Fulham’s Calvin Bassey and Nathan Collins, of Brentford, are two options being considered.

It’s possible that one of those deals may have to be a loan, however, with United’s delicate financial situation set to be spelt out in black and white when the results from the first quarter of the financial year are released on Thursday.

Although Pochettino is going to have money for latest players, United will have to be smart in their transfer dealings and spend wisely.

In the short term, Luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford may be available again for the first time in more than a month against Liverpool.

However, there is a concern over Patrick Dorgu who was deemed to be unfit for international duty with Denmark, even though he returned to action for Michael Carrick’s final game after a month-and-a-half out with a hamstring injury and headed a stoppage-time equaliser in the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa.

Fulham's Calvin Bassey (right) is among the centre-back options United are considering.

Marcus Rashford may be available for the first time in more than a month against Liverpool.

It was the departure of Travis Binnion, rather than Carrick, that raised the most eyebrows among figures working in football over the past few days.

Agents and first-team players privately described their surprise at seeing Binnion – a long-time academy figure before stepping up to the first team to help Darren Fletcher as interim and then Carrick – leave the club.

United have cleaned house for the arrival of Pochettino’s backroom team, and Binnion was one of those who paid the price.

A popular figure in the dressing room, Joshua Zirkzee, Benjamin Sesko and Kobbie Mainoo are among the players who are known to have enjoyed working with Binnion.

Despite stepping up to the first team, where he would watch matches from the press box alongside United’s team of analysts, Binnion remained in contact with numerous academy player representatives to ensure their development was progressing as planned.

Binnion, who first arrived in 2019 as an Under 16s coach and progressed to the Under 18s, Under 21s and then head of player development and coaching, isn’t expected to be out of the game for too long though.

Recent findings suggest that agency sources believe rival Premier League academies will move rapidly to recruit him, with Everton seen as one potential landing spot. United’s former academy head Nick Cox is now Everton technical director and has already recruited Dave Hughes and Tom Curtis, who worked under Binnion at United.

Confidential pored over the first-team squad, as well as numerous rising stars in the academy, that Pochettino and his staff will promptly have to run the rule over.

While some of the voting went as expected – your votes demonstrated an overwhelming desire among the fanbase to see Noussair Mazraoui, Diogo Dalot, Shaw and Harry Maguire leave this summer – there were a few surprises.

In a recent development, many voted for captain Bruno Fernandes, who just recently rejected a second contract extension offer, to be moved on, along with Rashford. Amad Diallo and summer signing Andrey Santos are two who divide opinion, but both made the cut.

Confidential understands JJ Gabriel’s representatives held fresh talks with Real Madrid at the club’s training ground on Monday as a move to the Santiago Bernabeu edges ever closer.

Now that Gabriel has officially unveiled a partnership with Epic Sports Agency, his agent Ali Barat is leading negotiations and has been at Real’s Valdebebas headquarters along with the 16-year-old and his family.

Gabriel confirmed his departure from the club in an Instagram video on Tuesday night, as he and the Spanish giants hope to get a move over the line.
