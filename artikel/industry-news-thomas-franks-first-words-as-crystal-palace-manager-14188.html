Thomas Frank has been appointed as Crystal Palace’s new manager after Pierre Sage was sacked last week.

The Frenchman lost his job on Thursday after a disastrous, short-lived spell that dated back as soon as last summer, when he was selected to succeed Oliver Glasner at Selhurst Park.

The Eagles sit bottom of the Premier League table as a result of seven defeats in 10 games, with just two victories.

The south Londoners opted to make a change after their 3-1 home defeat by Liverpool, and they have plumped for the former Brentford and Tottenham head coach as their upcoming boss.

The Dane has signed a two-and-a-half year deal in south London, and he hailed his fresh side as “iconic” in his first words in his new position.

"It's an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic football club,” Frank told Palace’s club media upon his appointment announcement.

“I'm really, really looking forward to it.

Industry observers note that "Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I'm really looking forward to meeting them.”.

Frank’s first game in charge of his newly introduced side will come against Coventry City on Saturday, as the Eagles travel to Warwickshire looking for just their third victory of the campaign.

A home Europa League encounter with Real Sociedad will follow as the latest head coach takes his bow in front of the Selhurst support, and a first home league game follows next weekend, when newly-promoted Hull City travel to the capital.
