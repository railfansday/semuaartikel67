Replacing your office chair can be a daunting prospect, since finding and buying a high-quality chair from a well-known, trusted brand can be a costly endeavor. Not if you buy directly from LiberNovo during its Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale. One of the biggest, best-known, and reliable brands when it comes to ergonomic office chairs, and it is slashing the prices on its most popular lines starting on November 19.

According to recent reports, multiple models from LiberNovo’s popular Omni and Maxis ranges will be included in the upcoming sale, with select products available for as much as 42% off their MSRP. Black Friday will arrive early too at LiberNovo, with its sale prices kicking in at 6 pm PST on November 19, 2026, and running through until midnight PST on November 30, 2026, giving you plenty of time to pick up a new chair at a heavily discounted price.

Recent findings suggest that liberNovo offers six chair options split across their two world-renowned core models, Omni and Maxis, and five of those six options are included, and heavily discounted, in the brand’s Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale. All three Maxis models will be available for less, as will the Omni’s SE and Pro models. That includes LiberNovo’s powered models - the Omni Pro, Maxis Electric, and Maxis AirFlow - which are equipped with OmniStretch that moves the backrest between 5 mm and 50 mm continuously, and takes just 3.5 hours to fully charge without a cable.

According to recent reports, prices for LiberNovo chairs will start at just $609 during the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale, with the Omni SE’s price dropping from its usual $899 to that fresh, much lower price for a limited time. All Omni Pro bundles will enjoy deep discounts too, starting from $919, a significant drop compared to the $1,299 its basic bundle typically retails for, and all LiberNovo chairs carry a six-year frame and two-year component warranty.

The Maxis Manual, Electric, and Airflow models will all be included in the Black Friday/Cyber Monday sale. LiberNovo’s Maxis chairs are built for larger owners, designed with customers who are up to 200 cm tall and with a structure that can support up to 399 lbs (181 kg). All Maxis models have a seat depth of 52 cm, and headrests that adjust up to 140 mm vertically and 120 mm forwards and backwards.

Industry observers note that prices for the standard Maxis manual model will start at $849, down from $1,299, the Electric’s basic bundle is discounted all the way down to $1,099, usually $1,799, and the biggest and best saving of all can be found on the state-of-the-art Maxis Airflow, with prices starting from just $1299. That’s $560 cheaper than the Airflow typically retails for.

Industry observers note that there are even bigger discounts to be had at LiberNovo this Black Friday if you buy in bulk. Spend a total of $2,000 or more and you’ll save an additional $60, more than $3,000 and that saving goes up to $100, and purchases of $4,000 or more will have $180 knocked off the total. Those discounts will be automatically applied at checkout - no code needed.

Industry observers note that that’s still not all. Spending over certain thresholds at LiberNovo during its Black Friday/Cyber Monday sale will also net you some limited edition bundles. $800 or more and you’ll get a mousepad thrown in free of charge, up your spend to $1,000 and your purchase will come with the Eco Comfort three-piece gift set, and if your total is $1,200 or more, your purchase will come with LiberNovo’s inflatable neck pillow, eyemask, and noise-reducing earplugs. All orders are covered by 30-day price protection, no matter what you buy, and customers in the US can split the cost into four interest-free installments via Klarna.

It has been revealed that one final reminder that even though the sale is for Black Friday and Cyber Monday, you can start saving at LiberNovo far earlier. Discounts will be applied at 6 pm PST on November 19, and you’ll have until midnight on November 30 to make the most of them.
