Today, Team Cherry has announced the release date of its first DLC expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, and it's really good news, because it's completely free!

Here's what the developers had to say about the Sea of Sorrow reveal in their official blog post:.

"In Sea of Sorrow, players will voyage above and beneath Pharloom’s forgotten ocean, across islands, through forts and into deep caverns beneath the waves. We’ve spent the whole year stuffing this adventure full with all the things you’d expect from Hollow Knight: Silksong - crazed characters, high adventure, and epic bosses! It’s also overflowing with more exceptional music by the eternally talented Christopher Larkin! We’re pretty darn excited to get it into all of your hands on December 11, so please look forward to it.".

As mentioned earlier, Sea of Sorrow is a completely free expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong. Both existing owners and Xbox Game Pass subs will be able to jump in at no extra cost when it launches on December 11.
