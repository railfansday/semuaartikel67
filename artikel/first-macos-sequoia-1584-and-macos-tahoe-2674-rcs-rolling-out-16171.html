📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that in addition to macOS 27.3 beta 2, Apple is also rolling out the first Release Candidates for macOS Sequoia 15.8.4 and macOS Tahoe 26.7.4. Here are the details.

Two weeks ago, Apple released macOS Tahoe 26.7.3 and Sequoia 15.8.3, along with macOS 27.2.1 Golden Gate, all of which included security fixes related to AirPlay, Bluetooth, and Messages, among others.

Today, the company released the first RCs for macOS Sequoia 15.8.4 and macOS Tahoe 26.7.4, along with the second developer beta for macOS 27.3.

As is usually the case with direct-to-RC rollouts, Apple’s notes mention only that the updates provide ”security fixes for your Mac.” The actual release notes will likely become available soon after Apple officially releases macOS Sequoia 15.8.4 and macOS Tahoe 26.7.4 in the coming weeks.

The build for macOS Sequoia 15.8.4 RC 1 is 24H319, and the build for macOS Tahoe 26.7.4 RC 1 is 25G422.
