In Iceland, Ali Wong Listened to Björk’s Jazz Album and Covered Herself in Silica

🌐 Source: Cntraveler • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (354 words, 📖 Standard)

Industry observers note that “I was on tour in June and July for my fresh stand-up hour and managed to escape the crazy heat wave in Europe when I performed in Reykjavík, Iceland. It was my first time there, so it was quite exotic to experience the cold in July. (I live in LA, where it feels like it's getting hotter every summer.) There is a crispness that is so unique. I was with my family—my kids and my baby daddy—and Sheng Wang, who was opening all my shows. The landscape is so breathtaking. Just driving by geysers bursting and steaming out of the ground felt like witnessing magic. Whenever I travel my favorite thing is going to the biggest supermarket and buying any local snacks you can't get anywhere else. Our tour guide, Aron Karlsson, had taken us to the Bónus grocery store, where we got a ton of fish jerky and black licorice with marzipan, and we were snacking on them on our way to the Blue Lagoon while listening to Björk's jazz album, Gling-Gló. (A very fun part of being in Iceland is passing by every stranger and asking yourself, Is that Björk? Is that Björk? Please be Björk.) Then we got there and went straight into this opaque, milky-blue water; it was kind of like being in a luxurious version of that cloudy bath in Minority Report that [Samantha Morton] lies in. There was a mask bar in the lagoon, where you can apply a silica mud mask, an algae mask, or a mineral mask to your face, and it all felt very thick, goopy, and smooth on the skin. We're Asian and all have eczema, so we experienced a lot of relief afterward. It was very cute seeing families with members of all ages, including my children, wading around the lagoon together with spa masks on their face. Then Sheng—and this is very Sheng—started harvesting the silica off the walls and putting it on his face. Since it was free, we all copied him. I'm not sure if that was good for us or not.”.
