Man Utd manager debuts ranked since Sir Alex Ferguson: Pochettino next

After replacing Michael Carrick in the dugout, Mauricio Pochettino starts his reign as Manchester United head coach with a game against Liverpool at Anfield.

Pochettino is set to become the 12th manager to take charge of a United game since Sir Alex Ferguson retired at the end of the 2012/13 season.

We’ve taken a look at how his predecessors fared in their first competitive game in charge of United, ranking them from worst to best.

Following the appointment of Van Gaal and six wins out of six in pre-season, United fans were full of optimism ahead of the 2014/15 campaign.

But the mood changed just 28 minutes into the season when Ki Sung-yueng gave Swansea City the lead at Old Trafford.

Wayne Rooney acrobatically levelled for United early in the second half, before Gylfi Sigurdsson fired home to give the visitors all three points.

In a recent development, “When you have preparation, win everything, then lose your first match, it cannot be worse,” Van Gaal stated. “We have built up a lot of confidence. It will be smashed because of this.”.

It was also the first time United had lost at Old Trafford on the opening day of the season since a 3-2 defeat against Ipswich Town in 1972.

Eight years after Van Gaal’s United debut, Ten Hag followed in the footsteps of his compatriot by losing 2-1 at home in his first game in charge.

United began the 2022/23 season by welcoming Brighton to Old Trafford and Ten Hag’s team news raised a few eyebrows, with Christian Eriksen debuting in the false nine role and Cristiano Ronaldo named on the bench.

They were outplayed by the Seagulls in the opening period and Pascal Gross’ first-half double saw them trailing 2-0 at the interval.

Ronaldo was brought on in the second half and Alexis Mac Allister’s own goal halved the deficit, but United were unable to find an equaliser.

Despite wanting to stay at Sporting CP until the end of the 2024/25 season, Amorim ultimately agreed to take over at United in November 2024.

His first game was against Ipswich Town at Portman Road and it got off to a perfect start, with Marcus Rashford scoring inside the opening two minutes.

But the hosts were the better side for the rest of the first half, and Omari Hutchinson scored the equaliser just before the break.

United dominated possession in the second half but didn’t create many chances, and they needed a brilliant save from Andre Onana to prevent Ipswich from taking all three points.

One of five former United players on this list, Fletcher was placed in caretaker charge following the decision to sack Amorim in January 2026.

His first game saw United face Burnley at Turf Moor, and he immediately moved away from the Portuguese coach’s 3-4-3 set-up in favour of a back four.

But the change in formation didn’t have an immediate impact, and they fell behind in the 13th minute when Ayden Heaven turned into his own net from a Bashir Humphreys cross.

Benjamin Sesko netted a brace in a 10-minute spell at the start of the second half, before the hosts drew level with a brilliant strike from Jaidon Anthony.

Shea Lacey hit the crossbar in the final stages as United dominated proceedings, but they were unable to find a winner.

Rangnick agreed a six-month deal to become United’s interim manager in November 2021, but work permit issues delayed his arrival.

The German, who is renowned for a high-pressing style, was finally in the dugout for Crystal Palace’s trip to Old Trafford at the start of the following month.

His influence was clear in a breathless opening 30 minutes as United pressed from the front and played at a noticeably quicker tempo.

Their performance petered off after that strong start, but a 77th-minute strike from Fred gave United a fully deserved 1-0 victory.

“With just the one training session, I was really impressed,” Rangnick mentioned after the game. “We did much better than expected.”.

The first competitive game in the post-Ferguson era saw Moyes lead United out at Wembley to face Wigan in the 2013 Community Shield.

United were strong favourites against a side that had just been relegated from the Premier League, and Robin van Persie headed home the opening goal inside the opening six minutes.

Van Persie’s deflected strike doubled United’s lead on the hour mark, and that signalled a comfortable end to proceedings.

The celebrations were mooted, and it proved to be both the first and last piece of silverware that Moyes lifted at United.

Van Nistelrooy joined Ten Hag’s coaching staff in the summer of 2024 and took over as interim manager a few weeks later.

He delivered goals throughout his playing career at Old Trafford, and his first game in the dugout was a seven-goal thriller against Leicester City in the Carabao Cup.

Casemiro and Bruno Fernandes both netted braces in the last-16 tie, while Alejandro Garnacho also got on the scoresheet in a 5-2 home win.

Despite still being part of United’s first-team squad, Giggs was named interim manager after Moyes was sacked in April 2014.

His first match in the dugout saw United face Norwich City at Old Trafford, and he was met with a rapturous reception as he emerged from the tunnel.

But United started slowly, and the first half looked set to finish goalless until Danny Welbeck was felled in the area and Wayne Rooney converted a 43rd-minute penalty.

Rooney doubled their lead at the start of a much improved second half before Juan Mata came off the bench to score a brace of his own, giving United a comfortable 4-0 victory.

One of two Carrick entries on this list, the former United midfielder was initially placed in temporary charge after Ole Gunnar Solskjaer was sacked in November 2021.

He began his first spell as interim manager with an away game against Villarreal in the Champions League group stage, and David De Gea kept United in the game with two great saves either side of halftime.

Carrick’s substitutions helped change the flow of the game, and Cristiano Ronaldo broke the deadlock in the 78th minute before Jadon Sancho made it 2-0 in injury time.

The win against the Yellow Submarine saw United secure top spot in their Champions League group with a game to spare.

Despite winning the FA Cup final in 2016, Van Gaal was sacked by United and Mourinho was in charge when they returned to Wembley for the Community Shield.

Mourinho went into the game having lost his three previous Community Shields and United had to face the then-reigning Premier League champions Leicester City.

United started brightly in the traditional curtain-raiser, and Jesse Lingard added to his FA Cup final-winning goal with a stunning solo effort.

Jamie Vardy equalised at the start of the second half before Zlatan Ibrahimovic marked his competitive United debut by heading home the winner in the 83rd minute.

Mourinho’s third-season syndrome kicked in once again, and Solskjaer replaced him on an initial interim basis in December 2018.

His first game in the dugout saw him return to the Cardiff City Stadium, having had a 30-game spell in charge of the Welsh side in 2014.

Marcus Rashford fired United in front with a brilliant free-kick in the third minute, while goals from Ander Herrera and Anthony Martial gave the visitors a 3-1 lead at half-time.

A second-half brace from Jesse Lingard made it a comprehensive win as United scored five goals in a Premier League game for the first time since Ferguson retired.

Carrick and Solskjaer were both interviewed by United in January 2026, with the former ultimately being handed the job until the end of the season.

But he began his second spell in the Old Trafford dugout with a daunting first assignment: a Manchester derby.

The manager was still able to oversee an immediate improvement, and second-half goals from Bryan Mbeumo and Patrick Dorgu gave United a 2-0 win over Pep Guardiola’s Manchester City.

It was a thoroughly deserved victory for United, who also hit the woodwork twice and had three goals ruled out for offside.

According to recent reports, “It’s an easy thing to say but I couldn’t ask for any more from the boys,” Carrick remarked after the game. “They gave everything.

“We only had three days to prepare and they took everything on board, tactically, and to be able to deal with it emotionally was exactly as we hoped for.”.
