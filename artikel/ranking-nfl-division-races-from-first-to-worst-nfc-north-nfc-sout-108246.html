The NFC East might be the best example of the NFL's unpredictable nature. Earlier this season, it made history by becoming the first division to go winless over a two-week span in which every team played both weeks. Yes, that included the infamous Commanders-Giants tie that featured a fumble and an all-time clock mismanagement on the final two drives.

Then in Week 11, the division went 4-0 collectively, with the Eagles, Giants, and Commanders all springing upsets over AFC teams right now in the playoff field. A $10 NFC East parlay of the Eagles beating the Steelers, the Giants beating the Jaguars, the Commanders beating the Bengals and the Cowboys beating the Titans would have netted you a handsome $345.51.

As Al Pacino, playing Michael Corleone in The Godfather Part III, remarked: "Just when I thought I was out, they pull me back in!".

Ahead of a packed Thanksgiving week chock full of divisional matchups, we rank all eight division races, taking into account the quality of the teams involved, the quality of the races themselves and the potential for pandemonium.

It has been revealed that the NFC North's main quest is potentially being the first team to place all four teams in the postseason. But the division race itself is a thriller, especially with Lions-Bears looming on Thanksgiving. Yes, Detroit looks like the clear NFC North favorite for now, but a Chicago win on Turkey Day would boost the Bears' chance to win the division to 45%. Regardless, this race projects to end in a thriller: In Week 18, the Lions play the Packers, and the Bears play the Vikings.
