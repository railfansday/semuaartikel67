Athlete support under spotlight as South Africa swimmer switches to Netherlands

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (66 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

South Africa's sports minister remarked Tuesday he had ordered an investigation into the national swimming federation following the departure of the country's most decorated Olympic athlete, swimmer Tatjana Smith, for the Netherlands. In an emotional video on social media, the 29-year-old two-time Olympic gold medallist confirmed last week she would no longer represent South Africa at international events because of financial challenges.
