You can start stealing GTA 6 ATMs from about midway through Chapter 3 and it'll net you some serious cash, but it's a tricky job to carry out. Technically, you can steal ATMs far earlier with a winched vehicle, but don't try it until you've picked up the dye pack jammer - it's not worth it.Since there are a few nuances to ripping ATMs out of the wall in GTA 6, including actually getting the cash out of them, I'll explain how to steal an ATM and how to get a winch in GTA 6 below, so that you can carry out these jobs like a pro.

After completing the Credit Check mission in Chapter 3, you'll have possession of a GTA 6 Dye Pack Jammer, and only then should you consider stealing ATMs out of the wall. Do it beforehand, and you'll find the cash explodes, potentially destroying your vehicle, your clothes - and the cash itself. At the right time, though, stealing ATMs can be one of the best ways to make money fast in GTA 6.

According to recent reports, once you've got the dye pack jammer, you can freely start nabbing ATMs with a winched vehicle, but it's only those wall-mounted to the outside of buildings. If you've also completed the Yasmani's Jewelery side quests, he'll highlight three specific locations for you where your takings will be high, but you'll only have one in-game day to grab these ones. To do any of this, you need a winch. To get a winch, you need to take a suitable GTA 6 car, preferably some form of pick-up truck or large off-roader, to a mod shop. You can also steal a tow truck or other vehicle that's already got a winch, but it's probably easier to just buy one than to hunt around.

Recent findings suggest that if you're planning on doing this often, it's probably also worth registering your favorite truck to yourself and putting a winch on that, so that you can call it up whenever you want to hit a few ATMs. If you aren't sure how to do this, take a look at our separate guide to vehicle ownership in GTA 6.
