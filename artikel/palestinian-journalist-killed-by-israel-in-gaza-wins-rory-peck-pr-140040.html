Mariam Dagga shares award with two of her colleagues from AP for ‘powerful coverage’ of human cost of war.

Mariam Dagga, the Palestinian war correspondent who was killed at the age of 33 in Gaza in an Israeli strike, has posthumously won the prestigious Rory Peck prize for freelance video journalists, the organisers declared on Wednesday.

She shares the award with two of her colleagues from the Associated Press (AP), Abdel Kareem Hana and Jehad Alshrafi, “for their powerful coverage of the human cost of the war” in the Palestinian territory, the Rory Peck Trust noted.
