Consumers, oblivious of being targeted, find online ads helpful

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (54 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Most online shoppers know that ads strategically follow them across sites, a mechanism known as "ad retargeting." A newly introduced study led by Penn State researchers found that consumers find these targeted ads helpful, but explaining why they are seeing an ad can backfire by increasing concerns about being watched.
