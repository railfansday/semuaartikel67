A selection of matches in the Scottish Premiership have been rearranged for live television coverage in the fresh year.

Games involving Celtic, Rangers and Hearts in January and February 2027 have been given upcoming dates and kick-off times after being selected by broadcasters.

The match beween Falkirk and Hearts on Saturday, January 23, has been moved back to a later 5.45pm kick-off for live coverage on Premier Sports.

St Mirren v Celtic has been pushed back 24 hours to Sunday, January 24, with a 3pm kick off and will be shown live on Sky Sports.

Aberdeen will host Rangers under the Pittodrie lights on Friday, January 29, at 8pm after the match was brought forward at the request of Sky Sports.

Hearts will host St Mirren at Tynecastle on Saturday, January 30, live on Premier Sports, with a 5.45pm kick-off.

A full Premiership fixture card takes place on Wednesday, February 3, with the match between Motherwell and Celtic pushed back by 15 minutes.

The game at Fir Park will now start at 8pm in front of the Sky Sports cameras.

In addition, BBC Scotland has picked up the rights to show Greenock Morton v Partick Thistle with the game moved to Friday, January 22, kick-off 7.30pm.

BBC ALBA will also show Cove Rangers v Ross County with the match now taking place on Saturday, January 30 at 5.30pm.
