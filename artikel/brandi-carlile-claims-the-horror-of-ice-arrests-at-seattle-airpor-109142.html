📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The arrests of Sofia Hernandez Meija and her father, Ricardo Larrondo Mendo, by masked federal immigration officers as they boarded their flight at Seattle-Tacoma international airport on Sunday have renewed outrage and protests against Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

On Tuesday, Brandi Carlile took to social media to speak out against ICE and call those horrified by the administration’s policies to take action. “I, along with many of you, have seen the horrific video this week of a father and daughter being violently detained by ICE agents at the Seattle Airport. The horror and heartache I experienced watching this, hearing their cries of desperation and fear, cannot be overstated,” wrote the musician.

Carlile highlighted the upcoming Thanksgiving holiday: “Many of us will be doing the same in the coming weeks as we gather with those we love for the holidays. The ability to do so safely, to have a home we can return to, to have the freedom of movement and travel without fear is one we all deserve.” She added, “That right is being threatened across the U.S every day for immigrants and their families and we need to do something about it.”.

For those who cannot make a donation, Carlile asked them to contact your representatives at (202) 224-3121) and send the simple message to “Abolish ICE.”.

Earlier on Tuesday, Pearl Jam also called out ICE agents in a statement, writing, ““It is heartbreaking and infuriating to watch the human toll of a system driven by cold cruelty. Seeing a family torn apart and dragged off a jetway right here in our own airport by masked individuals is unconscionable and we stand firmly against this kind of inhumane treatment.”.
