No, REI does not have a Black Friday sale in 2026. The outdoor retailer will close all 190-plus store locations (while giving all employees a paid day) and encourages customers and members to #OptOutside for the day. REI did run its Holiday Sale from November 13 to 23 in which customers could score up to 60 percent off outdoor gear.

While those specific savings are over, there is still the brand’s deals and Outlet sections offering discounts. No word yet on whether there will be any Cyber Week savings next week (stay tuned as we get closer).

Do also keep in mind that REI will not process online orders on Black Friday either.

Recent findings suggest that almost everyone who loves the outdoors knows that REI is ground zero for gearing up for hiking, biking, kayaking, climbing, fishing—pretty much any type of adventure, really. REI Outlet is the retailer's dedicated discount section, a roundup of last season's gear and its famous flash sales. It's the single best place to shop if you're looking for the absolute best deals on the world's best outdoor brands. You'll find great deals year-round, but the deepest discounts are almost always during REI's holiday sale events. Don't forget to check back early and often as REI Outlet's inventory changes frequently. Sale prices can drop suddenly and things sell out fast!

REI offers many of its best perks and sale event access to REI Co-op members. Even if you're not a frequent REI shopper, it's worth the one-time $30 fee to join. REI members also receive a 10% dividend on most in-store and online purchases, as well as receiving discounts on gear rentals and during REI's best annual sale events (like the holiday sale). That $30 can easily pay for itself in just one visit!
