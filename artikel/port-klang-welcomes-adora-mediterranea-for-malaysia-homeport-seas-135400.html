The Adora Mediterranea has begun its first homeport season in Malaysia, marking Adora Cruises’ first overseas homeport program and Hwajing Travel’s latest initiative in bringing international cruise experiences to Malaysian shores.

Hwajing Travel & Tours said in a press release that over the past three consecutive years, Hwajing Travel has introduced five international cruise homeport deployments to Malaysia, building on previous charter programs with the Costa Serena in 2024 and the Piano Land in 2025.

Hwajing Travel added that the arrival of the Adora Mediterranea reinforces the tech giant’s commitment to expanding cruise choices and strengthening Malaysia’s position as a regional cruise departure hub.

Kenny Cheong, managing director, noted: “To have Adora Cruises entrust Hwajing Travel with its first overseas homeport deployment is a meaningful recognition of the experience and commitment we have built over the years.”.

The press release stated that the Adora Mediterranea combines Mediterranean-inspired design with international dining, live entertainment, themed bars, family-friendly facilities and immersive onboard activities.

The ship offers a varied holiday experience for families, multigenerational travelers, couples and first-time cruisers.

The ship will offer Muslim-friendly meals and a multilingual service team, including Mandarin-, English- and Malay-speaking crew.

The Adora Mediterranea arrives at Port Klang, Malaysia, for its first international homeporting season.

The Adora Mediterranea offers four itineraries from Port Klang. Eight sailings are scheduled from November 24 to December 14, 2026.

The itineraries range from short getaways to a longer one-way voyage to Guangzhou, China, with options for families, multigenerational groups and both first-time and returning cruisers.
