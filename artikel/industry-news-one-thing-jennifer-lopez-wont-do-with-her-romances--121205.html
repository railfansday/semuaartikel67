📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jennifer Lopez has been a household name for decades, thanks to her beloved romantic comedic, career as a global pop star and (of course) her headline-making personal life. From her relationship with Ben Affleck to current rumors she's dating Office Romance co-star Brett Goldstein, the public is very invested in her romantic world. Although she recently claimed there's something she won't do with her relationships moving forward. Let's break it all down.

Sources indicate that recently Jennifer Lopez joked about her divorces, showing that the 57 year-old icon has a good sense of humor about it all. Still, it sounds like she's changing the way she approaches future relationships. In a conversation on the Fresh York Times podcast, she shared how she wants to keep her romantic life more private moving forward. In the "Let's Get Loud" singer's words:.

It has been revealed that I’m guarded in the sense that I want to protect my loved ones, and I want things to be more sacred in my life. And I’ve learned lessons about being out there with relationships and stuff. That’s not something I ever want to do again.

Honestly, this makes a great deal of sense. Fans have been obsessed with JLo's love life for years now, making her personal life into a very public matter. Before her divorce from Affleck was finalized there were rumors about them being separated for months. That's to say nothing of her famous relationships with Alexander Rodriguez, Marc Anthony, and more.

Sources indicate that while in the podcast, the Selena actress spoke about what she's learned from her very public celebrity relationships. She went on to say:.

That’s not something I ever want to do again, as far as sharing that part of my life with people. It’s not that people won’t know. They’ll see me in the street, they’ll follow you. They’ll do this, and they’ll see you. But at the same time, it’s not something I want to comment on. It’s not something I’m inviting people into, it’s not something I’m putting on display. That’s not something I’m interested in anymore.

Honestly, I wouldn't blame her! She's made countless headlines and been photographed by paparazzi for decades now related to who she was dating and/or married to. Now that she's in a newly introduced stage in her life, some privacy might be exactly what Jennifer Lopez needs.

Fame is a double-edged sword, and JLo knows all too well how difficult it can be to have one's life under a microscope. She spoke more about the toll that it's taken over the years, saying:.

Sources indicate that I really feel this thing, that energy, whatever it is, this mechanism that exists this machine…. whatever we want to call that thing, is a destructive, hurtful machine, you know what I mean?

Perhaps it's because of this that we haven't gotten any official confirmation about Jennifer Lopez's rumored relationship with Brett Goldstein. Only time will tell if they're actually dating, but her comments might offer some context about why it's still firmly a rumor a this time.
