In as much as any derby can ever be a free hit, there was always a sense that Mauricio Pochettino’s first game as Manchester United manager wouldn’t matter too much; nobody expected them to win away to Liverpool. This was not a game on which anybody was going to judge him, and that feeling only magnified when Leny Yoro was sent off after eight minutes. United’s victory at Anfield last season was their first for a decade; what’s difficult to do with 11 men is only going to get harder playing a man down for 82 minutes. But that does not mean that it did not matter.

It has been revealed that although Roy Keane was determined to reject the suggestion, there was perhaps even some credit to be gleaned from having lost only 4-0. Certainly when they were 2-0 down after 13 minutes, there was reason for United to fear an embarrassment akin to their 7-0 defeat against Liverpool in 2023. Perhaps Pochettino deserves credit for his switch to a back three, which brought a degree of stabilisation, but United could easily have lost far more comfortably than they did. Liverpool had 18 shots in the game three-and-a-half years ago, eight of them on target; on Sunday they had 28, 11 of them on target. When you’ve conceded an xG of 4.38, there’s not much point quibbling over the details; you’ve been hammered.
