It is Brighton v Newcastle United up next.

Sunday afternoon on the south coast.

Fabian Hurzeler and his Seagulls taking on Matthias Jaissle’s Magpies.

Brighton v Newcastle United has never featured a Premier League away win for United.

In more than 35 years we have seen only one win for Newcastle at Brighton, a 2-1 Championship victory in the 2016-17 season, featuring that astonishing Mo Diame wonder goal!

Since the 1991-92 season there have only been 12 of these Brighton v Newcastle United matches but it is still not a place high on the expectation list for NUFC fans.

Which brings me to this one on Sunday….

Brighton are second top in the Premier League of course and whilst Newcastle United got that excellent 2-2 draw with Arsenal, Fabian Hurzeler and his team smashed them 3-0 at the Amex Stadium.

Brighton have a Premier League record this season of Played 11 Won 7 Drawn 1 Lost 3.

In all competitions, Brighton’s record is Played 19 Won 12 Drawn 3 Lost 4.

However, the very daunting stats only appear ahead of this Brighton v Newcastle United match when you look at how the Seagulls have got on home and away.

In their 11 away matches in all competitions, Brighton have won 5, drawn 2 and lost 4, scoring 17 goals and conceding 13.

In their 8 home matches (all competitions), Brighton have won 7, drawn 1 and lost none, scoring 27 goals and conceding only 1!!

In over three months, the 1-1 Premier League draw against Leeds on 5 September 2026 is the only time this season that Brighton at home have conceded a goal, failed to win and not managed to score at least two goals on home turf.

The challenge is on for Newcastle United.
