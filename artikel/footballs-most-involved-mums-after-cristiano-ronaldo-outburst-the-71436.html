Cristiano Ronaldo may have finally departed the international stage, but he still remained a major part of Portugal's story over the past week.

That came after they suffered a humiliating 4-1 defeat by Denmark in the first game since his retirement, their heaviest home loss in competitive history.

In the aftermath of that match, Ronaldo's mother Dolores released a furious and unprompted attack on the national team, claiming the defeat was just, and added she was happy to see the result.

Despite an emotional and historic tribute to Ronaldo pre-game, Dolores also expressed her unhappiness at what had unfolded, before she claimed the veteran's former international team-mates failed to show him the respect he deserved during his career.

Dolores has not always been an outspoken presence over the years, but has often stepped up to defend her son when she feels necessary.

So, in light of her outburst, Daily Mail Sport has taken a look at other examples of when the mums of footballers have got involved in their careers.

Cristiano Ronaldo's mum became embroiled in her son's row with Portugal's national team after saying she was happy to see them suffer a heavy defeat (Source: Instagram/doloresaveiroofficial).

Veronique Rabiot – Adrien Rabiot.

Adrien Rabiot's mother Veronique has developed a reputation as an uncompromising and fiercely protective negotiator in her role as his agent.

This has seen the French midfielder face several flashpoints in his career, beginning  when Veronique pulled him out of Man City's academy after just six months in 2008 because she felt they were not looking after his interests like they should be.

During his time at PSG, where Rabiot ultimately broke through and spent seven seasons in the first team, Veronique then frequently clashed with the club's hierarchy.

Potential transfers to Roma and Man United during his career have also both collapsed late on, a factor some reports attach to Veronique's role in the deals.

There have been several notable flashpoints on the international stage too, not least when she backed her son's controversial decision to withdraw from France's standby list for the 2018 World Cup.

But that was nothing compared to the aftermath of France's shock exit from Euro 2020 at the last-16 stage.

Veronique made headlines at the time for engaging in a heated confrontation with the parents of Kylian Mbappe and the entourage of Paul Pogba in the stands over on-pitch errors and perceived arrogance during Les Bleus' campaign.

Adrien Rabiot's mother Veronique (centre) has developed a reputation as a formidable force.

As the mother of one of the best footballers on the planet, Fayza Lamari was always likely to be a major powerbroker, and she has played a key role in Kylian Mbappe's career, acting as his chief negotiator and business manager.

This has seen Fayza oversee Mbappe's commercial obligations and image rights, while she played a central role in his dramatic contract extension at PSG in 2022.

Recent findings suggest that she was similarly crucial when Mbappe joined Real Madrid in 2024. On that occasion, she was credited for helping to secure a huge signing bonus and favourable image rights split for her son.

Fayza hasn't been afraid to publicly stand up for the forward, and she was key in the legal dispute over unpaid salary and bonuses that he felt he was owed by PSG after his exit.

PSG were ultimately ordered to pay Mbappe €60million (£52.5m) by a French court in a major victory for Fayza.

Kylian Mbappe's mum Fayza (also pictured with his brother Ethan) has played a key role in the commercial side of his career.

Sheila Ebana has been a foundational and supportive presence throughout Lamine Yamal's childhood and rise into professional football.

But, alongside regularly attending Yamal's games, Sheila has become a celebrity in her own right after embracing her public profile.

She regularly provides an insight into her life and shares behind the scenes clips with Yamal and his half brother Keyne on social media, racking up 2.8million followers on Instagram and 2.6million on TikTok.

Sheila has also regularly made paid appearances and attended VIP events, including a bizarre Christmas gala dinner that Daily Mail Sport went to in November 2025 where members of the public paid hundreds of pounds for a three-course meal, champagne, entertainment and a meet-and-greet.

To demonstrate how Sheila has embraced her fame, she once responded to online critics by retorting: 'I don't just think I'm famous - I am.'.

Lamine Yamal's mum (pictured below together with his half-brother Keyne) has embraced her celebrity fame.

Denise Bellingham – Jude Bellingham.

Jude Bellingham has often credited his mother Denise for helping to manage his life off the pitch and keep him grounded.

When he moved to Germany to join Borussia Dortmund as a 17-year-old, Denise went with him – before subsequently following the midfielder to Spain after his transfer to Real Madrid – while his father Mark stayed with his brother Jobe.

She is understood to handle his domestic affairs and finances, while Bellingham inadvertently revealed during the 2022 World Cup that she still made his bed.

Denise has generally provided a constant source of advice, logistical support – which has included driving him to training in the past – and organisational help throughout his time in the game.

The England star is clearly appreciative of all she has done, describing her as his 'queen' and 'backbone', and crediting her with helping to keep him level headed throughout the highs and lows of his career.

Jude Bellingham (pictured also with dad Mark and brother Jobe) has described his mum Denise as his 'queen' and 'backbone'.

Marcia Lewis – Myles Lewis-Skelly.

Myles Lewis-Skelly's mother Marcia Lewis has played a crucial role in his rise into Arsenal and England's first team.

When Lewis-Skelly entered the Arsenal academy system at the age of eight, Marcia wanted to learn more about football to ensure she could protect and guide her son's progression.

She subsequently qualified as an agent, meaning she now represents Lewis-Skelly, and also enrolled in a Master's degree in Football Business.

Marcia has since taken part in contract negotiations and filtered external pressures away from her son during his development.

She was later inspired to set up the No1Fan.club, a platform that aims to support families who have children in academy football.

The outlet is dedicated to helping navigate various challenges that arise, such as dealing with scouts, agents and the general pressures associated with youth football.

Myles Lewis-Skelly's mum Marcia Lewis tried to learn more about the business of football so she would be able to support her son on his journey (Source: Instagram/myleslewisskelly).

Harry Maguire's mother Zoe has taken a more low-profile approach to her son's career than others, yet she has never been afraid to speak out when she feels the need to.

This included back in 2023 when she snapped after years of watching her son face significant abuse, both online and in stadiums.

At the time, Zoe labelled the heat on the defender as 'disgraceful' and 'unacceptable', and said it went 'far beyond' football.

She re-emerged into the public eye three years later when Maguire was snubbed from England's World Cup squad.

After that, she tweeted: 'Absolutely disgusted.'.

In another post addressing Maguire, she added: 'You couldn’t have done anymore. Hold your head high …Disgraceful.'.
