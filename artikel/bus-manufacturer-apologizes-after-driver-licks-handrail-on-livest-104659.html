A bus driver in Japan has been removed from driving duties after licking the hand straps and handrails inside his bus during a livestream, apparently at the request of a female streamer.

The driver works for Keikyu Bus, which runs services across Tokyo and Kanagawa, including routes to Haneda Airport, and the incident took place on November 16 after the bus had finished service for the day.

Footage of the livestream, which spread quickly on social media, shows the pair chatting on a split screen, with the streamer directing him to specific parts of the bus.

“I want you to lick the part you hold onto, a bit further up,” she said, according to Japanese outlet coki, to which he replied, “The part you hold?”.

The streamer has been identified on social media as Riakyun, who reportedly streams on the Japanese livestreaming app Fuwatch, though Keikyu Bus did not mention her in its statement.

The driver reported himself to the manufacturer the same day, and on November 18, Keikyu Bus posted an official apology on its website.

“We sincerely apologize for the great discomfort and concern this has caused,” the manufacturer remarked, pledging to “thoroughly educate all employees” to prevent it from happening again.

Keikyu Bus told J-CAST News that the driver has been taken off driving duties, and that it plans to take disciplinary action against him in line with its internal rules.

The firm also confirmed the bus involved has been identified and fully disinfected and cleaned.
