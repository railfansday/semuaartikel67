Shared membrane protects tuberculosis bacteria from antibiotics

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (70 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Tuberculosis ranks among the world's deadliest infectious diseases. More than 1.1 million people around the globe died from tuberculosis in 2025, according to the World Health Organization. The disease is caused by mycobacteria that are difficult to combat with antibiotics. Multidrug-resistant strains of Mycobacterium tuberculosis that are impervious to many of the most effective tuberculosis antibiotics are particularly problematic, creating an urgent need for new drugs.
