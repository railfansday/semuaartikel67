He was missing some key players for the trip to Merseyside, with England internationals and the club’s first-choice striker all sidelined.

With that in mind, here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

That’s because of a hip issue that continues to hamper him.

There has been no update on his recent condition, but he didn’t make the squad for the visit to Anfield, despite having the international break to continue his recovery.

Potential return date: Wednesday, November 25 vs Sporting CP (A).
