Manchester United learn Leny Yoro red card appeal verdict after controversial suspension

Leny Yoro will be available for Manchester United’s Premier League meeting with Brentford this weekend after his one-match suspension was struck off on appeal.

The centre-back was dismissed after just eight minutes as United visited Liverpool on Sunday for a clumsy tackle on Alexander Isak.

The Swede was through one-on-one with Senne Lammens and took a heavy first touch before being caught by the backtracking Yoro, just outside the box. Dominik Szoboszlai scored the opener from the resultant free-kick and United ultimately lost 4-0.

United were quick to appeal the decision, which new head coach Mauricio Pochettino and a number of his players questioned in post-match media duties.

And their appeal has been upheld, meaning Yoro will be available this weekend for the visit of Brentford to Old Trafford.

A statement from the FA read: Leny Yoro’s one-match suspension has been removed following a claim of wrongful dismissal. The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goal scoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November.”.

Speaking post-match, United captain Fernandes questioned the decision to send Yoro off, saying Isak drew the foul and was not in control of the ball anyway.

“We have four officials at the game, plus another two on VAR. I understand it’s a difficult situation, but VAR can watch it again and again, with replays, slow motion, everything,” he told ESPN Brasil.

It has been revealed that “Isak puts his leg in front of Leny simply to try to win a foul. The ball is knocked forward, towards Senne’s hands. He has absolutely no control of the ball.

“For me, fine, it’s a foul. But you can’t send him off because there’s no control of the ball. It’s not a situation where Isak is going one-on-one with the goalkeeper. So I think the punishment was a bit harsh on Leny.”.

In a recent development, pochettino echoed his complaints, adding: “The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, he never had the intention to connect with Isak.

“When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down. In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal, everyone who plays football can feel it.”.
