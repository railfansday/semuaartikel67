Ubisoft Employees Reportedly Need To Justify Not Using AI When Pitching Projects

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A latest report from Vertical Slice reveals that Ubisoft is now requesting that its internal teams explain how they intend to utilise generative AI to accelerate and streamline production of new projects. Any staff not intending to use the technology must explain why it's not a suitable fit.

It has been revealed that anonymous members of staff have spoken out to claim that if neither of the above are adhered to, projects will not be given the green light to proceed. One source states that while Ubisoft is not outright mandating the use of generative AI, the firm is nevertheless "all-in" on the technology:.

"We were told that using AI will increase the speed at which pitches can be made by 'small lean agile teams,' [meaning] prototypes could be built faster. We were told that as the goal was to move fast, iterate and discard projects to sift out the good stuff. AI was essential in maintaining a rapid pace. If you planned on not using it, you would have to have a reason.".

It's also been claimed that Ubisoft now believes generative AI to be "unavoidable", and is under the impression that most gamers simply don't care about its inclusion, with only a vocal minority opposing it.

According to recent reports, "Ubisoft is in a top-down nightmare of AI psychosis. Yves [Guillemot] has hitched his wagon to the tech, demanding the rest of the company follow on.".

Another source described the mood at Ubisoft as "abysmal", citing aggressive cost-cutting and a latest mandate to work from the office full time.

Sources indicate that "Ubisoft still isn't replacing most people leaving the tech giant, leaving some teams stretched unbearably thin. Going-away parties [are] happening every couple [of] weeks it feels. The headless-chicken-core approach to generative AI is just the cherry on top, and Motherbrain is the smaller cherry on top of the cherry.".

As a reminder, 'Motherbrain' is the internal name devised for Ubisoft's AI initiative.

According to recent reports, all this comes as Ubisoft continues to close studios and shed its staff. It most recently laid off 166 employees from Ubisoft Montréal and Québec while also shutting down RedLynx, the studio behind the critically-acclaimed Trials series.
