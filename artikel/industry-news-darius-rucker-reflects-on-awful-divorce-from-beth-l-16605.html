Daris Rucker is opening up about the highs and lows of his life in the spotlight.

In a recent development, “For a lot of years, I experienced some real low lows,” Rucker, 60, shared in his new Hulu documentary Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie. “One of the hardest parts of my life is getting divorced.”.

Recent findings suggest that the country singer and ex Beth Leonard declared in July 2020 that they had split after 20 years of marriage. During their relationship, the pair welcomed daughter Daniela in 2001 and son Jack in 2004. (Rucker and his ex Elizabeth Ann Phillips also welcomed daughter Carolyn in 1995.).

“Beth and I stayed together for a lot of years after I stopped partying,” Rucker shared in a new interview featured in the documentary. “I just think we had gotten to a point where the growing apart thing sounds so cliché, but that’s really what it is. It was an awful time.”.

The former Hootie and the Blowfish frontman added, “Divorce is awful. It was an awful time, and I look at it as another chapter of life that’s closed.”.

In the documentary, old quotes from Rucker appeared on the screen where he discussed the end of his marriage.

“My ex-wife did everything to make my life great,” he once mentioned, “and I did everything in my power to mess it up.”.

Although he’s been able to receive a second chance of love by getting engaged to singer Emily Deahl, Rucker still can’t help but feel sentimental when looking back on that admittedly painful chapter of his life.

“I tried to come out of it as a better person and a better dad,” he said in the doc. “It hurts. You feel like a failure, but at some point you have to move on.”.

Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie explores the triumphs and hardships that shaped Rucker’s life, from his Charleston, South Carolina, childhood  to global music superstardom. In the project, the “Wagon Wheel” singer gets deeply personal about the cost of fame, revealing his struggles with drugs and alcohol, the breakdown of his marriage and near-death experiences.

Viewers are also treated to candid interviews with friends, fellow musicians and even some family members, like daughter Daniela.
