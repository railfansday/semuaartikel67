📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, spaceX shares rose 4.83% in Friday's shortened trading session, amid broader Nasdaq gains and after CEO Elon Musk expanded his SpaceX share-donation plan for U.S. children.

Other tech-related stocks, including Micron, Qualcomm, Lumentum and Tesla all gained over 5% on Friday as the sector rebounded from a heavy sell-off earlier this week. SanDisk was Friday's biggest mover as investors rotated back into some AI trade names, surging 15.96%.

Musk posted on X on Thursday, "I will be donating another share of SpaceX to all kids in America every year.".

Industry observers note that after Musk's post, SpaceX shares jumped more than 7% in overnight trading on Blue Ocean ATS, an alternative trading system for U.S. equities that runs during the nighttime hours. U.S. stock exchanges were closed Thursday for the Thanksgiving holiday and closed at 1 p.m. ET on Friday.

Shares closed 1.30% higher on Wednesday ahead of Thanksgiving.

The latest pledge follows SpaceX's announcement Wednesday that it would donate one share of its Class A stock to all active Trump Accounts, tax-advantaged investment accounts for American children, with Musk personally matching the gift.

In a separate post on X, Musk said SpaceX's contribution would be transferred next month, while his personal donation would follow when his lockup period expires in June next year.

Nearly 70 million Trump Accounts had been created as of October, according to the White House.

Since the program's launch in July, families and friends have contributed more than $1 billion to the accounts, bringing total deposits from all sources to more than $9 billion, CNBC reported earlier this week.

The FCC also announced Wednesday that it granted SpaceX approval to deploy 1 million data center satellites for the tech giant's Starmind constellation.

Speaking on CNBC's "Squawk on the Street" on Friday, FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said the authorization of SpaceX's satellites will have ripple effects within the industry as well.

"Not just SpaceX, but all sorts of different players in this market. Because at the end of the day, we want everyone to compete and succeed. I think we're going to see a lot of players entering this space soon," Carr remarked.
