According to recent reports, the International Emmy Awards, the global version of the U.S. TV honors, were handed out in Latest York Monday night.

The top prize of best drama went to French foodie-themed period series Carême from Apple TV. The series beat out Steven Knight’s Hulu boxing drama A Thousand Blows; Prime Video’s Argentine political drama Menem; and underdog sports drama Real Kashmir Football Club from Indian pay-TV group Sony LIV.

Netflix went into the 54th International Emmys the clear frontrunner with nine nominations, including all four best comedy noms — for the Rowan Atkinson slapstick series Man vs. Baby; the Spanish sitcom Old Dog, Newly introduced Tricks; Argentine satire División Palermo; and Vir Das: Fool Volume, the latest comedy special from the Indian stand-up star. División Palermo won the award.

Still that prize was the only one that went to a program from the streamer.

Best actor went to Bobby Schofield for BBC drama Unforgivable, beating out nominees Javier Cámara, up for his lead performance Spanish dark comedy Jakarta for Moviestar Plus+; Ricardo Darín for Netflix’s Argentine sci-fi survival thriller The Eternaut; and Ian Roberts for Prime Video’s South African road movie A Kind of Madness.

Recent findings suggest that best actress, despite Netflix boasting two of this year’s nominees, went to Belgian actress Mona Mina Leon, nominated for Flemish dramedy Holy Sh!t. Leon beat out Bárbara Mori from Lucca’s World, Park Ji-Hyun from You and Everything Else and Sheridan Smith for the Brit true-crime drama I Fought the Law from ITV.

In total, there were 64 nominees across 16 categories spanning 22 countries. Shrinking star Michael Urie hosted this year’s ceremony at the Fresh York Hilton in Manhattan.

According to recent reports, in addition to the competitive awards, former Netflix chief content officer Bela Bajaria received the Founders Award and former Warner Bros. Discovery international president Gerhard Zeiler received the Directorate Award, presented by former WBD CEO David Zaslav.
