Breaking: Anthropic closes below $83 IPO price, but analysts are still largely positive

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Shares of Anthropic closed down 6.5% on Tuesday, sending the stock below the artificial intelligence organization's $83 IPO price.

Sources indicate that the AI lab's stock has been sinking steadily since opening at $111.50 on the Nasdaq on Thursday, after Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in the largest offering of all time. Investors eagerly awaited Anthropic's debut in hopes that the firm would revitalize the broader AI trade, which has been volatile in recent weeks over concerns about the feasibility of a multitrillion-dollar infrastructure buildout.

In a recent development, chip stocks Nvidia, Advanced Micro Devices and Intel, as well as neoclouds CoreWeave, IREN and Nebius, all fell during intraday trading on Tuesday. The market is still reeling after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, a dramatic reversal from his ouster earlier this month. Nvidia shares surged more than 30% on Monday, but pared those gains Tuesday.

In a recent development, analysts at Canaccord Genuity said Tuesday that they're focused on Anthropic's long-term potential. They said they believe that the company is building "one of the most key enterprise technology franchises of the AI era" and that the "opportunity is considerably larger than traditional software spending.".

Canaccord Genuity initiated coverage of Anthropic with a buy rating and a $135 price target.

"The risks are unusually large, particularly around infrastructure commitments and the durability of competitive differentiation, but we believe the combination of exceptional growth, emerging earnings capacity, and an attractive entry valuation support a compelling investment opportunity," the analysts wrote in a Tuesday note.

Wedbush analysts initiated coverage of Anthropic with an outperform rating and a $120 price target on Monday. They stated Anthropic went public in a "tumultuous market" as debate around the AI buildout continues, but that they expect the tech giant to navigate the competitive environment and "capture a uniquely large addressable market.".

"While there's going to continue to be many ups and downs, and winners and losers as this ecosystem gets built, in our view there is no real question that it does get built over time," the analysts wrote in a Monday note. "The genie is out of the bottle. And ANT is a strong play on that theme.".

Analysts at Oppenheimer initiated coverage of Anthropic with an outperform rating on Tuesday, and they set a $150 price target for the stock.

According to recent reports, they said their "bullish stance" on the organization stems from their belief that Anthropic will capture growing enterprise AI spending, that its improving compute efficiency lowers cost per task and that its multi-chip strategy optimizes price performance. The analysts added that Anthropic's focus on the enterprise also relies less on consumer subsidies, and that rising customer adoption creates "a substantial runway" for the company.

"While competition and compute commitments remain risks, we believe Anthropic can translate its leadership into durable growth and operating leverage," the analysts wrote in a Tuesday note.
