Milan are breathing a sigh of relief after reports Mike Maignan underwent tests in France showing there is no serious injury, so he could play against Frosinone on Sunday.

The goalkeeper started for France last night in the 2-0 Nations League victory over Turkiye, but went off at half-time.

It had been suggested there was a muscular injury with adductor pain, so it would require tests to understand the extent of the damage.

According to L’Equipe, initial tests today have been reassuring, highlighting only discomfort and no lesion or even a strain.

It is believed that coming off at half-time prevented the situation getting any worse, so Maignan could be available for this Sunday’s Serie A match against Frosinone.

The Rossoneri were particularly concerned because their second choice goalkeeper Pietro Terracciano has been out injured for several weeks already.

It means if the captain had not recovered in time, then Lorenzo Torriani would’ve been given his Serie A debut this weekend.

Torriani already stepped in for the Europa League after Maignan was sent off in the game with Bournemouth and then suspended for the defeat to Ferencvaros.
