Original Story: Last night, we saw some news starting to do the rounds about The Witcher 4 and its developer CD Projekt Red, with Chief Financial Officer Piotr Nielubowicz stating that the studio has entered into fresh "agreements" with Sony.

These agreements cover the presence of CDPR games on subscription services such as PlayStation Plus, but also "our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign" - which is the bit we're particularly interested in.

Here's a bit of the transcription via our friends at VGC:.

"Just today, we signed upcoming partnership agreements with Sony Interactive Entertainment covering the presence of our games on PlayStation and in PlayStation Plus subscriptions, as well as our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign.".

"This cooperation will support our business and financial outlook in this and future years.".

Industry observers note that A lot of people are now speculating that The Witcher 4's "collaboration" with Sony may be akin to what Take-Two has done with GTA 6, pushing the whole "plays best on PS5" campaign and restricting how Xbox is allowed to advertise the game. For example, GTA's launch trailer was solely focused around PS5 for an exclusive window.

It's vital to highlight that this is just speculation though, and there may be aspects of these agreements that aren't exclusive at all. We've seen CDPR titles like Cyberpunk 2077 on Xbox Game Pass in the not too distant past, for example, so the PlayStation Plus agreement doesn't necessarily mean Game Pass is out the window.

Let's wait and see what happens with this, but just keep in mind that we could be looking at a situation where The Witcher 4 gets a bigger push on PlayStation than Xbox when it (hopefully) releases in 2028. We'll also leave you with a fun GTA comparison quote from joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski about the difference between Witcher 3 and 4!

It has been revealed that "I’m not going to make a deep dive, full-blown review of GTA 5 versus GTA 6, but of course, we did look at the game, we did see the differences... we believe there’s going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4. It’s been 11 years. We are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better-looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game.".
