Recent findings suggest that this weekend, all of Spain paid tribute to Jesus de Miguel. Posters from La Liga to amateur matches, minutes of silence and various tributes took place to honor the memory of the 30-year-old striker, who died at the start of the week. For his part, Jesus Owono, the goalkeeper of FC Andorra, is waiting to know his sanction.

In a recent development, and as the Spanish media already reported earlier this week, the 25-year-old goalkeeper is at rock bottom, holed up at home with his loved ones. According to Mundo Deportivohe even plans to end his career. Monitored by psychologists and by the staff of his club, he could have been affected to the point of no longer wanting to set foot on a pitch, also aware of the hostile reception that could be reserved for him.
