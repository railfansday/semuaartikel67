A buyer does not wake up wanting more AI interactions. They want the service request resolved, the qualified lead handed over or the accounts reconciled. Yet those ordinary outcomes contain awkward questions. When is the job finished? Who decides? What happens when it reopens tomorrow?

Those questions will shape AI product strategy in 2027. Charging for completed work is appealing because it sounds close to customer value. Making that promise precise enough to put on an invoice is where the real product work begins.

Salesforce offers several Agentforce purchasing models, including actions, conversations, licenses, and help-agent resolutions. Zendesk introduced automated resolution tiers in May 2026. These are materially different units, even when each is presented as a way to align spending with value.

Sales qualification, internal IT service and back-office reconciliation are plausible territory because each has recognizable milestones. A message sent or a tool called will not be enough; the offering will have to name the completed milestone for which the customer pays.
