Black Friday isn't until Nov. 27, but the early Black Friday sales are in full swing. I've been keeping an eye on all the best fitness trackers and smartwatch deals so that you can snag the best possible price from now until the end of Cyber Monday (Nov. 30). Whether you want a full-featured fitness smartwatch or a more recovery-focused tracker, here are the discounts you can find right now, including on brands like Apple, Oura, Garmin, and more.

The Oura Ring 5 is at its lowest ever price: $299 for black and silver, $399 for all other colors. These are the lowest prices ever for these rings, which usually go for $399 and $499, respectively. The Oura Ring 5 is the smallest and most comfortable smart ring I've tried, although it's worth noting it requires a $5.99/month subscription fee.

In a recent development, for an even cheaper deal, the older Oura Ring 4 is $249—a full $100 off its list price of $349. If you opt for "stealth" (matte black), the price is $279; for gold, it's $286.61, down from $399. While this model has since been replaced by the Oura Ring 5, it's still an accurate and comfortable smart ring—especially if tracking your sleep is your priority.

Whoop may be one of the most expensive smart bands out there, thanks to its subscription-only model. But that price is particularly low right now, since the Peak membership that normally goes for $239 is now $199. Peak is the middle tier, and it's the one I recommend. While you can get Whoop One for $189 in this sale, the $10 you save isn't really worth it given the features you'd be missing. I also wouldn't normally recommend Whoop Life, since its ECG readings and blood pressure estimates aren't really worth the extra $100+. That remarked, if you do want it, the Life tier is $299 right now instead of $359.

The fresh Luna band is on sale for $119.20, down from a normal price of $149. The Luna band is made by Indian smartwatch tech giant Noise, the same company behind the Luna Ring that I reviewed last year. The ring is no longer available in the U.S., but the band is. It's a Whoop-style screenless fitness tracker, potentially a worthy competitor to the Fitbit Air and other smart bands.

In a recent development, this is the brand-new Apple Watch model, and it's actually worth the upgrade. One of the biggest reasons is that the Series 12's new processor (called, confusingly, the S11 chip) supports some features older Apple Watches can't, and a good rule of thumb is that whenever there's a break in software compatibility, it makes sense to get the newer model. Right now, I'm seeing it for as low as $329, down from the list price of $399. (And if you're stuck deciding between an Apple Watch and a Fitbit, Beth breaks it all down in her comparison of the two watches.).

The CMF Watch 3 Pro is Nothing's latest smartwatch, and it incorporates AI features powered by ChatGPT; you can presently pick it up at a major discount. Originally $99, you can get the CMF Watch 3 Pro for $55. This is the lowest price this smartwatch has been.

If you're looking specifically for a sporty, outdoors watch, Garmin sets the standard. (You could definitely call me something of a Garmin enthusiast.) Here are the best Black Friday deals on Garmin watches that I've seen so far:.

The 44mm Garmin Instinct 3 is 249.99 (down from $449.99). The 1.2-inch color AMOLED display makes the screen more readable in bright daylight conditions, and battery life is best-in-class—a must for those going off-grid.

Industry observers note that the Garmin Fēnix 8 in titanium with a gray band is $749.99 (down from $999.99). While this is last year's model, it's a perfectly solid option if you don't specifically need the titanium case or the latest mapping engine in the latest Fenix 9 Pro.

Recent findings suggest that the Garmin Forerunner 265 is $299.99 (down from $449.99). Although it isn't the latest and greatest anymore, this is still a fan favorite among Garmin customers. It's a step above entry-level options, with dual-band GPS, power meter compatibility, and advanced analytics (like training status).
