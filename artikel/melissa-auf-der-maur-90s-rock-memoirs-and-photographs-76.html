Melissa Auf Der Maur: 90s rock memoirs and photographs

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (100 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that melissa Auf Der Maur grew up in Montreal, the daughter of a feminist activist mother and a journalist-turned-politician father. At just 22, she joined Courtney Love’s band Hole as its bass player, going on to become a role model for young female fans – and not only female fans – of alternative rock. She later joined The Smashing Pumpkins before striking out on her own as a solo artist. She dropped by FRANCE 24 to talk to Marjorie Hache about the epic decade that brought the 20th century to a close.
