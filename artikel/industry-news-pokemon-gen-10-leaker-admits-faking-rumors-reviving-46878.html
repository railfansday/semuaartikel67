A Pokemon Winds & Waves leaker has admitted to faking rumors about the Gen 10 games, giving fans fresh hope for voice acting and Eevee’s first new evolution in more than a decade.

Recent findings suggest that moot, who posts as @Mootmonthly on X, had built a reputation for sharing information about the Pokemon anime. Centro Leaks had publicly vouched for him as a reliable anime source, and his claims about an internal delay for Winds & Waves made it into Dexerto’s November 9 coverage.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, a separate account called @kurwashibamaste had been feeding Centro Leaks rumors about the games since July. It claimed a development build appeared to lack latest evolutions for Eevee and Tropius. It also claimed voice acting had been planned but had disappeared from recent builds.

Industry observers note that on November 17, an X user posted a screenshot appearing to show Moot and @kurwashibamaste in the same account switcher. Moot responded with a GIF captioned “How it feels to spread misinformation and lies.” Centro then said Moot had admitted to running the alternate account and faking many of its Winds & Waves rumors.

Moot later mentioned he had accomplished a “specific goal” with the account and described keeping up the act as a “larp.” He also said his reputation had made him feel limited in what he could post and that he’d “prefer being viewed as a clown.”.

Industry observers note that the reveal frustrated fans who had spent months following the rumors. Pokemon creator Light said he had gotten it wrong himself and criticized other accounts for allegedly staying quiet when they suspected what was happening.

Others were happy to see those rumors fall apart. Pokemon creator SoulSilverArt remarked a Tropius evolution, a new Eeveelution, and voice acting were “back on the menu.” Eevee’s last new evolution was Sylveon, which debuted in Pokemon X and Y in 2013.

Well there’s some real confirmation, Moot is Kurawashi…Looks Tropius Evo, latest Eeveelutions, and VA ARE BACK ON THE MENU BOYS! https://t.co/4chqJMxX6G pic.twitter.com/45M3tN4Yi7.

Recent findings suggest that centro said some real details may have been mixed in. It pointed to the rumor of around 300 new Pokemon and forms as one it had heard separately, adding that development plans could have changed.

Industry observers note that the Pokemon Company has yet to announce voice acting or a upcoming Eevee evolution. It has confirmed a 2027 release on Nintendo Switch 2 and revealed Gen 10 starters Browt, Pombon, and Gecqua.
