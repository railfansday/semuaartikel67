GTA 6 developers at Rockstar are worried about being laid off after shipping "the biggest game of all time"

🌐 Source: Eurogamer • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (445 words, 📖 Standard)

  • But that may not be enough to prevent layoffs at Rockstar, some people working within the studio fear.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Rockstar has successfully shipped Grand Theft Auto 6, after years of work and more than a billion dollars spent. GTA 6 is already one of the highest-rated games ever - Eurogamer awarded it five stars in our GTA 6 review - and it's expected to be among the most successful, best-selling games ever.

But that may not be enough to prevent layoffs at Rockstar, some people working within the studio fear. Nestor Uriarte, an animation programmer at Rockstar North, wrote about their concern for their job and those of their colleagues in an article on The Guardian.

"After the major day, we have had no indication that layoffs are coming, but it's hard not to worry about what the future holds," Uriarte wrote. "It notes a lot about the industry that even my colleagues and I, working on the biggest game of all time, are worried about getting laid off. But the truth is that workers across the sector are helpless to resist the waves of redundancies crashing around us, until we get organised.".

Industry observers note that uriarte's piece comes as Rockstar staff's efforts to establish a union reach a critical point. A tribunal between the studio and fired IWBG union members just concluded, though the verdict may not arrive for many weeks or months.

Industry observers note that rockstar's UK studios are presently voting on recognising an official Rockstar workers union. Uriarte is in support of it and has urged colleagues to vote in favour of it. Results of that vote are expected today.

"Winning union recognition would be a huge step forward in securing a meaningful say over our conditions at Rockstar, but its significance would reach far beyond that," Uriarte pointed out.

"As one of the biggest and most influential game studios in the world, what happens here could open the door for other workers across the UK to imagine an alternative future for the industry - one in which workers' voices are taken into account more.".

Across its 12 offices around the world, Rockstar's headcount grew to 4,711 during GTA 6's development - double its size when it released Red Dead Redemption 2.

In a recent development, as popular and profitable as Rockstar's games are, the firm has initiated layoffs in the past after shipping a major project. While there's no way of knowing whether the same is going to happen for GTA 6, the justifiable concerns - given the current state of the industry - are there. According to the ASGC Games Industry Layoffs Tracker, more than 12,000 developers have lost their jobs this year so far, up from more than 9,000 people last year.
