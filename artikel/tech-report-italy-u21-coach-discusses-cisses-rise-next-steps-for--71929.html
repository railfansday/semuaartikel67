Italy’s U21 head coach, Silvio Baldini, has shared his thoughts on the Milan trio Alphadjo Cisse, Francesco Camarda and Davide Bartesaghi.

In recent years, Milan have shifted more towards giving some of their academy graduates more playing time with the first team. Bartesaghi was the first to properly get in, cementing his spot in the starting eleven last season, while Cisse has been the big breakthrough this season.

It has been revealed that camarda has had a tougher time after a loan spell at Lecce, but Milan have made it very clear through renewals and words that they will focus more on the youngsters. It’s exciting times as well, given how they have all performed with the U21 national team too.

Sources indicate that speaking to Gazzetta dello Sport this morning, Italy’s U21 coach Silvio Baldini was asked about the breakthrough of Cisse, Camarda’s current state of mind and why Bartesaghi is a ‘luxury’ for his team. Safe to say that he likes the trio a lot.

Cisse has had a proper breakthrough. Did you see that coming?

“Compared to a year ago, I can see a different guy, who’s more mature and prepared, with higher self-esteem, psychologically changed, simply stronger.

According to recent reports, “He has Milan to thank for signing him from Catanzaro in Serie B, believing in him and making room for him, then giving him consistent playing time. If he then makes the national team and does well, so much the better.”.

Milan deserve credit, and you also deserve credit for discovering him as a centre-forward….

It has been revealed that “Analysing training sessions, a series of situations emerged where he managed to score by attacking space and depth: if you can do it on the right and left, it means you can also do it in the centre. But even there, the credit goes entirely to the staff.

In a recent development, “They had the intuition, realising that Cisse could be useful to our game system in that way and at the same time, allowing Alpha to discover a latest role successfully. I’m simply honest and meritocratic in my choices.

“Even though the goal against Armenia actually came from him as a second striker, at the end of that match we had two centre-forwards, him and Camarda.”.

In a recent development, camarda, by the way, has struggled at Milan but scored for the U21s. How is that possible?

Sources indicate that “I can only speak about the work we do together. I spoke to him at length, I dedicated time to him, I asked him to explain his concerns, his fear of not being able to express himself as he would like. I told him stories of my own life, of how I came out of it.

It has been revealed that “A dialogue not as a coach-player but as a son, or rather a grandson, as he could be, and a grandfather: from the heart. I’m not interested in performances as an end in themselves; they will come if he feels in the right place.

“I try to make him understand that I see him, I follow him in many small ways: he’s a conscientious boy who shouldn’t become obsessed with the goal, but rather experience football for what it is: fun.

“If, instead of fun, it becomes a test, then it worries you, and if you worry, you no longer enjoy it and it doesn’t help you perform at your best.”.

To have more fun, do you recommend that he should leave Milan for another team?

“I’m not going to get involved in this. There are clubs, agents, and a family: they know what the best path will be for him. Let’s remember that he’s 18 years old, he plays for AC Milan and in the Under-21s.

It has been revealed that “It’s clear that the more he plays, the better it will be for AC Milan and for everyone. We’re talking about a great player, a champion in my opinion, who needs to finish his development: he’s still growing, he already has a great eye for goal, and he’s sacrificing himself for the team.”.

Camarda-Cisse: Can Milan provide the national team with one of the forward duos of the future?

“For me, yes. I see them as a permanent fixture in the national team, and of course they need to prove it and earn it on the pitch. But they have all the characteristics to do it. I found Alpha to be better prepared, even in his game decisions. Amorim did well to develop him.”.

The third Rossoneri player is Bartesaghi: more of a full-back like with you or a winger like at Milan?

Industry observers note that “Davide is one of the best players in his position I’ve coached in many years. He’s talented, very professional, mature, introverted yet open, and that’s not a contradiction: he has enormous potential, and he highlights it every time he comes to us.

It has been revealed that “If I were any Serie A coach, I’d take him with me. For the Under-21s, Bartesaghi is a luxury. He plays with heart, passion, and intelligence, as well as possessing high-level technical, physical, and running skills.

According to recent reports, “He can play in any position on the flank. In my opinion, if he starts 50 meters from the goal, he can perform at his best.”.
