Mauricio Pochettino insisted his full focus remained on the United States ahead of his final match in charge before departing for Manchester United.

Pochettino was confirmed as Man Utd's fresh head coach last week after Michael Carrick was sacked, with the Red Devils languishing in the Premier League relegation zone.

The Argentine signed a contract with the USA until 2030 in August after reaching the round of 16 at the World Cup.

It was confirmed that Pochettino would take charge of the Stars and Stripes' CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final against Haiti before heading to Manchester, with the USA winning the first leg 6-0.

Pochettino's decision to remain in charge has been criticised, but he insisted there was no conflict of interest ahead of the second leg at FC Cincinnati's TQL Stadium.

"For me, it's not difficult. I think we ​are well-prepared to switch promptly – like a player, from the national team to the clubs," Pochettino told reporters.

"From one game that you don't play well to another that you need to be ​ready again. From one action to the next action.

According to recent reports, "The business is to be focused here ‌for ⁠the game against Haiti. But for sure, in the moment that [we] finish, we are going to start to be 100% focused on our next project.".

Pochettino returns to the Premier League two years on from being sacked at Chelsea, with United the fourth different team he has managed in England during his career.

He had been linked with a move to Old Trafford back in 2022, but United instead opted to appoint Erik ten Hag.

Pochettino is United's eighth permanent boss since the retirement of Alex Ferguson in May 2013, and he put pen to paper on a two-and-a-half-year deal.

During his time at Tottenham, Pochettino oversaw the most Premier League games in the club's history and recorded the most wins (113), while also leading Spurs to the Champions League final in 2019, in which they were beaten 2-0 by Liverpool.

And Liverpool will be Pochettino's first opponents when he sits in the United dugout for the first time on Sunday.

Pochettino is expected to land in England on Thursday, but he confirmed members of his coaching staff will already be there when he arrives.

"That was the ⁠plan when ​we agreed terms with Manchester United and ​with the national team," Pochettino added.

"They are preparing everything there because on Wednesday, when we arrive, it's ​going to be non-stop.".
