The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra is the highest-end model in Samsung’s 2026 Galaxy S26 lineup. Released back on March 11, it pairs a 6.9-inch display with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, a built-in S Pen, and a four-camera system led by a 200MP main sensor.

According to recent reports, it’s a powerful device, built for photography enthusiasts and professionals who want to get the most out of their phones while on the go. Its large screen size belies a relatively compact, portable device that’s a pleasure to do work on, from writing emails to taking photos to taking meeting notes using the S Pen.

Its most distinctive feature is Privacy Display. Rather than relying on an awkward screen protector, the Galaxy S26 Ultra can reduce off-angle visibility when needed. You can apply protection to selected apps or sensitive notifications instead of obscuring everything all the time. That’s useful when checking messages on a train or opening personal information in a busy restaurant.

In our Galaxy S26 Ultra review, we talked about how the Privacy Display is flexible and useful, able to be enabled for certain apps and left off for others.

Recent findings suggest that we also found dependable everyday endurance from the 5,000mAh battery, and now that the phone supports 60-watt wired charging, quick top-ups become more useful. Samsung also promises seven generations of OS upgrades and seven years of security updates from launch, an notable consideration if you strategy to keep your next phone for several years.

The Galaxy S26 Ultra’s main advantages over the Galaxy S26 and S26+ are Privacy Display, the integrated S Pen, and its more versatile camera hardware.

Sources indicate that at launch, US pricing started at $899.99 for the Galaxy S26, $1,099.99 for the Galaxy S26+, and $1,299.99 for the Galaxy S26 Ultra, each with 256GB of storage. But those prices have dropped over the past few months, and you may be able to get them for less. We’ve seen the S26 Ultra, for example, as low as $999.99 during Prime Day.
