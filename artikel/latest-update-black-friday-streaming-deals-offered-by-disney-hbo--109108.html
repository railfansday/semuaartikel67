The merger of Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount closed last month, creating Skydance, but the organization’s flagship streamers HBO Max and Paramount+ for now are marketing themselves independently. (Skydance expects to launch a bundle of the two early in 2027.).

Both levels of NBCUniversal’s Peacock are available at a discount, with the Select proposal priced at $2.99 for three months (66% off) and the Premium level at $8.99 for three months (30% off).

Amazon Prime Video has had Black Friday deals going since early November, an increasingly common practice across many retail categories. It is offering savings on add-on subscriptions, among them Apple TV, which can now be tacked onto a Prime subscription for $6.99 a month for the first six months, compared to the standard rate of $14.99.

Netflix, the largest global streamer, is famous for its reluctance to take part in bundles or engage in frequent discounting. Befitting that strategy, it is not offering any Black Friday deals.
