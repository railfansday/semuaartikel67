Big news: Cosmopolitan’s creator community is growing. We are joining Hearst Creator Studios (HCS), a newly introduced platform for handpicked creators to co-produce and distribute original, editorial, and branded content across Hearst brands, including Delish, ELLE, Good Housekeeping, MotorTrend, and, of course, Cosmopolitan. Think of it as a mix of talent agency and creative studio, bringing together creator talent and editorial authority.

Industry observers note that in light of the news, we called up nine of our favorite friends and creators to help kick this partnership off. Some are already very familiar to Cosmo readers, including beloved columnists Ella Snyder and Julia Mervis. Others are joining the Cosmo world for the first time, and each brings a strong connection with their audience and a strong POV on the things Cosmo readers really care about.
