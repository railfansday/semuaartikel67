In a recent development, it's Grand Theft Auto VI day around the world--the game is really good, as it turns out. While players are out exploring the massive in-game map or checking their in-game phones as much as they check their real-life ones, Rockstar has celebrated the occasion by releasing the full cast list for its new crime epic. Nestled among celebrity names like Bad Bunny, "Weird Al" Yankovic, Brigitte Nielsen, Stephen Root, and Bobby Moynihan is a name that will surprise many longtime Rockstar fans. It's also, potentially, a GTA VI spoiler, so we're going to put it below a selfie of co-protagonist Jason Duval. Seriously, if you want to go into Grand Theft Auto VI spoiler-free, look away now. Do you think Jason's last name is a Jacksonville Jaguars reference? DUUUUUVAL! Alright, if you're still here, then you're in for the spoiler. Ready? Here goes: One of the names listed on the credits is Michael Hollick, playing a role known only as "The Hitman." If that's setting off a signal to established Grand Theft Auto fans, it's because Michael Hollick is also the voice of GTA IV protagonist--and occasional hitman--Niko Bellic. Hollick's involvement in GTA VI is surprising, considering how just after GTA IV's release in 2008, he publicly criticized Rockstar for his payment on the project. As reported by CNET by way of the New York Times, Hollick reportedly was paid $100,000 for 15 months of work on the project. Whether or not this is actually a spoiler remains to be seen, but with Grand Theft Auto VI now available on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, we imagine the truth will be revealed soon enough.
