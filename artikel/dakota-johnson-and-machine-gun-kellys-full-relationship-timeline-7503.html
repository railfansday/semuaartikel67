Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly have yet to define their relationship publicly, but the two stars have been spending plenty of time together.

Industry observers note that the actress and musician were first linked in September 2026, when they were photographed together in New York City. Days later, a source told People that Johnson and Kelly, whose real name is Colson Baker, “have been hanging out and seeing each other under the radar for months now.”.

The two stars had known each other before, but according to the source, they became closer after attending Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s wedding in July.

Since then, Johnson has watched Kelly walk the runway in Milan, and sources have shared more about how their connection developed.

Below, everything we know about Johnson and Kelly’s rumored relationship so far.

Johnson and Kelly both attended Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s July 3 wedding at Madison Square Garden in New York City.

The two stars already knew each other, but a source close to Kelly told People in September that they “hit it off” at the wedding.

On September 28, a source gave Entertainment Tonight similar intel, telling the outlet that Johnson and Kelly “really connected at Taylor and Travis’s wedding.”.

On September 12, Johnson and Kelly were photographed together in Latest York City, getting out of the same SUV and entering Swift’s Tribeca apartment.

Four days later, a source close to Kelly told People that Kelly and Johnson “have been hanging out and seeing each other under the radar for months now.”.

The source explained that Johnson and Kelly “started off as friends and were texting often, but now they’re spending more time together.”.

“It’s a very new start, but they’re smitten with each other right now,” the insider added. “They haven’t put a label on their relationship yet. They are just seeing where spending time together will take them.”.

A second source characterized things as “super casual” at that point.

On September 22, Johnson and Kelly both traveled to Italy for Milan Fashion Week and attended the same event.

Three days later, Johnson addressed the broader attention her dating life receives during an appearance on Vogue’s The Run-Through podcast, though she never mentioned Kelly specifically.

“Even if I go to dinner with my manager or my lawyer, it’s like, ‘Seen with mystery man,’” she noted. “I also don’t care.”.

When asked whether she ever feels compelled to correct speculation about her personal life, Johnson explained why she prefers not to engage with it.

According to recent reports, “I think it’s just futile. People are gonna believe what they wanna believe, and I don’t have the energy to fight for that,” she said. “I would much rather focus on other things in my life. Every day, there’s something on the internet that’s ridiculous and true or not true, and I don’t care.”.

On September 28, a source shared more about Kelly and Johnson’s relationship with Entertainment Tonight: “Dakota was the one who made the first move. He’s very shy around her, but he likes her a lot. It’s still upcoming, but they’re having fun and seeing where things go.”.

The insider added, “Dakota has been a fan of his music and has always found him cute. He likes her a lot, too.”.

Sources indicate that on October 19, a source close to Kelly told People that he and Johnson were spending more time together. “They have been out on dates that people don’t know about because there aren’t any photos.”.

The insider remarked that they’re in the “dating phase,” and “aren’t ready to go official yet.”.

Recent findings suggest that “They both really like each other and have been seeing each other more frequently,” the source shared. “They haven’t put a label on their relationship yet, but they’re growing closer every time they hang out.”.

Recent findings suggest that in the past few months, they’ve especially connected over their “same dry and dark sense of humor,” the source mentioned. “[They] make each other laugh.”.

Recent findings suggest that days later, Kelly and Johnson were photographed out in Los Angeles on October 23. They first left a music studio together, riding off in Kelly’s red Maserati. That evening, they were seen together again, leaving the Chateau Marmont.

In a recent development, A source close to Johnson gave People an upgrade on their dynamic on October 28. “She’s happy and enjoys spending time with him,” the insider noted. “There’s a lot of chemistry and she’s been going to the studio with him while he works. For now, it makes her happy.”.

On November 3, Kelly and Johnson were photographed leaving a restaurant in Calabasas, California, after a dinner date. They were smiling and talking to each other in the shots.

Two days later, on November 5, Johnson and Kelly stepped out for another evening together in Los Angeles, attending a Khaite dinner at Dan Tana’s in West Hollywood. For the occasion, Johnson wore a black Death Row Pictures zip-up hoodie over a lace-trimmed Valentino miniskirt, paired with knee-high Gucci boots and statement diamond jewelry. Kelly opted for a more casual look, wearing a green satin bomber jacket over a yellow hoodie and gray sweatpants.

Recent findings suggest that the next week, Johnson and Kelly stepped out for another evening together in Los Angeles on November 11. The pair first attended a screening of Johnson’s short film Flesh Impact, before heading to dinner at the Tower Bar inside the Sunset Tower Hotel with friends. Kelly opted for a casual look while Johnson indicated up in a satin corset-anchored sheer slip dress and floral mules.

Industry observers note that they later made their way to Kelly’s recording studio, where they were photographed leaving shortly before 3 A.M. For the late-night outing, Johnson wore a more relaxed look with a standout tiger-striped Nour Hammour coat.

According to recent reports, the following day, the pair was spotted outside of the studio again. This time, Johnson wore a floor-length black two-piece set with a floral pattern.
