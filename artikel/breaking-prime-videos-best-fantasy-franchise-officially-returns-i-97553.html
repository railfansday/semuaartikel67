Sources indicate that prime Video’s fantasy offerings are far from perfect. The Amazon streamer’s highlights like Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power and the canceled Wheel of Time have divided fans and critics alike. However, the streaming platform struck gold with its Dungeons & Dragons adaptations, which are among the best fantasy franchises currently on TV.

As fantasy grows more popular in various media, streaming platforms are investing more in the genre, especially because it's such a crowd-pleaser when executed well. So in that vein, Amazon’s interest in Critical Role’s Dungeons & Dragons shows is not in the least bit surprising, given that the franchise has steadily moved from being niche to becoming mainstream.

Sources indicate that while The Legend of Vox Machina has deservedly earned its place as one of Prime Video’s best fantasy shows, The Mighty Nein is also just as perfect. The series debuted in 2025, and fans were expecting it to return for a sophomore season this year, but that will not be the case. After all, animation, especially on that scale, does take time to create.

Industry observers note that prime Video continues to expand its animated fantasy universe based on Critical Role, with The Mighty Nein set to return for a second season. Following the success of its first season, the series is bringing its unlikely group of adventurers back in 2027. Though The Legend of Vox Machina and The Mighty Nein hail from the same creative minds, they tell completely different fantasy stories. In many ways, The Mighty Nein is even darker than its Critical Role predecessor.

Industry observers note that the Mighty Nein season 1 concluded on a compelling note with the titular group finally uniting and Yasha asking for their help. Season 2 looks to push the group dynamic further, especially as Fjord's growing powers threaten to lead him down a darker path and the mysterious Beacon presents the team with fresh opportunities that could come at a high cost.

The synopsis of the fresh installment has established that the pirate arc will unfold earlier than it did in the D&D campaign, but alterations to the source material are expected in any adaptation. With eight new episodes arriving together on Prime Video on March 24, 2027, the return of The Mighty Nein could further establish Critical Role's animated universe as one of streaming's most exciting fantasy franchises.

The future of the Critical Role franchise on Prime Video is both promising and sad. The Legend of Vox Machina season 5 will be its last, bringing an end to the critically acclaimed series. With how much Vox Machina has changed how audiences perceive D&D stories, more so because it has been consistent across all of its four seasons, it's sad to see it go, but it's also the right move. Typically, series that go on for too long tend to have quality control issues.

There is a possibility that after The Legend of Vox Machina concludes, the franchise might continue with spinoffs. However, there hasn’t been any conclusive news on that front. The Mighty Nein just has one season, with a second set to premiere next year. If the series follows the same trajectory as The Legend of Vox Machina, then it might also have a similar longevity in terms of the number of seasons. Critical Role or Prime Video, however, have yet to announce when exactly The Mighty Nein will end.

Sources indicate that while Critical Role is known for their fantasy offerings, they revealed that they have other deals in play with Prime Video. According to the creative team, the unnamed upcoming projects will be in the sci-fi genre (via Collider). Critical Role has thus far proved they are great at creating fantasy reveals with the success of The Mighty Nein and The Legend of Vox Machina. It will be interesting to see what they come up with when they move away from the genre that has defined their careers thus far.
