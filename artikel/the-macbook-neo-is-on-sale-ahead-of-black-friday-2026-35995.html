The MacBook Neo Is on Sale Ahead of Black Friday (2026)

🌐 Source: WIRED • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (297 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • Recent findings suggest that the MacBook Neo does come with a number of compromises compared to the MacBook Air.
  • Sources indicate that still, if you're after an affordable laptop that doesn't feel cheap, the MacBook Neo is excellent at playing that part.

The MacBook Neo is on sale (finally), bringing the price down from $699 to $649. Fifty dollars might not sound like a lot, but it's the largest discount the MacBook Neo has ever received. The previous best deal was just $10 off. As Apple's first foray into the truly budget-tier laptop, the MacBook Neo's price has been stubbornly steady, more so than any other MacBook in the lineup. It's one of a few great early Black Friday deals we're spotting.

Recent findings suggest that the MacBook Neo does come with a number of compromises compared to the MacBook Air. The most notable is that it only has 8 GB of unified memory. This is what keeps it from feeling like a truly capable work or student laptop. If you spend most of your working hours sitting in front of a computer, the MacBook Air is worth springing for over the Neo.

Sources indicate that still, if you're after an affordable laptop that doesn't feel cheap, the MacBook Neo is excellent at playing that part. The display is bright and beautiful, and the build quality is as sturdy as you'd expect an Apple product to be. It even comes in a few unique colors. There are a lot of people who could just use a basic laptop, and $649 is a great price for one.

Industry observers note that I should note that Apple does sell the laptop for $100 off as part of its education discount, but it requires having an active student ID. That's the way to go if you happen to be a student or teacher. For everyone else, though, this is the cheapest the MacBook Neo has been since late June, when Apple increased prices across the entire MacBook lineup.
