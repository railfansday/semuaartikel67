Latest Avengers: Doomsday footage has emerged after the final trailer.

Avengers: Doomsday is less than one month away, with anticipation only growing for the Marvel Cinematic Universe's next movie as fan theories intensify over these last few weeks before the film's December 18 release. The Avengers movie franchise is known for bringing as many Marvel heroes together as possible to fight massive threats. To take on Doctor Doom, Marvel assembled a mighty Avengers: Doomsday cast with MCU and multiverse heroes, and after a long wait, the final Avengers: Doomsday trailer teased their fight when it was released online on Monday, November 23. However, an exciting newly introduced teaser has just been shared.

On X, the Disney Korea account has shared a newly introduced Avengers: Doomsday international trailer.

It has been revealed that the Marvel footage features new scenes that were not present in the original final trailer, with the biggest inclusion being the reveal that Chris Evans' Steve Rogers and Chris Hemsworth's Thor will confront Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom in his throne room, seemingly all by themselves. Other moments include the Fantastic Four's Human Torch flaming on, as well as the MCU villain's dialogue, with Victor von Doom claiming that the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, and the X-Men will all kneel before Doom.

According to recent reports, thor and Steve had previously been teased to head off on a mission of their own in the upcoming Avengers movie, as other teasers featured the God of Thunder summoning the Bifrost to transport them somewhere. Whether that is meant to directly take them to Doctor Doom's throne room, which is most likely set in Latveria, a key location set up by Marvel in The Fantastic Four: First Steps, or somewhere else first remains to be seen. Still, given their status as the only original Avengers members confirmed for the MCU movie, it makes sense that Steve Rogers and Thor would take on Doom.

As for the rest of the new Avengers: Doomsday international trailer, there is a new shot with several heroes together, and it is seemingly located in Steve and Peggy Carter's home, judging by the interior design, with a matching painting. This meeting will feature at least three superhero teams, with all Fantastic Four members but Reed Richards in the shot, the New Avengers, and a few members from Captain America's Avengers team. Given how no X-Men characters are present, this could be set before the mutants enter the story, though that remains to be confirmed when the film hits theaters.

Avengers: Doomsday has already shown that Thor is no match for Doctor Doom when fighting him alone, as even after summoning thunder with Stormbreaker to take on the villain, Doctor Doom easily swats the Avenger away using magic. With Steve Rogers by his side, carrying Mjölnir, perhaps the God of Thunder might have a bigger chance against the villain, though the duo's odds of taking down Doom without other heroes are low. The fresh Avengers: Doomsday footage raises the stakes for the MCU movie, while teasing exciting team-ups not shown in the original final trailer.
