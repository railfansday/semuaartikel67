Motorola has kicked off its Android 17 rollout for the Razr Fold, marking the first major update for the organization’s first book-style foldable, and the start of a potentially interest 7 years of software support.

Sources indicate that the Motorola Razr Fold rolled out with Android 16 back in May 2026, and it remains a really good option for those looking to pick up a book-style foldable in the US. On top of solid specs across the board, it also carries the promise of 7 years of software support, something that few other Moto devices can tout.

According to recent reports, but nowhere was it promises that Motorola would speed things up at all. A little over 3 months after Motorola announced the update and several weeks after other Motorola devices were updated to Android 17, the Motorola Razr Fold is finally getting its own Android 17 update.

Rolling out now, Android 17 for the Razr Fold is a 4.15GB~ update that appears to be available at least somewhat broadly. Reports are popping up on Reddit including one report from the EU (Hungary, specifically), with another user apparently in a Spanish-speaking region also seeing the update.

We’re still waiting to see if the enhancement is available in the US (there’s a backlog of updates on our unit), but it’s certainly an update to look forward to. Apparently, Motorola finally fixed a weird UI issue that affected the Telegram app, something we ran into when using the device earlier this year.

In a recent development, it’s great to see the revision out, but it’s noteworthy that Motorola is not setting a particularly encouraging precedent here. Razr Fold is one of the firm’s first devices to be promised 7 years of OS updates and software support, and its flagship-tier status certainly would have most expecting it to be updated ahead of some of Motorola’s more affordable, lower-tier devices that were updated over a month ago. Whatever the case, hopefully Motorola will speed things up over the next few years, especially with its $1,600 flagship Signature 27 on the horizon with the same software promises.
