The Blues head to the City Ground on Friday night looking to get back to winning ways after successive Premier League losses, with a 4-1 home thrashing by their old rivals following a 1-0 defeat at struggling Sunderland prior to the international break.

Xabi Alonso’s side have been criticised for their poor defending and a perceived lack of intensity in recent performances, and summer signing Chavarria is keen for them to put that right against a Forest team that presently sit nine places below them in the table.

“We have to give a lot more because, in the end, we’re at a club where the demands are at a different level,” he told Chelsea’s official media channels.

“We know the responsibility we have and I think we’ve realised that we can’t repeat a match like that [the Leeds game] again.

Recent findings suggest that “Obviously, on any given day we can win, we can lose, or we can draw, but the attitude we had, I think, wasn’t right, and we players know that it was our fault. So we’re already mentally focused on going all out with the bit between our teeth.

According to recent reports, “We’re going to treat Nottingham Forest like it’s the last game we play, and we truly believe it, we take it seriously. That’s the only way, because football now is so competitive that any team can beat you.

“We are clear that we want to work and we want to be a competitive team, so the only way forward is to stay united and work even harder.”.

Chavarria, who joined Chelsea for £16.3million from Rayo Vallecano in August, continued: “I think it will be like these last two games we had against Sunderland and Leeds – very physical teams playing with a back five, playing very directly, with very powerful and quality players up front.

Sources indicate that “Ultimately, it will be a hard-fought match with a lot of individual duels, decided by small details, like most games are. We have to stay extremely focused so that we land the first blow.”.

Recent findings suggest that discussing his own performances, the 28-year-old added: “I’m trying to give my absolute best. I know I can give a lot more. I think in these matches that progression is going to show.”.
