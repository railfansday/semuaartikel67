There's no better time to buy a Garmin watch than the Black Friday sales, because it's almost always the period you'll find the lowest prices available on any Garmin.

This year the sale is especially good, because not only have excellent older Garmin watches like the Forerunner 265 and Forerunner 965 returned to their lowest-ever price, but the latest Garmin watches like the Fenix 9 and Forerunner 170 have also been significantly discounted for the first time.

I've been testing Garmin watches for a decade and I think the only watch I can't find a deal on right now is the Enduro 4, but even there you don't have to worry, because the almost equally good Enduro 3 is down to $699 at Amazon.

I’ve picked out 17 deals on the best Garmin watches available right now, from cheaper Forerunner models all the way up to the Garmin Fenix 8 Pro, which is $849 in the Amazon sale and still a better buy than the Fenix 9 in the sale, in my opinion.

Recent findings suggest that I think the Forerunner 70 is the best beginner running watch available, and it actually has all the features runners of all levels really need. It's accurate and comfortable to wear 24/7 thanks to its small design, and this is the first time it's dropped under $200 on sale.

The Forerunner 165 is the predecessor to the Forerunner 70 and Forerunner 170 watches, and a good pick, especially for latest runners. This is one of the few Garmin deals I'm a little disappointed by this Black Friday, though, because the Forerunner 165 has dropped as low as $129 in the past.

Industry observers note that the Forerunner 170 has a few extra features compared to the Forerunner 70, most notably music storage, and also comes in this delightful teal color ( which I'd say makes it worth the upgrade alone). It's a great running watch and is getting its first significant discounts this Black Friday.

Industry observers note that this is the biggest percentage discount I've seen on a Garmin watch right now, and it makes the Instinct 3 AMOLED a strong, adventurous alternative to the Fenix and Enduro. It offers incredible battery life and a distinctive, rugged design, as well as reliable sports tracking, but it does lack the maps you get on pricier models.

This is one of the best deals going on for a Garmin right now, because even though the Forerunner 265 is an older watch, it's still an excellent multisport tracker with a bright AMOLED display. It even comes in two sizes and I'd say the only watch that can rival it for this price is the Coros Pace 4.

In a recent development, the Vivoactive 6 is an attractive smartwatch with impressive sports tracking credentials. It has dropped as low as $229 in the past, so a better deal might be yet to come on the watch this Black Friday, but this is still a good price on a well-rounded watch.

Recent findings suggest that the Forerunner 965 is the best alternative to newer top models like the Forerunner 970 and even the Fenix lineup if you're looking for value this Black Friday. It has brilliant sports tracking and navigation features, and a thin, lightweight design. You'll be hard-pressed to find a better watch from any brand for $449.

The Garmin Venu 4 combines its attractive, smartwatch-style design with Garmin's excellent sports tracking, and even as a marathoner, I prefer it to the more sports-focused Forerunner 570, partly because it has a built-in flashlight.

In a recent development, I don't think the Forerunner 570 offers better value than the Venu 4 or Forerunner 965 at the same price, but it does have some more striking design options than those watches, which might tempt you ot pick it up instead. It's a great sports tracker, of course, but lacks maps and a flashlight.

According to recent reports, the Venu X1 stands out from other Garmins thanks to its ultra-thin design, but even though it looks like a smartwatch, it's actually packed with the same brilliant sports and navigation features as you get on the Forerunner 970. At $300 off, it might be the sleeper hit of the sales.

The Epix Pro is the value alternative to the latest Fenix watches, but I'm a little disappointed it hasn't dropped to a lower price than this, because it has been as cheap as $499 in the past.

Industry observers note that I rate the Garmin Forerunner 970 as the best overall sports watch on the market, so getting any kind of discount on it is welcome, and this is a return to the lowest price it's ever been available. It has all of Garmin's top sports tracking and training analysis features, plus maps, all in a lightweight, durable design.

In a recent development, this is a good price for a great adventure watch if you prefer a MIP display, but the fact that the Fenix 7 Pro has been superseded by three rounds of Fenixes at this point makes me hope there might still be better deals to come. If not, this is still worth looking at as the cheapest titanium Garmin watch with maps and a MIP display on sale.

Industry observers note that I've just tested the Garmin Enduro 4 and I'd say the Enduro 3 is the better value buy for sure, because it has all the same key features as the new watch for $200 less on sale. Those features include the longest battery life of any Garmin, thanks to the Enduro 4's MIP display and solar panels.

According to recent reports, the Fenix 8 has now been superseded by the Fenix 8 Pro and Fenix 9, but I think it's still worth considering because it's cheaper than those watches on sale. You don't get the connectivity features available on newer Fenixes, but otherwise the Fenix 8 matches up well with regard to its core features, and it's also a little thinner than the upcoming models.

The upcoming Fenix 9 Pro has a few upgrades over the Fenix 8 Pro, including an all-titanium case, and is also on sale, but the Fenix 8 Pro is still a savvier buy thanks to this $350 saving, in my opinion. It has LTE and satellite connectivity on top of its outstanding sports tracking and navigation features, and is $150 less than the Fenix 9 Pro right now on sale.

It has been revealed that all models of the Garmin Fenix 9 are reduced by at least $100 for Black Friday, including the Garmin Fenix 9 Pro with InReach, which is the watch I'm using as my main tracker right now. If you want the latest and greatest Garmin watch, this is the time to buy it, because it's the first time it's been discounted anywhere.
