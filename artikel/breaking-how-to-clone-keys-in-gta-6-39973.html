📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, vice City's glow-up in GTA 6 ushers in a new era of smart car tech, bringing keyless ignition to the series. That's great for car owners, but a pain for our criminal duo since you can't hotwire a keyless car. Luckily, that doesn't mean you can't steal a car with high-tech security. Modern problems call for modern crimes, and that's where the key cloner comes in.

Standard trackers on stolen cars aren't much of a problem—they just forbid you from making modifications at a mod shop unless you register the car as your own.

Advanced trackers, however, are a whole other beast. They relay your exact location to the cops, but you can outrun the signal if you're fast enough. Keep your foot on the gas, and the cops won't be able to track you until you slow down. Apparently, the solution to beat high-tech surveillance is still a good ol' fashioned pedal-to-the-metal joyride.
