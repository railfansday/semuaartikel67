Breaking: Atmos Rewards Business Card review: A sleeper hit for Alaska and Hawaiian loyalists

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (195 words, 📖 Standard)
  • A newly refreshed business travel card is ideal for companies that frequently book Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines flights, especially those needing employee cards or using a dedicated business travel platform.
  • Key benefits include quarterly lounge passes, waived fees for eligible same-day confirmed flight changes, a free first checked bag, preferred boarding and complimentary points sharing.
  • Points earned can be redeemed for flights on Alaska, Hawaiian and various partner airlines, with notable value for long-haul premium-cabin awards and the ability to add free stopovers.
  • The card is most valuable for businesses with regular travel needs on these airlines, while those with infrequent travel may find less benefit.
  • The card charges a flat annual fee per company, with no extra cost for employee cards.
  • The highest earning rate is reserved for flights booked through the business travel platform, accessible only to companies spending at least $5,000 annually on Alaska and Hawaiian flights.
  • Existing cardholders retain their previous benefits and are not automatically transitioned to the new version.
