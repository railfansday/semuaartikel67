AC Milan produced a stunning second-half comeback to beat Olympiacos 4-2 and boost their chances of reaching the Europa League knockout stage.

According to recent reports, after 50 minutes in Greece it looked like being another miserable European night for Milan, who trailed through a Mike Maignan own-goal and a strike from Jota Silva. However, Omari Hutchinson inspired a comeback, scoring after a solo run then setting up Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic.

Diego Moreira finished off a counter-attack in the closing seconds to make sure of the three points, which boost the Rossoneri back into the top 24 with three games to go.

Below are how the sports newspapers in Italy look, with each of them mentioning Milan’s comeback in Piraeus.

In a recent development, la Gazzetta dello Sport: “Comeback. Milan rally and gets back in the hunt. Trailing by two goals, the Rossoneri win in Athens (4-2), inspired by Hutchinson. Amorim eyes the play-offs.”.

Sources indicate that corriere dello Sport: “Milan’s time. They turn the tables on Olympiacos: coming back from 0-2 to win 4-2 in just 39 minutes. “The playmaker scores with a spectacular coast-to-coast run, provides assists for Cissé and Pulisic, and finally launches the counter-attack finished off by Moreira. Amorim finds a glimmer of light in Europe, too: ‘It’s the beginning of something’.”.
