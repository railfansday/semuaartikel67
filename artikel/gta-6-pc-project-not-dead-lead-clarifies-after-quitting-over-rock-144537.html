GTA 6 has notably debuted without a PC release, landing on PlayStation 5 and the Xbox Series X|S only – a debacle that has, of course, led to fans attempting to get the Grand Theft Auto entry running on computers.

Industry observers note that there are various "projects," so to speak, attempting to tackle an unofficial PC port – but one in particular made rounds recently after Rockstar allegedly took its Discord server down and prompted its lead – an anonymous 15-year-old (as per their own words) – to say "goodbye," noting that "managing school and sports is hard enough without having middle-aged men yell and complain to you on Discord." Well, they're back… and so is their project, Leonida. And, according to a fresh post on Twitter from "NotRexed," the Leonida lead, they'll provide more information on their supposed GTA 6 PC port in the coming days. "Leonida is NOT dead," they write. "I have been doing a lot of work behind the scenes. I'll have some stuff to show all of you soon." They follow up on this with a response stating that Leonida should feature "native Linux support," with "more on that later.".

According to recent reports, obviously, it's difficult to verify anything they say, seeing as this is a (very) unofficial port, should it be happening at all. Rockstar itself hasn't actually spoken on the matter publicly. It all stemmed from notorious GTA 6 leaker "Cyberleek," who recently uploaded an executable file and the Xbox build of the game online. Shortly after, fans like NotRexed picked it up and began working toward a PC version of Grand Theft Auto 6.

Sources indicate that I'm not so sure what'll happen from here, but I am personally left wondering when Rockstar will announce an official PC release of the game. It's no small launch, after all, smashing records as the highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic and proving "the biggest launch in PlayStation history" (with Sony reporting "more than 39 million unique players" playing on the PS5 alone). Imagine how those numbers could grow….
