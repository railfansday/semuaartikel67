Amazon's Black Friday sale is really ramping up, and it's hard to believe the official big day is still a few days away. The retailer kicked off its discounts last week, and we've seen a good range of items drop in price. For trading card fans, there's plenty to get excited about.

As of Nov. 24, this Star Wars: Unlimited Ashes of the Empire Prerelease Box has dropped to a newly introduced low price of $25.49.

The set gives you everything you need to get started with the new expansion, which focuses on the heroes and villains from the Battle of Endor. Inside, you'll find six booster packs, two exclusive prerelease promo cards featuring Hyperspace versions of Luke Skywalker and Emperor Palpatine, 19 counters, a deck-building guide, and a deluxe deckbox.

The box is specifically designed for sealed tournament play, but you can also use the cards to build or upgrade your existing decks for head-to-head, multiplayer, or draft games.

Whether you're a Star Wars fan or an avid card collector, you can find this deal at Amazon right now.
