Beer Cheese Stuffing

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Thanksgiving is equally about food and football in my house, so this tailgate-inspired stuffing was a no-brainer creation for me. Filled with beer cheese sauce and topped with crispy bacon, this twist on the classic holiday side is so irresistible, it’ll be gone long before halftime of that first Thanksgiving Day game.

It has been revealed that how we’re making the beer cheese:A high-quality cheddar and flavorful beer are both key to making beer cheese sing. Make sure to choose a beer you love, since you’ll definitely taste it in the finished stuffing. I recommend a lager or an amber ale, since hoppier beers like IPAs can be too assertive in the larger context of a stuffing. When it comes to cheese, I love extra-sharp cheddar here, but you could also try a milder cheddar if you like.

Sources indicate that to achieve a glossy, silky texture, this beer cheese harnesses the dual powers of cornstarch and evaporated milk. Together with the lager, they form a smooth, stable base that helps keep the sauce smooth and emulsified, even when it’s heated to bubbling in the oven.

Make ahead & storage:I don’t recommend assembling this stuffing in advance, but if you want to work ahead, you can make the beer cheese up to 2 days before. Let it cool completely, then store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator. When you’re ready to assemble, reheat it slowly on low heat before layering it with the bread.
