Sources indicate that I think we’ve reached a point now where we need some more clarity from West Ham United about the future direction of the football club. Whilst I understand there is an overriding desire to get the Hammers promoted, something which has been described to us by club insiders as a priority, I’m increasingly concerned about how we intend to achieve it.

In a recent development, because as things stand at the moment, West Ham appear rudderless. In fact, I’d go as far as to say we’re listing. We’re getting progressively worse, and there is a worrying lack of direction at the football club.

Whilst there may be a desire to reach a certain destination, I see very little from West Ham at the moment to suggest that we know the route. And that’s why I believe we really need to hear from Daniel Křetínský.

Sources indicate that we’ve waited an awfully long time for the restructuring of West Ham’s shareholding to go through. And whilst Claret & Hugh reported earlier this month that the transactions had been completed, we’re still waiting for public confirmation from the football club.

Křetínský himself has indicated that he will engage with supporters once the share transaction has been finalised, but quite frankly, I don’t think that’s good enough anymore.

Sources indicate that west Ham are in desperate need of leadership. Whatever the situation with his shareholding, Křetínský is already in a position of authority at the football club.

West Ham are still waiting for Daniel Kretinsky to address supporters.

Whilst I don’t expect him to be shooting from the lip in the way David Sullivan used to, I do think he needs to provide some leadership, some direction and some communication with supporters as to what the actual plan is here.

Because almost everybody at every level of the football club, from the players right up to the very top, appears to be struggling.

Ideally, I’d like to know what the direction of travel is. What is the strategy for West Ham United?

It has been revealed that they want to take us forward, but how are they going to do that? What’s the plan for January? What’s the plan for investment?

We sold around £200m worth of players during the summer, we’ve yet to see the anticipated injection of funds from the shareholders, and we presently don’t look anything like a team capable of mounting a successful promotion challenge.

And that, by the club’s own admission, is supposed to be the number one priority.

Industry observers note that as things stand, the team are getting worse and the club appear to be getting worse. I don’t think West Ham supporters are being unreasonable in wanting to know what the people running our football club intend to do about it.

We need to hear from Daniel Křetínský.
