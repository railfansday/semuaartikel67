📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, open J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing if you bank with Chase and want commission-free trades in stocks, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), and mutual funds in the same app as your checking account. Skip it if you trade options spreads or futures, neither of which it offers.

The account is built into the Chase Mobile app and Chase’s website, next to your Chase checking and savings, and cash moves from checking into it instantly. You don’t need a Chase bank account to open one, and there’s no minimum to start.

Chase customers who invest in funds and stocks get commission-free trades and instant transfers without leaving the Chase app, though anyone moving money in just for the bonus should compare other brokerage account bonuses first. Options traders who want spreads do better at Fidelity, which allows them with approval.
