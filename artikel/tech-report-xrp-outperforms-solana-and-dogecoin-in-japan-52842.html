📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Although XRP is at risk of losing its top-four position in the global cryptocurrency market, the asset still ranks among the three largest crypto assets in Japan.

In a recent post shared by the renowned Ripple-backed XRP treasury firm Evernorth, the firm highlighted XRP’s growing performance in the Japan crypto market.

Earlier today, Evernorth shared monthly data from the Japan Virtual and Crypto Assets Exchange Association (JVCEA) revealing that XRP recorded about ¥132.8 billion in spot trading volume in September 2026.

While this accounted for about 15% of Japan's total crypto spot trading volume during the month, XRP emerged as the third most-traded cryptocurrency in Japan after Bitcoin and Ethereum.

This reveals that XRP has outperformed other major altcoins that are right now trading in the Japan crypto market, hitting about $880 million in trading volume.

While further data indicated Solana and Dogecoin ranking next to XRP on the list as the fourth- and fifth-largest cryptocurrencies in Japan, respectively, the data also showed a wide gap from XRP’s ¥132.8 billion spot volume.

This means that XRP is significantly ahead of Solana and Dogecoin in the Japan crypto trading market, as Solana recorded only about ¥19.47 billion in trading volume during the same month.

Dogecoin, on the other hand, saw less participation, as it recorded only ¥11.27 billion in spot volume during the same period.

With XRP sitting in the third spot, the asset has become one of the most actively traded cryptocurrencies in Japan despite its persistent price volatility.
