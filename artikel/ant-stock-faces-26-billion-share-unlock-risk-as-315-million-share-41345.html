Anthropic could see up to $26 billion worth of shares become eligible for sale on its first trading day, according to details in its IPO prospectus. Around 315 million shares may become available immediately, while another 3.4 billion shares could unlock before the 180-day lockup period ends. The prospectus also outlines longer restrictions for the company’s co-founders, who face lockups of up to one year. The early share unlocks could create selling pressure as ANT begins trading on Nasdaq, giving investors another factor to watch beyond its $83 IPO price and $1.5 trillion valuation.
