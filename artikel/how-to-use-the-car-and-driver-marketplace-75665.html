📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We at Car and Driver pride ourselves on our institutional knowledge and in-depth testing. It's why we've spent the last 70 years analyzing, reviewing, and testing the latest vehicles on the market.

If you want to know the ins and outs of just about any car sold in the United States, then you come to Car and Driver. And now, if you want to buy a newly introduced or used vehicle, then you . . . come to Car and Driver.

Specifically, the Car and Driver Marketplace. Powered by Autotrader, which offers one of the largest inventories of vehicle listings on the web, C/D Marketplace makes us a one-stop shop for researching and purchasing a vehicle.

Not sure where to find or how to use our Marketplace? Then we've got you covered. Read on to see how easy it is to find your next car using C/D Marketplace.

Car and Driver Marketplace is for all shoppers in the market for a latest or used vehicle—enthusiast and non-enthusiast alike. Whether you’re looking for a sports car to scratch your driving itch, a minivan or SUV for hauling the family around, or a pickup truck to take to work, you can use Car and Driver Marketplace to search through thousands of new- and used-vehicle listings.

Car and Driver Marketplace is a vehicle shopping tool for consumers in the market for a fresh or used vehicle. We have thousands of new- and used-vehicle listings for vehicles being sold across the nation.

Whether you’re daydreaming about a certain make or model of vehicle, in the early stages of researching your next vehicle, or ready to reach out to sellers to purchase a fresh vehicle, you’ll find there’s no wrong time to use Car and Driver Marketplace.

Powered by Autotrader, Car and Driver Marketplace hosts one of the web’s largest inventories of upcoming- and used-vehicle listings. But Car and Driver Marketplace doesn’t just help you find a new or used vehicle; it also helps you purchase one. With the ability to message sellers directly, submit offers, and calculate lease and finance payments, Car and Driver Marketplace can support just about the entirety of your car-buying journey from start to finish.

Industry observers note that enter the C/D Marketplace in one of two ways: from our home page or by going to caranddriver.com/cars-for-sale (feel free to bookmark it too).If you opt to enter C/D Marketplace from our home page, click on "Shop for Cars" on the top left of the page. Using a mobile device? Tap the drop-down menu on the top left of the page, then press "Shop for Cars" at the top of the list.

Industry observers note that once on the C/D Marketplace page, we recommend adjusting the filters. They appear on the left side of the page on desktop, and as a dropdown menu on the top left of the page on mobile.

Don't let the sheer number of filter options intimidate you. You can mess with as many or as few of them as you like. We do recommend at least filling out the "Location" field with your ZIP code and adjusting the radius of your search, which you can set from as few as 50 miles to the entire United States.

Some filters might be more key to you than others, such as the one labeled "Price & Payments" that allows you to set a price range for your search.

That's easy to figure out if you're a cash buyer, but what if you're looking to finance or lease your next car? Then click on the "Set Budget" link (or "Revision Budget" if you want to make adjustments to your already set budget). Toggle "Show Payments on Listings" too.

After you click the "Set Budget" (or "Enhancement Budget") link, a pop-up window appears. It allows you to input your budget, the trade-in value of your vehicle (assuming you have a trade), and the amount you're planning to spend toward the down payment.

Once you enter this information, you have the option to further granulate your monthly payment looks for the car you're interested in. You can set your budget for either leasing or financing. (Click the "Return to Dashboard" link after making an individual adjustment to get back to the pop-up window's home page.).

Now, go to the upper right side of the screen and click on the "Sort By" drop-down menu to make sure C/D Marketplace is presenting listings in your preferred order. Mobile users will find this setting under the "Filters" menu at the top left of the screen.

The page defaults to sorting by relevance to your query, but you can also have it sort by price (lowest or highest), distance (closest), date (oldest or newest posted), vehicle mileage (lowest or highest), or model year (newest or oldest).

In a recent development, you're ready to scroll through listings now. See something you like? Click on it! Here's an example of what an individual listing page looks like. We picked a listing of our EV of the Year–winning 2025 Porsche Taycan in GTS form as an example.

As seen here, individual listings allow you to open up additional images of the vehicle, see basic specs (more detailed ones are available if you scroll farther down the page), adjust your spend, and even send a message or an offer to the seller.

Now that you know the basic steps of how to use the Car and Driver Marketplace, it's time to take it for a spin yourself. Your next car may be just a few clicks away.
