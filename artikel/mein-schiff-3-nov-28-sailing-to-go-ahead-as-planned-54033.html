Mein Schiff 3 will resume its sailings later this month, TUI shared in an update.

Following repair work at Lloyd Werft’s Bremerhaven pier, the ship will embark on its scheduled four-night cruise from Bremerhaven to Kiel via Oslo on Nov. 28.

“We know that many of our guests have been eagerly looking forward to their upcoming voyage. We’re all the more delighted to be able to welcome them back aboard Mein Schiff 3, and we wish all our guests a wonderful trip,” TUI noted.

The ship was damaged on October 25, when it collided with a container ship while leaving Bremerhaven in stormy conditions.

Two cruises were canceled as a result, the first one being a cruise to the Canaries, where the ship was headed when the collision occurred.

The second one was the “Enchanting Norway” cruise that was scheduled for  November 14-28, canceled because TUI judged repairs would take longer than planned.
