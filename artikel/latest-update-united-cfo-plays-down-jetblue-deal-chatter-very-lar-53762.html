As merger talk with JetBlue has made a comeback, United is shutting it down, with CEO Scott Kirby still set on a mega-merger with American.

United Airlines threw cold water on the possibility of a JetBlue acquisition, even as industry chatter of a deal grows following JetBlue’s bond selloff in October.

“As far as acquiring other airlines, you’ve got to look at the whole enterprise value, not just the equity value,” stated United chief financial officer Mike Leskinen at the Wings Club in Upcoming York on Thursday when asked about JetBlue. “And the carrier we spoke of has a very large debt balance.”.

The question asked whether JetBlue could provide United better connectivity to regions like the Caribbean and Latin America. Leskinen noted United’s hubs in Washington Dulles and Houston already give it access to those destinations.
