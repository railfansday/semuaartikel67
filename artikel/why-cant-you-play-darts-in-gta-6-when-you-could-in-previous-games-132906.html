Why can't you play darts in GTA 6 when you could in previous games? Because Rockstar "didn't have the resources" to do it to a standard it'd be happy with

According to recent reports, if you've been a fan of Grand Theft Auto for a while, you might have noticed a strange absence in GTA 6. While the game is packed full of different activities, the recurring pastime of darts is missing, having been present in GTA 5, GTA Online, and GTA 4.

In a recent development, the reason for the lack of darts in Grand Theft Auto 6? Well, according to Rockstar North co-studio lead Rob Nelson, it's a simple case of Rockstar having too little time and resources to make it to a high standard.

Sources indicate that nelson told VGC: "The darts one is a contentious one. Even we... You hear they have unlimited money or unlimited time, even we do not and so sometimes you just have to admit we are not going to be able to make this to the level we feel we need to.".

It has been revealed that nelson elaborated that since Red Dead Redemption 2, Rockstar has taken to expanding what were small mini-games into full-on activities. He pointed to basketball in GTA 6 as an example, which you can play in a way that feels like an extension of gameplay with "fluid" movement. Even if it's a relatively small chunk of the game, a lot of time went into making it feel like "a game in its own right", Nelson stated. And the same is true of pool.

"A lot of work [is needed] to get it tuned right. Darts is one we just didn't get to," Nelson mentioned. The studio "didn't have the resources" to include it in the game and have it up to par. "It's better not to do it than to do it and have it not be good enough," he said.

Even without darts, there are plenty of side activities in Grand Theft Auto 6 that help expand Vice City. This includes diving and treasure hunting and fishing, both of which you unlock near the start of the game.
