Sources indicate that for those of us who grew up watching Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings movies, it's quite difficult to go back and read J.R.R. Tolkien's novels without picturing Bag End or Rivendell as they appeared in the movies. Much of that is down to the work of Alan Lee, whose association with Middle-earth predates that of Jackson by quite some time, and whose illustrations helped inform the look of the world the director subsequently built in Upcoming Zealand. There's something particularly lovely about going back to Tolkien's books and recognizing elements of Lee's artwork that eventually made their way onto the major screen. And if you've been contemplating another trip through Middle-earth over Christmas, there's never been a cheaper way of doing so with Lee as your guide.

As per reports, Amazon has dropped the price of Lee's illustrated collection of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings to an all-time low of $61.83 for Black Friday. The four-volume hardcover collection ordinarily retails for $150, meaning you'll be saving just over $88 on the set, and each of the four books works out at a little over $15. The collection comes with its own illustrated slipcase and contains The Hobbit, The Fellowship of the Ring, The Two Towers, and The Return of the King.

Industry observers note that one of the nice things about having all four books illustrated by the same artist is being able to follow Bilbo and Frodo's respective journeys through a version of Middle-earth that feels consistent. Tolkien's original text remains unchanged across the four volumes, although Lee's work extends beyond the illustrations inside the books themselves, with each hardcover featuring his artwork on both its cover and spine.

In a recent development, given how much material Jackson and his team had to work with when adapting Tolkien's novels, having somebody who'd already spent years thinking about what the author's world might look like was particularly useful. Lee worked as a conceptual designer on Jackson's trilogy alongside fellow Tolkien illustrator John Howe, with Lee subsequently winning an Academy Award for his work on The Return of the King. Both artists would later return to Middle-earth for Jackson's The Hobbit trilogy.

It has been revealed that lee's illustrated collection of The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings is available for $61.83 at Amazon at the time of writing. Stay tuned to Collider for more news and updates on all things Middle-earth.
