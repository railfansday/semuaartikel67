Davide Bartesaghi is the latest Milan player to report an injury concern ahead of the team’s Europa League match away against Olympiacos on Thursday and will not travel with the Rossoneri squad for Athens according to the latest updates.

Reports on Wednesday morning confirmed that Francesco Camarda was a strong doubt for the Olympiacos match after suffering an ankle issue earlier in the week.

Now according to reports from Gianluca Di Marzio, Bartesaghi has also pulled up with peritendinitis in his right leg during Wednesday morning’s training session and will not be able to play on Thursday.

Bartesaghi started for Milan in their first game back after the recent international break over the weekend, which ended in a 5-1 victory at home against Frosinone in Serie A.

Milan now have a list of nine players who will not be able to feature in Athens on Thursday night: Bartesaghi and Camarda add to an injury list that already included Luka Modric, Strahinja Pavlovic, Matteo Gabbia, Alexis Saelemaekers, Samuel Chukwueze, Ruben Loftus-Cheek and Pietro Terracciano.

Recent findings suggest that it will be a tough test for the Rossoneri on Thursday night, up against an Olympiacos side that sit among the top eight after winning three of their first four matches in the Europa League. Ruben Amorim’s side, meanwhile, have one just once and lost three of their first four matches in the competition.
