Bruno Fernandes scored a bizarre long-range effort in Manchester United's Champions League contest with Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday night.

The 20-time English champions trailed at the interval of the European fixture, with Luis Suarez scoring from the penalty spot after a rash challenge from Senne Lammens.

Suarez had missed a first-half penalty following a tackle from Lisandro Martinez but was able to register at the second time of asking to send the Portuguese outfit ahead.

However, the match turned in the early stages of the second period, with Fernandes first scoring from the penalty spot to level it at 1-1 before netting a second two minutes later.

There did not appear to be much danger for Sporting when Fernandes sent an attempt towards the home side's goal, but it took an incredible bounce off the turf before flying past goalkeeper Kaique Pereira, who could not believe what had happened.
