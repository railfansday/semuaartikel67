📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A paloma is a classic summer cocktail in my rotation, but I still want an excuse to enjoy it throughout the colder-weather months. Enter: the cranberry paloma. Just like a classic paloma, this version is made with a base of tequila and grapefruit. But it gets a boost of sweetness and tartness with the addition of cranberry juice cocktail. This sweetened cranberry-based beverage replaces the need for sugary sodas or simple syrup. Bonus: It adds a gorgeous red hue to your glass, perfect for the holiday season.

Sources indicate that reposado tequila:The type of tequila you use is ultimately up to you, but I recommend either reposado or blanco tequila. Reposado tequila is aged for up to a year, typically in oak barrels, and takes on a warm sweetness with notes of vanilla and caramel. It’s a great choice for cocktails (especially when grapefruit is involved) because the smoothness balances out the tart and bright citrus.

According to recent reports, blanco tequila:Blanco tequila, on the other hand, is for people who want to double down on the brightness. It’s barely aged, if aged at all, so it retains the sharp, vegetal flavor of the agave plant. This recipe works well with either option, so it all comes down to personal preference and what you already have on your bar cart.
