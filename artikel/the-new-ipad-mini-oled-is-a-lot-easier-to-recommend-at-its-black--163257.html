In a recent development, apple's newest tablet would be nearly perfect if it wasn't so expensive. The new iPad Mini OLED is undeniably great, and Apple upgraded this tablet in a lot of ways.

Sources indicate that but as I noted in my iPad Mini review, I definitely do not love the $699 price. That makes this tablet almost as expensive as the iPad Air, which has a larger display and Apple's powerful M4 chip. Even though this tablet was just released in October, Amazon has gone ahead and reduced the price by $100 for Black Friday.

Industry observers note that and at its upcoming $599 price tag, the iPad Mini OLED suddenly looks a lot more attractive. If you've been wanting a new Apple tablet for watching TV demonstrates and movies on the go, playing games on the couch, or talking to ChatGPT, then I think you'll really appreciate this Black Friday tech deal.

In a recent development, the new iPad Mini has some subtle but vital design changes. Apple removed the speaker grilles at the bottom of the device in favor of its brand-new Acoustic Display technology. Why does that matter? Because the iPad Mini now has IP67 water resistance. In theory, you could drop it in the tub, and it would keep on kicking.

Most importantly, the iPad Mini now has an OLED display, and it looks incredible. The sharpness and clarity will be noticeably improved from the last iPad you used (unless you have the tandem-OLED iPad Pro, in which case, what are you doing here?). The color vibrancy, deep blacks, HDR brightness, and contrast all shine here. You can even use this iPad in bright, direct sunlight.
