Jeff Stelling had backed QPR for relegation from the Championship, as they now chase promotion to the Premier League.

It has been a positive campaign so far for the R’s, who are in the play-off places, and they have played some good football under Julien Stephan.

This has come on the back of an inconsistent first season under the Frenchman, which ultimately saw QPR finish 15th.

Therefore, it was hard to predict exactly how QPR would perform this season. Whilst there had been some encouraging signs under Stephan, the Londoners lost eight of their last 13 games in the previous season, which included four straight defeats at the end.

So, there was frustration among the supporters that they couldn’t build on a decent start, and many wondered whether they would take that negative momentum into the 2026/27 season.

And, it clearly influenced Stelling, with the TalkSPORT presenter and former Soccer Saturday host predicting relegation for QPR back in August.

It should be mentioned that Stelling made it clear he had no problem with the R’s, and he even admitted he would ‘offend’ their fans, and that he ‘hopes’ he would be wrong.

Pleasingly for QPR, it looks like he will be, as Stephan’s side are 16 points clear of the bottom three, and they’re just seven points behind leaders Middlesbrough, so they are certainly focusing on the other end of the table right now.

We all make calls that are way off, and that looks to be the case with Stelling and QPR here.

The Championship is notoriously difficult, and that unpredictability is why it’s such an enthralling league, even if it was a bold shout to say the R’s could be relegated.

Nevertheless, they are having a good season so far, and most fans would’ve snapped your hand off to be in the play-offs right now, particularly with the way the team are playing.

That’s probably the most encouraging aspect of everything for the R’s, as it doesn’t feel like they’re in a false position, and, if anything, they maybe deserve to be higher in the table.

All credit for that goes to Stephan, as he has built a stylish, attacking side that has proven they can compete with the best teams in this league.

So, the focus for QPR will be to make Stelling’s prediction look even worse come May, when they will hope to be back in the Premier League.

That may seem like a long shot when you consider the resources of the clubs they are competing against, but, as outlined above, they are a very good Championship team right now.

There will be concerns about how sustainable a promotion push is, and the lack of depth in certain areas, such as the final third, could be their undoing.

Therefore, January will be crucial, and the R’s may need reinforcements if they are to make this season a truly memorable one.
