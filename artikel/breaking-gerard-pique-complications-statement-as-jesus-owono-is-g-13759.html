FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono has been heavily punished after he was sent off in an incident that led to the death of CD Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel.

De Miguel was left fighting for his life after a heavy collision with Owono during the match at Estadio Heliodoro Rodríguez López on November 2.

The Spaniard was rushed to hospital after suffering a blunt cardiac injury on the pitch. He was resuscitated but passed away the following day.

The Disciplinary Committee of the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF) have handed Owono a 12-match ban following the incident.

Industry observers note that they remarked in a statement: "The Disciplinary Committee, as an independent body within the federation's structure, has agreed to suspend Jesús Lázaro Ferrer Owono, a player for FC Andorra, for twelve matches for his actions during the CD Tenerife – FC Andorra match. Twelve matches is the maximum penalty allowed by the Disciplinary Code for the events described in the referee's report.

"The referee's report is the only document in the disciplinary proceedings file, and the Committee must determine the sanction based solely on this information. The report describes a violent action, with the leg raised and excessive force used, while the ball was in play. In light of these circumstances, the Committee has determined that the events fall under Article 102 of the Disciplinary Code.

"Furthermore, the subsequent consequences of the action, which are also documented in the referee's report and are public knowledge, have been taken into account to increase the sanction to the maximum level permitted by the regulations. Under no circumstances may the Committee exceed the threshold expressly stipulated in the applicable article.".

In a recent development, FC Andorra owner Gerard Pique has now reacted to Owono's ban in an emotional statement. The 39-year-old is adamant that the Equatoguinean did not deserve such a heavy ban.

He said: "This Friday afternoon, we received word that the Committee had handed down a 12-game suspension to Jesús Owono. Let me be clear: Jesús will need more than 12 games before he’s available to play again. No suspension will bring Demi back to us.

It has been revealed that "It’s been incredible to see the outpouring of support and affection from all his teammates and family members, and it’s clear that the void he’ll leave behind is enormous. It’s evident that this situation has had a massive impact, not just on Spanish football but worldwide. It’s something completely inexplicable that has never happened before in football, and even now we’re still wondering why.

Sources indicate that "Why did this happen? Players, former players, coaches, goalkeepers... they’ve all called it an accident. I truly believe that imposing the maximum penalty on Jesús for this incident puts him in a situation he doesn’t deserve, because he isn’t to blame for any of this. My take on all this is that the 12-game suspension for Jesús Owono seems to me to be placing the blame on Jesús for consequences that weren’t his fault. I believe the penalty should be based on the action itself, not on the consequences.".

Sources indicate that pique then referred to an incident in the match between Albacete and Sabadell earlier this month. José Corpas was given a straight red card for kicking Quadri Liameed in the head. Fortunately, Liameed did not suffer a serious injury and Corpas was only given a three-match ban as a result.

He added: "With all this in mind, my reflection is on this: What is being punished, the action or the consequences? I’m making this video because this situation has affected me very deeply on a personal level. First, I was in Tenerife when it happened, and when I went down to the locker room and saw our players’ reaction, I realised that it’s an experience that will stay with all of us who were there for the rest of our lives.".

It has been revealed that pique then referred to an incident involving him and former Barcelona goalkeeper Victor Valdes in 2012. In their Champions League semi-final clash against Chelsea, Pique took a blow to the head after colliding with Valdes. He had to leave the pitch and woke up in hospital. Pique said Valdes wasn't to blame and that the situation with Owono was very similar.

He finished: "And we humans often need to blame someone because, otherwise, we can't understand why this happens on the field. It's an accident. I think imposing the maximum penalty on Jesús puts him in an unfair situation, because he isn't to blame for what ended up happening.".

Tenerfie manager Alvaro Cervera disagrees with Pique, saying Owono's ban may seem light, but that the rules don't allow for anything else.

According to recent reports, owono, meanwhile, is said to be distraught following De Miguel's death. It was revealed last week that he wanted to reach out to De Miguel’s family in order to ‘share in their grief and offer his condolences in person’. It is unknown when he will return to football, if at all.
