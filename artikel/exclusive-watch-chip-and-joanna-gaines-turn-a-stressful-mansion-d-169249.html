When Chip and Joanna Gaines started “sweating” over their massive remodel being documented on “Fixer Upper: Chip Bought a Mansion,” they both leaned into the laughs to turn a challenging moment into a scene out of “a "Saturday Night Live" skit.”.

The six-episode spinoff series, which is due to premiere on Dec. 27 on Magnolia Network and HGTV, sees the husband and wife team take on what they deem “the biggest fixer we’ve ever done”: a 15,000-square-foot mansion that sits on a 5.7-acre lot in their hometown of Waco, TX.

Recent findings suggest that chip had unilaterally decided to purchase the outdated manse three years ago. Once he realized the palatial investment property had more issues than anticipated, he turned to Joanna for help transforming the space. Adding to the pressure was a three-month timeline to transform the place.

In an exclusive clip shared with Realtor.com from the back-to-back premiere episodes, the Gainses hold a pre-renovation open house with two of their designer friends to get a fresh perspective and some professional guidance on what changes are necessary.

The four experts gather in the mansion’s oversized living room that Jo previously described as something out of “Beauty and the Beast” due to the high ceiling, multiple decorative columns, and golden paint covering the walls. The room is anchored by honey-toned hardwood floors and adorned with elaborate trim, molding, and millwork throughout.

Standing in front of a cream-colored fireplace with a sprawling floral arrangement on its mantel, Chip asks the women what task they want him to tackle.

“What curtains would you like me to demo?” he inquires.

When one of the designers comments that Chip “needs a man job” to do, he signals he’s totally up for the challenge.

“A man job. A real man,” notes Chip.

Joanna explains that “they really want to see the room without curtains,” so Chip makes his way toward a wall of windows and French doors that are concealed by a peachy-orange, grid-patterned window covering lined with pom-poms and tassels.

As the impromptu demo day begins to unfold, Jo nervously places her hands on her head while Chip confidently tugs on one side of the curtain.

“This is turning into a ‘Saturday Night Live’ skit,” Joanna indicates. “I’m sweating.”.

“I feel like this is going to be totally doable,” Chips responds.

According to recent reports, chip’s prediction is correct. The curtain comes down easily, so Joanna praises her husband and directs him to continue.

According to recent reports, “My hero! OK, do the other thing,” she notes.

Chip proceeds, but he rapidly discovers the second side requires a bigger effort to bring down the drapery due to the metal track that held up the coverings in the first place.

Industry observers note that “Oh, God. There was a little more to that one,” admits Chip.

Jo and the designer guests are pleased with what’s exposed. Natural light pours into the large living space from the glass doors and windows that now clearly have a view of the property’s mature trees and a covered patio built with beautiful red brick.

It has been revealed that “Yes, I’m feeling it. Pretty awesome,” the designers say.

Recent findings suggest that typically, it’s Chip who often displays his silly side on work sites, while Jo plays the part of his professional-minded foil. However, pleased with the results of the curtain reveal, a noticeably relieved Joanna momentarily pivots from interior design to fashion design.

“Style me. I want a dress,” she says as she wraps herself up in the downed curtains with an assist from Chip.

Industry observers note that “Well, twist. Turn, turn, turn. There you go. Now that’s it. Yeah, just that. Wow,” he comments when he sees the finished frock on his wife.

“Very Italian. That’s cool. Pretty awesome,” notes the admiring group.

After Jo models her latest look, more of the mansion’s ground floor is visible. Just past the double glass door entrance of the home, a staircase with an ornate wrought-iron railing leads to the second floor, and a black table fills space in the grand foyer.

According to recent reports, behind Jo, a rendering of her vision for the room reveals the area styled with an oversized mirror above the fireplace and several comfy chairs placed opposite a couch. With that blueprint in place, she lays out the next steps for the rest of the residence.

“So I think at this point, it’s mainly the kitchen, and then it’s just a refresh,” states Joanna.

The pair of experts agree and reassure the Gainses that a less is more approach is the way to go.

In a recent development, “You don’t need to add any panelling. You don’t need to add anything. This is just, paint it. Simplify. Just, like, slow the roll, relax. Yeah, you got this,” they say.

As the action begins on “Fixer Upper: Chip Bought a Mansion,” the upgraded Waco Lake estate is currently listed on the market by Amanda Nesbitt of Magnolia Realty. While one qualified buyer was reportedly turned off by the new renovations, the $7.39 million price tag will hopefully make the rollercoaster of emotions Chip and Joanna have experienced throughout the process well worth the ride.

Recent findings suggest that “Fixer Upper: Chip Bought a Mansion” premieres Sunday, Dec. 27 at 9 p.m. on Magnolia Network and HGTV and streams the next day on discovery+ and HBO Max.
