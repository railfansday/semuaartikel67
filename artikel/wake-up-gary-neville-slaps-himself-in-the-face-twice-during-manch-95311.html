Manchester United’s heavy defeat by Liverpool led to Gary Neville slapping himself in the face twice on live television as he took aim at their dreadful defending.

Mauricio Pochettino’s debut as Man Utd boss ended in a 4-0 loss at Anfield, with the Red Devils collapsing after Leny Yoro’s early red card.

Again criticising United’s defence, Neville called out the players’ body language, accusing them of being asleep.

He mentioned on Sky Sports: “I never see any of them on their toes, alert, looking like they’ve got something fixed in here,” before pointing to his head.

Sources indicate that “I never see any of them look like that. They look like that,” Neville added, providing a gormless look and pausing for several seconds, before stating “wake up” and slapping himself in the face twice.

With fellow pundit Jamie Carragher bursting into uncontrollable laughter, Neville finished his impassioned rant by adding: “You play for Man United!”.

According to recent reports, “I thought United were desperate, I thought they were so bad, and Neville who has played for United hundreds of times stated he’s okay with four nil? Obviously it [the red card] doesn’t help.

“But are you telling me they couldn’t have done better? At least show a bit more fight, a bit more quality.
