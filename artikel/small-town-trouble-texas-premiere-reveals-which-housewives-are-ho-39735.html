The series premiere of Small Town Trouble: Texas introduced a new set of Housewives — and revealed which ones were sleeping together outside of their marriages.

During the Wednesday, November 18, episode of the Bravo show, Aaryn Williams, who is married to husband Nick Williams, took the next step in her flirtatious relationship with friend Annari Uys, who is also married.

According to recent reports, aaryn and Annari’s connection was the talk of their small town despite both of their husbands knowing that they were exploring their feelings for each other. There was also Gabby Hernandez Huffman, who was learning more about her sexuality in her marriage to husband Jordan Huffman.

Gabby revealed visible interest in Aaryn but they didn’t appear to take things beyond a friendship — yet — due to Aaryn’s arrangement with Annari. The various entanglements in the series premiere could appear similar to Netflix’s The Hunting Wives, which is a scripted show centered around housewives in Texas — many of whom are hooking up.

Small Town Trouble: Texas, meanwhile, is described as an introduction to Boerne’s “most-talked-about social circle,” which includes a “charming yet unconventional group of wives, mothers, and social insiders.”.

According to recent reports, bravo’s fresh show will offer a glimpse at how “conservative values of small-town life collide with forbidden attraction, messy friendships, and shocking secrets,” according to the official synopsis. “Here, perception is everything, temptation is everywhere, and the truth is never as buttoned-up as it looks.”.

Industry observers note that before appearing on Small Town Trouble: Texas, Aaryn made headlines when she competed on Big Brother 15 in 2013. She received backlash for making derogatory statements about fellow housemate Helen Kim‘s Asian heritage and using a homophobic slur toward contestant Andy Herren, which she addressed on her new show.

“It is my biggest regret in my life,” she remarked in the premiere about how her upbringing caused her to develop “deeply ingrained” prejudice. “It was actually who I was.”.

It has been revealed that aaryn, who shares daughters Skyla, 10, Adeline, 8, Reese, 7, and Kodi, 4, with Nick, claimed her beliefs have changed in recent years, adding, “My daughters will never have to worry no matter who they become one day. I’m going to be supporting them, Nick and I both, but had I not been called out for my behavior, I don’t think I would have been the one to break the cycle within my family.”.

Small Town Trouble: Texas airs on Bravo Wednesdays at 9 p.m. ET. New episodes will be available to stream on Peacock the next day.
