📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Utah on Tuesday dismissed criminal charges against Kalshi and its two co-founders following the decision by the prediction market platform on Monday to suspend operations in the state.

Recent findings suggest that the state originally filed the 14 criminal charges against the company and founders Tarek Mansour and Luana Lopes Lara on Oct. 28, claiming that Kalshi operated an illegal gambling operation in the state.

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission — the federal regulator for prediction markets which views itself as the sole body that can develop rules over the growing industry — sued Utah the day before the state announced its charges.

Recent findings suggest that mansour and Lara were slated to appear in a Utah district court on Dec. 4, according to court documents.

Industry observers note that however, Kalshi suspended operations in Utah on Monday, citing legal uncertainty. Utah Attorney General Derek Brown celebrated the decision in a statement announcing the move to dismiss the state's charges.

"Every company in this space should take note," he said. "If they promote, support, or enable gambling, we will hold them accountable.".

Brown's office noted in the statement that dismissal is without prejudice, meaning if Kalshi were to begin operations again in the state the charges could be refiled.

"Good faith jurisdictional disagreements should never be criminalized," Kalshi's general counsel Yaakov Roth stated in a post on X. "We stand ready to work with State AGs on reasonable interim measures until courts resolve the dispute over who regulates prediction markets.".

Recent findings suggest that while Kalshi faces legal setbacks in Utah, it faced some relief in New York on Monday when a state judge denied an emergency temporary restraining order against the company to halt its operations in the jurisdiction. The court will hear arguments on the matter again in January.

Disclosure: CNBC and Kalshi have a commercial relationship that includes customer acquisition and a minority investment.
