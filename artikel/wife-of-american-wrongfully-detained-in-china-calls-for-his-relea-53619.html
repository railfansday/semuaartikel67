It has been revealed that the wife of U.S. citizen Min Zin, whom the State Department has declared "wrongfully detained" in China, issued a fresh call for his release Thursday after President Donald Trump met with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

"It has now been nearly six months since my husband, Min Zin, a United States citizen and independent academic, was lured to China and wrongfully detained," Sylvia Zin noted in a statement.

Industry observers note that "My husband is being held by the Chinese government on false charges at an undisclosed location in solitary confinement without any access to legal counsel," she added. "We have no idea why they detained him and why they continue to hold him.".

It has been revealed that "This is the second in-person meeting that President Trump has held with Chinese President Xi since Min Zin was arrested. I am grateful for the President's commitment in continuing to raise my husband's wrongful detention and his work to secure Min Zin's immediate and unconditional release," Sylvia Zin also noted. "My daughter and I remain hopeful in the President's ability to bring him home to us in time for the holidays.".
