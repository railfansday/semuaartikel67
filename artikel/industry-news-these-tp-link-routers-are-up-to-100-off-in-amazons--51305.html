Black Friday isn't until November 27, but Amazon's early Black Friday 2026 sale is already bringing some decent discounts on TP-Link routers, with prices starting at $44.99 and savings of up to $100. The deals cover quite a few different networking needs, from compact travel routers that let you connect multiple devices to hotel wifi to more powerful home routers designed for faster internet prepares and households with lots of connected devices.

It has been revealed that TP-Link’s Roam routers are compact enough to travel with and let you share a hotel or public internet connection across your devices through a private Wi-Fi network—and right now, Amazon has three models on sale: the Roam 6 AX1500 for $44.99 (was $59.99), the Roam 6 AX3000 for $55.98 (was $79.99), and the Roam 7 BE3600 for $87.99 (was $119.99). All three support VPN connections and multiple operating modes, with the main differences coming down to speed. The AX1500 offers up to 1.5Gbps, the AX3000 doubles that and adds a 2.5Gbps WAN port, while the Wi-Fi 7-enabled Roam 7 reaches up to 3.6Gbps.

The TP-Link Archer AX21 V5 AX1800 is down to $54.99 from $89.99, saving you $35 on an affordable Wi-Fi 6 router for everyday home use. Its dual-band connection offers combined theoretical speeds of up to 1.8Gbps, which should be sufficient for typical activities like streaming, video calls, and browsing across several devices.

Recent findings suggest that for homes with faster internet intends, multiple gamers, or lots of connected devices, TP-Link's Archer BE700 and BE770 offer tri-band Wi-Fi 7, including access to the 6GHz band for compatible devices. Both support EasyMesh for extended coverage and have 10Gbps WAN ports, although the BE770 provides faster wireless speeds and more wired connectivity. As for pricing, the TP-Link Archer BE700 is down to $248 from $349.99, saving you just over $100. It offers theoretical speeds of up to 15Gbps, a 2.5Gbps LAN port, and three Gigabit LAN ports. For just $52 more, the Archer BE770 (now $299.99, was $369.99) bumps speeds up to 18Gbps and adds a dedicated 10Gbps LAN port, making it better suited to high-speed network storage and large file transfers.

It has been revealed that the TP-Link Deco BE25 is down to $99.98 from $129.99, saving you $30 on a Wi-Fi 7 router designed to work as part of a mesh network. It offers dual-band wireless speeds of up to 5Gbps, two 2.5Gbps Ethernet ports, and AI-driven roaming that helps connected devices switch between Deco units as you move around your home.
