For those still waiting for GTA 6 to unlock, it's possible you have something of a mistaken impression of what the early hours will look like. Despite partially sharing a setting with GTA Vice City, you should not expect to be walking up the game's equivalent of Ocean Drive and Miami Beach in the opening minutes, as in the 2002 game. Far from it. As we noted in our video discussion on why we gave GTA 6 a 10/10 in our review, Tamoor and Jake did some expectation-setting. Rather than starting the game in Vice City, you'll instead spend the opening hours in a smaller corner of the state of Leonida, Key Lento. Vice City is undoubtedly the crown jewel of the game's world, but it could be as much as 20 hours before you reach it, depending on how you play. This seems to be a deliberate decision that, as Tam says, allows you to progress through the world at roughly the same pace as the ambitions of co-stars Lucia and Jason. The opening area may not be as glamorous as Vice City, but it is no less detailed than the rest of the game.
