Arsenal face Newcastle in the Premier League today as Bruno Guimaraes returns to St James’ Park.

The midfielder left the Magpies to join Arsenal in a £75million move in the summer and this will be his first time back at the club where he previously spent four-and-a-half seasons.

Guimaraes is yet to fully establish himself in Mikel Arteta's starting lineup but this is likely to be an occasion that he will relish.

Asked if he expects Guimaraes to get a warm reception on Tyneside, Arteta noted at his pre-match press conference on Friday: "Well I hope so.

"I think he was one of the main players there and I think he came to the club in a really important part when they had obviously a massive lift in terms of performance and what they have achieved all together, which was really impressive.".

It looks like a decision between Guimaraes and Myles Lewis-Skelly in midfield, after Martin Zubimendi was ruled out for "a few weeks" with a hamstring glitch.

Bukayo Saka will miss another month with a quad problem and Max Dowman remains suspended, but otherwise Arteta has a fully-fit squad to choose from.

Recent findings suggest that one big decision will come at centre-back. William Saliba continues to step up his fitness and is pushing to replace Ezri Konsa in the starting lineup.

At a notoriously difficult ground for the Gunners, Arteta could opt to stick with Konsa for now and give Saliba minutes in the Champions League against Borussia Dortmund in midweek.

Noni Madueke will hope the international break has not disrupted his strong run of form, while Kai Havertz often gets the nod over Viktor Gyokeres up front.

Predicted Arsenal XI vs Newcastle (4-2-3-1): Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Guimaraes; Madueke, Odegaard, Tzolis; Havertz.

Time and date: 5:30pm GMT on Saturday November 21, 2026.
