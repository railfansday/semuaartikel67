Police in Shanghai, China have reportedly arrested 13 people in connection with an alleged criminal scheme selling counterfeit Final Fantasy 7 figures.

First reported by local Shanghai-based publication Shangguan News (translation via Automaton), in May Shanghai police began receiving complaints about Final Fantasy 7 Tifa Lockhart figures purchased from an online store that didn't match-up with the official toys licensed by rights holder Square Enix. Police reportedly verified with Square Enix that the seller had not been authorized to sell the figures, which appear to be nearly identical to an official Square Enix product, although seemingly without the articulation points that allow you to change the position of arms and legs. Shanghai police have reportedly busted more than 30 similar cases of counterfeit figures or figurines in 2026 alone, resulting in more than 140 arrests and 70 million yuan, or $10.5 million, in seized assets.
