Sources indicate that chili Piper is a B2B SaaS company that helps GTM teams turn inbound leads into qualified meetings - instantly. Our Demand Conversion Platform makes it easier for buyers and sellers to connect fast, without the usual friction in the funnel.

According to recent reports, we’re a fully remote, global team that loves building solutions that truly help our customers, moving fast, and having fun while creating something meaningful. We’re growing promptly and aiming major!

Industry observers note that ***AT THE MOMENT ONLY HIRING IN THE US***Enterprise is where Chili Piper lands its largest new customers. As Enterprise Sales Director, you’ll lead a small, senior pod of Enterprise AEs and Enterprise Solutions Architects, and carry your own book alongside them. Once a deal closes, our Account Management team partners with you to grow the relationship from there. Reporting directly to the co-CEO, this is a player-coach role built for a proven enterprise closer who is also a leader.

Own and close your own book of enterprise new business, from first meeting to signature.

Coach, develop and help hire a pod of 2 Enterprise AEs and 1 Solutions Architect.

Report on team and individual results each week: latest ACV, win rate, cycle time and pipeline coverage.

Run deal reviews and call a forecast the business can trust.

Decide where the Solutions Architect’s time goes across the team’s pipeline, so technical wins come early.

Build a clean handoff to Account Management and CS so every new customer has a smooth start.

Partner with Marketing and the SDR team on account-based plays for target accounts.

Take what you hear in the field back to Product and leadership.

5+ years closing enterprise SaaS deals: $100K+ ACV, long cycles, procurement and security reviews.

Presently in a Director (can be recent) or Team Leader role.

A consistent record of 100%+ quota attainment, ideally including time leading or mentoring others.

Experience improving AE KPIs such as win rate, cycle time, ACV and pipeline coverage.

Experience in a land-and-expand model, working alongside an expansion or account management team.

Confidence selling to senior Marketing, GTM, Operations and Sales leaders.

Based in the US. Access to a major tech city is an advantage for in-person meetings and events.

A closer who gets real energy from helping others close.

A direct, generous and specific coach.

Curious about how revenue leaders think, and comfortable challenging the status quo.

Organised, accountable and calm in a fast-moving team.

Why you'll love being a part of our team:.

Our team loves Chili Piper because we’re building the company of the future.

Work with an immensely talented global team.

Work in a business that’s run fairly and transparently.

Competitive pay based on market rate wherever you live.

We lead by example--showing industry peers and leaders how to innovate, improve, and grow--while having fun.

In a recent development, freedom and flexibility. We’re a 100% distributed team working from around the world, and we've been fully remote since 2016. Our team members can work from wherever they want in the world, as long as they show up on our weekly all hands meeting on Zoom.

Solve interesting problems. The software landscape has exploded. There are dozens of solutions for each problem. We want to be different. We come up with fresh angles on existing problems or invent better solutions to help companies with their sales and marketing. Then we turn these ideas into beautiful, smart software.

Autonomy and ownership. Working on a distributed team means you don’t have someone micromanaging you or looking over your shoulder to make sure you’re getting things done. We’re a team of do-ers who take full ownership for their results.

In a recent development, be helpful. Our first value as a tech giant is help. Help our customers be successful. Help our prospects get the right information and make the right decision whether or not it includes our products. Help our team members reach their full potential.

Unlimited vacation (and we do mean it, and we do take it!).

Generous health, dental, and vision insurance.

Any equipment/software/tech that you need to do your job.

Annual professional development stipend of $2,000 USD.

Equity - all Pipers receive stock options.

Annual organization retreat in epic locations (past trips: Paris, Morocco, Tulum, Iceland, Ibiza, India). Check out the video from last year’s retreat in Tenerife.
