Eric Quill believes FC Dallas are a "well-oiled machine" going into the MLS Cup Playoffs and is excited to see what they can do in the first match of Round One against the Portland Timbers.

Dallas finished second in the Western Conference to ensure they had home advantage in the best-of-three series, having finished the regular season with just one loss in their last six games (W3 D2).

They recorded their best finish since 2016 when they topped the West, and they have qualified for the playoffs in back-to-back seasons, though they were knocked out in Round One last year.

Quill is confident Dallas can improve on that result, though, claiming the drop in temperatures will only play into his team's strength on the press.

"We are a well-oiled machine right now, as far as across the board, just guys, how bought in they are and how much they're up for this," Quill told MLSSoccer.com.

"And our team speed, our pressing ability, the change of weather going into the high 60s, low 70s, is only going to help our game model, because we want to press and be really proactive, front-footed.

"We're going to be on people from minute 1 to 95.".

Portland, meanwhile, just snuck into the automatic playoff spots due to a strong finish to the regular season.

They went unbeaten in their last five games, winning each of the last four (D1), including a 2-0 win over Austin FC on Decision Day, which meant they pipped the Seattle Sounders to seventh.

"When we look at the progress, there have been some improvements, but you need to be consistent in that," head coach Marti Cifuentes said.

"We have to learn to be ourselves, whatever the game scenario. If we start well, then we need to keep on our philosophy.

Industry observers note that "But obviously there is an opposition as well. There is always an opponent that prepares well for the game, and that's why the game is so beautiful, because sometimes you just need to cope with the reality of the game.

"I would say we need to at our best in our key identity and principles of the game.".

Joaquin Valiente got the scoring started for Dallas in their win over the Rapids on Decision Day, netting just his third goal of the season.

He has been an effective playmaker, though, with nobody on the team bettering his 13 assists, while he also topped the charts for crosses (115) and chances created (48).

Jimer Fory has helped keep the Timbers ticking throughout the season, with no Portland player registering more touches (2,016), interceptions (63) or possessions won (161).

Only Finn Surman (1,570) attempted more passes than the Colombian (1,437), but Fory was a solid presence at the back with more successful tackles (32/54) than anyone else.

MATCH PREDICTION: FC DALLAS WIN.

It has been revealed that dallas have never won a postseason clash with Portland in 90 minutes (D2 L2). They lost in the first round in both 2015 and 2018, but advanced in 2020 after winning on penalties.

Dallas are unbeaten in their last six regular-season matches against Portland (W3 D3), with all three victories coming on home soil. In fact, they are unbeaten in their last seven at home against the Timbers.

Portland's last win against Dallas came back in June 2023 (1-0 at home), and while they have not won at Toyota Stadium since November 2018 (2-1), that victory was in the postseason.
