Recent findings suggest that samsung’s upcoming Galaxy S27 series is getting a redesign, with a reliable leaker saying it’s definitely happening. But, do you like it?

To say that Galaxy smartphone design has been stale is a bit of an understatement. The same core design has been in use for over half a decade at this point, with only the smallest tweaks in the past few generations. And it’s been a problem, too. While Samsung insists that the vertical triple camera array is the firm’s “core identity,” it also means that the industry’s move to magnetic wireless charging and accessories are essentially incompatible with Galaxy devices.

According to recent reports, we’ve seen repeated leaks of the Galaxy S27 series so far with a fresh full-width camera island on the Ultra and Pro models, and leaker Ice Universe indicates that this design is now “100%” confirmed, with the final design having two larger camera lenses on the left side, a smaller lens on the bottom right, and a flash above that. We saw this same design in a leak earlier this month, which Ice stated at the time was “wrong,” presumably because the flash was replaced by a fourth camera in the dummy unit.

But the question at this point is, do you even like this new look?

Recent findings suggest that personally, I’m on the fence. I’m thrilled Samsung is finally doing away with the table-rocking, Qi2-ruining vertical array, but I’m not exactly happy we’re getting an iPhone clone as a result. I’d say beggars can’t be choosers, but this is Samsung we’re talking about – I think it’s fair to ask for a bit more.
