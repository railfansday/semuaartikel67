In a recent development, XRP is having its worst November on record, but one prominent crypto entrepreneur still sees a four-digit price ahead. EasyA co-founder Dom Kwok has reiterated his $1,000 XRP target for 2030, even as the token crashes toward $0.90 and institutional investors pull money from spot exchange-traded funds (ETFs).

XRP has dropped 25% this month, surpassing its previous November record decline in 2019. The token briefly touched $0.862 over the weekend before recovering toward $0.94. It remains below $1, a level XRP has failed to reclaim since November 14.

The timing makes Kwok’s forecast look almost absurd on a chart. Yet the sell-off is now testing a level where historical capitulation, ETF positioning, and XRP’s long-term adoption thesis collide.

Sources indicate that the institutional story has taken a serious hit, with spot XRP ETFs recording $30.76 million in November outflows, marking their third consecutive week of withdrawals. Assets across the funds have slipped from around $1.42 billion at October’s end to below $1 billion.

November has also produced no daily net inflow for XRP ETFs so far. That is a sharp reversal from the buying streak that helped fuel institutional optimism earlier this year. Still, cumulative net inflows remain near $1.78 billion since launch. Investors have pulled money recently, but the institutional trade has not disappeared.

Right now, XRP’s chart is already showing signs of trying to stabilize. The token has bounced from its weekend low around $0.86, with buyers defending the $0.90 area. The daily Relative Strength Index sits near 32, following its recent oversold dip below 30.

That is not a confirmed reversal. But XRP does not need a massive catalyst to produce a sharp bounce after a decline this aggressive.

LiquidChain is tackling one of crypto’s biggest problems: liquidity scattered across Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana. Its Layer 3 network aims to connect these ecosystems through a unified execution environment, allowing developers to build applications accessing multiple networks without deploying separate versions everywhere.

Industry observers note that that could unlock cross-chain trading, liquidity pools, and DeFi applications without the usual bridging complexity. Bitcoin brings capital, Ethereum brings DeFi depth, and Solana offers fast execution. LiquidChain wants developers to access all three through one infrastructure layer.

It has been revealed that the APY will attract attention, but the product is the bigger bet. If LiquidChain delivers its unified liquidity vision, it could offer developers a reason to build beyond a single blockchain ecosystem. That is what makes the project worth watching beyond its presale numbers.

Kwok, a former Goldman Sachs analyst and EasyA co-founder, has publicly said he believes XRP could reach $1,000 by 2030. His thesis centers on institutional adoption, blockchain utility, and the possibility that XRP becomes part of a much larger global payments infrastructure.

According to recent reports, that forecast is a long way from today’s price. At $0.94, XRP would need to rise more than 1,000-fold to reach his target. Kwok XRP price prediction is extremely ambitious, but it puts the current price collapse into perspective: Kwok is betting on what XRP could become, rather than what the chart looks like today.

Industry observers note that the immediate battle is much less dramatic. XRP needs to hold $0.90–$0.91 before buyers can seriously challenge $0.95. A break above $0.98 would bring the $1 psychological barrier back into reach. Reclaiming $1, followed by the $1.03 area, would be the first convincing sign that the November downtrend is losing control.

Failure to hold $0.90 could send XRP back toward $0.862, with $0.80 becoming the next downside target.

Historical data offers a mixed warning. XRP’s five worst November performances before this year were all followed by December losses, averaging 10.4%. However, the losses varied widely. December 2018 and 2016 were comparatively mild, while 2019 extended the damage with another 14.8% decline.

The pattern is not encouraging, but it is not uniform capitulation either. XRP has already experienced a historic flush, and the next move could depend heavily on whether ETF selling finally slows.
