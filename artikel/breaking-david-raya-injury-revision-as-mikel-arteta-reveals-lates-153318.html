Mikel Arteta has not ruled out the possibility of David Raya returning for Arsenal’s Premier League showdown with Manchester City this weekend.

The Spain international has been in sensational form yet again for the Gunners but was forced off midway through the second-half in last Saturday’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle United.

Raya was involved in what appeared to be an innocuous coming together with William Osula in the penalty area.

The Spain international was in real distress as he left the pitch, clutching his left arm and using his sleeve as a makeshift sling as he was replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga.

Raya missed the Champions League clash with Borussia Dortmund midweek but Arteta eased concerns over his no1 somewhat after confirming he would not require surgery which would have meant months on the sidelines.

The former Brentford star underwent further assessment this week with Arteta giving little away when asked for an revision at Friday’s press conference.

‘I don’t know. We still have a few hours before the match, and we’re still trying to get all the players fit.,’ Arteta remarked.

Asked if Raya could still play, Arteta added: ‘Yes.’.

Kepa filled in for his countryman on Tuesday against Dortmund and will be ready to step in again on Sunday if called upon.
