Tech Report: Nanogap architecture could help integrate quantum light sources onto chips

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (86 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that quantum light sources that emit light with special quantum properties, such as single photons, are critical for quantum photonic technologies, including quantum communication, computing and sensing. In this regard, monolayer tungsten diselenide (WSe₂), a two-dimensional (2D) semiconductor material, hosts localized emissive states that enable light emission with sharp or narrow line widths, meaning that the emitted light has a narrow range of photon energies. This sharp luminescence makes this material a promising candidate for implementing on-chip quantum light sources.
