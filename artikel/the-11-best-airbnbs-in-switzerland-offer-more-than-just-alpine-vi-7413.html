The 11 Best Airbnbs in Switzerland Offer More Than Just Alpine Views

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that there are few places as beautiful as Switzerland, with its dramatic Alpine peaks, wildflower-dotted meadows, and glacier-fed lakes. Few countries are as easy to explore, either, thanks to a superb public transportation system that makes reaching even the most remote hiking trails a scenic train or bus ride away.

Switzerland is also, of course, famously expensive. Though its hospitality industry is world-renowned, to experience the country at a different pace—and perhaps even get a more intimate experience of what it’s like to call one of its quintessential Alpine villages home—consider booking an Airbnb. Whether you’re planning a ski holiday with a group or getting a head start on a romantic summer escape for two, these vacation rentals offer a chance to experience Switzerland’s beauty up close.

From mountain chalets and cozy cabins to lakeside villas and city condos, here are 11 Airbnbs in Switzerland to book this winter.

Recent findings suggest that the views are almost too beautiful to believe at this two-bedroom apartment. Whether you’re gazing out on the glacial-clear Lake Thun from the bedroom, or meditating on the Alps (the visible peaks here include Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau) from the wrap-around balcony, everywhere you look is a feast for the senses.

Nestled in the Alpine village of Beatenberg, here, you’re not far from Interlaken and its renowned snow sports. The home has been recently renovated and marries the charm of its surroundings with the comforts of modern living, including a contemporary kitchen stocked with cooking basics as well as a raclette machine and fondue pot. If you’re visiting in the summer, hiking trails leave from the front door.

Bern, Switzerland’s capital city, is often overshadowed by the country’s glitzier towns and larger cities, but there’s so much here to enjoy. This sun-drenched one-bedroom apartment makes an ideal base for exploring it all. Offering enough space and amenities to feel like home, it’s particularly well suited for a longer stay: There’s a generous dining table that can accommodate a crowd, a dedicated workspace, and speedy internet.

From here, it’s a 15-minute walk from the UNESCO-listed Old Town and its many splendors—including the medieval Zytglogge, the famous Bear Park, the Aare River, and one of the country’s best-known Michelin restaurants, Wein & Sein. The superb Zentrum Paul Klee museum is also just a short tram ride away.

Recent findings suggest that the minimal, open floor blueprint and neutral palette of this chalet lets its views of Lake Thun take center stage. Set directly on the shores of the lake, the chalet’s balcony gives visitors a front-row seat to the view; you would be forgiven for spending all of your time there by the outdoor fireplace.

While it’s tempting to lounge the days away, the draw of nearby ski resorts makes this an ideal spot for travelers seeking adventure. The home is particularly ideal for families, thanks to a toddler bed, crib, and optional second apartment within the chalet available to rent for larger or multi-generational groups. While family-friendly ski resorts are very close by, the larger towns of Thun and Interlaken are also very easy to reach from this perch.

Dating back to the 18th century, this recently renovated home retains both its traditional Engadine layout with a sulèr entrance hall and attached barn and much of the original charm via its sgraffito façade and wood-paneled rooms.

Inside, old and new come together charmingly, with traditional Tyrolean chairs paired with Bauhaus-inspired sofas. It’s also spacious enough for a large group (the home can sleep up to nine guests). Set in the heart of the village of Scuol, directly opposite the town’s central fountain, the property offers a glimpse of idyllic Alpine life, with the Swiss National Park just beyond its doorstep. It’s a wonderfully peaceful setting, yet only an hour from the glitz and glamour of St. Moritz.

Perched on the banks of Lake Thun, this newly built glass cottage is designed to make the most of its spectacular setting. Floor-to-ceiling windows and a contemporary palette of soft neutrals blur the line between the indoors and outdoors. This is an ideal retreat for those drawn to the water (and therefore, perhaps better-suited for warmer weather), with complimentary paddleboards at the ready and easy access to swimming. When you’re ready to venture further afield, the fairy tale Hünegg Castle is a short walk and the historic town of Thun a 15-minute drive away.

This gorgeous penthouse pairs postcard-worthy views of the Matterhorn with an enviable location, just steps from the ski lift and a short walk from the center of Zermatt, one of Switzerland’s most quintessential Alpine towns. The appeal extends well beyond its superb setting, however. An airy open floor proposal living space is anchored by a striking hanging fireplace, while the three bedrooms each come with their own en-suite bathroom, making this an ideal spot for groups of couples or friends. What’s more, the booking includes housekeeping, as well as fresh flowers and fruit, and the owners can arrange everything from lift tickets to massage appointments.

According to recent reports, located in a 1907 chalet that would look just as home in a country landscape as it does in a residential neighborhood of Zürich, this two-bedroom apartment is a good option for a quiet base for families who want to be near the center of things, but not in the center of things. Dark wood paneled walls, cathedral-like windows, and low-slung furniture feels reminiscent of the city’s iconic Café Bar Odeon. (Speaking of: Both the Odeon and the Kunsthaus are about a 20-minute walk away.).

Few places radiate old-world glamour than St. Moritz, and this apartment lets you enjoy the resort town like a local. Built in 1900 in a traditional Engadine style with thick stone walls and a sloping Dutch roof, the two-bedroom apartment feels plucked from the pages of a story book. At its heart is a Stube-style room anchored by a massive fireplace and filled with traditional furniture, sheepskin rugs, and oil landscapes.

In the kitchen, you’ll find both cowbells and a fondue pot—fitting reminders of where you are. Although you may be tempted to end each day on the balcony enjoying the views of the Engadine Valley and Alps, here, you’re not far from the town’s famous après-ski scene, which pulses around Badrutt’s Palace and Chesa Veglia. Book a table and get dressed for people-watching.

Nestled in the sweeping Val D’Anniviers valley, in the high Alpine village of Ayer, this light-filled chalet is a place to immerse yourself in your surroundings, waking up each morning to the sound of cow bells and ending each evening sitting by the fire and listening to the record player. Although the four bedrooms can sleep a crowd, this is also an idyllic romantic retreat with a terrace offering views of the Alps. Here you can reach ski slopes by a ten-minute drive or start hiking directly from the front door.

According to recent reports, built in 1910, this elegant villa on the shores of Lake Lugano offers a private slice of paradise with plenty of room to settle in and make yourself at home. There’s room to spread out across three bedrooms and an abundance of outdoor terraces and seating with lakeside views.

In a recent development, set in Ticino, Switzerland’s only Italian-speaking canton, the villa is framed by palm trees and flowers, and enjoys Ticino’s distinctive Mediterranean climate. The home is also an excellent base for exploring the city of Lugano, or discovering some of the gems hidden in the countryside, like the hiking trails of Parco Naturale del Monte Caslano, a museum dedicated to writer Hermann Hesse, or enjoying Ticino’s renowned (and not often exported) white Merlots.

Located in the village of Schönried just five minutes from Gstaad, this elegant apartment is well suited for both avid skiers and those who prefer the après. Inside, wood-coffered ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass doors give the space a chalet-like feel, while a balcony and outdoor hot tub offer sweeping views of Gstaad and the Saanenland. When you’re ready to venture out, ski lifts and hiking trails are within walking distance, and the shops, restaurants, and scenery of one of Switzerland’s chicest Alpine towns are just a five-minute drive away.
