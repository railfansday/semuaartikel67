Microplastics in nearly 8 in 10 fish sampled off Oman's Sohar coast

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (55 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, researchers at Sultan Qaboos University detected microplastics in the gills or digestive tracts of 95 of 120 fish sampled off Sohar in the Sea of Oman. Published in Scientific Reports, the study provides a baseline for tracking microplastic pollution in fish along this stretch of Oman's coast.
