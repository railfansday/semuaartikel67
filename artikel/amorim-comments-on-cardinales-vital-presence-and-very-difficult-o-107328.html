AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim has spoken about Gerry Cardinale’s presence, as well as the challenge Frosinone will present.

It has been revealed that milan are hoping to jump back into the top four by beating Frosinone, after Como overtook them with their win on Saturday. However, it will not be a straightforward task against a side that have surprised many upon their return to Serie A, and with a point they would go back above Juventus.

Industry observers note that amorim has chosen to name a strong line-up for the game, with Christian Pulisic and Diego Moreira both starting, in addition to Luka Modric, Adrien Rabiot and Goncalo Ramos. The only absentee from the ‘first-choice’ side is Strahinja Pavlovic, who is still not 100% after a kidney matter but makes the bench.

Not long before kick-off, Amorim spoke to Sky to answer questions regarding Gerry Cardinale’s visit to Italy and tonight’s challenge, with his comments relayed by MilanNews.

How vital is constant presence of Cardinale?

“It’s very crucial for everyone. It’s a responsibility to always have the owner close to us. We’re trying to pay attention to improve. Now we have to win.”.

Frosinone are the surprise of the season….

Sources indicate that “Certainly. Watching Frosinone’s games, I can say they’re a very difficult team to face. We’ve had a lot of time to prepare for this match. I’ve noticed they have four central defenders, and sometimes they play like that, sometimes not, so we have to pay attention to that too.”.

Ten out of the 11 players started the derby. Are they the most reliable?

“For today, yes, but we’ll see about the others.”.
