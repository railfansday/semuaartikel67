In a recent development, now that Rockstar's GTA 6 is available, people are discovering all manner of things in the game's gigantic open world. One such finding is that GTA 6 has its own take on Easter eggs, and Rockstar was quite literal.

It has been revealed that twitter user xGarbett posted a video of Jason climbing to the top of a cell tower where there is a bird's nest with eggs in it. Except these aren't normal eggs. They're painted to look like Easter eggs, and Jason can throw one of them (with an impressively tight spiral), sending it hurtling through the air until it crashes down on a scummy-sounding real estate agent or lawyer of some type.

xGarbett noted in a follow-up post that it took about seven minutes to climb the tower, so unlocking this Easter egg will take some persistence.

Given the scale of GTA 6's world, there are no doubt going to be countless more Easter eggs that players discover as they make their way through Leonida.

Another secret that players have already discovered is that Bigfoot is back, and he's very smelly.
