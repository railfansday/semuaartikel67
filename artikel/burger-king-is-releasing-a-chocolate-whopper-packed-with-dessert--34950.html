Burger King is turning its iconic Whopper into a dessert, replacing the beef patty with a brownie and swapping traditional burger toppings for chocolate and jelly.

Fast-food chains have been experimenting with increasingly bizarre menu items, but Burger King’s latest creation takes things to another level.

Sources indicate that the Chocolate Whopper, unveiled on Instagram, looks almost identical to a regular burger. However, every ingredient has been replaced with a sweet alternative.

Instead of a beef patty, customers get a thick, chewy brownie sandwiched between two chocolate buns.

The usual tomato and onion slices have been recreated using jelly, while the lettuce and pickles are made from chocolate.

To finish things off, Burger King has added Nutella, cream cheese mousse, and raspberry sauce, creating a dessert that looks like something you’d normally order with fries.

A post shared by 버거킹코리아 공식 인스타그램 (@burgerkingkorea).

According to The Korea Herald, the Chocolate Whopper launches on November 19 at Flameground, Burger King’s flagship restaurant in Seoul, South Korea.

The dessert costs 9,800 won and will be prepared by hand, with limited quantities available each day starting at 11 AM.

Once the daily supply sells out, customers will have to return the following day. There are presently no announced intends to release the Chocolate Whopper outside South Korea.

Interestingly, the creation actually dates back to 2018, when Burger King originally introduced the concept as an April Fools’ Day joke.

A post shared by 버거킹코리아 공식 인스타그램 (@burgerkingkorea).

“Chocolate Whopper was originally conceived as a fictional product for an April Fools’ Day campaign eight years ago before being turned into an actual menu item,” a Burger King official explained.

The unusual dessert follows several other strange fast-food experiments.

Burger King also got creative with desserts in September, releasing Nutella and Biscoff ice cream burgers in France.

Unlike KFC’s Oreo creation, however, the Chocolate Whopper contains no meat at all, making it a full-blown dessert disguised as a hamburger.
