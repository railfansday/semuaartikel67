It's no surprise that there's so much great tech out to help content creators craft ever more professional quality work. Top-end tech comes at a price of course, so content creators should check out the Black Friday sales just as they might for deals on TVs, gaming hardware, or Apple devices.

Sources indicate that as of Nov. 22, the DJI Mic 3 is down to $242.24, which is an overall savings of $42.75 from the regular list price of $284.99.

This has everything you need for pro-level audio recording. The bundle, which comes in a compact charging case, includes two DJI transmitters (with windscreens), one receiver, charging cables, adapters, magnetic clips, and other accessories.

The receiver can support addition transmitters, which is great for group-based content, while the transmitters have multi-level noise cancellation and a selection of preset voice tones to choose from. Both the transmitters and receiver boast enough battery to last a full working day. But if you need even longer, five minutes of quick charging will give you two extra hours.
