Global payments giant Mastercard recently posted on X about the outcome of the XRP Ledger Hackathon. In the post, the payments firm detailed that 179 developers submitted 70 projects during the 36-hour event in Manhattan.

The focus of the competition was how to enable AI-powered agents to autonomously execute transactions and micropayments, engage with verified merchants and so forth, all while operating within budgets set by developers.

The hackathon was held as part of the preparations for Ripple’s annual conference called Swell. Mastercard, Amazon Web Services, QuickNode and Ledger supported the event, which had a prize pool of over $40,000.

Mastercard published an official article in which it mentioned the challenge given to developers. They had to build AI agents that could autonomously make payments to verified merchants without human input.

In a recent development, also, the agents had to operate within a specified budget. They had to settle payments using Ripple’s USD-backed stablecoin called RLUSD. They had to do this on the XRP Ledger.

Sources indicate that one of the purposes of the challenge was to showcase how blockchain infrastructure can enable micropayments. This is especially the case for micropayments smaller than one cent.

In the future, these micropayments could be valuable as AI agents start buying computational resources or requesting data from other AI agents and then making payments themselves for digital services.

Mastercard’s vice president of digital assets, Michael Broderick, stressed that the company's technology called Agent Pay for Machines could enable such payments.

According to recent reports, the project that won the competition is called Wraps. Its creators are Adarsha Mishra and Hrishabh Ayush. Their project dealt with how to secure user privacy in AI-based transactions. Their project demonstrated a method to prove that search logs were not being saved.

One of the most interesting things mentioned in the Mastercard report might be how the XRP Ledger is increasingly being used for payments by machines.

Mastercard mentioned that over 500,000 transactions are being conducted daily on the XRPL by AI agents.

Also, it’s reported that Ripple has recorded over 10 million payments using the x402 internet payment protocol on XRPL. This comes less than three months after the protocol crossed the 1 million payment milestone on the network.
