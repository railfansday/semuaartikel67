When you're traveling for work, a canceled flight is more than a hassle. It can mean missing an vital meeting or paying up for a last-minute fare.

Through the end of January 2027, Capital One is giving select business cardholders a upcoming way to manage flight disruptions. Eligible flights booked through Capital One Business Travel will automatically include free rebooking on another airline after a qualifying cancellation or delay, with Capital One covering the new ticket up to $5,000 per traveler.

Industry observers note that when travelers book flights through Capital One Business Travel, Capital One will monitor eligible itineraries beginning 24 hours before departure. If the flight is canceled or delayed by at least three hours, the traveler will receive a notification and gain access to a self-service rebooking tool.

The benefit covers several types of disruptions, including:.

Last week's ground stop, which backed flights up for days, is a good example of when this benefit could be a huge help.

And unlike traditional airline rebooking, which may be limited to flights operated by the original carrier or its partners, Capital One's tool allows eligible travelers to select a replacement flight on another airline, with the new ticket covered up to $5,000 per traveler.

Industry observers note that the flexibility to jump to a competing airline is the standout feature. When one carrier is sold out, another airline may still have space. Re-routing through a different connecting airport can also be strategic to avoid weather issues altogether.

In a recent development, replacement flights must depart on the same or following day, serve the original destination or a nearby airport and be booked in the same (or lower) fare class. Despite these terms, this will still give travelers far more flexibility than handling things independently.

Travelers can also contact a live agent for 24/7 support.

Capital One's newly introduced feature will help you with rebooking options, but it won't cover the other costs of getting stranded.

According to recent reports, for example, if you're rebooked on a flight the following day, you may need to pay for a hotel, meals or transportation. That's where eligible credit card trip delay coverage could help.

Trip delay insurance covers unexpected costs related to qualifying delays, such as accommodations, meals or transportation while waiting out your delay.

That gap can be especially important during weather or air traffic control disruptions, when airlines generally do not pay for stranded travelers' hotels or meals.
