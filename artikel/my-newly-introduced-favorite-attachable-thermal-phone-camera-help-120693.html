Yet the P3 Lite offers broader, more practical applications that make it perfect for projects around the house. Here’s why.

Recent findings suggest that from sniffing out cold spots in your home, to seeing what's hiding in the dark outdoors, the Thermal Master P3 Lite makes it a breeze. Having a single device that I can effortlessly swap between my iPhone and Android phones makes quick checks around the house much easier.

Sources indicate that what really stands out when moving from room to room in my home is how responsive the Thermal Master P3 Lite is, adapting easily to sudden temperature changes without lag. Inside the Thermal Master app, it blends the infrared feed directly with my phone's standard camera — merging the two seamlessly so I can actually read labels, see edges, and pinpoint fine details. It has been revealed that putting the Thermal Master P3 Lite to work around my house immediately paid off.
