What better way to kick off the holiday season than by planning your next vacation?

Whether you’re looking to bask in the sun on a tropical Caribbean getaway or explore glaciers along Alaska’s mesmerizing coastline, Norwegian Cruise Line is offering a noteworthy Black Friday deal you don’t want to miss.

In a recent development, NCL is offering 50% off all sailings* — nearly 350 destinations — regardless of itinerary or ship. This lucrative cruise deal lets families, couples or even solo travelers make their dream vacation aims a reality and map out an itinerary well within their budget.

In addition to this Black Friday promotional discount, guests can enjoy an unlimited open bar, shore excursion credits and much more.

Keep reading to find out how you can save money and get more than $2,000 in value on your next cruise booking with this Black Friday deal.

NCL’s 50% off Black Friday deal* applies to all cruises and is available to book for sailings through 2028. To browse sailings for this promotion, visit NCL.com and filter by destination, port of departure, ship and duration to narrow down your perfect itinerary.

Plus, as an added bonus, this promotion can be combined with NCL’s “Free at Sea” package. Delivering more than $2,000 in value, guests can enjoy benefits such as an unlimited open bar, specialty dining, shore excursion credits and Wi-Fi.

This all-inclusive package is great for guests who want to take advantage of key cruise perks without paying a la carte for additional benefits.

It has been revealed that and, as if this Black Friday deal couldn’t get any better, on select sailings, kids sail for free. This is a great perk for families looking to travel at a greater cost-saving value.

Note that fare prices shown are in U.S. dollars on a per-person basis and include taxes, fees and port expenses.

With nearly 350 destinations to choose from, including sailings to the Bahamas, Alaska, Europe and the Caribbean, there is no shortage of adventures and options to choose from.

For instance, you could book a four-day getaway to the Bahamas, departing from Orlando, Florida (Port Canaveral) and stopping at Nassau and NCL’s private island, Great Stirrup Cay.

It has been revealed that the Bahamian oasis is now home to a 6-acre private water park that features 19 waterslides, cliff jumps, a lazy river and more. Great Tides Waterpark opened in September 2026 and gives guests flexibility to choose their own adventure.

Industry observers note that if a full day of thrills isn’t for you, guests can also opt to relax and enjoy the Great Life Lagoon, a 28,000-square-foot pool. For families or couples looking for a quick vacation, this could be the perfect option.

For those wanting to explore several European countries — without the hassle and additional out-of-pocket cost of booking trains, flights or hotel rooms — guests can embark on a nine-day Eurotrip starting from London and cruising to ports in France, Germany, Belgium and Denmark.

This can be a nice option for couples, those traveling with friends or even families.

As a mom of a two-year-old, it can be challenging (and stressful) to navigate trekking across Europe with a toddler. I like that cruising takes the guesswork off of meal intends, nap times and travel between countries.

Recent findings suggest that plus, unlike other cruise lines, Norwegian is known for its "freestyle cruising," which means there are no enforced dress codes or assigned dining times. As a toddler mom, I like that NCL gives guests the flexibility to book the vacation they want.

If cruising on a megaship is not your style, NCL offers a resort-like ship class known as the Prima Class. These smaller, more upscale ships feature a more modern aesthetic, larger outdoor areas and elevated experiences.

In a recent development, in March, NCL's newest ship, Norwegian Luna, set sail to destinations in the Caribbean, the Bahamas and Bermuda. This ship has signature Prima Class touches, such as Indulge Food Hall and Cagney's Steakhouse, as well as some must-try additions, including the authentic Thai restaurant Sukhothai.

Standout ship attractions include the Aqua Slidecoaster, the world's first hybrid waterslide and roller coaster, waterfront infinity pools, kids' splash pad, adults-only pool area, game room, and so much more.

Basically, the new Norwegian Luna will make it hard for guests to decide to even leave the ship.

According to recent reports, if exploring destinations and being one of the first to set sail on a new ship is enticing, NCL is also extending this promotion to its recently unveiled ship, Norwegian Aura. The biggest ship in Norwegian's fleet will set sail in April 2027 and will feature more expansive spaces, waterslides and attractions around every corner. Itineraries for this ship include ports in the Caribbean, Greek isles, Italy and Spain.

With this 50% off Black Friday deal*, now is a great time to book your 2027 or even your 2028 travel aims and experience these brand-new cruise ships.

Whether you're planning to take advantage of this Black Friday deal or book an NCL cruise, be sure to check out the company's Latitudes Rewards program. This loyalty program is free to join and lets cruisers earn rewards and get access to exclusive discounts, onboard experiences and more.

Members can earn 1 point for every cruise night, and 1 additional point per night for reserving select rooms or when booking with a Latitudes Rewards insider offer.

As you reach a certain threshold of points, you can unlock new status tiers, which may include specialty offers, cabin upgrades, dining benefits and more.
