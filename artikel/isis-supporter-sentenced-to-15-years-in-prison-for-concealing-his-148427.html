Anas Mentioned, 30, of Houston, Texas, has been sentenced to 15 years in prison and lifetime supervised release for concealing material support to a foreign terrorist organization and making false statements to the FBI.

According to recent reports, noted is a pledged supporter of Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham (ISIS). He pleaded guilty on July 24.

At the hearing, the court heard additional information that Mentioned concealed his role in the creation of videos and posters glorifying ISIS and its violence, including messages pledging newly introduced ISIS attacks that that would make 9-11 pale in comparison.

As part of his plea, Said admitted he used various social media accounts to post, share, or disseminate ISIS propaganda, including the ISIS propaganda that he concealed his role in creating.

He also discussed ISIS propaganda with other ISIS supporters using Telegram, where he received feedback on the media he personally created. In one message, he discussed sending an ISIS propaganda video he created to the Sarh al-Khilafah Institution, which agreed to publish it on their channel. According to court records, Sarh al-Khilafah, also known as Tower of the Caliphate, is a media entity associated with ISIS that uses social media platforms to produce ISIS and other terrorist content with information ranging from operational security tips to guides on chemicals and explosives. Sarh al-Khilafah is also known for aggregating ISIS content online.

Mentioned admitted to knowing Sarh al-Khilafah was a media entity working on behalf of, and in coordination with, ISIS. Prior to publishing his media, Said removed his name as designer of the video, thus concealing his material support of ISIS.

According to recent reports, said also pleaded guilty to lying to law enforcement regarding a weapon he possessed and discussed on social media in 2023. As part of the plea, he admitted to making the following statement: “I am thinking and hoping to kill the infidels with a weapon and as many people as possible in order to heal my heart.”.

Said has been and will remain in custody pending transfer to a Federal Bureau of Prisons facility to be determined at a later date.

FBI Houston’s Joint Terrorism Task Force (JTTF) conducted the investigation with special assistance from police departments in Houston and Sugar Land, as well as the Harris County Sheriff’s Office. The JTTF also includes Montgomery County Sheriff’s Office, Homeland Security Investigations, the Department of State’s Diplomatic Security Service, IRS Criminal Investigation, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, Secret Service, Houston Metro Police Department, Customs and Border Protection, and Federal Air Marshals Service.

According to recent reports, assistant U.S. Attorneys Steven Schammel and Liesel Roscher for the Southern District of Texas prosecuted the case with assistance from Trial Attorneys John Cella and Ryan White of the National Security Division’s Counterterrorism Section.
