Manni L. Perez – the actress who plays Lucia in GTA 6 – has thanked fans for their support in her first message since the game’s release last week.

Perez, whose life is about to change permanently, thanked fans for the “beautiful” messages and the positive reception to the game.

According to recent reports, “I’ve dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life,” she wrote on her Instagram Story. “She’s been such a significant part of mine, and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours.”.

Perez is nominated for Best Performance at next month’s The Game Awards, which she will almost certainly win in my opinion.

Sources indicate that her performance is funny, warm, and filled with energy. But the most remarkable thing is just how many lines both of the protagonists deliver – there must be hours and hours of recorded dialogue in this game.
