Breaking: Meta brings Muse to a Chrome sidebar used by 200,000 people

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (385 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • The Muse Charm is a keychain-sized device for talking to the agent.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jolly, the mascot of Meta’s Muse AI agent.

Meta has put its Muse AI agent into a Chrome sidebar. Alexandr Wang, Meta’s chief AI officer, unveiled the Muse Chrome extension in a post on X on Friday. He said 200,000 people were already using it.

Recent findings suggest that muse browses in the user’s own Chrome and acts only inside its own group of tabs, Digital Trends reported from the Chrome Web Store listing. Meta says Muse cannot see saved passwords, browsing history or Chrome settings. The listing also warns that Muse may take unexpected actions. Its activity may show up in a user’s Google account.

The Muse Charm is a keychain-sized device for talking to the agent. Meta prepares to charge $300 for it, The Information’s Jyoti Mann reported on Thursday, citing a person familiar with the matter. The Charm will come with up to 12 months of free 5G data from Verizon, according to the report.

Meta has delayed its smartwatch to next year to focus on the Charm, The Information reported. Mark Zuckerberg showed the device at Meta Connect in September and said it would ship in time for the holidays.

“We’re charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we’ll have more to share soon,” Meta’s Tyler Yee told The Information.

Some Instagram customers are asking Muse to find and unfollow accounts that do not follow them back, WIRED’s Jason Parham reported on Friday. One user told WIRED he had heard of people losing their accounts for purging followers with third-party apps. He did not believe Muse could do it until he tried.

Muse went live in Australia on Tuesday, Meta announced. Users can connect it to Gmail, Google Calendar, Instagram and OpenTable. Local partners such as Canva, Domain, Kogan, Officeworks and Ticketek will follow gradually.

“People stay in control, including choosing what Muse can access and approving purchases before they happen,” mentioned Meta’s Will Easton.

Muse has drawn 10 million customers since its September launch. Meta says it runs on Muse Secure VM, a dedicated cloud computer. It keeps passwords and payment methods out of the agent’s view.
