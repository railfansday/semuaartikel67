McDonald’s Is Reportedly Testing A Major Chicken Menu Overhaul—Here’s What We Know

🌐 Source: Delish • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (400 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • McDonald’s has been paying close attention to its chicken menu lately.
  • McDonald’s is reportedly testing five hand-breaded chicken menu items in select U.S.
  • The biggest difference with this potential menu overhaul is how the chicken would be prepared.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

McDonald’s has been paying close attention to its chicken menu lately. Between the return of the Snack Wrap, the introduction of McCrispy Strips, and several new dipping sauces, it’s clear the chain is looking to become a bigger player in the fast-food chicken game. If the latest reports are true, some of its biggest changes might still be ahead.

McDonald’s is reportedly testing five hand-breaded chicken menu items in select U.S. markets, including Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and the Tucson, Arizona, area. The information comes from McFranchisee, an account on X that regularly shares details about the chain’s operations and upcoming menu changes.

The biggest difference with this potential menu overhaul is how the chicken would be prepared. Rather than simply introducing newly introduced flavors or sauces, McDonald’s is reportedly experimenting with having employees bread the chicken by hand inside its restaurants.

Industry observers note that among the alleged test items is a upcoming McSpicy chicken sandwich. According to the report, all five items are also available as meals, with prices ranging from $8.19 to $9.99 at the location pictured in the social media post.

A similar test is also taking place in Ireland.

In a recent development, I’m particularly interested in how hand-breading could change the texture of McDonald’s chicken. Having taste-tested plenty of fast-food chicken sandwiches and tenders, I’ve found that the breading can make or break the entire experience. A crispier, more substantial coating could make McDonald’s a much stronger competitor to chains like Popeyes and Chick-fil-A.

Rolling out hand-breaded chicken across thousands of restaurants, however, is easier said than done.

According to recent reports, the reported tests are designed to evaluate customer demand, preparation costs, and how the additional steps could affect kitchen operations. Some franchisees have reportedly expressed concerns about increased labor requirements and potential kitchen renovations.

While McDonald’s hasn’t officially confirmed the five-item lineup or specific U.S. test locations, the idea of hand-breaded chicken isn’t entirely out of left field.

In a recent development, during its November earnings call, the company confirmed that it had begun setting up operational pilots for hand-breaded chicken in the U.S. and Ireland. It also unveiled prepares for larger U.S. sales tests at roughly 100 restaurants in early 2027.

According to recent reports, for now, there’s no confirmed nationwide U.S. release date or guarantee that all five reported items will make it onto the permanent menu.
