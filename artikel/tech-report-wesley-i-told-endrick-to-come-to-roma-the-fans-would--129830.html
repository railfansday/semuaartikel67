Roma wing-back Wesley Franca gave a post-match interview on Brazilian television on Wednesday night and revealed that he told compatriot Endrick to join the Giallorossi this winter: ‘The fans would love him’.

Endrick has been linked with a move away from Real Madrid in January and there has been plenty of interest in Serie A, particularly from Roma and Milan.

Recent reports claim that Roma are interested in signing Endrick on loan, whereas Milan could be willing to invest heavily in order to sign the Brazilian on a permanent basis: Recent reports have pointed to a price tag in the region of €60m-€70m.

Wesley, however, is doing his best to make sure that Roma win the battle for Endrick.

“I told Endrick to come to Roma,” Wesley revealed on TNT Sport Brazil after Roma’s 2-2 draw against Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

“He will be the one to decide, but he knows that the fans would love him here and his teammates would gladly welcome him.”.
