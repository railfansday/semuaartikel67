Guy Fieri’s Thanksgiving was filled with turkey, taste and transformation.

While celebrating the delicious holiday on Thursday, November 26, Fieri, 58, surprised some fans when he remarked goodbye to his signature blond hair and hello to a more natural gray look.

It has been revealed that “Hope you all had an amazing Thanksgiving!!” the Food Network star wrote via Instagram. “Leave a comment sharing something you’re thankful for.”.

Instead of sharing what they were grateful for, many followers quickly began commenting on Fieri’s new look.

“PLEASE TELL ME YOU’RE KEEPING THE GRAY HAIR!!!!” one fan wrote. Another user added, “Lookin old AF, but great at the same time!!! 😂🩶.”.

Haircuts (and colors) aside, Fieri had a Thanksgiving feast Food Network fans could only dream of. Planning started earlier in the week when the Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives host worked alongside his two sons, Hunter and Ryder.

“Prep day in the Fieri household,” Fieri wrote via Instagram on Wednesday, November 25. “The calm before the storm!!”.

After collaborating with a few more helpers, the Fieri family was able to enjoy a feast complete with turkey, stuffing, green beans, salad, cranberry sauce and all the staples.

While Fieri may look like a total pro in the kitchen, the bestselling author admits things can still get a little unpredictable when cooking.

According to recent reports, “Thanksgiving morning, I’m in my flip-flops, in a tank top, maybe having myself a refreshing morning beverage. I don’t take the temperature of the oil because I’m a cook, right? I’m a chef. I know what I’m doing,” he recalled in a Food Network clip shared via Instagram on November 20. “I drop the turkey in, it hits the oil, it overflows, and there is a volcano about six feet in the air blowing flames. It’s like a jet engine, within two feet of the eave of my house.”.

Sources indicate that after attempting to save the turkey — and avoid a major fire — Fieri thought he saved the meal. His wife, however, had other plans.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m holding the turkey, and I can just feel that something’s not right,” he recalled. “And I turn back to see my wife, Lori, standing in the doorway, and she says, ‘There’s no way in hell we’re going to eat that turkey.’ That was the day we had Chinese food.”.
