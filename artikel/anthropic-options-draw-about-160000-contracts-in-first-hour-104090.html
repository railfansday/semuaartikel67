Reuters reported on November 23, 2026 that about 160,000 Anthropic options contracts changed hands during their first hour of trading that day. The launch added a listed instrument for hedging the AI manufacturer’s shares or taking speculative positions following its initial public offering.

According to recent reports, the early activity placed Anthropic among the 25 most heavily traded names in the options market, according to Mercury Alerts, the options analytics service that supplied the figures. Volume was roughly evenly split between calls, which give holders the right to buy shares at a fixed price, and puts, which give holders the right to sell at a fixed price.

Mercury Alerts projected that the opening pace would produce more than 600,000 contracts over the full session. Anthropic shares were down 2% at $87.33 in early trading when Reuters published its report.

It has been revealed that that full-session projection would remain well below the record 1.8 million contracts traded when SpaceX options introduced in June 2026. Cboe Global Markets’ November 20, 2026 preview had explored a higher scenario: roughly two million Anthropic contracts on opening day would set a record, with only Nvidia ranking higher among single-stock option classes. The observed pace therefore indicated substantial early participation without the record-breaking scale Cboe had described.

Cboe’s preview presented its SpaceX comparison as a rough guide because the companies differ, noted the difficulty of predicting newly introduced-issue option pricing, and mentioned liquidity in the form of bid-ask spreads and market depth could take time to develop. Those qualifications separate the opening volume signal from the harder question of whether traders can consistently execute large orders efficiently.

The listing came on the second business day after Anthropic’s share debut, the earliest timing allowed under Cboe’s rules for an IPO valued at $3 billion or more at the offer price. Cboe expected all 20 U.S. options exchanges to list the contracts on the launch date or shortly afterward.

In a recent development, cboe’s planned expiration lineup combined standard monthly contracts with longer-dated expirations, followed by Friday weeklies. Initial strikes were set to extend roughly 50% above and below the share price. Monthly and longer-dated strikes were expected at $5 intervals near the prevailing price, with possible $2.50 intervals below $100 and tighter increments for weekly contracts.

Cboe’s preview specified five-cent quote increments below $3 and ten-cent increments at $3 or above, with penny pricing possible through auctions and other price-improvement mechanisms. Physically settled Flexible Exchange options were also expected at launch, permitting customized expiration dates, exercise styles and strikes.

It has been revealed that anthropic priced its Class A initial public offering at $83 per share on November 18, 2026. The shares began Nasdaq trading on November 19, 2026, when more than 850 million changed hands, and finished their first session more than $14, or 17%, above the offer price, according to Cboe.

The options debut establishes immediate interest in listed Anthropic exposure, but opening volume alone does not establish durable liquidity or a clear directional consensus. The central question for subsequent sessions is whether bid-ask spreads and market depth develop alongside volume as the shares establish a trading history, shaping how useful the new contracts become for hedging and speculation.
