How to Style Straight-Leg Jeans This Winter

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

All denim is timeless, but straight-leg jeans might be the most trend-resistant silhouette of the bunch. That said, they’re trending! The classic style has been making a comeback (or perhaps it never really went away), cropping up in the fall collections of Dior, Stella McCartney, Toteme, and Balenciaga. Amid the revolving door of denim trends, from ultra-baggy shapes to the much-debated return of the skinny jean, there’s something about the simplicity of a straight leg that feels very much like an aesthetic homecoming.

It has been revealed that with their clean lines and easy proportions, straight-leg jeans are a true wardrobe chameleon. They’re a key part of the model-off-duty look when worn with a white tee, leather jacket, and retro sneakers, but they also look chic dressed up with a pump or heeled boot. Their streamlined silhouette also lends itself well to winter layering, balancing out both oversized knits and heavy coats. And while the shape is relatively straightforward, there’s plenty of room for interpretation: A faded blue pair can feel laid-back, while a dark-wash style can look almost as polished as trousers; a bone-colored pair works as a palette cleanser, while a warm brown brings softness to the look.

Below, a few of our favorite styles—with ways to wear them this season.

Dark-wash straight-leg jeans can look just as polished as trousers. Lean into the sophisticated vibe with a pussy-bow blouse, a sharp leather jacket, and a pair of sleek kitten-heel boots.

Sources indicate that play up the Americana roots of classic blue jeans with a plaid flannel shirt and a chocolate brown suede jacket. A pair of supple leather loafers and Balenciaga’s City bag keep the look from feeling too rugged.

Take a cue from the model-off-duty set, and style your straight-leg jeans with a zip-up hoodie and retro Nikes.

Recent findings suggest that washed brown jeans make a surprisingly versatile foundation for colorful separates—like an argyle knit and a bright red suede jacket. Keep the look grounded with black leather accessories.

Sources indicate that pale blue jeans pair particularly well with various shades of brown. A belted, fur-collar suede jacket and matching ankle boots lend the look a subtle ’70s feel.

In a recent development, the streamlined silhouette of a straight-leg jean works well with voluminous outerwear, like a plaid, fringed-trim scarf jacket. Ivory ballet flats worn with tonal socks don’t detract from the action up top.

Style slightly more relaxed straight-leg jeans with a collared cashmere cardigan, a lived-in leather jacket, and suede moccasins for a look that strikes a balance between polished and nonchalant.

According to recent reports, black straight-leg jeans are a winter wardrobe workhorse. Layer a navy crewneck over a classic white tee, then add a belted wool coat for an understated look.
