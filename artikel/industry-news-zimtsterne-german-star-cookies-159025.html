Beat the cinnamon and vanilla into the remaining meringue, then remove the bowl from the stand mixer, and using a flexible rubber spatula, fold in the almond flour until incorporated. Cover the bowl and refrigerate so the mixture can stiffen up. This should take about 30 minutes, but you can also leave it overnight.
