AC Milan could be a destination for Bruno Fernandes, who is thinking about leaving Manchester United to return to Italy.

According to recent reports, it is no secret that Milan are looking at potential opportunities to bolster the attacking department, given the links with the likes of Endrick and Nicolo Tresoldi in recent days. However, there is always the possibility that Ruben Amorim might turn to players that he already knows well.

Industry observers note that the Portuguese has racked up 113 goals and 111 assists in 341 games across all competitions for United, having been their shining light in difficult recent years. He has also played in Italy before, for Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria before his switch to Sporting CP.

In a video posted on their YouTube channel last night, Matteo Moretto and Fabrizio Romano discussed the possible deals that Milan could pursue to strengthen their squad, and they commented on Fernandes.

According to recent reports, “We can’t deny that Bruno would like to return to Italy, and the idea of ​​doing so with a substantial club really appeals to him. It’s true that there were contacts with AC Milan this summer, but Juventus were also reportedly interested in pursuing a possible deal for the Portuguese,” Moretto began.

“In fact, Comolli, along with Bernardo Silva, also wanted to speak more in depth with Bruno, while Juve were still in contention for the Champions League, so we’re talking about the season underway. The Bianconeri made their move before the Rossoneri.”.

Romano added: “Yes, Milan also made moves to try to talk to the Portuguese, also thanks to the relationship they had with Ruben Amorim at United.

“The Rossoneri remain vigilant about the Manchester captain and didn’t want to scupper this potential move because there were so many changes and developments over the summer, first and foremost with Cardinale, who is now present and has great ambition for the club.

“Another curious anecdote I want to tell you is that, and I can say this 101%, Bruno Fernandes and Amorim have an exceptional relationship. There’s great respect between the two; Bruno wasn’t even happy about Amorim leaving Manchester.

It has been revealed that “Clearly, there’s no concrete negotiation yet. But Amorim could be crucial for the future of Bruno Fernandes, whose contract with United is currently expiring and who still doesn’t have a clear and complete idea for his future.”.
