Chef and cookbook author Esther Choi takes Eater on a food tour of NYC.

Celebrity chef and cookbook author Esther Choi, the chef behind Korean restaurants like mǒkbar and Ms. Yoo, highlights us the cafe, bakery, and restaurants she visits weekly for everything from taro-mochi doughnuts to soup dumplings.

In a recent development, choi starts her day with co-fermented coffee at Tera Espresso Bar, where all the beans are roasted in-house and signatures are co-fermented with fruits. She grabs her coffee made with beans that were fermented with tangerines and heads to pastry chef Dominique Ansel’s bakery, Papa D’Amour. Ansel reveals her how he makes his labor-intensive taro-mochi doughnut that is filled with a strawberry jam and frozen before being fried up. Next is the viral butter-dipped vanilla soft serve that Choi gets to dip in the warm butter herself.

In a recent development, choi then heads to NYC icon Pastis for lunch, ordering the cheese-crusted French onion soup, fries, and a dirty martini. The deeply flavorful French onion soup takes four hours to make. Choi recommends ordering your own — this is not a dish you’ll want to share. Her super briny vodka martini hits the table as Choi reminisces about working at Food Network nearby and coming to Pastis with co-workers.

Recent findings suggest that for her final spot, Choi heads to Pinch Chinese in Soho for her favorite soup dumplings in the city. Her son Leon and husband Josh join her for dinner, digging into pork soup dumplings, Peking duck, steak fried rice, and pea shoots. Of course, two-year-old Leon already knows exactly how to slurp a soup dumpling.
