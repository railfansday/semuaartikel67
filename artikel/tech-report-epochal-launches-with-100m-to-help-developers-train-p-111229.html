Tech Report: Epochal launches with $100M to help developers train predictive AI models

UPDATED 20:30 EST / NOVEMBER 24 2026.

Artificial intelligence tooling startup Epochal AI Inc. released today with $100 million in funding.

In a recent development, the capital was provided by Chief Executive Officer Guodong Zhang, a machine learning researcher who earlier co-founded xAI Corp. He established Epochal earlier this year with two other xAI co-founders, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss. The organization’s staff also includes former researchers from Anthropic PBC, OpenAI Group PBC and Google DeepMind.

Sources indicate that epochal’s first offering is a cloud service called the Epochal Time Portal. According to the tech giant, it enables developers to train AI models that can predict future events. The service does so by providing models with access to historical web data from the past 30 years.

Sources indicate that if a bank wishes to train an AI that can predict quarterly earnings, it could use the service to fetch financial results from the first three quarters of 2025. It could then ask the AI to estimate fourth quarter results from the same year. After the model generates its predictions, developers can compare them against real fourth quarter earnings to check the model’s accuracy. They can make adjustments if necessary and then repeat the training workflow until the model starts generating accurate forecasts.

Industry observers note that the Epochal Time Portal features more than 1 trillion historical web pages on launch. Each entry includes a timestamp that indicates when it was created. According to the manufacturer, its timestamps make it possible to track how a webpage changed over time.

Sources indicate that AI models access the content through a purpose-built search engine. It enables them to specify the time periods from which they wish to retrieve webpages. According to Epochal, its platform provides one-minute granularity for the most frequently updated pages.

Like consumer search engines, the Epochal Time Portal prioritizes the webpages that are most relevant to each given query. The difference is that it considers webpages’ creation date when evaluating relevance. If a query contains the term “Meta” but asks for pages created before 2021, the year Facebook Inc. rebranded, the Epochal Time Portal will not bring up pages that relate to the social networking giant.

Industry observers note that today’s frontier models can determine that they’re being trained on historical data rather than up-to-date information. For example, a model might compare the creation date of a webpage to the virtual clock of the operating system on which it deployed. Such discrepancies can decrease the effectiveness of training runs.

According to Epochal, its service mitigates the issue by deploying AI models in sandboxes synced to their training data. If a company is training a model on webpages from 2025, the Epochal Time Portal will run it on a Linux version from that year. It applies the same precautions to the software tools and programming language versions that models use.

Recent findings suggest that the Epochal Time Portal is initially available in a single free tier with low usage limits. Epochal aims to follow up the launch by sharing the results of several AI experiments that its researchers have carried out using the service.
