Home » Altcoins » HBAR Price Hits a Double-Bottom ‘Discount Zone’.

Big-timers are hedging the long-term accumulation zone after Hedera dropped below confluent resistance.

Recent findings suggest that hedera Hashgraph’s native HBAR coin is coiling within a macro ‘discount zone’, analyst Karl Moon notes. In his latest HBAR analysis based on the weekly crypto charts, HBAR has hit the major demand territory between $0.03525 to $0.0516, bouncing off to approximately $0.0555 very briefly.

$HBAR is entering the deep discount zone.Macro range lows are getting very close.This is where bulls need to start showing up. pic.twitter.com/zOfRD6DjuX.

According to recent reports, next up, a confluent resistance area at $0.07 comes into play. From here, the OG altcoin may retrieve the $0.075 resistance before claiming the quarterly lows above $0.10. The support from big-time crypto investors looking for the bottom will be key: a positive CMF record is driving that trend switch.

In a recent development, as we can see in the HBAR price chart above, the altcoin is still trading inside the Bollinger Band envelope, slightly below the mid-tier Bollinger Band (BOLL) of $0.05166. Meanwhile, the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) flipped to 0.07, hinting at crypto currency whales buying on the 4-hour time-frame.

Measured by the Bollinger Bands, the $0.0535 level described by Karl Moon is as the upper boundary of Hedera’s (HBAR) ‘discount zone’ perfectly coincides with the Red-Label Bollinger Band, typically intended to mark a bullish trend switch.

Industry observers note that however, the Elliott Wave theory presents a dimmer picture. More Crypto Online’s trading team has established that a rebound above roughly $0.12 has been labeled as a Wave 4. The seasoned traders even expect HBAR to break below the 2024 lows.

Recent findings suggest that hedera-hashgraph:native is approaching the 61.8% retracement at $0.046 and the 2024 low. There is no sign of a low yet, and more downside is likely, possibly including a break below the 2024 low. pic.twitter.com/rU6ZKIUURk.

Price is now viewed as being in a Wave 5 (final downward impulse leg) of a dipping sequence.

According to recent reports, the count anticipates more downside. There are no clear reversal signs yet. The first major test is the 61.8% Fibonacci level near $0.046. Given that Hedera’s native digital currency jumps back above the $0.0535 for good, the bearish thesis would be invalidated.

Industry observers note that the technical view anticipates more downside, including a possible break below the 2024 low, based on wave patterns and lack of reversal evidence in the HBAR/USDT 4-day timeframe. Meanwhile, Futures participants are betting on an HBAR price bounce back.

Sources indicate that the 24-hour liquidation stats also point to short-sellers taking in a higher deficit than HBAR’s bulls, accounting for $145.12K and $87.69K in excessively-leveraged position liquidations, per CoinGlass’ real-time data. While some long positions are unwinding, the scene’s still crowded.

Top trader accounts on major exchanges are mostly bullish, eager for this trend to continue.

Overall HBAR Futures volume grew by 83% since the day before, whilst the Open Interest (OI) slid by 4% to drop below $100 million. Bitcoin’s (BTC) ability to stay above $60K will contribute towards setting the near-term tone.
