Danny Hollands’ side went out after a 3-2 reverse to the SCFL outfit, meaning the Rocks are now out of all the cup competitions.

It has been revealed that the loss came on the back of a 2-1 derby day victory over Littlehampton Town in the Isthmian South Central division on Saturday. And the Nye Camp aces get the chance to improve their points tally on Saturday when they host Hayes & Yeading.

The victory – after two reverses on the spin -- against the lowly Sportsfield outfit maintained the club’s spot in third in the league standings, with Egham Town top and Jersey Bulls, who the Rocks visit next week, second.

Another positive to come out of that game was the full return of striker Cal Laycock after a long lay-off -- and similarly against United a bonus was the goal-scoring return of long-term absentee Billy Allcock.

It was assistant manager Ben Kneller who spoke to the club’s YouTube channel about the cup exit to Eastbourne, against whom youngsters such as Dennis Wooldridge, Noah Light, Toby Kingswell, Archie Bennett and Louie Williams all featured -- and some of those had actually played the night before in an U18s game, too.

According to recent reports, kneller stated: “We need to keep moving forward but all in all, if we were stood here having the conversation last year when we first game (to manage) and we said, 'listen, we are going to be third or fourth in the league come mid-November, we have lost two games in 26, but you're out of the cups, would you have taken it? You probably would have done. We need to step back to see where we are, (compared) to where we were.

It has been revealed that "It was a little bit of real reality check after Saturday when we had 1,300 in (and beat our local rivals). We are not happy to be out and not happy with a few in the dressing room – but there are positives within the negatives.

According to recent reports, "Noah is a very intelligent footballer (for instance). They (some u18s) train with us on a Thursday and they are around the environment all the time and just because they don't feature in games they are still in around along with others.

Recent findings suggest that “We want to progress young players in the area and that is what Bognor is all about. It's not all about winning this and winning that, it is very much about building from the base and we believe in that. From our side of things there is a pathway for the 18s and we have shown it.".

On being out of all the cups, he added: "We would like to have a cup run but you can never guarantee it but we would have liked to deliver it Maybe we strip the squad back a little more now we are out of the cups and we realign.".

Here’s Liam Goodley’s full report from the Senior Cup defeat.

Bognor were knocked out of the Sussex Senior Cup under the lights at Nyewood Lane as their makeshift side were put to the test by well organised, Eastbourne United.

According to recent reports, tommy Block went into the book early on for his challenge after a back pass. Rhyle Overden crossed it in from the free-kick but straight into the arms of Archie Bennett in the Bognor goal.

Charlie Gunson latched on to a loose ball after a long ball into the area and his strike was deflected away from the back legs of a defender on 11 minutes. Archie Bennett did well to stop two players twice after James Hull's cross that was hit low into the box on 15 minutes, keeping the scoreline goalless.

Gunson did well to wind into the box but his shot was deflected into the path of Ollie Lovell who was also denied by an Eastbourne body. Joe Overy did well on the attack for the visitors. He cut inside after some swift passing play but fired it over the crossbar from outside the area.

Anderson hooked the ball high into the area from the freekick. Block was unmarked as he leapt up to head that towards goal. The ball looped up kindly for the goalkeeper though.

In a recent development, sorge Nones did all the hard work winding his way through the area but blasted over the bar with his left foot on 37 minutes. Secka had to punch away a dipping direct freekick frm Gunson off the left side on 41 minutes. HT 0-0.

Recent findings suggest that changes at half-time saw Block, Benny Read and Gunson come off for Josh Batt, Toby Kingswell and Ethan Robb. Batt got the ball forward to Louie Williams, but after rounding his marker he fired it low and into the grateful arms of Secka from a tight angle on 54 minutes.

Bognor made another change on 60 minutes that saw Maadhav Jagota replaced by Callum Laycock and Lovell came off for Billy Allcock.

Recent findings suggest that A swift move down the left saw Eastbourne United take the lead on 67 minutes. Overy used his pace to run down the left before diverting a low ball into the area and ex-Rock Callum Barlow knocked it in from close range.

Williams set Kingswell on his way down the right wing but Kingswell smacked his cross out of play for a goal kick in a poor half for Bognor.

Industry observers note that robb's free-kick curled dangerously into the area on 78 minutes. The ball was knocked down wide of the right post as the goalkeeper watched it roll past the right post.

James Stone, off the bench, did really well to compose himself running in front of defence before powerfully driving the ball into the net beyond a diving Bennett on 79 minutes, extending United's lead.

Harvey Whyte put in a great delivery from the right and Laycock got their ahead of his marker to get something onto it, and managed to divert the ball low, of which Secka managed to save low to his right.

Another forward ball saw Billy Allcock run through and his first strike was blocked before he followed up to net it on the rebound on 84 minutes for 2-1.

However, that reduced lead didn't last long as Stone worked hard on the right before putting it on a plate for Overy to knock into the net on 85 minutes.

In a recent development, bognor got another goal back direct from Robb's corner as Laycock netted it with his head on 91 minutes. Bognor were attacking towards the end for a last gasp equaliser but Eastbourne United were good defensively to stave off the danger late on.

So the Rocks were knocked out of the Sussex Senior Cup at the third round stage and are now able to fully focus on their league campaign with some massive games coming up, including next up, a home tie against Hayes & Yeading United this Saturday (21st November, 3pm).
