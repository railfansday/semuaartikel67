Determining the mass of a solitary white dwarf is challenging. There are billions of them in the galaxy, but precise mass measurements of them are very rare. Only a handful of masses have been determined for nearby white dwarfs. Determining their masses in binary stars is easier, and because the newly introduced white dwarf interacts gravitationally with the red dwarf, the researchers could determine its mass dynamically, rather then depending on its brightness or its spectrum.

In a recent development, they found that the new white dwarf companion has about 0.62 solar masses. "The dynamically determined mass of the inner white dwarf is one of only a handful of such measurements for white dwarfs within 20 pc, and the most precise, at the sub-percent (0.7%) level," the researchers explain.
