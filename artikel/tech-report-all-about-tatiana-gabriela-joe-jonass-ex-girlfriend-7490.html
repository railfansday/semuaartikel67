Joe Jonas and model Tatiana Gabriela have split after more than a year of dating. The pair was first linked in summer 2025, months after Jonas finalized his divorce from Sophie Turner in 2024. In May 2025, the singer opened up about the challenges of dating again following the end of his marriage, describing the experience as “really scary and intimidating.”.

Industry observers note that jonas and Gabriela’s relationship first made headlines in March 2026, when they were photographed kissing at Miami’s airport. They went Instagram official the following month, after Jonas had already sparked speculation about their romance by commenting on a January 2 Instagram post of Gabriela’s with a “🤤” drooling emoji. Days later, on January 7, People confirmed they were dating, with a source revealing that they “first started to get to know each other late last summer.”.

After more than a year together, the couple called it quits. Here’s everything to know about Gabriela and her relationship with Jonas.

It has been revealed that gabriela is originally from Puerto Rico, per her portfolio with Killer Management, but she has made her home in New York City. She started modeling following film school and has worked with Diane von Furstenberg, For Love and Lemons, and Dolce Vita.

She also appeared in a music video for rapper Bad Bunny’s early 2025 track “BAILE INoLVIDABLE” in January 2025.

On Instagram, she wrote, “Very grateful to have been part of this project 🇵🇷🇵🇷🇵🇷 THANK YOU!!!”.

In January 2026, a source told People that they became involved in summer 2025.

“They were spotted at low-key coffee dates in Los Angeles,” the source began, adding, “They seem to have gotten more serious since then and have been hanging out in several cities, including Latest York and Miami.”.

The insider noted that the pair’s relationship “seems different” for Jonas compared to previous romances.

According to recent reports, neither has really spoken about their romance. In October 2025, Jonas told Esquire about his experience dating after his divorce, though.

“Five indicates in a row doesn’t make it easy to meet someone for coffee,” he shared, saying he wasn’t on any dating apps. “I guess Instagram and TikTok are apps, and I’ve met people that way.”.

On Jan. 16, 2026, Jonas shared a latest carousel captioned, “Enjoying my pinecone.”.

The images included scenes from his recent life.

Sources indicate that one of the photos was of two feet, presumably Jonas’s foot in a black loafer and a woman’s foot in a pair of black high heels. Comments assumed it was the musician’s first picture of Gabriela on the timeline.

TMZ ran photos of Jonas and Gabriela kissing as the musician picked her up from the Miami airport. The affectionate shots, taken on March 21, offered a candid look at the pair’s romance in spring 2026.

On April 18, Jonas shared a very clear and official photo of himself with Gabriela in a carousel of pictures taken in Puerto Rico. In the black-and-white image, she has her arm around his neck as they pose for the camera.

He also talked about Gabriela in a newly introduced YouTube video about his experience on the island, showing them working on his Spanish, eating, and strolling around.

In a recent development, on July 6, Jonas and Gabriela were photographed spending time together during a vacation in Portofino, Italy. The couple was seen relaxing by the pool, swimming together, and enjoying drinks throughout the day.

For the outing, Jonas wore black-and-white patterned swim trunks with dark rectangular sunglasses, while Gabriela opted for a red bikini and slim oval sunglasses.

It has been revealed that on November 14, a source confirmed to People that Jonas and Gabriela had gone their separate ways after dating for more than a year. The insider did not specify the reason or when exactly they broke up.
