It has been revealed that XRP is close to a $61 billion market cap, but its $0.9763 price still needs a real catalyst to recover. Dogecoin is extremely oversold after a 30% breakdown, with indicators hinting at a bounce. Solana is approaching $43 billion in market cap, yet it is stuck in the $70-$75 range at $73.75.

According to recent reports, XRP's market capitalization is on the verge of breaking the $60 billion barrier after the massive drop. According to some aggregators, the price has already crossed the mark, while CoinMarketCap still demonstrates $61 billion.

The state of the market is very far from bullish: there's a good chance we won't see a proper, full-blown recovery sooner than next year, especially after the $6.1 billion wipeout on the cryptocurrency market.

However, XRP has multiple foundational advantages over competitors, such as stronger institutional backing and the use of its network for overseas transactions.

XRP is trading at $0.9763 after dipping to around $0.89 during the drop, and it is below $1 for the first time since mid-August. RSI touched the oversold zone near 30 and has bounced slightly since. The nearest moving average sits around $1.13, while the longest one is above $1.30.

Sources indicate that the asset has reached the area of the previous recovery, which will most likely push the market to the limit, as most traders want to break even on their positions rather than keep losing money. A potentially better time to buy XRP could be tied to the overall market recovery, hence the occurrence of a better market catalyst, which doesn't exist at this point.

The confirmation of a market recovery would only come if we witness specific signs of a bull run on larger-cap altcoins: higher highs and constant inflows into institutional products like ETFs.

According to recent reports, dogecoin lost more than 30% of its value in the breakdown that started on October 8. It destroyed all the gains Dogecoin had made in the past: September's peak is gone, and the price dropped to the lowest level in 4 years. However, it's not all doom and gloom.

Recent findings suggest that DOGE is trading at $0.05963, which is around 43% lower than the September peak near $0.105. Dogecoin has been slowly tumbling for weeks, showing a continuous bearish dynamic, and it is now below all the moving averages. Volume spiked during the drop and has faded over the last two days.

It has been revealed that according to multiple long-term momentum indicators, Dogecoin is extremely oversold. RSI and MVRV both suggest a bounce coming in the foreseeable future. While it's not guaranteed, obviously, there is potential. Most altcoins didn't see any spikes in selling because they were already oversold. The $0.068 area, where Dogecoin held in July and August, is already lost.

The cryptocurrency market moves in cycles, and the recent risk-on cycle in the global economy was exclusive to AI and tech stocks. The crypto market, on the other hand, missed out on it, and with no substantial gains and increased pressure in November, we got the double hit that destroyed hopes for a quick recovery.

Crypto might benefit from a transitional period on the market, even though it's not guaranteed by any means, as both risk tolerance and available funds are running dry.

Sources indicate that solana has almost reached the threshold of $43 billion market capitalization, according to data from CoinMarketCap. The asset is slowly moving back into the bullish zone after it joined other altcoins in a massive price drop. That's around 70% of XRP's $61 billion.

Sources indicate that solana mostly followed the general uptrend but tumbled promptly due to the general reversal of the cryptocurrency market. SOL is trading at $73.75, down around 41% from the late September peak near $125. It dipped to approximately $67.5 during the drop and has bounced for two days, with RSI turning up from the oversold zone.

It has been revealed that every moving average on the chart sits above the price, with the nearest one around $87. Investors aren't buying en masse, but there is some accumulation, which could be tied to automated market making.

The correction on Solana is no different from other assets, and, unfortunately, SOL didn't show any additional resilience on the market.

From a price development perspective, the only positive outcome we are witnessing is the bounce from the August low zone, which might become support at some point, though that is not happening yet.

The dip below $70, which was the point of reversal back in August, is where some buy orders might be concentrated.

Currently, Solana is stuck in the approximately $70-$75 price range, and it seems unlikely to break out until it gets back to the moving averages at around $94-$96.
