Grand Theft Auto 6 cost a lot of money to make, but Rockstar and Take-Two’s gamble paid off, bringing in three times its initial billion-dollar budget.

Fans of the Grand Theft Auto franchise were frustrated by the numerous delays and gaps in announcements for the sixth entry in the franchise. While many believed this was a positive sign, as it proved Rockstar wasn’t going to run the game out the door, it also led to speculation about a project that was facing troubles behind the scenes, like many titles before it.

On November 19, 2026, Grand Theft Auto 6 finally rolled out, and many people were there on day one. The true scope of Grand Theft Auto 6’s success has been revealed by Take-Two in a statement to Variety, revealing just how much money the game has made so far.

According to Take-Two, Grand Theft Auto 6 has made over $3 billion, triple its production budget. The entire day-one take (including pre-orders) was 32.5 million units sold, making it the single biggest entertainment launch of all time and beating anything produced by big names like Minecraft, Star Wars, Marvel, or Harry Potter.

It is worth mentioning that this is only the console launch for two platforms, as Grand Theft Auto 6 is presently available only on PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X/S. The PC release in 2027 is also expected to be huge, and if Rockstar can get the game running on the Switch 2, then Nintendo fans can join in too.

Sources indicate that there’s also the future to consider, as Grand Theft Auto 6 is having some performance woes on the current hardware. If Rockstar has an enhanced version waiting for the PlayStation 6 and XBOX Project Helix, then that’s even more money coming in. That might take a while, however, as those platforms aren’t expected until 2028 at the earliest, and if hardware prices keep rising, no one will buy them.

The game could have been rushed out in an incomplete state back in 2025 or 2026, but as Cyberpunk 2077 proved, that can completely ruin a series’ reputation, and Rockstar would have had to work hard to earn it back.

As it stands, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the most highly acclaimed and best-reviewed video game of all time, with relatively few performance issues and glitches at launch. If it had launched in a rough state, that would have tanked the review scores, which will be a huge part of the game’s post-launch ad campaign.

It has been revealed that grand Theft Auto 6 has become the biggest media moment in recent memory, and it’s just getting started. With more platforms and the online mode still on the way, there’s no telling just how far the game will go. Perhaps the time has come for a video game to finally outsell Minecraft, a feat that no one thought possible, but may finally come to pass.
