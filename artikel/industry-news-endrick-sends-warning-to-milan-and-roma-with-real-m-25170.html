Milan and Roma have been linked with Endrick, but the Brazilian and Real Madrid talent insists he wants to spend his whole career with the Merengues.

Roma are looking for attacking reinforcement in the January window, and Endrick has long been linked with a move to either club.

The Brazilian scored and assisted in Brazil’s 7-0 win over Singapore on Tuesday and spoke to reporters at full-time about his situation at Real Madrid.

The 20-year-old has struggled for playing time under José Mourinho this season, making just seven appearances and scoring one goal in a Champions League match against Roma.

“I think it’s Mourinho’s decision, isn’t it?” he told reporters when asked about his playing time in Madrid.

The Brazilian also spoke about his future with the Merengues, suggesting that he’d like to remain at the club for his entire career.

It has been revealed that “I’m always available to help Real Madrid, which is the biggest club in the world. I hope I can spend my whole career there, helping Real Madrid and my teammates,” he concluded.

It has been revealed that endrick is not the only attacker linked with Milan and Roma. A fresh Gazzetta report claims that the Rossoneri are ready to enter talks with Club Brugge for Nicolò Tresoldi, while Roma are reportedly interested in Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens and Nottingham Forest’s Callum Hudson-Odoi.
