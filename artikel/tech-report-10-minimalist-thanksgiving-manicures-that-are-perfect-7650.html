Sources indicate that first and foremost, Thanksgiving is about spending time with family, and then, eating all the food you can possibly ingest in the stretchiest pair of pants you own. (The two priorities can be slightly interchangeable.) And while you’re planning your outfit for the holiday, why not have a chic manicure to match? I mean, your fingers will be the ones at center stage holding a fork all night.

In a recent development, if you don’t want to go all out with adorned turkeys and food-themed designs, there are plenty of Thanksgiving manicure ideas that are equal parts chic and on-theme. “Minimalist doesn’t have to mean forgettable,” states celebrity nail artist Max Estrada. “Sometimes a beautiful neutral, a little sparkle, or the perfect French tip says everything.” Below, I chatted with manicurists for their favorite nail looks for the holiday feast.

Keep scrolling minimalist Thanksgiving nail ideas, from earthy polishes and elevated designs that will look gorgeous well into the end of November.

“For those who prefer a minimalist look, I’d recommend a classic French manicure with a seasonal twist, such as chocolate brown tips on a sheer nude base,” notes celebrity nail artist Vikki Swan.

In a recent development, “Another beautiful option is using different shades from the same color family across the nails, transitioning from lighter to deeper tones,” Swan explains. “It’s a simple yet elegant way to capture the fall mood without intricate nail art.” This easy gradient requires no intricate art skills, and brings the fall foliage to your fingtertips.

Recent findings suggest that “Cat-eye effects give those rich colors movement and dimension, so the manicure becomes interesting through the finish itself,” says Estrada. “The color and light are doing the work, and make a statement without adding any other nail art.” All it takes is your favorite shifting polish and a magnet to get the look.

Recent findings suggest that polka dots are yes, chic, but more importantly super easy to DIY if you still want to lean into a low-effort design. Choose your favorite contrasting autumnal hues and use a dotting tool (or even a toothpick) in the lighter polish to create the look. Or go for the reverse finish, by using the lighter color as the base.

Burgundy French tips are another favorite minimalist option of Swan’s, and adding a cat-eye effect gives some dimension to the classic yet seasonal manicure.

“Deep burgundy and wine red are also beautiful seasonal choices,” Swan states. “These colors complement fall wardrobes perfectly and create that cozy autumn feeling.” Some of our favorite quintessential fall reds include essie’s Bordeaux (how fitting!) and Sally Hansen’s Pomp & Plum.

Recent findings suggest that “During this time of year, warm, rich shades are especially popular, including chocolate brown, espresso, and caramel,” states Swan. To get the coffee-esque hue, try OPI’s Espresso Your Inner Self.

If you want to lean into the theme of the feast, go for this subtle pumpkin pie-coded manicure. Choose two shades of orange and create a light-to dark gradient by blending the hues outwards with a small sponge.

According to recent reports, these translucent jelly nails have the same decadent finish as a sweet Thanksgiving treat. To get the look, apply a sheer coat or two of your favorite polish, then seal it all in with a high-shine top coat for that signature glassy effect.

According to recent reports, pumpkin spice season goes well into November, and this reflective manicure is proof. Start with an orange base, then layer on a silver or orange chrome topper for a metallic finish that feels festive but still wearable well beyond the massive feast.
