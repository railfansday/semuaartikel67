Recent findings suggest that original Story: If there's anything we could add to GTA 6 as things stand, it'd be an official 60FPS mode. That's obviously not possible right now due to how demanding the game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S hardware, even in the case of the PS5 Pro.

Interestingly though, over the weekend, players have discovered the ability to activate what some are calling a "60FPS Glitch" or "60FPS Bug" on Xbox consoles, forcing the game to unlock its FPS and run at a higher frame rate than it's actually supposed to.

Recent findings suggest that now, as mentioned, there are multiple drawbacks here. The first one is that the glitch speeds up most aspects of the game, making it run faster than intended (and look like it's in fast-forward mode!). This is obviously not an ideal way to enjoy GTA 6, and serves as an immediate deal-breaker in my view.

In a recent development, another one is that reaching 60FPS is pretty much impossible under normal gameplay circumstances. If you're driving down a highway, for example, you're likely only going to get around 35-40FPS at max, with 60FPS and above being saved for situations that require no graphical intensity whatsoever.

Recent findings suggest that and finally, it should be noted that GTA 6 was never intended to be run this way on Xbox Series X|S, and therefore you could run into all sorts of problems - crashes, bugs, maybe even your console overheating. That last one is unlikely, but the game has been specifically built to run at 30FPS on these machines, so it's not worth pushing it in our view.
