A demo for ghost photography survival horror game echograph is now available for PC via Steam, developer Pappy’s Pipe Dream announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Pappy’s Pipe Dream:.

Influenced by classics of the genre, echograph perfectly blends pulse-pounding survival horror gameplay with an intimate narrative about a generational family curse.

Recent findings suggest that players will cross the threshold into a haunting and decaying manor as Layla, the sole survivor of a dark and terrible night that claimed her entire family. Years after the incident, she returns to her familial home to uncover what really happened—and why she was the only one who survived.

Industry observers note that as they explore a labyrinthian manor filled with secrets and environmental puzzles, players will encounter the vengeful spirits that now stalk its halls. With an occult-tinged camera as their only defense, they’ll have to engage in heart-pounding photo-based combat and careful resource management if they wish to survive the night.

Featuring a crunchy PSX-inspired aesthetic and tense isometric to first-person perspective switching gameplay, echograph delivers a stylish and highly-atmospheric take on a beloved genre.

The demo includes approximately 45-minutes of classic survival horror content from the game’s opening, and supports Japanese, Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish (Latin American & European) localization.
