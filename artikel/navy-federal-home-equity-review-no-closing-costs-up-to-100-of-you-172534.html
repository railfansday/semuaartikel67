Take out a Navy Federal home equity loan or line if you’re a member who needs to borrow most of your equity, since Navy Federal pays the closing costs and lends up to all of it. With a top credit score and plenty of equity, get a quote from a bank too before you choose.

Sources indicate that navy Federal Credit Union pays the closing costs on its fixed-rate home equity loan and home equity line of credit (HELOC), and charges no application or origination fee, but only members can borrow. Membership is open to service members, veterans, Defense Department civilians, and their family and household members.

Navy Federal fits members who need to borrow most of their equity or want a line with no annual fee to keep for years. Anyone who can’t join needs another lender, and a member with a 730-plus credit score wanting a $50,000-plus line, with total borrowing at 60% of the home’s value or less, should get a quote from U.S. Bank where it lends.
