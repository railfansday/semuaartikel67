Whether you’re stocking up on household staples or preparing for Black Friday deals to tackle your holiday shopping, there are many ways to save on Amazon orders.

And if you have an American Express card that earns American Express Membership Rewards points, you may be eligible for a newly introduced offer that can help you save up to 50% on Amazon purchases.

Typically, using Membership Rewards points for Amazon purchases isn’t a great option, as the value per point is below that of most travel redemptions. However, right now, you can save as much as 50% on an Amazon purchase by cashing in as few as 429 Membership Rewards points with some current targeted offers.

Other cards also have Amazon offers right now.

Some TPG staffers received offers as high as 50% (up to $50), which is one of the highest we've ever seen.

Others had smaller offers of 15% off (or none at all).

Recent findings suggest that the requirement to spend at least 429 Membership Rewards points is better than many of the times we've seen this offer. Back in November 2025, the requirement was 714 points, but previously it had been as low as 1 point.

According to recent reports, not all Amex cardholders will be targeted with the same Amazon offers, so check your account. For example, at the time of publication, some saw offers to save 15% (up to $10 or $15) — but, as I mentioned, a few of us saw an offer for savings of 50% (up to $50).

In a recent development, remember that these are targeted offers, so they will vary. Hopefully, you are targeted for the larger offers.

It used to be that only products sold by Amazon were eligible for the discount, but now, the terms indicate that it has expanded beyond items shipped and sold by Amazon to include "a wide selection of eligible products."
