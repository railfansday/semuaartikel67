Pisces Daily Horoscope

🌐 Source: Elle • 📅 31 Mar 2018 • ⏱️ 1 min read (163 words, 📖 Standard)

In a recent development, hold that swipe, Pisces! Your money instincts may be a little off on Saturday as the impulsive Aries moon in your second house of income opposes desire-driven Venus in your eighth house of shared resources. You might feel the urge to splurge on a shiny new gadget, only to lose interest a week later. Save it to a wish list and compare prices at a few retailers. A gently used or certified pre-owned version might be the smartest buy. On Sunday, the Sun moves into Sagittarius and your tenth house of career until December 21. Professional triumphs are practically written in the stars, IF you’re willing to put in the effort. You might arrive fashionably late to a party because you’re wrapping up a project or hopping on a call with overseas clients. It’ll pay off when year-end bonuses roll around. Meanwhile, treat those festive soirées as networking ops and bring your best conversational game!
