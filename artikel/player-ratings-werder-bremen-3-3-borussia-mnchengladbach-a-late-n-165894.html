Werder Bremen’s early-season engine spluttered on Friday night, as they were forced to settle for an entertaining draw with the Bundesliga’s bottom club Borussia Mönchengladbach.

Sources indicate that alexander Blessin’s game plan was evident for all to see from the opening whistle. With the Gladbach forward line pressing the Bremen defence in numbers, they had their hosts camped in their own third for the opening 25 minutes.

After Zento Uno failed to convert from close range and Florian Neuhaus also went close, Gladbach’s early pressure paid dividends when they were awarded a penalty following a handball from Youri Regeer. Captain Kevin Diks made no mistake from the spot as he sent Karl Hein the wrong way.

Though against the run of play, Bremen restored parity before half-time when Eren Dinkçi let fly with an outstanding volley from the edge of the box.

Gladbach didn’t let their heads drop, however, and hit back immediately after the interval as they caught Thioune’s side sleeping. Uno’s through ball found Franck Honorat, who had the simplest of finishes.

It has been revealed that the game soon began to open up, developing into a seriously end-to-end affair, with both teams squandering numerous chances to score. Perhaps most surprisingly, the in-form Niklas Füllkrug spurned what could best be described as a sitter.

In a recent development, blessin’s team extended their lead via Honorat once again, this time thanks to a moment of individual brilliance. Picking the ball up near the halfway line, he was given too much space by the Bremen defence and let fly with a superb strike into the bottom corner.

However, Bremen were far from finished and soon cut the deficit back to one through Dinkçi. The game had one final twist as Füllkrug completed the comeback with a typically clinical poacher’s finish deep into stoppage time.

Equally relentless in the press and clinical in front of goal, French forward Honorat epitomised everything good about this Gladbach performance.

His two goals came despite a combined xG of just 0.55, underlining the exceptional quality of his finishing, particularly for his second of the night.

According to recent reports, with Gladbach battling to preserve their Bundesliga status, Honorat could yet prove instrumental in their survival hopes this season. His ability to combine tireless work off the ball with genuine attacking quality gives Alexander Blessin a valuable weapon as they look to climb away from the relegation zone.
