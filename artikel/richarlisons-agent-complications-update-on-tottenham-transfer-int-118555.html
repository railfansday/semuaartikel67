Richarlison is open to moving to Saudi Arabia to end his Tottenham exile.

The striker has been frozen out at Spurs by manager Roberto De Zerbi after failing to agree a summer exit.

Everton, Vasco da Gama and Trabzonspor were all mentioned to have explored deals for Richarlison, who ended up staying put at Spurs despite being left out of De Zerbi's Premier League squad.

Richarlison has since been forced to seek game time with the Under-21s, making an appearance against Cheltenham Town in the EFL Trophy.

The 29-year-old has a contract running until the end of the season, which means the January window is Spurs’ final opportunity to secure a transfer fee.

Recent reports have linked Richarlison with Saudi Pro League side Al-Ahli, which has seen his agent, Fidel Carvalho Soares, play down claims of negotiations but talk up the chance of a move to the Middle East.

"What is being said about negotiations between Al-Ahli and the player are just rumours," he told Al-Maidan.

"However, Richarlison would welcome the opportunity to play in the Saudi league soon," he added.

The agent also confirmed Trabzonspor had not renewed their interest after their summer approach.

"As for Trabzonspor, they made an official offer this summer, but Tottenham rejected it, and they haven't contacted us again," he mentioned.

Speaking to Al Arabiya, Soares reiterated that Richarlison is keen on the possibility of playing in Saudi Arabia, but is waiting for a formal approach.

Industry observers note that "Richarlison really likes the idea of moving to Saudi Arabia, but there has been no contact from any clubs so far. These are just rumours. We are waiting for official offers, and at that point, we will explore the possibility of Richarlison's transfer," he remarked.

Richarlison has scored 27 goals in 103 Premier League appearances for Tottenham since joining from Everton in 2022.
