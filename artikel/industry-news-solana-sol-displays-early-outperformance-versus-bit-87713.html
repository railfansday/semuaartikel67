Major-time speed performance makes Solana edge the apex crypto asset, but both are in losing key thresholds.

Solana is showing relative strength against Bitcoin while traders monitor a key level that could support a short-term bottom, according to recent analysis from More Crypto Online and Crypto Tony.

More Crypto Online highlighted that Solana (SOL) is starting to outperform Bitcoin (BTC) on the 4-hour and daily Solana price charts.

The pair has been working through a corrective structure, with price recently holding above main support zones near the 78.6% and 88.7% Fibonacci retracements on the higher timeframes.

$SOL Starts to Outperform Bitcoin pic.twitter.com/qQ8Lgrfmgr.

On the shorter-term chart, SOL is attempting to stabilize after a sharp decline, with analysts watching for a potential wave completion that could open room for a bounce if local support holds.

Industry observers note that crypto Tony noted that a short-term bottom case can be made for SOL if price reclaims a marked level on the 4-hour chart. At the time of the enhancement, SOL was trading near $74.

The analyst indicated that a reclaim of the horizontal level around the recent swing area would improve the structure, though no new entries were recommended until confirmation.

$SOL – Revision A case for a short term bottom can be made if we can reclaim that level marked out. No newly introduced entry for now though. pic.twitter.com/revlkbsA1s.

The chart indicates a potential path higher if that level is taken back, with local resistance sitting overhead near $78–82.

As of November 20, 2026, Solana (SOL) was trading just below the $70 range after dropping furhter from the mid-$80s recorded earlier in the month.

The popular digital asset has underperformed over the past 30 days but is now displaying early relative strength against Bitcoin on lower time-frames. Broader market conditions remain cautious, with Bitcoin itself consolidating after recent lows.

Outperformance versus Bitcoin is an early signal, but sustained strength requires SOL to hold its local lows and reclaim the identified level.

An unfortunate try to reclaim that zone would keep the short-term structure corrective. Traders are watching the interaction between the SOL/USD support and the SOL/BTC relative chart for confirmation that the recovery attempt is gaining traction.
