📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, pumpkin pie bars are the easiest way to get the classic dessert on your Thanksgiving table. It requires no crust, chilling, or crimping. All you have to do is press it into the pan and your crust is ready to go! Way less time, way more glory. The crust is more similar to a shortbread crust, but the filling is just like the one you know and love. Looking for more ways to use all of that pumpkin you stocked up on? We have every single pumpkin puree recipe you'll ever need.

Arrange a rack in center of oven and preheat it to 350°. Grease a 12" x 17" baking sheet with cooking spray. In a large bowl, mix flour, butter, confectioners’ sugar, and salt until the mixture looks crumbly.

Press the dough evenly into prepared baking sheet, and bake until it is barely set and just starting to turn golden. This process should take about 20 minutes. In a large bowl, whisk pumpkin puree, cream, brown sugar, eggs, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, vanilla, and salt until smooth. Pour the filling over crust and bake until set and only slightly jiggly in the center. This should take about 40 minutes.

Let it cool completely before you cut the set mixture into squares.

Dollop with whipped topping and dust with cinnamon before serving. Enjoy!

The full list of ingredients and instructions can be found in the recipe below.

It has been revealed that store leftover pumpkin pie bars in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Add the whipped topping just before serving.
