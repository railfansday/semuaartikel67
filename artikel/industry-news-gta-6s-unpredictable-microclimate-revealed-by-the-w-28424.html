GTA 6’s marketing has kicked into overdrive days ahead of its PS5 release, with even The Weather Channel offering an exclusive look into Leonida’s microclimate.

In a viral social media post, the US TV network mentioned “nobody knows weather like we do [so] that’s why Rockstar gave us an exclusive look at the forecast for Leonida”.

Owen Shepherd, who worked on lighting and rendering in the game, explained that Florida – the state the game is based on – has one of the most “unpredictable weather systems in the US”.

Recent findings suggest that “To match the fidelity of all aspects of what is the deepest, most immersive take on the Grand Theft Auto series yet, we had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localised, persistent, and above all dynamic. Weather can range with everything from clear blue skies to towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds, grounding players in a world through weather we hope Florida locals – and upcoming visitors to Leonida – can appreciate.”.

Newly introduced PS5 screenshots show off the range of weather scenarios that can occur, including driving rain, dense sea fog, dramatic thunder storms, and ethereal mist.

It all looks remarkably impressive, doesn’t it? Just a few more days, folks.
