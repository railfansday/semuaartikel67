Ibrahim Osman scored his first Brighton goals as his side returned to winning ways by cruising to a 4-0 success over 10-man Universitatea Craiova in the Conference League.

Talk of the high-flying Seagulls potentially being shock Premier League title contenders this season was dampened slightly by Sunday’s surprise 2-0 defeat at Hull.

But Brighton were back to their best against Romanian champions Craiova at the Amex Stadium, with winger Osman putting the Seagulls ahead in the third minute.

Jack Hinshelwood stepped over the ball following Ferdi Kadioglu’s low cross from the right before being played in by Charalampos Kostoulas and squaring to leave Osman with a simple tap-in.

Matt O’Riley had the ball in the net in the 27th minute but the effort was ruled out for offside against Chema Andres, but Maxim De Cuyper scored Brighton’s second six minutes later.

Things went from bad to worse for Craiova, who at the moment sit top of their domestic league, as defender Vladimir Screciu was sent off in the 39th minute for stamping on O’Riley’s ankle.

Osman set up Kostoulas for the third goal before promptly claiming his second with a fine individual effort as Brighton made it 10 points from four games in the competition.

The 21-year-old should have had another assist but his inviting low cross was blazed over the open goal by substitute Femi Azeez, who later curled narrowly wide.

Azeez’s glaring miss mattered little as Brighton comfortably extended their unbeaten home record to eight games ahead of attempting to put the league campaign back on track when Newcastle visit on Sunday.

Elsewhere on Thursday night Hearts’ Conference League hopes suffered a hammer blow as Folarin Balogun’s stoppage-time goal consigned them to a 3-2 defeat at home to Monaco.

Theylooked on course to pick up their second league-phase point after stunning first-half strikes from Jordi Altena and Sabah Kerjota either side of goals from visiting pair Folarin Balogun and Edian Diop.

But in the last of four added minutes, USA forward Balogun got himself free in the box to slot home and maintain Monaco’s perfect record in the competition.

Hearts will now require high-scoring victories over Borac and Pafos in their last two games to have a chance of scrambling into the top 24 and qualifying for the knockout phase.
