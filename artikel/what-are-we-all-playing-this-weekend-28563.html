As such, it will likely be spent either remembering all the things my partner and I've not organised for the time we're away or racing to clear through the pile of things I've already remembered I've not organised for the holiday. Whatever the case, it shall not be restful.

Where am I going? Let's just say, I hope to return with some upcoming pairs of glasses and a pair of trainers. If that doesn't give it away, I don't know what will.*.
