Arsenal must seize title-race opportunity as Paul Merson questions Manchester City’s chances

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Arsenal have the chance to put Manchester City’s Premier League title hopes under serious pressure this weekend. And with Pep Guardiola’s side already six points behind, this is an opportunity Mikel Arteta cannot afford to waste.

According to recent reports, paul Merson believes another City defeat could effectively end their title challenge. That might sound dramatic in November, but the gap is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

Sources indicate that arsenal welcome City to the Emirates Stadium on Sunday, sitting top with 28 points from 11 matches. Their visitors have collected 22 points and gone four consecutive league games without a win.

The Gunners have been diligently building on this lead and can now add to the misery of one of their main competitors.

In a recent development, but Arsenal must be wary of thinking that Manchester City will continue to find life difficult. In fact, Guardiola’s men often thrive in such situations.

Whilst Merson has not ruled out City’s attacking prowess, two players in Rayan Cherki and Jérémy Doku have the ability to turn games around.

However, it is in City’s defensive phase, where more alarm bells should be ringing. The back-line has appeared susceptible and has consequently been exploitable.

And that is where Arsenal must show steel.

In a recent development, arteta’s team have only scored seven goals in 11 Premier League games, compared to City’s 14. Much can be stated from this stat alone.

Whilst Arsenal have based their title aspirations on great defensive organisation, City have failed to merge their potent attack with the structure that Guardiola requires.

Rather than passively allowing City to attack, the Gunners should exploit the instability in their opposition’s defence. Arsenal are more than capable of capitalising on these openings.

Industry observers note that psychologically, City are well aware that they cannot afford to continue to trail. In addition, if Arsenal dominate the early stages of the game, then City may well become even more desperate.

However, we must not underestimate Guardiola. City have previously turned around games that had gone against them. Arsenal need to ensure this does not happen here.

Merson’s comment that City stands virtually no chance of success following further failure is also quite an assertion.

Arsenal do already appear to have found their feet and can continue to accrue points thanks to their solid defence.

Whilst it would be unwise to totally dismiss City, Guardiola’s side does have sufficient quality to enable them to embark on a successful winning streak. And this cannot be switched off by one result.

As well as having challenges to address, Arsenal have also shown problems scoring recently – both in draws with Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund. Against Dortmund, despite having good build-up play, the Gunners were especially frustrated as they failed to score from good opportunities.

And this cannot happen again when they face City.

Whilst this game may not decide the title race, there are still many games left to play, and Arsenal will also go through their own tough periods.

Sources indicate that however, the significance of Sunday’s game should not be downplayed. A win would put nine points between the Gunners and City and further entrench Arsenal’s lead at the top.

It will also compel Guardiola’s men to come from behind.

Recent findings suggest that despite Merson perhaps being willing to dismiss City, Arsenal must ignore such punditry. They must then go on and produce a good game, seize their opportunities and capitalise on their lead.
