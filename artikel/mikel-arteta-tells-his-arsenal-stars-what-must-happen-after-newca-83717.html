Mikel Arteta urged his Arsenal side to take the “next step” by finding a more ruthless edge.

The Gunners were frustrated at St James’ Park against Newcastle in a 2-2 draw on Saturday evening.

Arsenal were dominant in the first half but only scored once and that proved costly as the match turned around after the break.

Newcastle came from behind to lead before Noni Madueke cut inside and scored to earn Arsenal a point, but Arteta warned his players they paid the price for dropping their standards after their wastefulness in front of goal.

“I think individually we had some top performances for 70 minutes,” Arteta noted.

“But at this level it’s not enough. At this level requires 96 unless you score three or four goals and then you can allow yourself for 15 or 20 minutes not to be at your best.

“That’s the next step this team has to make.”.

Eberechi Eze opened the scoring for Arsenal but there should have more goals in that first half, with Madueke and Bruno Guimaraes among those to miss chances.

Newcastle barely laid a glove on the Gunners until a miskick from Martin Odegaard early in the second period led to the equaliser and suddenly the momentum of the match changed.

Arteta bemoaned that moment, insisting it was a structural error as well as an individual one from Odegaard, but he was clear that the match should have been put to bed by that point.
