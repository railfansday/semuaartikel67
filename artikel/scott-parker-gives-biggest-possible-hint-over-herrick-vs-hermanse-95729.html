Fin Herrick made his league debut on Saturday in West Ham United’s disappointing draw against bottom side Preston North End.

The young Hammers ‘keeper had a challenging start: A mix-up from Preston’s very first corner saw Joel Piroe’s header take the ball away from the oncoming keeper’s fists and onto the far post   –  from which point Liam Lindsay had a simple tap-in to put the visitors ahead.

It has been revealed that after such a confidence-sapping start, Herrick rallied well with some fine saves and good footwork – would it, we wondered, be enough for Scott Parker to continue with Herrick in the fast-approaching game tomorrow night against Portsmouth? Or would he return to Mads Hermansen?

In the pre-match presser, Scott Parker appeared to give the biggest hint possible that his mind was made up over his choice for the trip to Fratton Park, as reported on the club web site: “Head Coach Scott Parker has given his public backing to every member of his West Ham United squad ahead of Tuesday’s EFL Championship fixture at Portsmouth….”.

Recent findings suggest that “My main aim, first and foremost, is to help Mads [Hermansen] and he understands that. [Remarked Parker] He’s got my full backing and my full support. I’ll take him out and I’ll make decisions what I think ultimately is best for Mads and this decision I made on Saturday I thought was best for him and that’s exactly what it was…. “Fin’s come in on Saturday and done really well [on Saturday] so it is what it is. That’s not my main focus at this present moment in time. I’m really pleased with Fin because he’s done brilliant. Also now, let’s give Mads some air, which everyone needs sometimes, and he’ll be back.” (whufc.com) Which is a pretty clear statement that, whilst Hermansen ‘will be back’, it seems unlikely to be the Dane back between the posts tomorrow night against Portsmouth.
