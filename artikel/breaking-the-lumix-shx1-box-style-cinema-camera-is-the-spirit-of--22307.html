Breaking: The Lumix SHX1 Box-Style Cinema Camera Is The Spirit of Varicam Reborn

Panasonic has unveiled the Lumix SHX1, the first compact cinema camera to bear the Lumix branding. Previously, Panasonic separated its cinema and consumer imaging divisions between the two brands, but the SHX1 is the unification of the two under one roof.

In a recent development, “For decades, Panasonic has supported the video industry from broadcast to cinema, and its Varicam cinema cameras have been used to make films, dramas and documentaries worldwide. This expertise has also been carried into Lumix, and it led to the Lumix S1H, introduced in 2019 as a cinema-quality professional video model,” Panasonic says.

According to recent reports, “In 2024, Panasonic took this further by integrating its professional AV and consumer imaging businesses, which share a commitment to supporting customers’ creativity. The newly introduced Lumix SHX1 brings together the expertise of both businesses in a compact cinema camera.”.

The combination of the two Panasonic camera divisions and the existence of the SHX1 effectively means the end of the Varicam brand, but should result in a more cohesive overall camera line.

The SHX1 is, effectively, the S1 II packed into a box-style cinema body, with a couple of exceptions. Firstly, Lumix included an anti-aliasing filter to reduce moire at the cost of some sharpness while the camera also offers the ability to shoot BRAW, albeit with a caveat: it can’t capture it to the CFexpress or SD card slot and must instead use an external SSD.

Sources indicate that beyond that, the camera is capable of capturing basically what the S1 II already does. It features a 6K full-frame partially stacked CMOS image sensor with Dual Native ISO and Dynamic Range Boost, giving the camera effectively 15 stops of dynamic range when in V-log. The SHX1 comes with ARRI LogC3 pre-installed as well, allowing users to choose between V-Log and ARRI LogC3.

Sources indicate that in standard recording formats, the camera supports 6Kp30 and 5.1Kp60 in 4:2:0 10-bit, as well as in 3.8Kp48 4:2:2 10-bit, which is a new option compared to the S1 II. It also, of course, supports open gate recording and in-camera anamorphic de-squeeze.

According to recent reports, the sensor is equipped with a Phase Hybrid autofocus system with 779 points and the SHX1 has the same AI technology as found in the S1 II which enables eye and face detection. Also on-board is Panasonic Lumix's excellent five-axis in-body image stabilization system along with support for Dual IS 2 when combined with a lens that supports OIS and the feature.

In a recent development, professional interfaces include 12G-SDI and HDMI output, timecode input/output, and internal 32-bit float audio recording through the 3.5mm microphone input. The thermal design supports unlimited recording time in up to 4Kp120 and the design of the box camera means it supports micro V-mount batteries, too. Of note, there is no in-camera ND filter, which is an odd exclusion given the target market.

Panasonic also confirmed two optional accessories: the XLR Handle Unit AU-XHU1 and the Control Grip AU-GRP1.

Sources indicate that the Panasonic Lumix SHX1 is slated to become available in January 2027 for $4,699.99. A kit that includes both the handle and the control grip will be avaialble for $5,499.99.
