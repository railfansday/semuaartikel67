Atalanta are still waiting for several key defenders to return to full training ahead of Sunday’s clash with Inter.

According to the club’s official training report, Isak Hien, Odilon Kossounou and Thomas Kristensen did gym work and individual drills on the pitch rather than training with the group on Thursday afternoon. Gianluca Gaetano did the same, while Pompei had treatment and worked in the gym.

There was better news on the internationals. Sead Kolašinac, Eljif Elmas, Lazar Samardžić, Nicola Zalewski and Obrić have all rejoined the squad at Zingonia after their national team duties. Kolašinac’s return is a welcome boost for Maurizio Sarri, who had been short of fit defenders before the break.

The rest of the squad worked on defensive organisation and attacking moves at the Centro Bortolotti, and they’ll train behind closed doors again on Friday afternoon.

Atalanta are 12th on 14 points after beating Monza 1-0 last time out. Second-placed Inter visit the New Balance Arena on Sunday at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT], with Marcus Thuram set to be available for Cristian Chivu’s side.
