Carnival Cruise Line Confirms New Ship Class, Celebration Key Expansion

🌐 Source: Cruiseindustrynews • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (600 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, carnival Cruise Line aims to introduce a new class of ships to its fleet. Two LNG-powered ships of approximately 130,000 gross tons each will be built by Fincantieri for delivery in 2033 and 2035, according to a press release.

It has been revealed that the company stated the newly introduced ships were intentionally designed on a smaller scale. That will give them access to more ports and widen the brand’s destination and itinerary options.

“With a 29-ship fleet, we know our guests appreciate choices,” said Christine Duffy, president of Carnival Cruise Line.

Recent findings suggest that “They love the incredible experiences and amenities on our Excel Class ships, the largest in our fleet, but they also love the intimacy our smaller ships offer. This upcoming class gives us the best of both worlds, helping future-proof our fleet with the flexibility to take America’s Cruise Line to even more places in the years ahead.”.

At the New York event on Thursday, Duffy also gave a first look at the upcoming Carnival Destiny. She outlined expansions at Celebration Key and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, and announced plans to extend popular onboard benefits ashore.

Sources indicate that the Carnival Destiny will debut in 2029 as the first of three Ace Class ships. It will have more outdoor pools than any other Carnival ship: eight in total, including six infinity pools, with more than 11,200 square feet of water surface. They include the brand’s first outward-facing main pool zone.

It has been revealed that other features include an ocean-front lanai with several pools and dining and bar venues near the sea. There will also be new premium venues with multideck ocean views, including an exclusive pool on Deck 18 Aft. The ship will offer 25 new dining and bar experiences and a reimagined Limelight Lounge.

It has been revealed that compared with Carnival’s newest ships, the Carnival Destiny will have nearly twice the space for family venues and attractions. They will be grouped in a single family zone with mini golf, arcades and dining. A newly introduced Dr. Seuss Splash Station will build on Carnival’s 13-year partnership with Dr. Seuss Enterprises.

According to recent reports, for adults, the ship will introduce Carnival’s first adult-only zone. It will span four decks and include the brand’s first adult-only staterooms, an expanded Serenity retreat and an exclusive rooftop pool deck.

Carnival is also planning two expansion phases at Celebration Key, each adding 3,000 guests of land-side capacity, for 6,000 in total.

The first phase is due in 2028. It will add a new portal with a heated beach-entry pool and swim-up bar, a social waterslide for four to five riders and an aqua play area for younger children.

The second phase, in 2030, will bring a major water park with 18 waterslides. It will also have what the organization called the cruise industry’s longest lazy river, plus an exclusive area with an infinity pool, more beach access and upcoming dining and bar concepts.

It has been revealed that in spring 2027, Carnival will open Serenity Beach at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay. It will be a complimentary retreat for guests 21 and older, with daybeds and cabanas available for an extra charge.

Recent findings suggest that starting in February 2027, Carnival’s CHEERS!, CHEERS! Zero Proof, Bottomless Bubbles and CHEERS! Social drink packages will also apply at Celebration Key and RelaxAway.

“Guests are seeking more choice, more flexibility and more ways to make the most of their time together,” Duffy said.
