Latest Update: This is going to hurt: Liverpool reportedly preparing for massive departure

Liverpool are preparing for a massive departure that will change the side dramatically. This one is going to hurt.

In a recent development, liverpool have mentioned goodbye to a series of legends over the last few years. It really started in 2021 when Gini Wijnaldum left for Paris Saint-Germain and then Sadio Mane decided to leave for Bayern Munich a year later. Slowly but surely, Jürgen Klopp's team has disappeared.

Jordan Henderson, Roberto Firmino and Fabinho left in 2023. Klopp himself left in 2024, as did Joel Matip and Thiago. 2025 was a significant one - Trent Alexander-Arnold joined Real Madrid. Then things got even more heartbreaking in 2026 as Mohamed Salah, Andy Robertson and Ibrahima Konate departed.

Sources indicate that salah's exit was arguably the most difficult of the lot. That was the biggest sign that things had moved on - the icon of Klopp's team deciding it was time to leave.

Recent findings suggest that and now 2027 awaits and things are set to continue down the same path. The final stars of that great side have expiring contracts and it increasingly looks like it's time to say goodbye.

Liverpool are reportedly preparing the exit of Alisson Becker, whose contract will expire at the end of the campaign. Alisson's deal was due to end in 2026 but the Reds triggered an option to extend it by a year.

Juventus did try to get a deal done last summer regardless of that, offering a minimal fee to Liverpool. Nothing came of it, however, as no one at the Reds seemed keen to sell the Brazilian.

But Alisson may leave next year. Liverpool won't want him to go but offering a fresh contract to a goalkeeper with his injury history feels like a significant risk. A risk they may not want to take if other options are available.

In a recent development, of course, 'other options' of Alisson's calibre are few and far between. L'Equipe, though, understand that Liverpool have found one. They're eyeing Robin Risser of RC Lens, who was voted the best in Ligue 1 last season.

The 21-year-old is being discussed at Anfield internally as a potential option should Alisson leave. It's the first real sign we've had that the club are very much open to moving on from arguably their greatest-ever goalkeeper.

But we wonder if that could change because of Risser. There's intense competition for him and if he picks somewhere else, we imagine Liverpool will 'settle' for renewing Alisson's deal.

It's very much wait and see, then, but the Reds are certainly preparing for Alisson's exit.
