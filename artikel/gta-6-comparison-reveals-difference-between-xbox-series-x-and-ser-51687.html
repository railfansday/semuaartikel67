One of the best sources for raw Xbox gameplay footage is the YouTube channel Fuzion Xbox Testing, and they're already uploaded their initial look at both the Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S versions of GTA 6.

On Xbox Series X, it's very much what you'd expect - a really amazing-looking game with an internal resolution of 1440p, upscaled to 4K. The 30FPS performance is largely smooth, but does drop frames on occasion.

I also played GTA 6 on Xbox Series X for our Pure Xbox review, and here's what I mentioned about it:.

It has been revealed that "Of course, it's almost a given at this point that GTA 6 looks visually incredible, and I can confirm that it's definitely a stunner on Xbox Series X. There's some occasional pop-in, but it's minimal, and I only ran into a few tiny bugs during the review period (which may have been ironed out for release anyway). The performance is mostly very smooth considering the circumstances as well...".

"It should be noted that I played around 30 hours on a VRR-enabled display during the review period, and around 10 hours without one. I was perfectly happy with the performance in both cases, with the only noticeably big frame rate drops occurring during very rare and visually intensive gameplay sequences.".

In a recent development, on Xbox Series S, GTA 6 still looks really impressive from a visual standpoint, even though some drawbacks have clearly been made in terms of ray tracing and texture quality. The frame rate in the game's initial opening is shown to be a locked 30FPS in some sequences, dropping into the mid-20s during busier moments.

In a recent development, overall though, the feedback we've seen from Xbox Series S players thus far has been very positive, especially in terms of how impressive the game looks. Rockstar Games actually talked to Wccftech about the Series S version as well, revealing they're using AMD FSR Upscaling 4 to scale the resolution to 1080p.

"On the Xbox Series S, we’re starting from a base resolution of 720p, which is scaled to 1080p using AMD FSR Upscaling 4.".

"...[our] focus on lighting made it vital for us to ensure we could use the same lighting solution on each console, including the Xbox Series S, where memory pressure posed a bit of a challenge. The results made that a challenge worth overcoming.".
