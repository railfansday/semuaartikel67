Having fully repositioned into the luxury market in late 2025, Oceania Cruises is planning a significant fleet enhancement that will see its older vessels withdrawn or refurbished.

With five newbuilds entering service over the next decade, the firm recently confirmed plans to retire four ships, starting with the Regatta.

One of the organization’s original ships, the vessel left the fleet in September and will be followed by the Insignia, the Nautica and the Sirena.

Cruise Industry News looks at the company’s prepares for the four ships, which originally debuted between 1998 and 2001.

Sirena Capacity: 684 Tonnage: 30,200 Year Built: 2000 Fate: Sold to Ambassador Cruise Line Farewell Cruise: March 24, 2028.

Sold by Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings earlier in July, the Sirena will leave Oceania’s fleet in late March 2028.

Recent findings suggest that the Ambassador Group later announced plans to operate the vessel in Germany under the new Ambassador Kreuzfahrten brand. Renamed the Ambella, the 2000-built ship will also operate Semester at Sea’s educational cruising program starting in late 2028.

Nautica Capacity: 684 Tonnage: 30,200 Year Built: 2000 Fate: To become Oceania Aurelia Farewell Cruise: October 15, 2027.

The Nautica is being retired from Oceania Cruises’ fleet to undergo a major refurbishment ahead of becoming the Oceania Aurelia.

Recent findings suggest that according to the manufacturer, the 2000-built ship will be reimagined as the ultimate world explorer, with a latest design customized for world cruises and longer voyages. Boasting refreshed public areas and larger suites, the 2000-built vessel is set to debut in late 2027.

Regatta Capacity: 684 Tonnage: 30,200 Year Built: 1998 Fate: Chartered to My Cruises Farewell Cruise: August 17, 2026.

The Regatta was the first ship to leave Oceania Cruises’ fleet, having completed its farewell cruise for the brand in September 2026.

Industry observers note that as reported by Cruise Industry News, the 1998-built vessel was chartered to an Australian tour operator and is now offering world cruises under the Explorations by Norwegian brand. The operator recently renewed its charter contract with Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings through late 2028.

Insignia Capacity: 684 Tonnage: 30,200 Year Built: 1999 Fate: Sold to Phoenix Reisen Farewell Cruise: September 2, 2027.

The Insignia is also scheduled to leave the organization’s fleet in 2027, after being sold to Phoenix Reisen this October.

The 684-passenger vessel is scheduled to undergo a refit before debuting for the German operator as the Alvara in November 2027.
