Earlier this month, we mentioned that Xbox's Black Friday Sale for 2026 was set to begin on November 19th according to a leak, and now Microsoft has officially declared that it will indeed start showing up as of this Thursday.

The leak mentioned it had a start time of 3pm EST, but Xbox actually notes that it'll be 9pm PST / 12pm EST / 5am GMT on November 19th going into November 20th, so you might need to hang around a bit longer until the deals appear.

Recent findings suggest that "The best time to grab a gift for gamers is launching on November 19 at 9 PM PST. During our Black Friday sale, save up to 80% on select XBOX and PC games, and check out discounts on select XBOX accessories.".

We'll obviously have a whole bunch of Xbox Black Friday roundups for you beginning later this week on Pure Xbox, but we should also mention that Microsoft is promoting discounts on various Xbox accessories (like controllers and Xbox Design Lab purchases) for the Black Friday period, along with a special Samsung TV deal that includes a free Xbox Nano controller along with two months of Xbox Game Pass Ultimate.
