Need a pick-me-up for Black Friday shopping? Dunkin’ has your back.

The coffee chain is bringing back one of its popular promotions just in time for the busiest shopping day of the year, along with a collection of festive merchandise that might take care of a few people on your holiday shopping list.

Beginning Friday, Nov. 27, Dunkin’ Rewards members can score a free classic doughnut with the purchase of any beverage. To get the freebie, make sure you’re an active Dunkin’ Rewards member and have the app downloaded on your phone.

It has been revealed that if this week doesn’t work, you’ll have two more opportunities to take advantage of the deal. The promotion will run for three consecutive Fridays, meaning customers can also snag a free doughnut on Dec. 4 and Dec. 11.

This year’s seasonal packaging features the Dunkin’ Donutcracker, a colorful reimagining of the traditional holiday nutcracker.

Dunkin’ is taking the cute character beyond its coffee cups and doughnut boxes with a merchandise collection. Fans can shop a Donutcracker limited-edition tote for $50, a “dad hat” for $28 and acrylic ornament for $18 at DunkinRunsOnMerch.com.

Additional holiday-themed item like mugs, tumblers and ornaments ranging from $12.99 to $34.99, will arrive at participating Dunkin’ locations in early December.

The coffee and doughnut chain is even bringing its festive mascot to life with a towering 15-foot Donutcracker installation in Steubenville, Ohio, which, if you weren’t aware, is home to the world’s largest collection of life-size nutcrackers. Visitors can see the installation at Historic Fort Steuben’s Christmas at the Fort celebration beginning Nov. 27.

So, will you be picking up your free doughnut this Friday?
