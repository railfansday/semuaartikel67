Liverpool have earned a reputation for recruiting some of the best young players in world football in recent seasons.

The additions of Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni and more recently Jeremy Jacquet and Luca Brughmans are proof of the philosophy in place within Liverpool's recruitment team.

With Julian Ward at the helm, the recruitment strategy doesn't seem to have changed, and the Reds continue to be linked with some of the most promising young talents from across Europe and beyond.

Liverpool's mission remains the same but that doesn't mean that they are automatically the favourites for every wonderkid, and they appear to be falling behind in the race for one highly rated defender.

The 18-year-old has started all but one game for FC Twente in the league this season, and has established himself as a central part of John van den Brom's side. Standing at 6'5, Nijstad already holds the stature of a Premier League defender, and his impressive performances for Twente, both domestically and in the UEFA Conference League, have led to interest from several top sides.

The 18-year-old has started all but one game for FC Twente in the league this season, and has established himself as a central part of John van den Brom's side.

Standing at 6'5, Nijstad already holds the stature of a Premier League defender, and his impressive performances for Twente, both domestically and in the UEFA Conference League, have led to interest from several top sides.

A report from Ekrem Konur recently named Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Chelsea and Manchester United as the four clubs with the most advanced interest, and it appears that Liverpool are already falling behind in the race for the next Dutch colossus.

Nijstad is also capable of playing at left-back and is left-footed, making him an ideal long-term replacement for Virgil van Dijk.

Having the chance to learn from the man who captain's his national team could be vital for Nijstad, who could command a fee of over €30 million (~£25m) according to Konur.

Liverpool clearly aren't leading the race for Nijstad, but he would be an excellent January pick up and Ward & Co should definitely be taking a look at the 18-year-old.
