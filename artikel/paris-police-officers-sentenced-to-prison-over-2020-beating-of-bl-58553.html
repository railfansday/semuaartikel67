Paris police officers sentenced to prison over 2020 beating of Black man

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (49 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A Paris court on Friday sentenced three policemen to serve time over the 2020 beating of Black music producer Michel Zecler, a rare such ruling. The assault, which was captured on camera, sparked nationwide outrage and renewed scrutiny of police violence and racism in France.
