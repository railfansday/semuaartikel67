TCL NXTPAPER phones and tablets are all on sale well more than a week ahead of Black Friday, and many models are down to their lowest prices ever.

OLED is wonderful and all, but many of us are feeling the strain from looking at the ultra-bright, extra-vibrant output from these devices. That’s where TCL NXTPAPER technology comes in. NXTPAPER devices were designed to give you all the features and power you need for everyday tasks, all with a screen that’s soft and eye-friendly.

My favorite budget phone this year just so happens to be the TCL NXTPAPER 70 Pro, which is down to just $259 (21% off) right now for the 128GB model. You can opt for 512GB of storage for just $319 (25% off), too, so folks who need more won’t have to spend an arm and a leg. This sale is even better than the one TCL had just 10 days ago and is the company’s official Black Friday pricing, so while it lasts through November 30, there’s no reason to wait for a better price.

Industry observers note that if you’re looking for a tablet, I’ve got two picks that stand out from the rest. The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus is down to $199 right now, making it almost 30% off the regular price. This size is perfect for reading books or manga, both of which look superb on the textured, matte NXTPAPER display. I find that this size tablet is sort of the Goldilocks size — major enough to be more useful than a phone, but not too large to be unwieldy.

Sources indicate that folks who are more into taking notes should check out the TCL Note A1 NXTPAPER, which is TCL’s most premium tablet to date and is on sale for $449 right now (25% off). This one has a wildly different UI compared to other TCL products and is intended mostly for note-taking and ideating, although it still runs Android and the Play Store, so you can load anything you want on it. The stylus feels marvelous on the textured, matte NXTPAPER display, and this one packs a newer-generation display than the rest of TCL’s NXTPAPER tablets, too.
