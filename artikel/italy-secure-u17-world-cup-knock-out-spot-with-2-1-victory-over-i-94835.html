Italy have secured a spot in the U17 World Cup round of 32 with one group stage game to spare thanks to a 2-1 victory over the Ivory Coast in Qatar on Monday.

A first-half rebound from Atalanta’s Francesco Gasparello and a second-half free-kick from Empoli’s Francesco Olivieri sent Italy into a 2-0 lead, which was enough for three points against Ivory Coast, despite a 100th-minute penalty from Harafat Diabate.

It has been revealed that atalanta’s Diego Perillo also had the ball in the back of the net in the 22nd minute, but saw his strike ruled out for offside. Ivory Coast also had a penalty decision overturned by VAR in the 77th minute.

This was Italy’s second victory in as many matches in the U17 World Cup group stage, following a 6-0 win over Jamaica in their opening game of the tournament on Friday.

As things stand, the Azzurrini sit at the top of Group E with a maximum six points from two matches. Ivory Coast are second on three points, Uzbekistan are third on three points and Jamaica are fourth on zero.

Italy are guaranteed to finish lower than third place in their group, and in the event that they do finish in third place, they are now guaranteed to be one of the eight best third-placed sides, which will be enough for a spot in the round of 32.

Gasparello 28’, Olivieri 70’, Diabate 90+10’.
