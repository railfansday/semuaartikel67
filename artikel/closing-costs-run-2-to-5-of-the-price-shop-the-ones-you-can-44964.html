Closing Costs Run 2% to 5% of the Price – Shop the Ones You Can

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Budget for closing costs that typically run 2% to 5% of the home’s price, due at closing on top of your down payment. Then cut them by comparing Loan Estimates from at least three mortgage lenders and shopping for the title and other closing services your lender lets you pick.

Even 3% on a $350,000 home bought with 10% down adds $10,500 to the $35,000 down payment. Not all of it is fixed.

Each lender’s Loan Estimate, the form it must send within three business days of your application, sorts those costs into groups. To cut the lender’s own charges and the services it orders, such as the appraisal, compare lenders. Transfer taxes and recording fees go to the government.

Three items aren’t fees at all: prepaid interest for the days left in the month you close, often a year of homeowners insurance, and the first deposit into the escrow account that covers your property taxes and insurance. They still take cash at closing.

It has been revealed that ask at least three mortgage lenders for a Loan Estimate on the same loan. Apply to them all within two weeks, and the credit checks count as one. Then get your own quotes for the services the form says you can shop for. The biggest is the title company’s charge to search the records and insure against a later claim to the home. Your lender may accept a title company that isn’t on its list of providers.

Sources indicate that say that for the same rate and title work, the cheapest of three Loan Estimates comes in $1,500 under your first quote, and a title company you found charges $800 less than the lender’s pick. That’s $2,300 off the $10,500.

Recent findings suggest that if cash is short, lender credits can cover part of the bill in return for a higher interest rate, so you pay more over time. A seller may cover some too, if you ask in your offer. And closing late in the month shrinks your prepaid interest.

According to recent reports, your Closing Disclosure, with your final costs, must reach you at least three business days ahead of closing. Hold it beside your latest Loan Estimate and ask the lender to explain every line that went up. Unless your loan, finances, or appraisal changed, the lender’s own fees can’t rise at all, and recording fees plus the services you chose from its list can rise only 10% in total.
