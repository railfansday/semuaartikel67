It has been revealed that apple raised its prices this year, but who didn't? It's something we've seen across the board with tech. That's why we're all grateful for Black Friday, which is here once again. This is your best chance to dodge the price hikes and land a tech bargain, so we're committed to paying extra close attention to the best Black Friday Apple deals.

Yes, Apple does do Black Friday, but not in the way you might expect. Shopping directly on Apple's website usually means getting a gift card for future purchases. That's not quite what most shoppers are seeking. To get the real deals, you need to look at retailers such as Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, and Target. These retailers duke it out to see who can offer the best prices on AirPods, MacBooks, and iPads. Walmart and Amazon are famous for outdoing each other by a single cent. Which is fine by us.

Usually, prices on Apple products drop to record lows during the hectic Black Friday weekend. However, especially good deals can also sell out rapidly. In years past, Mashable's team spotted many a record low on AirPods only for them to sell out before we could finish writing a 200-word story about it.

Industry observers note that best Buy, Amazon, and Target's official Black Friday sales all technically started well before Black Friday proper on Nov. 27, and Apple prices are showing it. The AirPods Pro 3 dropped to a record low of $149, the Apple Watch Series 12 is $100 off and down to a upcoming record low, and the 13-inch MacBook Air dipped below $1,000 for the first time since its price increase earlier this year, but has now popped back up to $1,049. Expect prices to keep nudging up and down like that.

According to recent reports, we were hoping our favorite AirPods would return to their record-low pricing of $169. Our expectations? Met and surpassed, as our favorite AirPods dropped down to $149, marking a new record-low price by a margin of $20. All told, that cuts an impressive $100 off their $249 list price (a total discount of 40%). Barring any freak price drops (never say never), this will be the absolute best price you can get for these earbuds that come with excellent noise-cancellation, great battery life at eight hours per charge, impressive balanced sound, and a built-in heart rate monitor.

The AirPods 5 haven't received a proper discount yet, as Apple's newest AirPods. The version without a wireless charging case got a few dollars off the $129 price around its release, while the $149 wireless charging case edition hasn't had any price drops. If these buds are going on sale in 2026, Black Friday is likely when.

In a recent development, though not quite the lowest price we've seen on the 13-inch Air, it's still a great deal considering that post MacBook price-hike, this laptop hasn't fallen below $1,000. According to our laptop expert, this is a great laptop for college students because of its size and zippy M5 power — and it's also an overall daily driver that will more than suit most folks' needs. If you prefer a slightly larger display, the 15-inch Air with the same specs is $300 off at the time of writing.

In a recent development, the M6 Mac mini is on sale for $779 at Amazon, down from $899. That's $120 in savings on the model with 16GB of memory and 256GB of storage. If you already have a monitor, keyboard, and mouse, it's worth a look — just keep in mind that none of those come in the box.

According to recent reports, our sister site PCMag gave the M6 Mac mini an Editors' Choice award, praising its performance, compact design, and ability to stay cool and quiet. It measures just five inches across, but still has room for two front-facing USB-C ports, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, and an HDMI connection, with support for up to three displays. Let's start with the very expensive elephant in the room. In June, Apple raised prices on MacBooks, Macs, iPads, the HomePod, Apple TVs, and even the Vision Pro headset, followed by iPhone price increases in September. With some prices rising by $200 or more, the likelihood of them falling to record lows is low. Instead, we research the market to identify products that have been at or near record lows over the past five months.

We also took into account the frequency of deals. If, like the AirPods Pro 3, there's a $50 deal that's nearly always on, we don't necessarily consider that a deal worth highlighting. On the other hand, if an iPad is $100 off for the first time in a month, we'll include it and note its pricing history.

Finally, we considered our reviewers' first-hand experiences with these products. Most of the items in this guide have been hands-on tested by at least one person on the Mashable staff. Our team also prioritized Mashable award winners, choosing the best deals based on overall product performance and value.
