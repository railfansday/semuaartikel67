Mauricio Pochettino has defended the mentality of his Manchester United players despite their poor start to the season.

According to recent reports, the Argentine suffered a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday in his first match since replacing Michael Carrick. United sit 19th in the Premier League after 11 matches, with only Crystal Palace below them. However, speaking ahead of Wednesday’s Champions League fixture against Sporting in Lisbon, Pochettino rejected suggestions he had inherited a fragile group of players.

In a recent development, “The situation is fragile,” Pochettino told reporters, as quoted by the Press Association. “We are not fragile. We are a strong team.”.

The United head coach pointed to last season’s third-place finish under Carrick as evidence of the squad’s capabilities. He also spoke of the commitment shown by his players in the dressing room at Anfield despite the manner of Sunday’s defeat.

It has been revealed that united played much of that match with 10 men following Leny Yoro’s eighth-minute dismissal, a decision that has since been overturned on appeal. Wednesday’s fixture will give Pochettino another opportunity to assess his players.

Unlike their Premier League campaign, United’s European results have been mixed rather than consistently poor. They have won two and lost two of their opening four Champions League fixtures, while Sporting have collected nine points.

Pochettino insisted his immediate priority was the match in Lisbon rather than Saturday’s visit of Brentford to Old Trafford.
