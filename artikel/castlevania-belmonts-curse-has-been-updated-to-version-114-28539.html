Konami has released a upcoming update for Castlevania: Belmont's Curse on the Switch and other platforms this week.

In a recent development, although it's only a small one, it does include some adjustments and fixes. This includes a slight change to the "effective range" of the "Life Drain" spell. Here's the rundown from Konami's official website about everything included in the Version 1.1.4 update:.

Industry observers note that the following changes and fixes have been made in this update: - The version number is now displayed at the bottom of the title screen. - Adjusted the effective range of the “Life Drain” spell. - Fixed an issue where the screen could remain black after loading under certain conditions. - Fixed various other issues.

According to recent reports, belmont's Curse is at the moment available for the Switch. While there's no Switch 2 Edition, if you do play this title on Nintendo's latest hardware, you'll get a frame rate boost. And if you haven't tried it out yet, you can download a demo from the eShop.
