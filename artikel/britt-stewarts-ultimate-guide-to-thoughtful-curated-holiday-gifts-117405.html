In a recent development, the last thing anyone wants for the holidays is a gift that feels impersonal! To avoid giving something to your loved ones that they'll just toss to the side and not actually appreciate, it's time to get serious with your gift-giving this year.

Ahead of the Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale, E! exclusively chatted with pro Britt Stewart, who is not only a dancer with moves we envy, but a very intentional gift giver:.

Sources indicate that "I’ll always choose giving an experience over things because creating memories with the people I love most is incredibly crucial to me," she told us. "If I chose to gift something physical, it has to be thoughtful and curated specifically for the person receiving it. As a self-care advocate, my favorite gifts are the ones that remind people to pause, reset, and take a little time for themselves.".

And as for her favorite part of celebrating the holiday season, it's building holiday memories with her husband, actor and her former DWTS partner, Daniel Durant.

"For me, the best part of celebrating the holidays is spending quality time with my family. My husband Daniel and I got married last August and we’re still creating what our own holiday traditions look like, which makes it extra special. I also love that the end of the year gives us all a genuine reason to slow down, be present with the people we love most and reflect on the past year," she shared.

Scroll on for all of Britt's thoughtful gift ideas and tips, including her Los Angeles favorites, just in time for the holidays.

Do you have any current favorites in your beauty routine that you think would make great holiday gifts for beauty lovers?

According to recent reports, "My favorite beauty tool right now is the Therabody Face depuffing wand, which cools and heats, and is one of my favorite things to do during my morning rituals. I put on a daily sheet mask and use the cool setting first to help my face depuff, then switch to the heated setting to help the serum really penetrate my skin. It is a must have and would make an amazing gift for the beauty and skin care enthusiasts in your life.".

This celeb-loved depuffing tool has cold and heat treatments to help reduce under-eye puffiness, enhance radiance and diminish the look of dark circles.

What’s a gift you’ve received that you get so much use out of?

In a recent development, "One of the best parts about gift-giving is getting someone something they wouldn’t normally buy for themselves, but actually really need. My husband Daniel is always up to date on the latest technology, so last year he gifted me a new laptop. It was something I hadn’t done for myself in over ten years, and it’s a gift that I use on a daily basis!".

Lightweight and portable, this MacBook is available in four different colors, each with a vibrant 13-inch display.

Industry observers note that "Not every gift needs to be as big as a fresh laptop and sometimes gifts that are inspired by a conversation with a friend mean so much! I have organized Secret Santa for the Dancing with the Stars Live tour cast for the past couple years, and one year Emma Slater gave me a productivity and idea building journal. It was so intentional, as she knew how much I love journaling, but picked something specifically aligned with a goal I had at that time.".

This productivity planner by Papier has space to plan and track your goals, with pages for monthly, weekly and daily planning.

If you were going to a white elephant gift exchange with your fellow DWTS pros - what would you bring and why?

Sources indicate that "For my fellow pros, I would bring a gift card for a massage. We all put ourselves through so much both physically and mentally and a massage would be the perfect gift that we all need! I’d specifically choose The NOW Massage, which is a local favorite here in the Los Angeles area, and gift them their specific signature treatment called The Stretch.".

According to recent reports, for those not in L.A., give them a gift of a massage at home! This jojoba oil-based massage candle is full of skin-loving ingredients.

This body brush is made with copper bristles, ideal for using before a massage or luxurious bath to remove dead skin and boost circulation.

What’s your go-to host/hostess gift when attending holiday gatherings?

It has been revealed that "If you want to step away from the usual bottle of wine or bouquet of flowers, I love finding a nice and unique bottle of olive oil as the perfect holiday gift. Everyone needs a good bottle of olive oil in their kitchen! Flamingo Estate, a local tech giant here in Los Angeles that ships nationwide, is such a great brand for this. They also have produce boxes filled with seasonal produce and curated gift boxes, which is another gift option that is fresh and unique.".

According to recent reports, this Flamingo Estate trio is perfect for anyone who loves to cook, or has it on their list of resolutions for 2027. It includes the brand's olive oil, plus wildflower honey and salsa macha.

You spend a lot of time on the road for DWTS tour - what are some must-haves you always bring that a travel lover might appreciate for a holiday gift?

"A must-have for traveling are my packing cubes. I love to stay organized and I have a place for almost everything, so packing cubes for your clothes and a nice travel toiletry bag are always the way to go for anyone who travels a lot.".

Available in multiple sizes and colors, the packing cubes by BÉIS fit seamlessly into their bestselling luggage, with labels to help you stay organized for any trip.

Recent findings suggest that "I also always travel with a candle. Being away from home for long periods of time has taught me that even something as small as a candle can make an environment feel cozy and a little bit more like home.".

Can't get enough of Flamingo Estate? This luxe candle includes notes of vanilla, cypress, guaiac and green vanilla.

What are some holiday gift ideas for newlyweds?

In a recent development, "Tying back to my love for experiences over material gifts, giving newlyweds a fun activity or memorable experience they can do together is such an amazing way to help them make upcoming memories and stay connected. With how busy Daniel and I are, carving out time to create memories together is something we hold really close to our hearts. For our first anniversary, my mom secretly paid for our dinner and we only found out afterward. Those little surprises and thoughtful touches go a long way whatever stage of your relationship you’re in.".

Turn a happy memory from their wedding day into a fun activity with this photo puzzle, which can be framed and hung at home!

"If you lean more towards sentimental gifts, finding something unique to your relationship and turning it into a keepsake is always so special. For Daniel and I's wedding, we actually printed real love notes we had sent each other onto our cocktail napkins. You could do something similar like getting those framed to hang in your home. It is deeply personalized and serves as a daily reminder of all the little moments in your relationship.".

What gifts would you recommend giving the cat parent in your life for the holidays?

"I would recommend gifting a personalized custom portrait of your cats for something both meaningful and fun! I have used the firm Perkie Prints in the past to create a beautiful picture of my cats. They also print those portraits onto a sweatshirt, which is really cute so you can always have your pets with you no matter what.".

You probably already treat your pet like royalty, so get a one-of-a-kind canvas that captures their true essence.

Sources indicate that "Another idea that is a bit more serious, but so essential is gifting a month or two of pet insurance for your friends who are pet parents. We recently lost one of our senior cats, Hudson, and his brother and litter mate, Orlando, is currently experiencing some health concerns due to his age. We also have a 1.5 year old Tuxedo cat named Midna and we set her up with pet insurance as soon as we rescued her. Having pet insurance has been extremely helpful during these times as our beloved cats go through serious health issues and get older. Giving the gift of pet insurance would be something really meaningful and intentional for pet parents that can set them and their pets up for anything that may come up in the future.".
