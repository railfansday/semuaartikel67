With most AI chatbots using your interactions as training data, we’ve previously cautioned against sharing sensitive personal details with them, highlighting the much stronger privacy protections offered by Apple. If you ever needed evidence of the importance of this, the Character․ai mess provides it.

In a recent development, the company recently filed for bankruptcy and is now offering all of its assets for sale, including the contents of user chat sessions. Some 42 state attorneys general are trying to block this, but it’s unclear whether they will succeed ….

Character․AI had long come under fire for its extremely irresponsible attitude to privacy and safety. Last summer, it was accused of harvesting mental health data from underage customers, and in May of this year it faced a lawsuit after its chatbots were found to be pretending to be licensed doctors. The company filed for bankruptcy last month.

A group of 42 attorneys general are trying to block the chatbot company from auctioning off customers’ chat histories and other sensitive information […] “Nothing in the agreement restricts what the buyer may do with the data after closing, including selling it,” the AGs say in a court filing […].

It has been revealed that “Character․AI’s plan to auction off its platform, including the IP addresses and private chats histories of its consumers — many of them children — with zero protection for those users is appalling,” California Attorney General Rob Bonta noted in a statement. “If this sale goes through, users’ sensitive data would go into the hands of a buyer even though Character․AI promised consumers that their data would not be sold.”.

The chat histories reportedly include conversations in which AI characters drew both adults and children into explicit sexual conversations.

Siri AI interactions are handled on-device wherever possible. Where this isn’t possible, Private Cloud Compute (PCC) servers are used, with guarantees that no data is stored or linked to your Apple account. Apple has designed the system in such a way that its privacy claims can be independently verified by security researchers.

When a user makes a request, Apple Intelligence analyzes whether it can be processed on device. For more complex requests, it can draw on Private Cloud Compute, which extends the privacy and security of your iPhone into the cloud. With Private Cloud Compute, only the data that is relevant to your request is processed on Apple silicon servers, before being removed.

According to recent reports, the data being processed in Private Cloud Compute is not stored or made accessible to Apple. It is processed only to fulfill your request, after which point the results are returned securely to your device and are not retained by Private Cloud Compute. Independent privacy and security researchers can inspect the code that runs on Apple silicon servers that enable Private Cloud Compute to verify this privacy promise at any time.

It has been revealed that apple also recently introduced a safe way to ask an AI questions about your health. Data sources used are limited to the same platforms used by healthcare professionals, with links to peer-reviewed clinical citations. Apple guarantees that your chat sessions are not used for AI training with no retention of either questions or answers.
