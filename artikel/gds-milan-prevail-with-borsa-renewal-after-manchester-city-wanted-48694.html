Gabriele Borsa is a name that AC Milan will hope to hear more of in the coming years, and those years have now been secured.

Earlier this year, Gerry Cardinale admitted that he wanted to prioritise pathways from the academy to the first team, even with looks to invest more. Indeed, after the failure to re-sign Mattia Liberali, he’s been focused on ensuring they remain in control of the stars of tomorrow.

According to recent reports, milan have done fantastically with Borsa, though. As Gazzetta dello Sport writes, the 16-year-old has already signed his first professional deal with the club – posing in front of the Futuro logo – and this may seem like just another renewal.

However, he’s an investment for the future. The Rossoneri believe he’s one of the best prospects in the academy and they want to take a chance on him and they weren’t the only ones. In fact, Manchester City had been sniffing around, but they’ve been pushed back by this renewal.

A ‘modest striker who lives for goals‘, Borsa is certainly one for the future and the jump to the Futuro will hopefully be another easy step for him. Importantly, he’s not another Francesco Camarda and he will be given the time to find his feet.

With consistent goals – which come in all manners already – he’ll learn with the Futuro and probably struggle occasionally. With Sergio Navarro’s guidance, though, he could become special.
