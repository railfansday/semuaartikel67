Sources indicate that olivia Rodrigo has been letting the holiday spirit guide her lately: Just weeks after releasing her first Christmas song, “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” she debuted the song’s B-side “Miss This Christmas” live in Nashville. Now, she’s dropping the official version of the track exclusively on Amazon Music.

Like her album you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, the two holiday songs capture different sides of love and romance: “Take Me Home (4 Christmas)” is an upbeat, enthusiastic track, inspired by Christmas songs from the Eighties as Rodrigo sings about the anticipation of meeting a significant other’s family. A playful video starred SNL cast member Ben Marshall as the guy bringing her home for Christmas, while Cynthia Nixon took on the role of Marshall’s mom meeting Rodrigo for the first time.

“Miss This Christmas” is a sadder, melancholy ballad, written about longing for someone during the holiday season. “Happy Christmas to the lonely/Broken hearts that feel just like me,” she sings on the track, which she wrote with Dan Nigro and Amy Allen. “Yeah, next year’s gonna look real different/Tell your folks I’ll miss their Christmas.”.

It has been revealed that when Rodrigo performed “Miss This Christmas” at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena this week, she called it a “really sad Christmas song” and shared that “no one’s ever really heard it before.” Two days before that, she’d stopped by Jack White’s Blue Room in the city, recording two 20-minute sets for a live-to-acetate session for Third Man Records. Earlier this month, she performed “Take Me Home (4 Christmas) (Songline Version),” recorded at the Brooklyn Botanic Garden in New York City and directed by Bradley J. Calder. That performance is also streaming exclusively on Amazon Music and available on YouTube.

Industry observers note that rodrigo was one of the artists with the most Grammy nominations this year: She scored seven nods for you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, including Album of the Year, Song of the Year, and Record of the Year. Her Unraveled tour will run through Dec. 20 in Las Vegas, and then pick up with a residency in Los Angeles from Jan. 12 through 29.
