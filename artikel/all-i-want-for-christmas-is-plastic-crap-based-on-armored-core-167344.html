I'm a great lover of action figures, miniatures, model kits, basically anything made out of plastic that's based on the cool videogames I like. When thinking of things that we might recommend people purchase on this Blackest of Fridays, my eyes alighted on the Bandai Namco 30 Minutes Missions Steel Haze Rusty who lives on my desk. He is one of my truest friends.

According to recent reports, I know a lot of enthusiasts recommend HobbyLink Japan, but I've had better experiences with the USA Gundam Store. They're offering site-wide deals, but I have yet to be indoctrinated as a Gundam Guy—my mecha gateway drug has been Armored Core, so that is what I can recommend.

V4 Rusty's iconic Nachtreiher mech, "Steel Haze." He comes with a Sampu SMG, Ransetsu burst rifle, Vvc-703PM plasma missile launcher, and, of course, the iconic Vvc-7HLS Darth Maul-ass double-bladed laser slicer. He is the finest buddy you could ask for, and I eagerly await an official kit for his upgraded mech, Steel Haze Ortus.

It has been revealed that G1 Michigan is undoubtedly one of the most swagged-out pilots in Armored Core 6, even if my boy Rusty was able to take him and like, 50 other guys no sweat. Liger Tail's quad base design is visually striking, while its heavy duty weaponry like the Songbird double mortar and HU-BEN chain gun are so true to Michigan's bombastic personality. This is also one of the bigger deals I noticed among USAGS's Armored Core offerings.
