There are some familiar faces taking the reins at Serie B club Empoli, because Lorenzo Tonelli is the latest coach, with former Napoli and Spain defender Raul Albiol on his staff.

The club decided to sack coach Matteo Andreoletti after a truly disastrous campaign left them rock bottom of the Serie B table after two wins, three draws and eight defeats.

They formally appointed Tonelli today, although as he does not have the right coaching badges yet, he is only allowed to take the job on a temporary basis.

The 36-year-old former defender spent much of his career at Empoli, coming up through their youth academy, then returning in 2021 before his retirement in 2024, via Napoli and Sampdoria.

According to Sky Sport Italia, Tonelli asked his former Napoli teammate Albiol to join his coaching staff, initially as a match analyst.

It is the first job for the Spaniard in management after hanging up his boots in 2026, when his contract with Pisa came to an end.

The 41-year-old spent his playing career at Valencia, Getafe, Real Madrid, Napoli, Villarreal and Pisa, while he was in the Spain squad that won the World Cup and two editions of the European Championship.

Another former player, striker Francesco Tavano, is the assistant manager.
