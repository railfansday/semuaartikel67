GTA 6 Riata locations: How to restore the second car for Wyman

According to recent reports, you'll have to track down the GTA 6 Riata locations to fix the second Classic Car Collection vehicle for Wyman. He'll text you a little while after you complete the Transgressor, this time sending you to the Key Lento area, unfortunately a rather long drive when you're likely still at the motel near Port Gellhorn.

There are three Riatas you need to scan with the WAiNK It app to fix up this classic off-roading 4x4, one in Key Lento itself, one near the naval base at Goose Key, and a final one in Shoeless Keys.

This is the first one Wyman asks you to find, near the PD in the Keys. It's under a brown stilt house north of Leonida Key Lento Mini-mart and the Shrimp 'n Save, on that northernmost road running through the Key. Find the brown stilt house, as in the video above, then just head underneath to scan the car.

Sources indicate that this second one is located in Southwind, on Goose Key, the island with the Mile Zero Air Station to the west of Key Lento—you will have visited here for a mission early in the game. The junked car is under another brown stilt house at the end of the road down to the east from the Xero Gas - Southwind station, on the right just before the sea.

This third one is located behind the ruined gas station just to the north of Grassrivers Cafe & Deli in Shoeless Keys. The tricky part is that this is a four-star area (because of the final missions in Chapter 1), so you'll want to be quick as otherwise a police chopper will come and spot you. When you turn off the main road, the garage is on your left before you reach the cafe itself, with the car just behind.

Somehow, I forgot to record this one, but I still show the location in the clip above behind the gas station (just without the car since it was gone when I re-recorded).

According to recent reports, once you have all three, Wyman will tell you to wait a little while, and then will call and ask you to Buck him $1,500 for the labour. Select the payment request in the text, or do it manually in the app. A little while after the money is sent, you'll get a map marker for Wyman's scrapyard, where you can pick up this completed classic car.
