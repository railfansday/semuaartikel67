Gift-giving on a tight budget is tough, but constraints can also encourage some creative thinking. We thought for a long while (and asked other Verge staffers) about the best items out there, and we’re pretty happy with what we came up with.

For this list, we’ve rounded up a barrage of fun gadgets and stocking-stuffer specials. You’ll find nostalgic ways to spruce up a room like Play-Doh’s Blooms, fancy ways to jot down notes like the Pilot Kakuno fountain pen, and plenty of tech-forward extras like the Time Timer Mod. It’s time to get creative with your budget buys, and this list can get you there.

They also work great behind your entertainment center or wherever else is convenient.
