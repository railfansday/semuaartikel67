Sources indicate that A coffee subscription is one of my favorite gifts for the person who has everything. It's fun; it's educational; and it's consumable, so it won't clutter up their house. We have a whole guide to our favorites, but Atlas has topped the list for years, and just launched its Black Friday deal.

Recent findings suggest that once or twice a month—or quarterly—your recipient will receive specialty roasted beans from a different region of the world, along with a colorful postcard and tasting notes. The location will be a surprise—anywhere from Thailand to Burundi to China.

For Black Friday, now through December 7, you'll get a free bag with any two-bag subscription purchase. You can choose whole bean or ground (or Nespresso or Keurig pods), roast type, regular or decaf, and—upcoming this year—"Smooth & Balanced" or “Bright & Expressive” profiles. (A subscription to the latter, for more adventurous drinkers, runs an additional $2 per bag.).

Also newly introduced this year is the lower-priced quarterly gift option (three bags every three months), featuring three bags, each from a different country—a significant value over the traditional monthly delivery (see below).

Recent findings suggest that if your giftee is more interested in mushroom coffee, Atlas tops our list of the best there as well. Our reviewer liked the Latte Superblend so much he even considered replacing his morning coffee with this easy-blend powder.

Right now you'll get a free starter kit when you order either one or two bags of Atlas’ adaptogen-infused mushroom coffee, which includes a measuring scoop, milk frother, and a “wellness booklet." Shipping is free.

Just note that ordering will automatically initiate a monthly subscription, but you can cancel it at any time. (Or keep it going, if it's a gift and your giftee likes it.).
