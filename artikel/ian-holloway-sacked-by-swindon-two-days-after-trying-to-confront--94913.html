Published: 05:52 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 10:57 EST, 23 November 2026.

Ian Holloway has been sacked by Swindon just two days after attempting to confront angry fans following defeat to Newport on Saturday.

The journeyman manager had himself called the 2-0 loss on the road 'unforgivable' after he was taunted by home supporters chanting that he would be 'sacked in the morning' after the result.

Holloway had intended to go down the tunnel after the final whistle, and passed the away stand on his route out, taking off his cap as he held up his hands in resignation.

But as boos rained down on him, the 63-year-old could not resist the urge to engage, looking to answer back to the fans before he was restrained by security.

Last month, Holloway described himself as a 'dead man walking' with the Robins mired 17th in League Two, but Monday morning saw him despatched after two seasons in charge.

'Since joining the club towards the end of 2024, Ian has played an notable role in stabilising our on-field position and helping the club make progress season upon season,' the Swindon statement announcing his departure read.

The journeyman manager oversaw two seasons at the club, saving them from relegation in 2024-25.

'However, following careful consideration of recent results and performances, the club believes that now is the right time to take a different direction.

'This has not been an easy decision, but the board firmly believes that it is important to make difficult decisions when necessary, always with the best interests of Swindon Town at heart.'.

Holloway cut a disillusioned figure at Rodney Parade as he gave a heartfelt assessment of his side's performance after defeat to the Exiles.

'It's devastating,' stated Holloway. 'I'm bitterly disappointed. Did I expect my team to do that? Not in a million years.

In a recent development, 'We're getting regular mistakes from almost everyone in that backline and in that midfield. We knew what type of game it was going to be. There's no excuses. I've just noted to them "what on Earth do you think you're doing?" To start that way? It was so passive it was unforgivable.

'It's very painful at the minute. It'll come back at the manager in the end if I listen to all these people (from the stands).

'This is football. I've been in it a long time. This is my life.'.

Holloway also questioned the desire of his players, continuing: 'Bitterly disappointed in all of them and disappointed for the fans. I get it, that didn't look like our team.

'They scored two ugly goals. All our chances that we had, normally we take them.

Recent findings suggest that 'Where's the fight? It's a local derby. I'm sorry, they've done their bits better than us.'.

Holloway has endured an uneven stint at Swindon since 2024, saving the club from relegation in his first season but failing to challenge for the promotion places as time wore on.

Memorably, the former Crystal Palace and Queen's Park Rangers manager claimed that the training ground was haunted shortly after his arrival in Wiltshire.

Holloway had first suggested that there had been 'some strange things happening' after the club captain Ollie Clarke had ruptured his ankle in training, before enlisting his wife Kim to free spirits potentially trapped in the South West.

'I'm absolutely devastated so I'm going to try and cleanse the training ground area because people are telling me it's haunted,' Holloway said on BBC Radio Wiltshire in November 2024.

Sources indicate that 'There's a graveyard somewhere near. Honestly, I'm not joking.

'I think our training ground is very close to an ancient burial site so I'm going to get my wife to come up and say sorry to all these people and hopefully we'll have a bit more luck.'.
