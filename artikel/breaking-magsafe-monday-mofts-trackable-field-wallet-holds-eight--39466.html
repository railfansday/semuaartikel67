Sources indicate that the one thing Apple’s own MagSafe wallet does that a few third-party wallets have is Find My. If you misplace your wallet, your iPhone tells you where it is. Most MagSafe wallets either skip tracking entirely or have a pocket for an AirTag, which adds thickness and it’s another thing to buy. The MOFT Trackable Field Wallet builds the tracker into the wallet itself, so it indicates up in the Find My app.

MagSafe Monday: Every Monday, Bradley Chambers looks at the latest and greatest in the MagSafe and wireless charging industry to help you get the most out of your Apple devices that support wireless charging.

MOFT has been making the origami-style folding wallet for many years, and this model works like any other Find My accessory. So that means you get left-behind alerts when you leave without it, and the ability to make it trackable through Apple’s network if it’s across town instead of under the couch. It also works with Google’s Find Hub on Android, which matters if you’ve got a mixed household of devices.

The wallet is powered by a built-in battery rather than a watch-style battery, and MOFT says a charge lasts about six months. You charge it by placing the wallet on a Qi charger for about an hour and a half, which, in practice, means dropping it on a MagSafe dock twice a year. That’s a better setup than an AirTag in a wallet as well.

The wallet holds up to eight cards, or closer to four to six if you also use the hidden compartment for cash. A pull tab in the wallet brings your everyday cards out in one motion, while your driver’s license and cash stay tucked inside of it. It’s made from MOFT’s vegan leather and at 14 mm thick, it’s slimmer than a traditional wallet but noticeably thicker than Apple’s card-only wallet, which is the price you are going to pay to hold up this much stuff.

According to recent reports, the stand is the usual MOFT origami fold-out, and it works in portrait and landscape for FaceTime or watching a video on the plane. You can buy it without the stand if you’d rather have the thinner version. It comes in Jet Black, Misty Cove, Terracotta, and Peony.

In a recent development, two things to know going in. There’s no RFID blocking in this version (MOFT has a non-Find My Snap Field Wallet if you want that). MOFT also warns that the magnets can affect magnetic-stripe cards, so avoid putting a hotel key in it.

I’ve spent the past month on a Pelican accessory beat, using wallets designed for outdoor use. The MOFT Trackable Field Wallet is the other end of that accessory spectrum: more cards, a stand, and Find My built in. If Find My is the reason you’ve stuck with Apple’s wallet, this is a really nice option that holds a bunch and is trackable.

The MOFT Trackable Field Wallet is available on Amazon and directly from MOFT.
