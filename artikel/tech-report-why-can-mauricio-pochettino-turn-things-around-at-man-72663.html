Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of his first United game against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, with plenty of questions surrounding the players who will benefit from his arrival.

Manchester United have struggled badly this season, sitting 18th in the Premier League after collecting just eight points from their opening 10 matches.

Pochettino replaces Michael Carrick and his job has been described as one of the toughest jobs in the Premier League. Former defender Wes Brown believes Manchester United have a chance with Pochettino to change things despite it being very difficult.

Brown believes the upcoming manager has enough quality at his disposal to improve the situation, despite the difficult start and the pressure that comes with the Manchester United job.

Asked whether Pochettino faces an impossible task after previous managers failed to bring success to Old Trafford, Brown was confident the Argentine could make a difference.

According to recent reports, “Definitely not impossible. Regardless of what has happened so far this season, Manchester United do have good players,” Wes Brown noted while talking to Flashscore.

Brown expects Pochettino to demand more effort from the squad.

“He looks like a personable manager, so can work with these players individually to get their confidence up and really play for the shirt and create that positive vibe that seems to be missing.”.

Asked about how long Manchester United will take to be a title challenger again, Wes Brown was cautious in putting any numbers.

Recent findings suggest that “Well, it isn’t going to happen overnight. You will need a reaction rapidly, that is for sure. They are very far away, but you have to start with consistency and that is something Pochettino has to install and deliver a consistency of play, to be fitter than your opponent and show fight.”.

The former defender also highlighted Bruno Fernandes’ drop in form, suggesting the captain could benefit from a change in management.

“You have to see Bruno’s form has dropped, so it will be great to see this pick up under the new manager.”.

Pochettino’s priority will be getting a response on Sunday against Liverpool from his senior players. United have been in this cycle of repeating managers and not finding much success. Mauricio Pochettino needs to take things one at a time. Sunday would be that first step.
