UPDATED after market close: Anthropic shares ended their first day of trading at $97.17, up 17% from their $83 IPO price, but off their high for the day, in a volatile session.

Early investors in the company behind the Claude chatbot cleaned up with the WSJ reporting that the majority of the AI lab’s more than 5,000 employees are expected to become millionaires, and the seven founders multi-billionaires.

According to recent reports, overall, markets fell. The Nasdaq where Anthropic now trades dropped 2.25% as tech stocks wobbled. The Dow Jones Industrial Average shed 886 points. According to recent reports, PREVIOUSLY: Anthropic shares jumped over 30% in the first minutes of trade at their Nasdaq debut before backtracking as the AI pioneer splashed down with the largest ever initial public offering in corporate history. Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that the first publicly traded, pure play AI organization priced its hotly anticipated IPO last night at $83 a share, well above the $70 to $78 range initially anticipated, and valuing the company at $1.5 trillion. It opened just before noon Thursday up 30% at about $111, pushing its market value to $2 trillion, before ascending to $115. The stock, listed under the ticker symbol ANT, then skidded to $95 rose, and dipped again. Shares are at the moment changing hands at about $95, still above where the IPO priced, but also well off their highs.

The listing is seen as a litmus test of investor sentiment on the AI sector, which has taken its lumps lately amid heavy spending, a string of outages across sectors, growing public pushback on data centers, safety and jobs, and the ouster of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang from the firm he founded.

In a recent development, the IPO raised more than $107 billion, the largest capital raise at an initial public offering on record, ahead of Elon Musk SpaceX’s $86 billion in June. The base deal covered 825 million Class A shares, some 800 million from the seven co-founders, with a $26 billion private placement layered on top.

Anthropic CEO and co-founder Dario Amodei with top executives and investors rang the opening bell at Nasdaq headquarters in Times Square Thursday with the image beamed onto a jumbotron on the building’s façade. Below, a crowd of onlookers, media and protestors gathered. Signage at the modest #StopTechOligarchs demonstration remarked the IPO endangered workers pension funds and the global economy and noted the company’s own language citing potentially catastrophic risk of AI.

In a note last night, Dan Ives, partner and senior managing director at Yorkville Ives & Co., called the $83 a share pricing “a clean read on demand: investors wanted exposure to a frontier lab at scale and the pricing reflects it, following SpaceX five months ago as the second core tech stalwart of this cycle to reach the public markets, and we expect OpenAI to follow in 2027.”.

The sector “is going through a very turbulent, white-knuckle period,” he acknowledged. But “that does not change our longer-term view that $3 trillion to $4 trillion of capex will be spent over the next few years on the AI Revolution, and this sets the stage for the next phase of tech and infrastructure investment with Anthropic a central player in the buildout. phase of tech and infrastructure investment with Anthropic a central player in the buildout.”.
