Whether you're hosting your first Thanksgiving dinner this year or are a seasoned pro, now's the time to pick up any last minute touches that will take your space over the top.

Sources indicate that E! spoke exclusively with HGTV star, interior designer, and Marshall's and TJ Maxx home style expert Taniya Nayak about putting together a space that feels inviting, cozy and elevated, just in time for Thanksgiving.

While having things coordinated can be great, you can still build a cozy and cohesive space without everything being overly matchy-matchy. "One of the biggest misconceptions in design is that everything must match perfectly. Some of the most beautiful homes are the ones that feel like they've evolved over time rather than being purchased all at once," she shared.

Reminder, "it is less about perfection and more about creating a home that tells a story," she added.
