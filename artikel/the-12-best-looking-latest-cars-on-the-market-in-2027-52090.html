The average car has about 30,000 pieces, if you count individual nuts and bolts. It’s difficult enough to put all of that together, but it’s even more difficult to make it look good. While there are plenty of decent-looking new cars on the market, the ones that go above and beyond in the design department truly shimmer. You’ll notice that our preferences trend towards sports cars, but we’ve also included plenty of sedans, a single SUV, and a wide range of price points. Among the plethora of vehicles that have cycled through our office, here are our picks for the best-looking cars on the market today.

Recent findings suggest that nothing turns a head quite like a Ferrari, and we think the mid-engined 296 is the stunner of the current lineup. It looks fantastic as both a coupe and as a convertible. The active rear spoiler manages to maintain the sports car’s sleek profile whether it’s engaged or not, and the low nose with a thin, sweeping grille is classic Ferrari. The 296 drives as good as it looks, too, thanks to its hybrid powertrain that uses a twin-turbo V-6 engine.

Recent findings suggest that the Miata has been an enthusiast staple since it entered the market wide-eyed and grinning in 1990. Since then, it’s grown up a bit, replacing pop-up headlights with angular, cat-like eyes and turning the grin into a bit more of a snarl. It’s a head-turner in any generation, but the newest ND is the sportiest model yet, and it’s best enjoyed with the top down. We also love the look of the Retractable Fastback (RF) body style pictured here. For an incredibly fun and affordable sports car, the Miata is truly always the answer.

More About the Mazda MX-5 Miata.

Sources indicate that while the Genesis brand isn’t quite as well-established as Mercedes or BMW, its flagship G90 looks every bit the part of a luxury sedan. Thin, sweeping taillights punctuate the nicely shaped rear end, while the classic diamond-shaped grille integrates beautifully with the slim, low-profile headlights. The G90 looks, sounds, and feels silky, and its sub-$100,000 starting price tag is surprising given how special it is.

In a recent development, like the G90, the GV70’s sophisticated design language helps it punch above its weight against the Porsches and Mercedes-Benzes in its segment. The GV70’s sporty body lines and interior details, including a knurled glass shift knob, make it feel like a true luxury vehicle. Genesis added a Prestige Graphite trim to the lineup for 2027, which makes the GV70 look even sportier with carbon-fiber pieces, darkened trim, and red brake calipers.

It has been revealed that when Maserati brought back the GranTurismo nameplate in 2024, the sports car got a subtle redesign that brought out all of the best qualities found in the previous generation. Since then, it’s only gotten better thanks to a styling refresh for 2027. With a cheerfully aggressive front end and a sloped, sleek rear that tapers nicely, the GranTurismo coupe exudes high-performance fun in a stylish two-door package.

More About the Maserati GranTurismo.

Recent findings suggest that the Lotus Emira looks great from any angle, and its gorgeously proportioned styling rivals much more expensive exotics. The Emira is a true stripped-back sports car, with just the necessities inside the cabin and a heavy focus on performance and agility. It’s unmistakably a mid-engined car, as the rear fenders flare out to make room for the hefty air intakes behind the doors, and the sleek roofline melts seamlessly into an angular rear end for a spunky-yet-elegant silhouette.

In a recent development, lucid recently took pole position in our top-ranked car brands list, and both the Air and the Gravity SUV have won our 10Best award in the past. Beyond its stunning performance, the Lucid Air’s styling promotes a sense of practical luxury that looks upscale without shouting about it. Modern-looking light signatures at the front and rear lean into minimalistic design trends, and the sedan’s interior looks just as stealthy as its exterior.

The muscular Vantage is full of British charm as either a coupe or as a roadster. Its long-hood, short-deck proportions perfectly complement the twin-turbo V-8 under the hood, and the cabin looks tastefully upscale. The detailing is exquisite, too. There’s a reason James Bond’s car of choice is so often an Aston Martin.

More About the Aston Martin Vantage.

The current Nissan Z brings the gorgeous 240Z of the past into the modern day while preserving much of its original characteristics. There’s also a hint of the 300ZX in its taillight shape. For 2027, the Z got a slight makeover, which makes the front grille a bit slimmer than its boxy predecessor and refines a few body lines at the rear. The Z perfectly toes the line between nostalgia and future-focused styling.

Recent findings suggest that the two-door CLE-class takes everything great about the C-class and E-class sedans and packages those characteristics into a far more sophisticated-looking coupe. From the side, the CLE looks long and slender, and the nicely shaped headlights and taillights further cement the car’s sleek proportions. The long hood gives off a hint of AMG GT proportions for about $50K less, and if you seek a more aggressive appearance, the AMG CLE53 version amps up the look with a wide-body stance and other tweaks.

More About the Mercedes-Benz CLE-Class.

Though it’s been around for a long time now, the Giulia remains an enjoyable sports sedan, and its exterior styling flaunts its unique Italian flair in a class dominated by German brands. Alfa’s classic three-part grille dominates the front end without being gaudy, and the signature teledial wheels are a focal point along the sides. Giulias are a bit of a rarity in the United States, so they’re instant eye-catchers on American roads.

More About the Alfa Romeo Giulia.

The Lexus LC was discontinued after the 2026 model year, but it looks so good that we included it anyway. As both a coupe and a convertible, the LC rocks a striking yet refined look that’s worth every penny of its six-figure price tag. The LC looks like nothing else on the road, and its wedge shape with many sharp angles still feels like a concept car for the street. Lexus offers plenty of unique interior color schemes, too, which are just as vital as the exterior when you want to put the top down and show off a bit.
