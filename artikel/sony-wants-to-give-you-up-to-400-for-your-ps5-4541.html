Sony Wants to Give You Up to $400 for Your PS5

🌐 Source: Lifehacker • 📅 05 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (596 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

  • Sources indicate that such is the case with Sony and its PlayStation console.
  • Recent findings suggest that it's a bizarre deal, especially when you consider who you're trading your console to.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, companies usually want you to buy something from them. They might cut prices to make their products more enticing, or introduce perks and upgrades to make stagnant prices (or even price hikes) more appealing. But rarely do I see companies encourage customers to sell their products.

Sources indicate that such is the case with Sony and its PlayStation console. This week, the tech giant began reaching out to some customers with an odd prompt: Rather than buy a new PS5 or PS5 Pro, you can sell your existing PS5 console for up to $400. There are stipulations there, of course: Your console needs to be in good working order, and, apparently, come with two controllers—even though Sony only includes one in the box. The price could also fluctuate depending on your console model, storage capacity, and included accessories. But, still, the company says it'll help you net up to $400 for your existing PS5.

Recent findings suggest that it's a bizarre deal, especially when you consider who you're trading your console to. Sony isn't necessarily accepting trade-ins itself. Instead, it's partnering with BackMarket. The thing is, you can trade in your PS5 directly through BackMarket and get a better value than Sony is offering through its direct link. When I try to trade in my standard PS5 with one controller, BackMarket offers $435, $35 more than Sony's highest offer. If I add a second controller to that deal, BackMarket bumps up the total to $455.

Sources indicate that you might also be wondering why Sony would do this. Not only is it weird for a firm to encourage you to sell one of its products, but the PS5 is about to have a major moment. Grand Theft Auto VI is finally about to launch, and it'll likely be one of the biggest video game launches, if not the biggest video game launch, of all time. Why, then, would Sony encourage users to jump ship?

In a recent development, while Sony doesn't say, the answer likely has to do with how expensive it is to pick up a PS5 right now. Even though the console rolled out back in 2020, units are few and far between, jacking the price up to unreasonable levels. Sony raised international prices due to tariffs last year, but the current shortage is more probably tied to the ongoing memory crisis that's putting a strain on all tech, including game consoles. A new PS5 on Amazon will easily cost you over $800. If you want to find a PS5 Pro, don't be surprised to find it well over $1,000. Sony likely wants players who aren't using their consoles to sell them, so that active players (who will also buy games like GTA VI) will pick them up. It's not just Sony, either: Microsoft has been offering similar deals to inactive Xbox gamers this year.

If Sony reached out to you directly, you can take advantage of the deal without glitch—just click the link in the email. But if you never received such an offer, you still have options. For one, that BackMarket deal is live for anyone. That links takes you to the deal for the standard PS5, but you can also head to this link to enter the info for your specific PS5 model. GameStop, of course, will happily take your PS5 off your hands, but it doesn't seem as generous as BackMarket. Right now, it seems you can get up to $275 in store credit, or $192.50 in cash for a standard PS5.
