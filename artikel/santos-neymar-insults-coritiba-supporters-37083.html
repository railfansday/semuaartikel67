In a recent development, neymar has once again been talked about for his behavior outside the game. Wednesday evening, after Santos’ victory on the Coritiba pitch (1-0), during the 35th day of the Brazilian championship, the 34-year-old striker lost his temper against several opposing supporters who were provoking him from the stands of the Couto Pereira stadium. Visibly annoyed, the former Paris Saint-Germain and FC Barcelona player responded with a series of insults, going so far as to predict the relegation of Coritiba to the second division. “ Stop messing around! You’re going to Serie B, you idiot. Too bad for you! You must respect me, you broken arms! “, he debuted towards the supporters. An altercation occurred after the final whistle, when Santos had just scored a valuable away success.

In a recent development, back in the starting lineup after missing two matches due to suspension, Neymar was nevertheless particularly active during the match. The Santos number 10 increased individual initiatives, notably attempting several small bridges over his opponents and creating three scoring opportunities. Two of his attempts ended off target, while the third was saved by goalkeeper Pedro Rangel. Also involved in the action that led to Gabriel Barbosa’s goal, the Brazilian had some tense exchanges with opposing midfielder Thiago Santos. Neymar returned to competition after being sanctioned for an accumulation of yellow cards and an expulsion during the match against Bahia on October 24. His return was therefore accompanied by a victory for Santos, but also by a fresh episode of tension with the opposing supporters.
