Endrick’s entourage are expected to meet Real Madrid soon, Fabrizio Romano confirms, with AC Milan awaiting developments.

In a recent development, it seems as though Endrick is Milan’s number one target in view of the winter window. It will take a big fee to prise him away from Real Madrid on a permanent basis, but Gerry Cardinale’s recent words suggest that the Brazilian is the chosen one, and there has been talk of a €70m bid.

It has been revealed that now, it remains to be seen what the margins are for a potential agreement. Los Blancos may seek a buy-back clause, while the will of the head coach Jose Mourinho and of course the player himself must be taken into consideration, given Endrick has spoken of his love for Real.

There is a upcoming patch from Fabrizio Romano on his YouTube channel regarding the future of Endrick, and it seems as though a crucial few days are coming up regarding the former Palmeiras starlet.

“This week, some of the most important figures from the agency that represents Endrick, Roc Nation, are expected in Madrid to speak with Endrick and obviously the issue of his situation will have to be addressed,” he said (via MilanNews).

“I have already told you that José Mourinho continues to insist, not only publicly but also privately, to keep him. AC Milan are there, Cardinale has been working on it for some time, and Roma is also interested in Endrick. This operation, for the moment, remains tied to Real Madrid.

According to recent reports, “However, the revision I am giving you today is precisely this visit by the agents to Endrick. We need to speak with the boy and his family. His father Douglas is also very involved in the decisions, as is Endrick’s wife, it is also a family matter, so we will have to understand the direction to take.

According to recent reports, “I repeat that Real Madrid, and especially José Mourinho, intend to keep Endrick until the end of the season, then we will have to see if he will change. something between now and the end of December.

“Milan and Roma should be kept there because their interest is truly concrete and strong, but then everything hinges on Real Madrid. And speaking of visions and strategies, we’ll soon understand if other teams might enter the fray.

“Let me give you an example: in the final days of the summer transfer window, Liverpool, before signing Barcola, had considered adding Endrick to their squad and had even spoken to Endrick’s agents, but Real Madrid wouldn’t hear of it.

“At the moment, the Reds are completely complete in attack, so I don’t think they’ll be among the competition for Endrick in January, but we’ll see what kind of situation unfolds.”.
