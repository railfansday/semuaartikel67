Here is Andrew Crisp’s Heath v Heath match report….

On paper this midweek trip to Southern Combination League leaders Haywards Heath Town looked a potential banana skin for Chris Simmons’ Broadbridge Heath.

The hosts may be a division below the Bears but they have made a great start to the season, five points clear at the top of the Premier Division, unbeaten at home after ten matches and having knocked cup favourites Brighton & Hove Albion out by 2-0 in an earlier round, but things don’t always work out as some may predict.

Simmons made five changes to the starting line-up that won 2-0 at Margate three days earlier, four at the back including Bailey Cotton on his way back from injury and making his first start since September and Ollie Rawlins making just his second start of the season after injury.

Broadbridge Heath got off to a great start on 3 minutes when Ben Holden received the ball on the halfway line and drove forward into the Haywards Heath half before playing the ball out wide to Ben Royall advancing down the left, and the Bears full-back cut inside and struck a low right foot shot from distance into the bottom right corner of the net.

The Bears doubled their lead on 24 minutes with Holden and Royal involved again: this time Holden, in the centre of the park, played the ball into the feet of Royall with his back to goal and closely marked, the Bears defender turned his marker and ran towards the Haywards Heath penalty area before striking a low left foot shot across the goal into the far corner.

At the other end Jack Barnes powered a header from a corner onto the top of the crossbar and Bears skipper Brad Peters reacted quickly to clear the ball off the goal-line but it was Broadbridge Heath who increased their lead on 32 minutes.

An interchange of passes ended with Luke Bejashvili playing the ball into the feet of Holden who played a first time pass to Ollie Rawlins some 25 yards out and with his back to goal and the Heath striker turned sharply to power a right foot shot into the bottom right corner giving the hosts’ keeper Charlie Roberts no chance of stopping.

Minutes later Broadbridge Heath’s Sal Marino played a defence-splitting ball to send Charlie Connell through on goal but Roberts was off his line promptly to force the player wide of goal and instead Connell played the ball back into the path of Rawlins on the edge of the “D” who blasted his shot high and wide.

A minute before the break the hosts pulled a goal back when Josh Wright crossed the ball from the right flank into the Broadbridge Heath penalty area where the ball bobbled around the six yard box before falling kindly to Charlie Meehan to tap in from close range.

Broadbridge Heath restored their three goal advantage almost immediately when a long ball was played down the left channel and chased down by Rawlins who delivered a left foot cross into the path of Zac Young, who hit a sweet left foot shot from 12 yards which was saved brilliantly by Roberts. Young pounced on the loose ball and was brought down by a clumsy challenge, and the match referee immediately pointed to the penalty spot with nobody from Haywards Heath questioning the decision and Young got up to convert the spot kick for his sixth goal of the season to make it 1-4 at the break.

According to recent reports, haywards Heath needed an early second half goal to get back into the game but the reverse happened 2 minutes into the restart when Broadbridge Heath goalkeeper Stan Williamson intercepted a right wing cross and threw the ball out quickly to Young in the centre of the park. He drove forward to the halfway line before playing the ball wide right to Charlie Connell, who took the ball towards the goal-line and then delivered a right wing pass across the goal to Rawlins to score from six yards.

Heath added a sixth goal on the hour mark when Rawlins received the ball wide on the right and played a long ball towards the far post where it was headed in by Connell from 8 yards to near enough secure the win for the Bears.

Simmons was able to swap his players around which included the welcome return to the side for Mason Doughty, the Heath striker with eight goals from 13 games this season has missed the last eight games through injury.

Haywards Heath added a second in time added-on when a combination of Wright and Barnes won the ball off a Bears defender and linked up well down the right channel before the ball was played inside to Barnes who finished with aplomb.

BBH: Williamson, Cotton (Thiselton 60), Royall, Marino, Peters, Salaam (Richardson 65), Bejashvili, Holden, Rawlins (Doughty 70), Connell (Hards 61), Young.
