Liverpool will be welcoming back a transformed left-back this week.

It's thanks to Andy Robertson that over the last decade the Reds haven't really had a lot of left-backs.

The Scotsman made the role his own from pretty much the first moment he arrived at the club in 2017.

Before him, Alberto Moreno and Jose Enrique had also featured in the role but with less success.

Robertson became not only one of the best left-backs to ever play for the club but probably one of the best left-backs on the planet in his prime. Subsequently, he's also become one of the best in general to ever play for Liverpool.

He is just a phenomenal talent and perhaps Liverpool are ruing letting him go so soon considering how well he is doing at Tottenham right now.

The reason why the Reds were open to allowing Robertson to leave and did not extend his contract is because Milos Kerkez.

When Liverpool signed the Hungarian international, they were getting the best player in his position in the Premier League. There was a strong consensus among the club's recruitment staff that he was the best young left-back on the market.

Kerkez has since struggled to rekindle the same form he had been producing at Bournemouth and AZ Alkmaar in a Liverpool shirt.

Industry observers note that there have been debates about whether Liverpool are even using him properly. But there is no denying that his own form has also been disappointing.

The good thing is that Kerkez is young, he has time on his side, and on top of that, he has a manager in Iraola who knows how to get the best out of him.
