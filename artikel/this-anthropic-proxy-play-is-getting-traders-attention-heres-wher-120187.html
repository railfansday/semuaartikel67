Recent findings suggest that who states you can't tailgate an IPO?Traders are preparing for this week's blockbuster Anthropic IPO in different ways, including buying options on one publicly-traded fund that invests in private assets.The Destiny Tech100 (DXYZ) is a publicly-traded, closed-end fund that invests in equity, equity-linked instruments, or shares of private funds and venture companies to gain exposure to private companies. At the moment, 14% of the fund's assets are tied to Anthropic, according to the manufacturer's website.Options volume on the fund jumped to more than 10x the 30-day average Monday, according to Cboe LiveVol data. Of the 42,300 contracts traded by midday, 41,800 were calls, with just 141 puts likely opened by buyers.

Just shy of $5.5 million in options premium on DXYZ traded on Monday, with almost all of it tied to calls, but just marginally more spent buying than selling calls, according to SpotGamma. The highest-volume contract is at the moment the 30-strike call expiring Friday, a 90-cent contract that needs the fund to rally 19% into the weekend to pay off.Traders may want to be wary of how the fund traded around the SpaceX IPO: DXYZ surged almost 150% in May before unwinding all the gains in the weeks ahead of SpaceX's debut. Since then, the fund has barely moved, trading around $26.There are other bullish signs surrounding Anthropic's IPO in the derivatives markets. Current prices for perpetual futures on the blockchain exchange Hyperliquid are implying an opening price of $98, a big pop from the $70-$78 price range quoted in the company's amended S1 filing. The AI giant is expected to price Wednesday evening and begin trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT.
