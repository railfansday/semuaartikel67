From Xbox Live to Xbox Game Pass, Microsoft paywalled multiplayer on its platforms, but the organization reportedly aimed to break down that barrier for its next-gen console, Project Helix. According to Windows Central, Project Helix was conceived under the previous Xbox leadership team as the device that would bridge the divide between PC and console gaming. If Microsoft ditches paid multiplayer, it loses a revenue stream for its next-gen push at a time when component prices have risen steeply.
