People are increasingly using AI for travel planning, but here’s an interesting twist on that. Can AI help you find award flights and secure upgrades? It’s something I’d like to study in a bit more detail and write about in the coming weeks, but I just saw a post on Instagram that piqued my interest, so I figured it would make for a fun story. Consider this just a little teaser, for the time being.

The idea is simply that ChatGPT helped someone secure an upgrade through the standard means — it’s not like ChatGPT used his/her/their charm to sweet talk someone into an upgrade by dressing up nicely. 😉.

I don’t want to suggest AI isn’t useful, but I’d say that the example given is actually very poor. First of all, let’s be clear about what’s actually going on here. He had booked a business class ticket on American from Los Angeles (LAX) to London (LHR), and then upgraded to first class.

Now, in the first place, something isn’t fully accurate here:.

Okay, maybe he edited the dates, or something, which is fine, and it’s besides the point, but as a detail oriented airline nerd, I couldn’t help but notice those things. Anyway, let’s assume this is all accurate:.

To me, this is kind of where AI falls flat, and it isn’t the correct way to use it. The prompt was reportedly to “use my miles if the math is worth it, and place the upgrade bid at the smartest number, or get me on the upgrade list right now.” This is where I get confused — you’d think that AI would be able to scour reviews online, and see that American first class isn’t (well, or wasn’t) really viewed as a product that’s worth the premium.

If AI were really providing useful advice here, it would’ve recommend waitlisting with the systemwide upgrade, and otherwise recommending the guy save his 70,000 miles, and use them for something else. While there are definitely some strong use cases for AI, I think this is actually a pretty poor showing of what it can do.

Sources indicate that by the way, keep in mind that under United’s updated contract of carriage, using AI to help service reservations is banned, and could potentially get your ticket canceled. Whether there’s any enforcement of that remains to be seen.

While the thought of using AI to score an upgrade might sound great, don’t count on it actually getting you the best deal. An AI whiz claims he has the secret to using AI to upgrade to first class. All AI did was use the instant upgrade offer that was available directly on American’s website, and it didn’t even encourage him to skip the upgrade due to it not being worthwhile, or waitlisting the upgrade with a systemwide upgrade, which is objectively a much better deal than 70,000 miles.
