In a recent development, technology has become so thoroughly integrated into our lives that it’s easy to overlook the design behind it. But look beyond the specifications, and you’ll find plenty of objects worth paying attention to: a clock that uses light and sound to mark the passing hours, a digital frame that resembles a printed photograph, and a cassette-playing boombox that brings back the pleasure of physical controls.

It has been revealed that for this year’s tech gift guide, we’ve selected 18 products that put as much thought into how they look and feel as what they do. Some embrace the past, others experiment with new technology, and a few make the everyday business of charging, organizing, and staying connected a little more enjoyable.

Photography courtesy of MoMA Design Store.

The boombox is back, complete with oversized speakers, analog controls, and illuminated VU meters. Studebaker’s retro-inspired design brings the 1980s into the present with Bluetooth connectivity, a CD player, USB playback, and a cassette deck that can even record music. A rechargeable battery and built-in carrying handle make it easy to take the nostalgia wherever the party goes.

The SB214OS Bluetooth Boombox is available at store.moma.org for $250.

Photography courtesy of Polaroid.

Polaroid’s Mod puts creative controls directly on the camera, inviting experimentation without the distraction of a smartphone screen. Dedicated physical controls make it easy to explore effects like double exposures, light painting, motion freeze, and split-shot compositions. It’s a refreshingly hands-on approach to photography that encourages a little spontaneity and plenty of happy accidents.

The Polaroid Mod Instant Camera is available at polaroid.com for $169.99.

Photography courtesy of Therabody.

Recent findings suggest that therabody takes a different approach to workout recovery with a device designed to fit between your hands. To help manage body heat during workouts, the handheld device uses temperature-controlled surfaces to cool both palms simultaneously, with additional heat and contrast therapy settings. Compact and cordless, it provides an alternative to ice baths and other more cumbersome temperature-based recovery equipment.

The CryoTherm Palm is available at therabody.com for $399.99.

Photography courtesy of Balmuda.

Inspired by traditional pocket watches, Balmuda’s The Clock reimagines how we experience time through light, sound, and movement. Its carved aluminum body houses 75 LEDs that create an animated display, while built-in speakers play ambient soundscapes designed for focus, relaxation, and sleep. With customizable alarms, app connectivity, and USB-C charging, it’s a thoughtful alternative to the conventional bedside clock.

The Clock is available at us.balmuda.com for $499 in Silver and $519 in Champagne.

Photography courtesy of Bang & Olufsen.

It has been revealed that bang & Olufsen’s Beoplay H100 headphones are undeniably a splurge, but they’re designed with longevity in mind. The Danish brand developed the H100 with replaceable components, including the ear cushions, headband, and batteries. The custom titanium drivers, soft leather detailing, and rich color options give them a distinctly luxurious feel, while advanced noise cancellation and immersive sound make the listening experience just as considered as the design. It’s an investment piece for someone who appreciates exceptional craftsmanship as much as great audio.

The Beoplay H100 Headphones are available at bang-olufsen.com for $2,200.

Photography courtesy of Warby Parker.

Smart glasses are getting a stylish update thanks to Warby Parker’s collaboration with Google and Samsung. Available in three of the brand’s familiar silhouettes, the lightweight frames incorporate a camera, open-ear speakers, and Gemini AI for hands-free photography, calls, navigation, and more. Subtly translucent temples offer a glimpse of the technology inside without compromising the classic eyewear design. With prescription, nonprescription, reading, and sunglass options, they’re a surprisingly versatile gift.

Intelligent Eyewear is available for preorder at warbyparker.com, starting at $395.

Photography courtesy of Flowtica.

What looks like an ordinary ballpoint pen has a few technological tricks up its sleeve. The Flowtica Scribe combines a functional writing instrument with an AI-powered voice recorder, allowing users to capture conversations while taking notes on paper the old-fashioned way. A companion app can transcribe recordings and turn them into summaries and action items, bringing digital convenience to a familiar analog tool. An optional vegan leather charging case gives the device a place to rest between meetings.

The Flowtica Scribe is available at flowtica.ai for $159.

Photography courtesy of Bellroy.

Recent findings suggest that anyone who’s ever untangled a mess of charging cables at the bottom of a bag will appreciate Bellroy’s thoughtfully designed Tech Kit. The small organizer opens flat to reveal a series of mesh pockets and elastic loops that keep chargers, cables, earbuds, and other tech essentials neatly in place. Its structured shape makes everything easy to see and access, while the streamlined exterior, made from recycled woven fabric, slips neatly into a backpack or carry-on. It’s a simple solution to one of travel’s most persistent little annoyances.

The Tech Kit is available at bellroy.com for $59–$69, depending on color.

It has been revealed that amazon’s latest Kindle redesign brings a more refined look and feel to the familiar e-reader. The Paperwhite Signature Edition pairs a sleek aluminum body with a 7-inch, glare-free display, adjustable warm lighting, and double-tap page turning. Waterproof construction and wireless charging make it an easy travel companion, while two finishes, Graphite and Seaglass Green, offer a choice between classic and colorful.

The Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition is available at amazon.com for $250.

Photography courtesy of Daily Objects.

It has been revealed that who notes charging stations have to be boring? DailyObjects’ Surge Foldaway turns an everyday tech essential into a sculptural desktop accessory. Its adjustable design accommodates an iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods simultaneously, consolidating three charging spots into one tidy footprint. The bold Cosmic Orange finish adds a welcome pop of color, giving the accessory a presence even when the devices are elsewhere.

The Surge Foldaway 3-in-1 Wireless Charger is available at dailyobjects.us for $175.

Photography courtesy of ModRetro.

Recent findings suggest that A love letter to handheld gaming, ModRetro’s Chromatic brings the original Game Boy experience into the present with a few thoughtful upgrades. Its magnesium-alloy body houses a custom, pixel-perfect display designed to play original Game Boy and Game Boy Color cartridges. The colorful finishes include four translucent options that expose the internal hardware, turning the console’s components into part of its visual identity.

The Chromatic + Tetris Bundle is available at modretro.com starting at $199.

Shark’s CryoGlow Plus combines two skincare technologies in a single wearable device: LED light therapy and under-eye cooling. Red, blue, and infrared lights power several treatment modes, while adjustable cooling pads help refresh tired eyes. The upgraded design offers improved LED coverage around the eyes and mouth, and the soft Pastel Berry finish makes this wearable gadget surprisingly stylish.

The CryoGlow Plus LED Face Mask is available at sharkninja.com for $379.99.

In a recent development, in a market crowded with phones competing for attention, the Mudita Kompakt is designed to demand less of it. Its minimalist design centers on a 4.3-inch E Ink display, favoring a restrained interface over colorful notifications and app-heavy screens. Essential features like calling, messaging, offline maps, and a camera remain, while a dedicated offline switch offers a physical way to disconnect.

The Mudita Kompakt is available at mudita.com for $399.

Industry observers note that who says smart technology has to look high-tech? Birdfy’s Feeder Wood combines the charm of a traditional wooden birdhouse with a built-in camera, letting bird lovers watch their feathered visitors up close. Made from FSC-certified wood, the solar-powered feeder captures 1080p video. Its companion app can identify more than 6,000 bird species, bringing a layer of digital discovery to an otherwise familiar backyard ritual.

The Feeder Wood is available at birdfy.com for $189.99.

Wallie’s approach to the digital photo frame starts with a simple idea: make the screen look less like a screen. Its 10-inch color E Ink display gives photographs the appearance of printed images, while real wood construction and a choice of four finishes help it blend into a gallery wall or shelf arrangement. Rounded or square corners offer further customization, and a rechargeable battery eliminates the need for a visible power cord. Photos can be updated remotely through an app.

The Wallie 10 is available at shop.wallieframe.com for $299.

With its simple spherical form, Eve Flare looks more like an illuminated object than a conventional lamp. The rechargeable, water-resistant light offers millions of customizable colors, with app and voice controls for setting the mood indoors or outside. Its wireless charging base and portable design mean it can move effortlessly from a bedside table to the patio, adding a little atmosphere wherever it lands.

The Eve Flare Portable Smart LED Lamp is available at apple.com for $99.95.

Photography courtesy of Best Buy.

8BitDo borrows the visual language of the original Nintendo Entertainment System for a mechanical keyboard that feels straight out of the 1980s. Chunky gray keys, red accents, and tactile mechanical switches establish the retro aesthetic, while an included joystick and two oversized programmable buttons bring arcade-style controls to the desktop. Bluetooth and wireless connectivity keep the throwback design compatible with contemporary setups.

The 8BitDo Retro Mechanical Keyboard N Edition is available at bestbuy.com for $79.99.

Photography courtesy of Orbitkey.

Even the most organized desk tends to accumulate charging cables, adapters, and other small tech accessories. Orbitkey’s Nest v2 gives them a designated home in a compact storage box with adjustable dividers and a fabric-lined interior. The removable leather-covered lid doubles as a valet tray and 15W wireless charger, keeping everyday essentials within reach while concealing the clutter underneath.

The Nest v2 is available at orbitkey.com for $125.
