Matteo Moretto spoke in a video on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel and he revealed that Milan have sent a scout to watch a midfielder in action in the second division in Spain.

Industry observers note that “I want to talk about a young midfielder who plays for Oviedo. He’s a very promising young player. The information I have is that AC Milan have been to see him several times in recent weeks, or at least in the past few months. They even went to see him live recently,” he said (via MilanNews).

“Milan are one of the clubs closely monitoring the development of Enzo Perez, an 18-year-old midfielder for Oviedo. He has a release clause. He’s been playing consistently lately. I can say that there are other clubs besides AC Milan, including Espanyol, Betis and Brentford.

Sources indicate that perez has established himself in the Oviedo first team, having started the last five matches and played the full 90 minutes in the last three. He only missed the weekend’s league match against Girona because he is on duty with Spain’s U19 national team.

In a recent development, he has one goal and three assists to his name, all in the last five games. The club gave him his first professional contract last July, running until June 2029. His release clause was €5.5m until 9 November, but now it could take a lot more to prise him away.
