Fortnite Treasure Hours are now live, running from 2-4pm ET (7-9pm GMT), and today is your last chance to get the Golden Hatsune Miku skin.

During the event, everyone spawns with The Leekcapper and a Self-Revive Device, and on top of the usual boosts, Fortune and Cursed Sprites appear more often.

Drop rates across the board are increased for the full two hours of the event, much like past weeks:.

It's worth noting that Epic Games teased when it first disclosed the odds that "these rates may be higher in future," so with GTA 6 now in everyone's hands and over half of all active console players taking a vacation to Leonida, maybe we'll be treated to better drop rates this time around. Though nothing has been announced yet.

The second Treasure Hours session goes live at 9-11pm ET (2-4am GMT), though unfortunately, Epic Games has not responded to the growing frustration from European and Asian players, who are forced to stay up past midnight if they want better odds.

In a recent development, in fact, the official French social channels for Fortnite mused last week that there would be another session between 3-5am "for the bravest," dismissing these concerns outright. It should go without saying, but don't stay up until 5am opening chests. Even with 2x odds, it's incredibly unlikely you'll get Pirate Shop Access.

Sources indicate that for those in more reasonable time zones, you can look forward to increased odds in the second session, with a 10x boost to the Golden Cap'n Miku drop rate — who has become as much an obsession for her vocaloid fans as the Pirate Shop Access tokens this week. Though with a drop rate of 1 in 1.56 million, let's just say Golden Hatsune Miku is pretty rare, even with a 10x boost.

According to recent reports, we'll keep this article updated if Epic Games reveals a special GTA 6 incentive or any other details for today's Treasure Hours, but otherwise, if you want Golden Hatsune Miku, you'd best get opening chests. Just don't stay up too late trying your luck.
