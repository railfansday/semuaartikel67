📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recently, UGREEN introduced a newly introduced Qi2.2 25W 10000mAh MagSafe battery bank for iPhone. There have been plenty of these, but this one has an interesting new twist: liquid cooling. For Black Friday, it’s on sale for 40% off, making it more affordable than ever.

Industry observers note that on the back of the battery, there’s a window that showcases the liquid within the battery bank moving throughout it. This should help keep it a little bit cooler than most MagSafe battery banks.

Sources indicate that UGREEN indicates that it’ll be around 18 degrees cooler than usual, and while that’s probably true – it’s worth nothing that it still isn’t a cold battery bank, it’s just cooler than usual. It’ll still feel warm in your hand.

On the bottom of the battery bank, UGREEN integrates a USB-C cable for charging your phone or any other USB-C device that can’t (or you prefer not to) charge wirelessly. I found it a bit handy at times, since of course wireless charging is never as energy efficient as wired charging.

Recent findings suggest that I will admit, the battery bank is a little bit thick, and the lack of a kickstand is somewhat bothersome at this price point, especially when so many others offer one. That said, if heat is a concern of yours with MagSafe battery banks, this one will definitely solve that for you.

On the side, it offers a small display for showing battery percentage and charging speed.

Industry observers note that one last thing of note: if you’re buying an iPhone Duo, this will stick out from the bottom of the phone. This is true for most MagSafe accessories on iPhone Duo, though.

In a recent development, the UGREEN MagFlow battery bank comes in at $149, which is quite pricey for a MagSafe battery of any kind. For Black Friday, it’s on sale for just $90.23 on Amazon for Prime members, making it much more palatable if you’re looking for a MagSafe battery bank that doesn’t get as hot as the competition.
