It has been revealed that the controversy surrounding Jamal Musiala is far from falling on Bayern Munich. For several days, the Bavarian management’s comments on the future of the attacking midfielder and his imposing salary have sparked a wave of indignation in Germany. After Uli Hoeness and Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, it is now Maik Franz who has stepped up to the plate. Guest of the show Doppelpass on Sport1the former defender did not mince his words to denounce the attitude of the Munich board, which he considers particularly inappropriate towards a player facing health problems. At the heart of this controversy, the statements of Rummenigge, who publicly questioned Musiala’s salary conditions in view of his physical difficulties and his level of performance.

Industry observers note that an outing which provoked numerous reactions, notably from Maik Franz: “what the Bayern leaders are doing, and I’m not talking about Kompany and Eberl, who are behaving in an exemplary manner, but about MM. Hoeness, Dreesen and Rummenigge, is pathetic and even, in my eyes, constitutes harassment. We can discuss a player’s performance. But it is not appropriate to talk publicly about an illness and the salary associated with it, at this level. We must not forget that Jamal tried everything to come back. He indicated a lot of courage and transparency in discussing his diagnosis. For this to turn against him today is not acceptable. To be completely honest, we are no longer in “Mia san Mia”, but in “Mia san money”. Money takes precedence over people. And this must not happen.”.

The controversy even went beyond the borders of Bavarian management to affect the locker room. Michael Olise actually liked an Instagram post from Fabrizio Romano relaying the press release from FIFPRO, the international players’ union, which called on Bayern to respect Jamal Musiala. A particularly notable gesture from the French international, renowned for his discretion on social networks. This support takes on an additional dimension as Bayern wish to extend Olise’s contract, at the moment linked to the club until 2029. Maik Franz also underlined the risk that this affair could represent for decision-makers: “With Michael Olise, you may have the next best player in the world in your ranks. He too must extend and receive a significant salary. But you keep in mind that if you got sick or injured and didn’t perform, you would be publicly thrown out to waste. Frankly, I don’t think they did themselves any favors.”.

According to recent reports, FIFPRO also strongly criticized Bayern’s communication, saying contractual issues should be discussed privately with the player and his representatives. The union notably recalled that an injury or decline in form did not justify publicly calling into question salary conditions already agreed upon. While Musiala tries to regain his best level after a long period of absence, the Bavarian club finds itself under fire from critics. Between the declarations of its leaders, the exit of Maik Franz and the remarkable gesture of Olise, the file has become a real image glitch for Bayern Munich….
