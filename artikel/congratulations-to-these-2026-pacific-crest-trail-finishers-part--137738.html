Recent findings suggest that the Pacific Crest Trail is one of North America’s most beautiful and iconic thru-hikes, stretching 2,650 miles from the Mexican border all the way to Canada. Every year, thousands of hikers set out on this unforgettable journey, pushing their limits both physically and mentally as they navigate this incredibly diverse terrain.

Industry observers note that this year, wildfires devastated many thru-hikers’ intends. In July, the northern section of the PCT from Harts Pass to the Canadian border was closed due to the Ptarmigan Fire. Because of this, many finish photos look different than in years past.

Thru-hiking teaches lessons in perseverance and resilience, and many hikers were able to pivot from a traditional finish and found creative conclusions to their multi-month journey before the northern terminus officially reopened in mid-September. We’re thrilled to spotlight the incredible hikers who completed this journey in 2026, no matter the end point!

Have you just finished your PCT thru-hike? If so, please celebrate your finish with us by submitting a photo through the form below so we can feature you in a subsequent post. We can’t wait to share your incredible achievement!
