Environmental and animal welfare concerns, and taste preferences, are behind rising popularity of vegan alternatives.

According to recent reports, has milk gotta lotta bottle? It may not have as much as it used to, with official data showing Britons are buying less cow’s milk than at any point since records began.

Guardian analysis of the government’s family food survey shows the average person bought 1.8 pints (1,024ml) of cow’s milk a week in 2024-25 – the lowest level in the record’s 52-year history and less than half the amount of milk bought in 1974.
