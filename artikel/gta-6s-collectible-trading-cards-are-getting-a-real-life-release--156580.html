It's been less than a week since Grand Theft Auto 6 came out, and Rockstar Games has already found another way to get us to spend money on the game. If you've been collecting sports cards while exploring Leonida, you'll soon be able to do the same outside the game. Rockstar has partnered with Panini America to bring these fictional sports trading cards to life, and they'll be out in December 2026.

It has been revealed that this goes beyond your usual video game merchandise featuring the main characters. The cards are based on collectibles that actually exist within GTA 6's world, so you might recognize some of the athletes and teams when you open a pack. And if you're hoping to get your hands on some of the rarer cards, there are even a few that are limited to a single copy each.

It has been revealed that panini America confirmed its partnership with Rockstar Games on November 24. The officially licensed set is called the GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection, and it'll be the first trading card collaboration between the two companies.

According to recent reports, if you've ever collected football or basketball cards, chances are you're already familiar with Panini. They're also known for reprinting comics in Europe, and I still have a copy of the X-Men: The Last Stand Annual 2007 published by Panini Books.

In a recent development, what's different this time is that instead of collecting cards featuring actual sports stars, you'll be collecting ones based on athletes from Rockstar's fictional universe. The promotional artwork shows a mixture of illustrated cards and ones featuring character renders, much like those found in the game itself.

The cards will come in boxes containing four packs each. Panini states a box will typically include two Rookie cards, a Retro card, an Illustrated card and four Special Silvers. These are average figures rather than guaranteed contents for every box.

There'll also be different colored variants of the cards, including silver, pink, teal and light blue, along with team-colored parallels. The rarest variants have much smaller print runs. The Legend Bronze cards are numbered out of 49, the numbered Silver variants out of 25, and the Gold variants are one-of-a-kind cards.

Even if you don't care about the numbered cards, there's some appeal in having the regular ones. GTA's fictional athletes aren't exactly household names like the ones you'd find in Panini's other sports collections, but you can actually go looking for their cards in Leonida while playing the game.

Rockstar has been selling GTA merchandise for years, but this particular collaboration reminds me of the sort of extras that used to come with physical copies of video games. The maps that came with older GTA titles, for instance, were something you could actually use while playing and keep long after you were done with the game.

Ironically, GTA 6's boxed edition doesn't even come with a game disc. So it's nice to see Rockstar making something physical that draws directly from the game rather than simply putting its logo on another piece of merchandise.

Rockstar could've easily gone with a conventional trading card set featuring Jason, Lucia, and other major characters. Instead, it's making replicas of things you might stumble across while taking a break from the story. I'd love to see Rockstar do something similar with other fictional brands from the series, like Sprunk or eCola.

Unfortunately, Panini hasn't announced an exact release date, pricing, or which countries the cards will be available in.

Depending on when the cards go on sale in December, they could make for a pretty good Christmas gift for a GTA fan.
