According to recent reports, jump starters, tire inflators, dash cams, and roadside emergency kits are the things that will keep them safe and prepared while traveling. Look for things that appeal to their passions. Some prefer customizing or detailing their own vehicle, while others might desire books, Lego sets, or clothing that celebrates their favorite cars. Basic mechanical essentials can also be a good starting point for young wrenchers.

In a recent development, when looking at car-specific products like floor mats, seat covers, or sunshades, you can usually put in the vehicle information (make, model, year, and sometimes trim level) to ensure a proper fitment. Products that claim to be “universal” can work for other items that aren’t as vehicle-dependent like phone mounts or car trash cans.

In a recent development, things like Hot Wheels models or car air fresheners can be great value-oriented gifts. Most basic detailing products are also relatively inexpensive, and can provide a way for teens to bond with their vehicles. Smaller Lego sets are another great option, along with car enthusiast apparel like socks and shirts.
