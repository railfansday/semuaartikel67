Dell Ups Annual Sales Forecast Again on Rising AI Server Demand

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (125 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that dell Technologies boosted its fiscal year revenue sales outlook for the third time as the rapid build-out of AI data centers fuels surging server sales. Revenue in the fiscal year ending in January will be about $202 billion, including $78 billion in AI server sales, compared with a September forecast of $192 billion, the Texas-based tech giant remarked Tuesday in a statement. Analysts polled by Bloomberg had expected sales of $193.5 billion. Dell said its board has also boosted the company’s share buyback plan by $25 billion. Woo Jin Ho, Bloomberg Intelligence Senior Analyst for Hardware and Networking expects to see the impact of the midterm election on Dell's data centers in mid -2027. (Source: Bloomberg).
