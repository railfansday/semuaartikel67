The Nations League draw has been made on Friday for England and the other seven League A teams.

England’s stunning win away to Spain meant that they won their group.

That meant they would be drawn against one of the teams who had finished runners-up in their group.

The Friday draw seeing England draw Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals.

It will be a two-legged tie in late March 2027, with Norway hosting the first leg, then England the benefit of home advantage in the second and deciding leg.

Then if they get through that, England will face either Belgium or Germany in the Nations League semi-finals in June 2027.

Fair to say that England have got what appears to be the easier half of the draw, as the four nations that they could play in the final are Netherlands, Portugal, Spain or France. Spain v France obviously looking the pick of the quarter-final ties.

I have seen some in the media claiming a Nations League victory would end 61 years of hurt with a first major tournament win since 1966.

It would be great for England to win it but nobody is seeing this surely on any kind of similar level to the Euros and World Cup.

At best I think you could see the Nations League on a similar level as the Conference League at club level, with then Euros and Europa League also compared to each other, as well as World Cup and Champions League, when comparing national sides and club ones when they play in the various competitions.
