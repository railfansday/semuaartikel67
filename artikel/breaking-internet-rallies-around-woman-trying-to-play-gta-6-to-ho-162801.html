A woman says she wants to play Grand Theft Auto 6 to “honor” her late husband, but actually has the wrong console for it.

On November 19, Grand Theft Auto 6 finally introduced after 13 years of waiting, with players eager to jump into Jason and Lucia’s story, and see just how big the Leonida map actually is.

With such a long gap between the release of Grand Theft Auto 5 and GTA 6, plenty of fans have passed on, with friends and family paying tribute to them in the upcoming game.

In the case of Ashley Reese, who goes by OffBeatOrbitor on social media, she revealed that she wanted to get involved with the upcoming game to honor her late husband, Rob, but quickly ran into some hurdles.

In a recent development, reese noted that she had a PlayStation 4 and had been struggling to navigate the PlayStation Store. The PS4 doesn’t actually run GTA 6, meaning she’d have to shell out on a PS5 or PS5 pro to be able to play.

In a recent development, “Wait this is how i find out that it doesn’t work on a ps4 LOOOOOOOOOL  well, it was the thought that counts. Maybe later lol, i really do want to play it!” she posted on X (formerly Twitter).

Additionally, the writer took to Instagram to offer some further details about her situation, noting that her gaming experience is limited to early Tony Hawk games and slightly helping her husband play Red Dead Redemption 2.

“Am I truly so sentimental and so nostalgic that I will buy an entirely new games system that’s like over $500 in honor of my late husband?” Yeah, I am,” she added, noting Rob would have been “prepared for this” scenario.

“I haven’t bought it yet, I haven’t pulled the trigger on that purchase just yet, but I feel like I have to.”.

According to recent reports, other viewers have shared similar stories as a result of seeing Ashley’s video as well. “When I found out the newly introduced GTA came out, all I’ve been thinking about is how excited my late husband would’ve been. Am I gonna learn how to play this fucking game for him? Yes, yes I am,” one commented.

According to recent reports, “This is beautiful man. I know he’ll watching you game like a super exclusive twitch session,” another said.

Elsewhere, a woman revealed that her ex-boyfriend called the police on her as she finally locked him out of her Xbox account after he bought GTA 6.
