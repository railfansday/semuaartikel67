Xabi Alonso called on Chelsea’s fans to support Wesley Fofana after their away end spent their latest defeat chanting for their own player to get out of their club.

Industry observers note that fofana started against Nottingham Forest after apologising for the unauthorised interview he had conducted with a French YouTube channel. The content of that chat included him laughing off how he missed the penalty which condemned Chelsea to their Carabao Cup exit at Liverpool.

From the first minute at the City Ground, he was booed on the ball and subjected to abusive singing from Chelsea’s supporters, who chanted: ‘F*** off Fofana, you’re not wanted here.’.

Fofana went on to give away a penalty by fouling his former team-mate Liam Delap and was then replaced by Levi Colwill, who had been dropped from the line-up instead of the Frenchman.

‘We are going through a difficult moment of confidence, things are not clicking, not going the way we want,’ Alonso added. ‘We know in football, this can change quickly or can continue. It's down to us to work to find those solutions to have a better feeling on the pitch and to get better results because that's what we need.
