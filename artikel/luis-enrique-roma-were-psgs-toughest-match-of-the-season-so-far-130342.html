Luis Enrique insisted Paris Saint-Germain's 2-2 draw with Roma in the Champions League on Wednesday was their "hardest match of the season so far".

PSG dropped points for the first time in Europe this season, with the stalemate ending their run of 12 consecutive victories in the competition in the process.

Mario Hermoso's fifth-minute goal was cancelled out by Illia Zabarnyi's header in the first half at the Parc des Princes, with the hosts getting themselves in front just after the hour.

Achraf Hakimi was brought down by Wesley inside the penalty area with Ousmane Dembele scoring from 12 yards out, but Roma were awarded a spot-kick of their own late on.

Vitinha was penalised for a handball and Donyell Malen stepped up to seal a share of the spoils, though Roma may count themselves unlucky, given they finished with an expected goals (xG) total of 2.65 from their 13 shots to PSG's tally of 1.73 from their 15 attempts.

Indeed, PSG managed just four shots on target against Roma, their fewest in a Champions League group stage match since November 2024 against Bayern Munich (three).

PSG were, of course, without long-term absentees Desire Doue and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, though Luis Enrique acknowledged there were problems elsewhere in his team.

According to recent reports, "We knew before the match that it was going to be tough. Right from the first few minutes, they pressed us relentlessly, and it was very difficult," Luis Enrique mentioned.

Industry observers note that "We had problems right from the start of the game, and we could even have lost this one. They deserved a draw, perhaps even a win.

According to recent reports, "It was very tough; I think it's been the hardest match of the season so far. That's how tough this competition is. There are good games and tough games.

"We need to be aware of that, accept it and stay on our toes, because it's a very tough competition. We've still got three matches left in this group stage.

"We need to prepare and get the players back that are working their way back to full fitness.".

Roma are one of four teams to go 1-0 ahead in all five of their Champions League games this season, alongside Arsenal, Como and Galatasaray.

They have, however, failed to win four of those (D2 L2), the most games failed to win in a group campaign after scoring first since Atalanta in 2021-22 (also four).

It was a result that also saw PSG concede first, later lead, but failed to win a Champions League group stage game at home for the first time in the competition.

It has been revealed that there were positives, though. Only PSG themselves in 2017-18 (24) have scored more goals across their opening five games of a Champions League campaign than Luis Enrique's side in 2026-27 (22).

Industry observers note that "It was a difficult match. They were strong defensively, and it was a bit of a challenge for us, particularly in the penalty box," Luis Enrique added. "It was a very, very tough match.

"They'd analysed our team well and I think that made a difference. We tried to play our own game, to keep possession from start to finish, but we didn't manage it.

"We dropped two points, but we didn't lose, and that's very notable. I think it's good for the squad; we're staying strong, and now we're already focusing on the next match.".
