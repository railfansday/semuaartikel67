TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection Day (Espeon) and Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection Night (Umbreon) are available to buy for close to market price at Walmart.

We're doing our very best to track stock for the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections, but stock is scarce, and finding listings anywhere close to market price is exceptionally tough. And honestly, we can't see the situation changing anytime soon.

We're still holding onto the idea that Amazon will drop some reasonably priced stock this week, but given that the $59.99 listing for the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box has now disappeared, we're getting less and less confident.

In a recent development, so where should you shop right now? The answer is Walmart, with consistently live listings for fairly close to market price. TCGplayer's market price for the Day Box is currently $306, compared to Walmart's $349.90. The market price for the Night Box is $344, compared with Walmart's $399.89. Yes, you're paying over market price, but that's as good as you're getting from a leading retailer this close to Black Friday.

According to recent reports, the Ultra-Premium Collection comes with 29 30th Celebration booster packs, one Classic Collection booster pack, custom metal coins, deck boxes, card sleeves, and damage-counter dice. The Day collection includes the exclusive Espeon ex promo card with a Daytime Pikachu ex promo card and an Espeon playmat. The Night box showcases the exclusive Umbreon ex promo card with another Pikachu ex promo card and an Umbreon playmat.
