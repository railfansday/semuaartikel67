Manchester United will have Leny Yoro available for selection against Brentford this weekend, though a fellow defender remains at risk of a Premier League suspension.

Yoro was dismissed after just eight minutes in Man United’s defeat to Liverpool last Sunday, as Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge after succeeding Michael Carrick as head coach swiftly turned into a nightmare.

The Frenchman was given his marching orders for bringing down Alexander Isak just outside the box, with the Premier League revealing the red card was shown to the United defender “for denial of an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO)”.

However, Man Utd appealed the decision and it has proven successful with Yoro cleared to face Brentford on Saturday.

A statement from the FA read: “Leny Yoro’s one-match suspension has been removed following a claim of wrongful dismissal.

“The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goal scoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November.”.

According to recent reports, united have had to deal with their fair share of suspensions already this season. Yoro, Lisandro Martinez, Matheus Cunha and, more recently, Youri Tielemans, have all been sent-off at some point to raise concerns over a disciplinary concern within the squad.

A further suspension concern is in the form of central defender Harry Maguire, who is presently on four Premier League yellow cards.

As per the rules, players who pick up five yellow cards in the first 19 matches of the Premier League season are hit with a one-match ban.

Man United's cut-off point in which suspensions will be handed out for a fifth yellow card is the away game against Brighton on January 2, 2027. From there, players who are booked 10 times in the first 32 matches of the campaign are suspended for two games.

Maguire’s Premier League yellow cards this season have come against Everton, Manchester City, Bournemouth and Chelsea.

As such, if the central defender is booked against Brentford, he will have to serve a one-match ban against Newcastle at St James’ Park on December 2.

Even if Maguire avoids a yellow card, he will still be walking the disciplinary tightrope in upcoming games against Newcastle, Coventry, Crystal Palace, Arsenal, Nottingham Forest, Sunderland and Brighton.

The other Man United players in danger of a Premier League ban are Bruno Fernandes, Diogo Dalot, Ayden Heaven, Bryan Mbeumo, Andrey Santos and Luke Shaw, all on two yellow cards.
