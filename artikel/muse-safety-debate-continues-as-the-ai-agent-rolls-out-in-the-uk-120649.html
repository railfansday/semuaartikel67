Sources indicate that the debate over the wisdom and safety of using Meta’s Muse AI agent continues as the app rolls out in the UK. It follows a warning by a number of regulatory bodies about the potential risks of AI agents ….

Meta yesterday confirmed the UK availability of Muse.

Sources indicate that muse, Meta’s personal Al agent, is now available to adults in the UK. Think of it as a helping hand for the jobs that pile up, like chasing a refund, renewing car insurance or keeping on top of the school calendar. To get those jobs done you can connect Muse to the apps you already use, from your email and calendar to the retailer apps you shop on.

A forum representing four different UK regulatory bodies last month issued a warning about the potential dangers involved in using AI agents. The Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum said:.

Industry observers note that AI agents may amplify and accelerate risks and introduce fresh ones. These risks may increase as the capabilities of personal agents grow and adoption rises. Questions of consumer protection, competition and data protection – for example the risk of fraud and scams, personal information sharing not in line with user expectations and misleading recommendations, decisions or information provided – must be addressed to ensure innovation develops in ways that earn and maintain public trust.

Recent findings suggest that meta has pushed back against suggestions that Muse has accessed personal data without permission, insisting that owners are in control and must approve crucial actions. The manufacturer has admitted to some “isolated incidents,” but says other reported breaches were either hallucinations or took place on internal versions of the app while undergoing testing. In a new blog post, Meta says:.

Muse only works with the apps and services you choose to connect it to, and you control the access it gets […] Once you’ve decided what Muse is connected to and what you want it to do, with your oversight, it can handle tasks like searching, comparing and planning for you. But before it does anything like sending an email or buying something, it shows you exactly what it’s about to do and waits for your go-ahead. A separate security agent checks Muse’s actions and the information it sends out against the permissions you’ve granted. You can also choose how often it checks in with you in Settings.

It has been revealed that at the time of writing, Muse remains the second most popular free app in Apple’s US App Store, with more than 10 million downloads. We reported yesterday that some banks are already blocking AI agents, while others are warning that they will not be responsible for the consequences of any actions taken by them.
