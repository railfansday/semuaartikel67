📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There's no telling what caused the Nvidia board to vote unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO less than a week after saying his return wasn’t under consideration – except reportedly the threat of having one's head ripped off and brain peed on – but that really doesn't matter, at least as far as shareholders need to be concerned: Jensen's back in charge and all’s right in the world of chip stocks.

"I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," Huang noted of the decision to reinstate him, calling it an honor.

Nvidia kicked its former-cum-current CEO and president to the curb on November 12, but not over allegations that he may have been sleeping with a suspected Chinese spy – it was affairs at Nvidia with his subordinates that appears to have been the last straw with Nvidia directors.

Nvidia stock has tumbled since Huang’s ouster, and much of the rest of the tech stock world followed suit. Nvidia insiders and tech heavyweights have been pushing for Huang’s return for about as long as he was unemployed, but calls for his return from the US federal government may have been the catalyst for a change of heart among Nvidia leadership who didn’t want Huang back.

Recent findings suggest that "In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk," Nvidia stated in its statement announcing Huang’s return.

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," board chair Harvey Jones added. Jones was made Nvidia's first board chair upon Huang’s resignation, and will remain in that role.

The White House has since confirmed that the senior-level official involved in Huang’s reinstatement was President Trump.According to a White House statement to Semafor, Trump personally dialed in to an Nvidia board meeting hours before Huang was reinstated to argue for his return.“President Trump was totally involved and this is a great decision by the NVIDIA board,” White House comms director Steven Cheung told Semafor. “We have to protect our geniuses.”.

"The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to Nvidia poses any risk to National Security," Trump noted in a post on Truth Social. "The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.".

It has been revealed that the surprise return echoes Sam Altman's return as CEO of OpenAI after the board fired him for a brief five days in 2023 over allegations of dishonesty. Both reinstatements happened around the US Thanksgiving holiday, suggesting that the spirit of forgiveness waxes with the anticipation of that roasted turkey smell wafting through the house. ®.
