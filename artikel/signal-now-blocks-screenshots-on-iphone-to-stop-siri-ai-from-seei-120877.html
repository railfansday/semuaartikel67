In a recent development, popular secure messaging app Signal has rolled out a new enhancement that automatically disables screenshots for the sake of blocking Siri AI from reading your chats. Here are the details.

Signal’s latest app patch on iPhone disables a key system feature: taking screenshots.

As Signal told Reuters, that’s because Apple doesn’t presently provide better tools for iOS developers to safeguard app content from Siri AI and third-party AI agents.

By default, consumers who update and try to take a screenshot will see a message that states: “Screenshot prevented by ‘Block Siri AI’ setting.”.

Sources indicate that fortunately, there’s an easy workaround. People who want to re-enable screenshots can do so by visiting Signal’s privacy settings and turning off the fresh toggle that’s labeled ‘Block Siri AI.’.
