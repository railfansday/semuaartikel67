Major U.S. law firms are giving associates raises and establishing a fresh market rate ranging from $235,000 for first-year lawyers to $455,000 for senior associates, plus additional bonuses.

Cravath, Swaine & Moore’s associate salary scale was increased Tuesday, and it rivals the raises confirmed in June at Milbank, according to coverage by Reuters.

Industry observers note that milbank increased the associate salary range from $235,000 to $455,000. In July, it offered special bonuses between $6,000 and $25,000 for associates. Reuters is also reporting pay increases at Fried, Frank, Harris, Shriver & Jacobson and Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton.

Cravath’s special bonuses—$11,000 to $40,000—based on class year, exceeded Milbank’s scale. Its year-end bonus scale starts at $15,000 and extends to $115,000, which is a continuation of what the firm and others offered in past years.

No comments were given by Cravath.
