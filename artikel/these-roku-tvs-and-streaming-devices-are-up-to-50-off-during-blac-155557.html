Amazon has marked down Roku’s smart TVs and streaming devices as part of its Black Friday 2026 sale, taking up to $220 off the TVs and up to 50% off the streaming players—giving you a good opportunity to upgrade your home entertainment setup or simply get more out of the one you already own. Prices start at $20 for the Roku Streaming Stick HD, which works with older HD TVs, and go up to $429.99 for the 65-inch Roku Plus Series, which has a bigger 4K screen and a Mini-LED backlight.

At just $229.99, down from $349.99, the 55-inch Select Series Smart TV (2026) is an inexpensive way to get a 4K smart TV for a bedroom, apartment, or secondary living space. It offers a large enough screen for comfortable viewing from the couch, while Roku's familiar interface gives you easy access to major streaming services.

Sources indicate that for a larger screen and better picture quality, the 65-inch Plus Series Smart TV is down to $429.99 from $649.99, saving you $220. Its 4K QLED display delivers richer colors than the entry-level Select Series, while Dolby Vision support helps bring out more detail in bright highlights and darker scenes when watching compatible content. The extra screen space also makes it a better fit for a main living room TV.

Industry observers note that you don’t necessarily need a fresh television to enjoy Roku’s streaming platform, because the 2025 Streaming Stick HD can add it to the TV you already own, and right now, it’s $19, down from $39.99. It draws power from a compatible TV’s USB port and streams in 1080p, making it a good fit for older HD TVs, guest rooms, and even hotel TVs. Read PCMag’s review for more details.

It has been revealed that for TVs that support 4K, the Streaming Stick Plus is a step up, offering sharper resolution and support for HDR10 and HDR10+ while keeping the same compact, USB-powered design as the HD model. It's currently $49, down from $34.99, and also supports Apple AirPlay for streaming content from an iPhone. You can read more about its features in PCMag's review.

For anyone looking to improve their TV's sound along with its streaming capabilities, the Roku Streambar SE combines both in a single device and is at the moment $89, down from $149.99. It supports 4K streaming with HDR10 and HDR10+ and uses two 1.9-inch full-range drivers with a bass port to deliver fuller audio than most built-in TV speakers.
