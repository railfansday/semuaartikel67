Yes, that was Robert Downey Jr.'s voice in the VisionQuest post-credits scene

The VisionQuest finale sent the internet aflame with surprise post-credit cameos from Doctor Doom and the Scarlet Witch, directly setting up Avengers: Doomsday.

In the scene, Doom revives Wanda Maximoff and recruits her to his cause. "Scarlet Witch, awaken," he claims. "Your power will be mine." Then, Wanda gasps and opens her eyes.

According to recent reports, "That's Downey's voice," VisionQuest showrunner Terry Matalas told Gold Derby, clarifying that he didn't direct the scene in question. "I can't talk too much about how it was constructed, because I don't want to give away, but yes, that line was designed for this moment, specifically," he explained.

That's a pretty intriguing choice of words, suggesting there's a twist hidden in the scene somewhere. However, we'll probably have to wait until Doomsday arrives to find out what exactly there is to "give away.".

This moment isn't the only time Matalas wanted to enlist the use of Downey Jr.'s voice, either, though he was foiled by the Marvel Phase 6 show's budget. "You know, things cost money. We're on a television show budget," he commented. "There was a moment where I wanted to hear Stark call in and talk to Mary McDonnell['s character Katerina Wells] and say, 'Your time's up on this code.' And it was the old Tony, it was the Tony before the Jericho missiles. And he's like, 'Sell me the code, so I can turn it into my things.' But, you know, it would have been a big ask to get Downey to come in and do it.".

Doomsday arrives on December 18, while all episodes of VisionQuest are streaming on Disney Plus now. For more, check out our VisionQuest review, or our guide to all the other upcoming Marvel movies and TV shows.
