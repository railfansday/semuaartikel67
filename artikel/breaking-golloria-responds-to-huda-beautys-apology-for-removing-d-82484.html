Golloria can’t conceal her thoughts on Huda Beauty’s apology.

After the makeup brand’s founder Huda Kattan apologized to the beauty influencer for how her firm handled discontinuing their darkest setting powder Coffee Cake and replacing it with Coco Truffle, Golloria shared her thoughts on the response.

In a recent development, “I appreciate your apology and you taking the time to get back to me,” Golloria posted in the comments of Kattan’s Nov. 20 Instagram post. “Speaking from both my experience as a shade consultant and as a dark skinned woman whose shade options are directly affected by this decision, I don’t think Coco Truffle should replace Coffee Cake. And that’s just the honest truth.”.

The TikToker explained that she heard Huda Beauty’s reasoning that Coffee Cake wasn’t doing well with consumers, but added that she didn’t think the two shades were “interchangeable,” noting that “a shade occupying a similar depth in a range doesn’t necessarily mean it serves the same complexions.”.

Industry observers note that “You’ve stated several times that you want to better serve dark skinned women, and part of that is listening to the women telling you that Coffee Cake already works for them,” the 25-year-old continued. “That feedback should help guide what you preserve as you expand the range.”.

According to recent reports, she also added that darker skinned women “deserve variety in both depth and undertone,” and that having both shades would help create that inclusion. Instead, Golloria shared in her own Instagram caption that she felt Huda Beauty was “doing what's easiest for the brand instead of what's needed by the women you say you want to serve.”.

“Inclusion means making room for our differences,” she wrote, “not asking us to settle for whatever is most convenient for you to offer.”.

As for Kattan, while the beauty founder did not reply to Golloria’s comment, she explained in her apology video that she was sorry for how the conversation about the shade replacement had been handled with Golloria, especially as it was happening during a busy time for the brand and when Kattan herself had to undergo emergency surgery for a ruptured breast implant in April.

According to the 43-year-old, her team found that Coffee Cake was “not something that a lot of people have shared positive feedback with us on,” adding that they “definitely thought we could make it a lot better.”.

The decision led them to launching Coco Truffle as a replacement for Coffee Cake, first as a pressed powder, then as a loose powder—although it was not yet widely available.. But after hearing from their consumers, Kattan realized they should have done a better job being more inclusive with their shades.

Sources indicate that “Inclusivity is not about, ‘Yes, done, check, we're inclusive.’ That to me is some of the things that are wrong with the industry,” she said. “It's 2026. We can do better. We can do better and I think brands should be doing better in 2026.”.

And while they will be reworking Coco Truffle to make sure it’s available “as quickly as possible,” she also regretted fully removing Coffee Cake from their lineup.

“We should have kept it in stores,” Kattan said, “because at the end of the day, if some people liked it, we should have kept it in stores until there was something better available.”.

She also once again apologized for not having better communicated all of this to Golloria initially, although she was hopeful that the beauty influencer and Huda Beauty fans would appreciate Coco Truffle.

“I'm hoping to give you guys a shade that you guys really, really love, and all the beautiful rich skin tone girls and boys feel really represents them,” Kattan shared, “and is perfect for their skin tone.”.
