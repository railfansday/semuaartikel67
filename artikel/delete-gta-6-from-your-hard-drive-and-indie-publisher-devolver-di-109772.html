GTA 6 is out at midnight everywhere (19th November), but it's far from the only game in town. Indie publisher Devolver Digital is taking on the open-world behemoth with its very own Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed, out the same day, and you can win a free copy of the platformer by proving you've deleted GTA 6 off your hard drive.

According to recent reports, "Want a free copy of November's hottest release? Repost this tweet with a video of you deleting your GTA6 pre-load along with #MakeRoomForVolvy for a chance to win a copy of Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed on Steam," the publisher wrote on social media.

Industry observers note that it's not every day you see a publisher promote its own games by asking folks to delete another company's release, but arguably the wildest part of the giveaway is that Volvy's Adventure isn't launching on consoles. Even if you were to scrub GTA 6's 212GB pre-load from your PS5 or Xbox, you're definitely not making room for Volvy.

"Heads up: Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed is launching exclusively on PC. While we expect to see console GTA deletions in your submissions, Steam keys are the only prize available for this giveaway," Devolver noted.

Devolver made quite an amusing GTA 6 parody trailer, as well, which sees Volvy and Miss Volvy running from the police and getting steamy in the shower. I've embedded it up above for your viewing pleasure.

For the unaware, Volvy used to be a common fixture at Devolver Digital's frequently bonkers notE3 presentations. He's now somewhat of a tech giant mascot. But while Volvy's Adventure: Reslimed is being presented as a "much-anticipated remaster of the original remake of the hit action-platformer," this is, in fact, Volvy's first proper game. Making up an elaborate history for Volvy is just part of the publisher's long-running and increasingly questionable gag.

Gunbrella and Gato Robot developer doinksoft is behind this one, a throwback side-scrolling platformer where our mouse-like mascot uses his oversized hand to helicopter over gaps, punch through roadblocks and, erm, fist himself underground, per the game's Steam page.

Devolver Digital has been waiting to steal Rockstar's thunder for well over a year now. The publisher threatened to release a game on GTA 6's release date way back in May 2025, and has pushed back its blueprint with each latest GTA 6 delay. Gotta respect the commitment, at least.
