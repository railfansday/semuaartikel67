📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Phil Spencer is a legend of the Xbox division, having helped redefine the brand after a disastarous Xbox One launch in 2013, and spearheading such initiatives as Xbox Game Pass, Xbox backwards compatibility, crossplay, and a renewed focus on gaming rather than continuing the push towards "TV, TV, TV" that had proven so controversial.

It's very possible that without his influence, Xbox would have shut its doors over a decade ago.

We haven't heard anything from Spencer since he announced his retirement earlier in 2026, but he's still very actively playing games on his Xbox consoles, including some of this year's fresh releases.

For example, he's racked up 940/1000 gamerscore in Echo Generation 2, 650/1000 in Forza Horizon 6, 585/1000 in Replaced, and he's also been playing the likes of Halo: Campaign Evolved, Gears of War: E-Day, Sopa - Tale of the Stolen Potato, Baldur's Gate 3, Vampire Crawlers and even Sunset Overdrive in recent months.

One of Phil's most beloved qualities as Xbox boss was that he proved himself to be a hardcore gamer, being someone who actively seeks to play games in his spare time. Upcoming Xbox CEO Asha Sharma is somewhat the opposite, admitting back in February that she doesn't "pretend to be the best gamer" and that it's ultimately not her goal to be.

Industry observers note that anyway, Phil is busy enjoying his retirement right now, and we wish him all the best from everyone here at Pure Xbox. We unfortunately never got a chance to meet the man ourselves, but always admired his leadership from afar.

Here's hoping he might start hitting the interview circuit and doing what the likes of Peter Moore and Nintendo's Reggie Fils-Aimé have done in the past, as we'd love to see him sticking around the industry in some capacity going forward.
