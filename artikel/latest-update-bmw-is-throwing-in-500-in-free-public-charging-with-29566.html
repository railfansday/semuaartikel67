Latest Update: BMW is throwing in $500 in free public charging with its newly introduced 2027 iX3, i5, and i7 EVs

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

BMW introduced a fresh public charging package for those who buy or lease the new 2027 iX3, i5, or i7 electric vehicles.

BMW’s electric vehicle lineup will look much different in 2027. The German luxury automaker is shaking things up with the arrival of its Neue Klasse EVs, bringing significantly longer ranges, faster charging times, and advanced upcoming tech and features.

To sweeten the deal, BMW launched a new public charging promotion on Thursday for eligible model-year 2027 electric vehicles, including the new iX3, i5, and i7.

Sources indicate that the latest BMW ChargingNetwork package provides $500 in total credits. $300 of which includes a BMW ChargingNetwork credit that can be used at one of 15 public charging providers, such as ChargePoint, EVgo, Shell Recharge, and IONNA. The other $200 is for IONNA locations only.

In a recent development, on top of this, the promo includes a free one-year BMW Membership at IONNA. The membership provides a 25% discount on charging during the free year trial.

BMW stated the charging credits will automatically apply after you enroll in the BMW ChargingNetwork.

Industry observers note that you can enroll through the My BMW app. Under Charging, select Services, then Charging Wallet, and then BMW ChargingNetwork. Once complete, the credits and other benefits will show up in the Digital Charging Wallet and will be automatically applied to eligible charging sessions.

Which one to choose? The 2027 iX3 is BMW’s first Neue Klasse vehicle. Based on its sixth-generation (Gen6) eDrive, the iX3 delivers an EPA-estimated range of up to 434 miles.

Recent findings suggest that inside, it features BMW’s revamped Neue Klasse interior design. The setup includes the latest BMW Panoramic Vision, a full-length display spanning the base of the windshield, and a large 17.9″ infotainment screen running the latest BMW Operating System X.

The 2027 i7 also incorporates elements and tech from BMW’s Neue Klasse platform, with up to 362 miles of range.

The i7’s interior also gets the BMW Panoramic Vision display, a 17.9″ infotainment screen, and a standard 14.6″ passenger screen. For the rear passengers, BMW’s flagship sedan includes a 31.3″ 8K theater screen that folds down from the ceiling.

Meanwhile, the 2027 i5, which still offers up to 310 miles of range, is not built on the dedicated Neue Klasse platform.

As part of a mid-cycle refresh, it adopts BMW’s new style but lacks some Neue Klasse tech and features found on the iX3 and i7.

Recent findings suggest that in the first quarter of 2027, BMW will launch the latest iX5 and i3. Both are based on the Neue Klasse platform.
