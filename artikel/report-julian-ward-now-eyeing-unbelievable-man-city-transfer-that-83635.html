It has been revealed that right now, the Reds have a lot of reasons to be aggrieved at their fresh nemesis. For years, Liverpool have contested with City and fought for parity.

But now all of those contests seem futile and meaningless in the gaping abyss of a mess that is unfolding in front of our eyes.

City have been found guilty of 114 charges by an independent Premier League panel. Now, they are appealing those charges, so their punishment and guilt is pending that appeal.

However, if the decision stands and it is ratified, there should be outrage from the Reds.

Sources indicate that it will have meant that Liverpool missed out on trophies and glory not on merit but because another party had an unfair advantage. For the integrity of the sport that is abhorrent.

What will happen to Manchester City if their appeal fails?Well, the punishment is going to be unprecedented. The Premier League has never been in a position like this before and everything will be on the table. Sides like Liverpool and other big clubs who were directly impacted by City will want as harsh of a punishment as possible. This could involve a severe points reduction, transfer bans, even relegation and potentially a big fine as well. Whether City will be able to survive that storm remains to be seen. The chances are that there will be an opportunity for rival clubs to try to poach City's best players.
