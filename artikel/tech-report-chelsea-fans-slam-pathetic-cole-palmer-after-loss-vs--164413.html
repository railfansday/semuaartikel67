Chelsea slumped to yet another defeat on Friday night, this time at the hands of Nottingham Forest at the City Ground, and Blues fans aren't best pleased with one player in particular.

The West Londoners fell behind after just five minutes when Igor Jesus spectacularly volleyed past Emi Martinez after excellent work from Daniel Munoz down the right. Xabi Alonso's team mustered up just two shots in what was a dire opening 45 minutes from the away side.

Recent findings suggest that forest doubled their advantage shortly after the break when Morgan Gibbs-White sent Martinez the wrong way from 12 yards after Wesley Fofana had clumsily brought down Liam Delap inside the Chelsea penalty area. The Blues hit back minutes later, with substitute Emanuel Emegha on hand to pounce on a loose ball and slot beyond Matz Sels.

The home team restored their two-goal cushion with 18 minutes left to play, when Gibbs-White added to his tally by clipping the ball over the on-rushing Martinez after Xaver Schlager's exquisite through ball. Emegha pulled one back late on with a neat header, but it wasn't enough for Alonso's men.

The result sees Forest move away from the relegation zone and up into 14th, while Chelsea remain eighth, five points behind Liverpool in fourth.

Alonso's outfit are on a dismal run, losing each of their last three Premier League matches, and fans are subsequently growing frustrated.

While there have been a few murmurings around the manager's future, the focus of Blues supporters' discontent has largely centred around the players.

Fofana, who conceded the penalty that gifted Forest a second goal, was the subject of hostility from the travelling crowd, who booed the Frenchman whilst he was in possession.

However, online, Chelsea fans took aim at a different player. Palmer, who created just one chance and took no shots on the night, was heavily scrutinised by his own supporters on social media.

One fan bluntly wrote on X: "Cole Palmer. What a waste of space.".

"Cole Palmer has actually fallen off a cliff, what am I watching here?" another added.

Meanwhile, one fan had a team selection suggestion for Alonso, urging him to drop Palmer for the next game and start Estevao instead.

"Palmer needs to be benched for a game at this point. Let Estevao fight for his spot.".

Industry observers note that "What happened to Palmer? He looks to have lost his attacking prowess," another wrote.

This theme continued, with another fan stating that the playmaker is 'a shadow of himself' and another accusing the player of 'ghosting again'.

Meanwhile, two more roped Morgan Rogers into the criticism.

"Rogers & Palmer, I have no words for you both," one posited.

Finally, a supporter described the pair's displays as 'pathetic': "Palmer and Rogers have been pathetic.".

In a recent development, what makes Palmer special when he's performing at his best level is his ability to create chances and get shots off from difficult positions. Against Forest, he did neither.

He played within himself, opting against risky moves in the final third and instead choosing to retain possession. Creating one chance, taking no shots and completing just two dribbles, it was a completely ineffective performance from the number ten.

While Palmer must step up and take more responsibility, his coach needs to concoct a system that elicits more from his star attacker.
