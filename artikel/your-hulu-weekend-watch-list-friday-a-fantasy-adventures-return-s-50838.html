According to recent reports, this Walter Salles-directed bio-drama made history last year, becoming the first Brazilian production to win Best International Feature Film at the Academy Awards. It also deservedly received awards-body kudos for its stunning lead performance by Fernanda Torres, who earned an Oscar nomination and a Golden Globe win for Best Actress, making her the first Brazilian to win a Globe for acting.

Recent findings suggest that based on Marcelo Rubens Paiva's biographical book, the political drama sees Torres play Eunice Paiva, a mother of five children living in 1970s Rio de Janeiro, who becomes a human rights advocate following the abduction of her husband — the dissident politician Rubens Paiva (Selton Mello) — during the military dictatorship in Brazil. Eunice spends decades searching for the truth of her spouse's disappearance and the regime's involvement in the shattering of her family.
