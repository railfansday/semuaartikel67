Summer’s Coolest Manicure Is Fall-Approved, Too, According To Hailey Bieber

Summer may be over, but that doesn’t mean the beauty trends we loved in warmer times have to completely disappear. Sparkles are still going strong—perfect for the holiday season!—and Hailey Bieber just proved that a summer of blue manicures will keep going through icier months, too.

Recent findings suggest that earlier this year, Bieber, Charli XCX, and Charli D’Amelio all reinventing the idea of summer blues, with shades of sky, periwinkle, ocean, cobalt, and more spotted on their fingers and toes. First, Bieber was seen on a post–World Cup date night with husband Justin, sporting matching tips and toes in a bright (and unexpected) shade of periwinkle. It was the perfect pop of surprise color to go with her vintage red mini slip dress from John Galliano’s Dior. A handful of days later, Charli XCX also chose the unexpected color for her pedicure—pairing an entirely black outfit (very Music, Fashion, Film of her) with a wash of sky blue peeking through her open-toe pumps.

Now, Bieber is back at it again with a shade of almost robin’s egg blue on her long almond nails. Painted by celebrity nail artist Kim Truong (known online as Kim K Nails), the “jelly blue” manicure is surprisingly versatile: Bieber wore the look to the The New York Times’ DealBook Summit, the WWD Beauty Inc Awards, and though her birthday celebrations.

In a recent development, blue was one of the colors Vogue predicted would be a major nail color trend for this year—and six months in, celebrities are proving the forecast was spot on. So how to choose the right shade for you? Clearly, there’s no wrong choice. Try looking around and see how inspiration strikes.
