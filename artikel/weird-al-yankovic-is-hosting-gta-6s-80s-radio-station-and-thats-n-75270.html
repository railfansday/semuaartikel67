According to recent reports, if you're hearing a familiar voice between the '80s hits on GTA 6's Vice City FM, you're not imagining things. 'Weird Al' Yankovic is one of the station's hosts. And Rockstar didn't just bring him in to introduce songs. He's also written some of the game's commercials and voiced the ads for Burger Shot.

Industry observers note that yankovic confirmed the news in a Bluesky post on November 19, saying he's playing DJ Al on the game's 'non-stop '80s' station. PC Gamer reports that he shares hosting duties with actress and model Brigitte Nielsen, with both appearing in Rockstar's credits.

Recent findings suggest that the radio gig would be enough to get my attention on its own, but Yankovic also said he wrote some in-game commercials and provided the voice for Burger Shot's advertisements. If you've spent any time listening to GTA radio, you'll know how much Rockstar enjoys making fun of advertising, so that feels like a particularly good use of him.

I'm a Weird Al fan (who isn't?), and I'm both surprised and not particularly surprised to see him here. He's the sort of person who can turn a ridiculous idea into something genuinely funny, whether he's parodying a hit song or doing a completely original bit. He's ridiculously talented, and getting him to write fake ads for a GTA game just makes sense.

There's also the fact that Yankovic is a longtime GTA fan himself. He sounded pretty happy about the appearance in his announcement and ended his post by saying he was getting back to playing the game. Hard to imagine a better fit for somebody with his sense of humor.

As I covered when Rockstar revealed The Chamber 106.6, GTA 6 also has a dedicated metal station hosted by Slayer's Tom Araya and Kerry King. Its preview featured Black Sabbath, Pantera, and Slayer. If anyone could convince two Slayer members and Weird Al Yankovic to host radio stations in the same game, it was probably going to be Rockstar.

In a recent development, that alone tells you how varied the lineup is. Vice City FM is built around '80s music, while The Chamber gives metal fans somewhere to go. There are country, hip-hop, dance, and plenty of other stations for people who'd rather listen to something else.

If anyone could convince two Slayer members and Weird Al Yankovic to host radio stations in the same game, it was probably going to be Rockstar.

Recent findings suggest that personally, I'm much more interested in the older music, metal, punk, rock, and alternative side of things than whatever's dominating the pop charts right now. I knew Rockstar would have a wide selection, but the radio-station announcements have pretty much exceeded my expectations. And now they've added Weird Al to the mix.

The commercials are worth paying attention to, too. GTA's radio stations have never been just playlists. The fake products, strange callers, obnoxious hosts, and commercials that sound almost believable are a substantial part of what makes driving around these cities entertaining.

Some of the older games also had excellent celebrity DJs. Axl Rose hosted K-DST in San Andreas, while Max Cavalera took over Liberty City Hardcore in The Lost and Damned. Weird Al feels like another name that belongs on that list, especially with Rockstar letting him write some of the material he'll be performing.

I'm curious to hear what his Burger Shot ads sound like. The fast-food chain is already one of GTA's running jokes, and giving those commercials to someone who has spent decades making audiences laugh at pop culture seems like a pretty safe bet.

Lazlow's departure had a lot of us longtime GTA fans worried about the future of the radio stations, but with Rockstar bringing in people like Slayer's Tom Araya and Weird Al, I'd say they've got that side of things covered.

According to recent reports, I would've been happy just knowing there's an '80s station to spend a few hours with while driving around Leonida. Having Weird Al on the microphone, along with the return of a proper metal station, has made exploring GTA 6's radio stations one of my favorite things to do in the game.
