Following the November 2026 Pixel Drop announcement, Google is letting Pixel 10 and 11 owners try Create My Widget ahead of broader availability.

In a recent development, create My Widget and other Pixel Drop features are rolling out over the coming weeks. Ahead of that, Google is opening a Create My Widget beta on the Play Store.

Industry observers note that visit the Play Store listing here and look for the “Join the beta” card. Afterward, go to the Check for updates page and install the latest version. (It might take a few minutes after sign-up for the upgrade to appear.).

According to recent reports, create My Widget requires Gemini Intelligence on the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. It’s available in English in Australia, Canada, India, United Kingdom, and the United States.
