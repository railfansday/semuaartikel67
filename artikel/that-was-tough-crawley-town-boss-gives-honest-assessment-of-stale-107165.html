Industry observers note that “Wow, bloody hell, that was tough”, said Blackwell. "It's a very direct game, that's for sure, and they played it quite well, so I'm really pleased with the way the players dealt with it.

It has been revealed that “They're an experienced side and we had a lot of youngsters again today, so all credit to them. "I thought Ben Heneghan, once again, at the centre of the back three has done really well.

“It's a typical system to play against and I can see why they're high up in the league, it's causing teams problems. But I think overall, you know, Vito's not had many saves to make it, has he?.”.

Blackwell was pleased with how his reduced the amount of gilt-edged chances Dean Brennan’s side had. “There wasn't clear-cut chances,” he remarked. “It's a system that I've not seen for a long time, but it just meant we had to be focused all the time, they do things rapidly, they're long throws, long balls, the keeper kicks it from box to box.

Recent findings suggest that “They know that the big nine is going to flick it on and they make runners in behind and your midfield and your centre-halves have to stay with runners. So it was a disciplined performance tonight against a team, like I say, who play a very direct style, but are good at what they do.”.

And the former Leeds boss highlighted that despite the gap in league positions, there was not much in quality between the two sides.

In a recent development, blackwell said: “You look at it, it was top versus bottom, but you wouldn't have known. I don't think you'd have known.

“Yes, they put us under a bit of pressure towards the end and exactly what a team at the top, full of confidence, you'd expect them to do that. Like I said, you know, Pemi [Aderoju] and Osman [Kamara] and Duke [Stephen Duke-McKenna, they've got a lot of youngsters we have and you're going to learn a lot from that.
