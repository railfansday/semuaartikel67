Carlo Ancelotti has had his opportunity to respond to Romario, who called for the coach’s head after leading Brazil to a 3-2 friendly defeat against Japan over the weekend: ‘He talks a lot, it’s his right’.

Ancelotti is facing criticism after a relatively underwhelming start to life as head coach of the Brazil national team. Their 2026 World Cup campaign ended with a round of 16 defeat against Norway, and the criticism has since increased after a 3-2 defeat against Japan in Singapore on Friday night.

Former Brazil national team star Romario was one of Ancelotti’s loudest and fiercest critics in the aftermath of the Japan defeat, calling upon CBF President Samir Xaud to dismiss Ancelotti from his position.

It has been revealed that “Ancelotti, it’s done! Take your things and go,” wrote Romario in a post on Instagram.

“Nobody is questioning your history. You’re one of the greatest coaches the world has ever seen, but that was in club football. With the Brazil national team, so far, things haven’t been the same. We can’t take it much longer.

In a recent development, “Do you want to continue coaching? Go back to the clubs where you made your name, because the Brazil national team can’t keep paying for it.

“The message now is for the President of the CBF, Samir Xaud,” Romario’s Instagram post continued. “You are young, the Brazilian people and I think you want what’s best for our football. Now it’s time to show that you have the courage to make a decision.”.

Ancelotti was asked for his take during his pre-match press conference ahead of Brazil’s next game against Singapore, which kicks off at 10.00 GMT on Tuesday morning.

“In a democratic country with freedom of speech, everyone is allowed to express their opinion,” Ancelotti began.

“He can tell lies, he can tell the truth or talk nonsense. It’s not up to me to say whether the things he states are correct, wrong or just stupid. The truth is that Romario talks a lot. A lot. It’s his right.”.
