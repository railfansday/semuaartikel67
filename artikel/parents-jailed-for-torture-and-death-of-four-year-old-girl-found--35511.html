The mother of a four-year-old girl killed in central France and found along a motorway almost 40 years ago has been sentenced to 20 years in prison.

Halima El Bakhti, 72, was found guilty on Tuesday of "torture and acts of barbarity resulting in death without the intent to kill", French media reports.

The girl's father, 74-year-old Ahmed Touloub, was handed a 15-year sentence as an accomplice.
