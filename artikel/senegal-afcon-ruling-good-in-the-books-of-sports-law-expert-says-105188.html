Senegal AFCON ruling 'good in the books of sports law', expert says

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (88 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Speaking with FRANCE 24's Jennie Shin, sports presenter and journalist Thando Sibiya explains that the decision by the Court of Arbitration for Sport to reinstate Senegal as ⁠winner of ​the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations "from a jurisprudence perspective or creating precedent, it is going to be good in the books of sports law, because if every single decision of a referee is taken to a court of law all the time, it really tampers with the quality, governance and legitimacy of the game".
