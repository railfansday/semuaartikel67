Danny McBride has teamed up with Kalshi again, writing and starring in another ad for the prediction market platform.

In the one-minute spot, McBride plays a know-it-all uncle at the Thanksgiving dinner table, spewing predictions while his niece fact-checks him with odds on Kalshi. “Charlie and the Chocolate Factory” star Deep Roy stars as McBride’s Thanksgiving companion.

It has been revealed that “My family is cool, but they can also be annoying. Just smile, enjoy the food and give thanks,” McBride tells him in the car. Soon, he’s arguing about interest rates, inflation and the Buffalo Bills. “Ooh, the markets. Major whoop!” he states. “I can formulate my own theories without using silly little data points.”.

Recent findings suggest that the ad is directed by Randy Krallman, who helmed McBride’s last Kalshi ad, which featured the “Eastbound & Down” star opposite Shane Gillis as feuding meteorologists. Roy also appeared in that ad as some sort of “weather oracle” who was really getting his information from Kalshi.

According to recent reports, “Every Thanksgiving I show up with a lot of opinions and a lot of boxed wine, so when Kalshi asked me to play an uncle who gets reality-checked by his niece and Kalshi, I didn’t have to do a whole lot of acting,” McBride said in a statement. “Getting back on set with Deep Roy is always a blast. We feel like we are doing public service by handing every dinner table in America the secret weapon for shutting up know-it-all family members.”.

Apps like Kalshi — which allow customers to bet real money on real-world events in sports, entertainment and politics — have used their marketing spend to attract massive-name talent. Timothée Chalamet signed a multi-year creative partnership with Kalshi and starred in a widely publicized ad, while competitor site Polymarket released a splashy campaign with LeBron James, Spike Lee, Emily Ratajkowski and more celebs. Even Jake Paul’s sports-gambling app Betr made headlines (and received a cease-and-desist letter from HBO) for an ad reuniting “Entourage” stars Jeremy Piven and Adrian Grenier.
