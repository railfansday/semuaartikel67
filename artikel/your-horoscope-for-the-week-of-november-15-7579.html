📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that last week, Mercury Retrograde and Venus Retrograde finally ended. This week, you get a moment to catch your breath as Scorpio season draws to a close (it'll end next Sunday, November 22). Along with enjoying the remainder of the Scorpio vibes while they last, there are two smaller cosmic events that you might notice this week.

It has been revealed that first, Jupiter and Neptune Retrograde form a harmonious biquintile aspect on Sunday. In English, this means that the two planets are connecting in such a way that their energies are working together. You might notice a little extra luck or motivation today. Then, on Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon in Aquarius continues the "get to work" message. A First Quarter Moon marks the halfway point between a New Moon and a Full Moon, and it's a reminder to stay focused on your goals.

Make something, Aries. Sunday's Jupiter-Neptune aspect ignites your creativity and your motivation. Start working on that novel/album/podcast/whatever you've been thinking about. Then, on Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon encourages you to reach out to your friends for support or advice as you work on this project.

Recent findings suggest that phone home, Taurus. On Sunday, the Jupiter-Neptune connection could turn your thoughts towards your childhood. Now's a great time to catch up with a family member or old friend. Then, on Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon gets you even more motivated to work hard at the office. This is a crucial time of year for you, and you're determined to make a good impression.

Catch up with your friends, Gemini. You're feeling even more social than usual under Sunday's Jupiter-Neptune aspect. Go out to brunch with your friends or at least talk to your bestie on the phone. On Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon encourages you to explore beyond your bubble by meeting latest people, studying a new subject, or traveling somewhere new to you.

Ask for that promotion, Cancer. Sunday's Jupiter-Neptune aspect gives you some extra luck when it comes to money matters and some extra bravery when it comes to your career. (If you don't work weekends, ask your boss to talk on Monday instead.) On Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon reminds you that you're in the middle of a personal transformation. Who is the New You?

Industry observers note that yes, you should get bangs, Leo. If you're single, do you want to change anything about your dating life?

Let it go, Virgo. Sunday's Jupiter-Neptune biquintile makes it easier for you to leave something in the past so you can move forward unencumbered. Then, on Tuesday, the First Quarter Moon gives you creative inspiration. Follow it wherever it leads and take notes, because these vibes won't last for long.

Today's a good day to reach out.
