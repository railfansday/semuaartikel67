Early in the second quarter Wednesday night in NBA Cup group play, LeBron James pulled up from Broad Street.

In a recent development, he drilled a 35-foot 3-pointer amid a torrid stretch that saw him make a trio of triples and score 14 straight points for the 76ers on Thanksgiving Eve. Before that sequence, the Sixers were trailing the Miami Heat by three points at Philadelphia's Xfinity Mobile Arena. Afterward, they were up eight.

Sources indicate that while becoming the oldest player in NBA history to notch a triple double — at 41 years and 330 days old — James continued his recent hot streak from beyond the arc, going 4 of 6 on 3-point attempts. Philadelphia shot 14 of 35 from long range en route to a 111-104 victory.

The Heat (10-7) would have clinched East Group B had they come out on top, but Philadelphia (15-4) denied Miami an eighth consecutive win, despite losing Jaylen Brown to a hamstring injury late in the first half and Joel Embiid being sidelined by illness.

Nick Nurse's squad is now 2-1 in NBA Cup competition; Erik Spoelstra's Heat dropped to that same mark.

It has been revealed that james finished with a season-high 25 points and 12 assists, plus 10 rebounds, with maybe his most notable dime taking place early in the fourth quarter. The 22-time All-Star revealed his elite court vision, timing a halfcourt lob perfectly to connect with VJ Edgecombe for a layup. James later dialed up an alley-oop for forward Dominick Barlow to throw down.

In a recent development, edgecombe was among the Sixers players stepping up in the absence of Brown and Embiid, as Philadelphia protected its nine-point halftime lead and kept Miami at arm's length over the final two quarters. He had 18 points, and Tyrese Maxey poured in 30.

The Heat, who wound up trailing by 16 late in the final frame, were led by Giannis Antetokounmpo, who clocked out with 32 points, 14 rebounds and 4 assists.

In a recent development, kevin Durant is now one of five NBA players to score 33,000 points. The 38-year-old Houston Rockets forward achieved that feat on Wednesday night in a 116-104 home victory against the Phoenix Suns. He joined James, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Karl Malone and Kobe Bryant.

Durant did so while piling up 20 points for Houston (14-5), which, despite already being eliminated from NBA Cup knockout-round contention, kept pace in the upper echelon of the Western Conference standings.

It has been revealed that the Rockets improved to 2-2 in Cup group play, while the Suns (9-8) slid to 1-2. They, too, have been eliminated from knockout-round contention in West Group A.

Along the way, Houston center Alperen Şengün passed Hakeem Olajuwon for the second-most triple-doubles in franchise history. Şengün, only 24 years old, secured his 15th performance of that kind with 23 points, 11 rebounds and 11 assists.

Late in the game, Suns wing Dillon Brooks got into it with Durant and was ejected after receiving his second technical foul of the night.

Although the Minnesota Timberwolves lost LaMelo Ball to an ankle injury Wednesday, they held on to defeat the Oklahoma City Thunder 113-106.

According to recent reports, jaden McDaniels came through, major time, in the West Group B matchup. In fact, by game's end, he was the third player in Wolves history to log at least 30 points, 10 rebounds and 5 assists in consecutive games. That elite organization also includes Karl-Anthony Towns and Kevin Garnett.

After going for 34 points, 10 rebounds and 6 assists on Monday in a win over the Brooklyn Nets, McDaniels put up 32 points, 10 rebounds and 5 assists this time out versus the Thunder (10-9), who are 2-7 in their last nine games and eliminated from Cup knockout-round contention.
