The Mauricio Pochettino blueprint and one non-negotiable to fix broken Man United

Mauricio Pochettino is the manager Manchester United could, time and again, have appointed in the past. He marked his eventual arrival at Old Trafford by trying to shrug it off; theirs more than his. Pochettino made it to the last two in 2022, when Erik ten Hag was instead chosen, and may have seemed United’s second choice in 2016 and 2019, when Jose Mourinho and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer were preferred. But by pivoting to the future was a way of avoiding the concern of why none of them – nor David Moyes, Louis van Gaal, Ralf Rangnick, Ruben Amorim or Michael Carrick – has truly succeeded.

Pochettino’s initiative to fix United may draw on a more distant past, the identity he described that seemed to stem more from Sir Alex Ferguson than his eight immediate successors, but reliant heavily on a positivity about the future. “I don't think too much in the past. I think if we want to create something different and positive and the people feel proud about that, it’s to build our own way.”.

Sources indicate that his first target, he smiled, was to sleep; unsurprising given that he had a transatlantic night flight after his final game in charge of the United States, in Cincinnati, on Tuesday. A coach who long coveted the United job is relishing a return to club football, away from the stop-start nature of the international game. “I think we are animals of the grass,” he remarked.

Industry observers note that as United are found in the relegation zone, without a league win since August, the temptation is to say his new charges might not be. But Pochettino is seeking to bring empathetic man-management, drawing on his record of making footballers better and fashion change; the hint was that his football will be different from Carrick’s even if a mid-season appointment facing a packed fixture list may have to compromise at first. “Our job is to adapt,” he said.

But it was notable that he noted: “We arrived with the idea to create something from scratch, from our own ideas and philosophy, in the way that we love to work. Like always, we need to improve our players.”.

According to recent reports, and always Pochettino teams have run, normally further and faster than most. Maybe it was unsurprising, then, that he said: “I think the training session was a little bit longer than it normally is in [matchday] minus two.” Yet the United team he inherited have tended to be outrun; only Sunday’s opponents Liverpool have covered less distance in the Premier League this season.

Recent findings suggest that pochettino argued the statistics do not tell the whole story. “We cannot talk about things without context, and data is about context,” he stated. “If you don't put context on the data, I think it's very dangerous to talk and I think it's easy to manipulate the data, but it's true that that sometimes data indicates something that you can see on the field. But we are going to be very focused on trying to improve our data, our numbers in every single area of the field.”.

Sources indicate that other figures that need an upgrade are the numbers of wins, a mere one, the defensive record, the third worst, the league position of 18th and the numbers that have contributed to United’s worst league start in four decades. If all of that is an indictment of an underachieving squad, Pochettino countered: “The commitment from all the players that we were chatting with was amazing from day one.” Whether all his predecessors would concur with that is another matter, but Pochettino spent three hours in his office on Thursday, talking to them individually. One decision has been made already: he will retain Bruno Fernandes as captain.

“The crucial thing is to find a way, through our experience, to create that platform for them to express their talent,” Pochettino mentioned. There have been times in the last 13 years when the United players have appeared intimidated rather than liberated. The burden of playing for the club has been great. Pochettino wants to turn the pressure and expectation into a positive. “We need to transform that pressure into energy,” he added.

And he wants them to attack. Carrick bowed out with two games that felt throwbacks, the 3-2 win over Roma and the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa. In the seven goals they scored in five days, Pochettino may have something to build on. “We found a group of players that we proposed to do the football that we feel: being brave, being Manchester United,” he said.

According to recent reports, one of the questions of the post-Ferguson era is what being Manchester United really is now. Pochettino has his answer. “I think the culture of the club and the identity of the club is very clear,” he remarked. “It's an historic club with amazing years competing and being one of the best clubs in the world. It's difficult to find another club the size of club that is Manchester United.”.

Certainly there is not one of a similar size in a relegation zone. That is attributable in part to bad recruitment. If hiring Pochettino may change that, a manager with a two-and-a-half year contract wants an input on transfers. “In the future, I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that will happen on the squad,” he mentioned.

That seems crucial in his long-term proposal. The short-term part starts at Anfield on Sunday for a manager who is short of sleep but high on optimism.
