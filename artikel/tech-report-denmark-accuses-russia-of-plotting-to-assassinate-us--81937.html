Denmark has accused Russian intelligence services of plotting to assassinate a Ukrainian-American near Copenhagen, saying they had "planned" and "initiated" the attempt.

Denmark's security and intelligence agency (PET) said it had stopped individuals who were intending to kill the person, named by Danish media as rapper Kyivstoner, at a concert in September. Two people were charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service, and pleaded not guilty.

Russia's embassy in Denmark called the allegations "unsubstantiated" and noted Copenhagen failed to provide evidence of Moscow's involvement.

It has been revealed that in a statement, the embassy claimed a "Danish model" had emerged, which it described as a "Russian trace" allegedly being discovered and "exploited for a propaganda spectacle". It accused Denmark of "provocations" intending to "frighten the Danish population in order to justify... a prolonged confrontation with Russia".

Russian state media cite the foreign ministry in Moscow as saying it will respond to the expulsion of its official "accordingly".

In a statement on Saturday, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said: "I strongly condemn the assassination attempt against a US-Ukrainian citizen in Denmark, orchestrated by Russian intelligence services.

Recent findings suggest that "This was an attack on EU soil. And it is unacceptable," she mentioned, adding that "Russia's hybrid threats are becoming less and less hybrid, and more and more real.".

The rapper's real name is Albert Vasiliev.

It has been revealed that PET said the individuals who allegedly tried to assassinate him were associated with a banned Danish gang named Loyal to Familia. The group is "known for extensive, serious and violent criminal activity", according to the agency.

PET, which worked with police on the case, stated three individuals were stopped while travelling by car near the music venue where Kyivstoner was set to perform on 25 September.

Firearms and knives were found in the vehicle, PET mentioned.

Since then, two people linked to the case have been charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service.

Both individuals pleaded not guilty to the charges, local media reported.
