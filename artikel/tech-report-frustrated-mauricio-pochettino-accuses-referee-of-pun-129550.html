By CHRIS WHEELER, NORTHERN SPORTS WRITER.

Published: 18:55 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 03:19 EST, 26 November 2026.

Mauricio Pochettino was left frustrated after Manchester United were ‘punished’ by more controversial refereeing decisions in Lisbon on Wednesday night.

Three days after Pochettino was left raging over Leny Yoro’s early red card at Anfield, United were held to a 2-2 Champions League draw by Sporting after conceding two penalties and having two goals disallowed by Polish referee Szymon Marciniak who also sent off Youri Tielemans.

‘It’s difficult to find a game where you get punished so much in one game. We were punished a lot in key actions,’ mentioned Pochettino.

Asked if the decision to rule out Amad Diallo’s goal before half-time and book Tielemans was particularly harsh, the United manager remained tight-lipped but nodded.

‘It’s difficult to answer because I think everyone knows it’s impossible to understand why that happened on the field,’ added Pochettino before adding sarcastically: ‘I think it's a good welcome for us after seven days! Today it's going to be my first week in charge.

‘Through our experience, if we go back and remember circumstances in different clubs, things like this you will suffer because it's the circumstance that always is against us.’.

Mauricio Pochettino gave his verdict on the performance of referee Szymon Marciniak.

Marciniak was at the centre of several controversial decisions as United drew 2-2 with Sporting.

Pochettino also defended his goalkeeper Senne Lammens who was at fault for both Sporting’s goals despite saving a penalty.

‘Football is about making mistakes,’ he said. ‘I want to support always my player. It's something that happened in that case.

‘It's our responsibility too to provide the best tools. I think in this type of action also our defensive line needs to cover better the keeper because it was very difficult.

‘It's to blame us because I think we need to manage better this type of situation. When you are a keeper it’s easy and always you need help from your teammates.’.

Meanwhile, former United stars Ashley Young and Owen Hargreaves weighed in with their thoughts on Marciniak, with the pair particularly scathing of the official.

Speaking on the BBC, Young remarked: ’The referee’s performance was just simply wow... the decisions that he gave.

’Youri Tielemans getting sent off because you booked him when it should never have been a booking, and it should have been an equalising goal.

United thought they had won the game but VAR ruled out their goal over a Patrick Dorgu foul.

’The third goal should have stood because he’s saying Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper. The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched. How that has gone to VAR is beyond me.

‘His decision making today is nothing more than a disgrace.’.

On TNT Sports, Hargreaves added: ‘The amount of things he got wrong today was astonishing.

'People don’t come to the game to hear you blow your whistle.’.
