Jamie Carragher indicates he ‘felt sorry’ for Matthijs de Ligt during Manchester United’s Premier League defeat to Liverpool.

Ten-man United were thumped 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game as Old Trafford manager.

The visitors were reduced to ten men inside ten minutes as Leny Yoro was shown a straight red card for bringing down Alexander Isak as he raced through on goal.

If that early blow was not bad enough, Liverpool took the lead from the subsequent free-kick as Dominik Szoboszlai smashed home yet another set-piece goal.

Manchester United were fearing an embarrassing battering when Florian Wirtz put Liverpool two goals ahead on 13 minutes with a neat finish from Rio Ngumoha’s cross.

England international Ngumoha, 18, turned provider to goalscorer in the second-half before Victor Munoz added Liverpool’s fourth late on.

Carragher was particularly critical of De Ligt after the match but noted he ‘felt sorry’ for the Manchester United centre-back who made his first start of the season at Anfield.

Industry observers note that ‘I thought it was a really strange decision,’ Carragher noted on Sky Sports when asked about Pochettino’s decision to start De Ligt. ‘It was such a bizarre decision.

‘Not because of his quality because I think he’s a better centre-back than Harry Maguire when you look at what he’s achieved in his career and the clubs he’s played for.

Sources indicate that ‘But he hasn’t played football so to play him at Anfield away, it felt so wrong. He was all over the place.

‘It got to the stage where I was actually feeling sorry for him and I was actually pleading with the manager to take him off and take him out of the firing line.

‘He’s played for some top football clubs – Bayern Munich, Ajax and Juventus – those are some of the biggest clubs in the world.

‘So to watch him perform like that… I felt sorry for him.’.

Yet another deflating result leaves Manchester United 19th in the Premier League with just eight points from 11 games.

When that fact was put to Bruno Fernandes, the United captain stated: ‘It’s very painful and it hurts a lot. It’s a difficult moment for us but we are the only ones who can get out of that situation.

‘You want to get results, but today it was not possible for us. There were a lot of things we can look at and I saw a lot of character from the team.

‘The result won’t show that but I am very proud of everyone in the dressing room.’.
