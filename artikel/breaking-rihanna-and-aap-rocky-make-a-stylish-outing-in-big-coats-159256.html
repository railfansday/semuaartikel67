It has been revealed that when it comes to stylish couples, Rihanna and A$AP Rocky are a match made in fashion heaven. Ever since their relationship turned romantic over five years ago, the pair have made it seem like they’ve been pulling from a his-and-hers closet. From their dramatic plush coats at the Met Gala to their matching olive moment on the Black Panther: Wakanda Forever red carpet, their coordinating looks are always on point. But, even more impressively, they always manage to complement one other without losing their individual sense of style.

On Monday afternoon, that impressive display continued as the stars were seen in Beverly Hills, California, where the dress code of the day appeared to be “open jackets and big pants.” It was the first time in weeks that the pair has been seen in public together, as A$AP has been on his Don’t Be Dumb Tour across Europe for the past couple of months.

For the outing, Rihanna curated a watermelon color scheme up top, layering a soft pink T-shirt underneath an army-green parka, presumably from Celine.

According to recent reports, the utility-style outerwear featured rows of pockets in front and a plush brown shearling collar that also lined the inside of the jacket and cuffs. Notably, Rihanna may have borrowed the coat from her stylist, Jahleel Weaver, as he posted an Instagram Story yesterday afternoon in which he was seen wearing the exact same one.

Recent findings suggest that to complete her fit, the “Diamonds” singer wore baggy white jeans and a pair of strappy white stilettos. Oh, and our queen of statement glasses added a set of cranberry-colored visor shades.

Industry observers note that for his part, Rocky stacked a dark-wash jean jacket and a creamy corduroy one over a black tee, completing the fit with obsidian wide-leg slacks and inky leather Oxfords. As for his sunglasses, the Ray-Ban creative director chose a pair of the brand’s classic aviators.

The couple’s outing comes days after their daughter, Rocki, made her high-fashion debut in a newly introduced Dior campaign, proving that every member of this family really is programmed to have incredible style.
