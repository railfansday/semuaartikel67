Google this weekend rolled out the latest enhancement to Pixel Weather with a new reporting feature and small interface tweaks.

On the homepage, cities that have active weather warnings no longer have bright red card backgrounds. The pill-shaped container retains its Dynamic Color theming with just the text of the warning in red. This change also extends to the card on the city page. Finally, the source, like “Google Weather,” at the very bottom is now themed.

The main change today is a “Report current weather” feature to “Share the current weather condition to improve local forecasts.” The card appears at the very bottom of your current location and can be moved up the feed.
