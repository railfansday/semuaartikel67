This is TVNewser’s basic cable network ranker and cable news report for the week of November 9, 2026.

As expected, the three cable networks could not maintain their midterm election-week momentum, declining in total viewers and the Adults 25-54 demo during primetime and total day.

Viewer interest remained high during the just-concluded week, driven by the breaking news of the FAA issuing a nationwide airplane groundstop on Friday, Nov. 13. Fox News was the most-watched network in total viewers on that day, while CNN finished on top in the prized demo.

Interestingly, the FAA breaking news kept CNN in first place in the primetime demo for the second week in a row.

According to recent reports, according to Nielsen Big Data + Panel, for the week of Nov. 9, Fox News averaged 1.584 million total viewers and 109,000 A25-54 viewers during primetime. The network saw losses of 15% in total viewers and 33% in the demo compared with the prior week (the week beginning Nov. 2).

Sources indicate that during total day viewing, Fox News averaged 1.018 million total viewers and 93,000 A25-54 viewers. Based on the previous week’s average, the network was down by 9% in total viewers and 25% in the demo.

Fox News remained second in total primetime viewership and slipped from third to fourth in demo viewership among all basic cable networks. During total day, it remained first in total viewers and was in third place in the total day demo.

It has been revealed that MS NOW averaged 1.077 million total viewers and 109,000 viewers in the A25-54 demo for the week that just concluded. The network dropped by 30% in total viewers and 52% in the demo compared with its prior-week primetime average.

During total day, MS NOW averaged 674,000 total viewers and 73,000 demo viewers. It was down 27% in total viewers and 42% in the demo compared with the network’s average the prior week.

Among all basic cable networks, MS NOW continued to be in third in total primetime viewers and fell from fourth to ninth in the demo. It ranked third in total viewers and was sixth in the total day demo.

In a recent development, CNN averaged 802,000 total primetime viewers and 160,000 demo viewers. It saw declines of 40% in total viewers and 57% in the demo compared to the previous week.

Sources indicate that during total day, the network had 530,000 total viewers and 85,000 demo viewers. For CNN, total viewers were down 29%, and demo viewers were down 49% during this daypart.

It has been revealed that among all cable networks, CNN remained in fourth place in total viewers and was the No. 3 network in the demo during primetime. It continued to be in fourth in total day among total viewers and was in the same position in the demo.

In a recent development, fox News held 9 of the 15 spots among the week’s most-watched cable news demonstrates, with The Five in first place with 2.562 million total viewers at 5 p.m. ET. MS NOW’s top show was the 9 p.m. ET Monday airing of The Rachel Maddow Show in fourth place with 1.904 million total viewers. MS NOW had a total of six programs in the top 15 for the just-concluded week.

Sources indicate that the 15 top spots in the Adults 25-54 demo were populated with highlights from all three cable networks. CNN’s Anderson Cooper 360, airing weeknights at 8 p.m. ET, was in first place with 229,000 demo viewers. CNN had a total of five shows in the top 15. Fox News’ Jesse Watters Primetime, airing weeknights at 8 p.m. ET, finished second with 223,000 demo viewers. Fox News had seven shows on the chart. Finally, MS NOW’s The Rachel Maddow Show was the network’s top show, with 166,000 demo viewers, ranking 12th. MS NOW had four shows in the chart.
