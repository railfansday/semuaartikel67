GRAND CAYMAN, Cayman Islands, Nov. 24, 2026. Stacks Labs today revealed 1,050 BTC of capacity for Bond 4 of Bitcoin staking with Stacks, a Bitcoin layer where BTC holders earn BTC. The bond starts ~Dec. 5, and institutions can register at stacks.co/institutional-bitcoin-staking.

In a recent development, bond 4 carries a 3% target annual yield, paid in BTC weekly over a six-month term. Each BTC staked is paired with STX worth 5% of its value, so filling Bond 4’s 1,050 BTC of capacity requires a corresponding STX commitment. Rewards come from Stacks miners, who commit BTC through Proof of Transfer (PoX), the consensus mechanism Stacks has run since January 2021.

It has been revealed that every bond since the Genesis Bond launched in September has filled. 21shares returned for Bond 3, and Bullish (NYSE: BLSH) joined as the first top-10 public Bitcoin treasury organization to stake BTC.

“Opening more than 1,000 BTC of fresh capacity for institutions reveals how strong the demand for Bitcoin yield has become,” mentioned Muneeb Ali, CEO, Stacks Labs, and founder of Stacks.

Institutions can stake from qualified custody, including BitGo. During the bootstrap phase, self-custodial Bitcoin staking is whitelisted. Holders who are not on the whitelist can take part through pooled Bitcoin staking at stacks.co/bitcoin-staking.

Industry observers note that stacks is a Bitcoin layer where BTC holders earn BTC. With Bitcoin staking, holders stake BTC with STX, and their BTC stays on the Bitcoin L1 under their own custody. Stacks has distributed over 4,200 BTC in rewards to participants since 2021. Learn more at stacks.co.
