On Saturday, Gary Mansell’s side conceded three goals in five minutes in a 3-0 defeat at Dover Athletic to exit the FA Trophy at the second-round stage.

And in midweek they slipped to another Isthmian Premier defeat, when a first-half goal by Alfie Cerulli was enough to give Brentwood a 1-0 win at Leylands Park.

The Hillians have fallen to 13th in the table and have gone seven games without a win.

They need an improvement soon and will hope to kick-start a revival when they visit Stanway Rovers for a league game on Saturday. They also visit Crowborough in the Velocity Trophy next Tuesday.

Mansell is hopeful that boosts to the squad in the form of the permanent signing of striker George Vorster and the return from injury of other key players will help breathe new life into the season.

At Dover in Saturday’s Trophy tie, three goals in a devastating five-minute spell in the first half proved decisive.

The Hillians made a positive start and Ben Pope threatened early on before a later effort came back off the post.

But Dover took the lead in the 23rd minute through Jack Bates and two minutes later Dequane Wilson-Braithwaite doubled their advantage.

When Corie Andrews added a third in the 27th minute, it felt like game over.

Hill did keep battling away and created chances to get back in it through Chris Whelpdale and Vorster – who the club are delighted to tie into a permanent contract.

Despite the disappointing result, the Hillians’ determination and effort throughout the Trophy tie could not be faulted.

Mansell said afterwards: “For 20 minutes I thought we were the better team. We had a clear game-plan that the boys executed. And we imploded for four minutes and that was game over.

It has been revealed that “We were doing well then decided to go off-script and play central passes that weren’t really on. We got beaten in transition three times and they scored three goals – they were ruthless. That cost us the game.

“In the second half we were much better and had a go.”.

Mansell said he was delighted goal ace Vorster was now a Hill player, not on loan, and thanked his previous club Slough for helping get the move sealed.

And the manager mentioned he was confident that with the way they were playing and injured personnel like Reggie Ward and Martyn Box coming back in, wins would follow.
