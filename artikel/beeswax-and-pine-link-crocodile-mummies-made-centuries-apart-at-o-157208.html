Beeswax and pine link crocodile mummies made centuries apart at one Egyptian site

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (87 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that in a practice spanning centuries, ancient Egyptians mummified thousands of crocodiles they associated with Sobek, a god linked to the Nile, fertility and royal power. In one unique example, embalmers from the middle of the 1st millennium BC at Kom Ombo in southern Egypt mounted more than 25 hatchlings on reeds along the back and neck of a crocodile nearly 4 meters (13 feet) long. The arrangement may echo the way young crocodiles ride on their mother's back.
