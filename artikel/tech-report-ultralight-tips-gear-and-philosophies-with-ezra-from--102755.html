In today’s episode of Backpacker Radio presented by The Trek, brought to you by Topo Athletic, we are joined by Ezra known as Extra on trail- who’s a Triple Crowner who recently completed his third thru-hike of the PCT and a shares his obsession with super ultralight backpacking on his Extra Ultralight YouTube channel.

In this one, Extra walks us through his evolution from a teenage disciple of Ray Jardine to carrying a four-and-a-half-pound base weight on the CDT, dives into a lucid dreaming obsession that left him struggling to tell the difference between dreams and reality, and explains how eating almost entirely nuts for a year and a half went from a nutritional holy grail to disaster pants.

We also cover why his luxury item is a scalpel, the tradeoffs of a five-and-a-half-ounce poncho that doubles as his shelter, and how he wrestles with anxiety of returning to traffic, phones, and grocery stores after months on trail.

We wrap the show with Trek Propaganda on Peg Leg’s rain-soaked Arizona Trail adventures, whether the AT is actually overcrowded, we introduce This Week on Trail, a brand latest podcast from Jess and Katie!, we invent new national holidays, the Triple Crown of movie quotes, and dive into some more feedback on our crying-baby-at-a-campground debate.
