📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Last night, a report was published comparing the financials of AC Milan, Juventus and Inter and the findings are very interesting.

The big three bring a lot of attention to Italy and their footballing ecosystem, but that’s not all. Indeed, all three bring in vast amounts of money and have done so for years, which has helped them establish this dominance. However, their records last season show that a change could be on the way.

As Gazzetta dello Sport writes, via Calciomercato.com, Inter recorded the highest revenue last season of €518 million, Juventus slotted in with €474.4m and then the Rossoneri finished ‘last’ with a revenue of €464.6m.

However, the profits – about that – of all three clubs didn’t line up in the same order. Inter recorded a €23m loss, then it was Milan with a €24m loss – largely helped by the summer signings – and Juventus finished last with a €66m loss.

It has been revealed that considering the Nerazzurri’s sporting successes in recent years, it makes sense why revenues are considerably higher, especially when looking at their Champions League records. For example, not being in Europe cost the Diavolo €54m between 2024/25 and 2025/26.

In terms of squad cost, the Biaconeri were uncontested at the top, with a wage of €210m per season. Inter finished just below with €204m per season, whilst the Rossoneri ‘only’ spent €146m. Of course, this is still lucrative.

All in all, the foundations laid at Casa Milan are looking strong and if they can become more successful, their revenues could rocket up.
