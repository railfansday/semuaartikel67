There’s no stopping the Grand Theft Auto VI (via Atlantic/Warner) soundtrack, as it motors to No. 1 on the ARIA Albums Chart.

Spanning 34 tracks, including songs by Dua Lipa, Justice, Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen and more, GTA is the first soundtrack to top the national albums tally in 2026.

Meanwhile, its focus track “Flower In A Hurricane” (Ed Banger/Universal) by Justice, Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard And The Lizard Wizard roars 17-5 on the national singles chart, for Tame Impala’s third top 10 this year following “Dracula” and “Loser,” and marking a first-ever appearance in the top 50 for King Gizzard and Justice. Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest video game drop of the year, or any year, raking in almost $4 billion globally on its first day of release.

Recent findings suggest that following the release of the deluxe version of Daughter From Hell (Interscope/Universal), featuring four new tracks, Gracie Abrams’ third album bounces 26-6. Daughter From Hell logged one week at No. 1 in late July, for her second leader after 2024’s The Secret Of Us.

The all-star Forever Young (Virgin Music Group/Inertia) project from War Child Australia, the domestic arm of the international War Child organization, opens its account at No. 12. The fundraising collection houses songs by Paul Kelly, Missy Higgins, Ruel, Sarah Blasko, The Temper Trap, King Gizz, Grace Cummings, and a new, Paul Mac remix of Youth Group’s cover of Alphaville’s “Forever Young.” It’s the first ever benefit album for War Child Australia, with 100% of proceeds supporting children living through conflict.

Industry observers note that other fresh releases on this week’s albums tally, published Friday, Nov. 27, including Trivium’s Crown In The Grave (at No. 31 via Roadrunner/Warner), the soundtrack to The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (No. 38 via MCA/Universal), and Down’s Volume V (No. 45 via Nuclear Blast/ADA), while Ninajirachi’s debut LP I Love My Computer (Orchard) returns to the top 40, at No. 40, after the Aussie producer and DJ won best dance/electronic release for “WannaCry” (shared with Porter Robinson) at the 2026 ARIA Awards.

Sources indicate that over on the ARIA Singles Chart, Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” (Sony) returns to No. 1 for its 17th non-consecutive cycle at the summit. With that result, it’s tied with The Kid Laroi and Justin Bieber’s “Stay” and Alex Warren’s “Ordinary” for the third longest run at the top. Olivia Dean’s “Man I Need” is second with 18 weeks, while the all-time champ is Tones And I’s “Dance Monkey” with 24 total weeks at No. 1.

No newly introduced debutants impact the latest ARIA Top 50 Singles Chart.
