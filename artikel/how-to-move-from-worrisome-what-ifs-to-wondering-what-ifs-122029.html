How to Move From Worrisome What-Ifs to Wondering What-Ifs

If you're tired of feeling like you're never quite enough, or that you're never going to get "there" (wherever there is), consider the following questions.

Instead of skimming through these questions the way you might read a typical blog post, I'd like to suggest that you wait until you have a quiet moment. Sit or lie down. Read the words silently or out loud, and really consider them. Try them on, the way you might try on a piece of clothing to see how they fit.

Notice what happens inside your body as you read. See if any of these ideas can sink in. If they can't right now, that's OK, too.

Sometimes old programming runs deep and can feel hard to change. With patience and practice, old beliefs can be questioned and updated, and new ways of thinking and being can take root.

So get comfortable, take a slow, deep breath, and consider these possibilities.

What if I remembered that self-critical thoughts aren't necessarily facts, and I didn't have to believe everything I think?

What if I could begin choosing newly introduced thoughts and beliefs that are kinder, more supportive, and actually feel good?

What if I got off my own back, got on my own side, and decided that I am a good person doing the best I can?

What if I remembered that kindness, compassion, and encouragement help people grow, while berating, pressure, and perfectionism often get in the way?

What if my worth didn't depend on how much I accomplished, how productive I was, or how well I did things?

What if I allowed myself to do things imperfectly, and let good enough be good enough?

What if I could let myself be a beginner, make mistakes, change my mind, and learn as I go, rather than expecting myself to get everything right every time?

What if I spent less time living in worst-case scenarios about the future and more time in the present moment?

What if I slowed down, ever so slightly, and sprinkled small moments of conscious breathing and quiet awareness throughout my day?

What if I stepped out of the never-ending race to get somewhere and spent more moments in actual, factual reality?

What if I softened my gaze when I looked at myself and softened my thoughts when I thought about myself?

What if I spent less time comparing myself to others, remembering that I know nothing about what their lives actually feel like on the inside, and instead offered myself more moments of grace?

What if I became curious about what enoughness feels like?

What if I decided that, in this exact moment, I am already enough? I can still do what I'm moved to do, pursue what matters to me, and take care of what needs to be done without staying trapped on the endless treadmill of never-enoughness.

What if I simply noted, Thank you for what I have, kept the welcome mat out, and stayed open to whatever comes next?

What if, just for today, I let myself be enough?
