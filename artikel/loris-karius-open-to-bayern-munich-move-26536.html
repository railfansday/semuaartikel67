Schalke goalkeeper Loris Karius has not ruled out a move to Bayern Munich, according to Sport Bild, who report that the 33-year-old could imagine himself becoming the No.2 to Jonas Urbig, with both Manuel Neuer’s (40) and Sven Ulreich’s (38) contracts set to expire in 2027.

Karius’ contract, though, doesn’t expire until 2028, meaning Bayern would have to negotiate with Schalke, who have no intention of selling their No.1 goalkeeper, who has received four Man of the Match awards this season upon the Royal Blues’ return to Germany’s top flight after a three-year absence.

Sky Germany earlier reported that there’s a 99.9 percent chance that Neuer will retire when his contract expires at the end of the season.

Should Karius move to Munich, he would “live significantly closer to his family, who live in Milan.” However, there are other names on sporting director Max Eberl’s shortlist, including Hoffenheim’s Oliver Baumann (36) and Lens’ Robin Risser (21).
