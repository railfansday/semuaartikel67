I realize this is a hiking website, but as a hiking newb, I do not have a library of scenic selfies to choose from. Until now, my outdoor leisure time was spent on or near the water. So much so that after 25 years as a college professor I went to Sea School, got my USCG Captain’s License, and have been running charter boats for the past five years. I have now decided a land-based adventure is in order, so here we are.

In a recent development, as a hiker, to say I’m a beginner would be a disservice to beginners. I’ve never camped outside a campground and the last time I carried a backpack with any regularity was books in college. But I’m going to give this AT thing a go and try to share my newbie experiences along the way.

It has been revealed that so far I have way more questions than answers. My first few posts will address some of these questions where I’ll explain my reasoning, which way I’m leaning, and open the door for input.
