Recent findings suggest that the essence of the best grilled cheese lies in the delicious combination of melted cheese and crisped bread. There are many methods to accomplish such a pairing, with plenty of tips to give you the best results imaginable. If you're after a version that's especially ooey-gooey, try making the sandwich with cheddar cheese spread.

In a recent development, available from several brands at widespread grocery stores like Walmart, cheddar cheese spread is essentially cheddar mixed with butter. The foodstuff is easily spreadable on bread and melts when it's heated, making a grilled cheese with a pleasantly runny interior. While it's creamier than classic cheddar, the flavor is no afterthought, with this cheese's sharpness still perceptible.

Using butter alongside cheese is common in grilled cheese recipes; the spread lets you conveniently lather on both components at once. The fat enhances the bread's crispiness, all while retaining a moist interior. As a sole filling, the spread turns the dish luxuriously decadent — but complemented with a few slices of cheese, it's a grilled cheese rendition to remember.

Industry observers note that there are many international grilled cheese styles to explore; cheddar cheese spread fits into a wide range of renditions. Combined with a store-bought loaf of sourdough and sliced cheddar, the spread makes a mouth-watering classic. However, you can consider layering in other ingredients, too — thereby making a cheddar grilled cheese with a twist.

For example, the cheese's sharpness welcomes a sweet contrast. Spread a jam or jelly into the sandwich, whether a rich fig jam, tart cranberry preserves, or a sweet blackberry jam. For a sweet touch with less sugar, you could also use fresh fruits to upgrade any grilled cheese; the spread would go great with apples, for example, as apples and cheddar are a match made in sandwich heaven. And you could also consider leaning into the spice, smearing on spicy brown mustard or hot pepper jelly for a light dose of heat.

Industry observers note that consider trying out other ingredient add-ins, too. Caramelized onions will lend a grilled cheese a subtle nutty sweetness, while bacon bits bring a savory touch. Cheddar cheese spreads allow the many different flavors to come together, letting you easily craft a sandwich to your tastes.
