It has been revealed that beamable is the backend and LiveOps infrastructure platform for game developers, the system that lets studios build, operate, and scale live games without constructing the underlying architecture themselves. Backend services, identity, analytics, meta-game systems, virtual economies, and AI-native tooling: all integrated into a single platform that eliminates the point-solution stitching that has defined game development for too long. Beamable also powers Wingman, an agentic LiveOps product that gives game teams a unified operating system to understand, optimize, and act on live game performance in real time. This is the future of how games are operated.

Beamable is part of FIRY, a founder-led global technology portfolio operating at the intersection of gaming, AI-powered performance marketing, and developer infrastructure. Acquired in January 2026, and together with Skillz, our competition platform, and RZR, our AI-driven ad network, Beamable sits at the foundation of a compounding ecosystem that no standalone competitor can replicate. If you want to build the infrastructure that games run on at a company with the vision and resources to back it — Beamable is the right place to do it.

Industry observers note that beamable is seeking a Head of Product to shape its platform vision and lead its next phase of growth in the game backend market. This is an opportunity to build the product function from the ground up, guide the platform from early access through public release, and advance AI integration to deliver meaningful value for game studios.

As the firm’s senior product leader and sole product practitioner, you will own product strategy, roadmap, launch milestones, and success metrics, combining strategic direction with hands-on execution. You will work directly with studios and design partners, represent Beamable’s product vision at industry events, and translate customer insights into clear priorities. Success in this role means delivering a successful public launch, developing early-access studios into reference customers, and establishing a product foundation that drives adoption and sustained revenue growth.

Total Starting Compensation including Base + Bonus + Equity: $212,625 + Bonus + Equity.

Location: Remote - CA, NV, or NY.

Travel Requirement: Less than 10%.
