Amazon’s early Black Friday sale keeps growing, and we’ve now found 51 deals that tie or beat their lowest prices of 2026. Amazon’s early Black Friday deals started November 1, and newly introduced waves since then have added espresso machines, Ring and Blink cameras, Lego, laptops and more cordless tools. Most of them run through the end of the month, though a few kitchen deals end before Thanksgiving. We checked every price in this post against Amazon’s own deal data Monday evening, November 9.

The Black+Decker 20V Max brushless drill kit comes with a 2Ah battery, a charger and a two-speed gearbox, and at $59.99 it’s $20 below the lowest price Amazon charged for it earlier this year. Black+Decker rates it at 486 inch-pounds of torque, plenty for furniture assembly, shelf brackets and pilot holes in studs. For more options, see our guide to the best cordless drills.

A battery spotlight camera for under $90.

Recent findings suggest that the Ring Spotlight Cam 2K (Battery) costs $89.99, down from $169.99 and tying its lowest price of 2026. It runs on a battery, so you can mount it on a fence, a shed or a garage corner that’s nowhere near an outlet. Ring lists 2K video, 6x enhanced zoom and motion-activated spotlights that flip on when something moves in the yard at night. We rounded up more Ring discounts in our Ring early Black Friday deals post, and our guide to the best outdoor security cameras covers other brands.

No freezer bowl to chill overnight.

Recent findings suggest that the Cuisinart ICE-90 ice cream maker costs $169.95, its lowest price of 2026 and about $18 under its earlier low. Cuisinart indicates it needs no pre-freezing, so there’s no bowl taking up freezer space the night before, and it makes up to a quart at a time. It also does frozen yogurt, which makes it an easy gift for anyone who already has every kitchen gadget.

A 1,088-piece Phantom Menace build for $84.

Recent findings suggest that the Lego Star Wars Battle Droid with STAP costs $83.99, down from $139.99 and tying its lowest price of 2026. Before this sale started November 9, its lowest price in the previous 30 days was $116.99. The 1,088-piece set builds a battle droid riding its STAP speeder from The Phantom Menace, and Lego includes a stand and plaque so it can live on a shelf instead of in a toy bin. Lego rates it for ages 12 and up, which makes it a gift for adult Star Wars fans as much as for kids.
