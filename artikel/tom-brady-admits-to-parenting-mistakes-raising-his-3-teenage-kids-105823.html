Tom Brady knows that all parents drop the ball sometimes.

Since becoming dad to son Jack, 19, with ex-girlfriend Bridget Moynahan, as well as son Benjamin, 16, and daughter Vivian, 13, with ex-wife Gisele Bündchen, the seven-time Super Bowl champ admitted there are some losses among his many wins as a parent.

“Well, I’ve made a lot of mistakes,” Brady stated on the Nov. 22 episode of the High Performance podcast. “So, a lot like quarterback. I threw a lot of interceptions. And with every one I’ve tried to learn.”.

Sources indicate that and with that effort, the 49-year-old added, “I hope I’m a better parent today than I was when I first started having kids. And hopefully I’m a better parent 10 years from now.”.

So, what is Brady’s game proposal for becoming the best dad he can be?

According to recent reports, “The important thing for me in my personal life is to go where my kids are at, too,” he explained. “Not to say, ‘Hey kids, pay attention to the things I'm doing or what's important to me.’ It’s been a really important lesson for me over the years to go to my kids and say, ‘What’s really important to you? What’s driving you? Or what are you scared of, or how can I pay attention to the things that you value?’”.

Finding ways to connect with his kids is a skill Brady mentioned he developed from leading football teams during his 23 seasons as an NFL quarterback.

“Part of that is just being really open and present and also trying to be really relatable to the younger [generation],” he continued, “which I was very fortunate to learn over the course of my playing career.”.

And now, Brady emphasized that he is simply trying to be “really present” and “non-judgmental” with his kids while imparting the wisdom he’s scored through his “years of experience.”.

As he put it, he is “just trying to understand their perspective, but also trying to get them to understand my perspective a little bit, too.”.
