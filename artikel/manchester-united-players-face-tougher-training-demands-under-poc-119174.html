Manchester United players have reportedly been told to expect a further increase in training demands under Mauricio Pochettino after Sunday’s Premier League match against Liverpool.

According to recent reports, the new manager arrived at Carrington on Wednesday following his departure from the United States national team and has had less than four days to prepare for his first match in charge. According to The Sun’s report, published on Saturday evening, United’s players have already noticed longer and more intense training sessions compared with those under former manager Michael Carrick.

Recent findings suggest that the newspaper reports that Pochettino has initially concentrated on introducing his tactical approach, with more demanding physical work expected once the Liverpool fixture is over. An unnamed source claimed the players had already felt a significant change in tempo and workload, despite the Argentine having only a limited period to work with the squad.

Pochettino is also reported to have taken a hands-on approach during training, regularly stopping drills to explain his instructions directly to players. The report adds that the 54-year-old has been communicating in English with United’s staff and has asked his predominantly Spanish-speaking coaching team to do likewise.

Pochettino’s first assignment is Sunday’s visit to Anfield, with United looking to improve on a difficult opening to the Premier League season. The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager conducted an extended training session on Saturday as preparations for the fixture continued.

However, the reported emphasis on fitness and conditioning suggests that his wider changes at Carrington may become more apparent after the Liverpool match. United have not publicly confirmed the details of the training programme described in the report.

Today’s game kicks off at 4.30pm GMT.
