Duetti Secures $665 Million Financing Round

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The financing comes in the form of a $450 million ABS anchored by Carlyle, $165 million of additional credit from Duetti’s longtime bank, Viola Credit, and a $50 million side facility from Flexpoint Ford and Truist Bank. Flexpoint Ford is also an equity investor in Duetti.

Sources indicate that fast-growing Duetti has raised $1.3 billion since its launch in 2022 from equity investors like The Raine Group, Nyca Partners, Viola Ventures and ROC Nation, as well as from the debt markets. This securitization, worth more than three times Duetti’s last ABS of $125 million confirmed in January and issued with a higher rating, is a sign that institutional investors view Duetti as a maturing and stable business.

“We anchored this securitization because independent music royalties can generate the kind of recurring, diversified cash flows that asset-backed finance is built around,” Akhil Bansal, Carlyle’s head of asset-backed finance, mentioned in a statement. “Duetti pairs industry expertise with the data and technology to build and manage catalogs at scale, and that’s what gives us conviction in the platform.”.

Carlyle’s asset-backed finance team sits within its global credit platform, which has deployed more than $17 billion into the sports, media and entertainment sectors since 2017.

Duetti’s portfolio includes 50,000 master recordings and compositions by more than 3,000 recording artists, and the company continues to do around 80 transactions per month. The capital from this round of financing will be used to “significantly accelerate the pace and breadth of investment activities across genres and geographies, as well as different types of music rights,” the company said in a statement.

“Our third ABS transaction in just two years is more than five times the size of our inaugural deal in 2024, demonstrating our rapid growth, strong track record and increasing institutional demand for our catalog and business model,” Duetti CEO and co-founder Lior Tibon remarked in a statement.

Barclays, which handled both Duetti’s prior ABS transactions, co-led placement on this issuance with Truist.

“This latest transaction achieved a ratings upgrade and was placed with a broader group of institutional investors than the Company’s prior issuances, marking the continued expansion of its ABS program and investor base,” Salina Sabri, managing director at Barclays, remarked in a statement.
