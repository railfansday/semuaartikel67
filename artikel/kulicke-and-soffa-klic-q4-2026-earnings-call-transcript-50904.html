Kulicke and Soffa (KLIC -8.08%) reported significant growth in revenue and profitability for the fiscal fourth quarter, supported by a resurgence in ball-bonding demand and the rapid scaling of its Advanced Solutions portfolio. Management stated that artificial intelligence infrastructure investments are driving increased packaging complexity in logic and photonics while supporting high-volume wire bonding for memory and power management devices. The company is tripling its Advanced Solutions manufacturing capacity in Singapore to support projected demand for fluxless thermo-compression bonding and hybrid bonding. Management expects the served available market to expand from $2.7 billion to more than $4.5 billion by fiscal 2030 as industry transitions toward heterogeneous packaging accelerate.
