📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

I am not the first person to point out that MacBooks are expensive. I will not be the last. If you monitor this stuff like we do, you’ll have seen the irony that Apple released the budget-conscious MacBook Neo earlier this year—only for the global computing shortage to almost immediately raise the price from $599 to $699.

But the Neo is still cheap. You could buy it for full price and get your money’s worth—although scoring it on sale makes the whole thing even sweeter. As part of its admittedly impressive Black Friday sale, Amazon shaved $50 off of the 256 GB version and $100 off of the 512 GB model with Touch ID. Chalk it up to a Christmas miracle.

Apple 2026 MacBook Neo (256 GB).

Apple 2026 MacBook Neo (512 GB).

Industry observers note that designed as Apple’s answer to the lower-budget laptops running Windows, the Neo’s target audience is technically students—hence the good branding and cool colors. That being said, if you need a personal computer for watching Netflix and doing your taxes but don’t want a full desktop, it’s a perfect choice.

According to recent reports, the Neo runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip—the same one that powers the iPhone 16 Pro and Pro Max. If the idea of buying a laptop that runs on a phone chip gives you pause, your stress is better channeled elsewhere. While this isn’t the right device for someone who is planning to run heavy-duty software, it can handle standard Internet browsing, video calls, and the like with ease. Just keep in mind that it has only 8 GB of RAM, so it’s best suited to basic workflows. And in addition to double the storage space, the 512 GB model also features Touch ID.

Of course, this computer has the added benefit of four jazzy color options: blush and citrus for the bold, indigo for those more subdued, and silver for that classic MacBook aesthetic. Wrapped in an aluminum body with a 13-inch Liquid Retina display, the Neo weighs in at just 2.7 pounds (same as the MacBook Air) and is 0.5 inches thick. (For reference, the iPhone 17 Pro Max is 0.35 inches thick.).

Sources indicate that despite our best efforts, we don’t quite know how Apple determines its deals for major sale events. Other MacBooks—including the brand-new M6 and touchscreen MacBook Pros—are up to 23 percent off for Black Friday, but barring a Cyber Monday surprise, it looks like this discount is as good as it’s going to get for the Neo right now.
