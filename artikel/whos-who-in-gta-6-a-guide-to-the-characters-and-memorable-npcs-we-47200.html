📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto VI follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as a botched job drags them into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. But the most interesting details emerge from the people they encounter along the way — from smugglers in the Keys to rappers chasing stardom in Vice City.

It has been revealed that we've compiled the characters we know about so far, including those Rockstar introduced before launch, and other GTA 6 NPCs who've appeared in reviews, gameplay clips, and our own playthroughs. We'll keep updating this list as we discover more.

Spoiler alert: This article contains plot and character details about the missions, side quests, and characters of Grand Theft Auto VI.

Jason Duval — A former soldier who works for drug runners in the Leonida Keys. He wants an easier life, but when a job goes wrong, he and Lucia get caught up in a much bigger criminal conspiracy. He's also a pretty good dancer.

Lucia Caminos — Jason's partner in crime. Her father taught her to fight growing up, and after a stint in Leonida Penitentiary, she's determined to build a better life for herself.

Sources indicate that brian Heder — A veteran smuggler who runs his operation out of a boatyard and lets Jason live rent-free in exchange for helping with jobs. His wife Lori is also involved.

Cal Hampton — Jason's friend and one of Brian's associates. He listens in on Coast Guard communications and has questions about why birds fly in formation.

Donnie — A member of Brian's crew who helps with drug and package runs.

Mandy — One of Brian's tenants. Jason goes to collect her overdue rent in the early mission "Pipework" and winds up helping with repairs. Their flirty exchange rapidly made Mandy a launch-day fan favorite.

Shirley — Jason's neighbor in Key Lento. Her first request is a bottle of rum. Her second is a little more involved.

In a recent development, peggy — Shirley's neighbor and the owner of the scooter Shirley wants Jason to steal. Peggy comes out to confront him when he tries to take it.

Dre'Quan Priest — An aspiring music mogul who books acts at Boobie’s club and signs Real Dimez.

Boobie Ike — The owner of the Jack of Hearts strip club and Dre'Quan's business partner at Only Raw Records.

In a recent development, bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad — High school friends who perform together as Real Dimez. They built a following with rap tracks and social media.

Raul Bautista — A seasoned bank robber always looking for people willing to take a chance on his next job.

It has been revealed that captain Aldo Méndez — A Vice City police officer who knows Jason and Lucia and takes part in a raid on Raymond's operation. Raymond — Jason and Lucia cross paths with him on a package pickup at the Venture Apartments.

Andrés de León — A VIP Jason and Lucia are hired to protect during a visit to Megamundo's U.S. headquarters.

Petra Navarro — Megamundo's chief operating officer, who appears in the Netflix extended gameplay preview.

Valentina — A fixer who appears in the Megamundo storyline.

Counselor Stefanie Gates — Lucia's correctional social worker at Leonida Penitentiary.

Bret Vogel — Jason's old Army friend, who teaches him to track deer and sends him on hunts.

Kenton Rees — Hires Jason to dive at a shipwreck.

Billy — A contact Jason can meet through a mud club, with several jobs that follow.

Andy — A character Lucia meets through the Cellerica side missions.

Industry observers note that A very angry woman on a mobility scooter — An older woman appears to hold someone at gunpoint from her scooter. When the player steps in, she turns the gun on them, too.

A furry in a fox suit — While driving around as Lucia, a reviewer for Dexerto saw a police helicopter shine its spotlight on a man in a fox costume. They ended up helping him and his friends escape. Apparently, furries are where Leonida draws the line. (Seriously, there's apparently an anti-furry association in the game.).

A Hulk-like red giant — IShowSpeed ran into a towering red figure who tossed people and cars around and took on the police. The encounter reportedly ended with a hug before the giant leapt into the sky. Fans pointed out the plotline's connection with the villain in Captain America: Brave Upcoming World.

According to recent reports, animal rights protestors — During one part of the game, a group stops a truck taking a bear to a zoo. In a separate encounter, an animal rights protest leads to the player helping free a group of caged apes. GTA 6 gameplay prevents you from killing any protestors (yes, people tried).

Recent findings suggest that parrot smugglers at a liquor store — One reviewer for Fast Company came across a parrot-smuggling operation inside a liquor store. There's a side quest I wouldn’t have thought to look for, but GTA 6 is full of unexpected animal encounters.

According to recent reports, leonida's very own Bigfoot — Players on Reddit and X have been posting sightings of the Skunk Ape. In several clips, they find him near a dirt road with a deer carcass.
