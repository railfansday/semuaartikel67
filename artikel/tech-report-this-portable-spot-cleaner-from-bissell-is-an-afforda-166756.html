A carpet cleaner may not be your first thought for a Black Friday purchase, but it’s actually a great time to buy one, since one of my favorite new cleaners I’ve tested is on sale. The Bissell Little Green Mini Portable Carpet Cleaner is $85 at Amazon, down from its usual $130 price. That’s a $45 savings and, according to Amazon, its lowest price in 365 days. That’s considerably cheaper than the Shark StainForce, which goes for around $200 and is $130 for Black Friday.

The Bissell Little Green cleaner offers tons of cleaning power in a small package at a reasonable price, though there are one or two catches regarding portability and setup that are worth knowing about before you decide which one to buy.

The Bissell Little Green Mini is a powerful portable spot cleaner. / CNET/Ajay Kumar.

The Bissell Little Green Mini is a corded portable spot cleaner, but it’s still lightweight at 7 pounds. Setup is definitely a little more involved, with a clean water tank that you need to mix cleaning solution into and the need to have an AC outlet somewhere in the area. The trade-off is a significantly deeper, more powerful cleaning than what you get with a handheld battery-powered spot cleaner like the Shark StainForce. Using the Little Green on the toughest and oldest dried stains left by my pukey old man cat left me impressed with just how deeply it cleaned my carpets. The pristine result makes this corded cleaner well worth the extra setup, and even at full price ($130), you won’t find fault with its power or portability.

At $85, its lowest price in a year on Amazon, the Bissell Little Green Mini is an easy buy if you have pets and carpet.

Shark StainForce cleaning a stain off my carpet. / Ajay Kumar/CNET.

The Shark StainForce is a unique handheld vacuum because it doesn’t just clean dry messes — it’s primarily intended to remove stains and wet messes. It resembles other handheld vacuums, but comes with a caddy and a custom Shark stain cleaner that mixes a deep cleaner and oxy cleaner in the spray bottle to lift stains.

It works incredibly well — I was blown away by how easily it lifted the worst messes off my white carpets. It removed yellow cat puke stains, wine stains and tomato sauce off both the carpet and couch. It works equally well for older dried stains and wet stains, using a mix of the stain cleaner spray and agitation with the included rubber scrubbing brush head that suctions up the mess at the same time. There’s also a smaller nozzle-brush cleaner included for tight spots.

The StainForce will suction up dry waste like cat hair and chunks of half-digested cat food, but the waste tank is fairly small, and it’s not really meant for dry cleaning of a large area due to the limited battery life.

Industry observers note that the tank will also need to be cleaned out and dried after every use because wet messes will fester and smell if left too long. With all that mentioned, this is the only portable spot cleaner I’ve seen with the design and usability of a handheld vacuum, which makes it an excellent choice if your primary need is portable spot cleaning. The $200 price may sound steep, but I’ve never seen a compact cleaner as good at tackling such tough stains.

I love the Shark StainForce for its service as a grab-and-go cleaner. It’s cordless, it lives in its caddy ready to go, and for a fresh spill I can spray, scrub, and suction in under a minute with no setup at all. I’ve used it on my cats’ worst messes, including hairballs, vomit from eating too fast, and dirty paws tracked in from the litter box. It works remarkably well for something so small and it’s still the go-to cleaner for my wife and I. The dual-spray bottle that comes with it does an incredible job of lifting stains, even from my white carpet.

Sources indicate that here I am scrubbing away with the StainForce powered on. You can still see some stain, but it’s going away.

Recent findings suggest that the Little Green Mini is a different kind of machine, and that difference highlights in the results. On a day when my cats were in rare form and decided to throw up on the bedroom carpet three times in a row, I knew a small portable cleaner like the StainForce wouldn’t cut it, not with the sheer quantity of debris they’d left behind.

Recent findings suggest that I pulled the Bissell Little Green, which, worth acknowledging, is corded. That means more setup: fill the 16-ounce clean tank with water and the included Oxy formula, plug in the 12-foot cord, and work within reach of the cord and its 4-foot hose. It’s still light at just 7 pounds, and it has a useful handle for moving it from room to room.

The StainForce, by contrast, has no clean tank at all. You spray from a separate bottle and only a tiny dirty-water collection tank of under 5 ounces, with about 15 minutes of battery before it needs a recharge. The Mini’s clean and dirty tanks (16 and 13.5 ounces) are much bigger, and because it’s plugged in, it never runs out of power mid-clean.

According to recent reports, that proved to be the lifesaver in cleaning my cat’s horrible messes. The Bissell generously saturated the stain with its built-in sprayer that can dispense water and cleaning solution straight from the hose. The hose digs in deep, rinsing and pulling stains, mess and dirt from deep out of the carpet layers. The larger chunks were safely suctioned into the dirty tank, along with the used water.

According to recent reports, what I can say is that it did an excellent job of deep-cleaning stains from the carpet — noticeably deeper than the StainForce. It completely removed our cat’s mess, with no trace left behind, though our carpets were left a little moist. My wife’s verdict was that she’s never seen the carpets so clean before and she audibly wondered if it was worth investing in a full-size carpet cleaner because clearly the StainForce and vacuuming aren’t cutting it.

Recent findings suggest that if you want the fastest possible cleanup for a fresh spill, get the Shark StainForce; it’s hard to beat a cordless cleaner with zero setup. For a smaller mess that I just want to get off my carpet, the StainForce more than does its job. But if your problem is stains that have soaked into the carpet, the Bissell Little Green Mini is the better cleaner, and at $85 on Black Friday — its lowest Amazon price in a year — it’s $45 less than the StainForce right now. It’s the one I’d buy for a house with pets, kids and carpets where you don’t want a big and bulky carpet cleaner and don’t want to worry about running out of charge for a bigger mess.
