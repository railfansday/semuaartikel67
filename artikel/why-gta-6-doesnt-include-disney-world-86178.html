Why GTA 6 Doesn’t Include Disney World

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that walt Disney World is synonymous with Florida, but the iconic theme park doesn't show up in Rockstar's GTA 6, which takes place in a fictional Florida called Leonida. Many are wondering why, especially when GTA: Vice City had its own parody of the theme park--Fun World--many years ago. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson stated the decision to came down to resources and story reasons. “A theme park would represent a lot of work that we didn’t have, and we didn’t have a strong narrative reason to send them there,” he told VGC. Nelson added that Rockstar isn't known to cut corners, so if GTA 6 were to have its own version of Walt Disney World, it would be a "fully functioning theme park," and that just wasn't doable. Walt Disney World is much more than just its most famous park, Magic Kingdom. The property includes three other main parks, Animal Kingdom, Hollywood Studios, and Epcot, along with the Disney Springs shopping district, multiple water parks and golf courses, and more than two-dozen hotels. The entire property is more than 25,000 acres. Even if Rockstar were to replicate only a portion of the scale of the real Walt Disney World, it would surely be a massive job. Players might expect fully functioning rides, and Nelson said the challenge for Rockstar would be, "Where do you stop? When do you feel like it's enough?" Nelson said people think Rockstar has unlimited time and money, and that isn't true. "So sometimes you just have to admit we are not going to be able to make this to the level we feel we need to,” he said. Plus, as Nelson pointed out, there would need to be a story reason to send Jason and Lucia hundreds of miles north from Miami to Orlando, where Walt Disney World is. GTA 6 has a huge map, but it's contained to Vice City and its surrounding areas--it doesn't go that far north.
