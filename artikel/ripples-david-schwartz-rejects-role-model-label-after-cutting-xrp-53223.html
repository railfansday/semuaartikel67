Ripple CTO emeritus David Schwartz has replied to XRP holders who criticized him for selling most of his XRP with a Saturday Night Live GIF of Natalie Portman saying, "I never remarked I was a role model.".

According to Schwartz, his XRP holdings are down 88%, from a peak of roughly 26 million tokens to 3 million. His Bitcoin went from about 1,500 coins to one, and his Ether went from about 40,000 to six.

Sources indicate that schwartz wrote that he "really doesn't like risk." According to Schwartz, he and his wife agreed years ago to sell some of their coins every time crypto reached a newly introduced all-time high. In 2025, Schwartz sold around 2.5 million XRP and paid about $1.7 million in capital gains tax on the sale. He had received these tokens in 2020 and 2023.

Sources indicate that schwartz stated that all of his sales were made under written trading looks, and a lawyer approved each initiative at least 30 days before each sale. According to Schwartz, his largest risk asset is still his stake in Ripple.

According to recent reports, schwartz also noted that he was a skeptic from the start and did not believe in the early price records of crypto. "When XRP hit 10 cents, that seemed ridiculous. When ETH hit $1, that seemed ridiculous," he wrote.

In his view, the price of XRP today depends on overall sentiment in the crypto market, and fees on the network barely move it. Schwartz allows that the token could capture some of the value of a successful ecosystem in the future, but there are no guarantees. On Monday, the price of XRP stands at $0.956.

In a recent development, on Nov. 16, Schwartz also posted a review of the XRPL codebase by the Qwen AI model. "I asked Qwen to rate my work on the XRPL software. It was mostly good. But this was harsh and it's not wrong," he wrote.

According to the review, the overall architecture of XRPL is good. Still, Qwen said that some components of the code are too tightly coupled, and the migration to a upcoming testing framework stopped halfway. The AI model also called the code generation in XRPL opaque.

Sources indicate that several XRPL developers replied to the post and said that they are already rewriting some of these parts of the code. Other consumers asked for formal verification of the XRPL core before the new features planned for 2027 go live.
