Apple will soon be adding a video podcasts section to Apple TV, which will also be available in the Apple TV app on other devices like smart TVs. That means it will come to more than a billion devices in total.

In a recent development, access to the video podcasts will be free, with no requirement for an Apple TV subscription. Testing begins today, with the full rollout promised “soon” ….

Apple shared the news with The New York Times.

On Monday, Apple will add a video podcasts section to Apple TV, initially just for testing with developers. They will soon be free to all users […] Apple also plans to increase the number of video podcasts on its regular Podcasts app.

The firm notes the move will benefit creators and listeners alike.

Recent findings suggest that “We want podcasts to be available wherever people want to enjoy them — the small screens, significant screens,” mentioned Oliver Schusser, a vice president at Apple who oversees services including podcasts, television and music. “It’s really about discovery and helping the creators to reach a new audience. We didn’t approach this in a way that would financially benefit us.”.

While podcasts began as audio only, it’s become increasingly common in recent years for them to be released as video also. This expands the reach to video platforms like YouTube, Hulu, and Netflix. Creators have welcomed Apple’s latest move, even if some are a little sad about what it states about the medium.

In a recent development, “It’s so helpful that people can go on their phones, they see a clip on Instagram, TikTok or Twitter and open the Apple app and now watch it on video,” he noted. “People are already on their iPhones and they’re used to watching videos on their phones so it was the next logical step” […].

“Everything is TV now, which is a little bit heartbreaking for a lifelong audio person to say,” Ms. Martin of NPR said.

No date has yet been declared for the public rollout.
