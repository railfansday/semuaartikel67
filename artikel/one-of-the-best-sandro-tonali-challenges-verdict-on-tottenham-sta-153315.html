Sandro Tonali has hailed Tottenham team-mate Destiny Udogie as “one of the best” left-backs.

The Italian pair were already familiar with one another before Tonali’s big-money move to north London from Newcastle United in the summer for a fee worth £100million.

They had first played together in at Under-21 level on the international stage, before reuniting for the senior Azzurri side and at club level.

Udogie, who was told by manager Roberto De Zerbi what he must do to start more games, was given the nod from the off against Ipswich Town last time out, as Spurs won 2-0 at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

The left-back impressed behind Luca Williams-Barnett, with the young star netting his first goal for the club on his first senior start.

Udogie, meanwhile, was making just his fifth start of the campaign amid injury problems.

He has also had to contend with former Liverpool defender Andy Robertson arriving to add competition in his position after Djed Spence’s departure for Inter Milan.

Sources indicate that on Udogie, Tonali told Stadium Astro: "He's a lovely guy. He's I think he's one of the best left wing[-backs]. He has to work every day now. He has a little bit of a problem with injuries, but I think he's a good guy for us.

"I think he played one of the best games against Manchester [United], and he pushes up like a high winger, and I love him because he's always happy.

"If you win, he's happy; if you lose, he's happy. If you have a bad day, he's happy, and I think when I look at him, I'm happy.".
