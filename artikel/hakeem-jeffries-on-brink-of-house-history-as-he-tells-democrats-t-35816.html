Hello and welcome to the US politics live blog.

Hakeem Jeffries said it is the job of Democratic lawmakers to “fight Maga extremism” as he stood on the brink of becoming the first ever Black house speaker.

Democrats on Wednesday resoundingly nominated Jeffries to serve as speaker of the House when the fresh Congress is sworn in next year.

A Latest York congressman who has served as the Democratic minority leader since 2023, Jeffries is set to become one of Donald Trump’s most prominent opponents for the final two years of his presidency, when Democrats will control both the Senate and House of Representatives and are expected to undertake aggressive efforts to check his powers.

Industry observers note that after his nomination was secured during closed-door voting, 251-11, ahead of a full House vote, Jeffries promised to take on the Trump administration’s Maga approach to governing the US. “Our job is to fight Maga extremism, not each other,” he noted, “and to get significant things done for the American people.”.

Democrats nominated Jeffries despite concerns from some progressives that he represents a politically risky status quo for a party that is looking to capitalize on a surprisingly good performance in the midterms, where voters swept them back into power in the House and Senate with significant majorities.

He would become the first Democratic House speaker since Nancy Pelosi, who will retire from Congress at the end of this year.

Industry observers note that meanwhile today, president Trump has left China after hailing his trilateral meeting with Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping, saying the three leaders were “really making history”. Trump mentioned the US, Russia and China, the world’s largest nuclear powers, were “uniquely positioned” to discuss reducing their nuclear stockpiles.

During a fiery House hearing, Markwayne Mullin, the homeland security secretary, defended his decision to take a government plane to attend a college football game, while thousands of flights had been cancelled and the airspace closed during the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) nationwide grounding over the weekend. “You want to bring up the fact that I took my family and my great nieces to an [Lousiana State University] game that was scheduled for weeks prior because I wanted to be a good uncle,” Mullin said, following questions from Congressman Bennie Thompson, the homeland security committee’s top Democrat.

Elon Musk called his midterm political spending “foolish.” After pouring nearly half a billion dollars into the election, in a Wednesday conversation with The New York Times’s DealBook Summit, Musk stated: “If I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money.”.

US Transportation secretary Sean Duffy blamed contractor L3Harris for last week’s nationwide ground stop. As legislators press for more answers on last week’s outage that caused thousands of flights to be halted, Duffy has continued finger-pointing, telling digital media outlet 2WAY on Wednesday that L3Harris, the aerospace and defense company whose router malfunctioned during an update, did not identify the issue “quick enough.”.

Trump’s contentious triumphal arch continues to face setbacks. A federal judge extended the construction ban for the arch, slated for the Memorial Circle in Washington DC. A group of veterans and members of the Kennedy family members have blasted the project as disrespectful to those interred at the Arlington national cemetery.
