Jennifer Lopez isn't waiting for marriage again.

The Hustlers actress gained some latest perspective on her love life during a Q&A after a screening of her new The JLo Show: Live in Las Vegas concert film at the 92nd Street Y in New York.

"It's a major part of my story," Lopez shared, before noting that she's been married and divorced "four times.".

After someone in the audience yelled out "you have six more fingers," the 57-year-old—who has been married to Ojani Noa, Cris Judd, Marc Anthony and Ben Affleck—laughed and remarked, "Yes, I do!".

According to recent reports, "Oh, my god," Lopez continued, "that is the funniest thing I’ve ever heard in my life. That’s a T-shirt: ‘I got six more fingers.'".

According to recent reports, although, quips aside, she’s not necessarily thinking about hiring a wedding planner once more. The "Dance Again" singer later added of getting married again, "I don't know if I have it in me.".

In fact, Lopez shared a similar sentiment during an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon that same day, noting that she's content with not hustling into another relationship.

According to recent reports, "It’s the first time since, like, my early 20s that I haven’t been with someone," she remarked. "I’m alone, I’m free.".

"I can do whatever the f--k I want!" Lopez, who was also engaged to Alex Rodriguez before her renewed relationship with Affleck, went on. "It's, like, amazing, but also weird, but also amazing.".

The Wedding Planner star isn't just riding solo romantically right now, either. Having recently sent her and Anthony's 18-year-old twins Max and Emme off to college, Lopez's reality as an empty nester has also hit hard.

"When they go away," she shared in a Sept. 25 video posted to her skincare brand JLO Beauty's Instagram account, "and I'm sitting here being an empty nester—crying, thinking about them, looking at baby pictures of them and all that stuff—these routines are the things that are going to keep me kind of sane.".

The "On the Floor" singer encouraged parents in similar situations to keep their skincare and workout routines going during times of change.

Industry observers note that "It's a scary time," she said. "It's a transitional time. It's a upcoming beginning in a lot of ways for them, certainly, but also for you.".

"You're so excited for them to step into this upcoming era of their life," Lopez added, "but we have to remember it's a new era of our lives too.".
