Jason Duval is a pretty quiet guy, as far as Grand Theft Auto protagonists go, but there's more to this character than meets the eye, including his love of books. In Grand Theft Auto 6, players will find several books scattered around Jason and Lucia's home in Key Lento, such as Ashes of Glory, Lethal by Design, and Orca, A Whole Different Animal. They can interact with each book to add it to their collection. Unfortunately, they can't pick up The Battle for Zantoroth, the book on his nightstand.

In a recent development, thankfully, Jason actually carries it around and reads it at the beach, giving players the chance to sneak a look at it. As it turns out, The Battle for Zantoroth is the second book in the in-game King of Gorgollia romantasy series. Hey, who's to say that a hardened criminal can't enjoy a romantasy book? It's a popular genre for a reason.

Sources indicate that the blurb on the back cover gives us a good idea of the plot of the book. The Battle for Zantoroth is about Austin Livingstone, a man who works in a tavern, who is sent by the king on a harsh journey out to sea alongside Jonathan "Sticky Broom" Ritchey, a sailor and tavern regular. As the two embark on the voyage, they come across a plethora of dangers and challenges that ultimately bring them closer. Eventually, the lines between friendship and lust become blurred. They come to learn that they are not just lovers, but actually... father and son. At this point, they have gone too far, and it is too late to turn back.

The description and accessible content of the book highlight the intensity of its author, Timothy D. D. Ewell, "former plastic surgeon and part-time self-defense instructor." The plot is certainly something, so it's no surprise that the King of Gorgollia series gained a cult following in the fictional world of GTA 6 and led to several award-winning TV and film adaptations.

Recent findings suggest that it is unknown how Jason got his hands on this book, whether it was a present from a friend or whether he wanted to supportthe Men for Women's Rights, since 3% of the proceeds go to this foundation, confirming Jason truly is the perfect boyfriend. Regardless, he keeps it by his nightstand, brings it to the beach, and is already on book two of the series, which suggests he's already somewhat invested. Hopefully, we'll come across more entries in the series at some point in the game.
