Latest interest in QPR forward emerges with Arsenal among growing admirers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

QPR boss Julien Stephan has shown no hesitation in offering first-team opportunities to upcoming starlets in his time at Loftus Road. Among those to break into the senior squad this season has been Leon Scarlett, and reports recently stated the 17-year-old has interest from AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt.

Industry observers note that now, it is separately claimed by Football Insider that Scarlett has interest on UK shores too. They state that the Hoops prospect is also on the radar of Premier League sides Arsenal and Brighton & Hove Albion, as well as Scottish giants Rangers.

It has been revealed that it is remarked that the R’s remain confident they can keep Scarlett, given the first-team opportunities on offer at Loftus Road. However, the teenage winger did start his academy career in the Arsenal setup before switching to West London in 2025, so the chance to return and push for a breakthrough could be tempting.

It has been revealed that aged just 17, Scarlett has become a part of Stephan’s first-team squad. His performances in the youth setup have caught the eye, leading to his quick ascent to the senior group in West London.

Attacking injuries and the previous unavailability of Rayan Kolli brought chances for Scarlett – perhaps a little quicker than expected. Nonetheless, he has earned his five Championship appearances, starting in the 2-2 draw against Preston North End, alongside four substitute outings.

Scarlett is very much in the developmental years of his career. His early involvement at Championship level does not mean he is a fixed member of Stephan’s squad yet, especially with previously sidelined players like Kolli and Ilias Chair back in the fold, while Rumarn Burrell nears his comeback.

Interest emphasises belief in Scarlett’s potential and highlights the good work going on in the Hoops’ academy ranks. However, they must remain patient with the teenager’s progression as he continues to grow on and off the pitch.

According to recent reports, interest from clubs of the stature of Arsenal and AC Milan could prove tempting for Scarlett. Rangers would offer a unique and exciting opportunity too, while Brighton & Hove Albion and Eintracht Frankfurt are esteemed developers of young prospects.

In a recent development, all of those teams would be able to offer a high level of academy coaching and access to elite facilities. That is where they may have an edge over QPR.

It has been revealed that however, the Hoops are more likely to be able to offer senior minutes and a quicker ascent to regular first-team selection. It means a potential Scarlett move could come down to his personal preference – does he want top coaching in elite academy facilities? Or does he want to progress with more senior exposure?
