Sources indicate that right now is an exciting time to be a fan of James Bond, especially after Amazon finally unveiled this week who is next in line to take over the mantle of 007 from the great Daniel Craig. After months of anticipation and varying reports, Amazon confirmed earlier this week that Robert Pattinson will play Bond in the new film from director Denis Villeneuve and writer Steven Knight.

In a recent development, pattinson was not one of the first names attached to star in the film, as it was originally thought that Bond would be played by another star, like Jack Lowden or Callum Turner, but his last-minute audition reportedly helped win him the job after being a personal favorite of executives and even Jeff Bezos. Pattinson's film is expected to begin shooting next year, though it's still without a release date.

It has been revealed that robert Pattinson will have his work cut out for him taking over the mantle of 007 from Daniel Craig, who may not have been the top choice from the fans when it was declared over 20 years ago. However, Craig is widely hailed as one of the best James Bonds ever now, and he's set a new standard for what modern stars can do with the character.

Craig first suited up as Bond in the 2000s with Casino Royale, and it wasn't until almost 20 years later that he bid farewell to the character with the 2021 blockbuster, No Time to Die. The movie is still a controversial talking point among fans due to its polarizing ending, but that hasn't stopped it from becoming one of the most-watched titles in the world on Prime Video, along with other films such as Skyfall and Spectre.

Recent findings suggest that practically nothing is known about the new James Bond movie at this time, other than it will star Robert Pattinson, and be directed by Denis Villeneuve and written by Steven Knight. There are rumors swirling that Sydney Sweeney could be cast as the Bond girl in the film, but this is nothing more than conjecture right now, as Villeneuve has confirmed that additional casting has not yet begun. If Bond 26 is able to begin production before the end of 2027, the most likely window of release lies in late 2028 or possibly even early 2029.

Check out No Time to Die on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of the new James Bond movie in the works at Amazon.
