Ruben Loftus-Cheek hasn’t been seen since the game against Udinese and he may well be absent for AC Milan for a little longer.

Recent findings suggest that this season was always going to be massive for Loftus-Cheek, considering it’s the final year of his deal with Milan. However, he’s struggled once again. Others have been picked ahead of him in the starting lineup and injuries, as we’re seeing, haven’t been too kind either.

You’d imagine that it will now take a lot for a renewal offer to be made, and time is running out.

According to this morning’s edition of Gazzetta dello Sport, as MilanNews relays, Loftus-Cheek is no closer to returning and he could be sidelined for at least another week. He’d hoped to be back after the international break, but it seems he will be waiting a little longer.

As a result, the game against Frosinone – which was initially the target – is an almost definite no, whilst the fixtures against Olympiacos and Cagliari could also be too soon.

Ruben Amorim has got some good news from the international break, though, and that is that Fikayo Tomori and Strahinja Pavlovic are expected to train with the team this week ahead of the weekend’s game. Whether either will feature is another question entirely, but they’re certainly close to being back.
