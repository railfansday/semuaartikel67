Despite the rumors I had heard about long lines and purchase limits, the new Pokemon Happy Meal was easier to get (and more adorable) than I thought.

When my son came home from summer camp a few years ago with a small pack of gifted Pokemon cards and a new hobby, I had no idea what lay ahead. Very promptly, locating Pokemon cards on shelves changed from finding them behind the register for a slightly higher price to not being able to find them at all. A renewed interest in collecting Pokemon cards has made everything Pokemon hotter than ever. And, somehow, I became a Poke-person.

Sources indicate that unwilling to pay (high) market price, my son and I have joined long lines before stores open to try and get cards. I’ve seen frenzied parents, kids and scalpers hoping to nab hard-to-find collections. One time the police were called on potential line-jumpers. So, when I heard McDonald’s Pokemon Happy Meals were coming, I had no idea if I’d be able to purchase one or if they were going to sell out immediately. I’m too deep into the world of Pokemon not to try, and what shocked me the most was how simple it was!

Melissa Gaman For Taste Of Home.

A McDonald’s Pokemon Happy Meal is a regular Happy Meal housed in one of three Pokemon-themed boxes, though sadly ours was in a regular red box. Each meal has a small box with one of 15 Pokemon toys and two cards. There are 15 cards in this promotion, and seven of them have shiny foil holographic elements. Each toy features a Pokemon character in the shape of a mini Happy Meal. It has a hinged plastic clip on the back so you can attach it to a bag or flip it up and use it as a card holder. The meal also had unexpectedly fun pouches of Pokemon-designed apple slices.

Some websites claimed that during the five-week promotion, there would be specific Pokemon toys allotted for each week, but if that was the plan, it’s already off track. We received two Pokemon that were supposedly marked for week three, and various Poke-loving social media posts indicated off photos with an assortment of all 15 characters.

Melissa Gaman For Taste Of Home.

Before the launch, there was speculation that Pokemon Happy Meals would be limited to one per customer or potentially sold only if a child was present, in an effort to fight scalpers. I couldn’t find anything official from McDonald’s or Pokemon Center on this policy, and it wasn’t my experience. When I ordered with my son, we asked for two, and there was no mention of limits. The solo woman behind me ordered without issue.

The line at my local McDonald’s was long, but mostly because we headed there right after school, when plenty of hungry teenagers were there. I saw only a few other people opening Pokemon toys, so for all the buzz around this collab, it was my easiest Pokemon experience in years.

In a recent development, pokemon Happy Meals will be available in the U.S. until Dec. 14. However, the most vital thing to remember is that this promotion is while supplies last. Considering that reseller sites like eBay are already buzzing with toys and cards, there is a good chance supplies will sell out before then.

Sources indicate that even though our fries were cold, the toys are adorable, and the art on the cards is Poke-perfection. I don’t think we will catch them all, but hopefully we can get a few more while they are available.

Will you be trying to collect one or all of the toys and cards? Let us know in the comments!
