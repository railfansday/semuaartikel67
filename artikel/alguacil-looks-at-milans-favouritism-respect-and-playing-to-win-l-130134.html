Tomorrow’s game is significant for both AC Milan and Olympiacos, with both hoping to rise up the Europa League standings.

For Milan, tomorrow is a must-win game. Of course, they all are, but they’re now at a stage where any defeat could seriously hinder their chances of qualification to the latter stages of the competition.

If they win all four of their final games, they’ll have a chance of a top eight finish, a slim one, but a chance nonetheless. However, a point would mean that they’ll likely have to settle for a top 24 spot.

With the squad thinning out, any possibility of avoiding extra games would be ideal for Ruben Amorim, but it will not be easy at all.

Speaking to the press before tomorrow’s game, Imanol Alguacil – Olympiacos’ head coach – looked at the strength of the Rossoneri, praise for Luka Modric and it being ‘life-or-death’ for both teams, as MilanPress relays.

Industry observers note that “What if we change something? We’ll do it if necessary; I have no problem with that. I’ve already planned some things and we’ll adapt depending on the opponent. The same goes for Milan, we’ve done some tests and we’ll see how to approach the match.

“We’re not looking for excuses with the injuries in midfield, everyone is ready to play this game. Milan are strong; they’re one of the favourites to win the Europa League, even if they only have three points, and also Serie A.

In a recent development, “We have great respect for them but also great ambition because we’ll be in front of our fans. It will be a life-or-death match for both us and them.”.

Both teams are struggling with injuries….

“Injuries happen, I’m not a coach who makes excuses. I brought some players up from the Primavera, but we want to make our fans happy. I want a team with character and I want everyone to give their all.

In a recent development, “Is Modric’s absence an advantage? It’s a shame he won’t be there because all coaches would like to play against the best players. All football fans would love to see someone like Modric play; I hope it’s nothing serious.

Recent findings suggest that “He’s done great things in Spain and he’s doing it with AC Milan too. What if I sign for a draw? We’re playing to win, then we’ll see at the end of the match what the fairest result was. We must treat AC Milan with respect because they’re a great team.”.
