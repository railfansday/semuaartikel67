Mauricio Pochettino has confirmed Bruno Fernandes will remain his captain at Manchester United as he bids to transform a club in crisis.

Fernandes has come under criticism this season, failing to replicate his from from a historic assist-making season last term.

Yet Pochettino is adamant Fernandes will be United’s “leader” as he bids to inspire a more physical approach to mirror his preferred pressing philosophy ahead of Sunday’s first game against Liverpool at Anfield.

In a recent development, “All the players, we were chatting, it was amazing from day one, Bruno is our captain, an important player,” Pochettino confirmed. “But all the players need to think they are important, from the bottom to the top.

“I spent three hours, chatting in my office, with all the players, maybe I miss some injured players. In the next few days, we will talk in an individual way with all of them. It’s crucial to listen to what they need to say, understand the place they are. Not only on the field, off the field, what’s going on. Some conversations, some collective way, meetings, but focus is on Sunday.”.

While Pochettino added that his priority is to provoke a response after the downturn under Michael Carrick this season, rather than build a long-lasting culture that he insists could take many years to cultivate.

“We arrived with the idea to create something from scratch, our own ideas and philosophy, in the way we like to work, we find supportive people in the club, that make it easy, the adaptation, at the same time, we need to improve the players, to create the way to find a way to perform,” Pochettino noted.

“We are not focused on culture, it’s a massive work, you need to spend 10 years to focus on culture, it’s clear, a historic club.

In a recent development, "When we arrive, we found a group of players that we propose to do the football that we feel, being brave, be Man United, you know? It's about proposing the idea, they buy that idea, to be brave and try take that to the field. It's a debate that I don't like to go in, we cannot talk about things without context.

It has been revealed that “On the data without context, it's easy to manipulate the data, I need to be honest, sometimes the data can show some things. We'll try improve the data and numbers in every single area of the field. We are here, not to denounce things, we're here to build things, perform and win games.
