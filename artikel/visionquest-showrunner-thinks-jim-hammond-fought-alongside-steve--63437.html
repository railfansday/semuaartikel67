VisionQuest showrunner Terry Matalas has confirmed Jim Hammond fought in World War 2, alongside Steve Rogers' Captain America.

According to recent reports, jim Hammond finally made his MCU debut in the Disney Plus show, though his costume could first be glimpsed in Captain America: The First Avenger, way back in 2011. He was created by Phineas Horton.

According to recent reports, "I think he was doing exactly that," Matalas shared in an interview, when asked if Hammond fought Nazis or saved people in London. "I think he was part of the Invaders. In the comic books, he kills Hitler. The idea is there is a whole series that happened that we didn't see that's about that father and son, and the gang in World War 2.".

When the interviewer asked if, during The First Avenger, Steve Rogers was also part of the Invaders and had a side mission, Matalas confirmed: "You would see Jim Hammond." Check it out below.

Terry Matalas claims that he envisioned Jim Hammond to have been on the Invaders and fought alongside Steve Rogers in WWII‼️ Let him make a special presentation where Jim kills Hitler NOW https://t.co/J586M2qezlNovember 19, 2026.

In Marvel comics, the Invaders was a team of superheroes that fought in World War 2, which included Steve Rogers, Bucky Barnes, Namor the Sub-Mariner, Jim Hammond, and his sidekick Toro.

According to recent reports, however, the movies steered clear of this team – likely due to rights complications – and instead we saw Steve Rogers and Bucky Barnes allied with the Howling Commandos. Still, thanks to Matalas, we can now imagine the Invaders were involved in the war, just off-screen.

In a recent development, visionQuest also resurrected Wanda Maximoff in a shock post-credits scene directly setting up Avengers: Doomsday. What's next for the Maximoff family is anyone's guess.
