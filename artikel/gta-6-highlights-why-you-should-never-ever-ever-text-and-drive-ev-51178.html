GTA 6 Highlights Why You Should Never, Ever, Ever Text and Drive (Ever)

🌐 Source: Polygon • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (591 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • You can pull off a lot of heinous crimes in Grand Theft Auto 6.
  • It has been revealed that the most important tools that Jason and Lucia have in GTA 6 are their cell phones.
  • Rockstar Games deliberately plays into that with one of GTA 6’s most devious features.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

You can pull off a lot of heinous crimes in Grand Theft Auto 6. Stealing a car, mugging an old lady at gunpoint, killing a bird and then beating up a cop with its corpse. All of those are sure to earn you at least a one-star Wanted level. But there’s an even worse crime in GTA 6 that you’ll probably wind up doing more than murder: texting and driving.

It has been revealed that the most important tools that Jason and Lucia have in GTA 6 are their cell phones. Rockstar makes much of the game’s UI elements diegetic by cleverly cramming them into phone apps. People will text you mission locations to explain why quest markers are appearing on the map. A news app full of absurd stories will teach you Leonida lore without you needing to find scattered logs. You’ll even manage your workout routine through a fitness app. You can easily get distracted by your phone in-game, just as you would in real life.

Rockstar Games deliberately plays into that with one of GTA 6’s most devious features. You can pull out your phone at any time and use it in first-person – and that includes while driving. Pick up your phone behind the wheel, and you’ll barely be able to see out your windshield as your focus is drawn to your glowing screen. It's one of the many small, but effective ways that GTA 6 lampoons everyday life in the 2020s.

In a recent development, considering that driving already requires care and attention, the task becomes even more difficult with a phone in one hand. You’ll find yourself swerving more erratically, and most assuredly getting into accidents. I have read-ended cars and run down pedestrians because I was too busy liking memes on Snapmatic. I’m not proud of that; scratched my damn car!

What’s especially devilish about the system is that GTA 6 constantly tempts you to pick up your phone. Jason and Lucia will get text messages ad nauseam. Sometimes they’ll learn an important story detail, or get the location of a mission location they’ve been waiting for. Other times, Jason might just get a friendly text from Lucia asking what he’s up to. As an obsessive communicator, I always felt like I needed to respond right away or else I’d wind up leaving these people hanging. So anytime I’d get a text while driving, I’d look at the road, determine when I could look down for just a second, and then fire off a reply. And every time, I would wind up skidding over a bridge.

Sources indicate that you can argue, as many politicians and panicked parents have for decades, that GTA glorifies a lot of bad behavior. This, on the other hand, is probably the closest that the series has ever come to approaching a dangerous real-world act like a PSA. You absolutely should not text and drive in GTA 6, just as you should not in real life. It doesn’t matter how quickly you can speed read a text; things happen quickly on the road. I mean, one time a drunk dude on a tractor cut me off out of nowhere! If you need to check Snapmatic, pull over to the side of the road and listen to some talk radio while you text. You wouldn’t want to mess up that paint job of the car you’re about to fence after stealing it from an old lady by beating her unconscious, right? Stay cool, follow the rules!
