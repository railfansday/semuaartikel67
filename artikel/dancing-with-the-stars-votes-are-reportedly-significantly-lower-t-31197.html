‘Dancing With the Stars’ Votes Are Reportedly “Significantly Lower” Than Last Season

Industry observers note that dancing With the Stars might be airing one of its most-watched seasons yet, but apparently audience participation isn’t as high as usual. Despite fans tuning into the show week after week, turns out not that many people are picking up their phones to actually vote—or, at least, not as many as last season.

“The amount of votes coming in right now is significantly lower than this time last season because the Alix [Earle] vs Robert [Irwin] face-off sparked a ton of participation,” a source tells The Sun. “Producers are somewhat seeing that with Amber and Ezra and Tatyana. But there’s no one garnering that level of response this season and it highlights with the numbers.”.

Sources indicate that despite the low voting, Deadline reports that Dancing With the Stars’ opening episode saw a 24 percent increase from last season’s premiere. And fans even crashed the voting site! Meanwhile, Super Bowl Night saw the show get a “nine-year ratings high among adults 18-49” with 6.59 million live/same-day viewers.

Last year’s season 34 finale brought in 72 million votes (more than double the 32 million in season 33), and while votes are allegedly down this season, it’s possible next week could change things. Mostly because—for the first time ever!—ABC is airing Dancing With the Stars live on both the East Coast and the West Coast, meaning West Coast viewers will be able to watch the show as it airs and vote in real time. This should inevitably get the numbers up, so stay tuned…and remember to vote for your favorites during the finale next week at 8 p.m. ET!
