West Ham United have received a boost ahead of Saturday’s Championship clash with Preston North End, with striker Johnny Kenny ruled out through injury. The 22-year-old has suffered a hamstring tear and is expected to remain sidelined beyond Upcoming Year’s Day.

In a recent development, preston manager Graham Alexander confirmed the extent of the injury in an interview with the Lancashire Evening Post, having lost Kenny during the defeat to Sheffield United earlier this month. The striker’s absence adds to Preston’s injury problems ahead of their visit to the London Stadium.

However, Preston have already demonstrated they can win without Kenny, having beaten Southampton in their previous fixture. Midfielder Léo Leroy is also available again after suspension, although captain Ali McCann remains sidelined.

Soungoutou Magassa was reinstated to the West Ham squad following the arrival of Parker.

Personally, I have a suspicion that Scott Parker will drop Mohamadou Kanté for this game and reinstate Soungoutou Magassa in midfield to protect his back four, irrespective of Preston’s attacking options.

Parker has already shown that he’s prepared to use Magassa, having recalled him for the Cardiff City match.

And with West Ham’s defensive problems continuing, I suspect the fresh manager will want somebody sitting in front of the defence to provide additional protection.

It’s a possibility we’ve previously discussed when examining Parker’s prepares for the international break, and the manager has now had considerably more time to work on his tactical approach.

Kenny’s absence is undoubtedly welcome news for West Ham, but it won’t dictate Parker’s team selection.

As recent training footage has demonstrated, Magassa has been involved in Parker’s preparations.

Whether that results in a starting place against Preston remains to be seen.
