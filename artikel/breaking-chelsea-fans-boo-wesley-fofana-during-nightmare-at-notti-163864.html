Wesley Fofana had a night to forget for Chelsea at Nottingham Forest as he gave away a penalty and was booed by his own fans at the end of a week where he courted controversy.

The Blues defender, who has been singled out for poor performances during the team’s bad run, needed a strong showing, having spoken for almost two hours with French YouTuber Zack Nani on a range of topics in a controversial, unauthorised interview.

Fofana admitted that he came close to quitting football whilst he was sidelined with an anterior cruciate injury, while also appearing to joke about missing his penalty during last month’s Carabao Cup shootout defeat to Liverpool.

He apologised for the interview and boss Xabi Alonso mentioned the matter was closed, but Friday’s Premier League game was a bad one for him.

The visiting supporters consistently jeered the centre-back when he was in possession in the first half. Fofana and his teammates endured a tough start at the City Ground as they fell behind to Igor Jesus’ brilliant fifth-minute strike.

A bad night got worse in the early stages of the second half when Fofana brought down Liam Delap for a penalty that Morgan Gibbs-White converted to double Forest’s lead.

He was substituted soon after, following chants from travelling Blues fans of “f*** off Fofana, you’re not wanted here.”.

Emmanuel Emegha did score to halve the deficit but Gibbs-White’s second goal restored Forest’s healthy advantage and although another late Emegha strike saw the Blues pile on the pressure to try and snatch an injury-time equaliser, they ultimately suffered a 3-2 loss that their poor performance deserved.
