McDonald's Is Underperforming the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100. Could January Mark a Turning Point?

Industry observers note that mcDonald's (MCD +1.18%) stock has been trailing the major indexes this year, down about 10% year to date. But since the end of September, when it fell to around $230 per share, McDonald's stock has jumped nearly 20% to its current $275 per share.

Industry observers note that but the real turning point for the fast-food restaurant giant could come in January 2027. Here's why.

According to recent reports, in January, or early next year, as CEO Chris Kempczinski remarked on the third-quarter earnings call, McDonald's plans to roll out its newly introduced value menu nationally. Why is this a significant deal? For a few reasons.

One of the reasons that McDonald's stock has struggled is because it lost its value proposition to its traditional base of lower- and middle-income customers with a series of price hikes over the past few years. Its higher-priced menu items basically priced out its core consumer base.

NYSE · As of 3:36 PM ET 11/16/26.

But McDonald's rolled out some initiatives to boost its digital and value offerings in September that resulted in an increase in comp sales in the final month of the quarter. Then, in October, McDonald's introduced a test program for its latest value menu at some 1,900 restaurants.

In a recent development, "We're testing mix-and-match bundles that give customers greater choice and provide familiar ordering behaviors for prior buy one, add one offers. We're also testing fixed bundles that give customers a set combination at an attractive price and make ordering straightforward," Kempczinski noted on the call.

Another 4,000 franchisees are moving forward with similar value bundle options. The results of the eight-week test will allow management to determine which menu structure resonates with customers, works operationally, and generates sales and traffic.

"Our goal is to launch a fresh program nationally in early 2027," the CEO said. "Customers should see a clear and compelling value proposition every time they consider McDonald's, whether looking for an affordable individual item, a complete meal, or a digital offer in the mobile app.".

So far, the initiative has shown positive traffic trends in September and October, so the hope is that this new entry-level, value-oriented menu structure will help improve sales and efficiency in 2027. It sits alongside the company's long-term NEXT program, which seeks to improve the menu, customer experience, efficiency and productivity, and gain market share.

McDonald's stock price has jumped significantly in the past month, mainly due to investor interest around its NEXT plan and its commitment to value and efficiency. It also reported better-than-expected results in Q3, with global systemwide sales up 4%, comp sales rising 1.5%, and earnings jumping 10% year over year to $3.51 per share.

In a recent development, there has also been renewed interest in McDonald's as a dividend stock amid the recent tech tumble. McDonald's is a Dividend King, a designation for companies with at least 50 straight years of dividend increases. It presently pays a dividend of $1.93 per share at a yield of 2.82%.

It has been revealed that mcDonald's stock is not all that cheap, with a P/E ratio of 21. But it could have the earnings to support that P/E if this turnaround plan takes hold. Keep an eye on McDonald's stock and look for news early next year on its latest menu options.
