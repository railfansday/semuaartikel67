Oceania Cruises has unveiled a fresh Chef’s Studio aboard the Oceania Marina, introducing a venue for culinary education, destination discovery and chef-hosted experiences at sea.

Beginning November 25, 2026, the venue will welcome guests for a upcoming series of culinary workshops, seminars and test kitchen experiences.

“Since introducing the first hands-on cooking school at sea aboard the Oceania Marina, Oceania Cruises has continually evolved the ways guests can connect with destinations through food and culture,” mentioned Jason Montague, chief luxury officer of Oceania Cruises.

“The latest Chef’s Studio complements The Culinary Center while offering a different style of culinary enrichment, allowing guests to explore technique and the importance of linking place, history and gastronomy.”.

Debuting following the Oceania Marina’s extensive bow-to-stern refurbishment in October, the Chef’s Studio builds on the ship’s pioneering culinary legacy.

Designed for 18 guests, the Chef’s Studio offers a upcoming perspective on learning opportunities with a roster of seminars, workshops and destination-focused discussions led by Chef Instructors, the manufacturer said in a press release.

The company stated that the venue’s fresh Test Kitchen Series features one-hour classes offered on sea days for $49 per person.

It is designed for travelers seeking a deeper understanding of food and culture.

Sessions focus on techniques and culinary fundamentals with topics ranging from The Spice of Life, knife skills, pantry organization and cooking with high heat to destination-inspired classes exploring regional ingredients, traditions and culinary practices from around the world.

The Chef’s Studio also serves as the gathering place for Oceania Cruises’ signature Culinary Discovery Tours.

According to the press release, guests meet before these small-group tours for an orientation and overview, and once the tour returns, guests and the chef return to the Chef’s Studio for a glass of wine to continue conversations and ask follow-up questions.

In 2027, Oceania Cruises expects to offer more than 100 chef-led Culinary Discovery Tours in destinations spanning Iceland, Australia, Uruguay and beyond.

The Chef’s Studio program will expand next year and debut aboard the Oceania Riviera and the line’s latest additions to the fleet, the Oceania Sonata and Oceania Aurelia.
