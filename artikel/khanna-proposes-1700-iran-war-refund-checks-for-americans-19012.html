Khanna proposes $1,700 Iran war refund checks for Americans

🌐 Source: Thehill • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (64 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that rep. Ro Khanna (D-Calif.) on Monday proposed $1,700 refund checks to reimburse Americans for the economic impact of the Iran war. Khanna, a progressive and potential 2028 presidential contender, said on social media the checks would be sent to every American to cover higher gas, food and electricity prices caused by disruptions to oil transport….
