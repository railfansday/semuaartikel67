📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Louis Carr, who took over as president of BET Media Group last year after the departure of former president/CEO Scott Mills, is exiting. Carr had previously served as BET’s president of media sales but was elevated to president last December while maintaining his media sales role, reporting to George Cheeks, then chair of TV media at Paramount Skydance. Cheeks, now co-chair and chief content officer for Skydance TV, unveiled the exit on Monday.

In a recent development, “I’m deeply grateful to Louis,” Cheeks mentioned in a memo to staff. “For four decades, he has been one of BET’s greatest champions, helping shape the brand, its business and its connection to Black audiences and culture. Louis built lasting partnerships with some of the world’s leading brands and helped cement BET as a cultural force. As President, he led BET with clarity and purpose, creating renewed momentum for the brand. According to recent reports, carr will remain through the end of the year. Cheeks didn’t immediately announce Carr’s replacement, but remarked “we will have more to share soon on BET leadership and structure.”.

According to recent reports, in his own memo to staff, Carr mentioned: “For 40 years, I have had one of the best jobs in the media and entertainment industry. Having a position that gives one the opportunity to carry out a purpose that is bigger than oneself is a privilege. Working for a brand whose mission from day one was to uplift and empower the Black community is an honor I’ll never forget.”.

In a recent development, “That remarked, even as I write this email, I wrestle with my decision that the time has come to make a transition. Based on recent events within our community and our country, I’ve decided to chart a newly introduced path and my last day will be December 31.”.

Besides his four decades at BET, Carr founded the Louis Carr Internship Foundation to help improve diversity in corporate America through paid internships. He also wrote the book “Dirty Little Secrets” and “Little Black Book: Daily Motivations for Business and Personal Growth.” In 2023, Carr was inducted into the Advertising Hall of Fame and received the Chicago Advertising Federation Silver Medal Award.

Sources indicate that last year, Mills’ exit came after the acquisition of Paramount Global by David Ellison’s Skydance Media; similarly, Carr’s departure comes following the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. by Skydance.
