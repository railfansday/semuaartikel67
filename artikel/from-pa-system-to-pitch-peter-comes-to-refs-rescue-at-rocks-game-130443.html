But that’s the leap that Peter Kelly-Sullivan made when a stand-in assistant referee was needed in the latter stages of the Rocks’ Isthmian South Central clash with Hayes & Yeading on Saturday.

In a recent development, the referee pulled his calf and one of his linesmen stood in, so a spare flag man was needed. But there was no need to call for one over the ground’s public address system – for the man operating it was just the man for the job, Peter being a qualified official.

He told us: “The game stopped and the physio came on to check on the referee. It looked like he pulled his calf.

“I heard a couple of people say ‘go on Pete, get ready’.

“I waited until the referee was treated and then Rocks general manager Simon Cook came over and asked if I could help out.

Sources indicate that “Luckily I was in a tracksuit. I went on to the pitch and discussed with the fresh referee about how long was left.

“The senior assistant moved up to be referee and the junior assistant became senior assistant.

"There were three minutes of normal time then seven of stoppage time left. I got a warm round of applause when I ran over to the touchline. Thankfully I didn't have anything major to do, just a couple of goal kicks.”.
