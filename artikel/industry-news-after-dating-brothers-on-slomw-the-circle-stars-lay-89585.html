📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A fresh season of The Circle is here, but it’s not just civilians competing this time around. In the Hulu revival series, celebrities are also part of the competition, including The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star Layla Taylor—though her fellow competitors don’t know that, since she’s playing as a former Mormon bisexual bro named Doug. She’s not doing it alone, either, as she entered the Circle with her bestie Kate Racker. Who is Kate, you ask? I paid a visit to her and Layla’s apartment in Atlanta to get to know her (and her SLOMW lore) a little better.

Recent findings suggest that as you might recall, Kate was first introduced during SLOMW season 2 as Chase McWhorter’s on-again, off-again girlfriend. She later explained to People that she and Chase met just three weeks after she split from her ex-husband (with whom she shares a son, Rowan), and it was through Chase that she met the MomTok crew, including Layla (though fun fact: Kate already knew Jessi Draper, who was her neighbor growing up, as well as Jacob Neeley, who she used to work with in private equity).

Sources indicate that it wasn’t until summer 2025, however, that Layla and Kate really cemented their friendship. “Before she started dating Mason [McWhorter], and I was kind of off with Chase, Layla and I were just single together. So we’d go out, we talk every day,” Kate previously told People. “We were kind of each other’s person. We’d FaceTime 24/7 and just got really, really, really close.”.

Industry observers note that now they’re spending 24/7 together in the Circle, where—according to Kate—they’ve only gotten into “one tiny little tiff” so far. “We are sisters at this point, and I consider her my sister,” she indicates, adding, “I think the thing about me and Layla is we’re both like pretty sensitive, but we both love each other so much, and we’re committed [to winning]. Like, I have goosebumps.”.

My significant question for them: Why did they decide to step into the Circle as a catfish rather than themselves?

“It’s kind of fun to like step into somebody that you’re not,” Layla says.

In a recent development, kate agrees, adding, “It’s a new experience. I mean, we’re all about new experiences and trying new things and role play. We love role play. We’ll try anything three times.”.

And if you’re wondering whether they have a certain man (or a certain pair of brothers) in mind while playing “Doug,” the answer is...not really. “I feel like he’s kind of a collection of the men that we have encountered in our life or even dated in the past,” explains Layla. “Like, all of them.”.

In a recent development, “I think we’re trying to lean into the douchebag,” adds Kate. “Not to say all men are douchebags, but no, there’s some great ones. Or, you know, a few.”.

According to recent reports, layla then clarifies, “We’re not men haters. No, not at all. We are men observers. And we’ve observed poor behavior from the men that we do know, and we’re just emulating and dramatizing that.”.

It has been revealed that I also ask Layla if she’s missing her girlfriend Kelsie Chesser while she’s in the Circle, and she states that—while she says, “For sure, you know, I love her dearly”—the harder part is being away from her kids. “Yeah, we both cry every day,” Layla says. “Cried myself to sleep last night. But Kate is my bestie, and there’s no one I’d rather be trapped in here with.”.

Since there are players who have yet to be revealed in the Circle, I need to know: Which of the Mormon Wives would they be most excited to see pop up on their screen?

“I would love to see Jessi,” Kate claims. “Obviously, Miranda [Hope]. Miranda would be great. Mayci [Neeley]. Like, any of them.”.

Layla grins before adding, “Yeah, I think I can say everyone...but one.”.
