Should you give the statue to Benedict Halifax in GTA 6?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that deciding whether to give the statue to Benedict Halifax is a decision you'll need to make during the fourth story mission of Chapter 3 in GTA 6, called Dubious Provenance. This mission will kick off after Cal visits the Bayside apartment you're borrowing from Boobie to inspect the hard drive.

Once Cal leaves, you'll see an icon of a statuette at the closet door. Remember, you didn't just acquire a hard drive but a bronze statuette in Chapter 1, and if you interact with it now the mission will begin. First, you need to drive to Capri, a ritzy town on the other side of the map.

At the antiques store, Evelyn Andrews tells you she isn't interested in the obviously stolen item, but art dealer Benedict Halifax approaches you on the sly and claims he can have it appraised for you. Should you give the statue to him?

In a recent development, so, you might as well just give it to him. Things shake out the same way no matter what you choose, but not giving it to him just means the mission will ultimately take longer to complete.
