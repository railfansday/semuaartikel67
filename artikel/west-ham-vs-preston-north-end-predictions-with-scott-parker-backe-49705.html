It has been revealed that west Ham turned to the ex-Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley boss Scott Parker earlier this month. WHUFC picked him as their replacement for Nuno Espirito Santo, who was sacked after a poor run of form.

According to recent reports, the Hammers drew 3-3 away at Cardiff City in Parker‘s first game at the helm before their 2-1 defeat against Blackburn Rovers at Ewood Park last time out. They are presently 9th in the Championship table and are one point off the play-offs behind both QPR and Southampton.

Sources indicate that preston North End are bottom of the league after only two wins from their first 15 fixtures this season. PNE are in the drop zone with Burnley and Derby County and they are seven points from safety behind Watford and Lincoln City.

Sources indicate that nevertheless, the Lilywhites secured a surprise 1-0 win over promotion chasing Southampton at home in their last outing before the international break. Jordan Thompson scored their winner against the Saints.

Recent findings suggest that “West Ham are the top scorers in the Championship so far this season with a whopping 37 goals to date. WHUFC have real firepower in their squad, especially in Jarrod Bowen, and they can cause problems for any opponent on their day.

In a recent development, “Scott Parker will be eager to get a win in his first match at the London Stadium. If he can tighten up the Hammers’ defence, they should be able to rise up the league and challenge for the top two.

Industry observers note that “Preston North End are at serious risk of slipping into League One in this campaign, despite their win over Southampton last time out. PNE have given themselves a lot of work to do over the coming weeks and months following their poor start to this term.

Industry observers note that “Only Burnley have conceded more than Lilywhites and they are winless away from home. Therefore, they are likely to lose again in London this weekend.”.

According to recent reports, “It feels like West Ham need a big result here. Scott Parker was up against it in the first game against Cardiff City but they were really poor against Blackburn Rovers and are now four without a win in the Championship.

“A comfortable win in front of the home faithful would go a long way for Parker in his bid to find some momentum and show the supporters that he’s the man to turn around their fortunes.

Sources indicate that “Sitting bottom of the league with 11 defeats in 15 games and 30 goals conceded, there aren’t many better teams for WHUFC to face than Preston North End – on paper. But, the team really looked like they were playing for Graham Alexander as they battled to a shock win over Southampton before the break, and they will be out to spring another surprise this weekend.

“It is one thing doing that at home, but on the road is another. PNE have got one point away from Deepdale this term with a goal difference of -12 in seven games.

“For that reason, I will be backing Parker for a first Hammers win this weekend.”.
