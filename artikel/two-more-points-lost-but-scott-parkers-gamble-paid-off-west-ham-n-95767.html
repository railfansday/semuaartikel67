Just over one year ago, West Ham’s Finlay Herrick was stepping out on loan for Boreham Wood in the National League – the fifth tier of English football. A far cry from London Stadium yesterday as he replaced Mads Hermansen in goal and made his Championship debut, apparently hearing about his impending start just on the morning of the game.

And despite the first half concession from the corner, Herrick did a pretty fair job with excellent double saves later in the game and some assured glove work catching the ball – which had seemed like a lost art at times in the last couple of years.

Herrick after his unforgettable FA Cup penalty shootout debut last year.

Sources indicate that this was a massive gamble by Scott Parker which could have backfired dreadfully. With no Areola, the temptation must have been to revert to Hermansen yet the Hammers’ head coach stuck with his instinct. After the first half goal concession, Parker must have wondered – briefly – whether he’d made a huge howler.

Has Herrick done enough to keep his place? It seems Parker is dead set on replacing Hermansen so until Alphonse Areola is ‘fit and ready’ then Herrick should be prepared for more pitch time. He certainly has the ambition:.

“I hope this is the start. I hope I’ve done myself justice and proved to the fans that I don’t just want to be third choice, I want to be playing, I want to get back to the Premier League and be part of that”. (whu/youtube).

Describing the competition amongst the trio of goal keepers, Herrick was quick to praise Hermansen for the encouragement and help he’s received recently, describing the three keepers as a team more than individuals fighting amongst themselves for the sole starting spot:.

“There’s one goal keeper shirt and there’s three of us – but we’re like a band of brothers and we always stick together, me Mads and Fonz- no matter what. “.

Difficult to believe Herrick is still only 20 – he seems to have been around West Ham’s fringes for ever- but he joined the academy at six years old and looks to have the talent to go all the way. From yesterday’s solid start, he didn’t let his manager or his team mates down and should feel rightly pretty proud of himself.

The question for Scott Parker is whether he continues between the sticks at Fratton Park on Tuesday night.
