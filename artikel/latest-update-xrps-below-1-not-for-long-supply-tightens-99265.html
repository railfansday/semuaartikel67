XRP has slipped below the $1 mark even as the amount available on Binance is tightening sharply.

XRP has fallen below the $1 level even as available supply on Binance appears to be shrinking, leaving the token between constrained exchange inventory and a lack of fresh institutional buying.

It has been revealed that recent reports put XRP near $0.95 after a steep monthly decline, with the token briefly trading under $0.90 during November’s broader sell-off. Intermittent rebounds have occurred, but price has not yet established a durable hold above the $1 mark.

US spot XRP ETFs have gone through their longest stretch without a net inflow since launch, according to SoSoValue data. The funds last recorded an $8.43 million net inflow on October 29 and have since seen roughly $26 million in withdrawals, though flows on November 18 and 19 were described as flat rather than net negative.

Sources indicate that the pause matters because ETF buyers had previously provided incremental demand. Total net assets in the funds stood at about $1.08 billion by November 19, down from roughly $1.77 billion in late September, while cumulative inflows since launch remained near $1.8 billion.

XRP’s price action reflects that gap. The token was down about 3.6% over one recent seven-day period and roughly 28% over the prior month in another snapshot. Its 30-day Sharpe ratio was reported near negative 0.41, indicating that recent returns have not compensated for the asset’s volatility.

It has been revealed that cryptoQuant’s XRP Supply Shortage Index for Binance rose to 2.4 on November 19, above its July reading of 2.09. Readings above 2 are described as severe tightness, and net withdrawals from the exchange exceeded $127 million between November 10 and 16.

It has been revealed that lower exchange balances can reduce immediately available selling inventory, but they do not form a standalone bullish signal. One analyst noted that part of the movement could reflect internal Binance reserve management rather than accumulation by long-term holders.

XRP’s price action has not yet confirmed stronger demand. XRP remains below its 200-day moving average, sitting very near $1.26, and repeated closes below $1 have turned that former support into nearby resistance.

Traders are watching the $0.94 area first, followed by support around $0.88. A sustained break below the latter could open a path toward $0.80, while a recovery would need to reclaim roughly $0.985 and then $1 before a broader reversal appears more credible.

According to recent reports, the more relevant signal for the market is whether ETF flows resume as Bitcoin and broader risk sentiment stabilize. Tighter supply can amplify a move higher, but without returning demand it can also coexist with a fragile price floor.
