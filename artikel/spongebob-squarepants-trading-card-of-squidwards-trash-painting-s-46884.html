SpongeBob SquarePants trading card of Squidward’s ‘trash’ painting sells for nearly $60,000

A one-of-a-kind SpongeBob SquarePants trading card featuring Squidward’s “Bold and Brash” self-portrait sold for $58,560 at Goldin’s November 14 auction.

The card comes from Topps’ 2025 SpongeBob SquarePants 25th Anniversary set, its first SpongeBob release since 2009. Topps made 25 copies of the standard “Bold and Brash” card and just one gold-foil version, known as a Superfractor. That’s the copy that went up for auction.

The artwork comes from “Artist Unknown,” half of a 2001 Season 2 episode that aired alongside “Sailor Mouth.”.

A true masterpiece 🖼️From 2025 Topps Chrome SpongeBob, this 1-of-1 Superfractor of Squidward’s “Bold and Brash” self-portrait just sold for $58,560. pic.twitter.com/uBaMOxS3SS.

It has been revealed that PSA shared the sale price on November 17 alongside a photo of the card in its Topps-sealed case. The result came within $3,050 of the $61,610 Goldin previously took in for a PSA 10 card from the same set featuring SpongeBob himself. That red-foil card was one of five copies.

It has been revealed that in “Artist Unknown,” Squidward teaches a community art class and gets SpongeBob as his only student. When art collector Monty P. Moneybags drops in looking for pieces for his museum, Squidward shows him “Bold and Brash,” an abstract self-portrait with his long nose and drooping limbs set against a bright yellow background.

Sources indicate that “More like ‘Belongs in the Trash,’” Moneybags replies. A janitor then tosses the painting into his garbage can while Squidward watches.

A subreddit dedicated to the meme appeared in 2013, with fans posting edits that swapped in characters like Sonic the Hedgehog. Others made their own replicas, including one that ended up in the collection of Squidward voice actor Rodger Bumpass.

Industry observers note that the joke has since made its way onto real wall art. Hot Topic has sold both wood and canvas versions of the portrait that Bikini Bottom’s art collector sent to the trash.
