Kiwi pioneered a better way to find flights, but the economics of selling and servicing them proved harder.

It has been revealed that in September 2019, Skift ran a story with the headline: “Kiwi.com CEO Wants to Build a Rival to Expedia.” Oliver Dlouhý, then 31, was about to speak at Skift Global Forum in New York. The organization had just brought in General Atlantic as its majority investor.

It has been revealed that seven years later, Expedia has agreed to buy Kiwi.com for an undisclosed sum. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions.

Sources indicate that what Dlouhý and his co-founder Jozef Képesi built out of Brno, Czechia, was a remarkable entrepreneurial achievement. Kiwi developed some of the most inventive flight search products of the past 15 years.

Sources indicate that founded in 2012 as Skypicker, the company found an opportunity in the fragmented European airline market.
