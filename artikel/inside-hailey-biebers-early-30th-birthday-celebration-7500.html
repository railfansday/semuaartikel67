Recent findings suggest that hailey Bieber is a little over a week away from turning 30 on November 22, and the festivities are already underway. Naturally, she came dressed for the occasion.

Industry observers note that on the evening of Friday, November 13, Rhode co-founder Lauren Ratner and influencer Anastasia Karanikolaou shared videos on Instagram of Hailey blowing out her birthday candles at an early celebration. Hailey’s husband, Justin Bieber, and friend Kendall Jenner were also in attendance.

Industry observers note that for the kickoff to what will likely be a string of festivities, Hailey opted for a vintage Dolce & Gabbana red-and-black python-print corset featuring an exposed black lace bra and mesh trim. She paired the top with black pants for a sleek finish.

The outfit’s red color palette complemented the celebration’s centerpiece: a two-tier pale pink birthday cake decorated with cherries and candles.

Hailey’s early birthday celebrations have become something of a tradition. Last year, ahead of turning 29, she celebrated with her Rhode team at a party featuring a multi-tiered pink birthday cake decorated with bows and elaborate frosting. For the occasion, she wore a vintage Versace fall/winter 2002 black leather coat with fringe detailing and a backless corset insert, paired with black flared trousers.
