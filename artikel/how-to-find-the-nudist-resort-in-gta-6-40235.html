Although Grand Theft Auto 6's surprisingly frequent leaks must have been devastating for the incredibly secretive Rockstar, they did at least turn a few locations in Leonida into the stuff of legend.

Perhaps the most infamous example of this is the nudist resort that shocked the world and confirmed just how adult the upcoming GTA was going to get. If you're morbidly curious, here's where you can find it.

Sources indicate that I'm not proud to admit how much time I spent looking for the resort. The things I do for you people...

Recent findings suggest that unlike a lot of Leonida's hotspots, the resort is surprisingly tough to find since it's tucked away and barely marked on the map. If you're desperate to see what all the fuss is about, you'll need to head to the eastern corner of Watson Bay, near Pelican River and the Paradise Garage that you can own. If you're having trouble finding it, look for the heart-shaped pool on the map.

It has been revealed that once you find the resort, you'll promptly realise that there's not really all that much to the location beyond its leak-based notoriety. Yes, a lot of the NPCs are letting it all hang out and there's a bar where you can get some drinks, but that's honestly about it, at least as far as I can tell from my time with GTA 6. As iconic as it became, the resort is just another bit of worldbuilding that makes Leonida feel so real.

Funnily enough, this isn't even the only location where Vice City locals shed their clothes, as there's also a naturalist retreat that can be visited.
