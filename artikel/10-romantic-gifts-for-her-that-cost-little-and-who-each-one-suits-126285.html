A romantic gift for your girlfriend or wife lands best when it shows you know her, and many of the most personal gifts cost little. Choose one drawn from your history together or her everyday life, and match it to the kind of partner she is.

Valentine’s shoppers keep spending more anyway. Those planning to celebrate in 2026 budgeted an average of $199.78 each for gifts, a record, and expected to spend $7 billion on jewelry, more than on any other category, by the National Retail Federation’s survey.

In a recent development, A gift’s price matters less than givers think. In a Stanford study published in 2009, givers expected recipients to appreciate a gift more the more it cost, since they took price as a sign of thought, but the recipients reported no such link.

It has been revealed that givers also misjudge sentimental gifts. In a 2017 study in the Journal of Consumer Psychology, givers chose between a gift matched to a recipient’s tastes and one tied to a loved one or a shared memory. They picked the first much more often than recipients wanted, and the researchers traced the gap to givers feeling less sure that the recipient would like the sentimental one.
