Industry observers note that the Reds want to build the greatest possible team on the planet. In order to do that they want to assemble an empire. Possibly the best empire that has ever been.

Julian Ward is leading the operation, but behind him one of the brains behind the project, is the aforementioned Marques.

He was poached from Benfica in order to bring some of the best young players to Liverpool and he has already done a phenomenal job at that.

Liverpool are already bursting to the brim with exciting young talent and there is more to come.

Industry observers note that liverpool's mission to dominate the youth marketIt all started with Armin Pecsi last year. Liverpool won the race for the Golden Ball nominee's signature and brought him to Anfield. Since then Liverpool have signed Mor Talla Ndiaye, Ifeanyi Ndukwe, Samuel Martinez, Lucca Brughmanns, Djylian N'Guessan. They have taken Sheriff Bah and Musa Jatta on trial and are also expected to sign Sidy Barhama Ndiaye when he turns 18-years-old. It's already a spectacular cohort of talent but there will be more on the way.
