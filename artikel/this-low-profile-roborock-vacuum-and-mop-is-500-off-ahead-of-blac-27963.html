According to recent reports, the Roborock Qrevo Edge 2 robot vacuum and mop is down to $799.99 from $1,299.99 in an early Black Friday deal, saving you $500 and bringing it to its lowest price to date. Much of its appeal comes from a slim 3.1-inch profile that lets it reach under beds, sofas, and other low furniture where taller robot vacuums often can’t fit. It manages this with a feature Roborock calls RetractSense, which checks the clearance as the robot approaches a tight gap and lowers the laser navigation tower into the body, leaving a camera with a 100-degree field of view to guide it underneath.

Industry observers note that beyond its low profile, the Qrevo Edge 2 pairs 25,000Pa of suction with a split DuoDivide main brush that guides hair toward the suction opening, so long strands and pet fur are less likely to end up wound around the roller. Edges get extra attention too, since its FlexiArm Arc side brush reaches out into corners while its mop module swivels to get closer to walls, with the two mop pads spinning at 200 RPM as they work. It also adjusts its cleaning based on whether it encounters dry or wet dirt, boosting suction for dust and lifting its main brush over spills. For all that adaptability on the floor, getting between rooms can be trickier—the robot lacks an adjustable chassis to lift itself over obstacles, so it can struggle with thresholds higher than 0.8 inches.

Recent findings suggest that once a cleaning run wraps up, most of the upkeep happens at the dock, which washes and dries the mop pads and empties dust into a bag rated for up to 65 days. That noted, its onboard water tank is relatively small at 2.3 fluid ounces, potentially requiring extra trips to the dock during larger mopping jobs. There's also no dedicated cleaning solution tank, meaning you'll have to add approved cleaning additives manually to the fresh water. The rest of the fine-tuning happens in the Roborock app, where you can set cleaning schedules, adjust suction power and water flow, and choose how the robot handles carpets.
