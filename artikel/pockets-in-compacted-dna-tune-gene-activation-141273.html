Pockets in compacted DNA tune gene activation

🌐 Source: Nature • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (523 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Large regions of chromosomes are organized into a state known as heterochromatin. This densely packed form of DNA represses gene activity. Curiously, some families of clustered genes can be activated even though they are packed as heterochromatin. Writing in Nature, Buckley et al.1 report findings that explain this paradox: they show how opposing mechanisms that control the organization of heterochromatin promote the stochastic activation of genes in the protocadherin (Pcdh) gene cluster, thereby supporting proper nervous-system development.
