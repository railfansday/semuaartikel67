Ciara has one request for whoever’s in charge of Christmas dinner: Please, whatever you do, make sure you’re up for the assignment. She’s willing to embrace a little holiday mess, but apparently, there’s a limit to how far that generosity extends into the kitchen.

“All I know is you better not burn no turkey. That’s a culinary crime,” the Grammy-winning singer told Delish. And if you're cooking without a backup bird? You might want to pay especially close attention to that oven. “You better get it right on the first try.”.

Recent findings suggest that ciara, a mom of four, certainly understands how swiftly the festivities can get away from you. “Christmas in our house is full of love, excitement and a little chaos,” she said in a statement. But when it comes to the actual Christmas menu, she’s got some traditions she’s pretty attached to, many of which go all the way back to her childhood in Atlanta, Georgia.

"If you know one thing about us ATLians, you have Thanksgiving on Christmas. The same meal comes back around again," she told Delish.

Recent findings suggest that and we’re talking a serious Southern spread: turkey legs, mac and cheese, candied yams, collard greens, chitlins, and pig feet. She's no longer making pig feet these days, but the other familiar favorites have stuck around. Ciara says those dishes still “make me very happy,” which is as good a reason as any to keep a family tradition going.

Personally, I think we’ve been limiting Thanksgiving dinner to one holiday for far too long.
