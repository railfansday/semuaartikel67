Last night, Diego Moreira scored one of the most crucial goals of his career – his first for Belgium – and AC Milan are left smiling.

In a recent development, moreira left for international duty in fine form; he’s seemingly discovered himself again at San Siro and is clearly enjoying his football. It’s understandable why, too. In seven games, he’s got seven contributions in Serie A and he’s firmly on course for his best ever season.
