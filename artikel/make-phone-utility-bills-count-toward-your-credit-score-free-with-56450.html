Make Phone & Utility Bills Count Toward Your Credit Score – Free With Experian Boost

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that on-time phone and utility payments rarely reach your credit report, so they don’t help your score. Link the bank account you pay them from to Experian Boost, a free tool that adds them to your Experian report. It’s most worth doing when your credit history is short or your score sits just under a lender’s cutoff.

These bills usually reach a credit report only when one goes unpaid and lands in collections, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau says.

Boost doesn’t wait for the phone tech giant or utility to report. It reads two years of payments in the account you connect and lets you pick which qualifying bills go on your Experian report. Experian indicates you right away whether your FICO Score 8, one version of the FICO score, went up.

Only your Experian report gets those payments, so lenders checking your reports at the other two credit bureaus, Equifax and TransUnion, won’t see the gain. Experian also says not every lender uses a score that Boost affects, and most mortgage lenders don’t.

Payments by check or through apps like Venmo don’t count. Some rent paid online counts too, though Boost is just one of several ways to get rent reported. You also need enough history for a FICO Score, meaning at least one account active six months or more and one reported in the last six.

Sources indicate that take a renter with one credit card who links the account she pays her phone and electric bills from. Boost finds two years of on-time payments on both. Experian claims people whose score rose gained 14 points on average. The trade-off is letting Experian see that account’s payment history. Some scores also stay flat or even drop. If hers drops, she can disconnect, and Experian says the score should return to where it was.

Connect your bill-paying account now, ahead of any credit application, and leave it linked so Experian keeps checking for fresh on-time payments.
