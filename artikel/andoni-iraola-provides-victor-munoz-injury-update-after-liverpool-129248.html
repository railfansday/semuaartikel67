Andoni Iraola has provided an revision on Victor Munoz after the winger was forced off injured in Liverpool’s 3-0 win over Club Brugge.

The only downside on a night the Reds they kept their first clean sheet in the Champions League this season, Munoz took a heavy blow to the knee and was replaced by Rio Ngumoha in the 42nd minute.

The Spaniard, who scored his third Premier League goal in the routing of Manchester United on the weekend, was in visible agony as he was being treated but Iraola has revealed that the pain had subsided by the end of the match.

It has been revealed that “He was with a lot of pain when he left the pitch. He was a lot more positive when the game has finished,” said Iraola.

“It has been a knock on the knee so now we have to see how it presents tomorrow and see if it is just a knock – but it is very early to tell if there is something more.

“In the moment it was very painful and after the game I talked to him and he was more optimistic.”.

Iraola was nevertheless delighted with his two £100m men Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz as the pair’s goals edged Liverpool closer to Champions League qualification..

Isak scored at either end of the game to take his tally to 10 for the season – on target in his last six away games – with Wirtz continuing his good form with a sixth in five games for club and country.

Victory lifted Liverpool into fifth place on 12 points – in the two seasons of the new format, 16 has been enough to guarantee the top-eight finish needed to avoid a two-legged play-off in February.

Industry observers note that “I am very pleased with the result and the performance overall. The result, 3-0, you can think it was an easy game but I don’t think it was,” mentioned Iraola.

Industry observers note that on Isak, he added: “Obviously he is in a great moment right now. In the first half especially he helped us a lot in the link-up, we were finding him very easily.

“The way they pressed was very aggressive and you have to find the last line straight away. Alex was helping us a lot in those situations.

Sources indicate that “In the second half it was more about defending a little bit lower. We knew they would push forward and give spaces to exploit.

“Sometimes they were leaving him one-v-one against the centre-backs and in one of those moments he has killed the game.”.

Isak mentioned he was “in a good place…hopefully it gets better from here”.

He added on TNT sports: “The attack in general is beginning to click. We have a lot of different combinations and whoever comes in we have a dangerous attack. It feels good.”.

Isak and Wirtz are starting to form a profitable partnership, but Iraola believes the overall team performances are helping them flourish.

“Both are now in a good place,” he said. “When the team overall is playing well and we are getting good results, everyone looks a little better. When everything collectively is not there, everyone looks a little bit worse.

“The important part is they help the team to play well, to win games, to get results, and if we play well then we have chances, we are solid, everyone will play their part and will look better. Alex and Flo right now are in a good moment.”.
