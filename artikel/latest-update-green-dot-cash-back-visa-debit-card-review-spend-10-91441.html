Get the Green Dot Cash Back Visa Debit Card only if you’ll spend at least $1,000 a month on it and claim your cash back every year. That much spending waives the next month’s $9.95 fee, and most purchases earn 2% back. If you can open a regular bank account, compare a free one first.

At 2% back, it takes $497.50 a month in purchases that earn cash back just to cover the $9.95 monthly fee, so a lighter spender pays more than the card returns. It’s a reloadable debit card from Green Dot Bank, sold in stores or opened online, for people who use a card instead of a traditional bank account.

According to recent reports, A cardholder without a bank account who spends $1,000 a month on purchases that earn cash back collects $240 a year and pays the fee only in the first month. At lower spending, a free checking account costs nothing a month, and so does Green Dot’s own GO2bank after a payroll or government benefits direct deposit.
