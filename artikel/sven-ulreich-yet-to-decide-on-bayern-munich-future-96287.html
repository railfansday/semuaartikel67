Bayern Munich goalkeeper Sven Ulreich stated that he is yet to make a decision about his future.

“I will be speaking with Bayern in the near future about what happens next, what the club wants,” he exclusively told Kicker.

In a recent development, ulreich had been number two behind Manuel Neuer for several years at Bayern before dropping behind Jonas Urbig in the pecking order. With the likely situation that the World Cup-winning keeper can retire at the end of the season, Ulreich calling it a day on his career is also a possibility. “I can’t speak for Manu about what happens after the season, but it’s not like I’m making everything dependent on him,” he added.

Recent findings suggest that sitting on an expiring contract, the 38-year-old is open to anything that might happen going forward and understands things can change rapidly. “Anything is possible as long as I feel good, healthy, and fit. That’s the case at the moment. But I’ve also learned that things can change quickly. I live in the here and now, and right now I enjoy going out on the pitch every day,” he explained.

It has been revealed that bayern may struggle to find a reliable keeper like Ulreich, who admits that giving it your best in training and keeping the No.1 on their toes comes as part of the parcel. “It’s vital to always try to deliver strong performances in training,” he said. “If only to show the goalkeeper in front of you, ‘hey, you have to give your all.’.

“He should also signal to the team that there’s someone back there who gives top performances in every training session.”.

While 23-year-old keeper Urbig is expected to be the long-term replacement for Neuer, Bayern are still looking for a good backup keeper – Finn Dahmen of Augsburg, Marvin Schwäbe of Cologne, and Loris Karius of Schalke, according to Bild’s ‘Bayern Insider’ podcast.
