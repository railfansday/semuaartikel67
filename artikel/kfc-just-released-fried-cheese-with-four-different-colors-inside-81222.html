KFC has unveiled a bizarre new fried cheese snack that looks ordinary on the outside, but reveals four different colors of melted cheese when pulled apart.

The fast food giant is no stranger to unusual menu creations, but its latest offering in Malaysia might be one of its most colorful yet.

Called Colourful Cheezy Cheese, the latest item launched on November 18, 2026, featuring a crispy, golden-brown exterior packed with stretchy, multicolored cheese.

According to local promotional listings, Colourful Cheezy Cheese is available at participating KFC restaurants across Malaysia for RM7.49, equivalent to approximately $1.80 USD.

It has been revealed that the snack resembles a large, breaded mozzarella stick flattened into a patty. However, pulling it apart reveals four different colors of gooey cheese rather than the usual white or yellow filling.

Despite the unusual appearance, KFC hasn’t publicly detailed how the different colors are achieved or whether they have distinct flavors.

The product is also being offered as part of KFC Malaysia’s fresh Holiday Bucket meals, with six-piece and ten-piece chicken bundles including one or two colorful cheese patties, respectively.

It’s another unusual creation from KFC’s international restaurants, which have been experimenting with increasingly outrageous menu items throughout 2026.

In August, KFC China left the chain’s main social media account baffled after unveiling a fried chicken burger featuring Oreo-flavored buns and sweet dipping sauces.

More recently, KFC South Korea introduced a massive Doom Triple Down sandwich as part of its Avengers: Doomsday collaboration, stacking three pieces of fried chicken together without a traditional bun.

For now, Colourful Cheezy Cheese is exclusive to Malaysia, with no announced prepares to bring the rainbow-colored snack to restaurants in the United States or other markets.

KFC has also yet to confirm how long the colorful fried cheese will remain available.
