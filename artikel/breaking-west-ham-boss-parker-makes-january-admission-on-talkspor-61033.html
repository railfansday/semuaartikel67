West Ham United manager Scott Parker has made it clear that January transfers are not at the moment at the forefront of his thinking, despite the Hammers’ obvious need to strengthen their squad. Speaking to talkSPORT this morning, Parker suggested that his immediate priority is working with the players already at his disposal.

Asked directly whether West Ham would have money to spend and bring in fresh faces, Parker acknowledged that the January window would be considered if necessary. However, he explained that since arriving at Rush Green, his attention has been almost entirely focused on his players and coaching staff.

According to recent reports, personally, I think he’s absolutely right. Even with the best will in the world, and assuming West Ham bring in some brilliant players in January, there are still nearly two months’ worth of results to fight for with the players presently available. Parker would be absolutely mad to spend too much time concentrating on transfers now.

Speaking during his talkSPORT interview, Parker explained that he had barely spoken to anybody outside his immediate coaching environment since taking charge.

“I’m sure there’ll be an eye in January if need be,” he noted, before explaining that his main priority was establishing the habits and principles he wants from his players.

That seems perfectly sensible to me.

Sources indicate that west Ham have a director of football in Nils Koppen, who can deal with precisely this type of thing. There is no reason for Parker to be spending his first few weeks in the job obsessing about transfer targets when the current squad desperately needs coaching.

After all, Claret & Hugh has already revealed that Koppen is working on January recruitment, including an approach to a potential striker signing.

The work is happening behind the scenes, and Parker doesn’t need to be personally involved in every conversation.

Parker can outsource his transfer concerns to Koppen.

As Claret & Hugh has already revealed following contact with the football club, January business is being planned.

Every message we’ve received from sources inside the club indicates that promotion back to the Premier League is the overwhelming priority.

Industry observers note that of course, I expect funds to be made available in January. That doesn’t mean West Ham have unlimited money to spend, but the club’s determination to get promoted has been made abundantly clear.

And whilst we’ve previously reported on the financial constraints surrounding January recruitment, that doesn’t mean the club will simply sit on its hands.

But the January transfer window cannot rescue the points we fail to collect in November and December.

That is why Parker is right to concentrate on coaching.

Another interesting aspect of the interview was Parker’s discussion of the mentality required to succeed in the Championship.

He explained that players coming down from the Premier League have often spent months losing football matches, only to find themselves suddenly expected to win almost every week.

That psychological transition, he suggested, can be enormous.

The players clearly have ability, but Parker needs to establish the mentality and habits that allow them to produce it consistently.

If he can do that over the next few weeks, West Ham will be in a considerably stronger position when January arrives.

And if Koppen can then strengthen the areas that need improving, all the better.

For now, though, I’m delighted Parker is concentrating on the job immediately in front of him.
