West Ham have, thankfully, avoided some of the problems which competing clubs have endured with huge swathes of first team players injured. Cardiff City struggled with eight players injured before the West Ham tie, but it didn’t stop the Bluebirds racing into a three goal lead and almost inflicting what would, in view of their first team being ‘decimated’, have been a humiliating defeat on their visitors.

It has been revealed that by comparison, the Hammers have largely escaped such wholesale problems. Manor Solomon’s back problem and Kyle Walker Peters’ muscle matter seemed to be the main worries until sportwitness.co.uk revealed another squad injury that had been flying largely under the radar:.

Recent findings suggest that “West Ham United‘s Keiber Lamadrid went under the knife to resolve the physical problems that hampered his growth and development in England….During pre-season, he again suffered a knee injury in the friendly against 1. FC Magdeburg. There were concerns about a serious setback, but he returned to the pitch in August.

Lamadrid hasn’t featured for West Ham – now we know why.

The player hasn’t made it to the match day squad since mid-September. Meridiano [news outlet] in Venezuela now states that this is because of fitness concerns, which have bothered him since moving to England.

Industry observers note that keiber Lamadrid has ‘put an end to the series of physical problems’ that stopped him from enjoying regular minutes with the Hammers. He did this by undergoing knee surgery.”.

An interesting take on Lamadrid’s absence from match day squads.

It had been assumed that he was simply not impressing Nuno in training but the reveal that he’s been dealing with a long standing knee problem might well offer optimism that there’s a lot more to come from the young midfielder-slash-winger when he’s fully recuperated.
