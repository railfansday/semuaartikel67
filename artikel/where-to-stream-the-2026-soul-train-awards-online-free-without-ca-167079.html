Recent findings suggest that the re-branded Soul Train Awards are back tonight after a two-year break. The show was put on indefinite hiatus last year before it was revived for 2026 thanks to a partnership with Spotify.

In a recent development, the 2026 Soul Train Awards took place earlier this month in Chicago, where Don Cornelius created Soul Train in 1971.
