Mauricio Pochettino will be hoping to put his nightmare Manchester United debut behind him when he takes his new side to Portugal to face Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

The Argentine endured a horror start to his tenure at Old Trafford with a heavy 4-0 defeat by Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

The Red Devils were down to ten men inside eight minutes when Leny Yoro was sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity.

United suffered no injury blows on Merseyside, but the result was “painful” according to their newly introduced head coach, and he will be looking to put things right immediately in Europe’s top competition on Wednesday.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss sprung a surprise with his inclusion of Matthijs de Ligt in his starting XI at Anfield, with the Dutchman having been sidelined for over a year with a persistent back injury.

The centre-half looked awkward for much of the match, and his selection was scrutinised intensely afterwards.

It remains to be seen whether he will play again here, with Harry Maguire introduced in the first half and shoring up United’s back five.

The England international should start here, perhaps alongisde Yoro, who will not be suspended for this match with his ban only for league games.

That could see Lisandro Martinez plugged at left-back once more, although the Argentine had to shift back inside after Yoro’s red card, with Dorgu shifting back to wing-back from his left wing berth.

Diogo Dalot failed to truly impress on Merseyside and could see himself rotated for Noussair Mazraoui, while in the engine room, Youri Tielemans was hooked for Mason Mount in the second half.

Bryan Mbeumo was the man sacrificed following the sending-off as Pochettino resorted to damage limitation, but the Cameroon international should get the nod here.
