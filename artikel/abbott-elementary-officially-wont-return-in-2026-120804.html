Recent findings suggest that it's a big season for Abbott Elementary, as the ABC school-set sitcom celebrates a big milestone. The series will mark its 100th episode next month with a big trip to Disneyland, a theme park that just happens to be owned by ABC's corporate parent Disney. However, after airing its mid-season finale the next week, the show will go on hiatus over the holidays to return in the New Year.

In a recent development, much like the Super Bowl MVP, Abbott Elementary is going to Disneyland. The school's step team overcame the odds to win their regional finals, and the prize was an all-expenses-paid trip to the Anaheim theme park. Naturally, the school's teachers have been recruited as chaperones, and hijinks will ensue in the show's 100th episode, "One Hundred": it will premiere on December 2. A week later, on December 9, the show will air its winter finale, "Parents," which in which Jacob Hill (Chris Perfetti) will celebrate the holidays with a visit from his parents.

Don't worry: the show won't be gone for long. Abbott Elementary is set to return from its winter hiatus on January 13, 2027, with "Viral," an all-upcoming episode penned by Rebekka Pesqueira, a writer and story editor on the series. In fact, as that's the ninth episode of the season, it probably doesn't even stand as the midway mark, as Abbott's previous seasons (save for its first, when it was a mid-season replacement, and its third, which was interrupted by the Hollywood strikes) have all had 22 episodes.

There's been a bit of a shake-up at Abbott Elementary this season. After narrowly avoiding closure in the season 5 finale, Abbott has had to deal with an influx of new students from schools that weren't so lucky. That's strained their resources to the limits and necessitated bringing in a new assistant principal: Gregory Eddie (Tyler James Williams). It's appropriate, since he originally applied to be Abbott's principal, only to be relegated to supply teacher. He's also planning another big change: he's going to propose to girlfriend and fellow teacher Janine Teagues (Quinta Brunson). Meanwhile, Principal Ava Coleman (Janelle James) is still adjusting to being in a relationship with the working-class O'Shon (Matthew Law), Jacob and Melissa Schemmenti (Lisa Ann Walter) are still at-times uneasy housemates, and Barbara Howard (Sheryl Lee Ralph) is contemplating retirement. Janitor Mr. Johnson (William Stanford Davis) continues to be Mr. Johnson.

Abbott Elementary will return to ABC on January 13, 2027, with "Viral." Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
