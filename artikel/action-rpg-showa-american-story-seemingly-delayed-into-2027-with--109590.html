Action RPG Showa American Story seemingly delayed into 2027, with no comment on why from developer NEKCOM

Despite being announced back in 2022 with an initial release window of 2025, NEKCOM's action RPG Showa American Story has been set for a late 2026 release date for a while now. That seems to have changed, however, as the release date on its Steam page has slipped to 2027 without any sort of announcement as to why.

In conversation with Automaton last month, the game's director Xiangyu Luo stated that "The game's content is already completely finished, and we're now at the stage of polishing its overall quality." As such, it would seem that this delay is to give the game a little more breathing room for bug fixes and quality assurance work, rather than rushing a holiday release just to hit its stated release window.

Based on my own time with Showa American Story, taking a little more time to clean it up would be a welcome choice. When I played a demo at Gamescom 2026, I was a little stunned by how awful it felt to play. The protagonist was a blank slate designed to be pretty and nothing more, and the combat was mind-numbingly oversimplified, its challenge mainly derived from the need to wrestle with its janky controls and the noticeable delay between button presses and attacks. In that same interview, Luo notes an awareness of the game being compared to the Yakuza/Like a Dragon series in being a 'wacky' open-world action RPG, saying that he's "happy that players are making that association", as "it means they've recognised Showa American Story as a rich RPG with plenty of content to sink their teeth into." I can respect him taking the comparison in stride, but what I played seemed like a pale imitation of LAD that missed what makes that series more than it seems on the surface.

I can hope that this delay can result in improvements to the critiques the game has faced — Luo did mention that "we've heard many valuable comments regarding the feel of the controls and how the action is presented" — and that Showa American Story releases in a good state sometime in 2027.
