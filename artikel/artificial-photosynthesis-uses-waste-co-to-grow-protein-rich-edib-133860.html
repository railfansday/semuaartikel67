Artificial photosynthesis uses waste CO₂ to grow protein-rich edible fungus in seven days

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (82 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that while many people think first of meat when they think about dietary protein, most protein ultimately depends on plants, since livestock feed on grass, hay and other plants. Meeting the demand for protein is taxing on the environment, requiring massive amounts of land and water while creating greenhouse gas emissions. And that demand is expected to require nearly 50% more agricultural production by 2050 than in 2012, according to the U.N. Food and Agriculture Organization.
