Bitcoin Price Slips Below $64,000 as Strategy (MSTR) Falls Below $100

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, bitcoin price slipped near $64,000 on Tuesday afternoon, as Strategy (MSTR) and Coinbase (COIN) shares fell and traders waited on Nvidia’s first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO. The bitcoin price had climbed back to about $65,000 before the U.S. open, after holding near that level on Monday. Selling picked up once stocks turned lower, and bitcoin price slipped to about $63,300 at its low.

Strategy shares were recently down more than 5% to about $97, dropping below $100 and trading under the company’s STRC preferred stock, which was little changed near $99.

It is the first time the common has traded below the preferred since mid-August. The stock is also down about 20% from its Nov. 10 close near $121.

Strategy added 485 bitcoin last week, lifting its holdings to 850,849 BTC. The shares have kept falling with the bitcoin price. Coinbase was recently down about 3% to about $116. Robinhood (HOOD) and Circle (CRCL) dropped about 4% and 5%.

In a recent development, bitcoin miners were mostly lower. MARA Holdings (MARA), CleanSpark (CLSK) and IREN (IREN) each fell about 3% to 4%, and Cipher Mining (CIFR) was down about 2%.

Recent findings suggest that hut 8 (HUT) was a modest exception, recently up about 1%. Riot Platforms (RIOT) and Galaxy Digital (GLXY) were little changed, while Core Scientific (CORZ) and BitMine (BMNR) were also lower.

The broader market was also weaker late in the session. The Dow Jones Industrial Average was recently down about 1.7%, after falling as much as about 1,000 points, or 2%, at its session low.

The S&P 500 was down about 1.2% and the Nasdaq Composite about 1%.

It has been revealed that nvidia shares were recently down about 1.4%, after falling more than 3% earlier in the session. The chipmaker reports results after the bell, with analysts expecting revenue of about $109 billion. Interim CEO Colette Kress will lead her first earnings call in the role.

Huang, in his first public statement since resigning last week, mentioned he would be “honored” to return as CEO if the board decided it was in the best interest of shareholders, employees, the company and America.

Industry observers note that sources told CNBC on Monday that the board is not considering bringing him back. The bitcoin price bounced from its low as Nvidia shares pared some of their losses, and was recently near $64,000.

Recent findings suggest that new York Fed President John Williams noted Tuesday that the AI investment boom could mean “we may need to raise rates more.” He played down a half-point hike in December. “I don’t think the situation calls for dramatic action,” Williams said.

It has been revealed that fed Governor Christopher Waller did not address the December decision in a payments speech Tuesday. Last week, he said a half-point hike should be considered if October’s core inflation ran at 0.3% or higher, and it did.

Markets still point to a quarter-point increase at the Fed’s Dec. 8-9 meeting. Minutes from the October meeting are due Wednesday. The dollar was little changed, still near the 19-month high it touched on Monday.

Sources indicate that U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday, ending a seven-session outflow streak. Wells Fargo more than doubled its bitcoin ETF stake to $2.35 billion, and Goldman Sachs did the same, lifting its holdings to $1.28 billion.

In a recent development, anthropic is expected to price its IPO on Wednesday, with shares set to start trading Thursday. A move back above $65,000 would reverse Tuesday’s decline, while a break of the weekend low near $62,000 would put last week’s flush back in view.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $64,000.
