Nick Candy: UK Seeing Wealth 'Exodus'

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that there has been an 'exodus' of wealth out of the UK due to tax and regulations notes Nick Candy, CEO of Candy Capital and Treasurer of political party Reform UK. Instead, London's richest residents are moving to places such as Monaco, Italy and the UAE. Candy spoke to Bloomberg's Francine Lacqua on 'The Pulse'. (Source: Bloomberg).
