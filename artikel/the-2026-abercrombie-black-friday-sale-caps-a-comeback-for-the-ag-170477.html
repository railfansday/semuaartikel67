Sources indicate that this era of Abercrombie & Fitch has a lot going for it: the cuts are killer, the fabrics are resilient (and soft as hell), and the prices are right, year round. The only thing that can juice the metrics any further, really, is a substantial ol’ Black Friday sale. And because the Moose is always down to play ball, we have pretty much everything from this season and beyond marked down in a big ol’ way. For those into specifics, that means that it’s all discounted 25-50% for the weekend.

But we do mean everything, which is, in scientific terms, a whole lot of clothing, which is why we thought a little guide might be nice. So, we honed in on knitwear, denim, pants and the perpetually-underrated outerwear, and boiled it down to the best of the best. According to recent reports, this year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 27, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.

Cyber Monday always falls on the Monday after Thanksgiving, which means this year it will be on November 30. Most Cyber Monday sales will extend through the end of the week, but that’s not a given. Cyber Monday begins Monday, December 2nd.

What are the best things to buy at Abercrombie & Fitch?

We’ve written about Abercrombie’s polos plenty of times because they’re just so good for the price. Start there. Then, hit up their jeans and cargo pants. You’ll be surprised by the former and not the latter (the cargo pants were always good). Then, grab some outerwear. The Moose does a great job of referencing vintage workwear garments and their jackets and coats are where it shines.
