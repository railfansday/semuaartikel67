Jude Bellingham has played down comparisons to Real Madrid legend Zinedine Zidane after inspiring victory in the Champions League with a sensational goal against PSV.

The England international juggled the ball in the air before swiping a volley into the back of the net, leaving fans and teammates in awe, including Bernardo Silva: “It was one of those moments when you just go, ‘Wow!’”.

The win maintains Madrid’s perfect Champions League record, elevating them to serious contenders to dethrone two-time defending champions Paris Saint-Germain, while rising up The Independent’s power rankings.

And after Mourinho described Bellingham as “without any doubt, the best midfielder in the world,” comparisons continue with one of Madrid’s all-time greatest players: Zidane.

But Bellingham was quick to distance himself from the conversation, instead placing the France manager in an entirely different category.

"I was a bit surprised, honestly. I knew that I could control the ball well and get it into my grasp. But normally from there, I'm looking to see who's on as an option to play through,” Bellingham said when asked about his goal before addressing talk that he is on the same level as Zidane.

According to recent reports, "It popped up nicely and I thought I'm just going to hit it. So yeah, it was a nice goal and notable for the team, being the second goal, and gave us control.

"I think Zidane is much better than me. He's much more elegant and he's my idol.”.

Mourinho was left stunned by his player’s latest impact, pinpointing just what makes him so good.

It has been revealed that "He is an amazing player that has everything," the Portuguese manager remarked. "He has physicality, he understands the game, he has absolutely everything. Today, he has changed how the game was evolving.".

Sources indicate that “Obviously Jude is a different type of player and he gave us what we didn't have in the first half,” Mourinho mentioned. “He changed the game, scored an incredible goal.”.
