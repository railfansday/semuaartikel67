Jennifer Lopez is opening up about life as an empty nester after her 18-year-old twins, Max and Emme, started college this past fall.

“They went to college. It’s crazy. I still have on my phone [a photo of] them on my lap as babies. It goes so fast, just like everybody indicates,” Lopez, 57, shared on the Sunday, November 15, episode of The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.

The Office Romance actress remarked that she was incredibly emotional from the time her children — whom she shares with ex-husband Marc Anthony — graduated from high school right through to their departures for college.

“It’s all the feelings. It’s one of those moments in life where you’re just like, you’re sad, you’re melancholy, but you’re excited and you’re hopeful. There’s so many feelings that go with it,” Lopez explained. “I cried for six months straight [from the] two months before they graduated [high school] until I dropped them off [at college] and a few weeks after that.”.

Despite missing her children, Lopez shared that she is feeling a sense of freedom now that her children are college-aged, describing herself as being in a “happy era.”.

She said, “[It’s] the first time since I was like, in my early 20s, that I haven’t been with someone, had my kids. I’m alone. I’m free. I can do whatever the f*** I want. It’s kind of amazing but also weird.”.

According to recent reports, the multihyphenate star is now back in the studio recording fresh music and embracing her freedom. (One of Lopez’s upcoming tracks is inspired by her kids leaving for college, she revealed in October.).

According to recent reports, “When the kids were leaving, I was having so many feelings and so many emotions that I went into the studio and started kind of playing around and making music, and now I’ve made 20 songs,” she said. “I feel like I’m only halfway done. I need to be in there longer.”.

Sources indicate that she added, “This does feel like a happy era where I feel unencumbered with anything kind of unhealthy. It’s just a very healthy time of my life. I’m on my own. I feel like it’s another coming of age that happens in our lives. I think a lot of parents will relate to that.”.

Back in May, Lopez said she had been “crying for two months” as her children prepared to graduate from high school.

“It’s fine. I want them to be happy, go where they want to go, and do what they want to do,” the proud mama shared on Jimmy Kimmel Live! at the time.

“I remember being that age and thinking I couldn’t wait to get out in the world and do my thing. I really wanted to get out,” she noted. “All year people have been asking me, ‘Oh, they’re going away to college, is it going to be terrible?’ No, it’s going to be great. I want them to go out there. I want them to do what they want to do. They have big dreams. They have things they want to do. It’s going to be great.”.
