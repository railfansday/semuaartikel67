Industry observers note that one of the biggest discounts is the 2.ZERØGRAND Laser Wingtip Oxford, marked down 58 percent. It’s like a mashup of an athletic sneaker and a traditional dress shoe. The nubuck and polyester upper features a laser-cut wingtip pattern that puts a modern twist on classic broguing, while the lightweight sole keeps things from feeling too stiff or clunky. I’ve found that the Grandfoam—the brand’s proprietary cushioning—has a slightly squishy feel underfoot, but in a good way. There’s enough give to keep my feet comfortable without making the shoe feel unstable. Plus, the hybrid design looks just as good with chinos as it does with dress pants.

For something a little more laid-back, check out the Grand Atlantic Sneaker at 44 percent off. It has that clean, tennis-inspired look that plays well with relaxed, business-casual outfits or straight-leg jeans and a sweater. What really sells me is how lightweight it feels. There’s a rubber sole underneath, but I barely notice it when I’m walking on these New York City sidewalks that aren’t exactly forgiving. I always throw this sneaker on for our photo shoot days that tend to go over eight hours.

Now, if you’re looking for something a little more traditional, the Harmon Grand Cap Toe Oxford (30 percent off) is the way to go. Unlike the other two styles, this lace-up model leans more into classic dress-shoe territory, with a sleek cap-toe design that feels right at home with tailored trousers or a suit. I’d pull this out for a holiday party or any occasion that calls for looking a little more buttoned-up.

Sources indicate that I’ve covered a number of Black Friday sales, but this is Cole Haan’s best one yet. I’m already seeing sizes fly off the shelves, so there’s no telling what deals will be left by the end of the day. If you’ve been eyeing a new pair of dress shoes or dress sneakers, I’d take advantage of these markdowns ASAP.
