Kalshi halts Trump mention markets

🌐 Source: Thehill • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Kalshi has halted markets allowing customers to trade on what President Trump might say on specific dates in response to an order by a federal regulator. The prediction market platform mentioned the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) issued an order on Friday to pause Trump-related mention markets, and the organization is “complying with the order.”….
