Sources indicate that bringing one of literature's greatest works to life is no easy task, but Netflix is rising to the challenge with Pride and Prejudice. Written by Jane Austen, Pride and Prejudice was her third novel and the second to be published, released in 1813. At the center is the dynamic between Elizabeth Bennet (whose personal growth drives the story) and the proud, wealthy Mr. Darcy. Through their time together, Elizabeth recognizes the mistakes of judging people too quickly and learns how to distinguish fake charm from true kindness.

Recent findings suggest that in the century since Pride and Prejudice was published, it has been adapted for the screen several times, beginning with a 1938 BBC television broadcast written and directed by Michael Barry. Unfortunately, that version is permanently lost because it aired live before television recording technology existed. Memorable adaptations include the Academy Award-winning movie starring Greer Garson and Laurence Olivier, partly based on Helen Jerome's 1935 stage adaptation; Joe Wright's acclaimed 2005 feature starring Keira Knightley and Matthew Macfadyen; andthe 1995 BBC miniseries starring Colin Firth and Jennifer Ehle. Now, the upcoming Netflix version joins the bunch, premiering in just one week.

Starring Emma Corrin and Jack Lowden as Elizabeth and Darcy, Netflix's Pride and Prejudice series was first reported to be in development in October 2024 and officially revealed in April 2025. Filming began in the UK in the summer of 2025, with first-look behind-the-scenes images released in July. Bestselling author and screenwriter Dolly Alderton wrote the series, and Euros Lyn directed. The six-part series premieres on December 3, 2026, as Alderton announced at the Jane Austen Festival in September.
