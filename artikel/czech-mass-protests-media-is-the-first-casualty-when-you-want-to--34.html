Czech mass protests: Media is the first casualty 'when you want to attack democracy'

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (135 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that as hundreds of thousands of people gather in Prague to protest the Czech government's proposal to replace public broadcasting licence fees with direct state funding, Delano D'Souza is pleased to welcome Bára Stárek, Executive Director of Díky, že můžem. Stárek warns that the proposed changes offer "no real safeguards" against political influence, pointing to developments in Slovakia and Hungary. She also drew a parallel with the Velvet Revolution of 1989, recalling that "we didn't have a free media and we only had a state media" under communist rule. "Media is the first (line of) attack when you want to attack democracy," she stated. With the legislation expected to reach Parliament in January, Stárek believes the demonstrations will continue if the government refuses to engage in any discussions.
