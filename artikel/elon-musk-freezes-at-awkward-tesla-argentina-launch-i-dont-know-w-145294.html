Sources indicate that tesla officially launched in Argentina on Friday with President Javier Milei on stage in Buenos Aires, and Elon Musk joined by video from his plane to welcome the brand’s newest market. It didn’t go well.

Musk got through one line about Tesla before stalling out: “Sorry, I don’t really know what I want to say.” The call ended about two minutes in, without any real pitch for the Model 3 or Model Y that Tesla had just put on sale.

Recent findings suggest that the launch took place at Arenas Studio in Buenos Aires’ La Boca neighborhood. Milei spoke for less than eight minutes, then the host asked the room to welcome “our next guest,” and Musk popped up on the giant screen from what looked like an airplane cabin. Milei burst out laughing and bounced on his feet.

According to recent reports, musk started fine: “Hello, Argentina. Well, here we are. It’s a joy to share Tesla with all of you and to establish ourselves there.”.

JAJAJAJ QUE ES ESTO, Milei lo llamó de sorpresa a Elon Musk para que diga algo sobre el local de Tesla inaugurado y es lo mas INCÓMODO que vas a ver en tu vida, se quería ir a la mierda y COLGÓ, durísimo, ni Elon Musk lo soporta al gordo pelotudo pic.twitter.com/CrJkv4iu1x.

Recent findings suggest that then he started glancing off to each side. “Actually, I don’t really know what to say, other than thank you for this welcome to Tesla in Argentina,” he stated. He briefly mentioned a charging network with state-owned oil company YPF, thanked the audience again, and then: “Sorry, I don’t really know what I want to say.”.

Sources indicate that and that was it. The feed cut before he even said goodbye.

Industry observers note that milei tried to save it. Back on stage, he told the crowd that “those who do a lot, talk little,” and called Musk “the Leonardo da Vinci of the 21st century.”.

The clip is now spreading fast in Argentina, with one post of the video racking up over 1.1 million views overnight.

Tesla opened online orders on Friday for five versions of the Model 3 and Model Y, all imported from Gigafactory Shanghai. Here are the list prices, VAT included:.

Industry observers note that tesla adds about $600 in reservation, destination, and documentation fees on top of that, and first deliveries aren’t expected until March or April 2027. There are no dealers. Sales happen online, with showroom space at the Unicenter and Alcorta Shopping malls in Buenos Aires.

Argentina is Tesla’s fourth direct-sales market in South America after Chile, Colombia, and Uruguay, which launched in July. Tesla is late to the party, though. BYD already leads Argentina’s EV market after Milei’s government cut import taxes on electric vehicles.

What I really found awkward was hearing Musk stating "I'm not MAGA, I'm not sure what MAGA means" in his interview with CNBC.

Especially surprising after he declared, "I’m not just MAGA; I’m 'Dark MAGA,'" after spending half a billion dollars to support MAGA candidates, and after promoting the MAGA political agenda (which he has, in part, personally implemented through the DOGE).

At this point, I expect him to reappear in the guise of a Democrat and write a major, fat, one-billion-dollar check to a Democratic super PAC for the 2028 election cycle.

I am sure Musk has a lot on his mind right now, but selling Tesla vehicles clearly isn’t one of them, because he couldn’t come up with anything to say about it.

Launching a new market has one job: get people excited to buy the cars. Musk had a sitting president cheering for him on stage, a room full of fans, and every camera in Buenos Aires pointed at him. He couldn’t come up with a single reason to buy a Model Y.

This isn’t stage fright. Musk has been telling investors for two years that Tesla’s future is robotaxis and Optimus robots, not selling cars. Meanwhile, Tesla’s deliveries fell 8.6% to 1.636 million in 2025, its second annual decline in a row. The guy who is behind the brand doesn’t care about selling these cars anymore, and this clip shows it better than anything I’ve seen.
