📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Black Friday is officially here—and whether or not you made it through the roster of early sales that broke last week, it’s time to reap the benefits of waiting for the main event. With fresh discounts, deeper markdowns, and plenty of coveted finds now up for grabs on the significant day, there’s no shortage of pieces worth adding to your cart (especially as sales continue through Cyber Monday). With so much to sift through, I always like to start with recommendations from my fellow Vogue editors for inspiration.

Industry observers note that this year, the discounts are even better than we could have imagined, and Vogue shopping editors are finally pulling the trigger on the pieces they’ve been eyeing all season. From luxe outerwear and cozy cold-weather accessories to covetable footwear (think elevated mules, sleek leather boots, and everyday loafers), plus plenty of chic home and beauty finds, these wish lists are filled with all manner of luxuries, both major and small, at some of the best prices of the season.

I myself am grabbing a few fall wardrobe heroes with a twist. From Nour Hammour’s sumptuous suede barn jacket in ruby red—it’ll look so good with my usual black sweater-and-jeans uniform—to Emme Parsons’s leopard-print mules (after writing about the return of the slip-on shoes, I had to have a pair!), these are the kinds of pieces that will earn their keep in my wardrobe. And a little cashmere alert: Banana Republic has some seriously good basics for under $100. Why wait when some of the season’s best finds are finally within reach?

Consider this your inside look at what’s actually worth adding to your cart this Black Friday, as our team continues to scout the standout deals and wish-list-worthy pieces worth shopping through the Cyber Sale weekend.

“I have been waiting for months to pull the trigger on Alex Mill’s cardigan; the brand historically doesn’t do sales so I will finally take the bait. For the quiet luxury in us all, Khaite’s private sale is on now, with a significant discount on this Matrix-like Samuel jacket, which I have my eye on. Jamie Haller’s private sale has the white Trap pumps in its lineup, which I’d love to wear to help wean me into non-black shoes. And to keep my face glowing in the winter months, I want to try Marie Veronique’s new Senolytic Serum,powered by encapsulated fisetin, which is 20% off.” —Dalya Benor, senior shopping writer.

It has been revealed that “My brain is at the moment on some wicked, shopping hamster wheel: tall boots, leather biker jacket, apartment decor (read: rugs, living room furniture, bathroom accessories, and as many candles I can hoard into 500 square feet) body-care. I basically refuse to buy anything else otherwise, so I’m laser-focused on finding the best product for the best price.” —Kiana Murden, beauty shopping editor.

“Now that Black Friday is officially here, I can finally start narrowing down my wishlist. Nour Hammour’s red suede barn jacket is calling my name—especially with the additional 20% off sale that Net-a-Porter is offering for Cyber Weekend. A perfect black cashmere sweater is always a must, and Banana Republic’s comes in under $100. I’m getting one for my sister, too. I can never resist a little leopard, and Emme Parsons’s mules are just the thing. After recently writing about the return of the slip-on shoes, I’m officially sold! I also love a longline statement necklace to add a little something extra to classic T-shirts and simple layers. Chan Luu’s is too cute to pass up.” —Kristina Rutkowski, contributor.

In a recent development, “I discovered heaps of floaty nightgowns at Dôen—they may not be seasonally appropriate, but they are on sale. I wear my wool mockneck from 440me nearly weekly at home. I have it in the camel color but am eyeing the white now that it’s on sale, as well as their black boatneck wool-silk shirt. In the home department, I’m slightly desperate for a upcoming pair of slippers—plus anything jasmine-scented, I’m a fan.” —Elly Leavitt, lifestyle shopping editor.

Sources indicate that “For this year’s sale, I’m looking for accessories, accessories, accessories! Whenever I find myself in a wardrobe slump, the best way to make my essentials feel fresh again is to introduce newness in small doses. From bags to jewelry and shoes, the more the merrier!” —Minty Mellon, shopping market editor.

Recent findings suggest that “This Black Friday, I am looking to buy some items I have had on my wishlist from fashion to beauty. I have had my eye on this pair of Róhe trousers, heeled booties by Jude, and an easy woven bag by Dragon Diffusion to wear all year round. On the self care end, the Dyson Airwrap and Therabody depuffer are two things I have been wanting to adt to my everyday routine. Of course, can’t forget to restock on bedsheets!” —Andrea Zendejas, shopping market editor.

Recent findings suggest that “This Black Friday, I’m checking off items I’ve had on my wishlist all fall: a novelty coat, colorful mules, and animal-print accessories. I’m also picking up some layering basics and stocking up on my favorite nail polish!” —Fred Sahai, associate commerce producer.

“Table lamps, floor lamps, portable lamps… I love lamp and always have room for one more, even if space suggests otherwise. The Lumens sale is where I’ll be spending most of my time, mulling over Gubis and Poulsens and whether or not I need to replace my current set of stools with these from Artek… I think the answer is yes.” —Talia Abbas, shopping director.

“From all the wear and tear throughout the year, I use the end of year sales to restock on the best footwear to carry me through the months ahead. I usually stick to neutral colors to make sure I have options with every outfit. On my way to check out I’m usually tempted by a few spontaneous accessories, too…” —Kasey Busiel, marketing & production manager, shopping.
