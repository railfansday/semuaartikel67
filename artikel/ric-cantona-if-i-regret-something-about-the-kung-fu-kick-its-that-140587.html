Before the release of a new documentary, the unrepentant Manchester United icon reflects on his deep bond with English football and his darker instincts.

Industry observers note that “I’m an angel and a demon,” Éric Cantona claims intently during a gripping new documentary about his life on and off the football pitch. “And an angel or a demon, you love him or you hate him. I’m far from being the person with the greatest integrity. I’m far from being the person who always tells the truth. I’m far from being the person who has never been hypocritical. Maybe I’m a good person, but I can also be a flower of evil. It’s the first time in my life I’ve realised this. I am a flower of evil, and I find it very beautiful.”.

It’s hard to know what to expect from the only written interview pinned to the release of Cantona: Flower of Evil. Joel Cantona has warned that his famous brother will walk out at any mention of the enduring crisis surrounding Manchester United, whether it’s a question about Mauricio Pochettino’s arrival or the Glazer family’s ruinous ownership of the club.
