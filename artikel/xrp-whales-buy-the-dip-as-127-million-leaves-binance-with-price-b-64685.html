XRP Whales Buy the Dip as $127 Million Leaves Binance With Price Below $1

🌐 Source: Zycrypto • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (605 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • XRP whales appear to be taking advantage of the price dip, withdrawing more than $127 million worth of tokens from Binance since November 10.
  • According to CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost, whale activity on Binance has shifted noticeably over the past week.
  • The trend continued on the latest day covered by the analysis, when whales withdrew approximately $21 million worth of XRP from Binance.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

XRP whales appear to be taking advantage of the price dip, withdrawing more than $127 million worth of tokens from Binance since November 10. The outflows suggest that large holders are using the downturn to accumulate rather than sell as XRP trades below $1.

According to CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost, whale activity on Binance has shifted noticeably over the past week. Daily net outflows have ranged from $12 million to $33 million, indicating that large holders have increasingly moved their XRP off the exchange.

The trend continued on the latest day covered by the analysis, when whales withdrew approximately $21 million worth of XRP from Binance. Cumulatively, net withdrawals have exceeded $127 million since November 10.

Industry observers note that this marks a notable shift from earlier whale activity, which was largely dominated by transfers to Binance. Following XRP’s nearly 72% rally over several days in August, large holders increased their exchange deposits, with inflows reaching approximately $75 million as the cryptocurrency climbed back above $1.50.

Recent findings suggest that the trend was even stronger after XRP climbed above $3.55 in July 2025. During that period, whales regularly transferred large amounts of XRP to Binance, with daily net inflows surpassing $100 million several times and reaching nearly $270 million in a single day.

Industry observers note that however, whale withdrawals are not the only activity worth watching. XRP exchange-traded funds (ETFs) recorded $19.1 million in net outflows during the week of November 9–13, contrasting institutional investment products with the movements of large on-chain holders.

Recent findings suggest that darkfost cautioned that some of the withdrawals could be internal Binance transfers rather than genuine whale accumulation. Similar activity occurred between October 29 and November 4, when the exchange replenished its reserves following a security incident, meaning not all recent outflows necessarily reflect buying activity.

Meanwhile, the decline below $1 has attracted attention from technical analysts, with some identifying the current price range as a potential long-term buying opportunity.

In a recent development, analyst Crypto Patel believes XRP is approaching an important accumulation zone between $0.90 and $0.60. He suggested that investors could be positioning themselves for a larger recovery while market sentiment remains weak.

Industry observers note that in a recent post on X, Crypto Patel wrote, “XRP at $1 again. Panic or Opportunity? Everyone wants to see XRP at $10+, but very few are prepared to accumulate when the market looks ugly.”.

The analyst maintained that the $1–$0.70 range represents a macro accumulation zone but warned that a confirmed breakdown below $1 could expose XRP to another 30%–40% correction.

Recent findings suggest that additionally, Crypto Patel maintained a bullish long-term outlook, pointing to $3.67 as a major resistance level before potential targets of $10 and $20. He also stressed the importance of accumulating during market dips rather than chasing price rallies.

Furthermore, analyst Ali Martinez has identified another important level that could determine XRP’s next major move.

According to the pundit, a large ascending triangle appears to have been forming on XRP’s weekly chart since 2017. With the cryptocurrency now more than 43% below its October high of $1.55, the analyst believes the ongoing correction could extend further.

Martinez pointed to the triangle’s rising trendline around $0.78 as a potential bottom, suggesting that buyers could emerge around this level to prevent further losses.

Sources indicate that if $0.78 holds, XRP could rebound toward $3.50. A weekly breakout above that resistance, followed by a successful retest, could confirm the pattern, with Martinez projecting a potential rally toward $18.

At press time, XRP was trading at $0.9, down 7.31% over the past 24 hours.
