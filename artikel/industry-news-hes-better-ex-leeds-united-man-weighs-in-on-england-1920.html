Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford is better than his England rival Jordan Pickford, according to ex-Whites winger Jermaine Pennant.

Trafford made a £40m move from Manchester City to Leeds in the summer transfer window and has enjoyed a stellar start to life in West Yorkshire.

He has made no secret of his desire to become England’s number one and across the last two international breaks, minutes have been shared evenly between Trafford and Everton’s Pickford.

Trafford delivered a strong showing in England’s 3-2 Nations League win over Spain last night (November 15) and Pennant believes he is now the better option for the Three Lions.

He told talkSPORT: “He’s better than Pickford. Pickford with England, when he puts on an England shirt, yes, he does great. But you always think there's a mistake in him. Whether it’s his kicking, whether it’s the ball at his feet or shot-stopping, he is good, but I just think from today's performance, Trafford has made a massive statement.

“And if you're Jordan Pickford, you're thinking, ‘oh, am I the number one now moving forward? Because that was a solid performance’.

“It will really, really be interesting how Tuchel goes moving forward and if he still relies on Jordan Pickford as his number one.

“But for me, I just think moving forward now with the young guns, the new era, the new style of play, that I think you may have to start looking at Trafford to be the number one.”.

Trafford made a particularly impressive double save in the dying embers in Madrid, enabling England to hang on and secure victory.

He will now head back to Leeds as Daniel Farke prepares his players to visit Chelsea on Saturday (November 21).
