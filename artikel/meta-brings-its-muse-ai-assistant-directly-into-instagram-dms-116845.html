Meta brings its Muse AI assistant directly into Instagram DMs

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Instagram customers can now chat with Meta’s AI agent Muse in a DM thread, the manufacturer declared on Tuesday. The social media network says users can chat with Muse to get more context about a post, place, or product they come across, or catch up on conversations they may have missed in their other DMs.

The new integration gets rid of the need for customers to toggle between Instagram and the Muse app when they want to interact with the AI agent. It also reflects the growing competition among tech companies to make AI assistants a part of users’ everyday digital lives. By embedding Muse directly into Instagram, Meta is making it easier for users to access the AI agent, while also giving Muse access to a platform where users already discover products, places, and content.

Consumers can share Instagram posts with Muse and ask it to take action, such as adding a coffee shop to their Mexico City itinerary or finding a Black Friday deal on a pair of shoes.

Meta notes that conversations with Muse on Instagram will appear as a side chat in the main Muse app.

Muse in Instagram DMs will begin rolling out on Tuesday for iOS and Android customers in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, Japan, and Singapore.

It has been revealed that meta also revealed that consumers can now do more with the posts and reels they’ve bookmarked on Instagram. For example, they can ask Muse to organize their saved content into custom folders, known as Collections. They could also ask Muse to turn a recipe video into a grocery list, turn the travel content from their favorite travel creators into an itinerary for an upcoming trip, or have Muse send them outfit ideas based on fashion reels they’ve collected.

Starting Tuesday, when users save a post on Instagram, they will see a prompt for Muse to organize it for them. This feature is launching in the U.S. and Canada.

In a recent development, meta claims that users remain in control of their Muse experience on Instagram. Nothing is shared, published, or sent without their approval, and they can ask Muse to forget information or undo actions at any time.
