📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The British cult classic spy series The Prisoner will soon be remade for the second time.

The Prisoner debuted in 1967 with Patrick McGoohan playing an intelligence agent known as Number Six. He gets kidnapped and taken to an unusual location called The Village, where no one is called by their real names. Always monitored and under threat, the agent tries to figure out a way to escape and refuses to accept his fresh normal.

Recent findings suggest that it's been almost 60 years since The Prisoner ended, but now a remake is on the horizon. THR reports that Amazon Studios is presently developing a latest and modern twist on the short-lived series. Susan Montford, Don Murphy, Philippe Maigret and Lucienne Papon have signed on as executive producers, but a showrunner has not been selected yet.

Sources indicate that maigret and Paon work at ITV, while Montford and Murphy have their own production tech giant called Angry Films. Murphy has produced every single live-action Transformers movie, as well as the animated flick Transformers One. He also worked with Montford on Real Steel, Shoot 'Em Up, While She Was Out and Vampire Academy.

There have been attempts in the past to create a movie based on The Prisoner, with significant names like Christopher Nolan, Ridley Scott and Simon West all interested at various points, but nothing ever materialized. Now Amazon Studios is taking a stab at a fresh TV show.

ITV only aired 17 episodes of the original Prisoner back in 1967 and 1968, but it did receive a nomination for Best Dramatic Presentation at the Hugo Awards. McGoohan's co-stars included Angelo Muscat, Peter Swanwick, Denis Shaw and Fenella Fielding.

Reviews were overwhelmingly positive. The Prisoner, considered one of the greatest sci-fi fantasy TV shows of all time, currently holds a perfect score among critics on Rotten Tomatoes and a 93% with audiences. That strong reception wasn't enough to convince the network to renew the series, though.

It took 41 years after the end of The Prisoner for any remake to finally make it to air. The six-episode 2009 miniseries had a solid cast that included Ian McKellen, Jim Caviezel, Ruth Wilson, Jamie Campbell Bower, Hayley Atwell and Lennie James. McKellen and cinematographer Florian Hoffmeister even scored Emmy nominations.

Unfortunately, reviews weren't kind to the show, which has a very low 17% critic score and 41% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.

The Prisoner on Rotten Tomatoes.

According to recent reports, now a second remake is on the way from Amazon Studios. As it's still very early in the development process, no actors have been cast, and a production schedule isn't known at this time, though executives at the studio reportedly consider The Prisoner a priority.
