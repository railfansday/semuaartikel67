Markus Krösche will be announced as Milan’s new Head of Football in January, according to Tuttosport, and an vital task awaits him.

After a summer during which Gerry Cardinale personally worked alongside an internal group on the mercato business, he’s expected to get some help soon. Indeed, an agreement has been reached with Eintracht Frankfurt for Krösche, who is expected to be announced at the start of January.

In a recent development, in other words, the new Head of Football will be thrown straight into the fire as Milan are expected to make some reinforcements during the winter mercato. The start of the season has indeed uncovered some depth problems which need to be fixed.

Industry observers note that according to today’s edition of Tuttosport, as transcribed by MilanNews, Milan are looking to make three signings in January: a centre-back, a right-back and a striker. This means that Krösche is in for quite the task, even if there is a budget to support him.

Sources indicate that for the centre-back position, Goncalo Inacio remains the substantial target but the Rossoneri also have some other names in mind. On the right flank, the early return of Zachary Athekame from Lyon still cannot be ruled out, though there are other options here too.

Up front, Nicolo Tresoldi appears to be the favourite but the price tag could make things difficult, especially since we all know how stern Club Brugge are in negotiations. In any case, once the Krösche announcement arrives, we can expect to at least see some movement.
