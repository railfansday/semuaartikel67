The game-tested altcoin restored $1, but then slipped right back under the fundamental Fibonacci pocket.

Sources indicate that XRP ran into its first Fibonacci resistance pocket near $1.00 and worked into the daily 20 EMA at $1.01 before rejecting, according to technical analyst Chart Nerd. The token is now ranging between roughly $0.85 and $1.00, with the Stochastic RSI still overbought.

Recent findings suggest that XRP’s price right now sits near $0.92 after failing to hold the $1.00 area. The analyst noted that both XRP and Bitcoin face nearby resistance (XRP in the $1 to $1.20 zone and Bitcoin between $63K to $65K, and until those levels are reclaimed, further weakness remains plausible.

Sources indicate that the XRP price chart shows the OG altcoin testing the 0.236 Fibonacci level near $1.00 before turning lower. The daily 20 EMA, marked around $1.01 to $1.03, acted as immediate overhead resistance.
