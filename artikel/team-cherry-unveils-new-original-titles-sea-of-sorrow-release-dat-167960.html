Team Cherry has done it once again with its eccentric reveals, as its latest livestream presentation Cherry Time has revealed not only the release date for the upcoming DLC for Hollow Knight: Silksong, but also some brand-upcoming original titles.

Previously known mostly for the original Hollow Knight, the studio is now expanding its catalogue to actually include some more indie titles from different genres, but not without giving some love to the original game that first made them famous.

Sea of Sorrow, a free expansion adding tons of new areas, bosses, challenges, quality-of-life improvements, and some new lore pieces for Silksong, is described as an “expansion like they never did before” and will be dropping on December 11, free for all players.

The Cherry Time livestream has revealed the latest trailer for the Silksong expansion, complete with its release date and a small tease for its bosses. Just like with the base game, the release date was revealed exactly two weeks before its launch.

Sources indicate that first revealed earlier this week, the stream was a welcome surprise for the fans, filled with latest footage from the new areas Hornet will visit and small showcases of what we can expect in this new adventure. And if we can take the original Hollow Knight expansions as a baseline, we’re in for a huge treat here.

It has been revealed that we can see newly introduced scenes of Hornet running across the new areas and the new enemies she’ll be facing in these distant waters. A favorite of mine is the new pirate-like boss, which is one of the main focuses in this trailer. We can also see some platforming sessions and at least one new of the dreaded enemy gauntlets in the trailer, so get ready to put those tools to use.

Industry observers note that and speaking of tools, the biggest highlight obviously goes to the final scene, where Hornet actually unveils a new tool being used for the most ancient practice in any good RPG: fishing. Love it or hate it, the game wouldn’t be complete without it, and you know it. And in classic Hollow Knight fashion, expect some obscure secret to be hidden beneath the fishing waters.

For now, I’m hoping for more challenging areas such as the Cogwork Core and fewer areas that are just a torture session in a trench coat, such as Bilewater.

In a recent development, aside from Sea of Sorrow news, Team Cherry also promised more “exciting announcements”, and they did deliver. Previously known as the studio that wouldn’t speak with its public while working on their game (in a very justified way), this now seems to be in the past as the small South Australian studio came forward not only with a release date for the expansion, but also with brand-latest titles developed by the studio.

Sources indicate that these include Blokan: Secret of the Walking City, a newly introduced game by Hollow Knight artist Rohan Fraser. It follows a detective solving cases in a traditional puzzle game style above a fantasy-inspired city, fully drawn by hand. It is inspired by the traditional golden age adventure games, according to Fraser himself.

Radio Rain is another one, a side-scrolling title made by Lukas Stobie, who previously developed Rising Dusk. This new side-scrolling adventure game follows the protagonist as she finds her way across the ruins of a rainy world filled with music, all in an amazing pixel art style.

It has been revealed that the final reveal is The Botanist and The Translator, developed by Makoto Kuji (the voice of Hornet herself). In this 2d adventure game, you’ll play as the botanist-investigator Hatti and her animal translator partner Oliver, who work together to unveil a mystery while exploring a beautifully hand-drawn Sailor Moon-inspired world.

Recent findings suggest that it’s amazing to see Team Cherry actually growing up as a studio, as they can now reliably deliver more titles and not be bound to the same franchise for years. While the Hollow Knight games might still be their magnum opus, knowing that we’ll see more of its DNA implemented across different titles is an even better gift than I could’ve asked for. Especially now that Sea of Sorrow is coming.
