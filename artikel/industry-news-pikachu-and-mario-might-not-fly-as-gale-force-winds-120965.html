Tomorrow morning, millions of people are planning to tune in to watch the famous annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in Upcoming York City, with its iconic giant balloons. However, balloons like Mario and Pikachu might remain grounded due to forecasts predicting extremely nasty weather.

Meteorologists are forecasting a truly nasty Thanksgiving morning in NYC, with a high-wind warning currently set to kick off at 10 a.m. Eastern Time. Gusts are projected to possibly reach as high as 60 mph and sustained winds could clock in at around 35 mph, reported Hell Gate. Weather experts are also predicting power outages and fallen tree limbs across the city.

Sources indicate that this is the kind of weather that could force parade officials to keep balloons lower to the ground or even prevent them from flying at all due to safety concerns and legal restrictions that were placed on the parade after the infamous 1997 parade that saw multiple balloons, including Sonic the Hedgehog, damaged by winds and multiple injuries reported. During that disaster, wind gusts reached around 43 mph, well below what’s forecasted for tomorrow.

As reported by ABC 7, parade officials won’t make a decision until early Thursday morning, before the start of the parade. One option is to fly balloons much lower to the ground. Another option being floated by officials is to start the parade earlier than 8:30 am to hopefully avoid the worst of the incoming storm.

It has been revealed that “This is a challenging week. This is a challenging forecast,” Newly introduced York City Emergency Management Commissioner Christina Farrell told ABC7.

In a recent development, “You may remember, there was some lightning and storms coming through on July 4th. It’s been that kind of year. The fireworks were moved up a little bit. That is one option. If the weather is coming in a little later, can we start things a little earlier?” noted Farrell.
