Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso may have a selection headache on his hands after Emi Martinez returned to action.

Martinez played the full 90 minutes as Argentina thumped Costa Rica 7-0, producing two saves in a comfortable outing for what was his first outing since sustaining his jaw injury against Everton.

The goalkeeper had been an unused substitute for Argentina during their 2-1 defeat to Uruguay on Friday, watching on as Manchester City’s Geronimo Rulli got the nod.

Contrary to reports, Martinez did not don a protective face mask, with his fractured jaw recovered.

According to recent reports, speaking after the game Martinez stated: "The desire is always there. Several things have happened to me lately, and I had to be well because my team-mates need me.”.

Martinez’s return to action may represent a selection headache for Alonso ahead of Chelsea’s Premier League clash against Leeds this weekend.

Mike Penders has impressed during the Argentine’s absence, recovering from a shaky performance in Chelsea’s shock 2-0 loss to Tottenham, to prove dependable and stake claim to be the first-choice option in between the sticks.

Penders has already faced Leeds once this season, keeping goal as the Blues roared back from 2-0 down to win 6-3 in the Carabao Cup third round back in September.

However, the 21-year-old’s run of games for Chelsea was not enough to earn a starting role for Belgium during the international break.

Manchester United’s Senne Lammens kept his place as number one for Belgium, as Penders watched on as victories over Turkey and Italy secured a spot in the Nations League quarter finals.

In total, Martinez missed four Premier League games, with Chelsea winning one, drawing the other, and losing twice.
