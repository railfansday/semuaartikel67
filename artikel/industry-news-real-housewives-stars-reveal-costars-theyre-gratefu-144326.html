This Thanksgiving, the cast of the Real Housewives franchise are giving thanks for the gift of tight friendships.

Whether fans are watching ladies whoop it up in Orange County or debate popcorn ceilings in New York City, Bravo’s hit reality franchise has proven time and time again to have its fair share of OMG moments, feuds and drama.

But underneath all the playful disagreements and dramatic “To Be Continued” teases are strong bonds worth celebrating.

Sources indicate that “In real life outside the show, I am good friends with many of them, and it makes sense that I would be invited to certain things,” Denise Richards exclusively told Us earlier this month at the launch party of Dorit Kemsley’s new beverage partnership with Maison d’Or. “I love a lot of the women, and some of them not so much, but most of them I do.”.

It has been revealed that in honor of Thanksgiving, Us asked a few Real Housewives to share which Bravolebrities they are thankful for this season. Prepare for no shade, but plenty of love and affection:.

I’m grateful all year round, first and foremost, for my babies Jagger and Phoenix, who are my why. They are absolutely the ones that get me up in the morning, and no matter what’s going on in my life, I put on a smile, and I put one foot in front of the other, and [Maison d’Or] really is for them.

Sources indicate that I will say that the show has introduced me to some incredible women,  and I’m very grateful to have them in my life. The world got a chance to see Boz [Saint John-Watson] and I meet for the very first time, and inside of 20 seconds, there was like a love that had been born. She and I are very close. It is one of the greatest friendships I could ever have hoped for or could ask for. She’s a champion, but she’s not the only one. Erika [Jayne], Kyle [Richards]. I mean, these are friendships that I’ve had for over a decade. I’m very, very grateful.

I have so many. Oh my god, I have so many. I just filmed Ultimate Girls Trip with Lisa Barlow. I have besties in every city. I really do, like Alexia [Nepola] and Larsa [Pippen]. Cynthia Bailey, Porsha [Williams]. I mean, I love them.

In a recent development, the girls that I did Ultimate Girls Trip with: Gizelle [Bryant], Kyle [Richards], Luann [de Lesseps], Vicki [Gunvalson]. We really formed a sisterhood. We’re on a group chat with each other, and we support each other. We’re there for each other. We really do have a special bond.

According to recent reports, I’m especially grateful for Mary Cosby of Real Housewives of Salt Lake City and the courage she’s shown in sharing her grief so openly. Having lost my first daughter and my husband, I know how difficult it is to navigate heartbreak, let alone do it publicly. My heart is with her.

Sources indicate that and this Thanksgiving, I’m especially grateful that even after unimaginable loss, my heart is full again with the joy of marrying Keely and watching my gorgeous, college-bound daughter, Lael, prepare for her next chapter. It’s a reminder that grief and joy can coexist.

It has been revealed that I’m grateful for Tamra Judge. I’m thankful to Tamra because she’s the one who brought me into this crazy world, and we have really benefited from this platform and we’ve also been able to give back in a really terrific way to the LGBTQIA community.

I’m truly grateful to all of them. I really do feel like we’re a sorority, and it’s fun. … You have this common bond. It’s very nice.

It has been revealed that well, I am thankful for all my sisters in Beverly Hills on The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills, and naturally, my sister Kyle [Richards] and the crew. We can’t do it without them.

Sources indicate that for this Thanksgiving, I would like to say I am very grateful to Andy [Cohen] and the whole Bravoverse. It really is a family, and I would also say once a Housewife, always a Housewife, and there’s a whole community out there. When I went to BravoCon, I met so many wonderful people, and I’m just grateful to be a part of this.

I’m so grateful for all the girls in my franchise. There are so many that I love out there. I’m very close with Lisa Barlow. I have had a relationship with Dorinda [Medley] and Luann [de Lesseps], and I love Kenya [Moore] for so many years. I’ve been around for 16 years with these women, seeing them at Bravo Fan Fest and all these things. I really made so many relationships.

In a recent development, I’m very close with Chanel Ayan. I love so many of our Housewives. We fall under, like, this umbrella. We all get each other, but I happen to be in love with Chanel. She’s amazing.

Industry observers note that I’m really grateful, especially from last year, for the support that I got from Lisa [Barlow] when kind of the whole world was against me, so that was really, really huge. She’s been very supportive, and I am very grateful to have had the chance to witness Mary Cosby’s strength this year, and she has been an absolute inspiration to me beyond. There are plenty of others, but we’ll just leave my first two for now.

In a recent development, I’m really thankful for the Housewives of this franchise because it’s been wonderful. This is such a unique situation, unlike anything I’ve ever done before, and to really put our lives out there, and then also have a bond outside of it, it’s really, it’s really amazing. It’s unlike anything I’ve ever done.

Industry observers note that there are so many Housewives that I’m thankful for. I’m just thankful for all the Housewives. I can’t pick one. I mean, I’m here because of Housewives. So, all of them.

I have to say Dolores [Catania] because right before I stepped onto this carpet, I sent her a message, saying, ‘I love you, and I am literally so grateful for you.’ And I noted, ‘If I haven’t said it enough, I’m saying it again.’ And that was minutes ago. And my sister [Xio Cammarota], obviously, right? She’s bloodline given. That was like a given answer.

I’m grateful for Melissa Gorga. She is a true best friend, and she’s always there for me. Every call, she’s never missed a call. She’s never missed an ‘I need you right now’ phone call. She’s there. 5:00 a.m.? She’s there.

I’m grateful for my friend Nat [Rufolo]. We’ve had our ups and downs, but I think that ultimately, everything is a testament of our relationship. We always continue to work at everything. And I think that’s every relationship in the world, whether it’s a romantic one or friendship, it’s always about figuring things out because you’re never going to be on the exact same page and there’s always going to be misunderstandings.
