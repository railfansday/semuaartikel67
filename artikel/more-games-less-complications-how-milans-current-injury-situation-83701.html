In the past few weeks, Ruben Amorim has been battling a long list of absences due to injuries, but how does it compare to one year ago?

The second international break brings a useful moment to pause and reflect on the injury issues within the squad. Given that there have been at times as many as eight players out, it feels as though Amorim has been rowing against the tide a bit.

Sources indicate that the Portuguese asks for a physically demanding style and he consistently emphasises the importance of proper rotation. Yet, at present, the likes of Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers and Strahinja Pavlovic are all out.

According to recent reports, from Stefano Pioli’s final years into Massimiliano Allegri’s one-season stint, there was a recurring problem of muscle issues. For example, last season – despite no European football – the Rossoneri suffered 31 total injuries during the season, leading to a crisis at certain points.

In the previous season, which included the Champions League, the figure hit 33. In Pioli’s last season in 2023-24, he battled a staggering 40 different problems. How does the current season compare to the last though? MilanNews provide the data.
