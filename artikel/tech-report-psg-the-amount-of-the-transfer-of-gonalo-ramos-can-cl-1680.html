Tech Report: PSG: the amount of the transfer of Gonçalo Ramos can climb up to €86 million!

At 25, Gonçalo Ramos has won almost everything with Paris Saint-Germain. But after three years spent in the French capital, the Portuguese striker wanted to leave, tired of his status as a substitute. “I wanted to leave PSG, but in January, it’s always more complicated to leave. It wasn’t the best option, even if I wanted it to be. Now I understand better. I was already at a stage where I just wanted one thing: to leave”he recently told the media Channel 11. Initial desires that PSG fulfilled last summer. Taking advantage of its status as reigning double European champion, the red and blue club was able to negotiate an XXL transfer for its striker. Recruited by AC Milan for €74 million, Ramos became the most expensive recruit in the history of the Rossoneri and the third biggest sale in the history of PSG, behind Neymar (€90 million) and Bradley Barcola (€125 million).

Since then, the Lusitanian international (31 caps, 14 goals) has not really thrilled the San Siro public. With 3 goals and 2 assists in 14 matches in all competitions (only 2 goals in Serie A) on the clock, Gonçalo Ramos has been facing a wave of criticism for several weeks already. A performance that leaves something to be desired… and which does not suit PSG! Like many teams, the Parisian management included numerous bonuses linked to the performance of its former player. The site Football and Finance tells us that if Ramos is a success in Milan and therefore in Serie A, he could increase the amount of his transfer to €86 million!

Recent findings suggest that the media explains that the real value of the basic transfer is €70 million. Several bonuses can be added to this: €2 million in the event of a victory in Serie A, €1.2 million in the event of qualification for the Champions League, €1 million if Milan reaches the quarters of the C1, €1 million for a semi-final of the C1, €1 million for a final and €1 million in the event of a European coronation. Finally, if Ramos finishes top scorer in the Italian championship, PSG will receive €2 million. In total, these bonuses amount to €9.2 million per season. Given the current performances of Ramos and his team, PSG is still far from receiving all these bonuses, but the transalpine media specifies that there are certain conditions.

These bonuses will only be paid if Gonçalo Ramos plays at least 60% of Milan’s official matches in which he was able to participate. On the other hand, a clause provides that Paris will not be able to claim any bonus (except the bonus linked to the title of top scorer in Serie A) if Ramos is physically unavailable during 50% of the official matches of the season. Finally, if the total amount of these bonuses per season is €9.2 million, Football and Finance makes it clear that Paris cannot hope to receive €46 million (€9.2 million/season during Ramos’ five-year contract in Milan) in the best case scenario. Milan will no longer pay bonuses once the €16m limit is reached. The Rossoneri still have time to see it coming….
