📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that if you shopped, streamed, or read the news online on Monday, you may have loaded malware without knowing it. Hackers planted rogue code on 178 websites, including Zoom, CNN, IKEA, HSBC and Salesforce, according to Reuters. They got in through a flaw in Optimizely, which is a tool many major brands use to test changes to their sites.

Security researcher Scott Helme of Report URI, who uncovered the attack, says the “malicious JavaScript” was served to site visitors on Nov. 16. It tried to swap crypto wallet addresses for the attacker’s, skim card numbers typed into checkout forms and push hacking tools onto iPhones. Optimizely says it has fixed the vulnerability and removed the code.
