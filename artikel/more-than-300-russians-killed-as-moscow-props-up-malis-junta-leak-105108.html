Exclusive: Records list 349 deaths in three years in Mali and also shed light on Russia’s military activity elsewhere in Africa.

Recent findings suggest that when the attack on Kidal began before dawn on 25 April this year, the Russian fighters were caught off guard. Within the first hour, checkpoints on the outskirts of the remote desert city nestled among the rocky hills of northern Mali had fallen to the insurgents, who pushed towards the police station where fierce fighting erupted.

“Everything was happening at once. Explosions were going off all around us, people were shouting,” recalled a former member of Russia’s Africa Corps who was stationed in Mali at the time and spoke on condition of anonymity. “Nobody knew where the enemy was coming from.”.
