Mulder and Scully’s ‘The X-Files’ Return Gets Exciting Enhancement

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

One of the most iconic sci-fi television franchises of all time is coming back thanks to Ryan Coogler. The Sinners filmmaker's pilot for The X-Files reboot received the green light from Hulu last month. The reboot will introduce a new cast of characters but, at its core, will aim to preserve the DNA of the original series with an identical premise. Original series stars David Duchovny and Gillian Anderson, who brought the iconic Fox Mulder and Dana Scully to life, have both expressed interest in the reboot, but while their involvement hasn't been officially confirmed, their story, as fans know it, is far from over.

The X-Files has been reopened multiple times before with Mulder and Scully, but each time, series creator Chris Carter tends to leave their conclusion open-ended. This subtle tease for more has somewhat frustrated fans, many of whom have been itching to see their iconic duo get a truly conclusive ending. Carter now reveals that The X-Files Season 11 intentionally left its characters' fate ambiguously open to provide a takeoff point for future stories. Eight years later, Carter has not moved on from the world he created and has now confirmed that his strategy to revisit the original is active and alive.

It has been revealed that during a recent interview promoting his latest project, Queens for a Day, which reunites several The X-Files alums, Carter expressed readiness to return to The X-Files to continue Mulder and Scully's arc. Confirming that he intentionally set up the continuation with the previous The X-Files reboot, he mentioned, "I’d love to do it as a series or as a movie. I went to great lengths with four episodes during the last two reboots to set up [a new] story, whether it be a movie or a limited series." While this isn't the first in recent times that Carter has shared intends for a new chapter, a continuing emphasis like this serves well to reassure fans and keep hope alive.

Industry observers note that the X-Files world that Carter created wouldn't be the same without Mulder and Scully, and the creator is firm on keeping it that way with the potential new installment. While the genius creator is yet to share details of what he's mapped out, he has made it clear that it involves Mulder and Scully and that he won't proceed without Duchovny and Anderson returning to reprise their roles.

Carter previously shared that he'd already let both stars in on his prepares for a continuation and that they were on board, but cited their busy schedules as the major hindrance. Carter has also revealed that he wouldn't want his continuation to interfere with Coogler's upcoming reboot out of respect for what he's building. He's hinted at wanting to give Coogler the room to get his series up and running before moving full steam with his continuing chapter. Coogler's reboot is yet to set a release date.

According to recent reports, two F.B.I. Agents, Fox Mulder the believer and Dana Scully the skeptic, investigate the strange and unexplained, while hidden forces work to impede their efforts.
