Amazon’s Black Friday Week starts November 19, but Bluetti’s portable power stations are already marked down on Amazon. These battery-powered backup devices are much quieter than a gas generator, and you can use them indoors without risking suffocation. The same boxes run a campsite or a van just as happily. Several of these deals are pre-orders, so check the ship date before you check out. We pulled every price in this post from Bluetti’s Amazon storefront Tuesday afternoon, November 10.

Runs 1,800-watt loads and refills to 80 percent in 45 minutes, per Bluetti.

In a recent development, you don’t always need to power your whole house during an outage. Sometimes it’s enough to keep crucial devices running (even if that just means keeping Netflix streaming so the kids stay calm). The Bluetti Elite 100 V2 is $498.99 (was $899) and stores 1,024Wh in a LiFePO4 battery. Bluetti rates it for 1,800 watts of continuous output with a 3,600-watt surge. It indicates the battery goes from empty to 80 percent in 45 minutes. Bluetti also lists a 10-millisecond UPS switchover, so the Elite 100 V2 can sit between the wall and your modem and take over when the power cuts out. This listing is a pre-order, and Amazon gives a November 12 release date. The Elite 100 V2 with a solar charging cable is the same $498.99 if you strategy to add panels later.

The least expensive 1,000Wh-class Bluetti in this sale.

The Bluetti Elite 100 mini is $428.99 (was $799) and holds 1,004.8Wh, nearly the same capacity as the Elite 100 V2, for $70 less. The difference is output. Bluetti rates the mini at 700 watts, with a Power Lifting mode it rates up to 1,400 watts for some appliances. That covers phones, laptops, lights and a CPAP machine, but it won’t run as many big appliances at once as the V2. Bluetti says it charges from zero to 80 percent in 1.5 hours. Unlike the V2, this listing isn’t a pre-order. The Elite 100 mini and Charger 1 bundle is $629.98 if you want to top it off from your car, too.

The top pick in our portable power station guide.

It has been revealed that the Bluetti Elite 200 V2 is $898.99 (was $1,399), and it’s the best overall pick in our guide to the best portable power stations. It roughly doubles the capacity of the Elite 100 models to 2,080Wh. Bluetti rates it for 2,600 watts of output across four AC outlets, with Power Lifting up to 3,900 watts. Bluetti says the LiFePO4 battery charges from zero to 80 percent in 50 minutes and is built for a 17-year lifespan. The Elite 200 V2 and Charger 1 bundle is $1,098.99, which works out to $18.98 less than buying the two separately at these prices.

Turns every drive into a fast charging session for your power station.

Industry observers note that A 12-volt car socket is a slow way to refill a significant battery, which is a problem when you’re running a fridge or a laptop at the campsite every night. The Bluetti Charger 1 is $218.98 (was $399) and taps your vehicle’s alternator to charge a power station while you drive. Bluetti rates it at 560 watts and notes that’s six times faster than a cigarette-lighter charger. Bluetti also says it works with 95 percent of solar generators, so it isn’t limited to Bluetti’s own stations. It’s a smart add-on for car campers and anyone who keeps a power station in the truck for emergencies.
