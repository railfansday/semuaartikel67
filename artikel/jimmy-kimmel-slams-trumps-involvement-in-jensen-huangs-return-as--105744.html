Recent findings suggest that it's been quite the turbulent couple of weeks at Nvidia, with CEO Jensen Huang resigning after news of three affairs — two with employees, and one with an alleged Chinese spy — came to light. But now, mere days later, he's back in the chair.

Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO after Donald Trump reportedly "convinced" them to do so, prompting the company's stock — which the president himself owns — to shoot back up.

"If you think that sounds fishy, it's not. It's called being a good friend," says Jimmy Kimmel in the clip above. "There's nothing unusual about a president who owns a huge amount of stock in a manufacturer putting his little carrot thumb on the scale in a way that immediately boosts that company's stock price. I'm sure it also didn't hurt that Huang donated $25 million to the big, stupid ballroom he's building".

Kimmel goes on to quote a Trump Truth Social post, with the president writing: "The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen's return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security.".

Industry observers note that "Claims the man who stashed classified documents in the toilet at his country club," responds Kimmel. "What a profoundly stupid time to be alive.".
