Exoprimal to end service on September 29, 2027

🌐 Source: Gematsu • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (105 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Dinosaur-themed team-based action game Exoprimal will end service on September 29, 2027, after which it will no longer be playable, Capcom announced.
  • Exoprimal launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on July 14, 2023 for $59.99.
  • “We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement,” Capcom said.

Dinosaur-themed team-based action game Exoprimal will end service on September 29, 2027, after which it will no longer be playable, Capcom announced. The game will be delisted from digital stores on March 30, 2027.

Exoprimal launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on July 14, 2023 for $59.99. A Digital Deluxe Edition was also available for $69.99.

“We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement,” Capcom said.
