Sources indicate that black Friday is nearly upon us. From now through Cyber Monday, retailers and companies alike are offering deals and discounts on their wares. While I always keep an eye out for Nintendo deals offered by stores like Amazon and Best Buy, it seems the tech giant is offering its own discounts this year—and, surprisingly, they're actually pretty solid.

On Thursday, Nintendo revealed its Black Friday discounts. We've already seen Switch 2 sales ahead of the Black Friday season, but now, Nintendo has confirmed an official discount for the console. Starting Nov. 22 through Dec. 5, you can pick up Nintendo's Switch 2 Mario Kart bundle for $499.99, down $50 from $549.99. That includes the console, a download of Mario Kart World, and a 90-day Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership.

If you already have a Switch 2 console and/or Mario Kart World, you're not left out of the Black Friday deals here. From Nov. 22 through Nov. 29, Nintendo is also running discounts on several high-profile Switch and Switch 2 games. During this promotion, each of the following is $39.99:.

Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2.

Nintendo rarely offers official discounts on some of its biggest titles, especially in this current era. Games like Donkey Kong Bananza retail for upwards of $69.99, so you're saving as much as $30 on these games during this week-long promotion. In addition, Nintendo declared a sale on its digital store that runs from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2, though it didn't say which titles would be on sale. Just keep an eye on all these dates: The Switch 2 console promotion runs longer than the physical and digital games discounts, while the digital sale runs longer than the physical sale.

It has been revealed that nintendo says that these discounts will be available "at select retailers," as well as its brick-and-mortar locations in New York and San Francisco. You can also buy the Switch 2 bundle through Nintendo's website.
