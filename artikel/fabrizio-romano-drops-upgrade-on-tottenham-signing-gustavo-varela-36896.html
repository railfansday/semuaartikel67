Tottenham Hotspur have made contact over a deal to sign a 'remarkable' striker in the January transfer window, Fabrizio Romano has confirmed.

Spurs had a very busy summer window as they brought in 10 new players for Roberto De Zerbi, including forwards Savio, Omar Marmoush and Mykhailo Mudryk, while a host of first-team stars left the club or were left out of the squad after not being included in the manager's prepares.

But the north London side have won just three of their opening ten games so far this season in the Premier League, and with centre-forward Dominic Solanke managing just one goal, a new forward is believed to be among the priorities for the winter window to strengthen the squad.

Speaking in a YouTube video alongside journalist Matteo Moretto, it was revealed that Serie A top goalscorer Gustavo Varela could well be on his way out of Monza in the upcoming year after a spectacular season so far, and Tottenham have already made contact over a potential deal to sign the Portuguese striker.

"With Varela, it's not just Juventus in Italy," Moretto said.

"In addition to Napoli, there are teams in Europe, especially in England. They mentioned two clubs that recently enquired about him: Tottenham and Brighton.

"In any case, the club has significant financial resources that can certainly match both Monza and the player's demands, so we'll understand better in the coming weeks, but let's say there are many teams interested.".

Romano then added his own information, claiming that the player's wishes would be key.

"Yes, also because I think it will make a difference from the player's point of view. You mentioned the salary earlier, Varela arrived at Monza on very low wages, obviously speaking about the player's level. Today we're talking about one of the most in-form players in Serie A.

"He arrived on a salary that was clearly a gamble, an unknown, for Monza, then he became a fixture in this league. So we're keeping an eye on that aspect because it will be key who reaches an agreement with Monza, yes, but also who can offer the player, not only a technical but also a completely different financial situation.

"The feeling I have is that unless we see a drop-off in the boy's performance, I believe there's an excellent chance of seeing him go as early as January.".

Tottenham are looking for players who can make an instant impact, and the 21-year-old has shown that he is very good at adapting to a new environment after hitting the ground running with Monza since joining in the summer.

He also earned his first call-up to the senior Portugal team, where manager Jorge Jesus sang his praises.

Recent findings suggest that “He's the top scorer in Italian football. To achieve that, you need remarkable quality.

“Starting from that, I decided to put my faith in him, also because we must not forget he's only 21.”.

Considering his arrival for a nominal fee in the summer, Monza are unlikely to be able to demand a huge fee from Spurs, and the player is clearly going to be interested in making that step up at this stage of his career.

With a relatively young forward line, Varela could come in and provide the exact thing missing from the side right now - goals.

With contact made, it's clear that the interest in genuine, and this is a situation to watch in the coming weeks as we approach the January window.
