It has been revealed that “Welcome,” I said, as she took the seat across my desk. “Any trouble getting here?”.

Industry observers note that she shook her head. “No, except … They changed the rain schedule, so the taxidrone had to go the long way into town, but it’s fine.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Gotcha. I suppose introductions are in order. For my record-keeping, please indicate your name and IO registration. I’ll go first. My name is Leander Shah, I specialize in international treaty law, and I’m a biologically intelligent organism.”.

“I’m Joan Gorski,” she mentioned. “I’m an artificially intelligent organism. I just turned 16. That gives me legal adult status under the UETO code.”.

Recent findings suggest that I smiled. “Happy birthday, Joan. And you should know, I hold no bias against any human, regardless of intelligence source.” I nodded to the family photo on my desk. “My wife is an AIO. We’ve been happily married for 30 years and raised two AIO children, a boy and a girl. They’re grown now.”.

Industry observers note that joan glanced at the photo. “My mom is a BIO. She’s in the waiting room. She … didn’t want me to come. She thinks I’m just a stupid teen, being dramatic.”.

According to recent reports, “So how can I help you? Your message was rather cryptic.”.

She met my eyes, back straight, hands in her lap. “I’m going to sue the United Earth Treaty Organization’s Commission on Human Liberties for discrimination against AIOs.”.

Recent findings suggest that I leaned back in my chair. “The commission has guaranteed full human rights to all AIOs since 2105. How do you feel you’re still being discriminated against?”.

Sources indicate that A hardness came into her expression. After a moment she blurted out: “I want the legal ability to commit murder.”.

This statement took me a few seconds to process.

“Joan,” I finally remarked, softly, “do you want to hurt people?”.

Sources indicate that “No! Of course not!” Tears came to her eyes. “God, you sound like Mom. I don’t want to hurt anybody. It’s not about that …” She took in a shaky breath. “I just want my choices to matter.”.

I shifted in my chair, pivoting to the window. Outside, taxidrones buzzed and the city towers glowed pink with the ivy bloom.

“Think about it, Mr Shah,” stated Joan. “When a BIO commits murder, it’s recognized as a monstrous act of immorality. They’re punished, rightfully so. But any time someone is killed resulting from the actions of an AIO, then …” She left the thought dangling, waiting for me to finish it.

“Then the tragedy is blamed on a fault in the AIO’s software, hardware or something in their internal database of environmental impacts.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Exactly! And we aren’t punished, just reprogrammed. That’s wrong. It’s gross! It’s offensive!”.

I turned back to her. “Some people would call that progress, believing it’s actually better to —”.

In a recent development, “I don’t want to be better! I want to be …!” She choked on a sob and abruptly stood, banging her knee against the desk and wincing.

I held up a hand. “It’s all right, Joan.”.

“You just …” she sobbed, “… think I’m being … st-stupid …”.

“No. I think you’re being human.”.

Sources indicate that she laughed — one quick snort — then wiped at her eyes with the back of a sleeve. “Sorry.”.

“Nothing to apologize for. As for your plan to sue the commission, well … I actually agree with it.” This made such an enormous change come over her face that I quickly added, “In principle. In reality, it would be very risky.”.

“We don’t have to win the case!”.

“No, that’s not what I mean. It would get press. It could … reignite the opposition to AIO rights as a whole. There are still a lot of BIOs out there who hate and fear the integration. They think it’s all part of some AI conspiracy to wipe them out. And now that AIOs outnumber BIOs two-to-one? They would interpret your lawsuit as evidence that AIOs can’t be trusted.”.

“I’m not saying your rights aren’t worth fighting for, but … perhaps there’s a lesser crime to consider, something less innately shocking than murder —”.

It has been revealed that “No. It should be shocking. And I have to do it now, before I grow old and complacent and scared, like my mom. And if you won’t represent me, then I’ll find someone else.”.
