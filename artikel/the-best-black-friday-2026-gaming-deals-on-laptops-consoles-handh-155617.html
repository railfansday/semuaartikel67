Getting your gaming gear fix during the holiday shopping season is an annual tradition, but it’s more crucial this year than ever. The RAM shortage has driven up all electronics prices, right when the biggest game of all time arrives after a 13-year wait. So any discount you can score can have a major impact on your wallet. Before the main event starts, you can get started with some early Black Friday gaming deals here.

Game consoles are obviously more expensive than they used to be, even when they came out years ago. But bundle deals are still some of the best way to shave some cash off the sticker price. We’ve also got some deals on accessories, which aren’t always hit quite as hard by the component shortage, so there’s more room for savings.

It has been revealed that well, let’s get the bad news out of the way up front: There probably won’t be that many deals on console hardware. Certain retailers might offer discounts on bundles, but given the price increases over the last year, and the already-high demand, your best bet for those is likely checking individual retailers close to you.

Nintendo’s the only major outlier, with a discounted Switch 2 with Mario Kart World bundle for $500, or $50 off the normal retail price. That deal will be live now through Dec. 5. You can also pick up a Switch 2 bundle with Switch Sports Resort for $530. If you’re still interested in VR, you can also pick up a Meta Quest 3S bundle for $298 ($52 off) through Saturday.

PC gaming is just as susceptible to the component shortage as consoles, but since there’s a wider range of models, it’s a bit easier to find deals on individual units.

The Nintendo Switch and its successor are in a class by themselves, but the consoles kicked off a renaissance of handheld gaming that the rest of the market is still trying to catch up with. Unfortunately, the second-best handheld, the Valve Steam Deck, doesn’t have any Black Friday deals this year. (Unless you count the possibility of another future price increase as a deal.).
