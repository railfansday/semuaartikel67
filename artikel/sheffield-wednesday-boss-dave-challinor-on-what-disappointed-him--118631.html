Challinor noted: "Disappointed not to win, and that's not saying we deserve to win.

Sources indicate that "We got into some good areas. There were bits that were better than Friday.

"We've got a clean sheet, and at times we look much, much better defensively.

It has been revealed that "We got into some decent areas, but our quality in the final third was nowhere near good enough, resulting in us not creating enough opportunities. We created a lot of good situations but didn't work the goalkeeper enough, which again is a disappointment.

"We gave simple passes away. It became a little bit like basketball at times. We then got some control and played with a back four. We had five v four on the top line. We didn't exploit that enough.

Industry observers note that "Thinking down the side, Jordi Liongola and Louie Barry could cause problems. We didn't give them enough service, and their quality wasn't good enough. In the second half, it was a similar story.".
