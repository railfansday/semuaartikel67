AI is very good with document manipulation. My daughter needed a spreadsheet of relatives' names and addresses for wedding invitations. We only had them in one form: a Word document of pre-formatted Christmas card labels. Some spots were blank because the addresses were removed. I thought "I bet AI can handle this." I went to ChatGPT and noted "convert this document of address labels into a spreadsheet with the columns Name, Address 1, Address 2, City, State, Zip." I uploaded the Word document, and boom: ten seconds later, it gave me an Excel spreadsheet with the data. I then had another thought. We now have this nice spreadsheet of everyone's name and address. It would be nice to maintain just this going forward, and generate address labels when needed. I went back to ChatGPT and said "convert this spreadsheet of names and addresses into a PDF file of formatted address labels using Avery label format #8160," Boom, done: a PDF of labels three across and 15 down.Statistics: Posted by Tom_T — Thu Nov 19, 2026 8:57 am.
