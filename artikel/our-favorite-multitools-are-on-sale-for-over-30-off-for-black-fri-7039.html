As we rapidly approach the holiday season, the year's biggest sales event, Black Friday, is almost here. During this massive shopping weekend, we’ll see discounts across several major categories, including power tools, TVs, tech, and, of course, multitools.

While we are still a couple weeks out from the day itself, many multitools, including ones from some top brands like Leatherman, Gerber Gear, and Victorinox, are on sale right now. You can nab full-size models with powerful pliers, steel blades, and screwdrivers, as well as keychain multitools only a few inches wide. In fact, most models here are on sale for over 30 percent off.

Of the full-size options on sale right now, the Gerber Armbar multitool is one of our favorites. The foldout screwdriver provides some extra reach for tight spaces that most multitools don’t offer. At the same time, the stainless steel saw and file are great for cutting through rope, branches, or even metal.

Industry observers note that the Gerber Dime stands out among the pocket-sized multitool deals we’ve encountered so far. It’s our best overall keychain tool thanks to its comprehensive toolset and how useful it is, sometimes despite its size. In fact, it's one of the few keychain multitools we trust for more than just minor inconveniences.

Sources indicate that if you’re looking for something straightforward that gets the job done without too many bells and whistles, it’s hard to beat the Leatherman Wingman multitool—especially when it’s nearly 30 percent off. Highlights include its spring-action plier-based design and stainless steel construction, while the Leatherman Bond is a similar tool that’s lighter and features a more minimalist design.
