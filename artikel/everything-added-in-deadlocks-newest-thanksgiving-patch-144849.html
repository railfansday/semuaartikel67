While a portion of Deadlock's player base is busy getting ready to celebrate the holiday, Valve has released another massive patch with a unique Thanksgiving-themed twist. Acting as a follow-up to Deadlock's limited Halloween event, the newest update detailed on Deadlock's Steam Page brings countless balance changes, quality-of-life improvements, features, and some themed mechanics to get players into the holiday spirit.

According to recent reports, one of the most notable features of the limited-time event is a series of massive parade floats right outside the window, leading all the way down Blue Lane and visible right after loading into Deadlock's lobby. Aside from being simple decorations, these balloons also introduce a new mechanic by dropping Turkey dinners that can be collected for a new buff.

While the parade doesn't do much to start, after three minutes, refreshing crates will spawn hanging beneath them that drop the Turkeys when destroyed, alongside the chance of dropping additional goodies like buff items or Souls.

After eating a Turkey, you'll immediately gain two stamina and the 30-second Stuffed buff, granting you 5 percent Bullet Resist, 5 percent Spirit Resist, and progressively healing you for a total of 90 health. 20 minutes into a match, the health increase jumps to 90 instantly, and 170 over the 30 seconds.

You don't just want to go around eating every Turkey you can, however, as eating another while you have the Overstuffed buff will disable sprinting and give you 60 percent stronger gravity.
