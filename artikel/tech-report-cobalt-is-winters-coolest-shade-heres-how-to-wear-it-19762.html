In a recent development, every season, there’s a new breakthrough color. Last year, we saw butter yellow and tomato red take over, and this fall we’ve seen burgundy star as the hue of the season. Now, cobalt blue is taking center stage, and we’re here for it.

In a recent development, striking and statement-making, cobalt blue is one of our favorite standout color trends yet. It’s bold, yes, but somehow it can seamlessly transition from a star-of-the-show satin top or sparkling pump to a foundational cardigan or cashmere sweater. The color gained traction on last season’s runways, but it’s since permeated street-style culture around the world. One of our favorite ways to wear cobalt is with an eye-catching accessory, and Dior’s Mini Book Tote is currently topping our holiday wish lists.

But even if you’re not in the market for a latest handbag, there are plenty of ways to wear the shade. Whether you’re headed on a Sunday stroll through your local holiday market or hitting a fancy festive soirée, we’ve found a cobalt blue-inspired outfit that’s bringing a little colorful cheer to your winter ensemble.

Make cobalt the main event with a brazen blue overcoat. Paired with patent heeled boots and sporty styling underneath, this is the perfect off-duty outfit—taking you from a workout to brunch. We’re also loving @designedbychep’s custom embroidered sweaters (which can come in cobalt threading!), and a lighter blue-hued bag for contrast.
