Earlier this year, Activision and Toys for Bob lifted the lid on Spyro: A Realm Beyond for the Switch 2 and other platforms.

Following the announcement trailer in June, it seems the next look at the game might be at The Game Awards 2026, airing next month on 10th December.

Here's the teaser about this from Activision, which also gives fans another sneak peek of the "Painted Plains" area (via Gematsu):.

“[The Game Awards 2026] are around the corner, and Spyro is getting fired up. Before we get there, here’s a look at some of the Spyro: A Realm Beyond assets [of the Painted Plains area] you might have sneaked a peek at in [Xbox’s 25th anniversary] commemorative magazine. Who is ready to see more of the first all-newly introduced Spyro adventure in nearly 20 years? Sheep beware.”.

This fresh game, where Spyro is stranded and must find his way home, is described as the purple dragon's "boldest evolution yet" - giving him the freedom to soar above vibrant landscapes, weave between treetops, and dive off massive landmarks.

Here's some more about this fresh adventure, in case you missed the initial announcement:.

"When Spyro finds himself stranded, his journey to discover a way home is interrupted by the arrival of a vicious invading force known as the Scavs. Spyro must make upcoming allies and rise to protect this realm before it’s changed forever.

"Feel the freedom of flight as you soar above vibrant landscapes, weave between treetops, dive off massive landmarks and wing your way through this latest realm in Spyro’s boldest evolution yet.

There's no exact release date just yet, but Spyro's latest game is scheduled to arrive at some point in Spring 2027.
