Manu Koné is a key figure in Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma side.

The French midfielder returned to the manager’s side in the last match before the international break, when the Giallorossi won 3-0 against Sassuolo.

The 2001-born player could have left the capital this summer, with Chelsea offering a last-minute offer of around €60 million and the loan of Gittens.

A few months later, ahead of the January transfer window, the situation hasn’t changed.

In England, there’s a sense that the Blues are preparing to renew their bid for Manu Koné against Roma. Manchester United are also keen to snatch the French midfielder from Gasperini, whose contract worth around €3.2 million plus bonuses expires in 2029.

Gian Piero Gasperini believes Manu Koné remains unsellable, unless a series of factors come together that ultimately make the Frenchman’s departure less painful than expected.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, if the departure of the 2001-born player leads to the arrival of the renowned left-sided striker, then the Grugliasco-born coach could give his approval.

Provided another midfielder arrives as well. The fact is that both Xabi Alonso at Chelsea and Pochettino at Manchester United have mentioned Koné as a way to strengthen their respective squads. Roma values ​​the Frenchman at between €55 million and €60 million; only at these figures could negotiations begin.
