Hera's Didymos asteroid in eclipse

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (58 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A solar eclipse in deep space was captured by the European Space Agency's Hera mission for planetary defense. In this view of the Didymos binary asteroids, the smaller Dimorphos moonlet is seen casting its shadow across part of its Didymos parent body, to blank out the sun across the shaded stretch of rocky surface.
