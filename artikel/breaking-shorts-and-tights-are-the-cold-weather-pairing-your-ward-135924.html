Have you tried wearing tights with shorts lately? If not, we highly suggest you hop on board. Before you question the concept, allow us to explain why it’s the quickest way to extend your shorts’ lives beyond the occasional summer outing (we’re looking at you, silky lace shorts). Meanwhile, fall styles in suede, cashmere, and leather are ready to dress you up for the cooler months ahead.

Recent findings suggest that the key is treating tights as a styling layer rather than an afterthought. A sheer black pair softens the edge of leather shorts for evening, while opaque wool tights give suede or cashmere styles a cozy, cold-weather feel. Keep the proportions balanced by pairing shorts with a longer coat or an oversized knit, then finish with knee-high boots or a dressy pump to complete the look.
