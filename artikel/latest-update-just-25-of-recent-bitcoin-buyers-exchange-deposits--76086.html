Sources indicate that from Nov. 12 to 15, CryptoQuant recorded $1.1 billion in long liquidations and $174 million in short liquidations. A liquidation forces the trader out when a leveraged position can no longer meet margin requirements.

Closing a bet on rising prices can add sell orders to the futures market even as prices fall.

Sources indicate that open interest, a measure of outstanding futures contracts, fell 19% over five days. Funding rates, which govern payments between long and short traders, stayed close to neutral.

In a recent development, sell orders that traded immediately also outweighed similarly aggressive buy orders at all five futures venues CryptoQuant tracked. Together, those figures are consistent with traders shedding leverage during the drop.
