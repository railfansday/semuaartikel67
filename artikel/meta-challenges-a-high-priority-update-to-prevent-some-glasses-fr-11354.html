The latest Meta Glasses (not Ray-Ban or Oakley-branded) may temporarily overheat if they get too wet, but a software patch is here to help prevent that.

Industry observers note that folks using Meta Glasses in the Adventurer, Fury, Starfire, Nova, or Capri styles should open the Meta AI app on their smartphones and get their glasses updated as soon as possible. Meta sent out an email to all Glasses owners noting that the organization has “an important software enhancement” related to performance when “excessive moisture or sweat” enters the device. Meta specifically claims this matter does not affect Ray-Ban or Oakley-branded Meta glasses.

Meta sent out an email to all Meta Glasses owners with the tagline “Action required,” noting that this could be a potentially serious issue:.

“We’ve implemented a software patch to fix a rare problem that caused a small number of Meta Glasses to get too warm when moisture entered the device. It works similarly to a phone shutting off automatically when it needs to cool down. If people experience this, they can contact Meta Customer Service for a replacement.”.

Recent findings suggest that the upcoming software revision brings your glasses to version 130, which includes a special software hook that shuts them down if they were to get too warm. Meta told me that the temperature for this threshold is quite low, and that the glasses shut off to prevent any potential challenges. The company was very specific about the wording in the statement, carefully using the word “warm” instead of “hot,” so as to avoid the stigma that comes with that term on wearable technology.

Sources indicate that while I haven’t seen any specific reports of Meta’s newest glasses models overheating, there are plenty of Reddit threads citing overheating complications on Ray-Ban Meta glasses over the years. Ironically, the one post I found related to sweat-related overheating was from a year ago on Ray-Ban Meta glasses, which this software update does not address.
