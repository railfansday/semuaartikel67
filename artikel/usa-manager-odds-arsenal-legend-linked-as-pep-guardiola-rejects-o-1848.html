The USMNT are still on the hunt for a Mauricio Pochettino replacement, with their incumbent head coach days away from taking up the role of head coach at Manchester United.

Plenty of names have been linked to the international job, with Pep Guardiola stunningly reported as the top target. The bookies markets are starting to form, providing odds on recognisable figures such as Thierry Henry, Gareth Southgate, and Julian Nagelsmann.

Argentine boss Pochettino will finish his stint off as head coach of the United States men’s national team with the second leg of the Concacaf Nations League quarter-finals, welcoming Haiti Stateside with a 6-0 aggregate lead in the early hours of Wednesday.

Pochettino will then cross the pond to start life as Manchester United manager, ahead of his first game in charge of the Red Devils against arch-rivals Liverpool on Sunday, November 22.

US Soccer have until March to appoint Pochettino’s replacement, with their next head coach’s first games likely to be the semi-finals of the Nations League.

Former Manchester City boss Guardiola was linked with the role, but after reports he is set to reject any approach from US Soccer, the ex-Barcelona manager has drifted to third with the bookies, at odds of 5/1.

Recent findings suggest that despite all the star coaches being mentioned with the vacancy, the current frontrunners are two Americans. Nashville SC head coach BJ Callaghan is the favourite at the time of writing, given odds of 3/1.

His fellow countryman Steve Cherundolo, currently in charge of the USA’s Under-23 squad, is second-favourite, priced at 4/1.

Industry observers note that arguably the most recognisable American name in world football at the moment, Jesse Marsch has rather long odds of 30/1. This is explained by his current employment as manager of neighbours Canada, and a history of negative comments towards the USSF.

According to recent reports, pellegrino Matarazzo is another American coach, currently working in Spain as head coach of Real Sociedad in La Liga. Having impressed enough to be given a new two-year deal ahead of this season, Matarazzo is priced at 6/1, reflecting the difficulty of both matching the required compensation and convincing the head coach to leave a job managing in the Europa League.

A host of out of work managers have also been linked to the vacancy. Former England boss Gareth Southgate and ex-Germany head coach Julian Naglesmann have been priced at 25/1, some distance from Thierry Henry and Roberton Martinez - who both sit on odds of 14/1.

Odds according to Canada Sports Betting. Correct at time of publish.
