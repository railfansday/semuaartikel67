Airbnb Just Did Something Guests Have Been Asking for Years — Here’s What Changed

Airbnb is putting limits on checkout chores, including washing dishes and stripping beds. Here's what hosts can still ask guests to do.

Everyone loves a vacation. But the cleanup and tidying as the trip is finishing up? Not so much.

Industry observers note that it’s not always clear what guests are responsible for cleaning. Dishes, sheets, towels, and taking out the trash are sometimes part of checkout instructions at Airbnb rentals. With checkout typically at 11 a.m., the last morning of a trip can turn into a mad dash through an unreasonably long chore list, even when a cleaning fee has been tacked onto the bill.

That frustration, along with a growing number of travelers returning to hotels for more consistent quality and fees, is behind Airbnb’s newly introduced checkout rules. Learn which tasks guests may still face, and how hosts are responding.

Industry observers note that starting with reservations booked on or after November 20, 2026, Airbnb will require hosts to clearly explain their house rules and checkout requests before guests book. Hosts won’t be allowed to add latest requirements through messages or signs inside the rental after a reservation is confirmed.

“House Rules are now displayed more prominently on your listing, where guests can easily see them before they book,” according to Airbnb’s announcement. Guests can also find these instructions in their Trips tab and reservation reminders.

Under the latest policy, hosts generally can’t require guests to strip beds, start laundry, wash and put away dishes, or take trash to an off-site disposal facility. However, hosts can still request simpler tasks, such as placing dirty dishes in the sink, gathering used towels, or putting trash and recycling in designated bins. Guests may also be asked to turn off lights and return keys before leaving.

Airbnb’s policy distinguishes between house rules and requests. “Guests must follow hosts’ house rules, and are encouraged to comply with hosts’ requests,” according to the tech giant. Some exceptions may apply. For example, rentals in areas with bears may require guests to use animal-proof trash containers, while drought restrictions could affect water use.

It has been revealed that guests are still responsible for leaving the property reasonably tidy. Airbnb’s ground rules for guests prohibit excessive messes and require guests to take responsibility for damage beyond normal wear and tear. Hosts can still charge standard cleaning fees and request reimbursement for unexpected cleaning costs or damage.

Hosts who don’t follow Airbnb’s newly introduced checkout rules could receive warnings or requests to upgrade their listings. Repeated violations may lead to notices on their listings or lower placement in search results. Hosts can also appeal flagged requests.

The policy also prevents hosts from charging their own fines for missed chores or restricting access to amenities already included in a reservation.

However, not every host supports the changes. Some believe washing dishes after cooking is a reasonable responsibility for guests, while others worry the latest rules could increase cleaning costs. A Change.org petition has called on Airbnb to reverse its restrictions on requiring guests to wash dishes.

Guests who encounter unexpected chores or requests that violate the newly introduced policy can contact Airbnb for help.
