📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Thanksgiving is stressful enough without realizing you’ve forgotten something at the grocery store. Between the turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, and countless other sides, there’s a lot to keep track of. Thankfully, Panera is stepping in to make sure you can cross at least one thing off your shopping list.

Industry observers note that the bakery-café chain is giving away free baguettes on Wednesday, November 25, just in time for Thanksgiving dinner. And considering how expensive holiday grocery shopping can get, I’ll happily take a little free bread.

The giveaway is pretty straightforward: Starting at 11 a.m., customers can visit select participating Panera locations and receive a free baguette with any in-café purchase. That means you can stop by for your usual soup, sandwich, or coffee and leave with an extra loaf of bread for your Thanksgiving table.

Sources indicate that of course, there is one key catch: You’ll need to make a purchase to qualify, so the baguette isn’t entirely free with no strings attached. Plus, each participating café will have only 60 free baguettes on hand, limited to one per person, so you’ll need to act fast if you want to get your hands on one. You can check whether your local Panera is participating on Panera's website or app, or by contacting your local café.

The giveaway is part of Panera’s effort to bring its bread-making tradition to Thanksgiving celebrations nationwide. And while the holiday might be better known for dinner rolls and biscuits, I certainly wouldn’t complain about adding a baguette to the spread.

Industry observers note that having an extra loaf of bread around on Thanksgiving is never a bad idea. Between soaking up leftover gravy, serving alongside dinner, and making sandwiches with whatever turkey survives the night, there are plenty of ways to put it to good use.

And with the giveaway happening the day before Thanksgiving, the timing couldn’t be more convenient for anyone finishing up their last-minute holiday preparations.
