EXCLUSIVE: The Unofficial Bridgerton Musical creators Abigail Barlow and Emily Bear have another shortform project on the way.

Industry observers note that the pair have penned the score for To Die For Love, an original musical micro-series co-produced by Ampersand and TikTok. Barlow wrote the script with Jane Austen’s Period Drama‘s Julia Aks. The series will premiere on December 21 on TikTok and PineDrama.

“Making To Die For Love has been one of the most rewarding creative experiences of our careers,” Barlow and Bear remarked in a statement to Deadline. “We love a pressure cooker, and building an entire musical in under five months proved that meaningful storytelling isn’t about time—it’s about the story and the people bringing it to life. We’re endlessly grateful to our extraordinary partners at Ampersand as well as Knockout Shorts for turning this into something beyond what we imagined.” Related Stories News TikTok Creator Videos Coming To Disney+ In December As Hulu Integration Takes Full Effect Legal Google, TikTok And Meta Sue To Block California's Social Media Law That Restricts Addictive Features For Children.

In a recent development, to Die for Love follows an ambitious half-witch vying for her coven’s crown. When she falls for a mortal, their romance takes an unexpected turn, forcing an impossible choice between family and the love of her life. Watch on Deadline.

The series stars Barlow (SIX) and Derek Klena (Savannah Bananas, Moulin Rouge) as star-crossed lovers, and features Anna Maria Perez De Tagle (Hannah Montana, Camp Rock), TikTok star Jess Val, Amy Manford (Phantom of the Opera), Kelsie Watts (SIX, Moulin Rouge), Jalen Thurman (Swolemates), Ceci Balagot (Girl Meets World, Monster High), Davied Morales (Sorry We’re Dead), Cayman Cardiff (Fated to My Homeless Billionaire Alpha, The Billionaire’s Fake Wife) and Heather Dubrow (RHOC).

To Die for Love also marks the return of Barlow and Bear after the duo’s creative journey introduced on TikTok with an original song inspired by Shonda Rhimes’ Bridgerton. The song eventually sparked The Unofficial Bridgerton Musical, which was written, recorded, produced, and engineered independently in just six weeks with lead vocals by Barlow and orchestration, production and additional vocals by Bear. It earned a 2022 Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album, and they were the youngest winners ever in the category.

The pair followed up that success with songs for the animated feature Moana 2. They are the youngest composers in history to write all the songs for an animated Disney film, and the first female writing team to do so, collaborating with Mark Mancina and Opetaia Foa’i to craft the sounds of Motonui and beyond.

It has been revealed that “TikTok has a passionate community of musical fans, and we’ve seen how talent like Barlow & Bear can build audiences and fandom on the platform,” Dawn Yang, Global Head of Entertainment & Micro-series at TikTok, remarked in a statement. “Bringing them back with To Die For Love is a natural fit. It speaks to the evolution of musical theater fandom, where stories and communities can originate on TikTok just as they do on Broadway stages.”.

Added Agnes Chu, CEO of Ampersand: “Barlow & Bear are one of our favorite songwriting duos, and To Die For Love showcases what happens when we partner world-class creativity with TikTok. Together with a 125-person union cast and crew in Los Angeles, we are bringing original musical storytelling from imagination to vertical screen at unprecedented speed.”.

All 81 episodes of To Die For Love will be exclusively available on TikTok and PineDrama on December 21. The original soundtrack will be released by Atlantic Records.

Recent findings suggest that executive producers of To Die For Love are Agnes Chu, Benj Pasek, Justin Paul, Danielle Perelman, Chris Crema, Matthew Ko and Kevin Weaver. The production is directed by Paul Becker (Julie and the Phantoms, LEGO’s Heartlake The Musical). Filming for To Die For Love took place in Los Angeles. Knockout Shorts is the physical production partner.

For Atlantic Records, Noah Hubbell serves as executive music producer and Cynthia Meng as music director, with all tracks produced by Emily Bear, Abigail Barlow, Noah Hubbell, and Grant Steller, and mixing and mastering by Jay Marcovitz.
