Rayman Legends Retold, Ubisoft's upcoming remake of the ludicrously good Rayman Legends, has revealed another musical level, and it's wild.

It has been revealed that we've had tunes in the past like Black Betty by Ram Jam and Eye of the Tiger by Survivor, but this new level goes for something a little bit more modern... A lot more modern, actually.

It's Dynamite by South Korean boyband BTS. Yeah, really!

Released in 2020 and later included in the album Be, it's the first song from the group to be recorded entirely in English. It went on to achieve enormous success, storming to the top of the Billboard Hot 100 chart and staying there for three weeks. Chances are you've probably heard it at some point, even if you haven't necessarily associated it with BTS.

Anyway, the level itself looks cool, if a little tame in terms of platforming. Hopefully it builds to a huge crescendo with come challenging moments, but we're definitely keen on the overall vibe and music. It's cool.

Raymen Legends Retold is scheduled to launch on 3rd December 2026 and will come bundled with Rayman Origins: Enhanced Edition.
