The wife of San Francisco 49ers offensive lineman Colton McKivitz reacted after her husband’s ankle was seen bending in a horrifying direction in a game against the Minnesota Vikings.

“My whole entire ❤️,” Abby McKivitz posted via her Instagram Story on Monday, November 23, sharing the 49ers’ message of support for Colton.

In a recent development, colton suffered the injury on Sunday, November 22, when his leg was rolled up during the second quarter of the game in Mexico City. He was placed in an air cast before being taken off the field via a cart.

After the game, 49ers head coach, Kyle Shanahan, said the team believed Colton’s ankle was “broken and dislocated.” San Francisco has yet to release an official statement about Colton’s injury, but it is widely presumed he will miss the rest of the season.

“That was really crushing,” Shanahan, 46, said on Sunday. “Colton has had an unbelievable year. … It was tough to see Colton go down like that.”.

Colton and Abby got married in June 2025 at The Clubhouse at Talons in Ankeny, Iowa, Abby’s home state.

“Forever & a day 🤍,” Abby posted via Instagram at the time.

The couple has been together since at least July 2020, when the football star — who played at the University of West Virginia before being drafted by the 49ers in the 2020 NFL Draft — first popped up on Abby’s social media.

Abby commemorated her husband’s 30th birthday with a celebratory social media post in August.

Recent findings suggest that “Dirty 30!!! Happy Birthday to my guy ❤️ @cmckivitz53 You’re hot & funny,” she wrote via Instagram. “Cant wait to spend the next decade (and life) with you 🎂🎉.”.

As for Colton’s injury, he had the full support of his 49ers teammates in the aftermath.

In a recent development, “He’s somebody that is one of the heart and souls of this team, a guy who indicates up every day with the right attitude,” running back Christian McCaffrey told reporters after the game on Sunday. “He’s the standard, and we’re going to really miss him. We all love him. We’re praying for him. It’s one of the worst parts about this game is stuff like that, but we love him and we’re going to play for him from here on out.”.
