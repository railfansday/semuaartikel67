Addendum to: Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/nature06543 Published online 14 February 2008.

The Editors would like to alert the readers that concerns have been raised regarding the data presented in the figures, specifically:.

It has been revealed that fig. 3c and Supplementary Fig. 7a appear to share some highly similar bands;.

Industry observers note that fig. 5e tEIN5 lanes 2 and 3 appear highly similar to Fig. 5i MKK9a lanes 4 and 2, respectively;.

According to recent reports, fig. 5h WT Cont lanes 1 and 2 appear highly similar to mkk9 Cont lanes 2 and 3, respectively.

Supplementary Fig. 3: LB2/RP gel appears to show some splice marks and background inconsistencies;.

In a recent development, supplementary Fig. 8a: the darker areas under the bands in lanes 2 and 3 appear highly similar;.

Industry observers note that supplementary Fig. 11: ELF4a lanes 4 and 6 appear highly similar.
