Jonathan Rinderknecht has been found guilty of starting the devastating 2025 Palisades Fire, which killed 12 people and destroyed entire neighborhoods across Los Angeles and Malibu.

According to multiple reports, jurors convicted former Uber driver Rinderknecht, 30, of igniting a fire on January 1, 2025, that smoldered undetected beneath root systems before reigniting days later on January 7.

According to recent reports, the blaze, which became known as the Palisades Fire, tore through the region, leaving widespread devastation and claiming 12 lives. The inferno was one of the most destructive wildfires in California history.

Rinderknecht pleaded not guilty to federal charges of destruction of property by means of fire and arson.

The first charge was narrowed to focus on the January 1 blaze, known as the Lachman Fire, while the second related to the Palisades Fire itself. He was ultimately convicted on both counts.

Recent findings suggest that the Palisades fire verdict comes after prosecutors pursued a retrial following a deadlocked jury in Rinderknecht’s first trial in June. That jury reportedly voted 10-2 in favor of acquittal after two weeks of proceedings, prompting prosecutors to continue building their case.

A third charge of timber set afire was later dropped when a new indictment was filed ahead of the retrial.

Industry observers note that during the second trial, prosecutors presented a more streamlined case, relying on digital forensic evidence, expert testimony and details about Rinderknecht’s state of mind in the hours before the fire. As they sought to establish Rinderknecht as the Palisades Fire starter, they emphasized the evidence surrounding the fires and his actions and put less focus on his alleged resentment toward wealthy and powerful people than they previously did.

In October 2025, authorities publicly unveiled they had arrested a Palisades Fire suspect.

“Today we are announcing the arrest of 29-year-old Jonathan Rinderknecht on a criminal complaint charging him with maliciously starting what became the Palisades Fire in January,” Bill Essayli, acting U.S. Attorney for the Central District of California, wrote via X on October 8, 2025.

“The complaint alleges that Rinderknecht started a fire in Pacific Palisades on Latest Year’s Day — a blaze that eventually turned into one of the most destructive fires in Los Angeles history, causing death and widespread destruction.”.

Industry observers note that the Palisades Fire burned more than 23,000 acres of land in communities in and around Los Angeles, including Pacific Palisades, Malibu and the Santa Monica mountains. It was fully contained on January 31.
