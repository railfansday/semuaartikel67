A fresh reason for magic numbers: Scientists discover the secret behind an unusually stable molecular cluster

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (90 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that why are some groups of molecules remarkably stable while others readily break apart? Researchers at Bar-Ilan University have discovered a surprising explanation for why clusters containing exactly six molecules of glycine, the simplest amino acid, are exceptionally stable—making six a "magic number." Moreover, they have uncovered the reason for this increased stability. The secret lies in a transformation that takes place when the molecules come together: they change their structure and become zwitterions, allowing them to form stronger bonds with one another.
