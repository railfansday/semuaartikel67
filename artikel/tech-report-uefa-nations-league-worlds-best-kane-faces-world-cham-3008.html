Thomas Tuchel's England face World Cup winners Spain at Metropolitano Stadium. The visitors need three points if they have to finish ahead of the team who have now gone 43 games without a win in Group A3.

FC Bayern's Harry Kane scored in the reverse fixture, but his penalty-kick miss was costly in a 3-2 defeat at Wembley. Jarrell Quansah will hope to perform better than Thursday's game against Croatia as he's now partnered with Arsenal’s Ezri Konsa in the center-back position.

The world champions have Lamine Yamal back in the team after the Barcelona superstar missed the win over Czechia with a minor injury.
