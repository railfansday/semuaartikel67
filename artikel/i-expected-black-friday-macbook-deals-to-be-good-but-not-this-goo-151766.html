Black Friday MacBook deals are live. And as someone who keeps an eye on MacBook prices year-round, I'm sure I speak for most shoppers when I say: finally. Between Apple's summer price hikes on the MacBook Air and MacBook Neo, and its expensive upcoming "Pro" releases this fall, these holiday savings are a sight for sore eyes — and bank accounts.

Sources indicate that so far, I'm happy to report that most of the MacBook deals I'm seeing are even better than I expected. Black Friday discounts on the MacBook Air peaked at $250 in 2025, but this year Apple's ultraportable is on sale for $300 to $400 off, depending on the configuration. Of special note, the 13-inch base model has dipped under $1,000 for only the second time ever since June.

Then there's the budget MacBook Neo, which is $100 off right now in every color — its biggest discount yet.

The newly introduced M6 MacBook Pro and MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display are both on sale for the first time ahead of the holidays, to the tune of $250 to $500 off. (That's right in line with last year's numbers.) If you've been holding off on upgrading to either model, now's the time to splurge. It'll feel a little less splurge-y than usual.

It has been revealed that below, I've rounded up all of the best Black Friday MacBook deals that I've spotted across the web. To make sure I'm only recommending the lowest prices available, I've cross-checked every featured model across several different retailers, including Amazon, Best Buy, B&H Photo, and Costco.

The Mashable Choice Award-winning MacBook Neo is our favorite budget laptop on the market. "For students, young people, and casual people, there's nothing else like it," said Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth, who rated it a 4.7/5.
