Prince Harry and Meghan Markle's kids have been rather chuffed about their royal return to the United Kingdom.

More than two months after Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5, began the school year, their dad shared insight into how they've adjusted to classes across the pond.

"They love being back," Harry explained on the Nov. 20 episode of This Morning, though with him and Meghan having lived in the United States since 2020, he clarified, "I say back—they never really knew.".

Sources indicate that archie was born in London in 2019, leaving just a year later when his parents moved to the U.S.—where they were living when Lilibet arrived in 2021. For the kids, school has been a major way of acclimating to life in the U.K. And though that hasn't been without some hiccups, as they had to switch schools two days after classes began because of security concerns, they've reigned supreme through the ups and downs of life in a newly introduced country.

"It's a massive culture shift," the Duke of Sussex explained. "The kids in school uniforms is a big change for them, but it's certainly helped. The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since being at a U.K. school, which is really, really nice.".

While they spent the first few years of their lives under the California sun, they're sounding more and more English every day. He quipped, "I'm squeezing the accents out of them. Their friends at school are doing the same.".

It has been revealed that life in the U.K. may feel foreign for Archie and Lilibet, but for Harry, returning after leaving with Meghan following their decision to step away from the royal family, was like coming home.

Sources indicate that "It's great being back on home soil," the 42-year-old remarked. "I'm throwing myself into the fresh winter weather, which is also nice. I missed that a bit.".

According to recent reports, "It's always interesting when you're living overseas and looking back in, you can see all sorts of stuff happening," he continued. "Now that I'm back, everything feels quite familiar and similar.".

It's the most magical time of the year for Archie and Lilibet, since the holidays are right around the corner. In fact, they enjoyed their first public family outing since moving to England at the Winter Wonderland holiday event earlier this month.

According to recent reports, "I was back in my youth when I used to go there and go on all the rides," he reflected. "It's different when you've got kids. Santa Land and running around.".
