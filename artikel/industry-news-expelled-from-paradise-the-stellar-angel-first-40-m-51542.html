Publisher Toei Animation and developer Studio51 have released the first 40 minutes of gameplay from visual novel Expelled from Paradise: The Stellar Angel.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Expelled from Paradise: The Stellar Angel is the first game in the series: a brand-new science-fiction visual novel depicting what happened after the 2014 anime film Expelled from Paradise.

According to recent reports, civilization has been destroyed by the Nano Hazard, a runaway nanomachine disaster, and most of humanity has given up their physical bodies to live on as data in the virtual world of DEVA. Angela Balzac turned her back on that world and chose to live on the surface with her partner, Dingo. Theirs is a story shaped by the conversations they have shared and the choices they have made—and it is your choices that will determine where it leads.

The Story Stirs Once More, Down on the Surface.

In a recent development, A.D. 2400. Ever since the Nano Hazard, a catastrophic event that laid waste to the surface world, 98% of humanity has abandoned both the ravaged Earth and their own flesh and blood to migrate to the virtual world of DEVA. Now, a strange newly introduced lifeform has suddenly begun sprouting on the surface: the Paraflora. As it spreads at alarming speed, colossal worms have started to appear nearby, attacking people one after another. At the heart of the outbreak lies Svarga, a desert town that was long ago abandoned due to a plague. Meanwhile, an agent of DEVA’s Security Bureau has stolen Grey Goo, a top-secret file holding data on the Nano Hazard, and fled to the surface. Their trail, too, leads to Svarga. And now, in this ghost town shrouded in mystery and conspiracy, several competing agendas are about to converge.

With the K2-System, Angela’s Heart is in Your Hands!

It has been revealed that in the film, Angela felt the wind on the surface, met Dingo, and changed little by little. This game takes her growth even further, telling a story of Angela that no one has ever seen before.

The K2-system, the choice system at the heart of the game, allows you to follow her emotional journey through the choices you make.

In a recent development, the story has more than one ending. Some paths lead to endings from which there is no turning back.

Recent findings suggest that expelled from Paradise: The Stellar Angel is due out for Switch and PC via Steam on November 26. It will support English and Japanese language options.
