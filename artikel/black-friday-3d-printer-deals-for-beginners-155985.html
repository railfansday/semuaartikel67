Sources indicate that if you're buying a resin 3D printer, you'll need one of these - a wash and cure machine. That's because models aren't ready once they're made in the printer; they've got to be rinsed off with either IPA or water (depending on the resin you've used) and then cured using UV lamps. It's not a particularly drawn-out process, but it is crucial. If you're getting Anycubic's machine, I'd go for its accompanying Wash and Cure system for ease.UK: £113 £79 at Anycubic.
