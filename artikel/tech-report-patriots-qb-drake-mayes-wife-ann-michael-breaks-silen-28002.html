New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye will “always” have a fan in wife Ann Michael Maye, even if he’s sidelined from an injury.

Ann Michael, 23, has broken her silence after Drake, 24, suffered an ankle injury during the team’s Sunday, November 15, game against the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany.

“ILY always,” she wrote via her Instagram Story on Tuesday, November 17, alongside a red heart emoji.

Ann Michael, who has been married to the NFL player since June 2025, also shared a photo of the couple kissing before Drake took the field in Europe.

It has been revealed that ann Michael wore a camel coat, over a navy top as she leaned in and smooched her husband. She wore a red clip in her hair as well, giving a subtle nod to the Patriots’ red, white and blue color palate.

Drake was dressed in the team’s white uniforms, holding his silver helmet in his hand as he paused for the pregame good luck kiss.

Ann Michael’s sweet message comes two days after Drake was sidelined in Sunday’s game during the first quarter after Lions’ defensive end Aidan Hutchinson sacked him.

Hutchinson, 26, was also hurt after celebrating the play, exiting the game shortly after Drake with a knee injury. (NFL Network’s Ian Rapoport reported that Hutchinson’s initial tests revealed a torn ACL in his right knee, but the Lions haven’t released an official report.).

According to recent reports, ESPN reported on Monday, November 16, that Drake will miss “an extended period, and possibly the remainder of the season” with a suspected fractured ankle. The team hasn’t confirmed his exact injury.

Industry observers note that patriots head coach Mike Vrabel explained on Monday’s WEEI’s The Greg Hill Show that Drake “stayed back” in Germany “out of caution” before making aims to return to the U.S. for further evaluation.

“He’ll be traveling back here shortly, but [we] just wanted to make sure that everything was good for him to travel back and safely,” Vrabel, 51, said.

Drake, meanwhile, has yet to address his injury, but his wife has already indicated that she’ll be by his side through thick or thin.

The couple, who are childhood sweethearts, have already handled a whirlwind first year of marriage — and they aren’t showing any signs of stopping. After tying the knot in summer 2025, Drake led the Patriots to his first Super Bowl appearance in February 2026.

While the team lost to the Seattle Seahawks, Drake and Ann Michael have bonded over their fresh life together in Boston, far from what they both grew up knowing.

In a recent development, “Moving to somewhere so completely different, being away from all of our friends and family is probably the most challenging part,” Ann Michael exclusively told Us in August. “Luckily, we have great teammates and great friends here that have really made the New England area feel like home, which is great.”.

It has been revealed that she noted, “But you know, we’re both from North Carolina. Our parents live in the same neighborhood, and then we went to college in North Carolina, so we were never too far from family. That’s been the biggest adjustment.”.
