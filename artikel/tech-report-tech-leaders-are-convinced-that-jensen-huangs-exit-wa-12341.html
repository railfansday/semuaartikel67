Recent findings suggest that jensen Huang’s stunning resignation from Nvidia after more than 33 years has sent shockwaves throughout the tech industry. Now prominent figures are calling for Huang’s reinstatement, and many are also hopping on board with a theory spreading throughout Silicon Valley: that the scandal is the result of a plot against the former CEO.

At the center of this theory is the reporting from The New York Times that former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, one of multiple women Huang is accused of having an affair with, is a Chinese spy. US agencies haven’t confirmed whether this is the case, and President Donald Trump claims he was unaware of any spy allegations. Jensen Huang’s alleged relationship with Alice Huang set off the events that led to his resignation; subsequent reporting suggested he had an affair with two employees at Nvidia, including one of his direct reports. The revelation raised new questions around whether Nvidia customers, including Megaspeed, were funneling chips to China in circumvention of export restrictions. Last year, Nvidia intervened in a chip-smuggling probe surrounding the Singapore-based firm, according to Bloomberg.

David Sacks, a venture capitalist and former White House AI crypto advisor, has made it clear that he’s skeptical about The Fresh York Times’ reporting. In a post on X, Sacks points to an article from CNN, which claims US officials still can’t confirm that Alice Huang was a spy. “The spy narrative is collapsing already,” Sacks writes. He continued to question the spy allegations during Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World event on Monday, saying: “The reason why Jensen lost his job is because of this lurid Chinese spy allegation. So if no one’s willing to back that up now, then again, I question why this entire chain of events happened.”.

Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney similarly expressed concern about how Huang’s resignation played out. In a reply to Sacks’ post about the CNN article, Sweeney said, “What?! So journalists with sketchy motives portrayed rumors from god-knows-who (lobbyists?) as facts sourced from intelligence officials and gaslit the NVIDIA board to push out the founder, CEO, and once-in-generation visionary?” Amjad Masad, the CEO of the AI app and website builder Replit, called the timing of Huang’s resignation “suspicious,” adding that “if Jensen, the premier champion of open models, is taken out then anyone who wants AI closed, centralized, and controlled might benefit.”.

Sources indicate that semiAnalysis, a research and consulting firm for the semiconductor and AI industry, posted a “conspiracy theory take on the situation,” speculating that Huang’s removal is a way to “remove opposition to an extremely hawkish China export control bill,” referring to the Remote Access Security Act (RASA). The bill, which Huang opposed, would extend AI export controls to cloud servers that allow companies in restricted countries to rent access to Nvidia’s AI chips. The Department of Commerce released an interim final rule on Monday in an attempt to address this loophole.

More than 185,000 people, including Sweeney, Masad, Elon Musk, Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick, Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan, and Shopify CEO Tobias Lütke, have signed a “Stand with Jensen” letter, calling for his reinstatement as CEO. The letter similarly calls for a closer look into the circumstances surrounding Huang’s resignation. “We have not been shown evidence substantiating the allegation that the woman at the center of this controversy was a spy,” the letter notes. “Suspicion or association alone is not a sufficient basis for ending Jensen’s leadership. The inquiry must establish the facts, not merely justify an outcome already imposed.”.
