William Shatner is speaking out after Halloween director John Carpenter recently called him “piece of s***” for allegedly ignoring him when he tried to introduce himself to the Star Trek legend at an event in Orlando, Fla.

Industry observers note that “This guy, he’s sitting in the greenroom, on his phone like this [scrolling]. And I just thought, ‘Well, I’m going to go in and introduce myself,'”, Carpenter remarked during a Nov. 18 appearance on the Dropping Names podcast. “I said, ‘Hello, Mr. Shatner, I’m John Carpenter,’” the director continued. Carpenter then mimicked Shatner staring at his phone, and Shatner saying only “‘Pleasure.”‘ Carpenter told podcast hosts Brent Spiner and Star Trek: The Next Generation alum Jonathan Frakes: “Did not look up from the phone. “I don’t know why I didn’t look up, but obviously I didn’t hear you. I was probably reading a great review of one of your movies and it occupied my attention to the point where I didn’t look up,” he wrote.

Shatner went on to say that he “would’ve relished the chance to talk” to Carpenter, expressing his admiration for the filmmaker and calling him “obviously one of the great directors in film.”.

Sources indicate that “Next time you see me, please come over and introduce yourself, because I really don’t know what you look like and apparently you know what I look like,” he wrote. “But I would enjoy saying hello to you, and this piece of s— would confide in you his admiration.”.
