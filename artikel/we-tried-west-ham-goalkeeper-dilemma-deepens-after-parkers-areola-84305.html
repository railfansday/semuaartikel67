West Ham United manager Scott Parker has admitted that Alphonse Areola is unlikely to be available for Saturday’s Championship clash with Preston North End, leaving the Hammers with a familiar goalkeeping dilemma.

Speaking during his pre-match press conference, Parker explained that the club had attempted to push the Frenchman’s recovery but were struggling to get him ready.

“We tried to push it a bit, but it looks a little bit unlikely and we’ll leave it as long as we can before making a decision,” mentioned the West Ham boss.

This isn’t particularly surprising. It’s something we’ve discussed previously on Claret & Hugh, with concerns surrounding Areola’s match fitness and reports that he has been carrying too much weight.

And considering Parker has worked with the Frenchman before, one has to assume he’d be really keen to play him.

The fact that he isn’t selecting Areola suggests the goalkeeper simply isn’t ready for action, particularly following his recent injury setback.

Personally, my choice would be to give young Fin Herrick a chance.

I was speaking to my Hammers Chat counterpart Geo during our West Ham v Preston preview this week, and he made an excellent observation.

The one thing Mads Hermansen has consistently been good at is his distribution with the ball at his feet.

Unfortunately, his clanger for Denmark in their last game means even that aspect of his performances is now under scrutiny.

He’s got the yips: West Ham goalkeeper Mads Hermansen, whose recent performances have raised concerns about his confidence.

I actually think the best thing we could possibly do for Hermansen would be to give him a rest.

His confidence has clearly taken a battering, and his latest international mistake has only increased the pressure.

Let’s be fair, young Herrick can’t perform any worse than the Danish keeper has in the last few games.

And as our recent coverage of supporters’ goalkeeping preferences demonstrated, there is already considerable dissatisfaction with the current situation.

I appreciate throwing a youngster into such an important game carries risks, but there comes a point when continuing with an out-of-form goalkeeper becomes an equally significant gamble.

For me, Saturday presents an opportunity to try something different.
