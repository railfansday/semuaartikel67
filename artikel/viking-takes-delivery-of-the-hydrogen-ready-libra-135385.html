Viking Takes Delivery of the Hydrogen-Ready Libra

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Viking has taken delivery of the Viking Libra, its newest ocean ship, at Fincantieri’s shipyard in Ancona, Italy.

The 998-guest ship will sail itineraries in the Mediterranean and Northern Europe in her inaugural season.

She is a sister to the rest of Viking’s ocean fleet, but unlike the others she has a hybrid propulsion system designed to run on liquefied hydrogen and fuel cells.

The tech giant’s next ocean ship, the Viking Astrea, is under construction and will have the same system. Viking expects both ships to be operating on hydrogen in 2027.

“The delivery of the Viking Libra is a proud moment for Viking and an crucial milestone in our growing ocean fleet,” said Leah Talactac, president and CEO of Viking.

“Together with the Viking Astrea, she will set a upcoming standard for innovation as we bring this technology into operation in 2027 and will allow us to welcome even more curious travelers to explore the world in Viking comfort,” she added.

Under its committed orderbook, Viking expects to take delivery of 25 more river ships by 2030, 10 more ocean ships by 2032 and two more expedition ships by 2031.

Those deliveries would bring the tech giant’s fleet to 123 river ships in 2030 and 28 ocean and expedition ships in 2032.
