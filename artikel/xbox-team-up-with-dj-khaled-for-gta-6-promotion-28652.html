Industry observers note that GTA 6 is just days away, and the highly anticipated release is leaving players looking for an excuse to take time off work to explore Leonida, Rockstar’s take on the state of Florida. XBOX is stepping in to give gamers a day off work, but it’s unlikely their strategy will work with your boss.

As we gear up to launch, a multitude of celebration events have already been revealed, including exclusive Twitch rewards for tuning into GTA 6 streams for the remainder of November, and even an in-person concert in Miami.

DJ Khaled has extended his resume and rebranded to Dr XBOX, and is handing out sick notes for those who want to play GTA 6 on launch day and avoid any other responsibilities.

“After extensive research (and a whole lot of gaming), I’ve come to one conclusion: there’s a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency around the world.” According to Dr XBOX, the cure is as simple as taking the day off work.

The promotion doesn’t explicitly mention GTA 6, but there are several clues. First of all, DJ Khaled is sporting an XBOX-branded lab coat and showing off his XBOX University certificate by a poolside, wearing a casual leaf-patterned outfit, and is signing doctor’s notes “from the sunny shores of tropical Miami.”.

Without naming GTA 6, the Dr XBOX states, “a significant week in gaming is coming, and I don’t want anybody missing a single moment. We’ve got games to play, worlds to explore, achievements to unlock and victories to celebrate.” GTA 6 is undoubtedly the biggest release not only this month, but in recent years, so it’s easy to piece together what XBOX is getting at here.

Since PlayStation has promotional rights over GTA 6, it’s not surprising to see XBOX trying to take advantage of this huge release through as many unofficial paths as possible.

Recent findings suggest that to get your sick note from Dr XBOX, you must visit the dedicated Dr XBOX website and enter your name. You could always use your gamertag for that gaming feel if you wish, but you’re unable to enter numbers or symbols.

According to Dr XBOX, the cure is as simple as taking the day off work.

It has been revealed that once you’ve typed in your name and hit enter, the note will say you’re experiencing a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency, along with the need to avoid work, your symptoms, and the doctor’s recommendations. The note is signed by Dr XBOX and has an official XBOX stamp of approval. You can then share your note with friends, on social media, and even attach it to an email to your boss, but it’s unlikely to fool anyone.

According to recent reports, A recent survey pointed towards 12.5 million American gamers planning to adjust their schedules for the massive day. Of course, the doctor’s note has no official standing, but it’s a fun little gimmick to get into the GTA 6 spirit. XBOX’s disclaimer even outlines that “XBOX is not responsible if your employer doesn’t have a sense of humor.”.

The wait is almost over, as GTA 6 will launch worldwide on November 19, 2026, on XBOX Series X|S and PlayStation 5.
