Houston Rockets star Kevin Durant has listed his sprawling Arizona property for $13.9 million—jut three years after he picked up the palatial pad.

The NBA icon, 38, purchased the property in 2023 for $12.9 million from fellow athlete DeAndre Hopkins when he was traded from the Brooklyn Nets to the Phoenix Suns.

Now, following his Texas trade, the basketball star is looking to part ways with the property, listing it with Alexa Kort of The Beverly Hills Estates and Adam Bowman and Brian North of Compass, as first reported by the Wall Street Journal.

In a recent development, durant initially put the property on the market in September 2025 for $14.4 million. He then trimmed the price to $13.9 million, before removing it from the selling block.

The home, which was designed by acclaimed architect Jack DeBartolo, offers up 10,865 square feet and is situated on a 1.72-acre lot.

It comes complete with five bedrooms, eight bathrooms, and several other lavish amenities.

Upon entering the home, guests are greeted with poured-in-place concrete walls, gallerylike corridors, floor-to-ceiling glass, and custom finishes.

The living room offers guests the perfect place to lounge thanks to its cozy fireplace.

Although minimalist, the home offers a moody aesthetic complete with dark tones.

The chef's kitchen boasts a black marble island with matching black cabinets and state-of-the-art appliances.

The primary suite offers enough space for a king-sized bed and opens directly to the outdoor area.

The primary bathroom is certainly the crown jewel of the property thanks to its Calacatta marble bath and dual vanities.

The home also features a sleek movie theater room, a walk-in closet with custom storage space, and an office space.

Elsewhere in the home, there is a sauna and steam shower.

The property also features a 2,000-square-foot garage designed to show off spectacular vehicles.

Moving outside, guests can enjoy an 82-foot lap and reflecting pool as well as panoramic views of the Camelback Mountain, the Praying Monk and Piestewa Peak, as per a press release shared with Realtor.com®.

This isn't the first time Durant's property holdings were at the center of headlines.

In 2025, his former luxury townhome in Oklahoma City, OK, which boasts a prime downtown location, four bedrooms, two kitchens, and a private elevator—hit the market for less than the cost of a bag of groceries.

"The owner said, 'Let's just put it out there for $35 and see if someone wants to own a piece of NBA history,'" listing agent Ieasha Larkpor, of Thunder Team Realty, told Realtor.com® at the time.

The jaw-droppingly low price tag is meant to spark interest, and so far the ploy has worked, with Larkpor fielding hundreds of offers—some even in the high six figures, close to the price the owner is hoping to get.

But that hasn't stopped the occasional joker from taking the abnormally low price tag literally.

Recent findings suggest that "Yes, I've gotten offers for $35," mentioned the agent. "And I have to present them to the owner. But luckily, they can be weeded out fast.".

Durant bought two three-level townhomes at 422 NE 2nd Street for $1,769,000 in 2013 while playing with the Oklahoma City Thunder.

The NBA and Olympic star put his stamp all over the four-bedroom, four-bath townhouse, including an extra-tall shower and an extra-long bathtub to accommodate his 6-foot-11-inch frame.

"Everything in the unit was designed by Kevin Durant himself or his designers according to his taste at the time," remarked Larkpor. The furniture and artwork could be negotiable with the sale.

It has been revealed that in 2017, the sports legend sold one of the 3,904-square-foot units for $1.15 million to a neighbor in the complex. The neighbor happened to be a fan who kept most of Durant's stylistic choices, including the paint colors, bold wallpaper, and light fixtures.

"The new owner wanted to leave [Durant's] stamp, as it is a very nice stamp," noted Larkpor.
