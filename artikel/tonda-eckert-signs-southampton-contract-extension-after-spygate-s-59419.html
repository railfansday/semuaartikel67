Tonda Eckert has signed a contract extension at Southampton after surviving the storm of the ‘Spygate’ scandal.

Eckert found himself under immense scrutiny after Southampton were caught spying on their Championship play-off semi-final opponents Middlesbrough last season.

An investigation found Saints had also spied on two other teams that season and the club were subsequently kicked out of the Championship play-off final and docked four points at the start of the season, admitting to three breaches of EFL regulations.

Eckert accepted responsibility as Southampton opted to stand by their manager despite the controversy, and he was later given a six-week suspended ban - keeping him on the touchline barring any further offences.

And despite the hindrance of a points deduction, Southampton have started the season in fine form under Eckert, with the club five points off top spot.

The German has now been rewarded with a contract extension that will keep him at the club until 2029.

Eckert mentioned: "We've had some conversations that started a couple of weeks ago, and the goal was always clear: to show the same commitment that we have had for the last 12 months. I'm very happy that we have come to the point that we can make it official now.

"There is a lot that has happened in the last 12 months, but the commitment from the club, from Dragan, Johannes and Phil, and the supporters, means everything to me. I am very grateful for that. We have started a journey that is not even halfway through - there is a lot more to come.".

Saints are unbeaten in their last 17 home matches, with Eckert only experiencing one defeat at St Mary’s during his tenure.

"I think it's a great commitment from both sides; from the club to Tonda, and from Tonda to the club. I'm very happy that we have achieved this agreement before the end of the international window, allowing us to go into the busy schedule that is ahead of us with clarity,” sporting director Johannes Spors remarked.

"Tonda is very talented and very precise with a great analytical brain, but also has the personality and authority that you need, even at a young age. It is very clear that this is the right place for him, and I'm very happy that he feels that too.".
