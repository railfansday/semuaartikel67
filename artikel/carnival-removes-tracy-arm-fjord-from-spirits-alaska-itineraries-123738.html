Carnival Removes Tracy Arm Fjord from Spirit’s Alaska Itineraries

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Carnival Cruise Line has removed planned visits to Tracy Arm Fjord from the itineraries of the Carnival Spirit.

According to a statement sent to booked guests, the change will apply to all cruises scheduled to depart in 2027.

“Due to the continued closure of Tracy Arm Fjord from unstable ice and geological conditions, we will be cruising Endicott Arm Fjord instead,” Carnival mentioned.

The company added that Endicott Arm offers equally breathtaking scenery and an enjoyable experience for passengers.

In addition to replacing the call at Tracy Arm Fjord, Carnival also remarked that the Spirit will sail from its homeport in Seattle half an hour earlier than planned, at 3:00 PM.

The statement noted that in preparation for departure all guests must be onboard no later than the final boarding time specified on their boarding pass.

The company explained that the change is designed to allow guests more time to enjoy the onboard experience and amenities.

Sailing roundtrip from Seattle, the Carnival Spirit is scheduled to offer itineraries to Alaska and Canada during the 2027 summer.

In addition to scenic cruising at Endicott Arm Fjord, the cruises feature visits to Ketchikan, Skagway, Juneau and Victoria.

The ship’s regular itinerary in the region will be offered between late April and mid-September, when a longer cruise is also scheduled.

Wrapping up its Alaska season, the Carnival Spirit offers a nine-night itinerary that adds a visit to Icy Strait Point, in addition to a longer stay in Victoria.

The Carnival Spirit is then scheduled to Galveston during the fall of 2027 before arriving in Tampa for the 2027-28 winter season.

The ship’s 2028 deployment includes a return to Seattle for another season sailing to Alaska and Canada from Washington.
