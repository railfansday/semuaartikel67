The Princess of Wales touched down in India this Monday for a whirlwind three-day visit to the country with her husband, Prince William.

The couple visited Mumbai for the sixth Earthshot Prize Summit, which took place on Tuesday. It marked the Waleses’ first joint overseas visit since Catherine’s cancer diagnosis.

In a recent development, the busy schedule of engagements kicked off with a game of gully cricket—an informal version of the game played on the street. For the occasion, the Princess of Wales struck a balance between polished and casual, opting for crisp white Alexander McQueen trousers, paired with a white top layered under a pistachio blazer by Veronica Beard.

The Princess of Wales debuted a pistachio blazer by Veronica Beard for the outing.

The finishing touch? A matching set of green amethyst earrings and a drop necklace from one of Catherine’s favorite jewelry labels, Kiki McDonough. Catherine has at least 20 pairs of earrings from the jeweler, ranging from the blue topaz drops she wore to Royal Ascot to the green tourmaline, green amethyst and diamond earrings set in gold, designed specially for her to mark Princess Charlotte’s birth.

The apple green amethysts, which she wore in Mumbai, are among the lesser-seen designs in her collection but proved the perfect accessory for this first engagement.

While in Mumbai, the prince’s program focused on The Earthshot Prize and its mission to accelerate solutions to the world’s most pressing environmental challenges. The princess joined the prince for a number of engagements during the visit.

The Prince and Princess of Wales in Mumbai on Monday.

During their time in Mumbai, the prince and princess also celebrated several of the deep cultural connections between the United Kingdom and India: cricket, fashion, and cinema.

Beginning with an engagement focused on the uniquely Indian passion of gully cricket andconcluding with a visit to the heart of Mumbai’s film industry, their royal highnessescelebrated the creativity, talent, and cultural influence that continue to shape modern India andstrengthen ties between the two countries.

Industry observers note that the highlight of the visit was the Earthshot Prize ceremony on Tuesday, November 17. Five Earthshot leaders were given 1 million euros (about $1.1 million USD) to accelerate the impact of their innovative solutions. Mumbai made perfect sense as the host city; India is home to more Earthshot Prize winners and finalists than any other country.

The Princess of Wales looked elegant in a lime green blazer layered over an all-white ensemble.

Recent findings suggest that since founding the prize in 2020, Prince William has attended all of the awards ceremonies: in London, Boston, Singapore, Cape Town, and Rio de Janeiro. Catherine joined the prince in London in 2021 and again in Boston the following year, but has missed recent editions in the further corners of the globe. This was partly due to the princess’s restricted work schedule while she underwent treatment for cancer and spent time in recovery.

In a recent development, it marked a return to the country for Prince William and Kate, who carried out a state visit in April 2016. The week-long tour saw the then Duke and Duchess of Cambridge explore Mumbai, Latest Delhi, Assam, and Bhutan, racking up a total of 22 engagements that saw them try their hand at cricket, meet Bollywood stars and royalty, and champion charitable causes close to their hearts.

According to recent reports, it was one of four overseas trips taken by the couple that year. Although William and Kate still travel, it is with far less frequency as the couple prioritizes a stable family life. Their last major overseas tour was to the Caribbean in 2022.
