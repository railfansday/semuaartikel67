Publisher Oro Interactive and developer Mike Klubnika will release violent party game collection Machine Party for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch, and PC via Microsoft Store on November 26, the companies announced.

Machine Party first launched for PC via Steam on July 30, where it has sold over 2.5 million copies.

Here is an overview of the game, via Oro Interactive:.

Welcome to Machine Party. In this series of dangerous, and some would say highly illegal, party games you will have to use every trick in the book to survive. Play through classic party game formats and fresh twists on the genre for sessions of fun, screaming, and horror.
