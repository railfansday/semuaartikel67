iOS 27.3 is coming soon, packed with latest features for iPhone users. Here’s when to expect the next major iPhone software update.

If you follow Apple closely, the organization’s software release patterns rapidly become clear.

For example, iOS 27.2 debuted late last month around the same time as releases in years past. The only difference is that normally iOS 27.1 would launch in late October rather than 27.2. But since Apple made iOS 27.1 exclusive to iPhone Duo, all software releases for the iOS 27 cycle will be off by one number—despite following the same timing as ever.

That means iOS 27.3 is up next, and its release should align with historic patterns for x.2 updates.

Apple has confirmed that iOS 27.3 will launch to all users in December once beta testing concludes.

Based on this pattern, iOS 27.3 is expected to release by mid-December.

According to recent reports, apple always tries to ship its last software enhancement of the year with a little buffer before taking a holiday break. That way if any major concerns arise, the organization can develop a quick fix and push it out before much of the company takes time off.

Expect to see iOS 27.3 launch as early as the week of December 7, or on Monday, December 14 at the latest.
