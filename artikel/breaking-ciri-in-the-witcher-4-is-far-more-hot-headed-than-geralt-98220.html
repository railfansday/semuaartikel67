Breaking: Ciri in The Witcher 4 is "far more hot-headed" than Geralt, CD Projekt Red lead indicates

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After three Geralt-centred installments, things are changing a little in The Witcher 4, since Ciri will be the main character instead. This presents a whole other perspective and personal tapestry to the player, and one of the storytelling leads at CD Projekt Red recently spoke on exactly how.

Sources indicate that marcin Blacha, the VP of story at the studio, explained the contrasts between Geralt and Ciri to Inven Global. "Ciri isn't calm and composed like Geralt, nor does she hide her emotions easily," he states. "She is far more hot-headed and tends to be emotional when making decisions. That difference changes a lot of things.".

The guiding factor isn't necessarily what's strictly right or wrong, but also how intertwined you've become with any given situation. "When playing as Geralt, players acted more like an arbiter judging how to resolve a situation," Blacha continues. "When playing as Ciri, you are much more deeply involved in the story through her personal perspective.".

This impacts the way quests are structured, because CD Projekt Red isn't falling back on the grumpy stoicism of Geralt. "It's no longer just about deciding which choice is morally right; how emotionally invested you are in the character will also shape your decisions," he explains. "Because Ciri is a different person from Geralt, the quests in The Witcher 4 will take on a different shape compared to The Witcher 3.".

He notes at the top of his answer that "changing the protagonist is a huge factor," and that goes for all avenues. Geralt is very much the face of the franchise so far, making the jump to Ciri a massive creative turn, but she presents all sorts of opportunities for expanding the lore - and the property's reach, too.

After three games, and now yet more DLC for The Witcher 3, Geralt's had plenty of time in the limelight. It's time for someone else to take charge, and it seems like we'll be seeing a whole other side of The Continent in turn.

The Witcher 4 is going to be "an unforgettable experience that's true to the core of the Witcher," narrative lead says as the RPG wins a most-wanted award.
