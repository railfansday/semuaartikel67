📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Restricted NVIDIA Blackwell chips have been discovered at a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang and Megaspeed, Bloomberg reported. Regulatory filings identified a GB300 NVL72 rack and a GB200 server installed by two Chinese companies. The GB300 hardware was also pledged as collateral for a $14 million loan. The discovery raises concerns about the enforcement of U.S. semiconductor export controls. However, how the restricted chips reached China remains unclear. Bloomberg found no evidence that Megaspeed diverted the hardware, while the company has denied any wrongdoing.
