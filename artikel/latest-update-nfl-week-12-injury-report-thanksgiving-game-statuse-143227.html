In a recent development, the NFL is in the midst of its most chaotic week of the season. Due to the Thanksgiving holiday, we've embarked on a six-day stretch that includes five days of games. While the Packers and Rams get things started on Wednesday night, we still have the Thanksgiving triple-header and the Black Friday game before we even start talking about the regular Sunday slate and, of course, Monday Night Football.

When you sprinkle in some utter madness between Aaron Rodgers and his now-former team, the Pittsburgh Steelers, there will be plenty to talk about around the dinner table this holiday season.

According to recent reports, of course, one of the topics of conversation will also be injuries. They're an omnipresent aspect of the NFL and are a factor as we step into Week 12. Below, we examine every injury report across the league, as well as highlight the game statuses for Thanksgiving.

All games are Sunday unless noted.

Sources indicate that while Wednesday's practice was conducted as a walk-through, Tyrique Stevenson Sr. did pop up on the injury report with a groin injury. He was limited for the session and officially listed as questionable.

Recent findings suggest that gill Howard nor Rakestraw practiced this week, so it's not surprising to see them ruled out. On a more positive note, Chuck Clark returned to practice on Tuesday and remained a limited participant during Wednesday's session. Moreover, pass rusher DJ Wonnum was limited on Wednesday after missing the first two practices of the week with a back injury.

Recent findings suggest that quinyon Mitchell carries no game designation, so the Eagles star corner is slated to play. He was a full participant during Wednesday's practice after missing Monday's session and being limited on Tuesday due to a hamstring injury.

According to recent reports, dak Prescott was listed on the injury report this week with a left hand injury, but the Cowboys quarterback was a full participant throughout and has no game designation. Meanwhile, Booker and Ferguson did not practice at all this week and will be sidelined for Week 12.

For the third straight week, the Chiefs will be without Kenneth Walker III. The star back continues to deal with a groin injury and, after practicing on a limited basis on Tuesday, did not participate on Wednesday. Emmett Johnson will once again assume the role as Kansas City's lead back.

Industry observers note that after missing Monday's session, Bills corner Christian Benford logged back-to-back limited days of practice and is officially questionable to play. Meanwhile, Kincaid, Sanders, Torrence and Williams were limited throughout the week.

Guard David Edwards (hamstring), offensive tackle Taliese Fuaga (knee), center Erik McCoy (biceps), offensive tackle Asim Richards (knee) and linebacker Pete Werner (hamstring) were limited.

Pass rusher Abdul Carter did not practice on Wednesday due to ankle and calf injuries. John Harbaugh noted Carter is day-to-day, but should play this weekend. Defensive tackle Shelby Harris and corner Andru Phillips also missed practice due to illness. Tight end Isaiah Likely (groin) and linebacker Arvell Reese (back) were limited.

On a more positive note, receiver Romeo Doubs (shoulder) and left guard Alijah Vera-Trucker (hamstring), who are both on injured reserve but had their practice windows open, were limited.
