Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers: Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search, all deeply integrated.

Recent findings suggest that developers decide what to build with by listening to people they trust, much of it on Twitter, where Supabase's community, launches, and competitors are all discussed in public. We want a senior DevRel leader who is already a known, trusted voice there, to be a brand ambassador for Supabase. You'll work alongside our New Media and DevRel teams and help shape how Supabase highlights up in these conversations. We hire for the voice and following you already have.

Be a visible, trusted voice for Supabase on Twitter: post, reply, and join developer conversations daily.

Share sharp, useful content, from rolls out and tips to opinions on where building is going.

Represent Supabase at events, on podcasts, and in the wider developer community.

Bring developer feedback to engineering and product, and work with New Media, DevRel, and PMM on launches.

In a recent development, you have an existing, engaged Twitter following. We'll look at who you talk to and how people respond, as well as follower count.

You have senior experience in DevRel, developer advocacy, or a similar developer-facing role.

You're credible with a technical audience and a strong writer with a distinctive voice.

You're comfortable representing a firm publicly, on camera, on stage, and online.

According to recent reports, we hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the manufacturer. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Recent findings suggest that supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are key to us.

Once a year, the entire organization gathers in a latest city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F).

According to recent reports, we move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.
