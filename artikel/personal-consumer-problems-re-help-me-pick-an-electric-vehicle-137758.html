Sources indicate that you have a lot of good options. More importantly, there are no bad options. (I'll put a plug in for Chevy - Equinox and Blazer and Hyundai/Kia. The European brands - the UIs are poorly designed. This may not be true on the very recent models. Rivian is the basis of all VW brands going forward.)I'd suggest used - two to four year old models. Find 2-3 that fit your budget. And test drive them. (Nothing wrong with leasing.)Ideally, you might even want to rent one for a few days. Several rental car companies offer EVs still. Even if you rent a Tesla - just get hands on experience. I wouldn't buy any car that cannot charge on the Tesla network. This will give you options on long road trips. You drive mostly local. You don't need to spend money on a charger. On a 110 outlet, most cars will charge at 4 miles/hour plugged in. I commuted for a year, 40-50 miles daily. I only used 110 except for longer out of town trips.If you are looking for self driving/related, Tesla is the only game in town. However, it seems Rivian and Mercedes are right behind.
