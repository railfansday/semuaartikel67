📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The unused areas give the developer ‘options in the future.’.

Industry observers note that it's no secret by now that the Grand Theft Auto VI map is absolutely enormous. According to Kotaku, walking across the map from south to north would take more than three hours, while running the same route and distance would take almost two. Meanwhile, it would only take you roughly 30 minutes to run across GTA V's map from one end to the other. In addition, there are plenty of locations you won't encounter or visit if you just play the game by following its story. Turns out there's a reason for both: Rockstar is making sure it's prepared for future updates and expansions.

Rockstar's VP of design Stephen Donkin told Kotaku that GTA VI's map "has been built for the long term and with many factors in mind." He mentioned that these extra areas that aren't included in the story give Rockstar options in the future if it has "a gameplay or a narrative need for them.".

Donkin revealed the developer's plans for those areas when the publication asked about two large casinos on the map, which you can't enter (yet) and aren't even referenced in-game. He explained that it didn't seem right to introduce gambling in Jason and Lucia's story, "especially with the fine balance [Rockstar] sought throughout the economy." Kotaku also specifically mentioned a zoo and a small town in the north that the author never got to visit during their playthrough.

While Donkin didn't give specific examples of what the developer intends to do with these extra locations, his statement clearly indicates that Rockstar could add more content in the future that will use them. If it's not a major expansion, it could be smaller storyline updates, new mini-games or perhaps an online experience of sorts.
