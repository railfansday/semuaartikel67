Xabi Alonso has pleaded with fans to support Wesley Fofana after the Chelsea defender had a night to forget against Nottingham Forest.

The Blues defender, who has been singled out for poor performances during the Chelsea’s bad run of form, needed a strong show, having spoken for almost two hours with French YouTuber Zack Nani on a range of topics.

Fofana admitted that he came close to quitting football whilst he was sidelined with an anterior cruciate injury, while also appearing to joke about missing his penalty during last month’s Carabao Cup shootout defeat to Liverpool.

He apologised for the interview and Alonso said the matter was closed.

Sources indicate that “We have addressed that with him [Fofana]. He has apologised," Alonso told reporters in the pre-match press conference.

“It was not right, he has said it is not going to be repeated, these types of things.”.

However, Friday’s Premier League game at the City Ground was a bad one for the Frenchman.

The visiting supporters jeered the Fofana when he was in possession in the first half and a bad night got worse in the early stages of the second half when he brought down Liam Delap for a penalty that Morgan Gibbs-White converted to double Forest’s lead.

He was substituted soon after, following chants from travelling Blues fans of “f*** off Fofana, you’re not wanted here.”.

Speaking after the 3-2 loss to Forest, Alonso jumped to the defence of Fofana, insisting now is the time for solidarity as Chelsea aim to arrest a poor run of form.
