Sources indicate that beats has a long history of making on-ear headphones that stand with the plushest and best-sounding cans on the market. As Black Friday deals are here, it's a great time to upgrade: Walmart has discounted the Beats Solo 4 by more than 50% to $99.95 (originally $199.95); the sale applies to the black, pink, and slate blue variants.

Sources indicate that these headphones don’t have ANC, just passive isolation, but they still deliver solid sound, comfort, and strong battery life. They use physical controls and support one-touch pairing with most Apple devices, as well as hands-free Siri. The Solo 4 headphones are compatible with Bluetooth 5.3 and work with the AAC and SBC codecs. They also support wired audio via 3.5mm or USB-C.

The headphones last around 50 hours per charge (about 10 hours longer than their predecessor), and 10 minutes of charging gives you around five hours of playback, with a full recharge taking approximately two hours. In its review, PCMag describes the sound as “sculpted with accentuated bass and highs.".

It has been revealed that although they lack ANC and adjustable EQ, these headphones still have plenty of extra features for Apple owners, and those shortcomings are much easier to forgive at 50% off. For $99, the Beats Solo 4 headphones are a compact and well-equipped option for casual listening, with long battery life and that classic Beats aesthetic.
