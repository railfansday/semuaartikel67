📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Rocket Lab(RKLB -1.17%), a developer of reusable orbital rockets and other spacecraft components, has successfully debuted its Electron rocket 99 times to date. But its second rocket, the larger Neutron, has been repeatedly delayed since its introduction in 2021.

Sources indicate that rocket Lab originally planned to launch the Neutron in 2024, but it eventually pushed that target back to 2025 and 2026. But in October, it postponed the Neutron's first launch again to 2027.

It has been revealed that those setbacks were discouraging, since the Neutron would have helped Rocket Lab compete more effectively against SpaceX's (SPCX +1.78%) Falcon 9 rockets. But during Rocket Lab's third-quarter conference call on Nov. 11, CFO Adam Spice claimed the manufacturer's adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) would turn positive within roughly six months of Neutron's first successful test flight.

NASDAQ · As of 9:52 AM ET 11/17/26.

Rocket Lab believes that will happen as Neutron's launch removes the rocket's heavy development expenses from its bottom line. It also expects the Neutron to unlock a much larger, higher-margin commercial market for its Launch Services segment.

According to recent reports, assuming that it can achieve those goals, analysts expect Rocket Lab's adjusted EBITDA to come in at positive $41 million in 2027 -- compared to negative $55 million in 2026. So while Rocket Lab hasn't given us a clear date for its first Neutron launch yet, we can optimistically assume it will happen sometime within the first half of 2027 to hit Wall Street's targets.
