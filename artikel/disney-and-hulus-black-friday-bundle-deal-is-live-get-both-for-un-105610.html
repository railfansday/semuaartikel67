It has been revealed that unlike typical streaming promos that only discount the cheapest ad-supported tier for a couple of months, this offer locks in promotional pricing for a full 12 months across both the ad-supported and ad-free Duo plans. Here's how the two Black Friday tiers break down:.

Disney+ and Hulu Bundle (With Ads) — $4.99/month for 12 months (regularly $12.99/month — save $8/month, or $96 over the year).

Disney+ and Hulu Bundle (No Ads) — $14.99/month for 12 months (regularly $21.99/month — save $7/month, or $84 over the year).

Sources indicate that both bundles give you full access to Disney+, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic, Hulu Originals, next-day fall TV, and movies, plus select live ESPN College Football games and College GameDay on Saturdays inside Disney+. You can stream through both standalone apps or watch select Hulu content directly inside the Disney+ app (up to two simultaneous streams for Hulu content via Disney+), with 4K UHD and Dolby Atmos support on compatible titles, parental controls, and Disney+ Perks included.

If you spring for the Premium tier, you also get offline downloads and ad-free viewing across most on-demand titles (though ads still appear in select live and linear programming).

There is no annual lock-in, so you can cancel anytime (cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period, with no refunds or credits for partial months), though once your 12-month promotional year wraps up, your plan will automatically renew at the regular monthly rate ($12.99 or $21.99 per month, or the then-current regular price) unless you cancel beforehand.
