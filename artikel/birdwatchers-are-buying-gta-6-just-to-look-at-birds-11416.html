GTA 6 hype has even reached the birdwatching community, where some players are already planning to put the heists on hold and see how many birds they can spot.

With GTA 6 launching on November 19, the wait for the first fresh game in the series in over a decade is almost over. It’s already expected to break sales records when it drops and could cost the US as much as $1 billion in sick days as fans abandon work to play.

But it’s not just video game players who are ready to lose hours in Vice City, even birdwatchers are pumped for the release.

GTA 6 reportedly has 62 types of birds to find and record in a wildlife logbook, giving birdwatchers plenty of reasons to explore Leonida with a pair of binoculars.

Recent findings suggest that A November 15 post on r/birding picked up more than 1,700 upvotes after one user asked if any other birdwatchers were looking forward to the game. “I think the crime sprees are going to have to wait,” they wrote.

It turns out they were far from alone, as plenty of fellow birders replied, saying they were ready to take their hobby into GTA 6.

“I’m so hyped. Probably the first thing I do if we’re given the binoculars right away,” stated one reply.

Sources indicate that “My birding group is planning to stream the week after it comes out,” added another. “We streamed Red Dead last year, and we’re hoping there’ll be tons of birds to spot for GTA.”.

GTA 6 forces you to take your time with the main story instead of speedrunning it.

GTA 6 will feature over 170 different animals including “Legendary” ones.

Police can’t wait for GTA 6 to be released so they can “catch a break”.

“I feel like such a dork being excited for the wildlife in a GTA game of all places but I truly am excited to see it all LOL,” wrote a third.

This isn’t the first time that a Rockstar game has appealed to birdwatchers either, as many commented that Red Dead Redemption 2 got them into the hobby.

“RDR2 flipped the birding switch on for me. I have identified actual birds irl because I saw them in the game,” noted one Redditor. “No shooting though!”.

While these players will be heading out into Leonida to find birds, others will be looking to find the best weapons and cars.
