Thierry Henry believes Arsenal can end Manchester City’s title hopes this weekend.

Mikel Arteta’s table-topping champions take on Enzo Maresca’s struggling contenders on Sunday, November 29, with six points currently separating the two sides.

As such, victory for Arsenal would extend the margin between the Gunners and City to nine points after 12 games.

Henry is predicting his former side to “dominate” City, despite a stuttering return to action, with draws with Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund, since returning from the international break.

Recent findings suggest that he told CBS Sports: “You have two teams who have to rectify two things at the weekend. Arsenal, like we’ve been saying recently, and Mikel Arteta has been saying, when you’re on top of a team, don’t let them come back to attack like we’ve seen in the last two games.

Recent findings suggest that "If you go back to City, and I’ve said it since the beginning of the season, the balance of the team isn’t right. Simple as that.

"They have quality, they have goalscorers who will bail them out, Rayan Cherki bailed them out [against Napoli] but the cohesion of the team, the way they don’t stop counters, they don't control the game on and off the ball like they used to.

"That didn't stop them losing games under Pep [Guardiola] but they were more in control of games.

Recent findings suggest that "You have two teams who have been dominating the league in the last two, three years. But I want to see an Arsenal team that's going to dominate City, and they are there to be dominated.

"But on the other side, it might be the day that City wake up. But if they play the same way they’ve been playing recently, being open and all over the place in midfield, it could be a long day.".

In a recent development, jamie Carragher agreed with his CBS Sports colleague, adding: "I think Arsenal will win. I can’t see City getting anything from that game.

"They’ve been really poor of late, the results have shown that, not strong defensively at all.

Sources indicate that "And that's not because Arsenal are amazing. Listen, they are a top team, don’t get me wrong, but in terms of the Champions League, I don’t think the Premier League teams can match… only Arsenal can really match what we’re seeing from the top teams.”.

Carragher went on to argue that Arsenal’s proven strength in the Champions League automatically made the Gunners favourites for a second Premier League crown in two years.
