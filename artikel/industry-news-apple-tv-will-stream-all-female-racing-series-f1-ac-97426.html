Recent findings suggest that the F1 Academy season starts on March 19, 2027. And with this news, you’ll be able to watch it through the Apple TV app with an Apple TV subscription, joining the Formula 1 coverage. The schedule runs across seven races through November.

Apple TV will feature full coverage of Academy qualifying and race sessions.
