Mikel Arteta has warned Ethan Nwaneri that "moments are not enough" if he is to turn his Borussia Dortmund loan around.

The young forward joined the German club in the summer on loan from Arsenal and he was initially handed opportunities in the starting lineup by Niko Kovac.

However, those have been few and far between in recent weeks. Nwaneri has not started a match in over a month and has played just five minutes of football across Dortmund's last three league matches.

The German side, who sit top of the Bundesliga, travel to north London to take on the Premier League champions on Tuesday in the Champions League and Nwaneri is eligible to face his parent club.

It appears unlikely, though, that the 19-year-old will start against the Gunners considering Dortmund's fine form, and Arteta has warned the teenager that nothing will come easy.

"We're talking here about the highest level in Europe," Arteta noted.

"To play at that level is extremely difficult. You can have patches, but to find that consistency and to play a big amount of minutes and have a huge impact at such big football clubs like Arsenal and Dortmund is extremely difficult.

"That's the reality. You can just shine in moments, but moments are not enough. Now you need to show the consistency.

"Ethan has the capacity and the mentality to do that but it takes some time.".

Industry observers note that arteta added: "He needs to earn the right to play. He's playing at the top of the Bundesliga and the players have huge talent and quality and they are showing that and that's the reality of football.

"You go there and because you come from Arsenal, it doesn't give you any advantage, so you need to earn it.

"Ethan has, first of all, the mindset to do it and then the quality to do it and these things are part of a journey and part of a season and he needs to reveal himself and earn the right to win.".

Nwaneri spent the second half of last season on loan at Marseille and barely played after Roberto De Zerbi's departure in February.

Arsenal are understood to have the option to recall Nwaneri in January if his loan at Dortmund is proving to be similarly unproductive.

For now, though, Arteta remains "unconvinced" that the move can still benefit Nwaneri's development.

"He was in Marseille as well," the Spaniard remarked.

"In Marseille he had a completely different context for a lot of things that are so unexpected to happen.

"But that builds a player and builds the character. I'm so convinced of that. You are about to fulfil a role that you are given on the day and try and change that for the one you actually want. That's football.".
