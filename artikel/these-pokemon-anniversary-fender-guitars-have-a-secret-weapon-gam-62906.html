The Pokemon Manufacturer revealed that it teamed up with guitar manufacturer Fender to produce a Stratocaster model inspired by Pokemon Red, and a Telecaster model inspired by Pokemon Green. Each guitar features an aesthetic that pays tribute to the original Pokemon Game Boy adventures, including pixel art pulled from the games, graphics, and color overlays for an extra pop. The guitars really shine with a special toggle that warps the music they produce into an 8-bit retro sound that’s reminiscent of vintage gaming hardware. The neck also pays homage to Pokemon, with a special design that commemorates the collaboration between Fender and Pokemon.
