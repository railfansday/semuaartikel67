Premier League highlights: Nottingham Forest 3-2 Chelsea.

Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea at the City Ground for the first time since 1997.

Igor Jesus' brilliant volley opened the scoring after just five minutes, before Gibbs-White struck twice in the second half as Chelsea were consigned to a fourth defeat in five matches.

Forest ran out 3-2 winners despite Emmanuel Emegha twice pulling goals back.
