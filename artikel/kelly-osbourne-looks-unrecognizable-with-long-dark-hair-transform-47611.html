Kelly Osbourne wants to be where the people are.

It has been revealed that so much so, the Osbournes alum channeled Ariel from The Little Mermaid with a dark red hair transformation for her Nov. 19 appearance at the ZYN Rolling Stone U.K. Awards.

Osbourne—who has been rocking a blonde, shoulder-length hair in recent months—stepped out with long, auburn tresses, which she styled in loose waves. She paired the look with a shimmery, ocean blue gown from Rebecca Vallance and gold jewelry.

Sources indicate that sharing a selfie set to "Part of Your World," the 42-year-old wrote on her Instagram Story in reference to the Disney song, "It's give.. you want thingamabobs I got 20.".

But this wasn't the first time Osbourne flipped her fins in a new 'do this year. In September, the former Fashion Police host debuted a jet black wig featuring blunt asymmetric bangs while walking in designer Natasha Zinko's London Fashion Week.

Osbourne explained that she's been playing around with faux hairpieces since experiencing extreme weight loss in the wake of her dad Ozzy Osbourne's 2025 death.

In a recent development, “I looked like the f--king Crypt Keeper," she admitted in a September interview with The Mail on Sunday. “I noted, 'This is scary. I didn't know I looked like this.’".

Sources indicate that "I wore a wig to cover my face, so you couldn't see how skinny my cheekbones really were," Osbourne continued, explaining how her stylists were "trying to find clothes to not make me look skinnier" as she navigated her grief. "My spine—you could see every single bone.".

However, she's since developed a healthier relationship with her body, recently sharing that she's gained back 30 lbs.

Industry observers note that “I didn’t know that I looked like that until I saw the pictures,” she told The Sun last month of her weight loss photos. “When I saw them, I couldn’t f--king believe it. I was as shocked as everyone else.”.
