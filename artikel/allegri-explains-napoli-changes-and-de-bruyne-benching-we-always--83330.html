Max Allegri explained his Napoli transformation ahead of tonight’s meeting with Torino, including the fact Kevin De Bruyne is on the bench. ‘We always look vulnerable.’.

It kicks off at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT].

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The Partenopei went into the break for international duty coming off heavy defeats to Juventus and FC Porto, then holding Serie A leaders Lazio to a 1-1 draw.

The injury crisis managed to get even worse, as Alex Meret is out for a couple of months after a fracture in his back, joining Benoit Badiashile, Alisson Santos, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo and Alessandro Buongiorno on the treatment table.

“It’s vital because we are trailing in the table, we drew the last two home games with Roma and Lazio, so we must try to get back to winning ways,” Allegri told Sky Sport Italia.

“Torino are doing very well, Ignazio Abate is in his first Serie A season as a coach, he has learned a lot already and I am happy for him. We need a lot of precision and technical quality. We’ve got Spinazzola and Favasuli back in the squad, one starts, the other is on the bench.”.

Napoli change system for this game to a 3-5-2, giving Rasmus Hojlund no rest despite featuring on international duty and with the Champions League trip to Manchester City coming up on Tuesday.

It is a further sign of how little trust the club has in Lorenzo Lucca and Noa Lang, who are expected to move on in the January transfer window, as Antonio Vergara is in a latest support role.

“Antonio is a very good player, he can find spaces between the lines, can run into the box. The problem is that we concede too many goals, we don’t give a sense of security when the opponents have the ball, we always look too vulnerable.”.

Kevin De Bruyne is rested initially, as the veteran was also given a couple of extra days off following his two Belgium starts in the Nations League against Turkiye and Italy.

“He played a lot of games, started both for international duty, tonight the substitutions will be more important than ever. De Bruyne has great technical quality, his fitness has improved a lot, but don’t forget he was out for a very long time.

“We want to see the right approach, and above all to be more practical when we don’t have the ball.”.
