The inspirational documentary short, which was the Vatican's first project submitted to the Oscars, features a young Italian man named Giacomo Mattivi who lives with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy.

“Green Lava,” an inspirational Vatican-produced documentary short in the running for the Oscars, has unveiled its fresh global trailer which features Justin Bieber’s song “Everything Hallelujah.” The doc follows a 22-year-old Italian man named Giacomo Mattivi, who lives with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy.

It has been revealed that use of Bieber’s “Everything Hallelujah” was secured through a licensing agreement with Universal Music Enterprises, a division of UMG Recordings, Inc., following an introduction facilitated by music manager Daniel Weissman, according to a statement. “The song’s themes of faith and gratitude echo Giacomo’s outlook on life and the documentary’s exploration of family, perseverance and hope,” the statement noted. Financial terms were not disclosed. Sources indicate that as previously reported by Variety, “Green Lava” marks the first time in the Academy Awards’ 97-year history that the Vatican has submitted a film for Oscar consideration. That said, the film will not specifically represent the Vatican in the race. Unlike the international feature film category, short films do not need an official submission or endorsement from a national government or a country-specific committee.

It has been revealed that the 39-minute doc, which is set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Dolomites, is directed by Lia Beltrami and Ali Aksu. “Green Lava” follows Giacomo “as he faces life without his brother Mattia who has just succumbed to Duchenne,” indicates the film’s synopsis.

“From university lectures and football matches with friends to running a small egg-selling business and spending time with family, Giacomo embraces life with remarkable determination,” according to promotional materials, which add: “Defying societal expectations, Giacomo challenges the limitations imposed by his condition and finds strength in his community and in the natural world that surrounds him.”.

“We chose ‘Everything Hallelujah’ because Justin matches the color and emotional frequency of ‘Green Lava’ so precisely,” remarked its co-directors Beltrami and Aksu in a statement to Variety.

“The film is rooted in Giacomo’s deep faith and his view of life as a blessing, and Justin carries that same spirit through his gratitude for the small details of his own life,” they continued. “Giacomo wants to inspire people to embrace love and to approach death, and our transition, with honesty. It’s the subject we avoid most in daily life, even though none of us knows when that moment will come. The key is finding balance: treating each day as a new blessing and staying grateful for everything God created,” they concluded.

Industry observers note that the latest trailer featuring Bieber’s “Everything Hallelujah” arrives after, earlier this month, former Amazon MGM Studios executive Glenn S. Gainor and industry veteran Sandy Climan joined “Green Lava” — via their film production outfit and consultancy Hollywood Ventures Group (HVG) — as executive producers, along with Nabeel Laher and Nelly Kim and John Shufeldt MD.

“Green Lava” completed its Oscar-qualifying theatrical run by screening between Sept. 18-24 at the Lumiere Music Hall in Beverly Hills.

It has been revealed that more recently, a screening for Academy members was held on Nov. 8 at The London West Hollywood in Los Angeles, attended by Paolo Ruffini, who heads the Vatican’s Dicastery for Communication and representatives from the Archdiocese of Los Angeles. The dicastery for communication controls the Vatican’s television, radio, online, publishing and newspaper operations.

Upcoming “Green Lava” screenings are planned in London and Upcoming York, with the doc also set to screen during Rome’s Tertio Millennio Film Festival.

It has been revealed that beltrami’s manufacturer Emotions to Generate Change and Untold Production produced “Green Lava” in collaboration with Vatican News, Radio Vaticana and Vatican Media. Aksu and Beltrami serve as producers, together with Los Angeles-based David Dubinsky and Dominique Appleby. Hollywood Ventures Group also serves as an executive producing partner, with Glenn S. Gainor, Sandy Climan, Nabeel Laher and Nelly Kim serving as executive producers alongside John Shufeldt, MD.
