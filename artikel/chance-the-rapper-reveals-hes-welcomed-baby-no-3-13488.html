Blessings keep falling in Chance the Rapper’s lap.

After all, the “Blessings” rapper shared that he recently welcomed a baby girl, Hayli, with a woman whose identity he did not share.

In a recent development, “Welcome to the world Hayli,” Chance (real name Chancelor Jonathan Bennett) wrote in a Nov. 15 Instagram post, alongside a photo of the baby’s mom and the baby laying in a hospital bed. “Mom n Dad love you. God is with you.”.

Chance—also dad to Kensli, 11, and Marli, 7, with ex Kirsten Corley—did not tag his partner in the post, but shared a glimpse at the couple’s adjustment together during her pregnancy, including painting the mom’s belly like a pumpkin for Halloween and building a shelf in preparation. As he wrote over the latter video, “In this b---h nesting.”.

Sources indicate that the Grammy winner’s newest addition comes two years after he and Corley separated, with them finalizing their divorce in January 2025. Last year, the “Cocoa Butter Kisses” rapper shared insight into the former couple’s adjustment to co-parenting, admitting it was the hardest part of their split.

“The reality of it is everybody deals with their family different," Chance, 33, explained during an appearance on the Upcoming Rory & Mal podcast. “The family I come from is very, very close. It’s very important for us and for her and her family for us to be tight. So we still travel together, we still family forever. It’s just a different format of it.”.

Despite his and Corley’s determination to keep their family traditions, Chance highlighted that their upcoming dynamic wasn’t without its roadblocks.

“I ain’t gonna act like it’s not tough," Chance continued. "I ain’t gonna act like it’s perfect and we shook hands and dipped. It’s weird. I think the toughness is that it’s a reality. I think that’s the toughest part.”.

Chance—who was with his ex-wife for over 10 years after meeting as kids—emphasized that he was giving himself grace throughout the fresh life chapter.

Industry observers note that "You might feel like you’re over it and you’re not over it,” he considered. “Or you might be used to a certain pattern of operating, and because it don’t work like that, you have to allow yourself grace to deal with that and operate in reality.”.

Nevertheless, Chance noted that both he and Corely had each other’s—and their kids’—best interests in mind.

“We try and always remember the importance of supporting each other, loving each other—not just for the sake of our kids, but for the sake of each other’s sanity,” he added. “It’s the rest of your life you will be connected, no matter what, emotionally, to this being.”.
