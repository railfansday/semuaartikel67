Sources indicate that leeds United secured their first win at Stamford Bridge since 1999, completing a remarkable 4-1 rout of their old rivals Chelsea. Hull also claimed three valuable points as Oli McBurnie notched a stoppage-time brace to secure a 2-0 win over Brighton & Hove Albion.

Coventry City were 3-2 victors over Crystal Palace, Newcastle United drew 2-2 with Arsenal and Tottenham Hotspur swept Ipswich Town aside.
