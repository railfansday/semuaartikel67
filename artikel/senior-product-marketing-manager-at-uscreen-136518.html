📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Uscreen has a powerful product and a clear opportunity to sharpen how we articulate its value, who it is for, and why it matters.

Sources indicate that we’re looking for a Senior Product Marketing Manager to establish and lead Product Marketing at Uscreen. You’ll bring customer insight, market intelligence, and strategic clarity to unify product strategy, roadmaps, and go-to-market execution.

Industry observers note that this is not a feature-marketing role. You’ll partner with Product, Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success to define our customers and markets, shape the product narrative, influence product decisions, and bring compelling stories to market.

You’re a senior individual contributor who can set the strategy, build the operating rhythms, and do the hands-on work. You’ll report directly to the VP of Marketing.

A great Product only matters if customers understand its value.

This role will help Uscreen get closer to our customers, tell a clearer Product story, build smarter pricing and packaging, and turn what we build into measurable business growth.
