Dolly Parton, who died this summer at 80, will be honored with a special award at CNN‘s 20th annual Heroes All-Star Tribute — and her goddaughter, Miley Cyrus, will be on hand to receive it on the family’s behalf.

“The 20th Annual CNN Heroes All-Star Tribute,” which honors everyday people who are making extraordinary contributions to their communities, will be held Saturday, Dec. 5. The special, hosted by CNN anchors Anderson Cooper and Laura Coates, culminates with the announcement of viewers’ choice for the 2026 CNN Hero of the Year. This year, the fourth annual CNN Heroes Legacy Award will continue the tradition of honoring prominent figures dedicated to a lifetime of service by recognizing Dolly Parton with a special tribute and exclusive appearance by Miley Cyrus, who will accept the award on behalf of the Parton family. Wynonna Judd and others will present the posthumous honor.

Parton’s philanthropic endeavors spanned her efforts to increase literacy, boost graduation rates, support disaster relief and health initiatives, create jobs and reduce poverty where she grew up in East Tennessee.

Parton was part of Miley Cyrus’ life since she was a baby: Cyrus’ father, Billy Ray Cyrus, toured with Parton in the 1990s and asked the “Jolene” singer if she would be his daughter’s godmother before she was even born. On the TV show “Hannah Montana,” Parton appeared as Miley’s Aunt Dolly.

“The feeling of losing my Aunt Dolly is beyond what feels right to share,” Miley Cyrus wrote in a heartfelt Instagram post after Parton’s death. “Our most sacred moments were private and they’ll stay there behind the stage, beneath the glamour & between our hearts. What I KNOW is Dolly would want me to feel it, write a song about it, and then continue on and be strong. I have an angel by my side. I always have. One of our last conversations was about music and metamorphosis.”.

Other notable guests joining the 2026 CNN Heroes special on Dec. 5 include Jon Bon Jovi, Kelly Ripa and Mark Consuelos, and actor Edward Norton.

Over its 20-year history, the CNN Heroes franchise focused on “uplifting, purpose-driven storytelling” recognizing regular people, while “bringing together some of the biggest names in entertainment, culture and philanthropy,” per the network, which is now part of David Ellison’s Skydance following the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros.
