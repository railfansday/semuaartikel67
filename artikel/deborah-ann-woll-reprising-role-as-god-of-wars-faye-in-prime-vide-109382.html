She will star in the show alongside Dave Bautista as Kratos. Ólafur Darri Ólafsson (Severance) will play Thor in the God of War TV show, taking over for Hurst, who played Thor in the latest God of War games. The God of War cast also includes Teresa Palmer as Sif, Max Parker as Heimdall, Mandy Patinkin as Odin, and Alastair Duncan as Memir, reprising his role from God of War Ragnarok. Callum Vinson plays Atreus. The God of War TV show will follow some of the events of 2018’s God of War and 2022’s God of War Ragnarok, telling the father-son story of Kratos and Atreus. As for the game series, God of War Laufey was recently delayed to May 2027. A newly introduced Kratos-led game is coming after that, and it will pick up where Laufey left off.
