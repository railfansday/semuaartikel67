23-year-old explains main reason behind ’emotional’ West Ham summer exit

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

West Ham sold the midfielder to the Belgian side in the last transfer window. WHUFC gave their academy graduate the green light to head out the exit door on a permanent basis.

Potts, 23, penned a long-term contract with Club Brugge running until June 2030. He has made 16 appearances in all competitions since his move abroad, four of which have come in the Champions League, and he has chipped in with one assist.

In a recent development, he has opened up about his exit from the London Stadium on BBC Radio 5 Live’s Euro Leagues Show: “In pre-season my minutes with West Ham were limited, they weren’t as much as I wanted and then I spoke with my agent and remarked ‘let’s explore options because this season I’m 22, turning 23’. It’s a massive part of my career and I wanted to play. The opportunity of Club Brugge came along with Champions League football, the best club in Belgium and it was an opportunity I couldn’t really turn down with how my minutes were going down with West Ham.

“I grew up in the area, going to the stadium watching the games. It was always going to be a tough decision but it was something that I had to do for my career. It was an emotional decision because it had to be made quite swiftly. It’s been the right decision for me and so far I’m enjoying it, I’m loving life here.”.

According to recent reports, west Ham are well stocked up with options in Potts’ position. WHUFC have the likes of Arne Engels, Divine Mukasa, Edson Alvarez and Tomas Soucek, among others, at their disposal.

In a recent development, therefore, Potts wouldn’t have played many minutes for the Hammers this season. His decision to leave for Club Brugge has worked out well for him so far.

Industry observers note that the Barking-born man has become a key player for his current club. He is also getting exposure to European football which will do him good.

Sources indicate that potts rose up through West Ham’s ranks and was a regular for them at various different youth levels as a youngster. He was loaned out to Wycombe Wanderers and Portsmouth in the EFL to get some experience under his belt and to boost his development.

It has been revealed that he made his first-team debut for the Hammers back in 2021 in a Europa League clash against Dinamo Zagreb. He went on to make a further 32 outings for the London outfit, 25 of which came in the last campaign.

Potts has settled into life at Club Brugge with relative ease. His side take on Liverpool at home in the Champions League on Wednesday night and they are 2nd in their league table behind Royale Union Saint-Gilloise.

It has been revealed that west Ham will be disappointed to have seen him depart but they couldn’t offer him consistent minutes anymore due to too much competition for places. The Hammers have seen their form in the Championship slump over recent weeks.

In a recent development, they have recently turned to Scott Parker as their new manager but the ex-Fulham and Burnley man has struggled to make an impact so far. His side drew 1-1 at home against Preston North End last time out and couldn’t find a winner.
