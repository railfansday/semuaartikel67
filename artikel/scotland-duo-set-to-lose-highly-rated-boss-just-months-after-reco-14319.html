A trio of former Scottish Premiership stars are set to lose the manager who took them to the EFL Championship as part of the club’s massive summer revamp.

Scotland internationals Stephen Welsh and Ross Stewart both made Bosman free transfer moves to Swansea City in the summer from Celtic and Southampton respectively, while New Zealand forward Elijah Just joined them for a club-record fee of £6million from Motherwell following a stunning debut campaign at Fir Park.

However, the trio are set to see highly rated head coach Vitor Matos complete a shock exit from the club just three months after they arrived in South Wales, with the Portuguese boss remarked to be edging closer to a move that would see him appointed by Ligue 1 outfit Strasbourg, who are expected to sack current manager Hugo Oliveira, after a run of five defeats in six matches.

Transformed under Matos since his arrival last November, the 38-year-old has enjoyed a strong start to the newly introduced campaign and has the second-tier side just three points from the automatic promotion spots in the English second tier, with eight wins in the opening 15 league matches.

It has been revealed that the move will come as a major blow for former Celtic and Motherwell centre-back Welsh. A regular starter under Matos since his summer arrival, he credited the former Marítimo boss as a key reason behind his maiden call-up to the Scotland senior squad in September.

A revelation on loan to Motherwell last season under Jens Berthel Askou, the 26-year-old was prized away by Swansea in the summer when his contract at Celtic expired in June. Detailing the reasons for moving across the border, Welsh specifically credited the impact of Matos on his decision.

According to recent reports, “They made it clear that they really wanted me,” remarked Welsh upon his arrival. “With the way the head coach spoke about the club, the environment he wants to create and the way he wants to play; it was a no-brainer. The Championship is a brilliant division, and one I am excited to be playing my football in and challenging myself in. I have experience but I am also young enough to be ready for a new opportunity and a new chapter in my career.”.

Meanwhile, former Ross County forward Stewart has found the net once in five games for Swansea, while Welsh’s former Motherwell teammate Just has started eight league games for his newly introduced side, assisting one in the 3-1 win over Burnley in September.

According to recent reports, despite his side’s strong start to the EFL Championship campaign, though, Matos looks destined to leave for France, with a switch to the western bank of the Rhine River, with several reports claiming a deal has been agreed for him to become the new Strasbourg head coach. He is expected to sign a deal until the summer of 2028, with a club option for an additional year through 2029.

The expected appointment will see him become Les Bleus' third manager in under a year after Gary O’Neil left to join Ipswich Town just four months after his appointment at Stade de la Meinau. His replacement, the aforementioned Oliveira, has been in charge for just 10 games.
