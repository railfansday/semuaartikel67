The Capital One Venture Card is currently offering a limited time, lucrative, two-part welcome offer, whereby you can earn 75,000 Capital One bonus miles, plus a $300 Capital One Travel stays credit, after spending $4,000 within the first three months.

In a recent development, for context, Capital One miles can be redeemed for a minimum of one cent each, meaning the 75,000 miles will get you at least $750 in value. However, I value the miles at 1.7 cents each based on my latest analysis (see my methodology here), thanks to the value you can get by transferring the miles to travel partners. That means that to me, the 75,000 miles are worth $1,275.

It has been revealed that there are lots of great ways to redeem Capital One miles, as they can be transferred to Capital One’s roughly 20 airline and hotel partners. That’s my preferred use of them, as you can get some amazing travel experiences this way.

Recent findings suggest that partners include programs like Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, Qatar Airways Privilege Club, Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club, and more. That doesn’t include the value of the $300 Capital One Travel credit, which posts upon account approval (rather than upon completing minimum spending). The idea is that this can be used for a hotel or vacation rental booking through Capital One Travel in your first cardmember year (in the first 12 months), and it’s valid across one or multiple stays. In other words, you could use it to pay virtually nothing for a $300 hotel stay. All-in-all, I’d say the Capital One Venture bonus is worth up to $1,575.

According to recent reports, by my valuation, the current Capital One Venture Card bonus is the best publicly available offer that we’ve seen in over five years, so that makes this the ideal time to apply, and I absolutely wouldn’t wait for a better offer. Quite to the contrary, when this limited time offer gets pulled, it’ll almost certainly be worse.

When you look at the bonus terms on the Capital One Venture Card, you’ll find the following language:.

You are not eligible for this product if you have received a new cardmember bonus for the Capital One Venture card or the Capital One Venture X card in the past 48 months.

As you can see, the card follows a 48-month rule, so you’re not eligible for the welcome bonus on this card if you’ve received a new cardmember bonus on this exact card or on the Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card (learn more) in the past 48 months.

Other than that, eligibility is unrelated to having any other credit card, and Capital One doesn’t have many consistent restrictions when it comes to getting approved for cards.
