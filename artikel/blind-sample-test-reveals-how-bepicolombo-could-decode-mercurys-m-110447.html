Blind sample test reveals how BepiColombo could decode Mercury's mineral makeup

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

An international team of researchers, including scientists from Planetary Science Institute (PSI), has demonstrated that instruments aboard the BepiColombo spacecraft can operate together to identify the minerals that make up Mercury's surface, addressing a longstanding challenge in planetary science. The findings, published in Planetary and Space Science, come as the joint European Space Agency (ESA) and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) mission entered orbit around Mercury this week.
