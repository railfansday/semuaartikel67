Court documents released Monday night highlighted U.S. Department of Justice prosecutors battling with U.S. Magistrate Judge Matthew J. Sharbaugh to obtain a search warrant for the Washington, D.C., home of Rep. Ilhan Omar, D-Minn.

The DOJ applied to the magistrate for a search warrant on Oct. 2, eventually receiving the warrant which led to a Homeland Security Investigations search of the home on Oct. 12.

However, the newly released petition for the warrant reveals the court raised concerns that the DOJ could be violating the Constitution’s Speech and Debate Clause.
