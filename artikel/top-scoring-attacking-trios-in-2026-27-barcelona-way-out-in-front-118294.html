📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bayern Munich had the best front three in Europe last season, but there’s already a different runaway leader this time around.

We’ve taken a closer look at the highest-scoring trios across the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 this season. It doesn’t matter whether the three players were on the pitch at the same time or not, just how many goals each team’s three best scorers have got.

It’s very much the early days of this ranking, so expect plenty of change as the season wears on.

But for now, here are the top 10 attacking trios in Europe’s top five leagues this season.

Recent findings suggest that barcelona have banished any fears they’d have a void up front after the Pole’s exit this summer, with Raphinha reborn as a centre-forward. And a pretty good one at that, too.

Lamine Yamal is in his typically outrageous form as well, and so Barca are way out in front with their best three-way combination for goals this season.

We imagine eight-goal Fermin Lopez might not be part of the trio for much longer, since he isn’t really playing as an attacker anyway. Anthony Gordon, your time will come.

Jose Mourinho’s second Real Madrid reign wasn’t the most convincing to start with, but it’s helped that some of his star attackers are still doing the business.

That’s especially the case with Kylian Mbappe, who’s scored 13 goals in La Liga so far – including a brace in El Clasico.

Jude Bellingham has chipped in with a few as well and summer signing Yan Diomande has netted three times.

You’d have to go back to the 1950s to find the last time a Serie A side scored as many goals after 12 matches as Roma have this season.

More than half of those have come from Donyell Malen, Rodrigo Mora and Matias Soule, who have become their best option for a front three while Paulo Dybala has been injured.

It’s Malen doing most of the heavy lifting with 12 goals in the league so far, including four in his most recent game alone.

But Mora, who could become Roma’s record signing once his full fee is activated, is making an impression too. He’s scored in four league games in a row.

Esteban Lepaul is the star here, with seven goals already after netting 20 last season. No wonder he recently made his international debut for France.

Boulaye Dia and Sebastien Szymanski have chipped in with six and four each respectively.

Sources indicate that four games without a win for City suggest all those off-field distractions might be starting to take their toll. But Haaland’s still Haalanding.

The massive striker has gone nine from 11 so far in the Premier League and Rayan Cherki has already scored five, one more than he did last season.

No one quite knows what these goals are going towards, but City’s frontline has still been lethal.

Highlights have included a 3-0 win at Fiorentina, beating Cesc Fabregas’ Como and knocking four past Sassuolo and five past Lecce.

Antonio Raimondo leads the way with eight, with midfielder Giacomo Calo adding five and winger Giorgi Kvernadze contributing three. Consider them firmly on our radar.

It has been revealed that raimondo will be out for a few months though with an injury, just as he was starting to flourish. Football can be cruel.

Milan will be hoping major summer signing Goncalo Ramos can start repaying their faith, but for now, their main scorers have been Adrien Rabiot, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic.

Cisse has been something of a breakout star in his debut Serie A season.

As the three main strikers vying for two places in Inter’s attack, this trio don’t play together all the time, but they’re all bringing the goals.

Pio Esposito looks the real deal, with as many goals to his name as Marcus Thuram and two more than captain Lautaro Martinez.

Nusa has grabbed the headlines, Chelsea flop Nkunku might grab your attention and Romulo will tickle that part of your brain that fires up Wikipedia at the drop of the hat.

Leipzig are ticking over nicely in the Bundesliga, sitting fourth with all three of the aforementioned players chipping in with goals.

Monza have one of the breakout stars of the Serie A season in Gustavo Varela, who’s right up there as one of the leading marksmen.

And keep an eye out for English talent Jay Robinson, who’s added a few goals himself while on loan from Southampton.
