📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is estimated to have sold nearly 25 million copies in one day, generating approximately $2.5bn in sales revenue - a staggering result that's beyond even what even observers were predicting.

The estimates come from data-tracking organization Sensor Tower (spotted by VGC), which has been watching GTA 6 pre-orders closely for weeks, updating estimates daily based on (unspecified) data that it collects.

Should the results be accurate, it'll dramatically eclipse GTA 6's $1bn in three days result. It'll also put GTA 6 on track to reach the eye-watering $3.25bn to $5.2bn week-one sales estimates for GTA 6.

So far, Rockstar has noted nothing in relation to GTA 6's sales since the game was released around the world yesterday. Parent company Take-Two has said the game's pre-orders were tracking "well above expectations, and GTA 6 broke pre-order records on both the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store, though, so all signs point to a record-breaking release. The question is "by how much?".

Reaction to GTA 6 has been glittering. Chris awarded GTA 6 five golden stars in Eurogamer's review, calling it "a game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale". "Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that.".
