GTA 6's strong and silent Jason was inspired by Steve McQueen and former Army Rangers, states Rockstar

If you've noticed that Grand Theft Auto 6's Jason Duval isn't much of a talker, that's by design, as his quiet nature was inspired by Steve McQueen and the studio's research into former Army Rangers.

Speaking to Destructoid over email, Rockstar's senior vice president of narrative, Rupert Humphries, explained that the team looked at classic couple-on-the-run films when it decided on GTA 6's story.

"When we decided we wanted to tell a story about a couple on the run, we looked at all the similar kinds of movies, and one of the ones that really stood out was Peckinpah's The Getaway," Humphries mentioned. "Steve McQueen was famous for pulling lines out of scripts and saying things with his presence alone. We wanted to see how close we could get to that with a game character, where so many of our protagonists have been the opposite.".

Industry observers note that that approach also fitted the studio's research into veterans. "This also resonated with the research we were doing around veterans and particularly former Army Rangers (in our world we call them Marauders)," Humphries continued. "These guys typically aren't running their mouths or reacting to something unless it's strictly necessary, while still being strong and still asserting themselves.".

Game writer Michael Wiafe mentioned Florida's real-life crime stories were "an obvious starting point" for GTA 6's characters more broadly.

"From then on, it was about coming up with authentic characters that didn't stray too far from reality but also were not totally bound by it either," Wiafe noted. "The majority of our main characters are undeniably reprehensible on paper, full-blown criminals, but also decent human beings. Their moral makeup isn't necessarily cut-and-dry, and we don't attempt to make excuses for them.".

It has been revealed that fellow writer Elizabeth Gauvey-Kern added that while "the story takes some pretty heavy turns", Jason and Lucia's goal remains consistent. "So sharing their sense of determination, the focus on their goal, through all the highs and lows - that's what I hope people feel more than anything. You really want them to make it.".
