MIAMI, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Gen. Gary M. Brito, U.S. Army (Ret.), the 18th and final commanding general of U.S. Army Training and Doctrine Command (TRADOC), has joined Qtonic Quantum to lead the Army Readiness Council and serve as Senior Military Advisor to the Founders. His appointment comes 39 days before NSA's CNSA 2.0 compliance date for fresh national security system acquisitions.

Sources indicate that under CNSSP 15 and NSA's CNSA 2.0 guidance, all upcoming national security system acquisitions are required to be CNSA 2.0 compliant, meaning quantum-resistant, by Jan. 1, 2027. NIST finalized its first post-quantum cryptography standards, FIPS 203, 204 and 205, in August 2024. Adversaries can harvest encrypted data today and decrypt it once quantum computers mature.

Sources indicate that brito retired as a four-star general after a 38-year Army career. At TRADOC he led the command responsible for the Army's institutional training, education and leader development. He also led the strategy, planning and early integration of TRADOC's merger with Army Futures Command, which culminated in the activation of the new U.S. Army Transformation and Training Command in October 2025. He previously commanded the Maneuver Center of Excellence and Fort Benning and the Joint Readiness Training Center and Fort Polk, served as the Army's 49th Deputy Chief of Staff, G-1, the Army's senior human resources officer, and deployed to Iraq and Afghanistan.

As leader of the Army Readiness Council, Brito will drive the council's work on how post-quantum migration is planned and measured as a readiness problem across Army and joint environments. He will also advise the founders on how Qtonic Quantum serves defense and government leaders. He serves in a personal capacity, and his views do not represent the Department of the Army or the Department of War. He joins a team that includes senior retired military leaders.

It has been revealed that "Readiness is built, not declared," remarked Lt. Gen. (Ret.) Roger L. Cloutier Jr., former commander of NATO Allied Land Command and Founding Chair of the Allied Defense Council at Qtonic Quantum. "General Brito spent 38 years building it at the scale of an institution. Post-quantum migration is a readiness problem, and he will help us put evidence in front of the leaders who set the schedule.".

In a recent development, "Leaders at all levels are charged to ensure unit readiness against all threats," said Brito. "Communications and data readiness is no different. Leaders need to inventory what they depend on, test it and fix it on a schedule they set, not one an adversary sets.".
