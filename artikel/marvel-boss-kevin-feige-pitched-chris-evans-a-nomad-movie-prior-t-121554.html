Chris Evans notes Marvel bosses Kevin Feige and Lou D’Esposito pitched him a Nomad movie prior to Avengers: Doomsday.

It has been revealed that "They stated, ‘We’d love to continue making movies with you. Steve would no longer be Captain America; he would be Nomad," Evans told Entertainment Weekly. "They sent me a pitch and it was a great pitch. It really was. But for whatever reason at the time, I was very happy with how things wrapped up. It didn’t feel like it was the thing I was looking to do at the time.".

Steve Rogers took on the persona of Nomad in Captain America #180, having given up the Cap mantle after becoming disillusioned with the United States government. Taking the name Nomad because felt like a "man without a country," Steve still defended the innocent from the evils of the world at large... but as more of a vigilante. Think Hawkeye becoming a mask-wearing, street-level vigilante known the Ronin after the Blip. Steve would become Cap again in Captain America #184, however.

According to recent reports, how is Steve Rogers even in Avengers: Doomsday, you might ask? Well, all we really know is that it definitely has to do with Loki. In one of the Avengers: Endgame Encore post-credits scenes, he appears at Steve and Peggy's door and tells them he's there to help.

Recent findings suggest that avengers: Doomsday arrives in US theaters on December 18 and a few days earlier in the UK on December 16. For more on the MCU, check out our guide to the Marvel timeline and how to watch the Marvel movies in order.
