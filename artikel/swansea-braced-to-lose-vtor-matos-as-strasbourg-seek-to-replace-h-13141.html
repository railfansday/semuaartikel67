According to recent reports, swansea City are increasingly resigned to their head coach, Vítor Matos, joining Strasbourg after he indicated a desire to join Chelsea’s sister club. The Ligue 1 club have approached Swansea with a view to Matos succeeding Hugo Oliveira.

Strasbourg, acquired by Chelsea’s parent company BlueCo in 2023, have identified Matos as a replacement for Oliveira, who has won three of 10 league matches since succeeding Gary O’Neil in July. The French club are hovering two points above the relegation zone.

Matos is under contract until 2030 and Swansea would be due significant compensation for the 38-year-old, a former Liverpool academy and development team coach.

Matos has won admirers since taking charge of Swansea last November and, despite one win in their past five games, has the south Wales club in the Championship playoff spots. Swansea finished last season in 11th and backed Matos in the transfer market this summer in the hope of pushing on.

Swansea’s next match is at home to West Brom on Saturday, before a trip to Middlesbrough and a south Wales derby against Cardiff in the Welsh capital.

In a recent development, matos, who is due to celebrate a year in charge at Swansea this month, joined the club from Marítimo, a Portuguese second-division club. His 12-game spell in charge of Maritimo represented his first senior management job.

Strasbourg and Chelsea share a close relationship and have traded players, with Emmanuel Emegha and Valentín Barco joining the English club in recent months.

In the most notable move between the clubs, however, Liam Rosenior left his role as Strasbourg head coach to take charge of Chelsea in January, but the former Hull manager was sacked in April. O’Neil succeeded Rosenior but joined Ipswich in June.
