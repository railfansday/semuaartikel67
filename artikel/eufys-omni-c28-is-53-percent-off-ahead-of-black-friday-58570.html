Hosting Thanksgiving can be fun, but cleaning up afterwards? Not so much. Eufy’s Omni C28 robot vacuum / mop hybrid can help, and thanks to an early Black Friday deal, you can pick it up at a fresh low price of $379.99 ($420 off its original price) at Amazon, Best Buy, or directly from Eufy (with code WS24C28200NEW1). While this model has dropped to around $499.99 a few times before, it commonly sells for a lot more, making now a great time to consider buying one.

It has been revealed that the Verge’s smart home reviewer Jennifer Pattison Tuohy named the Eufy Omni C28 one of her top picks after testing 16 robot vacuum mops. She thinks it’s a particularly great fit for apartments and smaller homes. It offers features typically found in pricier flagship models, including a multifunction dock that automatically washes the mop roller with hot water and dries it with hot air, yet it doesn’t take up too much space. The robot itself is also relatively narrow and maneuvers easily around furniture and into tight spaces. It boasts excellent navigation, and had no trouble climbing over the legs of Jen’s lounger, which have tripped up many of the more expensive models she’s tested.

The Omni C28 also does a great job cleaning with its mops, particularly on hardwood floors. Surprisingly, it’s powerful enough to lift up dried oatmeal. Unlike most other robot vacuums Jen’s tested, this one lacks a camera, making it a good option if you’re concerned about privacy. Unfortunately, the trade-off is mediocre obstacle detection, so you’ll want to pick up socks and cables before running it.
