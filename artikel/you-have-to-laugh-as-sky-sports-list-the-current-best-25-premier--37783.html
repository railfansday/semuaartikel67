Sky Sports have now published a list of the 25 current best Premier League players.

This list published as the November 2026 international break comes to an end and this weekend brings the 11th round of EPL matches.

So who would be in your list of the very best 25 Premier League players?

Would your list include 22 players from only four clubs?

Would your top 21 include only one token player who isn’t from a ‘Premier League Major Six’ club?

Sky Sports explaining how they produced their list of the top current 25 Premier League players: ‘We asked our reporters and journalists to rank a longlist of 40 of the best players in the competition right now and took an average of their answers to produce the Sky Sports top 25.’.

This is that Sky Sports list of the top 25 current Premier League players, in reverse order counting down: 25. Alisson 24. Bruno Guimaraes 23. Riccardo Calafiori 22. Morgan Gibbs-White 21. Jeremy Doku 20. Antoine Semenyo 19. Ezri Konsa 18. Dominik Szoboszlai 17. Pascal Gross 16. Moises Caicedo 15. Virgil van Dijk 14. Bukayo Saka 13. Cole Palmer 12. Alexander Isak 11. Bruno Fernandes 10. Martin Odegaard 9. Joao Pedro 8. Elliot Anderson 7.Rayan Cherki 6. William Saliba 5. Morgan Rogers 4. Gabriel Magalhaes 3. David Raya 2. Declan Rice 1. Erling Haaland.

So the best current 25 Premier League players is made up of nine from Arsenal, five Man City, four each from Liverpool and Chelsea, plus Bruno Fernandes?

Oh yes, plus the token couple from Forest and Brighton.

Brighton have been by far the best team I have watched this season, they are second in the table, yet only one of their players makes this list???

Not a single one from Aston Villa, not a single one from Bournemouth…both of those teams have got some exceptional players.

No Lewis Hall either, who has been exceptional with Newcastle United (and England!) as usual???

Recent findings suggest that arsenal do clearly have the most effective team, even though they are all but unwatchable at times as they bore and grind the opposition down. However, to claim that nine of the Arsenal squad (some of them aren’t even first choice this season for the Gunners…) and another 13 from only three other clubs deserve to be in this current top 25 list of Premier League players, ahead of all those (with the exception of Gibbs-White, Gross and Bruno the (other) rat) at every other club….
