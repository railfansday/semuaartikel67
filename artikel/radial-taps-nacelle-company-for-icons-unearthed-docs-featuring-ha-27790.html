EXCLUSIVE: Radial Entertainment has tapped with The Nacelle Organization for Icons Unearthed pop-culture docs about Harrison Ford, Arnold Schwarzenegger, Quentin Tarantino and Transformers.

The deal comprises Icons Unearthed: Transformers, Icons Unearthed: Harrison Ford Movies, Icons Unearthed: Arnold Schwarzenegger Movies and Icons Unearthed: Quentin Tarantino Movies – all of which be branded as Radial Originals.

It has been revealed that each title comprises six hour-long episodes, making a total of 24. Rather than launching as a series, each title will release as a standalone offering. Recent findings suggest that the Nacelle Firm has been making and selling Icons Unearthed docs since 2022, with previous seasons of the Brian Volk-Weiss-created show playing on the likes of Vice TV and Amazon Freevee. They have covered topics such as Star Wars, the Marvel Universe and James Bond. The reveals included in the Radial deal were made for latter seasons of the franchise. Watch on Deadline.

Icons Unearthed: Transformers will races the famous franchise from Hasbro’s search for a hit toy line and its collaboration with Marvel Comics through to the animated stories, Michael Bay’s blockbuster films and the subsequent big-screen reinvention. Glenn Morshower, who played two separate characters in the Transformers movies, will contribute.

It has been revealed that icons Unearthed: Harrison Ford Movies follows the actor’s path from carpenter to leading man, exploring his work with George Lucas and Blade Runner and the evolution of his action-star persona through films including Patriot Games, Clear and Present Danger and Air Force One. Alison Doody, who appeared with Ford in Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade, features.

Icons Unearthed: Arnold Schwarzenegger Movies charts the star’s journey from championship bodybuilding to Hollywood, looking at his breakthrough roles, action-film collaborations and move into comedy with Twins and True Lies. It will feature Jesse Ventura, who starred with Schwarzenegger in Predator and The Running Man and similarly went into politics.

Icons Unearthed: Quentin Tarantino Movies explores the filmmaker’s rise from video-store clerk to Oscar-winning Hollywood auteur, along with how his passions for kung-fu and Westerns have influenced his work. The career-spanning series covers Reservoir Dogs, Pulp Fiction, True Romance, Natural Born Killers, From Dusk Till Dawn, Kill Bill, Inglourious Basterds and Django Unchained, and features Bronson Pinchot, who acted in True Romance.

“Our first pop-culture factual series is about going behind the screen to explore how iconic films and franchises took shape,” said David Buoymaster, Chief Investment Officer at Radial, which has a deep catalog of shows of films and shows launched across various platforms.

“Nacelle is the perfect partner to bring that ambition to life through four distinct Icons Unearthed titles, each with the space to tell its own story. From Transformers to the films of Ford, Schwarzenegger and Tarantino, we’re excited for our platform partners and pop-culture fans to discover the creative choices, collaborations and business decisions behind these screen legacies.”.

Industry observers note that “We couldn’t be more thrilled to partner with Radial Entertainment to bring these upcoming seasons of Icons Unearthed to the audience,” added Nacelle CEO Brian Volk-Weiss. “These demonstrates are packed with the kind of brand-new, behind-the-scenes stories and nostalgia fans love, and Radial is the perfect partner to help them reach even more viewers.”.

The deal was negotiated by Katie Carroll, Director, Originals & Business Development at Radial Entertainment, and Hud Woodle, VP International Sales at The Nacelle Manufacturer.
