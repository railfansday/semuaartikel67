AC Milan’s shirt sponsor Emirates are set to make a change ahead of the 2027-28 season, according to a report.

As per Footy Headlines, the ‘Fly Better’ text will be removed from the shirts of Arsenal, Real Madrid and Milan ahead of next season. Instead, the airline will simply display the word ‘Emirates’ across the middle of the clubs that they sponsor.

In a recent development, as mentioned, it will not take effect immediately and will be something that is adopted from next season. The ‘Fly Better’ tagline started being seen in 2020 as a upcoming marketing slogan that replaced the iconic ‘Fly Emirates’.

The idea is to create a more ‘streamlined’ look on the kits, and that is also why we have been seeing leaked designs for next season that feature just the singular word.

Of course, this is not the only alteration from a kit perspective that Milan are gearing up for. In case you missed the significant news of the past few weeks, Adidas are set to take over from PUMA ahead of 2028-29, for what will be a fourth stint as the manufacturer of the Rossoneri’s shirts.

Sources indicate that it is believed to be a record-breaking deal at least in Serie A, one that will net the club around €500m over the duration of the agreement. For many, it is a welcome return to a brand that became synonymous with Milan given the length of their last stint as the kit maker.
