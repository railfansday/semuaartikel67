The chance to shake Mattia's hand in GTA 6 comes up right at the start of the mission Day Release, seemingly like a key decision that might affect the tone of the rest of the mission and future job prospects. Multiple GamesRadar+ writers have gone through both options, and it's a lot less notable of a decision in GTA 6 than you think, so here's what we know about the significance of shaking Mattia's hand.

Be aware of some very mild spoilers for later missions in Chapter 1 below.

Sources indicate that shaking Mattia's hand in GTA 6 makes no difference to any events in the Day Release mission. If you shake his hand, you shake his hand, and if you don't, you don't. Mattia doesn't even acknowledge that you left him hanging.

In a recent development, later in the mission, Mattia offers Jason and Lucia some drugs in a nightclub and, again, accepting or refusing has no clear repercussions. Comparing videos of the choices side-by-side, there were no obvious differences between either outcome as the same thing happens regardless - Mattia either takes the drugs back or hands them over, stands up and leaves, then Jason and Lucia take to the dancefloor. The rest of the mission then plays out as normal.

Recent findings suggest that as far as I can tell, turning Mattia down in either of these situations in Day Release has no long-term impact either as the "job" that he mentions at the end of the mission is critical to the story and can't be avoided. Just make sure you've done everything before the GTA 6 Tourist Trap mission as it's an crucial one – and if you get stuck, my GTA 6 Tourist Trap walkthrough can help.
