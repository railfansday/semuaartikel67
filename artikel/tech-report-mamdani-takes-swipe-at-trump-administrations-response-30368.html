Fresh York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani took a swipe at the Trump administration Tuesday following days of tech outages sweeping the nation, saying that "the response that we heard from federal officials was far from what many wanted to hear.".

Mamdani spoke after the U.S. experienced a slew of technology disruptions that halted flights, email services and online banking platforms across the country.

Industry observers note that "What I will say to New Yorkers is both an understanding of their frustration, we are speaking about New Yorkers who had made aims for months, some of them years, about going on a trip with their family or coming home from a trip with their family. And frankly, the response that we heard from federal officials was far from what many wanted to hear," Mamdani remarked.

"And it did not leave many New Yorkers with any greater sense of confidence about our national capabilities in case of this occurring again," he continued. "And I'm looking forward to seeing Democratic majorities in the House and the Senate utilize that power to also ensure that we're delivering more answers at a national level, because what we were hearing from some of the secretaries that day was in no way confidence building.".

"And we can be fairly confident in that because we know those things exist and we have been looking for them in the past," Clayton continued.

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said two routers from Federal Aviation Administration facilities in Texas and Arkansas are undergoing extensive testing as investigators examine whether faulty equipment or an installation problem triggered the nation's longest nationwide ground stop in 25 years this past Friday.

The shutdown occurred as contractor L3Harris was replacing aging copper wiring with fiber-optic technology in the FAA's communications system.

Duffy then stated during an appearance on Fox News' "Hannity" Monday that he intends to modernize America’s aging air traffic control system and build newly introduced architecture that is "worthy of America." The secretary also said intelligence agencies found no evidence of a cyberattack.
