Banchou Tactics and Neko Odyssey developer Secret Character has announced Mello & Omega, an incremental mining and blacksmithing game with shop automation. It will launch for PC via Steam in spring 2027.

Here is an overview of the game, via Secret Character:.

Recent findings suggest that mello & Omega is an incremental game starring Mello, a young blacksmith, and Omega, her robot best friend. Take Omega into the mines, bring ore back to Mello’s forge, and sell weapons to adventurers.
