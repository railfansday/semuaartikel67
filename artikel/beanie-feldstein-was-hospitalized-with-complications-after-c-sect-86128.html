Beanie Feldstein faced unexpected postpartum complications after welcoming her first baby with wife Bonnie-Chance Roberts, making her glamorous attendance at the Focker-In-Law world premiere even more special.

According to recent reports, “The fockers go to England!! the land of MY in-laws!!” Feldstein, 33, wrote Instagram on Friday, November 20, sharing photos from the Fockers-In-Laws premiere across the pond earlier in the week. “No but for real, I had [a] c-section four months ago and could barely walk, so this is all deeply surreal!!!”.

Feldstein, who plays Ben Stiller and Teri Polo’s adult daughter in the latest installment of the Focker franchise, gave birth in July.

It has been revealed that “OK but to be real for a second, I had a C-section four months ago and ended up rehospitalized with complications,” Feldstein noted via her Instagram Stories on Friday. “[I] literally could barely move for weeks, so to get to this point is truly surreal in every sense of the word.”.

Sources indicate that she continued, “I’m so in love with my Focker family and so glad I could be there to celebrate our movie!!! And all the postpartum people are superheroes.”.

Feldstein and Roberts, 35, announced in May that they were expecting their first baby, a daughter.

“Limited Edition Scouse Beanie Baby coming soon!! 🎀🍼,” a joint Instagram post read.

The fresh parents have not publicly revealed their child’s name, though their daughter is already a dream come true for the actress.

“I really always wanted to be a mom, like, from the moment I was kid and I understood what that meant,” Feldstein gushed on Today With Jenna and Sheinelle earlier this month. “I always wanted to be a mom, but I look at her and I’m like, ‘You’re beyond anything I could have dreamt of.’ She’s just heaven.”.

The Booksmart actress further offered a glimpse of her brother Jonah Hill’s duties as an uncle.

It has been revealed that “He’s the best. He’s Uncle Jonah; he’s so good with kids,” Feldstein gushed of her sibling, 42. “He has a big beard now, so he tickles with the beard. [It’s] so cute.”.

Feldstein and Roberts have been together since 2018, going on to say “I do” in 2023 in a camp-themed wedding in upstate New York.

“It is our happy place together,” Feldstein told Vogue of their nuptials. “I grew up going to summer camp for 10 years, and my parents and both sets of my grandparents met at summer camp, so camp is a lineage of love through the generations of my family. Even though we met in London and fell in love on a film set, to get married at a camp was a truly beautiful emotional homecoming.”.
