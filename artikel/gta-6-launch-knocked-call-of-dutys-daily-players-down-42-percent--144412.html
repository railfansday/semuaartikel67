GTA 6 launch knocked Call of Duty's daily players down 42 percent, Alinea estimates

Grand Theft Auto 6's launch took a big bite out of Call of Duty’s player base, according to new estimates from Alinea Analytics, and four days on, it still hasn't recovered.

Writing in Alinea's newsletter, Rhys Elliott said Call of Duty HQ's daily active users (DAUs) across PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam fell 42 percent when GTA 6 rolled out on 19th November, dropping from an average of 7.5 million to 4.4 million. On PlayStation, which Elliott describes as "CoD's main platform", the drop was 44 percent.

According to recent reports, "Sadly, CoD has barely bounced back after four days," Elliott wrote. "It's still down 41% from its baseline.".

Fortnite and FC 27 took a hit too, but both are already recovering. Fortnite's DAUs on PlayStation and Xbox fell 28 percent, from 17.8 million to 12.8 million, but by the end of the weekend it had clawed its way back to 19 percent below its baseline.

Epic is also holding a live event to close out the current season on 5th December, which Elliott says the studio "hopes will pull players back after they’ve polished off GTA 6’s story".

FC 27's DAUs dropped by around 27 percent, from 5.9 million to 4.3 million, and it's already back within 20 percent of its baseline.

According to Alinea, of those who'd played GTA 6 by the end of its launch weekend, 29 percent had also played Fortnite this month, 16 percent had played Call of Duty HQ, and 11 percent had played FC 27.

Elliott noted the pattern is largely what Alinea had predicted: the big live service games took an instant hit from GTA, but bounced back "because they're ingrained habits". Call of Duty is "the outlier here", he added, "and Xbox should - candidly - be worried about one of its premier franchises".

According to recent reports, elliott also points out that all of this is the impact of GTA 6's single-player mode. He expects its online component and PC port to arrive "some time next year", and when they do, he states, "the bounce back will not be so obtainable".

Alinea also estimates that GTA 6 has sold 44.9 million copies and made more than $4 billion since launch.

GTA 6 released on Thursday, 19th November, for PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. While it's the biggest entertainment launch of all time, selling 32.5 million pre-release copies and making $3.79 billion by the end of its first day, you wouldn't know it from its Metacritic user score, as players review bomb it over its limited FPS and purported "woke" agenda.
