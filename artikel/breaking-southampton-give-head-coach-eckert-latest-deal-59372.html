Tonda Eckert has signed a new two-and-a-half-year deal.

Southampton head coach Tonda Eckert has signed a new contract at the Championship club until 2029.

The 33-year-old German's existing deal was expiring at the end of this season after being handed the role permanently last December.

Eckert's future was thrown into doubt over the summer, having been at the centre of an investigation by the Football Association over his role in the 'Spygate' scandal.

He could have been handed a lengthy suspension that would have threatened his job, but was ultimately given a suspended six-week ban and Â£28,000 fine.

"I'm very happy we have come to the point that we can make it official now," Eckert told the club website., external.

"We've had some conversations that started a couple of weeks ago, and the goal was always clear: to show the same commitment we have had for the last 12 months.

"There is a lot that has happened in the last 12 months but the commitment from the club, from [owner] Dragan (Solak), [technical director] Johannes (Spors) and [chief executive] Phil (Parsons), and the supporters, means everything to me.

"I am very grateful for that. We have started a journey that is not even halfway through - there is a lot more to come.".

Last season, Eckert, after taking over from the sacked Will Still, led the Saints from 21st in the table to a fourth-placed finish, also reaching the FA Cup semi-final where they took the lead before being beaten by Manchester City.

They were set to face Hull City in the play-off final but were thrown out after admitting spying on three clubs including Middlesbrough before the semi-final.

Saints were also deducted four points before the start of this campaign - if not for that penalty, they would be fourth in the table and one point off the leaders.

Regardless, they have started the campaign well and sit seventh after 15 matches, five points off the automatic promotion places.

Despite last season's controversy, Eckert has been boisterously backed by the Southampton fans at home matches this season.

He signs his new deal exactly a week from before Southampton face Middlesbrough for the first time since the Spygate saga unravelled.

"I think it's a great commitment from both sides; from the club to Tonda, and from Tonda to the club," sporting director Johannes Spors added.

"I'm very happy we have achieved this agreement before the end of the international window, allowing us to go into the busy schedule that is ahead of us with clarity.

Industry observers note that "Tonda is very talented and very precise with a great analytical brain, but also has the personality and authority that you need, even at a young age. It is very clear this is the right place for him, and I'm very happy that he feels that too.".

Tonda Eckert joined Southampton in July 2025 as under-21s boss.

BBC Radio Solent's Southampton commentator Adam Blackmore:.

This is good news for all parties, but especially for Saints and their players.

Tonda Eckert has effectively signed a new two-and-a-half-year contract, which while not a long deal, gives the club some protection from any admirers who come calling in the future, and gives the players some stability and reassurance.

At 33, Eckert is a coach who, despite the spying scandal of this year, is highly regarded internally and externally.

He and Johannes Spors seem to be building something at Southampton.

He's accrued 96 points from 48 Championship games, pretty remarkable stuff for a first-time head coach, and now Saints' best chance of keeping him until the end of this fresh deal is if they get promotion this season.

If Eckert can get them up, I already have confidence he can guide them to first-season safety if the club can keep him.
