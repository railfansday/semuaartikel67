Baubles are so done. This year, we're adorning our trees with stacks of chairs from Backrooms.

On Wednesday, A24 added decorations inspired by Kane Parsons' hit film to its truly cursed collection of holiday ornaments found in its lauded online store, alongside ornaments themed around Ari Aster's Midsommar.

Slowly growing in size and varying in level of messed up, the studio's "Trim the Tree" collection features deeply horrifying ornaments including multiple decapitated heads from Hereditary and a Pearl tree topper, a bloody axe, Theda the alligator, and the Mother Goose coke jar from the X Trilogy (X, Pearl, and MaXXXine).

Now, you can spruce up your tree with added sparkling stacks of chairs from Backrooms, the likes of which Chiwetel Ejiofor's protagonist found lurking around most corners in the liminal spaces of the film.

But if A24 thinks I'm paying $15 for the upcoming Backrooms wrapping paper, they're going to have to buy me a drink first.

It has been revealed that if you're more of a Midsommar freak, A24 also just added $36 hand painted glass ornaments of a sacrifice tablet, bear carcass, and Hårga temple, to complement its already-released, exquisite May Queen tree topper, released in 2024. Sure, Midsommar itself is an entirely different summer solstice holiday from Christmas, but with severed pigeon heads from Hereditary already on the tree, what's the harm in adding a little bear carcass?

Recent findings suggest that in October, A24 also added an adorable handmade felted $48 wool tree topper of Anya Taylor-Joy's Thomasin from The Witch, to celebrate 10 years since the modern horror classic's release. Will she watch over your festive shenanigans or cause a little otherwordly chaos herself?

Yes, people will have Opinions about the level of gore and horror on your tree, but it's your tree.
