Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and you'll want to really maximize your firepower if you want to survive this dangerous world. To improve your chances of not becoming past tense, you'll want to find the various legendary-tier weapons hidden throughout Leonida. One of the earliest you can get is the Kingfishers W-22 small game rifle.

In a recent development, however, you'll need to do a bit of fighting if you want to snag this weapon. If you're looking for an above-average weapon with solid accuracy, here is where to find the Kingfishers W-22 and what you need to do to acquire it.

In a recent development, to find the Kingfishers, you need to head to the Pelican Valley region in the southern portion of the map. Specifically, we are looking for Wetman Bay, which is the southwestern portion of this region and is represented on your map by a bunch of tan islands. You can only reach this area by water, so make sure to use Jason's fishing boat to go to this area.

Sources indicate that we are going to the Fishing Shacks, which are under the control of the Kingfisher gang. This area will become available after a few story missions, as Donnie will give you a side quest to go rob them and steal their cocaine. This will be marked on your map by a gold icon with a black crocodile on your map.

I strongly recommend bringing a long rifle and to make sure you buff your health beforehand. There are quite a few enemies you'll need to fight through, so ensuring you have a way to quickly dispatch them is key. The shotgun is especially good here since the enemies have a bad habit of just rushing you, making it easy to kill them with this gun.

According to recent reports, when you arrive, deal with the Kingfishers and fight your way to the large red house that's sitting on a section of the dock over the water. Enter the house, kill the guard inside, and approach the table on the left side of the room. You'll see an open weapons case with the Kingfishers W-22. Additionally, you can find several bricks of cocaine around the room, all of which can be looted and sold back to Donnie or Brian in Key Lento.

There's also another small shed with a couple bricks of cocaine in the shed directly across from the crocodile the Kingfishers were skinning.

As for the Kingfishers W-22, it will come with a red dot sight and a suppressor already attached. This makes it a great weapon if you're trying to accurately eliminate enemies from a distance, without causing a huge panic. It's also a solid hunting weapon if that does interest you; however, you'll want something with a bit more stopping power if you're killing big game.

Remember, even if you lose the Kingfishers W-22 it will be accessible in any weapons locker, making it nice to grab early and use whenever you want. Just make sure to store it in the trunk of your car so you don't cause a panic by running around with it!
