I’ve played over 150 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6. You might think I’ve seen it all, and that I know everything there is to know about the game. Nope! I’m still discovering fresh mechanics, features, and open-world moments after all this time. This includes some very handy control shortcuts I recently learned about that will make your life in Vice City a little easier.

Industry observers note that first off, let’s start with the whole reason I wanted to write this blog. Did you know that you can quickly toggle your car radio on and off without having to enter a submenu? All you have to do is double-tap left on the D-pad, and Jason or Lucia will turn the radio on or off.

I had no idea you could do this until I saw people sharing it on Reddit. The game doesn’t explain this shortcut, as far as I can tell. I couldn’t even find it mentioned in the in-game help menu, which does include lots of other nifty tips and tricks.

Here’s another trick I just learned about: You can double-tap the circle/B button while in a car to cycle the headlights between off, high beams, and low beams.

I thought you had to hold left on the D-pad to open up the vehicle submenu to turn off the headlights, but nope! And again, this isn’t mentioned in the help menu and if a prompt appeared during the normal course of the game to explain this, I and many others must have missed it.

Here’s another useful thing you can do inside cars: If you double-tap the triangle/Y button while driving, you’ll exit the vehicle without turning off the engine or radio.

Recent findings suggest that GTA 6‘s help menu does call this out and mentions you can do this at speed to have your car drive into something while you roll out the side to safely. But again, I can’t remember a single time when the game forced me to do this or taught me that it was possible. I learned from a random post online. I guess I should have dug through the help menu more, but come on, who is doing that in Grand Theft Auto?

Another feature mentioned in the help menu that I just learned about is the ability to click the right stick in and yell at nearby drivers. Rockstar refers to this as the road rage button, and it’s a good thing to have in a game where NPC drivers, inspired by real Floridian drivers, suck shit and drive like maniacs.

One more useful control trick: You can double-tap the triangle/Y button while driving a boat to get up and walk around the deck while the boat keeps on cruising.

The next time I boot up GTA 6, I’m going to start double-tapping random buttons during different parts of the game to see what other hidden shortcuts and control options Rockstar has buried in this massive game.
