David Moyes remained tight-lipped on his team news ahead of the 249th Merseyside derby, explaining Everton have "bits and pieces" going on when it comes to his squad's fitness. The Blues host Liverpool at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Sunday (12pm GMT kick-off). James Tarkowski definitely misses out, with the Everton captain serving the first of a three-match suspension after being shown a red card during the defeat at Brentford on Monday night. When asked about other issues facing his side during Friday's pre-match press conference at Finch Farm, Moyes stated: "We've got bits and pieces going on, which I'm sure every other team has as well." On the absence of Tarkowski, Moyes added: "Tarky will be a miss because of his leadership and a lot of things he brings to the side, so we'll miss him, but, look, it's one position where we have got some cover so we're not short there. "Michael Keane has been great form – any time he plays for us, he's played really well. He and Jake [O'Brien] are very much part of [the thinking]. We've got pretty good competition at centre-back and there'll be opportunities for one or both of those players in different games."The Opposition Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola will be without Conor Bradley, Hugo Ekitike and Giovanni Leoni for Sunday's game, while Cody Gakpo is also expected to miss out. Victor Munoz and Ryan Gravenberch face late fitness tests.
