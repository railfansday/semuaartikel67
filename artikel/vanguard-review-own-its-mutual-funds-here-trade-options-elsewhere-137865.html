According to recent reports, open a Vanguard account if you’ll buy low-cost index funds and hold them for years. You pay no commission online on its own mutual funds and ETFs, and cash you haven’t invested earns a money market fund’s yield without your moving it. If you trade options often, look elsewhere, since each contract costs $1.

According to recent reports, vanguard’s brokerage account holds its own funds beside stocks, options, bonds, CDs, and other companies’ funds, with no minimum to open. It costs least for investors who buy online and rarely sell.

Recent findings suggest that buy-and-hold investors who want Vanguard’s mutual funds pay no commission to buy them online here, against a $100 transaction fee for each online purchase at Fidelity Investments. Frequent options traders pay $0.65 a contract at Fidelity or Charles Schwab, against Vanguard’s $1.
