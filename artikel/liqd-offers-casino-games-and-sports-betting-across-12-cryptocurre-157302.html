According to recent reports, the cashier also highlights a minimum for each coin and network, and LIQD’s terms say a deposit below it is not credited. A transfer to the wrong address or network may not be recoverable, since a blockchain transfer cannot be stopped once it has been sent.

It has been revealed that LIQD takes deposits in crypto only, and USD, EUR and CAD serve as display currencies. The figure shown in those currencies moves with the coin’s market price.

Sources indicate that at a Bitcoin price of $80,000, a balance of 0.01 BTC shows as $800. If the price drops to $76,000, the same 0.01 BTC shows as $760 without a single bet in between.
