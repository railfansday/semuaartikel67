Chivu: ‘Inter had right attitude, cannot dominate start to finish in Champions League’

Cristian Chivu insists Inter had the right attitude in their 2-0 win over VfB Stuttgart, warning ‘in the Champions League you cannot dominate from start to finish.’.

The Nerazzurri were feeling the pressure after fumbling a 4-2 lead to draw 4-4 with Atalanta in Serie A at the weekend, and knew this was a must-win game to leapfrog VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League table.

It was a tough match, decided only by the Petar Sucic penalty for handball, and Lautaro Martinez coming off the bench to lock down the result for 2-0.

“We relied on mentality, organisation, intensity, accepting that Champions League games are not easy, that you cannot dominate them from start to finish,” Chivu told Sky Sport Italia.

“At times you need to stay in the match, try to make the most of opposition errors, but also try to be proactive when possible. The moments of a match are important and you need to read them.

Sources indicate that “We did well not to get swayed by their intensity in the first 20 minutes, so we accepted that we had to defend a bit deeper, but still with energy and pressing them on the ball. We did well to defend the crosses, as we knew they would be dangerous down the wings.

“It is also true that we created chances in the first half and with a little more sharpness or determination, we could’ve taken the lead before going into the break. We stayed in there, and were eventually rewarded with the victory.”.

This result means Inter are feeling more solid in the Champions League after three consecutive victories, having started the campaign with back-to-back defeats against Real Madrid and Club Brugge.

There are still concerns that the Nerazzurri lose intensity at times and need to be woken up before reacting.

Industry observers note that “The job of a coach is to transmit the right motivation in every match. It’s not always easy to maintain the same level, sometimes we do it better than others, but the important thing is that the team remains united and nobody starts pointing the finger at anyone else,” added Chivu.

“We must always try to do better in every competition. We cannot always be perfect, but what we must never lack is the right attitude and mentality. We saw that tonight, and it is no coincidence that we brought home the victory.”.

Did Chivu have to give the squad an intervention after they collapsed from 4-2 to draw 4-4 with Atalanta on Sunday?

“There always need to be interventions, when things go well or badly. We made a clear analysis of that match, because for around 75-80 minutes we played well, we lacked something in the final 10 minutes, but that doesn’t mean throwing away all the good things that were done that night.

“We are trying to keep a clear head in analysing the situation and whittling down those errors that can be tactical, technical or mental,” replied the Inter coach.

There was bad news too, as Alessandro Bastoni limped off with a thigh injury after 40 minutes, replaced by Federico Dimarco, moving Carlos Augusto to central defence.
