Black Friday hype stems from a desire 'to be part of a collective event', expert indicates

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (138 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that once a strictly US affair, it's become a truly global phenomenon. From shoppers hunting for bargains in Louisiana, to queues forming outside stores in Venezuela, the Black Friday sales event has become the biggest day of the year for retail foot traffic. FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney speaks with Olaya Moldes Andres, Associate Professor in Marketing and Strategy at Cardiff Business School. She explains that, beyond the sales, the success of Black Friday may also be due to a desire by shoppers to "join the collective action. Whether it is spending money or going to the mall, shoppers feel part of a collective event, so perhaps this is where the whole hype comes from, to feel that we're being part of something, same thing as Christmas or any other social event".
