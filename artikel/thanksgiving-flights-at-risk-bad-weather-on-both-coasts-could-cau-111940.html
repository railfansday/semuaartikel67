Thanksgiving flights at risk: Bad weather on both coasts could cause major backups

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (177 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Major storms on both U.S. coasts threaten to disrupt Thanksgiving air travel, with significant delays expected at key airports.
  • Travel waivers are in place for San Francisco International Airport, offering flexibility for affected passengers as heavy rain and wind approach.
  • East Coast airports, including those in Atlanta, Charlotte, Miami, Orlando, Washington D.C., Philadelphia, New York City and Boston, are at high risk for weather-related delays starting Wednesday.
  • Thanksgiving Day is typically quieter for air travel, but the Sunday after remains the busiest and could see further disruptions.
  • Travel waivers may allow for flexible itinerary changes at certain airports, but not all carriers or routes are covered.
  • Airlines generally do not cover costs for hotels, meals or transportation due to weather delays, though travel insurance or certain credit cards with travel coverage may offer reimbursement.
  • Flight disruptions can cascade across networks, so monitoring weather and airline advisories in all relevant cities is crucial.
