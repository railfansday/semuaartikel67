Every whale accumulation can cut both ways.

The logic is simple: whales buying during a bear cycle can be seen as a bullish sign, but within an extremely volatile market condition, it could shift rapidly and become bearish again as indications from on-chain data and technical analysis continue to worsen.

In a recent development, this makes the recent PEPE accumulation worth watching. Lookonchain noted that a whale has accumulated another 667.34 billion PEPE tokens (worth $2.27 million).

According to recent reports, the key takeaway? A total of 2.565 trillion PEPE tokens have been accumulated by this whale over the last five days alone (worth around $9.42 million).

So, does this whale know something the rest of the market hasn’t priced in yet?

The question becomes more interesting when looking at its current position. According to one analyst, this is interesting because despite this whale having a paper loss of around $713k, it is still buying.

Industry observers note that in fact, the whale has just added to their stack, with the latest two transfers seeing it add 667.34 billion PEPE, worth $2.27 million. This shows a lot of confidence in the token despite being underwater.

It has been revealed that according to AMBCrypto, the timing is also important. Pepe [PEPE] is currently seeing consolidation take place around  $0.0000035.

Sources indicate that it may be an early indication of accumulation, with large whales in the market buying at these low prices. But can one whale’s conviction alone be enough to turn the trend around?

The PEPE vs. SHIB debate has been heating up again.

As AMBCrypto flagged, PEPE’s price rose by 16% in October, bringing the market capitalization of the asset to over $2 billion. This ignited a debate in early November about whether PEPE would surpass SHIB.

According to recent reports, at that time, PEPE was only 15% off becoming the second-largest memecoin. However, things have clearly shifted.

As the chart below highlights, PEPE has fallen out of the top three memecoins, now ranked 4th, after seeing a larger market cap decline (-30%) than Memecore (M) at (-15%) this month.

This suggests PEPE’s weakness goes beyond the broader market sell-off and reflects added selling pressure on the token itself. It is also the worst-performing memecoin this week, down more than 9%.

This underperformance makes the recent whale buying harder to read as a bullish signal.

With PEPE already forming two lower lows since rallying on the back of the election results in 2024, and now losing ground to SHIB, the risk of breaking below the $0.0000035 level is becoming harder to ignore.

If this occurs, the whale could see its $713k paper loss deepen significantly, leaving the memecoin exposed to further capitulation risk.
