Andoni Iraola was keen to play down Liverpool’s Premier League title hoeps in the wake of his side’s 4-0 win over Manchester United.

The rampant Reds ensured it was a miserable day for latest Red Devils boss Mauricio Pochettino, who saw his side 2-0 down and reduced to 10 men after just 13 minute.

In truth, the margin of victory could have been much greater were it not for some wasteful finishing, but second half strikes from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz put the gloss on an emphatic victory.

With Arsenal having dropped points at Newcastle on Saturday, Liverpool have reduced the gap at the top to seven points and recovered some ground following their defeat to the champions at Anfield nearly a month ago.

Asked, after the first landmark victory of his tenure, whether Liverpool were in the title race, however, Pochettino was keen to play down expectations.

In a recent development, ‘It’s still early days,’ Iraola told Sky Sports. ‘I think we are missing the two significant games we lost here at home between Arsenal and [Manchester City] they are making the difference in the standings.

‘This is still so early. This doesn’t stop because we have a very difficult game against Borussia Dortmund and then we have the major derby.’.

There was no getting away, though, from the chasm that exists between the two north west heavyweights and Iraola was thrilled with what his burgeoning team delivered in front of their own fans.

Florian Wirtz and Ngumoha were particularly to the fore and the Spaniard was keen to praise two key members of his attacking cohort.
