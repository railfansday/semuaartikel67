📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that the open world Rockstar has created for Grand Theft Auto 6 is massive, but it’s not just about the weird random encounters you will find yourself in. There is a massive, 40+ hour-long story with a whole cast of characters to explore. One of the best characters introduced has to be Raul, a suave thief who Brian introduces to, and one who plays a key component to everything that happens in both chapter 3 and 4.

According to recent reports, unfortunately, like a number of minor characters in the story, there is a scene very late in chapter 4 where his fate seems to be put into your hands. We will go over both scenarios, although you might be a bit disappointed with the outcome.

Spoiler warning for anyone who hasn’t reached past the VCI Takedown mission.

Industry observers note that the first option is pretty simple. Raul rapidly tells you what happened, specifically, Valentina sold them out and Mendez personally came and tortured him. After that, he tells Lucia and Jason to put him out of his misery.

This is where the “choice” comes into play. This option will initiate a firefight outside the boat, as whoever finds Raul tied up will shoot him in the head. Thankfully, Raul comes ready for anything and has a gun locker in the other room.

According to recent reports, still, doing this yourself is arguably the best thing to do for Raul as he likely would have been killed more horrifically afterward. Still, if you don’t want to pull the trigger yourself, there is another option.

According to recent reports, despite Raul pleading and begging for Jason and Lucia to put an end to his suffering, there is the option to restrain yourself like you can in various other situations thus far. You don’t have to pull the trigger, but unfortunately, Raul’s fate is secured no matter what.

If you decide you can’t put Raul out of his misery yourself, your partner, specifically whoever doesn’t find Raul in the bathroom, will automatically shoot him instead. It’s a pretty anti-climatic conclusion, all things considered, especially considering the shot itself alerts a task force which is converging on the boat.

In the end, Raul must die for the story to continue, and either Jason or Lucia will be the one who pulls the trigger. It’s just a matter of if you, as the player, does it.
