According to recent reports, apple has released a firmware update for the new Siri Remote for Apple TV 4K, according to AppleDB. Here’s what you should know.

Last week the newly introduced Apple TV 4K officially rolled out, and with it an updated Siri Remote that adds a built-in speaker and Precision Finding so it’s harder to lose.

Although it’s rare for the Siri Remote to get firmware updates, Apple pushed out newly introduced firmware for the latest Siri Remote just yesterday per AppleDB.

You can’t manually update your Siri Remote’s firmware. But as long as it’s sufficiently charged and connected to your Apple TV, the update will install automatically in the background.

This update is only available for the upcoming Siri Remote (4th gen).

If you’re interested in the upcoming Siri Remote, but don’t want to buy a whole new Apple TV 4K, it’s available here as a $59 purchase and works with all Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD models.
