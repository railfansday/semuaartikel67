In a recent development, huge news for the Beyhive and anyone who wants good hair: Beyoncé’s award-winning haircare brand Cécred is having one of its biggest sales of the year right now. Through Sunday, November 29, Cécred is offering 30% off sitewide with code BF30, plus a whole bunch of free gifts with your purchase.

It has been revealed that if you’ve ever thought Beyoncé has good hair, Cécred is partially why. Co-created with her mother and longtime hairstylist Tina Knowles, Cécred is a full line for all hair types, but many of the products are strengthening and nourishing for any kind of dry, damaged hair. At initial launch, Cécred started with the Foundation Collection, featuring a Clarifying Shampoo & Scalp Scrub, Hydrating Shampoo, Moisturizing Deep Conditioner, Reconstructing Treatment Mask, Fermented Rice & Rose Protein Ritual, Moisture Sealing Lotion, and the Nourishing Hair Oil. But the hero was the Fermented Rice and Rose Protein Ritual: a two-step system that uses fermented rice, amino acids, and biotin to help fortify hair strands and repair damage.

But since its launch in 2024, Cécred has expanded with a whole line of stylers, the viral Restoring Hair and Edge Drops, and more.

Sources indicate that and it’s not just another cash-grab celebrity brand, by the way. Cécred’s won multiple Cosmo beauty awards, including best hair game changer in our 2024 Holy Grail Beauty Awards and best hair oil for the Nourishing Hair Oil. And it’s topped many of our best-of lists. We named the Moisturizing Deep Conditioner the best overall deep conditioner last year, and the brand is #6 best celebrity beauty brand (ahead of 30+ others).

“I definitely reach for this when I’m looking to tap into some main character energy. It’s rich, creamy, and deeply hydrating in a way that sets my hair up to stay bouncy and beautiful.” —JULEE WILSON, BEAUTY EDITOR-AT-LARGE.

It has been revealed that “Leave it to Beyoncé to dream up a new way of harnessing the power of rice water. This innovative treatment, which also includes the Silk Rinse, helped to boost my hair’s strength, shine, and overall dopeness in one use.” —JW.

It has been revealed that “Beyond the fact that I get to channel my inner Beyoncé every time I use this oil, my hair comes out looking glossy and gorgeous. Just a few drops seals in moisture without weighing me down. Shine, softness, and strength all in one.” —JW.

“My shower feels incomplete without this on the shelves. It’s the perfect weekly reset shampoo to help get rid of product buildup and oil in my roots. The scrub feels like it really gets my scalp clean, but the particles aren’t so small that they’re impossible to wash out. There’s some tea tree oil in this too that is so invigorating.”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR.

“When I had extensions and my ends were quite dry but my scalp was still oil-prone, I fell in love with this shampoo and its accompanying conditioner. It hydrates with hyaluronic acid and lightly smoothes with a mix of oils. It’s not too heavy for my fine hair, surprisingly, but it definitely left my ends feeling nourished.”—BG.

“I’m on a hair-growth journey right now, and TikTok pointed me to this scalp serum. It’s loaded with peptides to encourage collagen production, boost scalp blood flow, and calm inflammation, all of which can potentially support hair growth over time. But I really love how this feels on my scalp. It absorbs quickly and doesn’t make my roots feel greasy.”—BG.

In a recent development, “I got to try the styling collection a few weeks before the launch and—unsurprisingly—I was impressed. I opted for the Cécred Signature Blowout, which is all about substantial, bouncy volume. What stood out the most to me was watching my stylist work the fresh Volumizing Mousse through my damp hair before blow-drying. Not because I don’t use mousse, but because I don’t use it for my silk presses. It can feel too heavy, especially before blow-drying and the heat of the flat iron. But in this moment, it was perfection. My hair was light, airy, and a full-on slay! I couldn't stop touching it in amazement.”—JW.
