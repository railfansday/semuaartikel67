Vincent Kompany will have to make at least two changes to his Bayern Munich team that drew 1-1 with Cologne last weekend, when they travel to Hamburg’s Volksparkstadion on Saturday afternoon.

Both Harry Kane and Michael Olise picked up first-half injuries on Matchday 10. Kane is ruled out until 2027, while it was suggested that Olise could make his return this weekend after missing Bayern’s UEFA Champions League trip to Lille on Wednesday. However, Kompany confirmed in Friday’s press conference that the Frenchman will miss out.

Sources indicate that not only that, Jamal Musiala is sidelined for the foreseeable after aggravating a muscular injury. They join Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry on Bayern’s growing injury list, although the returning Luis Díaz could feature at some point – but Tim Binder is expected to start, with Lennart Karl leading the attack.

Jonas Urbig – Konrad Laimer, Dayot Upamecano, Minjae Kim, Josip Stanisic – Joshua Kimmich, Aleksandar Pavlović – Tim Binder, Tom Bischof, Nathaniel Brown – Lennart Karl.
