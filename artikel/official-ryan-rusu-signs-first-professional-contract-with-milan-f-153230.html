AC Milan have revealed that another of their young talents has put pen to paper on their first senior contract.

According to recent reports, A statement on the official website reads as follows: “AC Milan can confirm that Ryan Eugen Rusu has signed his first professional contract. The midfielder, born in 2009, will be part of the Milan Futuro Project, an initiative dedicated to nurturing and developing young talent.”.

It has been revealed that rusu has played as a deeper-lying midfielder, as a box-to-box player and as a playmaker during his time with Milan’s youth sector. He seems to have settled into a more advanced role for now, having made eight appearances for the U18 team so far in the current campaign.

Then next step for him from there will be Giovanni Renna’s Primavera side, and then it is Milan Futuro after that. Both the Primavera and the B-team are on a good run in their respective leagues, having racked up four wins on the spin.

Last month, Emanuele Borsani signed his first pro contract after spending part of the preseason with the senior squad. That came after defender Matteo Pagliei also penned senior terms, while midfielder Tommaso Mancioppi extended his deal.

Most recently it was the turn of Riccardo Grassini and Christian Vechiu to pen professional deals, with a clear emphasis being placed on rewarding young talents with security.
