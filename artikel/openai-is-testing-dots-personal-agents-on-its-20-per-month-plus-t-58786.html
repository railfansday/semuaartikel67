Dots are also coming to all ChatGPT Business Standard consumers starting today.

It has been revealed that openAI is expanding access to Dots, the proactive, always-on AI agents that can toil at tasks for you without any supervision or assistance. After launching the feature for higher-tier intends from ChatGP Pro and up, the company declared that it's testing Dots with a small number of people on the cheaper $20 per month ChatGPT Plus plan.

"Heads up, we're starting to test dots with a small number of ChatGPT Plus customers," OpenAI's ChatGPT engineering lead Thibault "Tibo" Sottiaux wrote on X. "Looking forward to the continued feedback as we expand and I'm recently seeing a lot more positives around how good the proactivity has been after many improvements.".

It has been revealed that dots can interact with over 4,000 apps thanks to their access to OpenAI's plugin ecosystem. The agents can work autonomously on tasks and make progress even when you don't interact with them. They can even connect to your PC, allowing them to control it on your behalf and work alongside you. You can check up on their progress by opening a Dot's "computer" at any time.

Originally powered by GPT-6 Astra, Dots can now run on GPT-6.1 Terra as well. The company just released several new features for them as well, including iOS widgets and Shortcuts integration, along with iMessage and RCS support, plus iOS notification replies. If you're on a Plus plan, you may gain access starting today, but OpenAI has yet to say whether the upgrade will be permanent.
