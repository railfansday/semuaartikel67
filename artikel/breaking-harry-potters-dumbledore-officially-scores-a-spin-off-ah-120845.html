Recent findings suggest that it's been almost 20 years since J.K. Rowling brought Harry's adventures at Hogwarts to an end with Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows, although anyone who's been keeping up with the books over the last few years will know that there's been plenty of new material to add to their bookshelves. Between the illustrated editions of the original seven novels and the new full-cast audiobooks, there are now quite a few ways for parents who grew up reading Harry Potter to introduce their own children to the Wizarding World. And with HBO's adaptation of Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone arriving on Christmas Day, Bloomsbury has something that might make for a rather appropriate stocking filler this year.

Two of Rowling's most beloved characters, Albus Dumbledore and Luna Lovegood, have officially been given their own books as part of the Pocket Potters series. Published on February 24, the two 32-page hardbacks followed the release of individual books dedicated to Harry, Ron Weasley, and Hermione Granger in August 2025, with each book focusing on the characters' lives and some of their most memorable moments from the original novels.

Dumbledore's book features illustrations by Paola Escobar and takes readers inside the headmaster's office, where they'll learn a little more about his phoenix, Fawkes, and some of his favorite things. Luna's book, illustrated by Helen Brady, explores everything from her father's magazine, The Quibbler, to her fascination with magical creatures whose existence is still very much up for debate. We never did get to the bottom of the Crumple-Horned Snorkack, did we?

Having been played by Richard Harris (Gladiator) and Michael Gambon (Gosford Park) across the eight original movies, Dumbledore will be back on our screens this Christmas, with John Lithgow (The Crown) taking over the role for HBO. Fans will have a little longer to wait for the introduction of Luna, however, given that the character, played by Evanna Lynch (My Name Is Emily) in the movies, didn't make her debut in the books until Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix.

The Pocket Potters collection has since expanded to include Rubeus Hagrid and Dobby, with both released in August, while books dedicated to Professor McGonagall and Fred and George Weasley are due to follow in February 2027.
