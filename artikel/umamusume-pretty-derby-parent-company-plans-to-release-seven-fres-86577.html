Cyberagent - the parent firm of Umamusume: Pretty Derby developer Cygames - has boasted its recent successes in the mobile gaming sphere in a recent quarterly earnings presentation.

Sources indicate that as part of this presentation (covered by Automaton), the manufacturer has established looks to launch seven "major releases" to the mobile platform by 2030. There is no word on what these games will be, but one will be released in 2027, while another two will come in 2028.

Recent findings suggest that cyberagent boasted an 88 percent "hit" rate for its mobile releases so far, with seven of its eight releases having a development budget of around a billion yen, and reaching a point where it makes that figure back every month from its playerbases. Those games include Umamusume: Pretty Derby, Final Fantasy Ever Crisis, Hololive Dreams, and Jujutsu Kaisen Phantom Parade.

CyberAgent CEO Takahiro Yamauchi - speaking during the presentation - noted that it was still difficult to predict the success of a mobile game ahead of its release. However, he also claims that the experience Cyberagent has gained with its big releases to date have allowed the company to gain a clearer perspective on profitability and sustainability.

It has been revealed that this presentation comes after a notable month for Cygmes and Umamusume: Pretty Derby. The developer issued refunds for hotels and flights following the sudden cancellation of an LA live show for the game last month, then shortly after became a major sponsor of the Japanese Formula 1 Grand Prix. The game was also temporarily taken offline due to the major PHP vulnerability exposed last month, an outage that was thankfully short-lived.
