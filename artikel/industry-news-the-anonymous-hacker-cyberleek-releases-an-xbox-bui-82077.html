We've been covering the hacker or hackers known as Cyberleek for months now, as they've attempted to profit from releasing footage of Grand Theft Auto VI well in advance of the official launch date. In that time, Cyberleek dropped a manifesto (the "CYBERLEEK Edict") championing consumer rights while simultaneously launching and cashing out on a meme coin (named $CYBERLEEK, of course) before disappearing into the void.

In a recent development, many people assumed that once the game was fully released, we would see the last of Cyberleek, who was thought to possess only an early build of the game. But the pesky leaker, having already avoided all of Take-Two Interactive's attempts to unmask him, is back with an even larger threat. On the Cyberleek website (which we won't link to for legal reasons), Cyberleek claims to have posted a 209 GB Xbox build of GTA VI: "It is done. CL holds the full November 2026 build of Grand Theft Auto VI, and it is going out by torrent." But, as always with Cyberleek, there is a catch. The download does not include the executable, so the game isn't playable from those files, though. Reddit users have posted what they say are assets from the leak. Cyberleek is threatening to release the executable on Nov. 23. "The countdown is the timer for the final file. Zero, and the executable drops, no conditions. Unless the CYBERLEEK token reaches 15 million market cap first — then it drops immediately.".

Because the game is already available for download on Xbox, you might be wondering why Rockstar Games should feel threatened. Cyberleek claims PC modders could use the leaked build to run the game through emulation. Cyberleek, again: "This community will have it emulated inside a week, running far past 30fps. CL told you not to preorder for a reason. This is one of the reasons.".

In other words,Cyberleek is now claiming fans could get GTA VI running on PC before Rockstar releases an official port.
