Industry observers note that after days of rumors circulating about Kate Middleton’s possible participation in the trip to India, confirmation came only at the last minute. This news came as a surprise in many ways, even though the princess’s presence was expected. Her involvement in this tour of India deserves attention, especially in light of the increasingly notable role she plays alongside Prince William, who will be king. The trip to India, significant in its own right, also included an important meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, aimed at strengthening relations between the United Kingdom and India. This high-profile diplomatic meeting was added after it was confirmed that Modi would return in time from his trip to the Philippines.

The Prince and Princess of Wales arrive at the Earthshot ceremony.

Kate’s participation in the royal tour was made official by Kensington Palace only the day before departure to allow the princess to assess her condition right up until the last minute and determine whether she could handle such a demanding trip, following her recovery. But once she arrived in Mumbai, Catherine proved she was ready to do more than initially planned. The princess appeared, as always, impeccable and smiling—perhaps even more so than usual—in her customary understated and polished style. She greeted the crowd and met with people who had come to see her, participating in events alongside her husband.

It has been revealed that from the first day of this three-day tour—which began on the anniversary of their official engagement— Kate immediately displayed her characteristic good-natured spirit, evident in past tours as well, by joining Prince William in a game of gully cricket—a street version of the sport—alongside local children and teenagers. Serving as host was Sachin Tendulkar, an Indian sports legend whom the couple had already met during their previous visit to the country in 2016. The two princes put their skills to the test with little formality. They then took a ride on the Mumbai metro—a scene we’d already seen in London in candid photos when the Princess of Wales traveled on a bus in celebration of London Poppy Day.

The Princess of Wales at the awards ceremony.

According to recent reports, the highlight was the awards ceremony in Mumbai on the evening of the second day, when the Princess of Wales unexpectedly took the stage to deliver a speech dedicated to the power of nature, health, and healing. Her speech was kept secret until the last moment, so much so that not even the celebrities and other guests in attendance knew the Princess of Wales would be speaking. “Nature is precious,” the princess said. “We are not separate from it. We are part of it, deeply connected in ways both visible and unseen…the health of the planet and our natural world is inseparable from our own, and when it thrives, so do we,” she continued. “I have seen firsthand how nature has the power to heal and enrich our daily lives, but so much remains under threat.” “There will be good days and bad days, that it will take time.".

Sources indicate that catherine then presented the award in the “Protect and Restore Nature” category, dedicated to the protection and restoration of ecosystems. “The people we’re celebrating tonight are a huge part of that story. Their work will inspire and create seismic change that will improve lives for generations to come,” she noted.

The Prince and Princess of Wales at the awards ceremony.

Sources indicate that over the past three days, William and Kate have been busy meeting about sustainability, innovation, transportation, culture, and relations between the two countries. William is, in fact, assuming an increasingly prominent role on the international stage, supporting the United Kingdom’s diplomatic relations through his travels, which Middleton’s presence reinforced. This time, however, it was not simply a princess accompanying her husband on an official visit, but a woman who, after her illness and a long hiatus from major trips abroad, chose to return to her rightful role—and one the public has long expected her to fill.

The Prince and Princess of Wales with some Earthshot Prize winners.

Industry observers note that it’s worth noting that this is the first time in four years—since her illness—that Kate has accompanied Prince William on an official overseas mission. The last such occasion was in December 2022, when the couple traveled to Boston, Massachusetts, for the Earthshot Prize ceremony. Her participation in the trip to India thus reflects her desire to gradually resume her international role, as well as her awareness of just how notable her presence is to the monarchy.

After her cancer diagnosis in 2024, Catherine prioritized her health and family, returning to public engagements without rushing things. However, her trip to Reggio Emilia, Italy, last May marked an important first step. On that occasion, she appeared visibly engaged and enthusiastic, and in subsequent statements, she hinted at her desire to travel more and gradually resume international engagements. “So I’m so glad it went well for her. I think she came back buzzing.” William remarked, describing his wife’s mood after that trip. Court circles had also hinted that Catherine wished to gradually resume a more active schedule.

The Prince and Princess of Wales at the 2026 Earthshot Prize ceremony.

It has been revealed that her return to work, however, was approached gradually, respecting the time needed for her recovery. It’s a journey that Kate herself described with great sincerity in a recent interview published last week in Country Life, a magazine for which Prince William served as guest editor. “I was told in hospital that my recovery would not be linear. There will be good days and bad days, that it will take time,” the princess remarked, emphasizing that recovery does not necessarily proceed in a steady and predictable manner. India, therefore, did not come as a complete surprise. Rather, it was the realization of a desire that Middleton had already begun to express, and that the princess agreed to undertake such a demanding trip indicates she felt ready to take the next step.

The Prince and Princess of Wales in India.

Before the trip, Kensington Palace issued a statement emphasizing how much the princess cares about her husband’s environmental initiative and how meaningful it was for her to return to participate in the ceremony in person. “She’s enormously proud of what he’s achieved, and it has been particularly special for them to experience this moment together. They are very much a team.”.

This refers to the project’s growth; since her last participation in Boston in 2022, it has significantly expanded its international reach and the impact of the initiatives it supports. “This year’s program in Mumbai promises to be the biggest and most ambitious yet, and she’s excited to see first-hand just how far some of it has come and meet some of the extraordinary people behind that success.” The spokesperson then recalled the couple’s previous trip to India in 2016, explaining that both have particularly fond memories of that visit. “The prince and princess are both really looking forward to what promises to be a fantastic week in India. They have such fond memories of their last visit together in 2016, and are excited to be returning a decade later.”.

Recent findings suggest that as mentioned, the most key diplomatic event of the tour took place on November 18 in Upcoming Delhi, where Prince William and Kate met with Prime Minister Narendra Modi. This was the only occasion during the visit when the royal couple left Mumbai, the city that hosted most of their scheduled engagements. Modi returned to the Indian capital specifically for this occasion after attending a major international summit in Manila, Philippines, to take part in the bilateral talks.

Recent findings suggest that A spokesperson for Kensington Palace had emphasized the strategic importance of relations between the two countries, characterized by deep and ever-growing ties in numerous sectors, which include trade, investment, defense, security, technology, education, and culture. Kate’s presence alongside William during the meeting was considered particularly significant. “This meeting reflects the importance of that relationship, as well as the role their royal highnesses can play in celebrating and strengthening the ties between our two countries.”.

Recent findings suggest that “For the prince, it also represents another significant engagement as he continues to take on a growing role on the international stage. We’ve seen him increasingly support the UK’s diplomatic relationships through his overseas engagements, and this meeting will provide an opportunity to build on those connections at the highest level.”.

The Prince of Wales on the Mumbai Metro.

In a recent development, india, after all, holds a special place in the history of relations between the British monarchy and the Commonwealth. King Charles has visited the country numerous times, on both official and private occasions, strengthening a personal and institutional relationship over the years. For William and Catherine, however, this trip represented an opportunity to build a direct relationship with a nation destined to play an increasingly notable role in the international balance of power.

A close-up of the Prince and Princess of Wales.

Recent findings suggest that with a population of over 1.4 billion people, India is now the world’s most populous country and one of the fastest-growing economies. Hosting the Earthshot Prize meant engaging a potentially massive audience on complications of environmental sustainability, resource conservation, and innovative solutions to combat climate change.

A beautiful image of the Prince and Princess of Wales.

For William and Kate, returning to India also meant revisiting a country to which they are bound by personal memories. As mentioned, their last visit was in April 2016, when the Prince and Princess of Wales—then the Duke and Duchess of Cambridge—undertook an official tour of India and Bhutan at the request of the British government. During that trip, they visited Mumbai, met with representatives from India’s cultural and sports communities, and traveled to Agra, where they admired the Taj Mahal.

Right in front of the famous mausoleum—a symbol of eternal love—William and Kate sat on the same white marble bench where, 24 years earlier, Lady Diana, Princess of Wales, posed during a visit to India. That photograph of Diana, portrayed alone in front of the monument, became one of the most iconic images of her public life. Taken in 1992, when her marriage to Charles was going through a deep crisis, many interpreted it as a visual representation of her loneliness. In 2016, William and Kate’s decision to recreate that shot inevitably brought Diana to mind, adding a personal and symbolic dimension to their visit.

In a recent development, for Catherine, therefore, this trip is above all a newly introduced step on her journey back to public life following her illness. Not a demonstration of recovery to be celebrated as a race against time, but an opportunity to resume playing an crucial role while moving at her own pace. And to stand once again alongside William on a mission that concerns not only the future of the monarchy, but also that of the planet.

The Prince and Princess of Wales in 2016.
