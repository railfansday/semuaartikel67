Alejandro Balde’s name is in fashion once again, with AC Milan potentially facing a battle to sign him next year.

Sources indicate that with Pervis Estupinan’s potential departure, it’s very possible that Milan will try to sign a left-back in January. It’s an area of weakness and there is a clear desire to change that, even if Sankhoun Diawara has offered a helping hand in an unfamiliar position.

So, a move for Balde could appeal, with Barcelona looking to offload the left-back.

This morning, it was reported that the Rossoneri were set to battle Juventus for Bruno Fernandes, but that may not be the only player they both try to sign next year.

Indeed, Tuttomercatoweb (via MilanNews) reports that they are also set to battle for Alejandro Balde. The Barcelona defender is likely to head away from Catalonia, and has been for some time, with Italy seeming like a possible destination.

Industry observers note that unfortunately for the Blaugrana, their €50 million wish is unlikely to be matched in January. However, both clubs are taking a look and Jorge Mendes is likely to be key as the player’s agent.

It’s also worth noting that several Premier League clubs have taken an interest in the Spaniard, so the Italian route may not be the only one open to him.
