energyRe Will Continue as Construction Manager Through Commercial Operation; Sale Reflects Strength of the Tech giant’s South Carolina Development Platform; Transaction Expected to Close in First Quarter of 2027.

Industry observers note that energyRe today unveiled that it has agreed to sell 100% of a portfolio of utility-scale solar projects in South Carolina to Enel Green Power North America, Inc. (EGPNA). The projects have a combined total capacity of roughly 317 MWdc of new generating capacity across the Palmetto State. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary conditions, including required U.S. regulatory approvals.

“This transaction is a strong validation of the projects we have built in South Carolina and of the team that developed them,” remarked Ryan Brown, Chief Operating Officer at energyRe. “Enel Green Power is a world-class owner and operator, and we are pleased to hand these projects to a partner that shares our commitment to delivering reliable, affordable power to South Carolina homes and businesses. Our team will remain on the ground as construction manager to see these projects through to commercial operation.”.

As part of the transaction, energyRe will continue to serve as construction manager through commercial operation.

It has been revealed that energyRe has broken ground on approximately 450 MW in the last year and has nearly 700 MW in operation or late-stage development. The organization’s 1.8+ GW portfolio spans 20 projects in South Carolina, North Carolina, Nebraska, and Oklahoma.
