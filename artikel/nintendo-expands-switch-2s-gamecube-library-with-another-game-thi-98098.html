Nintendo's GameCube title Wave Race: Blue Storm has today made a splash on the Switch Online + Expansion Pack service.

If you have an active subscription and access to a Switch 2, you can play it right now via the 'Nintendo Classics' GameCube app. Here's Nintendo's official description:.

Industry observers note that "Storm warning! Prepare to ride the waves! Take to the water in this watercraft-racing game, released for the Nintendo GameCube™ system in 2001. Compete in contests of speed and technique—either racing against your rivals or going up against your own records. As you race across the waves, you can see the skies changes from calm and sunny to surging storms... Weather conditions can affect visibility, water levels, and the height of the waves over the course of a race, so you'll need to be prepared for whatever comes!".

This jet ski racer was a follow-up to Wave Race 64 (also available on the Nintendo Classics service) and arrived in 2001/02.

It has been revealed that the game's water and weather effects were a stunning showcase on Nintendo's new hardware at the time, and the water physics in the title also raised the bar. Japan has received the same title on the Nintendo Classics service this week.

It has been revealed that alongside this latest game update (Version 2.2.0), you can now also turn off the "border graphic" when playing GameCube titles. However, the player icon remains on the screen.
