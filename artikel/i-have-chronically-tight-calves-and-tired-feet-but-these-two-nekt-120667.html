Anyone who deals with chronically tight calves knows how stubborn that tension gets. You can stretch before bed, roll around on a foam roller until you're out of breath, and still wake up the next morning feeling like your lower legs are wound up like springs.

Pair that with aching arches at the end of a long day on your feet, and your evening routine swiftly turns into trying to coax your legs into relaxing.

It has been revealed that over time, I’ve found that two very different Nekteck gadgets have done more for my tired feet and tight calves than any quick stretch ever has. Since both happen to be discounted on Amazon for Black Friday right now, they’re worth having on your radar.

It has been revealed that for my feet, I rely on the Nekteck's heated foot massager. Most days, my soles and heels feel tight from standing or walking, and slipping my feet into this thing while watching TV is easily the quickest way to take the edge off.

It combines deep-kneading rollers under your arches with air compression across the tops of your feet, plus an optional heat setting that feels especially good when your feet are cold and stiff.

One crucial tip, though: it’s super notable to wear socks while using this. I’ve pressed my arches against everything from the corner of a coffee table to a doorframe in a pinch when my feet were aching, and even I couldn’t handle those rollers bare-footed.

I was also a little confused when I first opened the box about how you’d ever clean the inside lining, until I realized the breathable foot sleeves actually unzip so you can pull them right out and wash them, and the 15-minute auto-shutoff means I don’t have to think about timing anything.
