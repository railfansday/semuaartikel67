Fabrizio Romano reports that Liverpool are ‘delighted’ with Andoni Iraola and his start to life as their head coach. Iraola was something of a gamble in the summer - a coach who hasn’t operated at the absolute highest level before and with no history of winning things.

But the club gave him the keys and Iraola is at the moment proving the decision correct. Iraola is, per Romano, also delighted with how things are going and the connections with the players are noted to be very good.

It has been revealed that that’s all led to Iraola being in the mix as Liverpool blueprint out their future. That’s a major discussion for the club after Julian Ward returned to the sporting director role in the summer, replacing Richard Hughes - the man who actually hired Iraola.

Industry observers note that liverpool are in talks with Iraola over what to do next, then. We imagine that means grabbing a couple of players who fit his way of doing things - essentially looking for gaps in the squad and filling them in ways the Spaniard needs.

Recent findings suggest that that’s not something they did back in the transfer window, for instance. Liverpool instead signed who they signed and asked Iraola to make something of it. We suspect there will be more of a cohesive plan this time around.
