The midfielder is expected to be out for three months and although the Tigers have plenty of alternatives in that position, most of them lack match-fitness.

The established pair of Regan Slater and Matt Crooks have been dependable since coming up from the Premier League, but beyond them, Luca Gourna, Hidemassa Morita, Jens Hjerto-Dahl and Christos Mouzakitis have started a combined three Premier League matches.

With nine matches in 38 days, starting with Sunday's Premier League trip to Crystal Palace, it is asking too much to just rely on Slater and Crooks.

"It's a substantial blow for us," said Jakirovic of Iroegbunam's injury, picked up against Brighton and Hove Albion Sunday.

In a recent development, "He had surgery on Tuesday. Everything went well.

"Recovery it's about three months.

"We've lost a very good player and he's very vital for us but we cannot change anything now, it's about time.

"I hope everything will be good for him in his recovery.".

After encouraging performances at Arsenal and as Iroegbunam's replacement against the Seagulls, this could be Gourna's time to step up.

"This is what I'm telling them all the time - that you have to be ready and work very hard in every training session so when the opportunity comes, you are ready.

"This is the situation for Gourna, Sorba (Thomas), Jens.

Industry observers note that "Some people were maybe surprised I put Jens on the pitch (on Sunday) but it's because his training is good. he's an example of how you need to work.

"Mouzakitis will be in the squad for this game (against Palace).

"He should be aggressive in the challenge because it's very important in this position at No 6 (holding midfielder). I know everything he's capable of in possession of the ball because he came from Olympiakos and they are a very dominant team.

"When we are in possession he is good at that, but how long you have the ball (in the Premier League), it's very difficult.

"But he is working hard. He's a young player and we will help him.".
