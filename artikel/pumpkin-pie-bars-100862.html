Pumpkin Pie Bars

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Pumpkin pie bars are the easiest way to get the classic dessert on your Thanksgiving table. They require no crust, chilling, or crimping. All you have to do is press the crust into the pan, pour on your filling, and you’re ready to go! Way less time, way more glory.

Recent findings suggest that arrange a rack in center of oven and preheat it to 350°. Grease a 12" x 17" baking sheet with cooking spray. In a large bowl, mix flour, butter, confectioners’ sugar, and salt until the mixture looks crumbly.

Recent findings suggest that press the dough evenly into prepared baking sheet, and bake until it is barely set and just starting to turn golden. This process should take about 20 minutes. In a large bowl, whisk pumpkin puree, cream, brown sugar, eggs, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, vanilla, and salt until smooth. Pour the filling over crust and bake until set and only slightly jiggly in the center. This should take about 40 minutes.

Let it cool completely before you cut the set mixture into squares.

Dollop with whipped topping and dust with cinnamon before serving. Enjoy!

According to recent reports, store leftover pumpkin pie bars in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Add the whipped topping just before serving.
