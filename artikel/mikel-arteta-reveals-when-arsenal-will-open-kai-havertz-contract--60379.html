Mikel Arteta gave a coy enhancement on Kai Havertz’s future at Arsenal, as he suggested a decision was not imminent.

The Gunners have moved to agree long-term deals with Declan Rice, Jurrien Timber and David Raya this month, while Piero Hincapie has been handed an extension as well.

The two obvious contract situations still to resolve are Martin Odegaard and Havertz.

Both are about to enter the final 18 months of their contracts, which run through to 2028, and it is understood that talks are yet to begin with either player.

Speaking on Friday, Arteta was glowing in his praise of Odegaard and made it clear that he "definitely" wants his captain to stay at Arsenal beyond the end of his current deal.

Asked if he felt the same way about Havertz, Arteta was less committal and indicated that there was no rush.

"With Kai, again, very, very happy with him, what he's doing, what he brings to the team," Arteta remarked.

"In the right moment we will discuss these things. We haven't discussed all the things that are coming now for a while and suddenly they are coming.

"So in the background, the club ownership, Andrea [Berta] in particular, is very much into that and I leave it to him because I fully trust him.".

Rice has agreed a latest contract that will earn him a pay rise up to around £350,000-a-week, moving him into the bracket of Bukayo Saka as the club's best-paid players.

The midfielder's workload has again been a topic of conversation this season but Arteta emphasised that there was no need for concern.

"I think he’s in a really good place," Arteta said.

According to recent reports, "In the last game against Hull as well, we managed to take some minutes out of him. We're going to have to manage that to get the best version of him.

Sources indicate that "But he's more than capable to do what he's doing, doing it again and doing it better. He needs to tell himself that as well because that’s the start of everything.".
