PARTNER CONTENT: Building on 20 years of backbone network and data center deployments in Kyrgyzstan, ZTE presented integrated telecom and intelligent computing solutions for local operators.

Industry observers note that ZTE successfully hosted its annual ZTE Day Kyrgyzstan 2026 technical seminar series in Bishkek. Held under the theme "Creating an Intelligent Future", the event brought together representatives from operators and industry professionals to discuss trends and technological developments in telecommunications.The seminar series covered radio access network and microwave transport, optical transport and IP networking, core network. Other topics included AI-based energy solutions, computing servers, mobile devices, infrastructure for digital transformation, and digital lifestyle applications.

Sources indicate that ZTE has operated in Kyrgyzstan for more than 20 years, working closely with major operators including NUR Telecom, Megacom, Kyrgyzstan Telecom, and Beeline. The tech giant has participated in the modernization and construction of backbone optical transport networks to improve improve data transmission speeds and quality. It has also supported network deployment in remote rural areas to expand internet access and cooperated with operators to build data centers in support of the country's digital development strategy.

In her opening remarks, Cao Hong, CEO of ZTE Kyrgyzstan, mentioned, "Kyrgyzstan's digital strategy demonstrates the country's commitment to digital transformation across all sectors of its economy. The goals set out in the 'Concept of Digital Transformation of the Kyrgyz Republic for 2024–2028' and other government initiatives emphasize the use of innovative technologies to benefit society. These goals align with the mission that ZTE has pursued in Kyrgyzstan for more than two decades. Under our 'Connectivity + Computing' strategy, with AI as a core direction, ZTE will continue working with local partners to enable communication and trust everywhere.".

ZTE continues to work with operators and enterprises in Kyrgyzstan to modernize telecom infrastructure. The company's products and services are widely used across wireless, fixed, and transport networks, virtualization systems, and other areas. Through projects aimed at expanding access to digital services nationwide, ZTE supports the goals of the country's digital transformation concept for 2024–2028 and other government initiatives.
