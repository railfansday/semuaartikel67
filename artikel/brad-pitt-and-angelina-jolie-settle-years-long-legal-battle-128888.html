The ongoing legal battle between Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie has finally come to an end.

The Ocean’s Eleven actor and Maleficent star have settled their years-long dispute over the sale of their French winery, Chateau Miraval, which they purchased in 2008 and was the location of their 2014 wedding.

It has been revealed that “We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” reps for Jolie and Pitt said in a Nov. 25 statement to E! News. “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”.

The former couple’s legal woes began in 2022 when Pitt sued Jolie for allegedly selling her interests in their once-shared French estate, according to court documents obtained by E! News at the time.

The Bullet Train actor mentioned in the docs that he had a "mutual and binding commitment" with Jolie that neither one of them would sell their shares to a third party without the other's consent. However, according to the lawsuit, Pitt learned in October 2021 that Tenute del Mondo, a organization with ties to Russian billionaire Yuri Shefler, had allegedly bought Jolie's shares of Miraval.

Jolie then filed a $250 million countersuit against Pitt in September of that year, alleging he devised a campaign to "seize control" of the once-shared estate after their 2016 split. This lawsuit, obtained by E! News at the time, alleged that Jolie only sold her portion of the estate to an outside organization when Pitt refused to remove the NDA stipulations he'd included as part of the deal.

The legal saga got even more intense when Jolie accused Pitt of abuse in a cross complaint obtained by E! News in October 2022. In the court docs, Jolie addressed a 2016 airplane incident that allegedly occurred days before she decided to file for divorce, in which she alleged that Pitt had choked one of their children.

According to recent reports, more allegations came out in April 2024 when Jolie filed documents stating, “While Pitt's history of physical abuse of Jolie started well before the family's September 2016 plane trip from France to Los Angeles, this flight marked the first time he turned his physical abuse on the children as well. Jolie then immediately left him.".

Industry observers note that while Pitt did not comment on the allegations at the time, a source with knowledge of the litigation told E! News, “This is a pattern of behavior—whenever there is a decision that goes against the other side they consistently choose to introduce misleading, inaccurate and/or irrelevant information as a distraction.".

The source continued, "There was a lengthy custody trial that involved the entire history of their relationship and a judge who heard all the evidence still granted him 50/50 custody.".

Sources indicate that pitt—who shares kids Maddox, 25, Pax, 22, Zahara, 21, Shiloh, 20, as well as twins Knox and Vivienne, 18, with Jolie—also denied any abuse regarding the 2016 plane incident and the FBI closed its investigation by November 2016 with no charges against Pitt. In the same month, the Department of Children and Family Services also concluded its investigation into the incident with no findings of abuse.

Sources indicate that amid their legal battle over Chateau Miraval, the two were also attempting to come to terms over their 2016 split. The duo finally settled their divorce in 2024 after eight years.
