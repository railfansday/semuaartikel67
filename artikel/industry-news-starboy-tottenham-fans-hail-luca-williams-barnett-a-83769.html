Tottenham fans have a latest hometown hero as Luca Williams-Barnett scored his first goal for the club on his full Premier League debut.

Much is expected from the teenager, who was named in the starting line-up for Saturday’s visit of Ipswich.

The 18-year-old made his senior debut earlier this season, in a 5-1 win over Charlton in the Carabao Cup, before two short cameos in the Premier League, against Crystal Palace and then Leeds.

And Williams-Barnett had done enough to convince manager Roberto De Zerbi to throw him into the starting line-up, and was rewarded with the opening goal after only 15 minutes.

The youngster nipped in ahead of Abdoul Ouattara to latch onto a Pedro Porro cross and poke to ball inside the near post.

Williams-Barnett later turned provider to put the ball on a plate for Dominic Solanke to score, as Spurs ran out 2-0 winners.
