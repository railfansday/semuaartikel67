Hexed marks Disney's last animated movie in 2026. From the directors behind Moana 2 and Wish, the fresh fantasy movie puts a new spin on the "Chosen One" trope and tells a story about a daughter and mother healing their relationship with each other while discovering and rediscovering their relationships with magic.

In a recent development, hailee Steinfeld voices the protagonist of Hexed, Billie Doe, a misunderstood and unconventional teenager, while Rashida Jones voices Billie's mother, Alice, who is a witch herself. Jodie Foster takes on the role of the villain and queen of the witches in the realm of Hexe.

Hexed has many charismatic but refreshing features that resemble the world of Harry Potter. From a talking book to a pen that's alive and a three-eyed cat, the Disney film builds a whimsical world that fans can immerse themselves in.

The trailer for Hexed offers fans a glimpse of what they can expect from the upcoming fantasy movie. Much like Harry Potter and Alice in Wonderland, where the magical world remains separate and hidden from the ordinary world, Billie can only access the realm of Hexe through a magical portal.

According to recent reports, like Harry Potter, Billie is regarded as a problematic teenager. She's different from other kids, and when the bracelet that suppresses her power accidentally falls off, her magic creates an incident that leads to her being expelled from school. However, the exact thing that makes her different turns out to be the very thing that makes her special.

Billie discovers a world where witches like her live in peace and harmony, a world where she truly belongs, which also brings forth mysteries and secrets about her own family.

In a recent development, the new Disney movie tackles a similar theme to Harry Potter, where the protagonist, who always feels displaced and isolated from the world she grew up in, finds belonging through adventures, friendships, and being embraced for who she truly is. Hexed also doesn't stop at the existing winning formula. It adds a twist to a story about magic.

Even before Hexed, Disney movies such as Turning Red and Encanto had already experimented with the multi-generation angle, placing magic in the context of family, tradition, and culture. Hexed further puts its emphasis on a parent-child relationship, which serves as a spiritual theme in Harry Potter.

Harry Potter wouldn't have survived Voldemort as a baby without his mother sacrificing herself to protect him. He also inherited his father's talent for Quidditch. Even though Harry's parents aren't physically present in the story, they still play a huge part in the story. Part of Harry's journey in the magic world is about learning more about his parents and encountering people who knew them.

Lily and James' presence is deeply woven into the series' most powerful moments. Even though they are deceased, their spirits are carried throughout the series. From the Mirror of Erised to Snape's Patronus taking the same form as Lily's and the final battle, where their spirits stand by Harry while he faces Voldemort, they are always with Harry.

Hexed doesn't miss the opportunity with Alice and Billie either. The movie places an emphasis on their relationship with each other as Alice follows her daughter and travels to Hexe. She also fights alongside Billie to defeat the villain.

Many of the animated coming-of-age movies focus on the protagonist's journey of growing up. What makes Hexed unique is that it breaks the trend of making teenage adventures only about friendship or being on their own and places Alice's motherhood journey at the forefront alongside Billie's adventure and reveals them finding their way back to each other.

Impulsive teenager Billie discovers that her unusual abilities are actually magic, sending her from suburban life into Hexe, a vast hidden realm of witches.
