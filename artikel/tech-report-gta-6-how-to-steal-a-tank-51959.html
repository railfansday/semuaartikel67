Tech Report: GTA 6: How to Steal a Tank

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There is no shortage of vehicles in Grand Theft Auto 6, something that’s made very apparent with the huge catalog listed under the progress bar in the menu. You’ll be stealing various types of cars, from high-end supercars to more ragged vehicles held together with duct tape. With that mentioned, one popular vehicle makes it return: the tank. This behemoth of a wrecking ball can destroy anything in its path, but where to find one can be a bit tricky. Thankfully, this guide will show you exactly where to find them.

Industry observers note that finding a tank isn’t like finding a pickup truck or a motorcycle. A tank is a military grade vehicle, and considering Vice City isn’t some military compound, you won’t just drive by one. And after the years, even getting a five-star Wanted level, you won’t see them coming after you anymore.

According to recent reports, cops are more aggressive, and you’ll have a lot of helicopters on your tail, but tanks are a special breed that are hidden away. In fact, they are somewhat hidden under your nose. That is because you are technically able to find and take control of a tank almost immediately in the campaign.

You start the campaign south of Vice City in Key Lento, and you likely won’t think about what’s to the south. This is because it’s not only obscured in darkness, but the game does a good job pointing you north with Lucia and the prospect of the big city.

Sources indicate that well, as you can imagine, tanks are located in the south. If you have that long road across a couple of bridges, you’ll eventually come across the Mile Zero Air Station next to the Southwind community. Here, you’ll need to break in to find a tank.

It has been revealed that you’ll want to enter from either the southeast or east entrance, preferably the former. The tanks are all nicely parked in the back, behind the southern hanger all the way to the western part of the base.

Pick one and get rolling, as you’ll likely have to avoid a five-star rating.

Recent findings suggest that now that you have a tank, the substantial question is: can you actually escape with it? Sadly, while you won’t immediately get a 6-star Wanted rating, it’s likely you will get one after a short while. This is because tanks have high firepower and can destroy anything outside of other armored vehicles with a single shot.

Sources indicate that sadly, the likelihood of you escaping with a tank is very low. While tanks have been the ultimate vehicles in the past, they still take damage. Other tanks can bring you down quickly, but even gunfire and rocket launchers will destroy them over time.

Because of this, best enjoy your time with the vehicle as it’s likely going to be a short one.
