Every once in a while, Instagram rolls out an update that addresses one of its users’ most longstanding requests. Today is one of those days.

As spotted by The Verge, Instagram head Adam Mosseri confirmed today that the app will finally let users edit a Story after publishing.

This means consumers will be able to add stickers and drawings, fix typos or adjust text, and even change the music without having to delete the Story and publish it again, which would mean losing any views it had already received.

The catch is that customers will only have 15 minutes after publishing a Story to make changes by tapping the ellipsis button at the bottom of the screen, and then selecting “Edit story.”.

Here’s Mosseri’s post announcing the news:.

A post shared by Adam Mosseri (@mosseri).

Today’s update is the latest in a series of changes Instagram has made this year to address longstanding user requests.

Recent findings suggest that back in March, the app introduced the ability to reorder photos and videos in a carousel after publishing. In April, it added an option to edit comments (also within 15 minutes of posting), and in June, it began letting customers manually rearrange posts on their profile grid.

More recently, Instagram also brought its free Edits video editor to the Mac, with multi-track editing, advanced color grading tools, and 4K exports.
