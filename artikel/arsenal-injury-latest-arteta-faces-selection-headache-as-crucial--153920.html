Arsenal injury latest: Arteta faces selection headache as crucial fixtures approach

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that arsenal could not have been in a more difficult position to suffer multiple key player injuries. Mikel Arteta will face a busy period with a Manchester City visit to the Emirates on Sunday and then in December face Tottenham and Real Madrid.

It has been revealed that although David Raya, Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi are all sidelined due to injury, Arteta has separate issues to deal with for each. However, the timing of their injuries is especially annoying.

The good news is that William Saliba is back in contention. But Arsenal can’t expect a select group of players to magically fix all of their issues. Over the coming weeks the true strength of the squad will be put to the test.

Raya is right now the most affected player. He suffered a shoulder injury while challenging William Osula in the 2-2 draw against Newcastle. As a result, he was also absent for the Champions League game against Borussia Dortmund.

In a recent development, whilst there had been initial concerns over how long Raya would be out for, scans showed that there was no need for him to undergo surgery. Arteta has called the preliminary reports positive. However, he has yet to state if Raya will play against City.

It has been revealed that kepa Arrizabalaga has stepped in for Dortmund, but Arsenal are affected by the absence of their preferred keeper. Raya is reliable and composed and has great passing ability.

These attributes will be of value especially when facing City’s potent attack. Although Kepa has top-level experience, it is by no means a game that Arteta would prefer to be playing with an unstable goalkeeping situation.

Recent findings suggest that raya may be available again either on November 29 or when facing Brentford on December 2. Much will hinge on the progress of his shoulder in the days ahead.

Arguably, though, Saka’s injury represents a bigger worry. He was sidelined after hurting his quadriceps in a match against Bayern Munich on 21st October.

In a recent development, arteta has previously suggested that it will take around eight weeks for Saka to make a full recovery. 20th December provides a possible return date, but there are no assurances that he will be fit to play at this time.

We have already analysed how vital Saka is to Arsenal – both as a creator and in scoring and providing width. The new right winger will need to be able to create key moments in close games.

Recent findings suggest that as well as losing Saka, Zubimendi is also set to be out for a number of weeks after sustaining a hamstring problem during his Spain duty. He may be available for the December 13 game against Bournemouth.

Without Zubimendi, Arteta will need to make further decisions regarding his midfield. Like Saka, Zubimendi is good at maintaining good structure, and this is key to Arsenal’s game. Finding a suitable replacement will not be an easy task.

Industry observers note that there is some good news. Saliba has overcome his back issue and is once again in the squad. In addition, Max Dowman has served his 3-game ban.

Saliba’s return is particularly timely with City’s attacking threat heading to the Emirates. Arsenal need stability at centre-back, especially if Raya is unavailable.

Arteta still has decisions to make. Although there is not a great deal of time, Arsenal do not always find suitable substitutes. The squad is deep enough to still challenge, it will be in these next games that it will become apparent how much Arsenal miss key players.
