Lego Godzilla Includes Two Free GWP Sets For A Limited Time

Recent findings suggest that godzilla has officially joined Lego’s army of IP sets, entering the fray as the toy brick brand’s substantial Black Friday release for 2026. There is naturally no Black Friday discount on the Lego kaiju, but if you buy it by November 29, it will come with not one, but two free gifts with purchase (GWP).

In a recent development, lego’s Godzilla started life as an Ideas submission. Lego Ideas is a program where fans can submit their creations, and if they create enough buzz, and Lego likes them and can make them a reality, they’re eventually turned into real sets that are mass-produced and sold.

That means the person who submitted this particular Godzilla idea will receive a percentage of the profits from it.

In a recent development, godzilla’s first Lego set is a pretty big one. As it should be - he is the King of the Monsters after all. 5,363 pieces from the tip of his blue and grey tail to the end of the heat ray you can attach to his mouth to make it look like he’s about to lay waste to Ginza, like he does in Godzilla Minus One.

Industry observers note that if you buy the Godzilla set between now and November 30, you’ll get the Godzilla Logo & Footprint set thrown in for free. The 359-piece freebie is, as the name implies, a little Lego build that gives you a big Godzilla footprint to display with the iconic monster’s name above it in neon blue.

Lego Godzilla will also come with a second free set if you buy it between now and November 29. The Retro Jukebox, so totally unrelated to Godzilla, but Lego is throwing them in with any purchases of $250 or more. Since Godzilla costs $449.99 - yes, the set has a gargantuan price to match its massive size - you’ll get the jukebox too, even if you buy nothing else, since it comfortably clears the qualifying threshold.

Both of these sets will not only be included with Lego Godzilla (or any purchases of $250 or more for the jukebox) for a very limited time, but once those November 29 and November 30 deadlines have passed, they will likely be gone forever. Lego rarely sells its GWPs separately, and only on the rarest of occasions does it make them available for a second time. The Lego Pokemon Center got restocked, a set you needed Insider points to buy, along with the Kanto Badge Collection GWP.

Lego has published instructions on which bricks you need to build your own badge collection. Handy, as it was tied to the $650 Pokemon set, so not many people have it.

Godzilla will likely be the last new Lego set released this year. Lego doesn’t tend to release new sets in December and instead gets geared up for January 1, which is its busiest day of the year for new arrivals. We’ve already been shown the new Star Wars sets releasing that day. That might also be the day we can buy the recently teased Lego Yoshi, and Marvel Rivals' first Lego set modeled after Jeff the Land Shark will also be available to buy from New Year’s Day.
