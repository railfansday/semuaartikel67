Toby Fox, the creator of Deltarune (and Undertale), has this week released his 'Fall 2026' newsletter, giving fans a "status enhancement" on Chapter 6...and Chapter 7.

Firstly, Chapter 6 is "progressing well", and the game is now a "structurally complete draft" that can be played from start to finish. In saying this, it will still take a "bit longer" to finish up some other scenes and details:.

"Much of the game looks complete… but, some vital scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation, so it's going to take a bit longer before we can really move to full-on testing.

According to recent reports, "Even though Chapter 6 is a rather short chapter, I would like it to be as good as possible. I won't be able to rush this.".

In a recent development, as for the recent vote, Queen is officially the winner, taking out 65% of the votes. And the reward is a "stupid scene" from Chapter 6.

And if this wasn't already enough, there's the Chapter 7 development revision. The team has "only just started", and Fox has read out some of the events and dialogue to the team, but is withholding the rest of the story until they "complete more of the game".

I read out the events and dialogue of Chapter 7 to the team. However, just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped reading… I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens.

Everyone agrees it's more fun this way.

Sources indicate that team members who completed their work on Chapter 6 have moved onto Chapter 7 now. Unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence...!

Recent findings suggest that fox ends the latest enhancement, reminding fans how the team has expanded since Chapter 4, but "more and more time is necessary" to keep the game's development going at the "fastest pace possible". The next update will apparently take place when the localization of Chapter 6 is done.
