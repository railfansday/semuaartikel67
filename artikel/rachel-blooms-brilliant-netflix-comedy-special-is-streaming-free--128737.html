Rachel Bloom fans, we've been gifted a treat.

The comedian and Crazy Ex-Girlfriend creator has uploaded her poignant, smart, hilarious, 77-minute, one-woman comedy special Death, Let Me Do My Special to YouTube. Yes, Bloom's lauded show is also available to stream on Netflix but not for free.

It has been revealed that recorded in 2024 after her 2023 show tour, the special is just over an hour with Bloom and her ditties such as "Darling Meet Me Under the Cum Tree" and "I Feel Just Like Dear Evan Hansen." But it's also a genuinely moving reflection on life and death, from pregnancy and giving birth listening to her favourite song (the Space Jam theme) to the death of her longtime collaborator Adam Schlesinger in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic. It's frank, real, and brilliant.
