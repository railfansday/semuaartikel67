According to recent reports, this year you can be thankful for Justin Timberlake. The pop star spent his holiday on The Tonight Show, where he sang, danced, appeared in ridiculous sketches, celebrated the 20th anniversary of his album FutureSex/LoveSounds, and hinted at what’s to come in his forthcoming documentary series.

During the late-night show takeover, Timberlake performed “FutureSex / LoveSound,” “SexyBack,” “Summer Love,” and “My Love” for a live audience in the Rainbow Room in Upcoming York City.

He also showcased “LoveStoned,” “Until the End of Time,” and “What Goes Around…/…Comes Around.”.

Teaming up with host Jimmy Fallon, Timberlake offered a history of party song for the studio audience. The performance included quick-fire renditions of Caron Wheeler and Soul II Soul’s “Back to Life (However Do You Want Me),” Montell Jordan’s “This Is How We Do It,” and Daft Punk and Pharrell’s “Get Lucky.” They concluded with an anthem take on the Backstreet Boys’ “I Want It That Way.”.

Elsewhere on The Tonight Show, Timberlake dressed as a turkey and relived 1980s camp memories.

Timberlake also sat down with Fallon to discuss the anniversary of FutureSex/LoveSounds, released in 2006, and his forthcoming four-part documentary, Timberlake.

“I was really reluctant to do it,” Timberlake told Fallon. “I’m sort of famously private. And I have a relationship with Fisher Stevens, who is a wonderful director and a great human. When Fisher first approached me to do this, I was reluctant because I was like, ‘I don’t wanna talk about myself,’ you know?
