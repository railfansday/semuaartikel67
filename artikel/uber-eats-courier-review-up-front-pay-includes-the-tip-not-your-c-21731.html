Each offer demonstrates its pay, with any tip added at checkout, before you accept, so take only offers that pay more than the trip costs you. Drivers approved to carry passengers can add rides in the same app.

It has been revealed that the pay an Uber Eats offer shows is what you earn before self-employment tax and, if you drive, the cost of your miles. Uber pays couriers by the completed delivery, not the hour, so subtract both to work out what each hour pays you.

In a recent development, uber Eats suits couriers in busy delivery markets who want to set their own hours, especially drivers who can also take ride requests. Couriers who’d rather be paid for their time should compare DoorDash, whose Earn by Time mode guarantees a minimum hourly rate from acceptance to drop-off in select U.S. cities.
