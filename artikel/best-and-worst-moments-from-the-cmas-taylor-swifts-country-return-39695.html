The 2026 CMA Awards were full of show-stopping moments — and a few misses — that had fans talking all night long.

Lainey Wilson returned as host of country music’s biggest night for the third year in a row on Wednesday, November 18, emceeing the awards show from Bridgestone Arena in Nashville.

After walking the red carpet with her newly-minted husband, Devlin “Duck” Hodges — and sharing a sweet kiss for the cameras — she kicked off the show in style with a touching Dolly Parton tribute alongside Vince Gill.

It has been revealed that wilson, however, wasn’t the only strong country female who turned heads at the awards ceremony in a fun and flirty yellow dress — Taylor Swift had everyone’s attention from the moment she stepped out in Music City. Her performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song” only added to her epic comeback.

Keep scrolling for a breakdown of the best and worst moments from the 2026 CMAs:.

Wilson kicked off the 2026 CMAs by bringing bonafide Swiftie Brad Paisley on stage to help with her monologue. As the pair rewrote some of country music’s biggest hits with funny and timely lyrics, they gave a nod to Swift and husband Travis Kelce’s July wedding.

“This is bigger than me: We have to talk about the biggest story of the year. Somebody got married,” Paisley, 54, stated, revealing the bride was a “blonde singer” who married a “football player.”.

While Wilson thought he was talking about her May nuptials with former Pittsburgh Steelers player Hodges, 30, Paisley joked he had no idea she was a newlywed.

“You got married? What arena did you get married in?” Paisley asked, referring to Swift and Kelce’s Madison Square Garden affair.

Sources indicate that he later quipped, “I can’t believe I wasn’t invited to your wedding,” before looking into the crowd and making eye contact with Swift. “I did go to Taylor’s. Hi, Taylor! Remember me? I ate all the shrimp,” Paisley joked.

During Musgraves and Lambert’s performance of “Horses and Divorces,” their newly introduced song that squashed their past highly-publicized beef, lyrics were not once, but multiple times bleeped out during the broadcast. While it was understandable that the curse words would be cut from the show, it took away from the viewers’ experience — and the words weren’t (arguably) that scandalous.

“You know that I’ve mentioned some things about you,” Musgraves began, before Lambert chimed in, “And I’ve done my fair share of s** talkin’ too,” however, “s** talkin’” was bleeped.

Elsewhere in the song, the pair was supposed to sing in unison, “What asshole doesn’t like Willie?” about their longtime friend Willie Nelson, only to have that line ruined by another bleep.

Wilson was joined by country legend Gill at the top of the show to perform “I Will Always Love You” in honor of Parton, who died in August at the age of 80. The heartfelt rendition of Parton’s hit song had Swift crying in the audience and Luke Combs singing along from his seat.

According to recent reports, to bring a little levity to the night, Wilson and Paisley also gave Parton a shout-out in their joint monologue. Paisley teased that the next time there is an FAA shutdown at the airport, all she needs to do is put on the pair of “magic red cowboy boots” he gifted her and say, “Dolly Parton airport, Dolly Parton airport, Dolly Parton airport.”.

“Where every gate is Double D!” Wilson added.

Fans waited with baited breath to see how Swift would return in her first CMAs performance in 10 years and she showed up.

The singer kicked off her “I Knew It, I Knew You” song backstage, reuniting with some of her former country collaborators, including Little Big Town and Sugarland before taking the stage with three-time collaborator Keith Urban, who played the banjo beside her.

Swift, who paid homage to Toy Story 5 with her set and cloud-adorned guitar, then pivoted from the movie track to her 2006 hit “Our Song,” flashing fans back to her country roots.

Langley was the biggest winner of the night, taking home trophies for Single of the Year, Song of the Year, Album of the Year, Female Vocalist of the Year and Entertainer of the Year.

“I just want to say I have so much love and respect for every single woman in this category. Not only every single woman in this category but the women who have gone before me,” the “Choosin’ Texas” singer noted after winning her fourth award. “This is what I have lived and breathed … genuinely my whole entire life.”.

She gushed, “I love what I do.”.

“At this point I’m sick of me,” Langley joked after taking home the night’s biggest accolade. “I’m at a loss of words. I don’t know what else to say. Truly, trust the people around you. Thank you CMAs, this has been the most special night.”.

Wilson came out on stage with a bedazzled broom to honor Langley’s sweep. “I love how weird she is,” Langley said with a laugh.
