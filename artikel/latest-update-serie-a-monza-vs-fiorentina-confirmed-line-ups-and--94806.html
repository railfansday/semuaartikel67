Gustavo Varela leads the line for Monza, and Fiorentina have made just one change to their starting line-up ahead of the meeting of the two sides in Serie A on Monday evening.

Monza are hoping to avoid making it five consecutive defeats after losing four on the bounce prior to the international break. They find themselves in the relegation zone in 18th place with seven points to their name after the first 11 matches of the season.

According to recent reports, fiorentina went in for the international break on a three-game unbeaten run, with draws against Atalanta and Juventus and a victory over Sassuolo at the beginning of the month. They have nine points to their name ahead of kick-off, which is enough for 15th place as things stand.

In a recent development, varela, who is beginning to attract the interest of some of Serie A’s biggest clubs, will continue to lead the line for Monza this evening. The recent Portugal international has nine goals and an assist to his name from 10 Serie A outings so far this campaign.

Recent findings suggest that fiorentina, meanwhile, have made just one change from their 2-2 draw against Juventus before the international break, with Wilfried Gnonto replacing Alieu Njie on the left wing. He will be joined by Franco Mastantuono and Mateo Pellegrino in attack.

Monza (3-4-2-1): Thiam; Kouadio, Goglichidze, Carboni; Birindelli, Akinsanmiro, Colombo, Mangas; Pessina, Robinson; Varela.

Fiorentina (4-3-3): De Gea; Jimenez, Dragusin, Viery, J.Mario; Ndour, Oulai, Goncalves; Mastantuono, Pellegrino, Gnonto.
