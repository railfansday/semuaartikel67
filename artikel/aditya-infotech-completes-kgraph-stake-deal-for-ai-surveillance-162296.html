📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Aditya Infotech Limited said in a stock-exchange filing on November 27, 2026 that it had completed a ₹45.39 crore cash purchase of 30% of KGraph AI Solutions Private Limited on a fully diluted basis. KGraph becomes an associate manufacturer, with its BLUE video-analytics technology slated for integration into CP PLUS surveillance products.

It has been revealed that the manufacturer’s November 5, 2026 announcement described the consideration as a mix of fresh equity and purchases from existing shareholders, with an implied post-money valuation of approximately ₹151 crore. Managing Director Aditya Khemka noted: “Video compression is foundational to the next phase of surveillance. As cameras multiply and resolutions rise, the cost of storing and moving video becomes the real constraint.”.

In a recent development, the price was based on a registered valuer’s report. The transaction was not a related-party deal, none of Aditya Infotech’s promoters, promoter group or group companies had an interest in KGraph, and no governmental or regulatory approvals were required.

Recent findings suggest that KGraph’s Semantic Codec adds a perception-aware compression layer to the standard H.264 video codec, identifying redundant visual information while retaining essential details. Based on deployment data, Aditya Infotech noted the technology could reduce video storage and bandwidth requirements by up to 90%.

The codec is designed for cost-efficient edge hardware without GPU infrastructure and works with existing cameras and recording systems. The company stated this could extend video retention and lower deployment costs without replacing installed infrastructure, giving CP PLUS a potential upgrade path for customers already running surveillance equipment.

KGraph’s broader BLUE platform turns continuous video streams into searchable enterprise data. The investment is part of CP PLUS 2.0, Aditya Infotech’s move from hardware-led surveillance toward integrated security covering data management, edge computing, AI analytics and cloud integration.

KGraph will continue to operate independently while collaborating with Aditya Infotech’s R&D and engineering teams in Noida, Ahmedabad and Taiwan. The collaboration pairs KGraph’s computer-vision, embedded-systems and compression expertise with Aditya Infotech’s engineering, manufacturing and customer reach.

In a recent development, KGraph’s six co-founders bring 15 to more than 20 years of experience and academic backgrounds from IIT Bombay and IIM Bangalore. The company has a 10-patent pipeline, with three applications filed and seven in progress.

Sources indicate that incorporated on August 16, 2023, KGraph develops AI-powered software for compliance, automation, safety, monitoring and regulatory reporting across industrial and commercial sectors. It operates in India, and BLUE is its principal product. The completion filing reported audited turnover of approximately ₹80 lakh in FY2025-26, compared with approximately ₹36 lakh in FY2024-25 and no turnover in FY2023-24.

It has been revealed that aditya Infotech’s board approved the investment on November 5, when the parties executed a share subscription and purchase agreement and a shareholders’ agreement. The original transaction filing allowed 30 days from execution, or a later mutually agreed date, for completion. Closing on November 27 therefore fell within the initial timetable.

The completed stake gives CP PLUS access to KGraph’s compression and analytics expertise while leaving the startup independent. The commercial question now is whether joint engineering can turn the company-reported efficiency gains into deployed CP PLUS products and a larger revenue base for KGraph.
