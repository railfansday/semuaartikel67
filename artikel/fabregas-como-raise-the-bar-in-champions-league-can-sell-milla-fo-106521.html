Fabregas: ‘Como raise the bar in Champions League, can sell Milla for €20m’

Cesc Fabregas feels Como really made a Champions League statement in their 1-0 victory over AEK Athens, and jokes that they can ‘sell Luis Milla for €20m in January’ after his goal.

The Lariani struggled to break down AEK Athens at the Stadio Sinigaglia this evening, especially after Lucas Da Cunha drilled a penalty off target in the first half.

Also hitting the woodwork a couple of times, they eventually found the winner thanks to a Luis Milla curler into the far top corner from outside the box.

“It is a victory that was crucial for us, because the level of the Champions League is very high and demanding,” Fabregas told Sky Sport Italia.

It has been revealed that “Whether we won or not today, we proved that this team does not struggle to raise the bar when the match takes on a different context. In our five games, we were always competitive, and the most important thing I wanted to see this season was that.

It has been revealed that “The results are going our way and we need to keep going. Serie A remains the most notable thing for us, but I cannot deny that it is truly wonderful to experience Champions League nights at the Sinigaglia.”.

Milla got the only goal of the game, and his path to Como was not an easy one, as it took several attempts to finally sign the midfielder.

“We signed him for €6m, maybe we’ll sell him in January for €20m,” smiled Fabregas with Milla waiting nearby for his interview.

“No, I’m kidding, he’s a great player, has had a high level for a long time, his career saw him constantly improve and now he has the kind of character and experience, he is above all a strong presence in the locker room.”.

The Lariani ran a few risks late on too against AEK Athens, so Fabio Capello in the Sky Sport Italia studio asked if Fabregas was worried by their fitness levels in the final 20 minutes?

Recent findings suggest that “I am always worried! I don’t have your experience to be calm in these situations! The Champions League is of such a level that you can pay for any tiny mistake,” replied the coach.

“The truth is, there aren’t many of us, because there were eight players missing today due to injury and the UEFA squad list limitations, so I think for the first time in my career I only made three substitutions today.

“I did like the character of the squad, the mentality and hunger. Obviously, there are always things to improve, but I liked the approach.”.

This success means Como have won four of their five Champions League games so far this season, losing only 3-2 away to Feyenoord, and have practically guaranteed at least a spot in the play-offs.
