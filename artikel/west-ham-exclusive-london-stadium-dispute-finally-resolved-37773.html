📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Claret & Hugh understands that West Ham and the London Stadium have reached an agreement to host a West Ham Women’s WSL game at the London Stadium on a date yet to be scheduled.

Late last year, the two parties had a dispute over a West Ham WSL game being played at the London Stadium and looked set for another legal battle in the courts.

Under the 2013 concessionaire agreement, West Ham are allowed to play up to 25 competitive games at the London Stadium in return for an annual usage fee.

The index-linked usage fee has inflated from £2.5m in 2016 to £4.8m each year in 2025, meaning each of the 25 games cost West Ham around £192,000 per match last season.

West Ham wanted to use one of those 25 competitive games to play a West Ham Women’s WSL game, which is mandated by regulations, but the London Stadium owners refused to allow it at the time.

The breakthrough came during negotiations for West Ham’s backing for the 2029 World Athletics Championships, which London Stadium bid to host.

The bid required West Ham’s backing, which was secured in August this year, but ultimately the bid failed in September, having lost out to Nairobi.

Part of the concessions agreed in return for West Ham’s backing for the athletics championship bid included hosting a WSL game at the London Stadium.
