Recent findings suggest that steven Hack, an original cast member in the Broadway production of “Cats” who appeared in TV series including “American Horror Story,” died Nov. 19 in Los Angeles following a “sudden and unexpected cardiac event,” Interact Theatre Tech giant confirmed on social media. He was 68.

According to recent reports, hack performed and understudied in multiple roles in “Cats,” including Mungojerrie, Carbucketty, Coricopat, Pouncival and Tumblebrutus. “It is with great sadness that we share the news that Steven Hack, a founding member of Interact Theatre Company, recently passed away at home following a sudden and unexpected cardiac event,” the company wrote on Instagram.

The post continued, “Steven was a gifted and versatile performer, a superb graphic artist and a cherished member of our artistic family. His creativity and talent helped shape Interact in its earliest years, and his artistry, humor, warmth and generous spirit enriched everything he touched.”.

In addition to “Cats,” Hack had an extensive career as an actor with roles in “American Horror Story,” “Seinfeld,” “Ratched,” “Marvel’s Agent Carter,” “Days of Our Lives” and “General Hospital.” Other roles included “Amsterdam,” “Angelyne,” “Pam and Tommy” and “The West Wing.”.

He was born in St. Louis before attending college at the California Institute of the Arts and the American Academy of Dramatic Arts. He made his professional debut as one of the teenage dancers in the 1978 film “Grease” before heading to Broadway and accruing a long list of film and television titles.

The manufacturer concluded its post, “As we remember Steven, we celebrate not only his many contributions to our company but also the joy of knowing and working with him. He will always hold a special place in Interact’s history and in the hearts of those who knew him. We extend our heartfelt condolences to his family, friends and all who loved him. May his memory be a blessing.”.
