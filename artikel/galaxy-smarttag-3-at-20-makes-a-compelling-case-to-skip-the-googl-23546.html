Barely a week after its launch, Samsung’s Galaxy SmartTag 3 is already on sale for as little as $20, making it a fairly compelling alternative to Google’s new Pixel Tag, assuming you have a Galaxy phone, of course.

Sources indicate that while Google’s Pixel Tag is a brilliant launch, it’s not perfect. Still based on a Find Hub network that struggles to keep up, the Pixel Tag is a great choice for most Android consumers, but Galaxy devices have an alternate option.

Surprisingly, Samsung’s network often outperforms Google’s in real-life use, as many have found.

Industry observers note that the Galaxy SmartTag 3 went on sale just a week ago as Samsung’s latest tracker. It’s still $29.99 and has a fresh, smaller design without a built-in mounting loop, but it brings upgrades in longer battery life, limited iPhone support, and a new NFC feature that can show your contact info if someone finds your lost tracker.

In a recent development, woot is selling an international version of the Smarttrack 3. In addition to lacking Samsung's warranty it is unclear if the UWB functionality on these trackers will be compatible in the U.S.

And, already, it’s being discounted.

According to recent reports, woot has the Galaxy SmartTag 3 for $23.99. That’s for a single tracker, but there are also discounted bundles. $43.99 for a 2-pack, $62.99 for a 3-pack, and $79.99 for a 4-pack. The 4-pack is the best deal per tracker at $20, but you’re saving money regardless of how many you pick up.

Meanwhile, Galaxy SmartTag 2 is also discounted at Amazon, should you prefer that hardware.
