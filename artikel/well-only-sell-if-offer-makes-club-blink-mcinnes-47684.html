Derek McInnes joined Rangers from Hearts in the summer.

Manager Derek McInnes states Rangers will only sell a player in the January transfer window if they receive an offer that "makes the club blink".

Players such as German striker Ryan Naderi, Mauritania winger Djeidi Gassama and Belgium midfielder Nico Raskin have been linked with moves while centre-backs Olwethu Makhanya and Emmanuel Fernandez have attracted interest after their starts to the season.

However McInnes will oppose moves for players who have featured regularly in his first season in charge at Ibrox - unless a club makes a startling offer that is too good to turn down.

"If somebody comes in way over the top then you have to obviously consider these sort of things," he remarked.

"But in the main, we're not looking to move players out, particularly players that have been playing regularly. We want to shut that down really early.

"We don't want agents flirting with clubs, giving them any encouragement. We know and understand that keeping that core of players and having that stability is so crucial for us.

It has been revealed that "We don't want January window to be excitable or dramatic. Just to come and go and hopefully we can just keep doing what we're doing.".

McInnes noted that Lawrence Shankland and Cammy Devlin are progressing well in their rehabilitation and that his players returned unscathed from international duty - although Kevin Kelsy was back later than expected from the Venezuela squad.

"Kevin is the only wee anomaly. We didn't get him back from Russia on Wednesday night because of a few issues with transport, but we managed to get him back in at lunchtime on Thursday," McInnes noted.

Rangers face Motherwell in Lanarkshire on Saturday and have a solid away record - but dropped points in two away draws prior to the win at St Mirren.

McInnes is expecting a stiff test as he believes the Steelmen are beginning to perform like the side that impressed throughout last season and qualified for Europe.

Recent findings suggest that "I think they have got a lot of traits and similarities to the team that got a lot of success last year. I think over the last two or three games they've started to look a wee bit more like that," he added.

"They'll be a difficult opponent, but if we want to get to where we want to get to this season, we want to make sure we leave Motherwell with all three points.".
