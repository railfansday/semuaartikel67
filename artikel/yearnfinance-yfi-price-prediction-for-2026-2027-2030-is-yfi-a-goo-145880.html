Sources indicate that yearn Finance (YFI) is a pioneering decentralised finance (DeFi) yield aggregator built on the Ethereum blockchain. It automatically shifts user funds across various DeFi lending protocols like Aave and Compound to maximise interest and auto-compound returns.

Its native token, YFI, serves primarily as a governance token, giving holders voting rights over protocol decisions, vault strategies, and fee structures.
