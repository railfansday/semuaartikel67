The highly anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto VI shattered video game sales records, earning $3.79 billion in the first 24 hours of its launch.

Already, the 13-year wait for the latest installment of the crime spree series had racked up more than 32.5 million pre-orders, according to Rockstar Games and GTA parent manufacturer Take-Two. After Thursday’s numbers came in, the companies dubbed the franchise the “highest-grossing entertainment property” and the “biggest entertainment launch in history.”.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar, said in a statement, per The Hollywood Reporter. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.” Trending Stories Marlon Jackson Reveals Stage 4 Prostate Cancer Diagnosis Some Gamers Are Pissed That ‘GTA VI’ Makes Them Be Nice to Their Girlfriend Selena Gomez Shares Her Reaction to Martin Short Ruining Her Wedding Cake Trump Got Played by Putin and Xi.

According to recent reports, GTA VI was already competing against itself for the highest release-day sales. Its predecessor, GTA V, earned $815.7 million within 24 hours of its September 2013 release, while Call of Duty: Black Ops II pulled in $500 million in its first day on shelves in November 2012.

Industry observers note that the video game has earned rave reviews, including an aggregated score of 99 on Metacritic and perfect scores from gaming critics. “It raises the bar at every level that matters,” Gene Park wrote for The Washington Post. “Grand Theft Auto VI is the most extraordinary game I’ve ever reviewed.”.
