The Hunting Wives author May Cobb is addressing the show’s deviation from the book ahead of the highly-anticipated season 2.

“I feel like [creator] Rebecca [Cutter] did such a great job of keeping the spirit of the book — the unhinged spirit — very much intact,” Cobb exclusively told Us. “The first scene that I got to see them film was the skeet shooting scene — and that scene to me in the book was near and dear to my heart. So I was so happy that it stayed and it was the first one I got to see.”.

Cobb called it an “out of body experience” watching her scenes come to life, adding, “So that was really really cool because that was Sophie’s initiation into this world. That’s the moment where she’s going to cross over and decide to descend with the hunting wives.”.

Recent findings suggest that based on Cobb’s book of the same name, The Hunting Wives introduced Sophie (Brittany Snow), who moved from Boston to an East Texas town where she found herself drawn to prominent socialite Margo (Malin Åkerman). While some became invested in the murder at the center of the series, The Hunting Wives also provided plenty of entertainment with guns, drinking and steamy onscreen hookups.

“Rebecca told me when we first met that she was going to change up the ending to where Margo lives. My response to her was, ‘Where were you when I was writing this book? That’s so much better,'” Cobb recalled. “So I was so happy with that, I was just so happy with all the story they added — making Dermot Mulroney’s character Jed a fully-fleshed character. He really didn’t have much of a presence in the book and then now he’s running for governor and I thought that was such a cool story line. I thought that was also genius.”.

According to an official synopsis, season 2 of The Hunting Wives picks up with Sophie and Margo “on the outs.”.

“Rebecca, the writers, the cast and just everyone involved creatively have turned a show that was already at an 11 and dialed it up even more exponentially,” Cobb teased. “There are higher stakes. It’s just wild.”.

In addition to The Hunting Wives returning on Thursday, November 26, Cobb is continuing to expand her book universe with a sequel to All the Little Houses — which is also about to be brought to life on screen.

“When I was writing All the Little Houses, I got almost almost halfway through when I realized I had way too much for one book — and way way too much drama — so I knew that I needed to make it a series,” Cobb exclusively told Us about All the Little Secrets, which is out now.
