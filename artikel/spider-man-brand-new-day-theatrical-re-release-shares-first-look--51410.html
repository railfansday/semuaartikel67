In a recent development, spider-Man: Brand Newly introduced Day is returning to theaters with a brand new theatrical cut... and Rosario Dawson's previously deleted Claire Temple scene in tow.

It has been revealed that "It's a brand new BRAND NEW DAY with our friendly neighborhood Spider-Man!" Dawson wrote, via Variety. "So excited to enjoy this record-breaking movie on the big screen (starting on December 4th) with an extended cut of new scenes and surprises… Ahhhh! Join me in getting tickets NOW!!!".

You can see the first look photo of the previously deleted scene below, which demonstrates Peter Parker and Claire aka Night Nurse, in a Latest York hospital. No idea where this scene will fit into the film exactly, but probably sometime after he meets with Yelena.

According to recent reports, in case you need a refresher, Claire Temple made an appearance in all of Netflix's Marvel projects, including Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, and, ultimately, The Defenders. She began her character arc as a night shift nurse who took care of Upcoming York's vigilantes, including Daredevil whenever he was injured after some late night drama.
