Youssouf Fofana continues to impress with Sevilla, and AC Milan are already thinking about what to do ahead of next summer.

It has been revealed that as Calciomercato.com write, Fofana put in a solid performance in Saturday’s Seville derby at the Estadio Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán, which finished 1-1. He was positioned alongside Lucien Agoume in the double pivot at first, but as the game went on he took up more of a box-to-box role.

The home side’s first chance came from him, around the sixth minute, following a long-range free kick that went just over the top of the net defended by Alvaro Valles. He was a constant presence in the Betis penalty area, operating even behind the striker at times.

After Antony’s opening goal, Fofana continued to push forward in the second half, coming close to equalising in the 82nd minute. Sevilla did draw level in the 91st minute thanks to a powerful left-footed strike from Kike Salas that Valles failed to hold.

Fofana experienced a frantic end to the summer window after Ruben Amorim opted to exclude him from Milan’s intends. A move to Lyon collapsed on deadline day, allowing Sevilla to swoop and land him on a dry loan for the season.

In a recent development, the Frenchman has racked up 10 appearances in total in LaLiga, nine of them as a starter. He has been described as a ‘gift’ by his head coach, having scored a goal against Barcelona and having amassed three assists, provided against Levante and Osasuna.

What does the future hold beyond 2026-27? In July, barring any surprises, he will return to Milan to discuss the next steps. Having contract expiring in 2028 remains a factor in the background, and renewal right now appears unlikely with Juventus, Spurs and Manchester United all linked in recent weeks.
