📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

E.J. Carroll, a veteran character actor whose credits include The Deuce, Boardwalk Empire, Blue Bloods three Law & Order series and Grand Theft Auto V, died November 19. He was 66. His rep Jon Mason of Mason-Lewis Entertainment stated Carroll died suddenly while en route to a voice-over booking, but he did not provide a cause of death.

In a recent development, born on May 21, 1960, Carroll appeared in features, television, short films and video games during a nearly three-decade screen career. His first screen credit was playing a cop in a 1998 episode of NBC’s Law & Order, and he would appear as other characters in three other episodes through 2004. He also had roles in two early-2000s episodes of its juggernaut spinoff Law & Order: SVU and two more in Law & Order: Criminal Intent that decade.

He would appear in a number of movies during the later 2000s and into the 2010s including Darren Aronfsky’s acclaimed 2009 drama The Wrestler, along with The Cake Eaters, The Capers, The Winning Season, The Hungry Ghosts, A Fish Story and the Robert De Niro-Kate Beckinsale pic Everything’s Fine, among others.

Carroll played Sheriff Victor Sickles in two 2012 episodes of HBO’s lauded period crime drama Boardwalk Empire, from Tenrence Winter and MArtin Scorsese and starring Steve Buscemi, appeared in episode of FX’s Glenn Close-Rose Byrne legal thriller Damages that same year. He went on to guest on NBC’s The Blacklist, CBS’ Blue Bloods, HBO’s High Maintenance, FX’s Pose and Netflix’s Marvel series The Punisher.

It has been revealed that he landed perhaps his most recognizable role in 2017, recurring on the HBO’ ‘s The Deuce as Fat Mooney, who ran a Upcoming York City sex shop owned by Frankie Martino (James Franco). He appeared in a half-dozen episodes of the series about the rise of the porn industry in the 1970s and ’80s.

Recent findings suggest that most recently, Carroll filmed a role in A Delicate Arrangement, the upcoming film written and produced by Jayce Bartok and directed by Nick Sandow. It stars Carla Gugino as Vivien Leigh in the true story of two-time Oscar winning actress’ relationship with a Philadelphia florist (Matthew Modine). David Hyde Pierce also stars in the film, which is in post-production.

“E.J. Carroll was a consummate actor known and loved for his intense commitment to the characters he played,” noted who noted that Carroll was passionate about A Delicate Arrangement and helped ensure the project would be completed. “There isn’t an actor in Newly introduced York that hadn’t worked with or crossed paths with E.J., known for stealing scenes alongside Robert De Niro, Mickey Rourke, Bruce Dern and most recently Jalen Brunson and Quinta Brunson. He will be greatly missed by the acting community which he lived for.”.
