If someone on your shopping list picked up an iPhone Duo, a case is the first thing they need, and it’s also the hardest one to get right. As we’ve seen with Android foldables, you’re protecting two halves, a hinge, and a phone that changes shape throughout the day as you use it, all without adding bulk to a device people bought because it folds thin. ESR has two options for iPhone Duo owners, and the choice mostly comes down to the look you want to achieve.

Sources indicate that the ESR Classic Hybrid Magnetic Case with Camera Control and Flex Stand is your choice if you want to show off your iPhone Duo’s colors. It combines a rigid polycarbonate and acrylic back with a softer TPU frame. The rigid back keeps the case from flexing and lets the iPhone Duo’s design show through, while the flexible frame absorbs the shock if you drop it (please don’t).

In a recent development, the ESR Atelier Vegan Leather Magnetic Case with Flex Stand is perfect for those who want a more premium look for their iPhone Duo. It’s made from organic silicone leather with a soft, skin-friendly feel and a stain-resistant finish. If you’ve ever used a light-colored leather case before, you know it usually shows every stain and smudge it comes across, but ESR built the Atelier to resist daily wear and wipe clean.

In a recent development, both cases share ESR’s Flex Stand, and it’s one of the most innovative and versatile parts of the lineup. It’s a 180-degree rotating stand made from zinc alloy, so it holds its shape even after months of propping up your iPhone. A precision hinge with damping control lets it open smoothly and lock at the angle you choose, whether the iPhone Duo is folded or unfolded, and ESR claims it has passed 3,000 open-and-close tests. In real life, that means propping the iPhone Duo open on a desk for a video call or on an airplane tray table for a movie, then using the stand as a grip when you’re holding it.

Both cases add extra hinge protection and raised edges where a foldable is most exposed. If you follow our MagSafe Monday column, you know magnetic strength really matters for MagSafe wallets, car mounts, etc. The Classic Hybrid has 1,500g of magnetic hold, and the Atelier has 1,400g.

The same two incredible cases come in iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max versions, and they also fit the iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max. The ESR Classic Hybrid Magnetic Case with Camera Control and Flex Stand has a clear back, a TPU frame, and a PU anti-yellowing coating. In simple terms, TPU is the flexible, shock-absorbing plastic used in most case frames, and it’s also what usually turns clear cases slightly yellow the longer you use them, so ESR adds a polyurethane (PU) coating to shield it. ESR upgraded the case’s UV protection for longer-lasting resistance to yellowing, and it’s SGS-certified for anti-yellowing. A clear case is supposed to show off your iPhone’s color, so staying clear longer is the whole point. The ESR Atelier Vegan Leather Magnetic Case with Flex Stand retains the same soft silicone leather and stain-resistant finish as the iPhone Duo version.

It has been revealed that on these models, the Flex Stand adjusts from 15 to 69 degrees for portrait and landscape viewing. That has you covered for StandBy mode on your nightstand and a show over your lunch break, with no separate stand to carry around.

Protection is where the two cases differ. ESR rates the Classic Hybrid for 4x military-grade protection with 16-foot drop protection, plus a full-coverage camera bar. The Atelier is rated for 3x military-grade protection, including 12-foot drop protection, and features a soft microfiber lining. Military-grade refers to the MIL-STD-810 drop test, which uses a 4-foot drop, so 4x and 3x mean ESR rates the cases for four and three times that height. Both include a built-in Camera Control cover and raised edges, and both use a precise magnetic ring layout that automatically aligns with MagSafe chargers, car mounts, wallets, and batteries.

For a student, that means taking notes in class with the keyboard attached, then detaching it to annotate a PDF or review flashcards on the couch.
