Opinion: Sensational player has officially announced himself as Liverpool's newest star

Liverpool have a new star. One player has officially declared himself as a key player with a fantastic performance.

According to recent reports, liverpool went into this season with a very imbalanced squad. It was one that required a lot from newly introduced head coach Andoni Iraola, essentially asking him to improve and experiment with players to get the most out of it.

Of course, that's largely the job of a head coach but it's rare that so much experimentation was necessary from a UEFA Champions League side that won the Premier League two seasons ago. But that's what Liverpool offered Iraola after two transfer windows that tore up large portions of the team.

No defensive midfielders, no recognised right-back, four left-wingers, no right-wingers, and one fit centre-forward. Hardly a 'win now' group of players. Iraola, though, has worked some real magic.

According to recent reports, and that includes solving the defensive midfielder problem. At least for now.

Sources indicate that the midfielder problem was a surprise one. We fully expected Liverpool to invest in the middle of the pitch over the summer but they simply ignored the issue. Even more bizarre was a decision to sell Curtis Jones without any sort of replacement.

It left Iraola short but it's a problem he appears to have fixed. Trey Nyoni has stepped up incredibly well, moving from a mere youth player who hoped for minutes to a genuine starting threat in the span of just a few months.

Wednesday night, though, felt like an announcement of sorts. Nyoni started away from home in the UEFA Champions League and thrived, holding down the midfield against a rampant Club Brugge.

Liverpool’s clean sheet made them the first team to hold out against Brugge for a very, very long time. The first team to beat Brugge in months, too.

Nyoni played his part. He wasn’t necessarily a defensive force by the way he won tackles here, it was more about his efforts on the ball to keep Liverpool ticking over.

92 per cent pass completion away from home in the Champions League is fantastic. It’s even more fantastic that he did it while completing 11 line-breaking passes - this wasn’t a case of Nyoni simply picking the simple ball every time.

Industry observers note that that 11 was the third-highest figure in the match and second-highest for Liverpool (Jeremy Jacquet had 13). It’s exactly the kind of midfield play that the Reds have been crying out for and doing it at a difficult ground in the premiere competition is just outstanding from a 19-year-old.

Nyoni is special and this felt like an announcement. Confirmation that he is a proper star player and one Liverpool can count on in major fixtures.
