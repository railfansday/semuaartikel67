Recent findings suggest that 2026 has been a knockout year for Harlan Coben, dating all the way back to the first few days of the year when his show, Run Away, went toe-to-toe with Netflix at the top of streaming charts. Later in the year, Coben returned to Netflix to debut his latest series, I Will Find You, but no one predicted that the show was going to become such a hit. Starring Sam Worthington (Avatar: Fire and Ash) and Britt Lower (Severance), I Will Find You earned over 100 million views during its first 90 days on streaming, which was enough to make it one of the most-watched shows of the entire year. Coben is also set to return to Netflix sometime in 2027 with a new adaptation of his best-selling novel ever, Myron Bolitar, which stars KJ Apa.

Harlan Coben has a healthy partnership with Netflix, and while he has had other projects over the years, such as Lazarus, stream on different platforms like Prime Video, most of his shows can be found on Netflix. This afternoon at the Texas Book Festival in Austin, Netflix announced yet another Harlan Coben project that's officially coming soon, which is an adaptation of his novel, The Innocent. This news is especially exciting for fans of I Will Find You, as it brings back creator Robert Hull, who also served as the lead writer and producer on I Will Find You alongside Coben. Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien, who also worked on I Will Find You, have been tapped to executive produce The Innocent. The book, which was released in 2005, follows a man named Matt who goes to jail after breaking up a bar fight. Years later after getting out, he receives a distressing call that turns his life upside down.
