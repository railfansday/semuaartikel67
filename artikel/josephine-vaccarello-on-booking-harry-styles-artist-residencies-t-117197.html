Industry observers note that from Radio City Music Hall to Madison Square Garden and Sphere, Josephine Vaccarello has had a front-row seat to the evolution of live entertainment. The president of MSG Entertainment and Billboard’s 2026 Touring Executive of the Year joins Billboard On The Record host Kristin Robinson live from the Billboard Live Music Summit to discuss how the live-music landscape is changing, from the rise of immersive concert experiences and artist residencies to growing fan expectations. Following a record-breaking summer at Madison Square Garden, Vaccarello offers an inside look at what it takes to bring the world’s biggest artists to iconic venues and book long-term residencies.
