It has been revealed that UPCOMING YORK, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Payoneer, the global financial technology organization powering business growth across borders, today confirmed that it has received preliminary conditional approval from the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) to form PAYO Digital Bank, a national trust bank designed to support stablecoin functionality for global business. Today's approval creates a path for Payoneer to build a federally regulated foundation for the future of cross-border commerce, supporting the businesses as they adopt stablecoins and simplifying how they pay and get paid globally. PAYO Digital Bank is now able to operate in its organizational phase, while it seeks final approval to open for business.

Sources indicate that once PAYO Digital Bank receives final OCC approval to open and concern payment stablecoins, it aims to issue PAYO-USD, a fully reserved, GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin. PAYO-USD, which will not be listed on exchanges, will serve as a balance within the Payoneer ecosystem that will drive interoperability between traditional and blockchain-based currencies. This delivers real-world utility, giving businesses access to safe stablecoin balances and allowing for frictionless movement into local currencies where they operate. This builds on Payoneer's existing network, delivering local currency payouts in 190+ markets.

It has been revealed that "The promise of stablecoin is to simplify global commerce. That can only happen when stablecoin is useful in the markets where our customers operate," stated Rob Morgan, CEO of PAYO Digital Bank. "Morer than one third of our customers already use stablecoin today. PAYO-USD is designed to improve the experience, making it seamless for businesses to convert their stablecoin balances into the local currencies needed to run their business, giving them more choice, faster movement of funds, and an easier way to transact globally.".

PAYO Digital Bank will offer a wide range of stablecoin services, allowing customers to hold stablecoin balances in bank-approved digital wallets, enable businesses to pay or be paid in a variety of third-party stablecoins, and convert their balances into fiat in the local markets where they operate. The bank will operate under direct federal oversight, enabling global businesses to adopt stablecoin payments from a safe and trusted partner.

Recent findings suggest that payoneer is the financial platform for cross-border business and global payments. Payoneer empowers millions of businesses with the financial tools and services they need to grow and transact globally with confidence. Payoneer makes it easier for businesses, particularly in emerging markets, to connect to the global economy, pay and get paid across borders, manage their funds across multiple currencies, and grow their businesses.
