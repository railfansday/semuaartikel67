SAN JOSE, Calif., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Market Updates News Commentary - Cybersecurity stocks moved higher on Thursday. Palo Alto Networks reported fiscal first-quarter results after Wednesday's close and raised its annual forecast, and Reuters reported that the outlook lifted cybersecurity stocks as investors bet companies will spend big on defending against sophisticated AI-enabled digital attacks. The move came in a session where the major U.S. indexes spent the afternoon sharply lower as Anthropic PBC made its Nasdaq debut. With two more security names reporting in the first week of December, the group heads into month-end with fresh numbers on the board. Active Companies from around the markets with current developments this week include: Palo Alto Networks, Inc. (NASDAQ: PANW), Zscaler, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZS), SentinelOne, Inc. (NYSE: S), Okta, Inc. (NASDAQ: OKTA), Cloudflare, Inc. (NYSE: NET).

Reuters described AI as reshaping the cyber arms race, helping attackers find and exploit vulnerabilities faster while increasing demand for software that can identify, block and respond to threats. The five companies below each report the business a little differently, through Next-Generation Security ARR, total ARR or remaining performance obligations, and their latest releases show where each one stands going into the next round of reports.

In a recent development, palo Alto Networks, Inc. (NASDAQ: PANW) reported fiscal first-quarter 2027 results for the quarter ended October 31, 2026, with total revenue up 39% year over year to $3.44 billion and Next-Generation Security ARR up 66% to $9.73 billion. Remaining performance obligations grew 35% to $21.0 billion, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.05, compared with $0.93 a year earlier.

For fiscal 2027, the company now expects Next-Generation Security ARR of $11.475 billion to $11.525 billion, total revenue of $14.52 billion to $14.57 billion and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $4.26 to $4.30. Palo Alto Networks also declared its intent to acquire Applied Compute, a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models.

Zscaler, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZS) reaffirmed its first-quarter and full-year fiscal 2027 guidance at its 2026 Investor Day on October 6. For the first quarter, it expects revenue of $935 million to $939 million and non-GAAP net income per share of $1.15 to $1.16. For fiscal 2027, it expects ARR of $4.396 billion to $4.426 billion and revenue of $3.908 billion to $3.938 billion.

According to recent reports, sentinelOne, Inc. (NYSE: S) will release results for its fiscal third quarter 2027, which ended October 31, 2026, after market close on Thursday, December 3, 2026. In its most recent quarter, the second quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue grew 21% year over year to $292 million and ARR grew 22% to $1.218 billion, and the company raised its revenue and operating income outlook for fiscal 2027. Earnings materials are posted on its investor relations site.

According to recent reports, okta, Inc. (NASDAQ: OKTA) will release results for its third quarter of fiscal 2027, ended October 31, 2026, after the U.S. market close on Tuesday, December 1, 2026. In the second quarter, Okta reported GAAP net income of $116 million, compared with $67 million a year earlier, and RPO of $4.858 billion, up 17% year over year. The release and webcast will be available on its investor relations site.

Sources indicate that cloudflare, Inc. (NYSE: NET) reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $782.5 million, up 39% year over year, with non-GAAP income from operations of $137.1 million, or 18% of revenue, and current RPO growth of 37%. The firm said the quarter was its best ever and that it crossed $3 billion in annualized revenue.
