While Pixel Weather serves my needs quite well, I’ve been looking for a way to check Apple Weather (previously Dark Sky) forecast data on Android, and I’ve finally found it with Hello Weather by Electric Dream Machine.

Hello Weather started out on iOS before coming to Android a few years ago. On Apple devices, the app got a massive redesign last year, and it just came to Android last month with a promise that updates will roll out to both platforms at the same time going forward.

According to recent reports, the app has a four-tab layout with the bottom bar aligning with the latest Material 3 Expressive height, avoiding a common pitfall. One small customization is being able to turn on/off tab names.

According to recent reports, locations reveals a list of all your saved cities, while Forecast is the main view. The current condition with a large icon — five different sets are available — appears first. Be sure to enable the stats row so you can see cards like Wind, Cloud Cover, and UV Index without having to tap for the bottom sheet. When open, you get the following stats presented as a grid: Today (temperature range), Precipitation, Wind, Humidity, UV Index, Cloud Cover, Visibility, Pressure, Air Quality, Pollen, Daylight, and Moon Phase.

Industry observers note that the Hourly Forecast section is a scrollable carousel, with the Bonus Info toggle in Settings adding another stat. “Automatic” will intelligently select “relevant info for you.” You can tap to browse the hourly breakdown in a sheet view.

Finally, there’s the Daily Forecast section that gives you an eight-day overview via a temperature-range layout, which is my favorite way to visualize this information. Again, this can be tapped to get a bottom sheet explorer.

I've lost count on how many weather apps on Android I've tried out over the years but since the Pixel Weather app became a thing I've tried far fewer and the few I have tried while many have been prettier to look at, I've always ended up coming back to the stock PW app. It never hurts to have options though and this one looks like a good one. Thanks for the mention, I hadn't heard of it before.

In a recent development, the Radar tab is up next, with Google Maps powering this view. From the three-dot overflow menu, you can customize the Animation Speed, Map Layers, Style, and Options.

In a recent development, settings is where you can make all the aforementioned customizations, including changing which Forecast Data source you’re using. The usual players are here, like The Weather Channel and AccuWeather. I also like the stylized in-app changelog.

In Notifications, you can have a persistent silent alert appear. There’s also the ability to receive Morning and Evening Reports, along with Precipitation and Severe Alerts. This is joined by a trio of homescreen widgets.
