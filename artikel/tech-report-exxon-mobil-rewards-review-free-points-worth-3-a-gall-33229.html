📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, join Exxon Mobil Rewards+ if most of your gas comes from Exxon or Mobil, since the free program pays 3 cents a gallon in points on regular and 6 on premium. Points expire on Dec. 31 of the year after you earn them, so use them up at least once a year.

You earn and spend the points at participating Exxon and Mobil stations, where 100 points take $1 off gas, a car wash, or snacks. At 3 cents a gallon, the 423 gallons an average car or small SUV burned in 2024, by the Federal Highway Administration’s count, earn about $13 in points a year.

Exxon and Mobil regulars get the points free, and paying from checking with Direct Debit+ adds 10 cents off, for 13 cents a gallon in all on regular. Walmart+ members who pay by card save more at these pumps in the Walmart app, whose 10 cents a gallon off beats 3 cents in points.
