Breaking: “Lows” Gang Member Sentenced to 10 Years in Prison For His Participation in a RICO Conspiracy

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that A federal judge sentenced Deontae Jackson, 37, of Minneapolis to 10 years in prison for his role in a Racketeering Influenced Corrupt Organization (RICO) conspiracy that included violence and fentanyl distribution. Jackson is the first of 14 defendants sentenced in this case, U.S. v. Alowonle et al. (24-cr-293).

According to court documents, beginning on or about 2009 and continuing through at least April 2026, Jackson was a member of and associated with the Lows gang. The Lows gang does not have a formal structure or initiation process as it is comprised of several affiliated subsets. Members would prove their loyalty by “putting in work,” which included committing acts of violence and drug trafficking.

Industry observers note that between February 2023 and May 2024, Jackson sold fentanyl as part of, and to benefit, the Lows gang. The Lows drug trafficking activities during this period involved over 1.2 kilograms of fentanyl.

In a recent development, on April 26, 2024, law enforcement searched a home in Fridley, Minnesota, where Jackson was staying. In Jackson’s bedroom, officers found approximately 945 M-30 fentanyl pills, which collectively weighed around 94.5 grams. At the time of the search, Jackson was on electronic court monitoring in connection with his conviction for a gang-related drive-by shooting. After the search, Jackson removed his ankle monitor to avoid having his location tracked.

Sources indicate that in May 2024, law enforcement tracked Jackson to Bismarck, North Dakota. Officers then searched Jackson’s hotel room where he was staying with a fellow Lows member and two others. Police recovered a bag containing 2,599 M-30 fentanyl pills that weighed approximately 270.4 grams. In a second bag, officers found 20 more M-30 fentanyl pills that weighed 2.08 grams.

According to recent reports, assistant Attorney General A. Tysen Duva of the Justice Department’s Criminal Division; Special Agent in Charge Joseph Persails of the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) St. Paul Field Division; Special Agent in Charge Christopher D. Dotson of the FBI Minneapolis Field Office; Acting Special Agent in Charge Rafael Mattei of the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) Omaha Field Division; Acting Special Agent in Charge Robert J. Kuszynski of the IRS Criminal Investigation (IRS-CI) Chicago Field Office; Special Agent in Charge Travis Pickard of the Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) St. Paul Field Office; and Inspector in Charge Brian Musgrove of the U.S. Postal Inspection Service (USPIS) Denver Division made the announcement.
