Mysterious GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek has reemerged in the wake of GTA 6's release with another leak: This time, they have released what they claim to be a near-complete build of GTA 6 on Xbox from November 2026.

In their post, Cyberleek states they'll drop the one file they've withheld—the main executable—if their crypto coin reaches a $15 million market cap. Yeah, we're doing this again. Comments on a now-removed Reddit post say the leaked file is around 200 GB, which is close to the size of GTA 6 on consoles.

Cyberleek claims they will release the exe on November 23, no matter what.

"It is an Xbox build this time, not a PC build. CL does not care. This community will have it emulated inside a week, running far past 30fps. CL told you not to preorder for a reason. This is one of the reasons," the posts read.

"If the Xbox build cannot be made to work and support holds, CL will work to get a PS5 build—a fallback, most likely never required. Either way: the Xbox build is going out. No double dipping for Take-Two/Rockstar.".

Last we heard from Cyberleek was over a month ago, when they were threatening Take-Two with a "dead man's switch", which many took to mean they'd either post major story spoilers or leak the game in its entirety.

According to recent reports, we've yet to see confirmation that the build is genuine, but that could come as soon as tonight. If it is, it'll be anywhere between a few days or hours before the executable comes: According to Cyberleek's site, their crypto right now sits at a $1.83 million cap.
