A “Captain Phillips” reunion is upon the Best Live Action Short Oscar race.

Screenwriter Billy Ray (“The Hunger Games,” “Shattered Glass,” “Richard Jewell,” Nicole Kidman’s AMC ad), who penned the 2013 Oscar-nominated “Captain Phillips, has boarded the film “Magan’s Fare” as an executive producer. The upcoming film stars Barkhad Abdi, the Somali actor who broke out in the Paul Greengrass film and earned a Best Supporting Actor Oscar nomination.

In a recent development, directed by Christopher Knoblock and Dana White, “Magan’s Fare” is vying in the Best Live Action Short race this year, having qualified out of the Raindance Film Festival where it won the top short prize. The 26-minute drama stars Abdi as Magan, “a Somali-born rideshare driver who takes an older woman, Jorani (Alexis Rhee), back to her nursing home, only to discover the facility has given away her bed. With his passenger suddenly displaced and no one else willing to take responsibility for her, Magan must figure out what to do next,” per the announcement.

It has been revealed that “The last two years have demonstrated on a grand level that small businesses need more help than big ones do,” Ray stated at a recent Los Angeles screening of the film. “Well, in our community, small movies need more help than big ones do. I’m happy to be helping this small movie. I hope all of you will too. It’s so beautifully written, it’s so beautifully directed, it’s so beautifully performed. The music, the cinematography, all of it, just executed with such love and care, and integrity.”.

It has been revealed that knoblock and White added, “We are deeply honored to have Billy Ray join our ‘Magan’s Fare’ FYC campaign as an executive producer. Billy is a remarkable storyteller whose writing creates complex, deeply human characters that stay with you long after the story ends. His work is an inspiration to us, and we’re especially excited by the opportunity to bring Billy and Barkhad Abdi back together through a film that speaks to the power of compassion and our responsibility to one another. Having Billy’s support means a great deal to us and to the heart of this film.”.
