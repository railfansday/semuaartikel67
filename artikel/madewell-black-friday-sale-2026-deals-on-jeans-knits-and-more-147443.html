In a recent development, when Madewell rolled out a tightly-edited menswear collection a few years back, it seemed like a fun experiment at best, and a way to occupy otherwise-bored boyfriends at worst. But in the intervening years, the label has emerged as one of the most consistent purveyors of eminently-wearable, negative-fuss menswear on the planet. What’s more, even fewer brands are easier to integrate into a fully-formed wardrobe or provide as many foolproof paths to build one from the ground up. So with that in mind, virtually any discount that’s offered is a welcome one. This Black Friday, you can get yourself a whole-ass 40% off the entire site, even already discounted inventory.

40%. Whole site. Does that include the timelessly relaxed denim? Well, that’s on the site, so yes. How about the cozy-ass sweats? Same deal, on the site. The classic, unfussy outerwear? Also on the site, also yes. You get the idea.

And because the sales are fleeting and the inventory is seemingly endless, we thought it might be helpful to highlight some of our favorites down below. Otherwise, head on over to (you guessed it) the Madewell site to check it all out.

What are the best things to buy at Madewell?

Madewell jeans and pants are some of the best you can find, regardless of pricepoint, so we’d recommend starting there. But their shirting is great and varied, their knits are also fantastic, and we would recommend you keep an eye on their wide range of outerwear. The brand's suit selection, while limited, features one of the best full-fitting options available.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 28, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.

Will Madewell have a Cyber Monday Sale?

If last year is any indication, they will, but we can’t guarantee that the deals will be the same or that the selection will be, either, which means that you shouldn’t wait until Monday. If you see something you’re into add it to your cart and checkout before your size or the deal itself is over.
