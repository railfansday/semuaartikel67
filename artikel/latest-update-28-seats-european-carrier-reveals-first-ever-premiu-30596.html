Irish flag carrier Aer Lingus has become the latest airline to join the premium economy revolution, with the airline having just unveiled its upcoming product offering ahead of its launch next October. It plans to fit 28 spacious recliner seats on ten of its Airbus A330 widebody twinjets. This will represent the bulk of its twin-aisle fleet, with ch-aviation showing that it currently has two Airbus A330-200s and 12 A330-300s.

While the introduction of premium economy for the first time in the Irish flag carrier's history is the main headline, Aer Lingus will also use this refurbishment as an opportunity for a nose-to-tail reimagining of its A330 interiors, with business and economy class also changing. Passengers flying on Aer Lingus' US routes will be the first to benefit from the patch, with Dublin to New York JFK being the first route.

Aer Lingus has chosen the RECARO R4 seat for its new premium economy cabin on the Airbus A330, which will be able to accommodate 28 passengers in four seven-abreast (2-3-2) rows. These flyers will be able to look forward to an increased seat pitch of 38 inches, with the seats measuring 18-19 inches in width. They will also offer guests a greater deal of recline, at eight inches. The soft product is strong too.

Recent findings suggest that on this front, Aer Lingus says that its premium economy passengers can expect, among other things, "an upgraded three-course dining experience, complimentary drinks throughout the flight, comfortable bedding, an amenity kit and noise-reducing headphones." All the while, Starlink WiFi will help them to stay more connected inflight than ever before. Lynne Embleton, the CEO of Aer Lingus, remarked that:.

Sources indicate that "Our ambition here is simple. We want to give our customers greater choice, comfort, and connectivity, while creating an experience that feels unmistakably Aer Lingus.".

Sources indicate that it is no secret in the world of modern commercial aviation that the transatlantic market between Europe and North America is one of the most lucrative corridors around. As such, premium demand is often high, and Aer Lingus is set to tap into this trend by launching its newly fitted A330s on the long-haul route from Dublin (DUB) to New York (JFK). These flights will begin in just under a year, on October 1, 2027.

According to recent reports, initially, Aer Lingus looks to deploy its premium economy-equipped A330s on the EI104/105 rotation. These flights are operated today by the A330-300, which is the larger and more numerous of Aer Lingus' two variants of the Airbus A330. At the time of writing, these widebody twinjet aircraft feature a two-class configuration with 30 business class flatbeds and either 283 or 287 economy class seats on board.

As more and more of Aer Lingus' Airbus A330s are retrofitted with the fresh cabins, the carrier will be able to roll the product out on a growing variety of, as it puts it, "additional North American routes." According to Cirium, an aviation analytics firm, its present A330 services across the pond from Dublin serve Boston, Chicago, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Miami, New York, Orlando, and San Francisco.

It has been revealed that with ten of Aer Lingus' A330s being taken out of service for the premium economy refit, this presents an opportunity for the Irish flag carrier to undertake a nose-to-tail reimagining of its widebody twinjet fleet. This is exactly what it looks to do, hence the aforementioned addition of Starlink WiFi. This will also see it refit its business class cabins with 28 Collins Elevation suites in a four-abreast 1-2-1 layout.
