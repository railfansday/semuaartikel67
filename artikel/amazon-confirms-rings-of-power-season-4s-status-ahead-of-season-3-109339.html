📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that the third season of Rings of Powe​​​​​​r, Amazon's Lord of the Rings series on Prime Video, is set to conclude on Tuesday, Nov. 24 with the release of its final two episodes. That is, of course, if you didn't catch the seventh episode early in theaters. Before the credits roll, Amazon has revealed that fans will be getting more of Rings of Power soon as the show has been renewed for a fourth season.

In a recent development, the announcement was made on the show's official social media accounts in a post that promised "Tonight's finale is only a beginning." Showrunners and executive producers J.D. Payne and Patrick McKay will return for season 4, bringing them one step closer to completing their stated five-season plan​​​​​​​. As reported by Deadline, season four is currently in pre-production and will begin filming at Shepperton Studios in the U.K. in the spring​​​​​​​.

It has been revealed that the show's renewal will come as a welcome, if somewhat surprising, piece of news to fans. While Rings of Power's​​​​​​​ first season made massive waves, perhaps because viewers were curious to see what the most expensive show in history would look like​​​​​​​, the series has steadily dropped off in popularity. Despite its less-than-stellar viewership​​​​​​​, Amazon seems committed to continuing the story.

Recent findings suggest that the latest Rings of Power season served as something of a soft reboot for the show. It began with a five-year time jump and a narrative focus more closely aligned with what fans may know from Peter Jackson's film trilogy​​​​​​​. The main storyline of season 3 followed Sauron's attempt to finally forge the One Ring. Amazon has revealed that the mission will ultimately be accomplished in episode 7, setting a upcoming status quo going forward.

In a recent development, season 3 has also leaned even more into​​​​​​​ the strange relationship between Sauron and Galadriel, with the added wrinkle of finally introducing the latter's husband, Celeborn​​​​​​​. Rings of Power isn't the only Lord of the Rings project in the works. Next year, The Hunt for Gollum will be released in theaters. The film takes place during the original trilogy and will see the return of Elijah Wood, Ian McKellen, and Andy Serkis (who also directs) alongside newcomers including Jamie Dornan​​​​​​​ as Aragorn.
