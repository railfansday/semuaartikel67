For Black Friday, Orion is taking $600 off every size of its smart cooling and heating mattress cover system, with no monthly subscription fee.

The system comes with two main parts: a thin Smart Cover (made with a breathable 60 percent polyester and 40 percent bamboo top knit) that stretches over any standard mattress between 10 and 18 inches deep like a fitted sheet, and a compact bedside Control Tower (24 by 9 inches) that plugs into a single outlet.

Industry observers note that the Control Tower circulates temperature-controlled water through anti-leak, tear-resistant tubing inside the cover, maintaining a temperature between 50 and 113 degrees Fahrenheit. Because it uses dual-zone control, each side of the bed can run its own temperature — so one person can sleep at 73 degrees while the other stays cozy at 82 degrees.

Sources indicate that you can set automated schedules in the Orion app, so your bed cools down before you get in, drops lower for deep sleep, and gently warms up before your alarm goes off. There is also a Hot Flash mode that drops your side's temperature fast before easing back to your schedule, plus a Quiet mode that turns down the tower's sound at night.

It has been revealed that built-in sensors track your heart rate, breathing rate, HRV, and movement without making you wear anything to bed, delivering sleep insights each morning. You only need to top off the tower's water once or twice a year with a purification tablet. And while the cover itself is spot-clean only, you just put your regular fitted sheet over it and wash it as usual.
