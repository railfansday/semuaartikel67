Here is a roundup of headlines from India from Wednesday, November 18:.

The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) lifted Stage III of the Graded Response Action Proposal (GRAP) across Delhi-National Capital Region on Tuesdayafter the capital's air quality improved, local media reported.

The commission's sub-committee withdrew its November 6 order – issued two days before the festival of Diwali – with immediate effect after Delhi's daily average Air Quality Index reading reached 204 (indicating "poor" air quality), the cleanest this month.

In a recent development, stage III was imposed on November 6, when the AQI hit 382. The 50% cap on office attendance has been withdrawn. Meanwhile, rules on older petrol and diesel vehicles have also been eased.

Stage I and II measures and the CAQM's October 30 order, which covered November 5 to 20, remain in force. Curbs on construction and demolition also continue.

The decision followed several days of steady improvement.

Leaders of the Quadrilateral Security Dialogue, which groups India, the US, Japan and Australia, met in Manila on Tuesday for their first summit in more than two years. It was the first Quad leaders' meeting of Donald Trump's current term.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Trump, Japan's Sanae Takaichi and Australia's Anthony Albanese held closed-door talks on the sidelines of the ASEAN meetings.

The joint statement said the leaders "strongly oppose any destabilizing or unilateral actions that seek to change the status quo, including by force or coercion, which escalate tensions and undermine regional peace and stability," which local media called a signal to China.

The Deccan Herald stated the language largely repeated earlier Quad statements but Trump avoided a strong message to Beijing ahead of his Thursday talks with Xi Jinping in Shenzhen.

The statement also condemned terrorism "in all its forms and manifestations, including cross-border terrorism." It was the Quad's first summit since the 2025 Pahalgam attack.

A fresh initiative called the Quad Business Leaders' Forum is set to be hosted by India in early 2027. Additionally, a joint Quad exercise by maritime patrol aircraft in 2027 and a new maritime surveillance collaboration have also been planned.

Industry observers note that "From maritime security to supply chains, upcoming technologies to disaster response, the Quad provides trusted solutions for the entire Indo-Pacific," Modi remarked. Meanwhile, Trump praised Modi as a "great leader.".

The Princess of Wales made a rare speaking appearance at the Earthshot Prize awards in Mumbai on Tuesday. "Their work will inspire and create seismic change that will improve lives for generations to come," Catherine stated of the winners.

Prince William, who founded the prize, mentioned in his keynote speech that history is shaped not only by challenges but also by the people who rise to meet them. The prize is now at the midpoint of its planned 10 years.

Each winner received £1 million ($1.3 million, 1.2 million euros). They included Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, the maker of the electric rickshaw William drove earlier with Catherine as a passenger.

Catherine presented the Protect and Restore Nature award to Bhutan, which has written nature protection into its constitution and keeps nearly 70% of its land under forest.

Actor Rami Malek and cricketer Sachin Tendulkar were among the presenters. The royal couple wore outfits by Indian designers: a Mahima Mahajan "anarkali" for Kate and a Manish Malhotra "bandhgala" jacket for William.

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi, during a conversation at Columbia University in Latest York City on Tuesday, mentioned that India faces a looming economic crisis.

Sources indicate that answering a question about India's youth, the opposition leader said: "The blunt thing is that India is sitting on an economic disaster. That's the reality. One can brush it aside, the media can brush it aside, but India's got a massive unemployment problem. India's got a massive problem with its education system.".

Prime Minister Narendra Modi's ruling Bharatiya Janata Party responded sharply, calling him the "leader of anti-India propaganda.".

Gandhi also reiterated his vote theft charge.

"140 million people who would have voted in 2024 have been removed from the electoral roll. We ask the Election Commission: how can an election be considered valid under these circumstances?" he was reported in Indian media as saying.

The Election Commission has rejected allegations of wrongdoing over the voter-roll revision.

In response, BJP spokesperson Pradeep Bhandari took to social media to say: "His inherent hate for India's progress and Indian democracy makes him speak ill about India every time he goes abroad!".

Gandhi, on a two-day US visit, is also due to visit the University of Chicago on Wednesday.
