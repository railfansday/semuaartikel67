‘Threads: My Songs in Symphony’ To Celebrate Dolly Parton’s Legacy on 2027 Orchestral Tour of U.S., U.K. and Australia

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The legacy of late country icon Dolly Parton will be celebrated on the road next year with the touring multimedia symphonic experience Dolly Parton’s Threads: My Songs in Symphony. The production from Schirmer Theatrical — part of Wise Music Group and Sony Music Publishing — will follow-up on the 2025 touring production that brought Parton’s songs and stories to audiences around the world.

Recent findings suggest that next year’s run of shows is slated to kick off on Jan. 12 in Rochester, N.Y. at the Kodak Hall at Eastman Theatre, followed by gigs in Seattle and Pittsburgh, before moving to the U.K. in March and April for gigs in Edinburgh, Glasgow, Manchester, Liverpool, Bristol, London and Birmingham. The run will then continue in Chicago and Baltimore in April and May and conclude with two shows in Australia in July.

“From our earliest creative conversations with Dolly, we envisioned Threads: My Songs in Symphony as a musical celebration that could connect audiences across the world,” noted Schirmer Theatrical senior vice president Betsey Perlmutter in a statement. “At its heart, this concert experience captures the warmth, heart and joy of an evening spent with Dolly, centered around her extraordinary songs and singular spirit. Taking Threads to major cities around the world is a natural evolution of that original vision, and we’re beyond proud to bring this one-of-a-kind experience to fans around the globe.”.

Recent findings suggest that tickets for the U.S. dates are available now here and here. Pre-sales for the U.K. shows kicked off on Wednesday (Nov. 18), with a general on-sale slated for Friday (Nov. 20) at 10 a.m. local time here.

Sources indicate that the show premiered in 2025 with the Nashville Symphony before touring 31 cities in North America. The orchestra for the production created and co-produced by Parton will be led by David Hamilton. According to a release, the “innovative multimedia symphonic experience features Dolly’s songs and stories accompanied by original video content, with Dolly herself appearing on-screen to lead audiences through a visual-musical journey.”.

It will also feature a cast of guest vocalists and musicians, selected by Parton, performing such iconic hits as “Jolene,” “Coat of Many Colors,” “I Will Always Love You” and some of Parton’s personal favorites.

In the meantime, Parton’s Broadway musical, DOLLY: A True Original Musical, is slated to being preview performances at the St. James Theatre in New York on Dec. 7, with opening night scheduled for Jan. 19, which would have been Parton’s 81st birthday. Parton died in August at 80 following a brief battle with cancer.
