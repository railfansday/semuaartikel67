Recent findings suggest that germany advanced to the Round 2 of the U17 European Championship qualification campaign after their 4-0 win over Northern Ireland helped them to a perfect record from their group. Bastian Assomo, who scored hat-tricks against Azerbaijan and Lithuania, netted the only goal before the break at the midpoint of the half.

Union Berlin's Linus Güther registered two assists after the restart for 15-year-old Shevin Matondo and Samuel Passariello, in addition to creating the best moment of the game just before the hour mark. Güther, who already featured in three Bundesliga games at the age of 16, joined the scoresheet with a beautiful curling effort from the outside of the box.

The winger has four goals and four assists in his first six games for Germany U17. Meanwhile, FC Bayern's striker Assomo scored eight goals in his first three appearances at this level.

Industry observers note that lithuania are the other team to progress to Round 2 from Group A2, while North Ireland and Azerbaijan will now compete in League B. The draw for the next round will be conducted next month in Nyon, Switzerland.
