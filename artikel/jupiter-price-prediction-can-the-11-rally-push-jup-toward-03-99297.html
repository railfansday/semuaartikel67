Jupiter Price Prediction: Can the 11% Rally Push JUP Toward $0.3?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

JUP's rebound from the demand zone has brought the price back toward a critical resistance area, but bulls still need a decisive breakout to strengthen the recovery.

A sustained move above $0.2593 could open the way toward $0.2712, while rejection may leave Jupiter price range-bound or expose lower support.

As the crypto markets turned bullish after experiencing a massive sell-off, the Jupiter price initiated a strong rebound. The token showed signs of a short-term recovery, pushing the price up more than 11% to reach $0.25. Besides, the derivative positions suggest the shorts remained tilted into the move, while the daily chart shows improving momentum. As the markets continue to remain highly volatile, the question is whether the current rebound can transform into a strong upswing and push the JUP price towards upper resistance.

It has been revealed that the daily chart shows early signs of improving momentum as the price rebounded after hitting the local support at $0.20. Moreover, the token is attempting to rise above a crucial ascending resistance level that has been acting as strong support since the August reversal. However, the bears are restricting the rally below the range, which raises concerns about the next price action.

Recent findings suggest that the Chainkink Money Flow (CMF) has turned largely positive as it sustains above the average levels. This indicates that buying pressure has strengthened as capital inflows are supporting the recovery. IF CMF continues to rise, the buyers are expected to gain control and turn the short-lived price rally into a major recovery. On the other hand, MACD is about to turn positive after a small bearish phase as the levels are heading for a bullish crossover.

Despite the improving technicals, the JUP price remains vulnerable to further corrections as the bears continue to restrict the rally below the ascending resistance.

Recent findings suggest that after the rebound, the token has entered a critical price range, with the volume profile highlighting substantial trading activity around $0.24 to $0.25. This concentration of traded volume makes the zone vital, as price may need to sustain buying pressure to move through it. This also suggests that JUP has seen considerable trading activity around current levels, indicating an area where market participants have previously exchanged positions. Hence, a move above $0.2593 could help bulls gain momentum.

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, the market order block and demand zone around $0.21 to $0.23 also remain relevant. Interestingly, the range between $0.24 and $0.27 is also worth watching, as it is one of the major supply zones. JUP remains caught between the two zones, as buyers defend price declines while sellers step in during upward moves, keeping the token range-bound. With JUP now recovering toward the upper boundary of its immediate trading range, bulls need to push through the $0.2593 resistance level to strengthen the recovery.

A breakout supported by sustained buying activity could shift the short-term structure, while rejection would keep the price caught between resistance and the lower demand region.

Bullish scenario: JUP’s recovery could extend if buyers sustain momentum and push the price above $0.2593. A confirmed breakout could bring $0.2712 into focus, followed by the 0.28–0.29 zone. Continued positive CMF readings and a confirmed MACD bullish crossover would provide additional momentum confirmation, although price acceptance above resistance remains the more important signal.

Industry observers note that bearish scenario: Failure to reclaim $0.2593 could trigger another rejection, pushing JUP back toward the 0.24–0.25 region. A sustained break below this area could expose the 0.21–0.23 demand zone. If the marked support fails to hold, the next downside levels to watch would be around $0.18 and, further down, $0.1582.

JUP’s 11% rebound has brought momentum indicators into a more constructive position, with CMF turning positive and the MACD approaching a bullish crossover. The reaction from the $0.21 to $0.23 demand region also suggests buyers are attempting to defend lower levels, but the token still faces resistance around $0.2593.

According to recent reports, A sustained breakout above this level could open the door toward $0.2712 and potentially the $0.28 to $0.29 region. However, until JUP clears resistance, the recovery remains tentative. Failure to sustain the rebound could send the price back toward the demand zone, leaving the broader trend vulnerable. The next move around $0.2593 may therefore determine whether JUP’s latest rally develops into a stronger recovery or fades into another range-bound move.
