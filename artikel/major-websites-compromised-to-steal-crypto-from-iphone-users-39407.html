A security researcher has discovered that some of the world’s most popular websites were compromised in an attempt to steal cryptocurrency from iPhone consumers.

It has been revealed that A total of 178 websites were affected, including BBC, CNN, HSBC, IKEA, and Zoom. Reasonably recent versions of iOS are safe, and so far it appears that only a few thousand dollars have been stolen ….

Reuters reports that hackers attacked Optimizely, a website optimization platform used by many major websites.

Several of the affected companies stated that they have disabled the Optimizely scripts and are investigating potential impacts. Helme says almost all of the rogue code has now been removed, and that Apple patched iOS after an identical attack method was detected last month.

The Coruna loader is byte-identical to a sample we analysed in October from a different campaign. The kit’s own comments say iOS 18.7.3 and later, and 26.3 and later, are patched.

Helme monitored the attacker’s wallets and discovered that only $7,040 has been stolen, though it’s not possible to see how many payment card numbers were obtained.

As always, the best protection against this type of attack is to promptly update all your Apple devices to the latest OS versions they support.
