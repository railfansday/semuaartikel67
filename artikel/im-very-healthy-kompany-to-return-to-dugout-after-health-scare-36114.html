Kompany was appointed Bayern Munich manager in May 2024.

Bayern Munich boss Vincent Kompany states he is "very healthy" and grateful for all of the support he has received as he prepares to return to the dugout on Friday.

The 40-year-old former Manchester City captain was taken to hospital after falling ill while watching the club's second team train last month.

Kompany was diagnosed with meningeal irritation - an inflammation of the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

Speaking at a news conference on Thursday he thanked the club, medical staff, players, coaches and fans for their support.

"It was very intense," he stated.

In a recent development, "At the time, it was a new experience. Looking back, everything went perfectly. I received support right away, from the club and the doctors. I'm very grateful.

According to recent reports, "You try to stay calm and wait until you have the results. It was a positive process, all the tests were positive.

In a recent development, "I have to say, I'm very healthy. We did a lot of tests, and they all came back fine. In those quiet moments, you also try to learn.

"Now I feel very good again and I'm happy to be back with the team.".

While he has been recovering Kompany's assistant Aaron Danks has led the side to four wins and a draw.

It has been revealed that "I'd also like to thank the coaching community," Kompany added. "We're always competing, but almost every coach has reached out to me, even if we've played against each other and perhaps argued as well. That's a beautiful thing.".

Bayern, for whom England captain Harry Kane has been in fine form, are top of the Bundesliga, level on points with Borussia Dortmund.

Defending champions Bayern face Cologne on Friday after the end of the two-week international break.
