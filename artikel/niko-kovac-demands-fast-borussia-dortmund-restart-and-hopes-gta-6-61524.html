Niko Kovac demands fast Borussia Dortmund restart, and hopes GTA 6 doesn’t get in the way

Niko Kovac had an unusual concern to contend with ahead of Borussia Dortmund’s return to Bundesliga action: the release of Grand Theft Auto 6.

Asked at Thursday’s press conference whether the eagerly anticipated video game had become a major topic inside the BVB dressing room, Kovac joked that he hoped none of his players had been staying up deep into the night playing it.

The Dortmund head coach conceded that several members of his squad were likely already heavily involved with the game, quipping that he hoped they would not “confuse day and night” ahead of Saturday’s trip to Borussia Mönchengladbach.

The light-hearted exchange came at the end of a press conference otherwise dominated by Dortmund’s need to hit the ground running following the international break.

In a recent development, BVB travel to bottom-of-the-table Gladbach on Saturday at 15:30 CET with 21 points from their opening nine Bundesliga matches. Kovac’s side remain unbeaten in league play and sit second, level on points with leaders Bayern Munich. Dortmund have also conceded only seven goals, the best defensive record in the division.

Industry observers note that “We can’t afford to take any time to get going,” Kovac noted. “There are still eight games left this year. In these games, we want to cement our place at the top of the Bundesliga. The other teams are pushing from behind. We have to be ready right away.”.

Industry observers note that saturday’s match begins another demanding stretch for Dortmund. BVB face eight competitive fixtures in 29 days before the winter break, having only just completed a run of nine matches across 29 of days prior to the November international break.

The experience of the previous international break also provides an obvious warning.

Dortmund failed to win any of their first three matches after returning to club action in October, drawing 2-2 with Werder Bremen and 0-0 with Union Berlin in the Bundesliga either side of a 1-0 Champions League defeat to Bodø/Glimt.

Kovac nevertheless believes the circumstances are somewhat different this time. The Dortmund coach noted that the latest international window involved a more conventional two-match schedule rather than the unusually heavy international programme surrounding the previous break, leaving his players closer to their normal competitive rhythm.

Getting into matches more quickly has become an increasingly prominent theme for Dortmund. BVB’s last ten Bundesliga goals have all arrived after half-time, an unusual run that Kovac would preferably bring to an end in Gladbach.

“If you take an early lead, the game opens up much more swiftly,” Kovac said.

Recent findings suggest that the trend has done little damage to Dortmund’s results so far, but it has repeatedly forced BVB to remain patient before finding breakthroughs. Five of their nine Bundesliga matches this season have reached half-time without Dortmund scoring.

Kovac will also have several late personnel decisions to make. Serhou Guirassy, Waldemar Anton, and Carney Chukwuemeka remained in Dortmund during the international break while dealing with muscular problems. Kovac stated that their availability would be assessed ahead of the weekend and stressed that “nothing is ruled out.”.

Samuele Inacio has meanwhile returned from international duty carrying a minor complaint, leaving another question mark over Dortmund’s squad for Saturday.

The mood became considerably lighter when Kovac was questioned about the release of Grand Theft Auto 6.

A reporter asked whether the Dortmund coach had encountered an unusual number of players requesting a “sick day” following the game’s release and whether GTA had become a major topic inside the dressing room. Kovac was also asked whether he owned a PlayStation 5 himself.

Sources indicate that “I don’t have one,” Kovac responded. “I reached the age at which I’m out. I believe I heard or read some time ago that this game was coming out. I hope my lads haven’t been playing deep into the night. How does one do something like that these days? Per App or does one need hardware [a gaming console]?”.

The reporter clarified that the game could be downloaded on a console, adding that BVB striker Fabio Silva had commented on downloading it in the three days since its release.

“Okay, then I’ll act on the assumption that they [the players] are playing it,” Kovac continued. “I hope they don’t play it so excessively that they can’t tell day from night anymore. That could be problematic. But today is a wonderful day not just because of the fact that this game is being released, but because we also have two in-house birthdays with Costa [Konstantinos Karetsas] and Joey [Veerman].

Recent findings suggest that “That’s much more notable than this game,” Kovac concluded. “But I have heard that a few [of the players] are intensively involved [with the video game].”.
