Sources indicate that baby, this is an award-winning podcast. Apple Podcasts has named Baby, this is Keke Palmer its 2026 Show of the Year, recognizing the Mashable 101 creator's podcast for its quality and cultural impact. Unveiled Tuesday, Nov. 17, the honor celebrates Palmer's approach to interviews, which move easily between celebrity stories and conversations about relationships, creativity, and personal growth.

"Keke's curiosity, humor, and openness make every conversation feel personal, and video brings that connection even closer," mentioned Oliver Schusser, Apple’s vice president of Podcasts, Music, Sports, and Beats, in an announcement. "We’re proud to celebrate her talent across Apple Podcasts and Apple Music, from the conversations she creates to the music she shares.".

As she reached the reveal that her show had been named Apple Podcasts’ Show of the Year, Palmer grabbed a tissue.

“I can’t ruin this look with tears," she remarked. "Oh my gosh!”.

In an accompanying statement, she thanked Apple Podcasts, Wondery, her guests, and everyone who listens and brings their own perspective to the conversation.

Sources indicate that "I'm so honored," Palmer noted. "I love how a conversation about entertainment can open up a bigger conversation about life. There’s so much to explore in the things we watch, laugh at, and connect with, and this show gives me room to follow that curiosity.".

"This recognition means so much to me," she added.

Palmer was also named to Mashable’s 101 most influential creators of 2025, where we celebrated her humor, celebrity interviews, and knack for turning unscripted moments into internet favorites.

Since launching the podcast in 2022, Palmer has welcomed guests including Usher, Tina Knowles, Ciara, and Tyler Perry, bringing the same quick wit that's made her a fixture on our feeds to celebrity conversations.
