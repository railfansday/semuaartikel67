"Everyone agrees it's more fun this way": Toby Fox won't tell his team how Deltarune ends until they've built enough of it

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Toby Fox is a purposefully mysterious person. It was probably a wise choice on his part, obfuscating his current appearance with everyone's favourite annoying dog, essentially never speaking aloud in livestreams and instead using a text-to-speech bot, and posting only when there's notable enough Deltarune updates of if it would be very funny. But as it turns out, that sense of mystery extends to the team that's making Deltarune too, as Fox won't tell his devs how the game ends until they've built enough of it.

Industry observers note that earlier this week, the fall edition of the Deltarune newsletter went out, offering a brief update on the game's next and sixth chapter. That one's apparently now a "structurally complete draft that can be played from the beginning to the end," but that doesn't mean it's quite ready for release, as "some important scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation." I'd expect a 2027 release for this chapter, which is going to be a shorter one too.

It has been revealed that but in typical tricky Tony fashion, there's an enhancement for chapter seven where he makes it sound like no one but him knows how the whole game is going to end. "Well, we've only just started…," he explains of the progress. "I read out the events and dialogue of Chapter 7 to the team. However, just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped reading… I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens. Everyone agrees it's more fun this way.".

I'm glad they all think it's fun, the wait for me personally certainly ain't. I do wonder if this speaks to a certain anxiety over how the game will shape up. Following the release of chapter five I was subjected to all manner of theories about what's to come by my partner gorging on YouTube videos from folks who mostly just seem quite excited about it all, even if a lot of them sound wrong. So I imagine there must be a little bit of weight to all of those varying expectations.

According to recent reports, fox did note in the newsletter that "unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence...!" so perhaps I'm speaking a load of hogwash! That stated, I'm furious that a character called Apple Girlington didn't make it into chapter five, so you can expect to hear from my lawyers, Mr. Fox.
