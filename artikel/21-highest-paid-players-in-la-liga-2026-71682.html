Sources indicate that from the electric Kylian Mbappe to the dazzling Lamine Yamal, some of the very best players on the planet at the moment ply their trade in the Spanish top-flight. La Liga is home to incredibly talented superstars and even some of the front-runners for the 2027 Ballon d'Or.

While there often appears to be more money floating about elsewhere, like in the Saudi Pro League and the Premier League, the top clubs in Spain aren't shy about handing out lucrative contracts to attract and retain world-class talent either.

Sources indicate that with Real Madrid and Barcelona players at the forefront of this list, there can be little surprise that the purse strings are often loosened as the biggest stars are tied down to extremely lucrative deals. With that in mind, below is a list of the 21 highest-paid players in La Liga right now.

Top 21 Highest-Paid La Liga Players (21-11).

When at his best, Rodrygo is among the best wingers in the world. He was just 18 when he turned up at Real Madrid, having established himself as the next major talent to emerge at Santos. Since then, the Brazilian has won three La Liga titles and the Champions League twice.

Recent findings suggest that although he might play second fiddle to the likes of Mbappe and Vinicius Junior (more on those two later), Rodrygo is still among the best-paid players in Spain, earning a hefty £271,200 p/w. Sadly, he suffered a torn ACL in March 2026 and might not play again until 2027.

Sources indicate that when he was just about saving Leeds United from relegation in 2022, few would have thought Raphinha was destined to prove himself as one of Barcelona's most key players in the coming seasons. But that's exactly what he has done in Spain.

In a recent development, the brilliant Brazilian can play across any of the front three attacking positions and works incredibly hard for the team, while still delivering moments of magic. This has seen him win three Spanish league titles, and he was even named La Liga Player of the Season in 2024/25. As such, it's more than fair that he is so well-paid.

In a team full of superstar midfielders, Federico Valverde has endeared himself to the Bernabeu faithful through his hard-working performances and his willingness to play anywhere asked of him. That's not to suggest he lacks talent, far from it, as he's now regarded as one of the best midfielders in the league.

Sources indicate that the 28-year-old is well compensated for his efforts, too; walking away with close to £300,000 p/w. For a while, he was among the most underrated names in world football, but his club always recognised his value, and that's well reflected by his handsome wages.

A rare non-Barcelona or Madrid star in this list, Nico Williams has been rewarded for his loyalty to Athletic Club. In 2025, amid strong links to the likes of Barcelona and Arsenal, he committed his future to the club, signing a mammoth 10-year contract worth £279,660 p/w.

For a player on such significant wages, he doesn't always deliver as many goals or assists as some would expect, but he's still only 24 and could yet grow to become a little more consistent. Even so, when he's on it, he's amongst the best in the world. This was clear at Euro 2024, with Williams delivering a serious electric performance as Spain won the tournament.

According to recent reports, aurelien Tchouameni has been one of the first names on the teamsheet for Real Madrid since signing for the club in a €100 million move from Monaco in 2022. Los Blancos beat the likes of Liverpool to his signature.

It has been revealed that following Toni Kroos' retirement and Luka Modric's departure, Tchouameni has only become more important for the Spanish giants. In the summer of 2026, Man Utd are believed to have made a 'very aggressive move' to sign the Frenchman, but Real Madrid didn't have any intention of letting him go.

A few months later, he penned a six-year contract worth a huge £305,039-per-week, making him one of La Liga's highest-paid players.

Only the second player in the top 10 not to play for either Real Madrid or Barcelona. The Atletico Madrid icon had significant shoes to fill when he was chosen to replace long-standing loanee Courtois in 2014. In the decade that followed, Jan Oblak demonstrated himself to be one of the best number ones on the planet.

Recent findings suggest that interest in his services was commonplace. Despite that, Oblak stayed loyal to Diego Simeone and has been financially rewarded in return, now earning more than Courtois. Now 33, it will be interesting to see if Atletico offer him quite such a massive salary when it comes time to renew in 2028.

Sources indicate that this figure is bound to increase in the years to come. In just three seasons in the Spanish capital, Jude Bellingham has established himself as arguably the most crucial player in Los Blancos' superhero-like squad. The Englishman finished as the club's top scorer in La Liga and was named the La Liga Player of the Season during his debut campaign in Spain.

It has been revealed that mbappe's arrival meant that the 23-year-old fell a little out of the limelight, but his progress has also been hampered by a pesky shoulder injury which required surgery. Even so, the Englishman remains one of the best players on the planet, and proved that with his seven goals at the 2026 World Cup. Madrid will be relieved to have him tied down on a major wage until 2029.

It has been revealed that lamine Yamal has risen to be seen as one of the best players in world football and a strong contender to lift the 2027 Ballon d'Or at the age of just 19. The youngster has always been compared to Barcelona great Lionel Messi, but came out on top as Spain beat Argentina in the 2026 World Cup.

Recent findings suggest that what's more, Yamal is certainly being more handsomely rewarded than Messi was as a teenager, with the Spain international putting pen to paper on a newly introduced deal at the end of the 2024/25 season. He now rakes in £372,880 every week, a wage proportionate to his outstanding talent.

After starring for Spain in their second World Cup triumph this summer, Rodri emerged as a target for both Barcelona and Real Madrid. He soon made a decision to join the Catalans and follow in the footsteps of one of his idols, Sergio Busquets.

Barca agreed on a £65 million deal with Manchester City to bring the 2024 Ballon d'Or winner to Camp Nou. He penned a mammoth deal worth £440,588-per-week, making him the third highest-paid player in La Liga and the highest earner in Hansi Flick's squad.

In the case of Vinicius Jr, the Brazilian felt he deserved a much bigger contract, and he was finally convinced Real Madrid of that as well. After months of negotiations, and with Arsenal lurking in the background, Los Blancos upped their offer and the winger put pen to paper in August 2026 on a deal worth more than £500,000 p/w.

According to recent reports, vini is certainly one of the best players in the world currently and has scored in two Champions League finals, becoming one of Los Blancos' most crucial attacking threats. With that in mind, it's no surprise he wanted better terms.
