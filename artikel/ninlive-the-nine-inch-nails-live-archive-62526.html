Besides a database driven site. The second most requested feature for ninlive is streaming capability. The future is now. TJ has been working hard behind the scenes to make it happen. You are now able to stream all of your favorite NIN live recordings from 1988-2026. I can't believe how far the site has come in the last year. Some truly incredible work. Login with your discord account and you can save your favorite recordings to stream and keep your faves organized. As always, feedback is always welcome. Reach out via email if you have comments/suggestions about it.

I am at the moment adding all of the ticket stubs, setlist scans, passes that I have for each show. Please get in contact if you have anything to contribute. I am also looking for photos from past reveals. If you are a photographer and would like to contribute some highlights, I'd love to add them to the archive with a link directed to your site of choice.

In a recent development, other than that. A few tapes trickling in and I do have some that I am slowly working on. None that are insane upgrades. But would need some work and comparison of previous versions. Notably, I have a 1st gen cassette of the 07/12/1991 NOLA Tipitina's show that sounds killer. I recently transferred the elusive collection of acc123456 and have a queue of VHS/Cassette transfers to check over. I also just finished a 200 DAT tape project for a fellow taper out of NYC. The transfer backlog is clear, for the first time in a while. So if you need anything transferred, or have some old NIN hanging around that isn't on the archive. Email us!
