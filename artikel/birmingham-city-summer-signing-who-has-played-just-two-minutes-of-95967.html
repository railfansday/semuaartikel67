Birmingham City summer signing who has played just two minutes of Championship football will start vs Burnley: Opinion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Birmingham City saw their impressive form halted on Saturday as they lost 1-0 away at fellow high-flyers Stoke City. It was a frustrating day for BCFC, but recent evidence suggests they’re well placed to return to winning ways when they face Burnley on Wednesday night.

According to recent reports, the Blues are 4th in the Championship table despite Saturday’s loss, but the result wasn’t the only dampener on a tricky day at the office. Club captain and standout defender Christoph Klarer was booked in the first half, and as it was his fifth yellow card of the campaign, he now faces a one-match suspension.

Birmingham City aren’t blessed with strength in numbers at centrre-back, but in rarely-seen summer signing Dael Fry, Chris Davies has a fine player to call upon to cover for the absence of star man Klarer.

Fry has been a Championship mainstay for almost his entire career. He came through the academy of hometown club Middlesbrough and barring a stint on loan at Rotherham United, he had spent his full playing career at the Riverside Stadium.

Industry observers note that he marked a strong free transfer signing for Birmingham City in the summer. However, after 16 games of the Championship campaign, Fry has made just two substitute appearances, totalling only two minutes. On both occasions, he came on off the bench to help see out 2-1 victories – first over Wrexham, and then Derby County.

Fry has been unused on the BCFC bench on 12 occasions, but as the only other centre-back alongside regular starters Klarer and Phil Neumann, the 29-year-old is bound to come into the team at Burnley.

Recent findings suggest that losing Klarer is far from ideal, but Fry is a fine replacement. Wednesday night will bring his 275th Championship appearance, and he offers a strong aerial presence at the back while thriving in duels and one-on-one defending. He can be trusted in possession as well, so should slot into Davies’ defence with ease.

Upon swapping Middlesbrough for Birmingham City, Fry will have hoped for more minutes than he has found. Klarer and Neumann have been an unbreakable partnership though, forcing the summer signing to watch on from the bench more often than not.

Given his status as a Championship stalwart, that will have frustrated the former Boro ace. But, Fry’s influence will have been felt through his leadership and professionalism, offering a strong presence in the dressing room and on the training ground.

This clash with Burnley offers a welcome chance for Fry to show BCFC fans that he is more than ready to deliver when called upon, despite the fact he is a clear third-choice central defender at St Andrew’s.
