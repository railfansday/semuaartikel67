Sources indicate that grand Theft Auto 6 isn’t just about … well, Grand Theft Auto, but instead, you’ll be stealing anything and everything you get your hands on. To do this, though, you’ll need access to certain facilities and compounds. There are houses and buildings that can net you a nice profit, let alone be able to get into the back door of a bank and sneak your way into their vault. To do this, though, you’ll need a special item called a Snap Gun.

According to recent reports, the Snap Gun is Grand Theft Auto’s handy little tool that’s similar to a lockpick. Why Jason and Lucia can’t just pick a lock is beyond me, but this item’s main purpose is to ensure that the two have a quick and painless time getting into doors.

Sources indicate that this will only work on doors, so garages will need a different tool, but suffice to say, this becomes one of the most crucial tools in your arsenal simply because it ensures that you won’t trip an alarm when entering. Normally, you’d have to force your way into a locked door, but this bypasses that entirely.

In the third chapter of the campaign, you will have a couple of heists that you can do for Yasmani, someone who fleeces your marked bills (and narcotics but at a much lower than Tyronne). There will be three of these jewelry stores you’ll be able to take down, but really, you only need one for this trick.

It is very notable that you prioritize at least one of these “missions” as it will help you get the Snap Gun very quickly, and considering its importance in staying undetected, it comes in handy early on.

Head to one of the shops on the map and rob it blind. It doesn’t matter if you play it smoothly or go loud, so long as you get into the safe from the code Yasmani gave you, you will be in the clear.

Sources indicate that after robbing the first one, return to Yasmani to get your take on the haul. He will give you your cut for the job but will give you something in return: The Snap Gun. It’s just that easy. Do one of the jobs requiring a safe for Yasmani, and you’ll be able to break into houses and locked doors with ease.

All you need to do is find the right pressure point on the left stick and then spam the RT/R2 button until it’s open.
