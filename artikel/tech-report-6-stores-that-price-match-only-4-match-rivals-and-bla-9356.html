It has been revealed that best Buy, Staples, Home Depot, and Lowe’s still match a competitor’s lower price, while Target and Walmart now match only their own. Nearly all refuse a third-party seller’s price or a Black Friday deal, so read the store’s policy and pull up the listing on your phone before you pay.

Recent findings suggest that best Buy and Staples both match a lower Amazon price, but only on items Amazon sells and ships itself, not ones from the independent third-party sellers on its site. Neither honors a rival’s Black Friday deal, and Best Buy refuses any rival’s price from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after.

Each store sets its own exclusions, and the policies differ again after checkout, since only some refund the difference when the store’s own price drops.

In a recent development, all six require the identical item, so compare the model number and color before you ask. On a price comparison site, also check who sells each listing, since a third-party seller’s price won’t count.

Best Buy’s list of qualified competitors names 19 retailers, among them Amazon, Apple, Walmart, Target, and Home Depot. The item must be new, sold by Best Buy, and ready to ship or pick up from the rival right away, and clearance, open-box, and refurbished items don’t qualify. Neither does a rival’s daily or hourly sale.

Industry observers note that in a store, show an employee the rival’s ad or web page with the lower price still in effect. For a BestBuy.com order, use Best Buy’s online chat or call 1-888-237-8289.

It has been revealed that after you buy, only Best Buy’s own lower price counts, and only through your return window, which is 15 days for most shoppers and 60 for members of its My Best Buy Plus and Total programs. This season, most items bought from Oct. 30 through Dec. 31 can be matched to a lower Best Buy price through Jan. 15.

According to recent reports, beyond Amazon, Staples matches any retailer that sells under the same brand both in stores and online. The match works only when you buy in a Staples store, so bring proof of the lower price to the customer service desk.

The item must be fresh, identical down to the model number, and in stock at the rival. Clearance, limited-time sales, Cyber Monday deals, and in-store pickup orders don’t qualify. If a Staples store or Staples.com lowers its price within 14 days of your purchase, show the lower price and Staples refunds the difference.

According to recent reports, home Depot’s price match guarantee covers stores in your area and online rivals including Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Lowe’s, and Menards, as long as the rival can deliver to you. It won’t match third-party sellers or membership and auction sites such as Costco, Sam’s Club, and eBay, and it skips clearance items and Black Friday or other limited-time promotions.

Show the cashier the ad, printout, or web page at checkout, or use live chat or call 1-800-430-3376 for a homedepot.com order. Within 30 days of purchase, Home Depot will also refund the difference when its own price or a matched rival’s drops, though not on clearance items or anything bought during a promotional event.

In a recent development, lowe’s Lowest Price Guarantee matches a lower price on the identical in-stock item at another local store, or at an online retailer that ships to you, with shipping or delivery added to the rival’s price. On Amazon, it matches only what Amazon sells itself, and it skips membership warehouse clubs, third-party sellers, clearance, and one-time promotions such as Black Friday. Its stores also won’t match a lower Lowes.com price.

According to recent reports, in a store, show an associate the ad, web page, printout, or photo. For a lower price at an online retailer, chat with Lowe’s or call 1-877-465-6937. The policy notes nothing about refunding a price that drops after you buy, so get the match before you pay.

According to recent reports, target’s price match guarantee covers only Target’s own prices, so a lower price at Amazon or Walmart doesn’t count. It matches a lower price at Target.com, in Target stores, or through a Target Circle member deal, at checkout or within 14 days after you buy. This season, an item bought from Nov. 1 can be matched to a lower Target price through Dec. 24.

According to recent reports, A store purchase can’t be matched to a different Target store’s price, and clearance, closeout, and open-package items are excluded. Show the online price or digital ad on your phone, since the lower price must still be posted when you ask. It has been revealed that walmart’s price match policy works only in its stores. A store will match the identical item’s lower price on Walmart.com if it’s in stock online when you ask, but Walmart.com itself matches nothing, and neither one matches another retailer.

Point out the online price to the cashier at checkout, since the store matches only then and Walmart.com won’t adjust an order whose price later drops. Third-party sellers’ listings and Walmart.com’s clearance, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and other limited-time prices don’t count.

It has been revealed that after checkout, four of the six still refund the difference when the price drops, though each waits for you to ask. Home Depot allows 30 days and also counts a matched rival’s lower price. Best Buy matches its own lower price through your return window, and Target and Staples match theirs for 14 days. Best Buy and Target both stretch those windows for holiday buys. The Walmart and Lowe’s policies promise nothing after you pay.
