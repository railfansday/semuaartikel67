Napoli scraped a 1-0 victory over Torino thanks to Scott McTominay, but Antonio Vergara sustained a fresh injury and the woodwork rescued them from Rolando Mandragora.

The Partenopei reshuffled amid the usual injury crisis, as Alex Meret was ruled out by a couple of months, joining Benoit Badiashile, Alisson Santos, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo and Alessandro Buongiorno on the absentee list.

They moved to a 3-5-2 formation, with Vanja Milinkovic-Savic facing his former club, and Scotland internationals Scott McTominay, Billy Gilmour and Che Adams all starting. Kevin De Bruyne was rested ahead of Tuesday’s Champions League trip to Manchester City. Toro had one point from the last three rounds going into the break for international duty, but Giovanni Simeone was against his old teammates, with only Franco Israel missing.

See how it all unfolded on the Liveblog.

McTominay’s angled drive flashed wide of the far post, then Lucas Perri got down well to parry the Matteo Politano finish, with Ardian Ismajli preventing McTominay turning in the rebound.

Antonio Vergara was visibly distraught after picking up a muscular injury, limping off the field in tears after just 11 minutes to make way for De Bruyne.

McTominay’s deflected effort was fingertipped round the far post by Perri, then the Toro goalkeeper made a fine save on Rasmus Hojlund from a De Bruyne slide-rule pass.

Hojlund nodded the Leonardo Spinazzola cross over from seven yards, then moments later De Bruyne’s diving header from 10 yards was charged down by Saul Coco.

It has been revealed that torino were also forced into a change, as Rafik Belghali had a knee injury, replaced by Zakaria Aboukhlal. Nonetheless, they nearly took the lead on the stroke of half-time with a Rolando Mandragora screamer from distance that smacked against the crossbar.

The Granata pushed after the restart too, as Zakaria Aboukhlal saw his angled drive flash across the face of goal to skim the far post, then Simeone didn’t make the most of the situation when he caught Rafa Marin napping.

McTominay stung Perri’s gloves on a De Bruyne assist, while Mandragora’s free kick went around the wall to force a difficult Milinkovic-Savic save at the near post.

Perri parried the attempts from Noa Lang and Politano, then had more difficulty with the Hojlund strike from just inside the area.

Industry observers note that ardian Ismajli was forced off by a muscular glitch, and Napoli took the lead moments later. Lang sprinted after Aboukhlal to stop the counter-attack, immediately turning it into an attacking situation by finding McTominay, who drilled into the far bottom corner from the edge of the box.

Andre Frank Zambo Anguissa threw himself in the path of a Cesare Casadei volley on a corner, and Duvan Zapata nodded the Mandragora corner wide.

With the last kick of the game, Lang went on the counter from a Torino corner, but Perri stuck out a boot to deny the angled drive.
