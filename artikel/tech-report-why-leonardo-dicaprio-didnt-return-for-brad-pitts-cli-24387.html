Leonardo DiCaprio is missing out on The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.

Though Oscar winner starred in 2019's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, he opted not to appear in the Quentin Tarantino movie's upcoming sequel due to time constraints, according to his former costar Brad Pitt.

"It would have been great fun, but it required a lot of prosthetics, which is a good couple of weeks prepping to get him," Pitt told The Hollywood Reporter Nov. 16, referencing how the follow-up's trailer teased that DiCaprio's Rick Dalton had become "too fat" for his titular stuntman to serve as a body double. "It’s hardly worth the cost for a cameo.".

However, it wasn't for a lack of trying.

"It was talked about for a while, but it just seemed like a big ask," the 62-year-old explained, adding that DiCaprio's character does get a few "sweet tributes" in the movie.

But the Titanic alum wasn't the only notable name who declined to return for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. After all, David Fincher stepped in to take the directing reins from Tarantino, who opted to only write the script of the latest installment.

Industry observers note that "I don’t bemoan that all because it’s such a commitment, especially for a director," Pitt shared. “Things sat around for about nine months and Fincher and I were talking about doing something again, and it just happened.".

As for DiCaprio's thoughts on the new movie?

Sources indicate that "Ultimately, I cannot wait to see the Cliff Booth story," he told Deadline last year. "I think David Fincher’s the perfect man for the job. Quentin is a huge fan of his work, I’m a huge fan of his work, and there’s nobody better to carry on that lineage and tell that story.".

DiCaprio added, "It’s the next phase of Cliff Booth’s life. I’m excited to see it.".
