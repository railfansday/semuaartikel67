Sources indicate that michael Bay has developed a name for himself as one of the most prominent action directors in the world, and he holds this status despite slowing down a bit in recent years. A few years ago, Bay returned to blockbuster filmmaking with Ambulance, the action thriller starring Jake Gyllenhaal (The Day After Tomorrow) and Yahya Abdul-Mateen II (Wonder Man). The 2022 film grossed just over $50 million at the box office against a $40 million budget, and it’s now streaming on Netflix for those who missed out on seeing it on the major screen. Bay was briefly attached to direct the new Netflix action thriller Fast and Loose, starring Will Smith, but he backed out of the film due to creative differences that arose between him and the leading star — the Bad Boys reunion will have to wait.

It has been revealed that the future is a bit murky for Bay right now, and while it’s been reported that he’s returning to the Transformers franchise to direct a upcoming film, practically nothing is known about the project at this time. Many fans would like to see Bay get back to the same schedule he had in the early to mid 2010s when he was directing films at least every other year. One of Bay’s most polarizing and entertaining movies came back in 2016 when he teamed up with John Krasinski (The Office) for 13 Hours, which is based on Mitchell Zuckoff’s book of the same name. It tells the story of the six U.S. operatives stationed at an off-the-books CIA base in Benghazi who are called into action when the compound falls under attack by a horde of armed locals.

13 Hours, formerly titled 13 Hours: The Secret Soldiers of Benghazi, is streaming on Paramount+ in America, and the film has even surged into the top 10 in the last few days and is one of the most-watched titles on the platform. For those without a Paramount+ subscription, the film is also available to watch on Peacock, and it’s even streaming for free via Pluto TV. Additional cast members for the film include Pablo Schreiber as Kris Paronto, James Badge Dale as Tyrone Woods, and David Denmam as Dave Benton. The film is a must-watch for fans of other classic war thrillers like Black Hawk Down and Zero Dark Thirty.

Check out 13 Hours on Paramount+ or Peacock and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Michael Bay’s past and future projects.
