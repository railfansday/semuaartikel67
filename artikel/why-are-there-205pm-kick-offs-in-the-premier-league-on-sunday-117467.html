Why are there 2:05pm kick-offs in the Premier League on Sunday?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Everton's derby with Liverpool has been moved from Saturday to Sunday.

The Premier League kick-off times look a little unusual this weekend, with no Saturday 12:30 GMT slot, and games starting at 12:00 and 14:05 on Sunday.

Recent findings suggest that everton will host local rivals Liverpool on Sunday at 12 noon because the match has been selected for live broadcast by TNT, who have a mid-day broadcast slot on that day. The game was originally scheduled for Saturday.

This has had a knock-on effect on the remaining fixtures.

On a typical Premier League weekend, the first matches will begin at 14:00 followed by a final 16:30 kick-off.

Pushing start times back five minutes to 14:05 reduces the risk of overlap with the unusual early 12:00 start time, while avoiding clashing with the 16:30 fixture.

Midweek European games have also affected the schedule, as the Premier League aims to prevent clubs from playing two matches within 60 hours of each other.

Crystal Palace, Sunderland, Bournemouth and Brighton are all set to play in the Europa and Conference Leagues on Thursday, ruling them out of Saturday Premier League fixtures.
