Recent findings suggest that the iPhone Duo has proven to be one of the most exciting — and hard to get — smartphone releases from Apple in years. Declared in September, the Duo was the buzziest hardware announcement amid what’s been a very busy season for Apple, including the release of a smart home collection and the first-ever touchscreen MacBook Pro.

In a recent development, the Duo marks Apple’s first foray into the foldable space, and as expected, it comes with a high price tag. At retail, it starts at $1,999, but there are ways to get it for less, particularly through a carrier. T-Mobile is offering $1,200 off the device, with $0 out of pocket for qualifying aims. The deal applies to monthly payment plans for 36-month repayments. Members can also get discounts across the device’s storage tiers, including 256GB, 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB. Additionally, T-Mobile offers the largest and fastest 5G network and was awarded the best mobile network in the U.S. by Ookla Speedtest.

Storage: 256GB – 2TBBattery: Up to 31 hours video playback (inner display)Display: 7.6” (inner), 5.4″ (outer).

According to recent reports, if you’re not sold on the folding form factor, T-Mobile’s other deals include up to $1,200 off the iPhone 18 Pro Max model, which is also available with the same storage options as the Duo. The iPhone 18 Pro Max is available with no trade-in and $0 upfront but requires a new line and bringing your number on the Beyond blueprint.

In a recent development, the Duo is available in “Star White” and “Night Sky” from T-Mobile and features a dual display; there’s a 5.4‑inch Super Retina XDR outer display and a 7.6‑inch Super Retina XDR folding inner display. As for battery life, the phone boasts up to 31 hours of video playback with the inner display and up to 44 hours of video playback with the outer display.

Recent findings suggest that as for the cameras, there’s a 12MP Center Stage front camera and a Dual Fusion camera system that features a 48MP Fusion main lens and a 48MP Fusion Ultra Wide lens. The phone also utilizes the folding design to take better photos — you can let the subject preview the shot before you take it using the outer display.

The displays are designed to be used in a variety of orientations — the “standing” display (think a tent) can be used as a bedside clock or calendar display, while the “seated” display allows for hands-free content viewing without needing to prop the phone up.

Sources indicate that if you’re curious about the new Duo but are feeling the sticker shock, T-Mobile’s latest deal is one of the most promising. Demand is high for the Duo, so it’s worth reserving now.
