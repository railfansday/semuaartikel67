Sources indicate that as countless people flock to Grand Theft Auto 6 following its launch earlier this week, a common highlight gamers have been highlighting are the many radio stations present in the game. While packed with songs, one artist has expressed her frustration with not being included among the list of featured artists.

According to recent reports, doja Cat, a rap-pop artist and avid gamer, took to Twitter to vent about the situation. In three back-to-back posts, her thoughts were made especially clear.

Recent findings suggest that "I would’ve loved to have my music on gta6 i’m so f****n bitter," She wrote. "@RockstarGames eat my d**k all yall I love your stupid f****n b***h *** game." Her final on the matter reads: "@RockstarGames.

you broke my heart p***e c****o".

Doja Cat has long been public about her love for the medium. She often streams games live on Twitch, including Fortnite, and is among the most popular Final Fantasy 14 streamers. She's also mentioned games in her songs, including a reference to Grand Theft Auto 6 in her song 'All Mine'.

Personal desires aside, having your music featured in Grand Theft Auto 6 has proven to be a rich opportunity for musicians. Tom Petty's Love is a Long Road soared by around 36 percent immediately after the release of a Grand Theft Auto 6 trailer in which it was included. So, the game acts as a spotlight on artists and their works.

Still, there is hope for Doja Cat and other artists who want their music included in the game. Rockstar Games has a history of expanding the number of songs featured on in-game radio stations post-launch as seen in Grand Theft Auto 5, so fingers crossed for the future.
