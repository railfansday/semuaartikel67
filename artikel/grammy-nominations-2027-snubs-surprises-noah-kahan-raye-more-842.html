Get ready to turn up the volume on the biggest night in music.

Recent findings suggest that after all, the 2027 Grammy Awards nominations were declared on Nov. 16 with a pitch-perfect lineup of presenters, including David Guetta, Kehlani, Olivia Dean and Yungblud.

Taylor Swift proved she knows what it takes to put on a show, earning five nominations for her album The Life of a Showgirl as well as her Toy Story 5 track "I Knew It, I Knew You," including Album of the Year and Song of the Year.

Meanwhile, Olivia Rodrigo is ready for a golden evening after being nominated seven times over for her album you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love in categories including Album of the Year and Song of the Year, as well as Best Rock Performance and Best Rock Song for "What's Wrong With Me?" featuring Robert Smith.

Phoebe Bridgers made a triumphant return with her third solo album Lost Boys—her first in more than six years—earning seven nods like Album of the Year and Best Alternative Music Album.

Raye, for her part, was on every nomination shortlist, but she exceeded even the most elevated expectations, earning 11 nominations, tying Beyoncé's 2025 record for the most nominations by a single female artist in a single year.

In a recent development, that's not to say all the most-listened-to songs or albums made the list. Although fans expected Noah Kahan to be among the contenders for Album of the Year, The Great Divide appeared to fall flat with the Recording Academy, save for one nod for Best Contemporary Folk Album.

Industry observers note that the same goes for Harry Styles, who failed to score a single nod for his album Kiss All The Time, Disco Occasionally. Meanwhile, despite Stella Lefty's massive hit "Boston" dominating charts this year, she failed to score a Best Latest Artist nomination.

And while Adéla prays every single night, she won't be sitting right at the Grammys for her earworm song "Nicole Kidman"—a decision she made for herself.

Recent findings suggest that "I think it's a nomination you need to earn," she told Who What Wear earlier this month of the Song of the Year category. "I have so much more work to do before I even think of that. I have my tour, and I want to start writing my next album.".

But that's not to say the 22-year-old won't return to try to prove her weight in gold.

It has been revealed that "I think I need to do all the work I can this year and next in order to really earn it," she added. "Maybe in 2028, I'll feel like I truly deserve to be in that room.".

As fans wait to see who wins major at the 2027 Grammy Awards on Feb. 7, keep reading for more snubs and surprises.

Recent findings suggest that abrams failed to snag any nominations for her latest album Daughter From Hell, which she submitted for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album. Her single "Hit the Wall" was also absent from the Song of the Year, Record of the Year and Best Pop Solo Performance lists.

Abrams has previously received two nominations for Best New Artist in 2024 and Best Pop Duo Performance for "Us" which featured Taylor Swift in 2025.

While Grande picked up some surprise nominations for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance for "For Good" alongside Cynthia Erivo, as well as Song of the Year and Best Music Video for "hate that I made you love me," and Best Pop Vocal Album for Petal, she was snubbed for Album of the Year.

Although Kahan scored one nomination for Best Contemporary Folk Album, he was not included in the major categories like Album of the Year and Song of the Year for his hit The Great Divide.

Beyoncé is famously the most decorated Grammy-winning artist—the 35-time winner scored her 100th nomination this year— but her Best R&B Solo Performance nod for "Morning Dew (Donk)" was somewhat of a surprise considering its delayed release in celebration of B'Day's 20th anniversary in July.

This year was significant for Hobert, who released her debut album Who's the Clown last year, but she failed to score a nod for Best Latest Artist. (Her brother Malcolm Todd was also left off the shortlist.).

Surprise: The Devil Wears Prada 2.

That's actually not all for this much-anticipated sequel. The Devil Wears Prada 2 scored a surprise nomination for Best Compilation Soundtrack For Visual Media alongside Stranger Things season five, Wicked: For Good, The Wizard of Oz at the Sphere and Wuthering Heights.

It's been a long time coming for the first-time Grammy nominee, who scored her first nods for Best Dance/Electronic Recording for "Melatonin" as well as Best Dance Pop Recording for "Too Easy.".

It has been revealed that swift wasn't totally snubbed by the Recording Academy—she earned nods for Record of the Year, Album of the Year, Song of the Year, Best Pop Duo Performance alongside Sabrina Carpenter, Best Song Written for a Visual Media and more. But The Life of a Showgirl was not among Best Pop Vocal Album contenders, while "I Knew It, I Knew You" was snubbed for Best Country Song and Best Country Solo Performance.

Still, it was a surprise to see "I Knew It, I Knew You" score a nomination for Song of the Year, a category introduced by Swift herself during the nomination livestream, given Swift's other hits from The Life of a Showgirl.

The hype hasn't died down, Wicked is still oh so very popular. Wicked: For Good scored two nominations including Best Song Written For Visual Media and Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance.

Disco All The Time, Grammy Occasionally. The "Aperture" singer failed to score recognition for his latest album in any category. Styles has three Grammy wins under his belt, including Album of the Year for Harry's House in 2023.

It has been revealed that her comeback is far from over. The pop star received her first Grammy nominations in 16 years, racking up nods for Best Dance Pop Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Album and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for "Bring Your Love" alongside Carpenter.

Although Lefty rose to massive acclaim this year for hits like "Boston," she failed to receive any recognition by The Recording Academy.
