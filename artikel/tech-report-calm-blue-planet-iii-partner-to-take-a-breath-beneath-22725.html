Sources indicate that the ocean has an extraordinary ability to transform how we feel. Its rhythms slow us down. Its vastness helps us gain perspective. Its beauty invites us to pause. Its resilience inspires hope.

And this impact may actually be backed by science. It’s called the Blue Mind Theory. Essentially, research has revealed a link between time spent near water, especially on the coast, and both better well-being and lower distress.

According to recent reports, that’s why Calm has partnered with Blue Planet III from BBC Studios, encouraging you to take a mindful moment to release stress, experience awe, and escape beneath the waves, wherever you are. Together, we’ve created a latest collection of content that spotlights the soothing sounds and stunning sights of the deep blue sea.

Twenty-five years after the groundbreaking original series first brought the ocean into homes around the world, the new Blue Planet III illuminates incredible new stories from some of Earth's most important and least understood environments.

And now, the most calming elements of the series are being reimagined in the context of Calm.

Here are the highlights of the collection:.

💙 Dive into Blue Planet III — a latest immersive soundscape that takes you on a rhythmic journey through tropical seas alive with dolphins, the boundless open ocean, and finally seasonal seas that feature the mating calls of southern right whales.

💙 Drift Through the Reef — a mesmerizing, meditative montage of colorful coral.

💙 Find Your Flow — a delightful dance between sea lions and a massive bait ball, a school of thousands of small fish all swimming in unison.

💙 Bask in the Blue  — an entrancing visualscape of a seahorse floating through glistening sunbeams.

💙 The Sound of Blue Planet III — the series’ enchanting theme song, from Hans Zimmer, Thom Lukas & Laurentia Editha for Bleeding Fingers Music.

So dive in (pun intended) to the mindful magic of the ocean and find out where to watch Blue Planet III from BBC Studios.
