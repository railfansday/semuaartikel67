More information has come to light about Ken Urker’s death.

A little more than one month after the 34-year-old—who shared daughter Aurora, 21 months, with Gypsy Rose Blanchard—was found dead in his Louisiana home on Oct. 1, Urker’s cause of death has been officially attributed to accidental poly substance drug toxicity, also known as an accidental drug overdose.

The Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed the case is now complete, and no further testing is pending.

Prevously, the Sheriff's Office stated they found drug paraphernalia, including syringes and a spoon, in Urker's bathroom next to a "brown powdery substance" after his body was discovered in bed.

"Deputies located a syringe and a handgun on the nightstand next to the bed," read a police report obtained by E! News Oct. 2. "Mr. Urker was found lying on the bed on his back, cold to the touch, and appeared to have been there for an extended period of time.".

Noting his face and arms were "blue," officers said Urker had "a paper towel placed on his left arm with what appeared to be blotches of blood.".

The confirmation echoes what Blanchard previously stated of her boyfriend’s passing, telling People he’d overdosed before passing away in his bed.

At the time, Blanchard, 35, grappled with whether or not Urker’s overdose had been intentional.

Industry observers note that “He was not in a good state of mind lately,” she explained to the outlet on Oct. 1, adding that her partner had endured “an unimaginable level of public scrutiny and relentless cyberbullying across social media platforms.”.

"The cruelty directed toward him was something no human being should have to endure,” the Life After Lockup star continued. “I believe the tremendous weight of that scrutiny and harassment had a profound impact on him, and I hope his passing serves as a reminder that there is a real person behind every screen, every headline, and every comment.”.

Blanchard's family also noticed that Urker had been struggling before his death. Her half-brother Dylan Blanchard and his wife Anita Blanchard told police that Urker "made comments of being depressed and upset" while attending church with them the night before he died, according to the police report.

The couple went to "check on" him at home Oct. 1 when they discovered his body, with the sister-in-law telling police that Urker was "in a 'dark place' and he did not show up to work today," per the command log obtained by E! News. She also told authorities Urker had made threats to kill himself.

As Gypsy Rose Blanchard grapples with the loss of her partner, she’s dedicated to preserving Urker’s legacy and ensuring Aurora will always know who her father truly was.

Sources indicate that “I will make sure she grows up knowing how deeply her father loved her and who he was beyond what strangers on the internet chose to say about him," Blanchard added. "There are no words that can express the depth of this loss. I ask for privacy and compassion for our daughter, our families, and everyone grieving Ken during this incredibly painful time. He will be deeply missed, forever loved, and never forgotten.".

But in the meantime, she was letting herself feel her grief.

Industry observers note that “I’m in disbelief. I can’t think,” she admitted. “I’ve screamed until my throat burns.”.

In a recent development, "He will forever be my soulmate and my red string, a connection that time, distance, and even death can never erase," she said. "Above all, Ken loved our daughter, Aurora. She is a beautiful reflection of his enduring spirit, and through her, a part of him will always remain with us.".
