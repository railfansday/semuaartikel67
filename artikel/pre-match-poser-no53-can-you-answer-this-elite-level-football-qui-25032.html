Can you tell us the answer to this quiz question?

Only 10 managers have ever had it. Brian Clough was the first, Sir Alex Ferguson was the longest, Sean Dyche and Eddie Howe were the last Englishmen, and Mikel Arteta is one of three Arsenal managers. What are we talking about?

We'll reveal the answer this time next week when we reach the next edition of The Pre-Match Poser – for now, here's the answer to last week's poser…Q. Tell us a player who could appear on this list and why: Jens Lehmann, Rogerio Ceni, Antonios Nikopolidis and David Seaman.A. Yes, they’re all goalkeepers, but that’s not integral: they’re all players who earned their first Ballon d’Or nomination after the age of 30: Seaman in 1996 (aged 33), Nikopolidis in 2004 (aged 33), Lehmann in 2006 (aged 36) and Ceni in 2007 (aged 34).

You could’ve had Teddy Sheringham (33, 1999), Marco Materazzi (33, 2006) or Antonio Di Natale (33, 2010) – or if you really wanted a goalkeeper, Andreas Kopke, who was 34 when he got the nod after Germany won Euro 96.
