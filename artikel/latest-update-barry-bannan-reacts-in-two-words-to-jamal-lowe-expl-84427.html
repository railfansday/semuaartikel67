Barry Bannan has issued a two-word reaction to the performance of Sheffield Wednesday teammate, Jamal Lowe, as the Owls made it two league victories in succession under newly-appointed head coach, Dave Challinor.

Having seen off Plymouth Argyle last Saturday, Challinor's first League One outing in charge of Wednesday on the road came in the form of a South Yorkshire derby against Daniel Stendel's lowly Barnsley outfit.

Backed by over 4,800 supporters, Challinor's side didn't disappoint as they raced into a two-goal lead within the first 14 minutes, with Lowe netting his first brace of the campaign.

The Tykes responded five minutes before half-time through the highly-rated Vimal Yonganathan, but back-to-back victories for the first time since September lifted the Owls up to third.

Upon taking the managerial role at Hillsborough, Challinor has plenty of quality and experience at his disposal, with both attributes possessed by Bannan and Lowe in particular.

The latter opened the scoring at Oakwell just eight minutes into the encounter as he side-footed Liam Cooper's knockdown from a free-kick past Cieran Slicker, before doubling the advantage six minutes later as he was found in acres of space by Bannan, then drifting inside and slotting the ball into the bottom-right-hand corner.

Those strikes were the Jamaican's first since his dramatic 98th-minute penalty against Huddersfield Town last month, taking his overall tally up to seven for the season.

Speaking post-match, Lowe claimed that he is capable of taking his tally up to 15 goals, which will be music to the ears of Owls fans, as well as Bannan, who issued a two-word reaction of: "Goals Like", with a picture of him and the versatile figure in celebration.

Across his 93 appearances for the Owls, Lowe has endured a mixed time, even after producing seven goal contributions last term.

In the wins over Plymouth and Barnsley, the 32-year-old has been stationed at left-wing-back, and received praise for his all-round performance from many Wednesdayites.

"Testament to the new manager people say oh J Lowe is lazy can’t run etc and not only does Dave have him run he puts him at LWB where he has to run and gets goals from there on top," said one of those supporters.

It has been revealed that another Wednesday fan commented: "Gave Jamal Lowe stick last couple of weeks but effort for tonight was brilliant. Credit where credits due well done 2 goals along the way! Long may it continue. Honolulu Wednesday!!!".

"Jamal Lowe has turned into peak Roberto Carlos," was a rather humourous summation of his exploits.

Owls fans, how did you view Jamal Lowe's all-round performance against Barnsley? Comment below!

Another verdict read: "Playing Lowe in (his) best position. GOAL. Well done Jamz.".

However, not all were overly convinced, with one supporter claiming: "Lowe has no clue how to defend.".

Challinor recently went viral due to an image of his notes during the FA Cup defeat to Oldham Athletic in his first game in charge, and despite a youthful side being defeated heavily in the EFL Trophy to Accrington Stanley, his league ventures have started strongly.

Wednesday are still not flawless, but the outlook of their season is certainly more rosey than in the final weeks of Henrik Pedersen's tenure, as they can only drop to fifth depending on Saturday's results.

With Plymouth expected to challenge for promotion, last weekend's 3-0 success was seen as a statement, whilst different characteristics earned three points against their local rivals.

Challinor will now hope his side benefit from an extra day's recovery for when they travel to Stevenage on Tuesday.
