Newly introduced Zealand's tourism board has taken advantage of all the excitement surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6's launch, as hordes of players who've changed their Xbox timezones to that of the world's second best rugby team (sorry) to jump into the game early.

"One perk of seeing the sun first? Getting GTA 6 first too," reads a post on the New Zealand tourist board's Instagram account: PureNewZealand. "We heard a few of you are trying the New Zealand trick to join us. We get it, and we'd love to have you." The post also includes a video of folks looking at all the natural beauty of New Zealand, presumably before they all look themselves indoors to play the video game, alongside the caption: "life, because we're getting GTA 6 first.".

Recent findings suggest that for those unaware, Xbox owners have been swapping their console time zones for years to Upcoming Zealand in order to take advantage of the region's access to games before anywhere else in the world. When games have, let's say, a worldwide release time of midnight across all nations, those living in New Zealand are able to jump in first as a result.

In a recent development, microsoft has for years been happy to let this practice continue on its platform, and as such a sort of cultural norm about hopping on a digital plane for a game's release has been established. If you want guidance, we have a guide on how to pull off the GTA 6 New Zealand trick yourself which can walk you through it.
