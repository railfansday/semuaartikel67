Nebraska Sen. Deb Fischer’s husband Bruce Fischer’s cause of death has been revealed.

“There has been a sad patch in the death of Senator Fischer’s husband, Bruce,” a spokesperson for the senator’s office stated in a statement via X on Friday, November 27. “Initially, it was believed that Bruce passed peacefully at home. Upon further investigation, it was learned that Bruce chose to end his life.”.

In a recent development, the statement continued, “The family is deeply grateful for the recent outpouring of sympathy for their loss, and asks for understanding in this time of mourning. There will be no further comment and no other details will be provided.”.

Deb, 75, announced her husband’s death on Monday, November 23. He was 75.

“It is with a heavy heart that I share the passing of my beloved husband, Bruce,” she said in a statement shared via X. “Bruce was my partner in life, my greatest source of strength, and the heart of our family. His kindness, integrity, and quiet strength left a lasting impression on all who knew him.”.

The statement then noted that Bruce died “peacefully” on November 22, “while doing what he loved most, spending time outdoors.”.

It has been revealed that “Our family is heartbroken by his passing, but we find comfort in the life we shared with him. I will cherish the decades we spent building a family in the Nebraska Sandhills,” Deb continued. “Above all, Bruce was a loving husband, a devoted father and grandfather, a man of faith and a rancher.”.

It has been revealed that deb and her husband shared sons Adam, Morgan and Luke. The couple had six grandchildren, as well as a Goldendoodle named Fred.

The statement concluded, “We sincerely appreciate the prayers, kindness, and support extended to our family during this difficult time.”.

Deb and Bruce, a cattle rancher from Valentine, Nebraska, met at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and tied the knot in 1972. After their wedding, the couple moved to his family’s ranch to raise their kids. Their family continues to operate the ranching business in the Nebraska Sandhills.

Fellow Nebraska politicians paid tribute to Bruce after his death.

“Senator Deb Fischer and her husband, Bruce, have been dear friends of ours for years,” Congressman Mike Flood wrote in a Facebook post. “Together, they raised a wonderful, hardworking family that reflects Bruce’s incredible work ethic and deep love for the Sandhills. Nebraska has lost one of its very best, and Bruce leaves behind a family that will forever be proud of the man he was. [My wife] Mandi and I are heartbroken by his passing, and our hearts are with Deb, their boys, and the entire Fischer family.”.

In a recent development, sen. Pete Ricketts added, “I am very sad to hear of the passing of Bruce Fischer. Bruce left his mark on this world through his love for family, his faith, and his ranch. [My wife] Susanne and I send our deepest condolences to Deb, the entire Fischer family, and all their loved ones during this difficult time. The Fischer family is in our prayers.”.
