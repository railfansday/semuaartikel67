Fabian Hurzeler will let Pascal Gross decide if he needs a rest ahead of Brighton's clash with Craiova to the experienced midfielder himself.

The 35-year-old has been an ever-present for the Seagulls this season, starting all 18 of his appearances across the Premier League, Conference League and Carabao Cup.

Gross has accumulated 1,526 minutes of football in all competitions, underlining his importance to Hurzeler's side and the workload he has carried during the campaign.

The German midfielder has been key since returning to the Amex from Borussia Dortmund, but with Brighton facing another managing his fitness is becoming an increasingly notable consideration - especially with Newcastle at the Amex Stadium this Sunday,.

Hurzeler, speaking ahead of the meeting with Romanian league leaders Craiova, remarked: "We are in communication, and I think the only person who can really listen to himself is himself,” said the German.

"I know he's an experienced player, so if the time comes, we will have that conversation and, of course, make the decision.

"He's a clear team player, so if he feels he can't play at his highest level because of his physical condition or because he's tired in certain moments, then he will speak up.

"We have a very trusting relationship, so we will make the decision together.".

Gross has already demonstrated remarkable durability this season and whether Thursday's European fixture provides the moment for a rest remains to be seen.

Injuries will also play a factor in Hurzeler’s thinking. Malick Yalcouye is out for “several weeks” with a knee issue and as yet, midfielder Mats Wieffer has not been added to the Conference League squad.

“I'm not really sure,” said Hurzeler to Sussex World when asked if they can include Wieffer and perhaps Yankuba Minteh to the Uefa list.

“We're trying to get an understanding of how things can work regarding this, but I can't say anything so far because we're still discussing what the best way to solve it is.

“He's definitely out for the next three Conference League games, but hopefully there's another possibility to replace him.”.

Diego Gomez and Olivier Boscagli are also sidelined for this one, while Chema Andres - who played in the 2-0 loss at Hull - is managing his fitness after withdrawing from Spain’s under-21 squad at the previous international break.

Yasin Ayari is also a midfield option for tonight.
