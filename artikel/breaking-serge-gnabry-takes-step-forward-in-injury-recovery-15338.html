Bayern Munich have delivered another encouraging injury update, confirming that Serge Gnabry returned to individual training at Säbener Straße on Monday morning.

This represents a significant step towards a comeback for the 31-year-old, who is recovering from muscular injury sustained in the 2-2 draw against Borussia Dortmund at the end of October.

However, despite this progress, Gnabry isn't expected to return to action until after the winter break, according to Bild.

Bayern's injured attacking players have generally made progress during the international break.

Recent findings suggest that luis Díaz has been able to resume partial team training, while Jamal Musiala is expected to return to the squad for Friday's game against 1. FC Köln.
