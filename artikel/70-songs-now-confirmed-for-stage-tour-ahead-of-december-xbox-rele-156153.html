We've got quite a few interesting Xbox releases still to come in December, and one of them that we've been keeping a close eye on is Stage Tour, which is a new Guitar Hero / Rock Band style game launching on December 10th.

According to recent reports, they're not messing around with this one either, having snagged loads of famous bands for the tracklist, which now stands at well over 70 songs. There are going to be 90+ by the time we get to release, so there's more to come.

Let's take a look at all the confirmed songs so far:.

Stage Tour is currently available to pre-order on the Xbox Store at a price of £31.49 / $35.99 (with Xbox Play Anywhere support included), but obviously that assumes you already have an instrument to play the game with, such as the Riffmaster that we reviewed last year.

You'll also find hardware being sold alongside the game at various retailers, with the game and guitar bundle retailing at £119.99 here in the UK. There's also a drum kit, and band kit, and much more on the official Stage Tour website.

I'm personally just planning to stick with the Riffmaster and see whether Stage Tour can do enough to hold my attention amidst the likes of Rock Band 4, Clone Hero and YARG across console and PC, and I'm particularly intrigued by the "all-new roguelite Tour Mode" that apparently makes for an endlessly replayable campaign.

Let's see how it goes, eh? We'll try and bring you some hands-on coverage of Stage Tour ASAP.
