AC Milan have found a new Italian opponent in the potential race to sign Bruno Fernandes this morning, it seems.

It’s no secret that Milan are interested in Bruno, nor is it a secret why. He got on fantastically with Ruben Amorim, and the duo could reunite next year if the Portuguese magnifico doesn’t renew at Old Trafford.

According to recent reports, the Red Devils do have the option of a year’s extension on his deal, without a need to enter negotiations – which is a hold-up point at the moment. However, this would be far from ideal for any suitors, as they’d need to negotiate with the club rather than pursue a pre-contract move.

According to Tuttosport, via Goal, Juventus are beginning to express an interest in signing Bruno Fernandes next year, with his deal expiring in the summer.

According to recent reports, of course, they won’t be alone in that race, though. Indeed, Milan are believed to be in the mix too. He has a connection with Amorim and they share an agency, so logic would suggest that they may have the lead in any potential proceedings.

Recent findings suggest that he’d also reportedly ‘welcome a return to Italy’ after his experiences with Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria. In something of a boost to their chances too, he has no interest in a move outside of Europe. Ruling out an exit to the MLS or Saudi Arabia.
