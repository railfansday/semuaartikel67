Costco's Thanksgiving Dinner Kit Is Officially Back—Here's What It Comes With

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Depending on who you are, cooking a full Thanksgiving dinner can bring either excitement or complete dread. If you fall into the latter camp, Costco is here to help with its newest offering in the deli department.

Laura Lamb of @costcohotfinds shared on social media that the warehouse’s Thanksgiving dinner kit has officially returned for 2026. The prepared meal includes all the holiday essentials, with much of the prep work already taken care of.

Recent findings suggest that each kit comes with a seasoned half turkey breast over a bed of herbed stuffing, along with Yukon Gold mashed potatoes, green beans, cranberry sauce, and gravy. Costco is pricing the meal at just $3.99 per pound, which works out to around $40 to feed seven to eight people.

Industry observers note that the label includes simple instructions for preparing all the components. The uncooked turkey, naturally, takes the most time. Since it’s placed over the stuffing, the turkey drippings add even more flavor and moisture as it roasts in the oven.

Recent findings suggest that the warehouse keeps this dinner kit pretty consistent from year to year, although one Costco shopper wasn’t as impressed with a particular component this time around. Costco fan and content creator @photogami reports that the gravy is a bit underseasoned and not as good as it was last year. (Thankfully, this glitch has a pretty easy solution.).

According to recent reports, lamb also noted that you’ll still need to buy a few extras to round out the meal, like dinner rolls and pumpkin pie. And if you’re a stuffing lover, the amount that comes in Costco’s kit might not be enough for you.

Still, it’s hard to argue with the price and convenience—and members are already eager to pick up a kit for their own holiday celebrations. “Got this last year and it was all absolutely delicious,” one user on Instagram wrote. “Highly recommend.” Another commented, “This is such a great idea for a smaller Thanksgiving!”.

Industry observers note that if you want to grab a kit for yourself, plan accordingly. All Costco locations are closed on Thanksgiving Day, so you only have a few days to snag one.
