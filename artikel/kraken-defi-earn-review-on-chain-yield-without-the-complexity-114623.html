According to recent reports, use Kraken DeFi Earn for cash or dollar-pegged stablecoins you keep at Kraken and could afford to lose, since its vaults lend them out for a variable yield without a crypto wallet for you to manage. Kraken keeps 25% of the rewards, and nothing insures the deposit, so keep your emergency fund in an FDIC-insured bank account.

DeFi Earn converts the cash or other stablecoins you deposit to USDC, a stablecoin pegged to the dollar, and lends it through blockchain lending markets such as Aave, where borrowers pay the interest you earn. It suits Kraken customers who want those lending rates without running a crypto wallet of their own, approving each blockchain transaction, or paying network fees.

Kraken customers holding stablecoins they could afford to lose get blockchain lending rates here, less the 25% of rewards that pays for the convenience. Savings you can’t risk belong in an FDIC-insured high-yield savings account instead.
