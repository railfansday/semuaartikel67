📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

When it comes to tactical stealth action, you can't get more definitive than Metal Gear Solid, even if it sometimes stretches the boundaries of belief in the process. Fortunately, those of you looking to experience it on mobile have a new option, as Delta Force once more teams up with Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater for a brand-new collab!

Running from November 27th until December 31st, the Delta Force x Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater collab sees the debut of the new Naked Snake appearance for Nox, available through research, and you can emulate Snake's typical 'stealthy' moves with the Cardboard Box Stealth action, entirely free for all players to claim.

Finally, keep your eyes peeled for Metal Gear-themed boxes scattered around Operations maps, giving you a chance to pick up further collab-exclusive items, like themed figurines to add to your collection!

While collabs can seem a bit samey after a while, you'd be hard-pressed to find a more fitting partnership than Metal Gear Solid with an equally tactically focused shooter. Admittedly, it does seem as though the collaboration has seen more than a few delays, so I'm sure many of you are just happy this collab is arriving and available from November 27th to December 31st!
