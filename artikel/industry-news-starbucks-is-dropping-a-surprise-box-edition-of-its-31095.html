Starbucks' biggest Bearista collection yet features five festive glass characters, including one rare mystery bear named Goldie.

Recent findings suggest that if there’s one thing Starbucks knows how to do during the holiday season, it’s give us another reason to celebrate. Between festive drinks in the iconic red cups and cute seasonal merchandise, there’s always something new to add to your holiday wish list. And if you’re a collector of Starbucks merch, you’ll want to mark your calendar.

It has been revealed that the coffee chain is getting ready to unveil its biggest Bearista collection yet, and this one comes with a little surprise. In fact, think of it as a holiday collectible meets a treasure hunt.

Starbucks is making the holiday season a little more exciting with the debut of its Glass Bearista Cold Cup: Surprise Box Edition. Arriving at Starbucks coffeehouses on Nov. 10, this collection features five hand-blown glass characters, each tucked inside a surprise box.

Recent findings suggest that that means you won’t know which Bearista you’ve gotten until you open it! The characters include Reiny, Joy, Aspen and Bunny, along with Goldie, a rare mystery bear that collectors will undoubtedly be hoping to find. Each character is super cute and guaranteed to bring a smile to your face as you sip your favorite Starbucks drink.

I love that Starbucks is jumping on the mystery box trend. It’s a great way to make something small like a coffee run just a little more exciting. I mean, who doesn’t love a surprise?

Sources indicate that ready to try your luck at finding Goldie? The Glass Bearista Cold Cup: Surprise Box Edition arrives at Starbucks coffeehouses on Nov. 10. Each box will be available for $39.95 each, while supplies last. There is a limit of two per customer, but I just know this is going to be popular. So if you’re excited, mark your calendar and be at your local Starbucks early.
