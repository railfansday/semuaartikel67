Ligue 1 outfit AS Monaco have reached an agreement with German youth international Paris Brunner (20) regarding a contract extension beyond 2028, according to RMC Sport, as reported via our colleagues at Get French Football News.

The young attacker is expected to sign a contract through to 2030 after scoring eight goals and providing one assists in 13 matches for Monaco in 2026/27 after joining the Principality club from Borussia Dortmund in the summer in search of senior football, having not made an appearance for BVB’s first team.

Brunner’s performances also coincided with a run in Antonio Di Salvo’s Germany U21 squad during the recent international break where he featured in defeats against Belgium and Croatia. He was, though, one of the winners in Germany’s youth team, which also saw the youngster earn praise from Di Salvo. “Moving Paris [to the left] was something that we wanted as we wanted to have a player like that on the left [wing]. It paid off on our second goal,” he explained.
