Industry News: Krispy Kreme Is Coming For Your Thanksgiving Dessert Table With Four Limited-Edition Doughnuts

It has been revealed that thanksgiving dessert tables have a pretty established pecking order. Pumpkin pie gets a spot, apple pie gets a spot, and someone always insists there’s a need for something chocolate. But who decided doughnuts couldn't join the party?

In a recent development, fans are already making their Thanksgiving donut preferences known. "Double chocolate crumble please," one person wrote, while another declared the “cinnamon apple looks amazing 😍." Others were a little less selective, commenting, "OKAY I NEEDDDDD 🔥" and “Ya I need these asap!”.

All four doughnuts arrived at participating Krispy Kreme locations nationwide on November 12 and will stick around through Thanksgiving Day, November 26, while supplies last. They're available individually or by the dozen, including for pickup and delivery through the chain's website and app.

If someone happens to bring a dozen to my Thanksgiving dinner, I certainly won't be the one complaining.
