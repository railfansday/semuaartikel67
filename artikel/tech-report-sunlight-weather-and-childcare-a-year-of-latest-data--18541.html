In 2026, Redfin added information about sunlight, weather, childcare access and more to home listings. Here is everything you need to know about what’s upcoming.

A home listing has always been good at telling you about a house: the square footage, the year it was built, the number of bedrooms. Historically, it has been less good at telling you what living there is actually like. Does the kitchen get morning sunlight? How hot are the summers? Is there a daycare within walking distance?

Those are the questions we set out to answer this year. Multiple new data sets arrived on Redfin in 2026, and they now sit alongside other information that was already there, including scores about a property’s walkability, transit access and climate.

Sunscore shows exactly how much sunlight a home gets.

Industry observers note that natural light is one of the hardest things to judge from listing photos or a single showing, and it matters more than you might expect. In a recent Redfin survey, nearly 70% of people said the amount of sunlight in their home affects their satisfaction with where they live, and 11% said it is non-negotiable when searching for a home.

Sunscore is a property-level rating from 0 to 100 that shows how much sunlight a home gets. We co-created it with Shadowmap, a sunlight analysis platform, using 3D modeling that tracks how shadows fall across a home throughout the day and across the seasons. Here are the score breakdowns:.

☀️ 90-100: Exceptional sunlight.

In a recent development, each score accounts for the home’s 3D shape and structure, surrounding buildings, trees and terrain, the sun’s position throughout the day, and seasonal changes across the year. In other words, it catches the things you would only discover after moving in—like a neighboring building that blocks the afternoon light in winter.

It has been revealed that and it is not only about comfort and aesthetics. A home’s sun exposure also states something about its solar potential, which can translate into real savings on energy costs over time.

Weather data helps predict precipitation and heat.

In a recent development, weather turns out to be one of the biggest reasons people move. In a recent Redfin survey conducted by Ipsos, 22% of Americans planning to move to another state said better weather was motivating the move—the most cited reason out of nearly 30 options, ahead of affordability, job opportunities and moving closer to family. Another 21% said concerns about climate change or natural disasters were shaping where they wanted to live.

According to recent reports, in July, we partnered with The Weather Company, owner of The Weather Channel app and weather.com, to bring historical weather data to every for-sale listing on Redfin. As our exclusive historical weather provider, it supplies the same data hundreds of millions of people check every month.

Childcare data displays daycare and preschool options.

For families with young children, childcare access can determine a neighborhood’s fit as much as school boundaries do. However, it’s historically been missing from the home browsing experience. Redfin is the first major real estate portal to partner with Winnie, the largest marketplace for childcare and early education in the U.S., to bring local childcare information to for-sale listings.
