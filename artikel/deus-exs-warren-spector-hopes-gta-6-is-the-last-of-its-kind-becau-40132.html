Deus Ex's Warren Spector hopes GTA 6 is "the last of its kind" because of its $1 billion budget

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that rockstar seems to have really, really stuck the landing with GTA 6. After a decade of astronomically high expectations, GTA 6 is launching this week to universal acclaim, tying the original Zelda: Ocarina of Time for highest rated game of all time on Metacritic. As the GTA 6 release time opens up for different players around the world, people are just beginning their rampages around Leonida and sharing latest discoveries by the minute. And yet, one very prominent RPG developer hopes we never see the likes of a game the size and scale of GTA 6 ever again.

In a upcoming Linkedin post, Warren Spector, best-known for creating the Deus Ex series, sounds plenty happy for Rockstar fans and developers celebrating a monumental critical achievement, but also concerned about the precedent it might be setting for other game developers.

"First, congratulations to everyone who worked on GTA 6. It looks like it will be a high point of sorts for our medium and our business," Spector claims, before pivoting. "However… It’s billion-dollar development cost and an undoubtedly crazy marketing budget don’t represent a business model anyone else can or probably should emulate. That’s especially true when thousands of developers are out of work.

It has been revealed that "A billion dollars pays for a lot of games and a lot of salaries. Personally I'd rather have hundreds of games and full employment than one game no matter how amazing it is.".

I definitely understand having an instinctual negative reaction to someone saying, quite explicitly, no more GTA ever again, or at least not at the scale of GTA 6, but my read here is that Spector is saying the amount of money that went into the game could support several game studios and hundreds of game developers at a time when game studios and game developers seem to constantly be on the chopping block.

Spector also notes GTA 6's success proves a point he's been making "for years," which is that "no game that matters ships on time and/or on budget," pointing again to GTA 6's staggering $1 billion budget and multiple delays.

Industry observers note that "Give a game the time and money to prove its potential (key word, potential) and you have a chance of making something great," he says. "Underfund a game or force it out the door before it’s ready guaranteed failure no matter how much potential it has.

According to recent reports, "So, again, congratulations. Ambition and innovation are necessary for the medium to reach its potential. I just hope GTA 6 is the last of its kind even if it’s the best we have to offer.".

According to recent reports, it's impossible to predict the full economic impact of a game like GTA 6. Even with its incredible reviews, some Rockstar developers are already worrying about potential layoffs now that the game is shipping. On the other hand, GTA 6's inevitable gargantuan commercial success could generate unprecedented revenue and have a reinvigorating effect on the gaming industry as a whole. One thing's for sure, we probably won't see another $1 billion game for a very, very long time, so I guess Spector is getting his wish.
