📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, 2026 has been quite the year for Anya Taylor-Joy, and it’s not over yet as she’s confirmed to reprise her role as Alia Atreides in Dune: Part Three, which opens in theaters on December 18. Just over one month ago, Taylor-Joy returned to Netflix to star in the twisty fresh thriller, Sacrifice, along with MCU veteran Chris Evans. Over the summer, she returned to Apple TV to headline one of the most underrated crime thrillers of the year, Lucky, along with Timothy Olyphant. This return was particularly noteworthy as Taylor-Joy is considered one of the streaming queens of Apple TV — her 2025 sci-fi thriller, The Gorge, is among one of the most-watched titles the platform has ever released. The fate of Lucky beyond Season 1 has not yet been decided.

Between all these new projects, Anya Taylor-Joy added to her box office total with the release of The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, which was the first feature film of 2026 to reach the $1 billion milestone at the box office. It fell short of the $1.3 billion total held by its 2023 predecessor, but it still did well enough to warrant another film, which isn’t expected to be released until 2029. At the end of its theatrical and VOD runs, The Super Mario Galaxy Movie was added to streaming on Peacock, but the film is soon to begin a conquest on a new platform. Netflix has confirmed that The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is going to join its streaming library in America on November 30. The film is expected to be a hot commodity on American watchlists as soon as it joins the platform.

Recent findings suggest that after she returns to Arrakis to star in Dune: Part Three, Anya Taylor-Joy has another big-budget blockbuster coming next year. She will star as an Elf named Seren in The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum, which is expected in theaters on December 17, 2027, the same day as Avengers: Secret Wars. Jamie Dornan is taking over the role of Aragorn in the film from Viggo Mortensen, but Ian McKellen and Elijah Wood are set to return as Gandalf and Frodo.

Be sure to check out The Super Mario Galaxy Movie when it’s released on Netflix later this month and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Anya Taylor-Joy’s future projects.

First declared during Nintendo's 2024 MAR10 day celebration, The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is the follow-up to the 2023 animated adventure film starring Nintendo's famous plumber mascot.
