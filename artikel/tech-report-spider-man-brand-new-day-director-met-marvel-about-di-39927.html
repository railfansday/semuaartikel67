Spider-Man Brand New Day director Destin Daniel Cretton had "one meeting" with Marvel about Spider-Man: Homecoming but it "didn't go anywhere.".

“Yeah, I had one meeting on Spider-Man: Homecoming. They were meeting a bunch of directors at that time I went in and met with producer Jeremy Latcham," Cretton revealed to Empire. "And it was a conversation that didn't go anywhere because I didn't really have a pitch. But that was my only interaction until I went in to seriously pitch for Shang-Chi.”.

Not only is that a fascinating insight into a sliding doors moment that eventually saw Cretton helm the most successful Spider-Man movie of all time (with over $2.5 billion at the box office), but it also shows just how early Marvel Studios have relatively unknown directors on its radar.

Cretton, of course, directed Shang-Chi before Brand New Day, sandwiching a stint as co-creator on the beloved Wonder Man series earlier this year. He was also once lined up to direct Avengers: The Kang Dynasty.

The director has been praised for his superhero action scenes – something that should stand him in good stead before he takes on Naruto on the massive screen.

Curiously, the only name Cretton mentioned – Jeremy Latcham – is just weeks away from taking the top job at DC Studios, replacing the ousted pair of James Gunn and Peter Safran.

Brand Newly introduced Day is returning to cinemas on December 4 for an extended cut which features never before seen scenes and extra "surprises." Then, Avengers: Doomsday gets set to blow us all away from December 16 in the UK and December 18 in the US.
