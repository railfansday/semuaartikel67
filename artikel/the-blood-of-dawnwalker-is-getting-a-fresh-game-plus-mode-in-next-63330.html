ENHANCEMENT 17TH NOVEMBER: The Blood of Dawnwalker update 1.2 - the update that brings a New Game Plus mode, predominantly - is now available on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series S/X.

Industry observers note that new Game Plus, as mentioned below, raises the level cap to 100, and scales enemies and regions in the game around you. Previously, the game was zone-scaled, so some zones were tougher than others. In New Game Plus, this will no longer be the case, so you can go and explore Xanthe's region at the back of Vale Sangora straight away, if you want to.

Recent findings suggest that there'll be latest versions of equipment with new stats to find, and they'll be labelled with "NG+" so you know when you've picked one up. You can unlock New Game Plus from the main menu after choosing your base save and difficulty option. All of your accrued skill perks and abilities will be available to you at the start of the Withering Away quest in the game's prologue.

ORIGINAL STORY 13TH NOVEMBER: A New Game Plus mode is coming to vampiric RPG The Blood of Dawnwalker next week.

Developer Rebel Wolves has now detailed everything coming with The Blood of Dawnwalker's Update 1.2, at the moment planned to go live sometime next week, and top of the list of changes is a New Game Plus for players who have already rolled credits on the game.

It has been revealed that "If you completed the Epilogue, you can dive right back into Vale Sangora with New Game+," the developer revealed earlier today. "Your level, skills, and inventory will carry over." Rebel Wolves is upping the level cap to 100 for New Game Plus, which will also have items with updated stats and enemies "balanced and scaled uniformly across all regions.".

Elsewhere in Revision 1.2, the studio is making tweaks to "enemy health pools and how often they use their abilities during combat," and improving terrain geometry to allow for "smoother movement during combat and exploration." We'll need to wait for more comprehensive patch notes before finding out which enemies are getting spongier or flimsier, however.

While the incoming patch doesn't seem to be as beefy as the last - the one that added animal petting and an easier Story mode - it'll be a good way to sink your teeth deeper into a game that's already begging to be replayed, thanks to a time management mechanic that can drastically change any given run.

The new game from some former The Witcher 3 veterans came out in September and seems to have been a hit. Eurogamer's The Blood of Dawnwalker review called the game a "familiar dark fantasy adventure, but one with bold and provocative ideas of its own, that's mostly, enormously successful.".
