Klinsmann: ‘Inter have to be very careful against Stuttgart, Esposito a step ahead of Tresoldi’

Jurgen Klinsmann has warned that Inter will need a strong defensive performance against VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on Wednesday evening and feels that Pio Esposito is at the moment a ‘step ahead’ of Nicolo Tresoldi, who remains eligible to represent both Italy and Germany at international level.

Klinsmann spoke in-depth to La Gazzetta dello Sport ahead of Inter’s Champions League clash against Stuttgart on Wednesday evening, discussing the ‘superior’ squad that Cristian Chivu has, the quality of his centre-forwards and the pending decision that Tresoldi has to make over his international future.

Inter are heading into their Champions League game against Stuttgart off the back of a 4-4 draw against Atalanta. The Nerazzurri had led 3-1 midway through the second half, but a particularly poor end to the game saw them drop two crucial points in Serie A.

“Unfortunately I only saw the last half hour, the bad part,” Klinsmann told La Gazzetta dello Sport.

“Inter always score a lot of goals, but sometimes they suffer a bit too much. But I wouldn’t worry, everybody knows the team’s value and they’re well aware of it in Germany as well.

It has been revealed that “They’re normal mistakes. When you play a more attacking game, like this year, you also risk conceding more goals. Every game is also different from the last, and it’s always difficult in Bergamo. It was in Inter’s hands, but it slipped away once they felt that they’d already won. Atalanta were also underestimated, it’s still the team that amazed all of Europe.”.

It has been revealed that asked for his assessment of Chivu as a head coach, Klinsmann stated: “He’s created a fluid team. He has a system that suits the players’ characteristics, the 3-5-2. And then, obviously, I’m crazy about Pio Esposito, who always puts on a show.

“Now Chivu has three heavyweights in attack. As well as Pio, Thuram has also refound his rhythm, and after his injury, Lautaro will return to the level we all know of him.

“It’s only November. The team has to still be in the running when it gets to the spring and everything is going at 200 miles per hour. As for the league, they shouldn’t look at the standings. Gattuso has Lazio’s enthusiasm, Gasperini is making Roma fly, but nobody has Inter’s squad.”.

Klinsmann was asked whether it could be a ‘problem’ later down the line to have three strikers who all deserve to start, when only two spots are available in the XI.

Recent findings suggest that “As long as you’re playing in all competitions, especially the Champions League, it’s fine to have three exceptional players and do some rotations,” he replied. “Given the number of matches, you can’t just have one starting pair to rely on.

“There is a bit of a risk to the group’s spirit, though, because all three are rightly ambitious and always want to play. And who do you leave out? Marcus has unique characteristics, Lautaro is the face of Inter and Pio makes Italy smile.”.

Speaking of Italy, Tresoldi has still not yet decided which nation he will represent at senior international level. The Club Brugge talent was born in Cagliari but moved to Germany as a teenager and is a regular for the Germany U21 squad. He is also eligible for Argentina.

Klinsmann indicates that he doesn’t know which way Tresoldi will turn either way, but he does not think it will be a major glitch if he does not select Italy, given Esposito’s talents.

“At the moment, Pio is a step ahead of Tresoldi, who is, however, still an enormous talent. We’ll see if he can make the step up in the coming months, but I really think he can.

“It would be great to have him for either nation, for us and for you, but certain decisions, made with the heart, have to be respected.”.

Klinsmann was also asked whether he sees any of his own playing qualities in any of Inter’s three first-choice strikers.

Recent findings suggest that “At a glance, I wouldn’t say any of them. To me, Pio reminds me of Aldo Serena: He’s always in the right place, he’s strong and intelligent, both protecting the ball and scoring. I was more about finding the space. I ran a lot, I could go to the left or the right. Maybe I’m a bit more of a Thuram.”.

Previewing Inter’s upcoming opponents, Stuttgart, Klinsmann remarked: “Stuttgart have returned to their previous level in recent years under Seb Hoeness. They won’t win the league every year given that there’s Bayern Munich, but they do have to compete at the top.

“As a club, they’re much more stable today than they were 10 years ago. They play beautiful, entertaining football in a stadium that was renovated for the last EUROs and it’s a little gem. Always sold out, 60,000 people. Stuttgart are all about the fans, you’ll see it in San Siro, those fans marching to the stadium before the match is a show within the show.”.

He also pointed to a few players to watch out for: “Undav and Demirovic can punish you. Out wide, Fuhrich somewhat resembles my old friend Alessandro Bianchi: You never know which way he’ll go. He always knows how to be dangerous and always gets a lot of assists.

Industry observers note that “As a squad, Inter are superior, but be careful: If Stuttgart catch you on the counter, you might struggle to fix it and turn the game around. INter might be the favourites, but they have to be very, very careful in defence.”.
