If there's one outerwear trend I can't stop noticing this season, it's faux fur. And Kylie Jenner's fashion brand KHY has certainly caught my attention. I can’t scroll on TikTok without someone mentioning how nice Jenner’s coat selection is. And given that they’re always sold out, I wanted to see what the hype was all about. KHY released in 2023, and while the brand has been releasing interesting pieces, their regular clothing hasn’t always grabbed my attention. But these coats seem to be the rising notable staple fashion people are wanting to buy.

Sources indicate that online shopping isn’t always the easiest, especially when it comes to coats. So, for my Los Angeles fashion lovers, you can experience KHY a little more close-up in the coming weeks. KHY will be opening up the I <3 LA POP-UP, its first space of its own, on Sunset Boulevard in West Hollywood. The limited-time pop-up will be open from Friday, December 4 through Sunday, December 13, and will feature an assortment of KHY's best sellers and exclusive pieces, including the KHY After Dark coats—which are the pieces I was able to snag for this review that are currently sold out on the website.

Industry observers note that because there is a ton of interest, I tried two of KHY's faux-fur coats for myself, photographing each to see how they look away from the perfectly styled campaign images. From the fit and feel to the overall finish, here's my honest verdict on whether Kylie Jenner's faux-fur coats deserve a place in your winter wardrobe.

This coat immediately caught my attention while on the KHY site. I love a dramatic moment and this fits the bill. I got the most compliments while wearing this black faux-fur coat and it makes sense because it’s the epitome of opulence. I’m wearing a 3X and I will say it’s a bit snug. I normally wear a 16/18 U.S. women’s, so that would translate to a 1X or 2X depending on the brand, so definitely sizing up would be ideal if you want a bit more room in your fit. The arm length was great and I dealt with minimal shedding.

Funny enough, I didn’t love this coat when I first pulled it out of the package, but my coworker and I laughed at how once I put it on it was actually so much cuter. This was also a fan favorite from one of my friends, as he said it seems “more versatile” than the dramatic all-black one. So if you’re looking for an everyday faux-fur coat, this one is definitely top of mind. This was also a 3X and it actually fit much looser than the Marmont Sculpted Coat, but the fur trim did interfere with the zipper that’s tucked beneath the chest area. So, if you’re a fuller-chest girl like myself, still sizing up would be your best route.
