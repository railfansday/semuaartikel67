Sources indicate that soFi Technologies (SOFI -4.31%) started out refinancing student loans. Today, it's a bank with eight separate businesses, each bringing in more than $100 million a year.

Alongside its third-quarter results on Oct. 27, management laid out a much bigger goal for the end of the decade. Here are the details and how it could get to the next level.

By 2030, SoFi wants to reach 100 million products, up from 26.7 million today. It also wants five businesses generating more than $1 billion in annual revenue, five more generating more than $500 million, and five more generating more than $100 million. That would mean 15 meaningful businesses, nearly double the eight it has now.

Recent findings suggest that going from 26.7 million products to 100 million by 2030 works out to roughly 36% annual growth. That sounds aggressive, but SoFi's product count grew 44% over the past year. In other words, SoFi can hit its goal even if growth slows from here.

NASDAQ · As of 3:39 PM ET 11/16/26.

There's plenty of room to run. SoFi's unaided brand awareness has risen to 11.6% from just 4.5% in 2022, so most Americans still don't know the organization well. And 51% of fresh products are now opened by existing members, up from 35% in mid-2025. This makes each additional product cheap to sell.

Lending is already SoFi's biggest business, generating about $807 million of adjusted revenue last quarter alone. Beyond lending, SoFi Money has 8.5 million products, SoFi Invest has 4.2 million, and credit card products grew 67% to about 656,000. Its loan platform business, which originates loans for other investors, brought in $153 million last quarter.

Sources indicate that soFi is also building newer businesses in crypto, payments, and business banking that could become meaningful contributors by 2030, and the company prepares to roll out latest products regularly going forward. If SoFi can maintain its current growth momentum and a similar cadence of new product rollouts, its revenue targets could be more achievable than they appear.

To be sure, SoFi is trying to do a lot at once, and a consumer credit downturn could sharply slow its lending business. The stock is also volatile, so even if SoFi hits its targets, the ride is unlikely to be smooth.

In a recent development, soFi's 2030 goals are bold, but they don't require the manufacturer to grow faster than it already is. Most of its eight $100 million businesses are still growing by more than 30% annually, and it has several younger businesses just getting started. If it keeps executing, today's stock price could look like a bargain by the end of the decade.
