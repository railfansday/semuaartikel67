Hotel groups have spent years buying lifestyle brands. Accor just committed $1.8 billion to buyout the minority investors in its Ennismore joint venture. A comparison with past deals adds some enlightening context.

Accor's buyout of its partners in its Ennismore joint venture, announced Tuesday, is the largest amount a hotel group has paid for a lifestyle brand firm to date.

The Paris-based group will pay about €1.6 billion (nearly $1.8 billion) in a multi-stage deal that values the lifestyle venture's equity at about €3.7 billion ($4.1 billion).

It's the latest in a string of lifestyle-brand purchases by major hotel groups. Marriott bought the citizenM brand for $355 million. Hilton got Graduate for $210 million. Hyatt paid $150 million upfront for The Standard, in a deal that eventually totaled about $335 million. IHG picked up Ruby for about $116 million.

Accor's buyout will be more than the four deals above, combined. A look at the deal and recent comparisons helps cast some light on the lifestyle trend and what Accor is betting on.
