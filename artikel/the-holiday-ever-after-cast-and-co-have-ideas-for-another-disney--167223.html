📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that with Hallmark’s upcoming movie, Holiday Ever After: A Disney World Wish Come True, set to premiere on the 2026 Christmas movie schedule, it’s hard not to think about a possible sequel for this Disney World-set movie. Considering it took place in Florida, it only feels natural that it could expand to more Disney Parks around the world. So, I asked the cast, director and a producer where they want to go next for another Hallmark romance, and I’m obsessed with their answers.

In a recent development, despite feeling like vampires while filming at Disney World at night, the cast of Holiday Ever After: A Disney World Wish Come True had a blast working and living in the parks. So, they were all thrilled when I brought up the idea of doing another one in a fresh location. For director Ryan Landels, Tokyo is the dream, as he explained:.

My gosh! I mean, Disneyland's in my backyard. I have a soft spot in my heart for it. But I have to say, I had the good fortune to go to [Tokyo DisneySea], and that park is unbelievable.

As he said that, Richard Kind, who plays Alan, jumped in to ask why DisneySea differs from the other parks. In response, Landels explained that it takes some inspiration from “Jules Verne,” and he noted that there are rides there he’d “never seen” before.

Recent findings suggest that kind was over the moon to learn all about this, as he excitedly remarked that he “had no idea” the park was like that. And I have to say, my excitement about taking a Hallmark movie to Japan matches Kind’s. In response to that, the director of Holiday Ever After said:.

According to recent reports, no, it has its own thumbprint. It has its own identity. And I mean, I know that all the Disney parks globally distinguish themselves in certain ways, but to me, that one was just a lovely experience going there. So that would be my vote.

Again, I think this would get my vote too. As someone who has only been to the parks in the United States, I think it'd be so fun to live vicariously through these characters as they head to DisneySea. Seconding that, Richard Kind excitedly mentioned:.

Industry observers note that that is my desire to go to Tokyo. But I had no idea that it was different, that Tokyo Disney is different. I would love it. I would love it. I would love it.

Christy Carlson Romano, who was sitting between the two men and plays Nicole in the movie, also said “sign me up” in regard to that idea. Bryce Durfee, who plays Sam, also said “let’s go to Tokyo” during my interview with him, Kiera actress Taegen Burns and producer David Wulf. However, while he name-dropped Tokyo (and Paris) as well, there’s a different location he discussed with Durfee and Burns that I think is worthy of (another) movie too.

While the cast and creatives named a few great places Holiday Ever After could go next, there’s also something to be said for staying in Disney World. To that point, producer David Wulf told me:.

Industry observers note that I still feel like we could stay in all the same parks and still do an entirely amazing movie and see more. You know, I mean, there's more to see. So I feel like you don't have to go far. I can still stay here.

He makes a fair point: Disney World is a massive place, and they could easily make a sequel there. To that point, I asked them which rides they’d like to highlight if they were to keep the Holiday Ever After magic in Florida. Here’s what they had to say:.

Well, there’s certainly more magic to see at Disney World. However, I’m also hoping that Holiday Ever After’s team finds themselves in Japan too. Really, I’ll be happy with a movie at any of the Disney Parks. However, these ideas the cast and creatives threw my way are phenomenal.

Considering Hallmark has made multiple NFL romance movies now, I feel like traveling to various Disney Parks for romances is not that far-fetched. So, here’s hoping Hallmark sees this and turns Holiday Ever After into a franchise that hops to the Disney Parks around the world.

In a recent development, now, if you’d like to see their adventures in Disney World, you can catch Holiday Ever After on November 28 on the Hallmark Channel at 8 p.m. ET. You’ll also be able to stream it on Hallmark+ or with a Hulu or Disney+ subscription the following day.
