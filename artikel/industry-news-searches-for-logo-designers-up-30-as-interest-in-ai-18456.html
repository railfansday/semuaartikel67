It has been revealed that NEWLY INTRODUCED YORK, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Search interest in logo designers is climbing as interest in AI logo generators declines, according to a new analysis of Google Trends data by Digital Silk. Worldwide searches for "logo designer" rose 30% over the past 12 months compared with the preceding year. Searches for "AI logo generator" fell 50% over the same period.

Industry observers note that the shift matters because consumers judge companies by their logos. According to recent logo statistics, 60% of consumers said they would avoid buying from a manufacturer if they don't like its logo.

Below are two things businesses should know before using an AI-generated logo and a case study of an AI concept developed into a finished logo, followed by tips for assessing a logo.

What Businesses Should Know Before Using an AI-Generated Logo.

When Is It Better to Hire a Professional Logo Designer?

According to recent reports, AI logo generators are useful for exploring ideas, but the results often need further refinement before they're ready for commercial use. A professional designer can help develop custom typography, establish a consistent color palette and ensure the logo works across websites, social media, print materials and other applications.

This approach is particularly useful when a business already has an AI-generated concept but needs a more complete visual identity. For example, Gehrke Law, a family law and criminal defense practice, had an AI-generated logo concept that lacked defined typography, colors and supporting visual elements. Digital Silk refined the concept into a finished logo and developed a consistent visual identity for the firm.

Sources indicate that rather than replacing the original idea, professional design helped turn it into a consistent brand identity that could be used across the firm's digital presence. This illustrates how AI can support the early creative process while professional expertise addresses the finer details of brand development.

It has been revealed that one way to test your logo's effectiveness is to show it to five people unfamiliar with your business for just five seconds. Ask them what they think your company sells or what industry it operates in. If their answers are consistently off the mark, your logo may not be communicating your brand clearly enough.

According to recent reports, it's also worth checking how distinctive your design is. Use a free AI logo generator to create several examples for businesses in your industry and compare them with your existing logo. If the results look noticeably similar, your design may lack the originality needed to stand out from competitors.

According to recent reports, finally, test your logo at different sizes, particularly on browser tabs, social media profiles and mobile screens. Fine details, thin lines and small lettering can become difficult to recognize when scaled down. If crucial elements disappear or become blurred, simplifying the design can improve its readability and versatility.

"AI tools are useful for exploring ideas, and plenty of business owners tried them first," said Grant Visser, Director of Brand Strategy at Digital Silk.

In a recent development, "Once a logo has to work on a storefront, a website, packaging and a contract, people come back to a designer. When six in ten customers say they'd skip a tech giant over its logo, a free draft is a risky place to stop," Visser added.
