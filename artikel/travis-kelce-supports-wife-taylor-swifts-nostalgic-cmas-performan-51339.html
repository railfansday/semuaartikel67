Man, it’s been a while since Taylor Swift graced the Country Music Association Awards stage, and, naturally, husband Travis Kelce didn’t miss a moment of it.

Recent findings suggest that “Brad Paisley, Sugarland, Little Major Town, Eric Church, Lainey Wilson, Brooks & Dunn, Bill Anderson AND Keith Urban were all in Taylor’s first #CMAawards performance in 13 years!?” the official Taylor Nation account wrote via Instagram on Wednesday, November 18, alongside footage from the 2026 CMAs set. “Oh, the rivers we just cried.”.

Kelce, 37, was quick to drop a “like” on the upload, as did the couple’s friend Brittany Mahomes. Brittany, 31, the wife of Kelce’s Kansas City Chiefs teammate Patrick Mahomes, even attended the red carpet occasion with Swift, 36. (Kelce and Patrick, 31, both skipped the CMAs, likely due to their NFL commitments.).

Swift attended the CMAs for the first time in 10 years, where her song “I Knew It, I Knew You” was up for Single of the Year. (Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” ultimately took home the trophy.).

According to recent reports, in addition to her CMA nod, Swift performed “I Knew It, I Knew You” on Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena stage. Dressed in a sparkly Erdem minidress and cowboy boots, Swift paid tribute to her country roots via cameos from the likes of Paisley, 54, Church, 49, and Urban, 59. The pop star then surprised fans when she launched into a rendition of “Our Song” from her debut album — the first song she ever sang at the CMAs.

“I Knew It, I Knew You” has marked Swift’s long-awaited return to the country music genre.

Sources indicate that “I love my country roots more and more as I go further in this path,” Swift said during a Grammy Museum retrospective in August. “I want to honor them more and more, the artist who shaped who I am, and that all happened in Nashville.”.

Sources indicate that swift, who ventured into the pop music genre with 2014’s 1989, was first romantically linked to Kelce in summer 2023. They tied the knot three years later in a star-studded wedding held at Madison Square Garden, which even became fodder in CMAs host Lainey Wilson’s monologue on Wednesday.

“This is bigger than me: We have to talk about the biggest story of the year. Somebody got married,” Paisley remarked, crashing Wilson’s opening to reveal the bride was a “blonde singer” who married a “football player.”.

Wilson, 34, married former football star Devlin “Duck” Hodges and jokingly assumed Paisley was referring to her May nuptials.
