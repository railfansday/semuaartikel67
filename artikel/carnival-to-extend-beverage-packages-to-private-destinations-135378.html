Carnival to Extend Beverage Packages to Private Destinations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Carnival Cruise Line is expanding its onboard beverage packages to two of its private destinations: Celebration Key and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

According to a recent press release, passengers will be able to order drinks included in their packages at the destinations starting in February 2027.

The extension will be available for Carnival’s CHEERS!, CHEERS! Zero Proof™, Bottomless Bubbles™ and CHEERS! Social drink packages.

Carnival noted the move will “give guests who purchase these packages greater value for their vacation dollars.”.

The change brings Carnival closer to other cruise lines that already extend their beverage packages to private islands.

While some brands, including Norwegian Cruise Line and MSC Cruises, limit the land extension to their premium packages, others include all packages.

In related news, Carnival also announced the first details of the upcoming Carnival Destiny, which will enter service in 2029, as well as an order for two smaller newbuilds.

The company is also investing in Paradise Collection, its portfolio of private destinations across the Caribbean and the Bahamas.

Celebration Key will see the biggest investment, with a significant expansion project set to take place over the next few years.

The private destination in Grand Bahama will see its daily capacity increased by 6,000 guests, with the expansion divided into two phases.

The first will be ready in 2028 and will feature a heated, beach-entry pool with a swim-up bar, as well as six waterslides, a dry climbing structure and a drenching bucket for children.

In 2030, the second phase will add a major water park with 18 waterslides, a lazy river with float-up bars and swim-in/swim-out cabanas, as well as a new exclusive area with an infinity pool, more beach access and new dining and bar concepts.

Carnival noted that, with six exclusive destinations in five countries, its lineup is the largest in the industry.
