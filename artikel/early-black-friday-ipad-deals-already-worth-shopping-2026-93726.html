There's no denying that 2026 has been a slower year for the iPad lineup. Only the iPad Air and iPad Mini received updates (with nothing latest for the iPad or iPad Pro since 2025), and in June, the entire lineup was subject to a painful price hike of either $100 or $200, depending on the model. But that's not to say you need to abandon your dreams of getting a good deal on a great iPad this year.

Industry observers note that in the lead-up to Black Friday, there are already some solid sales that make now a smart time to shop for an iPad. For more shopping advice from our team of product testers and deal hunters, check out our Best Early Black Friday Deals, Best Amazon Early Black Friday Deals, and Best Early Black Friday Laptop Deals.

Even though it's more than two years old at this point, the 2024 iPad Mini is the best iPad deal ahead of Black Friday. It's $100 off, bringing the price down to $499. That's just $50 more than the base iPad (the most affordable in the lineup), below. With its tiny size and weight, the iPad Mini is the perfect device to take on the go with you. You can also conveniently use it one-handed, which is especially helpful if you're using an Apple Pencil Pro in your other hand. Don't get me wrong: The improvements to the newer iPad Mini are substantial and worthwhile to the right person. The updated OLED screen is gorgeous; the chassis is even thinner; and the front-facing camera is better for video calls, having been moved to the side bezel. But because it's $300 more expensive, most people should stick with the older model—especially since the iPad Mini is better-suited for more casual tasks, like reading and note-taking.

Recent findings suggest that if you're trying to save the most cash, the base iPad is the absolute cheapest. You might appreciate the larger screen over the iPad Mini, too. While $50 isn't a huge discount—and it's also a fairly common price—hopefully we'll see deeper cuts in the future. Even so, this is a good buy right now, particularly if you're looking for a tablet to use immediately, whether for an upcoming flight or to keep kids busy on a long car trip.

The iPad Air is the in-between iPad. It's not as cheap as the base iPad, nor does it have the Tandem OLED screen and high-end features of the iPad Pro. And yet, I find that it's the iPad people should buy if they intend to use it for work. That's primarily because it has both a powerful M4 chip and can be connected to a Magic Keyboard with full trackpad support. The most recent versions of iPadOS make windowing much easier, and Apple even made the cursor work more like macOS. After all, most people don't need an OLED screen to do some word processing, answer emails, and take the occasional Zoom call.

When you're ready to use it more like a traditional tablet, just pull it off its magnetic cover, and it's ready for your fingers or an Apple Pencil. The 11-inch model is still small enough to not feel overwhelming in your hands. And while the $649 sale isn't particularly rare, it's not a bad price for a tablet that functions pretty well as a laptop replacement.

Recent findings suggest that iPad Mini (2026) for $599 ($100 off) : The latest iPad Mini came out just last month, and this is the first time it's gone on sale. It's a significant upgrade from the 2024 iPad Mini, with its OLED display and improved performance. But as a small, one-handed tablet, the previous-gen iPad Mini (A17 Pro) for $499 is the better deal right now—and the smart buy for most.

According to recent reports, iPad Pro 11-inch for $1,099: It's a little surprising that the iPad Pro hasn't been discounted at all for Black Friday. At $1,099, it's cheaper than the 13-inch MacBook Air, which uses the same M5 chip. The difference? You still have to buy the Magic Keyboard separately to pair with the iPad Pro. All in all, the iPad ends up being a much more expensive device.
