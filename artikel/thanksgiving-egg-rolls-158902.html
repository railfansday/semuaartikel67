📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Thanksgiving leftovers are a thing of beauty, but it’s time to move beyond the classic Thanksgiving sandwich. When we want to turn our holiday leftovers into a satisfying appetizer, these egg rolls are what we turn to. They have a perfectly crunchy exterior and are filled with all of our favorite Thanksgiving dishes: leftover turkey, mashed potatoes, cranberries, and stuffing. It’s the perfect way to use up every last bite of those beloved leftovers—here’s how to make them:.

Making Thanksgiving egg rolls:The key to keeping your egg rolls together when frying? Don’t overstuff them. If you add too much filling, you run the risk of your egg rolls exploding when fried. Plus, when it comes to fillings, you don’t have to limit yourself to what we included here. If you have any leftover veggies, or want to switch up your mashed potatoes (garlic mashed potatoes, anyone?), feel free to adapt these to what you have in your fridge.

Sources indicate that variations:Prefer to leave your oven off? Follow our air fryer egg rolls method and adapt for this filling.

Serving Thanksgiving egg rolls:Of course, these egg rolls wouldn’t be complete without leftover gravy to dunk them in. That being noted, if you just can’t get enough of cranberry sauce (or have way too many leftovers), feel free to serve these alongside a bowl of that as well.

According to recent reports, make-ahead & storage: If you want to freeze these egg rolls, fill and prepare them, then space them out on a lined freezer-safe tray to avoid any sticking. Wrap them tightly, then store in the freezer for 2-3 months. When you’re ready to enjoy, simply take them out and pop them into the fryer (or air fryer).
