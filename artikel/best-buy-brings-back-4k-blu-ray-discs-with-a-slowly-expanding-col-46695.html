It’s been nearly three years since Best Buy decided to ditch selling physical copies of movies, stripping its inventory from both its brick-and-mortar stores and its online catalog. But with the growing rise of 4K Blu-ray collections as a vinyl-esque collector’s item, the electronics giant has slowly begun dipping its toes back into the physical media game.

Sources indicate that as spotted by TheHDRoom on Twitter and on the 4K Blu-ray subreddit, Best Buy is now selling 14 different 4K Blu-rays on its website, marking another step in the brand’s slow return to selling physical media. These films include:.

Industry observers note that technically, Blu-ray returned to Best Buy’s online storefront in August of 2025, when it opened a third-party marketplace similar to Amazon or Walmart. These titles, however, are shipped and sold by Best Buy. Everything’s still online-only, unfortunately, save for The Odyssey, which launched in-store shelf displays alongside yesterday’s release date. It shouldn’t come as too much of a surprise that The Odyssey is getting some special treatment; in addition to its status as a box office juggernaut, Nolan’s previous movie, Oppenheimer, initially sold out of physical copies in its first week of release in 2023.

Outside of these 14 titles, additional Blu-rays continue to be sold through Best Buy’s third-party marketplace.
