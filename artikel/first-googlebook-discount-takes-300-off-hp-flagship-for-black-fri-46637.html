It has a 14-inch 2.8K OLED touchscreen and is powered by the Snapdragon X Elite (like the Dell XPS) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. Premium features include a 19-hour battery life, haptic touchpad, and face unlock to complement the fingerprint sensor. There are two USB-C ports and a headphone jack.
