There's no shortage of limited and unique Magic: The Gathering cards in the world, from old cards with limited copies left in the wild to newer serialized cards, but what makes the card we're talking about today so cool is that it may be the very first card of its kind, and it has a fun story behind it.

According to recent reports, I spend a lot of time looking for cool and interesting Magic stories around the world, but this one found me! A Magic player named Mike Austin reached out about what is currently the only copy of the foil borderless Bruce Banner / The Incredible Hulk card (#0359), signed by actor Lou Ferrigno and graded by CGC. As someone who loved the look of the Incredible Hulk card when it was previewed, especially due to its ability to go infinite when paired with a card like Caltrops and something to give it invincibility, I wanted to know more about how this card came to be. So, here's the story of how Lou Ferrigno ended up signing his first Magic: The Gathering card.

According to recent reports, the story of this awesome card starts with owner Mike finding a copy of Bruce Banner / The Incredible Hulk inside a Marvel Super Heroes Pack. It's the borderless foil variant with the green background, and while the comic covers are considered the most valuable cards in the set, this one is just as cool because the artwork pops off the vibrant green background.

Industry observers note that mike then took this card to GalaxyCon with the goal in mind of having the original Hulk actor, Lou Ferrigno, sign it. Luckily for Mike, he achieved this goal and even did so with a CGC witness to verify the signature in person. On top of that, during a polite back-and-forth with the actor, Mike notes Ferrigno claimed, "I've never signed another Magic: The Gathering Incredible Hulk card before today.".

According to recent reports, this was an absolute dream for Mike, and not necessarily because of the value it adds to the card; of course, there is always the reality where Ferrigno simply didn't know he was signing a Hulk Magic card previously, and it could be that this was just the first time he was seeing one of the borderless foils, which are very eye-catching. On the flip side, given that the Marvel MTG set had been released just a few weeks before this encounter, it's highly likely this was the first MTG card he signed; sadly, that is virtually impossible to prove. Regardless, it's a great story to carry around with the card and share with friends and enthusiasts alike.

You can read a more detailed timeline of events over on MTGCommand where Mike has documented the journey of having the card signed and graded.

Industry observers note that after submitting the card for grading, Mike waited just over two months for it to be returned, and when it was, the card itself received a CGC grade of 8, which he admits he was a little disappointed by. The good news, however, is that the autograph grade came back as a 10, more than making up for the lost points on the card itself.

Industry observers note that there are at the moment only five CGC-graded versions of this card; the other four have higher grades than Mike's, but none are signed by Lou Ferrigno, and from a quick look across other grading services and online listings, we can find no other Lou Ferrigno-signed Hulk MTG cards. We'd be fairly confident in saying that, right now, this is a one-of-a-kind Magic card, not just because it's signed by Ferrigno, but also because it's graded, with the signature verified and authenticated.

Since the Marvel Universes Beyond set released, more time has passed for people to potentially get their cards signed by Ferrigno, but as of right now, none of those cards are in the public domain as sales listings or gradings.

If you have a Magic card that you feel is just as unique as the one Mike reached out to us with, feel free to do the same!
