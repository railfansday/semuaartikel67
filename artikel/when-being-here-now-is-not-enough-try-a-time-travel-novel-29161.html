Updated November 17, 2026 | Reviewed by Lybi Ma.

I’ve always loved time travel tales. A psychological theme of my life has been a sort of nostalgia: a yearning to return to earlier times. It’s also a longing to jury-rig the implacable pace of time itself, the way it goes forward day by day, making our pasts inaccessible while our futures sneak up on us.

Industry observers note that my fantasy of traveling back or zooming ahead for a peek seems common; lately, many novels and films have made time travel a popular subject. I decided to catch up on some newer novels. What follows are my thoughts on one I especially enjoyed.

It has been revealed that claire North is my upcoming favorite author. With a backlist of numerous award-winning novels, the newest one is the first one I’ve read. As We Fall Through Time is a gripping adventure that maintains a fast pace and an imaginative journey from the distant past to the far future. Especially toward its complex and breathless finale, I found myself seeing this book as a film, complete with great CGI or other technological methods that activate all of our senses at once.

Still, we don't really need a film with an author who showcases this level of verbal talent. The novel is cross-cultural, diverse in countless ways, including a protagonist who switches from male to female and back (North is nonbinary), a grand mix of smart old-style and up-to-the-minute writing: I loved it. Not to mention scenes set in a multitude of world cities and pre-cities.
