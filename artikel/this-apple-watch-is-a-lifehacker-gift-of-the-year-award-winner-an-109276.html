📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Apple has three main Apple Watch "lines." There's the flagship, currently the Series 12, which represents Apple's mainstream wearable. For athletes and people who think they'll put their watches through some extreme scenarios, there's the Ultra line, currently the Apple Watch Ultra 4. And while both the Series 12 and Ultra 4 are excellent devices in their own right, they can be pretty pricey (particularly the Ultra). That's why the third category, the SE, is actually best for most people, and Lifehacker's choice for best smartwatch gift of the year. Better yet, the third-generation SE is currently $50 off ahead of Black Friday, perfect for most people asking for an Apple Watch this year.

The SE 3 does just about everything you'd expect an Apple Watch to do. It, of course, has a heart rate sensor to keep tabs on your pulse, including high and low heart rate alerts; the ability to track dozens of different workouts and exercise types (including swimming, thanks to the device's water resistance); a sleep tracker with daily analyses available in the Health app; notification forwarding to check in on your alerts without opening your iPhone; and the ability to send and receive text messages.

Sources indicate that it comes in two sizes (40mm and 44mm) depending on how large or small you prefer your watch. No matter which model you get, the watch comes with the S10 chip, Apple's last-generation watch processor. While it isn't as fast as the current S11 chip, the S10 is more than capable of running watchOS 27. Apple claims the SE 3 can run for up to 18 hours on a single charge, and up to 32 hours in Low Power Mode, with fast charging from 0% to 80% in 45 minutes.

Industry observers note that the majority of the features missing from the SE 3 aren't essential. Apple's other watches have things like bigger, brighter displays, additional health tracking tools like hypertension alerts and an ECG sensor, longer battery life, and Audio Intelligence—the latter of which might be a benefit to miss, depending on who you are.

Industry observers note that if your giftee wants or needs any of those additional features, Series 12 or Ultra 4 might be a better choice. But for anyone who wants the essential Apple Watch experience, the SE 3 is simply the best-value watch Apple sells right now—especially at this discount. Right now, the watch starts at $199 for the 40mm watch, or $229 for the 44mm. You can also save on the cellular versions of these watches: $249 for the cellular 40mm watch, and $279 for the cellular 44mm model.

Yes, Apple participates in Black Friday, though you may want to compare their sales with other retailers like Best Buy and Walmart. So far, the best Black Friday sale on an Apple product is the M5 MacBook Air on sale for $200 off, the lowest price since the price hike this summer.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, Nov. 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early, and we expect sales to wind down by Dec. 2, 2026.
