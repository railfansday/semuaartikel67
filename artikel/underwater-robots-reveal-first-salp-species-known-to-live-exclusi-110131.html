Underwater robots reveal first salp species known to live exclusively in deep sea

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (94 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, salps are barrel-shaped gelatinous animals that play an notable role in ocean ecosystems. More than 20 years ago, researchers observed an unfamiliar salp in the deep waters of Monterey Bay. Using specimens and data collected by MBARI's advanced underwater robots, Larry Madin, deputy director emeritus at the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution and an MBARI adjunct, recently published a detailed description of the glass salp (Vitreosalpa gemini) in the journal Marine Biodiversity. This striking species is the first salp known to live exclusively in the deep sea.
