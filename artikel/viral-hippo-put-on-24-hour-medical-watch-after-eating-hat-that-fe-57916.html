Fiona, the viral hippo at the Cincinnati Zoo, is now under a constant 24-hour medical watch after she ate a knitted hat that fell into her enclosure.

When you go to the zoo, there are plenty of warnings around enclosures that say don’t feed the animals or toss anything into their enclosures. If something falls in, your best bet is to ask a zookeeper to see if they can help – not to try to jump in and deal with it yourself.

Over in Cincinnati, Ohio, one zoo-goer accidentally ended up feeding Fiona the viral hippo something that isn’t in her typical diet – a knitted hat.

Industry observers note that as per CNN, the hat had been tossed up into the air from in front of the habitat and got caught by a ceiling fan. That fan then slingshotted the hat through a small one-foot gap above the 12-foot-tall viewing window.

“We probably couldn’t make it happen again if we tried,” Cincinnati Zoo’s spokesperson told CNN.

They also noted that Fiona – who initially went viral for being born underweight and surviving – is getting “24-hour care and attention” as a result of the incident, which happened back on November 9.

In a recent development, “Our best chance for success with Fiona would be if she can pass it on her own,” the spokesperson added, noting that hippo was given medication and laxative-like supplements to help “push things” along. That didn’t happen, though, and they’re still waiting for the hat to come out of her body.
