Carla Ward was appointed Republic of Ireland manager in January 2025.

Republic of Ireland manager Carla Ward states she is close to agreeing a contract extension, as she named her squad for their 2027 Women's World Cup play-off final with Poland.

The former Aston Villa manager replaced Eileen Gleeson at the helm in January 2025 and has led the side to promotion to League A in the Nations League and their highest ever position in the Uefa rankings.

The Republic of Ireland have lost just one of their past six games and now face Poland in a play-off for a spot at the World Cup in Brazil next summer.

Ward's side travel to Gdansk for the first leg on 1 December before the reverse fixture four days later at Dublin's Aviva Stadium.

The 42-year-old's current deal expires at the end of the 2027 World Cup qualification cycle.

Football Association of Ireland (FAI) chief executive David Courell said earlier this month that there were "live conversations" around Ward's contract.

"I want to get one thing straight - last time I said I wanted to focus on the next four games," said Ward.

"What I mean by that is, I want the focus to be on the players and certainly not myself.

"But what I can say to everybody is, I think you all know that I love being here. I want to be here. I have absolutely no intention of going anywhere.

In a recent development, "Thankfully, the FAI feel the same. So yes, just to echo David's words, we're close. And look, we're happy. I'm happy. So, yeah, I'm sure you'll have me for a little bit longer.".

Ward in talks over Republic of Ireland contract extension.

Two down, two to go - Irish target back-to-back World Cups.

Republic of Ireland to 'lock in' for World Cup dream.

Saoirse Noonan returns to the Republic of Ireland squad for the Poland games after sitting out the play-off semi-final victory over Kazakhstan because of an ankle injury.

The Watford forward comes in for Jess Ziu, who has a shin injury, in the only change to the squad.

Ward stated that "now is not the time for trying upcoming things" with the squad, which includes Crystal Palace midfielder Shanade Hopcroft after her first call-up for the Kazakhstan games.

Republic of Ireland secured 3-1 and 1-0 victories over the Poles when the sides met in World Cup qualifiers in April.

Poland edged out Belgium on penalties in their semi-final and Barcelona striker Ewa Pajor poses a dangerous attacking threat.

"They're a top side, they're a really good side," added Ward.

Sources indicate that "They've proven that. They've got arguably one of the best forwards in the world and I think that their performance against Belgium was superb, home and away. I think they were excellent.

Recent findings suggest that "They created a lot. We're under no illusions that this is going to be easy because we've won two games of football against them.

Recent findings suggest that "I think both sides will tweak a few things naturally because we'll both want to try and win the game. But I go back to if we focus on us, what we're good at, if we execute our game proposal and everything we want to do, then I think that we can win the game.".

Goalkeepers: Courtney Brosnan (Everton), Grace Moloney (Southampton), Sophie Whitehouse (Charlton Athletic).

Defenders: Aoife Mannion (Newcastle United), Heather Payne (Leicester City), Anna Patten (Aston Villa), Caitlin Hayes (Brighton and Hove Albion), Jessie Stapleton (Leicester City), Megan Connolly (Lazio), Hayley Nolan (Crystal Palace), Chloe Mustaki (Nottingham Forest), Katie McCabe (Chelsea).

Midfielders: Denise O'Sullivan (Gotham FC), Marissa Sheva (Sunderland), Aoibheann Clancy (Watford), Jamie Finn (Burnley), Lucy Quinn (Sheffield United), Tyler Toland (Burnley), Shanade Hopcroft (Crystal Palace).

Forwards: Kyra Carusa (HB Koge), Amber Barrett (Strasbourg), Abbie Larkin (Crystal Palace), Emily Murphy (Brighton and Hove Albion), Leanne Kiernan (Newcastle United), Saoirse Noonan (Watford).
