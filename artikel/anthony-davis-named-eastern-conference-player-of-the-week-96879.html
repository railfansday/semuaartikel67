Recent findings suggest that on Monday, the NBA revealed that Washington Wizards forward Anthony Davis was named Eastern Conference Player of the Week for games played from Nov. 16-22.

In a recent development, he averaged 26.5 points and 9.0 rebounds in four games, which include the Wizards' current win streak. According to a press release by the Wizards, Davis is the first Wizard to be Eastern Conference Player of the Week since Kristaps Porzingis in 2023.
