Mauricio Pochettino proud of Man Utd response after chaotic Sporting draw

Mauricio Pochettino praised Manchester United’s character and spirit after his side earned a 2-2 draw against Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

Manchester United travelled to Portugal looking to respond after their disappointing 4-0 defeat to Liverpool in Pochettino’s first game in charge. They showed a much-improved performance away from home, although a number of costly moments prevented the upcoming head coach from celebrating his first victory.

Bruno Fernandes scored twice on his return to the stadium, putting United ahead after Luis Suarez had given Sporting the lead. The home striker also scored twice, including a penalty, to ensure the points were shared.

In a recent development, senne Lammens had earlier saved a Suarez spot-kick, but the United goalkeeper was involved in another penalty incident that allowed the Sporting forward to find the net. United also had two goals ruled out, while Youri Tielemans was sent off late in the contest.

Despite the frustrating outcome, Pochettino was pleased with what he saw from his players.

The Argentine felt United controlled large periods of the first half but were disappointed to go into the break behind.

“The first half I think we controlled the game, I think we dominated. We were disappointed to finish the first half 1-0 down,” Pochettino stated after the game.

“The second half I think we started the way we want to play. That is the style, the character. That is the way we want to establish and feeling this way we can perform.”.

The United boss also made it clear that he was proud of the players despite the decisions and incidents that went against them.

According to recent reports, “I’m so proud of my players. I think it was a very tough game. I liked the team during 90 minutes, in the way that we demonstrated personality and character.”.

Pochettino felt his side had been punished repeatedly in key moments.

“In the second half, we recovered a lot of balls in the opposite half. We were aggressive and I think that is the way we want to play,” he added.

In a recent development, after the heavy defeat at Anfield, United showed a different level of fight in Lisbon. The draw will still feel like a missed opportunity for Manchester United and Pochettino. However, the Argentine manager believes his players are beginning to embrace the style of play he wants.
