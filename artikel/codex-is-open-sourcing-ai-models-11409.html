For tiny models under 1B parameters, t4-small works well. These models train promptly—expect $1-2 for a full run. This is perfect for educational or experimental runs.

For small models (1-3B), step up to t4-medium or a10g-small. Training takes a few hours and costs $5-15.

In a recent development, for medium models (3-7B), you need a10g-large or a100-large with LoRA. Full fine-tuning doesn't fit, but LoRA makes these very trainable. Budget $15-40 for production.

For large models (7B+), this HF skills job is not suitable for this scale yet. But stay tuned because we are working on it!
