📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tendo Nagenda, a former executive on Netflix’s film team, has been tapped as president of Proximity Media, the production company founded by Ryan Coogler, Zinzi Coogler, and Sev Ohanian, the founders unveiled Thursday.

Nagenda spent four years at Netflix under film chief Scott Stuber, exiting in 2022, and in that time he shepherded films like Spike Lee’s “Da 5 Bloods,” “The Old Guard,” “Ma Rainey’s Black Bottom,” “The Harder They Fall,” and “Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery.”.

Industry observers note that nagenda will oversee all aspects of creative development and production at Proximity, including its film, TV, and non-fiction divisions, and will report directly to the founders. The heads of Film, Television and Nonfiction will all report directly to Nagenda, and he’ll work alongside marketing and operations lead Terra Potts and music lead Ludwig Göransson. He starts on November 30. “Tendo has spent his career working alongside talented filmmakers, championing original ideas, and helping make projects that resonate with audiences around the world,” stated Ryan Coogler, Zinzi Coogler and Ohanian in a joint statement. “As Proximity continues to grow across film, television, and nonfiction, we’re excited to have his creative instincts and experience helping guide this next chapter. He shares our belief in the power of storytelling to connect people, challenge perspectives and create meaningful impact, and we’re thrilled to welcome him to the team.”.

“I’m honored and excited to join Ryan, Zinzi, Sev, and the entire Proximity team,” said Nagenda. “Their commitment to ambitious, meaningful storytelling reflects everything I’ve always believed in, and I look forward to building on what they’ve created, collaborating with extraordinary filmmakers and creators, and bringing compelling upcoming stories to audiences around the world.”.

Since leaving Netflix in 2022, Nagenda introduced 10 by Ten Entertainment, which produced and released Jeymes Samuel’s “The Book of Clarence” in 2024. Prior to working at Netflix, he had a long track record at Disney and oversaw films on the live-action slate, including “Beauty and the Beast,” “Cinderella,” “Saving Mr. Banks,” “Mulan,” “A Wrinkle in Time,” and “Queen of Katwe.”.

Proximity Media had a banner year last year with the release of “Sinners,” which grossed $365 million worldwide. Some of its other features have included “Judas and the Black Messiah,” “Space Jam: A Newly introduced Legacy,” and “Creed III.” It’s now working on a reboot of “The X-Files” for Hulu starring Danielle Deadwyler and Himesh Patel. Proximity recently entered into a multiyear TV deal with Netflix for scripted and unscripted projects. And it also has produced some nonfiction work including Nat Geo’s hit “Hurricane Katrina: Race Against Time,” as well as other titles like “Stephen Curry: Underrated,” “Homeroom,” and “Anthem.”.

Industry observers note that nagenda is a graduate of Claremont McKenna College and a member of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences and Producers United. He also serves on the Social Services Committee of the Motion Picture & Television Fund and the board of the UCLA Jonsson Comprehensive Cancer Center.
