Recent findings suggest that sonia Bompastor attempted to deflect suggestions that the removal of Kadeisha Buchanan 12 minutes into Chelsea’s top-of-the-table clash with Manchester City was tactical, but few were convinced by the claim that it was down to injury. The manager said the centre-back, who had not asked to come off and walked straight down the tunnel, had taken a knock to the head in training and been assessed and cleared, but that during the game “she was not herself”. On came Veerle Buurman and the threat of Khadija Shaw was nullified, the Dutch defender restricting the golden boot favourite so well that Shaw did not manage a shot on target. Bompastor said after the game that she had not spoken to Buchanan but that she would “explain myself to her”. “I always have good relationships with the players, bring them clarity, and I’m always direct and honest so they have the information,” she said. “It won’t be any different.” Enforced or not, the substitution was a huge gamble that paid off, even after the loss of Melvine Malard to injury in the first half reduced their number of remaining in-game substitution windows to one.
