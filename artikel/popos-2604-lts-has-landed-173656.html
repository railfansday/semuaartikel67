And when you're ready to make it your own, you can customize it until it looks and feels exactly the way you think a desktop should.

It all starts with Constellations.

Constellations are COSMICâs all-in-one bundled themes.Â  With a single click you can change the wallpaper, accent colors, icons, and fonts. It's literally a one-click switch for a full aesthetic change of your desktop. Instead of having to go to Desktop > Wallpaper, then Desktop > Appearance, then Desktop Panel, then Desktop Dock, then Desktop Window Management, all you do is go to Settings > Desktop > Appearance, select between dark or light mode, enable or disable automatic mode switching, and then select your theme.

You can also apply the Frosted Glass effect to a Constellation, and edit nearly every aspect of a Constellation. Once you have your Constellation exactly as you want, export it so you have it as a backup, or to share with others.

With Constellations, the desktop is truly yours to customize and keep.

In a recent development, pop!_OS 26.04 LTS adds drawing tablet support, which brings precise, natural pen input and unlocks features like tilt sensitivity. That opens the door for graphic design, photo editing, animation, note-taking, CAD, and GIS applications.

This release also adds an on-screen keyboard with touch and gamepad input.

You can now enable automatic updates during the initial setup or within Settings > System & accounts > System & application updates. No missing crucial updates to the OS!

Additionally, the growing family of native applications includes COSMIC Viewer, COSMIC System Monitor, and a fully redesigned COSMIC Store.

As COSMIC is a rolling release desktop, consumers on 24.04 will have experienced many of these features. For the full 26.04 experience, do a fresh install. Pop 24.04 to Pop 26.04 upgrades will be activated in December.
