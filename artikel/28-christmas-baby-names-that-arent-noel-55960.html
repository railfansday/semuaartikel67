Whether your baby will be born in December, or you just love the holidays that much.

Of course when parents are thinking about what to name their new baby, they look toward the things they value most. Whether it’s a lifelong love of literature, a sentimental meaning, or simply a certain vibe that fits your lifestyle, choosing a name you really adore often starts with thinking about what already brings you happiness. And for some parents, that means Christmas. It’s a holiday all about togetherness, giving, joy, tradition — all wonderful values on their own. For many, Christmastime is also a source of some of their best memories of a magical childhood.
