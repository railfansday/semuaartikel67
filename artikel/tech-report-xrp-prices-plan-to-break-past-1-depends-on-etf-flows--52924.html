Tech Report: XRP price’s plan to break past $1 depends on ETF flows, sentiment, and…

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

At press time, XRP seemed to be attempting to stage a short-term recovery on the 4-hour chart. Unfortunately, persistent institutional outflows and a critical technical hurdle at the $1.00-level have been keeping the altcoin’s bullish hopes in check.

It has been revealed that as per data for 17th November, U.S Spot XRP ETFs saw net outflows of $6.14 million. This bleeding was mainly concentrated in two products — The Canary XRP ETF (XRPC) saw $5.01 million leave the fund, while the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF (GXRP) recorded outflows of $1.12 million.

These outflows were in line with XRP Spot ETFs’ broader trend. For the uninitiated, the week ending 13th November saw outflows of $19.1 million — the largest single-week withdrawal in nearly eight months.

That’s not all either as the recent exodus also hinted at a lack of institutional conviction around XRP’s press time price levels.

AMBCrypto’s look at XRP’s 4-hour chart hinted at the altcoin showing signs of life following a steep downtrend that bottomed out near $0.89 on 15th November.

At the time of writing, the price was hovering at $0.9979 after having been rejected from its 24-hour high of $1.0078.

In a recent development, however, momentum indicators appeared to be turning positive. The Relative Strength Index (RSI) had crossed above the neutral line, sitting at 55.19 at press time. This implied that buyers were regaining control.

Furthermore, the MACD registered a bullish crossover, with the histogram flashing green bars — a signal that short-term momentum may be shifting to the upside.

The 4-hour chart recorded XRP struggling to break the $1.00-$1.01 psychological barrier, in line with the top of its recent consolidation.

Sources indicate that if XRP can secure a 4-hour close above $1.0078, it would confirm a short-term breakout. Its next target will then be $1.05, followed by $1.09.

And yet, it is likely that XRP can face a rejection at $1.00. This will then cause a retest of the $0.95 support zone. Conversely, a break below $0.94 would expose $0.89 to further falls.

Industry observers note that XRP’s 4-hour MACD and RSI hinted at a relief rally. On the contrary, XRP’s $6.1 million ETF outflows and its hard resistance at $1.00 suggested that traders should still remain cautious.

In the short term, unless XRP can close above $1.01, it will remain vulnerable to a pullback.
