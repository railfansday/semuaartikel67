According to recent reports, this holiday season, we’re taking our decorating inspiration off the page—and into a Greenwich Village townhouse. ELLE Decor has partnered with Walmart to curate a living room for Walmart’s Holiday Wonderland, a free, two-day experience filled with finds for your home and your gift list just ahead of the store’s Black Friday Deals event. Set inside a restored 1850s townhouse at 16 West 10th Street, the event runs from Friday, November 20 from noon to 6 p.m. ET and Saturday, November 21 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET.

It has been revealed that inside the ELLE Decor Living Room, you’ll find our Walmart picks in a festive setting with a fireplace that doubles as the backdrop for complimentary digital holiday portraits. Santa will even be dropping in on Friday from 3 to 6 p.m. and Saturday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Come for a little decorating inspiration, stay for a photograph, and shop your favorite finds via QR code as you explore.

Industry observers note that can’t make it to Greenwich Village? Our ELLE Decor Living Room collection is shoppable wherever you’re spending the season. Browse our picks for a thoughtful gift, a finishing touch your holiday gathering needs, or something you’ll be quite happy to keep yourself.
