Silverprint Pictures, producers of the long-running BBC drama series Shetland, is launching Silverprint Pictures Scotland, based in Glasgow’s Merchant City.

Led by Scottish executive producer Denise Paul (Shetland) alongside Charlotte Daniel as script executive (Shetland), the team states it will look to partner with established and emerging voices to champion author-led Scottish drama.

ITV Studios label Silverprint Pictures, led by Creative Director, Kate Bartlett and Development Director, Antonia Gordon, is known for shows including A Cruel Love: The Ruth Ellis Story, fourteen series of Vera, Flesh & Blood and The Long Call. Popular crime drama Shetland has just been recommissioned for a six-part 12th series. Bartlett and Paul have collaborated on eight series to date. The drama is filmed exclusively in Shetland, Glasgow and the surrounding area, with a team made up predominantly of Scottish writers, directors, cast, crew and suppliers. Watch on Deadline.

Shetland has been sold to more than 120 territories worldwide, with 2025’s series averaging 7 million viewers across its run on BBC iPlayer and BBC One, in line with the previous series.

Kate Bartlett, Creative Director of Silverprint Pictures, said: “Having worked closely with Denise for many years, setting up Silverprint Pictures Scotland with Denise and Charlotte at the helm, feels like a natural next step. They are both exceptionally talented Scottish creatives and it’s exciting to expand our team as we partner with established and emerging voices to champion bold, original and author-led Scottish drama.”.

Denise Paul, executive producer, Silverprint Pictures Scotland, added: “I’m thrilled to be heading up Silverprint Pictures in Scotland and can’t wait to get started. It’s a fantastic opportunity to build on the great relationships we’ve established through the years on Shetland, whilst also developing newly introduced projects with Scottish creatives at their heart.”.
