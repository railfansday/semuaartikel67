Sources indicate that if your pet eats the same food month after month, buy it on autoship. On eligible items, the substantial pet retailers take 35% off the first order, up to $20, and 5% off every one after. Set the delivery interval to how long a bag lasts, and check the price before each order ships.

It has been revealed that say your dog goes through a $70 bag of food every month and the price holds all year. At 5% off, autoship saves $3.50 on each bag, or $42 a year.

Recent findings suggest that chewy, Petco, and PetSmart all run autoship the same basic way. On eligible items, each takes 35% off your first autoship order, up to $20, then 5% off every order after that, and each offers free shipping on orders over $49. You can cancel anytime, and skip or reschedule a delivery until a day or two before it’s processed.

Make your first autoship order about $57 or more, the point where 35% off reaches the $20 cap. In the example above, the first bag saves $20 instead of $3.50, which brings the first year’s savings to $58.50. If the food alone comes in under $57, add litter, treats, or your pet’s monthly preventives to the order. Chewy and Petco ship prescription items on autoship too, once your vet verifies the prescription.

Each order is charged at the item’s current price, not the price you signed up at. All three stores email you before an order goes out, so check the price then, and skip or delay the delivery when the price has jumped or the current bag is still half full. Now and then, compare the autoship price with a sale elsewhere or a warehouse club’s price, since 5% off a $70 bag is only $3.50.

Start autoship the week your current bag runs low, since Chewy and Petco ship the first order right away. Then write the date on each newly introduced bag you open, and set the interval to the number of days it lasts.
