Sources indicate that have you recently started playing Elemental Powers Tycoon and are unsure which powers are the best? Well, you’re in luck because our Elemental Powers Tycoon tier list will assist you in figuring this out. We have rated all powers from S to D, with S being the highest and D the lowest. This tier list lets you focus on mastering powerful characters and avoid struggling with the lower-tier options. We've added a description for each character to help you understand the reasoning behind their ranking.

Industry observers note that elemental Powers Tycoon is an enjoyable Roblox game that draws inspiration from various anime, merging the typical tycoon format with powers acquired using money. The developers made the skins of their characters resemble famous figures from many anime series and movies, including Naruto, One Piece, and HxH.

Sources indicate that additionally, I’ll be incorporating numerous anime references. If I’ve overlooked any anime or characters, please let me know in the comments.

Now, let’s jump straight to the tier list.

Thunder Thunder is among the super-broken characters in EPT and sports a Killua-like appearance. It’s understandable why it’s OP; it has an exciting and all-around kit with damage and a long-duration stun. Even though you can fly around on Nimbus like Goku, there’s no Kamehameha. Instead, you have a flashy and strong thunder attack that can one-shot almost everyone, which is sufficient to rank the character at the top of our Elemental Powers Tycoon tier list. Time This character can stop time and slow down enemies, holding an exciting kit of skills and well-balanced damage. He is among the most OP champs, although the S-tier rank is for the excellent invisibility skill. You can go unnoticed by your adversary for a short time, making for a comeback moment in every fight if used strategically. If you are into time manipulation and want to become the invisible man, here’s your chance. Nature Hashirama is the master of nature manipulation, an influential figure from the Naruto series. In the game, he is famous for his wood and tree attacks. One of his skills is the ability to pull any enemy in a specific direction, which you can use to drag them into your deadly attack range. I also consider the damage high, which makes him easily qualified for the S tier. Technology Now we have my favourite character, and you will understand why once you try technology. He has the best skill kit with high damage output. The sword he holds can throw projectiles every third strike with no cooldown, and he can summon two large hexagon-shaped cells that fire towards the cursor’s position. But these are not the only perks! You can summon a turret that knocks enemies around, levitates, and becomes invulnerable to damage while being able to cast skills. And finally, create a tesseract that sucks in anyone approaching it to deal 63 damage over nine pulses. This character is your best pick if you want to dominate EPT easily. Bones Bones is another OP champ, well-known for having almost average skill damage, but that one “jealousy” skill that can one-shot everyone sets the bar high to be in the S tier. I would recommend it for those who want to abuse one skill and get the most out of their kills.

Industry observers note that darkness2024-03-05 Darkness is powerful right now. If you’re a Madara Uchiha wannabe, you will enjoy this pick. The kit resembles purple shadows and the special Giant Hand move that pulls opponents underneath. Catching someone in it is an inevitable knockout. There’s also the ability to teleport forward for a surprising attack or to dodge enemy overkill skills. Overall, darkness is considered strong, but it is not the best choice. The downside is that it’s hard to hit your main skill, which makes winning a gamble. Light Light is Admiral Kisaru from One Piece. All fans of the series will recognise them instantly. The character has a fantastic set of skills and abilities. You can run fast, teleport, and damage your enemies significantly. Remember that high mobility is necessary in this game, and people who can master this character will also be hard to kill. Space Space, represented by an astronaut’s skin, is an above-average character with a mix of solid and weak weapons. However, it made it easy to reach the A tier thanks to the UFO skill and the significant damage some of his guns inflict. Another downside is his melee weapon, which is not the best to swing around. Devil Denji represents the devil from the Chainsaw Man series. Boasting above-average skills made it easy to get into the A-tier list. The damage and the mobility skills are well-balanced. The character mainly focuses on wielding a devil sword that deals hellfire damage over three cycles, teleporting in the cursor's direction, inflicting damage upon landing, and throwing puddles of black fluid as projectiles. Devil is very entertaining and strong at the same time. However, why not in the S tier? The answer is simple: I’m considering how tough it is the land some skills. Venom Venom is a toxic character, as the name implies. He uses poison, which deals continuous damage and empowers attacks. He holds a stun ability, self-buff (health and speed) and bubbling venom, which covers a wide area. The character is a good choice, with many players already invested in mastering him. The melee damage could be better, and is the only downside of this character.

Ice Ice, a character as cold as Esdeath, has the most exciting skill in their stun, which limits the enemy's mobility and jump power. It is super-efficient against high-mobility tycoons. However, it does come without a downside. The skill set doesn’t hit hard for overkill, and she lacks burst damage, making her a laid-back character requiring patience. She is undoubtedly the best pick if you’re into that play style, however. Gravity Admiral Issho represents gravity from One Piece, but this time, the character is in the okay-ish category. It has some good stuff and skills, but remains in the B tier if we talk about the best powers, as other Tycoons could easily overpower it. The other downside is his firepower hitbox, which is hard to land and needs to be more dominant on the battlefield. Crystal We place Crystal in the B category, the epitome of average characters, because her kill options aren’t as deadly as they should be, making it a bit hard to deal with enemies. The more you play her, the more you discover how challenging she is to use. Supersonic Supersonic is all about speed and hitting from a distance. His moves include Sonic Blaster for ranged damage, Sonic Barrage for a quick burst of projectiles, and Sonic Twister to zip around the map. He’s a great pick if you like fast, agile play rather than relying on raw power or heavy crowd control.

In a recent development, fire Fire is somewhat useless, as sadly represented by Naruto, but it has one of the best skins in the game. The number of combos you must do to defeat someone is tremendously annoying. I wouldn't recommend this character if you are seriously trying to beat some enemies. It's only a good choice if you're entirely into aesthetics.

In a recent development, earth Earth is low because you have to be lucky to hit your skills. It’s hard to use since the hitboxes are small and your range is short, so you must get close to your opponent. Even if you miraculously land some hits, they won’t be deadly enough to eliminate your adversary, which is annoying. It's not my cup of tea, but feel free to try him out.
