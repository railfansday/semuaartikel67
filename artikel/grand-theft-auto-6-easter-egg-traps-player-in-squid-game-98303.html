A disturbing Easter Egg has been discovered in Grand Theft Auto 6, one that traps the player in a life-or-death situation inspired by franchises like Saw and Squid Game, leading to a battle with a murderer.

According to recent reports, the Grand Theft Auto series is well-known for its movie parodies and media references, some of which break the game’s realistic setting and move into sci-fi or supernatural territory. This has led players on wild-goose chases in search of UFOs, Bigfoot, and sea monsters, some of which do exist in the Grand Theft Auto universe.

One thing that definitely exists in the Grand Theft Auto universe is killers, with the protagonist being the worst of the bunch. Grand Theft Auto 6 has a far more calculated murderer than most, in a sequence discovered by The Easter Egg Hunter YouTube channel, one who can trap the player in a deadly game.

In Grand Theft Auto 6, it’s possible to encounter a woman with a broken arm in a cast, who is trying to move furniture into a van. If the player helps her, they get knocked out with a stun gun. This references a scene in the movie The Silence of the Lambs, where the killer Buffalo Bill captures his victims this way.

In a recent development, it’s possible to escape this scenario by walking away or killing the woman, but those who are trapped wake up in a storage container with a bomb on their neck. They are mocked through a speaker, similar to Jigsaw in Saw, and prompted to take part in a survival game.

The player is trapped on an island, with weapons and items strewn around, and a killer on the loose. This begins a battle royale-style scenario, where the player goes one-on-one with the killer, with the hunter becoming the hunted, much like in Squid Game.

It’s possible to escape this scenario by walking away or killing the woman, but those who are trapped wake up in a storage container with a bomb on their neck.

In a recent development, unfortunately for the person planning the hunt, they didn’t count on facing a Grand Theft Auto protagonist, who can brutally turn the tables and kill their captor. Winning the game gets the player their items back, along with a cash prize and a boat that’ll let them return to the mainland.

According to recent reports, the Grand Theft Auto series loves its references, and the hunted game packs multiple in at once. While fans are still waiting for news of Grand Theft Auto 6’s online component, this game feels like something that could appear as a battle mode, though people will quickly compare it to Fortnite and similar titles.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a gargantuan game, one that took a long time to develop. As of the time of writing, the game has been out for less than a week, but fans are already uncovering all kinds of secrets, including the ability to travel back to the PS2 era via a magic portal. This is just scratching the surface, especially as most fans haven’t even finished the main storyline yet.

It has been revealed that it took years for fans to work out all the secrets in Grand Theft Auto 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, and there’s a good chance that Grand Theft Auto6 will also be teasing fans with its hidden content for a long time. This will only get worse when the online component begins, and Rockstar can keep adding content for players to puzzle over for the next decade.
