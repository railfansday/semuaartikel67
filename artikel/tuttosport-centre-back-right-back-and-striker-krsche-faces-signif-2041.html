Markus Krösche will be announced as Milan’s newly introduced Head of Football in January, according to Tuttosport, and an notable task awaits him.

According to recent reports, after a summer during which Gerry Cardinale personally worked alongside an internal group on the mercato business, he’s expected to get some help soon. Indeed, an agreement has been reached with Eintracht Frankfurt for Krösche, who is expected to be confirmed at the start of January.

In other words, the latest Head of Football will be thrown straight into the fire as Milan are expected to make some reinforcements during the winter mercato. The start of the season has indeed uncovered some depth issues which need to be fixed.

Recent findings suggest that according to today’s edition of Tuttosport, as transcribed by MilanNews, Milan are looking to make three signings in January: a centre-back, a right-back and a striker. This means that Krösche is in for quite the task, even if there is a budget to support him.

For the centre-back position, Goncalo Inacio remains the massive target but the Rossoneri also have some other names in mind. On the right flank, the early return of Zachary Athekame from Lyon still cannot be ruled out, though there are other options here too.

According to recent reports, up front, Nicolo Tresoldi appears to be the favourite but the price tag could make things difficult, especially since we all know how stern Club Brugge are in negotiations. In any case, once the Krösche announcement arrives, we can expect to at least see some movement.
