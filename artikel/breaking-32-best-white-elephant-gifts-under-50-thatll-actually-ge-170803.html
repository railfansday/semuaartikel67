In a recent development, white-elephant gift exchanges are fun, but first you have to come up with the perfect gift everyone will want—within the established budget. Need a refresher on the game? Here’s how the chaos unfolds: Everyone brings a present, draws a number, and then, going in order based on which numbers everyone drew, either unwraps a mystery gift or steals one that’s already been revealed.

Sources indicate that some common caveats are that there’s a cap on steals per item (usually two or three), you can’t immediately steal back what was just swiped, and your turn ends when you open your gift (or exchange it for an already-opened one). The final player gets the power position—and sometimes a last swap—depending on house rules. You might also hear the game called Dirty Santa: same energy, same drama, whether it’s with coworkers or cousins.

Recent findings suggest that what to bring under, say, $50? (That’s a pretty nice price ceiling.) Go funny but functional, like the stocking stuffers people will actually use. Think cozy comforts, clever kitchen gadgets, mini games, candles that actually smell good, and little indulgences that feel pricier than they are. Skip anything weirdly personal—you want universal hits that sisters, brothers, moms, dads, friends, and coworkers can all fight over with a smile. Bonus points if it’s something you’d happily steal back yourself.
