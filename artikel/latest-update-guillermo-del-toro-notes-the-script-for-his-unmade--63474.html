📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Guillermo del Toro has revealed that the script for his unmade Jabba the Hutt movie was already written.

Industry observers note that "[David S. Goyer] and I did write the script and I even developed some art and sculptures," del Toro wrote on Twitter, even confirming the movie's title: The Rise and Fall of Jabba the Hutt.

Guillermo del Toro wanting to make a Jabba the Hutt movie is something that has been bubbling away for over a decade.

In 2015, he told Yahoo of his initial pitch and love for Star Wars' most fearsome crime lord. He said, "I would do the sort of Godfather saga that Jabba the Hutt had to go through to gain control. One, because it's the character that looks the most like me, and I like him. I love the idea of a Hutt type of mafia, a very complex coup. I just love the character.".

For many years, there had been radio silence on the project, but del Toro revealed in 2023 that the project had been shelved.

Industry observers note that “We had the rise and fall of Jabba the Hutt, so I was super happy. We were doing a lot of stuff, and then it’s not my property, it’s not my money, and then it’s one of those 30 screenplays that goes away," the director recalled to Collider.

In a recent development, he added, “Sometimes I’m bitter, sometimes I’m not. I always turn to my team and say, ‘Good practice, guys. Good practice. We designed a great world. We designed great stuff. We learned.’.

According to recent reports, but there is still hope in a galaxy far, far away. As recently as this year, The Mandalorian and Grogu director Jon Favreau suggested in an interview with Fortress of Solitude that "someday [del Toro] will collaborate on that.".

For now, though, The Rise and Fall of Jabba the Hutt joins the pile of Star Wars projects that never were – alongside George Lucas' prohibitively expensive idea for a TV show.
