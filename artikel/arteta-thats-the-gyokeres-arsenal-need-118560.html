Mikel Arteta lauded Viktor Gyokeres' performance against Borussia Dortmund, stating "that's the Viktor we needed".

Arsenal were held to a 1-1 draw by Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday, with Maximilian Beier cancelling out Gyokeres' stunning opener.

The Sweden international recorded 50 touches, his most in a Premier League or Champions League game for Arsenal, while also winning six of his 14 duels.

Gyokeres has scored in back-to-back Champions League starts for just the second time, after also doing so for Sporting CP in October-November 2024.

And only Erling Haaland (27) and Joao Pedro (18) have more goals among Premier League players in 2026 than Gyokeres (17), who earned high praise from his manager.

"I think he had a brilliant performance," Arteta mentioned of Gyokeres.

"I think he scored an unbelievable goal. He has the technique and the quality to do that.

In a recent development, "What I love the most is how connected he was to the team. We used him many times in different ways.

"His link-up play, the way he held the ball, the way he threaded in behind and stretched the pitch. There was a lot to like in that performance.

"That's the Viktor that we needed.".

Gyokeres told Sky Sports: "It's been a good last couple of games for me.

"Obviously, we probably should've won [against Dortmund] with the amount of chances we had.

"Overall, I think we played really well, that was good at least. In one way it's a bit frustrating but the way we attacked and the amount of chances we created, it's just a matter of time until there's going to be a lot of goals.".

Arsenal's 30 shots against Dortmund are their most on record in a Champions League game (since 2003-04), and the Gunners' most in a game that they failed to win across all competitions since December 2023 vs West Ham– also 30.
