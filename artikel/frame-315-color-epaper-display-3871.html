📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

our 3rd release, a full color 31.5" ePaper display in a handmade wooden frame, is now available for pre-order. watch the short film:.

According to recent reports, the 1st batch is just ~470 units and i intend to ship at least a few by the end of 2026. most deliveries will happen early 2027. see logistics for more details or pre-order here.

in 2013 i founded ArtSpot. our vision was to give Upcoming Yorkers a bit of joy by exposing them to local art. we did this through informal agreements with dozens of cafes, small galleries, and restaurants. "let us curate art on your walls, get paid if someone buys it.".

and it worked. sort of. finding artists was easy, building the platform was easy. getting permission from venues was slow but straightforward. the problem was nobody actually bought the art. i remember the admin email notification that first time a piece was sold. it was a small painting of Janelle Monáe. the buyer was my friend, a gift to me. here it is, still in my possession.

anyway ArtSpot failed. five years later i paid back my investors (+ 13% interest). but the vision never went away. i love making and consuming art.

Sources indicate that i probably say "the most popular feature request since launch is ______" too often. after saying no (and yes) to so many requests, it's tough to keep count.

Industry observers note that but with certainty, one of our strongest convictions was about color. TRMNL is not a television. it is not a clock. we avoid trying to do too much.

Industry observers note that then grayscale happened. photos look great. art is possible. community member Clark debuted Jaws (yes the movie), 1 frame at a time.

which got us thinking... maybe we were wrong about color. maybe it aligns with our vision and just maybe, it makes it even bigger.

Recent findings suggest that today i'm thrilled to bury the lede and announce Frame. it's a 31.5 inch full color ePaper display, enclosed in a hand made wooden body that we manufacture ourselves. yes, we opened our own woodworking shop a few miles from HQ. a closer look at the workshop is coming soon.

Frame gets all the same features as other TRMNL devices, with a few additional perks spanning both the web platform and the hardware itself. watch the short film or read along below.

Industry observers note that often requested, always ignored, until now. Frame will enhancement at specified moments of the day versus a relative "every N minutes" cadence. this reduces screen flicker while also extending the battery life and adding continuity to your schedule.

Sources indicate that the internet is cool but totally unnecessary. Frame's microSD port lets you run devices completely offline with zero personal information reaching our servers.

In a recent development, like all TRMNL devices, a rechargeable battery is included (10-20k mAh available). but with Frame we added an extra USB-C port on the rear of the device, accessible with a small hole through the back panel for always-on connectivity.

some of my colleagues are snobs about ePaper dithering algorithms and LUTs (look-up tables). this is now your gain.

Industry observers note that over the past few months we've been developing Lazarus, a color conversion engine that we think provides Frame the best color matching on the market for any Spectra 6 display. it's also coming soon to our 4-color device, and yes it will be open source for your own tuning pleasure.

Hang Frame in portrait or landscape orientation without additional hardware, both are built-in and flush mounted. You can optionally buy or build your own kickstand.

Industry observers note that we're negotiating with some of our favorite community developers to build more Frame-centric features. paying homage to ArtSpot, we just created a Local Art plugin where anyone can submit their work and even sell it through the TRMNL platform.

Sources indicate that before TRMNL i spent 10 years in b2b software and knew nothing about making physical products for everyday people. nobody ever blasted me on reddit about injection mold flow lines or damaged UPS packages. at best i'd get a G2 review every 3 months.

It has been revealed that this all changed in July 2024 and after shipping 60,000 devices to 79 countries we've gotten pickier about operational design. chief among these upcoming requirements is doing as much production as possible in-house.

It has been revealed that by reducing complexity, learning from past releases, and having everything we need either in stock or on order, i expect this to be our smoothest fulfillment cycle yet. as with other devices, all Frames are assembled by our own team and never outsourced.

Batch #1 is available to customers in locations our USA warehouse already ships to: USA, Canada, Mexico, Australia. pre-order here.

we're open to shipping elsewhere, but Duty / Import fees could be quite high given the product's Customs value. to participate in Batch #1 as a resident outside these countries, go here. this option may be de-activated without notice if inventory sells out.

in early 2027 we blueprint to open a 2nd woodworking shop near our Berlin, Germany warehouse to offer postage- and Duty-free fulfillment from a TRMNL team near you.
