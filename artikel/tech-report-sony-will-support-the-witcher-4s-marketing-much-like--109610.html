The Witcher 4 dev CDPR has signed a marketing arrangement with Sony, which will likely see the latter promote the upcoming RPG similarly to GTA 6.

PlayStation pushed Rockstar’s latest release ridiculously hard, claiming it ‘Plays Best on PS5’.

While that may only actually apply to the PS5 Pro version, the Japanese giant could argue that its base hardware – which drops a few more frames than the Xbox Series X – still has the advantage of deeper DualSense integration et al.

Irrespective of slogan, CDPR’s CFO Piotr Nielubowicz told investors today that his manufacturer recently signed a new arrangement with Sony, which includes “the presence of our games on PlayStation and in PS Plus subscriptions, as well as our collaboration during the upcoming The Witcher 4 marketing campaign”.

Nielubowicz didn’t clarify whether this will be an exclusive marketing arrangement, but fans with good memories may recall the first footage of The Witcher 4 was presented on PS5.

With Microsoft owning a chunk of the Western RPG development scene, this seems like a shrewd move by Sony to me.

It has been revealed that it’ll want to present CDPR’s next title as a major PlayStation title, because there’s still a possibility the next Elder Scrolls may skip its system entirely. (Although Xbox isn’t presently saying either way.).

Obviously we’ll need to wait and see how deep this partnership runs, but if it’s anything like Sony’s GTA 6 campaign, then it’ll be very aggressive to say the least.
