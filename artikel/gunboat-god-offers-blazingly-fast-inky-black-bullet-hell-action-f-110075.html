Nowadays, with the popularity of Vampire Survivors, it seems as though the humble bullet hell has become somewhat passe in the face of the bullet heaven. But Gunboat God, now available on iOS and Android, may just prove that the bullet hell genre isn't dead by a long shot!

It has been revealed that gunboat God is an inky black-and-white take on the bullet hell and shoot-em-up genre, where your only aim is to slay as many enemies as possible and make your way back to the Citadel in the sky. Along the way, you'll fly into the air, dive beneath the waves and skirt along the surface, blasting foes to bits as you go.

Gunboat God isn't a simple arcade format either, as you'll gradually make your way through a variety of levels, beat down towering bosses and get your fresh friend Yeti the crocodile (confusing, I know) to help rebuild your boat.

Recent findings suggest that gunboat God is blisteringly fast-paced, exciting and difficult, but rewardingly so. And with such a distinctive art-style which really makes it stand out even if the action can get a bit hard to follow. Either way you certainly won't be forgetting this distinctive mashup of platformer, bullet hell and run 'n gun anytime soon!
