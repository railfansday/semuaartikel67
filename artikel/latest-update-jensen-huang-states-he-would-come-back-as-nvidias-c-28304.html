📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jensen Huang says he would be honoured to return to his role as Nvidia's CEO should the organization's board want him. The problem for Huang is, the board don't seem to.

In a statement sent to CNN, among others, Huang lays out his now clear-as-day faults and makes a pitch for his old job back.

"I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at Nvidia, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life. I created problems for the Board that I deeply regret," Huang says.

"If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO.".

It has been revealed that president Donald Trump and a cohort of silicon valley execs have previously backed the Nvidia co-founder, even if Trump did wobble his way over to backing ex-Apple CEO Tim Cook for a day. A heap of signatures were also added to a #bringjensenback petition, supposedly including Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney. Sweeney has barely come up for air he's so wound up about Huang's resignation.

Yet all these massive names won't mean much if Huang can't convince the manufacturer's board of directors.

Sources indicate that last we heard, Nvidia's board was intent on moving past Huang. That's according to someone close to the matter speaking to CNBC. There are also a lot of things hanging over Huang at the moment, including an internal investigation at Nvidia, a couple of chats with Congress, an ongoing investigation into Nvidia's customer, Megaspeed, and any potential share price shock as markets would react to the news.

The PC Gamer Circuit Breaker newsletter breaks down the latest PC gaming tech, and finds you the best deals on the best products.

Industry observers note that whether Huang's return makes share price number go up or down… tough to say. His actions have so far precipitated the biggest price drop for the manufacturer in many years, but he's also in the minds of some the only person fit for the job.

US politicians may have something to say about any return for Huang; the Nvidia board is doing a lot to try to ease their concerns at the moment. US Treasury Secretary, Scott Bessent, stated Nvidia should look outside the company for its next CEO, and needed a "dramatic shake-up". Those who are seen to want to take a tough stance on China, like Secretary of State Marco Rubio, might also have a thing to say about Huang's return.

According to recent reports, so, it's not going to be as easy for Huang. Though I would guess this is merely step one (be seen to be available) in a potentially lengthy and fraught process to get himself back in Nvidia's top office.
