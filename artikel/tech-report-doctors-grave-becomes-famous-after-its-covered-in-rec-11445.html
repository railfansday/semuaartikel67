A traditional Chinese medicine doctor’s gravestone covered with herbal recipes has gone viral after her family fulfilled her wish to make the formulas public.

Many graves around the world feature the name of the deceased, their age, occupation, and words left behind by their loved ones.

However, a traditional Chinese medicine doctor by the name of Yuan Shufang is getting a lot of attention after people discovered her gravestone had been inscribed with hundreds of herbal recipes.

Industry observers note that nearly 20 years after she died in 2006 at the age of 97, Yuan’s gravestone is attracting widespread attention in China. According to the South China Morning Post, before her death, Yuan asked her son to engrave a selection of her herbal formulas on her grave so they would remain publicly available.

While her name, date of birth, and date of death are still featured on her grave, Yuan’s grave is also filled with rows of ingredients and measurements for various medicinal remedies.
