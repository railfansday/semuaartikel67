Peninsula Energy has temporarily shut down its Wyoming uranium operation after a "cybersecurity incident" affected plant and wellfield control systems.

The Australian uranium producer stated on Wednesday that it had activated its response procedures and brought in outside specialists following the attack on its US subsidiary, Strata Energy, which operates the Lance uranium project.

Peninsula Energy mentioned it believes the attack is "confined to the plant and wellfield operating and control systems," and that back-office functions, such as payroll and other corporate systems, in both the US and Australia, remain unaffected.

"The organization's immediate priorities have been to protect the safety of its employees, secure its operating systems, and work towards the restoration of operations in a safe and controlled manner," it noted in an enhancement to the Australian Securities Exchange.

The notification [PDF] went on to say that Peninsula Energy's investigations into the attack, including whether any data was compromised, remain ongoing.

According to recent reports, peninsula has not given a restart date and states it cannot yet assess the impact on its 2027 production guidance. Operations will remain suspended until recovery and safety checks are complete. "Peninsula will provide further updates to the market as material information becomes available," it said.

The disclosure of additional information came two days after Peninsula secured a short trading halt, which referenced a cybersecurity incident at its Wyoming site.

The November 23 filing [PDF] dated the incident to Friday evening in Western Australia. Wednesday's update clarified that Strata detected it on the morning of November 20, local US time.

Peninsula subsequently requested a voluntary suspension of trading until the earlier of November 30 or an announcement on restarting operations and any resulting change to production guidance.

Peninsula's Lance project is a uranium mining operation, extracting the element from underground deposits and turning it into uranium concentrate, aka yellowcake or U₃O₈.

Uranium concentrate is feedstock for nuclear fuel production, typically undergoing conversion, enrichment, and fabrication into uranium dioxide pellets before use in reactors.

The Lance central processing plant was constructed to produce a maximum of 2.0 million pounds (Mlb) of U₃O₈ annually, and is licensed for 3.0 Mlb.

Recent findings suggest that peninsula previously forecast production of 500,000 to 600,000 pounds in 2027, having withdrawn its 2026 guidance earlier this year following operational problems at Lance. It now says it will reassess the 2027 outlook once operations resume. ®.
