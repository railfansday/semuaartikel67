Shares of Anthropic closed up 17% in first-day trading Thursday amid an otherwise down day for the broader market.

Anthropic priced shares for its upcoming stock market debut at $83 on Wednesday evening, solidly above the expected range of $70 to $78 laid out in its IPO filing.

According to recent reports, the offering promised to be the public market event of the season, with the Claude creator seeing investor demand for shares well in excess of its capital-raising goals. The stock is trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT.

Industry observers note that for the IPO portion of its fundraise, Anthropic is selling 980 million shares, including 23.6 million from selling shareholders, up from it’s earlier plan to sell just over 800 million shares. At that upgraded size, the offering is bringing in around $80 billion for the company.

Recent findings suggest that anthropic also expects to pull in another $26 billion from a private placement slated to close immediately following the IPO. Investors in this include AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC.

Sources indicate that put together, the IPO and private placement total around $106 billion. It’s a record-setting capital haul for a firm making its market debut. SpaceX, by comparison, raised $86 billion in its June offering.

The IPO follows a revenue growth trajectory for the history books.

For the first nine months of the year, the company had $33.2 billion in revenue, up from $2.5 billion in the same period a year ago.

Anthropic claims it has the fastest enterprise adoption of any technology to date, growing revenue from zero at commercial launch in the first quarter of 2023 to $17 billion for the three months ended Sept. 30, 2026.

Recent findings suggest that as of yet, however, it’s not profitable. Anthropic posted an operating loss of $1.3 billion for the first nine months of the year, down from $5.2 billion a year earlier. Its net loss widened to $104 billion, up from $25.1 billion a year ago.
