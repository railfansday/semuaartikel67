It's Thanksgiving weekend, so what better time is there to kick back and relax after a good meal with a new movie or TV show? However, if the thought of combing through all of the many streaming platforms fills you with dread, that's where we come in. We've compiled the ultimate list for what you can stream this weekend, including new movies arriving on streaming, original releases, and the finale to one of our favorite indicates.

So whether you're after sci-fi and fantasy, like the fresh Blade Runner show, or you're in the mood for an epic war movie, like The Mosquito Bowl, then we've got something for everyone. In our list below, we go through each of the major streaming services pulling out all of the best things you can watch this week.

Recent findings suggest that we don't include any upcoming digital releases for purchase on this list either so as long as you have a subscription, everything below is completely free. This gives you more loose change for your Black Friday purchases too.

As a quick note, we haven't got any HBO Max recommendations this week so check out our guides to the best HBO Max highlights and the best HBO Max movies for what to watch there.

Available: US/UKWhere to watch: Prime Video.

It has been revealed that blade Runner 2099 tells a newly introduced story set in the beloved franchise, some 50 years on from when we last entered the neon-streaked sci-fi world. It's a very different setting this time around too as replicants have now taken power following an uprising. In this harsh landscape, Cora (played by Hunter Schafer) forms an unlikely pairing with a dying replicant called Olwen (Michelle Yeoh).

Sources indicate that across the eight-part drama, the pair try to track down a runaway who is harboring a truth that could collapse their fragile city. Reviews have been pretty positive for this one with plenty of praise for the impressive worldbuilding. All eight episodes are out now as well, making the perfect binge-watch for this holiday weekend. Available: US/UKWhere to watch: Netflix.

Sources indicate that bill Skarsgård and Nicholas Galitzine lead this new Netflix war drama from Friday Night Lights helmer Peter Berg. Based on the non-fiction novel of the same name, the movie follows four American college football players who enlist together in the marines during World War Two following the attack on Pearl Harbor.

As well as charting their lives and their experiences on the battlefield, the film focuses on the iconic football game that was played before the Battle of Okinawa, a fascinating part of military history. Emotional and uplifting in equal measures, it's an epic story that's available to stream right now.

Industry observers note that the final two episodes of Amazon's fantasy epic The Rings of Power season 3 are available to stream right now. In episode 7, we get to witness the crafting of the One Ring for the first time on screen, an epic sequence that any Tolkien fan will rejoice at. Then the finale sets up perfectly where the show will be heading next in the just-confirmed The Rings of Power season 4.

Available: US/UKWhere to watch: Apple TV.

In a recent development, marvel star Chris Pratt returns to the screen in this heartwarming movie about a Navy SEAL who trains his nephew how to become a "warrior kid" before he enters high school. Dealing with an injury that rules him out of serving, he focuses all his attention and military training to help out the youngster in this comedy from Charlie's Angels and Terminator Salvation director McG.

Based on a children's novel of the same name, it's a light-hearted bit of escapism to enjoy this weekend. The movie hasn't blown critics away but audiences on Rotten Tomatoes have already been scoring the film much higher, landing an 86% rating at the time of writing. Not bad at all.

Available: USWhere to watch: Hulu.

Industry observers note that after arriving in theaters earlier this year, this Alan Ritchson and Owen Wilson starrer finally arrives on Hulu. It follows a former soldier played by Ritchson as he is thrown into a brutal race against time with Wilson's courier to try and deliver a liver to a dying young girl. In their way is a ruthless cartel who will stop at nothing to secure the organ.

It has been revealed that plenty of action, comedy, and an unlikely buddy pairing, the movie comes from former stunt performer Scott Waugh, who previously helmed Expend4bles. Generally positive reviews praised the lead duo's performances and the epic scale of the action, making this our pick for one of the best newly introduced movies to stream this weekend.
