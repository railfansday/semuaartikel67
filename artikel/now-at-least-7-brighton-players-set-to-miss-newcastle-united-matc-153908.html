Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler has been hit by yet another injury ahead of facing Matthias Jaissle and his Newcastle United team on Sunday in the Premier League.

The Seagulls were in action on Thursday night before lining up against Newcastle United.

The south coast club playing Universitatea Craiova in the UEFA Conference League.

Fabian Hurzeler needs as many players available as possible as he looks to compete in both Europe and the domestic competitions.

In the 39th minute of last night’s match, the Brighton boss saw Matt O’Riley on the end of a really poor challenge from Craiova defender Vladimir Screciu, who was then sent off after VAR review.

O’Riley tried to carry on but had to be replaced at the break and Fabian Hurzeler admitted after the match that it doesn’t look good for the Denmark international. The Brighton Head Coach revealing that the player’s ankle was very swollen.

This looks set to make it at least seven Brighton players ruled out of the Newcastle United match.

Malik Yalcouye was the latest long-term injury to hit the Seagulls ahead of last night’s game, whilst also ruled out are Kaoru Mitoma (knee), Stefanos Tzimas (thigh), Evan Ferguson (ankle) and Zadok Yohanna (thigh).

Making it at least now seven Brighton players set to miss the Newcastle United match, as Joao Costinha is suspended after having been sent off during the 2-0 defeat at Hull City last weekend.

Other than the new injury for Fabian Hurzeler to deal with though, there wasn’t a lot of encouragement for Newcastle United from Brighton having this extra fixture to deal with ahead of Sunday.

The weak Conference League is very different to the demands of the Champions League.

Fabian Hurzeler was able to make eight changes and the Seagulls still cruised to victory against this weak Romanian opposition.

Only Maxim De Cuyper, Pascal Gross and Chema Andres stayed in the team from their Premier League defeat against Hull City and once they took the lead inside three minutes the result was never in doubt.

They were two up after 33 minutes and then when the opposition went down to ten men it was easy street for the Brighton players. The final score ended up 4-0 but if they had really needed a bigger scoreline, who knows what it could have been if they had went all out in this game, rather than having both eyes on Newcastle United less than 72 hours later.
