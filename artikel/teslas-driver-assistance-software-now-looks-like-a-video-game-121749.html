📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that tesla is rolling out the 15th iteration of its Full Self-Driving (Supervised) software, and the update comes with some pretty impressive graphics. It’s called Tesla Reality View, and it’s exactly that, using the vehicle’s cameras to record what’s going on around you, then re-create the scene on the central screen accurately.

The intent is to show a visual representation of what the latest FSD driver assistance is going to do, as represented by onscreen arrows. Turns are represented, and the screen shows what parking spot the vehicle has selected when using auto park.

It all looks extremely slick, just like the real world rendered into a video game in real time. Tesla claims the actual driving assistance has also been greatly improved, with an increased ability to spot and react to potholes, small animals, and road debris. The system also uses fleet learning from the trips other Teslas take on the same route, something Tesla has been doing for several iterations of FSD.

Although Tesla claims that this new iteration is 25 times safer in critical interventions, this claim isn’t backed up with provided data such as miles driven; it’s just Tesla’s claim thus far.

Industry observers note that people’ experiences since the rollout have indicated that V15 appears to be more cautious in certain situations, and in some cases a little overcautious. Posts on Reddit’s Tesla forum report a vehicle aggressively avoiding random items in the road and reacting to a road repair site as if it was a pothole. Other forum users point out that early FSD updates often are a little shaky right at rollout but more polished by the time the release goes out more broadly.

Our own experience with Tesla’s FSD is that it works flawlessly—right up until it doesn’t and the driver needs to take over. Tesla’s own literature notes that drivers are expected to keep their attention on the road. The pretty new graphics look attractive, but if you’re staring at the screen instead of out the windshield, FSD should theoretically automatically disconnect.
