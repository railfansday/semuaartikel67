President Donald Trump remarked the U.S., Russia and China are "uniquely positioned" to reduce the world's nuclear weapons stockpile during his historic trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on Thursday.

"As the world's three largest nuclear powers by far, the United States, Russia and China are uniquely positioned to discuss how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons on earth and also to lessen the risk that those weapons will ever be used," Trump said in opening remarks.

"The power is unbelievable, unthinkable," he remarked, adding that it's "a very hard thing" for countries to reduce nuclear weapons.

That would include not letting Iran have nuclear weapons, Trump mentioned.

He stated the three leaders would also discuss the war between Russia and Ukraine, and criticized "rogue globalist organizations," including the International Criminal Court.

The closely watched meeting ended about an hour and a half after it kicked off late Thursday afternoon, following a working lunch between Trump and Xi that started at least an hour later than originally planned.

Trump told Xi and Putin they were "really making history" with the first-ever trilateral meeting between the the three countries' leaders.

He remarked it was an "honor" to be at the meeting with Putin and Xi, whom he congratulated on a very successful APEC summit.".

Recent findings suggest that "Too often past leaders have avoided talking to nations with whom they had disagreements," Trump added. "We feel the opposite.".

At the trilateral, Xi stated that for large nations, having good interactions is not a choice but a necessity, according to a translation by state media.

Sources indicate that in the report, Xi noted China's partnership with Russia, work towards constructive strategic stability in U.S. relations, and called on the three countries to respond to the world's challenges.

Xi laid out four "correct" ways for large countries to interact: mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and adherence to dialogue, according to state media.

The Chinese president echoed Trump's comments on nuclear weapons, calling for such arms to be comprehensively prohibited and destroyed, adding that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.

In a recent development, putin mentioned Russia, China and the U.S. can strengthen global stability if they cooperate constructively, calling for the three leaders to discuss energy security and global and regional issues — including the war in Ukraine.

Sources indicate that beijing only confirmed the closely watched meeting on Monday. The three leaders had briefly gathered Wednesday evening as part of a welcome banquet for the APEC leaders more broadly.
