This year’s Meta Quest 3S Black Friday deal just got even sweeter with an additional bonus from Amazon or Target.

The Meta Quest 3S started going on sale for Black Friday two weeks ago, but it’s since been sold out several times. Now, it’s back in stock at Amazon for Black Friday and even comes with an extra $49.51 Amazon digital gift card that you can use for any physical items on Amazon’s massive store. If you’re not a fan of Amazon, Target is running the same deal, offering the Meta Quest 3S for $297 and giving you a $50 gift card in return.

Best Buy also has the $297 sale, but the company is no longer offering $50 gift cards with every purchase as it did two weeks ago. That makes it an obvious choice between Amazon or Target for this year’s best Meta Quest 3S Black Friday deal.

No matter which retailer you choose, the discounted headset comes with a copy of Golf+ (a $29.99 value), as well as 1,800 in-game credits on Golf+ (a $9.99 value).

The Meta Quest 3S features the same chipset and RAM as the more expensive Meta Quest 3 (which is still $599!), so it can play all the latest games and Quest 3 exclusive titles like Batman Arkham Shadow and Deadpool VR. Plus, with the VR Forever Gaming Awards coming up, you’ll have no shortage of great fresh games to play.

In a recent development, every Meta Quest also comes with a trial of Meta Horizon+, which currently includes over 100 games you’ll actually want to play, and is even rumored to include 3D movies soon. The service is normally only $7.99 per month, anyway, and even includes over 50 Xbox Game Pass games, making it a wildly good value. If you’re looking to pick up a cheap fresh headset this year for yourself, a friend, or even one of your kids, this is the time to do it.
