According to recent reports, the battle for the US TV rights to the tournament will pit the traditional broadcaster against the ‘whatever-it-takes’ streamer. It could be a tipping point in the industry.

The awarding of broadcast rights in the United States for this year’s World Cup was a cut-price deal to avoid legal action from the existing rights holders, Fox Sports. This week’s tender process for the 2030 tournament will be anything but.

Industry observers note that media rights and broadcast experts canvassed by the Guardian in the US and Europe are united in agreement that Fifa will benefit from the most competitive and expensive auction in the competition’s history. It is expected to fetch between $1.5bn (£1.13bn) and $2bn, with some predicting the outcome could be a landmark moment for the sports and entertainment industry.
