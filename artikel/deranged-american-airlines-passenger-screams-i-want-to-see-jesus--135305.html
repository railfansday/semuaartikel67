In a recent development, passengers and flight attendants worked together to restrain a man with zip ties and duct tape after he allegedly tried to open an exit door on an American Airlines flight to Chicago. The aircraft diverted to Phoenix, and the FBI says the man is now in custody. A frightening start to Thanksgiving travel, but also a reminder of how promptly strangers can come together when help is needed.

American Airlines flight 1494 was traveling from San Diego (SAN) to Chicago O’Hare (ORD) when a passenger disturbance forced a diversion to Phoenix (PHX) early on November 25, 2026.

According to Phoenix police, the man left his seat, moved toward the front of the aircraft, refused instructions to sit down, and attempted to tamper with an exit door.

Sources indicate that flight attendants and passengers restrained him until the aircraft landed safely in Phoenix around 1:00 am. The man sustained a minor injury and was taken to a hospital for evaluation. No injuries were reported among the other passengers or crew. Take a look at these videos:.

It has been revealed that passenger Sean Bennett, who posted the videos above, noted that he had been sleeping when a flight attendant’s shouting woke him. Another passenger, Hunter Glegory, recalled the man saying:.

“I want to see Jesus, get me off this plane.”.

According to recent reports, .Passenger video captured the struggle and the use of zip ties and duct tape. One witness also described a flight attendant using a beverage cart to keep the confrontation from moving farther down the aisle. The FBI subsequently confirmed that the man had been taken into custody. It is interviewing witnesses and consulting federal prosecutors about possible charges.

Passengers eventually continued to Chicago later Wednesday.
