One Piece Chapter 1199 reveals Luffy’s new mysterious transformation, so here’s everything you need to know.

Undoubtedly one of the greatest chapters in the Elbaf Arc, the new installment follows the events of Chapter 1198, where the Straw Hat captain debuts a new form. This comes after his futile attempt to defeat Imu with the help of Loki and Hajrudin.

As Killingham is about to be defeated by Sanji, the Rain God Zaza tries to board the giant children on the ship. This is when Luffy appears in his upcoming form, resembling a monkey with marks around his body, and saves the children.

Industry observers note that one Piece Chapter 1199 also promises to reveal more about the Void Century. With Robin, Saul, and Chopper receiving the centuries-old chest from Biblo, they learn about a human named Lili who used to live in Elbaf, which may unveil some mysteries of the forgotten era.

Recent findings suggest that titled ‘Things Passed Down Through Generations’, One Piece Chapter 1199 focuses on Luffy’s upcoming monkey-like form and its powers. The cover page features Vivi asking Morgan to take her picture for her wanted poster.

Recent findings suggest that after ripping Zaza away from the ship, Luffy beats his chest. It creates a beat that the giants recognize as the Drums of Liberation. However, they soon realize that the rhythm is a little different, hinting that Luffy’s Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, has more in store than we previously thought.

Sources indicate that luffy rips a giant house from the ground and throws it at Dorry and Brogy’s ship. However, they cut the house in half in a single slash before it can crush them.
