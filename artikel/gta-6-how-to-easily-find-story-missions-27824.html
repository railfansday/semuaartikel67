Sources indicate that GTA 6 is a stunningly deep open-world game with a lot to do, but naturally, the story mode is what a lot of players will gravitate toward. While many of the transitions to these missions are going to be natural, in classic GTA fashion, there are a handful of missions that aren't so obvious, and require a degree of waiting or a proactive approach.

Here's how to always stay on top of what missions you need to advance the story, and how to move things along if they need a little nudge. Given the direct control you have over both Jason and Lucia as protagonists, it's important to know all the tricks so you don't get stuck.

Press the touchpad on PS5 to open the map, which brings up a variety of missions and points of interest. Think of your map as a master list of everything to do, with icons for drop points (to stash/sell illicit objects), side quests, shops, banks to rob, and, of course, story missions.

Industry observers note that these are indicated by a "book" icon near the top left of the screen, listing all story missions you can take part in. For the most part, this list will include at least one mission, all of which are required to progress "Jason and Lucia's Story," the campaign of GTA 6.

Note that occasionally, Lucia or Jason will be forced to go on solo missions. Think Lucia meeting up with her mom for a mother-daughter day, or Jason doing an odd job for an old friend that Lucia isn't interested in attending.

In a recent development, when these missions arise, make sure you say goodbye to your partner before entering the mission area. Sometimes the mission won't progress unless the required character's significant other is completely out of the zone, so if you're having trouble starting a story mission, that might be why.

We recommend knocking these story missions out as soon as possible, as they can be finicky and block progress on other missions.

When story missions aren't listed, here are the two things you can do to speed things up:.

The game doesn't make it immediately apparent, but sometimes calling a key character is enough to advance the story. As you call each contact in the recents menu, they will disappear, indicating you have all the info you need from them, and a story mission may populate as soon as the call ends. By going to bed afterward, you can accelerate this story mission prep process and move things along.

It has been revealed that if you're looking to go through the story of GTA 6 at a brisker pace, we recommend doing both in tandem. Go to bed at your home location, sleep until the next day, then call as many people as you can. It might take a few days, but story missions will pop up over time.
