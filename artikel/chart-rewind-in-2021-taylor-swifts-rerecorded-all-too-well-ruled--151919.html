“I was going through a bit of sad time,” Taylor Swift told Jimmy Fallon in 2021 about the lyrics in “All Too Well,” which she cowrote with Liz Rose. “I started ad-libbing what I was going through and what I was feeling.”.

Originally released on Swift’s 2012 album Red, at just under five-and-a-half minutes in length, the song became the focus of her rerecorded Red (Taylor’s Version), after growing into a beloved entry in her catalog among Swifties.

“All Too Well (Taylor’s Version)” rolled out at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated Nov. 27, 2021, as Red (Taylor’s Version) opened atop the Billboard 200.

Industry observers note that two versions made the set: one mirroring its original run time and a 10-minute, 13-second take (both of which combined into one listing on Billboard’s charts). They accompanied the 14-minute, 56-second-long All Too Well: The Short Film, directed by Swift and starring Dylan O’Brien, Sadie Sink and Swift.

Given the longer mix of “All Too Well (Taylor’s Version)” (which led its versions in consumption that week), the song became the longest No. 1 in the Hot 100’s history. Don McLean’s “American Pie (Parts I & II),” at 8 minutes and 37 seconds, had held the mark for nearly a half-century.

Plus, with the original “All Too Well” having charted on the Hot 100, at No. 80, for a week in November 2012, Swift notched the first No. 1 cover of a previously charted Hot 100 hit (not counting samples or interpolations) in more than 20 years, since Christina Aguilera, Lil’ Kim, Mya and P!nk’s “Lady Marmalade” in 2001, after the original by Labelle led for a week in March 1975.

Swift additionally became the first artist to send an update of an act’s own prior Hot 100 hit to No. 1 since Elton John, whose “Candle in the Wind 1997″/”Something About the Way You Look Tonight” reigned for 14 weeks in 1997-98. His live version of “Candle” hit No. 6 in 1988, after he had introduced its studio recording in 1973.

In a recent development, in 2022, All Too Well: The Short Film won the Grammy for best music video. Swift also claimed three Moonpersons for it at the MTV Music Video Awards: video of the year, best longform video and best direction.

In a Q&A at Latest York City’s Beacon Theatre during the Tribeca Festival in June 2022, Swift reinforced that the expanded “All Too Well” was a gift to her fans, sharing to applause, “You guys just wouldn’t let it go!”.
