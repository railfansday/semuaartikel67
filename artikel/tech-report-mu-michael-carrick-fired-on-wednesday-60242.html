In a recent development, michael Carrick’s future on the Manchester United bench hangs by a thread. According to the English press, he will be sacked, but the decision has not yet been taken according to information from the BBC. Manchester United manager Michael Carrick’s future will be decided at a meeting in Monaco on Wednesday attended by owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and managers Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox.writes the serious British media.

And if the decision was then made to part ways with the English coach, it could only be communicated on Thursday. We will therefore have to wait a little longer before knowing what direction Manchester United is taking. Mauricio Pochettino is named as the favorite to succeed Carrick. The Red Devils are in a sad 18th place in the Premier League.
