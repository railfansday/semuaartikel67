The Marvel Cinematic Universe is constantly putting out new projects and most hotly anticipated upcoming Marvel movie is definitely Avengers: Doomsday. Fans who spent years watching the Marvel movies in order are hyped to see this crossover event, which will feature Robert Downey Jr.'s return to the MCU. Kevin Feige recently spoke about his original plans for Kang and Doctor Doom, and fans are not exactly buying his comments.

According to recent reports, what we know about Avengers: Doomsday is limited, but from the outside it seems like the title character was brought in after Jonathan Majors was fired as Kang by the studio. Although in the Doomsday edition of Empire Magazine (via Twitter) Feige painted another picture, saying:.

It probably would still have been called ‘The Kang Dynasty’, but it would have been a set-up for Doom, midway through that movie.

Well, my mind is blown. For years it seemed like the MCU was setting up Kang to be the shared universe's next major bad, thanks to his roles in Loki and Ant-Man and The Wasp: Quantumania (both of which are streaming with a Disney+ subscription). Although according to Feige, the next Avengers movie was always going to pivot the focus over to RDJ's Doctor Doom. That definitely would have been a plot twist, but the Marvel fandom doesn't necessarily think that Feige is telling the truth.

The Kang actor was notably fired after Jonathan Majors was found guilty on third-degree assault and harassment in the trial against his ex. Fans assumed this is why the character was written off, with Doctor Doom instead brought in as the antagonist of the next Avengers movie, formerly titled The Kang Dynasty.
