📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that there's an unmistakable quality in a Richard Madden-led thriller. The actor defines the modern thriller lead through simmering intensity that doesn't overshadow human vulnerability. Rather than playing invincible action archetypes, he anchors high-tension narratives with a vigilant stillness that sets up the rough parts perfectly. From Bodyguard to Citadel, Madden has shown that a thriller doesn't have to be all gas, no brakes. The braking part is what makes the thrill worth it. And for his next series, Madden brings this quality to life in a star-studded Netflix thriller.

Think of this show as The Last Ship meets Bodyguard. In fact, it reunites Madden with Bodyguard and Lady Chatterley's Lover creator Jed Mercurio. The actor co-stars as Webb Preston alongside Gugu Mbatha-Raw, who plays Katherine Decker. "A heroic female Naval Officer becomes involved with the outwardly charismatic Secretary of Defense, only to discover he may be at the heart of a dangerous conspiracy," reads the short but dangerous logline for the thriller titled Trinity, teasing espionage in the waters. Katherine is the second in command aboard the USS Kansas, a nuclear submarine. The stakes have never been higher than on a nuclear submarine, and her primary responsibility is to the vessel. But when she's caught in Webb's charm, she forms a bond that draws her into a top-secret program named Trinity.

Mercurio wrote and serves as the showrunner on the seven-episode thriller that also stars Marcia Gay Harden (Margaret Vandenburg), JD Pardo (Tom Reyes), Kirk Acevedo (John Campbell), Bruce Greenwood (Eric Colby), James Remar (President Banard), Robert Wisdom (Griff Tatum), Wern Lee (Lee Jones), and Mark O'Brien (Brian), among others. Trinity premieres on Netflix on Tuesday, December 1.

“The ballistic nuclear submarines in the US Navy are the most powerful weapons ever created. They can hide in the ocean depths undetected for months and carry the firepower to wipe out entire countries. There’s no deadlier or more claustrophobic setting for a thriller," Mercurio noted in an interview while explaining why he chose Trinity as his next project. Netflix has already released a tense, claustrophobic trailer that introduces Mbatha-Raw's hero and Madden's morally ambiguous character.

It has been revealed that from the Pentagon to the ocean, Trinity covers all channels of Marine life. But nothing can compare to the submarine's environment, deep in the ocean's belly. Katherine nearly endangers her crew and the rest of the world by deploying nuclear weapons to fight a nonexistent enemy. So she seeks answers, only to find herself tangled in a web of mysteries and half-truths. Webb projects an air of calm but isn't above crashing out when stressed, and there's plenty to be stressed about. Katherine will need all her prowess to navigate what could be a catastrophic event by taming a rogue secretary of defense.

Stream all episodes of Trinity on Tuesday, December 1, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
