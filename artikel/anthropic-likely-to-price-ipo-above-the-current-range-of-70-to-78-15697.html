Anthropic likely to price IPO above the current range of $70 to $78, sources tell CNBC

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic is likely to price its IPO above the current range of $70 to $78, multiple sources told CNBC's David Faber on Tuesday.

Sources indicate that the artificial intelligence company is planning to close the order books and lock in total demand at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, according to the people, who asked not to be named because they were not authorized to speak publicly. Anthropic is slated to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "ANT" and start trading on Nov. 19.

Industry observers note that if Anthropic's IPO proceeds as planned, the organization will debut roughly five months after SpaceX went public in a record offering that raised $85.7 billion. But Anthropic is facing a public market that's grown increasingly skeptical of the AI trade, as well as mounting and widespread antipathy toward the technology in the U.S.

A group of researchers, including Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, co-founded the company in 2021 after defecting from OpenAI, now its chief rival. Anthropic is best known for developing a family of AI models called Claude, and it underwent a period of explosive growth in 2026, driven largely by the popularity of its AI coding tool, Claude Code.

In a recent development, for the nine months ended Sept. 30, Anthropic noted it generated $33.24 billion in revenue, up roughly 1,200% from the same period last year, according to its prospectus. It reported a $104 billion net loss during the nine-month period, though most of that was due to the change in fair value of convertible notes and derivative liabilities. Anthropic lost $25.1 billion over the same stretch in 2025, the filing said.

Anthropic has long worked to establish a reputation as a safety-conscious AI company, and its prospectus included more than 90 pages of risk factors for investors to consider ahead of its listing.

Recent findings suggest that "I think if we do this right, we can create an age of miracles where humanity is able to solve problems that have eluded us for millennia," Amodei said in Anthropic's roadshow video. "We have to make sure that all the risks, all the ways that things can go wrong with this technology, we very carefully address those risks as we bring the benefits and as we have a viable business at the same time.".

"I invite you to join us on the next phase of this journey," Amodei said.
