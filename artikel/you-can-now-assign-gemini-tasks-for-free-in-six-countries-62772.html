Starting today, the Gemini app’s “tasks” feature, previously known as Spark, is now available for free users after requiring AI Pro in the past.

Industry observers note that google previously referred to this capability as a 24/7 personal agent before integrating directly into the main Gemini app experience in October. Above the prompt box on mobile (or below on the web), you’ll see “Chat” and “Task.” The first option is the existing chatbot, while the latter lets you “hand off even more tasks to Gemini.”.

With tasks, Google’s “goal is for Gemini to save you time and money.” Google shared use cases like:.

With today’s announcement, Google is emphasizing that “Gemini is your best choice as a personal assistant because [your emails, calendar, passwords, photos, files, and payment cards] already lives at Google securely, and you don’t have to send it elsewhere.”.

Behind the scenes, Gemini tasks work in your own “hardware-isolated virtual machine” that’s sandboxed and has protections against prompt injection, while leveraging Google Safe Browsing when browsing the web.

Other safety features include having you confirm purchases and asking “clarifying questions if an instruction is ambiguous.”.

Powered by the fresh Hydrogen model, free Gemini tasks are available in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India.
