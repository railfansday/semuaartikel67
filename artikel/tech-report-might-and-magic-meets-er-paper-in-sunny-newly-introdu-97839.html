Literal decades ago, I tried to make a first-person RPG in HyperCard. I got as far as a fateful town gate confrontation before hitting the cap on slides for the shareware version. I mention this because latest, 100% "hand-drawn" first-person dungeon crawler Rise of Furax is uncannily redolent of my long-lost HyperCard game.

In a recent development, this risks being a) a terrible indictment of the game's visual direction, and b) an insinuation that developers Rap2h are dastardly rip-off artists, so let me elaborate that c) I think Rise of Furax looks far, far lovelier than my lousy primordial hypermedia confection and d) it is a legally distinct creation. That unfinished HyperCard concept never left the confines of a Macintosh Performa that is even now rusting on a riverbed. If only I'd pitched it somewhere, I'd probably be a MILLIONAIRE by now. A million pounds is still a lot of money, right? Anyway, here's a teaser video for Rise of Furax.

Sources indicate that the plot setup here is thusly: some apocalypse cult is raising havoc on three islands off the coast of Rivelune. Five heroes – a snake minstrel, a rhino barbarian, a badger knight, a goat sorcerer and… whatever the hell critter that monk is - have been sent to investigate the whole "world is ending" sheebang and do a little levelling.

As in the very first Might and Magic, you steer the party as one, moving around a grid map. Combat is turn-based and there are "secrets, puzzles, and hidden items everywhere". Also schmürpfs. These confuse me even more than the monk character. They look like angry green omelettes. Any pointers? I googled the word and the closest match I could find was Swiss footballer Pascal Schürpf, who appears neither green nor egg-based.

Recent findings suggest that "Every element, from sprites to textures, was hand-drawn on paper," promises the Steam page. It's out April 2027. There's no demo as yet nor even a proper trailer, but god I enjoyed looking at this, in a week otherwise full of screenshots of, like, suggestive scallion distribution in GTA 6. I should finish that HyperCard game. I still remember which river I threw the Performa into.
