Industry observers note that UPDATE: Nov. 17, 2026, 2:54 p.m. ET: This story has been updated with Apple’s statement attributing the disruptions to an internal update, a list of affected services, and the latest status of Apple Music and iCloud Mail.

In a recent development, some Apple owners are having trouble signing into their accounts, downloading apps, and accessing music on Tuesday, Nov. 17, just days after a banking disruption and a United Airlines system outage caused problems for customers and travelers.

Apple has since attributed the disruptions to an internal enhancement and said many of its systems are already back online. The company said it was working to restore the remaining services and that the outages were not caused by a cyberattack, according to Bloomberg.

The Apple disruptions add to a busy stretch of technical failures. Last week, a Microsoft outage disrupted Outlook and other Microsoft 365 services, while a separate telecommunications failure prompted a rare nationwide FAA ground stop. (There's no established connection between those incidents and Tuesday’s Apple problems. But there sure are conspiracy theories.) FEMA also conducted a scheduled test of the emergency alert system on Tuesday afternoon, adding to the sense of generalized anxiety.

Apple's System Status page has changed throughout the afternoon. Apple Music, which was listed as experiencing an outage earlier in the day, is now marked as available. The company has also marked an iCloud Mail performance glitch as resolved, while Apple Pay remains listed as available. According to recent reports, meanwhile, Downdetector shows an uptick in user reports involving Apple Pay, Apple Support, and iCloud. Apple had not listed active incidents for Apple Pay or Apple Support on its status page, as of this writing. Downdetector tracks user complaints, not verified challenges.

Apple has not provided a cause or an estimate for restoring the affected services. People can check the company's status page for updates. As of 2:52 p.m. ET, Apple listed ongoing disruptions affecting the following services:.

Volume Purchase Program: Outage, alongside a separate resolved outage.

iCloud Account & Sign In: Matter.

iCloud Web Apps (iCloud.com): Performance problem.

According to recent reports, earlier in the day, some people reported being unexpectedly signed out of their accounts on iPhones and Macs, then unable to get back in. According to 9to5Mac, users encountered verification failures and unknown errors when attempting to sign in. Others report that the App Store won't load or download apps, while some are having trouble using Apple Pay.

Sources indicate that the problems extended to some of Apple's tools for developers, too. MacRumors reported that Apple's separate developer status page listed outages involving Accounts, App Store Connect, Apple Music API, Apple Music Connect, and Apple Podcasts Connect. Developers have also reported trouble accessing App Store Connect, the platform they use to manage their apps and updates.
