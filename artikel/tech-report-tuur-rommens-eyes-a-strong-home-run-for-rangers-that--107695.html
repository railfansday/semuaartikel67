Tuur Rommens has urged his Rangers team-mates to use a breakneck run of three home games in eight days to build a platform from which to launch a real assault on the Premiership title.

Derek McInnes’ side play host to Aberdeen on Tuesday and then have Falkirk and Dundee United visiting – with Belgian left-back Rommens clear on the need to use the Ibrox atmosphere as a weapon.

‘At the away games, there’s a lot of fans, but at home there’s even more, so it gives an extra boost,’ he said.

‘I think, at home, we have to maintain our good run.

‘We’ll try to keep on winning these three games and then we’ll see from there. If you keep winning, you’ll be up there.

‘We’re back to Ibrox (on Tuesday) and it’s been a long time, but, yeah, it’s nice to play in front of our fans again.

Tuur Rommens (right) celebrates with match-winner Ryan Naderi at Fir Park.

Emmanuel Fernandez headed home the opener in Rangers' 2-1 victory at Motherwell.

‘I guess Aberdeen, it’s a difficult one, but we’ll try to keep on winning.’.

Rangers dug out a crucial 2-1 win at Motherwell on Saturday thanks to an 88th-minute Ryan Naderi winner and sit four points behind leaders Celtic with a game in hand.

Rommens believes the side’s ability to pull wins out of the fire and show they are willing to keep pushing right to the end of matches is helping build unity within the squad and with their followers.

‘Yeah, exactly,’ he noted. ‘Also with the fans, when you score you feel a lot of emotions. You feel that they’re so happy, we’re so happy, so it gives us extra boost to go on Tuesday again and try to get a win.

Recent findings suggest that ‘I think the fanbase is unbelievable. It doesn’t feel like an away game because there are so many fans with us.

It has been revealed that ‘Especially for the upcoming guys, that’s new. It’s the same for me. I’m not even here one year so I’m getting surprised every time that there are so many fans of Rangers when we’re playing outside.’.

Rommens also took time to lavish praise on Naderi and revealed what a deeply loved figure the Czech international has become within the dressing room.

Asked about the progress Naderi has made with his game in recent months, he replied: ‘It’s unbelievable.

‘You can always give the ball to him and he just keeps it.

Recent findings suggest that ‘He’s very strong and I think he deserves all these goals that he gets. So yeah, I’m very happy for him.

Recent findings suggest that ‘He delivers, he scores a lot of goals, he gives assists, so everybody’s happy for him and he’s a good guy. Everybody loves him in the group.’.
