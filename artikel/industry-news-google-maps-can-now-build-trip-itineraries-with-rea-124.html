Industry News: Google Maps can now build trip itineraries with real-time details about each stop

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google unveiled on Monday that it’s rolling out newly introduced Maps features that are designed to help people plan trips and everyday tasks using AI. The tech giant is launching the ability for Maps to create trip itineraries and find appointments for users.

In a recent development, the newly introduced features reflect a broader shift in how tech companies are positioning AI, which is less as a tool that just answers questions and more as an assistant that can complete tasks on users’ behalf. While many companies have released AI agents to complete these tasks, Google is working to bring that approach into a service that millions of people already use every day.

Google indicates users can use the navigation platform’s “Ask Maps” tool to build step-by-step trip itineraries and get real-time details about their route right when they pull up directions.

Recent findings suggest that for example, you could use Ask Maps to plan a holiday shopping day trip or something more complex, like a three-day road trip featuring coastal hikes and oceanfront dining, Google states. You’ll get a day-by-day itinerary with insider tips and numbered map pins for every stop. Once you’re happy with your itinerary, you can share it with friends and family or export it to Google Docs on desktop.

When you’re ready to head out, you can use Ask Maps to reopen your itinerary or find the “itinerary” badge in your chat history. You’ll have access to real-time information, including traffic conditions, business hours, and a look at what the “vibe” is at your next stop, the firm says. You could also adjust your schedule by asking Maps to do something like add a scenic viewpoint or lunch stop along your route. You can then tap any destination in your itinerary to get directions and navigate to your next stop.

Additionally, customers can use Ask Maps to find beauty, pet care, auto, and home repair appointments online and call local businesses for you. For example, you could ask: “Find me a 4 p.m. nail appointment near me.” Ask Maps will then display open slots across nearby businesses.

Recent findings suggest that the new agentic calling feature arrives as tech companies continue to introduce AI agents designed to handle time-consuming tasks, such as scheduling DMV appointments and making reservations. For example, Meta’s AI agent Muse recently gained the ability to call businesses on users’ behalf.

Google also declared that you can now use Ask Maps to swiftly find places on your lists by asking something like: “Which spots saved in my lists have availability for five people in the next 30 minutes?”.

According to recent reports, the tech giant indicates the new updates are starting to roll out on Monday on Android and iOS. As part of the news, Google announced that Ask Maps is expanding to 30 countries across Europe, including Spain, Germany, and the U.K.
