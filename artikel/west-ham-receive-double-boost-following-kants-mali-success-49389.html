West Ham Receive Double Boost Following Kanté’s Mali Success

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that west Ham United midfielder Mohamadou Kanté has helped Mali qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations following a 2-0 victory over Rwanda on Tuesday evening. It’s another significant milestone for the young Hammer, who has only just made his senior international debut, and the good news doesn’t end there.

Sources indicate that mali secured qualification with two matches to spare, thanks to goals from Modibo Sagnan and Gaoussou Diarra in Kigali. Kanté came off the bench in the 64th minute, having made his international debut in Friday’s 3-2 victory over the same opponents.

Sources indicate that mali are now guaranteed a place at next summer’s finals in Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda. And having discovered precisely when the tournament will take place, I have to say I’m absolutely delighted for Kanté. In fact, it’s double delight as far as I’m concerned.

First and foremost, massive congratulations to Kanté.

Having decided to switch his international allegiance from France to Mali, he’s already playing senior international football and now has a major tournament to look forward to.

It’s a tremendous achievement, particularly when you consider how rapidly things have happened for him this season.

Kanté has been one of West Ham’s most impressive players, and his international recognition is thoroughly deserved.

As Claret & Hugh recently reported following his international debut, his performances for the Hammers have now been rewarded with an opportunity to establish himself on the international stage.

But there’s another reason I’m so pleased about this news, and it’s one that makes a massive difference from a West Ham perspective.

I’ve always disliked the fact that the Africa Cup of Nations has so often been plonked directly in the middle of the domestic football season.

As we saw during the last campaign, the losses of both our full-backs, Aaron Wan-Bissaka and El-Hadji Malick Diouf, had a massive impact on West Ham.

Hammers youngster Mohamadou Kanté will be playing in the AFCON.

Indeed, I wrote about the problem before their departure for AFCON, when we were facing the prospect of losing two important defenders at a particularly difficult time.

It was hugely frustrating, and I’ve never particularly liked the idea of clubs losing key players for several weeks in the middle of their league campaigns.

But this time, thank goodness, it’s different.

The Confederation of African Football has confirmed that the 2027 tournament will take place from 19 June to 17 July, rather than during the winter months.

That means Kanté can enjoy the experience of representing Mali at a major international tournament without West Ham having to lose him in the middle of the Championship season.

Of course, there’s still the question of whether Kanté will even be at West Ham next summer. Who’s to say he’ll still be at the club next year?

But assuming he is, the timing means Scott Parker won’t suddenly have to manage without one of his most important midfielders for several weeks during a promotion campaign.
