📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Users are seeing the option to mute a video appear in the YouTube app on Android, but it isn’t showing up for everyone.

In a recent development, screenshots of a new video control button have started appearing on Reddit over the last week. The posts haven’t exactly begun hot debates, but it’s an interesting UI change. A mute button now sits next to the fullscreen option in the left section of the video player. Tapping it does nothing other than cut audio, until it’s tapped again to unmute.

One user details the app version on their Pixel 11 Pro XL – version 21.46.522. They also note that other devices they’ve checked on the YouTube app versions don’t appear to show any sort of mute button in the UI. Without many comments on either post, it’s hard to nail down just how wide this rollout might be.

In reality, this seems like a very small UI design test for the YouTube app. YouTube will often select a small number of users to push experimental features to while they polish them further before release. Unlike features that Premium users get to test, these are locked.

It’s not necessarily a massive change, but it’s sort of a confusing one. One wonders why a mute button is necessary. It also adds another button to accidentally hit when trying to tap the fullscreen option.
