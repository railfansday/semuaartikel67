Real Madrid look in ominous form under Jose Mourinho, with the 15-time Champions League winners chasing a tenth straight victory in all competitions for the first time since December 2021.

Sources indicate that however, dig a little deeper and Los Blancos are not as bulletproof as they appear. Mourinho's men staged late rescue acts to come from behind to beat Roma and AEK Athens last month, while they fought back from 1-0 down to edge past Celta Vigo 2-1 in La Liga on Saturday, courtesy of goals from Jude Bellingham and Carlos Espi — the latter's third late winner this season.

PSV Eindhoven last faced Madrid in the quarter-finals of the 1988/89 European Cup, when the Dutch side were reigning champions and Los Blancos were in the middle of a 32-year trophy drought on the continent's biggest stage.

Tonight's visitors have progressed beyond the last eight only once since then, while their opponents have won the competition nine times in that period — and that gulf is likely to be reflected at the Bernabeu.

While PSV are top of the Eredivisie, they have taken only four points from their opening four league phase games, with their best result a 1-0 win at Porto last month. That was sandwiched by a 1-0 loss at RB Leipzig and a 2-1 home defeat by Club Brugge, having drawn 1-1 with Shakhtar Donetsk on opening night.

They are without captain Mauro Junior after he suffered a muscle injury in the 4-1 victory against NEC Nijmegen on Saturday, while Adamo Nagalo, Jerdy Schouten, Sami Ouaissa and Alassane Plea are also sidelined.

Madrid's perfect home record and star quality makes it difficult to see anything other than a comfortable win for Los Blancos.
