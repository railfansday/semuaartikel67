Liverpool strategy on loaning out 13-goal starlet in January – QPR should consider a move: Opinion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

QPR had a fairly quiet summer transfer window but recruited well. Their six signings saw key areas addressed, improving in goal with Pierce Charles and Calum Ward while depth was added at full-back with Boy Kemper, Dennis Cirkin and Tariq Lamptey signing.

It has been revealed that the Hoops sit 8th in the Championship table after 15 games, so they’re in the play-offs conversation. January will be key in deciding whether they remain in the hunt or slide into mid-table.

There aren’t glaring areas of the squad that require improvement, but the frontline was untouched in the summer, and there is a case to be made for upgrading. One player who could breathe fresh life into QPR’s striker department is Liverpool prospect Will Wright.

Sources indicate that the Daily Mail has reported that Liverpool are planning a January loan move away for 18-year-old hotshot Will Wright. The Anfield outfit signed him from Salford City in 2025 and he has rapidly become one of their standout youth players.

It has been revealed that wright has 13 goals and three assists in 12 games for Liverpool’s U21s this season, starring in the Premier League 2 and UEFA Youth League. Standing at 193cm tall, he can hold up play and win the ball in aerial duels, but he is deceptively quick and isn’t afraid to carry the ball with mazy runs either.

And, as would be expected of someone with his youth goal record, Wright knows how to put the ball in the back of the net too.

Sources indicate that liverpool feel he needs to develop physically against stronger opponents, and he will certainly get that in the Championship. QPR could be a good place for him to do so, as they have a goalkeeper in Charles who will be able to pick out Wright with long balls into the channels, or onto his chest or into his feet.

Furthermore, the existing physical forwards like Richard Kone means the onus won’t be solely on Wright to be the target man. He’ll get exposure to tough physical duels without having the responsibility of seeing to this task all alone.

The R’s have created 37 major chances this season – the 10th most in the Championship – so Wright will get goalscoring opportunities with his smart movement. And, given his finishing instincts, he’d be backed to net a few goals.

Industry observers note that the question for QPR will be whether Wright is an upgrade on what they already have. In Kone, Rumarn Burrell, Rayan Kolli and Justin Obikwu, Stephan has enough depth to go without a striker signing; especially considering Paul Smyth and Alfie Lloyd can play there too.

Recent findings suggest that but, Wright has shown more promise than Obikwu, who could benefit from a loan of his own. Furthermore, there has been past uncertainty over Kolli’s situation, while Kone and Burrell have had injuries.

In a recent development, wright could be a necessary reinforcement and an upgrade on the existing depth. He could perform well in the Hoops’ setup and would get the physical test Liverpool desire as they plot the next steps for one of their most exciting prospects.
