Sick, Tired Climbers Bail on Everest

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

At last word, the Russian team attempting Everest’s West Ridge was waiting out high winds in their tent at 5,600m. Twenty-four hours later, the team has revealed their expedition is over.

“We’re back at the lodge; we just don’t have the strength left,” leader Andrei Vasiliev told MountainClub today from Lobuche. He explained that the entire team was suffering from colds and coughs that had accompanied them on their previous trips up the mountain. In addition, Vitaly Shipilov has an injured shoulder.

“We might have been able to push through this current outing, but we objectively wouldn’t have enough left for another push,” Vasiliev went on.

This most recent push was the last acclimatization round the team needed before considering a final summit climb. They intended to reach 7,500m and then rest before the final effort. However, the climbers are exhausted and ill, and the conditions on the mountain are no better. Vasiliev noted the seracs are falling more frequently than they were at the start.

“We’ve decided not to take any unnecessary risks,” he explained.

The team will now focus on a upcoming attempt next spring. “I’m channeling my frustration and dissatisfaction as fuel for a future attempt,” he concluded.
