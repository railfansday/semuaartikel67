Mario Hermoso explains how Roma’s unique approach under Gian Piero Gasperini saw defenders like him ‘bring something extra’ scoring goals in the 2-2 with PSG. ‘We play with no fear.’.

The defender opened the scoring after just five minutes when coming forward to combine with Matias Soulé, then PSG turned it around through the usual free header on a corner by Illia Zabarnyi and the Ousmane Dembele penalty.

Donyell Malen converted his own spot-kick off the bench late on, after a Vitinha handling offence in the box, for the final 2-2 result.

“Everyone knows what the coach does, this is his identity, his style of playing,” Hermoso told Sky Sport Italia.

“We defenders get the opportunity to be protagonists, so I am very happy to come to a stadium like this, and see Roma playing in this special way, with no fear. We are on the right track and must keep taking these big steps forward.”.

There were some very unexpected decisions from Gasperini in the starting XI, as he left Malen, Paulo Dybala and Bryan Cristante on the bench.

Perhaps this was a way of giving them something to come on during the match when the legs are beginning to tire from their man to man approach?

“Everyone in this squad is important, we are set out in a way that we can all be protagonists. Dony, Paulo and Bryan had more minutes in this season, but they came on with the right mentality, and were ready to help get a good result.”.

Paris Saint-Germain only managed to surprise Roma from a corner and a penalty, so it was a strong overall performance from the Giallorossi.

It has been revealed that “The coach gives you confidence to believe, to have this mentality of reacting to difficult moments that we suffered not just this season, but over the years. He brought this identity to Roma, and we are happy to carry it on for a great club like Roma.”.

Hermoso also had a chance to score another goal from open play and got it wrong, so this is not a latest experience for him.

“With these teammates who play in attack, we do try to make these moves in training sessions, everyone gets to have a go, and that means us defenders can bring something extra to the team, it’s an added benefit.

“I am also glad to see the young players coming through, we’ve got strong ideas on how to play, and I’m happy to be a part of it,” concluded the Spaniard.
