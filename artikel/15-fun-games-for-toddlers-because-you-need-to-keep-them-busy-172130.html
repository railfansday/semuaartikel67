According to recent reports, toddlers are bundles of energy: always on the move, always growing, always learning. Learning with play is essential for their development because it improves small and large motor skills, muscle strength, emotional intelligence, language development, spatial awareness, and more. According to an article by Michael Yogman, MD, FAAP, on Healthychildren.org, “research shows play can improve children’s abilities to strategy, organize, get along with others, and regulate emotions. In addition, play helps with language, math, and social skills, and even helps children cope with stress.”.

It has been revealed that figuring out how to keep toddlers engaged is no easy feat. There are outdoor games, indoor games, toys, beginner board games… You get the idea. The key is to decide which activity is right for your kiddo, and that depends on factors like their age, energy level, developmental stage, and other milestones.

Recent findings suggest that one of the most important things to consider is what interests your toddler. Do they love Ms. Rachel? Are they obsessed with a sport? Do they love baby dolls or animals? Once you figure out what will keep them interested, you can go from there.

Another factor is something called Play Schemas, a concept first used by Swiss psychologist Jean Piaget. As stated in My Play Cupboard, Play Schemas are repeated patterns of behaviour you notice children engaging in when they play. Nine schemas can be commonly observed in young children, including connection, transforming, enveloping, enclosing, and positioning.

Once you figure out which Play Schema your toddler is drawn to, that will help you decide what type of games they’ll enjoy the most.

What are the benefits of toddler games?

Playing games with your toddler is not only a great opportunity to enjoy quality time with your child, but they help develop crucial life skills like problem solving, small and large motor skills, physical coordination, hand-eye coordination, and learning how to be social with others.

What types of games are best for toddlers?

In a recent development, while it depends on your child’s age, interests, and development, the best toddler games are simple and fun. Popular ideas include pretend play, memory games, music games, and basic movements, like a simplified version of Simon Indicates.

How much screen time is appropriate for toddlers?

According to the American Academy of Child & Adolescent Psychiatry, toddlers should have very limited daily screen time.

According to recent reports, there are online toddler games that are designed specifically for young children. Parents should always review the game or content before allowing their child to watch, monitor usage, and opt for an ad-free, child-friendly tablet or platform.

What are some fun indoor games for toddlers?

Toddler indoor games should be fun and easy to set up: memory, obstacle courses, block building challenges, sensory stations, and pretend play like “the floor is lava,” “vet clinic,” and “grocery store.”.
