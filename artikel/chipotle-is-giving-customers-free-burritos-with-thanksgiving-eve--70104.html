Chipotle is bringing back its Thanksgiving BOGO promotion, giving customers a chance to score a free meal ahead of the holiday.

The fast food chain unveiled on November 20 that its Back Home BOGO deal will return for one night only, allowing Rewards members to get two meals for the price of one at participating US restaurants.

The promotion first released in 2025 and became Chipotle’s largest sales-driving campaign of the year. Its return comes as millions of Americans prepare to travel home and reconnect with friends and family over Thanksgiving weekend.

On Wednesday, November 25, Chipotle Rewards members can receive a free meal when purchasing another one of equal or greater value between 5 p.m. and 9 p.m. local time.

To redeem the offer, customers must visit a participating US Chipotle restaurant and scan their Rewards membership card at checkout.

The promotion is available exclusively in restaurants, meaning orders placed through the Chipotle app, website, delivery services, or third-party platforms will not qualify.

It has been revealed that customers can receive up to five free meals per check, provided they purchase an meal of equal or greater value for each free item. Kids’ meals do not count toward the promotion.

“Thanksgiving Eve is when the hometown group chat comes to life,” mentioned Stephanie Perdue, Chipotle’s Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing.

“Whether Chipotle is the first stop of the night or where the crew ends up later, we’re giving friends a place to get together over real food. The response last year demonstrated us how much our guests embraced the occasion, and we’re excited to bring it back as another reason for hometown crews to reconnect before Thanksgiving.”.

Chipotle is giving away free food to anyone with a tattoo for one hour only.

Chipotle is offering $6 entrees on Halloween to customers who wear costumes.

Chipotle rolls out School Spirit BOGO with free entrees for back-to-school season.

The tech giant cited Morning Consult research showing that 76% of Gen Z consumers visit restaurants to socialize with friends and family, highlighting the role dining out plays in holiday gatherings.

Chipotle is also offering a separate promotion following Thanksgiving, giving customers a $0 delivery fee on qualifying orders from November 27 through November 30.

Recent findings suggest that to claim the delivery offer, customers must enter the code CYBER26 when ordering through Chipotle’s app or website. The deal requires a minimum purchase of $10 in the US or $12 in Canada, excluding taxes and fees.

Although the delivery fee will be waived, customers may still face higher menu prices and additional service fees.

Both promotions are available for a limited time at participating locations, with the Thanksgiving BOGO ending at 9 p.m. local time on November 25.
