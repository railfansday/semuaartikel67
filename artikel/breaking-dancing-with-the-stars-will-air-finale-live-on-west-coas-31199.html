Breaking: ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Will Air Finale Live on West Coast for First Time Ever

🌐 Source: Cosmopolitan • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (306 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Dancing With the Stars is making a major change heading into the finale next Tuesday! The show will air its three-hour finale live on both the East Coast and the West Coast, instead of only going live on the East Coast and having West Coast viewers watch the show late and risk being spoiled.

In a recent development, according to Deadline, the Dancing With the Stars finale will air Tuesday, November 24, at 8 p.m. ET and 5 p.m. PT. It will then re-air at 8 p.m. PT for everyone on the West Coast unable to catch the live viewing.

Of course, this means that an enormous amount of viewers on the West Coast will be able to vote live in the finale for the first time ever, which could definitely influence the outcome of the show!

So, why doesn’t Dancing With the Stars air live on both the West and East Coasts every week? Showrunner Conrad Green told The Hollywood Reporter that it conflicts with local news on the West Coast. As he put it, “local news and the coveted primetime programming slots” get in the way of a live show—but there’s also an issue with rights.

Sources indicate that “It’s a significant issue for [affiliates] is if everyone starts watching the show at 5 p.m. when it airs,” he said. “If you’re in L.A. and you watch at 5 p.m. when it airs [at 8 p.m.] on the East Coast, then it’s going to massively affect the people who will watch the show when it comes over to these different territories and those markets. The affiliates are hugely crucial in the bigger picture for the overall economic structure of the show and for the history of ABC and all of those things.”.

Clearly ABC made an exception for the finale!
