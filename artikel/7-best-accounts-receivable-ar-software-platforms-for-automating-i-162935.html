The best accounts receivable software for your manufacturer is the layer that closes the stage where your cash leaks. If buyers settle from an emailed link and outreach starts late, a reminders tool covers most of it. If customers prefer to pay online by card or ACH, add a payments network with a customer portal. If clients require invoice uploads to Coupa or Ariba and clear multiple invoices with one blank-memo wire, you need an AR automation layer on top of the ERP. Every platform listed here serves B2B businesses invoicing other companies, excluding consumer payment apps and collection agencies.

In a recent development, what invoice-to-cash software does not handle is core billing operations. Billing schedules, revenue recognition, and GL accounting remain within the ERP. Contract-first platforms initiate workflows from signed agreements and map customer billing prior to collection efforts; invoice-first applications pick up after ERP invoice generation.

Most finance stacks have four layers: the ERP that creates the invoice and holds the books, a payments network, a reminders tool, and an AR automation layer working the steps in between.

The ERP covers the last row, and a reminders tool covers timing. The rows in between depend on reading what a customer or a portal sent, which is AR automation work. That share keeps growing. Paper checks fell to 26% of B2B payments in 2025, per the AFP 2025 Digital Payments Survey as reported by Nacha, and FedNow settled about 2.5 times as many payments in Q3 2026 as a year earlier, per Federal Reserve FedNow statistics. Heading into 2027, more money arrives over electronic rails while its remittance detail lands somewhere else.

Sources indicate that on Xero or QuickBooks with customers paying from the emailed link, the leak tends to be timing, and a reminders tool covers it. Once W-9 requests and payment questions fill your team's week, the replies need a different layer.

Portal rejections and lump-sum wires put the leak in the table's middle rows, where an AR automation layer on the ERP fits. In a demo, we'd ask each vendor to show how fast its software flagged a rejected portal invoice, then to apply a multi-invoice wire with a blank memo live.

Sources indicate that if your team still keys card numbers or chases ACH details, start with the payments layer. A payments network with a customer portal gives buyers stored methods and autopay. Unless the vendor notes otherwise, it won't read replies or file into buyer portals, so plan an AR layer for those stages.
