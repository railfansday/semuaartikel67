In a recent development, like other Google apps, Messages for Android A/B tests many features. It can take the RCS/SMS client a rather long time to actually launch fresh functionality in stable. From various reports, Google announcements, and devices we’ve checked, this is the current state of Messages.

These are Messages features that Google confirmed or have been spotted in the wild by beta customers.

In the US, Google Messages now lets you “Text with Gemini” using the “Channels” feature introduced in November. This uses RCS Business Messaging and Gemini “tasks” (previously Spark) for a more proactive experience that also lets you direct the agent to accomplish actions from the Messages app. Use the chat with the four-color Gemini logo after setting up via gemini.google.com/channels.

Instead of pinned chats appearing at the top of the list, Google Messages is elevating them above all conversations as circular contact photos.

You now access “Search” — which has a redesigned page — from a centered floating action button (FAB) alongside “Start chat.”.

Google has replaced the Gemini FAB with a button in the top-right corner next to your profile image.

Read receipts are now placed in a circle at the bottom-right corner of message bubbles and images. You swipe left to see timestamps and the encryption lock, while you can swipe right to reply. This first started appearing in August of 2024, but is now seeing wide beta and stable availability.

Enhanced Protection adds “Advanced detection to prevent spam and scams” across three features. Go to Google Messages settings > Protection & safety.

To see timestamps and the RCS encryption lock, you can now swipe left anywhere in a conversation. To reply to a message, you have to swipe right on the bubble. Tapping a message still shows the read receipts.

Recent findings suggest that instead of a top toolbar, long-pressing on a message or image opens a floating menu as the background blurs. Another press lets you copy just a part of the chat. Previously, you had to copy the entire message.

With iOS 27’s September launch, Android-iPhone conversations over RCS gain support for inline replies that include quotes, as well as photo reactions with the actual emoji instead of just the text description.

The new Google Keep integration lets you swiftly create and share notes inside chats. If you don’t see a contextual suggestion to “Create list,” open the ‘plus’ menu for a new “Keep Notes” tile.

In a recent development, this will slide up a sheet that shows your list of notes and the “Create a note” button. Upon selecting or starting a note, you’ll see a send button in the bottom-right corner. Afterward, there’s a live preview in the conversation thread.
