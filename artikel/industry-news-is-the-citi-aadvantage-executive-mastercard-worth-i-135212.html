Industry News: Is the Citi / AAdvantage Executive Mastercard worth its $695 annual fee?

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (176 words, 📖 Standard)
  • A premium airline card offers full lounge membership, statement credits and travel perks that can offset its high fee for frequent flyers.
  • The card provides access to nearly 50 airline lounges and over 60 partner lounges worldwide, with guest privileges for family or two companions.
  • Travel benefits include priority check-in, priority boarding, a free first checked bag on domestic flights and credits for Global Entry or TSA PreCheck.
  • Additional perks include elite status with select hotel and car rental programs, travel protections and no foreign transaction fees.
  • Lounge access is valid only when flying the airline or its Oneworld partners with a same-day boarding pass.
  • Statement credits for vacations, dining, car rentals and in-flight purchases have specific eligibility rules and may require advance planning to maximize.
  • Bonus points can help achieve elite status, but some credits (such as for car rentals) may not always post automatically.
