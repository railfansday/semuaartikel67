Donald Trump has returned from his trip to Asia, and in a press briefing before a White House press corps eager to learn what had taken place in his closed door summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, as well as Russian President Vladimir Putin, whom the president gleefully referred to as “my friend” this week in front of a room full of cameras.

When CNN’s Kaitlan Collins pointed out that Russia is one of Iran’s biggest allies and asked Trump if Russia is a “friend to America,” the president of the United States of America said he ”can’t speak to America.”.

So who can he speak for? Himself, of course, and the autocratic rulers like Putin whom he considered his close personal friends.

“I can’t speak to America,” Trump remarked, speaking from his position as the chief representative of the American people, government, and territory. “I’ve been dealing with Putin during the fake Russia-Russia-Russia hoax, and to be honest, he was very nice about it … and I consider him to be somebody that I have a very good relationship [with].”.
