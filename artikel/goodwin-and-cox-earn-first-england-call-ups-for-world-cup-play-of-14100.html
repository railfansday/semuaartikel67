Isobel Goodwin and Katie Cox have received their first England call-ups for the World Cup play-off against Ukraine, while Ella Toone returns.

Goodwin scored her third Women's Super League goal of the season in a 1-1 draw against Arsenal at Emirates Stadium on Sunday, when Lionesses boss Sarina Wiegman was in the stands.

Cox, 20, is on loan at WSL 2 side Nottingham Forest from Chelsea and has kept one clean sheet in four appearances this term.

After initially being dropped from the side in October, Toone replaced the injured Lucy Bronze and has done enough to convince Wiegman she warrants a place against Ukraine.

There is also a spot for Aggie Beever-Jones due to Chelsea team-mate Lauren James' injury, while Grace Clinton and Poppy Pattinson return to the fold too.

Grace Frisk, Frey Godfrey, Erica Meg Parkinson and Lexi Potter all hold onto their places after debuting in the 8-1 aggregate victory over Greece in the first round of the play-offs.

The first leg against Ukraine will take place in Murcia on December 1 before the return leg at Wembley on December 5.

Wiegman said: "We know what’s at stake in these games and our focus is on producing two strong performances to secure our place at the World Cup.

"We have worked hard to put ourselves in this position and now we have to finish the job. Ukraine will provide a challenge and we will prepare with the same focus and determination that we always do.

"We know what Wembley can be like for our massive games and that special connection we have with our supporters. We've shared many happy moments with them in the stadium and hopefully this will be another.".
