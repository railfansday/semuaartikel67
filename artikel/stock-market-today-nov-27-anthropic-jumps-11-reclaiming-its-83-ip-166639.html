By RJ Fulton – Nov 27, 2026 at 4:51PM EST.

Anthropic surged 10.98% on Friday, November 27, closing at $91.88 as renewed buying interest in artificial intelligence (AI) stocks fueled a broad market recovery. The rally pushed shares further above their $83 IPO price following a volatile first week of trading, as investors reassessed the growth prospects of the newly public AI manufacturer.

Trading volume reached 143.6 million shares, reflecting substantial investor activity despite falling below recent trading levels. Anthropic's strong performance comes amid growing interest in its Claude AI models and enterprise applications. However, questions remain about its elevated valuation, intensifying competition, and its ability to translate rapid adoption into sustainable profitability.

NASDAQ · As of 1:15 PM ET 11/27/26.

Wall Street finished the holiday-shortened trading week with strong gains as investors returned to technology and artificial intelligence (AI) stocks following recent selling pressure. The S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) climbed 1.60% to close at 7,297.49, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +2.31%) surged 2.31% to 24,129.50, reflecting renewed optimism across the technology sector. Other major AI companies also participated in the rally, although their gains were more modest than Anthropic's. Microsoft (MSFT +0.60%) rose 0.60% to $492.85, while Alphabet (GOOGL +0.68%) (GOOG +0.54%) gained 0.68% to $274.96.

With a valuation approaching $2 trillion at its IPO, Anthropic faces substantial expectations for revenue growth, market share expansion, and eventual profitability. Even strong business performance could disappoint shareholders if it falls short of what is already reflected in the stock price.

Recent product developments provide reasons for optimism. Anthropic continues to expand Claude's capabilities, including fresh coding tools and more competitive model pricing. However, lower pricing also intensifies pressure to control the considerable computing costs associated with developing and operating advanced AI models.

According to recent reports, investors should focus on Anthropic's enterprise revenue growth, customer retention, gross margins, and cash consumption. These metrics will help determine whether growing adoption is translating into a sustainable business or whether infrastructure spending and price competition are absorbing much of the financial upside.

In a recent development, anthropic has an opportunity to become a major enterprise software platform, but its valuation leaves little room for execution missteps. Until the tech giant demonstrates that revenue can scale faster than its operating costs, investors should distinguish between excitement surrounding its technology and the potential returns available from its stock.
