📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that my very first Defcon was Defcon 2, back in 1994. A friend said, “hey do you want to go to this underground computer/hacker thing in Vegas? I read about it on Usenet.” I like Vegas and I liked thinking of myself as a hacker at the time, so I went. I learned a little and was confused by a lot. What is this colon-slash-slash thing that one dude mentioned?

Industry observers note that the following year, I was a lot more dialed in, as was the conference itself. I had regular access to a UC computer lab and understood more about the tech, skills, and culture. I was semi-regularly going to the LA 2600 meetings. More folks from the LA/Orange County scene were planning to attend. Somebody had the brilliant idea of forming a caravan. That same person had the bright idea of facilitating ride-matching between people who needed rides with people who had spare seats in their car. Unfortunately, that person also dropped out at the last minute, handing off responsibility to me and my friends, without having performed ANY of that carpool ride-matching work. This is how we ended up with “seven sweaty hackers in a Ford Escort,” with one person in the hatchback trunk space. That’s a story (and camcorder video) for another time.

According to recent reports, fast-forward two more years. By then, I was confidently (or at least, as confidently as my social anxiety and imposter syndrome would let me) running the caravan, thankfully without the ride-matching. Defcon 5 / Caravan 3 was a well-oiled machine. We had a radio antenna lashed down to the bed of a pickup truck, fed by a pirate radio station, hooked up to a mixer, microphone, and small PC providing the music files. We had timetables and routes. We even had a printed program to distribute to the participating cars!

Industry observers note that in digging through old papers, my mum ran across the zine/program for that year’s caravan. I no longer have the digital files, but I’ve scanned all of the pages as a PDF for download or as an image gallery, below.

Edit: I’m not clear on why the cover says 3 and the intro text says 4. According to my records, it was my fourth year attending, but still only the third year for the caravan.
