Ahead of a do-or-die clash against Olympiacos for AC Milan in the Europa League, Ruben Amorim spoke to the media.

There’s still time for Milan to turn around their Europa League campaign, but so far, it’s been one to forget. Their successes in the league have often been reduced thanks to poor performances in midweek, and that cannot happen again.

After a dismantling of Frosinone at the weekend, Amorim’s side should be full of confidence and wholesale rotations are not expected in Greece tomorrow night. So, logically, that confidence should continue and there should be several reasons to feel hopeful of a result.

In a recent development, however, as we know, there have been similar feelings heading into Europe this season and the Rossoneri have often not followed up with performances of similar levels. Tomorrow must be different.

Speaking alongside Ardon Jashari, the Portuguese discussed the challenge his team will face tomorrow, as MilanNews relays.

Industry observers note that what do you ask of the team for tomorrow? More volume in attack? How do you proposal to support Ramos?

In a recent development, “It’s not about helping Ramos, but also Camarda and everyone up front. It’s a fact that we need to improve in the final third. The only thing we can do is improve our principles. We need to improve on these little things, and that’s the only way I can help my players.

“Against Olympiacos, it will be a different match than the one against Frosinone, just as it will be different than the one against Cagliari.

In a recent development, “We need to improve, even in our defending. We’ve almost always conceded a goal, so that’s another area we need to improve.”.

How would you describe Olympiacos as a team?

Sources indicate that “Thank you for the welcome. They’re a strong team. They’re aggressive, especially at home. I’ve played here twice, and it was tough for me. I know their coach and what he wants to do.

It has been revealed that “They play compact, positive football, 4-3-3; they change a lot, they rotate, they have Jota, a lot of Portuguese players. They’re a strong team, and with their fans there, it’ll be a tough match.

Sources indicate that “Chiquinho is a great talent, but very impulsive. I hope he plays well tomorrow, but I hope he still has a great career ahead of him.”.

Does AC Milan’s growth also involve Europe?

It has been revealed that “To be a good team, you have to do well both in the league and in Europe. That’s the only way to be a good team. We have to be, especially at Milan.

Sources indicate that “My compliments? I’m always waiting for things to go badly, because it doesn’t take much to change the situation. That’s what I’m trying to make my players understand, because it won’t always be this way.

Industry observers note that “We’re at the beginning of a process, and I think we need everyone. We lost Pavlovic, Modric, Saelemaekers, Chukwueze, and everyone has to be ready, because there are no excuses at major clubs. We have to be ready for this.

According to recent reports, “I can’t believe we lost all these games in Europe because we rotated. We have to always be ready and approach the games like against Inter, Frosinone, and Genoa. We’re building so many things. Tomorrow is another test for us.

“I believe in all competitions, but if we can win them all, no. Scudetto? I haven’t changed my mind. This year is difficult, but if we have the chance to fight, we’ll do it. I won’t change for the compliments, because I’m waiting and so are you.

“You’re waiting [for criticism]. Tomorrow we have to win, because if we don’t… I’m ready for that.”.

Is football becoming dangerous for the mental and physical health of players and coaches?

Industry observers note that “It’s difficult to protect players considering the times we’re living in. Younger players will be more willing to live with criticism. It’s different from my reality. It’s difficult to control this.

According to recent reports, “When you asked me about GTA, I’d rather my players play every day rather than scroll through social media. What I mean is that we want more time between games. I’m talking about the product, not my life.

In a recent development, “I’m talking about the quality of the football product. I don’t know what Fabregas meant, but it’s the evolution of society, of football, which is a business, and we have to be ready.

“Social media, and all that stuff, is very difficult for players, who in any case live with more criticism than I do. After Frosinone, I haven’t read anything, because that’s how I want to live my life.”.

You have a lot of experience as a player: what do you expect from the stadium tomorrow?

Recent findings suggest that “I know the atmosphere here. It’s incredible. It’s key for my players to have this experience, even though there’s no stadium more difficult to play in than San Siro. We have to be ready in these kinds of stadiums.

Sources indicate that “We lost 5-1 once, but I’m very focused on what we have to do, how we’ll start the match, I’m focused on these things. The atmosphere should be normal for these players, who are used to substantial events. We have to be competitive and ready.”.
