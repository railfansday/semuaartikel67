Liverpool can do it to Chelsea all over again, as a latest Rio Ngumoha emerges.

It sent shockwaves around English football when the Reds secured the arrival of their exciting mercurial talent from Stamford Bridge.

Ngumoha had come through the ranks at Cobham, but upon celebrating his 16th birthday and having been offered scholarship terms with Chelsea, he rejected them.

It has been revealed that the pathway to the first-team was blocked for Ngumoha at Chelsea, or at least that is how he and his representatives saw. Liverpool offered an opportunity.

The club has a good track-record with younger players and they presented Ngumoha with the kind of pathway to first-team football that was easy to see.

While Chelsea sign every other talent on the planet, Liverpool are much more selective with their choices and that strategy has paid off.
