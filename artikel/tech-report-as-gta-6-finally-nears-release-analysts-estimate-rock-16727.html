GTA 6 isn't even out yet, but it's been estimated that Rockstar's next game has already surpassed $1.45 billion in revenue, with almost 15 million copies sold via pre-orders.

That's according to market intelligence firm Sensor Tower, whose latest sales tracker estimates – updated yesterday – continue to put PS5 pre-orders well ahead of Xbox Series X|S, with sales for Sony's console thought to account for 74% of the lot, at 11.13 million copies. Between both platforms, Sensor Tower estimates 14.94 million units have been sold.

According to recent reports, what's more, as of November 10, Sensor Tower estimated that a whopping 87% of all GTA 6 pre-orders were for the more expensive Ultimate Edition. As a reminder of the different GTA 6 versions, the Standard Edition is $80, while the Ultimate Edition comes in at $100, the price everyone was so worried about before pre-orders were announced. For your extra $20, you get some unique cars and several exclusive shops, among other things – I imagine those shops may have been the selling point for many, given that you're literally missing out on content otherwise.

To an extent, I'd imagine a bit of a 'go big or go home' mindset might apply here, too. After all, we've been waiting 13 years since the last game in the series, so a lot of people are just going to want the, well, ultimate experience. It's this sort of thinking that could get you to spend $400 on a box of mostly expensive tat, though, so be careful with that.

It has been revealed that anyway, there was never any doubt GTA 6 sales would be massive, but it'll be interesting to see exactly where they land at and after launch. Sony and Microsoft have already unveiled that it's the most pre-ordered game of all time on their respective digital storefronts, and there are still a couple of days to go until release. According to Sensor Tower, though, the biggest spike in pre-orders came right after the release of Netflix's Extended Look – sales rose 6x compared to the previous tracked day, with August 28 (the day after the premiere) thought to have brought in around $16.3 million in revenue. So, it sounds like most people were already sold once they got a proper look at the gameplay footage.

GTA 6 preloads are already live, so if you've pre-ordered the digital version – or the code-in-a-box release, if you have yours – you can start getting it downloaded now so you're ready for launch on November 19.
