Sources indicate that there figured to be some growing pains in Miami early this season after it traded nearly half its roster to Milwaukee this summer to get Giannis. But they seem to be figuring things out, winning four straight (2-2 ATS) to get over .500 for the first time this season – but still only 4-9 ATS.

The Greek Freak was terrific in Saturday's 113-117 upset home win vs. Detroit with 34 points on 13-for-17 shooting, with 14 rebounds and three blocks. It was his third straight game with at least 30 points. Only three weeks ago, the Heat lost by 17 at home to these same Pistons.
