It has been revealed that iOS 27.3 is at the moment in beta testing with developers and public beta owners. The enhancement includes multiple new features for iPhone users, including the redesigned Health app, iPhone Duo updates, and much more. Here’s everything you need to know about what’s new in iOS 27.3 ….

It has been revealed that as my colleague Ryan explained, Apple will likely release iOS 27.3 to all iPhone consumers sometime in December. Typically, Apple would release its x.2 software updates in December, but this year is different because iOS 27.1 was exclusive to iPhone Duo. That means that, in terms of the timeline, iOS 27.3 is essentially the equivalent of iOS 27.2.

So with that in mind, we expect iOS 27.3 to be released sometime during the first half of December.

Sources indicate that the redesigned Health app is back in iOS 27.3. This was first available during testing of iOS 27.2, but did not ship as part of that final enhancement.

The redesigned Health app is split into three tabs: Insights, Longevity, and Browse. The app features an all-new design that dynamically adapts based on your data, including Apple Intelligence. The Longevity tab includes a new Health age feature that uses Apple Watch data to show how your metrics track relative to your actual age.

For Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 users, it also offers easy access to view your Readiness score every morning.

In a recent development, for Apple Fitness+, iOS 27.3 adds a new Gym Strength feature. This creates progressive, four-week plans based on your strength goals, experience, schedule, and available equipment.

As you complete each week of workouts, the following week adapts based on the reps and weights you logged.

It has been revealed that for iPhone Duo customers, iOS 27.3 adds Reachability support. This setting shifts the interface on the outer display to the right or left, making it easier to reach the far corners. It’s designed to make iPhone Duo easier to use with one hand, despite the wider outer display. You activate it by swiping right on the right edge of the screen or left on the left edge.

Industry observers note that reachability for iPhone Duo is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Reachability.

In a recent development, iOS 27.3 also brings a fresh face unlock feature to iPhone Duo. This is not full-fledged Face ID. Instead, Apple is using the front-facing camera on the iPhone Duo’s outer display to recognize you, then unlock the device. It doesn’t work for things like Apple Pay and passwords.

iOS 27.3 also tweaks the iPhone Duo’s software keyboard by making the keys slightly narrower and the font slightly smaller.

According to recent reports, finally, iOS 27.3 includes a fresh option in the Settings app to have your iPhone Duo lock when closed. Once enabled, your iPhone Duo will lock when you fold it, rather than transition content from the inner display to the outer display.

When it debuted, the iPhone Duo included a more robust version of StandBy with upcoming clock faces and expanded customization options. iOS 27.3 brings the iPhone Duo’s StandBy experience to all iPhone models.

This includes the redesigned Analog and Digital Clock faces, as well as additional customization options for colors and complications. The revision also adds support for Shared Albums to the Photos face.

iOS 27.3 brings several key Siri AI updates. First, the patch expands Siri AI with many new capabilities across Apple apps. Apple explains:.

We started rolling out these newly introduced capabilities across many Apple apps, including Calendar, Files, Finder, Journal, Mail, Maps, Messages, Music, Notes, Photos, Preview, Reminders, Safari, Shortcuts, and Weather, so users can do things like find a coffee shop they visited last week or archive files that haven’t been opened in over a year.

Recent findings suggest that there are many more actions available now as examples for you to reference. To see what you can do in each app, just ask Siri AI.

With iOS 27.3, Apple is also starting to test fresh ways for developers to create Siri AI integrations for the first time. This is described as an “early Developer Preview.” Apple explains:.

Industry observers note that today’s preview allows any app to bring its content and actions directly into Siri AI by using App Intents, making it easier for all apps to tap into Siri AI’s newest capabilities, including personal context understanding, onscreen awareness, natural language, and more. So people can just ask for help in their own words, and Siri AI carries out any custom action you’ve built, from checking a delivery to booking a reservation to placing an order.

While this is being tested as part of iOS 27.3, Apple says it will start shipping to customers in a few months.

iOS 27.3 also expands Siri AI to latest languages, including French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish.

Industry observers note that you can unlock higher usage limits with an eligible iCloud+ proposal, starting at $2.99 per month. If you hit your usage limit, basic Siri requests will still work. That includes things like setting timers and alarms.

Recent findings suggest that iOS 27.3 brings the original set of Apple Intelligence features to China for the first time. This does not include Siri AI or other iOS 27 updates, but it does include the following features:.

iOS 27.3 is a major patch to Safari. First, the update adds support for a newly introduced vertical tabs design layout on iPhone Duo’s inner display. If you choose this option, your Safari tabs will be arranged vertically along the left-hand side of Safari, rather than along the top. Vertical tabs are also available on Mac and iPad with macOS 27.3 and iPadOS 27.3.

It has been revealed that iOS 27.3 also adds a new Adaptive Dark Mode feature. This feature can automatically apply Dark Mode to websites that don’t natively support it. You can choose to have Adaptive Dark Mode automatically kick in or use it on a site-by-site basis.

For Apple Music people, iOS 27.3 adds a newly introduced “Selfie Sing” feature that lets users record a video of themselves while singing karaoke. To use this feature, start playing a song and go to the lyrics view. Then tap the new camera button that appears on the right-hand side of the lyrics.

The enhancement also refines the design of viewing lyrics while your iPhone is in landscape mode.

There are two key changes to the Apple TV app in iOS 27.3. First, the update introduces video podcasts in the TV app. You can now watch your favorite video podcasts in the TV app, and sync your playback progress between the TV app and the Podcasts app.

Recent findings suggest that second, iOS 27.3 adds vertical video to the TV app. The app now includes a new “Explore Clip Collections” section where you can browse clips from Apple TV content in a TikTok-style design.
