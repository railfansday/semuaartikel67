It has been revealed that engagement season is officially upon us (I should know; I got engaged in December!), and with it comes a very particular kind of pressure: the perfect manicure for the perfect moment. Because once the ring is on, your hands become the main event, shared in photos, texted to loved ones, and passed around group chats.

But a great engagement manicure is about more than looking good on camera. At the end of the day, it comes down to preference and the way a set of nails makes you feel. Some brides-to-be find their calm in a sheer, milky pink; others feel most like themselves in a soft almond shape or a crisp, modern French tip. A glossy top coat might be your idea of perfection, while a hint of shimmer feels more you. There’s no wrong answer here. The best engagement nails are the ones that make you want to hold your hand out and admire it, because when you feel polished and put together, it reveals in every photo, and in every happy-tear hug that follows.
