Quinnen Williams likely has opinions about the Dallas Cowboys' frustrating season and the team's decision to take play-calling duties away from defensive coordinator Christian Parker. The defensive tackle isn't willing to share publicly his thoughts, though.

On the day the Cowboys put special defensive assistant Mike Pettine in charge of defensive play-calling, Williams met with reporters and gave the same response 12 times to a variety of questions.

When asked about his relationship with Pettine, how the team turns around its 4-6 season, whether another playoff chance is slipping away, and other inquiries, all Williams would say is, "I'm gonna keep that to myself.".

Marshawn Lynch would be impressed.

It has been revealed that williams did provide responses to other questions from reporters that weren't related to coaching. He spoke about his golf game, going up against his former head coach Robert Saleh and the Tennessee Titans on Sunday and how much the team needs to step up to right the ship.

According to recent reports, "Everything is on us," Williams said. "We're on the field no matter what play gets called, no matter who calls it, we've got to execute the play. That's going to be around the NFL, that's going to be youth football college football, NFL, once the play gets called, no matter who called it, the players on the field got to execute it. And that's what we haven't been doing, we've got to do it going forward.".
