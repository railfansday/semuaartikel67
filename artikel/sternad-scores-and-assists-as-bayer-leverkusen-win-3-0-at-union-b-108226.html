Industry observers note that two goals inside 2 minutes settled Friday night’s round 11 match at An der Alten Försterei early. By the 11th minute Leverkusen were in control, and Valentina Mädl added a third on the hour to give Union Berlin a long evening on 20 November.

Sources indicate that sternad opened the scoring in the 9th minute, finishing a move set up by Cornelia Kramer. Two minutes later she turned provider. Her pass found Marlene Müller, who doubled the lead.

In a recent development, sternad was rated 8.53, the highest on the pitch, and created 2 chances in total across her 90 minutes. Kramer’s assist added to a productive season: her 6 league goals put her level with Bühl, Imade and Linberg among the division’s top scorers, two behind Wolfsburg’s Peddemors on 8.

It has been revealed that union Berlin’s Lina Hausicke was booked for a foul in the 58th minute. Within 2 minutes the hosts were three down, Mädl scoring without an assist. She had 3 shots before making way after 76 minutes and earned a rating of 8.2.

Recent findings suggest that the hosts did create chances. Union finished with 10 shots, 5 of them on target, and an expected goals figure of 1. Every one of those efforts on target was dealt with. Borggräfe made 5 saves in the Leverkusen goal and was rated 8.22.

Leverkusen were the more dangerous side overall, with 16 shots, 7 on target and expected goals of 1.64. They also had 56% of the ball, completed 380 passes to Union’s 294 and won 10 corners to 5. Three goals from that total made for an efficient night.

According to recent reports, this was a third consecutive victory for R. Pätzold’s side, following a 2-0 win over Hamburger SV and a 3-2 success against Stuttgart. That run came straight after defeats to Nürnberg and Bayern Munich.

In a recent development, leverkusen are third on 22 points from 11 matches, two ahead of fourth-placed Eintracht Frankfurt, who have played once fewer. VfL Wolfsburg are second on 24, while Bayern Munich have taken 30 points from 10 games.

Industry observers note that the head-to-head has gone one way. Leverkusen also won the previous two league meetings, 3-2 in September 2025 and 2-1 in Berlin in January 2026.

Sources indicate that for Ailien Poese’s Union Berlin, it is a second defeat in a row after a 2-0 loss to Werder Bremen. They are eighth with 11 points and a goal difference of -7.

Both sides return on Sunday 13 December. Union host Mainz 05, who have lost all 10 of their league games. Leverkusen welcome FC Köln, sixth on 18 points.
