Players are fitter, more durable and more focused on their individual statistics than ever. The best goalscorers in the world are racking up barely credible tallies for their national teams and pushing on well into their thirties and even forties. But football is a team game, and some teams have reliable goalscorers without ever making it to the World Cup. They're the red herrings in this FourFourTwo quiz.
