📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that use Wellfound to look for a startup job if you want to see the pay before you apply. It’s free, and most listings show a salary range and any equity on offer. Judge each offer by its salary, the pay you’ll live on, and count the equity as a long shot.

According to recent reports, wellfound, formerly AngelList Talent, lists jobs at startups and other fast-growing companies. The equity on a listing is a percentage of a private company’s shares, and it usually turns into cash only if the company is bought or goes public, which can take years or never happen.

According to recent reports, anyone hunting for a startup job in tech, design, product, or sales gets a free search with the salary on most listings. Job seekers who need steady pay and full benefits will do better at established companies.
