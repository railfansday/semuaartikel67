Kim Kardashian is keeping up with the quality of products on the TikTok Shop.

After the SKIMS founder learned how to use Apple Pay, she confessed she’s putting it to good use as her “TikTok Shop bills are outrageous.”.

The most surprising purchase she most recently made? Well, SKIMS dupes, of course.

According to recent reports, “They have so many dupes,” Kardashian told Amanda Hirsch on the Nov. 17 episode of her Not Skinny But Not Fat podcast, “and I want to see how s--tty they are.”.

As for her review of the wannabe SKIMS products, she doesn’t quite see the competition as up to par, revealing, “It didn't fit because it was like a bodysuit and the sizing was so off.”.

But despite the low-quality dupe, Kardashian—who has her kids North, 13, Saint, 10, Chicago, 8, and Psalm, 7, to thank for setting up Apple Pay—has found herself obsessed with filling up her TikTok Shop cart.

According to recent reports, “I buy everything,” the 46-year-old continued. “I am influenced. I buy lashes and I don’t even put on fake lashes myself. I buy every makeup because I want to know what’s out there. And they make it look so good.”.

In fact, the Kardashians star is so in love with swiping through the virtual aisles, she created a fresh project around the hobby.

It has been revealed that “I filmed this whole TikTok series [where] I was gonna test the products and give a real, honest answer, because sometimes a TikTok Shop product is such bulls--t,” she explained. “I have a whole list of things that are amazing but then I have a whole list of s--tty things.”.

According to recent reports, however, Kardashian’s candid reviews may not see the light of day because she admittedly “chickened out” on finishing the series. And now, the All’s Fair actress is left with a slight problem.

In a recent development, “I bought all these products. I’m not kidding you, I have a room full of hundreds of products,” she mentioned. “I buy everything that probably exists on the TikTok Shop.”.
