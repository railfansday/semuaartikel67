Grand Theft Auto 6 leaker Cyberleek appears to have followed through on their threat to release the executable of the Xbox build of GTA 6 they put into the wild last week, calling it "an early Christmas gift" for followers and explicitly linking the leak to efforts to recreate the game on PC: "The torch passes to the open source recomp and emulation projects.".

Industry observers note that cyberleek released a near-complete build of GTA 6 late last week, holding back only the main exe—the part that makes everything else run, basically—until either their crypto coin achieved a $15 million market cap, or November 23, whichever came first. The crypto missed badly, but time marches on, and so here we are.

Recent findings suggest that the release of the exe, naturally, includes a call for more "donations" in its readme file (via Reddit) which Cyberleek noted will go toward a secretive project "for the gamers." They also taunted Take-Two Interactive's efforts to track them down: "Months of takedowns, subpoenas and wallet tracing later, the hunt is still on and the uploads keep arriving. No budget makes a lock unbreakable, and no court order deletes what the internet already has.".

It has been revealed that leaks in the game industry aren't anything new, but this case is unique: GTA 6, effectively the biggest videogame of all time, still hasn't been unveiled for PC, as is Take-Two's way. But the advent of AI means the game could be emulated on our platform far more rapidly than was possible in the past—some modders estimated it could happen within a week.

One of the leads on the Leonida recomp project predicted that things will start to move rapidly from here:.

At the same time, Shayder tempered expectations in a message on the Leonida Discord, saying "it will take at least a week" for the project to reach the point where GTA will be playable on PC.

Recent findings suggest that how that unofficial emulated made-with-AI build would compare to the real deal is an open question, and I strongly suspect the answer will be "not well," but even just having something GTA 6-ish playable on PC is a headache that I'm pretty sure Take-Two would rather not have to deal with. I also wonder whether it will prompt Take-Two to release GTA 6 on PC more quickly than it has with previous releases. A rumor to that effect surfaced in October and I had serious doubts, but it's possible that ongoing emulation efforts will expedite its arrival..
