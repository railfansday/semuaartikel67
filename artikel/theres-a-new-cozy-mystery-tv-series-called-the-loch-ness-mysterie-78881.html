It has been revealed that as longtime CrimeReads readers (“CrimeReaders”) know, the editors of this website are always interested in news related to the Loch Ness Monster. Especially me. For example, several years ago, I wrote about how, in 2016, a bunch of Norwegian scientists had sent a high-tech robot down into Loch Ness to scan the, and the robot sent back sonar scans of a creature that looked exactly like the Loch Ness monster, but actually, it turned out to be a model of the Loch Ness Monster built for the 1969 film The Private Life of Sherlock Holmes that had accidentally sunk during filming. So that was a bummer. A HILARIOUS bummer, but a bummer nonetheless.

According to recent reports, any Loch Ness Monster-related news intrigues me. So you can imagine my delight when I came across news of an upcoming series from the BBC and BritBox. It’s called The Loch Ness Mysteries.

It’s set near Loch Ness, obviously, and it’s about a young, irascible, beautiful London detective who winds up being kicked out (I think?) of her job, only to wind up investigating a personal matter near Inverness… and being drawn into a strange mystery near Loch Ness.

So, in the words of the genius who wrote the BBC’s press release, “from Scotland Yard to Scotland Scotland.”.

Also, what’s the beautiful, brilliant, difficult detective’s name? DCI Vanessa “Nessie” Keen.

The series will star Melissa George, Jamie Sives, and Ashley Storrie, and will be six episodes long. Ed Sellek of Sister Boniface Mysteries is the creator and head writer.

Filming begins before the end of the year, so stay tuned!
