What is Ethereum actually for? A judge and Vitalik Buterin both left it open

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that the 16th of November, a federal judge acquitted Anton and James Peraire-Bueno in the “first of its kind” wire fraud prosecution over a $25 million Ethereum exploit. This judgement came about after the government failed to prove the brothers made any “material misrepresentation.”.

The ruling, filed in the Southern District of New York (24-CR-293), came the same day as when Bitwise CEO Hunter Horsley pitched stablecoins as a backup for banks.

It has been revealed that on a similar note, Ethereum [ETH] co-founder Vitalik Buterin argued that digital assets do not match up to investing in houses. These three developments, when taken together, compound the question of what crypto is actually for.

On the 2nd of April 2023, the brothers seemingly exploited a bug in the MEV-Boost relay software, due to which they could view transactions that sandwich traders believed were private.

The brothers’ alleged proposal for replacement transactions, in turn, resulted in massive losses of roughly $25 million within seconds.

The government called the prosecution,.

It has been revealed that finally, on the 16th of November 2026, Judge Jessica G. L. Clarke, when granting an acquittal, wrote,.

The question before the Court is not whether the Peraire-Buenos’ conduct was right or moral, or even whether it violated uncharged crimes.

It has been revealed that it is simply whether the evidence at trial, construed in a light most favorable to the Government, could support wire fraud and related convictions in this case. Because the Government failed to prove that the Peraire-Buenos made any material misrepresentations in the execution of their scheme, the motion is granted.

The court analyzed the government’s theories – including the “lure transactions” and the so-called “false signature,” and found nothing that could support a wire fraud conviction.

A couple of days later, on the 18th of November, Horsley posted on X:.

Rolling bank outages in the AI era – Consider having some stablecoins or crypto just in case.

The post came as thousands of Chase, Bank of America, and Wells Fargo customers reported being unable to access their accounts.

Vitalik Buterin, in a separate post on November 18, argued that younger generations have been investing in digital assets over real estate options; doing so, he noted, reduces –.

Perverse incentives that drive people to push to keep housing expensive.

But he added a condition: housing had the advantage of being –.

A culture and policy-encouraged default of investing in a safe non-crazy thing that you can’t easily liquidate.

Modern platforms encourage the opposite of this, leading to more irresponsible behavior… We need the equivalent of those good properties that housing investment has, for modern digital assets.

The three developments have been framing crypto’s developing role separately, but these assets have a long way to go before they can fully replace TradFi.

Vitalik Buterin himself leaves the question open, as he observed,.

We need the equivalent of those good properties that housing investment has, for modern digital assets.

The ruling did not answer that question, nor did the outage.
