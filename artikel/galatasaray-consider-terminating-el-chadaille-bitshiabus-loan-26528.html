El Chadaille Bitshiabu (21) has played just 21 minutes for Galatasaray in 2026/27 following his summer loan move from RB Leipzig, and now the Turkish side could be ready to end the Frenchman’s loan deal.

BILD cite Turkish reports that Galatasaray are ready to send Bitshiabu back to Leipzig after arriving at the Süper Lig leaders in poor condition, reportedly 110kg and a body fat percentage of 18 percent – thus, he’s barely featured for Okan Buruk.

It’s important to note that Bitshiabu also takes up one of the foreign spots in Galatasaray’s squad, although BILD add that there have been no discussions between the two clubs regarding an early termination.

In a recent development, bitshiabu played 1,127 minutes across 21 Bundesliga games in 2024/25, but played just half that across 11 league games in 2025/26 under Ole Werner. Should the central defender return, then it’s likely that he will be shipped out on loan once again, with French outfit RC Lens one club that came close to signing the defender last summer, but that moved collapsed after both clubs failed to agree on the final conditions of the loan.
