The Witcher 4 Will Be a GTA 6-Sized Leap Forward, CD Projekt Red States

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We're living in a post-Grand Theft Auto 6 world now, meaning that almost every other game in development will inevitably be compared to Rockstar Games' latest open-world crime drama. The developer behind two other big open-world brands, The Witcher and Cyberpunk, is now fielding questions about how its future games will stack up against GTA 6.

It has been revealed that during an earnings call on Tuesday, CD Projekt Red joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski addressed the Leonida-sized elephant in the room: how GTA 6 will influence his manufacturer's upcoming games, The Witcher 4 and Cyberpunk 2. Nowakowski stated that he believes The Witcher 4 will deliver a GTA 6-like leap forward for players.

Industry observers note that "Of course, we did look at the game," Nowakowski said of GTA 6, adding, "We did see the differences" between it and 2013's Grand Theft Auto 5. "We believe there's going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4. It's been 11 years. We are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game. So, yes, you should expect us to make a much, much bigger jump between Witcher 3 and Witcher 4.".

It has been revealed that "We have certain ambitions when it comes to [The Witcher 4] and Cyberpunk 2," he said. "They have not changed when it comes to GTA 6. If anything happened as a result of GTA 6, it's [that it's] always great to see games like that because they show you how much cool stuff can be done, and that's for sure an inspiration in many ways for our developers.".

Nowakowski also addressed The Witcher 4's through the lens of GTA 6's budget. Rockstar's latest release is rumored to have a development budget of more than $1 billion, and Nowakowski categorized CD Projekt Red's upcoming games as not having "budget-driven developments.".

It has been revealed that "I'm not saying we don't care about the budgets. We do," he said. "I'm pretty sure [Rockstar] did as well. But you cannot make games like that having in mind This is the fixed number of money I want to spend and that's it, otherwise we’re gone. They're ambition-driven developments and money's notable. The budgets are not indefinite, that's not what we're saying. And honestly that philosophy has not changed for us because of GTA 6's release; it has just always been like that.".

As for other lessons learned from GTA 6's massive launch, Nowakowski noted that "If you want to reach a certain critical mass, you do have to get out of the core main gaming bubble, [which is] something we did achieve in the past as well. Our games are different and they require a certain different approach. But in terms of the ultimate effect in terms of breaking out to the mass market, awareness, this is something we consider critical.".

In other words, while CD Projekt Red may take some cues from Rockstar Games, it's not trying to copy the Grand Theft Auto developer's homework.

The Witcher 4 is slated for release in 2028. Cyberpunk 2 does not have an revealed release window.
