📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Among the most significant updates are the installation of water dispensers and the upcoming restaurant, Best Pizza@Sea.

AIDA said in a press release that during her drydock stay, 53 water dispensers were installed in the cabin corridors. A reusable glass carafe is provided in every cabin and can be refilled as needed during the voyage.

The water dispensers will be installed gradually across the entire AIDA fleet during scheduled drydocks.

Next in line for manufacturer drydocks are the AIDAmar, starting December 20, 2026, and the AIDAblu in the spring of 2027.

For the AIDAcosma, additional work included The Yacht Club Restaurant, which has also been given a visual refresh: the area now features a modern carpet design and newly designed seating.

AIDA stated that, as part of the maintenance, guest areas have been renovated, and furniture in the cabins and public areas has been reupholstered. Mattresses, flooring and tiles have also been upgraded.

Technical systems were modernized and included work on the electric Azipod propulsion system, the bow and side thrusters, as well as the stabilizers.

Recent findings suggest that the AIDAcosma also received a newly introduced biocide-free underwater coating. The coating reduces fouling and drag, lowering fuel consumption.

It has been revealed that the onboard IT infrastructure was also upgraded, resulting in the rebuilding of IT data centers and the updating of the network architecture. Restaurants, bars and shops were also equipped with newly introduced point-of-sale systems.

After its drydock, the AIDAcosma will first set sail on the “Pearls of the Mediterranean” cruise.

The “Andalusia & Canary Islands” itinerary will then reposition the ship to the Canaries for the winter.
