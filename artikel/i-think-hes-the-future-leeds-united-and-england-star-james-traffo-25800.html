Leeds United star James Trafford has been described as “the future” of England’s goalkeeping department by Premier League and Three Lions icon Steven Gerrard.

Everton’s Jordan Pickford has spent the bulk of the last decade as England’s first choice but is being pushed hard by Trafford.

Playing time has been evenly split between the pair across the last two international breaks and Trafford was given the gloves against Spain on Sunday (November 15).

He delivered a stellar display in the 3-2 Nations League win, intensifying calls for him to replace Pickford as the number one.

In a recent development, gerrard told The Overlap Daily: “I really like Trafford, I think he’s the future. But I also like Pickford. I don’t think we can sit here after one performance and dismiss Pickford.

Sources indicate that “We’ve probably got two England number ones and the manager’s just got the major decision and that’s why he gets the big bucks. But the future’s Trafford. I think that’s healthy.

Recent findings suggest that “I don’t like it when it’s just a foregone conclusion. He’s the England number one and it doesn’t matter how he plays because he’s the outright number one. Why can’t there be competition, why can’t there be healthy competition?”.

Trafford produced a stunning double save in the dying embers, enabling England to hang on and secure a valuable victory over the reigning world champions.

Recent findings suggest that gerrard mentioned: “It made us win the game. It’s as good or as vital as a goal. The second save is world class. There was also a shot straight after it which bounced just in front of him on a greasy surface and it was just like velcro.

In a recent development, “It just stuck to his body. I was really impressed with that as well. It looked simple on the TV but you see so many goalkeepers spill them or want to push them away. He’s just like velcro.”.
