Gary Neville was left ‘absolutely stunned’ by Mauricio Pochettino’s decision to recall Matthijs de Ligt after the defender’s blunder allowed Liverpool to make their breakthrough in a rampant 4-0 victory over Manchester United.

A seemingly innocuous hoiked pass over the top from Liverpool full-back Ronald Araujo wreaked havoc inside United’s half in the tenth minute, leading to Dominik Szoboszlai’s superb opener.

De Ligt had been thrown in for his first appearance of the Premier League season and there were obvious signs of rustiness early on as the Dutchman bizarrely let Araujo’s hopeful ball run through to Alexander Isak.

Isak’s eyes will have lit up as he raced in behind in acres of space and United were guilty of yet more calamitous defending just moments later as Leny Yoro clattered into the back of the Liverpool striker on the edge of the penalty area.

Referee Samuel Barrott wasted little time in showing Yoro a straight red card for the clumsy foul, reducing the visitors to ten men in a nightmare start to Pochettino’s reign as manager.

United’s woes were soon compounded as Szoboszlai struck the resulting free-kick emphatically beyond a despairing Senne Lammes to break the deadlock and send the Anfield crowd into raptures.

Liverpool were not done there, though, and it was Florian Wirtz who directed home Rio Ngumoha’s cross with an audacious flicked finish to double the hosts’ advantage inside only 13 minutes.

A pair of well-taken goals from Ngumoha and Victor Munoz further increased the deficit in a dominant second half for Andoni Iraola’s men, who finished the weekend three points clear of Bournemouth in fourth place.

When watching back replays of the catalogue of errors leading to Liverpool’s opener, former United full-back Neville revealed just how ‘stunned’ he had been left by the decision to select De Ligt in the first place.

‘When I saw Matthijs de Ligt’s name on the teamsheet, I have to be honest with you, I was absolutely stunned,’ Neville said on Sky Sports’ commentary of the match.

‘The reason I was stunned was because of this: if you watched him against Como four weeks ago, he was very much well of it.

Industry observers note that ‘That is just a basic ball over the top of him. It’s his problem to start with.’.

Though De Ligt was the main focus of Neville’s criticism, Yoro did not escape without receiving some barbs of his own.

‘Leny Yoro can be further across but if you look at De Ligt in this channel, he’s miles away from him,’ Neville added.

Industry observers note that ‘Yoro should be across, yes, he can get further around. He has to cover and he doesn’t. He reacts late, his anticipation is really poor.

‘And because he doesn’t play the ball, Yoro, it could be outside the box but it is a red card.

‘It’s so clumsy from Yoro, he makes a lot of these kinds of mistakes.’.

According to Neville, Yoro had displayed great ‘naivety’ in getting himself sent off so early in the Premier League contest.

‘He’s not happy with it, Mauricio Pochettino,’ the ex-England international continued.

‘You could argue with it, Isak just delays slightly to make sure that Yoro runs into the back of him.

‘But unfortunately, when you’re a young defender, you’re going to get caught like that and it’s naivety.’.

Switching his focus back to De Ligt, Neville noted the Netherlands international had likely been ‘exposed’ as a result of playing so little football over the last 12 months.

Industry observers note that ‘We saw it against Como and he’s been out for a year, trying to come back to this level of football. It’s really tough,’ he went on.

‘To have De Ligt out there in a game like this, in a back four, he’s been exposed inside the first ten minutes.

Recent findings suggest that ‘I have to say, Yoro’s positioning covering is really poor. It’s poor.

‘Yoro has got to be across and around the other centre-back. You’ve got to cover your mate and he doesn’t. It’s really just bad all round from Manchester United.

‘Michael Carrick will be watching this at home and thinking, “Yeah, that’s what I was having to deal with two weeks ago”, it’s so poor.’.

Neville’s Sky Sports’ colleague Jamie Carragher was similarly unimpressed by United’s defending, branding the visitors’ start ‘absolutely shocking’.

‘It’s as bad as it gets from two centre-backs,’ the former Liverpool and England defender mentioned.

‘The ball from Ronald Araujo… it wasn’t even a pass, it’s just a clearance, a hopeful ball through the middle.
