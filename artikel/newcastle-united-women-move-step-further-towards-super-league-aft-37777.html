Newcastle United Women move step further towards Super League after Stadium of Light v Sunderland

It has been revealed that in the lead up to this huge Sunderland derby match, Newcastle United Women revealed that star player Beth Lumsden has signed a fresh contract extension with the club. She has made 67 appearances and bagged 21 goals since signing in January 2024 and played a major role in helping get the team promoted later that same season.

Beth also scored twice in the first ever meeting between these two sides at the Stadium of Light, earning the title of the Mackem Slayer in the process.

Days prior to her contract extension, Beth was announced as being included in the latest Scotland squad to face Sweden, qualifying to represent the Tartan Army through her grandparents.

Prior to kick off both sets of fans were seen co-existing outside the stadium wearing their respective colours with no problems reported in what was mostly a family atmosphere. The weather had other ideas though with it being bitterly cold and damp throughout.

Sources indicate that during the warm ups both club crests were shown on the scoreboards but by the time the game got underway Newcastle’s name was reverted to that of ‘Visitors’. A perhaps unnecessarily childish move. While, the overuse of Elvis love songs before each half and at the final whistle was just tiresome.

Newcastle Manager Tanya Oxtoby made a couple of changes to her line up with goalie Anna Tamminen making a first league start since the beginning of October and Hildur Antonsdóttir returned to midfield in place of Katie Bradley.

Newcastle arrived on Wearside with their unbeaten start in WSL 2 to protect. While they have never lost a competitive fixture at the Stadium of Light and never lost a league game to their North East rivals either.

Photo by Adam Mackman via The Mag.

The game took on the feel of a typical derby game at times with neither side prepared to lose in what was also a top of the table clash with second hosting top, a sub plot that neither side had previously experienced prior to this season.

When the sides last met a month ago in the League Cup, Katy Watson proved to be the difference and the in form winger was at it again straight from kick off as she got past United’s left back Kaitlyn Torpey on the byline before finding Sunderland striker Elise Hughes at the near post, but the latter’s effort failed to test goalie Anna Tamminen and instead found the side netting.

Newcastle then took control of the next 25-30 minutes, with right back Rachel McLauchlan going close to breaking the deadlock ten minutes in, when her shot from 25 yards out was tipped over the bar by an excellent Fran Stenson save in the Sunderland goal.

The ‘visitors’ then went close from the resulting corner when Jordan Nobbs sent a deep cross to the back post, but forward Kathleen McGovern sent her free header just wide of the target.

Sources indicate that newcastle continued to test Stenson and she perhaps pulled off her best save around the half hour mark when Beth Lumsden’s low cross found Martha Thomas. The league’s top scorer stole in ahead of the home defence and her effort looked to be trickling over the line but a last-gasp low dive from Stenson saw her push the ball around the post.

Photo by Adam Mackman via The Mag.

The final ten minutes of the half saw some nervy defending from United as the hosts enjoyed their best spell of the game. Again it was Watson’s direct running which was causing the problems and the half time whistle seemed to arrive just in time.

Newcastle made a change at the start of the second half with midfielder Mollie Lambert tasked to play down the left and offer some support to Torpey in dealing with Watson. The move clearly worked as Sunderland failed to recapture their earlier form and Watson was largely kept quiet.

That allowed Newcastle to dominate the second half with further chances created. The best one came midway through the session when Jordan Nobbs did well down the right before sending a perfect ball across for Thomas who had a golden chance to secure all three points but sadly fired wide.

According to recent reports, soon after, substitute Leanne Kiernan forced Stenson into another save. The rebound fell to Lumsden, but the ball sat up and she could only fire wide. And with that the points were shared.

Where that leaves Newcastle United Women.

Most probably would have taken a point before kick-off but given how much Newcastle dominated, particularly in the second half, there was a feeling Sunderland were there for the taking and it was a chance missed and two points dropped.

In a recent development, the positives were perhaps the fact the Lasses restricted Sunderland to just one shot on target all game. While a point on the road also ensures they remain three points clear of their opponents at the top of the table in their bid to reach the Super League (and win the second tier title).

Tanya Oxtoby pointed out in her post-game press conference that United had faced three of their toughest opponents on the road; Bristol City, Nottingham Forest and Sunderland, and remain unbeaten, which is another positive note as we edge towards the Christmas break.

Avoiding defeat to the second placed side also means it’s another game closer to the main objective of promotion. There are now 14 league games remaining with only top spot good enough for automatic promotion to the top flight this season.

The two teams that finish second and third will play off for the right to face the team that finishes second bottom of WSL1 and a chance at promotion, although that represents a much tougher route.

What’s coming up for Newcastle United Women.

Newcastle only have one WSL 2 game remaining before the Christmas break, against Watford on Sunday, 20th December.

Before then United take on Nottingham Forest this Sunday (22 November) in a League Cup group game at Gateshead International Stadium.

It has been revealed that there then follows an international break before an FA Cup Third Round tie away to Manchester Unity on the weekend of 12-13 December with the host team based in Chadderton, Oldham. The Lasses faced the same opponents in the cup back in November 2023, winning 9-2 away from home on that occasion. But following the game both sets of fans developed a great likeness for one another with many Newcastle fans supporting Unity in their games over the past three years.

The following Wednesday sees Newcastle bring their League Cup campaign to a close with current leaders Manchester United set to visit St James’ Park for a rare midweek game under the floodlights.
