It has been revealed that black Friday is just days away, and some of the week’s most compelling deals are already live. Don’t wait until after Thanksgiving, because the days leading up to it are actually the best time to shop for a upcoming pair of sneakers.

Industry observers note that we’ve spotted markdowns on the season’s most popular trending styles from Dries Van Noten, Toteme, Nike, and more. Whether you’re looking for classic everyday sneakers, a stylish low-profile pair, or statement-making metallic shoes, there’s something in the mix to suit your taste.

Note: Sizes are already selling out quickly, so shop the best Black Friday sneaker deals while they last.

Mary Jane hybrid sneakers aren’t going anywhere soon—and this Tokyo style from Adidas is a steal at more than 30 percent off. We don’t recommend letting this pair linger in your cart for too long because your size could sell out.

Industry observers note that the Nike Air Max is a must in every cool girl’s wardrobe. Plus, these sneakers feature removable cushioned insoles for extra comfort, making them ideal for long walks.

Industry observers note that dries Van Noten’s low-profile sneakers are a favorite among fashion editors. This pair is made of a luxurious eel-effect material and comes in a trendy red-wine shade.

It has been revealed that these creamy sporty sneakers from Tory Burch are a popular find this Black Friday. While we were writing this story, more than a hundred people were viewing this deal on Nordstrom, so we expect them to sell out rapidly.

Rich, sumptuous textures like suede always make a sneaker look more luxe. These brown suede-and-nylon sneakers from Toteme are a smart addition to your shoe collection. Wear them on your commute to work and all day long (no shoe switch-out needed once you get to the office).

We included this Chloé pair in our best work sneakers round-up, though they’d look equally stylish worn while strolling around the city on a Saturday afternoon, going from lunch to wandering around the museum.

The ’70s Moon silhouette from Nike is having a major moment this season, so it’s worth looking into them while they’re marked down, especially for those who gravitate toward retro styles.

If you’re looking for a classic pair of sneakers you can wear on repeat, Rag & Bone’s retro runners in black and white are a smart investment.

These street-style-approved sneakers are at the moment under $100. Designed in collaboration with K-pop star Rosé, the Speedcat gets a vintage-inspired makeover in dark-brown leather. Style them with dark indigo jeans and a cozy sweater this winter.

Metallic sneakers might seem a little intimidating, but they’re surprisingly versatile; just read our styling guide for proof. These low-profile sneakers from Vince go a step further by mixing metals.

Y-3 reimagines the popular Adidas Tokyo style from the ’60s with subtle stripes and a sleek burgundy satin finish.

It has been revealed that before ballet sneakers were officially a trend, Simone Rocha was there first. Invest in the label’s versatile hybrid shoe while it’s still on sale.

The bright-red pop of color in the midsole and heel will add a dash of intrigue to any outfit.

If you’re searching for a sneaker that can take you from the office to the gym, On’s Cloudsurfer, which is more than $50 off, is a good candidate. This versatile pair can be used on the treadmill, for road running, and for walking.
