Target has already run out of the deal, but Amazon still seems to have stock of the 41mm Pixel Watch 5 Black Friday deal.

Folks looking to pick up a Pixel Watch 5 this year will be happy to know that it just hit its lowest price ever on Black Friday, dropping to just $284.99 at Amazon, making it a whopping $115 off. That price is for the 41mm Bluetooth version in the Obsidian (black) colorway, which pairs directly with your phone and comes in a wonderfully compact size that looks good on any wrist.

Industry observers note that all other 41mm colorways are down to $299, which is still $100 off the regular price. Likewise, the larger 45mm model is down to $326, a $103 price drop that’ll make you feel good about buying.

All three retailers also have the Pixel Watch 5 LTE on sale for Black Friday. The watch is not only $100 off this Black Friday, but you also get 2 years of Google Fi data included for free when you buy it through Amazon, Best Buy, or Target. Google rates that at a $600 value, which is honestly mind-blowing to think about!

Industry observers note that that makes the LTE model the best deal this year since it comes with all that lovely cell data for free, making it infinitely more useful if you regularly find yourself leaving your phone behind. It’s a particularly great deal if you’re a runner, kayaker, or love any other type of sport that doesn’t play well with a phone in your pocket.
