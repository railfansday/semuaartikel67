📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There’s a fresh star shoe on the block, and it’s a little academia-core with a lot of fun. Yes, heeled loafers are the shoe fashion people are obsessing over, so you’re going to want to tap in early while you can.

It has been revealed that we saw the shoe making its way onto several runways this past season, from Louis Vuitton and Mulberry to Diesel and Chanel. For a while now, dark academia and studious fashion have been super popular. Regular loafers are the go-to for many, whether it be wearing them to actual school or work.

The style has caught the eye of many fashion insiders, including our senior fashion editor, Jessica Neises. “The first heeled loafer I fell for this season is by Sofia Testino, an emerging NYC-based designer whose debut included the most luscious, candy-colored patent wedge loafers,” Neises indicates. “Other downtown brands like Vaquera have also followed suit with this shape. There’s something a little studious but still unserious about them, and the wedge is the perfect middle ground for anyone who isn’t ready to commit to a full-blown heel but doesn’t love a flat loafer either, comfortable yet still fab.”.

Below, I’ve gathered some heeled loafer inspo so that you can figure out just how to style your pair.

Staying in the realm of academia, but upping the fashion, pairing your heeled loafers with a cinched blazer (and don’t forget the accessories) alongside a colorful patterned skirt is the surefire way into this trend.

Industry observers note that as a Floridian who struggles with layering during the colder months, a good dress will always take you far. And this style works great with a more fashion-forward heeled loafer.

According to recent reports, similar to throwing on a dress, a good matching set will get the job done every time. Pairing a sleek leather pair of heeled loafers with this sweater set is not only stylish but super comfortable.
