According to recent reports, the Witcher 3 Remastered continues to recieve tweaks ahead of the Songs of the Past expansion arriving next year to whisk Gerry from the River off on a fresh adventure. Following some bloomin' improvements in October, its latest patch does a bunch of skill and mutation balancing, alongside further technical tweaks and fixes for some funky bugs.

Sources indicate that "Fixed an issue where Reforging the Mastercrafted or Grandmaster Feline armor gave Geralt an unusual hairstyle," reads the first of numerous notes in the visual section of patch 5.02's notes about weird stuff happening to Gerry's luscious mane. "Fixed an issue where Geralt could become bald after enabling HairWorks on PC or Xbox Play Anywhere and then continuing the game on Xbox via Cross Progression," reads another that sounds slightly more serious. "Fixed an issue where growing a beard with the Rabble-rouser hairstyle made Geralt's head invisible," reads a third, which takes the cake.

According to recent reports, there've also been instances of Gerry or NPCs missing their faces or "other body parts". The NPCs, bless them, have also had to deal with contracting "overly bright red areas on their legs" when moving through crowds. Oh, and a building near Novigrad's docks has been cheeklily disappearing whenever Geralt gets close to it. One would assume it's just a bit shy, though that's a tough thing to be when your location means people embarking and disembarking from ships are constantly walking past. To be honest, it also sounds like it could have been adapted into an extra contract for Gerry if CD Projekt had fancied. The studio have also "fixed an glitch where installing and uninstalling mods could cause merchants' inventories, Geralt's inventory and storage to be emptied".

Recent findings suggest that beyond the giggle-worthy bug fixes, the most consequential aspect of the patch to my eye will likely be the tweaks to the power of certain skills and mutations, which folks may well be testing out if they're trying to settle on a fresh build before Songs of the Past. Sources indicate that generally speaking, sign-heavy Gerry builds look like they'll be most acutely affected and be less effective following this patch. I typically only rely on Quen for a bit of shielding, because I'm an unimaginative monster slayer, so I'm not sure I'll notice it too much, but if you really make the most of your magical abilities it might hit your Gerry pretty hard.

Sources indicate that it's worth skimming through the full notes if you've time, which also contain a few PC-specific fixes and tweaks. Starting a Hearts of Stone or Blood and Wine-only playthrough on Steam Deck should no longer lead to long load times, for example, while there've been some improvements made to path tracing and ray reconstruction .
