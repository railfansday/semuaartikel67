Sources indicate that bloomberg News reported on November 20, 2026, citing people familiar with the matter, that Cognition AI was discussing a new funding round at a $75 billion valuation and considering raising $2 billion or more, in an account carried by Reuters. The talks were ongoing and the details could change.

Recent findings suggest that the report described a potential financing rather than a completed investment. Cognition did not immediately respond to Reuters’ request for comment.

It has been revealed that cognition unveiled its Series E on September 8, 2026, raising more than $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation. Newly introduced investors Andreessen Horowitz and Accel led the round, joined by existing backers Founders Fund, General Catalyst and Avenir. The proposed figure is $27 billion higher than that completed valuation, calculated as the difference between the two stated valuations.

The earlier May 27, 2026 financing raised more than $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation and was led by Lux Capital, General Catalyst and 8VC. The completed May and September rounds provide reference points for the new report, while the latest terms remain under negotiation.

It has been revealed that reuters describes Cognition’s autonomous software-engineering agents as able to handle code planning, writing, testing and deployment with limited human intervention. Devin is Cognition’s product for this work.

Cognition’s own disclosures report run-rate revenue growth from $492 million in May to almost $900 million by September, alongside enterprise usage growth of more than tenfold between the start of 2026 and the May announcement. The revenue increase is roughly $400 million, calculated from the two reported run-rate figures.

Cognition states Devin is used by engineering teams at NVIDIA, GE Aerospace, Citi, Mercedes-Benz and Modal, spanning chip design, aviation, financial services, automotive and AI infrastructure. Devin Auto-Triage can handle an initial incident investigation, Devin Security Swarm identifies and prioritizes vulnerabilities, and Devin Automations can begin tasks in response to activity in Slack, GitHub, Linear and other systems without requiring a separate chat for each task.

The September announcement also noted Cognition had opened offices in Washington, D.C., Tokyo, Singapore, London, São Paulo and Madrid during the preceding year, alongside its existing San Francisco, Latest York and Austin hubs. The office expansion accompanied the manufacturer’s reported revenue growth and broader customer adoption.

It has been revealed that cognition also describes itself as an independent agent lab that can select and combine models for particular tasks, including its own, so customers are not confined to a single provider. That approach makes model choice part of Devin’s product design as the manufacturer expands into incident response, security and event-triggered automation.

Until a transaction is announced, the open question is whether the growth and broader Devin deployment documented in September can support the proposed terms, or whether the negotiations produce a different valuation or funding amount.
