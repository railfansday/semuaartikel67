Andrew Scott Claims He’s a ‘Bit of a Minimalist’ When It Comes to Interior Design at Home—and Reveals the Decor That ‘Freaks’ Him Out

Through the genre-spanning roles that have made him famous, Andrew Scott has proven that his skills are quite versatile. But when it comes to home design, he prefers to stay in a very specific lane.

During the Nov. 17 episode of comedian Amy Poehler’s podcast, “Good Hang,” the typically private Irish actor offered up some rare details on his home life.

Each week, the podcast opens with a brief chat with a secondary guest, who provides some additional context to the episode and gives the former “Saturday Night Live” star a question to ask during the interview with the marquee guest.

In this case, Scott’s co-star from both “Elsinore” and “Fleabag,” Olivia Colman, called in to suggest Poehler do some digging around his penchant for interior design.

Colman encouraged Poehler to ask Scott what kind of a career he would pursue if he wasn’t an actor, and guessed that he might begin discussing art or design. The Oscar-winning actress even said she’d love to see a world where Scott was an interior designer.

“I’d like to employ him to do up my house,” she stated.

“I wonder if he would show me pictures of his apartment” Poehler asked, which yielded a very excited response from Colman.

“He might not, because he’s quite private,” Colman teased, adding that the actor’s abode is “f-cking amazing.”.

When it came time for Poehler to ask the question, it appeared Colman was right on the money.

Sources indicate that scott revealed he “would for sure” be in an artistic field. “I would be drawing or painting. I like design…” he said.

According to recent reports, the “Spectre” actor was flattered to hear Colman’s compliments, as he considers her design acumen to be pretty impressive, too. Scott explained that the two have often spent their time on set discussing design, and at one point, joked that they’d love to host their own reality television show centered on the topic.

“We noted, ‘Wouldn’t it be great if we had a show together where we just slagged off other people?’” Scott laughed, explaining that the premise would be that he and Colman would circle a participant’s home and point out all of the design choices they don’t particularly agree with.

Poehler joked that the decor show could be called “I Would Not Have Done That.”.

So what does Scott like? “I love design. I’m a bit of a minimalist,” he revealed.

“I don’t like the accumulation of stuff,” Scott explained. Although he noted that he’s “not crazy” when it comes to the desire to purge unnecessary clutter, he does have a few preferences that help keep a space looking neater. For example, he generally steers clear of stainless steel, as it’s prone to revealing finger prints.

Poehler and Scott also bonded over their mutual love of clean surfaces, but noted that even an intentionally sparse decor scheme needs some detail.

In a recent development, “Too minimal? I don’t love,” mentioned Poehler, to which Scott agreed, “You’ve got to have some softness.”.

They were also on the same page with their similar philosophies on buying things, with Poehler noting that in an “ideal world,” she would do lots of research before purchasing anything to ensure it’s the very best option.

Scott also finds pleasure in having one really great item instead of simply acquiring things: “I’ve got a bottle opener that I love. You know what I’m saying?”.

It has been revealed that he went on to note that his family says he’s “quite hard to buy for for Christmas,” even though he always makes a big show of being excited no matter what kind of a gift he opens. He’s not one for unnecessarily kitschy pieces, and expressed a distaste for “things that sort of say ‘Prosecco o’clock’ on them.”.

“All those kinds of things freak me out,” he revealed.

In a May 2026 interview with The Gloss, Scott revealed that although when he “speaks of home,” he’s talking about Ireland, he’s spent a lot of time living between New York, London, and his home city of Dublin.

“One of the things that I have had to develop is how you can live in different places and think of them as home, too,” he shared.
