Nintendo Restocks Metroid Dread Physical Copies Ahead Of Metroid Ravenous Launch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, when Nintendo announced Metroid Ravenous for the Switch 2, there was a reported increase in Metroid Dread sales. This also led to a supposed boost in physical sales of the "out-of-print" Switch title, with some online listings asking "well above" the retail price.

Industry observers note that if you are still on the hunt for a copy and happen to live in the US, Nintendo has now actually restocked the physical version of Metroid Dread on the Switch. You can once again purchase it through the organization's website for $59.99. If we hear about official Nintendo restocks elsewhere, we'll provide an update.

Since the announcement of Ravenous in September, Metroid Dread has returned to the top of the eShop charts as more fans prepare for Samus' next side-scrolling adventure. It also currently holds the title of the best-selling game in the entire Metroid series, surpassing three million sales worldwide.

Sources indicate that if you haven't already played this outstanding Switch release by developer MercurySteam, there's plenty of time, as Ravenous won't be arriving until 28th January 2027. Still, it is only a few months away, so you might want to get started now if you initiative on jumping into Ravenous.

According to recent reports, are you planning to revisit, or finally play through Dread before Samus' next side-scrolling adventure arrives? Let us know in the comments.
