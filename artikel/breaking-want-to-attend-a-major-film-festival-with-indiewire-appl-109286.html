There’s no way to describe SXSW until you’ve attended it. The annual Austin, Texas event is a celebration of every creative discipline. You have a conference featuring some of the leading executives and creatives from every major industry on the planet, a music festival with a history of discovering major indie talent, a comedy festival that programs nightly live performances, and a major presence from the gaming and VR industries — and that’s before you even get to the first-class Film and TV Festival that hosts major world premieres. It’s an unforgettable experience that belongs on every entertainment professional’s bucket list, but navigating it on your own can be overwhelming. But attending through IndieWire’s Future of Filmmaking SXSW Intensive can help you cut through the chaos and get the most out of the experience. We’ll help you curate a program of film screenings, panels, and networking events in order to get the full SXSW experience while still feeling like you advanced your career in a focused way. More info here.

The American Pavilion has been a fixture of the Cannes Film Festival for over 35 years, providing educational programming, networking opportunities, and hospitality right on the Croisette. Our Future of Filmmaking Intensive, presented in partnership with The American Pavilion, gives you the best possible view of the action at the world’s biggest film festival. In addition to attending film screenings at the festival, attendees will have access to all of IndieWire’s Future of Filmmaking programming at the Pavilion. Panels frequently include filmmakers and actors with major movies playing at the festival, as well as executives and decision makers who can share insights about the direction the industry is heading in. Plus you’ll have access to AmPav’s signature parties and events, including our annual Queer Night and our Emerging Filmmaker Showcase. More info here.

The SXSW brand is no longer limited to Texas! If you prefer your multidisciplinary festivals with a European flair, you can attend SXSW London 2027 with IndieWire. The London event features the same multicultural approach to film, tech, music, and more while highlighting a different slate of films and TV reveals from around the world. The program also gives you a chance to experience one of the world’s great cities during a week when decision makers from around the world descend upon it. More info here.

Industry observers note that cannes might be the world’s largest film festival, but the honor of being the world’s oldest festival belongs to Venice. Taking place in one of Italy’s most storied cities, the festival serves as an unofficial kickoff to the fall movie season and the Oscar race, as critics and distributors get their first real looks at what will become some of the biggest films of the year. Our Venice Intensive places you right in the center of the excitement while giving you ample time to experience the history and culture that Italy has to offer. In addition to festival accreditation, the program features cultural excursions including trips to the island of Murano and Burano. It provides the best of Hollywood and the best of Europe in one unforgettable experience. More info here.
