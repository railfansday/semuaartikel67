📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

XCOM and Midnight Suns studio Firaxis Games has lost roughly one-third of its workforce in a round of layoffs today.

Around 60 people lost their jobs, and studio head Heather Hazen is also leaving the developer.

Sources indicate that in a statement, a spokesperson mentioned the team is "restructuring and recentering" around "designing world class strategy games". As a result, Firaxis is being downsized to focus on "design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.".

Quite how cutting one-third of your overall staff only now means you can refocus on making the best strategy games in the business makes little sense to me.

Apparently, the developer does actually have a third XCOM game in the works, but it's been hit with development complications. The Bloomberg report from Jason Schreier stated Firaxis Games missed a "significant milestone over the summer".

Development of the game continues, but it may be affected by today's layoffs.
