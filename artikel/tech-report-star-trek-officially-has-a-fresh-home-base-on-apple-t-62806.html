The Apple TV Store currently looks less like a digital storefront and more like Starfleet Command, thanks to the ongoing discounted sale on the store. One particular franchise seems to be taking an exceptional lead during the sale, which proves that Trekkers are still solid even 6 decades later.

According to FlixPatrol's November 18 U.S. rankings, six of the platform's 10 most-popular television titles belong to Star Trek, while a seventh has cracked the Top 20. The sale went live around November 16 and includes titles like even Game of Thrones, Friends, The Sopranos, Supernatural, and The Big Bang Theory, among others. Most of these titles are cult favorites, but Star Trek still seems to have had a very strong breakthrough, which is what makes the dominance so impressive.

The whole Apple TV Store covers a large slice of Star Trek. For instance, The Original Series is $20 at the time of writing, instead of the original $60 price tag. The Animated Series is at $10 instead of $30. The Next Generation is at $40 instead of $100, while Star Trek: Enterprise, Star Trek: Picard, and Star Trek: Lower Decks are at $20 from their original $50 prices. There’s also Prodigy, which is at $15 (at a 50% discount) right now. In contrast, all eight seasons of Game of Thrones are currently available at $60 (original price $100), down 40% from its original price and still not making it to the Top 20 on Apple TV Store.

Sources indicate that paramount spent this year running a major anniversary campaign around six decades of the franchise, which now encompasses 11 television series, 14 files and more than 900 episodes. There were anniversary screenings, worldwide events, a Guinness World Records initiative around the Vulcan salute, merchandising collaborations and other celebrations throughout 2026. That whole advertising grind from Paramount’s end seems to have beautifully allowed the Apple TV Store to lock in on that traction.

Other than the ones already mentioned, there’s Star Trek: Deep Space Nine available on Apple TV Store for $35, but its shallower discount may help explain its lower #16 placement. Strange Latest Worlds is still an ongoing series sold season-by-season, which explains why it’s not on the list.
