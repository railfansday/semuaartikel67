The Robins may have won just twice in their past 10 Championship matches – with head coach Michael Skubala starting to come under growing scrutiny – but the sight of the Teessiders seems to bring out the best in the West Country outfit.

The red side of Bristol have won the last five of their last six league fixtures against Boro while drawing the other.

All told they have triumphed in 16 out of 28 league meetings in the 21st century, losing just six.

Kim Hellberg suffered his first defeat as Boro chief against City – who renew acquaintances with former loan goalkeeper and player-of-the-season Radek Vitek – last December in a 2-0 pre-Christmas defeat at Ashton Gate.

The reverse fixture on Teesside in March, a 1-1 draw, was far more damaging in the final analysis for Boro, who conceded a painful and needless stoppage-time equaliser at a critical juncture of the run-in when their automatic promotion credentials were placed into jeopardy.

In all competitions, the Robins have lost just once in 15 visits to the Riverside Stadium, winning on eight occasions.

Hellberg, whose high-flying side head into the game on the back of no wins – and no goals – in their past two games against Watford and Wrexham, said: "I know that sometimes things happen in football when you face a team you haven't beaten for a long time.

"Things like that happen; it's difficult to describe why. I think last season, we played some very good games against them and created a lot of opportunities and thought we maybe should have won both of them and came away with one point at the end.

Sources indicate that "We are trying to prepare for a team who have been very strong away from home and struggling at home. But they have been one of the best sides away from home and picking up points.

"It will be a tough game like always and hopefully we can win.".

After their international exertions with USA and Australia, midfielders Sebastian Berhalter and Riley McGree only returned to full training on Friday, when Jeremy Sarmiento was also due back following his commitments with Ecuador.
