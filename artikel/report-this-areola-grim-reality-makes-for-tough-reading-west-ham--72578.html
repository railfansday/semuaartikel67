In a recent development, west Ham United supporters have grown more accustomed to seeing second choice goal keeper Alphonse Areola dressed up for DJ’ing than dressed for action on the pitch this season. The French shot stopper is unlikely to be fit for todays’ clash against bottom side Preston North End despite the club’s best endeavours to fast track him back to fitness, according to Scott Parker’s press conference exposé.

In a recent development, which is a little galling, to say the least. That is the whole point of having a second choice ‘keeper. He should be ready when you need him. Areola it would appear had grown so disillusioned under Nuno that he’d not expected to be called upon any time soon.

Hermansen’s crisis of confidence means Areola is urgently required but not ready to answer the call.

And in a latest report today the grim reading for West Ham is that their second choice keeper, by far the highest paid in the division (even after the famous relegation clause has reduced his earnings to a modest £3,120,000 a year, ) now ranks virtually bottom in the valuation stakes of Championship keepers as produced by EFL specialists footballleagueworld.co.uk:.

“Top 20 most valuable EFL Championship keepers: Number 17: Alphonse Areola £2.4 million”.

The news that Areola’s salary is higher than his value is nothing latest: The most galling part is that he isn’t ready at a moments notice to don the gloves and answer the call from his new manager.

You or I would sleep in our kit in a state of permanent readiness for even ten per cent of Areola’s pay.

Sources indicate that nothing speaks to the state of this club under it’s former manager more than the amounts forked out on players who were frozen out or wanted out: Todibo, Wan Bissaka, Fullkrug, Alvarez, Magassa, Ward Prowse, Areola.. all in their time left to fester after falling out with Nuno. Any wonder we are in the sticky stuff right now?
