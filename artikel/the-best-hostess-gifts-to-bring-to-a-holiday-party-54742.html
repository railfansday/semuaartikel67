It has been revealed that it’s an established social etiquette rule that showing up to a party empty-handed is a bit of a faux pas. Of course, there are more intimate gatherings where a simple bottle of wine or an extra cheese for the board will suffice, but the holiday season runs on a different track. Homes are dressed to the nines, menus are calibrated to merrier flavors, and the stakes somehow feel higher. Arriving with a gift for the hostess not only signals your appreciation, but also adds to the evening’s celebratory air. But what to bring?

Recent findings suggest that there’s zero shame in going the traditional route—no one is going to turn their nose up at a Le Labo candle, after all. If your host is a hardcore holiday enthusiast, why not bring a handcrafted ornament to dress their tree with? Or a fresh stack of illustrated Christmas cards? Something pretty for the pantry will always go to great use, and if you have a strong sense of their taste in interiors, a sleek vase or decorative tray will surely make your host feel extra special.
