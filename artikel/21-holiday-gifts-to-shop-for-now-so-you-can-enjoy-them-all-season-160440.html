From custom wrapping paper to cozy matching PJs, these are the gifts worth grabbing before the December scramble.

If there’s one thing that’s wildly helpful to keep in mind heading into this final stretch of the year, it’s that the holidays aren’t just one day.

Right now, we’re in the warm-up period, getting ready for what’s ultimately a months-long marathon of hosting, gift-wrapping, card-writing and cozy holiday cheer. So while, sure, there may be bags of half-eaten Halloween candy presently littering your countertops, it’s time to start thinking about the stuff you need to do (and buy) now to max out the holiday merriment and minimize the holiday stress.

What most of us have learned the hard way, of course, is that personalized gifts need a little extra time to make and ship. Placing those orders now gives you the head start you need! But also, why save all the good stuff for December anyway? We here at Scary Mommy are significant believers in gifting early, both to ourselves and the people we love, especially when it’s stuff that’ll get used all season long, like matching PJs for every hot cocoa night and puzzles to work on as the weather turns.

We’ve rounded up what we’re buying now so that the holidays can be more about the fun stuff and less about the December scramble.
