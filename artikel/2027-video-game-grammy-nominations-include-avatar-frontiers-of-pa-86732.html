📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that since 2023, the Recording Academy has included an official category for video game soundtracks, adding an air of legitimacy to a diverse musical medium we know and love. Today, we learned the list of nominees for the 2027 Grammy ceremony, and it's an interesting lineup. The nominations are as follows:.

As is often the case, what stands out most about these nominees is how, generally speaking, they don't fully align with gaming culture's favorite soundtracks of the year, and instead often trend toward the most recognizable IP (often franchises with films) of the year. On that note, it's most surprising to see that Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora has been nominated for the third year in a row: once for the base game, once for the Secrets of the Spires DLC, and now for the From The Ashes DLC. None of these soundtracks are bad by any means, but their Grammy representation far outweighs their cultural footprint.

Recent findings suggest that the standout here is Ghost of Yōtei, which is not an adaptation of the massive franchises the other games fall into. However, it's also a Sony-published game, which have a pretty good track record of getting nominations, potentially due to the tech giant's massive presence in the music and film industries. Still, Ghost of Yōtei has a great, unique score that's deserving of its spot, and also one previously nominated for best music at the 2025 Game Awards. The Grammys accept any releases between October 1 of one year and September 30 of the following year, so Ghost of Yōtei barely made the cut for this window. The Game Awards nominees will be announced tomorrow – it will be interesting to see how much overlap there is between the categories.

Recent findings suggest that this batch of Grammy noms also has fewer recurring nominees than past years. This is Gordy Haab's third nomination (he won in 2024 for Star Wars Jedi: Survivor) and John Paesano's second nomination (he was nominated in 2025 for Marvel's Spider-Man 2). It's also the first year since the awards' creation without Bear McCreary or Austin Wintory nominations.

Sources indicate that last year's nominations left a sour taste in the mouths of many, with several DLC nominees and a complete lack of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, the year's standout hit that many specifically praised for its music. This year certainly has snubs (Mewgenics, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and Control Resonant all come to mind), but none as egregious as last year.
