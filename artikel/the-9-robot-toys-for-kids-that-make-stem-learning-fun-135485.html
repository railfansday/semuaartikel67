STEM-based schoolwork is a key part of your kid’s curriculum, but encouraging genuine enthusiasm for science, technology, engineering, and math that extends beyond the classroom requires more than a good teacher. For the right kid at the right age, a robotics kit may be a perfect fit. It can give a budding inventor the chance to learn about science in a practical way, on their own terms, and allows them to get creative. It also shows that science can be fun, and more than just a subject at school.

According to recent reports, A good robotics kit balances out learning and problem solving with play. Not all of them do that effectively, though: Choosing the right one for the child in your life may not be so straightforward—particularly if you’re unfamiliar with the basics that robotics kits teach. To help you find a STEM gift this holiday season that's both fun and educational, we’ve compiled a lineup of editor-approved robotics kits. Whether you're getting ahead of shopping season or looking for a great last-minute holiday gift, these toys are both fun and give your child's mind a chance to grow.

During our research, we drew from our collective experience covering STEM toys and equipment for kids, including topics like the best microscopes and the best graphing calculators. We also read through studies on the importance of STEM education.

To make our selections, we looked for kits from reputable companies within the robotics space, such as Learning Resources and Wonder Workshop. We also took a look at toys and items that parents on the staff have bought for their own kids, making special note of any that ended up as household favorites.

While evaluating each kit, we gauged value for the price (especially because some kits cost upwards of $100), the STEM topics it covers, as well as how intuitive and (just as importantly) fun it is to use, according to parents’ reviews. Furthermore, we considered a wide variety of toys to account for different kids’ interests, ages, and skill levels.

Studies show that in addition to STEM literacy, robotics can help develop core skills like hands-on creativity, problem solving, communication, and teamwork. So teaching robotics isn’t just about setting up your kid to have a career as a computer programmer or engineer—it equips them with tech know-how and critical thinking they’ll need for virtually any type of education or work.

Industry observers note that choose toys that allow your kid to use their imagination and play out multiple different situations, both real and made up. Furthermore, make sure even if you want the toy to help them learn STEM skills, it still fits their interests and passions. A toy that gets played with often will help them learn a lot more than a more educational one that collects dust.

While you may think robotics clubs are only for middle and high schoolers, research has shown that children are also able to master basic robotics and programming skills when they’re introduced in pre-K to second grade.

Many of these kits are appropriate for children as young as 3 years old, while some are more advanced with an age suggestion of 8 and up. Before you buy, check the recommended age range listed by the company or on the box to make sure you’re choosing a robotics kit that challenges your kid while still being accessible to them.

You don’t have to shell out hundreds of dollars right away on a robot, especially if your kids are still learning what they like. There are plenty of robotics kits that run under $50 if you aren’t sure your kids are going to enjoy this kind of play and learning. Pricier options over $100 offer more complexity and can often grow with the kids, since they’re customizable to continue challenging them as they get older and gain experience.
