Luca Williams-Barnett starts for Tottenham against Ipswich today.

Destiny Udogie, Savio, James Maddison and Lucas Bergvall were all pictured training at Hotspur Way this week and are now in contention for minutes on Saturday.

Udogie was left out of Roberto De Zerbi’s squad as Spurs lost 1-0 to Leeds before the international break, though the Italian left-back took to social media to suggest that he was not injured.

De Zerbi had previously mentioned that Udogie’s injuries were becoming “problematic”, with the 23-year-old starting just two league games this season.

Savio, meanwhile, is in line to return to action for the first time since October’s 1-1 draw with Manchester United. The Brazilian winger has missed Tottenham’s last four league games with a muscular concern.

Spurs sorely lacked a creative threat against Leeds. But despite naming Maddison on the bench at Elland Road, De Zerbi didn't bring him on in search of a goal, raising concerns about his fitness.

Maddison has struggled to train consistently since the start of the season and came off midway through Spurs’ 2-0 win over Chelsea with a suspected groin problem.

The 29-year-old has not featured since that historic victory at Stamford Bridge, despite being available for selection. Still, it is hoped that during the international break he has had time to work on building his fitness.

Bergvall is also expected to be in contention for minutes against Ipswich, having returned from a knee injury.

The Swedish midfielder was not called up for international duty by Graham Potter, but could make his first appearance since the start of October after returning to Spurs training.

Mathys Tel is expected to be out for up to three weeks with an Achilles injury sustained following a heavy tackle from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle.

It is also unclear whether reserve goalkeeper Martin Dubravka has recovered from a knee injury, while Tosin Adarabioyo remains out with a calf matter.

Long-term absentee Xavi Simons is ahead of schedule in his recovery from an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury, but remains some time away from a return to match action.

Wilson Odobert is also out with an ACL injury, while Chelsea loanee Mykhailo Mudryk is still missing with an ankle ligament injury that has kept him out since September.
