Lorenzo Bettoni is joined by Empire of the Kop editor Farrell Keeling to discuss the latest developments involving two Italy internationals with strong Liverpool connections: Giovanni Leoni and Federico Chiesa.

The conversation provides an revision on Leoni’s recovery and expected return to action, while also assessing Chiesa’s situation at Anfield and what the future may hold for the former Juventus and Fiorentina star.

Sources indicate that the discussion then turns to one of the most intriguing transfer stories involving Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk. Italy World Cup winner Fabio Cannavaro recently suggested that the Dutch defender is tempted by a move to Serie A before the end of his career, sparking fresh speculation about his long-term future.

Farrell offers insight into how Van Dijk is viewed at Liverpool, whether any links to Italy have substance, and what the defender’s next steps could look like.

From Liverpool’s plans for Chiesa and the progress of highly rated young defender Leoni to the possibility of seeing Van Dijk in Serie A one day, Lorenzo and Farrell break down the latest talking points surrounding three players sparking debate across Europe.
