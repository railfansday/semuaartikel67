Shares of ASML (ASML +9.28%) were among the winners today as the semiconductor sector surged on news that Jensen Huang was returning as the CEO of Nvidia.

The announcement reinjected confidence in the AI boom, lifting sector stocks like ASML, which finished the session up 9.3, while the Nasdaq Composite closed up 6.5%.

ASML is the world's only maker of extreme ultraviolet lithography (EUV) machines for making the world's most advanced chips.

These are highly complex, very expensive machines, and the firm sells only dozens of them each quarter.

According to recent reports, it's a cyclical business downstream of broader chip demand, including more advanced chips used for AI. Therefore, the strengthening of the AI ecosystem is good news for ASML, and investors believe AI demand and its potential growth are stronger with Jensen Huang back running Nvidia.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

According to recent reports, because of the nature of its business, ASML's results can swing quarter to quarter, so the long-term outlook is crucial for the stock. It already stands to benefit from the shortage in memory as orders from memory chipmakers look to expand capacity, and it should benefit from the continued growth of AI demand, which is supported by having Huang back as the Nvidia CEO.

Sources indicate that ASML is targeting revenue of 44 billion-60 billion euros for 2030. While the organization won't benefit directly from the return of Huang, investors should look for that figure to move higher as a sign that ASML is benefiting from tailwinds from AI growth.
