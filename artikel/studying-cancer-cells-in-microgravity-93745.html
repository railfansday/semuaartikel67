According to recent reports, this Nov. 19, 2026, image shows gastric microtumors grown within an Encapsulates biochip. The Encapsulate-1 investigation studies patient-derived tumor samples aboard the International Space Station to better understand how cancer cells move and interact in microgravity. The results could help researchers develop further testing to predict a patient’s risk of metastasis and support more personalized cancer treatments.
