Latest Update: Marc Levinson on How UPS Created Modern Logistics

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (160 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, UPS was founded nearly a century ago in the basement of a Seattle saloon by James E. Casey and his business partner Claude Ryan. It is now hard to imagine modern, globalized life, and the logistics required to prop it up, without the innovations Casey and his partners at UPS cooked up during the tech giant's early decades. Marc Levinson has just written a biography of Casey and the company he founded — UPS Man: James E. Casey and the Creation of Modern Logistics — which was a lot harder to do than you might imagine, because Casey shunned the spotlight and his contributions were virtually unknown outside of UPS. In today's episode, Levinson tells us why UPS is so secretive about its history, how Casey and his partners helped figure out the last mile problem and popularized parcel delivery, and how scientific management theories influenced everything the company did. (Source: Bloomberg).
