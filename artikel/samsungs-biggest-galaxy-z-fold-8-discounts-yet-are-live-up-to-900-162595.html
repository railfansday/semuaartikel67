Samsung’s hit Galaxy Z Fold 8 saved its best deal for Black Friday, with a new low price on Amazon and a targeted Samsung.com offer that drops the price by up to $900 – here’s what you should know.

The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is usually available starting at $1,899, but discounts have been available to cut that down around Black Friday.

It has been revealed that for everyone, Samsung’s Black Friday discount for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is up to $450 off. That cuts the 512GB model down to $1649, while the base 256GB tier drops to $1,499. That discount is available through both Samsung.com and Amazon.

According to recent reports, for some customers, Samsung.com has a much bigger discount available. $500 off of the 512GB model, and then $900 off of the 1TB version of the Galaxy Z Fold 8 through a doubled-storage discount specifically on the “Lavender” color variant. It’s a huge discount, but it’s not showing for everyone. Samsung.com silently targets offers like this at certain users (logged in or not), so it’s essentially luck of the draw on whether or not you’ll see it. If you’re in the market, though, this is easily the best Fold 8 deal you’ll find. Let us know in the comments if you’re seeing this discount.
