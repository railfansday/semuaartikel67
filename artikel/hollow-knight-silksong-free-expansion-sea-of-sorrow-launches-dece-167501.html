Hollow Knight: Silksong free expansion “Sea of Sorrow” will launch on December 11, developer Team Cherry revealed.

“In ‘Sea of Sorrow,’ players will voyage above and beneath Pharloom’s forgotten ocean, across islands, through forts and into deep caverns beneath the waves,” Team Cherry said. “We’ve spent the whole year stuffing this adventure full with all the things you’d expect from Hollow Knight: Silksong—crazed characters, high adventure, and epic bosses! It’s also overflowing with more exceptional music by the eternally talented Christopher Larkin! We’re pretty darn excited to get it into all of your hands on December 11, so please look forward to it.”.

Hollow Knight: Silksong is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam, GOG, Humble Store, and Microsoft Store. It is also available via Game Pass.
