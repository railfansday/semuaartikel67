Fans of clubs can often play down the importance of games, or brand something as "their cup final, not ours," when discussing a matchup between two new rivals, but deep down, both Southampton and Middlesbrough fans want to get one over each other on Friday night.

Peart-Harris, who took his chance back in the starting eleven for the first time in two months on Tuesday night with a goal and an assist in the 2-1 win over Swansea City, watched on from home as Spygate played out back in May, given he wasn't at the club.

However, the 24-year-old knows how much the game means on Friday, and is looking forward to being a part of it.

"Everyone was watching it [the spygate developments]," he said, per the Northern Echo. "I remember watching the semi-final, and then I think maybe a week later you hear the news about what happened. So now, it's definitely something I’m excited to be a part of.

"Friday's a massive game. It’s a massive football game before anything else, but the rivalry that we now have makes it even better.".

"Our aim will be to go down and just get a win.".

As Peart-Harris said, first and foremost, though, Friday night will be a great chance for two of the best sides in the division to go head-to-head.

Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert are widely regarded as two of the brightest up-and-coming coaches in the game, and in the past year since they both took over at their respective clubs, the brand of football being played has been fascinating to watch.
