Recent findings suggest that british Scattered Spider member Tyler Robert Buchanan has just been sentenced to six years in prison, according to the DOJ. Buchanan, who is 24 years old, pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft.

The five Scattered Spider hackers took part in a massive phishing campaign which targeted employees of major technology companies, telecommunications companies, and entertainment companies between September 2021 and April 2023.
