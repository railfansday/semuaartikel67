Pistons overcome highlight-reel Victor Wembanyama to stun Spurs, snap San Antonio's 12-game win streak

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Victor Wembanyama made highlights, and the San Antonio Spurs took early control in Tuesday's showdown with the Detroit Pistons.

According to recent reports, but when Wembanyama left the floor, the Pistons feasted. And they rallied from a 33-24 first-quarter hole behind a strong defensive effort for a 105-93 statement win over the Spurs in San Antonio.

According to recent reports, the win snapped a 12-game win streak for the now 12-3 Spurs, who hadn't previously lost since dropping their first two games of the season. And a Pistons team that flamed out in the playoffs after posting the best record in the East last season now has a signature win of the new campaign to improve to 9-4.

Cade Cunningham has been among the NBA's hottest players with 52 and 33 points in consecutive games before Tuesday. He led the way again Tuesday night with 27 points, 5 rebounds and 6 assists.

While his teammates repeatedly attacked the rim when Wembanyama was on the bench, Cunningham took it right at the Spurs MVP candidate with a highlight-reel attack and dunk to end the first quarter.

Wembanyama went to the bench in the second quarter with the Spurs holding a 42-34 lead. When he returned, the Pistons had cut their deficit to 47-46 as shots at the bucket went repeatedly unchallenged.

Industry observers note that the Pistons then took control with a 23-18 third quarter advantage as San Antonio's shooting went cold. The Spurs played from behind for the entirety of the fourth quarter and weren't able to retake the lead.

Cade Cunningham's first-quarter dunk over Victor Wembanyama set the tone for the Pistons to control the rest of the game.

Recent findings suggest that when it was done, a Pistons team playing without injured center Jalen Duren (hamstring) won the battle inside with a 60-44 advantage in points in the paint. Ausar Thompson was a force all over the court with 14 points, 12 assists, 9 rebounds and 5 steals while shooting 7 of 13 from the floor.

Recent findings suggest that daniss Jenkins provided a boost from the perimeter with 25 points while shooting 3 of 7 from 3. The Pistons shot 49.5% from the field while holding the Spurs to a 41.6% clip and a 9-of-40 (22.5%) effort from 3.

Wembanyama was a force inside and put on a show with a pair of first-quarter dunks that came off passes from himself off the backboard.

He finished the game with 23 points and 18 rebounds. But he struggled more than any of his teammates from long range with a 1-of-8 effort from 3.

Sources indicate that dylan Harper added 20 points, 4 rebounds and 2 assists on 8-of-14 shooting from the field. But he missed all four of his 3s and left late with what appeared to be a groin injury.

With the game in the final minute, Harper slipped and did a split as he tried to plant his right foot. He remained down on the ground in pain and clutched his groin as the Pistons played 5-on-4 on the other side of the court.

The precise nature and severity of his injury wasn't initially clear, but he needed help getting up before limping to the locker room on his own before the game's conclusion.

According to recent reports, harper's thrived since joining San Antonio's starting lineup in his second NBA season with averages of 18.3 points, 5.5 rebounds and 4.7 assists per game. An extended absence would be a difficult blow for the Spurs on top of Tuesday's loss.

It has been revealed that the Spurs are left to regroup from a thorough defeat on their home court and a potentially significant injury that abruptly snapped the high of a 12-game win streak. They have upcoming games against the Clippers, Pelicans and Kings before a difficult three-game stretch against the Trail Blazers, Lakers and Celtics.

The Pistons will remain on the road Wednesday against the Pelicans before facing the Raptors and Hawks over the weekend.
