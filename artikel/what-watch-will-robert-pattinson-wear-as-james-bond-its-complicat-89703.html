Sources indicate that perhaps you've heard? There's a new James Bond in town!

Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next 007, and while most of the speculation has understandably focused on how the former vampire/current Batman might approach Britain's foremost secret agent, watch aficionados are asking another question: What will he wear on his wrist?

According to recent reports, watches, after all, are almost as much a part of Bond's wardrobe as his dinner jackets, Aston Martins, and vodka martinis. Sean Connery famously wore a Rolex Submariner, Roger Moore experimented with Seiko's digital watches, and Omega has been 007's watch of choice since Pierce Brosnan strapped on a trusty Seamaster in GoldenEye in 1995. That brand remains, of course, a paid partner of the franchise.

Yesterday's reveal revealed no watch on the newly anointed 007's wrist, and Pattinson's own taste in watches is rather different from the rugged, sporty timepieces traditionally associated with Bond.

In fact, he doesn't seem particularly keen on massive watches at all.

"Whenever a watch gets too thick and it keeps your cuff up, I don't really understand the appeal," he told one interviewer as recently September. "A lot of guys really like that, but I don't think I can really pull that off. I need to only have a half reveal.".

And when asked what he looks for in a watch, he was even more specific.

Recent findings suggest that "I definitely like really thin watches. That's the main thing. In general, I would probably prefer a leather strap.".

Which is quite a contrast with the substantial divers we've become accustomed to seeing on Bond's wrist.

It has been revealed that the fact is, until recently, Pattinson wasn't much of a watch enthusiast at all. That changed at this year's Oscars, when he wore a then-unreleased rose-gold Jaeger-LeCoultre Master Control Chronomètre Perpetual Calendar. Other actors apparently began asking him about it at the afterparty, and something clicked.

Industry observers note that he subsequently became an ambassador for Jaeger-LeCoultre, although his relationship with the brand goes back much further. As a teenager, he borrowed his mother's Reverso to impress his schoolmates. More recently, he's revealed that Bruce Wayne will wear a Reverso in the next Batman film.

On screen, Pattinson's watch choices have been interesting. He wore a Chanel J12 in David Cronenberg's Cosmopolis, while Christopher Nolan's Tenet, in which he played a mysterious secret agent, featured a Bond-adjacent Omega De Ville.

Meanwhile, the announcement has prompted some entertaining activity on Instagram. Within hours of Pattinson being named Bond, Jaeger-LeCoultre posted pictures of its upcoming ambassador wearing the Master Control Chronomètre Perpetual Calendar. Omega, meanwhile, posted an image of its James Bond Seamaster. And today, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been back with more Pattinson pictures.

It's a little social-media tit-for-tat, although there's no reason the two relationships can't coexist. For now, at least, there's a straightforward distinction: Pattinson can wear whatever he likes. Bond has his own watch.
