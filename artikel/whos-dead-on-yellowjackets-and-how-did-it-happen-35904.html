Whos dead on Yellowjackets? (And how did it happen?)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Buzz buzz, Yellowjackets hive! Season 4 is upon us, and we're ready to be dragged back into the creepy woods of teen chaos and middle-aged mayhem. The Season 3 finale offered some major moments, including the revelation of the Pit Girl's identity and Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) sending out a distress call to the civilized world.

Trailers for Season 4 have teased the titular soccer team's return home. However, if the first three seasons have taught us anything, it's that you can take the girl out of the wilderness, but you can't take the wilderness out of the girl. In both the past and present timelines, Shauna (Sophie Nélisse/Melanie Lynskey), Misty (Samantha Hanratty/Christina Ricci), and Taissa (Jasmin Savoy Brown/Tawny Cypress) are grappling for survival and control, so buckle up for a wild ride.

But hey, if the off-season has you feeling foggy on which teammates (and rivals) have bit the dust (or been bit up), we've got you covered with a recap of all the deaths in Seasons 1, 2, and 3.

And keep an eye on this space — the final season of Yellowjackets is sure to see more folks fall, and we'll be updating this list accordingly.

It has been revealed that also, wherever possible, we have images with sliders of before and after. Slide 'em if you can stomach it.

Having just moved up from the junior varsity team, Rachel has the grim distinction of being the first Yellowjacket to die. Like the plane's pilots, she died in the crash, from a laceration to her throat.

In a recent development, father of Travis and Javi, Coach Bill (Carlos Sanz) died as a result of the plane crash that dropped the team in the Canadian wilderness. He'd fallen out of the plane into a tree. In an added bit of brutality, he died in front of Travis, as the teen boy tried to save him from his painful perch in a tree.

It has been revealed that good Christian girl Laura Lee (Jane Widdop) died a hero. After the team found an intact plane in the wilderness, Laura Lee began reading the manual to figure out how to fly it. She eventually took off with her teddy bear Leonard — and her personal lord and savior Jesus — as her co-pilots. But the plane exploded in the air, killing Laura and the Yellowjackets' chance of sending out a member for help.

Industry observers note that the queen bee who couldn't hack it in the cabin, Jackie (Ella Purnell) froze to death while sleeping outside. And then she had the bizarre honor of becoming the first to be eaten by the team.

Travis's younger brother Javi (Luciano Leroux) would also be eaten, though the Yellowjackets weren't directly to blame for his death. While the team was hunting Natalie, Javi offered her a hiding place. But before they could get to safety, he fell through the ice of a frozen lake and died. From there, he was eaten.

Industry observers note that despite the best efforts of the team, Shauna's baby was stillborn. This was a sad one.

Sources indicate that remember when Misty made a friend? She and Kristen (who was dubbed Crystal the Pistol by the team) bonded in the wilderness over their shared love of music and Steel Magnolias. They grew close enough that Misty shared her secret about destroying the plane's emergency transmitter, which Kristen — understandably — judged. From there, Misty pushed her off a cliff, and proclaimed the girl "missing.".

Real talk: Ben (Steven Krueger) lived way longer than we expected. The plane crash severely damaged his leg, which Misty then amputated on the spot. And incredibly, he survived possible infection, likely starvation, and the burning down of the cabin. But he couldn't escape the Yellowjackets. In a kangaroo court, they convicted him for the cabin's fiery annihilation (despite Misty's best efforts of a defense), and Taissa was chosen to carry out the execution. However, this was not how Ben died. Instead, he was tortured with crippling violence and deplorable conditions — that is, until Natalie took mercy on him and stabbed him fatally in the heart.

Poor Edwin (Nelson Franklin) just wanted to study frogs with his colleague Hannah (Ashley Sutton) and their guide Kodiak (Joel McHale). But then he stumbled in the Yellowjackets camp as they were feasting on Ben's remains. And, well, Lottie welcomed him with an axe to the head. RIP Edwin.

After Edwin was killed, the Yellowjackets imprisoned his surviving friends like they did Ben. When Kodiak tried to escape, he wasn't ended by the girls but by Hannah, who stabbed him in the eye. This was Hannah's bid to show the girls she could be trusted to keep their secret. And it got her through the rest of Season 3 at least.

Yellowjackets began — to quote Sarah McLachlan — building a mystery by introducing the show with the death of the Pit Girl. Underdressed for the snowy weather, she raced through the woods, pursued by masked figures, and ultimately fell into a pit of spikes, which killed her. Season 3 revealed her identity: Mari Ibarra (Alexa Barajas), a persistent rival of Shauna's. Mari too was eaten.

How Travis (Andres Soto) died was a big mystery in Season 1, with a real bummer of a reveal. In the present, accidentally hanged himself while attempting to talk to the darkness. Lottie was supposed to save him but had a technical malfunction.

Sources indicate that in Season 1, Adam Martin (Peter Gadiot) was a mechanic who was having an affair with Shauna. But when Shauna began to suspect he had betrayed her, she stabbed him to death. His murder was covered up with the help of Misty, Nat, Taissa, and Jeff, because Yellowjackets are ride or die. (Sometimes both!).

Masquerading as a journalist, Jessica Roberts (Rekha Sharma) got on the wrong side of Misty Quigley, who fatally poisoned her with fentanyl-laced cigarettes.

Taisaa's family pet was murdered by Night Taissa. Then, his head was placed on creepy basement altar, which would prove a foreshadowing for Ben's fate.

In Season 2, Detective Kevyn Tan (Alex Wyndham) was put in the awkward position of investigating his former high school classmates as he looked into the disappearance of Adam Martin. When his search led him to Lottie's cult's headquarters, he was felled — not by a Yellowjacket! — but by one of their fans. Specifically, Walter Tattersall (Elijah Wood), a citizen detective who fell for Misty. As the two were talking, Walter subtly poisoned Kevyn with a laced hot cocoa.

Nat (Juliette Lewis) died in the Season 2 finale, and we're still not over it. Of all the survivors, she seemed the one who likely to succeed in escaping their past. Worse yet, she was killed by her friend, Misty.

According to recent reports, it was an accident. Misty was trying to kill one of Lottie's cult members, Lisa (Nicole Maines), but Nat intervened by throwing her own body in front of the syringe, which was filled with poison. However, Natalie's story continued in the flashbacks. And yet, that only promised to make her ending sadder.

Who killed Lottie Matthews (Simone Kessell)? Well, to the surprise of many, it wasn't Shauna. It was her daughter, Callie Sadecki (Sarah Desjardins). Sure, Callie and Lottie had bonded, no matter how much that enraged Shauna. But when Callie confronted Lottie about stealing the incriminating DAT tape from the Sadecki home, Lottie began saying cryptic things about how Callie was a child of the Wilderness' darkness. In a rage, Callie reacted by shoving Lottie down the stairs.

Industry observers note that taissa's high school paramour (Liv Hewson) rejoined to the fold to reignite their romance and proved a solid teammate (Lauren Ambrose) in the present timeline. But their bliss was punctured by Van's cancer diagnosis, and yet that was not how she died! Hopeful the whatever of the wilderness would save her if she sacrificed someone else, Van tried to kill Melissa (Hilary Swank), a ball-capped Yellowjacket who was long thought dead, but had resurfaced under weird circumstances.

It has been revealed that ultimately, however, Van decided not kill Melissa. And in response, Melissa fatally stabbed her and fled, leaving Taissa to bury Van in an unmarked grave. Naturally, she took the opportunity to cut her lover open and eat her heart.
