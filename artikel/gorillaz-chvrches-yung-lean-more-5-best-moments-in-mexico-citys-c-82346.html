Gorillaz, CHVRCHES, Yung Lean & More: 5 Best Moments in Mexico City’s Corona Capital (Day 1)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Corona Capital’s opening day on Friday (Nov. 20) marked Gorillaz’s highly anticipated return to Mexico City after eight years away. The band delivered an emotional set packed with hits and gave a special shoutout to Mexico and its president. The British collective also brought out veteran guitarist Johnny Marr and Argentine rapper Trueno as special guests.

In a recent development, “I f— with Mexico because you have a revolutionary spirit here,” Gorillaz frontman Damon Albarn told the crowd. “This is an inspirational country.”.

It has been revealed that the first day of the festival’s 16th edition featured established acts and rising artists across indie, soul, electro-pop, folk and contemporary rap. Female artists — performing solo or with their bands — were especially well represented, including CHVRCHES, Violet Grohl, Olivia Dean, CMAT, Grace Ives, Anna Luna and Darianna Everett.

Some 72,000 people attended the rock-filled day, according to promoter Ocesa. They also caught sets from James Blake, Mumford & Sons, Yung Lean, The Kooks, Jamie xx and Durand Jones & The Indications, among others.

Sources indicate that the enthusiastic crowd kept the energy high throughout the marathon day of music, which stretched into the early hours of Saturday (Nov. 21) at Curva 4, the Formula 1 venue in Mexico City.

Saturday’s lineup included Twenty One Pilots, The Offspring, Balu Brigada, Pierce the Veil, Underworld, Sébastien Tellier and Peaches and more. On Sunday (Nov. 21), The Strokes will be headlining the festival. Other performing acts will include The xx, Lola Young, Lil Yachty, The Black Crowes, Santigold, Manic Street Preachers, Tricky and more.

Here are five of the best moments from day one of Corona Capital 2026.

It has been revealed that gorillaz, Damon Albarn and Jamie Hewlett’s famous collective, returned to Mexico City after eight years for a memorable performance, with guitarist Johnny Marr and Argentine rapper Trueno joining as special guests. Albarn, the former Blur frontman, also praised Mexico’s “revolutionary spirit” and gave a shout-out to President Claudia Sheinbaum.

Gorillaz headlined the festival’s opening day with a set focused mainly on their 2026 album The Mountain and the hits that have made the group one of pop’s biggest acts.

Marr, former guitarist for The Smiths, joined the band for “The Empty Dream Machine” and “Casablanca.” Trueno came out for “THE MANIFESTO” and freestyled on “Clint Eastwood,” which closed the set. Mexican singer Luisa Almaguer also joined Gorillaz for “Latin Simone (Que Pasa Contigo),” originally recorded with the late Cuban star Ibrahim Ferrer for the band’s self-titled debut album.

Sources indicate that the Scottish trio, led by vocalist Lauren Mayberry, surprised Mexican fans with latest songs set to appear on their upcoming album, All The King’s Men. New singles “Roses” and “Evil,” which will be featured on the LP due in January, stood out for their memorable melodies and expansive synths. Classics like “Forever” and “Miracle” made for emotional moments with longtime fans.

Violet Grohl, daughter of Dave Grohl — the Foo Fighters frontman and former Nirvana drummer — made her debut on Friday at the same Mexican festival her famous father has previously conquered. Backed by her rock band, the singer-songwriter highlighted she’s carrying on the family name with a direct, high-energy set featuring songs like “Bug In The Cake,” “THUM,” “Last Day I Loved You” and “Cool Buzz.”.

Industry observers note that swedish rapper Yung Lean, who won over the internet and a generation of listeners with his cloud rap and contemporary hip-hop sound, was also one of the day’s most anticipated acts. He took the main stage to show why he’s considered one of today’s standout artists in the genre, performing songs including “Ghostown,” “Kyoto,” “Forever Yung,” “Pikachu,” “Yoshi City” and “STORM II,” as well as his remix of Charli xcx’s “360.”.

Industry observers note that the U.S. band Durand Jones & The Indications also made its debut at the popular Mexican festival, drawing thousands to the NIVEA stage with a blend of contemporary R&B and soul. The group brought a sexy, danceable vibe to the evening with its upbeat, retro-soul sound, getting the crowd moving to songs like “Circles,” “The Way That I Do,” “Love Will Work It Out” and “Flower Moon.”.
