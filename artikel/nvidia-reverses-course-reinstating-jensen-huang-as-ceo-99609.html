Nvidia reverses course, reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO

UPDATED 10:50 EST / NOVEMBER 23 2026.

It has been revealed that artificial intelligence chip giant Nvidia Corp. today revealed that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as chief executive, president and board director effective immediately after resigning almost two weeks ago.

According to recent reports, the firm’s rockstar leather-jacket-wearing CEO faced turmoil earlier this month after it was revealed that he had been embroiled in a scandal involving multiple extramarital affairs, including with an Nvidia executive and a suspected Chinese spy. The board did not address the affairs, customarily a firing offense at most companies, in its statement.

News of his affair with alleged Chinese spy Alice Huang, an executive of Singapore-based Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd., broke on Nov. 10, reported by The Information. She was reportedly instrumental in helping China earn over $7.5 billion in chip sales through Nvidia, which is currently sanctioned by the United States from shipping to the country under trade restrictions.

In a recent development, huang resigned amid reports that Nvidia’s board was in talks with prepares to replace him. Today’s announcement marks a major about-face by the tech giant.

In today’s announcement, the board said it voted unanimously to reinstate him to his vacated roles after a series of discussions and that it was “satisfied with his candor and ability to lead.” The board added that it received input from “senior levels of United States government” on its decision, and is “confident” that Huang poses no national security risk.

Huang thanked Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress, who stood in as interim CEO, for taking up the helm in his absence before taking back control of the firm.

“I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” mentioned Huang. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”.

Sources indicate that nvidia shares surged more than 31% today. The stock had fallen almost 33% after Huang admitted to the affair with Alice Huang earlier in November.

It has been revealed that “Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as Nvidia CEO is a stunning newly introduced development, but not an altogether surprising one,” eMarketer Inc. analyst Jacob Bourne told SiliconANGLE. “After more than three decades at the helm, Huang has become so central to Nvidia’s identity and the broader AI trade that his departure fueled uncertainty during an already volatile period for the industry.”.

Sources indicate that bourne added that Huang’s return may assuage investor upset and restore confidence in the tech giant, reflected in the current stock upsurge, but the upheaval echoes the brief ouster of OpenAI Group PBC CEO Sam Altman in 2023. Between Nvidia and OpenAI, removing and then welcoming back CEOs amid disagreement and now scandal raises pertinent questions about corporate governance and how the outsized influence of individual leaders in the AI industry will affect the future.

In a recent development, in its statement, the board added that it formed a committee of independent directors to investigate foreign intelligence targeting Huang. The same committee has retained independent counsel to assist it and intends to reach a conclusion despite his reinstatement.

Huang announced his full support for the investigation and mentioned he would cooperate fully.

The board also confirmed the formation of a National Security and Export Control Committee, composed of independent directors, to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. The manufacturer added that it will make certain the committee oversees national security matters and trade controls and is currently searching to fill a upcoming chief compliance officer role.
