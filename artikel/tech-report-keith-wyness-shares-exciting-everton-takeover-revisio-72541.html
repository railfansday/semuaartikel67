The prospect of taking over at Everton is likely to be hugely attractive for several American investment groups.

That is according to former Toffees chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that European football clubs remain “very sought after” across the Atlantic.

This comes after recent reports suggested that the appetite among US investors for owning European clubs is slowly beginning to die off.

However, Wyness does not believe that is the case, and believes that there is still a strong market for clubs in Europe.

He previously told Football Insider that the USA is the most likely source for Everton’s next ownership group, with The Friedkins already looking to sell.

However, any takeover deal is unlikely to be completed until next summer, with the saga still in its early stages at the moment.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Premier League clubs still have significant appeal for US investors, and Everton should have no issues finding a buyer.

The Merseysiders are already part of a group of 11 Premier League clubs to be owned by Americans, and are most likely to remain part of that group.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that Everton won’t be concerned about a lack of interest.

Recent findings suggest that “I don’t know where that’s coming from. In general, in the US, as you say, the US investor appetite for European clubs is good,” he mentioned.

It has been revealed that “I think there is a lot of interest still from American investors in general. So I'd have to argue with that one.

Recent findings suggest that “European football clubs in general are still very sought after, and sports clubs as an asset class are still very sought after. So, no, I don't see that being an issue, and as a result I think there'll be a good market, which is good news for Everton.”.

Former Everton scout Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that Everton winger Carlos Alcaraz and his agent are both unhappy with his level of game time right now.

The Argentine has played just 14 minutes of Premier League football so far this term, and has become something of a forgotten man at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Alcaraz is also out of contract next summer, and King believes that if Everton don’t have the offer of a new deal on the table before January, then his agent will push for an early exit.

The 23-year-old has hardly featured in David Moyes’ prepares across the last 12 months, and could now be heading towards the end of his time with the club.
