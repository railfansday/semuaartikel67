Lainey Wilson is stepping away from bell bottom country.

The “Heart Like a Truck” singer ditched her signature flare pants and Western hat for a classic Oscar de la Renta gown at the 2026 CMA Awards.

Sources indicate that wilson—who is serving as host for the evening’s festivities—looked stunning in a light yellow feathered gown, which featured a short train and a halter neckline. She kept her accessories simple for the Nov. 18 ceremony at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, only donning earrings and a few rings to finish off her simple look. (See all the stars at the show here.).

The 34-year-old—who is also nominated for Entertainer of the Year, Female Vocalist of the Year and Single of the Year for her hit “Somewhere Over Laredo”—has made no secret of her penchant for bell bottoms and a cowboy hat over the years, explaining that she was inspired to create her signature look after arriving in Nashville.

“I get to Nashville and I realized pretty quickly that as a female in this business, you have to do something that's a little outside of the box,” she shared during an interview with the Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum in April. “I wasn't gonna get away with wearing a ball cap and skinny jeans.”.

The realization made her think back to her childhood, as she remembered the first pair of bell bottoms she got at the age of 9—which was also the year she wrote her first song.

According to recent reports, “They were blue leopard print,” she recalled. “I remember putting them on as a child and just thinking—I just felt sassy. I felt like I could do anything.”.

Recent findings suggest that “If I still had those pants, I'd still be trying to fit in them ‘cause I felt good,” she quipped. "I felt just like I could take on the world. It was kind of like my superhero costume.”.

According to recent reports, and that superhero costume promptly became her signature, alongside the cowgirl hat that she started wearing in the late 2010s. In fact, the look has become so associated with the country star, that fans even dress up in their own version of the outfit at her highlights.

Recent findings suggest that “It's cool to look out into the crowd now and see everybody in their bell bottoms and their hats,” she shared. “It feels like a little bit of a movement and it just makes me happy.”.
