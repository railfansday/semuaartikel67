Jobe Bellingham revealed one of Borussia Dortmund’s coaches was responsible for identifying a ‘mismatch’ that helped his side earn a valuable Champions League point against Arsenal.

The Gunners could’ve been out of sight at half-time but had only Viktor Gyokeres’ spectacular early goal to show for their superiority.

Bundesliga table toppers Dortmund, were a different side immediately after the break and claimed a deserved equaliser through Maximilian Beier.

Dortmund’s physical approach and man-to-man system gradually saw them take control and they were millimeters from taking the lead when Bellingham was ruled offside and saw his close-range effort disallowed.

The 21-year-old was substituted shortly afterwards as was the Arsenal star he had be targeting having been instructed to make the most of a physical mismatch.

‘One of the coaches pointed out to me that I was against Myles [Lewis-Skelly],’ he explained on Amazon Prime Video.

‘For the long ball – I don’t want to say a mismatch – but I’m obviously bigger.
