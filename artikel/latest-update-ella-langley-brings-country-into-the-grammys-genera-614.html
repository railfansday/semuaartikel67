Unless you’ve been living under a rock or have never set foot in any retail store, Ella Langley‘s chart-topping country anthem “Choosin’ Texas” has been unavoidable over the last year.

Sources indicate that the wildly catchy track, co-written by country icon Miranda Lambert, launched Langley into a new league, which was reflected in the 2027 Grammy Awards nominations. The 27-year-old was tied for the third-most nominated artist of the year, with seven nominations, four of which are in the general field categories.

Langley earned nods for best fresh artist, album of the year, song of the year and record of the year, along with best country song, best country solo performance and best contemporary country album. Through “Choosin’ Texas,” Langley’s represented in all general field categories, with collaborators Miranda Lambert and Joybeth Taylor getting nominated for producer of the year and songwriter of the year, non-classical. Sources indicate that released in October 2025, “Choosin’ Texas” has continued to dominate on the charts. The track, at the moment sitting at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, has spent 27 weeks atop the chart, making it the longest-running song to accomplish such a feat.

Sources indicate that langley is the only country artist nominated in the four major categories — album of the year, song of the year, record of the year and best fresh artist. Coming into the year, it was unclear whether or not the singer would even be eligible for the best new artist category. Historically, artists could only submit to the category three times, which Langley had already hit. However, the Recording Academy announced that they’d be expanding the category to allow four entries per artist, making her eligible.
