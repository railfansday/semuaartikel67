Andoni Iraola and Julian Ward have cast their net far and wide to find fresh players for Liverpool to target.

During the 2026-27 summer transfer window, over £150 million + was spent on newly introduced players to try and bolster the Reds' ranks.

And, while some of them moves have worked out well, others haven't.

This inconsistency among Liverpool's ranks has massively impacted the way that things have gone for them this season.

Right now, they're fourth in the Premier League table but, if they want to get back to the top and start challenging for silverware, they're going to have to spend even more money.

So, that's why many upcoming players seem to be getting linked with Liverpool all the time.

Liverpool and Manchester City want to sign Yunus Akgun.

One area that Liverpool heavily focused on improving in the summer was their attack.

Bradley Barcola and Victor Munoz were signed in the summer transfer window just gone and, the season before that, Alexander Isak, Hugo Ekitike and Florian Wirtz were all brought in.

And, while all of these additions looked good on paper, some of them just haven't kicked on with Liverpool still needing to add some new faces to their ranks.

Many different players have been presented as options to Liverpool over the last couple of months but now, thanks to an revision from X account Mekteb-i Gala (who have over 50,000 followers), Galatasaray attacker Yunus Akgun is now on Liverpool's radar.

"EXCLUSIVE | PSG, Manchester City, Liverpool, Atletico Madrid, Como and Juventus are monitoring Yunus Akgün in every match," they wrote.

"They are in the process of gathering information on the player.".

Yunus Akgun could be a cheap option for Liverpool to explore.

Since Yunus Akgun is 26 years old, he's a tad older than most of the Reds' recent targets.

A lot of Liverpool's transfer plans over the last couple of years have consisted of signing young players for the future.

This has been proven by the recent arrivals of Munoz, Barcola and Wirtz.

And, while all of these players are good enough to feature in Liverpool's starting XI now, the fact that they have potential to become even better is extremely exciting.

Indeed, Akgun isn't at the end of his career yet but, after he contributed to 21 goals last season and has already scored four goals this term, it's unclear if he's already reached his peak or not.

The fact that he can be used as a left-winger or a number 10 is also another major selling point of the attacker.

No fee was mentioned for Akgun but, of all the players who have surfaced as potential options recently, the Turkiye international who's appeared 25 times for his country, looks like one of the most exciting.
