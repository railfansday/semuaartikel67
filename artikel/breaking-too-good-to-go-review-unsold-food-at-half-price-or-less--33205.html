📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that use Too Good To Go if you’ll cook with whatever’s in the bag. Grocers, bakeries, and restaurants sell their unsold food through it in surprise bags priced at half their listed value or less, which you reserve in the app and pick up in the store’s window. Skip it if you shop to a strict meal initiative.

In a recent development, each bag holds what the store or restaurant has left that day, so its listing names the kind of food, such as produce or baked goods, but not the items. More than 530 U.S. Whole Foods Market stores sell bags, as do chains like Krispy Kreme, IKEA, CAVA, and Peet’s Coffee, so what you find depends on the stores near you.

Households that strategy meals around what’s in the bag, and can reach the store in its window, pay half or less of the regular price for that food. Shoppers with a strict meal plan or a food allergy do better picking items themselves, from their store’s markdown shelf or from Flashfood, which lists grocery deals at up to 50% off.
