Recent findings suggest that in August 2023, Bilt launched a upcoming way to win free rent (up to $2,500) for a month: the "Rent Free" game show. Bilt has since offered the "Rent Free" game show each Rent Day, with a new creator or personality taking the stage each month. This month's guest is Brittany Snow.

Sources indicate that you can visit the Bilt app to play the game between Nov. 20 at noon EST and Dec. 1 at 3 p.m. EST. Then, return to the app after Dec. 1 at 4 p.m. EST to see your score.

Industry observers note that you'll earn points by matching your answers in the game show with the celebrity guest's. The top 10 winners will have their rent paid for a month (up to $2,500) and get a signed copy of Snow's "September Letters: Finding Strength and Connection in Sharing Our Stories" book. Plus, Bilt will add additional rent winners based on how many answers Snow gets correct. The next 100 winners will earn 1,000 Bilt points, while the next 500 winners after that will earn 100 points.

Other Bilt members can buy Snow's book through Bilt with points or cash starting Nov. 20 at 9 a.m. EST. The book costs 2,000 Bilt points or $20, but you can use up to $20 of the monthly Bilt Cash allowance. Only 100 books are on offer, and they should ship within two to three weeks of purchase.

Each Rent Day, Bilt offers a variety of neighborhood experiences you can book. Here’s what you need to know about the neighborhood experiences available this Rent Day.

Bilt is offering comedy reveals at 20 venues across 14 cities — Atlanta; Austin; Boston; Chandler, Arizona; Charlotte; Chicago; Denver; Los Angeles; Upcoming York City; San Diego; San Francisco; Seattle; Somerville, Massachusetts; and Washington, D.C. — throughout early and mid-December.

In a recent development, each Bilt member can buy up to four tickets, with bookings opening Nov. 21 at 11:50 a.m. EST for Platinum and Gold Bilt members and at noon EST for other members.

The December comedy show lineup features some comedy club experiences that cost $30 to $40 or 2,000 to 2,500 Bilt points per person and include two drinks. However, the lineup also includes bespoke comedy experiences at premium venues priced at $75 or 5,000 Bilt points per person, with drinks and light bites covered. You can apply up to $50 in Bilt Cash each month toward a comedy experience (the remainder must be paid in cash).

Bookings close when reservations sell out or at 11 a.m. EST the day of each show, whichever occurs first.

Recent findings suggest that bilt is offering its Neighborhood Dining experiences at 40 restaurants in nine cities — Arlington, Virginia; Boston; Chicago; Fort Lauderdale; Jersey City, New Jersey; Los Angeles; Miami; New York City; and Washington, D.C. — on Dec. 1.

Dining experiences cost $35 to $78 or 2,500 to 5,000 Bilt points per person and include a three- to four-course tasting menu or omakase, depending on the venue. You can add a drink package at the time of booking for meals at select venues. You can apply up to $50 in Bilt Cash to a single dining experience (the remainder must be paid in cash).

Each Bilt member can buy up to four seats per booking; bookings open Nov. 21 at 11:50 a.m. EST for Platinum and Gold Bilt members and at noon EST for other members. Bookings close when reservations sell out or Nov. 30 at 5 p.m. EST, whichever occurs first.

It has been revealed that one of Bilt Rent Day's most popular aspects for many members is the complimentary Rent Day fitness classes. Bilt members can book complimentary Dec. 1 SoulCycle and Barry's classes for this Rent Day. Specifically, 1,175 bikes are available for Rent Day SoulCycle classes, and 580 spots are available for Barry's Rent Day classes.

Industry observers note that bookings for these experiences become available Nov. 21 at 11:50 a.m. EST for Platinum and Gold members and at noon EST for other members. This month, cobranded Bilt × September Letters mailboxes are landing in select Bilt Alliance building lobbies. If you live in one of these buildings, you can write a letter to yourself and drop it in the mailbox. You'll then get that letter back in the mail one year later. But if you choose to share the letter, it could be featured in a "Letters from Home" collection on the September Letters website.

Even if you don't live in a participating building, you can write a letter to yourself in the Bilt app. You can keep your letter private or opt in to allow it to be shared with the Bilt community.

While supplies last, Bilt members can stop by between 11 a.m. and 5 p.m. EST on Dec. 1 to collect a complimentary bento box containing lobster vol-au-vent (a vegetarian option is also available), warm apple chausson, a hot chocolate shake and warm spiced cider.
