Interesting to read through these BBC Sport ‘neutrals’ comments, following on from England defeating Spain.

The hosts going 3-0 down inside an hour as the visitors blew them away.

England goals coming from Kane (pen), Konsa and White, though it could have been five or six up if Thomas Tuchel’s side had turned their superiority into the goals it deserved.

Spain had not lost a competitive game since March 2023, some 44 matches ago.

A daft red card for Elliot Anderson and goals gifted by Quansah and Gordon caught in possession, Spain threatened to keep that unbeaten record going.

However, the final scoreline read Spain 2 England 3 and Tuchel and his players absolutely deserving of the victory against the World Cup winners.

Always interesting to hear the ‘neutral’ perspectives on the England players.
