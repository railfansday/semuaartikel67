Set inside Wrocław's historic 1904 Feniks building (formerly the Barasch Brothers' Department Store) at 75 Szewska Street, Oki Doki is a minimalist matcha bar. The design weaves exposed architectural heritage with a bold Yves Klein blue mural, creating a contemporary urban sanctuary focused on "togetherness in solitude." The site previously housed a fabric store, and this historical continuity of self-expression through commerce served as a vital reference for the project.
