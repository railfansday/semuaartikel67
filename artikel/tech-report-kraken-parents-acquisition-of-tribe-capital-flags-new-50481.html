📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Payward, the owner of crypto exchange Kraken, is pushing into private markets with its latest acquisition, underscoring a broader shift in cryptocurrencies, from trading-led growth to the buildout of financial infrastructure.

Sources indicate that payward said late Wednesday that it's buying venture capital and asset management provider Tribe Capital Management, led by Payward's co-CEO Arjun Sethi. No purchase price was disclosed.

Tribe, where Sethi is co-founder and chairman, invests across early- and late-stage private companies and has $2.7 billion in assets under management.

In a recent development, "Joining Payward gives us the opportunity to bring Tribe's investment approach to a global financial platform," Jonathan Hsu, another co-founder of Tribe Capital, stated in a statement. "We see a strong fit between our investment approach and Payward's infrastructure, distribution, and product-building capabilities.".

Payward didn't respond to a request for comment.

The acquisition is novel among crypto exchanges. Unlike exchange-owned venture arms such as Coinbase Ventures or Binance Labs, Payward is looking to integrate Tribe's investment and fund-management capabilities into an existing custody, trading, collateral and tokenization infrastructure to develop private-market investment products.

Payward is trying to position itself to capture value from a broader crypto-driven transformation of financial services, not use the venture vehicle to draw more capital into the crypto ecosystem.

According to recent reports, the acquisition is another step in the evolution in the crypto exchange business model. With cryptocurrency prices continuing to decline this year, crypto exchanges have worked to move past their reliance on trading volumes and transaction fees, focusing instead on building the infrastructure connecting digital assets with traditional finance.
