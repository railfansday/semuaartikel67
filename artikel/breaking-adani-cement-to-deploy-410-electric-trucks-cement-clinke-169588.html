📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ahmedabad, India — Adani Cement, part of the Adani Portfolio and one of India’s leading building materials solutions providers, flagged off Heavy-Duty Electric Trucks at its Sanghi Plant in Gujarat. This is the first-ever deployment of EV trucks by Adani Cement and a defining milestone in the manufacturer’s sustainability and green logistics journey.

Industry observers note that mr. Vinod Bahety, CEO of Cement Business, Adani Group, said, “The deployment at Sanghi is just the beginning. We have identified multiple routes across India for EV-based transport of clinker and cement. Every truck we electrify reduces our carbon footprint and insulates our logistics cost base from diesel price volatility. This is smart sustainability—it benefits the people, the planet, and the balance sheet. This is our commitment in action—to build a greener, more competitive cement company that serves India’s growth while protecting its future.”.

It has been revealed that the economic case for heavy-duty EV trucks in India’s cement sector has reached a structural tipping point. Over the past decade, diesel prices rose 60%, while Li-ion battery pack costs fell by approximately 70% and solar tariffs declined 47%.

Diesel trucks emit approximately 0.7375 kg CO₂ per km and when powered by renewable energy, EV trucks can eliminate up to 100% of these freight emissions, making logistics electrification a powerful lever for Scope 3 decarbonization. Adani Cement’s 410-truck fleet is projected to reduce over 25,000 tonnes of CO₂ annually, displacing the equivalent of 11 million litres of diesel per year, with plans to expand across additional routes and plants.

Recent findings suggest that india is the world’s second-largest cement producer, and the movement of limestone, clinker, and finished cement across vast supply chains is an essential, but carbon-intensive, part of the sector’s operations. Adani Cement’s EV fleet deployment addresses both the cost and climate dimensions of this challenge directly.
