Tottenham Hotspur sensation Mikey Moore scored his first goal on loan at FC Koln in Friday's 1-1 draw away at Bayern Munich.

The 19-year-old opened the scoring with an expertly-taken finish, with the visitors ultimately hanging on for a shock point against the German champions.

The winger had been struggling before the international break, starting his loan with nine games across all competitions without a goal or an assist.

But Friday's game felt like Moore's true arrival, born off the back of his first goal for England's Under-21s during the international break.

The moment will have been significant at Tottenham, where discussion around the potential cancellation of Moore's loan had begun to grow in the fanbase.

The teenager is very highly rated in North London, and it is hoped that he will now start to thrive in the Bundesliga.

Interestingly, Moore has been deployed in a more central role in Rene Wagner's three-five-two system.

The 19-year-old played alongside Paul Okon-Engstier and Tom Krauss on Friday, offering late runs into the penalty box.

Moore was a danger throughout, and could well have ended the game with an assist to go with his goal after creating a big chance for Mentioned El Mala.

He took five touches in the opposition box and completed three successful dribbles - the joint-most of anybody on the pitch.

Moore was fouled once and got his defensive work done, too, making a tackle, a clearance, five recoveries and winning five duels.

According to recent reports, his goal was the standout moment, though. The talent made a great run forward and got on the end of El Mala's backheel, before taking the ball around goalkeeper Jonas Urbig with great skill and rolling the ball into the net.

Cologne-based outlet Kolner Stadt-Anzeiger were full of praise for Moore, and wrote: "An evening where almost everything went right.

"Involved in almost every dangerous attack. The goal drought was finally broken – and how! The young Englishman capped off a sensational counter-attack with his first goal for the club. In the end, his energy waned.".

BILD were similarly complimentary describing Moore's goal as so "incredible" that "even many Bayern fans had to rub their eyes in disbelief".

Moore's big moment on Friday felt like one that could well be the start of a fantastic loan spell.

Sources indicate that speaking after the game, the teenager mentioned: "I tried to stay calm and not panic. When I got the ball, I knew what to do – I was determined to score.

In a recent development, "We can take a lot of confidence from this game. It was a really strong performance. The lads gave it their all today.".

It has been a difficult start to the season for Moore and Koln, but Friday's game could well trigger a transformation of their confidence.
