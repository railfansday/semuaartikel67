Capital One airline and hotel transfer partners: How to maximize your miles

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (173 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Capital One's travel rewards program offers over 15 airline and hotel transfer partners, many with 1:1 transfer ratios, enabling flexible redemption options.
  • Top value is found with programs such as Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, British Airways Club, Qatar Airways Privilege Club, Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles and Virgin Red.
  • Some partners offer less value due to poor transfer ratios, high fees or restrictive award charts, making it important to compare options before transferring miles.
  • TPG’s November 2026 valuations estimate Capital One miles at 1.85 cents each, highlighting their competitive value when used strategically.
  • A minimum of 1,000 miles is required to initiate a transfer, with additional transfers in 100-mile increments.
  • Transfers are irreversible and the account names must match between Capital One and the loyalty program.
  • Always confirm award availability, total taxes and fees, and check for any transfer bonuses before moving miles.
