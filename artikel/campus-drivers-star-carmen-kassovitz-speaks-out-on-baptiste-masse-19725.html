According to recent reports, anyone else finish Campus Drivers on Prime and immediately get to work digging into whether or not Carmen Kassovitz and Baptiste Masseline are dating in real life? Fair: their chemistry is off the charts and they’d hardly be the first couple whose on-screen romance has translated into something real. But Carmen just spoke out about the rumors and confirmed that—sorry!—she and Baptiste are just friends.

It has been revealed that when asked if she’d ever had a crush on a costar by Pardon My Frenchie (via Swoon), Carmen joked, “I know where you’re going,” and then added, “No, because this is only my job, and you know what’s really weird is I had a crush on a costar that is not playing my lover. Because the guy who’s playing my lover…so, like, Baptiste, listen up, baby. No, it’s just Baptiste is my friend, and Baptiste is like…I have a friendship crush on Baptiste. I love him, but I love him as much as I love Felix and Madior and Esteban.” (Note: those are the other Campus Drivers crew!).

Sources indicate that as for any lingering looks between them that you may/may not be reading into, Carmen has seen them too. And there’s nothing going on! “I had a reel of me also looking at him, but I think we just love each other so much,” she said. “Before any interviews, we were like, ‘You got my back, and I got your back,’ and that’s why I think, when I look at him, or he looks at me, it’s more like, ‘I’m proud of you.’ It’s more that feeling of, ‘OK, we’re killing it right now.’”.
