It has been revealed that rockstar's lengthy credits reveal just how massive the GTA 6 song list truly is, but you don't start the game with every radio station unlocked. Leonida has two secret playlists (both needed for 100% completion), and neither is particularly hard to unlock once you know where to look.

Industry observers note that the Sniper of La Perle, Kodak Black's 22-track playlist, becomes available as soon as you scan four posters around La Perle at TWA Ponya Tattoo, Drip Drops, Aunt Zola's Soul Food, and the Jack of Hearts nightclub. Then there's The Vault Radio, which only requires you to scan a single poster at the nightclub of the same name.

Sources indicate that the shops automatically marked themselves on my map as I drove around La Perle and Crosstown, but I've included screenshots highlighting their specific locations in Vice City's Haitian district. Once you arrive at one, Jason or Lucia's phone should alert you to something nearby you can scan with a notification from the Waink It app.

In a recent development, look right as soon as you enter the parlor. There's a Sniper of Perle poster on the small table by the door.

You'll find another Sniper of Perle poster lying on the cash register counter.

The Sniper of Perle poster is immediately to the left as you enter, on a corkboard in front of the cash register.

Head upstairs to the second floor and enter the Jack of Hearts recording studio. Pass through the lobby and into the control room, then you'll see the Sniper of Perle poster lying on the table beside a fan.

It has been revealed that once you arrive at the nightclub, head through the entrance corridor and look at the back wall. The poster is right there, and one quick scan is all it takes to unlock The Vault's playlist.
