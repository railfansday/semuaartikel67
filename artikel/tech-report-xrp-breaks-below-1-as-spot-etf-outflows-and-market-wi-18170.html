📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that XRP has fallen to its lowest level since Nov. 2024, suggesting a shift in market sentiment and liquidity dynamics. The token associated with the Ripple protocol dropped below $1, touching $0.89 on Sunday, marking its lowest level since Nov. 2024. The asset currently trades at $0.964, down 13.12% over the past month, suggesting reduced investor engagement amid rising volatility.

Recent findings suggest that the asset slipped below $1, settling at a low of $0.89 on Sunday as selling pressure surges, pointing to heightened caution among investors. The wider crypto market is right now in an intense downtrend, with significant pressure pushing XRP down 26.13% over the past month. It is trading at $0.89-$0.96, its lowest level since Nov. 2024, which coincides with Trump’s election.

The cause of the plunge is clear, as investor interest has been hurt by political shifts in the US (seen in the 2026 midterm election), the Binance hack, rising inflation concerns, and monetary policy tightening. All these triggers have collectively intensified selling pressure on the crypto market and weakened investor risk appetite for digital assets.

Recent findings suggest that $18 million in outflows from U.S. spot XRP ETFs over the past two days is another good indicator of decreased appetite among institutional investors.

The fall broke the crucial $1.25 support, raising the risk of a deeper drop below $1.

In her market analysis, crypto analyst Cleo Schmidt identified that the current 2026 XRP downtrend resembles the historical 2017 bear market structure. Despite user panic, the analyst pointed out a common similarity between the two markets: long-term conviction appears more powerful, with dip-buying behavior replacing blind selling.

Sources indicate that on-chain data shows significant distribution happening underneath, as whales decrease exposure while long-term holders absorb selling pressure. This reflects a prolonged accumulation period, which normally follows major market bottoms.

With dip-buying activity and future-focused conviction, XRP’s long-term price structure reveals renewed underlying strength, as the analyst expects the third wave of its explosive expansion to be underway. Once completed, the pattern would push XRP’s price towards $50 per coin, the analyst predicted.
