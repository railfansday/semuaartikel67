Stellar recorded $207.2 million in tokenized funds in the seven days to November 22, reaffirming continued institutional interest in the blockchain.

Industry observers note that the RWA Foundation shared data in a post using figures from Token Terminal showing that Stellar ranked among the top blockchains in tokenized fund growth in the seven days from November 15 to November 22, 2026. Stellar recorded $207.2 million: Franklin Templeton added $108.0 million and Spiko added $68.2 million.

Sources indicate that .@injective led tokenized fund growth this past week. @StellarOrg had a good week also. https://t.co/7Ip2oK6c1X.

This means Stellar continues to grow in tokenized assets, a major highlight of strong growth in Q3, 2026.

Real-world assets tokenized on Stellar amounted to $4.1 billion at the end of Q3 2026, up 36% from Q2 and up 548% from Q3 2025. Two assets drove the bulk of the quarter’s growth: Tradable’s new tokenized private credit and continued inflows into the Spiko Amundi overnight swap fund.

Sources indicate that in late August 2026, Stellar held more tokenized non-US government debt than any other blockchain, with about $490 million in sovereign instruments. These included Spiko’s euro Treasury bill fund, Mexican CETES and Brazilian Tesouro bonds through Etherfuse, South Korean Treasury bonds, and a Marshall Islands digital sovereign bond.

Stellar has widened access to tokenized funds in the United States, Europe and Asia. Franklin Templeton’s BENJI has been on Stellar since 2021. In August 2026, the SEC's Division of Investment Management issued a no-action letter confirming that the Franklin Templeton's registered mutual funds and ETFs may use BENJI for cash management. This expands the set of institutions permitted to hold it. Franklin Templeton made a similar move in Europe, registering 'grBenji' as a Luxembourg UCITS share class for third-party distribution, now live on 21X.

It has been revealed that in Asia, Marketnode announced in August that it will offer funds from BNY Investments on Stellar, with Marketnode acting as the tokenization and distribution platform. Together, these developments expand access to tokenized funds on Stellar for institutions in the United States, Europe and Asia.

State Street Investment Management and Galaxy brought their Onchain Liquidity Sweep Fund to Stellar in September 2026.
