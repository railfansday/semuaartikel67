It has been revealed that california Gov. Gavin Newsom's calls for federal reforms to presidential pardon powers are garnering intense backlash for "hypocrisy" after he used his state-level executive powers to spare hundreds of death row inmates from receiving the death penalty.

Trump vowed Monday to direct the Justice Department (DOJ) to seek the death penalty for child murderers and speed up federal executions after Newsom’s announcement.

Online fury erupted after Newsom posted a video defending his decision to commute the death sentences of 548 criminals while accusing President Donald Trump of turning "the power of mercy into a business.".

"Gavin, is the arsonist calling for a ban on fire," Rep. Nancy Mace, R-S.C., posted on X. "Man who commuted 548 death row sentences is now deeply concerned about abuse of the pardon power.".

One X user noted how Trump "just told the DOJ to fast-track the death penalty for child killers and predators. [Gavin Newsom], on the other hand, is proudly defending his decision to spare 548 of the worst of the worst.

"Tells you everything you need to know.".

It has been revealed that another user posted, "Are you f---ing kidding me? His arrogance and hypocrisy are immeasurable.".

"After pardoning everyone on death row in California, Newsom calls for reform of the president’s pardon powers," another wrote on X. "You can’t make this stuff up.".

Sources indicate that trump called out Newsom at the DOJ alongside Attorney General Todd Blanche, FBI Director Kash Patel and family members of victims of the 548 death row inmates. He referred to the inmates as "the most evil monsters on the face of the Earth," and accused Newsom of stealing closure for the victims' families.

Some of the crimes committed by inmates who will no longer face the death sentence include slaughtering entire families, torturing and murdering children and killing police officers.

In Newsom’s video, he defended his decision to commute the death sentences to life without parole, accusing Trump of abusing his pardon powers.

"Donald Trump doesn’t oppose mercy. He sells it," Newsom said in the video. "If you’re poor, Black or Brown, you get the needle. If you’re rich and know Donald Trump, you get a pardon and a personal phone call.".

Trump issued 220 total pardons in his second term as president and 143 during his first term. Part of the blowback against Newsom also stems from his decision to override California voters' rejection to abolish the death penalty, defending his clemency authority as an example of "when law as applied cannot be squared with justice.".

In a recent development, "And the Governor of CA should not be able to sell out crime victims and prosecutors by overriding juries, judges and the WILL of CA voters," a former Sacramento District Attorney posted to X. "Worst Governor in history.".

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier said Monday he led 14 attorneys general in condemning Newsom’s initiative, arguing Newsom had overridden juries, courts and California voters.

Some critics have speculated Newsom's move to revoke California inmates death sentences is a tactic gearing up for his bid as the Democratic nominee for the 2028 presidential election. He is seen as one of the top candidates for the party.
