Industry observers note that of the numerous signals that investors look for as green flags for buying a stock, one of the most common is insider buying. And this is precisely what's contributing to the rise in USA Rare Earth (USAR +4.73%) stock today.

Industry observers note that as of 3:06 p.m. ET, shares of the rare-earth mining stock are up 4.7%, retreating from an earlier rise of 11.6%.

Michael Blitzer, who has served as chairman of USA Rare Earth's board of directors since the organization's 2025 public listing, bought $3 million in USA Rare Earth stock in three transactions this week, according to a regulatory filing.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/19/26.

As a result of the transactions, Blitzer now directly owns 1,855,537 shares and has indirect exposure through Tristram Ave LLC, which owns 350,000 shares.

According to recent reports, USA Rare Earth is developing a fully integrated rare-earth and permanent-magnet value chain across the United States, Brazil, and the United Kingdom. In the third quarter of 2026, the organization's magnet facility in Stillwater achieved a milestone with its first commercial magnet sales.

Although insiders will sell company stock for a variety of reasons, they'll rarely buy stock on the open market for a reason other than the belief that shares are poised to run higher. And while it may seem logical to follow their lead, it's risky to blindly follow the actions of company insiders. Main Street investors would be much better off conducting their own due diligence and investigating USA Rare Earth further to determine whether the stock is right for them. Since it's still a speculative stock, investors looking for industry exposure may feel more comfortable opting for a rare-earth ETF.
