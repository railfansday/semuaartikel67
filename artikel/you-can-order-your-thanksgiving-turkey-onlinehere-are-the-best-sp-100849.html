Sources indicate that ordering things online and getting them delivered right to your house isn't just for everyday convenience. You can buy shockingly high-quality food on the internet—even good enough for the holidays. From fresh seafood to juicy steaks, you can get almost anything delivered these days, including a whole Thanksgiving turkey. So you might as well just cut some of that work out of the long day of cooking. You can order your bird raw, already roasted, and even stuffed. All you have to do is make the sides. But maybe you could order those too! Here are the best places to order your bird and save yourself time in the kitchen.

D'Artagnan has a reputation for offering one of the most extensive collections of specialty meats you can order online. So it's no surprise that they offer meat from turkeys raised without animal by-products, antibiotics, or growth hormones. Organic turkey is available in several sizes, starting at $112.99.

Check out Fossil Farms—they're the real deal. Their birds are raised in Pennsylvania with plenty of room, and they're fed "a vegetarian diet of local rye, oats, alfalfa, and soybean meal." Turkeys start at $73.

Sources indicate that they sell way more than just steaks, people! In addition to this generous-sized turkey that comes with "easy-to-follow instructions," you can also get your sides delivered. They offer Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes, Steakhouse Creamed Corn, and much, much more.

You can also buy a whole roasted turkey or just a regular turkey breast, but this stuffed turkey breast (it comes with two!) will make your holiday dinner special, especially if you're having a smaller meal.

Thanksgiving can be stressful. Why not just sit back, relax, and let someone else do the cooking? This year, Harry & David will send you a precooked turkey. Just heat it up, and it's ready to serve.

It has been revealed that fried turkey is delicious, but it can be quite time-consuming (and a little dangerous) to do on your own. This fried turkey is rubbed with Cajun seasonings and injected with over a quart of proprietary "Geaux Juice" for the ultimate flavorful bird.

Industry observers note that in addition to meat and poultry subscriptions, ButcherBox offers whole turkeys for Thanksgiving. According to the brand, these birds come from a fifth-generation, family-owned and operated farm in Pennsylvania.
