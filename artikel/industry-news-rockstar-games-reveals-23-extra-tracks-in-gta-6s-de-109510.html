For those of you out there in love with Grand Theft Auto 6's soundtrack, you'll be happy to know that Rockstar Games have revealed an additional 23 tracks as part of the game's deluxe edition album.

In a recent development, these tracks, available via streaming services, include songs from artists like HEALTH, Denzel Curry, Playboi Carti, and more. You can find the full track list on the official Rockstar Games music website.

Some of these artists played a significant role in the game. Justice, for example, helped compose the game's original soundtrack and several of their tracks appear throughout the game. There are others, too.

Both Sexyy Red and Denzel Curry voice characters in the game's later chapters. Others like Kodack Black and Freddie Gibbs also show up, too - that's not to mention the huge number of musicians who make an appearance in the game's many radio stations. If you listen through those, you'll hear the voices of Keith Richards, Lana Del Ray, The Alchemist and others.

Musical talent also played an additional role in creating the TV advertisements and highlights you can watch in the game. Zack Fox is one example, as is Weird Al Yankovic. Both wrote parts of the game's enticing and absurd big screen entertainment - which makes sense given the pair's shared comedy background.
