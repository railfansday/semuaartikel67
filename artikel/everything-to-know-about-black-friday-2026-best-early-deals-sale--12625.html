We're officially less than two weeks away from one of the biggest shopping events of the year: Black Friday.

It has been revealed that though most sales officially kick off this week, deals have been rolling out for the past few weeks. However, price trends are inching ever closer to record lows, harkening the true start to peak Black Friday season.

Industry observers note that that doesn't necessarily mean you should start frantically adding deals to your cart right this instant, but it does mean that it's good to head into the mega-sale event with a blueprint. From knowing when sales from retailers like Amazon and Best Buy start to knowing which products will likely go on sale to preparing your own shopping list, we've got you covered on everything you need to know ahead of Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

This year, Black Friday falls on Nov. 27. While retailers often start featuring their best markdowns on Black Friday itself, there are plenty of early deals worth shopping in the lead-up. Most retailers' official sales do start before the actual day, so shopping strategically ahead of time can save you from missing out on a deal entirely (or dealing with the chaos the day after Thanksgiving).

While Black Friday brings many of the best prices of the year, it's not the end-all, be-all for every product out there. According to Google-analyzed holiday trends, fancy cookware and suitcases hit their lowest prices in late December, while tech like TVs and gaming consoles are likely to hit their lowest prices during Black Friday and Cyber Week (with the latter seeing one final dip right before Christmas).

There can be some variance from category to category, so we recommend keeping track of Mashable's deals coverage. Our team has been covering Black Friday (and deals in general) for nearly a decade, so we can tell your firsthand whether or not a product is worth grabbing during the height of sales.

With Black Friday proper bringing some of the lowest prices, what deals are worth shopping now? It's an especially relevant question in 2026, a year that saw many historic price increases across everything from laptops to Kindles. Sources indicate that the more apt question is what doesn't go on sale during Black Friday. That's to say, if there's a product you're looking to grab on sale, whether that be a fresh TV or paper towels, Black Friday is the time to do it.

Sources indicate that beyond the deals we have listed above, we expect to see more savings on MacBooks, Windows laptops, video games and consoles, robot vacuums, regular stick vacuums, drones, trading cards, books, board games, and power tools. This list is by no means exhaustive — if there's a good deal worth writing up, you'll likely find it in our deals coverage.

Sources indicate that the best Black Friday deals generally last through Cyber Monday, which falls on Nov. 30 this year. Some deals do linger through the week, and some Cyber Monday sales (such as Walmart's) are scheduled to extend beyond Cyber Monday proper. And as Target's deal-of-the-day sale demonstrates, there are almost always Black Friday-worthy sales through essentially Christmas. That said, for the best shot at scoring massive savings (and for catching a deal while it's still available), shopping during Black Friday weekend is typically a good rule of thumb.
