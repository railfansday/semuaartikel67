One of the first partner brands selling Android XR glasses, “Intelligent Eyewear” as Google calls it, has already completely sold out, with Gentle Monster no longer accepting pre-orders just days after launch.

Sources indicate that google’s Gemini-powered Intelligent Eyewear lineup introduced last week with collections from Gentle Monster and Warby Parker, with pricing starting at $395. Just a few days later, though, over half of the lineup is sold out.

Every single Intelligent Eyewear style from Gentle Monster is currently sold out, with pre-orders no longer available.

Sources indicate that warby Parker, meanwhile, still has most styles fully in stock. Some are delayed, mainly “Reid” which has the wide fit delayed as far as December 28, but others are still shipping on launch day.

According to recent reports, it’s unclear how many units were actually available, so it’s tough to say whether or not these are actually a hit yet, but it’s certainly unexpected to see a whole collection sold out this rapidly. The first batch of Android XR glasses go on sale on December 10 – are you getting a pair?
