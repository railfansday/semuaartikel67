Recent findings suggest that publisher 2K and developer Visual Concepts, in partnership with sister organization Rockstar Games, have a collaboration between NBA 2K27 and Grand Theft Auto VI. This includes NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition, which features three-time NBA All-Star and Miami HEAT center Bam Adebayo on the cover of the limited-time collaboration, and an in-game season of content featuring Vice City-themed content and rewards across The City and MyTEAM.

Sources indicate that “Take-Two is home to some of the most beloved series in entertainment, and this partnership unites our two biggest communities for the first time,” said Take-Two Interactive CEO and chairman Strauss Zelnick in a press release. “Bringing Vice City to NBA 2K27 is a testament to the cultural impact of the games that our teams at Rockstar Games and 2K, led by Visual Concepts, have built, and we’re excited to give players an entirely newly introduced and engaging way to experience both worlds.”.

According to recent reports, starting November 27, players can pick up the NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition featuring three-time NBA All-Star and Miami HEAT center Bam Adebayo on the cover of the limited-time collaboration. In-game, Season 3 brings an extensive update with Vice City-themed content and rewards across The City and MyTEAM. The partnership also includes bonuses and access to NBA 2K27 for GTA+ Members in addition to a live activation at the “A Night in Vice City” Miami HEAT game on November 18.

In a recent development, the neon glow and hustle of Vice City comes to NBA 2K27 in an extended, seven-week Season 3, running November 27 through January 15 (11:00 a.m. ET). Vice City-inspired content and rewards span every corner of the Season experience, from a brand-latest Vice City-inspired Park in The City – the online social hub of NBA 2K—to a wealth of themed apparel including Vice City and Los Santos jerseys, Burger Shot and Cluckin’ Bell Employee Outfits, and much more:.

NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition and GTA+ Member Benefits.

The collaboration extends even further with a one-of-a-kind cover. The NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition puts Miami HEAT superstar Bam Adebayo front and center, with cover art inspired by the neon glow of Vice City, and comes packed with Vice City-themed content. GTA+ Members can also play NBA 2K27 and unlock Vice City bonus content for a limited time.

NBA 2K27 at “A Night in Vice City”.

Recent findings suggest that finally, NBA 2K will take over 601 at Kaseya Center as the Miami HEAT take on the Milwaukee Bucks during “A Night In Vice City” on November 18. The 2K Mart meets Vice City in this immersive activation, featuring a Vice City-themed 2K Mart with limited-edition merch giveaways, and photo opportunities.

NBA 2K27 is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam.
