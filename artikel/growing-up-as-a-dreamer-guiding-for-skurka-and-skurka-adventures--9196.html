📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In today’s episode of Backpacker Radio presented by The Trek, brought to you by LMNT, we are joined by Jose “Plants” Mendez, a thru-hiker, ultrarunner, and outdoor educator who guides for NOLS and Andrew Skurka Adventures, alongside our longtime pal Andrew Skurka.

In this one, Jose walks us through growing up undocumented and how endurance sports helped him navigate addiction and depression, he shares why camping alone in the Sierra was the first time he’d ever felt truly safe, and recounts the terrifying final miles of his southbound PCT hike, where he experienced the most intense mixed emotions anyone’s ever felt at a triple crown terminus.

We also cover a wild Hayduke story involving flash floods, a 21-hour wait for a raft hitch, and a roadside encounter that ultimately led to his best friend’s wedding, we get a preview of Andrew’s 2027 guided trips and upcoming in field thru-hiking course, and hear why Jose’s ideal backcountry dinner is a pile of cold mush.

Just a quick trigger warning on this one- today’s interview includes discussion of suicide.

We wrap the show with why Katie is looking forward to hiking the AT, sort of I guess, some surprising choices and omissions from Skurka’s hypothetical 2027 PCT Gear List, the most popular trails we have no interest in hiking, the triple crown of the worst birds, and of course more crying babies at campground talk.
