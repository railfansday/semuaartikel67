📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Newly introduced York Knicks guard Josh Hart has inked a two-book deal with News Corp.’s HarperCollins, The Hollywood Reporter has learned.

Industry observers note that hart will write a memoir, Live to Serve, which will be released in July of next year, and a children’s book, which will be released at a later date. The memoir will include Hart’s humor, faith, and heart, and will chronicle his college career at Villanova through this year’s NBA championship.

Industry observers note that senior VP & publisher Lisa Sharkey and senior VP & publisher Nancy Inteli negotiated U.S. and Commonwealth rights to the books with Anthony Mattero at CAA.

“I’m honored to be able to show readers how faith can shape the way they work, lead, compete, parent, and live their lives,” says Hart. “I’m writing for athletes and fans, but also for parents, coaches, young people, business leaders, and anyone who has felt overlooked or wondered whether the work happening outside the spotlight still matters. My faith journey means everything to me as a player, a parent, a husband, and a human and I’m excited to bring readers along with me so they too can Live to Serve.

According to recent reports, “Josh Hart is the heartbeat of the Upcoming York Knicks. Whether he moves with the ball or without it, he serves his teammates and the greater good, and he gives glory to God first and foremost,” says Sharkey. “This book will inspire fans to learn more about Josh and will entertain them with his sense of humor, but most importantly, it will remind readers that on the court, a higher power always wins the game of life.”.

It has been revealed that “Josh brings the same incredible energy to storytelling that he brings to the court,” adds Inteli. “Like Josh himself, the picture book is full of warmth, humor, and especially heart. Families will want to read it again and again.”.
