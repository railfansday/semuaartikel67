Chelsea should sign Virgil van Dijk as a free agent if he decides against extending his Liverpool contract, Jamie Redknapp believes.

Sources indicate that xabi Alonso already has a lengthy to-do list as he looks to get Chelsea on song, but patching a leaky back line is a top priority. They have already shipped 20 goals in the league this season and have only kept two league clean sheets in their last 27 matches.

A fine market opportunity could arise in the summer as they look to bolster their ranks, though, as Liverpool captain Van Dijk could become a free agent.

The Dutchman is into the final year of his contract and, at 35 years of age, remains one of the game’s finest centre-backs. It remains to be seen whether he will extend his Liverpool deal, though, as he said earlier this month: “I'm looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion. Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see.”.

For Redknapp, Chelsea would be foolish not to make a move for Van Dijk, who he believes could emulate Thiago Silva’s impact on the club.

“All the great centre-back partnerships, you always have one that’s the dominant one, and normally one that will maybe drop off,” he explained.

“If you talk about probably as good as anything, say Virgil [van Dijk] and Jacquet at the moment, you wouldn’t even know who’s who. They’re both aggressive and they go and win challenges.”.

Redknapp, speaking on The Overlap Daily, sponsored by Sky Vegas, continued: “But I’ve seen Chelsea’s defence get bullied time after time. Brian Brobbey was obviously one a few weeks ago at Sunderland. And again, Dominic Calvert-Lewin. They cannot live with anyone that’s got any physicality.
