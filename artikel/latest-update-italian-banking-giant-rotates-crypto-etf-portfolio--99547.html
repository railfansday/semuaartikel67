Latest Update: Italian Banking Giant Rotates Crypto ETF Portfolio From Bitcoin to Solana and XRP

🌐 Source: Zycrypto • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (387 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • It has been revealed that the bank’s disclosed crypto ETF holdings grew in Q3 2026 to $108.56 million, up $17 million, while digital asset prices moved sideways.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Italy’s largest banking group, Intesa Sanpaolo, increased its spot altcoin ETF holdings according to a latest disclosure. This follows capital rotation out of Bitcoin products by a slew of traditional firms amid dwindling market sentiment.

It has been revealed that the bank’s disclosed crypto ETF holdings grew in Q3 2026 to $108.56 million, up $17 million, while digital asset prices moved sideways. In June, total assets were worth $90 million as it looked to diversify and grow holdings systematically.

Bitcoin, the largest asset in the portfolio, was reduced to make room for altcoins in a possible bid to diversify assets. At the end of Q2, BTC holdings stood at $69.32 million but dropped slightly to above $61 million last quarter. The firm’s Ethereum exposure also dropped to $5 million.

In a recent development, however, Solana and XRP products filled the vacuum, highlighting institutional appetite for these assets. At the time of writing, the bank’s spot Solana ETF exposure is at $14.1 million after its investment in the Bitwise Solana Staking ETF ballooned from seven shares to 875,407 shares.

Intesa’s XRP investment also grew over the same period to $27.2 million, driven mainly by 402,750 shares in the Bitwise XRP ETF. The organization’s shares in the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF remained unchanged across both quarters.

Industry observers note that more broadly, the bank reduced its Bitcoin ETF exposure and increased SOL and XRP products. Most experts believe it follows the current market trend, and firms, especially traditional companies, need to diversify to make up for Bitcoin’s decline.

Sources indicate that historically, during bear phases, there’s a huge rotation of capital into altcoins, though it’s not sustained for long. As expected, Solana and XRP become institutional favorites, while Ethereum trades similarly to Bitcoin.

The former have the potential to record spikes within the red wave, like in 2022 before the approval of spot ETF products in the United States. These products opened up digital assets to a new stash of traditional capital.

Industry observers note that intesa and other banks have since increased their crypto exposure, driven by the need to diversify assets and a broader adoption spree. Heightened institutional and retail interests pushed Bitcoin to an all-time high above $125k last year. Right now, BTC trades at $63,819, while Ethereum is slightly below $1,800.
