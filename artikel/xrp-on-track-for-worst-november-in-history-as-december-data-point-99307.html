XRP has lost 25.4% so far in November 2026, according to CryptoRank. Before this year, the token's biggest November loss was 23.3% in 2019, according to CryptoRank data, and the token lost another 16.8% in December 2019. On Nov. 22, XRP traded near $0.90, according to TradingView.

According to recent reports, the token also lost 10% and 11.8% in November of 2021 and 2022, respectively. In November of 2023 and 2024, XRP gained 1.4% and 281.7%.

In November 2018, the crypto market crashed after the Bitcoin Cash hard fork, and XRP lost 19.3% that month. In December 2018, the token fell 2.25%. Two years earlier, XRP dropped 18% in November 2016 and 4.07% in December 2016.

In a recent development, XRP's median December return is -4.07%, and its average December return is +63.1%, according to CryptoRank data. Most of that average comes from December 2017, when XRP gained 818.9%.

Sources indicate that XRP has closed December in the red in eight of the past 13 years. In December of 2021, 2022 and 2025, the token lost 17%, 16.9% and 14.8%, respectively, and in December 2024, it gained 6.94%.

In 2020, XRP's December drop came as the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission sued Ripple on Dec. 22, 2020. The token lost 66.5% that month, and in January 2021, it gained 122.1%.

According to recent reports, in 2025, XRP's December drop came after the token set its all-time high of $3.65 on July 18, 2025. XRP lost 13.8% in November 2025 and 14.8% in December, and in January 2026, it fell another 10.6%.

According to recent reports, so far in 2026, XRP has lost value in seven of 11 months. The token's biggest monthly drops this year came in June and November, when it lost 22.1% and 25.4%, respectively, according to CryptoRank. Its best month was August, with a 30% gain.

On Nov. 17, U.S. spot XRP ETFs had $1.08 billion in assets, according to SoSoValue. Since the start of November, the funds have lost about $26 million to outflows, and $19.13 million of that amount left in the week ending Nov. 13. Ripple's next monthly escrow release of 1 billion XRP is scheduled for Dec. 1.

In a recent development, ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse also commented on the sell-off on CNBC on Nov. 19. "What happens is you've got risk-on assets and people are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included — Bitcoin, XRP — and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Anthropic IPO. What we saw after the SpaceX IPO was a rally in the crypto markets. I think you'll see that happen here as well," he mentioned.

XRP's November drop took the token to its lowest level in two years, according to TradingView data.
