Industry observers note that with 10 years of warfare under its belt, and many more if you count the rest of the Supremacy franchise, Supremacy: World War III has undoubtedly seen plenty of conflict. Now, developer Twin Harbour Interactive has unveiled their first ever collaboration, featuring none other than the forces of G.I. Joe!

If you're not familiar, G.I. Joe started life as a toy franchise during the plastic action figure craze of the 80s and 90s. Modelled after real-world special forces (and some fictional ones) the multinational forces of G.I. Joe did battle with the sinister, and far more stylised, terrorist army of Cobra.

For Supremacy: World War III it's a bit of a break from the norm considering the game has mostly hewed closely to a realistic interpretation of warfare. But, hey, it seems that nowadays everyone's being bitten by the collaboration bug, so who can blame them?
