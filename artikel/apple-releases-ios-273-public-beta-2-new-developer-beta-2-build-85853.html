📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that apple has released iOS 27.3 public beta 2 for iPhone. Today’s release also includes a new build for iOS 27.3 beta 2 for developers. The update brings new Calendar controls, more Siri AI capabilities, and new features for iPhone Duo. It also includes the redesigned Health app and other changes introduced earlier this month.

The revision follows yesterday’s second developer beta and the first public beta on November 5.

It has been revealed that apple has also detailed higher daily usage limits for Siri AI and other Apple Intelligence features through eligible iCloud+ prepares. Plans with 200GB or more include extra capacity; the 50GB blueprint keeps standard limits.

The first public beta brought back the redesigned Health app after its earlier preview during iOS 27.2 testing. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence support, Insights collects trends across your health data, while Longevity includes a Health Age comparison based on Apple Watch measurements.
