The FromSoft-inspired RPG Queen's Domain might have rolled out in one of the busiest periods of game releases in recent memory, but that's not to say nobody has been picking it up.

Indeed, the game has got off to a rather strong sales start, with developer Freshly Baked and publisher Future Friends confirming that the devilishly difficult indie has already surpassed 300,000 sales across all platforms.

Recent findings suggest that "We cannot believe we are reaching milestones like this," the dev shared in a celebratory post on Bluesky. "Thank you to everyone who has played, enjoyed and shared their Queen’s Domain journey".

The media landscape has been particularly preoccupied with the likes of Ocarina of Time and GTA 6 throughout November, but it seems that almost everyone who has played this one has fallen in love with its throwback visuals and classic King's Field vibes.

Industry observers note that we're certainly significant fans of it here at Nintendo Life, calling it "one of the best surprises of the year" in our 9/10 review. Here's hoping that its audience continues to grow in its second month on sale.
