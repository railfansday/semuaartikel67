We've all been wrecked by the global memory crisis, aka RAMageddon, this year. Hard drives, microSD cards, portable SSDs, and other tech essentials have been rapidly rising in price and there's no sign of that upward trajectory slowing any time soon. In fact, experts warn that things are going to get worse before they get better. Yikes.

Recent findings suggest that whether you've been wanting to build out your PC, secure offline data backups, or give your console a little breathing space, Black Friday is the best time to take advantage of portable SSD and hard drive deals. Compared to prices we saw in early November, these deals are considerably better. We'll be tracking all of the best SSD deals from brands like SanDisk, Lexar, Samsung, Western Digital (WD_Black), and beyond leading up to and throughout Cyber Week.
