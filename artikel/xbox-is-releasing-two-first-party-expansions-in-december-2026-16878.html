December is often a very quiet month for new game releases, and that's particularly going to be the case in 2026 due to GTA 6's arrival and the desire from publishers to get as far away from it as possible, but we've still got the likes of Rayman Legends Retold and Stage Tour to look forward to, along with a couple of first-party Xbox expansions.

Recent findings suggest that in terms of first-party stuff, Xbox is initially releasing a paid DLC pack for Minecraft Dungeons 2 on December 1st called The Humblers Awaken. This DLC is included with the Deluxe Edition of the game if you bought it, and it'll be available separately as well.

You can expect "new quests, unexplored environments, colorful cosmetics, and an expanded arsenal of powerful gear" in The Humblers Awaken, while base game players will also be treated to a free upgrade on the same day, adding in new challenges, weapons and armour.

Then, just a day later, Xbox and Obsidian are releasing a first expansion for The Outer Worlds 2, known as Side Effects Guaranteed. This DLC includes a newly introduced narrative story (that'll be concluded in the second part next year), a new region of Elysium Valley to explore, new threats, new perks and an increased level cap.

There's also newly introduced weapons, armours, outfits, flaws, a new radio station - you get the picture!

Side Effects Guaranteed will cost $14.99 USD when it arrives on December 2nd, or you'll get it included if you bought either the Premium Edition or Premium Upgrade Edition of The Outer Worlds 2.

Perhaps there's a chance we might get even more from Xbox's first-party studios in December, but as mentioned, it's typically a month where the releases die down. In any case, it's nice to see two expansions on the way very soon!
