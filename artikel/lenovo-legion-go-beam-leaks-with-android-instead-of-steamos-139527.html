The variety of gaming handhelds from Lenovo is about to get larger. Leaked details suggest the Lenovo Legion Go Beam will run Android instead of SteamOS on a high-refresh display.

Lenovo is sticking with the Legion Go branding to offer something a little different to consumers. Instead of a SteamOS or Windows device, the Legion Go Beam is noted to run Android (via Windows Latest). Specifically, Android 16.

That doesn’t limit it to Android titles alone. Android happens to make the perfect platform for cloud game streaming. Services like GeForce Now, Xbox Game Pass, and more offer a comparable experience to what a PC does for a fixed subscription, and that’s without the hardware internals something like the Legion Go 2 would need to run games natively.

The leak also notes the Lenovo Legion Go Beam will have a 7-inch 120Hz display, though there’s no information on what resolution or panel type the handheld might have. It’s likely 1080p or higher, though a lower resolution obviously will net users better performance. Battery is up in the air, too, other than a standard “long-lasting battery life” claim attributed to Lenovo.

This handheld takes a lot from the Legion Go 2 in design, as it should. It’s a comfortable device, and this shouldn’t be any different. It lacks a trackpad in favor of a traditional controller layout. It also has standard ports like one USB-C and 3.5mm headphone jack.

It has been revealed that pricing is a mystery. Devices like the Logitech G Cloud were such a hit because they delivered the needed performance at a good price. But those handhelds had a lower refresh rate. Still, hoping for a price tag around $350 or less isn’t such a bad thing.
