According to recent reports, liverpool would have a €23m offer accepted if they make it. The Reds are weighing up a deal that could help transform their season.

Do Liverpool need a upcoming left-back? Really, the question is 'when' - everyone expects Kostas Tsimikas to leave the club at the end of the season when his contract expires.

So it's a matter of what kind of replacement Liverpool opt for. Do they simply want to find a backup player to Milos Kerkez? Or is it a chance to replace him?

According to recent reports, there are other possibilities here, too. Liverpool could take advantage of the squad space to find a versatile player who fills a few roles. Or perhaps they want an experienced player who can fit into the leadership group.

Industry observers note that then there's the homegrown question. Liverpool could make things easier for themselves by adding a couple of homegrown players and here's a good opportunity to add one. Should they wish.

Or maybe, above all, they just want an elite left-back who could potentially replace Kerkez as first-choice.

Turkish outlet Takvim reports that Liverpool are in the running for Fenerbahçe left-back Archie Brown. The 24-year-old is wanted by several Premier League clubs and for good reason - he's homegrown.

Brown made a significant impact against Liverpool this season, of course, as he both scored and assisted in his side's 3-2 win over the Reds. It's something that's catapulted Brown into the spotlight as one to watch, particularly for Premier League sides.

Recent findings suggest that the interest is significant enough that we can't imagine the player moves anywhere where he isn't first choice. He's set to have his pick of clubs, too, as a release clause in his contract guarantees that the fee won't get out of hand.

That price is €23m, which is arguably a complete steal for a UEFA Champions League-level left-back right now. Especially one who's English.

It has been revealed that we expect Brown to be one of Liverpool's most-wanted in 2027 and there's half a chance that they get the deal done in January. After all, that clause won't be around forever - someone is going to trigger it sooner or later. Another club, then, could force the Reds' hand by making a winter offer.

Absolutely, then, this is one to watch.
