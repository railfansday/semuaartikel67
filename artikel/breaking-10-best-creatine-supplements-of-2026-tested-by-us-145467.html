Sources indicate that CREATINE SUPPLEMENTS CAN be an effective way to tap into more power during explosive exercise and build strength. Aside from protein powder, the best creatine supplements are the only supplements that can actually help you improve your fitness.

Industry observers note that when you consume creatine (by way of supplements or real food), it turns into creatine phosphate in your muscles. This process helps create a key energy source called adenosine triphosphate, which is what your body uses for quick bursts of energy during intense workouts. In short, consuming creatine helps your muscles produce energy faster, and that extra energy lets you do more intense exercises, helping your muscles to grow bigger and stronger.
