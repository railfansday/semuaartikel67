Jack O’Connell is being talked about as a Bond villain, but Danny Boyle states he could have been much more than that.

Boyle, who directed O’Connell in The Sun newspaper movie Ink, was speaking on the Alchemy with Anthony Mason podcast when he made the comment. The conversation turned to the skills of O’Connell and Robert Pattinson, the fresh James Bond, prompting Boyle to discuss the next 007 movie.

There were some rumors that O’Connell was among those considered for the plum role before Twilight and Dune star Pattinson was selected by Amazon.
