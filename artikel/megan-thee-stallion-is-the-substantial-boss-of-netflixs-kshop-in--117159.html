This is not your regularly store, and these aren’t your typical buyers.

A creation of Netflix’s Brand Creative Studio, the mini-episode and others will roll out on the KShopDemonHunters Instagram channel. Your precious KShop merch awaits at the online stores of Kayou, Mattel, Hasbro, Vans and LEGO.

The pairing of Megan Thee Stallion with KPop Demon Hunters should be a winning combination. Megan is a star in her game with three career No. 1s on the Billboard Hot 100, and 45 total tracks on the singles chart. Her collection of accolades include four American Music Awards, four MTV Video Music Awards, three Grammy Awards, a Billboard Women in Music Award, and, in 2019, the Powerhouse Award at the 2019 Billboard Women in Music ceremony.

It has been revealed that the Houston Hottie has been heating up ahead of her Act III album. She’s dropped a trio of Tina Tuesday Freestyles, including the standout “Thin Ice,” which reached a upcoming peak of No. 64 on the Billboard Hot 100 in its second week on the chart. And keep an eye out for her return as Denise for the season 2 finale of The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins on NBC, which will beam out sometime in early 2027.

In a recent development, since it premiered on Netflix in June 2025, KPop Demon Hunters has crushed records for fun. Following the fictional girl group HUNTR/X as its members Rumi, Mira and Zoey bring the fight to the evil demons of this world, the animated film and its official soundtrack has been a monster hit.

Just two months after it premiered, KPop Demon Hunters became Netflix’s most-watched film of all time, surpassing 236 million total views. Its standout song “Golden” spent eight weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, and is one of four original songs from the film to crack the top 10 on the Billboard Hot 100, a first for a soundtrack. Then, at the February 2026 Grammy Awards, “Golden” made history as the first K-pop song to win a golden gramophone, taking home best song written for visual media.

Sources indicate that the following month, in March 2026, Billboard announced that HUNTR/X singers EJAE, AUDREY NUNA and REI AMI would be sharing the title of Woman of the Year at the Women in Music Awards, making them the first group to collectively win the honor. In the same month, “Golden” became the first K-pop track to ever win an Oscar, doing so for best original song. “Golden” previously won the same category at both the Golden Globes and Critic’s Choice Awards.
