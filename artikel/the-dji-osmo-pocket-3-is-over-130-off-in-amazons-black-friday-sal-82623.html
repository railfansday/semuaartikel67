Industry observers note that despite its small size, the DJI Osmo Pocket 3 has plenty going for it. It features a 1-inch CMOS sensor and can shoot 4K video at up to 120 fps. Plus, it has built-in 3-axis mechanical stabilization that helps keep footage smooth, even when you're filming on the move. There's also a rotating 2-inch touchscreen, so you can easily switch between filming horizontal videos and face-to-camera content for social media.
