Unai Emery explained the reasons for his premature departure towards the Old Trafford tunnel this Sunday, during the spectacular draw between Manchester United and Aston Villa (4-4). While Patrick Dorgu had just equalized in the 96th minute, the Spanish coach was seen returning to the locker room even before the final whistle. A scene which raised many questions, while the Villans had just let slip a two-goal lead.

Industry observers note that questioned after the meeting, Emery assured that his gesture was not linked to any reaction of anger or annoyance, but simply to confusion about the remaining time. “No emotion. They scored in the 96th minute and there were six minutes of added time. I thought the match was over.”he explained. Faced with the insistence of the journalist Sky Sportsthe technician even ended up getting annoyed: “They scored the goal in the 96th minute. You asked me why I left before. Because the 96th minute and 30 seconds, that’s when we kicked off. My English is very bad, but is it clear or not? ».
