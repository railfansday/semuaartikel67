📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Apple has just released beta 2 for iPadOS 27.3, watchOS 27.3, visionOS 27.3, and more. It’s available for developers, but a public beta will likely follow in the next couple days.

Two weeks ago Apple introduced an exciting wave of beta updates as iOS 27.3 and companion releases introduced many newly introduced features.

It has been revealed that siri AI upgrades are a substantial focus in 27.3, with fresh languages, Siri Recap, and advanced in-app actions. There’s also a preview of new third-party app capabilities for Siri.

The all-newly introduced Health app has returned in iOS and iPadOS 27.3, this time with the expectation of launching in the public release. Fitness also adds a new Gym Strength program.

Apple Music now has a Selfie Sing feature for creating personal karaoke videos, and an improved lyrics view. Safari adds Vertical Tabs, Adaptive Dark Mode, and more.

Sources indicate that that’s just a sampling of the fresh features in beta 1. And now today, beta 2 is here for iPadOS 27.3, watchOS 27.3, visionOS 27.3, and more.

Industry observers note that we’re expecting to see additional upcoming features and refinements today, like video podcasts being added to the Apple TV app. We’ll keep you posted on everything noteworthy we discover.

watchOS 27.3 has one of my personal favorite fresh features in Siri Recap, so I’m curious to see if Apple keeps refining the experience throughout the beta in response to user feedback.
