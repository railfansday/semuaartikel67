Taco Bell is turning up the heat with a new ‘Make It Flamin’ Hot’ customization option that lets customers add spicy Fritos to some of their favorite menu items.

The fast-food giant has announced a latest limited-time feature that allows customers to upgrade eligible orders with Fritos Flamin’ Hot chips, adding an extra layer of spice and crunch.

Starting November 24, the customization will be available at participating locations through December 9, giving customers the chance to experiment with some fiery combinations.

According to Taco Bell’s official announcement, customers can add the chips to popular items including the Crunchwrap Supreme, Cantina Chicken Rolled Quesadilla, and the returning Naked Chicken Chalupa.

The announcement follows Taco Bell’s recent introduction of the Flamin’ Hot Grilled Cheese Burrito and Flamin’ Hot Mini Taco Salad, the latter of which was first revealed alongside more than 20 new products earlier in 2026.

“When Taco Bell fans love a menu item, they find ways to make it their own,” remarked Liz Matthews, Taco Bell’s Global Chief Food Innovation Officer.

“That creativity constantly inspires us, and Make It FLAMIN’ HOT is our way of putting some of that Test Kitchen magic directly into their hands.”.

Customers can access the latest customization through Taco Bell’s mobile app, website, self-service kiosks, or by ordering at participating restaurants.

Simply select an eligible menu item, choose the “Customize” or “Modify” option, and add Fritos Flamin’ Hot from the available toppings.

According to recent reports, however, there are some restrictions. Crunchy tacos, the Cheesy Gordita Crunch, a la carte Nacho Fries, beverages, and desserts are excluded from the promotion.

The spicy upgrade also comes at an additional cost, although Taco Bell has not announced a standard price for the customization.

The option will also be available through participating third-party delivery services, with prices potentially higher for delivery orders.

The promotion runs from November 24 through December 9, while supplies last, giving fans just over two weeks to create their own Flamin’ Hot combinations.
