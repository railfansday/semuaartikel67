The real reason why United States women's national soccer team star Trinity Rodman and tennis ace Ben Shelton split up has emerged following their high-profile break-up.

Rodman, daughter of NBA legend Dennis Rodman, and Shelton went public with their relationship in March 2025 after rumours sparked on social media.

They became a sporting power couple and frequently supported one another at various events, including Shelton's impressive run at this year's US Open.

He reached the first Grand Slam final of his budding career, beating the likes of Carlos Alcaraz and fellow countryman Frances Tiafoe. However, he fell short in his bid to become US Open champion following his defeat to Alexander Zverev in four sets.

The 24-year-old forward was captured crying inside Arthur Ashe Stadium following the world No.4's crushing defeat to Zverev, who was accused of flirting with her in his post-match victory speech.

After nearly two years together, Rodman and Shelton have split up. That's according to The Daily Mail, who have revealed they have recently decided to call time on their blossoming relationship.

Per the report, their conflicting work schedules and constant travel made it 'too challenging for the couple to continue maintaining their relationship', given Shelton is traveling around the world while playing on the ATP Tour, with his latest venture taking him to Turin, Italy, for the ATP Finals, where he lost to Alcaraz in the semi-finals.

Rodman was also in action the same evening as the Washington Spirit lost the NWSL final to the KC Current.

They attempted to make it work, with multiple sacrifices and last-minute trips to attend each other's matches, but it appears the strain has taken its toll, resulting in their split.

Despite ending their romance, Rodman and Shelton are believed to remain close.

Shelton also travelled long distances to support Rodman during their relationship, including the 7,300-mile trip from Shanghai to Chester, Pennsylvania, for USWNT's game against Spain last month.

A month prior to watching Rodman compete for her national team, Shelton opened up on the challenges of their relationship, revealing that it was tough for them to continuously make time for one another due to their busy workload.

Speaking to US Weekly, he admitted: "It is really difficult.

"I think what a lot of people don't see is when we actually do have time or a couple of days off, we're so exhausted that it's tough to do anything. It is tough to make time.".

The 24-year-old added: "Sometimes I leave these tournaments and I'm a bit of a zombie. I'm not the most fun person to be around. But we make a lot of sacrifices for our sport. I'd say both of us are chasing things for our sport. We're not where we want to be yet, we're not in our primes. So this is kind of crunch time.

"This is going to be the hardest, most grueling time in our relationship, from my perspective. None of it is easy. But being able to have someone to lean on who's such an elite athlete and doing things at a really high level as well, it helps in so many ways.".
