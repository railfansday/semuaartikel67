Skate, the free-to-play skateboarding game that's still in Early Access, is ditching its seasonal approach moving forward, publisher EA has confirmed.

Recent findings suggest that the always-online, shared world game has been getting fairly regular updates since its launch back in September 2025, and it's all been modelled around Seasons. Following the next patch — 0.33, launching 1st December — this system will be dropped.

No more Seasons also means no more Skate Passes or Tix; instead, the development team is prioritising "regular, agile content updates", saying it'll be able to launch features earlier and get active feedback from players:.

Sources indicate that "Moving away from rigid season blocks gives the development team the freedom to launch features earlier in their lifecycle. This means we can deploy work-in-progress mechanics and gather your direct feedback to continuously shape and build upon San Vansterdam. We’ve started this with in-game testing of features such as Spot Battles, Block Parties, and Prox Chat before rolling it out officially, and we’re going to continue with this process to ensure we get YOUR feedback on what works/what doesn’t.".

On top of this incoming change, the game's next update is a fairly hefty one:.

The competitive game mode S.K.A.T.E. is being added, with various spots on the map where you can play, or you can use the Throwdown menu to create your own challenges.

Another major feature in this enhancement is (optional) proximity chat, which will be available on all maps in the game.

For those that don't know, proximity chat is a version of voice chat that means you can only hear players that are close by.

Version 0.33 is also adding and updating locations in San Vansterdam, such as the RINQ Hotel, the Loading Bay of The Vault, and Pump House Park on the Isle of Grom.

On top of that, the upgrade introduces upcoming cosmetics, music, and more.

According to recent reports, it seems there's a good amount to check out in the upcoming update. It'll be interesting to see how the game evolves now that it's discontinuing Seasons.
