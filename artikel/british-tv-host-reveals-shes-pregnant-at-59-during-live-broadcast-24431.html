In a recent development, this just in: Dr. Renée Hoenderkamp is pregnant at 59.

The British talk show anchor of GB News broke the news during a live broadcast that left her co-hosts stunned into silence.

As Hoenderkamp said in part during the Nov. 14 show, “I’m a mother of a grown-up boy and a very young girl. And I have some news to break to the panel today that they’re not aware of or anybody else for that matter: I’m actually going to be a mother of three shortly.”.

Currently 12-weeks pregnant with a baby girl, the general practitioner is also mom to 37-year-old son Calum and 8-year-old daughter Alice.

Later in the show, Hoenderkamp explained how her fertility process began 18 months ago with the thought that she’d “like to do it again,” and eventually led her to travel to Poland to have her previously frozen eggs implanted.

But while the panelist is thrilled by her pregnancy news, she also detailed the commentary she previously faced for having her daughter at 50.

In a recent development, “I had criticism that I was selfish. What if I died in five years?” Hoenderkamp shared. “Well, you know I keep myself very fit, as you know. So hopefully that won’t happen, but it might.”.

It has been revealed that still, she added, “My point is this: Alice has a wonderful life. She adores her life and I hope this child will be the same. What would be better? That they were never given the chance of life?”.

Plus, Hoenderkamp—who will be 60 when her daughter arrives—sees her latest baby as an opportunity to excel at parenthood the way she couldn’t do with her first child.

“At 21, I had youth on my side, but far less understanding of what motherhood required of me,” she wrote in an essay published in Daily Mail Nov. 17. “Nearly four decades later, while I have fewer years ahead of me, I also have the wisdom, patience and freedom to make the most of every moment.”.

According to recent reports, as Hoenderkamp sees it, “My new daughter will arrive into a family where she is desperately wanted, surrounded by people who already love her. In a world where much of our news is cause for enormous gloom, isn’t that something to celebrate?”.
