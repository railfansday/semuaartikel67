Patch [Tue 24th Nov 2026, 2:45pm]:.

Sources indicate that A few weeks ago, we caught wind of a mysterious countdown taking place on The Pokémon Manufacturer's official social media accounts, marking the hours remaining until 30th November at 2pm UTC. We wondered whether it was all for a surprise eShop drop, a Pokémon Presents or a new Winds & Waves trailer, but it looks like it might all be for something much more disappointing.

As pointed out by Serebii, several of the official Pokémon game social media accounts have begun promoting an upcoming Pokémon music video for a track called 'Evergreen', which will drop on YouTube at, you guessed it, 2pm UTC on 30th November.

Recent findings suggest that it could be that this track is part of something bigger — a Presents segment, or maybe some Gen 10 news — but there's a big part of us now that thinks the countdown was focused on this alone which... we should have tempered our expectations for, to be honest.

The official Pokémon X account even shared a trailer for the music video, and attached another of the countdown images in the replies thread, noting that there were 151 hours to go. So yes, it certainly looks like this is what all the mystery was about.

*Sigh* We'll pop our tinfoil hats away for now, then...

Original Story: The Pokémon Firm is no stranger to a little over-hyping every now and then, but its latest bit of marketing has us seriously thinking that there is a substantial announcement in the pipeline.

In a recent development, you see, TPC is counting down to... something. As brought to our attention by Serebii's Joe Merrick, the tech giant has been sharing images on social media of its 30th anniversary icons, with a number underneath. Interestingly, these numbers decrease by the number of hours that have passed between each post — 1,025 on 18th October, 843 on 26th, 676 today.

Keeping the countdown going, this means that we'll hit zero on 30th November at 2pm UTC (thanks for doing the maths there, Joe!). The latest post even came with a full message under the logo, roughly translating to "Continue with Pokémon".

Hmm. The big question is, what's this all about? 2pm UTC sure feels like the right time for a big announcement (it's when most eyes, worldwide, will be on it), and we can't imagine that TPC would put this much planning into something inconsequential.

Sources indicate that what is it, then? Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire were rumoured to be coming to NSO last month, could it be that? Is it time we got another look at Winds and Waves, perhaps with a firm release date attached? Is there another full Pokémon Presents around the corner? Only time will tell, but it sure is an exciting prospect.

We'll be keeping an eye on the countdown throughout November and will let you know when any major news comes out of it.
