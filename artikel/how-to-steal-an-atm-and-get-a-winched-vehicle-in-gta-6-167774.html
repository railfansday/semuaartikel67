Stealing an ATM in GTA 6 requires a few different things: a winched vehicle to haul it out of the wall and drive off, plus the dye pack jammer that stops marked bills from exploding pink all over your lovely cash.

There are two ways to get a winched truck. The video above demonstrates a location at the crop duster landing strip in Frogswater where you can steal one, but you can also mod any truck at a mod shop to add a winch.

If you don't have a truck to mod, there's a unique one you can steal from the Kolt & Associates June Warehouse robbery in Port Gellhorn, just south of Sarena's Bargain Store, which you could then register with Moses and mod at Wasteland Off-Road in the north of the town. You can also get a winched truck around the back of Yasmani's vape shop in Little Cuba—the fence who gives you the ATM tip offs.

According to recent reports, you'll also need the dye pack jammer, which you get in the Chapter 3 main mission, Credit Check, for Raul. Once you have both of these, you're ready to rob an ATM. If you complete all three of Yasmani's jewelry thefts (also in Chapter 3), he'll call a little later and offer tips about full ATMs, giving you three for free that you have a 24 hour window to hit. Here's how to steal them.

For Yasmani's tipped-off ATMs I got about 11k-ish per time, so not terrible, but likely not the best way to make money unless you get really efficient at it and invest in a proper getaway winch truck.
