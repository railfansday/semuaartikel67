📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas have made it to the semifinals on Dancing With the Stars, but it’s been a tough week of rehearsals for Maura thanks to a persistent rib injury. Maura opened up about her rib pain last week, and apparently the situation hasn’t improved. In fact, she’s reportedly had to postpone multiple rehearsals this week—including one right before semifinals.

“She reinjured the rib over the weekend and had to postpone another rehearsal and get looked at by medics again,” a source tells The Sun. “She’s trying to give it her all but giving it her all is costing her physically. Her body is hanging on by a thread but she’s more determined than ever to push through.”.

So, will Maura be able to dance in the finals next week (assuming she and Mark score well and get the necessary votes)? She plans to push through.

Sources indicate that “She knows after this dance she only has one more week left and she thinks she can do it,” notes the insider. “So she’s still in it to win it. She wants it for herself but I think she wants the trophy for Mark even more because she knows how much it would mean to him and they’ve gotten so close this past year. But she does want it for herself, both because she came in not being a dancer and genuinely thinking she’d be an early boot so getting this far would mean a ton to her. Plus she knows the career opportunities that would come from it would be incredible.”.

Here’s to hoping Maura gets a chance to rest and heal between episodes!
