📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hulu's long-promised integration with Disney+ is nigh.

Sources indicate that the subscription streaming platform has a robust library of original content, including the Emmy-winning dystopian drama The Handmaid's Tale, the mega-popular comedy whodunit Only Murders in the Building, the Predator prequel Prey, and the 2022 horror reboot Hellraiser, as well as prestige series from FX on Hulu such as The Bear and Shōgun. Disney acquired a majority stake in Hulu in 2019 and closed deals to acquire the other ownership interests in 2025. Since 2024, they have been integrating Hulu content more and more into the proprietary service Disney+ while maintaining both platforms as largely separate entities in the U.S.

According to recent reports, they added that "we are also adding more vertical video, live sports and podcasts to the [Disney+] platform, while continuing to explore other emerging content genres.” In December, this will include content from TikTok creators that is "strategically aligned" with the release of the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday on December 18. This is part of Disney's recent deal with the social video platform.

Avengers: Doomsday is the first Avengers teamup film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe since 2019's Avengers: Endgame, which is the highest-grossing movie of all time.

In a recent development, the addition of more vertical content to Disney+ is part of a content expansion blueprint that has been taking place for some time. In 2026, they launched a series of Locker Diaries shorts, which followed interactions between characters from some of their most popular brands, as viewed from inside an open locker in a school hallway (thus mimicking the typical vertical frame of a TikTok video). This series included characters from across the Disney+ spectrum, including the musical franchises Z-O-M-B-I-E-S, Camp Rock, and Descendants, the animated series Phineas and Ferb, and the upcoming supernatural teen drama Coven Academy.

There is some amount of overlap between the casts of the Locker Diaries shorts, particularly when it comes to Malachi Barton, who plays characters in Z-O-M-B-I-E-S, Camp Rock, and Coven Academy. Below, see a breakdown of the actors who play multiple characters in the vertical series:.

Z-O-M-B-I-E-S (Victor, 3 episodes), Camp Rock (Fletch, 3 episodes), Coven Academy (Jake, 2 episodes).

Z-O-M-B-I-E-S (Vera, 7 episodes), Coven Academy (McKenna, 2 episodes).

Descendants (Pink, 5 episodes), Camp Rock (Sage, 7 episodes).

It ultimately remains to be seen if and when Hulu folds entirely into Disney+ in the United States. However, that is the only major region where it is still a standalone service. At the time of writing, it exists solely as a content hub on Disney+ in territories including Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, Canada, the Middle East, and North and South Africa.
