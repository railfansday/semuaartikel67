It has been revealed that bayern Munich’s Harry Kane became the first Bundesliga player to win the Ballon d’Or in 30 years, lifting the prestigious trophy ahead of Real Madrid’s Kylian Mbappé and Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal. Speaking exclusively to t-online, Bayern president Herbert Hainer discussed the significance of Kane claiming the coveted prize, awarded to the world’s best footballer, for both him and Germany’s record champions.

“The significance of this cannot be overstated, both for Harry Kane and for Bayern and the entire Bundesliga,” explained Hainer. “We have the World Player of the Year in Germany, that’s an international exclamation mark. On Bayern’s club media channels, we shattered all previous records in terms of reach that day.

“Harry has developed even further here. For him, it wasn’t just an award for one season, but for his achievements over the past few years – as a footballer and as a person. He’s a role model off the pitch as well. Furthermore, he’s shown that at Bayern, you can achieve anything individually.”.

Kane returned 73 games across all competitions for both Bayern and England last season, and he’s already in unstoppable form this term, plundering 10 goals and providing a further 10 assists in 15 appearances across all competitions for die Rekordmeister.
