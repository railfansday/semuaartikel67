The 3 Body Problem season two trailer is here and it features time jumps, alien threats, outer-space action and zero-gravity sex.

In a recent development, below is the first look at the return of Netflix‘s adaptation of Liu Cixin’s acclaimed novels. The tagline for the new season is “They Are Here.”.

Sources indicate that the show from showrunners David Benioff, D.B. Weiss and Alexander Woo also has an official description: “The alien invasion nears and humanity prepares to meet them — on Earth and beyond. While a space fleet embarks on a mission that spans centuries, a secret locked inside of one man’s mind might be humanity’s only hope for survival.” As one character warns in the trailer, “They are coming to exterminate us.” Related Stories TV Netflix's '3 Body Problem' Gets Season 2 Premiere Date, Trailer Coming Tomorrow Movies Netflix's Next 'Untold' About NY Giants' Super Bowl Win vs. Undefeated Patriots Arrives in Time for Xmas (Exclusive).

The show is set to return on Jan. 14.

The second season is reportedly shorter than the first, with Netflix confirming it will have six episodes instead of eight. Two years ago, Netflix renewed the series for second and third final seasons which will complete the adaptation.

The season has two directors familiar to Game of Thrones fans: Jeremy Podeswa helms the first half of the season and Miguel Sapochnik directs the second half.

Industry observers note that the first season spent seven weeks in the streamer’s global Top 10 list, reaching the Top 10 in 93 countries and hitting No. 1 in 44 countries, with 46.6 million views.

The cast of 3 Body Problem season two includes returning stars Jess Hong, Benedict Wong, Jovan Adepo, Eiza González, Liam Cunningham, Marlo Kelly, Sea Shimooka, Saamer Usmani, Alex Sharp and Josh Brener. Newly introduced cast members include Claudia Doumit, Ellie de Lange, Alfie Allen, David Yip and Jordan Sunshine.
