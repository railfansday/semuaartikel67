William Saliba could make his first start of the season for Arsenal against Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League.

The French defender had a long spell on the sidelines after aggravating a back injury at the World Cup over the summer.

Saliba did not require surgery but a significant spell of rest was needed and he is yet to play a single first-team minute so far this season.

He played the first half for Arsenal’s Under-21s in their PL2 win over Aston Villa before the international break and then featured in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Watford.

Having come through those games without any problems, Saliba is in contention for a first-team return and he could be brought in against Dortmund on Tuesday night.

Goalkeeper David Raya will miss the match with a shoulder injury suffered late on during Saturday’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle in the Premier League and deputy Kepa Arrizabalaga is expected to start in his place, over Illan Meslier.

Ben White could get the nod at right-back, as he continues to share minutes with Jurrien Timber, while Myles Lewis-Skelly will hope to get his opportunity in midfield.

Arsenal have a huge Premier League match at home to Manchester City to come on Sunday but Bundesliga leaders Dortmund represent tough opposition and manager Mikel Arteta is likely to go strong with his team selection.

That would mean more minutes in midfield for Declan Rice and captain Martin Odegaard, with Martin Zubimendi still sidelined, while Noni Madueke has become an essential part of the attack on the right wing in the absence of Bukayo Saka.

Up front, Viktor Gyokeres' chances to start have largely come in the Champions League this season and it would be no surprise if he was chosen to lead the line against Dortmund, ahead of Kai Havertz.
