Just as Peretz's brilliant save from Tahith Chong was undone by Sydie Peck beating him in the 90th minute, so it looked like the midfielder's 90th-minute goal and Jordan Ayew's brilliance to create it might have been in vain when Finn Azaz played Samuel Edozie through in stoppage time.

But Cooper came out on top in the one-on-one to earn his side a 1-1 draw against opponents who completely outplayed the Blades in the first half-hour.

If only they had played the first half as they had the second, perhaps they might have been celebrating victory rather than a point which felt like one, thanks to the timing of Peck's goal.

If the first half highlighted how short the Blades are of the very best in the division, the second showed what they have in their locker to try to do something about it.

Southampton will have walked off more relieved to take the point than the home team.

The Saints took the lead in the 11th minute when Hamza Choudhury gave the ball away and was unable to close Lewis Dobbin down before he curled a beautiful shot inside the far post.

Already by then it was on the cards, with Saints dominating possession as James Ward-Prowse pulled the strings in midfield.

The only saving grace is that the Blades only trailed 1-0 at the break.

Mads Roerslev had a shot blocked and Kuryu Matsuki thrashed an effort into the side netting from a tight angle.

The Blades did at least improve as half-time approached, Mark McGuinness heading a Peck corner, and Romelle Donovan - switched to the right - came inside and shot at the goalkeeper.

With Patrick Bamford introduced at the break, the Blades were much sharper in the second half.

Peck's shot from distance in the 50th minute indicated ambition, if not accuracy.

A couple of minutes later, Bamford headed over at a corner nodded to him by Mark McGuinness. Next was Ayew, who headed at Daniel Peterz after Donovan got to the byline to tee the chance up.

Ayew was released down the left but delayed too long, and could only force a corner from a tight angle.

Donovan had a penalty appeal waved away, perhaps not helped by the fact he was clearly looking for it.

But the football was as one-way as the first half had been, only with more chances to show from it.

Chong was unable to keep his shot down when the ball dropped to him 16 yards out, and Harrison Burrows had an effort blocked.

Southampton briefly provided some variety in the 80th minute when Donovan blocked Cyle Larin and Ward-Prowse ballooned the follow-up over, but the play swiftly moved back to the Kop End.

Chong's cross went all the way through to Burrows at the back post, but he could only loop his header up for Peretz to catch.

Industry observers note that but the gilt-edged chance fell to Chong in the 83rd after a lovely move through Ayes and Bamford. The winger steered his shot into the right place, but Peretz stretched out brilliantly to his right to save it.

When that was squandered, it felt like the Blades might run out of time.

But in the 90th minute Ayew did brilliantly to dribble along the byline and pull the ball back for Peck to find the net through a busy penalty area.

It could have counted for nought had Cooper not intervened in the 95th minute.

As saves go, it was a pretty major one.

Sheffield United: Cooper; Choudhury (McCallum 87), McGuinness, Kitching, Burrows; Chong (Campbell 87), Peck, Arblaster (Phillips 69), Donovan; Cannon (Bamford HT), Ayew (Sidibe 90+3).

Unused substitutes: Davies, Norrington-Davies, Baptiste, Riedewald.

Southampton: Peretz; Roerslev (Bree 59), Abbott, Wood, Welington; Bragg (Azaz 59); Downes, Ward-Prowse; Matsuki (Stephens 87), Mubama (Larin 59), Dobbin (Edozie 70).

Unused substitutes: Manning, Fellows, Long, Williams.
