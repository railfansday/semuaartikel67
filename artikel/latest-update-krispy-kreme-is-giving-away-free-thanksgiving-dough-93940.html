TL;DR: Krispy Kreme has two Thanksgiving doughnut deals running right now, including a $1 Original Glazed dozen and a freebie that runs through Nov. 23.

According to recent reports, does your Monday morning WFH routine need an immediate morale boost before Thanksgiving travel chaos completely derails your week? Krispy Kreme is quietly running a free promotion that's worth the detour.

It has been revealed that through Nov. 23, Krispy Kreme rewards members (it's free to join) can score one free Thanksgiving collection doughnut with any purchase at participating U.S. shops and online. And if you miss today’s deadline, Krispy Kreme is also offering a $1 Original Glazed Dozen when you buy any dozen through Nov. 29 in the app.

You can choose from four seasonal flavors: Cinnamon Apple Filled, Double Chocolate Crumble, Caramel Pumpkin, or Berry Cobbler.
