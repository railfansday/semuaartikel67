Rasmus Hojlund has laid bare the suffocating pressure of playing for Manchester United and given his insight into what Mauricio Pochettino’s squad will be suffering while marooned in the Premier League relegation zone.

The Dane was back in town this week after spending two seasons at United, mocking Manchester City fans when scoring the opener of Napoli’s 2-2 draw at the Etihad Stadium on Tuesday night.

Hojlund secured a £38million permanent move away from Old Trafford in the summer but remains in touch with plenty of old team-mates and believes the lack of equilibrium at the club has contributed to a steady decline.

‘There are so many people following the club around the world and you can’t really escape from it,’ Hojlund said.

‘It is almost too high when it is good and almost too low when it is bad. It is all about trying to be in that middle. I had both the highs and the lows there.

‘The pressure is part of the club, it comes with playing for United. There has been a lot of change but hopefully they will get it right with Mauricio.’.

Rasmus Hojlund mocked Man City fans after scoring for Napoli at the Etihad on Tuesday night.

A United supporter, Hojlund laughed off the idea that the club is in a relegation scrap despite their worst start to a season since 1972. And the 23-year-old revealed that the players were ready for ‘change’ following the sacking of Michael Carrick.

‘I spoke with Bruno [Fernandes] and Diogo [Dalot] during the international break and they were all obviously devastated that Michael was going but also looking forward to some change,’ Hojlund added.

Recent findings suggest that ‘I am still speaking with Patrick [Dorgu] and I just wish the guys all the best. I am sure they will turn it around this season. They have got a lot of quality. They have got a great squad, good leaders, good professionals.

According to recent reports, ‘It is a temporary picture, I think they will get back to the top. The Premier League is like this. I read the other day that Bournemouth was in the relegation zone after a couple of games, now they have won the last five games and are in the top half of the table.’.
