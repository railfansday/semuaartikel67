Grand Theft Auto 6 features the first non-binary character in the series' history, according to actor Jesse James Rose, who plays the role of a museum curator in the game.

"I can FINALLY share that I play the first-ever nonbinary character in the Grand Theft Auto franchise!" Rose wrote on Instagram.

"Absolutely wild to find out THIS was the super-secret game I spent weeks and 17 NDAs filming 3 years ago.".

In a recent development, "I shot this back in my they/them days, and everyone on production ensured I was comfortable and gendered correctly," Rose wrote. The project was kept so secret that Rose wasn't told what it was during filming and only recently learned it was GTA 6.

"GTA was one of the most inclusive & caring sets I've ever been on," they added.

A post shared by Jesse James Rose ✨ (@jamesissmiling).

It has been revealed that the role was shot entirely through motion capture, which meant wearing "a unitard made of dots every day". Rose also states steps were taken to protect the character in-game: "To mitigate anti-trans violence, I was assured that players would not be able to inflict violence against my character.".

A whole day of the shoot was spent scanning Rose's face, including hours of pulling different expressions. At one point Rose couldn't move a particular muscle, and worried the scan had been ruined, but the director was thrilled.

"That right there is exactly why we'll never use AI," he remarked, according to Rose. "A computer could never come up with the face you just made.".

"Remember, in a world consumed with Generative AI, the largest video game in history chose human labour to preserve the integrity of the game," Rose added.

Rose did raise some concerns about pay, though.

Recent findings suggest that "Despite being paid well for my time on set, I'll never see a residual check for my work," Rose wrote, explaining that this is part of SAG’s contract with game developers. As a union leader at one of SAG's sister unions, Rose remarked there's still "a long way to go to ensure arts workers are fairly compensated for our labour".

Rose also notes many other trans people worked on GTA 6, both in front of the camera and behind it. "SO many trans people besides me are featured in GTA VI as other roles, designers, writers, etc... take a note, theatre and TV! We are employable & profitable!!!".

Finally, Rose voiced support for Rockstar's UK staff. "UK-based workers at Rockstar Games are at the moment voting on whether to unionise, and I sincerely hope their efforts are successful," Rose wrote. "Every arts worker should have a stable job with benefits and fair working conditions protected by a union.".

It has been revealed that GTA 6 is out now on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. It introduced on Thursday, although some have been getting in on the action earlier, as a whopping 2m Xbox players in the UK and US took advantage of the trick of changing their console's country. Even New Zealand's tourist board tipped a wink to the sudden influx of virtual 'visitors', telling them "We'd love to have you". It also cheekily offered a video of cheerful tourists admiring the country's resplendent natural beauty. "Life," read the accompanying caption, "because we're getting GTA 6 first.".
