Best Buy's Black Friday sale has officially started, and the sale prices certainly reflect that.

Industry observers note that the sale officially kicked off Nov. 19, a full week ahead of Thanksgiving. Deals will run through Saturday, Nov. 28, so you have some time to shop. The Best Buy Cyber Monday sale actually kicks off on Sunday and lasts through Monday, Nov. 30.

In other words, we're full speed ahead into the best Best Buy prices of the year. From MacBooks to gaming consoles, TVs and even kitchen appliances, this is your chance to score serious savings on your Christmas wish list.

To make sorting through all the thousands of deals a little easier, Mashable's shopping reporters and editors compiled the most noteworthy deals from Best Buy's Black Friday sale.

Selecting deals is about more than choosing the best items with a price cut. For a deal to make our list of the best recommendations, it has to match, beat, or come within 99 cents of the current lowest price available. (Amazon loves to beat Best Buy's prices by 99 cents, but Best Buy shoppers can take advantage of in-person, same-day pickup — no membership required). We also take into account how often an item goes on sale, the quality of the item itself, and any price increases the item may have seen over the past year or two.
