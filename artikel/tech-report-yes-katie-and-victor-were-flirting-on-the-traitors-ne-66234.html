Industry observers note that the Traitors may be a game of deceit, but Victor Vollbrechthausen and Katie Fites’ flirtation? That was apparently very real.

As the first civilian-only season of The Traitors: Latest Blood came to a close, host Alan Cumming asked Katie, “Is there a possible date on the cards for you and Victor?”.

She seemed tainted by the fact that Victor had banished her, jokingly saying, “No date for you,” until he kept the banter going and claimed that he “earned it” after finding out she was a Traitor. Now, the star-crossed Faithful and Traitor romance has seemingly made its way out of Alan’s Scottish castle.

During a joint postseason interview with Entertainment Weekly, Victor and Katie revealed they followed through on their prepares and actually went on a first date.

“We have gone on that date. After incessant begging, let the kids back home know that persistence works,” Katie joked, before revealing that it was “great.”.

According to recent reports, “It was fun to get to know each other outside of her trying to murder me, me trying not to get murdered. It was nice to take a moment just for us,” Victor, who played as a Faithful while Katie was one of the season’s Traitors, told the outlet.

It has been revealed that as for what their first date entailed? Well, there was certainly no discussion about their time on the show, since Victor banned any mention of it. And, honestly, who can blame him? After all, he was onto Katie’s strategy to potentially target him for murder.

Industry observers note that while her crush on Victor helped her game, Katie admitted that she genuinely enjoyed hanging out with him and even grew close to him throughout the season. She also revealed that she didn’t immediately kill Victor off because she wasn’t aware of his suspicions toward her—though he had been side-eyeing her earlier on than she realized.

In a recent development, the pair remained coy about whether they’ll keep their showmance going offscreen. When asked if they had aims to go on a second date, Victor shared, “Let’s see. Listen, man, it’s gonna... I don’t know. Getting that first one was an uphill battle, but I think there could be.”.

“I think there could be,” Katie added. “But we’ll see. Gonna have to keep persisting.”.
