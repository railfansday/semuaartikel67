President Donald Trump thanked U.S. service members enforcing the naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz during Tuesday’s annual White House Thanksgiving turkey pardoning ceremony, asserting no Iranian ships have gotten through and vowing Tehran will never obtain a nuclear weapon.

Industry observers note that president Donald Trump paused Tuesday's annual White House Thanksgiving turkey pardoning ceremony to offer a special tribute and thanks to U.S. service members defending the Strait of Hormuz and enforcing a naval blockade against Iran.

Speaking from the White House South Lawn during the 79th National Thanksgiving Turkey presentation, Trump thanked American troops deployed overseas and praised those working to prevent Tehran from acquiring a nuclear weapon.

In a recent development, "We want to say a special thank you to them, the people that are guarding the strait know or the blockade we call it," Trump remarked. "It’s a blockade, the greatest naval blockade in history.".

It has been revealed that trump asserted that the U.S. maritime cordon has stopped every Iranian vessel from passing through since its establishment, save for a brief pause earlier in the standoff.

"Not one Iranian ship has gotten through to the blockade since we established it, other than we had a little moratorium. And we learned that you can’t trust them," Trump said. "But not one ship from Iran has gotten through. Our naval blockade is the most incredible naval blockade, probably in the history of the world. It’s amazing.".

Recent findings suggest that the U.S. naval blockade targeting vessels entering and leaving Iranian ports began in April amid the Strait of Hormuz crisis and resumed in July following a temporary pause.

In a recent development, before returning to the holiday ceremony, Trump offered a Thanksgiving prayer for deployed U.S. troops and expressed pride in the country.
