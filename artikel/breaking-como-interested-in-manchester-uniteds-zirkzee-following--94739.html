Reports in Italy claim that Como have joined a list of Serie A sides interested in Manchester United and former Bologna attacker Joshua Zirkzee following a long-term set-back for Italy international Moise Kean in his recovery from injury.

Cesc Fabregas confirmed over the weekend that Kean is set to be sidelined for another few months, ‘not weeks’, following a set-back in training towards the end of last week.

Kean has been struggling with a recurring tibia matter for many months, a problem that has affected him for several months, before the completion of his €40m transfer to Como over the summer.

Since the summer, Kean has played through pain but has had set-backs along the way: His most recent appearance came for the Italy national team during the September-October international break.

Just as he was due to return to full training with Como, Kean suffered another setback, which may now require surgical intervention, and is expected to keep him out of action for another few months.

According to Monday’s reports from Gianluca Di Marzio, the setback suffered by Kean could lead Como to intervene in the January transfer window, and Manchester United’s Zirkzee could be a potential option.

The Netherlands international, who joined United from Bologna at an initial cost of €42.5m in the summer of 2024, is out of favour at Old Trafford and has not started in any of United’s competitive fixtures so far in 2026-27.

He has played for a total of 231 minutes across all competitions, divided across 11 substitute appearances in the Premier League, Champions League and EFL Cup.

Though he may have been considered surplus to requirements under former Manchester United head coach Michael Carrick, it remains to be seen how his predecessor, Mauricio Pochettino, will handle the Zirkzee situation.

Como are said to be ‘exploring their options’, although there have not been any formal negotiations between them and Manchester United at this stage.

Como are not the only club interested in a potential deal for Zirkzee in January: Napoli will also be on the hunt for a new striker in the event that Lorenzo Lucca is moved on, and Gianluca Di Marzio claims that Zirkzee is of interest to them as well.

Zirkzee has previously been linked with moves to Juventus, Roma and Milan as well during his time at Old Trafford.
