Director Martin McDonagh has become as well-known for making memorable films as he is for writing the complex male friendships within them.

"I like mining down into the sensitivity that I think a lot of male relationships do have, but aren't as – especially in American films – explored sensitively," he stated about the subject during a panel conversation at the inaugural Vanity Fair Screening Room at LACMA on Friday. "It's not just a buddy film, and it's not a romance, and it's not just about cool CIA guys," McDonagh continued. “It's sort of the opposite in other ways. And that I think is interesting to explore because I think my male relationships are that way. They can be sensitive and sad and happy and all the things that a female relationship can be. So I think that's why I keep coming back to it.”.

It has been revealed that the conversation, which featured McDonagh and the film’s stars John Malkovich and Mariana di Girolamo, was moderated by VF's Senior Hollywood Correspondent Rebecca Ford following an exclusive screening of their new film, Wild Horse Nine. The acclaimed dark comedy follows two CIA agents (Malkovich and Sam Rockwell) who are not only behind the proposal to overthrow the Chilean government in 1973, but have also had a hand in several other significant historical moments in the US and abroad.

Maria di Girolamo, John Malkovich and Martin McDonagh, in conversation with Rebecca Ford at Vanity Fair's Screening Room at LACMA on Friday, November 13, 2026.

Malkovich and Rockwell's characters engage in pithy, often hilarious banter throughout the film, and their onscreen chemistry is a highlight.

John Malkovich at Vanity Fair's Screening Room at LACMA on November 13, 2026.

"I love Sam," remarked Malkovich. “I knew him a bit socially before this, but I'd worked with him once before, which was a great pleasure on a film called A Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. He's like a kind of expert trainer at SeaWorld who can get the whales and the sharks and the dolphins to show things in exchange for a fish. And Sam is a great student of acting. He is a great student of film and of theater, and he's great fun, very hardworking, and just an ideal colleague. I couldn't imagine a better colleague than Sam and I've had some excellent colleagues.”.

"He's one of the best actors of his generation, if not the best," adds McDonagh of Rockwell. "He's always surprising, always dangerous, always sensitive, always smart, always coming up with latest stuff. And I think there's a danger to him that's lovely to write for. We always hope the characters will always be surprising, and with Sam's voice in the head, that really helps.".

Mariana di Girolamo at Vanity Fair's Screening Room at LACMA on November 13, 2026.

For rising star Mariana di Girolamo, a Chilean actress making her English language movie debut with Wild Horse Nine, she was excited and only slightly intimidated to be a part of the cast.

Recent findings suggest that "It was quite a challenge to perform for the first time in English," she noted. "I prepared myself with a coach and we rehearsed with John, Sam and Parker [Posey] and Ailin [Salas] in a public theater in Fresh York where I heard the script for the first time. That was amazing for me, for us." di Girolamo, who is very familiar with the fraught period of Chilean history depicted in the film, said she appreciated how McDonagh put it all in context. "It was my first time approaching that period of time in this way with this very beautiful dark humor. I was laughing about these things and feeling empathy with these CIA agents that committed a lot of horrific, horrific things. I've never read something like that, and the arc of my character is so beautiful, unique, I really wanted to be part of this.".

Martin McDonagh at Vanity Fair's Screening Room at LACMA on November 13, 2026.
