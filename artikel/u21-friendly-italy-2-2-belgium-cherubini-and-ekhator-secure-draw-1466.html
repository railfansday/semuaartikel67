Italy earned a 2-2 friendly draw against Belgium as they continue preparation for the European Under-21 Championship, with Luigi Cherubini and Jeff Ekhator on target.

The Azzurrini have already qualified for the European Championship, so have begun a series of friendly matches to prepare for the tournament.

They followed up the senior side’s victory in the Nations League by beating France 2-1 on French soil on Friday with goals from Francesco Camarda and Luca Lipani.

Silvio Baldini made many changes to his starting XI in Livorno this evening, and a deflected Matteo Dagasso cross almost caused an own goal in the opening minutes.

Samuele Inacio at one end and Romeo Vermant at the other forced saves, before Italy took the lead with Matthias Pieklak parrying the Inacio effort into the path of Lipani, eventually bundled over the line by Luigi Cherubini.

Belgium equalised moments later in similarly chaotic fashion, this time Robin Mirisola managing to get the final touch after a mad scramble.

Dagasso drilled wide after a well-worked move with Camarda lay-off, then Camarda himself at full stretch turned inches wide of the target.

After the restart, Inacio got on the end of a Cherubini cross to test the goalkeeper, but it was Belgium who turned the game around with Bologna’s Samuel Mbangula and Joseph Ngonge combining to set up the Mirisola tap-in.

Italy began to pour forward, Dagasso off target, yet Giovanni Daffara had to make a big save on Lucas Noubi.

Juventus striker Ekhator was very lively, and after initial penalty appeals were rejected, he was brought down by the goalkeeper and converted the spot-kick to equalise.

There was still time for Daffara to seal his Man of the Match award with another save in stoppages to deny Julien Duranville’s drive as it was heading to the far bottom corner.

Cherubini 25 (I), Mirisola 27, 52 (B), Ekhator pen 88 (I).

Italy U21: Daffara; Lulli (Kouadio 55), Chiarodia (Mané 55), Comuzzo, Bartesaghi; Lipani, Faticanti (Amatucci 55), Dagasso (D’Uffizi 79); Cherubini (Cherubini 55), Camarda (Ekhator 62), Inacio (Fini 55).

Belgium U21: Pieklak; Noubi (Monamay 82), Ngonge (Fofana 71), Delorge, Mirisola (Lokesa 62); Lapage, Burlet (Tytens 82); Vermant (Bassette 71), Sagrado, Mbangula (Duranville 71); De Vlieger (Van de Perre 81).
