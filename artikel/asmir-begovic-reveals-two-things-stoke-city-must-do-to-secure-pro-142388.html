Stoke City have continued their incredible run of form under Mark Robins with a 2-1 home victory over Millwall on Wednesday night to send them joint top of the Championship alongside Middlesbrough.

Everything is rosy right now in the Potteries for Stoke, who can’t stop winning at the moment, with their most recent victory over Millwall extending their winning streak to five games on the spin.

The last time the Potters were on such a run was back in 2008 with Tony Pulis at the helm; they would go on to win promotion to the Premier League that year, as now with Robins in the dugout, Stoke supporters will be hoping history can repeat itself.

However, there is still a long way to go, and the club cannot afford to rest on their laurels or become complacent anytime soon, as one former fan favourite has revealed the two things they must do to secure a Premier League return this season.

Although Stoke sit in the automatic promotion places in the Championship right now, a mere ten points separates them from 12th place; as for how good things have been, it only takes a poor couple of results to go back to reality.

Former goalkeeper and fan favourite Asmir Begovic, speaking to The Sentinel, has now revealed the two things the Potters must do this year to fire their way back to the top flight.

Begovic said: "It's not easy to get out of the Championship, but it's good to see them having a positive start. Listen, there's still a long way to go. Now we're getting into the meat and bones of the season going into the Christmas period, and it's really notable to try to continue being consistent.

"They've obviously made some really good signings as well, Ethan Galbraith has been absolutely outstanding, a great signing. So for some of the things that have gone wrong, you've got to give the board and Jon Walters credit for strengthening the squad.

Industry observers note that "Now it's about keeping that consistency, maybe adding the latter piece or two in January given their resources and positioning. The fact that the play-offs have also extended has given a lot of teams a lot more hope as well.".

Do you agree with Begovic's take on the matter? What do you think Stoke must do to secure promotion? Share your thoughts below!

Begovic, 39, has had an exceptional career in professional football, beginning his career with Portsmouth before spells with Macclesfield, Bournemouth, Yeovil Town, Stoke, Chelsea, Qarabağ, AC Milan, Everton, Queen’s Park Rangers, and Leicester City.

It would be with the Potters where he would play the most games in his career, making 173 appearances across all competitions in North Staffordshire, having joined the club from Portsmouth in January 2010 for around £3.25 million, and eventually being sold to Chelsea for around £8 million.

Speaking to The Sentinel, the Bosnian has been full of praise for current Stoke number one and Sweden international Viktor Johansson, saying: "I think he certainly does have Premier League potential. I think you can see now he's being very selective with his next move. I think he wants to continue to play.

"I'm sure he would have had opportunities to go in as a number two at times, but I think the fact that he wants to play and be number one for club and country shows a lot about his character and his mentality. I think he definitely has the capabilities and talent to play in the Premier League.".

Johansson penned a fresh four-year deal at the bet365 Stadium over the summer, having initially joined the club from Rotherham United in the summer of 2024 for just over £1 million, and has been exceptional ever since.

Certainly, Johansson will be hoping to play Premier League football one day, and at this rate, there is no reason it can’t be with Stoke.

Robins has been in similar positions before, but to date, has not quite gotten over the line, at least not in the Championship, falling short with Coventry City and suffering a major dip in form last year with Stoke.

The 56-year-old will be well aware of the position he is in, what he has to do, and how to do it, but should still listen to the advice the former Bosnian international has laid out before him.

The January window presents Stoke with an opportunity to add one or two fresh bodies to the mix, and if they are to get over the line, those additions could be pivotal in their year, as transfer aims must be in place ahead of the coming months, whilst remaining consistent has to be the priority for now.
