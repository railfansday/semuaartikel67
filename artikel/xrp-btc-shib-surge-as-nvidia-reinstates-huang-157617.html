Cryptocurrency prices are bouncing back, with Bitcoin (BTC), XRP and Shiba Inu (SHIB) all in the green after the surprising announcement that Jensen Huang has been reappointed as chief executive officer of Nvidia.

The sudden leadership reversal comes as tech stocks bounce back following the sharp market sell-off that has also affected major crypto assets.

Nvidia made a major announcement on Monday, Nov. 23, stating that its board of directors had unanimously approved Huang's reappointment as the firm’s president, CEO, and a board director, effective immediately.

The news is a surprise move by the tech giant, which had previously mentioned that Huang, the company’s longtime CEO, wouldn’t be coming back after his resignation earlier this month.

As stated in Nvidia’s official press release, the board made its decision following a series of discussions with Huang.

Additionally, the firm consulted with high-ranking U.S. government officials to determine that the reappointment would not present a national security concern.

Huang himself acknowledged the reappointment, thanking employees, shareholders and the firm’s leaders.

“I can’t wait to get back to work,” noted Huang.

The organization also said that the independent inquiry into the events leading to Huang’s departure would continue.

In a recent development, the news of Huang’s reappointment comes as Nvidia shares were already recovering from their recent decline. Nvidia stock was up some 8% in premarket trading.

The news comes as crypto markets start to bounce back after several days of intense selling pressure.

As per the supplied market snapshot, Bitcoin is trading at $62,795.99, up 1.5% in the last hour.

Still, the biggest cryptocurrency is up just 0.3% in the last 24 hours and down 5% over the past week.

On the brighter side, XRP is up 2% in the last hour at $0.9691. The Ripple-associated coin is 1.2% higher over the past 24 hours, though it is still down 1.8% over the past week.

Shiba Inu is also trading at $0.00000362. The coin has recorded a small daily increase of around 0.8%, as per CoinGlass data.

Other major crypto assets are also bouncing back. Ethereum is up 1.8% in the last hour at $1,767. Solana, meanwhile, is up 2.2% at $74.69.

The move higher comes after Bitcoin dropped to around $55,500 this past weekend, falling to its lowest price in over two years.
