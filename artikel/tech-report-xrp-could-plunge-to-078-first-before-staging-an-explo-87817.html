📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that XRP investors may need to brace for a painful detour before the payments-focused cryptocurrency makes its next major move. According to prominent market analyst Ali Martinez, XRP could first suffer a steep correction to $0.78 before reversing course and eventually embarking on a staggering rally toward $18. While the projected downside could unsettle bullish investors, the analyst’s chart suggests the potential plunge may be setting the stage for one of XRP’s most explosive rallies yet.

XRP could face another brutal correction, with crypto analyst Ali Martinez warning that the cryptocurrency may plunge to $0.78 before finding a meaningful bottom.

Industry observers note that in a recent analysis shared on X, Martinez pointed to a massive ascending triangle that appears to have been forming on XRP’s weekly chart since 2017. According to the analyst, the long-term formation suggests that the ongoing downturn may not be over, leaving room for another significant leg down.

The warning comes as XRP struggles to recover from a steep decline that has erased much of its recent gains. The token has already fallen more than 42% from its October high of $1.55, placing it under renewed selling pressure. Martinez believes the correction could extend toward the ascending triangle’s rising support trendline, which at the moment sits around $0.78.

A drop to that level would represent a further decline of approximately 40% from XRP’s $1.30 reference price. However, the projected move would also bring the cryptocurrency closer to a major long-term technical support zone that could lay the foundation for its next recovery.

According to recent reports, despite the bearish outlook, Martinez believes a slide to $0.78 could eventually create an attractive buying opportunity for investors. The analyst expects buyers to step in around the ascending triangle’s rising support line, potentially preventing further losses and setting the stage for a broader recovery. If this support holds, XRP could begin climbing toward the formation’s horizontal resistance, which is around $3.50.

Industry observers note that however, reclaiming $3.50 would be a critical turning point for the cryptocurrency. According to Martinez, XRP would need to break above this resistance on the weekly chart and then successfully retest the level as support. Such a move would provide confirmation that the multi-year ascending triangle is playing out as anticipated, strengthening the bullish view.

Industry observers note that if that breakout materializes, the analyst sees room for XRP to launch into a more parabolic surge toward $18. From XRP’s current price of $0.9925, this would represent a whopping 1714% growth.

That being noted, the ambitious target remains conditional on XRP first defending $0.78, recovering toward $3.50, and clearing the resistance with a confirmed weekly breakout.
