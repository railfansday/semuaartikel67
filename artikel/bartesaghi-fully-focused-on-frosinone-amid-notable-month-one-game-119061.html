One change has been made to the starting eleven, with Davide Bartesaghi replacing Alphadjo Cisse. The youngster’s form has been a little bit up and down lately, so this is a good opportunity for him to show that he should be starting regularly at left-back.

Speaking to Sky Italia just a few minutes ago, as transcribed by MilanNews, Bartesaghi shared his thoughts on the game and the fact that he’s starting in defence again, as opposed to in midfield/attack.

On the game and the upcoming schedule..

“This month is important, we know that, but let’s try to take it one game at a time. Let’s start by thinking about tonight, which is the most important thing.”.
