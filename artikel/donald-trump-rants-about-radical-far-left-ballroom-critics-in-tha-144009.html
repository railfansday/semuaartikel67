Donald Trump celebrated Thanksgiving by posting a lengthy screed taking aim at the “radical far left,” critics of his ballroom project, and “murderers, drug dealers, and criminals of all shapes, sizes, and colors.”.

The president, writing on Truth Social, opened the post with a sincere note: “HAPPY THANKSGIVING TO ALL AMERICAN PATRIOTS WHO HAVE WORKED SO HARD TO MAKE OUR COUNTRY GREAT!” He then introduced into a rant-filled tirade that was far less grateful.

“Additionally, I would like to wish a Happy Thanksgiving to even those who issued fake Economic Numbers, just prior to the Election, and corrected them yesterday, three weeks late, showing that the GDP was actually 4.3%, versus 3.4%, A RECORD SETTING ERROR, and one so grave that it should be immediately investigated,” Trump wrote. “Also, I would like to wish a very Happy Thanksgiving to all of those people that have worked so hard to bring our Country to the Radical Far Left, those that are looking to change our Court System to that of Sharia, those who want to redistribute Property, so that all of the hardworking people that have built up, over a lifetime, a certain Wealth, will now be forced to distribute it throughout the Country, even to those who refuse to work.”.

Industry observers note that he continued, “I want to thank the 2% of our Somalian population that have a job, and are actually willing to work, without receiving Government Assistance. These are wonderful American ‘citizens,’ and should be allowed to stay in our Country, and celebrate Thanksgiving with us. The others should be thrown out, immediately, thereby saving the Great State of Minnesota, which has no chance if they are allowed to stay.”.

He then shifted focus to the White House and his ballroom, which has yet to be completed. “I want to commiserate with those Reporters and Citizens who don’t understand filth, dirt, lack of maintenance, and corruption, and are upset with me because I took time out of my very busy schedule to fix the 17 broken windows, numerous chipped and decaying columns, broken, dilapidated, and missing moldings, and lack of a proper plaster and paint job on the White House,” Trump noted. “It hasn’t been properly painted, or taken care of, for years. I felt strongly that, after being built in 1792, it was time to fix up the White House which is, after all, a primary symbol of our Country.
