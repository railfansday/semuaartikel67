Breaking: GTA 6: How to Avoid Dye Packs from Marked Bills

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that if you want to earn significant bucks in GTA 6, you’ll want to rob banks and ATM machines. However, robbing these locations will earn you marked bills, money that has been rigged with dye packs.

According to recent reports, picking up marked bills and getting sprayed by dye packs will expose Jason and Lucia and make it easier for cops to notice you. Additionally, dye packs completely ruin the money you just robbed, meaning you won’t be able to spend it.

In order to avoid dye packs and actually claim marked bills, you must first unlock a very important item. Read further to learn how to avoid dye packs from marked bills in GTA 6.

In order to avoid getting sprayed by dye packs when picking up marked bills in GTA 6, you must first unlock the Dye Pack Jammer. This valuable item is unlocked in Chapter 3 during the “Credit Check” mission.

Unfortunately, there is no other way to get the Dye Pack Jammer in GTA 6, meaning you just have to naturally progress the story until it becomes available. The good news is that it's unmissable.

Credit Check is the 16th mission in Chapter 3, so it’s pretty far along in the game. For reference, it took me about 30 hours to reach this point. Granted, I have been exploring the world thoroughly and completing side objectives, so how long it takes you to reach this mission depends on your playstyle.

According to recent reports, during Credit Check, you’ll meet Raul’s group of thieves who are planning a huge bank heist. One of his companions gives you the Dye Pack Jammer to prepare you for the heist.

Recent findings suggest that the Dye Pack Jammer is automatically equipped when you unlock it in Chapter 3. In other words, you don’t have to manually equip it or anything.

According to recent reports, the Dye Pack Jammer allows you to rob any bank in Leonida, as well as towable ATM machines, without worrying that you’ll get sprayed by pink dye. However, the marked bills you earn from these locations aren’t available to use right away.

It has been revealed that in order to turn marked bills into money you can spend, you must bring them to one of the Fence NPCs. Each Fence offers money for marked bills, but Yasmani in Vice City pays the most. You will unlock Yasmani after completing the main story “Boobie Time” in Chapter 3.

Recent findings suggest that if you haven’t claimed the Dye Pack Jammer yet and happened to get covered in dye, then Jason and Lucia will remain exposed to the cops, making exploration and committing robberies much harder. It’s for this reason that you want to remove the dye from your clothes and body as soon as possible.

According to recent reports, there are a couple of ways to remove the dye from your characters. First, you can go back to your apartment, take a shower, and change your clothes to remove the dye. Besides that, you can simply wait until it goes away, which takes a long time, or force yourself to get Wasted or Busted.
