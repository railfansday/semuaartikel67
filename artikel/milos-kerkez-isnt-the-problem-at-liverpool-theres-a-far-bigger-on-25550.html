Milos Kerkez isn't the problem that many think he is at Liverpool. Instead, there's a far bigger one and Andoni Iraola has got to fix it.

Milos Kerkez has been getting stick this season as he's struggled for form. He's far from alone in that, of course, but the left-back has looked quite shaky at the back while offering little going forward.

According to recent reports, he does, in his defence, have to take on an incredibly difficult task as the long-term replacement for Andy Robertson. Robertson is by far the best left-back Liverpool have had in 30 years and one of the greatest to ever play in the Premier League.

Some could argue that it was a bizarre decision to force him out and move on when he's still clearly capable of delivering at the top level. But it's a little late for that - Kerkez is now the first-choice left-back.

The centre of the pitch doesn’t really offer space for wingers to run into. The effect of that is a clogged wing, too, which essentially always has a winger sticking wide on it.
