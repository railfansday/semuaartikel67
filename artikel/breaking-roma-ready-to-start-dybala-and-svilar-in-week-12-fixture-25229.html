Paulo Dybala and Mile Svilar are expected to be back in the starting XI for Roma when they face Parma on Saturday, after one and two months out of action respectively.

The break for international duty closes and Serie A returns this weekend with the Week 12 fixtures, and the Giallorossi take on Parma on Saturday at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

The good news for coach Gian Piero Gasperini is that the treatment room is finally starting to empty out after an injury crisis.

Sky Sport Italia report that both Dybala and goalkeeper Svilar were in action during today’s training session, and are expected to be in the starting XI with Parma.

La Joya has not featured since suffering a calf strain during the 4-3 defeat to Real Madrid on October 14.

As for Svilar, he sustained a serious strain during training and his last game was all the way back on September 19 against Inter.

His return is particularly notable, because second choice Pierluigi Gollini also sustained a back injury, forcing Devis Vasquez to step in for two massive matches.

Manu Koné and Gianluca Mancini have also recovered after missing out on international duty, so the only absentees are Lorenzo Pellegrini and Devyne Rensch.
