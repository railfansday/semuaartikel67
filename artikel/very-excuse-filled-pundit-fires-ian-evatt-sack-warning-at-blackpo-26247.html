Tom Garratt has suggested that Blackpool must replace Ian Evatt, as he criticised the manager for the number of ‘excuses’ he comes up with following their defeat at Wimbledon.

The 44-year-old, who had a successful time as a player at Bloomfield Road, returned to the club as head coach in October last year.

Evatt inherited a team in a mess, as the Seasiders had picked up just nine points from 13 games, which had left them bottom of League One.

However, the former Bolton chief inspired a turnaround, and after finishing the season with six wins in seven, Blackpool ultimately secured a mid-table finish.

Therefore, fans went into the current season with hopes that they could kick-on, but it has been a difficult first few months for Evatt and the players, as they are only outside the relegation zone on goal difference.

The latest defeat, a seventh in 13 games, came on Saturday, as AFC Wimbledon beat Blackpool 1-0 at Plough Lane.

That brought more anger from the Blackpool fans towards Evatt, although the boss revealed that up to 15 players and staff missed the game due to a sickness bug that had gone through the squad.

Yet, speaking on The Pyramid Podcast, pundit Garratt revealed that he felt this was another example of Evatt refusing to take responsibility this season, as he questioned the approach of the Blackpool chief about how he deals with the media, as well as stating a change in the dugout might be needed.

“I don’t like the way he talks. It’s very excuse-filled. If that’s my gaffer, I’m going ‘look at yourself here’. There are a lot of excuses, and especially away at Wimbledon who aren’t that good.

“I think Blackpool have too much quality (to go down), but I think they need to get rid of the manager.”.

In isolation, you could argue that Blackpool were unfortunate at Wimbledon, as they obviously had to deal with a lot going into the game in terms of losing players, and they did have more possession, shots and territory than the visitors.

Yet, a lot of that is down to the game state, and Wimbledon were happy to contain the visitors after taking an early lead.

Either way, Blackpool fans are becoming increasingly annoyed at how this season is playing out, and many will agree with Garratt’s assessment.

There is a feeling that Evatt doesn’t take responsibility, which he must do as the head coach, as he is picking the team and working on the approach all week.

The ex-Barrow boss will know that the manager is the one that will pay the price, and the reality is that Blackpool are underachieving right now, as they would have hoped to be competing much further up the table.

So, he will know that he needs to get results rapidly if he is to keep his job, and Blackpool are facing a huge game against Wigan at home this weekend as they look to get back to winning ways.
