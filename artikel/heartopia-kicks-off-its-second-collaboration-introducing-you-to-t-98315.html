📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that heartopia kicked off its entry into the life sim genre in grand fashion back when it launched, including a high-profile collab featuring none other than My Little Pony. Now, developer XD Games are returning to that well, with the launch of phase two of their My Little Pony collaboration starting today!

Industry observers note that running until December 27th, the upcoming MLP x Heartopia collab sees you once more working to safeguard the Tree of Harmony, this time against the vicious Windgos. Not only that, but it also sees the introduction of new outfits and furniture, as well as returning content from the first phase of the collaboration.

Speaking of racing, you might want to get your running shoes on because the Cloud Gallop activity sees new MPL-themed parkour courses arrive in Heartopia, with plenty of exciting rewards on offer if you manage to complete them, ranging from recipes to a brand-new pet bed.

Recent findings suggest that and of course, as mentioned above, this collaboration also sees the return of phase one content, such as the Mane Six outfits themed after the main characters of the series. All this and even more scenery to explore themed after My Little Pony. Not bad even if you're not a fan of the series!
