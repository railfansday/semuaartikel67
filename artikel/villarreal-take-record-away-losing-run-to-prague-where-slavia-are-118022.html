Recent findings suggest that villarreal visit Slavia Prague after seven straight Champions League away defeats, a record for a Spanish club. Their hosts have gone 23 games without a win in the competition, so one of the two runs must end today.

In a recent development, villarreal face Slavia Prague in the Champions League today carrying a losing run that UEFA indicates is a record for a Spanish club. They have lost each of their last seven away games in the competition. Their hosts carry a burden of their own. Slavia have eight draws and 15 defeats in their last 23 Champions League games. That run equals the competition’s worst, set by Steaua București between 2006 and 2013.

Sources indicate that one of the two sequences has to end. A Slavia win would end their drought. Any other result would stop Villarreal’s run of away defeats. Kick-off is at 17:45 GMT.

That single win was a 3-1 home victory over Napoli on 13 October. The rest of the league phase has gone badly. Villarreal lost 3-2 at Borussia Dortmund on 8 September and 4-3 at Liverpool on 20 October. PSG then won 6-0 at La Cerámica on 3 November.

According to recent reports, that leaves Villarreal 29th with three points. Another defeat would leave them close to elimination with three games to play.

According to recent reports, the defence is the clearest problem. Villarreal have not kept a clean sheet in any of those 15 games, a club record. None of their last 35 games in the Champions League proper has ended goalless.

Recent findings suggest that falling behind early has also been fatal. Villarreal have never won a Champions League game when trailing at half-time, with two draws and 13 defeats. On eight occasions they have gone into the interval losing on the road, and every one of those matches ended in defeat.

Recent findings suggest that nathan Saliba, Juan Foyth and Ilias Akhomach are all missing in Prague, EFE reported. Gerard Moreno trained before the game and could return.

Recent findings suggest that trpišovský has six draws and 12 defeats from his 19 Champions League matches, all in charge of Slavia. UEFA describes that as the longest winless run of any coach in the competition. Slavia have also never beaten a Spanish club from the group or league phase onwards, with two draws and four defeats. The two clubs have not met since their two games in 2017.

Villarreal coach Iñigo Pérez still expects a hard night. Speaking in Prague, he said no team in the Champions League is as aggressive as Slavia. He called the game “transcendental” for how the league phase plays out. He added that the city’s cold weather would not be an excuse.

Recent findings suggest that defender Sergi Cardona remarked the team must learn last season’s lesson. He expects the game to be extremely demanding.
