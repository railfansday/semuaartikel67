Ahead of its milestone year in 2027, Aqua Expeditions has launched its 20th Anniversary Amazon Special with 20 percent savings on select Design Suites aboard the Aqua Nera (Suites 108, 109 and 110) and the Aria Amazon (Suites 101 and 102).

The company said in a press release that the offer invites guests to explore the Peruvian Amazon, where its story started in 2007.

Founded by Francesco Galli Zugaro, Aqua Expeditions began with the aim of redefining small-ship exploration in the Peruvian Amazon. Twenty years later, the region remains at the heart of the organization’s story, the company added.

“When I founded Aqua Expeditions twenty years ago, my vision was to create intimate, small-ship expeditions that would bring guests closer to the nature, wildlife and cultures of some of the world’s most remote destinations,” remarked Zugaro.

Sources indicate that “That philosophy has guided us as we’ve grown, and the Amazon remains a very special place for me. I can’t think of a more fitting way to celebrate this milestone than by inviting guests to experience where our journey first began.”.

The press release stated that aboard the Aqua Nera and Aria Amazon, guests can expect contemporary design, personalized service and cuisine by Chef Pedro Miguel Schiaffino, a pioneer of Amazonian cuisine.

Other highlights include twice-daily excursions led by expert naturalist guides.

The firm stated that the anniversary savings are available to all guests, with additional privileges for members of Circolo Aqua, Aqua Expeditions’ loyalty program.

Navigators and Explorers enjoy upgraded onboard benefits corresponding to the next loyalty tier for their expedition, while Legends receive a complimentary spa voucher and a private airport transfer.

The offer is valid for latest bookings on eligible 2027 departures, excluding Christmas and New Year sailings, and does not apply to solo travelers.

Recent findings suggest that it cannot be combined with other promotions, although Circolo Aqua benefits may be applied. Travel advisors can book eligible departures through Aqua Expeditions’ agent online booking system.
