Industry observers note that readers rejoice, Archive of Our Own has returned. The Organization for Transformative Works (OTW) has unveiled that the popular fanfiction website has been restored after a security incident late last month required it be taken down for emergency maintenance.

Recent findings suggest that the shutdown galvanized online fan communities, many of which resorted to sharing databases of downloaded fics to tide readers over until AO3 could come back. But such emergency fandom measures were themselves the source of discussion and debate concerning the ethics of redistributing fandom works outside of protected spaces like AO3, potentially without authorial consent and in defiance of the etiquette that generally exists between people who write fic and those who read it.

In a recent development, those discussions aren’t going anywhere, but they may take a backseat now that AO3, like Aragorn busting through the gates of Helm’s Deep, has returned. Some features, including the ability to download fics in specific e-reader formats, are still disabled for now and OTW reports that the site may respond more slowly than normal due to everybody and their mom rushing back to get to their Heated Rivalry fics.

AO3 is taking steps to address the concern.
