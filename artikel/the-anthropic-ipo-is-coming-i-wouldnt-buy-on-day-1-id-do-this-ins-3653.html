Recent findings suggest that anthropic's IPO is coming this week, with the tech stock expected to trade by Thursday under the ticker symbol ANT. Its valuation will be high, projected to be around $1.4 trillion in market cap, but that's not nearly as high as it was expected to be previously, when analysts thought it could fetch close to $2 trillion, which would eclipse the roughly $1.8 trillion valuation at which Space Exploration Technologies Corp (SPCX +0.17%) went public earlier this year.

Even though it won't be a record-breaking IPO, Anthropic will be among the most valuable companies in the world when it goes public this week. It'll come with high expectations that will be hard to meet. Rather than buying the stock on its first day of trading, I'd do this instead.

According to recent reports, when SpaceX went public back in June, there was tremendous excitement around the IPO. The market was in much better shape than it is now. There weren't as many worries about rising interest rates. The mood was fairly positive. SpaceX initially soared in its first couple of days, at one point hitting a high of more than $225 -- well above its IPO price of $135.

Those early gains, however, would fizzle out. On Monday, it was back around its IPO price. While the business has been growing at a fast pace, with revenue totaling $14.6 billion during the three-month period ending Sept. 30, up from $5.6 billion a year ago, that kind of growth may already be priced in for a stock that's trading at such a high valuation.

NASDAQ · As of 3:36 PM ET 11/16/26.

Anthropic may face even worse challenges, as not only will expectations be high for the business, but it's going public at a time when investors are growing concerned about tech stocks as a whole. A decline looks to be inevitable for the stock because even if its valuation is lower than previously expected, I think it'll have a hard time proving to investors that it's worth even $1 trillion in market cap, let alone $1.4 trillion.

There are many risks associated with Anthropic that should give investors pause. There's significant competition in the tech sector, with many companies trying to develop the best and most advanced chatbot. While Anthropic's Claude appears to be a preferred option for many enterprise clients today, there's no guarantee it will remain so in the long term. The lack of a sustainable competitive advantage, combined with its high valuation and lack of true accounting profits, makes this a high-risk stock to own.

A sell-off may come swiftly for Anthropic's stock, and it's only after a sharp reduction in value that I'd consider buying it.
