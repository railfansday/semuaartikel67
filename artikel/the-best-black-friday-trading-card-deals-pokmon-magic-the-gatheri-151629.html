The best Black Friday trading card deals: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, and more on sale

With Black Friday here at last, Amazon has unleashed some of its biggest trading card discounts of the year, with prices on select Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and Yu-Gi-Oh! products dropping to latest lows — with highlights including a 29% discount on MTG's Marvel Super Heroes Play Booster Box, plus $20 off the Pokémon TCG's Mega Evolution Pitch Black Booster Bundle.

There are plenty of other bargains worth snapping up, as well, including price cuts on One Piece starter decks, K-pop Demon Hunters cards, and Disney Lorcana sets. We've rounded up the best Black Friday trading card deals at the moment available across Amazon, covering everything from individual booster bundles to larger display boxes. We'll also keep updating this guide as more offers go live throughout the whole BF weekend, so be sure to keep this guide bookmarked for the latest discounts.

The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Pitch Black Booster Bundle is $49.99 at Amazon, down from its $69.99 list price. That's $20 off (29%), or about $8.33 per pack for the six-pack bundle. For context, Amazon's list price is above the bundle's $26.94 MSRP, and TCGplayer's market price is $35.93, so you're still paying roughly $14 over market. That's the trade-off with this one. It's a solid pick if you can't find it near retail.

Sources indicate that the bundle includes six booster packs from the Pitch Black set, which features Mega Darkrai ex and Mega Zeraora ex, along with Mega Chandelure ex and Mega Excadrill ex. As of this writing, only five were left in stock.

Surprisingly coming in with a bigger price drop than ever for Black Friday’s trading card deals, the 30-pack box for Magic: The Gathering’s Marvel Super Heroes crossover expansion is now only $104.90 at Amazon.

With that $43.05 price cut off the current typical price of $147.95, you’re only paying $3.50 per pack — and only $5.99 above TCGplayer’s estimated $98.91 market price. The lowest price Amazon has ever seen for this box, we don’t expect this deal to last very long.

Each booster contains 14 cards, including a traditional foil and one to four rare-or-higher cards, with the chance to pull special borderless variants featuring Marvel favorites like Iron Man, Hulk, and Doctor Doom. This box is even shipped and sold directly by Amazon rather than a third-party seller, although the listing is marked as a final sale.

Konami's Magnificent Monsters has hit a 90-day low on Amazon, now $29.87 instead of $34.99. That’s actually the lowest price this newly released box has ever been. If you’re a fan of the original anime series, this is a must-buy for grabbing revamped versions of classic cards, like Dark Magician and Elemental Hero Flamewingman.

It has been revealed that you get three five-card packs of Ultra Rares and three additional Secret Rares, each with a chance to upgrade to Starlight Rare. There are 18 reimagined anime cards to collect altogether.

If you've been curious about getting into the One Piece Card Game, this Black Friday bargain is the best way to jump in, with Bandai's Learn Together Deck Set now down to just $57.99, down from $74.99 — a brilliant 23% discount.

Recent findings suggest that built around Luffy, Zoro, and Nami, it includes three ready-to-play decks of 51 cards and 10 DON!! cards apiece. You'll also receive guides, storage cases, three five-card extra packs, and a two-player playsheet.

It has been revealed that it's an excellent time to get into the K-pop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards series, with Kayou's 12-pack Energy Edition reduced from $45.88 to $37.34 on Amazon in a limited-time 19% off deal. This works out to roughly $3.11 per pack, with eight cards in each.

Unlike the Classic Edition, Energy Edition features 11 rarity levels, with 130 different card types to collect. These include the more sought-after SE cards, inspired by the song Golden, featuring laser-etched finishes and individual numbering from 1 to 366. There are also exclusive BP cards numbered up to 999, featuring HUNTR/X in their black stage outfits.

Recent findings suggest that for the Black Friday season, Disney Lorcana's Illumineer's Quest: The Great Hunny Rescue has slipped to a mega-low price of $52.74. Instead of opening packs, you'll be battling a self-playing Vine deck to rescue Piglet. The box has two prebuilt 60-card decks, a 50-card scenario deck, encounter cards, tokens, and a board. Play solo or with up to three friends, provided two bring their own decks. It's sold by Kings Games, shipped by Amazon, and marked as a final sale.
