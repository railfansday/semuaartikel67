City drew 2-2 with Basingstoke Town last Saturday – and the two goals they conceded came when they were playing against ten men.

That was after Kristian Shevchenko – son of one-time Ballon D’or winner Andriy – was sent off in the first half.

Chi coach Darin Killpartrick credited Basingstoke with making it difficult for his team by changing shape when they went down to ten men.

But he noted he was disappointed by the way City defended for the two they let in – and it had to be viewed as an opportunity for three points passed up.

Chi are back at Oaklands Park this Saturday and it’s leaders Uxbridge who are the visitors.

According to recent reports, uxbridge handed City their biggest defeat of the season to date at home in mid-September – it finished 5-0 in Middlesex. And Killpartrick has warned Chi will need to be at their very best to try to get anything out of the game.

Will Sweet and Emmett Dunn were the scorers against Basingstoke as City led 1-0 and 2-1.

Killpartrick said: “It was a missed opportunity for the win against a team who ended with ten men; however when they went down to ten, they changed to a shape that was very difficult to break down.

“We cannot defend – as a team – like we did for their two goals.

Recent findings suggest that “Their first goal upset me the most. Their player has weaved around four or five players and from us that’s not the level we need to produce.

“If you defend like that at this level of football, you will get punished – particularly if you are playing top teams like that.

“But we’ve had a look at it and talked it through with the players and they know we need to defend better than that.”.

One major relief for City is that their long run of Tuesday night matches is over, at least for now.

Killpartrick said the relentless schedule since September had stretched their small squad to the limit and it was great to have a Tuesday when they could concentrate on training, and having a bit of fun as a group too.

Recent findings suggest that another boost is that two key players – midfielder Lloyd Rowlatt and defender Curtis Da Costa – are fit again and pushing for spots. James Sweet has trained again after a hamstring injury.

A massive crowd is hoped for on Saturday, Killpartrick saying: “You’ll see two good teams trying to play entertaining football – we hope for plenty of support.”.
