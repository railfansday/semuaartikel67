This month, Hunter College’s student government posted a flyer for Cultural Arts Night. Six student groups would share one evening: the Peruvian Students Association, the Chinese Students and Scholars Association, the Taiwanese Student Association, Centro PR, Students for Justice in Palestine, and Hillel.

After several participating groups withdrew — seemingly because Hillel, a Jewish organization, was on the flyer — the student government’s president and vice president declared that the event “doesn’t align with our values or the values of the students we represent,” that it “should never have moved forward.” And, remarkably, she added, “To be blunt, we failed.”.

Shabbos Kestenbaum was the lead plaintiff in a federal antisemitism lawsuit against Harvard University, his alma mater.
