📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Fresh York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani was pressed on why he is not retraining NYPD officers on de-escalation instead of sending an unarmed civilian unit to handle protests.

Florida’s former top law enforcement officer issued a call for members of an NYPD counterterrorism unit to relocate to the Sunshine State as many New Yorkers already have, after New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and Councilman Chi Ossé announced the disbandment of the specialized unit.

Mamdani and Ossé, D-Brooklyn, revealed this week that the Strategic Response Group (SRG) will be dissolved in alignment with a key campaign promise by the mayor, as critics, including former NYPD Commissioner Bill Bratton, who created the unit, warn it will make the nation’s largest city more dangerous.

In a recent development, "Florida is always looking for well-qualified new officers," Sen. Ashley Moody, R-Fla., said in reaction to the news.

MAMDANI PREPARES TO FULFILL PROMISE TO AXE NYPD UNIT RESPONSIBLE FOR CONTROLLING PROTEST VIOLENCE.

"The Sunshine State welcomes members of the NYPD Strategic Response Group to join our mission of protecting Florida communities," said Moody, who was previously the state’s attorney general.

NYPD officers who accept the invitation would add to the nearly 376,000 Fresh Yorkers who relocated to Florida between 2019 and 2023, according to figures reported by New York's WPIX-11 News.

Miami-Dade County Commissioner Roberto Gonzalez, a Republican, also issued a call for disillusioned NYPD officers to relocate.

According to recent reports, "In Florida, it’s always our priority to back the brave men and women that wear the badge. Miami-Dade [Sheriff’s Office] is the best in the country. NYPD’s finest: We’re hiring," he mentioned.

According to recent reports, fox News Digital reached out to Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis, a Republican, and Florida Gov.-elect Byron Donalds, R-Fla., for further comment but did not receive an immediate response.

Sources indicate that the invitations come months after another Florida law enforcement leader rented Times Square advertising space to draw NYPD officers southward after Mamdani defeated former Gov. Andrew Cuomo, D-N. Y., and activist Curtis Sliwa in the 2025 mayoral election.

Volusia County Sheriff Michael Chitwood — who hails from Philadelphia — publicly encouraged NYPD officers to come to Daytona Beach.

Recent findings suggest that "Dear NYPD, Sunny Florida is waiting," read one of the billboards from the Volusia County Sheriff’s Foundation. The displays in Times Square included a QR code for officers to apply on the spot.

"Serve a community that appreciates you," it went on.

"You’re going to get support here, and you’re going to have a tremendous quality of life," Chitwood told Fox’s Orlando affiliate.

MAMDANI UNVEILS SWEEPING NYPD USE-OF-FORCE REVIEW, BUT STOPS SHORT OF FULFILLING KEY CAMPAIGN PROMISES.

It has been revealed that chi Ossé, a Newly introduced York City Council member, speaks at a campaign rally for mayoral candidate Zohran Mamdani at United Palace in New York City on Oct. 13, 2025. (Christian Monterrosa/Bloomberg).

On Monday, Mamdani said in a video announcing the end of the SRG that counterterrorism and protest response should have separate enforcement entities, while Ossé added that the government has a responsibility to protect constitutional rights and "keep communities safe.".

"Those priorities are not in conflict," Ossé noted, as the two announced a replacement agency within the mayor’s office called the Community Dialogue and De-escalation Division (CDDD).

MAMDANI CLASHES WITH DEMOCRATIC SOCIALISTS' PUSH TO ABOLISH POLICE AND PRISONS.

The CDDD will be staffed with unarmed civilians without arrest powers who will seek to engage in dialogue with protesters while reserving the right to call in uniformed police if necessary.

The two referenced their own prior arrests, remarking deadpan, "We both know something about that," when laying out how officers can still respond to arrest people involved in "civil disobedience.".

Sources indicate that ossé was arrested in April while protesting the scheduled eviction of a constituent, while then-Assemblyman Mamdani was arrested in 2023 during a traffic-blocking protest outside the Brooklyn home of Sen. Chuck Schumer, D-N.Y.

Fox News Digital reached out to the NYPD and Mamdani’s office for comment.

Sources indicate that mamdani's office responded by sharing a transcript of his news conference on the SRG. At one point, Mamdani said, "The point here is looking for de-escalation, looking for engagement and dialogue, and that being a constant both in advance and during. And when you're asking about — as the march continues, if you have a sense that this march is transforming into something else, then it's not for civilians to be on the front lines of that.".

Some fellow Democrats also criticized Mamdani for the decision.

Fresh York City Councilman James Gennaro, D-Queens, mentioned on X that transferring the SRG’s responsibilities to the new division the mayor described raised concerns about whether the NYPD will be able to "maintain the specialized training and experience to respond to volatile and potentially violent situations.".

"It also raises concerns about the city’s ability to respond to large-scale events and major incidents, including active-shooter attacks," Gennaro noted.

Former NYPD Commissioner Bill Bratton, who served under Mayors Rudy Giuliani and Bill de Blasio, stated the city can reorganize the NYPD but not "reorganize away the threats that require" the SRG’s specialized response.
