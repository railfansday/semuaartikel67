Recent findings suggest that first thing’s first: I am a massive Gap Inc. fan. I’m of the opinion that you can fill out nearly an entire wardrobe with clothing from the company’s four brands: Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta. Today though, we’re here to talk about Athleta, known for its higher-quality athleisure and workout wear. It’s a great place to shop for travel day clothing, no matter where you sit on the pajamas-to-business-casual spectrum of dressing for a flight.

In a recent development, if you’re a sweats kind of traveler, it's got you covered (I love the Forever Fleece high rise joggers). For something a little more put together and on trend, look to the wide-leg Seasoft mid rise pants. For something you can wear from the place into an office or work meeting, consider the Brooklyn mid-rise ankle pants. Then of course there’s the workout wear—soft, stretchy leggings and sports bras well suited to yoga or Pilates classes; T-shirts and lightweight jackets to wear on your way to and from the studio; and more.

Athleta does tend to be the most expensive athleisure option in the family (compared to Old Navy and Gap Body), so if you’re looking to save, you’re in the right place—keep reading for the latest Athleta promo codes and sale items available this Black Friday.

Today's steepest discounts include 60% off the comfortable Favorite Cotton Relaxed Top, Foundation Cap Sleeve Tee, and the Elemental Puffer Jacket (bringing the winter coat's cost down to under $100!).

Recent findings suggest that I recommend the Brooklyn mid-rise ankle pant, a great work-travel hybrid pant; most colors are 40% off, with a pretty brown shade 50% off. The Seasoft mid-rise straight pant (up to 50% off) is another favorite—I just flew in them the other day. Fall and winter-friendly pieces are marked down too, like the Seasoft classic quarter zip (40% off) and Elation ultra high-rise leggings (these are SO soft and comfortable, and as much as 50% off in certain colors).

Of note, some pieces may be final sale, meaning no returns are allowed, so keep an eye on the fine print when checking out.

Of note, if you have a separate Mastercard, you can earn 15 points for each $1 spent in Athleta's family of retail brands, and 5 points for each $1 spent everywhere else Mastercard is accepted.
