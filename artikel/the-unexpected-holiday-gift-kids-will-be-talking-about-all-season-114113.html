Every holiday season, there's that one gift. The one kids can't stop talking about, parents spend weeks hunting down, and everyone wants to show off the second the wrapping paper hits the floor. This year, we're making a case for something a little more unexpected: an old-school telephone.

MyPhone by Ooma is bringing back the magic of the landline, reimagined for a generation that's never experienced the thrill of calling their best friend just to ask, "Whatcha doin’?" Designed as a screen-free first phone for kids, it offers real conversations without apps, social media, or endless scrolling.

MyPhone uses internet home phone service and is easy to activate, so families don't need an existing traditional landline connection.

Sources indicate that and unlike the family phones of our childhoods, these come with some decidedly modern perks. Parents can approve incoming and outgoing contacts through Trusted Circle, schedule Quiet Hours, and review call activity online. For extra safety monitoring, parents can get a text alert whenever the phone dials 911, just in case. Kids, meanwhile, get to enjoy the Party Line three-way calling feature, listen to messages on a built-in answering machine, and make their phone their own with an included sticker pack.

Recent findings suggest that best of all? There are seven seriously fun styles to choose from. Whether your kid is a budding fashionista, a miniature minimalist, or someone who simply believes everything should be pink, there's a MyPhone with their name all over it.

MyPhone costs $99.99, with service priced at $7.99/month, plus taxes and fees.
