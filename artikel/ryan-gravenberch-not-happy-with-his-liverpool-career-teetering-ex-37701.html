Ryan Gravenberch will have to improve if he is to reclaim his position at Liverpool this season.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 24-year-old once-untouchable appears to have been usurped by teenager Trey Nyoni, who has broken into Andoni Iraola's team in recent weeks.

The 19-year-old was selected from the start in midfield for the visit of Arsenal at the start of the month, with Gravenberch forced to sit on the bench in a seeming change of the guard.

The Netherlands international has been a key man for Liverpool over the last couple of years but has allowed mistakes to creep into his game this season.

Despite only signing a upcoming deal until 2032 in March, some are now even questioning Gravenberch's future at Anfield.

Gravenberch missed Liverpool's last two games before the international break through injury.

He will hope to return to the squad against Manchester United on Sunday with renewed energy, with his position under threat.

"He won't play every game. He'll probably get rested for a little bit and then Gravenberch will come in," King told Football Insider when asked about Nyoni's place in the team.

"For me, he's the future, this type of player. I like the boy [Jeremy Jacquet] they brought in at centre half as well. I like him.

According to recent reports, "I think those two players are players that can can certainly be taking Liverpool to the next step. Gravenberch will be part of the squad.

"Of course, he's not happy because he's not in the team, but that's football. There are 11 starting positions. When you've got somebody who's in there performing like this kid's performing, you're just going to have to wait your time because you'll probably get a chance.

"Maybe he picks up an injury or it might be time to rest him. But I think Gravenberch has certainly got to up his game if he's going to get a regular place back in the side because this kid is a breath of fresh air.".

Just a few months ago, Gravenberch was recognised among the best midfielders in the Premier League and the future of Liverpool's midfield.

But it has taken just a poor start to the season and some bad decisions for the midfielder to lose some of his manager's trust.

The Netherlands international was sent off for a silly challenge against Manchester City last month and gave a penalty for a needless tackle in the Carabao Cup against Chelsea.

Nyoni brings calm to the Liverpool team and until Gravenberch can again, he will be Iraola's second choice.
