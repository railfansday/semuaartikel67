According to recent reports, even better? It’s a breeze to make — just 1 pot required! What are you waiting for? Get that pasta water boiling!

Bolognese is an Italian sauce that gets its name from the Italian city of Bologna. It’s typically made with meat, soffrito (onions, celery, and carrots), white wine, milk, and tomato paste or sauce, all simmered to create a rich, flavorful sauce.

Our eggplant version strays from the traditional as it excludes meat (and includes eggplant) and incorporates dairy alternatives. But the rest of the dish is very similar to traditional bolognese and feels so hearty it might convert even the most adamant veggie skeptics!

This 1-pot eggplant bolognese is everything you want for a cozy pasta night, minus the fuss! It starts with sautéing onion and carrots to create a simple, savory-sweet base. Next, we add eggplant (cut into very small cubes) and let it sauté until it has softened and browned (hello, flavor!).

Sources indicate that build the flavor even further with garlic and red pepper flakes and by deglazing the pan with wine. Crushed tomatoes and tomato paste ensure a bright, tomato-forward sauce, and a small amount of dairy-free milk (we like homemade cashew milk) gives it a gentle creaminess and richness. The final flavor touches include dried Italian herbs (basil + oregano), sea salt, and maple syrup to balance the acidity of the tomatoes.

Industry observers note that once all the ingredients are in your pan, it’s time to let the heat do its magic. Simmering for at least 20-30 minutes allows the sauce to thicken and concentrates the flavors as liquid evaporates away. If you have the time, you can let it continue simmering for up to an hour for even more flavor!

According to recent reports, when it’s done simmering, you’ll be left with an oh-so delicious, plant-powered sauce perfect for serving with your favorite pasta! It would also be lovely over polenta and is elevated by garnishing with fresh basil and our homemade vegan parmesan cheese.
