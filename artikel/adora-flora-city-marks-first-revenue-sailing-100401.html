📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Adora Flora City departed from Guangzhou Nansha International Cruise Home Port on Sunday, November 22, beginning a six-day, five-night commercial maiden voyage.

Sources indicate that adora remarked that the departure marks a step forward in scaling up operations. At the ceremony, Olympic diving champion Guo Jingjing served as the ship’s Launch Ambassador.

Jingjing stated: “I am honored to serve as the Launch Ambassador and to witness this ‘national treasure’ set sail.”.

In 2027, the Adora Flora City will operate more than 50 international cruise itineraries from its homeport. In addition to five- and six-day Southeast Asian routes, the ship will introduce nearly 10 nine-day, eight-night “South Seas Charm” itineraries.

The ship will also offer several no-destination “cruise-to-nowhere” voyages.

Shi Dai, Chairman of China Tourism Group, said the group aims to set a upcoming benchmark for sea-based holiday destinations and “Chinese service.”.

The group is implementing decisions and arrangements made by the Party Central Committee, leading to the establishment of a central state-owned enterprise cruise operations platform organization.

Dai added: “The Adora Flora City embodies the spirit of scientific innovation and craftsmanship of CSSC.

Recent findings suggest that “The Adora Flora City integrates Chinese aesthetics, Lingnan culture and the comfort of a modern cruise experience. It carries people’s aspirations for a better life and reflects the firm confidence of China’s cruise industry in pursuing innovation and progress,” he said.

As the sister ship of the Adora Magic City, the Adora Flora City has undergone upgrades to its spatial design, technology and onboard cruise experience.

According to Adora, it aims to provide guests from China and abroad with a “more beautiful, more technological, and more Chinese” cultural exploration journey at sea.
