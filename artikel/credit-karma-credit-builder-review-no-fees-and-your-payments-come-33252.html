Sources indicate that open Credit Karma Credit Builder if Credit Karma prompts you to apply and you can pay on time every pay period. It charges no fees or interest, reports your payments to all three credit bureaus, and returns what you pay as savings, $500 at a time. Set it to pay automatically so no payment runs late.

Sources indicate that each pay period, Credit Builder moves the amount you choose from a $1,000 line of credit into a locked savings account, and you pay that amount back. Credit Karma offers it to members with little or damaged credit history, and those whose TransUnion credit score is 619 or lower may see a prompt to apply.

It has been revealed that for members Credit Karma prompts to apply, building a payment history here costs nothing, and every dollar they pay stays theirs as savings.
