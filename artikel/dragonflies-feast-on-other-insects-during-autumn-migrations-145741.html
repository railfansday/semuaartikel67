Dragonflies feast on other insects during autumn migrations

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (48 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that dragonflies feast on a host of insects—and each other—during their autumn migrations, new research demonstrates. Scientists studied dragonflies at the Pass of Bujaruelo—a 30-meter (98-foot) gap between two high peaks in the Pyrenees, through which millions of insects migrate every year.
