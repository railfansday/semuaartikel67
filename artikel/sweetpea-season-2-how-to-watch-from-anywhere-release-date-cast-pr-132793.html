It has been revealed that after her serial killer antics in the previous outing, Rhiannon is on a cover up mission, but with a copycat killer on the loose, no sooner is one body hidden than another drops. But her biggest enemy is her own conscience, as looking at deeds similar to hers from afar causes Rhiannon to question her supposedly righteous crusade of violence.

Sources indicate that as if that wasn’t enough to deal with, there’s also problems at work thanks to a upcoming, demanding boss (Taj Atwal), while Rhiannon’s sister (Alexandra Dowling) is pestering her to hurry up with her part in selling the family home.
