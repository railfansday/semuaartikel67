Angie Katsanevas Reveals Estranged Husband Shawn Trujillo Bought a New $1.1 Million Home Near Family’s Modern Utah Mansion: ‘We Are Not Reconciling’

Four months after announcing their split, “The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City” cast member Angie Katsanevas and her estranged husband, Shawn Trujillo, are now living in separate homes.

Recent findings suggest that “Recently, Shawn has bought a place that’s two minutes down the street. And I’m at home with Elektra,” Katsanevas revealed in a new People magazine interview.

Industry observers note that according to a Page Six report, Trujillo's new pad is a $1.1 million condo he purchased in September. The two-story dwelling spans 2,568 square feet and includes three bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms.

The couple, who married in June 1999, have yet to legally divorce, but are "moving toward" that status.

“We are separated. We haven’t put it on paper, but we are not reconciling as far as staying married,” the reality TV star, 53, explained. “I hate the big D-word, but I’ll just have to say that, yeah, we are moving toward the big D.”.

The salon owner’s home—which viewers will recognize from the reality series—is a sprawling hillside estate that has been on and off the market for the past two years.

The four-bedroom, three-bathroom modern abode was first listed for $4.5 million in September 2024. The price was decreased to $4.38 million in March 2025, then to $3.85 million just two months later.

According to recent reports, by December of last year, the listing was removed. It hit the market again in January 2026 for $3.85 million, where it sat for most of the year before being removed on Oct. 14.

In a recent development, the couple purchased the 6,369-square-foot residence for an undisclosed sum in 2020. With soaring ceilings, white ash floors, and lots of sleek, marble surfaces, the modern abode has a distinct luxurious aesthetic.

Sources indicate that the home has two primary suites, with one that includes a fireplace, sitting area, and double-vanity bathroom with a striking view of the surrounding Utah landscape. There are also dual walk-in closets.

The second is located on the main floor and also has two-walk in closets and a gorgeous bathroom.

According to recent reports, features in pairs seem to be a feature of the home in general, as there are also two kitchens. In addition to the chic white minimalist space at the center of the home, a concealed catering kitchen is equipped with Miele appliances, a separate refrigerator and freezer, and a space for staff to prepare food.

According to recent reports, it seems as if, despite the separation, daily life hasn’t changed too much in the home. Katsanevas noted that Trujillo stops by most days to pick up their 15-year-old daughter, Elektra, and take her to school.

Industry observers note that “He’ll come in and have coffee,” she explained. “He’ll bring her home from lessons, and we’ll have dinner together. We are still just spending time together. We’re best friends. We are peaceful, it’s positive. We have a lot to do together.”.

She noted that Trujillo remains close with her father and will celebrate the holidays with the family.

It has been revealed that “My dad sends him all of his favorite dishes and continues to cook for him,” mentioned Katsanevas. “Shawn will spend Thanksgiving and Christmas with my family.”.

Although they are still connected in many ways, Katsanevas noted that after nearly three decades together, “space and doing things independently” have brought clarity.

Recent findings suggest that “We are even going to different churches now,” she shared. “And that’s huge for me. We started out going to the same church.”.

Katsanevas also shut down rumors that she had initiated the split over their opposing political stances, a rumor that started after she cited their “different views” in a confessional interview during a recent episode of “The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m not throwing a 30-year marriage away because of rumors or political views,” she said. “There are so many things that Shawn and I have grown apart and have different views on, a lot of things at this stage of life. And it’s not one thing. It’s just, we’re very different people.”.

They’re still business partners, however, and are set to open a new hair salon together in Miami.

“We are continuing to grow our brand together, even though we are separated,” she said.

Katsanevas and Trujillo currently operate nine locations of their business, Lunatic Fringe Salon, across Utah, Ohio, and Idaho.

While she’s focused on remaining positive, Katsanevas noted it took some time to work through the complicated feelings that accompanied the change.

According to recent reports, “It does take a minute to mourn the loss of the way your life once was and what it looked like,” she said. “I’ve processed, I’ve cried, I’ve accepted. I’ve had to swim away from the muddy waters to get to the clear water and say, ‘OK, I’m coming out of this and I’m going to be OK.’”.
