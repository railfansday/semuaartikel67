Happy 25th anniversary of Xbox day! To celebrate the occasion, we've got eight games joining the Xbox Game Pass Essential tier, mostly from the Xbox 360 era - and all classic games in their own right.

All three Banjo-Kazooie entries are fantastic, of course (even Nuts & Bolts!), while Perfect Dark is an FPS masterpiece of its time, and Viva Pinata just celebrated its 20th anniversary. Halo: Spartan Assault is a fun twin-stick shooter, and Gears of War: Judgment is the perfect next entry if you've just finished Gears of War: E-Day's campaign.

Just to clarify, you've already got access to all of these games if you're part of Xbox Game Pass Ultimate or Xbox Game Pass Premium already, but this marks the first time they've become available to Xbox Game Pass Essential members.
