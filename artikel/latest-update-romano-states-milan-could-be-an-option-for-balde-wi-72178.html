According to Fabrizio Romano, AC Milan could be involved in the market for Alejandro Balde once again this winter.

According to recent reports, A central defender would be the main target for most Milan fans ahead of the winter mercato, but the possibility of signing another wing-back wouldn’t be turned down. In fact, with the left-side still proving to be a puzzling area, someone may need to be recruited.

In the past, Balde has been linked and there have been several other suggestions. However, the Spaniard may be the most accessible mid-way through the season and it would seem that the Rossoneri are considering him again.

Speaking on his YouTube channel, Romano looked at the left-back’s future and didn’t deny that the Diavolo could be involved for another mercato, as MilanNews relayed.

“In the last few hours, Barcelona’s sporting director Deco spoke about Alejandro Balde and said that they are not planning on Balde’s exit because we are only in mid-November; there is a month and a half to go until January.

Recent findings suggest that “Barça play every two or three days and a player like Balde can clearly still be useful between now and the transfer market. It’s true that Barça has left-back cover at the moment, but Barça doesn’t want to put a player like Balde on the market publicly already in mid-November.

“The reality behind the scenes is different: Balde remains a player who could leave in January, because Jorge Mendes, the player’s agent, was in Barcelona this week to speak with the player and discuss a strategy.

“Balde could therefore leave, but we must be realistic because there are significant figures at stake, given that he earns more than €5 million net and in the summer Barcelona were asking for €50 million for him.

“We’ll see if the formula can change in January, but on the one hand, Deco says so, on the other, there’s the reality: if Balde doesn’t play as well as he hasn’t been playing between now and January, the idea of ​​Balde leaving could become concrete.

Sources indicate that “We told you that he’s right now being offered to Roma, but the Giallorossi haven’t really picked up steam yet. They’ve also offered him to Milan; the player was initially proposed last summer, but without a transfer, nothing happened.

“We’ll see what happens with Alejandro Balde, but he’s a name for the international or possibly Italian market that we need to keep there.”.

Considering Sankhoun Diawara is now being considered as a left-back, the situation is certainly not ideal. So, Milan could certainly do worse than signing Balde.
