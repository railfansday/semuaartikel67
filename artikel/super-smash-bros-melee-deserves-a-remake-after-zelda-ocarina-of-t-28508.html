Super Smash Bros Melee deserves a remake after Zelda: Ocarina of Time, say ex-Nintendo marketing leads

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nintendo's former marketing leads Kit Ellis and Krysta Yang believe that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake will reinforce how lucrative remakes can be for the company going forward.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is a pretty massive success so far; it instantly became the biggest physical launch of 2026 in the UK so far, as well as the US, and the second best-selling Nintendo Switch 2 game after Mario Kart World (which was bundled with the console), with its 5.2 million sales.

This comes after Star Fox had a pretty good time with its N64 remake released earlier this year, and of course years and years of Sony, Capcom, Konami, and everyone else going to the remake well over the years.

Ellis notes it "does reinforce" Nintendo's direction with these projects, which is "remakes can be really lucrative and really popular.".

While remakes can be effectively used to fill a place in a schedule without the need to go all-in on a newly introduced game – as we saw with Star Fox earlier this year – "if you have the right game and you do it in the right way it can be one of your biggest releases on the platform," which seems very likely to hold in Ocarina of Time's case.

Yang adds, "You're looking at the numbers, and you think, you're saying to yourself like, 'Wow, I didn't put as much effort into something' in comparison to something like Tears of the Kingdom or Breath of the Wild, which had very long development cycles, but still 'I made a lot of money.'".

As for what Nintendo should remake, Yang points out franchises that could be reignited by a remake – like Star Fox seemingly has been – such as Golden Sun. While Ellis puts out there that Super Smash Bros. Melee should be the one. There's been a lot of talk about "what do you do after Ultimate?" and a remake of the series' most beloved game could give the devs "some time to figure it out.".

However, due to the fanatical community Melee has, he doesn't know if it could happen, as "this is the ultimate example of the fan base being ready to revolt on the slightest change.".
