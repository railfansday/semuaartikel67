Hearts will attempt to claim a major European scalp when they host French giants Monaco in the Uefa Conference League on Thursday evening.

Wouter Vrancken’s side go into the match as underdogs but are in need of a positive result after collecting just one point from their opening three league phase fixtures to sit 32nd in the Conference League table.

A 1-1 draw against Norwegian side Nordsjælland at Tynecastle was followed by away defeats at Trabzonspor (2-0) and FC Thun (3-1) to leave the Tynecastle club facing an uphill task to qualify for the knockout stages.

Teams typically need seven or eight points from their six league phase matches to finish inside the top 24 and reach the knockout play-off round. Eight points virtually guarantees progression, while seven points usually puts a club right on the cut-off line where tie-breakers decide who advances.

Hearts know this only too well, having failed to progress on goals scored after collecting seven points to finish 25th during their 2024-25 Conference League campaign.

According to recent reports, monaco arrive in Edinburgh fourth in the table with a maximum haul of nine points following victories over CSKA Sofia, Nordsjælland and Freiburg. Their domestic form has been less impressive of late with Filipe Luís’ side right now on a five-game winless run on Ligue 1, where they are eighth in the standings.

The Conference League match between Hearts and Monaco takes place at Tynecastle Park, Edinburgh, on Thursday, November 26, 2026. Kick-off is 5.45pm.

The match will be shown live on TNT Sports 3 with coverage starting at 5pm.

Fans can stream the match live via HBO Max or Amazon Prime Video with a TNT Sports subscription.

Free highlights will be posted on the TNT Sports Football YouTube channel shortly after full-time.

Recent findings suggest that hearts welcome midfielder Blair Spittal back into their Europa League squad following his recovery from an ankle injury. Malachi Fagan-Walcott drops out with a groin problem. Two players who featured in the 8-0 win over Dundee on Saturday, Eduardo Ageu and Leart Kabashi, cannot play as they are not registered in the Europa League squad list.

Sources indicate that midfielder Tomas Magnusson is still completing concussion protocol before he can be declared available. Defender Craig Halkett and the versatile Oisin McEntee were back on the Riccarton training pitch on Wednesday, although they will not be back until next month. Winger Calvin Miller has also undergone surgery on his knee and will be out for between six weeks and two months.

Stephen Kingsley (hamstring), Harry Milne (knee) and Tom Renaud (ankle) are also unavailable.

It has been revealed that monaco are without five players. Goalkeeper Philippe Kohn, defender Mohammed Salisu, midfielders Stanis Idumbo and Aleksandr Golovin, plus forward Paris Brunner were all left in the Principality for treatment.

Recent findings suggest that an all-German officiating team will take charge of the match. Daniel Schlager is the on-field referee and will be supported on the touchlines by assistant referees Sven Waschitzki and Tobias Fritsch. Robin Braun has been appointed as the fourth official. In the video operations room, Benjamin Cortus will serve as the Video Assistant Referee (VAR), alongside Nicolas Winter as the Assistant VAR (AVAR).
