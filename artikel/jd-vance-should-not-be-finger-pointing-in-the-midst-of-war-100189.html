It has been revealed that president Donald Trump told Fox News’s Jacqui Heinrich that as for the Iranians who continue to find latest ways to threaten Americans, “I’m going to finish it off — soon. One way or the other, we’ll finish it off.” In that same interview, he remarked he never liked the infamous Memorandum of Understanding, which gave the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps a cash infusion even as it continued firing on commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.

Recent findings suggest that the president has removed Vice President JD Vance, the face of and defender of the MOU, from leading negotiations with the Iranians, whom Vance claimed the U.S. had reformed. Weeks and dozens of attempts to kill Americans later, in response to Senator Tom Cotton’s questioning, National Counterterrorism Center Acting Director Joseph Weirsky told Congress what every American can see: “There’s no indication there’s any turning over of leaves with the regime.”.
