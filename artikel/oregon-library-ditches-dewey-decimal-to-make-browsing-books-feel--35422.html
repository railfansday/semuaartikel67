Oregon library ditches Dewey Decimal to make browsing books feel more like a bookstore

🌐 Source: Flipso • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (50 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, deschutes Public Library in Bend, Oregon, redesigned its branches around browse-friendly categories, lower modular shelving, and a Wayfinder system that makes books easier to discover. Staff say the new layout has made the collection more inviting and books move swiftly off display.
