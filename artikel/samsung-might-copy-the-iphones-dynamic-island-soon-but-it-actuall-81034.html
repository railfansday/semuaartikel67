A upcoming leak suggests that Samsung is preparing to copy the iPhone’s Dynamic Island feature in the not-too-distant future, to an extent that not many other Android brands have even done thus far.

Copying the Dynamic Island was briefly like a rite of passage for some Android brands, with many wasting almost no time in delivering their own version of the feature. But, on many of those devices, it’s a surface-level ripoff at best. The feature is primarily limited to music apps and timers, rarely working with any third-party apps in meaningful ways. Android has a built-in feature similar to Apple’s but not in the same styling.

Samsung is now preparing its own version of the Dynamic Island, and it seems this one has a bit more meat on its bones.

SamMobile on Twitter/X posted a video and two still images of what appears to be a Dynamic Island clone coming to Galaxy devices. It’s mentioned as potentially coming to the Galaxy S27 series (so, One UI 9.5) and being called “Moment Effects.” A portion of the software explicitly mentions integration with Google Sports, the leak claims, while it seems Samsung is using Live Notifications here, which already support a number of third-party apps.

The kicker from a UX perspective is that Samsung’s interpretation allows you to swipe between activities, similar to Apple, but the core premise is similar with the island showing around the selfie camera cutout.

When this feature might surface does remain unclear, but a One UI 9.5 debut would make plenty of sense. That update is in beta now and due to launch on the Galaxy S27 series early next year.

What do you think of Samsung’s alleged Dynamic Island clone? Personally, I’m on board as long as it proves useful, and it seems to be on the right track compared to most other copycats.
