According to recent reports, the overall market picture is grim, to say the least. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index was at 12 on Nov. 27, in extreme fear territory. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs had lost $453 million two days earlier. HYPE, SOL and FET all bounced into that market, with four-hour momentum improving across the board. On the daily charts, all three are still below important moving averages.

Hyperliquid (HYPE) successfully bounced above $60 price level and even touched the 200 EMA on its way up. The touch itself doesn't provide a bullish signal on its own, but a substantial improvement to a market dynamic in general.

The MACD histogram that measures both momentum and trend strength is turning positive, so the cryptocurrency market is aiming at picking up the momentum. Even though the overall dynamic remains negative, early signs of a price recovery are there.

According to recent reports, to confirm the price recovery traders quite often look at momentum indicators like RSI–RSI reflects how strong or weak the current trend is. With the RSI of HYPE at 43.8 it's safe to say that the assets remain in the neutral region, where the momentum is somewhat sidelined.

Recent findings suggest that without a major psychological threshold, moving averages are the only benchmarks for us. The price has reached the 20-day moving average after violating $67 price level. That would be the first resistance that should be broken. Without it, the short-term rally is premature and the trend we see here is loose.

Sources indicate that longer-term moving averages are way higher and provide little to no options for the retrace in the short term. What we should be looking at here is whether or not HYPE is capable of securing a position above the 20-day moving average. A failure to do so would put the asset back in the bearish range.

The $60 price level remains the main benchmark for the downward price dynamic. A move below it would essentially invalidate the majority of the progress HYPE has made so far.

According to recent reports, SOL ended 27th Nov. at ~$73.06, after a modest 3.3% rise. However, as seen with other names, this has failed to generate any convincing positive technicals on the daily. The 14d RSI is currently 37.5 and SOL trades under all 8 MAs monitored.

Sources indicate that the MACD histogram has risen to +0.85, showing reduced bearish pressure, but the MACD line remains in negative territory. The 4h RSI is again more favorable at ~63, but the 7d RSI highlights continued heavy selling.

Sources indicate that as noted above, SOL's next resistance is $74.19, beyond which $76.72 presents the following target. As discussed, a significant MA block exists in the form of the 20d (~$75-$76) and 50d (~$90.6). SOL is 28% down off its high in late October at ~$102, meaning only a limited correction has been offset to date.

According to recent reports, as mentioned above, FET saw the biggest bounce out of the three with a daily gain of ~12.7% and is right now trading near $0.1278 as of November 27. That being said, although momentum appears to have the upper hand at the moment due in part to broader risk appetite, the bounce higher could still be viewed as an overreaction considering FET’s technicals.

In a recent development, on the daily chart, FET has bounced back towards its 20-day moving average after bottoming out. For a bounce to morph into a potential reversal FET will need to close the day above $0.1347 with $0.1529 and $0.1685 also acting as resistance. Longer-term, there is still plenty of overhead resistance with FET still trading well below its 50-day SMA ($0.161), 100-day SMA ($0.174) and 200-day SMA ($0.176).

Recent findings suggest that on the other side of the coin, $0.1178 serves as the first level of support with $0.1122 and $0.1047 also acting as support. In addition, although FET has seen a bounce higher its RSI (44.9 on the daily chart and 73 on the 4-hour chart) and MACD histogram (marginally positive) paint a picture of a technicals that are stretched on the 4-hour chart and still not confirming a reversal on the daily chart (weekly RSI is also weak at 40.7).

It has been revealed that all things considered, a pullback from current levels wouldn’t be surprising. That being noted, the Alliance’s AI-infrastructure narrative could continue to pique interest down the line.

Overall, for HYPE to continue higher in the short-term it will need to break through resistance at $68.63. For SOL to continue higher in the short-term it will need to reclaim $74–$77. For FET to continue higher in the short-term it will need to hold above $0.1178 and break through $0.1347. For November 28, confirmation will be key for all three.
