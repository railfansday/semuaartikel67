XRP ETF Buyers Suddenly Return

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

U.S. spot exchange-traded funds for XRP recorded net inflows of $4.07 million on Nov. 25. XRP was the only cryptocurrency among the 10 ETF categories tracked in a SoSoValue snapshot shared by BankXRP to register net inflows.

In a recent development, wednesday's inflows narrowed the net outflow gap for XRP ETFs. Following the latest inflows, XRP funds had recorded $21.25 million more in investor outflows than inflows over the 30 days ending Nov. 25.

That means the net outflows over the rest of the period totaled $25.32 million. In a single trading day, investors covered about 16% of that shortfall.

Recent findings suggest that the five U.S. spot XRP ETFs tracked by SoSoValue at the moment have total net assets of $1.04 billion. This is marginally higher than the $1 billion held by nine spot Solana ETFs.

However, there is a larger disparity in cumulative net inflows between XRP and Solana funds. Cumulative net inflows total $1.79 billion for XRP and $1.51 billion for Solana.

In a recent development, the two figures measure different things. Cumulative net inflows represent the total amount of money invested in the funds minus redemptions.

In a recent development, net assets, on the other hand, represent the current value of a fund's holdings. This figure changes with the price of XRP.

This explains why XRP funds have a little over $1 billion in net assets despite attracting nearly $1.8 billion in cumulative net inflows.

Trading activity was slightly higher in Solana ETFs on Wednesday, with total volume reaching $35.54 million, compared to $33.40 million for XRP.

However, Solana ETFs recorded zero net flows for the day.

Investors can trade existing ETF shares with one another without affecting the total amount of money invested in the funds.

The contrast with Bitcoin ETFs was stark.

Industry observers note that U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $123.41 million in net outflows on Wednesday, according to preliminary figures reported by SoSoValue.

However, the total for Bitcoin was still being compiled when BankXRP shared the numbers. SoSoValue marked the XRP total as complete.

With U.S. markets closed for Thanksgiving, XRP ETF buyers have only one more trading day this week.

According to recent reports, trading resumes on U.S. equity markets on Friday, Nov. 27. However, the session will end early, with markets closing at 1 p.m. Eastern time.
