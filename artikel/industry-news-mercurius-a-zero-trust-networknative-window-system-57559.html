Pick up your work exactly where life made you stop — on the same machine or from elsewhere.

Mercurius gives you long‑lived graphical sessions that you can leave, return to, and resume whether you’re on the same laptop or somewhere else on the network. The session model works locally without any networking at all; the network simply lets your sessions inhabit more powerful machines when you want them to.

Industry observers note that sometimes life interrupts. Work calls, dinner is ready, or you need to switch tasks. Mercurius treats sessions as first-class objects so your applications belong to the session, not the physical device in front of you.

In a recent development, suspend a coding session, switch to a gaming session, and resume either one later with every window exactly as you left it. Built for powerful workstations, modern GPUs, and fast local networks, Mercurius uses the network to extend your experience, not to justify its existence.

Sources indicate that it used to be normal to share the big, expensive computer in the corner. Today everyone has a laptop, but the economics of computing have changed again: RAM and GPUs are costly, and workstation‑class machines deliver far more capability than most people carry around. Mercurius lets you use that capability without giving up the convenience of your own environment.

In a recent development, mercurius keeps your work on your own machines, so you’re never at the mercy of a cloud subscription just to read your own documents. With the rising cost of local RAM and high‑end GPUs, sharing the expensive computer again is a smart alternative. Mercurius lets an organisation concentrate its investment in a few powerful GPU‑rich workstations and make full use of them, instead of buying every developer a high‑end laptop that can’t run the heavy workloads anyway.
