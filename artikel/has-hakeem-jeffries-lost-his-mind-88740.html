Per Politico, the incoming speaker of the House, Hakeem Jeffries, wishes to massively increase federal entitlements:.

Industry observers note that I’m sorry: What? What is Jeffries talking about? Have he and those praising him taken leave of their senses? The United States is $41 trillion in debt. The federal government is running annual deficits of $2 trillion. We paid more in interest last year than we spent on defense. Medicare is unsustainable as it is. Social Security is just six years away from mandatory benefit cuts. And the Democratic Party’s leader in the House wants to add a vast new spending program onto the pile. Do my eyes deceive me?

Charles C. W. Cooke is a senior editor at National Review and the host of The Charles C. W. Cooke Podcast.
