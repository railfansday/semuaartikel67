Argentine goalkeeper Ezequiel Bacher scored perhaps the most bizarre own goal ever during his side's recent Primera B play-off game.

Comunicaciones were taking on Arsenal Sarandi at the weekend with the score 0-0, and the goalkeeper had caught the ball from a cross with the aim of getting the game back under way.

As he tried to throw the ball out, though, he got it all wrong and ended up launching the ball into his own net.

Bacher, 30, essentially failed to let go of the ball at the right time, leaving his team-mates stunned.

The player himself covered his face with his hand before retrieving the ball to get play started again.

Recent findings suggest that arsenal players wheeled off in celebration, only for Comunicaciones to level things up in the game soon after. Arsenal, though, scored again, and eventually won 3-2 in the first leg of the tie.

Argentine goalkeeper Ezequiel Bacher scored a bizarre own goal during his side's third-tier play-off game.

The 30-year-old failed to release the ball from a throw and released the ball into his own net.

Fans flooded to social media to react to the scarcely believable own goal.

It has been revealed that one wrote on X: 'Haha, those who think it's because of betting. That's the level of Argentine football.'.

Another posted: 'RIP Argentinian football.'.

A third shared: 'The Arsenal lot celebrating... you've got some nerve.'.

Bacher joined the club at the start of last year, but has always played in his homeland of Argentina.

His Comunicaciones side finished ninth in their league group of Primera B, the third tier of Argentinian football, and were taken on by an Arsenal side that came second.

They will face off in the return leg next Saturday.
