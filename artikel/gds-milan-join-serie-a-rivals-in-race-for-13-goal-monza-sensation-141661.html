AC Milan have positioned themselves as one of the contenders in the race to sign Gustavo Varela, according to a report.

Industry observers note that if someone had told Varela in August that three months later he’d be on double figures for goals in Serie A (11, 13 all competitions), perhaps even he wouldn’t have believed it. As a result of his exploits, La Gazzetta dello Sport confirm many top clubs in Italy are watching him with growing interest.

It has been revealed that napoli were the first team to make a move, but Milan are also in the mix, as are Inter and Juventus. That’s not all: several teams in the Premier League are following him, and worries about Moise Kean’s condition have seen Como enter the picture too.

Sources indicate that more specifically, Napoli want to offload Lorenzo Lucca and have already made contact with Varela’s entourage. However, they must first sell to reach the figures Monza wants. Juventus also need a striker, but Financial Fair Play requires Juventus them to make a sale too.

Como spent €40m on Kean this summer but risk having to return to the market due to the Italian’s long lay-off. Inter are also interested in Varela, but they don’t need to strengthen their attack immediately.

Industry observers note that milan are looking for more goals and have their eye on Nicolo Tresoldi, but Varela could become a worthy alternative, considering he’s already familiar with Serie A. Compared to the other domestic competitors, the price matters least to the Rossoneri.

Recent findings suggest that after scoring a gorgeous back-heel goal against Fiorentina, Varela was asked about his future: “The backheel goal was pure instinct. It’s not something I try in training. A great cross came in, I was right there near the goal, and I decided to give it a try.

According to recent reports, “I continue to thank Monza for letting me be here. It’s all been crazy so far: I feel very lucky to be part of this team, and I only hope it can continue like this. Transfer rumors are part of football. I’m having a great time right now, and I’m just trying to enjoy the present without paying attention to the news.”.

Unfortunately for Varela this feels impossible, considering everyone’s talking about him at the moment, and will continue to while he is scoring goals at the rate he is.
