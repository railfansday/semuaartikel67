There has been an official Arsenal injury revision on Friday morning ahead of the match against Newcastle United on Saturday.

A mixed bag of news for the Gunners.

Arsenal set to be missing at least three players, though one key player is back available.

The Arsenal injury update has come via Mikel Arteta when talking to journalists.

Mikel Arteta asked about England star Bukayo Saka: “We had another test. He is evolving really well but it looks like it’s going to be an eight week injury more or less, so I think it is four at the moment. It is going to be a while for him to be back with the team.”.

In a recent development, the Arsenal boss quizzed on Martin Zubimendi: “He is out [of this Newcastle United match]. Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring. He wasn’t too concerned but the MRI scan indicated there’s an injury there and he’s going to be out for a few weeks.”.

With Max Dowman still suspended after his Fleetwood Town red card, it means at least three Arsenal players miss out at St James’ Park.

One key Arsenal player returns though, Mikel Arteta, speaking to the media about William Saliba: “He is ready. He is in good condition. He played two games, one behind closed doors and one more official and he did really well and he’s in good condition.”.

It has been revealed that mikel Arteta also asked whether Declan Rice is close to signing a new deal : “I need to call the owner. At the moment it is no deal but I think we are quite close but he needs to confirm that. I need the phone call.”.

Industry observers note that after the game against United on Saturday, it will be Saturday 16 January 2027 before Arsenal need to travel outside London for a Premier League match, Mikel Arteta: “We love travelling…we love travelling every time. It is what it is. The schedule brings you these things which on paper are quite unique. First of all, we just want to focus on the game tomorrow, a really tough one and we go again, and again.”.
