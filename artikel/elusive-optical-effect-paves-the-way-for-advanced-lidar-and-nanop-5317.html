Elusive optical effect paves the way for advanced lidar and nanophotonic technologies

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (62 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

An international research team, led by scientists from Skoltech, has experimentally demonstrated for the first time the photonic spin Hall shift for light scattered off an optically small particle. This work, published in the Advanced Photonics journal, overcomes a long-standing challenge in optics and could contribute to the development of next-generation optical technologies, advanced radar and lidar systems.
