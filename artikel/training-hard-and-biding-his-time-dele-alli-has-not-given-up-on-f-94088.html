The 30-year-old former England star combines Twitch streaming with working to return to the top as a player.

Consider the training regime Dele Alli has followed since he left Como 18 months ago and it becomes clear why he takes exception to suggestions he has given up on football in favour of becoming an internet personality.

He trains five or six days a week with Connor Peters, an elite performance coach who has worked with players including Trevoh Chalobah. They spend four days on the pitch and days in between in the gym. A typical day begins at 9am with an hour of activation and stretching before Dele heads to non-league Windsor & Eton at 11am, where he trains on the pitches and uses the facilities. Then it’s back home to use his own oxygen chamber and sauna for a recovery session.
