This salad has me texting all my friends to let them know I have found my new favorite holiday salad.

For years now I’ve been wanting to get a holiday salad in my recipe lineup that works well for all kinds of eaters at a holiday gathering.

Normally, my go-to salads for holidays are the brussels sprouts salad (YUM) and the layered beet and kale salad (GORGEOUS). Those are 10/10 for me. But not everybody in my family loves brussels, kale, and beets, if you know what I mean?

In a recent development, so this is a salad that speaks to almost everybody – myself included. And if you have any nut allergies in your group, candied pepitas are perfect!

(Pictured here with some BBQ meatballs and mac and cheese – because I’m already hooked on it and making it for non-holidays, too!).

Sources indicate that the dressing is SO fun – it’s sweet and festive with the tang of cider vinegar and shallot, and the warmth of cinnamon and maple. And the add-ins on the salad are easy and familiar (store-bought candied nuts, thank you very much).

As the annual salad-bringer in my family, this is what I’m bringing for Thanksgiving this year! And probably Christmas, too!

Three main things: mince the shallot, and thinly slice the apple, and grate a bunch of fluffy parm. 🙂.

This can be done ahead of time! Just store it in the fridge.

Do this right before serving! (I like to reserve some of the apples, walnuts, and Parmesan for making it pretty on top once it’s been tossed up.).

This works well in one significant serving bowl, or on individual salad plates!
