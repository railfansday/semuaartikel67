Money is more vital in Grand Theft Auto 6 than it ever has been before, with Jason and Lucia having countless opportunities to rake in some much-needed dough and make life in Leonida easier.

Just like in GTA 5 and GTA Online, robbing banks is one of the most reliable ways to earn a lot of cash. You won't be able to do so until you unlock a key item, though, so let's get into it.

According to recent reports, if you're brave enough to try and rob a bank or any location with Marked Bills in the early hours of GTA 6, you'll very quickly learn that it's a bad idea. In a nice bit of realism, stealing Marked Bills will set off a dye pack when you leave the bank, ruining your entire haul and covering Jason or Lucia in paint.

Thankfully, there's a way to bypass those annoying dye packs and make robbing banks a viable opportunity. To do so, you'll need to unlock the Dye Pack Jammer, a key item that does exactly what it indicates on the tin and prevents dye packs from exploding. The Dye Pack Jammer is unlocked during the Credit Check story mission in Chapter 3, which means it's unmissable if you put enough time in.

Recent findings suggest that chapter 3 is one of GTA 6's longer acts, and it took me around 40 hours to reach Credit Check. Your mileage will vary depending on how fast you're taking things, however.

Credit Check sees Jason and Lucia being tested on their robbing ability and tasked with hitting up a few different locations. It's one of the game's best missions and, once you've beaten it, you'll be able to steal from any location on the map. In fact, the map will even point this out to you by highlighting a bunch of newly introduced opportunities and pointing you in their direction.

Recent findings suggest that it's worth noting, however, that any location with Marked Bills will have higher security and will need to be more carefully planned. The pay day is usually worth it, but just keep that in mind.
