According to recent reports, the award-winning portable smart resistance trainer is now available online to U.S. customers at NikeStrength.com.

PORTLAND, Ore. and SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Dimension 6 Fitness and Beyond Power today declared a partnership bringing VOLTRA I and a curated selection of Beyond Power equipment to NikeStrength.com.

Smaller than a shoebox and weighing 13 lbs, VOLTRA I delivers up to 230 lbs of resistance and records speed, power and range of motion on every rep. Recognized by TIME, Men's Health and GQ, it is used by teams across the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL and Premier League, and by programs in every NCAA power conference.

VOLTRA I is designed to integrate with the entire Nike Strength training equipment line. It mounts to rack uprights in seconds and moves between stations, adding cable work, variable resistance and performance data to a Nike Strength gym.

In a recent development, "Our customers are building serious gyms, and they expect every piece to earn its place," said Dimension 6 Fitness Founder Drew Conant. "Offering VOLTRA I on NikeStrength.com gives athletes another way to train with intent.".

"Together, we're helping athletes and everyday lifters get more out of their training, wherever they work out," stated Kevin Song, Vice President of Marketing at Beyond Power.

VOLTRA I and select Beyond Power equipment is now available on NikeStrength.com for customers in the United States, with additional markets to follow. The products are sold online only and are not available in stores.

About Dimension 6 FitnessBased in Portland, Oregon, Dimension 6 Fitness is a licensee of Nike with the exclusive rights to design, develop, market and distribute Nike Strength and Jordan Strength training equipment for elite and everyday athletes, from home gyms to professional facilities. Find our full catalog at nikestrength.com.

Sources indicate that about Beyond PowerFounded in 2021, Beyond Power is the sport technology company that invented VOLTRA I. It was built on the belief that resistance training has gone too long without meaningful change. Started by athletes and engineers who ran into the limits of traditional equipment firsthand, the company develops intelligent resistance systems that deliver precision, versatility and data on every rep. Its mission is to democratize advanced training methodologies, so that what was once confined to elite programs is within reach of anyone who trains. More at beyond-power.com.
