📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Leonardo Balerdi has quickly become an important player for Gian Piero Gasperini.

Upon his arrival, the Grugliasco-born coach called him one of the new captains, given that he wore the captain’s armband at Marseille.

Born in 1999, the defender arrived on loan from OM, and the buyout is contingent on the defender’s appearances. To trigger the €11 million obligation, Balerdi will have to play 40% of the matches.

The figure increases if he makes more appearances: €14 million, with over 55% of matches lasting at least 45 minutes.

In a recent development, the former Marseille player could be satisfied with just 20% of the matches in the second half of the season. In this case, Roma would have to pay the French club €9 million.

Gasperini considers him an key player, and in a short time, he’s carved out a significant niche for himself, considering the starting lineup alongside Mancini, Ndicka, and Hermoso: 639 minutes, highlighted by a brace away to Napoli.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, if an automatic buyout were to arrive, all the conditions are in place for this to happen: the Giallorossi club and the player are already in full agreement regarding the contract renewal. In fact, the parties have already put everything down in black and white. The desire of all is to continue together.
