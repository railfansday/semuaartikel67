Birmingham City, Norwich City named among clubs targeting out-of-contract 25-year-old

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that birmingham City and Norwich City are both going to be eyeing reinforcements in the January transfer window. BCFC will pursue additions to strengthen their automatic promotion bid, while the Canaries are hunting down the Championship play-offs.

Now, a latest report from TEAMtalk claims the second-tier duo could go toe-to-toe for one target. Both the Blues and NCFC are interested in Charlotte FC’s out-of-contract winger Liel Abada, it is claimed, after Wolves were named as admirers of the Israeli international at the start of this week,.

Abada will be available as a free agent in January given his expiring contract. Previous reports had said he could be signed and loaned out initially due to a potential work permit matter, but TEAMtalk states those keen are ‘relaxed’ about finding a solution.

Free agent signings are usually reserved for mid-season emergencies or the summer transfer window, when players’ contracts expire. They then get the pre-season to settle into upcoming surroundings and gain fitness before tackling the usual August to May football calendar.

However, Abada’s situation presents a unique opportunity. As one of many overseas leagues that run over the summer, plenty of MLS players are approaching the end of their contracts this winter. And, Charlotte’s Israeli star is among them.

Industry observers note that his availability means that Birmingham City, Norwich City and any other interested club can sign a high value player for nothing in the middle of the campaign. It would be a shrewd way to bolster the ranks, with only signing-on fees and a salary to be thrashed out.

Abada has been a hit in America with 25 goals and 11 assists in 98 games for Charlotte. He previously impressed with Celtic too, notching 29 goals and 22 assists in 112 appearances.

As a result, he would slot into a team towards the top end of the Championship and be expected to deliver. However, Birmingham City and Norwich City both have strong wide options, and that could block Abada’s route to a starting role.

According to recent reports, BCFC have Liam Millar starring on the left, while Carlos Vicente and Patrick Roberts are on the right. With Demarai Gray also in contention, Abada would face a battle to break into the team, and someone will be left on the fringes.

Industry observers note that as for the Canaries, they have Andre Brooks and Anthony Musaba as starters on the flanks at present. Papa Amadou Diallo is also on the books, and he will be expected to compete for a starting berth once fit again.

It means there isn’t a glaring need for Abada at either club despite his quality. On a free, he could prove to be a valuable signing, but he would have to impress to earn a place in their first XI’s.
