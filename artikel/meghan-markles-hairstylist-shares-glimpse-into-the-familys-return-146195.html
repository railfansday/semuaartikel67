Meghan Markle and Prince Harry are settling into life in their new home in the Cotswalds, and according to the Duchess of Sussex's wedding hairstylist, George Northwood, the grass is looking very green in the United Kingdom.

Northwood shared a look inside the family's latest digs in a Nov. 22 Instagram post, noting that he and Markle had recently spent quite a bit of quality time together, including a lunch date and a visit to her fresh property.

It has been revealed that during an early fall trip to Markle's abode, Northwood stated they "picked fruit and vegetables" from her garden. The stylist has recently welcomed a son, seven-month-old Elijah, "who has just started weaning" and is exploring fresh foods.

According to recent reports, "She was very excited for all the new flavors he was going to enjoy from the garden," Northwood recalled. "I remember Archie being the same age when we went on tour to South Africa, so it was a lovely circular moment.".

Later, the duo would head to The Park Restaurant—a New York-inspired eatery just blocks from Kensington Palace. In a blog on the eatery's website, Northwood divulged more about their catchup.

According to recent reports, "I invited Meghan as she has just come back to the U.K. and, though we have stayed in touch via text, we hadn’t had the chance to have a proper download yet," Northwood noted of their lunch date. "Since we last saw each other, she’s had her second baby and I’ve had my first. Catching up was like old times, when we used to have dinner together when I accompanied her on royal tours around the world.".

Northwood added that Markle—who he refers to as "M"—is a wonderful lunch companion because she is "someone with good chat who enjoys a laugh and, crucially, who wants to share the food so that I get to try a bit of everything.".

Industry observers note that the pals enjoyed sea bass, French fries, and key lime pie. Northwood noted that "Meghan was happy to go along" with his pescatarian diet.

He also noted that "there is a lot of baby chat" given his recent "solo surrogacy journey," and that he's picked up plenty of "parenting tips" from the mom of two.

Northwood also shared a sweet photo of Markle smiling with his son at her home, posed beside a basket of colorful garden-picked veggies.

Markle, 45, and Harry, 42, are reported to be residing in a property on an estate belonging to their close friends, according to the Daily Mail.

It has been revealed that they first made intends to establish a private residence in the U.K. in August of this year, six years after quitting their roles as working members of the British royal family to relocate to a $14.7 million home in Montecito, CA.

Although it was recently reported that the seemingly unexpected move was motivated by visa troubles for Harry—with a US government source telling Page Six "his visa expired and wasn't immediately renewed"—the couple's spokesperson denied the claim, telling Newsweek it is "entirely false.".

In an October interview with GQ, Harry expressed that the decision was driven by a desire to seek out "opportunities for the family.".

"Being able to put the kids in a British school has meant a lot to me," he said of Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5. "I didn't know how important it was to me. I always thought it was going to be something that we would try and do, but now they’ve been in school for two weeks. They’re loving it and it’s just giving them a whole different experience.".

He also admitted that "it's nice to be back" on "U.K. soil for longer than a few days or a week here or there.".

The family prepares to stay put for the holidays, marking the children's first British Christmas.

During a Nov. 20 appearance on the British morning show, "This Morning," Harry revealed the family's plans for Thanksgiving and Christmas, noting that mother-in-law Doria Ragland will be joining them.

"My wife is very good in the kitchen and so is my mother-in-law, so I'll leave that to them," he stated, noting that he hopes turkey and gumbo are on the menu.

The decision to stay in England for the holidays also comes from a desire to keep things simple during a difficult time for Markle's family, as her estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr., has been indicted in Florida on two counts of possession of child pornography.

According to recent reports, "Everything going on with her dad is still weighing heavily on her," a source told People. "She wants the holidays to be as quiet and enjoyable as possible for the family.

"Traveling back and forth would be too much for everyone," the source added.
