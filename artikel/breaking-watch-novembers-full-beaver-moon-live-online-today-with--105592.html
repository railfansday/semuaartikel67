November's full moon rises tonight (Nov. 24)! You can see live telescopic views of the "Beaver supermoon" courtesy of the Virtual Telescope Project (VTP).

In a recent development, the free YouTube livestream begins at 10:30 a.m. EST (1530 GMT) and will feature live views of the moon captured by a suite of robotic telescopes in Manciano, Italy, weather permitting.

Today's stream features expert commentary from VTP founder Gianluca Masi, along with photos of previous full moons shining above historic landmarks in the heart of Rome.

November's full moon is often called the Beaver Moon because it rises around the time the industrious animals are hard at work building dams and preparing for the winter months, according to the Old Farmer's Almanac.

It is also set to be a spectacular supermoon, which reaches peak illumination while within 90% of perigee — the closest point to Earth in the moon's 27-day elliptical orbit.
