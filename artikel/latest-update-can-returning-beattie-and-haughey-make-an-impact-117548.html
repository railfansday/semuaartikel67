Latest Update: Can returning Beattie and Haughey make an impact?

Beattie won the last of her 13 caps last year while the uncapped Haughey was last called into the senior squad in 2021.

Michael McArdle has decided the upcoming friendly double-header against Greece is the perfect time to bring back Kerry Beattie and Ella Haughey from the international wilderness.

Glentoran midfielder Chloe McCarron is also in under McArdle for the first time after a lengthily absence, but the Glentoran midfielder would likely have been included sooner if not for a spell on the sidelines through injury.

Beattie won her last cap a year ago while Haughey will hope to make a senior debut in one of the two games against Greece at Inver Park after she was an unused substitute in friendlies against England and Scotland in her sole call up in 2021.

The inclusion of both players, who have been in good form at their clubs, shows McArdle is willing to keep the door open for a return to the international squad if players are performing at club level.

Magill returns to NI squad for Greece friendlies.

Beattie has four goals in her 13 NI caps.

Portsmouth striker Beattie has been in and out of squads since her debut in 2021.

The 24-year-old has four goals in 13 caps, the last of which she won against Iceland in the Nations League play-off a year ago.

A prolific forward during two spells at Glentoran, she has struggled for consistency in moves across to Scotland with the likes of Glasgow City, Hearts and Aberdeen.

She left the Dons in the summer and consulted with McArdle when deciding upon her next move, before joining WSL 2 side Portsmouth.

Beattie has eight goals in all competitions for a Pompey side languishing at the bottom of the table and McArdle is pleased she has settled in well.

"Kerry had spells at Hearts and Aberdeen and for whatever reason it didn't quite work out at both, she had good spells but not a consistent run to express herself," McArdle said.

Sources indicate that "The move to Portsmouth, I was delighted she reached out to me for some advice, it's an alignment for her and it's been a great move on and off the pitch. It's allowed her to enjoy herself but also play 90 minutes and be that focal point striker.

"She will have been disappointed not to make the squad for the [World Cup] play-off [against Portugal] with her form but she worked hard and she's back in, I'm delighted for her.".

Beattie faces stiff competition for the starting striker role from the likes of returning captain Simone Magill and Kess Elmore, who impressed in the last camp and scored against Portugal, but she should receive some minutes across the two games and will be keen to grasp her chance to make an impact.

Left-back Haughey was called up to the senior squad as a 16-year-old under Kenny Shiels, but did not feature.

West Brom defender Haughey's last involvement with NI came when she represented the under-19s in 2023.

A Lisburn Rangers player when Kenny Shiels included her in the senior squad as a 16-year-old, Haughey then moved to Nottingham Forest before settling at the Baggies, who play in the National League.

The left-back, 22, has been a regular this season for the club in the third tier and combines playing with her university studies.

Left-back has been a problem position for NI which McArdle has been trying to address as demonstrated by him bringing in Lucy-Jayne Grant in the last camp and Haughey for this window to allow Rebecca Holloway to play as a central defender.

Haughey will be competing with Grant to fill that full-back role on the left side and McArdle believes she is more than capable of doing so having tried to bring her back into the squad earlier in his reign.

"Ella's been at West Brom for a few years now. She has real potential, strong, athletic potential. We tried to call her up sooner but there were some challenges," he explained.

"She's probably been one of their top performers and one of the top performers in the league. Delighted she's earned this opportunity and hopefully she can make her debut and achieve her first cap.".

McArdle has made a concerted to continue to widen the player pool available to him as he aims to build depth ahead of the Nations League campaign next year, but if Beattie and Haughey catch the eye it will show that giving chances to those previously involved under different managers could be another possibility.
