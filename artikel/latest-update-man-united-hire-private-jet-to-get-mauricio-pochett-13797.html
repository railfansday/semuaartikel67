By CHRIS WHEELER, NORTHERN SPORTS WRITER.

Published: 11:27 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 13:55 EST, 16 November 2026.

Manchester United have laid on a private jet for Mauricio Pochettino to fly straight to England in the early hours of Wednesday morning after he finishes work as US national team coach.

According to recent reports, pochettino’s last game in charge is against Haiti in Cincinnati, which kicks off at midnight on Tuesday UK time. The 54-year-old will speak to the media and say farewell to his players before jumping on a plane to Manchester which has been organised by United.

It will enable Pochettino to sleep before he lands in the UK on Wednesday morning ready to go straight to Carrington and start work.

He will be accompanied by first-team coach Miguel D’Agostino, strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya and analyst Alec Scott.

It has emerged that three other members of his trusted coaching team – assistant Jesus Perez, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez and his son and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino – were allowed to travel to Manchester at the weekend after the US won the first leg against Haiti 6-0 in Miami.

Recent findings suggest that it means that Pochettino’s back-room staff have been able to make a headstart in their preparations for his first game in charge against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday. It is a huge fixture in the calendar for United, but even more so because he takes over a team languishing in the relegation zone.

Pochettino's long-time assistant Jesus Perez is one of three trusted staff members who have already been given permission to travel to Manchester.

Pochettino will be able to work with the players as they begin reporting back from international duty, and is expected to speak to club media before conducting his pre-match press conference on Friday lunchtime.

Speaking ahead of the second leg against Haiti at the TQL Stadium, Pochettino remarked: ‘I am with Miguel, Sylvia and Alec and after they are going to join me in Manchester.

Industry observers note that ‘The next question is where are Jesus, Toni and Sebastiano? They are ahead. After the game (in Miami), they flew to Manchester and they are there in advance. That was the proposal when we agreed terms with Manchester United and with the US national team.

‘They are there preparing everything because on Wednesday, when we arrive, it’s going to be non-stop and then we start with playing games.’.
