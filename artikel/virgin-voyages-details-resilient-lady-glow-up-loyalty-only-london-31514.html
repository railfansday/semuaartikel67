Virgin Voyages will put its refit of the Resilient Lady and a new loyalty-only venue at the center of its 2027 intends, announced today at Uncharted, a keynote-style event covering all four ships.

“We can’t stand still because the reality is, we’ve set the bar really high, but what our sailors are looking for from the next phase of Virgin Voyages is the bar is even higher,” remarked Nirmal Saverimuttu, chief executive officer, during a media preview.

Saverimuttu estimated that about 60 percent of the announcements started as comments from sailors, travel advisors and crew.

“I feel like we’ve built this business together with our sailors, our first mates and our crew,” he said.

According to recent reports, opening onboard the Resilient Lady in fall 2027, The London Club will be open only to repeat guests. It draws on Virgin’s British roots and record-label history, with records, memorabilia, tea and scones.

We’ve had some feedback that we need to do more with Sailing Club … that it’s probably not as full a program as it should be,” he noted.

He said Virgin would not follow the airline and hotel model.

“Loyalty isn’t about points. Loyalty is about recognition. It’s about belonging,” he explained. “Every CFO in the world probably tells me I’m crazy saying that, but the way our Sailing Club is set up, it rewards people who come back.”.

He added that the tech giant had underestimated repeat business when it designed the tiers around three and five voyages.

“The reality is a lot of sailors have been back 10, 15, 20, 30 times now,” he said, calling The London Club “just the first step” toward bigger loyalty programming.

In a recent development, “It is not some place that you can buy your way into,” added Michelle Bentubo, chief operating officer. “It is genuinely a place for our loyal sailors to come together.”.

Virgin is also extending Sailing Club benefits through Dec. 31, 2028, as it passes 2 million sailors. Sisters at Sea, the fleetwide meetup in February, will count as two voyages toward status.

The September 2027 drydock covers technical systems as well as guest areas.

“It’s been five years. She needs a freshen up,” Bentubo mentioned. She credited the technical team for fitting the work into 14 days.

Yim will replace Razzle Dazzle as the ship’s new Thai restaurant.

Sources indicate that in addition is a new cabin category that focuses on “cool, dark, quiet spaces,” Bentubo said, with blackout conditions and cooling linens. A new space called Replenish will add a red-light sauna, a cold cabin and a hyperbaric chamber for two, and Redemption Spa will be renamed The Well.

According to recent reports, all of it falls under Vibrance, a latest wellbeing platform. It debuts on a 10-night round trip from Barcelona starting Sept. 2, 2027.

“It’s not really about wellness for me. It’s about being well,” Saverimuttu said.

Sources indicate that the fleetwide news included the Brilliant Lady’s first Hawaii voyage, an 18-night round trip from Los Angeles from March 11 to 29, 2028, opening for booking Dec. 1.

Virgin also revealed à la carte dining at The Test Kitchen, upcoming shows and a New Year’s Eve sailing on the Valiant Lady with two midnight countdowns.
