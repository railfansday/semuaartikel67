Amazon is doubling down on The Terminal List expanded universe.

Days after announcing that it was adapting Jack Carr’s Savage Son as the next move in the world of the military thriller, the streamer has revealed it is developing a prequel series set in the 1960s.

It is developing an adaptation of Carr’s Cry Havoc, which was published last year and is the first book in the Tom Reece series.

In a recent development, tom Reece is the father of James Reece, played by franchise star Chris Pratt. In The Terminal List, he is briefly played by David Keeley in an episode. Cry Havoc takes place in 1968 Vietnam and follows youngest protagonist, Tom Reece, a brash Navy SEAL assigned to the top secret MACV-SOG unit which ran harrowing cross-border operations in Laos. When Tom’s Recon Team comes across plans for the upcoming Tet Offensive, it unleashes a chain of events that pulls the young warrior out of the jungles and into the shadows of Cold War espionage.

According to recent reports, A pilot script has been written by The Terminal List showrunner David Digilio and Max Adams, a former U.S. Army Ranger who writes and exec produces on the franchise with the book developed for television by Digilio, Adams, Carr and Jared Shaw.

Sources indicate that the group will exec produce alongside Pratt and Antoine Fuqua and Kat Samick for Fuqua Films with DiGilio serving as showrunner if it is greenlit. Amazon MGM Studios produces with MRC.

It marks the latest project in the franchise, which just dropped season two of The Terminal List last month, has prequel The Terminal List: Dark Wolf and is working on Savage Son.
