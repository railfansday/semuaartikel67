Breaking: GTA 6 has a smut book club and a surprisingly long reading list

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Stealing cars, robbing banks, and getting into shootouts are all familiar ways to spend an afternoon in Grand Theft Auto VI. Apparently, there's also time for a little bit of reading.

In a recent development, rockstar stocked its fictional Florida with books, magazines, and comics that players can pick up and flip through. There's a fishing magazine, little libraries across Leonida with their own reading lists, advertisements for "romantasy" novels on the radio, and even a backyard book club. Here's where to find something to read, what flipping through the pages can unlock, and how to drop in on that aforementioned book club. (I know I want to.).

GTA 6 has 34 reading collectibles, according to Power Pyx. The game groups 27 under Magazines, Books & Comics and tracks another seven at roadside libraries called Book Swaps. There's war fiction, crime memoir, self-help, sci-fi, and adventure, along with comics and magazines about everything from bodybuilding to fishing.

The books in GTA 6 range from crime memoirs to war novels and self-help parodies, and players can read a short excerpt from each.

At Jason's house in Key Lento, Lethal by Design opens to six pages from Chapter 7, where a small-time crook recounts working for Liberty City’s Ancelotti crime family.

By Jason's bed, the World War II novel Ashes of Glory offers six pages in which Captain Liberty and his squad of convicts survive a mission and realize no one planned for them to get out alive.

Sources indicate that there's even a military self-help parody called ORCA: A Whole Different Animal, in which a veteran turns his training into life advice and brags about waking up at 2 a.m. to carry a 350-pound log to work.

An concern of Body Copy also gives players a magazine to flip through, from a fitness influencer's morning routine to gym etiquette and places to meditate outdoors.

The game tucks reading material into everyday stops, too. There's a copy of self-help book Help Me Help You Help Me at the Starlet Motel's front desk, and the comics Ratface and Special Friends Force share a rack at a food mart in Vice City's La Perle neighborhood. A trailer in Port Gellhorn has something a little harder to classify, though Dicked Down Deep in Space certainly sounds like sci-fi.

Some of Leonida's books can be spotted from the road. The game features seven roadside libraries called Book Swaps, each holding a few pages from a different adventure novel. For example, down the street from Jason's house, a turtle-shaped bookcase on Dolphin Drive holds Killer Prom. On Vespucci Island, a miniature lighthouse contains Goodnight, Dollface, Goodnight. You'll also find a boat-shaped shelf by Lake Leonida in Ambrosia with Humanity vs Space and a coffin-shaped bookcase outside Capri's Halloween House with Badlands Revenge inside.

The collection doesn't stop there. Three more Book Swaps are scattered across Leonida, including The Wizard's Ruin: Loud Clap of the Night in Watson Bay, Thighlander: A Tale of True Scotsmen in Southside, and Outback Gas Junkie in Waning Sands. Once a book or magazine catches your eye, look for the Examine prompt. On PlayStation, R1 turns the page, L1 goes back, and Triangle displays the text. Square flips the publication over to show the back cover.

It has been revealed that to log a Book Swap, take the book out and put it back. It counts whether you flip through the pages or immediately get back in the car.

Reading in GTA 6 can be a fun detour that leads to new locations and side quests.

At Jason's house, pick up the fishing magazine Off the Hook from the workbench in the shed. Turn to the fishing guide, and five spots appear on your map, including a stretch of water east of Key Lento where you can catch barracuda and a spot beneath the Draper Island bridge where you can fish for tarpon.

Recent findings suggest that reading can also prompt to a phone call. Outside Three Sheets Liquor, there's a flyer from a man named Kirk who's looking for a creature called the Skunk Ape. Read it to add Kirk to your in-game contacts, then call him at night to start a side quest that takes you looking for the creature near Pelican River.

The phone comes in handy with style magazines, too. Scanning them with the WAiNK app can unlock hairstyles, makeup, and tattoos, which count toward the game’s separate Discovered Styles checklist. And back at Jason's house, Insider Gaming found that flipping through books and magazines gives his Focus meter a boost. Focus lets players slow down time in combat, so a little reading can come in handy the next time Jason gets into a shootout.

In a recent development, reading also counts toward 100 percent completion, according to Power Pyx. Players need any 30 of the 34 unique titles across Magazines, Books & Comics and Book Swaps. Both collections are under Progress → Collections → Exploration.

Some copies become inaccessible as the story moves on, so Chapter 1 is a good time to look around Jason's place.

In a recent development, and if you prefer reading in a group setting, don't worry. There's even a book club in GTA 6. In the Chapter 3 side mission "Climbing the Cellerichain," Lucia drops off a package for her friend Andy at a house in Waning Sands. When she knocks, someone calls her around to the backyard, where a group of women are discussing a seemingly steamy novel called Two Pricks in the Bush. Lucia can leave once she drops off the package, or she can stick around and give her opinion on the book.

There's clearly a market for these "romantasy" novels in Vice City: During his playthrough, Mashable's tech reporter Alex Perry heard an ad for a smut novel on the in-game radio.
