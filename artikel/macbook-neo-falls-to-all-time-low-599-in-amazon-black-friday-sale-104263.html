Apple's 256GB MacBook Neo in citrus is $100 off at Amazon, bringing it back to the price the laptop started at before the memory crisis drove prices up across the organization's lineup.

The affordable laptop made a splash earlier this year, putting a fire under the entire PC industry and proving to be one of the best laptops if you're on a budget.

Apple's base MacBook Pro in the citrus colorway, with an A18 Pro chip, 8GB of RAM, 256GB of storage, and no Touch ID option.

Industry observers note that the other colors — blush, indigo, and silver — are all $649 as of this writing for the 256GB configuration, or $50 off. All of the 512GB options that add a Touch ID fingerprint reader are $749 (also $50 off).

The A18 Pro in the MacBook Neo proved to be a decent performer for a basic laptop in our testing, excelling in single-core performance where it's needed most. The Mac can fall under memory pressure with 8GB of RAM, though macOS is pretty good at managing multitasking.

Industry observers note that if you're looking for an affordable laptop, this is the best option for the Mac. If you want to get the Neo for the price Apple envisioned it to be, now is your chance. It's especially good for the student, young professional, or person in your life with simple computing needs who is (or wants to be) part of the Apple ecosystem.
