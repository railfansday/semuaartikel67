Nick Woltemade continues to make headlines.

The German international striker had a rollercoaster first season at Newcastle United, starting off in great form and then a bit like the team overall, found things more difficult as the 2025-26 campaign progressed and a 58 game season was far too much for a small squad to deal with.

A lot of nonsense written and spoken about Nick Woltemade with ridiculous claims he had been a total flop, despite having 14 direct goal involvements including scoring 11.

The decision was made for Nick Woltemade to spend the 2026-27 season with Juventus, Matthias Jaissle wanting to have pace as his main focus up front.

In a recent development, the hope being that a year of regular football in Italy will help Nick Woltemade improve his skill set, become better at holding the ball up, polish his goalscoring technique and so on. Helping him then to come back and be a bigger asset for the Newcastle United team, or command a significant transfer fee boosted by his time with Juventus.

Big Nick has had mixed fortunes with Juventus, not getting the amount of game time he would like (seven starts in all competitions, eight sub appearances) but doing very decently in the time he has spent on the pitch (five goals and three assists so far).

Industry observers note that last weekend with Juventus drawing 0-0 at home and booed off at half-time, Nick Woltemade was introduced for the second half and within 14 minutes had turned the game around, scoring one and then getting an assist. Juventus finally winning 4-0 at the end against Venezia and the United loan star picking up the MOTM award.

It was bench duties again last night, Nick Woltemade coming on in the second half with Juventus leading 3-1 against Omonia Nicosia in the second half and the Newcastle United loan striker helping his side to an eventual 4-1 victory.

A lot of daft transfer claims floating about and now the United striker’s agent has shot down one of the daftest.

Al-Maidan contacted Danny Bachmann, Nick Woltemade’s agent, to ask about media claims of Newcastle United doing a January 2027 swap deal, with 30-year-old Ivan Toney (reported to be on £20million a year/£400k a week, plus Toney has a court date to face in London after having been arrested and charged with an alleged serious assault in a nightclub) to come to United from Al Ahli and 24-year-old Nick Woltemade to join the Saudi club.

Danny Bachmann dismissing this speculation as laughable nonsense: “These reports are completely untrue.”.

In a recent development, the player’s agent making clear what the obvious reality is: “The player’s future will be decided at the end of the season when he returns to Newcastle United. We don’t know yet whether he will stay or leave.”.
