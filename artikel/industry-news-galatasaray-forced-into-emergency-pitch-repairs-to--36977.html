Published: 09:55 EST, 18 November 2026 | Updated: 10:07 EST, 18 November 2026.

Aston Villa have been told their Champions League clash with Galatasaray on Tuesday will still take place at the home of the Turkish champions despite the worrying state of the pitch.

Galatasaray have switched their home league fixture against Samsunspor this weekend to the stadium of top-flight rivals Istanbul Basaksehir to save the playing surface from further punishment.

Images released this week show a pitch that appears to be in alarming condition, with Galatasaray working around the clock to ensure it is playable. The club are in dialogue with UEFA.

At this stage, Daily Mail Sport understands UEFA are satisfied that the pitch will be in suitable condition when the sides take the field, though a pitch inspection will take place at 10am local time as is the case before any European fixture.

UEFA Article 5 states: ‘The field of play must be smooth and level and comply with the IFAB Laws of the Game.’.

Villa will look to strengthen their position in the league phase after winning three of their opening four games, though they lost their last fixture, at Barcelona.

Galatasaray are working around the clock to ensure their pitch is up to standard for their Champions League clash against Aston Villa (Source: X/Forza Cimbom).

Unai Emery’s men have already beaten Turkish opposition this term, thrashing Fenerbahce 6-0 for their record European win, though that fixture was at Villa Park.

After the chaotic 4-4 draw at Manchester United before the international break, Villa meet Sunderland at home in the Premier League on Saturday.

Emery has doubts over Matty Cash and Johan Manzambi, who picked up injuries while on international duty and may struggle to recover in time.
