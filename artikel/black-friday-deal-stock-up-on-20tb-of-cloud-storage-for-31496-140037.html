Recent findings suggest that TL;DR: Scramble Cloud’s 20TB lifetime subscription is down to $314.96 (reg. $1,440) this Black Friday with code CLOUD, offering encrypted cloud storage without recurring fees.

Nobody needs 87 nearly identical photos of their dog, but who has the heart to delete them? If you’re the sentimental type when it comes to your digital files, Scramble Cloud is offering 20TB of lifetime cloud storage for $314.96 (reg. $1,440) this Black Friday with the code CLOUD.

Sources indicate that that’s plenty of room for years of family photos, vacation videos, work documents, and those random files you’re convinced will come in handy someday. And with that much personal data sitting in one place, privacy is worth considering.

Recent findings suggest that developed in Germany, Scramble Cloud uses end-to-end encryption to protect files before they even leave your device, along with a zero-knowledge architecture designed to prevent the provider from accessing your stored content. Two-factor authentication adds another layer of account protection.

It has been revealed that scramble Cloud isn’t just a digital dumping ground, either. You can organize documents using a familiar folder structure, preview compatible files in your browser, and stream MP4 videos directly from your account. And if you’d like to subject your family to all those vacation photos, private sharing links, encrypted groups, and customizable file permissions let you decide who gets access to what.
