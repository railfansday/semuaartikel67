One of the year’s most hotly anticipated exhibitions, Van Eyck: The Portraits, opens at the National Gallery in London this weekend after a rapturous response from the critics. Uniting all 10 of the Flemish master’s surviving portraits, including the one miraculous drawing attributed to him, it is truly a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Ben Luke takes a tour of the show with its co-curator, Emma Capron.

It is auction week in Upcoming York, with hundreds of millions of dollars of art coming under the hammer. Ben asks Melanie Gerlis, an art market columnist for the Financial Times and The Art Newspaper, about the week’s results so far.

Specialists bidding on behalf of clients at Sotheby's Modern Art Evening Auction on 18 November.

And this episode’s Work of the Week is a maiolica wine cooler, made in 1574 by the workshop of Flaminio Fontana, in Urbino. It was made for Cosimo I de Medici, who is the subject of a small exhibition that opened this week at the Wallace Collection in London. Ben speaks to the curator of the display, Julia Van Zandvoort, about this remarkable piece.
