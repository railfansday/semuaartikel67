📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that updated on Monday, Nov. 16 at 10:13 p.m. ET / 7:13 p.m. PT — Bank of America has confirmed to Mashable that it is not experiencing any concerns with its systems. "Bank of America systems have been operating normally today. Clients experiencing delays with other providers may wish to contact them directly for the latest information.".

Updated on Monday, Nov. 16 at 8:30 p.m. ET / 5:30 p.m. PT — JPMorganChase transactions are reportedly back online, a spokesperson telling NBC that they "are now posting normally." The bank has yet to make any direct announcement on X, its pinned post from 2:57 p.m. ET / 12:57 p.m. PT still stating that it's "working diligently to resolve this by the end of the day today.".

JPMorganChase confirmed on Monday afternoon that it's experiencing a software bug that's causing delays for some Chase Bank customers; however, the financial institution said that the problems are not related to a cyberattack.

The company also confirmed that the bank outages reported today are a result of slowness in transactions with Chase, not a systemic matter with U.S. financial institutions. Recent findings suggest that on Monday afternoon, the platform DownDetector reported elevated user error reports for major U.S. financial institutions such as Chase, Bank of America, US Bank, Zelle, and Wells Fargo.

The reports closely followed a rare nationwide FAA ground stoppage and a global Microsoft Outlook outage. That may be why reports of a major banking outage spread so rapidly. At this time, Chase is the only financial institution with a confirmed outage, and a JPMorganChase spokesperson confirmed to Mashable that customers are still able to access their funds.

"JPMorganChase has experienced a temporary issue related to a software bug in on-premise hardware systems — it is not cyber related. It is taking longer than usual to service some customers," a spokesperson confirmed in a statement to Mashable. "This is not a systemic issue — other banks may have been impacted by slowness in transactions only to the extent that they were a party to a transaction with us.".

JPMorganChase mentioned that it was hoping to resolve the concern by the end of the day.
