Liverpool's midfield has been a major topic of conversation for a while now.

Ever since Fabinho left in 2023, fans have demanded that new players were brought in by the Anfield outfit.

Obviously, that still hasn't happened with Arne Slot and Andoni Iraola actually making Liverpool's midfield weaker thanks to their sales of Tyler Morton and Curtis Jones over the last couple of seasons.

In the summer, most fans thought that at least one midfielder would be brought in to replace Jones but, instead, Trey Nyoni was given a more prominent role.

And, while he's done well this term, since he's still so young, he can't be playing two 90 minute games every week which is why a latest midfielder now needs to be brought in.

Gavi could be Liverpool's surprise solution to their problems.

Over the last few years, many different players have been discussed as potential options for Liverpool to explore.

Recently, Lamine Camara, Adam Wharton and Alex Scott have been the most linked with an Anfield switch.

However, in the upcoming January transfer window, each of them will only be available for crazy high fees.

And, with how many clubs have shown an interest in acquiring their services, there's going to be a huge battle for every single player.

That's why this latest update from journalist Juan Jesus is exciting.

"In addition to Alejandro Balde, Barça are now pushing Gavi out this very winter to fund the signing of a centre-back, seen as the club’s absolute priority," he wrote.

"The main target to bolster the defence is Nico Schlotterbeck, but the current wage bill leaves no room to pull off a move of that magnitude in January.

"To comply with financial fair play rules and try to sign him in this winter window, the board is pushing for two key sales: Balde on one hand, and the bombshell departure of Gavi on the other.".

Gavi would be perfect for Liverpool.

At the start of the 2026-27 season, nobody thought that Gavi would become available on the market.

He's been a regular feature in Flick's plans for as long as anyone can remember and, since he's only 22 years old, there's so much of his career left to go.

Obviously, a deal wouldn't come cheap for the Spaniard but, if the La Liga giants are as strapped for cash as they seem to be, an agreement could probably be reached.

Gavi can also play as a number 10, eight or six which is massively beneficial for the Reds.

This versatility coupled with his endless experience of the Champions League and La Liga all join together to make him the perfect player for Liverpool to sign.

Here's hoping that an agreement can be reached.
