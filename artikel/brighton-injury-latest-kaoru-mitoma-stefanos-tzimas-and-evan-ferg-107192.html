Brighton continue to deal with a number of injury problems as Fabian Hurzeler prepares to face juggle Uefa Conference League and Premier League competition.

It has been revealed that the Seagulls are second in the Premier League table despite last Sunday’s painful 2-0 loss at Hull City. This Thursday they welcome Romanian League leaders Craiova for a Uefa Conference League clash and on Sunday they return to Premier League action as Newcastle visit the Amex.

Here is the latest injury news, including Hurzeler's comments and potential return dates.

Recent findings suggest that mitoma remains one of Brighton's biggest injury concerns. The Japan international has been sidelined with a lengthy hamstring problem and is yet to feature this season.

Hurzeler told Sussex World last week that the winger is getting closer to a comeback and indicated that Mitoma was around one or two weeks away.

The Nottingham Forest fixture on December 5 could be a potential return date.

Tzimas is recovering from a serious knee injury, having suffered an ACL problem which has kept him out for an extended period.

The Greek striker has returned to training with the first team, but Hurzeler has stressed that patience is required.

"Stefanos is a special character," he stated, praising the forward's determination during his rehabilitation.

The manager added that Tzimas would not immediately be an option, despite being pleased with his progress.

There is chance that he could return at Bournemouth on December 2 but Brighton will not take any chances.

Ferguson continues his recovery from an ankle/foot injury and remains a long-term absentee.

Hurzeler previously explained that Ferguson's recovery was similar to Tzimas's situation, saying: "We have to wait some more weeks.".

December seems increasingly unlikely for Ferguson with January perhaps a more realistic option.

Yalcouye is the latest player to suffer an injury setback. The midfielder picked up a problem after returning from international duty with the Ivory Coast, where he featured in two matches.

Hurzeler confirmed on November 22 that the youngster would be unavailable for several weeks. "Malick had a bigger problem coming back from the Ivory Coast with an injury and therefore we have to expect that he is a few weeks out," he said.

Brighton vs Everton on December 13 is a potential return date.

Yohanna has suffered a frustrating setback after picking up another thigh injury while representing Nigeria's Under-23 side.

The winger had been working towards a return following an earlier muscle problem but suffered a recurrence during international duty.

Industry observers note that hurzeler mentioned: "Unfortunately, he got a re-injury when he was with Nigeria. Unfortunately, it wasn't perfectly managed.".

He added that Brighton would support the youngster through what he described as a tough period.

There is presently no confirmed return date.

Hadjam is another recent addition to the injury list after picking up a knee problem.

The defender withdrew from Algeria's international squad and subsequently missed Brighton's match against Hull City.

Hurzeler provided a brief patch on November 22, saying: "Hadjam has a small problem.".

Brighton will need to assess his fitness and Sussex World will bring an update from Wednesday’s press conference ahead of the Conference League clash against Craiova.

Recent findings suggest that azeez was “available” for the Hull match but did not make the matchday squad. There is hope that he will feature against Craiova on Thursday night.

The Portuguese right back is available for the Conference League clash on Thursday against Craiova despite his dismissal for two bookable offences at Hull City.

His domestic suspension kicks-in for the Newcastle match and will be available again in the Premier League on December 2 at Bournemouth.
