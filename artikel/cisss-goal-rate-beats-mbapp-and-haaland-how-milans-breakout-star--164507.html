Cissè’s goal rate beats Mbappé and Haaland: how Milan’s breakout star compares

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Alphadjo Cissè already has 10 goals in his first four months at Milan, and on goals per minute he is ahead of Kylian Mbappé and Erling Haaland.

According to recent reports, as noted by Sky Sport Italia, the 20-year-old’s header against Olympiacos on Thursday took him into double figures for the season. He has six in Serie A and four in the Europa League, from just 15 appearances.

It has been revealed that those numbers are even more impressive given how little he has played. He has started and completed the full 90 minutes only twice, and has played 782 minutes in total. That works out at a goal roughly every 78 minutes. He has also been ruthless in front of goal, converting 40% of his 25 shots.

Recent findings suggest that across all competitions in Europe, only Raphinha (a goal every 58.8 minutes) and Roma’s Donyell Malen (67.4) have a better rate. Cissè sits ahead of Mbappé (84.7), Haaland (95.7) and Lamine Yamal (111.2).

Sources indicate that in Serie A alone, Malen is again the only forward ahead of him. Cissè’s rate of a goal every 74.3 minutes beats Lautaro Martínez (91.5), Francesco Pio Esposito (109.3) and Marcus Thuram (135.5).

He is also in form, having scored in three straight games against Genoa, Frosinone and Olympiacos.

Sources indicate that his 10 goals already match Rafael Leão’s total for the whole of last season, which took the Portuguese 1,968 minutes. Cissè has done it in less than half that time.
