By MATT BARLOW, FOOTBALL WRITER.

Published: 12:22 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 06:34 EST, 22 November 2026.

Industry observers note that luca Williams-Barnett will score better goals. That was the consensus on a day when the Tottenham starlet marked his first Premier League by scoring the first and creating the second.

Roberto De Zerbi compared him to Lamine Yamal after watching the 18-year-old steal the show as Spurs beat Ipswich, their fourth win in five to move within three points of the top four.

'He is a special player, I am not surprised,' stated De Zerbi. 'I decided 10 days ago he would play and I asked him two things. First, to play like he is on the park with his friends and then to work hard without the ball, like Raphinha and Yamal.

'In football today players with talent have to work hard and attackers are the first defenders.'.

In a recent development, asked if Williams-Barnett could aspire to the levels of Barcelona’s Yamal, the Spurs boss replied: 'Yes, I think so'. And told how on the teenager had spent the bus ride to the stadium watching clips of Ronaldinho, Neymar and Eden Hazard on YouTube.

His goal was a picture of youthful desire and determination to get across his marker at the back post and be first to a delicious teasing cross delivered by Pedro Porro.

Roberto De Zerbi rates Williams-Barnett as ‘one of the best’ young players he has worked with.

Williams-Barnett slid and stretched past Abdoul Ouattara with his right foot as the ball skipped and hit him high on the left thigh before rebounding into the bottom corner.

Industry observers note that you make your own luck. The Spurs crowd erupted. They have been waiting to hail another of their own. Maybe this is the one. A goal on his first start, just like Harry Kane, and at 18 years and 51 days, the club’s youngest league scorer since Gareth Bale.

Sources indicate that there has been a buzz growing around Williams-Barnett for the last 18 months as he rose through the academy ranks. He made his England Under-19 debut in September, coming on as a sub for Max Dowman, Arsenal’s teenage sensation.

De Zerbi adores his attitude as much as his talent, and a confidence bordering arrogance on display in pre-season when he dinked in a ‘Panenka’ style penalty in a shootout in Australia.

He is right-footed but started against Ipswich wide on the left, in the role no player has made their own since Heung-min Son.

Dominic Solanke, who scored Tottenham's second goal, congratulates his young team-mate.

Williams-Barnett might have had a second before half time, a half-chance miscued and a header saved.

Recent findings suggest that spurs fans responded. When he picked up the ball on halfway early in the second half, they roared him forward into the space ahead. He duly obliged, through the gears into a swagger and into the Ipswich penalty box without meeting a challenge.

Sources indicate that belatedly, Darnell Furlong tried to hold him up. But Williams-Barnett made a yard on the outside and whipped over a cross with his left foot, which fizzed past Walton and was turned in from close range by Dominic Solanke.

The teenager booted the ball jubilantly into the crowd and collected his first yellow card from killjoy referee Rob Jones.

Williams-Barnett left to a standing ovation when he was replaced by Savio in the 64th minute. 'Luca was magnificent,' remarked Solanke. 'To see him do that on his first Premier League start is crazy.'.

Spurs coasted in. It had been comfortable, even without some senior players including Sandro Tonali who had been unwell.

They were solid at the back and creative up front, peppering the Ipswich goal with 18 shots. Mohamed Kudus struck a post from a first-half free kick, won by Williams-Barnett.

Christian Walton made strong saves to deny James Maddison and prevent Dara O’Shea turning into his own goal.

Industry observers note that savio cross hit a post with a deflected cross before he was struck down by a recurrence of the hamstring injury which ruled him out for six weeks. It was the only blemish for De Zerbi, although one to enhance the opportunities ahead for Williams-Barnett.
