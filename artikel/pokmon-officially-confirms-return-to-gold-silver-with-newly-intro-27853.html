Pokémon Officially Confirms Return To Gold & Silver With Newly introduced 2027 Release

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, an official return to Gold and Silver’s Johto region has been officially confirmed for Pokémon Trading Card Game, with a new release set to arrive in 2027. There are still a few arrivals scheduled to happen this year, such as the release of Pokémon TCG: Day and Night in November, but most fans are already looking to the new additions to the Trading Card Game in 2027. The Pokémon Manufacturer International has already made a few announcements regarding what collections we’re getting in early 2027, but a new confirmation by Creatures has stirred the community, as we’re returning to Johto.

In a recent development, according to a new report by PokéBeach, Creatures, which is responsible for the TCG in Japan, has confirmed three new set releases for next year. Magma Heartbeat, Lightning Impact, and Aurora Ripples are three separate Pokémon TCG sets that will be released simultaneously on May 21, 2027, in Japan. Entei, Raikou, and Suicune are, respectively, the faces of these expansions. This is a major callback to the well-known Legendary Beasts of Generation 2’s Johto region. They had been teased earlier this year during Worlds in August, but have now been confirmed as the mascots for these new sets.

According to recent reports, unlike the first Pokémon TCG release of 2027, these three sets will be exclusive to Japan in May and will likely be fused into a single set for release in Western markets later on. This will be the first time that three sets are released simultaneously – usually, in Japan, dual sets are released together and are then combined into a single set for Western markets. One of the most key aspects of these three fresh sets is that they will kick off the Pokémon ex★ series for the TCG. How this new mechanic will work is still unclear.

It’s also unclear if the release of this Pokémon ex★ series will represent the end of the Mega Evolution ex series in the Card Game. If so, the release of Pokémon TCG: Flowing Fists in February may be one of the final ones from the Mega Evolution ex series, before the game transitions into the Pokémon ex★ series. On May 21, Creatures will also release six Battle Start Decks, all of which will also be part of the upcoming Pokémon ex★ series. The headliners for these decks are Decidueye, Blaziken, Dondozo, Dedenne, Alcremie, and Lucario.

The release of these upcoming sets and decks will coincide with the confirmed 2027 price increase for Pokémon TCG products. This, however, will happen exclusively in Japan. Booster packs will be 15% more expensive starting in May, so the new series will also officially represent the start of the price hike.

Sources indicate that the return to Gold and Silver’s Johto region with the three Legendary Beasts is exciting, even if it remains to be seen exactly how these sets will be adapted for the Western markets – it hasn’t been confirmed if the three sets will be fused into a single one, for example. Even so, for now, it’s the closest we’re getting to anything related to Gen 2. For now, we’ll have to be satisfied with this upcoming good-looking Pokémon Trading Card Game release.
