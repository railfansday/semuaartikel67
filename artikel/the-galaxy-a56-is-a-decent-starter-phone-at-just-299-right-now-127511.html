The Galaxy A56 is a decent starter phone at just $299 right now

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you are looking for a reliable, everyday handset without spending flagship money, Samsung’s mid-range lineup is usually a safe bet. Right now, the Samsung Galaxy A56 has dropped to just $299 on Amazon, which makes a sizable $200 dent in its original $499 retail price.

Industry observers note that at under $300, the biggest selling point here isn’t just the upfront savings; it is the longevity. Samsung committed to six years of OS and security updates for the Galaxy A56, meaning this device will stay current and secure well into 2032. For a budget-tier purchase, getting that kind of software runway is hard to beat and only matched by Google with the Pixel 10a.

Hardware-wise, the A56 punches above this sale price. You get a crisp 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED display, an IP67 dust- and water-resistance rating, and a 5,000mAh battery with 45W wired charging, matching the charging speeds of Samsung’s flagship models. The triple-camera array, led by a 50MP main sensor with OIS, won’t win any photography awards against Ultra-tier phones, though it is plenty capable for casual snaps. Plus, One UI brings plenty of handy Samsung ecosystem features and baked-in AI tools like Object Eraser.

Of course, the Google Pixel 10a is the obvious rival in this space. If you can catch the Pixel 10a on sale around the $350 mark, its cleaner Android experience, seven-year revision promise, and superior computational photography make it a very tempting alternative.

It has been revealed that that noted, it hasn’t been available at that price point for a while and can only be picked up for $400 if you buy renewed. Which annoyingly means the Pixel is sitting closer to its $499 MSRP and has last years specs anyway, so in many ways that makes the Galaxy A56 potentially a smarter buy for your wallet despite being a year older.

In my opinion, for first-time smartphone owners, kids, or anyone who just wants a large, smooth screen and years of updates on a budget, $299 is tough to argue with.
