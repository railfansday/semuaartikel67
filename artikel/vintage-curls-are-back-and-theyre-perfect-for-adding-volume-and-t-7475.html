Industry observers note that vintage curls are having a moment, and Pamela Anderson recently made a case for bringing them back. Spotted at the Academy Museum Gala, the actor stepped out with a softly sculpted hairstyle that channeled Old Hollywood, proving how retro inspiration can feel thoroughly modern.

Sources indicate that with its voluminous shape, polished ringlets, and romantic texture, vintage curls have long been a Hollywood favorite. From 1960s starlets to Halle Berry, who famously paired a pixie crop with soft curls in the noughties, the style has since remained a timeless staple.

Sources indicate that “I love a shorter, sculpted curly crop because it allows you to create beautiful fluidity around the face while maintaining a really strong overall shape,” states Adam Reed, hairstylist and founder at Arkive Headcare. “It feels playful and confident.”.

Industry observers note that beyond its enduring appeal, there’s a reason why vintage curls deserve a place in your repertoire, especially when looking for flattering hairstyles for mature hair. The right shape can create the illusion of fuller, thicker-looking hair without sacrificing softness or movement.

In a recent development, the look is a master class not just when it comes to embracing shorter lengths, but also when it comes to rethinking how a pixie or a bob can be styled. As hair changes with age, so may its density and natural volume. Anderson’s approach offers a modern take on vintage-inspired pinup curls, channeling Old Hollywood glamour while still being effortlessly wearable for every day.

Sources indicate that the key lies in finding a version that works for your hair type. “Ask your hairdresser to consider not just your face shape, hair type, and texture, but also your lifestyle,” indicates Sam McKnight MBE, hairstylist and founder of Hair by Sam McKnight. “This style is all about layers; they help create movement within wavy and curly textures. My go-to for styling is always my Dressed to Kill Crème.”.

According to recent reports, for finer hair, Reed recommends opting for a more considered haircut that maintains density. When it comes to styling, focus on creating irregular curl patterns with rollers or tongs before finishing the look with a hairspray that delivers control while ensuring the hair retains movement.

Whether worn polished or undone, vintage curls are simply all about finding a look that offers confidence and effortless glamour at any age.
