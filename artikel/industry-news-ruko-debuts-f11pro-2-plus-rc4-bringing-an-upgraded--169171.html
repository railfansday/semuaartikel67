In a recent development, HONG KONG, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ruko is excited to announce the launch of its latest drone, the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4), now available on Amazon. Designed to deliver an upgraded aerial photography experience, the new drone features an integrated 5.5-inch touchscreen remote controller, advanced 8K imaging, enhanced flight stabilization, and intelligent flight capabilities. Combining intuitive controls with stable flight performance and creative aerial features, it makes capturing memorable moments from above easier and more enjoyable.

Enhanced Imaging for More Detailed Aerial Photography.

Sources indicate that whether you're capturing a family vacation, exploring scenic landscapes, or documenting outdoor adventures, the Ruko F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) helps turn everyday experiences into memorable aerial footage. Its advanced camera system supports high-resolution 8K (7680 Ã 4320) photography and 4K/30fps video recording, capturing rich details and vibrant scenes from above. The AI-enhanced Night Mode further improves image brightness and detail in low-light conditions, making it easier to capture the beauty of evening landscapes, sunset views, and nighttime gatherings.

An Upgraded Touchscreen Remote for Greater Convenience.

Industry observers note that A standout addition is the RC4 touchscreen remote controller, which offers a more direct and convenient way to enjoy aerial photography. Its built-in 5.5-inch 1080P HD display provides a clear view of live footage, while its 1200-nit high brightness helps users see the screen even under strong sunlight. With automatic connection upon startup and support for simultaneous viewing on a smartphone, the controller simplifies setup and makes the entire flying experience more intuitive.

Advanced Flight Stabilization for Greater Control and Confidence.

In a recent development, the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) incorporates multiple positioning technologies, including VIO, TOF, GPS, and a barometer, to support stable hovering and more precise flight control. These technologies work together to help the drone maintain its position and navigate more steadily in suitable environments. Intelligent safety features, including landing pad recognition, multiple Return-to-Home options, and a built-in buzzer for locating the drone, provide additional reassurance throughout the flight, helping even beginners enjoy a more confident flying experience.

In a recent development, to further enhance image quality during flight, the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) features a 3-axis brushless gimbal that helps maintain stable, smooth footage. By combining mechanical stabilization with its high-quality imaging system, the drone helps reduce unwanted camera movement, allowing users to capture clearer aerial videos and more refined photos across a variety of outdoor settings.

Intelligent Tracking and Automated Flight for Creative Shots.

For those looking to create more dynamic content, the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) offers intelligent subject tracking and automated camera movements. Users can choose from different tracking modes to follow moving subjects, while one-touch cinematic flight options make it easier to capture creative shots without complicated manual operation. Combined with GPS-assisted flight modes such as orbit flight and waypoint planning, these features open up more possibilities for capturing travel memories, outdoor adventures, and engaging social media content.

10KM Digital Transmission for Expansive Aerial Exploration.

The drone also offers a 33,000 ft (10KM) long-range digital transmission system, delivering a stable live-view experience over extended distances under suitable conditions. Whether flying across open landscapes or capturing expansive outdoor scenery, users can enjoy greater flexibility in choosing their aerial perspectives.

With its combination of advanced imaging, convenient touchscreen controls, intelligent flight assistance, and enhanced stabilization, the Ruko F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) is designed to make aerial photography more accessible and enjoyable for both new pilots and experienced drone enthusiasts.
