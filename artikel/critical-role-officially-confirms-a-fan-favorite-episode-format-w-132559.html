Critical Role Officially Confirms a Fan-Favorite Episode Format Will Not Return

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that one of the best things about Critical Role’s live highlights is that, in many ways, they're a phenomenal way to address story threads the cast couldn’t get to by the end of the campaign. For example, The Mighty Nein Reunion: Echoes of the Solstice 2023 live show is set shortly after the Apogee Solstice, an event primarily associated with Campaign 3: Bells Hells but also directly affecting The Mighty Nein adventuring group, particularly Beauregard Lionett and Caleb Widogast.

Recent findings suggest that however, it isn’t all story-based. There have been plenty of wacky shenanigans at the live shows too, such as PvP in2025’s Total Party Kill and 2026’s Projekt Funball.

In a recent development, that being said, not all of these one-shot Dungeons & Dragons adventures are created equal. In the most recent episode of Fireside Chat, Critical Role co-founder and creative director Marisha Ray made it crystal clear that there is a particular D&D one-shot idea she would prefer not to revisit.

Fireside Chat is a Beacon-exclusive talk show where Critical Role’s co-founders answer questions submitted by Critters through the organization's Discord server. Along with discussing future projects, Ray took the time to answer a question about which storyline she’d like one of her characters to take center stage in during a future live show.

In a recent development, immediately, Ray acknowledged that she felt the tech giant had explored a lot of stories with Vox Machina and the Mighty Nein, and that she’d love to explore new things with Bells Hells or “other stuff,” like one-shots from other eras of Exandria, such as Calamity or Age of Arcanum. But there was one format that Ray shut down right away, despite being one of the most popular live demonstrates in Critical Role history.

According to recent reports, “No more wedding one-shots!” Ray remarked, acknowledging a comment on the Beacon Discord server about her character, Keyleth, getting a wedding. “No! This is going to be a hot take and I’m really sorry, but we had the craziest wedding live show at Radio City Music Hall. I don’t want to top that, man.”.

Ray acknowledged that weddings were a favorite among Critters. “I understand everybody loves weddings, I get it,” she said on the show. “But I don’t want to keep re-treading old territory.”.

Ray isn’t wrong, especially in her comment about revisiting old territory. Just a year ago, Travis Willingham and Laura Bailey reprised their roles as Fjord Stone and Jester Lavorre in “Jester and Fjord's Wedding - Live from Radio City Music Hall” one-shot. Though it still felt very much like a Dungeons & Dragons adventure, with the party fighting off hags and the like, it also featured an actual wedding ceremony. Bailey and Willingham both dressed in wedding outfits, as did the rest of the cast, making it undoubtedly one of the biggest and most elaborate live highlights Critical Role had ever done.

Critical Role also featured the wedding of Percy and Vex in “Dalen's Closet,” a special one-shot rather than a live show. That one also included some dressing up, but unlike the Fjord and Jester one-shot, it was filmed in Critical Role’s studio.

Recent findings suggest that even so, as Ray says, there are only so many one-shots the cast and crew can do. There are plenty of romantic pairings in Exandria we’d love to see celebrated at weddings, such as Imogen and Laudna or Beau and Yasha, but repeating the format could become stale in both gameplay and narrative. How else can you spice up a wedding in a way that feels natural without turning it into a TV soap opera?

Sources indicate that ray’s answer doesn’t rule out another wedding one-shot; opinions can change over time, of course. That mentioned, Critters hoping to hear wedding bells for their favorite characters should wish for something else for now.
