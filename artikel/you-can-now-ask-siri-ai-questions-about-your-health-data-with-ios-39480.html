You can now ask Siri AI questions about your health data, with iOS 27.3 beta 2

It has been revealed that while Siri AI expanded on what kinds of things you can ask Apple’s voice assistant in many ways, one area where the system took a step back was health data. Since Siri AI’s launch in iOS 27.0, the system has enforced guardrails that do not allow it to access data from the Health app, despite the older Siri Classic being able to do just that.

However, it seems Apple is about to close the gap. In the latest iOS 27.3 beta, just released to developers today, customers are now able to ask their iPhone about health data related questions and Siri AI will answer.

For instance, you can ask about your recent step count or heart rate readings, find out about activity rings progress, log medications and more.

When asking Siri for health stats, the assistant generates a human natural language response and renders a little inline card that uses familiar user interface elements from the Health app. Similarly, if you ask about your activity rings, a visualisation of your activity rings is shown in the chat transcript.

According to recent reports, again, this is catch-up compared to what you’ve been able to do with old Siri for several years at this point. However, empowered by the LLM behind the scenes, you can ask questions about your health data that are a bit more open-ended.

You can also do more complicated requests like ‘Send a message to Chance about the status of my activity rings’, or ‘How many steps am I on track to do this week?’. These are the kind of tasks that old Siri would never be able to do.

Sources indicate that at least on this current build, the upcoming Siri AI Health functionality appears to have been enabled on iOS. It’s currently unclear if the functionality will also be available through watchOS.

iOS 27.3 also introduces a revamped Health app, with a radically latest design that includes new insights, suggestions and views about your health and fitness.

iOS 27.3 is presently in beta and is expected to be released publicly later this year, likely in early December.
