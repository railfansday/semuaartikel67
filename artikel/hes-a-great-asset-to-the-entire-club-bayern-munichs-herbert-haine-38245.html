Vincent Kompany has lost only 12 of his 125 games in charge of Bayern Munich since succeeding Thomas Tuchel, but the Belgian tactician brings much more to Germany’s record champions than just a tactical nouse.

According to recent reports, speaking exclusively to t-mobile, Bayern president Herbert Hainer discussed Kompany’s approach. “Vincent Kompany truly brings a clear vision and a strong personality and, ultimately, it’s crucial what energy he unleashes in our team game after game. Through his approach, he consistently manages to forge a cohesive unit from our many individual world-class players. Kompany is a very intelligent, thoughtful and likeable person, he’s a great asset to the entire club.”.

In February 2026, Kompany gave a 12 minute monologue addressing racism in football and strongly criticising José Mourinho’s reaction to the alleged racial abuse of Vinícius Júnior. Highlighting the Belgian’s importance as an ambassador for Bayern, Hainer added: “He himself emphasises that this speech arose spontaneously.

In a recent development, “He doesn’t want to put himself in the spotlight or win any awards for it. I think that’s typical of him and completely understandable. Credibility comes from understanding one’s convictions as mere posturing. Regardless, he certainly made an impression with this speech, and it has once again demonstrated how well Vincent Kompany’s values ​​align with Bayern.”.
