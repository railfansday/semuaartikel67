Industry observers note that it’s now official. Fired by Manchester United, Michael Carrick was replaced by Mauricio Pochettino at the head of the Red Devils. A classic fuse after the catastrophic start to the season for the Mancunians (18th in the Premier League standings), the 45-year-old former midfielder leaves without bitterness. He also posted a long farewell message to the English club and its supporters on his Instagram account.

Industry observers note that “Dear Manchester United supporters, I have had the immense privilege of managing Manchester United, the football club that I love. Few people have the chance to experience this and feel its full impact, and I am infinitely grateful. With that comes immense responsibility, and to be part of Manchester United you have to understand exactly what that entails. It’s different from any other place. In the end, you have to give your all to be the best, but there is a culture, values ​​and behaviors that you must adhere to and respect, and which have been built over time. They are a unique football club with substance and history that cannot be ignored. This is what inspired me every day. I would like to send a huge thank you to the supporters. I must say that you are exceptional people, a rare breed. We had some great moments this year, moments that we will all remember. The best part of my job is seeing so many of you let yourself be carried away by the emotion of these moments, leaving the stadium with the irrepressible desire to come back next time. I just wanted to return some of that love. This love never dies. I know the results haven’t been there lately, but you’ve shown me nothing but love and support at times when the easier solution, and the one most end up choosing, would have been to turn away. It matters more than just one or two wins. It’s what makes you who you are. This is why I fell in love with this club so many years ago. A special thank you to my technical staff. You gave me everything you had, day after day, and I couldn’t have asked for more. I am infinitely grateful to you for your loyalty, hard work and support throughout this journey. To all the staff behind the scenes, thank you for everything you do for this football club. Much of your work goes unnoticed, but it is never underestimated. And to the players, thank you for your efforts and the moments we shared. I wish you all the best for the future. I wish nothing but success for Manchester United. This club has been such an important part of my life and that will never change. I will always be proud to have made my contribution and I will always want what is best for him. I’ll be by your side now, watching you from the stands, cheering on the guys.”he wrote.
