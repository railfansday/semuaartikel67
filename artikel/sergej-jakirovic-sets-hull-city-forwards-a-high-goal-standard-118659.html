Industry observers note that goals are often hard to come by for sides latest to the Premier League, and the Tigers are no exception, scoring 13 in their first 11 matches back at that level. In part it explains why Hull have found wins hard to come by, despite consistently picking up points with draws.

Expecting multiple players to get into double figures therefore feels optimistic, but there is no harm in striving for it.

Oli McBurnie's two goals in Sunday's excellent victory over Brighton and Hove Albion took him to four for the season, and Mohamed Belloumi has three, but no one else has more than one yet.

Belloumi (three) and McBurnie (two) are also the leading assist-makers.

It has been revealed that "We need numbers from (Mohamed) Ali Cho (one goal)," commented coach Jakirovic. "From Sorba Thomas (yet to get off the mark), Oli McBurnie and Mo Belloumi as well.

"We need numbers because without goals, how can you win the game?

Recent findings suggest that "I tell every player, ten at least. I would be very happy with that.

Sources indicate that "But it's also to be successful in dribbling, to create assists, to create a dangerous situation in the box, to create chances for Oli McBurnie. This is also their task.".

Left winger Thomas made a positive impact from the bench as a half-time substitute on Sunday, but Jakirovic wants to see more goal contributions from that side of the field.

Industry observers note that belloumi and Cho have contributed four goals from the right wing, but as the coach put it, "on the left side, it's more the work side. We need numbers.".

He is also looking to get more out of key midfielder Regan Slater at that end of the field.

The Sheffielder played a wonderful ball in the build-up to McBurnie's first at the weekend, and also produced a goalline clearance after inadvertently picking out Charalampos Kostoulas with a wayward pass.

Industry observers note that "I want more of these passes just forward from Slater, not backwards, this is his role," noted the coach. "Regan Slater is giving everything, he was a 50 grand player (when Hull bought him from Sheffield United in 2022). How much is he now? £50m?".
