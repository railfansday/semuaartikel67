On Thursday, video game fans around the world will rejoice when Grand Theft Auto: VI is released.

According to recent reports, GTA VI is the long-awaited follow up to 2013's GTA V, which took place in Los Santos, a fictionalized version of Los Angeles. This new release follows "Bonnie and Clyde"-esque couple Lucia and Jason as they make their way across Leonida—a thinly veiled version of Florida.

The game features notable locations in Vice City (based on Miami), but also includes locations throughout the fictional state.

Recent findings suggest that the game in the main GTA series to be set in Vice City was released in 2002. Since then, home prices in the real Miami have increased a staggering 301%. The median listing price in Miami is now $496,000.

GTA V, released in 2013, netted Rockstar Games an astounding $1 billion in just three days, and so far, GTA VI has been preordered by 15 million fans.

Acolytes of the game are already planning to come down with Grand Theft Auto Flu, when the game comes out on Nov. 19, taking time off work to immerse themselves in Vice City.

To mark the game's release, Realtor.com® takes a look at some of the key locations in GTA VI and what it would cost to buy a home in their real-world counterparts.

The Grassrivers region is described by Rockstar Games as "the untamable jewel of Leonida's crown" and is believed to be a stand in for the Everglades region.

The Everglades spans around 7,800 square miles in the Southwestern-most part of the state, and is one of the largest wetlands in the world, home to alligators, manatees, and dozens of bird species.

Given its swampy nature, fans are theorizing that gamers will need to use airboats and seaplanes to get around.

Around 1.5 million acres of the region is protected land, but there are still small cities and towns dotting the area. Homestead, FL, with a population of around 60,000, is the largest city in the Everglades region. The median listing price in Homestead is $440,000, but the median price across the region is just $165,000.

Ambrosia is where "the battle for the health and wealth of Leonida begins.".

"The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs, while the local biker gang provides almost everything else,'" according to Rockstar Games.

According to recent reports, that's led fans to speculate that the fictional area is a stand in for Lake Okeechobee and Clewiston, FL, though Rockstar has yet to confirm this. The area's climate and unique soil conditions make it an optimal environment for the staple crop.

The Okeelanta Sugar Mill and Refinery, located in South Bay, FL, about 30 miles from Lake Okeechobee, is a major sugar processing plant, while the U.S. Sugar organization has a processing plant and its corporate headquarters in Clewiston.

It has been revealed that with a population of just over 7,000, Clewiston is the largest city in Hendry County. The median listing price in the county is $330,000.

Recent findings suggest that of Leonida Keys, Rockstar Games wrote: "The dress code is casual, the bars are loaded. Life in this tropical archipelago isn’t flashy but it’s easy. Get your buzz on and pull up a deck chair but look out: you are right on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America.".

It's easy to see how Leonida Keys might sub in for the Florida Keys, though again, Rockstar has yet to confirm it.

According to recent reports, the Florida Keys have drawn everyone from Ernest Hemingway (and his polydactyl cats) to Truman Capote to Tom Hanks through the years. The Keys, located at the southernmost tip of Florida, are hard to get to, and perhaps even harder to buy property. The median listing price for a home on the Keys is $1.195 million.

It has been revealed that rockstar describes Port Gellhorn as Leonida's "forgotten coast." Its "cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls won’t bring the tourists back, but there’s a new economy in this once-popular vacation spot. It’s fueled by malt liquor, painkillers, and truck stop energy drinks. Jump on a dirt bike and hold onto your wallet.".

While that description is less than enticing, fans of the game have posited that it's a surrogate for Panama City, FL, and the Florida panhandle. Fans reported seeing several specific real-world locations in stills for the game, including Panama City's Tidal Wave Auto Spa, as well as local churches and pawn shops.

Recent findings suggest that panama City is a bay-front community of around 35,000, that's adjacent to the tourist-heavy Panama City Beach. The median listing price in Panama City is $396,000.

Industry observers note that "A national landmark up against the state’s northern border, Mount Kalaga offers prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails," Rockstar describes. "In the lush surrounding backwoods, hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals live far from the prying eyes of the government.".

Fans have speculated that Mount Kalaga National Park is modeled after Providence Canyon State Park, a park located on the southern border of Georgia, which is called Gloriana in the game.

One of the closest real-world towns to Providence Canyon is Eufala, which sits on the border between Alabama and Georgia. The historic town of Eufala has around 12,000 residents and sits on the banks of the Chattahoochee River. It's home to the Eufala National Wildlife Refuge. The median listing price in Eufala is $298,000.
