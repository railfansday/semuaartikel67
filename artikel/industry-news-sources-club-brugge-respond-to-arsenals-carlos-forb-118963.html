Arsenal have added Club Brugge winger Carlos Forbs to their list of targets ahead of the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The Gunners are hoping to add a fresh left-sided attacker to their squad, and right now have the 22-year-old towards the top of their shortlist for the role.

Mikel Arteta's side are "keeping tabs" on the youngster's progress, and have been hugely impressed by what they have seen from him of late.

However, sources say that Brugge have already made it clear to Arsenal that Forbs is not for sale in the middle of the campaign.

The Belgian outfit are still competing in the Champions League, and have no desire to lose their star man while they're still in Europe.

Forbs has notched five goals and seven assists in all competitions so far this term, and Arsenal's interest in him is unlikely to die down any time soon.

Forbs is no stranger to English football, having previously been part of the academy setup at Manchester City.

His experience with the Citizens never went beyond the stage of playing EFL Trophy football for their U21 side, but he then got a brief taste of the Premier League during a loan spell with Wolves in 2024-25.

Brugge picked the wideman up from Ajax back in 2025, and since then his form has flourished.

Forbs ended last season with 22 goal involvements in all competitions, including six in the Champions League, and he is impressing again so far this term.

He is also now gaining international experience for Portugal, and recently scored the winner in their Nations League victory over Wales.

Forbs' pace and direct style fit the criteria for what Arsenal want on their left flank, and they are set to continue tracking his progress long-term, even with a move set to be out of reach this winter.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Arsenal will open talks over a fresh contract for striker Kai Havertz once they have tied down some of their other stars.

Declan Rice is already on the verge of a fresh deal at the Emirates Stadium, while the trio of Jurrien Timber, Piero Hincapie and David Raya are all next on the agenda.

However, sources say that it is merely a "matter of time" before the Gunners move to tie Havertz down, ahead of his current deal expiring in 2028.

Arteta remains a huge fan of the German and has no intention of losing him any time soon.
