Erling Haaland stated that Manchester City were "not good enough" after snatching a 2-2 Champions League draw against Napoli at home.

Rayan Cherki struck in the 86th-minute to equalise, allowing City to come from behind a second time and avoid defeat against the Italians on Tuesday.

The outcome prolongs a frustrating spell of form for City, who have secured victory in just one of their last seven matches across all competitions.

The City striker told TNT Sports: “We tried to push at the end for a goal but we couldn’t – not good enough and we let them in too easily.

“We were not able to control and attack the game in the way we want.

“Football is easy and difficult – when you win it’s easy and you know what to do. When you don’t, you start questioning everything.

“The perfect thing would be to not think but it’s not so easy.

“We have so much quality so it’s about getting the quality out in every player.

“We have to regroup, stay positive and bring energy on the pitch and try to do our best.”.

After a tame first half, City fell behind to a goal from former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund after 55 minutes.

Ayyoub Bouaddi pulled them back into the game but City needed to fight back again after Noa Lang restored Napoli’s lead with a fine volley 14 minutes from time.

Cherki’s leveller was a fine individual effort but the Frenchman felt the overall performance had been a flat one.

He said: “I don’t care about scoring, I just want to win. We played good but we didn’t play with energy, we need to do more.

“Today was like the last games, we need to work more.”.

City manager Enzo Maresca laughed when told of Cherki’s comments, insisting there were positives to build on.

The Italian mentioned: “No, I think tonight we pressed very aggressive, we recovered the ball most of the time. Off the ball I think the team was good, from my point of view.

Sources indicate that “We created a lot of chances but it was difficult. We expected Napoli to play a waiting game in a low block.

“We conceded few chances, and the goals we conceded could have been avoided.

Sources indicate that “There is a degree of regret we didn’t win but when you can’t win, don’t lose. We take the point and go forward.”.
