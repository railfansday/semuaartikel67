Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond has left Google feeling shaken, not stirred.

Following the announcement that Pattinson would be our next 007, Google introduced a new Bond-themed Easter egg. If you search up Pattinson's name, the search results shake a little, and a small twist of lemon falls from the top of the screen to rest daintily on Pattinson's head.

Industry observers note that the Easter egg is a nod to James Bond's classic cocktail order: a vodka martini, shaken, not stirred. Google has essentially turned Pattinson into the cocktail he'll be ordering onscreen.

Pattinson's casting as Bond came after weeks of speculation and reported screen tests for potential Bond candidates, including Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor, and Nicholas Hoult.

Sources indicate that pattinson will star in the as-yet unnamed 26th Bond film, the first since Daniel Craig took his last bow in 2021's No Time To Die. Dune helmer Denis Villeneuve is set to direct. The pair have also worked together on the upcoming Dune: Part Three. Villeneuve and Pattinson's Bond film does not yet have a release date.

Bond won't be the first franchise Pattinson has led to be commemorated with a Google Easter egg. In 2022, tied to The Batman's release, if you searched terms such as "Bruce Wayne" or "Gotham City," a yellow Bat-Signal would appear alongside the results. Clicking on it turned the page into a moody Gotham night, complete with thunder, the Bat-Signal, and Batman swinging around the screen.

Plus, if you search up "Twilight" now, you'll be treated to a small line of scrolling text that reads "hoa hoa hoa season," flanked by falling leaf emojis. The Easter egg references Twilight fans' loving nickname for fall, drawn from the so-called "hoa hoa hoa" vocals of Blue Foundation's "Eyes on Fire" from the Twilight soundtrack.
