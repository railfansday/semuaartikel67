Glenn Close Details the Greenwich Upbringing That Served as a Beacon During Her Time in a ‘Restrictive Cult’ and Inspired Her ‘Family Sanctuary’ in Montana

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Glenn Close has told many incredible stories on-screen, but during a recent acceptance speech, “The Hunger Games” star shared some insight on her own eventful past.

The actress, 79, was awarded an honorary Oscar at the Governors Awards on Nov. 16. After receiving a standing ovation from the audience, she revealed, “It’s been very difficult for me to process this incredible honor because the truth is, I carry the epigenome of an outsider.”.

The Greenwich, CT, native added that “from the ages of seven to 22,” she was “under the thumb of a restrictive cult.”.

Industry observers note that she also rattled off a few of the rules she was forced to follow: “Don’t look at yourself in the mirror. No flirting. Dress this way. Talk this way. Say it’s OK when your parents are routinely sent away from you for months on end.”.

In a 2025 interview with the Guardian, Close explained that her father, a surgeon named William Taliaferro Close, decided to move to Switzerland and join Moral Re-Armament, a group the publication described as a “rightwing religious cult.”.

The “All’s Fair” actress has shared little of her experience in the cult, but she has noted the importance of the earliest years of her upbringing—when her family lived in Fresh England and the “landscape” of her childhood and she was a “little feral child” enjoying the “pastoral” land of her grandfather’s farm in Greenwich.

Industry observers note that “I was always on the outside looking in,” she remarked in the speech. “But due to my earlier childhood, running free in the woods and fields of Connecticut, I was able to retreat into my imagination.”.

Close went on to describe how the “great Disney animated features” such as “Fantasia” and “Snow White” became a “secret world” that “sustained” her and “fed” her “insatiable imagination.”.

“In 1970, I found the strength to leave the cult, and enrolled as a 22-year-old freshman at the college of William and Mary,” she continued, noting that her first move was to head “straight to the theater department,” where she auditioned and was cast as Olivia in Shakespeare’s "Twelfth Night.”.

While Close would become a successful star, she stated she never shook the feeling of being different.

According to recent reports, “As an outsider, I have never worried about whether I was going to win or not,” she noted. “Though some years, I thought that I might have a chance. I have always felt that the great honor is to be in the room.”.

She explained that she now feels like she is no longer “looking in the window,” but is a meaningful part of the “extraordinary community” she’s worked with in her 43-year career.

Although Close holds Newly introduced England in high regard, she has since settled down in a home in an equally-beautiful destination on the other side of the country: Bozeman, MT.

Industry observers note that according to property records, Close purchased her three-bedroom, three-bathroom abode through a trust in 2016 for an undisclosed amount. In a 2021 Mountain Outlaw interview, she revealed she had relocated to be closer to her sisters and brother, who have homes in the area.

Close shared more details about the home in a 2025 chat with the Wall Street Journal, where she detailed the homes she’s lived in throughout her life.

“Today my home is in Bozeman,” she explained. “All of my siblings live here. My modest, 1982 house has a porch where I can see the mountains and say hi.”.

At the time, she revealed she was “building a larger house about a half-hour outside of town,” that will serve as a “Zen farmhouse” and a “family sanctuary.” Close said a stone cottage, designed to remind her of the “best years” of her childhood, were also part of the prepares.

It has been revealed that close maintains a very close relationship to her siblings, and even thanked her “beloved sisters, Tina and Jesse” in her award acceptance speech. She often shares glimpses of their family life in Montana with her Instagram followers, capturing the idyllic views seen from the property.

It has been revealed that in an Aug. 25 post, Close posted a video to mark how grateful she felt to be home, showcasing the stunning Montana mountains.

“I’m home, calming down, wearing my mama’s shirt, letting the wind blow through my white hair, watching the sun go down,” she boasted. “I feel blessed.”.
