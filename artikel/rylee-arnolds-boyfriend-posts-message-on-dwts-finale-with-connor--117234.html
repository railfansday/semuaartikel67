Rylee Arnold's boyfriend thinks Connor Wood is all that jazz.

After Wood addressed fans who hoped that his partnership with his Dancing With the Stars coach might translate off the dance floor, the pro's real-life match, BYU football player Walker Lyons, chimed in with support for the team taking home the season 35 title.

In a recent development, "Today is the day! So proud of you and the work you have put in to get here, so deserving," Lyons captioned a shot of Arnold holding a supersized bouquet of roses on Instagram Stories Nov. 24. As for the influencer known as Fibula, he added, "Vote for Connor.".

And the 21-year-old treasured the romantic gesture from her boyfriend of two years, calling the floral display that arrived that morning, "the best surprise ever.".

In a recent development, wood, 31, previously tap-danced past speculation about his relationship with Arnold in a Nov. 10 TikTok Live, sharing one message for viewers wishing to turn his shot at the Mirrorball into fan fiction: "I say, 'Thank you for watching.'".

However, Arnold had plenty of words to express how much she valued finding a romance with Lyons.

It has been revealed that "There were so many crazy coincidences that actually brought us together," she told E! News following the Halloween episode Oct. 30. "He’s just such a great guy, I really found a good one who makes me feel so special. And we have so much fun together.".

The Dancing with the Stars: Juniors alum added regarding the attention around her onscreen chemistry with Wood that she told the 22-year-old, "'It's scary I know,'" noting how Lyons has been "the best sport about it—he’s just the best.".

As such, Arnold didn't hold back when remarking how excited she was to find a platonic partner in crime in the podcaster for her fourth season as a pro.

Sources indicate that "It just feels natural," Arnold told Entertainment Weekly in September. "I think naturally we just have so much fun together. We feel comfortable with each other.".
