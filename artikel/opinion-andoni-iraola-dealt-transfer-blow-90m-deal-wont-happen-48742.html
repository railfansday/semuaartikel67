Andoni Iraola was sort of thrown in the deep end when he took over from Arne Slot as Liverpool's manager.

The Reds were coming off the back of a fifth-place finish in the Premier League, a result which ended with Slot being relieved of his duties.

Fenway Sports Group knew that they had to get their next appointment right which is why fans were so happy with the Spaniard being appointed.

His heavy metal style of football and his previous Premier League experience looked like two of the main reasons why he was perfectly suited to the role.

However, since his appointment, things have been a bit topsy-turvy with Liverpool even losing to Fenerbahce in the Premier League.

This caused many questions to arise with the upcoming transfer windows now seeming rather vital.

Atletico Madrid will not sign Martin Zubimendi which could stop Pablo Barrios from joining Liverpool.

Liverpool's midfield is one of their main problem areas at the moment.

Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni have done a stellar job but, since neither of them can play every single game, others such as Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch have been called upon.

And, even though both of those players were handed newly introduced long-term deals last term, they just can't seem to get the hang of playing in a double pivot.

This realisation has dawned upon Liverpool recently which is why Atletico Madrid midfielder Pablo Barrios was linked with a move.

Of course, it was just a rumour but of all the midfielders mentioned, Barrios felt like a rather exciting option.

However, thanks to a fresh update from journalist Matteo Moretto, Liverpool's move for Barrios seems to have just hit a major roadblock.

"Martín Zubimendi is not a financially viable target for Atlético Madrid ahead of the winter transfer window," he shared via X.

"Arsenal would demand a fee of over €70 million, and the Gunners intend to keep him despite Chelsea’s interest.

"Martin Zubimendi is not on Atlético’s radar at the moment.".

Liverpool may have to turn their attention towards Lamine Camara again.

Liverpool have been linked with fresh midfielders ever since Fabinho left.

The Brazilian was the Reds' last natural number six and, since he hasn't been replaced yet, the Anfield outfit are still on the lookout for latest talent.

Many different players have come close to joining Liverpool with Lamine Camara being one of their most-linked players in the summer.

Obviously, that deal never came to fruition but, since the Senegal international didn't move to another club, talk of him being of interest to Liverpool has emerged again.

During his time with AS Monaco, Camara has become one of their most key players and, thanks to this, their asking price for him has sky-rocketed.

However, with Liverpool's options for upcoming midfielders now dwindling, their deal for Camara may have to be back on, even if he is going to cost an arm and a leg.
