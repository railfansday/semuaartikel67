The Shiba Inu ecosystem may be set for a privacy boost, as longtime SHIB community member and developer Mazrael hints at privacy potential coming to the blockchain.

Shiba Inu's privacy potential draws from Oxbow's Privacy Pools, the privacy design coauthored by Ethereum cofounder Vitalik Buterin, which is right now a plug-in in the Ethereum Foundation's Kohaku wallet SDK.Mazrael noted that Shibarium is now ready for Oxbow's Privacy Pools, with the groundwork already being done.

It has been revealed that WOOF 🐶🪖🫡 And it's privacy done the regulation-friendly way. Privacy Pools come from a 2023 paper Vitalik co-wrote: deposits are screened, so stolen funds can't hide in the crowd, while honest users stay private. No KYC, no backdoors, and you can always exit publicly. Two ways to bring it to Shibarium: 0xbow deploys it themselves, or the community deploys the same contracts and runs a relayer. Shibarium is ready either way, and ShibWallet already runs it on testnet 🔥.

The groundwork done on Shibarium (Chain ID 109) includes Cancun opcodes, BN254 pairing support for zero-knowledge proofs, CreateX and Arachnid factories and verified PoseidonT3 and PoseidonT4 contracts at Ethereum addresses.

Recent findings suggest that mazrael shared a GitHub document, "Shibarium (chain 109) support: chain is prepared," which highlighted this fact. Addressed to the Oxbow team was a request: "Hi 0xbow team! We build on Shibarium (chain ID 109) and would love to see Privacy Pools there. We've done the groundwork so a deployment would be as easy as possible, and ShibWallet aims to integrate it through Kohaku from day one.".

Recent findings suggest that mazrael explained that Privacy Pools originated from a 2023 paper Ethereum cofounder Vitalik coauthored: deposits are screened, so stolen funds can't hide in the crowd, while honest users stay private. No KYC, no backdoors and users can always exit publicly.

Two ways to bring it to Shibarium include 0xbow deploys it themselves, or the community deploys the same contracts and runs a relayer.

For the first option, Mazrael has relayed a GitHub request to the Oxbow team, which is currently showing signs of a green light, with Mazrael remarking that "they did the approval thing when we posted basically.".

In a recent development, A 0xbow privacy pool deployment for Shiba Inu will bring 'entry point', 'verifiers', and 'PrivacyPoolSimple' for native BONE (Shibarium's gas token). It will also bring 'PrivacyPoolComplex' for SHIB and other ecosystem tokens later.

The Shib ecosystem would become privacy-complian and ShibWallet would integrate it through Kohaku from day one.
