Tomorrow evening, AC Milan will entrust Ardon Jashari with the job of filling Luka Modric’s shoes, after the Croatian’s injury.

If you missed last night’s reporting, Milan have been dealt a bit of a blow ahead of their game against Olympiacos, as Modric has suffered a muscle strain. It’s not a major one, and he should be back for next weekend’s game.

Nevertheless, it creates a short-term concern and in a midfield where only a few names have convinced, it plunges Ruben Amorim into the water somewhat. There is a solution, though, and it would be ideal if he stepped up.

Industry observers note that since he arrived, Jashari has been seen as the next in line for Modric, but he’s never shown performances to match that eventual rise. However, Gazzetta dello Sport (as seen below) write that he could take that step against Olympiacos.

Sources indicate that it’s a potential turning point for the Swiss, who still has the faith of the club and the fans, especially after his first goal at the weekend. So, with it being a necessity that he comes into the team here, a solid performance is needed.

It has been revealed that it opens a space for Jashari, but filling Modric’s boots will not be an easy task. In fact, he’ll have to be at his best to replace him, especially in a game with so much on the line.

Looking at the Europa League table, the Rossoneri need to be perfect if they’re to have any chance of a top eight finish. For him, though, Jashari must be perfect if his journey at San Siro is to continue.

So far, his time in Italy has been marred by injury and a failure to establish himself as a starter, or even a key rotational option. Yet, the Diavolo never gave up on him, refusing a sale in the summer.

Now, it is time to repay that in a game where ‘everything looks different’. For Jashari, it’s a simple task: be solid. He’s got a guaranteed place, reliable teammates around him and the trust to act.

Sources indicate that no one can challenge him for the next five days; the spot is guaranteed. It’ll be up to him if those five days become 10 or more. If not, there’s a market waiting for him.
