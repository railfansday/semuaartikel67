Black Friday MacBook deals are live. And as someone who tracks MacBook prices year-round, I'm sure I speak for most shoppers when I say: finally. Between Apple's summer price hikes on the MacBook Air and MacBook Neo, and its expensive fresh "Pro" releases this fall, these holiday savings are a sight for sore bank accounts.

So far, some of the MacBook deals I'm seeing are even better than I expected. Black Friday discounts on the MacBook Air peaked at $250 in 2025, but this year I've already found two configurations of Apple's ultraportable on sale for $300 off. Then there's the budget MacBook Neo, which is $50 off right now.

Sources indicate that for their parts, the latest M6 MacBook Pro and MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display are both on sale for the first time ahead of the holidays. Based on last year's numbers, I suspect they might see further discounts as we head into Cyber Weekend, but it's up to you to decide if you're willing to wait.

The Mashable Choice Award-winning MacBook Neo is our favorite budget laptop on the market. It has the same premium build quality as Apple's other MacBooks, and its A18 Pro processor — a pared-down iPhone chip, wouldn't you know it — makes it much faster than competing Windows PCs and Chromebooks. "For students, young people, and casual customers, there's nothing else like it," said Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth, who rated it a 4.7/5.
