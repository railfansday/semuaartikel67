As Republicans continue to pick up the pieces from their 2026 midterm losses, one Trump administration official is catching some GOP lawmakers' eyes as the party's next great hope.

"[Secretary of State Marco Rubio] is the whole package. He's intelligent, charismatic, level-headed, cool-tempered and very knowledgeable about the challenges and eloquent in explaining them," Rep. Nicole Malliotakis, R-N.Y., told Fox News Digital. "I think there is a lot of interest from Republicans in seeing him explore a run.".

Indeed, multiple House Republicans who spoke with Fox News Digital said they and their colleagues are pushing Rubio to run for president in 2028. The Cabinet official himself has publicly dismissed any talk about his future political plans, but his name is on the rise among Republicans who have said the party needs to pivot from the complications that cost them votes in November.

"Now that we lost majorities, we can't push policy, so we will have to do better at communicating," Rep. Marlin Stutzman, R-Ind., told Fox News Digital on election night. "I am all in for Marco. He can be a unifier.".

Rubio has long been held up as a potential 2028 contender, despite a previous unsuccessful White House bid in 2016.

He and Vice President JD Vance have been regarded as the top contenders for the GOP nomination, but it's Rubio's name that has come up in conversations among House Republicans, Fox News Digital was told.

Carlos Gimenez, R-Fla., told Fox News Digital. " And it's not just a few. There's a number, a pretty good number, that have told me that.".

And Malliotakis, who is half Cuban herself, said Rubio could "reach" Hispanic voters who largely turned away from the GOP in November.

But within the administration itself, it appears no clear successor has emerged.

One source familiar with talks told Fox News Digital that Trump administration staffers were "about 50/50" when asked if they favored Vance or Rubio.

"I think it’s pretty split," one administration official mentioned. "I don’t think the president really knows. I think if you asked him today, he’d say Rubio.".

"50/50 feels right to me," a second administration official said.

White House spokeswoman Anna Kelly referred Fox News Digital to President Donald Trump’s previous comments on the matter.

Over the weekend, Trump told Fox News' "Saturday in America" that Vance and Rubio are "doing fantastically" and are "both very, very popular" when asked who he would like to see run on the Republican and Democratic tickets ahead of the 2028 presidential election.
