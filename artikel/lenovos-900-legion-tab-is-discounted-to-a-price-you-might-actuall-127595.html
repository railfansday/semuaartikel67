Lenovo’s Legion Tab Gen 5 is incredibly expensive at its usual price tag of $900, but Black Friday has it discounted pretty heavily, to a price you might actually want to pay.

As part of Lenovo’s Black Friday 2026 sale, the firm has the Legion Tab Gen 5 discounted from $899 to $599, a 33% discount. That price still includes a folio case in the box and the 12GB/256GB setup for the tablet.

As a reminder, Legion Tab Gen 5 has an 8.8-inch 165Hz display, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, Android 16, and a 9,000 mAh battery.

We just reviewed the Legion Tab Gen 5 recently, with Will Sattelberg finding the tablet to be great for Android games, and for the DroidDeck project that brings full Steam games to Android:.

Getting Steam emulation — complete with the Deck’s usual Big Picture mode UI — available as an application on the Legion Tab Gen 5 takes all of two minutes and a handful of taps on your display, and I have to say, I’m pretty shocked at just how impressive the overall experience is right now….
