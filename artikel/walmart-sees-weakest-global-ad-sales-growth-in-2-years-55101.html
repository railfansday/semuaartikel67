37%: Walmart U.S.’ year-over-year advertising growth, including Vizio and Vibe.co. Excluding those two acquired businesses, Walmart Connect grew 36% within the U.S.

17%: Growth in Walmart’s global ecommerce sales. Walmart U.S. ecommerce sales grew 23%.
