Covering TOPCon, XBC, HJT, and Perovskite-Silicon Tandem Technologies; Over 1,000 Automated Module Lines Delivered Globally, More Than 40 in North America.

RE+ 2026 was held at the Las Vegas Convention Center from November 17 to 19. Driven by the U.S. IRA policy that fuels domestic PV capacity expansion amid diversified cell technology roadmaps, SC Solar showcased turnkey automation solutions for high-efficiency modules, including TOPCon multi-cut, BC, HJT, and perovskite-silicon tandem. The team held face-to-face business discussions with global manufacturers on factory planning, equipment configuration, process adaptation, and production line optimization.

In 2026, the U.S. solar market continued to evolve amid sustained demand and structural adjustments. According to a report released by the Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA) and Wood Mackenzie, the United States added 11.4 GW of solar capacity in the second quarter, up 45% year over year, with utility-scale projects accounting for most of the increase. An SEIA brief updated in September 2026 put U.S. domestic module nameplate manufacturing capacity at approximately 75.3 GW per year. While installation demand remained substantial, the pace of manufacturing capacity expansion moderated. Changes in market competition, policy and investment conditions raised the bar for factory operations and cost control.

For PV manufacturers, capacity expansion requires balancing the need for rapid commissioning with long-term operating value. Key priorities for equipment providers include matching equipment solutions closely to product processes, reducing losses across production-line workflows, improving yield during ramp-up and long-term operation, and lowering manufacturing costs. Drawing on years of project implementation experience in China and overseas, SC Solar combines R&D with the practical needs of factories, providing robust support for PV manufacturing in North America through turnkey automation solutions.

Sources indicate that at this year’s exhibition, SC Solar presented customized module automation solutions for the distinct manufacturing needs of TOPCon multi-cut, BC and HJT technology pathways. Integrating production, logistics and digital management, the solutions demonstrated coordinated factory-wide capabilities spanning module manufacturing, warehousing, packaging and truck loading and delivery. Through process adaptation and system integration, they help customers coordinate production planning and factory operations. At the exhibition, the SC Solar team held in-depth discussions with visiting customers and partners on newly introduced factory planning, upgrades to existing production lines and operational optimization. The team also explored equipment selection and solution configurations for specific project scenarios, laying a solid foundation for future cooperation.

With years of experience in the U.S. market, SC Solar continues to build on its project delivery and customer service expertise. Since 2017, the company has delivered more than 40 PV module production lines to the U.S. market and established its U.S. subsidiary in 2024. Supported by a local technical service team and spare parts warehouse in Texas, SC Solar provides technical training, operations and maintenance (O&M), capacity upgrades, spare parts supply and remote diagnostics, continually strengthening service support throughout the operating life cycle of customers’ production lines.

According to recent reports, from equipment delivery to life-cycle services, SC Solar remains focused on the stable operation and sustainable growth of its customers’ factories. Looking ahead, the manufacturer will continue to advance technological innovation and strengthen its local service capabilities. Working with partners around the world, SC Solar will turn manufacturing needs into practical, implementable solutions and support the high-quality development of the global PV manufacturing industry.
