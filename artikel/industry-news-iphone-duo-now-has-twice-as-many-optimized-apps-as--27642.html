Apple marketing SVP Greg Joswiak stated today that the number of iPhone Duo-ready apps on the App Store is twice what it was when Apple last reported the figure. Here are the details.

While it was obvious that the number of iPhone Duo-ready apps would increase over time, their limited availability was a substantial part of the conversation around early reviews and online commentary in the first days after the device rolled out.

That remarked, it was clear even from the early reviews that app updates were picking up promptly, with some reviewers having to rewrite or re-record parts of their coverage as more apps gained support for the device.

Earlier this month, Apple put a number to that progress when it confirmed it had surpassed 100,000 optimized apps.

In a recent development, now, just 10 days later, that number has doubled, according to Apple’s senior vice president of Worldwide Marketing, Greg Joswiak. In a post on X, he touted that more than 200,000 apps are now optimized for iPhone Duo, adding that he is “excited by how quickly developers have embraced” the device.

According to recent reports, over 200,000 apps are now optimized for iPhone Duo! That number DOUBLED in just over a week.I’m excited by how quickly developers have embraced iPhone Duo, and I can’t wait to see what they build next.

Joswiak’s patch came on the same day that Apple released iOS 27.3 developer beta 2, which addresses two other common complaints from early iPhone Duo reviews: the lack of Face ID, and the inability to automatically lock the device when closing it.

As 9to5Mac covered earlier today, the second developer beta of iOS 27.3 introduces a new unlock option that uses the outer display’s camera to recognize the user’s face, as well as a setting to automatically lock the device when folded shut.

The latter addresses a common complaint from owners who found it strange that closing the phone didn’t automatically lock it, requiring them to press the side button instead.
