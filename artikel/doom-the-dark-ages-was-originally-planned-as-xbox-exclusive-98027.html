DOOM: The Dark Ages was originally designed as an Xbox exclusive, but late decisions led it to being ported to PS5.

The news comes from a Kiwi Talkz podcast, where host Reece Reilly was joined by three former id Software devs to discuss all things DOOM, Xbox Game Pass, id Software work culture, layoffs, and more.

Interestingly, quite far into the conversation, ex-id software employee Jordan Turner claims that "originally [DOOM: The Dark Ages] was going to be Xbox exclusive" up until "fairly late in the process".

Sources indicate that "And in Dark Ages' case too, originally the game was going to be Xbox exclusive, and then fairly late in the process, we were porting to PS5. And at that point, we were still like, is our goal premium sales? In certain studio meetings, Marty [Stratton] would say stuff like, when we got it on Battle.net - 'it's another premium platform to put the game on'".

Reacting to Turner, Reilly goes on to critic the Xbox Game Pass model, saying he doesn't "understand it" and that it "devalues games". The conversation continues with a discussion about a lack of clarity and protection from Microsoft when developing games for Xbox Game Pass, and how id Software exec Marty Stratton felt porting to PS5 was "another premium platform to put The Dark Ages on" (as seen above).

Speaking to IGN in 2024, former Xbox CEO Phil Spencer described DOOM as a franchise that "everyone deserves to play", recalling a meeting between Stratton and himself in which the id Software exec said "he wanted to sell it on all platforms. Simple as that".

Back in July, the DOOM developer was dealt a massive blow as part of Xbox's planned 'Reset', which saw id Software lay off a significant portion of its team.

id Software still remains an Xbox first-party developer, and is "already working on its next game", according to Xbox boss Asha Sharma's recent post on social media.
