Olivia Colman debuted a sophisticated hair transformation Monday night at the premiere of Only Murders in London.

Celebrating the sixth season of the Disney+ show, in which she guest-stars alongside Steve Martin and Selena Gomez, Colman wore head-to-toe Celine, walking the red carpet in a striking silk cape top, tailored trousers, and pointed boots.

It has been revealed that equally elegant was the Oscar-winning actress’s upcoming hair color, her second in as many months. Earlier this autumn, Colman debuted a polished “smoky steel” hue; however, she stepped out in the capital this week with an inky black shade.

Hairstylist Earl Simms created Colman’s upcoming look, and the actress joins a handful of other celebrities embracing dark, moody tones this winter. This month alone, Dakota Johnson was seen sporting fresh, jet-black hair, while Billie Piper and Hailey Bieber have also gone considerably darker in recent weeks.

As the nights draw in and summer becomes but a distant memory, many stylish women opt for deep, cozy hues at salon appointments, and while dark brunette shades are generally lower maintenance than bright blondes, there are still a few housekeeping rules, as Luci Green, a colorist at London salon Larry King Hair, previously told Bazaar.

Recent findings suggest that while brunettes might need less frequent touch-ups than blondes, any chemically colored hair still needs care and attention. “The best way to treat freshly coloured hair at home is to always use a professional color protect shampoo and conditioner,” Green explained. “It’s also important to avoid using too much heat,” she added.

According to recent reports, but first things first: Always get a consultation. Green shared: “I’d always advise having a consultation with a colorist before any color change, so they can work out what the best shade is for your skin tone.” Noted!
