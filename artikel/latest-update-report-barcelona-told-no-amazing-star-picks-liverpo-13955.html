According to recent reports, barcelona have been told no - one amazing star wants to join Liverpool. He's ready and waiting to agree a deal with the Reds.

Liverpool’s defence needs work. The Reds aren’t happy with what they’ve got and change will be forced on them purely through contract situations. The biggest of those being Virgil van Dijk.

Industry observers note that the Dutchman is out of contract next summer. We hope Liverpool renew the deal but he’ll also turn 36 ahead of the 2027/28 campaign. There is every chance that the Reds just let him walk away.

Joe Gomez, too, is out of contract. It would be a massive surprise if he re-signed, given his injury history, and that would force Liverpool into some difficult squad decisions as they’d lose yet another homegrown player.

Add in the bizarre right-back situation where Conor Bradley is out long-term and Jeremie Frimpong simply isn’t trusted. Liverpool have very little on their books guaranteed next season - but they do at least have one semi-guarantee.

Ronald Araujo is presently on loan from Barcelona, so he's not actually certain to be with the squad beyond this campaign. Though, there is a buy clause for €55m that could change things.

And Marca reports that Araujo would sign for Liverpool if they triggered it. He's ready to turn down a return to Barcelona, picking a long-term future with the Reds over taking a step backwards.

Recent findings suggest that that's golden news for Liverpool. Araujo is someone who helps significantly with both the centre-back and right-back problem areas, as we've seen already this season. He's also a very experienced player - something Liverpool desperately need as at the moment, the only centre-backs guaranteed to be with the squad next season are Jeremy Jacuqet (22) and Giovanni Leoni (19).

Recent findings suggest that now, it's key to note that things could change. Liverpool can't trigger their buy option until the end of the season and perhaps Araujo has a change of heart by then. Even if that feels unlikely at this stage.

In a recent development, because as things stand, he wants to stay with Liverpool. We think it will ultimately prove to be a sensible signing a real win for the squad in what looks like a complicated summer.

So fingers crossed things stay on track.
