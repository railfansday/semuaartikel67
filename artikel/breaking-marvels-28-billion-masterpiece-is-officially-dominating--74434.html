Recent findings suggest that doomsday is closer than ever. The biggest cinematic event of the decade is upon us, and it's hard to keep track of exactly who is involved. The Russos have assembled their largest batch of heroes and villains yet, including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Hemsworth, Anthony Mackie, Tom Hiddleston, Florence Pugh, Vanessa Kirby, Pedro Pascal, and many more. The film is also set to be the late, great Patrick Stewart's final on-screen appearance after he sadly passed away at the age of 86 last month.

According to recent reports, so, what's the latest revision on Doomsday? The most recent news came from Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige, who promised fans a more emotionally nuanced version of Channing Tatum's Gambit, following his humor-heavy appearance in Deadpool & Wolverine. "He's a standout. It's fun to see Gambit be as charismatic as he was in Deadpool & Wolverine, and yet in Doomsday it's not all for laughs. He's a fully-rounded character.".

Recent findings suggest that as anticipation grows for Doomsday, Marvel's biggest movie to date is flying high on the streaming charts. Avengers: Endgame, the highest-grossing movie of all time, is presently the most-watched film on Disney+ in the world. Two of Disney's blockbuster 2026 releases, the live-action Moana movie and the fifth installment in the Toy Story franchise, are no match for Endgame right now, which also tops the Disney+ charts in the U.S. The film was a global phenomenon, scoring incredible reviews from critics, as many were left stunned that Marvel managed to pull off a seemingly impossible task.

Sources indicate that director James Cameron was no stranger to the #1 position at the global box office when Avatar first debuted, as it took the worldwide record from his own previous blockbuster, Titanic. Then came Endgame, which stole the title until an Avatarre-release won it back. Then, in anticipation of Doomsday, Endgame was re-released to the world earlier this year, adding another $126 million to its global total and taking the crown back once again. At the time of writing, Avengers: Endgame has earned $2.942 billion in global box office revenue, but can Doomsday overtake it?

Avengers: Endgame is available to stream on Disney+. Make sure to stay tuned to Collider for the latest streaming news.
