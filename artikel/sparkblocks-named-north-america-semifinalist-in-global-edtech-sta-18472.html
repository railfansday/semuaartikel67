It has been revealed that WEST VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Block and Code Electronics Inc., developer of SparkBlocks, has been selected as one of eight North America semifinalists in the 2026 Global EdTech Startup Awards (GESAwards), described by organizers as the world's largest edtech competition. The organization will present its approach to teaching electricity and engineering through experimentation and play at today's regional competition, hosted by LearnLaunch.

It has been revealed that learnLaunch Fund + Accelerator reviewed more than 200 applications before selecting the eight semifinalists, who pitch to a panel of venture capitalists, angel investors and education specialists. Ventures are evaluated on the educational problems they address, their teaching approaches, user experience and potential for sustainable growth.

Sources indicate that "Children can learn to build a working circuit before they know the vocabulary to explain it," said Mark Vaughan, CEO, inventor and co-founder of SparkBlocks. "They experiment, discover relationships and develop questions of their own. We build on that experience with definitions, measurement and scientific principles. Being selected for the GESAwards North America semifinals is a meaningful opportunity to bring that approach to the international education community.".

It has been revealed that sparkBlocks puts real electronic components into LEGOÂ®-compatible building blocks. Children connect lights, motors, switches and sensors directly into their constructions, creating working circuits they can take apart, modify and rebuild.

That exploration provides a foundation for structured science learning. A student might start by making a motor turn, then investigate how changing the circuit affects its behaviour. Turning the motor by hand to light an LED introduces the reverse process: converting movement into electrical energy.

"The moment a student asks why something happened is an opportunity to teach," said Michelle Vaughan, Director of Education and Outreach at Block and Code Electronics Inc. "Our curriculum builds on those discoveries, helping students link what they built and saw to circuit diagrams, measurements and the scientific principles behind them. They have an experience to reason from, and a reason to want to understand more.".

Designed for ages eight and up, SparkBlocks supports open-ended invention and guided learning, with classroom kits, teaching resources and curriculum covering electricity, energy and engineering.

The GESAwards selection follows SparkBlocks' Editor's Choice Blue Ribbon at Maker Faire Bay Area 2026.

GESAwards is organized by MindCET Labs with a worldwide network of regional partners. The North America regional winner will advance to the global finals in London in January 2027.
