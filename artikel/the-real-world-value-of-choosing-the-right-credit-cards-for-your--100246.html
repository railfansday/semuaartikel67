The real-world value of choosing the right credit cards for your everyday spending We frequently tout the incredible value you can get from top rewards credit cards — from bonus categories to welcome offers, lucrative benefits to monthly statement credits.

But exactly how much value can you get from matching the right credit cards to your spending habits?

Well, we recently set out to answer this very question.

We challenged three American households with a simple question: Can you make the money you’re already spending work harder for you?

The answer was a resounding yes.

Mollie and Kevin were up first. The pair recently moved in together ahead of their wedding, and they’re working through how to combine their finances while also saving for their dream honeymoon to Thailand and Vietnam. Both had travel rewards cards before the challenge, but they weren’t coordinating their purchases.

Plus, they were still using debit cards for some everyday expenses.

In short, they had room for improvement in how they made the most of the cards in their wallets.

They started by sitting down to map out their spending habits, which uncovered three primary categories being spread across multiple cards, without any rhyme or reason:.

Plus, they had some major wedding expenses coming up, which gave them a great opportunity to snag a fresh card with a lucrative welcome bonus.

By coordinating their household spending, closing duplicate cards and opening a newly introduced one for the welcome offer, they’re now earning more from the same purchases they were already making.

The remaining balance of the catering bill for their wedding alone hit the minimum spending required to earn the welcome offer, and when all was said and done, the couple took home 120,000 points — worth nearly $2,500 based on TPG’s most recent valuations.

In a recent development, next stop for Mollie and Kevin? Southeast Asia.

Next, we meet Amy, a single mom of two daughters in Maryland. She’s been earning rewards on her favorite hotel cobranded card for years, using it for basically every purchase she could — from utilities to groceries to clothing. She’s racked up nearly 400,000 points in the process, which have come in handy as her daughters have begun traveling for dance competitions.

However, she was concerned her hotel loyalty meant she was sacrificing some value. Could she shift some of her spending to a latest card to diversify her rewards?

With back-to-school shopping and dance classes making up the bulk of her spending in the next month, she opted to add a latest card to her wallet that offered solid rewards on all purchases — along with some easy-to-use statement credits.

And a large home repair bill accelerated her progress toward earning the welcome bonus — a great way to make the best of an unexpected expense.

Recent findings suggest that she’s still keeping her old hotel card open, since it features a low annual fee, and she’ll still want to earn (and redeem) those points when it works. However, the rewards on her new card give her the newfound flexibility she didn’t have originally.

“I really had tunnel vision with the hotel points,” she admitted. “I was leaving money on the table.".

Our final household focuses on Denver, a travel enthusiast who was thrown a pair of major life curveballs last year when her grandfather passed away, and her mom lost her job (and thus her source of income). She moved back home to help, and travel (understandably) shifted to the back burner.

In a recent development, while Denver charged many purchases to her favorite travel rewards card, she had other purchases pulled straight from her bank account. She was also worried that putting everything on her credit card would encourage her to spend more. However, there was a key opportunity in her newly introduced role as a caretaker: focusing on everyday spending rather than travel rewards.

Denver opted for a upcoming cash-back credit card with bonuses at grocery stores, on streaming services and at gas stations. It even offered a one-time $300 cash-back bonus. These rewards can immediately help offset her household bills, like medication costs, be saved for an unexpected expense, or even go toward a future getaway with her family.

She’s continuing to pay her statement balance in full and on time, so it’s not about spending more; it’s about smarter spending based on her current situation.

After just a month, Denver is tracking toward nearly $1,500 in cash-back rewards in the first year — just by being more intentional with her everyday spending.

One of the most common questions we get here at TPG is, “What’s the best credit card for me?” And there’s a simple answer: “It depends.”.

Sources indicate that everyone’s situation is unique, so there’s no one-size-fits-all approach to maximizing your everyday spending. And these three households demonstrate that exact phenomenon.

One went with a upcoming travel card to maximize their wedding purchases. Another opted for a flexible rewards card to diversify her earnings. And the third focused on a cash-back card to support her family’s bottom line.

All of them are now getting even more value from the purchases they were already making.

This is the power of matching the cards in your wallet to your daily spending — while keeping a keen eye on earning the rewards that work best for you.

Don’t miss out on the chance to make your everyday purchases work harder for you. Analyze where you’re spending your money today, so you can unlock even more value tomorrow.
