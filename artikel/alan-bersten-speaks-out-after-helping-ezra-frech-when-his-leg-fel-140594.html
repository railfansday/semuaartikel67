Alan Bersten Speaks Out After Helping Ezra Frech When His Leg Fell Off

🌐 Source: Eonline • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 5 min read (1042 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • The Dancing With the Stars pro proved he was Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach’s secret weapon during the Nov.
  • Following Frech and Karagach’s win, Bersten shared how honored he was to have taken part in the dance alongside the duo.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Alan Bersten deserves the Mirrorball for best backup dancer.

The Dancing With the Stars pro proved he was Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach’s secret weapon during the Nov. 24 finale while performing during their freestyle routine to Linkin’ Park’s “Numb.” When the Paralympian’s prosthetic leg accidentally fell off mid-show, Bersten quickly grabbed the device and held it for safekeeping until Karagach could give it to Frech to reattach during the song. Plus, Bersten was locked into the choreography and didn’t even miss his cue to tear off Frech’s rip-away clothing.

Following Frech and Karagach’s win, Bersten shared how honored he was to have taken part in the dance alongside the duo.

According to recent reports, “I just want to take a second and congratulate Ezra and Daniella,” he said in a Nov. 25 TikTok. “Ezra, your resilience and your composure and your courage is so inspiring.”.

The 32-year-old—who was eliminated during week two alongside pro partner Giada De Laurentiis—added that the moment Frech reattached his leg mid-dance was one that “transcends Dancing with the Stars.”.

“It's a moment that will live on forever,” he mentioned. “And I hope you are so proud of yourself, Daniella. You've created something so magical. Congratulations, you guys.”.

Karagach thanked her fellow pro for not only grabbing the prosthetic when it fell, but also for clearing away Frech’s tearaway suit from the stage so it wouldn’t get in their way.

“The way @alanbersten slid me that leg and made sure Ezra's rip away was cleared was true professionalism at its finest,” the 33-year-old posted to her Instagram Stories Nov. 24. “Grateful you had our backs. Can't thank you enough.”.

The moment was also one that Frech himself would never forget as he recounted what was going through his mind the moment his prosthetic flew off.

“The leg just wasn't on correctly, and I felt it,” the 21-year-old told Variety following the episode. “Sometimes it'll readjust and I'll be okay. And so, as I was running up the steps, I'm like, ‘There's a 50 percent chance my leg will resuction and I'll be OK.’ And that wasn't the case.”.

And while he was tempted to do the entire routine on one leg, he recalled hearing Karagach say, “‘Put your leg on,’ and I was like, ‘Yes, OK, yeah, good idea. I'll put it on right now.’”.

Frech was able to reattach the limb before the duo gracefully jumped back into their routine, with Karagach explaining she promptly realized there was no other place in the dance that he could have taken a moment to reattach it.

According to recent reports, “Nothing's better than him just clicking on his leg properly and continuing on with the dance,” she noted. “I was like, ‘This is our only shot to do that.’”.

Still, with all the adrenaline coursing through him, Frech actually had trouble remembering how exactly he got his prosthetic back.

Industry observers note that “Did somebody give me the leg? Because I blacked out,” he asked aloud, to which Karagach confirmed she gave it to him. “You gave me the leg? Really?”.

And as expected, the routine left its mark on the series, with judge Derek Hough telling the dancing duo, “This performance will have a ripple effect far beyond this show. What you did tonight was forever. Kids will look up to you and see what's possible.".
