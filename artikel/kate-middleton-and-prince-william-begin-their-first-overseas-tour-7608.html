In a recent development, kate Middleton and Prince William have arrived in India for a three-day official visit, their first joint overseas tour since 2022. Announced by Kensington Palace over the weekend, the November 16–18 trip is in honor of the sixth annual Earthshot Prize awards in Mumbai. It also marks a significant step in the Princess of Wales’s return to public duties following her 2024 cancer diagnosis.

The couple will attend the awards ceremony on Tuesday, November 17, when five environmental initiatives will each receive £1 million to expand their work. Founded by Prince William in 2020, the prize recognizes solutions across five areas, including restoring nature, reducing waste, and addressing climate change. Mumbai is the awards’ first Indian host city, following past editions in London, Boston, Singapore, Cape Town, and Rio de Janeiro.

According to recent reports, the royal itinerary extends beyond the ceremony. Kensington Palace said the visit would highlight sustainable urban transportation, responsible manufacturing, youth-led environmental projects, and India’s creative industries. Today, the couple joined cricket great Sachin Tendulkar and local children for a game of street cricket in Ballard Estate, a historic neighborhood in South Mumbai. The Prince and Princess of Wales are also scheduled to attend the inaugural Children’s Earthshot Awards, recognizing environmental solutions developed by young people ages 9 to 17.

The trip is Catherine’s first overseas tour with William since she disclosed her cancer diagnosis in 2024, and her first appearance at an Earthshot ceremony since the Boston awards in 2022. It is also the couple’s second official visit to India, a decade after their April 2016 tour.
