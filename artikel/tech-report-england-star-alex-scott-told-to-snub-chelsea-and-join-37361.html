Bournemouth and England star Alex Scott should set his sights ‘higher’ than Chelsea and aim to join ‘one of the biggest teams in Europe’ like Arsenal.

That’s according to Chris Waddle, who claims Scott has taken his game ‘to a new level’ over the last 18 months and is looking more and more like the ‘complete’ central midfielder.

Waddle’s comments come amid mounting speculation over Scott’s future, with Bournemouth bracing themselves for incoming offers to steal their prized possession away in the January transfer window.

Scott’s eye-catching breakthrough in the international arena has done little to quieten rumours that the 23-year-old could soon be destined for bigger and better things, with Chelsea, Arsenal, Manchester City and Tottenham all rumoured to be circling.

Though Scott is right now sidelined with a thigh injury, his stock continues to soar – and it’s understood the Cherries could be prepared to do business should they receive a bid in excess of £80m this winter.

In an interview with iGaming.com, ex-England international Waddle revealed how he had been keeping close tabs on Scott’s progress since he was first alerted to his potential at Bristol City.

Industry observers note that ‘I’ve always liked Alex Scott. I think he’s a lovely, cultured player. There’s no doubt in my mind that in the last eighteen months, the kid’s taken his game to a new level,’ Waddle explained.

‘I followed him at Bristol City, and when he was there, I was speaking to someone that knew him who told me he was a Tottenham fan.

‘Tottenham have been collecting players in the last six months, but he is a top-class player and someone that would walk into any squad.

Chelsea are reportedly leading the hotly contested race to sign Scott and are said to be increasingly confident of reaching an agreement with Bournemouth for the central midfielder.

However, Waddle insists Scott should ideally be aiming higher than a move to Stamford Bridge, name-checking Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Paris Saint-Germain and Arsenal as potential options.

‘According to reports, Chelsea retain the strongest interest in him. No disrespect to them, but I think he can look higher. I think he’s got the ability to play for the biggest teams in Europe: Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, PSG, Arsenal,’ he added.

‘The guy looks like he’s a top player to me. He looks like he’s only going to get better. If I was a manager or a sporting director and I needed a midfielder, I would be looking at him.

In a recent development, ‘He’s a complete midfielder. He can play as a 10, an 8, and a 6. He offers you a bit of everything. He reminds me a little bit of Rodri in the way that he likes to take the ball off the centre-back and start making things happen.

‘His composure and technique make him a standout player for any team looking to sign someone who can run a midfield and control the tempo.’.

While Bournemouth are in no hurry to part with Scott, the club’s hierarchy recognise that the England international may have ambitions to move on elsewhere near future.

For now, though, the Cherries are adamant that the Vitality Stadium remains the ‘right place’ for Scott.

‘At this point, we’re convinced that we’re the right place for Alex to be, for Alex to stay, and we’ll keep on addressing contract challenges with Alex as we move through this year,’ Bournemouth owner Bill Foley told talkSPORT last month.

‘We’d love to have Alex stay here, sign a new contract and be part of this team for the long haul, and we’re still working on it. We haven’t given up.

‘But sometimes players get stars in their eyes and they think that Chelsea is the right place to go or Manchester City.’.

Scott’s current contract on the south coast runs through to June 2028.
