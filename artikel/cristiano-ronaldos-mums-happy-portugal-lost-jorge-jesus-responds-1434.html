Cristiano Ronaldo's Mum's 'Happy' Portugal Lost - Jorge Jesus Responds

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, for a while, it looked like the day might never come, but Cristiano Ronaldo finally decided to retire from international football in November 2026. He left as a Portugal legend, winning Euro 2016 and scoring more goals for his country (146) than any other player.

Recent findings suggest that however, his departure was no smooth thing. Indeed, the 41-year-old fell out with manager Jorge Jesus in September, prompting him to walk out of Portugal’s training camp. He was subsequently handed a 30-day suspension, plus a €255 fine, by the Portuguese Football Federation, before ultimately deciding to call it quits on international football.

Industry observers note that in Portugal's first game without Ronaldo, they were thumped 4-1 by Denmark. This sparked some rather interesting questions from the icon's mum, and Jesus has now responded to them.

Sources indicate that ronaldo's mother, Dolores Aveiro, is clearly still angry about the way Ronaldo's international retirement came about. Reacting to the Denmark loss, she told the media:.

“The defeat was well deserved, and I was happy about it.”.

It has been revealed that she also criticised the tribute to her son. Portugal's players walked onto the pitch wearing his number seven shirt, while fans applauded in the seventh minute, but Dolores Aveiro claimed:.

“The tribute should have been paid in his presence. It’s here, at Sporting, that we’re going to pay tribute to him. Here, they recognise his worth.”.

Finally, she remarked: “With the national team, he was not respected, not even by his teammates.”.

Recent findings suggest that it'll come as no surprise that the post-defeat focus has been on Ronaldo. When asked about the player directly after the Denmark game, Jesus was asked if he missed the player. He replied: “Missing what? He’s no longer on the national team, so what’s missing? The only thing missing is his presence here to keep me manufacturer.”.

According to recent reports, now, ahead of their game against Wales on Tuesday night, the Portugal boss was asked what he thought about Ronaldo's mother's comments. He fired back:.

“Now I have to respond to Cristiano Ronaldo’s mother?”.

He added: "Cristiano Ronaldo’s mother is a lady I respect greatly. A mother is still a mother, there’s nothing you can do about it. I understand her statements.”.

Despite the constant focus on the retired star, Jesus noted he was happy to keep discussing the matter long into the future, concluding: “I don’t know when it’s going to stop. If you guys (reporters) ask the questions, I have to answer. If you ask about Ronaldo next year, I will answer.”.
