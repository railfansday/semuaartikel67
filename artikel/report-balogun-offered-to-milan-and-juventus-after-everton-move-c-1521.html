AS Monaco and USMNT striker Folarin Balogun has reportedly been offered to both Milan and Juventus for January, after explaining why he torpedoed his transfer to Everton at the last minute.

The 25-year-old had undergone the medical tests with the Premier League side on deadline day, and the move was all set at €50m including add-ons when suddenly he decided not to put pen to paper.

Sources indicate that “My position was very clear. I haven’t remarked anything publicly, but I flew to Everton, obviously, with a set contract,” explained Balogun while on international duty with the USMNT.

It has been revealed that “And then by the time I left, the contract had been changed. So, that was the situation, in short. The contract was changed, and in the end, I didn’t feel comfortable to sign.”.

According to Calciomercato, his agent Jorge Mendes is still busy trying to find him another club in the January transfer window.

Juventus have already been linked with a potential swoop, but Balogun has also now been proposed to Milan, with Goncalo Ramos struggling to find the net.

Francesco Camarda is expected to go out on loan again in January, so this opens up a slot in the Rossoneri attack.

Balogun’s injury record is a substantial concern, and one of the reasons why Everton reportedly changed the terms of their agreement.

He has scored one goal in seven competitive appearances for AS Monaco so far this season.
