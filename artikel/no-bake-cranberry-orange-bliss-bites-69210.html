In a recent development, cozy, bright, and bursting with holiday flavor — these Cranberry Orange Bliss Bites are the perfect little treat when holiday baking season is in full swing and you’re out of time and energy. Inspired by Starbucks’ Bliss Bars, they pack the tart-sweet magic of cranberries and fresh orange zest into tender, no-bake bites you can whip up in minutes!

Recent findings suggest that even better? They’re vegan, gluten-free, and require zero baking — making them a lifesaver during the busy holiday season. A drizzle of white chocolate (or creamy coconut butter) makes them extra festive, but they’re just as delicious on their own. Let’s make bliss bites!

In a recent development, besides tasting absolutely magical, these bliss bites get their name because they’re inspired by the legendary Bliss Bars from Starbucks (IYKYK!). The Starbucks bars have a chewy blondie base with cream cheese frosting, cranberries, white chocolate, and orange zest — total swoon!

The addition of orange juice + zest and dried cranberries kick things up a notch and sends them into Bliss Bar territory!

It has been revealed that the final touch is an optional drizzle of melted white chocolate chips, coconut butter, or our favorite — a mix of the two! While the white chocolate creates a thicker, sweeter, and more decadent frosting, the coconut butter creates a drippier texture and adds a more pronounced coconut flavor that we enjoy. The combination is the best of both worlds!
