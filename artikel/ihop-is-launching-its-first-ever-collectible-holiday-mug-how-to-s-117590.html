📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

IHOP is officially entering the holiday merch wars, but keeping supplies super thin.

It has been revealed that starting Nov. 30, IHOP is launching its inaugural Collector’s Holiday Mug for $15 at participating restaurants nationwide. The catch? Each IHOP location is receiving just 24 mugs for the entire holiday season, making this the first-ever branded merchandise item sold inside IHOP dining rooms.

According to recent reports, at just $15, it undercuts virtually every rival coffeehouse collector mug on the market, including the newly introduced Starbucks FriendsWithYou ceramic mug. And you're not forced to buy a pricey combo meal or download a loyalty app just to unlock access.

The real issue is that 24 mugs per restaurant is virtually nothing.

Once the first 24 mugs at your local IHOP sell out on Nov. 30, they are gone for good. There are no online restocks, no mobile-order reservations, and no second shipments scheduled for December. If you genuinely want one for your shelf or a holiday gift, you will need to visit a participating restaurant in person right when doors open on Nov. 30. The only alternative is to pay obnoxious reseller markups, and nobody wants that.
