📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, every year, the Baby2Baby Gala brings together the biggest names in philanthropy, entertainment, and fashion for an evening of fundraising and celebration. And last night, the 15th edition was no exception—with the likes of Demi Moore, Andrew Garfield, Jessica Alba, Barbara Palvin and Dylan Sprouse, Kerry Washington, and Katy Perry showing up in West Hollywood to support the organization’s mission to help kids in need.

According to recent reports, from the gala’s inception, Baby2Baby co-CEOS Norah Weinstein and Kelly Sawyer Patricof wanted to create an event that felt “less like a traditional fundraiser.” Year after year, the night has offered its star-studded guests more than just an excuse to get dressed up and a chance to make a difference with their wallets—pairing fundraising efforts with exclusive dining experiences from top chefs and intimate performances from award-winning musicians. (Last night, Carbone flew in from New York City and Cardi B took the stage.).

Industry observers note that “We wanted to create an event that would appeal to a younger generation of donors through our approach to food, fashion, and celebrity, while making sure that nobody left the gala without understanding our mission,” Weinstein and Patricof told Harper’s Bazaar. “Fifteen years later, the gala has evolved in ways we never could have imagined, but some themes have stayed exactly the same. Every flower and fork is underwritten by our generous sponsors so that every dollar raised can go directly to Baby2Baby and the children we serve.”.

In a recent development, each year, the funds raised from the gala help get millions of basic essentials—from diapers to formula to clothing—to children who need them. But the organization, with an all-female board of directors, spends all year working towards the Baby2Baby mission. “The gala brings this extraordinary community together for one night, but it’s their commitment throughout the year that makes our work possible,” the CEOs shared.
