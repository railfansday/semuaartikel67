Keeping your car's paint looking its best doesn't have to mean paying for professional detailing. Chemical Guys makes the process more approachable with its TORQX Random Orbital Polisher Kit. The TORQX is our Value Pick for Best Buffer of the year and one of our favorite products. Right now, the nine-piece set is 25 percent off for Black Friday.

Recent findings suggest that designed for beginners and experienced detailers alike, the TORQX uses a dual-action polishing motion to help remove light scratches, swirl marks, oxidation, and water spots while restoring shine.
