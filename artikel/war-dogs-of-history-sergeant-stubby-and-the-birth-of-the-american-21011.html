Stubby served nearly a quarter century before the United States Army established a formal War Dog Program in 1942. He had no training pipeline, no breeding program, and no structure to recognize what he did. He was, in the truest sense, an amateur who became indispensable through instinct, loyalty, and repeated exposure to danger alongside the men who fed and sheltered him.
