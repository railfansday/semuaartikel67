Grocery Delivery Costs More Than the Delivery Fee – Check the Pickup Price First

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A grocery delivery order can carry a service fee, a tip, and marked-up prices besides its delivery fee. Before you pay for delivery, price the same cart for curbside pickup in your store’s app, and buy a delivery membership only if you’d order often enough for it to save more than it costs.

Recent findings suggest that say a cart costs $100 in the store and $110 on a delivery app, where the store marks its prices up 10%. With a $3.99 delivery fee, a 5% service fee, and a $10 tip, the app order comes to about $129 before tax.

It has been revealed that the $3.99 delivery fee is the smallest of those four charges, since the markups add $10, the service fee $5.50, and the tip $10. On Instacart, each store sets its own prices, and some stores add a markup to help pay for delivery. The service fee varies with the store and the size of the cart.

Each store’s page on Instacart states, under its logo, whether its prices match what it charges in the store. Check that line before you fill the cart.

Curbside pickup through your store’s own app has no delivery fee and no driver to tip. At Walmart, pickup is free on orders over $35, and delivering the same order costs up to $9.95 without a membership.

Delivery can still come out ahead. It saves the drive and the time in the store, and ordering from a list can cut impulse buys. Pay for it when that time is worth more to you than the extra charges. It makes the least sense on a small order, where the delivery fee takes a bigger share of the bill.

A membership pays off only for frequent orders. Divide its yearly price by the delivery fee it saves to find how many orders it takes to pay for itself. Walmart+ costs $98 a year and waives that fee on orders over $35, so at $9.95 a delivery, 10 orders cover it. Amazon Prime members already get free same-day grocery delivery in most areas on any order above $25.

Build your next cart in your store’s app, and check its pickup total against the delivery total before you pay.
