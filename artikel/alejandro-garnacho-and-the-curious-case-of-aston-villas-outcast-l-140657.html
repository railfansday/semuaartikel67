Unai Emery wanted the winger but, like Harvey Elliott last season, he has been left out in the cold despite Villa’s attacking struggles.

Sources indicate that it is three years this week since Alejandro Garnacho opened the scoring for Manchester United in a 3-3 draw at Galatasaray in the Champions League, on a typically boisterous night in Istanbul when Victor Lindelöf and Aaron Wan-Bissaka, now his teammates at Aston Villa, were also among the starters. At the time, Garnacho was a teenage star flourishing on the grandest of stages, a fixture in the United team and fresh from scoring an outrageous overhead kick at Goodison Park a few days earlier that would later clinch the Premier League’s goal-of-the-season award.

It has been revealed that he went on to score twice at Old Trafford on Boxing Day, in a 3-2 victory against Villa, and a couple of months later his double at home to West Ham was the catalyst for that iconic celebration photo of Garnacho, Kobbie Mainoo and Rasmus Højlund sitting on the advertising hoardings at the Stretford End, beaming smiles all round. All three were at the heart of United’s prepares for a bright future, beacons of hope. At the end of that season, Garnacho signed off in style, he and Mainoo scoring against Manchester City at Wembley to win the FA Cup.
