Anne Hathaway has added a little princess to her life.

One day after multiple outlets confirmed the Princess Diaries actress had given birth to her and husband Adam Shulman’s third baby, The Devil Wears Prada 2 costume designer Molly Rogers seemed to reveal the sex of their fresh family member.

Rogers posted the news to her Instagram Stories Nov. 17, sharing a photo of a teddy bear in a colorful quilted shirt and beige hat, writing, “Congrats are in order for proud mama @annehathaway & her new addition to family ....a baby girl!”.

Hathaway first shared the news of her pregnancy in June, one month after The Devil Wears Prada 2 debuted, posting a video of herself to Instagram cradling her baby bump, set to the tune of Barbara Lewis’ “Baby, I’m Yours.”.

And while the 44-year-old kept her bump on display during her odyssey of movie promotions throughout 2026—which included Mother Mary, The Odyssey, The End of Oak Street and Verity—she made sure to keep mum on the sex of the baby.

When a spectator asked if she would be having a “boy or girl” at Disney’s D23 convention in August, Hathaway quipped, "Oh, just be patient.”.

She then added with a laugh, "You're gonna find out in five minutes.".

But she and Shulman—who tied the knot in 2012—did indeed know the sex before their baby girl’s birth, although as Hathaway noted on Jimmy Kimmel Live! during the summer, “We’re not saying.".

According to recent reports, "Thank you for the support," she continued after her response was met with applause. "I always feel so rude saying that. I feel so bad.".

In a recent development, being a girl mom will certainly be a fresh experience for the Idea of You actress, as she and Shulman are already parents to sons Jonathan, 10, and Jack, 6. But as she reflected, raising boys was all about having the same mother’s instinct as she would have with a girl.

"The thing that to remember about boys is they have feelings," she told Access Hollywood in July. "Sometimes, we think about that more in the realm of girls, but it's just as important to give boys that emotional foundation and make sure they feel just as safe as we make our girls feel.".
