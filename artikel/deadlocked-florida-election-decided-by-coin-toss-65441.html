Jennifer Lagner won the election for Atlantic Beach city commissioner in Florida via a coin toss after successive recounts left her tied with her opponent, according to the Fresh York Post.

Lagner and Lisa Herrold each sat at 3,162 votes after Duval County's Supervisor of Elections Jerry Holland initiated both a manual and a machine recount.

The deadlock initiated the unique tiebreaker last week in which each candidate would have the chance to win two of three coin flips.

According to recent reports, under the city's election rules, each candidate had a chance to call the coin's landing. Herrold went first, calling heads, but the coin landed tails, giving Lagner a 1-0 lead.

Lagner went next, accurately predicting heads to win the contest, securing her election victory to become city commissioner.

Industry observers note that supervisor of Elections Jerry Holland holds up his challenge coin to the tail's side, to be used to determine the winner of the best two-out-of-three coin-flip tiebreaker conducted by the Duval County Supervisor of Elections on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, in Jacksonville, Fla., to decide Atlantic Beach Commission Seat 4. Candidates Lisa Herrold and Jennifer Lagner were tied with 3,162 votes each. Herrold went first and guessed heads and the coin landed tails. Lagner guessed heads with the coin landing heads, winning the seat. (USA Today Network via Reuters Connect).

"This demonstrates how great and free our country is," Lagner told a local news outlet.

"I am honored to have the opportunity to serve as your next City Commissioner for Seat 4," she wrote on Facebook. "A coin toss was certainly an unexpected and unforgettable way to bring an election to a close! Thank you to Jerry Holland and his entire team for their work throughout this election process.".
