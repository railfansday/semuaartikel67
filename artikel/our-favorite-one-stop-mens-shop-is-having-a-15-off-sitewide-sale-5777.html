It has been revealed that the holiday season comes in hot as soon as Halloween is over, and I’m all about it. It’s a swift transition into family gatherings, Christmas parties, gift-giving (and receiving!), and cozy vibes. It’s also prime shopping season, as stores roll out the year's biggest sales. One of which is a site-wide sale at one of our favorite retailers—Huckberry. From November 13 to November 15, you get 15% off almost any purchase (exclusions apply to a handful of items).

Sources indicate that huckberry is the online one-stop men's shop that has everything imaginable for the discerning eye: apparel, workout gear, home goods, you name it. In its early years, Huckberry sold only established brands, but has since expanded to stock smaller IYKYK names as well as its own in-house line. We’ve waxed poetic on the Flint and Tinder Trucker Jacket, and I personally bought a bunch of Huckberry-branded shorts before summer hit. All this to say, they not only stock good stuff, but they also know how to make it.

The holidays are a strange time to step out in your nicest duds and then relax at home after copious amounts of food. From button-up shirts to fleece to footwear, Huckberry has the gamut covered. Here’s what I’m shopping for in the sale, but—seriously—take the time to browse the site. No code necessary, and the 15% off price will show up in your cart.
