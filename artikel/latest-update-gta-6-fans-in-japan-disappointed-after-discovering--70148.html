GTA 6 players in Japan have voiced disappointment over the game’s censored content, which tones down nudity and gore compared with the Western release.

Japan is no stranger when it comes to censoring video games, with recent examples like Resident Evil Requiem famously featuring black textures over decapitated body parts and less blood.

However, on November 10, it was revealed that the Japanese version of GTA 6 was 1 GB smaller than its overseas brother. This promptly sparked fears among players that the game would be missing content to make it pass Japan’s ratings board. Well, it turns out that those fears were indeed true.

In a recent development, A screenshot of protagonist Jason taking a shower in the Japanese version of the game has gone viral, and no, it’s not because he’s getting all soapy. Rather, it’s because he’s showering in what can only be described as a tiny black Speedo.

For contrast, the overseas release has Jason showering in his birthday suit, with there being no steam or swimwear to obstruct his bare buttocks from view. As you can imagine, many fans aren’t exactly happy with these watered-down scenes.

“Isn’t it strange that in a country where people go completely naked in public baths and hot springs, they make people wear swimsuits to take a shower?” wrote one Japanese player.

According to recent reports, “If they’re going out of their way to give it a CERO Z rating for 18+, what’s the point of censoring the content? I don’t get it,” replied another commenter.
