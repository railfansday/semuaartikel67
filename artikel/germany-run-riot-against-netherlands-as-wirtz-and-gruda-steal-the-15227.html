In the final match of the Nations League group stage, Germany welcomed the Netherlands on a rather unusual evening, with the hosts playing their final match with the Adidas stripes knowing that only a win would be enough to secure progression.

After scoring on his debut against Serbia last Thursday, Brajan Gruda provided the first moment of excitement in the sixth minute, producing a lovely piece of footwork to earn Germany their first opportunity to deliver the ball into the area.

Derry Scherhant, who impressed last time out on home soil in the last international break, was also looking lovely once again, with the visiting Dutch side offering little in the opening stages, outside of a fluffed chance from Crysencio Summerville, who slipped when looking to get a shot away in the German box.

Kai Havertz's 23rd-minute header would be the first effort on target of the match, while the Netherlands should have taken the lead just two minutes later.

A sweeping attacking move released Liverpool man Cody Gakpo out wide, before Donyell Malen was found inside the box, before the striker failed to readjust himself and direct his header towards goal - a significant let-off for Germany.

The hosts would come closest of either side in the 32nd minute, when Seherhant struck the post after linking up with Freiburg teammate Phillip Treu, before the wideman opened the scoring for Jürgen Klopp's side with four minutes of the half remaining.

Cutting in from the left once again, the Berliner hammered home at his old stomping ground, with Bart Verbruggen's touch not enough to keep the ball out.

The momentum would continue in the early stages of the second half, with widemen Scherhant and Gruda remaining at the heart of proceedings - the latter firing wide with a curling effort from distance.

Xavi's Dutch side would respond with a promising spell, with Denzel Dumfries and Gakpo both threatening, before a moment of individual brilliance from Florian Wirtz doubled Germany's advantage with a wonderful strike.

After exchanging passes with Scherhant on the edge of the penalty area, the Liverpool midfielder initially lost possession when Dumfries intervened. However, Wirtz recovered the ball, shifted it onto his left foot and curled an exquisite effort from just outside the box into the bottom-left corner, with the ball crashing in off the post.

No less than five minutes later, the hosts had effectively put the contest beyond doubt.

Delivering an inviting free-kick into the area, Gruda found Nico Schlotterbeck, who stooped to meet the ball and found the net with a well-placed header, while Germany looked to really hammer the nail into the coffin of a dismal evening for the Netherlands.

Starting a move, Wirtz spread possession out to Gruda on the right before continuing his run into the penalty area and firing a first-time finish under the crossbar following a perfectly weighted ball from Gruda.

And after a sensational evening, Gruda would finally get his goal, as he bundled home into an empty net after Scherhant unleashed an effort against the post on the opposite flank.

For the Netherlands, the evening ended up being one to forget, while what had begun as a potentially nervy evening for Klopp's side turned into a procession, as Germany secured their place in the next round of the Nations League in emphatic fashion, as they look to be fully clicking into gear under Klopp.
