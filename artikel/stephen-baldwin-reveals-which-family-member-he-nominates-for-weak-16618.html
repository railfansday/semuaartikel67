Stephen Baldwin is keeping it in the family by nominating a loved one for the next season of Celebrity Weakest Link.

“[My brother] Alec Baldwin would be great on Celebrity Weakest Link because I don’t know if you know this, he’s really smart,” Stephen, 60, exclusively told Us on the set of the Fox show before appearing on the Monday, November 16, episode.

Stephen praised his sibling’s skills, adding, “He’s a smart guy. And it’s only because he had more food than me growing up that he’s more intelligent.”.

He continued: “I don’t think Alec Baldwin would do better than me on Weakest Link. I’m quite certain Alec Baldwin would do better than me on Weakest Link. … But what we should do is when Alec Baldwin is on Weakest Link, I should cohost then then he might not do as well, which wouldn’t be very nice but it would be very entertaining.”.

According to recent reports, celebrity Weakest Link brings together eight celebrities competing to win up to $1 million for a charity of their choosing. After answering general knowledge questions, the contestants bank money as a group before voting out who they consider to be the weakest link at the end of each round.

Stephen’s episode — titled “’90s Night” — has him competing opposite Mario Cantone, Zac Hanson, Jasmine Guy, Jonathan Lipnicki, Sisqo, Kel Mitchell and Daphne Zuniga.

Recent findings suggest that “I think it’d probably be wiser for the Weakest Link producers to maybe think about bringing me in as a cohost at some point,” Stephen told Us. “That would probably work better.”.

Jane Lynch, for her part, cosigned the idea after returning to host the revamped series.

It has been revealed that “If I could have Stephen Baldwin as my cohost, I would do it. He was on the show. He was so good and so smart and so entertaining,” she told Us. “And it was so great to get to know him because I’ve never met him.”.

In a recent development, lynch continued: “And it was just really great to have him on the show. He got a lot of the questions right and he was just a real doll to have.”.

Stephen had a playful response when asked about watching Lynch, 66, at work.

Recent findings suggest that “I [have been] married 36 years and I don’t care who Jane’s spouse is. Jane Lynch is hot,” he quipped. “She’s hot. She’s tall. She’s rich. She hosts a hot, rich, Weakest Link TV show. She’s in shape and she he doesn’t even work out. That’s how hot Jane Lynch is. So whoever that significant other is, you better stay on your toes because Jane is looking good. That’s all I’m saying.”.

Sources indicate that lynch, who is married to Jennifer Cheyne, told Us she had a similar conversation with Stephen. “He said that to me and I said, ‘Are you yanking my chain?’” she teased. “He thinks I’m pretty — and he looks great too. … He’s a lovely guy. I would love to have him as my cohost.”.

Stephen’s appearance on season 2 of the game show marked his return to Fox after a short stint on Special Forces and allowed him to raise awareness for a good cause.
