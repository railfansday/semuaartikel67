📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that germany and the U.K. have pushed back against U.S. criticism of Ukraine's deep strike campaign inside Russia, underscoring tensions between Washington and European allies over Kyiv's war strategy.

The clash comes ahead of a highly anticipated trilateral meeting on Thursday between U.S. President Donald Trump, Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum in Shenzhen, China.

Geopolitical flashpoints such as trade, artificial intelligence and nuclear weapons are expected to be a major focus of Trump, Putin and Xi's meeting, with external observers closely monitoring any discussion of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine.

Recent findings suggest that speaking at NATO's headquarters in Belgium on Tuesday, U.S. Undersecretary of Defense Elbridge Colby likened Ukraine's deep strikes inside Russia to a spectacle, adding that they "have not been effective in driving an end to the war, nor do they contribute to blunting Russia's advances on the front line.".

The Pentagon policy chief acknowledged Ukraine's mid-range strike campaign using novel drone technologies and tactics to deny Russian advances on the front line has proven "very effective.".

He added that Ukraine's Armed Forces gained more territory than Russia for the second consecutive month in October.

However, Colby noted Ukraine's deep strikes targeting "civilian infrastructure," citing Russia's biggest online retailer Wildberries, "have actually probably hardened the Russian public's resolve and the Kremlin's own willingness to escalate.".

It has been revealed that kyiv has remarked that warehouses targeted by Ukrainian drones were involved in providing Russian forces with drone components, navigation equipment and other military components. Wildberries and the Kremlin have denied the company is involved in Russia's war in Ukraine.

Asked whether he shared Colby's position on Ukraine's deep strike campaign inside Russia, German Defense Minister Boris Pistorius stated criticizing strikes designed to destroy strategic military and military-use targets in Russia "is quite a stupid thing, I think.".

Recent findings suggest that "Instead of criticizing that, we should rather criticize and talk about the terroristic strikes every night and every day on civil energy, water and heating infrastructure in Ukraine. This is kind of terrorism," Pistorius remarked at a news conference.

British Secretary of State for Defence Wes Streeting echoed those comments, saying it is "not for us to tell Ukraine how to fight their war.".

He added: "There is no doubt in my mind that it has helped to degrade Putin's war machine, buying time and saving lives.".

Trump has previously called on Ukraine to halt strikes on Russian oil refineries and infrastructure used to produce and distribute diesel, saying the attacks were responsible for a global shortage.

Ukraine has stepped up its strikes on Russian oil facilities and logistics infrastructure in recent months, seeking to raise the cost of the war for Moscow after more than 4½ years of conflict.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on Tuesday lauded an "unprecedented operation" targeting the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea in what marked a symbolic blow to the Kremlin's war effort.

A coordinated drone and missile strike on the bridge, which provides a road and rail route for Russian forces and is one of Putin's signature projects, damaged the crossing.

In response, Russia stated the bridge would be restored and "will be even better than before.".
