📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that sell on Fiverr if your service can be packaged at a set price, like logo design or voiceovers, because buyers search for it and order without a pitch from you. Fiverr keeps 20% of every order, tips included, so build that cut into each package’s price.

In a recent development, fiverr is a freelance marketplace where you sell your skills as packaged services, called gigs, at prices you set. On a $100 order, you keep $80 after Fiverr’s 20% cut and pay no Fiverr fee to withdraw it to PayPal, so price a package at $125 to net $100.

Freelancers with a service they can package and price up front, such as logo design, editing, or voiceovers, get a storefront that buyers already search, and that’s worth the 20% cut. A consultant whose work needs a custom quote does better on Upwork, where freelancers send proposals to the jobs clients post.
