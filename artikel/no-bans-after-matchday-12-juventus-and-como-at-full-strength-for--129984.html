No bans after matchday 12: Juventus and Como at full strength for the Sinigaglia

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Serie A disciplinary judge suspended no players after matchday 12, so neither Juventus nor Como lose anyone for Sunday's game. Como were fined again for a slow restart, and Juventus head to Lake Como five points behind them.

Juventus travel to Como on Sunday with no suspension worries. Nor do their hosts. Serie A disciplinary judge Gerardo Mastrandrea banned nobody after matchday 12 in a ruling published in Milan on 24 November.

In a recent development, the decisions covered the games of 21, 22 and 23 November. Nobody was sent off across the round. No player one booking from a ban picked up a yellow card either.

In a recent development, juventus had no cards from their own fixture. The club’s match sheet for the 4-0 win over Venezia at the Allianz Stadium lists no bookings, with Arena as referee.

Recent findings suggest that all four goals came after the break. Woltemade (53′), Koopmeiners (59′) and Cabal (65′) scored before Kolo Muani converted a penalty (71′).

Two Lecce players, Danilo Veiga and Jamil Siebert, picked up their fourth booking of the season and are now one caution from a ban, Virgilio Sport reported. No Juventus or Como player reached that threshold this round.

Como did not escape the judge entirely. The €8,000 fine was imposed under strict liability for delaying the start of the second half by about four minutes without good reason. The judge treated it as a repeated repeat offence.

Industry observers note that it was the largest of the round’s penalties for late restarts. Fiorentina and Milan were also fined as repeat offenders. Genoa received €3,000 for a two-minute delay to kick-off.

The problem is not new for Como. In the previous round, the communiqué of 10 November fined them €10,000, again as repeated repeat offenders. On that occasion the second half started about two minutes late.

According to recent reports, juventus were not blameless that week. The same 10 November ruling fined them €5,000 for holding up the start of the first half by the same margin as Como’s latest delay.

It has been revealed that the match carries weight in the race for the top four. Como have 8 wins, 2 draws and 2 defeats from their 12 games. They have scored 20 goals and conceded 10.

Industry observers note that juventus have 6 wins, 3 draws and 3 defeats. Their attack, with 19 goals, has almost kept pace with Como’s. The difference lies at the back, where Juventus have conceded 12.

A win on Lake Como would narrow the gap to their hosts. Defeat would push Juventus further from the top four.

Como host Juventus at the Sinigaglia on 29 November.
