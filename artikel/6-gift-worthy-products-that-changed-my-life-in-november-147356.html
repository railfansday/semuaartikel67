Chances are you are reading this while awash in the joyful glow of an active oven and/or open laptop chock full of Black Friday sales. As someone who can’t help but enjoy America’s annual week of consumption, I have some advice: You can probably skip that fourth slice of pie and also, when it comes to gift-giving, bigger is not always better. Personally, some of the best presents I’ve received over the years are small but meaningful items that come with a sense of personal connection and discovery: A set of irresistible Japanese incense from an aspirational boutique my fiancé found in Tokyo’s Aoyama district. Some adorable silk painted ornaments that my mom stumbled upon at a local maker’s market. Employee merch from NASA, where my stepfather worked. All that to say, the ritual of gift-giving is always made to be much more meaningful when we can collect all the little, delightful items we’ve collected over the year and share them with those whom will appreciate them most. Maybe that’s a simple, but utterly practical thingamajig that has made your life that much more convenient. Or something you came across in your travels. Even an item you have at home for years that you couldn’t imagine living without. Here are a few self-care accessories that I've encountered both recently and not-so-recently that fit that bill.

Ever since the days of chewable Flintstones vitamins, we as a human race have been striving for new, inventive ways to get our daily dose in. Straight pills are boring and require water. Powders are messy and not easily transported. Gummies can be overly sugary and harden over time. But strips are nearly weightless, easy to travel with, and consumable on their own. The ones made by StripIt dissolve on your tongue, a little like a Listerine mint strip, and come in four different formulas: Vitamin C, D3 + K2, B12, Sleep, and Digest. Though they can take a little bit longer than a mint strip to dissolve, they’re still a really convenient way to make sure you’re keeping up with your supplements. I’ve been using the D3 + K2 ones while I travel. They taste pretty good and are colorful and novel enough to function as a small stocking stuffer or Hanukkah gift for the health hawk in your life.

Industry observers note that I will never tire of a massive, beautiful block of soap. Especially if it's so fragrant that it fills a whole room with its smell. My upcoming favorite in this category is Life Research Lab, an herb-forward brand that takes inspiration from (and incorporates) the local LA flora into its soap blends. There’s the eucalyptus and grapefruit pumice slab that draws from common neighborhood trees, a classic “cowboy cologne” mix of cypress and California sagebrush so commonly found in Angelenos’ gardens, and a juniper bergamot number that lifts scents from the desert. Many of them contain bits and pieces of the very plants they’re named for, which function as light exfoliants as you scrub. I was especially charmed by the Christmas Morning Soap, which is infused with balsam fir essential oil and pinyon pine leaf. Its name, price, and the fact that it comes in both 9-ounce blocks and 4-ounce cylinders make it a perfect stocking stuffer. T’is the season.

When I traveled to Seoul a few years ago, I popped into the Hinok store and spent about 20 minutes smelling basically every one of its products, which all contain ingredients sourced by foraging detritus from old cypress forests on Jeju island (not cutting them down). Alas, I couldn’t bring anything home because my carry-on was already stuffed. But recently I learned the brand is available to buy in the US and had a chance to try a few things out. My favorite is the thick, gooey body wash. It has the consistency of honey, and emanates a light, fresh natural scent without being too overpowering. Most crucially, the formula is gentle enough to clean your body without stripping it of its natural oils. After searching for a light body wash I can use daily for a while now, this qucikly became my fresh favorite. (The deodorising spray is really good for households with pets too.).

In a recent development, when I was in New York last month, my future sister in-law suggested we take a quick field trip to Senti Senti, a little skincare store in Chinatown. To my delight, they carried imported Korean and Japanese formulas of lotions, cleansers, and—most importantly—sunscreen from popular brands like Beauty of Joseon and Bioré. (Don’t ask me how this is allowed, Chinatown’s gonna Chinatown.) In the 15 minutes we spent there, we observed at least three people walk in and beeline for this Dr. Althea face lotion and I couldn’t help but be tempted. Using it for the past month, I’ve been impressed with how light, hydrating, and easily absorbing it is—an excellent goop to apply in the morning. For anyone who hates the lingering feeling of a heavy day cream, this one’s a no-brainer.

Recent findings suggest that we’re days away from the start of winter, meaning we’ll all be heating up our homes and braving, windy, sub-zero temperatures more regularly for the next few months. All of that means our skin has a far higher chance of getting dry and flaky. Enter Bein All-Over Body Oil. The blend of organic sesame seed, squalane, and camellia oil rubs on smoothly and absorbs like a dream, leaving no sticky residue behind. Body oil can be especially helpful for feet, knees, elbows, and hands, post-shower. And is light enough that you can apply lotion on afterward, too. Best of all, the San Diego-brand’s products smell faintly of bitter citrus, like a little Aperol spritz for your skin. It’s bright-red packaging makes it feel especially festive for the holidays.

Recent findings suggest that A good robe, in my humble opinion, must rise to a few crucial tasks. It should be able to absorb water well, dry out promptly, and let your skin breathe after a long warm soak in the bath or shower. It should also allow you to lounge around your household without flashing your living companions or getting furniture wet. That’s why I’m partial to a long linen kimono, which checks all of those boxes. I was gifted a sophisticated blue-striped one from Deiji Studios nearly six years ago for Christmas, and it’s now a perfectly worn-in, indispensable bathroom staple. No wonder Deiji still sells it. Even better, it’s on sale for Black Friday.
