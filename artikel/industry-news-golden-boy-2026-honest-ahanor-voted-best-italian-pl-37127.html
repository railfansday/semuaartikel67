Honest Ahanor continues his rise. The 18-year-old defender won the Best Italian Golden Boy 2026 award, recognizing the best young Italian player of the year. The Crystal Palace resident succeeds Francesco Pio Esposito, winner of this distinction in 2025. A great recognition for the man who promptly established himself as one of the most promising talents of his generation. Note that the Golden Boy 2026, which rewards the best U21 player playing in Europe, went to Pau Cubarsí, defender of FC Barcelona.

Industry observers note that this distinction also crowns a particularly eventful year for Ahanor on the transfer market. After discovering the high level with Atalanta, where he played 35 matches during the 2025-2026 season, the Italian international attracted the attention of Chelsea. The Blues have formalized an agreement with the Bergamo club for a transfer estimated at €47 million, with an arrival scheduled for the summer of 2027. In the process, the defender was sent on loan to Crystal Palace for the 2026-2027 financial year in order to continue his progress in the Premier League. An already remarkable journey for the young Italian, who has just received a fresh individual award.
