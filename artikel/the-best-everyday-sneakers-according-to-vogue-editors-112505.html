The Best Everyday Sneakers, According to Vogue Editors

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that whether you’re walking down Broadway to run weekend errands, hiking through the Adirondacks, or logging miles in preparation for a marathon, sneakers are the backbone of a modern wardrobe. Designed to keep up with long days, busy schedules, and whatever the calendar throws your way, they’ve earned their place as true essentials—and our editors have strong opinions on which styles make the cut.

For some on the team, comfort and versatility matter most. For others, it’s about injecting color and personality into pared-back, off-duty looks. Sneakers that offer a sense of play—like Onitsuka Tiger’s Mexico 66 in silver or one of the ongoing Wales Bonner x Adidas designs—are among the favorites. After all, if the shoe doesn’t gel with the wearer’s personal style, it won’t stay in rotation for long. Our editors’ picks have earned their keep: They can go the distance.

I love these Kallmeyer Nike sneakers as a fancy weekend shoe and these brown Asics for my fall palette. For exercising, I have been running in Brooks Ghost sneakers for 20 years, and I’m not planning to stop anytime soon. I wear Alo Runners for other cardio.

When Phoebe Philo designed her take on an elevated Vans sneaker, I sourced the originals and have kept them in my sneaker rotation ever since. Paired with tailored wide-leg pants, they’re the perfect casual-cool shoes. Jamie Haller and Toteme are other favorites; I love minimalist sneakers from brands I regularly shop.

A Stan Smith is the definition of timeless style. Some might argue it’s no longer as relevant, since the Tokyo and Samba have overtaken it as Adidas’s bestselling styles, but I still reach for my Stan Smiths when I need a classic, chic, subtle sneaker for work. Forever engraved in my brain is the image of Phoebe Philo bowing at one of her Celine indicates in a gray sweater, gray pants, and Stan Smiths—chic.

Recent findings suggest that my favorite sneaker has to be the Adidas Taekwondo. The laceless design eliminates all unnecessary fuss, while the flat sole keeps the silhouette clean and streamlined. Lightweight and easy to pack, they have earned a permanent spot in my closet and carry-on!

I never felt that sneakers and real clothes looked great on me, so I’ve long been a fan of the understated, retro look that is so popular now. Slim in silhouette, they wear more like ballet flats than sneakers. My go-to white sneakers are a Marni pair I found on The RealReal ages ago. I was happy to see this latest iteration from the brand pop up at the end of last year. My other favorite pair is my original Loewe ballet runners; Vogue fashion editor Max Ortega inspired the purchase. They don’t produce the exact style anymore, but here they are in 2.0!

Industry observers note that I rarely feel moved by sneakers unless they’re of the running kind, but every so often I’ll reach for this all-white pair from Dries to wear on the weekend. I also have these Nike x Jacquemus Moon sneakers that, for lack of more weather-resistant options, have somehow become my rainy-day shoe of choice.

I’m really not the biggest sneaker wearer, but I’m a fan of Michael Rider’s Jazz sneaker for Celine. I know they’re somewhat controversial, but as someone who danced jazz in my youth, these shoes hold a lot of nostalgia for me. Otherwise, I love a sneaker that feels a bit more fancy, like the Nike x Jacquemus Moon satin trainers.

For the weekend, the Nike Air Max 90 Futura remains my go-to. The colorway I own (white, gray, and black) is sold out, but it’s still available in a variety of options.

I love an all-white sneaker for ease and versatility, and my go-to is the Tory Burch Howell Court sneaker. Its minimalist design takes the guesswork out of getting dressed, and it’s elevated enough for the office.

According to recent reports, I am not a major sneaker person, but I have developed an obsession with the ongoing Wales Bonner x Adidas sneaker collabs. They suit my style—slightly 1970s—while offering the comfort of a sneaker. Win-win!

It has been revealed that the evolution of my footwear needs to be studied, because I swore I was a heels girl, but then I found myself only wearing flats, and now I have a newfound interest in sneakers. Maybe it’s because I’m a runner and am always on the hunt for the best pair of running shoes, but I take my sneaker search seriously. Running shoes aside, the sneakers I wear for my long walks are equally important, and this pair of Nike Air Max Moto 2K sneakers has been a staple in my shoe rotation—especially as the weather gets colder. They’re pretty versatile, and I’ve even worn them to the office!

According to recent reports, my favorite sneaker has to be the Prada America’s Cup Original sneaker. Originally conceived in 1997 for the Luna Rossa sailing team, it refines the familiar classic with a rugged, functional design that stays true to its original proportions. I own this style in multiple colors, as well as vintage and reissued versions. It is the perfect sneaker to elevate a casual outfit and has become a symbol of the intersection of luxury and streetwear.

Sources indicate that I have a tough time with sneakers! Every time I try to make a fashion sneaker work, I end up not feeling like myself. Maybe one day I’ll find a more dressed-up sneaker I love, but for now my go-to is a more technical option: the Salomon XT-Whisper sneakers in all black. I wear them at the gym, for short hikes, or on the weekends while running errands around the city.

I’m both a longtime Converse fan and Vogue’s resident tennis nerd—well, our other tennis nerd—so you can imagine my delight when Palmes, the tennis-forward Copenhagen label run by my genius friend Nikolaj Hansson, collaborated with Converse on a killer version of the original court shoe, the Jack Purcell. If you can still find those, jump on them—they’re scarce. If you can’t—or if your tastes run a bit more uptown compared to the resolutely downtown feel of the Palmes collab—the more recent Converse First String Jack Purcells are essentially the most haute version of the Jacks that one could imagine. Both are the sturdiest, chunkiest, coziest sneakers I’ve found in years.

It has been revealed that I’m completely obsessed with my Adidas Japans (so obsessed that I have them in two colorways), and have gotten my entire family on board. They’re sleek, soft, and so comfortable—no break-in required. If you’re into the low-profile look but aren’t keen on a wacky sneakerina, this is the shoe for you.

It has been revealed that living in Brooklyn, I was skeptical about hopping onto the Salomon wave taking over the street style scene, but in the two years since I bought a pair, they’ve become my go-to sneakers—unseating my former true love, my Upcoming Balance 990v5s. They’re lightweight, comfortable enough to wear all day, and you don’t even have to tie the laces. Swoon!

The higher the platform, the more likely I am to make it mine. Of course, my first foray into Rick Owens footwear had to be one of the more dramatic offerings, not a standard-height sole. Initially testing them out on the ground at Vogue World: Milano, I found the shoe’s roomy toe box and comfortable leather insole carried me through the night on the cobblestone streets of Milano and all the way back to the United States—on the plane and in the airport.

Sources indicate that I wear Vans checkerboard slip-ons pretty much every day! They are the perfect sneaker; truly, they go with everything. They are so classic that they are neither trendy nor not cool. I’m from LA, and wearing them always makes me feel connected to Southern California. I love them!

Living in Latest York calls for a comfortable shoe, and personally I’m a huge fan of a good sneaker. I wear these Asics sneakers everywhere—to the gym, around town, and even to the office, depending on the look.

I am not a sneaker person, but my Latest Balance 574s have been in my wardrobe for years and are still my go-to travel shoes.

A sneaker, in my opinion, should keep a low profile. I’ve tried chunkier, sportier styles but found my footing with the Jacquemus The Tennis sneaker. The chocolate brown suede is impossible to resist.

In a recent development, few things excite me more than an unexpected fashion collaboration, and Hoka x Marni is a pairing I can wholeheartedly get behind. Where high performance meets high fashion, this duo strikes the perfect balance between function and flair. Hoka’s signature comfort meets Marni’s playful, offbeat design sensibility, transforming a performance sneaker into a bona fide fashion statement.

Back in Vogue’s earlier days, the idea that a sneaker could elevate any look would never have made it past the edit—yet here we are, celebrating the classic silhouette that can seamlessly carry you from desk to dinner. Recent runways inspired a desire for Vuitton’s Sneakerina!

Recent findings suggest that I am a huge sneakerhead. I have every Wales Bonner x Adidas collaboration sneaker because they’re the best shoes ever. They’re not only super comfortable, but they also come in great colorways and textiles. My favorite pair is a sequin-and-croc-leather style that I got two years ago. Tory Burch also makes a great pair of sneakers that feel comfy and look great. My favorite colorway has been the brown suede. The only downside is that I wear them so much, the flat soles are already wearing thin!

Industry observers note that the low-profile sneaker isn’t going anywhere, and I particularly love this sleek, Mary Jane–inspired silhouette from Zendaya’s On collab. I’m a longtime On fan for Barry’s workouts (Cloudpulse Pro to the end!) and was glad to add this put-together pair to my stomping-around-the-city rotation for when I’m not on the treadmill. They also fared pretty well on a recent trip I took to the Australian outback. Who notes you can’t traverse 500-million-year-old rock formations in your balletcore best?

While you might have spied Lili Curia’s ornamental, Victorian-style Isadora boots on the feet of all the pop stars this past summer, their sneaker version is the girl you need to know: all the same chic and sophisticated character, translated into a sportier shape. I love the contrasting black leather and brown suede upper, which makes it super versatile.

Industry observers note that it’s only recently come to my attention that I gravitate toward sneakers that look like little soccer cleats (pardon me, football boots!). For starters, nothing beats the arch support of an Onitsuka Tiger, even though they look anything but orthopedic. I completely understand why Uma Thurman sported these in Kill Bill.
