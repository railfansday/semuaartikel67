To help you find the best savings, I'm sharing all the best Nintendo Switch Black Friday deals right as they appear in this live blog. Not only am I a Deals Editor at Tom's Guide with five years of experience tracking price drops, I'm also a Nintendo fan through and through (I've played my Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 consoles for well over 1,000 hours in total.).

Welcome! With Black Friday two weeks and plenty of other sales happening online, it's going to be a big week for savings. Whether you already have a Nintendo Switch 2 or you're picking one up for a loved one for the holidays, we'll be highlighting the best, editor-approved deals on games, controllers and accessories to help you save.

Recent findings suggest that one of the most under looked Nintendo Switch 2 accessories, the official Nintendo Switch 2 Camera is on sale for $43 at Amazon. Now you don't need the Nintendo Switch 2 Camera to use GameChat since the Switch 2 has a built-in microphone. However, adding a camera lets you put your face in games like Mario Kart World.

Recent findings suggest that there are also a ton of games that use the camera for actual gameplay. For instance, there are mini games in Super Mario Party Jamboree that use the camera and in Fitness Boxing 3, you can track and display the movement of your punches on screen.

If you're looking for a long JRPG you can really sink your teeth into this winter, look no further than Persona 3: Reload. Right now, you can pick up Persona 3: Reload for just $25 on Amazon which is more than half off its normal price.Whether you enjoyed Persona 5 or just want a nice JRPG you can play for hours, Persona 3: Reload is a fantastic option. In my Persona 3: Reload review, I praised the game's quality of life improvements, latest animations, vibrant visuals and fresh voice cast.

If you're a fan of the series that hasn't played the game responsible for the modern Persona formula yet, you owe it to yourself to give this one a shot, especially since you can now play in handheld mode.

Besides a screen protector, the next accessory you should definitely pick up for your fresh Nintendo Switch 2 is a carrying case. If you want full protection without the bulk, then the Tomtoc Slim Carrying Case is $29 at Amazon right now.

Sources indicate that available in 11 different colorways, this slim case also has a carrying handle. Then on the inside, there are 12 game card slots to store your favorite titles.

Nintendo's Joy-Con 2 controllers are great but during longer play sessions, you'll probably need a full-sized controller. Right now, the PDP Afterglow Wave is $29 at Amazon which is half off its original price.It's worth noting that only the white version of this controller is currently on sale. Still, you get built-in motion controls, remappable rear paddles and built-in RGB lighting to illuminate those late-night play sessions. We tested the Xbox version of this controller and called it an excellent budget option compared to first-party controllers.

Recent findings suggest that after It Takes Two, Split Fiction is Hazelight's latest split-screen, co-op adventure. Right now, you can pick up Split Fiction for just $34 on Amazon which is its lowest price yet. You can get the game as a digital code or as a physical copy with Game-Key Card.Split Fiction is a true co-op adventure that sees Authors Mio and Zoe trapped in a machine that brings their stories to life. In our Split Fiction review, we praised this sci-fi/fantasy mash-up even if it fell slightly short of It Takes Two overall. It's worth noting that with Friend's Pass, you only need one copy of this game to play through it online with a friend on their Switch 2 console.

Pick up a (digital) copy of Lego Star Wars: The SkyWalker Saga for just $7.99 via Nintendo, a massive 90% off its launch price. This family-friendly RPG-style game covers all nine Star Wars movies, from The Phantom Menace to Return of the Jedi. Light-hearted and jam-packed with surprises, it's the perfect single-player game for Star Wars fans young and old.
