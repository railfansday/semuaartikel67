Fulham defender Calvin Bassey is a top transfer target for Manchester United as Mauricio Pochettino plots a major rebuild.

This is according to the Telegraph, who claim the Nigeria international will feature prominently on a list of potential left-footed central defensive targets for the new Man United head coach.

Capable of playing at left-back but at the moment deployed by Fulham boss Alvaro Arbeloa at centre-half, Bassey has played every single minute of the Cottagers’ Premier League campaign so far - including the 1-1 draw with Manchester United back in September.

Valued at £23.3million on transfermarkt, Bassey is approaching the final six months of his contract at Craven Cottage,.

However, Fulham do hold an option to extend his stay by another year and Arbeloa revealed that the club are keen on agreeing a upcoming deal.

Everton’s Jarrad Branthwaite, meanwhile, is another left-footed defender in the Premier League that Man United are reportedly tracking, although the Toffees would likely demand a far more expensive transfer fee for the 24-year-old England international.

Man Utd’s defence has come in for heavy criticism in recent weeks, with club legend Gary Neville sharing his belief that the current backline will lead to relegation for the Red Devils.

No senior defender has been signed by Man United since wing-back Patrick Dorgu arrived at Old Trafford from Lecce in January 2025.

The signing of another left-footed defender would raise questions over Lisandro Martinez’s future at Old Trafford.

A World Cup winner with Argentina, Martinez has struggled on the pitch in recent seasons - while creating controversy off the field by calling out the club’s hierarchy over a perceived lack of investment in the transfer market.
