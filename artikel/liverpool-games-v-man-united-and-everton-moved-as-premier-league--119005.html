Two more Liverpool fixtures in the Premier League have been rescheduled for live TV coverage, as confirmed on Thursday.

With the November international break now concluded, the Reds’ next couple of domestic fixtures are against Manchester United and Everton, with the reverse of those games against our two biggest traditional rivals taking place on consecutive weekends in January.

The fixture list for the remainder of this year had already been locked in, and the first month of 2027 has now been set, other than prospective FA Cup and Carabao Cup matches.

On Thursday, the Premier League unveiled updated dates and times for matches in January, with two of Liverpool’s games being moved.

The home clash against Crystal Palace remains a 3pm kick-off on Saturday 16th, but the subsequent two games have unsurprisingly been chosen for TV coverage.

The Reds will now face Manchester United at Old Trafford on Sunday 24 January at 4:30pm (live on Sky Sports), with the Merseyside derby at Anfield taking place six days later with a 12:30pm kick-off (live on TNT Sports).

The newly introduced dates and times for the Man United and Everton games have left Liverpool with a rather difficult week towards the end of January.

Just three days after the game at Old Trafford, the Reds have their concluding fixture in the league phase of the Champions League at home to Lens, and then a turnaround of just over 50 hours to hosting the Toffees at Anfield.

*FA Cup third round draw and fixture details to be confirmed in December.

**If Liverpool advance to Carabao Cup semi-finals.

The hope is that, by the time the Ligue 1 outfit visit L4 on 27 January, we’ll already be assured of a direct passage to the round of 16 and there won’t be anything riding on that game for Andoni Iraola’s side.

Liverpool at the moment have seven matches in the diary in the first month of 2027, with an FA Cup third-round tie to be added for the weekend of 9/10 January and a potential Carabao Cup semi-final first leg in the subsequent midweek, should we defeat Bournemouth at Anfield next month.

It’s quite plausible that the Reds could have two matches against Everton in January, with both teams still in the Carabao Cup and favourites to win their respective quarter-finals (the Toffees are away to Fleetwood of League Two).

Three Merseyside derbies in the first five weeks of 2027 would be the definition of familiarity breeding contempt, but for now the sole focus for Iraola and co is the small matter of Man United at home on Sunday.
