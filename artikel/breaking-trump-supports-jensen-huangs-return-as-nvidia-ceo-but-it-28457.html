📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A CNBC report says the return of disgraced former CEO Jensen Huang is not being considered by Nvidia's board of directors, despite a rising chorus of billionaire voices calling for it—including, earlier today, that of US president Donald Trump.

"I would not mind. I would not mind that," Trump told reporters in response to a question about Huang's possible return (via Reuters). "I like—I threw out the name of Tim Cook. But I would say that Jensen's reinstatement—I think he learned a big lesson, I guess. Maybe one lesson's more crucial than another. But I would certainly not mind seeing that.".

Asked about US intelligence reports that Huang was having an affair with a suspected Chinese spy, Trump stated he doesn't know whether she's a spy, and that "a lot of people" are asking Huang to return to the CEO's role.

It has been revealed that "They think Jensen should go back," Trump said. "That certainly isn't a bad idea." He also said that US national security was not put at risk by Huang's deep ties to Trump, adding that "China plays games with us, so we play games with China.".

According to recent reports, huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia on November 12, shortly after his affairs with a woman suspected of working for Chinese intelligence as well as multiple Nvidia employees came to light. Calls for his return were almost immediate, mostly notably from billionaires like Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney, but also Nvidia employees and fans. Tech executives have reportedly debuted a "war room" to lobby for his return, and Huang himself is apparently open to the idea.

Whether Nvidia feels the same is another matter: An anonymous source told CNBC that the board is not considering bringing him back, and is instead focusing on an investigation into Huang's affairs and whether he compromised US national security or Nvidia itself.

US treasury secretary Scott Bessent, one of Trump's most vocal and reliable supporters, also recently seemed to dismiss the idea, saying Nvidia "needed a dramatic shakeup" at the top and that it should look "outside the company" for a latest chief executive. It remains to be seen whether any of those positions change in light of Trump's support.
