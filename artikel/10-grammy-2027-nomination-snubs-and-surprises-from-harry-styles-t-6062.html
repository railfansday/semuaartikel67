Industry observers note that anyone who opened the 2027 Grammy nominations expecting to see the year's biggest sellers at the top found a different set of names. Voters at the Recording Academy reshuffled who counts this year, and several acts with the most commercial weight came away with little or nothing to show for their year. Rapper A$AP Rocky received no nominations at all, according to Rolling Stone. His shutout was not an isolated case among artists with large followings. Artists with a strong year with listeners had no assurance of a place on this ballot.

In a recent development, the Academy describes its ballot as open to everyone. Harvey Mason jr., the Recording Academy's CEO, told The Associated Press that "it’s inspiring to see musicians at every stage of their careers represented." Career stage predicted little this year, though, because veterans and first-time nominees landed on both sides of the line. The two sides do not stand on equal footing. An act left off the ballot has no further chance this cycle, while every surprise nominee still has to win over voters again in final voting, which runs from Dec. 10 to Jan. 7. The Academy will name the winners at the 69th Grammy Awards on Feb. 7 in Los Angeles, according to St. Louis Public Radio. That timing is why the upsets matter now, since they show what voters valued before anyone has handed out a trophy.

Recent findings suggest that W Magazine set out to find the biggest upsets of the 2027 Grammy nominations. The first surprise sits at the very top of the ballot, where for the first time all six of the most-nominated artists are women. Several men who had substantial years, Harry Styles among them, drew less recognition than expected. The upsets also reach well past chart stars. Madonna and the women of country music appear among them, and so does a masked experimental duo that few casual listeners know, which earned multiple nominations in the Best New Artist race. The duo comes from Quebec, according to CBC Music, and it now competes on the same ballot as a pop icon. The acts that follow include both artists left off the ballot and artists who forced their way onto it, and each kind helps explain the other. Together they show the Academy rewarding something other than the year's biggest names, and the slides that follow show where that line fell.

Harry Styles went from the Grammys' top prize to no nominations at all. Rolling Stone reports that his album Harry's House won Album of the Year in 2023, and that Styles was left off the 2027 ballot entirely, with zero nominations. That result is not a near miss in the all-genre races at the top. The shutout covers every category on the ballot, including the genre awards.

Styles did not make his fresh record with a new team. He worked again with Kid Harpoon and Tyler Johnson on Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally, according to Rolling Stone, so the album reunited him with collaborators from his past work. That reunion did not bring the nominations his last album had earned.

Styles also came into this cycle with well-known hits behind him. His earlier songs include "As It Was" and "Watermelon Sugar," according to W Magazine, and listeners still connect him with both. Those hits gave him a large audience going into this year. Neither that audience nor his return to familiar collaborators earned the upcoming album a place in a single race. His case shows that an artist's past record carried little weight on this year's ballot, even for a singer who had won the biggest award only a few years earlier.

Three years after Album of the Year, Styles has no nomination to show for his newly introduced record.

Raye's upset is an outsider leading the Grammy ballot, not a favorite being passed over. She topped every artist with 11 nominations, according to W Magazine. That total matches the most any woman has earned in a single year, a record Beyoncé set and Raye ties rather than breaks. She reached it with little history at the Grammys. Before this year she had four career nominations and had never won, according to The Associated Press. Voters had nominated her before, but never on anything close to this scale, and now they place her at the head of the field. She arrives at the top of the ballot as a newcomer, not as an established star adding one more honor.

Raye competes in the races that judge every genre at once. The Associated Press reports that her song "Where Is My Husband!" is up for record of the year and that she, Rosalía, Taylor Swift and Olivia Rodrigo are among the album of the year nominees. Rosalía is next in the nomination count with nine. Her closest rival is also her opponent in the biggest category, so Raye's lead gives her no margin where it matters most.

Rosalía also arrives with a top prize that Raye has never won. Her album "Lux" won album of the year at the Latin Grammys, according to The Associated Press. Raye's nomination count reveals how many races she entered, not what she will win. The ballot has made her the year's breakthrough, and whether that breakthrough becomes her first Grammy is still open.

According to recent reports, madonna is back on the Grammy ballot, but voters stopped short of placing her among the year's frontrunners. She earned four nominations for the 2027 awards with her fresh album, Confessions on a Dance Floor: Part 2, according to W Magazine. E! News reports that the Recording Academy had gone 16 years without nominating her before this year. Her return to the ballot is a real comeback after a long absence. None of the four came in the Big Four, the all-genre categories at the top of the ballot. Voters welcomed her back but kept her out of the races that decide the night.

The nominations reward newly introduced music that listeners had already embraced, not a long career. Fans streamed the album 13.07 million times on Spotify $SPOT on its first full day, according to chartdata figures reported by CBC. The album also drew praise as personal work rather than a return to old hits. "This is Madonna as a memoirist. This is Madonna as a storyteller," music journalist Emilie Hanskamp noted. A large audience and serious praise still did not carry her into the top races.

In a recent development, voters' respect for Madonna's work has never been in doubt, and the new nods bring her career total to 32 Grammy nominations. They gave her a place on the ballot this year, but they did not give her a chance at the night's biggest prizes. Her legacy was already secure. The open question was whether voters still see her as a frontrunner.

This year, their answer is not yet.

This year's ballot leaned toward women, but that lean stopped short of the young women behind some of the year's biggest pop runs. Gracie Abrams, Stella Lefty and Sienna Spiro each came off a strong year, but only Spiro drew a nomination, in Best Upcoming Artist, while Abrams and Lefty were left off the ballot entirely, according to W Magazine. Where the Recording Academy recognized these three at all, it treated them as newcomers rather than as artists ready for the top races.

Abrams aimed highest and was turned away both in the all-genre field and in her own genre. She submitted her album for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album, E! News reports, and her single "Hit the Wall" went unrecognized along with the album. Before this year, voters had recognized her as a newcomer and as a duet partner. Her first nomination came in 2024 in Best Latest Artist, the same category that holds Spiro's only nomination this year. In 2025 voters nominated her for Best Pop Duo Performance for "Us," a track she shared with Taylor Swift. This year she asked voters to judge her on her own in the biggest fields, and they declined.

Sources indicate that lefty had a hit and still drew no nominations. E! News reports that "Boston" was a hit for her, yet voters named her in no category, from the newcomer race to the top fields. A hit song did not guarantee a nomination this year.

Recent findings suggest that spiro's nomination confirmed a verdict another awards show had already reached. She won Best Newly introduced Artist at this year's VMAs, and Grammy voters likewise recognized her as a new artist. Her one nomination is in Best New Artist, not for her song "Die on This Hill." Grammy voters agreed with the VMAs that Spiro is a new artist to watch, and they went no further than that.

Audrey Hobert and her brother, Malcolm Todd, were both shut out of the 2027 Grammy nominations, according to W Magazine. The miss was total for two sibling pop acts whom many expected to see in the Best Latest Artist race. That expectation came from buzz, not from a forecast. Unnamed observers had speculated about Best New Artist bids for both, and neither the Recording Academy nor any named source had made that call. Their shutout is a test of whether talk of a breakout turns into a place on the ballot, and for this pair it did not.

Hobert had the stronger claim to a breakout. Rolling Stone reports that she had a Billboard Hot 100 entry with her song "Sue Me." With that chart entry, she had evidence behind the Best Newly introduced Artist talk around her, not just rumor. Voters still left her out of the new-artist field. No reason for the omission has surfaced, so her miss is a verdict without an explanation.

Todd's music reached the ballot even though he did not. Rolling Stone reports that Don Toliver sampled Todd's "Chest Pain (I Love)" on "E85," and voters nominated that track for Best Rap Song and Best Melodic Rap Performance. Those nominations belong to Toliver's record, and Todd does not count as a nominee on it. Both races are in rap categories, far from the new-artist race where the speculation had placed Todd. A sample on another artist's hit gave him no credit of his own with voters.

It has been revealed that the sibling Best New Artist story needed both of them to get in. Instead, voters passed over each of them, and neither breakout earned a nomination.

In a recent development, women made this year's surprise in country by taking a whole Grammy race outright. No man is in the running for Best Contemporary Country Album, because for the first time all five nominees are women, according to W Magazine. Kelsea Ballerini, Carter Faith, Ella Langley, Megan Moroney and Kacey Musgraves fill the slate, CBC News reports, and that makes the result a breakthrough rather than a grievance. In this race the story is how completely women held the field, not who was left off it.

Industry observers note that langley is at the center of the sweep with seven nominations, according to CBC News. Only Raye and Rosalía have more, and Langley's total ties her with Olivia Rodrigo, Phoebe Bridgers and Miranda Lambert. Lambert reached that group partly through Langley's hit, "Choosin' Texas." She co-wrote the song and shares four of Langley's nominations. Two country women now stand among the year's most-nominated artists, and part of each woman's total comes from the same song.

The women swept only half of country's album field. CBC News reports that country albums now compete in separate contemporary and traditional categories, and the sweep took place in the contemporary one. None of the five women from that race appears among the traditional nominees, so their hold belongs to one style of country. Langley and Musgraves do reach past the album race, with nominations for Best Country Solo Performance as well.

The country women's breakthrough is real, but it is a takeover of contemporary country, and whether it extends to the rest of the genre is still open.

Recent findings suggest that voters gave Noah Kahan far less recognition than his audience would suggest. The singer has been hugely popular since 2022, but his only nomination this year is for a folk album, according to W Magazine. This year, voters measured him against folk records rather than against the year's biggest names.

Sources indicate that kahan missed the top of the ballot, where artists from every genre compete. None of his work appeared in the races for record, song or album of the year, according to Quartz. Several unnamed experts had expected him to land in those races, according to Hollywood Reporter forecasts reported by Quartz. A nomination in any of the three would have put him in contention against the whole field rather than against folk records alone. The distance between those predictions and his single nomination makes his result an upset rather than a routine genre outcome.

Industry observers note that voters were judging a different album from the one that made Kahan famous. He broke out with Stick Season in 2022, according to W Magazine, while the album on this year's ballot is The Great Divide. The result passes no verdict on the songs that built his following and applies to the latest album alone. Kahan kept his public response short. "Life goes on," he wrote in a tweet on nominations Monday.

He can still win the folk race. This year he did not earn a place among the contenders in the all-genre races.

This year's left-field pop acts reached the Grammy ballot through dance and electronic music. Voters named Slayyyter, Tinashe and Underscores as rivals in a single race, Best Dance/Electronic Recording, according to W Magazine. Three acts from outside the mainstream now compete in one category. Slayyyter and Tinashe also face each other for Best Dance Pop Recording. Underscores is absent from that second race, so the three acts did not share equally in the recognition. Slayyyter earned four nominations in all, according to St. Louis Public Radio, and she is a real presence in the genre's categories this year.

Sources indicate that slayyyter was no unknown by the time voters filled out their ballots. She gave a breakthrough performance at Lollapalooza. She also played to much larger audiences over the year. In the spring she took the stage at Coachella, and she appeared on national television for the first time as a guest on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon," according to St. Louis Public Radio. She had performed for major festival crowds and a television audience across the country well before the nominations came out. Her nominations went to an artist whom many listeners had already seen for themselves.

Listeners who have not yet heard Slayyyter, Tinashe or Underscores can start with this year's dance categories.

Industry observers note that justin Bieber did not lose a close race this year. He is missing from the ballot entirely. His album Swag earned a Grammy nomination, according to CBC Music, so voters had backed his previous album. Yet Swag II, the album that followed it, brought him not a single nod for the 2027 awards, according to W Magazine. Bieber also submitted the songs "Everything Hallelujah" and "Speed Demon," and neither one made the cut. Voters turned down his whole year, not a single release. For an artist the Academy had nominated so recently, that is a clean break rather than a quiet miss.

It has been revealed that voters stopped rewarding Bieber before they stopped nominating him. He holds two Grammy wins over his career, yet CBC Music reports that he left the 2026 ceremony without a trophy. His most recent turn with the Academy ended in a nomination and no win. Seen across two years, his standing with voters fell from a loss on Grammy night to no place on the ballot at all.

It has been revealed that the Academy had not pushed him aside on that earlier night, though. Bieber performed at the 68th Grammys on Feb. 1, his first time on the Grammy stage in four years, according to CBC Music, and he went home empty-handed the same evening. Organizers wanted him on the show even as voters passed him over for awards.

On this evidence, his zero reflects what this year's voters prized rather than a break between Bieber and the Grammys as a whole. They moved past an artist they had nominated not long ago.

Recent findings suggest that grammy voters recognized Angine de Poitrine on two fronts at once, as a newcomer and in the rock field. Voters placed the experimental rock duo in the Best New Artist race, according to Rolling Stone, so it now faces every other act hoping to break through this year. That newcomer race was not its only nomination. Its four nominations in all, CBC Music reports, reach well past Best New Artist into Best Rock Performance, Best Rock Album and Best Alternative Music Performance. Three of the four fall in rock and alternative categories, so the duo's place on the ballot rests on more than a single nomination as a new name. Angine de Poitrine is still only a contender, though, and it has won nothing yet.

For Canadian listeners, these nominations confirm a rise they had already watched. The Canadian duo won both the Polaris Music Prize and the Song Prize in September, according to CBC Music, and it reached the Grammy ballot with those honors already in hand. The duo had also announced a 2027 tour before the nominations came out. Fans were already waiting for those reveals, however Grammy voters decided. Much of the Grammy audience is now hearing the name for the first time, while listeners in Canada heard the music months ago.

Recent findings suggest that with this pick, voters did not overrule the year's buzz. They caught up with buzz that had already built in Canada.
