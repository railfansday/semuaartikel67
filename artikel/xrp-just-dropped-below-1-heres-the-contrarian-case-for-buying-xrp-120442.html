Just 18 months ago, many crypto investors predicted that XRP (XRP -0.99%) was about to go parabolic. But after trading as high as $3.65 in summer 2025, XRP's price has collapsed. Heading into December 2026, XRP's price has fallen below the psychologically key $1 mark.

According to recent reports, but don't sleep on XRP. There's a contrarian case for buying this popular crypto token at heavily discounted prices.

It has been revealed that the primary use case for XRP right now is related to cross-border payments. Financial institutions and payment providers use XRP as a bridge currency to move value between different fiat currencies.

Recent findings suggest that the problem here is that stablecoins are beginning to take over some of this functionality, significantly diminishing XRP's utility. So it's up to Ripple, the $50 billion fintech giant behind the XRP token, to develop upcoming use cases for it.

Recent findings suggest that since 2023, Ripple has spent more than $3 billion on acquisitions to create an end-to-end payment and liquidity network, and XRP remains a big part of that strategic vision. According to Ripple executives, the XRP token and the Ripple stablecoin can play complementary roles here.

In a recent development, in terms of finding fresh use cases for the XRP token, there's increased focus on using the XRP blockchain ledger for asset tokenization initiatives. If tokenized assets on XRPL scale meaningfully, demand for XRP could skyrocket.

Another knock against XRP is that institutional adoption is not taking place as swiftly as many originally predicted. Dozens of banks and financial institutions use XRP, but that number needs to scale dramatically for there to be any upward pressure on the price of XRP.

In a recent development, A big catalyst here was supposed to be passage of the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (Clarity Act). But that piece of groundbreaking crypto legislation stalled in the Senate this year, dashing the crypto industry's hopes for greater regulatory certainty around blockchain payments.

According to recent reports, ripple was one of the biggest proponents of the legislation, so it's easy to see why XRP has taken a huge hit ever since the Clarity Act failed to pass. Over the past 90 days, XRP is down a shocking 34%.

Given the above, it's easy to see why sentiment on XRP is so negative right now. But sentiment in the crypto market can change on a dime. Case in point: The Crypto Fear & Greed Index has tumbled from a reading above 80 ("extreme greed") in summer to a reading below 10 ("extreme fear") in winter.

That's why I'm keeping an eye on flows into and out of spot XRP ETFs. If inflows start to return, that could be an early signal that investors are ready to buy XRP again.

Investors may not see a repeat of late 2024 and early 2025, when the price of XRP skyrocketed by 580% in just weeks. But XRP could be headed much higher sooner than many people currently expect.
