📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that GTA 6 has been shattering records since before it came out, becoming the top-rated game in the history of Metacritic before it had the biggest entertainment launch (by dollars) in history. Sony is now joining in that victory lap by sharing GTA 6 milestones as they pertain to PlayStation.

"More than 39 million unique players have experienced Jason and Lucia's story in Grand Theft Auto 6 on PlayStation 5," Sony content communications manager Tim Turi wrote in a blog post today. That is a staggering number, especially considering GTA 6 also introduced on Xbox and will (eventually) hit PC.

But that's not a sales record—Rockstar still has a ways to go to surpass GTA 5's 230 million lifetime sales. What is unprecedented, at least for PlayStation, is GTA 6's concurrents over launch weekend.

"GTA 6 set a upcoming record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, surpassing previous records across multiplayer, free-to-play, and single-player titles," Turi noted.

In a recent development, that's the wild one to me, because that means more people were playing GTA 6 at the same time on the largest console platform than Call of Duty, GTA 5, or even Fortnite has ever achieved. Turi didn't share the concurrent figure itself, which is too bad—Steam remains the only platform that hands out exact player data. If the figure is theoretically higher than some of the biggest concurrent peaks we know about on Steam, then it could've been as high as 3-4 million players.

On average, those 39 million PS5 players logged 17.4 hours over four days. Let's just say my hour count represents the higher end of that average, and I'm still less than halfway through the main story.
