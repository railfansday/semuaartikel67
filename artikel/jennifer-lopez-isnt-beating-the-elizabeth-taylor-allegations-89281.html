Jennifer Lopez has more than a little in common with Elizabeth Taylor. Both have lived out their love lives in the glare of the public eye—comparisons between the two have only intensified since J Lo’s fourth divorce, with Taylor famously having walked down the aisle eight times—and both know the power of an extravagant outfit.

It has been revealed that for the Los Angeles premiere of her concert film The JLo Show on Thursday night, Lopez channeled old Hollywood glamour in a Dolce & Gabbana lace gown and an elaborately embroidered opera coat. But it was her jewelry that would really have caught Elizabeth Taylor’s eye: a white-gold Alta Gioielleria Sicilia necklace featuring a whopping 100.10-carat yellow diamond, with matching earrings.

Taylor’s own huge diamond, ruby, and gold archive have been called upon a’plenty: Margot Robbie wore Taylor’s iconic heart-shaped Taj Mahal diamond set in a Cartier necklace while promoting Wuthering Heights, and Kim Kardashian walked the Balenciaga fall 2025 couture show in Paris wearing diamond pendant earrings once worn by Taylor, on loan from jeweler-to-the-stars Lorraine Schwartz. Taylor Swift remains the most ardent modern collector.

Elizabeth Taylor knew a thing or two about a statement jewel. A passionate collector, her jewelry box included a Bvlgari ring featuring a 2.62-carat yellow diamond—a 30th-birthday gift from her fourth husband, Eddie Fisher—and a yellow diamond bracelet gifted to her by Michael Jackson, which fetched £119,700 at auction in 2025. Jennifer Lopez might have a few marriages to go before she catches up with Taylor, but in the jewelry stakes? She’s giving her a run for her money.
