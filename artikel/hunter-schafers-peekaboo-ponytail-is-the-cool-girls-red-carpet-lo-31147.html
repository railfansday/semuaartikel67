Hunter Schafer’s Peekaboo Ponytail Is the Cool Girl’s Red Carpet Look

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that from intricate braids and gravity-defying knots to elaborate, architectural chignons, the red carpet has seen plenty of sculptural hair looks as of late. Leave it to Hunter Schafer to offer a decidedly cool-girl interpretation of the trend.

Attending the London premiere of Blade Runner 2099 on Tuesday evening, Schafer—styled as always by Dara—wore a custom ivory Celine ensemble comprising a strapless satin bustier dress, with a dramatic train trailing behind her, layered over tailored tuxedo trousers for that nonchalant elegance. Her beauty followed suit. Working with her go-to hairstylist Rena Calhoun, Schafer wore her honey-blonde lengths in softly tousled, loose waves, parted loosely at the center and falling around her shoulders. From the front it was a classic and romantic, and a rather effortless-looking hairstyle. But turn around, and there was a delightful little surprise: a tiny, twisted, extremely low ponytail woven into the ends of her hair at the nape of her neck, secured with a wrap of blonde strands. The mini braid was a lovely, blink-and-you’ll-miss-it detail. Makeup artist Sandy Ganzer kept things similarly soft, with subtly defined eyes, flushed cheeks, and a rosy pink lip.

In a recent development, it’s another clever beauty moment in what’s proving to be an excellent press tour for Schafer. At the Los Angeles premiere last week, Ganzer paid homage to Daryl Hannah’s replicant character Pris in Ridley Scott’s original Blade Runner, creating a dramatic, futuristic strip of gunmetal eyeshadow across her eyes and nose. Calhoun fashioned her hair into a disheveled updo, with flyaways escaping at the back for a cyberpunk heroine to red carpet look.

Then there were the hair-matching fuzzy earmuffs from Undercover’s fall 2007 collection, which Calhoun incorporated into a double-ponytail hairstyle. Working alongside Dara and Ganzer, Schafer has been having enormous fun with beauty references that are both deeply considered and playful, often drawing on the sci-fi world of her forthcoming series and beyond.

In a recent development, the up-do renaissance is firmly upon us. At this year’s Golden Globes, Audrey Nuna, Mia Goth, and Jenna Ortega were among those experimenting with interesting shapes and structures, while Anya Taylor-Joy recently sported a sleek knot at the LACMA Art+Film Gala.

What’s so great about Hunter Schafer’s latest look is its restraint. While everyone else is twisting, sculpting, and shellacking their hair into elaborate works of art, Schafer makes a case for something more low-key. Sometimes the most interesting beauty detail is the one you almost miss.
