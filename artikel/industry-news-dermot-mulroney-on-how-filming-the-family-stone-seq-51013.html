More than 20 years later, The Family Stone sequel has become a real-life family reunion of sorts for the cast of the 2005 holiday movie.

Dermot Mulroney, who reprises his role as Everett Stone in the upcoming sequel The Families Stone, recently expressed that he’s “so moved to be reunited” with his “incredible, incredible” co-stars from the original Thomas Bezucha-written/directed film.

“Everybody’s had this remarkable career advancement and paths since we were all together,” he told Entertainment Weekly. “So we’re making a movie about a family returning all together for the first time in years and years, and we’re doing that in real life too, returning and reuniting with this incredible cast.”.

Mulroney added of his onscreen brother, “Luke Wilson and I always had a real tight thing going, so that snapped right back into place.” Watch on Deadline.

The Families Stone picks up 20 years after the death of Diane Keaton’s character, matriarch Sybil Stone — something that was depicted in the original film — as the Stone family again reunites for Christmas, only to face the season’s biggest surprise: their father (Craig T. Nelson) has unexpectedly invited the woman (Jean Smart) he’s fallen in love with to meet the family.

Following Keaton’s death at age 79 in October 2025, Bezucha revealed that he was writing the sequel, which was set at Searchlight in September and is now filming in New York. In addition to Mulroney, Nelson and Wilson, original cast members Claire Danes, Rachel McAdams, Sarah Jessica Parker, Tyrone Giordano, Elizabeth Reaser, Paul Schneider and Brian White are returning.
