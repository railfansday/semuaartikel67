Tempus AI entered a senior secured credit agreement on November 25, 2026, providing $385 million of term-loan commitments and $115 million of revolving commitments to help finance its pending Personalis acquisition, according to its Form 8-K.

According to recent reports, in a separate November 24, 2026 decision, Tempus elected to pay approximately $600 million in cash and issue approximately $900 million in Tempus Class A common stock to Personalis shareholders. These are financing commitments, not evidence that the full amounts have already been drawn. Initial funding requires the acquisition to have closed or be reasonably expected to close within one business day after funding.

Recent findings suggest that tempus expects borrowings to fund part of the acquisition consideration and related transaction costs, as well as working capital and general corporate purposes. Ares Capital Corporation is the administrative and collateral agent, Ares Capital Management LLC the lead arranger and bookrunner, and ACF Finco I LP the revolving agent.

According to recent reports, term loans bear interest at either the agreement’s Base Rate benchmark plus 4.50% or its Term SOFR benchmark plus 5.50%; revolving loans use Base Rate plus 2.75% or Term SOFR plus 3.75%. Term SOFR has a 1.00% floor, the Base Rate a 2.00% floor, and interest on the loans is payable solely in cash.

According to recent reports, both facilities mature on the fifth anniversary of the effective date. A springing provision can move maturity to 91 days before Tempus’s 0.75% convertible senior notes due 2030 unless those notes are repaid or converted first or specified liquidity requirements are met.

Sources indicate that certain Tempus subsidiaries will guarantee the obligations, which are secured by substantially all assets of Tempus and those subsidiaries. The agreement includes minimum-liquidity and minimum-revenue covenants. Revolving availability depends on eligible accounts receivable and certain qualified cash, subject to reserves and other limitations, and revolving interest can be charged on a minimum balance even when actual borrowings are lower.

It has been revealed that the cash election uses flexibility described in Tempus’s July 20, 2026 acquisition announcement. The merger agreement priced Personalis at $16.25 per common share, representing approximately $1.5 billion in enterprise value net of Tempus’s existing ownership. Consideration was originally structured as all stock, with Tempus holding an option to pay cash capped at 50% of the consideration and a floating exchange ratio capped at 0.3356 Tempus shares per Personalis share.

The cash estimate assumes 108,740,631 Personalis shares outstanding immediately before closing and no perfected appraisal demands. The number of shares designated for cash can automatically decrease, with a corresponding increase in the stock portion, to satisfy the merger agreement’s reorganization threshold, determined using Tempus’s closing-date trading price.

Tempus states the acquisition pairs its multimodal data, AI capabilities and commercial infrastructure with Personalis’s tumor-informed molecular residual disease (MRD) technology, including the NeXT Personal test, which detects small traces of circulating tumor DNA to monitor treatment response, residual cancer and recurrence.

Sources indicate that the transaction builds on a partnership established in November 2023, through which Tempus invested in Personalis and commercialized NeXT Personal. Tempus CEO Eric Lefkofsky called MRD “a large and rapidly growing market” and said the collaboration had demonstrated the strength of combining Personalis’s technology with his company’s commercial infrastructure.

Tempus originally expected the acquisition to close in late 2026 or early 2027. Closing remains subject to Personalis stockholder adoption of the merger agreement and other customary conditions. Tempus has now aligned the debt package with its cash-and-stock election, but the borrowing timetable and the proposed AI-supported MRD expansion both remain tied to completion of the acquisition.
