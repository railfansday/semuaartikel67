Redmond, Wash.-based RBC Signals has declared a $10 million strategic growth investment aimed at accelerating the company’s expansion into government satellite data services and the commercialization of emerging space technologies.

RBC Signals supports satellite operators by arranging access to a global network of ground stations, including its own assets and antenna facilities operated by partners.

In August, for example, Blue Origin and RBC Signals revealed a partnership to build out the ground infrastructure for Blue Origin’s Quartz communications network. RBC Signals is also a provider for the U.S. Space Force’s Joint Antenna Marketplace.

The newly unveiled investment was led by Washington Harbour Partners, a private investment firm based in Washington, D.C. The firm ranked sixth among all defense technology investors in the Silicon Valley Defense Group’s 2026 NatSec100 Report.

“Our vision has always been to build a global, secure network of networks that makes space connectivity more accessible, flexible and reliable,” Ron Faith, RBC Signals’ president and CEO, remarked today in a news release.

“By bringing together infrastructure, partners and emerging technologies, we’re creating a dual-use platform that can evolve alongside the needs of the national defense and the commercial space industry,” Faith noted. “This investment from Washington Harbour Partners will help us accelerate that vision by expanding our government services business and bringing emerging capabilities such as higher frequency bands, optical connectivity, intersatellite links and space domain awareness to market.”.

Mina Faltas, founder and CEO of Washington Harbour Partners, highlighted RBC Signals’ role in addressing the growing demand for space connectivity. “We see tremendous potential in the firm’s ability to deliver mission-critical capabilities, and we’re excited to support the team as they enter this next phase of growth,” Faltas mentioned.
