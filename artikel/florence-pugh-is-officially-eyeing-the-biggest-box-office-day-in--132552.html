In a recent development, when Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer and Greta Gerwig's Barbie opened on the same day in July 2023, what had initially been viewed as a rather peculiar bit of scheduling ended up being one of the best things to happen to the theatrical industry in years. Among those who benefited from Barbenheimer was Florence Pugh, who worked with Nolan for the first time on Oppenheimer, and while Pugh has since become a fairly integral part of two of Hollywood's biggest franchises, she's about to find herself in the rather unusual position of competing against herself at the box office. Either way, it's going to be a pretty good weekend for Pugh.

Recent findings suggest that we're now officially just three weeks away from Dunesday, with Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday and Warner Bros.' Dune: Part Three both scheduled to open in theaters on December 18, and Pugh has a role in both movies. Having made her debut as Yelena Belova in Black Widow in 2021, Pugh went on to join Denis Villeneuve's adaptation of Frank Herbert's novels as Princess Irulan in Dune: Part Two, and both characters are expected to have plenty to do this time around.

According to recent reports, neither studio has opted to move its movie out of the other's way, although Villeneuve does have one considerable advantage over Joe and Anthony Russo, having secured an exclusive IMAX window for the conclusion of his trilogy. According to Boxoffice Pro, Dune: Part Three is presently tracking toward a domestic opening weekend of between $85 million and $100 million, which would give Villeneuve his biggest opening weekend to date, having previously opened Dune: Part Two to $82.5 million in March 2024.

Industry observers note that alongside Pugh (Midsommar) as Yelena Belova, Marvel's first Avengers movie in seven years features Robert Downey Jr. (Iron Man) as Doctor Doom, Chris Hemsworth (Extraction) as Thor, Anthony Mackie (The Falcon and the Winter Soldier) as Sam Wilson, Pedro Pascal (The Last of Us) as Reed Richards, Patrick Stewart (Star Trek: The Next Generation) as Charles Xavier, and Ian McKellen (The Lord of the Rings) as Magneto.

Dune: Part Three, meanwhile, features Timothée Chalamet (Wonka) as Paul Atreides, Zendaya (Euphoria) as Chani, Pugh as Princess Irulan, Jason Momoa (Aquaman) as Duncan Idaho, Anya Taylor-Joy (The Queen's Gambit) as Alia Atreides, and Robert Pattinson (The Batman) as Scytale.
