Liverpool are among Premier League clubs keeping a close eye on young defensive talent Joane Gadou, reports Mark Brus.

The Borussia Dortmund centre-back (19) signed for the Bundesliga giants this summer and has featured 17 times for them since (across all competitions).

Reporting for CaughtOffside’s The Daily Briefing, Mark Brus said: “I’m reliably told by sources with ties to the agents industry that Arsenal and other Premier League clubs have been closely monitoring Gadou for some time. The Frenchman only joined Dortmund this summer after impressing at Red Bull Salzburg, and he also had a spell in PSG’s academy earlier in his career.”.

Recent findings suggest that he went on to add that a source mentioned: “Arsenal got a close look at him yesterday, but the reality is that they have been monitoring Gadou for months. A lot of the top clubs know about him, even if they weren’t ready to move for him yet. I see him developing at Dortmund for a bit longer, but it’s clear how talented he is and if he carries on like this they won’t hold on to him for long.”.

Liverpool, Manchester United and Real Madrid are ‘keen’ on Gadou, but may have to come up against competition from PSG if the player continues his current developmental trajectory.

And that should come as no surprise to onlookers when the Frenchman is putting in top-quality displays of the calibre he produced against Arsenal in the Champions League on Tuesday night.

According to recent reports, combative, solid in the air, and against Mikel Arteta’s tricky Premier League champions. Notably, this hardly seems a fluke either, judging by the kind of numbers Joane Gadou is putting up for Dortmund in the Bundesliga.

* Joane Gadou’s stats in the 2026/27 Bundesliga season (via FotMob).

Between Giovanni Leoni, Jeremy Jacquet, Mor Talla Ndiaye and Ifeanyi Ndukwe, high-potential, young centre-backs are hardly in short supply at L4.

That’s not to say there’s never any justification for adding more talent to the club ranks, but the problem Liverpool are facing at the end of the 2026/27 season dictates that the club should be looking at experienced operators.

As things currently stand, Virgil van Dijk has no clear idea whether he’s staying or going in the summer, and there’s yet to be any kind of guarantee around Joe Gomez or Ronald Araujo.

According to recent reports, we strongly suspect that Liverpool will have serious conversations about the Dutchman and his Uruguayan teammate given the pair’s strong performances this term. However, there remains a possibility that the Reds could be left incredibly short on experienced heads by the summer transfer window.

If that’s the case, this should be an absolute priority at the back over landing someone of Gadou’s profile.
