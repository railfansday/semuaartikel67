📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Italy maintained their 100 per cent record in the FIFA Under-17 World Cup, winning all three group games to secure top spot and passage to the Round of 32.

The Azzurrini had already thumped Jamaica 6-0 and saw off the Ivory Coast 2-1, then this afternoon got the better of Uzbekistan 3-1.

It sees them top Group E with nine points out of nine, guaranteeing access to the Round of 32.

However, Riccardo Cassano will not participate in that fixture because he was sent off for two bookable offences, and will therefore be suspended.

Tommaso Casagrande, Federico Croci and Thomas Corigliano had the result all sewn up before half-time, with Sukhrob Sadirjanov netting a consolation for Uzbekistan.

Italy are among the favourites to win the tournament in Qatar, having already won the European Under-17 Championship under coach Daniele Franceschini.

The Azzurrini will find out the names of their next opponents on Friday, to see which are the best third-place finishers.
