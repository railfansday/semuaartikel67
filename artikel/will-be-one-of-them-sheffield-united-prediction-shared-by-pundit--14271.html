Sheffield United have been backed to remain in contention for the Championship play-offs by former Blades goalkeeper Carl Ikeme.

Chris Wilder’s men sit 10th in the table after six wins, four draws and five defeats across their opening 15 games.

There are six play-off places up for grabs this season, therefore the Blades are just two points adrift of a spot in spite of their inconsistency.

Retired stopper Ikeme, who had a brief spell on loan at Bramall Lane from Wolverhampton Wanderers in 2009, does not expect Sheffield United to fade away.

Recent findings suggest that he told The Yorkshire Post: “There's extra play-off positions this year, I think a lot of the clubs will be looking at that. I think Sheffield United will definitely be up and around it, especially with everything they've got. They've got a strong team as well still.

“I don't think they'll go up automatically, I don't think they're stronger than the other teams but I think they'll be around the play-offs in those positions, along with 10-12 teams that’ll be thinking they're going to make the play-offs this year already, and I think Sheffield United will be one of them as well.”.

Opinions may be forming at this stage of the season in a 46-game campaign, there are plenty of twists and turns yet to come.

Ikeme said: “The Championship’s not about now, it's about March, April, and I think teams will hit their run of form and will hit a brick wall at some stage.

“It will be up to them to see if they can get through it and see if they will go up.

“[Sheffield United have] great support, a great stadium, great atmosphere, but obviously Chris Wilder is a really experienced manager and he knows what he's doing.”.
