It’s what our family reaches for whenever we need a simple, nourishing dish that warms from the inside out. For the smoothest porridge, let the rice soak in cold water before cooking.

Industry observers note that if you have time, soak the rice, which makes the texture softer and can aid in digestion. Place washed rice in a bowl and pour in water to cover by 1". Soak at least 30 minutes and up to 4 hours. Drain off excess water before starting the recipe. The wakame seaweed, which you’ve probably had in miso soup, can typically be found dried anywhere Asian groceries are available.

Recent findings suggest that combine 1 cup short-grain rice, washed, soaked if desired, ¼ cup dried wakame (seaweed), 1 Tbsp. dried red shiso, and 6 cups low-sodium broth (any kind) or water in a large donabe, medium Dutch oven, or other heavy pot. Cover and bring to a boil over medium-high heat (you’ll hear bubbling). Uncover and stir, making sure nothing is stuck to bottom of pot. Cover and reduce heat to low. Continue to cook porridge 30 minutes more (resist the urge to peek).

Remove pot from heat and let porridge sit (still covered) 10 minutes. Uncover and stir. Rice grains should be cooked through, broken open, and creamy, but porridge should still be fairly loose. Season porridge with fine sea salt.

Ladle porridge into bowls and serve, topped with toasted sesame seeds, toasted sesame oil, and/or thinly sliced scallions.
