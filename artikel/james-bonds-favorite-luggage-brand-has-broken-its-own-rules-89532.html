James Bond’s Favorite Luggage Brand Has Broken Its Own Rules

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, james Bond has always travelled in style. And while Daniel Craig's 007 was most immediately associated with his Aston Martins, Omega watches, and Tom Ford suits, his luggage of choice was Globe-Trotter, the Hertfordshire-based manufacturer whose cases have also found favor with everyone from Queen Elizabeth II to Kate Moss.

Now the company has done something completely left-field. After 129 years of making essentially the same kind of suitcase, it has changed the formula.

The upcoming GT897 is Globe-Trotter's lightest case yet, and a significant departure from the design that made the brand famous. Out go the traditional rigid frame and locks, replaced by a zip-around opening and softer, more streamlined silhouette.

That mentioned, some familiar details keep it firmly on-brand: the vulcanized fiberboard, leather trim, nickel hardware, and those trademark exposed rivets. And the cases are still handcrafted in Hertfordshire.

According to recent reports, at the moment available in two color combinations—bland-and-black and green-and-brown—the GT897 is priced at $1,395. A black-and-oxblood version follows in February.

It has been revealed that it's an interesting move for a firm whose USP has always rested somewhat on its refusal to change. Whether the new James Bond will follow Craig's lead remains to be seen. But at least there's now a new option for packing his tuxedo.
