Now These Apple Services Are Down

If you're having trouble accessing certain Apple products and services today, you are not alone. As of early Tuesday afternoon, Downdetector indicates user reports of issues across multiple Apple services. While Apple's System Status page initially demonstrated far fewer issues than Downdetector users reported, the site has since updated to reflect general outages.

According to Apple, the disruptions aren't quite as widespread: Apple claims that the App Store, Apple Music, AppleCare on Devices, and Schoolwork are all experiencing outages. Apple says that the App Store and Apple Music "may be slow or unavailable," while AppleCare purchase is at the moment unavailable. The tech giant's System Status page demonstrates an "concern" with iCloud Account & Sign In. Other services with user reports on Downdetector, including the Apple Card, Apple Online Store, and Apple Business, read as "Available." Apple also says there was an outage with the Volume Purchase Program, but that has since been resolved.

In a recent development, it's not clear what's causing the complications with the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud Account & Sign In, or other Apple services at this time. However, the timing is odd. It comes after a number of banks experienced outages on Monday, as well as Microsoft's massive outage the previous week. In addition to all of these outages, the FAA unexpectedly grounded all U.S. flights on Friday, for only the third time in the agency's history.
