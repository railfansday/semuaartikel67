Andrew Giuliani described his father, former New York City Mayor Rudy Giuliani, receiving the Presidential Medal of Freedom from President Donald Trump in a closed-press ceremony Tuesday.

President Donald Trump will award former Fresh York City Mayor Rudy Giuliani the Presidential Medal of Freedom on Tuesday during a closed-press ceremony at the White House, honoring the longtime prosecutor and "America's Mayor" for his lifetime of public service.

The Presidential Medal of Freedom is the nation's highest civilian honor, alongside the Congressional Gold Medal, awarded to individuals who have made especially meritorious contributions to U.S. security, national interests, world peace, or significant public and private endeavors.

According to recent reports, "I am honored and humbled to receive the Presidential Medal of Freedom from my nation and from our Commander-in-Chief, President Donald J. Trump," Giuliani, 82, said in a statement.

Quoting his former boss, President Ronald Reagan, Giuliani added: "'There is no limit to what a man can do or where he can go if he doesn’t mind who gets the credit.' I share the credit with all the men and women who worked with me, disagreed with me, and loved me — particularly those who lost their lives in the causes we undertook together.".

After the ceremony, Giuliani's son Andrew Giuliani described the day as "incredibly special.".

Recent findings suggest that "To see your father receive the Presidential Medal of Freedom is an unbelievable honor," Andrew Giuliani told reporters on the White House grounds. "It's tough to describe. But to see him receive it from such a consequential president like the 45th and 47th president, it makes it all the more special.".

White House assistant press secretary Olivia Wales praised Giuliani’s decades-long record in law enforcement and city leadership, noting that his work had "left a lasting impact on Newly introduced York City and our Nation.".

"As U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Mayor Giuliani took down mob bosses and led a relentless war on crime," Wales said. "As Mayor, he transformed New York from a crime-ridden city into one of the safest major cities in the world, fostering economic opportunity and cultural greatness. And in the aftermath of the tragic September 11th attacks, he displayed strong American leadership in the face of evil.".
