Max Dowman has added another incredible achievement to his burgeoning career after becoming the youngest player to play for the England Under-21s.

The Arsenal youngster was introduced from the bench in Friday’s 1-0 friendly loss to Portugal at Ashton Gate. The 16-year-10-month-13-day-old player is over three months younger than Jude Bellingham was when he played his first game for the Under-21s.

Sources indicate that dowman replaced former Arsenal academy player Amario Cozier-Duberry in the 61st minute. He came on alongside debutant Trey Nyoni as England tried to shift the momentum of a challenging game.

According to recent reports, an early strike from Afonso Moreira gave Portugal the lead in the 28th minute. An ineffective England team failed to respond. However, the replacements gave Lee Carsley’s team some late momentum.

Recent findings suggest that despite the hosts’ attempts to level the score, they failed to break down the Portuguese defence. The result means England have lost for the second game running. However, Dowman had much to be pleased with personally.

Dowman has added to his growing collection of records.

He became the youngest player to play in the Champions League, the youngest goalscorer in the history of the Premier League and the youngest to win the Premier League title.

Industry observers note that breaking Bellingham’s England Under-21 record is another significant step. It reveals how promptly Dowman has progressed through the international age groups.

He has already made a major impression at Arsenal with Mikel Arteta’s love of giving younger players a chance, but Dowman will have some stiff competition for first team minutes.

Recent findings suggest that another positive is his progression on an international scale. He will improve both physically and tactically playing against older players and gain experience at a higher level.

In a recent development, england will host France in their next friendly on Monday, 16th November. Dowman will be hoping to feature after his instant impact on the record books.

Despite the disappointing result against Portugal, it was a landmark evening for the Arsenal players.

Dowman is already building a legacy at club level, but now he can add to his list of records by becoming an England Under-21 record holder.

At just 16, he has plenty of time to improve and show consistent performances to get more opportunities with the senior Arsenal squad.
