Grimes is taking a swipe at her ex.

The “Oblivion” singer called out her ex-boyfriend Elon Musk—with whom she shares kids X Æ A-Xii, 6, Exa Dark Sideræl, 4, and Techno Mechanicus, 4—for his fascination with AI characters, specifically the artificially created singer Claudia.

“The fixation on fake women feels like cope,” Grimes wrote on X Nov. 18 in response to a post from Musk about the AI singer. “I’m a fan of Claudia, but she doesn’t have a birkin deal and she isn’t a she, she’s an alien creature who’s both blessed and haunted by the entirety of ancestral memory.”.

“Secualizing [sic] a species that cannot have sex is the ultimate definition of Nietzsche’s last man,” the 38-year-old added, “and we can do better than reducing ‘it’ to a sex slave.”.

Instead of responding to her callout, Musk responded with a repost of Claudia and her creator anabology’s song “WHITEPILL,” urging Grimes to “take the White Pill.”.

The dad of 14 added in a follow up message, “Such a jolly song!”.

Of course, this is hardly the first time Grimes and Musk, 55, have gone toe-to-toe since finally calling it quits on their four-year on-again, off-again romance in March 2022. The two have often been at odds over how public to be with their children, with Grimes (real name Claire Boucher) claiming she has pleaded with the Tesla founder to keep their kids out of the spotlight.

"I unfortunately do not control the internet and cannot wipe this from the internet," she wrote on X in 2025. "I have tried begging the public and my kids dad to keep them offline, and I've tried legal recourse.".

"The state of my children's lives being public is of grave concern to me and I think about how to solve this every day,” she added. “It's insane to me that theres no way to deal with this.".

The “Genesis” singer—who recently debuted her romance with music producer Elijah Weinkauf—added that she hoped there would be “some law that would allow a parent to veto small children from living public lives,” although she was skeptical of getting any help from the legal system, writing, “I don't even trust the law to help me now if I tried to invoke it tbh.”.
