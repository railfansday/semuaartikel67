Saturday’s reports suggest that Bayern Munich could dive into the January transfer market following an injury and surgery for Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane, and that the Milan-linked Nicolo Tresoldi is among the options for the Bavarians.

Kane is expected to miss around three months of action after undergoing surgery on his ankle on Friday, and with injuries to Luis Diaz, Serge Gnabry and a minor knock for Michael Olise, the Bundesliga giants have been left short on options in attack.

According to reports from BILD just a few days ago, Bayern were not expected to make any significant moves during the January transfer window unless any fresh problems arose. The situation has changed since then, though, and Fabrizio Romano suggests that Bayern “will have to consider the possibility to move in the January transfer window.”.

According to reports from Calciomercato.com, Bayern will be on the lookout for a striker who is capable of covering out wide if required.

Saturday’s report claims that Club Brugge’s Nicolo Tresoldi is among those options and that Bayern have been monitoring the 22-year-old’s progress in recent weeks. Bayern are also stated to be fond of Bournemouth’s Eli Junior Kroupi, who, however, could cost somewhere in the region of €80m. A more affordable option could be Monaco’s Paris Brunner, whose price tag reportedly stands at around €20m.

It has been revealed that reports earlier this week claimed that Tresoldi is also an option for Milan, who are reportedly willing to invest significantly on a fresh attacker in the January transfer window. Tresoldi, who has 13 goals from 14 appearances across the Belgian top flight and the Champions League this season, is expected to cost around €60m.
