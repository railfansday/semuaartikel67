Daniel Farke will be keen to make additions to the Leeds United squad in January to continue these impressive results.

Leeds are sitting seventh in the Premier League table after 11 games, long after many rival fans thought they would fall by the wayside.

Farke is in advanced talks over a new contract at Elland Road, and he absolutely deserves it for what he's achieved over the last couple of seasons.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin has been a sensation for Leeds, as have Ethan Ampadu, Tarik Muharemovic and James Trafford, to name just a few.

The Whites are, obviously, unlikely to compete with the bigger teams financially in the Premier League, but that hasn't stopped the owners from investing heavily.

Leeds could achieve so much more this season, but only if they're willing to splash more cash and invest in the current squad at Thorp Arch.

The signings of Jean-Matteo Bahoya, Michael Zetterer and Melvin Bard have not fully worked out yet, but the potential is there.

Despite spending £82million on transfers in the summer, Leeds could be back in the market once again.

Football finance sources have told iNews (26 November) that Leeds have enough "headroom" to be active in the January window if needed.

But the expectation is that the manager will be more "reactive than proactive" when it comes to transfers for the second half of the season.

Those behind the scenes are stuck on the conundrum of whether to invest more to cement a European spot or trust the club's summer business to achieve those goals.

Links and rumours are already appearing at Elland Road, and that is only normal when the squad are doing so well in the early stages.

Football Insider understands that Leeds have been joined by several clubs in the race to sign Racing Santander's Yassir Zabiri.

There could be far more attention on potential exits, though, with Calvert-Lewin catching a few eyes, as well Muharemovic in defence.

The centre-forward looks far more likely to sign a latest contract instead of leave, but it's difficult to rule anything out in football these days.
