Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta had positive words to say about Borussia Dortmund ahead of Tuesday’s UEFA Champions League game clash.

“I am very impressed with them, and you see their results so far, where they are in the Bundesliga and where they are in the Champions League – the way they are playing, the way they are dominating games,” the Spaniard told reporters at Monday’s press conference.

It has been revealed that arteta also recalled the pre-season game the two teams had. “We had them here in pre-season, and they revealed their quality and what they are capable of, so we are very much aware of that, but we will play at home in front of our crowd and go and attack the game; that’s for sure,” he added.

In a recent development, the two sides met in pre-season in the Emirates Cup where Dortmund came out 3-2 winners. Before that, the last time the two teams faced each other was back in 2014 in the Champions League group stage when BVB beat Arsenal 2-0 at home before losing away by the same scoreline.

Unbeaten in this season’s competition, Arsenal sit third in the league phase standings on 10 points, while Dortmund sit 12th on nine points.
