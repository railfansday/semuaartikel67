EU, UK reach reset deal after Brexit

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (105 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that the UK and the EU signed three agreements on Friday at a summit in Brussels, marking a upcoming effort to reset post-Brexit relations. The deals aim to reduce red tape for food exports, link emissions trading schemes and allow tens of thousands of young people to live and work more freely across the Channel. Prime Minister Andy Burnham has called Brexit an “obvious failure” to deliver on its promises and wants to deepen ties with the EU, a position criticised by veteran Brexiteer Nigel Farage, who has warned of a political backlash.
