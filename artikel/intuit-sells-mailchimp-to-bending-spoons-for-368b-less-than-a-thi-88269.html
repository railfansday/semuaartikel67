Intuit sells Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68B, less than a third of what it paid

UPDATED 17:17 EST / NOVEMBER 19 2026.

It has been revealed that financial software manufacturer Intuit Inc. today revealed that it has agreed to sell its Mailchimp email marketing business to Italian technology holding company Bending Spoons S.p.A. for about $3.68 billion.

It has been revealed that coming five years after Intuit agreed to acquire Mailchimp for $12 billion, the sale price is less than a third of what the organization paid. The 2021 purchase was the largest in Intuit’s history at the time.

According to recent reports, although Bending Spoons describes the transaction as all-cash, Intuit will reinvest $368 million of its proceeds in newly issued Bending Spoons shares. That leaves Intuit with $3.31 billion in cash from the sale, according to the companies. Included in the deal is The Rocket Science Group LLC, the legal entity behind Mailchimp. Closing is expected in early 2027, subject to regulatory approvals.

In a recent development, selling Mailchimp is mentioned to sharpen Intuit’s focus on the artificial intelligence-native tools it has built to help businesses win new customers, manage their pipeline and grow. Once the deal closes, Intuit intends to use the cash proceeds to buy back its own shares before the end of fiscal 2027.

Jack Tam, a senior vice president at Intuit, said the organization is confident Bending Spoons “will lead Mailchimp to continued success in this next chapter.”.

Growth at Mailchimp has stalled in recent quarters. Revenue from the business fell 1% to $313 million in the quarter ended Oct. 31. Intuit’s full-year guidance has Mailchimp revenue coming in flat to down 1%. In November 2024, Chief Financial Officer Sandeep Aujla told analysts that Intuit was seeing higher churn from smaller Mailchimp customers.

According to recent reports, “Mailchimp is a powerful brand, built over two decades of reshaping the email marketing category and redefining how companies grow their audiences,” stated Luca Ferrari, co-founder and chief executive of Bending Spoons. “We’re honored to welcome it to Bending Spoons and excited about what we can accomplish together.”.

It has been revealed that ferrari added that Bending Spoons prepares to invest substantially in Mailchimp after closing. According to Bending Spoons, the transformation of a business it acquires is typically deep, entailing reorganized teams, overhauled technology and redesigned user interfaces.

Recent findings suggest that founded in 2013, Bending Spoons has built a portfolio of digital businesses that includes AOL, Brightcove, Eventbrite, Evernote, Tractive, Vimeo and WeTransfer. Since listing on the Nasdaq in July, the company has also completed acquisitions of Airtable Inc. for $1.285 billion and whiteboard platform Miro for $1.355 billion.

Recent findings suggest that intuit declared the sale alongside its fiscal 2027 first-quarter results. For the quarter, it reported adjusted earnings per share of $2.91, up 56% from $1.87 in the same quarter of the previous year, on revenue of $4.35 billion, up 12% year-over-year. Analysts were expecting adjusted earnings of $2.70 per share on revenue of $4.31 billion.

For its fiscal second quarter, Intuit expects adjusted earnings per share of $3.17 to $3.22 on revenue of $4.93 billion to $4.95 billion. Intuit lowered its full-year revenue guidance to between $23.08 billion and $23.37 billion.

Behind the cut is Credit Karma, the company’s personal finance and credit score service, which is now forecast to grow 3% to 7% this fiscal year, down from 11% to 13% previously. Intuit reiterated its full-year adjusted earnings guidance of $22.88 to $23.12 per share. None of the guidance includes the effect of the Mailchimp sale.

Intuit shares fell more than 10% in after-hours trading.
