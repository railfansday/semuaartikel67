Newly elected Democratic Caucus Chairman Ted Lieu, D-Calif., and progressive lawmakers told Fox News Digital that proposals to abolish ICE, expand deportation protections and pursue slavery reparations will be part of the party's legislative discussions under Speaker-nominee Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., despite concerns from moderates that the agenda could distract from affordability.

Lieu told Fox News Digital that he is "excited" to have "discussions on everything" with members of the caucus, including proposals involving reparations and abolishing ICE.

In a recent development, the California Democrat remarked he would also be willing to consider other priorities of Rep. Ayanna Presley, D-Mass., including Medicare for All, "abortion justice" and extending Temporary Protected Status for Third World migrants.

Industry observers note that rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, D-N.Y., a prominent supporter of the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA), told Fox News Digital that progressives and Jeffries have found common ground on several priorities outlined in Pressley's agenda, including expanding healthcare access and public housing.

"Of course, there are going to be areas where leader Jeffries and DSA are going to be in strong opposition, but there are also areas like expanding Medicaid, public housing, and housing issues in general, where there are shared values that unite everybody," Ocasio-Cortez, a potential 2028 presidential contender, told Fox News Digital.

Ocasio-Cortez has also begun working with Democratic leadership on the House Energy and Commerce Committee to advance her healthcare priorities, a senior congressional democratic staffer told Fox News Digital.

However, moderate Democrats were skeptical that party leadership would embrace the progressive agenda, with some questioning whether Jeffries and other leaders had promised to consider those demands in exchange for support during the leadership elections.

In a recent development, "Our agenda is to focus on costs," Rep. Josh Gottheimer, D-N.J., told Fox News Digital. "What's clear is we had an overwhelming majority. I think some of my colleagues seem to be unhappy with winning in a place we've never won before.".

Gottheimer accused progressive colleagues of wanting to "squawk and complain and make their demands" rather than focus on the complications that helped Democrats win the midterm elections.

The New Jersey Democrat also rejected proposals to "defund police, have open borders, or get rid of the United States Senate," arguing that neither he nor Democratic leadership would support such policies.
