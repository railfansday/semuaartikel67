📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sui [SUI] recently reported that over the last 30 days, the stablecoin market cap on the Sui Network expanded by 10% to $538.35 million. In comparison, the total crypto stablecoin market cap fell by around 5% to around $284.9 billion at press time.

According to recent reports, USD Coin drove the majority of this growth, according to DeFiLlama. In fact, gasless transfers on the network lowered costs and drew liquidity and DeFi activity to the ecosystem.

According to recent reports, this is not enough reason to anticipate a SUI token price bounce by itself though. Especially since the wider crypto market has suffered heavy selling pressure in recent weeks.

It has been revealed that AMBCrypto reported that the $0.827-level was an important support level and was being tested a few days ago. It was predicted that a lack of reaction at this level would mean further weakness for the token.

This came true as SUI’s reaction from around $0.82 was weak. After closing a daily session below this support level, since 12th November, the altcoin has shed 10.6% of its value in four days.

Industry observers note that the weekly chart showed that SUI already had a bearish swing structure. The bounce to $1.25 in September was part of a retracement.

There have been seven straight red weekly candles since that rally. A drop below $0.65, the June swing low, may be very likely.

Last week, the $0.82-area was swept and a bounce appeared to be underway on Monday, 9th November. The price bounce proved to be muted.

A build-up of short liquidations just under $0.90 went untapped as selling pressure intensified.

The $0.795 and $0.745 pockets, where derivatives traders bet a price bounce would commence from, were also breached. The short-term price outlook was firmly bearish.

According to recent reports, in the coming days, the $0.74-$0.76 area is expected to be a key supply zone to watch for traders to sell from. A move beyond $0.829 is needed to flip the short-term bearish bias.
