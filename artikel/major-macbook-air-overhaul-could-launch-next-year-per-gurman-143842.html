According to recent reports, apple has a fresh MacBook Air with M6 chip coming early next year, but per Mark Gurman on his latest podcast episode, a “major MacBook Air overhaul” could launch as early as 2027 too. Here’s what he noted.

Today on the latest episode of Mark Gurman’s Power On podcast, the well-sourced reporter answered a listener question that involves the future of the MacBook Air.

When asked about the next-generation MacBook Air, here’s what Gurman said:.

According to recent reports, the next MacBook Air project, J913 and J915, the 13-inch version and the 15-inch version, that is coming in spring 2027 alongside the MacBook Neo 2. And this is going to be another “chip and ship,” so a chip refresh.

And we are getting to the end of the road here for the current MacBook Air design. The current MacBook Air design was introduced in ’22, was refreshed in ’23 with the 15-inch model. We’re now in 2027. MacBook Air is Apple’s most popular computer, and they need to keep it very fresh. And so my anticipation is 2027-2028 is when we’re going to get the big MacBook Air overhaul. And I think OLED will be part of that conversation.

There are several noteworthy takeaways here:.

Recent findings suggest that it would be unusual for Apple to launch two distinct MacBook Air models in the same calendar year. However, if that does happen, it would follow the exact same playbook Apple used with the MacBook Pro this year.

Of course it's getting an 'overhaul' (whatever that means for a MacBook that will largely look and work the exact same) because it needs to replace the MacBook Pros pricing structure. Now that the Neo exists, the Air line needs to be exponentially more expensive because that's how Apple operates these days. Give you the absolute bare bones minimum at a huge premium and then absolutely soak anyone who needs anything of value.

Sources indicate that this March, a latest MacBook Pro debuted with M5 Pro and M5 Max chips but the same design that’s been around since 2021. Then in October, Apple unveiled the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR display and a new industrial design.

It sounds like a very similar rollout could happen for the MacBook Air in 2027.
