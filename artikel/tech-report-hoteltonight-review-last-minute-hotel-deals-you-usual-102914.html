It has been revealed that open HotelTonight when you need a hotel room tonight or this week and your intends are set. You get its app discounts plus 10% of the room rate back in Airbnb credit, but most rooms can’t be canceled. Its price guarantee doesn’t count a hotel’s member rate, so check the hotel’s own site before you pay.

Industry observers note that hotelTonight, an app and website owned by Airbnb since 2019, sells hotels’ unsold rooms at a discount. Its short list of hand-picked hotels suits a traveler who wants to decide fast, not one who wants to compare every hotel in town.

HotelTonight fits travelers who need a room tonight or this week and won’t change aims, once they’ve checked that booking direct isn’t cheaper. Anyone booking weeks ahead does better elsewhere, with a wider choice of hotels and a refundable rate that lets them rebook when the price drops.
