More than a decade after Grand Theft Auto V was first released, its sequel is finally launching. On November 19th, GTA VI will be available on both the PS5 and Xbox Series X / S, and it’s quite possibly the most anticipated video game launch of all time.

Sources indicate that part of this has to do with the success of its predecessor, as GTA V sold more than 230 million copies over the last 13 years. Some of it has to do with the sheer length of time between releases, a period that was plagued by leaks and a handful of delays that made the wait even harder for fans.

But GTA VI is now a real thing you can play, and you can also keep track of all of the important post-launch information right here. That includes our own review and interviews with the development team, as well as news on just how the game ends up resonating with players.

Industry observers note that lost in the excitement of GTA VI finally launching is the fact that Rockstar employees are in the process of voting on whether or not to unionize. Developer Nestor Uriarte explains the importance of the moment in an essay in The Guardian:.

In a recent development, winning union recognition would be a huge step forward in securing a meaningful say over our conditions at Rockstar, but its significance would reach far beyond that. As one of the biggest and most influential game studios in the world, what happens here could open the door for other workers across the UK to imagine an alternative future for the industry – one in which workers’ voices are taken into account more.

In a recent development, now that reviews have dropped for the game, it has skyrocketed to the top of Metacritic’s highest-rated list, surpassing Ocarina of Time (the original, not the remake). Of course, this could change as more reviews trickle in, but for now it’s safe to say that GTA VI was worth the long wait.

Grand Theft Auto VI isn’t just the biggest GTA game to date; it’s also the most romantic. Though the series is well known for its sordid assortment of criminal leads, GTA VI switches things up by starring a pair of thieves who also happen to fall in love after meeting while stealing a car. It’s still an open-world action game full of chaos and mayhem. But many players will also be attempting to nurture the budding relationship in between heists. The sprawling game is designed to support both audiences.

According to recent reports, “It’s a risk,” explains Rob Nelson, producer on GTA VI and co-studio head at developer Rockstar North. “It’s a real change for Grand Theft Auto. You can still play it the old way, you can reject all of this stuff. But for people who do engage, it’s meaningful — you’re earning it, you’re working at it, just like in a normal relationship.”.

According to recent reports, how do you follow a game like Grand Theft Auto V? Thirteen years ago, Rockstar’s crime-fueled epic went on to redefine what an open-world game could be and became an enormous success, selling more than 230 million copies across three generations of game consoles. It sits just behind Tetris and Minecraft on the list of the best-selling games of all time, and its lucrative online mode has helped it remain popular for well over a decade.

In a recent development, that level of success has allowed Rockstar to take its time with a sequel, which has further increased the hype around the next GTA. A series of major leaks and delays only exacerbated this. At a time when much of the video game industry is struggling, the next edition of GTA stands alone, a cultural moment so major (almost) everybody has been forced to get out of the way. The buildup to Grand Theft Auto VI’s launch is unlike anything the game industry has ever seen.

Sony has confirmed the 1.267GB download is mandatory to play the latest Grand Theft Auto when it introduces at midnight tonight. As with most PlayStation firmware patches, we don’t know what it does though, beyond “some security fixes to the system software.”.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar hasn’t publicly remarked who is playing the two leads in GTA VI, but The Game Awards’ nominee video claims that Manni L. Perez has been nominated for Best Performance for her work in the game.

GTA VI earned eight nominations, the most of any title, including one for Game of the Year.

Arion Kurtaj, a member of the Lapsus$ cybercrime group who leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022 and hacked other significant companies, pleaded guilty on Monday, according to the UK’s Crown Prosecution Service (CPS).

Kurtaj, who is now 21, hacked BT/EE sometime between August and November 2021, and starting in early November of that year, the organization “noted a marked increase in unauthorised SIM swaps of their customers mobile phones.” He was charged for those offenses and moved to a hotel for his “own protection” in September 2022, but “breached his bail conditions to carry out further hacks on major companies using a mobile phone, the hotel TV’s Wi-Fi and a firestick,” including hacks on Rockstar Games, Uber, and Revolut, the CPS indicates.
