Wesley Fofana is clearly on track to leave Chelsea after he angered fans during an ill-advised interview with a French YouTuber.

Fofana has struggled to make a real impact at Chelsea for a multitude of reasons since he arrived from Leicester City in 2022.

The 25-year-old has been limited to just 81 appearances for the club in the last four years but is currently a regular in Xabi Alonso's side.

His off-field problems and injury concerns have been well documented, but things have looked brighter for Fofana at Chelsea in recent months.

The Frenchman now looks to have sealed his Blues fate after a bombshell interview with a media personality from his home country.

Fans have reacted irately to his shocking comments, and it is difficult to imagine that Alonso will be best pleased either, with a sale now surely on the horizon.

Fofana, who cost Chelsea around £71million in 2022, has started seven Premier League games this season despite his inconsistent form.

The 25-year-old ranks poorly for numerous key metrics against top-flight centre-backs so far this season, with 11.5 line-breaking passes (59th percentile), 1.38 tackles (54th percentile) and 1.85 xG against while on the pitch (28th percentile) per 90 minutes.

He is not that well-liked among Blues fans because he has failed to live up to his price tag and hype, as well as off-field mishaps that have seen him accumulate over 50 driving penalty points in recent years.

Fofana's latest misstep saw him speak out of turn in an unarranged interview with French YouTuber Zack Nani, where he made light of his Carabao Cup penalty miss against Liverpool and admitted that he considered ditching Chelsea while he was out injured in the past.

He said: "On the pitch, there were a lot of young players. You had to take responsibility. If I miss it's not serious, it's definitely annoying, but if someone attacks me, it's not the first time, I don't care. I know there's another match in three days. If you win, you forget about your penalty. It's how football works.

"I missed. I had a beautiful shot, did you see it? I was shooting very high. The roof of Anfield. I remember a supporter very high up caught the ball. Yeah, I missed, now I laugh about it because it's part of football. That's how it is.

Recent findings suggest that "[After being injured] I sat down with my agents and mentioned 'I’m not going to play football anymore.' Then they told me about the contract, et cetera. They told me, 'It’s not that simple. There are years on your contract to take, there is money to take. Even if you're going to stop playing football, you're going to smile. Even if you get hurt. But you have a contract. You have to finish it. Even for your future.' But I was like, 'I don't want it anymore.' In fact, I was like, 'Football makes me more unhappy than happy. Why should I continue?'".

The 25-year-old is clearly not the right fit for Chelsea, and does not deserve his spot in their squad based on recent form and these shocking comments.
