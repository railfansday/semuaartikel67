In the opening 45 minutes, it was the Gunners who were the bullies, bringing an intensity and a level of physicality that Newcastle simply could not live with. By half-time, Arsenal had registered 18 touches in the opposition’s box. Newcastle had managed just one.

Arteta’s side led 1-0 through Eberechi Eze’s emphatic finish but it should have been more. Noni Madueke wasted two major chances. Bruno Guimaraes flung himself at a cross and turned it wide.

It has been revealed that there was no reason for this encounter to follow the trend of previous showdowns. It was a Saturday evening stroll for Arsenal and it should have remained as much.

Recent findings suggest that instead, the Gunners fell apart. Alarmingly so. All it took was one shanked volley from Martin Odegaard, letting Newcastle away on the break to equalise, and the match flipped.
