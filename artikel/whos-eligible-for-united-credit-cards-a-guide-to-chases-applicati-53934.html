Who's eligible for United credit cards? A guide to Chase's application rules

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (172 words, 📖 Standard)
  • There are six airline credit cards available, ranging from entry-level to premium options with lounge access and business versions.
  • Eligibility for these cards is straightforward: you cannot apply for the exact card you currently hold or if you received its welcome offer in the past 24 months.
  • Applicants must also be under a five-card limit for new accounts opened in the past 24 months to qualify.
  • Holding multiple cards is allowed, and each offers distinct benefits, making it possible to pair personal and business cards for additional perks.
