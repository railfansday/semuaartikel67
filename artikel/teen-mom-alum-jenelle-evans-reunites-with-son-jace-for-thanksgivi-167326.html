Former Teen Mom star Jenelle Evans is kicking off the holiday season with all three of her children.

“All my babies together 🦃🧡,” Evans, 34, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 26. “#HappyThanksgiving.”.

Industry observers note that for the special holiday, the former MTV reality star posed for photos alongside her three children. Evans shares Jace, 17, with ex Andrew Lewis, Kaiser, 12, with ex Nathan Griffith and Ensley, 9, with ex-husband David Eason.

Although Evans didn’t disclose where the family dinner took place, she “liked” several comments on Instagram from fans supporting the drama-free gathering.

It has been revealed that “They’re so so big! Time flies, beautiful!” one comment “liked” by Evans read. Another stated, “It’s great to see you and all your children together! It’s a Mommas full heart! Happy Thanksgiving!!”.

Evans’ relationship with her eldest child has made headlines over the years.

In October, Jenelle shared with her fans that Jace was thriving while staying with his grandma, Barbara Evans.

Industry observers note that “Jace is doing good. He is living with my mom,” Jenelle shared via a TikTok Live. “I know a lot of you guys are like, ‘Oh, my God, it’s because he doesn’t want to be with you’ or ‘Oh, because no one wants him to be there.’ Well, Jace is used to growing up kind of like a single child because, you know, [he was] living with my mom. And then coming to my house every weekend.”.

Recent findings suggest that the former Teen Mom 2 star continued, “When I got custody and the dynamic of him coming into a household with two other siblings, things just didn’t — and I’m not going into details — but it just did not work out, and I tried and tried and tried. And my mom tried for years. And, just, like, his mental health got the best of him.”.

Evans’ Thanksgiving celebration comes as the mother of three struggles with complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS) following a knee surgery.

The author previously told followers that she is “literally in pain from the base of my skull to my tailbone.”.
