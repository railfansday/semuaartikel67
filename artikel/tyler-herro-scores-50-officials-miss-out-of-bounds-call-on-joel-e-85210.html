Tyler Herro scores 50, officials miss out-of-bounds call on Joel Embiid in 76ers' 132-129 win over Bucks

Tyler Herro scored a career-high 50 points Saturday, but that wasn't enough for the Milwaukee Bucks to get their second win of the season in a 132-129 defeat to the Philadelphia 76ers.

Milwaukee was deprived of an opportunity to take the lead in the closing seconds when officials missed Joel Embiid stepping out of bounds.

Industry observers note that rookie Brayden Burries hit a corner 3-pointer for the Bucks to cut the Sixers' lead to 130-129. After a timeout, LeBron James threw an inbounds pass to Embiid, who attempted to dribble out the clock near the Milwaukee basket. Embiid stepped out of bounds at the baseline with his left foot, but the officials did not call the turnover and Herro was forced to foul.

The Bucks could not challenge the non-call because they were out of replay challenges.

Embiid made his two free throws to put Philadelphia ahead by 3, but the Bucks still had 1.5 seconds remaining to try for a tying basket.

Herro received the inbounds pass from Kyle Kuzma at the top of the arc and took one dribble to break free from VJ Edgecombe. Shooting from the right elbow, Herro's 3-pointer went in. However, the shot left his hand after the buzzer.

It has been revealed that the eighth-year guard shot 15 for 23 from the floor, including 7 of 13 on 3-pointers. Herro also hit all 11 of his free throws, adding 11 rebounds and seven assists. Burries finished with 26 points off the bench with 8 rebounds and four assists. Kuzma was the third Bucks player to reach double figures, scoring 12 with five boards.

Herro's previous career-high was 41 points, reached in a December 2022 win over the Houston Rockets while he was with the Miami Heat.

Milwaukee lost its 13th consecutive game to drop to 1-16 on the season, the worst mark in the NBA. The Bucks' only win came nearly a month ago on Oct. 26, a 110-102 victory over the Indiana Pacers. Up next for them is the Toronto Raptors (6-10) at home on Monday.

Industry observers note that embiid led the Sixers with 26 points, nearly registering a triple-double with 17 rebounds and nine assists. James added 21 points with 6 boards, 6 assists and 3 steals. Seven Philadelphia players scored in double figures, with Anfernee Simons getting 18 off the bench and Tyrese Maxey tallying 17 points.

The 76ers improved to 14-3 with the win, tying the Los Angeles Lakers for the NBA's top record. Philadelphia has won five consecutive games and next faces the Portland Trail Blazers (10-5) on Monday.
