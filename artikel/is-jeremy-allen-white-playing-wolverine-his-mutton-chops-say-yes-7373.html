📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, marvel has filled most of the seats for its latest X-Men film. Sadie Sink is Jean Grey, Kit Connor is Cyclops, Christopher Abbott is Professor X, Samara Weaving is Emma Frost, Inde Navarrette is Rogue, and Maya Boyd is Storm. But there’s still one grizzly gap in the line-up: Wolverine.

According to recent reports, earlier this month, Jeremy Allen White was linked with the role after insider Jeff Sneider reported that he had met with Marvel about a small part that could become much bigger later on. Nobody has confirmed that the part is Wolverine (and Sneider has since stressed that White isn’t officially in talks) but the internet did the math pretty rapidly. And now there’s a smoking gun: facial hair.

According to recent reports, while walking around the Studio City Farmers Market over the weekend (because, of course), The Social Reckoning star was spotted carrying a bouquet of flowers bigger than his torso and wearing some extremely Wolverine-y mutton chops. Normally, growing out your sideburns wouldn’t count as casting news. But when you’ve just been linked with Logan and suddenly look ready to join the X-Men, people are going to ask questions.

It has been revealed that white would make a lot of sense. He’s 5’7”, which puts him much closer to the comic book Wolverine’s famously stocky 5’3” frame than Hugh Jackman ever was. He also spent months getting absolutely massive for The Iron Claw, so the muscle is already there. And after years of watching him play Carmy in The Bear, we know he can do angry and exhausted and permanently one minor inconvenience away from losing it. That’s basically three quarters of the Wolverine job description.

In a recent development, jackman has owned the role since the first X-Men film landed in 2000. He’s played Wolverine across 10 movies, most recently returning for 2024’s Deadpool & Wolverine. His version became so definitive that it’s difficult to picture anyone else with the claws (other than the time Troye Sivan briefly had them in 2009’s X-Men Origins: Wolverine), even though the 6’2" actor looked very different from the shorter character drawn in the OG comics.

Maybe White just fancied some giant sideburns. Maybe the flowers are covering three adamantium claws. Either way, Marvel should probably give him a call—assuming it hasn’t already.
