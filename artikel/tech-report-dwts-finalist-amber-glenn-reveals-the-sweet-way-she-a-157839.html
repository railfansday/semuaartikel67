All season long, the "Dancing with the Stars" judges recognized Amber Glenn's competitive nature, but now that the season has come to an end, the Olympic figure skater is proving that she has been a team player all along.

Glen came in second place in the competition dance series, with two-time Paralympian Ezra Frech taking home the coveted Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy.

And while the contestants share a spot on Team USA—for which they've both brought home gold medals—they swiftly bonded in the competition as their professional dance partners, Pasha Pashkov and Daniella Karagach, are married.

When asked how she felt about the results after the finale, Glenn told Extra she "couldn't ask for a better result," and described a "triumphant feeling" as she walked away from Season 35 having earned 10s across the board in all three of her final performances.

"From day one, we all met up, had a little dinner over at their house, and we mentioned, 'We wanna go 1-2,'" she explained to the outlet. "And we didn't specify, like, whatever happens, happens, but the fact that we came in here and we both got those perfect scores. We both did everything we could and it was truly in the hands of the people at home. I'm really proud of what we did and I'm really, really happy that Team USA took it home.".

The group—who have been unofficially dubbed "Team USA and the Pashkovs" by fans and even the official U.S. Olympic and Paralmypic team all season—have been close collaborators throughout the competition.

During the Nov. 10 episode, when teams were paired off and required to compete in two-on-two dance-offs, Glenn and Pashkov and Frech and Karagach put a friendly spin on the format by switching partners mid-choreography as they performed a Rumba to "The Boy Is Mine" by Brandy and Monica.

Sources indicate that "It felt more like a team dance than anything else," Karagach told Us magazine. "We came together ,and we had the best time sharing the dance floor, and we couldn't also not end swapping partners because I've always wanted to dance with Amber, and Ezra and Pasha secretly wanted to dance with each other. So that was fun.".

The competitors-turned-pals took to social media to share plenty of heartwarming behind-the-scenes moments throughout the experience, providing a glimpse at what their down time is like between rehearsals. In one Sept. 18 post, Pashkov wrote, "We're a tightly-knit fam now.".

Sources indicate that in a post-finale interview with Deadline, Glenn described the moment she had realized Frech would win. The Plano, TX native remarked, "I don't know if I felt as many emotions watching a performance as I did when watching Ezra and Dani. It was like, at first, it's excitement. I'm like, 'I love that Linkin Park [song].' And then horror, fear, and like, panic.'".

In a recent development, the moment Glenn was referring to was the malfunction in which Frech's prosthetic fell off mid-performance. Frech and Karagach promptly communicated while dancing, allowing him to secure it into place properly and complete the routine.

"I think I gasped so loud I threw my rib out again," Glenn continued. "So then, the physical pain and just like, fear, panic, and then just awe, inspiration, something you feel in your core. I know that's a saying, but like you feel it in your gut, and it takes your breath away, and that's what it was.".

Glenn stated that in that moment, she "was like, 'They won.'" She even added that she "would feel bad" if she had taken home the trophy after seeing Frech and Karagach pull off "such a perfect dance" after "working so hard for it all season.".
