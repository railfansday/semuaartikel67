Tech Report: Anthropic’s $81 Billion IPO Is the Biggest Ever — Here’s Who’s Getting Rich and Who Missed Out

🌐 Source: Entrepreneur • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (424 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Anthropic, the tech giant behind the popular AI chatbot, surged more than 30% in its Nasdaq debut today after the largest IPO in history.
  • The biggest winners of the Anthropic IPO are the tech giant’s seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei and his sister, president Daniela Amodei.
  • The FTX founder led Anthropic’s $580 million Series B in 2022.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic, the tech giant behind the popular AI chatbot, surged more than 30% in its Nasdaq debut today after the largest IPO in history. It sold more than 980 million shares at $83 apiece for $81.3 billion, topping the then-record $75 billion SpaceX raised in June, according to The Latest York Times. Shares opened at $111.50 under the ticker “ANT,” valuing Anthropic at about $2 trillion.

The biggest winners of the Anthropic IPO are the tech giant’s seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei and his sister, president Daniela Amodei. Each holds a stake that could be worth $20 billion or more, according to the Times. On the VC side, Lightspeed Venture Partners and Iconiq hold stakes worth more than $25 billion at the IPO price, The Wall Street Journal reports. Menlo Ventures’ stake is worth roughly $22.5 billion. Amazon, Google and Gulf sovereign wealth funds are also in line for huge paydays.

Then there’s Sam Bankman-Fried. The FTX founder led Anthropic’s $580 million Series B in 2022. After FTX collapsed, its bankruptcy estate sold the $500 million stake in 2024 for about $1.3 billion. At the IPO price, those shares would be worth roughly $37 billion today. China was also locked out of the offering.
