If you're trying to get the best gaming performance possible at a reasonable price-to-performance ratio, this card is the way to go.

Whether you're targeting raster or RT gaming, this card has the strong baseline performance necessary for a good time at 1440p and even 4K. And you can rest easy knowing that this card's superior DLSS 4.5 upscaling and framegen features are implemented in virtually every game, so you can tune your experience to taste in most any title you want to play.
