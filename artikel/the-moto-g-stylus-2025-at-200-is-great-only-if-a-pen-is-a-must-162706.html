Okay, so here’s the deal (literally): last year’s Moto G Stylus (2025) is on sale right now on Amazon for just $199, and if you desperately need a digital writing tool with your phone, it’s a good buy.

It has been revealed that that is the short answer; the long answer is a little more complicated, as due to some challenges with the 2026 model, you are missing out on exactly…nothing. Yes, at under $200, you are getting essentially the same phone because Motorola decided that, due to RAM and storage shortages and price hikes, the newer model would forgo any major upgrades. The 2026 model starts at $499, so if you had your eye on it, yeah, it’s a silly way to waste $300 in our opinion.

In a recent development, it has the same Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 processor, 8GB RAM, and 128GB storage. There’s a 120Hz display too and the good news is you get a microSD card slot to expand the storage if you desperately need it. Other notable features include a 5,000mAh battery, 68W wired charging, 15W wireless charging, a 50MP camera setup, and, as of writing, it should be due for an Android 16 upgrade.

According to recent reports, the star of the show of the Moto G Stylus (2025) is the stylus. It boils down to this: if you want a phone that isn’t a Galaxy Ultra, then this is the only other line that makes sense for document signers.

However, there are better deals out there. I think the Galaxy A56, at just $299, is an incredible offer right now and way more deserving of your hard-earned cash. Even so, if you want clean software or that stylus, the Moto G Stylus (2025) at $199 is at least an option especially for a family member or a backup to your existing phone.
