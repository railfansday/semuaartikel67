Black Friday is the time to stock up on expensive devices that you can never quite bring yourself to buy otherwise. That means devices like power stations, which are incredibly practical — a must-have item in this day and age, you could say — but they seem so boringly practical that it's hard to fork out significant bucks. There's very little pleasure in spending $1,500 (or perhaps even more!) on a power station. But with hundreds of dollars shaved off in the Amazon Black Friday sale, now's the time to buy one.

Here's a unique power station that we recommend checking out while it's discounted: the Jackery Fridge Guard, which Mashable reviewer Laura Allain liked very much.

As of Nov. 22, the Jackery Fridge Guard is down to $1,099, saving you $260 off the regular list price of $1,359. In fact, it's the Jackery Fridge Guard's first Amazon discount of any kind. The best tech deal this week The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile.

In a recent development, as the name suggests, Jackery Fridge Guard is designed specifically to keep your refrigerator working during power outages. It will keep the fridge running for up to 30 hours when you use a battery expansion (which comes included with this particular listing). That means your fridge will stay cool and your food won't spoil.

All you need to do is plug it into the mains and then plug your fridge directly into the Jackery Fridge Guard. When the power goes down, the Fridge Guard kicks in. And when the power returns, the Fridge Guard recharges, ready for the next power emergency. It's slim and relatively lightweight and sits right on top of the fridge.

It's worth noting that, due to having just one AC connection, this is only really designed for use with one appliance. And not necessarily your fridge. You could also use it for your WiFi router, for instance, but it won't be much good for taking on the road like you might a regular power station.

It has been revealed that the Jackery Fridge Guard is now available for $1,099 as part of the Amazon Black Friday sale. Don't miss your chance to save $260 while keeping your fridge powered up at all times.
