Luis Suarez believes "the time for excuses is over" for Inter Miami as they prepare for a must-win playoff clash with the Columbus Crew.

Miami fell to a 3-0 defeat in Game 1 of their best-of-three series, with Gonzalo Lujan receiving an early red card for the Herons.

Cristian Gonzalez's side struggled after going a man down, accumulating just 0.99 expected goals (xG) to the Crew's 1.97.

The Herons' MLS Cup title defence could be over as soon as Friday if they lose at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field, while victory would see them force Game 3 back in Miami.

Suarez has urged his Miami team-mates "to deliver" in their win-or-go-home matchup against Columbus.

"It's clear we are at a club where we have an obligation to win," said Suarez.

In a recent development, "If we get eliminated on Friday, it will be a disappointment. We have to be that clear about it. The time for excuses is over. It's time to face reality.

"If we are eliminated, we will be the first to be self-critical, although we hope that doesn’t happen.

“I believe the squad are at a point where we understand that it’s time to deliver.

It has been revealed that "This is our final opportunity. This format has given us another chance to play this Friday, and it's the last shot we have to bring the series back home.".

Miami boss Gonzalez shared the same views as his striker, and hopes his players will show their resilience in Game 2.

"In soccer, it's not about failure; it's about trying," mentioned Gonzalez, who took the helm in late August.

Sources indicate that "Sometimes things go well, sometimes they don't – that's just life. I know the responsibility lies with us; it's our obligation as a team, as the reigning champions, to advance.

"It's not about feeling pressured, but rather acknowledging that we are at a club built to reach these stages.

Sources indicate that "There are moments when you have to dig deep, tap into that sense of self-respect and the passion that drives you. That’s how I see the game, and I truly hope we can demonstrate that this Friday.".

Taha Habroune scored his first career playoff goal in Game 1, becoming the youngest player to score a playoff goal (20y 289d) for the Columbus Crew.

Habroune surpassed the previous record held by Edson Buddle (21y 127d) in September 2002, against the San Jose Earthquakes.

Lionel Messi recorded a goal and three assists in his last road playoff game in November 2025, against FC Cincinnati.

Messi is one of two players in MLS history to register four or more goal contributions in such games.

Despite a comfortable win for Columbus in Game 1, the Opta supercomputer expects a closer encounter, which could be decided by penalties, this time around.

With their 3-0 Game 1 win, Columbus have won consecutive games in the same season against Miami for the first time in club history.

The Crew have won both of their meetings with the Herons outside of the regular season (Game 1 and 2024 Leagues Cup Round of 16).

Saturday's victory was Columbus' 30th MLS Cup playoff victory. The Crew are the second team in MLS history with at least 30 playoff wins, joining the Los Angeles Galaxy (51).

Miami scored more goals in that match than in their previous four away playoff matches combined (two).
