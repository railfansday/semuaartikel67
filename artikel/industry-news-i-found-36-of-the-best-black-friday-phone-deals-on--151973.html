In a recent development, this holiday season, the best Black Friday phone deals help offset the price hikes that have affected almost every brand. A fresh phone is more expensive these days, but it's easy to avoid paying full price if you shop strategically.

In fact, you might pay as little as $0 by upgrading through your carrier. The only catch is that their deals typically require a trade-in and a lock-in to an extended contract. For a upcoming phone with no strings attached, go the unlocked route instead.

Industry observers note that retailers like Amazon and Best Buy are presently discounting unlocked smartphones from Apple, Samsung, Google, Motorola, and Nothing during their Black Friday sales. After spending a few hours combing through their deals, I've identified three dozen good ones that can save you up to $600. Whether you want a upcoming phone that takes incredible pictures, offers multi-day battery life, flips, folds, or comes in well under $500, you've got plenty of options.

The ultra-thin iPhone Air is the best choice "for minimalists and aesthetes who care more about their phone's looks than its performance," to quote my iPhone buying guide. It's not the fastest or longest-lasting iPhone, nor does it have the best cameras, but it is a gorgeous phone. (Mashable's Senior Editor Stan Schroeder called it "the sexiest iPhone I've ever tested.").

Thanks to Best Buy, the base model now costs $100 less than the standard iPhone 17; it's usually $200 more.
