The Commission just granted a multi-faceted sanction package going ham on Russia’s various uses of crypto.

The European Commission’s 22nd sanctions package against Russia extends the European Union’s (EU) transaction ban to over 60 Russian banks and indicates all 60 of those will be removed from SWIFT, President Ursula von der Leyen said in Strasbourg on 23 November 2026.

This comes just weeks after Russia officially legalized crypto currency trading, starting with the substantial-cap coins like Bitcoin (BTC) & Ethereum (ETH). In the 22nd sanction packet, Russian-linked crypto services & platforms were specifically named as banned for transactions.

“The measures include the possibility for EU firms to sue their non-Russian contractual parties in the EU to recover damages.”, – states the document. “We will continue to propose fresh sanctions until Russia shows genuine intent for a lasting peace in Ukraine”, further said the Chief Commissioner.

Industry observers note that with this package, we are increasing the pressure.We are striking harder at Russia’s ways to finance its war, by targeting its banks and crypto platforms.We are further isolating Russia. While focusing on Russian SWIFT sanctions, the EU added 51 entities to the anti-circumvention list, while the majority of them, 27, were outside Russia: 14 came from China, including four in Hong Kong, four in Türkiye, three in Kyrgyzstan, two in India, two in Kazakhstan and two more in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

It has been revealed that the European Union (EU) freshly assembled the Crypto Task Force to counter new crypto-related ways of evading the huge pile of sanctions, including limitations of Russian business abroad via ‘third-world’ parties. The Council will decide when the ban enters into force, after the necessary measures are activated.
