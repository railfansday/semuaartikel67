Windstar Cruises has confirmed that the organization has welcomed Pierre van Breda as its director of global charters and incentives.

In his role, van Breda will help lead the growth of the firm’s global charter and incentive business, focusing on strategic partnerships, new market development and expanding opportunities across corporate charters, incentive programs, affinity groups and special events.

van Breda will work alongside Amy Conover, Windstar’s director of charters and incentive business, and the North American charter team to further develop the company’s global strategy, strengthen operational processes and identify fresh revenue opportunities.

“This is an exciting next chapter for Windstar as we continue to build on the success of our charter business,” remarked Janet Bava, chief commercial officer at Windstar Cruises.

“The expansion of our fleet and introduction of shorter sailings have opened new opportunities for corporate incentives, destination weddings and private celebrations, demonstrating just how versatile the small-ship experience can be.

Industry observers note that “With Pierre joining our team, we’re bringing in a proven industry leader who understands how to turn those opportunities into lasting partnerships. His appointment is part of a broader investment in our sales organization as we continue to strengthen our presence in key markets,” added Bava.

The company said in a press release that van Breda, originally from South Africa, brings 19 years of cruise industry experience, including more than a decade specializing in charter, incentive and group sales.

Most recently, he served as director of global charter and incentives sales and GSA Partnerships at Seabourn and Holland America Line.

His career began aboard what is now Windstar’s Star Legend. With experience spanning onboard operations, international business development and strategic partnerships, van Breda brings a deep understanding of the global charter and incentive travel market.
