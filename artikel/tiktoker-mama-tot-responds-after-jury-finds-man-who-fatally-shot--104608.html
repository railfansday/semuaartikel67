TikTok star Ophelia Nichols, better known as Mama Tot, has spoken out after the man who fatally shot her teenage son was acquitted of murder.

Nichols, who has amassed over 12 million followers on TikTok, lost her 18-year-old son Randon Lee on June 24, 2022, after he was shot during an arranged drug deal outside a gas station in Prichard, Alabama, just one day before his 19th birthday.

On November 20, 2026, a jury found 25-year-old Reuben Gulley not guilty of murder following roughly 90 minutes of deliberations. Gulley had admitted to shooting Lee but maintained that he acted in self-defense.

In a post on Facebook following the verdict, Nichols described watching her husband break down in tears inside the courtroom.

“I have watched my husband break down sitting in that courtroom today while I did my best to hold it together for myself, for him, my children, our entire family,” Nichols wrote.

Recent findings suggest that “As I could feel the excited energy from the other side because their son was coming home, I slowly melted into my seat. Knowing that Justice had been stripped from my son and us, I couldn’t even describe what it did to my soul.”.

Industry observers note that during the trial, Gulley testified that he had entered the back seat of Lee’s car to purchase two ounces of marijuana for $300. He claimed Lee pulled a gun on him after he took out the money, prompting him to open fire.

Prosecutors argued that Gulley shot the teenager to avoid paying for the drugs. Surveillance footage indicated him fleeing Lee’s vehicle with a gun in his hand, although it did not capture the shooting itself.

Gulley’s defense attorney, Chase Dearman, questioned the police investigation and argued there was insufficient evidence to convict his client.

Following the acquittal, Nichols stated her family would continue to question how the jury reached its decision.

Industry observers note that “We don’t know what, why or how the jurors came to that decision and I’m sure we will question that moment the rest of our lives,” she wrote. “No remorse for our family. Zero regards for Randon’s life. What was done to our family was downright cruel and knowing that we are not alone in these situations is scary.”.

The TikToker also described the emotional toll the verdict had taken on her husband and daughter.

“I watched my daughter cry her eyes out today, I’ve only seen my husband cry like three times in the 20 years we’ve been married and he couldn’t control himself today,” she said. “He lost it.”.
