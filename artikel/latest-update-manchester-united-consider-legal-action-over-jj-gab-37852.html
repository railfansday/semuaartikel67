Manchester United are prepared to consider legal action if evidence emerges that another club approached academy forward JJ Gabriel without permission.

In a recent development, the 16-year-old announced his departure from Old Trafford in a social media video on Tuesday and is expected to join Real Madrid in January. According to The Times and ESPN, United remain concerned about the circumstances surrounding his proposed move, although no breach of transfer regulations has been established.

According to recent reports, gabriel has been absent from United since September, when he requested the cancellation of his registration. The teenager remains registered with the club, and United could explore legal or regulatory options if evidence of an unauthorised approach emerges.

The Independent reports that United do not want to punish Gabriel for actions they believe may have been taken by adults involved in his future. Real Madrid have been widely linked with the forward, but his transfer has not been formally completed.

Gabriel joined United’s academy in 2020 and was named the club’s young player of the year last season. He featured for the senior side during pre-season but has not made a competitive first-team appearance.

According to recent reports, the youngster is eligible for a move within the European Union under an exception to FIFA’s rules on international transfers involving players aged under 18. In his farewell message, Gabriel thanked United for helping him develop during his six years at the club.

According to recent reports, his expected move to Madrid would bring that association to an end, although questions remain over the process leading to his departure. United have not declared legal proceedings, and there has been no finding of wrongdoing by Real Madrid or any other club.
