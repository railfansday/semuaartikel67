Latest Update: Which restaurant reservation platform offers the best rewards: OpenTable, DoorDash, Seated or Resy?

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (226 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Dining reservation platforms like OpenTable, DoorDash, Seated and Resy now offer rewards for booking and completing restaurant reservations, each with distinct earning and redemption models.
  • OpenTable awards points redeemable for dining credits or experiences, DoorDash provides credits for future orders, Seated offers a percentage back as rewards for gift cards and Resy grants additional loyalty points at participating venues.
  • The value and structure of rewards vary: OpenTable is widely available but offers lower returns, DoorDash is best for smaller bills, Seated can be most lucrative for larger spends, and Resy delivers a strong return but is currently limited in scope.
  • All programs are free to join, but eligibility, reward expiration and redemption options differ, so choosing the best platform depends on your dining habits and location.
  • Some programs require app activations or enrollment steps, and certain benefits are only available in select cities or on specific devices.
  • Rewards may expire after periods of inactivity or have usage restrictions, such as not covering taxes and fees or being limited to certain brands for gift card redemption.
  • Stacking reservation rewards with eligible payment methods can further enhance overall value, but terms and availability vary by platform.
