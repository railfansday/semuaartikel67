📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

You don’t need us to tell you that Salomon sneakers are perfect for low-key weekend looks. You will, however, appreciate us scoping out a few on sale this Black Friday.

Industry observers note that for years, Salomon’s rugged trail hikers were reserved for outdoorsy pursuits; they’ve become mainstays in a modern everyday wardrobe (particularly those of models off-duty) for a few reasons, chief among them their sporty look, which signals a cool readiness for whatever the day may bring. It certainly helps, too, that the shoe’s main design features—a grippy sole, a waterproof upper, and a quick-lace system—all allow for easy, versatile wear no matter the weather.

Recent findings suggest that but it’s not just models who love this particular sneaker: designer Jonathan Anderson has made Salomon sneakers an integral part of his uniform, which typically consists of straight-leg Levi’s, a simple crew-neck sweater—often in navy blue or green—and a white T-shirt peeking out just below the collar. (When Anderson’s not in a knit, you can find him in a plaid button-down.) Many stylish A-listers—including Emily Ratajkowski, Kendall Jenner, and Jennifer Lawrence—have also sported the shoe on off-duty days.

Beyond playing well with jeans, Salomon sneakers also happen to look great with more sporty separates, like voluminous track pants and leggings. They have just enough heft to add character to an outfit without throwing off the proportions.

I’m partial to a neutral (black or beige) for everyday, especially if I’m thinking about pairing them with a tailored coat—but the shoes reward the adventurous, and thanks to these Black Friday sales, experimenting with color is a little easier.
