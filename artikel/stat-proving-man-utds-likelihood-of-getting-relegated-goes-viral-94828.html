It has been revealed that it seems wild to even consider, but Manchester United are in a relegation fight right now. After 11 Premier League games, the Red Devils sit one point and one place above the bottom of the table.

Michael Carrick managed to win just one game, picking up eight points from a possible 30 before he was sacked. Mauricio Pochettino has replaced the Englishman, but any hopes of an immediate upcoming-manager bouncequickly evaporated at Anfield over the weekend.

Sources indicate that man Utd were trounced 4-0 by their bitter rivals. After the game, Pochettino conceded his team were in a relegation battle, stating: "Relegation battle? That is our reality, no…? We are there. We need to win games.".

There are all sorts of stats to tell you just how badly things are going for the Red Devils. As per TNT Sports, for instance, across Europe's top five leagues this season, no side has conceded more goals than Man United (34).

Sources indicate that moreover, Pochettino's 4-0 debut loss to Liverpool was the joint-worst result for any upcoming United boss, matching the 4-0 loss Lal Hilditch suffered against Bolton in October 1926. The English coach lasted 30 games and never managed another team.

But there is one stat that really captures the doom and gloom at Old Trafford right now. Indeed, a post from ESPN writer Mark Ogden has gone viral as it outlines just how likely Man Utd are to be relegated from the Premier League this term. He wrote:.

"Spurs had 18 points after 11 games last season and stayed up on the final day. United have 8 points from 11 games.".

Spurs avoided the drop by two points, with West Ham going down instead. Last term also saw Burnley and Wolves relegated with ease. Things could be a lot tighter in 2026/27, though, with no obvious candidate to go down as things stand.

The post has almost two million views on X and has generated thousands of likes and hundreds of comments. One noted: "Penny beginning to drop, United are in serious trouble.".

Another said: "Most people haven’t clocked it yet but we are actually getting relegated.".

And a third added: "This is when it really hits home." Someone else summed up the mood, noting: "This is bleak for United.".

Sources indicate that if Man Utd do go down, it won't be uncharted territory for the club. While they've not been relegated in the Premier League era, they have suffered that misery on five occasions across their history.

The first came in 1893/94, when they were still known as Newton Heath, and finished bottom of the First Division. Relegations then followed in 1921/22, 1930/31 and 1936/37.

The most famous and most recent relegation came just six years after they became the first English club to win the European Cup. In the final round of fixturesfor the 1973/74 season, club legend Denis Law scored for rivals Man City as his former team were beaten 1-0 in the Manchester Derby.

In a recent development, law barely reacted, but later described himself as "inconsolable" as Man Utd were relegated. Perhaps it came as some small consolation that Law's goal didn't end up sending the Red Devils down. Birmingham City's win over Norwich City and West Ham's draw with Liverpool meant United were relegated regardless.

Neutrals will be hoping for something just as dramatic in 2026/27.
