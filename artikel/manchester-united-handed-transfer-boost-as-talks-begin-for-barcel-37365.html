Manchester United could well have the chance to sign Alejandro Balde from Barcelona in January.

The Red Devils are desperate for cover at left-back with Luke Shaw and Patrick Dorgu both hampered by injury, and Noussair Mazraoui and Diogo Dalot, both natural right-backs, used in that position as deputies.

Mauricio Pochettino has started work as United’s new manager and will be looking to reinforce his options come the upcoming winter transfer window, but it remains to be seen how far he is backed so early.

Balde, 23, has previously been linked with a move to Old Trafford, but is thought to have turned down an exit from Barcelona, citing his desire to prove himself to manager Hansi Flick.

The La Masia academy product has struggled for game time this term, and could now be looking at a departure, but this time on loan.

RAC claims that the player’s agent, Jorge Mendes, is holding conversations with Barcelona about his position and a potential move, with a loan deal not out of the question.

The sticking point with a loan move is how much the loaning club will pay of the player’s wages, and it is thought that Barca could be open to an agreement where then Premier League side wouldn’t have to pay a significant share of Balde’s salary.

It is belived that the La Liga club are so desperate to let the defender leave - so they can free up space in their wage budget for new signings - that they could agree to a deal that would favour United considerably.

Pochettino takes charge of his first game in charge of United on Sunday, when they take on Liverpool at Anfield.
