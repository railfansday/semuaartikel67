25-year-old set for starting XI recall as Sheffield United bid to end 17-year Southampton hoodoo

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sheffield United face Southampton in the Championship tonight. The Blades come into the game in good form, having defeated Watford 4-0 on Saturday.

Recent findings suggest that SUFC have lost one of their last six league matches, and at Bramall Lane, they’ll be eager to give the promotion-pushing Saints a good test. However, Chris Wilder will have to undo a 17-year hoodoo to do so, and he’ll be without captain and key defender Japhet Tanganga for the tie.

Tanganga picked up his fifth booking of the season in the Watford victory and will serve his one-match suspension against Southampton. It means that 25-year-old Mark McGuinness is set to be recalled to the Sheffield United starting XI as they look for a first league point against the South Coast side since 2009.

Sources indicate that mcGuinness was touted for a move away from Bramall Lane in the summer, but he ended up staying put. He’s had a part to play at Sheffield United despite the past uncertainty too, starting seven Championship games so far this term.

However, summer signing Liam Kitching has become the preferred centre-back partner for skipper Tanganga. That partnership has been increasingly impressive, providing a solid defensive foundation for SUFC to build on in their improved form of late.

In a recent development, but, barring a surprise call to start Jamal Baptiste for the first time since September 19th, it will be McGuinness who lines up against Southampton. The Irishman will hope his strong aerial presence and no-nonsense, box-guarding defensive style can help keep the Saints and their dangerous attackers at bay.

The likely partnership of McGuinness and Kitching will have to deal with the fit-again Cyle Larin, who scored on his return to the side against Derby County on Saturday. The Canadian international now has nine goals to his name in 14 games across all competitions this season.

Sheffield United are in for a tough task tonight, there can be no illusions over that.

Southampton have history on their side in this fixture with six consecutive league wins over the Blades since 2009. The last time SUFC were triumphant in this fixture, a late goal from Jamie Ward secured a 2-1 triumph at St Mary’s.

Recent findings suggest that 17 years on, the Saints are under Tonda Eckert‘s lead and pushing for Championship promotion. They sit 6th in the table, but would be 3rd if it wasn’t for their four-point deduction as part of the Spygate saga.

In a recent development, the visitors are the favourites, but they are not invincible away from home – far from it, in fact. Southampton have won two, drawn three and lost three of their eight league matches on the road this term, so SUFC certainly have an opening there.

McGuinness will have to step up and fill Tanganga’s shoes to deal with Larin and the rest of the Saints’ attacking threats. If they can do so, Sheffield United might just pull off an impressive win on Tuesday night.
