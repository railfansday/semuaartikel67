Burger King unveils Snoopy meals for adults and kids with exclusive Peanuts collectibles

Burger King has declared a new collaboration with Peanuts, bringing Snoopy, Charlie Brown, and the rest of the gang to its restaurants for the holiday season with limited-time meals and exclusive collectibles.

The fast food chain revealed the partnership on November 19, confirming that the holiday menu will launch at participating US locations on December 1, 2026. The promotion includes fresh food items, collectible packaging, and special merchandise for both adults and children.

The collaboration comes as Peanuts celebrates more than 75 years since its debut in 1950, with Burger King introducing two different meal options inspired by the franchise’s characters.

The main offering is the Peanuts Pack, which comes inside a collectible Snoopy Doghouse Box and includes one of two exclusive Snoopy holiday beanies.

Customers can choose between a Whopper with Cheese or 10-piece Chicken Nuggets, with the latter served alongside a fresh Woodstock Golden Ranch dipping sauce.

The meal also introduces Charlie Brown Loaded Potato Tots, featuring crispy, coin-shaped potatoes filled with cheese, bacon bits, sour cream, and chives.

It has been revealed that each Peanuts Pack comes with one of two collectible Snoopy beanies. One features a brown knit design with Snoopy resting on his red doghouse, while the other is red and depicts Snoopy wearing a Santa hat.

Recent findings suggest that burger King is also introducing a Peanuts-inspired King Jr. Meal, which includes a hamburger, cheeseburger, or four-piece Chicken Nuggets, alongside a choice of fries or applesauce and milk or apple juice.

Each kids’ meal comes in its own Snoopy Doghouse Box and includes one of eight collectible Peanuts toys.

Burger King launches Hello Kitty kids meals with rare golden collectible toys.

Burger King unveils bizarre new burger with popcorn as a topping.

Burger King is releasing a Chocolate Whopper packed with dessert toppings.

The collection features Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally, three different holiday-themed Snoopy figures, and a Snoopy Doghouse that comes with stickers for customization.

“Peanuts has been part of holiday family traditions for generations, and we can’t wait to bring Snoopy and the gang to Burger King this holiday season!” remarked Joel Yashinsky, Chief Marketing Officer of Burger King US and Canada.

“At BK, we go all out on partnerships to create fun experiences for everyone – with upcoming menu items, creative packaging and crowns, collectible Snoopy beanies and Peanuts toys and more, we’ve created something really special. It’s holiday fun for all, and we can’t wait to welcome families and fans to BK to celebrate throughout the month of December!”.

Both meals will be available beginning December 1 at participating Burger King restaurants across the US, through the company’s website, and via its mobile app while supplies last.

Burger King has not unveiled pricing for either meal or specified when the promotion will end.

The Peanuts collaboration isn’t the only holiday-themed fast food promotion arriving in December.

McDonald’s has also announced a limited-time Home Alone Meal launching December 1, featuring Little Nero’s Pizza McShaker Fries and collectible socks inspired by the holiday movie. The promotion also brings Macaulay Culkin back as Kevin McCallister as part of the chain’s seasonal campaign.
