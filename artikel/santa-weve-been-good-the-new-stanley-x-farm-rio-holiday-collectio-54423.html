Stanley is bringing a little tropical sunshine to the holiday season. The drinkware brand is teaming up with FARM Rio once again for a colorful latest collection. The new drop features 14 products (yes, 14!) inspired by the Brazilian fashion brand’s signature vibrant prints. Think festive drinkware and giftable accessories dressed in FARM Rio’s signature bold colors, tropical florals and playful patterns.

According to recent reports, stanley fans already know and love the brand’s iconic Quencher tumblers, but their special-edition releases have given us plenty of other reasons to shop, too. From their first collaboration with FARM Rio earlier this summer to their Kacey Musgraves collection, Stanley has a knack for making drinkware feel like a must-have accessory.

Sources indicate that the new Stanley 1913 x FARM Rio collection drops Tuesday, November 17 at 9 a.m. PST/12 p.m. EST. Here’s what we’re eyeing from the festive new lineup.

This latest drop brings festive tropical prints to classic drinkware and gift-ready sets just in time for the holidays.

Sources indicate that stanley 1913 and FARM Rio first teamed up in August for a colorful summer collection that brought the Brazilian fashion brand’s signature tropical prints to Stanley’s iconic drinkware. Now, the brands are back for a second edition—this time with a festive holiday twist.

And this collaboration is even bigger. The holiday collection expands well beyond the previous drop, with a variety of FARM Rio prints across six sizes, ranging from the iconic 40-ounce Quencher FlowState tumblers to the petite 10-ounce stackable cups.

Industry observers note that drinkware is just the beginning, too. The 14-piece lineup also includes festive ornaments, a wine set and other giftable finds. Whether you’re sipping something cold, keeping your coffee hot, pouring a glass of wine or decorating the tree with festive ornaments, there’s something for Stanley collectors and FARM Rio fans alike.

Industry observers note that tropical birds, vibrant florals and a little holiday magic? It’s no wonder the Snowy Rio Tumbler stole our hearts. The festive print is packed with colorful details, with pops of red, green and pink that give it a fun holiday feel. And the coordinating Macaw Straw Topper is the cherry on top—or should we say, bird on top? It’s a cheerful take on the classic Stanley tumbler, and one we’d happily keep on display between sips.

According to recent reports, candy-cane-inspired stripes give the Painted Farm Red Tumbler a festive personality, with a vibrant red-and-pink color combination that’s anything but ordinary. The built-in flip straw makes sipping easy, and double-wall vacuum insulation keeps drinks cold for hours. The folding handle is the perfect finishing touch, making this tumbler easy to grab and go wherever the winter day takes you. This style is also available in an Artisanal Birds print.

Rosé all day? Don’t mind if we do. The Reserve All Day Wine Set is almost too pretty to pass up, with FARM Rio’s festive tropical print covering both the 34-ounce bottle and two coordinating 11-ounce wine tumblers. The insulated bottle holds an entire bottle of your favorite rosé, keeping it chilled until you’re ready to pour. Forgot to chill your wine? Unscrew the collar, add ice, and you’re one step closer to happy hour.

Not every Stanley is meant to be sipped from; these are meant to be admired. This four-piece miniature Quencher ornament set features FARM Rio’s colorful tropical prints, bringing a playful pop to your Christmas tree. Each comes with a ribbon for hanging, but you can also attach it to a backpack or purse as a charm. Even better, the removable lid reveals a tiny hidden compartment for stashing a little surprise.

Cold mornings call for something warm, and this Stay-Hot Stacking Cup in Painted Farm Pink is just the ticket. Designed for sipping espresso, coffee or tea, this double-walled vacuum-insulated cup keeps every sip hot for hours. The best part? These cups stack neatly, so there’s no need to stop at one. This 10-ounce cup also comes in Painted Farm Red, making a matching pair a pretty tempting addition to your kitchen shelf.

You can shop the fresh Stanley 1913 x FARM Rio holiday collection starting Tuesday, November 17 at 9 a.m. PST/12 p.m. EST. The 14-piece collection will be available on both the Stanley and FARM Rio websites. Prices range from $25 for a stacking cup to $120 for a wine set.

With colorful tropical prints and a festive seasonal twist, these limited-edition designs are sure to sell out. Set a reminder so you can shop your favorite designs before they’re gone.
