Hull City star Konstantinos Tzolakis would be the perfect goalkeeper to challenge David Raya for Arsenal's No.1 spot.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 24-year-old put in a Man of the Match performance as the Tigers secured an unexpected 2-0 win over Brighton on Sunday, 22 November.

He made seven incredible saves at the MKM Stadium, allowing Oli McBurnie's late brace to make all the difference.

Tzolakis presently leads the way in terms of save percentage in the Premier League this season, boasting 4.4 saves per 90 so far [FotMob].

Interestingly, the Tigers signed him from Olympiacos for £20million, a Greek club owned by Evangelos Marinakis.

King has already suggested to Football Insider that Marinakis will be frustrated, having not decided to bring him to Nottingham Forest in the summer.

Tzolakis is now turning into one of the signings of the 2026-27 campaign, and he may be on the move much sooner that Hull anticipated.

"It was one of those days that you would say, 'He isn’t going to get beat today', this guy," King told Football Insider.

"He seemed to be in the right place at the right time for everything. It was as good a goalkeeper performance as you'll see all season from anybody.

Sources indicate that "He was a decent buy, him. What is he? 24? He costs, what, £20million? And to be fair, that's a lot of money for a goalkeeper coming from Greece.

Konstantinos Tzolakis was a MONSTER between the sticks for @hullcity against Brighton 👊 pic.twitter.com/xfgqketvcJ.

Sources indicate that "He certainly looks better than the Arsenal reserve goalkeeper. I think they've got a good chance of staying up, and primarily because they've got a decent goalkeeper who's performing well.".

It certainly wouldn't be a surprise if Arsenal registered an interest in the 24-year-old ahead of the January transfer window.

Fans are not aware of how long Raya might be sidelined for, with Kepa Arrizabalaga filling his spot for the last two matches.

Kepa has not made many mistakes, if any, in that time, but the 32-year-old doesn't exactly strike confidence in the hearts of his defenders.

Tzolakis would be an ideal replacement, but he's unlikely to want to sit on the bench and watch once Raya returns from injury.
