AST SpaceMobile: Is the Stock a No-Brainer Buy at $30?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that concerns are growing that AST SpaceMobile (ASTS -12.29%) won't be able to compete with Elon Musk's Space Exploration Technologies Corp. It's evident in the downward spiral AST SpaceMobile has been on this year. On Monday, it was trading at around $30 -- nowhere near its 52-week high of around $134.

For AST SpaceMobile to compete with SpaceX, investors know it's going to need to spend heavily. That raises the risk of more intense cash burn and, of course, dilution. Many growth investors are clearly having second thoughts about the business, despite it possessing some tremendous growth opportunities.

While there's plenty of uncertainty around its future, has the stock become a no-brainer buy at such a drastically reduced valuation?

AST is in its early growth stages, so there is inevitably going to be risk with this investment given its heavy spending. In its most recent quarter, which ended on Sept. 30, the organization reported revenue of $42.3 million, which was close to three times what it posted in the same period last year -- $14.7 million.

Its operating expenses, however, also doubled to more than $211 million, easily putting the company in the red. Overall, AST posted a net loss of $132.4 million, which was a deeper loss than the $122.9 million loss it posted in the prior-year period.

According to recent reports, the tech giant is eyeing significant opportunities and unveiled Blacklift, a 20-ton reusable rocket that it plans to use to deploy satellites by late 2028. That will give AST more control over its rolls out, but it will undoubtedly be a capital-intensive undertaking, which may only exacerbate concerns about cash flow and lack of profitability.

NASDAQ · As of 3:36 PM ET 11/16/26.

Industry observers note that due to its sharp decline in value this year, AST's market cap is now around just $12 billion. That's fairly low given its grand aspirations. And that's a reason why the stock may be worth buying right now, because if it's able to build a rocket that helps it build out its broadband network in space and generate much more revenue growth in the future, and potentially get it out of the red, the stock could have a lot of room to rise higher.

Sources indicate that however, that's also easier said than done. AST SpaceMobile is a highly risky and volatile stock that's primarily going to appeal to investors who have a high tolerance for risk. Given its deep losses and the high likelihood of future share offerings, it's likely to be a bumpy ride for investors.
