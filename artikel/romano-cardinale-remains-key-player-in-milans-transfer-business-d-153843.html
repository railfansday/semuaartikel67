Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that Markus Krösche will soon get to work at AC Milan, but Gerry Cardinale will remain involved.

It has not been a very well-kept secret that Milan have been pushing for the arrival of Krösche since the summer, when it looked as though the German director was all set to arrive alongside Ruben Amorim, before Eintracht Frankfurt put up a wall.

Rather than turn to an alternative appointment, the Rossoneri decided to internally promote some figures while leaving the vacancy open. Ultimately they were waiting for developments on Krösche, and he officially left the Bundesliga side last month, leaving him available to finally join.

Industry observers note that on Monday, there was talk of a release clause paid to Eintracht. In a video published on his YouTube channel, Fabrizio Romano revealed some added information about Krösche’s imminent arrival and what it will mean regarding Cardinale’s involvement.

In a recent development, “Confirmation has arrived, and I would say finally, also from Germany of something we have been telling you for weeks: Markus Krosche will be the latest Head of Football at Milan. In Germany there has been a much slower narrative, much more, let’s say, Eintracht-centric,” he said (via MilanNews).

In a recent development, “The reality is that Milan has had an agreement with Krosche for some time, Milan also has everything ready for the official announcement which is expected shortly. Milan will not pay 5 million, but 4 million euros in compensation to Eintracht Frankfurt to free Krosche, who already noted goodbye to the German club some time ago.

Sources indicate that “He will be ready and operational from the beginning of December to work on the winter transfer market with Gerry Cardinale, Ruben Amorim and the entire work group that is dealing with Milan. So there were never any doubts whatsoever.

Sources indicate that “Markus Krosche is ready to take control of the Rossoneri mercato with Gerry Cardinale, who will be absolutely present, a key player, and involved. After Milan’s excellent victory against Frosinone, the Milan owner reiterated that if Milan competes for a player, it will be to win, not to participate. In short, this is another sign of Milan’s ambition.”.
