More than two years after a Hulu tile button was added to Disney Plus, the two streaming services are now set to become a merged offering in the US.

The services themselves won’t merge — it’s still unclear whether there will be latest packages and pricing models once the merged version is fully complete. Disney raised prices on its subscription plans this past September.

An integrated Hulu and Disney Plus streaming experience has been in the works for nearly two years, since it was announced during former CEO Bob Iger’s tenure. In August 2025, Iger mentioned the two apps would be combined in 2026, and Hulu would be available to international customers.
