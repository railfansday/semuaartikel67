📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that banff World Media Festival executive director Jenn Kuzmyk is exiting after nine years in charge of the event, as well as her role as senior VP and publisher of Playback. Kuzmyk will depart both roles next month, but continue in a short-term consulting role with Brunico Communications with help with the transition.

In a recent development, A permanent replacement has not been named for Kuzmyk; instead, in the interim, Brunico president and CEO Russell Goldstein will oversee the commercial aspects of next year’s Banff festival while Brunico chief content officer Mary Maddever will handle the event’s programming. Kuzmyk helped grow Banff into a popular international media festival, establishing new features such as Showrunner Superpanel and the Summit Series Keynotes, as well as the “Country of Honour” program. Industry heavyhitters who attended under her watch included Jeffrey Katzenberg, Ted Sarandos, Pearlena Igbokwe, Jesse Armstrong, George Cheeks, Channing Dungey and Rob Wade. Countries honored included South Korea in 2025 and Spain in 2026.

Also under Kuzmyk, Banff attracted attendees from all over the world, who also participated in the annual Rockie Awards.

According to recent reports, “Jenn’s vision and passion have left an indelible mark on both Banff and Playback,” Goldstein noted in a statement. “Under her watch, she has elevated these iconic brands with a consummate blend of strategic leadership and innovation. Building on this exceptional foundation, Banff 2027 is poised for growth under the stewardship of our remarkably talented and dedicated team. We are deeply grateful for Jenn’s contributions and wish her every success in her next chapter.”.

Added Bell Media president Sean Cohan: “On behalf of the Foundation Board of Directors, I want to extend our deepest gratitude to Jenn for her transformative leadership and unwavering commitment to both the Canadian and global screen-based industries. Over the past nine years, she has steered Banff through an era of rapid industry evolution with grace, foresight, and integrity, expanding its international reach while creating vital, career-defining opportunities for industry participants at every level. We thank her for her tremendous service and look forward to seeing all that she accomplishes next.”.

Kuzmyk also helped create programs such as Netflix-Banff Diversity of Voices Initiative, the Banff Spark Accelerator for Women in the Business of Media with Paramount+ and the Indigenous Screen Summit.

In a recent development, “Leading Banff and Playback has been the privilege of my career,” said Kuzmyk in a statement. “I want to thank Sean and the Banff board of directors for their trust, guidance and commitment to the festival, and our supporters, partners and sponsors, whose belief in Banff makes everything we do possible. To the Banff and Playback teams: you are the reason the festival and publication are as strong as they have ever been, and it has been a joy to work alongside you. I’m especially grateful to Russell Goldstein for his years of support and partnership. I leave knowing both Banff and Playback have never been in better shape, and I’ll share more about what’s next in the New Year for me.”.
