That noted, there are only a select few thrillers with true broad appeal. They're so good, so gripping and strongly written, that almost anyone will be hooked from the opening pages.

If you're looking for a mystery or suspenseful story to dive into, the titles below have you covered. They run the gamut from sun-drenched psychological battles and dark character studies to tangled domestic dramas and gripping real-life investigations.

Big Little Lies follows three women, Madeline, Celeste, and Jane, whose children attend the same primary school. Their lives are complicated by marital difficulties, schoolyard rivalries, and long-buried secrets, which begin to boil over after someone is killed during a school fundraising event (though the victim's identity is kept from the reader).

That's a juicy mystery to keep us hooked, and author Liane Moriarty fills it out with relatable domestic drama, sharp social satire, a welcome touch of dark humor, and a cast of flawed characters. They're all intense but believable, with their petty arguments and insecurities providing plenty of entertainment even when the central mystery temporarily takes a back seat.

The characters are impressively realistic, and the writing itself is great the whole way through, pulling the reader from page to page.
