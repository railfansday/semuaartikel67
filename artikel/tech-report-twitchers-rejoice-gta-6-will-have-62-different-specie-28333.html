According to recent reports, there's a laundry list of things people are excited to do in Grand Theft Auto 6 once it finally releases in a couple of days. Perhaps you want to know what a loving relationship feels like, maybe you're just concerned about what kind of cars you'll be able to drive around in, or it could be that all you want to do is go bird watching.

It has been revealed that well it really does seem like the sky's the limit in GTA 6, because you can do all three of those things; the healthy relationship will take some extra work though. During a recent interview with The Guardian, Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North explains that players will have a wildlife logbook, which includes 62 different species of birds, for those who want to see all the wildlife Leonida has to offer.

It has been revealed that aside from the gull that was mentioned in the interview, I'm not aware of which specific birds can be found in GTA 6. Actually I don't even know what gull was sighted. It could have been a laughing gull, a herring gull, or perhaps a small ring-billed gull. I'd say the possibilities are endless but they aren't. Officially, 529 species of bird can be found in Florida as of 2019, so 62 is but a fraction of that number.

It's probably a safe bet that we'll see a bald eagle and the american flamingo in GTA 6, but I personally hope to find a snowy plover or perhaps a swallow-tailed kite, but I'm not holding my breath.

Aside from birds, Leonida will be full of all sorts of other species, over 170 according to GameInformer. There's going to be manatees, pelicans, alligators, green iguanas, cane toads, and even a few rhesus macaque monkeys. So by the sounds of things twitchers and other fans of wildlife watching will have heaps to do in GTA 6, besides all the crime and driving, that is.
