Milan are decimated by injuries, so Diego Moreira, Omari Hutchinson and Alphadjo Cisse support Goncalo Ramos for a Europa League trip to Olympiacos.

It kicks off at the Stadio Georgios Karaisakis in Piraeus at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game, Juventus-Omonia Nicosia and Borac-Atalanta on the Liveblog.

The Rossoneri might be doing well in Serie A, but their Europa League form has been appalling, losing three games without scoring a goal, making the 4-0 away to RB Salzburg an outlier.

Coach Ruben Amorim doesn’t even have the option of squad rotation today, because injuries ruled out Luka Modric, Francesco Camarda, Davide Bartesaghi, Strahinja Pavlovic, Samuel Chukwueze, Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers and Pietro Terracciano.

With all that in mind, Goncalo Ramos is allowed no rest, supported by Diego Moreira and Cisse, but potentially moving Hutchinson into a central trequartista role.

Mike Maignan returns from suspension after his red card against Bournemouth.

Milan have never won away to a Greek side in Europe, managing just four draws and two defeats, including a 3-1 loss to Olympiacos in December 2018.

Olympiacos are still unbeaten in the Europa League so far this season, beating Jagiellonia, Olympique Marseille and Sparta Prague before the 0-0 at Stade Rennais.

Notably, they conceded only one goal in those four games and sit in sixth place.

They too have a few injury problems, because former Roma flop Leon Bailey, Chiquinho, Gustavo Puerta and Santiago Hezze are out, plus long-term absentees and cup-tied Yusuf Yazici, Balsa Popovic, Ayoub El Kaabi, Marius Mouandilmadji, Clayton, Joel Roca and Lorenzo Scipioni.

Former Inter defender Lorenzo Pirola is starting, with ex-Torino midfielder Marcus Pedersen and former Bologna midfielder Remo Freuler in the side.

Olympiacos: Ortega; Retsos, Smajlovic, Pirola, Bruno Onyemaechi; Freuler Gustavo Sá; Pedersen, Joao Silva, Gelson Martins; Gonzalez.

Milan: Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Estupinan; Jashari, Rabiot; Moreira, Cisse, Hutchinson; Ramos.
