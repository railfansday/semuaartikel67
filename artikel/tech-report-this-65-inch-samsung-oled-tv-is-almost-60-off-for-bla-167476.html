It has been revealed that if you've never owned an OLED before, there are some things you need to know to make sure it's a good fit for you. Perhaps most important is that they don't get as bright as QLEDs or LED TVs. Since this is an entry-level OLED, it likewise won't reach as high brightness as more premium OLEDs. It has been revealed that the S84H offers the near-perfect black levels you can expect from any OLED TV, making the contrast look incredible. The pixel-level dimming and viewing angles are also on par with other high-end OLEDs, and this TV also offers great features for gamers, including 4K support at 120Hz across all of its HDMI 2.1 ports, a variable refresh rate, and auto low latency mode.
