Mauricio Pochettino has made it clear he wants to be involved in Manchester United’s major squad decisions, while also addressing questions surrounding captain Bruno Fernandes ahead of his first game against Liverpool.

The new Manchester United head coach explained that he wants to have a voice in decisions that shape the squad.

“Sometimes maybe you are a coach and maybe you have more power in the decisions,” Pochettino said in his first Manchester United press conference.

“It depends on the structure, it depends on the communication, the relationships, you know, with all the areas.”.

Mauricio Pochettino highlighted his responsibilities are clear about who he selects and plays in games.].

“I am the guy that is going to decide the squad for Sunday, and I’m going to pick the team, I’m going to make the changes, and of course in the future, I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that will happen on the squad.”.

Pochettino was also asked about Bruno Fernandes, whose critical comments about Michael Carrick’s tactics became a talking point during the former manager’s final weeks at Old Trafford.

The Argentine was really keen about the squad’s commitment, dodging the blame on Fernandes and describing Fernandes as a key figure in the dressing room.

“The commitment from all the players was amazing from day one,” he said.

“Bruno is our captain and a very vital player but all the players need to feel they are important from the bottom to the top.”.

Pochettino revealed that he had already spent time speaking with the players to understand their situation both on and off the pitch.

“Yesterday we spent three hours chatting to the players in my office and maybe I missed the injured players.”.

Pochettino admitted the first few days at United had been extremely busy as he tried to get up to speed before his first match.

He is excited by the opportunity but knows there is little time to prepare for Sunday’s trip to Anfield.

“It’s a happy time but at the same time, so busy,” he explained.

“It’s been about adapting in the last few days, and it’s a race to arrive in condition to be competitive against Liverpool, and now our focus is 100 per cent on Liverpool in the game on Sunday.”.
