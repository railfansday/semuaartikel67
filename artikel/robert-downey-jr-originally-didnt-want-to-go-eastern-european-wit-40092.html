Robert Downey Jr. has revealed how he found his Doctor Doom voice in Avengers: Doomsday, as well as opening up about his initial trepidation in bringing the Marvel supervillain to life.

In a recent development, "Part of me didn't want to go Eastern European," Downey Jr. told Empire magazine.

But the process evolved beyond the one-time Tony Stark actor's doubts, and began to crystalize after Downey Jr. performed a speech Doom gives to his Latverian subjects.

Recent findings suggest that "I had written down this manifesto speech that he makes, and I laid down this voice note of him addressing his people, and I started to find it there," Downey Jr. recalled.

Industry observers note that it wasn't just the voice that took Downey Jr. some time to find. It took a few days on set to finally get into the villain 'me against them' mindset that would prove so crucial to getting into Doom's headspace.

"Remember Michael Douglas at the end of Falling Down? He goes 'I'm the bad guy?' I had that realization a couple of days into shooting because all the heroes are over there, and I'm the problem," Downey Jr. said. "Then I leaned in, and it was just this glorious time.".

Early responses to the Doctor Doom voice were mixed, with some comparing it unfavorably to a Dracula impression. Since then, the fanbase has cooled on their reaction – with recent Doomsday teasers and trailers passing by without Doom's accent being the center of attention.

In a recent development, the recent VisionQuest post-credits scene, though, featured a Doctor Doom that sounded suspiciously unlike Robert Downey Jr. Showrunner Terry Matalas later confirmed it was the actor, however.

"I can't talk too much about how it was constructed, because I don't want to give away, but yes, that line was designed for this moment, specifically," Matalas told Gold Derby.

Avengers: Doomsday revolves around Doom's introduction into the MCU, with the Avengers, Fantastic Four and X-Men fighting to save the universe from world-colliding forces known as incursions.

Avengers: Doomsday hits UK cinemas on December 16 and US theatres on December 18.
