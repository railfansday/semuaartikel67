The discounts follow an August price increase across the Fire TV lineup. For more editor-vetted tech deals, check out the rest of our Black Friday coverage.

According to recent reports, the newest Fire TV Stick HD is $15.99, down from $39.99. This 2026 version of Amazon's entry-level stick is about 30% slimmer than before and able to run off a TV's USB port. It streams in 1080p with HDR10+ over Wi-Fi 6 and adds Alexa+ for more conversational voice search. That remarked, with the 4K Select costing $2 more and the new Stick 4K $5 more, the HD makes the most sense for TVs that top out at 1080p. This PCMag review has more on its performance.

In a recent development, fire TV Stick 4K Select is $17.99, down from $49.99, and brings 4K HDR10+ streaming and Alexa+ to Amazon's lowest-priced 4K stick. It launched in October 2025 as the first Fire TV stick to run Vega OS, and it skips Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, and Wi-Fi 6, using Wi-Fi 5 instead.

According to recent reports, the latest Fire TV Stick 4K is down to $19.99 from $59.99 and is the version Amazon released in September with a quad-core processor, Wi-Fi 6, and Vega OS. According to Amazon, apps launch over 35% faster than on the previous model, while the ability to draw power directly from your TV's USB port makes setup a little easier. The redesigned remote also has buttons that are easier to identify by touch, and you get support for HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG content at up to 4K and 60fps. Check out PCMag's review for a closer look at its performance and features.

Sources indicate that the Fire TV Stick 4K Plus with AI-powered Fire TV search is $29.99, down from $69.99, and is built on the second-generation Fire TV Stick 4K hardware that PCMag reviewed in 2023, now paired with AI-powered search that finds titles by actor, plot, or a remembered quote. It supports Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, which the 4K Select lacks, and it runs Fire OS rather than Vega OS, so sideloading still works.

Sources indicate that fire TV Cube is $89.99, down from $199.99, the largest discount in this roundup and $30 below its 2022 launch price. It's Amazon's fastest streaming device, with an octa-core processor, Wi-Fi 6E, a built-in Ethernet port, and far-field microphones for hands-free Alexa. Its HDMI input and IR blaster also let it connect to and control a cable box. For more on its performance, check out this PCMag review.
