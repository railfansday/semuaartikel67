Ambassador Changes Cruise Itinerary Due to Fuel Supply Matter

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ambassador Cruise Line changed the itinerary of the Ambience’s current cruise due to an matter with a fuel supplier.

Sailing roundtrip from Tilbury, the 70,000-ton vessel is offering a 31-night cruise to the Eastern Mediterranean and the Adriatic Islands.

According to an enhancement published on the company’s social media profiles, the change came on Friday, November 20, 2026.

“Unfortunately, the Ambience has been let down by local fuel suppliers. They were booked to refuel the ship today but have not been able to deliver,” Ambassador explained. The bunker operation was scheduled to take place during the vessel’s visit to Trieste in Northern Italy.

“The Ambience has fuel for several days; however, we do not want to get close to our reserves and have investigated several options to arrange alternative fuel supplies with the minimum of disruption,” the tech giant continued.

As a result, the 1,578-passenger ship will skip a planned visit to Syracuse in Italy’s Sicily region, sailing instead to Katakolon, Greece, for the bunkering operation.

The manufacturer said that the Ambience will visit the upcoming destination on November 23, remaining docked between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

The rest of the month-long itinerary, which started in the United Kingdom earlier this month, will not be impacted, Ambassador continued.

Industry observers note that “We are very sorry for this disruption and for any inconvenience. Katakolon is a very interesting port with many opportunities for guests to explore,” Ambassador added.

In addition to the new port of call, the Ambience is scheduled to sail to destinations in Spain and Portugal before returning to its homeport in England, including Palma de Mallorca, Lisbon, and Ferrol.

The upcoming cruises onboard the ship are also scheduled to sail from the Tilbury Cruise Terminal as planned.

In service for Ambassador since the company’s debut in early 2022, the Ambience recently completed its 35th year of service.
