Industry observers note that it's the most wonderful time of the year to upgrade your outerwear, and REI has the best jacket deals of Black Friday 2026 so far. Editor-approved staples from Arc’teryx, Patagonia, and The North Face are discounted by as much as 39%.

One of the most tempting deals is on Patagonia’s lightweight Windseep Jacket for only $119 at REI, reduced from $199. Other primo bargains include Columbia’s cozy, chic Timber Crest Fleece for only $44 at REI.

Industry observers note that save 25% on the Columbia Timber Crest Fleece Half-Zip Pullover in three styles, including my favorite, Black Icey Peaks, shown here (alas, my size is sold out). A staple layer, the Timber Crest Fleece is as soft as they come, with a decent amount of water resistance, just in case.

Industry observers note that if full-zip fleeces are more your vibe, I recommend this deal on the Steens Mountain 2.0 from Columbia. Available in four styles, including two two-tone options, the fleece material is a little thicker/more durable than the Timber Crest above, but just as soft.

This sleek, sporty full-zip from The North Face is stretchy, sweat-wicking, and 28% off for Black Friday. Available in three styles (for the widest size selection, see "Mushroom Grey"), the grid pattern maximizes breathability, making this a great choice for workouts.

In a recent development, real talk: I own three versions of Patagonia's Houdini Jacket, including a full-zip hoodie, full-zip jacket, and the Patagonia Stash Houdini Half-Zip shown here. Weighing just 3.7 ounces, this little wonder crams into a back pocket with ease. Yet, when worn and zipped up, it provides a surprising amount of warmth, wind- and water-resistance.

The North Face Cabin Fleece Full-Zip is an ultra-plush, heavy-weight option with a stylish diamond pattern along the upper two-thirds. The lower body and arms, meanwhile, are high-pile fleece. Score one for 21% off in "Smokey Brown" or "TNF Black.".

Full disclosure: I have not personally tested the newish Patagonia Windsweep Jacket; however, it's apparently an all-cotton ripstop version of my favorite Houdini packable jacket above, with a similar water-resistant treatment. 40% off for BF; I'm considering grabbing this deal myself... for research purposes.

The Arc'teryx Squamish Jacket is similar in design to Patagonia's Houdini line of ultra-light packable wind- and rain-resistant shells. And like the Houdini, I'm a huge fan of the Squamish line (I own the hooded one, also on sale below). This deal is on the "Arctic Silk" style for a cool 29% off.

The sporty, workout-friendly Arc’teryx Delta Jacket is a tempting 30% off right now in Bracken/Mongoose (shown here). Similar in design to the Cedar Trail Grid Fleece from The North Face above, the Delta is stretchy, breathable, and sleek.

The North Face has a reputation for making ultra-warm, high-quality puffers for very cold weather, and the Terra Peak Insulated Jacket is a shining example of what the brand does best. Save a massive 34% on this built-to-last puffer (or save 25% on the hooded version below).

I've mentioned it before, and I'll say it again, the Arc’teryx Squamish Hoody is one of my all-time favorite featherweight, packable pieces of outerwear, and a staple for outdoor adventures and travel.

Industry observers note that when it comes to cold-weather puffers, I prefer mine to have a hood. If you feel the same way and were tempted by the Terra Peak Jacket deal above, I have good news: The hooded version is also on sale, though for a slightly more modest 25% off.
