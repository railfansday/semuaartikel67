📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A hiker died Sunday morning on Southern California’s Mt. Baden-Powell after slipping on ice and falling roughly 500 feet, according to AV Scanner News, a local Southern California emergency news-monitoring page.

A GoFundMe arranged by the victim’s niece identifies the deceased as ultrarunner Guillermo Alejandro Batres and indicates he had been attempting to help his son, who had slipped and fallen, when he himself lost his footing and fell down a steep gulch.

Industry observers note that authorities have not confirmed the victim’s identity or cause of death and have not released additional details about the incident. According to AV Scanner News, the fatal accident occurred roughly 3 miles up the trail from Vincent Gap and “family members of the hiker were also reported to be at the scene.”.

Los Angeles County Fire Department and Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department Palmdale Station responded to the incident and an air unit was used to recover Batres’s remains.

It has been revealed that mt. Baden-Powell is a 9,407-foot summit in the Angeles National Forest. The summit lies 0.1 miles off the Pacific Crest Trail at northbound mile 377.9 and is a popular destination for PCT hikers and Los Angeles residents alike. Snow and ice can make the route treacherous during the winter months.
