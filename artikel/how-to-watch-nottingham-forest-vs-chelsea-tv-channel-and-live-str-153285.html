Chelsea take on Nottingham Forest this evening looking to get back to winning ways.

The Blues were thrashed 4-1 by Leeds United last time out as pressure piled on Xabi Alonso.

The west Londoners were dealt a harsh lesson at home by Daniel Farke’s Whites, and the result left Chelsea eighth in the Premier League table, five points adrift of the top four after 11 matches.

Now, they travel to the City Ground to take on a Forest side who have had a poor start to their first term under Oliver Glasner.

The Austrian arrived in Nottinghamshire to great fanfare after his Europa Conference League success with Crystal Palace, but just two victories in their first 11 games sees them lying precariously above the relegation zone in 17th position.

They were beaten 2-0 by Bournemouth last time out, to make it two wins from their last seven.

Alonso’s Chelsea, meanwhile, have just one victory in their last five games, and they are missing star striker Joao Pedro through a thigh injury that has kept the Brazilian out for over a month.

They have been handed an injury boost, however, with Moises Caicedo expected to return to the matchday squad after being forced off after 20 minutes against Leeds last weekend.

TV channel: You can watch this game live on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Premier League, with coverage starting at 7pm GMT ahead of the 8pm GMT kick-off.
