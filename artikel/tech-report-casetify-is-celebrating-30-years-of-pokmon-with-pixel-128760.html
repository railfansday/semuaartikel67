TL;DR: Casetify is celebrating 30 years of Pokémon with a newly introduced pixel-art tech collection — including a 3D Poké Ball earbuds case and light-up Pikachu charms — launching Dec. 1, with waitlist access open now.

In a recent development, casetify is celebrating 30 years of Pokémon with a brand-new pixel-art tech accessory collection — and the waitlist is officially open ahead of the Dec. 1 launch. Rather than starting completely from scratch, the brand is bringing back one of its most popular collaborations of all time alongside a fresh batch of special-edition gear.

For longtime fans, the big draw is getting a second shot at retired favorites without paying scalper prices. Casetify's previous pixel-art Pokémon collaboration was one of the brand's most popular Co-Lab drops, with hero pieces like the 3D Poké Ball earbuds case selling out and routinely reselling for much more online.

In a recent development, the newly introduced 30th-anniversary lineup puts retro pixel-art Pokémon designs across Casetify's protective phone cases, charms, and tech accessories. While the full catalog spans multiple smartphone case styles, four main hero items anchor the drop:.

Pokémon Earbuds Case — A returning fan favorite sculpted into a chunky 3D Poké Ball with a tactile pixel-art finish made to protect your AirPods.

Pokémon Adventure Shake Shake Case — An interactive phone case packed with loose pixel-art Pokémon and Poké Balls that slide and shift across the back of your phone whenever you move it.

Pikachu Flashy Charm — A light-reactive accessory featuring a pixel-art Pikachu unleashing its signature electric move, flashing to light up whenever it catches a spark on the go.

Pokémon Phone Charm — A stylized wrist strap adorned with multiple starter and fan-favorite Pokémon charms so you can carry your dream team wherever you head next.

Once it unlocks, you will be able to shop the collection online, through the Casetify Co-Lab app, and at Casetify Studio's 80 retail locations worldwide, including an in-store selection at the Casetify concession inside Selfridges on Oxford Street in London.

Hop on the waitlist at Casetify ahead of Dec. 1 — your phone (or the Pokémon fan on your holiday gift list) will thank you.
