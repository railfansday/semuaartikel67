BIT Asset Management’s regulated fund brings direct spot Bitcoin access to Swiss retail accounts.

Industry observers note that BIT Asset Management’s Bitcoin Fund ETF (BBIT), Switzerland’s first spot Bitcoin exchange-traded fund, began trading on the SIX Swiss Exchange on November 26, 2026. Structured as a regulated Swiss collective investment fund, the product gives retail investors direct Bitcoin exposure through standard Swiss bank or brokerage accounts without self-custody.

The passive fund tracks the SIX Bitcoin Reference Rate and holds physical Bitcoin offline in cold storage at regulated Swiss custodian AMINA Bank AG, ring-fenced from the manager’s and custodian’s own assets.

BIT Asset Management describes BBIT as Europe’s currently active spot Bitcoin ETF, though public offering is approved only in Switzerland.

Industry observers note that BBIT is listed on SIX Swiss Exchange and trades in both U.S. dollars and Swiss francs, with a target annual management fee of 0.19%. The fund launched with an initial concern price of $10 per unit and an allocation of 350 BTC, valued at more than $20 million at launch.

Switzerland’s Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA) approved BBIT’s fund contract on October 20, 2026, following an 18-month review.

FINMA granted an exemption from standard investment diversification requirements, allowing the fund to invest exclusively in Bitcoin based on the cryptocurrency’s established trading history and liquidity.

However, the fund is classified as an alternative investment fund with specific risks, reflecting Bitcoin’s volatility and potential for substantial capital losses.

Sources indicate that unlike most European crypto exchange-traded products, which are structured as debt instruments and expose investors to issuer credit risk, BBIT operates under Swiss collective investment scheme legislation. This means investors hold fund units backed by ring-fenced assets, legally separated from the fund management company’s own assets.

Pernet von Ballmoos AG (PvB) acts as the fund management company and issuer, while BIT Asset Management serves as the sponsor and investment manager. Flow Traders has been appointed as the market maker, supporting secondary-market liquidity.

The fund also provides daily disclosure of its Bitcoin holdings and supports in-kind subscriptions for eligible institutional participants.

It has been revealed that while BBIT is Switzerland’s first retail spot Bitcoin ETF, a physically backed Bitcoin product—the Jacobi FT Wilshire Bitcoin ETF—previously debuted on Euronext Amsterdam in August 2023 before Euronext listed it as inactive in February 2026 and liquidation proceedings began later that month. Meanwhile, SIX has hosted physically backed crypto ETPs since 2019.

The listing arrives with Bitcoin price hovering near $60,500 after heavy November outflows across U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs.

BIT—formerly known as Matrixport—expanded its Swiss fund footprint after acquiring and rebranding its Swiss asset management arm (originally rooted in Crypto Finance Asset Management).

This approval is a major step for Switzerland, as it allows everyday investors to gain direct exposure to Bitcoin through a regulated Swiss investment fund. It also highlights that regulators are becoming more open to Bitcoin as a mainstream investment asset.
