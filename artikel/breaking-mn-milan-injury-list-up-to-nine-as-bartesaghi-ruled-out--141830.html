AC Milan will be without another defender in Greece tomorrow, with news emerging about Davide Bartesaghi being absent for the clash.

Industry observers note that in the past 24 hours, Milan have had to deal with several injury problems. Luka Modric was ruled out last night, whilst Francesco Camarda didn’t take part in today’s training session.

Before this, Ruben Amorim’s squad was already looking short – an problem which most have felt was the case since before the summer – and now it’s gotten shorter again, somehow.

It has been revealed that according to MilanNews, the Rossoneri will also be without Davide Bartesaghi, who is suffering from a bout of peritendinitis. The young Italian – going off brief searches – could be out for two weeks to six at most, but there’s no more information offered on the matter and its severity.

Of course, the best-case scenario is that it’s a precautionary absence and he’s back for this weekend’s game. However, there’s no information on the matter just yet.

All that is known, though, is that the Diavolo are now up to nine injuries at this moment, with Bartesaghi joining Samuel Chukwueze, Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers, Strahinja Pavlovic, Modric, Camarda and Pietro Terracciano.

It’s a pretty brutal list, indeed and it makes you realise how limited Milan are on the flanks, which is perhaps why another left-back could be signed in January.
