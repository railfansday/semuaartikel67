The problematic state of the market isn't getting better: most assets attempting a short-term recovery faced resistance they were incapable of breaking and, unfortunately, the positive outcome might be unlikely here.

According to recent reports, pressure on Hyperliquid isn't decreasing: below $60 made bears even hungrier than before the September high of $98 is long gone with one lower low forming after another. The technical situation on the chart is suggesting a clear momentum aggravation (despite the small recovery beforehand).

It has been revealed that all indicators that analyze and measure the momentum of the asset clearly hint at the lack of endurance among bulls who are not witnessing any signs of rapid retraces here. There's also a good chance that things will get even worse.

Hyperliquid's main benefit over others is the existence of a clear sustainable cash flow fueled by operations on the platform. With the rising volatility, Hyperliquid receives fresh funds injection directly, so it can gain upper hand over alternative cryptocurrencies.

In a recent development, the buyback mechanism is a key to creating a sustainable trend for Hyperliquid, but unfortunately it's not as active as it could've been. $78 million was directed toward buybacks earlier, but the decline still wasn't prevented with that much injection.

The $60 level is now the first threshold bulls need to reclaim, and without a convincing move above it, the latest decline is likely to keep defining the structure. A short bounce on weak volume would not change much, especially while the market continues to punish assets that fail to attract consistent demand.

It has been revealed that hyperliquid still has a stronger fundamental argument than many alternative cryptocurrencies, but this advantage needs to translate into sustained buying pressure. Until that happens, buybacks alone are unlikely to reverse the current trend, and another attempt to test lower levels remains a realistic scenario in the near term.

It has been revealed that zcash has fallen the most out of the three, dropping from its September high of around $1,700 to its current price of around $702.79. Technically, the bearish trend has strengthened as ZEC has fallen below its long-term MA and all major MAs are currently above the price.

Sources indicate that short-term, ZEC will need to move back above $750-$800 to start seeing any improvement in its technicals. Its former long-term MA around $870-$880 is also an vital level of resistance.

Its NU7 network upgrade went live on November 6, reducing the target block interval from 75 seconds to 25 seconds and making several other changes to improve the performance of private transactions.

Recent findings suggest that zcash holders also have an active proposal (expires on November 30) in which they'll vote on whether or not to move 50,000 ZEC out of the Community Grants treasury and repurpose future treasury funds for development and legal costs. While Zcash has positive developments to improve its technological and governance narratives, market sentiment has yet to see improvement.

Immediate support is in the $690-$700 region and a decisive move below this region could see ZEC fall to the $650 and $600 region respectively.

Its RSI is headed towards the oversold region, although there are presently no signs of a sustainable bullish reversal. Its next level of resistance is around $800-$870 (its former long-term MA).

Recent findings suggest that like Zcash and NEAR, Polkadot's price has been in a downtrend since its October high. Unlike Zcash and NEAR though, Polkadot's price action hasn't been accompanied by positive developments to improve its technological and governance narrative.

NEAR Protocol's price has been in a downtrend since its October high of around $5.90 and has fallen below its long-term MA that offered support around the $3.00 region. Its price is right now around $2.80 and is trading below all major MAs. Attempts to stabilize around its long-term MA have failed to see NEAR's price rally convincingly.

Recent findings suggest that its RSI is headed towards the oversold region which could see NEAR's price rally in the short-term. Its immediate support is in the $2.75-$2.80 region and could see its price fall to $2.60 and $2.40 (stronger historical support) respectively in the event of a breakdown.

NEAR Intents reportedly surpassed $40 billion in cumulative transaction volume on November 23, and NEAR's cross-chain infrastructure is said to support more than 35 different blockchains and 100 different assets.

Its next level of resistance is in the $3.40-$3.60 region (confluence of several MAs) and reclaiming 3.00-3.10 would be NEAR's first step higher.
