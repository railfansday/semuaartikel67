Through the daze of the Grammy nominations, Taylor Swift came back when it mattered.

According to recent reports, the 14-time Grammy winner made a surprise appearance during the nomination ceremony on Nov. 16, making sure the announcement of the final two categories hit just the perfect note.

Industry observers note that “Good morning America, it's Taylor,” Swift mentioned as she appeared on screen dressed in a simple back long sleeve shirt and necklaces. “I'm very honored that the Grammys have asked me to be here to help reveal the last two categories for the Grammy nominations.”.

“I first wanted to start off by saying congratulations to anyone who's nominated for a Grammy this year in any category,” she continued, “because what this means is that your peers, your fellow musicians, producers, engineers, artists, writers, they love what you made, and that's the coolest feeling in the world. I hope you have a really wonderful day.”.

She then revealed the last two categories of the ceremony were to be Album of the Year and Song of the Year—two categories, as she joked, she’s quite familiar with.

Industry observers note that "The last two categories to be confirmed—one of them I've had a lot of luck in,” the 36-year-old mentioned, before quipping. “The other one I'm zero for eight.”.

After all, she has been nominated for Album of the Year seven times—earning her eighth just seconds after her onscreen appearance for The Life of a Showgirl—and has won the category a record four times.

As for Song of the Year, the Midnights artist has indeed been nominated eight times but has yet to bring home the Grammy. Maybe the ninth time will prove the charm, having been nominated for “I Knew It, I Knew You,” the song she wrote for Toy Story 5.

She’s facing some stiff competition, however. Also nominated in the category are “Berghain” by Rosalía, Björk and Yves Tumor, “Choosin’ Texas” by Ella Langley, “The Cure” by Olivia Rodrigo, “Hate That I Made You Love Me” by Ariana Grande, “Man I Need” by Olivia Dean, “Risk It All” by Bruno Mars and “Where Is My Husband!” by Raye, who earned the most nominees of any artist with 11.

The Album of the Year battle will be easily as fraught, with powerhouses such as Phoebe Bridgers’ first album in six years, Lost Weekend, Dean’s The Art of Loving, Langley’s Dandelion, Mars’ The Romantic, Raye’s This Music May Contain Hope, Rodrigo’s You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So In Love and Rosalía’s Lux.
