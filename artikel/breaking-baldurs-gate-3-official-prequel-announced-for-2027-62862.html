Recent findings suggest that the world of Baldur’s Gate 3 is expanding beyond video games once again, with a upcoming official prequel novel set to explore the darker corners of the Forgotten Realms. The upcoming book will take readers beneath the streets of Baldur’s Gate, where a murder threatens to expose the secrets of a secretive religious order.

In a recent development, titled Baldur’s Gate 3: Shadowheart, the upcoming fantasy novel comes from Tasha Suri, the World Fantasy Award-winning author of the Burning Kingdoms trilogy. It is scheduled to arrive on June 8, 2027, and is available to preorder now at major retailers.

As the title suggests, the story centers on Shadowheart, the cleric of Shar who became one of the most popular companions in Larian Studios' hit RPG.

According to the official book description, Shadowheart is a devoted follower of the goddess of darkness and loss. Living beneath Baldur’s Gate in a secretive underground church, she has embraced the belief that sacrificing memories is the key to gaining strength.

Sources indicate that however, when a member of her religious order is murdered in the city's wealthy Upper City, Shadowheart is tasked with investigating alongside several fellow acolytes. Among them are an ambitious Griefguard named Mirie, a cynical propagandist named Caldan, and Nocturne, a charming quartermaster whose intentions Shadowheart has reason to question.The investigation soon reveals that Baldur’s Gate's wealthiest district has plenty of secrets of its own. As Shadowheart encounters unfamiliar temptations and begins questioning the comforts of her faith, she must solve the murder before her devotion is called into question.

Industry observers note that the novel offers another opportunity to explore the backstories and world of Baldur’s Gate 3 beyond the game itself. While the book's description doesn't reveal exactly how its events connect to the RPG's broader timeline, its focus on Shadowheart's life within Shar's church should give fans a closer look at the character's history.

Industry observers note that the Shadowheart novel is the latest expansion of the Baldur’s Gate 3 universe beyond video games. Astarion, another fan-favorite companion, has already received his own prequel novel written by T. Kingfisher, alongside other character-focused stories and comics. The franchise is also heading to television, with HBO developing an official Baldur’s Gate series from The Last of Us co-creator Craig Mazin. Unlike the upcoming Shadowheart novel, the show is expected to pick up after the events of Baldur’s Gate 3.

Baldur’s Gate 3: Shadowheart releases June 8, 2027.
