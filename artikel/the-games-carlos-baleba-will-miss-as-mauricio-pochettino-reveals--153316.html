Manchester United midfielder Carlos Baleba will not play again this year with Mauricio Pochettino revealing the full extent of his injury blow.

Baleba was forced off in the second-half of United’s Champions League clash against Roma on 3 November with a hamstring injury.

The former Brighton star made a bursting run into the box but pulled up just as he was about to shoot on goal and appeared to be in tears as he limped off the Old Trafford pitch.

Baleba has not featured since with Michael Carrick explaining in the days that followed: ‘We don’t know exactly, but it’ll be a number of weeks.

Recent findings suggest that ‘It’s definitely not this week and it won’t be any time over the international break. It’ll be a little bit longer than that.’.

Carrick has since been replaced by Pochettino at Old Trafford but the Argentine will not get the chance to work with Baleba just yet.

Asked for an update on the Cameroon international, Pochettino revealed he would be sidelined for 10-12 weeks, dating back to when the injury first occurred.

That timescale rules him out of the entire festive period and possibly until the end of January.
