Upcoming details regarding Kirby and the World Beyond have been revealed via Nintendo's winter catalogue in Japan (via Genki_JPN on X). While hardly comprehensive, it provides a little more insight into the game's story along with the abilities and environments available upon its release.

Of course, we know from the debut trailer that this will boast a much broader world than The Forgotten Land, with one detail noting how you can use Kirby's abilities to explore at your own pace. We also get a touch more context behind King Dedede's role, with the article stating that "a mysterious girl" has been kidnapped by the antagonist.

Here's a rundown of the newly introduced information:.

According to recent reports, - Use copy abilities to adventure freely in a wide world. - Explore a vast and diverse 3D field with grasslands and waterfalls etc. - Use Kirby’s copy abilities to explore at your own pace. - Kirby’s copy abilities have been powered up! - Ride a Cutter to travel at high speed. - Ride a Bomb and do huge jumps with the blast. - Glide softly through the sky with a Parasol. - A mysterious girl has been captured by King Dedede. - Dedede was sent flying leaving a huge hole in the sky. - “What awaits Kirby beyond other side of the massive wall he destroyed with his newfound powers?”.

Kirby and the World Beyond is right now scheduled to launch in Spring 2027. It follows Metroid Ravenous on 28th January 2027, while Xenoblade Genesis and Pokémon Winds & Waves are also expected to launch in the same year.
