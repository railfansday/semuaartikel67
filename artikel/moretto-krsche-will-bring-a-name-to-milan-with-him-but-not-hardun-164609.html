Markus Krösche will soon begin his work as AC Milan’s newly introduced Head of Football, but Timmo Hardung will not be joining him.

It has not been a well-kept secret that Milan have wanted to welcome Krösche as the latest head of the management since the summer. Eintracht Frankfurt put up a wall a few months ago, but the German has since left his position and now seems all set for a new adventure in Italy.

Compensation has been agreed to release him fully from Eintracht, and the only question mark swirling concerned whether he would be joined by his right-hand man Hardung, with whom he has worked for years.

Recent findings suggest that in a video published on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, Matteo Moretto took stock of the situation regarding the possible arrival of Hardung at Milan as sporting director alongside Krösche. His words are via MilanNews.

“I wanted to focus on Timmo Hardung, in the sense that I would like to specify a situation linked to the Rossoneri organisation that already exists, what he will become and what could be in the future,” he stated.

“Everything has been said about Krosche at AC Milan and we are only waiting for the official announcement. Krosche will certainly bring someone with him, but this person, at the moment, is not Timmo Hardung, sporting director of Eintracht, Krosche’s right-hand man for a long time.

“There’s been talk that Hardung isn’t coming to Milan because of a deteriorating relationship with Markus Krösche. I actually wanted to deny this, in the sense that the two are on very good terms and still have a great rapport. This summer, the two worked closely together at Eintracht. So, in fact, they have an excellent relationship.

It has been revealed that “If Hardung doesn’t join Krosche at Milan as sporting director, it’s certainly for other reasons, namely that perhaps there’s no room for Hardung in the structure and organisation designed by Gerry Cardinale. So it would be a choice tied to other dynamics within the Rossoneri.

According to recent reports, “I can confirm that Krosche will bring one name, we’ll see if he then adds another name, which would be Hardung, but I think we’ll find out later. Contacts are in progress, but it’s not yet 100% clear whether Hardung will be able to join Krosche.”.
