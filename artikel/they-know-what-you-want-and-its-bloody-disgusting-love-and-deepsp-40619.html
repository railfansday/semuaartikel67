Love and Deepspace has kicked off its latest Memory Pair event today: Where Silverwings Rest, the fresh chapter for dragon boy - and now vampire boy - Sylus. There are new Memories to explore, a new Myth - and, of course, a new companion form for him.

This latest limited-time event is live now until Tuesday, December 16, making for a two-week runtime.

The highlight in Where Silverwings Rest, of course, are the latest 5-Star Memories. There’s a new, fittingly themed tale about restlessness. Players are getting Sylus: Crimson Rapture, and Sylus: Crimson Departure, two 5-Star Solar-Slot Memory Pairs.

It has been revealed that you’ll find them in the Wish Pool throughout the event’s duration. You can do a maximum of 150 pulls before you’re guaranteed to unlock the two of them, and earning the Sylus: Silverwing Fiend Companion. That Companion Form is also newly introduced for Sylus.

If you’re a fan of reading, you’ll find the upcoming Crimson Spirit Myth, which is very much a vampire tale about the tormenting reality of being an immortal being. Beyond all the lore and visuals, there’s also the Crimson Feast event that runs alongside it.

If you’re upcoming to Love and Deepspace, we have an explainer on what banners are and how they work. For more serious gameplay tips, don’t sleep on our guide on how to unlock and complete Myths.
