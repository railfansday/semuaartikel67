The Luis Milla curler into the far top corner proved enough for Como to see off AEK Athens and surge towards the Champions League Round of 16, despite Lucas Da Cunha’s missed penalty.

The Lariani had won three of their first four Champions League games, despite missing Moise Kean and Assane Diao, with Maxence Caqueret not at 100 per cent either, but Trevoh Chalobah returned five weeks after his muscular injury.

AEK Athens lost their last three in Europe, but were leading Real Madrid twice until capitulating 3-2 in stoppages. Aboubakary Koita was sent off for dissent and therefore suspended, with midfielder Kervin Arriaga injured, but ex-Lazio goalkeeper Thomas Strakosha made his Champions League debut for this season after a wrist sprain.

See how it all unfolded on the Liveblog.

Lazaros Rota made a decisive defensive block to stop Tasos Douvikas meeting the Nico Paz through ball at full stretch, then Stavros Pilios got back to block Nico Paz on the counter, but the Argentine was booked for flailing an elbow into the defender’s face.

There were a few technical problems with the referee’s communication system, both before kick-off and during the first half, but he didn’t need help from VAR when Lovro Majer was very late to trip Alex Valle in the box.

Sources indicate that however, Lucas Da Cunha slipped a little in the run-up and fired the penalty off target for Como. It was the first time in his career that the France international had failed to convert from the spot.

Jacobo Ramon couldn’t keep his header down from eight yards, and Alex Valle wasted a promising move with his scuffed finish past the near post.

Robert Sanchez needed a huge double save on the stroke of half-time, first on the Mijat Gacinovic deflected effort, then the Rota rebound too.

AEK had a huge chance after the restart, but Milan Vitalis couldn’t keep the finish down under pressure from a promising position after doing such good work to get into that situation.

Como survived the scares and took the lead when Luis Milla bent a right-foot curler into the far top corner from outside the area, on the Martin Baturina lay-off.

Nico Paz so nearly made it 2-0 when AEK gave it away playing out from the back, so Valle sent the Argentine through to clip the base of the near post.

Jacobo Ramon’s header was just wide on a free kick, with the same for the Moukoudi attempt on a free kick from ex-Cagliari midfielder Razvan Marin.

Maxi Perrone had a great chance to add another in the final minute, but was denied by a desperate one-handed Strakosha save.

Missed penalty: Da Cunha 29 (C).
