In a recent development, jurgen Klopp has dropped a major hint for Liverpool. It's one they simply have to take advantage of.

According to recent reports, jurgen Klopp left an incredible legacy at Liverpool and it's one that few have ever matched. It makes the decision to essentially move on very promptly from his time at the club a strange one.

Recent findings suggest that the reaction to his exit was an overhaul. While that could be seen as an acceptance that things must be done differently without Klopp, it felt drastic. Especially as the squad he ultimately built did go on to win the Premier League.

It has been revealed that but Liverpool tore it up the following summer. A large portion of that squad built for the future has already left, scattered across the Premier League and beyond after transfers.

They also immediately moved away from his high-intensity style of play and shifted the formation. Everything has changed very promptly - and not necessarily for the better. In fact, few could say it was for the better.

Hopefully, then, Liverpool can listen to him a bit now.

Sources indicate that klopp is now in charge of Germany, of course, and that means he's working closely with Florian Wirtz. Wirtz is widely regarded as the best German talent - just one that hasn't found his feet at Liverpool in over a year.

Industry observers note that not that you'd know that by watching him under Klopp. Wirtz quite plainly does look like the top talent, and he indicated that again in a destruction of the Netherlands on Monday evening.

The playmaker scored twice and earned all sorts of plaudits. Deservedly so, too, as his two goals were fantastic.

Industry observers note that “I only knew him from television. I was shocked when I saw him on the pitch for the first time,” Klopp noted after the match.

According to recent reports, “An extraordinary footballer. We have also found the right position for Flo. It helps that he is so vocal. He is the first pressing player, can press two players without getting tired.”.

In a recent development, and there's the hint for Liverpool - they've found the right position. This is something the Reds haven't really done, with Arne Slot asking Wirtz to play the same congested position for almost the entirety of last season.

Even when it was apparent that it wouldn't work, he persisted. The result was Wirtz just not looking like Wirtz. Barely involved and unable to influence things.

Recent findings suggest that andoni Iraola has tried to fix that. Wirtz is afforded more space and more of a license to roam around the pitch, particularly between the lines. That gives us a better player, certainly, but there's still room to grow here.

But Klopp's hint is the key. It's about finding the right position for him - not just a no.10 but specifically with the team's shape and the options around him. Do that and you get an elite player.

The one Liverpool paid all that money for.
