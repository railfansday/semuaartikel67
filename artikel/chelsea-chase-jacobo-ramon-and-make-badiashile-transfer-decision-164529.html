Chelsea are planning to bring in defensive reinforcements in 2027, and while they continue to follow Como’s Jacobo Ramon, they have no intends to recall Benoit Badiashile from his loan spell at Napoli, reports The Athletic.

Chelsea’s transfer looks are strongly tied to Serie A, especially in defence.

The Athletic reports that the Blues are closely monitoring Como centre-back Ramon, who has recently received his first senior call-up with the Spanish national team.

The report claims that the Spaniard is admired at Stamford Bridge, though a deal would only be possible in the summer transfer window.

Ramon is formally a Como player, but Real Madrid have an option to re-sign him at the end of the season, just as they do for his teammate Nico Paz.

Chelsea’s Premier League rivals Arsenal and Manchester United have also been linked with Ramon in recent weeks.

The Athletic adds that Chelsea have no plans to recall any of their first-team loanees in the January window, including Napoli’s Badiashile.

The French defender moved to Napoli on loan last summer with a €27m option to buy and has recently returned to group training after an injury that sidelined him for several weeks.

The 25-year-old has made five appearances for Napoli in all competitions this season.
