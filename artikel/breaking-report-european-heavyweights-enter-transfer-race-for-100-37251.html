Liverpool are one of the most well-known football teams in the world.

The Anfield outfit have always been challenging for titles and silverware with the expectations of their fans rather high.

That is, especially after what Jurgen Klopp did while in charge of the club.

Since his departure, the Reds have won a Premier League title and have spent well over £600 million on new players.

And, while this may seem like a lot of money, many more changes need to take place before the Reds are back to their best.

So, not only does this mean more money needs to be spent but, it also means that Liverpool will have to cast their net far and wide to sign all the players that they need to.

Bayern Munich and Barcelona have joined the race for Aleksey Batrakov.

One of the main problems Liverpool have faced this season is a lack of creativity.

The Anfield outfit have tried to rely on Florian Wirtz to create their chances but, he's constantly failed to meet expectations.

This has forced other players among the Reds' ranks to try and step up but, with the club currently sitting fourth in the table, that clearly hasn't worked out.

So, that's why a lot of Liverpool's recent transfer links have been with playmakers such as Aleksey Batrakov.

The Russian has been in fine form for Galatasaray this term with four of his seven goals coming in the Champions League.

In fact, his form has been so good that, according to Turkish outlet Takvim, Bayern Munich and Barcelona have also entered the race for the 21-year-old.

"Galatasaray's 21-year-old Russian star, Aleksey Batrakov, has attracted the attention of leading European clubs," they wrote.

"While Barcelona and Bayern Munich are reportedly watching the young footballer closely, Galatasaray's management has taken steps to increase his €55 million release clause to €100 million.".

Liverpool can't spend €100m on Aleksey Batrakov.

Right now, Batrakov has a release clause worth around €55 million.

In pounds, that equates to around £45 million.

Obviously, this is a lot of money for a 21-year-old but, considering Liverpool spent £40 million on Milos Kerkez, it doesn't seem all that bad.

Since joining Galatasaray in the summer, Batrakov has contributed to 10 goals in 13 games.

The Turkish Super Lig outfit have mostly played him as a number 10 but, there has been the odd occasion when he was used as a number 8.

Compared to someone such as Wirtz or Dominik Szoboszlai, he may not seem as good but, from the games he's featured in this season, you can tell that the youngster has a bright future ahead of him.

Right now, it's unclear what Liverpool are going to do but, if Galatasaray do manage to up his release clause to €100 million that would be far too much money to risk on a relatively unproven youngster.
