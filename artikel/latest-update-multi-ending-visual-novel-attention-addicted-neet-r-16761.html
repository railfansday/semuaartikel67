Publisher Kniv Studio and developer Dreamery have unveiled Attention Addicted NEET, a multi-ending visual novel where players experience the changes in a girl who shuts herself away at home because she doesn’t want to get a job. It will be available for PC via Steam. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Attention Addicted NEET is a visual novel with a multi-ending story, where you experience the changes in a girl who shuts herself away at home because she doesn’t want to get a job.

When her influencer little sister cuts off her allowance, telling her to ‘go out and get a job,’ she reluctantly turns to social media.

She thought social media influencers made money easily.

Since then, she has been carried along by attention unlike anything she has ever experienced, gradually becoming addicted to it.

The attention she gets from the fools in internet communities makes her sad, excited, and sometimes angry.

The job her little sister hoped she’d get grows more distant, while her follower count keeps rising.

Will our protagonist, Miyu, give up all this social media influencer business and find a stable job, as her little sister wishes?

Watch and see what becomes of her life.
