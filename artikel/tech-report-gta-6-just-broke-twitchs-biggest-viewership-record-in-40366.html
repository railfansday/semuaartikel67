If you thought the hype surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6 couldn't possibly get any bigger, the game's launch has already found a way to prove you wrong! After becoming one of the highest-rated games ever, more impressive numbers are popping up.

While most players are still waiting for November 19 to arrive (less than 24 hours now, folks!), fans in regions like New Zealand have already gotten their hands on Rockstar's long-awaited open-world adventure. And it took just minutes for the game to make streaming history.

According to data from Streams Charts, GTA 6 already attracted more than 5.3 million concurrent viewers across major livestreaming platforms shortly after becoming available in New Zealand. That figure was already enough to smash the previous record for a video game launch, and the numbers have continued climbing as the release rolls out to more countries.

By the time the game became available in Japan and South Korea, its combined audience had reached an astonishing 7.18 million concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick. And that's before every other region has even had a chance to jump into Vice City!

In a recent development, to put things in a broader perspective, the previous record for the biggest video game launch in streaming history belonged to Valorant. Riot Games' shooter attracted approximately 1.74 million concurrent viewers during its closed beta in April 2020, a number that seemed almost impossible to beat at the time.

In a recent development, well, GTA 6 has more than quadrupled that figure. According to Streams Charts' preliminary data, the game right now leads its ranking of the biggest game rolls out by Peak Viewers with 7,183,979 concurrent viewers. Hogwarts Legacy follows in third place with approximately 1.36 million, while Path of Exile 2 and Lost Ark round out the top five.

By the time the game became available in Japan and South Korea, its combined audience had reached an astonishing 7.18 million concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.

Recent findings suggest that the numbers are particularly impressive considering that the game's worldwide release is still underway. As more players gain access, additional streamers are expected to go live, potentially pushing the record even higher!

Industry observers note that it is also worth looking at how the audience is distributed across platforms. Twitch currently leads with a peak of 3.06 million viewers, followed by YouTube with 2.74 million and Kick with 1.36 million. Twitch's figure makes GTA 6 the second-most-watched gaming category in the platform's history, narrowly behind League of Legends and its 3.11 million peak.

According to recent reports, besides, the game's launch has also been an vital moment for individual creators. Streams Charts reports that HoneyMad right now leads the list of streamers with the highest concurrent audiences, reaching 576,313 viewers on Twitch. And Brazilian streamer alanzoka also had a huge launch, setting a fresh personal record with 313,548 live viewers.

And to be completely honest, it is hard to imagine anyone being particularly surprised by these numbers! GTA 5 has remained one of the biggest games on streaming platforms for more than a decade, and its successor has arrived with an unprecedented level of anticipation.

In a recent development, of course, it is vital to remember that these figures are still preliminary. Streams Charts notes that its latest data was collected at 12:30 UTC on November 18, and the numbers are expected to change as the game becomes available in more regions. Still, GTA 6 has already accomplished something no other game launch has managed before. If this is what the first few hours look like, I can't wait to see what happens once everyone can finally play.
