Where Was 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping' Filmed? Behind the Scenes in Spain and Germany

Industry observers note that on Location peels back the curtain on some of your favorite films, television reveals, and more. This time, we take a look at The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

In a recent development, we’re heading back to Panem for the sixth time this autumn, as the latest Hunger Games prequel hits screens. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is set 24 years before the action of the first film and tells the origin story of Katniss Everdeen’s grumpy tutor, Haymitch Abernathy, as he enters the Games himself.

Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy and Whitney Peak as Lenore Dove Baird.

Based on the books by Suzanne Collins, the dystopian series is set in a futuristic America in which each district must send a teenage tribute to fight to the death in an annual competition—the idea being that, by doing so, the public will be too afraid to rebel against President Snow.

It has been revealed that this edition of the games is known as the Quarter Quell, with double the number of tributes being sent into the arena, meaning the stakes are so much higher. Interest in the film is high, not least because Haymitch is so beloved in the original series, portrayed by Woody Harrelson. Newcomer Joseph Zada takes the lead role in the latest film, while Hollywood heavyweights Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Elle Fanning, and Kieran Culkin also star.

One of the most crucial sets in the film is the arena where the games take place, and for this, director Francis Lawrence opted not for CGI and a green screen, but instead a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in northern Spain. Below, we look into the locations used in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Ben Wang as Wyatt Callow, Percy Daggs IV as Ampert Latier, Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy, and Mckenna Grace as Maysilee Donner in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Recent findings suggest that within the book and film series, the settings for the Hunger Games are always known to be virtually spectacular. For this film, filmmakers settled on the vast and untouched landscape of Somiedo Natural Park in the Asturias region of northern Spain, which boasts mountain ranges, glacial valleys, beautiful meadows, dense forests, and tranquil lakes. The remote region is also home to the last brown bear population in Europe.

In a recent development, when the tributes are launched into the arena, they are presented with a vast Cornucopia, a pile of weapons, food, and other supplies that might be useful to them in the competition. These scenes were filmed at Somiedo Natural Park, with the Asturian mountains and a glacial lake in the background.

“Somiedo is a spectacular place,” said Pedro Aráez, supervising location manager. “The valleys, forests, and mountains have a very wild, untouched feeling, which was exactly what the filmmakers were looking for. It’s remote, dramatic, and visually unique. When you arrive there, you immediately understand why it was chosen.”.

Located close to Somiedo, the Teverga beech forest, Hayedo de Montegrande, was used for some of the dense woodland scenes, in which contestants battle it out and hide in the woods. Other scenes filmed here utilized a series of glacial lakes, the Saliencia Lakes and the Lago de la Cueva.

Iris Apatow as Proserpina Trinket and Elle Fanning as Effie Trinket in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Before the tributes are released into the arena, they are first held in special little pods underground which are arranged in a circle around the Cornucopia. The perfect location for this? A velodrome, of course, which is steeply circular so cyclists can race around it. The film crew utilized Berlin’s Velodrom for this very purpose, with its cold, clinical silver decor perfect for matching the hi-tech aesthetic associated with the Capitol.

Ralph Fiennes as President Snow.

Prior to entering the Games, tributes must introduce themselves on the public stage, during a televised grand chariot parade in the Capitol. Another Berlin location with links to the sporting world was used for this purpose: the Olympiastadion. For fans of the earlier films, this is the moment when Jennifer Lawrence’s character Katniss becomes “the girl on fire” with her incredible flaming dress.

The futuristic architecture of Edificio Calatrava in Oviedo, Spain—with its striking, claw-like structure—stands in for a building in the Capitol, where President Snow and his government live. The complex was designed by Spanish architect Santiago Calatrava, whose work is known for resembling living organisms. Meanwhile, the 14th-century Ehreshoven Castle, just outside of Cologne, was also used as a filming location, with local press reporting that its lush interiors might have been used to stand in for somewhere inside the Capitol.

A former smelting factory complex in western Germany, Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord provides the backdrop for one of the most atmospheric settings in the films, Haymitch’s home of District 12. The vast complex is able to stand in for multiple places within the district, a former mining town that has fallen on seriously bad times. Rather than being a desolate area stumbled upon by the filmmakers, Landschaftspark is popular for its nightly light installation by British artist Jonathan Park. There are also regular al fresco events here, from film screenings to the annual art-meets-sport Ruhr Games and Traumzeit Festival. Another location in Germany, Lindlar Quarry, was used for District 12, with wooden huts built here to resemble the outskirts of the village.
