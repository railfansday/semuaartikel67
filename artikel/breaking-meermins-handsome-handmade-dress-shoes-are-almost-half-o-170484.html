Sources indicate that packing so many high-quality details into a single shoe is tough, but only Meermin can sell them for as low as they do. Now the Meermin Black Friday sale is taking the Spanish label’s already affordable, handmade shoes down even further in price. Goodyear-welted construction, top-notch French calfskin and shell cordovan hides, and finer details like beveled waists and closed-channel stitching were just some of the reasons to believe that Meermin’s loafers and boots were worth at least half a grand. Thankfully for the menswear enjoyers of the world, the expert craftsmanship is served up DTC which means we can have our Goodyear-welted cakes and eat them, too.

Recent findings suggest that and for Black Friday, the value gets better. While most of Meermin’s shoes will only run you about $250, the brand is cutting its prices down a full 40% across the board. That means handsome, do-it-all penny loafers, rain-cruising boots, slick Chelseas—even accessories like belts and passport holders—are all on sale. If that doesn’t get you out of your turkey-trodden stupor, we don’t know what will.
