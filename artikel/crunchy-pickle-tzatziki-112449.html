The only ingredient that can make creamy, fresh tzatziki even better is pickles! Our spin on the classic Greek dip adds finely chopped pickles to make an upgraded dip that's briny, tangy and crunchy. Just like the classic version, our pickle tzatziki recipe is perfect for serving with fresh veggies, pita wedges, meat and Greek-inspired dishes. —Sarah Farmer, Waukesha, Wisconsin.
