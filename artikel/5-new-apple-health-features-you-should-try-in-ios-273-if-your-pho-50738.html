5 new Apple Health features you should try in iOS 27.3 – if your phone qualifies

According to recent reports, apple is kicking off a upcoming and improved Health app next month with the release of iOS 27.3. Instead of just recording and displaying your health-related stats, the app will tap into Apple Intelligence to analyze the data and dispense advice on how to improve your health and fitness. But that reliance on AI means you’ll need the right type of iPhone to enjoy all the benefits.

Also: Apple could build its AI health chatbot from this study – how to enroll.

Here are five helpful new features built into the app, along with what you’ll need to access them.

First up, the app will offer suggestions based on data from different sources. For example, it might advise you to go for a walk at a nearby park if it detects that you’re not getting enough exercise. It may show you articles and videos about sleep if it finds that you’re not well-rested. It could also highlight possible conditions based on your lab and test results. You can easily view these recommendations from the Insights screen.

Also: Health is Tim Cook’s defining legacy – and your Apple Watch proves it.

Recent findings suggest that A upcoming feature called Health Age compares your physical health to your actual age to show how you’re doing. The analysis draws on certain stats and lab data, such as your resting heart rate, sleep, heart rate variability (HRV), cholesterol, and A1C (average blood glucose levels), to deliver a health age, which may differ from your chronological age. The results can show you areas that may be of concern or that you need to focus on.

Also: Apple Watch Series 12 review: Finally, the health tracker I’ve been waiting for.

With a new feature called Movement Evaluations and an iPhone and Apple Watch, the Health app can assess your flexibility, strength, balance, VO2 max (maximum oxygen absorption), and overall movement. You perform a physical routine while your iPhone “watches” you via the camera. Using built-in AI, the app will provide audio and visual feedback in real-time to tell you how you’re faring.

Apple promises that the analysis is performed on your device without recording, storing, or sharing the video.

According to recent reports, the upcoming app will be able to correlate your test results with data collected by your iPhone and Apple Watch to give you a more comprehensive picture of your health. Plus, Apple and Quest Diagnostics have teamed up to offer people in the US an optional $119 bloodwork panel that covers more than 50 biomarkers to scan for different health and medical conditions.

It has been revealed that normally, you need an Apple Fitness+ subscription to fire up workout videos to help you exercise. But the upcoming Health app will come with its own comprehensive set of training videos. Available from the Insights section, a series of strength exercise videos are designed to help you build your body strength. The videos cover all the key areas of your body to give you a full workout.

According to recent reports, some of the fresh features, such as the strength videos, will be available to all iPhone customers. But the ones that rely on AI will run only on phones that support Apple Intelligence. That means you’ll need an iPhone 15 Pro or Pro Max, any iPhone 16 or 17, an iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max, an iPhone Air, or an iPhone Duo.
