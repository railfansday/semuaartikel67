By TAMARA PRENN, SENIOR SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 11:10 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 04:10 EST, 23 November 2026.

Footage has emerged of Ange Postecoglou locked in a fiery exchange with a reporter after Al-Nassr's defeat to Al-Ahli - after he questioned Sadio Mane's commitment to the Saudi Pro League club.

The side sits third in the league standings, but were humbled in the 3-1 loss to their fourth-placed rivals on the road.

Mane was not named in the matchday squad despite registering two assists in the club's last outing, a 6-2 victory against Asian Champions League opponents Pakhtakor FC.

One Saudi journalist is believed to have used Mane's absence to suggest that he was not sufficiently engaged with Al-Nassr's campaign this season.

But even the suggestion was enough to incense the former Tottenham head coach, who immediately asked if the journalist was 'a father?'.

'Do you have children?' he queried, with the journalist responding that he did not.

Ange Postecoglou snapped at the journalist who questioned the strength of Sadio Mane's commitment to Al-Nassr.

Should footballers ever be criticised for choosing family over club commitments?

The Senegal forward missed the match as he and his wife await the birth of their second child.

'That's probably why you ask the question,' Postecoglou hit back. 'Because when you do, you'll realise it's the most important thing, the biggest blessing you can have.

'(Mane) is with his wife while she is having a baby, OK? Nothing is more vital in life.

'Yes, he had my permission, my blessing, and Sadio Mane is one of the best people in this world, mate.

'Never question him or his commitment to this football club.'.

Mane welcomed his first child with wife Aisha Timba last year, after marrying the then-18-year-old in January 2024.

In his first match following the happy news, Mane scored and celebrated by rocking an imaginary baby in his arms.

Postecoglou and Mane have only worked with one another since the beginning of the season, but appear to have formed a strong bond with Mane a frequent starter in the Riyadh-based starting XI.

The Antipodean manager joined the club following a spell without work after his ignominious departure from Nottingham Forest last season.

Since arriving in the Gulf state, Postecoglou has overseen a strong domestic campaign, with Al-Nassr registering just one previous defeat this season, against Al-Ettifaq.

But Friday's loss may have stung all the more after Al-Nassr initially led against their bitter rivals.

Cristiano Ronaldo had opened the scoring for the visitors on Friday evening in Jeddah, but missed a penalty striking the far post and failed to double his side's lead.

Instead, it was Ivan Toney who was nerveless from the spot as he netted to put Al-Ahli 2-1 up in the 71st minute, with Roger Ibanez providing the winner four minutes later.
