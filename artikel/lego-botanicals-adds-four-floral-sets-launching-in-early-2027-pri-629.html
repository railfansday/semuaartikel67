Lego Botanicals adds four floral sets launching in early 2027: Price and release details

Sources indicate that if you want a tabletop centerpiece that never wilts or asks for water, the Lego Botanicals line just revealed its next wave of builds. The toy giant unveiled four fresh sets arriving in early 2027, expanding its collection of plastic greenery with a mix of standalone potted arrangements and multi-stem bouquets.

Recent findings suggest that the new drop covers a wide range of styles, from rare blue orchids and blush peonies to delicate sakura branches and a pastel mix designed to fill your own vases. True to the collection's signature design quirks, the builds repurpose familiar Lego elements in clever ways, such as bright green dinosaur eggs posing as budding peonies and minifigure crowns serving as cherry blossom centers.

Industry observers note that three of the sets arrive on New Year's Day, while the pastel bouquet lands just in time for Valentine's Day. Here's a closer look at each new build coming to the garden:.

In a recent development, unlike most flower builds that require you to find a spare jar or vase around the house, the Lego Botanicals Cherry Blossom Branches comes as an all-in-one display unit. The 405-piece build features realistic branches packed with white sakura petals and pale pink buds, all seated directly inside an ocean-blue brick-built vase. It also carries Lego’s signature design quirks, repurposing minifigure crowns in fresh colorways to build the buds and flower centers.

Measuring over 8 inches tall, 7 inches wide, and 5.5 inches deep, it hits a rare sweet spot for home decor: compact enough to fit neatly on a crowded bookshelf or work desk without dominating the whole surface.

Most Lego botanical sets recreate garden-variety stems, but the Lego Botanicals Wild Orchid tackles one of the most distinctive, hard-to-grow plants in nature: the rare blue Vanda coerulea. The 606-piece build stands over 11.5 inches tall, 13 inches wide, and 4.5 inches deep, functioning as a complete self-contained forest ecosystem rather than just loose flowers.

According to recent reports, instead of sitting in a traditional pot, the vibrant blue blossoms and aerial roots sprout directly from a textured, mossy branch accented with miniature wildlife. Lego introduced new printed petal elements specifically for this release to mirror the natural veining and delicate look of real orchid petals. You can reposition the stems and flowers to customize the shape, giving you an eye-catching tabletop centerpiece that captures the detail of a live botanical display.

In a recent development, peonies are notorious for shedding petals and drooping within a few days of bringing them home, but the Lego Botanicals Peony Bouquet gives you the full, layered look without the short lifespan. The 633-piece set features a mix of single blooms, semi-double flowers, and buds at different stages of opening, blending shades of ombré peach, pale pink, and deep pink with bright yellow stamens.

Recent findings suggest that in classic Lego fashion, the designer's use of parts steals the show: bright green dinosaur eggs are repurposed along the branches to recreate rounded peony buds about to open. With the longest stem measuring over 16.5 inches, the entire arrangement fits in standard household vases without extra props.

In a recent development, if you already own a vase and want a wide variety of stems to arrange yourself, the Lego Botanicals Perfectly Pastel Flower Bouquet delivers the most diverse floral mix in the entire 2027 drop. The 650-piece set includes 16 individual stems spanning eight different varieties: three hybrid tea roses, three coneflowers, two columbines, two anemones, two pincushion flowers, a stock flower, phlox, and sword ferns.

It has been revealed that lego kept the color palette soft with muted pastel tones, but hid a neat Easter egg inside: a tiny Lego beehive element tucked directly into the cone of one of the flowers. Measuring over 15 inches tall, the stems are fully adjustable, so you can lengthen, shorten, or reposition each blossom to suit different vase heights or mix them with earlier Lego bouquets for an even fuller display.

Between Latest Year's Day shelf refreshes and early Valentine's Day shopping, you can shop all four upcoming arrangements on Lego.
