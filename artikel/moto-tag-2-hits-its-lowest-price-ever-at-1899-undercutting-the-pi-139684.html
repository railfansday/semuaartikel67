Motorola’s Moto Tag 2 is the only Find Hub tracker for Android with UWB support besides the new Google Pixel Tag, and the former is presently at its lowest price ever for Black Friday at just $19.

Industry observers note that nearly half off its retail price, Moto Tag 2 is right now just $18.99 on Amazon for Black Friday. The discount only applies to the beige color variant, but the more exciting orange color is also down to $29.98 right now, way lower than the $40~ it’s usually sold at (which is because Motorola technically only sells it in a 4-pack in the US).

In a recent development, the Moto Tag 2 may not be quite as deeply integrated into Google’s Find Hub as the Pixel Tag is, but it has all of the same core features. UWB for precision finding beyond just a beeping sound or dot on a map, support for Google’s “left behind” alerts, and the ability to ring your phone (via Motorola’s app). It also comes with the advantage of having a design that works alongside Apple’s AirTag accessories.

Sources indicate that if I were looking to stock up this Black Friday, I’d be picking Moto Tag 2 over the Pixel Tag at these prices. Even in a 4-pack, Pixel Tag is much more expensive, and Moto Tag 2 works great. Plus, Pixel Tag shipments (at least from Amazon) are delayed right now anyway.
