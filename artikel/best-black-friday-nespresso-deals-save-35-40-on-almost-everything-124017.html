In a recent development, introduced in 2014, Nespresso’s Vertuoline modernized the brand’s original pressure brew method and marked the true beginning of Nespresso espresso and coffee machines. Most Vertuo machines feature just one button and brew both coffee and espresso-style shots using a centrifugal method. Once you choose your preferred pod based on style, roast, and cup size, the machine takes care of the rest. That’s because the rim of every dome-shaped Vertuo capsule features a barcode that tells the Nespresso Vertuo machine how much water and what extraction speed to use for each pod—and can brew everything from a single and double espresso shot to a 14-ounce cup of coffee. Some Vertuo machines can even fill an 18-ounce carafe from a single coffee pod.
