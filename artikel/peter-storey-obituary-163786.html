Arsenal midfielder capped by England 19 times whose life after football suffered a drink-fuelled decline.

The footballer Peter Storey, who has died aged 81, was a tough-tackling midfielder for Arsenal when they won the double of the First Division championship and the FA Cup in 1971.

Arsenal were only the fourth team to achieve the double at that point (it has since been won another nine times), and Storey, who appeared in 49 of the 51 league and FA Cup games that season, played as big a role as anyone in securing the honours with his defensive solidity, ball-winning skills, “good engine”, sound distribution and reliable occasional penalty taking.
