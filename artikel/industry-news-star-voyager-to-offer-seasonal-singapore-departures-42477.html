Industry News: Star Voyager to Offer Seasonal Singapore Departures Through 2029

StarDream Cruises has partnered with the Singapore Tourism Board (STB) for a three-year seasonal deployment of the Star Voyager in Singapore through 2029.

The deployment of the nearly 2,000-guest ship from StarCruises’ fleet will provide travelers with more cruise itineraries to explore Southeast Asia by sea, according to a press release.

It is also expected to create opportunities to attract regional and international fly-cruise visitors to sail from Singapore.

“Singapore has been an key market for our cruise operations for more than 30 years, and the seasonal deployment of Star Voyager builds on our long-standing presence here,” noted Michael Goh, president of StarDream Cruises.

“Through our partnership with STB, we look forward to offering travelers more opportunities to discover Asia by sea, while contributing to Singapore’s continued growth as a leading cruise hub in the region.”.

Jean Ng, assistant chief executive, Experience Development Group, Singapore Tourism Board noted that the three-year seasonal deployment of the Star Voyager, alongside the Genting Dream’s year-round sailings, reflects the cruise line’s continued confidence in Singapore as a leading cruise hub.

With itineraries departing from Singapore, the Star Voyager will take guests to destinations across Southeast Asia, including Jakarta, Krabi, Phuket, Melaka, Langkawi, Penang, Port Klang (Kuala Lumpur), Pulau Pangkor and more.

Onboard, guests can enjoy international entertainment, regional-themed programs and signature events, including the Red Carpet Gala.

According to the press release, Singapore’s strong international air connectivity and diverse tourism products and experiences make it an attractive homeport and cruising destination for international fly-cruise visitors.

Additionally, the Star Voyager’s mid-sized profile enables access to a wider range of ports across Southeast Asia, bringing more cruise visitors to regional destinations.

The Star Voyager recently wrapped up its fall season from Hong Kong, which ran from September 30 to November 13, 2026, offering two-, three- and five-night cruises.
