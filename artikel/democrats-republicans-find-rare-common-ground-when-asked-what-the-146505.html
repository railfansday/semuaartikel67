House lawmakers on both sides of the aisle spoke to Fox News Digital about what they are most thankful for as they get ready to celebrate Thanksgiving with their families.

An ocean of ideological differences separates Democrats and Republicans on Capitol Hill, but they're largely in agreement over one thing this Thanksgiving, and it's usually miles away from Washington, D.C.

"I'm thankful for family," Rep. Eric Burlison, R-Mo., told Fox News Digital. "It's what drives me to keep going, to keep doing this, keep fighting for the country.".

Families across America are gathering for Thanksgiving, kicking off a holiday season that's meant for reflection and refocusing on things that matter most. And members of Congress who spoke with Fox News Digital about that special day are no different.

But it's the family, really, and I'm grateful that my wife and my three kids who are in their 20s are so supportive," Rep. John Mannion, D-N.Y., said. "So that's what I'm thankful for.".
