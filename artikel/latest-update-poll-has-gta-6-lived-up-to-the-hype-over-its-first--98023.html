With GTA 6 launching worldwide last Thursday, I'm hoping that most of you reading this will have spent at least a few hours with the game over the weekend.

As you've no doubt noticed, it's basically been impossible to avoid Rockstar's latest release; here in the online space, it's absolutely everywhere — just as you'd expect of what's supposed to be the biggest entertainment launch of all time.

But has GTA 6 actually lived up to the hype so far? It's no stretch to suggest that this has been the most anticipated video game release ever — but that doesn't mean you have to agree with all of the 10/10 review scores the title was met with last week.

Recent findings suggest that now, obviously, I'm not asking you to give GTA 6 a review score of your own just yet (although that is coming, don't worry). I'm just wondering if the adventures of Jason and Lucia have met your expectations so far — even if you're only a couple of hours in at this point.

For what it's worth, us here at Push Square have been blown away.

Sammy made his opinion clear in our 10/10 review, of course, but now the rest of us have had a chance to jump in, it's clear that this is an incredibly special game — a generational release.

But it's also clear that GTA 6 is big — really big. For reference, I'm still taking my sweet time on Chapter 1, and the game's scale has already taken me by surprise. I can see myself playing this thing for 100 hours or more quite easily.
