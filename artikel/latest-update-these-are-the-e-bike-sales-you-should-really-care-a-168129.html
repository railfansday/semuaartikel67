It’s that time of year again; Black Friday approaches! And while the e-bike sales have been pretty good all month, there’s no better day than today to find an amazing deal on a upcoming way around your city (or a fitness e-bike, leisure e-bike, cargo e-bike, etc…)!

But this isn’t that. I’m a professional e-bike rider and tester, and the deals below are only for bikes I actually ride, and for companies I would actually recommend to my own family and friends. Basically, this is the good stuff.

Let’s start with Lectric eBikes, since, as the largest e-bike company in North America, they have long been setting the standard for incredible deals and value in the e-bike industry. I’ve long said that you can’t get better value than here. Sure, you can find lower-priced e-bikes out there, but not at this quality level and with this level of customer support.

Lectric’s sales are usually defined by huge collections of free accessories added to their already low-priced e-bikes, further increasing the value. But this year there are also some legit price drops as well. See all of their Black Friday Sales packages here.

The major standouts for me have always been the XP and XP Lite lines.

The latest of each, the XP4 and the XP Lite3, offer bundle deals with savings of up to $731 and $257, respectively. For price drops, you’ll find that both the Lectrix XPLite 2 and the XPTrike2 are marked down to newly introduced record-low prices.

I’m also a huge fan of their cargo bike, the Lectric XPedition2, which has savings of up to $405 thanks to those impressive bundles.

But you should also explore the rest of their deals because just about everything is either marked down or comes with a crazy amount of free accessories that you can’t get for this price any other time of the year!

Ride1Up is another major value leader in the space, and in fact, I’ve been using the Ride1Up Roadster V3 as my everyday commuter e-bike since the summer.

I’m set to surpass 1,000 miles on it (1,600 km) by the end of the month!

That bike is marked down $100 to just $1,295 right now, making it a major deal for a lightweight, 40 lb (18 kg) commuter e-bike that doubles as a gravel bike. Ask me how I know that it can handle hard off-roading, too!

You can also save $150 on the Vorsa, which is an awesome all-around e-bike with multiple levels for riders who want higher or lower performance.

It has been revealed that the new Portola 02 folder is a killer deal at $100 off for just $795 (that’s a crazy price!). If you want to go significant and powerful, the Revv1 will crush Super73 and is doing so for $500 off!

Last but not least, the Prodigy V2 Eco is just $1,295 after the $100 deal, which is literally the most affordable Bosch-powered electric bike in the world. The only thing stopping me from buying that bike is that I already have one, though at this price, part of me wonders if I should get a spare for friends to use!

Basically, Ride1Up has a ton of Black Friday Sales going on now, and you can check them all out here.

If you want to go a bit further up the quality ladder to get a more premium e-bike, but you still don’t want to spend $2k+, then you should check out Monarc Bikes. It was founded by Lectric eBikes as an offshoot that could focus on building higher-end models while still leveraging Lectric’s industry heavyweight status to get better pricing. That means the company can offer higher-end e-bikes that rival some of the bike shop brands, while still offering direct-to-consumer pricing, 5-year warranties, and industry-leading local customer support by phone and email.

Sources indicate that the two models they’ve launched so far are the urban-focused Tracer and the off-road-optimized Marker, though you better believe they’ve got more interesting stuff coming, likely next year. For now, though, these two models cover most folks’ needs and come with higher-end parts like quad-piston hydraulic disc brakes, reverse arch forks, Bafang’s powerful motors, large batteries, and impressive add-ons like rear-facing radar to keep tabs on approaching cars.

According to recent reports, the organization started by selling e-bikes that came with a free spare battery, but has recently opened up options for single or even no-battery bikes, meaning if you previously bought a Monarc model and already have that free spare battery, you can save a bunch of money by buying only the bike and using your spare battery across the line. Yep, it’s like the power drill model!

You can see all of the sales and deals here!

My JackRabbit OG is one of my prized possessions, and I just love how much performance and convenience JackRabbit has been able to pack into small-format two-wheelers that start at barely 25 lb (11 kg).

Literally every one of their micro e-bikes is 20% off, meaning you’re saving hundreds of dollars. But then the rest of their gear is up to 50% off, such as 30% off their significant RangeBuster batteries.

I’m never parting with my own JackRabbit, and I love seeing new riders discover just how much fun these awesomely strange bikes are!

Juiced Bikes was revived this year, and the tech giant is back with a vengeance! The high-performance bikes are better than ever, and the prices are better, too!

The recently launched Scrambler Mini leads the pack in pricing, starting at just $949 thanks to its Black Friday promotional price. That’d be an awesome model for any young riders this season. But the larger versions are also pushing the value war forward with their own great sales.

Recent findings suggest that there’s a lot to like here, from higher-end parts to 5-year warranties to the backing of the nation’s largest e-bike company, Lectric eBikes. Basically, it’s hard to imagine finding a better deal on a moto-style electric bike these days than from Juiced.
