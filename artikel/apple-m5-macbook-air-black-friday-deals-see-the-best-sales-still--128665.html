It has been revealed that black Friday is here again — one of the great holiday shopping traditions. We have our own tradition here at Mashable: making a beeline for the MacBook deals in Amazon's Black Friday sale. For most of the year, Apple prices are what they are, so it's always exciting to see just how heavily MacBooks are discounted for Black Friday.

It has been revealed that if you're in the market for the lighter, slimmer, more portable MacBook Air, you're in luck. Amazon has slashed prices across the 2026 range, with savings of up to $300. These models are powered by the mighty M5 chip, which was until very recently the most powerful processor in Apple's MacBook arsenal. (You can also find a deal on the M6 MacBook Pro if you need something heftier.).
