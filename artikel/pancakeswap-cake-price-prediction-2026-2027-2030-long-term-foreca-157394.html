A sustained move above this region could help CAKE regain momentum toward $1.84–$1.90. If buying pressure strengthens further, the $2.0 to $2.20 mark could become the next recovery target. On the downside, $1.50–$1.55 is the first area to monitor. A failure to hold this region could expose the broader support zone around $1.25–$1.35. Technically, CAKE first needs to recover the $1.65–$1.70 region and then overcome resistance around $1.84–$1.90.
