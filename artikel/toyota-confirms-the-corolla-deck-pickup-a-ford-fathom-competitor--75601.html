It has been revealed that interested in the Ford Fathom but want some of that famed Toyota reliability? You, my friend, might be moving to Brazil in the near future. Toyota just confirmed the eventual arrival of the Corolla Deck, a hybrid urban pickup based on the Corolla Cross.

It has been revealed that not much is known about the Corolla Deck yet, but we like what we’ve seen so far. The announcement (covered by Motor1 Brazil) came during the inauguration of Toyota’s fresh Sorocaba, São Paulo, manufacturing facility. Toyota debuted a version of the truck wearing XRX badging (the top Corolla Cross trim sold in Latin America), as well as HEV badging, suggesting the same hybrid setup as the Corolla and Corolla Cross.

Recent findings suggest that in that market, the hybrid Corolla Sedan and Corolla Cross subcompact SUV come with a naturally aspirated 1.8-liter combustion engine paired to an electric motor and a 1.3-kWh battery. In both vehicles, the system combines for a modest 122 horsepower, which is routed through an e-CVT automatic transmission to the front wheels. Sure, that’s not a ton of power, but it’s considerably more go juice than the Scion Kei-01, the mini pickup that Toyota does have aims to bring to the United States. Since the Scion is limited to just 25 mph, the Corolla Deck should be quite a bit more practical on U.S. roads.

It has been revealed that you won’t have to move to Brazil specifically if you want to buy the Corolla Deck; you just can’t live in the United States. According to Motor1, Toyota confirmed that the pickup will be exported throughout Latin America and the Caribbean. Car and Driver reached out to Toyota about the possibility that the tiny truck will make its way to the United States. We will patch this story when we hear back.
