The best designers know when and where to shop at most home decor stores. For example, IKEA suits tight budgets and DIY-friendly spaces, while Anthropologie is perfect for a whiff of whimsy. However, if you want to treat yourself to some sleek, splurge-worthy pieces, consider this your sign to mosey over to Design Within Reach, or DWR.

Since its founding in 1998, DWR has been the ultimate resource for buying midcentury and modern pieces. Though its physical and digital shelves are stocked with iconic contemporary furniture like the Eames Lounge Chair and Eero Saarinen’s Tulip Table, it’s possible to pick up some great wares without the astronomical sticker shock.

In a recent development, to help find high design for a great value, we asked several designers about the DWR items they reach for time and time again. Best of all? Everything’s under $1,000, so you won’t have to completely break the bank to treat yourself.

“One of my favorite things to purchase from DWR—whether as a gift for a client, friend, or a treat for myself—is Flamingo Estate’s candles. My go-to scents are the Tuscan Rosemary in the fall, Agrigento Olive Tree for any time of year, and Heirloom Tomato for any of my gardening friends and family. Beautifully packaged and incredibly clean-burning, these are a must!”—Darci Hether, founder of Darci Hether New York.

Sources indicate that “We recently used the Fin bedside tables in a client’s home in Sag Harbor. I love how the pill-shaped silhouette softens the whole bedside setup. There are so many straight lines in a bedroom, and that one curve on either side of the bed gave the room a little personality while keeping it calm. I could see them working just as easily in a more tailored city bedroom as they do in a relaxed Hamptons home.” —Sarah Tract of Sarah Tract Interiors.

“We love to incorporate the Seine Portable Lamp into our designs for its beautiful balance of form and function. The textured glass casts a soft, ambient glow, while the antique brass detailing adds a timeless, sophisticated touch. Its cordless design makes it incredibly versatile, perfect for creating an intimate atmosphere on a dining table, bookshelf, or anywhere a little extra warmth is needed. It's one of those small details that makes a space feel thoughtfully layered and inviting.”—Lindsey Christie, owner and principal designer of Lindsey Grace Interiors.

Sources indicate that "We love to source outdoor chairs from DWR. The Palissade Side Chair is a favorite: It’s a great value, a true workhorse, and its modern lines and colors look just as good indoors as they do on a terrace."—Melissa Hall Simmons, principal of Studio MH.

“I’m all about a well-designed dog bed. They take up a surprising amount of space, yet they’re often overlooked when designing a room. This one feels considered enough to fit seamlessly into the space while giving your dog a comfortable place of their own.”—Julia Newman, founder of Julia Adele Design.

“The Eames Hang-It-All by Herman Miller is especially great in a guest bedroom to give guests a place to hang a robe, a tote bag, or anything they'd like to keep off the floor.”—Christine Varela, architect at ODE Design.

“I am loving the Eames Hang-It-Hall for hooks in clients’ mudrooms. The versatility of colors, plus size and height variation of the knobs, makes it a widely adaptable piece for all different types of spaces. I’ll even cluster two to three together in a row for a larger space.”—Jenny Williamson, founder and chief creative officer of West Rose Design.

“The Drum Pouf is incredibly versatile—with the added Drum Pouf Tray—both to put your feet up on and use as a coffee table. I love to use them in multiples, and they are lightweight and can be moved to suit your space.” —Linda Eyles of Linda Eyles Design.

“An ideal mix of whimsy and utility, this vase adds interest to any space. We have used this beautiful mouth-blown glass piece to enliven a foyer center table or set the tone for a decadent dinner party throughout the year.”—Matt Donahoe, founder and principal of Bureau Interior Design.

“DWR’s Story Bookcase is a go-to item. At only 15 inches wide and eight inches deep, it allows you to fit book storage just about anywhere! I used this in my daughter's bedroom: She's an avid reader, and there wasn't an obvious place for her books, so this was the perfect solution!”—Lauren McKay, founder of Lauren McKay Interiors.

According to recent reports, “We love the Story Bookcase because it’s great for small spaces and adding height to rooms. Plus, books on display are always a conversation starter.”—Jeanne Hayes, founder of Camden Grace Interiors.

“I like how the Bacco stools have the style and shape to blend seamlessly and chicly into their surroundings, like in one of our kitchen projects. The quality is also impeccable, making it a great choice for families and loved-on kitchens.”—Barrett Oswald, founder of Barrett Oswald Designs.

Industry observers note that “We love a piece that works hard but doesn't look like it's trying! The Arcs Trolley is incredibly versatile: It can function as a side table, bar cart, or even a stylish home for desk supplies and a printer. It is playful (we love the color choices!) and architectural, with just enough personality to make a statement—without taking over the room.”—Megan Helmuth, owner and principal interior designer of Riverwards General.

In a recent development, “I’m also a big fan of DWR’s smaller office pieces. I love this in Glacier blue for a home office, especially if your office is also your dining table. You can grab the whole thing by the handle and put your chargers, pens, and supplies away when it’s time for dinner.”—Brad Ramsey, founder of Brad Ramsey Interiors.

According to recent reports, “The best pieces bring a little poetry to daily life, and this bowl does exactly that. Its flared, almost Belle Époque silhouette makes a bowl of fruit feel like a proper occasion, while its playful proportions sit happily among antiques and contemporary pieces.”—Anu Jain, founder of Atelier Oleana.

It has been revealed that “One item we recommend to clients, and that I even have in my own home, is the Hay About a Stools. They look great, are comfortable to sit in, and are easy to clean. The seat comes in several colors, and you can get wood legs in dark, light, or black wood, or you can choose a metal base, making them a design chameleon.”—Flynne Nathanson, cofounder of Refresh Your Space.
