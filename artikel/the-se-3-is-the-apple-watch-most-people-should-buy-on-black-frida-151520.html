According to recent reports, during Black Friday, the Apple Watch SE 3 is on sale in the 40mm / GPS configuration at Amazon, Walmart, and Target starting around $179 ($70 off), $20 less than its previous lowest price. The 44mm variant, meanwhile, is on sale at Amazon, Walmart, and Target starting at around $209 (also $70 off), also beating its previous low by $20.

Despite having launched over a year ago, the Apple Watch SE 3 is still the Apple Watch model we recommend for most people. It includes many of the trademark features of higher-end Apple Watch models like the Series 12, including an always-on display, double-tap and wrist flick gestures, fast-charging, and on-device Siri. The compromise is that the SE 3 lacks some of the more advanced health features found on more expensive models, like blood oxygen and EKG sensing. The battery life also isn’t quite as strong, but if you don’t need as many health-tracking sensors, it’s a much better value, especially at this price.
