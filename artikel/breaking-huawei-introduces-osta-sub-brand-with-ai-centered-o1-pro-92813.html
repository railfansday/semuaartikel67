Huawei used its November 20, 2026 brand event to introduce OSTA (星芒), a new smartphone sub-brand, and launch the O1 Pro as a HarmonyOS AI phone with a starting list price of CNY 5,699.

The new brand’s first handset makes Huawei’s own software and assistant its defining features. The official product presentation introduces HarmonyOS 7 AI Pioneer Edition and Xiao Xingmang, an OSTA-specific interactive companion form of the Xiaoyi assistant.

Huawei’s Vmall listing labels the O1 Pro as a latest product and lists the 12GB/256GB version at CNY 5,499, a CNY 200 discount from CNY 5,699. The range also offers 12GB/512GB, 16GB/512GB and 16GB/1TB configurations.

Recent findings suggest that huawei describes an interaction model in which the Xiaoyi AI screen is available immediately when the display is activated, using frequent usage scenarios and habits to present services proactively. Xiaoyi Space draws on likes, favorites and long-term memory, while natural-voice interaction supports continuous conversation and dialect recognition.

Sources indicate that the product’s more consequential claim concerns execution. In Huawei’s coffee example, Xiaoyi uses location and preferences to recommend a drink, incorporates discounts and completes the order. Other demonstrations offer restaurant suggestions during travel, return guidance after a concert, meeting-schedule reminders and food-order pickup notifications, extending the assistant from responses into task completion and timely prompts.

Recent findings suggest that additional Huawei examples connect viewing records to film recommendations and post-viewing discussions, remember food preferences for personalized suggestions, and provide game information and match reminders. Together, those examples place the assistant across entertainment, dining, travel and work, with Xiao Xingmang supplying the companion interface for OSTA people.

In a recent development, the official specifications list a Kirin 9030S processor and HarmonyOS 7.0, alongside a 6.39-inch OLED display with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio, 2232-by-1320 resolution and up to 120Hz refresh. The handset is 6.68mm thick and weighs about 200 grams.

The specifications also list a 7,000mAh typical-capacity battery supporting up to 66W wired and 50W wireless charging, with the chargers required for those maximum rates sold separately. The rear camera system combines a 200-megapixel optically stabilized main camera, a 50-megapixel optically stabilized periscope telephoto camera with approximately 3.7x optical zoom, and Huawei’s second-generation Hongfeng color camera.

According to recent reports, for meeting capture, a four-microphone spatial array supports directional pickup. Huawei says its AI recording functions provide real-time transcription, summaries and translation, giving the phone an administrative use case beyond the consumer-oriented assistant demonstrations. The device also supports eSIM alongside two physical nano-SIM slots, although eSIM use requires carrier service.

Huawei’s footnotes set key limits on that pitch. The phone ships with HarmonyOS 7, but some functions and cross-device experiences are still being adapted, and certain listed features await a later software upgrade. Dialect recognition presently covers a specified set of regional dialects, with Huawei cautioning that other local variants may be recognized inaccurately.

AI-assisted camera composition must be enabled manually, and pose recommendations work only at the 4x setting in photo and portrait modes. Huawei also identifies AI-assisted recording output as reference material and states actual performance depends on the application involved. The AI experience therefore varies by feature, application and software readiness.

According to recent reports, OSTA gives Huawei a dedicated consumer brand for making HarmonyOS AI the main reason to choose a handset. The O1 Pro’s most consequential feature is the connection between Xiaoyi’s proactive suggestions and its ability to complete tasks, because that connection distinguishes Huawei’s system-level AI pitch from a phone that merely includes an assistant.
