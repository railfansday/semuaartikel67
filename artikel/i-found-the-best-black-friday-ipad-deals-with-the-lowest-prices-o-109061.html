Sources indicate that apple makes the best tablets around, but they're definitely not cheap, with every model getting a price hike in 2026. Black Friday deals are your chance to reverse that trend, with several models now up to $100 off.

One of the best Black Friday iPad deals right now is the brand-new iPad mini 8 for $100 off. It offers a larger OLED display, faster A20 Pro chip in a thinner and lighter design., The 11-inch iPad Air M4 is also $100 off, as well as the 13-inch iPad Pro.

Industry observers note that here are the best iPad Black Friday deals I've checked today. And be sure to check out our best Apple Black Friday deals live blog for all of the latest Apple deals on AirPods, Apple Watches, iPhones and more.

The iPad (11th Gen) is a great option for kids or anyone who just wants a simple tablet for surfing the web, playing videos and playing less demanding games. It remains the best Apple tablet for most people. In our iPad 11 review, we said it has significantly improved performance thanks to its A16 chip, longer battery life, and a larger 128GB starting storage option; Apple's entry-level tablet is among its best.

Recent findings suggest that the last-gen iPad mini 7 is a fantastic compact tablet that's now on sale for Black Friday. As we said in our iPad mini 7 review, it delivers excellent performance and battery life while retaining its predecessor's vibrant display and portable design. Though it keeps the portrait-oriented front camera and is incompatible with Apple Pencil 2, this tablet’s strengths outweigh its flaws.

The iPad mini 8 is the most significant upgrade to Apple’s smallest tablet. In our iPad mini 8 review, we loved its thinner, lighter design, as well as the stunning newly introduced 8.5-inch OLED display. Powered by the A20 Pro chip, it also delivers fast performance and more than all-day battery life. Though pricier and missing features like Face ID and a 120Hz refresh rate, it still delivers a first-rate iPad experience.

Sources indicate that the iPad Air M4 retains everything we loved about the M3 iPad Air, but now it has a faster processor and Wi-Fi 7 — all for $100 off. While not perfect, as we remarked in our iPad Air M4 review, that doesn't prevent it from being the best tablet for the money.

Industry observers note that this is the same iPad Air M4, only in a larger 13-inch size. This configuration packs an M4 chip and 128GB. This is worth getting if you want a 13-inch iPad that doesn't cost as much as an iPad Pro.

Recent findings suggest that the iPad Pro M5 retains the same elegant, lightweight design and beautiful OLED panel as its predecessor. Like I said in my iPad Pro M5 review, even though it’s not a substantial upgrade, the M5’s fast performance and the productivity-focused iPadOS 27 make this the most powerful and useful iPad Pro yet.

If you want a larger screen, you can get the 13-inch iPad Pro model. This tablet is my daily driver thanks to its gorgeous tandem OLED panel that makes everything I watch look its best. And when paired with the Apple Magic Keyboard, it's also a decent laptop replacement.
