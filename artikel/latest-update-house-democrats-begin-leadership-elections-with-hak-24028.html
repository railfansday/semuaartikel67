Today, House Democrats are set to begin their closed-door leadership elections. One particular race to watch: minority leader Hakeem Jeffries is expected to seek the caucus’ formal backing as its candidate for Speaker in the next Congress through a recorded vote, according to several reports.

In a recent development, jeffries is widely expected to win and, with Democrats poised to hold a sizeable House majority, secure a glide path to the speakership when the full chamber votes in January. If elected Speaker, Jeffries would become the first Black person to hold the gavel in US history.

Jeffries is not facing a challenger, and despite the possibility of some defections from the incoming progressives, he is still expected to have the 218 votes needed to win on the House floor.

Democrats will cast votes for House majority leader and whip, and the caucus chair and vice chair, among other positions.
