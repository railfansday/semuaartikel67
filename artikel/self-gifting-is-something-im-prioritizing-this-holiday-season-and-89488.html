With all the talk about finding the best gifts for everyone in your life — whether it’s the girl who has everything, your picky brother, or your BFF who swears she doesn’t want anything— there’s one person we’re forgetting: ourselves! In the stress of finding the perfect gifts for everyone else, we deserve a little holiday treat, too.

For me, I see self-gifting as a way to upgrade some of the everyday things I wouldn't think twice about buying for myself. Take lingerie, for example. As much as I love my 10-year-old bra-and-underwear rotation, it's nice to have a set that's sexier than the usual basics. When I came across this red babydoll set from Adore Me with the cutest red bow, it immediately caught my eye. It's festive without being over-the-top, and honestly, a much more fun addition to my lingerie drawer.

According to recent reports, before you’re like, “I’m single, I’m not buying lingerie!” Hear me out: Who says we need to be in a relationship to have a sexy set? Because you don’t! I love a good excuse to wear something flirty that’s not just my boring thongs. And if you need any endorsement besides mine, reviewers have remarked things like, “My girlfriend looks so hot in this,” “Looks even better in real life,” and “It was a wild night.” Or if you have a partner, hey, they can enjoy it with you too!

Sources indicate that available in sizes XS to 4X, the set includes a satin babydoll featuring underwired cups, adjustable straps, and a functional satin bow that lets you customize the fit. It also comes with a matching G-string if you really want to lean into it. For something cozier, pair it with some short lounge shorts for a more laid-back look. And honestly, I could even see styling the babydoll with a pair of denim and a long leather coat for a fun, going-out moment.

Recent findings suggest that but don’t forget, Adore Me has plenty of other gorgeous bras, sets, and rompers to choose from if you’re looking to add a little variety to your lingerie lineup. And if you’re all about keeping things cozy, the brand also offers on-theme pajama sets and slips you’ll want to live in all December long. Check them out below!
