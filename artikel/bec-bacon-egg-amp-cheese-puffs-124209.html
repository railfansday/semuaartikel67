BEC (Bacon, Egg & Cheese) Puffs

🌐 Source: Delish • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Landing somewhere in between a Upcoming York-style bacon, egg, and cheese (BEC) and a quiche Lorraine, these bite-size appetizers are sure to be party-pleasers. Tangy Grey Poupon Dijon Mustard, creamy Gruyère, and smoky bacon are whisked with egg and poured into prepared mini phyllo shells for a simple starter that comes together in a half-hour.
