As the workday progresses, you face small problems. Your phone is almost dead, with no charger in sight. You can’t find the piece of paper with yesterday’s meeting notes.

Black Friday is one of the best times to shop for office technology. The right purchase can remove everyday irritations — or simply make your workspace more pleasant — at a discounted price.
