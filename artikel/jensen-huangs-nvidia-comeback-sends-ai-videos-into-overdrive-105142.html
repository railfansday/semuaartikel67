Jensen Huang's Nvidia comeback sends AI videos into overdrive

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Just 11 days after resigning as Nvidia CEO, Jensen Huang’s return has unleashed a fresh wave of AI-generated videos. But the internet has changed its tune: while last week’s memes mocked Huang, many now celebrate his comeback. One viral video purportedly showing him dancing is actually AI-manipulated footage of a basketball coach from 2016. From humiliation to hero worship, the technology Nvidia helped popularise is rewriting the narrative surrounding its own CEO, as Vedika Bahl explains.
