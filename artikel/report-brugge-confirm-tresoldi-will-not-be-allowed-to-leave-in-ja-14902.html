It seems AC Milan will not be signing Nicolo Tresoldi in January, with Club Brugge making a statement to the press.

In recent weeks, if not longer, Milan have been constantly linked with signing Tresoldi. A fan of the club, the German-Italian striker is an obvious candidate to make a move to San Siro in the near future, especially with Gerry Cardinale being a significant fan.

It has been revealed that however, the finances of the deal make it difficult to really imagine it happening in January, after the Rossoneri paid €75 million for Goncalo Ramos in the summer. Now, a new obstacle has emerged and it’s both financial and literal.

In a recent development, this afternoon, MilanVibes have relayed a statement from Club Brugge, published in the popular Belgian paper Het Laatste Nieuws, saying that the striker will not be leaing in January.
