📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that magnolia Pictures has snatched up North American rights to “Trash Mountain,” a dramedy film starring Caleb Hearon, who co-wrote the script with Ruby Caster, and Zooey Deschanel. A theatrical release is in the works for next year.

“I Used to Go Here” filmmaker Kris Rey directs “Trash Mountain,” and Colin Trevorrow serves as producer. The movie held its premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival in September. According to an official logline, “‘Trash Mountain’ follows the story of Gavin (Hearon), a young gay Chicago man who is forced to return to his hometown in rural Missouri following the death of his hoarder father. The story takes us on a journey of emotional acceptance and resilience as Gavin and his half-sister Abbi (Deschanel) navigate challenging family dynamics and their complicated relationships withthe past.”.

Other cast members include Jacki Weaver, Jaboukie Young-White, Kyle Marvin and Holmes.

“’Trash Mountain’ is such a funny, smart, joyful work, which is no surprise coming from the brilliant mind of Caleb Hearon,” remarked Magnolia Pictures co-CEOs Eamonn Bowles and Dori Begley. “We’re so excited to collaborate with Caleb, Kris, Colin and this fantastic cast to bring it to audiences.”.

Sources indicate that metronome Film Company presents, in association with Sight Unseen, Pigasus Pictures, Five Sticks Productions and Streeterville Productions. Rebecca Linfoot co-produced “Trash Mountain.” Executive producers include Zachary Spicer, Julia Lebedev, Eddie Vaisman, Hearon, Lilly Wachowski, Annys Hamilton, Carrie Holt De Lama, Kelley Sims, Chris Cafero, Scott L. Goldstein, George Rush and Willie Trieger.

Recent findings suggest that “’Trash Mountain’ is a film about home. The physical homes we reside in, the emotions that we make home in, the place where we grew up, and the way that many of us make home elsewhere when we’ve spilled out over the brims of our hometowns,” remarked Hearon. “I’ve been a fan of Magnolia films for a very long time and we’re honored to be partnering with them.”.

Hearon, a 31-year-old stand-up comedian and host of the “So True with Caleb Hearon” podcast, has seen his star rise in recent years, landing memorable supporting roles in films like “Pizza Movie,” “The Devil Wears Prada 2” and “Little Brother.” His other film credits include “Jurassic World: Dominion,” “I Used to Be Funny” and “Sweethearts.” Deschanel, best known for her turn as Jess in the long-running Fox sitcom “Latest Girl,” was last seen in the Prime Video holiday rom-com “Merv” with Charlie Cox. Her other recent major-screen credits include “Harold and the Purple Crayon” and “Trolls Band Together.”.

The deal was negotiated by Magnolia Pictures SVP of Acquisitions John Von Thaden and VP of Acquisitions Miranda Hill, and by CAA Media Finance and WME on behalf of the filmmakers.
