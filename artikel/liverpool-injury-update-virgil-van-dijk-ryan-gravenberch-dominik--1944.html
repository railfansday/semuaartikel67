Liverpool are sweating over the fitness of several key players ahead of their clash with Manchester United this weekend.

The Reds will take on United, who welcome Mauricio Pochettino to their dugout at Anfield, but they could be without some of their star names for the fierce rivalry.

They lost three players during the international break to respective injuries, and Andoni Iraola will be hoping that a few days of down time will allow for sufficient recovery.

With that in mind, here is the latest Liverpool injury news and return dates….

Van Dijk was forced to withdraw from the Netherlands’ squad to face Greece and Germany in the Nations League last week.

The Reds captain was forced to pull out of the squad for fitness reasons, according to a statement issued by the Oranje FA.

Sources indicate that it read: “Virgil van Dijk [and Brian Brobbey] have been dropped from the Dutch national team's upcoming international matches. Both players did not return fit from the past weekend's matches and are therefore unavailable.

“The squad thus currently consists of 23 players.”.

There had been no indication of a knock before the international window, so Liverpool fans will be hoping that their star defender is fit to face the Red Devils.

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H).
