It’s been nearly three years since Best Buy decided to ditch selling physical copies of movies, stripping its inventory from both its brick-and-mortar stores and its online catalog. But with the growing rise of 4K Blu-ray collections as a vinyl-esque collector’s item, the electronics giant has slowly begun dipping its toes back into the physical media game.

As spotted by TheHDRoom on Twitter and on the 4K Blu-ray subreddit, Best Buy is now selling 14 different 4K Blu-rays on its website, marking another step in the brand’s slow return to selling physical media. These films include:.

Technically, Blu-ray returned to Best Buy’s online storefront in August of 2025, when it opened a third-party marketplace similar to Amazon or Walmart. These titles, however, are shipped and sold by Best Buy. Everything’s still online-only, unfortunately, save for The Odyssey, which debuted in-store shelf displays alongside yesterday’s release date. It shouldn’t come as too much of a surprise that The Odyssey is getting some special treatment; in addition to its status as a box office juggernaut, Nolan’s previous movie, Oppenheimer, initially sold out of physical copies in its first week of release in 2023.

Outside of these 14 titles, additional Blu-rays continue to be sold through Best Buy’s third-party marketplace.

Upgrade, 11/20: Best Buy confirmed to The HD Room on Twitter that The Odyssey is just the first 4K Blu-ray to return to physical locations, as it looks to continue adding “some” Blu-rays back to its stores.
