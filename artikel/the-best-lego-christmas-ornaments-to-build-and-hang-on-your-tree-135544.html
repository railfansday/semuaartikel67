Sources indicate that we love a multi-purpose product, and these LEGO Christmas ornaments fit that bill. Unlike the elaborate builds that inevitably take up half your bookshelf, these smaller sets are easier to assemble, and can help get buyers into the Christmas spirit. Many come with hanging loops, so you can put them directly on your Christmas tree once you’ve finished building.

Industry observers note that they’re also relatively inexpensive compared to most full-size LEGO sets, making them great stocking stuffers, Secret Santa gift, or activity to keep the family occupied during holiday movie marathons. And considering how often LEGO retires its seasonal sets, some of the older designs have become fun little collectibles in their own right.

Industry observers note that this duo snowmen set is particularly cool because of how much personality LEGO managed to pack into such a small set. The larger snowman sports a top hat and scarf, and carries a candy cane and gift, while the smaller one has glasses, a winter hat, and a tiny lantern. Both come with hanging loops, though they look just as cute sitting together on a windowsill. It's also a great little project for a parent and child to work on together.

Sources indicate that if you’re hoping to decorate your tree with more than just one or two LEGO ornaments, this is the set I’d go with. It comes with four builds: a wrapped Christmas present, Santa Claus, a snowman, and a particularly expressive reindeer. They're all relatively flat, colorful designs, so they should fit nicely among your existing ornaments without taking up an entire branch.

Upcoming to LEGO’s holiday lineup for 2026, this ornament collection picks up where the previous selection left off. This time around, you get a decorated Christmas tree, a candy cane, Mrs. Claus, and a festive cat appropriately named Cataclaws. The Christmas tree ornament also features glow-in-the-dark elements, which is a fun little detail once the lights go out.

In a recent development, not every Christmas ornament needs to be red and green. This retired seasonal LEGO set features a little penguin bundled up in red earmuffs and a geometric snowflake, both of which come with strings for hanging.

Industry observers note that if you're willing to graduate from tree ornaments to something a little more substantial, LEGO's Christmas Stocking is one of the more interesting additions to its 2026 holiday collection. This 337-piece build comes with a hanging attachment for displaying it on a wall, as well as a kickstand if you'd rather prop it up on a shelf.
