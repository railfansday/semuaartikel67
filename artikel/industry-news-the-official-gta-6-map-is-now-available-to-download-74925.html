Ahead of launch, Rockstar Games mentioned that instead of including a map inside the physical edition of GTA 6, they'd be making it available to download for free on their website, as well as providing a double-sided physical edition.

The downloadable version is now officially available for free on the GTA 6 website, and there's even a special "printable" option for those who don't just want to look at it in digital form.

It comes with a substantial version of the GTA 6 cover art as well - you can see both of these down below:.

Sources indicate that the physical edition still isn't available yet, but Rockstar Games says that it will be "made available at the Rockstar Store shortly after launch". We'll keep an eye out and update this article when that happens.

Honestly, I don't think the map is that great, as I hoped it might include things like store and minigame locations rather than just each area that's available to visit, but as a poster on a wall, it's definitely still worth displaying somewhere.

I'd rather wait for the physical version, but it's certainly nice that it exists as a freebie as well.
