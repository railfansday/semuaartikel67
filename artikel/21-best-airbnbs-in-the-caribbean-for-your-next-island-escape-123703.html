How many countries are in the Caribbean?

The Caribbean is a diverse region made up of over 700 islands, islets, reefs, and cays, but not all are independent countries. In total, there are 13 sovereign nations and 17 dependent territories spread across the Caribbean Sea. The independent countries include popular destinations such as Jamaica, the Bahamas, Barbados, St. Lucia, and Trinidad and Tobago. The dependent territories—like Puerto Rico (US), Martinique (France), and the Cayman Islands (UK)—offer a mix of cultural influences and governance structures. While each island varies in size, language, and history, the entire region is united by its tropical climate, vibrant cultures, and rich mix of African, European, and Indigenous heritage.

What is the best island to visit in the Caribbean?

Sources indicate that there isn’t one “best” Caribbean island—it just depends on what kind of experience you’re seeking. Families often love Jamaica for its all-inclusive resorts, kid-friendly activities, and easy flights from the US. Surfers gravitate toward Barbados and Puerto Rico, which boast consistent swells and a laid-back surf culture. Solo travelers often choose Puerto Rico or Curaçao, thanks to their safety, walkable cities, and friendly locals. For romantic getaways, St. Lucia and Antigua are top picks—think private villas, lush rainforests, and stunning beaches perfect for couples. And for multi-generational trips, the Bahamas or Turks and Caicos stand out, offering luxury accommodations, calm beaches for kids, and plenty of excursions for all ages. Each island has its own rhythm and charm, so the “best” one depends on your travel style and interests.

When is the best time to travel to the Caribbean?

Sources indicate that the best time to visit the Caribbean typically falls between December and April, during the region’s dry season. This period offers warm temperatures, low humidity, and minimal rainfall—ideal conditions for beach days and outdoor adventures. However, it’s also the peak tourist season, meaning higher prices and more crowds. For travelers seeking better deals and fewer tourists, the shoulder months of May, June, and November are excellent alternatives, offering good weather with occasional rain showers. The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June to November, and while severe storms are rare on most islands, it’s wise to stay informed about forecasts if traveling during this time.

Which islands in the Caribbean are the most affordable?

Sources indicate that jamaica and the Dominican Republic are good places to start if you're looking for budget-friendly rentals. But keep in mind that no matter which island you head to, the neighborhood and season you're traveling in matter just as much as anything else, and an exclusive beachfront address can rapidly change your budget. To get the most bang for your buck, consider traveling during shoulder or off seasons, and take a look at rentals outside of the typical tourist hot spots.

Are there any islands in the Caribbean you can visit from the US without a passport?

Recent findings suggest that yes. US citizens traveling directly from the mainland can visit Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands without a passport. The US Virgin Islands include St. Thomas, St. John, and St. Croix, and travelers can also head to Puerto Rico’s offshore islands of Vieques and Culebra.
