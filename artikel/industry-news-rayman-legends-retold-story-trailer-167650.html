Publisher Ubisoft and developers Ubisoft Montpellier and Ubisoft Milan have released the story trailer for Rayman Legends Retold.

Here is an overview of the game, via Ubisoft:.

Embark on a bold reimagining of the acclaimed platformer, filled with latest content and narrative twists. When a shadowy villain spreads corruption across the Glade of Dreams, journey through its charming worlds solo or in up to four‑player couch cooperative in a fast‑paced, side‑scrolling adventure where every stumble and surprise adds to the delightful chaos.

Rayman Legends Retold is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Ubisoft Store on December 3.
