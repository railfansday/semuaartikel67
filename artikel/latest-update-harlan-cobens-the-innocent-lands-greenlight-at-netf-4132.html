Latest Update: Harlan Coben’s ‘The Innocent’ Lands Greenlight At Netflix As Streamer Strikes Overall Deal With StoryMath

Harlan Coben has been on a tear at Netflix with his most recent, I Will Find You, becoming the streamer’s fifth most popular series of all time.

Coben is back with limited series The Innocent, marking his 15th adaptation for Netflix. The streamer has greenlit the project, after Deadline revealed it was in development earlier this year.

The Innocent reunites Coben with Robert Hull, showrunner of I Will Find You, which starred Sam Worthington, and Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien, who served as writers and exec producers.

Hull launched production tech giant StoryMath earlier this year with Wynbrandt and Lilien, who created CBS’ God Friended Me, and the company has now signed a fresh overall deal with Netflix. Watch on Deadline.

Interestingly, The Innocent has already been adapted by Netflix as Spanish series El Inocente in 2021.

Recent findings suggest that the book follows Matt Hunter, who served nine years in prison after innocently trying to break up a fight-and ending up a killer. Now nine years after his release from prison, his innocence long forgotten, he’s an ex-con who takes nothing for granted. With his wife Olivia pregnant and the two of them closing on a house in his hometown, things are looking up. Until the day Matt gets a shocking, inexplicable video call from Olivia’s phone. And in an instant, the unraveling begins.

A mysterious man who begins tailing Matt turns up dead. A beloved nun is murdered. And local and federal authorities–including homicide investigator, Loren Muse, a childhood schoolmate of Matt’s with a troubled past of her own–see all signs pointing to a former criminal with one murder already under his belt… Matt Hunter. Unwilling to lose everything for a second time, Matt and Olivia are forced outside the law in a desperate attempt to save their future together.

Hull, Wynbrandt and Lilien will serve as showrunners, writers and exec producers with Coben and Ben Coben serving as exec producers through Final Twist Productions.

Industry observers note that the news comes six months after Coben extended his deal at Netflix, which came to life at the streamer in 2018 with the adaptation of Safe. It was followed by British series The Stranger, Polish adaptation of The Woods, the Spanish adaptation of The Innocent, the French adaptation of Gone for Good, Poland’s Hold Tight and Just One Look, the British series Stay Close, Fool Me Once, Missing You, Run Away, and U.S. series I Will Find You.

Sources indicate that next up, is a British adaptation of The Woods and a U.S. adaptation of Myron Bolitar from David E. Kelley and Kyle Long.

Netflix’s Head of UCAN Scripted Series Jinny Howe told Deadline earlier this summer that Coben “really understands his audience” and the partnership has been “very, very successful for us”.

Industry observers note that cole Galvin, Netflix Vice President of Drama Series, who was promoted last month, stated, “Harlan Coben is one of the great creative partnerships at Netflix, and The Innocent marks our 15th story together. Nobody builds a can’t-look-away thriller like Harlan, and Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien brought their own incredible creativity to I Will Find You, a global hit for us. We’re thrilled to welcome them to the Netflix family, and we can’t wait for the world to get hooked on their Innocent.”.

Recent findings suggest that “We’re thrilled to call Netflix our home and couldn’t be more grateful to our friends Jinny Howe, Cole Galvin, James Flannery, Mike Azzolino, and of course, the incredible Harlan Coben, for their continued partnership,” added Hull, Wynbrandt and Lilien. “Bringing I Will Find You to life and seeing it connect with audiences around the world was an amazing experience. We’re excited to bring our signature brand of twisty and propulsive storytelling to The Innocent and the many latest stories we look forward to telling at Netflix.”.

StoryMath is represented by CAA and Range, with Lilien and Wynbrandt represented by attorney Todd Rubenstein and Hull represented by attorney Dave Ryan.

Book adaptations at Netflix are clearly working.

The firm noted that Coben’s I Will Find You, which was published by Grand Central Publishing, saw a 500% increase in sales after the announcement of the show.

Elsewhere, The Whisper Man, which starred Robert DeNiro, saw a similar increase in sales, Remarkably Bright Creatures saw sales across all formats rise 560% from its first-look launch to its peak in May, East of Eden, published by Penguin Classics, hit the number one on Amazon’s best sellers list some 74 years after publication with a 200% rise in sales and Little House on the Prairie helped drive 110% more sales for the Storytide and HarperCollins-published book.

On screen, book adaptations account for nearly 20% of total view hours and over 13B global views since 2025, per the streamer.

The company was at the Texas Book Festival this weekend for the first time with a panel featuring The Hunting Wives author May Cobb and Guarding Stars author Katherine Center as well as Virgin River showrunner Patrick Sean Smith and exec Cole Galvin.

“People come to comfort and heartland stories for connection and optimism—the kind of story you put on to unwind. Those titles have drawn more than a billion views on Netflix since 2023, and they’ve reached viewers in more than 90 countries,” added Galvin. “But what matters most to me are the readers who loved these books first. We work with authors and creators to honor what made the stories special, and then we invite new fans in. From Virgin River to Little House on the Prairie to Guarding Stars, our job is to give audiences more of what they love.”.
