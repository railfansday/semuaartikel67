Good morning, everyone, and welcome, and a really warm welcome to those joining us online as well. I'm Amanda Bardwell, Woolworths' Group CEO and Managing Director of Australian Food. And for those of you who I've not met before, I have worked my entire career in retail. That's 35 years. I started when I was 14 in my local food store on the outskirts of Brisbane. And I've worked at Woolworths for over 2 decades now in supermarkets, in liquor, I led our WooliesX division before becoming Woolworths Group CEO just over 2 years ago.
