ESR x Craig & Karl Bring a Pocket Full of Joy to Everyday Technology

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the objects we carry often reveal more about us than we realize. From the jewelry we wear to the accessories we keep within reach, even the smallest possessions become expressions of personal style. Yet as technology plays an increasingly central role in our lives, the devices we interact with most remain largely defined by their utility. Bringing more personality to these everyday essentials is the idea behind Pocket Full of Joy—a new collaboration between ESR, a consumer tech accessories brand, and creative duo Craig & Karl—which transforms phone cases and wallets into colorful, functional accessories.

Known for their vibrant compositions and playful iconography, Craig & Karl bring their distinctive visual language to a collection of iPhone cases and magnetic wallets. The collaboration draws upon the duo’s affinity for bold colors and familiar imagery, translating their graphic sensibilities into objects designed to accompany their owners throughout the day. The result is a collection that explores how artistic expression can become part of everyday life.

Across the collection, sticker-like illustrations, saturated hues, and expressive typography lend a sense of character to otherwise utilitarian surfaces. A collage of playful symbols evokes the spontaneity of a personal scrapbook, while the graphic “OK” design introduces a lighthearted note of optimism. Elsewhere, the cheerful “HI” motif transforms a magnetic wallet into a small, portable greeting. Each composition reflects Craig & Karl’s recognizable aesthetic while giving everyday accessories a more personal character.

It has been revealed that behind the playful graphics are the practical features that define ESR’s accessories. The Classic Hybrid Magnetic Case with Flex Stand incorporates a built-in adjustable stand, MagSafe compatibility, and protective construction, allowing it to move between different activities without sacrificing its colorful design. Coordinating magnetic wallets hold cards and cash in compact silhouettes, while the Magnetic Wallet Stand with Grip adds an adjustable viewing stand and finger loop for greater versatility. Together, these features bring Craig & Karl’s expressive graphics to everyday accessories.

Recent findings suggest that the collection’s appeal also comes from the way it brings art into objects that have become nearly inseparable from modern life. Where traditional artworks invite observation, these designs encourage interaction, becoming more personal through repeated use. A phone placed on a café table, a wallet retrieved during a morning commute, or a device propped against a desk becomes a small, unexpected moment of visual interest.

According to recent reports, with Pocket Full of Joy, ESR and Craig & Karl make a case for bringing more creativity into the everyday. Sometimes, the most memorable design details are found in the things we carry without a second thought.

The ESR × Craig & Karl Pocket Full of Joy collection will be available Nov. 14, 2026, at esr.com and amazon.com, with iPhone cases starting at $49.99 and magnetic wallets starting at $25.99.
