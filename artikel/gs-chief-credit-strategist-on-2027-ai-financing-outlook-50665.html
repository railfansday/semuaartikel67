GS Chief Credit Strategist on 2027 AI Financing Outlook

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (76 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that amanda Lynam, Goldman Sachs Chief Credit Strategist, claims hyperscalers issued debt early - starting in 2025, before their free cash flow turned negative - and that a recent surge pushed 2026 borrowing above Goldman’s forecast of $250 billion. For 2027, Goldman expects debt to finance 35% of hyperscaler capital spending, equivalent to about $440 billion. She speaks with Romaine Bostick & Ed Ludlow on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg).
