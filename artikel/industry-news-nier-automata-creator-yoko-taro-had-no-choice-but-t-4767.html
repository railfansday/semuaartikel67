Nier: Automata mastermind Yoko Taro is enjoying Nintendo's new Ocarina of Time remake, much like many of us Legend of Zelda fans – and how could he not, considering the part the 1998 original played in many future games' own success.

According to recent reports, like, say, Nier: Automata itself. The creator admits as much in a new post over on X (as machine-translated via DeepL, Google, and verified against native speakers like Genki), explaining that he "used to own The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time" – and, judging by the attached image of the recent remake's Switch 2 case, he does once again. "It had such a huge influence on Nier that I had no choice but to buy it," after all.

ゼルダ・時のオカリナ買ってました（過去形）。これに大影響を受けてニーア作ったので買わざるを得ない。 https://t.co/ixvNxIExUYNovember 15, 2026.

Yoko Taro follows up on this with a response that sees him presume he wasn't actually the only developer to draw from Ocarina of Time's undeniable influence – all devs at the time may have in some shape or form. "Actually, I like to think everyone making games back then was influenced by it," he writes. "When I first saw a third-person 3D action game with a semi-open world and progression systems, I was blown away by how it had 'everything.'".

Industry observers note that heck, who wasn't? Its non-linear gameplay and ambitious design had the gaming world in a chokehold, and that's no secret. As for the remake itself, the Nier: Automata lead appears to be enjoying it. A further reply sees him dub the "distinctive facial features" in the remake as having "a cool, understated look" he'd "love to see" again… like, perhaps, in a Majora's Mask remake (Nintendo, take notes, we beg of you, please).

今回のリメイクの独特な顔立ちは、なんかＮＨＫの人形劇（三銃士とかホームズとか）とかをリファレンスにしてそうな渋いアプローチで、かなり好き。この方向でムジュラの不気味さも描いてほしい。November 15, 2026.

I mean, it's not all that outside the realm of possibility, is it? Former Nintendo marketing leads Kit Ellis and Krysta Yang said a Majora's Mask remake is "a foregone conclusion" after Ocarina of Time, asserting that "they probably already started making it." I sure hope so myself, seeing as I've managed to 100% everything the upcoming Ocarina of Time has to offer and, sadly, there's no Master Quest version to enjoy afterward.
