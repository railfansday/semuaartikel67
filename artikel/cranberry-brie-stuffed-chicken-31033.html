📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that all our office talks about around the holidays are our melty, crispy cranberry brie bites, and I gotta tell you, I totally get why—they’re a superstar app. So, taking cranberry and brie and stuffing it into chicken breasts for a main meal was a no-brainer. This is my favorite way to shake up my weeknight chicken routine in the fall, and it's going to be my go-to dish to impress come Friendsgiving season.

It has been revealed that preparing the chicken:I wouldn’t typically recommend a larger chicken breast for searing, but it’s necessary for stuffing. Butterfly or cut the breast, starting at the top of the thickest point, and slowly create a slit about three-quarters of the way through. Season with salt on the inside and outside of the breast. Place a piece of brie and a spoonful of cranberry sauce toward the inner seam and close. Sear each stuffed breast until the outside is browned. Doing this in a heavy-bottom pan like a cast-iron will allow you to transfer right to the oven without removing the breast to a sheet pan. Finish cooking the breast until the internal temperature is 165°.

Storage:Store the chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. To avoid the chicken from drying out, I suggest wrapping the pieces in foil and slowly heating them in a 350° oven.
