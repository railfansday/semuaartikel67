Apple’s newly introduced HomeView (review here) and Apple TV 4K (review) arrive in stores this Friday, November 13. And that same day, the first Apple Home Collection accessories will be available for pre-order.

If you’re looking to upgrade your smart home, Apple will soon have a lot of new options for you.

The HomeView and new Apple TV 4K launch this Friday, November 13. We’ve just published our HomeView review and Apple TV 4K review.

Joining these products, Apple has worked closely with LG and Schneider to develop a suite of smart home accessories unveiled as part of a new ‘Apple Home Collection.’.

These accessories include smart thermostats, security cameras, video doorbells, and more.

The first Apple Home Collection products will launch in December. And now Apple has confirmed to 9to5Mac that pre-orders for select accessories will open this Friday, November 13 on Apple.com.

Apple hasn’t yet said which accessories will be part of this initial batch, but we’ll keep you posted as more information emerges.

Recent findings suggest that the pitch for Apple Home Collection products is that Apple co-developed them, and they’re designed to offer easy setup in the Home app with no third-party apps or accounts required. The accessories also take advantage of the latest Apple Home technologies, such as Adaptive Temperature for smart thermostats.
