It has been revealed that morpho’s largest listed vault for EUR CoinVertible (EURCV), a euro stablecoin, highlighted about 13.8 million EURCV less liquidity than its biggest address would need for a full exit on Nov. 26.

A withdrawal would return EURCV to a wallet, but eligible holders seeking euros directly from issuer SG-FORGE would follow a separate redemption timetable.

Morpho’s Steakhouse Prime EURCV vault on Ethereum showed 144.3 million EURCV in deposits and 107.2 million in displayed liquidity, including tokens the app could pull from other lending markets.

The top address accounted for 121 million EURCV (83.8%), according to the same page. Steakhouse Prime was the largest EURCV vault by assets in Morpho’s public ranking.

The comparison assumes the top address sought to withdraw its entire position at that moment. Morpho’s public data show balances by address, which can pool funds for more than one owner.

The European Banking Authority’s Nov. 25 report on stablecoins in decentralized finance found that, in its July analysis, more than half of circulating EURCV was supplied to a Morpho vault.

Sources indicate that A standard vault withdrawal draws on idle tokens and funds that can be pulled from the market designated for withdrawals. At Ethereum block 26,500,641, Morpho’s address-level position data put that route’s limit at 103.1 million EURCV against a position worth 120.99 million.

That left about 17.9 million EURCV beyond the ordinary withdrawal limit at the indexed block.

Under Morpho’s withdrawal rules, the app can pull available funds from other markets when the vault configuration allows it, a process called forced deallocation.

Morpho’s withdrawal-options data demonstrated about 102.8 million EURCV idle and about $340,000 available in the designated market, plus about 4 million that could be pulled from other markets, based on rounded amounts.

Industry observers note that that last source of funds explains why the app highlighted more liquidity than a standard withdrawal could use. What a depositor can withdraw depends on what is still available when the transaction executes.

In a lending market where borrowers are using most of the funds, repayments or fresh deposits can free up tokens for another withdrawal.

On eligible vaults, Morpho also offers an in-kind exit that can return some EURCV to the wallet and convert part of the redeemed amount into a direct position in a lending market.

According to recent reports, two Nov. 26 liquidity readings show how much of the largest position the vault could cover in a hypothetical full exit.

Vault liquidity controls how many tokens can leave. Direct issuer redemption has its own eligibility checks and cash deadline.

Recent findings suggest that the app view and indexed ordinary withdrawal limit are separate Nov. 26 readings. The issuer path applies to eligible EEA residents under the September 2026 white paper, subject to checks and transfer requirements. A submitted withdrawal may face changed liquidity.

SG-FORGE’s September 2026 EURCV white paper notes holders resident in the European Economic Area can request direct redemption at one euro per token.

The issuer applies identity, anti-money-laundering, sanctions and other checks, and may require the holder to transfer the tokens to it.

SG-FORGE notes it will transfer euros to the holder’s cash account by the last business day of the month after the checks clear. For example, if the checks clear in December, the white paper’s timetable sets the payment deadline for the last business day of January.
