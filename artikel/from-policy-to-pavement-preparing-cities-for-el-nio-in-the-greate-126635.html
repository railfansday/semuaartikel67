To limit the toll of El Niño's October-December rains, IGAD's specialized climate institution, the Climate Prediction and Applications Centre (ICPAC), has released a practical guide with UN-Habitat for Horn of Africa cities. The Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD) is a regional bloc in Eastern Africa of governments from the Greater Horn of Africa, and their High-Level Call to Action on El Niño Preparedness establishes a regional commitment to move from reactive disaster response to proactive action for long-term resilience. Emphasizing cross-border coordination and crediting local knowledge, the call states: "We recognize that climate and disaster risks do not respect national borders, and that no single country or institution can manage them alone.".

At the national policy level, the Office of the Prime Minister (OPM) secures anticipatory governance through The Uganda Roadmap on Anticipatory Action (2026-2031). It was developed in collaboration with government ministries, such as the Ministry of Water and Environment; agencies, including the Uganda National Department of Meteorological Services, district local governments; civil society organizations; academic institutions, namely Makerere University, and development partners, including WFP, FAO, ICPAC, URCS, UNDRR, the World Bank, IOM, Save the Children and others. Formulated around six strategic pillars, the framework aims to strengthen Early Warning, develop standardized Anticipatory Action protocols, integrate those into national Policies and Advocacies, improve Governance and institutional Coordination, secure sustainable Financing Mechanisms, and strengthen Monitoring, Evaluation, Research and Learning.
