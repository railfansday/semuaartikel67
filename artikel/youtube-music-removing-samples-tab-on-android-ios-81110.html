The recent changes to YouTube Music are really shaping it into a nice app.

While it’s no longer accessible from the navigation bar, Samples remains available on playlist, artist, and album pages in the top-right corner for a more focused experience.

This server-side change has been slowly rolling out in recent weeks, and we’re now seeing wider availability. For free users, “Upgrade” remains as a fourth tab.

Meanwhile, YouTube Music has rolled out visual tweaks to buttons on the album and playlist pages. Play is now on the right, while download, add to library, and comments are grouped together at the left.

Looking ahead, YouTube announced in September that Ask Music will soon be accessible from the top-right corner. This Gemini spark icon is slowly rolling out to users.
