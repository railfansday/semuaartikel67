Could Bitwise’s $667M XRP ETF inflows come to the rescue as bear trap takes shape?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitwise has released its annual XRP ETF report.

According to the report, the ETF has seen net cash flows of $667 million since it began trading on the NYSE in November 2025. Meanwhile, assets under management were in excess of $376 million while total trading crossed $3.5 billion, averaging around $14 million a day.

In essence, despite XRP’s lackluster performance, interest in the ETF continues to receive institutional investment, implying a high level of investor demand.

Sources indicate that in a post on X, Bitwise highlighted XRP’s decade-long presence in the crypto market. It also noted how the asset has remained relevant through multiple market cycles and continues to shape the industry’s future.

XRP has been part of crypto’s story for more than a decade, through every cycle, and it’s still helping shape what comes next. We’re grateful to everyone who’s been part of the journey so far. Here’s to year two!

The timing is particularly interesting here.

Bitwise’s report comes as XRP’s technical and on-chain metrics continue to deteriorate. Meanwhile, prediction markets are also increasingly pricing in a year-end target below $0.80. Against this backdrop, the key question is whether Bitwise’s report can strengthen institutional interest and turn the current bearish setup into a bear trap.

The impact of Bitwise’s one-year XRP ETF performance could already be triggering FOMO.

Notably, another Wall Street firm has disclosed exposure to an XRP ETF. Atlanta-based Arkadios Wealth Advisors, which looks after $8.79 billion in client assets, stated this in its most recent SEC document.

Furthermore, there is some institutional interest in Turkiye too. One of the nation’s biggest private banks, Yapi Kredi, is leveraging Ripple Custody to provide digital asset security solutions.

The key takeaway? XRP’s derivatives continue to be significantly net short, with the long/short ratio showing a pronounced sell-side sentiment. At the time of writing, XRP seemed set to close its 10th consecutive weekly down candle, with broader market FUD and prediction markets pricing in a year-end target below $0.80 to reinforce the cautious outlook.

Interestingly, however, the XRP/BTC ratio has risen by almost 5% following three successive weeks of upside.

In essence, broader market weakness could create room for more capital to flow into XRP.

This makes it an vital setup to watch over the coming months. The logic is simple —  Entering Q4, market participants are already factoring in further downside potential. In this context, Bitwise’s recent report, growing ETF exposure, and Ripple’s expansion in Turkiye could encourage further capital rotation into XRP.

If this trend continues, XRP’s ongoing consolidation around $0.90 could turn out to be an accumulation phase before a potential bear trap.

Bitwise’s XRP ETF has attracted $667 million in net inflows, while growing institutional exposure and Ripple’s expansion in Turkiye point to sustained demand.

Despite bearish derivatives and weakening market sentiment, rising XRP/BTC strength could signal a potential accumulation phase.
