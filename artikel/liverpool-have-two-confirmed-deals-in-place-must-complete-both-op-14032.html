Liverpool have two deals in place already. We believe both should be completed ASAP.

In a recent development, liverpool must sort their defensive situation out as it's one that risks getting massively out of hand. And that'll take some work.

As things stand, there are only two centre-backs in the first-team squad who are contracted to the club beyond this season. One is Jeremy Jacquet and the other Giovanni Leoni.

Sources indicate that only one of those has actually finished 90 minutes for Liverpool. It is, in other words, a less-than-ideal situation and one which will give Julian Ward and organization a real headache.

Virgil van Dijk is out of contract, of course, as is Joe Gomez. Ronald Araujo's loan will end, too, and that trims the squad down dramatically. Fortunately, there's a relatively easy way to fix things as Liverpool do have two centre-backs deals lined up and agreed.

They just need to trigger them.

Liverpool's centre-back double-dealsAraujo, of course, may not leave. Liverpool have the option to sign the defender for €55m from Barcelona as long as he wants to commit to the Reds. Marca is among those claiming he wants to do just that, even if Liverpool need to wait until the end of the season to actually trigger the option. It appears, though, that the club are in favour of signing Araujo and that he will be a centre-back option beyond this season.

According to recent reports, and he’s not the only one with a clause. Jarell Quansah left Liverpool in 2025 but the centre-back may be on his way back. There’s a buy clause in the deal with Bayer Leverkusen - one for 2026 and one for 2027 (Liverpool Echo).

Liverpool didn’t trigger the one in 2026, of course, but may well do so in 2027. That was always going to be the more likely of the two clauses, anyway, and the timing couldn’t be more ideal for a homegrown centre-back re-joining the club.

The Reds, then, are in a difficult situation but it’s one that they have created some room for manoeuvring in.
