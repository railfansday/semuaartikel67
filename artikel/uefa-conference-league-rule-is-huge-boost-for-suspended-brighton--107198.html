Brighton should have right-back Joao Costinha available for Thursday's Uefa Conference League clash despite his late dismissal against Hull City.

The Portuguese defender was sent off in the 89th minute of Sunday's 2-0 Premier League defeat at the MKM Stadium after receiving a second yellow card for his foul on Sorba Thomas.

The dismissal proved costly for Fabian Hurzeler's second placed side, who conceded twice in stoppage time through Oli McBurnie.

However, Costinha will escape a European suspension because his red card was received in a domestic competition.

Under FA rules, receiving a second caution in the same match carries an automatic one-game suspension, which only kicks in for the domestic league and domestic cups.

That means Costinha will miss this Sunday’s match Premier League clash against Newcastle at the Amex Stadium but should be available for Albion’s trip to Bournemouth on December 2.

Uefa operates separately to the Premier League which means Costinha can feature against Universitatea Craiova at the Amex Stadium on Thursday.

Hurzeler admitted the sending-off had a significant impact on Brighton's defeat but insisted the referee was not responsible for the result.

"Of course when you are one man down, the red card plays a role," he said.

The German added: "For 65 minutes we indicated a good performance then we dropped our intensity, levels and standards.

In a recent development, "In the Premier League they can punish you especially after a red card. Overall a frustrating afternoon, a frustrating game but we have to keep going.".

Hurzeler also refused to blame referee Peter Bankes, saying: "The referee wasn't the reason why we lost.".
