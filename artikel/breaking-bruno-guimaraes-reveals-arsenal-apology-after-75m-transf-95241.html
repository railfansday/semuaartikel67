Bruno Guimaraes revealed he apologised to the Arsenal squad after joining the club in the summer.

The Brazilian spent five years at Newcastle before completing a £75million move to Arsenal in August.

During his time on Tyneside, Guimaraes had several heated clashed with Arsenal’s midfielders, including Jorginho and Declan Rice.

Once he had switched loyalties and become a Gunner, the No39 addressed those battles with his newly introduced team-mates.

“I remember the first time I had to speak in front of everyone, the first thing I stated was sorry to the boys.” Guimaraes said.

It has been revealed that “Because definitely, [those] who knows me know how much I hate to lose. I always play hard and I know I have some fights against them.

“So I say sorry now [we’re] in the same team, ‘I can’t wait to compete for you guys’.”.

It has been a patchy start to life at Arsenal for Guimaraes, who has had his best moments off the bench as a substitute.

He has struggled more when starting and his partnership with Rice has not looked a particularly balanced or natural one.

However, the 29-year-old insisted there is still “much more” to come from him, and he is determined to play his part in an Arsenal side challenging on all fronts.

“We push each other to always be playing well, because if you don’t play well, other players will come and play well,” Guimaraes said.

It has been revealed that “I think that’s very vital for Arsenal. We want to make a dynasty here; we want to win everything possible. I think since I came to the club, the first thing Mikel said to me was create a dynasty for the club, so we want to win everything that’s possible.

“I know how much the Champions League means for the fans, and for us here. Tomorrow we have a massive test with a top team, they have been unbelievable this season, so tomorrow we have to play a very good game because it’s going to be against a top side.”.
