Counterpart is the first Agentic Insurance™ platform, combining trusted insurance expertise with cutting-edge AI to deliver tailored management and professional liability solutions. By integrating Agentic Underwriting™, Agentic Broker Services™, Agentic Risk Mitigation™, Agentic Claims Management™, Agentic Coverage™, and Agentic Capacity™ into one powerful platform, Counterpart empowers brokers - and the businesses they serve - to do more with less risk. Backed by A rated carriers, Counterpart has been recognized by CB Insights as a 2025 “Top 50 Insurtech,” by Great Place to Work, and by Business Insurance as one of its 2025 “Best Places to Work.”.

According to recent reports, we're seeking a Financial Data Manager to design and deliver the data workflows that power Counterpart's financial and accounting systems. You will report to Pricing & Analytics and serve three primary customers: the finance function, the analytics function, and broker services (billing). You will translate insurance accounting concepts — written and earned premium, unearned premium reserves, commissions, and cash application — into clear data requirements. You will then partner with engineering to build them. This is a design and project management role at the intersection of finance, data, and product.

Our estimated pay range for this role is $150,000 to $180,000. Base salary is determined by a variety of factors, including but not limited to, market data, location, internal equitability, and experience.
