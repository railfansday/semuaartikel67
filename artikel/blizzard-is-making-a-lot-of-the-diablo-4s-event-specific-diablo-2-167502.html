Blizzard is making a lot of the Diablo 4's event-specific Diablo 2 anniversary content permanent because everyone loved it in Season 15

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Blizzard has officially unveiled Diablo 4's next live-service season: Season of Tyrael's Gambit. This is the ARPG's 16th season, and it follows on from the popular and well-received Season of Hell's Legacy.

In a recent development, in an unexpected move, Blizzard announced it's keeping some of the nostalgia-laiden content from the previous season, Season of Hell's Legacy, as a permanent addition. This is unusual, as the ephemeral nature of live seasons means new content goes away at the end of each season.

Season of Hell's Legacy paid tribute to Diablo's legacy and its 30th anniversary by introducing a lot of content inspired by previous Diablo games, chiefly Diablo 2. The list includes the return of iconic character Deckard Cain, a recreation of Old Tristram, and a substantial fight against the three Prime Evils - Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto - in their classic forms.

Sources indicate that "We heard overwhelming requests for features from Season of Hell's Legacy to carry forward as permanent game features," Blizzard wrote in Season 16's announcement post. "While the entire season couldn't come along for the ride, we are implementing the following features with the launch of Season of Tyrael's Gambit that will carry forward in both Eternal and Seasonal realms.".

So here's what's sticking around:.

Season of Tyrael's Gambit kicks off 1st December, so you still have time to access all of that in Diablo 4 until Season 15 ends. Certain things, such as craftable items, won't return, so you may as well try them while you can. That said, Blizzard is aware of the love some of those items have, and the developer said it may bring them back in the future.
