Bayern Munich had a very difficult evening when they received Cologne (1-1). Harry Kane came off with a serious ankle injury after 20 minutes. At the break, it was Michael Olise who had to give up his place, also affected, less severely, but he still left the stadium on crutches. For the Frenchman, we do not yet know his length of unavailability but it should not be too long, compared to the Ballon d’Or. The Englishman will have to be absent for several months after rupturing an ankle ligament.

Sources indicate that jamal Musiala played the last 20 minutes of the game. He is still facing health problems, he who suffers from a neurological dysfunction preventing him from being able to last the length of a match. Problem, he is one of the highest paid players at the club and his situation is starting to get talked about. Until then, he had always received the full support of his management but the first flaws appeared last night with a vitriolic interview with Karl-Heinz Rummenigge. The latter let go a little about the future of the 23-year-old player.

Recent findings suggest that to summarize, the Bavarian legend considers that this situation is no longer tenable. “It’s entirely possible (that he regains his level, editor’s note) and we cannot rule it out. Unfortunately, what we know suggests that it will be difficult to reach the level he has so often displayed here.immediately hits the member of the supervisory board, and former chairman of the club’s board of directors, before continuing. “We need to talk to the family, the player and especially his representatives. These sums are no longer possible – sorry, with all due respect.».

The former striker continues his explanation by highlighting the interests of the club. “I can’t imagine Jamal leaving the club or another club buying him in these circumstances. This is problematic. We handled the situation very tactfully with the player, and we all want him back, ideally at the top of his form. But what will happen if this is not the case?he asks. It will then be necessary to look at the financial aspects. And this is a task that unfortunately falls to the board of directors and the supervisory board.».

Recent findings suggest that A meeting will soon take place, Rummenigge promises, between Bayern, Musiala, who is under contract until 2030, and his representatives to try to find a solution. “We prefer to wait and observe how the situation develops in the coming weeks. We will then have to carefully monitor events before making a decision.» Asked about these statements after last night’s draw, Vincent Kompany preferred to kick into touch. “If Jamal regains his level and form, and if we achieve it together, FC Bayern will come out on top.».
