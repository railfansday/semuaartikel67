P&O Cruises has partnered with Harding+ and Bunnahabhain to offer guests at sea the Bunnahabhain 17-Year-Old Ruby Port Cask Finish, an exclusive Islay single malt.

The drink was selected by the P&O Cruises & Harding+ teams and bottled exclusively for guests.

Limited to just over 300 bottles, the Bunnahabhain release marks a first for both P&O Cruises and Bunnahabhain, the organization stated in a press release.

The exclusive release will be available across all seven P&O Cruises ships from early November. Onboard the Iona, the launch will take place through a Bunnahabhain pop-up activation across November and December.

Carnival UK and P&O Cruises president Paul Ludlow stated: “Creating memorable experiences for our guests is at the heart of everything we do and this exclusive partnership with Harding+ and Bunnahabhain is a great example of that.”.

“By working together to select and bottle a whisky available only on board our ships, we’re giving guests the opportunity to discover something genuinely unique, while bringing to life the sense of exploration and discovery that makes a holiday with P&O Cruises so special,” added Ludlow.

Lynsey Eades, head of global travel retail, CVH Spirits (Bunnahabhain Distillers), said: “We’re incredibly excited to bring this exclusive launch to life through our partnership with Bunnahabhain and Carnival UK.”.

“This marks an exciting first at sea for Bunnahabhain and is a fantastic example of what can be achieved through strong collaboration between Bunnahabhain, Carnival UK and Harding+,” added Eades.

“Together, we’re focused on bringing distinctive brands and unique experiences to guests, and this launch reflects our shared ambition to create something truly special in the cruise retail environment. We look forward to building on this strong partnership and seeing the launch come to life.”.

According to the press release, the activation will be supported by dedicated in-store marketing materials, a whisky tasting masterclass and gifts with purchase on selected Bunnahabhain expressions, throughout the activation period.

Harding+ COO Linzi Walker added: “P&O Cruises brings its understanding of its guests, Bunnahabhain its exceptional whisky-making heritage and Harding+ our insight into what our guests want to discover and experience at sea, alongside our focus on creating distinctive onboard retail experiences.”.

“Together, we can offer something genuinely special that guests won’t find anywhere else,” added Walker.
