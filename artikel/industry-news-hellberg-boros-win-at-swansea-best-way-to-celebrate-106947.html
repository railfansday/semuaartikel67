Kim Hellberg said there was no better way to celebrate his one-year anniversary in charge of Middlesbrough by earning another victory in the Championship on Tuesday.

A year on from being appointed as the club's second overseas-born head coach, Hellberg led his side back to the top of the table with a 2-1 win at home to Swansea City.

Myles Peart-Harris opened the scoring at the Riverside Stadium in the ninth minute before Joseph Opoku levelled things up for the visitors 13 minutes later.

However, the Championship's top scorer, Will Lankshear, swept home his 14th league goal of the season nine minutes into the second half to seal the win for Middlesbrough.

Middlesbrough have now won seven of their last nine league games against Swansea (D1 L1), as they moved one point clear at the top of the second tier.

Hellberg's side missed out on promotion to the Championship in 2025-26 after losing to Hull City in the play-off final, but the Middlesbrough boss was not getting too far ahead of himself, instead focusing on this Friday's clash with Southampton, their opponents amid the "Spygate" controversy last season.

"That makes it one year, 46 league games. It was a nice way to celebrate that," Hellberg said.

Industry observers note that "I think over 90 minutes we were quite good against a different challenge from the last game. It was more of an attacking game.

"I think we should have scored more goals, but we take 2-1 today and move on to Friday [against Southampton].

"Happy with the performance, happy with how much we created and happy with how few chances we gave away, even if they could have scored another one.

"I always felt we would create enough chances to win.".
