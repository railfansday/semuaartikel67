No ricotta? Reach for a scoop of protein-rich cottage cheese. It's the secret to these tender, moist and protein-rich meatballs.

Recent findings suggest that my kids have never been huge meat eaters. In fact, my son is more likely to polish off a giant bowl of cherry tomatoes than he is to dig into a burger. Meatballs, though, have always been the exception—and these cottage cheese meatballs have been a long-time favorite. My kids love how tender and juicy they are, and I love that they sneak a little extra protein into their diet.

Many traditional meatball recipes already include ricotta cheese, which binds and adds moisture to the meat mixture. Cottage cheese is a natural swap, adding fat that keeps the meatballs irresistibly moist and tender. Once these protein-packed meatballs are simmered in your favorite jarred pasta sauce and served over pasta, people will never guess that cottage cheese is the secret ingredient.

According to recent reports, editor’s Tip: For the softest, most tender meatballs, handle them gently as you shape them. Avoid overhandling or compacting the meat too much (one of Grandma’s best meatball tips), as it can make the meatballs tough and dense.
