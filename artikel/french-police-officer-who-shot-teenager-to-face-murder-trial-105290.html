French police officer who shot teenager to face murder trial

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (58 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A French police officer will stand trial for murder in the killing of a teenager in 2023 that sparked violent protests across the country, the Versailles appeals court said on Tuesday. Nahel Merzouk, 17, was shot in the Paris suburb of Nanterre after he failed to comply with an order to stop his car.
