In a recent development, I bought the Cuisinart 14-cup drip coffee maker at Costco for $59.99 back in... 2011? And I'm sipping coffee I just made with it as we speak. The same Cuisinart at other stores is $120+ but Costco has consistently kept something like it in stock for many years. The "4-cup" setting stopped working recently, so I only have the Regular and Bold settings.Kind of detoxifying my life and don't like mixing heat + plastic any more (or using plastic regardless of temperature), and darn near every drip coffee maker uses plastic everywhere. I might get a French press or stainless steel Aeropress or something. Or pourover with a Chemex type setup.I'm tempted by some of the Ninja drip machines at Costco, but I decided not to buy.Statistics: Posted by Pancakes-Eggs-Bacon — Fri Nov 20, 2026 5:28 pm.
