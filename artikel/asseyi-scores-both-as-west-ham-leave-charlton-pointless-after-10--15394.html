West Ham beat Charlton Athletic 2-0 in the WSL on Sunday 15 November, with Viviane Asseyi scoring both goals for Rita Guarino’s side. Kelly Gago set up the first, and the visitors managed only 1 shot on target all afternoon.

The breakthrough came 2 minutes before the interval. Gago provided the assist for Asseyi’s 43rd-minute opener, and West Ham went in at the break ahead.

According to recent reports, tempers were tested in first-half stoppage time. Nadine Riesen was booked for a foul, and Grace Johnston was shown a yellow card for dissent moments later.

Recent findings suggest that asseyi doubled the lead in the 53rd minute to settle it. The forward was rated 8.78, the highest on the pitch, after taking 7 shots and creating 2 chances for teammates.

Recent findings suggest that her brace takes her to 7 goals in the competition. Only Manchester City’s Shaw, with 12, has scored more in the WSL this season.

Industry observers note that the numbers reflect a one-sided afternoon. West Ham had 17 shots to Charlton’s 3, with 10 of theirs from inside the box, and generated 2.06 expected goals against 0.14. They also held 57% of the ball and won 7 corners. Charlton’s goalkeeper made 4 saves to keep the margin at two.

Charlton committed 20 fouls to West Ham’s 8. Flannery was booked in the 56th minute and Cassandra Korhonen followed in the 70th, giving Abigail Byrne 3 yellow cards to show the visitors in total.

Industry observers note that for Hills’ side, the table makes grim reading. Charlton are 14th and bottom with 0 points from 10 games, a goal difference of -25. Their only win in their last 6 matches in all competitions came against Ipswich Town.

West Ham are 9th on 12 points from 4 wins and 6 defeats. Guarino’s team have won 3 of their last 4 in all competitions, with the 5-0 loss to Manchester City the exception.

This fixture keeps going one way. West Ham have now won all 5 recent meetings between the clubs, including a 2-1 WSL Cup win at Charlton on 28 October, only 18 days earlier.
