What a November for Newcastle United teenager Matheos Ferreira.

The exciting 18-year-old winger got a first ever call up for his country this month, named in the Brazil Under 20s squad for a double header with Peru.

Last Monday (16 November 2026) he then made history, Matheos Ferreira becoming the first player developed at the Newcastle United Academy to go on to represent Brazil at international level. The 18-year-old is from Berwick but is eligible to represent the country through his Brazilian father, Thiago.

Not only did Matheos Ferreira make his first ever start for the Brazil Under 20s last Monday, it only took him 14 minutes to score! The United winger putting Brazil into the lead against Peru Under 20s in Belem. The Berwick teenager seeing his dream international debut get even better when he also supplied an assist in the win over Peru.

Flying back to Tyneside he must have been on cloud nine.

Last night it was back to the more bracing wintry conditions at Whitley Park, an Under 21s Premier Cup match against Nottingham Forest.

However, Matheos Ferreira brought a little bit of Brazil back with him.

On 34 minutes the winger cut in from the left before unleashing a long range wonder goal past the Forest keeper, just look at the reaction of the other players!!!

As for the match, Forest quickly equalised and then when they took the lead with eight minutes remaining, it looked like defeat on the cards for a very young NUFC Under 21 side.

However, there was one last memorable moment to come.

Sources indicate that muawiya Ghanem was signed at the age of 14 from West Brom in summer 2025, ahead of competition from Spurs and many other Premier League clubs. Newcastle United continuing to channel resources into bringing in what will hopefully be the stars of the future.

Now aged 15, Muawiya Ghanem made his Under 21s debut with 15 minutes to go last night and with only a few minutes remaining, capped that U21 debut with a headed equaliser from Johnny Emerson’s excellent delivery.

The match ending 2-2 and an especially memorable night for this 15 year old and 18 year old Matheos Ferreira.
