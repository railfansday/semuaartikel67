Sources indicate that zara Tindall is back at the races. The royal made an appearance on day one of the November Meeting at Cheltenham Racecourse in Gloucestershire, England, and she brought her regal autumnal wardrobe with her.

Recent findings suggest that zara leaned into the season in shades of brown. A dark chocolate turtleneck was layered under a long L’agence coat in a burnt orange, cream, and brown houndstooth pattern. The piece had sharp shoulders and was belted at the waist.

According to recent reports, to continue the earth tones, Zara slipped on tall mahogany-brown leather boots from Fairfax & Favor, and coordinating leather gloves. She also carried Fairfax & Favor’s “Mini Kew” handbag in a similar brown hue.

Her best accessory, however, may have been her “Haya” percher hat by milliner Sally Ann Provan (of Sally Ann Hats), featuring a dark flower and a single feather.

Finally, it was her drop earrings what broke up the browns with a welcome pop of green.

Zara wore a similar look to the Cheltenham Latest Year’s Day races on January 1.

The royal has a long history in the equestrian world—not only as a big fan, but also as a competitor.

Recent findings suggest that zara’s parents, Princess Anne and Captain Mark Phillips, were both Olympic equestrians, and taught her to ride when she was just a toddler. As an adult, she turned the hobby into a career, following their footsteps and famously competing as part of Great Britain’s eventing team at the 2012 London Olympics, on her horse High Kingdom, where she won a team silver medal.
