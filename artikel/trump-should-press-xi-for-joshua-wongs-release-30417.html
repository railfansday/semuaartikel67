Industry observers note that when President Donald Trump meets Chinese President Xi Jinping in Shenzhen this Thursday, the agenda will be crowded with tariffs, chips, Taiwan, and the war in Iran. One more item should be on it: Joshua Wong, a 30-year-old Hong Kong democracy activist who was sentenced to ten years in prison for the “crime” of talking to Congress.

In a recent development, on September 2, Wong pleaded guilty in Hong Kong’s High Court to “conspiring to collude with foreign forces,” a crime under Beijing’s notorious National Security Law. As prosecutors laid out their case, they cited Wong’s prior meetings with sitting U.S. lawmakers, including then–Senator Marco Rubio, along with testimony he previously gave to the Congressional-Executive Commission on China. On November 5, Wong was sentenced to ten years in prison.
