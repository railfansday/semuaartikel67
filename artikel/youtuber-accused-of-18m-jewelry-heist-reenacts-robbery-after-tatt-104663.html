An Indian YouTuber accused of stealing $1.8 million worth of jewelry has been forced to reenact the alleged heist for police after a distinctive tattoo helped investigators identify him.

On November 12, approximately 11.4kg of gold and diamond jewelry worth roughly 150,000,000 Indian rupees ($1.6 million) was discovered missing from a newly opened Tanishq jewelry showroom in Madurai, India.

Police believe the thief entered the building overnight using scaffolding at an adjacent construction site before descending through an unfinished elevator shaft and escaping with bags of valuables.

According to India Today, CCTV footage highlighted that the suspect had visited the store three times before the robbery, apparently to familiarize himself with its layout.

Despite wearing a mask, a distinctive tattoo visible in the footage reportedly helped investigators identify him as 25-year-old YouTuber Nishanth Krishna.

Days later, Nishanth was arrested after a bus driver recognized him and alerted police. According to The New Indian Express, he allegedly attempted to escape through a bus window as officers approached.

His girlfriend, fellow content creator Meenakshi Nachiyar and his father were also arrested in connection with the heist, with police alleging they helped conceal the stolen jewelry.

On November 20, Nishanth was taken back to the showroom and instructed to reenact the robbery, demonstrating how he entered the building, collected the jewelry, and escaped.

As reported by Hindustan Times, he was subsequently returned to Madurai Central Prison and remanded in judicial custody.
