As part of PortuGOAL’s efforts to constantly evolve and serve lovers of Portuguese football with top-quality and increasingly accessible content, we are replacing the PortuGOAL Android and Apple APPs with a new Progressive Web Application (PWA) as from tomorrow, Tuesday 3 April.

Recent findings suggest that this significant step forward in phone apps technology loads the content instantly, even in areas of low connectivity, stays up to date at all times, displays the most recent version upon launching, is more efficient than native apps, works on-demand and is always accessible, without taking up a smartphone’s valuable memory or data. This is a football site, not a technology one, but for those of you of a techy nature, click here for more on why PWAs are the future of phone applications.

Sources indicate that in another improvement, the website itself will be implementing SSL technology at the same time, leading to more secure and faster navigation. PortuGOAListas should be directed to the newly introduced URL automatically from tomorrow, if not simply type http://portugoal.net into your browser’s address bar then bookmark the page. Just a quick detour… now back to the football!
