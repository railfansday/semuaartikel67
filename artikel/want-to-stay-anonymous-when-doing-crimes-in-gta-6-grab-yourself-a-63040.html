The GTA 6 mask neckwear lets you commit crimes with a bit more anonymity. As you might have noticed, cops are a lot more savvy in this new entry, pursuing you based on even your physical appearance. One way to hinder them identifying you is a mask.

In a recent development, the earliest mask you can buy is at Chuck's Tackle & Shot just down the road from Jason's house in Key Lento—if you're struggling to spot it, you can use the map index and navigate down to the Ammu-Nation tab. Any Ammu-Nation store should stock masks for purchase. In this case:.

In a recent development, buy and equip the mask and you're good to go. Jason will wear it on his neck by default but you can cover your face with it in the items menu. If you hold L1 then press R1 to cycle to items, move the stick over the mask, then press up on the D-pad to change the "Wear Style" to 1. Now you're ready for some crime.
