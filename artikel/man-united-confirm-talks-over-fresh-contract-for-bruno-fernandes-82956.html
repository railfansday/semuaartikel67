Omar Berrada has revealed Manchester United are in contract talks with Bruno Fernandes as they bid to extend his stay at Old Trafford.

The United captain’s current deal expires next summer, though the club have an option to extend it for a further year, but they are keen to keep the reigning PFA Player of the Year for longer.

Fernandes will turn 33 next September and has attracted lucrative interest from both Saudi Pro League clubs and Galatasaray in recent years but United hope he will stay.

While Fernandes was dropped by former manager Michael Carrick for the defeat to Bournemouth, after the Portugal manager had criticised the tactics in the loss to Como, newly introduced head coach Mauricio Pochettino has confirmed he will keep the captaincy.

And chief executive Berrada told Sky Sports: “We’re very proud to have him as captain. It’s true that we’re having conversations about his upcoming contract, but I prefer to keep that private. I think it’s something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club.".

Fernandes has scored 113 goals in 341 games for United, winning their player-of-the-year award a record five times, since joining in January 2020.

Berrada added: “What he’s done for this team over the last six, seven years is absolutely incredible. Last year he won, I think, all the individual awards you can win.

“And we’re very proud to have him as captain. It’s true that we’re having conversations about his new contract, but I prefer to keep that private. I think it’s something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club.”.

Berrada also insisted United could not have done more to keep JJ Gabriel, the teenage talent who has confirmed his departure from Old Trafford and who is expected to join Real Madrid.

“We did everything, absolutely everything," he added. “Him and his family have taken the decision to leave. We can only wish him the best. We want players that want to be here, that feel being at Manchester United is a privilege.".

Berrada, the former chief football officer at the City Football Group, also noted he is “was not involved in any wrongdoings” at his old club after Manchester City were found guilty of 114 charges of breaking the Premier League’s financial rules.
