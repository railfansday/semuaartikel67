Over the last few days, I've been running into various bits of discussion on social media about how GTA 6 is crashing or even "frying" some people's consoles, and for the most part it's been talked about in regards to the PS5 version.

This particular clip has been getting a ton of attention this week (profanity warning):.

The Verge editor Tom Warren ended up picking up on this, suggesting that "GTA 6 is really stress testing the GPUs inside consoles", and then pointing out that the Xbox Support Reddit community highlights issues on Xbox as well.

And after visiting it, yeah... there's no doubt it's got multiple reports from people who claim GTA 6 has been responsible for either crashing their console or putting stress on the GPU and causing the visuals to stop functioning properly.

Now, as scary as this might seem, it's worth keeping in mind that you're unlikely to run into any major issues. The majority of GTA 6 players are enjoying the game just fine on their Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S and PS5 consoles right now, and I'm willing to bet most people haven't had a single crash whatsoever.

It also should be noted that Rockstar aimed to reduce crashes in their latest revision for GTA 6 this week.

Sources indicate that at the same time, it's also impossible to say how widespread the problem is, but most people speculate that it's limited to anywhere between 0.1% and 5% of Xbox players at most. GTA 6 is an incredibly demanding game without a doubt, and it might unfortunately be exposing some weaknesses in certain consoles for whatever reason.

Industry observers note that that's why we're only saying "a small number of" consoles in the headline here, because we don't want you to get scared off from playing GTA 6 over the next few weeks and months. Just keep in mind that it's a good idea to ensure your console isn't too clogged up with dust every now and again, and hopefully you'll never run into issues like this.
