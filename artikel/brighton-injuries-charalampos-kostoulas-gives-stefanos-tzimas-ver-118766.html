Charalampos Kostoulas is convinced Stefanos Tzimas has the mentality to come back stronger as Brighton's Greek connection prepares for a potential reunion on the pitch.

The two forwards arrived at the Amex in 2025, with Brighton investing a combined total of around £50m in the pair of young talents from Greece.

Tzimas signed from FC Nurnberg in January 2025 for a reported £20.8m, before joining up with the Seagulls that summer and Kostoulas followed from Olympiacos, signing in June for a reported £29.8m.

Sources indicate that but their hopes of developing a partnership have been interrupted by injury. Tzimas suffered a serious ACL injury during Brighton's 4-3 defeat by Aston Villa last December, going down while pressing defender Pau Torres after 24 minutes.

The 20-year-old has been sidelined for almost a year. He made nine appearances for Brighton before the injury, scoring once and providing one assist.

Kostoulas - who has impressed this term with five goals and four assists - is delighted to see his compatriot make progress following his return to full training.

"I'm really happy for him," he told Sussex World. "It was a long time, but he was always really clear in his mind.

“He was looking at every day as one day closer to his comeback. He's really good. He has a really good mindset and I have big trust in him.

The pair have both featured in the same Brighton match, their competitive debuts in the 6-0 Carabao Cup win at Oxford United in August 2025.

However, they have yet to share the pitch: Tzimas was substituted in the 77th minute and Kostoulas came on a minute later.

Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler has been encouraged by Tzimas' return to first-team training, but is keen not to rush him back.

Industry observers note that speaking ahead of the 2-0 loss at Hull City, Hurzeler noted: "He's a great personality, and that's also shown by his teammates. Everyone was happy for him.

“Everyone really appreciates that he fought that hard for this comeback.

It has been revealed that "Now, of course, we have to be patient. It won't be that he will immediately be an option for us. He was out for a long time, so we need to give him time.".

Tzimas was pictured in full training yesterday and there’s a slim chance he could be included in Thursday night’s Uefa Conference League matchday squad matchday squad to face Romanian league leaders Craiova at the Amex Stadium.

Asked what it would mean to have two Greek forwards representing Brighton, he added with a smile: "Also, two offensive players!".
