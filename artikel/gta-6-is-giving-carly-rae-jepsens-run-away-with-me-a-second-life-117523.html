GTA 6 is giving Carly Rae Jepsens Run Away With Me a second life

Grand Theft Auto VI is a massive pop culture event that is affecting a whole lot of people from a whole lot of different angles. Arguably, few are benefiting more from the game's launch last week than Carly Rae Jepsen.

The Canadian pop star broke substantial worldwide with "Call Me Maybe" in 2012 and has followed up with an extremely respectable career that includes a devoted fanbase and eight studio albums, including the recently released Day & Night. However, one of her most beloved (and slightly less-well-known) songs is blowing up on streaming for the first time in a decade thanks to its inclusion in GTA 6, per Billboard Canada.

Recent findings suggest that that would be "Run Away With Me," the opening track to Jepsen's highly acclaimed 2015 record Emotion. Aside from being featured on GTA 6's in-game radio station Flash FM, "Run Away With Me" also soundtracks an important early-game mission. According to Billboard and an X account called Carly Rae Jepsen Charts, the song was streamed more than 100,000 times on both Saturday and Sunday, its two biggest individual streaming days since the song was new.

To be clear, this is not necessarily a case of GTA breathing life into an obscure old song. "Run Away With Me" made a small splash on music charts in Canada and a handful of other territories back in 2015, and was a critical darling both at the time and now. But the GTA bump is clearly very real, nonetheless.

Warning: The following section contains spoilers for an early mission in GTA 6.

If you're curious as to the specific context in which "Run Away With Me" and its iconic distorted saxophone riff are used in GTA 6, allow me to explain. During the game's first story chapter, players will run into a mission called "Day Release" that involves protagonists Jason and Lucia going to a party on an expensive yacht. Lucia's parole ankle monitor is temporarily disabled for this mission, and the whole idea is that she and Jason get to have a fun day of rich people debauchery together for the first time since she got out of prison.

A few minutes into the mission, Jason and Lucia are invited to ride jet skis with the host of the yacht party, and this is where the "Run Away With Me" needle-drop happens. As the two lovers speed around the waters of a fictional Miami stand-in, Jepsen's catchy chorus reverberates throughout the action. The best part is that the mission keeps going and includes several little gameplay vignettes, most of which are scored by a longer remix of the song provided by Overmono and Pegassi.

Industry observers note that what makes this great for those who enjoy both GTA and Carly Rae Jepsen is that the song isn't used just because it's good. It also perfectly evokes the excitement of the early stages of being in love and sonically fits the high-speed action during the mission.

According to recent reports, needless to say, it's been stuck in my head ever since I played the mission a few days ago. I hope it gets there for you, too.
