Recent findings suggest that open a Fidelity Charitable Giving Account if you want a donor-advised fund that takes any amount to start and accepts stock, crypto, or private tech giant shares at no extra charge. Work out its fee on the balance you’ll keep, since its $100 minimum costs more than the standard 0.60% on any balance under about $16,700.

Recent findings suggest that fidelity Charitable runs the Giving Account, a donor-advised fund that lets you take a tax deduction the year you donate, if you itemize, and recommend grants to charities over time. Its donors recommended $18.3 billion in grants in 2025, the most of any U.S. grantmaker by its own count, and the account suits givers who want to donate stock that has gained value or private organization shares.

Givers who keep $16,700 or more in the account pay the standard 0.60%, and nothing extra to donate private shares or crypto. Anyone keeping a few thousand dollars there pays the $100 minimum, 2% on $5,000, while DAFgiving360 charges the same 0.60% with no minimum fee, which comes to $30 on that balance.
