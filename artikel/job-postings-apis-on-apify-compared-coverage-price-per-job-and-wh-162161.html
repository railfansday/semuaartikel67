Recent findings suggest that disclosure: I built one of the Actors in this comparison (mr.wolf/ats-jobs-mcp-server). The numbers below come from the public Apify Store API on 27 November 2026. Prices change, so check each Store page before you decide.

If you need job postings as data (for a job board, a recruiting tool, sales signals or an AI agent), the Apify Store has several Actors that read employer career sites directly instead of scraping LinkedIn or Indeed. They look similar from the outside. They are not: they differ in coverage, in how you select companies, and in price per job by a factor of three.

This post compares three of them so you can pick the one that fits your job, not the one with the best title.

You need the whole market (a job board, labour-market research). Use one of the two index-based APIs. Four ATS will never cover Workday, which most large enterprises use, and you cannot list 275,000 boards by hand. fantastic-jobs adds AI enrichment and LinkedIn organization data and costs three times more per job. jobo.world costs the same as mine with much wider coverage. If coverage is what you buy, mine is the wrong tool.

According to recent reports, you track a known list of accounts (sales signals, competitor hiring, a VC portfolio). Here a board list is an advantage, not a limit:.

This is the case I built mine for. Tech companies and startups mostly use Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, so the four ATS cover most target lists in SaaS sales.

In a recent development, you want an AI agent to answer hiring questions. Mine runs as a Streamable HTTP MCP server with three tools:.

The last one matters for cost. An agent asking "which of these 20 accounts are scaling sales?" gets one summary for $0.005 instead of downloading hundreds of jobs into its context.

Twenty target companies, filtered to sales and data roles, 120 matching jobs per day:.

At the same price, the choice between the last two is about selection and freshness: an index you search, or a list of boards you read live.

If the agent only needs the summary, one detect_hiring_signals call costs $0.005 whatever the number of jobs behind it.

The Actor that finds the roles you care about at the lowest monthly cost is the right one, whichever it is.
