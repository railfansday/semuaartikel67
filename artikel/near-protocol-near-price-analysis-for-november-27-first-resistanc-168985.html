Industry observers note that as seen in the chart, prices have rebounded from November’s drop and the $2.60 area and are trading around $3.09 after having broken above its long-term moving average (near $3.00). Prices will need to hold above the $3.00–$3.10 area for support to begin to solidify buyers’ foothold above its long-term moving average.

Sources indicate that from here, $3.20–$3.30 could act as resistance before the aforementioned moving averages (near $3.45–$3.75). For now, NEAR’s trend remains pressured though early signs of stabilization are starting to show after its extended drop from its October high near $5.90.

In the above chart, we can see that momentum has started to improve though the shorter- and medium-term moving averages still slope downwards and lie above prices.

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) has started to improve after having approached oversold territory and though it has started to improve, it still sits below 50 which would indicate that prices still don’t show signs of momentum.

For buyers to start solidifying their foothold above its long-term moving average, it would need sustained price action above resistance as well as increased trading volumes to confirm a reversal.

If sellers were to retake control and push prices back below $2.80, it could start to chip away at NEAR’s recovery and put its November low near $2.60 back into focus.

NEAR Protocol has confirmed that its House of Stake (HoS) has ratified HSP-029 which looks to reduce the maximum rate of token issuance in the NEAR network from 2.5% to 1.6% over the course of 24 months. The proposal, which passed with 99.96% of votes in favor and no veto from its Security Council, will see the issuance cap decrease in each epoch as the change goes into effect.

NEAR estimates that implementing HSP-029 would prevent the issuance of 11.8 million NEAR tokens over the next two years relative to keeping the 2.5% cap in place. The current breakdown of NEAR issuance (90% to staking participants and 10% to NEAR’s treasury) would remain unchanged.

As mentioned above, there is a grace period of one full quarter before HSP-029 can go into effect once ratified. For HSP-029 to go into effect, a network upgrade would need to be implemented and passed by validators.

Though it may provide NEAR token holders with some measure of protection from dilution in the long-run, it’s unlikely to cause any sort of immediate shift in demand given its implementation would also reduce gross staking rewards.
