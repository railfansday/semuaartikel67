📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roboto Studio rebuilt the Flowhub brand identity around one mark, Helvetica Now and Neon Moss green, from the website to the checkout screen.

Flowhub runs point of sale, payments, ecommerce, marketing and reporting for specialty retailers across the United States, from cannabis dispensaries to liquor stores and CBD shops. More than $4B in sales passes through it every year. Its old purple identity is gone. In its place, Roboto Studio built a system on a single mark, one typeface and one bright green, then carried the same parts into the website and the product interface, so the homepage, the dashboard and the payment screen at the counter are made from the same pieces. The line that runs through it all says it plainly: Retail moves fast. We move faster.

Helvetica Now does the talking. Display Bold carries the headlines at sizes that fill the frame, white on Rich Black, while Display Medium, Text Medium and Text Regular step down through the interface without changing voice. The specimen page lines the weights up beside a full character set, and the restraint reveals in how little else the layouts need. The mark is a compact flowing form that reads at a glance and crops into a poster motif at bus stop scale. On the homepage the grid keeps real product screens in view, a strip of retailer logos leading into the hardware and apps, Maui OS, ecommerce, kiosk and delivery, each shown as the actual screen.

According to recent reports, two materials carry the system. Liquid chrome gives the mark its weight, a molten rendering that turns up on the poster, the SOC 2 shield and the map of backers. Frosted glass does the quieter work, layering the interface panels, the one platform stack and the deal cards Max drafts over photography. Color stays disciplined. Pearly White and Rich Black cover most of every surface, Pearl holds the panels, Neon Moss appears only on the one action that matters, and Sunburst orange is kept for anything that needs attention. The Intelligence dashboard opens on what needs attention today, sales, margin and stock value at the top, with Max drafting a reorder before a bestseller runs out. At the counter, the payment screen turns fully Neon Moss with a single word, Paid, large enough to read from the customer side.
