While mobile doesn't have an auteur developer on the level of something like Hideo Kojima, by now you've probably noticed there are plenty of names that crop up which we like to cover, such as Bart Bonte or solo developer Ovidiu Tepes, who has just opened the store page for his newest game, Sticky Snap!

Just like Tepes' previous releases, this is a deceptively simple-looking puzzler, but one that has some truly engaging mechanics to it. You play as a slime with three sticky limbs, and have to alternate between them as you travel across levels, dodging obstacles and seeking out your gold coin as you go.

It has been revealed that and that, I reckon, is why we like covering Tepes' stuff. It's a throwback to the days of mobile when a solid, original concept done well could become the new darling of the app store. Even if now, for better or worse, we have so much more variety.

Sticky Snap! is set to boast 41 different levels, unlockable skins and even a leaderboard. So if you want, you'll be able to prove your puzzling acumen. Keep an eye out for the release of Sticky Snap! on iOS and Android, projected to land January 6th next year!
