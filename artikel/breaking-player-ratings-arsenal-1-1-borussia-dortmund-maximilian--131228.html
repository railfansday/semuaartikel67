Maximilian Beier secured Borussia Dortmund a valuable point against unbeaten Arsenal in Tuesday night’s UEFA Champions League clash in London.

In a recent development, the Bundesliga leaders had to withstand early pressure, but Viktor Gyökeres left Gregor Kobel with no chance with a superb strike after ten minutes. The Swede gathered the ball just outside the Dortmund penalty area, before striking it over Kobel and into the top corner. Dortmund’s custodian and Daniel Svensson then thwarted chances as the onslaught continued. “We had an extremely weak first 15 minutes, where we were very hesitant,” Nico Schlotterbeck told Amazon Prime.

Dortmund continuously looked to exploit Arsenal’s high defensive line, but it was the Gunners who had the next golden opportunity through Declan Rice, only to be thwarted by the excellent Schlotterbeck who executed a timely block on the Englishman, who was through on goal.

In a recent development, arsenal took a slender 1-0 lead into the break, but Dortmund equalised ten minutes after the restart. Joane Gadou won the ball back in his own half before going on a superb run into the attacking third, before playing in Beier who cut back onto his left foot and slotted the ball under Kepa, who was deputising for the injured David Raya.

That goal clearly gave Dortmund confidence, as Jobe Bellingham saw his strike ruled out for offside after following up Julian Ryerson’s parried effort. Bellingham and Beier’s departure, though, seemed to take the sting out of Dortmund’s game, as Arsenal were on top in the final 20 minutes.

First, Gyökeres missed a sitter in the 76th minute after placing his free header wide of Kobel’s post, before a goalmouth scramble ensued four minutes later. Rice then hit the post just two minutes later, but Dortmund withstood the storm to secure a share of the spoils on a bitterly cold night.

Borussia Dortmund: Kobel (7) – Gadou (7) , Anton (6), Schlotterbeck (7) – Ryerson (5), Veerman (6), Nmecha (6), Svensson (4), Bellingham (6), Beier (7) – Silva (4).

Others: Guirassy (5), Karetsas (4), Sabitzer (5), Mane (-).
