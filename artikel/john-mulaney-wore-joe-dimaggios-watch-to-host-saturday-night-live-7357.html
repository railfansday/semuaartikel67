In a recent development, between sniffing out paternal autism and perfecting his Robert F. Kennedy impression, you may have noticed that John Mulaney was wearing an extraordinary watch while hosting Saturday Night Live this weekend. It’s a beauty—a gold vintage Patek Philippe with swoopy Breguet numerals—but its backstory is even more impressive. On December 12, 1948, Fresh York Yankees legend Joe DiMaggio purchased this timepiece and owned it until his death in 1999.

The Patek Philippe, a ref. 130 circa 1947, is already a rare bird before you add in its Joltin’ Joe provenance. The model is one of just 180 made in yellow gold with Breguet numerals, and only 55 with this same combination have ever reached the secondary market. In late October, dealer Eric Wind of Wind Vintage put this specific watch up for sale with a $1.2 million asking price. “I think it’s the greatest Patek that’s known and tied to any athlete,” Wind told Robb Report last month.

Industry observers note that for his seventh time hosting SNL, Mulaney came to Wind in search of “an important watch,” the dealer explains now. After surveying Wind’s entire catalog, the comedian landed on this piece, whose ties to a legendary New Yorker felt right for helming one of the city’s most hallowed institutions. To be clear, Mulaney only borrowed the watch for the show, which means its legend will have only grown by the time it reaches an eventual buyer. Talk about an illustrious provenance: A DiMaggio-owned Patek that also appeared on Saturday Night Live. “It was a very special moment and another cool moment of history for this important Patek Philippe,” Wind indicates.

Recent findings suggest that as a known history buff and devoted baseball fan, the watch likely appealed to Mulaney on several levels. But its eggshell-hued dial also happened to go perfectly with the brown suit Mulaney sported during his opening monologue. “John has classic styling,” Wind says, “and the Joe DiMaggio Patek Philippe was the chef’s kiss on his wrist.”.

Recent findings suggest that over the past several years, Mulaney has emerged as one of Hollywood’s most prolific and tasteful watch collectors. He is an eager and omnivorous admirer, bouncing between his beloved Rolex Air-King, loud Chopard chronographs, and glamorous Grand Seikos.

Mulaney, styled by Michael Fisher, wearing Cavour.

During our watch-focused interview last May, Mulaney told me that he steers away from more obvious models like your garden-variety Rolex Submariner. “I don’t ask any questions about it, I know what it is,” he explained. “And I’m just more interested in watches that [are a little more] mysterious.” As an example, Mulaney specifically referenced a Patek Philippe Calatrava his wife Olivia Munn bought him.

Sources indicate that the DiMaggio Patek is full of intrigue. The watch obviously has important provenance, but it’s historic on its own accord, too. The ref. 130 is named for the movement inside, known as the Calibre 130. That engine was the basis for Patek’s 1518, the first serially produced watch to combine a perpetual calendar and chronograph.

According to recent reports, funnily enough, this isn’t the first Mulaney, DiMaggio, and SNL crossover. In his 2018 special Kid Gorgeous at Radio City, the comedian explained what it was like working with famous SNL hosts during his time as a staff writer. “It’d be like if you went into your childhood bedroom and Joe DiMaggio was sitting there,” Mulaney quipped. “Yeah, he’s Joe DiMaggio, he’s a legend, he had sex with Marilyn Monroe, but only you know where the bathroom is.” And only Mulaney has had DiMaggio’s watch on his wrist, for now.
