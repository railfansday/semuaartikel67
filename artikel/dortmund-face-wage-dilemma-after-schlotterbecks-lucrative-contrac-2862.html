📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Waldemar Anton has rejected an initial offer from Borussia Dortmund to extend his contract beyond 2028, as he wasn't satisfied with the wages proposed by the club.

According to Bild, Anton was offered a salary worth only around half of the €14 million that his centre-back partner Nico Schlotterbeck can earn per year, including bonuses.

As reported by the German tabloid, Anton considers the offer too low, as he also sees himself as a "leader and an important pillar of the team".

The 30-year-old is in a strong negotiating position, particularly with Atlético Madrid and Manchester United reportedly continuing to show interest in him.

Recent findings suggest that bild suggests that Gregor Kobel and Serhou Guirassy could also point to Schlotterbeck's salary figures during their own contract negotiations, potentially creating further problems for the Black and Yellows. Like Anton, both players are under contract until 2028.
