If there's one thing L.L.Bean is going to do, it's stand the test of time. The quintessentially American outdoor brand supplies everything from hunting shoes and field coats to wool sweaters and flannel sheets—all made with exceptional quality. Whether you're fly-fishing in Montana, camping in the Great Smoky Mountains, or holed up in a cabin somewhere in the Hudson Valley, L.L.Bean has the gear you need to feel at one with nature. And stocking up on the essentials doesn't have to break the bank; there's always a sale, promotion, or coupon code within reach.

Industry observers note that founded in 1912 by Leon Leonwood Bean, L.L.Bean started out as a purveyor of the Maine Hunting Shoe, from a one-room operation in Freeport, Maine. The brand first expanded internationally in 1992 with a store in Japan, and today, some of its most popular items have reached cult status. Take the Boat and Tote, for example. Originally introduced as a bag to haul ice and wood in 1944, the iconic tote is often sold out, leading to vintage markups on Ebay and designer spin-offs. Whether you're a seasoned hiker or an it-girl who just wants to get in on the canvas tote trend, read on for the best ways to save at L.L.Bean this Black Friday.

Recent findings suggest that when you register for L.L.Bean's newsletter—where you'll find regular updates on the latest arrivals, upcoming sales, and exclusive coupons—you'll get 10% off your first order, delivered to your email within an hour or two after signing up. Newsletter signees will also be treated to free shipping on orders of $75 and more.

For Black Friday, the retailer is offering 15% off your purchase with code GIFT15. Now's the time to get that Boat and Tote, a can't-go-wrong gift for just about anyone. This coupon code also applies to the daily refreshment of upcoming deals, so you can end up saving up to 60% or more on your favorite styles, like this Popham puffer jacket for women, or this West Branch fishing shirt for men. Sales on shoes and camping gear are listed every day from 6 a.m. to midnight ET, like this do-it-all puffer blanket that's now 17% off. When it comes to clothing, you'll find new deals posted every day at both 6 a.m. and 2 p.m. ET. Whether you're looking to purchase a cozy PJ set, a quarter-zip pullover, or performance flannel, take advantage of this extra discount on top of clearance prices.

L.L.Bean gives thanks to those who have served our country, medical professionals, first responders, students, and teachers. If you are an active, veteran, or retired military personnel (or a family member of one), simply upload an official government-issued documentation with your name, branch of service, and current military status to receive 10% off future orders. Medical professionals and first responders—including police, firefighters, EMTs, and search and rescue workers—can receive the same discount of 10% off. After verifying name, school name, and date showing current enrollment status, college students and teachers can also get a 10% discount.

Industry observers note that if you're a tried and true fan of the beloved outdoor brand, consider opening an L.L.Bean Mastercard. You'll get a 25% off coupon once you're approved, and can earn ‘Bean Bucks’ to use on future purchases: The rewards structure offers 4% for L.L.Bean purchases, 2% for restaurants, and 1% for all other purchases. There's no annual fee attached to the card, and 1 Bean Buck translates to $1 in future savings. Cardholders can enjoy a few other perks, too, like free shipping and monogramming.
