Sen. Elissa Slotkin, D-Mich., said Thursday she will back Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., as he challenges current Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer for the majority leader slot.

Van Hollen revealed a bid for the role last week.

"I've been clear as well that I don't think that Senator Schumer is best positioned to be our next leader. I'm encouraged that people like Chris Van Hollen got in the race, and because I've been clear that I wasn't supporting Schumer, I will be supporting Chris Van Hollen," Slotkin stated in a video posted to social media.

"But I think no matter the outcome, it's going to be imperative for our leaders to demonstrate how they're going to incorporate that next generation, and what big and bold ideas, specifically, they're going to put forward in the first 100 days," she noted.

Sen.-elect Abdul El-Sayed — who will become the junior senator from Michigan early next year and is backing Van Hollen for the majority leader role — also shared Slotkin's post.

Sources indicate that sen.-elect Peggy Flanagan of Minnesota, who is also supporting Van Hollen's leadership bid, shared Slotkin's post and added, "Americans spoke. Democrats are listening.".
