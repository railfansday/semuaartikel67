📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that I feel like I say this once a week at this point, but gaming monitors occupy one of the only bright spots in the dark cloud that is PC hardware right now. They are getting better and cheaper by the day, and it seems I'm not alone in thinking this, as global OLED monitor shipments doubled in the last year. Woo, go OLED monitors.

This is according to Trendforce, which notes Asus is the market leader for global OLED monitor sales (at 21.7%), with MSI coming in second (14.5%) and Samsung coming in third (11.3%). LGE take fourth place with 8.6%, and Dell closely follows that with 8.2%. The rest of the industry has the remaining 35.7% to split among themselves.

As for total sales, Trendforce notes global OLED sales got a 13% bump from last quarter to this year, and more impressively, sales for OLED monitors reportedly doubled compared to the same quarter last year. That means OLED monitors have significant upward momentum right now.

And who can blame people for buying in? OLED monitors are known for having super vibrant colours, true blacks, and they tend to have wicked fast response times and refresh rates. The difference between IPS and OLED is so apparent when you put them beside each other, and I know, as I'm rocking a dual monitor setup with both panel technologies.

In a recent development, they are a bit more expensive than IPS, and older panels can suffer from burn-in, but I can say swapping from an IPS panel to an OLED one is one of the most monumental changes I've made to my gaming setup in the last decade. Though, in fairness, that also came with a resolution bump from 1080p to 1440p.

Recent findings suggest that OLEDs have been getting cheaper over time, too—they're far from the ultra-expensive pieces of kit we saw just a couple of years ago. And with upcoming manufacturing developments such as inkjet printing (IJP), there's every reason to believe the downward trend in pricing could continue.

What great news to hit just as Black Friday has started at Amazon. If you're feeling the hankering to stare into the vibrant blacks of an OLED panel, there are already some sweet savings to be had. Now that OLED gaming monitors are as cheap as $270, they're a seriously attractive upgrade option.
