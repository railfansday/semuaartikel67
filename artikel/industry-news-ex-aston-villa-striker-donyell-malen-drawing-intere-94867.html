Donyell Malen is drawing Premier League interest, GIVEMESPORT understands, after a sensational season so far at Roma.

Sources indicate that the former Aston Villa forward scored four times in Saturday’s 5-1 win at Parma to take his tally to 26 Serie A goals in 2026. That equals a Roma record for league goals in a calendar year, set by Pedro Manfredini in 1960 – and is more league goals than Harry Kane or Kylian Mbappe have managed in that time.

A January move would be difficult, though. Roma, two points behind leaders Lazio, do not want to sell Serie A’s top scorer, who is under contract until 2030. Roma could even offer Malen improved terms to stave off any interest.

Industry observers note that manchester United and Tottenham have both looked at the 27-year-old in the past, including during his time at Borussia Dortmund. Newcastle United are also believed to have discussed the striker over the summer.

Finding a new striker is a high priority in United's January transfer plans. Benjamin Sesko is out until the new year with a shin injury, Joshua Zirkzee could leave in January, and Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge ended in a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday that left them second from bottom.

They got a close look at Malen earlier this month, too, when he scored a penalty in Roma’s 3-2 Champions League defeat at Old Trafford.

In a recent development, but it is believed United are looking for more of an old-fashioned No 9. Leeds striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin is one name to have been considered, but he is expected to sign a newly introduced deal at Elland Road.

Malen’s time at Villa did not go to blueprint. He scored 10 goals in 46 appearances after joining from Dortmund in January 2025, and left on loan for Roma a year later. The Giallorossi made the move permanent at the end of last season.

Industry observers note that the key has been his position. Only around half of Malen’s league minutes at Villa came as a central striker. Under Gian Piero Gasperini, he has been utilised through the middle.

Malen told Corriere dello Sport last month that leaving Villa was his decision.

It has been revealed that “I wanted to play and be part of a team where I could take the field every weekend and play an important role,” he stated. “At that stage, I didn’t feel important, so I think I made the best choice.”.

Gasperini, speaking at the Festival dello Sport in Trento earlier this season, remarked: “There were a few names on the table, suddenly we heard that Aston Villa were selling Malen due to tactical issues.

It has been revealed that “He wanted to be a centre-forward, and I always thought that’s where he was stronger too, as he’s deadly near the penalty area. Admittedly, I didn’t expect his numbers to be so astonishing. I told him Gonzalo Higuain scored 36 Serie A goals in a season, but I’d be happy if he got to 30.”.

Malen needs one more goal to make Manfredini’s record his own. Roma host Monza on Sunday, after a Champions League trip to Paris Saint-Germain on Wednesday.

PSG have also been linked with a move for the Roma forward, but GMS understands should he leave Roma, a switch back to the Premier League would be of appeal in the long-run, but he is happy at Roma presently.
