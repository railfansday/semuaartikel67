The Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences (AIAS) has announced that Shigeru Miyamoto, the creator of iconic Nintendo franchises like Super Mario and The Legend of Zelda, will receive the inaugural Legend Award at the upcoming 30th Annual D.I.C.E. Awards ceremony on Thursday, 18th February 2027.

The award, which will only be presented once per decade, 'recognises an industry icon whose enduring vision continues to redefine interactive entertainment'. It's designed to honour creators whose achievements have already earning lasting universal acclaim, while celebrating their 'immeasurable impact and influence'.

Meggan Scavio, President of the AIAS, said the following:.

“Shigeru Miyamoto is widely recognized as one of the most respected and impactful figures in the games industry and beyond. We believe he perfectly embodies our inaugural Legend Award through his impact and reach, and we are so humbled and excited to have Mr. Miyamoto accept this award at the 30th anniversary of the D.I.C.E. Awards.".

Though his current focus is on the production of movies and theme parks, Miyamoto-san is arguably the most recognisable figure in Nintendo's history, and it's difficult to imagine whether the company would even exist today without his creations.

Sources indicate that he most recently appeared during a Pikmin Day Direct presentation, during which he revealed a newly introduced demo for Pikmin 4 on Switch 2 along with some Pikmin Bloom updates and the commencement of monthly Hello! Pikmin movies on the Nintendo Today app.
