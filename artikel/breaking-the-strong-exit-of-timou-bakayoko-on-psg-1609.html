Recent findings suggest that paris Saint-Germain no longer just shakes up Ligue 1. Since its coronation in the Champions League, the capital club has changed dimension in the eyes of its European adversaries. In any case, this is the observation made by Tiémoué Bakayoko, former AS Monaco and Chelsea midfielder, in the columns of The Team. According to him, Parisians have long suffered from a lack of consideration on the continental scene, particularly in the face of the historic institutions of European football. “ For a long time, clubs thought they could win against Paris more easily than against Barcelona, ​​​​Bayern or Real because there was a lack of history around this club. “, he explained. A perception that would now have completely changed. “ There was generally a lack of respect, the clubs arrived on the pitch saying to themselves that it was “only” Paris. Everything has changed. When you take a volley, behind fear settles », added the former French international.

Recent findings suggest that this new reputation is based in particular on the impressive performances of PSG since the start of the European campaign. On Tuesday, Luis Enrique’s men crushed Villarreal (6-0), signing their fourth victory in as many Champions League matches. With 20 goals scored in four matches, an average of five goals per match, Paris displays formidable offensive efficiency. Before their demonstration in Spain, the European champions had already largely dominated Slovan Bratislava (6-1), Manchester City (4-2) and FC Barcelona (4-2). Results which give weight to the words of Bakayoko, for whom PSG’s opponents no longer approach these confrontations with the same state of mind. Accustomed to imposing its domination in the championship, where it remains on five consecutive titles, the Parisian club now seems to inspire similar fear beyond the French borders.
