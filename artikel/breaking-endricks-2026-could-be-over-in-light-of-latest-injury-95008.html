A few minutes before kick-off for Parma vs. Roma, Giallorossi sporting director Tony D’Amico spoke to DAZN, discussing, among other topics, the transfer market.

“It’s premature to name names today; there are so many variables between now and January. Our goal is to improve the team, the same goal we have every transfer window. Leaving Koné doesn’t mean strengthening the squad; that’s our approach. We discuss the players’ characteristics with the coach every day. The January transfer window is difficult; we’ll evaluate the opportunities.”.

The sporting director preferred not to dwell on names so as not to distract attention from the pitch, which is currently the priority. But among the most talked-about names at the moment in Roma is undoubtedly Endrick, the Brazilian talent currently owned by Real Madrid.

The Roman club isn’t the only one eyeing the Merengues’ Brazilian talent. In Italy, for example, there’s strong competition from AC Milan, who appear poised to snatch him from the Spanish club, even on a permanent deal. The Endrick affair will inevitably continue to be a hot topic in the coming weeks.

However, in the meantime, as reported by Fabrizio Romano, who was playing in the La Liga match against Celta Vigo this past weekend, the Real Madrid player, born in 2006, has suffered a muscle injury to the adductor in his left leg. The Brazilian’s recovery time will be announced in the coming days.
