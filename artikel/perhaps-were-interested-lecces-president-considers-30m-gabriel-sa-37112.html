AC Milan have had a long-standing interest in Tiago Gabriel and they may now have found an open door for the mercato.

In a recent development, there have been plenty of links between Milan and defenders in recent weeks and months. Of course, a few names are repeated in this time and Goncalo Inacio is the one that most Milanisti will be familiar with.

Recent findings suggest that however, Gabriel’s is another. Since even before the summer, the Portuguese defender has been linked with a move to San Siro and it could well come true in the next mercato if things don’t align with other targets.

In fact, Tuttomercatoweb (via MilanNews) now reports that, thanks to help from Jorge Mendes, the club could sign him in the upcoming mercato. Similarly to Mario Gila, he’d be valued at around €30 million, but that price could be negotiated, of course.

TMW also relayed comments from the club’s president, Saverio Sticchi Damiani, so it’s certainly possible that he does make a move to Milan in the next 12 months….

“There was some discussion on the last day of the [summer] transfer window, but when it comes to deals as significant and key as that of Tiago Gabriel, there isn’t even enough time to set up negotiations on the last day of the transfer window.

Sources indicate that “Also, because the transfer window is very long, so starting discussions on the last day of the transfer window, all things considered, perhaps means we’re not really interested in the deal. Or perhaps we’re interested in a later, future transfer window.”.
