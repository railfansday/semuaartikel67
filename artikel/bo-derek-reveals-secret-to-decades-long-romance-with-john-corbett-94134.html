For Bo Derek, it's all about love.

The Bolero actress revealed the secret to making her relationship with husband John Corbett with after over two decades together.

"I fall in love," Derek, 70, told The Hollywood Reporter in an interview published Nov. 20. "I fall hard, and I think that when someone else truly loves you, it’s such a gift to cherish. It’s kind of a miracle with all the billions of people in the world, so I cherish it.".

Her comment comes after she provided some rare insight into her life with the Sex and the City actor—whom she wed in 2020 after 18 years of dating—including how he commemorated her recent 70th birthday.

It has been revealed that "He’s a great gift giver," Derek told People earlier this month. “I have a friend with a company. He’s very successful, and he’s starting a new company. And John went and got me shares in his company. It’s so much fun. I am not a wheeler dealer or a trader by any means. It’s all just algebra to me.".

Ultimately, though, it's Corbett's presence in her life that is the biggest gift of all.

In a recent development, "He keeps me laughing," Derek said. "He’s very youthful, enthusiastic.".

While the couple occasionally hosts a couple of friends for small dinner parties at their 110-acre ranch near Santa Barbara, Calif., they tend to prefer spending time at home with one another and their two German Shepherds.

In a recent development, "When we first met, he didn’t want to have a girlfriend with a dog because they just require too much time," Derek shared. "You’re just off going off having fun, and you got to rush back to the dog. And now I can’t get him to go anywhere with me because he doesn’t want to leave the dogs.".
