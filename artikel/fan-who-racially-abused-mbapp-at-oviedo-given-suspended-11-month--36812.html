Recent findings suggest that A court in Asturias has convicted the Real Oviedo supporter who made monkey gestures at Kylian Mbappé of a hate crime. The case adds to a growing list of criminal convictions for racist abuse of Real Madrid players in Spanish grounds.

Sources indicate that the Real Oviedo supporter who racially abused Kylian Mbappé at the Carlos Tartiere has been convicted of a hate crime. The Second Section of the Audiencia Provincial de Asturias delivered the verdict on 17 November 2026. It was a verdict by agreement, known in Spain as conformidad.

The abuse came during Real Oviedo v Real Madrid on 24 August 2025. The fan made monkey gestures and noises in the 37th minute. Real Madrid’s players were celebrating Mbappé’s opening goal at the time.

It has been revealed that the man will not go to prison as long as he keeps to the terms of his sentence. The term is suspended on condition that he stays away from football stadiums hosting official competitions for two years, Real Madrid said in an official statement. The prosecuting parties also tied the suspension to a ban from LaLiga stadiums and a ban on approaching Mbappé. The defendant accepted these conditions.

The fine was set at six months at a daily rate of €8. He is also barred from working in education, teaching, sport or leisure for three years and 11 months.

The case was charged under article 510.2.a of the Spanish Criminal Code. LaLiga filed the original complaint. It also supplied expert lip-reading evidence that proved key to resolving the case. Real Madrid later joined the proceedings as a private prosecutor.

Industry observers note that the ruling is LaLiga’s twelfth conviction for racism in Spanish football, Servimedia reported. The league presented the outcome as proof of its commitment to fighting racism.

The Carlos Tartiere has featured in this list before. In April 2026, a supporter was convicted over abuse of Marcus Rashford at Real Oviedo v FC Barcelona. That person received a nine-month prison sentence. Another conviction, handed down in September 2025, followed abuse of Iñaki Williams at an Espanyol v Athletic Club match from the 2019/20 season.

Industry observers note that for Real Madrid, the verdict extends a run of cases involving its own players. The club has listed earlier convictions for racist abuse at Mestalla, Son Moix, Vallecas and the José Zorrilla. The Valladolid case ended on 21 May 2025. The Audiencia Provincial de Valladolid convicted five people of hate crimes for abusing Vinícius Júnior at the José Zorrilla on 30 December 2022. Each received a one-year prison sentence.
