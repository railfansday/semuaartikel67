EXCLUSIVE: Vin Diesel is stepping outside the Fast & The Furious and Guardians Of The Galaxy franchises to make original action movie Burial Hill, we can reveal.

Diesel is teaming up on the project with Mike McGrale, writer on upcoming John Wick spinoff Caine, and director Jamie Payne, known for directing both of Netflix’s Luther movies and on the original Idris Elba series, as well as on indicates including Outlander, The Alienist and Doctor Who.

The hope is to create a newly introduced franchise around the protagonist (played by Diesel) who is a reclusive groundskeeper with a past. The screenplay follows a corrupt State Police unit that pursues a young witness to their crimes into the cemetery which Diesel’s character calls home. He is then forced back into the world he abandoned to save her and bring down the cops determined to bury the truth. Additional casting is in process. Industry observers note that this has been a hot project coming together behind-the-scenes in recent months. There was a chance it went to the AFM but the level of studio interest meant otherwise. Watch on Deadline.

Recent findings suggest that in similar fashion to their big pre-buy of A24’s Elden Ring, Amazon MGM Studios has gotten in early. We can reveal that the studio is finalizing a deal for most of international rights (barring a few territories such as CIS) to Burial Hill. Amazon will have all-rights in those territories in a pact we hear comes in at around the $35M-$40M mark. We understand talks are also going on around a potential North American deal but it’s not clear at this stage who the suitors are.

Mad River Pictures’ Marc Butan (Plane) will produce the film with Vin Diesel and Samantha Vincent’s One Race Films. Mad River’s Matt Berger will executive-produce.

According to recent reports, we understand the project is being fast-tracked with a plan to shoot in Q1 next year, most likely in Australia. We’re told additional funding is in place via the producers and the pic has a budget in the $60M-$70M range.

Industry observers note that if the movie shoots next year, it’ll mark Vin Diesel’s first non Marvel or Fast & Furious film in six years and only his second in the past decade. It’s rare to get the action star out of his hit franchises, which also include the xXx films.

The actor-producer is presently in production on Fast Forever (for a 2028 release) and was recently declared as an exec producer on a Fast & Furious TV series, both for Universal.

In a recent development, director Payne is in post on the second Luther movie starring Idris Elba and most recently directed multiple episodes of Apple’s Star City and Outlander: Blood Of My Blood. Writer McGrale recently co-wrote the story for Netflix’s The Rip and co-wrote the screenplay for Lionsgate’s upcoming Donnie Yen-directed John Wick spinoff, Caine.

CAA Media Finance arranged financing and handles domestic rights for Burial Hill.

Sources indicate that vin Diesel is represented by CAA and Linden Entertainment. Jamie Payne is represented by UTA and Independent Talent Group. Mike McGrale is represented by CAA and Heroes and Villains Entertainment.
