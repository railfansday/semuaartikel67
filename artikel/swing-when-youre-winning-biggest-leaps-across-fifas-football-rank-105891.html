Industry observers note that “The Scotland men’s team’s highest Fifa ranking was 13th (in 2007) and the worst was 88th place (in 2005) – a difference of 75 positions,” writes Bogdan Kotarlic. “Is this the biggest gap between the best and worst ranking for one national team?”.

According to recent reports, the gap between Scotland’s best and worst Fifa rankings isn’t the biggest in men’s international football; in fact, Pete Tomlin calculates that a 75-place swing puts them joint 141st with Ghana and Seychelles. And, as Chris Lewis and others point out, there are a couple of other home nations who have had more dramatic swings. Wales and Northern Ireland both moved up 109 places: Wales from 117th to eighth under Gary Speed and then Chris Coleman, Northern Ireland from 129th in 2004 to 20th in 2017.
