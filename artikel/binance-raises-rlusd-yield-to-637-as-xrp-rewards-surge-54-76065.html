According to recent reports, binance’s base reward rate for holding Ripple USD (RLUSD) rose from 4.48% to an annualized 6.37% for the seven days through Nov. 20.

At a steady qualifying balance of 10,000 RLUSD, the estimated weekly payout rose from 8.25 XRP to 12.72 XRP as the exchange also used a lower price to convert the reward into XRP.

In a recent development, A separate 600,000 XRP rewards campaign began at 00:00 UTC on Nov. 20. The upcoming round runs through Dec. 18 and gives eligible customers a reason to keep more RLUSD on Binance.

Binance’s distribution table indicates base APRs of 4.84% on Oct. 30 and 5.29% on Nov. 6, with XRP conversion references of $1.2379 and $1.2072 for those payouts. The base rate fell to 4.48% on Nov. 13, with a $1.0413 reference, before climbing to 6.37% on Nov. 20 as the reference dropped to $0.9603.

On a constant qualifying balance of 10,000 RLUSD, Binance’s seven-day formula estimates rewards of $9.28, $10.15, $8.59, and $12.22 across the four weeks before conversion.

Dividing by each week’s reference and rounding down to two decimal places gives 7.49, 8.40, 8.25 and 12.72 XRP.

Each column is the annualized base APR Binance reported for the seven days ending on that distribution date, excluding reward multipliers.

Source: Binance’s distribution table.

The estimated payout rose about 54% in XRP from the third week to the fourth. The higher base rate increased the dollar value under the formula, and the lower conversion price turned each dollar of reward into more XRP.

Users qualify through net RLUSD balances in eligible Spot, Funding, Flexible Earn, or Margin accounts. For each day, Binance uses the lowest balance it finds in hourly snapshots, then averages those daily lows over seven days.

According to recent reports, eligible participants generally receive XRP in their main Spot account. The formula rewards maintaining a balance through the week because a low reading counts for the entire day.

In a recent development, the renewal also applies a 1.1x boost to eligible RLUSD in Flexible Earn and 1.2x to qualifying balance increases above a baseline from the prior two weeks. The portion that meets both tests can earn 1.3 times the base reward.

Recent findings suggest that A seven-day illustration holds the qualifying balance at 10,000 RLUSD. The higher base APR raised its dollar reward, and a lower conversion price yielded more XRP per dollar.

For the same qualifying balance, the estimated payout rose from 8.25 XRP to 12.72 XRP.

It has been revealed that the balance is illustrative, with no boost or compounding. Binance’s conversion reference is separate from an independently timestamped spot trade. The calculation does not measure total XRP distributed.
