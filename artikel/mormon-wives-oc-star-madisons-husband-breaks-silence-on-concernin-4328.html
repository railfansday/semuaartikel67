The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star Madison Bontempo‘s husband, Kyler Fisher, broke his silence on concerns about his behavior toward his wife.

“I’ve been scared trying to find the words for this, but I want to speak honestly,” he wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 15. “Being on reality 1V means people see selected moments from a much bigger life together. There’s history, love, and everyday life that doesn’t make it onto the screen.”.

Fisher, 39, asked that people “leave room for that context.”.

“At the same time, I’m responsible for my words and behavior, including the moments people have concerns about,” he continued. “Madison deserves to feel loved, respected, and heard, especially when we disagree. Frustration doesn’t excuse hurtful words, and I’m sorry for the ways I’ve let her down in our 16 years together.”.

Sources indicate that fisher clarified where his marriage stands now, adding, “I love my wife. I want to listen without getting defensive, understand what she needs from me, and follow through. Becoming a better husband takes consistent work and accountability beyond anything I could say here.”.

Sources indicate that he concluded: “Madison has her own voice. I respect her right to share how she feels, but she doesn’t owe anyone a defense of me like she put out publicly on her stories. I hear the concern for her, and I’m taking it seriously. My focus is showing up for her with patience, kindness, and respect every day. Madison I’m committed to you and our family forever.”.

Bontempo, 34, also released a statement after offscreen backlash.

“I love my husband. We’ve been best friends for 16 years! Been through the best moments of life together and through hard times!” she wrote via Instagram on Sunday. “Could he have phrased things better on the show? Yes. Absolutely! I’m sure every person has regretted saying certain things or doing something they regret. But, he is a good husband and a wonderful dad!! And I’ll keep him forever.”.

Recent findings suggest that bontempo stood by her relationship, writing, “I am so grateful for the life we built, the opportunities we have had because of our teamwork together. The beautiful kids we created together. I know who he is and that’s all that matters.”.

It has been revealed that season 1, which is streaming on Hulu now, introduced the couple as they navigated concerns in their relationship. Bontempo’s friends called into question whether she wanted out of her marriage, and she was even surprised with an intervention.

In a recent development, “I feel like it’s hard when you’re watching it back sometimes because you don’t see the full picture,” Bontempo exclusively told Us. “Kyler and I are aware that we argue in our marriage. We’ve been together for 16 years, so we’re gonna have those times where we argue together. We really did want to showcase that. I think the girls really did seem concerned, and I really appreciated everybody coming together and trying to rally around me.”.

Bontempo pointed out that the Hulu show captured regular ups and downs in a marriage.

Sources indicate that “We’re all just trying to be there for each other. I choose to focus on that, and we just continue to grow together. All we can really do is we’re trying to showcase real moments.”.
