Brooksville Man Sentenced to Over 33 Years for Receiving, Distributing, and Possessing Child Sexual Abuse Material

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (98 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that tampa, Florida – Julian Russell Clawson (43, Tampa) has been sentenced by U.S. District Judge Mary Scriven sentenced to 33 years and 11 months in federal prison, followed by a lifetime of supervised release, for receiving, distributing, and possessing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) while on supervised release for possessing CSAM. Clawson was also ordered to pay $55,500 in restitution to victims of the offense and register as a sexual offender. A federal jury found Clawson guilty on July 14, 2026. U.S. Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement.
