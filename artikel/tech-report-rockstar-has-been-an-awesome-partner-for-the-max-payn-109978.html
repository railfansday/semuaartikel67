Tech Report: Rockstar has "been an awesome partner" for the Max Payne 1 and 2 remakes, says Remedy CEO

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that remakes of the first two Max Payne games are on the way, and Rockstar's been helping Remedy get the releases in order. According to the Control and Alan Wake studio, collaborating with the purveyor of GTA 6 has been absolutely grand so far.

Jean-Charles Gaudechon, the CEO of Remedy, described the working relationship between the two outfits in glowing terms for Game File. "Honestly, it's been great," he indicates. "You know why? Because I think they're super respectful of Remedy.".

Respect is a good start to any collaboration. Gaudechon then doubles down, just to make it clear everything's positive right now. "I'll say it out loud: They've honestly been an awesome partner," he adds. "And it's no bullshit.".

Well, it seems he doesn't have any complaints, anyway. Remedy and Rockstar actually have quite a bit of history, since the studio that gave us GTA actually worked on ports of the first Max Payne, then published the second outright.

Recent findings suggest that it only makes sense, then, that they'd team back up to give both games a lick of paint and enhancement them for modern hardware. As you may well be aware, it's been a while since Rockstar's done anything outside of the Grand Theft Auto or Red Dead Redemption mines.

Time was the developer and publisher would be involved in all sorts of projects, but nowadays the focus tends to be a little more streamlined. I guess that's what happens when you make the biggest games in the industry.

Recent findings suggest that the two have been working on these do-overs since around 2022, and we don't have a release window yet. Perhaps now that Alan Wake 2 and Control: Resonant are out of the way, Remedy can put in more resource, while Rockstar can finally do something that isn't GTA 6. I mean, how much is there to do post-launch for such a game? Our boy Max is waiting, people – come on.
