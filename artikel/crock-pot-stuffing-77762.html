There are only so many dishes you can make in your oven on Thanksgiving, so we’ve made it easy for you with a no-bake stuffing that doesn’t require taking up oven space. Instead, we use a slow cooker that’ll help you whip up your stuffing alongside the stars of the show (roast turkey and pumpkin pie, obviously!). While you buzz around the kitchen, the slow cooker will cook your stuffing for you — resulting in a delicious Thanksgiving side dish waiting for you to serve up.

According to recent reports, arrange a rack in the center of the oven and preheat it to 350°. Spread bread on a baking sheet. Bake the bread, tossing it halfway through, until it is crispy and feels dry. The edges should just be starting to turn golden, which should take about 15 to 20 minutes. Meanwhile, melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat. When melted, add celery, onion, and herbs. Season with salt and pepper and cook until the onions are translucent, about 7 minutes. Remove from heat and let cool.

Recent findings suggest that grease your Crock-Pot bowl and add the toasted bread. Pour the vegetable mixture, eggs, broth, and parsley over the bread. Toss to combine and cover.

According to recent reports, cook on low for 3 to 4 hours, stirring after 2 hours have passed. Garnish with parsley before serving.

Thanksgiving is hectic, we get it. This is the perfect dish to set aside while you prepare everything else. After the 3 hour cook time, switch the pot to the "keep warm" setting—just give it a stir every 30 minutes or so to make sure the bread around the edges doesn't dry out. If the stuffing is ready hours before dinner time, you can add up to ¼ cup of broth every few hours to keep things hydrated.
