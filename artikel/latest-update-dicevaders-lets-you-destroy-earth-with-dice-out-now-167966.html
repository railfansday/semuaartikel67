📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As we head towards the weekend, it's becoming increasingly cold and dark, and that means you'll probably want to sit and find a mobile game to sink plenty of time into. Well, you're in luck, because there's a constant glut of great latest releases, including the launch of Dicevaders on iOS!

It has been revealed that dicevaders sees you taking on the role not of defender of the Earth, but of its invaders and destroyers. To do so, you'll need to gradually overwhelm the defences of each of Earth's bastions, and you'll do so with dice (naturally). In essence, you place units, build up multipliers and gradually overcome the defence rating of each planet to destroy it.

It makes for a rather interesting combination of luck and strategy, where you'll need to think about when you might need to reroll and keep your supply of currencies stocked up to do so. The graphics are, of course, the kind of crunchy, colourful pixels I'm fond of, so you won't hear any complaints from me, and the mix of blues, purples and reds makes for a thoroughly alien colour palette.

There's plenty of synergy, different commanders and units to take advantage of too. So if you're looking for a game with plenty of depth to enjoy this winter, why not check out Dicevaders on iOS?
