QoR sweep Maryville to reach VCT Americas Kickoff North America playoffs after opening loss

QoR's 2-0 win over Maryville University completed a recovery from an opening defeat at the VCT Americas Kickoff North America qualifiers. A Kickoff place and a Red Bull Home Ground spot both stay within QoR's reach as a result.

In a recent development, the line-up of ExpMoe, calamity, vanitas, yearn and dynamic was one of eight teams Riot Games invited directly to the North America Qualifier Playoffs. Cloud9, ENVY and two Shopify Rebellion line-ups were also on that list.

Industry observers note that before facing Maryville, QoR edged Shopify Rebellion Gold 2-1 in round two. In the Swiss stage, two wins sent a team through and two losses knocked it out. QoR sit 99th in the Elo power rankings, with a rating of 1590 and a 37-34 record across 71 rated matches.

Maryville came through the second open qualifier, held from 12 to 15 November, which drew 512 teams. They finished in the top two of that bracket. They then opened this event by sweeping Shopify Rebellion Gold.

Industry observers note that A 0-2 loss to Cloud9 followed, and defeat by QoR left them with two losses. In the other round-three matches, Oafs beat SaD Esports, counterfeit beat FlyQuest and Wildcard beat Winthrop, each without dropping a map. The Elo table places Maryville 161st, with a rating of 1521 and a 6-7 record in 13 rated matches.

In a recent development, the playoffs are an eight-team double-elimination bracket. The winner takes North America’s direct place at the 2027 VCT Americas Kickoff in São Paulo. The runner-up goes to the Americas Last Chance Qualifier, also in São Paulo, on 17 and 18 December. That event is the last route into Kickoff. Eight partner teams are invited to Kickoff, and four places go to non-partner teams.

According to recent reports, riot runs the North America qualifiers with Red Bull. They also serve as the North America qualifiers for Red Bull Home Ground. The Los Angeles event runs from 10 to 13 December. Ten teams will play for a total prize pool of $100,000 USD. Eight are invited from around the world, and two earn their places through regional qualifiers. Riot will also reveal the 2027 Kickoff draws live on stage there.

According to recent reports, fnatic are EMEA’s invited team. The organisation is rebuilding its roster after parting with several players, including long-serving captain Jake ‘Boaster’ Howlett, Red Bull mentioned.
