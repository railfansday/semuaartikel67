Taken in isolation yesterday’s team performance at London Stadium doesn’t look quite so poor: A draw against the bottom side who had just beaten promotion prospects Southampton symbolises everything good about this Championship: Predictable it is not.

West Ham hit the wood work four times and on another day could easily have romped home. Trouble is, after the losses and draws of recent weeks a solitary point does nothing to halt the downward slide. It does seem a little early to start demanding change to Parker’s starting eleven – who as a unit were paying together for the first time yesterday.

Nuno was ‘the tinker man’ and you’d hope Scott Parker will persevere, despite criticism and articles today demanding the immediate axing of key players.

Piroe scored on his debut – surely it is a little early to consider discarding the striker who needs a run in Parker’s side.

Footballfancast.com is typical in calling for Joel Piroe to be dropped after his poor game:.

“The left-footed attacker delivered a dismal display in the centre-forward position and must be dropped by Parker ahead of the trip to Portsmouth in midweek”.

Industry observers note that the real problem with that knee-jerk reaction is that there is, simply put, nobody else to fill that role: Castellanos was invisible yesterday and fluffed his one chance, a header at the far post that didn’t get within a yard of the target. Pablo is, to all intents and purposes, finished now that his benefactor Nuno has left and until January, the cupboard is bare unless Parker gambles all on one of the youngsters.

Piroe needs the same kind of run which Castellanos had in the team before discarding him: He’s already outscored the Argentine forward by 4 goals to 1 this season. Give him a break.
