Garmin and Bark are competing for your money this year, and these sales make it easy to get something fun that’ll keep your kids safe and in touch.

Industry observers note that if you’re anything like me, you didn’t give your kids a phone or tablet the second they were out of the womb. Instead, I’ve slowly introduced them to personal devices as I felt it made sense, and a smartwatch was one of the first easy ways for them to stay in touch while away from home without being introduced to the dangers of the internet. This year’s Black Friday sales on Bark and Garmin kids’ smartwatches slash up to $64 off the regular price, making them an easier decision than ever to pick up.

Industry observers note that I’ve tested a lot of kids’ smartwatches over the years, and I can easily recommend the two in the picture above: the Bark Watch and the Garmin Bounce 2. Choosing between the two is easy enough. Garmin’s watch is simpler and comes in a slightly more durable case with fun colors. It’s slightly cheaper than Bark, but doesn’t have any health sensors on board. It mainly tracks steps, allows your kids to message or call you, and gives you geofencing and location tracking through a $9.99 per month LTE subscription initiative. It’s got a 5ATM rating and a polycarbonate case, so it’s quite durable.

The Garmin Bounce 2 is preloaded with a few mini games and educational apps, plus the Garmin Jr. app has built-in unlockables and rewards parents can set for doing chores, completing schoolwork, etc. Battery life is roughly 2 days with normal use, but can be less if you’re in an area with poor LTE reception. The Garmin Bounce 2 is best for kids 10 and under.

According to recent reports, the Bark Watch uses Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 hardware and a custom version of Wear OS. It’s a much more powerful watch and only gets a day of battery life because of it. This one feels like a midpoint between a kids’ watch and a standard Wear OS watch, as it has all the power of Wear OS but doesn’t feature the Google Play Store. It doesn’t have any games, social media, or browsers.

Sources indicate that since it uses Samsung’s hardware, it includes cutting-edge health sensors that your kids can use, plus great multitasking and a svelte, attractive design. Bark charges $15 per month for its initiative, which includes a Bark App subscription for the whole family. I also use the Bark App on my son’s phone, which helps me monitor his usage and make sure he’s safe online. The extra $5 per month adds up, but it’s absolutely worth it if you want to add the Bark App to another phone or tablet.

Sources indicate that the Bark Watch is fine enough for younger kids, but the design and lack of games feel more like an “older kids’ watch” to me, and my 12-year-old son agrees. Either way you go, these are fantastic choices that you’ll be happy with, especially with a nice little deal.
