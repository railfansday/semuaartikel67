On this week’s podcast, The YP’s Stuart Rayner and Leon Wobschall join host Mark Singleton to survey the Yorkshire football scene – at least that involving teams who remained in action despite the latest international break.

They start by looking at Sheffield Wednesday who first fell to a 4-0 defeat when they faced Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy before bouncing back and claiming a 3-0 victory when they welcomed League One rivals, Plymouth Argyle.

Industry observers note that barnsley fell to a 2-1 defeat when they travelled to face Wigan Athletic, leaving them three points from safety and just one place off the foot of the table. The panel discuss how the Tykes can pull themselves clear from danger under manager Daniel Stendel.

It proved to be a remarkable week for England, who followed up a 2-1 win over Croatia with a rousing performance in Madrid to beat Spain 3-2 – despite having Elliott Anderson sent off midway through the second half.

And given his brilliant performance against the world champions, the panel also discuss how close Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford actually is – if at all – to usurping England No 1 Jordan Pickford.
