If you're looking for a discounted iPhone over the Black Friday season, you don't have a whole lot of options when it comes to retailers. Apple has never been one to offer direct discounts, and retailers like Amazon and Best Buy tend to avoid slashing the price of new Apple phones. Thankfully, the major carriers are willing to cut iPhone prices, though there are always strings attached.

What I mean is that the likes of AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon are willing to offer discounted (or sometimes free) iPhones to customers — but only if they meet certain criteria. I'm talking about signing up for a specific phone plan, trading in a recently-released older device, being a brand fresh customer or some combination of them all. That's especially true for newer models, like iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo, but it still applies to older iPhones, too.

If you're looking to get an iPhone for cheap, and are willing to accept those caveats, here are the best deals the major carriers have on iPhones ahead of Black Friday.

The waiting list for iPhone Duo is still pretty long, and some carriers won't be able to dispatch your foldable until early next year. But that doesn't mean you can't save some money on the most versatile iPhone Apple has released so far.

According to recent reports, save $1200 on the cost of the iPhone Duo at AT&T. All you need to do is trade in an eligible smartphone and activate an AT&T premium 2.0 Plan or higher.

Industry observers note that verizon won't discount the iPhone Duo, but if you buy one with a Simplicity strategy you'll be able to claim a free Apple Watch SE 3 and AirPods 5. The Apple Watch comes with the phone, and the AirPods will arrive after you set up the watch and take out a service plan.

According to recent reports, the newest iPhones that are readily available are pretty expensive devices, and that means the savings on offer are pretty slim. But the carriers still have various deals available that could net you one of these two phones absolutely free — or close enough that it might as well be. That way you get to enjoy the high-performance power of the A20 Pro, the variable aperture camera and all those other upgrades.

The iPhone Air did a lot of things right, especially when it comes to durability, but it has yet to get a successor. So if you want to pick up the thinnest and lightest iPhone available make sure to check out this carrier deal.

Recent findings suggest that if you'd rather have a different kind of iPhone 17, the carriers are still offering a bunch of different older phones as well. The iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max have been officially discontinued, but that doesn't mean they've all vanished from circulation. Likewise, with no iPhone 18e, the lower-cost iPhone 17e is easy enough to get your hands on.
