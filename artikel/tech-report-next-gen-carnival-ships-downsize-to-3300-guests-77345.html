Fincantieri has unveiled more details about the two new ships it will build for Carnival Cruise Line.

Following yesterday’s announcement of a deal for the construction of upcoming vessels between the two companies, Fincantieri has now shared that the ships, scheduled for delivery in 2033 and 2035, will be built at its Marghera shipyard.

Fincantieri noted in a press release that the value of the contract is deemed “very notable,” a designation it uses for agreements whose value exceeds €2 billion.

The ships will be 130,000 tons and have around 1,650 guest cabins each, the shipbuilder said.

The vessels will be dual-fuel powered, with LNG as their primary fuel.

Additionally, the new design has been developed as a multi-brand platform for Carnival Corporation with a view to its potential future implementation across several of its cruise lines.

It has been revealed that “With 12 new ships now on order for delivery through 2039 across Carnival Cruise Line, Princess and AIDA, we have a strong pipeline that supports our measured approach to growth. The variety of ship sizes and features gives us flexibility to expand the choices and destinations we offer guests,” said Josh Weinstein, CEO of Carnival Corporation.

According to recent reports, “At the same time, each new generation of ships brings advances in technology and efficiency that help us further reduce emissions and our overall environmental footprint. Fincantieri has been a terrific partner throughout this journey, and even after 76 ships and 30 years, there’s still something special about seeing the next one come to life.”.

Pierroberto Folgiero, CEO and managing director of Fincantieri, noted: “Our relationship with Carnival Corporation continues to evolve through a shared ambition to push the boundaries of ship design, technological innovation and sustainability. These vessels embody a newly introduced paradigm of shipbuilding, where product innovation goes hand in hand with the transformation of our industrial system.”.

This follows the order for three latest Ace-class ships the duo confirmed in July, the first of which, the Carnival Destiny, is already being built at the Monfalcone shipyard.

As previously reported, the first Ace-class vessel will carry the name previously used by the 1996-built Destiny.
