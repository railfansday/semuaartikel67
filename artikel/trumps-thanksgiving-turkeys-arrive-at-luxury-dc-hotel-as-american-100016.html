‘Fox & Friends Weekend’ dives into the historical tradition of pardoning turkeys before Thanksgiving.

Two feathered VIPs have checked into one of Washington's most luxurious hotels, complete with plush beds, a red-carpet welcome and a crowd of eager photographers, ahead of President Donald Trump's annual Thanksgiving turkey pardon at the White House.

The presidential hopefuls arrived Sunday at the historic Willard InterContinental Washington, D.C., where they were photographed strutting across a red carpet, posing for pictures and standing atop the hotel's beds before their significant day on the South Lawn.

THE PENNSYLVANIA FARM BEHIND A WHITE HOUSE THANKSGIVING TURKEY TRADITION.

Two Thanksgiving turkeys make themselves at home on separate beds at Washington's luxurious Willard InterContinental hotel ahead of President Donald Trump's annual White House turkey pardon on Nov. 24, 2026. (Courtesy of USDA via X).

According to recent reports, the U.S. Department of Agriculture celebrated their arrival on X, announcing a "SPECIAL DELIVERY TO WASHINGTON, D.C.!" and describing the birds as "two VERY special guests" receiving the presidential treatment.

Industry observers note that photographs shared by the USDA, credited to the White House and National Turkey Federation, showed the plump white birds making their grand entrance before an audience of hotel guests and photographers. One turkey was even pictured riding through the hotel's ornate hallways inside a travel crate perched on a brass luggage cart.

The pair appeared equally comfortable with the accommodations, with one bird spreading its feathers atop a neatly made bed while its companion stood nearby.

But before the turkeys meet the commander in chief, Americans have been invited to settle a pressing matter of national importance: What should they be called?

SOCIAL MEDIA CUSTOMERS ARE EATING HUGE AMOUNTS OF CARROTS AND SHRIMP FOR A NATURAL TAN.

According to recent reports, two Thanksgiving turkeys strut down the red carpet at the historic Willard InterContinental hotel in Washington, D.C., on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, ahead of President Donald Trump's annual White House turkey pardon. (Courtesy of USDA via X).

The White House debuted an online contest offering three possible combinations: "Commander & Chief," "Freedom & Feathers" and "Honey & Ham.".

Voting on the names closed Sunday at 5 p.m. ET, with the winning pair expected to be announced before a second round of voting opens Monday at 8 a.m. That final vote will determine which turkey receives the official ceremonial pardon.

The birds, raised by American turkey farmers Scott and Becky Rightsell at Deans Gap Farm in West Virginia, have already received their own playful presidential biographies.

Sources indicate that both hatched July 15, but their tastes apparently differ. According to the White House, one enjoys Golden Delicious apples and scenic country drives, while the other prefers Dr Pepper and dreams of vacationing in Aggieland.

The pair was selected from a larger flock based on appearance and temperament, according to West Virginia Gov. Patrick Morrisey's office. Their preparations included getting accustomed to crowds, camera lights and noise.

And for Americans who cannot get enough turkey-related excitement, the White House has unveiled "Gobble Getaway," an online game challenging players to help a turkey dodge a chef armed with a net and roasting pan while making its way across the South Lawn to the pardon podium.

The annual Thanksgiving tradition dates back to 1947, when the National Turkey Federation first presented a holiday bird to President Harry Truman.

FOX NEWS MEDIA KICKS OFF HOLIDAY SEASON WITH SEVENTH ANNUAL ALL-AMERICAN CHRISTMAS TREE LIGHTING.

One of President Donald Trump's Thanksgiving turkeys gets a ride on a hotel luggage cart at the Willard InterContinental in Washington, D.C., ahead of Tuesday's presidential turkey pardon. (Courtesy of USDA via X).

President John F. Kennedy spared a gift turkey in 1963, while President George H.W. Bush established the formal presidential turkey pardon in 1989.

In a recent development, trump and first lady Melania Trump are scheduled to participate in this year's ceremony on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 2 p.m. ET on the White House South Lawn.

Although the public gets to vote on which turkey receives the ceremonial pardon, both birds are traditionally spared the Thanksgiving dinner table.
