The 20 Best Colognes to Give as Holiday Gifts

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, fragrance makes a fantastic holiday gift. It’s something they can wear every day, a good bottle lasts for ages, and it feels undeniably luxurious. The only catch? Dropping serious cash on a scent they don’t actually like would, frankly, suck. Luckily, we’re pretty obsessed with fragrance here at Esquire. We’ve smelled, tested, and worn more colognes than we can count—and we know which ones tend to be crowd-pleasers.

Recent findings suggest that it helps to know what your giftee is into. Do they want something light and fresh they can wear year-round? Something dark and smoky for winter? Or are they newly introduced to cologne, meaning a beloved classic is the safest bet? (And we know the fan favorites by heart—as well as all the best new fragrances from the past year.) Whatever their taste (or level of fragrance expertise), we put our heads—and noses—together to find the best colognes to gift everyone on your list.

A little clichéd but very much tried and tested. It’s unabashedly smoky and resinous, which makes for a captivating cloud of mystery. —Alfonso Fernández Navas, senior style and market editor.

It’s a classic for a reason. Aromatic and bold. I like to wear it for the important kind of evenings when I need an extra bit of refinement. —Mark Burger, social-media editor.

Industry observers note that fulton & Roark makes so many delicious fragrances, but Ramble is one of my favorites. It’s inspired by the famous Central Park enclave of the same name, so it’s perfect for any NYC native. The notes of forest air, lavender, and lemon rind create a fresh, woody scent that will transport you to the wilderness. It’s sophisticated and lush, and it works for literally everyone. —Bellamy Richardson, associate commerce editor.

Sources indicate that snif’s newly introduced Double Take cologne is one of our new favorite scents—and at $74, it’s a much more affordable option than some of the more luxury picks on this list. With juicy apricot and fresh orange blossom at the top, creamy sesame milk at the heart, and a classic woody base, it’s beloved by everyone in our office who’s tried it. —B.R.

I ended up going with Baie 19, which brings an earthy and refreshing feeling. It paired well with my body smell, which is a huge factor. Le Labo also offers a huge discount with its refill program; you just have to bring your bottle back. —Mike Kim, digital design director.

Sources indicate that byredo Mojave Ghost smells effortless, and even my friends who don’t usually like scents always compliment it. The bottle design also makes it perfect to put on display—it looks so chic sitting on a desk or counter! —Emma Saxon, fashion assistant.

Ranger Station is a little perfumer out of Nashville, and as such it’s got a lot of country-music references in the lineup. This one, done with the Waylon Jennings estate, embodies the man himself: gruff, masculine, old-fashioned.

It’s a curveball as a gift because it’s heavy on musk, leather, smoke, and spice. It smells like the Stetson Original my grandpa used. (It smells more like it than the modern Stetson Original does, funnily enough.) But that’s why I love the idea. This type of scent has been lost to time, so these days it’s really, really unique. —L.G.

Sources indicate that if you know someone who can’t live without coffee, they’ll probably love a coffee-scented cologne. This one from Maison Margiela’s Replica line is one of our favorites thanks to its nice blend of sweet and spicy notes. It also strikes a perfect balance of light and fresh (from top notes of lemon, lavender, and spearmint) and rich gourmand (from notes of warm coffee, milk mousse, and sweet vanilla), so it’s not too heavy in either direction. —B.R.

Pretty much any Dolce & Gabbana cologne is a winner in our books, but The One for Men collection is a good bet for any guy. The eau de parfum is a woody, ambery fragrance centered around orange, sage, and tobacco, giving it a sophisticated overall vibe that works well at the office or on a date. —B.R.

I gravitate toward gifting warm, cozy scents and this one feels perfectly timed for the season. With notes of almond, rose, and vanilla, this powdery gourmand feels soft and intimate. A touch of honey adds some nostalgic sweetness. —Andrea Ríos, fashion assistant.

Iris Germanica and vanilla give it a floral sweetness that takes the edge of the manly musk typical of Legend. Plus, it’s hard to go wrong with anything served in a flask. —Bryn Gelbart, associate commerce editor.

In the colder months, the warm, earthy heart stands out. It’s soft without being too femme, sturdy without the usual smoke and whiskey smells. I think the difficulty with this fragrance would be finding someone who it doesn’t jive with. —L.G.

Industry observers note that iris Silver Mist by Serge Lutens was first introduced in 1994, and there’s a reason this unisex scent remains so coveted. This fragrance blends the scent of moist, green earth with the light, powdery, and slightly metallic qualities of iris. Let this be your introduction to niche perfumery. —E.S.

It has been revealed that if you know anyone who likes warm scents for the colder months, this newly introduced release might just be perfect. The notes of cognac and antique wood make me feel as though I’m sitting in an old home next to a roaring fire with a drink in hand. But it’s not overwhelming or cloying—just inviting. —Jonathan Evans, style director.

I don’t wear scents on a daily basis, but whenever I spritz one on for a special occasion, it’s always Diptyque. Lazulio is my latest go-to, a more natural scent that is a perfect blend of fresh and classic in a single bottle.

A gift from the brand always stands out thanks to its beautiful packaging. No matter what, the recipient will feel the luxury. Plus, I can bet they don’t have this scent yet. But if you’re not exactly sure what they like, you can play it safe with this discovery set of five scents. —Krista Jones, senior director, commerce and partnerships.
