Industry observers note that parking is one of the most overlooked parts of a ski day, but it matters more than most skiers realize. Reserving or paying in advance for a spot is increasingly common, and so are lot closures and long walks to the lifts. Add little ones who need help carrying gear, and you’re lucky to make it to the lifts at all. Thankfully, not all resort lots are created equal when it comes to easy parking options.
