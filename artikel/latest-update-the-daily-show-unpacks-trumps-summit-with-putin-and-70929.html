Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping staged a historic trilateral meeting around an enormous table on Thursday. During the APEC summit in Shenzhen, the leaders of the U.S., Russia, and China spoke for 90 minutes before issuing a "joint declaration of peace".

There's a lot to unpack about the meeting, so The Daily Show's Michael Kosta examined the major moments in the video above, starting with Trump's overtly friendly greeting with Putin.

Recent findings suggest that "Wow, that was chummy," noted Kosta. "Putin's an evil dictator who we're in two proxy wars with — can you maybe not greet him like you pledged Sigma Chi together? Holy shit! Is that V-Dog? Get over here, you son of a bitch! You still throwing people out of windows? Just kidding, but he does do that, though.".

The Daily Show host then took a look at one of the major topics Trump brought to the table: how global superpowers like the U.S., Russia, and China can wriggle out of oversight by the United Nations and the International Criminal Court.

"It's never good when the American president proposes an idea and it makes Vladimir Putin smile like he just stole Christmas," remarked Kosta.
