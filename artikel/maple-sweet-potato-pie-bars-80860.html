Maple Sweet Potato Pie Bars These maple sweet potato pie bars feature a buttery Biscoff crumb crust and a smooth maple-flavored sweet potato filling. Top with a generous layer of cinnamon-spiced, lightly sweetened maple whipped cream. They're a wonderful make-ahead dessert for Thanksgiving, fall gatherings, or anytime you're craving something a little different from traditional pumpkin pie!
