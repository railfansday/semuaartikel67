JetBlue TrueBlue program: Earn and redeem points, transfer partners and more

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (162 words, 📖 Standard)
  • JetBlue's TrueBlue loyalty program offers flexible points earning and redemption, with no blackout dates for JetBlue-operated flights.
  • Points can be earned through flights, partner activity, shopping portals, and transferable rewards, and do not expire.
  • The program features Points Pooling, allowing groups to combine balances, and a tiered elite status system with benefits that increase at higher levels.
  • TrueBlue's expanding partner network, including a new partnership with United, enhances both earning and redemption options.
  • JetBlue uses a revenue-based model, so points required for flights typically reflect cash prices, and partner award availability may be limited.
  • Points Pooling requires all members to contribute their full earned points while participating, and only trusted individuals should be included.
  • Dynamic pricing means the value of points can vary, with TPG’s November 2026 valuations placing them at 1.35 cents each.
