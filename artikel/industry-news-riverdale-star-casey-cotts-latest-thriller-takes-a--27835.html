How far would you go to fit in? Would you play a game to the death if it meant winning over your boyfriend’s friends? That’s the question at the heart of Games We Play, a new psychological thriller starring Riverdale’s Casey Cott and FBI’s Margot Pitts. Pitts, who makes her feature debut with the film, co-wrote the script with Molly Scharlin Ben-Hamoo, while Rachael Dahl makes her feature directorial debut. Dylan McTee (Wrong Turn) and Adam Faison (Hellraiser) round out the cast, and Collider is excited to exclusively debut the film’s brand-new trailer.

According to recent reports, “Tapping into the increasingly resonant themes of social pressure, groupthink and the universal desire to belong, Games We Play follows Imogen (Pitts), who joins her boyfriend Jack (Cott) and his two childhood best friends, Gage (McTee) and Freddie (Faison), for a weekend getaway. When she finds herself the unmistakable outsider, Imogen becomes desperate to win their approval. But as she is forced to participate in a tournament of high-stakes games, the experience takes a sinister turn, pushing her to compromise her identity and instincts while questioning both her sanity and her safety. What begins as a pursuit of acceptance spirals into a chilling psychological game where the stakes are far more dangerous than fitting in.”.

Vertical, which holds North American rights, will release Games We Play in select theaters and on VOD on December 4. Blue Fox Entertainment has acquired the film’s international rights and is introducing it to buyers at the American Film Market.

Recent findings suggest that the trailer finds Imogen playing along with Jack’s friends, joining a round of Sardines and surrendering her phone for their tournament in an effort to become part of the group. Things soon take a darker turn as Gage’s interest in Imogen becomes a little too intense, and rather than questioning his friends’ behavior, Jack starts questioning her. The result looks twisted and genuinely unsettling, turning the kinds of games that felt harmless as kids into something far more menacing.

According to recent reports, games We Play appears to borrow the same scare tactic that made Ready or Not work so well: take a game everyone knows how to play and raise the stakes until it becomes a fight for survival. Because the rules are so familiar, it’s easy for viewers to put themselves in Imogen’s shoes. She goes along with everything for her boyfriend’s sake, and who hasn’t wanted to win over a fresh group of friends? It’s a relatable impulse, just not one worth following once the games start turning deadly.

“Games We Play is a timely examination of the universal struggle for social survival, where the desire to belong can become just as dangerous as the fight to stay alive,” said Scharlin Ben-Hamoo, who also serves as an executive producer alongside Pitts.

“Rachael Dahl has crafted a compelling psychological thriller that taps into the pressures we all recognize, while taking them to a terrifying extreme. We are excited to partner with Vertical Entertainment and Blue Fox Entertainment to bring this story to audiences around the world.”.

It has been revealed that produced by Taryn Gates and Raza Rizvi (Lemonade Blessing), Games We Play arrives in select theaters and on VOD on December 4. Check out the exclusive trailer above.
