Woody Allen is spending time with his family.

The Annie Hall director and his wife, Soon-Yi Previn, caught up with their eldest daughter, Bechet Allen, for dinner in Spain.

The 27-year-old shared a rare glimpse inside life with her family on Instagram Nov. 16, writing, "One can consume a lot in a week.".

At the table, the mother and daughter smiled for the camera while wearing subtly matching black and white striped tops. Meanwhile, the Manhattan actor was occupied with the spread of crab legs laid out for their decadent meal.

Along with enjoying the seafood feast, Bechet also included footage of Woody, 90, and Soon-Yi, 56—also parents to Manzie Tio Allen, 26—walking hand-in-hand through a park among her many candid snaps of the streets of Madrid.

And the couple's private life being put on display was a rare occasion, as they have maintained a marriage outside of the public eye since getting together in 1990, coinciding with Woody's controversial split from Mia Farrow, who adopted Soon-Yi with composer André Previn more than a decade prior.

As such, in 2025, the Vicky Cristina Barcelona writer spoke out about how the relationship surprised him.

"If anyone had told me years ago that I would be married to a girl much younger than me with no background in New York or none of the similar interests growing up and that she’d be a Korean orphan," Woody remarked to The Wall Street Journal in 2025, "I would have said, 'No chance.'".

In fact, the author, who also dated Diane Keaton, noted that he initially expected he'd end up settling down with a Hollywood star.

According to recent reports, "'I’m going to be married to a Upcoming York actress,'" he recounted thinking before he married Soon-Yi in 1997. "But it didn’t work out that way.”.
