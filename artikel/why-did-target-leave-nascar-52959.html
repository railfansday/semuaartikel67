📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, target ended its long run in NASCAR at the close of the 2017 season, walking away from a 16-year sponsorship with Chip Ganassi Racing. The move, declared in July 2017, left rising star Kyle Larson without a confirmed sponsor for most of the next year. The reason was simple. Target wanted to spend its sports marketing money on soccer instead. The company said the sport gave it newly introduced ways to reach shoppers at every level, from professional teams to youth leagues. It signed deals with Major League Soccer, Minnesota United FC, US Youth Soccer and the US Soccer Foundation. Target first entered motorsports in 1990 with a $4 million deal and joined NASCAR in 2002. It ran a clever system where suppliers paid $1.5 million to $5 million to put their brands on its cars, which helped cover the cost. The NASCAR exit came a year after Target dropped its IndyCar deal with Ganassi, ending a 28-year partnership.
