Industry observers note that warning! Spoilers ahead for the Season 10 fall finale of 9-1-1, “Et Tu?,” which is available to stream with a Hulu subscription.

According to recent reports, 9-1-1 has officially wrapped up the first half of its 10th season on the 2026 TV schedule, and it was anything but calm. Thursday’s fall finale included some intense calls, and even more intense scenes between Buck and Eddie as their latest fallout continues. The best friends have not really been on the same page for a while now, and I’ve been worried about them. Luckily, the executive producer is explaining where things stand with them following the midseason finale.

It has been revealed that buck’s massive decision in the Season 9 finale to foster his biological son following the death of his parents has led to some difficulties at the start of Season 10. The toddler, Theo, is a bit rambunctious and doesn’t take well to authority. This has put a strain on Buck’s relationship with Eddie, who knows what it’s like to be a single father. It doesn’t help that after Buck decided to give Theo up, Eddie became his foster dad through emergency placement. This has put even more pressure on them, and EP and showrunner Tim Minear told TV Insider why they went in this direction:.

Sources indicate that I think conflict is drama, obviously, but it felt… To me, it was sort of an interesting way to have Eddie come in and take Buck’s story away from him in a sense. They both have stories, right? Obviously, Theo is Buck’s biological kid. Buck had every good intention of fostering and growing into that role, which he still has an opportunity to do. But also, I just believe that Eddie would not let that kid go into a group home. And I think there was some truth in what Buck said about Eddie’s guilt about being ambivalent about his own son in the first five years of his life.

It has been revealed that buck had donated sperm to his friend and his wife, who were having trouble conceiving a few seasons ago, and after helping her deliver, it didn’t really come up again...until last season. Then, after the couple died in a car crash, Buck felt responsible for the kid and took him in. There was some concern for Buck going into Season 10, and it was obvious this wasn’t going to be easy, especially after seeing Theo’s chaotic energy. So he ultimately believed that Theo would be better with someone else.

Sources indicate that however, Minear is right in believing that Eddie, who was the only one who knew what Buck was doing, wouldn’t let Theo go to a group home. Unfortunately, this creates more conflict between them, but it’s what makes the situation more realistic:.

So again, a lot of the stories here this season — look, we’re in Season 10, and a lot of the stories this season are an accumulation of canon that we’ve built up over the past decade with these characters. And that always makes for a richer story, it seems to me. But I actually think Buck and Eddie, conflict is natural, particularly between people who are really close. Nobody can piss you off as much as someone who you’re really close to, and I just think that’s real.

According to recent reports, despite the growing tension in the midseason finale, Buck and Eddie had somewhat made up towards the end of the episode. While Buck wasn’t quite ready to see Theo, he understood why Eddie did what he did. It’s hard to tell where things will go for them in the upcoming year, but with Theo staying with Eddie and Buck coming to terms with everything, it should be interesting. On that note, Minear shares that both of them will have some growing to do for multiple reasons:.

Industry observers note that yeah, I think they’re at an interstitial stage where Buck sees that it is really best for Theo right now to be with Eddie. Maybe neither one of them handled it perfectly, but if they handled it perfectly, then you’d have a five-episode season as opposed to 18. But I think they’re in a good place in their friendship. I think that Theo becomes a spoiler a little bit, and until Buck feels like he can walk into that house and face that kid, there’s kind of a natural barrier there between whatever kind of closeness that he can enjoy with Eddie off the job.

Industry observers note that I have been worried about Buck and Eddie’s relationship since the beginning of the season, when it was clear that Buck fostering Theo would bring some problems. And it didn’t get any easier when Eddie became the foster parent. I’m definitely glad that they’ve crossed a barrier, and they both realize how they acted in this situation might not have been the best. However, I am looking forward to seeing what happens now that they have an understanding with each other and Theo.
