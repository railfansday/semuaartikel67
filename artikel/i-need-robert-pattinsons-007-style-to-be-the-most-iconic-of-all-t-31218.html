I Need Robert Pattinson’s 007 Style to Be the Most Iconic of All the Bonds

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There is no one more booked than Robert Pattinson, so it makes perfect sense he’s set to be the next James Bond, too. And if there’s anything I’m sure of, this newly introduced iteration of the popular franchise has room to introduce some seriously cool fashion in the suiting department.

Playing 007 comes with some pretty big fashion expectations. We're talking impeccably tailored suits, perfectly cut tuxedos, and very debonair action shots. Now, I’ve only recently ventured into the world of James Bond. My father sat me down a few weeks back and showed me his favorite ones from when he was a kid. But now that Pattinson is entering the world, I’m tuned in. As a diehard Twihard, worlds are colliding with this selection, and that means there’s room for a lot of modern fashion with everyone’s favorite “Gen Z” star at the helm. While Daniel Craig gave us a masterclass in modern Bond tailoring, and Pierce Brosnan brought a little more old-school polish, I need Robert Pattinson to bring a sort of off-kilter edge.

If I were in charge of dressing the next Bond, here's where I think they should start, based on Robert’s previous red carpet suiting.

This striped white suit is surprisingly one of my favorites on him. Now, the fit—while uber beloved by a lot of young people—might get in the way of all his super key action movements. But a loose-fit, light-colored suit really does stand out on the 40-year-old actor. Especially when he has a little tan on him.

Robert has a long-standing partnership with Dior. He is the face of Dior Homme and a notable brand ambassador, and frequently wears custom and seasonal Dior tailored suits on the red carpet and at film premieres. So, we could definitely see some Dior in his upcoming Bond film, given their work together. This shade of suit specifically really brings out the blue in his eyes, so I personally think a little swooning in these action-packed moments will be worthwhile.

Fit-wise, I think this is the style they need to lean into when building out his outfits for the movie. The sharp tailoring and slim fit is not only the definition (to me) of a sharp, modern James Bond, but it also looks great on his frame.

It has been revealed that there’s a lot of room to play with menswear, but I also believe there’s room to expand upon the past. I’d also love to see a world where a few newer namesake brands get into the conversation, like Connor Ives and Willy Chavarria. Both brands have a unique POV and make suits fashion people are always talking about. RPats isn’t a typical James Bond. He’s a bit mysterious, but also incredibly online in some regards, so playing up his eccentric personality with some cheeky and edgy suit options would be my dream come true.
