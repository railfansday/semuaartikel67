A move to Italy could be on the cards for Diogo Dalot, who is a target for both AC Milan and Juventus, reports suggest.

Dalot is not an unfamiliar name to either club; he’s been linked regularly in the past years and it’s not an impossible move. Since leaving San Siro, his time with Manchester United has been up and down and he could now head back to Milan.

Considering his popularity at Old Trafford, it’d hardly be a move opposed by fans, but could this be a case of everyone needing a newly introduced start?

According to Portuguese outlet A Bola, Dalot would have plenty of interest if he left Manchester next year. Indeed, both Juventus and the Rossoneri are interested in a move for the full-back whose deal expires in 2028.

Industry observers note that the 27-year-old’s fate rests in the hands of fresh coach Mauricio Pochettino – much like Bruno Fernandes – and a renewal is not completely off the table. With more than 250 games played for the Red Devils, he provides welcome experience.

If a decision is made to part ways, though, he could start looking at a departure quickly as his value will only go down. This is where either of the Serie A clubs could step in, with both paying close attention to him.
