Joe Kasper, who served as chief of staff to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth during the early days of the Trump administration last year, has now said that Hegseth should resign.

In a recent development, "Pete's ability to be effective has been constrained and very soon he’ll have lost any ability to be effective," Kasper stated, according to Politico. "For that reason and others, it’s time for Pete to call it quits.".

"Pete's been belligerent in his stewardship of an office that will outlive him or any memory of his service," Kasper asserted, the report states.

The outlet reported that Kasper suggested that Hegseth would not be able to work with the nation's legislature or obtain appropriate funds for department priorities.

Industry observers note that "He’s not just burned bridges up on the Hill," Kasper reportedly noted. "He’s napalmed them. … He’s going to be suffocated, and it’s not like it’s going to be a slow suffocation. It’s going to be a quick asphyxiation.".

According to Politico, Kasper noted that he understood within a week of joining the Pentagon that Hegseth's focus was "all about Pete rather than the institution and the men and women who support it.".

Industry observers note that "There’s quite an emphasis on the showmanship," Kasper reportedly stated. "The virtues of humility and basically service before self and those things have disappeared. I didn’t see those at the start, and see them ever less so now.".

The Pentagon chief "overindulged in ego, in attitude, and in entitlement," Kasper stated, according to the report.

According to recent reports, he noted that he would rate Hegseth near the bottom 10% of defense secretaries in U.S. history, Politico reported. "I think history will be much crueler to him than I could be," he noted.

Industry observers note that A senior department official said in a statement obtained by Fox News Digital, "It’s no surprise that a disgruntled former staffer—someone who could barely survive ninety days in the building—is now peddling made up gossip to the media. Secretary Hegseth is laser focused on restoring lethality to the force and supporting our warfighters, while Mr. Kasper is more focused on rewriting history to cover for his own failed tenure.".
