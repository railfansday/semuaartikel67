According to recent reports, whatsApp just unveiled that it is rolling out scheduled messages, which it says is one of users’ most requested features. Here are the details.

From WhatsApp’s blog post announcing support for scheduled messages:.

You can write a message when you think of it and send it anywhere from 10 minutes to 14 days later, in both your 1:1 and group chats.

Sources indicate that to schedule a message, press and hold the send button and pick the date and time you want it to arrive. It will look like any other message to the person receiving it. You can see all future messages you have lined up, and delete them beforehand if you change your mind.

Even if your phone is off or out of battery, your scheduled message will still show up at the time you chose, and it remains end-to-end encrypted like all your personal messages, so no one else can read it, not even us.

WhatsApp notes this feature is “rolling out globally over the coming weeks.” So you might not see it right away, but even if you don’t, it should show up on your account soon.

Scheduled messages work not only for text, but also when sending media like photos and videos.

To get access to scheduled messages, make sure you’ve updated to the latest WhatsApp app update, which was just released today.

In other WhatsApp news, earlier today we reported that stickers with audio are coming soon, including user-created options.
