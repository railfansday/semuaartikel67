Jamie Redknapp has been talking about Newcastle United.

The Sky Sports pundit reflecting on Saturday at St James’ Park.

An ultimate game of two halves.

Newcastle United unrecognisable after the break, when compared to the opening 45 minutes.

Interesting to get a neutral perspective on what occurred, what changed.

In a recent development, jamie Redknapp declaring on Sky Sports: “I think first half for any player in that Newcastle team that would have been a lonely place, because you were getting played off the park, the spaces seemed so substantial and your home crowd have been booing you. You’re like, ‘this is going to be a really tough [second] half’.”.

Jamie Redknapp: “Matthias Jaissle made three great changes. Elanga added pace, Livramento good energy down that right-hand side, and Ramsey was one of the best players on the pitch. Newcastle United, full respect from me, I thought they revealed a great deal of effort and they played against a very difficult side in Arsenal, but they got after them. It just highlights you what a bit of energy can do.”.

The home fans had been as out of form, lacking the necessary zip and energy, as their team had been before the break.

Recent findings suggest that jamie Redknapp: “And once that crowd get up and you get that first goal, anything was possible. And they will look at that second half thinking, actually, we could have won this game. Really good performance from Newcastle. I would give Jaissle more credit because he’s taken a lot of stick. It’s been really hard for him, but he’s definitely got some money in the bank tonight. He will be proud of his team. The fans will be proud of their team. And they made it very difficult for Arsenal that second half.”.

I am not always his biggest fan but that is an excellent summary for me, of what we all witnessed on Saturday.

It has been revealed that jamie Redknapp also looking at the 2-2 draw from the Arsenal perspective: “You are getting so much time on the ball [in the first half]. Declan Rice was able to turn. Bruno Guimaraes had so much time in the first half and then all of a sudden Jacob Ramsey comes on and he is all over you. He is closing you down. Myles Lewis-Skelly is getting put under pressure and that was full credit to Newcastle. The fundamentals that you have to do to win a football match by winning second balls, by putting teams under pressure, by getting in people’s faces, Newcastle did well in the second half.”.
