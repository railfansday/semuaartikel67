Similar-sized clubs, the pair punched above their weight in the second tier in the first half of the 1980s and again in the early noughties, for instance.

Historically, the pair also have a right to view themselves as League One clubs rather than fourth-tier ones.

In a recent development, grimsby's direction of travel, under Rotherham-born Dave Artell, is upward again following some time in the National League. They are third in League Two.

The Mariners' course hasn't been plain sailing. Their time in the fifth tier provides a bit of a warning to the Millers in the present as they aim to 'bottom out' and start turning their fortunes around.

What the Mariners now have is stability and consistency under Artell. It's a place where Alex Bruce wants to get to.

Bruce said: "Dave's been with Grimsby now for two or three seasons. He's had a chance to build a team and squad.

"They've got some good players and they're sitting where they are in the league for a reason. I came up against them in the play-offs last year with Salford City, so I know quite a lot about them.

"It's easy for me to sit here and say consistency's the way forward and that's what brings success. When you have two or three years in charge of a club and can build your squad and work on things, you get the fruits of that. Dave has done a good job there.".

Second-from-bottom Millers are winless in seven league matches, but were buoyed by their recent FA Cup win at League One side Bromley.

Events and game management, as opposed to general performance levels, are what have hurt them of late at league level.

Bruce added: "I know I've taken a little bit of a stick for talking about sending-offs and mistakes and things like that. The positives are, there have been signs of good performances.

Sources indicate that "Let's not lose sight of the fact that when we've taken on teams, gone 2-0 up, we were all saying: 'That's more like it.' We've got to build on those moments and build on Bromley. I've got every confidence that my players can be more consistent.".
