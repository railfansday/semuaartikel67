Team: Customer Engineering and Success, CircleCI.

It has been revealed that support Engineering is part of Customer Engineering and Success at CircleCI. We give technical support to customers after they buy. Our team works around the clock across North America, Asia Pacific, and Europe.

Industry observers note that we build and manage AI agents that answer customer questions and solve technical problems. People step in when a problem needs deeper work. We also share what we learn with Product and Engineering to make CircleCI better.

Senior Support Engineers take on the hardest customer problems and incidents. You help set how our AI support agents handle those problems, and you step in when agents cannot. You also mentor teammates and help the team grow its skills.

Industry observers note that you use AI tools every day. You check all AI output, and you help others on the team do the same.
