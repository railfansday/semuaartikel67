Brighton U21s manager Shannon Ruth has emerged as a candidate to take the vacant manager's job at Blackpool, sources have told Football Insider.

The League One side are on the hunt for a new boss after sacking Ian Evatt last week, and have now set their sights on Ruth.

The 35-year-old is one of the most highly-regarded young coaches in English football, with his Brighton academy sides catching the eye in recent years.

Blackpool are right now considering several options for their next appointment, but Ruth has now emerged towards the top of their list.

His Brighton U21 side ended last season as Premier League 2's play-off winners, and also reached the quarter-finals of the EFL Trophy back in 2023-24.

The Seagulls have built one of the country's strongest up-and-coming academies, and Ruth has been a central part of that climb.

Ruth's reputation within academy coaching has seen him steadily climb the ladder at the Amex Stadium, and it has now also earned him recognition from elsewhere.

He initially joined the Seagulls as their U21s assistant coach back in 2018, but was promoted to the top job in the academy in 2022 and has held the role ever since.

Ruth previously worked as part of Chelsea's youth setup as a goalkeeping coach, and has also had Head of Goalkeeping roles at both Aldershot Town and Reading.

However, he could now be about to take the step into senior management for the first time.

Blackpool are clear admirers of Ruth's coaching credentials, and the way that he has been able to develop young players over the years.

He has emerged as one of the leading candidates to take the reins at Bloomfield Road, but is far from the only option at the moment.

Football Insider revealed on Wednesday that former Everton and Wolves defender Conor Coady is also emerging as a contender for the Blackpool job.

The 33-year-old joined the England U21 setup as an assistant coach back in September, and has since impressed FA officials with his work at St George's Park.

Coady's work with the Three Lions has caught Blackpool's eye, and he is now a genuine contender to take the job at the League One strugglers.

The former centre-back only confirmed his retirement from playing at the start of November, but could soopen be about to turn his hand to senior management.
