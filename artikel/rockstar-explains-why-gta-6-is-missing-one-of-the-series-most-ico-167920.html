Rockstar Explains Why GTA 6 Is Missing One of the Series' Most Iconic Weapons

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6 has taken us back to Vice City for the first time in two decades, but one of the weapons I most associate with the city hasn't returned. You can find chainsaws in Leonida, but you can't pick them up and use them like you could in GTA Vice City or San Andreas. Rockstar has now explained why.

As Dexerto reports, Rockstar felt that the violence associated with the weapon would be too much for GTA 6. It seems the studio is a little more selective about what it lets us get away with now that its games look this good.

It has been revealed that in an interview with VGC published on November 26, GTA 6 producer and Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson discussed some of the things that didn't make it into the game. Be warned that the interview contains story spoilers.

When asked about the absence of chainsaws, Nelson remarked, “I think it would have been too much for this game at this resolution and these characters.” He then added, “It’s not a resource thing.”.

Katanas, another staple of the PS2-era GTA games, are missing from the arsenal as well. It's a little odd to see a chainsaw lying around and not be able to pick it up, especially considering how interactive GTA 6 is compared to its predecessors.

I've always seen the chainsaw as more of a Hollywood and video game weapon than a practical one. In real life, using one against a person would hardly be as straightforward as the movies make it look. The saw could jam, and it would be an absolute mess. I can understand why Rockstar wouldn't want to depict that sort of gore in such detail, given how controversial the GTA series has been over the years.

Recent findings suggest that this is, after all, the same studio that gave us Manhunt. Manhunt 2 even ran into trouble with ratings boards before its release, and I don't think Rockstar is going back to the series anytime soon. It's one thing to make a horror game where the brutality is part of the premise, but quite another to let someone loose with a chainsaw in a modern recreation of Miami.

Recent findings suggest that for me, the chainsaw will always be associated with GTA Vice City. Colonel Cortez gives Tommy Vercetti one in Treacherous Swine, a fairly early mission in the game. It returned in San Andreas, both of the PSP GTA games, and Chinatown Wars. It was already absent from GTA 4 and GTA 5, though, so it's not as if every GTA game needs one.

Industry observers note that what I find more interesting about Nelson's answer is his reference to Jason and Lucia. There's a difference between what a protagonist would do in a cutscene and what you can get up to when you're not following the story. Part of the fun of the older GTA games was seeing how far you could stray from the character you were supposed to be playing.

It has been revealed that CJ, for instance, had plenty of reasons to look after Grove Street, but San Andreas never stopped you from abandoning your responsibilities and spending an afternoon flying a jetpack around the desert. Even the weapons you could carry in that game included some pretty ridiculous choices that had little to do with CJ's life as a gang member.

Sources indicate that I can live without the chainsaw in GTA 6, especially if the alternative is something closer to Manhunt than the cartoonish violence of Vice City. I'd just hate to see the personalities of the protagonists become a reason to leave out some of the sillier weapons and activities in future GTA games.
