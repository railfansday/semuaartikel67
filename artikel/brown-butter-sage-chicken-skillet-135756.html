Brown Butter Sage Chicken Skillet

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, boasting a brief ingredient list and a short cooking time, this easy chicken dish is one you’ll be making all season. Basic butter becomes the star here—it turns brown and nutty, then gets infused with sage and garlic for a savory sauce that’s basted over simply seasoned chicken breasts. A big squeeze of lemon, plus its zest, helps balance the richness. This skillet dinner is perfect for a speedy weeknight meal, but it’s also special enough for tech giant.

Industry observers note that the protein:You don’t have to limit yourself to chicken here. Feel free to replace the breasts with pork chops, shrimp, scallops, or skinless salmon or halibut fillets. Simply adjust the cooking time to properly cook the protein, but be sure to maintain a medium heat. While meat and fish are often cooked on medium-high heat for a nice char, it needs to be a bit lower to avoid bitter residue in the pan, since the butter will be browned immediately afterward.

The crunch:The sage leaves crisp while the butter browns, but there are also ways to add more texture. Stirring in a handful of slivered almonds toward the end of cooking adds to the nuttiness of the dish; pine nuts, pistachios, or roughly chopped walnuts would also work. If you have a container of them, feel free to sprinkle some fried onions on top tableside.

Storage and serving ideas:This is not a make-ahead situation; it should be eaten immediately after it’s cooked. Serve with a simple salad dressed in a light vinaigrette to balance the richness. To soak up the sauce, mashed potatoes—traditional or sweet—are ideal. Or, to add another nutty element, roast some Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, or butternut squash to eat alongside and round out the meal.
