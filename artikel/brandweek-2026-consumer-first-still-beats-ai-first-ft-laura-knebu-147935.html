AI can raise the ceiling on marketing, or it can expose every weak foundation underneath it.

Live from the Marketing Vanguard stage at Brandweek 2026, Jenny Rooney sits down with Laura Knebusch, senior vice president of CPG marketing and customer experience at Georgia-Pacific, to explore what modern brand building really requires when media, data, retail, and AI are all evolving at once.

Laura gets into why Georgia-Pacific spent 18 months consolidating data sources, and how that foundation now supports in-house capabilities and agentic AI.

She also discusses why consumer-first and AI-first marketing are not actually competing ideas.

This episode is part of a special vidcast series that was recorded live during Brandweek 2026 and presented in partnership with Tatari.

Laura Knebusch is Senior Vice President of CPG Marketing and Customer Experience at Georgia-Pacific, where her team supports consumer and B2B brands across insights, content, media, promotions, marketing technology and analytics.

She began her marketing career at Procter & Gamble after earning her MBA from Duke and later joined Georgia-Pacific, where she has spent 18 years across brand management, category leadership and marketing capability roles.

Today, her focus is on combining classic brand-building fundamentals with the data, technology and operating models needed for modern CPG marketing.

It has been revealed that private Label Is Waiting, So What About Your Brand? — Laura points out that consumers have been adjusting their behavior for years, not reacting to one sudden economic moment. In household essentials, private-label options are credible and easy to access. There’s a warning lying there for marketers: if the brand does not create a real reason to choose it, loyalty becomes much harder to defend.

According to recent reports, 100 Data Sources, One Source of Truth, Eighteen Months of Work — When Laura stepped into her current role, Georgia-Pacific needed to become more digitally capable. The first step was not another tool. It was fixing the data. Over 18 months, the team connected roughly 100 different sources, cleaned and harmonized them, and built one accessible system that now powers multiple in-house capabilities.

Sources indicate that consumer-First vs. AI-First Is the Wrong Debate — At Brandweek 2026, Laura describes an internal discussion that paired behavioral science with generative-engine optimization. At first, the advice sounded different – Humans need simplicity, distinctiveness and repetition, while AI needs clear, consistent information. But the conclusion was the same: strong fundamentals work for both.

Sources indicate that why Georgia-Pacific Merged Shopper Marketing and National Media — Retail media has changed how consumers discover and buy, so Georgia-Pacific changed how it organizes around that reality. Laura explains why shopper marketing and national media were brought closer together, helping the team better understand where partners create strategic value and where investment should stay tactical.

Jenny Rooney is Chief Brand and Community Officer, leading strategy for the overall ADWEEK brand as well as the ways in which we serve and support our audiences with high-value content, products, partnerships and experiences, notably through our community programs such as Marketing Vanguard.
