TUI River Cruises and Marella Cruises have unveiled Black Friday deals on ocean and river cruise holidays in 2027, with offers available to book a week before Black Friday, from November 20, 2026, until midnight on December 1, 2026.

Chris Hackney, CEO of Marella Cruises, stated: “Black Friday is the perfect time for customers to start planning their dream cruise, with fantastic savings across our all-inclusive ocean cruises.”.

“Customers can save £200 on selected Canary Islands and Mediterranean sailings or £300 on selected Caribbean and transatlantic repositioning sailings, with regional flying from airports across the UK. With departures running until April 2028, there’s never been a better time to book that next adventure at sea.”.

Katy Berzins, head of River Cruises at TUI River Cruises, added: “We’re thrilled to be giving upcoming and existing customers the chance to set sail on one of our river ships with savings of up to £300 per booking.”.

“This year, we’re also offering a free all-inclusive upgrade on selected European river sailings next summer, plus free cabin upgrades on selected Nile sailings. Our deals launch a week ahead of Black Friday and run until December 1, giving customers more time to find the river cruise that’s right for them.”.

Customers can set sail with Marella Cruises and save £200 on selected Canary Islands and Mediterranean sailings with code “BFSAIL200.”.

They can also save £300 on selected Caribbean and transatlantic repositioning sailings with code “BFSAIL300.” The offers apply to departures between January 1, 2027, and April 30, 2028.

TUI River Cruises stated that it is offering savings of up to £300 per booking on sailings departing between January 1 and December 31, 2027.

Using code BFRIVER, customers can save £200 on holidays costing £2,500 or more, or £300 on holidays costing £3,500 or more, on river cruises of six nights or longer.

River cruise fans can explore some of the world’s most famous waterways, including the Rhine and Danube in Europe and the Nile in Egypt.

For those looking to upgrade their river cruise, TUI River Cruises is also offering: a free all-inclusive upgrade on selected European river sailings departing between July 1 and August 31, 2027, and free cabin upgrades on selected River Nile sailings departing between January 1 and May 31, 2027.
