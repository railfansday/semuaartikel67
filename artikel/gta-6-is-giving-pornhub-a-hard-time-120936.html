Not even porn is immune to the GTA 6 effect, as it's just one of several forms of entertainment that has seen a decline in viewership since the massive open-world game debuted. It's not surprising, given the game's record-breaking start, with 32.5 million sales from preorders alone. On PS5, GTA 6 is the "biggest launch" in PlayStation history, and players spent an average of 17.4 hours playing in its first few days--which probably didn't leave much time for a quick browse of after-hours material. According to Mashable, US traffic on Pornhub--which I've been told is one of the largest pornography websites, by traffic--dipped by as much as 15.5% an hour after GTA 6 launched. Paradoxically, Pornhub traffic had actually been up by 16.5% compared to average days, likely indicating that people were bored and randy. Up to 15 million search queries were made on November 18 alone, with users searching for GTA 6, the GTA 6 strip club, and content depicting the co-protagonist Lucia specifically. The fan-favorite stalker--and possible serial killer--Mandy also appeared in search results, while Lucia's partner-in-crime Jason ranked low. Thursday Night Football also saw a massive dip last week, with the lowest viewership of the season so far for a TNF game. While Rockstar's fresh open-world adventure can't be blamed entirely for that dip, it certainly must have had an impact, given that the football game was held on GTA 6's launch day. That effect didn't extend to the box office, as The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opened to a massive $120 million in the US, almost triple that of the last film in the series, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.
