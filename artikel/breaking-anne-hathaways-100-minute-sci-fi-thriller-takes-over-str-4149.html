In a recent development, anne Hathaway has had a particularly busy year, with major projects including Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, the psychological thriller Verity, and the highly anticipated The Devil Wears Prada 2. She's also ventured into more unconventional territory with A24's Mother Mary, a thriller that takes a considerably stranger approach to the world of pop stardom.

The End of Oak Street made its HBO Max debut on Nov. 13 and wasted little time finding an audience. The movie climbed to No. 1 on the platform's U.S. movie chart by Sunday, Nov. 15, according to FlixPatrol's rankings.

Directed by David Robert Mitchell (It Follows, Under the Silver Lake), The End of Oak Street follows the Platt family, whose ordinary suburban life is turned upside down after a mysterious cosmic event transports their entire neighborhood to an unfamiliar location.

Industry observers note that suddenly separated from the world they know, the family discovers that their newly introduced surroundings are filled with dangerous and unusual creatures. With their survival at stake, the Platts must work together to navigate their strange new environment and find a way to make it through the ordeal.

According to recent reports, hathaway stars as Denise Platt alongside Ewan McGregor (Star Wars, Trainspotting), Maisy Stella (Nashville) and Christian Convery (Sweet Tooth). Despite its unusual premise, The End of Oak Street has earned a positive reception from critics and audiences. The film at the moment holds an 86% critics' score and 76% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.
