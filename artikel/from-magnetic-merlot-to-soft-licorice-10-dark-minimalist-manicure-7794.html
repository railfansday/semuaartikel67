📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that while soap manicures, subtle petal nails, and longevity techniques once dominated the summer months, a darker color palette is swiftly taking over now that the clocks have gone back and evenings are early. Yet if you're hesitant to venture into "too bold" territory or fear a pop of color on your fingertips, a dark manicure doesn't have to feel overly attention-grabbing.

In fact, as professional nail technician Samantha Rudge shares, dark manicures with a more minimalist finish are proving to be winter's most popular manicure trend. "For this season, I think we'll be seeing darker classic manicures with minimalist touches," she shares, adding that she has already seen an uptick in shade requests for Bio Sculpture's rich, chocolate-toned Decadent Cocoa and the ever-popular Garnet Noir, a moody red-black hue. "Nail designs like chrome, cat eye, micro French tips, and tortoiseshell will also dominate this season," she adds.

Industry observers note that "Short nails are also very popular right now, no matter the nail art or shades," she adds. Regardless of shape or color, Rudge stresses the importance of prioritizing nail health at this time of year. To protect nail longevity and prevent chipping, Rudge recommends assessing your natural nail condition, choosing the right base (BIAB or gel), and using a nourishing cuticle oil to repair and strengthen brittle nails.

Ahead, discover 10 upcoming dark manicures for those who love a more minimalist finish, from “Garnet Noir” nails to subtle lines.

Industry observers note that taking over the SS27 runways this season, purple is finally having a moment in the spotlight. When it comes to nail colors, look for richer hues with a jewel-tone finish to keep the manicure elevated and modern. Rudge recommends Bio Sculpture's Erica shade.

All shades of wine are a staple as the festive season approaches, but why not add a chrome effect for extra sparkle?

Coffee tones are becoming one of the most popular color stories in nail salons this winter. These teeny-tiny dots make for a unique switch-up and can be created using a micro dotting tool.

If you're taking a break from acrylics or longer lengths, a super-short licorice manicure is the way to do it. Add a glossy top coat and keep the nails short and square.
