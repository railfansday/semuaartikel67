Recent findings suggest that the iPhone Air impressed us with its super-svelte form factor. Battery life isn’t stellar, and the main camera is a lone 48-MP shooter, but it’s so thin. Discounts on iPhones can be hard to find, so if you’ve had your eye on the iPhone Air, this deal is worth grabbing.
