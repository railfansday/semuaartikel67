With BTC dipping below $60K, the downward slope carries onto the most fragile section of the altcoin compartment.

Meme coin dominance has plummeted to historically low levels following the broader crypto market correction. Back in November 2024, the meme coin compartment had concentrated around 11% of all-altcoin market liquidity.

Right now, the picture is a lot different: the 3% share in late November testifies to long-term investors leaving the meme coin sphere. Some major crypto platforms have seen a massive imbalance between buying and selling positions.

For instance, Binance’s customers started heavily selling off meme coin-related assets since July, exceeding $1 billion during the month. Having that in mind, the slimming meme coin market capitalization at $19,631,290,906 does make sense.

On the other hand, meme coins have been the key driver behind the 2024 rally, even edging the most-promising niches of Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Real-World Asset (RWA) based cryptos. Most low-to-mid meme coins have been wiped out since.

According to recent reports, the last ones left are the major dogs or timeless symbols of internet culture. Dogecoin (DOGE), Shiba Inu coin (SHIB) & PEPE Token (PEPE) have been equipped with stronger long-term liquidity due to the spontaneous community burns & continuous developer work.

According to recent reports, shiba Inu’s (SHIB) are still running their own Layer-2 blockchain, even though the activity majorly dried down since the NFT fall-off. SHIB’s expansion to Asian markets has brought in fresh liquidity for spontaneous pumps, but the Japanese guard-dog meme coin still added another zero.

According to recent reports, however, the token deflation doesn’t make the substantial argument without proper utility and constant trading demand. So far, the game’s been brutal even for blue-chip meme coins, with top dog cryptos Dogecoin (DOGE) & Shiba Inu  (SHIB) 1Y ROI sliding by 63.8% & 57.55%, respectively.

According to recent reports, the same trend is witnessed across Solana’s (SOL) meme coins. The compartment got cut in half in value from August, 2026. Significant-hitters like Dog Wif Hat (WIF), Official Trump coin (TRUMP) & Bonk coin all fell by at least 68% from last year, with Trump coin taking the top hit, down 89%.

According to recent reports, in a nutshell, the retail sentiment has taken its toll on the meme currency section without much support from institutional players. To illustrate, the Dogecoin ETF by 21Shares hasn’t pulled in any cash in months. With no inflows, the risk-reward profile of memes deteriorates even further.

Bringing back institutional interest heavily relies on global macroeconomic factors, as well as the dependency on Bitcoin (BTC). The apex crypto asset is testing the $60,000 middle boundary of a multi-month support line, with many charterers assessing the odds of another $55,000 retest.
