Shiba Inu's (SHIB) market structure remains bearish. SHIB trades near $0.00000365 on November 26. Its daily chart is showing selling pressure, with no validated reversal. Therefore, realistic recovery scenarios are largely tied to a general market retracement rather than local developments.

Shiba Inu quite often follows general market trends, despite seeming like a memecoin leader. The illiquid market always creates certain challenges for assets like Shiba Inu. Apart from poor liquidity, another major factor is the lack of inflows into these assets.

Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and other larger assets have been printing outflows throughout November. When larger assets do not attract enough capital, SHIB and smaller assets almost always witness outflows or passive positioning among investors.

Shiba Inu is a risky asset, so risk appetite and a general crypto market recovery should be considered the primary recovery scenario for the asset.

The second scenario precedes the first one: in a blossoming economy, exposing yourself to risk is much easier than under the conditions we are witnessing right now.

In a recent development, once investors regain confidence, an improvement in overall sentiment is just a matter of time. Even though there is always a chance that funds will go toward larger assets rather than risky memecoins prone to exploits, scams, and security risks, sentiment can still improve.

It has been revealed that despite mixed on-chain signals, the bearish trend may persist. Shiba Inu is already oversold, and conditions might become even worse.

Meanwhile, SHIB is approaching oversold territory and remains below declining moving averages. This could allow for a bounce, but oversold conditions do not guarantee a market bottom.

For now, the continuation of the downtrend remains the most realistic scenario, even if short-term rebounds remain possible. Until inflows improve and SHIB reveals a sustained move above nearby resistance, any recovery is more likely to remain corrective.
