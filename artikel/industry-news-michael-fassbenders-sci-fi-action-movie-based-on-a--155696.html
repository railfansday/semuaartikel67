One of Michael Fassbender’s sci-fi action movies is finally returning to streaming soon, a decade after its theatrical release.

According to recent reports, fassbender starred as a death-row inmate who discovers that he’s a descendant of an ancient line of Assassins in 20th Century Fox’s Assassin’s Creed. The 2016 sci-fi action adaptation was based on Ubisoft's bestselling video game franchise. During its theatrical run, Assassin's Creed underperformed at the box office, grossing around $240 million against a $125 million budget. It also received negative reviews from critics and audiences, who felt that the live-action adaptation failed to capture the true essence of the games.

Despite the poor box office performance and disappointing reviews, Assassin’s Creed ultimately found success on streaming years after its release. In March 2025, it claimed the number one spot on HBO Max’s top 10 movies chart, surpassing popular titles like Men in Black, White Chicks, and Flow. Now, Fassbender's live-action film has finally landed a upcoming streaming home, nearly ten years after its theatrical debut.

The live-action Assassin's Creed movie will officially stream on Paramount+ starting Tuesday, December 1. In the 2016 adaptation, the mysterious corporation brings Cal (Fassbender) to an advanced facility, where he's forced to unlock his ancestor's memories to find the location of an ancient artifact. The painful procedure not only unveils the secrets of his heritage, but it also allows Cal to adopt his ancestor Aguilar de Nerha's lethal skills in the real world.

It has been revealed that australian filmmaker Justin Kurzel directed the 2016 movie, which was penned by Michael Lesslie, Adam Cooper, and Bill Collage. Although it was widely panned, reviews for Assassin's Creed mostly praised Fassbender's physical performance and commanding presence as one of its few highlights. The ensemble cast also included Marion Cotillard as Dr. Sofia Rikkin, Jeremy Irons as Alan Rikkin, Charlotte Rampling as Ellen Kaye, Brendan Gleeson as Joseph Lynch, and Callum Turner as Nathan. The adaptation right now holds a critic rating of 18% on Rotten Tomatoes, while its audience score is slightly higher at 42%.

Following the 2016 movie, Netflix is finally reviving the Assassin's Creed franchise with a live-action TV adaptation. Unlike the film, Netflix's upcoming Assassin's Creed show will be a high-octane historical thriller set in 64 AD Ancient Rome. According to its logline, the story introduces two mysterious, opposing factions in a secret war that will determine mankind's future.

It will feature characters in pivotal historical events as they battle to shape humanity’s destiny. The series stars Toby Wallace, Lola Petticrew, Zachary Hart, Laura Marcus, Tanzyn Crawford, Nabhaan Rizwan, Claes Bang, and Noomi Rapace. Further details about their characters are still under wraps.

Production on Netflix's Assassin's Creed series has already wrapped. However, the streamer has yet to announce its target release date. While waiting for the fresh live-action adaptation, audiences can rewatch Fassbender's version on Paramount+ when it arrives on December 1.
