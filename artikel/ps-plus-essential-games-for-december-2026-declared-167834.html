These games will be available to claim until 4th January, 2027 after they’ve been added to the sub next week.

It’s the final revision of 2026 and PS Plus Essential is going out with a bit of a bang in my opinion.

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3 + 4 is the most recent remake in Activision’s long-running skateboarding series, and a very good compilation at that.

We awarded it an 8/10 in our review, writing: “Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3 + 4 is another fantastic skateboarding title that revives some all-time classics, albeit with some caveats.”.

Meanwhile, Dragon Ball XenoVerse 2 is arriving just ahead of its sequel next year. This game has received years of support, including a fairly recent PS5 port, included here as part of your subscription.

According to recent reports, of that PS5 version, we wrote in our 7/10 review: “The only problem with XenoVerse 2, here in 2024, is that its core gameplay has never truly evolved. Yes, more and more ways to upgrade and customise your character have been added over the years, but the actual combat feels basic and a touch too finicky by today's standards.”.

Finally, Amnesia: The Bunker is an acclaimed horror game from specialists Frictional – and one we ultimately liked.

We noted in our 7/10 review: “Amnesia: The Bunker sticks a little too close to what Frictional Games has been doing for over a decade now, but with a more free-form approach to gameplay, the team is back on the right track again.”.

Recent findings suggest that that rounds out the lineup for December, then – and 2026 as a whole? How are you feeling about PS Plus Essential this year? Let me know below.
