If you tab across in the laptop, you can read Vanessa's messages, which give a clue as to what happened to her. You'll also find a mysterious message that mentions "Auntie's Place in La Perle" and gives you a code to use.

The garage this code unlocks is just around the corner from Aunt Zola's Soul Food in the La Perle district of Vice City. Keep going down the street, turn right onto the main road where the Twa Ponya tattoo shop is on the left side. Drive a little way down, but before you reach Aline's Thrift Store, turn right into a little parking area with a take-out restaurant labelled as Auntie's Place, with a little awning.

It has been revealed that the garage is over on its right. Enter the safe code just below the graffiti bow on the right side and you'll unlock the Del Rio Boulevard Garage and Vanessa's Pink Growler.
