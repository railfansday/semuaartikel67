According to recent reports, media reports suggest she flew to China on China Southern. Specifically, she traveled on Sunday, November 15, 2026, on CZ8032, from Mexico City (MEX) to Shenzhen (SZX), with a refueling stop in Tijuana (TIJ), and the journey covering a distance of 8,803 miles.

Sources indicate that the Airbus A350-900 took off from Mexico City at 12:49AM, arriving in Tijuana at 2:02AM, after a flight time of 3hr13min. Then it took off again at 4:34AM, and landed in Shenzhen the following morning at 11:31AM, after a flight time of 14hr56min. All-in-all, the flight took around 21 hours, including the refueling stop.

The shocking thing here isn’t that she traveled commercial, but instead, that she reportedly flew in economy! Reports suggest she was in seat 33B, which would be a middle seat in economy, no less!
