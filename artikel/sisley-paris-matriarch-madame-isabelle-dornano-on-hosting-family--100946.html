Madame Isabelle d’Ornano’s early gatherings at her family’s Loire Valley estate were dressy affairs. Over time, the Sisley Paris matriarch—whose plant-based approach helped redefine the beauty industry—let the formality fall away. Grand dinners became generous buffets of moussaka, poached eggs over lentil salad, and even “pizza à la d’Ornano.” The mood is more relaxed now, but the thoughtfulness remains—a sensibility she shares in her new book, Imagine: Words & Cushions, and has passed on to her children, including Countess Christine d’Ornano.

“I started to embroider the words ‘love’ and ‘peace.’ People wanted to buy my cushions. I said no,” she remembers with a laugh. “My cushions are much, much too precious.”.

That sense of personal attachment runs through d’Ornano’s home, too. At 89, she lives on Paris’s Left Bank in a maximalist duplex layered with Henri Cartier-Bresson photos and objects by Jean-François Fourtou. (She shared the home with her late husband, Hubert d’Ornano, with whom she co-founded Sisley.) It’s also where she welcomes family for the holidays, a tradition that reflects her easy approach to entertaining: She likes a mélange of guests, from the glamorous to those for whom an invitation “feels like a treat.”.

Sources indicate that her family knows just how much the cushions mean to her—and still hopes to get one. “My daughter-in-law and my granddaughter say, ‘If you give me one present, give me a cushion,’” she says. “Because I’m quite stingy with my cushions.”.

In a recent development, if it’s a big party, the more glamorous people you have, the better. But you also need people who really like you, and some for whom it’s a treat to be there. It’s a mixture.

Recent findings suggest that I would not be able to live in a home that has been created and decorated by an architect, because I like having my mess and books around. You must not have too many rules.

Christmas is a wonderful tradition in our home. I made a cushion embroidered with “Noël”—it’s such a lovely word in French. Our family has come together every Christmas since I got married. And of course, we love presents, too.
