The first fully electric Porsche Boxster is finally in the home stretch of development. Ahead of its official debut in early 2027, Porsche offered an early look at the two-door electric sports car.

Despite rumors that the all-electric 718 Boxster was canceled, Porsche confirmed on Tuesday that the Boxster EV will debut in the first quarter of 2027.

“Starting next year, the all-electric 718 Boxster will offer our customers a compelling entry point into the world of two-door sports cars,” Porsche CEO Dr Michael Leiters said.

Ahead of its upcoming debut, Porsche is putting the electric sports car through the paces to ensure it preserves the DNA of its predecessors, a top priority.

Porsche developed a latest “E-Core” architecture specifically for the 718 Boxster to lower the seat position and optimize weight distribution.

Sources indicate that with the 800-volt battery mounted directly behind the seats, where the internal combustion engine would typically be found, the weight is concentrated toward the middle of the vehicle. The electric Boxster has a weight distribution of 40% on the front axle and 60% on the rear, matching that of a traditional mid-engined sports car.

It also features an “intelligent lightweight construction,” as Porsche calls it, so the vehicle’s overall weight matches that of the current 911 Cabriolet models with its T-Hybrid powertrain.

“Our engineers have succeeded in transferring the Boxster’s unique concept to the electric world, further enhancing it in the process,” Porsche’s CEO said.

Industry observers note that the electric Boxster shares many chassis components with the 911, including the steering, which is based on the 911 Carrera GTS. It will offer rear-axle steering, a first for the 718 Boxster.

Meanwhile, the wheel and tire dimensions and braking system are based on the Porsche 911, and the electronically controlled PTV+ limited-slip differential is also pulled from the 911.

Porsche stated the electric Boxster will be “the most powerful 718 ever,” but didn’t offer a specific output figure.

The range-topping Boxster EV can sprint from 0 to 62 mph (0 to 100 km/h) in under three seconds, according to Porsche, and it can reach a top speed of over 155 mph (250 km/h).

In a recent development, all electric Boxster models will be RWD and come standard with E-Shift. Like Hyundai’s performance N EVs, including the IONIQ 5 N and IONIQ 6 N, the Porsche Boxster EV features a virtual gear shift that simulates the feeling and sounds of a seven-speed PDK transmission.

When E-Shift mode is not active, the paddle can be used to toggle between three levels of recuperation.

The electric Porsche 718 Boxster will make its official debut in the first quarter of 2027, so we should learn more details soon. IT will be built at Porsche’s main plant in Stuttgart-Zuffenhausen, Germany.
