Mario Kart World just got a massive new upgrade in version 1.9.0, finally bringing Nintendo's Switch 2 racing game into the limelight – but not all fans are satisfied.

Nintendo unveils 1.9.0 in a colorful trailer on YouTube, featuring eight "new" tracks and two Knockout Tour routes – and yes, the quote marks around the word "new" are intentional on my part. That's because the tracks are sequels of sorts, acting as the "two" counterparts to already existing maps: Airship Fortress 2, Desert Hills 2, DK Pass 2, Maio Bros. Circuit 2, Koopa Troopa Beach 2, Salty Salty Speedway 2, and Wario Stadium 2.

In a recent development, fans are jokingly calling 1.9.0 the "enhancement of 2s" in the comments, noting that there isn't one truly latest map in the lineup here. They're just reverse takes on old tracks, really. "This update certainly has 'The Power of Two' in it," reads one reply. Another jests, "This is like if McDonald's slapped a McChicken inside a McDouble, put it in a new box, and called it a brand-new menu item," clarifying they're "not mad at this update, though.".

There is still 2.0 to look forward to, after all – and players note that's likely why Nintendo hasn't dropped anything too significant in 1.9.0. "People don't seem to realize we're getting close to a 2.0 patch," as one response puts it. "That's the big one that will have a lot of stuff that probably people are looking for and stuff that people aren't expecting." As for the Knockout Tour routes, this week's patch also brings two: Feather Rally and Lucky Cat Rally.

In a recent development, the fresh version includes plenty of other changes, as outlined on Nintendo's official support website. They range from technical to wholesome, like the fact that "confetti now falls during the results presentation in Grand Prix and Knockout Tour." Folks can also now pass through the river route to Cheep Cheep Falls from DK Pass, Sky-High Sundae, Salty Salty Speedway, Starview Peak, and Wario Shipyard.

All in all, it's not a bad update – heck, at least it's something seeing the love Nintendo's given Mario Kart 8 recently – but it does leave ample room for a banger 2.0, if you ask me.
