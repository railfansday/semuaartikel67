WHEELING, W.Va., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- WesBanco, Inc. (Nasdaq: WSBC), a diversified, multi-state bank holding firm, confirmed today that Matthew Coppola has been appointed Executive Vice President and Chief Credit Officer, effective November 16, 2026. In this role, Coppola will report to Nathan Jones, Senior Executive Vice President, and Chief Risk Officer, and succeeds Bob Friend, who will retire at the end of 2026 following nearly 15 years with WesBanco.

"Matthew understands that growth and strong credit discipline go hand-in-hand," remarked Nathan Jones, Chief Risk Officer, WesBanco. "His combination of credit expertise, leadership and practical understanding of commercial banking make him the right leader to build on the strong foundation Bob has established and partner with our business leaders to support continued growth. We are pleased to welcome him to WesBanco.".

"I am excited to join Nathan and the WesBanco team at an vital time for the firm," mentioned Matthew Coppola, incoming Chief Credit Officer. "WesBanco has a clear commitment to disciplined growth, and I look forward to building on that strength and partnering with our leaders and bankers to help deliver an even better experience for the customers, businesses and communities we serve.".

In the Chief Credit Officer role, Coppola will be responsible for overseeing the Credit organization including credit adjudication and decisioning, secured credit, credit resolution, and retail credit operations.

Coppola has more than 20 years of experience in commercial credit, credit risk, and lending. He joins WesBanco from UMB Bank, where he most recently served as Executive Vice President and Regional Credit Officer, with responsibility for credit adjudication and risk management across several commercial lending verticals, including capital funding, asset-based lending, franchise finance, and healthcare.

Previously, Coppola held a variety of senior credit leadership roles at Huntington National Bank, including Regional Credit Officer, Credit Policy Officer, and Credit Reporting Manager. Earlier in his career, Coppola held credit and commercial lending positions with MB Financial Bank, Bankers' Bank, Independent Bankers' Bank and Merrill Lynch Business Financial Services.

He earned bachelor's degrees in finance and economics from Michigan State University.
