Liverpool have a lot of decisions to make about their current crop of players.

A decent chunk of their contracts are set to expire in June 2027 and, right now, no extensions have been agreed.

Obviously, the Reds will have certain players who they'd prefer to keep over others but, the decision may not be up to them.

Thanks to how good most of Liverpool's players are, clubs from across the world have shown their interest in acquiring their services which means that the L4 outfit will have a fight on their hands to try and keep most of them.

And, for one player, even though it felt like he was going to be locked down, his future has now been blown back open once again.

The future of Rio Ngumoha is still undecided.

Rio Ngumoha is a player who Liverpool need to keep hold of at any cost.

The young Englishman has been a regular starter for Andoni Iraola and, even though he's not really managed to get off the mark yet, his performances have still been impressive.

People forget that the former Chelsea man is still only 18 years old so, the fact that he's even being chosen to start for Liverpool in the first place is a big thing.

The talent that Ngumoha possesses is obvious to anyone who watches him play so, even though his current contract doesn't expire until 2028, fans can see why Liverpool want to lock him down.

However, a fresh update from Fraser Fletcher has shared a bit more of an insight into what the future has in store for Ngumoha.

"Bayern Munich are very keen on him [Rio Ngumoha],” Fletcher mentioned.

“They have now moved to make him and his representatives aware that they would like to bring him in.

“Of course, Liverpool don’t want to sell such a brilliant young talent in January.

“At just 18 years old he’s already lit up Anfield on a couple of occasions and Andoni Iraola is keen to keep him.

“Liverpool are keen to push him through their development initiative for him and get him tied down to a latest deal.

“No new deal has been signed yet meaning his future is [still] open.”.

Liverpool can't let Bayern Munich sign Rio Ngumoha.

The fact that talk of Ngumoha exiting Liverpool is even making the light of day should be questioned.

The 18-year-old has had a huge impact during his short time as a first-team regular with Thomas Tuchel even calling him up to England's national team.

As mentioned, he may not have found a purple patch of form yet but, from the movements and chances that he creates, you can tell that Liverpool have a gem on their hands.

Bayern Munich are one of the biggest teams in the world and, the fact that they want Ngumoha proves how good he must be.

Harry Kane has just won the Ballon D'Or thanks to his performances for the Bundesliga outfit which demonstrates that they only have elite talent in their ranks.

Obviously, they'd have to pay Liverpool to sign him right now but, it doesn't feel like any sort of fee would be worth them accepting.

Nobody knows how these contract talks are going to go but, if they don't end with the attacker signing a new deal, some serious questions will need to be asked.
