Hollywood’s biggest stars are expressing gratitude as they celebrate Thanksgiving 2026.

Before turkey was served, many celebrities documented their family celebrations on social media while others shared what they were grateful for.

For Hoda Kotb, she found the silver lining in the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade being postponed until Saturday, November 28.

“It’s OK,” Kotb, 62, remarked on the Wednesday, November 25, episode of Late Night With Seth Meyers. “We waited 100 years for this parade… we’re gonna wait two more days. We’re gonna have it on Saturday. It’s gonna be a beautiful, sunsplash day.”.

According to recent reports, although the parade may be delayed, Americans still were able to enjoy football games and the 25th annual National Dog Show. Keep reading to see how all of your favorite celebrities marked the holiday below:.

Industry observers note that “Hoping everybody has a happy Thanksgiving!!! “ Blake Shelton wrote via Instagram while posing for a selfie next to wife Gwen Stefani. “Thankful for y’all!!!!.”.

“The amount of goodness we experience in life depends on our ability to notice it. Gratitude is contagious, and the more we can appreciate the beauty around us, the more joy we bring into our lives and the lives of others,” Gisele Bündchen wrote via Instagram. “May we always find something to be grateful for, not just today, but every day. Happy Thanksgiving! Sending you all so much love. 🤍.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Going to turn off the noise for a moment. To all out there in this vast environment that we opt into, that if you’re lucky enough to have one, the Thanksgiving meal is not hard. It’s a roasted bird and potatoes,” Jamie Lee Curtis wrote via Instagram after enjoying a morning walk by the beach. “What’s hard are the people with their binary and rigid ideas and judgments and petty selfishness. Today, let’s all be like Dolly [Parton] and be welcoming and gentle and generous. XO.”.

After Us learned that Britney Spears was in Louisiana ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday, her mom, Lynne Spears, posted a message of gratitude.

“God has blessed us with so many blessings, some we see every day and others we sometimes take for granted,” she wrote via Instagram. “This Thanksgiving is especially special. May we always remember to pause, pray, and thank God for His many blessings. 🙏🍂.”.

In a recent development, happy Thanksgiving! 🧡🍂Feeling so thankful for my family, dear friends, and all the love, laughter, beautiful moments, and cherished memories we share,” Camille Grammer wrote via Instagram. “My heart is also with those facing difficult times today. Sending love and warm wishes to everyone. May we always find reasons to give thanks. ❤️”.

Sources indicate that “Thanksgiving is for gratitude, good food, and getting a head start on Christmas! ❤️🦃🍗,” Shark Tank star Barbara Corcoran wrote via Instagram. “Happy Thanksgiving to you and yours!”.

“Thanksgiving was never ordinary with my mother [Joan Rivers]. Whether she was hosting a beautiful holiday dinner in New York or riding through the streets in the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, my mother never did anything halfway!” Melissa Rivers shared via Instagram. “Thankful for the memories, the laughter, and a mother who was truly one of a kind. Happy Thanksgiving!”.

Industry observers note that “Time for a little more peace, love and cowboys ✌️❤️🤠,” country singer Lainey Wilson wrote via Instagram. “Let the holiday shenanigans begin 🤪. Happy Thanksgiving from my family to y’all’s! 🦃.”.

“This Thanksgiving, my heart is filled with gratitude. For the love of my family, the unwavering support of my fans, and the incredible gift of returning to the stage this year. I am thankful beyond words,” Céline Dion wrote via Instagram. “Returning to the stage after so many challenges was made possible by an extraordinary circle of people — my medical team, my personal team, the incredible people who brought the show to life, and countless others around the world whose efforts, substantial and small, made this moment possible. To each and every one of you, and to my family and fans who never stopped believing, thank you. I am forever grateful.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Happy Thanksgiving! Here’s where I’ll be all day…my happy place!” Giada DeLaurentiis wrote via Instagram with footage from her kitchen. “Love to u all 🦃🍂🍁💛.”.
