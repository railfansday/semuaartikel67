Round Up: GTA 6 Is Not Just the Most Acclaimed Game of 2026, It's the Highest-Rated Game of All Time

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The initial reviews have arrived for GTA 6, and it's safe to say the game has been an absolute smash hit with critics.

In a recent development, the metascore has settled on a 99, making it one point off a "perfect" score of 100. In a year stuffed with fantastic games massive and small, it's become the highest rated title of 2026.

More than that, though, it's the highest rated game of all time.

Reaching 99 is almost unheard of; only one other game has achieved the same level of acclaim — The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998).

According to recent reports, however, GTA 6 has held onto its 99 score after more than 160 reviews; Ocarina of Time's metascore counts just 22 — though that's of course indicative of the era in which it released. There are simply more gaming outlets now, but that just means it's tougher to get consistent scores.

Rockstar's crime caper has received insanely consistent acclaim, and that might just give it an edge over the Nintendo classic. It's incredibly rare to see near-total agreement on a subjective work like this.

Critics are unanimous in their praise of GTA 6's open world, the relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia, and the complex systems behind its myriad characters, activities, and missions.

Here are some snippets from just a few of the reviews — including our own, of course.

Industry observers note that "GTA 6 is the deepest, densest, most accomplished open world since Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has set a new sandbox standard here, with a world that feels alive and populated with real people; Leonida is a place where it appears almost anything can happen at any time.".

"Somehow, GTA 6 lives up to the hype. Rockstar Games has crafted the most incredible open world I've ever experienced in a video game, packed with so much detail that I'll want to continue exploring it for months and likely years to come. It's coupled with a campaign that's full of extremely entertaining missions, led by two main characters who I've become much more invested in than I'd anticipated.".

It has been revealed that "Like all videogame protagonists, Jason and Lucia are ciphers through which the player exerts their will on the digital world. And yet, through the co-authored interplay of the characters Rockstar has written, and the people you personally tune them into becoming, they form one of the great partnerships in entertainment: beautiful and deadly, open to the possibilities of their world and, despite the violence they inflict on those beyond their circle, driven forward not by fear, but by love.".

"A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that.".

"Rockstar Games' latest release is a monumental achievement in storytelling, immersion, gameplay, and breadth of content.".

Industry observers note that "GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation. Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality. There's a degree of sophistication to everything GTA 6 does, and there's no aspect of the series' gameplay that hasn't been improved over past instalments.".

"Only Rockstar commands the time and money necessary to make a game as dedicated to small details. And only Rockstar can execute on the realising such a granular vision unimpeachably. The obsession with the minutia both explains and justifies the 13-year wait. This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7.".

"Exquisitely detailed, endlessly ripe for experimentation, and uncompromisingly engineered, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played.".

"Grand Theft Auto 6 is a modern masterpiece. Rockstar's developers have created a remarkable open world that excels in storytelling, gameplay, visuals, music and technology to achieve something truly unmatched. Minor performance concerns don’t dull the sparkle of an absolute must-play experience. GTA 6 was more than worth the wait.".
