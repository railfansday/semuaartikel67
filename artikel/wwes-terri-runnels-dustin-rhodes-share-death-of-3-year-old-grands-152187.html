Terri Runnels and Dustin Rhodes are facing a devastating loss.

The former WWE wrestling duo shared that their 3-year-old grandson Rowan has died, although they did not share how the toddler passed.

Terri confirmed the news on Facebook, sharing a statement for her and Dustin’s daughter Dakota Runnels and her husband Bleau.

“On behalf of Dakota, Bleau, and their families, we share the tragic news of the untimely passing of their sweet beautiful boy, Rowan Dane Oney,” the Nov. 25 post read. “We ask that you respect their privacy at this time. Please no calls or visits. We will post more details soon. Please be in prayer for our family.”.

Sources indicate that dustin also asked for prayers for his daughter and her husband, writing in a follow-up message on X, “Guys my grandson Rowan has passed. Please keep us all in your prayers during this time. I am here with @DakotaRunnels22 now.”.

It has been revealed that the 57-year-old went on to remember the young boy by sharing a photo of himself and Rowan, writing, "Rest in Peace baby boy. I love u so much Rowan.".

According to recent reports, terri and Dustin were married from 1993 to 1999. Dustin—the son of WWE Hall of Fame Dusty Rhodes—wrestled for the WWE on and off from 1995 to 2019 under the ring name Goldust. Terri—who was her ex-husband’s manager at the time—joined the WWE under the name Marlena, playing the part of Goldust's cigar-smoking manager.

During her years with the WWE, Terri made sure that she was still able to be there for her daughter, explaining that she told founder Vince McMahon she would not do non-televised shows.

In a recent development, “I wanted to be at home for Dakota,” the 60-year-old told Slam Wrestling in 2021. “Back in the day, Dakota was the only [wrestler’s child] who had both of her parents wrestling. Every other male wrestler had a wife at home that was taking care of the kids, or whatever.”.

In a recent development, “Before she was school age, she would fly with me to TVs and pay-per-views,” Terri continued. “Once she got old enough to go to school, I got a live-in nanny. I was comfortable with her being home for three days with the nanny, but then I would be home for four days and I would take over as full-time mom.”.
