Royal Caribbean’s 2026-27 season in Australia is now underway, with two Quantum-class ships sailing from ports in the country.

In addition to the Anthem of the Seas, which recently repositioned to Sydney, the company’s seasonal deployment in the South Pacific also includes the Quantum of the Seas.

The 2014-built vessel arrived at its homeport in Brisbane in late October, kicking off a series of 28 sailings in the region.

Before completing its national season in mid-April 2027, the ship is scheduled to offer three- to seven-night itineraries to Australia’s East Coast, visiting destinations that include Airlie Beach and Cairns.

The Quantum of the Seas will also offer weeklong cruises to the South Pacific, calling at Nouméa in New Caledonia, as well as Port Vila and Mystery Island in Vanuatu.

“We’re excited to bring our award-winning Quantum of the Seas back to Brisbane, giving more Queensland families the unforgettable Royal Caribbean holiday experiences they know and love,” said Gavin Smith, vice president and managing director, Australia and Newly introduced Zealand, Royal Caribbean.

“With more short getaways and weekend departures, this season makes it easier than ever to escape, whether it’s a quick weekend away with friends or a longer adventure with the whole family,” he added.

In addition to sailings from Brisbane onboard the Quantum, Royal Caribbean will also offer 21 cruises from Sydney onboard the Anthem of the Seas this season.

The ship will offer three- to 11-night cruises to destinations in Australia, Newly introduced Zealand and the South Pacific through mid-April 2027.

Royal Caribbean is planning to expand its operations in the region in 2027-28, with three ships sailing from Australia.

As previously reported by Cruise Industry News, the Ovation of the Seas will join the Anthem of the Seas and the Voyager of the Seas for itineraries from Brisbane and Sydney.
