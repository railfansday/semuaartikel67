Alex Consani is ready for a spin around Vice City.

With the supermodel lending her voice to Grand Theft Auto VI—which is poised to be the biggest video game of the year after making $3.79 billion in preorder and first-day sales, according to parent company Take Two—it was only appropriate that she channel that car vibe into her clutch look for the game’s launch party.

According to recent reports, consani buckled up for the Nov. 18 event at the Gekko restaurant in Miami wearing a short dress from Paris-based label Sevali’s spring 2025 collection that was made out of upcycled seatbelts. The belts crisscrossed around the 23-year-old’s svelte figure, coming to a click—literally—with a buckle that was strapped across her shoulder.

The Victoria’s Secret model completed her look with clear kitten heels and her long blonde hair parted down the middle and flowing behind her.

Recent findings suggest that in the newly released video game, Consani can be heard as a DJ for the in-game radio station Flash FM alongside singer Robyn. The two play songs from the likes of Olivia Rodrigo, Charli XCX, Zara Larsson and more as they put their commentary into high gear.

After playing Rodrigo’s “good 4 u,” Consani can be heard saying in a clip posted from the game, “How do you even fumble a baddie like Olivia?”.

According to recent reports, she also had some love for Charli XCX, saying, “Whatever Charli XCX does, she’s my medicine. That is my f--king doctor, y’all.”.

As for Larsson? After playing the Swedish singer’s hit “Fast Emotion,” Consani commented, “God, I love Zara so much.”.

Of course, the model isn’t the only star to lend her voice to GTA VI, with Bad Bunny coming in as host of Latin radio Cocoteo FM, PinkPantheress DJing the indie dance station Stockyard FM, “Weird” Al Yankovic hosting the ’80s station Vice City FM, Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey co-hosting Back Country Radio and Rolling Stones guitarist Keith Richards playing his part as DJ Keith on Gloriana Blues Radio.

Grand Theft Auto VI marks the latest installment in the long-running video game series, coming out 13 years after the record-breaking Grand Theft Auto V.

The upcoming game features characters like Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as they take part in fan-favorite activities from previous games like car-jackings, shootouts, street-racing, robberies, swimming, jet-skiing, sky-diving, visiting the strip club and, yes, even petting dogs. GTA 6 is set around locations inspired by Floria, allowing players new playable vehicles like kayaks and airboats to roam around the new locale.
