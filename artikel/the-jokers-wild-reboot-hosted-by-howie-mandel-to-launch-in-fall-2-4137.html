Sony Pictures Television is bringing back game show The Joker’s Wild as a half-hour syndicated program hosted by comedian and television personality Howie Mandel.

Sources indicate that the reboot, which combines the gameplay of the original with upcoming twists and a high-stakes Jackpot bonus round, will launch in broadcast syndication in fall 2027 on the CBS Owned Stations. It is unclear yet what the stations will drop for next fall to make room for The Joker’s Wild.

In a recent development, production on the fresh The Joker’s Wild will begin in mid-November on the Sony Pictures studio lot in Culver City, Calif. where Jeopardy! and Wheel of Fortune also are taped. “I love that we’re bringing The Joker’s Wild back,” Mandel noted. “It’s such a fun, crazy show, and getting to be a part of that is a real honor. I’m excited to get in there, have some laughs, and probably make a little bit of a mess along the way. That’s usually when I’m having the most fun.” Watch on Deadline.

Industry observers note that mandel has gameshow credentials with his long stint as the host of Deal or No Deal, for which he has Emmy nominations. He has also served as a judge on America’s Got Talent for more than 17 seasons and is the new host of The Price Is Right Tonight.

In a recent development, on The Joker’s Wild, two contestants compete by taking spins on the High Roller slot machine, using their luck and knowledge to win big. The contestants answer questions based on categories that come up in their spins, and Jokers are wild, increasing the points up for grabs. After three rounds, the contestant with the most points wins the game. That winner moves on to the latest Joker’s Jackpot bonus round where they’ll face tempting deals with the Devil as they try to take home up to $50,000.

“The Joker’s Wild is one of the most recognizable brands in game show history, with a format that combines trivia, unpredictability and significant-money stakes,” mentioned Suzanne Prete, President, Game Reveals, Sony Pictures Television. “Howie brings exactly the kind of wit, energy and spontaneity that this game demands.”.

Created and originally hosted by Jack Barry, The Joker’s Wild has gone through a number of incarnations, including syndication runs, since first launching in 1971. The most recent revival with host Snoop Dogg wan on TBS and TNT for two seasons.

The Joker’s Wild is produced by Sony Pictures Television and executive produced by showrunner Mashawn Nix, whose credits include Deal or No Deal, Match Game and Red Table Talk. Ad sales for the series will be handled by CBS Media Ventures.
