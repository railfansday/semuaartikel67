It has been revealed that readers have traditionally learned about markets in one place and traded them in another. A story about Bitcoin, a public company or a commodity might shape a view, but acting on it meant opening an exchange or a trading app and starting over.

Decrypt set out to close that gap in September 2026, when it confirmed The Information Exchange: a network that brings together news, data, market prices, probabilities and the ability to trade, around the subjects readers follow.

The Information Exchange sees Decrypt delivering both the reporting and the market infrastructure that links information to trading, while the Solana blockchain provides the network on which trades settle, including token swaps and perpetual futures.

This guide explains what each one is and how to trade it on Decrypt.

Everything on The Information Exchange runs through a Decrypt Money Account, which released on Solana in October 2026.

Recent findings suggest that A Money Account holds one balance that funds every product on Decrypt: swaps, perpetual futures and prediction markets.The account is self-custodial, meaning Decrypt does not hold customers' private keys and cannot act on their behalf. It is designed to work without seed phrases, and network fees are covered, so users don't need to hold SOL, Solana's native token, to pay for transactions.

It has been revealed that in November 2026, Decrypt added confidential transfers, using Solana Privacy Rings from infrastructure firm Helius. Users can send funds without the amounts appearing on Solana's public ledger, and can switch the feature on by selecting "Enable" on the Private Balance panel of the Portfolio page. As of November 2026, privacy applies to transfers only, not to swaps or prediction market positions.

Solana Privacy Rings use zero-knowledge proofs, which let people prove they own and can spend a balance without revealing how much it holds.

According to recent reports, A swap exchanges one token for another at the current market price. Instead of placing an order on an exchange and waiting for it to fill, the trader sends one token and receives another in a single transaction.

Recent findings suggest that swaps on Decrypt are powered by Jupiter, a Solana trading platform that offers swaps, lending, borrowing, perpetual futures and portfolio tools. Jupiter finds prices across Solana's trading venues, so owners don't need to choose where a trade is executed.

At time of publication, swaps cover a select group of major cryptocurrencies and Solana tokens, as well as tokenized stocks, commodities and other real-world assets.

As of November 2026, the Assets panel of the Portfolio page groups holdings into crypto, stocks, commodities and collectibles, and lists tokens including SOL, BTC, ETH, XRP and HYPE.

According to recent reports, perpetual futures, or perps, are contracts that let traders bet on whether an asset's price will rise or fall without owning the asset. Unlike traditional futures, they have no expiry date. A trader who expects the price to rise goes "long," and one who expects it to fall goes "short.".

Industry observers note that perps are usually traded with leverage, which lets a trader control a larger position than their deposit would otherwise allow. Leverage magnifies gains and losses alike, and if the market moves against a position far enough, it can be liquidated, or closed automatically, wiping out the trader's margin.

Perps on Decrypt are powered by Phoenix, a fully onchain perpetuals exchange on Solana developed by Ellipsis Labs—making Decrypt the first media platform to offer perps trading alongside its journalism.

Sources indicate that as of November 2026, Phoenix offers more than 100 perps markets on Decrypt, covering crypto, U.S. stocks, gold and pre-IPO companies. Markets trade around the clock, with leverage of up to 40x, according to Phoenix.

Decrypt aims to keep adding products to The Information Exchange, including Situation Rooms: pages that combine breaking news, live data, prices, probabilities and trading around a single subject.

Decrypt operates The Information Exchange, Decrypt Money Accounts and the swaps, perpetual futures and prediction market products described in this guide.

Perpetual futures are leveraged products and carry a high risk of loss. Availability may vary by region. Nothing in this guide is investment advice.
