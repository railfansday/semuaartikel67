A nine-year-old boy in Italy sparked a 48-hour search for a missing python after using AI to create a fake image of a snake emerging from a toilet.

The incident took place in Castelfidardo, near Ancona, Italy, on November 12, when the youngster demonstrated adults a convincing image of what appeared to be a royal python poking its head out of a toilet bowl.

Believing the snake was real, the adults raised the alarm at around 8pm, prompting firefighters from the nearby town of Osimo to respond.

However, after searching the bathroom and the rest of the property, emergency crews were unable to find any trace of the reptile.

As reported by Need To Know, firefighters initially suspected the snake may have escaped through an open window or hidden itself somewhere inside the home.

The search continued the following day, with specialist officers from the Carabinieri Forestali’s CITES unit, which handles cases involving protected and exotic species, joining the investigation.

It was only after officers examined the original photograph on the boy’s phone that they discovered the truth.

The nine-year-old admitted he had used an AI app to digitally insert the python into a photograph of the toilet.

He had been visiting a friend when he created the image in the bathroom before showing it to the adults and claiming he had spotted a snake.

After realizing how much trouble his prank had caused, the boy reportedly apologized to the officers.

Despite the emergency response and subsequent investigation, he will face no legal consequences, as children under 14 cannot be held criminally responsible under Italian law.
