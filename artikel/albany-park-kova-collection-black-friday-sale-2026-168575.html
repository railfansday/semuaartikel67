It has been revealed that if Albany Park’s Kova sofa ranks high on your furniture wish list, now’s the time to clear some living room space. Recently reimagined by the brand, two distinct silhouettes make up the new Kova line: The Kova Box is the more structured of the two, with squared-off cushions that give the sofa a neatly defined profile, whereas the Kova Pillow offers softer contours and a more casual look. There’s 32 upholstery options available across each version, as well as the ability to dictate just how much you’ll sink into your sofa.

To sweeten the deal, the collection is included in Albany Park’s Black Friday sale, which runs through 12/6 and offers up to 30%, complimentary white-glove delivery, and three free throw pillows with any sofa purchase.

“I think of them as the same architecture with two different personalities,” notes Paul DeVries, CEO of Albany Park. “Nobody buys a mattress without thinking about how firm or soft it is, [but] people have been buying sofas, particularly online, from photographs and a paragraph of copy. [And yet,] somehow, [they’re] expected to know exactly how that sofa is going to feel.”.

The Kova’s modular design allows for a wide range of configurations, starting from a compact two-piece sofa, up to a six-piece U-shaped sectional. There are also three- and four-piece arrangements with chaises, as well as freestanding and storage ottomans that can be incorporated into either style. Anyone deliberating over colors and textures can order up to 12 complimentary swatches, and those wary of light-colored upholstery will appreciate that the covers are machine-washable.

The redesign also includes a sturdier hardwood foundation and a more personalized approach to comfort. For the first time, shoppers can choose how firm or soft they want their Kova cushions to be, with three options available. The brand recommends its medium cushion option for most shoppers (those partial to plush seats might opt for soft, while anyone who favors a more supportive sofa might choose firm). Luckily, if you discover your preferences are different in practice, Albany Park will send a technician to your home to exchange the cushions once, free of charge, within 60 days of delivery.

Industry observers note that equally noteworthy is the turnaround time. “Your Kova doesn’t exist until you order it,” claims DeVries. Customizing a sofa often means waiting several months for it to arrive, but every Kova is made to order at Albany Park’s factory in El Florido, Baja California, Mexico, and ships within seven days. “Your sofa isn’t sitting in a warehouse waiting for somebody to buy it. When your order comes in, we make it specifically for you,” he adds.

Recent findings suggest that as for how DeVries would configure his own Kova? He’s partial to the five-seat Box sectional with a left chaise and a storage ottoman (all the better for concealing spare blankets and pillows). Even with a dog who has a penchant for tracking the outdoors inside, he’s not inclined to forgo light-colored upholstery, believing that we shouldn’t be afraid to live with our furniture. “A sofa is one of the most vital pieces of furniture in a home,” DeVries told us. “Something that important should feel more personal.”.
