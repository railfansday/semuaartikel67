Having to refuel cars in GTA 6 was weirdly one of the most talked about features before launch—I guess people didn't like the idea of having to pull over for gas while being chased by half the Vice City's police department. But the reality is that refuelling cars is something you'll barely find yourself doing.

When your ride does run low on fuel, you can find a gas station on the map via the gas pump symbols:.

Easy, right? And you'll barely have to do it since, just like real life, cars can go quite a long time without refuelling if they have a full tank, even if you're going over 60mph, which I assume you are. Note that if you steal a stranger's car, their tank may nearly be empty already.

According to recent reports, if you're driving an electric car, check the map for recharging stations. Pull up close to one, select "Charge Vehicle," and it'll display the cost. You'll see a quick scene of the readout on the charging station filling the battery to 100%, and when it's done, you can drive away.

Now, the better reason to visit Vice City's fuel stations is that they can repair your vehicle, and if you drive like I do, you'll need this a lot. This is a separate interaction at the same gas pump: on the"Services" menu, just select "Repair" instead. The camera will whoosh into the sky for a moment, and when it returns to ground level your ride will be fixed.

The cost depends on how much damage you've done to your vehicle, but it's well worth it if you want to retain your ride, considering the usual vehicular wear-and-tear that comes with being a professional criminal.

Since you can customise your beloved vehicles at the Mod Shop, or even claim fancier cars by removing their trackers at the Pay 'n' Spray, once you unlock this in the story, keeping your car in good condition is a worthwhile investment.
