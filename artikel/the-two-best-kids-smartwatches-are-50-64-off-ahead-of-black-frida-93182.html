Garmin and Bark are competing for your money this year, and these sales make it easy to get something fun that’ll keep your kids safe and in touch.

Many parents struggle with the idea of getting a smartphone for their kids. If you want your kids to stay in touch but not have access to all the garbage on the internet, a smartwatch is the best alternative. I’ve tested a lot of kids’ smartwatches over the years, and I can easily recommend the two in the picture above: the Bark Watch and the Garmin Bounce 2.

Both watches are on sale well ahead of Black Friday, too! The Bark Watch is $11 off at Amazon ($274 total) and $50 off at Bark, while the Garmin Bounce 2 is $50-$64 off (starting at $235) depending on the color you choose. The least expensive Garmin Bounce 2 is the blue color in the photo I took above, which my son loved.

Choosing between the two is easy enough. Garmin’s watch is the least complicated and comes in a slightly more durable case with fun colors. It’s slightly cheaper than Bark, but doesn’t have any health sensors on board. It mainly tracks steps, allows your kids to message or call you, and gives you geofencing and location tracking through a $9.99 per month LTE subscription plan. It’s got a 5ATM rating and a polycarbonate case, so it’s quite durable.

The Garmin Bounce 2 is preloaded with a few mini games and educational apps, plus the Garmin Jr. app has built-in unlockables and rewards parents can set for doing chores, completing schoolwork, etc. Battery life is roughly 2 days with normal use, but can be less if you’re in an area with poor LTE reception. The Garmin Bounce 2 is best for kids 10 and under.

The Bark Watch uses Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 hardware and a custom version of Wear OS. It’s a much more powerful watch and only gets a day of battery life because of it. This one feels like a midpoint between a kids’ watch and a standard Wear OS watch, as it has all the power of Wear OS but doesn’t feature the Google Play Store. It doesn’t have any games, social media, or browsers.

Industry observers note that since it uses Samsung’s hardware, it includes cutting-edge health sensors that your kids can use, plus great multitasking and a svelte, attractive design. Bark charges $15 per month for its proposal, which includes a Bark App subscription for the whole family. I also use the Bark App on my son’s phone, which helps me monitor his usage and make sure he’s safe online. The extra $5 per month adds up, but it’s absolutely worth it if you want to add the Bark App to another phone or tablet.

The Bark Watch is fine enough for younger kids, but the design and lack of games feel more like an “older kids’ watch” to me, and my 12-year-old son agrees. Either way you go, these are fantastic choices that you’ll be happy with, especially with a nice little deal.
