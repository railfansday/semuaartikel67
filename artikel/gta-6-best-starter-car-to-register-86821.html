GTA 6: Best Starter Car to Register

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that during your Grand Theft Auto 6 playthrough, you will eventually get the opportunity to register a car. Registering cars allows you to call them easily from your phone with a small BuckMe fee.

According to recent reports, this opportunity comes at the start of Chapter 2, where you learn about a man named Moses who deals with stolen cars. To unlock Moses and his shop for good, you need to bring him your very first car to register.

Instead of stealing the coolest car to register, I recommend going a different route. Here’s the best starter car to register in GTA 6.

During the first mission of Chapter 2 in GTA 6, you will naturally meet a girl named Vicki. During this mission, Vicki will eventually mention Moses to Lucia, and once she does, Moses shop will be marked on the map with the objective to bring him a starter car to register.

According to recent reports, after bringing Moses one car and registering it with him for free, you will unlock the ability to register most cars in Leonida. The only cars you can’t register with Moses are the ones with trackers, so make a mental note of that.

Also, keep in mind that even though you can register your starter car for free, registering every other car from here on out will cost you money. The price of registration depends on the type of car.

While players may want to steal and register the flashiest car in Leonida as their starter, this isn’t necessarily the smartest move. The nice sports cars won’t have the most trunk space and can be hard to control.

Instead, it would be best to choose a pickup truck for a starter car, as they have a ton of stash space and are generally easy to drive.

In a recent development, it may seem unimportant, but having more stash space will allow Lucia to transport more money and robbery loot on a single trip. This means you can gather money faster, without constantly needing to visit ATMs.

In a recent development, additionally, as previously mentioned, pickup trucks are generally on the easier side to control when driving. This is important early in the game since it will allow you to have an easier time exploring, crashing less, and avoiding the need to pay for repairs.

Once you've built up wealth by using your starter car as a getaway and stash for loot from robberies and muggings, you can use any extra cash to register nicer vehicles.

Simply put, the coolest cars tend to have the highest registration price, so you want a reliable one that will help you get around easier and stash more money to start off.

According to recent reports, while there are many pickup trucks to find across the map of GTA VI, I have had the best luck finding them in parking lots. More specifically, I have found pickup trucks commonly in parking lots in front of bars, clothing shops, barbershops, etc.

In other words, pickup trucks are very easy to find, considering they aren’t that special when it comes to style and speed and aren’t considered “rare.” If you are worried about finding one in a specific color, you shouldn’t be, considering Moses makes you change the color when you bring it in.

Once you find a pickup truck (or a different car if you decided not to listen to my advice), bring it on over to Moses and tell him you’d like to register it.

After the quick transaction with Moses is complete, you are now able to call your starter car to your location whenever you like for a small BuckMe price via the Scooter Bros app on your phone.
