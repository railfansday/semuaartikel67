In and Out of Patagonia

“I counted days, I counted miles, to see you there.” –Taylor Swift (From Miss Americana and the Heart Break Prince).

Last night I found a hack, if you put your puffy in between your legs it’s a knee pillow plus it’s warm in the morning. I am so smart. 😉.

In a recent development, after a good night’s rest I awoke to my 5 alarms. I wanted to not sleep in with a 25er in front of Team Athena. I didn’t eat anything just started the trek, getting on trail before 5 am.

Recent findings suggest that 1) Hiking before 7 am is called “Sleep Miles,” because you could be sleeping. They are like bonus points.

According to recent reports, 2) “Morning Miles.” Self explanatory. 😊.

3) “Afternoon Miles.” Also, self explanatory.

4) “Star miles” After 5 pm “Star miles” begin. They are sluggish and not as fun.

So, when we finished our Sleep Miles we had a little break. I had a chocolate brownie Truvani bar. Not as good as the pb chocolate one I dived into on day 1. Then I had pb and strawberry jelly on bread. 🥜🍓 🍞.

Sources indicate that 19 miles to go at 8 am and I’m cooked. So damn cooked.

Sources indicate that the terrain was… not difficult, but not “straight line glory.” Santa Rita mountain range! We went around and around hills for about 10 years today. Maybe not quite, but 7.5 hours of contouring around hills felt like 10 years. I didn’t keep too close of track.

Recent findings suggest that we found a stream at 11:45 am and it was rushing so fast! We just could not go up another foot without dying! Our lunch break went 15 minutes over our limit when I didn’t remove my toes from the water and LSL was drying out the tent. On 25 mile days we can’t let our lunch breaks run too long.

Industry observers note that when we hit the trail it went a bit up and my tunes went in. It was still hot and tiring even with Zara Larson’s infamous Midnight Sun on.

I was having Déjà vu of both last spring in the heat on the AZT and our New Zealand Te Aarora trip when I hung around 100 yards behind my tramily the whole hike.

Sources indicate that we kept on and FarOut did a weird thing, we checked and it was 4.5 miles out from the highway. About 10 minutes later it stated we only had 3.5 to go. It was happy and true, but the app must not be working correctly. We never cover a mile in 10 minutes.

It has been revealed that at our afternoon break we were both ready to quit and there was not much shade at our break spot. We took a quick break and headed on, because we really wanted to make it all the way to Patagonia before dark.

It has been revealed that the rest of the day went by in a hot blur other than one thing. We saw a neon green wildlife tank. So gross and am glad we were fine on water. I asked Landslide,.

“Would you rather swim across that with your head above the water or drink an unfiltered drop?” She remarked swim.

At the highway we got a hitch within barely 5 minutes. God, I love AZ when it comes to rides. On the OCT in Oregon it was a nightmare trying to hitch.

We popped into the little grocery store in Patagonia to get some coconut water and then headed for TerraSol, a hiker/biker campground.

When we got to TerraSol I showered. Amazing is 🚿+🧼=😊! I ate ramen then 2 pieces of toasted Nutella and ghee toast. So good! 😋 I was having writer’s block as I blogged and snacked because I could not get over the sweet nutty flavors.

Industry observers note that the morning was chilly even with my fur coat on. Gunnar (My Viking friend who rides opposite me) was not awake and I was ready for action. So I hopped off into a grassy patch and had PJ snap my picture. Can you find me?

In a recent development, at 6:50 am my eyes fluttered open in TerraSol. I relaxed for a while longer, so glad to sleep in a bit, but then I couldn’t stop thinking about buttery croissants. So I got up and packed my things. First, I finished my bag of bread with peanut butter and Nutella. So good.

Sources indicate that the trail angel, Mary, let us ride for free down to Gathering Grounds Café for that croissant! It was nice having a leisurely morning. Then we hit up Red Mountain Foods for some sourdough bread and went to hitch.

In a recent development, within 10 minutes an orange Subaru pulled up. A 20 something year old got out. “Hi, I’m Nanaman,” he told us. The name sounded so familiar! Lizard thought so too. Then it came to her, our hiker friend named Trucker (you have probably heard of him) met him a couple years ago and mentioned him to us. Crazy how last spring we met hiker-celeb Handstand on the AZT, then a few days ago PegLeg and now Nanaman.

Recent findings suggest that it was hella hot on trail before lunch. That’s the problem when we don’t get on trail til 10 in the desert. So after that 6 miles in we found a sweet shade spot. Like SUPER fire. I wasn’t really hungry because I had a significant breakfast. But I ate some of my really yummy bread I had picked up.

Recent findings suggest that after that I was about to burst into tears. It was so hot! Landslide Lizard can always tell so she saved the situation with a game of Would You Rather. We played for about an hour. One of the questions she asked is.

“Would you rather have pie right now or ice cream?”.

Sources indicate that then I asked her if she would rather live in Vegas or LA for 3 years. For some reason she chose Vegas. To each her own.

The trail was being a bitch- we’d be walking and it would END, just stop and 15 feet later the trail would become visible again. I do not recommend doing this as Star Miles. When we got to the water source we had planned for a bit before our campsite we found it didn’t look great, so we kept on. I had a little over a liter and LSL had 2.5 liters. If we had rice it would work because we wouldn’t need much water to make dinner and two miles down the trail tomorrow there was a cache. We could make it through the night.

My mom and I played a game of imagining what all my classmates would be doing if they were here with us. It was fun and we got some good chuckles out of it all.

It has been revealed that A mile and a half from the site there was an old dirt road for a trail. So nice. I LOVE dirt roads for the evening. It got dark and our site already had a tent. Did not look like a hiker cuz it was huge and there were vehicles. Hunters likely. So, we decided to keep going a bit farther and see if we could find another spot.

.2 past we found a coo coo bananas spot. It is flat but there are dry grass clumps underneath the tent that make for a bumpy floor.

Industry observers note that today was interesting. At lunch the pack was thrown down and I was in the dirt. Only for a moment luckily. We saw something that didn’t smell good. Landslide Lizard and PJ called it a well. I always thought of well as an adjective. Learn new things on a daily basis. I’m a sleepyhead.
