Chelsea’s former techincal director Michael Emenalo has taken up an advisory role at Leicester City.

The Athletic reports that the Nigerian, who was with the Blues for five and a half years, is part of a team of consultants that is helping the Foxes with their future planning.

The fallen giants are now in League One after back-to-back relegations from the Premier League and the Championship, and the 2016 top-flight champions right now sit 11th in the third tier.

Russell Martin, the ex-Southampton and Rangers manager, is the man tasked with taking the east Midlands side back into the second division, but financial restraints have hindered their progress.

Now, they have turned to Emenalo and his team of Deloitte consultants to help them strategise for the coming months.

Emenalo, who was a left-back in his playing days, was most recently the ‘chief football executive’ for the entire Saudi Pro League, taking up the position in July 2023.

He departed in the summer of 2026 after three years in the post, where he headed up the ‘Player Acquisition Center of Excellence’ programme.

It was his job to help draw high-profile players to the Middle East in a bid to expand the league’s popularity.

The former Nigeria interrnational now makes a return to England to assist Leicester, but it is believed that Deloitte’s task does not extend to helping blueprint for the sale of the club.

The 2021 FA Cup winners’ owners, the Srivaddhanaprabha family, sought the expertise of Citibank to help them with a prospective sale, putting together a brochure to showcase the club’s assets to send on to investors.

The Foxes were reportedly valued at £200million, although no interested party is thought to have met that figure.
