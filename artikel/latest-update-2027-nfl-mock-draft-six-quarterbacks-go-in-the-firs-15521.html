The number of opportunities to make an impression on NFL talent evaluators this college football season may be as low as two for some prospects. The once vaunted 2027 NFL Draft class no longer appears as strong as it had in the summer, but there are still elite talents at the top.

Sources indicate that in this thought exercise, the assumption is that Texas quarterback Arch Manning and Missouri quarterback Austin Simmons will return to school. The former has not reached his final form, and one would think his retired NFL uncles would encourage him to return to school until he is ready. However, there are still six quarterbacks taken in the first round.

The draft order is based on current win-loss records and strength of schedule was utilized for tiebreakers. It is also worth noting that five teams do not have a first-round pick, but each of those teams makes a pick below the first-round mock draft.

Without further ado, let's kick this off!

The 2027 NFL Draft will take place April 29-May 1 at the National Mall in Washington, D.C. You can find more draft coverage at CBSSports.com, including weekly mock drafts and updated prospect rankings.

Indianapolis traded Josh Downs and Keenan Allen, leaving them essentially with Alec Pierce. The Colts are going to need more help at the position.

One criticism of Los Angeles' draft approach last season was not using a Top-100 pick on an offensive tackle, even though Alaric Jackson had been dealing with off-field problems.

Indiana's Carter Smith could play guard or tackle, which is a quality Green Bay often looks for among its offensive line.

It would not be a surprise if Ben Johnson continued throwing resources at wide receiver or running back, but linebacker may prove to be a bigger need.

Jacksonville addressed safety and the defensive interior at the trade deadline, but cornerback opposite Travis Hunter Jr. is a question mark.
