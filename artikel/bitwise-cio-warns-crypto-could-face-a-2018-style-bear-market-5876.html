📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin plunged to $64,000 on November 15. Risk aversion has increased among some investors, while others see a historical pattern repeating itself. Based on his market analysis, Bitwise chief investment officer Matt Hougan identified that the current, revived crypto downtrend mirrors the 2018 bear market.

In data shared today, Hougan explained that the current market structure resembles the 2018 downtrend, when Bitcoin saw significant corrections before making headway.

Industry observers note that on Nov. 1, Bitcoin dropped below $78k after the Binance hacking incident, which pulled its value to a low of $76,100. The asset further slipped below its $73,000 crucial support, touching $72,727 on November 11.

It has been revealed that pressure on the crypto market intensified further after Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and board member of Nvidia on Nov. 13. The fall below $70,000 last Friday signaled rising selling pressure and declining investor appetite. Currently, BTC trades at $64,923, indicating increased cautiousness among investors as the asset struggles to reclaim its psychological price level at $68,000.

Sources indicate that in his market assessment today, Bitwise CIO admitted the crypto market is under serious selling pressure. He named the 2026 U.S. midterm election setbacks, Binance security breach, and monetary tightening as key drivers for the continuing crypto market corrections.

October’s inflation data came in higher than economists expected, prompting fears that the Fed will continue tightening monetary policy, with odds of interest rate hikes next month high. This anticipation has added pressure on riskier assets, particularly cryptocurrencies, apart from the adverse catalysts mentioned above.

Recent findings suggest that hougan acknowledged that the broader crypto market has entered a phenomenon recognized as “crypto winter.” He warned that the downtrend may persist, comparing it with the 2018 yearlong bear market. According to the Bitwise executive, Bitcoin’s fall appears to be similar to the 2018 bear market, which saw the asset lose a third of its value over time.

Hougan pointed out DeFi as a technological breakthrough that triggered market recovery at the end of 2018. According to him, the current slump may continue for some time as the market awaits the next major breakthrough.
