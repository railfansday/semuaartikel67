Audrie Pelosi found climbing after moving to Baraboo, Wisconsin. It wasn’t long before the quartzite cliffs of Devil’s Lake became a cornerstone of her life. She climbed there, worked there, and eventually became its steward as a guide with Apex Adventure Alliance (AAA), bringing fresh climbers to the same arena in which she’d begun.

In a recent development, “She was an exceptional climbing guide and human being,” Apex Adventure Alliance wrote in a statement to Outside. “She was a force in our community and at our guide service. We all will miss her dearly.”.

Originally from Duluth, Minnesota, Pelosi had long structured her life around her various passions: snowboarding, cycling, and, eventually, an all-encompassing love of climbing and the community around it. Her passion radiated outwards: she volunteered locally and, in 2026, ran for a Wisconsin Climbers Association board seat, hoping to become more involved in stewardship and community building. “I believe in showing up, doing the work, and listening to the community,” Pelosi wrote in her candidate statement.

It has been revealed that “With her friends and community, Audrie was so generous,” noted Garrett Hopkins, AAA’s owner. “That generosity flowed into her climbing, biking, and snowboarding too. When I was living on the road, I knew her couch was always open for a spot to crash if my van was being repaired. She spent countless hours with young climbers in an effort to elevate the safety of the next generation coming up.”.

According to recent reports, pelosi met her husband, Jimmy “The Fox” Robinson, at a snowboard event at Welch Village, Minnesota, and in 2023 the two married atop Mount Bohemia (1,465ft) on Lake Superior. From then on, Pelosi had the nickname “Mrs. The Fox.” One of her friends, Anastasia Exterovich, set up a GoFundMe to raise money for Pelosi’s family in the wake of her passing. She called the pair the “raddest couple.”.

“In my last conversation with Audrie, she joked about selling her handmade bike chain link earrings to anyone who wanted them for ‘free.99,’” Exterovich wrote. “It was such a simple statement made in passing, but it perfectly encapsulated who she was as a person. Even when she didn’t have much, she would give things away freely without hesitation, whether that was earrings, a smile, a laugh, a quick bike tune-up, or a belay.”.

It has been revealed that pelosi passed on May 30, 2026 working as a guide at  Devil’s Lake State Park, teaching a climbing course near the park’s East Bluff. During the course, Pelosi fell from the bluff and suffered fatal injuries. She is survived by her husband, her family, and the broader climbing community in Wisconsin.
