Kai Havertz was self-critical when reflecting on his performances for the German national team ahead of Monday's UEFA Nations League clash with the Netherlands.

The 27-year-old Arsenal forward, who started up front in Friday's 3-1 win over Serbia, told reporters on Sunday:.

"The national team is something completely different from club football. We meet every few months, then play in a major tournament and, if we're being honest, our performances as a team at the last few tournaments have been too negative for anyone to say: 'Kai, you've done brilliantly for the national team so far.'.

It has been revealed that "Ultimately, we need results. I don't think I've got everything wrong here. But I know there is potential and room for improvement.

"I'm the first to hold myself accountable, roll up my sleeves and keep working to turn things around and perform even better for the national team.".

Also speaking ahead of the Netherlands game, Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp defended Havertz amid criticism.

“I've played against Kai many times, and I can tell you that preparing for a game against him is no walk in the park.

“You get a real sense of just how complete a player he is,” Klopp noted, reflecting on his time as Liverpool coach when facing Arsenal.

"You always have to be prepared for him to produce an outstanding performance because he has all the qualities to do so.

Industry observers note that "He's a top player. Mikel Arteta is someone who, as a person and as a coach, is absolutely determined to win.

"And unless several negative scenarios all come together, Kai Havertz starts games for Arsenal.

“The fact that he plays here is not simply because I don't want to leave him out, but because I want to win games,” Klopp added.

Havertz has scored 25 goals and provided 19 assists in 64 games for Germany.
