In a recent development, the best things to do first in Grand Theft Auto 6 are all about money management, unlocking open-world activities, and taking good care of Jason's slithery friend. While there's no right or wrong way to play, these tips and tricks should make your first visit to Leonida, specifically Key Lento, more useful and fun.

Here's how to spend your first hours in GTA 6.

It has been revealed that GTA 6's story introduction takes a while and can't be skipped, so if you'd like to start a second playthrough (in the future) without having to sit through the opening cinematic, a post-prologue save file is handy. The first missions, which mainly serve as tutorials, are hardly the most entertaining parts of the story, so you may want to save your game after they're done. Your first visit to Jason's house would make a great save point.

Recent findings suggest that at the start of your GTA 6 adventure, money is tight. Before you blow it all on weapons and clothes, beware that you'll need your limited funds to meet certain mission objectives, such as saving for the future and buying flowers for your partner. Since there are fewer and less rewarding opportunities to commit crime in the early game compared to mid-game, it's best to save the outrageous spending sprees for later.

According to recent reports, if you die with cash on hand, you will lose it all. So, as soon as you've received some income from the first missions, place it underneath the loose planks in Jason's bedroom. If Jason dies, the money in his stash remains untouched.

It has been revealed that while you should certainly take your time to explore side activities and optional missions in GTA 6, don't do so in the first few hours of playtime. The game takes a while to open up; you can't sell stolen cars, fast travel, or engage in most side activities before you're at least halfway through the first chapter. You won't have many robbery opportunities either. Don't worry, though; the open world will become far more interesting from Chapter 2 onward.

While activities are restricted, you should still keep an eye out for events and NPC encounters as soon as you're set free in Leonida. If you hear someone calling you, notice someone getting arrested, or spot anything else that seems out of the ordinary, be sure to engage with it – it won't add to your main mission progress, but it makes the task more fun.

In a recent development, improving your focus stat in GTA 6 is a bit of a chore, but there are two excellent ways to improve your focus early in the game. The first is feeding Jason's pet snake, whose terrarium is placed near the kitchen in his living room. You get 3% focus each time you feed the snake (limited to once per day). The second activity is scoring points at the basketball hoop below Jason's house. The hoop is near the stairs, and the ball is found somewhere nearby, on the ground. You'll score 2% focus per hit, up to five times. It takes a while for the activity to reset (and gather more focus).

It has been revealed that why should you do this now instead of later? Well, let's just say you won't always live around a snake and a basketball hoop. Besides, building focus as soon as possible is very useful, as the ability to slow down time and shoot weak spots consumes focus.

"Provoke the cops and see what happens" doesn't sound like the best advice, but in GTA 6, understanding the crime system is very useful. The longer it takes to rob people, steal cars, and escape the police, the higher the chance it'll end badly for you. So, start practicing until things like hot-wiring cars, escaping buildings, making U-turns to lose the cops, knocking out guards, and hitting weak spots become second nature.
