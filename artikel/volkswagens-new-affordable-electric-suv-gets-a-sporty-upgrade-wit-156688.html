In a recent development, volkswagen gave its entry-level electric SUV a sporty makeover with GTI-inspired performance. The upcoming ID. Cross R-Line tops the range with added power and style, but there’s more to it than meets the eye.

It has been revealed that the newly introduced Volkswagen ID. Cross R-Line is now available to order in Germany, starting at €37,225 (about $41,000), including VAT.

Sources indicate that while VW’s R-Line is typically just a style and equipment upgrade rather than a true performance upgrade, the range-topping ID. Cross version borrows its electric motor, battery, and chassis from the ID. Polo GTI.

The flagship ID. Cross R-Line model, which will go on sale in March 2027, will use the same 222 hp (166 kW) front-mounted electric motor with up to 290 Nm of torque, which VW mentioned enables it to sprint from 0 to 62 mph (0 to 100 km/h) in 6.9 seconds.

It will also use the same 52 kWh nickel-manganese-cobalt (NMC) battery, providing a WLTP range of up to 266 miles (428 km).

The range-topping version comes with VW’s custom chassis technology, also used in the ID. Polo GTI, which includes an electronically controlled front differential lock, DCC adaptive chassis control, and progressive steering.

As such, the flagship R-Line model is 15 mm lower than the standard ID. Cross. The lower ride height and front differential lock help to improve traction, driving stability, and control, while DCC automatically adjusts to road conditions.

The 222 hp (166 kW) version debuts with an exclusive fresh Traction profile with different drive modes, including Eco, Comfort, Sport, and Individual.

Sources indicate that while pre-sales for the flagship version will open in March 2027, the entry-level ID. Cross R-Line is now available to order in Germany starting at €37,225 (about $41,000), including VAT.

The entry-level version uses a 132 hp (99 kW) electric motor and a 37 kWh battery, providing a WLTP range of up to 195 miles (314 km).

The entry-level version gains “extensive” R-Line standard equipment, including R-Line style bumpers, vertical daytime headlights, and 19″ alloy wheels.

Other standard features include Volkswagen’s interactive ID. Light with LED matrix headlights, Keyless Access, Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC), and a rear-view camera.

It has been revealed that inside, all ID. Cross R-Line models feature sports comfort seats, R-Line trim, and progressive steering as standard. It also gains the Innovision Pro infotainment package with the “IDA” voice assistant.

Volkswagen has yet to reveal prices for the flagship ID. Cross R-Line with the 222 hp (166 kW) and 52 kWh battery, but it will likely be closer to €40,000 ($44,000) when it goes on sale next March. Volkswagen’s ID. Polo GTI electric hot hatch starts at €39,995.

The newly introduced R-Line trim comes after Volkswagen opened orders for the standard entry-level ID. Cross last month, starting at €27,995 ($31,000).
