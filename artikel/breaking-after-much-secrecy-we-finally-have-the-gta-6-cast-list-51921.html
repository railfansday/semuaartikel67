Prior to the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 today, Rockstar had been doing its utmost to play just about every aspect of the game’s production outside of explicitly-revealed details as close to the vest as possible. That’s the whole reason why that little saga of GTA 6 leaks was such a substantial deal, because so much about the game was in a proverbial black box before launch. Now, though, the game is finally in the world’s collective hands, and that means every little itty-bitty detail is readily-available for everyone to obsessively comb through.

One of those details, and arguably one of the most crucial to the production, is the game’s cast of voice actors. Ahead of the game’s release, the vast majority of the cast was shrouded in mystery, with the single exception being veteran actor Stephen Root revealing his role as Brian Heder, though naturally, that didn’t stop the rampant speculation from the precious few soundbites we got in the trailers.

Recent findings suggest that now that the game is out, the full cast list has been revealed via the in-game credits, which you technically don’t even need to finish the game to see. Perhaps unsurprisingly for such a high-profile production, many of the game’s voice actors come from TV and film backgrounds, a testament to Rockstar’s star power magnetism.

According to recent reports, first and foremost, let’s get into our two lead characters, and the most important roles in GTA 6, Jason and Lucia, the duo that had successfully captured hearts and minds before the game was even out. Jason is voiced by Dylan Rourke, a television actor who has had a small spattering of roles since 2013, with some standouts including Modern Family, Grey’s Anatomy, Station 19, and In the Vault.

Sources indicate that meanwhile, Lucia, the series's first female protagonist, is voiced by Manni L. Perez, who has previously provided incidental roles in GTA 5 and GTA Online. Outside of games, she’s had a lot of experience in various crime shows, such as The Blacklist, Chicago P.D., and Law & Order: Special Victims Unit.

Surprisingly, one of the biggest names in the cast aside from the aforementioned Stephen Root is Bobby Moynihan in the role of Jason’s bumbling buddy Cal. Moynihan is a seasoned actor who got his start all the way back in 1996 with roles in live-action and animated film and television like Portlandia, Aqua Teen Hunger Force, and We Bare Bears, though his most decorated gig was various roles on Saturday Night Live since 2008, most notably his recurring character on the “Weekend Upgrade” sketches.

Sources indicate that some other names dropped in the credits include Dre'Quan as Naiqui Macabroad, Orlagh Cassidy as Lori Heder, Brad Carter as Donnie, Mallory Wu as Andy, and rappers Hood Brat and Krissy Celess as Bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad, respectively. You can check out the credits yourself either by beating the game or just checking the in-game settings menu.

If I were a betting man, I’d put money down on most, if not all of the biggest names in this cast making appearances at The Game Awards next month. In fact, Manni L. Perez has already been nominated for Best Performance as Lucia, among the many, many other categories that GTA 6 has been nominated for alongside Game of the Year.

It has been revealed that it’s gonna be a star-studded event, as it often is, of course, but by appearing in the biggest game of the release, quite possibly the entire decade, just about everyone in the GTA 6 cast have just earned some major street cred in the entertainment industry. Obviously, Stephen Root and Bobby Moynihan were already well-established actors, but hopefully, we’ll be seeing a lot more of Dylan Rourke and Manni L. Perez both in and out of the gaming scene going forward.

Industry observers note that you leave a mark like that on entertainment history, and people will start actively seeking you out in every other production you appear in. Hey, maybe they’ll make cameos if we ever get a GTA movie.
