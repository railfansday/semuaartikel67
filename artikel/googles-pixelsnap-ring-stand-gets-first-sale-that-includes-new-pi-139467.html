Google’s Pixelsnap Ring Stand is a simple add-on for Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones and, for Black Friday, it’s got its first sale since adding newly introduced colors alongside the Pixel 11 series.

Industry observers note that pixelsnap Ring Stand launched last year alongside the Pixel 10, taking advantage of the built-in magnets also used for Qi2 charging to give customers an easy kickstand on the back of their device. This year, Google added three new colors to go with the Pixel 11 series including Olive, Canyon, and Obsidian.

And, for Black Friday, they’re all on sale for the first time.

Industry observers note that the Pixelsnap Ring Stand is 20% off on Amazon, and it’s the only place it’s on sale. It’s also the first sale that includes the latest colors. In fact, last year’s “Moonstone” is a few dollars more than the new colors right now.
