Recent findings suggest that the whole world has been counting down to Grand Theft Auto VI finally being released, and now that it’s here, people are calling out of work and school to play. One person who wasted no time? Tinashe, who dove straight into the video game for her fans through a hilarious livestream. The singer hopped on X right after midnight, tweeting: “Streaming GTA on twitch lets gooo.”.

Once she was locked in, she got to work. The stream captures her cracking fans up by trying to make an epic getaway by flying an airplane and hysterically crashing it into the ground. She also screams her head off as she gets in a shootout but promptly runs out of ammo and has to rely on hand-to-hand combat. “I have no bullets!” she screams. “Holy shit, I’m going to have to beat the fuck out of them myself.”.

Sources indicate that at one point, during another shootout, she gets so stressed out that she starts singing the bridge of her own hit “Melatonin,” yelping, “Fucking with me, hope you know I’m what you asked for, asked for! Tinashe is a massive gamer; in the past, she’s played a lot of Call of Duty: Warzone under the name SniperNashe and streamed it on Twitch and YouTube for gamer fans. She’s also talked about being a fan of Mortal Kombat and Diablo IV. Plus, she’s chronically online — she was all over X earlier this month to clap back at conservatives during the midterms — so it makes sense she’d be celebrating GTA VI‘s big arrival on social media with everyone else.

She wasn’t the only celebrity excited about the video game: After several days of sold-out shows in the U.K., Noah Kahan kicked back after his shows and stayed up wreaking havoc on Vice City while his wife was asleep. F1 champion Lewis Hamilton and actress Millie Bobby Brown also shared how excited they were for the game’s sixth edition.
