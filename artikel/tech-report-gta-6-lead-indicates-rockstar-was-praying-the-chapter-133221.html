Grand Theft Auto 6 producer Rob Nelson has admitted that he was “praying” the game’s major plot twist in Chapter 1 didn’t get spoiled ahead of time.

According to recent reports, leading up to the release of GTA 6, it felt like Rockstar had shown us plenty of what the game was about. We had a general idea of Jason and Lucia’s circumstances in Leonida, detailed reports on the game’s expanded mechanics, and enough footage of the world for some dedicated fans to piece together a mapping project.

In a recent development, and, of course, there were the infamous Cyberleek leaks that showed much more than Rockstar ever wanted us to. Now that the game is actually out, though, it’s painfully clear just how little we knew about GTA 6, which all starts in the game’s opening hour when perhaps its biggest plot point is revealed after years of hiding it away.

I’m sure anyone interested is already well past this point, but we’ll be discussing GTA 6’s main Chapter 1 twist below.

I am, of course, talking about the major reveal that Jason and Lucia have a child together, Tomas, who is taken away from them both after Lucia is arrested early on in the game. It’s as much of a surprise to Jason as it is to the players, as Rockstar somehow managed to keep it out of all the marketing and even didn’t get it ruined by Cyberleek.

Sources indicate that VGC’s Jordan Middler recently had the chance to chat to Grand Theft Auto 6 producer Rob Nelson about that surprise moment. Nelson confirmed that Rockstar was “never” going to reveal that plot point, and that he was “praying it stays under wraps” before the game’s launch, especially with “everything that we’ve gone through”.

Nelson is surely referring to just how many leaks GTA 6 was hit with, which somehow didn't result in such a massive point being spoiled.

It has been revealed that nelson went on to say that the twist is meant to shock the player, especially with how much everyone was speculating on Jason and Lucia’s relationship. Like with many of GTA 6’s elements, it’s also meant to take the game and your actions “in a completely different context”, and give you more to aim for than just money.

It has been revealed that of course, while most players were surprised by Jason and Lucia being parents, it’s fair to say that there was at least some speculation about it. Many thought Ryan Gosling’s The Place Beyond the Pines, which has a similar leading duo with a child, was an inspiration point, but I don’t think anyone was certain Rockstar would follow through on such a big story focus.
