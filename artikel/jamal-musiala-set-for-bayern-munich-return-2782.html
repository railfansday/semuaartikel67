Jamal Musiala is set to return to Bayern Munich’s matchday squad for Friday’s Bundesliga clash against Cologne, according to Sport1.

The 23-year-old has been sidelined since suffering a torn muscle fibre during a warm-up prior to Germany’s UEFA Nations League game against the Netherlands on 24 September.

Musiala has featured just three times in the Bundesliga for Germany’s record champions this season, returning a goal and assist, while also scoring in his only UEFA Champions League game in 2026/27.

It goes without saying that this is providing that Musiala, who has been training for the last 10 days, doesn’t suffer any setbacks in training during the week.

Bayern could, however, be without Alphonso Davies. The 25-year-old sustained an injury while on international duty with Canada, lasting just six minutes of his nation’s 3-2 CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final win over Guatemala after picking up an injury on 13 November.

Speaking to Canada’s One Soccer, Davies noted: “At the beginning of the game everything seemed good. I fell down. They’re analysing the footage. I think my back twisted when I slipped. I tried to get up as quickly as possible to block the shot. As soon as I raised my leg and put it down to block the shot, I felt something […] Hopefully it’s not too bad.”.
