📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that the Senate confirmed Justice Joseph Story to the Supreme Court on this day in 1811. When he joined three months later at age 32, he became the youngest justice in Supreme Court history, as we noted in a Closer Look in May.

On Tuesday, the Supreme Court indicated that several notable petitions for review will be considered at the justices’ next private conference on Friday, Dec. 4. The disputes address such issues as the Trump administration’s ban on transgender servicemembers and its approach to appointing acting U.S. attorneys. For more on the conference, see the On Site section below.

It has been revealed that the December argument session will begin on Monday, Nov. 30.
