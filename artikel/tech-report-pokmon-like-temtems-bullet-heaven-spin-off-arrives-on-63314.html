Sources indicate that crema Games' Temtem was one of the better Pokémon-likes we had played when it arrived on Switch back in 2022. The dev released its final upgrade a couple of years back, but soon, we'll get a chance to play its Survivors-style spinoff.

Temtem: Swarm launches on Switch on 21st January 2027 after coming to Steam back in April. An initial glance made us think this was Crema's take on Pokémon Unite, but on closer inspection, it's far more Vampire Survivors.

In a recent development, this one sees you choose your Pokémon Tem fighter and face off against swarms of corrupted creatures to survive for as long as you can. There are mini-bosses to tackle along the way, and a stream of upgrades and abilities to keep you on your toes as you progress through runs.

Excitingly, the Switch version will launch with full cross-platform co-op, so you'll be able to team up with pals on PC or PS5 right from the jump.

Here's a brief rundown of the game's key features and a handful of screenshots:.

It has been revealed that team Up to Survive the Swarm: Battle relentless Tem swarms solo or with up to 3 friends in intense co-op! Strategize, share resources, and unleash overpowered synergies to defeat bosses. Booming Boss Battles: Conquer unique stages by strategizing against challenging mini-bosses and epic stage bosses. Face iconic Temtem like Gharunder, Yowlar, and Nessla in hand-crafted stages. Unleash Unstoppable Ultimates: Unleash devastating attacks, defensive abilities, or map-clearing power-ups with each Tems Ultimate ability. Master timing and unleash these incredible powers to turn the tide of battle. Evolve to Endure: Earn XP, level up your Temtem, and unlock powerful final evolutions. Each evolution grants massive boosts to skills and strength for ultimate survival. Discover All the Tems: Collect and capture every Temtem to unlock powerful Traits and unique Ultimates. Master each Temtem in solo and co-op to overcome any challenge. Revive and Thrive: No fear of defeat! Support fallen teammates in Ghost Mode until they can revive you to rejoin the fight. Online co-op ensures the adventure never ends, even after a tough encounter.

We had a good enough time with Temtem on Switch back in 2022, and the Survivors-like spin-off is an interesting idea on the surface. A 'Mostly Positive' review streak on Steam has us hopeful that it'll be worth a look in the early days of 2027.
