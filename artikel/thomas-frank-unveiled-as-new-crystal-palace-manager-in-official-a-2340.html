Crystal Palace have appointed Thomas Frank as replacement for the sacked Pierre Sage.

The Selhurst Park club with an official announcement on Monday.

Crystal Palace only though the second Premier League club to change manager during this 2026-27 season, as Manchester United beat them by dismissing Michael Carrick and appointing Mauricio Pochettino as their new manager.

Thomas Frank did a brilliant job for years at Brentford before becoming yet another manager to fail at Tottenham.

He heads into Crystal Palace to find a team rock bottom of the Premier League with only seven points after ten matches.

Recent findings suggest that the official announcement beginning: ‘Crystal Palace Football Club are pleased to confirm the appointment of Thomas Frank as Manager. The 53-year-old Dane has signed a deal to become the Eagles’ boss until June 2029. Frank possesses a wealth of Premier League experience, having previously managed at both Brentford and Tottenham Hotspur. He made an outstanding impact at the former, taking them from the Championship to the Premier League after winning promotion in the 2020/21 season. Frank then stabilised Brentford in the top-flight, with league finishes of 13th, 9th and 16th across the following three seasons.’.

The official release going on to say: ‘In 2024/25, Frank once again secured a top-half finish at the Gtech Community Stadium, and guided the Bees to a run of just one defeat in their final eight league games. Frank won plaudits for his final season in West London, when only four other teams scored more goals than Brentford, and was subsequently nominated for the Premier League Manager of the Year award, before moving across London to join Tottenham Hotspur. Prior to his time in England, Frank managed his country at various youth levels, as well as Brøndby in his native Denmark, leading them to consecutive top-four finishes and qualification for the UEFA Europa League.’.

Crystal Palace Chairman Steve Parish: “I’m delighted to be able to welcome Thomas to Crystal Palace. He has a proven track record of both creating winning teams and improving players, making him the perfect fit for our club. I’m confident Thomas’ extensive experience in the Premier League will help us achieve our goals for the rest of the season.”.

Thomas Frank: “It’s an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic Football Club. I’m really, really looking forward to it. Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I’m really looking forward to meeting them.”.

According to recent reports, the official statement finishing with: ‘Frank’s coaching staff will include current Assistant Manager, Paddy McCarthy, and Goalkeeping Coach, Andy Quy, the latter having previously worked with him at Brentford. Additional members of Frank’s backroom team will be confirmed in due course.’.
