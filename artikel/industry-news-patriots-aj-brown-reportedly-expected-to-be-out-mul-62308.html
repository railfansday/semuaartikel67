Industry News: Patriots' A.J. Brown reportedly expected to be out multiple weeks with hamstring injury

New England Patriots receiver A.J. Brown is expected to be sidelined for multiple weeks due to a hamstring injury, according to ESPN's Mike Reiss and Adam Schefter.

Brown, 29, injured his hamstring during Week 10's 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich. He got hurt in the fourth quarter and did not return, finishing with two catches (on six targets) for 27 yards.

According to recent reports, in his first season with the Patriots, Brown has already missed six of the team's 10 games. First, he sustained a high ankle sprain in the season opener and sat out four games while on injured reserve. Then he was sidelined for an additional two games due to a strained knee.

The news on Brown comes as the Patriots also officially announced that quarterback Drake Maye suffered a fractured right fibula against the Lions and could miss the remainder of the regular season.

Latest England dropped to 5-5 following the defeat versus Detroit and has lost three of its past four games. The Patriots have a bye for Week 11, then visit the Los Angeles Chargers (1-8) in Week 12. New England is still in the AFC playoff race, just one game behind the Cleveland Browns (6-4) for the conference's third wild-card berth.

In a recent development, with Brown injured, the Patriots' receiver depth chart consists of Mack Hollins, DeMario Douglas, Efton Chism III and Kyle Williams. Romeo Doubs could join them after the bye week when he's eligible to be activated from IR. Douglas is the team's leading pass-catcher among wideouts with 32 receptions for 438 yards and four touchdowns.

Brown was acquired from the Philadelphia Eagles during the offseason, trading a 2028 first-round pick and 2027 fifth-round selection. He registered 1,003 receiving yards last season with the Eagles (his lowest total in four seasons). However, Brown had reportedly become frustrated with quarterback Jalen Hurts — and his role in the Eagles' offense.

Philadelphia traded Brown before a three-year, $96 million extension kicked in that takes him through the 2029 season.

According to recent reports, brown is in his eighth NFL season, beginning his career with the Tennessee Titans. He was the team's second-round selection (No. 51 overall) out of Ole Miss and played with the team for three seasons.

Industry observers note that the 6-foot-1 receiver was dealt to the Eagles during the 2022 NFL Draft for first- and fourth-round picks. He subsequently received a four-year, $100 million extension from Philadelphia.

A three-time Pro Bowler and two-time second-team All-Pro selection, Brown has 535 career receptions for 8,157 yards and 56 touchdowns.
