In a recent development, WACO, Texas, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- What causes neck and shoulder pain, and how do structural limitations and functional limitations influence treatment? That question is answered in a HelloNation article featuring insights from Andrew Yakubik, D.C., of Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center in Waco, Texas.

The HelloNation article explains that neck and shoulder pain often develop together because these areas share muscles, joints, and movement patterns. When one area is affected, it can swiftly influence the other, making it notable to understand the underlying causes before beginning neck and shoulder pain treatment.

The article describes structural limitations as physical restrictions within the joints or spine that limit normal movement. These may include joint stiffness, misalignment, or degenerative changes that reduce the range of motion. Individuals experiencing structural limitations may notice persistent stiffness or difficulty turning the head or lifting the arm without discomfort.

Functional limitations are different and relate to how muscles and surrounding tissues work together during movement. The article notes that even when the joint structure is intact, poor coordination or muscle imbalance can lead to inefficient movement patterns. Over time, this can place added stress on certain areas and contribute to ongoing neck and shoulder pain.

The HelloNation article emphasizes that effective neck and shoulder pain treatment often requires addressing both structural limitations and functional limitations. Focusing on only one aspect may leave underlying issues unresolved, increasing the likelihood of recurring discomfort.

Posture is highlighted as a key factor in the development of both types of limitations. The article explains that forward head posture and rounded shoulders can place continuous strain on the neck and upper back. This strain can lead to joint restriction and muscle imbalance, reinforcing patterns that contribute to chronic neck and shoulder pain.

Muscle imbalance is another notable factor discussed in the article. When some muscles become overactive while others weaken, movement becomes less efficient and more stressful on the joints. This imbalance can reduce range of motion and increase discomfort during daily activities, making posture correction and targeted care essential parts of treatment.

It has been revealed that the article notes that a proper clinical evaluation is necessary to determine whether structural limitations, functional limitations, or both are contributing to the condition. This evaluation may include assessing the range of motion, observing movement patterns, and identifying areas of weakness or restriction.

Treatment approaches often combine multiple strategies to address both structure and function. The HelloNation article explains that joint-focused care may help improve mobility and reduce stiffness, while corrective exercises can help retrain muscles and restore more balanced movement patterns. This coordinated approach supports more consistent progress.

Consistency is also emphasized as an important part of neck and shoulder pain treatment. The article explains that improving movement patterns and joint function takes time, especially when habits have developed over long periods. A structured blueprint that addresses both structural limitations and functional limitations can help support steady and lasting improvement.

It has been revealed that the article concludes that understanding the difference between structural and functional issues provides valuable insight into why neck and shoulder pain develop. By focusing on both aspects, care becomes more precise and better aligned with the root causes of discomfort, leading to improved mobility and long-term outcomes.
