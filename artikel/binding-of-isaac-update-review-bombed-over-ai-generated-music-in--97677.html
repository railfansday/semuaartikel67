Recent findings suggest that the Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ was a free patch to the beloved roguelike that arrived on PC in 2024, but introduced on console earlier this week in a head-to-head matchup against Grand Theft Auto 6. The marketing stunt may have been a success, but AI-generated music tracks discovered in a special newly introduced Vice City mode have turned the launch into a nightmare.

While Repentance+ came to PS5, Xbox Series X/S, and Switch 2 this week, the Steam version of the game also got a free update. “Be sure to get in and play today’s Daily Run for a very special crime-packed surprise. Enter the Basement, and go out in a glorious blaze of bullets and vehicular homicide!” read the patch notes on on Valve’s storefront. Players could enter the seed “V1CE BSMT” into the roguelike before starting their run to launch a GTA-inspired version with special weapons and music.

Earlier today, however, Reddit user ShweetsStuff posted a comprehensive breakdown of the upcoming music tracks alleging they were made with Suno, an AI-based music generation tool. “The new upgrade includes 14 music files for the run; seven full songs, plus some derivatives (intros/outros),” the wrote. The post continued:.

Many have blamed Nicalis, the publisher and co-developer of the Repentance+ expansion, in particular. Complaints about ongoing bugs, latency, and quality control issues have seemingly been building for some time. The latest mishap results in a review-bomb of the expansion’s Steam page, which currently sits at an abysmal user rating of just 9 percent positive. “AI generated music in GTA mode; Edmund please take back control of the game,” reads one of the latest Steam reviews. “Not to mention there’s like single digit bug fixes in a year+ long awaited revision with no other changes.”.

The AI music was news to Edmund McMillen, the game’s creator. “Woke up to a shit show, obviously I had no idea it featured generated music in the daily run, looking into how that happened and getting it removed, stay tuned,” he posted earlier today on X. An emergency patch has since gone out to remove the AI-generated music as well as address some other versions with the latest version of the expansion, but Nicalis has yet to take responsibility for the use of AI or explained how it made it into the game.
