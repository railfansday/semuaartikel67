Prepare for a showdown Get as much ammo as you can, and choose your spot for the last stand. The hunter's dog will be a pest, so deal with it first. It's easy to spot, with a red light on its collar. Trees also make for good cover against the hunter, so once he's in range, start nailing him with shots to critical areas. Once he stumbles, unload a few shotgun rounds and finish him off. Collect your rewards Once the hunter is down, search his body for the Island-Poacher FF-50 sniper rifle and ammo for it. Head west to the boathouse to reclaim your personal items and $15,000 in cash.
