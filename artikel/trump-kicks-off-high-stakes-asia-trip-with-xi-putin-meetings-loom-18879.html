Former State Department senior advisor Christian Whiton joins 'Fox & Friends Weekend' to discuss President Donald Trump's upcoming summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Whiton breaks down the potential for nuclear arms agreements, ongoing trade negotiations and the shifting geopolitical dynamics between the U.S., Russia and China.

President Donald Trump is in Asia for historic talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin this week, with nuclear arms reductions and Iran on the agenda and Trump signaling Monday that he also plans to raise Russia's war in Ukraine.

Trump's trip began Tuesday in the Philippines at the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) summit before he travels to Shenzhen, China, for Thursday's meeting with Xi and Putin on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit.

The rare gathering comes amid simmering U.S.-China trade tensions and ongoing conflicts involving Russia and Iran, with Trump signaling aims to raise denuclearization and efforts to end the fighting.

It has been revealed that at the ASEAN summit, Trump is expected to sign a joint statement with Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul announcing progress toward a trade agreement. The president is also set to meet with Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. before departing for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen, China.

TRUMP TO CONFRONT XI AT HIGH-STAKES SUMMIT OVER CHINA BACKING FOR IRAN, RUSSIA.

President Donald Trump shakes hands with Chinese President Xi Jinping as they hold a bilateral meeting at Gimhae International Airport, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit, in Busan, South Korea, on Oct. 30, 2025. (Reuters/Evelyn Hockstein).

The president has also raised the possibility of meeting with North Korean dictator Kim Jong-un during his visit to China this week, telling reporters Monday that talks could take place.

When reached for additional comment about the floated meeting, the White House referred Fox News Digital to Trump's remarks to reporters on Monday.

Trump is expected to discuss Iran and Russia's ongoing war in Ukraine with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin during Thursday's highly-anticipated trilateral meeting, despite U.S. officials previously telling ABC News that Ukraine likely would not be on the agenda.

Trump notably told reporters on Nov. 8 that he intends to discuss denuclearization and ending the war in Iran during the trilateral meeting, DW News reported.

"We'll talk a lot of things, including denuclearization, but we'll be talking a lot of things. We're trying to end the war in Iran," the president said at the time.

From right to left, President Donald Trump, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick meet with Chinese President Xi Jinping at Gimhae International Airport in Busan, South Korea, Thursday, Oct. 30, 2025. (AP Photo/Mark Schiefelbein).

Recent findings suggest that trump's trip also comes amid a scandal involving Nvidia, the American tech giant whose powerful AI chips are at the center of growing tensions between the U.S. and China.

Industry observers note that nvidia's former CEO, Jensen Huang, resigned last week following reports of an affair with a woman suspected by U.S. intelligence officials of sharing information with Beijing, raising fresh concerns about Chinese espionage and access to sensitive American technology.

According to recent reports, trump cast doubt on the espionage allegations Monday, telling reporters he was unsure whether the woman was a Chinese spy. He also said he would be open to Huang returning as CEO.

TRUMP-XI REUNION COMES WITH UNFINISHED BUSINESS — AND A BIGGER BATTLE LOOMING.

President Donald Trump greets Russian President Vladimir Putin on the tarmac after they arrived at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson in Anchorage, Alaska, on August 15, 2025. (Photo by Andrew Cabellero-Reynolds/AFP).

The president will be joined by several high-profile figures during his Asia trip, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who is accompanying Trump to the Philippines and China this week, according to Rubio's public schedule.

According to recent reports, tesla CEO Elon Musk is also expected to join Trump for part of his trip to China for the APEC summit, U.S. officials confirmed to CNBC on Friday.

Rep. John Moolenaar, R-Mich., chairman of the House Select Committee on Strategic Competition between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party, told CBS News’ "Face the Nation" in an interview on Sunday that he does not anticipate any agreements related to the reduction of nuclear weapons will be made this week.
