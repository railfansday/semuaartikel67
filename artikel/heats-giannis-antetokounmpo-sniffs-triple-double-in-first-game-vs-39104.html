More than four months after the news broke that Miami Heat substantial man Bam Adebayo punched Milwaukee Bucks guard Tyler Herro, Adebayo delivered another blow against Herro — this one in the form of a vindicating 21 first-half points in their first game between one another.

During a Wednesday night showdown rich with storylines, Adebayo at one point scored nine in a row and connected on a trio of 3-pointers over the opening two quarters.

Recent findings suggest that of course, all the while, Giannis Antetokounmpo was facing the team he was synonymous with for 13 seasons. And the matchup was taking place in Miami's Kaseya Center, which doubled as "Vice City" on the eve of the highly anticipated nationwide release of Grand Theft Auto VI.

Industry observers note that antetokounmpo played the facilitator role with nine assists and still managed 27 points in the Heat's 137-130 victory over the Bucks. Adebayo finished with 26 points. Herro, meanwhile, was booed throughout and clocked out with just eight points on 4-of-11 shooting.

Sources indicate that miami (9-6) has now won six consecutive games after a 3-6 start. Milwaukee (1-14), on the other hand, remains dead last in the East.

Herro is a Milwaukee native, but Miami is where he began his NBA career. In that way, Wednesday was a homecoming for him. The same could be said for Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware and Kasparas Jakučionis, who also started their NBA journeys with the Heat before being shipped off to the Bucks in the Antetokounmpo trade.

The blockbuster deal also delivered the Bucks the 13th overall pick in this year's draft — which they used to select Tennessee forward Nate Ament — as well as future draft considerations, including two more first-round picks.

In addition to Antetokounmpo, the Heat received Bobby Portis, who became a fan favorite in Milwaukee while helping the "Greek Freak" lead the franchise to its first NBA championship in 50 years back in 2021.

The end of Antetokounmpo's tenure with the Bucks was dominated by drama, particularly as the two-time league MVP and 10-time All-Star feuded with the organization over his health.

Miami gave him a fresh start, and the Antetokounmpo-piloted Heat are taking off under longtime head coach Erik Spoelstra.

Herro's Bucks are on the other end of the NBA spectrum, clearly entrenched in a Taylor Jenkins-directed rebuild. Herro heard the jeers of Miami fans over the course of the night.

Even though he won NBA Sixth Man of the Year during the 2021-22 campaign and earned All-Star recognition with the Heat in the 2024-25 season, his reputation in Miami took a hit this offseason.

Critical commentary Herro shared about Adebayo on social media led to the infamous physical altercation between the former teammates in Las Vegas ahead of Summer League action. They shared the floor together in Miami from 2019-26. In that span, the Heat made a pair of NBA Finals appearances.

But there was ostensibly no love lost by the end of that seven-season stretch. As reported in July by the Miami Herald, around that time, Herro posted a graphic on Instagram that listed the league's worst midrange shooters last season. Antetokounmpo topped that unfortunate leaderboard at 26.8%, and Adebayo came in 10th at 35.9%, per the Herald.

Plus, screenshots circulated of a direct message conversation, in which comments seemingly questioning Adebayo's play and contract were attributed to Herro online.

Herro noted shortly after the dustup that he wanted to "move on" from the incident, except it was top of mind for everyone watching Wednesday's game.

Adebayo stated afterward that he didn't get to talk to Herro but that he's not someone who holds grudges. He noted that he's "on to the next thing.".

"I feel like I got great karma," Adebayo added, via Will Manso of WPLG Local 10 News.

In a recent development, adebayo's nine straight points arrived in the second quarter. He capped that surge by stepping in front of a pass from Jaquez and turning the steal into a two-handed flush that staked Miami to a 48-28 advantage.

The Heat were in control from the jump. They went 22 of 48 from 3, including 13 of 28 in the first half. Klay Thompson continued his hot streak with four triples and 13 points.

Recent findings suggest that antetokounmpo orchestrated several of the team's 3-pointers. His dribble-drive penetration set the stage for kickouts, most notably a no-look dish that rewarded Adebayo early on.

Industry observers note that antetokounmpo's first points didn't come until the 5:11 mark of the first quarter. His first field goal gave the Heat their 70th point. In the process, though, he had an effect that extended far beyond his scoring.

Maybe his most impressive moment was when he rose up to deny Bucks rookie guard Brayden Burries with a two-handed rejection at the rim late in the second quarter.

In the third quarter, Antetokounmpo swung a shrewd shovel pass to Andrew Wiggins, who promptly cashed in from long range.

Then Antetokounmpo slammed home a lob from Adebayo, showing off what they can do in the two-man game.

Sources indicate that antetokounmpo stacked 14 rebounds and ended up an assist shy of a triple-double. He told reporters postgame that he didn't think it felt weird facing Milwaukee.

In a recent development, "Look, I think since I was a kid, when I play basketball, I try to lock in as much as I can," Antetokounmpo said, per The Athletic's Eric Nehm. "People that have been around me know how I operate on game days. I try not to say much. I try not to interact as much. And the only thing on my mind is, 'How can I help my team win and how can I do my job?' It doesn't matter who I play.

According to recent reports, "… I could have been playing against my four brothers and my two sons. It doesn't matter. I try to do my best and try to do the things that I've worked on and try to have fun. It doesn't matter what uniform I go against.".

His old team made made things look closer than they actually were down the stretch. The Bucks were paced by Kyle Kuzma (26 points), Burries (20) and Jakučionis (20).

Milwaukee is a ways from competing for a playoff spot again, whereas Miami is shaping up to be a threat in the Eastern Conference, just like the Bucks were all those years with Antetokounmpo.

The franchises are linked because of this summer's trade and what happened in its aftermath.

And on Wednesday, the Heat led by as many as 30 points, as Antetokounmpo and Adebayo flexed their value.
