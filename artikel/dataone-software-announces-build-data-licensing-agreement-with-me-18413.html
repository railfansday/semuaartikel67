DataOne's VIN Decoding Now Incorporates OEM Build Data for Mercedes-Benz Vehicles.

Recent findings suggest that BEVERLY, Mass., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DataOne Software, a Dominion Enterprises manufacturer and a leading provider of automotive data and software solutions, today announced a new OEM build data licensing agreement with Mercedes-Benz USA (MBUSA). This agreement enables DataOne's VIN decoding services to provide OEM-sourced, option-level data for MBUSA's complete portfolio of vehicles, which includes Mercedes-Benz, Mercedes-AMG, Mercedes-Maybach and Mercedes-Benz Vans.

Sources indicate that dataOne customers will now have the option to utilize Mercedes-Benz build data when decoding a vehicle with DataOne's VIN Decoder API. OEM build data is instrumental in identifying all the optional features and equipment present on the vehicle, including transmission, color, and trim level, in addition to factory-installed options, marketing packages, and safety features.

According to recent reports, "We are extremely excited to announce this data collaboration with Mercedes-Benz USA, which will provide immediate benefits to our customer base across the automotive industry," states DataOne Software President Jake Maki. "DataOne users are now empowered to more easily identify the innovative vehicle technology and customization incorporated into Mercedes-Benz vehicles and to utilize this insight to better support their organization's needs.".

DataOne Software's option-level VIN decoding returns both the manufacturer-specific terminology as well as DataOne's standard vehicle descriptors. The manufacturer-specific terminology is optimized for use in generating OEM marketing-compliant vehicle descriptions, while DataOne's standard vehicle descriptors make this information easy to integrate and use across all brands.

For more information about obtaining Mercedes-Benz build data through DataOne's VIN decoder web service, contact DataOne at 877.438.8467 or e-mail [email protected].

DataOne Software (www.dataonesoftware.com) is a premier automotive data source providing the data and software solutions needed to power modern businesses across all segments of the automotive industry. DataOne's comprehensive data solutions cover everything from VIN decoding and vehicle descriptions to specifications, optional equipment, and market pricing. With the acquisition of Cross-Sell in 2026, DataOne has expanded our product offering to include PII-free vehicle sales and registration data. Our high-quality solutions are delivered with industry-leading support, making them easy to integrate and scale.

Mercedes-Benz USA (MBUSA), headquartered in Atlanta, is responsible for the distribution, marketing and customer service for all Mercedes-Benz products in the United States. MBUSA offers drivers the most diverse lineup in the luxury segment with 18 model lines ranging from the sporty GLA SUV to the flagship S-Class and the dynamic all-electric vehicles from Mercedes-Benz.
