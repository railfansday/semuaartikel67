XRP has spent a week unable to get back above $1. In November 2026, the token hit a two-year low of $0.895, and on Nov. 20, it was trading near $0.96. XRP is down 27.7% in November and about 51% since January, according to CryptoRank data.

Sources indicate that the last time XRP lost more than 28% in a month was February 2025, when the token fell 29.3%. That month, the crypto market was also hit by a $1.5 billion hack of the Bybit exchange on Feb. 21.

XRP did not recover right away. From March to June 2025, the token gained about 4.5% in total, according to CryptoRank. In July 2025, XRP jumped 35%, and on July 18, 2025, it set its all-time high of $3.65. On the same day, U.S. President Donald Trump signed the GENIUS Act, the country's first stablecoin law. XRP is now 73% below that record.

Recent findings suggest that in November 2021, XRP lost 10%, and it fell another 17% in December and 25.6% in January 2022. In November 2022, the month of the FTX collapse, the token dropped 11.8%, and it lost 16.9% in December. XRP finished 2022 down about 59% and gained 20.1% in January 2023.

According to CryptoRank, XRP's median return is -10.2% in November and -4.07% in December.

According to recent reports, soSoValue data shows spot XRP ETFs have lost about $26 million to outflows since the start of November. About $19.1 million of that amount left the funds in the week ending Nov. 13.

On Nov. 19, Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said on CNBC that investors had been selling crypto to buy shares in big listings. "People are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included — Bitcoin, XRP — and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Anthropic IPO. What we saw after the SpaceX IPO was a rally in the crypto markets. I think you'll see that happen here as well," he said.
