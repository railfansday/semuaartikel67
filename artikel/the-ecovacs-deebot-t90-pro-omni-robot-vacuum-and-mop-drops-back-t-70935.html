Sources indicate that the Deebot T90 Pro Omni pairs 30,000Pa of suction with anti-tangle brushes designed to lift embedded dirt, crumbs, and pet hair from carpets and hard floors without getting clogged. When it switches to mopping, it ditches flat spinning pads for a 10.6-inch pressurized roller mop that rinses itself with fresh water in real time, scrubbing away sticky messes instead of smearing dirty streaks around your kitchen. The roller also extends 0.6 inches past the body to clean up against baseboards and corners, and lifts 0.6 inches into the air so it will not dampen your rugs when transitioning onto carpet.

Sources indicate that the included Omni station handles nearly all the messy upkeep on its own. It also uses adaptive charging to top up the battery during wash cycles so it can finish large, multi-room homes in a single run.
