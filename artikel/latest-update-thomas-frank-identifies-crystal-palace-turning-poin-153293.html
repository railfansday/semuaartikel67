Thomas Frank believes his first win as Crystal Palace manager can be a “turning point” in the club’s season.

Palace sit bottom of the Premier League table but tasted victory for the first time in Frank’s tenure with a 2-1 win over 10-man Real Sociedad in his second game in charge.

The Eagles came from behind after trailing to a second-half goal by Chelsea loanee Mamadou Sarr.

But Jean-Philippe Mateta levelled from the penalty spot after Jon Martin was shown a second yellow card and sent off for the side sat fifth in La Liga.

Palace then scored their eventual winner through substitute Dwight McNeil but almost threw it away when Dean Henderson was required to make a last-gasp save after Yeremy Pino had seen the Eagles’ second spot-kick of the night saved.

Palace’s victory came five days after their 3-2 defeat at Coventry in Frank’s first match in charge.

“In the second half I felt we kept the ball much better,” Frank mentioned after his side’s victory on Thursday.

“What I really liked was the belief from the players. It is so important to keep doing the right things and keep believing.”.

Palace were guilty of giving the ball away in poor errors and handing Sociedad some cheap chances despite eventually closing out a victory.

Industry observers note that frank noted: “We should just control that game. But we probably don’t have the confidence right now.

It has been revealed that asked whether the win against Sociedad can be a turning point in a so-far difficult season for the south Londoners, Frank replied: “I think it could be. But we need to follow it up, of course, with a good performance against Hull on Sunday. But there were a lot of positives from this game.”.
