Werder Bremen host basement dwellers Borussia Mönchengladbach in Matchday 11’s curtain-raiser on Friday night as Daniel Thioune’s charges look to continue their excellent form.

Recent findings suggest that establishing the Weserstadion as a fortress, Bremen have won four and drawn one of their home games – scoring 14 goals, while keeping successive clean sheets in their last two games. Their latest result at home being last weekend’s 4-0 thrashing on Hamburg in the Nordderby.

According to recent reports, in total, Thioune – who recently signed a contract extension – has guided his team to five wins, three draws and two defeats from their opening ten games, which places Bremen in sixth. A win tonight will move the Grün-Weißen above Bayer Leverkusen.

In a recent development, marco Grüll is sidelined with a calf injury, but could “possibly [return] even next week,” says Thioune, who also offered an enhancement on Justin Njinmah: “At the moment, I’m very concerned and not convinced he’ll be able to support us. He did, though, leave the door open for a potential start.

With 10 goals in 10 Bundesliga games in 2026/27, Niclas Füllkrug will look to add to his tally against the Bundesliga’s joint-worst defence this season.

Karl Hein – Arthur, Amos Pieper, Marco Friedl, Olivier Deman – Ludovit Reis, Youri Regeer – Eren Dinkci, Chuki, Justin Njinmah – Niclas Füllkrug.
