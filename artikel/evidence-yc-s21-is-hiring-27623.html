Evidence (YC S21) Is Hiring

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Evidence is building the fresh standard for business intelligence, designed for a world where humans and AI agents work together on analytics.

At the foundation is Evidence Core, our open source framework for building reports and data products as code. On top of it, we're building Evidence Studio: a complete enterprise business intelligence platform combining an AI analytics agent with access control, enterprise security, and governance.

In a recent development, thousands of organizations use Evidence, from startups to Fortune 100 companies. We've built a product people love, a substantial open source community, and a growing enterprise business.

We're now looking for someone to help us bring Evidence to a much larger audience.

You'll work directly with our cofounder and CEO to build Evidence's growth engine. We're a small team, and you'll have enormous ownership over how we reach fresh customers.

Our goal is to build repeatable systems that consistently generate qualified opportunities for our team.

According to recent reports, there's a lot to work with. Our large open source community (7,000+ GitHub stars) and growing customer base give you a head start: an established audience, a steady stream of new product capabilities, and interesting customer stories waiting to be told.

Some of the things you'll work on:.

You'll be encouraged to experiment, measure results, and double down on what works. We care much more about generating real customer interest than producing perfect strategies.

We move promptly, and you'll start making an impact right away.

By the end of your first month, you'll have shipped real work, learned from the results, and started building repeatable processes for reaching newly introduced customers.

This is an opportunity to build a growth function from the ground up, working directly with the founders of a growing software firm.

If this sounds like you, we'd like to hear from you.

Evidence is the leading BI-as-code platform and the best way to run business intelligence with AI agents.

In a recent development, evidence Core is the most popular framework for building reports, decision support tools, and dashboards with code. It powers analytics at thousands of organizations, including several Fortune 100 companies.

Evidence Studio is a complete enterprise business intelligence platform built around Evidence Core, combining an AI analytics agent with access control, enterprise security, and governance. Evidence Studio powers analytics at hundreds of organizations.

Evidence was founded in 2021 by Sean Hughes and Adam McAskill. Prior to founding Evidence, Sean and Adam built the data science team at Canada's leading private equity fund.

Evidence is venture-backed, with support from leading institutions and angels, including Y Combinator, SV Angel, A Capital, Tristan Handy (founder of dbt), and Andrew Miklas (founder of PagerDuty and YC partner).
