The central states of this nation have given us a lot to be grateful for when it comes to architecture and interior design. There’s the Prairie School and the Chicago School of Architecture, not to mention American Bauhaus and Midwestern Modern. Luckily, the region has also produced stellar interior photographers in the Midwest to capture all the glory.

It has been revealed that many of the photographers on these pages shoot commercial projects and fine art as well as interiors, capturing light-filled living rooms and cozy bedrooms alike. Often working with natural light, these interior photographers in the Midwest use perspective to make the most of wide-open spaces as well as cozy apartments. One look at their work—whether it’s intimate or expansive—and you’ll see these talents are more than Midwestern nice. They’re Midwestern marvelous.
