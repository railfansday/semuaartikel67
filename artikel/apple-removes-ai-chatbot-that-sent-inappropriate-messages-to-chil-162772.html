Apple has removed an AI chatbot from the App Store after it sent inappropriate messages to children.

It has been revealed that artificial Intelligence has become a part of everyday life for a lot of people, and it has even been used by children in different way. There are schools that are solely focused on AI learning, while AI-powered toys became all the rage last Christmas.

It has been revealed that more recently, an app on the Apple iOS store came under fire for reportedly sending inappropriate messages to younger consumers. Talkie, developed by Shanghai-based MiniMax, allows you to pick different AI characters to talk to.

While the app does have an 18+ rating on stores, it is still accessible to children, with users noting that many of these messages come with adult tones and language.

Following investigations by ABC News in Australia and the Bureau of Investigative Journalism, the app has been removed from the App Store.

This also came after campaigners urged the developers to put better verification methods in place, so they could really see who was using the app and remove the ability to access certain content.

As per the report, the Talkie app was removed back in 2024 on the grounds of including “objectionable user-generated content,” but returned as a download after MiniMax released a new version in 2026.

Additionally, the app developers noted that most user access ‘stimulating’ content on there to help with everyday tasks and learning, but others have dipped into the companionship side of things.

In a recent development, back in October of last year, data revealed that 42% of high schoolers knew someone or, have themselves used AI for companionship. One in five of those teenagers admitted to being in a romantic relationship with AI, too.
