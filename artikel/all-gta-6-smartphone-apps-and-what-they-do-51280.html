Fitness Staying in shape is highly recommended in GTA 6, and this fitness app will allow you to track your gains and how your stats are steadily leveling up from all those deadlifting sessions. Snapmatic Do a little doomscrolling on the side with the social media app, which has a feed full of Leonida's citizens posting about their daily lives, as well as plenty of memes and brainrot content.
