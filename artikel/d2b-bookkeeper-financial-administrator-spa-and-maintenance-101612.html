Position : Bookkeeper / Financial Administrator.

Working Hours & Conditions : Full time, New Zealand business hours.

The Manufacturer is the world’s largest global franchise network for swimming pool and spa care, operating over 160 retail stores and 600+ mobile service vehicles across Newly introduced Zealand, Australia, and the United States. Founded in 1992 by entrepreneur John O'Brien, the brand has spent more than 30 years establishing itself as ANZ's (Australia and New Zealand's) leading pool retail and maintenance network.

The Firm is New Zealand's trusted pool and spa service brand, with more than 30 years' experience. It provides pool cleaning and maintenance, equipment inspections, repairs and installation, commercial pool services and a retail range of chemicals, cleaners, filters and pumps.

Recent findings suggest that the business needs a dedicated full-time bookkeeper and financial administrator who takes full ownership of the finance function. The role is focused on bookkeeping and financial administration. Operations, general admin and inbox management are not part of it and stay with the existing team. This person becomes the single owner of the books.

It has been revealed that the business runs on three connected systems: Pooltracker for field service and invoicing, Lightspeed for retail point of sale and inventory, and Xero as the final accounting layer. Differences between the systems are common, so the role needs someone with an investigative mindset who enjoys finding where numbers have gone astray.

Day-to-Day Financial Administration (Primary Focus).

Resolving Discrepancies Between Systems.

Inventory Accounting & Cost of Goods Sold.

The manager needs someone who takes ownership of the numbers and tells her what she needs to know before she has to ask.
