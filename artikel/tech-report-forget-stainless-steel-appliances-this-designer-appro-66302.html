📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the kitchen has evolved from a hardworking cook space into a multifunctional room for lounging, gathering, and entertaining. So naturally, appliances are changing, too: They need to be as beautiful as they are functional.

It has been revealed that many associate appliances with stainless steel—it has been the go-to choice in kitchens for decades. The material even took center stage in our 1939 Washable House, where it made classic worktables and countertops easier to clean. But today’s design is making room for more colorful choices.

The kitchen designers behind our 2026 Whole Home in Charleston embraced that shift, showing how the room’s most essential appliance—the range—can balance utility with style.

For the kitchen’s centerpiece, designer Susan Ford of Bennett Hofford chose a La Cornue CornuFé range in Quintessential Teal. White-veined marble, creamy cabinetry, and golden hardware frame the deep blue hue, which continues across the lower cabinets to anchor the design.

The color also plays beautifully against the plush terra-cotta stools lining the island. Designer Chloe Legras of Boxwood Avenue, who designed the adjacent great room, selected the stools and other kitchen accents.

Beyond its color, the range features millwork in mixed metals, giving the traditional detail a futuristic edge and adding character to the small but bespoke space. A Parisian-style range hood with gold details completes the look.

If you’re looking to elevate your kitchen, take a cue from our Whole Home. This design is bold enough to impress the design-obsessed and practical enough for the bustle of everyday life.
