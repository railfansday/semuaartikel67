Industry observers note that behaviour Interactive has never shied away from bringing strange, completely out-there IPs into its asymmetrical multiplayer horror game, Dead by Daylight. The game has featured Tokyo Ghoul, Tomb Raider, and even — get this — Nicolas Cage.​​​​​​​ Yes, the actor.

In a recent development, this time around, however, Behaviour is​​​​​​​ taking things a giant step further. A newly released teaser trailer suggests that the next IP to arrive in the game​​​​​​​ could be none other than the King of Monsters himself: Godzilla. If the tease proves accurate, the iconic kaiju would bring an entirely new scale of terror to the game.

In a recent development, dead by Daylight’s teaser trailer for the kaiju raises more questions than it answers. It focuses on a young Dwight Fairfield, a survivor doomed to spend eternity in the Entity’s Realm, trapped in an endless cycle of murder and generator repairs.

In a recent development, in the trailer, a young Dwight spots a Godzilla-like kaiju figurine and reaches for it on the shelf. Clearly, not knowing shopping etiquette means you have to pay before you unwrap your toys. As Dwight unwraps the toy, dark shadows — presumably adults — gather around him​​​​​​​, causing him to panic and hide. However, as he hides in what appears to be a service corridor, a familiar roar rings out before the scene goes dark.​​​​​​​.

Sources indicate that dead by Daylight players and lore enthusiasts know the Entity often corrupts and uses survivors' and killers' memories to torment them. The same is likely true of Dwight’s fresh lore, arriving with the Hartshorne Mall Map on Nov. 24. The trailer reveals a childhood memory of Dwight’s that scared him, and the Entity is now twisting it into reality to haunt him.

What does this mean for Godzilla’s involvement in Dead by Daylight? The more likely move is that Godzilla will come to the Fog as part of a crossover featuring customizations, with some survivors and killers receiving skins that make them resemble well-known characters from the Godzilla film franchise and spin-offs.

According to recent reports, however, some speculate that Godzilla himself will appear as the killer. Frankly, this seems unlikely: Godzilla is enormous, and survivors would have little chance of countering him with windows and pallets. Behaviour couldfind a workaround, of course. For example, making the toy Dwight dropped the killer and making Godzilla "normal-sized" would be quite the cop-out for a character whose size helps define its monstrous nature.

Industry observers note that at the time of writing, it’s unclear how Godzilla will make his way to the Fog, though his arrival seems inevitable. For now, fans will have to wait and see whether — and how — the Big G arrives in the coming days.
