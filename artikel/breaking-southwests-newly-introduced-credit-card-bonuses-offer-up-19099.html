Recent findings suggest that southwest’s Companion Pass lets someone fly with you for just taxes and fees, even when you redeem points for your own ticket. For someone who flies Southwest with a partner a few times a year, it’s the single best deal in domestic air travel. And the easiest way to earn it is about to disappear.

Industry observers note that there’s still a final window to use an 80,000- or 90,000-point credit card initial bonus toward a pass valid through December 31, 2027. You need to apply by December 2, 2026 and get approved. Neither bonus gets you all the way there on its own, but both give you a huge head start.

The Southwest Rapid Rewards® Priority Credit Card (See rates and fees) offers 90,000 bonus points after spending $3,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening. It has a $229 annual fee. This is the premium Southwest co-brand card, really designed for folks who fly the airline. It makes those trips better, and spending helps earn both elite status and Companion Pass®. The travel benefits cover the cardmember and up to 8 additional passengers on the same reservation: First checked bag free. Preferred or Standard seat selection at booking, at no additional charge, when available. Extra Legroom seat upgrades within 48 hours of departure, at no additional charge, when available. Boarding with Group 5 or earlier if another benefit qualifies you for a better group. You earn 4 points per dollar on Southwest Airlines® purchases, including flights, inflight purchases and Southwest gift cards; 2 points per dollar at gas stations and restaurants; and 1 point per dollar on other purchases. There are also 7,500 anniversary points each year. The Southwest Rapid Rewards® Plus Credit Card (See rates and fees) offers 80,000 bonus points after spending $1,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening. Its annual fee is $99. That’s a much lower spending requirement for a bonus that’s only 10,000 points smaller. First checked bag free for the cardmember and up to 8 additional passengers on the same reservation. Standard seat selection within 48 hours of departure, when available, for the same group of travelers. Group 5 boarding or earlier if you otherwise qualify. 3,000 anniversary points each year, plus a 10% anniversary flight discount code that excludes Basic fares. The Plus card earns 2 points per dollar on Southwest Airlines® purchases, and 2 points per dollar at gas stations and grocery stores on the first $5,000 in combined purchases per anniversary year. Other purchases earn 1 point per dollar. I’d choose Plus for the lower cost of getting the bonus. Priority costs $130 more per year, but gives you better seat benefits, 4,500 more anniversary points and a larger initial bonus. For a family that flies Southwest regularly, those seating benefits can easily be worth the difference.
