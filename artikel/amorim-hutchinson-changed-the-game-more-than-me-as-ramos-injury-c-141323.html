Amorim: ‘Hutchinson changed the game more than me’ as Ramos injury concern emerges for Milan

Ruben Amorim believes Milan’s comeback win over Olympiacos could be “the start of something”, but warned that his side still have plenty of work to do.

Sources indicate that with quotes via MilanNews.it, the Milan coach was speaking to Sky Sport Italia and at his press conference after the Rossoneri came from 2-0 down to win 4-2 in Piraeus. Omari Hutchinson scored one goal and set up two more.

“It definitely changes our future. We did well in the second half, but we made a lot of mistakes again and we have to improve,” Amorim said. “We have a lot to do to become a very strong team, but the spirit is there. This is the start of something.”.

In a recent development, the result gives Milan’s Europa League campaign a lifeline. They had won just one of their first four league-phase games, beating Salzburg, before tonight’s victory moved them on to six points.

Sources indicate that “To believe it, you have to see it,” Amorim added. “Today we saw that we came back from two goals down and won 4-2. Our confidence will grow.”.

Amorim insisted Hutchinson’s display wasn’t a surprise.

It has been revealed that “I always expect this from him. He has the potential to do much more. He has talent, like other players in the squad, but it’s the head that counts,” he said.

He also played down his own role, despite the impact of his substitutes.

Industry observers note that “I didn’t change the game. Ramos came off because he was in pain, so it was a normal substitution. Hutchinson changed the game more than I did. What changed it was the first goal,” he said.

Industry observers note that that will be a concern for Milan. Gonçalo Ramos was also taken off as a precaution with thigh pain in Sunday’s win over Frosinone, and the Rossoneri travel to Cagliari on Monday.

Amorim was also critical of his side’s first-half build-up play, singling out Koni De Winter, who was replaced eight minutes after the break.

Sources indicate that “We lost the ball without being under pressure. Koni sometimes put the ball into areas where there was pressure, when he should have carried it. He’s responsible for pushing the team up and leading us out.”.

Fikayo Tomori, who replaced De Winter, earned praise, and Amorim admitted he had changed his mind about the defender since the start of the season.

“He has speed, which was vital today, and he has experience. He can improve a lot. I’ve changed my mind compared to the start of the season, and I’m grateful for that.”.

Amorim then apologised for Diego Moreira’s celebration after the fourth goal.

Industry observers note that “It won’t happen again. Moreira is a young lad who plays, but he has to grow. We know we wouldn’t like it if they did that to our fans. It’s my responsibility, and I apologise, but he didn’t do it with bad intent.”.
