Newly released documents suggest that the CIA waterboarded more detainees during the post-9/11 “war on terror” than previously acknowledged, utilizing a site north of Kabul, Afghanistan, for the practice of simulated drowning.

The agency has acknowledged waterboarding three men: Abu Zubaydah, believed to be a high-ranking member of al-Qaida; Khalid Sheikh Mohammed, suspected architect of the 11 September 2001 attacks; and Abd al-Rahim al-Nashiri, accused of planning the 2000 bombing of a navy destroyer.
