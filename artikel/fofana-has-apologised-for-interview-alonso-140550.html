Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso says defender Wesley Fofana has apologised for a controversial interview he gave on YouTube.

Fofana has been criticised for comments he made in the interview with YouTuber Zach Nani in which he appeared to joke about his penalty miss in a Carabao Cup shootout defeat at Liverpool.

He did not have permission to hold the two-hour interview, which was aired after Chelsea's humiliating 4-1 defeat at home to rivals Leeds on Saturday, but Alonso mentioned the matter was now "closed".

"It was unfortunate. He has apologised. He knows it was not right, the moment, it was not right, the content," said the Blues boss, who was speaking before Friday's Premier League match at Nottingham Forest.

In a recent development, "We are here to take things seriously. He's said it will not be repeated. We don't want these kind of things and they won't be repeated.".

Alonso refused to confirm whether Fofana had been punished by the club, but mentioned "we have addressed that with him.".
