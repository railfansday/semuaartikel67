But the former Leeds United boss is no stranger to firefighting and and helping teams get out of trouble – and he believes he can do that again as long as they ‘really, really’ pull their finger out on the pitch.

"I think certainly towards the last five or six years, myself and Neil [Warnock] were going firefighting at Rotherham, Middlesbrough, Cardiff and we'd take them out the bottom three and Cardiff into the Premier League.

“This here would be a great achievement as well. As I say, there's a lot of good stuff here. It's not like going into a club where a lot of it is in a bad shape.

“A lot of stuff is good here, but the one thing that matters is what happens on the pitch and that is where we have to really, really pull our finger out.”.
