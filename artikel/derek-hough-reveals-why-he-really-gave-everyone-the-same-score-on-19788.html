📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that it seems like Derek Hough’s rage-bait isn’t limited to Coach Jackie J. (IYKYK!) The Dancing with the Stars judge seems to have fans spiraling over his scores from last week’s Grammy Night performances.

Derek scored each couple a 9 for each of their performances throughout the night, and DWTS devotees were understandably curious as to why.

When pro dancer Ezra Sosa chatted with the judge during the after-show livestream on TikTok, Derek joked, “Listen, I’ve had three hours of sleep. I came from Seattle this morning on tour. I’m going to Florida right after this Disney special, and then back on tour…That’s why I think I’m giving nines to everybody today.”.

The joke seemingly went over some people’s heads, as one person criticized the longtime judge for “flying under the radar” as the “nice guy.”.

It has been revealed that “Did derek actually say he was tired so he gave everyone a 9.. #dwts,” one person wrote, while another chimed in, “Yes!!!!! I can’t believe there isn’t more people talking about this right now!!!! That was so unprofessional not only that, but why are you acting like the semifinals aren’t on Tuesday??? Just handing 9s to whoever or whatever the dance was.”.

Another person even joked that Coach Jackie should have been in the ballroom to “keep eyes on Derek.”.

“What do you actually mean derek was ‘too tired to judge’ so he just gave everybody a nine,” they wrote alongside a clip of Jackie in the DWTS audience a few weeks prior.

What’s more: Derek retroactively shared a vlog recounting the day he got “three hours of sleep.”.

“So this is why you were funky during the show 😭,” someone commented, while another criticized, “Next year, maybe delay the tour so that the contestants can be fairly judged by someone who isn’t just throwing in the towel and scoring all 9s because they’re tired.”.

Derek has yet to comment further on the matter, if at all, but we’ll see what he brings to the judging panel during the semifinals on Tuesday!
