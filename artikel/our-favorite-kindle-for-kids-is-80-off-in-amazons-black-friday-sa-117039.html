SAVE $80: As of Nov. 25, the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite Kids is on sale for $149.99 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $80 off its full price of $229.99.

Amazon recently launched some fresh Kindles, but the 2024 models are actually having a moment in the retailer's Black Friday sale. This selection includes our top pick for little bookworms, the Kindle Paperwhite Kids, which is discounted by $80.

Sources indicate that after a price hike earlier this year that raised its list price from $179.99 to $229.99, this deal lets you snag the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite Kids for $149.99 instead. If a younger reader is on your holiday shopping list this year, it's a discount well worth jumping on while it's still available. According to recent reports, the Kindle Paperwhite Kids comes with 16GB of storage space, which is plenty of room for youngsters to fill up a digital library, and features a glare-free 7-inch Paperwhite display with an adjustable warm light. It also comes with six months of Amazon Kids+, a subscription service for kids ages 3-12 that offers unlimited access to a library of age-appropriate books (though keep in mind it automatically renews after the six months are up). Plus, it's waterproof.

It's a Kindle we consider to be the best option for kids. Mashable's Samantha Mangino notes that a real highlight is that it's a "Distraction-free device. It doesn't come with ads, notifications, or the temptation of games or video." Plus, "It's designed to be kid-proof, not just physically, but also with built-in parental controls.".

If you think the young reader in your life would love to get their hands on the Kindle Paperwhite Kids, don't miss out on this great deal on the 2024 version at Amazon.
