Transcend Cruises has launched a partnership with Your Health Companion (YHC) to introduce a personal health benefit designed to enhance the well-being, confidence and peace of mind of its charter river cruise guests.

“At Transcend Cruises, we are continually looking for innovative ways to elevate the guest experience. Our partnership with Your Health Companion reflects our commitment to providing guests not only with extraordinary travel experiences but also with greater confidence and peace of mind while exploring the world,” said Edie Rodriguez, CEO, Transcend Cruises.

Through this partnership, Transcend Cruises guests will receive complimentary seven-day access to Your Health Companion, a personal health management app that puts essential medical information, health resources and AI-powered symptom guidance directly at their fingertips.

After the complimentary period, guests who want to keep enjoying the app’s comprehensive features can purchase an annual subscription at a reduced price, extending these benefits beyond their river cruise and into their everyday lives.

Transcend said in a press release that Your Health Companion helps guests access the informational and organizational benefits of having their doctor’s office in their pocket, available directly from their smartphone wherever internet connectivity is available.

“We believe that traveling away from home should never mean being disconnected from your personal health information,” noted Scott Grody, CEO, Your Health Companion.

Industry observers note that “Our partnership with Transcend Cruises represents an exciting advancement in bringing personalized health technology to the travel industry. We are providing travelers with many of the benefits of having their doctor’s office in their pocket, wherever their journey takes them,” added Grody.
