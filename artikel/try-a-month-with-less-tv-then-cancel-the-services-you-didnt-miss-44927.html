Try a Month With Less TV – Then Cancel the Services You Didn’t Miss

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that spend one month watching less TV, and you’ll see which streaming services your household uses and which it only pays for. When the month ends, cancel every service and premium channel nobody missed, and put the hours you got back toward sleep, family, exercise, or a side project.

Americans 15 and older average 2.6 hours of TV a day, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics’ American Time Use Survey. That’s about 18 hours a week, half of all their leisure time.

Sources indicate that much of that viewing happens by default. The TV goes on after dinner out of habit, and autoplay starts the next episode before anyone picks it. As long as you watch that way, every service gets a little use and seems worth keeping. For a month, watch only what someone chooses to put on, and the services nobody chose stand out.

According to recent reports, before the month starts, list every streaming service and premium channel your household pays for, with its monthly price. Each time someone watches one, put a mark beside it.

It has been revealed that at the end of the month, cancel each one without a mark. Say you pay for four streaming services, and two of them, at about $15 a month each, go unwatched all month. Canceling those two saves $30 a month, or $360 a year. If nobody missed cable at all, cut cable too.

To trim the bill further, rotate your streaming services and pay for only one or two at a time. When you make the list, add each billing date to your calendar, so you can cancel the unmarked ones before they charge you again.
