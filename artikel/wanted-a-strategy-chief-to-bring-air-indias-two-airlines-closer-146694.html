IndiGo hired Air India Express' former chief as its strategy officer in March. Now Air India is creating a strategy role of its own. It looks like both airlines agree the job is worth having.

Air India is creating a newly introduced group strategy officer position as part of its efforts to get full-service carrier Air India and its low-cost arm Air India Express working together more closely, sources said.

Industry observers note that rival IndiGo made a similar move earlier this year, naming former Air India Express managing director and CEO Aloke Singh as its chief strategy officer. Singh ran Air India Express for five years before joining IndiGo.

The newly introduced strategy job is one of two group-level roles being added. The other is a chief of the training academy, who will be responsible for bringing the group’s flying academy, flight training organizations and technical training under one roof.

Air India CEO and managing director Tewolde Gebremariam outlined the changes at an employee town hall on Wednesday. He also spoke about operational improvements, cost cutting, network changes and the airline's longer-term plans.
