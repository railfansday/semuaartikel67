Starting after the scheduled maintenance on December 1st, players will be able to hop between North American and European data centers at will, or pop over to the Oceanian data centers if they so choose. Here’s the breakdown of the fresh server traffic flows:.

Cross-region Travel Available Beginning Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

To help alleviate server congestion following the release of Patch 8.0, we will be enabling cross-region Data Center Travel between the North American and European data centers during scheduled maintenance on Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

Square Enix’s reasoning is quite simple; peak player times vary across regions so when the servers are slammed in North America, players can choose a European data center to potentially sidestep thousand-player-deep log-in queues and vice versa. ot only is this a great way to get ahead of the impending server traffic jam, but it’s also a neat social feature that eases some of the siloing that happens between servers.

In a recent development, currently, if you have a cross-continental friendship, you’d have to make an alternate character on the appropriate data center in order to play together. Cross-region travel will let you port your main character over, letting you quest together and, critically, run endgame content like raids without the need to start from scratch on a fresh server. That’s huge and players are suitably hyped for it, hoping that the option to data-center-hop remains available permanently.

Square Enix warns that visiting other data centers might increase latency and encourages players to test it out before Evercold releases in February 2027 to get a feel for it. Also, sorry to Oceanian data center players, you still don’t get to go anywhere for now.

In preparation for Evercold, Square Enix is giving away 30 days of free game time to eligible players and has a newly introduced item for purchase that'll let you skip Dawntrail.
