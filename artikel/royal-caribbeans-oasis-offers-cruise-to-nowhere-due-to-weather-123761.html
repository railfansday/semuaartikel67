Royal Caribbean’s Oasis Offers Cruise to Nowhere Due to Weather

A recent short sailing onboard Royal Caribbean’s Oasis of the Seas became a cruise to nowhere due to adverse weather conditions.

Departing from Fort Lauderdale on November 16, 2026, the vessel was initially scheduled to operate a four-night cruise to the Bahamas.

The sailing’s original itinerary included visits to Nassau, as well as Royal Caribbean’s private island destination of Perfect Day CocoCay.

The ship initially cancelled its visit to Nassau before being forced to skip the call to the private destination as well.

“Adverse weather conditions producing high winds and ocean swells impacted our operations at Perfect Day CocoCay, preventing us from safely welcoming guests ashore,” Royal Caribbean said in a statement shared with guests onboard.

“As a result, we won’t be able to visit the port and will instead enjoy a relaxing day at sea,” the company continued.

Royal Caribbean added that any shore excursion booked through the organization for the destination would be automatically cancelled and refunded to their original form of payment.

“We truly appreciate your understanding and flexibility regarding this unexpected change. To thank you for your continued loyalty and to help make up for the inconvenience we are pleased to offer a non-refundable onboard credit,” the organization continued.

According to recent reports, the credit was based on passengers’ cabin category and occupancy, with inside and outside cabins receiving $50, while balcony cabins received $75 and suites received $150. Cabins with third, fourth or more guests received a $25 extra credit per additional person.

“We’re sorry for this last-minute change caused by the weather – your safety is our top priority,” Royal Caribbean added.

Having repositioned from Newly introduced Jersey earlier this month, the Oasis of the Seas is currently offering a series of short cruises to the Bahamas from Fort Lauderdale.

The three- and four-night itineraries will be offered through April 2027 and are highlighted by regular visits to Perfect Day CocoCay.
