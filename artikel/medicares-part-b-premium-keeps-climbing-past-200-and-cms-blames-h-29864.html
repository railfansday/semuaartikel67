Medicare’s Part B premium keeps climbing past $200, and CMS blames health care spending

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (51 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services set the standard monthly Medicare Part B premium at $209.40 for 2027 on Tuesday, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026. The agency linked the higher premium and deductible to expected increases in health care spending.
