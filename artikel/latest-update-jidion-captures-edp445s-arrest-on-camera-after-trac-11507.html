JiDion has released footage showing the exact moment EDP445 was arrested in Nevada, after the YouTuber spent three days staking him out and calling police himself.

EDP445, real name Bryant Moreland, was arrested on November 4 on a warrant out of Kern County, California, after comments he made in the documentary The Sick Mind of EDP445 led prosecutors to reopen a 2021 case.

In a newly introduced YouTube video, JiDion highlights officers surrounding Moreland’s car outside a dialysis center in Henderson, before taking him into custody as he walked out of the building.

“They got him. They got him surrounded,” JiDion said as he filmed the arrest from his car. “This is absolute cinema.”.

Industry observers note that the arrest took place at the same dialysis center where JiDion confronted Moreland in 2023. “The place where we first caught him is going to be the place where he finally gets arrested,” he said.

Industry observers note that jiDion explained that he flew to Las Vegas after spotting the warrant on Kern County’s website, and reported it in person to the Henderson Police Department, where an officer told him to call if he saw Moreland in public. Three days later, he called 911 when Moreland arrived at the center.

Sources indicate that moreland faces multiple felony charges related to arranging a meeting with a minor, according to FOX5 Vegas. He waived his right to fight extradition at a hearing on November 9, and faces a $30,000 bail once he is returned to California.

Industry observers note that despite his role, JiDion played down his involvement. “I had nothing to do with EDP getting arrested. The only thing I did was call the cops,” he noted, crediting Alex Rosen, Mike Clum and Chris Hansen instead.
