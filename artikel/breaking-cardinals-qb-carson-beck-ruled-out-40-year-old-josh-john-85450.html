Breaking: Cardinals QB Carson Beck ruled out, 40-year-old Josh Johnson to start vs. Chiefs

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Arizona Cardinals are turning to their fourth quarterback of the season.

On Saturday, the team ruled out quarterback Carson Beck for Sunday's game against the Kansas City Chiefs. Beck has been dealing with back spasms and did not practice on Friday. Head coach Mike LaFleur noted that the team was preparing for Josh Johnson to be the team's starter for Week 11.

Industry observers note that beck, who is a rookie, was thrust into the role of starter after Gardner Minshew went on injured reserve with a torn UCL. The Cardinals' original starter, Jacoby Brissett, was traded to the Baltimore Ravens after being benched for Minshew mid-game the week before.

Beck had been listed as questionable on Friday's injury report.

LaFleur said that this is an issue that Beck has dealt with before, but wasn't able to provide a clear timeline for a return.

Industry observers note that in the meantime, the 40-year-old Johnson, who was signed off the Cincinnati Bengals' practice squad after the Minshew injury, will step up for Sunday's matchup against the Chiefs. At 40 years, 191 days, Johnson will be the second-oldest non-specialist to appear in a game for the Cardinals behind only Jim Thorpe (41 years, 185 days old), according to the team.

The itinerant backup quarterback also has some history with LaFleur: Johnson and LaFleur's careers overlapped in 2021, when both were with the Latest York Jets (LaFleur was an offensive coordinator at the time). LaFleur had high praise for Johnson, noting some of the reasons he's been able to keep his career going this long.

In a recent development, "If it is Josh, I've called plays for him before, and had a really great experience calling plays for him," LaFleur remarked. "He is incredible in the quarterback room, he's incredible for the starters. He's incredible for young quarterbacks, in terms of the way he sees the game, his energy, his positivity, how he keeps it real. He's mature. And he can still sling it. … If that's the route that ends up happening, let's go.".

It has been revealed that arizona is the 15th NFL franchise that Johnson has been a part of in his career, as the veteran quarterback has bounced between half of the teams in the league. Across 16 seasons, he has only started 11 games in his NFL career.
