According to recent reports, being the giving Sagittarius she is, Hailey Bieber actually gives us something every year for her birthday: a new Rhode collection. Sometimes it’s a special-edition shade or flavor of her award-winning Peptide Lip Tint. Other years, it’s a brand-new product, like the Peptide Eye Prep Hydrogel Patches and a new snap-on lip case. But the 1996-er went all out for her 30th birthday, dropping new products, exclusive styles and colors on some of the brand’s go-to items, and even its first-ever apparel.

Today, Rhode announced Pocket Plush, the brand’s new matte blush in two shades: Baldwin, a berry rose that’s allegedly Hailey’s go-to shade, and Tucson, a rich burgundy. For now, it comes in a limited-edition mini navy packaging that’s v cute (and has been teased for a while on Rhode’s IG). What makes it different from Pocket Blush? Well, the creamy stick is stated to leave a velvety, soft matte finish on skin. Think blurring and smoothing, not dewy and fresh like the OG. You can also wear them on cheeks and lips, which Hailey loves to pair together for a monochromatic look.

Sources indicate that but that’s not even the most exciting newly introduced launch, IMO. For the first time ever, Rhode is launching apparel. It’s only on pre-order right now, but the HB Windbreaker was inspired by Hailey’s love of vintage sportswear. It’s an easy “throw on and go,” oversized jacket that can be worn two ways. There’s navy nylon on one side and gray fleece on the other. If you wanted to match your Rhode phone case, lip balm, and Glazing Milk, now’s your chance. I’m pretty confident this one will sell out, especially ahead of gifting season.

According to recent reports, oh! And that’s not all! (Told ya, Hailey went all out this year.) There’s a limited-edition flavor of the Peptide Lip Boost, a rich, moisturizing lip mask that Cosmo editors have loved since it launched. This one has a shimmery sheer pink tint and a cherry vanilla cola scent. Sounds delicious.

Plus, there are exclusive styles of the Peptide Eye Prep, both in navy to match the collection. You can also grab the Rhode Carry Case and Snap-On Lip Case, both now available in navy just for Hailey’s bday.

Of course, if you wanted to pick up a set to gift (ahem, as a fellow Sag, I can confirm anyone wIth a bday coming up soon would love it), grab The Birthday Set: it contains both shades of Pocket Plush and the Peptide Lip Boost in Cherry Vanilla Cola.

Everything introduces (including the windbreaker preorder) on Friday, November 20, at 9 a.m. PT. As all Rhode things go, these will absolutely sell out swiftly. If you have your eye on something, I wouldn’t wait.
