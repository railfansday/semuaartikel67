Recent findings suggest that on its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, FormFactor (FORM +11.71%) delivered its fourth consecutive quarter of record revenues and raised its baseline non-GAAP gross margin to 53%. The semiconductor test equipment supplier is benefiting from expanding demand in artificial intelligence applications, but existing plants are running at maximum capacity.

Three things management said stand out.

Sources indicate that existing manufacturing facilities are operating at their output limits, making the latest factory in Farmers Branch, Texas, critical for meeting customer demand. The site expands production of probe cards, which are specialized test interfaces used to inspect semiconductor wafers before packaging.

Industry observers note that dynamic random-access memory (DRAM) customers shifted orders toward standard DDR5 memory chips to capture higher market prices. Internal manufacturing cost reduction programs kept probe card profitability high despite lower volumes of high-bandwidth memory cards.

High-performance computing relies increasingly on co-packaged optics (CPO), a technique using light instead of electricity to transfer data between microchips. Demand for TRITON optical testing systems accelerated throughout 2026 as chipmakers adopted the technology.

Demand for advanced test systems is accelerating across graphics processing units (GPUs), central processing units (CPUs), and application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs). Broad exposure across chip architectures provides multiple growth vectors while new factory capacity comes online.

Factory efficiency gains and disciplined operating expenses are expanding operating leverage as annual revenues approach $1 billion.
