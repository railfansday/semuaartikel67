This GTA 6-Inspired Keycap Set Will Make The Wait For A PC Version Slightly Easier

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is now just a couple of hours away in some parts of the world. That is, if you're planning to play on either Xbox or PlayStation. The wait for a PC version is still ongoing, with Rockstar yet to indicate when it looks to release that port. In the meantime, however, you can start ensuring that your setup is at least ready for when it arrives. High-end keycap manufacturer GMK has revealed a new set that is clearly inspired by GTA 6 and Vice City, without explicitly mentioning either. The new set, named Neon Vice, features black keycaps with an array of colored alphas and numerical keys, all drawing from the pink, orange, and purple colors of Rockstar's impending sequel. Novelty keycaps include images of speedboats across choppy water, palm trees, and alligators, and the Vice City skyline. https://www.instagram.com/p/DfzSlehirjY/?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet Like most GMK sets, the keycaps are constructed from double-shot ABS plastic, which should ensure fantastic legibility with use over time. What isn't normal is the price, however. GMK keycap sets are priced at a premium, and Neon Vice is no exception. Expect to pay $170 for the base kit, and an additional $45 if you want the novelty keys on top of that. Preorders open for the kit today across multiple vendors around the world. That's a lot more than the game itself, so if you haven't been bitten by the custom keyboard bug, it's probably best to just continue waiting. Thankfully the gap between console and PC versions for GTA 6 might not be as large as previous Rockstar titles, with a report suggesting that it might arrive in early 2027. If you are playing on console you can pre-load the game right now ahead of its launch, which will start for lucky players in New Zealand very soon.
