A couple has been going viral after they were spotted arguing at a college football game, with television commentators narrating the whole thing.

If you go to a sports game, there is a chance that, at some point, you may end up on television or the jumbotron at the stadium. At that point, you don’t really want to be caught doing anything embarrassing, or you just might become a meme.

Well, on November 21, a couple attending the Wake Forest and Georgia Tech football game were caught in the middle of an argument towards the end of the game.

Now, there weren’t any mics attached to them, so you’d have to be a pretty good lip reader to figure out what exactly was said, but that didn’t stop the ESPN cameras from following along. “He wants an answer, and he’s not leaving. I mean, they are in each other’s personal space. He’s not happy with whatever she just told him,” ESPN announcer Jesse Palmer said.

Palmer’s co-commentator, Joe Tessitore, also weighed in with his own breakdown of what was going on, with the pair eventually urging the couple to settle things.

It has been revealed that “This is kinda one of those moments where you just say, ‘I’m sorry. Please take a moment, and please say your goodbyes,'” Palmer concluded before the cameras turned their focus back to the on-field action.
