David Raya has made 163 appearances since joining Arsenal in 2023.

Injuries are never good news, but Arsenal's latest one to first-choice goalkeeper David Raya has come in the worst possible position.

The 31-year-old was forced to leave the pitch 70 minutes into Saturday's 2-2 draw with Newcastle, appearing to hurt his shoulder in an innocuous challenge with Magpies forward William Osula.

The Gunners arguably have the best depth in the league with at least two quality options in all outfield positions after a period of squad strengthening.

In goal, however, cover appears much thinner.

Raya was a key component in Arsenal's Premier League title-winning campaign last season, was named in the Fifpro Men's World 11 and was unlucky to not win the Yashin Trophy, awarded to the world's best goalkeeper, which went to Spain team-mate Unai Simon.

Behind him, Kepa Arrizabalaga still holds the record as the most expensive goalkeeper in football history following his Â£72m move from Athletic Bilbao to Chelsea in August 2018, but is far behind Raya in terms of quality.

Raya's passing range and ability on the ball are key to how Arsenal build their attacks, and his presence is a big reason why they have the best defence in the league.

They have conceded just seven Premier League goals this season, with only Brighton able to match that tally - having played a game fewer.

Arsenal also brought in former Leeds goalkeeper Illan Meslier on a free transfer in the summer but his only appearance this season came in the embarrassing Carabao Cup defeat by League Two Fleetwood.

When asked about Raya's injury, Arteta noted he did not know how severe the matter is.

"Well, unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news," noted Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta.

"He will get assessed now, and probably he will have an MRI [scan] tomorrow to see what it is exactly he has done.

"I don't know how it happened, and what the injury is at the moment, so we'll have to wait and see tomorrow.".

Arsenal are about to enter a gruelling period of games - with or without Raya.

In the next four weeks, they face Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City, Brentford, Tottenham Hotspur and Real Madrid.

In a recent development, "The one player that Arsenal cannot afford to lose is David Raya," stated former England forward Alan Shearer, who is a pundit on Saturday's BBC Match of the Day. "He has made such a difference since joining from Brentford in 2023.

"They have top-class cover in just about every other position but he is the one individual that Mikel Arteta will be thinking 'we don't want to play without him for an extended period'. They could, of course go into the transfer market if they need to in January, but Arteta will be desperately hoping Raya is not out for long.".

Raya is reportedly close to agreeing a new deal at the club, which demonstrates his importance to the Gunners with two years remaining on his contract.

Ex-England midfielder Jamie Redknapp said on Sky Sports that Raya is "irreplaceable".

Recent findings suggest that "Kepa is not up to the level of him - I actually think he struggles in the Premier League," added Redknapp. "Obviously we've seen in the Carabao Cup last year in the final.

"He is now going to have to play some games and that's going to be difficult for him.

"It will change everything for Arsenal for a while so that's a massive blow depending on how long David Raya is out because he is irreplaceable - probably the best goalkeeper in the Premier League, if not one of the best in the world.".
