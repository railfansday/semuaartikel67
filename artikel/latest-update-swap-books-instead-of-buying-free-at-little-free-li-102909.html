Trade the books you’ve finished for ones you haven’t read. A Little Free Library box or a swap party with friends costs nothing, and swapping by mail through a book-swap site runs about $5 a book in postage and fees. Take only what you’ll read, and bring your extras so the next swapper finds something latest.

A book you won’t read again still takes up shelf space, and the next one you want costs money.

It has been revealed that start with a Little Free Library, one of the small book boxes anyone can take from or add to. Little Free Library’s map and its free app show boxes near you, though only those their owners registered and chose to list. The organization’s rules don’t require you to leave a book to take one, but keeping a box stocked is up to the people who use it, so bring a few of yours.

In a recent development, for a specific title, try a book-swap site. On PaperBackSwap, open to U.S. residents over 18, each book you mail to a member who requests it earns a credit. Each credit gets you a paperback or hardcover that another member mails at their expense.

You pay to mail yours. A typical paperback weighs under a pound and costs $4.39 to send by Media Mail, the Postal Service’s low-cost option for books. PaperBackSwap’s free blueprint charges a 99-cent swap fee for each book you request, but printing your postage through the site costs 55 cents and earns you a request with no fee. That puts each book you get at about $4.94 in postage and fees.

For a swap party, ask friends or coworkers to bring books they’ve finished and would recommend. Set them out by genre, and draw numbers so people take turns picking one at a time. Donate the leftover books.

Say you have 12 finished paperbacks, each under a pound. Mailing six to PaperBackSwap members who request them, with postage printed through the site, costs $29.64, and the six credits you earn get you six books with no swap fees. Leave the other six in a Little Free Library box over a few visits and take six you’ll read. That’s 12 books you haven’t read for $29.64, about $2.47 each.

List 10 finished books on PaperBackSwap, and its two startup credits, which go to one account per household, get you two books before you mail any.
