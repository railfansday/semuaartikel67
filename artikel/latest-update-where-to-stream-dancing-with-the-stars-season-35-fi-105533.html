It has been revealed that the season 35 finale of Dancing With the Stars marks the show’s very first time being broadcast live coast-to-coast — zero tape delay in any U.S. time zones.

According to recent reports, the three-hour episode airs on Nov. 24 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET on ABC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The finale will also stream live on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial. At a Glance: How to Watch Dancing With the Stars Season 35 Finale.

It has been revealed that the season 35 finale airs live on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET on ABC (carried on live TV streamers like DirecTV, Fubo, Sling and Hulu + Live TV) and will be simulcast on Disney+.

Since the competition show airs on ABC, it’s also available to watch live on any streaming service that carries stated network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The finale will also stream live on Disney+, and while the service doesn’t offer a free trial directly, fans can get free access through any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which include the Disney+ and Hulu bundle and offer a five-day free trial.

It has been revealed that to claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package.
