📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that well folks, you've made it through another week – well done! Chances are many of you have completed your quest in Ocarina of Time by now, with many more of you eagerly awaiting your next jaunt across Leonida when GTA 6 launches on the 19th.

It has been revealed that going back to OoT for a moment, Nintendo confirmed this week that it managed to sell over 5 million copies in just 4 days – astonishing! The company also revealed some behind-the-scenes info about the ReDead enemies, while we took at look at what we think are some of the best 'tiny tweaks' in the remake.

Elsewhere, Nintendo unveiled it will be ceasing Apple and Google sign-ins on Nintendo Accounts, while the Switch 2 received yet another system upgrade.

Reviews this week include Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy (7/10), Queen's Domain (9/10), Metaphor ReFantazio (8/10), and Harold Halibut (7/10).

I had plans to possibly play the Xbox or PS5 Pro this weekend (they’ve been neglected) but it seems like I have bigger fish to fry on my Switch 2! I played majority of Harold Halibut this week for the review and I’m so close to the end. It’s only right I should finish it yeah?

It has been revealed that once that’s ticked off the backlog, I have more progress to make in Final Fantasy Resonance and I might even dare to play OoT more if I have the time. No doubt something will spoil my plans so my backup might be a Peak session with my pals.

Not the best of weekends for city integrity because both Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered and Earth Defense Force 5 continue to delight my free time. I am still hooked on Tape to Tape with a few teams left to unlock. I made the mistake of buying Warhammer Survivors and I fear the combination of 40k/Age of Sigmar lore with the Vampire Survivors addictive game loop will be the downfall of my productivity elsewhere. On the 16-bit retro-front I got Legend of Galactic Heroes on the Super Famicom. This has been on my bucket list for decades, and I'm glad to report the game lives up to the brilliant source material.

In a recent development, game of the week is The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Everyone has probably completed the game by now, but I am still on the way to Zora’s Domain. I was there, Gandalf, 28 years ago when the original released on N64, thus I know what to do and where to go to, rapidly breezing through the game like the Star Fox reboot. I am slowly enjoying the journey, a bit jealous for all the players experiencing this journey for the first time. Dare I dream for F-Zero X to get similar love next year?

Queen's Domain has got me royally hooked. After a somewhat confusing and frustrating start, I'm now deep into the game in all but actual progression, and its classic PC stylings and game design are just ticking so many boxes I'd forgotten I had.

It's still frustrating, but lord help me am I going to push on through this weekend.

According to recent reports, so I played Alone in the Dark last weekend, as promised. It's not great. However, Queen's Domain is great, so I definitely plan to put a bit more time into it over the next few days or so.

Despite the very minor upgrades, I'm also keen to dive into Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy. The visuals have definitely received a nice bump in quality, so it'll be nice to experience the campaign in glorious 4K.

I have other games that I want to squeeze in before 2026 is up, but I simply haven't been able to pry myself away from Ocarina of Time yet. I was so unsure about it from the initial reveal, but goodness me, I love this remake so much.

Despite my best efforts to stretch it out for as long as possible, I only have a few dungeons to go at this point. I'm going to be a wreck at the finale.

The kids and I are heading up Death Mountain this weekend, although by the time you read this we’ve probably already done in Dodongo and are beansing it to Zora’s Domain.

Unfortunately, playing through Ocarina my spawn means that I can’t continue once they’ve gone to bed. And yet despite having dozens of 2026's finest to be getting on with (finished Mixtape this week, great stuff), I’m stuck in a very enjoyable COD funk, with all sorts of numbers in multiplayer. Have a good one, folks.
