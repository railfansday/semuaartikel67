Rep. Brandon Gill, R-Texas, criticizes Rep. Delia Ramirez's, D-Ill., proposed legislation to abolish I.C.E. and CBP and replace DHS, calling the 'reimagining safety' policy dangerous, on 'The Will Cain Show.'.

A Democrat, who noted that she is seeking the House Homeland Security Committee's Oversight, Investigations and Accountability Subcommittee chair, remarked that she aims "to dismantle" the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) "from the inside.".

Sources indicate that "I introduced a bill to dismantle DHS, and I intend to dismantle DHS from the inside. Every investigation we do — whether it’s about detention, who is profiting at the expense of children who are literally in cages, or the expansion of these 287(g) agreements where local police [work] with federal agents to hunt down people — is critical to provide the evidence necessary to be able to bring the most justice," she mentioned Tuesday to Mother Jones, which noted that the discussion with the lawmaker had been edited for the sake of length and clarity.

Ramirez reportedly mentioned that "there’s no other agency that has consolidated the amount of power that DHS has with the amount of money that DHS has in order to terrorize the American people and literally rob us from our civil rights, while at the same time saying that they’re doing it under the guise of protecting us from terrorism. There is no democracy if DHS continues to operate the way it does.".

'SQUAD' DEMOCRAT FORMALLY INTRODUCES BILL TO ABOLISH ICE, BORDER PATROL AND DISMANTLE DHS.

"For me, investigations must lead into reimagining something new, where we protect cybersecurity infrastructure, where we protect FEMA, where we protect TSA, where we protect the customs functions at the border, and where we abolish ICE and CBP and reinvest funds towards healthcare, community safety and first responders," she said, according to the report.

Ramirez, who has served in the House since early 2023, just won re-election during the 2026 midterms.

Democrats will have the majorities in both chambers of Congress next year, and the congresswoman noted to Mother Jones that she is "pursuing the Homeland Security Oversight Subcommittee gavel.".
