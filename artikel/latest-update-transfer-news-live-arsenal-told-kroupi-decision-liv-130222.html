Latest transfer news and rumours.

Paris Saint-Germain could push to sign Chelsea star Estevao or rival Arsenal for Antonio Nusa, reports claim.

Reigning back-to-back European champions PSG are on the lookout for further attacking reinforcements in the January transfer window as they seek to ensure another trophy-laden campaign under Luis Enrique.

According to Diario AS in Spain, the French giants are interested in offering the little-used Estevao an escape route from Stamford Bridge.

The Brazilian is remarked to feature on a shortlist along with Arsenal target Nusa - of RB Leipzig - and Bayern Munich’s Luis Diaz.

Jarell Quansah could make a surprise return to Liverpool.

The versatile defender has continued to flourish since leaving Anfield for Bayer Leverkusen in a £35m deal in the summer of 2025, impressing in the Bundesliga and becoming a favoured option of England boss Thomas Tuchel.

That has led to reported interest in a swift Premier League comeback from the likes of Arsenal and Chelsea.

According to TEAMtalk, Liverpool are keeping a close eye on Quansah’s progress in Germany and haven’t ruled out activating a buy-back clause that is valid until next summer and believed to be valued at just over £50m.

Teenage defender Isaac Allen has confirmed that he has joined Tottenham after leaving rivals West Ham in the summer, writes Sam Tabuteau.

In August, reports claimed that Spurs had beaten Arsenal to Allen’s signature, and the 15-year-old has now announced the move on social media.

The England U15s captain wrote in a post on Instagram: “Happy to be signed with Tottenham.”.

Allen, who can operate either at centre-back or in defensive midfield, is regarded as one of the most promising young talents in England and his arrival continues Spurs’ busy youth recruitment strategy.

Tottenham look to be keeping a close eye on Benfica striker Vangelis Pavlidis.

Spurs are known to be on the lookout for a newly introduced forward ahead of the January transfer window to help boost Roberto De Zerbi’s inconsistent attack.

They have been linked with a swoop for Pavlidis for some time now, with Portuguese outlet Record reporting that they dispatched scouts to watch the Greek international in action again this week.

Arsenal appear to have a new transfer target in Carlos Forbs.

The 22-year-old winger has made quite an impact in Belgium with Club Brugge and already has three senior international appearances for Portugal under his belt.

Forbs previously played for Ajax and spent time on loan at Wolves during the 2024/25 campaign, while he played the full 90 minutes for Brugge in their 3-0 home Champions League defeat by Liverpool last night.

Football Insider claim that Arsenal have added him to their list of left-sided attacking targets before the January window, but Brugge have apparently told them already that he will not be sold mid-season.

Manchester United’s hopes of signing Dominic Calvert-Lewin look to be all but over.

The England international was thought to remain high on the transfer shortlist at Old Trafford ahead of their first window under new boss Mauricio Pochettino, who is focusing on the addition of a new striker in January as well as attempting to fix his much-criticised defence as he is handed an initial budget of around £50m.

But according to The Athletic, Calvert-Lewin has now agreed terms on a lucrative contract extension with rivals Leeds that will keep him at Elland Road until the summer of 2029.

There’s a done deal at Liverpool, who are said to have agreed to sign Senegal wonderkid Sidy Barhama Ndiaye.

Currently 16, Ndiaye plays for Diambars FC in Senegal, but has impressed for his country during the ongoing Under-17 World Cup in Qatar.

Fabrizio Romano claims that he will join Liverpool when he turns 18 in January 2028, having already spent time on trial at Anfield last year.

Other outlets claim that it is only an “option” for Liverpool to sign Ndiaye and does not have to be triggered.

Arsenal are said to have been informed of Bournemouth’s decision over selling top transfer target Eli Junior Kroupi in January.

The French striker appears to have moved to the very top of Mikel Arteta’s shortlist in north London, and may well have been pursued in the summer were it not for an ill-timed foot injury that required surgery and ruled him out for months.

However, talkSPORT claim that Bournemouth will refuse to sell the £100m-rated forward in the Newly introduced Year, with Kroupi also said to prefer a switch to Paris Saint-Germain over Arsenal.

Liverpool have successfully negotiated the option to sign Senegal wonderkid Sidy Barhama Ndiaye once he turns 18.

Ndiaye, currently 16, trialled with Liverpool’s academy in the summer of 2025 and now has a clear pathway to Merseyside.

Per reports from The Athletic, the Reds have pipped a number of European rivals to get right of first refusal for his signing in January 2028.

English clubs cannot sign players from overseas until they have turned 18, which Ndiaye will do on New Year’s Eve in 2027.

Keane Lewis-Potter has committed his future to Brentford by signing a fresh six-year deal.

He will now remain in west London until 2032, with the club holding an option for a further year.

It has been revealed that “Keane is someone who is constantly growing. He's become part of the fabric of the club over recent years, from a very young man coming from Hull to establishing himself in the group,” explained head coach Keith Andrews.

According to recent reports, “Over the period I've been here, we've seen real progression in his game and his versatility in terms of where he plays it. His consistency has also been very good.”.

Lewis-Potter has four goals and one assist to his name in 11 Premier League appearances this season.
