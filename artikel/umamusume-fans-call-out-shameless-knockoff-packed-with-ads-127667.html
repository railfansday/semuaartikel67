An Umamusume clone that uses artwork closely resembling the game’s popular horse girls and racing features has appeared on the Google Play Store, leaving the community wondering how it managed to slip through the net.

From free browser games that combine Pokemon with Umamusume-style races to mods that replace Need for Speed Underground 2’s cars with horse girls, Cygames’ popular mobile game is constantly galloping its way into fan games and community projects.

The anime girl racing game has even sparked an upcoming competitor where players will duke it out against anime girls themed after iconic racing cars.

However, a newly introduced game has appeared on the Google Play Store using artwork that closely resembles Umamusume, with players branding it a “shameless” knockoff riddled with ads.

The game in question is Anime Racing Academy, a latest app that was released on November 16 and has over 100 downloads. While there’s no gameplay shown off, developer HIFI PUBLISHERS has uploaded several screenshots of Anime Racing Academy to help give players a feel for what they can expect.

In a recent development, “Get ready for an exciting anime racing adventure in Anime Racing Academy! Experience thrilling racing challenges, compete on exciting tracks, and master your driving skills in this action-packed anime racing game. Enjoy fast-paced gameplay, smooth controls, and exciting racing competition,” reads the game’s official description.

Recent findings suggest that as you can see from the images below, the game looks eerily similar to Umamusume. There are images of horse girls running full speed across race tracks, idol performances where the characters sing after their victory, and training segments with Uma-style stats shown off.

Umamusume mod perfectly puts anime horse girls into Need for Speed Underground 2.

Umamusume fans can actually race and place bets in newly introduced Steam game.

Umamusume has been transformed into a D&D game with newly introduced race.

In a recent development, some of the anime horse girls themselves also closely resemble Kitasan Black, Oguri Cap, Special Week, and Silence Suzuka. “They’ve got all my favorites: Mejiro McDuke, Commonplace Week, Bronze Ship,” joked one commenter. “That’s not Umamusume, it’s ripoff Musume Bootleg Derby,” added another.

Some players also download the game to see exactly what the “bootleg” version entailed, only to be left shocked by the amount of ads.

It has been revealed that “I had the guts to download and play it. Holy f**king sh*t, what a sh*tty game. There are so many f**king ads that it took five minutes of loading and ads just to get through one race. Brutal,” revealed one player.

Interestingly, the developers have also uploaded another game titled Silver: Open World Detective, which appears to use promotional artwork from the upcoming gacha game Silver Palace.
