The Pixel Tag will begin selling in Europe soon, according to a recent leak. The Android Find Hub tracker sold nearly everywhere else during the initial launch.

Sources indicate that google’s very first tracker joined the dozens of other options that work on the Android Find Hub network, launching alongside the Pixel 11 earlier in the year. It’s a sleek little tracking tag, with some first-party care that makes it stand out.

Leaker Roland Quandt posted an update on the Pixel Tag in a recent Bluesky post. It states the Pixel Tag will, in fact, be sold in Europe. There’s no timeline indication in the post, other than a wide-open “pretty soon” tagged on at the end.

According to recent reports, it’s a wonder it didn’t launch in Europe to begin with. Google initially listed 8 countries, including the US, Canada, Australia, India, and Japan. The United Kingdom is included in that list, but the larger portion classified as the European Union is left out.

Industry observers note that the leak also eases a major concern for people – whether the Pixel Tag will be operational in Europe at all. The Pixel Tag would likely function among the network of Android users in the varying regions, but there’s no definitive answer. But if the Pixel Tag officially lands in Europe, then users won’t need to worry about whether network support will be available.

The Pixel Tag is presently available for nearly every other region online.
