Prince William and wife Princess Kate Middleton have arrived in India for their first joint overseas tour in four years.

According to recent reports, “Catherine and I are delighted to be in India for this year’s Earthshot Prize. India has made an extraordinary contribution to Earthshot over the past six years and is home to more Earthshot Finalists and Winners than any other country,” William, 44, wrote via Instagram on Monday, November 16. “It has also become one of the most exciting places in the world for environmental innovation, with people across the country developing practical solutions to some of the greatest challenges we all share.”.

He added: “We look forward to meeting the innovators, entrepreneurs and young people whose ideas are helping to shape a more sustainable future,” signing the message with “W.”.

The trip to Mumbai has been on William’s professional agenda since earlier this year — with Kensington Palace confirming over the weekend that Kate, 44, would be by her husband’s side as they took part in “celebrating innovation, sustainability, youth leadership and the enduring connections between the United Kingdom and India.”.

Recent findings suggest that the pair started off by visiting the Ballard Estate in Mumbai before taking part in a game of gully in the street. William wore a sleeveless jacket for the occasion while Kate rocked a green jacket with white pants.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to visiting places around Mumbai, William is in India to focus on his environmental awards, the Earthshot Prize. The royal couple greeted Earthshot Prize finalists later on Monday during their third stop.

According to recent reports, “The Earthshot Prize has seen what happens when bold ideas are backed and given the chance to grow. They create jobs, improve lives and change what we believe is possible,” William said in a speech. “And nowhere is that possibility more evident than here in India. Too often, the world’s emerging economies are presented with a false choices: growth or the environment; prosperity or protection.”.

William highlighted how India indicates “a picture full of color, complexity and possibility.”.

“Perhaps that is how we should see the future: not in black-and-white, not presented as false choices between people and planet, prosperity or nature … but in all its colors,” he continued. “A world in which solutions can come from anywhere, and progress benefits us all.”.

William and Kate’s ongoing tour marks the first time they have traveled together on behalf of the royal family in four years. The trip is also part of Kate’s return after her cancer diagnosis in March 2024. The Princess of Wales declared in January 2025 that she is now in remission.
