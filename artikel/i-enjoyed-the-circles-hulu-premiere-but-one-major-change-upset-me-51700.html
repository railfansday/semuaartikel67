Recent findings suggest that the Circle's Hulu premiere dropped this past Tuesday, and it was mostly what I hoped it would be. For starters, I was right that adding celebrities to the mix made the start of the season even better to watch. That said, there is one thing I really miss in comparison to the long-running version I enjoyed with my Netflix subscription, but I'm willing to continue watching despite that.

In a recent development, michelle Buteau served as The Circle's host for all of its original run on Netflix, but as we knew ahead of its premiere on the 2026 TV schedule, she wasn't invited for this latest installment. I didn't think it would be a big deal before watching the premiere, but I have to admit, I really miss her.

Sources indicate that buteau, who readers may know from her Netflix series Survival of the Thickest, was more than just a narrator for The Circle. She brought a lot of comedy to the game, which I felt was a little lacking in Hulu's premiere. Buteau's personality helped shape the tone of the show, which helped make it one of the best reality series.

Industry observers note that during the premiere, I couldn't help but feel like I was watching American Idol without Ryan Seacrest. That feels like a problem, because while Hulu clearly felt The Circle needed retooling for its newly introduced era, the show lasted seven seasons for a reason. Again, I still enjoyed the episode, but there was no denying that there was a definite shift in tone due to the comic's absence.

It may just take time for me to get used to Tinx (Christina Najjar), who comes into The Circle with plenty of experience elsewhere. Before she was hosting the Hulu series, the influencer rose to fame through her no-nonsense advice. This evolved into a podcast, a New York Times best-selling book and a deal with SiriusXM.

Tinx has the resume for the role, but one episode in, I don't really see the same personality in her that I saw in Michelle Buteau. Granted, it's been one episode, and if we were all judged by our first day on the job, many of us would probably in trouble.

There's already so much happening after the first episode, and we need someone who can navigate all the craziness that will unfold over these next few weeks before the $100,000 winner is crowned.
