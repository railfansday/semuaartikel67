It has been revealed that nioh 3 is getting a 'Complete Edition' early next year, Koei Tecmo has confirmed. It'll include the base adventure alongside its two expansions, Hell Rising and Bloody Insurrection.

The latter isn't actually out yet — it rolls out on the 17th December, and promises a brutal all-out battle between multiple factions. Your character will, naturally, be tasked with carving out a solution to the bloodshed.

Anyway, I think this Complete Edition — set to release on the 28th January — is worth highlighting because it really does feel like Nioh 3 got left behind this year.

According to recent reports, team Ninja's sequel received super strong reviews and it was fairly popular at launch, but it's fallen off a cliff in terms of recognition. That's largely because it debuted so early on the 6th February, which has meant that it's been buried over the course of 2026.

In particular, the success of other, much more recent action games like Onimusha: Way of the Sword and Phantom Blade Zero has prevented it from even being brought up in most conversations.

In a recent development, case in point: Nioh 3 hasn't received a single nomination at The Game Awards. It's nowhere to be seen under Best Action Game, Best Action / Adventure Game, or Best RPG.

Just anecdotally, I've seen a handful of Push Square owners expressing their love for Nioh 3 in various comments sections; it's clearly got fans, but it feels like the game needs a miracle if it's to show up in any end-of-year discussions.
