It has been revealed that think of chäshörnli as Switzerland’s answer to stovetop macaroni and cheese. This comforting Alpine dish combines pasta with a creamy, gooey sauce and is served with applesauce and crispy onions. With over 700 years of history, Appenzeller® cheese delivers its signature rich, savory flavor and melts beautifully into the sauce. The dish, a favorite at the restaurant in the Appenzeller® Show Dairy in the town of Stein, is simple, hearty, and exactly what you want to eat when it’s cold outside.
