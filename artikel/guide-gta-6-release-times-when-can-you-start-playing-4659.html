Probably the most anticipated game of all time, GTA 6 is now just a few days away!

As the launch draws even closer, we're covering absolutely everything you need to know about its PS5 launch, including preload options and release times. In this GTA 6 guide, we cover the GTA 6 release times.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar has confirmed GTA 6 will officially be playable from midnight on 19th November in your local time zone. If you have preloaded the game, it'll unlock at midnight, and then you can begin playing immediately.

Recent findings suggest that as such, the game will unlock and be available first in New Zealand. Availability will then roll out worldwide as each time zone reaches midnight on 19th November.

When the game rolls out in the UK, we'll have a hub live on the home page documenting our coverage and providing a place for you to share your first experiences with GTA 6.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 preload is available now! If you've pre-ordered from the PS Store, your download of the game should have started and will then be ready for you to start playing at release.

In a recent development, rockstar has now officially confirmed the GTA 6 file size on PS5, and what you'll need to download, is 212GB. This is one of the biggest downloads and installs on console in history, and will probably only get bigger through patches and the addition of GTA Online.

According to recent reports, GTA 6 will unlock and be playable in Latest Zealand first, as it's located in one of the furthest along timezones. The chances are New Zealand is further along in the day compared to wherever you live in the world, so yes, New Zealand will be the first to gain access to GTA 6.

In a recent development, because of this, there's been a rising trend in people creating Newly introduced Zealand PSN account to play games as soon as possible. This can be done with GTA 6 too, but you'll need to set up a fresh New Zealand PSN account and buy the game through its PS Store.

Sources indicate that when you have done, you'll be able to play GTA 6 a few hours ahead of other countries like the USA. Keep in mind, though, that you can't transfer your save data from the New Zealand account you set up to your normal profile.

If you were to play GTA 6 early via the New Zealand method detailed above, you get access to the game at these times:North America: 3am PST / 4am MST / 5am CST / 6am ESTUK/Ire: 11am GMTEurope: 12pm CET / 1pm EETAsia/Oceania: 8pm JST / 7pm AWST / 10pm AEDT.

According to recent reports, GTA 6 is available digitally on the PS Store, or in a basic physical version that ships without a disc. The latter provides a code in the box that you redeem on the PS Store to get the digital version.

The two PS5 digital versions on offer are: Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition.

Ultimate Edition / Ultimate Edition Upgrade Contents:.

Industry observers note that rockstar is only selling GTA 6 in a digital format; you can't buy the game on a physical disc in any way. Even the version you buy in retail shops or from online retailers won't contain a disc. Instead, the actual GTA 6 box will have a code inside you can use to download the game. There's no version with a physical disc of any kind.

Rockstar has now confirmed a GTA 6 map will be made available, but it won't be included in the physical versions like for previous games. Instead, a physical GTA 6 map will be put on the official Rockstar store for $0.99. Or, it'll be included for free in all other purchases from the storefront.
