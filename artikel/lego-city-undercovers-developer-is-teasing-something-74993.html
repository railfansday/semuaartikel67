Original Story: If you're itching to jump into a busy city filled with chaos, you might want to keep an eye on TT Games this week.

Industry observers note that the developer behind multiple LEGO video games is apparently teasing "something" related to LEGO City Undercover. Here's its response to a recent Warner Bros. Games social media post highlighting some LEGO City Undercover gameplay:.

TT Games: "Speaking of LEGO® CITY Undercover, we got something for you all tomorrow!!".

It has been revealed that as noted, we'll apparently find out more about this tomorrow. LEGO City Undercover started life on the Wii U in 2013, and sees you take on the role of the undercover police officer Chase McCain. The title was most recently released on the Switch in 2017.

There's no other information about what exactly TT Games might be teasing here, but it's already led to plenty of requests - with fans calling for a Switch 2 version of the game, and even a sequel.

Notably, at a convention in Zurich last September, LEGO City Undercover was apparently spotted on a Nintendo Switch 2 banner alongside multiple other LEGO titles.
