We were all expecting Grand Theft Auto 6 to have a lot of easter eggs, secrets, and discoveries for players across the wide world of Leonida, but there's even more than any of us could have imagined.

The community has been finding all of these wild secrets over the past few weeks, but one of the most surprising comes in the form of a layered nod to Nintendo's flagship series. Here's how to see it.

Sources indicate that to reach one of GTA 6's most surprising easter eggs, you'll need to head to the bottom part of Moreau in Lummox County, to the north of the overall map. The location you're looking for is a dilapidated building, which we've marked on the map above since it can be quite difficult to make out until you know what you're looking for.

In a recent development, once here, you'll need to smoke the bong that you'll find on a table in the middle of the building, next to a yellow box you can open for some cash. Beyond being a reference to all of those obvious jokes about Mario and mushrooms, doing so will cause a nearly invisible block to show up at the top of the ruined building. You'll then need to climb up and shoot the block, which will cause it to drop two hats.

It has been revealed that these hats, a purple one with J on the front and a teal one with L on the front, are an obvious but delightful reference to Super Mario. You don't need both characters there to get both hats, as you can pick them up as either protagonist. Just be careful they don't go flying too far and get lost.

Recent findings suggest that as I found out the hard way, you really don't want both characters there while you're trying to climb the building, as they'll consistently knock each other off. It does make for a funny sight, though.
