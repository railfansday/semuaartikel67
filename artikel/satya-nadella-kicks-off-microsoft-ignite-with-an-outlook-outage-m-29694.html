Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella opened his Ignite keynote Tuesday in San Francisco by addressing the elephant in the room: the global Outlook outage that cut off email for people and organizations in more than 70 countries last week.

Before getting to Copilot, agents or any of the event’s news, Nadella remarked the outage was a reminder of how critical digital infrastructure has become as “the dial tone for the world.”.

Sources indicate that “We know the trust you place in our services and take that responsibility extremely seriously,” he mentioned. “And in that context, I know we have work to do to provide that resilience that you can count on, the world can count on, and we are committed to ensuring that we learn and we continuously improve.”.

The line drew applause from the crowd, interrupting Nadella as he moved to thank the IT pros in the audience who kept their own organizations running through the outage.

Recent findings suggest that it was a brief acknowledgment, and he didn’t go into what went wrong. Microsoft’s preliminary report blamed a bug in a fix it began rolling out in July, which its own automatic rerouting spread until most of the servers in a key Microsoft 365 gateway stopped responding. A final report is due Nov. 20.
