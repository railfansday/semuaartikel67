If you look to celebrities like Anne Hathaway and Kate Middleton for style inspiration, you should be carrying around a DeMellier handbag. The British handbag label is known for its A-list clientele, understated aesthetics, and luxury quality at an accessible price point. To kick off its massive Black Friday sale, DeMellier is offering ELLE readers early access—use the code ELLE20 at checkout for 20 percent off sitewide, starting now.

Shop DeMellier’s Early Black Friday Sale.

Industry observers note that as a longtime DeMellier fan, I’m here to guide you through the best celebrity-worn handbags to shop during the brand’s Black Friday sale. Many styles are expected to sell out when the event opens to the public on November 20, so it’s worth doing your shopping during ELLE’s early access period to secure your top picks.

If you’re fresh to the brand, start with the label’s bestselling New York bag. The timeless silhouette comes in four sizes and over a dozen different leather, suede, and calf hair finishes. The east-west New York shoulder bag that Anne Hathaway carried during the Mother Mary press tour would make for a memorable holiday gift—though you’ll be tempted to keep it for yourself after experiencing the quality materials and construction IRL.

Recent findings suggest that earlier this year, Lindsay Lohan wore the full-sized Latest York bag while filming the upcoming Hulu series Count My Lies. (A different DeMellier number Lohan wore on set has already sold out, so I can’t guarantee her brown leather tote will stay in stock, either.) Emily Ratajkowski, Gemma Chan, Emily Blunt, Katie Holmes, Renée Zellweger, and Reese Witherspoon have also been seen carrying DeMellier’s New York silhouette, should you need any more convincing.

Recent findings suggest that famously, DeMellier is a favorite bag brand of the British royal family. Kate Middleton has made more than ten public appearances with the Nano Montreal in tow. The Small Hudson bag the Duchess of Wales carried during a recent visit to India would make the perfect plus-one for holiday party outfits. Queen Camilla, Meghan Markle, and Princess Beatrice have also worn DeMellier bags, though their picks have sold out.

Recent findings suggest that earlier this year, Meryl Streep wore the full-sized version of Middleton’s Hudson bag during the Devil Wears Prada 2 press tour. Large enough for a laptop, the carryall makes a chic alternative to a traditional work tote and has also received the stamp of approval from Emma Roberts, Kristen Bell, and Elsa Hosk.

It has been revealed that the brand’s minimalist Stockholm silhouette has become another celebrity favorite—and it’s won over ELLE fashion editors as a work bag, too. Naomi Watts wore the versatile Stockholm shoulder bag while promoting Season 2 of All’s Fair. And if you need more convincing on this particular collection’s versatility, it’s been spotted on the likes of Rosie Huntington-Whiteley, Lili Reinhart, and Simone Ashley.

According to recent reports, after close to a decade in the spotlight, nearly every DeMellier bag has found a celebrity fan, so there are no wrong choices.
