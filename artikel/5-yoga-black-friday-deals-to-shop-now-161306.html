It has been revealed that black Friday feels better when you can approach it with mindfulness in mind. After all, having sales and discounts constantly waved in your digital face can make it all too tempting to snap up one of everything and in every color. But if you can release urgency and bring intention into equation, Black Friday becomes an opportunity to take inventory of what you already have and thoughtfully fill any obvious gaps or perhaps finally purchase that product you’ve been eyeing for awhile.

Sources indicate that among some of our most coveted sales picks this Black Friday are a luxury (and top-performing) yoga mat, two new takes on yoga dressing, a naturally supportive prop, and a smart ring for the meditative among us. And the best part? These items are on our list because we find them to be worthwhile investments. Each just happens to be on sale.

If your practice could use a bit of extra support, you might want to invest in a bolster. If you’ve been looking for a sustainable option that brings more structure than a regular pillow you might grab at home, this cork bolster from Yoloha might be your perfect fit—particularly at its currently discounted price. The natural, recycled cork bolster (yes, it’s also stuffed with cork) is comfortable, substantial, and easy to clean. It’s a durable yoga prop that’s designed to last, no matter how many Savasanas and Reclining Bound Angles it supports.

A form-fitting yet breathable bodysuit is more of an activewear want than a need. Which is why snagging one on sale makes a lot of sense. Lululemon’s Align Cami Bodysuit is stylish and comfortable, carrying you from coffee to yoga to a long walk with ease. Also, practicing in a skin-skimming bodysuit is surprisingly comfortable, with the single piece conforming to your body sans the distracting rolling or tucking that can come with separates. And as noted, it’s a piece that goes beyond your mat. Seriously, just add a hoodie and it’s an instant outfit.

This top-rated yoga mat might be a go-to for many yogis, but the price tag likely provides pause for others. That makes it an ideal candidate for your Black Friday considerations. Liforme’s sleek, smooth, and instantly recognizable mats are known for their functionality and grip, with the eco-polyurethane surface keeping you in place even during the sweatiest of hot yoga classes.

The brand’s annual Black Friday sale features up to 40 percent off, with an extra 15 percent discount when you purchase two yoga mats and an extra 25 percent off when you purchase three or more.

Sometimes a pair of tight yoga leggings doesn’t feel like the coziest option for class. For those in the market for a loose, wide-legged option, Athleta’s Dream Drape Mid-Rise Pant is soft and flowy, making it a viable option for your yoga practice and your daily lounge session. Choose from one of three colors, and a multitude of fits, all of which are currently on sale.

The Pulse Mindfulness Ring is sort of an anti-smart ring. All it does is pulse at random or dictated intervals; you get to decide what that pulse means. You can invite the pulse to serve as a reminder to breathe more deeply, focus, relax your shoulders, or whatever other intention fits your needs. After testing the ring for a month, I can attest to the lightweight little accessory’s power to elicit calm.
