With the new third season of Percy Jackson and the Olympians on Disney+, this is a great time to dive into Rick Riordan’s books, whether you’re reading them for the first time, rereading them, or picking up where you left off once upon a time. In true camp fashion, here’s your itinerary for reading all of the Percy Jackson books in order. (Or the Camp Half-Blood Chronicles, if you’d prefer to call them that.).

According to recent reports, in the world that Riordan has created, demigods roam the Earth in modern times. For the most part, they’re indistinguishable from regular kids. Some of them, like Percy Jackson himself, don’t even know that their parents are literally figures from Greek and Roman mythology. Since there are so many of those centuries-old tales, Riordan has been able to turn them into a ton of young adult adventures.

In the first book, Percy Jackson learns that he’s a demigod and enrolls at Camp Half-Blood. He meets Annabeth Chase and Grover Underwood, and the three of them go on a quest to find Zeus’ lightning bolt.

This time, the teenage demigods go looking for the Golden Fleece. They’re joined by Tyson, Percy’s brother who happens to be a cyclops.

It has been revealed that in the third book, which happens to be the basis for the current season of the Disney+ series, the group expands and they go on a rescue mission. For who exactly? You’re going to have to read to find out!

Camp Half-Blood is under attack (again), and our heroes enter the mythical Labyrinth to save it from invading forces.

In the (original) grand finale, the fight moves from Camp Half-Blood to Manhattan.

Fifteen years later, Riordan returned to his original hero with a duology about Percy’s last year in school. This means quests and college applications.

The final (unless there’s more) book is set at Halloween as he pet sits for Hecate. Should be easy!

But wait, there’s more! Riordan has dozens of other stories, long and short, in this universe. Here’s enough to get you started:.

This is a series about a group of seven demigods who embark on a quest together.

This series is, like it says on the tin, about Apollo.

Nico also appears in Heroes of Olympus. This continues his story.

This series is set in the same universe, but all about Egyptian mythology.

These short stories are Percy Jackson and Kane sibling crossovers.

This trilogy is about Annabeth’s cousin and his dealings in Norse mythology.

It has been revealed that the most recent book, co-written by Annabelle Oh, has a female protagonist: Katie Kim, daughter of Ares. It will get a sequel called The Rite of Aphrodite in March 2027.
