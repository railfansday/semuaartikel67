Manchester United have won their appeal against Leny Yoro’s red card at Liverpool, meaning the centre-back will not face a suspension.

Yoro will now be available for Saturday’s match against Brentford, which will be Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge at Old Trafford.

According to recent reports, the France international was sent off in the eighth minute at Anfield for bringing down Liverpool striker Alexander Isak. The score was 0-0 at the time but Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free kick and United went on to lose 4-0 in Pochettino’s bow.

The Argentinian voiced his displeasure with referee Sam Barrott’s decision to dismiss Yoro after the game on Sunday, calling it an “unfair situation”.

He explained: “I don’t agree because the ball wasn’t in Isak’s control.” Pochettino felt that United goalkeeper Senne Lammens would have got to the ball first.

United informed the FA on Monday that they intended to appeal against the centre-back’s second red card of the season.

And an FA spokesperson said: “Leny Yoro's one-match suspension has been removed following a successful claim of wrongful dismissal.

“The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November.

“The independent regulatory commission's reasons for its decision will be published in due course.”.

United were also aggrieved earlier this season when an “error of judgement” was made in allowing Manchester City’s winner at Old Trafford.

Defeat at Anfield left Pochettino’s side 19th in the Premier League, with just eight points from their first 11 games of the season.

Yoro is available to face Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday while Luke Shaw has returned from injury and will travel to Portugal.
