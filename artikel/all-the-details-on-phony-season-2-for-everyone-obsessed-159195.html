According to recent reports, it’s been a moment since a show’s had me more locked in than Phony on Hulu, and with just one more episode left, inquiring minds (me) want to know: WHEN IS SEASON 2? And also key: is there even going to be a season 2?

Phony follows high schooler Sonny Cooke, who wakes up in the hospital after a car accident to find that his mom seems...off. For starters, her facial features are ever-so-slightly different—but there’s also the fact that she doesn’t remember their special way of saying “I love you” (three hand squeezes), and the fact that she’s just acting generally weird. Was she swapped out by an imposter? Does Sonny have Capgras syndrome? Is there some sort of alternate reality happening? Hopefully we’ll find out by the end of season 1, but then what? Which brings us to...

According to recent reports, not yet. But given how freely the cast and creators are talking about a second season (and the fact that the show is pretty popular), it seems likely to get a sophomore pickup.

Yes...but it’ll also end on a cliffhanger. Creator Nick Paley told Us Weekly “I’m really excited about the cathartic emotionality of the [season 1] cliffhanger. This thing kicks us into the next story in this world because I don’t like constantly depriving people of information. I want this to feel like it means a lot and it feels very notable to the characters emotionally. So that cliffhanger does both. Every episode has very significant turns at the end. When you’re telling a mystery, it’s really important to show it to people who don’t know.”.

Paley told the Los Angeles Times that Phony Season 2 will feature “a whole new story” after the season 1 finale delivers “resolutions and answers.”.

The majority of Phony Season 1 was filmed from March through May of 2026, and it premiered in October—a pretty short production time. So, if the show follows a similar schedule for season 2 and starts filming this spring, we could be looking at another October premiere.

In a recent development, potentially a LOT. Connie Britton, who plays Ellen, told The Wrap, "I think Nick has this thing laid out through probably five or six seasons. He's so clear about what the story is, and so I would be very surprised if he has doesn't have Season 2 all ready to go.".
