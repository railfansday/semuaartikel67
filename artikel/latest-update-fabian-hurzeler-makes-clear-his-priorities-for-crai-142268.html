Fabian Hurzeler and Brighton host Matthias Jaissle and his Newcastle United team on Sunday in the Premier League.

However, the Seagulls have a match to play before they face Newcastle United.

Later on Thursday the south coast club play Universitatea Craiova in the UEFA Conference League.

Whilst Fabian Hurzeler insists that this next match is the only vital one he is giving focus to at this moment in time,  his actions suggest otherwise.

The Brighton boss admits he is resting Diego Gomez tonight ahead of Sunday.

Fabian Hurzeler is also tonight leaving out Olivier Boscagli who has been ill and Jaouen Hadjam who has had a minor knock, but both like Diego Gomez set to be involved against Newcastle United if selected.

It shouldn’t do Newcastle United any harm the fact that Brighton have this extra game to play, at the same time though, the demands of a Premier League club also competing in the third level UEFA competition are a little different to trying to cope with the Champions League as well as the Premier League schedule.

Industry observers note that brighton’s top player this season has been Pascal Gross and he could also be rested against the Romanian side tonight and kept for Newcastle United. With Fabian Hurzeler stating that he will rely on his “very trustful relationship” with Pascal Gross as to whether the midfielder will be up to the challenge of starting both matches.

Hurzeler revealed that Malik Yalcouye is out now for up to 10 weeks and misses both tonight and the Newcastle United match.

Joao Costinha is another who will definitely miss out against the Magpies, he is suspended after having been sent off during the 20 defeat at Hull City last weekend.

Kaoru Mitoma (knee), Stefanos Tzimas (thigh), Evan Ferguson (ankle) and Zadok Yohanna (thigh) are also set to be missing for both of these upcoming matches.

However, Femi Azeez could be back from a thigh injury tonight, though whether he would be able to cope with playing against both Universitatea Craiova and Newcastle United is an unknown.
