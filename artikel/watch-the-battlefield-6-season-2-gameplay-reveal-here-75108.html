📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that after a one-month delay, we’re finally days away now from the start of Battlefield 6’s Season 2 of live service. The second season is being revealed today, following weeks of teasers, and some Battlefield Labs testing of the first new map arriving with the new season.

It’s all going to kick off with a gameplay reveal, which is set to stream live later today.

It has been revealed that as we’ve come to expect, the Season 2 reveal will take place via a YouTube premiere. It’s set to kick off live at 8am PT, 11am ET, 4pm GMT on the official Battlefield channel on YouTube - and embedded below. Today’s reveal will also be followed by a more detailed roadmap of Season 2, which will give us a much better idea about the volume and cadence of content we can expect.

It has been revealed that season 2 goes live February 17, kicking off a new chapter in the continued conflict between NATO and Pax Armata. The medium-large new map, Contaminated, will be available immediately at the season’s launch. Contaminated takes placed in the mountains of Germany, centered around a once NATO-owned airbase.

It has been revealed that VL-7 psychoactive smoke appears to factor majorly into the gameplay of this new season, though we’re waiting to hear more details on that. We can expect new weapons, new gadgets, and the awaited return of the AH-6 Little Bird helicopter.

Look forward to more coverage from us as more details are revealed later today.
