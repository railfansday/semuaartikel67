On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 11, 2026, Telefônica Brasil (VIV +0.33%) announced a 7.2% year-over-year revenue increase alongside double-digit net income expansion. For long-term investors, the telecommunications operator is converting high-margin digital service growth and legacy network cost reductions into expanding free cash flow.

Three takeaways from management's comments highlight how the company is driving value.

Decommissioning older copper infrastructure is yielding immediate structural expense savings for the business. Fully depreciating legacy assets reduces ongoing depreciation charges, spreading asset costs over time, while selling copper generates incremental cash gains.

In a recent development, by integrating Starlink satellite links directly into customer smartphones, the operator expanded its service reach across rural regions. The strategy broadens coverage from 20% of Brazil's landmass to nearly 100% without heavy capital expenditures.

Operating leverage is driving earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA), a key gauge of core operating profit, higher as fiber convergence expands. Management is maintaining pricing discipline while monitoring capital allocation efficiency to widen its network advantage.

Robust cash generation and low net debt provide financial flexibility to balance network investments with shareholder returns.
