After taking over as Germany coach, Jürgen Klopp made Marc-André ter Stegen his first-choice goalkeeper.

Speaking to ARD on Monday, Klopp revealed that he has long been an admirer of the 34-year-old, who is right now on loan at Ajax from Barcelona.

In fact, the former Liverpool manager had previously tried to bring the German shot-stopper to Anfield during his time on Merseyside.

“I have always considered him an exceptional goalkeeper and wanted to bring him to Liverpool in the past,” Klopp said.

Ter Stegen ultimately opted to remain at Barcelona, but Klopp insists there are no hard feelings.

“I'm not one to hold a grudge,” the Germany head coach added with a smile.
