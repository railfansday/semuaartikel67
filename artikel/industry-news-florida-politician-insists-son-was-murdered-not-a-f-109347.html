Two days after a 19-year-old was fatally shot while camping, his politician father is insisting his son’s death “was not a freak hunting accident” but the result of “malicious intent.”.

Escambia County District 4 Commissioner Joe Vinson claimed that his son, Carey Washington Vinson, was inside his tent at a sandbar campground near Blackwater River in Florida on Sunday, November 22, when a bullet fired from outside of the campsite struck his son.

Chief Deputy Randy Tifft of the Santa Rosa County Sheriff’s Office previously mentioned that Carey, 19, and his friends had been camping in different areas and sandbars since Friday, November 20, according to People. When Carey was shot in the head at 11:30 p.m., a vehicle was reportedly parked on the other side of the river.

Carey’s friends claimed to authorities that they heard the vehicle turn on and start driving after the shots were fired. The friends also remarked they yelled in the direction of the vehicle to make it known that they were in the area.

Tifft added that no one should have been hunting in the area because it was so late at night.

On Tuesday, November 24, Vinson — who was sworn in as the first non-party-affiliated District 4 commissioner in Escambia County’s history on November 17 — issued a statement about the tragedy, saying he needed to “correct a misimpression” that the shooting was an accident.

“This was not a freak hunting accident — not someone firing carelessly in the dark,” he remarked in the statement posted via social media.

Vinson went on to say that he believed the gunfire was deliberately directed at the campsite where his son was.

“As we understand it, some person(s) stood on the opposite bank of the river and pointed a gun at the sandbar campground, knowing it was occupied,” Vinson said. “They were close enough to see the campfire and hear people talking. They fired shot after shot with malicious intent, and after a pause when the panicked campers screamed ‘stop,’ they fired another volley, then laughed as they drove away.”.

Vinson then shared insight into who his son was as a person, saying he went “from a sweet, goofy kid into a sweet, thoughtful young man.”.

“He could play Beethoven on piano and Jeff Buckley on guitar, and he had a beautiful singing voice,” Vinson remarked of his son, who was a student at Florida State University. “He was so outgoing and athletic, playing soccer, hiking mountains, and beating the rest of us to the top of Giotto’s Tower and Mount Fuji.”.

Vinson added that Carey “had a tender and loving heart — for his friends and family, for his girlfriend, for animals, for the marginalized.”.

Industry observers note that “He was quick to laugh and easy to love. I can’t believe I’ll never feel his hugs again, or hear him bickering with his siblings,” he said. “I can’t believe any of this. Every impatient moment, every unkind word, I will gladly take them back if you would just come back to us.”.

Vinson concluded the post by asking those with any information about the case to contact authorities.

“I know it won’t bring back Wash, but I would sleep better knowing they can’t hurt anyone else,” he remarked.

The sheriff’s office has also asked anyone with information about the shooting to call 850-983-1190, Option 1. Those who wish to remain anonymous can contact Santa Rosa Crime Stoppers at 850-437-7867 or submit information through the P3 Tips mobile app.
