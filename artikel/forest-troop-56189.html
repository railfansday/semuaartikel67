Industry observers note that baboons are assholes. After leaving his birth troop at around age seven, a male will find a newly introduced one and spend his prime years trying to fang his way up the hierarchy. Once he’s gained a modicum of status, he devotes his days to terrorizing lower-ranking males and females, who only grow to half his size. Gashes, puncture wounds, and missing ears are not uncommon among baboons.

It has been revealed that in the mid-1980s, Dr. Robert M. Sapolsky, a primatologist living in Kenya, documented an unprecedented cultural shift when he noticed that the alpha males of a baboon population suddenly vanished. The alphas had come upon a garbage dump near a tourist lodge, and they defended their spoils furiously from low-ranking males and females, who were relegated to leaves, seeds, and insects on the forest floor.
