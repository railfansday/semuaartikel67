Liverpool have to sign a midfielder and there's no getting away from that for Julian Ward.

The first few months of Andoni Iraola's era at Anfield have made it clear that change is not only imperative, but it's inevitable.

Like the tides move, and seasons change, Liverpool have also come to the end of the road for their current midfield cohort.

With Iraola persisting with his double pivot role, he needs midfielders who are capable of playing in that position in the way he wants them to play.

In a recent development, iraola's philosophy has always been about moving the ball forward rapidly. He wants midfielders who will advance the play not through carries but by clever line-breaking passes.

As the age old adage goes, the ball moves quicker than your feet.

Who should Liverpool's next midfielder be?So, the focus should be on bringing in a midfielder who can play those progressive passes while also maintaining stability in midfield. Liverpool have missed someone capable of winning duels and interceptions in the middle of the park in the same way Fabinho was able to. Many people have identified Lamine Camara as the perfect choice for the club's evolution. But what if there is someone else who might just be a better fit? Back in 2023, Liverpool came close to signing Pape Demba Diop. In the end, he suffered an injury and the Reds were unable to follow up on their interest. But now Diop is catching the eye in Ligue 1 and he's about to work under Jurgen Klopp's former disciple in Vitor Matos, who could be in his ears about a move to Anfield.
