Sources indicate that projections of future climate change show a relatively cooler world thanks to the growth of renewable energy. And yet the world is still on track to reach around 2.6˚C of warming by the end of the century — far beyond what many climate scientists say we need to avoid the worst impacts of climate change. So, what are we going to do about it?

In the second episode of Cooked? I talk to experts around the world about their big ideas of what we can do to tackle climate change. We talk about energy, food, economics and how the world needs to adapt to a warmer world.
