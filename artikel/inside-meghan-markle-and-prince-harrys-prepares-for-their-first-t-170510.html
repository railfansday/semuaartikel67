📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Meghan Markle and Prince Harry celebrated Thanksgiving a little differently this year. The couple remained in the U.K. for the holiday with their children, 7-year-old Archie and 5-year-old Lilibet. According to People, the family had activities in their local community on the calendar, though the Duke and Duchess of Sussex kept their exact prepares private on Thursday.

Recent findings suggest that finding time to give back to the community has become something of a tradition for them. In 2025, Meghan, Harry, and the kids joined a volunteer event at Our Massive Kitchen Los Angeles through the Archewell Foundation. Together, they helped prepare and package meals for local schools, shelters, senior centers, and other community organizations.

Recent findings suggest that ahead of Thanksgiving, Meghan also spoke with Bon Appétit about her approach to hosting. She explained that she prefers setting the table a day in advance so she can spend more time with guests once they arrive. For overnight visitors, she prepares the coffee machine ahead of time and stocks the kitchen with breakfast essentials.

“I still believe little touches are all you need to make something ordinary feel special, especially when you’re having loved ones over,” she told the publication. “It doesn’t have to be over the top.”.

Yesterday, Meghan’s lifestyle brand, As Ever, posted a video that showed her preparing a festive tablescape. In the promotional clip, Meghan lights a candle and arranges party favors in an ivory look.

The family is also reportedly planning to celebrate Christmas at their new home, with Meghan’s mother, Doria Ragland, expected to join them.
