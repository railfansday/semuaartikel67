Christos Tzolis has not improved Arsenal's left-hand side of attack since arriving.

The Gunners needed to add another name to the wide forward areas, as that was a clear weakness in their squad.

Greek international Tzolis was deemed to be that player, with Arsenal forking out £34million to bring him to the Emirates from Club Brugge.

His arrival then allowed Arsenal to sell Gabriel Martinelli for a club-record £60m fee, but in many ways, the Gunners may be weaker for that fact.

Tzolis, 24, has been a regular when fit, starting seven of his eight Premier League appearances so far.

But he may already be a player that Mikel Arteta is looking to upgrade on in the coming transfer windows.

Tzolis has struggled to make the adaptation from the Belgian Pro League to the Premier League.

While he produced 40 goal contributions (17 goals and 23 assists) in 2025-26, he has been far off the pace for Arsenal.

Tzolis has only managed to obtain one league assist and has yet to score in any competition for Arteta's side.

Additionally, as per FotMob, his underlying numbers do not make for a positive reading either.

This season, Tzolis has produced just 0.45 xG across his 538 Premier League minutes, which equates to just 0.08 per 90.

Hardly a threat for his opposition to look out for when facing up against the winger.

Furthermore, his creative numbers are not great either, with just 0.91 expected assists in total.

He sits in the 27th percentile for significant chances created with just one this campaign.

Overall, Arsenal need a lot more from Tzolis, as across the 11 games this season, it is evident he is the weakest link in Arsenal's common starting forward line.

Arsenal will undoubtedly be keeping tabs on the market for possible forward signings, and it appears they are once again looking in Club Brugge's direction.

At Football Insider, we have shared that Arsenal are well-placed to swoop for Carlos Forbs.

The Portuguese winger, like Tzolis, is impressing in the Belgian top flight; the Gunners must be tentative not to follow down the same path.

Tzolis is evidently not equipped for the Premier League, and the same issues could arise with Forbs.

Arsenal should spend more than £80m on a genuinely elite left-sided flyer like Antonia Nusa, who is headed for the very top.
