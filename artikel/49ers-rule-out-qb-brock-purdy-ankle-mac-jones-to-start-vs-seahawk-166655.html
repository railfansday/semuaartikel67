San Francisco 49ers QB Brock Purdy will not play this week against the Seattle Seahawks due to an ankle injury, the team announced Friday.

Mac Jones, who won five of eight starts last season while Purdy was out with a toe injury, will start the NFC West clash Sunday.

According to recent reports, purdy suffered the ankle injury during last week's win over the Minnesota Vikings. The quarterback continued playing after getting hurt in the second quarter and finished with 255 passing yards and three touchdowns as the 49ers improved to 9-1.

Entering Sunday, the 49ers have a three-game lead over the Seahawks and 2 ½ over the Los Angeles Rams in the NFC West. They are currently tied with the 9-2 Chicago Bears atop the conference for the most wins.

It has been revealed that while the 49ers will be without their starting quarterback, the Seahawks will be getting their QB1 back this week. Sam Darnold (quad) has missed the past two games, but has been cleared to return in Week 12.

Recent findings suggest that darnold needed to show Mike Macdonald that he could get through Wednesday's practice before the Seahawks head coach would be confident enough to start him. Darnold did just that and will play, though it will be behind an offensive line that will be without guard Quenton Nelson (knee) and right tackle Abe Lucas (ankle), who were ruled out earlier this week.
