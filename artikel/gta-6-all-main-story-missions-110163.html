There’s no denying that GTA 6 is one of the largest games to ever be released, that’s even excluding all of the ridiculous things you can do like hunting for the Kraken. There’s always something to keep you distracted in Leonida.

In a recent development, that being noted, a lot of people are eager to move the story forward and see what exactly awaits them in Vice City, and if they'll all involve fixing a house. If you’re one of those people then knowing how many missions await you can be a major deal.

In a recent development, below you’ll find pretty much all the info you need on the different chapter missions that separate the story of GTA 6. There’s a lot here to cover and you’ll need to be wary of spoilers as some of the mission titles give a bit away.

In totality there are 93 missions that fuel the story from beginning to end. You’ll have to complete all the missions in a chapter to move on to the next one.

The number of missions is divided into the chapters below:.

- Chapter 1: 22 missions - Chapter 2: 13 missions - Chapter 3: 26 missions - Chapter 4: 25 missions - Chapter 5: 7 missions.

With Chapter 3 and 4 being the largest of the bunch, you’ll likely want to spend more time there than in the early-game hunt for items. It’s going to take some serious game time to get that far, so don’t forget to enjoy the ride along the way.

Recent findings suggest that below you’ll find all of the titles of the missions and the chapter they belong to. This can contain some major spoilers for the story, so read at your own peril.

There are a ton of missions to complete for the story and even more side content to find and unlock. Whether it’s dealing with crazy NPCs out for blood or trying to piece together how to get Jason and Lucia to be romantic, you’ll always be busy in Vice City.
