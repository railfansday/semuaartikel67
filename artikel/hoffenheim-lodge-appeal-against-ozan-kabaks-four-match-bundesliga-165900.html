Hoffenheim lodge appeal against Ozan Kabak’s four-match Bundesliga ban

According to recent reports, TSG Hoffenheim have officially appealed the four-match suspension handed to Turkish international Ozan Kabak following his dismissal against Eintracht Frankfurt. According to Kicker, the 26-year-old defender’s case will be heard by the DFB sports court on Friday, December 4.

Kabak received a straight red card in stoppage time after grabbing and pushing Frankfurt midfielder Oscar Højlund in retaliation for a heavy challenge. The subsequent four-match ban would rule the centre-back out of Hoffenheim’s remaining Bundesliga fixtures before the winter break.

The Kraichgauer hope to have the suspension reduced to three matches, which would allow Kabak to return for their final league encounter of 2026 against Union Berlin. The DFB sports court could nevertheless also impose a harsher punishment following the hearing.

Recent findings suggest that the appeal comes at a particularly difficult time for head coach Christian Ilzer, who has just one fit specialist centre-back available in Koki Machida. Dutch midfielder Wouter Burger had been considered as a potential emergency replacement, but suffered an abdominal muscle injury during Thursday’s Europa League comeback victory over Sturm Graz.

Ilzer has nevertheless emphasized that Kabak retains his full confidence despite the disciplinary incident.

Recent findings suggest that “Ozan is someone I really enjoy working with,” Ilzer remarked after the Europa League tie. “He’s extremely reliable and always gives everything he has in a match. I’ve discussed it with him at length, and there has also been a consequence. Nevertheless, Ozan won’t lose my trust. He doesn’t need to make amends. He simply needs to learn from this situation.”.

Kabak will begin serving his suspension when Hoffenheim host Augsburg on Sunday evening.
