Liverpool have a lot of work to do on their team if they want to get back to the top of the Premier League table.

Most fans thought they'd be challenging for the title this term but, once again, they've failed to deliver.

Bradley Barcola was their marquee signing this summer and, even though he has managed to bag a hattrick, aside from that, he hasn't really been all that great.

Indeed, he has been used as a right-winger which isn't his preferred position but, considering how well he did for PSG and how much he interchanged with his teammates there, he should be performing a lot better.

However, it's not only Liverpool's attack that could do with an upgrade or two.

Liverpool could offer Alisson as part of a swap deal to sign Arthur Dias.

One player who has emerged as a potential option recently is Arthur Dias.

The Brazilian centre-back is one of Athletico Paranaense's best players at the moment and, despite him only being 21 years old, he's already made two appearances for Brazil.

In 2026 alone, the defender has featured in 37 games across all competitions and has even managed to bag himself a goal.

Even though he can only be used as a centre-back, his ability to command that role is immense.

A fee worth around €30 million is all that would be required to prise the youngster away from Brazil but, since Alisson has been linked with wanting to return to Brazil, a swap deal could also be on the cards.

Alisson's time at Liverpool could be coming to an end soon.

Liverpool only extended Alisson's stay at Anfield for one more season.

The Reds chose to activate their option to extend his contract after it was due to come to an end in June 2026.

Since then, he's made his position as Liverpool's number one 'keeper more than clear.

However, with Giorgi Mamardashvili among their ranks and Lucca Brughmans set to join next summer, Alisson could be on his way out.

Many different clubs have been linked with a move for him but, if the Reds can get someone such as Dias in return for their veteran 'keeper, they'd be laughing.

Right now, these links are just rumours and nothing is set in stone but, if this deal can be struck, it would be great news for the Anfield outfit.
