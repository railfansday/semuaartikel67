Former Valve developer Chet Faliszek – writer behind the likes of Half-Life 2, Portal and its sequel, Team Fortress 2, Left 4 Dead, and even Counter-Strike: Global Offensive – has one big regret about his time at the Steam firm: a joke he didn't make.

Yes, you read that right. He reveals as much in a newly introduced short over on YouTube, asking, "What's the thing I regret the most at Valve? I was there for 12 years. So, what's the thing that I regret, that I did or didn't do?" Apparently, after sitting on this question for some time, he's become brave enough to publicly share his answer – an answer he thinks of "almost every year," as it weighs that heavy in his mind. Brace yourselves for this recollection, y'all.

In a recent development, "If you remember, Valve had Steam Dev Days. We actually had a really early Steam Dev Days where I spoke at, and I spoke horribly, and I made a note that I've got to learn how to speak in public. I'm still not good at it, but it was just so bad. I was just shaking. It was just horrible. So, at this point, when Steam Dev Days two comes around, I'm like, 'Hey, I'll help out.'" Valve then decided to make him the host.

The joke Faliszek mustered up was "going to put Gabe on the spot" – a joke about his polo shirt, to be precise. "My joke was, 'And here's Gabe Newell modeling the latest in polo,' because he always wore a polo shirt. 'Today's selection is polo after dark.'" The dev knew Newell would "lean into it" had he said it, but he kept it to himself and laughed "so hard" without ever, uh, actually making the joke out loud.

Apparently that's nothing new for him, as he's often "laughing at jokes I don't say out loud because I'm thinking, 'That's going to push it.'" He does, again, regret not making this one in particular, though. "I regret that. I should have noted that joke. That was a good joke. I know it's not the greatest joke, but at the time with Gabe walking in his black polo, it would have worked." And, honestly, I'd have to agree.

According to recent reports, to this day, I seldom ever see Newell not wearing a polo shirt… if ever. I hope he tunes into Faliszek's channel and hears the joke, however, even if it's a good few years after it should've been spoken out loud.
