Nature writer reveals in his first Reith lecture that he finds ‘much more hope’ in emerging alliances.

We are “misplacing our trust” in the Cop climate summits and their “capacity to deliver consequential change”, according to Robert Macfarlane in this year’s Reith lectures.

In comments recorded before this week’s failure of the 31st UN climate summit to deliver an agreement on action to combat global heating, the bestselling author said he saw “much more hope” in emerging alliances such as the Santa Marta process, which is a smaller group of countries committed to phasing out fossil fuels as soon as possible.
