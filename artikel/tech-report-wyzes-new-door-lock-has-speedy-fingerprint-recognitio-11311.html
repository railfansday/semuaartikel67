Wyze is today launching its latest “Lever Lock,” a smart lock for your front door that has a year of battery life and fast fingerprint recognition, all for an affordable price tag.

Available starting today, the Wyze Level Lock is not your typical deadbolt smart lock. Rather, this is a smart lock designed for your standard single-bore latch, meaning it’s available for doors that other smart locks aren’t compatible with. You can still have a deadbolt, but you’ll need enough space for both (per Wyze’s guidelines below).

Industry observers note that the Lever Lock has a PIN entry and a fingerprint sensor, the latter of which Wyze states recognizes your fingerprint in about half a second. It uses local storage for biometric data and works to improve that data with each successful unlock.

According to recent reports, the PIN has a clever feature (though it’s presented in somewhat poor taste) that lets you input random digits before or after your actual code to prevent onlookers from being able to see your code. There’s also a physical key backup as well as a USB-C input on the outside in the case that the battery dies – Wyze states it lasts about 12 months on a charge using eight AA batteries.

The Wyze Lever Lock is available today for $99.98 and shipping immediately.
