According to recent reports, 15% is a decent rule of thumb. It’s a good starting point, especially for people early in their careers. Most people are saving a lot less. Nothing replaces individualized planning based on individual income and expenses. But that’s hard and requires a level of math most people aren’t comfortable with. For most people a rule of thumb is a good place to start.
