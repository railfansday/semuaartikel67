Collectible cards featuring fictional sports stars are dotted all over GTA 6, waiting to be discovered and sold if you need the cash. Now those trading cards are becoming real-life collectibles thanks to a Rockstar team-up with Panini.

Panini unveiled the good news on Tuesday via a press release, and although exactly when real-life GTA 6 trading cards will be available, and how many there will be to collect, remains unclear, we’ve broken down everything we know about what is sure to be an incredibly popular product so far.

There are 480 cards to find in Leonida, divided up between 24 sets. It’s unclear if all 480 of those cards will be getting real-life replicas. We do know that each GTA 6 Panini pack will include four cards. On average, each of those packs will include two rookies, one retro, one illustrated, and four special silvers.

Cards will also have pink, teal, and light blue parallels. Parallel cards are rarer versions of base Panini cards and stickers. Even rarer will be the bronze parallels that will be numbered up to 49, silver parallels of which there will only be 25 of each, and extremely rare 1-of-1 gold parallels of some cards.

It has been revealed that there isn’t an exact release date for the cards yet, but you won’t have long to wait until you can start collecting the cards you’ve been hunting down in Vice City in real life. Panini has confirmed they will be available from December 2026, and the hobby box store page is already live on Panini’s website.

No price confirmed just yet, nor is there any indication that the packs will be available to buy individually. The lack of any other listing outside of the page for the hobby box would suggest not. There is, thankfully, little-to-no risk that these cards won’t be available in the US like Panini’s recent The Legend of Zelda cards. The store page is listed on Panini’s US website.

Ten of the cards that will be a part of the collection have been revealed so far, spanning various sports, teams, and franchises that exist in the GTA 6 universe. Baseball, football, basketball, soccer - there's even a wrestling card in there. All ten cards we've been shown so far are listed below.

Stumbling upon these cards as I explore Leonida has been one of my favorite things about GTA 6 since it replicates the feeling I get in real life through collecting Pokemon and Magic: The Gathering cards. The news that these cards will soon be a thing I can buy in real life is fantastic news for my TCG obsession, but potentially bad news for my bank account. My real one, not my BuckMe one.
