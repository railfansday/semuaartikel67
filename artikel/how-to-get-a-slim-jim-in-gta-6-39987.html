Found your GTA 6 dream car but don't want to smash its windows? Or maybe you'd rather discreetly snag a getaway vehicle without setting off an alarm. There are plenty of ways to steal a car in GTA 6, and a slim jim is one of the more civilized options.

Unlocking a car with a slim jim also lets you pop the trunk without setting off an alarm, and you might find some goodies in car trunks like weapons, drugs, or cash.

Remember that unlocking the car is only half the battle. A slim jim won't start a car with keyless ignition, but some vehicles only need to be unlocked before you can hotwire them and drive off. Keyless cars are another can of worms, requiring a key cloner.
