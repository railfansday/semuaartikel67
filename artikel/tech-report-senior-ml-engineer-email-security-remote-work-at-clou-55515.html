CloudLinux is a global remote-first firm. We are driven by our principles: do the right thing, employees first, we are remote first, and we deliver high-volume, low-cost Linux infrastructure and security products that help companies to increase the efficiency of their operations. Every person on our team supports each other and does what we can to ensure we all are successful.

Check out our website for more information: https://cloudlinux.com/.

According to recent reports, cloudLinux Inc. develops the Imunify family of security products, designed to protect hosting environments from a wide range of cyber threats. One of these solutions is Imunify Email, an email security product that protects hosting servers against spam, phishing, and other email-based threats.

Imunify Email filters mail on about 5,000 hosting servers. Today the filter is rspamd with rules and a scoring model we train in-house. We are replacing it with a transformer we trained ourselves that runs on the customer's own CPU in under 100 MB and already beats rspamd at every operating point we measured. We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning Engineer to join our team and work alongside the engineer presently responsible for both the existing spam-filtering pipeline and the new ML model. Together, you will drive the development, improvement, and production rollout of our email security technology.
