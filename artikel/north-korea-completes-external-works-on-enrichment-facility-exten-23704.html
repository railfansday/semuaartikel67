North Korea completes external works on enrichment facility extension, indicates IAEA

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that A latest extension to North Korea's Yongbyon enrichment facility has been "externally complete" since mid-October, according to the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), which relies on satellite imagery and open sources to monitor the country's nuclear programme. IAEA chief Rafael Grossi on Wednesday mentioned expansion of Pyongyang's enrichment programme remains "cause for serious concern".
