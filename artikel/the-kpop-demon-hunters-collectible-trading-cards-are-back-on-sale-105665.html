It has been revealed that SAVE $5.70: As of Nov. 24, the KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards are on sale for $42.23 at Amazon. That's a 12% discount on the list price.

Recent findings suggest that black Friday isn't just a good time to pick up discounted tech. There are some great savings on collectibles, including trading cards inspired by one of Netflix's biggest movies. Right now, KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards are down to $42.23 at Amazon, saving you over $5.

It has been revealed that whether you're already an avid card collector or just can't get enough of KPop Demon Hunters, this premium collection is packed with references to the film. You'll get 96 cards per box, split into 12 packs of eight, featuring designs celebrating memorable performances, characters, and scenes. There are 11 different rarity levels to be collected, adding to the excitement.

Some of the standout cards to keep an eye out for include the individually numbered SE Cards, which feature laser-etched designs inspired by "GOLDEN," with serial numbers starting at 1366. There's also the chance to pull BP Cards, numbered from 1999, showcasing HUNTR/X in their black performance outfits before a concert.

If you've got a KPop Demon Hunters fan on your holiday shopping list, pick up this trading card set for less now in Amazon's Black Friday sale.
