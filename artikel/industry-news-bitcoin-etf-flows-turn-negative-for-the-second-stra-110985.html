Industry News: Bitcoin ETF flows turn negative for the second straight November — What’s changed?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that spot Bitcoin [BTC] ETFs have been facing a tough time thanks to consecutive days of outflows. According to SoSo Value data, U.S Spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $669 million in net outflows on 20th November, marking their fourth consecutive day of outflows.

That remarked, in 2026, it was only in the months of August, September, and April that Bitcoin ETFs attracted $3.52 billion, $2.65 billion, and $1.97 billion in inflows, respectively.

Sources indicate that on the contrary, the month of June recorded $4.51 billion in outflows, while May saw $2.43 billion leave these investment products. November has recorded approximately $4.14 billion in net outflows so far.

According to recent reports, november flows in Bitcoin Spot ETFs have swung sharply across the three years. For instance, November 2024 was the strongest, with net inflows of about $6.49 billion during the post-election rally, lifting cumulative inflows from $24.2 billion to $30.7 billion. However, November 2025 reversed to net outflows of 3.48 billion, while November 2026 has so far been worse. That hinted at a swing of roughly $10.6 billion from Nov 2024 to Nov 2026.

Glassnode’s analysis also pointed out this trend that Bitcoin ETF trading volume has fallen below its median since launch, indicating reduced market participation and interest.

Sources indicate that combined with sustained outflows, this alluded to weakening investor demand. However, lower volume can also reflect reduced speculative activity rather than outright bearish sentiment.

However, at times, periods of low interest and reduced market participation can offer better accumulation opportunities than periods of excessive excitement.

Here, it’s worth pointing out that these netflows coincided with BTC’s price trading close to $61,330 at press time, after a drop of 24% over the past month.

In a recent development, at the same time, Bitcoin has recorded several negative netflow readings throughout November, with the latest reading at around -1.1K BTC. In other words, more Bitcoin may be leaving exchanges than entering them. While this can reduce potential selling pressure on exchanges, it does not necessarily confirm accumulation.

Meanwhile, the Bitcoin Spot Volume Bubble Map revealed a noticeable change in market activity, with trading activity not particularly overheated during the price decline.

However, with recent readings showing some heating signs as the price approached the $60,000-level, caution will be useful.
