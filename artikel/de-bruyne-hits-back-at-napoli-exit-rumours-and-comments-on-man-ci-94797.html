Kevin De Bruyne had plenty to say ahead of his return to the Etihad Stadium with Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday night, criticising reporters at his press conference for creating an ‘unpleasant situation’ for him and his wife, while also commenting on Manchester City being found guilty of over 100 charges relating to the Premier League’s financial rules.

Industry observers note that de Bruyne is back in Manchester and is due to take on Manchester City in the Champions League league phase at 20.00 GMT on Tuesday night. This will be De Bruyne’s second visit to the Etihad since joining Napoli, having lost 2-0 against his former side in the league phase last September.

De Bruyne, who has been linked with a move away from Napoli in recent weeks, sat down for his pre-match press conference on Monday evening.

De Bruyne began with an assessment of his own game and said that he feels that he is having a decent season on an individual level.

Analysing the role he plays for Napoli, he said: “I’ve played in a lot of different roles in my career, I’ve changed positions a lot of times. Your role can always change in football.

“I play based on the things I feel on the pitch, where I see the space, where I believe I am most dangerous. I feel like I’m playing well this year, it doesn’t matter where on the pitch that is.”.

De Bruyne was then asked about his own future and the recent suggestions that he could leave Napoli in January, if not when his contract expires at the end of the season. Recent reports claim that PSV Eindhoven have made an offer for his services.

His wife was also the subject of a recent magazine interview in Belgium, in which she discussed the possibility of one day returning to the family home in Belgian Limburg, but De Bruyne feels that this was completely misinterpreted back in Italy.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m quite sorry about it all to be honest. If you go and read the article, which you have not read, you will understand what she mentioned. I was away in Belgium and then a lot of things were being said and there were a lot of negative reactions towards my wife.

“I saw photos of my house in the press, which is not good. I know that journalists in Italy have created this situation and I don’t know why. You do not know my private life, you have no right to comment on my private life. It was an unpleasant situation for me and my wife.”.

Pressed again on whether he could leave Napoli in the near future, De Bruyne stated: “I can’t say very much at this point. I’ve plated at 400% so far. I’m not 20 years old anymore, I’m almost 36, so there are lots of things to consider.

According to recent reports, “Right now, there’s not a lot to say. I’m playing well, I’m trying to help the team to do well. There have been lots of challenges this year and a lot of things have happened.”.

Sources indicate that on the prospect of facing Manchester City, with whom De Bruyne won six Premier League titles as well as the 2022-23 Champions League, he stated: “I understand the way that City play, obviously. I know Enzo [Maresca], I know the way he likes to play football and how he positions his players.

Members of the British press were keen to hear De Bruyne’s reaction to Manchester City being found guilty of over 100 breaches of the Premier League’s financial rules.

It has been revealed that he remarked: “This is my club, I love the owners. I’ve nothing but affection for this club and that will never change.

“Of course in life there are rules, everybody plays on the limit I would say and for me I play in between the white lines and I competed with other players and I had many experiences with my teams, with the players, so for me that is the thing that I value most and even later on in my career I think the most vital is the relationships between everybody that I had and how you manage these things.

“I’ve given my all and that is not going to change in my mind I don’t think. Other people will change in their mind how they view me as a player or how we played but everybody has their own opinion I guess.”.
