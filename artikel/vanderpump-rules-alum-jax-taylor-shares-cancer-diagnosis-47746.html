Jax Taylor is navigating a scary period in his health.

The Vanderpump Rules alum shared he’s been diagnosed with cancer after a tumor was discovered on his kidney during treatment for kidney stones in July.

In a recent development, “This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life,” Taylor wrote in a Nov. 19 Instagram post, which featured photos from his hospital bed and a video of him walking with the help of a walker. “What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined. My CT scan also showed a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”.

The 47-year-old—who starred on Bravo’s Vanderpump Rules for eight seasons and the first two seasons of The Valley—shared that the diagnosis was especially challenging given his father had died of cancer. As he put of hearing the word cancer, “It broke me.”.

“It put me in a really dark place,” he added. “The hardest part was the waiting. Knowing there was a tumor growing inside me while trying to go about my life like everything was fine.”.

Taylor confirmed that prior to posting, he’d undergone a five-hour surgery to remove the tumor. He noted that the tumor was larger than doctors had anticipated, however, and that they’re awaiting further pathology results.

The former reality star—who was previously married to VPR costar Brittany Cartwright, with whom he shares 5-year-old son Cruz—expressed his gratitude for his medical team at Cedars Sinai and for his girlfriend, publicist Lori Krebs, as he navigates this scary period.

According to recent reports, “I honestly don’t know how I would have gotten through everything without my amazing girlfriend by my side,” he wrote of Krebs, who was seen lying in a hospital chair in one of the photos in Taylor’s carousel. “She’s been my rock through all of this. She’s not just my partner, she’s my best friend and I feel so insanely lucky to have her in my life.”.

Taylor also reflected on how the last few months have affected the way he views his health and wellbeing.

According to recent reports, “If there’s one thing I hope people can take away from this, it’s never to take your health for granted,” he wrote. “I did for many years and I won’t ever do that again.”.

In fact, his surgery coincides with a personal milestone.

“Next week on November 28 will mark my 2 year sobriety anniversary and I’m so f--king proud,” Taylor added. “It’s crazy how things happen…if I hadn’t ended up in the hospital with that kidney stone, I probably wouldn’t have known this tumor was there until it was too late. I like to think my dad had a part in that.”.

It has been revealed that he added, “Life can really change in a second. I’ve been given a lot to think about these past few years and if there’s one thing I know for sure it’s that nothing else matters more than your health your kids and the people that show up for you when it matters the most.”.
