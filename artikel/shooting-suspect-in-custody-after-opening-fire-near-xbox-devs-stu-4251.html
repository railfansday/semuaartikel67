Shooting Suspect In Custody After Opening Fire Near Xbox Dev’s Studio

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A drive-by shooting in Southern California ended with no injuries and an arrest after the gunman reportedly opened fire near a business and office campus that houses Xbox subsidiary InXile Entertainment. According to Aftermath and local affiliate KTLA5, the shooting occurred on November 11 at an office building on the former Marine Corps Air Station in Tustin, California. The police department posted on Facebook that, after uncovering "multiple shell casings in the area," local detectives worked "around the clock" to locate the suspect. Sergeant Christopher Peterson told Aftermath on November 13 that although the investigation is still ongoing, an unnamed suspect had been apprehended "less than 48 hours" after the shooting. "Officers got there [on November 11]," Sergeant Peterson remarked. "They responded to a report of a drive-by shooting there. Located multiple shell casings. Nobody was hurt. Processed the crime scene, and then sometime [Friday] morning, a little before 11 [AM], they took a suspect into custody. And the investigation is still kind of ongoing as to where and why." An InXile Entertainment spokesperson corroborated the incident with Aftermath, expressing relief about the situation. "We're relieved that no one was hurt at our inXile office,” the spokesperson said. "Our focus is on supporting our team, and we are working closely with law enforcement as they investigate." Founded in 2002, InXile Entertainment has been under the Xbox Game Studios umbrella since 2018. Since its founding, the studio has developed several games, from the 2004 ARPG The Bard's Tale to the 2011 action-adventure Hunted: The Demon's Forge to the 2014 post-apocalyptic RPG Wasteland 2. Right now, the team is working on Clockwork Revolution, a steampunk ARPG not too dissimilar from immersive sims like BioShock and Dishonored. It has no release date right now, but InXile Entertainment is targeting a 2027 window for PC and Xbox Series X|S.
