As a Principal Executive Recruiter at GitLab, you’ll lead VP-level and other senior leadership searches across Engineering, Product, Security, IT, Data, and other technical teams. You’ll partner directly with senior executives to identify, engage, assess, and hire leaders, bringing market intelligence and a research-driven approach to complex, often confidential searches. Alongside owning searches end to end, you’ll help shape the strategies, frameworks, and practices that strengthen executive recruiting at GitLab, balancing search rigor with the agility of a fast-moving technology organization.

Sources indicate that you’ll join a globally distributed, remote-first Talent Acquisition team where clear communication and reliable follow-through matter. Guided by GitLab’s operating principles, including Speed with Quality, Ownership Mindset, and Customer Outcomes, we take ownership, move with urgency and rigor, and focus on hiring outcomes that matter to our internal customers. We share learnings and create reusable resources to continually improve the quality and consistency of hiring across Business teams.
