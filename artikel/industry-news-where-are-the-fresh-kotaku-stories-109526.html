If you logged onto Kotaku last night or this morning, you’ve probably been pretty confused. Why is the latest story from Sunday? What happened to everything we published on Monday?

Sources indicate that the answer is that the site is at the moment dealing with a network issue that’s forced us to operate off of an older backup. We’re currently working through the problem as rapidly as possible, but in the meantime we won’t be publishing any new stories since, as you can imagine, they’ll be part of the backup which eventually gets replaced once the more recent version is restored.

According to recent reports, A couple of days later and the outage wouldn’t have been as massive a deal. We’d all be eating turkey and introducing GTA 6 to our unsuspecting families. We’ll let you know as soon as things are back up and running. Better yet, you’ll check back and there will already be fresh stuff on the site.

Until then, let’s all listen to “Starting from Zero” one more time.
