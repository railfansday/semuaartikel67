Sources indicate that it was a big week for tech startup exits, headlined by Anthropic pulling off its massive IPO. On the down side, however, tech stocks took a huge dive in the past few days. Should that negative sentiment and downward pressure persist, we can expect it to spill over into private markets.

For this past week, however, U.S. startups continued to secure sizable rounds, led by brain-computer interface company Science landing a fresh $400 million. Another seven American companies declared venture or growth funding of $100 million or more, and across the Atlantic, UK-based AI biotech startup Isomorphic Labs landed $5 billion, the largest financing of the week.

1. Science, $400M, neurotech: Science, a startup focused on brain-computer interface technology, and neural engineering, secured $400 million in Series D funding. The round brings total funding to date for the Alameda, California-based manufacturer to more than $900 million.

2. Model ML, $250M, AI and fintech: Model ML, an AI platform for financial and professional services, closed on $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. General Atlantic and QED Investors led the round for the three-year-old company, which is based in New York and London.

3. Galvanize Therapeutics, $130M, biotech: Redwood City, California-based Galvanize Therapeutics, a developer of pulsed electric field ablation technologies for oncology and chronic disease, picked up $130 million in Series D funding led by HealthQuest Capital.

In a recent development, 4. General Flash, $128M, critical metals: General Flash, a developer of a new method to produce critical metals, announced that it has raised $128 million across seed and Series A rounds from a long list of investors.

It has been revealed that 5. Ramon.Space, $124M, space tech: Denver-based Ramon.Space, a developer of space computing infrastructure, pulled in $124M in Series B funding led by PSG.

It has been revealed that 6. Marrow Therapeutics, $120M, biotech: Watertown, Massachusetts-based Marrow Therapeutics, a developer of bone marrow-targeted therapies to treat severe blood disorders, announced its launch with a combined $173 million in Series A and B funding. Orbimed led its $120 million Series B round while Scion Life Sciences and venBio led the $53 million Series A.

7. Noeri, $110M, enterprise AI: Noeri, a developer of self-improving AI for enterprises and researchers, launched with $110 million in initial funding. Menlo Ventures led the round for the Palo Alto-based startup, which was founded by Stanford University computer science professors.

8. Seawolf Therapeutics, $100M, biotech: San Diego-based Seawolf Therapeutics, a biotech startup developing a novel DNA delivery platform, emerged from stealth and revealed that it has raised $100 million in funding to date, with Versant Ventures, MPM BioImpact and Novartis Venture Fund as lead investors.

9. BrightAI, $97M, physical AI: BrightAI, a startup developing a sensor, drone and robotics-driven platform to bring continuous observability to infrastructure-heavy industries like power, water, and energy, secured $97 million in upcoming funding. Smith Point Capital led the financing for the Silicon Valley-based firm.

In a recent development, 10. Vincue, $93M, vehicle intelligence platform: Kansas City-based Vincue, developer of a vehicle lifecycle intelligence platform, closed on $93 million in growth capital led by Wavecrest Growth Partners.
