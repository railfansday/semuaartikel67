TCL’s Black Friday deals are making the company’s most affordable NXTPAPER phones and tablets an even better price.

I’ve been a major fan of OLED for a long time, but these ultra-bright, ultra-vibrant displays have taken a toll on my eyes over the years. TCL NXTPAPER devices offer a gentler type of display that’s covered with a textured matte coating, an extra blue light filtering layer, and another circular polarized layer for ultimate display comfort.

My favorite budget phone this year just so happens to be the TCL NXTPAPER 70 Pro, which is down to just $259 (21% off) right now for the 128GB model. You can opt for 512GB of storage for just $319 (25% off), too, so folks who need more won’t have to spend an arm and a leg. These are the best prices we’ve seen on the unlocked models since they released earlier this year, making them a superb deal!

If you’re looking for a tablet, I’ve got two picks that stand out from the rest. The TCL NXTPAPER 11 Plus is down to $199 right now, making it almost 30% off the regular price. This size is perfect for reading books or manga, both of which look superb on the textured, matte NXTPAPER display. I find that this size tablet is sort of the Goldilocks size — big enough to be more useful than a phone, but not too large to be unwieldy.

According to recent reports, folks who are more into taking notes should check out the TCL Note A1 NXTPAPER, which is TCL’s most premium tablet to date and is on sale for $449 right now (25% off). This one has a wildly different UI compared to other TCL products and is intended mostly for note-taking and ideating, although it still runs Android and the Play Store, so you can load anything you want on it. The stylus feels marvelous on the textured, matte NXTPAPER display, and this one packs a newer-generation display than the rest of TCL’s NXTPAPER tablets, too.
