It has been revealed that GTA 6 hurricanes are a weather phenomenon that only unlock through the questline about Freddie's Bunker - specifically, Freddie Gibbs, acclaimed rapper, who has built a prepper apocalypse bunker that he wants you to test the security measures for. This ultimately leads to a choice about whether you want to stay in Freddie's bunker or leave when the Hurricane comes down on you, which I'll also explain the consequences of below.

Sources indicate that from that point on, Hurricanes will be a random sporadic weather occurrence. They don't change a huge amount about the game, with the exception that long-distance visibility is lower, and that roads are more slippery when driving. They might also impact any attempt to control flying vehicles (I've yet to test that).

To start this questline, you need to go to the unnamed compound on the west side of North Leonard County, near the north of the map. It's actually pretty hard to miss: this is a significant facility ringed by electric fences, marked by a sign called "Ripe Pickins Grove", and when you get close, an automated system will blare at you, telling you to leave.

There's no given reason for you to ignore this, except, y'know, video game logic. If somebody is telling you not to be somewhere, that's a place worth investigating.

Recent findings suggest that to get inside, head to the North section of the compound to see a water tower near the perimeter. You can climb up the tower, then jump down from the walkway over the compound fence to get inside.

Once you're in, you need to go to the Bunker at the South side. Be warned though: there are lots of landmines that explode when you get too close. Thankfully they do very little damage, but you can spot them by using Focus mode if you want to be certain.

According to recent reports, you'll also get attacked by drones. These have low health, which is good, but they're small and fairly agile, making them hard to hit. Take time to aim your shots and use focus to hit the propellers to bring them down.

Recent findings suggest that once you reach the bunker, interact with the door to speak to the occupant over the intercom. Though annoyed his defenses failed, he also sees the value in having somebody test them for weaknesses. He tells you he'll text you later when he's upgraded them further so you can try again.

For me, that text took 4-7 in-game days, roughly speaking, though it's possible it's tied to something else like mission progression instead of in-game time. Either way, just go and play more GTA 6, and you'll get the message eventually.

Recent findings suggest that on your second invitation, the water tower ladder has been blocked off, stopping you from getting past the fence that way. What you want to do instead is go to the Northwest corner of the compound, where you'll find a tractor and a section of fence that's collapsed at the top. If you drive the tractor to the fence and raise the scoop, you can climb up it and leap over the electric wires to get inside.

From here, I highly recommend you grab the excavator ahead of you. It's borderline undestroyable and you can simply roll over the mines and have drones bounce off you until you reach the Bunker again.

It has been revealed that once you reach it, you'll meet the occupants, including the owner Freddie Gibbs. He's built an apocalypse prepper bunker, but his guests are rapidly growing bored and abandon him. He'll give you the tour though, which is when he gets a hurricane warning and the choice about what to do comes up.

Industry observers note that when Freddie sees the Hurricane, he gives you the chance to either stay in his bunker or leave. There are actually some mildly significant consequences to this choice:.

Of these two, I'd say it's better to stay. As much as a hit to your GTA 6 relationship status can be a blow, you can always rebuild it with a little patience and care, and the unique car is valuable enough to make it worth it (it also counts as one of Randy's desired cars, as he's after the Benefactor Dubsta that Freddie's is a custom variant of).

After this point, no matter your choice, Hurricanes will occur randomly throughout your time spent playing GTA 6. As mentioned above, they're rare and largely cosmetic impacts on the world, but are still an interesting change to the landscape.
