Tim McGraw was happy to just see his daughters smile at the 2026 CMA Awards.

It has been revealed that the “I Like It, I Love It” singer was joined by his and wife Faith Hill’s daughters Maggie, 28, and Audrey, 24, for the Nov. 18 ceremony as the trio walked the red carpet together before being joined by Faith inside the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville.

The McGraws cut a matching figure for the evening, with Tim, 59, opting for a double-breasted black tuxedo paired with a white dress shirt and his signature black cowboy hat. Audrey looked smashing in a silky black gown, featuring cutout design patterns along the bodice, while sister Maggie donned a sleek black dress with a square neckline and thick black straps.

You’ll like it, you’ll love it that Faith—who also shares daughter Gracie, 29, with Tim—coordinated with the family as well, wearing a low-cut black ensemble for country music’s biggest night.

And coming from a musical family, it only stands to reason that at least one of the McGraw girls—who were all featured on their dad’s 2007 hit “Last Dollar (Fly Away)”—would follow in their parents' footsteps.

And that one is Audrey, who is at the moment giving her own musical career a go.

In fact, the “I Am…I Remarked” singer made a surprise appearance at her dad’s concert at the Jones Beach Theater in Wantagh, Upcoming York, in July where she performed her mom’s 2002 track “Stronger.”.

According to recent reports, “I don’t have words to express the honor I feel to sing my mama’s song last night, and for it to be at dad’s show,” Audrey captioned an Instagram post of her performance. “To be your daughter is an infinite gift. I love y’all so much, I am always so proud and so inspired by you. Thank you for sharing this crazy life with us. Proud to be a road dawg forever.”.

Sources indicate that “Family always,” she added. “Love y’all.”.
