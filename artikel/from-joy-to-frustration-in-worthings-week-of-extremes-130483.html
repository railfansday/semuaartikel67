Industry observers note that the high was Jordan Young’s double clinching Saturday’s 2-1 Woodside Road win over second-placed Harrogate. The low was letting in three in four minutes to scupper their hopes of a result at Woking on Tuesday night.

Boss Adam Hinshelwood was as frustrated in midweek as he had been pleased at the weekend – and is demanding more consistency from his players, particularly in their defending.

After Saturday’s win, Hinshelwood noted the display showed his squad were learning as they went.

It has been revealed that he remarked: “It was probably one of our best wins if you look at where Harrogate are in the league and that we’d come off the back of that late goal conceded at Scunthorpe the previous week. We wanted a response to that and got it.

“They probably shaded the first half but we scored at a good time – in fact Jordan scored twice at good times.

Industry observers note that “At 1-1 we had a real go and highlights how far we have come. Settling for a draw just isn’t in our mindset. We’ve had a few games that could have gone either way but we will always try to win games.

“The second goal came from the type of move we’ve been working on in training.”.

It has been revealed that hinshelwood is delighted by Young’s influence on the campaign. He signed from Torquay in September after Teddy Jenks left the Reds – and he’s proved the perfect replacement so far.

Industry observers note that “He’s a top talent who we’d chased for a while. He’d done it against us too many times and we knew what he could do for us.”.

Young has scored six goals in 15 appearances and Hinshelwood remarked: “He is scoring, assisting and creating.”.

But Tuesday’s loss at Woking – where goals by Ashley Nathaniel-George and Dominic Johnson-Fisher (2) proved in vain – left Hinshelwood hugely frustrated.

“We’re gifting games to our opponents. They don’t have to do anything to beat us. We scored two unbelievable goals but gifted them goals – and it’s a common trend,” he told club media.

“Good teams are consistent week after week but we have turned up and just thought we can do enough to win. At this level you get found out.

Recent findings suggest that “We have no-one to blame but ourselves. Three goals in four minutes – it’s nowhere near good enough. We’ve got to find a way to defend our box on a consistent basis.”.

The seventh-placed Rebels go to Altrincham on Saturday then visit Wick next Tuesday in the Sussex Senior Cup third round.
