Microsoft Copilot AI predicts that the key factor that could trigger a euphoric crypto bull market in 2027 is the successful passage and rapid implementation of comprehensive legislation governing the U.S. crypto market.

Combined with the launch of large-scale bank tokenized deposit networks and increased regulated real-world asset (RWA) issuance, this could set the stage for significant growth.

In a recent development, as of mid-November 2026, the market remains subdued. Bitcoin is trading in the $60,000 range, Ethereum is below $2,000, and Solana is priced between $60 and $80, with total market capitalization just above $2 trillion amid high fear and ongoing ETF outflows.

It has been revealed that however, several regulatory initiatives already in progress make a breakthrough in 2027 plausible, rather than just a distant hope. The GENIUS Act’s stablecoin framework takes effect on January 18, 2027.

Regulatory agencies continue drafting implementing rules, banks are preparing to launch tokenized deposit systems (including efforts linked to the Clearing House), and tokenization has shifted from pilot projects to full production at major institutions and exchanges.

A revived or newly introduced market-structure bill, building on earlier CLARITY Act momentum, could clear Congress in the first half of 2027, especially if economic conditions improve and both parties see political upside in clarifying digital-asset rules.

Under this scenario, by mid-to-late 2027 the legislation provides clear classification pathways for tokens, custody standards, trading venue rules, and guardrails for bank participation.

According to recent reports, at the same time, several of the largest U.S. banks launch interoperable tokenized deposit platforms, while issuers bring tens or hundreds of billions in tokenized Treasuries, funds, and private credit on-chain under the fresh clarity.

Industry observers note that stablecoin supply expands rapidly as regulated banks enter the market. Together, they remove the multi-year regulatory overhang that has capped institutional allocation.

Risk committees green-light larger crypto exposures, pension funds and endowments increase targets, and corporate treasuries add digital assets.

Spot ETF inflows reverse dramatically, leverage returns, and retail FOMO follows. The result is a classic euphoric phase: rising prices attract more capital, which drives further price appreciation, media coverage, and fresh product introduces across the industry.

Bitcoin remains the primary institutional and sovereign reserve asset. Once regulatory uncertainty lifts and banks can more easily hold and transfer BTC, ETF demand and corporate treasury adoption accelerate.

In a euphoric environment, Bitcoin could reclaim and exceed its prior cycle highs, reaching $180,000–$220,000 by the end of 2027 as it absorbs most traditional finance inflows and benefits from a renewed “digital gold” narrative amid elevated global debt levels.

It has been revealed that ethereum is positioned as the dominant settlement and smart-contract layer for tokenized assets and DeFi. Most RWA pilots and institutional tokenization efforts already favor Ethereum or its Layer-2 networks for security and composability.

Recent findings suggest that clearer rules plus bank demand for on-chain settlement would drive higher gas usage, staking demand, and ETH accumulation. In this scenario, Ethereum could climb to $8,000–$12,000 by late 2027, reflecting both the broader market multiple expansion and its unique role as the base layer for regulated on-chain finance.

Solana offers high throughput and low costs that appeal to consumer applications, payments, high-frequency trading venues, and certain RWA or stablecoin use cases that prioritize speed.

These targets assume the legislation passes in usable form, bank tokenized-deposit networks gain meaningful traction, and macro conditions remain supportive enough for risk assets overall.

If realized, the combination would mark the transition from a post-2025 correction into a genuine multi-year institutional bull market, with Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana as the clearest primary beneficiaries.

The crypto bull run of 2027 is projected to begin in earnest around June 15, following the expected passage of comprehensive U.S. market-structure legislation in the second quarter.

Combined with GENIUS Act effects and expanding bank tokenized-deposit networks, this clarity should unlock major institutional capital, reversing ETF outflows and igniting sustained euphoric price action across the market.
