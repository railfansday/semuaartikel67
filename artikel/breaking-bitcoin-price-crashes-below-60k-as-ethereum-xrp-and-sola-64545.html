Breaking: Bitcoin Price Crashes Below $60K as Ethereum, XRP and Solana Plunge

Bitcoin crashed below $60,000, hitting its lowest level since July amid a brutal crypto market sell-off.

Nearly 250,000 traders were liquidated as total crypto liquidations reached approximately $1.31 billion.

U.S. technology and AI stocks collapsed, wiping out around $500 billion in market value.

Recent findings suggest that bitcoin has just crashed below $60,000, hitting its lowest level since July as a brutal sell-off sweeps $63.09 billion from the crypto market. In the last two hours, BTC has plunged more than 5% as Ethereum, XRP, and Solana also collapse harder.

Meanwhile, $1.3 billion in leveraged positions have been wiped out, hitting the long traders the hardest.

So, what is driving the latest crash?

Bitcoin’s latest drop has turned an already weak market into a full scale sell-off. BTC has fallen toward $59,300, extending its decline from around $84,000 and wiping out nearly 29% of its value.

Bitcoin’s sharp drop has caught traders off guard as it falls below a major psychological support level.

The sell-off is also spreading across the wider crypto market.

Major altcoins like Ethereum have fallen below $1,657, while XRP has crashed under $0.90 and Solana has dropped below $67.

The collapse has triggered massive forced selling. According to CoinGlass, around 249,850 traders were liquidated over the past 24 hours, taking total liquidations to $1.31 billion.

A sharp fall in U.S. tech and AI stocks has triggered fresh selling across global markets. U.S. stocks lost around $500 billion in value within minutes of Friday’s market open, while the Nasdaq 100 fell 1.58%.

Anthropic (ANT) was down around 8% after its massive IPO, while the stock was already trading roughly 20% below its opening price, now at $87.

Major tech stocks, including SanDisk, dropped 8.07%, Western Digital 6.80%, Arm 6.41%, AMD 6.32%, and Micron 6.06%.

Marvell Technology also fell 5.97%, while Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms dropped 5.30%.

This weakness is also putting pressure on Bitcoin as investors move away from riskier assets.

Industry observers note that the selling is also hitting U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs. On 19 November, BTC ETF recorded around $582 million in outflows, while monthly outflows have crossed $3.47 billion. BlackRock’s IBIT alone reportedly lost more than $541 million.

Sources indicate that meanwhile, Tether has burned around $8 billion in USDT over the past 20 days as the stablecoin market contracts. Total stablecoin market capitalization has fallen to roughly $288 billion from its May peak of $311 billion.

With Bitcoin now below $60,000, traders are watching whether the market can recover quickly or whether another deeper leg lower is coming.

Robert Kiyosaki warned investors against rushing into the crash, writing, “Never grab a falling knife.”.
