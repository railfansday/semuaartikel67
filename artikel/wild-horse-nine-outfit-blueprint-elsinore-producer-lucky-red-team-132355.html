EXCLUSIVE: Wild Horse Nine and Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri producer Blueprint Pictures has taken English-language remake rights to German dramedy No Hit Wonder, the story of a one hit wonder fallen on hard times who ends up in a psychiatric ward where he motivates the eccentric members of the resident choir to national glory.

The original film, which took $3M for Warner Bros Germany last year, features choir-performed versions of Happy by Pharrell Williams and Wrecking Ball by Miley Cyrus.

Blueprint is developing and producing the “feel-good tearjerker” with leading Italian indie distributor-producer Lucky Red, which most recently produced Andrew Scott drama Elsinore and Helen Mirren starrer A Talent for Murder as it continues its push into English-language projects. Lucky Red will co-produce the English version, has rights to it in Italy, and may make an Italian version. Blueprint is also well known for hit comedy The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel, which feels in the same ballpark as No Hit Wonder. Watch on Deadline.

Both deals were struck with Guido Rud’s Argentinian sales and remake rights specialist, FilmSharks. As we previously revealed, French rights were picked up by Federation. Florian Dietrich’s 2025 film starred Florian David Fitz, Nora Tschirner, Udo Samel and Corinna Kirchhoff.

In a recent development, two-time Best Picture Oscar nominee Graham Broadbent of Blueprint remarked: “Blueprint Pictures are pleased to be working with Lucky Red on this exciting project. In a world increasingly obsessed with fleeting fame and notoriety, No Hit Wonder is an inspirational story about finding true happiness and connection. We are looking forward to bringing this moving, funny and hopeful story to an English speaking audience”.

Lorenzo Gangarossa (Fatherland, The Newly introduced Pope), the former Wildside exec who recently joined Studiocanal-owned Lucky Red to boost its international productions, mentioned today: “Lucky Red is very happy to continue its journey developing English-language international movies following Elsinore and A Talent for Murder. In this case we are working with Blueprint which is such a pleasure as we had already worked together in the past and we look forward to this new adventure together”.

Industry observers note that guido Rud added: “The market perceives us as taste makers for discovering high concept remakes and original scripts that have proven to work worldwide. We’ve worked with most of the major studios so to do another deal like this is proof of the level of partners we are collaborating with. We’re always proud to work with the most interesting European labels.”.
