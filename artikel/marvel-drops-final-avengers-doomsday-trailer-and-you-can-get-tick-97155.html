Recent findings suggest that all the predictions and teases for the next Avengers: Doomsday trailer have culminated in a big drop on Monday, with Marvel releasing the final look at what’s to come and setting the Doomsday clock for 24 days, 14 hours, and then some. The film hits theaters on Dec. 18, and fans can purchase tickets through Disney Plus.

In a recent development, robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom has arrived to take back the “stolen lives” of heroes who were resurrected in Avengers: Endgame after Thanos’s snap. Heavy is the heart of Steve Rogers (Captain America), whose retrieval of the Infinity Stones required a bunch of time travel and quantum maneuvering — but also saw him married to Peggy Carter and starting a family. The impact of his actions seemingly has led to Doom’s bone-picking plot.

As the trailer unfolds, you’ll see Shuri, Shang-Chi, Kate Bishop (Hailee Steinfeld’s Hawkeye), Thor, Yelena, the Fantastic Four, Ant-Man, Sam Wilson, Bucky, Cyclops and a bunch of X-Men, and myriad other heroes gather to take on Doctor Doom. Creeping in the background is Doc’s mysterious crew. Who are they? None other than the Latverian Witches, who are assumed to be Wanda Maximoff/Scarlet Witch, Clea (last seen in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness) and Sylvie from Loki’s spinoff show.

Industry observers note that you can watch the trailer in the Disney Plus app, and as a fresh offering, you can click the “Tickets” tab in the streamer to buy your movie tickets from Fandango. The option is available to fans with a bundle or standalone subscription to the service.
