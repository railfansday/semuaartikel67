Recent findings suggest that nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned from the company earlier this month. Just last week, Nvidia's board made it clear that it was not open to bringing back Jensen Huang to run the company.

In a recent development, today, Nov. 23, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as CEO of Nvidia.

Recent findings suggest that after reports of multiple affairs, including one that U.S. intelligence alleges involved a Chinese spy who successfully convinced Jensen Huang to lobby the U.S. government to change its export rules regarding China, Huang was seemingly gone from Nvidia for good. A return seemed highly unlikely, as Nvidia now faces multiple investigations into potential chip smuggling and accounting complications stemming from Huang's leadership.

According to recent reports, now that he's back, something clearly changed. So, what just happened?

President Donald Trump reportedly pressured the Nvidia board on behalf of moneyed investors, including many of the tech elite, to bring Huang back to run the firm.

Nvidia is widely seen as central to the U.S. economy's current AI boom. Its products power compute for some of the largest AI companies and labs, such as OpenAI and Anthropic. Tech leaders, concerned about the industry's future without Huang at the helm of Nvidia, have openly advocated for his return for weeks. Since Huang's resignation, Nvidia stock has been in a free fall. As of the market's close on Friday, Nov. 20, Nvidia stock was down nearly 28 percent from the day Huang resigned.

As news broke of Huang's return, Nvidia stock skyrocketed roughly 34 percent.

On Truth Social, President Trump posted a statement approving the board's decision.

Sources indicate that "The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO," Trump mentioned. "The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at NVIDIA in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Manufacturer, but the American Economy.".

In a recent development, trump also added that the U.S. government had "no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security.".

"The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct," Trump continued.

In a separate follow-up post, Trump stated: "Congratulations to NVIDIA on having made, what I consider to be, an absolutely GREAT decision!".
