Starting with the hosts, while Barcelona might have been left hugely frustrated last month as they slumped to a 2-1 defeat at home against arch-rivals Real Madrid, it appears that was just a minor blip for the reigning Spanish champions. Marking their return from November’s international break holding a three-point buffer at the top of the La Liga standings, Hansi Flick’s superstar squad entered the fortnight interval in the midst of another free-scoring flurry. Last seen collecting a heavyweight scalp in the Spanish capital as they secured a 2-1 win against Atletico Madrid, Barcelona have won each of their previous three appearances by an aggregate score of 11-2. Likewise, gaining a fearsome reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third, Flick’s side have registered a staggering 48 goals in their first 12 La Liga showdowns so far this season.

In a recent development, while big-money summer recruit Rodri might be ahead of schedule on his return from a recent muscle injury, the former Manchester City midfielder will not feature here. However, returning to first-team training this week, fellow Spanish international Dani Olmo could play some part on Sunday lunchtime.

Bagging both goals against Atletico Madrid prior to November’s international break and proving to be Barca’s hero, academy graduate Fermin will keep his spot from the start. Shattering a string of records this season and remarkably already registering 18 direct goal contributions in his first 11 La Liga appearances (15 goals, three assists), talismanic skipper Raphinha will lead Flick’s attack.

Sources indicate that as for the visitors, while Villarreal might have entered November’s international break with a 2-0 victory at home against Getafe, the Yellow Submarine are searching for some stability. Completely blown away against reigning European champions PSG earlier in the month as they slumped to a 6-0 drumming in Castellon, Inigo Perez’s men will be bracing themselves for another monumental challenge when they travel to Catalonia this weekend. Registering consecutive La Liga wins on just a single occasion so far this season, Villarreal should also be concerned by their most recent showdown against Barcelona. Falling to a routine 4-1 defeat when they last met the defending Spanish champions back on February 28th, Perez’s inconsistent squad find themselves pencilled in as a landslide underdog on Sunday lunchtime. It should also be highlighted, Villarreal have failed to register a single La Liga clean sheet on their travels since the middle of February.

Recent findings suggest that in what could be a real boost for Villarreal this weekend, the Yellow Submarine are hopeful that Argentinian international Juan Foyth will be able to make a first La Liga appearance since October 24th. However, undergoing hamstring surgery last month, 21-year-old Alassane Diatta will not feature in Catalonia.

In a recent development, now in his second campaign at the Estadio de la Ceramica, former Chelsea youngster Renato Veiga will start at the heart of Villarreal’s defence. Completing a permanent switch from German outfit RB Leipzig over the summer, veteran goalkeeper Peter Gulacsi will start between the sticks on Sunday lunchtime.

Marking their return from November’s international break leading the chase at the top of the La Liga standings, Barcelona will be looking to make another title statement in Catalonia. Heading into the fortnight interval with a sensational comeback in the Spanish capital as they clinched a 2-1 victory against Atletico Madrid, Flick’s men have also gained a reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third. Likewise, landing a routine 4-1 win when they last welcomed Villarreal to Catalonia back in February, we are expecting a similar outcome here.
