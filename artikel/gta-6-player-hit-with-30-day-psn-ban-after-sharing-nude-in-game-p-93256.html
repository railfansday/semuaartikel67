A GTA 6 player claims they have been banned from using PlayStation Network’s social features for 30 days after sending a friend an in-game photograph of nude NPCs.

Rockstar Games’ long-awaited GTA 6 released on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, allowing players to explore the fictional state of Leonida and its sprawling Vice City setting.

The game features a range of adult content, including nudity, which players can encounter while exploring its open world.

On November 20, X user DerrickGott007 claimed they had received a 30-day suspension from PSN’s social features after sending a friend a photograph of a nudist colony they had discovered in GTA 6.

The player remarked the image was captured using the game’s built-in camera feature before being shared through PlayStation’s messaging service.

However, it appears sharing such images violates PlayStation’s Code of Conduct, which prohibits obscene or pornographic content.

“Do not, I repeat do not send any photos taken with the in-game camera, across the PSN messaging network that contain nudity,” the post warned. “I just got a 30-day PSN social features ban for sending my friend a photo of the nudist colony in GTA 6 that I found.”.

According to PlayStation’s official guidelines, content shared through its services can be reviewed by its safety team, with violations potentially resulting in temporary or permanent restrictions.

Sony’s policies also apply to screenshots and messages shared between players, meaning content permitted within an age-restricted game is not necessarily permitted to be distributed through PSN.

The incident serves as a warning to GTA 6 players that sharing explicit in-game photographs with friends could carry consequences beyond the game itself.
