Alan Shearer believes Ezri Konsa has been the Premier League’s best player this season after an impressive start to his Arsenal career.

But the former Newcastle United striker has been impressed by the Gunners’ backline and feels Konsa should take a large amount of credit for the fact that they have not missed William Saliba so much.

Industry observers note that konsa’s arrival at Emirates has provided Mikel Arteta with a defender, but his performances have caused some issues for the Arsenal boss. Konsa is now a viable option for Arteta to play, as Saliba is fit again.

Shearer feels Konsa has done enough to be regarded as the best player in the division so far.

He stated in the Metro that Konsa has been effective while playing for Arsenal in the defence.

According to recent reports, the former Newcastle player believed that Konsa has been the best player in the Premier League during this season. He pointed out the proof of the performance in the defence, arguing that people did not notice the missing Saliba until now.

According to recent reports, konsa has proved just how much he has grown since moving to the Emirates. Arsenal have been very strong at the back with Konsa playing a massive part.

Saliba is still one of Arsenal’s most notable defenders, so Arteta will be delighted to have him back. However, he can’t ignore what Konsa has done in his absence.

Industry observers note that it would be a tough call to leave the Englishman out, as he has been in such great form. But it means an experienced first choice defender would be left on the bench.

Konsa and Saliba could make a good partnership if they are given time to understand each other on the pitch. They will need to play regularly to do this.
