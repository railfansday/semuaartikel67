Publisher Capcom has revealed the winning robot master in its design competition for Mega Man: Dual Override.

From a shortlist of six finalist designs, the development team chose Cleanser Man as the winner, and it has been made into one of the action platformer's eight boss characters.

Director Shinya Ikuta and Producer Hiroyuki Minamitani make the announcement in the above video, where Cleanser Man's in-game appearance makes its debut.

They thank all entrants for taking part, and talk a little bit about why the character was chosen to appear in the upcoming game.

A upcoming message on the official website provides slightly more detail on what Capcom liked about Cleanser Man:.

In a recent development, "It wasn't easy picking a winner, but we ended up deciding on Cleanser Man due to his whimsical motif and unique color pattern. The lid part on the detergent has a very Mega Man-esque design to it, and we like that the detergent itself is very colorful as well. You have to be careful not to introduce too many elements to prevent the design from becoming too cluttered, so we like that the artist kept things relatively simple. But there's still enough detail to it to give it that genuine Mega Man feel.".

It has been revealed that the competition has been running for nearly a full year, during which time thousands of contestants submitted their original artwork and Capcom narrowed it down to just a handful of finalists. Ultimately, artist @iregyuradabii took the win.

Cleanser Man is now the fifth robot master to be revealed for Dual Override, with another three to go.
