Martin Odegaard struck the decisive goal for the Gunners, who played host to the Tigers on November 7.

Hull were widely lauded for their defensive grit in the game, although the division’s key match incidents panel has claimed they should have been penalised.

Tigers defender Nobel Mendy held on to Gunners forward Kai Havertz yet even after a VAR review, a spot-kick was not awarded.

The panel stated: “Although Harvertz is clearly in an offside position, there is a clear and impactful holding action by Mendy before any potential offside offence by Havertz.

“Given the chronology of events, a penalty should have been awarded.

“The panel consdered the VAR intervention was correct; however, the outcome after the on-field review was considered to be incorrect as a penalty should have been awarded.”.

Arsenal will not feel too aggrieved given they secured three points, but it would most likely have been a different story had they not.

Sources indicate that reflecting on the game, Tigers defender John Egan told the club’s media team: “I feel like we put a really good performance in, put a lot into the game.
