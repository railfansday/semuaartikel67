Neto set for first Juventus game since 2017 and says his Turin chapter was still open

Norberto Neto is set for his first competitive game in almost six months when Juventus host Omonia in the Europa League on Thursday night. Speaking on the eve of the match, the 37 year old goalkeeper said he still felt he had unfinished business in Turin.

Neto last played for Juventus in May 2017, when he was part of the side that beat Lazio 2-0 in the Coppa Italia final. More than nine years on, the Brazilian is in line to start again in black and white, with first choice Guglielmo Vicario being rested for the visit of the Cypriot side to the Allianz Stadium.

According to recent reports, his most recent competitive outing came on 30 May with Botafogo, as Football Italia noted after Wednesday’s press conference. Since then he has been working as cover, a role he took on after joining as a free agent at the end of September.

Juventus moved for Neto after Kamil Grabara ruptured the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee in training on 19 September, only days after keeping a clean sheet on his debut against NEC. The club confirmed his return on 28 September with a contract running until 30 June 2027.

It has been revealed that asked what drew him back, Neto explained that he had been looking for a next step that genuinely meant something to him. “Deep down, I felt my chapter with Juve was still open,” he remarked, in comments reported by ilBianconero, which carried the press conference live. He added that he is enjoying his work and is in Turin to help, whatever the coming months bring.

Recent findings suggest that opening the press conference, he described his feelings simply: “It’s hugely satisfying. I’m really excited to be back on the pitch, and proud to be part of this team.”.

Neto was asked about Vicario, who is on loan from Tottenham and has been Luciano Spalletti’s regular choice in goal. He kept his answer short: “I don’t know how much my opinion matters, but he’s a top-level goalkeeper.”.

The veteran also spoke warmly about the younger players around him. He said their eagerness to contribute was infectious and that the squad has the quality, will and determination it needs. On the goals Juventus have conceded this season, he pointed to a side that is still developing, with several players latest to Serie A and finding their way. His own focus, he said, is on training at full intensity every day while the team looks for balance and consistency.

Sources indicate that juventus go into matchday five fifth in the league phase standings with ten points from four games. Omonia have won two and lost two. Neto warned against taking them lightly, saying they are a strong side who break forward with real quality, and revealed he knows their coach Fabiano from his years in Brazil.

He also stressed that Europe brings its own demands, noting that Juventus always carry a responsibility to compete to win in every tournament they enter.

Sources indicate that interest in the meeting was already strong after ticket sales for Omonia passed 35,000 last week. Next up in Serie A is the trip to Como, where both sides go in without suspensions.

For Neto, the night is a chance to add to the 22 appearances he made in his first spell, when he won two league titles, two Coppa Italia trophies and a Supercoppa Italiana as Gianluigi Buffon’s understudy.
