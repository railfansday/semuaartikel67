According to recent reports, it’s been four years since the cult hit “Hundreds of Beavers” first debuted and still two years since it started really gaining traction with wider audiences in 2024. But if it feels like the black and white, silent, slapstick indie comedy still hasn’t left the cultural conversation, that’s because in all that time it has continued to quietly play in theaters and attract audiences both loyal and fresh alike.

According to recent reports, technically, “Hundreds of Beavers” has never left theaters. It continues to play individual screenings in a city or two every weekend. Now, though, “Hundreds of Beavers” is set to return to theaters for what is its biggest theatrical expansions to date. The film will kick off its third annual Northwoods Christmas, which will feature 130 screenings across 85 cities, with more to likely be added in the coming weeks. It came about after another fan campaign pushed for the film to come to some markets it hadn’t previously thought about or targeted such as Indianapolis and Charlottesville, and it will now play in everything from concert venues to microcinemas. According to recent reports, the re-releases have become a holiday tradition for “Hundreds of Beavers,” which for all its wintery setting and its main character dressed like Santa, is being positioned as a latest Christmas movie. The first time “Hundreds of Beavers” was re-released at Christmas 2024, which was shortly after the Blu-ray for the film became available, it brought in $118K at the box office in a month, and the producers continued the tradition in 2025.

“There’s room for a Christmas tradition with the energy of a ‘Rocky Horror’ screening,” remarked producer Kurt Ravenwood. “You can watch ‘It’s a Wonderful Life’ and ‘White Christmas’ all you want, but for getting together with friends and family to laugh about the miserable winter ahead, ‘Hundreds of Beavers’ has them all beat.”.

Each of the screenings will be preceded by a short film featuring “Bing Crosbeaver,” which they did last year, but this will be a new short with a new twist in which another character named “Conspirabeaver” will be joined by a “sinister foe,” per the release. Some of the in-person beavers in costume will be present at the screenings, and fans will also get a promotional poster for the movie. The “Beavers” team recently released a few parody posters ripping off the recent releases of “You Can See Everything,” “Digger,” and “The Devils.”.

To date, “Hundreds of Beavers” has made $940,092 at the domestic box office, including another $618,014 internationally to bring its worldwide total to above $1.5 million. It’s done all this via first producing the film for just $150,000 and spending little more than $70,000 on ads, publicity, and its many roadshow screenings. It’s also been released entirely independently, starting with a run in the Midwest where the film is set before it started expanding to the coasts.

It has been revealed that incredibly, more than 70 percent of the film’s U.S. box office has come after “Hundreds of Beavers” began streaming way back on April 15, 2024, and it has even sold more than 20,000 Blu-Rays worldwide. It’s a rare, truly indie success story even in a day and age when many movies have pursued self-distribution strategies.

According to recent reports, for those who haven’t had the pleasure of seeing “Hundreds of Beavers” in theaters yet, the film is about a drunken applejack salesman who loses everything and has to survive the winter by becoming North America’s greatest fur trapper. It sees the trapper wrestling and fighting with dozens of beavers and other forest animals all wearing mascot suits, with the stark black and white cinematography and creative physical stunts embracing a zany video game logic and absurd silent comedy slapstick gags.

The film is directed by Mike Cheslik and was written by Cheslik and star Ryland Tews, with the film also starring Olivia Graves, Wes Tank, Doug Mancheski, and Luis Rico.
