It has been revealed that update [Sat 21st Nov 2026, 6pm]: Although this story started as a win for the Ocarina remake, it's taken a rather unexpected turn. As our colleagues at Push Square report, fans of GTA 6 are now allegedly going after Zelda's user scores on Metacritic.

Push Square: "In response to a coordinated review bombing effort on GTA 6, fans of the Rockstar release appear to have rolled out a counter-attack on The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, believing Nintendo gamers are to blame for the situation...The Switch 2’s universally adored remake of Ocarina of Time has seen its user score drop from 9.6 to 8.9 at the time of writing.".

Zelda has retained a user score of 9.6/10 on Metacritic since its launch earlier this month, but as mentioned above, it's now dropped. At the time of publishing this enhancement, it still has a "positive" rating after more than 32,000 user reviews.

As we noted in our original story, there can be all sorts of factors in play when it comes to Metacritic user scores, and they're subject to change over time (and can bounce back).

Original Story: It's been a huge year for the games industry with all sorts of heavy-hitters releasing over the past few months, and as we look towards the end of 2026, we're now checking in on Metacritic's highest-rated games of the year based on the user score.

While Grand Theft Auto 6 may have officially surpassed Ocarina's Remake as the highest-rated game of 2026 on the review aggregate website (and also matched the original N64 hit as the highest-rated critic Metascore of all time), it seems the classic outing on Switch 2 has won players' hearts worldwide.

Industry observers note that according to the latest update from the site, Ocarina's Remake is at the moment sitting in the top highest-rated games (above 90), based on user score. It's in good company with other games like Resident Evil Requiem and Splatoon Raiders.

It has been revealed that obviously, there are all sorts of different factors in play here when it comes to user scores (and the system in place isn't necessarily perfect). For example, Ocarina's Remake has over 25,000 user reviews, whereas other titles may have around 100 reviews. Keep in mind, user scores are also subject to change over time.

And if you're wondering how GTA 6 has been received by players, it's currently sitting on a user score of 5.5/10 after more than 30,000 user reviews. Even with supposed review bombings, it might take some time before it wins over some players. Despite top marks from critics, this upcoming entry in the GTA universe appears to have divided the fanbase in its opening week.

As attention shifts towards the end-of-year game awards, both Ocarina and GTA 6 have already been nominated for multiple industry awards.
