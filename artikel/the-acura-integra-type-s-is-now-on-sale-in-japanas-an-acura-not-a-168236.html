The Acura Integra Type S Is Now on Sale in Japan—As an Acura, Not a Honda

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that while it may be the luxury offshoot of Honda, Acura’s cars have never actually been sold in Japan in any official capacity. The luxury automaker has been focused on North America for almost the entirety of its four decades of existence, meaning that any Acura cars in the brand’s would-be home country are either imported or modified cars initially sold as Hondas.

Sources indicate that that finally changes today. As of Wednesday, the Acura Integra Type S is now on sale in Japan, fulfilling a promise made earlier this year. In addition to being the first Acura to reach that market, it is the first variant of the fresh Integra to be sold outside of North America in any form.

Sources indicate that acura has been teasing the possibility of the Integra Type S reaching the Japanese market all year, starting with a production model conspicuously on display among Honda’s Tokyo Auto Salon offerings in January. The Integra was shown again at another Japanese show in February before production looks were confirmed in March. Now, Japanese buyers will actually be able to get their hands on the Ohio-built Integra.

Industry observers note that the Acura shares an engine and many other parts with the Honda Civic Type R, but the pair diverge in a few key ways. While the Integra is softer and more refined, the Honda is sharper and more harsh. American buyers could pick freely between the two, but other markets have not had the option to do so until now. It’s good news for Japanese enthusiasts looking for a slightly different flavor of performance Honda—a list that includes noted Integra Type S fan Yuki Tsunoda.
