Michael J. Fox had the support of his family during his latest Parkinson’s gala.

According to recent reports, stepping out together at the Saturday, November 14, event’s red carpet, the Back to the Future star posed for photos with wife Tracy Pollan and their three daughters: twins Aquinnah and Schuyler, 31, and Esmé, 25. (The couple are also parents to son Sam, 37, who did not appear to be in attendance.).

The Michael J. Fox Foundation’s A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson’s event at the Waldorf Astoria in Upcoming York City saw Fox, 65, opt for a black jacket and black pants with a navy tee, while Pollan, 66, and their daughters wore long gowns and had their hair styled in soft waves.

The actor, who was diagnosed with young-onset Parkinson’s disease in 1991 when he was 29, also posed for additional photos beside Pollan as the pair grinned for the cameras.

Pollan noted in an interview with People at the event, which marked its 26th year, that the gala and wider foundation continues to raise vital funds for the scientists who need it. “Just the huge and amazing amount of money that’s been raised that goes directly into the scientists’ pockets, and they can just do all the work that’s necessary [to finding a cure],” she told the magazine. “We really are seeing so much progress.”.

Pollan also spoke about the family’s mindset as they continue to navigate Fox’s diagnosis. “I think you just have to do it day by day,” the actress, who married the actor in 1988 after meeting him on the set of Family Ties, told People. “You have to look at what’s in front of you … and you have to stay in the moment, and if the moment is OK, that’s where you are.”.

Industry observers note that fox stepped out with his son at March’s SAG-AFTRA’s 2026 Actor Awards for a rare public appearance. During an address to the cameras, Fox spoke directly about Sam, who was seated beside him. “Sometimes I like to remind them, ‘If I weren’t an actor, they wouldn’t be here.’ By the way, he’s not an actor — he’s my date,” the Shrinking star quipped while gesturing to his son. “I’m Michael J. Fox. I’m a dad and I’m an actor.”.

It has been revealed that in addition to fatherhood and acting, Fox’s contributions to Parkinson’s disease research were also recognized publicly this year. While receiving the Bob Hope Humanitarian Award at September’s Emmy Awards, Fox spoke on stage about how far his attitude toward his disease had come over time.

“I [later] understood that accepting Parkinson’s didn’t mean I had to surrender to it,” he told the crowd, who gave Fox a standing ovation once his speech had wrapped. “It just meant doing everything I could for everyone living with this disease … I’m proud of the progress we’ve made.”.
