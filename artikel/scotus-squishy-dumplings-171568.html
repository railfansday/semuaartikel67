SCOTUS Squishy Dumplings

And the idea came to me: Supreme Court squishy dumplings. It makes perfect sense, because all of the Justices are--at one time or another--squishes! This would make a perfect novelty gift. Open up the steamer and see how the Justice squishes in a different way. Some are rock solid like a NEEDOH cube, while others barely keep their own shape. But they are all satisfying to squeeze.

My family worked together to create just the right prototype, with the limitations of a free ChatGPT account. We even used a photograph of one of my daughter's dumplings as a model. This is the best ChatGPT can generate shortly before Thanksgiving dinner begins. My kids approve. Happy Thanksgiving everyone.
