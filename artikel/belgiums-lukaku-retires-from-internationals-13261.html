Romelu Lukaku scored three goals for Belgium at the World Cup this summer.

Romelu Lukaku has retired from international football, saying he has "enjoyed every single moment" of playing for Belgium.

Fenerbahce striker Lukaku, 33, made his international debut in 2010 and is Belgium's all-time leading men's goalscorer with 94 goals in 135 appearances.

He is the fifth highest men's international goalscorer of all time, behind Sunil Chhetri, Ali Daei, Lionel Messi and leader Cristiano Ronaldo, who has scored 146.

Lukaku scored eight goals across four World Cups, including in the run to the quarter-finals at this summer's tournament, and a total of six goals at three European Championships.

Writing on Instagram,, external the former Everton, Chelsea and Manchester United forward thanked his team-mates and coaches "for pushing and trusting me to help the team" during his 16 years with the national side.

He missed Belgium's Nations League win over Turkey last week with a hip injury and was an unused substitute on Sunday as they qualified for the last eight of the competition by beating Italy 4-1.

"These 16 years I gave all I had for the national team... I went through every emotion possible," Lukaku wrote.

According to recent reports, "To the Belgium fans thank you for the support you gave our team. I am proud to have represented my country in four World Cups and three European Championships.

"I will never forget those moments. I'm at peace with my decision to stop here. It was amazing. It was enjoyable. I'm grateful.".

In 2023, Lukaku scored 14 times in eight Euro 2024 qualifying appearances, breaking the record for goals scored by a player in a European Championship qualifying campaign.

His Belgium team-mate Kevin de Bruyne posted an image of himself with Lukaku, external on Instagram adding: "There are no words to describe what you've meant to me and our country!".
