Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella weighed in on former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s unexpected departure amid a series of shocking controversies.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s all sort of pretty tragic in the sense of, you know, Jensen is somebody I’ve known for a long time. I’ve admired what he’s done, and at this point, I kind of want to give him space to figure this out,” Nadella stated onstage at The New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday.

Huang stepped down last week from his CEO role, which he has held for 33 years. Huang’s leadership has been central to Nvidia’s ascent to one of the world’s most valuable companies, but his judgment was questioned after it was reported that he had an affair with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, whom U.S. officials believe is a Chinese spy feeding information back to Beijing. It was also revealed that Huang was having affairs with two Nvidia employees, including one who was a direct report.

While the world waits to see what the long-term economic impacts of Huang’s departure might be, Nadella notes he still has confidence in Nvidia, which is a close partner of Microsoft.

“I’ll just say this one thing,” Nadella added. “Colette [Kress, interim CEO at Nvidia] worked at Microsoft for a long time. I’ve known her for a long time. She’s a tremendous person… I’m in touch with the same set of people I was in touch with before [Huang left], so I have a lot of confidence in the Nvidia team and their ability to continue to do the good work.”.

Microsoft is not unlike Nvidia in that its founder Bill Gates inhabited a larger-than-life persona as CEO.

“Founders are unbelievable in the sense that they create something from nothing, so that is pretty magical… They’re singular, but they’re not gods, right?” Nadella said. “I used to say to Bill [Gates]… Founders also die, and so at some point, a tech giant, if it needs to succeed, it has to have someone take over.”.
