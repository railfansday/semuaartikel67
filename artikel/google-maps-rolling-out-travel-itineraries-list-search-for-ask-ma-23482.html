📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ahead of the holiday season, Google Maps is launching a handy travel itineraries feature alongside a slew of other newly introduced capabilities for Ask Maps.

Industry observers note that ask Maps can now build travel itineraries that feature numbered map pins for each stop. It supports everything from shopping excursions to multi-day road trips with several stops. When used for the latter, Google Maps will generate a day-by-day schedule with tips and the numbered pins that aid visualization. Plans can be shared with others and exported to Google Docs on desktop.

Once on the trip, Ask Maps will show directions and real-time details, including live traffic conditions, whether a place is open, and “even what the vibe is at your next stop.” You can conversationally have Google add more stops to the itinerary.

Meanwhile, Ask Maps can now tap into your saved lists to find bookmarked places and take action. This lets you ask: “Which spots saved in my lists have availability for five people in the next 30 minutes?”.

Before you start directions, you’ll see familiar “Know Before You Go” tips that surface information about your route, like parking locations, car charging options, weather, potential delays, and tolls.

New agentic capabilities let Ask Maps find online beauty, pet care, auto, and home repair appointments, as well as call local businesses on your behalf. For example, “Find me a 4 p.m. nail appointment near me” returns a list of open slots.

Finally, Ask Maps is expanding to 30 more countries across Europe, including Spain, Germany, and the UK.

These updates are rolling out starting today on Google Maps for Android and iOS. Travel itineraries and list search are available in all 200+ Ask Maps countries. Know Before You Go in directions and appointment booking are coming first to the US.
