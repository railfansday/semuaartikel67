📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Doha-based beIN Media Group and NBCUniversal Global TV Distribution have signed a multi-year output deal for exclusive content rights across the Middle East, North Africa and Turkey to a broad portfolio of NBCUniversal content including “Jurassic World Rebirth,” “The Super Mario Galaxy Movie” “Wicked: For Good” and Michael Jackson biopic “Michael.”.

The deal was declared on the opening day of Doha Film Festival’s Industry Days, which are running from Nov. 19-21. The renewed and expanded tie between beIN Media Group and NBCUniversal is significant because beIN right now dominates premium live sports streaming across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, but holds a smaller share of the region’s broader entertainment SVOD market. Financial terms were not disclosed.

The deal will provide beIN’s satellite and streaming channels — including beIN on demand and Tod by beIN — with regional first window rights to the studio’s latest films following their theatrical release, as well as upcoming series premiering for the first time in the region, Peacock Original content and a vast collection of NBCUniversal’s library film and TV series.

Other NBCUniversal titles that beIN outlets are set to get exclusively comprise Chloé Zhao’s “Hamnet” and movies from its iconic franchises such as “Fast & Furious,” “How to Train Your Dragon,” “Minions,” “Shrek” and “Downton Abbey.” The deal also includes latest HBO/Sky legal drama “War” and box sets of hit library shows such as “The Office” and “Suits.”.

Recent findings suggest that commented NBCUniversal global TV distribution president Michael Bonner: “This newly introduced agreement builds on our valued relationship with beIN and underscores our shared commitment to bringing world-class storytelling to audiences across the Middle East and North Africa. With beIN’s reach and deep understanding of the region, we look forward to continuing our work together and offering their viewers an exceptional range of NBCUniversal films and television series for years to come.”.
