The Golden Bachelorette’s Joan Vassos wants to assure fans her split from Chock Chapple was far from dramatic.

It has been revealed that “We spent the last two years really getting to know each other, and we traveled, we introduced each other to our friends and family, spent lots of time together,” Vassos, 63, shared via Bachelor Nation on Wednesday, November 18. “But I feel like in the last, you know, maybe six months, things have been — it’s not that they’ve gone bad. Maybe we were just drifting apart a little bit.”.

She continued, “We just finally decided that maybe we weren’t each other’s people, and there are no hard feelings.”.

Earlier this month, Vassos surprised some fans when she declared her split from Chapple, 63, after two years together.

Recent findings suggest that “We have decided to end our engagement,” she wrote via her Instagram Story on November 6. “We truly want each other to be happy, and I’m grateful for the chapter we shared.”.

While participating in a panel aboard Bachelor Nation’s Vacation at Sea, Vassos reiterated that she has no hard feelings about her ex.

Sources indicate that “I still want everything good for Chock in his life,” she shared. “He’s a really, really good man and a good guy. He just wasn’t my guy.”.

In a recent development, fans met Vassos when she appeared on season 1 of The Golden Bachelor with Gerry Turner in 2023. After she quit in week three due to a family emergency, the Pancreatic Cancer Action Network ambassador had the opportunity to become the lead of The Golden Bachelorette.

Since the breakup, Vassos said she has been receiving support from the many girlfriends she met on The Golden Bachelor.

“Certainly, Kathy [Swarts] and Susan [Noles] have called me every day and also our friend Nancy [Hulkower] from Golden Bachelor has been so supportive,” she said. “People who really understand this are the Golden women.”.

Although Vassos may not have found her forever person on reality TV, she remains grateful for the process.
