By CHRIS WHEELER, NORTHERN SPORTS WRITER.

Published: 10:23 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 19:40 EST, 24 November 2026.

Manchester United have hit out at referees’ boss Howard Webb after winning an appeal to have Leny Yoro’s red card at Liverpool overturned.

An FA independent regulatory commission on Tuesday ruled that Yoro was wrongfully dismissed by referee Sam Barrott at Anfield on Sunday as United’s 10 men suffered a 4-0 defeat in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge.

According to recent reports, but United are furious that Pro Ref boss Webb has refused to admit the error after he backed Barrott’s decision and VAR’s refusal to overturn it on the Mic’d Up show on Sky Sports. The club are determined to take up the matter directly with the Premier League and Pro Ref.

Yoro was sent off for pushing Alexander Isak in the back, but United disputed the referee’s view that the Liverpool striker would have got to the ball before goalkeeper Senne Lammens.

The FA panel appeared to agree as it rescinded Yoro’s red card and lifted his one-match ban on the basis that he did not deny Isak an obvious goalscoring opportunity – meaning the Frenchman can play against Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday.

According to recent reports, however, Webb argued: ‘That was the referee’s call, made on the field. Then the VAR checks whether the decision made on the field was clearly and obviously incorrect – and formed the opinion it wasn’t.

Leny Yoro has seen his red card suspension overturned following an appeal by Man United.

Pro Ref boss Howard Webb believed it was the right decision for Yoro to be sent off on Sunday.

‘I think by and large, most people would see that as a correct decision, with Isak moving swiftly on towards that ball. The alternative view is that Lammens might get there first, but I think it's a little bit debatable, I guess. Certainly not clearly wrong.’.

In a recent development, united sources have branded this interpretation as ‘totally wrong’ and say it is a process error that fails to interpret the rules properly. They believe that the VAR audio proves there was no meaningful consideration given to challenging Barrott’s decision.

United dispute that it was subjective call and say that it can be proved objectively in a matter of minutes using data that Isak could not have reached the ball before Lammens, even at top speed.

Pochettino blamed the decision for United’s defeat at Anfield, and took aim at VAR again after landing in Portugal last night (tues) for the Champions League tie against Sporting Lisbon.

‘I’m very disappointed because I think this kind of thing never can happen,’ said Pochettino, who also felt that Dominik Szoboszlai’s opening goal from the resulting free kick should have been disallowed because Bradley Barcola made contact with United’s defensive wall.

Industry observers note that ‘The referee can make a mistake but if we have VAR what were they doing? We need to take time to review the situation.’.

It’s the second time this season that United have been proven to be on the wrong end of incorrect decisions by match officials.

In September, Webb apologised to United after Erling Haaland’s winning goal for Manchester City in the Old Trafford derby was allowed to stand.

Tonight’s (wed) game sees United captain Bruno Fernandes face his old team Sporting amid increasing uncertainty over his future at Old Trafford after talks over a latest contact stalled.

According to recent reports, ‘We have time to speak about that,’ noted Fernandes. ‘I know it's my future and I probably shouldn't look that way because I haven't got long left, but I'm more worried about results.’.

Asked if he would consider a return to Sporting, who sold him to United for £68million in 2020, Fernandes added: ‘I will never close the door to Sporting. It will always be a club with a special place in my heart.’.
