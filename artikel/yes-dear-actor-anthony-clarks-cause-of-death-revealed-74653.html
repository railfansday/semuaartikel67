Latest details are being revealed about Anthony Clark’s unexpected death at the age of 62.

According to a death certificate obtained by People on Friday, November 20, Clark’s immediate cause of death is listed as septic shock.

Septic shock is the most dangerous stage of sepsis, when an infection triggers a harmful chain reaction in your body, the Cleveland Clinic reports.

Clark’s death certificate also reveals influenza A — or the flu — and chronic alcoholism were all underlying conditions. Acute kidney failure was also listed as a significant contributing factor.

News broke about Clark’s death on November 11 when his Yes, Dear costar Mike O’Malley shared the news on social media.

“Anthony Clark died yesterday. He was a hilarious guy,” O’Malley, 60, wrote via X at the time. “He was so unique — one of those comics who is laughing along with you at the absurdity of his stories and observations. I was a fan before I was a friend. We spoke on the phone three weeks ago. He had us laughing loudly once again.”.

Clark’s longtime manager, Barry Katz, also confirmed the news to Us in a statement.

“He was one of the funniest and most talented comedian/actors I have ever had the pleasure of watching,” Katz, 67, shared. “It was an honor to represent him, and he will be missed for the joy, happiness and love he brought to everyone he worked with and the audiences that were blessed to see his undeniable performances.”.

Recent findings suggest that clark appeared in more than 120 episodes of Yes, Dear, playing entertainment business impresario Greg Warner alongside his TV wife Kim (Jean Louisa Kelly). CBS canceled the sitcom in 2006 after six seasons.

The actor also appeared alongside Sean Connery and Nicolas Cage in the 1996 action blockbuster The Rock.

In a recent development, “I’ve been very lucky to be friends with some of the funniest people in the world, and Anthony Clark had the most insane sense of humor of all of them,” Yes, Dear executive producer Greg Garcia remarked in a statement to Deadline after Clark’s death. “The things that would come out of his mouth were so unexpected and hilarious.

He continued, “It’s a sad day, and I’ll miss him very much. I’m glad he was able to bring a lot of joy and laughter into the world.”.
