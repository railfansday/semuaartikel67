By Howard Smith – Nov 27, 2026 at 4:49PM EST.

NASDAQ · As of 1:15 PM ET 11/27/26.

Sources indicate that intel (INTC +5.38%), a PC, data center, and foundry semiconductor maker closed at $57.40, up 5.38%. Chip and AI-linked stocks rebounded on Friday after Thanksgiving, and investors are now watching semiconductor demand and Intel's foundry roadmap.Trading volume reached 76.2 million shares, coming in about 36% below its three-month average of 119.3 million shares.

Sources indicate that the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) rose 1.60% to 7,296, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +2.31%) gained 2.31% to 24,130. In semiconductors and integrated device manufacturing, sector rivals Advanced Micro Devices closed at $424.54, up 4.56%, and Qualcomm closed at $179.27, up 6.29%, as chip names recovered on broader AI enthusiasm.

There wasn't any meaningful manufacturer-specific news from Intel today, but it helped lead a broad rebound across chip and tech names in the holiday-shortened session.

Intel shares are still down 36% over the past month amid a technology-stock correction, so today's move partly reflected investors repositioning technology portions of their portfolios.

Last month, Intel reported Q3 results that beat guidance, with robust data-center revenue, but investors are still weighing that against concerns about a weak outlook. There will likely be more focus on names like Intel, where the U.S. government has become the chipmaker's largest single shareholder with a nearly 10% stake.

Congressional committee testimonies related to chip export controls begin next week, so investors should expect continued volatility in names including Intel.
