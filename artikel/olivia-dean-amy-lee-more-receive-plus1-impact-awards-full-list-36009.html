Olivia Dean and Amy Lee of Evanescence — both past Grammy winners for best new artist — are among the winners of the third annual PLUS1 Impact Awards, which recognize the artists, industry leaders and partners driving measurable social impact across music and entertainment.

According to recent reports, the 2026 honorees span a wide range of causes and initiatives, including food access, youth arts education, grassroots community support and sustained philanthropic efforts. The awards recognize contributions from across the music industry, including artists, agents, managers, promoters, venues, festivals and other industry professionals.

“The music industry has an incredible ability to bring people and resources together around causes that matter,” Marika Anthony-Shaw, founder and CEO of PLUS1, mentioned in a statement. “We’re proud to recognize the artists and partners who are using their influence to support communities and create lasting impact, both on and off the stage.”.

The PLUS1 Impact Awards recognize honorees based on their contributions to social impact through PLUS1, including funds raised, community engagement, ongoing philanthropic commitments and leadership in advancing meaningful initiatives.

“I think music has a really special ability to bring people together, and it’s key to think about how we can use that connection to do something positive,” mentioned Dean, who is receiving the Impact Leader Award for making the biggest impact through PLUS1 in 2026. “I’m grateful to be recognized by PLUS1 and to be part of a community that believes in using music to make a difference.”.

“Giving back has always been crucial to us, and feels more crucial than ever,” said Evanescence’s Lee, Outstanding Contribution Award honoree. “We are determined to use the platform we’ve built to bring attention and resources to communities around the world that need them. Food, medical care, things we can’t take for granted. We are so grateful to PLUS1 for making that work possible and for recognizing the artists who are committed to doing more.”.

“There’s so much work that happens behind the scenes to help artists bring their philanthropic commitments to life,” remarked Eli Gelernter of THE·TEAM, who received the Agent of Change Award. “It’s rewarding to be part of a community that recognizes the importance of that work and makes giving back a meaningful part of the music industry.”.

In a recent development, PLUS1, a nonprofit founded on the simple idea of investing $1 from every concert ticket into communities in need, has partnered with hundreds of artists and industry leaders to direct over $40 million to nonprofit organizations worldwide. Through its work, PLUS1 helps artists turn their platforms into tangible support for the causes and communities that matter to them. To learn more, visit PLUS1’s site.
