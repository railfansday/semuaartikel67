Arsenal have reportedly reached an agreement with Declan Rice over a new long-term contract.

Rice is right now on the deal he signed when he arrived from West Ham in a £105m transfer in the summer of 2023.

That contract runs until June 2028, with an option to extend by a further 12 months, but his current terms are set to be replaced by a latest deal.

The England international recently gave an patch on his Arsenal future, revealing that a fresh deal will "hopefully be arriving soon".

As per The Athletic, Rice has now agreed a upcoming five-year contract that will see him extend his Arsenal stay into the next decade, with an official confirmation expected in the near future.

The latest contract will keep him at the Emirates Stadium for his peak years, given he will be 32 by the time the proposed deal expires in 2031.

The report suggests the terms of the deal will reflect Rice's status as an integral member of the Arsenal squad.

The 27-year-old has made 173 competitive appearances for the Gunners, contributing 22 goals and 36 assists.

Rice started 35 of his 36 league appearances during the 2025-26 campaign, as he played a key role in ending Arsenal's 22-year wait for Premier League glory.

The Englishman is not the only player who is set to sign fresh terms, with The Athletic reporting that Timber has agreed to a contract extension with the Gunners.

Timber's proposed newly introduced contract will see him extend his stay beyond the end of his current deal, which expires in the summer of 2028.

The Dutchman, who arrived from Ajax in 2023, featured regularly in Arsenal's title-winning campaign, chipping in with three goals and six assists in 30 Premier League appearances last term.
