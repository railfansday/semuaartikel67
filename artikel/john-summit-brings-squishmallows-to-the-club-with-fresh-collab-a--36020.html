Squishmallows are going to the club via John Summit‘s newly introduced collaboration with the plush toy brand.

Industry observers note that inspired by the escaping the 9-to-5 themes of Summit’s 2026 CTRL ESCAPE album and its subsequent arena tour, the collection features two limited-edition 10-inch Bull Squishmallows and a keychain plush named “Summit.” These items will be available at select CTRL ESCAPE tour locations and via Summit’s website. See images of the collection exclusively below.

According to recent reports, “I’ve been a huge Squishmallows fan for years,” the producer says in a statement. “I have my own collection at my home; it’s like my second family. So when I got the opportunity to be able to create my own, it was honestly surreal. Being able to bring my crazy ‘John Summit world’ to life in a completely different way has been a fun experience and a dream come true!” mentioned John Summit. This collaboration rolls out the “Signature Series” Squishmallows Collection from Squishmallows manufacturer Jazwares that will feature limited-edition designs inspired by globally known artists and other pop culture trends.

Industry observers note that “John has been a huge Squishmallows fan, so it was a natural fit for us to collaborate with him and bring something he is truly passionate about to his massive fanbase,” adds Jazwares chief brand officer Gerhard Runken. “Our Squishmallows Signature Series opens the door for new creative exploration with today’s top talent from music, pop culture, fashion, and beyond, giving them a new platform to authentically engage their fandoms like never before.”.

Jazwares notes that since Squishmallows introduced in 2017, the toy has sold more than 590 million plush units worldwide. Meanwhile, Summit’s CTRL ESCAPE TOUR continues through December, with upcoming stops in Miami, Chicago, Philadelphia, Brooklyn, Oakland and Los Angeles.
