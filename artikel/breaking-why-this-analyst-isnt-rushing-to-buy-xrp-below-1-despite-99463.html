According to recent reports, XRP’s return below the psychologically vital $1 level hasn’t convinced every investor that it is time to buy the dip. Despite believing the payments-based token could eventually climb to a staggering $10, one trader is holding back. His cautious stance raises an interesting question: if XRP has such massive upside potential, why is he waiting on the sidelines?

In a November 19 post on X, crypto trader VirtualBacon identified the $1 price level as a potential bottoming zone for XRP in the current market cycle. The analyst suggested that the cryptocurrency may be approaching the lower end of its correction, even as selling pressure continues to weigh on its price.

“For me, $1 is roughly the bottom area for this cycle,” he stated.

While he described the $1 mark as the approximate bottoming zone for XRP during the ongoing cycle, he did not suggest that a definitive price floor had already been established.

His hesitation stems from the belief that investors will still have plenty of opportunities to accumulate alternative cryptocurrencies even after the broader market begins recovering. In his view, many altcoins could remain relatively cheap early in the next market rebound, meaning investors don’t necessarily need to rush into XRP before its downtrend runs its course.

“I’m not buying it yet,” he explained, making it clear that he would rather wait for a more favorable entry point than rush into the market.

XRP was trading at $0.9647 at the time of publication, down 2.3% over the previous 24 hours. The latest pullback followed a brief recovery to $1 on November 18, 2026, which proved short-lived as investors took profits, pushing the token back below the key psychological threshold.

Although VirtualBacon is not ready to accumulate XRP at current prices, he remains optimistic about its longer-term potential. The pundit believes the cryptocurrency could eventually reach $10, although he sees limited room for significant gains beyond that valuation.

Sources indicate that A rally to $10 would represent a major milestone for XRP, pushing its market capitalization to approximately $635 billion based on a circulating supply of 63.52 billion tokens. This would place the cryptocurrency among the largest digital assets by market value and require its valuation to grow more than tenfold from current levels.

Recent findings suggest that the projected price target also highlights the scale of growth XRP would need. At $10 per token, its fully diluted valuation, calculated using the maximum supply of 100 billion XRP, would reach approximately $1 trillion.

However, reaching such a valuation would require sustained demand, increased market participation, and significant capital flowing into the cryptocurrency. Notably, while VirtualBacon considers $10 achievable over the longer term, his outlook suggests that he sees this level as a more realistic upper target rather than expecting XRP to deliver substantially higher gains.
