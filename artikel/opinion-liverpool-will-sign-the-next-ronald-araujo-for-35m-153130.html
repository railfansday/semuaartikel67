Ronald Araujo's Liverpool future is still up in the air.

The defender has been top class for the Reds since he joined them in the summer and, although it took him a couple of games to get into Iraola's prepares, he's now earned himself a guaranteed place in his starting XI.

Indeed, he is being used as a right-back which isn't his natural position but, he's that good in both positions that it doesn't really matter where he's played.

He helps bring an extra layer of defensive stability to Anfield and he's also helped shore up their back line massively.

This has allowed Jeremy Jacquet the time to adjust to Premier League life and has ensured that he was one of the best defensive transfers made in the summer.

But, since Liverpool's option to buy the midfielder is rather high at €55 million, alternative options are now making their way onto the Reds' shortlist.

Liverpool can sign Mohamed Simakan for €35 million.

Right-back has been a problem area for Liverpool ever since they got rid of Trent Alexander-Arnold last summer.

Jeremie Frimpong was brought in to try and help plug the gap while Conor Bradley was expected to take over as Liverpool's first choice right-back.

But, thanks to the injuries that both players suffered, the Reds' looks didn't work out exactly as they wanted them to which resulted in Araujo being signed this summer.

As mentioned, the Uruguayan has been in fine form this term but, since he's not an actual Liverpool player, a decision needs to be made on his future.

But, with the way that things seem to be going, Araujo looks likely to return to Barcelona.

This has led to fans asking many questions about who their starting right-back is going to be with Caught Offside now throwing a newly introduced defender into the mix.

"Liverpool, Manchester United, Arsenal, Tottenham and Newcastle have all continued scouting Simakan, although no formal offers or advanced negotiations have taken place," they wrote.

"For Liverpool, Simakan’s versatility is particularly attractive.

"With long-term succession planning required in central defence, signing a player capable of operating centrally while also covering right-back would give the squad valuable flexibility.

"The asking price will be crucial. At €35m-€40m, Simakan would represent a relatively manageable investment compared with some of the €70m-plus defenders presently being linked with both clubs.

"There are still questions about adapting back to European football after his spell in Saudi Arabia, but at 26 he should be entering his peak years.".

Mohamed Simakan can cover all the positions Ronald Araujo does for Liverpool.

Not only is Mohamed Simakan younger than Araujo but, he's also worth a lot less.

€35 million for a 26-year-old in this market is unheard of, especially for a versatile defender such as the Frenchman.

As Caught Offside mention, his last couple of years being spent in Saudi Arabia do pose a bit of a worry but, within a few months of making a return to European football, he should be able to get back to his best.

The main stumbling block between Liverpool and Barcelona for an Araujo deal is the fee that the La Liga outfit want.

So, the fact that Simakan is younger, can play in all the positions that Araujo covers for Liverpool and the fact that he'd be cheaper for them to buy, makes a deal for the Frenchman a no-brainer.
