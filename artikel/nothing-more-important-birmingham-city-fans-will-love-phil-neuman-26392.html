Birmingham City have seen Phil Neumann star in their rise to the top of the Championship table. The German defender has grown into a key figure in his second season with BCFC, and his performances are not going unnoticed.

In a recent development, amid links with Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina, Neumann has now been quizzed on his future in an interview with Transfermarkt. While maintaining the belief that he can play at a higher level, he insisted that his full focus is on the Blues’ promotion push. He noted: “Of course I hear about those kinds of reports, but I don’t pay much attention to them. I have my advisors for that, people I trust who handle those matters.

Recent findings suggest that “My full focus is on Birmingham City and what we’ve set out to achieve as a team. There’s nothing more notable to me than doing everything I can to achieve our goals with the club. We have a massive task ahead of us, and that’s where I’m concentrating all my energy. Everything else is secondary at the moment.”.

In a recent development, neumann is under contract at St Andrew’s until 2028. He later went on to say that if he was to leave the Blues, “the whole package would have to be right”.

In a recent development, standout players in the Championship often have eyes on moves to the next level. In recent years, those to establish themselves in the second tier before making notable steps up include Hayden Hackney, Shea Charles and Borja Sainz.

It has been revealed that the level of Neumann’s performances for Birmingham City suggests that he may be one of the next Championship aces to earn a move to a higher level. But, his words show that he has nothing on his mind other than the Blues’ ongoing promotion push.

The commitment to the cause he has shown in his response to rumours is aligned with the heart he’s showing on his pitch. Neumann embodies how this BCFC side are all pulling in the same direction to achieve their goal after a challenging 2025/26 season.

It is exactly what the fans were asking for as the club look to reach their lofty targets.

At 29, Neumann will feel that he needs to take a chance to play in the top flight sooner rather than later. The clear aim is to do so with Birmingham City, but interested clubs could offer him a route to a higher level if the Blues can’t make it.

According to recent reports, given the centre-back’s comments, it would be a surprise – and a substantial blow – to see him leave mid-season. You have to feel it would be the perfect package for both him and BCFC to sway them into a deal.

Recent findings suggest that if Chris Davies‘ side can’t make it to the Premier League this season though, the chances of Neumann leaving will grow. He will be in the last year of his contract, and there will be sides ready to offer him a shot at a higher level.

For now though, Neumann’s full focus is on the promotion push at St Andrew’s.
