Your complete guide to the ALL Accor hotel loyalty program

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (190 words, 📖 Standard)
  • ALL Accor is the loyalty program for Accor Group, covering over 5,800 hotels across more than 45 brands worldwide, with a strong presence outside North America.
  • Reward points in this program have a fixed value in euros toward eligible hotel stays, making redemptions straightforward but limiting opportunities for outsized value.
  • Elite status can be earned through nights or eligible spending, with higher tiers offering perks such as lounge access, suite upgrades and complimentary breakfast in certain regions.
  • Paid ALL Accor+ memberships provide discounts, status nights and other benefits, potentially offsetting their cost for frequent guests.
  • Not all Accor brands or properties participate fully in the program, and some benefits are region-specific or subject to availability.
  • Points earned and elite status qualification are based on brand, status tier and eligible pre-tax spending, with annual resets for status each calendar year.
  • Transfers from U.S.-accessible partners generally occur at less than a 1:1 ratio, and there is no U.S.-issued cobranded card for this program.
