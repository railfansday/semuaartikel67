Christina Ricci has opened up about the alleged breakdown of her friendship with Mermaids costar Cher.

“I’m not [still in touch]. Unfortunately, she’s still mad at me, she holds a grudge,” Ricci, 46, said on the Tuesday, November 17, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen when asked by the host about their friendship after bonding on the set of the 1990 film.

Recent findings suggest that when Cohen, 58, expressed shock over Ricci’s response, the Yellowjackets star broke down her perspective on what went wrong with the pop icon, 80. Ricci said that during a joint magazine interview conducted when Ricci was 19 years old, Cher had said “all these things that were not that amazing about me.”.

Her story continued, “Then she left me a voicemail about how journalists are always trying to get the worst things and blah, blah, blah, and she was so sorry, and I was 19, and so I didn’t return that message.”.

Cohen, who pointed out that Ricci had spoken highly of her former costar previously on his show, then concluded, “She was mad at you for not returning the message,” to which Ricci agreed before further claimed that she’s reached out to the singer since and “gotten radio silence.”.

Recent findings suggest that despite the claimed friction, Ricci mentioned that she remains fond of Cher. “I adore her, she’s the best and I absolutely, 100 percent, can imagine being Cher and being like, ‘Oh my god, they misquoted me and I’m going to call this young person and explain to her that they misquoted me,’ and then the gall of that young person to not return the call,” she explained. “I would be so pissed, I get it. Do you know what I’m saying?”.

She then offered a direct message to Cher, turning to the camera and saying, “Love you, Cher, I love you so much.”.

While it’s unclear which magazine had published Cher’s quotes about Ricci, Ricci did tell Rolling Stone in 1999 — when she was 19 years old — that Cher’s early support saw her land the role of Kate Flax, the daughter of Rachel Flax, played by Cher, after six auditions. She added that she avoided doing her school work while on set by hiding “in Cher’s trailer, because my tutor was too intimidated to knock on her door.”.

Industry observers note that ricci also sung the praises of Cher in a 2024 interview with Entertainment Weekly, detailing how close they became while filming Mermaids, which also starred Winona Ryder as Kate’s older sister, Charlotte Flax. “Cher took me under her wing, and she recognized that sometimes you could be on a set, and you don’t know exactly what is going on behind the scenes — power struggles, or different issues,” Ricci told the outlet at the time. “And if you don’t know any better, and have no experience, you can think it was your fault.

She added that Cher served as a mentor at the time, concluding, “She never wanted me to feel insecure.”.
