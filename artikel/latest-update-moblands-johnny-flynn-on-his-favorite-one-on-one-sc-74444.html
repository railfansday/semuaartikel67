According to recent reports, and then, to wrap up, you guys have these really big firefight scenes where you're shooting, and there are guns everywhere, and there are a lot of stunts. What is it like filming on those types of days? Is it exhausting, or is it more somebody else's work, and you're just there for the shots where your face is involved?

According to recent reports, FLYNN: No, it is pretty full-on. It's exhausting, like, the major firefight at the safe house. That was a whole night of...
