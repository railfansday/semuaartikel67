📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the tabletop-to-digital pipeline is a tried-and-true method of bringing fresh and exciting games to mobile. And now, developer Chaotic Great are set to step onto iOS and Android with their roguelike deck-building strategy game Gudnak Digital! Arriving December 1st, it gives you the freedom to make strategic decisions, no matter how ill-advised.

Tongue-in-cheek tagline aside, Gudnak Digital takes you to a fantastical world orbiting a dying sun. With the planet slowly cooling and everyone, understandably, unhappy about the subject, factions are pitted against one another in constant battle to overwhelm the other as they duke it out on the field of warfare.

Industry observers note that the key, then, as the developers put it, is to develop incremental advantages over your opponent, slowly wearing them down and setting up for your inevitable victory. Of course, that also means one misstep, and you may find yourself open to an unexpected move that leads only to defeat.

It has been revealed that but you wouldn't end up like that, would you? Well, you'll just have to look for Gudnak Digital on iOS and Android come December 1st to find out for yourself. And if you want to be in with an early chance to play on iPhone, be sure to check out the Gudnak Digital iOS TestFlight.
