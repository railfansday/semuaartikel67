Holland America Line has announced that bookings are open for its 2028 Alaska Cruisetour season, giving guests their first opportunity to pair an overland journey into the Great Land with a cruise aboard the reimagined Oosterdam.

The Oosterdam will make its Alaska debut in 2028, bringing latest onboard experiences to a Cruisetour program that combines the coast with deeper exploration into Alaska’s interior.

Guests can choose from 19 Cruisetours itineraries aboard four ships, ranging from nine to 17 days, operating between May and September.

Each experience combines an Alaska cruise with an overland journey that includes a stay at the newly transformed Holland America Denali Lodge, following completion of its $70 million multi-year enhancement project in 2027.

Each Cruisetour also includes a tour into the park and travel through Alaska’s interior by rail and motorcoach, the tech giant mentioned in a press release.

The tech giant added that Classic, Signature and Ultimate Alaska & Denali Cruisetours pair the overland experience with a seven-day Glacier Discovery cruise aboard the Oosterdam or Eurodam, while Alaska, Denali & Yukon Cruisetours travel farther into Canada’s Yukon Territory with either a seven-day Glacier Discovery cruise or a shorter Alaska Inside Passage cruise aboard the Koningsdam or Zaandam.

Holland America Line is the only cruise line to take guests into the Yukon on a Cruisetour, traveling beyond Skagway to Whitehorse and Dawson City.
