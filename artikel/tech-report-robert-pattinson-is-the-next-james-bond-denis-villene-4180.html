It has been revealed that robert Pattinson is your new James Bond. Denis Villeneuve has cast Pattinson as the star in his 007 movie for Amazon MGM Studios. He will now be the seventh actor to officially star in the franchise and will lead what will be the 26th James Bond movie to hit the massive screen.

Villeneuve is directing Bond 26 from a script by Steven Knight, and the franchise is being guided by mega producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman, who took over from the long-time stewards of 007, Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, upon the rights to Bond being acquired by Amazon following its acquisition of MGM.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team,” mentioned Robert Pattinson. Pattinson this year starred in “The Drama” and “Primetime” at A24, and is presently in production on Matt Reeves’ “The Batman Part II.” Last year’s credits included “Die My Love” opposite Jennifer Lawrence and “Mickey 17,” from Bong Joon Ho.

According to recent reports, “Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond as we proudly introduce Robert Pattinson as the next 007,” mentioned Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios. “This iconic character has captivated audiences around the world for more than six decades, and there’s no doubt that Robert’s remarkable range, magnetic screen presence, and quiet intensity make him an exceptional choice to carry that legacy forward. With Denis Villeneuve at the helm, Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing, Tanya Lapointe executive producing, and a brilliant script from Steven Knight, we have a world-class creative team in place to usher in a fresh era of Bond, and we couldn’t be more excited about the journey ahead.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Few films carry the kind of anticipation that comes with James Bond, and we have strived to honor the passionate fans, the legacy, and the future of this iconic character. We are thrilled to officially welcome Robert Pattinson as our 007 and are confident he will bring something truly special to this role. Robert, Denis, Amy, David, and Tanya are embarking on an amazing adventure together,” said Courtenay Valenti, head of film at Amazon MGM Studios.

It has been revealed that “Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin,” stated director Denis Villeneuve.

According to recent reports, “James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times,” said producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman.

Recent findings suggest that it’s been well over a year of fervent speculation as to whom would take up the mantle as Britain’s top super spy, as Villeneuve was unveiled to be taking the directing job way back in June 2025. Rumors abounded about who could star, with reports about a sprawling wish list and group of young Brits who had tested for the part. Film chief Courtenay Valenti teased the upcoming Bond movie at this year’s CinemaCon but noted “it’s a responsibility we don’t take lightly” and would share more when the time was right. And with Villeneuve’s “Dune: Part 3” finally arriving this December, that time appears to be now.

The 26th Bond will be the first James Bond movie since 2021’s “No Time to Die,” which was the fifth and final entry for Daniel Craig as 007. That film, directed by Cary Joji Fukunaga, earned $774.1 million worldwide in its initial release (a UK re-release grossed another $132 million), putting it among the highest-grossing Bond movies ever, even when adjusted for inflation. The 25 films dating back to the ’60s have grossed $7.6 billion at the box office worldwide.
