The highest scorers in this derby between northern giants include England captains, Premier League legends and Ballon d'Or winners. But there are some notable absentees. Liverpool's all-time top scorer, Ian Rush, only netted three of his 346 goals against the Red Devils, while Manchester United icon Sir Bobby Charlton mustered just four.
