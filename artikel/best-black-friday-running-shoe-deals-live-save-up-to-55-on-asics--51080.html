There's no better time to buy running shoes than during the Black Friday sales, when widespread discounts mean it's smart to stock up on the sneakers you need for the many miles of running ahead of you.

As a running shoe reviewer, I've been telling my friends and family to wait for Black Friday to grab fresh sneakers, and tracking the prices of my favorites in the sales.

According to recent reports, I'll only be highlighting deals on shoes I rate highly and have tested myself. Let's see those sales!

Hello and welcome to our running shoe deals live blog! Throughout the day I'm going to be picking out the best deals on the best running shoes I've tested and reviewed and highlighting them here, kicking off with five deals on excellent sneakers.

In a recent development, of the five, I'd start by checking out the Nike Vomero Plus which is down to just $102 in the Nike sale if you stack discounts by using the EARLYBF code at checkout. That's a great price for my all-time favorite max-cushioned shoe, which is as bouncy as sneakers come.

The Saucony Endorphin Speed 5 has been discounted in the Amazon sale for a few weeks now, but the best price available on the shoe is now on the Saucony website, thanks to the fact you can use the code CHEER to get an extra 25% off pretty much everything.

Since the Endorphin Speed 5 is already down to $115 in the Saucony sale, using the code brings it down to just $86 at checkout, which is an outrageously good deal on a plated super-trainer like this.

I've reviewed every model of the Speed to date and just wrapped up testing the latest Endorphin Speed 6, which is a mild improvement on the Sped 5 but will cost you almost $100 when you take this deal into account. I'd definitely get the older shoe myself, when that kind of saving is on offer.

The best carbon plate running shoes are ludicrously expensive at full price, which means during deal season they're subject to some of the biggest discounts of any sneakers.

According to recent reports, of the many deals out there, the 55% saving on the Saucony Endorphin Elite 2 at Amazon is particularly exciting in my opinion. The Endorphin Elite is not my personal favorite racer, but it's exceptionally soft, bouncy and fun, and works well for a wide variety of runners.

Recent findings suggest that other carbon shoe deals I'd consider include the Brooks Hyperion Elite 5, which is $149 down from $275 at Amazon. The Brooks is a little more stable and lighter than the Endorphin Elite 2, if less springy.

The Glycerin Max 2 is the most comfortable shoe in the Brooks line-up, which is really saying something given that Brooks is a brand that excels in making comfortable cushioned shoes.

It's an expensive shoe at $200, but the $50 saving on the Glycerin Max 2 available at Amazon right now makes it a far more palatable purchase.

While that's still a significant outlay, this is one of the most durable shoes out there so the money for miles ratio will be in your favor by the time you've worn it out.

Industry observers note that I've run two marathons in the Puma Fast-R Nitro Elite 3, clocking 2:26 and 2:25. I rate it as the fastest racer you can get right now (with the possible exception of the Adidas Pro Evo 3, which is $500 and basically impossible to buy).

The Fast-R 3 is included in Puma's Black Friday sale and it's discounted by $100, bringing the price down to $199 in several colors, including the excellent Showtime design which is my personal favorite.

All carbon plate shoes are designed to boost your running efficiency, but I feel like the Fast-R 3 does it better than the rest thanks to its aggressive rocker and extended plate.

The Skechers Aero Razor impressed me greatly when I tested it earlier this year, and I rated it as good value at its full price of $140. Right now the women's shoe is just $63 at Amazon, a ludicrous price for such a versatile running shoe.

It's extremely light but still has enough cushioning for longer runs in my experience, and the rockered design pushes you onto your toes when running at faster speeds. It can handle a bit of everything in training, though some runners might like a bit more cushioning underfoot for easy efforts.

Nike's Black Friday sale is always one of my favorites to raid, because the Swoosh allows you to stack discounts to get incredible prices on its top shoes. It also encourages a bit of hunting, because each color of a shoe will have a different discount, so you can't just go by the main search results when looking for deals.

Industry observers note that for example, the Nike Zoom Fly 6 costs $180 at full price, which many colors still are. However, some are significantly reduced; the cheapest colors of the women's shoe are $112, and the men's shoe is $132 in one color.

Stick the women's Zoom Fly 6 in your cart at $112 and it will drop to just $84 with the code EARLYBF, which amounts to almost $100 off the versatile plate super-trainer. That's value.

While the Nike and Saucony sales have impressed me today, Adidas isn't offering enough to runners with its in-house discounts yet. There's no Black Friday code to stack on top of sale prices, and the deals are limited.

One excellent Adidas running shoe that is reduced is the Evo SL, which is down to $120 in the Adidas sale. However, it's cheaper still at Amazon, where you can get the Evo SL for $109, though colors and sizes are limited.

I'd hope to see the Adidas Evo SL available for under $100 at some point in the Black Friday sales, but $109 is still a great price for one of my all-time favorite running shoes.

Sources indicate that the Fresh Balance Rebel v5 is a lightweight daily trainer that's overlooked by too many runners in my opinion. It's very comfortable and impressively stable, and some colors are reduced to $91 in the Amazon sale.

If you can't find a color and size that suits you on Amazon, the good news is that the Rebel v5 is also reduced to $110 in the Newly introduced Balance sale, where there are lots of sizes and colors still available.

If you like to use one running shoe for everything, the Hoka Challenger 8 is a sneaker worth knowing about. It's exceptionally versatile thanks to its all-terrain outsole, which grips well on roads and light trails, and its cushioned, lightweight design.

Sources indicate that it's a great daily trainer for those who like to mix up the surfaces they run on, and handles gravel and dirt tracks particularly well in my experience. It's not as well know as significant Hoka hitters like the Clifton, but I think will actually suit many runners better, because it has a similar ride feel to the Clifton 10 and 11 while being more capable across a variety of terrain.

Right now the Hoka Challenger 8 is reduced to $124 in the Hoka sale. It's not a jaw-dropping discount, but a handy one to get on a versatile shoe that's perfect for trips away.

The On Cloudboom Volt is a shoe I enjoyed testing, but didn't deem worth its full price of $200, so I've been keeping an eye out for it to appear in deals, which it now has.

You can get the Volt for $140 in the On sale at the moment, at which price it's a far more appealing proposition as a lightweight fast trainer you can also use on race day.

This might not sway you as much as it did me, but it's also just about the best-looking running shoe I've tested this year, which is always a plus.

If you prefer trails to road running, the Fresh Balance Hierro v9 is a shoe to consider in the sales. It combines the comfort of a max-cushion road shoe with the grippy outsole you need on the trails, and works well for hiking as well as running in my experience.

It has been revealed that several colors of the Hierro v9 are discounted at Amazon, with the price varying based on the size and color you opt for. So far the cheapest I've found it is $112 for the yellow shoe, but I'd be tempted to spend $10 more to get the teal version for $119.

The Asics sale is a little disappointing so far, but does include the Gel-Nimbus 28 being reduced from $170 to $129, which matches the price the shoe is available for on Amazon.

It's a comfortable cushioned shoe that will last you hundreds of miles, but I'd actually look at the Asics Gel-Nimbus 27 for $100 in the Amazon sale instead, as I didn't notice a massive difference between the two shoes when I tested them out.

I'm also hoping to see a bigger discount on the Asics Novablast 5 appear at some point, as I prefer it to the Gel-Nimbus line myself as a lighter, livelier daily trainer.

Sources indicate that I can't stay away from the Nike sale today, because stacking discounts means some excellent shoes are going for a song. The Nike Structure Plus is the best stability shoe in the brand's line-up, offering a highly comfortable and supportive ride, and you can get it for less than half price.

To do so, you need to pick the color of the shoe that's on sale for $104, then use the code EARLYBF at checkout to get an extra 25% off. That brings the Structure Plus down to just $78.

Now if you're not a fan of this color, which is fair enough as it's a little wild, the good news is many other colors are on sale too, some for $106 so you're getting almost as good a deal after using the code at checkout.

The New Balance 1080v15 is the latest version of the popular cushioned running shoe, and one of the biggest updates to the line in years. A new midsole foam makes it lighter and springier than past versions, and it's a shoe I wasn't sure would hit the sales this Black Friday.

It's not reduced in the New Balance or Amazon sales, but REI has popped up with a $35 discount on one color of the 1080v15, with plenty of sizes still available.

If you want the most comfortable and supportive shoe on your feet during runs the Saucony Hurricane 25 is worth looking at, especially at the reduced price of $91 in Amazon's Black Friday sale.

In a recent development, the Hurricane 25 has a dual-density midsole with a softer, bouncier core surrounded by a firmer frame. This combination delivers both stability and comfort on the run, and the mild rocker on the shoe also helps in rolling you from heel-to-toe with each stride.

I've recently tested the Saucony Hurricane 26 and it is a big update on the Hurricane 25 thanks to a latest midsole foam, but it's also $80 more expensive right now, and the older shoe is still a great option for new and experienced runners alike.
