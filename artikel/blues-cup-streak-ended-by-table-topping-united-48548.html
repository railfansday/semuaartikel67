Everton tasted their first defeat in the Subway Players Cup as the Blues' four-game winning streak in the competition came to its conclusion with a 2-1 defeat away to Manchester United.

First half strikes from Mared Griffiths and Emma Watson put the Red Devils in to a two-goal lead, before Noemie Mouchon halved the deficit two minutes after the break.

Prior to Wednesday night's clash, the Toffees had comfortably beaten Birmingham City, Newcastle United and Sheffield United before thrashing Durham 6-0 in the competition.

Head coach Scott Phelan named a heavily-rotated XI from the side that were held to a goalless draw against Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday.

Rylee Foster-Inman started in between the sticks, with Issy Hobson making her first appearance in two months in front of her.

Up top, Mouchon led the line after netting a brace in the Blues’ 6-0 demolition of Durham seven days ago.

Sources indicate that foster-Inman produced a fine save when faced one-on-one with Griffiths inside six minutes. The keeper rushed out upon recognising the danger and did brilliantly to narrow the angle.

The shot-stopper denied Griffiths once more on 21 minutes as the youngster pulled the trigger from the edge of the box after a cute turn saw her evade Hobson.

Recent findings suggest that the lively Griffiths was in the thick of the action once more in the 34th minute as United came inches away from opening the scoring. Rebecca Bernal burst forward to meet her in swinging cross, the Mexican’s header sailing just over.

Griffiths would not be denied only 60 seconds later as the forward broke the deadlock in style. She sprinted down the left before chopping past Finnigan and firing past the outrushed Foster-Inman.

Watson then doubled the hosts’ lead on the stroke of half-time. Ella Toone wrong-footed Finnigan before pulling it back to the former-Blue. Watson finished emphatically from close range, with that concluding the action for the opening 45.

Defensive duo Hobson and Silcock made way for Hannah Blundell and Rion Ishikawa at the break.

It has been revealed that everton halved the deficit only 60 seconds after the restart as Mouchon netted her third goal from her last two Cup appearances. Quick feet from Vignola allowed the Spaniard room to free Hannah Cain down the right. The Wales international then squared it to the advancing Mouchon, who finished off the post.

Maz Pacheco then came agonisingly close from 20-yards out as her wicked free-kick rattled the upright. The full-back picked her spot from the set-piece – the ball crashing against the crossbar with the keeper well-beaten.

Phelan then made two further changes on 68 minutes as Ruby Mace and Hikaru Kitagawa replaced Clare Wheeler and Vignola.

With eight minutes remaining, Naomi Layzell – who claimed the Player of the Match award when the two sides met in the WSL – came on in place of Aurora Galli.

Despite a late push for an equaliser, United stood firm and succumbed the Toffees to their first defeat in the Subway Players Cup.

Everton return to Goodison Park on Sunday afternoon (2pm KO) as Brighton & Hove Albion travel to L4.
