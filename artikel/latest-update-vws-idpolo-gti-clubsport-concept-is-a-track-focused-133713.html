Sources indicate that the Volkswagen GTI badge has been a staple in automotive culture for 50 years, and it’s clearly not getting sidelined anytime soon. VW just released the ID.Polo GTI Clubsport concept, an electric track-focused GTI that slots in as the automaker’s fourth latest GTI variant this year.

It has been revealed that while it’s not heading to production, the Clubsport concept is a peek into the possibilities riding on the electric ID.Polo GTI, which became the first EV to wear the GTI badge when it was released earlier this year. The standard ID.Polo GTI, which isn’t sold in the United States, reaches 62 mph in 6.8 seconds thanks to a 223-hp electric motor mounted to the front axle.

The Clubsport concept ups the ante, with VW claiming it will hit 62 mph in just 4.7 seconds with the help of launch control and a more powerful APP550 electric motor. The upgraded motor, which is typically used to power the rear wheels of larger models like the ID.Tiguan and ID.7, sends 362 horsepower to the front wheels and nearly doubles the standard electric GTI’s torque output from 213 to 406 pound-feet.

In a recent development, volkswagen also equipped the concept vehicle with an electronically regulated front differential lock, a performance-tuned adaptive suspension system, and ceramic brakes with a one-box brake setup. The high-performance powertrain fits into an equally aggressive two-door build, complete with a large front splitter, diffuser, and a flared rear wing.

According to recent reports, rather than the typical five-seater setup, the Clubsport concept houses two Recaro bucket seats with red five-point harnesses. A Tornado Red roll bar replaces the rear seats, and the cockpit is solely reserved for racing needs, meaning the standard infotainment system is also omitted. The cabin is finished in a dark microfiber material and trimmed with red stitching.

Recent findings suggest that volkswagen calls this concept the “perfect match of heritage and future,” and the automaker claims its young employees were involved in much of the concept’s realization. The ID.Polo GTI Clubsport concept won’t ever make it to VW showrooms, but it’ll be displayed next week at the Essen Motor Show in Essen, Germany.
