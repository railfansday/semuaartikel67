Tottenham have been linked to Piero Ausilio, with the former Inter Milan sporting director earmarked for a similar role at the Premier League club.

Italian outlet Tuttosport report that Spurs are closing in on a deal to bring Ausilio to north London.

It is hoped that Ausilio could be installed in time to take charge of Spurs’ transfer approach for the January transfer window.

Tottenham’s executive structure has frequently changed since 2023.

Fabio Paratici joined the club in 2021 as sporting director, but stepped down in April 2023 ahead of a 30-month ban from all football activities handed to him by FIFA after a conviction for financial malpractice.

Paratici returned to his role in October 2025 after the conclusion of his suspension, sharing duties with co-sporting director Johan Lange.

But his return did not last long, as Paratici quit Spurs in February of this year, meaning Lange stepped up to become sole sporting director.

Ausilio ended a 28-year association with Inter Milan in the summer, with the Italian giants deciding not to renew his contract as sporting director.

Initially joining the club’s youth academy in 1998, Ausilio worked his way through the ranks to become sporting director in 2014.

Industry observers note that midway through his first season in his role, Ausilio was named as the TMW award’s best sporting director at the start of 2015. In 2024 Ausilio was recognised as the ‘best sporting director’ in football at the Globe Soccer Awards.

Retiring from football after injury as a teenager, Ausilio holds a degree in law, having studied at the University of Milan.

Over the years Ausilio has garnered a strong reputation for his scouting and squad-building, helping to construct a team at the San Siro that has won three Serie A titles and reached the Champions League final twice in the past five seasons.
