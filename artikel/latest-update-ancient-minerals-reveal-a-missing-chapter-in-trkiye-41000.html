Latest Update: Ancient minerals reveal a missing chapter in Türkiye's mountain-building history

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (107 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that A mountain range can bear the marks of events from tens of millions of years ago. The challenge lies in determining when each mark was created. In southern Türkiye's Central Taurides, scientists have developed a method to date some of these ancient movements by examining tiny calcite deposits that formed as faults shifted. This new timeline alters the region's geological history. Researchers discovered a previously unrecognized period during which parts of the crust slid laterally, between the Early Eocene and Early Miocene. They also found that the region's later extension began around 25 million years ago, earlier than previously believed.
