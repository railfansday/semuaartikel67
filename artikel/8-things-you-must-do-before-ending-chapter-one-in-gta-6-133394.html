8 Things You Must Do Before Ending Chapter One in GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that there is a lot that you can do in the first chapter of Grand Theft Auto 6. You can deep sea dive for treasure, hang out with friends, complete missions, set up for Lucia, and a lot more. However, some of this absolutely needs to be done before you hit the end of the first chapter.

After chapter one, everything in GTA 6 changes, and you don’t have the opportunity to get everything done that you might want to, so you want to be sure that you have a checklist and that you complete it before making your way to Vice City and starting bigger and better things.

Note: It is important to note that taking up Mattia’s offer will end the first chapter of the game. There will also be spoilers ahead about chapter one.

Industry observers note that jason and Lucia have a chance to be madly in love in the beginning of the game. If you prepare for Lucia to get out of prison, work on Jason’s appearance, and focus on how they interact with each other, it will build a solid foundation.

In a recent development, there is a hard reset that comes at the end of the first chapter, and for a while, Lucia will be on her own while Jason recovers. With that being said, it will take a bit of a toll on their relationship.

This means that if you raise it up very high in the beginning, you won’t need to worry about needing to rebuild it as much later in the game, saving you a bit of time later on.

In a recent development, immediately after setting up your BuckMe account, you are told that you need to start saving money. The first goal in the game is to have a total savings of $5,000 stashed away. This is a pretty easy goal to hit, especially since there are a fair number of money-making opportunities in the first chapter.

You want to make sure that you hit this goal, as when the hard reset comes at the end of the chapter, you will lose everything except what is in your savings account.

As an added bonus, putting money away into your savings account will also raise your relationship, so it is a win-win situation. After you have everything saved up, the rest of your money is stuff you can play with.

Sources indicate that now that you have saved up the necessary cash for your family savings account, you might have some money burning a hole in your pocket. It is time to go on a bit of a shopping spree.

Sources indicate that as I said, you won’t be able to take this money with you into chapter two, so you will want to spend all of it. Buy some clothes, and definitely upgrade your weapons.

There is no point in upgrading your car, as you will lose this after chapter one. Instead, focus on increasing your wardrobe and upgrading your weapons as these will carry over into the next chapter.

There are some missions that you only have access to in the first chapter, and some of them belong to Shirley - Jason’s neighbor. She is a kind lady, and she will occasionally ask you to complete a few tasks.

These consist of getting her some alcohol, and stealing a mobility scooter from a lady nearby. They are fun short missions that you can complete within a few minutes, but they are worth doing because they are quite fun.

In a recent development, don’t worry about the treasure hunting, as you will be able to come back to it later on. Only certain missions disappear after the first chapter.

Industry observers note that there’s tons of details in GTA 6, which means that there are a bunch of collectibles that you can find scattered about. Some of these collectibles are books and magazines, and several of them are found around Jason’s house.

It has been revealed that before you end chapter one, you will want to get the books around Jason’s house. These include one in the bedroom, two in the living room, a magazine on the back deck, and one in the shed. Each of these disappears after the first chapter.

Recent findings suggest that along with this, you will want to grab the sunglasses in Donny’s trailer. You will make your way over there during one of the missions in the first chapter, so you shouldn’t miss out on them, but make sure to pick them up while you have the chance.

Sources indicate that if you have been focused on upgrading your weapons, gathering clothes, and putting money into your savings, then you will probably need some extra cash in the first chapter. Instead of searching for treasure, you should instead focus on completing the lost drug missions.

Recent findings suggest that there are four lost drug missions around Key Lento and up to the Grassrivers. These are special missions that end up disappearing after chapter one ends, so you will not have the ability to go back and complete them later on.

Since this is the only time you have access to them, you may as well complete them for the extra cash and spend it on stuff that will help you later in the game.

Recent findings suggest that while I have already established that buying weapons is important because they carry over, it is also important to grab the unique weapons that are obtainable early in the game. There are a couple weapons that you can find around the islands in Key Lento, and they will carry you in later parts of the game.

For instance, the Assassin Pistol can be found on a small dinghy on an island and it is great for completing missions stealthily in later parts of the game, and it has become one of my go-to weapons for staying hidden.

It has been revealed that there is also a shotgun that you can find that is quite powerful. It gets outclassed by other unique weapons later on, but it is great to have in chapter two.

In a recent development, one thing that is nice about GTA 6 is that it gives you the option to manually save. This means that you can create save points at key points in the game so that you can return to them later on.

I highly recommend completing what you can in chapter one and then creating a save point right before you go to do the tourist trap mission, as this is the last mission of the chapter, and it is also the point of no return.

If you want to play the game over but don’t want to deal with the slower pacing of the first chapter, you can skip all of that with a save right before the chapter change.
