DIRECTV’s MyNews® Genre Pack® features more than 20 live news channels. It includes top networks, including CNN, FOX News, MS Now, C-Span, C-Span 2, CNBC, CNN International, FOX Business, i24News and Newsmax, as well as local channels, such as ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, The CW and MyNetworkTV, where available.

Right now, you can get DIRECTV’s MyNews Pack for just $39.99 per month for the first two months of service — an 11% savings per month, or $5 off per month ($10 in total). After the two months are finished, the monthly price auto-renews at its then-current price (currently $44.99 per month), unless canceled.

In a recent development, meanwhile, the Genre Pack has an unlimited cloud DVR to record your programs. So now, you can relive your favorite debate moments over and over again without the fear of losing DVR cloud storage.

Additionally, a subscription to MyNews Genre Pack can be paired with other Genre Packs, like  MyEntertainment  for even more content, such as access to HBO Max, Hulu, Disney+ and AMC+, and more than 60 live channels, including FX, HGTV, A&E, Bravo, BET, Comedy Central, Discovery Channel, E!, Food Network, Game Show Network, Hallmark Channel, ION, Lifetime and many others.
