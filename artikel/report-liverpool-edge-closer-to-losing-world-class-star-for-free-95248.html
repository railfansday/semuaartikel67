Liverpool have lost a number of their key players on free transfers over the last three years.

Trent Alexander-Arnold, Ibrahima Konate, Mohamed Salah and Roberto Firmino are just some of the players to have left Anfield at the end of their contracts in recent seasons.

The club have become accustomed to letting some of their best players run their contracts down, and it has cost them in the past.

Now, several of Andoni Iraola's key players are entering the final six months of their contracts, and negotiations can soon begin with potential suitors.

One of Liverpool's out-of-contract stars is attracting plenty of interest, and the Reds really could lose him for nothing.

Virgil van Dijk has been absolutely key to Iraola's defence so far this season and is still one of the best performing defenders in the Premier League.

The Dutchman will soon enter the final six months of his deal however, and will then be free to negotiate with clubs from abroad over a free transfer in the summer.

Juventus and AC Milan are the two clubs reported leading the way for the colossus, with the pair keeping a close eye on his contract situation.

That's according to an revision from Ekrem Konur, who claims that Italy's two most decorated clubs are ready to battle for Van Dijk's signature.

Conversations over an extension at Anfield are reportedly ongoing, but are yet to bare any fruit, and the future of the Dutchman still remains very uncertain.

Liverpool's recruitment plans seem to hinge heavily on whether or not the skipper puts pen to paper on a new deal, but the partnership formed between Jeremy Jacquet and Van Dijk is something that the Reds won't want to lose.

It isn't yet clear where Van Dijk's preferences lie if a upcoming deal cannot be agreed, but as January edges closer it feels more and more likely that the defender could be playing his final season in Merseyside.
