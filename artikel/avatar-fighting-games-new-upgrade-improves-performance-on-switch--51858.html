📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Earlier this year in July, Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game made its debut. While there were some features missing at launch on Nintendo's platforms, the situation has improved over time.

This week, the development team has rolled out the "Patch 4.5 Revision", and it comes with UI and character changes and adjustments.

Industry observers note that alongside this, there are also some Switch 2 "exclusive" notes. This includes improved performance on the latest hybrid platform, and according to the team, there are also some fixes when docking and undocking.

Here's the full rundown via the Paramount Games website:.

UI: ● Added a 15-second timer to the Ranked Rematch screen. Timer running out results in a forfeit ● Added an option to offer a mutual draw during a Ranked Match ● ART: Changed the image for Asami in the Character Vote menu ● BUGFIX: Matchmaking notification typo corrected ● BUGFIX: Fixed long usernames overlapping on the Rematch Screen KATARA: ● Newly introduced Novice 4 Combo Trial created AVATAR AANG: ● Intermediate 4 Combo Trial reassigned to Intermediate 3 Combo Trial ● New Intermediate 4 Combo Trial created ● New Master 1 Combo Trial created KORRA: ● BUGFIX: Fixed inconsistency on locked Flow regeneration on Avatar's Assault NIGHTMARE KORRA: ● BUGFIX: Fixed bug where inputting Phantom Fall with Down-Back or Down-Forward inputs would not activate Swift Terror Critical Hit ● BUGFIX: Fixed hitstun on Shattered Wrath (236ABC) when only one hit connects, made pull-in stronger on grounded hits as well to reduce situations where a stray hit leaves the opponent outside of Super ● BUGFIX: Fixed a bug where Air Throw would clash with normals ● BUGFIX: Fixed alignment of Round Win animation ● BUGFIX: Fixed dust colors when picking Seven Havens tie-in color ZAHEER: ● BUGFIX: Fixed a bug where Zaheer was vulnerable immediately after an Air Tech ZUKO: ● BUGFIX: 236EX (Mai Support): Final projectile now properly gives Redemption on block ● BUGFIX: 214EX (Ran & Shaw Support): Now properly gains +0.5 Redemption per hit ● BUGFIX: Minor Command List enhancement OZAI: ● BUGFIX: Combo Trial fixes; Auto Combo is now properly listed as "A > A > A > A > A" instead of "A > A > A > A > C" ● BUGFIX: Trials requiring Boosts have been accurately readjusted SWITCH 2 EXCLUSIVE NOTES ● Overall improved performance ● BUGFIX: Fixed challenges relating to docking and undocking the console.
