A recent auction of items related to Sasha & Digweed‘s seminal 1996 mix album Northern Exposure included one lot that fetched far more than the rest.

In a recent development, entering the auction directly from Digweed’s personal archives, a poster advertising the duo’s 1994 debut mix album Renaissance: The Mix Collection received a winning bid of $21,500. See an image of this poster below.

“The reason is that most of these posters were stuck on billboard sites or in the tube station so they would not have been recoverable even when taken down,” Digweed tells Billboard on what made the poster so valuable. “I had kept a poster from that release and had forgot about it. When I was in my record room searching out the Northern Exposure vinyl, I remembered this poster was on the top shelf. It was in perfect condition and a real collectors piece from such a classic mix album release. In a world of scarcity this poster is very rare indeed. I can see why a massive fan would want it but was blown away by how much.”.

Hosted by online auction platform Amplifyd, which has previously hosted auctions for items from artists including Danny Tenaglia, the Northern Exposure sale opened on Sept. 29 and closed Oct. 21. Featuring items from the producers’ personal archives, the auction included the actual records used to mix Northern Exposure, Northern Exposure 2, Northern Exposure: Expeditions and Communicate, in addition to various posters, flyers and other memorabilia.

All of the auction’s 113 lots brought winning bids, with the $21,500 poster being not only the highest-selling item of this auction, but the highest-selling item in Amplifyd history.

Sasha & Digweed have upcoming shows at The Roundhouse in London on Dec. 5 and EDC Mexico in February.
