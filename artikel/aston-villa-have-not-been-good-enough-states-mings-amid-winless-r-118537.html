Tyrone Mings conceded Aston Villa have not been good enough in recent weeks after their winless run stretched to six games on Tuesday.

Villa fell to a 1-0 defeat against Galatasaray in the Champions League, with their last win in any competition coming on October 24 against Manchester City in the Premier League.

While the Villans have lost three of their last six games in all competitions, they have drawn each of the previous Premier League games ahead of facing Ipswich Town this weekend.

According to recent reports, villa are unbeaten in their last seven league games (W3 D4), with each of their last three ending level. They last drew four consecutively in August/September 2011.

However, after their European defeat in Turkiye, Mings admitted it is on the players to turn their form around.

Recent findings suggest that "Everything is a collective responsibility. It's a real team effort," stated Mings.

"We're just trying to find, at the moment, our way, and trying to learn from the previous games, trying to do things that have given us success until now.

“At the moment, we're finding it in patches, but we’re not finding it consistently enough.

"We showed what we can do in patches, and that's not good enough when you come here and play at the top level.

"So, very frustrating, but it's on us to turn things around and to look at what we can do.".

Ipswich are just two points above the relegation zone after losing 2-0 at Tottenham last Saturday.

After winning three of their first six Premier League games this season (L3), Ipswich are winless in their last five matches (D2 L3), and Gary O'Neil believes that is the reality of the competition.

Industry observers note that "That's the nature of the Premier League. It's tough," said the Ipswich boss.

"We knew there would be some tough days in here and we'll get ready to go again.

"If we had highlighted more quality [against Spurs], we could have got a goal, and it would have made a difference I think.".

Against Spurs, Julio Enciso led his side in touches (53), passes in the final third (13), successful dribbles (4/8) and duels won (10/21).

He also recorded the most shots for Ipswich (four), but they amounted to just 0.12 xG as they all came from outside the box.

Nicolas Jackson's six Premier League goals have been worth eight points to Villa this season, the most of any player in the competition this term.

Only Alexander Isak (seven) and Erling Haaland (nine) have scored more Premier League goals than Jackson this season.

MATCH PREDICTION – ASTON VILLA WIN.

This will be Villa's 5,000th game in the top four tiers of English football (W2050 D1199 L1750), the 16th team to reach this milestone in English league history, and they boast a strong record in this fixture.

In a recent development, villa are unbeaten in their last 11 away league games against Ipswich (W5 D6), since a 3-0 loss in September 1984. In their league history, only at Coventry have they had a longer unbeaten run on the road (13 between 1938 and 1986).

The Villans are also unbeaten in all 22 of their Premier League games against promoted sides under Unai Emery (W15 D7).

Ipswich have won just one of their 12 Premier League meetings with Villa (D4 L7), a 1-0 away win in March 1994. Each of the last three between the sides has ended level.

Though only Hull City (114) have had fewer shots than Ipswich (120) in the Premier League this season, the Tractor Boys rank joint-eighth for goals scored (15). Only four teams have a higher shot conversion rate than O'Neil's side (12.5%).
