Recent findings suggest that the level of talent on Season 30 of NBC’s long-running singing competition The Voice, presently airing on the 2026 TV schedule, is undeniable. The cuts have been "brutal," and some really incredible singers already went home before the Live Indicates even started. The success of the show isn’t solely about the contestants. We also tune in to for the fun coach interactions, all of which has been noticeably absent during the live performances, disappointingly so.

It has been revealed that honestly, this is a problem that's typical with The Voice during live demonstrates, but I'd forgotten given the network dropped them for Season 29. As the competition narrows and the numbers of remaining singers gets smaller, I hope there will be an opportunity for this to return. Let's talk it out.

Earlier this season, Kelly Clarkson and newbie Riley Green really upped The Voice coach prank game. Whether it was a personalized gift from the American Idol winner attempting to help a fellow coach get over losing top-notch singers during the Blind Auditions, or it was the country music superstar using his duck call for a laugh, those moments have been glaringly lacking from the Live Highlights.

Industry observers note that honestly, the biggest disappointment of the Live Demonstrates doesn’t have anything to do with the performances themselves. There hasn’t been a bad performance. It instead comes from the fact that the episodes breeze by so swiftly that there hasn’t been time for coach banter or any of the silly moments that we’ve come to love.

According to recent reports, again, this season has been particularly fun with Clarkson’s zany antics, including trying to give a contestant her hair, and Green never missing the chance to poke fun at Adam Levine’s age. It also would have been the perfect opportunity for Adam to throw it in Riley’s face (or at least console him) that his team still has three singers left while the latter only has two.

Sources indicate that don’t get me wrong, the Live Demonstrates have seen all the contestants step up as they perform with musicians and back-up singers while vying for viewer votes. But the lack of coach banter is an obvious and unfortunate mistake that I hope the show fixes soon.

With the Blind Auditions, Battles, and Cut Week having two-hour episodes, cutting the Live Shows to one hour doesn’t even allow for much coach commentary and feedback. To me that's a substantial problem. When it comes time to announce which singers have made it through for another week and who’s going to be sent home, there’s barely the time to even say goodbye, right?
