Liverpool are looking to complete an incredibly crucial move, and are in advanced talks to get it done.

After a mass exodus of homegrown players, the importance of the Reds keeping hold of their club-trained young stars has intensified immeasurably.

Liverpool lost Trent Alexander-Arnold, Tyler Morton, Jarell Quansah, Ben Doak and Caoimhin Kelleher all in the same summer.

According to recent reports, these were players who had become key first-team members and had represented exciting potential. Most of them have gone onto do very well at their next clubs - with the exception of Alexander-Arnold - who must now be ruing whether thinking the grass was greener on the other side was the right call.

But the less stated about Trent the better. The focus for Liverpool is on making sure situations like that never happen again.
