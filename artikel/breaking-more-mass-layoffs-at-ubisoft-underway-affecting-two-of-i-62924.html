Breaking: More Mass Layoffs At Ubisoft Underway, Affecting Two Of Its Biggest Studios

🌐 Source: Gamespot • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (597 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Ubisoft has enacted another round of mass layoffs, affecting 166 additional jobs as the French gaming giant continues its cost-cutting program.
  • Ubisoft has enacted another round of mass layoffs, affecting 166 additional jobs as the French gaming giant continues its cost-cutting program.
  • The job cuts are happening at two of Ubisoft's biggest development hubs in Montreal and Quebec.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ubisoft has enacted another round of mass layoffs, affecting 166 additional jobs as the French gaming giant continues its cost-cutting program. The job cuts are happening at two of Ubisoft's biggest development hubs in Montreal and Quebec. A spokesperson for Ubisoft remarked the job cuts are coming to meet changing production needs, and IGN reported that the layoffs came from teams working on projects that have been "canceled or refocused." "We deeply value the contributions of these teams and recognise the impact this may have on our colleagues. We are committed to supporting them through this process with care and respect," the spokesperson said. "Those affected are being provided with severance, extended health care benefits, career transition services and other resources." Ubisoft Montreal is presently working on Assassin's Creed Codename Hexe, while Ubisoft Quebec most recently launched Assassin's Creed Shadows. No further updates are coming to that game, leaving the question of what the team is making next. In October, Ubisoft said there are no additional Assassin's Creed remakes currently in development, following the release of Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag Resynced earlier this year. “Our teams are already focused on the next chapter of Assassin’s Creed titles, including projects you haven’t heard about yet, featuring new settings and stories," the developer said. Ubisoft's restructuring has led to several canceled games, shut-down studios, and layoffs. Trials developer RedLynx is on the chopping block, while 72 people from Ubisoft Singapore–the studio behind Skull & Bones–were recently laid off. Just today, November 19, Ubisoft released its latest earnings report that mentioned that Ubisoft's third phase of its cost-reduction program remains "firmly on track." Ubisoft is targeting €1.25 billion ($1.37 billion) in cost reductions by March 2028, so it's possible there will be more pain in the firm's future.
