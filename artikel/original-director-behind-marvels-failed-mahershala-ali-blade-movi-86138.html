Bassam Tariq directed 2026’s critically-lauded action-thriller Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother starring Mahershala Ali. He was also supposed to direct Ali in Marvel’s period-piece Blade reboot, but getting that project off the ground ended up being a nightmare for everyone involved. Tariq said he’ll never put himself in that situation again.

“It was funny and strange to start casting and building without a proper script. You’re sitting there and explaining to actors what their role will be, and they just have to believe you,” he noted in a upcoming interview with New Arab (via IGN). “That’s also very dangerous because if the text comes and it doesn’t add up to what you were saying, your credibility gets completely shot. All I have is my integrity, and I’ve been thinking about that a lot.”.

To recap how the whole Blade mess went, Marvel first confirmed a new movie for its Black vampire hunter in 2019 with Ali as the star. Tariq, whose only credit at the time was 2020’s Mogul Mowgli, wasn’t signed on to direct until 2021. But there was still no script, and apparently nothing nailed down about what the final movie would look like.
