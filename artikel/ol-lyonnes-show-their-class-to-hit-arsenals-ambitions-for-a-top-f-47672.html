Arsenal’s hopes of earning automatic progression to the Champions League quarter-finals were dashed by OL Lyonnes, as the French side continued their statement form in Europe.

Goals in either half from Jule Brand and Melchie Dumornay were the ­difference at Meadow Park and Lyonnes are joint-top of the table and still yet to concede in the league phase with one game remaining.

It has been revealed that it feels like Lyonnes are playing with a fire and intensity fuelled by their loss to Barcelona in May’s Champions League final. They look a more formidable, coherent and hungry side on a mission to end a four-year run without lifting the trophy they won eight times in a 12-year period between 2011 and 2022.

“We are already at a better level for sure,” remarked the Lyonnes manager ­Jonatan Giráldez. “Sometimes you just need time. We had nine fresh ­players last season and I was new too as the coach. I think we have improved the rhythm of the game and have identified positions better and are staying more compact. The most important thing is speaking the same language in possession and out possession.”.

Sources indicate that the visit of the eight-time ­European champions was always going to be challenging for the ­Gunners and based on current form getting a result would be even tougher. Last season’s runners-up had scored 20 goals without conceding in their first four Champions League matches prior to their trip to Borehamwood and scored 49 in their nine Première Ligue games.

Recent findings suggest that the lack of competitiveness in the France top flight also meant they were able to rotate heavily at the weekend between their two European games. Giráldez made 11 changes for their 1-0 defeat of Saint-Malo on Saturday before returning to his first-choice lineup against the 2025 Champions League winners.

Clàudia Pina scored a hat-trick as Barcelona thrashed Servette 6-0 to go top of the Women’s Champions League standings. While Lyon were winning 2-0 at Arsenal, Barça edged clear at the top on goal difference as they dismantled their Swiss visitors.

Aitana Bonmatí fired in the opener in the 19th minute and then turned provider for Pina to drill home her first goal.

Esmee Brugts headed in a third in the 32nd minute, and Barça had their fourth just after the hour, with Caroline Graham Hansen hitting an emphatic penalty after Bénédicte Simon fouled Brugts.

Pina got her second in the 77th minute and tucked in from Brugts’s pull-back to complete her hat-trick late on.

Bayern Munich are two points behind after cruising to a 4-0 win over Austria Vienna. Klara Buhl headed the visitors in front after 27 minutes before Pernille Harder doubled the lead in first-half stoppage time.

Alara Sehitler’s stunning strike made it 3-0 with 15 minutes to go before Sophie Proost added a fourth in stoppage time.

Sofia Jahnukainen struck in stoppage time as Roma snatched a 3-2 win away to Benfica. Kayleigh van Dooren twice put the Italians in front, but her first goal was cancelled out by a thunderbolt from Caroline Møller, with Flourish Sabastine equalising after her second.

Sources indicate that paris FC got their first win of the competition, beating HB Køge 2-1 thanks to Célina Ould Hocine’s late penalty. Catherine Paulson fired the visitors in front 10 minutes in, but Sheika Scott bundled in a rebound to bring Paris level before the break. Press Association.

Industry observers note that arsenal were not afforded the ­luxury of an easy league match after their battling 1-1 draw against ­Barcelona last week, playing a critical and tricky game against London City Lionesses, which also ended 1-1. Renée Slegers made two changes to the team that hosted ambitious London City, with Ona Batlle ­having picked up a knock Taylor Hinds played at left-back and Kyra Cooney-Cross started ahead of Kim Little.

In a recent development, they had their backs against the wall at a bitterly cold Meadow Park, as Giráldez’s galacticos controlled the game. The away team had almost 69% possession in the first half and amassed 12 shots to Arsenal’s one.

The surprise was that Lyonnes were not further ahead at the break, with Brand’s 24th-minute goal the difference, the full-back sweeping in Vicki Bècho’s ball from the right.

Brand was back to haunt the ­Gunners, having scored in both legs of last season’s semi-final between the two teams, with the French side progressing to face Barcelona in the final with a 4-3 aggregate win.

Arsenal were better after the break, but Lyonnes soaked up brief spells of Arsenal pressure, Marie-Antoinette Katoto the only player left forward as they banked back and crowded the box to give the home players few options.

They increased their margin and dampened the mood soon after Arsenal’s brightest period, Tabitha Chawinga’s cross from the left was collected by Katoto and the France forward laid the ball off for Dumornay who took a touch before powering the ball into the roof of the net.

It was an emphatic and unstoppable finish from one of the best players in the world as a mist lowered over the pitch.

According to recent reports, “Congratulations to Lyonnes, they were better than us today,” said Slegers. “If you look at the block we’ve had, the challenges we’ve had with the run of games – ­Liverpool and Barcelona away, ­London City who are among one of the top teams in the WSL right now and Lyon, who are even better than last year – it’s a lot of games against strong teams with a short turnaround because Lyon were better than us.”.

In a recent development, arsenal need to promptly move on, with West Ham their last game before the international break. They had needed a win to have any serious chance of earning a place in top four, avoiding a quarter-final playoff place.

They now sit eighth and will face Inter in their final league phase match in December, with the Italians needing a win to keep any hopes of a playoff place alive. That will not be easy, but a seeded playoff place is in Arsenal’s hands, if they want it.
