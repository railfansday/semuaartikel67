Juventus start Francisco Conceicao, Jeremie Boga and Jeff Ekhator in a newly introduced 3-4-2-1 formation with Neto in goal as they host Omonia Nicosia in the Europa League.

It kicks off at the Allianz Juventus Stadium in Turin at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game, Olympiacos-Milan and Borac-Atalanta on the Liveblog.

The Bianconeri have done well in the Europa League so far this season, winning every fixture except their 2-2 draw with Stade Rennais.

This is a must-win match for their hopes of going through directly to the Round of 16 as one of the top eight teams, but Luciano Spalletti must also rotate the squad.

He is still missing Pierre Kalulu, Nico Gonzalez, Kerim Alajbegovic, Khephren Thuram, Manuel Locatelli, Arek Milik and Kamil Grabara, while Edon Zhegrova and Daniele Rugani are not on the UEFA list.

The good news is Juve have Francisco Conceicao back in the starting XI for his first appearance since the shoulder injury on October 18.

He is part of a 3-4-2-1 formation with Ekhator as centre-forward and Boga in the trident.

Neto also gets his first start in goal for Juventus, albeit in his second spell at the club, having last featured in this jersey in May 2017.

Omonia Nicosia sit fourth in their domestic league, and had originally been in the Champions League preliminary rounds, dropping into the Europa League.

They started this season strong, beating Celta Vigo and Celje, but then lost 4-1 at home to Benfica and 3-1 away to NEC Nijmegen.

They have Fotis Kitsos and Mihlali Mayambela injured, while Carel Eiting, Moses Odubajo, David Akintola and Giorgos Viktoros are cup-tied.

Lois Diony is by a long way their top scorer this season with 11 goals in 20 competitive appearances, but only one in the Europa League.

Juventus: Neto; Gatti, Lucumi, Kelly; Cabal, Douglas Luiz, Sarr, Cambiaso; Conceicao, Boga; Ekhator.

Omonia Nicosia: Kaminski; Duverne, Coulibaly, Satka, Nego; Andreou, Maric; Tanase, Tankovic, Montnor; Kakoullis.
