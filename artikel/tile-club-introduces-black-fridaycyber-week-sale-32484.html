Industry observers note that planning any home improvement projects in 2027? If you’re a homeowner gearing up for a kitchen renovation or a contractor with a slate of latest builds on the horizon, Tile Club’s promotional offer for Black Friday and Cyber Week may be your excuse to stock up.

From November 23 through December 1, retail and trade customers can take advantage of discounts when ordering any of Tile Club’s products. Check out the offer details below, along with a few recent arrivals that might just inspire you to plan an upgrade or two in the new year.

The new Brio Dot and Dash 3D Ceramic Subway Tiles are designed to provide a fresh visual and tactile experience to showers, backsplashes, or any indoor wall in need of a little personality. Select from an inverted scoop (Dot) or raised capsule (Dash) design in a variety of colorways.

The LEED-friendly Rowena collection features designs inspired by classical impasto paintings, which were created by applying thick layers of paint with a brush or palette knife. The raised design of the Rowena Impasto Mural Ceramic Tile adds subtle dimension and soft contrast, with a large-scale pattern that’s perfect for a focal moment that won’t overwhelm a space designed for relaxation.

Have a space that’s craving texture? The Baldwin Textured Ceramic Kit Kat Tiles have plenty. Glossy angled surfaces catch light and reflect back layers of visual interest, making the collection an attractive choice for bathroom walls or backsplashes.
