📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, things continue to get messy in the community of players working on getting Grand Theft Auto 6 working on PC. The previous frontrunner, Project Leonida, got their Discord server and GitHub repository taken down by Rockstar, but others have seemingly stepped in to finish the job.

Industry observers note that the most up-to-date build of GTA 6 was allegedly leaked earlier this week by notorious leaker CyberLeek. This is a supposedly near-final build of the game's Xbox version, and it's seemingly being used as the basis for the recent work to port the game to PC.

Unsurprisingly, most of these communities of modders rely almost exclusively on generative-AI tools like Claude and Antigravity to decompile and recompile the console version of GTA 6. That has always been the case, but the CyberLeek build only invited even more to ask AI chatbots to make the PC port for them.

X user chasm has been following developments in the GTA 6 decompile and recompile communities, and recently shared some of the prompts the so-called vibe-coders are using - a few of whom livestreamed the process.

"Make no mistakes," one modder prompted the AI chatbot. "Keep finding more stuff and working and pushing," another eloquently asked. "Work faster," asked another. "There has to be a faster way to iterate than this," they continued.

As for how far some of the remaining communities have managed to get, this video allegedly reveals the game running on PC. This goes beyond the boot-up screen, which modders were able to reach within hours of CyberLeek's build alleged leaking.

It has been revealed that the footage runs at sub-5fps, but the visuals look to have rendered normally. Assuming Rockstar doesn't also shut down the modders who remain active, we may see another progress enhancement.

If you're already playing Rockstar's latest on consoles, the Eurogamer GTA 6 walkthrough offers comprehensive guides on relationship mechanics, how the latest Criminal Profile system works, a list of all safe codes and more.
