Il Corriere dello Sport reports that Roma are closely monitoring Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens and Nottingham Forest’s Callum Hudson-Odoi, hoping that a new attacking signing from the Premier League will have the same impact as Donyell Malen.

Roma are still looking for attacking reinforcement, specifically, a left winger.

According to Corriere dello Sport, two of the primary names for the Giallorossi are Chelsea’s Gittens and Forest’s Hudson-Odoi.

Both English wingers seem to be in a similar situation: unhappy with the lack of playing time at their clubs and looking for a fresh start in the January window.

This is not the first time Gittens has been linked with a move to Roma, as the Giallorossi already held talks with Chelsea last summer.

The 22-year-old has played just 55 minutes across all competitions and is recovering from an ankle injury that has kept him sidelined for over a month.

According to the report, Roma and Chelsea have stayed in contact in recent weeks, and talks could intensify soon if Roma receive guarantees about the player’s fitness.

Hudson-Odoi has only played 45 minutes in the Premier League this season, making just four league appearances. According to the report, the 26-year-old is open to a move to the Olimpico in the January window.

Barely one year ago, in January 2026, Roma signed Malen from Aston Villa on an initial loan deal with an option to buy.

Back then, the Dutchman was looking to revive his career after an inconsistent spell at Aston Villa, and his 27 goals in 34 Roma appearances prove he succeeded.

Roma hope to achieve the same with a upcoming attacking signing from the Premier League during the 2027 January transfer window.
