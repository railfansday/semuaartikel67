📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Habby is a developer and publisher that has released some neat little mobile games over the years. You may recall Archero One and Two, as well as Dicero and Capybara Go! Now, Habby are once more tackling the roguelike genre, and fusing it with tower defence as pre-registration opens for Monster Must Die!

Monster Must Die, as mentioned above, mashes up two genres with tower defence and roguelike. You start each battle with a summoning tower, then gradually introduce up to four heroes to fight off near-endless waves of monsters.

The world could always use more heroes Of course, as you might expect, Monster Must Die takes some cues from Vampire Survivors, especially in having the myriad of strategies and skills that you can combo together to take on the forces of evil! That means plenty of opportunities to tinker with builds.

According to recent reports, and Habby are also eager to point out you have effectively unlimited stamina, meaning that you won't need to worry about waiting on timers to get back into the fight. You'll certainly need that stamina to take on the hordes and power up by visiting the Dungeon.

Recent findings suggest that overall, Monster Must Die looks like a lot of Habby's other releases: a perfectly competent take on a tried-and-true genre. About the only thing I can say is that it doesn't do much to stand out otherwise. Hopefully, when it does release, we'll see it making more of an impression, but for now it's just worth keeping an eye on.
