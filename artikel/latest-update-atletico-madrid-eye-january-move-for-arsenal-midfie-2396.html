Latest Update: Atletico Madrid eye January move for Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi

Atletico Madrid are looking to make an ambitious January swoop for Martin Zubimendi after the Arsenal midfielder failed to convince Mikel Arteta to give him a regular starting spot.

The Spain international has started all seven of his Premier League appearances this season, coming off the bench. This has reportedly caught the eye of Diego Simeone, who wants to bolster his midfield in the winter window.

Atletico have marked Zubimendi as a possible target, according to Spanish radio station RNE via SPORT. Sporting director Mateu Alemany feels that there is still more to do with Simeone’s squad despite big investments in newly introduced players this summer.

If Zubimendi’s situation fails to improve at Arsenal, he could become one of the most wanted signings of January.

Industry observers note that zubimendi played a major role in Arsenal’s Premier League title-winning campaign last season. He made 34 league starts, scoring five goals and providing one assist.

Industry observers note that he played a key role in the club’s run to the Champions League final. Since then Arsenal’s midfield has changed.

Injuries have hampered his campaign, but Myles Lewis-Skelly has returned to the midfield, and Bruno Guimaraes joined in the summer.

It has been revealed that the 27-year-old has also expressed frustration at the lack of playing opportunities available to him. He was signed for £60 million from Real Sociedad to Arsenal just 18 months ago and has three-and-a-half years left on his contract.

It has been revealed that any move to Atletico would be a battle. Real Madrid are also reportedly fans of the midfielder, and he has also been linked with a move to Chelsea in recent months.

Atletico are keen to bring Zubimendi in alongside Pablo Barrios in central midfield.

It would be a dream combination for Simeone, and the Spanish club are willing to fund any move.

Industry observers note that another option could see him involved in a deal for Julian Alvarez. There are currently no talks about a potential swap deal.

Arteta has previously played down concerns about Zubimendi’s importance to Arsenal. Earlier this month, he highlighted the midfielder’s commitment and suggested injuries had affected his opportunities.

The Arsenal manager also made clear that Zubimendi would have chances to play, but would need to take them when they arrived.

Industry observers note that that leaves the midfielder with an opportunity to work his way back into Arteta’s plans. However, Atletico’s reported interest could add another dimension to his situation as January approaches.
