After the announcement yesterday that the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed due to weather, the event’s co-host Hoda Kotb stopped by Late Night to express her relief that it has been rescheduled for Saturday.

Recent findings suggest that “The people who have been preparing this 100-year parade have been working tirelessly for a year-plus,” Kotb told host Seth Meyers. “The musical acts are incredible. So imagine spending all that time painting every little bit of every float, wondering if the float is going to fly away somewhere. So the good news is, to me, I’m relieved. Saturday is going to be amazing. Everybody’s still hanging out. We’re going to watch it anyway.”.

It has been revealed that she added, “It’s going to be literally out of this world. It’s amazing.”.

Weather experts have been warning U.S. residents about a rapidly approaching storm system making its way up the east coast, bringing with it high winds, heavy rains, and flood warnings. According to CNN, winds in New York City are expected to reach around 25 to 35 mph today — which would be dangerous for the parade’s famous floats and giant balloons.

“The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration,” Macy’s noted in a statement. “Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. This is the first time the Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade has been postponed because of inclimate weather. It was cancelled in 1942, 1943, and 1944 because the U.S. needed all excess helium and rubber for the World War II effort.

Sources indicate that kotb is set to co-host the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade alongside Savannah Guthrie and Al Roker. Grammy-nominated singer Raye is expected to open the parade with a holiday performance featuring her sisters, Amma and Absolutely; followed by floats and performances by Wyclef Jean, Smash Mouth, Natasha Bedingfield, Icona Pop, JoJo, Hunger Games star McKenna Grace, and Queen Latifah. No additional changes to the parade programming or lineup have been announced.
