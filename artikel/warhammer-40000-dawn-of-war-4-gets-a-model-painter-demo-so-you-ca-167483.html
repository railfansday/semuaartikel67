Sources indicate that in the grim darkness of the week before the release of Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War 4, developers King Art and publishers Deep Silver have released a demo model painter. It's your chance to pre-emptively run a brush over units from the latest strategy game's four major factions – Orks, Adeptus Mechanicus, Necrons and Space Marines. The Imperial Guard are in the game too, but mostly as a tutorial army. What a slap in the face for the Imperium's vanilla humans.

Recent findings suggest that deep Silver are calling it "the most powerful Painter in Dawn of War history", which doesn't seem all that dashing a boast given that Dawn of War 3 came out almost 10 years ago and today's PCs have a fair bit more RAM in them. You can opt for Basic Painter mode in order to promptly knock together livery for entire armies, or fire up the Advanced Painter and get stuck into the weeds of individual armour and body parts, with a choice of materials, patterns, decals and weathering. Here be trailer.

Gosh, don't your units look splendid, after literal moments of tinkering! What a colossal waste it would be to buy the full version of DOW4, import your custom designs, and watch them get all scratched and blown to pieces! Although that is obviously the idea - this is bog-standard preorder bait. You can also send your designs to other players using codes, which is nice. I plan to fashion my very own line of Space Marine chapters with colour schemes that can be deduced from the title alone: the dreaded Magpie Men, the valiant Traffic Lightbearers, and the Emperor's own Paisley Pillagers.

Sources indicate that there is surely no greater insult for a table-top Warhammer player than having your opponent show up to the battle with an unpainted army. Somebody did this to me once, which is partly why I don't play tabletop Warhammer anymore (the main reason, of course, is that tabletop Warhammer is mind-bogglingly expensive). I'd spent hours on my own High Elves, getting the blue of the pennants and the gilt of the heraldry just right, and here's some guy with an army of grey plastic and pewter. Might as well have moulded his troops out of chewing gum and saved himself £50!

Well, there'll be no excuse for showing up with a blandly dressed army in Dawn of War 4. Find the model painter on Steam. As for the actual warmaking component of DOW4, Julian worries the devs are playing it too safe.
