📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, spider-Man has teamed with a lot of superheroes over the decades, so much so that this is literally the premise of the multiple Marvel Team-Up volumes. Yet, for a long time, Spidey shied away from officially joining a superhero team. That changed in the 2000s when Peter Parker joined the New Avengers, and since then he’s rotated in and out of the Earth’s Mightiest Heroes roster. Now he’s not only back on the team, but leading it.

Industry observers note that following the events of Avengers: Armageddon, a new incarnation of the Avengers has formed, though it's one that’s keeping a lower profile due to the public’s distrust towards superheroes. This time, Spidey is calling the shots, but his tenure as leader has already gotten off to a horrible start. That’s evident by how he’s facing resistance from the other Avengers in the next issue of The Amazing Spider-Man.

According to recent reports, at the end of The Amazing Spider-Man #1003, J. Jonah Jameson was kidnapped by Itsy Bitsy, a warped clone made up of Spider-Man and Deadpool’s genetic material. Despite Jameson having been one of the Web-Slinger’s closest allies ever since learning he was Peter Parker during Chip Zdarsky’s Spectacular Spider-Man run, the media figure has returned to badmouthing Spider-Man on air to boost ratings. Itsy Bitsy didn’t take kindly to this and kidnapped him, and in the preview for The Amazing Spider-Man #1004, she ignores Jameson’s pleas to free him.

In a recent development, spider-Man highlights Luke Cage, Daredevil, Wolverine, Captain Marvel, and Miracleman the livestream of Itsy Bitsy torturing Jameson and allowing viewers to bet on what will come of this gruesomeness. Unfortunately for Spidey, his fellow Avengers scoff at his call to band together and rescue Jameson, feeling it’s not worth their time. They’re busy with other things, including Miracleman breaking up a HYDRA cell, Luke dealing with Subterraneans in the Upper West Side, and Captain Marvel clashing with the Serpent Society.

Industry observers note that though he apologizes to everyone for calling them away from their other concerns (except for Daredevil, whom it’s implied was simply meeting up for one of his romantic rendezvouses with Elektra), Spider-Man reminds them he’s the Avengers’ leader, so this mission to rescue Jameson should take priority. Judging from the solicitation for The Amazing Spider-Man #1005, it seems like all but Wolverine will nonetheless leave him in the lurch. That doesn’t bode well for Spidey being taken seriously as a leader.

It has been revealed that even though this is Spider-Man’s first time leading the Avengers, he has experience being in charge of a group of people. Most notably, he’s been the anchoring hero of Marvel’s Spider-Verse events, led different incarnations of the Fantastic Four when the main quartet has been absent, and he even assembled The Outlaws, comprised of Sandman and Rocket Racer, at a time when he felt he couldn’t trust the Avengers. In his civilian life, Peter also took over running Parker Industries after Doctor Octopus’ consciousness left his body.

Sources indicate that what makes Spider-Man’s tenure as the Avengers’ leader challenging is that, as mentioned earlier, the world is leery towards superheroes after the previous Avengers’ fight with Red Hulk led to thousands dying in Europe. The United Nations passed a resolution banning superpowers, and while the United States abstained from signing this, that’s only because roughly 80% of enhanced individuals live within its borders. Even then, the UN-funded S.H.I.E.L.D. is restricting the Avengers and other superheroes from operating outside the States.

As a result, the Avengers have to be cautious about what missions they take on, and the majority are in agreement that rescuing Jameson from Itsy Bitsy is, as Florence Pugh’s Yelena Belova put it in Spider-Man: Brand New Day, “small potatoes.” If Luke Cage, Daredevil, Wolverine, Captain Marvel, and Miracleman are already pushing back against Spidey’s orders this early, it’s possible this is indicative of how he’ll be treated in the main Avengers title. If Spider-Man can’t be properly respected as the team’s leader, then this version of the Avengers won’t be able to function properly.

The Amazing Spider-Man #1004 will be released Wednesday, November 25 by Marvel Comics.
