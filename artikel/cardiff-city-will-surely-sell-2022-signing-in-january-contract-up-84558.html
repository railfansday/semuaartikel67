Industry observers note that cardiff City have left the attacker out of their last five squads, despite him being fit and available to play. They are positioned in 14th place in the Championship table after their first 14 fixtures of the season.

Robinson, 31, has made seven appearances for the Bluebirds in all competitions in this campaign, six of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with two goals. However, he hasn’t played for the Welsh side since their home match against Watford in mid-October.

The Republic of Ireland international, who has 38 caps to his name, is now facing an uncertain long-term future at the Cardiff City Stadium ahead of the January transfer window and his current club will surely look to sell him this winter. He is out of contract at the end of this term and is due to become a free agent in June 2027.

Cardiff City beat Burnley 5-4 in a crazy game last time out before the international break. They will be in confident mood now as they gear up for their upcoming away trip to Norwich City this weekend.

Cardiff City risk losing Robinson for nothing when this season ends and he is unlikely to be offered an extension amid his lack of minutes. Therefore, most signs point towards a sale in the near future to ensure that they get a bit of cash for him.

The Northampton-born man has become surplus to requirements with the Bluebirds. He has found it hard to get into Brian Barry-Murphy’s intends ahead of Jack Moylan and Yousef Salach.

Robinson, who rose up through Aston Villa’s academy in his early career, has been with Cardiff City since joining them in 2022. He has since been a good servant and has played 137 games.

In a recent development, prior to his switch to South Wales, he had a spell at West Brom. He has also featured for the likes of Preston North End and Sheffield United in the past and is experienced in the EFL.

Robinson would benefit from a change of scene away from Cardiff City. Meanwhile, the Bluebirds would free up space and funds to pave the way for other reinforcements.

He played a key role behind their promotion from League One and found the net on eight occasions. However, he is out of favour now though and a departure seems inevitable.
