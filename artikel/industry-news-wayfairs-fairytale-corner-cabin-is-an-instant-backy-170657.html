Avid shoppers know Black Friday is packed with unbelievably good deals on mattresses, vacuums, and furniture, but if you want to make the most of today’s shopping extravaganza, don’t forget to pick up a shed. There are some great bargains on backyard builds, and Wayfair’s Outdoor Living Today’s Western Red Cedar Penthouse Corner Garden Shed is at the very top of our shopping list.

It has been revealed that sheds might usually be placed somewhere in your backyard, but that doesn’t mean they should skimp on style. Decked with a pair of French doors, two flower boxes, and a shingled roof, this red cedar setup delivers some storybook charm.

Of course, there’s more to this pick than good looks. The red cedar used on the shed—which one reviewer says “smells so good”—is naturally rot-, decay-, and insect-resistant, so it’ll hold up nicely throughout the ever-changing weather. Meanwhile, two screened, lockable aluminum windows will offer some extra light and ventilation, so you can use it as a home office, poolhouse, or kid’s zone. With 467 cubic feet at your disposal, there’s plenty of room to outfit the space with workout equipment, a makeshift bar, or patio furniture in the off-season.

In a recent development, and don’t be fooled: Outdoor Living Today’s shed might look round, but it’s actually a five-sided structure with an angular point in the back. Thanks to its clever design, you can stick this “corner cabin” in the very back of your yard for a stylish, space-efficient setup.

In a recent development, well, space- and time-efficient, that is. The brand delivers this shed as pre-clad, panelized cedar wall sections with all hardware included to help simplify installation. While one reviewer claims it takes some time to assemble, the shed “looks beautiful when finish[ed].” “Great material, very classy, very practical,” stated another. A third happy customer called the corner cabin “well-constructed.”.
