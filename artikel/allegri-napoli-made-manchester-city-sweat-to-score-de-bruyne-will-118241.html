Max Allegri admits Napoli did something ‘really well for the first time’ in the 2-2 draw with Manchester City to keep Champions League ambition alive, and assures Kevin De Bruyne ‘will remain until June.’.

The Partenopei came to the Etihad fearing the worst amid an injury crisis that left them without 11 players between fitness, suspension and the Champions League list.

Instead, they leave here with regrets after twice taking the lead through Rasmus Hojlund and Noa Lang, only to be clawed back by Ayyoub Bouaddi and the Rayan Cherki angled drive.

A 2-2 draw is still a positive result, especially away to Enzo Maresca’s side, and keeps them in the hunt for a play-off spot.

“This match was important, because if we win at least one of the last three, we should be in a good position for qualification,” Allegri told Sky Sport Italia.

In a recent development, “It was a very difficult game, the lads did well, and obviously there were moments when we had to suffer under pressure, but for the first time we really did well out of possession to slow Manchester City down. They did really well in that.

In a recent development, “It was a deserved draw, ultimately we leave here with a bitter taste in our mouths, but I told the lads that the belief, the courage is necessary in Europe, as there are spaces to play in. After this, we must prepare in the best way for Sassuolo, tonight was a good training session above all for our defence.”.

Considering the circumstances, it is remarkable to see Napoli leaving Manchester City feeling regret, as they were decimated with barely any options to come off the bench.

“We should be happy with the point, which is the most vital thing, but also the performance. There were moments when we should’ve made more of their high line, and had a couple of good situations, but we were against a very strong opponent. The lads applied themselves brilliantly.”.

Allegri was asked if this was his most satisfying evening yet on the Napoli bench, especially as there haven’t been many high points yet in his tenure since taking over from Antonio Conte.

Recent findings suggest that “I am above all happy for the lads. When you play in the Champions League, you need that extra belief, to even be arrogant, and trust your abilities.”.

This was an emotional return to Manchester for Kevin De Bruyne, who was given a hero’s welcome by his former fans, and a standing ovation when substituted late on.

“Unfortunately, age waits for no man, but he is an extraordinary player. It made me sad to take him off today, as he rarely makes a mistake with the ball at his feet. Players like De Bruyne and Luka Modric, for those who love football and admire the sport, they represent something special.”.

De Bruyne will be out of contract in June 2027, but there are multiple reports he could leave for PSV Eindhoven in January.

“We’ll discuss it with the club, but he is a very professional lad, and I think he will remain until June,” assured Allegri.

Napoli have been leaking goals all season, and Allegri took responsibility for their struggles.

“I think in the first part of the season, above all for my responsibility, we didn’t do enough to prepare defensively. We conceded too many really silly goals, for example against Bodo/Glimt when conceding for 4-3 straight after going 4-2 up.

It has been revealed that “That is a bad sign, it is a sign of a lack of respect and humility from the team in certain moments of the match. We made Manchester City sweat to score goals today, they had to do something extraordinary, and that is how it should be, with this determination in the tackle and total concentration in every game,” concluded the coach.
