If you live in a multi-story house or a larger layout, it is worth asking a simple question: If a smoke or carbon monoxide alarm goes off in the basement or garage, would everyone upstairs hear it? Upgrading your whole setup at once usually gets pricey fast, which makes this deal worth a look for Black Friday. Through Nov. 30, Amazon is taking 20% off a six-pack of the X-Sense SC07-W Wireless Interconnected Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Detector, dropping it from $269.99 down to $215.99.

Industry observers note that each unit handles two jobs at once, combining a photoelectric smoke sensor (which responds faster to slow, smoldering fires while reducing annoying cooking false alarms) with an electrochemical sensor to detect dangerous carbon monoxide. Better yet, all six alarms link together wirelessly across an open-air range of up to 820 feet — and you can connect up to 24 compatible X-Sense units total — with zero hardwiring required.

When one alarm detects smoke or carbon monoxide, every linked unit in the house sounds at the same time so nobody misses the warning, even if they are asleep on another floor.

Each detector runs on a sealed 10-year lithium battery and automatically checks itself every minute, so you will not have to swap batteries or deal with middle-of-the-night low-battery chirps for at least a decade.

A large backlit LCD screen on the front clearly displays the alarm type, battery status, and real-time carbon monoxide levels in parts per million (ppm), so you can see what is going on at a glance instead of guessing. Just keep in mind that while the alarms communicate wirelessly, the SC07-W does not connect to WiFi or send smartphone alerts.
