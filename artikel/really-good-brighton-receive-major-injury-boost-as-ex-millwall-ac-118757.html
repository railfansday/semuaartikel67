Femi Azeez is edging closer to his long-awaited Brighton debut after being spotted training with the Seagulls on Tuesday, ahead of Thursday’s Uefa Conference League meeting with Craiova at the Amex Stadium.

The 25-year-old winger is yet to appear for Albion since joining from Millwall last September on a four-year contract for around £15m.

Azeez arrived with a slight hamstring concern and also took a little time to adapt to Brighton’s demanding training programme.

In a recent development, head coach Fabian Hurzeler previously stressed the need for patience, but offered an encouraging update last week. “He’s been training now for two, two-and-a-half weeks with the team,” he noted. “He’s getting closer to his full potential. Now it’s about us not forcing things.”.

Sources indicate that hurzeler praised Azeez’s pace and finishing, adding: “He is a really good finisher. He has really good speed.”.

In a recent development, the Nigerian international was outstanding for Millwall last term with11 goals and eight assists, earning a place in the Championships Team of the Year. He can operate on either wing, giving Brighton another attacking option.

Brighton could also welcome back defender Pascal Struijk. The £18m summer arrival from Leeds has not featured since sustaining a thigh injury in the 2-0 win at Sunderland.

Hurzeler, speaking ahead of the 2-0 Premier League loss at Hull, stated: “Femi Azeez and Pascal Struijk are back in training.

“Now we have to make a decision if they're an option for the weekend already or if we give them a little bit more time.".

Brighton’s pre-Craiova press conference will be today at 2pm - check the Sussex World website for the latest team news.
