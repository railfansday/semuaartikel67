Bitcoin price slides to $55,300 early Saturday, marking its lowest since September 2024. Also, registered is yearly low surpassing the July low of $60000. At the moment trading at $57,922, down 6.13% in 24 hours.

Industry observers note that added the U.S. Spot marker on November 19, Bitcoin ETF lost $582 million, totaling $3.47 billion in November, with BlockRicks record single-day exit of $541.1 million.

On the other hand, the stablecoin market has experienced a $16B drop since the middle of October.

In a recent development, the highlight of the week is Anthropic’s massive IPO that pushed Bitcoin upward in safe zone, but it couldn’t hold for long. With shrinking stablecoin supply, heavy ETF outflows, and solid liquidations in the derivatives market. Where is the market heading to? whats the next Support for Bitcoin?

It has been revealed that anthropic (ANTUSD) made its listing with an IPO on NASDAQ on November 19, resulting in it raising $81.3B, the largest IPO in history. ANT stock opened at $83, rose to $111.50, and closed the first day’s session at $97.17, marking nearly 17% gains.

The market sentiment suggests that investors were driven towards the Anthropic purchase, and may come back again to Bitcoin after seeing the reaction.

It has been revealed that however, the market weakness is not just limited to Bitcoin. Since October 1, tech stocks have also fell drastically; Nvidia NVDA USDT) moved about 37%, and the Nasdaq 100 (⁠QQQ-USDT) slid to 13%. Overall, the AI Stocks are under a fear-driven sell-off.

It has been revealed that increased borrowing costs for Democrats in line with recent US elections, and profitability concerns regarding major AI firms’ spending and infrastructure also play a key role. So keeping only the Anthropic IPO isn’t fair thing.

Only since mid-October, a decline of nearly 5.3%($16B) was seen in the crypto stablecoin marketcap moving from $301B to $285B.

Adding the heat is the falling stablecoin supply, which reduced to $14.9B by Nov 12, recording the fastest 30-day decline since June 2022.

The Bitcoin ETF behaved the same. Compared to Q3, when institutions loaded an equivalent of 49,229 BTC, a record. But, there is a sharp reverse ion ETF flows now.

Only from Oct 1 to Nov 18, Bitcoin ETF’s recorded $3.4B in cumulative net outflows, of which nearly $2.9B were removed in 13 trading of November.

The two main pathways for money to flow into Bitcoin are shaken.

In a recent development, given the present condition, we look into the Open Interest: $57.7K to $58K is a strong support level. This is where the late June July level reacted upward.

If the above levels lose, then based on On-chain money flow that was recorded at the realised price on Nov 17 is at  $53K to $55K.

On the BTCUSDT 2H Chart, the cryptocurrency highlights the asset fighting at the support zone, with price remaining under *EMA 55, EMA 89, and EMA 233, and RSI at 22.

In a recent development, the overall analysis looks bearish; a relief bounce is visible from the support zone, but strong confirmation is needed. Otherwise, a break below $55,000 could lead to further downside.
