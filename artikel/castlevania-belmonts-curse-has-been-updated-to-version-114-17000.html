Konami has released a fresh revision for Castlevania: Belmont's Curse on the Switch and other platforms this week.

Although it's only a small one, it does include some adjustments and fixes. This includes a slight change to the "effective range" of the "Life Drain" spell. Here's the rundown from Konami's official website about everything included in the Version 1.1.4 upgrade:.

The following changes and fixes have been made in this enhancement: - The version number is now displayed at the bottom of the title screen. - Adjusted the effective range of the “Life Drain” spell. - Fixed an problem where the screen could remain black after loading under certain conditions. - Fixed various other complications.

In a recent development, belmont's Curse is currently available for the Switch. While there's no Switch 2 Edition, if you do play this title on Nintendo's upcoming hardware, you'll get a frame rate boost. And if you haven't tried it out yet, you can download a demo from the eShop.

Recent findings suggest that you can find out more about this new Castlevania entry iun our review here on Nintendo Life. We gave it 8/10 stars, calling it "one of the best Castlevania games in a long time thanks to its combat variety and smart whip integrations".
