A Grand Theft Auto 6 player says they've been hit with a 30-day PlayStation Network social features ban after sending a friend a photo they took in the game's nudist resort.

Posting on Twitter/X on Friday evening, DerrickGott007 stated they had "just got a 30-day PSN social features ban" for sending the photo, which they'd taken with the in-game camera, through PlayStation's messaging service, warning other players not to do the same.

It has been revealed that "Do not, I repeat do not send any photos taken with the in-game camera, across the PSN messaging network that contain nudity," they wrote. "I just got a 30-day PSN social features ban for sending my friend a photo of the nudist colony in GTA6 that I found.".

In a screenshot of the notice, PlayStation Safety says that content sent from the player's account via Message on PlayStation "breached the Code of Conduct", and that their account has been "temporarily suspended from using PlayStation social features for 30 days" due to "pornographic or obscene content". It also warns that "further offenses may result in temporary or permanent suspension from the whole of PlayStation, not just social features".

PlayStation's Code of Conduct asks players not to "share, solicit, access, distribute or store material that is obscene, pornographic or illegal", and indicates Sony "may take any action as reasonably necessary to protect PlayStation and our players".
