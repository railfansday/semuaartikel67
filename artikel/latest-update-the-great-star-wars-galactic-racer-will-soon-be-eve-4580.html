While Star Wars: Galactic Racer is an excellent arcade racing game in many respects, it did launch with a few missing features that left some speed freaks a little disappointed.

However, it seems developer Fuse Games is keen to address those concerns, as it's just confirmed a couple of much-requested modes are officially in the works.

Confirmed via social posts, Galactic Racer is getting local split-screen multiplayer as well as Free Play mode:.

Fuse Games notes these upcoming features are planned to arrive in "early 2027" and are right now being worked on within a "dedicated team.".

As the work-in-progress screenshot reveals, Galactic Racer will support four-player local multiplayer; there's no footage yet, so it's unknown how that's going to perform, but hopefully it can uphold the game's silky smooth frame rate.

Recent findings suggest that free Play, meanwhile, will be the home for split-screen, as well as other race types. "This new mode will allow you to choose your vehicle, character, planet, and race track to race alone or with up to 3 other players in local split-screen action," the post reads.

In a recent development, I'm really glad the studio is implementing these features in the new year. While Galactic Racer has plenty to enjoy already, I think the ability to make your own custom races is a logical next step.

To be honest, I wasn't expecting any split-screen support at all, but I'm pleased to be proven wrong there.
