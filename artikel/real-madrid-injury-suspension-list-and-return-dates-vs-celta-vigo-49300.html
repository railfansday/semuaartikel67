Real Madrid will continue their La Liga campaign against Celta Vigo on Saturday.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up Real Madrid's injury and suspension news ahead of the game with Celta, who sit in 11th position in Spain's top flight.

Rodrygo suffered a severe knee injury earlier this year, but the Brazil international has stepped up his recovery and is working towards being available at the start of 2027.

Mendy is still dealing with a long-term thigh problem, and the Frenchman may not be available for selection until the final stages of 2026.

Asencio recently underwent an operation on a stress fracture in his leg, and the centre-back is expected to be on the sidelines until the start of 2027.

Industry observers note that possible return date: November 21 (vs. Celta Vigo).

Bernardo needs to be assessed ahead of the clash with Celta, having pulled out of the Portugal squad during the international break due to an injury problem.

It has been revealed that possible return date: November 21 (vs. Celta Vigo).

Tchouameni did not play for France during the international break due to a groin problem, and he therefore needs to be assessed ahead of this contest.

Real Madrid have no players banned for this match, with Vinicius Junior available again after serving his domestic suspension.
