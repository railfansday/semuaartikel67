Chase Adds Qantas, Cathay Pacific as Transfer Partners

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Chase is growing its roster of airline transfer partners to include Qantas Frequent Flyer and Cathay Pacific Asia Miles.

That’ll bump the number of Chase’s transfer partners to 16, with 12 airline and four hotel loyalty programs. The new partners, which are live as of Nov. 18, 2026, will transfer at a 1:1 ratio from Chase Ultimate Rewards®.

You’ll need an eligible Chase card, such as the Chase Sapphire Reserve® and the Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card, to transfer points.

Qantas and Cathay Pacific are the flag carriers for Australia and Hong Kong, respectively.

Sources indicate that both airlines are part of the Oneworld alliance, which includes U.S. carriers like American Airlines, Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines. They also maintain relationships with non-alliance partners.

According to recent reports, your best shot at award seats on Qantas and Cathay Pacific flights is often through their own programs. That’s because airlines typically release the most award availability to their own loyalty program members.

While some partner airline programs might offer cheaper award pricing, it can be harder to find award availability through them. That’s why having points and miles with the airline you want to fly on can be so valuable.

According to recent reports, this is especially helpful for airlines that operate highly sought-after routes or premium cabin products. For example, Cathay Pacific's Aria Suite in business class is a popular choice for flyers. It's available on select flights from Hong Kong to North America, Europe, Australia and parts of Asia, so check the seat map before booking. For example, Cathay Pacific’s Aria Suite in business class is a popular choice for flyers and is only available on select routes.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, Qantas is expected to start flying the world's longest commercial route in October 2027 between Sydney and London, as part of its Project Sunrise initiative. It’s also slated to launch nonstop flights between Sydney and Newly introduced York the following year.

Those seats aren’t on sale yet, but they’re likely to be highly coveted for award travelers.

These additions aren’t particularly exciting if you already have access to other transferable currencies like Capital One miles, which transfer 1:1 to both Qantas and Cathay Pacific.

However, if you’re all in on Chase points, these upcoming airline partners can unlock more award redemption opportunities.
