West Ham have had a very poor season so far in the Championship, with the Hammers way off the pace for a top two finish.

Not only did many observers expect the Hammers to bounce back to the Premier League at the first attempt, but they were widely tipped to win the league.

Therefore, the fact they are in the bottom half after 17 games, and some 11 points away from second-place is a massive underachievement.

Nuno Espírito Santo paid the price for an inconsistent start by losing his job at the start of the month, but the decision to appoint Scott Parker hasn’t paid off just yet, as the former player is without a win in four.

However, with Parker having won promotion from the Championship with three different clubs, there’s no doubting his CV at this level. So, his early struggles have opened up a wider discussion on why West Ham are failing, and the players are coming under scrutiny.

In truth, you could identify several areas of the pitch that need addressing, but there’s no doubt that the goalkeeper position is giving Parker a major headache at the moment.

Mads Hermansen started as his number one, but the Dane has had a terrible time of things since he moved to the Londoners, and he was taken out of the firing line after a poor performance at Blackburn a few weeks ago.

With Alphonse Areola injured, youngster Fin Herrick has had the gloves for the past two games, but he didn’t convince for Preston’s opener last Saturday, and he made a mistake in the 1-0 defeat to Portsmouth on Tuesday.

So, it remains to be seen who starts against Stoke, with Areola likely to play if he is available. Yet, even if he is, the Frenchman hasn’t exactly played well for West Ham for a long time now, and there are doubts over both his fitness and mentality.

Therefore, bringing in a newly introduced number one could swiftly become a priority for Parker as soon as the window opens in January, and Blackburn’s Balazs Toth is someone who should be on their radar.

The Hungarian was heavily linked with Celtic in the summer transfer window, but Rovers managed to keep him at Ewood Park, although he's still thought to be a target for the Glasgow giants.

Toth has shown why he was so highly regarded after producing some significant saves for Blackburn this season, although he perhaps hasn’t got back to the heights he had set in the previous campaign.

Unfortunately for Blackburn and Toth, he is currently out with a hand injury sufered on international duty with Hungary that could sideline him until mid-January, but he has done enough over the years to earn a reputation as a reliable Championship keeper.

Due to his injury, it’s no exaggeration to say there’s a possibility that Toth has played his last game for Blackburn, as there are major doubts over his future going into the New Year.

That’s because Toth’s contract is expiring in the summer of 2027, so, unless fresh terms are agreed, then this will be the last window for Rovers to get a fee for the 29-year-old.

It has been claimed previously that Toth is not keen on signing an extension, so that will put clubs on alert going into January.

Whether Blackburn are willing to sanction a sale when they have a chance to make the play-offs remains to be seen, but it would certainly make financial sense to avoid letting an asset go for nothing.

From West Ham’s perspective, Toth’s contract means he is a very realistic target in what could be a challenging month for the club.

If West Ham remain off the pace in terms of pushing for the top two, selling clubs will know that the Irons are desperate.

We saw last January how the club spent big on the likes of Taty Castellanos and Pablo, and the latter in particular has proven to be a disastrous signing.

So, the club won’t want to waste more money in the winter window, and they also need players who are capable of making an instant impact at the London Stadium, as there will be no period to allow them to settle in.

That makes Toth an attractive option due to his experience at this level, and he should be fit in time for the home game against Swansea on January 15 if a deal is agreed.
