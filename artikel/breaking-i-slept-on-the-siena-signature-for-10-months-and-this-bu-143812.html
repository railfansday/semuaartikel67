Our Siena Signature mattress review: ★★★★.

Recent findings suggest that the Siena didn't have an easy ride into our best mattress guide. To be crowned the best cheap mattress we've put it through our rigorous lab-based mattress testing methodology as well as subjecting it to an extended 10 month at-home testing period.

Industry observers note that during that time the Siena continuously impressed with its full-body support. One of the best firm mattresses you can buy online, it holds the spine lifted and aligned, reducing back pain for stomach and back sleepers.

It has been revealed that as someone with a tendency to hunch over my laptop, I need a mattress that helps take strain off my spine. The Siena delivered, as the firm foams held me straight from neck to tailbone, allowing my muscles to unwind.

It also has phenomenal edge support (some of the best we've tested). You can sit, lie, or push against the side without ever feeling like you're slipping.

Industry observers note that the motion isolation is also excellent, as the dense foams stop movement from traveling across the bed. When someone jumped in on one side of the mattress, I could hardly feel them on the other.

It has been revealed that and finally, temperature regulation. This often trips up cheap all-foam beds but the Siena is one of the best cooling mattresses on a budget we've tested. While it doesn't feel cool to the touch it stays at a constant temperature, even during warm nights.

In a recent development, however, as a firm mattress, the Siena doesn't have enough cushioning for many side sleepers. If that sounds like a problem, we have a solution.

Sources indicate that I slept on the Zinus Green Tea and in my opinion, the best way to sum it up is "marshmallow". The plush foams deliver incredible cushioning to the hips and shoulders (just what you want from the best mattresses for side sleepers) but this full body hug can translate into hot sleep. However, as it's just $184 for a queen in Amazon's Black Friday sale, you might be able to overlook this.
