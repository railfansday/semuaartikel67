Google Search’s AI Mode can now tap into Drive and Wallet for more personalized responses

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that google confirmed on Monday that it’s expanding Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode with integrations for Google Drive, Google Wallet, and the Gemini app. Personal Intelligence is designed to offer consumers more personalized, context-aware answers by connecting to their Google apps and drawing on information from their digital lives. The feature can already connect to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Photos.

Sources indicate that by bringing Personal Intelligence in AI Mode to Drive, Wallet, and the Gemini app, Google is leveraging the wealth of user data that’s already within its ecosystem. Since people rely on these services, the tech giant can deliver more personalized experiences that rivals aren’t able to match. Of course, not everyone wants AI looking at their Wallet and Drive, so connecting the apps is optional, and you can turn Personal Intelligence on or off at any time.

Sources indicate that google indicates that connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence lets users track their financial goals, cut everyday spending, and make better use of credit card rewards. The tech giant notes that if users choose to connect their Wallet, AI Mode can only read information from accounts and isn’t able to move or manage their money.

In a recent development, as for Drive, the firm indicates important information related to a project or major decision is often scattered across documents and spreadsheets. By connecting Google Drive to Personal Intelligence, AI can draw on those files to provide more relevant, personalized answers, such as creating a tailored blueprint for tax season.

Consumers will also be able to share context between their Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, which means that if they explore an idea in the Gemini app, they won’t have to start over with AI Mode because it will already have the information, and vice versa.

Additionally, Google claims owners will soon be able to view the context that AI Mode uses via a latest “Personalization” icon that will appear below responses.

The company also declared on Monday that users will start to see more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode that will be tailored to users’ recent search context.
