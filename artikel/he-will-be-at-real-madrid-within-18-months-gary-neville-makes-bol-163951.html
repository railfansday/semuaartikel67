By MICHAEL PAVITT, SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 10:49 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 12:07 EST, 27 November 2026.

Gary Neville has claimed he is 'absolutely certain' Erling Haaland will leave Manchester City for Real Madrid within the next 18 months amid the impact of their legal battle with the Premier League.

Haaland has cut a despondent figure in recent weeks following Man City's underwhelming run of form.

The Norwegian star admitted to 'questioning everything' after their 2-2 draw against Napoli in the Champions League in midweek, while Man City fans expressed concerns over Haaland's downbeat assessment and body language.

The result meant City have won just twice in their last seven matches – one of which was achieved in a penalty shoot-out against Brighton in the Carabao Cup.

Man City have suffered defeats to Aston Villa, Brighton and Nottingham Forest in the Premier League in the run, as well as drawing with Fulham on the weekend.

In a recent development, neville, speaking on the Overlap, suggested the mood at Man City may have been affected in recent weeks after the club were found guilty of the vast majority of charges brought against them by the Premier League for financial wrongdoing. Man City have since appealed the decision.

Gary Neville has said he is 'absolutely certain' Erling Haaland will leave Manchester City for Real Madrid within the next 18 months.

Neville suggested Haaland's spell at Man City could be coming to an end after questioning whether the 'cloud' of the Premier League charges verdict has impacted the mood at the club.

The former Man United defender theorised that Haaland's time at the club could be coming to an end and that he expects the forward to join Real Madrid in the near future.

'It feels like something has changed in these last few weeks with City, it feels like maybe that mood, that cloud, is just starting to [impact],' Neville noted.

'I don't think Haaland is going to be here, I think he will be at Real Madrid in the next, maximum 18 months, maybe even at the end of the season.

'I can see Vinicius leaving, I can see Mbappe going a bit to the left and Haaland going up there [down the middle].

'If you look at Florentino Perez, he goes and signs the best strikers in the world, the best players in the world.

'I just think because of what has happened to City, I think Haaland is going to Real Madrid.

'I am absolutely certain he will be in a Real Madrid shirt in the next 18 months.'.

It had long been viewed that Haaland's career initiative had involved moving to LaLiga, with Real Madrid long-term admirers.

Real Madrid have held a long-standing interest in the Manchester City superstar.

Neville claimed Real Madrid president Florentino Perez could opt to sell Vinicius Jr before moving for Haaland.

The forward, however, signed a nine-and-a-half-year deal in early 2025, taking his Man City contract through to 2034.

Haaland had pushed back against suggestions the case had affected their form during the international break, while stressing he was committed long-term to the club.

The forward's first answer to Norwegian broadcaster TV2 had spanned two minutes and 14 seconds, as he reeled off the teams he had represented from 2009 to 2018, the years covered by the charges after he was asked about the club's situation.

'In 2009, I played for Bryne U9s. In 2010, the U10s. In 2011, the U11s. 2012, the U12s, 2013, the U13s. Shall we continue? We don't need the whole list. 2014, the U14s. 2015, the U15s and the national team,' Haaland said.

'Then the U16s, I think. In 2016, I moved up to Bryne's first team, second team and youth team. In 2017, I went to Molde. In 2018, I was with Molde. The first team, Molde 2 and a bit with the youth team.

'So this is a question I struggle to answer because it's a legal battle….

Sources indicate that 'It's a case between the Premier League and Manchester City that a panel will deal with. Am I supposed to answer that? I'm not really sure.'.

Haaland cut a despondent figure in midweek as he admitted to 'questioning everything' after Man City's draw with Napoli continued their underwhelming form.

Haaland has pushed back against suggestions the Premier League charges verdict has had an impact on Man City's form.

He continued: 'It's a difficult question to answer when I don't really know that much about it.

'I assume the answers will come… I don't know when. I haven't read much about it.

'To be completely honest, I'm focused on trying to perform for Manchester City.'.

Haaland, who has scored 176 goals in 214 appearances for Man City, will be hoping to spark Enzo Maresca's side back into form when they face Premier League leaders Arsenal on Sunday.

Arsenal are presently six points clear of Man City heading into the match as they bid to retain their title this season.
