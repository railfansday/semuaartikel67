According to recent reports, carpet cleaning equipment often features various cleaning modes and includes several accessories. Hose and cord length affect how long the unit can continuously clean before unplugging and moving. Keeping these factors in mind when shopping can help ensure you pick the best residential carpet cleaner for your needs.

Spot-cleaning machines are lightweight, portable, and less expensive than whole-house cleaners, but their suction isn’t as strong as larger units. A handheld carpet cleaner is ideal for use on stairs, in homes with just a couple of carpeted rooms, or on area rugs. These small carpet cleaners are also good for cleaning the carpets in the car.

In a recent development, whole-house or upright carpet cleaners have robust motors, powerful suction, and thick, rotating brushes that provide deep cleaning. Many models offer multiple cleaning modes and use their own cleaning solutions, making them better suited to larger homes with many carpeted rooms that see heavy traffic. A whole-house, heavy-duty carpet cleaner is ideal for lifting dirt and grime that’s settled deep into carpet or rug fibers.

It has been revealed that buyers will want to consider their physical limits, the amount of carpeting, and the number of levels in the home when making a decision. In general, the heavier the machine, the more powerful its suction motor and rotating brushes will be. Portable carpet cleaners, like handheld carpet cleaners, are lighter and easier to maneuver, whereas full-size upright machines capable of heavy-duty cleaning will be harder to move and store.

Recent findings suggest that cord and hose length both affect cleaning time; the less often you have to unplug and plug in, the more efficient the cleaning session. Long cords of 20 feet or more and hoses up to 10 feet are particularly helpful in large rooms and for cleaning stairs. Since some upright units don’t have hoses, buyers wanting to clean under heavy pieces of furniture will want to be careful when choosing.

Carpet-cleaner accessories are attachments that can be added to a hose or intake port for cleaning different materials, reaching difficult areas, or dealing with pet hair and odors. Some of the most common types include upholstery cleaners; crevice-cleaning attachments (handy for tight spots like the indent created where the baseboard meets the carpet); or spinning brushes that lift tough, stuck-on stains. Pet attachments include special brushes, guards, and even isolated tanks for trapping fur to prevent it from getting caught in the machine. Many machines include samples of the brand’s best carpet shampoos or cleaning solutions to help you start cleaning right away.

To reduce drying time, use a quick-cleaning mode, which releases less liquid, and increase ventilation by opening windows, turning on fans, and turning on heat or air conditioning.
