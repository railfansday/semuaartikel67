As you approach the final missions in the game, Jason and Lucia start working with Giorgio, a very rich shipping magnate who works with Eastern European criminals to smuggle drugs across the ocean on his big ships. And things aren’t going well, forcing Giorgio to work with Jason, Lucia, and others on a big, ambitious initiative to steal back all the cocaine confiscated by the Coast Guard as part of a large heist. But before they can pull off the heist, a higher-up from the big Balkan cartel Giorgio has been working with arrives to kill him for screwing up, but gives him one more chance to get back the coke. That badass hitman? Niko Bellic, the playable protagonist of 2008’s Grand Theft Auto IV.

Sources indicate that I asked Nelson if they’d planned to include Niko from the start, and he tells me that they hadn’t. Instead, he told me that the cameo came about “very organically.”.

In a recent development, “[Early in development], we had Giorgio and we had him working with a sort of Balkan cartel,” explained Nelson. The GTA 6 boss further explained that this whole storyline was based on research they’d done about actual Italian shipping companies that allegedly smuggled drugs for various gangs in South America. Apparently, a boat run by such a firm was carrying 20 tons of coke when it was raided by U.S. officials. That number should sound familiar to anyone who has played the later missions of GTA 6, as that’s the amount of coke seized by the Coast Guard in the game.Â.

“Giorgio is not really a criminal. He’s a sort of wealthy shipping magnate who’s in league with these criminals,” stated Nelson.

“But it was sort of that same [real-world] idea. And then we needed someone from the Balkan cartel to show up. It just grew very organically, and it’s like, well, who would he be working with from the Balkan end of the organization? When Giorgio fucks up, who’s going to come down to deal with him? And I got an idea.”.

It has been revealed that nelson notes Niko’s story was sort of “left open” after the events of GTA IV.Â “He’s still kicking around. He’s still in play,” he told Kotaku. Nelson also confirmed original voice actor Michael Holick returned for the role. When I asked if it was nice to have him back and work with him again, Nelson responded with a smile: “Very much, yeah.”Â.

I asked Nelson if this cameo confirms anything about which ending is canon in GTA IV, and Nelson wouldn’t say one way or the other and pointed out that nothing Niko says or does in either cutscene indicates a specific canon ending for that game. He did confirm that Niko will be going back to Liberty City after all this and that it’s been his home since GTA IV.Â.

Industry observers note that there’s one more great detail included in this cameo moment. After the cutscene ends, players hear the mission-complete jingle from GTA IV play instead of one of GTA 6‘s various mission-complete jingles. According to Nelson, that was a “late addition.”.

“For a long time it was like a normal [GTA 6] stinger, and we played it and…” Nelson made an meh face. “I got on with Yvonne and stated, ‘Can we put the [mimics the GTA IV mission complete jingle] in there?'” And they did, which was a wonderful touch to an already amazing moment.Â.
