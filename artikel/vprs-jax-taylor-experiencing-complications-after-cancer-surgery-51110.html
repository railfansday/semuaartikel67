Jax Taylor has hit a small roadblock in his recovery from kidney cancer surgery.

Recent findings suggest that the Vanderpump Rules alum's release from the hospital has been delayed due to after experiencing "some complications," a source close to Taylor exclusively told E! News Nov. 19.

"Physically, he’s very sore from the incisions," the source stated, "but mentally, he’s feeling relieved to have the surgery behind him, as it’s something that’s been weighing heavily on him for months.".

Fortunately, Taylor is surrounded by a strong support system, including his girlfriend Lori Krebs.

"Lori has been by his side," the source shared. "The last few months have really taken a toll on him and she’s always been someone who brings him a sense of peace. They’ve been through a lot together and there’s a very deep love and connection between them.".

The insider added, "They’re truly there for one another, and I think going through something like this has only made them realize just how much they mean to each other.".

It has been revealed that taylor, who confirmed his relationship with Krebs earlier this year, had been privately navigating his cancer diagnosis for a while before going public with his health journey. As he shared in a Nov. 19 Instagram post, “This is something I’ve kept private for the past few months because it’s been one of the scariest times of my life.".

Industry observers note that “What I thought was just a kidney stone turned into something I never imagined," the 47-year-old wrote. "My CT scan also highlighted a tumor on my left kidney. After many tests and appointments I found out it was cancer.”.

Ultimately, Taylor underwent a five-hour surgery to have the tumor removed. While the medical crisis put the Valley star in "a really dark place," he credited Krebs for being his guiding light.

Industry observers note that “I honestly don’t know how I would have gotten through everything without my amazing girlfriend by my side,” shared Taylor, who's dad to 5-year-old son Cruz with ex Brittany Cartwright. “She’s been my rock through all of this.".

He added, "She’s not just my partner, she’s my best friend and I feel so insanely lucky to have her in my life.".
