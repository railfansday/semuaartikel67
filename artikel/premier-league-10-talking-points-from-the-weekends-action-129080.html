It has been revealed that what to make of Manchester United’s utterly torrid start under Mauricio Pochettino? To go glass half full as the fresh head coach had only three days to prepare the side for the trip to Anfield, the hope will be that time will cast the 4-0 humbling from Liverpool as the falsest of false starts. The glass-half-empty scenario, though, is that here was an XI packed with internationals that had zero clue how to sort out a dire beginning themselves. Leny Yoro’s very early red card may have been questionable but for the wall to evaporate and allow Dominik Szoboszlai’s subsequent free-kick to arrow home was damning evidence of how immature Pochettino’s new charges are. From here it became a debacle and the players, if they have anything about them, need to apologise to the head coach, hold a meeting between themselves and demand they start taking responsibility. If not, United are heading only one way: down. Jamie Jackson.
