Meanwhile, Ukraine’s Volodymyr Zelenskyy has now arrived in Berlin for today’s meeting of the Coalition of the Willing.

It has been revealed that “More than 40 partners will be represented, including the team of the American President. Ukraine’s priorities are strengthening our air defence, energy and financial support, the diplomatic track, and the next steps toward ending the war and guaranteeing security,” he said.

Zelenskyy added Ukraine was “doing everything to ensure Europe’s full participation in the negotiation process and to make these meetings productive.”.

“Bilateral meetings with European partners are also planned, and, of course, with chancellor Friedrich Merz. There will also be a format involving the American side and European national security advisers.”.
