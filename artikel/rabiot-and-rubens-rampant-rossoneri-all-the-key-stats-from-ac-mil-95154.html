AC Milan produced their best attacking display of the season on Sunday night as they put five goals past Frosinone at San Siro.

According to recent reports, an own-goal from Gabriele Calvani early on set the tone, with Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic all netting in the rout. The ruthless counter-attacking display moves Milan back inside the top four, after wins from Como, Roma and Lazio earlier in the round.

The match stats make for interesting reading. Milan edged possession with 54%, generating 2.50 Expected Goals – so scoring double what they created – but also having five substantial chances which matches the number of goals scored.

Industry observers note that frosinone remarkably had more shots in total (15 to 12), but they were more wasteful with only three on target to the Rossoneri’s eight. The visitors also racked up nine corners, the most Milan have conceded all season.

Only Nereo Rocco’s (29) and Lajos Czeizler’s (31) Milan sides scored more goals than Ruben Amorim’s (28) in their first 12 matches in charge in Serie A.

Adrien Rabiot is the first Milan midfielder to be involved in at least one goal (including goals and assists) in seven consecutive Serie A appearances since Kaká in 2004, and he became the first midfielder in the last 20 years of Serie A with more than 10 goals in his first 10 games in a single season.

With his sixth goal in his 10th Serie A appearance for Milan at 20 years and 31 days, Alphadjo Cisse became the second-youngest Rossoneri player to reach this milestone in his first 10 appearances in the three-points-for-a-win era, after Alexandre Pato , who achieved it at 18 years and 189 days.

No team has scored more goals than Milan in the first 15 minutes of play in the five major European leagues currently underway (7, like Barcelona and Chelsea).

Milan are now unbeaten in all seven meetings against Frosinone in Serie A (W5 D2): among the teams against which they have never lost in the competition, the Rossoneri have played more games only against Pisa (14 – six also against Crotone and Lecco).

Recent findings suggest that milan have won five of their last six league games (L1), scoring at least two goals in each, after failing to win three of their previous four (D3). The last time they scored more than one goal in a run of Serie A matches was between September 2020 and January 2021, when they managed 15 games.
