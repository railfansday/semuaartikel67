UnCruise Adventures has unveiled its Cyber Monday week offer.

Running from November 30 to December 5, 2026, the deal offers savings on select 2027 cruise dates, reducing them by $500 per person as a giveback, which can then be used to book airfare or other travel costs.

The deal is valid for select 2027 cruises on new bookings only and can be combined with other current offers, resulting in potential savings of up to $1,000 per person on select sailings.

Recent findings suggest that “Our small ships carry fewer than 90 guests which lets us anchor in coves and ports the big ships can’t reach,” stated Captain Dan Blanchard, owner and CEO, UnCruise Adventures. “That’s the trip you’re getting at $500 less per person this Cyber Monday week.”.

To redeem, guests should ask for the offer “2026CYBER” at the time of booking either directly with UnCruise Adventures or through a travel advisor.

The Cyber Monday deal is offered on select dates in these UnCruise destinations:.

Seven ships sail in Alaska from April through September, with eligible dates available on all ships throughout the season.

Nine different itineraries range from seven to 13 nights on routes that depart from Seattle, Juneau, Seward, Sitka, Ketchikan and Dutch Harbor.

The 36-guest Safari Explorer sails a seven-night “Hawaiian Seascapes” itinerary between Kona and Molokai, visiting four islands with stops for snorkeling, ridge hikes, a night snorkel with Giant Pacific manta rays and a hike around Hualālai Volcano.

Dates are available March – April and November – December.

Eight-night and 11-night cruises aboard the 66-guest Safari Voyager explore the Gulf of California, where guests search for whales, snorkel with sea lion pups, explore scenic islands and beaches.

From January through May, cruises sail roundtrip from San José del Cabo with a pre-cruise hotel night included.

The 66-guest Safari Voyager sails two itineraries, a seven-night Costa Rica cruise roundtrip Liberia, and a 14-night Costa Rica and Panama cruise that includes a Panama Canal transit.

Cruises sail from August through December.

An eight-night expedition through the Galapagos Islands sails roundtrip from Quito, Ecuador aboard the 48-guest La Pinta, with excursions exploring seven islands and islets while searching for wildlife.

A visit to the Charles Darwin Research Station and two nights hotel pre-cruise in Quito are included.

Two dates are available for the Cyber deal: January 29 and July 16, 2027.
