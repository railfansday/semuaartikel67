Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando have kept things pretty low-key across their five-year relationship—so low-key, in fact, that their sporadic public appearances tend to be followed by questions on social media like, “Who’s Miley’s hot date?” Well, it’s Maxx Morando, drummer for the Los Angeles rock band Liily—and now, Cyrus’s husband.

According to recent reports, below, find everything you need to know about Maxx Morando, a.k.a. the upcoming Mr. Miley Cyrus, whom Cyrus married in a poolside ceremony over the weekend in Palm Springs.

Cyrus and Morando met on a blind date in 2021. (“Well, it was blind for me and not really for him,” Cyrus told British Vogue in 2023.) They’ve been living together in Malibu since at least 2024.

Morando and Cyrus at the Guicci Love Parade show in Los Angeles in November 2021.

Before joining Liily, Morando played drums for indie rock band the Regrettes from 2015 to 2018.

Cyrus invited Morando and Liily to perform on her New Year’s Eve special on NBC in 2022, with a source saying at the time: “Miley insisted that Maxx’s band perform at her NYE special, and this was not something up for debate. Music is what bonds them together and they love nothing more than creating beautiful sounds when it is just the two of them.” Additionally, Morando and Cyrus worked together on the songs “Handstand” and “Violet Chemistry” from her 2023 album Endless Summer Vacation; and he contributed to several songs on this year’s Bass Persuades.

The couple at the 2024 Grammy Awards.

Recent findings suggest that if you were Miley Cyrus, wouldn’t you ditch your phone occasionally? Apparently, that’s exactly what the star does, telling British Vogue of the couple’s phone protocol: “My boyfriend brings his phone and I don’t bring one.” If he’s willing to be plugged into the extremely online world for you, he might just be a keeper.

Sources indicate that cyrus and Morando had discussed getting engaged prior to a 2025 trip to Japan, but he finally pulled off a surprise proposal in what he described as “this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation” on their hotel grounds. “I could feel my heart pounding. You grow up as a kid watching the movies where the guy gets on one knee and does the whole thing, and you know that’s what you’re supposed to do. But then you realize, ‘Okay, I literally have to get on one knee.’ I didn’t want to fuck up and fumble or drop the [ring].” Well, it seems he didn’t (or if he did, Cyrus presumably found it endearing)!
