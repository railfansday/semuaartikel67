It has been revealed that the analyst marks a strong accumulation zone from $0.035 to $0.06, expecting a rally to $0.10 to repeat itself just the previous cycle. The longer-term HBAR price projection is capped near $0.30, a yearly high the OG altcoin would have to 5x from current price levels to reach again.

In a recent development, the current support level of $0.06 isn’t accompanied by major investor support. Several crypto whale sentiment-measuring metrics, including the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) & the Bull Bear Power (BBP), have flashed negative figures: the CMF even dipped to -0.84, a new yearly low.

Ultimately, this hints at immense selling power among the largest holders, making the $0.06 hard to sustain unless HBAR’s bulls cover that divergence. The popular altcoin has remained below all four main moving averages throughout much of the recent HBAR price drop.

In a recent development, on a brighter note, the analyst’s post signals a bullish outlook for HBAR, approaching macro bottoms where CrediBULL anticipates a strong, significant upward reversal. With conviction lacking on the whale side, this matches the scared retail sentiment felt lately.

Recent findings suggest that according to Crypto’s Fear & Greed Index, the meter has dropped to 12, showcasing ‘extreme fear’ amongst market participants. Momentum gauges underscore how stretched the sell-off has become. The Relative Strength Index has entered oversold mode with no buying power.

On Derivatives, the lackluster trading action on Tuesday comes after mass liquidation among bullish HBAR positions. The excessive leverage was wiped out during the weekend, even pushing the HBAR funding rate on leveraged markets below zero, but bounced back later.

With the $0.10 resistance line on deck in case of a $0.06 hold on the daily time-frame, HBAR’s own network developments could overshadow the high-price correlation with Bitcoin (BTC) & Ether (ETH). Hedera will upgrade its mainnet to v0.79 at 18:00 UTC on November 18th.

The upcoming upgrade replaces traditional Record Streams with Block Streams, a unified cryptographically verifiable format that merges previous data outputs, including events and signatures, while implementing the approved Threshold Signature Scheme improvements.
