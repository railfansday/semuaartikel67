Borussia Mönchengladbach head coach Alexander Blessin has highlighted his initiative to get a result against unbeaten Borussia Dortmund.

The two Borussias clash in the Bundesliga on Saturday afternoon, with bottom club Gladbach in desperate need of a victory with just one from their opening nine.

Sources indicate that “Dortmund obviously have enormous individual quality,” explained Blessin. “They have strong spells time and again, but there are also moments when you can get the better of them and perhaps right after the international break, that’s exactly when we can give them a hard time.”.

Relishing Saturday’s game, Blessin added: “We have to be highly focused and ready to give it our all for 90 minutes. It will be important to get away with it during the phases where Dortmund might have scoring opportunities or the upper hand. So that we can simply keep the game open until the 90th minute and then maybe score ourselves. It’s about having a high tolerance for suffering and showing the willingness to be really nasty.”.

Because Borussia-Park’s standing section has been increased, 54,909 fans will be in attendance on Saturday, beating the 54,042 record. “It’s going to be great,” added Blessin. “We’re looking forward to the game against a really good opponent, again whom we’ll give our all.”.
