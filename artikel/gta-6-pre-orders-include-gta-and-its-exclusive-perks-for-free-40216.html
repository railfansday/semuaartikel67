📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6 is here, and I imagine a lot of you who didn’t place a pre-order or haven’t bought a copy yet are feeling some serious FOMO right about now. If you are, and you do plan on playing, it’s worth pulling the trigger before the end of the day, as even though GTA 6 is out now, the game still comes with pre-order bonuses if you buy it before November 20.

It has been revealed that the pre-order bonuses are the Vintage Vice City pack, which gives Jason and Lucia 1980s Miami makeovers with a ride to match, and a free month of GTA+. It’s the Vice City pack that has received most of the pre-order attention ahead of launch, but you actually get a lot more with that free month of GTA+ than you might think.

Industry observers note that the headliner is Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy. A car well beyond the sort of thing we should be cruising around in when we first arrive in Leonida, but a GTA+ subscription allows for exactly that. The sub even comes with a free single-car garage in Crosstown so you can keep it safe. If you total it immediately, though, that’s on you.

In a recent development, if you are worried about wrecking your new ride, you can take different modes of transport around Leonida for less using your GTA+ membership. The transit services bonuses include a free upgrade from Scooter Bros. Premium, a premium RydeMe membership, free SeaShark rentals, and hop aboard any of Leonida’s many modes of transportation for free too.

It has been revealed that while that’s all for the GTA+ freebies, there’s money to be saved elsewhere by cashing in your free month. Any vehicles you sell will automatically get a 10% price bump, so more cash in your pocket for the key stuff in Vice City, and a 10% discount in pretty much all of GTA 6’s many stores. That discount applies to newly introduced clothes, weapons, vehicle mods, tattoos, and more.

In a recent development, again, provided you’re reading this before November 20, for the first month, you can get all of this for no additional cost, regardless of which edition of GTA 6 you bought. The free month of GTA+ is a pre-order bonus for everyone who bought the game on release day (November 19) or before.

Sources indicate that GTA+ comes with a lot more than exclusive GTA 6 perks too. If you still plan on going back to GTA Online, the subscription gets you GTA$500,000 each month and special shark cards. You also get access to a library of Rockstar games you can play at any time, including Bully and Red Dead Redemption.
