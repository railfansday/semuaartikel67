Keith Andrews noted his decision to make several changes to Brentford's starting line-up against Everton on Monday showed ambition to compete on all fronts this season.

Andrews watched on as his Brentford side responded from their 2-0 defeat to Brighton ahead of the international break with a thumping 4-0 triumph over 10-man Everton.

Keane Lewis-Potter netted twice in the space of 178 seconds in the first half to put the Bees in control before Everton captain James Tarkowski was sent off in the 40th minute.

Tarkowski was dismissed for catching Yehor Yarmoliuk high on his shin after clearing the ball, with referee Craig Pawson overruling his original decision after a VAR review.

Vitaly Janelt's 67th-minute thunderbolt was the pick of Brentford's goals, with substitute Fabio Carvalho climbing off the bench to add further gloss to the scoreline late on.

Brentford enjoyed their joint-biggest home win in the Premier League, equalling their 4-0 rout of Manchester United in August 2022, with their display coming after Andrews elected to make four changes to the side that were beaten by Brighton.

Callum Wilson replaced the suspended Igor Thiago up front, while Yarmoliuk, Mikkel Damsgaard and Jaidon Anthony all came in to replace Mamadou Sangare and Kevin Schade, while Aaron Hickey missed out all together through injury.

"Big impact, it's the nature of the game and the squad. We have the luxury of choosing from very good players," Andrews told Sky Sports on the reason behind his alterations.

In a recent development, "Some will be disappointed not to play more, but the reality is we need everybody. We want to be ambitious, so we need everybody. The boys that came in did really well.".

After missing out on European football last season, Brentford's win saw them climb up to sixth in the Premier League and just four points adrift of fourth-place Liverpool.

"We know what the other side feels like, but I wanted to keep our habits, be professional, try to control the game and wait for the right openings," Andrews added.

Recent findings suggest that "Each game is very different, very difficult. The expectation levels at this club have changed and, regardless of the performance, the win to go sixth in the league and still questions about 'should they be doing better?' – I think people need to get real.

Sources indicate that "I'm really happy with the group, the club and long may it continue. Long way to go. Onto Old Trafford on Saturday and we're really looking forward to it.".

Since Andrews took charge of Brentford ahead of the 2025-26 season, of London clubs, only Arsenal (113) have won more Premier League points than the Bees (70).

Brentford also hit the 300-goal mark in the Premier League in their 201st game in the competition (303 goals in total), the fifth-fastest side to do so, after Man United (165), Newcastle United (170), Liverpool (190) and Blackburn Rovers (191).

Lewis-Potter got the landmark goal, and in 11 appearances this season, he has already beaten his best-ever scoring season in the Premier League (three in 36 apps in 2025-26 and 3 in 30 in 2023-24), with his double taking him to four this term.

"He's been excellent, growing all the time since I've been here," Andrews noted of Lewis-Potter.

"Finds himself in unnatural positions – we give him the freedom to go and roam, and he's proving very difficult for the opposition to mark.".
