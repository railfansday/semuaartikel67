In a recent development, while video games generally drop in price quickly, Nintendo is a bit of an exception to the rule—it rarely discounts its first-party titles. But I've spotted a few Black Friday deals on official Nintendo Switch 2 bundles, Nintendo Switch 2 games, and original Switch games. These deals are live on the official Nintendo Store, but you can also expect to find them at all major retailers.

Sadly, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is not on the list, but here’s what is on offer. (We'll add more Nintendo deals to this list as they pop up.).

The original and best Nintendo Switch 2 bundle is this one, combining a Nintendo Switch 2 console, a full download of the Mario Kart World game, and a 3-Month (90-Day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership.

The ultimate party game, Mario Kart World offers open-world racing chaos with a huge cast of characters. Whether you play split-screen or go for online multiplayer battles against up to 23 other players, this title offers the greatest replayability and fun of any Nintendo Switch 2 game. This bundle costs $500, which is a decent $50 saving.

If you want something a little different, this is the other Switch 2 bundle that’s on sale. Once again, you get a Nintendo Switch 2 system and three months of Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership, but this time it’s paired with the Nintendo Switch Sports Resort game.

This title includes all your old Wii Sports favorites (Tennis, Golf, Bowling, and Boxing), everything from Wii Sports Resort (Archery, Basketball, Table Tennis, Power Cruising, and Prop Plane), and three new options (Volleyball, Skateboarding, and Thumb Wrestling). This bundle is available for $530, which is around $28 off the usual price.

There are several deals on Switch 2 games at $20 off the usual price, so Black Friday is a good time to fill out your library before the holidays.
