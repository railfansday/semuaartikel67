West Ham have slipped down to 12th place in the Championship table following their 1-0 loss away at Portsmouth on Tuesday night. WHUFC are now three points off the top eight behind Swansea City.

According to recent reports, the Hammers turned to Scott Parker as their head coach earlier this month after their decision to part ways with Nuno Espirito Santo. However, the ex-Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley man has struggled to make an impact so far and is yet to win in his first four games at the helm.

According to recent reports, brazil, who played for the likes of Ipswich Town, Tottenham Hotspur and QPR during his playing days, has delivered his verdict on the London outfit on talkSPORT: “It’s only four games (for Parker). I’m sure he can turn that around but defensively they are giving so many easy goals away. They’ve got to stop that.”.

Recent findings suggest that he added: “Listen, I still think they’ll be hovering top six (eight). I was hoping that they’d have a great start. I like West Ham but it hasn’t to be.”.

In a recent development, west Ham are back in action this weekend as they aim to return to winning ways. They take on high flying Stoke City at the London Stadium.

West Ham haven’t kept a clean sheet in their last six matches. As Brazil has alluded to, WHUFC have been leaking too many goals.

Recent findings suggest that the Hammers need to tighten up their defensive department if they are to turn their fortunes around over the coming weeks. They will get the chance to bring in some upcoming faces when the January transfer window opens up for business.

Industry observers note that parker will be eager to put his own stamp on his newly inherited squad this winter. He knows what it takes to get out of the second tier having guided Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley up in the past.

According to recent reports, he is under pressure already at West Ham though, despite only being four games into his spell. If they lose again against Stoke City on Saturday, the heat will be further intensified.

Industry observers note that west Ham are still the top scorers in the division. WHUFC have scored 38 goals in their 17 fixtures.

The Hammers are always a threat going forward and if they can bolster their backline, there is no reason why they can’t rise back up the league. They managed to keep hold of Jarrod Bowen in the summer and he has found the net on nine occasions this term.

Parker will be disappointed with his start as manager but the players are still getting used to his style. He won’t get patience from the supporters though and needs wins fast.
