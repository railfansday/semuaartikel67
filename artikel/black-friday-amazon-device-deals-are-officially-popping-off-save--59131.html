Sure, Black Friday technically falls on Nov. 27 in 2026, but Amazon's official Black Friday Week has already started with a bang. That's great news for anyone looking to save some cash and spread out their holiday shopping.

If there's an Amazon device on your list, there are tons of deals that just went live on Fire TV Sticks, Kindles, Echo devices, and Alexa tablets. Since many of these devices saw price increases in 2026 (thanks, RAMageddon), these discounts are more welcome than ever. We'll take anything we can get.
