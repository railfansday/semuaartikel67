England Under-21s manager Lee Carsley has told GIVEMESPORT that nothing has changed regarding Archie Gray’s international situation amid interest from Scotland, while backing Jobe Bellingham to have a ‘massive future’ with the Three Lions.

England were drawn in Group A alongside France, Poland and co-hosts Serbia for next summer’s European Championship on Thursday evening, with Carsley’s side looking to win the competition for a third consecutive time.

Having overseen successive European Championship triumphs in 2023 and 2025, Carsley has an opportunity to further cement his legacy within England’s youth setup.

The 52-year-old spoke to GIVEMESPORT following the draw in Belgrade, discussing the international futures of several of England’s brightest young talents, including Gray, Bellingham, Max Dowman and Manchester United defender Ayden Heaven.

Gray’s international future has been a talking point amid interest from Scotland, with the Tottenham Hotspur midfielder eligible to represent both nations.

Carsley previously revealed that he had held conversations with Gray regarding the situation, but the England Under-21s boss insists nothing has changed since those discussions.

Speaking to GIVEMESPORT, Carsley remarked:.

“Archie, like a lot of the players, is key to the Under-21s. He’s got a fantastic history in the pathway, a player that we really enjoy working with.

“There’s no change to what we spoke about previously.”.

Gray’s versatility makes him a valuable asset within England’s youth setup, although competition for places in Thomas Tuchel’s senior squad remains fierce.

One player who has already been rewarded with a senior international opportunity is Jobe Bellingham.

Having worked closely with the midfielder at Under-21 level, Carsley believes Bellingham was fully deserving of his opportunity and expects him to feature in the senior setup again in the future.

“I think it’s always difficult to get into that senior squad. Having been there myself, I realise how difficult it is to pick a squad.

“Jobe more than deserved his opportunity. I imagine he’ll be there in the future.

“He’s a really crucial player for us, a player that I love working with. He’s got a massive future with England. He deserves all the applause that he gets.”.

Few young English players have generated as much excitement as Arsenal youngster Max Dowman.

The teenager made history during the November international break, breaking Jude Bellingham’s record to become England’s youngest-ever Under-21 goalscorer with his winner against France.

Despite his age, Carsley believes Dowman has already demonstrated that he belongs at Under-21 level and is relishing the opportunity to play a role in his development.

Sources indicate that “I think he revealed last camp that he was more than capable of being there. He’s an outstanding talent on and off the pitch.

Recent findings suggest that “Great player to work with. Lovely kid as well also, which is really vital. Settled into the group nicely.

“Max is a player that we rate highly.”.

Manchester United defender Ayden Heaven has previously spent time training with Carsley’s Under-21 group and could be among those competing for a place in next summer’s squad.

Asked specifically about Heaven’s prospects of featuring at the European Championship, Carsley revealed he is monitoring approximately 65 players before selecting his final 23-man squad.

“I think there’s a lot of players that are potentially in and around that squad. I’m watching maybe 65 players in total to pick 23.

“We’ve also got our Under-20s and our Under-19s as well, who are a very strong age group.

“We just have to make sure that our squad is very competitive. It has to be a difficult squad to get into, but also to stay in that squad. It’s got to be a challenge as well.”.

Ethan Nwaneri and Mikey Moore have both taken the opportunity to continue their development away from English football this season.

While Carsley stopped short of encouraging more young English players to move abroad, he believes both youngsters have benefited from stepping outside their comfort zones.

It has been revealed that “I definitely wouldn’t encourage players. I think every player’s got an individual path that they have to go on.

“The two players that you’ve mentioned there, I think it’s really helped their development to move away from home and their comfort of being at home, to learn another language, to play another style of play.

“I was really impressed with Mikey on the last camp, as I was with Ethan.”.

Despite being handed a challenging group featuring France, Poland and co-hosts Serbia, Carsley insists England’s expectations have not changed following their successive European Championship triumphs.

The former England interim manager believes his players have a unique opportunity to create their own legacy next summer.

“I think you come to terms when you’re an England coach that expectation is very high every game, whether that’s a friendly or whether that’s a tournament.

“If you’re going to win a tournament, as we have done in the last two previous tournaments, you have to beat the best teams.”.

Asked about his excitement surrounding the next generation of English talent and the opportunity to win a third consecutive European Championship, Carsley added:.

“The players that I’m privileged to coach and work with and select have got a great opportunity now to make history of winning the tournament three times in a row in this format, and creating our own history and legacy within the Under-21s.

“To play a small part of that is something that we’re looking forward to.”.
