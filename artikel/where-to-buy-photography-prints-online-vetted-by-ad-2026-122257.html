In a recent development, photos have the power to add personality to your home’s art collection, showcasing a distinct mood or point of view while boosting your space’s aesthetic. Alicia Keys and Swizz Beatz’s San Diego home is a masterclass in this craft. Among the antique African woodwork and abstract ceramics sit carefully placed Gordon Parks photographs that layer in a latest, thought-provoking dimension to the artwork in their home.

This ability to infuse varied mediums in your house isn’t reserved for just the Grammy-winning artists of the world; as long as you know where to look, weaving photography into your home is quite an attainable undertaking. Below, we’ve rounded up our favorite places to buy prints online. Whether you’re searching for a one-of-one vintage photograph, a tool to help you exhibit your own snaps, or a photo collection that removes the guesswork from the curation process, these sites can help you add a personal touch to your interiors.

Want to display high-quality artistic photographs without breaking the bank? Desenio can help. The site offers contemporary and abstract art prints that can add visual interest to modern interiors, and many of the pieces cost far less than $100. Add natural sophistication with a dramatic leaf print or evoke a sense of intimacy with a close-up portrait of the human form. With multiple sizes and frames available, you can snag a couple of smaller photos to complete your gallery wall, or make a statement with one large print to hang over a bed or sofa.

In a recent development, it’s a shame for those candid moments among friends and stunning vacation sunsets to stay trapped and largely unseen in your phone’s camera roll. That’s where Framebridge comes in, printing your images onto high-quality photo paper and encasing them in custom frames. The company also offers photo layouts with life-size hanging guides to help you wall-mount your images with the accuracy and precision that they deserve. Essentially, Framebridge streamlines the entire framing process to be easy and accessible, elevating family photos and artistic stills alike.

If you’re a fine art aficionado who is hoping to track down rare finds, 1stDibs is the platform for you. While it also sells vintage and designer furniture, the fine art photography collection is unmatched. You can purchase pieces from legendary fashion photographers, like this signed portrait of Whoopi Goldberg by Annie Leibovitz, as well as avant-garde photographs like “Africaine, No. 2,” by Shawn Theodore. With photographs and photo books spanning styles and price points, it’s easy to get lost in a sea of beautiful images. Use the brand’s curated pages to narrow your search to specific focuses, like color photography or figurative photography.

If you’re a homesick West Coaster or an East Coaster with California dreams, your next piece should come from Lndn Gray. Peruse the site for landscape photography that embodies the surrealist loneliness of the Pacific shores or for a still life that romanticizes the raucous rock-star culture of Los Angeles. You can select from a variety of print sizes and frame options. Add a decidedly Western flair by pairing these prints with your favorite cowhide rug or leather couch.

Artfully Walls is the perfect destination for those who don’t know where to start their search for high-quality, wall-enhancing prints. It features a huge volume of works from a trove of emerging artists worldwide. If a high level of choice overwhelms you, or you simply want an expert’s opinion, take advantage of Artfully Walls’ collections of ready-to-hang art, curated by designers and industry professionals. Louisa Tratalos, founder of British interior design brand Colours of Arley, has an Artfully Walls collection that includes this print featuring colorful, Scandinavian-style architecture by Dutch photographer Sander Groenendijk. Or browse picks selected by Justin Huxol of Huxhux Designs, including this whimsical “sponge cake” photo by Julie Vielvoije.
