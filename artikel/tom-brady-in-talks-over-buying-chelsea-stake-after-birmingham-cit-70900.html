According to recent reports, tom Brady is in discussions about buying a small stake in Chelsea. The NFL legend sold a minority share in Birmingham City this month after three years, a move sources said was to pave the way for investing at Stamford Bridge.

Brady was a guest of Chelsea when they won the Club World Cup in New Jersey last year and was seen celebrating with the players after their remarkable 3-0 final win over Paris Saint-Germain.

The seven-time Super Bowl winner is understood to have a good relationship with Behdad Eghbali, whose fund, Clearlake Capital, owns 86.5% of Chelsea after Todd Boehly and Mark Walter sold their shares in September.

Brady bought into Birmingham in August 2023, less than a year after his second and final retirement from the NFL, after the club’s takeover by Tom Wagner’s Knighthead Capital.

Brady was appointed chair of the club’s advisory board and had a prominent role in the 2025 Amazon Prime documentary Built in Birmingham: Brady & The Blues, which made headlines when he questioned the “work ethic” of Wayne Rooney, who was the manager.

In announcing Brady’s departure the club stated: “Birmingham City Football Club expresses its sincere gratitude to Tom Brady for his involvement with and unwavering support for the club over the last three years.”.

Brady owns a minority stake in the NFL team Las Vegas Raiders, which he purchased alongside Wagner.
