Como beat Cagliari 2-0 with two headers from Yan Couto crosses, moving back into fourth place on 26 points. Juventus visit next and trail the side holding the last Champions League place by eight points.

Sources indicate that como beat Cagliari 2-0 at the Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia on Saturday, 21 November 2026. Both goals were headers, and Yan Couto crossed for both of them. The result lifted Fàbregas’ side back into fourth place. That is the last Champions League place, and Juventus are the next visitors.

In a recent development, ramón opened the scoring after 24 minutes. He met Couto’s cross from the right and headed past goalkeeper Caprile. Da Cunha, the captain, doubled the lead in the 56th minute. He headed in another Couto delivery from the centre of the area.

It has been revealed that fàbregas left Nico Paz and Perrone on the bench. The two Argentinians had rejoined the squad only hours before kick-off after international duty. Como play AEK in the Champions League on Tuesday. Even without the pair from the start, the hosts kept Cagliari out. Both men entered in the 57th minute, with Liberali and Milla making way respectively. It was Como’s first win since a defeat to Inter before the international break.

For Fàbregas, the performance was not perfect, but he was very pleased. Perrone had a minor problem, the coach explained, which ruled out a full 90 minutes for the midfielder.

It has been revealed that como have now won three Serie A home games in a row without conceding, which they last did in October and November 1984, according to Sportmediaset. Da Cunha has three goals in his last four games across Serie A and the Champions League. Fàbregas has never lost to Cagliari in five Serie A matches as a coach. He has won three of them and drawn two. Cagliari have lost each of their last four away league games. That is their first run of four straight away defeats in Serie A since November–December 2023.

Como have eight wins, two draws and two defeats from 12 games, according to Como 1907. They have scored 20 goals and conceded 10.

In that table, Juventus’ record reads five wins, three draws and three defeats. They have scored 15 goals and conceded 12. The gap to Como stood at eight points before Juventus’ game against Venezia.
