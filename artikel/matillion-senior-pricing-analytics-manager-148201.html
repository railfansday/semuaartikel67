Are you ready to shape the future of data?

Matillion is the company behind Maia, the AI Data Automation platform.

In the AI economy, demand for data is exploding. Manual data engineering can’t keep up. Maia fixes that by combining 15 years of data expertise with agentic AI to eliminate the manual work that slows data teams down. Organisations like Cisco, DocuSign, and Slack are already using it to deliver real business impact, faster.

We started in Manchester and now we’re global - with teams across the UK, US and India.

We are driven, curious, energetic people who move fast, think substantial and hold ourselves to a high standard. We’re here to make a dent in the universe bigger than ourselves. And we’re looking for people who want to be part of it.

As Senior Pricing & Analytics Manager in our Finance team (reporting to the VP, Strategic Finance), you will develop and optimise Matillion’s pricing and packaging strategies to drive revenue growth and customer satisfaction.

Combining data-driven analysis, competitive research, and customer usage and behaviour insights, you'll inform strategic pricing and packaging decisions across the business. You'll bring a strategic mindset and strong communication skills to partner closely with Product, Marketing, Sales, and Finance teams—turning complex analytical findings into clear commercial recommendations.

Pricing Strategy: Stay at the forefront of consumption-based pricing trends across the B2B SaaS and agentic landscape, partnering with Product, Marketing, and Sales leadership to design, evaluate, and refine product packaging, feature bundling, and pricing tiers.

Market & Competitive Insights: Continuously evaluate market landscapes, competitor packaging strategies, and customer usage patterns to formulate actionable recommendations for value capture, monetisation, and revenue expansion.

Pricing Analytics & Planning: Lead the pricing operations cadence, conducting profitability, price elasticity, and financial modelling alongside customer consumption and churn analysis to optimise margins and inform renewal and migration models.

Sales & Field Enablement: Design and iterate on innovative pricing structures and equip Sales and field teams with ROI calculators, deal tools, and enablement resources to navigate complex enterprise negotiations.

Pricing Governance & Operations: Establish corporate governance frameworks and discounting policies in collaboration with Deal Desk and Sales, building executive dashboards and audit protocols to ensure pricing integrity and consistency across regions.

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, Data Analytics, or a related field (Master’s preferred).

Demonstrable experience in pricing strategy, data analytics, or similar roles within SaaS, technology, or consulting.

Strong proficiency in data analysis tools (such as Excel, SQL, Python, and BI tools like Tableau, Looker, or ThoughtSpot).

Experience with SaaS pricing models (including subscription and consumption-based models).

Strong business acumen with hands-on experience conducting market and competitive analysis.

Excellent problem-solving and project management skills, with a track record of working cross-functionally in a fast-paced environment.

Excellent communication skills, with the ability to effectively convey complex technical and analytical concepts to diverse and non-technical stakeholders.

Bonus: Knowledge of behavioural economics or pricing psychology principles.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
