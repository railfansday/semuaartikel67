📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

WhatsApp is adding support for eSIMs in its app, designed for keeping you connected while traveling, though details are not fully clear.

In a brief blog post, WhatsApp indicates it will be adding data-only eSIM services to its app soon. Owners will be able to buy service and install the eSIM directly from WhatsApp, keeping the messaging app connected in another country without incurring pricey carrier roaming fees.

It has been revealed that whatsApp eSIMs will use providers such as Verizon and Jio, but exact details are unclear. There’s no word on pricing yet, and the functionality is only arriving “soon,” with no specific launch date announced yet.

In a recent development, to make this possible, we’re working with e-SIM providers and telco partners including Jio, Smart Axiata, Smart Communications, Verizon and others, and we’ll begin rolling this out in select countries soon. You’ll see it right in the app when it’s available for you.
