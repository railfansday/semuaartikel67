📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, when you first get started in Grand Theft Auto 6, Jason isn't exactly the most fiscally responsible person, and one of the first things you learn is that he's been keeping his money underneath the floorboards in his bedroom. Throughout the first chapter, you can actually deposit money into the floorboard stash anytime, and up until you open up your BuckMe account, this is really the only means of storing your money. However, players who are putting away a ton of cash in Jason's house are in for a rude awakening.

!!! WARNING: THIS GUIDE CONTAINS SPOILERS FOR CHAPTER 2 OF GRAND THEFT AUTO 6 !!!

Without giving too many story details away, players will essentially start Chapter 2 with almost no money due to the climactic ending of Chapter 1. If you're wondering where all the money went that you'd been storing in Jason's floorboards, I've got bad news for you: it's all gone.

In a recent development, you can return to Jason's house at the start of Chapter 2, but any money you deposited in the floorboard safe will no longer be there. It's a major blow that players likely won't be expecting, and if you've been planning on spending that money later down the road, the story is designed in a way that you really won't be able to recover those funds.

In a recent development, instead of depositing money into the floorboards at Jason's house, my advice is to invest as much money as you can during Chapter 1 into upgrades, gear, etc., and max out your BuckMe family savings account. Any money you don't spend will essentially be lost when you start Chapter 2, and the story is largely rooted in Lucia and Jason starting from scratch and climbing their way back from the bottom by doing odd jobs across Leonida.

That remarked, starting in Chapter 2, you will finally be able to claim the "Rainy Day Funds" envelope that's under the floorboards at Jason's house. This is, of course, a separate stash of money than the cash you earned and deposited there during Chapter 1, and was unaffected by the events of Chapter 2's conclusion despite the cash sitting right next to it being lost.

The "Rainy Day Fund" envelope contains $5000 and will come in very useful at the start of Chapter 2, but getting to Jason's house from the motel in Chapter 2 is quite a trek. Additionally, you'll be able to collect the family photos of Lucia and Jason that were in the box, and if you were worried about Lucia and Jason's pet snake Delores, she's safe and sound under the stairs and is sitting on a little surprise as well.

Further, if you're planning to return to Jason's house early in Grand Theft Auto 6 Chapter 2, note that it's heavily guarded by police and you'll need to use stealth in order to claim the "Rainy Day Fund" envelope. If you're being chased or alert the police, they'll shoot on site and you will not be able to grab the money or pictures.
