📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that U.S.-Canada trade is a little like gravity. You can push against it, but the size and proximity of the U.S. economy and the convenience of a G-7 market like Canada always pull us back together.

According to recent reports, trade negotiations between the two countries had been suspended, but the midterm results may be an opportunity to press forward as Democratic wins in both the House of Representatives and Senate shift the political calculus. President Donald Trump seemed to warm to the idea of a deal on the campaign trail. Some analysts are suggesting a deal is imminent, and Prime Minister Mark Carney noted Canadian and U.S. teams would be working to deliver a deal “in the coming weeks” following a “good conversation” with Trump.

Hendrik Brakel is the former chief economist of the Canadian Chamber of Commerce and former chief economic adviser to the leader of Canada’s Official Opposition.
