Tech Report: Box Office: ‘Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping’ Scores $21 Million in Previews, Second-Best Result of 2026

Audiences don’t know how to quit Panem.

Industry observers note that ““The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” earned $21 million in Thursday previews and early screenings on Wednesday and is expected to dominate the box office this weekend. Analysts believe it will earn north of $100 million. The preview figures are the second-best of 2026, behind “Spider-Man: Brand Latest Day’s” massive $72 million haul. It’s also the second-best for the “Hunger Games” franchise, just behind the $25.5 million that “Catching Fire” earned in 2013. It’s a massive improvement on the last “Hunger Games” prequel, “The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes,” which made $5.75 million in previews and kicked off with $44 million. That film would gross over $166 million domestically and nearly $364 million globally, figures that “Sunrise on the Reaping” is on pace to easily eclipse.

Industry observers note that “Sunrise on the Reaping” is based on Suzanne Collins’ bestselling novel of the same name. It tells the origin story of Haymitch Abernathy, the alcoholic former Hunger Games victor played by Woody Harrelson in the films. The sequel checks in with him at 16 as he’s forced to compete in gladiatorial combat. “East of Eden” breakout Joseph Zada plays Haymitch. He’s surrounded by an all-star cast that includes Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Kieran Culkin, Elle Fanning, Mckenna Grace, and Maya Hawke. Francis Lawrence, who helmed most of the series’ films, returns as director. Lionsgate, which is having a strong year at the box office thanks to “Michael,” is backing the “Hunger Games” sequel as it has the previous films.

The biggest competition for “Sunrise on the Reaping” this weekend isn’t another major movie, but the release of “Grand Theft Auto 6,” which is expected to be a phenomenon.
