Model Gigi Paris has declared her engagement to former Seattle Seahawks tight end Cooper Helfet.

“[I] mentioned yes to the man of my dreams,” Paris, 34, revealed via Instagram on Thursday, November 26.

The couple got engaged during a Thanksgiving week trip to Carcassonne, France, a historic European city known for its medieval architecture.

It has been revealed that “[We got engaged] on a mountaintop overlooking my family’s history, with a lifetime of our own ahead of us,” she revealed of his romantic proposal. “Feeling like the luckiest girl in the world. Je t’aime toujours @coophelfet.”.

The certified yoga instructor posed in front of a medieval citadel with her beau, writing, “My fiancé!”.

In a recent development, paris and Helfet, 37, also shared multiple Instagram Stories showing them announcing their big news to family and friends. The pair were congratulated on their engagement by former Love Island host Sarah Hyland, Southern Charm‘s Naomie Olindo and former Vampire Diaries star Claire Holt, among others.

Olindo, 34, wrote to the bride-to-be, “GIGI!!!! CONGRATS!!! Love you so so much!!!!”.

“Awww congrats,” Holt, 38, added.

It has been revealed that paris first stepped out with Helfet in September 2025 when they attended the 27th Annual Angel Ball in New York City. She later reposted a red carpet photo of the two via Instagram, calling her new boyfriend “papiiii.”.

Recent findings suggest that before dating Helfet, Paris was in a three-year relationship with Glen Powell, which ended in April 2023. Paris shared a cryptic message about knowing “your worth” and moving “onto the next” after unfollowing Powell, 38, via Instagram.

According to recent reports, during a June 2025 appearance on the “Too Much” podcast, Paris seemingly alluded to romance rumors between Powell and his Anyone But You costar Sydney Sweeney playing a part in her own breakup with the actor. (She did not directly name either Powell or Sweeney, 29.).

Recent findings suggest that “I had two options. I could either pretend like I was going along with everything and have everyone wonder, like, ‘Are they hooking up? Are they not hooking up? Is she OK with this? What the f***?'” Paris claimed. “Or stand up for myself and say, ‘No, I’m actually not OK with this, and I’m walking away.’ So that’s what I decided to do.”.

She remembered feeling “shattered” by the very public discourse about her breakup with Powell at the time.

“I just wanted respect, especially if it’s gonna be public,” she declared. “Like, don’t make an ass out of me. Like, just don’t make a fool out of someone you’ve been with for over three years talking about forever with. Just have some decency, you know?”.

Powell subsequently told GQ that same month that he will “always have nothing but love for her and respect” for Paris though he didn’t necessarily agree with the way she publicly framed their breakup.
