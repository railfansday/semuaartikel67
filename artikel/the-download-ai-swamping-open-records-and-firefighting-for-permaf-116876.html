Sources indicate that AI has intensified investors' interest in public records. Over the last few years, venture-backed startups have raised upwards of $100 million to collect data from thousands of agencies and turn it into AI-powered "signals" that help businesses sell to governments. At the same time, chatbots are making it easier for individuals to file requests.

Recent findings suggest that as agencies struggle to tell whether they’re dealing with a citizen, a chatbot, or a sales pipeline, some states are passing legislation that makes it easier to deny requests. That could potentially make public offices less transparent.

According to recent reports, in July 2021, a wildfire burned through a forest on the University of Alaska, Fairbanks campus, stripping away the moss and soil insulating the permafrost below. Five years later, we followed researchers into the forest to investigate how fighting fires could help keep that carbon locked in the ground.

Their probe revealed an escalating problem: As climate change fuels more frequent and severe fires, they’re accelerating permafrost thaw and releasing greenhouse gases. Now, researchers are trying to break that cycle. But how much should we spend, and what risks are worth taking?
