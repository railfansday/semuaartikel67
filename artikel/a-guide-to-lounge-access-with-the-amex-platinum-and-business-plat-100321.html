A guide to lounge access with the Amex Platinum and Business Platinum

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (210 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Premium travel cards can provide access to a wide range of airport lounges worldwide, including Centurion, Delta Sky Club, Priority Pass, Escape, Plaza Premium and other partner lounges.
  • Lounge entry policies, guest access and amenities vary by network and location, with some lounges offering complimentary food, drinks and unique features like family rooms or table service.
  • Guest access at Centurion Lounges in the U.S. typically requires a fee unless a significant annual spending threshold is met, which unlocks complimentary guest privileges for a set period.
  • Access to Delta Sky Clubs is limited to a set number of annual visits unless a high spending requirement is achieved, and guest entry is available for a fee.
  • Lounge access requires presenting your eligible card, government-issued identification and a same-day boarding pass; some networks require separate enrollment.
  • Entry windows are enforced, such as the three-hour rule before departure for Centurion Lounges and similar timing for Delta Sky Clubs; arrival access is generally not permitted except during layovers.
  • Guest policies and fees differ by lounge network and may change based on annual spending or specific lounge rules.
