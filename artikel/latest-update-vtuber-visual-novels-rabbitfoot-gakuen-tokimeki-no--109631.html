Sources indicate that rabbitfoot has revealed Rabbitfoot Gakuen: Tokimeki no Oto and Rabbitfoot Gakuen: Yasuragi no Koe, a pair of visual novels starring ASMR Virtual YouTubers in an academy setting. It will launch for Switch and PC via Steam and DLsite in spring 2027, followed by PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, iOS, and Android. It will support English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and other language options.

Recent findings suggest that each game features stories with three heroines. Rabbitfoot Gakuen: Tokimeki no Oto features Toiki Yasou, Minoru Nakai, and Naco Mochizuki, and Rabbitfoot Gakuen: Yasuragi no Koe features Itigori Ena, Kumonoue Yumemi, and Meme Misumi.

An ASMR x romance x academy visual novel featuring popular Virtual YouTubers playing themselves.

The distance between you and the girls gradually grows smaller throughout your everyday school life. Enjoy sweet and sour moments spent alone together, and get a glimpse of sides of them you wouldn’t normally get to see…?

Your heart may race at the sound of their voices so close to your ears, or you may find yourself soothed by their gentle whispers.

With immersive binaural audio, enjoy special moments together with the girls.
