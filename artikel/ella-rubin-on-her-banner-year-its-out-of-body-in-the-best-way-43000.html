Industry observers note that it’s a good time to be Ella Rubin. The Sterling Point star has had the breakout year actors only dream of, after her role as Annie Jacobson in the hit Prime Video series released her to viral heights. “It’s out of body in the best way,” she tells ELLE. “It’s a one-in-a-million chance to get to work as an actor, especially on a show that you love and you’re proud of.” Also on her agenda? An intimate holiday dinner with Chanel to celebrate its watches and fine jewelry.

It has been revealed that on an unseasonably warm Tuesday night in Fresh York, the French fashion house hosted ambassadors, friends, and more at Daniel Humm’s new, already hard-to-reserve restaurant, HUMM. As she got ready for her outing, the 25-year-old was as relaxed as ever. In a downtown hotel room, a scene unfolded that any 20-something woman would recognize: “Wide Open Spaces” by The Chicks played on a speaker (a song Rubin calls her “Annie anthem”), a light girl dinner of French fries and soda splayed across a table, and a friend on hand for moral support.

Rubin, an Upper West Side native herself, knows that for a Manhattan night on the town, sometimes less is more. The actress opted for a black tweed jacket—in true Gabrielle Chanel fashion—from look 40 of the Métiers d’art 2026 collection. Paired with a simple white tank and perfectly fitting Levi’s, “it’s my ideal dinner look,” she says.

Industry observers note that in terms of accessories, Rubin wore a Chanel Première Ribbon watch, with diamonds elegantly placed around the face. “A beautiful gift,” she says, “and also something you’ll have forever, which is sort of the cornerstone of why I love Chanel jewelry so much, as it’s pieces you’ll know and love, and hopefully, give to someone someday.”.

Despite not having her ears pierced, Rubin’s accessories game remains strong. The simplicity of Rubin’s outfit allowed for her jewels to truly shine as she finished off her look with stacked Chanel Fine Jewelry Coco Crush ear cuffs and rings in 18K white gold and diamonds. For glam, makeup artist Shayna Goldberg kept things natural and dewy, starting with a seamless base of Chanel Beauty Les Beiges Healthy Glow Foundation, topped off with the Les Beiges Healthy Glow Natural Eyeshadow Palette and Les Beiges Mascara. The finishing touch? Rouge Coco Longwear Satin Lipstick in 124 Marie and Le Crayon Lèvres Precision Lip-Defining Pencil in Nude Brun.

It has been revealed that below, Rubin reflects on her busy year, plots her holiday gifting list—Chanel included, of course—and reveals who she’d most like to guest star on season 2 of Sterling Point. Someone get Julia Roberts on the phone, stat!

According to recent reports, I’m in a really lovely structured jacket, with this high kind of collar, and really beautiful buttons. They’re just so ornate and detailed. Because of the high neck, we went with a ponytail, sort of like, a 60’s influence. So, I’m really glad that I’m getting to explore that side of jewelry. And then, I love a watch, and I love rings. Those are sort of my staples, my favorites.

Now we’re just rocking with it, and it almost feels like, “Okay, this is just where we're at, and where we're staying.” But because Chanel makes such wonderful clip-ons, I get the opportunity to wear lovely earrings now.

In a recent development, it’s just so wild. It’s very surreal. It’s out of body in the best way, but also, I feel unexpectedly grounded, because I’m surrounded by wonderful people, but also, it means I get to work more, hopefully, and be more committed to the work I am doing, and feel more self-assured within it. So that's really the ultimate goal. I just feel unbelievably fortunate to be in the position to keep working. So it's pretty wonderful. It's solely wonderful, yeah. I’m just going to choose to keep enjoying it, and sharing it with the people I love.

I’ve been in the theater almost every single day. Catch me at any AMC or Film Forum.
