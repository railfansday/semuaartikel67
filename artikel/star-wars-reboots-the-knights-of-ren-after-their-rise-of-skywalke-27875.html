It has been revealed that star Wars has opened up the door to an exciting possible future for the Knights of Ren, after the Sequel Trilogy failed to fulfill their potential and then unceremoniously killed them all off in Rise of Skywalker. Those deaths are all still very much canon, but the franchise has thrown a canon curveball by revealing that those were not officially the Knights.

According to recent reports, record scratch moment. What? Then who were they? Well, the short answer is that they were the former Knights of Ren. In a latest piece of the Sequel era puzzle, Star Wars has shed more light on the Knights of Rens' fate, as an order. As it turns out, one did escape their fateful confrontation with Ben Solo and Rey Skywalker, and she is still out there waiting for the next chapter of her story to be written.

It has been revealed that as for the Knights that fell in Rise of Skywalker, they had been stripped of their titles by that point. Before breaking good, Kylo Ren declared the order all but disbanded, with only one true Knight of Ren remaining in the galaxy.

Warning! Spoilers ahead for Star Wars: Fall of Kylo Ren #4!

Recent findings suggest that star Wars fans were hyped for the Knights of Ren, latest villain Kylo Ren's personal bodyguard, when their designs were first released ahead of The Force Awakens. As the Sequel Trilogy played out, though, the Knights were reduced to barely there bit players, all but cut out of the movies. Now, the franchise has provided a self-aware reason why: the Knights of Ren were actually posers all along.

Sources indicate that star Wars has hinted at this before, but it is the point of The Fall of Kylo Ren #4, as the miniseries finds itself on the precipice of The Rise of Skywalker. In the glitch, the Knights try to get back in Kylo's good graces, conspiring to murder his rival for leadership of the group, Tava Ren. Instead, he steps back and waits to watch as she slaughters them. Only for Tava to declare that they aren't even worth the blood on her hands.

Recent findings suggest that tava spares them and takes off. After she leaves, Kylo strips the survivors of their "Ren" titles. "That's done," he says. They will still be his lackeys, and warm bodies for him to throw into combat, but they haven't earned the right to be Knights of Ren. According to Kylo, only Tava Ren remains true to the spirit of the group. But what does that mean for Tava's future, and for the Ren legacy?

Industry observers note that "For now...only one remains," Kylo notes of the Knights of Ren in The Fall #4. Star Wars fans know what happens next. He dies after returning to the light side as Ben Solo, but not before taking the henchmen formerly known as the Knights down first. What is unknown is what might become of Tava Ren in the future.

Industry observers note that remember, she's still committed to the Dark Side, as she sees fit to serve it. That could make her an antagonist for Rey Skywalker in future Star Wars stories. Or, like her former leader, it might mean a redemption arc. Could the Knights of Ren become a more grey, or even light side organization?

That's the canon question The Fall of Kylo Ren dangles out there as it wraps up the Knights of Rens' Sequel storyline, teasing they still have a future in the Star Wars galaxy.

Star Wars: Fall of Kylo Ren #4 is available now from Marvel Comics.
