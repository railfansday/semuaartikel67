An innovation campus, a corporate headquarters, a family home, and a community development in a remote peatland environment are the four winners of the Steel Architectural Awards ASEAN 2026. Located in Thailand and Indonesia, the projects explore how coated steel can respond to different climates, construction conditions, and patterns of use. The winners were revealed at the ASEAN Awards Ceremony in Bangkok, Thailand, on November 20, 2026. They were selected from 12 finalists representing Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, and Vietnam, following awards programs in each country.

Under the theme "Shaping Resilient Futures: Timeless Design with Coated Steel," the awards examined how architectural ideas, construction details, and material choices contribute to a building's capacity to remain in use over time. Entries were assessed on their response to context and climate, technical resolution, innovation, and long-term material performance. Design Excellence accounted for 50% of the evaluation, Innovation for 30%, and Sustainability for 20%.

Desa Binaan Tampelas, IndonesiaArchitect: PT Pandega Desain Weharima (PDW).
