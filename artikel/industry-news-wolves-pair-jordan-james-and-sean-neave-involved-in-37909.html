Wolves duo Jordan James and Sean Neave were seen singing anti-West Brom chants at the Grand Slam of Darts in the city.

It has been an exciting start to the Championship season, with the Black Country rivals in the same league for the first time in five years.

And, they’ve both had a positive start to the season, with Cesar Peixoto’s side fourth in the table, and they’re only ahead of Albion on goals scored.

So, whilst there has always been a bitter rivalry between the two clubs, it is heightened this year, and Wanderers have the early bragging rights, having overcome Albion at Molineux by a single Fer Lopez goal back in September.

Such is the animosity between the two clubs, both sets of supporters can be heard singing about the other club at games every weekend.

However, it seems that it has now extended to the Grand Slam of Darts, which is being held in Wolverhampton this week.

That’s because, in a TikTok post uploaded by jackydangerbeard, shared on X by Talking Wolves, those inside the venue can be heard singing ‘**** the Albion’, with the footage showing that Neave and James are joining in.

As outlined above, this year has the potential to really ignite the rivalry between the two clubs, as it’s looking like both will be in the mix for promotion.

For Wolves, that won’t come as a surprise, as they were expected to be competing for automatic promotion following their relegation from the Premier League.

Albion are defying the pre-season predictions right now by pushing for a top two place though, but James Morrison will have full belief that his side can last the distance.

Having the two sides battling for promotion is nothing latest at this level, and it’s the Baggies that have had the upper hand over the years.

They overcame a ten-point gap in the final months of the 2001/02 campaign to finish in the top two, whilst they also beat Wolves over two legs in the 2006/07 play-offs, although they did then lose the final.

So, Wolves will feel they owe their rivals one this season, and it could add another dynamic to the rivalry if the two clubs are near the top of the table later in the season.

It’s good to see the players enjoying themselves on a night out, and most Albion fans will recognise that it’s part of the banter that exists between the two clubs.

James is right now on loan at Wolves from Rennes, with the club having an option to buy, and Neave signed from Newcastle for this season.

Despite the pair not being permanent signings, they have clearly bought into what the Wolves, West Brom rivalry is all about, and it’s no surprise that fans of the Old Gold enjoyed their players getting involved with the chant.
