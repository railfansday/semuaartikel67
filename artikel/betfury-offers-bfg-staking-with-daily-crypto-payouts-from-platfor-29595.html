It is one of the platform’s core utilities, rewarding holders simply for keeping the token on their balance.

Together, the two tracks let holders earn on the token they already hold and on mainstream coins from the same account.

According to recent reports, “Most staking asks you to lock your tokens and bet on emissions. We built ours on the opposite idea,” noted the CPO of BetFury. “BFG holders earn a daily share of real platform revenue, in stablecoin if they prefer, and on the standard tier they can unstake whenever they like. It turns simply holding the token into a steady, transparent income.”.

It has been revealed that staking sits in the same account as the rest of BetFury. Players earn BFG by wagering, stake it for daily payouts, and can move those payouts straight into Futures, Crypto Swap, or casino games without leaving the platform. The pools are replenished from the platform’s gaming revenue, so activity across BetFury feeds back into staking rewards, while a monthly buyback and burn steadily reduces the BFG supply. It is a single ecosystem where one balance works across markets, staking, and play.
