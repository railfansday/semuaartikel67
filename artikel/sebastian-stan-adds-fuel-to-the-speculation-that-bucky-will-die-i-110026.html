Sebastian Stan adds fuel to the speculation that Bucky will die in Avengers: Doomsday: "Maybe it's time"

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Avengers: Doomsday is fast approaching, and everyone seems pretty convinced Bucky Barnes isn't making it out alive.

Sebastian Stan has a role in The Batman 2, which set off alarm bells for Marvel fans that the Winter Soldier might not be returning for more in the MCU – and now an upgrade from Stan himself only seems to seal the deal.

Sources indicate that "I'm always happy to keep playing him. I don't know if the decision's up to me, ultimately," Stan noted on the Next Best Picture podcast.

When prompted by the interviewer that he must have some sway, Stan mentioned: "Well, I don't know, to be honest with you. I'm ready to take the character in all kinds of different ways, but I can't make that decision, it's the powers that be. And I think they have a lot going on, they have a lot on their plate. Do I feel we've explored that character, there's nowhere else to go, I think we did our best? I do think we've done our best so far.

"I don't think that that character totally got to fully live out his arc in the way that he has in the comics for instance, or that I've wanted," he continued. "I've been a big fan of the whole Black Widow of it all. But that's not the only way, you could take him in all kinds of ways. I've spoken about this Sicario-esque paranoid thriller where he just recognizes this person on the street and thinks it's a torturer from Hydra, like back in the day, and he becomes obsessed and starts following the person and realizes the person has a family. And then he's like, 'Am I crazy or is this the guy…?' and confronts the person, the guy denies it.

"Kevin just sort of went, "Sounds good, uh, we'll see,'" he added with a laugh. "So, I'm always ready, but we're at that point now where I can feel it, he needs to either evolve somewhere, or maybe it's time. It's one of those.".

Now, while it sounds like Stan still has tons of ideas when it comes to Bucky Barnes, it also certainly sounds like he's made peace with leaving the character behind. We'll find out when Avengers: Doomsday finally arrives on December 18.
