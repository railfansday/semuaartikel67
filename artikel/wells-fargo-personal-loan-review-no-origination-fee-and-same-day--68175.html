Industry observers note that get a Wells Fargo personal loan if you’ve been a customer there for at least a year and have good credit. It takes no origination fee out of the loan and usually pays out the day you sign. Check your rate before you apply, since that check won’t affect your credit score.

Recent findings suggest that on a $15,000 debt consolidation loan, choosing Wells Fargo over a lender such as Upgrade, which takes a 1.85% to 9.99% origination fee out of each loan, saves $278 to $1,499 in up-front fees, by our math. Only customers with a qualifying Wells Fargo product open at least 12 months can apply, and the lowest rate assumes excellent credit.

Sources indicate that wells Fargo customers with good credit pay no origination fee here and usually have their money the day they sign, so they should take this loan unless another lender prequalifies them at a lower APR. Anyone who hasn’t been a Wells Fargo customer for a year, or needs a co-borrower to qualify, should check SoFi, which requires no fees and takes co-borrower applications.
