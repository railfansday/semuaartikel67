We know Galaxy AI subscriptions are coming soon, but the details have been kept under wraps. A latest leak notes Now Brief’s custom cards are the next targeted feature to be adjusted, even though it was assumed only new features would require a subscription.

When Samsung’s paid Galaxy AI initiative leaked, only a couple of details were made public. For starters, the plan will reportedly cost $5/month and add new features like Bixy Action, Daily Briefing, and Gen Video. Those new features would require a paid subscription, but existing ones would be left alone, right?

According to recent reports, it’s a bit of both, apparently. A leaked screenshot reveals further information on the Custom Cards feature under Samsung’s Now Brief umbrella (via SamMobile). For the time being, Custom Cards are limited to three. The screenshot reveals Galaxy AI Premium will allow customers to create and edit more.

It has been revealed that custom Cards act as a sort of task for the Galaxy AI model, making it possible for users to set up complex widgets that appear in Now Brief. That ranges from weather to health information for very specific use cases. It’s a tailored widget – useful, but far from groundbreaking.

But even with the upped Custom Card limit, Samsung’s Galaxy AI Premium subscription doesn’t appear to equate to “unlimited.” The screengrab notes this:.

In a recent development, creating and editing cards uses Al Initiative credits. You can only create and edit cards if you have enough credits remaining.

Credits are shared across devices signed in to your Samsung account.

The Galaxy AI Premium initiative will let users buy “credit packs” on top of the $5/month subscription if they run out. It’s unclear how many credits Custom Card creation eats up.

Technically, this falls under “new feature,” since Custom Cards have long been limited. Still, it would have been nice if Samsung expanded the ability as Galaxy AI has improved in lockstep with other models.

Industry observers note that we don’t know what the majority of Galaxy AI Premium looks like beyond these few details, but we likely will soon. In any case, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 has been an excellent device for those of us who hardly use Galaxy AI.
