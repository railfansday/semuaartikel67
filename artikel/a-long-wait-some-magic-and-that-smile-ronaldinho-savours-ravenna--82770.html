Brazilian legend, an investor at Serie C club, revealed glimpses of old brilliance as he played 45 minutes for the promotion-chasing side.

In a recent development, it may have been the longest buildup in world football. In June, the former Barcelona and Brazil legend Ronaldinho was unveiled by the Serie C side Ravenna to make his comeback at the age of 46 – 11 years after his last official game. Then silence.

But on a chilly November day on the eastern coast of Italy, there he was, not only in the squad, but in the starting XI for the eagerly anticipated derby against Forlì as Ravenna chase promotion.

Recent findings suggest that the Ravenna manager, Andrea Mandorlini, deployed Ronaldinho in a central position, just behind the striker Simone Rabbi. The Brazilian held his position well, but was slow to get going. As the game progressed, he warmed up and showed his excellent skills; some things do not appear to change. Ronaldinho touched the ball 25 times, took three corners – one of which nearly led to a Ravenna goal – and initiated the move that led to the hosts taking the lead in the 35th minute.

According to recent reports, having trained with the team just once, whether he plays again remains to be seen. But he had already sprinkled some magic on a club and a region that has not made headlines in years. He played the first half as Ravenna won 2-0 to stay second in the division’s Group B (the Italian third tier is divided into three regional sections). Only the group winners earn promotion and Ravenna trail Spezia by six points.

It has been revealed that whatever happens for the remainder of the season this has put the club back on the football map. Ravenna’s highest finish was eighth in Serie B, in 1996-97, but this match was broadcast live by Sky Sports in Italy and shown in several other countries. It even appeared on the giant screens in Newly introduced York’s Times Square, where the club’s owners, the Cipriani family, have built much of their international reputation through Harry’s Bar.

Recent findings suggest that the town’s mayor, Alessandro Barattoni, who, as always, was watching the game from the stands, stated: “A champion like Ronaldinho, a world football icon, brings great visibility and today is an opportunity for us to showcase Ravenna around the world, from our stadium. It is something upcoming for us and together with the club the municipality will try to understand how this can have a positive impact on the town.”.

It has been revealed that the local community had been excited at the prospect of Ronaldinho representing the club, but there was some regret, too. “It would have been nice if he had come to take a stroll around the city, among the people who live here and own the shops,” the barista Barbara mentioned before the game.

Sources indicate that so how did he end up here? The Brazilian is a friend of Ignazio Cipriani, the owner of the Giallorossi. Lorenzo Tonetti and Ariedo Braida, old acquaintances from Ronaldinho’s time at Milan, are also part of the club. They convinced him to become an investor in the club and to pull on his boots again. In addition, Ravenna are now wearing his R10 sportswear line and one of the stands bears the same name.

Ravenna are aiming high. Cipriani has never hidden the fact he is aiming for Serie A, which is ambitious for a city with a population of 156,000. And Sunday brought hope, Dinho stepping on to the pitch in a gold-trimmed kit picked by the club to celebrate his arrival (and available for sale at €120). His hair was kept in check by a black headband and he was wearing gloves on a chilly afternoon. On his back, the No 10, something more than just a number for him, the man who became a global hero thanks to his extraordinary skill and contagious smile.

A younger generation has been able to discover Ronaldinho’s genius through the endless supply of online videos showcasing his greatest moments. Children who have never seen him play know his tricks and signature moves, while their parents can relive an era of football they may feel no longer exists.

“Dad, was he strong?” a young girl asked her father as they walked hand in hand towards the stadium. “Yes, he was strong. Actually, he is strong,” he corrected himself.

According to recent reports, one family had left Rome at 5am and travelled the 350km north just because their son had watched Ronaldinho on YouTube and wanted to see him live. Others had travelled even further, with supporters arriving from Turkey and Canada.

In a recent development, ronaldinho hahas scored 299 professional goals, but his landmark 300th on his return was not to be. However, in front of a record attendance of 6,743 the way he initiated the move for Giulio Donati’s goal was ample reward for those who had turned up. Donati made his Serie A debut on 29 August 2010 for Lecce, at San Siro, against Milan, marking the Brazilian that day.

As Ronaldinho walked off at half-time, he was surrounded by a media scrum. After the game, he spoke of his desire to play again. “It all went by too promptly,” he noted. “Football is like that, it’s always been like that for me.”.

Recent findings suggest that who knows, perhaps he enjoyed himself so much that he wants to extend his career even further. “I don’t know, but if the president calls me, I’ll come back. It’s always a pleasure to play, I told him: ‘when he needs me, I’m there’.”.

Sources indicate that mandorlini was effusive too. “We’re here and we’re truly proud to have been the architects of this match you played in,” he told the Brazilian. “So, if you want to play another one, that’s fine.”.

Sources indicate that who knows what the future holds? For now, Ravenna fans will always have this: a day when a true legend connected people from around the world and allowed them, for one afternoon, to dream.
