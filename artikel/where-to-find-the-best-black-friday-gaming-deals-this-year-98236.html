It has been revealed that it's safe to say that Black Friday isn't nearly the same massive event as it used to be, with plenty of outlets offering mediocre discounts or even flat-out lying about what discounts they have to offer. Thankfully, gamers are getting more than a few generous discounts this year for PlayStation, Xbox, PC, Nintendo Switch, and even some physical hardware to top it all off.

While some deals can only be found by going out of your way to dig online, several of the best discounts just went live from none other than Walmart, alongside several other sites detailed here to help you get the most out of your money before the holiday season.

While Nintendo isn't a stranger to offering some hefty Nintendo discounts, several brand-upcoming Nintendo games and even the Switch 2 itself are getting some record-low price reductions as a part of their newest Black Friday deals that went live this morning, as noted by Nintendeal on X.

Although some of the older titles being offered aren't much of a surprise, Walmart's limited-time sale has dropped several triple-A titles as recent as The Blood of Dawnwalker down by over 50%, with some of the most notable discounts being for the following games among numerous others:.

It has been revealed that unfortunately, several of the games have already sold out or are at the moment unavailable, leaving fans waiting for the chance of a restock. If you run out of luck on the gaming side of things, Walmart's deals are also offering some hefty discounts for hardware as well, with controllers and even Lego being discounted while supplies last.

While primarily used for Nintendo releases, the gaming site DekuDeals is also an excellent way to keep an eye out for any latest sales, which has an updated Black Friday Deals page that highlights all the current games currently at a discount, where to buy them, and also compares them to previous prices to let you know whether you're getting the best possible deal or not.

Countless games on the site are also being listed at their highest discounts yet, primarily through digital platforms, meaning you won't be under as much pressure to buy them before all the stock runs out as with Walmart.

While it's a bit harder to sort with the sheer number of games being offered, some notable titles include the Red Dead Redemption remaster, Sonic X Shadow Generations, the Skywalker Saga, Devil May Cry 5, and hundreds of others either matching or below their lowest sales prices.
