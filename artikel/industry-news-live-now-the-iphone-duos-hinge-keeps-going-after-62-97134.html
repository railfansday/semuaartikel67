According to recent reports, as we continue our fifth week of folding Apple’s iPhone Duo in our custom Fold-a-Phone machine, the $1,999 foldable phone has surpassed 627,000 folds and it’s showing. While the Duo is still capable of opening and closing, we saw during our fourth week of testing that sometimes the Duo has difficulty staying closed. While this appears to have resolved, it’s clear that stress is mounting on the Duo’s hinge.

In a recent development, you can watch as we continue folding the iPhone Duo on our YouTube channel, embedded above. At our CNET Labs location in Louisville, Kentucky, this robust custom-built machine has been opening and closing the foldable phone around 20,000 times per day to simulate years of wear and tear on the hinge.

The livestream isn’t just for watching a phone open and close — you’ll also be able to chat with CNET editors who will be available to answer your iPhone Duo questions live in the chat.

Industry observers note that will Apple’s first foldable live up to the likes of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold? Will its neat opening animation remain slick after half a million folds? Join us and find out.
