This is just a very quick Sunday post to say thank you to everyone who reached out after yesterday’s blog. I have been – in the very best way possible – overwhelmed with messages, comments, emails, DMs here there and everywhere, and so much more.

It has been revealed that your kindness and good wishes have been genuinely so helpful and uplifting. A little bit of light at the end of what was a dark week at times (at least in certain corners of my stupid brain anyway!). I have barely scratched the surface of them, so my job today is to sit down and go through them to give them the attention and the consideration they deserve.

I honestly don’t think I can reply to them all, but please know I will have read them and taken everything you wished to convey in positive fashion!

I know there’s a little bit of football stuff going on, but I think I’ll save that for tomorrow, it is an Interlull after all, and we can round up the Arsenal related stuff in the blog, and on the Arsecast Extra too.

For now, have a great Sunday everyone. And thank you all again, it means so much to feel that love and support.
