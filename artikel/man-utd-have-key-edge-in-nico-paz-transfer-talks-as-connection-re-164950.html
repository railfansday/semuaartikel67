Manchester United have a potential ace up their sleeve in transfer talks over Nico Paz.

Argentine star Paz is enjoying a burgeoning reputation across Europe after a remarkable start to the season with Como, which has seen him score six goals from attacking midfield - including two against Man Utd in the Champions League last month.

Paz joined Como in 2024 from Real Madrid in a deal worth around £5million, and it had long been expected that the player would return to the Spanish giants, who hold an €80m (£67m) buy-back clause for Paz.

But The Athletic report that Paz does not right now favour a return to Spain, where he will not be a key player, and is stated to be attracted by the prospect of joining Man Utd who need a long-term replacement for captain Bruno Fernandes.

The race for Paz is set to be fierce, but Man Utd will hope they have an advantage over rivals in the form of upcoming manager Mauricio Pochettino.

The Athletic also report that Pochettino, a fellow Argentine, shares agents with Paz, which could make transfer negotiations easier for all parties.

Man Utd are keen on the player and 22-year-old's agency connection to their head coach potentially a factor in any pursuit.

One sticking point is that Paz is likely to want to play in the Champions League, so qualification this season may prove key to his final destination.

Man Utd's interest comes amid uncertainty over Fernandes, who recently confirmed talks over a contract extension have been put on hold due to the club’s ongoing poor results.

Fernandes’s current deal runs until the end of the 2026-27 season, although Man Utd retain the option to extend it by a further year.

Paz is among the options being considered as Man Utd weigh up the possibility of Fernandes departing, but competition for the midfielder is expected to be fierce and the club are also assessing alternative targets.
