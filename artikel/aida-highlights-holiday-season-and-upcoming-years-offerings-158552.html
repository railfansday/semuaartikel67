AIDA has put the spotlight on its 2026 holiday cruises program.

The company mentioned that at Christmas, highlights will bring guests and crew together; they include a message from the captain, reflective entertainment, communal Christmas singing, and a toast to the festive season.

In a recent development, depending on the ship, local traditions or a special “Typhoon” moment complete the program. Cuisine onboard features a buffet, specialty, and à la carte, as well as special four- and six-course holiday menus.

Among the culinary highlights on Christmas Eve is the exclusive six-course holiday menu by Franz Schned at Rossini.

At the Buffalo Steak House or the Churrascaria, there will be a four-course holiday menu.

French Kiss will offer French-inspired festive flavors while Ocean’s adds maritime accents.

Mediterranean and international flavors round out the offering at Mamma Mia and Casa Nova.

Recent findings suggest that families can also look forward to Christmas crafts, the Clubbie Disco and a gift-giving celebration for the little ones. Christmas concerts, communal singing, a Christmas gala and festive sauna evenings will also be on offer.

The company added that there will also be onshore highlights in Oslo, Sweden’s Dyreparken and Tallinn.

For New Year’s Eve, the ship will offer guests fine dining for two, family time, an exuberant party and a relaxed end to the year.

Across the fleet, celebrations continue depending on the ship, with parties, DJs and late-night offerings.

Families can also shape the final evening of the year entirely at their own pace, the tech giant added.

At the Clubbie Latest Year’s Eve Disco, there will be music, dancing and children’s punch.

Anyone who stays awake until midnight welcomes 2027 together with all other guests on board during the massive countdown. Creative activities and a family Newly introduced Year’s celebration on January 1 round off the program.

Nine of AIDA’s eleven ships spend the midnight hours in port, and guests can welcome 2027 in Sydney, Singapore, Rotterdam, or Willemstad.
