By LEWIS BROWNING, SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 17:56 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 19:19 EST, 16 November 2026.

Liverpool midfielder Florian Wirtz scored a stunning brace for Germany as Jurgen Klopp's side thumped the Netherlands in the Nations League.

The 23-year-old has had a tough time of it at Anfield since his £116million move to the club last summer, but has never really struggled on the international stage.

And he continued that trend on Monday evening with two goals for his side to help them top their Nations League group.

His first was the pick of the bunch. Wirtz moved along the forward line as a Dutch side without Virgil van Dijk struggled to contain him. He picked up the ball around 25 yards out, cut inside onto his weaker left foot, and curled a pearling effort into the far corner off the post.

That goal, which came on the hour mark, put his side 2-0 up after Freiburg's Derry Scherhant had scored the opener, and Wirtz wasn't done there.

Centre back Nico Schlotterbeck made it 3-0 before the Liverpool man picked up his second of the night after drifting into the penalty box unmarked and finishing high after good work from Brajan Gruda.

Florian Wirtz scored a brace for Germany as they thumped the Netherlands 5-0 in Nations League action.

Liverpool midfielder Wirtz received a big hug from a smiling Jurgen Klopp at full time.

Gruda himself, who is on loan at RB Leipzig from Brighton, wrapped up the five-star win on a night that arguably saw Klopp announce himself as Germany boss.

After full-time, the ex-Liverpool manager gave Wirtz a huge hug as the duo beamed with major smiles on their faces.

While it was a night to remember for Germany, a Netherlands team that included the likes of Micky van de Ven, Jurrien Timber and Wirtz's Liverpool team-mate Cody Gakpo were left exasperated.

Van Dijk had pulled out of the squad with a knock after playing with a knock in recent weeks.

His side will still, though, compete in the Nations League quarter-finals after finishing second in the group, finishing ahead of Greece on goal difference.

As for Wirtz, he will hope his performance will help continue what has been an uphill climb so far this season.

He also found the net for Germany in their first match of the international break – a 3-1 win over Serbia – and picked up a goal and assist in Liverpool's 3-1 win over Crystal Palace before the international break.

In total, he has two goals and five assists in 15 games for Liverpool this season – though the Reds will hope those numbers increase as the season goes on.

His second goal came as he drifted into the penalty area and finished high into the net.

Last month, meanwhile, Reds manager Andoni Iraola insisted he has full faith in his midfielder.

'I think he has had a little bit of everything this season,' he said. 'He has had good games, probably the other day was not his best game, especially offensively. We trust Florian.

It has been revealed that 'Florian is a very good player. He was coming from good performances in the international break, I think. I think it is a matter of everyone to find his moment.'.

Klopp hailed him 'exceptional', meanwhile, during the last international break.
