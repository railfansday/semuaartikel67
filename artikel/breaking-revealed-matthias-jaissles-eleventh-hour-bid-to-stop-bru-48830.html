Matthias Jaissle made a last-ditch attempt to convince Bruno Guimaraes to snub offers from Arsenal and stay at Newcastle.

Guimaraes signed for Arsenal in a £75m deal in August - just three days after Jaissle’s appointment as Eddie Howe’s replacement - calling time on a four-year association with Newcastle, where he was a favourite of the club’s passionate fanbase.

Sources indicate that the Magpies were desperate to keep Guimaraes on Tyneside and dug their heels in to prevent the deal, but eventually gave in once it became clear the player had set his heart on the transfer. He has since scored twice in 14 appearances for the Gunners.

And, speaking ahead of Guimaraes’ first return to St James’ Park, Jaissle explained Guimaraes had already decided to leave before he was appointed Eddie Howe’s replacement.

But that did not stop him from trying to convince the Brazilian to stick around.

Recent findings suggest that “I met Bruno for a short time only,” the German explained. “But the decision was already made. I tried my best to persuade him but his decision was made.”.

Jaissle continued: “We counted on him as a leader last season, I just tried to explain the role he would have had with us.

According to recent reports, “The decision was already made, he informed me. I’d had the information beforehand, and I had been informed. It is my job to do my best anyway and at least I can look in the mirror and say I tried.”.

It remains to be seen what kind of reception Guimaraes will receive on return to Tyneside, but Harvey Barnes has already made clear that his former team-mates are keen to get one over on him.

Sources indicate that jaissle, though, called on his squad to keep their heads: “If you face the champions, that is motivation enough. We are playing at home in front of our fans; they can be the twelfth man. The fans can give us so much power and support. This is a great opportunity, a substantial challenge.”.
