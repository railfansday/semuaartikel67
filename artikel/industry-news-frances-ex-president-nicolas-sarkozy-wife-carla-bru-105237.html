Industry News: France's ex-president Nicolas Sarkozy, wife Carla Bruni to stand trial for alleged witness tampering

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that france's former president Nicolas Sarkozy and his wife, singer Carla Bruni-Sarkozy, are set to be tried in 2027 on charges of "criminal conspiracy with intent to commit fraud" as well as benefitting from witness tampering. The upcoming charges are related to the Sarkozy couple's actions during the case examining illegal campaign funding from Libyan strongman Muammar Gaddafi over which Sarkozy was imprisoned last year.
