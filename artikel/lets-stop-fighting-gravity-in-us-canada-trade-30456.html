U.S.-Canada trade is a little like gravity. You can push against it, but the size and proximity of the U.S. economy and the convenience of a G-7 market like Canada always pull us back together.

It has been revealed that trade negotiations between the two countries had been suspended, but the midterm results may be an opportunity to press forward as Democratic wins in both the House of Representatives and Senate shift the political calculus. President Donald Trump seemed to warm to the idea of a deal on the campaign trail. Some analysts are suggesting a deal is imminent, and Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canadian and U.S. teams would be working to deliver a deal “in the coming weeks” following a “good conversation” with Trump.

Hendrik Brakel is the former chief economist of the Canadian Chamber of Commerce and former chief economic adviser to the leader of Canada’s Official Opposition.

Daniel Dorman is the managing editor and director of operations at the Macdonald-Laurier Institute in Ottawa.
