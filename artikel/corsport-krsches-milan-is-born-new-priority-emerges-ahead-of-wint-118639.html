After months of rumours, AC Milan have finally got their man: Markus Krösche will soon officially become the new Head of Football.

Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) confirm that Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt have reached an agreement over the compensation to be paid for Krösche. Yesterday, the two clubs exchanged documents, and the Rossoneri will pay around €4m to land the German executive.

Now, Milan’s goal is to announce his arrival early so they can start working on the winter transfer window as early as December. The Rossoneri will be very active in January, as they need to strengthen their squad and address some glaring gaps.

It has been revealed that among their priorities, for example, is signing a left-back. Pervis Estupinan was kept in the summer at the request of Ruben Amorim but has not convinced in recent weeks and is now on his way out, while the coach sees Davide Bartesaghi as a more of a winger.

It has been revealed that gonçalo Inacio has been linked for weeks, but Amorim poured cold water on the idea on Sunday: “Inacio is a great player, but we have Pavlovic and Diawara. The latter is a young player who comes from the French second division. He cost us three million, but he’s responding, so we need to move forward and also consider other players in other positions.”.

In attack, Gerry Cardinale’s dream remains Real Madrid’s Endrick. Bruno Fernandes is also highly regarded for the attacking midfield role, having stated that he hasn’t entered into any negotiations regarding a renewal with Manchester United and could therefore leave in the summer.
