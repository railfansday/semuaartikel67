A rumored remake of The Simpsons: Hit & Run (the internet's most-wanted video game revival) may be in trouble.

Just earlier this year, Radical Entertainment made somewhat of a comeback as Fresh Radical Games - with the original studio's co-founder (Ian Wilkinson) and former developer (Tim Bennison) serving as New Radical's CEO and COO, respectively. The company best known for Prototype and the well-remembered open-world Simpsons game was back, in some sense.

Sources indicate that whatever project the studio was cooking hasn't been so lucky, it seems. Fresh Radical Games senior game designer Darren Evenson recently mentioned an upcoming project at the organization had been scrapped, and he's now looking for new work, per a LinkedIn post.

"It seems that not even a fantastic project with an absolutely incredible team is immune from the current state of the industry right now, as what we were working on at Fresh Radical Games (although mind-blowingly awesome) was part of a whole gaggle of cancellations," Evenson wrote. "A gaggle, I say!".

"Oh, you silly suits with spreadsheets... will you EVER learn? If you don't make the game, you can't make the money... and if you don't make the money, then you don't get the power... and... well, there's more to that quote, but I digress.".

"For the first time in my career, I am Available For Work and looking for a newly introduced exciting opportunity where I can make some fun games with some great people," he added. "Please reach out, shout out, or perform some interpretive dance if you know of an opportunity that a hip cat like me could groove to.".

Industry observers note that new Radical Games has never formally revealed its plans, but a couple clues pointed to something Simpsons-shaped. As VGC noted, Evenson's statement echoes a few popular lines from the iconic animated sitcom. "Will you ever learn" is suspiciously close to a particular Troy McClure quote, for one.

Then there's seemingly a Scarface riff in the developer's statement - one that was also parodied in The Simpsons, of course: "In America, first you get the sugar, then you get the power, then you get the women." It's worth noting Radical Entertainment did also make Scarface: The World Is Yours, so this one could go either way.

It has been revealed that but the most eyebrow-raising clue came from original Simpsons co-showrunner and creator Matt Groening. During a Disney D23 panel this summer, Groening and current showrunner Matt Selman were asked about the possibility of a Hit & Run comeback.

"I don't know [if] I'm speaking out of turn, I think the original game is coming back in some form," Groening mentioned at the time, before Selman swiftly chimed in: "Or not…".

Recent findings suggest that "Hit & Run is so interesting. I'm a thousand years old, and when I was in my mid to late 20s, I helped write Hit & Run," Selman noted back in March. "I had no idea it would become a cult game, a cult success. Of all the games, the thousands of Simpsons games... that one...".
