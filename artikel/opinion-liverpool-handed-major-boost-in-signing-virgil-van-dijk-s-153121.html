Virgil van Dijk is now in the final year of his Liverpool contract.

The Dutchman has been one of the Reds' best players since he joined in 2017 and, without him, Liverpool wouldn't have won half as much as they have.

However, by the time the 2027-28 season kicks off, the Dutchman will be 36 years old and will be entering the twilight stages of his career.

Indeed, he did hit his prime a bit later than most players so he could continue playing at the top level for a while.

But, because the Reds can't be sure of this, they're holding back on offering the Dutchman a latest deal for now.

Many different centre-backs have been linked with Liverpool as their next Virgil van Dijk but, there's one stand-out candidate who the Reds have just been handed a massive boost to sign.

Barcelona want to sign Ayden Heaven which could give Liverpool a clear run at Nico Schlotterbeck.

Liverpool, Barcelona and Manchester United have all been fairly intertwined with transfer links recently.

All three teams seem to want the same players in the same positions and, while one of them are bound to end up signing them, they'll drive the price up for each other which doesn't really make much sense.

However, since most of the world's biggest clubs all want to sign the best players, these links are rarely ever going to die down but, fortunately for Liverpool, Barcelona seem to have turned their attention away from Nico Schlotterbeck.

That is, if this latest upgrade from BBC Sport is to be believed.

"Fulham and Barcelona are among the clubs to have expressed an interest in Heaven, with both having tried to sign him when he decided to leave Arsenal," they wrote.

"The England U20s defender's preference is believed to be a loan move, though he would also entertain permanent offers.

In a recent development, "At United he has largely been used as a back-up option and, after enjoying a run in the team under former manager Ruben Amorim, he lost his place under Michael Carrick. He returned to the side only after an injury crisis hit the club last season.

"Heaven is also capable of playing at left-back and in midfield, but does not expect to be trusted as a regular starter amid the instability at Old Trafford, with the club 19th in the league.

"He is under contract until 2029 in a deal that includes an option for an extra year.".

Nico Schlotterbeck is who Liverpool need to replace Virgil van Dijk.

One of the best things about Schlotterbeck is the fact that he's left-footed.

The German can also be used as a centre-back or a left-back if needed.

His entire career has been spent in Germany but, most of his experience of competitions such as the Champions League have come with Borussia Dortmund.

Last summer, he signed a newly introduced contract with the German outfit but, there is a release clause in his contract for certain clubs across the globe, including Liverpool.

The exact price of this clause is unknown but, the fact that Liverpool are one of them means that he clearly wants to make the move.

He's also only 26 years old so, even though he has plenty of experience under his belt, there's still room for him to grow.

Here's hoping that Liverpool get this deal over the line, especially now that Barcelona seem to be turning their attention away from him.
