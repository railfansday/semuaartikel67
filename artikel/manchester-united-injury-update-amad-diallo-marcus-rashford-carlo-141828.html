Amad Diallo appeared to pick up a knock as Manchester United were held to a dramatic 2-2 draw by Sporting on Wednesday night.

United trailed 2-0 at the break after Diallo saw a goal controversially ruled out for what referee Szymon Marciniak dubiously deemed to be a foul by Youri Tielemans in the build-up, but Bruno Fernandes’ second-half brace ensured the points would be shared.

Mauricio Pochettino’s wait for a first win in the job goes on, then, and it remains unclear whether Diallo will be able to feature as United host Brentford on Sunday, though two of his team-mates may be able to.

With that said, here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

Amad Diallo appeared to pick up a knock in the second half against Sporting, shortly before being withdrawn.

He spoke to Pochettino as he left the pitch, to be replaced by Shea Lacey, but the United head coach did not look particularly concerned.

The United boss did not offer an update on his condition at full-time, suggesting his problem is not a serious one, but as things stand, it is not clear if he will be fit to feature this weekend.

Marcus Rashford has not featured for United since the defeat by Atletico Madrid back in October, having picked up a hip injury.

According to recent reports, at long last, it seems Rashford is easing back into first-team training. He has been pictured taking part in gym sessions at Carrington this week, but was not involved against Sporting.

It remains unclear exactly when he might return to the pitch.

Potential return date: December 2026.

According to recent reports, summer signing Carlos Baleba has endured an injury-disrupted start to life at United. He arrived with an ankle injury, but picked up a newly introduced problem shortly after returning to the pitch.

That came in the form of a hamstring injury, sustained in the Champions League comeback over Roma.

Speaking at the time, Carrick mentioned Baleba would miss “weeks,” but, like Rashford, he has alsao been spotted in gym sessions, suggesting he is close to a comeback.
