In 2026, young men are generally on top of their skincare. But in the before times, a splash of cold water to the face was the extent of most morning regimens. That’s probably why so many middle-aged guys find themselves with dried-out, sun-damaged skin that isn’t quite where they want it to be. The good news is that it’s never too late to pick up good habits.

With that in mind, we asked six of the glowiest people we know to share the products they reach for every day—and the lessons they’ve learned along the way.

Sources indicate that caroline Hirons, aesthetician and writer: "Men’s skin is slightly thicker, and a high majority of men shave daily, which can cause its own set of problems if not done properly. Using the right kit and products is crucial: shaving oils, facial shaving products, post-shave moisturizers … A lot of men with a heavier beard would also benefit from using a daily acid exfoliator to prevent ingrown hairs.”.

“Skin Rules” by Caroline Hirons is out now.

Saul Nash, menswear designer: "“Korean skincare is everything. I use Reedle Shot 100 masks—I love them and they leave my skin glowing. I do think it’s crucial, though, not to trouble your skin too much—I have combination [different conditions across the face] to oily skin, so I try not to touch my face a lot or put too many products on it.”.

Harris Reed, designer: “It’s all about eye cream and a silk eye mask—these are non-negotiables.”.

Dr Richard Devine, aesthetic doctor and founder of Devine Clinic: “A product that’s working shouldn’t leave your skin feeling persistently red, sore, itchy, or inflamed. Mild adjustment periods can occur with certain active ingredients, but ongoing discomfort is usually a sign that the skin barrier is becoming irritated rather than improved. If redness, burning, or sensitivity continues beyond the first few uses, it’s often worth reducing frequency or reassessing whether the product is appropriate for your skin.”.

Ziad Halub, co-owner of Iranian-Iraqi café bistro Logma in Hackney: “After a hot and sticky day, your skin needs help. Sweat, dead skin, SPF, pollution, and general city grime can build into a layer of filth. A good foaming cleanser, one that doesn’t strip the skin, is essential. You want fresh, not squeaky. I’m currently loving African Botanics’ Baobab Clay Oxygenating Cleanser.”.

In a recent development, marc Terlet, co-founder of French grooming brand Horace: “At a minimum, an effective skincare routine involves washing your face, applying moisturizer, and then sunscreen. Make things easier with an SPF moisturizer but remember to reapply every couple of hours. The sun is responsible for 80 per cent of your skin aging. Imagine how young you would look if you’d used sunscreen every day since you were 20?”.
