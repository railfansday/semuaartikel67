This evening, Ardon Jashari spoke in a press conference ahead of his expected start in AC Milan’s fifth Europa League game.

Tomorrow’s game could be dictated by Jashari, but he’s got big boots to fill. With news of Luka Modric’s injury, the media immediately listed him as the primary candidate to fill the role and Ruben Amorim will oblige by giving him a rare start.

It’s as good a chance as any to prove that he is the right man to replace Modric when the veteran eventually leaves San Siro. So, with that date looking very possible to be this summer, this could be the Swissman’s chance to show that he can be the player Milan hoped he’d be when they spent heavily on him in the summer before last.

Speaking alongside Amorim, as MilanNews relays, the Swiss midfielder admitted that he was looking forward to the clash and a chance to prove that he could return to his best.

Are you going back to being the player we saw in Brugge?

“I also like being the kind of player who can help the team with my personality on the pitch.

Sources indicate that “If we compare, for example, the competitions now, for us as a team and also for every player like me, it’s important to win matches, and so far we’ve done very well in Serie A. Now we’re starting a different competition; that’s the truth.

Recent findings suggest that “Tomorrow is a great, great opportunity, but also, let’s say, there’s a kind of pressure for all of us. And I think the match against Frosinone is Serie A, it’s a different competition, but still, for all the players, it brought good humour, a good atmosphere, and tomorrow we have to be ready to compete and win the match.”.

Tomorrow you’ll face Freuler, your national teammate….

“I’ll have extra motivation. We talked about this match while we were with the national team. He can’t wait to play against us, and I can’t wait to play against him, too. It’ll be a great match. I can’t wait to meet him and beat him.”.

You lit up on Sunday: what’s your level now? What more can you contribute?

Sources indicate that “Good question. I know I can do much better. I think the game against Frosinone was a good one for me, but it’s not the level I want to show. But I’m working every day to be the best version of myself, so the club, the coach, and the fans can see what I’m capable of.”.

What are you working on, and what aspects?

“In the past, where I’ve played, I had a central role in the team, or in the starting eleven. So I always played. I think with Milan I have to learn to be ready even if I don’t always play, and that’s something I haven’t developed in the past.

“I hope that at some point I can be that decisive at Milan too.”.
