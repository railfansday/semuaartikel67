Manchester United fans have ripped into one of their star players for logging on to GTA 6 following its midnight release, just hours before he was due to report for Mauricio Pochettino's first training session.

A upcoming era gets underway at Carrington on Thursday afternoon, with former USMNT boss Pochettino officially taking charge following Michael Carrick's departure. The Argentine will have little time to settle in, with his first assignment coming away at Anfield against fierce rivals Liverpool this Sunday.

It has been revealed that pochettino could hardly be taking on a bigger challenge at this stage of his managerial career, taking the reins of one of football's biggest clubs just as the Red Devils find themselves languishing in 18th place and the Premier League relegation zone. To make matters worse, they've won just three of their opening 15 matches across all competitions this season.

Understandably, tensions are running high among the fanbase, and that much was clear on Wednesday night when supporters were quick to pile into one of their star players for staying up late to get stuck into a latest video game, just hours before the start of a new chapter at Old Trafford.

Bryan Mbeumo, who is pushing to be at the forefront of Pochettino's intends to get United scoring freely again, posted a snap on his Instagram Stories.

The photo, which was shared in the early hours of Thursday, indicated the Grand Theft Auto VI title screen. And United fans were fuming when they spotted, with Mark Goldbridge among his critics, having told the Cameroon international to 'get out of Man Utd!' See his social media post below:.

Another X user said: “This is why we are in the relegation zone, unserious players," as a third wrote: "Nothing funny about this." A fourth continued: “I’m losing sleep over Sunday. Mbeumo is just chilling playing GTA VI. This club will finish me.”.

A fifth wrote: “No wonder we’re s***. Players not getting enough sleep when they’re supposed to be pro athletes.”.

In a recent development, meanwhile, a sixth concluded: "We’re 18th and this guy is staying up late in the night to play a game that can never help his career. And there’s training under a new manager in the morning. This is the rubbish we deal with at this club.".

From this coming weekend onwards, following a short but gruelling trip to Anfield to face rivals Liverpool, United players will no longer be allowed days off immediately after matches, with the exception of those returning from lengthy European away trips.

Another rule the Argentine is introducing will see players undergo regular body-fat assessments to ensure Bruno Fernandes and the rest of the squad are keeping themselves in check and looking after their bodies away from the pitch.

A third and final measure will also see the introduction of a pre-match squad meal, designed to keep tabs on players' nutrition while also bringing the group closer together.

Those three fresh rules appear to be aimed at stamping out any signs of complacency and ensuring nobody is allowed to rest on their laurels, following a succession of disciplinary concerns and dressing-room incidents that tarnished the tenures of Michael Carrick, Ruben Amorim and Erik ten Hag.

Whether it works or not remains to be seen as Pochettino steps into his toughest job yet. But it's clear the fans themselves are not happy with the conduct of their players right now.
