Industry observers note that how managers and fitness conditioning teams must dread these international breaks – a player coming back with a serious injury picked up on duty for the national side must be every coaches’ fear. it seems a case of ‘so far so good’ for West Ham’s “international brigade” with no reports filtering through to Hammers HQ of new issues.

Recent findings suggest that that does however leave the pre-existing condition: Manor Solomon’s frank admission to his national press will hardly reassure anyone at Rush Green. The winger has been struggling with a back injury and, according to sportwitness, this is neither a new concern nor one aggravated through too many games in the last window – which saw players asked to compete four times in twelve days for their nations:.

“My back is something I’ve been dealing with for many years, ever since I was a teenager. I felt it during Saturday’s game; when I joined the national team, I felt fine, but by Tuesday, it had got worse, and I couldn’t train,” he stated.

“Everyone knows how key the national team is to me and how hard it is for me to watch the team play while I’m not on the field. I hope I’ll be able to play on Tuesday.”.

Facing a late fitness test, it seems Solomon is putting caution to one side in his desperation to be part of the Israel national team to take on Austria tomorrow.

Sadly, there is nothing new here, the old ‘club v country‘ debate in which domestic football (paying the players’ massive wages) gets a lesser priority from a player who will stop at nothing and go through the pain to play for his country.

Solomon looked like the deal of the decade after his first couple of outings, having joined West Ham for a mere £5 million up front.

More disclosures about his back problems and many and varied other problems – which have led him to sit out an incredible 860 days since the start of his career in 2018 ( according to sportwitness) make the price look more understandable by the week.

It has been revealed that west Ham will have to cope with a player who has huge fitness complications but who doesn’t apparently, want to listen to his body, rather playing through the pain to represent his country. It’s not a combination that is likely preserve his longevity in the sport.
