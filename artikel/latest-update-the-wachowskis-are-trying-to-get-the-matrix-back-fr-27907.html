In a recent development, twenty-seven years have passed since The Matrix was released in cinemas, changing the entertainment landscape in perhaps more ways than its creators, Lana and Lilly Wachowski, could have envisioned. The Matrix was part of a three-movie deal that the Wachowskis landed with Warner Bros., and the rights to the franchise​​​​​​​ remained firmly in the hands of the studio, recently absorbed by Skydance in a much-discussed merger. But things may change in the future.

In a recent development, the Wachowskis are not involved with The Matrix 5, but, according to Gardner's undisclosed sources, they "have been encouraging of the newest sequel and have even increased their involvement lately." This means that, despite Lilly's statement in September 2026 that she was "kinda done" with The Matrix, the sisters are still involved in some way​​​​​​​, and their relationship with Warner Bros. isn't necessarily bad.

It has been revealed that overall, the most interesting takeaway from this story is that, in 2029, the Wachowskis could start working on a reboot of the Matrix franchise, freed from the shackles of corporate oversight.
