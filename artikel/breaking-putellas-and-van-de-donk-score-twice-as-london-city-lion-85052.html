Industry observers note that london City Lionesses were level at 1-1 at half-time at Princes Park in Dartford on Saturday 21 November, then scored five times after the break to put Crystal Palace away in round 11 of the Women’s Super League. Mapi León set up two of those second-half goals.

Danielle van de Donk opened the scoring in the 14th minute, finishing after an assist from Isobel Goodwin. Palace answered 10 minutes later through Abbie Larkin. Before the interval María Pérez was booked for a foul in the 38th minute.

In a recent development, the hosts took control early in the second half. Alexia Putellas restored the lead in the 51st minute, and van de Donk added her second at 54 minutes. Jamie-Lee Napier was then cautioned for Palace in the 56th minute.

Palace did reply. Annabel Blanchard pulled one back in the 64th minute, set up by Fuka Tsunoda, and finished with five shots and a rating of 7.74, the best of the visiting side.

Any hope of a comeback faded inside four minutes. Alanna Kennedy made it 4-2 in the 73rd minute and Goodwin added a fifth at 77 minutes, both goals created by León. Everett and León were each booked late on before Putellas completed her double in the 89th minute.

Putellas was rated 8.75, the highest on the pitch, with Goodwin just behind on 8.74 after a goal, an assist and six shots. León, who played 88 minutes, created five chances and was rated 8.48.

Sources indicate that the scoreline matched the balance of play. London City had 64% of the ball, completed 450 passes to Palace’s 218 and had 25 shots to 9, with 12 on target. Twenty of those attempts came from inside the box. Their expected goals figure was 3 against 0.76 for Palace, so the hosts scored twice as many as their chances suggested.

In a recent development, palace goalkeeper Yáñez made six saves. That still was not enough.

Industry observers note that the win leaves London City Lionesses third on 20 points from 10 games, five behind second-placed Manchester City, who have also played 10, and seven behind leaders Chelsea, who have played once more. Manchester United are fourth on 18 points.

It was the second meeting between the clubs in under a month. London City beat Palace 4-2 in the WSL Cup on 28 October, and have now won four straight meetings since Palace’s 2-0 win at London City in the Women’s Championship on 21 January 2024.

Palace have lost two in a row. They are 12th on 9 points, three clear of 13th-placed Birmingham City, with a goal difference of -11.

Both sides return to WSL action on Sunday 13 December. Palace host Manchester City, while London City Lionesses travel to leaders Chelsea.
