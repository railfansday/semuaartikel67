📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, with a name like Lootbox RPG, it's not hard to see developer Mario Gaida's turn-based dungeon crawler is a bit of a snapshot of 2019, back when lootboxes were all the rage. But, while lootboxes have gone the way of the DoDo, Lootbox RPG is still going strong with its largest upgrade yet that comes absolutely jam-packed with content!

Industry observers note that I really do mean largest too. Just for a taster, the 2.0 revision for Lootbox RPG includes five latest regions to explore, including an icy spire and sweltering desert pyramid, alongside a host of new bosses to face in these areas. You can now reach up to level 60 and gear level 70, offering even more opportunities for insane levels of power.

Industry observers note that again, it's interesting to see Lootbox RPG, a dungeon crawler at least partially built around parodying some elements of what, at the time, were an infamous part of gaming, suddenly embrace new trends such as base building. That's not to say it's a bad thing, but definitely interesting to note.

Either way, Lootbox RPG's 2.0 patch, available now, includes a whole host of newly introduced additions. All of this is built on an RPG that already has hours' worth of content to explore. So if you were interested, you can find Lootbox RPG available to play on iOS and Android today!
