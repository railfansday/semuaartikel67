By KIERAN GILL, MAIL SPORT REPORTER.

Published: 03:35 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 03:35 EST, 27 November 2026.

Xabi Alonso concedes Chelsea's defending has been nowhere near good enough – but the Blues boss believes his friend Jamie Carragher was wrong to brand Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana a 'massive problem' in his scathing analysis of the centre-back pairing.

Alonso claimed he had not seen Carragher's critique on Sky Sports' Monday Night Football after their 4-1 home loss to Leeds United.

When told his former Liverpool team-mate had said Colwill and Fofana 'will never work' as defensive partners and that Chelsea 'will never achieve anything' without fixing this problem, Alonso said: 'I must disagree. I disagree with Carra.

Sources indicate that 'For sure, you can have bad games. We were not good. But I've seen them play well, and I've seen them show good levels.

'This is the goal and the challenge – to be consistent in the performance and to be consistent in the basics and not to drop in certain games when we are facing a tough opponent. I understand it was not good enough.'.

Colwill and Fofana were bullied by Dominic Calvert-Lewin of Leeds, as they also were by Sunderland's Brian Brobbey. They will now face Liam Delap and Nottingham Forest on Friday night at a City Ground which will carry an intimidating atmosphere.

Xabi Alonso has hit back at former Liverpool team-mate Jamie Carragher after he labelled Chelsea's centre backs a massive problem.

Alonso and Carragher spent five seasons together as team-mates at Liverpool.

Alonso was strong in saying his side can have 'no excuses' for their slipping standards, having conceded 20 Premier League goals this season. 'When you have that feeling like we had on Saturday, it is not good,' the Chelsea manager said.

Sources indicate that 'We have talked about what we can do better and what we have to do better. There are no excuses not to do better. There are things that we all can do, even if you have a bad day in terms of feeling something you have to do at this level.

Recent findings suggest that 'The feeling of the last game is not good, but now it's about looking forward and showing a reaction. There are no excuses. There is no ifs, no buts. Just certainties and things we have to do.

'We can define on the training and the messaging. We need to show that, not just talk. Talk is cheap. It's about performing on the pitch.'.

Sources indicate that john Terry works for Chelsea's academy part-time, assisting the youngsters coming through at Cobham. Terry is a club legend whose two strengths were defending and leadership.

Asked if he had thought about bringing him on to his staff, Alonso said: 'We have talked because he's been around Cobham many times.

Recent findings suggest that 'He's working with the academy. We have people in the staff to prepare for that, and, for sure, the presence of John is always welcome.'.

Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana have endured poor starts to the season with Chelsea having already conceded 20 Premier League goals.

Pressed further on whether he had worked with the first team, Alonso added: 'This year? Not yet. But he's close to the team.'.

Meanwhile, Fofana has apologised to Chelsea for the unauthorised interview he conducted with a YouTuber which sparked a backlash from supporters online.

In it, the centre-back laughed off his penalty miss which condemned the club to their Carabao Cup exit at Liverpool, and revealed how his representatives advised him to honour his lucrative contract, worth £200,000 per week, when he was tempted to quit football amid his injury hell.

Alonso confirmed the club had spoken with Fofana and mentioned: 'He apologised. He knows it was not right, the moment, and it was not right, the content. We are here to take things seriously and he said that it's not going to be repeated.'.

When asked if Fofana had been fined, Alonso said: 'This is internal things.'.
