Kick and Twitch are both planning GTA 6 promotions ahead of Rockstar’s November 19 launch, giving stream viewers a chance to claim free copies of the long-awaited game.

Kick has debuted its Sunset Reward Case, which contains a chance to win one of 10,000 GTA 6 Ultimate Edition codes for PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Twitch has separately announced daily game-code drops from launch through the end of November, although it has not yet revealed how many codes will be available.

It has been revealed that grand Theft Auto 6 debuts on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19. The game follows Jason and Lucia in Vice City and the wider state of Leonida, with both streaming platforms using its release to draw more attention to their GTA communities. Rockstar lists the November 19 release date on its official GTA 6 website.

Kick’s Sunset Reward Case campaign began on November 12 and runs through December 7. Viewers must sign in to KICK or open the app, watch any livestream for 180 minutes, and then open the case beside their avatar.

In a recent development, each case gives viewers a chance to win, but a GTA 6 code is not guaranteed. Consumers can unlock another case each day, while latest users can receive a free entry case after completing the onboarding process.

Sources indicate that kick’s official terms list 10,000 digital codes for GTA 6 Ultimate Edition. Xbox codes are available worldwide, subject to Microsoft’s platform restrictions, while PS5 codes are limited to supported PlayStation Network regions. The codes will not be issued before the game launches on November 19.

No purchase, subscription, deposit, wager, or betting is required to enter. Kick also says the promotion is being run independently and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

In a recent development, the platform is promoting the giveaway with a truck-sized version of its loot case on the streets of Los Angeles. The truck is being driven by a Lucia cosplayer and features a QR code that directs people to the campaign. Kick notes it will travel through wider California during the promotion.

And finally, we will be doing daily drops of GTA 6 game codes from launch through the end of November to show appreciation, so be sure to tune in.

Sources indicate that twitch unveiled its promotion during the TwitchCon San Diego 2026 opening ceremony. The platform said it will offer several GTA 6-themed rewards from launch through November 30, including upcoming emotes and badges.

Twitch also posted that it will be “doing daily drops of GTA 6 game codes from launch through the end of November to show appreciation,” telling viewers to tune in. The announcement did not specify the total number of codes, whether a particular watch time will be required, or which channels will participate.

Twitch also plans to give GTA 6 streams a special HEIST-themed version of Hype Trains, with six exclusive emotes available during participating broadcasts.
