It has been revealed that ted Lasso is coming back for a fifth season, per the Hollywood Reporter. Final calls on the cast and a premiere date have yet to be confirmed, but the highly optimistic sports comedy will once again appear on Apple TV.

Sources indicate that according to THR, season four of Ted Lasso attracted about 9.8 billion minutes of viewing time during its run this year. Just as popular as when it premiered at the beginning of this decade, the fourth season will likely be the most watched original streaming series of the year. Recent findings suggest that ted Lasso follows the eponymous college football coach turned professional soccer manager (played by Jason Sudeikis) as he attempts to rehabilitate A.F.C. Richmond, a down-on-their-luck club. Lasso was based off of a character Sudeikis developed for NBC Sports’ coverage of the England’s Premier League which parodied American and English attitudes toward football (for better or worse). While the first three seasons primarily focused on a men’s football club, the fourth season turned its attention to a women’s team, the Lady Greyhounds.

Recent findings suggest that ted Lasso premiered in 2020. Its first two seasons won the Emmy for Outstanding Comedy Series, while Sudeikis, Hannah Waddingham, and Brett Goldstein were awarded for their lead and supporting performances. While the third season originally served as the series’ conclusion, it was renewed for its fourth season in 2025.
