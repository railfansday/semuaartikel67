Sources indicate that TL;DR: Burger King is launching a Peanuts holiday menu on Dec. 1. It comes packed in a Snoopy Doghouse Box with wearable knit beanies.

Dec. 1 is officially turning into a fast-food holiday turf war. Just days after McDonald’s announced its Home Alone meal with collectible socks for Dec. 1, Burger King fired back with a nostalgia-heavy Peanuts holiday menu launching on the same day.

Arriving at participating U.S. restaurants on Dec. 1 (while supplies last), the limited-time Burger King x Peanuts collab introduces two distinct meals: the adult-sized Peanuts Pack and a Peanuts-Inspired King Jr. Meal. Both meals are served inside a collectible Snoopy Doghouse Box.

Choice of Entrée — A classic flame-grilled Whopper with Cheese or 10-piece Chicken Nuggets served with Woodstock Golden Ranch.

Charlie Brown Loaded Potato Tots — Crispy, coin-shaped tots filled with melty cheese, savory bacon bits, sour cream, chives, and fluffy potatoes.

Medium Soft Drink — Choice of medium fountain beverage.

Snoopy Holiday Beanie — One of two limited-edition Snoopy holiday beanies.

In a recent development, the Peanuts-Inspired King Jr. Meal includes:.

Choice of Entrée — Hamburger, Cheeseburger, or 4-piece Chicken Nuggets.

Side — Choice of Applesauce or French Fries.

Drink — Choice of Milk or Apple Juice.

Peanuts Collectible Toy — One of eight collectible holiday Peanuts toys in every meal, featuring Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally, three festive Snoopy designs, and a Snoopy Doghouse packed with custom stickers for decorating.

It has been revealed that for collectors, the adult knit beanies and the eight King Jr. Meal toys are especially interesting. Supplies will be limited, and, much like the McDonald's collectible Home Alone socks, fans will scramble to collect them all.

"Peanuts has been part of holiday family traditions for generations, and we can't wait to bring Snoopy and the gang to Burger King this holiday season," said Joel Yashinsky, Chief Marketing Officer, Burger King U.S. and Canada. "At BK, we go all out on partnerships to create fun experiences for everyone — with new menu items, creative packaging and crowns, collectible Snoopy beanies and Peanuts toys and more, we've created something really special. It's holiday fun for all, and we can't wait to welcome families and fans to BK to celebrate throughout the month of December!".
