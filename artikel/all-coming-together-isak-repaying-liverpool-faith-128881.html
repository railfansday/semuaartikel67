'All coming together' - Isak repaying Liverpool faith

Clinical Liverpool beat Brugge to return to winning ways.

Liverpool fans have needed to be patient to see the best of Alexander Isak after an injury-hit start to his career, but the striker is now proving the wait has been worth it.

By scoring twice against Club Brugge in the Champions League, Isak took his tally for the season to 10 - more than double his total for his first season at the Reds.

Of course, that campaign was disrupted; he had no pre-season because of his protracted Â£125m move from Newcastle and then suffered a broken leg in December.

But Isak is now in a hot streak of form, having scored four goals in his past four games for Liverpool.

Asked whether fans were now seeing his full capabilities, Isak told TNT Sports: "It is always difficult to say exactly where you are and how good you are but I can definitely say I feel good, I am in a good place and the team is in a good place which is the most important thing.

"Hopefully it just gets better.".

Isak and Wirtz have proven their ability after last season - analysis.

When Isak scored his opener against Club Brugge, it marked a sixth consecutive away game in which he has struck.

That meant he joined an exclusive club of just eight players to have achieved that feat, following in the footsteps of Dick Forshaw, Sam English, Gordon Hodgson, John Evans, John Aldridge, John Barnes and Mohamed Salah.

Isak has now scored the joint-most away goals across all competitions this season (eight â level with Erling Haaland).

His total is also already more than any Liverpool player managed away from home in the 2025-26 campaign (six by Cody Gakpo, Dominik Szoboszlai and Virgil van Dijk) and it is only November.

Isak looks to be brimming with confidence, exemplified by his second goal as he latched on to a pass from Alisson to go through one-on-one and then chipped a confident finish past the goalkeeper.

But for Andoni Iraola, Isak's performance offered much more than goals, the Liverpool manager also praising the striker's overall contribution.

"He is in a great moment right now," he said.

"In the first half especially he helped us with the link up. In the second half it was more about defending a little lower.".

Alexander Isak scored twice against Club Brugge but also showed a willingness to drop back and help link up Liverpool's play.

Former Liverpool defender Stephen Warnock agreed that Isak had shown he has more to his game than the goals.

"There were a lot of questions about Isak last year, about his price tag and what Liverpool paid for him," he noted.

"There were a lot of people saying he doesn't take people on, but now he looks fitter and looks sharper and we are starting to see the quick, nimble feet again that we saw at Newcastle, with his nice touches and link-up play.

Recent findings suggest that "So we are starting to see a different side of him. My big question in this system would be - can he press? Can he close down, can he affect the game in the other way that Iraola likes to do? Now we are starting to see that.

"All of it is coming together, which is great for him.".

Isak and Wirtz in a 'good place' - Iraola.

It is not just Isak who is looking the part in the Liverpool attack.

Florian Wirtz continued his impressive run of form with a goal that means he now has eight goal contributions in his last five games for Liverpool and Germany.

"Both are now in a good place," Iraola stated of Wirtz and Isak.

"When the team overall is playing well and we are getting good results everything looks a little better.

"The vital part if they help the team to play well and get results. Alex and Florian right now are in a good moment.".

But the Liverpool attack as a whole is looking dangerous, regardless of who plays along with Isak and Wirtz.

In Wednesday's 3-0 win, it was Victor Munoz and Bradley Barcola who completed the front four, while in Sunday's 4-0 defeat of Manchester United it was Rio Ngumoha who played in Munoz's position.

"We have so many good players in our team," Wirtz told TNT Sports.

"Even when we can substitute some wingers, midfielders or strikers in the 60th minute or later it gives us a lot.

"It reveals how good our team is and it is important for a top team to have a strong bench.".

Liverpool's win against Club Brugge was their seventh from their last 10 games across all competitions.

"I spoke to Wirtz at the weekend, after the [Manchester] United game, and we were talking about his form - and he remarked 'well it helps that the team are playing well'," Warnock added.

"It's true because if you try to influence a bad team or a team that's not playing well, then it is much harder.

"With a fitter Isak, Wirtz looks a better player because now he is playing balls in behind that Isak is getting to, and getting in on goal on the back of.

"It has a knock-on effect, which is obviously like Wirtz said, he is playing in a team which is better equipped to suit him.".
