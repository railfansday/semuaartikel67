Mykhailo Mudryk has revealed that Xabi Alonso personally persuaded him to leave Chelsea on loan.

The Ukrainian, who returned from a drugs ban for meldonium in the summer, joined Tottenham in a Deadline Day switch that saw him reunite with his former manager Roberto De Zerbi, who coached him at Skakhtar Donetsk.

The winger was the subject of much Premier League interest when it came apparent that he was available for a temporary move, and he revealed on Ukrainian podcast ‘Guest of Gordon’ that Brighton, Brentford and Leeds all registered their interest in him.

Instead, he chose to move to the north of the capital, where he has made just one appearance before being struck down with an ankle injury he picked up in training.

Speaking of his move to Spurs, Mudryk said that Chelsea boss Alonso “told me straight up to my face: ‘You had better go and play and get some minutes then come back. I am counting on you.’".

The 25-year-old made his highly-anticipated Spurs debut against Nottingham Forest at the City Ground in September, but he has been sidelined since then with an matter that De Zerbi said was “stupid”.

He is expected to return to action this weekend when the north Londoners take on newly-promoted Ipswich Town at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, coming off the back of a run of three wins in their last four games.

Spurs’ recent resurgence was dealt an abrupt end by Leeds at Elland Road before the recent international break, with a Noah Okafor goal on 79 minutes the difference in Yorkshire to snap De Zerbi’s three-game winning streak.
