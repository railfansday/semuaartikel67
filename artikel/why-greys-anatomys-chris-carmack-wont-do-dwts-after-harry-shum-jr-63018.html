Chris Carmack doesn’t see himself joining Dancing With the Stars in the future after his Grey’s Anatomy costar Harry Shum. Jr competed on season 35.

“It certainly made me more interested in the show because of watching Harry go through it,” Carmack, 45, exclusively told Us. “But he’s a dancer and he’s so good and he just enjoyed it so much.”.

Carmack acknowledged that he wasn’t sure it was the best fit for him, adding, “It’s kind of how I feel about making music. I feel like I could throw my whole self into it. I don’t know if I would be able to feel that way about dancing.”.

Though he doesn’t see himself competing in the ballroom anytime soon, Carmack loved showing up to support Shum, 44.

It has been revealed that “It’s how he felt about it and it was a real joy to watch him on the show and his journeying on the show. Being on the sidelines next to the stage watching him perform was [great] and I got to bring my kids and they loved it,” he continued. “It was so much more of a fun event than I possibly could have imagined.”.

He concluded: “I might consider it more than I would have before, but I don’t know. I still got two bad knees, so it’d be rough.”.

Shum danced with pro Jenna Johnson on season 35, which premiered in September. In a surprising twist, Johnson, 32, and Shum were eliminated during the semifinals on Tuesday, November 17, after receiving perfect scores for their routines that night.

“You know what? It’s very bittersweet,” Johnson told Us exclusively on Wednesday, November 18. “I think this season — you never know what’s going to happen, I think we’ve seen that from the very beginning. It’s so exciting that it’s so celebrated. Everyone’s getting to watch dance, but you never know what’s going to happen, and so last night, obviously, I think I was just like a little taken back. But I am so incredibly grateful for my relationship and partnership with Harry, and that goes far beyond these just three months of Dancing With the Stars.”.

Johnson shared that she would “cherish” her connection with Shum “forever.”.

“That far outweighs any of the heartache I’m feeling right now of not being in the finals and getting to do a freestyle,” she added.

In a recent development, while Shum’s time on DWTS came to an end, he is still a prominent part of Grey’s Anatomy. The hit medical drama aired its fall finale on Thursday, November 19, which featured many shocking moments — including Link (Carmack) being forced to address his addiction struggles.

“At the end of the midseason finale, Link is really he’s in the crosshairs and he is at a crossroad because [his wife], Jo, obviously is going to find out that something’s going on — unless he can joyfully lie his way out of it, which maybe he can,” Carmack told Us.

The actor pointed out that Link’s ex Amelia (Caterina Scorsone) “is onto” him.

There’s going to be repercussions with Jo,” he continued. “Not to mention, he’s coparenting a child with Amelia. So he’s at the point where there is no way out.
