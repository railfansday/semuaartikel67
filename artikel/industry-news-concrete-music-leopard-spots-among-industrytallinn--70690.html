The winners of the 25th edition of the Industry@Tallinn & Baltic Event (ITBE) market portion of the Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival (PÖFF) have been unveiled, and they include the likes of Concrete Music and Leopard Spots.

A total of 17 film projects at various stages of scriptwriting, development, or post-production were honored, plus two rising talents from the Discovery Campus programs were recognized.

In a recent development, the PÖFF industry strand winners include the Baltic Event Works in Progress – Best Project Award recipient Concrete Music from director Saulius Baradinskas, who co-wrote the film with Eglė Vertelytė. “Musically gifted teenager Rokas flees his mafia family to avenge his mother’s death through music,” reads a synopsis for the project. Meanwhile, the International Works in Progress – Best Project Award went to Japanese-U.K. co-production Leopard Spots from screenwriter and director Josh Trigg. “Single mother Lilly moves back to her family home in a small factory town in Nara, Japan, along with her teenage daughter Riko,” reads a synopsis. “Struggling with past events and family traumas, Lilly starts to unravel, ripping apart the relationships around her and pushing her daughter Riko away through dark and twisted actions.”.
