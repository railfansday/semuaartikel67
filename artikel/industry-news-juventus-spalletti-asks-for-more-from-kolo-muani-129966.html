Large winner of Venice (4-0), Juventus was able to count on four goals in the second half signed Woltemade, Cabal, Koopmeiners and Kolo Muani. After a difficult first half, the Bianconeri finally made the difference. At the microphone of DAZN, Luciano Spalletti notably explained that he wanted to have an additional cutting-edge solution and was counting on Randal Kolo Muani to take on a bigger costume.

In a recent development, “With all these games I need another striker in midfield. I have three wingers on the right, Conceição, Zhegrova and Nico. On the left I have Alajbegovic, Yildiz and Boga. I have to encourage him to play up front. He must be able to stand out to attack deep. When he receives the ball in the middle with an opponent behind him, he is not comfortable. I also need him up front, even if Ekhator is there now”he remarked about the former Parisian striker.
