📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6 is a masterclass in open world game design, which is hardly surprising considering Rockstar's pedigree and the $1 billion budget at its disposal. Still, it's impressive how emergent and interactive Leonida really is, taking all the lessons learned from Red Dead Redemption 2 and sprinkling in a bit of Payday and Metal Gear Solid. But there are a few hang-ups that could be improved, and a few features players are hoping to see added in future updates.

It has been revealed that one request I keep seeing no matter where I go — Twitter, Reddit, the local grocery shop to get some sunlight between sessions — is more places to sleep. Trekking to Lummox County to go hunting and then having to go all the way back to Vice City to rest up is a slog, and often just means throwing hundreds at a RydeMe when a hotel (or motel) could easily plug that gap. Or, better yet, sleeping in your car.

Recent findings suggest that it might be a bit of a pipe dream, but sleeping in your car could really feed into the random, living and breathing scenarios you run into across Leonida; maybe a cop pulls up and tells you to move along, disrupting your rest. Or maybe if you sleep in a stolen car, you're more at risk of being busted. Maybe sleeping out in the open would make you vulnerable to someone else trying to steal your car. There's a ton of opportunities for such a simple system, and it would essentially turn your vehicle into a mobile Red Dead camp.

Another popular request, and something that has been bothering me personally, is being able to tell Jason or Lucia what to do and how to act when playing together. There's nothing more frustrating than sneaking into an M7 camp like Solid Snake and ruthlessly assassinating gang members one by one until you close in on the last-standing guard, only for Jason to whip out a revolver and blast someone in the knees. Going stealth together can be tricky, so fans are asking Rockstar to add a few options. Like, say, telling Jason to shut up and put a silencer on.

Or, as u/imlazytown suggested — since you can already mark targets — telling Jason or Lucia who to kill. You can tell Jason or Lucia to hold bags, stay put, or fence the loot, so there's already some functionality in GTA 6, but fleshing it out further to make them a tighter-knit pair in combat would go a long way to making the dual-protagonist gameplay more fluid. Even just being able to set NPC behavior and avoid the other character going OTT during robberies. Please, Lucia, don't butcher the security guard.

Rapid-fire round; motels, hotels, sleeping in your car, and partner commands have countless threads, but here are some other cool features players have been asking for in the comments:.

Industry observers note that the one request that really stuck with me is wanting the Hardened/Professional icons to be more distinct. Right now, they're incredibly similar. Enough that, at a glance during combat, it's hard to tell which way you're leaning. Players report that both ranks lead to the same ending, which might explain why the designs are so similar, but it'd be cool to have more varied icons that distinguish between them.

To offer my own suggestion as a closer, I'd love it if photo mode wasn't tethered to the character and allowed for tweaks to the weather and time of day. Lemme experiment more with the camera, Rockstar.
