Burkina Faso teenager Mohamed Zongo has signed for Chelsea and is set to move to Stamford Bridge when he turns 18 next year.

Owners BlueCo reached an agreement to sign Zongo, then 16, in December 2025 and he was originally expected to join the Blues’ French sister club Strasbourg.

But Zongo has now been fast-tracked and signed a pre-agreement contract with Chelsea at their Cobham training ground after turning 17 on Thursday.

Zongo’s current club, Sporting des Tannounyan in Burkina Faso, said that the Chelsea hierarchy decided to change their plans after he impressed during Chelsea trials.

Zongo made his senior international debut for Burkina Faso aged just 16 last month, when he came off the bench to play in the second half of a 7-0 friendly defeat by Argentina in Buenos Aires.

The teenager previously impressed when he starred for Burkina Faso on their run to the quarter-finals of the Under-17 World Cup in Qatar last autumn. The midfielder scored the winner as his nation stunned Germany in the round of 16.

Scouts from a number of top European clubs were in attendance at games across the tournament and many took note of the performances produced by Zongo, who was 15 at the time.

Zongo had previously been linked with several other suitors, including LaLiga outfit Villarreal.
