It has been revealed that mobile games bleed players all the time, especially if they're live service. It's little surprise then that luring players back in has become something of an art. And it's one that Clash Royale is eager to demonstrate in its continued bid to revamp the experience this year, with the launch of its new event, The Royale Interest.

I don't think that's a sign of desperation, so much as Supercell remembering how much player turnover they've had over the years, and figuring out, 'Hey, we should try and get these people back!' Although I'm sure existing players will feel a bit grumpy about not getting anything out of the event.
