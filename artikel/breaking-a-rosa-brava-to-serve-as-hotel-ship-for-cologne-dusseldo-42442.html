A-Rosa’s Brava will serve as floating accommodation for various events in Germany at the start of 2027.

The cruise line noted in a press release that the ship’s season would be extend by turning it into a stationary hotel ship in Cologne and Düsseldorf in January and February 2027.

Being moored near the Cologne Trade Fair and the Düsseldorf Trade Fair Center, the ship will offer overnight accommodation during several trade fairs and the Carnival season.

The 2011-built ship can accommodate a maximum of 202 guests in 99 cabins.

“The MICE business is an notable and growing segment for us. River cruise ships offer particular advantages here, as their manageable size often allows for centrally located berths. Trade fair and event attendees, as well as business travelers, benefit from short distances and direct access to the event venues,” said Stephanie Schrahe, director of omnichannel sales.

To kick things off, the A-Rosa Brava will be moored in Cologne from January 19-22, 2027, for the Cologne international furniture fair.

Following this, the ship will be docked in Düsseldorf January 23-29, 2027, during the boot Düsseldorf water sports trade fair.

From January 30 to February 3, 2027, the A-Rosa Brava will serve as a hotel in Cologne for visitors to the ISM confectionery trade fair and guests attending the Men’s Handball World Championship final.

Finally, February 3-10, 2027, the ship will welcome guests in Cologne who are there for the Carnival season.

The offer includes a breakfast buffet and is also available for solo travelers.
