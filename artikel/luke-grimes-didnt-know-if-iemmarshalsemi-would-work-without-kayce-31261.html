Luke Grimes Didn’t Know If Marshals Would Work Without Kayce’s Wife

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, coming back home isn’t easy, especially for Luke Grimes. The Yellowstone actor spent all of the hit Taylor Sheridan drama as Kayce Dutton, a man trying to leave the past behind and move on from the sins of his father, family patriarch John Dutton (Kevin Costner). But when the western ended and Grimes had to say goodbye to the cast, crew, and his onscreen wife Kelsey Asbille (who played Monica Dutton), he really thought it was for the last time.

“Hopefully everyone can see that it was time,” Grimes told Esquire when Yellowstone ended. “There’s always talks of spin-offs. I’d do it [but] I just don’t understand how it would work once the story ends. Kayce wants to cowboy and be happy with his family. He doesn’t want to kill people anymore. He doesn’t want the weight of a huge mega ranch that isn’t sustainable in today’s times. He wants his little slice of heaven. It’s that simple.”.

Paramount offered Grimes the option to return anyway on Marshals, a spin-off of Yellowstone that sees Kayce Dutton join the U.S. Marshals service in Montana. Reportedly, they won him over after pitching that they would take everything from Kayce so that he would be forced to start again. First on the chopping block? His wife, Monica.

“The thing I was most worried about doing Marshals was doing it without Kelsey,” Grimes told ScreenRant during an interview for season 2 last week. “I just couldn’t wrap my head around Kayce without Monica. That was the story. The rest of Yellowstone was about people fighting over land, and I just loved my wife. I had my own thing going on, and so I didn’t know what that would even look like.”.

Recent findings suggest that eventually he came around to the idea. “Once I sort of, you know, could wrap my head around the fact that that was going to open up all these fresh storylines,” he admitted, “we just [had] to make sure we do honor [their life together] and don’t try to skip over it or pretend it didn’t happen.”.

Marshals season 2 continues to air this fall, as Kayce Dutton explores a potential latest relationship with fellow Marshal Andrea Cruz (Ash Santos). Sometime this season, former Yellowstone fan favorite Jimmy (Jefferson White) will also make an appearance.
