I’ve gotta say, huge kudos to Mexico’s President, Claudia Sheinbaum, for setting quite the example in terms of how she recently traveled to China, as flagged by View from the Wing….

World leaders have just met in Shenzhen, China, for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders’ Meeting. As you’d expect, most leaders flew on their government planes. There were some exceptions, though I can’t imagine anyone else flew the same way as Mexico’s 64-year-old president.

Media reports suggest she flew to China on China Southern. Specifically, she traveled on Sunday, November 15, 2026, on CZ8032, from Mexico City (MEX) to Shenzhen (SZX), with a refueling stop in Tijuana (TIJ), and the journey covering a distance of 8,803 miles.

In a recent development, the Airbus A350-900 took off from Mexico City at 12:49AM, arriving in Tijuana at 2:02AM, after a flight time of 3hr13min. Then it took off again at 4:34AM, and landed in Shenzhen the following morning at 11:31AM, after a flight time of 14hr56min. All-in-all, the flight took around 21 hours, including the refueling stop.

Industry observers note that the shocking thing here isn’t that she traveled commercial, but instead, that she reportedly flew in economy! Reports suggest she was in seat 33B, which would be a middle seat in economy, no less!

Sources indicate that keep in mind that Mexico has an interesting history when it comes to how leaders fly. In 2016, Mexico’s government took control of a private Boeing 787, which flew around former President Enrique Pena Nieto. The purchase of the plane had actually been arranged under the previous president, Felipe Calderón, in 2012.

It has been revealed that then in 2018, when Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador took charge in Mexico, he fulfilled a campaign promise of selling that 787, which turned out to be pretty challenging, given that demand for a secondhand VIP 787 isn’t exactly that high (the plane ended up going to Tajikistan). Sheinbaum has kept that general legacy going, by flying commercial.

Mexico’s President, Claudia Sheinbaum, traveled to the recent summit in China differently than virtually all other government leaders. Not only did she fly commercial, but she traveled in economy, and reportedly even in a middle seat.

Industry observers note that there’s no denying that this is more about optics than anything, but I very much respect that. It’s nice to see heads of state who lead by example, as it’s pretty rare to see nowadays.
