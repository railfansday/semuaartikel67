GTA 6 and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake are being hit with waves of zero-score user reviews on Metacritic, with fans of each game appearing to target the other.

The two share more than a fanbase rivalry, as GTA 6 landed a 99 Metascore at launch, tying the original 1998 Ocarina of Time for the highest critic score in the site’s history.

While critics have been near-unanimous in their praise, GTA 6’s user score on PlayStation 5 has fallen to 5.0, with thousands of negative ratings arriving within a day of launch.

That’s in stark contrast to the PlayStation Store, where ratings can only be left by players who own the game, and GTA 6 presently holds a score of 4.84 out of 5.

People like this are so lazy. Metacritic is not the only place that consumers can rate games. But it IS one of the most accessible (meaning it's susceptible to review bombing).PS5 players can rate the games in the Playstation store, and it's currently at 4.84/5. https://t.co/BBo090644U pic.twitter.com/s3X7YnbjGX.

Some of the accounts leaving zero scores on Metacritic have barely rated anything else, with Nintendo’s Ocarina of Time remake the only other game on their profile, scored a perfect 10.

One of those reviews blasted Rockstar for “making us wait 13 years for an 80-100$ (maybe invisible or stolen) code in a box that only runs at 30 FPS,” calling it “a buggy mess at launch.”.

Another took aim at the game’s £100 ($132) digital-only price, adding, “This is the beginning of the end for Sony and Rockstar.”.

The Ocarina of Time remake launched on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5, and by November 8 held a 9.6 user score, the highest in Metacritic’s history.

That has since dropped to 9.0, with a fresh batch of zero scores aimed squarely at Nintendo fans. “Fan forever GTA 6. Go cry fans of Zelda,” one read.

Others were more direct about why they were leaving them, with one zero-score review simply reading, “Y’all losers review bombing GTA 6.”.

GTA 6 is the joint best-reviewed game of all-time.

Zelda Ocarina of Time remake sells 5.2 million copies in four days to break Switch 2 record.

It isn’t clear which side started it, with GTA 6 fans claiming Nintendo players flooded Rockstar’s game first, and some Zelda fans insisting it was the other way around.

Not every negative GTA 6 review is part of the feud, with some players taking issue with the game’s 30fps cap on consoles, frame rate drops, and open-world bugs.

Others have objected to the game’s optional romance system and claimed GTA 6 has gone “woke,” while some simply called it “the biggest disappointment in gaming history.”.

Unlike the PlayStation Store, Metacritic doesn’t require users to prove they own a game before leaving a rating.
