Your 40-hour work week is much easier on your joints and back if you’re sitting in the right office chair. For better or worse, the office chair might be the most key piece of furniture you own, given the sheer number of hours you spend sitting and working each day. An office chair that properly supports your back, neck, and shoulders can turn what could be a painful week into a comfortable one.

According to recent reports, that stated, a good office chair has to be a lot more than comfortable. Unlike loungers or couches, office chairs require upright support that promotes good posture for your back and neck. They also need to be flexible and mobile enough to move with you throughout a full work day, whether that’s leaning back during a quick break or rolling across the floor to grab something from the printer.

According to recent reports, A good office chair must be both ergonomic and supportive. Ergonomic design promotes good posture and keeps your body in an optimal position for focusing and working. Ergonomics and good posture go hand in hand with quality lumbar support. Office chairs with good lumbar support feature curved or cushioned backs that align closely with the natural curve of your lower spine, keeping you comfortable and upright for long stretches. Some office chairs have air pumps or adjustable curvatures to tailor the chair closely to your body.

Getting the right fit in an office chair depends on its adjustability. Most swivel and roll chairs have adjustable pneumatic bases that raise and lower the chair so your legs stay at a flat, comfortable level. Along with these basic features, some office chairs have an air pump for customizable lumbar support or attachable cushion straps or neck rests, and height-adjustable armrests that slide side to side.

Industry observers note that office chairs are typically made of polyester, leather, or cloth, plus foam. Most polyester or plastic chairs have lightweight, breathable mesh seats and backs that still offer solid lumbar support. Leather and cloth office chairs are typically cushioned for a bit more give. Foam gently molds to your body and may be more comfortable for your legs and back than mesh office chairs.
