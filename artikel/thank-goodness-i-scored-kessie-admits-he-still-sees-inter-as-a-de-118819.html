Last night, AC Milan watched their former midfielder Franck Kessie help their top four chances and even their Scudetto hopes.

After a 5-1 win at San Siro, things couldn’t have got much better for Milan last night. Yet, somehow, they did. Indeed, the late kick-off on Sunday saw Atalanta host Inter and it seemed that the Nerazzurri were heading for another three points, having gone 4-2 up.

However, a brace from Charles De Ketelaere – the second coming to make it 4-3 – and a late penalty from Kessie levelled the scoring and meant Inter stayed within two points of the Rossoneri.

Speaking to DAZN after his late equaliser, Kessie admitted that he still saw the game as a derby, even if he’s no longer a Rossoneri representative, as MilanPress relayed.

“We have great faith in coach Sarri and his staff. The season is still long. Tonight we responded strongly. A heavy penalty at the end? You can score or miss: it went well. For me, it was a small derby against Inter: thank goodness I scored… ”.

Given what it means for the title race, the Diavolo will thank the Ivorian for his contribution and if he plays a part in another successful season, he’ll only be thanked more for it.
