The USS Roosevelt aircraft carrier on Sunday reportedly entered the region overseen by U.S. Central Command, which means that the American military now has three carriers positioned in the region again.

According to recent reports, "The USS Roosevelt aircraft carrier has arrived in the U.S. military central command (CENTCOM) area of responsibility earlier on Sunday, a U.S. official tells me," Israeli journalist Barak Ravid wrote in a Sunday post on X. "The U.S. military now has three air craft carrier strike groups in CENTCOM.".

It has been revealed that in a Monday post on X, CENTCOM shared a photo and wrote, "USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN 71) transits the Indian Ocean, Nov. 15. The aircraft carrier is presently operating in the CENTCOM area of responsibility as part of a scheduled deployment.".

Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi has reportedly indicated that the nation will not grant the International Atomic Energy Agency access to facilities until the war has concluded.

"We will not grant the IAEA access to the facilities until Iran's conditions are met and the war has permanently ended," he mentioned, according to AFP.
