The AC Milan squad returned to training at Milanello on Monday, at least those who are not with their national team.

This morning’s edition of La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) has provided an upgrade on the various injuries that have massively depleted the depth available to head coach Ruben Amorim in the past few weeks.

Starting with Mike Maignan, both he and the French medical staff have reassured Milan regarding the physical concern that forced the goalkeeper to be substituted at half-time during Sunday’s match between Les Bleus and Turkey.

Industry observers note that the Rossoneri captain took a knock to the hip area and felt some discomfort in his right adductor. No medical examinations were conducted while he was away, but they have been scheduled for early this afternoon.

Maignan has put the club at ease, stating that he is not in any pain. Yesterday evening, he posted a photo of himself preparing to make a save, perhaps a way of saying he is ready. However, Lorenzo Torriani is on stand-by if needed.

Meanwhile, yesterday afternoon, Fikayo Tomori and Ruben Loftus-Cheek underwent examinations to assess their recovery from their respective muscle injuries. The tests took place in London, where both players spent the week off granted by the coaching staff.

The news is encouraging, and the recovery process is progressing well: the injury to the defender’s medial flexor in his right thigh is healing, and he will begin gradual work on the pitch over the coming days.

Whether he will be available against Frosinone or for the Europa League match against Olympiakos remains to be seen. The injury sustained against Bologna in late October is nearly behind him, but no unnecessary risks will be taken.
