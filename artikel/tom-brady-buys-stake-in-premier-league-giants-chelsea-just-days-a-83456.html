NFL icon Tom Brady has purchased a small stake in Premier League giants Chelsea, according to ESPN.

It would see the seven-time Super Bowl champion make a swift return to investment in English football after he stepped away from Championship leaders Birmingham City earlier this month.

Brady was in attendance at the MetLife Stadium in Newly introduced Jersey when Chelsea beat Paris Saint-Germain 3-0 in the 2025 Club World Cup final.

The Latest England Patriots icon, who also owns a minority stake in the NFL's Las Vegas Raiders, reportedly has a good relationship with Behdad Eghbali, who oversees the club’s Clearlake Capital ownership.

Chelsea declined to comment when approached, but sources have stood up the negotiations.

Brady, the greatest quarterback of all time, secured a minority stake in Birmingham in August 2023. That followed Tom Wagner purchasing a 45 percent stake at the club earlier that same year.

Recent findings suggest that when Brady exited Birmingham, the club stated in a statement: ‘Birmingham City Football Club expresses its sincere gratitude to Tom Brady for his involvement with and unwavering support for the club over the last three years. It was truly amazing to have someone with Tom’s experience and passion associated with the club, and the club looks forward to continuing to build on that foundation from the training ground to the Birmingham community.’.

Chelsea have invited minority shareholders with their women’s team.

NBA star Giannis Antetokounmpo previously purchased a stake in the side, partnering with Alexis Ohanian, the founder of social media platform Reddit who likewise invested in the team in 2025.
