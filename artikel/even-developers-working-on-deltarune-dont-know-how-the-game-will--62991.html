Deltarune‘s next chapter, Chapter 6, is “progressing well,” and Chapter 7 is in the works too. As far as we know, Chapter 7 will be the final chapter of Deltarune, meaning that developers working on it are actively working on the ending of the game, presumably. But apparently, some members of the development team still don’t know how the game ends.

In Toby Fox’s autumn 2026 Deltarune newsletter, he provided an update on the status of Chapter 7 as follows:.

In a recent development, I read out the events and dialogue of Chapter 7 to the team. However, just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped readingâ¦ I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens.

Everyone agrees it’s more fun this way.

Recent findings suggest that team members who completed their work on Chapter 6 have moved onto Chapter 7 now. Unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I’m doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence…!

According to recent reports, so essentially, the Deltarune developers are working on early parts of Chapter 7 without knowing what the entire chapter entails. Ridiculous as this sounds on its face, it makes a little bit of sense given the intricate way Deltarune‘s story has played out and how much speculation there is about how it will end. I really mean it when I say it’s impossible to predict what’s going to happen in this one, particularly with regard to the game’s two (three?) different paths, and its overall themes of breaking free from one’s destiny. And if you’ve played Undertale, you know how Fox likes to mess with player expectations!

Elsewhere in the newsletter, Fox confirms that Chapter 6 has begun localization and preliminary testing and is playable from beginning to end. It’s sounding more and more like we’ll see Chapter 6 in 2027, given the pace that Chapter 5’s development took.

(And I still think there’s going to be a Chapter 8 after all this, but that’s just my personal fantheory.).

There’s never a bad time to start Deltarune if you haven’t yet. The first five chapters are out now, and Chapter 5 was really, really good. The newsletter is also worth reading if you’re a fan, as it includes a short clip of a scene in Chapter 6 involving Queen and Mewmew that’s pretty goofy, as well as art of a cut character from Chapter 5.
