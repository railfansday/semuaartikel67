According to recent reports, apple has just released its first post-launch software update for the HomeView, version 27.2.1 which includes several bug fixes. Here’s how to install it.

Last week the Apple HomeView arrived in consumers’ homes and immediately required a software enhancement to version 27.2.

But now, Apple has already released its first post-launch patch.

HomeView software 27.2.1 is now available for all users.

You can install the upgrade by opening the Home app on your iPhone then tapping the three dots in the top-right corner, opening Home Settings, and going to the ‘Software Update’ section.
