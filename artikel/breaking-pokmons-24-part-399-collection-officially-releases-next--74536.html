📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Viz Media is releasing an Amazon-exclusive, limited-edition set of all 24 Pokémon phttp://polygon.com/movies on Blu-ray. It's a beautiful collection that will catch the attention of any fan, but its eye-watering price tag might be the reason potential buyers end up skipping this purchase.

Each disc has a themed design related to its story, and they can be stored in a hardcover book-style case. All of this is enclosed in a Poké Ball display that will suit the home of any Poké-fan. If you are excited about the recent announcement of a newly introduced Pokémon movie, then now it's the best time to catch up on the other 24. This limited-edition collection of the Pokémon movies is set to be released on Nov. 24, and is available for preorder now on Amazon.com.

Industry observers note that although these details make the collection quite desirable, it's a bit on the pricey side for many, as it costs $399.99 to pre-order. Details are still scarce about what's included in the set, as Amazon's page seems to be relatively bare-bones. The official description reads:.

Recent findings suggest that in this explosive 24-movie collection, experience Ash's journey to become a Pokémon Master! Featuring all 24 Pokemon movies, following Ash and friends' many adventures to be the very best. From the origins of Mewtwo in Pokémon: The First Movie to the Unova region in Pokémon Black & White, leading finally to the deep Forest of Okoya, these memorable movies take you back to the world of Pokémon.

In a recent development, fans hope the collection includes the original audio, the bonus short movies that premiere with each film, and various special features. However, so far, there is no confirmation that these features will be included. Hopefully, Viz Media will provide more details about the set's content in the future.

Pokémon movies have been a tradition for more than two decades. They all feature Ash/Satoshi, the protagonist of the original Pokémon anime, but they usually have no direct connection to the series. The recently-announced Pokémon: Wild Card will mark a significant shift for the franchise, focusing instead on the Pokémon TGC and featuring a whole new protagonist.

Viz Media's collection could be a boon for fans. Pokémon movies are currently scattered across several streaming websites, so having a high-definition Blu-ray collection would make the films more accessible. However, the product is only listed on Amazon.com and is currently unavailable in other Amazon stores.
