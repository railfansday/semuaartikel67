FIRST ON FOX: President Donald Trump and UFC President and CEO Dana White will announce six-figure personal contributions to one of America's oldest musical institutions.

The President’s Own United States Marine Band played a key role in America's 250th birthday celebrations and official state business this year — and a White House official tells Fox News Digital that the pair want to "express their appreciation for the band’s exceptional talent, professionalism and commitment to excellence.".

Sources indicate that trump and White confirmed Friday afternoon on the new White House helipad on the South Lawn that they increased their combined gift from $200,000 to $500,000 of their own money after realizing the size of the ensemble. No taxpayer dollars will be used for the $500,000 gift.

According to recent reports, "Now, it started off with 200,000, and I said, how many people are there? I thought there were 70, but it’s about 107. And a lot of people joined. So we decided to increase it," Trump said during the event, which was the first-ever held on the new South Lawn helipad.

The U.S. Marine Corps band performs ahead of the Freedom 250 Grand Prix in Washington, D.C., on Sunday, Aug. 23, 2026. (Elizabeth Frantz/Bloomberg via Getty Images).

"We’re now making it at 500,000. Now everyone’s going to get a lot of money," Trump added.

All 135 members of the band will attend alongside senior military leaders, including Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao and Commandant of the Marine Corps General Eric Smith.

"‘The President’s Own’ United States Marine Band proudly embodies American excellence and tradition – and their melodies have accompanied defining moments in our nation’s history," White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales told Fox News Digital.

"President Trump will partner with Dana White to generously celebrate their elite musical talent and unwavering dedication, conveying his gratitude to some of our Nation’s finest musicians.".

The band performed at UFC 250 on the South Lawn of the White House in June, learning each fighter's song in a first-of-its-kind event featuring seven fights.

It also performed at the White House in September when Chinese President Xi Jinping came to Washington for bilateral talks with Trump.

Band members serve as musical ambassadors for the Marine Corps at national and international venues.

Industry observers note that the Marine Band is the oldest continuously active professional musical organization in the United States. It was founded by an act of Congress in 1798 and has served every U.S. president except for George Washington.

Thomas Jefferson gave the band its nickname, "The President's Own." It traveled with Abraham Lincoln to his 1863 Gettysburg address.

For the first time ever, the Marine Band has been nominated for a 2027 Grammy Award. Specifically, the nomination is for Best Orchestral Performance.
