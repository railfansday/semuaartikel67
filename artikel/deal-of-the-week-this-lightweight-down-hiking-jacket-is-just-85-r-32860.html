When it comes to insulated jackets, you usually get what you pay for: The lighter and more compressible the fill, the more you can usually expect to shell out for it. Decathlon’s direct-to-consumer pieces have long been an exception. Packed with 700-fill duck down, the Xplore MT100 Hooded Down Jacket is a light puffy that packs down to about the size of a grapefruit in its own pocket. Decathlon’s own testing claims the Xplore is warm enough to keep the wearer comfortable down to about 27 F when layered over a synthetic baselayer and a light fleece; pair it with a light shell and we’d trust it as part of our layering system all winter long.

Industry observers note that nitpick: When Decathlon phased out the older MT100 in favor of the Xplore, many longtime fans in the ultralight community noted that the organization switched from 800- to 700-fill. While we suspect the average hiker won’t notice much of a difference, backpackers looking for an upgrade should consider laying out the extra cash for a piece like the Mountain Hardwear Ghost Whisperer—currently also on sale for 40 percent off.
