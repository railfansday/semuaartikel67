Shop Liffner’s Black Friday Sale.

Recent findings suggest that liffner is the sleek Scandinavian accessory label that has caught the eye of celebrities and Bazaar editors since it first debuted in 2012. Early adopters included Katie Holmes, Hailey Bieber, and Kendall Jenner. More recently, Liffner has expanded its roster of A-list fans to Gigi Hadid, Jennifer Lawrence, Taylor Swift, and former first lady Michelle Obama, who was spotted in September carrying Liffner’s elegant Pushlock clutch while attending Céline Dion’s Paris residency. The Pushlock is a minimalist everyday bag that can elevate any outfit and is available in 13 colors—five of which are marked down up to 25 percent for a limited time.

Another standout style in the Black Friday sale is the Belted Buckle Bag, which is currently 30 percent off. It’s a perfectly versatile tote bag. It’s big enough to use as a work tote, but the slouchy construction keeps it casual enough for everyday use as well. Earlier this year, senior fashion writer Camille Freestone wrote about how it became her perfect work bag. “I’ve been stopped by friends, editors, strangers, even accessories designers to ask where my bag is from.”.

It has been revealed that liffner bags typically retail for up to $795—which is why you don’t want to miss out on a rare sale like this.
