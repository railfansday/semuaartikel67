Leeds United ended their five-game winless streak with a much-needed victory over Tottenham Hotspur before the international break.

Noah Okafor came off the bench to score the winner and seal all three points, with players then shooting off to their respective national camps.

Results and performances have been a mixed bag away from Elland Road, but Daniel Farke can't afford for that to distract back in England.

The Whites are currently sitting ninth in the Premier League table, with the third-best defensive record in the division ahead of facing Chelsea.

Leeds travel to Stamford Bridge on Saturday, 21 November, and fans will be aware of just how difficult a clash it could be for the visitors.

Therefore, Football Insider has looked at three players who should be axed from Leeds' 1-0 win over Tottenham before heading to West London.

While midfielder Sean Longstaff will be well rested, his previous displays against Chelsea have left a lot to be desired, especially in the FA Cup.

Against Spurs, he missed one significant chance, completed 11 per cent of his crosses, was dispossessed five times, and only won 40 per cent of his ground duels [FotMob].

Leeds will require far more energy and skill in the final third, and that would be perfect for Harry Wilson or Okafor.

Fans have been crying out for Merlin Bard to be given more minutes, and that's what they got in the second half against Tottenham.

The Frenchman is now well rested, while Gabriel Gudmundsson, after some tough club games, gave it his all during the international break.

Up against the likes of Pedro Neto, Cole Palmer and Morgan Rogers, Farke needs players at their absolute best, and he's now got a dilemma at left-back.

The situation surrounding Lukas Nmecha speaks for itself. He was totally ineffective against Spurs and doesn't deserve to start.

He continues to edge closer and closer to a January exit at Elland Road, and Farke can show him the door by leaving him out once again.

Recent findings suggest that introducting both Wilson and Okafor could be the best play. Trying to hit Chelsea on the break could be the best strategy for the Whites.
