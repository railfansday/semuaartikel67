Atmos Rewards is the airline loyalty program for Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines. One of its newer features is Atmos Communities, which offer additional rewards and benefits tailored to different travel interests.

Industry observers note that there are six communities, including two reserved for Alaska and Hawaii residents. The other four are open to members regardless of where they live. But don’t get caught up in the community names. The best choice for you may depend more on your travel prepares than the groupings suggest.

Here’s how to decide which Atmos Community best fits your travel patterns.

» Learn more: The guide to Atmos Rewards.

Atmos Communities are like groups within the Atmos Rewards program that offer additional benefits based on different travel interests.

According to recent reports, they’re free to join for Atmos Rewards members. Four communities are available to all members, while community Club 49 is reserved for Alaska residents and community Huaka’i by Hawaiian is reserved for Hawaii residents.

Sources indicate that you can belong to only one community at a time and switch communities once per calendar year. Consider your upcoming travel looks before choosing.

Each Atmos Community has a list of 10 destinations where members can earn bonus points and status points. Although the destinations differ by community, the rewards structure is the same.

According to recent reports, through Dec. 31, 2027, members can earn up to 10,000 bonus points and 10,000 status points by visiting qualifying destinations.

It has been revealed that don’t be persuaded by the names of the communities. Look a little deeper, because there’s a lot of overlap between destination lists. Compare the options against your upcoming travel plans.

Recent findings suggest that you can change communities once per calendar year, so you aren’t locked in to your initial choice indefinitely. However, switching communities resets your destination-challenge progress, but you’ll retain any points you’ve already earned.

If your home airport (or one you visit frequently) is already on a community’s list, you may have an easier time earning points. Also consider whether a particular community benefit, such as free checked bags or points back on award flights for kids, outweighs the destination challenge.

Industry observers note that finally, pay attention to airport codes. For example, Tokyo’s Narita (NRT) and Haneda (HND) appear on different community lists. Fresh York’s qualifying airport is JFK, while it is DCA in Washington, D.C. If you have a choice between airports, check which one qualifies for your community’s challenge.
