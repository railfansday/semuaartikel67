Bacteria and nuclear power could grow ready-made homes on Mars before astronauts arrive

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

As we gear up to eventually land a human on Mars, more and more ideas are coming out about how to prepare for that. A recent one from Pioneer Labs, a research institute founded by bioengineer Erika DeBenedictis to develop biological technologies for space exploration, is now available on the arXiv preprint server, without any plans to release it in a paid journal as part of the "Open Science" policy of The Astera Institute.
