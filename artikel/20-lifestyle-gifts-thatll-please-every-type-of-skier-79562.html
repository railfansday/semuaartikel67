Are you overwhelmed by the sheer number of items in stores that simply slap the words “ski” or “après” on them in the hopes that the word alone makes it a good gift for skiers?

In a recent development, we hear you. Gifting items that skiers will actually love and use can be intimidating. Especially when it comes to a holiday gift where seemingly everything from cocktail napkins to candles is ski-themed (and no shade to either; we do love a perfectly scented pine candle).

Whether you’re shopping for the skiers on your list or just browsing for yourself, scroll away; our editors have chosen every item on this list wisely, and we have some pretty good taste. If you’re looking for gifts for hardcore skiers check out our recommended gifts for those who eat, breathe, and talk skiing.

According to recent reports, quickly gaining on pickleball as the newest pastime obsession, mahjong is all the rage right now. If you happen to have a mahjong-loving skier on your holiday gift list, you’re in luck. This alpine-themed “Let It Snow” tile set from Mahj & Co is absolutely adorable. It comes with 160 tiles adorned with different wintry scenes, including evergreen trees, a crackling fire, a snow groomer, snowflakes, and, of course, skis and skiers, with a canvas storage bag included for easy cleanup and stowing away. Surely your favorite maj-er needs a set for the ski condo this winter. —Samantha Berman, SKI Executive Editor.

Industry observers note that for anyone who spends winter chasing snow, Gray Malin’s newest photography collection, SNOW, is a stunning reminder of what makes skiing special. Known for his aerial photography, Malin shoots mountains, ski resorts and snowy landscapes from a perspective few of us ever see. The images range from the anticipation of a storm day to a mountain town blanketed in fresh snow—perfect for the artsy skier who loves to linger over a good book. —SB.

A great ski sweater should do more than look good by the fire. Made from 100 percent Responsible Wool Standard–certified merino, the Rockcraft Wool Crew is warm yet breathable, moisture-wicking and odor-resistant on long days on the slopes. Its chunky five-gauge Shaker knit traps heat without sacrificing comfort, and raglan sleeves allow easy movement. With timeless style and the durability to be handed down for generations, it’s the perfect sentimental gift for your favorite skier. Also comes in men’s. —SB.

We love UGGs, just not for clomping around a snowy ski town after dark. Until now. The latest men’s Classic Mini Outpost is a sturdier, more weatherproof option for hitting the bars or grabbing dinner after a day on the mountain. Waterproof suede and seam-sealed construction keep feet dry, a plush lining made with upcycled wool adds warmth, and a White Spider rubber outsole grips slick sidewalks. It’s a practical, stylish pick for post-ski hours. —SB.

It has been revealed that few things say effortless alpine style quite like a great pair of Vuarnets. The French eyewear brand has been part of ski culture for decades, and the Legend 02 sunglasses have the history to prove it. Jean Vuarnet wore the original frames when he won Olympic downhill gold in 1960, and more than 65 years later, they’re still in vogue. Made in France, these classic frames feature Vuarnet’s signature mineral-glass lenses, known for their optical clarity and contrast. Choose the Skilynx lens for a version designed specifically for mountain environments. They’re equally at home on a sunny ski-resort patio, wandering through a European mountain village, or skinning up for a few spring turns. For the skier who appreciates good design as much as good snow, these are a timeless gift. —Sierra Shafer, Editorial Director, Lifestyle.

Sources indicate that A cold beer after skiing is one of life’s simple pleasures. Cold fingers holding that beer? Less so. KAVU’s delightfully superfluous solution is a fleece mitten with a built-in pocket for your drink. Yes, it’s essentially a koozie for your hand and your beverage, and frankly, I wish I’d thought of it first. Is it necessary? Absolutely not, which is why it makes a great gift for your favorite parking-lot après regular. —SS.

Celebrating a ski day by dipping bread into melted cheese is an elite, unbeatable experience, but you don’t have to travel to the Alps to indulge. Le Creuset’s Alpine Signature Fondue Set brings the ritual right to your kitchen table. The enameled cast-iron pot retains heat beautifully, while six color-coded forks help everyone keep track of their bread. It’s a splurge, but it’s also the kind of gift that will become a tradition for years to come. As for the best recipe? My friend JF, an expert skier and even more accomplished fondue enthusiast, has entire books on the subject. His advice, which I’ll pass along as my holiday gift to you: Rub the inside of the pot with a cut garlic clove before adding the ingredients, use a combination of Gruyère and Emmentaler, and finish with a pinch of nutmeg. Whatever the recipe, double it. —SS.

I own several Rumpl blankets and use them year-round. Think of them as your favorite insulated jacket, only major enough to share. At parking-lot après, nothing beats throwing one over your lap in a camp chair. They’re just as useful wrapped around your shoulders while you cheer on your U12 racer bashing through slalom gates in subzero temps. A built-in clip keeps the blanket in place, leaving your hands free for whatever libations or cowbells the occasion calls for. I keep one in my car all winter, partly for emergencies but mostly because there’s always a reason to pull it out. With recycled synthetic insulation and a water-resistant shell, it’s warm, packable and endlessly useful. And yes, I’m suggesting you gift yourself one. Ho, ho, ho. —SS.

Industry observers note that hygge meets performance in this cozy fleece that doubles as an ultra-plush midlayer. We love the longer, relaxed cut that covers your backside, and the high, fuzzy collar is pure comfort when the snow starts swirling. The recycled pebbled pile is warm, quick-drying and moisture-wicking under a shell, though it’s so plush it’s almost overkill for skiing. Where it really shines is après and around town, thanks to fun prints in three colorways and men’s and women’s cuts. —Jenny Wiegand, Associate Gear Director.

Long winter nights call for warm pajamas. Hanna Andersson’s flannel PJ pants are so cozy you’ll want to wear them under your snow pants. They come in several ski-chic patterns that look good on the whole family, and they’re perfect for skiers who want something soft to climb into before dreaming of powder all night. —Giorgia Menetre, Senior Social Media Strategist.

Nothing beats taking off your ski boots and slipping into cozy slippers. If you’re like me, you want a pair that lasts, has a solid sole and isn’t the same old tan suede. L.L.Bean’s Daybreak slippers check every box. The wool clog-style upper keeps toes warm, the slip-on design makes them easy to pull on and off, and the durable rubber sole offers just enough traction. They’re perfect for the skier who wants to be cozy and stylish after a day on the slopes. —GM.

Industry observers note that lightweight and durable, this everyday helmet is inspired by the speed and precision of ski racing helmets. Equipped with Mips Air Node, BOA Fit System, and advanced adjustable ventilation, the POC Arcus gives you a precise and secure fit with great air flow to keep you cool and comfortable on every run.

Packing for a ski trip can be chaotic, so the more organized, the better. Bonus points if that organization comes in an adorable butter-yellow pouch with ski-themed details. At roughly 9″ W x 6″ H x 4″ D, it’s big enough for all your ski essentials but small enough to fit in any bag. It has already sold out on a few sites, so don’t wait! —Jamie Aranoff, SKI Senior Editor.

Recent findings suggest that ask any kid (or kid at heart) what’s on their holiday wish list, and you’ll likely hear “Jellycat.” These soft creatures come in all shapes and sizes, but the ski-themed critters are downright adorable. From skiing bunnies to spiced mulled cider wearing ski goggles, you’ll be able to find something to make your giftee smile. —JA.

Last season I wore a “Pray for Snow” trucker hat nearly every day, and despite the unironic message, it got tons of compliments. This year, Aviator Nation plastered the iconic phrase on the back of a cozy sweatshirt. The female-founded brand is known for exceptionally soft sweats hand-sewn in Southern California, and there’s nothing better to slip into for après. —JA.

In a recent development, I come from the camp that candles make excellent gifts, especially luxe candles. For anyone who wants their home to smell like fresh snow, this one is a surefire win. With notes of jasmine sambac and warm musk, it’s the kind of candle you secretly want but would never buy for yourself. It also makes a great white elephant gift for skiers and winter lovers. —JA.

Kelly Slater’s co-founded brand Outerknown makes clothing for the life we all wish we lived: calm, cool and California-chic. The brand offers plenty of responsibly sourced cozywear, but the Nostalgia sweater is a hands-down crowd-pleaser. Made from 100 percent organic cotton with a retro rainbow design, it’s the perfect sweater to throw on after a long ski day. —JA.

It seems like out of nowhere, Lola Blankets became the season’s biggest talking point. From billboards to Instagram, these blankets have quite literally taken over. And while there are plenty of dupes out there that look identical, there is something about this blanket that cannot be recreated anywhere else. It is the softest blanket I have ever felt, and that feeling does not diminish with wear. Getting cozy after skiing is a right for all skiers, and if you want to win over any skier’s icy heart, then look no further. —JA.

It has been revealed that last spring, I skied Aspen Highlands’ closing day. After a warm winter, I’d packed light, but it still wasn’t light enough, so I picked up the Hayden Hoodie to wear under a puffer vest. The fleece kept me warm on the snow and blocked some wind without overheating me, and it’s been in heavy rotation all fall. I also love that the fleece has a hoodie, which most of my other quarter zips lack. The fit is slightly cropped, so size up if you like to layer size up. —JA.
