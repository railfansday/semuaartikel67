📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Publisher Aniplex and developer French-Bread released the main trailer and latest information for fighting game Melty Blood: Twi-Lumina during a TYPE-MOON Times special news program today, revealing the key visual, playable character Satsuki Yumizuka, cross-play, Grand Scenario Mode, the “Act” and “Duel” control modes, and the early purchase bonus.

A Digital Deluxe Edition was also confirmed, which includes the base game and a digital art book.

In Japan, pre-orders for physical standard and limited editions for PlayStation 5 and Switch will begin on November 26. The latter includes a copy of the game, decorated box featuring artwork by Takashi Takeuchi, an art book, the original soundtrack, an arcade controller button-style tin case featuring artwork by Takeuchi, and a set of three 30-millimeter arcade controller style buttons (Len, White Len, and Satsuki Yumizuka), also by Takeuchi, with a strap.

Fresh Key Visual by Takashi Takeuchi Revealed!

Aniplex unveiled a newly drawn illustration by Takashi Takeuchi, who is handling the character designs for this title. In addition to the two characters, Shiki Tohno and Arcueid, the visual features Ren, White Ren, and Satsuki Yumizuka, who will make their debut as playable characters in the upcoming game.

Satsuki Yumizuka Makes Her Debut as a Fresh Playable Character.

The new trailer not only introduces the new character, and new game play systems added specifically in this title, but also the Grand Scenario Mode created by Kinoko Nasu.

The video also features the game’s theme song, Welcome To My Dream (feat. 310), produced by Spiral Ladder.

Upcoming Battle System and Cross-Platform Multiplayer.

Recent findings suggest that during the most recent stream of TYPE-MOON Times: Melty Blood: Twi-Lumina Latest Information Special, Ryosuke Kanemoto as Shiki Tohno and Yohei Azagami as Mikhail Roa Baldamyon were joined by development director Kamo Serizawa. Together they revealed the latest information on the game, including new game systems added in this title, which includes the series’ first cross-platform versus mode, as well as the Grand Scenario Mode created by Kinoko Nasu. The versus play also features the new playable characters Ren and White Ren.

First Cross-Platform Multiplayer Feature.

This title in the Melty Blood series is slated to be the first of its kind in supporting cross-platform play, which allows players to battle against more players across different platforms.

Recent findings suggest that the Act control scheme, which allows players to move the character with simple controls by activating special moves with the press of a single button. This is recommended for those latest to fighting games or the Melty Blood series.

Sources indicate that the Duel control scheme, which lets players enjoy more authentic battles by utilizing precise controls through traditional command inputs. This play style is recommended for those familiar with fighting games or players who want to delve deeper into the game mechanics.

Grand Scenario Mode includes newly introduced story by Kinoko Nasu.

The new Grand Scenario Mode is a large-scale scenario feature newly added to this title.

Players can enjoy a story written exclusively for this game by Kinoko Nasu that focuses on the protagonist, Shiki Tohno. The story is presented through a variety of narrative techniques, and more information is available on the official website, as well as during the TYPE-MOON Times: Melty Blood: Twi-Lumina Latest Information Special.

Round call voices for Ren, White Ren, and Satsuki Yumizuka will be added to the main game.

Melty Blood: Twi-Lumina is due out for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam on April 22, 2027.
