📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Major U.S. wealth management company EP Wealth Advisors has revealed that it holds an investment in an XRP exchange-traded fund (ETF). This was disclosed in the company's latest 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

Recent findings suggest that EP Wealth Advisors, a California-based investment advisory firm with about $24.4 billion in securities holdings, has revealed that it held 18,400 shares in the Volatility Shares XRP ETF (XRPI) as of Sept. 30, 2026.

It has been revealed that this was disclosed in the official SEC filing that the firm submitted to the commission on Nov. 13. The filing highlighted that the investment was valued at $147,936 at the end of the third quarter.

Industry observers note that furthermore, the filing revealed that the total portfolio value reported by EP Wealth Advisors stood at $24,380,879,896. This value comprises 1,603 items in the firm's 13F information table.

It's worth noting, however, that this isn't the company's total assets under management. It's the value of its reportable securities holdings. There are also assets that are not included in Form 13F.

In a recent development, interestingly, this isn't the first time EP Wealth Advisors has held an investment in XRPI. Data from its historical 13F filings indicates that in the second quarter of 2026, the wealth management manufacturer held 13,425 shares in XRPI.

Sources indicate that the recent filing indicates that the tech giant has now increased the number of shares it holds in the XRP ETF by 4,975 shares. This represents about a 37% increase in the company's XRPI holdings during the third quarter.

Sources indicate that nevertheless, this amount is very small relative to the firm's multibillion-dollar securities portfolio. It represents less than 0.01% of the total value of its reported securities holdings.

In a recent development, it's also worth reiterating that Form 13F is simply a filing that indicates institutional holdings at the end of a quarter. It doesn't necessarily represent the investment manager's current holdings.

The Volatility Shares XRP ETF was released in May 2025. Its ticker symbol is XRPI, and it is currently trading on Nasdaq.

Recent findings suggest that XRPI isn't a spot XRP ETF. This means it doesn't directly hold the cryptocurrency XRP. Rather, it primarily derives its exposure to XRP's price movements through XRP futures contracts.

This ETF essentially provides investors with a way to invest in XRP without actually having to hold or custody the cryptocurrency. The ETF aims to deliver investment returns that correlate with the performance of XRP.

As of Nov. 13, the most recent available data from the fund issuer shows that XRPI's net assets stood at about $77.9 million.
