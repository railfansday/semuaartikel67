It has been revealed that first, what is Backpack crypto? Why is it exploding? Backpack is suddenly becoming one of the market’s hottest stories after securing a major regulatory breakthrough in the United States. Its native BP token surged 30% in 24 hours as traders rushed to price in the exchange’s latest expansion. The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has effectively opened the door for Backpack to challenge established crypto derivatives platforms on American soil.

Sources indicate that the approval gives Backpack the regulatory foundation to operate a US derivatives exchange and clearinghouse. Its ambitions extend beyond simply offering another place to trade Bitcoin and Ethereum. The company wants to combine leveraged crypto trading, collateral management, and global liquidity under one regulated platform.

The development also arrives as competition heats up over who can deliver the next generation of crypto trading infrastructure.

Recent findings suggest that founded by Armani Ferrante, Backpack started as a Solana-focused crypto wallet and exchange. It has since expanded into securities, tokenization, and derivatives. The manufacturer then reports more than $450 billion in cumulative trading volume and customers across over 150 countries.

Recent findings suggest that its latest breakthrough involves two separate approvals from the CFTC. The Designated Contract Market (DCM) registration allows Backpack to operate a regulated derivatives exchange.

In a recent development, meanwhile, its Derivatives Clearing Organization (DCO) registration provides the infrastructure needed to manage collateral, margin, and settlement obligations. Together, these approvals establish the foundation for Backpack to offer leveraged crypto perpetual futures in the United States.

The proposed platform will support 24/7 trading, crypto and fiat collateral, cross-margining, and access to Backpack’s global order book.

It has been revealed that that last feature could prove particularly notable. Rather than isolating American traders inside a separate liquidity pool, Backpack wants to connect US customers with its international trading ecosystem.

It has been revealed that BP responded immediately. The token climbed by more than 30% over 24 hours to $1.10, pushing its market cap higher as trading activity accelerated. The rally ties to growing expectations that Backpack could become a serious competitor in the American derivatives market.

The regulatory breakthrough also strengthens its position against offshore platforms that have dominated leveraged crypto trading for years.

Bitcoin remains the industry’s largest liquidity pool, yet its base layer was never designed for sophisticated smart contracts, high-speed decentralized trading or complex margin products.

That creates an infrastructure gap developers are racing to fill. Solana demonstrated how rapidly decentralized applications can execute transactions. Bitcoin, meanwhile, offers a much deeper pool of established capital.

Bringing those strengths together could unlock an entirely different market for Bitcoin-denominated trading.

Projects exploring this direction, Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER), which combines Bitcoin Layer 2 infrastructure with Solana Virtual Machine technology. Its architecture aims to let users bridge BTC into a faster environment supporting smart contracts and decentralized applications.

Sources indicate that the potential goes beyond faster transfers. Bitcoin liquidity could eventually support decentralized spot markets, margin trading, and perpetual futures without forcing traders to abandon Bitcoin for another blockchain ecosystem.

Bitcoin Hyper has already attracted more than $33.2 million through its presale, exceeding Backpack’s previously disclosed $17 million Series A funding round. Its token currently sells for just $0.0137, while its staking program advertises rewards of up to 35% APY.

With its November 26 launch target approaching, the presale is entering its final stretch. The combination of Bitcoin liquidity and Solana-style execution is attracting attention as developers look for ways to bring more sophisticated financial applications to Bitcoin.

Recent findings suggest that backpack now has something many offshore competitors cannot easily replicate: a regulatory pathway into the American derivatives market. The firm still needs to turn its approvals into actual trading activity, but the potential market is enormous.

BP’s 30% rally demonstrates investors are already anticipating that expansion, and Backpack’s regulatory breakthrough could mark the beginning of a much larger shift in crypto trading.
