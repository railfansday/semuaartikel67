Quark Expeditions has announced its Black Friday Sale, offering travelers up to 40% off select voyages to the Arctic and Antarctic.

The promotion launches November 17, 2026, and runs through December 3, 2026.

“There’s no single way to experience the polar regions, and this sale gives travelers an incredible range of possibilities,” noted David Marathakis, senior director of sales, Americas, Groups and Charters.

“With our best pricing of the year, we’re giving travelers even more reason to make the journey they’ve been dreaming about a reality. We recommend booking early for the best choice of trips, ships and cabins,” added Marathakis.

The company noted in a press release that the sale opens opportunities for guests to explore destinations including the Antarctic Peninsula and Patagonia, as well as Svalbard, Greenland and Iceland.

Popular voyages in Svalbard and Antarctica, including “Antarctic Express: Fly the Drake,” are also available as part of this offer.
