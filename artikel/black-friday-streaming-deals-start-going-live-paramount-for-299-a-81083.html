Streaming has gotten more expensive, and Black Friday is the one time of year where you can often get a really good deal and, as of today, those streaming deals are starting to go live.

Recent findings suggest that through Amazon Prime Video, you can at the moment get huge discounts on select streaming add-ons for Black Friday. This includes up to 80% off of Paramount+.

In a recent development, the HBO Max deal is one of the best here, though it is the ad-supported tier. But, strangely, it’s not showing for all users at this time.

Notably, some of these services, including Paramount+, let you link your Amazon account so you can access the subscription from either Prime Video or the streamer’s main app.

The “catch” with these deals is that you’ll need an active Amazon Prime Membership, so get signed up if you want in on the discounts.
