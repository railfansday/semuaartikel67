Is there something going on today? A lot of talk about committing crimes and the like, I just can't quite put my finger on why… Ah well, couldn't be key or interesting. What is interesting, however, is Keepsake County, a "midwestern stealth game" that has released on this inauspicious day about stealing stuff from "hideous farmhouses in the middle of nowhere.".

With the sarcastic intro out the way, I will address the elephant in the room: Vovly's Adventure: Reslimed is out today too. Fine, fine, yes I know GTA 6 is the game everyone's talking about, but Forward Instinct, the devs behind Keepsake County, seem well aware of that in their launch day post for the game: "Folks, thanks for your incredible patience. Keepsake County is finally OUT! And yes, it is deeply ironic that it wound up being today, of all days, that we got the green light to finally release, but here we are.".

It has been revealed that keepsake Country was supposed to release earlier this month, but seemingly got caught up in the backlog Valve are faced with that's undoubtedly been caused by genAI games. In any case, the burgle-em-up does look quite delightful. I'm not sure if I can quite put my finger on exactly why I get this feeling, but it calls to mind a more complex version of the kind of game you'd find on Cartoon Network's website back in the day (which is a high point of praise, I'd like to be clear).

Each house in Keepsake Country is procedurally generated, meaning you never quite know what to expect when you enter. There's lockpicking, resident tie upping, and of course stealing to be done, but it doesn't just stop at the house. Parking your car, for instance, is an vital thing to consider; do you want it hidden so you're less likely to be discovered, or right at the front door so you can make a speedy escape? Not to mention you have to deal with the fuzz showing up at some point if you aren't quick enough!

Sources indicate that gear and the like can be improved between runs, meaning you can lockpick faster or delay the cops, it all seems quite systemically thoughtful. Very much some immersive sim vibes from this one, just on a much smaller scale. And most certainly worth considering picking up on a release day like today! If you're interested in doing so, you can pick it up on Steam right here.
