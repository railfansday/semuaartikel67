Adrien Rabiot’s contract renewal with AC Milan is expected very soon, according to Fabrizio Romano, as both parties want to continue together.

It’s safe to say that Rabiot has been a key player for Milan since he arrived in the summer of 2025. He got six goals and five assists to his name last season, but this campaign, he has already bagged five goals and five assists after 13 games (!).

In a recent development, in other words, he seems to be enjoying life with the red and black shirt, just as the Rossoneri are very happy with his performance. So much so that negotiations are presently ongoing to extend his contract with a pay rise.

Fabrizio Romano provided an patch on the Frenchman’s contract in his latest YouTube video, stating that he’s one of the next significant priorities for Milan. As the midfielder is happy to stay, the Rossoneri are convinced it can be sealed very soon, especially since they succeeded with two other stars.

Industry observers note that “The renewal of Rabiot is one of the next priorities and Milan are moving forward with great conviction with renewals in general. They did well with Pulisic and Pavlovic, and Rabiot is next on the list.

According to recent reports, “The timing will depend on various factors. We know that Rabiot’s entourage isn’t always the easiest one to deal with in that sense, but Milan and Rabiot are calm about the situation. In short, the direction is clear. I expect the renewal soon,” he remarked.
