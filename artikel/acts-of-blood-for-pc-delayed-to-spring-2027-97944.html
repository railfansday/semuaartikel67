Sources indicate that publisher Significant Fan Games and developer Eksil Team have delayed beat ’em up action game Acts of Blood from its previously planned December 4, 2026 release to spring 2027 for PC via Steam and Microsoft Store. PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series versions are also due out in 2027.

Here is the full message from Eksil Team on the delay:.

With heavy heart we’re making a difficult decision to postpone the release of the game on PC (Steam) into spring 2027.

We know the decision might disappoint for you, whom already looking forward to play our game on December.

But after assessing the feedback we got from players, play testers, and internal team, we found several challenges that still can be improved with a little bit more time, and hopefully it will deliver the best experience we intended.

We sincerely apologies for the delay.
