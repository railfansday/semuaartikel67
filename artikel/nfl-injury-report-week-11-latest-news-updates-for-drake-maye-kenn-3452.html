NFL Injury Report, Week 11: Latest news, updates for Drake Maye, Kenneth Walker III, Brock Bowers, more players for fantasy football

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It was a rough week for the Fresh England Patriots over in Germany. As we look over the Week 11 NFL injury report, the Patriots are going to pop up quite a bit going into their bye. We also have five other teams on bye — the Packers, Seahawks, Browns, Falcons and Rams. Those teams will hopefully get healthier over that span.

Below, we'll go over the latest injury news, providing updates on key players for fantasy football.

Drake Maye, NE (ankle) — Out indefinitely.

Geno Smith, NYJ (concussion/ankle).

Sam Darnold, SEA (quad) — bye week.

Baker Mayfield, TB (lower leg) — Could miss Week 11.

Maye: New England heads into its bye, though the season may be lost coming out of it. At this point, it's feared Maye has a fractured ankle and could need season-ending surgery. At the very least, he could miss multiple games or be placed on IR. It's almost helpful that New England is on bye in Week 11, as fantasy managers would already be looking for a Maye replacement or streaming option.

Industry observers note that if Maye is done for the season, it's difficult to trust anyone on this offense. There are injuries and ailments everywhere, from A.J. Brown to Rhamondre Stevenson and TreVeyon Henderson. The Pats are well behind the 8-1 Bills in the AFC East, though are still in the wild-card picture. But if Maye is out, that outlook could change fast. If you have any skill position player on Newly introduced England, be sure to have contingency looks. This team could turn into a fantasy wasteland soon.

It has been revealed that mayfield: The Bucs are coming out of the Week 10 bye but may be without Mayfield. He suffered a lower leg injury in Week 9's loss to Chicago, and the extra rest didn't appear to help. If Mayfield is out against Detroit in Week 11, Jalon Daniels, who started three games earlier in the season, would get another shot. With so many teams on bye and Maye managers searching for answers, Daniels isn't a bad waiver add. Tampa Bay has a pretty soft schedule the rest of the season. Expect the Bucs to continue to ride the running game with Bucky Irving and Kenny Gainwell.

It has been revealed that walker: The Chiefs added Walker to the injury report last Friday and he was swiftly ruled out for Week 10 against the Falcons. As a result, rookie Emmett Johnson thrived in lead-back duties, totaling 171 yards on 28 touches (six catches) with two TDs for 32.1 fantasy points. Reports suggest this will be a short absence for Walker and he should be back Week 11 against the Cardinals.

If Walker is back, he should return to his role as the lead back and get the majority of the work. Johnson has played well enough to warrant touches, however, and could get extra work given the opponent. Kansas City is a heavy favorite in Week 11, and if the team pulls away, we could see Walker rest and Johnson get work in garbage time. If Walker is active, you're playing him, of course. But if you were able to snag Johnson off the wire, he's also in play as a flex option.

In a recent development, texans RBs: Marks was forced to leave Week 10 against the Browns due to a concussion. He's unlikely to go Thursday against the Colts. Head coach DeMeco Ryans also doesn't know if Montgomery will be ready on the short week. If both backs are out, it's looking like Emari Demercado and Dare Ogunbowale will split the work out of the backfield.

According to recent reports, while it's a good matchup, if both Marks and Monty are out, neither depth back is a strong fantasy play. Each would be a risky flex option. Instead, expect the Texans to lean on the passing game, which has looked good over the past four games. Xavier Hutchinson, Tank Dell and Kayshon Boutte remain interesting adds off the waiver wire depending on league size/format.

Luther Burden III, CHI (concussion).

A.J. Brown, NE (hamstring) — bye week.

Marvin Harrison Jr., HOU (hamstring).

Burden: The Bears were on bye in Week 10 and Burden has had ample time to get right. We'll see what his practice status looks like but he'll need to clear protocol in order to play this week against the Saints. If Burden misses the game, it's a bump up for Rome Odunze and Colston Loveland in the pass game, while we may see more targets for Kalif Raymond.

Industry observers note that evans: The Niners may not be in a rush to get Evans back given the 46 points the offense scored on Sunday against the Cowboys. The veteran wideout missed his third straight game but will have a shot at returning in Week 11 against the Vikings, not an ideal spot. San Francisco also has a pretty crowded receiving room with Deebo Samuel Sr. returning and Keenan Allen coming over via trade, not to mention George Kittle and the emerging Jordan Watkins. If Evans can get back in Week 11, he's a low-upside WR2/3 given the matchup.

It has been revealed that bowers: Reports came out that Bowers avoided a major injury in Week 10's loss to the Seahawks, which is good news. He's considered day-to-day and has a shot to play in Week 11 against the Broncos. The tight end didn't get much time to develop chemistry with new starting QB Fernando Mendoza on Sunday, catching just two passes for 18 yards before exiting. With Mendoza under center, it's a slight downgrade for Bowers, but he's still a locked-in TE1. If Bowers ends up missing the game, Michael Mayer isn't a bad streaming option. The Broncos have been a soft matchup for TEs this season.
