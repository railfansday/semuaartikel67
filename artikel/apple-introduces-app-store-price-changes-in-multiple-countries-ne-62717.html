In a recent development, apple today revealed that App Store prices for apps and In-App Purchases will be adjusted in five countries starting December 3, following changes in taxes and foreign exchange rates. Here are the details.

In a latest post published on its developer portal, Apple today confirmed a series of changes to App Store pricing, as well as updates to tax rules affecting developers and customers in several countries, including the US.

According to the firm, developers’ proceeds from eligible app and In-App Purchase sales have been adjusted in four countries, reflecting the following tax updates:.

Specific to Botswana and Rwanda, Apple said that “Exhibit B of the Paid Applications Agreement will be updated to indicate that Apple collects and remits applicable taxes.”.

Apple added that a latest “Software as a Service (SaaS)” attribute is now available for several App Store tax categories, including App Store Software, Software Training Material, Dating, and Boosting. Developers can review and update their tax category selections in App Store Connect.

Additionally, starting December 3, developers who rely on Apple’s automatic pricing adjustments will see changes to app and In-App Purchase prices in:.

Apple usually makes these adjustments to account for changes in taxes and foreign exchange rates, and notes that prices won’t change in countries selected as the base storefront, for auto-renewable subscriptions, or in storefronts where developers manually manage their prices.

The announcement does not go into detail about how much prices will change in each country, or whether they will increase or decrease.

Finally, starting January 1, 2027, apps and In-App Purchases in the SaaS, App Store Software, Software Training Material, Dating, and Boosting categories will become subject to sales and use taxes in California.

Apple noted that “prices on the United States storefront are displayed to customers exclusive of taxes, so the tax change will not affect them,” but added that while developers’ proceeds will remain unchanged, “the total amount customers are charged, inclusive of tax, may increase.”.
