Industry observers note that from the blinding sunshine in Texas to the glare off the snow in Utah, interior photographers in the Southwest have a distinct set of challenges to work with as they strive to give the homes they shoot as much majesty and drama as the landscape that surrounds them. The pros on this list more than rise to that Texas-sized challenge, whether they’re shooting dens in the desert or living rooms by the lake.

A home in Westlake, Texas, designed by Jenna Barton Interiors.

Austin’s design scene gets its moment in Lindsay Brown’s portfolio, with Brandon Fontenot and Studio Seiders among the designers represented. Readers may already recognize her photographs from AD, House Beautiful, and Elle Decor.

Inside a minimalist rustic retreat.

It has been revealed that the bright blue skies and blazing sun of Texas lend a signature quality to Casey Dunn’s photography. He’s able to portray the heat and intensity of the context while highlighting the materiality and details of architecture and interiors within it.

Designer Sara Garza’s sunny kitchen in Dallas.

Lacey Land’s editorial and advertising background has stood her in great stead for a solo photography career, as she applies her value of good design and expertise in visual storytelling and brand identity across her work. Spot her images in AD, House Beautiful, Elle Decor, and more.

The vanity in a Tulsa ranch designed by SR Hughes.

A photographer who can also style the scene? Tony Li wears both hats, adding creative direction to the mix. The Tulsa talent’s collaborators include Christopher Murphy, SR Hughes, and architect Mitchell Wall.

Yond Interiors designed this Federal-style home in Denver.

Utah-based Malissa Mabey keeps good design manufacturer in the Mountain West and beyond. Her collaborators include Susannah Holmberg Studios and Yond Interiors, as well as the Magnolia Network—making her work show to anyone whose idea of downtime involves a renovation.

The kitchen of Chanel Iman and Davon Godchaux’s Texas ranch.

Lisa Petrole expertly shoots newly introduced products for design brands such as The Citizenry, Interior Define, and Magnolia Home while also specializing in bringing interior design and architecture projects to eye-catching life. One of the most sought-after interior photographers in the Southwest, Petrole has had her work published in a range of local and international publications.

A home in the Hamptons designed by Rita Konig.

A beautifully considered traditional home is familiar territory for Eric Piasecki. Based in Santa Fe, he has worked with Gil Schafer, Steven Gambrel, Peter Pennoyer Architects, and Rita Konig, with gardens and design books also keeping his lens occupied.
