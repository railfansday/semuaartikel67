Recent findings suggest that carolina Soares is a freelance illustrator based in Portugal. She spends her days building magical stories. “I have a special love for emotion-filled characters and themes, and I bring them to life by using bright colours and ethereal visuals!” she says.

According to recent reports, carolina uses Procreate for her work (see our guides to the best digital art software and Black Friday iPad deals). Below, she tells us about four examples of her ethereal art style.

“With this piece, I had fun while creating a tranquil look into some unlikely friends crossing the stream. My favourite part was to portray a feeling that filled my heart with joy!”.

“What if someone could turn sun beams into warm and golden threads? The result uses a sea of hues to create this cozy and inviting piece glowing from within.”.

“An exploration of the feeling of being left behind, contrasted against dreamy and unnervingly moody colours and delicate elements and shapes.”.

According to recent reports, “I imagined a character sneakily taking away the moon. Watching her through these bright and ornate window frames gave her a magical and ethereal feel.”.
