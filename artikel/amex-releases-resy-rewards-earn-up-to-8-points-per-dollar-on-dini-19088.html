If you like earning American Express Membership Rewards points while dining out, there's some good news: You can now earn up to 8 points per dollar at participating Resy restaurants thanks to a upcoming program called Resy Rewards.

The new program allows eligible U.S. American Express cardholders to earn an additional four Membership Rewards points per dollar spent at participating restaurants, in addition to the points they already earn with their cards.

Instead of launching a separate dining rewards currency, Resy is leaning further into the American Express ecosystem, which makes sense given that Amex acquired Resy in 2019.

Resy Rewards is available through a fresh Rewards tab in the Resy iOS app, where eligible cardmembers can find and book participating restaurants.

It has been revealed that the program is launching first at select New York City restaurants, including Carne Mare, Le Crocodile, Hearth, Kiko, OPTO and Le Pavillon, with intends to expand to additional U.S. cities and select Tock venues in 2027.

To start earning bonus Membership Rewards points through Resy Rewards, you'll first need to enroll an eligible U.S. Amex card that earns Membership Rewards points.

According to recent reports, once enrolled, you'll need to book a reservation through the Rewards tab in the Resy app, dine at a participating restaurant and pay with your enrolled Amex card to earn the additional points. Keep in mind that just dining at a participating restaurant without booking through Resy Rewards won't qualify for the bonus points.

And this is where things get particularly interesting for Amex Gold cardholders.

Industry observers note that the already earns four points per dollar at restaurants worldwide (on up to $50,000 in purchases per calendar year, then one point per dollar). Add the four bonus points from Resy Rewards, and you're looking at eight Membership Rewards points per dollar at eligible restaurants.

That means a $200 dinner could earn you 1,600 Membership Rewards points instead of the usual 800 points with the Amex Gold Card.
