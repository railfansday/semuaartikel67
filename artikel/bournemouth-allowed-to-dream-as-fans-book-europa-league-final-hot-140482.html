Marco Rose replaced Andoni Iraola - who ended up at Liverpool - in the summer.

Bournemouth are dreaming of winning the Europa League after staging a 10-man fightback to join a select band of teams to win their first five European games.

The Cherries trailed 1-0 to Iago Aspas' goal at Celta Vigo, and then went down to 10 men midway through the first half when Bafode Diakite was sent off.

But second-half goals from Alvaro Rodriguez and Rayan gave the Cherries a 10th consecutive win in all competitions, extending their club record run.

Bournemouth are only the third side to win each of their first five European matches (excluding qualifiers and including the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup), after Nuremberg in 1961-62 and Ararat in 1972-73.

They top this season's table as the only club to win five out of five - and are closing in on a top-eight spot, which would mean they skip a play-off round and go straight into the last 16.

Some fans have told the BBC they have already booked hotels in Frankfurt for the final on 26 May.

As supporters sang about reaching that game, midfielder Alex Scott was asked for his thoughts on TNT Sports.

"Of course that's our dream. When you grow up wanting to be a footballer you dream of winning trophies," Scott said.

"Where this club has come from to where it is now, full credit to everyone who has built it.

It has been revealed that "For players, it's a dream to be on the pitch playing for this club. We have still got wins to get to qualify into the top eight, and from there all focus is on pushing forward and hopefully we can go on and win it.".

The Cherries are in unprecedented times, with their biggest trophy to date being when they won the second-tier Championship in 2014-15.

In terms of cups, they won the 1983â84 Associate Members' Cup - which later became the EFL Trophy.

Head coach Marco Rose, who replaced Andoni Iraola in the summer, mentioned: "It's always allowed to dream.

"If you get a competition like this you want to achieve the best possible thing. We know it's far away to go. Many games. Now it's about getting into the top eight to avoid more games and then we can see step by step.".

As well as just winning their games, Bournemouth are firing in the goals. They have netted at least twice in each match during this 10-game winning run.

And they are the first side to score at least twice in each of their first five matches in Europe since Queens Park Rangers in 1976.

The win on Thursday was made even more impressive by the fact they contested 75% of the game with 10 men.

It came days after Manchester United seemed to implode after an early red card against Liverpool, losing 4-0.

Former England manager Glenn Hoddle said on TNT: "They have deserved this result. They've played beautifully with 10 men - probably as good as I've seen a team play defensively and with the ball.

Recent findings suggest that "I've not seen a team go down to 10 men for so long and dictate a game and deserve to win the game. They were absolutely brilliant.".

Cherries boss Rose told BBC Radio Solent: "What a team, what a spirit, what an energy.

In a recent development, "I stated to Ty [Tyler Adams] sometimes it's not about tactics and formations and stuff like this. [David] Brooks did a great job when we got one player less - he played right defender like he played it for 10 years. We changed in the right moment. Everyone did his job on a really high level.

"We were just relentless and we never stopped to believe in this win and that's why substantial, big, big congratulations to the team.

"You feel if you're one player down that it's a difficult night, but to handle a difficult night like this, it just indicates the character of the team and of the whole group.".

The coach's belief in what they are doing filters through to the players.

Amine Adli, who set up the equaliser, told the BBC: "At half-time, everyone still believed we can turn the game in our way and this is what happened.

Industry observers note that "Everyone was like 'guys we can make it, we have to believe. If we score the game can change.'".

Earlier this month, the attacking midfielder mentioned he saw a lot of similarities to the Bayer Leverkusen team he was part of in 2023-24.

That is not a bad comparison, considering they went unbeaten to win the domestic double, as well as reaching the Europa League final.

So who can blame the Cherries for dreaming?
