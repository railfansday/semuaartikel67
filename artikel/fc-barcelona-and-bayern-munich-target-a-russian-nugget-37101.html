Interesting figures for a player who had been targeted by Paris Saint-Germain.

And in fact, two other big names are now interested in it. With his four goals in the Champions League, the former Lokomotiv Moscow player caught the eye of FC Barcelona and Bayern Munich according to Fanatik.
