Shiba Inu’s lead ambassador Shytoshi Kusama has stayed silent on social media, with his last post dated May 13, 2026, leaving many questions in the Shiba Inu community.

Kusama remains a prominent figure in the Shiba Inu ecosystem and before then he had frequently communicated with the community about developments, project direction and broader ambitions. His limited public presence, which goes back to mid-2025, has therefore become a talking point for holders who want updates from the project's leadership.

Recent findings suggest that with no confirmed return date or fresh public statement from Kusama, speculation in the community continues. At the start of the year, Kusama revealed he was working on an independent AI project.

His silence on social media hasn't been a complete blackout, as Kusama updates his X location and bio in the meantime. As reported, Kusama changed his X bio to “polish” and his location to “close.”.

Industry observers note that A low profile on X, however, does not establish that Kusama has left the Shiba Inu project or stopped contributing behind the scenes. No such departure has been confirmed in the information available at press time.

The question remains as to when Shytoshi Kusama will address the community again, with the timing yet unknown.

But the Shiba Inu community might have something to look forward to on November 19, 2026, after a teaser was dropped by a longtime Shiba Inu community member, Mazrael, in an X post.

Industry observers note that mazrael wrote a brief post that stated, “Here goes something,” with the “eyes” emoji and a time: November 19 at 11 a.m. UTC.

Sources indicate that though the post did not disclose what is in the works, the timing has sparked the curiosity of Shiba Inu community members, with speculation mounting about a possible announcement or enhancement. At press time, no further details have been provided, and what the teaser leads to remains unknown.

In a separate post, Mazrael shared an update on the MetaMask X ticker widget, which demonstrated copycat tokens for BONE and TREAT, saying that MetaMask was fixing the root cause, not patching tokens one at a time.
