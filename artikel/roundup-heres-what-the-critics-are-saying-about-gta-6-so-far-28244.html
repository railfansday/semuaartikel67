Roundup: Here's What The Critics Are Saying About GTA 6 So Far

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The first reviews for GTA 6 are officially out now, and as you've probably already seen, we've got one here at Pure Xbox! We gave the game an "Outstanding" 10/10, which isn't entirely surprising considering the history of this series.

Sources indicate that but what about everyone else? GTA 6 is nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards, so does it live up to that recommendation? Are the critics overwhelmingly enamoured with it? That's what we've been intrigued to find out.

So, here's what the critics are saying about GTA 6 so far:.

Recent findings suggest that "Somehow, GTA 6 lives up to the hype. Rockstar Games has crafted the most incredible open world I've ever experienced in a video game, packed with so much detail that I'll want to continue exploring it for months and likely years to come.".

"GTA 6 is the deepest, densest, most accomplished open world since Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has set a fresh sandbox standard here, with a world that feels alive and populated with real people; Leonida is a place where it appears almost anything can happen at any time.".

It has been revealed that "A game so good I asked our tech team if I could somehow give it 11/10. GTA 6 is an absolute masterclass in genre-mashing. With meticulous detailing and levels of immersion that almost feel dangerous, it's steeped in steamy satire — with story beats that often cut close to home as they relish in laugh-out-loud absurdity. Given the state of the industry, it's sobering that we might never see another game like this again. Grand Theft Auto 6 is a cover-to-cover masterpiece without equal.".

Sources indicate that "Grand Theft Auto VI is a generational game that stands toe-to-toe with many of the great crime stories and satires across other media. It showcases both the entertainment value of interactive play and the current high-water mark for technical and artistic detail in games. Hunt for and count up all the minor faults or foibles you like, but then note how you just spent another hour lost in this remarkable escape into criminality, wanton violence, egregious sex, and the elusive potential of true love.".

It has been revealed that "This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7.".

According to recent reports, "GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation. Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality. There's a degree of sophistication to everything GTA 6 does, and there's no aspect of the series' gameplay that hasn't been improved over past instalments.".

"I regularly find myself in love with video games, but it’s extraordinarily rare that I find myself in complete awe of them. Put simply, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played.".

According to recent reports, "A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that.".

"Grand Theft Auto VI delivers a remarkably immersive and ambitious open-world experience, blending thrilling gameplay, diverse activities, and a compelling relationship between Jason and Lucia. While uneven storytelling, pacing challenges, and occasional performance drops prevent it from reaching perfection, the richly realized world of Leonida and its extraordinary attention to detail make this an unforgettable crime adventure that largely lives up to its monumental expectations.".

It has been revealed that "Grand Theft Auto VI is a great game that lives up to so much of the hype and expectations. Lucia and Jason are a well-written and compelling pair of protagonists, and the side characters you meet on their journey are great, even if the need to constantly raise the stakes across such a long story lessens its impact. You do have so much more freedom with how to tackle missions, though, and Leonida is a world filled with things to do and discover that will keep you coming back for a long time.".

As you can see, GTA 6 is a darling of the critics right now, and its initial rating on Metacritic sits at a whopping 99, which makes it the highest-rated game of 2026 (and literally the joint best game of all time if it keeps up this pace).

It has been revealed that it should be noted that the vast majority of reviews are for the PS5 version, but both the PlayStation and Xbox versions are both sitting at 99 as things stand. That figure will probably change, but not to any significant degree.

History tells us that we should have always expected a mainline Grand Theft Auto game to get incredible review scores, but it was really hard to see how they'd live up to expectations with this one. Nevertheless, they've somehow done it!
