Real-life sisters Dakota and Elle Fanning are sharing the screen for the first time and playing siblings in Sony’s adaptation of author Kristin Hannah’s popular novel, “The Nightingale.” It will be released in theaters on March 19.

Set during World War II, the Fannings play sisters in France who struggle to survive and resist the occupation by Nazi Germany in the country. Elle plays Isabelle Rossignol, while Dakota plays Vianne Mauriac. The film tells the “powerful, emotional story of two sisters forced to embark on separate courageous, dangerous paths in a fight for survival and freedom during World War II,” the logline reads. The “Nightingale” cast also includes Edmund Donovan, Shira Haas as Rachel de Champlain, Mark Rylance as Julien Rossignol, Albrecht Schuch, Douglas Hodge, Gwilym Lee and Vinette Robinson.

The Fannings previously both starred in the 2001 movie “I Am Sam,” with Sean Penn and Michelle Pfeiffer. Dakota starred as Lucy, the young daughter of Penn’s character, with Elle playing the 2-year-old infant version of the character on screen. This weekend, Elle co-stars in “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” prequel, where she plays a younger version of Effie Trinket, the fashionista previously portrayed by Elizabeth Banks. Dakota starred in this year’s drama “The Sun Never Sets” and TV highlights like “All Her Fault” and “The Perfect Couple.” She also executive produced the Apple TV series “Margo’s Got Money Troubles,” starring Elle.

It has been revealed that the film is directed by Michael Morris, with a screenplay by: Dana Stevens and Ann Peacock. It’s produced by the Fannings, Brittany Kahan Ward, Reese Witherspoon, Lauren Neustadter and Elizabeth Cantillon with executive producers including author Hannah, Emily Ferenbach and J. Michael Williams.
