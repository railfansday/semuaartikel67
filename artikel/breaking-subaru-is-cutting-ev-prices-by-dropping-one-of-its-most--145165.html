Subaru vehicles are known for their dual-motor all-wheel-drive (AWD) capabilities, but to cut costs, the company is now offering front-wheel-drive (FWD) versions on select electric models.

The Uncharted EV went on sale earlier this year as a smaller, sportier entry-level electric crossover positioned below the Solterra.

It was also the first Subaru model in about 30 years not to come with standard all-wheel drive. After unveiling the Uncharted EV last summer, Subaru stated, “For buyers focused on affordability and range, the entry-level Uncharted Premium FWD model will be offered in limited numbers.”.

It has been revealed that apparently, it wasn’t too limited. The 2027 Uncharted EV is now on sale, still with the base Premium FWD model equipped with a single 221-horsepower front-mounted electric motor.

Higher-priced Uncharted trims, including the Sport and GT, are upgraded with Subaru’s Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system with X-Mode, delivering a combined 338 horsepower.

The 2027 Subaru Uncharted EV Premium FWD starts at $34,995, while the Sport and GT are priced from $39,795 and $43,795, respectively.

In the US, the 2027 Solterra and larger Trailseeker are still equipped with standard AWD, but Subaru is introducing fresh FWD (4×2) variants in some markets at a lower price point.

In comparison, the 2027 Subaru Solterra and Trailseeker EV start at $38,495 and $39,995, respectively.

Subaru opened preorders for its upcoming 2027 electric range in Spain, including the Unchartered, Solterra, and E-Outback, which is sold as the Trailseeker in the US.

Sources indicate that all of which will be available with a lower-priced FWD variant. Like the Uncharted, Subaru will still offer AWD on higher-priced Solterra and E-Outback trims.

The new Subaru Uncharted (4×2) starts at €26,525 ($28,900), the Solterra (4×2) starts at €35,025 ($38,200), and the E-Outback (4×2) starts at €36,525 ($39,800) in Spain, including subsidies, discounts, and other incentives.

Preorders are open for the Uncharted, while the Solterra and E-Outback are expected to launch in mid-2027.

Industry observers note that will Subaru expand FWD (4×2) to other vehicles and markets, like the US? The Uncharted is still Subaru’s only EV sold with standard FWD. The 2027 Solterra and Trailseeker are equipped with AWD and X-Mode.

Industry observers note that for a small electric car driven on pavement I prefer RWD, which offers less complexity, better uphill traction, and a tighter turning circle. I love the handling of my last two cars, a RWD Miata and a RWD BMW i3 (old style).

Subaru is already selling considerably more EVs in the US, with three affordable, practical electric SUVs: the Uncharted, Solterra, and Trailseeker.

In Spring 2027, it will add its first three-row electric SUV, the Getaway. It wouldn’t be a surprise to see Subaru introduce a FWD option for other models, like the Solterra, at a lower price point.

Then again, AWD is used significantly more in the US than in Spain and other markets. For now, the Uncharted remains Subaru’s sole FWD model.
