Cole Palmer was involved in an angry exchange with Joao Pedro after the final whistle of Chelsea’s woeful home defeat to Leeds United.

Xabi Alonso’s side were thrashed 4-1 at Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon with the visitors scoring three times in the second-half to seal a famous win in the capital.

Emmanuel Emegha opened the scoring for the hosts after 25 minutes before Noah Okafor levelled the contest three minutes later.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin put Leeds back in front eight minutes into the second-half before Okafor added his second of the afternoon.

Brenden Aaronson wrapped things up with a fine solo effort in added time to seal win that moved Leeds ahead of Chelsea in the Premier League table.

Pedro was not in the squad, still recovering from a thigh injury that has now ruled him out of Chelsea’s last five games.

It had been hoped he would be fit after the international break but Xabi Alonso revealed on Friday the striker was still not back in training.

The Brazil international was in attendance on Saturday afternoon and was involved in a tense exchange with one of his teammates immediately after the game.

The Athletic report Palmer appeared to have an angry exchange of words with Pedro as he left the pitch.

In footage shared on social media, Palmer is seen approaching his teammate, covering his mouth with his hand as he speaks. Pedro puts his arms on his teammate’s shoulders, shrugging his shoulders in response as Palmer walks away unhappy.

During Alonso’s post-match press conference, one journalist suggested the disagreement was sparked when Pedro tried to get Palmer to go and applaud the fans with the England international seemingly reluctant.

Alonso said of the incident: ‘No, we were disappointed, the fans were disappointed because the expectations from them are high and we feel sorry for them and we need to go through together and to find solutions to that and now, as I said, for sure it’s not the easiest moment.

‘It’s a fragile moment but we need to work together and stick together because there is still so long way to go..

Palmer had played the full 90 minutes but was unable to inspire his side who have now won just one of their last five games.
