Sources indicate that elon Musk has questioned Nvidia’s decision to part ways with Jensen Huang, comparing it to taking a star player off a winning team. Musk spoke at The New York Times’ DealBook Summit on Wednesday, CNBC reported.

Recent findings suggest that “You’ve got a winning streak … and then you kick Michael Jordan off your team,” Musk told interviewer Andrew Ross Sorkin. “Doesn’t seem like a smart decision to me.”.

It has been revealed that huang resigned as Nvidia’s president and chief executive last week. He left after reports of affairs, including one with a woman US officials reportedly suspected of spying for China. Huang co-founded the chipmaker and had led it since 1993.

According to recent reports, musk said no one could really replace Huang. He added that President Donald Trump had also said publicly that Huang should have stayed, CNBC reported. Nvidia has ruled out a return for Huang.

In a recent development, last week, Musk replied to a post on X urging him to hire Huang, saying it would be an honour. He has not remarked what role Huang could take.

Recent findings suggest that musk also said he felt foolish about how much he spent on this month’s US midterm elections, Al Jazeera reported. He spent at least $474m backing Republican candidates, according to CNBC.

In a recent development, republicans lost heavily in the elections. Musk mentioned the results showed that money alone cannot decide an election, but that his candidates might have done worse without it.
