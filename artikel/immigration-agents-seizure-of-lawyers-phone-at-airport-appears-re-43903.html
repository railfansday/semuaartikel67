A federal judge ruled Friday that it is likely that U.S. Customs and Border Protection agents were retaliating against an immigration attorney for criticizing federal immigration policies when they seized his cellphone at the Boston Logan International Airport in September 2025.

Industry observers note that U.S. District Judge Allison D. Burroughs of the District of Massachusetts also found that federal agents likely violated the First Amendment rights of Andrew Lattarulo, a co-owner of the law firm Georges Cote in Massachusetts. Lattarulo runs an Instagram account featuring videos explaining immigrants’ rights and critiquing federal immigration policies.

After agents seized Lattarulo’s phone without a warrant and downloaded a copy of its contents, they returned the phone. The government has claimed that the seizure was part of an investigation into his firm for potentially employing unauthorized immigrants.

Lattarulo’s lawsuit alleges that agents targeted him because of his advocacy and seized information that included confidential communications with his clients.

Burroughs’ ruling mandates that federal officials preserve all copied cellphone data without accessing it as litigation continues, according to a story by Law.com.
