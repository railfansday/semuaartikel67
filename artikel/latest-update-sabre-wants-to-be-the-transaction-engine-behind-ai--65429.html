It has been revealed that AI platforms could become a newly introduced front door for travel bookings. Sabre wants to handle the transactions behind them, even if someone else controls the customer relationship.

According to recent reports, sabre spent years modernizing its shopping, booking, and servicing systems for AI applications to use. Now it's pursuing the major AI platforms themselves, aiming to keep travel transactions flowing through its systems even as the customer relationship moves elsewhere.

Sabre's latest Mosaic Travel Connector for Grok Bot is the latest step. Confirmed this week and still in private beta, it is designed to bring flight and hotel shopping, booking, and servicing into the AI assistant.

The stakes are high for airlines and hotels. AI assistants could send them customers without travelers ever visiting their websites, but the platforms could also decide which handful of offers consumers see.

Sabre CEO Kurt Ekert explained why that matters: "the consumer will not paginate through results. They're going to get back one, two, maybe three options and choose from that.".
