Cryptocurrency exchange KuCoin has rolled out its largest Black Friday promotional event to date, putting more than 1.1 million USDT in rewards on the table as exchanges compete for retail trading volume ahead of the holiday season.

Running from November 16 to December 1, the campaign triples the size of KuCoin’s previous November promotions, which historically ranged from 15,000 USDT to 360,000 USDT.

Unlike past years that focused on single-product giveaways, the 2026 edition spans five trading and payment verticals—and adds VIP hospitality packages for the Tomorrowland Winter 2027 music festival.

The event marks the opening stage of KuCoin’s two-part PROOF series, titled ‘Tis The Season, with a separate Holiday Edition scheduled to follow from December 11 to December 30.

Futures traders stand to receive the largest share of the rewards, with up to 550,000 USDT available through trading-volume competitions, TraderPro return-on-investment (ROI) contests and a lucky draw.

The remaining rewards are spread across other KuCoin services. Spot and margin traders can each compete for up to 100,000 USDT by completing trading tasks and reaching volume targets. VIP owners have up to 150,000 USDT available through trading challenges and volume-based leaderboards, while KuMining is offering up to 100,000 USDT to users who purchase cloud mining hashrate.

Another 100,000 USDT is allocated to crypto payments, with customers able to receive fee cashback when buying or selling cryptocurrencies through bank cards or peer-to-peer (P2P) transactions.

Beyond the trading and payment rewards, the campaign also features a Grand Lucky Draw with additional prizes, including USDT, KuCoin merchandise gift boxes and a Tomorrowland Winter 2027 festival package with accommodation.

The Tomorrowland prizes are part of KuCoin’s partnership with the festival brand. Under the agreement, which runs from 2026 to 2028, KuCoin serves as the Exclusive Crypto Exchange and Crypto Payments Partner for Tomorrowland Winter and Tomorrowland Belgium.

In a recent development, the advertised 1.1 million USDT in prizes covers several types of rewards, rather than direct cash payouts alone. These include fee cashback, trading-related rewards and prizes tied to competition results and trading volumes.

KuCoin has not yet provided details of the second phase, the Holiday Edition, scheduled to run from December 11 to 30. The exchange remarked information about its activities and rewards would be declared separately.
