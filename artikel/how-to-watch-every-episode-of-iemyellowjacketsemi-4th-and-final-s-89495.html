The girls are finally home. But the wilderness might’ve come back with them.

It has been revealed that in the fourth and final season of Yellowjackets, we finally see the titular high school soccer team return to suburban life in New Jersey after a year in the snowy Canadian woods following their plane crash. But just because they’ve been rescued doesn’t mean they’re safe. Shauna, Misty, Val, Taissa, Melissa, Lottie, and co. are haunted by their violent past in the ’90s timeline, and they continue to wrestle with their dark secrets in the present day.

In a recent development, some stars weren’t ready to say goodbye yet. “It’s been bittersweet. Neither of us were ready for it to be over,” Melanie Lynskey, who plays adult Shauna, remarked on Today. “I feel like we could’ve done this for years. Years and years,” Christina Ricci, who portrays adult Misty, added. While the pair reunited with their season 3 co-stars like Tawny Cypress and Hilary Swank for the final chapter, they were also joined by new additions like June Squibb, Molly Ringwald, and Dulé Hill.

Here’s how to tune in for the last season of Yellowjackets.
