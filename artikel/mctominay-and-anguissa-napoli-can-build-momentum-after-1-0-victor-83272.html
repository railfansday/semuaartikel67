Scott McTominay hopes Napoli can ‘get a bit of momentum going’ after his goal allowed them to beat Torino, while Andre Frank Zambo Anguissa insists ‘we can climb back up the table.’.

It was a difficult match for the Partenopei, who struggled to break Toro down and also ran a risk when Ronaldo Mandragora thumped the crossbar from distance.

McTominay finally broke the deadlock with a strike from the edge of the area, set up for him by Noa Lang after a defensive run to win back the ball, turning it into an attacking situation.

The Scotland international has a real history against Torino, scoring four goals past them in only three meetings.

It has been revealed that “In football it’s about the next game, and the next game, you have to do your best. We have a team that wants to work, wants to push, and we want to win more games,” McTominay told Sky Sport Italia.

Allegri turned to his bench after the goal and noted: ‘See, they score when they smile, I told them to smile more.’.

“I am feeling much better,” smiled McTominay.

“It’s an important goal, but more importantly we won, we had to win. I’m happy for the fans, we need to get a bit of momentum going, keep winning.”.

Now there is a trip to Manchester City in the Champions League, which will feel like a derby for former Manchester United midfielder McTominay.

“A great game, we’ll recover now and then think about it from tomorrow.”.

There had been some changes and tactical experiments, moving to a 3-5-2 formation, but Antonio Vergara limped off after just 11 minutes and is now expected to miss Tuesday’s Champions League trip to Manchester City.

Kevin De Bruyne had initially been rested with that in mind too, but had to come on to replace Vergara, meaning extra fatigue on 35-year-old legs.

“When you work as a team, it becomes easier for everyone,” added Anguissa.

“We know what Scott can do, we’re happy he scored and these are three very vital points. We have to keep working together, it is the only way to get out of this situation. We are very concentrated and know what we need to do.

“It is crucial to think positive, take it one game at a time. I have faith, and I feel that this team can do anything. There’s a long way to go this season, we can climb back up the table.”.
