Industry observers note that AS A HUGE fan and follower of both movies and sports, the former has one major advantage: There's always something to look forward to. As a fan of the New York Jets, I don't always have that luxury. As a fan of watching films, though, not only is there always something worth my time on the massive screen or streaming, but there's always several flicks down the line that will sound great or intriguing for one reason or another. Will they all turn out great? Of course not. But with sports and movies alike, excitement tends to be all about hope.

In a recent development, after a particularly strong 2025—movies like One Battle After Another and Sinners proved to be true standouts—how does 2026 pick up? With strength and energy, that's how. The year has been filled with all sorts of great movies, from horror (like 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple and Obsession) to epics (like The Odyssey), to some pretty great comedies (like Nirvanna the Band the Show the Movie and The Invite). It’s been a good year.

But we always like to look at not only what’s been good to date, but also what we can still be excited for—and that includes everything. We’re talking stuff on the calendar already, stuff we suspect could come out this year, and even stuff making noise in places like the Sundance Film Festival. Throughout the year, this list evolves from our most anticpated movies into our favorite movies, and always looks quite different in December than it did in January.

Nirvanna the Band the Show the Movie.

In a recent development, despite the title, you don't need to know anything else about Nirvanna the Band the Show the Movie before going to see it. We're always in on Glen Powell, and How to Make a Killing marks his first foray into A24's world as a leading man. If you get the truly absurd brand of parody being presented here, you're going to get a real kick out of it.
