AC Milan are desperate to add more quality and depth to their defence and two names are dominating the talk presently.

Ahead of the January mercato, there has been plenty of talk about Milan’s top target. It may well be Endrick or Nicolo Tresoldi, but the Rossoneri are also waiting to add a defender to Ruben Amorim’s squad and one name takes the headlines.

In this morning’s edition of Gazzetta dello Sport, as seen below, the headline is that the Rossoneri will make an assault for Inacio in January. Amorim is desperate to reunite with Sporting’s captain and the road there is certainly plausible to travel.

Industry observers note that if Goncalo Ramos was the man for the attack, his namesake will be the one to help the defence. So, whilst news of attackers dominates the headlines, at Casa Milan, it’s not all they’re thinking about.

Recent findings suggest that A deal for Inacio has been ‘pending since the summer’, and with the injury problems in the first half of the season, another defensive addition is a must. There is respect for the club from Inacio and whilst a deal still needs to be completed, their chances look good.

The order from Gerry Cardinale is clear: sign Inacio in January.

In the summer, there will be another reinforcement, hopefully. The idea of signing Virgil van Dijk has been touted heavily in recent days and Cardinale is determined to sign him for next season. So, on January 1, they can begin to start working on a pre-contract arrangement.

The Dutchman is ‘looking for a new challenge’ and a move to San Siro would provide that. A move similar to Zlatan Ibrahimovic’s return or even Luka Modric’s arrival, he’d bring a heap of experience at the top level, which is a must.

Inacio is the target for now, but then the attention will turn to van Dijk in defence.
