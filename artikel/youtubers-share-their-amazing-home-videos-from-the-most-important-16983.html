Happy birthday, Xbox! It's exactly 25 years today since the original Xbox console launched in North America on November 15th of 2001, and it turns out multiple people captured the moment via some amazing home video footage.

Recent findings suggest that we've talked about one of these videos before — the ridiculously cool one from MyRetroLife, who has become a YouTube star since uploading his footage multiple years ago. His short clip of "booting up an original Xbox on launch day" has a whopping 4.6 million views, while his more elaborate story about it (which you can watch down below) has almost 900,000 views.

We're not affiliated with him, but you should go check out his other stuff as well - it's really entertaining!

It turns out there's more footage on YouTube from November 15th that we weren't aware of though, with this next example coming from The Great Gambino. They have much less of a high-profile following than MyRetroLife, but their three-minute Xbox footage is equally as interesting and nostalgic to watch, so it's well worth a view!

Apparently, this video was somewhat quietly uploaded to their YouTube channel a few years ago, but exploded into the millions of views after other people reposted it to the likes of TikTok, Facebook and other social media platforms.

They uploaded the most complete version of it a couple of years ago, which you can see below:.

Of course, some of us Pure Xbox users were also there back on November 15th of 2001, unboxing our original Xbox consoles for the first time, but it's quite special to see the moment captured on camera for us to see in 2026!

There's more official footage of the OG Xbox launch out there as well if you go searching for it, but we reckon it's the home videos that paint a more accurate picture of what it was like to be an Xbox fan at the very beginning.

It's also the kind of thing that makes you realise how old you truly are!
