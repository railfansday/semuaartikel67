Liverpool and Manchester United have been linked with a lot of the same players over the last few months.

Even though both clubs are on the complete opposite side of the Premier League table to one another, they're still two of the most recognised teams in the world.

Thanks to this, no matter how they're performing or how they performed the season before, they can always attract some of the biggest stars.

However, with how insane the transfer market is at the moment, clubs are now trying to find talented young players before they reach their peak.

Not only does this allow them to develop young talent but, it also means that they don't have to spend an arm and a leg on new players.

Recently, this transfer regime has become standard across the board and now, the Reds are going to have to try their best to beat their arch-rivals to the transfer of this forward.

Liverpool are trailing behind Manchester United for the transfer of Gabriel Rajkovic.

Liverpool's main focus over the last couple of transfer windows has been improving their attack.

Mohamed Salah exited Anfield this summer and, even though Bradley Barcola was brought in to replace him, it still doesn't feel like their attack is right just yet.

And, with questions still surrounding the future of Rio Nguomoha and Cody Gakpo, nothing is set in stone.

This lack of knowing has caused many links to emerge with a fresh patch from TEAMtalk now linking the Anfield outfit with a new attacker.

"Gabriel Rajkovic is fast becoming one of the most-watched teenagers in world football and TEAMtalk sources can reveal that both Manchester United and Barcelona are leading the chase for his signature," they wrote.

"Sources indicate that Liverpool, Manchester City, Tottenham Hotspur, Newcastle United, Brighton, Aston Villa and other Premier League sides are interested observers.

"While Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, Borussia Dortmund, RB Leipzig, Inter Milan, Juventus and Como have also been noted with scouting the young attacker, who can operate on either wing or through the middle.

"Valerenga will want a substantial fee, potentially exceeding €18million (£15m), which would make Rajković the most expensive player ever sold from the Eliteserien.".

Gabriel Rajkovic would be an out of the box signing for Liverpool.

Over the last few years, Liverpool have tried to get better at signing youngsters before they become too expensive.

Recently, this has been proven by their acquisitions of Trey Nyoni and Ngumoha with other players also making their way into Liverpool's plans.

Right now, the Reds don't know how their team is going to look by the end of the season.

Several members of their squad have suffered injuries so, even by the New Year, change is likely to be afoot.

Rajkovic is someone who barely anyone will have heard of, however, this season alone, the 15-year-old has played in three games and has scored two goals.

Last term, he featured in 18 and scored three.

So, even though he's incredibly young, it's clear that he's very talented and, if someone such as Liverpool ends up getting a deal over the line for him, they'll have a young, versatile attacker to add into their mix.

That is, when he turns 18, of course.
