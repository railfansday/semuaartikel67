It has been revealed that team Cherry Presents, a fresh publishing label under Hollow Knight developer Team Cherry, and developer Studio Stobie have revealed Radio Rain, a moody platform adventure game set in a ruined, rainy world. It will be available for PC via Steam. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Moody radio frequencies and rain-soaked ruins.

A young girl, Cass, finds herself transported to an unfamiliar world in the shadow of a storm-covered mountain. City streets are coated in rain, the abandoned theme park is wracked by thunderstorms, and the great lake is enveloped by an eerie fog! After Cass frees the flame spirit Ember, they set off together to find a way home and uncover the truth behind the perpetual storm.

Along the way they’ll explore a range of rainy, run-down urban locations, battle an army of mischievous storm spirits, and befriend the city’s mysterious locals—all while vibing to the beats of a mysterious, moody radio station.
