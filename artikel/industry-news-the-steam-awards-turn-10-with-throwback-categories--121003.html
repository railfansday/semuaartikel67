Nominations for the 2026 Steam Awards are now open, and to mark the event's 10th anniversary, Valve has brought back some categories from years past and added a Hall of Fame.

According to recent reports, you've got until 6pm UK (10am Pacific) on 30th November to nominate your favourite games of the year across 14 categories. Returning throwbacks include Just 5 More Minutes, Defies Description, and This is My Job Now, which recognises games that simulate mundane real-life jobs.

The new Hall of Fame category will appear when voting opens, and will let players choose their favourite from Steam's previous Game of the Year winners to crown the best game of the past decade.

Most categories are only open to games released on Steam since last year's Autumn Sale.

According to recent reports, the exception is the Labo(u)r of Love award, which goes to older games that developers are still supporting with newly introduced content. Previous Labo(u)r of Love winners, including Warframe, Grand Theft Auto 5, Cyberpunk 2077, and Baldur's Gate 3, can't be nominated again.

Five finalists will be revealed in each category when voting opens alongside the Steam Winter Sale on 17th December. Voting closes at 10am Pacific on 2nd January, and the winners will be announced straight afterwards.

Nominating in at least one category also earns you the first level of Steam's Nomination Committee badge.

Earlier this month, Valve released a upcoming Steam update that adds Steam Deck framerate data to a game's store page, giving potential buyers an indication of how well a game runs on the handheld.
