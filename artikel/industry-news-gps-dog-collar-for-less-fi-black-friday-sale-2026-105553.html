Industry observers note that fi makes my favorite GPS pet collars and health trackers that I’ve tested. The devices make it easy to keep tabs on your dog or cat with live and historical location tracking. They also work much like wearable health monitors for people, as we see in smart rings, smartwatches, and fitness trackers, which help you stay on top of crucial wellness markers.

Fi devices help you keep an eye on your pet’s health by tracking activity and sleep. These pet trackers can also alert you to changes in your animal friend’s patterns, like slower movements or sleeping more than usual, which can be early signs of potential health concerns. I tested the Fi Mini for cats earlier this year, and I liked its long battery life, easy USB-C charging, and compact size that easily fits my cat’s existing collar. (Read my whole review here.).

If you’ve been curious whether one of these wearables would fit your pet, now’s a great time to try for less, with Fi’s Black Friday sale, which runs through December 1 and offers half off the Fi Mini, Fi Series 3+, and Fi Ultra.

In addition to these pet trackers, we’re already covering plenty of early Black Friday deals, and we’ll ramp up our coverage through Cyber Monday.

In a recent development, the Mini GPS and AI-powered tracker is the lightest and smallest I’ve tested, measuring 1.5 x 1.0 x 0.5 inches. It comes with a strap loop and two soft cases for attachment. I loved that it has a super-long battery life compared to other models, lasting up to nearly two weeks, and that it uses the same USB-C charger as my iPhone, which made it extra convenient. The Fi Mini tracks daily and historical location data so you can monitor movement patterns over time, and it alerts you when your pet leaves the designated safe area. Through the app, you can also measure health metrics like sleep and activity and compare historical data to your pet’s norms. You can even upload vet records, receipts, and insurance information, and add your vet to the app to share pet-related documents and get appointment reminders.

I’ve started testing this on my sister’s dog, so my review is forthcoming. In the meantime, WIRED Commerce Director Cooper Fleishman uses the Fi Series 3 GPS dog collar on his mini goldendoodle, Howie, when he leaves them with a caretaker. Cooper likes being able to track Howie’s steps and see their neighborhood walks from afar. Knowing he can rapidly find his young, adventurous dog if Howie slips their leash and bolts after a squirrel gives Cooper extra peace of mind. This is Fi’s longest-lasting GPS, with up to three months of battery life and an LED night light, plus escape alerts and a virtual geofence you can set up. It also offers tons of tracking metrics, including activity, sleep, scratching, licking, eating, drinking, and even barking detection.

This is the GPS dog collar you need if your dog spends most of its time outdoors, such as on multiple acres or a farm. The Fi Ultra promises an off-leash GPS tracking solution using T-Mobile’s T-Satellite with Starlink, enabling it to work beyond the limits of traditional cellular coverage and through thick vegetation. The tracker attaches to your dog’s existing collar, but its high-powered tracking system requires charging every one to two days. The integrated clip works with harnesses, working dog vests, and thick leather collars. It also charges via a standard USB-C cable, is IP68-rated waterproof, and features Fi’s “CallBack Technology,” which uses sound and vibration signals to help you find your dog.
