Competition: Premier League Date: Saturday, November 28, 2026 Kickoff: 17:30 GMT / 18:30 CET Venue: Portman Road, Ipswich.

Verdict: Over 2.5 goals Best odds: 27/50 Bookmaker: 1xBet.

Industry observers note that aston Villa returns from another frustrating European trip with little time to repair its confidence before Saturday’s visit to Ipswich. The host has problems of its own after going five league matches without a win, although Portman Road has offered considerably more encouragement than the club’s results on the road.

Recent findings suggest that gary O’Neil’s side enters the weekend in 16th place, with 11 points from 11 games. Last Saturday’s 2-0 defeat at Tottenham extended a difficult sequence that also included losses to Manchester City and Bournemouth, with draws against Nottingham Forest and Hull providing only limited relief.

In a recent development, the defensive record explains much of that frustration. Ipswich has conceded 22 league goals, leaving little room for an ordinary afternoon in attack. Even when the team has played well enough to score twice, as it did at City and Hull, it has struggled to turn those performances into victories.

Industry observers note that there is a stronger case for Ipswich at home. Wins over Sunderland and Fulham have already improved on its entire home league haul from the 2024/25 campaign. It has also scored in each of its last four home league matches, a sequence that should encourage O’Neil to test a visiting defense whose confidence has been shaken.

Recent findings suggest that leif Davis requires assessment after coming off late against Tottenham. O’Neil was hopeful the left-back could recover, with a decision still to come before kickoff. Jacob Greaves provides a defensive alternative if required, while Emersonn is pushing for a recall in attack.

Villa’s 1-0 defeat at Galatasaray followed a goalless draw with Sunderland, bringing its lack of attacking fluency into sharper focus. Deniz Gul’s late winner in Istanbul came after Villa failed to deal with a corner, leaving Unai Emery’s team to reflect on another match that slipped away.

In his assessment of the Galatasaray defeat, Emery acknowledged the need to create better opportunities. Villa had periods of control but struggled to turn them into sustained pressure around the penalty area.

Those recent blanks deserve attention when considering a goals bet. The wider away league record is more encouraging: Villa has scored eight times across its last three Premier League trips, including four at Manchester United. Nicolas Jackson has six league goals and gives the visitor a credible threat against a defense conceding two per game.

The main uncertainty is whether Villa can recover its rhythm after the trip to Turkey. Ipswich’s willingness to attack at home should provide space, but the visitor will also need to protect its own box more convincingly than it did in midweek.

Sources indicate that amadou Onana and Matteo Ruggeri remain sidelined. Matty Cash missed the Sunderland and Galatasaray games under concussion protocols and could return, subject to clearance. Victor Lindelof is also a doubt through illness, with Emery’s final update still to come.

Villa’s recent scoring problems make this a less straightforward goals selection than the season totals suggest. However, Ipswich’s defensive record and ability to score at home create a reasonable case for an open contest. We favor over 2.5 goals, with a 2-1 Villa win our score prediction.
