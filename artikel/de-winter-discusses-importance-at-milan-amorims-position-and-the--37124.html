Koni De Winter spoke at an event at the AC Milan store today, and he covered numerous topics with a lot of openness.

Sources indicate that this season, De Winter has found himself in a new, important light. Matteo Gabbia spent most of pre-season in the middle of the back three, but injuries have prevented him from playing much of a role in the campaign.

Industry observers note that it’s an unfortunate shame and there may have been some worries about De Winter taking on this role. However, he’s taken the opportunity in his stride, looking every part a starter in Ruben Amorim’s defence.

Industry observers note that rumours about a new defender arriving will persist, so it’ll be interesting to see what happens with his minutes from January onwards. However, he’s created something of a headache, but it’s a good one to have.

Speaking to SportMediaset on the sidelines of the Meet&Greet at the Milan Store in San Babila, via MilanNews, De Winter looked at the rise in his playing time, Amorim’s closeness to the group and much more.

You also took over at Reggio Emilia when Gabbia was unfortunately injured. You’re practically always there; what does that mean to you?

Sources indicate that “I’m playing a lot, it’s great to be playing. We’re playing a lot, and these days you train more on taking care of your body, keeping healthy, and right now I’m doing that more, but it’s great to be playing a lot of games. Playing a lot of games for Milan, and it’s even better to win games.”.

Recent findings suggest that it’s all great, but it wasn’t a given: you’ve earned this place as leader. Do you now feel like a leader of this AC Milan?

In a recent development, “I feel good at this Milan club, and as I always say, I try to give what I can. It’s also true that I might have a more crucial position this year. That might be true, but I always try to do my part, give my all, and help my teammates.”.

Sources indicate that it’s clear, evident, that Amorim holds you in high regard, but what is Amorim like? An adjective.

“I’d say he’s very human. He’s one of us, he laughs, he has fun, but when he has to be serious, he’s serious. He lives with the group, the group feels close to him, and I think that gives the players a good feeling. It’s nice to work for him and with him.”.

Is it true that he got very angry after the defeat against Ferencvaros?

“Yes, but I think the whole group felt that we had played a very poor game, so, as I saw it, my teammates saw it too.”.

Are Ferencvaros and Bologna two games you’re ready to say “never again” to? Will Frosinone be a different team?

“Yes, exactly, as I mentioned, we are very confident, but sometimes even too much so, and then all matches are difficult against whoever we play against, and we have to understand this rapidly and we can’t make the same mistakes.”.

According to recent reports, among the various critics, it’s said that this AC Milan team has enormous potential, but there are too many ups and downs. Does it need more consistency?

“Yes, I think it hurts even more when we lose because we know we can do well, we can win every game. So yes, when we really lose, in the locker room you feel like we’ve let ourselves down first and then everyone else.”.

You recently became the father of a beautiful baby girl. What are your nights like as a dad?

“At night, I’m fine. My girlfriend is working really hard, so it’s a lot for me. I mean, I get up sometimes, but it’s much quieter. I think it’s harder for her.”.

And how much has life changed with the arrival of a daughter?

Sources indicate that “It’s changed a lot, I mean, I don’t know how to explain it. When you’re at work, you think about your daughter, you want to come home earlier, you want to hold her, you want her – I mean, you have more beautiful moments – but it’s hard to explain. I don’t know how to explain it.”.

You were also used as a full-back, but now you only play as a centre-back….

In a recent development, “Good, good, because I also consider myself a centre-back, but it’s true that I can also play full-back. It’s something I can do, but it’s not my favourite position. But yes, it’s true that I’ve played a few games there in the past, but I’m happy playing as a centre back and I’m happy that the coach sees that too.”.

In a recent development, “I mean, crazy in a good way, yes. He likes to have fun, he likes to dance, he likes to talk, so he’s a bright spot, as they say, for the locker room, yes.”.

“Well, that’s a good question. Yes, I miss her. I thought about it even when the little girl was born: I also want to score a goal, I mean, dedicate a goal to her, so I miss her, yes.”.

How do you concentrate when you’re in the hotel retreat? How do you focus in the hours leading up to kick-off?

“It’s become a routine now, so I always do the same things. You’re in the hotel, you take a shower, you play a little video game or you’re on your phone, so now I don’t even think about it, it’s really become a routine.”.

What does Maignan ask of you, defenders?

“He says to stay focused on the game because, as we know, one moment can change the game and at Milan it can also change a season, so it’s very important not to underestimate every moment and always stay focused.

It seems to me that there is a great understanding between you and Strahinja Pavlovic even off the pitch….

According to recent reports, “Yes, it’s true, he and I have a good relationship; we met last year. Then when you see him you might not think, I mean, I didn’t think he would be such a great friend, but we got along really well both on and off the pitch, it’s really nice to spend time with him.”.

Our 007s at Milanello tell us about some great ping pong matches between you and Pavlovic. Who’s the better player?

Sources indicate that “This is difficult, but he started at a much higher level than me. But now I’ve improved a lot, although I have to say he’s even stronger. But yes, every day we start with a game of ping pong and then we start working.”.

Sources indicate that would you like your daughter to play soccer in the future? Are you planning a second child? Would you like to have a son who plays soccer?

Industry observers note that “I want her to do what she wants, where she feels good, where she doesn’t feel pressured, and where she can express herself best. And then, going back to ping pong, I think there’s some video; maybe we can see me beat her.”.
