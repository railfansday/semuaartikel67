📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Maytag Man is one of the most enduring characters in American advertising, lonely because no one needs his help repairing a Maytag appliance. The Maytag brand, too, has endured—dating back to the days when Frederick Maytag’s washers used wooden tubs and cranks. Maytag was the first manufacturer to put an agitator at the bottom of a washing machine, a design still in use today. It was also the first to stack a washer and dryer and one of the earliest adopters of smart appliances. But mostly, the brand has eschewed novelty and staked its claim on durability and reliability for its heavy-duty dishwashers, washers, dryers, refrigerators, and ovens. This is true even as Maytag came under the ownership of former competitor Whirlpool.

Sources indicate that maytag remains one of the few appliance companies to offer 10-year limited warranties on essential parts. But Maytag promo codes are more ephemeral, offering short-term deals on appliances that will hopefully last a decade. Here’s how to get a Maytag coupon code and Maytag promo codes, and to find closeout deals on last season’s Maytag appliances to save even more.

A simple way to find discounts on Maytag appliances is to check out overstock and closeout deals, which feature substantial markdowns on last season’s models as Maytag clears space for new products. These deals don't need a coupon code but do require knowing the right place to look. Visit the Maytag outlet website for appliance closeout offers. Usually, these deals are available only until supplies last.

Like many major companies, Maytag offers professional discounts for military, first responders, health care workers, students, and teachers. To receive up to a 15% discount as an active military member, veteran, or spouse, you’ll need to create a Maytag account and verify your military status through the SheerID program. Maytag first responder and health care worker discounts also require SheerID verification. There’s also a discount program for students and teachers, including a teacher savings program and up to 15% off sitewide. Verified students can access student-specific pricing when moving into their first off-campus apartment.

Recent findings suggest that maytag is also offering deals on select smart appliances. This includes a unique combination toaster-microwave that’s about $100 off MSRP, and a slide-in electric range with smart connectivity that’s steeply discounted to $1,260—hundreds of dollars less than even other recent sale prices. You can check out the special Maytag deals on connected and smart appliances by scrolling to the bottom of Maytag’s smart appliance page.
