According to a report from German outlet Fussball News, Liverpool and Arsenal and PSG are leading the race for the 25-year-old, who Brighton value at €60 million (~£50m).

AC Milan, Juventus and Roma are all also reportedly in the chase for De Cuyper, who moved to the Premier League from Club Brugge in 2025.

Liverpool have been interested in De Cuyper since before his move to Brighton, and the dynamic fullback was considered as an option before the club signed Milos Kerkez.

With so many top teams interested, it is not hard to imagine a world in which De Cuyper's final price ends up significantly higher than the €60 million Brighton are reportedly asking for.

With Liverpool's spend since winning the league title in 2025 already over £650 million, the club may be reluctant to enter a bidding war with the likes of PSG and Arsenal.

De Cuyper would be a great addition at Anfield, but a deal won't be straight forward.
