📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that wendy's sells two sides you simply cannot find at McDonald's or Burger King: cups of chili (which you can easily turn into a game-day nacho dip) and its famous baked potatoes. Long past when other fast food empires might have chucked the baked potato as a nuisance menu item, Wendy's continues the tradition of serving up convection-cooked spuds, as it has done for over 40 years. Now, if a baked potato sounds grand right about now, but you have a daily protein goal you need to meet, you're in luck. While the potato itself actually puts up decent numbers, with 7 grams of protein, adding a double portion of chicken to it will put the protein content basically through the roof.

Recent findings suggest that the grilled chicken has about 19 grams of protein per serving, so with a double portion, plus the baked potato, you're getting a whopping 45 grams of the essential macronutrient in one sitting. When the average 200-pound man requires 72 grams of protein, that's over half the suggested amount needed in just one meal (and if you're worried about getting too much protein, you could just order one serving of chicken to put on your baked potato).

According to recent reports, unfortunately, short of ordering two salads and a baked potato, this spud boost simply cannot be obtained through digital means. Instead, you'll have to walk up to the counter, or drive to the drive-thru speaker, and place the order in person. Is that a little extra work? Maybe, but the protein bump is worth it.

We have to be honest; the idea of eating a plain baked potato and two portions of grilled chicken, as-is, isn't the most appetizing or flavorful meal to cross our minds. However, there are plenty of additional ingredients that you can order while you're at Wendy's to help make it a bit more palatable (and one of them will add significantly more protein to the dish). For example, if you wanted to keep the core elements the same with just one additional ingredient, you could drizzle on any of Wendy's sauces; we're partial to the idea of Creamy Ranch or Honey Mustard for this application, but if you like a little heat, go ahead and use the Scorchin' Hot.

If your stomach can handle the dairy, you might double up by requesting the sour cream, and also ask for a side of nacho cheese sauce, to give the baked potato not just a tender chicken topping, but a creamy, luscious element, too (if you like your cheese on the stronger side, too, Wendy's carries blue cheese crumbles as well as plain old shredded cheddar). Heck, add bacon crumbles to the meal. Finally, you simply cannot forget the iconic baked potato and chili pairing. When added to the spud and the chicken, the whole meal comes to 64 grams of protein because the chili contains an extra 19 grams.
