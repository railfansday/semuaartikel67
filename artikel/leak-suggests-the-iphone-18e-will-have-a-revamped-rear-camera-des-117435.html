Apple's next iPhone release (probably) isn't until sometime next year, but that doesn't mean leaks aren't coming in hot and ready for our perusal.

The latest leak about Apple's flagship hardware product concerns the rumored iPhone 18e. According to digital renders of the device seen by Engadget, you can expect the next budget iPhone to have a camera bump that looks quite different from the iPhone 17e that introduced earlier this year.

While the 17e has only a single camera lens flush with the phone's back, Engadget's source suggests that Apple is looking to give the 18e a camera plateau similar to the one on the iPhone Pro models.

The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free right now with T-Mobile.

Sources indicate that by that, we mean it will allegedly house the camera lens in a nearly square-shaped module measuring about 28 by 35mm. Alongside the lens, the housing will contain the LED flash and a rear microphone. Yes, to be as abundantly clear as possible, Apple is apparently still sticking with just one camera lens here, even if the phone might look like it has more going on back there this time around.

Interestingly, Engadget's report goes further, as they also saw renders of the still-not-available base iPhone 18 and the next iPhone Air. The iPhone 18 reportedly looks similar to previous models, with a thicker camera module. However, the iPhone Air 2 renders show a second camera lens on that phone. As you may or may not recall, the previous iPhone Air model only had one lens, so that would be a pretty substantial upgrade for that device.

We don't know for certain when these devices will actually launch, but previous reporting suggests that we should see some or all of them by the end of March 2027. They may or may not launch alongside a new MacBook Neo and some new iPad models.
