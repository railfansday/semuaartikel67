Recent findings suggest that jennifer Lopez is known to love a dramatic coat. Whether performing on stage in a custom Art Deco-inspired Michael Ngo robe or hitting the red carpet in a floral-embroidered Dolce & Gabbana Alta Moda 2026 piece trimmed with caramel fur, the pop star has long perfected the glamorous art of statement outerwear. And for her latest outing, she turned to a label that knows a thing or two about just that.

Recent findings suggest that showing up to Broadway for a Saturday night showing of Much Ado About Nothing, Lopez was spotted wearing a brown leather jacket with a short boxy silhouette and a beige fur collar. Though the piece has yet to be released, it was instantly recognizable as the work of Charlotte Simone—the playful London-based brand known for its leopard prints, accent furs, and long Penny Lane-style coats. The label reshared a video of the singer, confirming her new outerwear will be made available during its next drop.

Charlotte Simone counts everyone from Taylor Swift to Gigi Hadid to Madonna to Charli XCX to Anya Taylor-Joy to Suki Waterhouse as customers, and mere days ago, longtime fan Dua Lipa shared photos of herself wearing a fur-trimmed black leather jacket from the same collection as Lopez’s newly introduced find. The forthcoming drop is scheduled for the end of this month.

Sources indicate that lopez paired her coat with light-gray trousers, designed with a high rise and wide-leg silhouette. She secured the relaxed bottoms with a black belt, and stepped out of the theater with her family.
