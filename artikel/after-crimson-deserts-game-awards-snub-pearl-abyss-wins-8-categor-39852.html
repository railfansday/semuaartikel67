After Crimson Desert's Game Awards snub, Pearl Abyss wins 8 categories at the Korea Game Awards

Crimson Desert might have missed out on a Game of the Year nomination at The Game Awards, but it did just take home the major prize – along with seven others – at the Korea Game Awards.

Recent findings suggest that per Inven Global, in addition to taking home the Grand Prize and the Global Game Award, Crimson Desert won four technical categories: sound, graphics, character design, and planning/scenario. That's in addition to a Best Developer Award and protagonist Kliff's Korean voice actor, Lee Hyun, receiving the Popular Voice Actor award.

In a recent development, according to The Korea Herald, this is a record-breaking sweep for a game at the show. The previous best was six awards for a single game, a feat Lies of P and Lost Ark had both achieved.

Fans will be pleased that Crimson Desert is getting some recognition here, at least – many felt that the open-world behemoth had been snubbed at The Game Awards when nominations were unveiled earlier this week. In addition to missing out on a Game of the Year nomination, Crimson Desert has been nominated for only one category – Best Community Support.

Recent findings suggest that pocketpair publishing and communications lead John "Bucky" Buckley was one of the many fans disappointed about this – especially the fact that it missed out on the RPG and adventure game categories. "To say I'm disappointed would be an understatement," he noted. "What a shame. What a terrible shame.".

According to recent reports, however, developer Pearl Abyss has shown nothing but gratitude for Crimson Desert's Best Community Support nomination. "This wouldn't have been possible without you, Greymanes," it writes on the official Crimson Desert account. "Thank you for supporting us on this journey, it truly means the world to us.".

Despite Crimson Desert's AI backlash, Pearl Abyss is now implementing the tech "throughout our entire development pipeline" – including for upcoming Pokemon-like DokeV.
