Mikel Arteta has ruled David Raya out of Tuesday’s Champions League game against Borussia Dortmund but delivered an upbeat bulletin on the goalkeeper’s health.

The Arsenal star sustained an injury during the second half of Saturday’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

While it was clear that the Spain international was in significant discomfort, Arteta was unable to clarify the extent Raya’s injury in the immediate aftermath of the game.

The former Brentford shot stopper was reportedly able to drive home once he and his teammates had arrived back from the north east on Saturday evening, but he was absent from Monday’s opening training session.

Asked for an upgrade on the health of his No.1 who underwent MRI scans yesterday, Arteta remarked: ‘He’s not fit for tomorrow unfortunately but it doesn’t look too serious and we need more tests tomorrow but it’s quite encouraging with the pain that he was showing immediately after the action.

‘We were all quite worried but it seems to be better than what we expected.

‘The doctor explained what happened but it’s quite technical and when you look at the action there doesn’t seem to be much, but the extent of the pain that he had and the pain he had was quite worrying.’.

Industry observers note that he added: ‘We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain he had at that moment. The first test we have done indicates something much more encouraging.

‘Tomorrow we need to reaffirm that to be able to give you something more specific information.
