Rangers manager Derek McInnes praised his side for having the mentality to grind out last-gasp winners and hailed their late victory over Motherwell as a ‘top-class performance’.

McInnes admits he would have liked a more comfortable evening from a match which saw his side fire 29 shots with 11 on target, but he was happy with the level of chance creation and delighted to see Ryan Naderi serve up yet another crucial goal with a deflected effort that made the difference two minutes from time.

Rangers had opened brightly with a Manny Fernandez goal on eight minutes, but looked like sliding to a third straight league draw away from home after Jake Girdwood-Reich had levelled for the home side in the first half.

Industry observers note that ‘I thought it was a very good, strong performance. I’ve just been told that every one of my outfield players had a shot,’ remarked McInnes, whose men are four points behind Celtic with a game in hand against Aberdeen at Ibrox on Tuesday.

‘I’ve never known a team who practice so much shooting, and crossing and finishing during the week. It was the only thing missing for us, I thought it was a top class performance.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Away from home in the Premiership, you’re never going to dominate a whole game. There were little spells Motherwell had on transitions, but our press was strong and we were aggressive. I’m just disappointed it took so long to get the winning goal.

Derek McInnes revels in the victory at Motherwell at full-time.

Ryan naderi scored the dramatic late winner for Rangers as they closed the gap on Celtic.

It has been revealed that ‘Equally, on the sidelines, I felt a goal was coming. It’s OK me feeling that, but I think the players feel it too and it’s important they do. It would have been such a body blow if we hadn’t found a winning goal, given the stats and the performance.

‘It’s a tough away game and it’s the first time we’ve won here in three years, so that illustrates that.

‘When we pick the bones out of it, there will be a lot of what we ask for in that game.

Sources indicate that ‘It’s good that we’ve got that in us, a last minute goal, because we know how important they are in a campaign. I’ve never found mental strength an issue for my side.

Recent findings suggest that ‘On this fantastic run, we’ve been very strong. We were by far the better team. We dominated the chances and a lot of what I was hoping for was there.

‘It was just that execution. We’ve had this story before, but, as long as we keep winning, I’m all right with that.

Industry observers note that ‘I’m getting asked about not putting teams to bed. That’s another away game with two goals. It’s four in a row now. In our last two home games, we’ve scored four and three, so scoring goals isn’t really causing a problem, even if people are looking for a negative.

‘I think that performance was an improvement from where we’ve been in recent weeks.’.

McInnes also reserved special praise for matchwinner Naderi.

According to recent reports, ‘Ryan’s game is really strong at the minute. He looks like a very confident boy and so he should be,’ mentioned the manager. ‘Wrongly now, when you think of Ryan or people ask me, it’s about him knocking his pan in.

‘His pressing is unbelievable and he’s a massive horse up there. He runs and works and puts people under pressure, but he gets goals and has an influence. In that system tonight, his was the most important position for us, in terms of running channels and getting hold of the ball.

Manny Fernandez opened the scoring for the Ibrox men in the 2-1 victory.

‘He led the line brilliantly. It was a proper No 9 performance.

Naderi admits he is delighted to be scoring messy goals that are making a real impact on his side’s challenge for the title.

‘I like those games. Of course we would like to win 3-0, but, when you win in the last minute, that's something special,’ noted the Czech internationalist.

‘I can't score nice goals, only ugly ones.’.

It has been revealed that motherwell head coach Alfred Johansson said: ‘It is obviously tough to concede that goal at the very end. We had a feeling we could finish the game with a draw.

According to recent reports, ‘I think Rangers were better than us. They had a good start to the game and it was tough for us conceding a goal from a set-piece when we are not aggressive enough.

‘At the end, we lost the energy. Rangers were turning up the heat in the last 15, 20 minutes. We were fighting and surviving and then they scored.

According to recent reports, ‘Alex Paulsen played fantastic in goal. He has been making a lot of saves for us this season and, at the end, it is tough for him to concede a goal like that.’.
