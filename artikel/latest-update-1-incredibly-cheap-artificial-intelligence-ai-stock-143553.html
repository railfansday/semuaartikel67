Though artificial intelligence (AI) stocks have taken a beating lately, the financial performance of companies in this sector remains solid.

We have already seen that several AI infrastructure companies, specifically semiconductor stocks, have delivered impressive growth in revenue and earnings this earnings season. And now, Dell Technologies (DELL +0.60%), another key component of the AI data center ecosystem, has delivered phenomenal results.

Let's take a closer look at Dell's performance and guidance and check why Wall Street is bullish about this tech stock's prospects over the coming year.

Dell released its fiscal 2027 third-quarter results (for the three months ended Oct. 30) on Nov. 24. The company's revenue jumped by 103% year over year to a record $54.9 billion, well above the $49.3 billion consensus estimate. Dell's non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) increased at a significantly faster pace of 265% year over year to a record $9.45. Wall Street would have settled for EPS of $6.59.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

The incredible demand for the company's AI-optimized servers is behind this stunning performance. The company sold $22 billion worth of AI-optimized servers last quarter, up by 291% from the prior-year period. What's more, even traditional servers and networking equipment were in terrific demand, with revenue from their sales increasing by 235% year over year to $15 billion.

What's worth noting is that Dell received way more orders than it fulfilled last quarter. Specifically, the firm booked $60.2 billion in upcoming orders for its AI servers, taking its total AI backlog to $132.3 billion. It is worth noting that Dell received $60.9 billion in AI server orders in fiscal Q2 as well, which helps explain why its revenue pipeline continues to improve.

Recent findings suggest that healthy demand for AI servers has prompted Dell to raise its annual guidance once again. The company now expects 78% revenue growth in fiscal 2027 to $202 billion, higher than the previous estimate of $192 billion. It was originally expecting a 23% increase in revenue at the beginning of the fiscal year, to $140 billion. Even better, Dell now sees its fiscal 2027 non-GAAP EPS jumping by 188% to $29.68, more than double its $12.90 EPS guidance at the beginning of the year.

Though Dell hasn't issued fiscal 2028 guidance yet, I won't be surprised if the organization sustains its phenomenal momentum next year. That's because Dell already has a massive backlog, pointing to a significant increase in AI server revenue next year. The company is on track to end fiscal 2027 with $78 billion in AI revenue, more than 3x the $25 billion it generated from this segment last year.

Dell's backlog is likely to increase again this quarter, as the organization notes that its order "pipeline grew sequentially and remains multiples of our backlog." That should set up Dell's AI server business for another year of exponential growth in fiscal 2028 (which ends in January 2028 and overlaps with most of calendar 2027).

So, Dell stock seems well-placed for another impressive year in 2027 following 125% returns in 2026 as of this writing. That's exactly what Wall Street expects from the stock.

Dell has a 12-month median price target of $512.50, according to estimates compiled by CNN. That's 72% higher than its current stock price.

It is easy to see why analysts expect such strong upside from Dell next year. The company is growing at a red-hot pace and has a price-to-earnings ratio of just 12.5, lower than the S&P 500 index's earnings multiple of 20.3. The average earnings growth of S&P 500 companies is expected to be 33% in 2026, and Dell is on track to grow at a significantly faster pace.

Also, Dell's healthy backlog should help it outperform Wall Street's expectations again next year. It is worth noting that analysts have raised their earnings-per-share estimates for Dell following its latest report.

Assuming Dell's EPS reaches $32.87 in fiscal 2028 (which ends in January 2028) and it trades at 20 times earnings, in line with the S&P 500's earnings multiple, the stock could jump to $657. That's 117% higher than the current stock price, suggesting that this AI stock could more than double over the next year. As such, buying Dell is a no-brainer right now considering its phenomenal growth and cheap valuation, which should set the stock up for a solid 2027.
