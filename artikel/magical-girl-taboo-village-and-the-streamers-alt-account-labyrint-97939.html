Magical Girl Taboo Village and The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth delayed

Acacia has delayed (2) visual novel Magical Girl Taboo Village to 2027, and visual novel RPG The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth to an unannounced date. Both were previously due out in 2026.

Sources indicate that on November 27 at 20:00 JST, The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth publisher G-MODE Corporation will announce a new release date for its PC (Steam) version during the latest Acacia live stream on YouTube. A Switch version is also planned.

On December 1, Magical Girl Taboo Village publisher Good Smile Tech giant will announce a upcoming release date for its PC (Steam) version. A Switch version is also planned.

Both projects are currently in the “final adjustments” stage.
