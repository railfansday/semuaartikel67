Dominik Szoboszlai believes Rio Ngumoha has the ability to reach the top in his career by declaring that the sky is the limit for the Liverpool prodigy.

Ngumoha became the youngest ever player to score for Liverpool against Manchester United in Sunday’s 4-0 thrashing, when he also set up Florian Wirtz’s goal and delivered a superb performance.

The 18-year-old, who is already a full England international, became the youngest goalscorer in Liverpool’s history last season, when he got a 100th-minute winner against Newcastle when still 16.

But vice-captain Szoboszlai, who opened the scoring against United, urged Ngumoha to heed the advice of senior players and to try not to take on all the opposition defenders at the same time.

“The sky is the limit for him,” the midfielder noted. “Now he has to be clever and listen maybe to the older players who has a little bit more experience. Some of us more, some of us less.

“We try to help him but it's also two parts. He's young, he’s 18, so it's a long way to go and he has to ready for everything, the ups and downs. He cannot be really up when he's up, but he also when he's down a little bit, he cannot be really down so he has to find the balance.”.

Ngumoha highlighted his fearlessness with his willingness to run at United as he tormented Diogo Dalot.

And Szoboszlai added: “He’s confident. He’s confident in his self. I think he knows his own qualities and that's what makes him special.”.

The Hungary captain believes the key for Ngumoha is knowing when to dribble at defenders – and sometimes not to if he is outnumbered.

Sources indicate that “At 18, to go in a one with two, he wouldn't maybe do it. He's doing it. Sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't.

“This is the thing he has to learn that [in a] one-v-one, go 10 out of 10 times. One-v-two go, five out of 10 times. One-v-three, don't go!

“This is the thing he has to learn but now he's into it and highlighted it again today. He assisted Flo and scored from Bradley [Barcola’s pass], so we need him and he's an unbelievable player.”.

Szoboszlai also revealed that manager Andoni Iraola did not talk to his team after a massive win over their rivals, and that he would not have done had Liverpool lost.

According to recent reports, “After a win he doesn't speak. After a loss he doesn't speak. So after games, he never speaks,” he explained.
