According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6 is finally out on PlayStation and Xbox. So, I’ve been watching my friends play it, which has been a blast. And all of them are loving the game, but one annoying bit of the massive game that they all seem to hate is using ATMs. And it’s not just them. A lot of people online desperately want the ATMs improved in GTA 6.

Recent findings suggest that money plays a substantial part in Grand Theft Auto 6. And for much of the game’s runtime, you’ll be counting dollars carefully as you save what you can and spend cautiously. Part of this loop also involves putting loose cash into your bank account via ATMs. If you don’t do that, you can lose much of it when you die or get arrested. So it is vital to put it into your bank and savings account. Yet, for some odd, confounding reason, Rockstar has made the act of depositing and withdrawing money from an ATM very annoying.

Recent findings suggest that if you haven’t played GTA 6,Â you might just assume you go to an ATM and type in how much you want to take out or put in. Nope! Instead, you have to hold different shoulder buttons on the controller to scroll through how much you want to deposit or withdraw. And this scrolling function starts to speed up very quickly, often leading to me blowing past the amount I wanted and having to slowly scroll back, sometimes missing it again. It sucks!

“I’m loving this game, but why can’t I just type in fucking number on the ATM, dude,” asked one of my friends in a PlayStation party chat last night. I had no good answer.

Others online are also confused and annoyed by the ATM controls.

According to recent reports, “This right here is my biggest gripe so far. Let me type in a number, JUST LIKE PUTTING JN THE STUPID CODES,” posted one user on Reddit.

Industry observers note that “I’m amazed more people aren’t pissed at using L1/R1 to withdraw/deposit. Like, just let me type it in,” said someone else.

“1, 2, 3, 4, …10, 20, 30, … 300, 400, … 10 000, 20 000, 30 000! … Fuck… I just wanted to withdraw 500,” complained a GTA 6 player.

“Dude, I spent straight up 2 minutes trying to withdraw [exactly] $900… It was so fucking annoying,” shared a user on the GTA 6 subreddit.

“It blows my mind that something this small wasn’t implemented over the course of development. Them keeping the exact same system as Red Dead Redemption 2 is extremely flawed in a game like this; I would honestly prefer the ATM mechanics of GTA V over what we got in this game,” explained someone in a Reddit post titled: My biggest complaint with this game.

In a recent development, that last comment brings up a good point and one which makes this all the more confusing to me. In GTA V and GTA Online, there are pre-selected amounts you select, like $100 or $1,000, that make banking easier. Not perfect, but it is so much better and easier than GTA 6‘s ATM controls that I’m genuinely confused as to why Rockstar didn’t just… do that.

In a game that tries (and often succeeds) to recreate as many small details of real life in a digital world as possible, including shirts getting damp with sweat and accurate reflections, it’s strange that Rockstar invented a latest, terrible way to interact with an ATM.

The best way to play is just to experience the game, even if most of us are too internet-pilled to do that.
