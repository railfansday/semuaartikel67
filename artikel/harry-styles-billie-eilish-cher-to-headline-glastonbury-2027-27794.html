📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Harry Styles, Billie Eilish, Cher, and Sam Fender will headline the 2027 edition of Glastonbury. It marks Eilish’s second time at the top of the Pyramid Stage, while Styles will be making his debut appearance at Glastonbury. Cher, who has not toured since 2020, is performing in the traditional legends slot on Sunday night.

Industry observers note that other artists on the bill include Massive Attack, Sombr, Lily Allen, the XX, Robyn, Chvrches, Kacey Musgraves, Lola Young, and Adéla. The festival will run June 23-27, 2027 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, U.K.

“We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year and, as we head into winter after that peaceful period, we wanted to give you an early musical taster of what’s to come at Glastonbury 2027 with this spread of incredible artists. We are beyond excited about how it’s all shaping up,” festival organizer Emily Eavis wrote on Instagram. “We will bring you even more names in the newly introduced year and can’t wait to see you down here on Worthy Farm in June for some much-needed good times!” Trending Stories Watch Buffalo Springfield Come Together for the First Time in Many Years Jennifer Lopez Teases New Music on ‘Fallon’: ‘This Does Feel Like a Happy Era’ John Mulaney Enlists Stepfather-in-Law's Country Band to Play 'Kimmel' The Lost Michael Jackson Tapes.

Glastonbury 2027 comes after a customary fallow year at Worthy Farm. Next year’s festival will mark the first time that ticket prices have broken the £400 mark at £408, an increase from £378.50 in 2025. Tickets sold out instantly when they went on sale on Oct. 4.

According to recent reports, although more acts are set to be revealed, the lineups puts to bed rumors that Oasis and Madonna might headline the festival. The Queen of Pop is instead heading to Coachella in April, while Oasis have their own global tour that kicks off in May.
