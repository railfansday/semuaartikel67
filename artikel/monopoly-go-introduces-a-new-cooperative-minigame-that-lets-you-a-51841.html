Whether you like it or loathe it, there's no doubting the popularity and staying power of Monopoly Go for Scopely. Now, the hit tabletop-to-mobile adaptation is receiving an all-fresh minigame. Paths challenge you to think cooperatively, or look out for yourself, and debut today with the launch of the new Franken Paths as part of the Monster Mash season!

In a recent development, paths are a straightforward cooperative minigame where you, and up to three other tycoons, team up to build a path and move a shared vehicle along it. You'll accumulate points, and have the choice to either use it to build your assigned portion (and reap the rewards) or send it to another player, with the aim of reaching the end together and getting even greater goodies.

Industry observers note that admittedly though, I feel as if Scopely are trying a little too hard to sell the 'send or keep' message with Paths. I'm really only extrapolating whether there's any benefit to you actually keeping your points, and given the whole team is working together to reach each section, is there really any benefit to holding onto them?
