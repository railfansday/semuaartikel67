Manchester United are reportedly leaning towards allowing Lisandro Martinez to leave next summer, with the club increasingly doubtful about extending the defender’s contract beyond the current season.

Recent findings suggest that according to GIVEMESPORT journalist Ben Jacobs, Manchester United are not expected to activate the one-year extension included in Martinez’s deal. The Argentine’s contract expires next summer, although the club retain the option to keep him until 2028.

The situation is surprising given United’s ongoing problems at the back. They have struggled to find consistency in defence, with Matthijs de Ligt still working towards full match sharpness, Leny Yoro continuing his development and Harry Maguire struggling for regular opportunities. In the latest development, Ayden Heaven is also leaning towards going on a loan to secure regular minutes as he finds limited opportunities at United.

The 28-year-old arrived from Ajax in 2022 for an initial £56.7 million but has suffered two serious ACL injuries during his time at Old Trafford. His performances have also come under scrutiny this season, adding further uncertainty to his future.

Speaking on the Market Madness podcast, Jacobs suggested that Manchester United are already considering a defensive rebuild ahead of next summer.

Industry observers note that “I think the Lisandro Martinez direction of travel is a departure, and there is the option to extend by one year. But my gut says that Manchester United, even if they can’t get defenders in the winter, will add further in the summer,” Jacobs said.

Jacobs also suggested that activating Martinez’s extension would not necessarily mean the club intend to keep him.

“Even if Man United extend the contract, that might just be a protection so they can still sell him,” he added.

Although the decision is not yet final but Manchester United are leaning towards letting Martinez go. They find themselves in a difficult place as far as their defensive cover is concerned. Despite their shortage of reliable defenders, the club appears increasingly open to moving on from the Argentine.
