According to recent reports, near Protocol tumbled toward the 200 EMA a week ago, but only now are we seeing a spike in volume and a surge in investor activity. The price is gradually moving upward, but the volume is, unfortunately, descending.

In a recent development, NEAR is at the moment trading at $3.386, while the 200 EMA sits at around $3.03. The 200 EMA acted as strong support, as the price dipped to around $2.87 and came back above it swiftly.

Moving averages on the NEAR chart are diverging and might create conditions for a rapid price recovery, as this directly affects volatility.

In a recent development, the descending volume in the long term suggests declining power among bears. The descending volume on the upside movement essentially hints at likely price weakening.

In a recent development, NEAR rallied from around $1.60 in August up to $6.00 in mid-October. It tumbled rapidly after that, and the current price is more than 40% below the peak.

In a recent development, the bounce from the 200 EMA is most likely a cool-off period following the drop from the highest point of the year. The possibility of a bull run is slim. For the recovery to turn into something meaningful, we certainly need a surge in trading volume.

The closest moving average above the price is the orange one around $3.60, which NEAR broke below in the first half of November. Whether buyers can defend the 200 EMA is the main test for the asset right now.

It has been revealed that in case of a 200 EMA breakthrough, we are most likely going to see accelerating selling pressure. However, there's a good chance that it won't happen, considering the lack of volatility on the market right now.

Industry observers note that RSI touched around 32 at the bottom and has climbed to around 40 since. RSI is forming a reversal, which is unlikely to contribute to volatility or price whatsoever.

Compared to the rest of the market, Near Protocol is showing somewhat solid performance with a realistic recovery possibility.
