One of the most exciting names linked with a Merseyside move as a potential successor to Alisson is Bart Verbruggen, who has been a standout at Brighton this season.

The Dutch international has kept 12 clean sheets in 16 appearances for the Seagulls already, and is rapidly becoming one of the most sought-after 'keepers in world football.

Verbruggen is on Liverpool's shortlist, but Real Madrid look set to beat the Reds to the24-year-old's signature.

That's according to a report from Football Insider, in which former Everton chief executive Kieth Wyness explained that he believes the Dutch 'keeper's preference is to move to Madrid.

Recent findings suggest that "I've got a funny feeling that the auction for a keeper like [Verbruggen] may mean that Liverpool might not be the winners," Wyness remarked. "I think Verbruggen might be looking at a club in Spain; that's my feeling.".

The report names Real Madrid as the Spanish club that could land Verbruggen over Liverpool, but doesn't completely rule out the Dutchman making a move to Merseyside.

It is unclear how much Brighton will demand for their goalkeeper, but the battle for his signature is sure to heat up as the season comes to a close.
