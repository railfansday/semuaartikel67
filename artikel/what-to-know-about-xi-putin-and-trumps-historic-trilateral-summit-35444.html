What to know about Xi, Putin and Trump's historic trilateral summit

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (62 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, china's Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin and his US counterpart Donald Trump met for a historic summit in Shenzhen, China, on Thursday, where they agreed on a "peace declaration". The three leaders pledged to reform the UN, increase supplies of refined petroleum products and uphold the immunity enjoyed by officials in international courts.
