📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Greece concluded their excellent debut campaign in League A of the UEFA Nations League with a narrow win over Serbia on Monday night. Borussia Dortmund’s Konstantinos Karetsas played an hour for the hosts before the captain Georgios Masouras scored the only goal less than ten minutes after his introduction.

Recent findings suggest that the goal from the former Bochum player put the underdogs level on points with Germany and the Netherlands, but they're the ones to miss out in the knockout stage due to the severity of their only defeat – 7-0 against the Dutch. Ivan Jovanović's side needed Germany to score nine more goals in their five-star performance against Xavi's men.

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, Serbia followed in the footsteps of Türkiye and Czechia as they ended their campaign with zero points. Greece will face a playoff tie to defend their League A spot in March's international break.

Switzerland already secured their promotion to League A, and they beat Scotland 4-0 on Monday with a much-changed team. Murat Yakin's side only dropped two points and conceded one goal during their group stage campaign.

According to recent reports, slovenia used the opportunity to finish ahead of Scotland in Group B1 with a 3-0 victory at North Macedonia. Danijel Sturm scored a brace either side of a first senior national team goal for Holstein Kiel's David Zec. Scotland will stay in League B, while Slovenia enter playoff for a chance at a sensational League A promotion.
