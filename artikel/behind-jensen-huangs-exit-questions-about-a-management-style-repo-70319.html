Earlier this week at the DealBook conference in Newly introduced York, Elon Musk, beamed in from a trip to China with President Donald Trump, was asked about the ouster of his longtime friend, former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Musk compared it to kicking Michael Jordan off the Chicago Bulls’ roster during his historic run. Of Nvidia’s employees, he mentioned, “They love [Huang]. They love him.”.

Recent findings suggest that well, maybe not all of them. Thousands of employees have signed a letter asking for Huang’s return, but others tell The Latest York Times he ruled through praise and fear, as well as by humiliating people.

Sources indicate that according to the Times, Huang told subordinates that Nvidia’s wealth meant it wasn’t possible to motivate employees with money, so humiliation was the only lever left. He reportedly told one executive his mother should have aborted him and told another employee he would rip his arms off and beat him to death with them.

Naturally, over a 33-year career, regrettable things may have been said, but the Times found a long paper trail that suggests the incidents weren’t isolated. In 2005, for example, Michael Winston, a former HR chief, threatened to sue and called Huang’s conduct the most abusive he’d seen. Nvidia paid him more than $1 million. Huang’s spokesman calls the allegations fabrications.
