Gasperini previews Parma-Roma: “Dybala and Svilar are back.”

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roma is ready to return to action after the international break.

The Giallorossi will fly to the Tardini to face Alberto Gilardino’s Parma, who spoke on the eve of the match about his relationship with the Grugliasco-born coach. The Giallorossi coach spoke at a press conference one day before the match against Parma.

It has been revealed that “Dybala and Svilar trained well. They have fully recovered. Pellegrini, who will have a longer return, and Rensch, who has started training with good intensity, are left out.”.

Recent findings suggest that “Last year’s comeback against Parma was crucial; it opened the doors to the Champions League for us. It was a fundamental step. This fresh season is different; the objectives are emerging along the way.”.

Sources indicate that “As for the transfer market, it’s appropriate for the CEO to speak about it. If necessary, he’ll be the one to say what Roma can do on the market, which is always a complicated and difficult one.”.

According to recent reports, “Soule? Clearly, travel and time zones have an impact. We’ll try to figure it out this afternoon during the final training session, including for Balerdi and Castro. We left Soulé in extraordinary condition, but we have three vital matches. Soulé isn’t a problem; the important thing is that he’s injury-free.”.

“Dybala? I don’t know his condition. We’ll see what happens as we play. It’s true he hasn’t played for a month, but sooner or later he’ll have to play, and we’ll evaluate him during the match. He’s recovered physically, so we’ll see how he’s doing from time to time.”.

It has been revealed that “Having Soulé and Dybala at our disposal is a strength. Soulé has reached a high level of quality, he’s been called up to the Argentina squad. He’s evolving, like Mora. They’re players who are growing, the important thing is to have them. Dybala and Soulé are a great fit; coexistence is a false problem.”.

Recent findings suggest that on the strength of the squad, despite the many absences. “We’ve had strong performances from other players, which have helped us avoid feeling the impact of the absences. We have to look ahead; the break always leaves you with some doubts. You think you can restart with the certainties of Sassuolo, but that’s not the case. There have been constant breaks lately, and some players have played less. So it’s also an individual assessment, especially regarding the first matchday. We need to get back on track immediately. Tomorrow is a tough match; the standings are starting to become more clear, and points are starting to count for more.”.

According to recent reports, “I don’t think there are any definitive transfer market situations today. There are still a lot of games to go, and players who aren’t playing can play more, or vice versa. We’re in constant conversation with D’Amico, but there’s been no transfer planning other than between the two of us.”.
