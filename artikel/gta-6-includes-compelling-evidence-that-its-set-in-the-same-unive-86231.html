Grand Theft Auto 6 contains a few new interesting pieces of evidence that seem to indicate Rockstar’s popular crime-sim franchise lives in the same universe as its also-popular cowboy series, Red Dead Redemption.

Let’s start with one of the most compelling and obvious connections between GTA 6 and Red Dead: a book you can find in the fresh game that directly references events from RDR 2. As spotted and shared online by GameRoll, there’s a book players can find early on in Brian’s Marina in GTA 6 that contains many references to both Red Dead Redemption and its prequel, Red Dead Redemption 2. For example, the book mentions a gunslinger players can meet in RDR 2, as well as mentions of locations from those games such as Blackwater and New Austin.

Every so often, you might spot a car with a Upcoming Austin license plate. In the world of Red Dead, New Austin is intended to be the game’s version of Texas. A sillier connection to Red Dead can be spotted at a western-themed mini-golf course in GTA 6. If you explore that place, you’ll quickly discover a small dog with a cowboy doll attached to his back. Look closer, and you’ll be able to identify that cowboy as Arthur Morgan.
