Alaska Cuts Four Routes

🌐 Source: Airlinegeeks • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Alaska Airlines will drop four destinations from its network in Portland, Oregon.

The carrier confirmed it will end service from Portland to Baltimore, St. Louis, Newly introduced Orleans, and Jackson Hole, Wyoming.

In a recent development, three of the connections released only months ago. Portland-Jackson Hole came online in June, and service to Baltimore and St. Louis started in May.

“As we add new routes to our network, we constantly assess existing routes to ensure we’re using our aircraft in the markets our guests want most,” the carrier said in a statement provided to AirlineGeeks. “As part of that process, we’ve made the decision to discontinue service between Portland and Baltimore, Jackson Hole, St. Louis, and New Orleans. We remain committed to Portland and will continue to invest in opportunities that strengthen our network there, including adding service in markets where we see the greatest opportunities for our guests.”.

Alaska serves over 60 destinations from Portland, and recently opened a newly introduced lounge there.
