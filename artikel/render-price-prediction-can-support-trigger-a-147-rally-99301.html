RENDER is back at a critical support zone, but the bullish setup comes with a catch. One more downside sweep could come before any meaningful recovery. The latest RENDER price prediction points to a potential rally toward $3.306, provided the $1.130 invalidation level stays intact.

Sources indicate that RENDER trades around $1.338, down roughly 45% from its recent high of $2.436. The daily chart indicates a descending structure pressing against flat support near $1.23.

According to recent reports, the analysts proposed scenario expects another liquidity sweep toward $1.228 before a possible reversal. That would test whether buyers are still willing to defend the accumulation zone.

Sources indicate that but support isn’t guaranteed. A repeated test can attract buyers, or expose just how weak demand has become.
