It has been revealed that capcom has today announced that its dinosaur PvPvE shooter Exoprimal will end service on September 29, 2027, meaning it will no longer be playable after that date. The game is also set to be delisted from digital storefronts, including the Xbox Store, on March 30, 2027.

"We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027.".

Exoprimal debuted on Xbox Series X|S on July 14, 2023, and suffered a pretty pants opening with only 4,995 players logging in through Steam to play Capcom's latest team-based shooter.
