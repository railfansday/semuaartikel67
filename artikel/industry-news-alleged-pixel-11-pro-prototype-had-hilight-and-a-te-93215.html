Google might have had a chance to keep the temperature sensor around, it seems, as a supposed prototype shows off a device that offered both the last-gen temperature sensor and the new HiLight LED in one.

I would much rather have the temperature sensor over the HiLight LED. I didn't use the temp sensor much but it was handy if whenever I was sick and wanted to check my temp without jamming an under tongue temp stick or wasting money on a temporal temp device -- because my phone already had one in it!

Images posted to Telegram (via NotebookCheck) show an apparent prototype of the Pixel 11 Pro XL. The device is seen with the markings that are common to other known Pixel prototypes, with the key difference compared to the final model being the fact that the camera bar has both a temperature sensor and HiLight.

We can tell this is HiLight due to the diffused rear LED, and we know this isn’t a Pixel 10 Pro XL because the camera bar has full-width glass instead of a metal portion.

In a recent development, google pulled the plug on the temperature sensor in Pixel 11 Pro after the prior three generations offered the feature. While the sensor was never particularly beloved, Google did see backlash from the lack of functionality in HiLight at launch. Having both features may have resulted in a different sentiment around the updated hardware.
