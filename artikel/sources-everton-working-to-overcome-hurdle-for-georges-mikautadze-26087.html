Everton may lack the finances to seal a move for Villarreal striker Georges Mikautadze in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Toffees are set to prioritise a move for a new frontman once the window opens, but their ownership situation is causing problems for them.

The Friedkin Group are looking to sell the Merseyside club and are not prepared to fund an expensive spree of signings in the winter market.

Sources say that Mikautadze's £33million price tag is providing a major "hurdle" as a result, and will make it difficult for Everton to strike a deal.

David Moyes is crying out for more options to lead the line, but he and the recruitment team will have to focus on more cost-effective targets.

Mikautadze netted 15 goals for Villarreal last term, and already has five strikes to his name this time around, including two in the Champions League.

Mikautadze has been continually linked with a move to the Premier League in recent years, and Everton are the latest club to take an interest in him.

The 26-year-old's reputation has steadily grown since his prolific stint with Metz back in 2023, and he has since excelled at European-level clubs in Lyon and Villarreal.

The Georgian combines pace with quality finishing, and is proving to be a significant threat to La Liga defences once again.

However, if Everton truly want to sign him this winter, they will need to find a creative solution.

If TFG are unable to stump up the £33m for his price tag, then they may have to explore the option of a loan deal or deferred payments.

Everton are tracking a whole host of striker targets at the moment, and they may ultimately have to look elsewhere to find their man.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider on Wednesday Christian Norgaard's return to action will feel like a "blessing" for Moyes.

The Danish midfielder scored in the recent behind-closed-doors friendly against Derby County, and played significant minutes in that game.

Wyness believes that Norgaard will become a "very valuable asset" for his former club, and will add even more quality to their flourishing centre-midfield group.

Everton paid just £7m to sign veteran from Arsenal in the summer, but have been unable to see him at his best so far.
