There was a moment Ezra Frech didn’t have a leg to stand on—but he had a ballroom supporting him.

According to recent reports, after the Paralympian’s prosthetic leg came off during his freestyle dance alongside partner Daniella Karagach on the Dancing With the Stars Nov. 24 finale, he revealed what went through his head before seamlessly reattaching the limb with the help of his partner as well as pro Alan Bersten.

“The leg just wasn't on correctly, and I felt it,” he told Variety following the episode, which saw him and Karagach crowned as the season’s winners. “Sometimes it'll readjust and I'll be okay. And so, as I was running up the steps, I'm like, ‘There's a 50 percent chance my leg will resuction and I'll be okay.’ And that wasn't the case.”.

Nevertheless, he was ready to do what had to be done to finish the dance. After all, it was a moment he and Karagach had long been preparing for.

“I was just like, ‘Okay, I'm gonna do the rest of the dance on one leg. Like I'll do the entire thing on one leg,’” the 21-year-old continued. “She's drilled in me since the beginning that no matter what happens, we keep pushing through. You never give up, and you fight for it. And in this case, regardless of the circumstance, I was gonna do whatever I can to fight for it.”.

Thanks to the quick thinking of his pro, however, Frech was able to get a leg back up the competition—literally. During a brief pause in the dance, Karagach appeared to yell something to her partner as she handed him his prosthetic, allowing Frech a few beats to reattach the limb.

“I heard her say, ‘Put your leg on,’” he recalled, “and I was like, ‘Yes, okay, yeah, good idea. I'll put it on right now.’”.

While Karagach had the utmost faith in her partner’s ability to persevere, she knew  taking the time to reattach the limb was worth it.

In a recent development, “I was just like, there is no other place in the routine that he can do this right now, and he cannot do the whole routine on one leg,” she explained. “So, I was like, ‘Just put your leg on.’ And it's not like—we had one little trick in there that he did on one, like a 540. It was really impressive. It was really great. But nothing's better than him just clicking on his leg properly and continuing on with the dance. I was like, ‘This is our only shot to do that.’”.

The moment was so adrenaline inducing, however, that Frech admitted he didn’t quite remember how his prosthetic made it back onto his body.

“Did somebody give me the leg? Because I blacked out,” he asked aloud, to which Karagach confirmed she did. “You gave me the leg? Really?”.

Thankfully, those few seconds were the only thing Frech needed.

It has been revealed that “I clicked it on,” he said, “and when I put it on the second time. I was like, ‘Ooh it's on now, we're good.’”.

And good he was, with he and Karagach crowned the season 35 winners by the end of the episode—an accomplishment as good as gold for Frech.

"This is the most special moment of my life," he told the audience. "Thank you, everybody, for voting. Thank you for believing in us. Oh my God.".
