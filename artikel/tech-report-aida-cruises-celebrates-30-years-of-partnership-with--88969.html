📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Madeira Ports Authority announced that AIDA Cruises celebrated 30 years of partnership with the ports with a gathering on the AIDAprima on November 5 at the Port of Funchal, bringing together institutional partners and representatives of the cruise tourism sector.

The event, part of the cruise line’s “30 Years of Strong Partnership–Destination Dialogue: Shaping the Future Together” program, brought together the Madeira Ports Authority (APRAM, SA), Funchal City Council, AIDA’s shipping agents and shorex agents.

“AIDA Cruises is an important partner for the Port of Funchal and Madeira, with a relationship spanning three decades that has contributed to the development and consolidation of cruise tourism in the Region,” stated Paula Cabaco, chairwoman of the board of directors of the Madeira Ports Authority (APRAM, SA).

This relationship, she stated, is built on trust, cooperation and joint efforts.

It has been revealed that “For 30 years, AIDA and Madeira have shared a close and trusting partnership. We are sincerely grateful to our partners and the people of Madeira for the openness, hospitality and trust they have shown AIDA and our guests throughout these years,” stated Steffi Heinicke, senior vice president, guest experience and OBR, at AIDA Cruises. “We look forward to continuing this special partnership for many years to come.”.

According to the press release, AIDA Cruises’ ships made 40 calls to Madeira last year, representing more than 23 percent of the total number of passengers at the Port of Funchal.

APRAM added that the partnership has been developed by several entities, including Funchal City Council, and represented at the event by Vice-Mayor Carlos Rodrigues, tourism organizations, authorities and industry stakeholders.

The collaboration also has an environmental dimension, as seen with the tree-planting initiative at Funchal’s Ecological Park.
