Surprise Serie A pace-setters Lazio head to Turin on Saturday night looking to maintain their position at the top of the table when they take on Torino at the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino.

While the visitors have not lost a league match since the start of the season and are beginning to attract genuine Scudetto talk, Torino enter the weekend battling to halt a four-match winless run that has dragged them toward the lower half of the standings.

Torino’s campaign has been one of peaks and troughs under new head coach Ignazio Abate.

Recent findings suggest that after a difficult start to the season, the Granata appeared to turn a corner by recording three consecutive Serie A victories. However, that momentum has faded promptly, and they now find themselves without a win in four matches. Their latest setback came last weekend in a 1-0 defeat away to Napoli, leaving them with just one point from their last four outings.

As a result, Torino find themselves closer to the relegation zone than the European places, despite showing signs of promise earlier in the campaign.

According to recent reports, home form has become a particular concern. Torino have conceded nearly two goals per game on their own turf this season and are right now on a club-record run of 11 consecutive Serie A home matches without a clean sheet. Those defensive issues will be under intense scrutiny against the league’s most in-form side.

It has been revealed that there is, however, some hope in the recent head-to-head record. Torino defeated Lazio 2-0 in the corresponding fixture last March and could record consecutive victories over the Biancocelesti for the first time since 2013.

Torino’s attacking hopes will largely rest on Che Adams, who has scored four Serie A goals this season, alongside Giovanni Simeone, a player with an impressive record against Lazio. The Argentine has scored 10 league goals against the Roman club during his career and will be hoping to add to that tally on Saturday.

Defensively, the hosts have suffered a setback with Ardian Ismajli, who suffered an adductor injury during the defeat to Napoli and has subsequently undergone surgery.

Industry observers note that abate is also dealing with fitness concerns involving wing-backs Rafik Belghali and Ricardo Rodriguez. Belghali continues to struggle with knee inflammation, meaning Zakaria Aboukhlal, Nicolo Fortini or even Nathan Patterson could feature on the right flank.

Lazio continue to defy expectations at the summit of Serie A.

Sources indicate that since beginning Gennaro Gattuso’s reign with a shock Coppa Italia defeat to Mantova, the Biancocelesti have been outstanding. They have dropped only four points across their first 12 league matches and head into the weekend three points clear of reigning champions Inter and two ahead of city rivals Roma.

Victory in Turin would give Lazio 11 wins from their opening 13 matches, matching a pace not seen since Napoli’s title-winning 2022-23 campaign.

Their most recent success came against Lecce, where Tijjani Noslin scored twice before Davide Frattesi added a memorable chipped finish to complete the victory. Those performances have helped fuel growing belief among supporters that Lazio could challenge for a first Scudetto in 27 years.

Gattuso will be keen to keep those ambitions grounded, however, knowing that a difficult trip to Turin awaits before next month’s potentially decisive Derby della Capitale against Roma.

With Lazio no longer involved in other competitions, Gattuso has generally played a settled side, although he is right now without two important defensive players.

Both Nuno Tavares and Josip Sutalo remain sidelined with flexor injuries. There is better news regarding captain Mattia Zaccagni, who has resumed full training and could return to the matchday squad after missing recent action.

Davide Frattesi enters the fixture as Lazio’s leading Serie A scorer with five goals, while Tijjani Noslin has been one of the division’s form players, recording at least one goal involvement in each of his last four appearances.

Sources indicate that this fixture pits two sides moving in opposite directions. Lazio have developed into one of the most consistent teams in Italy under Gattuso, while Torino have struggled for results in recent weeks and continue to battle defensive issues, particularly at home.

It has been revealed that although Torino can take confidence from their victory in the reverse fixture last season, current form strongly favours the visitors. With Lazio conceding fewer than one goal per game on average and boasting several players in excellent attacking form, they appear well equipped to continue their impressive unbeaten run.
