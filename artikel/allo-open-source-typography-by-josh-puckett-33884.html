Josh Puckett built Allo, a free open source typography family with three variable axes, true obliques, and a playful interactive specimen site.

You can see that personality right away in the colorful sticker pile and the multi-language greeting wall on the homepage. It feels approachable without turning cartoonish.

Under the hood, Allo runs on three variable axes: Weight from 100 to 900, Roundness from 0 to 100, and an Optical Size axis from 14 to 32 that adjusts automatically between body text and display headlines. The family packs 606 glyphs, support for over 50 languages, and true obliques drawn from scratch rather than slanted mechanically. Practical touches show up across the specimen cards, including tabular figures, fractions, lifted punctuation beside capitals, and arrows that form as you type.

In a recent development, stylistic sets make the system even more flexible for interface work.
