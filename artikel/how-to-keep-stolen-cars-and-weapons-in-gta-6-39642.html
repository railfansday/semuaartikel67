How to keep stolen cars and weapons in GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

To keep stolen cars and weapons in Grand Theft Auto 6, you'll have to go through some trouble. Stolen cars will disappear if you don't park them safely or register them in your name, while looted weapons will disappear unless you store them. Be careful not to lose your favorite upcoming guns and vehicles!

Here's how to claim ownership of found, looted, or stolen cars and weapons in GTA 6.

Found a fancy ride and want to keep it? In GTA 6, stolen vehicles are lost if you leave them behind, destroy them, or exit the game before claiming ownership. The only way to claim ownership of a stolen vehicle is to get it registered in your name at a Pay 'N' Spray shop or vehicle drop-off location (like Moses' or Randy's store).

Recent findings suggest that the registration fee varies between a few thousand and tens of thousands of dollars, depending on the car's value. Note that it takes a while to unlock the vehicle registration feature:.

Sources indicate that if you want to register a vehicle but haven't got the funds yet, you can park the vehicle in your garage for the time being. You can safely go on missions and exit the game; the vehicle will still be there when you come back. However, you shouldn't take it out until you have enough money to register the vehicle in your name, as the vehicle will be lost if you abandon it outside the garage. Garage parking is for safekeeping only – it does not give you ownership of a car!

It has been revealed that unfortunately, you can't claim ownership of regular stolen or looted weapons in GTA 6, but you can keep them in your trunk. So, if you find a weapon you like, store it in any of your vehicles, and you can take it out later (it doesn't have to be the same vehicle), even after your character dies or you exit the game. However, if you take such a weapon from your trunk and drop it or get wasted, you'll lose it permanently.

Recent findings suggest that to own a regular weapon permanently, you must buy it at an Ammu-Nation store. Owned weapons can be modded and are never lost (they'll reappear in the trunk). To tell the difference between owned and unowned weapons, look at the color of the name or weapon icon; blue weapons are owned, and white weapons are unowned.

According to recent reports, the picture below shows an unowned longarm on the left, and an owned longarm on the right. The sidearms in both pictures are unique.

If you find or steal a unique weapon (gold icon), however, it's automatically added to your arsenal. If you don't see it in your trunk, you can find it in a weapon locker at any Ammu-Nation or garage. Unique weapons are usually obtained from missions and cannot be modified.
