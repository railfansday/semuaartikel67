By RJ Fulton – Nov 19, 2026 at 5:45PM EST.

Sources indicate that wall Street's early rally unraveled on Thursday, November 19, as even the largest initial public offering in history proved insufficient to restore investor confidence. Despite Anthropic's (ANT +17.07%) successful stock market debut, selling pressure intensified throughout the session as concerns surrounding elevated valuations and the prospect of further interest rate hikes continued to weigh on equities.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.02%) fell 2.00% to 7,177, marking its sixth consecutive session of losses and its longest losing streak since April 2024. The Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.25%) reversed its early gains, closing 2.23% lower at 23,886, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -2.19%) plunged more than 1,100 points, falling 2.19% to 49,097.

Industry observers note that selling pressure extended beyond equities, with December gold futures settling 1.50% lower at $3,685.50, while the 10-year Treasury yield eased to 5.05%. All 11 S&P 500 sectors finished in negative territory, with consumer discretionary leading losses at 2.79%.

Industry observers note that despite the broader market sell-off, Insmed (INSM +20.58%) surged 20.58% following reports that French pharmaceutical giant Sanofi (SNY -2.25%) is considering acquiring the biotechnology organization. Palo Alto Networks (PANW +6.42%) was the S&P 500's best-performing stock, gaining 6.42% after reporting strong quarterly results and raising its fiscal 2027 outlook.

According to recent reports, conversely, Williams-Sonoma (WSM -15.16%) was the S&P 500's worst-performing stock, plunging 15.16% following its quarterly earnings report. Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -7.72%) fell 7.72% following a report from investigative outlet Hunterbrook alleging ties to a blacklisted Chinese server manufacturer.

Recent findings suggest that anthropic's record-breaking IPO demonstrates that investor appetite for artificial intelligence (AI) remains substantial, even as concerns surrounding technology valuations intensify. However, the sheer size of Anthropic's offering also raises questions about how much capital remains available to support other AI investments.

With technology companies competing for financing to fund increasingly expensive infrastructure projects, elevated interest rates could force investors to become more selective about which businesses warrant premium valuations.

It has been revealed that perhaps more concerning is the deterioration in market breadth over the past week. Selling pressure is no longer confined to high-growth technology stocks, suggesting that investor concerns are shifting toward the broader economic outlook. With the Federal Reserve signaling that additional interest rate hikes may be necessary, upcoming inflation and employment data could prove particularly influential in determining the market's direction.
