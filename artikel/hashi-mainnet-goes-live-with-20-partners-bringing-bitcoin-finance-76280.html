Hashi, Sui’s native Bitcoin finance infrastructure, is now live on mainnet — with more than 20 custody, retail, and DeFi participants on board, forming one of the broadest coalitions in Bitcoin finance to date.

Sources indicate that bitcoin has spent years becoming one of the world’s largest pools of capital. Now the infrastructure to put it to work has arrived. Supporting institutional and retail access are names including Anchorage Digital, Fluid, HoneyB, and Upshift — with BitGo, Inc. expected to follow in 2027.

According to recent reports, the ecosystem continues to expand. Among the latest to join Hashi are Merkl, an onchain incentive platform, and Current, a lending protocol — additions that broaden the range of DeFi products and services being built around Bitcoin on Sui.

Hashi keeps Bitcoin on the Bitcoin network and mints hBTC — a 1:1 Bitcoin-backed asset — on Sui. That hBTC can then serve as collateral for lending, borrowing, and other onchain financial products.

According to recent reports, crucially, Hashi is decentralized by design. It’s secured by validators rather than any single entity, using industry-leading cryptography. On that foundation, it connects leading providers across custody, trading, and decentralized finance — giving Bitcoin holders a wide range of ways to put their assets to work.

With hBTC now live, Bitcoin holders have a new set of opportunities within reach — starting with borrowing against BTC and tapping new financial strategies through lending markets and vaults.

It has been revealed that to meet the specific needs of institutions, several latest providers are rolling out dedicated lending and vault facilities. Fluid’s institutional-grade lending protocol is now live on Sui, beginning with Bitcoin-backed stablecoin loans. Holders can use Hashi to bring BTC onto Sui as hBTC, then post it as collateral on Fluid to borrow stablecoins — starting with USDC, with more flows to come.

According to recent reports, “Bitcoin is the largest pool of idle capital in crypto,” said Samyak Jain, founder and CEO at Fluid. “With Hashi, it can now power deep, capital-efficient credit markets on Fluid.”.

It has been revealed that for institutions, Hashi solves a key piece of the puzzle: custody. Institutions will be able to access Hashi through two leading crypto custodians — Anchorage Digital and BitGo, Inc. (“BitGo”) — giving them a clear path to hold or deploy BTC into Hashi.

Previously announced institutional partners, including Cumberland and Wave Digital Assets, will play roles as early participants — deploying BTC and stablecoins on Hashi and providing liquidity across a range of strategies on Sui.

On the DeFi side, Sui protocols including Blueprint Finance (Concrete), Bluefin, Cetus, Current, and Suilend will unlock newly introduced lending opportunities, stablecoin credit, and yield strategies — all backed by Bitcoin.

“Some of the world’s largest Bitcoin holders are sitting on enormous amounts of untapped financial potential,” noted Nathan McCauley, CEO and Co-Founder at Anchorage Digital. “Hashi gives our institutional clients a newly introduced way to access Bitcoin finance on Sui, with their BTC held in custody at Anchorage Digital Bank.”.

Sources indicate that if you’re an institution interested in deploying BTC or stablecoins on Hashi, please reach out to your custodian or hashi@sui.io. Users can explore more at sui.io/hashi.
