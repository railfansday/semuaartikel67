It has been revealed that edconic is a leading player in the alternative, experiential education space. We partner with the world's largest and most well-known brands to create and run schools that capture the legacy and value of each brand. Our students get truly unique, once-in-a-lifetime experiences that help shape their future and give them the skills they need to reach their goals.

Edconic exists to help learners stand out, move forward, and build meaningful futures in the fields that inspire them. We bring together education and industry, partnering with iconic global organizations to create transformative learning experiences. Our programs are created and delivered by educators and industry practitioners, giving learners access to professional experts, real world challenges, and practical skills. As early career opportunities shrink, we offer credibility, connections, and hands on experience that internships once provided. We prepare learners not simply for their first job but for a lifetime of growth.

• Transformative education: relationship-rich, experiential learning that draws out each learner’s potential.

• Belonging: a community where learners, educators, experts, and staff can bring their authentic selves and support one another.

• Stewardship: as a family-owned organization, we think in generations, developing talent, caring for our partnerships, and building an impact that lasts.

We're looking for an experienced Front-End Developer to join our web team, which owns every Edconic digital property – from our school websites to landing pages and email templates. You'll work alongside designers, IT, and MarTech to build solutions that serve real business needs while protecting each brand's unique identity.

You'll be one of two developers on the team, working directly with our lead developer. That means real ownership of your work, a genuine voice in how we build, very little process overhead, and a codebase that is actively moving toward a modern, standardized WordPress stack. We value craft: fast pages and code that the next developer can read.

Recent findings suggest that AI-assisted development is a new and evolving part of our workflow. We use AI coding tools to speed up research, scaffolding, review, and routine work, and you're welcome to do the same.

Industry observers note that AI is a tool here, not a substitute for expertise. You are accountable for every line of code that you ship, which means you must be able to write, review, debug, and explain production code entirely on your own. Expect to demonstrate those skills without AI assistance during the interview process.
