Where To Easily Find Planes And Helicopters In GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6's open world of Leonida is easily one of the biggest and most detailed maps in all of gaming history, and it can be pretty tough to get around and see it all.

It has been revealed that like previous games in the series, one of the best ways to travel is through the air via airplanes and helicopters. While there are some dangerous routes, you're best off finding some reliable locations.

Recent findings suggest that let's start off with the slightly more challenging planes, which are scattered around Leonida. Unlike previous GTA games, you can travel anywhere on the map without difficulty from the start of GTA 6, so we're not stuck in the Keys looking for a rogue airport. A great starting location is the area shown on the map above near Port Gellhorn, which has a small runway that you can easily get a plane from. You can also easily get a plane from the entrance to Southwind, near the starting Keys area.

Sources indicate that for a more surprising choice, head over to the housing area just above Bellville. This might seem like a weird place to find some planes, but many of the wealthy owners have easily stealable planes waiting in their driveway. Coming here at night is a reliable way of getting a plane without going through too much trouble.

If you're feeling brave, Vice City International Airport is a good choice for getting both massive and small planes, although you'll need to be very sneaky to avoid all of the police presence and security. It's not an instant five stars anymore, though, so it's less of a gamble than it used to be if you're playing smartly.

Industry observers note that on that note, avoid Mile Zero Air Station to the West of the Keys, as it's a military base with some really powerful enemies. Even in the endgame, this is a massive risk that's not really worth it.

It has been revealed that while planes are a little easier to take on long-haul flights around Leonida, they can also be a pain to get in the air. That's not the case for helicopters, and they're arguably a lot easier to find once you know what to look for. A great starting location is Southwind in the Southwest part of the map, close to the Keys where you start the game.

It has been revealed that right on the docks of Southwind is a helicopter pad that can have a wirlybird spawned for you. Considering how close it is to where Jason starts the game, it's a great choice with low risk, despite it being right next to the Mile Zero Air Station. It's not a guarantee of a helicopter, but it's a good bet.

Industry observers note that another great location is the Southside medical centre, as shown on the map above. This hospital has a helipad on its roof, and it can easily be reached by going to the left of the building and taking the stairs up. The helicopter that spawns here is usually unlocked and has no tracker, so it's a very safe bet for getting up in the skies early on in your playthrough.

Recent findings suggest that there are also helipads on many of Vice City's tallest buildings, but getting up there in the first place might require an aircraft. These also tend to come with advanced trackers, so it's a bit more of a risk.
