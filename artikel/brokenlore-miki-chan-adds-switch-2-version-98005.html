First-person psychological horror game BrokenLore: MIKI-CHAN will launch for Switch 2 alongside its previously announced PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store) versions in 2027, Serafini Productions announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Serafini Productions:.

BrokenLore: MIKI-CHAN is a first-person psychological horror game that follows Kazuaki, a Japanese student determined to pass the entrance exam for Teidai and win over the girl he loves.

When he finds a mysterious USB stick containing an innovative study support program, he decides to try it, convinced that it can help him.

In a recent development, waiting for him is Miki-chan, a sweet and reassuring virtual assistant. But something soon changes. Jealousy and possessiveness take over, dragging Kazuaki into a nightmare.

According to recent reports, kazuaki will face psychological challenges that push him to his limits. But he has the Rewriter at his disposal, a tool capable of altering reality.
