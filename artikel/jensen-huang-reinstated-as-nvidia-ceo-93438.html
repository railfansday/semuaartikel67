Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (79 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, in a stunning U-turn, Nvidia's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as the company's President and CEO. The announcement came just days after the board rejected a petition calling for the tech titan's return. US President Donald Trump said had personally got in touch with the board to advocate for his return. Nvidia's stock surged 32%, adding nearly $1 trillion to its market cap while the rest of Wall Street also rallied.
