AC Milan recorded their second Europa League win of the campaign on Thursday evening, beating Olympiacos 4-2.

Milan had lost three of their opening four games in Europe which had them languishing in the bottom 12 positions, at risk of direct elimination. They headed to Greece to face a side that had 10 points on the board already, so a tough test awaited.

It has been revealed that while the six goals tells a bit of a story, the scoreline does not reveal the full tale of what was a dramatic night in Piraeus on the outskirts of Athens. It started badly for Milan as they fell two goals behind inside 50 minutes, with Mike Maignan’s own-goal and Jota Silva’s strike putting the hosts in command.

In a recent development, just as it looked like being another night of misery on the continent for the Rossoneri, the Omari Hutchinson show began. He scored a wonderful goal after an excellent solo run to get Milan back into it, before setting up Alphadjo Cisse’s equaliser and Christian Pulisic’s go-ahead goal.

Olympiacos pushed for a late equaliser, but their hopes were crushed in the dying seconds when Diego Moreira scored to finish off a counter Hutchinson also had a major role in. The result moves the Diavolo back into the play-off places, with nine points up for grabs.
