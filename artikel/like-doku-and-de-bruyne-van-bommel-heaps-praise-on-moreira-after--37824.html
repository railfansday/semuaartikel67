Last night, Diego Moreira scored one of the most vital goals of his career – his first for Belgium – and AC Milan are left smiling.

Moreira left for international duty in fine form; he’s seemingly discovered himself again at San Siro and is clearly enjoying his football. It’s understandable why, too. In seven games, he’s got seven contributions in Serie A and he’s firmly on course for his best ever season.

Last season is his current best, with 11 contributions in 27 games, and he’s surely on course to beat that at this rate. It’s a seismic shift in his fortunes too. After that game in Turin, he was labelled a flop, but he’s flying now.

Speaking to Sky after his team’s 4-1 win against Italy, the former Rossonero Van Bommel was full of praise for the young winger, especially with him grabbing a goal in the evening’s proceedings, as MilanNews relays.

“Diego is a good guy; he’s a bit crazy, but he’s really important to the team. If you defend and attack well as a team, the individual player can express himself better on an individual level. Diego, like Doku and De Bruyne, can play at a very high level.”.
