Recent findings suggest that fresh news! This month, IJ client Libby Souder asked the South Carolina Supreme Court to let her resume teaching private swim lessons in her backyard pool, which she'd done for years with city approval and without incident. But after a nosy neighbor (whose life is in no way affected by the lessons) complained, officials reinterpreted an ambiguous provision of the zoning code to shut down Libby's business. Recent findings suggest that phooey! Over four years after Meridian officials evicted IJ client Chasidy Decker from her home, the Idaho Supreme Court ruled this week that her challenge to the city's irrational ban on tiny mobile homes is not ripe. That's because, the court remarked, she and her landlord failed to apply for a permit that officials repeatedly testified they would not and could not grant. The silver lining?
