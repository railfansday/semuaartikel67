A special-edition RingConn Gen 3 is The One Ring to rule them all, and you can preorder it now.

In a recent development, lots of people will be picking up smart rings this year as many models go on sale for Black Friday, but one special RingConn smart ring might make you forget about all that. RingConn’s Gen 3 ring has been transformed into The One Ring, which is up for preorder right now for $399 and ships starting December 8.

It has been revealed that the limited-edition gold ring features the legendary Black Speech inscription along the outside, which, thankfully, you won’t need to run through a fire to see. If you preorder now, you’ll receive a sizing kit in the first week of December so you can ensure The One Ring fits properly on your finger. Unfortunately, this one doesn’t auto-size to your hand the way it does in the lore.

RingConn highlights that none of The One Ring’s features require a subscription, which is the case with any of the rings in the organization’s lineup. The One Ring, like RingConn Gen 3, features a haptic vibration motor inside to alert you of notifications on your phone, something most smart rings don’t even try to do.

It’s also got all the great vascular health, sleep health, women’s health, heart health, and stress & recovery features of RingConn Gen 3, too. You’ll also only need to think about charging around 2 times per month thanks to the impressive 14-day battery life. The special edition box includes The One Ring, charging case, USB-C charging cable, quick start guide, and special edition cards.
