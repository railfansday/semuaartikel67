Sources indicate that in most Grand Theft Auto games, and most open-world games for that matter, the world is left open for you to explore once the credits roll. It could be Los Santos or a twisted take on Manhattan, but the idea is that the main story is just one part of the experience. After that’s done, there are usually still places you haven’t explored or side missions you left lingering. But Grand Theft Auto VI doesn’t work quite the same way. Because of its storyline, which involves a couple trying to get out of the criminal life for good, that kind of ending didn’t really fit.

“In this one it didn’t make sense,” Rob Nelson, producer on GTA VI and co-studio head at developer Rockstar North, told me ahead of the game’s launch. “Their story needed to end and have some kind of conclusion.”.

To be clear, you can still play GTA VI after the main storyline wraps up. But in order for that to happen, you have to zip back in time to a moment before a pivotal decision — I won’t spoil it for you — that alters the lives of main characters Jason and Lucia, along with their young son Tomás, who they’re attempting to get custody of. Nelson claims the team tried “all sorts of things” but soon realized that “in no way did it make sense” to have the game end like a traditional GTA experience.

“Their story needed to end and have some kind of conclusion.”.

Sources indicate that “You go back and have this open time again where you have the best single-player safehouse you’ve ever had in a GTA, with a garage full of supercars, a helipad, a gym, and all of the stuff you could ever want,” he indicates of the solution the studio landed on. “And you can spend as long as you want there. But you don’t have Tommy. So the characters don’t have the one thing that they wanted at the start. There’s the tension where you have to spend some extra time grinding to make some more money without knowing what it’s going to get you, and it plays out differently if you get that money.”.

One advantage of this structure is that it lets you try out different endings. GTA VI’s story can wrap up in multiple ways based on a variety of factors: how much money you have saved up, what kind of criminal you were throughout the game, and some key decisions you’re forced to make in the final moments. The new postgame structure creates an opportunity to see some of the alternate ways the story can go. “There’s a large number of potential outcomes based on how you played it,” Nelson indicates.
