West Ham United have reportedly joined the race for Stevenage defender Jack Taylor, with the 21-year-old at the moment impressing on loan at Exeter City. However, whilst the Hammers clearly need another centre-back, I’m struggling to understand why we’d be looking at another developing youngster.

Sources indicate that according to Graeme Bailey at TEAMtalk, West Ham are among eight clubs monitoring the 6ft 4in defender, alongside Everton, Brighton, Brentford, Crystal Palace, Fulham, Millwall and Norwich. Taylor recently scored in Exeter’s FA Cup victory over Huddersfield, although there is no confirmation that West Ham had scouts at the match.

It’s clear as day that West Ham need a central defender amongst other positions, but quite why we’d be looking at a 21-year-old from Stevenage is anyone’s guess.

Airidas Golambeckis has represented England at youth level and looks to have incredible potential.

Indeed, his recent performances for England’s Under-20s have strengthened his case for a first-team opportunity, particularly with Max Kilman suspended against Preston.

I’ve been impressed by Golambeckis whenever I’ve seen him, and his performance against Stevenage earlier this year was one of the first occasions when his potential really caught my attention.

Signing Taylor would appear to go against Nils Koppen’s stated recruitment principle of not blocking the pathway of youngsters coming through the academy.

And I think this would definitely be one of those examples.

Airidas Golambeckis has earned the right to play for West Ham.

Don’t get me wrong, I’m not averse to signing an experienced centre-back to see us over the line, in the same way that Axel Disasi came in during January last season.

West Ham’s lack of defensive depth is obvious, and we’ve previously highlighted the need for additional cover during a demanding Championship campaign.

But another young central defender to block the pathway of an exceptional talent at our own club?

And with Claret & Hugh having received confirmation from a club source that Konstantinos Mavropanos is not for sale in January, this shouldn’t be about finding his replacement.

If West Ham are going to strengthen at centre-back, I’d rather they signed somebody experienced enough to make an immediate difference to our promotion prospects.

Taylor might become an excellent defender, but we’ve already got a promising youngster who deserves his chance. Personally, I wouldn’t give this transfer rumour too much attention.
