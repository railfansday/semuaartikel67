📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Dell reported third-quarter earnings on Tuesday that topped Wall Street expectations for earnings and sales, and provided a strong guide for the fourth quarter.

Dell shares rose in extended trading.

Here's how the chipmaker did versus LSEG consensus estimates:.

Dell noted that it expected earnings of $8.40 per share on revenue of about $56.3 billion in the current quarter. Analysts polled by LSEG were looking for $7.50 per share on $53.61 billion in revenue.

The organization's overall sales more than doubled on an annual basis during the third quarter, driven by AI server demand.

The firm's largest line is its infrastructure solutions group, or ISG, which makes AI servers and systems, often based around AI chips from companies like Nvidia and AMD.

Sources indicate that ISG sales during the quarter were $41.97 billion, topping an LSEG estimate of $35.27 billion. Of that, $22 billion was for AI-optimized servers, up 291% on an annual basis. ISG now accounts for 85% of Dell's total revenue.

Sources indicate that "We are now a different firm, with Infrastructure Solutions Group driving the vast majority of our profit and growth," said Jeff Clarke, Dell's vice chairman and COO, in a statement. He said that Dell has 7,500 customers buying AI servers.

In prepared remarks, Clarke said that Dell has a competitive advantage over other computer equipment makers because it allows customers to get their computers working within hours of delivery.

Recent findings suggest that the organization also raised its forecast for AI-servers in the coming fiscal year to $78 billion, versus $74 billion expected. The company states it has a backlog of $132.3 billion in AI server orders it is working through.

"Every AI order is vetted for access to capital, end customer quality, trade and export compliance and access to land and power," Clarke said on an earnings call with analysts.

The manufacturer also sells laptops and PCs, in a group it calls CSG, or client services group. It reported $12.89 billion in sales, which was below LSEG expectations of $14.33 billion.

The overall PC market has been getting whacked of late, with some estimates calling the lull the worst ever, as rising memory prices have led to higher device prices and consumers are choosing to hang on to their old laptops longer.

Dell remarked that it had authorized $25 billion in share repurchases, and that it had spent $4.9 billion in the third quarter on share repurchases and dividends.

Dell shares are up over 135% so far in 2026.
