Switch to a card that pays more in one category only when a large share of your spending goes there. Either way, pay in full, since balances charged interest average over 22% a year.

According to recent reports, the average rate on bank credit card accounts that were charged interest was 22.36% in the third quarter of 2026, by the Federal Reserve’s preliminary figures. A dollar left unpaid for a year at that rate costs more than 22 cents in interest, several times what any of these cards pays back on it. So every pick assumes you pay the full balance each month.
