Earlier today, Geoff Keighley announced all of the nominees for The Game Awards 2026, the annual celebration of gaming held in Los Angeles. Among expected nominees Grand Theft Auto VI, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and Control Resonant was one surprising name: 2XKO has been nominated for Best Fighting Game, despite Riot Games announcing the end of the game's lifecycle earlier this year. Riot announced that "active development" for the fighting game, featuring characters from League of Legends, would end in December 2026. "Over the last few months, we’ve been monitoring 2XKO’s trajectory closely, and what we’ve seen is painting a clear, consistent picture," Riot noted at the time. "While a lot of you jumped in to try the game and there’s an incredible community that plays every day, we haven’t seen enough players stick with the game to get to a path toward sustainability,” https://twitter.com/Play2XKO/status/2090514358560579947 Riot's fighter is joined in the Best Fighting Game category by Invincible Vs., Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, Avatar Legends, and My Hero Academia: All's Justice. 2XKO is also not the only game on this list whose future is in jeopardy; soon after it introduced in April, Invincible Vs. developer Quarter up laid off reportedly 75% of its staff, while saying that future updates were dependent on the game's overall sales. Grand Theft Auto VI received the most nominations for a single game at this year's ceremony with eight, including Game of the Year. Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem tied for the second-most nominations with six each, while Phantom Blade Zero rounded out the top three with five nominations. Nintendo earned the most nominations for a single publisher, meanwhile, with 12 across the event's 30 awards. The Game Awards is set to take place December 10 live from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, California.
