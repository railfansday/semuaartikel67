Goo Goo Dolls fans are outraged after NBC cut short the band’s Thanksgiving Day halftime performance.

It has been revealed that the network is facing backlash after airing just 90 seconds of the John Rzeznik-fronted group’s nine-minute set during Thursday’s (Nov. 26) Buffalo Bills-Kansas City Chiefs game.

Sources indicate that the Goo Goo Dolls performed “Iris,” “Slide” and “Broadway” in the pouring rain at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, Upcoming York, just outside the group’s hometown of Buffalo. NBC aired only a portion of “Iris” before cutting away to an interview with NBC commentator Mike Tirico and former Pittsburgh Steelers coach Mike Tomlin about quarterback Aaron Rodgers, who had recently been released by the team.

“They are a great band. That was badly handled and disrespectful,” musician and actor Stevie Van Zandt wrote on X.

NBC Sports PR promoted the Tomlin interview on social media about an hour before kickoff, but many viewers weren’t happy with the network’s decision.

According to recent reports, “The fact that NBC won’t show Goo Goo Dolls in the rain in Buffalo is despicable,” one person wrote on X. Another added, “NBC only aired the last 30 seconds. Wasting time on useless clown Rodgers.”.

Industry observers note that A third wrote, “No one give a f#@k about a washed up Rodgers amd the dumpster fire he has left in Pittsburgh outside of Pittsburgh. @NBCsports should be embarrassed! Every other game on the other networks demonstrated the entirety of the halftime reveals but @NBC was amateurish by showing nonsense.”.

Unlike the Goo Goo Dolls, Thanksgiving’s other halftime acts — Wu-Tang Clan on CBS and Miranda Lambert on Fox — had their performances aired in full during their respective game broadcasts.

In a recent development, the Goo Goo Dolls hadn’t publicly commented on the situation at press time. Billboard has reached out to the band’s representatives for comment.
