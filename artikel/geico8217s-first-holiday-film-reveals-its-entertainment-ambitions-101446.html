This holiday season, the Geico Gecko is doing more than just helping people save 15% on insurance. He’s saving Christmas.

The insurance company has created its first holiday film, “Holiday Savings Special,” in partnership with The Martin Agency, Passion Pictures Animation, and Screen Novelties.

Sources indicate that in the spot, Gecko notices that holiday cheer around town is fading as residents ignore and bicker with each other. Other classic Geico characters, like Caleb the “Hump Day” camel, Maxwell the Pig, and Caveman, are also feeling down.

“Oh dear,” Gecko states, unsure what to do.

Then Maxwell the Pig gets a cryptic message from Santa over his ham radio.
