Sean Dyche enjoyed a fine playing career as a footballer, spending time with Nottingham Forest, Watford, Millwall, and Chesterfield, but he is best known for his time as a manager at the top level.

The Englishman has taken charge of 521 games during his managerial career, with spells at Watford, Burnley, Everton, and Nottingham Forest across the Championship and Premier League.

350 of those games have come in the top flight, which has seen the 55-year-old be able to manage some top players, who have represented their countries and gone on to play at the very highest level.

Playing at the top level, Dyche has coached some top stars such as Kieran Trippier, Morgan Gibbs-White, Ashley Young, Seamus Coleman, and Elliot Anderson.

But while speaking exclusively to GIVEMESPORT, via BetSelect.co.uk, the coach known for his strong defensive style of football was asked who the most underrated player of his coaching career was up to this point, and his response was surprising.

“You know what, the thing is, when I was manager at Burnley in particular, there’s so many of them," he mentioned.

According to recent reports, "I mean, the first three players I signed at Burnley – I always reference them because we signed them on free transfers. We didn’t have any money, or no real money.

It has been revealed that "I brought in Scott Arfield, Tom Heaton, and Dave Jones, all on free transfers. There’s three straight off the bat. But there’ve been so many who, not necessarily underrated, but like unsung heroes. So I wouldn’t say underrated. There’s not many I’ve worked with who I think were disrespected by being underrated. I think it was more, they’re a bit more under the radar: good club players, but top pros.

"So there are three that jump off the page, mainly because those three made such a difference to the side, and yet they were three free transfers, and we got promoted that season to the Premier League.

"So I look back at that and think, well, there’s certainly three players straight away – but like I say, I must reiterate it, not necessarily underrated in a disrespectful way, more that you didn’t realise how good they were until you worked with them.”.

He came through the academy at Manchester United and spent a number of years out on loan, before a spell with Cardiff and Bristol City prior to joining Burnley.

Sources indicate that he then went on to play for Aston Villa, and has since returned to Man Utd in the latter years of his career, working as the third-choice shot-stopper. He also earned three caps for England.

Arfield, meanwhile, spent five years at Turf Moor working with Dyche before joining Rangers, and becoming part of the squad that won the Scottish Premiership, Scottish Cup, and reached the Europa League final.

Dave Jones joined the club at 29 and was closer to the end of his career, and spent three years with the club before moving on to Sheffield Wednesday and ending his career at Wrexham.

None of the three players were ever really considered to be top-level stars, and it makes sense that Dyche names them as being underrated. But they were all hugely crucial to his time at Burnley, in particular, and it's clear that they made a big impression on him.
