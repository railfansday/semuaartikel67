Ann Michael Maye has given the Christmas tree she shares with her husband, New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye, a team dress code this year—and even the presents are in red and blue.

Ahead of Thanksgiving, Ann Michael teamed up with Minted to reveal a holiday display that draws as much from her kitchen and her mahjong nights with fellow Patriots wives and girlfriends as it does from the football field.

Photos from the collaboration offer a peek at the finished tree, the couple’s Christmas card, and a fireside game setup with a seat saved for the hostess.

According to recent reports, the tree occupies a sunny corner framed on two sides by tall, white-trimmed windows. It rises nearly to the crown molding, where a scarlet bow sends two wide ribbon tails cascading over the upper branches.

Sources indicate that A blue-and-white patterned armchair sits off to one side. On the other, the gifts have claimed the floor all the way to the window.

In a recent development, in one photo, Ann Michael reaches up to hang a clear ornament bearing a white No. 10, the same number Drake wears on the field. She’s dressed for the occasion in a striped sweater that picks up the reds and blues of the tree, a long brown skirt, and pointed black heels.

A closer view reveals at least two more numbered ornaments, including a 14 and a 74.

Industry observers note that some of the most personal decorations are easy to miss in a wide shot. Small rectangular frames hang vertically from pale ribbons, their blue-and-white borders standing out among the round ornaments.

Sources indicate that each holds a photograph from the past year. In one, two women pose with their arms around each other. Farther up the tree, another group photo is nestled just beneath a gold ornament.

It has been revealed that ann Michael also found room for a miniature version of her “Bakemas” kitchen. Glass cream puffs with ridges of golden pastry and white filling hang alongside embroidered gingerbread figures and a felt stand mixer.

In one close-up, a tiny cupcake with colorful sprinkles peeks out from a branch just below one of the photo frames.

According to recent reports, even the wrapping rewards a closer look. The boxes beneath the tree are covered in deep blue Minted paper with a small repeating pattern.

Some are tied with wide red bows that echo the tree topper, while others have navy or silvery blue ribbons. Personalized tags hang from the packages, several of which are stacked two high beneath the lowest branches.

The couple saved a different color combination for their Christmas card. In another photo, Ann Michael holds up a red-and-green plaid design featuring an outdoor portrait of her and Drake.

A white border leaves just enough space beneath the picture for a simple “Merry Christmas.”.

One ornament choice hints at what Ann Michael has planned beyond decorating.

According to Minted, she plays mahjong with other Patriots players’ partners and included personalized mahjong ornaments on the tree.

By the staircase, a sign gives the game its own holiday billing, welcoming guests to “Merry Mahjong,” hosted by Ann Michael Maye.

For that occasion, Ann Michael traded her striped sweater for a sleeveless red dress with a floral scarf tied at the waist.

Industry observers note that she poses beside a dark wood banister draped with evergreen garland. A long spray of greenery falls nearly to the floor at the foot of the stairs, secured with burgundy ribbon that picks up the color of her dress.

The game tables are set beside a lit brick fireplace, where patterned stockings hang from a white mantel topped with greenery and tall red candles.

At the nearest table, wooden tile racks frame a blue-and-white playing mat with a large flower at its center.

A second game is ready to go by the window, and a red drink garnished with a sprig of greenery sits within reach of the first.

Ann Michael carried the personalization right down to the seating. Ribbons fasten little name tags to the backs of the bamboo-style chairs, each fitted with a blue-and-white striped cushion.

In the final fireside portrait, she takes her place at the tiles. The tag tied behind her reads “Ann Michael.”.

In a recent development, drake and Ann Michael, both 23, tied the knot in June 2025, having met in middle school in North Carolina. The couple purchased their first home together in Massachusetts in April—and moved in together in July, after Ann Michael graduated from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where they both attended college.

Since graduating, Ann Michael has been by her husband's side for almost all of his games, traveling across the U.S. to many of the Patriots' matchups—while also continuing to share insights into her experiences online, earning a legion of followers in the process.

To date, Ann Michael boasts more than 1 million followers across TikTok and Instagram, where she reveals what life is really like as one of the NFL's most popular WAGs—a term that refers to the wives and girlfriends of high-profile athletes.

Ann Michael first rose to social media stardom for her baking skills, which she has heavily documented online.

Drake has admitted that he is not blessed with the same skills as his wife in the kitchen and leaves the baking to her, confessing that the most he can whip up is a "good bacon, egg, and cheese.".

During a press conference, Drake gushed about his wife, noting that he was grateful for her support during his career.

“After we got married, she came up here and had never lived up North. [She] had never seen this much snow on the ground, so obviously it was an adjustment for her," he said.

Recent findings suggest that "She’s loved it, she’s loved the people up here, living up here, [and] she loves the support. She’s got her own little journey that’s been awesome, being herself, and that’s the best thing about her and what I love her for.".

Recent findings suggest that he added, “Her watching my middle school football games playing quarterback to now getting a chance to watch me in the Super Bowl ... I wouldn’t rather do it with anybody else.".

Sources indicate that “My teammates have the right to whatever they choose, but I would definitely advise them to eventually get married. It’s one of the best things I’ve experienced in my life. There’s no better feeling than coming home to a wife that loves you and cares for you.

In a recent development, “I know my teammates, some of them are experiencing different parts of their life. I chose to get married young, and I don’t regret it one bit,” he said.
