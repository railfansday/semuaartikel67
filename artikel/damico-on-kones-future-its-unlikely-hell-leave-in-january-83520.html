📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In the pre-match of Parma-Roma, Roma sporting director Tony D’Amico spoke to DAZN.

“Our targets for the January window? It’s obviously premature to name names today. As the coach remarked yesterday, there are many variables between now and the January transfer window. Our goal is to try to improve the team.”.

In a recent development, “It’s a goal we must try to achieve in every transfer window. So I think Koné’s departure, as you asked, wouldn’t mean strengthening the team, in our opinion. I think that’s our philosophy, our approach.”.

According to recent reports, “We’ll evaluate what we can do a little later. We discuss the technical aspects and the characteristics of the players we imagine can bring something extra to the team in the January transfer window every day, which is not an easy one, it’s a market full of opportunities. But we’ll try to be ready.”.

“Kone leaving in January? We want to strengthen the team, so I think it’s unlikely.”.

“As the coach has always said since the beginning, expanding the squad also means this: seeing and testing all the players who are committed. We’re happy with everyone who, during moments of numerical difficulty, has contributed to maintaining the team’s high level.”.
