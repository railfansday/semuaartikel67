The Netherlands came crashing down to earth after hammering Greece as they were outclassed and outmuscled by Germany in Berlin.

Oranje went into the final match of the Nations League group stage with high hopes of recording their first win over their old rivals in seven matches stretching back to 2019.

Instead they suffered their second worst defeat ever at the hands of Germany, but still progressed to the knockout stages thanks to their thumping 7-0 win in Amsterdam on Friday.

Coach Xavi Hernandez was forced to make one change in midfield as Jurriën Timber replaced Barcelona’s Frenkie de Jong, who pulled out with an obturator muscle strain.

Germany shaded the first half and went ahead five minutes before the break when Freiburg striker Derry Scherhant cut in from the left hand side and beat Bart Verbruggen with a curling strike into the top corner.

Scherhant had struck the post minutes earlier, while the Dutch might have taken the lead earlier when Crysencio Summerville slipped in the penalty area as he tried to control a cross by Cody Gakpo.

But in the second half the Dutch fell apart. In the 60th minute Florian Wirtz shoved Denzel Dumfries off the ball and had space to tee up a left-footed shot from the edge of the area that gave Verbruggen no chance.

Nico Schlotterbeck added a third from a free-kick five minutes later, before Wirtz drifted past Gakpo on the right side of the penalty area to score his second.

The humiliation was complete when Brajan Gruda capped a fine attacking performance by firing in a rebound.

Sources indicate that dumfries made no excuses afterwards for what he admitted was a “terrible performance”. “We gave the ball away too easily, left too much space and were too weak in the challenges,” he told NOS. “We can’t let that happen.”.
