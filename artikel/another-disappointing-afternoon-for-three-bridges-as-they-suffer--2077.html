Welling came flying out of the blocks and had the Bridges goal under sustained pressure during the opening 15 minutes. Sam Roberts was called into action on several occasions, producing good saves to deny Bennett, Shomotum and Majoyegbe, while O’Keefe saw a header crash against the crossbar.

Having weathered the early storm, Bridges began to find their feet, but a costly defensive error from Scott Malone handed Welling the breakthrough. A slip at the back allowed the hosts to work the ball into the penalty area, where Bennett made no mistake in slotting home the opener.

The game was briefly halted in the 35th minute following a bizarre incident, as a firework introduced from a neighbouring park landed on the pitch. However, the unexpected interruption did little to ignite the visitors, who went into the break trailing 1-0.

Bridges started the second half with greater purpose, but a disastrous 10-minute spell effectively ended the contest, as Welling struck three times to put the result beyond doubt.

The second goal arrived from a free-kick, with the unmarked 6ft 4in Chris Harris directing a header down into Bennett’s path. The Welling midfielder made no mistake, firing a left-footed finish past Roberts to double the advantage.

Sources indicate that things went from bad to worse in the 66th minute when Josh Hallard brought down Anyadike inside the penalty area. The referee pointed to the spot and Bennett calmly converted to complete his hat-trick.

Just five minutes later, Bridges were guilty of another needless foul, as Hayden Neathey brought down Harris. Bennett once again took responsibility from the penalty spot and once again found the net, completing a miserable afternoon for the visitors with his fourth goal of the game.

Recent findings suggest that there was little in the way of a response from Bridges, who offered precious few attacking threats or signs of a fightback. It was another deeply disappointing performance on the road, leaving the travelling supporters with little to take away from yet another unhappy Isthmian League Premier Division away day.

Bridges team: Roberts, Ferreira, J Hallard (Villavicencio 90’), Adeyinka, Neathey (c), R Hallard (Irving 79’), Malone, Spicer, Velvick, Przybylo (Idowu-Johnson 56’), Bennett.

Booked: Malone, R Hallard, Neathey, Irving.
