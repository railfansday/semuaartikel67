The expectations are enormous, particularly after Nvidia’s market valuation declined from $5.8 trillion in early October to $3.9 trillion. Investors are looking for evidence that AI infrastructure spending remains sustainable despite the tech giant’s leadership transition.

Sources indicate that A strong earnings report could nevertheless change the narrative. Higher revenue guidance, resilient data center demand, and confidence in future AI spending could help stabilize technology valuations. Nvidia has repeatedly beaten analyst expectations for 8 straight earnings reports.
