An exciting German star now wants to join Liverpool, and the Reds are in the race for his signature.

Despite Jurgen Klopp spending close to a decade at Anfield, he never really brought a strong contingent to the club from his own home nation.

There were links to a myriad of players. Julian Brandt, Julian Draxler, and even Mario Gotze.

Liverpool did make a lot of signings from the Bundesliga as well. Loris Karius, Joel Matip, Naby Keita, Ibrahima Konate, Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai all came from Germay's top flight.

But Klopp's time at the club never heralded a German revolution.

Sources indicate that it's probably because Klopp wasn't completely in charge of recruitment. When he wanted to bring in Brandt, he was convinced to sign Mo Salah instead, and that's just one example.

Subsequently, Wirtz has become the face of this new era. He has also become an enigma at the same time. Wirtz was wonderfully talented in the Bundesliga. He remains that wonderfully talented player. However, the dilemma that concerns Andoni Iraola at the moment is how to bring the best out of Wirtz in a Liverpool shirt. He's been playing brilliantly for Germany under Klopp at international level. But he's not hit the ground running for the Reds just yet.
