Industry observers note that this was Aston Villa’s third trip to Istanbul in the calendar year but, this time, they departed with different emotions. Unlike on the banks of the Bosphorus at Besiktas Park in May, the scene of their unforgettable Europa League triumph, or at Fenerbahce in January, they came away with a bloody nose. Galatasaray’s match-winner, Deniz Gul, finished with similar, the substitute requiring a bandage after bravely taking a boot to the face from Nicolas Jackson as the hosts rippled the net on 77 minutes. It was the game’s first effort on target, Galatasaray maintaining their commanding form on home soil to extend Villa’s winless run to six matches in all competitions.

On the outside, this is a gladiatorial arena, a giant spaceship parked on Ali Sami Yen complex. But inside there is a poor pitch that began cutting up during the warm-up. John McGinn, one of four changes from Villa’s stalemate with Sunderland on Saturday, gestured towards the turf in frustration after a through ball went awry, but nobody of a Villa persuasion could blame defeat on the surface. As Unai Emery had stated, it was the same for both teams.
