Dax Shepard is opening up about the backlash over his marriage to Kristen Bell and how it has affected his mental health.

“One of the elements is a media storm about this continuing narrative that I’m an abusive husband, which is, like, very offensive,” Shepard, 51, said during the Monday, November 23, episode of his “Armchair Expert” podcast after cohost Monica Padman said he’s “in a depression” right now.

The Parenthood alum went on to recall the backlash he received last year after Bell, 46, shared an anniversary tribute in which she quoted her husband as saying, “I would never kill you. A lot of men have killed their wives at a certain point. Even though I’m heavily incentivized to kill you, I never would.”.

The post drew criticism as fans accused Shepard of abuse and making light of domestic violence.

In a recent development, “Kristen had a joke years ago on an anniversary. We were watching Dateline one time, and you know, everyone kills their rich spouse,” Shepard said on Monday. “This was actually me complimenting Kristen … So we’re watching it, and I go, you know, ‘I’m heavily incentivized to kill you, but I’m not going to.’ And she said that was one of the most romantic things anyone said to her. So she posts that.”.

Looking back at the aftermath of the controversial post, Shepard added, “Someone all of a sudden’s like, ‘Oh, it’s domestic abuse awareness month.’ Then she’s on the Today show and now we’re in this major thing.” (Bell skipped a Today show appearance amid the backlash.).

“And then this whole cottage industry of women defending Kristen that I’m like, I’m an abusive, dangerous man,” Shepard added.

The Zathura star then recalled another headline-making incident in January, when Cher told Shepard and Bell that the Frozen actress is “definitely the better half” in their marriage.

“We had Cher on, who said she wouldn’t pick me for Kristen,” Shepard said of the viral podcast moment, while Padman added, “That’s not what happened, but that’s what then it got taken as.”.

Shepard and Bell were the subject of controversy for the third time in the span of a year last month, when the couple revealed that Shepard flirts with his wife by complimenting other women – including telling a waitress she has nice eyebrows and tipping well.

“And then now this other thing about Kristen was saying, I flirt with her by tipping a ton to waiters. Like that gets her horny if I spoil someone. Additionally, if I make someone’s day and I, like, tell them that they have great shoes, I always try to find someone I don’t think gets a lot of compliments … I like to make people’s day,” he remarked.

Recalling the narrative that spawned from the confession, Shepard added, “The story is that I flirt with waitresses in front of Kristen. And this is, like, this continued behavior of her being in a controlling [relationship].”.

Shepard previously opened up about the continued backlash over his marriage earlier this month.

“For me, growing up around certain men who behaved a certain way, and then literally dedicating my whole life to being the opposite of all that … I don’t ever yell at my children,” he mentioned during the November 9 episode of his podcast, referring to his and Bell’s daughters, Lincoln, 13, and Delta, 11.

In a recent development, “I don’t swear at them. I don’t yell at my wife,” he continued at the time. “You know, like, to have given it my all to be the opposite of what, now, some group of people is saying I am is hard, of course. So hard. I don’t love it.”.
