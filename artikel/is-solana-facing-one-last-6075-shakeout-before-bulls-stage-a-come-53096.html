Questions are being raised about whether Solana (SOL) will face another painful leg lower before a major recovery.

According to on-chain metrics provider Tranquility Crypto, an immediate rebound may not be on the horizon because one more trip into the $60-$75 support zone might be needed to build a stronger foundation for an upside move.

Given that Solana is presently trading at $72.27 per CoinGecko data, the top altcoin might need a further drop to the psychological $60 level to rejuvenate.

Rather than chasing sudden green candlesticks, Tranquility Crypto believes a liquidity sweep followed by a decisive defense of the $60–$75 support level could offer a healthier recovery signal.

Well, if the technical structure plays out as expected, the on-chain metrics provider maintains that an upside target of $200–$240 could still see the light of day.

On the other hand, market analyst Alex Marzell sees a major development unfolding, as Solana has swept liquidity below a key support level, with the $70 area finally giving way after months of consolidation and repeated tests.

As a result, the focus now shifts to how SOL responds because a strong buying reaction and a reclaim of the lost trading range could signal that bulls are returning and potentially setting the stage for a recovery.

Nevertheless, failure to hold the $70 level could give room to the next major demand zone around $60.

Therefore, both Tranquility Crypto and Marzell agree that the zone between $60 and $70 should be watched closely, as it will determine Solana’s next move.

Beyond the short-term price action, Solana also has a significant institutional development working in its favor.

In a recent development, the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC), a major pillar of U.S. financial market infrastructure, is targeting a 2027 launch of tokenization services on Solana. This move represents another notable step toward bringing traditional financial assets onto blockchain networks.

If this undertaking gets the green light, it could strengthen Solana’s long-term investment prospects by connecting its infrastructure to a broader shift toward tokenized securities and institutional blockchain adoption.
