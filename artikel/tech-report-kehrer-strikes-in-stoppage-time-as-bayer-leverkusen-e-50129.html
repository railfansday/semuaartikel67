Bayer Leverkusen beat Stuttgart 3-2 in the Frauen Bundesliga on Monday 16 November, with Caroline Victoria Kehrer scoring the winner in the second minute of stoppage time. Stuttgart had just drawn level through Mira Arouna in the 87th minute. Julia Boike refereed the matchday 10 fixture.

Leverkusen led at the break. Loreen Bender opened the scoring in the 20th minute, set up by Cornelia Kramer, and the visitors took a 1-0 lead into half-time.

Sources indicate that stuttgart answered immediately after the restart. Dafina Redzepi equalised in the 46th minute, and the Stuttgart forward went on to have the biggest influence on the match. She was rated 8.86, the highest on the pitch, with a goal, an assist, 3 shots and 5 key passes across the full 90 minutes.

Industry observers note that leverkusen restored their advantage in the 59th minute when Maja Sternad finished from a Valentina Mädl pass. Sternad was booked 3 minutes later, after Luzie Zähringer had been cautioned for Stuttgart in the 54th minute. Those were the only cards of the night.

Redzepi then turned provider, laying on the 87th-minute equaliser for Arouna, who scored with her only shot in 21 minutes on the pitch. The draw lasted barely 5 minutes. Mädl delivered again, and Kehrer converted in the second minute of stoppage time, having been on the field for just 2 minutes. Mädl finished with 2 assists in a 33-minute appearance.

The numbers favoured the winners without being one-sided. Leverkusen had 54% of the ball, completed 366 of 483 passes against Stuttgart’s 266 of 387, and put 8 shots on target. Stuttgart goalkeeper L. Giuliani made 6 saves to keep her side in the game. Stuttgart actually shot more often, 15 times to 13, but only 4 of those efforts tested the Leverkusen goal and 8 went wide. Expected goals finished 1.43 to 1.2 in Leverkusen’s favour.

Recent findings suggest that leverkusen were caught offside 4 times. Stuttgart were not flagged once.

Recent findings suggest that the win leaves Leverkusen fifth on 19 points, level with Werder Bremen in fourth and separated only by goal difference, 4 against 6. Eintracht Frankfurt are third on 20 points. Bayern Munich lead on 30 points from 10 games, with VfL Wolfsburg second on 24.

It is a second straight league win for Leverkusen after a 2-0 victory over Hamburger SV. Kramer, provider for the opener, has 6 league goals this season, 2 behind leading scorer E. Peddemors of VfL Wolfsburg.

Sources indicate that for Stuttgart, the defeat ended a two-match winning run that had brought a 3-0 win over SGS Essen and a 3-1 victory against Mainz 05. Before that, they had lost three in a row, conceding 12 goals against Union Berlin, Bayern Munich and Eintracht Frankfurt. They are ninth with 11 points and a goal difference of -11.

Sources indicate that leverkusen travel to Union Berlin on Friday 20 November. Stuttgart visit SC Freiburg two days later, on Sunday 22 November.
