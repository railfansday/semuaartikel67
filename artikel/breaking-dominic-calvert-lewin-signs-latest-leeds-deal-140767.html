Leeds striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin has signed a newly introduced deal, taking his stay with the club until 2029.

The England international joined Leeds on a three-year deal after leaving Everton as a free agent in 2025, and has now extended for another two and a half years.

He scored 15 goals in all competitions last season and so far has six in this campaign, having rebooted his career following a difficult spell marred by injuries at Everton, and earned a recall to the England squad.

Leeds remarked: “Leeds United are delighted to announce Dominic Calvert-Lewin has signed a new contract with the club.

“The England international has put pen to paper on a two-and-a-half-year deal at Elland Road, keeping him in West Yorkshire until the summer of 2029.

“Everyone at Leeds United is thrilled Dominic has signed a new deal and we look forward to seeing him continue to impress in the famous white shirt.”.

Calvert-Lewin has started all 11 top-flight fixtures of this season and scored in the statement 4-1 victory over Chelsea on Saturday. Leeds currently sit seventh in the Premier League, four points off the Champions League places.

He won the Premier League’s Player of the Month Award in December after scoring in six consecutive matches - becoming the first Leeds player to win the award in 24 years.

His performances were recognised by Thomas Tuchel, who called him up to international duty for a 1-1 friendly draw with Uruguay in March, and he retained his place the squad for Nations League matches against Spain and Czechia.

His latest England appearance was against Spain earlier this month when he stepped off the bench to win his 15th cap in the Three Lions’ 3-2 win.
