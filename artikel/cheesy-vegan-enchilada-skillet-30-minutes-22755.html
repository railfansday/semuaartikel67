Cheesy Vegan Enchilada Skillet (30 Minutes!)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Everything you love about enchiladas, minus the fuss! This super flavorful, cheesy vegan enchilada skillet comes together in about 30 minutes with simple methods, minimal dishes, and no need to fill or roll tortillas.

This easy, nourishing dish features tender cubed sweet potato or winter squash, fiber- & protein-rich black beans, and a rich red enchilada sauce. Everything gets simmered in 1 pan and topped with a melty cashew “cheese” that makes it SUPER satisfying and delicious!

It’s believed that enchiladas may have originated from a similar Mayan dish called papadzules. But the first written recipe for enchiladas was in an 1831 cookbook called El Cocinero mexicano. Typically, enchiladas are made with meat, seafood, beans, cheese, potatoes, or vegetables stuffed in corn tortillas and topped with enchilada sauce.

Our “inspired-by” enchilada skillet has all the flavors of classic enchiladas, but it’s vegan, and instead of rolling the filling in tortillas, we slice the tortillas and cook everything together in one pan on the stovetop!

In a recent development, this 1-pan, 30-minute vegan enchilada skillet begins with a base of sautéed vegetables — onion, garlic, sweet potato, and poblano pepper. The veggies are flexible here, so feel free to experiment! We love kabocha squash for a fall variation and have found green bell peppers work well when poblanos aren’t available. We also think butternut squash would be nice, as would greens such as kale or spinach.

Once the onions are very tender and the squash and peppers are just beginning to soften, we stir in black beans + corn tortillas that have been sliced into strips.

Recent findings suggest that next comes the final ingredient in the skillet portion of this dish (hello, simple + flavorful!): red enchilada sauce. We love the bold flavor of our homemade enchilada sauce (and usually have it stocked in the freezer), but this recipe also works well with store-bought enchilada sauce.

According to recent reports, and lastly, it just wouldn’t be enchiladas without: the CHEESE! More specifically, a flavorful cashew “cheese” sauce inspired by our fan favorite Cheesy Vegan Pizza Beans. It’s made by blending up cashews, water, lime juice, garlic, tapioca starch, and sea salt. When poured over the enchilada skillet and covered with a lid, the heat and tapioca starch do their magic, creating a melty, cheesy layer! Total swoon!

According to recent reports, we highly recommend trying the cashew “cheese” sauce, even if you’re not vegan or dairy-free! It’s super easy and takes this dish to a whole upcoming level. However, this enchilada skillet is also delicious with shredded vegan or dairy cheese (such as pepper jack).
