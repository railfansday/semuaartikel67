Florian Wirtz has excelled in a central attacking midfield role for Germany since Jürgen Klopp took charge, most recently scoring once in a 3-1 victory over Serbia and bagging a brace in a 5-0 thrashing of the Netherlands.

It's a position Jamal Musiala has frequently occupied in the past, while Wirtz has often been deployed out wide for the national team.

However, given how impressive the Liverpool star has been in a central role during Musiala's injury-enforced absence, Germany legend Mario Basler sees little reason for Klopp to move him back out to the wing when the Bayern Munich star returns.

“Jürgen gives him that extra confidence because he believes in him and trusts him. And Florian repays that faith with his performances and his enjoyment of the game," Basler mentioned on his podcast “Basler ballert”.

It has been revealed that “I don't see why anyone would put him back out on the wing now. You would be taking away his strengths. For me, Florian is clearly the main man in that position at the moment. And I believe he will stay there even when Jamal Musiala returns.”.

According to the 57-year-old, both Wirtz and Musiala are at their best in a central role, leaving Klopp with a difficult selection dilemma once the Bayern Munich playmaker returns to full fitness.

In a recent development, “You can obviously play Musiala on the left or right, but you would also be taking away his strengths. And after these performances, you can hardly put Wirtz out on the wing anymore," he stated, adding:.

“That will be a really difficult decision for Jürgen Klopp.”.
