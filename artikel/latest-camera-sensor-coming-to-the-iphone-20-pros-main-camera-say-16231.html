Sources indicate that the biggest improvement to iPhone photo quality that Apple could make would be to give the device a larger sensor. There have been two reports suggesting this is on the way, although with a potentially rather large catch.

A leaker today says that the iPhone 20 Pro is getting a upcoming and more sophisticated sensor for the main camera, but details are unclear ….

There are two main reasons people still buy standalone cameras despite having an iPhone in their pocket wherever they go. The first is lens quality: iPhones have small plastic lenses compared to the far larger and more sophisticated glass lenses used with dedicated cameras.

Sources indicate that the second is sensor size. All other things being equal, a larger sensor will capture more light and provide a higher quality image. Current smartphone sensors are absolutely tiny compared to those used in even mid-range consumer cameras.

Industry observers note that we’ve seen two previous reports suggesting that a larger sensor is on the way for future iPhones. One of these suggested that it would be coming in 2027 or 2028, although a later one suggested the latter date was more likely.

The reports were not without a catch, however, suggesting that the larger sensor would only be for the telephoto lens and would have a 200MP resolution. Squeezing more pixels into a given sensor size tends to sacrifice low light capabilities in return for higher resolution. This is less likely to be an matter with a telephoto lens, which is normally used outdoors rather than inside.

What I’d really love to see is a significantly larger sensor for the main camera, with no increase in resolution. This would improve low light performance and overall image quality.

A brief report from leaker Ice Universe suggests that the iPhone 20 Pro will get a upcoming and more sophisticated sensor for the main camera.

iPhone 20 Pro to feature a fresh sensor using 17nm logic process for its main camera next year.

There are no further details, so we don’t know whether the size of the sensor will be increased or remain the same.
