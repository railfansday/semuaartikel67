📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Evening in peaceful waters for Real Madrid. Against Valencia this Saturday, the Merengues scored an eighth consecutive success in all competitions (0-3). It wasn’t the most complicated of the series, but it was certainly the most emotionally significant with an evening dedicated to Jesús De Miguel, who died at the start of the week during a Spanish second division match. The former Tenerife player was honored at kick-off, before receiving a moving tribute from Mestalla in the 9th minute of play, in reference to his jersey number.

Recent findings suggest that in this very particular context, the Madrilenians will have needed a little less than thirty minutes to play their football and extinguish any suspense. The only real warnings in favor of the locals? A loss of ball from Tchouameni forcing Courtois to work in front of Mayol (19th), then this goal refused to Rodriguez for offside a few seconds later (20th). As against AEK Athens in midweek, Federico Valverde played a central role in his team’s success. Preferred over Denzel Dumfries and Trent Alexander-Arnold at right-back, it was he who opened the scoring with a beautiful half-volley following a free kick from Kylian Mbappé deflected by the wall (28th, 0-1).

Left to rest for the trip to Greece on Wednesday in the Champions League (2-3), Arda Güler made the break after an outburst from Bellingham (36th, 0-2). The reward for a top match, which logically earned him the title of man of the match. A little less prominent than in his last outings, Yan Diomandé missed a 0-3 ball before the break (42nd). And yet, Madrid’s domination seemed to elude Pablo Maffeo, who let his frustration explode at half-time by pointing the finger at the refereeing decisions. A barely daring exit from the right side, more busy distributing blows during the first act.

Sources indicate that the second one-sided period proved Madrilenians right: Güler (55th), Mbappé (56th, 57th), then Mbappé again on an attempted return (65th), had opportunities to make the decision. But through insistence, the captain of the Blues finally found the solution in a Güler caviar. He eliminated Dimitrievski and scored his 13th goal of the season (75th, 0-3). Gaya was sent off for a foul on the Madrid number 10 (79th), who was then entitled to a friendly chant from the local supporters: “Mbappé beach ball”, then to whistles as he left. Mbappé will still leave Mestalla with the feeling of accomplishment this evening: he is two units behind Raphinha in the La Liga scorers’ ranking, and Real Madrid is temporarily back at the level of FC Barcelona.
