From the Stephen Lawrence inquiry in 1999 to Louise Casey’s landmark review in 2023, successive reports on the Metropolitan police have arrived at the same damning conclusion: Britain’s largest police force is institutionally racist.

It will, therefore, come as little surprise that three years after Casey’s report, an independent review led by Gillian Fairfield has found that institutional racism, misogyny and homophobia persist within the force.
