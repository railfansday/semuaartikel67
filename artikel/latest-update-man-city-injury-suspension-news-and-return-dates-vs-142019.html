Out-of-sorts Manchester City will be looking to end a four-match winless run in the Premier League when they lock horns with champions Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium on Sunday afternoon.

The Citizens were held to a frustrating 1-1 draw at home to Fulham last weekend, which followed three league defeats to Aston Villa, Brighton & Hove Albion and Nottingham Forest prior to the international break.

Enzo Maresca's side then came from behind twice to rescue a 2-2 home draw with Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday, a game that saw Matheus Nunes recover from injury to start at right-back.

Ahead of their latest top-flight battle with Mikel Arteta's Arsenal, Sports Mole rounds up Man City’s latest injury and suspension news for this weekend's clash in North London.

Sources indicate that possible return date: December 19 (vs. Hull City).

Antonine Semenyo has been sidelined since picking up a knee injury against Ipswich Town on October 17, and the attacker is not expected to return until the middle of next month.
