The biggest game in South American club football takes place this weekend with the 2026 edition of the Copa Libertadores final.

Palmeiras will take on Estudiantes at the Estadio Centenario in Montevideo on Saturday, but which faces might you recognise?

Here are seven of the players in the two squads who featured in the Premier League at some point.

Everton fans will remember Funes Mori, who was at Goodison from 2015 to 2018.

He signed for £9.5m and played a decent amount in the 2015-16 season with Phil Jagielka and John Stones regularly out with injuries.

A red card in the Merseyside derby relegated him back to the bench and he ended the following season by tearing his meniscus with Argentina.

It took until April of the following season for him to return to the squad but he was shipped out in the summer to Villarreal.

His path to Estudiantes has gone from Villarreal to Al Nassr to Cruz Azul to River Plate and then finally at his current club who he joined last year.

Lisandro Martinez may be Man Utd’s current underperforming Argentine centre-back but before him it was Marcos Rojo filling that role.

He was signed under Louis van Gaal who actually managed to get some decent performances out of him early on and he was at United for a surprisingly long time, staying into 2021 although he did not play more than 10 league games for the final four seasons.

After his United career came to an end, he moved back to Argentina to join Boca Juniors before joining Racing Club in 2025.

He left them in August before signing with Estudiantes, his former club, in 2026.

The Belgian-born Brazilian started his career at Manchester United as a member of their youth academy but struggled for game time and was loaned out to four different teams.

He found more of a home in West London, joining Fulham in 2022 and making 119 appearances for the club.

He joined Palmeiras in 2025 and was played in the 2025 Copa Libertadores final, losing 1-0 against his former club Flamengo.

In between two separate stints at Lazio, Brazilian winger Anderson joined West Ham.

West Ham being West Ham means they signed him for a club-record fee of £36m but they did not get much return on their investment.

His first season was okay with nine goals in 36 Premier League matches but that shrank to one in 25 for his second.

That level of performance saw him go out on loan to Porto before moving back to Lazio ahead of the 2021/22 season.

He left Europe in 2024, moving to Palmeiras where he is still playing at the age of 33.

If the pointless question was to name the most obscure Southampton player from the 2010s, then Guido Carrillo is your man.

The striker joined the St Mary’s club in the 2018 winter transfer window for a fee of £19m but would make just 10 appearances in his two years at the club.

He spent the latter of those on loan at Leganes who he joined the summer after he signed for Southampton with the Premier League club clearly feeling they had made a mistake.

In October 2020, his contract was terminated and he joined Elche before moving to the excellently named Henan Songshan Longmen and arriving at Estudiantes in 2023.

The Paraguayan had never played European football before making the move to the City Ground for £9.3m.

Sources indicate that the left winger signed a five-year deal and even managed to score in his second game but barely featured throughout the season. On just one occasion did he play more than an hour for the club as Nuno Espirito Santo clearly didn’t fancy him.
