Recent findings suggest that bitcoin dropped below $56,000 on Saturday, and Ethereum and XRP fell with it. BTC is trading at $55,693, down 8.9% in a day and 16.2% in a week. Ethereum fell 10.1% to $1,543.23 and is down 17.9% for the week. XRP lost 8.2% to $0.8701 and is down 12.4% over seven days.

Liquidations Hit as Stocks Slide.

Sources indicate that when Bitcoin broke below $57,000, its lowest level since September 2024, about $400 million in long bets were wiped out in just 10 minutes. Close to $160 billion left the crypto market in 12 hours. US stocks had a rough week too. The Nasdaq 100 had its biggest weekly fall since the Covid crash in March 2020.

Has the Bear Market Already Started?

In a recent development, counted from Bitcoin’s record high of about $126,198 in October 2025, the bear market is already about 409 days old. The 2013 to 2015 bear market ended after about 410 days, so a new low this week would make this one the longest in Bitcoin’s history.

It has been revealed that this drop has been shallower than earlier ones, though. Bitcoin is down about 55% from its peak. Past bear markets took it down 76% to 85%. A 76% fall from the 2025 high would mean a price near $30,000. A fall as major as the 2013 to 2015 one would take it close to $20,000.

Spot Bitcoin ETFs lost about $300 million in one day and nearly $600 million the next. Apart from one flat day, money has flowed out of these funds for 10 trading days in a row. Bitcoin miners are also said to be selling.

It has been revealed that bitcoin’s fall below $61,500 took it straight into the support zone of $58,000 to $56,000. It is now below that zone, and the next support area sits between $53,000 and $50,000. Large groups of leveraged positions sit near $56,000, $51,000 and $50,700, and those levels could pull the price lower.

It has been revealed that on short-term charts, Bitcoin keeps making lower lows. Bears now need to defend $64,000. A move above that would be the first sign the trend is turning. One early sign of a possible bounce is showing on the RSI, but it needs two green daily candles in a row to confirm. If the price closes below its latest low, that signal is cancelled. Until there is a clear turn, such as a trendline break or a major daily wick on heavy volume, the trend stays down.
