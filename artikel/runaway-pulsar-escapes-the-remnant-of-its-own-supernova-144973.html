Runaway pulsar escapes the remnant of its own supernova

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (64 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that A team of astronomers has spotted a rare example of a pulsar that has escaped the cloud of debris left by the supernova explosion in which it formed. Led by Rohit Dokara, formerly at the Max Planck Institute for Radio Astronomy, the team made the discovery by chance, adding to just a handful of similar known cases.
