The “Enough Phone” is trying to fulfill the small Android phone dream and, now, it’s getting some spec upgrades to the battery, RAM, and storage, all with no added cost.

Revealed earlier this month, the “Enough Phone” is an Android phone set to launch in 2027 at a size about the same as an iPhone 13 Mini.

According to recent reports, now mostly funded on Kickstarter, “Enough Phone” originally promised to back up its 5.3-inch display with 8GB of RAM, 128GB of storage, and a battery roughly on par with what you’d expect from a tiny phone. We’re now getting some upgrades on those original specs.
