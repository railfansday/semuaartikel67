Jude Bellingham has been praised for changing one person’s life ‘forever’ after Real Madrid’s win over PSV Eindhoven.

This week, Madrid were back in Champions League action as they welcomed Dutch side PSV to the Bernabeu, with Jose Mourinho’s men looking to continue their 100% record in the League Phase.

It has been revealed that PSV stunned the Bernabeu crowd into silence after Ivan Perisic scored from 12 yards with 32 minutes on the clock. However, goals from Federico Valverde, Bellingham, Vinicius Junior, plus a brace from Kylian Mbappe sealed a massive 5-1 win for Madrid, putting them top of the standings.

Bellingham’s goal was arguably the pick of the bunch as he showed delicate control of the football before unleashing a stunning volley into the back of the PSV net. As he ran away in celebration, Bellingham pulled out his now-famous celebration in front of the Madrid fans, who copied the Englishman’s action.

A picture of Bellingham celebrating against PSV has since gone viral, with the man himself tracking down the photographer behind the image.

Taking to social media, the photographer in question, Mark Verhees, posted a screenshot of the conversation he shared with Bellingham.

In a recent development, “Hi mate, finally tracked down the origin of the picture. Just wanted to say thank you for capturing such an amazing moment! I'm gonna upload it and tag you! Thanks again!”.

In response, Verhees noted: “Of course, brother! It's a pleasure. Thank you for the love!”.

Bellingham has since posted the picture on his official Instagram account, tagging Verhees in the process.

Reacting to Bellingham’s action, one fan mentioned: “He’s changed that photographer’s life forever.”.

Another tweeted: “Imagine being this polite when you literally have everything”.

Sources indicate that A third fan added: “Boy does Jude seem like a great human. Hard not to want the best for people like him.”.

Someone else wrote: “How can you even hate this guy?!”.

A fifth commented: “The man’s turning into a legend off the pitch as well!”.

Another fan said: “Moments like this are why a lot of people root for Bellingham beyond the talent. Genuine.”.

One user added: “People don't even know the influence of some footballer.”.

Verhees recently posted on his own Instagram account, which now has 11.9k followers: “I’ve said it once, and I’m saying it again: Grateful for what this life can be, thank you to every person supporting me, this is bigger than I could ever imagine.”.
