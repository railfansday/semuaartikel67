The Terminator and Total Recall actor is suiting up in bright red and white for Amazon Prime’s new holiday creative campaign, “Santa Schwarzenegger,” which debuts on Monday.

He then orders a Santa suit from Prime, allowing for anonymity as Schwarzenegger donates snacks to a dog rescue center, watches a basketball game, and heaves a Christmas tree onto the roof of a car.

Throughout his career, Schwarzenegger has played plenty of iconic roles. He’s portrayed a killing machine from the future, Batman’s nemesis Mr. Freeze, both Hercules and his father, Zeus, and an undercover cop teaching kindergarteners.

But Schwarzenegger states no role he has ever played has been as worldwide as this one.

“This is the perfect time for me to play Santa, because I’m at that age now and it’s more believable,” Schwarzenegger tells Fast Company. “With all the interesting characters that I played in my career—from Conan to Terminator—I thought that Santa is the ultimate.”.

For Amazon, the Schwarzenegger spot fits neatly into two goals: driving sales and attention to Prime, a core pillar of the online retail giant’s Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and other holiday sales events throughout the season.

But it also ties in neatly with Amazon Prime’s The Man with the Bag, an upcoming holiday film that debuts on December 2, starring Schwarzenegger as Santa for a second time.

Recent findings suggest that alan Ritchson, Awkwafina, and Ken Jeong are also featured in the film’s cast. The film also ties into Amazon’s 2026 holiday campaign, which is called “Joy Delivered.”.

“Arnold is the king of the kids, we saw that when he made Kindergarten Cop,” Jo Shoesmith, Amazon’s vice president and global chief brand officer, tells Fast Organization. “There is a warmth and charm to him that lives outside of the character of Santa.”.

“Santa Schwarzenegger” will run through December 23 across several channels including television, digital, social, and streaming audio. Yet another component of the campaign that Amazon is promoting is Schwarzenegger’s curated holiday gift guide, which includes plush dog toys, supplements, toys intended for children and grandchildren, and dumbbells.

“Think about it, Santa has to fly all the way from the North Pole, he has to be in shape to hand out the gifts at every single continent to go to every single house,” claims Schwarzenegger. “It is a lot of work. He has to be in shape.”.

There are also two books: Better Every Day, a self-help book penned by Schwarzenegger that’s due to be published in January, and Arnold, which is written about him.

There’s also a cigar lighter, which may seem counterintuitive for the fitness enthusiast, but apparently he’s smoked them for over 40 years, including during his interview with Fast Tech giant.

In a recent development, “We never want to twist the arm of the personality to put something in their mouth that doesn’t come across as authentic,” notes Shoesmith. “It’s very driven by his holiday ordering list.”.

As an Austrian, Schwarzenegger states his holiday traditions differed from how he raised his children in America. The holiday is celebrated on December 24 and the gift giver is a baby Jesus called Christkind, not Santa, though Schwarzenegger says the latter has become more popular there in recent years.

“I always question with every script I get: is this something that would just be good for America, or just good for Europe, or is this something that can play in every continent,” says Schwarzenegger.

Santa, he says, is a role that can “entertain the whole world.”.
