Industry observers note that leicester City sit 7th in League One ahead of this weekend’s clash with Mansfield Town. Russell Martin’s side have picked up 23 points from 15 matches, winning six, drawing five and losing four.

In a recent development, form has picked up in recent weeks but as the January window gets closer, talk of departures has ramped up. One of which could be loanee Harry Howell, who Brighton are considering recalling from LCFC, according to Pete O’Rourke.

According to recent reports, howell has featured primarily from the bench in League One this season, making just the one start. In all competitions he has two goal contributions in 13 games for the Foxes.

Brighton are considering a recall to send Howell elsewhere in January in a bid to get more game-time. A host of clubs both in England and across Europe are keen on the 18-year-old.

Howell’s loan spell at LCFC has so far failed to provide the regular senior football Brighton wanted for his development. The 18-year-old England U19 international joined the League One club in August to gain valuable first-team experience after limited opportunities at the Amex.

It has been revealed that martin has acknowledged Howell’s restricted role, explaining that illness disrupted his early progress. The attacking midfielder initially made a positive impression before a virus affected his fitness and forced the club to manage his minutes carefully.

By the time he returned, Leicester had established a rhythm and altered their formation, leaving Howell struggling to find a suitable place in the side.

According to recent reports, there have been encouraging signs when given opportunities, including a league goal and promising contributions from the bench in the FA Cup. However, the overall lack of playing time means the loan has fallen short of its primary objective.

In a recent development, howell was intended to be a key player for Leicester City this season but for a multitude of reasons that has not happened. With that in mind, it is no surprise that Brighton are considering moving him on.

However, that should not be a real issue for LCFC for the rest of the season. The Foxes have plenty of options in that area, with the likes of Kamari Doyle and Sammy Braybrooke currently in fine form.

Losing loanee Howell may mean that Leicester can go out and bring in another loanee in January with a squad spot opening up. With players like Conor Chaplin, Tommy Watson and Liam Cullen also in the squad, they can likely afford to bring in a play in another area instead of a direct replacement.
