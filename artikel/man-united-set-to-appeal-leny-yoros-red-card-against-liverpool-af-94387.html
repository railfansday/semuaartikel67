Manchester United are set to appeal the red card handed to Leny Yoro during their 4-0 defeat against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

The defender was sent off just eight minutes into the contest after being penalised for a challenge on Liverpool forward Alexander Isak as the Swedish international ran through towards goal.

Referee Sam Barrott deemed that Yoro had denied an obvious goalscoring opportunity, despite Isak appearing to have lost control of the ball prior to the tackle.

Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the subsequent free-kick to put Liverpool ahead, with the hosts then going on to pick a comfortable victory that marked a dismal start to Mauricio Pochettino’s tenure as United’s upcoming head coach.

It is understood that United now plan to lodge an official appeal in the hope of having Yoro’s suspension overturned.

Speaking to the BBC following the final whistle, United captain Bruno Fernandes insisted it was "never a red card", adding: "I think it’s too many referees on the pitch and too many on VAR, seated, with such a long time to see and put it back, go forward, put it back, do it in slow motion, whatsoever.

"To make a mistake like this can change a game. But obviously, then, it’s not because of them that we concede the goal from the free-kick, but it changes completely the flow of the game.".

United presently sit 19th in the Premier League table after suffering their fifth defeat of the season on Sunday. They have only won one of their 11 league games to date - a 5-2 victory over Ipswich Town in August.

Following Szoboszlai’s free-kick, Florian Wirtz doubled Liverpool’s lead within two minutes to give United a huge mountain to climb.

Pochettino opted to bring Harry Maguire on for Bryan Mbeumo to try and tighten things up at the back after Yoro’s dismissal, but Liverpool continued to create chance after chance.

They were eventually rewarded when Rio Ngumoha made it 3-0 in the 66th minute, before substitute Victor Munoz completed the rout eight minutes from time.

Liverpool return to Champions League action against Club Brugge on Wednesday, while United also play on the same day against Sporting Lisbon.
