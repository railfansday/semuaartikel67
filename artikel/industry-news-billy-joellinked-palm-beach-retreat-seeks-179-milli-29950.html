Industry News: Billy Joel–Linked Palm Beach Retreat Seeks $17.9 Million—With a Private Pool and Lavish Primary Suite

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A recently renovated Palm Beach, FL, townhouse associated with Billy Joel has been listed for $17.9 million. It features a private pool, an elevator, and a primary suite complete with separate bathrooms and walk-in closets.

Recent findings suggest that the four-bedroom property is being listed with Christian Angle of Christian Angle Real Estate. Its Mediterranean architecture and courtyard entrance set the scene for a residence that pairs formal entertaining rooms with outdoor spaces for dining and lounging.

According to recent reports, the asking price puts the home well above its previous purchase price. According to Mansion Global, which cited PropertyShark records, the limited liability organization that owns the townhouse paid $9.05 million in 2021.

A sale at the current asking price would represent an $8.85 million increase over that purchase, before accounting for renovation expenses, transaction costs, and other ownership expenses.

It has been revealed that it would also put the closing price within striking distance of twice the amount paid five years ago. Joel’s association with the property has been reported through the company that holds its title. In 2022, Palm Beach Daily News reported the ownership connection.

Upon entering the residence, guests are greeted with a soaring foyer, where a staircase with iron detailing rises beneath a coffered ceiling.

According to recent reports, the layout provides distinct spaces for hosting, working, and everyday meals, with a formal living room, a dining room, an office, and a breakfast nook adjoining the kitchen. A pecky cypress ceiling adds a wood accent to the cooking space.

The primary suite includes a Juliet balcony, along with its two bathrooms and two walk-in closets. Across the home, there are five full bathrooms and one half-bathroom.

Sources indicate that the property, which was built in 2022, spans 4,956 square feet. A two-car garage and paved driveway round out the practical amenities.

Recent findings suggest that beyond the interiors, the pool is accompanied by an open patio, a built-in barbecue, and a sheltered area for outdoor meals. Worth Avenue, Mid-Town beach, and the Australian Docks are all within blocks, according to the listing.

The property adds another chapter to the musician’s extensive history of buying and selling homes in Florida.

The townhouse’s arrival on the market comes as Joel prepares for a public appearance in Boca Raton next month.

Sources indicate that on Dec. 14, the songwriter is scheduled to take part in an event at Florida Atlantic University, where students will have the opportunity to ask him about his career and creative process, according to the Palm Beach Post.

Although the event’s title includes a reference to music, Joel’s representative clarified to the newspaper that the evening is intended as a conversation. Any playing would consist of short musical demonstrations to help explain his answers, with no band or prepared setlist.

The appearance follows a period in which Joel stepped away from his scheduled concerts after being diagnosed with normal pressure hydrocephalus.
