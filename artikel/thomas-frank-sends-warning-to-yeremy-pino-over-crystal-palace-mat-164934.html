New Crystal Palace manager Thomas Frank has called on Yeremy Pino to improve his disciplinary record.

The Spanish attacker has been one of Palace’s better players in a difficult season, but is suspended for Sunday’s clash with Hull after picking up his fifth yellow card of the Premier League season in last weekend’s 3-2 defeat at Coventry.

Pino was also booked in Thursday’s 2-1 Europa League win over 10-man Real Sociedad, and Frank admitted discipline was something the 24-year-old would have to work on.

Sources indicate that “Yes, I think it’s fair to say,” Frank remarked. “I think he’s a very good player — very key for us and has gone up a level this season — so of course it is disappointing.

“It is something he needs to work on. That is too many yellow cards. Of course, we would have loved to play with Yeremy and [Ismaila] Sarr and JP [Jean-Philippe Mateta] in an important match — three of our best offensive players — but others will need to step up and show what they can do.”.

Asked whether Palace had picked up any new knocks against Sociedad, Frank replied: “Not that I am aware of, so hopefully not.”.

Palace sit bottom of the Premier League table and have half the tally of 14 points 11th-place Hull have taken from their first 11 games.

“It will be difficult against a Hull team that is fighting with every inch of their bodies from all players on the pitch,” Frank said.
