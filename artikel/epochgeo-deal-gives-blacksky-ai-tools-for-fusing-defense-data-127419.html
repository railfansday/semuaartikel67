BlackSky Technology unveiled on November 24, 2026 that it had acquired EpochGeo, adding artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) tools that fuse multiple data sources to its real-time space-based intelligence business.

EpochGeo develops spatiotemporal AI/ML, which examines information across location and time, alongside data-management tools and mission-focused multi-source analytics. Its technology fuses sources including radio-frequency and signals intelligence for mission-critical defense applications. Those capabilities broaden BlackSky’s existing imagery and analytics business by supporting analysis across space-based and terrestrial inputs, with the stated aim of helping owners rapidly detect and respond to relevant changes.

BlackSky described the acquisition as a response to defense and national-security organizations investing in space-based intelligence and seeking to use AI to fuse diverse data sources, understand patterns and anticipate developments. The organization’s broader strategy is to deliver real-time intelligence on locations and events affecting national and economic security.

Sources indicate that founded in 2018, EpochGeo counts major U.S.-based defense prime contractors and the U.S. government among its customers. BlackSky will continue supporting those customers through existing contracts. The acquisition therefore includes established defense relationships alongside the analytics technology.

According to recent reports, blackSky CEO Brian O’Toole noted EpochGeo has developed and deployed mission-critical analytics with end users in secure operational environments, including support for U.S. combatant commands and international ministries of defense. “These are exactly the capabilities our largest customers are asking us to provide as an integrated extension to real-time imaging services,” he said.

According to recent reports, shortly after the acquisition’s closing, BlackSky intends to grant restricted stock unit awards to as many as 43 EpochGeo employees as an incentive to join the company in upcoming, non-executive roles. The awards would cover up to 565,000 shares of BlackSky common stock if all of the prospective employees agree to join.

The grants will use an inducement equity incentive strategy approved by BlackSky’s board on October 29, 2026 without stockholder approval under the Newly introduced York Stock Exchange’s employment-inducement exception. One-quarter of each award is scheduled to vest on December 10, 2027, followed by quarterly vesting of one-sixteenth of the underlying shares. Vesting remains subject to each recipient’s continued service through the applicable date.

BlackSky plans to make EpochGeo’s capabilities available through space-based intelligence and AI subscriptions or sovereign mission solutions, and to integrate them across its large-scale data contracts and mission programs. The stated goals are to increase the value customers derive from BlackSky’s collection capabilities, accelerate adoption of its analytics services and support more comprehensive situational-awareness and decision-support products.

According to recent reports, the acquisition links EpochGeo’s deployed defense analytics and customer relationships to those distribution channels. The central operational question is whether adding analytics that fuse radio-frequency and signals intelligence with other data to BlackSky’s existing imaging services will produce the broader analytics adoption the company is seeking.
