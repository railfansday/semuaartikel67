Industry News: Rivian's Winter Sale Is Live: Save on Chargers, Accessories, And More

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that rivian is discounting dozens of items in its online Gear Shop as part of a Winter Sale event that runs through December 4. The California startup is offering chargers, accessories, and apparel at prices that are between 15% and 30% lower than usual.

According to recent reports, charging equipment is front and center. The firm’s hardwired wall chargers, which are capable of delivering up to 11.5 kilowatts either through a NACS or J1772 connector, can be had for $680, down from the regular $800. The Rivian Portable Charger with a NACS connector, which plugs into a household outlet and can deliver up to 7.6 kW, is now $340, $60 cheaper than usual.

Next up are charging adapters. The Gen 1 R1S and R1T came from the factory with a CCS1 charging port, so they need an adapter to top up at NACS-equipped chargers. Meanwhile, the R2 and Gen 2 R1 EVs have a NACS port, so they need an adapter to use CCS1-equipped stalls. Over the course of the Winter Sale, Rivian’s NACS and Combo CCS1 DC adapters are discounted to $170, down from $200.

The portable outdoor Travel Kitchen, which has two induction burners, an output of up to 1,500 watts, a cutting board, and a slide-out drawer, is now $1,190, down 15% from the usual $1,400 selling price.

Crossbars are also on sale, with a set of R1 or R2 bars starting at $595, down from $700. A set of all-weather floor mats for the R1T or R2 now costs $192, down from $225, while an R1S set goes for $213, down from $250.

Recent findings suggest that bundles get lower prices, too. For instance, an R2 protection bundle, which includes a windshield sunshade, a set of all-weather floor mats, and a protection film for the digital instrument cluster, has been discounted from $390 to $332.

According to recent reports, apparel can be had for cheap, with discounts as high as 58%. For instance, an Adventure Shirt is now $50, down from $120, while a T-Shirt is 50% cheaper than before, and a hat is $25, down from $35.

Rivian hasn’t included any of its vehicles in the Winter Sale promotion, but it is offering some discounts separately. Customers who order any newly introduced R1 vehicle between November 27 and November 30 get a $1,000 discount. Furthermore, some 2026 R1s can be financed at 1.99% APR.
