A Florida man who used AI to impersonate Kick streamer N3on in order to steal approximately $260,000 in cryptocurrency has been sentenced to almost four years in federal prison.

Industry observers note that the scheme targeted organizers of a memecoin project, who were led to believe N3on had agreed to promote and participate in it. Prosecutors say the impersonator communicated with them as both the streamer and his representative.

Recent findings suggest that N3on has previously experimented with an AI version of himself on his own channel. In September, he defended broadcasts designed to continue while he slept, telling viewers he would still appear on stream himself.

In a November 24 announcement, the US Attorney’s Office for the Southern District of Florida confirmed that David Anthony Sanchez, 36, of Port St. Lucie, had received a 47-month sentence.

Sanchez pleaded guilty to money laundering and aggravated identity theft, with US District Judge Melissa Damian imposing the prison term.

According to court records cited by prosecutors, Sanchez set up accounts that appeared to belong to N3on and signed agreements using the streamer’s identity.

He also supplied an AI-generated recording mimicking N3on’s voice, falsely presenting it as confirmation that the creator was taking part in the cryptocurrency project.

The organizers then transferred around $260,000 in cryptocurrency to accounts Sanchez controlled.

In a recent development, WPTV reported that Sanchez converted a substantial portion of the funds into US dollars through transactions intended to hide where the money came from and who owned it. Prosecutors said he used the proceeds for his personal benefit.

“In this case, the defendant exploited artificial intelligence to manipulate the trust of unsuspecting victims and deprive them of their money,” Acting US Attorney Yara Lorenzo Klukas stated.

Sanchez was also ordered to pay restitution to the victims, although the announcement did not specify an amount. The FBI’s Miami field office investigated the case.
