Report warns attacks on critical infrastructure could lead to demand for retaliation and expose EU divisions.

Russian sabotage attacks on Europe are increasing and a “mass-casualty event” is now possible as increasingly skilled operatives target critical infrastructure with ever more complex methods, a leading strategic thinktank has said.

The International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS) noted in a report that Russian intelligence services and their proxies were targeting European railways, energy networks and defence industries in an attempt to weaken support for Ukraine.
