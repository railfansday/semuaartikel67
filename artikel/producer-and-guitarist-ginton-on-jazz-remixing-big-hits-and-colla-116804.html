Producer and guitarist Ginton on jazz, remixing big hits and collaborating on a track for Drake

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that amsterdam-based guitarist, DJ and music producer Ginton is known for blending reggae, Afro, Caribbean and electronic music. He told FRANCE 24 that his background in jazz was "the reason I can make the music I make today". He also told us of his "surprise" in collaborating on a track for Drake's album "Honestly, Nevermind". He spoke to us in Perspective.
