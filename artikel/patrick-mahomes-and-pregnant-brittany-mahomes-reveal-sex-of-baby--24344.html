Patrick Mahomes and Brittany Mahomes are excited to add another player to their starting lineup.

The Kansas City Chiefs quarterback and his wife of four years shared they’re expecting a baby boy who will join siblings Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months.

It has been revealed that patrick and Brittany revealed the news in a joint Instagram post Nov. 17, sharing a look at their gender reveal party with their team of family and friends. In the video, the two used champagne glasses to dip into a cake, pulling out blue pieces as matching confetti canons exploded in the kitchen. Patrick and Brittany shared a sweet embrace before quickly showering their kids with hugs and kisses.

According to recent reports, the NFL star and Sports Illustrated model first shared the news of the pregnancy on Nov. 10, posting a photo of their kids with their hands on Brittany’s baby bump and writing, “Another blessing from God. & then there were 6.”.

Patrick recently admitted that he would be delighted if the couple welcomed another boy to huddle up with him and son Bronze.

“Obviously it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way. I’m excited for it,” Patrick said during a Nov. 11 press conference. “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score.”.

Since welcoming their kids, Brittany and Patrick, both 31, have been candid about how much parenthood has changed their lives—especially when it comes to how Brittany calls the plays every day.

"When you become a mom, you learn to say no to things that aren't as key and focus on what's best for your life and your family," she told Yahoo Entertainment in July. "Keeping my eyes and my mind and my focus on my family and the things that matter the most.".

As for Patrick, the Super Bowl champ swiftly learned that patience was the key to fatherhood, as was acting as a cheerleader for his little ones.

"You learn how to try to really boost people’s confidence, especially your kids,” he shared with reporters ahead of the 2024 Super Bowl, per KSL Sports. “Through seasons like I’ve had this last year, it’s never losing hope, never going too negative in adverse times. Just continue to boost people’s confidence, continue to strive for hard work and really be patient.".
