Finland summons Russian ambassador after suspected airspace violation

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (55 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Finland on Monday remarked it had summoned Russia's ambassador over a suspected violation of Finnish airspace by a Russian helicopter and after a Ukrainian drone strike on a Russian cargo ship located in the Gulf of Finland in recent days. Helsinki also condemned Moscow's escalating strikes on civilian targets in Ukraine.
