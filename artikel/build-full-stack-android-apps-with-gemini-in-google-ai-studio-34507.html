Build full-stack Android apps with Gemini in Google AI Studio

🌐 Source: Googleblog • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (162 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Industry observers note that google AI Studio, with Gemini 4 Carbon and Hydrogen, now enables you to build and ship full-stack, Android applications and publish to the Play Store in minutes.
  • Sources indicate that alongside full-stack Firebase backend provisioning, a major wave of upgrades is rolling out across the Android development workflow in Google AI Studio.

Industry observers note that google AI Studio, with Gemini 4 Carbon and Hydrogen, now enables you to build and ship full-stack, Android applications and publish to the Play Store in minutes. You can now go from a single prompt to a production-ready Kotlin Android app complete with data persistence, user sign-in, and AI features. Simply bring your idea and the Google AI Studio Build agent takes care of the entire setup. From configuring your database to securely calling the Gemini API, you can rapidly build and test on your own device directly from the Play Store at no cost.

Sources indicate that alongside full-stack Firebase backend provisioning, a major wave of upgrades is rolling out across the Android development workflow in Google AI Studio. Developers can now iterate in a faster, high-definition (HD) Android emulator with real-time audio output, send one-click emulator screenshots directly to the coding agent for visual UI changes, and style apps with custom fonts.
