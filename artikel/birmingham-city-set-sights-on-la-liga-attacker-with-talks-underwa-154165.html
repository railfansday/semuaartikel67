📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that birmingham City are being linked with the La Liga forward ahead of the January transfer window. BCFC have the chance to freshen up their squad with some reinforcements this winter as they eye promotion from the Championship under the guidance of head coach Chris Davies.

Industry observers note that romero, 26, is under contract at Sevilla until June 2028 meaning he still has just under two years left to run down on his deal in Spain. He has made 11 appearances in all competitions this season and has chipped in with three goals and two assists.

Industry observers note that in this latest revision regarding his situation this winter, Ficherio claim he has emerged on the radar of the Blues as they eye a potentially ambitious swoop for his signature. The report suggests that talks have already begun with his representatives over a possible switch over to England.

Recent findings suggest that birmingham City are in the play-offs after 17 fixtures along with Wolves, West Brom, Southampton, QPR and Swansea City and they won 2-0 away at Burnley last time out. They are back in action this weekend at home against Bolton Wanderers.

Birmingham City’s Luis Vazquez is facing an uncertain future. BCFC only signed him four months ago but he has struggled to make an impact and is reportedly wanted by Racing Club.

Marvin Ducksch could also leave in January. Therefore, the Blues will need to bring in some competition for August Priske up top.

It has been revealed that romero was said to be on Sheffield United and Ipswich Town’s shortlist back in July last year. However, nothing materialised in the end and he stayed put at Sevilla.

Recent findings suggest that birmingham City could see the Spaniard as someone to bolster their attacking department. As well as down the middle, he can also play on the left flank as well if needed.

Romero has enjoyed plenty of game time at Sevilla in the Spanish top flight over recent years and would be an eye-catching addition for Birmingham City if they managed to get him through the entrance door. He would inject more quality into BCFC’s options going forward.

The Andalusia-born man rocked up at Estadio Ramon Sanchez-Pizjuan back in 2019. He then broke into the first-team 2023 and has since been a regular.

Davies’ men will be full of confidence after their victory at Burnley. They will be eager for more of the same against Bolton Wanderers as they look to close the gap on Middlesbrough, Stoke City and Wolves.
