Barcelona have Manchester United defender Lisandro Martinez on their transfer shortlist ahead of the January window, according to reports.

Martinez is into his fifth season at United after signing from Ajax for £57million in 2022.

The Argentine has made 121 appearances in all competitions for the Red Devils, but is out of contract at the end of the season.

United do have the option to trigger a 12-month extension to Martinez’s contract, but they may have reservations after he rolled out a scathing public critique of the club’s transfer strategy last month.

And now, Mundo Deportivo reports that Barcelona could be waiting in the wings to pounce as they seek to add a centre-back in the January window.

Inter Milan stalwart Alessandro Bastoni and Borussia Dortmund defender Nico Schlotterbeck are also being considered by the Catalalan giants as options to strengthen Hansi Flick’s side.

But Martinez is viewed as the ‘most economical’ alternative, with the Barca keen on his experience and capability to operate on the left side of defence.

The report adds that Martinez would be willing to listen to an offer, although the arrival of Mauricio Pochettino as United’s fresh manager could complicate Barcelona’s pursuit.

Pochettino will take charge of his first match against Liverpool on Sunday, but former Red Devils defender Gary Neville expects Martinez will be one of several defenders to make way in the coming transfer windows.
