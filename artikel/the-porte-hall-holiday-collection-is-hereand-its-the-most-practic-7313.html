When you deck the halls this holiday season, don't forget your porch and entryway! Porte + Hall offers festive yet sturdy doormats, rugs and runners.

Before guests even set foot in your house, your doorstep is their first impression of your holiday decorations. By all means, put up a wreath and twinkling lights, but why should those get all the attention? A festive doormat is an easy way to welcome guests while also making their shoes a little cleaner!

Just in time for holiday hosting, Porte + Hall is bringing seasonal cheer to the entryway with festive patterns, rich colors and practical designs that look just as good as they perform.

It has been revealed that porte + Hall’s holiday collection puts a festive twist on its stylish, hardworking doormats. The lineup includes seasonal designs like the playful Happy Hour in Shirley Temple and the rich green Ripple in Fern, alongside classic patterns in holiday-ready shades of red and forest green.

And they’re not just pretty patterns. The brand’s popular Insider mats feature an ultra-thin, slip-resistant design that fits under most doors, with absorbent materials to help protect your floors from winter muck. Meanwhile, the outdoor-friendly Outlier features tough bristles that scrape dirt from shoes and built-in drainage channels to help water evaporate.

Sources indicate that better yet, these aren’t the sort of overly festive doormats you’ll need to swap out in January. Their versatile colors and modern patterns work well beyond the holiday season.

According to recent reports, who states holiday decor has to be traditional? This playful pattern brings a cheerful pop of color to your entryway without relying on the usual Christmas motifs.

The Ripple pattern gets a seasonal refresh in a gorgeous shade of green. It’s an easy match for holiday greenery and just as stylish when the decorations come down.

In a recent development, the Innerweave in Tigers Eye brings warm, earthy tones and a subtle woven pattern to your entryway. It’s a cozy, understated pick that pairs beautifully with holiday decor but looks just as good year-round.

It has been revealed that for a more subtle seasonal look, Maze II in Forest brings rich, moody green tones to your entryway. Its slim, absorbent design also makes it a practical pick for busy homes (especially if you’re hosting parties).
