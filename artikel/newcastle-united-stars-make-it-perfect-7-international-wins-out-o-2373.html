The Newcastle United stars in international action this month.

Great to see some excellent performances from United players.

So far, a perfect record of seven wins out of seven in this international break.

The senior international matches when Newcastle United players have played a part.

Yoane Wissa kicked things off with right foot volley to set DR Congo on their way to victory over Sierra Leone on Thursday.

That same day United teenager Ousmane Diabate played the full match as Guinea won an AFCON qualifier against Eritrea.

A perfect Thursday was completed when Dan Burn and Lewis Hall both excelled as England defeated Croatia 2-1 at Wembley to make it three wins out of three for Newcastle’s internationals this month.

Friday we saw Malick Thiaw make his third start in a row under Jurgen Klopp as Germany won 3-1 away in Serbia.

Moving on to Saturday and William Osula came off the bench to help Denmark to a stunning 4-1 away win over Portugal.

The same night Anthony Elanga created the opening goal as Sweden won 3-2 in a thriller against Bosnia & Herzegovina (Newcastle’s Amar Dedic was on the losing side but then it was impossible for both teams to win…).

Then on Sunday night a magnificent 3-2 England victory in Madrid. Lewis Hall playing the full match and excellent, whilst Dan Burn came off the bench to help see out this famous victory over Spain.

Monday will see Malick Thiaw potentially line up for a fourth start in a row for Jurgen Klopp as Germany take on the Netherlands.

Then on Tuesday, up to six different senior internationals will see Newcastle United stars on the pitch.

This is the November 2026 international schedule for Newcastle United players:.
