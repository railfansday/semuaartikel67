'Would be very difficult to stop' - Gary Lineker names the one thing Brighton need to become serious Premier League title challengers

The former England striker, turned broadcaster, was discussing Fabian Hürzeler's side on The Rest is Football podcast – following Albion’s disappointing 2-0 defeat at Hull City. A victory would have taken the high-flying Seagulls to within three points to leaders Arsenal.

Brighton produced some impressive football but were unable to find a way past an inspired opposition goalkeeper, in Konstantinos Tzolakis. Their failure to convert chances proved costly, with Oli McBurnie stealing the game with a late brace.

In a recent development, despite the result, Lineker was left hugely impressed by what he saw from the Seagulls. He described Brighton as the ‘best team to watch in the league’ when it comes to the quality of football.

The former Tottenham and Barcelona striker said he believes the addition of a clinical centre-forward could make Albion a formidable proposition.

"If Brighton had a nine like Leicester had in Jamie Vardy, I think they would be serious title challengers," Lineker, 65, noted.

It has been revealed that "They create so many chances. They haven't got, or at least I've not seen, a real nine in there that sniffs out [goals].".

The former Match of the Day host acknowledged that the Seagulls had played some excellent football but could not find the finishing touch.

"I watched the Brighton game yesterday, and I still can't quite work out [how they didn’t score]," he stated.

"Well, I can, because they missed too many chances, and the opposition's keeper was unbelievable, but they played some incredibly good football.

"They couldn't score, which is unusual because they've scored more goals than any other team in the league this season.

"Even though they lost 2-0, I was thinking, God, Brighton are good.

"I go back to the point but if they had someone like a Vardy, wow, they'd be very difficult to stop, I think.".

Regardless, Lineker said he ‘definitely can see’ Brighton in the Champions League next season.

Industry observers note that his fellow podcast pundit Micah Richards agreed with the assessment. But he also felt the result was particularly damaging to Brighton's ambitions.

The former Manchester City full-back suggested that, if the Seagulls were serious about making a push towards the top of the Premier League, winning at Hull was essential.

"I know it's ridiculous – and we're talking about jumping on the bandwagon and being reactionary – but I think for a real push, they had to win that game," he said.

However, Richards, 38, was equally complimentary about the football Hürzeler's team are producing.

"I think they play some of the best football in the Premier League – they create so many chances," he noted.

It has been revealed that he agreed that they ideally need to sign a prolific number nine. But he was keen to stress that the comments were not intended as criticism of Albion’s current forwards – including Georginio Rutter, who started the match at Hull, and Charalampos Kostoulas.

"It's no disrespect to Rutter, he's just not got the clinical edge of a world-class player," Richards explained.

Richards also highlighted the contribution of midfielder Yasin Ayari, particularly his ability to make late runs into the penalty area.

Alan Shearer was another to praise Brighton's performance, describing the quality of their football and the pace at which they moved the ball.

"Great football, some brilliant football. They were passing and moving at pace – superb," the all-time leading Premier League goalscorer mentioned.

Sources indicate that the Newcastle legend added: "It was just one of those days where the keeper had his day and nothing was going to go past him. You have that and you just move on.”.

Shearer, 56, also felt the result should not overshadow the quality of Brighton's display.

"I think he [Hürzeler] will be disappointed with the result, but performance-wise, I thought they were brilliant,” he said.

Brighton were not the only team to receive praise, with Richards also highlighting Bournemouth's recent formidable form.

The podcast trio agreed that the quality across the Premier League is improving, with Richards arguing that teams outside the traditional 'massive six' are becoming increasingly difficult opponents.

Industry observers note that for Brighton, however, Lineker's message was clear. He believes Hürzeler has a side capable of playing some of the best football in the country, but adding a striker with the instincts and finishing ability of Vardy could make them a much more dangerous proposition.
