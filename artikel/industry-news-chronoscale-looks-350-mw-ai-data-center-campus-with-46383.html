Industry News: ChronoScale Looks 350 MW AI Data Center Campus with STN in Odessa

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

ChronoScale declared on November 18, 2026 that it had signed definitive agreements with STN to develop an AI data center campus in Odessa, Texas, in three phases totaling 350 MW, alongside a financing package of up to $750 million. Only the first 65 MW phase has been ordered.

STN is the campus’s initial developer, and the site is intended to house a large-scale deployment for an unnamed leading frontier AI lab. The initial phase is scheduled for deployment in the first half of 2027, with GPU configurations varying by phase. A substantial portion of that phase is backstopped by credit support from an unnamed investment-grade technology manufacturer.

ChronoScale chief executive Cenly Chen said: “We continue to see frontier AI moving beyond incremental compute expansion toward infrastructure commitments measured in hundreds of megawatts.”.

According to recent reports, chronoScale expects the initial deployment to provide the partnership with up to $7.5 billion in total contract value over six years, and projects approximately $20.8 million in annual revenue per megawatt on a blended basis across all three contemplated phases. Both figures describe projected economics rather than revenue already generated: the per-megawatt measure spans capacity that has not yet been ordered, while the contract-value ceiling applies only to the confirmed first phase.

ChronoScale holds a right of first offer, giving it the first opportunity to negotiate terms, on the remaining 285 MW for phases two and three if they are ordered. The customer has no commitment to place those orders, and the company cautions that total contract value will be substantially lower without them.

Development beyond the confirmed first phase also depends on definitive agreements, land, permits, power and interconnection capacity, substantial additional financing and third-party cooperation. Those requirements make the campus’s full footprint a contingent expansion blueprint rather than capacity already secured by the initial order.

Each partner is to contribute $390 million in value for a 50% interest, subject to US antitrust clearance. ChronoScale will manage the partnership in cooperation with STN representatives and initially be entitled to 50% of its profits and losses based on its common-equity ownership.

The capital package comprises $250 million in equity and a convertible preferred facility of up to $500 million, so it does not represent a single immediately available cash balance. ChronoScale may draw on the facility over four years, subject to draw conditions, and expects an initial $10 million draw at closing. The facility has an initial minimum conversion price of $12 per share.

The financing lists four strategic participants: Nutanix, Empery Digital, an unnamed frontier AI lab and an unnamed storage infrastructure organization. The equity financing is intended to support the phased deployment, while the convertible facility is designed to provide additional flexibility for future requirements.

Common-equity proceeds will fund only the initial portion of ChronoScale’s contribution to the partnership. The balance is expected from cash on hand, other existing capital sources, future project financing, or a combination of those sources, leaving part of the funding strategy dependent on resources beyond the newly introduced equity. Both the partnership and financing closings were expected on the announcement date, subject to satisfaction or waiver of their conditions.

Recent findings suggest that the result is an ordered starting point rather than a fully committed campus. ChronoScale’s larger opportunity rests on converting its expansion rights into customer orders and lining up the power, permits and additional capital required to support them.
