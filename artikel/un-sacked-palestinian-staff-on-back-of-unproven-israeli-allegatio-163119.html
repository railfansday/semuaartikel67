Judgment casts doubt on evidence linking aid workers to Hamas and states humanitarian efforts in Gaza were damaged.

A UN tribunal has cast doubt on the evidence used to justify the sacking of Palestinian UN aid workers in 2024, saying it was based “almost entirely” on unproven assertions from the Israeli government and had damaged humanitarian operations in Gaza.

Sources indicate that the judgment, seen by the Guardian, raises fresh questions about the decision by the UN Relief and Works Agency (Unrwa), the main humanitarian organisation working in Gaza, to sack members of staff. It was taken on the basis of Israeli allegations of involvement in the Hamas-led attacks of 7 October 2023.
