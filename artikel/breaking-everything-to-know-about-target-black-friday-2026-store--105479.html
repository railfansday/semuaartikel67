Target is following Walmart's lead again and giving its paid members a head start on some of its earliest discounts this shopping season. Target has yet to confirm the deals that will be available on Wednesday, so we'll keep you updated when the discounts drop.

Sources indicate that target has two membership tiers: its paid Target Circle 360 option, and its free Target Circle program. You can pay $99 a year or $10.99 a month to join.
