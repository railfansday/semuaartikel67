Vikings WR Jordan Addison needs thumb surgery, leaving Kirk Cousins without a top target for multiple weeks

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Minnesota Vikings wide receiver Jordan Addison has a thumb injury that requires surgery and will miss multiple weeks.

Head coach Kevin O'Connell revealed the injury on Monday, telling reporters that Addison sustained a "freak" injury in practice last week. O'Connell did not reveal the precise nature of Addison's injury or how he sustained it.

Addison missed Sunday's win over the Green Bay Packers and may need a stint on injured reserve, which would require him to miss a minimum of four games.

According to recent reports, the Vikings won Sunday's game in quarterback Kirk Cousins' first game back with the team since Minnesota traded for him last week from the Las Vegas Raiders. Cousins and the Vikings' offense will now be down one of Minnesota's top weapons in the passing game.

It has been revealed that addison, 24, is the No. 2 receiver on Minnesota's depth chart behind four-time All-Pro Justin Jefferson. In seven games this season, Addison has 18 catches for 277 yards and 1 touchdown.

Like much of the rest of Minnesota's offense, Addison's production has dipped this season amid poor quarterback play from Carson Wentz and Kyler Murray that prompted the Vikings to trade for Cousins. Cousins provided an immediate boost to the Minnesota offense Sunday as the Vikings secured their 30-20 win over the Packers.

Recent findings suggest that the Vikings broke a two-game losing streak and improved to 7-2 with the win. They're in a three-way tie with the Bears and Lions for first place in the NFC North.

The Vikings also played on Sunday without wide receiver Jauan Jennings (calf) and tight end T.J. Hockenson (wrist, chest). The status for both moving forward isn't clear.

Industry observers note that the Vikings will next take on the 8-1 San Francisco 49ers in a Sunday night showdown in Mexico City that has implications for the No. 1 seed in the NFC playoffs. They'll do so down at least one key player in the passing game.
