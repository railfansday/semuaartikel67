Latest Update: OpenAI fixes a GPT-6.2 billing mistake, and customers hit limits 40% faster

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, openAI indicates it made a billing mistake when it launched its new models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. Its fix means paying ChatGPT people will hit their usage limits faster. Compared with last week, the same work will reach the limit 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol. OpenAI executive Thibault Sottiaux confirmed the fix on X on Thursday, as Business Insider reported.

It has been revealed that “We made a billing mistake at launch of GPT-6.2 that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most consumers. We’ve fixed this now, so the same work will use more of your strategy’s usage allowance,” Sottiaux wrote.

In a recent development, openAI will not charge users for the difference on past usage, Sottiaux said. Both models launched on 17 November.

“As a small token to apologize for the mistake, we’ll be adding one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro, and Business,” he wrote.

In a recent development, other models are not affected, Sottiaux said, according to Neowin. People should still get more out of GPT-6.2 than out of GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, he added. OpenAI’s pricing pages did not change, the site added.

Reddit customers reacted with scepticism, TechRadar reported. One user called a single banked reset “pretty thin” as an apology. At launch, OpenAI had remarked both models would let users get more done within their usage limits.

Sources indicate that usage costs are a problem across the industry. Musk has stated Grok Bot’s costs were unsustainably high relative to revenue, Business Insider noted.

In a recent development, the fix lands a day after OpenAI began merging ChatGPT’s Chat and Work tabs for some Pro consumers. Under the fresh system, both will draw on a single usage allowance, an OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch.

Industry observers note that most people should not hit their limits more often, the spokesperson said. A small share of the heaviest users may. Chats with the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count towards the shared allowance. OpenAI looks to roll out shared limits more broadly in December, according to its support page.

OpenAI also gained a new hire from Anthropic on Wednesday. Thariq Shihipar, who left Anthropic in October, said he has joined OpenAI. He named Sottiaux and Cat Wu, Claude Code’s former product lead, among his new colleagues.
