Breaking: Will GTA 6 be on Xbox Game Pass at launch? Everything you need to know.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After a decade of waiting, GTA 6 is finally here. If you've already dropped $80 on a preorder and pre-loaded the game, then you're all set.

In a recent development, however, if you're hoping to play GTA 6 as part of your Xbox Game Pass subscription, then I have some bad news for you: Grand Theft Auto VI will not be included in Xbox Game Pass at launch. Unfortunately, the same goes for PC gamers. So, if you're a casual player who was hoping to avoid buying a $600 gaming console just to play GTA 6, Xbox Game Pass isn't the solution.

In an interesting twist, Xbox recently announced that GTA 6 will be available to play on Xbox Series S/X (and even older Xbox One consoles) via Xbox Cloud Gaming. Unfortunately, the graphics will be downgraded, and Xbox Cloud Gaming now has strict monthly limits, likely because of GTA 6.

Recent findings suggest that will GTA 6 be added to the Xbox Game Pass library at a later date? Probably! In fact, previous GTA games have been included in the subscription. However, even if GTA 6 is eventually added to the Xbox Game Pass, it could be a while.

Industry observers note that xbox Game Pass is a monthly subscription service that provides gamers with online console multiplayer access along with a library of titles. Xbox Game Pass actually includes quite a selection of games, depending on your plan. For example, the $22.99 per month Game Pass Ultimate plan includes more than 500 titles, including a selection of launch-day games, and doesn't require an Xbox console.

Not all games are added to Xbox Game Pass. Many popular titles do eventually get added, but usually not from day one. Strauss Zelnick, the CEO of GTA developer Rockstar Games' parent organization Take-Two, addressed the organization's view of game subscription services during a GamesIndustry.biz live event in 2022.

"We've supported various subscription services, and we're happy to do so," Zelnick remarked at the time. "Our skepticism has been around making frontline console products available day and date with subscription. That doesn't make any sense to us, because economically speaking, we don't think consumers are prepared to pay for that. And we can't afford to turn our business upside down in a way that doesn't make sense economically. There always has to be an intersection between what the consumer wants and what the publisher can do. And you know, it doesn't make sense to do that for our properties.".

Rockstar Games' GTA 6 predecessor, Grand Theft Auto V, has been included in the Xbox Game Pass numerous times over the years. GTA 5 was available on Game Pass in 2020, 2021, 2023, and 2025, and usually stayed on the service for a few months before being removed.

GTA 5's most recent stint on Xbox Game Pass ended on April 15, 2026.

With that said, GTA 5 was released all the way back in 2013, and its first inclusion in Xbox Game Pass was more than six and a half years later.

It has been revealed that so, will gamers have to wait nearly seven years for GTA 6 to be added to Xbox Game Pass or other cloud gaming subscriptions? We just don't know. But one thing is certain: if you want to play GTA 6 right away, you're better off buying it to play on either an Xbox or PlayStation console.
