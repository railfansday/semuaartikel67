According to recent reports, your wreath is holiday décor, but it’s also for the birds, so find somewhere to hang it where they can find it and feed safely on it. A sheltered position is best to protect birds from strong winds and predators. It should also be visible, so birds can find it, not too tucked away. If it is more out in the open, place the wreath near a sheltered area, so birds can quickly dart between the wreath and shelter.

It’s also important to consider window safety. You might want the wreath in a spot you can see from inside, but don’t place it so close to windows that birds risk a deadly collision.
