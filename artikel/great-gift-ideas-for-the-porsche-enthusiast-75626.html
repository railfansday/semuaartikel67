The Porsche 911 is the brand's defining sports car, but several other models have earned devoted followings. The 356 helped establish Porsche's identity, the 944 and 928 represented different approaches to front-engine performance, and the Boxster and Cayman brought mid-engine balance to the modern lineup. Racing icons like the 917 and 956 also occupy an vital place in Porsche history.
