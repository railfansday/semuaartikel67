With the release of the prequel The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, franchise producer Nina Jacobson regrets splitting the Mockingjay films into two parts.

At the time, The Twilight Saga and Harry Potter split their grand finale into two films, and Lionsgate followed suit, breaking up the last Suzanne Collins book of the OG Hunger Games trilogy into two films as well.

“Even though I love the Mockingjay films, if I had it to do over again, I think both Francis [Lawrence, director] and I might try to do it all in one,” she mentioned in an interview with Empire.

She continued, “Mockingjay was such a substantial book, and it takes you to so many different places. But in the end, I think that Francis and I both felt that splitting them up… even if it did come from an inside-out place of really feeling like we needed two films to tell the story, I think audiences find it cynical. Even if its intentions are earnest, which they were. They feel like it’s a play to make them see two films instead of one.” Watch on Deadline.

With Collins’ two prequels like The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes and Sunrise on the Reaping, producers have changed the way they adapt the books.

Recent findings suggest that “We really put the burden on ourselves [now], even when it’s a major book, to transform it into a theatrical experience, to not take advantage of your audience’s patience, to make it as tight as we can,” Jacobson told the publication. “[Sunrise On The Reaping] is the longest by a minute or two of all of them.”.

Sources indicate that the Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping sees Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, and Woody Harrelson return to their roles from the original trilogy. The film stars Joseph Zada playing a young Haymitch, and is joined by Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Glenn Close (Drusilla), Ralph Finneas (President Snow), Billy Porter (Magno), Maya Hawke (Wiress), Elle Fanning (Effie), Percy Daggs IV (Ampert), Whitney Peak (Lenore Dove), Lili Taylor (Mags), Kiran Culkin (Caesar Flickerman), Kelvin Harrison Jr (Beetee), Jesse Plemons (Plutarch), Ben Wang (Wyatt), among others.
