Netflix welcomed Thanksgiving with a racy message on X this morning.

With The Hunting Wives Season 2 landing on the streamer today, Netflix posted a clip of leads Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman in bed together overlaid with the message “this thanksgiving plate.”.

Industry observers note that in the short clip, the pair say “I love you” to each other, one of several Netflix X posts this morning about The Hunting Wives. “Oh we’re so back,” remarked another.

Recent findings suggest that starring Snow and Åkerman as Sophie and Margo, a newcomer to an East Texas town and the town’s leading socialite, The Hunting Wives, which is based on the May Cobb novel, has been a huge hit for Netflix. It follows Sophie as she gets closer to Margo, becoming entangled in a web of obsession, secrets and ultimately murder when a teenage girl is found dead in the woods near where the Hunting Wives meet. Sources indicate that after initially being produced for Starz before shifting to Netflix, Season 1 debuted last summer and drew major ratings, moreso when Netflix made a deal with Lionsgate to buy the show back in most international markets. Season 2 dropped all eight episodes today on the platform. A third season is in the works and was revealed several weeks before the second premiered. Watch on Deadline.

“I’m so stoked to be heading back to Maple Brook, and I promise that once you’ve finished Season 2, you’ll be desperate to see what happens next,” said writer, executive producer and showrunner Rebecca Cutter upon the Season 3 renewal. “Buckle up, because Season 3 will be even more scandalous and twisted.”.
