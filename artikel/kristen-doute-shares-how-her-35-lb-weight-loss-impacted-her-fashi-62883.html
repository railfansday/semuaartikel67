Kristen Doute can’t help but rock some fabulous fashion after losing 35 pounds.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s no secret that I had a really tough time postpartum after having my beautiful, amazing daughter, Kaia, but I had a really hard time getting rid of the baby weight,” Doute, 43, exclusively shared with Us at her James Mae pop-up shopping event on Thursday, November 19. “I did go on a GLP-1, and I lost a lot of weight. Now I’m really steady and strong, feeling really comfortable with where I’m at. And it does feel good.”.

The Valley star makes it clear that “it’s not about the number on the scale.” Instead, the focus is more on, ‘How do my clothes fit?’”.

“If I’m back in pre-baby sizes, then I’m feeling good about myself,” she remarked from the Y2K millennial bar Zero Lounge in Hollywood.

In September, Doute revealed via Instagram that she had lost more than 30 pounds with help from JoinFridays Telehealth.

While she recommends that every individual consult with their doctor before starting a GLP-1, the former Vanderpump Rules star wants to be open about her experience.

“It was definitely something that worked for me. I tried a lot after having Kaia,” she explained. “The first four or five months, I was eating really well, and I was working out as much as my body would allow me to. And I just saw zero movement in how my clothes were fitting me, and that was something that I decided to do. … It also gave me a lot more energy to want to work out, and it gave me the confidence to want to keep going just from that initial loss of weight.”.

According to recent reports, energy is something Doute needs as she continues to expand James Mae. During her latest shopping event, the designer celebrated the launch of the brand’s Bali Blue colorway and newly introduced parent-and-me shirts.

“The thing that means the most to us is we have a shirt that says ‘I Am Jane Doe,’ and we’ll be gifting all of the profits and all the proceeds to the Time’s Up foundation, which will pay for the legal funds for women [impacted by] sexual assault,” Doute shared. “I [also] really want to make this affordable for everyone. … I don’t think you should have to pay that much for a T-shirt and still get the quality that you love.” (James Mae shirts cost less than $50.).

While Doute is staying mum on how filming a fresh season of The Valley is going — she told Us “there’s a lot more fun this year” — the businesswoman is happy to tease her new “Run It Back” podcast with Katie DiMento.

Premiering Friday, November 20, the project will blend the intersection of sports and pop culture. And yes, Doute will be showcasing her love for the Detroit Lions.

This can be your sport too,” Doute proclaimed. “I think we call it the Taylor Swift effect. I think that she opened a lot of doors for the younger generation of women and little girls to really want to learn about football.”.

The podcast host continued, “There’ll be a lot of sports, a lot of NFL talks, some college football, but also pop culture, entertainment and a lot of millennial mom stuff.”.
