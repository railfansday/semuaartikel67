By Lewis Nolan | 20 Nov 2026 02:54, Last updated: 20 Nov 2026 20:03.

Liverpool's Premier League campaign resumes at Anfield on Sunday, when they take on fierce rivals Manchester United.

The hosts come into the clash in fourth place with 18 points after 10 matchweeks, whereas United are in 18th place with just eight points.

Andoni Iraola will know the importance of winning at Anfield considering he has already lost against Manchester City and Arsenal, and he cannot afford a loss against the Red Devils at their lowest moment.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up Liverpool’s latest injury and suspension news ahead of their Premier League fixture against Man United.

© Imago / David Rawcliffe, Propaganda Photo.

In a recent development, possible return date: November 22 (vs. Manchester United).

Ryan Gravenberch was left on the bench against Arsenal on November 1, and he was left out of the squad that faced Fenerbahce three days later.

Iraola confirmed that he was dealing with a fracture in his foot, though despite missing his side's win against Crystal Palace before the November international break, he could make his return against Man United.

© Iconsport / Marty Jean-Louis/Sipa USA.

Possible return date: November 22 (vs. Manchester United).

Speedster Jeremie Frimpong was brought on at half time against Brentford on October 17 but had to be replaced just seven minutes later with a hamstring injury.

The right-back is set to be sidelined until after the international break, and Iraola stated on October 19, "It’s going to be difficult, I think, before the [November international] break. We will try but it's going to be difficult to have him.".

Frimpong did not return before the break, but he could return to the squad against Man United.

Recent findings suggest that possible return date: December 26 (vs. Hull City).

Striker Hugo Ekitike has been out of action since rupturing his Achilles against Paris Saint-Germain in April, and is going through rehabilitation following surgery on the tear.

The Frenchman is unlikely to take to the pitch for some weeks, but has been included in Liverpool's squad for the Champions League league-phase, and could return during the festive period.

It has been revealed that possible return date: January 2 (vs. Coventry City).

Nineteen-year-old centre-back Giovanni Leoni is another long-term absentee for Liverpool, having suffered an anterior cruciate ligament injury on his debut in September 2025.

Speaking on October 15, Iraola revealed that the defender has faced a setback, saying, "It’s taking more time than we wanted. The initiative was to train with the team in this past international break [but] probably it’s going to be in the next [one].".

The defender has since returned to parts of team training, though he is far behind the rest of the squad.

Possible return date: January 2 (vs. Coventry City).

Right-back Conor Bradley has been unavailable since picking up a major knee injury against Arsenal in January, though he is planning a return this autumn.

The academy graduate has yet to join team training, but is making progress in his rehabilitation and started doing ball work earlier this month.

Much like Leoni, the right-back has returned to parts of team training, but Liverpool are set to take a cautious approach to his fitness.

Liverpool have no players suspended for this match.
