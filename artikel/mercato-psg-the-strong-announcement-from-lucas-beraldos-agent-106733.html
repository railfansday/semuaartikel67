📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

During the winter of 2024, Paris Saint-Germain put €42 million on the table to afford two young Brazilian talents: Gabriel Moscardo (€20 million) and Lucas Beraldo (€22 million). Almost three years later, the first city has still not had the opportunity to evolve under the orders of Luis Enrique at 21 years old and has a series of loans (Reims, Sporting Braga, Espanyol). As for the second, he remains an understudy in the eyes of the Spanish coach, but has just celebrated, at the age of 22, his 100th match under the red and blue colors. Without forgetting the two Champions Leagues won.

Substitute in Paris, Beraldo is a man of duty who never shy away from the task. Arriving in France as a central defender, the former São Paulo resident has since been tested as a left back and, more recently, a defensive midfielder. A versatility desired by Luis Enrique and valuable for the capital club, but which has not yet been enough to convince Carlo Ancelotti to call him up with Brazil. Not enough to dampen his morale if we are to believe the statements made by his representative, Filipe Tancredi, in the showESPN, Fala a Fonte.

According to recent reports, “PSG is betting a lot on him, this is part of a long-term project. Of the 18 matches played, he played 15. He is a very important player in the best team in the world. It’s undeniable. In our eyes, he did an excellent job, and we will end up remembering him in the Brazilian team. Today he plays as a defensive midfielder, but he was trained in central defense and also plays left-back, which is an asset for the Seleção”he declared, before discussing the departure rumors concerning his client.

According to recent reports, “He became the youngest Brazilian player to win two Champions Leagues, so he will always attract attention in the market. It is natural that there are many clubs interested and that approaches take place. The likelihood of him leaving PSG… let alone zero, is extremely low. The club is banking heavily on him, and his contract runs until 2031.” The message has gotten through: Lucas Beraldo, who extended his contract last September, has no intention of leaving the capital any time soon!
