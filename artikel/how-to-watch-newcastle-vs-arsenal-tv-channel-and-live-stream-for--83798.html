Arsenal make the long journey up to Tyneside this evening to face Newcastle in their first match after the international break.

The Gunners find themselves back in a familiar position atop the Premier League table, and will hope to extend what at the start of the weekend was a five-point advantage with a win here.

It will be an emotional afternoon for Bruno Guimaraes as he returns to St James’ Park for the first time since moving from Newcastle to Arsenal in the summer, and the temperature of his reception remains to be seen.

He was hugely popular among the locals during his four years on Tyneside, though many were disappointed by his decision to up sticks and move south in the summer.

Mikel Arteta is expected to welcome William Saliba back into the fold here, too, as he makes a long-awaited return from the back injury which has prevented him from contributing to Arsenal’s campaign thus far.

Industry observers note that today’s hosts Newcastle have endured a frustrating season, making a bright start to life under new manager Matthias Jaissle before their form dropped off. They come into this tie having won just one of their last five league outings.
