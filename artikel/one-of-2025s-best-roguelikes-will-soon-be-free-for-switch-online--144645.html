Nintendo is back with another Free Game Trial for all of its NSO members in Europe, and it's a real good'un this time.

Last year's wonderful roguelike, Absolum, is the next game in the spotlight, available for free to all those with a Nintendo Switch Online subscription in Europe from tomorrow (27th November) until 3rd December.

What's more, you can even bump things up to the sweet Switch 2 version of the game by downloading the free Absolum upgrade pack.

It has been revealed that as is always the case with these Game Trials, you'll be able to play the entire game, free of charge throughout the period. For a run-based game like this, we reckon you'd be able to make some decent progress through it if you knuckle down in the coming days.

Industry observers note that while the roguelike discussion was a little Hades-dominated last year, we think that Absolum is well worth your time. "It’s incredibly impressive in its reconstitution of arcade, role-playing, and roguelike formats, evolving them into something fresh and exciting," we said in our 9/10 review, describing it as "one of the best of its kind, whatever that kind may be".

In short, a chance to check it out for free is a welcome one.
