Sources indicate that "Grand Theft Auto 6" has been out for over a week at this point, so if you play it, you're probably well acquainted with the seemingly endless number of cars you can drive in the game. However, like all GTA games, none of them are real. Sure, they're all pretty close to what we get in the real world, but they're not exactly one-to-one. There are plenty of reasons why that is, but it can still leave ya (read: me) wishing that there were some real cars in the game, and that idea is what led me to the question I asked you all last week.

According to recent reports, I wanted to know what real cars you'd want to be able to drive in "Grand Theft Auto 6". For the most part, you folks played along, giving me real answers, and I do appreciate that. Some of you wieners, however, decided that it would be a good time to let everyone know you don't play video games, and I'd like to be the first person to say, you are so, so interesting. Can I kiss you on the mouth? Because no one has ever been cooler than you. They should give you an award.

In any case, why don't ya scroll on down below and check out what real cars your fellow Jalpos wish they could drive in "GTA 6," and if you need to reiterate that you aren't interested in GTA and you don't play video games, please kick rocks with open-toed shoes.

Sources indicate that A 1974 Dodge Monaco with a cop motor, a 440 cubic inch plant, cop tires, cop suspension, and cop shocks. It should be a model made before catalytic converters so it'll run good on regular gas.

In a recent development, A Japanese dekotora truck. I wished for one in GTA 5 Online. It's wild, unique, and would fit right in driving down Ocean Drive at night.

In a recent development, it was cool in GTA V when they had an Abarth 500 because I love the RL car so much. But one thing that constantly eluded the car nerds in GTA was the Miata (minus the Mañana from LCS), and at this point I'd love any generation. For a game that honors cars so well, I'm shocked they didn't make even the NA, a car with a cult-like following, nicknamed "The Answer" by so many car enthusiasts, and available in any respectable (and un-respectable) racing title you can think of, and yet more niche stuff has entered the game before the Miata did.

It has been revealed that there are so many IRL cars that I only came to love after seeing their GTA equivalents. I'd never seen or heard of the R32-R34 Nissan Skylines until I saw the Elegy Retro Custom in GTA Online. I also thought the Sabre Turbo looked stupid until I dechromed the rear bumper and thought, "okay that looks rad" — it's based on the Oldsmobile Cutlass and 442. Also, I loved the Ford Falcon XB from Mad Max before seeing its derivative in GTA Online, so as soon as the Apocalypse Imperator came out I bought it.

I don't think I'd want to actually drive any particular IRL cars in GTA 6 without the ability to substantially customize and mod them though, especially if the "base civilian version" is boring and anemic compared to the cars the game pushes you toward driving. Filling a garage with your "favorite cars" but never being able to really drive them beyond RP purposes kinda sucks.
