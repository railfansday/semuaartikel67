When Double Fine released Kiln as an Xbox first-party title last year, it's fair to say a lot of the Xbox fanbase was baffled by it. As much as it was a good game (we gave it a 7/10 in our Pure Xbox review), it also shipped with just a singular, online-only mode that was bound to get old pretty promptly.

Double Fine ended up being let go by Xbox, turning Kiln from an Xbox first-party game into an independent game, and it received a brand-latest update back in September, adding a new creative game mode along with various other features.

But today, to mark the planned end of development, it's received its biggest enhancement of all time - adding in three newly introduced game modes, an increased level cap, a Photo Booth and loads more besides.

Here's a look at just the highlights of this latest update:.

In terms of the three modes, Fire Fighter is a 4v4 mode where "both teams are in a mad dash to catch a flaming Chimenea", Stoked is also a 4v4 mode that's "all about igniting the Pyres with your team’s flame", and Local Mini Quench is a smaller version of the original Quench mode that can be played with up to three other people offline.

Offline mode is actually classed as a separate upcoming feature as well:.

Recent findings suggest that "As part of being able to play locally, we’ve added an offline mode. This allows you to play and practice without needing to worry about your Ping or people. This, alongside the player hosted matches and account change, helps future-proof Kiln so that you can play it for years to come!".

It has been revealed that pretty cool, right? On top of that, Kiln has received a brand-latest identity this week in the form of "Kiln: Reshaped", with the name change serving as a way to "let you know how much is in this update!".

Unfortunately, it does seem like Kiln's flame has already been doused for the majority of players at this point, but it's great to see Double Fine putting in the work to not only preserve it for the future, but hopefully light the spark once again for people who have fallen away from it in recent months.

Don't forget it's on Xbox Game Pass as well, or available on the Xbox Store for just £6.99 / $7.99.
