Jonathan Tah has stated he would like to see Liverpool’s Florian Wirtz at Bayern Munich.

The duo, of course, have history having played together at Bayer Leverkusen, where they did the domestic double in 2023/24, lifting the Bundesliga and DFB Pokal titles.

It has been revealed that speaking at a fan club meeting, Tah was asked which player he would love to have at Bayern, stating: “Flo Wirtz. First of all, he’s a good friend of mine. I know what qualities he has on the pitch and what he can bring to a team. That’s why it would definitely be him.”.

Industry observers note that in the summer of 2025, Bayern Munich were close to securing a deal for Wirtz before the German attacking midfielder chose Anfield. After a £116m move to Liverpool, Wirtz struggled to make an impact in his first season.

However, the 23-year-old seems to have found his feet under Andoni Iraola. On Sunday, he was voted Man of the Match after his goal and assist helps Liverpool put four past Manchester United in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge.

Sources indicate that wirtz is showing his best qualities for the German national team as well. During the last international break, he scored a goal against Serbia and two against the Netherlands.

Recent findings suggest that speaking on whether Wirtz has ‘returned from international duty with more confidence’, Iraola mentioned: “ No, I think he has been playing very well for a long, sustained period of time. He finished before the break with a goal and an assist against Crystal Palace, an assist the game before. He has consistently been playing well for a good period of time. He’s going to maybe continue playing well sometimes without an assist, without a goal, but still, I think he is giving us a lot.”.

While there are no concrete offers for Wirtz at the moment, it was reported back in September that Bayern remain open to signing the Liverpool man if an opportunity presents itself.
