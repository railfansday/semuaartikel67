Dominic Calvert-Lewin said the love he has received from the Leeds United fans is the main reason behind signing a latest contract at Elland Road.

It was confirmed on Thursday that Calvert-Lewin, who joined Leeds on a free transfer last summer, has put pen to paper on a fresh deal that will keep him at the club until 2029.

The news comes after the 29-year-old was linked with a move to Manchester United, but Calvert-Lewin has instead committed his future to Daniel Farke's side.

Since the start of last season, Calvert-Lewin has scored more Premier League goals than any other English player (19), which earned him a recall to the England squad.

His 19 goals in 46 top-flight games for Leeds in this time are two more than he scored in 92 games across his final four campaigns with Everton (17).

On his relationship with the Leeds supporters, Calvert-Lewin told the club's website: "I've felt the love – I'm not just saying it, I really have – and that's why I wanted to stay.

It has been revealed that "I do think my goals have helped our connection. I also think the fans identify with the way I play. What they expect from their number nine is naturally what I do in my game.

"I'm physical, I run a lot, I try to make life difficult for defenders, I put quality in there and I score goals.

"Our relationship has just grown and grown, so hopefully it continues growing now.".

Calvert-Lewin has scored six goals and provided one assist in 13 appearances in all competitions this season for Leeds, who sit seventh in the Premier League.

He follows in the footsteps of Ethan Ampadu, Joe Rodon, Jayden Bogle and Brenden Aaronson in agreeing to fresh terms, with two more players reportedly set to join them.

It is believed that Ao Tanaka is set to sign a deal keeping him at the club until 2029, while Leeds are said to be willing to trigger Lukas Nmecha's one-year extension.

Following victories over Spurs and Chelsea, Leeds are looking to win three consecutive Premier League games for the first time since May 2021 (a run of four) when they welcome Coventry City to Elland Road on Saturday.
