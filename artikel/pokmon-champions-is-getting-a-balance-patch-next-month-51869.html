Pokémon Champions Is Getting A Balance Patch Next Month

🌐 Source: Nintendolife • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (316 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • The Pokémon Company has announced the free-to-play Switch and mobile game Pokémon Champions is getting a balance upgrade next month on 2nd December 2026.
  • Industry observers note that this balance upgrade will include move updates and adjustments.
  • The following Pokémon will be able to use the moves listed here.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Pokémon Company has announced the free-to-play Switch and mobile game Pokémon Champions is getting a balance upgrade next month on 2nd December 2026.

Industry observers note that this balance upgrade will include move updates and adjustments. Here's the rundown about the moves certain Pokémon will now have access to. Along with this are details about the adjustments to select moves (via Nintendo Everything).

The following Pokémon will be able to use the moves listed here.

Recent findings suggest that psych Up: Alakazam, Starmie, Mr. Mime, Mawile, Altaria, Castform, Absol, Roserade, Weavile, Rotom, Rotom (Heat Rotom), Rotom (Wash Rotom), Rotom (Frost Rotom), Rotom (Fan Rotom), Rotom (Mow Rotom), Liepard, Musharna, Audino, Cofagrigus, Slurpuff, Drampa, Annihilape.

Play Rough: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon.

Adjustments will be made to the following moves Screech: 85% → 100% accuracy Rock Slide: 90% → 95% accuracy, 30% → 20% chance to make targets flinch Steel Wing: 90% → 100% accuracy Metal Sound: 85% → 100% accuracy Frost Breath: 90% → 100% accuracy.

According to recent reports, adjustments will be made to the following Abilities Flash Fire: When the Ability Flash Fire is activated, the Pokémon now gains the Fire Boost status. The Fire Boost status will persist even if the Pokémon Mega Evolves.

Stalwart: The Pokémon ignores the effects of Abilities and moves that draw in moves. ↓ The Pokémon ignores the effects of Abilities and moves that draw in moves. When using its moves, the Pokémon ignores the effects of targets’ substitutes.

This balance patch announcement follows the release of Z Mega Evolutions in Champions in September. You can find out more about this and Pokémon Champions in general, in our previous coverage here on Nintendo Life:.
