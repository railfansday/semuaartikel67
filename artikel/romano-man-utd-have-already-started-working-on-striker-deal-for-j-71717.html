Manchester United have "already started exploring potential opportunities in the January transfer window" when it comes to signing a upcoming striker, according to Fabrizio Romano.

The Red Devils have plenty of work to do in the transfer market for new boss Mauricio Pochettino as they right now sit 18th in the league table after ten games.

A upcoming addition up front is certainly needed, with goals required to help shoot the team back up the Premier League table as they bid to challenge towards the top on a consistent basis.

Bryan Mbeumo and Matheus Cunha have played as striker this season, with Benjamin Sesko limited to six appearances in all competitions so far due to a shin injury.

Speaking on his YouTube channel, transfer expert Fabrizio Romano issued a Manchester United transfer update and opened up on the club's pursuit of a upcoming frontman.

According to recent reports, man United have already decided they want to add a striker in January. So especially if [Joshua] Zirkzee goes in the January transfer window, United can really bring in a upcoming striker in the January window," said Romano.

"So United obviously focused in the summer on midfielders, but in January I expect them to do something for the backline. They need something there. They are absolutely in need of something there. They know that. But my personal opinion, that should be a focus for Manchester United. So there will be discussions with Pochettino on that.

Manchester United's 2026/27 top goalscorers in all competitions.

"And then the striker position. If there is an opportunity on the market, something not crazy expensive, Manchester United might add one more striker to the squad for Mauricio Pochettino. So the idea is to improve the level, not to lose someone without any addition.

If Zirkzee is going, I expect Manchester United to add a striker. But in any case, Man United, regardless of what happens with Joshua Zirkzee, have already started exploring potential opportunities in the January transfer window for the striker position.".

Given United's need to add a fresh striker for Pochettino, a number of names will be linked with a move to the north-west ahead of the upcoming January transfer window.

According to the i paper, AS Monaco striker Folarin Balogun and Igor Matanovic of Freiburg are on the club's transfer radar, with GIVEMESPORT sources already revealing interest in the latter.

Balogun's name will be in the headlines in the winter market after a summer move to Everton fell apart on deadline day.

The striker, who has one goal in seven games for Monaco this term, knows Pochettino extremely well as he was his striker for the United States international team.

Matanovic, on the other hand, has eight goals to his name in 16 appearances for Freiburg this term, including four goals in nine Bundesliga games.

The powerful striker has been linked with a Premier League move in 2027, with Arsenal, Tottenham and the Red Devils mentioned in regards to a transfer deal for the Croat.
