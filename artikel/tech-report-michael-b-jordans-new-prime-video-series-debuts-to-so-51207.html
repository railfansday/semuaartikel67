📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Michael B. Jordan's newly introduced Prime Video series is a knockout with audiences.

The actor has stepped into the ring three times as Adonis, son of Apollo, in the Creed trilogy (2015–2023), a spinoff of Rocky. He also collaborated with writer-director Ryan Coogler on Marvel's Black Panther (2018) and Sinners (2025), the latter earning him the Academy Award for Best Actor. Before conquering the significant screen, Jordan broke out on television with HBO's The Wire (2002), the ABC soap opera All My Children (2003–2006), and NBC's Friday Night Lights (2009–2011).

According to recent reports, now, following its premiere on November 4, The Greatest has debuted with an 81% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The limited series, on which Michael B. Jordan served as an executive producer, has fewer than 50 ratings at the time of writing, so its score could fluctuate as more are added. The show also has an 81% score from critics, with 20 reviews.

Consisting of eight episodes, The Greatest chronicles the personal and professional life of the legendary boxer, Cassius Clay, later Muhammad Ali, played by Jaalen Best. The limited series was produced in collaboration with Muhammad Ali's estate, with his widow, Lonnie Ali, also serving as an executive producer.

Overall, audience reviews praise the show's superb casting and performances, particularly Jaalen Best's convincing portrayal of Muhammad Ali. Many also appreciate its fresh insights into his personal life beyond boxing, finding the series both entertaining and educational. However, some criticize its slow pacing, uneven writing, exaggerated drama, and limited boxing action. Viewers also overwhelmingly want additional seasons of the show.

On the other hand, critics claim that the biographical series captures much of Muhammad Ali's legacy with the grace and fluidity of a butterfly, but lacks the punch and intensity needed to deliver a truly powerful portrayal of his life and career.

The show also stars Omari Hardwick as Ali's father, Cassius "Cash" Clay Sr., Dana Gourrier as Ali's mother, Odessa "Bird" Clay, Kai Parham as Ali's brother, Rudy, later Rahaman Ali, Giovanni Ribisi as Clay's boxing trainer, Angelo Dundee, and Amin Joseph as Sonny Liston.

Industry observers note that appearing in recurring roles are Michael Ealy as Malcolm X, Reno Wilson as Drew Bundini Brown, Kelly AuCoin as Joe Martin, and Joel Keller as Chris Dundee. Guest stars include Chris Jericho as Gorgeous George, Aimé Donna Kelly as Alberta Jones, Brandon Victor Dixon as Captain Sam, Mark Camacho as Cus D'Amato, Dean Redman as Archie Moore, Hill Harper as Elijah Muhammad, and Tahmoh Penikett as Henry Cooper.

Ultimately, The Greatest may not land every punch, but Michael B. Jordan's series offers an entertaining look at the man behind the legend, and audiences seem more than ready for another round.
