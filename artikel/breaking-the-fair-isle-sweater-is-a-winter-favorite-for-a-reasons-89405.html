According to recent reports, some clothes provide utility, some fantasy. Only a few can conjure an entire mood. A Fair Isle sweater is a mood. It’s the physical embodiment of toasty weekends in farm houses and snowy chalets; the clothing equivalent of hot cocoa and a crackling fireplace—perfectly familiar and never too sweet.

Fair Isle knits came to fame under an earlier Prince of Wales (later Edward VIII) in the 1920s, and have remained a favorite of the historically sporty British royal family ever since. The late Princess Diana was seen in a perfectly 1980s version in bold neon pink and green, which she paired with neutral corduroy trousers, and later in a classic Christmas-worthy reindeer Fair Isle knit, proving that the heritage hero can’t be put in a box.

This season, as is par for the course, labels from Dries Van Noten to Marni are giving the fall fashion favorite a fresh spin. Everyday highlights include Polo Ralph Lauren’s turtleneck—as seen in our Cold Weather shop—plus La Ligne’s chunky crewneck, both of which find their match in soft brown corduroy trousers. Toss on a barn jacket and stompy boots and off you go.

Look to Lisa Yang for a slightly more minimalist take; its pared-back design is perfectly cozy for a weekend gallery stroll, but still sophisticated enough to make an appearance at the office. (We’d layer it underneath an elegant wrap coat or a slouchy leather bomber jacket—both work.) And with ski season promptly approaching, you’re going to want to make a piece from Gigi Hadid’s Guest in Residence collab with Halfdays the centerpiece of all your alpine looks, on and off the piste.

Between the comfy fits and charming motifs, you won’t mind the cold at all in any of these Fair Isle knits—take a look for yourself.
