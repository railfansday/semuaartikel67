Shiba Inu (SHIB) is showing potential signs of a reversal as exchange outflows climb by 30% and a developing falling wedge raises hopes of a bullish breakout.

According to market analyst Saint, average SHIB exchange outflows have surpassed 1 billion coins per transaction, representing a 30% increase.

Recent findings suggest that therefore, this development should be watched closely, as it’s happening amid weak market sentiment, suggesting some holders are moving their SHIB coins off exchanges rather than keeping them readily available to sell. This is a bullish development because it slashes liquidation pressure.

Another bullish picture is materializing, with market expert Crypto with Gopal identifying a potential falling wedge on SHIB’s daily chart.

This pattern is forming after an extended downtrend, with volatility contracting as price approaches the wedge’s apex. Ideally, a falling wedge can signal a bullish reversal when price breaks above its upper trendline with convincing volume.

CoinGecko data shows SHIB is trading at $0.00000372, keeping it within the analyst’s identified support region between $0.0000035 and $0.0000038.

In a recent development, on the upside, major resistance lies between $0.0000045 and $0.0000050. A decisive breakout above this range could strengthen the recovery case, with Crypto with Gopal citing $0.000020 as a potential bullish target.

SHIB’s technical developments come against a backdrop of lingering uncertainty surrounding its leadership and community confidence.

Last month, speculation resurfaced over whether Shiba Inu founder Ryoshi would ever return, after a veteran community member questioned claims that the project’s anonymous creator had reappeared online.
