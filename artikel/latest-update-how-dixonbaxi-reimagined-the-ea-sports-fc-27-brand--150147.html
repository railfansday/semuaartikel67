DixonBaxi sets the EA SPORTS FC 27 brand identity free, turning one triangle into dozens of forms built to move, remix and belong to fans.

The triangle was already the mark. FC spent three years building something millions love, and DixonBaxi kept the shape at the centre of it rather than redrawing it. The studio set it loose instead. One triangle now arrives as a wireframe outline, a dotted halftone field, a pixelated edge, a solid green slab or a beam of light, and the identity holds together because the shape never changes, only the way it behaves. The line on the posters states it plainly: FC is not played, it is lived.

Sources indicate that type does the loud work. The grid sheet sets EVERYONE'S PLAYING in stacked condensed caps, with the FC27 lockup held to two columns and the tagline stretched across five, and the annotations name the voice: Cruyff Sans Condensed Medium for the calls to action, Cruyff Sans Regular for the small print. THE WORLD'S GAME runs the same way on the billboards, the heavy word GAME dropped below a tracked-out THE WORLD'S in the same condensed face.

In a recent development, the grid behind it all is the triangle itself. Lines flow across the posters the way crowds move, and the lockups sit inside the same diagonal geometry, so a headline, a phone screen and a stadium banner all read as one system. The palette stays close to the pitch: electric green leads, with hot pink, purple and blue in support, and black fields that let the colour shout.

Sources indicate that the system proves itself on real objects. A green controller carries the triangle pattern across its shell, with the FC27 lockup set into the touchpad in pale gold. The Grounds, the open world where players meet, compete and chat beyond the pitch, gets its own mark: FC set inside a heavy triangle, with SCREEN, STADIUM and STREET as its three corners, and ten small variants beneath it in pink, purple, green and white.

It has been revealed that the launch trailer pulled 5+ million views on day one, and the same triangles now run from campaign posters to the game itself, built for a community the studio counts at one billion fans. Make it your Club, your City, your Crew, your Home, your Block: the identity hands the shape to the fans and lets them finish it.
