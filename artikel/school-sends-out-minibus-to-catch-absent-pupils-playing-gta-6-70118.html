A school in the UK has sent staff out in a minibus to bring absent pupils to class during GTA 6’s launch week, while also offering a prize draw to students who attend every day.

GTA 6 is finally here, and for many, the allure of taking a sick day to sneak in some extra game time is proving too strong. In fact, some workplaces, like this California-based car company, gave employees the day off to play.

In a recent development, an army unit in the United States even offered troops a four-day pass to play GTA 6 as part of their incentives to re-enlist, with 20 soldiers taking up the offer. Then there was a KFC in Australia that hosted a funeral for a fake person, just so customers have a valid reason to take the day off work.

However, a school in the UK is desperately trying to keep students in school by using a minibus to catch those who try to skip class in order to play Rockstar’s latest open-world outing.

According to ITV News, Ninestiles Academy in Birmingham has sent staff into the local community in a minibus to bring pupils to school during GTA 6’s launch week.

“We have been working hard to make this a particularly engaging and positive week for our learners, with a range of activities designed to encourage strong attendance,” said principal Alex Hughes.

“This has included careers-focused assemblies and information events, colleagues visiting the local community in our school minibus to help bring learners into school and a prize draw for everyone who achieves 100% attendance across the week.”.

Ninestiles has not mentioned whether any pupils brought in by minibus missed class because they were playing GTA 6, but its more direct approach to tackling convenient absences around GTA 6’s launch is just one of several ways schools have responded.

Another school in England offered students the chance to win an $85 Amazon voucher for attending on launch day, while a professor in Spain actually canceled homework and postponed an exam to give students more time to play.
