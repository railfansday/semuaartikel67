Napoli change system with Antonio Vergara and Rasmus Hojlund upfront, leaving Kevin De Bruyne on the bench, as Scott McTominay and Billy Gilmour face Che Adams and Giovanni Simeone’s Torino.

It kicks off at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The Partenopei have been stuttering all season long and it is not all down to the injury crisis, as Max Allegri is again going to reshuffle his tactics this evening.

It would appear to be a 3-5-2 formation, with Vergara pushed up in support of lone centre-forward Hojlund, resting De Bruyne after the veteran started both Nations League games for Belgium.

Alex Meret is out for circa two months with a fracture in his back, joining Benoit Badiashile, Alisson Santos, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo and Alessandro Buongiorno on the absentee list.

It means Vanja Milinkovic-Savic faces up against his old club, along with an all-Scottish battle for McTominay and Gilmour with Torino striker Adams.

The Granata had been on fine form until hitting a brick wall, going into the break for international duty with one point from three rounds, and that was a disappointing stalemate with relegation strugglers Lecce.

Goalkeeper Franco Israel is the only remaining absentee, though Nikola Vlasic was dealing with a muscular concern after playing for Croatia.

Rolando Mandragora is from Naples, so this is always a special fixture for him, while Cholito Simeone faces his old club.

Napoli: Milinkovic-Savic; Beukema, Rrahmani, Marín; Politano, Anguissa, Gilmour, McTominay, Spinazzola; Vergara, Hojlund.

Torino: Perri; Coco, Ismajli, Comuzzo; Belghali, Gineitis, Mandragora, Braganca, Cacciamani; Adams, Simeone.
