According to recent reports, artificial intelligence website builder 10Web Inc. is introducing a fresh feature that it claims can automatically turn content from Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, Google Business Profiles and more than 30 other sources into functioning websites and online stores.

Called Start from Anything, the feature targets small businesses that lack the time and expertise to build their own websites. Owners can paste a link to an existing online presence or upload files, and 10Web’s AI extracts the information needed to construct a website.

The system can convert photos and videos into galleries and product pages, use captions to populate product descriptions, and turn service information into dedicated pages. Business hours, addresses and location information can automatically populate contact pages, while social media posts can become news items and promotional offers.

In a recent development, 10Web noted its software also determines what functionality a website needs. Businesses offering services automatically receive booking capabilities, while those selling merchandise get online stores equipped to process orders and payments. Owners can subsequently modify their websites by conversing with AI.

“Nobody opens a bakery because they love choosing plugins,” Chief Executive Narek Torosyan said in a statement. “Every question we ask an owner about software is a question we failed to answer ourselves.”.

The service departs from the prompt-based approach that has dominated generative AI website development. Although prompts can create attractive websites, they require users to describe their businesses and often produce generic content that must subsequently be replaced or customized.

10Web’s approach is to reuse information businesses have already accumulated, such as photos, customer-facing descriptions, product catalogs and promotional materials.

The company said it tested the technology using publicly available content, converting a concept store‘s Instagram presence and a vintage retailer‘s TikTok account into online stores. It also generated websites from a vacation property‘s Google profile and a small business’s Facebook page.

The new capability builds on 10Web’s existing generative AI platform, which creates and manages websites using the open-source WordPress content management system.

Sources indicate that in May 2025, the organization introduced an application programming interface that allows developers and software providers to incorporate AI website generation into their applications. The platform uses specialized AI agents to handle different aspects of website development, including design, content creation and functionality.

10Web said the latest feature includes hosting and is free to use for building and launching websites. The announcement did not specify charges for longer-term operation.

The company stated it serves tens of thousands of customers worldwide and attracts between 50,000 and 60,000 latest signups monthly.
