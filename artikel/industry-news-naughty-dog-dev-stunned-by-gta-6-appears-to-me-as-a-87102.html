Grand Theft Auto 6 has stunned players as well as other game developers, with Naughty Dog veteran and Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet world design director Michael Barclay praising the game with a mix of disbelief and hyperbole.

"I've made video games for 19 years and GTA VI appears to me as a Bugatti Veyron would to a nervous Victorian child," Barclay notes on Twitter. The Veyron 16.4 can reach 100km/h in 2.5 seconds, Bugatti claims, whereas a Victorian child would obviously need considerable setup time and mechanical assistance – that is, a trebuchet – to even approach such speeds.

In a recent development, barclay worked on Star Wars: Battlefront 3 at Free Radical Design, The Shoot at Cohort Studios, and also put in years at Crytek and Cloud Imperium Games before joining Naughty Dog in 2016. On his blog, he goes into great depth on the making of The Last of Us Part 2 among many other games and ideas.

In a recent development, "I'm always astonished that their environmental lighting is mostly dynamic and looks as good if not better than most baked lighting," Barclay states of Rockstar in a separate post. "They do matte style overcast lighting during cloudy days that looks so realistic I just sit and look at the street," he adds.

In a recent development, tim Soret, head of The Last Night developer Odd Tales, agrees with Barclay. "I concur. It truly is alien technology. Hard to comprehend," he says in a post.

GTA 6 is a top-to-bottom marvel. Our GTA 6 review calls it the highest-fidelity sandbox ever crafted, and its Metacritic score positions it as the highest-rated game of all time. Soon enough, we'll surely hear official figures from Rockstar and Take-Two enshrining it as the biggest media launch in human history. Early estimations put it at around 25 million copies and $2.36 billion grossed.
