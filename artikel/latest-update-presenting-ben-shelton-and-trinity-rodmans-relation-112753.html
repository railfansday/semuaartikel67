In a recent development, for the past two years, one of the sport’s brightest young stars, Ben Shelton, has been in a relationship with Washington Spirit soccer champ Trinity Rodman—and despite their busy schedules, they seemed to be head over heels in love with each other. That is, until November 24, when the Daily Mail broke the news that the power couple reportedly split.

Sources indicate that since you’re probs wondering how this couple came to be (and what led to their rumored breakup), I’ve done the work of rounding up all of their biggest relationship moments in a little timeline for your pleasure. Read it and weep!

According to recent reports, ben hard rolls out their relationship on Instagram by sneaking a photo of Trinity kissing his cheek into a carousel post. Swipe to the end to see:.

Soon after, Trinity clears up some of the discourse around her and Ben’s relationship—namely the story behind how they actually met—and makes a point to note that Frances Tiafoe, a friend of hers and Ben’s, did not introduce them to each other like he previously claimed.

“I can assure you Frances was not the reason we met or got together,” she remarked in an interview with ESPN. “I don’t know why he’s taking credit for that. Me and Ben can both cut that out. It was a solely me and Ben thing.”.

It has been revealed that for the record, their story went a little like this: “I was like, ‘Hey, when you gonna come visit me?’ and he was like, ‘I’ll come right now,’” Trinity explained in an Instagram video (below). “So then he flew out and the rest is history.”.

It has been revealed that by the end of the month, Ben appears on Trinity’s Instagram. In the photo, she leans back into Ben’s shoulder, while his face stays mysteriously hidden beneath a hat (despite her tagging him in the photo). Her caption? “Dibs 🤭.”.

He’s taking credit, [saying] ‘Oh, yeah, I put them together. I was like Danny Ainge.’ He’s a liar. He’s trying to make it about himself. It’s not about him, but that’s funny.”.

Trinity also sits down with Cosmo for an interview and reveals that Ben urged her to temporarily step away from soccer amid her injury.

She notes that birthdays and large celebrations can be difficult for her and Ben to do on the actual day, likely due to their sporty commitments. She added, “For us, it's so hard to celebrate literally anything. Even for our birthdays, we celebrated them, like the week before and the week after. We celebrate on the phone. Thankfully for preseason, with Spirit, I'll be going to Florida, and he'll be there.”.
