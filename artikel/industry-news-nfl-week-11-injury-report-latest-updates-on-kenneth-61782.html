According to recent reports, the regular season rolls on in the NFL with Week 11 already underway after the Houston Texans' victory over the Indianapolis Colts kicked things off on Thursday. Now, we have the rest of the slate on deck. While this is the smallest collection of games that we'll have this season (13) thanks to six teams being on a bye, it packs quite a punch. That includes a potential NFC Championship preview between the Vikings and 49ers in Mexico City.

Of course, as is the case with every week in the NFL, injuries are a substantial piece of the puzzle and remain a substantial storyline heading into each contest. This week, there are some heavy-hitters that headline the injury front. Namely, the Chiefs will once again be without star running back Kenneth Walker III, while Niners standout receiver Mike Evans (Achilles tendinitis) still has his status up in the air.

As teams across the league wrap up their week of practice, they've unveiled their final injury reports along with game statuses. Below, you can find updates for every game in Week 11.

Note: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, Los Angeles (Rams), Newly introduced England, and Seattle are on the bye in Week 11.
