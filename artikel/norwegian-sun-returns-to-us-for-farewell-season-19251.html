Norwegian Sun Returns to U.S. for Farewell Season

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Norwegian Sun recently returned to the United States for its final farewell season sailing from the country.

Set to be handed over to Cordelia Cruises in 2027, the Norwegian Cruise Line vessel is offering a series of 11- and 12-night cruises from PortMiami.

The itineraries are highlighted by partial transits of the Panama Canal, in addition to visits to destinations in Central America and the Caribbean.

Among the ports of call set to welcome the Norwegian Sun are Puerto Limón in Costa Rica, Cartagena de Indias in Colombia, Willemstad in Curaçao, Cabo Rojo in the Dominican Republic and Falmouth in Jamaica.

Most of the cruises also feature a visit to Norwegian’s private island destination in the Bahamas, Great Stirrup Cay.

The season runs through mid-April, when the vessel is scheduled to embark on a repositioning voyage to the Mediterranean.

The 78,309-ton vessel will then embark on a final season in the region, offering weeklong cruises to Italy, Croatia, Montenegro, Slovenia and Greece.

Departing from Ravenna and Civitavecchia, the itineraries are highlighted by visits to destinations that include Catania, Split, Kotor, Koper and Corfu.

For its farewell cruise, the Norwegian Sun is scheduled to offer a seven-night cruise that will sail from Ravenna to Piraeus on August 29, 2027.

In service for Norwegian since being delivered by the Lloyd Werft shipyard in 2002, the ship will be withdrawn as part of a long-term charter agreement signed in April 2025.

Joining the former Norwegian Sky and the former Empress of the Seas, the Norwegian Sun will be operated by Cordelia Cruises starting in late 2027.

The vessel will be renamed the Cordelia Sun for itineraries departing from ports in India starting next November.

For its maiden season in the country, the 2,000-passenger ship will offer short cruises from Mumbai to destinations that include Agatti Island, Mormugao and Goa.
