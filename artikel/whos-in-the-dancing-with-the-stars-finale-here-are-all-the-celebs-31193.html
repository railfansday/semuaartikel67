📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We’ve made it! Dancing With the Stars is heading to the Season 35 finale next week, and there are officially five couples left in line to win the coveted mirrorball trophy. Last night’s semifinals saw Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson get eliminated (a true shock), leaving us with these pairings:.

Ezra French and Daniella Karagach.

It’s pretty much anyone’s prize to win, and fans will have an even bigger opportunity to make their voices heard thanks to the show airing live on both the West Coast and the East Coast next week—for the first time ever.

It has been revealed that deep dive this way, but typically Dancing With the Stars only airs live on the East Coast because of conflicting local news programming on the West Coast. Clearly ABC made an exception this year, which means West Coast viewers will be able to log on and cast their votes while watching the show in real time. Considering DWTS is filmed in Los Angeles and a ton of the celebs have fan bases on the West Coast, this could be huge!

According to recent reports, as for next week’s format, the cast will perform a freestyle routine, a Judges’ Choice routine (where the judges pick a dance style for each couple that’ll challenge them), and an Instant Dance, where they have to choreograph and perform a dance live with very little notice. That’s three dances in one night—a huge challenge for everyone!
