The Anthropic (ANT +0.00%) initial public offering (IPO) is a landmark moment for artificial intelligence (AI) investing and sheds further light on the interrelationships among AI companies. One of these is Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +1.60%), which is set to receive a large share of the $900.9 billion in spending commitments outlined in Anthropic's latest Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filings.

In a recent development, most of Anthropic's expenditure relates to compute and infrastructure, with up to $120.5 billion allocated to SpaceX's SpaceXAI subsidiary. The terms extend through 2029, with about $3.1 billion payable per month after capacity is delivered. Anthropic expects that to occur beginning in December.

Interestingly, both parties have the right to cancel the agreement with 90 days' notice, but this only kicks in after a noncancellable period, which ends in January 2027 and March 2027. In theory, at least, this means Anthropic could write to SpaceX on Feb. 1 and on April 1 to initiate cancellation of the agreements 90 days after those dates.

This is notable because Anthropic's spending commitments with Microsoft (up to $49.2 billion), Alphabet's Google ($121.3 billion), and Amazon (at least $110 billion) are all noncancelable unless there's a material breach of the agreement.

Industry observers note that revenue from Anthropic is significant, validating SpaceX's vast AI infrastructure investments and strengthening its financial position ahead of any potential merger with Tesla. That mentioned, investors will have to keep an eye on developments, as Anthropic's ability to cancel the agreement means SpaceX is first in line to suffer should Anthropic decrease its investment looks.
