Shiba Inu’s layer-2 network, Shibarium, is experiencing a major slowdown as daily network activity has fallen to three-digit figures (fewer than 1,000 transactions), hitting lows not seen in months.

According to recent reports, shibarium processed 546 transactions, according to Shibariumscan data, marking a slower period for the layer-2 network after activity spiked to a high of 6,370 on October 31, 2026. Transaction activity declined afterward, and the decline intensified amid the sell-off that hit the market in recent weeks.

The latest figures suggest that fewer transactions are taking place across Shibarium compared with periods of stronger activity. The key question now is whether Shibarium can regain momentum in the coming weeks.

Meanwhile, development continues behind the scenes for the Shiba Inu layer-2 network, as long-time Shiba Inu community member and developer Mazrael revealed a substantial batch of contracts deployed on Puppynet, Shibarium's testnet.

But in the meantime, Shibarium’s fall to three-digit activity levels is a major slowdown that SHIB holders and ecosystem watchers will be watching closely.

Shiba Inu's on-chain holders increased as the number of wallets holding SHIB hit 1,700,930 on November 19, 2026.

According to the latest figures shared by Etherscan SHIB, an X account dedicated to providing updates on the SHIB ecosystem, the cryptocurrency added 236 upcoming on-chain holders in a single day, taking its total past the 1.7 million milestone.

It has been revealed that the month of November has also seen consistent growth, with SHIB adding 4,520 holders since the start of the month. The broader 2026 trend is even more pronounced, with the number of holders of the token increasing by 152,300 since the beginning of the year.

While Shiba Inu's latest milestone seems remarkable, Mazrael suggested that the Shib ecosystem, including Shibarium, might have more holders than indicated, with 1.4 million holders of Binance-pegged SHIB on the BNB Chain.

"Shibarium has more holders than those you listed, though. But yeah. Well, there are also 1.4 million holders of Binance-pegged SHIB on BNB. But that's not actually CCIP SHIB; it is a peg," Mazrael wrote.
