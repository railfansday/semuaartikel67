Recent findings suggest that kylian Mbappé monopolized the media attention yesterday, on the eve of the Nations League match against Italy. The Team kicked off the day by publishing information suggesting that the relationship between the captain of the Blues and his fresh coach, Zinédine Zidane, would not be as idyllic as one might believe. The day then continued with the announcement by the FFF of Mbappé’s absence from today’s training. The Real Madrid striker was first announced “in treatment”, before a hamstring problem was mentioned. This series finally concluded with the confirmation of the withdrawal of the French number 10 for this evening’s meeting, without him being ruled out for the rest of the gathering.

Faced with all this media uproar, Zidane had to explain at a press conference the physical state of his player as well as the rumors of friction with the Bondynois. “Yes, Kylian will be out tomorrow, he felt some pain and we won’t take any risks. It’s unfortunate to lose players, but it’s part of the game. I hope it’s not very serious and that we will have it on Sunday. For Kylian, it’s a small embarrassment and it could do it for Sunday. I find his state of mind good, the proof is that he remains with us: he is not happy, he would have liked to play tomorrow. When we have a feeling, we take zero risks, the season is long, that’s what I do when there is a small problem. ».

It has been revealed that however, the Spanish journalist Marcos Benito assured this night, in the show El Chiringuitothat if Mbappé did stay with the Blues, it would not really be by choice. Asked by presenter Josep Pedrerol about his surprise at seeing the player not return to Madrid, Benito responded straight away that the captain of the Blues was also thinking of packing up, like his teammate Aurélien Tchouaméni.

It has been revealed that “He thought so too (laughs). He feels discomfort. He had made a pact with the Real Madrid medical staff stipulating that he would return to Spain at the slightest physical problem. The surprise was to see Zidane hold him back despite his injury in order to observe the evolution of his condition in view of Sunday’s match (against Turkey, editor’s note). However, his private jet was ready in Paris to take him back to Madrid. He will have to wait, since he finally made the trip to Milan with the rest of the group. All of this generates rough edges in their relationship. » The series is far from over….
