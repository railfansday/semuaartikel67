Leeds United delivered a statement performance at Stamford Bridge, thumping Chelsea 4-1 to claim their first away victory against the Blues in more than a quarter of a century.

Noah Okafor scored twice to turn the game around after Emmanuel Emegha’s opener, with Dominic Calvert-Lewin converting from the spot and Brenden Aaronson adding a late fourth.

“The last time this club was able to win here was last century. We have created history, but it doesn’t feel like this. We fully deserved to win this game, and it’s great for everyone connected with Leeds,” Leeds manager Daniel Farke told BBC Sport after the final whistle.

“We came here to win the game. We spoke the whole week about this and wanted to make this our game where we play on the front foot. We didn’t want to allow Chelsea to control the mood.

“We need to have good fitness levels and I have smart players who understand how we want to press and play. We have to be more concentrated to execute what we want to do. Desire is second to none, and we are competitive in the best league in the world.”.

The result has seen Leeds leapfrog Chelsea and move up to sixth in the Premier League table. They currently sit just one point off a potential Champions League qualification spot.

“People talk about turning points and we have to learn about stuff that didn’t work and try to better that,” stated club captain Ethan Ampadu.

Industry observers note that “We know what the history of this game is like and what the fans expect – we know they have wanted a win here for a long time. It’s right we can enjoy this performance.”.

According to recent reports, “We have to be realistic and understand how hard this league is. We need to back this up next week, if we don’t then this performance is wasted. We know where we want to get Leeds back to. It could be a long road but we’re ready for it.”.

Here are 11 remarkable stats from Leeds’ big win away to Chelsea:.

– This was Leeds United’s first win at Stamford Bridge in the 21st century. Their last victory away at Chelsea came way back in December 1999.

– The rare away win for Leeds comes fresh from Chelsea’s defeat at home to Tottenham last month. That was Spurs’ only 2nd win at Stamford Bridge in the Premier League era. Two results that’ll take Xabi Alonso a long time to get over.

– Since returning to the Premier League last year, Leeds are now unbeaten in eight league games against Chelsea, Manchester United and Liverpool. Three wins, five defeats.

– Since Farke switched to a back three last November, Leeds sit eighth in the Premier League table – they’ve sustained near European qualification-level results over almost a full season’s worth of results, and have taken more points than the likes of Chelsea, Tottenham and Newcastle.

Recent findings suggest that – The scoreline was no fluke, Leeds comfortably beating the hosts on ‘expected goals’. Farke’s men notched 2.51xG compared to Chelsea’s 0.56xG.

– Chelsea goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez was forced into twice as many saves (four) as his opposite number James Trafford (two).

Recent findings suggest that – Per the statistics, Chelsea created zero ‘big chances’. Leeds registered seven in total, missing four alongside the three that were converted. It could’ve been a bloodbath.

– Ethan Ampadu completed 88% of his passes (58/66), including 88% in the opposition half (30/34). Covered 12.4km, registered 11 ball recoveries, won three of four ground duels and wasn’t dribbled past once.

Recent findings suggest that – Noah Okafor scored twice from just three shots, come from an xG of just 0.3. He also created one massive chance, played three key passes and notched three successful dribbles.

– Leeds covered 125.1km collectively, six kilometres more than Chelsea.

– Only Brighton and Arsenal have conceded fewer goals than Leeds in the Premier League this season, and only Tottenham (12.4xG) and Arsenal (7.6xG) have better underlying numbers when it comes to ‘expected goals against’ this season. Leeds have faced 13.1xG from 11 outings in 2026-27.
