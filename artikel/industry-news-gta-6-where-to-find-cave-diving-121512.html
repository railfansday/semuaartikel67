According to recent reports, ​There feels like endless activities to do in Grand Theft Auto 6. Whether it’s racing through the streets of Vice City, playing mini-golf with your loved one or robbing banks, there’s no shortage of things to do in this vast open world adventure.

Most of the side activities are hidden, not properly introduced or shown on the map where to find them, and that’s exactly the case for cave diving. This dangerous and claustrophobic activity might not be for everyone, but it certainly can yield you some good rewards if you decide to bring Jason or Lucia down.

Industry observers note that the cave system isn’t too far off from where you start in chapter 2 of the story. You will be able to access it in the first chapter, but it will be a bit of a drive. This is located all the way in Kelly County. This can be found in the western portion of the map.

The best way to find where you’re going is right in-between the L and E on the Leonida text if you zoom all the way out on the map. You will see a rather large network of swamps and rivers called the Emerald Springs. Here, you will want to go directly west of the “Emerald Springs” text on the map, around the rivers and on the western side.

Sources indicate that there will be a small hut area on the map that branches off from the road, and this will lead to Selkie Hole. The Selkie is a mythological creature from Norse and Celtic lore. Up here, you will spot various divers and a couple of them just leave their scuba gear out there. Grab them (they won’t care) and head inside the cave through the chain-link door. Down the stairs is the cave system. Happy exploring!

A light illuminates rocks in a dark underwater cave, with an Explorer’s Knife prompt on screen.

Sadly, most of the cave system is already mapped – literally. You’ll be able to open up your in-game map and see just how far the tunnels go, and that’s all there is. Now, we say “that’s all there is”, but in reality, it’s a surprisingly lengthy and sprawling system that will take quite a bit to fully explore.

According to recent reports, because of that, there will be some hidden items spread throughout. One of the most common items in this cave system is Meteorites. These are unique items spread throughout the open world in which you’ll be able to pawn for some extra cash, usually for quite a bit (especially early on). It’s the same as collectibles such as cards and doubloons, but yielding the most trade value.

On top of that, you will find other unique items such as an Explorer’s Knife pretty close to the entrance of the cave system. It’s nothing too special but one that you will always have considering it’s a legendary weapon.
