Zacks Premium is a stock research subscription that Zacks Investment Research sells to individual investors, separate from Zacks Trade, the brokerage that shares its name. The Zacks Rank at its center sorts stocks from #1 (Strong Buy) to #5 (Strong Sell) by how analysts are revising their earnings estimates.

Investors who research their own stocks and will act within weeks of a rank change get a rating with a long, published record for narrowing down stock ideas, at $249 a year. Buy-and-hold investors can look up a stock’s Rank on its free quote page and save the $249.
