David Moyes is hopeful he will have a fully-fit squad to choose from as Everton look to extend their impressive form away to Brentford on Monday night (8pm GMT).

After the recent international break, the Blues resume their Premier League campaign in west London aiming to cement their place in the top six of the table.

Ahead of his pre-match press conference at Finch Farm on Friday, Moyes was presented with a signed frame by his former Blues midfielder Leon Osman and current captain James Tarkowski to mark his record-breaking 593rd match in charge of Everton against Brentford on Monday.

And the Toffees boss was confident he will have all options available for his starting XI in the capital.

'Hopefully everybody will be fit and available on Monday night for the Brentford game," stated Moyes. "We've not had anyone return from international duty with any problems. We've got another few days to go before game day so hopefully we won't pick up any.".

Despite having a small squad to work with this season, Moyes also spoke about his desire to be flexible with his team selection when possible.

He added: "I want us to be flexible where we can alter things if we have to.

"I think the form of Harrison [Armstrong] and Jimmy [Garner] and Kiernan [Dewsbury-Hall] has made it easy to select [the midfield]. It's not always just the form.

It has been revealed that "We want to choose the right games and not overplay them. But it is quite difficult when you don't have quite so many players.".

Industry observers note that brentford will be without main striker Igor Thiago for the visit of the Toffees, the Brazil international serving the first game of a three-match suspension following his red card for violent conduct in the 2-0 loss to Brighton & Hove Albion before the recent international break. In his absence, veteran Callum Wilson is in line to lead the Bees' attack.

Meanwhile, centre-back Nathan Collins is a doubt after sustaining a knee injury during the Republic of Ireland’s 1-0 defeat by Austria last Saturday. The 25-year-old returned to Brentford for further assessment and missed the Republic of Ireland’s 3-0 victory over Kosovo on Tuesday.

Full-back Aaron Hickey is another concern after picking up a hamstring problem while playing for Scotland against Switzerland.

Assistants: Nick Hopton and Adrian Holmes.
