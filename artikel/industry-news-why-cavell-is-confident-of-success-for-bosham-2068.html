They rapidly found life difficult in Division 2 of the Southern Combination League with a relatively young side, with early results not what was wanted and early pressure on players and staff.

Success has come in the Sussex Intermediate Cup, in which Wadhurst will make the trip to Walton Lane this Saturday for a last 16 encounter, with the winners set for a home tie against Balcombe in the quarter-finals.

The draw has really opened up for Cavell and his troops and with a newly introduced group of players set to come through the door this week, hopes will spring eternal that they can go very deep this season.

On Saturday in a home league match, Bosham welcomed ASC Brighton Rangers, againat whom they had fought to a hard draw at Lancing earlier in the campaign.

But with several changes to the starting line-up, this return game was always likely to be awkward.

Bosham started brightly with Jack Lindsay and Bailey Rand looking sharp and the Yellows defence made to be on their game to prevent any clear sights of goal.

Midway through the half, and a little against the run of play, ASC edged ahead after seizing on frailties at the back from the Robins.

Bosham did steady the ship against the inevitable onslaught from the visitors and did get into the break with only a 1-0 deficit to attack after the resumption.

Sadly, Bosham weren’t able to find an early breakthrough and frustrations grew as the half wore on.

Good periods were had but, late on, following a corner, ASC were like lightning on the break and a sumptuous finish effectively ended the hopes of a turnaround.

Rangers saw out time to grab the spoils and leave Bosham 11th in the table for now, but hopes remain that improvements will come over the coming week.

Bosham: Alfie Lee, Edward Coles, Ilya Donets, Oscar Lee, Malachi Macarthy, Dan Morrison, Bailey Rand, Charlie Hazel, Alex Chapman, Bode Hammond and Jack Lindsay. Subs: Ollie Probee, Darcy Hinshlewood, Bobby Lee, Hugh Westmore.

Elsewhere in the SCFL on Saturday, Midhurst drew 2-2 at home to Wick while Pagham lost 5-0 at Lancing in the Premier. In Division 1, leaders Infinity won 6-2 at home to Reigate Priory but Selsey lost 3-0 at Jarvis Brook.
