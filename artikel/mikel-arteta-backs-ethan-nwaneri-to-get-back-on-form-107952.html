Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta noted that Ethan Nwaneri, who is presently on loan at Borussia Dortmund from the Gunners, needs to earn the right to play.

Recent findings suggest that “He had started playing quite a lot, and now recently he hasn’t played that much,” the Spaniard said at Monday’s press conference ahead of their UEFA Champions League clash on Tuesday. “He needs to earn the right to play because he is playing at the top of the Bundesliga and the players that they have, they have huge talent and quality. That is the reality of football: you go there, and because you come from Arsenal, it doesn’t give you any advantage so you need to earn it.”.

Nwaneri has only played six minutes of football in Dortmund’s last three games, and has only registered one assist – which came on his Bundesliga debut against Hoffenheim – since his arrival from Arsenal.

Arteta, though, believes the 19-year-old is capable of turning around his current situation. “Ethan first of all has the mindset to do it and then the quality to do it. These things are part of a journey and part of a season, and earn the right to win.”.

Industry observers note that nwaneri could potentially face Arsenal on Tuesday at the Emirates as there is no restriction in his loan deal that prevents him from playing against his parent club. Whether Niko Kovač opts to use him in the game remains to be seen.
