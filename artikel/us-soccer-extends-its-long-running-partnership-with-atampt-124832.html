📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The U.S. Soccer Federation is continuing its long-running partnership with AT&T in a multiyear renewal that will see the Dallas-based telecom organization support U.S. Soccer and its national teams through athlete storytelling, fan engagement, and long-term investment.

Sources indicate that “We’ve been proud to support U.S. Soccer since 2009, and we believe the sport’s greatest opportunities are still ahead,” mentioned Sabina Ahmed, assistant vice president of media and sponsorships at AT&T.

Soccer fans will also see content, match-day activations, and other programming. Women soccer players will continue to receive support through She’s Connected by AT&T, which tells their stories and showcases their impact well beyond the field.

“Soccer is reaching more people across the country, and partners like AT&T help us turn that interest into lasting fandom,” said David Wright, chief business officer at U.S. Soccer.

The 2026 FIFA Men’s World Cup, held in the U.S. this past summer, reinforced soccer’s growing stronghold in the country. Nowhere does that growth show more clearly than at the recently opened Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center located south of downtown Atlanta, Georgia, named for the owner of Atlanta’s Major League Soccer franchise, Atlanta United FC. The complex is the new home for all 27 U.S. Soccer National Teams, bringing the Men’s and Women’s Teams together at one training location for the first time.

AT&T is a founding partner of the upcoming national training center, bringing advanced connectivity and Connected Fields technology to the campus, giving players and coaches new tools to analyze performance and prepare for matches.

Christian Pulisic from the U.S. Men’s National Team stated the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center is what AT&T’s commitment can enable, “bringing our teams together in one place with the connectivity and technology to help us prepare and perform at our best.”.

In a recent development, attention now turns to next year’s Women’s World Cup, where the U.S. Women’s National Team looks to return to its dominant form with AT&T supporting and cheering for the squad the entire time.
