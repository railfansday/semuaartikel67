Home design’s future depends on how homes support everyday life

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (105 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The 2026 residential design conversation is placing considerable attention on tactile materials, minimalist interiors, natural stone, and visually distinctive finishes. According to Houzz Research’s 2026 U.S. Houzz Emerging Summer Trends Report, searches for sandstone increased by 257%, while interest in Venetian plaster rose by 94%, reflecting growing interest in sensory-rich interiors. These preferences offer insight into evolving tastes, although they also raise a broader question about how aesthetic priorities relate to everyday usability. As homes receive growing focus for their visual presentation, practical considerations such as accessibility, ergonomics, and long-term functionality may deserve an equally prominent place in the design process.
