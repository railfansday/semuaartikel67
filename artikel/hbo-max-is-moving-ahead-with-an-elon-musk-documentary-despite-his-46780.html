HBO Max is moving ahead with an Elon Musk documentary despite his $1B investment. Here’s why

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Skydance CEO David Ellison says the tech giant will honor the distribution deal to stream Alex Gibney’s Musk documentary, even after Elon Musk’s $1 billion investment in the company. The nearly four-hour documentary on the Tesla and SpaceX executive is expected to hit HBO Max in early 2027, but Musk’s recent investment had put into question whether the streamer would air it. HBO is now part of Skydance following the controversial merger between Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount earlier this year. While Bleecker Street Media holds the U.S. theatrical rights and Universal Pictures the international distribution rights, HBO has the North American streaming rights for the documentary. When asked about the potential conflict at the 2026 DealBook summit in Latest York on Wednesday, Ellison told New York Times columnist and DealBook founder Andrew Ross Sorkin that he intends to follow through with the commitment to A24. “We believe in independence. We don’t believe in censorship of any kind,” Ellison told Sorkin. He added: “We have that deal with A24, and of course we’ll honor it.”  According to Deadline, Ellison appears to have misattributed the deal to HBO’s pay-1 deal with A24, a multiyear licensing deal that gives HBO exclusive streaming rights to A24-made films. A pay-1 deal gives a premium cable network or streaming service the first window to exclusively air a film following its theatrical release. Pay-1 windows often last about 18 months, at which point other networks can acquire streaming rights. Deadline reports that the deal Ellison refers to is most likely a commitment with HBO Documentary Films instead. The statement from Ellison follows Musk’s own criticism of the documentary, which he referred to as a “hit piece.” The documentary in question features interviews with figures from Musk’s life, including his first wife, Justine Musk; Tesla cofounder Martin Eberhard; and Ashley St. Clair, the mother of Musk’s 13th child. Additionally, figures from the entrepreneur and tech world are also featured in the film, including LinkedIn cofounder Reid Hoffman and British Canadian computer scientist Geoffrey Hinton, also known as the “godfather of AI.” Ellison also took the stage to praise Musk, calling him “the greatest entrepreneur of our time.” He added: “To have his vote of confidence and to have him as a shareholder is an honor and a privilege. And if there was any opportunity to be able to collaborate with him in the future, from our perspective, it would be a privilege.”.
