📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Over half a decade since it was initially announced, there's been absolutely no sign of a new trailer for the upcoming Star Wars Knights Of The Old Republic Remake, let alone a release date for when we can expect the project. While recent updates suggest that the remake is still in development at the very least, there has still been painfully little for fans to actually look forward to.

Thankfully, Disney seems to be gearing up for some major Star Wars reveals in the coming months, and if the latest rumor is true, an official debut of the Star Wars KOTOR remake is coming in just a few weeks.

While rumors have been circulating for a few days now, YouTuber 100% Star Wars on X, who previously claimed that KOTOR would be getting re-revealed later this year, has doubled down in saying that the game will now be revealed at this year's Game Awards showcase.

Although fans have been taking the announcement with a grain of salt, the reveal also coincides with Lucasfilm Games publicly acknowledging KOTOR out of the blue with a post comparing the relationship between Cal and Merrin to Revan and Bastila.

Despite not meaning much on its own, the post acknowledging two games at the moment rumored for sequel debuts at this year's Game Awards show certainly feels like an oddly timed coincidence.

Even without an official confirmation yet, it's hard to imagine a better time to announce a fresh Star Wars game that fans have been begging to see more of at The Game Awards of all places, especially considering the franchise's 50th anniversary officially starts next year.

While there is still a chance we might have to wait until May for more details about the coming projects, Lego has already started the anniversary celebrations with a new line of Star Wars-themed Lego sets releasing in early January, showing there is no better time to start preparing fans for a big year than the present.
