Breaking: Pistons' Cade Cunningham out for Tuesday's NBA Cup matchup vs. Magic due to right knee soreness

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Detroit Pistons guard Cade Cunningham missed Tuesday's 104-86 win over the Orlando Magic in NBA Cup group play. He was a late scratch due to right knee soreness after being listed as probable on the injury report until a half-hour before tip-off.

Cunningham, 25, had previously missed only one game this season for the Pistons, sitting out last week's 122-109 win over the Upcoming Orleans Pelicans, also due to right knee soreness. Javonte Green replaced Cunningham in Detroit's starting lineup for that contest, scoring 18 points with seven rebounds.

According to recent reports, "He's battling through a lot, and I'll leave it at that," Pistons coach J.B. Bickerstaff told reporters Tuesday, via MLive's Jacob Richman. "He's going to go out and compete and give us what he's got, but he's fighting through a lot.".

Cunningham scored a season-low five points in Sunday's 107-97 loss to the Atlanta Hawks, shooting 2-for-9 in 33 minutes. His 15 assists were his highest total this season, but he also committed six turnovers.

The Pistons have lost two straight games and have a 10-6 record that has them tied for fifth in the Eastern Conference.

Recent findings suggest that in his 15 games this season, Cunningham leads the Pistons in scoring with 24.2 points per game, while averaging 8.3 assists, 5.1 rebounds and 1.3 steals. He's shooting 32% on 92 3-point attempts.

Detroit was also without Duncan Robinson (left quad strain) and Jalen Duren (strained right hamstring) against the Magic. However, Duren appears to be on his way back after being assigned to the Pistons' G League affiliate for Wednesday's practice.

The Pistons next play on Friday versus the Milwaukee Bucks.
