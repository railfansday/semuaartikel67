For Lindsay Lohan, there’s “nothing better” than seeing the holiday magic through her son’s eyes.

According to recent reports, “Traditions are starting officially, kind of, now with our son because he’s now more aware of Santa and reindeer,” Lohan, 40, exclusively told Us while celebrating the launch of her Marshalls Unwrap the Internet partnership. “A tradition that we have is we always watch Rudolph together — the original old one, the old cartoon one.”.

Lohan, who welcomed son Luai with husband Bader Shammas in 2023, shared that their family also reads “a lot of Christmas books” to ring in the holidays.

Industry observers note that “Wrapping the presents is going to be the fun part,” she gushed of their tradition. “And just seeing his face on Christmas morning.”.

Getting in the holiday spirit for their household, of course, also means time enjoying their favorite festive movies.

“I always have to watch Love Actually during Christmas time,” she told Us of her go-to flick in the holiday genre. “It makes me so happy, that movie. I love The Holiday. I love Miracle on 34th Street. We always watch Home Alone. That’s my husband’s pick.”.

Lohan herself has starred in several beloved festive films (think: Falling For Christmas and Our Little Secret) and taken on iconic roles across several genres — but there’s one particular movie that has her heart if she were to spend the holidays with a former character.

According to recent reports, “The best would be to have the best of both worlds, which would be Hallie and Annie, because then you’d be in London with all those Christmas lights, and then you’d be in Napa in this beautiful vineyard setting,” she noted of her iconic characters in The Parent Trap. “I would definitely choose them because then you get two for one. And that’s also like a Nancy Meyers Christmas. So you can’t really say no to that.”.

While ushering in the holidays, Lindsay teamed up with Marshalls and several influential personalities and creators — including the likes of Antoni Porowski, Danny Pellegrino, Robin Arzón — across reality TV, wellness, travel and more to curate gift guides for all the loved ones in shoppers’ lives.

According to recent reports, “I love giving gifts and I think any time that we can make gifting easier in one space in one store is a blessing,” Lindsay told Us. “I love a thoughtful holiday campaign where we can help people get gifts for the ones that they love and the right kind of gifts instead of kind of wondering, ‘Oh my God, what do I get?’”.

From stocking stuffers under $10 to luxe loungewear and designer accessories, the collection offers inspiration for gifts in every budget.

It has been revealed that “I love cozy blankets, cozy cashmere sets, and it’s so good because they’re affordable,” Lindsay remarked. “You can get them affordable at Marshalls, and even down to stocking stuffers under $10. You can find everything.”.
