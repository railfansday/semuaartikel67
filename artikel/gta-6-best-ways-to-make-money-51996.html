GTA 6: Best Ways to Make Money

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, money makes the world go round, and that certainly is the case for Grand Theft Auto 6. This is one of, if not the biggest game to ever be released, and in order to make it massive in the massive world Rockstar has established, you need to take on some jobs. Some are shady while others are slightly less shady.

Most require some sort of crime, but for the most part, it’s not too difficult to earn millions by the end of the adventure. This guide will help you make money from the early stages of the game to the late stages. For example, the first two chapters only has a couple of ways to make money, while the end game has a slew of options, but only one that’s worth it.

Sources indicate that for the first chapter, it’s primarily doing side activities such as Kenton’s scuba treasure dives or ripping off the Kingfishers gangs. Both pay decently for early game adventures and encounters.

In a recent development, by far the best way to make money in the second chapter, and really throughout the entire game, is stealing cars and selling them. Early in the game, you’ll only have access to some cheaper vehicles, so look out for any type of van that might be hauling some items, such as a news van. These will generally net you above $1,000. It’s a start.

In a recent development, when you get to Vice City, you will meet a man named Randy who wants some very nice cars, and in order to do this, you will need to obtain the ability to clone a key. Thankfully, he demonstrates you, and you can earn a lot of money with him. The downside is that the higher the price on the car, the more likely it’ll have an Advanced Tracker, which is a pain to deal with. It’s almost better to go with mid-payout vehicles with Standard Trackers and drive carefully, which will net you a condition bonus.

This is technically good in the early stages of the game, but when you get halfway through the campaign, you’ll likely have access to more hardware, faster getaway vehicles and body armor. At this point, you’ll have some cash put aside to get the job done easier, so take advantage of this.

Another means of getting paid is ripping off gangs. There will be various set-ups all throughout Vice City and the northern region with a decent pay day. They will also have key valuables that can be sold for quite a bit, on top of unique weapons and cars that you might want to add to your personal collection.

When you get into the five couple of chapters, there will be some very good opportunities to make some real money. As you learn through the campaign, you need more and more money, and bank robberies are the endgame ways to make money. This is because, while you can take on small banks for upwards of $20,000, some of the massive banks can make upwards of ten times that.

The problem is that these are very difficult to overcome. You will need to ensure everyone stays in line and no bank alarms go off too early. Then there’s getting away from the cops, which can be challenging. But if you’re able to do it, the pay is worth it.
