Gta 6 Won’t Launch Without This PS5 Anti-Piracy Enhancement Sony Just Released

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is the most anticipated release of the past decade, and Sony has been using it as its trump card to encourage players to keep their consoles up to date. If they don't update their console to the latest firmware version, the game simply won't run. GTA 6 is now within our grasp — just a few hours away from release — but gamers won't be able to jump in at launch unless they update their consoles right now.

Sony has been heavily targeted by hackers in the past few months due to the rise in piracy on PlayStation 5 systems. As more and more players jailbreak their consoles, the tech giant has been pushing PS5 updates left and right in an attempt to fight this. However, not long after an update goes live, hackers are usually able to modify their software to work around it and release it to the public again rather promptly.

Sony has released one last firmware update to ensure that it can slow down internet pirates and delay attempts at jailbreaking the PS5 post-launch. The short and vague patch notes make it clear that this is the update's purpose: "We’ve made some security fixes to the system software.".

Recent findings suggest that on X, the official PlayStation support account reminded players that they should download the update as soon as possible if they'd like to play at midnight. It's not a threat, really, but gamers won't be able to launch Grand Theft Auto 6 unless they update their PlayStation 5 to system software version 26.07-14.23.00. It requires a download of around 1.267 GB, so it's best to get to it right away. ​​​.

Rockstar has already sold millions of copies of Grand Theft Auto 6, but many more sales are almost guaranteed on launch day, so it's important for Sony to ensure that the game is protected from hackers. This is a multimillion-dollar release, and if homebrew developers are able to gain access, they might just be able to put it up on the internet for free. Many would surely be interested, especially considering GTA 6 is not going to be released on PC right away.
