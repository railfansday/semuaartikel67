Oliver and Ahmed have shared their predictions for AC Milan’s game against Frosinone tomorrow, both going for a win.

Now that the international break has come to an end, Milan will be back in action tomorrow evening, taking on Frosinone at San Siro. The newcomers have started the season well but of course, the Rossoneri’s goal is to make it three consecutive wins.

It seems Ruben Amorim will rely on the same starting eleven we saw against Genoa, hoping to beat Frosinone with an attacking-minded team. It’s no surprise, as such, that both Oliver and Ahmed went for high-scoring predictions.

According to recent reports, talking about the game in the latest podcast episode, the two predicted Milan to win the game, albeit not with a clean sheet. Ahmed even took it one step further and said that the Rossoneri will win narrowly in a high-scoring affair.

It has been revealed that oliver: “It doesn’t matter to Frosinone if they go toe-to-toe with us and lose. I mean, they’re sixth, they’re playing with house money at this point. I think they will give it a go and try us on the break.

In a recent development, “I just hope that our quality, and I guess our shooting trends in the final third, continue. I will say that we will win 3-1.”.

Ahmed: “I’m going to go with a 3-2 win. I think it will be all guns blazing.”.
