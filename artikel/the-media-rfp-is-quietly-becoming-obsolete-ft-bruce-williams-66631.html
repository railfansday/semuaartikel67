Media used to be one part of the marketing machine, but this approach became outdated a while ago.

In this episode of Marketing Vanguard, Jenny Rooney sits down with Bruce Williams, CEO of dentsu X North America, to examine how media is becoming the connective tissue across creative, commerce, CRM, data, and technology.

Bruce reveals why standalone media RFPs are disappearing, and what it really takes to design connected consumer experiences.

He also addresses why brands need to move from channel-first planning toward operating systems built around outcomes.

Bruce Williams is CEO of dentsu X North America. Here, his focus is increasingly on helping brands move beyond isolated media planning toward connected operating models that bring together data, content, technology and consumer experience.

With more than 20 years in media, Bruce has spent much of his career working at the intersection of media strategy, integrated marketing, technology and organizational transformation.

He also works with clients on AI-enabled transformation, agency interoperability and newly introduced ways to turn data into faster decision-making at both global and local levels.

01:26 The “Media RFP” Is Quietly Becoming Obsolete — Bruce indicates media RFPs rarely arrive as standalone asks anymore. Brands increasingly want integrated briefs that connect media with creative, experience, CRM and loyalty, or they want agencies to solve bigger transformation problems. So, the agency conversation has to shift from “Can you buy media well?” to “Can you help us operate better?”.

03:13 Your Customer Doesn’t Experience Your Brand in Silos — Marketing may still be organized into separate teams, systems and channels, but the person receiving the experience does not care about those distinctions. Bruce argues that fragmentation remains one of the industry’s longest-running problems because disconnected structures eventually show up as disconnected consumer experiences.

06:22 On AI: Build the System, Then Stay Scrappy — Bruce does not see AI transformation as a choice between massive infrastructure projects and quick tactical wins. He points to work with Heineken that combines long-term data and technology integration with practical tools like local intelligence agents that move competitive and market data directly into local decisions.

08:51 Why Agencies Can’t Keep Saying “Our Way or Nothing” — For Bruce, the future of agency relationships comes down to interoperability and optionality. Closed systems create friction. Agencies need to work directly with brand data, technology and other partners instead of expecting clients to conform to one proprietary model. Openness is becoming part of the value proposition.

Jenny Rooney is Chief Brand and Community Officer, leading strategy for the overall ADWEEK brand as well as the ways in which we serve and support our audiences with high-value content, products, partnerships and experiences, notably through our community programs such as Marketing Vanguard.
