Taylor Swift appeared to celebrate Turkey Day away from the throngs of Bills Mafia.

Swift, 36, seemingly skipped watching husband Travis Kelce and the Kansas City Chiefs play in Buffalo against the Bills on Thursday, November 26.

It has been revealed that her absence continued a trend of the pop star deciding against traveling to road games as a member of Chiefs Kingdom. Swift has attended every Chiefs home game in person this season.

The Bills led the Chiefs, 7-3, at halftime at the time of publication.

Earlier this week, New York Knicks superstar Jalen Brunson exclusively discussed Swift’s sports fandom with Us, which came on the heels of his teammate, Tyler Kolek, arguing that her support of the Knicks during the 2026 NBA Finals was “performative.”.

Brunson, 30, said Swift is “obviously” a welcome member of the team’s fanbase.

According to recent reports, “I can’t believe I have to say this,” Brunson noted. “But I didn’t say what was said. Being one of the leaders of the team — the captain of the team — I guess I now have to help somebody out. Yes, she’s a member of the Knicks fandom. Yes, she is.”.

Kelce recently reflected on Swift showing up to her first Chiefs game in the early stages of their relationship in September 2023.

Sources indicate that “I’m a lucky man,” the tight end told ESPN in a Wednesday, November 18, profile. “The fans in general make it so hospitable for everybody, not even just my family.”.

Swift and Kelce got married at New York City’s Madison Square Garden on July 3.

When Swift does show up to Chiefs games at Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium, it becomes nearly impossible to ignore — which beauty influencer and podcaster Ashlie Molstad explained after getting tickets “literally right in front of where Taylor was sitting” for the Chiefs game against the Los Angeles Chargers on October 18.

“We go to our seats. Everybody is in our seats,” Molstad explained via her Instagram Story. “We’re like, ‘Hey, we’re right here. We’re seats five and six.’ And one girl that was standing there with a sign, she’s like, ‘Oh, damn it. You guys were supposed to be late.’ We’re like, ‘Well, we’re not. We’re here.’ They were just annoying. They’re in our face the whole time. People didn’t even look at the field. They’re just standing watching the suite.”.

Industry observers note that she added, “Next thing we know, three massive ass dudes come in and they are there for security for Taylor. Then you just kind of start observing what the f*** is going on and what that world is like. [The security team] is head on a swivel as if they’re Secret Service, right? I mean, she is the most famous, at least American, celebrity that we have right. Anyway, there’s these three men and they’re very friendly to us. They’re very nice. But they are very locked into…they are not watching the game. They’re watching people. It was kind of wild to see how many people are constantly looking.”.
