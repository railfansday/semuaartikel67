Officials at Delhi airport stopped Saurav Das from boarding flight to Britain, where students have invited him to speak.

A leader of India’s youth-led Cockroach movement has asked the Delhi high court to give him permission to fly to the UK after immigration officials at Delhi airport stopped him boarding a flight early on Tuesday morning.

Industry observers note that the court is scheduled to hear Saurav Das’s case on Thursday morning. His lawyer asked the court for a quick hearing because Das has by now already missed some of his engagements at British universities and can only attend the other events on his itinerary if he is allowed to fly out as soon as possible.
