Anthropic is heading toward one of Wall Street’s biggest IPOs, and Bitcoin’s price could face another battle for institutional capital. The Claude developer is preparing to list on Nasdaq under ticker ANT tomorrow, after setting an indicative price range of $70–$78 per share.

Recent findings suggest that the timing is difficult for Bitcoin. Nvidia has just delivered another blockbuster earnings report, reinforcing the scale of the AI investment boom.

Industry observers note that anthropic IPO prospectus puts 825 million shares on offer, with an indicative price range of $70–$78. At the upper end, the public offering could raise up to $64.35 billion.

The company is also arranging a separate $26 billion private placement involving major technology partners. Nvidia and Google have each committed $10 billion, with AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC participating. Combined, the IPO and private placement could bring Anthropic about $84.6 billion in net proceeds at the midpoint of the price range.

That makes the Anthropic price particularly key as investors prepare for Wednesday’s pricing decision. Six-times oversubscription suggests demand substantially exceeds the shares available.

A price above $78 would then again strengthen the signal that institutional buyers are willing to pay more for exposure to the AI industry. The anticipated November 19 debut could also produce another explosive first-day trading session.

Sources indicate that anthropic’s growth story helps explain the appetite. Its annualized revenue run rate reportedly reached $81 billion by October 31, while New Street Research projects revenue could exceed $500 billion by 2030.

According to recent reports, bitcoin has already suffered a substantial decline ahead of Anthropic’s debut. BTC fell 23% during the 50 days preceding Anthropic’s expected listing. That closely resembles the 19% decline during the 50 days before SpaceX began trading in June.

Yet, the SpaceX comparison becomes more interesting when examining what happened after its debut. SpaceX introduced its record-breaking IPO at $135 per share on June 12. Shares closed their first trading session at $160.95, gaining 19.2%.

In a recent development, the stock subsequently climbed nearly 40% during its first week, briefly pushing its valuation above $2 trillion. Bitcoin did not collapse during the listing. It was trading around $63,500, remaining almost flat over 24 hours. Crypto markets eventually recovered alongside the wider risk-on move.

According to recent reports, it was somewhat expected; a $64 billion public offering creates a substantial fresh destination for investors. So, even a small shift in portfolio allocations could matter when crypto liquidity is already under pressure.

Nvidia has given the AI investment narrative another powerful boost. The chipmaker reported third-quarter revenue of $115.84 billion, beating analyst expectations of $109.11 billion. Revenue increased 103% year over year, while its fourth-quarter revenue forecast reached $130 billion, plus or minus 3%. Nvidia shares gained 4% in extended trading following the results.

Recent findings suggest that the earnings beat reinforces the investment case behind Anthropic’s IPO. AI infrastructure spending continues expanding, and investors have another opportunity to buy into that growth through public equity markets.

There is an interesting twist: Nvidia itself is committing $10 billion to Anthropic’s private placement. The same manufacturer delivering the latest earnings catalyst is helping finance one of the largest anticipated stock market debuts.

This creates a difficult competitive environment for Bitcoin. Institutional investors can now choose between established AI infrastructure leaders, rapidly growing AI developers, and crypto exposure.

Sources indicate that anthropic’s debut is more than another technology listing. It could become a defining test of Bitcoin’s ability to compete for investor attention during the AI boom.

Sources indicate that if Anthropic prices above $78 and its shares rally strongly after the debut, Bitcoin could come under further pressure. But if the IPO excitement fades after its debut, crypto could regain some of the attention at the moment flowing toward AI stocks.
