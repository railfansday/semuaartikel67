Recent findings suggest that the NFL is flexing its scheduling freedom, moving the Week 12 Las Vegas Raiders at Cleveland Browns matchup from 1 p.m. ET to "Sunday Night Football." The Fresh England Patriots were originally scheduled to play the Los Angeles Chargers at SoFi Stadium in prime time that week, but that matchup will now move to 4:05 p.m. ET.

Recent findings suggest that tennessee Titans at Jacksonville Jaguars, which was originally scheduled for 4:05 p.m. ET that week moves to 1 p.m. ET.

In a recent development, the Raiders-Browns matchup will feature Las Vegas's newest quarterback, Fernando Mendoza. The No. 1 overall pick took over after Vegas (4-6) sent Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings at the trade deadline last Tuesday, and Week 12 will mark the first prime-time matchup of his career. Mendoza completed 17 of 29 passes for 143 yards in his first career start on Sunday, which came in a 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks.

As for the Browns, they moved to 6-4 last week following a remarkable comeback win over the Houston Texans. The 17-0 deficit Deshaun Watson and Co. overcame made it the second-largest comeback win at home in franchise history, while the Browns are now a perfect 4-0 in their home stadium for the first time since 1963. Cleveland right now holds the last wild-card spot in the AFC (No. 7 seed), behind the 6-3 Pittsburgh Steelers and 7-2 Denver Broncos.

The 5-5 Patriots are being flexed out of prime time following their disastrous 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich. The team reportedly fears that quarterback Drake Maye, who suffered a lower-leg injury early in the game, could be sidelined for an extended period of time. The injury could force last year's MVP runner-up to miss the remainder of the season. Newly introduced England's Week 12 opponent, the Chargers, actually just tallied their first win of the season on Monday night, defeating Jacoby Brissett's Baltimore Ravens, 23-20.

The NFL implemented this "flexible scheduling" rule in 2006. For "Sunday Night Football," they can flex up to two games between Weeks 5-10 and any week during Weeks 11-17.
