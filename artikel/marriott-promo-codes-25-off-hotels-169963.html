And when I’m traveling for personal reasons, I’m always scouring Marriott's website to see what stays they have on offer for my destination of choice.

According to recent reports, as the umbrella firm behind popular hotel brands like Ritz-Carlton, Westin, Le Méridien, St. Regis, Edition, Sheraton, and so many more, Marriott’s portfolio has properties for every traveler—and every budget. And if you, like me, are always looking for the best deal when planning your next vacation, you’re in luck, because Marriott is offering a number of promo codes and exclusive discounts this Black Friday weekend, and I rounded up the best of the best below. Itching to book a trip? Boy, do I have good news for you. Marriott's Black Friday sale is live and includes deals from more than 7,500 participating hotels and resorts. Members get up to 25% off stays when booking in the app, while nonmembers and members booking online can get up to 15% off (becoming a member couldn't be easier, so get those extra savings!). Book by Tuesday, December 2, for stays through May 2, 2027, and use promo code QRI. It's worth noting, resort stays can be booked for any days of the week, while hotels are limited to weekend stays. You'll need to be locked in to the trip too: Rates are non-cancellable, non-changeable, and non-refundable.
