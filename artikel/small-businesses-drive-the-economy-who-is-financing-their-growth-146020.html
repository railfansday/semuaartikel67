Small businesses drive the economy. Who is financing their growth?

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (115 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that when a business looks to expand its operations or invest in new equipment, its growth often depends on access to the right financing. Across America, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that from 2013 to 2023, small businesses contributed 55% of the country’s total net job creation. Between March 2023 and March 2024, they accounted for 88.9% of net job growth, according to the U.S. Small Business Administration’s Office of Advocacy. These figures highlight the vital role small businesses play in supporting the economy. Yet, these small businesses that increasingly help the economy grow are the ones still struggling to get the support that they need.
