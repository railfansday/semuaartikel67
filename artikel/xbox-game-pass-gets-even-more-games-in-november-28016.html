Microsoft has revealed a handful of new additions to the Game Pass library, including a kid-friendly game to kick things off and a sneak peek at some sporty fun for the first week of December. These announcements typically come in two waves, with a sneak peek split between them, so this list isn't complete yet. The first game on the way is Paw Patrol: Dino World. If you're looking for action, Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 is worth checking out. All the thrill of long-distance sniping, with a surprising amount of close-quarters stealth gameplay as well. Also recommended is Motorslice, where you'll get to parkour through the ruins of a brutalist megastructure, climb massive bosses in tense fights, and hunt down living construction equipment. Starting in December, you can get Freestyle Football 2, which is an online multiplayer soccer game built around 5v5 matches. We already have that game in the form of Rematch from Sifu developer Sloclap, but if you want a different spin on that soccer idea, take a quick look.
