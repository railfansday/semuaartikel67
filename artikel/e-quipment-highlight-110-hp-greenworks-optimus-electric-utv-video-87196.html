Electric power tool brand Greenworks is expanding its battery-forward lineup in a huge way with the launch of OPTIMUS, an all-new, go-anywhere electric side-by-side utility vehicle that packs over 100 equivalent hp and a 40 mph top speed.

First shown at Equip Exposition 2026 in Kentucky last month, the newly redesigned Greenworks OPTIMUS UTV is the biggest, baddest, and newest addition to the organization’s battery-backed commercial ecosystem. Greenworks claims the newly introduced SxS brings together the brand’s purpose-built equipment and power platforms and is designed to work across the demands of its professional property maintenance customers.

According to recent reports, “OPTIMUS has always been about pushing battery power beyond what the industry expects, and the UTV is a natural extension of that vision,” notes Yin Chen, CEO of Greenworks. “We’re bringing professionals more ways to put battery power to work, from the equipment they use to maintain properties to the utility vehicles and charging systems that support their entire operation.”.

Greenworks’ OPTIMUS is built around the manufacturer’s 400V high-voltage electrical platform and mates a pair of compact electric motors that combine for 110 equivalent (?) hp. Those motors draw power from a 19 kWh lithium-ion battery pack with enough efficiency to hit a 40 MPH top speed and cover up to 68 miles of range under typical operating conditions.

According to recent reports, when it’s time to haul, OPTIMUS offers buyers a 2,500 lb. towing capacity and 1,300 lb. payload capacity along with 11 full inches of ground clearance and a beefy, 6,000 lb. electric winch. Independent suspension, oversized all-terrain tires, torque vectoring differentials, and multiple terrain modes round out the spec sheet and a picture of a machine that’s designed to handle some seriously demanding off-road applications.

Looks expensive but for a gov or company leasing or buying a new utv probably price completive with less headache.
