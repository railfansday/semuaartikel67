Martin Ødegaard sends clear message to Arsenal amid Bayern Munich transfer rumours

🌐 Source: Readarsenal • 📅 14 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (576 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Martin Ødegaard has spoken out about the speculation surrounding his future.
  • The captain has also admitted that talks about a fresh deal have not yet begun.
  • Bayern Munich are being linked with an interest in the midfielder according to reports from Germany.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Martin Ødegaard has spoken out about the speculation surrounding his future. He has stated he would like to stay at Arsenal for the future.

The captain has also admitted that talks about a fresh deal have not yet begun. Ødegaard is currently under contract until the summer of 2028. This leaves Arsenal with an vital decision to make over the coming months.

Bayern Munich are being linked with an interest in the midfielder according to reports from Germany. The Bundesliga giants are noted to be keeping their eyes on the player’s situation.

Speaking while on international duty with Norway, Ødegaard was quick to dismiss the speculation. He made it clear he is settled in north London and sees his future being at the Emirates Stadium.

There is no suggestion that the 27-year-old is seeking a move. Although comments do highlight a contract problem that Arsenal will be keen to resolve in the short term.

The Norwegian’s laid-back attitude should be encouraging for supporters.

Recent findings suggest that he has made it clear that he is happy at the club and wants to stay here for the time being. This means that there is no need to panic.

However, Arsenal will have to get him to sign on the dotted line. Leaving his captain in the last year of his contract with no progress would cause an unnecessary distraction.

Sources indicate that since his permanent move from Real Madrid in 2021, Ødegaard has become a key player in Mikel Arteta’s plans. He has grown into a team leader and is one of the most influential players in the dressing room.

Sources indicate that his importance has been shown by his inclusion in all 10 of Arsenal’s Premier League games so far this season. He has scored three goals.

This is a major improvement on last season, where he was hampered by injuries and failed to make much of an impact in front of goal.

It wouldn’t be a shock to see Bayern Munich show interest in Ødegaard, but don’t take this as a sign he is close to a move.

There is little reason for Arsenal to consider losing their captain. He brings so much more to the team than just his technical ability and has been relied upon by Arteta to lead by example.

The midfielder was also pivotal in the 1-0 win over Hull City on 7 November, scoring the winner in the 72nd minute to give the league leaders three crucial points.

Industry observers note that arsenal have put themselves in a good position in the title race. Keeping their key players happy will be vital in the second half of the campaign.

The Gunners will be focused on their game against Newcastle United on 21st November.

Arsenal fans have no real reason to panic at the moment, with the captain wanting to stay at the club and his comments suggesting he is happy.

According to recent reports, however, it would be a good idea for the club to try and ensure his long-term future at the club. Ødegaard is still an vital part of Arteta’s strategy, and ensuring he stays at the club would be a smart move.
