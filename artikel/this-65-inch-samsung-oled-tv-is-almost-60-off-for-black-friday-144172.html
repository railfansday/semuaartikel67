This is one of those rare opportunities that don't come often in the consumer tech space: Right now, you can get a 65-inch OLED TV from Samsung for $899.99 (originally $2,099.99) from Best Buy during its Black Friday sale. This 2026 TV is considered an "entry-level" OLED, but it's still an incredible value for the money and the lowest price it has ever had.

If you've never owned an OLED before, there are some things you need to know to make sure it's a good fit for you. Perhaps most key is that they don't get as bright as QLEDs or LED TVs. Since this is an entry-level OLED, it likewise won't reach as high brightness as more premium OLEDs. This makes it less suitable for viewing in very bright rooms. There is also no Dolby Vision support (though there is support for HDR10+, HDR10, and HLG). But that is where the cons end.

The S84H offers the near-perfect black levels you can expect from any OLED TV, making the contrast look incredible. The pixel-level dimming and viewing angles are also on par with other high-end OLEDs, and this TV also offers great features for gamers, including 4K support at 120Hz across all of its HDMI 2.1 ports, a variable refresh rate, and auto low latency mode.

Sources indicate that OLED TVs are not normally priced under $1,000, especially newer models like this one. It's an incredible value for the money. The last time a deal like this popped up was in February, and before that, last Christmas, but for the 2025 version of this TV. If you're reading this after the sale has ended, you'll likely see the deal again in the future—perhaps even next month.
