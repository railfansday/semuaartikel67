Interesting comments from this BBC Sport pundit on Newcastle United.

Chris Sutton speaking as United prepare to play their fifteenth match of the 2026-27 season.

Newcastle United up against Brighton in the Premier League.

A 2.05pm kick-off on Sunday afternoon.

Matthias Jaissle hoping to see his team build on that excellent second half performance and deserved point against league leaders Arsenal.

The BBC Sport pundit declaring ahead Sunday: “I was at Newcastle’s draw with Arsenal last week and, in fairness to Magpies boss Matthias Jaissle, he changed things around at half-time and got his reward.”.

Chris Sutton adding: “Jaissle needed that half of football, because I don’t think the Newcastle fans have been too convinced by his approach, but this is another very tough game for him.”.

The BBC Sport pundit with zero doubts though for what will happen with Newcastle United this weekend, forecasting a 3-1 defeat at the Amex Stadium: “Brighton probably still can’t believe they lost to Hull last week – but lightning is not going to strike twice.”.

You can’t disagree with Chris Sutton predicting Sunday as a home victory.

Brighton are huge favourites to win and they aren’t second in the table by accident.

However, last weekend showed just how unpredictable football can be.

Brighton totally dominated Hull City but then suffered a late red card and two goals for the home side to beat the Seagulls.

At St James’ Park, Arsenal totally dominated the first half and led at the break, yet in the end were lucky to escape with a point after Newcastle United were by far the better team in the second 45.
