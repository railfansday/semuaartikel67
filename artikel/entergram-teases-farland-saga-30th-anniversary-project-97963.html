Entergram has teased a newly introduced Farland Saga project in celebration of the series’ 30th anniversary.

Recent findings suggest that in a tweet, the manufacturer stated, “1996 to 2026. After 30 years, a new story is set in motion. The Farland Saga 30th anniversary project is now underway.”.

Entergram did not share any further information.

In June 2025, City Connection, in cooperation with Entergram, released Farland Saga I & II Saturn Tribute for PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC in Japan, which features ports of the 1996-released Farland Saga and its 1998 sequel Farland Saga: Toki no Michishirube.
