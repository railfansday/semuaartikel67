A newly introduced series based on the 2006 film Aquamarine is heading to Disney Channel and Disney+, with the movie’s original stars Emma Roberts and Jake McDorman coming on board, Disney confirmed in a press release.

In addition to diving back into their roles of Claire and Raymond, Roberts and McDorman are also suiting up as executive producers on the series.

In a recent development, the TV show will follow Kenzi Richardson’s Coral, who moves to a seaside town, according to the press release, and begins to unravel the truth about her mother’s disappearance. In a twist that’ll really have you flipping out, Cora learns her mother is a mermaid just as her own magical powers begin to surface. Briella Guiza, Theodore Dalton and Annika Reese will also star in the show.

In a recent development, the new series comes 20 years after Aquamarine hit the significant screen starring Roberts JoJo and Sara Paxton the titular character. In the movie, Paxton plays a mermaid who washes ashore and promises a wish to best friends Roberts and JoJo if they can help her prove true love does exist—specifically in the form of McDorman’s dreamy lifeguard—so she can avoid an arranged marriage by her merman father.

Roberts first made a splash in June, expressing how thrilled she was to be a part of Disney’s Aquamarine show.

“I’m so excited for the fresh series,” she told People at the time. “I absolutely cannot wait.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m so excited to bring Claire back to life as a grownup,” the 35-year-old added. “I remember when I read the pilot, I literally got choked up. I didn’t think I would have such an emotional reaction to it, but I definitely got a little teary-eyed.”.

And looking back on it now, the American Horror Story actress could hardly believe that fans are literally giving her compliments all these years later.

“There are certain projects that it just feels like lightning in a bottle,” she stated. “It feels really cool that we were all just a bunch of girls in Australia making a movie. And now it seems like it meant a lot to people, which means a lot to us.”.
