Recent findings suggest that for the better part of this year, Zcash (ZEC) gained notable traction, which enabled it to rise from a low of $188 (witnessed on Feb.6) to a massive multi-year high of $1,684 (recorded on Sept.26). Linking further possibilities, capital market analyst Matt Hougan highlighted a connection between Bitcoin and the privacy-focused cryptocurrency. In a podcast aired Sunday, the Bitwise CIO (chief investment officer) outlined a possible case for rotating Bitcoin capital into Zcash.

In the interview, Hougan revealed that privacy is the latest notable component in on-chain finance. According to him, this element is becoming increasingly crucial, particularly in the crypto landscape where data can be visible on-chain.

As traditional assets move on-chain, institutional investors may miss the element of confidentiality in the DeFi world, he narrated. He said this pursuit may prompt institutions to adopt privacy-focused crypto solutions like Zcash, Monero, and others.

Talk of a potential Bitcoin-to-Zcash rotation picked up momentum this week from the Bitwise executive, sparking further excitement in the already optimistic crypto narrative. The basis of this call is that as institutions continue moving RWAs onchain, they will require privacy-preserving DeFi protocols to protect user data. That’s where Zcash comes into the spotlight.

Hougan anticipates that as OG crypto holders diversify from older crypto assets into privacy solutions, institutional investors, especially Bitcoin whales, will join the wave. He, however, foresees a selective rotation rather than a complete institutional surrender.

Hougan’s optimistic views signal significant market shifts. His comments show that as institutional capital enters the on-chain space, the complete transparency of public blockchains may pose a major obstacle for serious players.

The well-known analyst highlighted the importance of confidential mechanisms, specifically Zcash.

Sources indicate that the Bitcoin-to-Zcash rotation narrative appears to be on the horizon. Recently, several early Bitcoin whales urged early Bitcoin investor Dan Held to buy Zcash months before ZEC topped $1,000 in September. Also, earlier last month, recognized Bitcoin whales, the Winklevoss brothers, pointed out their radical shift to Zcash.
