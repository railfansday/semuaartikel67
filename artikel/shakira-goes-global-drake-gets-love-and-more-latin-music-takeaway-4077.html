Shakira Goes Global, Drake Gets Love, and More Latin Music Takeaways from the 2027 Grammy Nominations

Recent findings suggest that the Recording Academy doesn’t always get it right when it comes to Latin music, but lately they’ve been trying. Last year, voters from the Latin Recording Academy were able to cast ballots in the standard edition, making for a more diverse voting pool. It’s a move that’s already paid off. Earlier this year, at the 2026 awards, Bad Bunny made history with the first Album of the Year win for a full Spanish-language album. Now, with the 2027 Grammy nominations, one of the biggest genres in the U.S. is still front and center — even if some of the nods are perplexing. Let’s take a look at all of the Latin music highlights from this year’s nominations.

Rosalía just made history at the Latin Grammys when she became the first woman to win Album of the Year three times. Now, the Spanish singer is the second-most-decorated nominee for the 2027 Grammys, and she could follow Bad Bunny’s lead with another historic AOTY win. If Rosalía’s Lux snags the highest honor of the night, she would become the first woman to win in the category with a Spanish-language project.

There were a handful of Latin music first-time nominees across categories. Ryan Castro secured two nods in the Best Música Urbana Album, one for his solo LP Hopi Sendé and another for his collaborative album with J Balvin, Omerta. Meanwhile, Argentine musician Milo J earned recognition in the Best Latin Song and Best Latin Rock or Alternative Album categories for his Latin Grammy-winning project La vida era Más Corta. Venezuelan American singer Elena Rose also received her first-ever Grammy nod, in the Best Latin Pop Album category. About that….

… Rose’s nomination leads us to the unfortunate catch-all that the Best Latin Pop Album category became this year. As we stated, the Academy doesn’t always get it right when it comes to Latin music. Nowhere is that more blatant than this category, which is jam-packed with an array of popular Latin artists who make distinct music from each other. Karol G’s alternative album No Me Arrepiento de Sentir Tanto makes some sense here, as does Mon Laferte’s Femme Fatale — but Carín León’s Muda runs the gamut of genres from norteño to salsa and soul. It makes no sense here, and Rawayana’s and Elena Rose’s genre-bending albums don’t really, either. Rather than find a way to honor these impressive works and the widely varied Latin genres they’re pushing forward, the Academy seems to be stuffing them under one confusing umbrella. Recent findings suggest that drake is up for Best Latin Song. Yes, you read that right: The rap superstar has received a nod for his Spanish-language feature on Karol G’s “Ahí.” It’s surprising in many ways, but especially considering Drake’s longstanding differences with the Academy; the last time he won a Grammy for one of his own songs, in 2019, he gave a speech about how little the awards mean to him, and he’s chosen not to submit his own work since then. The fact that the Academy nominated “Ahí” rather than Karol’s Bruno Mars collaboration “Still” is also a bit of a head-scratcher. But now Champagne Papi will go up against acts like Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Rosalía, Milo J, and Jorge Drexler. One of Latin music’s most sought-after collaborators is being recognized by the Academy once more. Edgar Barrera nabbed a third consecutive nod for Songwriter of the Year, Non-Classical. He’s up against a field that includes Amy Allen and Theron Thomas, both of whom previously beat him. But after working on some of the year’s biggest Latin music projects and writing for Gracie Abrams, could this finally be the year the Recording Academy honors Barrera’s songwriting power?

There’s no denying Latin music’s worldwide takeover — just look at what Shakira and Anitta are doing. Both stars submitted their music beyond the Latin music categories, and they were recognized for continuing to expand their reach. Anitta’s latest album, Equilibrivm, received a nod for Best Global Music Album, while Shakira’s World Cup hit “Dai Dai” was nominated for Best Global Music Performance.
