Kentucky Rep. James R. Comer, whose role as chairman of the House Oversight Committee gave him a powerful platform to investigate Democrats, including former Presidents Joe Biden and Bill Clinton, has launched a run for governor next year.

According to recent reports, the Republican, who was just elected to a sixth full term representing Kentucky’s 1st District, is seeking to succeed term-limited Democratic Gov. Andy Beshear. Kentucky is one of three states holding off-year gubernatorial elections in 2027, alongside Louisiana and Mississippi.

In his announcement at a tele-town hall Monday night, Comey emphasized pocketbook complications, pledging to eliminate the state income tax and boost job-creation in Louisville.

“If we get that income tax to zero, people are going to have more money to spend, and we're going to be able to attract more good-paying jobs to Kentucky, which will be able to keep our best and brightest in the state,’’ he said.

As the leader of the House Oversight panel, Comer has been at the forefront in the fight over the release of records related to convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. (Last week, the committee released a report concluding that the federal government mishandled criminal probes into Epstein’s activities.).

Comer is a staunch ally of President Donald Trump, who carried Kentucky by 31 points in 2024, and his hard-nosed style of politics is conveyed in a soft-spoken Southern drawl.

It has been revealed that known back home as Jamie, Comer has deep roots in rural Monroe County, dating back to the late 1700s. Prior to coming to Congress, he operated a farm before serving 11 years in the Kentucky House. In 2011, he was elected as the commonwealth’s commissioner of agriculture.

Sources indicate that he made an unsuccessful run for governor in 2015, losing the Republican primary by 83 votes to Matt Bevin, who went on to win the general election. The following year, Comer easily won election to the House from his western Kentucky district, succeeding Republican Ed Whitfield.

Industry observers note that comer has moved promptly to consolidate GOP support for his gubernatorial bid. He already has the backing of Kentucky Sen. Rand Paul and Sen.-elect Andy Barr. Michael Adams, Kentucky’s term-limited secretary of state who’d been seen a potential contender, gave Comer an early endorsement this summer.

Recent findings suggest that while Comer enters the race as the Republican front-runner, he won’t have the field to himself. Other declared hopefuls include local prosecutor Rick Hardin and distiller Charles “Bruiser” Martin, who appeared on the Discovery Channel show “Moonshiners: Master Distiller.”.

Industry observers note that while Kentucky has in recent decades grown reliably Republican at the federal level, the GOP has won just two gubernatorial elections there over the past half century. Two high-profile Democrats have already released bids to keep the governor’s mansion in party hands: Lt. Gov. Jacqueline Coleman and former state House Majority Leader and Beshear adviser Rocky Adkins, who ran for governor in 2019.

In a statement Tuesday, the Democratic Governors Association dismissed Comer as “a job-killing, pro-war politician who looks down on everyday Kentuckians.”.
