Nick Woltemade has scored in each of his last two Serie A appearances and already has nine goal contributions with Juventus this season, ironically outperforming any Newcastle attacker so far in 2026-27.

Industry observers note that germany international Woltemade played a key role in Juventus’ 4-0 win over strugglers Venezia on Sunday. The scoreline doesn’t tell the whole story about the Old Lady’s performance, as the first half ended 0-0 and drew boos from the home crowd in Turin.

Woltemade was introduced at half-time and made an immediate impact, scoring and assisting in 15 minutes.

Woltemade recently opened his Serie A account, finding the net for the first time in Italy’s top flight in the previous round against Fiorentina, and now has five goals and four assists in 16 appearances across all competitions for the Old Lady this season.

The 24-year-old has been particularly prolific in the Europa League, with three goals and two assists in four matches.

Ironically, Woltemade’s numbers so far are better than those of any Newcastle attacker so far this season.

In a recent development, left winger Harvey Barnes is the Magpies’ most prolific player this season, with five goals and two assists in 14 matches, while central midfielder Joe Willock has three goals and two assists in 14. Right winger Anthony Elanga has two goals in ten games.

As for centre-forwards, Yoane Wissa has one goal and one assist in 14 games, William Osula has one goal and two assists in eight matches, while Matias Fernandez-Pardo hasn’t yet scored in seven appearances.

Woltemade joined Juventus on loan from Newcastle last summer, but the Old Lady does not hold an option to make his move permanent.
