Andoni Iraola has tried his best to turn Liverpool's fortunes around at Anfield since his arrival.

Last term, the club were performing well below expectations and ended up finishing fifth in the Premier League.

In a normal season, managing to qualify for the Champions League would be seen as a decent outcome but, after spending £450 million on new players and winning the title just a season before, it was a bad performance.

Their lack of form resulted in Arne Slot being sacked and left Iraola to deal with all of his shortcomings.

Most of the time, things have gone well but, for them to get even better, changes are going to have to take place in the upcoming transfer window.

Liverpool would have to sell Dominik Szoboszlai or Ryan Gravenberch to sign Lamine Camara.

Liverpool's midfield has been one of their main problems since 2023.

The July of 2023 was when Fabinho exited Anfield and, since then, the Reds haven't had a natural number six among their ranks.

Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch have all been used there to try and plug the gap.

And, while Gravenberch did fairly well during the Reds' title-winning season, from that point on, he's failed to meet expectations.

That's why in the 2026-27 summer transfer window, fans were sure that a latest number six would be brought in.

Obviously, Richard Hughes and Iraola didn't feel the need to do that, even though the Spaniard's double pivot has been the thing to cause them the most challenges so far.

Several different players were linked as potential options for the Reds to explore but, according to an update from David Lynch via Anfield Index, the Reds would have to sell Gravenberch or Szoboszlai to make room for someone such as Lamine Camara.

"I know the reports, but still struggle to see Lamine Camara to Liverpool in January without a significant departure," he stated.

Recent findings suggest that "Endo doesn’t play at all, more a Gravenberch or Szoboszlai which it’s hard to see, otherwise there’s a questions of minutes. Liverpool don’t like to be over stocked as we know.".

Liverpool would have to spend around €70m to sign Lamine Camara.

Before the 2026-27 season got fully underway, Liverpool were heavily linked with Camara.

In fact, some outlets were even reporting that a deal for the Senegal international was going to be done before the end of the transfer window.

That obviously never ended up happening and now, they're being linked once again with the form of Szoboszlai and Gravenberch raising a fair few questions.

But, for the Reds to sign Camara, it's been reported that a fee worth around €70 million would have to be spent for AS Monaco to even think about letting him go.

Since they rejected his move to Chelsea during the dying embers of the window, he's gone from strength to strength and now, the Ligue 1 outfit are seeing that he's worth a lot of money.

Right now, nothing is decided but, sacrificing someone such as Szoboszlai feels like a big leap, especially for an unproven player in the Premier League.
