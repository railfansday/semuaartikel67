📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hancock 2 could be flying into theaters after all.

Hancock is considered a Will Smith classic by many. It is a dark comedy meets one-man action army meets farcical superhero film, focusing on protagonist John Hancock who is a not-so-super superhero whose actions end up damaging. Hancock changes his tune when someone he saves figures out a way for the "super" hero to improve the public's perception. The Peter Berg-helmed film is co-penned by Vy Vincent Ngo and Breaking Bad's Vince Gilligan, and in addition to Smith, features a leading cast including Charlize Theron, Jason Bateman, and Jae Head.

In an interview with Variety, Berg speaks about the possibility of Hancock 2. According to the director, Smith himself has sent him a script for the sequel that Berg would gladly sign on to. He referred to the script as "incredibly funny." While Berg admitted that "the universe has to line up" for him to be able to do the movie, he thinks that "the time for [him] is right for Will [Smith] to do that role." He then went on to note how much he appreciated Smith and likes working with him. Check out Berg's full quote below:.

“I don’t even know if I’m supposed to say it or not, but Will sent me an incredibly funny, great script for ’Hancock 2.' I would do it. The universe has to line up.

Recent findings suggest that I think the time for me is right for Will to do that role, and for many reasons. I think Will Smith is a really good human being and a very, very talented human being and just like me and I would guess you, not a flawless human being. I’m flawed. I’ve made some mistakes, and I’ll probably make some more. But I love Will Smith, and I always will. And I know a lot of other people do too. So if that ever happens, it would be, I think, a really special film.”.

Sources indicate that there is subtext to what Berg is saying here about Smith. The actor has fallen out of favor in the public eye among many, after his viral and now oft-memed moment of slapping Chris Rock live at the Dolby Theatre at the Oscars in 2022. Since then, Smith has been slowed to make new work, appearing in just a couple of movies, such as Emancipation and the sequel film Bad Boys: Ride or Die. When talking about flaws and making mistakes, Berg is seemingly in defense of Smith.

There would indeed need to be a lot of public forgiveness of Smith in order for Hancock 2 to do well. All that remarked, this script update is a significant step in the positive direction for the film. Hancock 2 has been in talks since 2009, and has gotten a series of disparate updates along the way. In 2025, Smith revealed that Zendaya was in talks for a role in the sequel film, but her involvement has not been confirmed. That said, a strong, developed script and a willing director is certainly a point in Hancock 2's direction.

Recent findings suggest that even with the Smith controversy, there is a good chance that Hancock 2 would have a fairly significant following. After all, the original movie grossed $629.4 million worldwide against an estimated $150 million budget. Smith has also proven his sequel movies can make serious money even in a post-Oscar slap landscape; Bad Boys: Ride or Die made $404 million on a $100 million budget. With a script in hand for Berg and Smith, it sounds like now all that is needed for Hancock 2 is for the stars to better align.
