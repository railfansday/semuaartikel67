Expedia is buying its way toward becoming a one-stop shop for travel sellers and customers, even as the economics of that strategy come under pressure.

Expedia Group agreed Wednesday to acquire Czech online travel agency Kiwi.com, expanding its European flight offering for consumers and B2B partners.

Recent findings suggest that the companies did not disclose the price, and Expedia declined to comment on it when contacted by Skift. Kiwi generated €227 million ($264 million) in 2025 revenue.

The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions. As Skift reported in September, Kiwi’s €100 million ($116 million) convertible bond matures June 28, 2027.

The deal could provide an exit for majority shareholder General Atlantic, which invested in 2019. Shareholder arrangements were not disclosed.

“Combining Kiwi.com's technology with Expedia Group’s scale will strengthen Brand Expedia and our B2B business’ position in air, giving travelers and partners more choice, confidence and value,” Expedia Group CEO Ariane Gorin said in the announcement.
