There's a Black Friday deal that offers Disney+ and Hulu (both with ads) for $4.99 per month for a whole year.

The timing is great for me on this one, taking into account what I personally like to watch. As I mentioned, I cancelled my subscription earlier this fall to save some money, but with Avengers: Doomsday arriving in theaters in a matter of weeks, I'm glad to have access to the various Marvel movies in case I get a hankering to revisit some of them. Maybe I'll rewatch Avengers: Endgame Encore (not just because I love Endgame, but also to revisit those Doomsday scenes).

There are other things on Disney+ that I absolutely love, like the Taylor Swift content, including The Eras Tour: The Final Show, and Folklore: The Long Pond Studios Sessions. There are also Christmas movies like National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation, Home Alone and Die Hard. I've also been meaning to rewatch The Mandalorian and Grogu since seeing it in theaters.

It has been revealed that as for Hulu, I can now watch Alan Ritchson's upcoming action comedy Runner. And after watching You Can See Everything (which is now available on VOD), I'm thinking about checking out The Dropout. Finally, while we still don't know when The Testaments Season 2 will premiere, I know I'm going to want to be signed up once the Handmaid's Tale sequel returns.

Those are just some of the things that jump out at me when I think about how I'm going to get my money's worth out of this fresh subscription. Really not bad for $4.99 per month, I'd say!
