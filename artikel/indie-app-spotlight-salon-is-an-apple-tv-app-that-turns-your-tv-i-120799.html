Sources indicate that you likely have a TV on your wall that generally sits idle throughout the day. While the Apple TV does offer loads of built in screensavers, you might be looking for something a bit more artsy. Salon offers a picture frame experience for the Apple TV, where it has over 1000 open-access paintings to display.

In short, this app replicates the Samsung Frame TV experience but for anyone who owns an Apple TV. As mentioned above, you can choose from over 1000 hand-picked art pieces to choose from the world’s museums, but you can also import your own photos from iCloud.

Sources indicate that salon lets you add a frame to the edge of your photos, or you can display them edge-to-edge if you’d like. You can also add a clock and date to the corner.
