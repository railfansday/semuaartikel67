It has been revealed that losing progress in a game is never fun, but imagine spending 11 hours exploring Grand Theft Auto 6 only to discover that none of it was saved. That's exactly what happened to content creator JasonTheWeen, who had a particularly rough launch day after making one unfortunate mistake with his PlayStation 5.

With around 1.3 million followers on YouTube, the content creator had been streaming an extended session of Rockstar's latest adventure. However, after accidentally turning off his console, he returned to the game only to find that his entire 11-hour save was missing!

According to The Economic Times, he later explained that his foot had accidentally hit the switch on an extension cord, cutting power to his PS5. The moment he realized what had happened was captured live, and his reaction was understandably heartbreaking. Here, take a look:.

Recent findings suggest that visibly devastated, the streamer struggled to hold back tears and repeatedly shouted about the 11 hours he had just lost. He then contacted someone from his team to ask whether there was any way to recover his progress, even if that meant getting back to the beginning of the second chapter rather than having to start the entire adventure again.

Unfortunately, it seems that it wasn’t possible, and he eventually brought a friend to help him rush through the sections he had already completed, hoping to get his marathon back on track.

In a recent development, the exact reason behind the incident remains unclear, but there's a possible explanation involving the game's save system. GTA 6 does feature autosaving, which records progress after completing missions and certain key events. But that doesn't necessarily mean every action is immediately stored.

According to recent reports, one possibility is that JasonTheWeen accidentally turned off his PS5 while the game was writing save data. If that happened, interrupting the process could have corrupted the save file, potentially explaining why so much progress disappeared when he introduced the game again.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 does feature autosaving, which records progress after completing missions and certain crucial events. But that doesn't necessarily mean every action is immediately stored.

It's vital to stress that this is only a theory, and neither Rockstar nor the streamer has confirmed what actually caused the loss. We also don't know whether the save can be recovered, so it would be unfair to draw conclusions about the game's autosave system based on this single incident.

Sources indicate that still, the situation is a good reminder that manually saving your progress is worth the extra few seconds. GTA 6 offers multiple manual save slots, allowing players to keep separate backups instead of relying exclusively on autosaving. This can be particularly useful after completing a long mission or spending hours exploring Leonida.

For anyone planning to sink an entire afternoon into the game, taking a moment to create a manual save might save you from a very unpleasant surprise later!

Unfortunately, losing his progress wasn't the only upsetting moment JasonTheWeen experienced during the stream. Shortly afterward, he heard shouting outside his home and informed viewers that police officers had arrived at his property.

Industry observers note that the incident appears to have been a case of swatting, a dangerous prank involving false emergency reports intended to send police officers to someone's home. It's an especially unpleasant situation for any streamer, and it made an already frustrating day considerably worse.

Sources indicate that following the incident, JasonTheWeen also reached out to Rockstar and Sony on social media, asking whether his lost progress could be recovered. For now, there's no confirmed response from either company about his request or why all the progress was lost.
