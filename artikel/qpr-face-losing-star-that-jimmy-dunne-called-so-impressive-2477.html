QPR have enjoyed uplifting progression under Julien Stephan into contention for a play-off place in the Championship this season, but the West Londoners may now be applying some focus to resisting the threat of transfer interest in one particular starlet from Italian giants AC Milan following fresh reports.

The Hoops have transitioned from mid-table mediocrity to Championship promotion hopefuls after completing what was widely felt to be an exciting summer transfer window, which included the arrivals of Pierce Charles, Dennis Cirkin, Glen Kamara and Tariq Lamptey.

As such, QPR find themselves positioned in eighth place amid the ongoing November international break and, having lost just two of their last ten matches against automatic promotion hopefuls in Birmingham City and Southampton, Stephan's outfit appear firmly capable of continuing to fight it out for a top-eight finish.

Away from the pitch, however, QPR are now facing a latest worry as AC Milan and others hone in their transfer sights on one Loftus Road star who Stephan's players would certainly hate to see depart out of W12.

As reported by Sky Sports' Anthony Joseph on Monday afternoon, QPR prodigy Leon Scarlett is attracting interest from mainland Europe in a development that will leave Hoops supporters worried about losing the club's top young prospect.

Indeed, Joseph has claimed that both AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt have already expressed an interest in the highly-rated 17-year-old's services ahead of the looming January transfer window, with the continental pair reportedly continuing to monitor his situation at Loftus Road.

Scarlett, who has made six senior appearances for the second-tier play-off hopefuls to date since heading across London to join from Arsenal in January of last year, has enjoyed a meteoric rise and is regarded as a future star in the making.

The talented winger made his debut in a 3-0 defeat at Ipswich Town on the final day of the previous season before climbing up Stephan's ranks throughout the summer and appearing in current-campaign clashes against Charlton Athletic, West Bromwich Albion, Preston North End, Derby County and Birmingham.

Scarlett has made quite the impression in some of those encounters, too, and while the ex-Arsenal academy starlet is yet to open his professional goalscoring account, QPR fans are desperate to see more appearances in the coming weeks and months.

That could change if either AC Milan or Frankfurt seal a swoop, though, with Scarlett's star only rising after collecting a pair of caps with Wales' U21 set-up for good measure.

Scarlett has earned rave reviews from supporters, players and coaches alike since being fast-tracked into Stephan's first-team fold, which is precisely why the R's will be eager to avoid losing such an exciting young asset.

Stephan, who knows a thing or two about developing top-grade talent after previously coaching world stars such as Eduardo Camavinga, 2025 Ballon D'Or winner Ousmane Dembele and Manchester City wing-king Jeremy Doku at Rennes, has already talked up Scarlett's quality.

In a recent development, the Frenchman has been keeping a close eye on the Welsh youth international's ever-accelerating development throughout the year, and was left impressed after promoting him to the senior squad in the closing stages of last term. Stephan told the Hounslow Herald: "The last game (Scarlett) did for the U21s, he did very well.

Recent findings suggest that "I saw him in a cup game with the U18s earlier in the season and he did a good first half. So I just wanted to see him during the session with us (the first team). And during the session, he did well.".

Scarlett has excited his teammates, too, with club captain Jimmy Dunne a notable example.

Speaking at a fans forum event back in September, the popular defender also explained: "Just watching Leon Scarlett today he’s so impressive, so bright, and so positive.

"You want to see him do well and thrive.".

Only time will tell, of course, whether the likes of Dunne, Stephan and QPR as a whole can see Scarlett reach his full potential at Loftus Road and become the beneficiaries of the wide-man's talent and footballing ceiling - or if a larger European club will end up reaping the rewards instead.

It's clear that QPR could just need to implement some form of action in order to guarantee Scarlett's longer-term future, with the prodigious forward surely capable of becoming a first-team regular in the next twelve months.

The worry right now will be that Scarlett remains contracted to the scholarship deal he penned under two years ago to join from Arsenal, and can be easily poached until he agrees to sign professional terms.

QPR would surely love to put that sort of contract on the table for Scarlett, and will need to find out exactly where the player's own desires lie in light of emerging interest from a juggernaut of European football and one of the continent's premiere talent-developers in Frankfurt.

QPR fans, how do you feel about AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt's interest in Leon Scarlett? Are you worried that the exciting winger will leave Loftus Road for bigger things already? Let us know your thoughts in the comments below!

Either way, the Championship side are susceptible to losing Scarlett before his Hoops career has really taken off, and it's no surprise that vultures are circling in an effort to exploit that situation and prize away the teenage prodigy.
