Back in April, rumors started floating around online about a new Hulu reality competition show filming in Tuscany, and tbh, I thought the cast looked too good to be true. But at the Hulu Get Real House event on April 22, it was confirmed: We were actually getting a Parker Posey-hosted, mafia-themed reality series from the same producers as The Traitors. Everyone say thank you, Hulu!

Here is how The Mob works: A group of random celebrities and reality stars are shipped to a lavish Italian villa, where they will go head-to-head in a series of mob movie-inspired challenges to win up to $250,000 in cash prizes…all without getting whacked. And when I say random, I mean this cast is truuuuly random. We’re talking Bruno Tonioli, Harry Jowsey, and Chelsea Lazkani all under one roof, just to name a few of the inaugural season’s participants.
