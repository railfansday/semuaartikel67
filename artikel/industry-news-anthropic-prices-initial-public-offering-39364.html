According to recent reports, today Anthropic, PBC priced its initial public offering of its Class A common stock at $83.00 per share. The offering consists of 980,000,000 shares of Class A common stock, 956,351,354 of which are being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 of which are being sold by certain selling stockholders. Anthropic will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.

According to recent reports, the shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.” The offering is expected to close on November 20, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. In addition, Anthropic has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 147,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock.

In a recent development, A registration statement relating to these securities was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 18, 2026.
