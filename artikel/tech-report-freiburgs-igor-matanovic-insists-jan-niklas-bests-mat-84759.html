According to recent reports, freiburg striker Igor Matanovic is convinced that teammate Niklas Beste deliberately set up his match-winning goal against Elversberg on Saturday afternoon. Speaking to Get German Football News in the mixed zone following Freiburg’s narrow 1-0 away victory, the Croatian international rejected the suggestion that Beste’s decisive touch had been accidental.

The 68th-minute goal finally broke the deadlock in an intensely contested encounter at the Kaiserlinde. Austrian defender Philipp Lienhart initiated the move with a long ball towards Beste, who managed to shield possession against Elversberg’s Maximilian Rohr.

It has been revealed that beste’s subsequent layoff appeared somewhat fortuitous, but Matanovic reacted immediately to steer the ball into the bottom right corner. The striker’s ninth goal across all competitions this season ultimately proved sufficient to secure all three points for Julian Schuster’s side.

Sources indicate that the 23-year-old believes that his understanding with Beste, rather than simple good fortune, made the decisive difference. Matanovic pointed to the familiarity between Freiburg’s attacking players, emphasizing that the entire team has developed an instinctive awareness of one another’s movements.

In a recent development, the striker also recalled his earlier observations about the special chemistry within Schuster’s squad. That collective understanding has helped Freiburg negotiate difficult Bundesliga encounters, including Saturday’s hard-fought victory over a spirited Elversberg side.

The latest success follows Freiburg’s 2-0 home victory against Union Berlin before the international break and further strengthens the Breisgau club’s position near the top of the Bundesliga table.

Recent findings suggest that the south Baden side responded to a 1-3 home defeat against Bayern on matchday seven by defeating Wolfsburg in the Pokal. They’ve not lost since, capturing five consecutive victories on the spin.

Industry observers note that “I know that Niki likes making that run,” Matanovic told Get German Football News in the mixed zone. “And the way he laid the ball off, I think a lot of people will say it was accidental. I think he wanted it exactly that way. I’m convinced of that.”.

It has been revealed that “I already told you at the beginning of the season that this team is really special,” the striker continued. “I had the feeling that we had an instinctive understanding. Actually, it’s not just a feeling. We really do have that understanding.”.

Industry observers note that “Everyone knows which runs the other players make, and Niki knows that I’ll naturally follow his run,” Matanovic explained. “He laid the ball off perfectly for me, allowing me to put it away nicely.”.
