Having never played a GTA before, I admittedly struggled a little bit with the controls the very first time I set out in Jason’s 1970 Vapid Ganado in Grand Theft Auto 6. I admittedly wasn’t sure which buttons were the turn signals, so I failed to signal properly a few times.

Even so, I was and am still the best goddamn driver in Leonida.

What isÂ wrong with these people, man? I don’t mean, “oh, people aren’t sticking within the lane lines” or “this guy cut me off,” though that happens a lot, too. I mean the drivers here actively seem to be trying to get people killed.

Industry observers note that for instance, on that same first drive, at one point I was hanging out in a left-turn lane on a green light, yielding appropriately to oncoming traffic and waiting for it to pass so I could finish the turn. As I sat there, Natasha Bedingfield blasting from the radio, I watched a small white van inexplicably veer off the road and slam into the traffic light pole. The van kept going down a side street as if nothing had happened while the traffic light collapsed, dropping its overarching beam directly into traffic. Everyone else kept going, driving over the beam as if it weren’t there. (They were at least honking madly, though!).

Another time, I was driving in a straight line on a straight road in the left lane, minding my own business, when someone came up behind me on the right and immediately tried to change lanes,Â without signaling, mind, directly into the space at the moment occupied by my own car. I laid on the horn but they still bashed me into the median, then had the audacity to exit their vehicle and start yelling at me, as if I had caused the accident! Fuck off!

On one hand, I can’t blame some of these folks for sucking at driving. I’ve had a few near-misses myself thanks to the indecipherability of Leonida’s roadway system. For one thing, the stop lights are too damn small. How am I supposed to know I need to stop so I don’t get T-boned if I can’t even see the traffic light? Sometimes, I can see it, but at a distance can’t tell if it’s green or red, particularly in the daylight. This is a huge problem that LEDOT needs to get on top of right now. Surely there’s some sort of federal law about this that requires road signs to be, I dunno, visible.

According to recent reports, but even beyond that, people are just maniacs. They speed and they swerve. They honk at me when I stop at stop signs or at red lights. No one lets me merge or change lines, even when I’ve been signaling for ages. Sometimes I’ll just be standing in a parking lot and see, over on the main road, someone wildly swerving around and slam into a tree. Is everyone drunk here? Should I be?

According to recent reports, I just think everyone in Leonida needs to go through a mandatory driver’s ed refresher before they continue to go about their business. How about they all go do that, and I’ll stand here and watch over all their nice cars to make sure no one steals them or the stuff in their trunks while they’re gone. Don’t worry, guys. Clearly I am the most responsible driver in Leonida. They’ll be safe with me.
