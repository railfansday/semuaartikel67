Italian sources suggest that Juventus held preliminary talks with Scott McTominay’s agent last summer, but the Scotland international will likely return to the Premier League in 2027 if he fails to sign a contract extension with Napoli over the next few months.

McTominay’s future at Napoli remains uncertain, as the former Manchester United midfielder has yet to reach an agreement with the Partenopei over a latest contract.

The midfielder’s current deal expires in June 2028, but TMW notes that the Serie A side is running out of time to reach an agreement with the 29-year-old.

Sources indicate that according to the report, McTominay requested a latest €12 million-a-year contract last season. Even if he has now lowered his demands to €7-8 million per season, the Partenopei are still unwilling to match them.

Talks will continue over the coming months, but if the two parties fail to reach an agreement before the summer, McTominay’s exit will become increasingly likely.

According to the report, Juventus held preliminary talks with McTominay last summer, but it soon became clear that the Bianconeri could not afford his hefty wage demands.

McTominay has not ruled out staying at Napoli, but if he fails to reach an agreement with the club before next summer, a move to the Premier League will become increasingly likely, as most English clubs could afford his salary requests.
