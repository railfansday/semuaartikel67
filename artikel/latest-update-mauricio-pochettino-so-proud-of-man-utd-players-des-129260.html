Mauricio Pochettino was proud of Manchester United’s performance and his players’ early progress in the action-packed 2-2 draw at Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

The former Argentina international began his reign with a chastening 4-0 loss at rivals Liverpool on Sunday, when Leny Yoro’s early – and subsequently overturned – red card was the catalyst for Anfield agony.

More frustration followed on Wednesday, when Senne Lammens gifted Sporting a penalty converted by Luis Suarez shortly after denying him from the spot before Bruno Fernandes’ double with the help of a bobble put United in front.

But Suarez would level in the second half after nudging Lammens when attempting to gather a high ball and Pochettino’s hopes of a first win ended when home goalkeeper Kaique Pereira’s own goal was ruled out.

Szymon Marciniak adjudged Patrick Dorgu to have fouled him having been sent to the monitor on a night when the referee’s early whistle meant a disallowed first half Amad Diallo goal could not be overturned.

Youri Tielemans was incorrectly booked for his role in that incident and picked up a second booking at the end of an eventful night that saw Pochettino focus on the positives in Portugal.

In a recent development, “I am so proud of our players, my players,” Pochettino told TNT Sports. “It was a really tough game.

It has been revealed that “I like the team during 90 minutes, the personality, the character. And it’s true that it’s difficult to find some game that (you will) be more punished.

“We were punished a lot in different actions that were key, but I think everyone that watched the game saw all the action.

Industry observers note that “Only what I can say is I feel proud about the players. I think we today revealed the way that we want to play. It’s only one week working together. I feel so proud. I told them after the game.”.

Referee Marciniak’s poor performance will dominate the fallout to the draw at the Estadio Jose Alvalade, as will goalkeeper Lammens’ latest shaky display in a season replete with individual errors by United players.

“Always we need to do double effort,” Pochettino remarked. “We need to concentrate more and focus more and try to (do) recovery better, do all the effort.

“I think in this type of situation we start to fix also with results, but with (a) performance like today that is for me is the best medicine to prepare the next game.”.

Pochettino’s frantic start to life in charge continues on Saturday afternoon at Old Trafford, where he will take charge of United for the first time against Brentford.

The embattled Red Devils have just one win to show from their opening 11 Premier League matches and languish 19th in the standings.

According to recent reports, “I think we need to encourage and we need to really believe in our play,” Pochettino added. “We need to believe more and more and more.

“I think we need to give the capacity and the tools to perform and of course improving things.

“But that is the way that we need to show our aggression, like in the second half. We recover a lot of ball in the opposite half.

“We were aggressive, and I think I think that is the way that we want to play.”.
