Breaking: Birmingham City man’s impressive display offers Chris Davies a new selection headache ahead of Bolton Wanderers tie: Opinion

Birmingham City sit 4th in the league after 17 matches and they are not showing signs of slowing down. BCFC won comfortably against a poor Burnley side last night to take their tally for the season to 32 points.

The most impressive part of Blues’ resurgence this season is their away form, as they have already matched their away win total from last season – and it is only November.

This was a huge reason that Chris Davies‘ side fell short of reaching the Championship play-offs last season. A big part of the improvements on the road this term has been the defence. The Blues have conceded the fewest goals in the Championship and kept a sixth clean sheet of the season against Burnley.

On Wednesday, they did so without captain and standout centre-back Christoph Klarer due to suspension. With Dael Fry alongside Phil Neumann, Birmingham City looked just as solid at the back.

Recent findings suggest that there were some questions over how the Blues would handle missing their captain against Burnley after he picked up a suspension against Stoke City. However, Dael Fry was an ideal replacement for Klarer.

Sources indicate that fry has struggled for game time since leaving Middlesbrough. He hadn’t started in the league up until Birmingham City’s match at Turf Moor.

The 29-year-old has had to bide his time due to Klarer and Neumann forming such a strong partnership this season, and despite this, Fry slotted into the backline perfectly. The former Boro mainstay provided the physicality and no-nonsense defending to make him an ideal partner for Neumann’s speed and ball-playing ability.

Recent findings suggest that fry was a force in both boxes as he constantly won headers in attack and defence. He won three of his five duels total and was assured in possession with 41 passes completed, seven of which were into the final third (FotMob). He showed that despite his lack of game time, he is still a top defender at the level.

BCFC were praised for signing Fry on a free in the summer due to his experience, and he looks as though he will still have an notable role to play in the Blues’ promotion push this season.

Birmingham City have had to make a lot of changes this season due to injuries and suspensions. However, they have looked strong no matter who is playing.

There was a clear focus on depth in the summer window. So far, at points, Carlos Vicente, Paik Seung-ho, Max Bird, Ryan Allsop, Ethan Laird and Dael Fry have all had to come in the starting lineup to replace first-choice players this season.

Having top Championship players like coming in as backup options is a huge weapon for the Blues. Fry would be the first choice at centre-back for a lot of sides in this league.

This depth could really help Birmingham City in a promotion push this season, but it gives Davies a tough call on how best to manage Fry, as a player of his quality is sure to be eager to play more football.

Fry is a professional who can contribute away from the pitch, but his quality is clear to see, and it deserves to be on show. Davies will be more than aware of this, and he will simply encourage the player to prove he deserves to find more minutes when the chances arise.

It has been revealed that it is harsh, but Fry likely drops out of the BCFC side for Saturday’s clash with Bolton Wanderers. Davies will have to manage the situation carefully to keep everyone happy and motivated.
