Partner Services Manager - Creative Studio.

In a recent development, at Big Think Creative, we collaborate with our partners to shape and share their biggest ideas. We’re a team of editors, directors, designers and strategists - but also dreamers, builders and lifelong learners. And we bring expertise, enthusiasm and endless curiosity to everything we do.

From emerging technology to human connection, we know how to make people lean in, think twice and expand their perspectives. We create custom content in all formats, including expert interviews, field documentaries, written features and social shorts. We work with a wide range of partners, and our campaigns have won several awards including Digiday’s In-House Studio of the Year 2025.

Watch this video to see what we’re all about: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CVyE4ex5P-E.

We’re looking for a Partner Services Manager to serve as the connective tissue between partner stakeholders, internal creative teams, and publishing operations —helping shepherd a high-volume slate of written, video, social, newsletter, and live event initiatives from concept through launch.

Salary Range: $85,000 base salary, with additional quarterly performance-based compensation available.
