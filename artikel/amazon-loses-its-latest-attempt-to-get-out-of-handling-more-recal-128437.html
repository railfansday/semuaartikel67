Industry observers note that amazon is on the hook to recall more than 400,000 hazardous products after losing its legal battle against the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC). The US District Court for the District of Maryland ruled in favor of the CPSC, which argued that Amazon is a distributor of goods, not a logistics provider, making it responsible for carrying out recalls of third-party products.

Amazon sued the CPSC in March 2025 after the agency ordered Amazon to recall over 400,000 “Fulfilled by Amazon” products that pose a “substantial product hazard,” including faulty carbon monoxide detectors, hair dryers without electrocution protection, and children’s sleepwear that doesn’t meet federal flammability standards.

As a result, Amazon will be required to implement the following measures, initially outlined in the CPSC’s January 2025 order:.

On the day CPSC publishes the recall releases, Amazon must notify the public by posting the recall releases to Amazon.com; notify original purchasers directly via email and by providing information regarding the recalls on each purchaser’s “Your Orders” page.

Amazon must provide one round of notice of the recalls to Fulfilled by Amazon participants that sold the subject products.

Incentive to Remove Products from Commerce:.

Amazon must concern a full refund in the amount of the purchase price to any consumer who submits proof of destruction or disposal of a subject product.

Maintaining Records of Recalls:.

Amazon must maintain the recall releases on the “Recalls and Product Safety Alerts” webpage on amazon.com for a minimum of five years.

Amazon must submit monthly progress reports for five years and maintain records of its actions to comply with the Order for the same period.

It has been revealed that “We have a longstanding commitment to removing unsafe and recalled products from our store and notifying customers, backed by an industry-leading recalls process,” Amazon spokesperson Sammy Brill says in an emailed statement to The Verge. “We’re reviewing our options following the ruling and look forward to continuing to work with the CPSC to put customer safety first.”.
