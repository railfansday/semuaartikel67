In a recent development, I've been using the XGIMI MoGo 3 Pro since it came out in 2024 and have been recommending it to friends and family every chance I get. It quickly became my favorite portable projector, and two years later, it's still my go-to for a spontaneous projector session. If you're on the lookout for a good deal on a portable projector, I recommend considering the following XGIMI deals.

Industry observers note that if you're looking for the most affordable option, the Vibe One projector is just $179 (originally $299) after a 40% discount. This is a 1080p resolution and 1500:1 contrast portable projector for casual watching at night with 250 ISO lumens of brightness, and the price reflects that. It already comes with Google TV, which is my favorite OS, so you can download and stream any app you want. It's battery-powered, which means you can take it anywhere with you (as long as you have wifi). The battery lasts up to 1.2 hours for projecting video (in Eco Mode), and up to four hours as a speaker (it has built-in dual 3W JBL speakers). The auto keystone from XGIMI is one of the best ones I've experienced among all of the projectors I've tested, and it will adjust to practically any surface you cast it on, which can, with the 160° adjustable built-in stand, even be the ceiling. The current bundle brings an 80-inch screen included in the price.

According to recent reports, if you have a bit more money to splurge, the MoGo 4 is the same concept as the Vibe One but takes its specs to the next level, and it's available starting at $359 (originally $499). It's still small and compact with 1080p resolution, but it nearly doubles the brightness, with 450 ISO lumens. The battery life more than doubles to two and a half hours in Eco Mode, while still keeping the projector under three pounds. It also has Google TV as its OS, but the projection is 360° with its adjustable built-in stand. You also have the option of getting the bundle with the PowerBase Stand, which extends the battery to five hours. Like the Vibe One, it has a built-in speaker, but this one uses dual 6W Harman Kardon speakers with Dolby Audio.The MoGo 4 Laser is a slightly upgraded version that uses triple laser technology for a better picture. It still supports 1080p resolution, but it's brighter with 550 ISO lumens and a 1000:1 contrast. The battery life, OS system, stand, speakers, and auto keystone technology are the same—but the bundles are different, so they're worth considering if they fit your needs.

If you're looking for a premium portable projector, the Horizon 30 Pro is a great option that competes with the best options on the market. It supports 4K resolution, uses RGB triple laser brightness that reaches 4800 ISO lumens (bright enough to see during the day), has a 120Hz refresh rate and gaming features to make gaming smooth, displays an 8,000:1 native contrast to make colors and blacks pop, and supports IMAX Enhanced, Dolby Vision, and HDR10+. It has an adjustable stand like the other XGIMI projectors and its own dual 12W Harman Kardon speakers with DTS Virtual:X and DTS-HD. The auto keystone is just as powerful as the other options, but the lens adds an optical zoom and a Lens Memory feature, which lets you save up to six custom lens and image setups and recall them with a click. Right now, it's down to $1,899 (originally $2,499) after a 24% discount.
