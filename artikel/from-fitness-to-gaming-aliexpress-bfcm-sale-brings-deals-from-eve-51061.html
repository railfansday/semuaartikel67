Sources indicate that holiday shopping season is here, and AliExpress’ Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale is kicking off Nov. 19 through Dec. 3, 2026. Shoppers can browse deals across a range of lifestyle categories, with brand-name finds spanning everything from kitchen appliances and fitness gear to gaming essentials and sneakers.

The sale offers an opportunity to explore AliExpress Brand+, a dedicated shopping destination bringing together products from trusted brands with enhanced servicing at competitive prices. According to AliExpress, 95% of U.S.-available products qualify for its fast delivery guarantee, with coupons offered for qualifying delays. Most Brand+ products also include free shipping, with no minimum order requirement. That means shoppers can browse for gifts, everyday upgrades, and a few seasonal treats without necessarily having to spend more to unlock shipping perks.

Here are a few products across different lifestyle categories that shoppers can check out during the sale, whether they’re looking for practical gifts or a little something to upgrade their everyday routines.

If someone on your holiday list is hoping to make more time for home workouts, this Merach adjustable dumbbell set is worth a look. Available in several colors, it offers four weight settings, ranging from 2.2 to 8.8 pounds, so users can switch up resistance without keeping several pairs of weights around. The non-slip grip, safety lock, and flat, anti-roll design are practical touches, too. Its compact build makes it an appealing option for smaller workout spaces.

Gym rats and athletes will appreciate finding a performance-focused pair of sneakers under the tree this year. Li-Ning’s Wade Shadow 7 shoes feature BOOM cushioning technology designed to provide responsive rebound, while an EVA insole helps absorb impact during movement. The breathable mesh upper and rubber outsole are designed with indoor court sessions in mind, offering a combination of comfort and traction. Available in six color options and sizes 7.5 through 13, they’re a sporty gift idea for players looking to refresh their footwear.

For the smoothie enthusiast, home cook, or anyone who loves experimenting in the kitchen, the Biolo Mix blender brings some serious blending power to the countertop. Its advertised 3HP, 2,200-watt motor and six-blade system are designed to tackle ingredients like ice, nuts, and tougher fruits, or, if you’re me, frozen fruit. There’s also stepless speed control for adjusting the consistency, plus a capacity of more than 1.5 liters for larger batches. With multiple color options, it could be a practical kitchen upgrade ahead of the fresh year.

Industry observers note that the Edifier G35 headset is an ideal gift for the gamer in your life. Designed for PC, PS4, Mac, and other devices, it features 7.1 surround sound for immersive gameplay, along with a noise-cancelling microphone for clearer communication. You can’t go wrong with memory foam ear cushions that make longer sessions more comfortable, while built-in volume controls offer easy adjustments.

In a recent development, aliExpress brings shoppers and independent sellers together, offering a huge variety of products sourced largely from across Asia. Their inventory includes well-known brands at wallet-friendly prices, and shoppers can browse across 24 categories, from fashion to jewelry and cell phones to home improvement. For extra bargains, customers can explore Dollar Express, Welcome Deals, Local Shipping, Super Deals, and the Today’s Deals page.
