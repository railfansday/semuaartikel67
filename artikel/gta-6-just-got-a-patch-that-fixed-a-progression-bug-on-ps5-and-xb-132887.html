Rockstar just issued a newly introduced patch for GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, targeting a few problems players have been reporting since the game rolled out last Thursday (19th November).

Sources indicate that the 23rd November patch for the game deploys seven major fixes for the game, including a pretty significant bug that prevented phone calls not triggering at certain points, resulting in a scenario where you could be locked out of progressing the game. The official update is titled 'version 01.002.002', and its weighs in at different sizes on different consoles: it's 603.8 MB on PS5, and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X/S.

Thanks to an update on the Rockstar website, you can read all of the updates that arrive with the latest pathc below.

Recent findings suggest that there have been a few fairly high-profile cases of the autosave not working as the developer intended, leading to a few cases of streamers losing plenty of real-world time thanks to unpredictable auto-save behaviour. JasonTheWeen lost 12 hours of progress on the PS5, for example.

But there's one problem here the developer hasn't addressed: vignetting. Plenty of players have taken to online communities to complain about the vignette effect that pops into the game - theoretically to help with motion smoothing and various grapihcal elements as the game runs in 30FPS. THe effect is worsened when you're fatigued, hungry, or have some other status effect, but it is present quite a lot of the time, too. Most of the responses to a tweet about the patch posted by the Rockstar Support account reference the vignetting in GTA 6, and the developer has issued no response at the time of writing.
