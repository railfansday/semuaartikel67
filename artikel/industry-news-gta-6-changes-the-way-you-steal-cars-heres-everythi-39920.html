Industry News: GTA 6 changes the way you steal cars: here's everything you need to know about slim jims, key cloners, and tracking devices

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that used to be that all you needed to steal a car in a GTA game was a few seconds and a sharp elbow. In GTA 6, you can still smash a window and hotwire some cars, but the car stealing system has changed a lot and the further you get into the game, the more tools you'll need.

Recent findings suggest that that means a quick getaway in a stolen vehicle isn't always that simple. Here are the methods for stealing cars, the new tools you'll get and when they become available in the story, and everything else you need to know about boosting rides in GTA 6.

Recent findings suggest that want to steal a car? Walk up to the door and press the interact button. That'll tell you if it's locked (most are), if it's alarmed (nearly all are), and if it's keyless—a new type of car that we'll cover more below.

If you smash the window, you'll then climb into the car and need to hotwire it, which just takes a second or two and uses the control sticks to find the "sweet spot." If it's a keyless car, you won't be able to hotwire it or drive it away—again, more on that below. If you smash a window on an alarmed car, the alarm will sound, which can attract attention.

Industry observers note that you can still pull people out of their cars, but be warned: many of them will fight back, meaning you'll need to hammer a button promptly to defeat them. Lose, and they'll clonk your head against the side of the car, knocking you down and speeding away. And even if you do pull them out of their car, this is Leonida: a lot of motorists are armed and aren't shy about opening fire.

You can't slim jim a car someone is already sitting in, so you'll have to break the window if the door is locked. Plus, there are car trackers to worry about now: more on that below.

According to recent reports, in Chapter 1, in the sixth story mission called Unwanted Attention, your buddy Cal will help you install the "Waink It" app on your phone, which lets you scan cars to see their make and model, if they're locked, alarmed, keyless, or have trackers. The app will also tell you the condition of the car, how much it can be sold for, and what it costs to register them—registering stolen cars at Pay 'n' Spray shops is how you take ownership of them in GTA 6.

Want to get into a locked car without breaking the window or setting off the alarm? You'll need a slim jim, a tool that lets you pop the lock and open the door quietly.

It has been revealed that you'll get this fresh tool in Chapter 2 of the main story, in the fifth mission called "Moses," where you meet an auto mechanic (Moses!) who will give you a slim jim and teach you how to use it. Now when you sidle up to a car door, you'll be able to use the slim jim to pop the lock: no broken windows, no alarms—though it takes a little longer than using your elbow. Again, you still won't be able to start keyless cars, even if you have a slim jim.

Industry observers note that here's the big one. Keyless cars can't be hotwired, so you'll need a key cloner. You'll get the key cloner in Chapter 3, in the 16th mission called "Steal the Zorruso for Randy." That's a long time to wait, but now you can stand next to the door of a keyless car and use your phone to clone its key.

Bonus: the key cloner will not only unlock the door but automatically start the car. That makes it worth the wait! However, keyless cars often come with another upcoming system: trackers.

It has been revealed that not a significant deal, really: if a car has a standard tracker, it just means you can't modify it at a mod shop unless you've paid to register the car as your own. I probably wouldn't modify a car unless I owned it anyway, so I never worried about standard trackers at all.

These can be a problem. Advanced trackers will notify the police that the car has been stolen and pinpoint your location—and the cops will respond quickly. To defeat the advanced tracker, you need to drive fast enough that the tracker won't be able to send a signal to the cops. Slow down, and it'll start beeping and you'll only have a few seconds to speed up again. If you fail to speed up enough, the tracker will pinpoint your location and the cops will be on you again.

Recent findings suggest that you have to drive pretty fast to keep the advanced tracker from triggering, which makes it tougher to get them to a Pay 'n' Spray or a Randy's garage without doing some serious damage to the car. Good luck!

According to recent reports, you can sell stolen cars at a Pay 'n' Spray, though there's a limit of three cars per day. You can also sell to your two automobile fences, Moses and Randy, though Moses only buys older model, low-end cars and Randy only buys certain flashy, expensive cars. Your Waink It app will tell you if a car is suitable for sale to either of them.

You can also store stolen cars at a garage if you've purchased one through the real estate app on your phone. Each garage only has room for a single car. You can also use a parking spot or spots outside your current safehouse to store a stolen car.
