Recent findings suggest that rayan Cherki presented himself at the microphone of TF1 to discuss the defeat of the France team against Italy (1-3). The most talkative of the French players took the time to explain, in his opinion, what went wrong this Thursday evening. “I think it was a fairly complicated, fairly close match. We started the match well. We did what was necessary, pressing them high, just like the goal. And after that it was a more complicated match. We should have rotated the ball faster. Unfortunately that’s not what we did, but it will allow us to learn a lot of lessons. Everything couldn’t be perfect. We tried to do our best, but tonight we are very frustrated”he started by saying, before detailing his main regret.

It has been revealed that “I’m of the opinion that yes, we switch well, we find ourselves well, but at certain moments in the game, we don’t play fast enough in the game and that made a lot of difference. When we found each other quickly, we directly put ourselves in danger. When we chained together sequences with a lot of keys, it became more complicated. It’s a shame because there was room. We must not forget that the road is very long. There is nothing to worry about tonight’s performance. (…) There may be a lack of success. When you enter the field, facing grouped blocks, you must not hesitate to turn from left to right, from right to left. Don’t just stay on one side, for example. I think I only touched my first ball in the 14th minute, Michael too. It demonstrates that we perhaps didn’t run enough.”. A clear and reasoned opinion, which will perhaps make his coach Zinédine Zidane react.
