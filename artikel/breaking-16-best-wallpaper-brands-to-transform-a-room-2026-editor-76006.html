According to recent reports, the celebrated 19th century British designer, William Morris, was a traditionalist devoted to his craft in the midst of the Industrial Revolution. He taught himself block printing techniques to create more than 20 nature-inspired patterns that encapsulate the Arts & Crafts Movement. His design brand Morris & Co. was established in 1861 and has shown no signs of slowing down as contemporary designers continue to incorporate the wallpaper patterns in their projects. Each wallpaper design is batch-printed in the United Kingdom, making it a good practice to order all rolls at the same time to ensure the dye lots are the same.
