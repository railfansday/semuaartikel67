In a recent development, specialty pharmacy revenue jumped 34% year over year, driven by limited distribution drugs (LDDs), which are medications restricted by manufacturers to select specialty pharmacies. Industry observers note that patient care continues shifting toward lower-cost home settings, and management is positioning its expanded network to capture that growing national demand. The company leverages scale across procurement, proprietary care coordination models, and automated administrative workflows to widen its operational advantage over smaller regional providers.
