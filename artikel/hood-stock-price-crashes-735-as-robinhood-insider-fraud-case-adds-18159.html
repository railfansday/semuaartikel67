HOOD Stock Price Crashes 7.35% as Robinhood Insider Fraud Case Adds to Selling Pressure

  • A repeated EMA death-cross setup resembles the bearish pattern seen during Q1 2026.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

HOOD fell 7.35% to $72.06 on November 17, moving closer to the $65 demand zone.

A repeated EMA death-cross setup resembles the bearish pattern seen during Q1 2026.

A former Robinhood engineer pleaded guilty to using confidential listing data to trade on Hyperliquid.

It has been revealed that the HOOD stock price is losing ground fast. Robinhood shares traded at $72.06 on November 17, down 7.35%, putting the stock dangerously close to revisiting its March lows.

Industry observers note that after nearly doubling from around $65 in late March to $126 in September, the rally has taken a brutal turn. The technical damage is mounting. And another leg lower can’t be ruled out.

Recent findings suggest that robinhood maintained an upward trendline from its late-March bottom through September. That structure broke in October, when the price slipped below trendline support near the $95 horizontal level.

November’s decline has pushed shares toward $72. The next major demand area sits around $65, close to the March low. If buyers defend that level, a reversal could develop.

But another failed test would leave the stock exposed to a deeper slide, with $40 emerging as a possible downside area. That’s a risk scenario, not a confirmed target.

The moving-average setup is adding to the concern. During Q1 2026, a death cross between the 20-day EMA band and the 200-day EMA appeared around $95. HOOD subsequently fell toward $65.

A second bearish signal involving the 50-day and 200-day EMA bands accompanied the extended decline.

Recent findings suggest that now, similar signals have appeared again in Q4. The 20-day and 200-day EMA death cross emerged around $95, followed by another crossover involving the 50-day and 200-day EMA bands.

It has been revealed that the resemblance is hard to ignore. Still, technical patterns don’t guarantee an identical outcome. The $65 area could trigger a strong rebound, or break under renewed selling pressure.

🇺🇸 A former Robinhood engineer has pleaded guilty to wire fraud after using confidential information about upcoming token listings to trade on Hyperliquid.The DOJ says he used advance knowledge of future token additions to make $125,523 in profit trading perpetual futures.… pic.twitter.com/YkL9T1Hnqh.

According to recent reports, the latest selling also coincides with damaging news about Robinhood’s crypto operations. Former engineer Huaisong “Jerry” Xiang pleaded guilty on November 16 to wire fraud after using confidential token-listing information to trade perpetual futures on Hyperliquid.

He agreed to forfeit $125,523. Prosecutors mentioned the activity involved at least 11 listing announcements between 2025 and 2026. Sentencing is scheduled for February 25, 2027.

It has been revealed that the case raises uncomfortable questions about internal controls and confidential listing data. However, the supplied information doesn’t establish that the guilty plea directly caused November 17’s 7.35% decline.

It has been revealed that for the HOOD stock price, the immediate technical test is now clear: $65. Hold it, and a recovery remains possible. Lose it, and the downside risk toward $40 becomes harder to dismiss.
