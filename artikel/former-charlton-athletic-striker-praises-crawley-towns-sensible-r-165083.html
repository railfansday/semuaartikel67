Industry observers note that the two 33-year-olds have been brought in by Kevin Blackwell to boost his squad as they fight for relegation. Both signed short-term deals until the end of January.

Heneghan has already made an impact at the centre of the back three while Wickham scored on his debut in the Jersey Mikes Trophy against Brentford u21s.

And former QPR and Charlton striker Connor Washington, as part of his weekly EFL column for BestBettingBonuses.co.uk, labelled the signings as ‘sensible’ given Reds’ relegation battle.

It has been revealed that "I think importantly they're adding quality as well as experience,” he mentioned. “Seeing players of that calibre come through the door can give the whole group a lift, and the younger players in particular will benefit from having them around the building.

“Given the short-term nature of their contracts, it's relatively low-risk recruitment from Crawley's perspective.

“Equally, you've got two experienced players who are effectively playing for their futures, whether that's at Crawley or elsewhere, so there's a real incentive for them to perform.

“Having players who can take some responsibility away from the younger lads in difficult moments of games is invaluable, particularly when you're in a relegation battle. I think it's sensible recruitment given the position they find themselves in.”.

Blackwell, who has been appointed as manager until the end of the season has already noted they have highlighted more signings for the January window as they look to climb up the table.
