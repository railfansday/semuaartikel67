I’ve been writing gift guides as a shopping editor for years. Usually, I’m very sentimental, wanting to recommend thoughtful gifts to make your loved ones smile. But all bets are off when it comes to White Elephant gifts. I want to cause chaos at your family gift swap. Your gift should be the one everyone fights over.

If you're not sure what a White Elephant exchange is or how it works, just know this: it's really fun, and it's one of the only times when stealing is not only totally acceptable, but encouraged. Basically, it's a blind gift exchange that, when the recipient reveals the gift they chose, they can keep it or put it back and steal someone else's gift. It's a cheeky little game that is a great way to get into the holiday spirit.

It’s also a really affordable way to exchange gifts as a big group. No worries about finding the perfect thing for someone specific. Just bring something cool enough for anybody to enjoy, and there’s usually a budget cap. So for this guide, I provided the best white elephant gifts under $50. They’re so budget-friendly and unique that you might want to do all of your gift shopping below, or buy some items of your own. Worry not; we won’t judge.
