Black Friday week is officially here, and there's no better time to load up on cut-price tech. You know all those devices and pieces of hardware that are hard to justify buying on any other day? Well, now's the time to grab them. Discounts are dropping every day on Amazon as we head towards Black Friday on Nov. 27.

Sources indicate that one tech category that's absolutely worth checking out is SSDs (solid-state hard drives). These devices are small but super expensive, so any kind of discount on a high-quality SSD is very welcome indeed.

An SSD is a piece of hardware you connect to your computer or console for data storage. They use flash memory to store data, which makes them faster and more efficient than HDDs (hard disk drives). It also makes them more expensive. Fortunately, there's a range of discounted Samsung SSDs among Amazon's Black Friday deals, from budget to premium level.
