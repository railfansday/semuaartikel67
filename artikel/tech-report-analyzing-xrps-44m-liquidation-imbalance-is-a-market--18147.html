Tech Report: Analyzing XRP’s $44M liquidation imbalance – Is a market reset underway?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ripple [XRP] continues to trade below the $1 psychological level since breaking below three days ago. At press time, XRP was trading at $0.94, with the decline coinciding with the largest daily liquidations since October 2025.

As per data from CryptoQuant, the amount of long liquidations exceeded short liquidations by about $44 million across three exchanges. The deltas were $21 million, $12 million, and $11 million for Binance, Bybit, and OKX, respectively.

In a recent development, this data demonstrated that Binance had the largest pressure on leveraged long orders. But the spikes happened concurrently across three exchanges, implying the activity was widespread.

Such a massive long liquidation imbalance may accelerate deleveraging as the Estimated Leverage Ratio (ELR) hits a three-month low. A few days later, the ELR crashed to 0.13, the lowest level since November 2024.

It has been revealed that the sharp drop in ELR implies a notable shift in trader behavior and reduced leverage usage. On the same note, Open Interest (OI) of the XRP Futures market on Binance fell to $325 million, its lowest level since November 2024.

This indicated the volume of active positions had reduced significantly. Both a decline in ELR and OI implied a cooling speculative environment in the derivative markets of XRP.

Industry observers note that these figures suggest that the volatility of the altcoin’s price action may cool off due to forced liquidations. The sharp decline in price is forcing traders to close their existing positions.

It has been revealed that in turn, liquidation risk was reducing, signaling a potential recovery for XRP. However, the altcoin was down about 2% in the past 24 hours of writing but with a significant trading volume of $1.63 billion.

Similarly, the price has been trading at the lowest level since late November 2024, just after the US general elections. Still, there is a likelihood that XRP could continue dropping to the $0.50 demand level, a key zone on the weekly timeframe chart.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, all Spot Ripple ETFs have been losing capital over the past week with a combined equivalent of $19.30 million. The losses were mainly driven by three days of outflows, with the largest of them being $10.42 million last Monday.

These figures imply that institutions were going bearish on the XR alongside other altcoins. Over $1.09 million was withdrawn from the XRP ETF on the 13th of November .

Therefore, the XRP ETF was showing bearish signals, though the deleveraging could mitigate the ongoing drop.
