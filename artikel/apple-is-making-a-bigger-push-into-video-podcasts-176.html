Apple is making a bigger push into video podcasts

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

You’ll soon be able to watch video podcasts through the Apple TV app.

Apple is making a bigger push into video podcasts by adding a dedicated section for them to the Apple TV app. Apple plans to increase the number of video podcasts that are available through the Podcasts app as well.

Developers will be able to check out the video podcasts section on Apple TV starting today before Apple makes it available to everyone. The section, which won't be locked behind a subscription, will be accessible on Apple's own set-top box, iPhone, iPad, Mac and CarPlay-enabled vehicles, as well as on the Apple TV app on third-party devices. (HomeView seems like a natural fit for video podcasts, but Apple didn't explicitly say whether they'll be available on the Apple TV app on that device.).

It has been revealed that "We want podcasts to be available wherever people want to enjoy them — the small screens, major screens," Oliver Schusser, an Apple vice president whose portfolio includes Apple TV and podcasts, told The Fresh York Times. "It's really about discovery and helping the creators to reach a new audience. We didn't approach this in a way that would financially benefit us.".

Sources indicate that like with just about everything these days though, Apple's push into video podcasts is part of a battle for consumers' attention. While the organization helped popularize podcasts in the 2000s with the iPod, the company hasn't kept up with Spotify, YouTube and Netflix as video versions of demonstrates became more prevalent. Earlier this year, Apple brought its HTTP Live Streaming video protocol to the Podcasts app, allowing customers to switch between watching and listening to shows. The company told the Times that, in a given month, over 35 percent of video podcasts users switch between watching and listening.
