Recent findings suggest that liverpool did not think one star was this good. He's proven them completely wrong and made some people look foolish.

Liverpool made some bold decisions in the summer as they looked to reshape the side after a difficult campaign. Of course, that came one year after reshaping the side on the back of a successful campaign.

In a recent development, regardless, things did need to happen and part of that was Liverpool locking down two midfielders: Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai. The pair were given massive newly introduced contracts as the club committed to them as the core of their midfield.

It was a bold decision and a brave one. Liverpool picked two players without a history of playing alongside one another while also committing to a head coach who plays with a two-man midfield.

According to recent reports, but the bravery was also seen in who they didn't commit to - an inevitable consequence of deciding on two players to lead the way forward. And you've got to say that they got that one wrong.

According to recent reports, alexis Mac Allister is the one Liverpool stated no to. Literally, in fact, as he asked for a new contract and was told the club wasn't in position to give him one. And that was right after they'd offered and agreed deals with Gravenberch and Szoboszlai.

It was a real message to people that Liverpool wanted to move on from Mac Allister and build around the other two. That's how Mac Allister took it, anyway, as he publicly discussed how disappointed he was with the club's stance.

Fast-forward a few months, though, and things look different. There is absolutely no question that Mac Allister has been the standout midfielder for Liverpool this season and any belief on the club's part that he was past his prime has been proven completely incorrect.

Industry observers note that andoni Iraola has come to rely on him above nearly any other player. Mac Allister is the core of midfield and in some ways, the core of the side - the one who does a bit of everything to make sure it all knits together.

Sources indicate that so not only is Mac Allister the best midfielder right now, he's also the easiest one to build around. It's making the contract decisions earlier this year look foolish, honestly.

Industry observers note that but perhaps Liverpool recognise that. The Mail is just one outlet reporting that they're to open talks with Mac Allister's father in the coming weeks as they look to find a compromise over a latest contract.
