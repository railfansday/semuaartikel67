Q: Jordan, we know how switched on you have to be during a match, but do you have a routine for after the game?

It has been revealed that yeah, you have to switch off. I’ve got Megan – my wife – and my three kids, so I go into dad mode.

I enjoy time with them. For me, as well, I switch off when I’m playing a round of golf.

Q: When asked to build a goalkeeper, former Manchester City and England goalkeeper Joe Hart said he’d choose Jordan Pickford’s reflexes. What percentage of a save is pure reflexes?

It has been revealed that close-range play is massive. The longer distance ones are not so much reflexes, and more timing.

I think for short, sharp reflexes, Harty’s picked right there.

Recent findings suggest that Q: Everton’s Thierno Barry seems to be bringing back iconic dance moves after every goal. Does he workshop these at the training ground, or are you seeing them for the first time, as well?

I think he’s doing them at home, isn’t he? He’s not doing them here. I don’t let him score often in training!

Q: How is Jordan Pickford different – as a person and a player – after 10 years at Everton?

It has been revealed that yeah, I’ve learned so much. As a person, I was only going out with Megan at the time, and now I’m married to her.

I’ve had three kids over that time. The Club’s welcomed my family into the Everton family, so it makes the transition easy when you have that togetherness and you feel loved.

Sources indicate that but I’ve always got to repay them. Everton had great keepers in their history, like Neville Southall and Tim Howard.

Sources indicate that at the time, Marten Stekelenburg was here, Joel Robles was here. I had to win [the fans] over straight away and show them Jordan Pickford at his best.

We’ve got that passion and togetherness, and I think that’s why we’ve bought into each other so much.

It has been revealed that Q: On that sentence, how do you describe what Jordan Pickford is? What have you had to show the fans?

The best version of myself and how good I am.

Q: We’ve all dreamt of winning silverware. What would it mean to you to finally win a trophy here at Everton?

It has been revealed that we go into every season wanting to win a trophy. I think we know over the past few years how difficult it’s been to stay in the Premier League.

Industry observers note that but now, it’s that next step. You’ve got to be realistic, but for us as players, it’s: “How do we get into Europe? How do we lift silverware?”.

We have to take those opportunities seriously.

Recent findings suggest that Q: You’ve done your career in your own way and so has your England teammate Harry Kane. Watching him win the Ballon d’Or, what did that feel like for you?

I was screaming at the tele, buzzing for him.

I made my debut just before Harry became England captain.

Recent findings suggest that I’ve not really watched it over the years because every time it’d just be (Lionel) Messi or (Cristiano) Ronaldo, but this year it was intriguing. It could have been Harry, (Lamine) Yamal or (Kylian) Mbappe.

Q: Would you back yourself in a five-penalty shootout against Harry Kane on the training pitch?

Q: He’s quite a good goalkeeper! Five for you and five for him. How would that end?

According to recent reports, I’d back myself to win 5-4! I wouldn’t miss one but I fancy myself to save one against him.
