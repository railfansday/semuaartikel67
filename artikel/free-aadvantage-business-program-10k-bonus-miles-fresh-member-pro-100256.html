AAdvantage Business is American’s free small business rewards program, and if you’re eligible and fly American with any frequency, it’s absolutely worth participating (it essentially lets you earn one bonus Loyalty Point and AAdvantage mile per eligible dollar spent on flights, beyond what you usually earn).

At the moment, we’re seeing a upcoming member bonus for those who register for an account, making it the ideal time to sign-up, if you haven’t done so already. In particular, this pairs very nicely with the Citi® / AAdvantage Business™ World Elite Mastercard® (learn more) limited time welcome bonus, which offers 75,000 AAdvantage miles upon completing minimum spending.

Through December 31, 2026, the AAdvantage Business program is offering 10,000 AAdvantage bonus miles to anyone who registers for the program with promotion code BNS10K. Bonus miles will post within six to eight weeks following the successful enrollment in the program.

For what it’s worth, we did see a bonus of 15,000 miles several weeks ago, but that’s no longer available, and this bonus is much better than usual, as there isn’t ordinarily a standard bonus for becoming a member.

It has been revealed that I should mention that in order to be eligible to participate in the AAdvantage Business program, you need to have a business in the United States or Canada with a Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) or Business Number. However, even small businesses are eligible for this program, so that’s pretty awesome.

In a recent development, there’s no activity required to earn the 10,000 bonus miles in your AAdvantage Business account. Let me emphasize that with AAdvantage Business, miles are accrued to the business, and can then be shared with any firm member, so they can redeem through their personal AAdvantage account.
