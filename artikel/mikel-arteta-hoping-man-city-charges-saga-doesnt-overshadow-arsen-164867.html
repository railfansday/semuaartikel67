Mikel Arteta has urged his Arsenal players to block out all talk of Manchester City’s Premier League charges and ensure they win the title on the pitch.

In September, City were were found guilty of breaching Premier League financial regulations between 2009 and 2018, while an independent commission also ruled that the club failed to co-operate with an investigation.

An appeal has been submitted by City, who deny any wrongdoing, and it remains to be seen when a final resolution in the case will be reached.

It is a shadow hanging over the league ahead of Arsenal’s showdown with City in north London on Sunday.

Victory would move the Gunners nine points ahead of Enzo Maresca’s side and leave them in strong shape to defend their league title, regardless of any potential sanctions imposed on City.

Arteta is clear that his players cannot afford any distractions, demanding that they get the job done and defend their crown without relying on a potential points deduction for City.

It has been revealed that “That’s the only thing that we should put the energy and the focus on,” Arteta said. “There’s nothing else.”.

Arsenal were defeated twice by City in the space of three weeks towards the end of last season, first in the Carabao Cup final at Wembley and then in a Premier League clash at the Etihad Stadium. Despite losing the latter game, Arsenal went on to win the title for the first time in 22 years.

A rivalry has built between the two clubs over the past five seasons, having gone toe-to-toe in several title races.

Asked if the guilty verdict against City and the wait for possible sanctions had diminished the significance of Sunday’s meeting, Arteta mentioned: “I hope that it doesn’t because it’s a process that is still open.

In a recent development, “It’s not resolved and we don’t know what is going to happen. So, no, I think when we have two teams at the level that both teams are at right now with the quality players that are on that pitch, it’s a massive game and a big game to enjoy.”.

However, when it was put to him that the atmosphere might be particularly emotional due to the strength of feeling from the Arsenal fans, Arteta sidestepped the topic.

“I don’t know,” he noted. “That’s something very individual and I don’t want to go there.”.
