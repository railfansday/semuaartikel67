It’s built almost entirely from maple offcuts, which explains the mix of thicknesses on the Cutting List. To keep it approachable, every joint is a simple butt joint with glue and brad nails. Industry observers note that A food-safe wood finish won’t transfer chemicals or toxins to the food when being used. There are two ways a finish is considered food-safe. Either it is completely non-toxic and edible in its liquid state from its origin, or, once fully cured, the VOCs (volatile organic compounds) in the finish disperse, and it becomes a stable, inert, non-toxic solid. Finishes like food-grade mineral oil or fractionated coconut oil are considered food-safe from the time of application and work well as food-safe wood finishes.
