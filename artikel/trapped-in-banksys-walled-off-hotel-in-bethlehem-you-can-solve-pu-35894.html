The International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) has unveiled the first works in its immersive strands, inviting audiences “into strange worlds where our choices have unexpected consequences.”.

IFFR recently revealed a first wave of world premieres for its 56th edition, taking place Jan. 28- Feb. 7. On Thursday, it revealed first offerings in its Art Directions, Lightroom and Sound//vision programs.

Art Directions focuses on “installations, expanded cinema, performance, XR and sound, exploring different ways audiences can encounter and inhabit moving-image work.” This year’s program includes its first-ever Artist in Focus, namely the Catalan collective Servicios Inmersivos. Meanwhile, IFFR’s Lightroom initiatives support immersive and interactive practices at different stages.“Art Directions has always been a space where we can challenge our understanding of what cinema can be,” remarked Melissa van der Schoor, chief of content at IFFR. “This year’s selection takes us beneath the streets of Upcoming York, explores the relationship between belief and everyday life, and invites us into strange worlds where our choices have unexpected consequences. What excites me is how these artists use such different approaches to make us question what we see, what we take for granted and how we experience the world around us.”Here is a look at IFFR’s 2027 first Art Directions and Lightroom offerings.
