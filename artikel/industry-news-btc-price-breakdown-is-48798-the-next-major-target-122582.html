BTC fell below $60,730 and trades near $57,297, with $48,798 marked as the next major downside level.

The September rally failed below the $82,322–$86,850 resistance zone.

Frequent small and medium Binance inflows may add to potential exchange supply as spot demand remains weak.

Recent findings suggest that the latest BTC price breakdown has put traders on alert. After losing the $60,730 support level, BTC is trading near $57,297, with the daily chart pointing toward another leg lower. And the selling pressure isn’t coming from whales alone.

In a recent development, BTC briefly slipped below $60,730 in late June and early July before recovering sharply. That rebound carried prices toward $86,850 by September, but the move failed to change the bigger picture.

According to recent reports, the rally stalled beneath the $82,322–$86,850 resistance zone. Since then, sellers have pushed prices lower through October and November.

Recent findings suggest that the broader structure remains bearish. After topping above $125,000 in October 2025, BTC has formed lower highs and lower lows. It now trades below all three marked moving averages: $65,582, $71,804 and $74,624.

CryptoQuant’s analysis of Binance inflows by spent UTXO size suggests selling pressure isn’t limited to large holders.

According to recent reports, smaller and medium-sized transfers appear more frequently, while large inflows arrive in occasional spikes. Individually, smaller transfers may carry limited selling potential. Repeated over time, however, they can steadily add potential supply to exchanges.

It has been revealed that the data also indicates Binance inflows haven’t declined significantly, while spot demand remains too weak to absorb incoming supply. That’s an uncomfortable combination for buyers.

It has been revealed that the BTC chart marks $48,798 as the next major downside level. If selling continues, a rising green trendline around $41,000–$42,000 offers a deeper potential target.

Neither level is guaranteed. A sustained decline in Binance inflows, alongside stronger spot buying, would help challenge the bearish outlook.

For now, the BTC price breakdown leaves $60,730 as a lost support level and keeps the lower targets in focus.
