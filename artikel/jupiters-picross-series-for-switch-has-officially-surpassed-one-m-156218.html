If you've ever bought one of Jupiter Corporation's Picross games on the Switch, you've helped the series reach a major milestone.

The Kyoto-based developer has this week revealed its Picross series for Switch has now surpassed one million downloads. Since the launch of Picross S on the Switch in 2017, Jupiter has gone on to release more than 20 Picross titles (including all sorts of crossovers).

Earlier this year in March, we saw the release of the Picross S Konami Antiques Edition, which featured popular Konami IP such as Castlevania and Contra. The news of one million sales this week happens to be taking place ahead of the release of the new game Picross S Climax.

Recent findings suggest that picross S Climax launches on the Japanese eShop on 10th December 2026. To help build some excitement, Jupiter has released a demo for the newly introduced title. There'll also be a series sale taking place on 30th November 2026, with the Konami Antiques Edition going on sale for the first time and Picross S 50% off.

Jupiter's history with the Picross video games stretches way back to the Super Nintendo and Game Boy era, with titles such as Mario's Super Picross and Mario's Picross. These games can now be accessed via the Switch Online 'Nintendo Classics' service.
