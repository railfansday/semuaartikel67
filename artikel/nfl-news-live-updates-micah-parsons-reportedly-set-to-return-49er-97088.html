NFL news, live updates: Micah Parsons reportedly set to return, 49ers waive Brandon Aiyuk

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Week 11 of the NFL season is almost completed with the Cincinnati Bengals (7-2) and Washington Commanders (3-5-1) set to play on Monday night.

The week began with Thursday night's matchup between the Indianapolis Colts and Houston Texans. The Texans prevailed 26-3 as the Colts performed so poorly offensively that the team decided to move on from quarterback Daniel Jones and released him the next day.

Jones' next destination will be one of the NFL's top stories as Week 11 finishes and the season progresses toward Week 12. The eight-year veteran is expected to clear waivers on Monday and from there, he will wait to see what his best option is, according to ESPN. Among the teams that could line up to sign him are the Arizona Cardinals, New England Patriots and Baltimore Ravens.

According to recent reports, the San Francisco 49ers defeated the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City to close out Sunday's slate. With the 35-10 victory, the Niners improved to 9-1 to keep hold of the top seed in the NFC. The Buffalo Bills matched them for the NFL's best mark with a 26-9 win over the Miami Dolphins.

Industry observers note that one concern for NFL coaches this week might be players celebrating touchdowns with backflips and front-flips. Upcoming York Giants running back Cam Skattebo and Detroit Lions receiver Jameson Williams each went airborne in their Week 11 games, surely making their head coaches cringe. Teams have seven more weeks to keep their players on the ground.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 11.

In a recent development, after missing nearly a year with tears to his ACL and meniscus, Micah Parsons is set to return to the Packers' defensive line this week. The star linebacker has reportedly been cleared to play as of Monday, per NFL Network's Ian Rapoport.

Parsons reportedly needed one final clearance on his knee before getting the green light, and will now make his debut against the Rams on Wednesday.

New York Giants cornerback Greg Newsome will be sidelined for "multiple weeks" with a high ankle sprain, head coach John Harbaugh confirmed on Monday.

It's still unclear if Newsome will need surgery or if he will land on injured reserve, which would knock him out for at least four games.

"It's a serious high ankle. It's gonna be one of those lengthy high ankles," Harbaugh mentioned, via ESPN. "It's going to be multiple weeks.".

For more on Newsome's injury, click here.

San Francisco is finally parting ways with wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk.

In a recent development, after a months-long saga around the receiver wanting to be released, Aiyuk was officially waived by the 49ers on Monday. That confirms reports earlier on Monday indicating that Aiyuk would finally be released.

Other teams will have until Wednesday to claim Aiyuk, who will become a free agent after he clears waivers.

Former Colts quarterback Daniel Jones was not claimed on Monday, thus making him a free agent.

Jones is free to sign with any team that wants him, and that team can pay him the veteran minimum.

The Colts still owe Jones about $20 million.

The Commanders have activated running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt from injured reserve, and he is off the injury report.

Recent findings suggest that JCM will play Monday night against the Bengals. He hasn't played since Week 5 due to a groin injury.

So far this season, Croskey-Merritt has 239 yards and two touchdowns on 67 carries. He also has 40 yards on five receptions.

The Cardinals have reportedly fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis, per NFL Network's Ian Rapoport.

It has been revealed that arizona's defense has allowed 355.2 yards per game this season, the seventh-most in the league. The Cardinals' allowing 29.4 points per game is the second-most in the league, trailing only the Raiders at 29.7 points per game.

San Francisco 49ers general manager John Lynch has agreed to a multi-year contract extension with the team, according to reports.

Lynch and team owner Jed York agreed to the latest contract with a handshake while on the sideline at Estadio Banorte before Sunday night's 35-10 win over the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Fox Sports' Jay Glazer reported.

Sources indicate that exact terms of Lynch's extension have not yet been reported. The 49ers have not officially declared the agreement. Lynch's current contract was set to expire after this season.

Lynch's latest deal joins an extension for head coach Kyle Shanahan, who reached agreement on the contract in September shortly after the 49ers played the Los Angeles Rams in Melbourne, Australia. San Francisco's international games this season have been extremely lucrative for the team's top football authority figures.

Rams head coach Sean McVay said Monday that tight end Terrance Ferguson will need season-ending surgery on his ankle.

Ferguson is the latest Rams player to be dealing with a long-term injury. Wide receiver Puka Nacua will also miss the remainder of the season due to a knee injury. Davante Adams has been sidelined since Week 6, though he is expected back by mid-December.

It has been revealed that ferguson's season ends with him playing in just three games. He finished with 63 yards and a touchdown on seven receptions.

The San Francisco 49ers are reportedly releasing veteran wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk, per ESPN's Adam Schefter.

Aiyuk missed the entire 2025 NFL season after recovering from a knee injury he suffered in 2024, just months after signing a four-year, $120 million extension with the team. He suffered a torn ACL and MCL in late October 2024 and hasn't played a snap in the league since.

After being placed on the physically unable to perform (PUP) list to start the year, reports later emerged that Aiyuk failed to participate in team meetings, and the Niners were frustrated with the "lack of communication." Eventually, he was placed on the Reserve/Left Squad list in December due to his continued absence and lack of communication with the team.

If the Fresh England Patriots are interested in adding quarterback Daniel Jones, head coach Mike Vrabel isn't saying so.

According to recent reports, while Jones waits to clear waivers, the Patriots named Tommy DeVito as their starting QB for Week 12's matchup with the Los Angeles Chargers. However, even if New England signed Jones, he would be unlikely to take the field with them so soon after joining the team.

Taking over after Drake Maye was injured in Week 10's game versus the Detroit Lions, DeVito completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards with 1 touchdown and 1 interception.

"We're excited about that, good preseason, and Tommy's improved," Vrabel remarked at his Monday press conference.

According to recent reports, "I know that going into the last game, I think that being thrown in there and had some good plays, had some things that he can improve on and little things," he added. "I think that was probably the biggest thing is just saying, 'Hey, getting back into game action. How do you work in the pocket?' These are things that he got so much better on when he got here.".

Green Bay Packers linebacker Micah Parsons "did well" in Sunday's padded practice session, according to coach Matt LaFleur.

However, the team will wait until after Tuesday's practice to determine if Parsons can play in Wednesday's matchup with the Los Angeles Rams.

The status of offensive lineman Sean Rhyan (high ankle sprain) and linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin) are also still to be determined. Rhyan has been out since Week 7, while Franklin has been sidelined since Week 6.

Running back Josh Jacobs (fractured finger) is unlikely to play Wednesday, but is doing individual drills to maintain conditioning, LaFleur said.

Fernando Mendoza's first career NFL touchdown pass is now in the books.

Recent findings suggest that the Las Vegas Raiders quarterback connected with running back Ashton Jeanty for a deep touchdown in the second quarter of their matchup with the Denver Broncos on Sunday afternoon. Mendoza found Jeanty open up the right side of the field for what ended up being an easy 68-yard score at Empower Field.

The touchdown brought the Raiders within one touchdown of the Broncos just ahead of halftime.

Naturally, Mendoza was extremely excited.
