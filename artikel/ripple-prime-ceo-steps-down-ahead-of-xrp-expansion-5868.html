XRP’s financial utility goes way beyond cross-border operations: leadership shift shakes Ripple’s prominent brokerage arm.

Sources indicate that ripple’s Ripple Prime institutional crypto brokerage platform is witnessing a major change. Ripple Prime founder Marc Asch is going to leave the position at year-end as Noel Kimmel assumes leadership.

Industry observers note that noel Kimmel, already serving as Vice President of Ripple Prime, will assume leadership of the business and their team within Ripple Labs. This marks Ripple Prime’s transition from a regular custody & brokerage platform to major-scale financial services & infrastructure provider.

In a recent development, marc Asch founded Hidden Road back in 2018, instantly becoming the growing brokerage’s CEO. The organization offered a wide variety of digital-asset powered services, including foreign exchange, derivatives and fixed income. Ripple made a deal to acquire the platform in 2025.

Since the massive acquisition, Ripple Labs expanded the newly-acquired subsidiary’s business towards leveraged ETF financing. The branch even processed $13 trillion in payments during 2025, but the vague regulations kept all of that trading action outside of crypto’s boundaries.

Ripple Prime founder Marc Asch is stepping down at year end, per CoinDesk, with Noel Kimmel taking over the prime brokerage Ripple bought for $1.25B in 2025. Founders leaving a year after a sale is normal. The test is whether institutions keep growing there. Red flag or nothing?

It has been revealed that right now, Ripple Prime serves over 300 institutions worldwide. The freshly-appointed CEO Noel Kimmel was already deeply involved in this expansion. He previously represented Ripple Prime in its $200 million financing agreement, designed to raise institutional margin capacity.

The brokerage platform went on to introduce equity-linked derivatives and financing for Ripple’s institutional customers. Another key milestone was the deal with Brevan Howard, an accounting company looking after $35 billion in assets under management (AUM) for clearing services.
