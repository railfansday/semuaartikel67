Anthony Clark’s cause of death has been revealed.

Recent findings suggest that the Nineties stand-up comedian who starred in the long-running sitcom died on Nov. 10 at 63. His death certificate listed his cause of death as septic shock, with influenza A and chronic alcoholism noted as underlying conditions, People reported. Acute kidney failure was added as a significant contributing factor.
