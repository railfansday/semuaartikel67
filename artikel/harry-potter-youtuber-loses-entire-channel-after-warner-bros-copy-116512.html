In a recent development, the Harry Potter franchise has experienced a resurgence in fan-created content thanks to the popularity of Malfoid, an internet trend that reimagines Draco Malfoy as a female character. The trend has spawned fan art, AI-generated animations, and alternate-universe stories featuring characters from the franchise.

Sources indicate that however, several creators have reported having their videos removed from YouTube amid preparations for HBO’s upcoming Harry Potter television adaptation. Warner Bros. has now confirmed its involvement in at least one channel’s removal.

“We confirm that the notices are genuine and sent from WB,” Rashid wrote.
