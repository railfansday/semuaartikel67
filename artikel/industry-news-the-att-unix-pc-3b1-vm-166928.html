Industry observers note that the AT&T UNIX PC, aka PC 7300 aka 3B1, is a 1985 Unix desktop developed by Convergent Technologies and sold by AT&T. Emulating it is nothing latest. Phil Pemberton started FreeBee in 2010, and Neozeed has covered it here since: booting the diagnostic disk in 2011, booting AT&T UNIX in 2014 thanks to Andrew Warkentin, and running the C compiler in 2020 thanks to shadyjesse. Arnold Robbins added bigger disks and keeps the 3B1 software alive at skeeve.com/3b1. However there was no networking, and the mouse had to be grabbed with F10.

Now it all has been patched up in my fork of FreeBee:.

It’s all in a pull request to Phil’s upstream FreeBee, still open: https://github.com/philpem/freebee/pull/57, and the CPU fix in one to his Musashi.

Industry observers note that the ultimate UNIX PC image! UNIX System V 3.51m with Fix Disk 2.0 and 187 packages: nearly every program made for the UNIX PC, like Microsoft Word, Multiplan, dBASE III, WordStar 2000, GCC 2.5.8, GNU Emacs 19, TeX and over 80 games. It comes from THE STORE!, the 3B1 archive and the disk images of Arnold Robbins, Alain Knaff and the VCF. Credit is due!

Behold! UNIX System V 3.51m and the Office on an emulated AT&T UNIX PC:.

Specs: AT&T UNIX PC (3B1), 68010 at 10 MHz (10x here), 4 MB RAM, 720x348 one-bit screen, 195 MB disk, 3B1 Ethernet board.

Everything runs in windows that you move, resize and overlap with the mouse, even the system panels:.

The Demonstration Programs are pure eye candy:.

Industry observers note that there are productivity apps too! Microsoft Word and Multiplan take the full screen:.

Now some newly introduced stuff, made in 2026 for this image!

Recent findings suggest that new Games! 2048, Bubble Breaker, Falling Blocks, Snake and Sokoban, all AI generated, as native 3B1 windows in the Office:.

Sources indicate that telnetu! The old WIN/TCP telnet chokes on today’s servers, so I wrote a upcoming one with its own VT100/xterm terminal, UTF-8 and DNS, full screen or in windows: https://github.com/tenox7/telnetu.

Archaic! A tiny web browser for WRP, which renders the page on a modern computer and sends it as a picture with clickable links: https://github.com/tenox7/archaic.

Industry observers note that bonus: the tall screen! Just for fun I patched FreeBee, the kernel, the window driver and the shared library to run at 720×696. It’s a super hacky hack, so it has its own branch and image:.

According to recent reports, as usual I have prepared a ready to run, portable image with FreeBee binaries for macOS (Apple Silicon), Linux (x64 and arm64) and Windows (x64). Just unpack and run run.sh (or run.cmd on Windows). At “Please login:” type tenox for the Office, or root for a shell. There are no passwords. For Archaic, start WRP on your computer first.
