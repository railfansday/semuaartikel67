📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that bitcoin fell below $60,000 on Friday, trading at $59,693, down 3.6% over 24 hours and 11.3% for the week, as heavy selling hit US stocks at the market open. The drop came within the hour, with Bitcoin losing 2.6% as the Nasdaq 100 slid 1.55% and the S&P 500 dipped 0.50%. The Nasdaq 100 is now almost 17% below its peak, close to bear market territory, while the S&P 500 sits about 9% under its record. Altcoins fell harder. Ether dropped 3.9% to $1,657.26, Solana lost 5.6% to $68.17, XRP slid 5.1% to $0.9078 and BNB fell 4.1% to $548.61. Tether and USDC held their dollar pegs. Total crypto market value has slipped below $2.1 trillion for the first time in almost five months, and nearly $1 trillion has been erased since the September top. Investors are pulling back from risk across the board, with markets pricing a near-certain December Fed rate hike and AI stocks under pressure since Anthropic’s IPO.
