Bayern Munich have insisted that Jamal Musiala’s health remains their "top priority" following a "constructive meeting" with representatives of the 23-year-old midfielder.

According to recent reports, earlier this year, the player collapsed on two occasions within four days during pre-season fixtures. It was subsequently disclosed that he was suffering from absence seizures linked to a form of "neurological dysfunction".

The German club faced scrutiny over their handling of the matter after public remarks from senior figures. Honorary president Uli Hoeness questioned if Musiala would "regain the performance level he once had", suggesting his 2025 contract might no longer match his potential on-pitch contributions.

Similar doubts were voiced by member of the supervisor board Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, who proposed renegotiating the agreement, while chief executive Jan-Christian Dreesen questioned the deal, should Musiala struggle to recover his peak conditioning.

These comments drew criticism from global players’ union FIFPRO, which voiced concern over repeated public remarks challenging the player's contract, saying that "a club of Bayern’s standing should know better".

Responding on Wednesday after Tuesday's discussions, Bayern sought to clarify their position in a joint patch with Musiala's agency, 11WINS.

The club stated: "Yesterday, Tuesday, FC Bayern Munich held a constructive meeting with Jamal Musiala’s management. The player’s health is the top priority for both parties. Jamal Musiala will receive full support throughout his recovery.

In a recent development, "Following numerous rumours over the past few days, FC Bayern and 11WINS, the agency representing Jamal Musiala, would like to clarify the following: Jamal Musiala was first examined for symptoms resembling absence seizures on 22 April 2025. This examination took place in consultation with FC Bayern and its medical department, immediately after the neurological symptoms first appeared.

"FC Bayern and Jamal Musiala’s management have agreed to continue their discussions in a spirit of partnership and confidentiality.".

On the pitch, Musiala returned as a substitute during last Friday's 1-1 draw against Cologne, marking his first outing since September after recovering from a hamstring injury. He has also been selected in Bayern's squad to play Lille in the Champions League on Wednesday.
