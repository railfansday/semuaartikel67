Ripple’s RLUSD Supply Hits Record $2.7 Billion, With Ethereum Holding Majority Over XRP Ledger

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, ripple’s RLUSD stablecoin has achieved another milestone. Its total market supply has reached $2.7 billion, making a fresh high level in circulation, according to data reported on the X platform. RLUSD supply climbed to the $2.7 billion record on Sunday, Nov. 15, a 57% rise from the $1.72 billion mark seen on Sept. 14, data revealed.

Recent findings suggest that the market supply of the Ripple-backed stablecoin has surged to a latest ATH, exceeding the previous $1.72 billion milestone set in September. According to the data, RLUSD’s circulating supply currently stands at $2.7 billion, up from the $1.2 billion level reached in January. This rise signals an expansion of RLUSD’s liquidity across the wider blockchain ecosystem.

In a recent development, the supply expansion indicates rising demand for RLUSD as global customers gradually embrace the stablecoin for payments and DeFi applications. This surge positions RLUSD as a ‘digital dollar’ that allows owners to execute cross-border payments and on-chain settlements.

The growth is pushing RLUSD higher into the stablecoin market. Now, the asset ranks as the 7th-largest stablecoin by market supply. The more supply means deeper liquidity across the public blockchain ecosystem, supporting cross-border payments and on-chain utility.

The Ripple-focused institutional stablecoin has reached another milestone, increasing its market supply to $2.7 billion. Interestingly, a large amount of this freshly issued liquidity operates on external public blockchains, not within the XRP Ledger network.

Metrics show that a large share of the new RLUSD supply sits on Ethereum, with Ethereum holding more than those in the XRP Ledger. This indicates rising use of Ethereum as a liquidity and settlement network for the stablecoin, while RLUSD continues to expand across protocols.

Industry observers note that on-chain data indicates that out of the $2.7 billion in RLUSD circulation, about $1.73 billion runs on Ethereum. The XRP Ledger holds around $855 million, representing about 30% of the circulation.

Over time, Ethereum’s share of the supply has risen to about 60%. This shift is because Ethereum offers a deeper DeFi market, including lending and more. XRP Ledger is closer to activating its native lending infrastructure.
