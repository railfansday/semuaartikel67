Arsenal, Rangers and Brighton are all plotting moves to sign Queens Park Rangers sensation Leon Scarlett, sources have told Football Insider.

The 17-year-old has placed a number of clubs on alert both domestically and abroad after breaking into the QPR first-team squad in the early stages of this season.

QPR are desperate to keep hold of the teenager, and are desperate to fight off interest, as they believe he will become a star player at Loftus Road.

Due to his age, Scarlett would not be able to move to Europe until next summer, and that has alerted Arsenal, Rangers and Brighton, who could move ahead of their rivals in January.

Clubs are aware that the European market will open up for the winger after his 18th birthday in August, and fear they could miss out on one of the Championship's brightest young talents.

Scarlett joined QPR in January 2025, and they are confident they can keep him around long-term, with his role in the senior side more than he would be offered elsewhere.

Scarlett's emergence as part of the QPR senior side has placed a host of clubs across Europe on high alert, while domestic sides are tempted to move quickly for him.

At just 17 years old, he has made six Championship appearances so far this season, and has also picked up two caps for Wales' Under-21s.

That has sparked interest from mutliple clubs, notably including Arsenal, Rangers and Brighton, who feel he could one day develop into a leading player at the top level.

Arsenal's interest may come as a particularly enticing option, with the winger having been on the books at the Emirates for around a decade during his youth career.

Eventually, Scarlett was allowed to leave his boyhood club in search of opportunities, and landed at QPR, where he has been allowed to prove himself at a higher level.

Arsenal's interest in Scarlett comes as part of a drive to strengthen their academy sides by bringing in top young talents from across the country to aid their development.

The QPR winger is, therefore, not the only gem on their radar, with scouts working to identify the best young players in the country.

Football Insider revealed on 4 November that Arsenal have set their sights on Rangers sensation Kai Urban, who is seen as an outstanding young talent at Ibrox.

Still only 15, Urban has shown promising signs of long-term development and could be lured away from Ibrox, with Arsenal strong admirers of his.

Mikel Arteta's side are hoping to snatch Urban away from his boyhood club, and feel that he could be a future Premier League player.
