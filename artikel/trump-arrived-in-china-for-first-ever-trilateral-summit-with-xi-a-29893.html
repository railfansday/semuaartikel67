Trump arrived in China for first-ever trilateral summit with Xi and Putin

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

President Donald Trump touched down in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday, setting the stage for Thursday's discussions at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit involving Xi Jinping, China's leader, and Russian President Vladimir Putin, according to CNBC.

The Thursday session will be the first trilateral meeting among the three leaders, according to CNN.

Industry observers note that xi greeted Trump on the tarmac. Xi remarked the gesture reciprocated Trump's welcome for him at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland this year, according to CNBC. In his current role, Xi has not previously met a visiting U.S. president at the airport, according to CNN.

Putin exchanged a handshake with Trump at the opening dinner for APEC leaders Wednesday night, local time, according to CNN. Marco Rubio, the secretary of state, Scott Bessent, who heads the Treasury Department, and Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, are accompanying Trump at the banquet, according to CNBC. Putin mentioned he expects "considerable potential benefits" from the talks, according to CNN.

On Thursday, Trump is due to attend an APEC conference session and hold a meeting with Xi. He will then join the meeting with Xi and Putin before traveling back to the White House, according to CNN. Trump and Xi are hoping to secure a trade truce that lasts beyond January 2027, according to CNBC. The two leaders agreed in September to extend the current truce until January 2027, according to Bloomberg.

The talks are anticipated to revolve around the conflict with Iran and Russia’s war in Ukraine, according to CNBC. A Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson mentioned Monday that hopes for any tangible result are slim, according to CNBC.

Recent findings suggest that trump has noted denuclearization would be the focus of the three-way meeting, according to Bloomberg. A senior administration official told reporters before the trip that Trump wants to talk about the Iran war with Putin and Xi, while Ukraine is not likely to be on his list, according to Bloomberg. Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said the discussion will probably touch on the fighting in Ukraine and may also address Iran, according to CNN.

Kremlin foreign policy adviser Yuri Ushakov noted Trump and Putin could also hold separate talks on Thursday without large delegations, according to CNN. The senior administration official said before the trip that no one-on-one session between Trump and Putin is anticipated, according to Bloomberg.

According to recent reports, xi met Putin on Wednesday before Trump landed. Xi urged deeper collaboration between Beijing and Moscow, and Putin noted Russia hopes to broaden its trade, energy and education ties, according to CNBC.
