There’s something undeniably chic about a head of long, luscious hair. Think glossy, waist-skimming, bouncy, voluminous lengths that make extensions an option rather than a long-term commitment. What’s more, long hair provides the ultimate canvas for experimentation, with styling possibilities that feel limitless.

As hair matures, however, maintaining the same level of shine, strength, and vitality can require more attention. As texture changes and dryness increases, the key is to adapt your routine to give your longer lengths the care they deserve. “Long hair can look beautiful at any age, but keeping mature hair glossy and healthy-looking is all about replenishing moisture and maintaining shine,” indicates celebrity hairstylist Naomi Brooks. “I always recommend prioritising hydrating products that help smooth the hair cuticle, improve softness, and enhance shine, alongside regular trims to keep the lengths and ends looking healthy.”.

Sources indicate that if you’re considering making the transition and letting your bob grow out, look to a host of celebrities for inspiration. From Demi Moore and Naomi Campbell to Andie MacDowell and Claudia Schiffer, countless iterations are proving just how effortless long hair can look on women over 50. As Brooks explains: “Demi Moore is a fantastic example of how beautiful long hair can look at any age. Her hair is consistently glossy, healthy-looking, and full of movement.”.

While genetics certainly play a role, Moore’s glossy lengths are also the result of great care and strategic products. Her hairstylist, Dimitris Giannetos, revealed on social media that he often relies on Kérastase’s Gloss Absolu Glaze Drops Anti-Frizz Hair Oil to reduce frizz, boost shine, and ensure hair remains sleek without looking too heavy. Indeed, experts recommend investing in a high-quality hydrating treatment to ensure mature hair is treated with extra care. “Mature hair can become drier, finer or more fragile over time, so maintaining moisture and shine is important,” states Warren Edwards, creative director at Warren & the Hare.

In a recent development, additionally, for those with gray or silver hair, session stylist Danilo Giangreco recommends incorporating a clear gloss into your routine to enhance shine. This can also help counteract any unwanted brassiness, ensuring hair appears brighter and healthier.

In a recent development, another expert-approved tip is to keep heat styling to a minimum where possible. If you do rely on hot tools, applying heat protectant can help shield strands from damage while preserving moisture, strength, and shine.

Finally, contrary to popular belief, long hair thrives on regular maintenance. Though it may seem counterintuitive when trying to grow your hair, routine appointments are essential to keep split ends at bay and prevent the ends from looking too thin or tired. In turn, this helps keep long, mature hair looking fuller, healthier, and overall more glossy.
