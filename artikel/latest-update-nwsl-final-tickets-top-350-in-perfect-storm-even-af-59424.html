Latest Update: NWSL final tickets top $350 in ‘perfect storm’ even after fall from $755 peak

🌐 Source: Theguardian • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (437 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Get-in price peaked at $755 before dropping to $362.
  • Previous NWSL finals never topped $100 on matchday.
  • Analyst cites Spirit home game and thin resale supply.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Get-in price peaked at $755 before dropping to $362.

Previous NWSL finals never topped $100 on matchday.

Analyst cites Spirit home game and thin resale supply.

The get-in price for tickets to Saturday’s NWSL Championship match plunged more than 50% this week but was still set to finish at more than three times the previous record for an NWSL final.

After the Washington Spirit booked their spot in the final against the Kansas City Current with a semi-final victory last Saturday, the get-in price for the championship match spiked as high as $755 that same night, according to TicketData.com. However, that price had dropped as low as $308 on Friday morning before climbing back to $362 by midday.

Industry observers note that while representing a 52.1% drop from Saturday’s high, the get-in price for previous NWSL championship matches has never settled higher than $100, according to TicketData.com. Last year’s championship match between the Spirit and Gotham FC in San Jose settled at a get-in price of $64.

Industry observers note that the Spirit will be playing in their home stadium at Audi Field, contributing to the record ticket-price pace. Unlike most playoff matches, the NWSL Championship is awarded to a host city well in advance, making the Spirit’s appearance in the final a particularly fortunate coincidence for the league. Kansas City earned their spot with a dramatic 2-1 win over Gotham FC on Sunday afternoon.

Recent findings suggest that US ticketing analyst Keith Smith described the extraordinary prices as a “perfect storm”, pointing to the Spirit’s home-field advantage and unusually limited ticket availability on the resale market. Because NWSL matches have not traditionally attracted significant interest from ticket resellers, he stated on social media, there has been little inventory available on secondary-market platforms throughout the week.

Industry observers note that for comparison, the get-in price for last year’s MLS Cup, won by Inter Miami in South Florida, settled at $271 after peaking at $806. Smith noted that tickets for that match, featuring Lionel Messi, were available in the mid-$200s on match day – considerably less than the cheapest seats for Saturday’s NWSL final.

Industry observers note that smith also pointed to the record prices for this year’s WNBA finals as further evidence of the growing commercial appeal of women’s sports. The two championship events, taking place just weeks apart, have underscored a surge in demand that would have been difficult to imagine in previous years, when NWSL final did not always sell out.

NWSL commissioner Jessica Berman said this week that high ticket prices for the championship represent a “good problem to have”.
