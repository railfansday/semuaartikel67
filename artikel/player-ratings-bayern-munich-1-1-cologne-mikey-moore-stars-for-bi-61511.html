Bayern Munich maintained their unbeaten start to the season but had to come from behind to secure a share of the spoils against struggling Cologne with Nathaniel Brown cancelling out Mikey Moore’s first-half strike.

The substantial news coming into Friday’s Matchday 10 curtain-raiser was Vincent Kompany’s return to the touchline, one month after he collapsed at the club’s training ground.

Bayern remained unchanged following their 2-1 victory against Mainz prior to the international break, but the notable inclusion on the bench was 19-year-old Noah Codjo-Evora, who was named in Bayern’s matchday squad for the first time, while Cologne made once change after their 2-1 derby defeat against Bayer Leverkusen, with Luka Lochoshvili coming into defence for attacker Marius Bülter.

It has been revealed that michael Olise threatened early on for the hosts, but it was Cologne who took an early lead at the Allianz Arena. Tottenham Hotspur loanee Mikey Moore opened his Bundesliga account for the season in the 12th minute, rounding Jonas Urbig and slotting into an empty net. It then went from bad to worse for Bayern when Harry Kane was forced off with what looked to be an ankle injury.

Bayern’s equaliser arrived in the 37th minute when Nathaniel Brown struck from a central position just outside of the area, stroking the ball into the bottom corner past Marvin Schwäbe to level proceedings heading into the interval.

After colliding with Cologne’s Tom Krauß in the first half, Olise remained in the dressing room at the break, with Kompany awarding Tim Binder his Bundesliga debut, as their injuries continued to mount up with Serge Gnabry and Luis Díaz already sidelined.

Sources indicate that shortly after the break, Moore came close to adding to his tally but was denied by Urbig from close range before Lennart Karl, who had been quiet in the first period, fired just wide minutes later. Urbig was called into action again, first to deny Remarked El Mala after a dizzying-run from Moore on the hour mark, before Linton Maina’s well struck follow-up volley stung the palms of the Bayern stopper – who is yet to keep a clean sheet this season.

According to recent reports, both teams had chances to win it in the dying embers. Jamal Musiala, returning from injury, placed the ball just the wrong side of the post after superb play from Josip Stanišić and Karl before El Mala placed his shot from close-range agonisingly wide at the other end following Sebastian Sebulonsen’s cross.

The result keeps Bayern top of the Bundesliga, but Borussia Dortmund can climb above the leaders with a win against Borussia Mönchengladbach on Saturday, while Cologne remain winless in five Bundesliga games.
