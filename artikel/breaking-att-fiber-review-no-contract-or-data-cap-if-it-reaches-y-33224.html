AT&T Fiber is AT&T’s fiber-optic home internet, sold where its network reaches in parts of 32 states. AT&T lists 300 Mbps (megabits per second) at $35 a month plus taxes for the first year with AutoPay and paperless billing, and at today’s prices it costs $50 after that, though prices can vary by address.

It has been revealed that households AT&T Fiber reaches should take it over a cable strategy with the same download speed and price once both promotions end, since its uploads match its downloads. Light customers and anyone whose address gets only AT&T Internet Air, its 5G home internet with typical downloads of 90 to 300 Mbps, should price the cable plans at their address first.
