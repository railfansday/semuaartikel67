Ripple's U.S. dollar stablecoin Ripple USD (RLUSD) has overtaken Gram (GRAM), the token previously known as Toncoin, in the CoinMarketCap ranking of cryptocurrencies by market capitalization.

It has been revealed that according to CoinMarketCap data, RLUSD had a market capitalization of $2.82 billion on Tuesday and ranked No. 26, and Gram dropped to No. 27 with a market capitalization of $2.79 billion.

Gram fell below $1 and was trading at around $0.97 on Tuesday, down about 6% over the past seven days, according to CoinMarketCap. The 24-hour trading volume of Gram stood at around $47 million, and RLUSD had about $368 million in trading volume over the same period.

World Liberty Financial USD (USD1) also had a market capitalization of $2.82 billion on Tuesday and was one spot higher, at No. 25. Among stablecoins, Tether (USDT) and USDC still lead the list with $174.64 billion and $69.22 billion, respectively.

Industry observers note that in late July, the market value of RLUSD was about $1.5 billion, according to RWA.xyz data. On Aug. 25, Ripple stated that RLUSD had crossed $2 billion in market capitalization, with close to $1 billion of the supply issued on the XRP Ledger. By mid-September, RLUSD supply stood at $2.44 billion.

In late October, the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) gave Ripple approval for Ripple National Trust Bank. A day later, on Oct. 27, Moody's Ratings assigned RLUSD an A2 long-term deposit rating and a P-1 short-term deposit rating. RLUSD is issued by Ripple subsidiary Standard Custody & Trust Firm. Also in late October, RLUSD became available on Coinbase and Robinhood.

The Open Network renamed its token from Toncoin to Gram in June 2026. On July 21, Telegram founder Pavel Durov announced a native non-custodial Gram wallet for every Telegram app. "This summer will see the largest rollout of a non-custodial crypto wallet in human history. Instant zero-fee crypto transactions for over a billion users are about to become reality," Durov wrote.

This week, Telegram published the terms of service for Gram Wallet. According to the terms, the wallet is operated by RAZOR UNIVERSAL CORP., a Panamanian manufacturer, and Telegram provides the apps through which customers access it. "Telegram does not operate Gram Wallet, does not hold your keys or assets, and is not a party to these Terms," the document claims.

In a recent development, the terms also say that "neither we nor Telegram can freeze, seize, move, recover or reverse your assets." At the same time, according to the terms, "anyone who visits your Telegram profile may be able to see your associated Gram Wallet address," as well as the total balance of the wallet. Access to the wallet "may be restricted or unavailable in certain regions or to certain owners," the document says.
