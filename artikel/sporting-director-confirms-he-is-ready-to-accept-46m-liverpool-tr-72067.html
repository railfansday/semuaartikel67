Deco confirms Barcelona happy with £46m Liverpool transferRonald Araujo has already become a fan favourite at Anfield. Liverpool have an agreement in place with Barcelona to buy him permanently for £46m at the end of his loan deal next year. Now speaking to Catalunya Radio via ESPN Barcelona sporting director Deco confirmed that his club are happy to accept Liverpool's agreement and would be open to allowing Araujo to leave, especially because they could use the money from the deal. "We work for Barcelona and want what's best for the club - to win, to build the best possible squad - but we are also humans and we also want to build relationships. "Ronald's been one of the club captains in recent years. He has had difficult moments, but also moments of great happiness and he has been one of the leaders of this team. "Coming back to the football, things weren't going especially well for him on the pitch. He took the decision [to join Liverpool] and we respect it. The most key thing for us now is seeing Ronald recovered, happy, enjoying himself and in a positive place. "If he decides to stay, Liverpool have an option to make the deal permanent. They would also have to negotiate with him -- it's not just about paying the clause -- but we would be happy for several reasons [if the deal goes through]. "Firstly, because the move has worked out and because Ronald is happy and is able to continue his career successfully. We want players to succeed. Also, the money would not hurt, either." Based on those words it's a confirmation that Barcelona are happy for Liverpool to proceed with a permanent transfer for Araujo. Whether Ward decides to activate the agreement remains to be seen. But Araujo being his first transfer looks very likely at this stage.
