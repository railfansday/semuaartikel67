Left-back has become a persistent concern for Liverpool this season.

The departure of Andy Robertson at the end of his contract in the summer marked the end of an era at Anfield, and there is a clear gap in Andoni Iraola's side.

Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas have both failed to make the position their own, leading to plenty of speculation that the Reds will move to sign a new left-back either this winter or in the summer.

Several top options are now being linked with Merseyside moves as a result, but Liverpool have just been told how much competition they'll face for one of their top targets.

They won't be unopposed in the chase for the Belgian however, with Arsenal, PSG, Juventus, AC Milan and AS Roma all closely monitoring the 25-year-old.

That's according to an update from Ekrem Konur, who states that De Cuyper's form has made him one of the most sought-after fullbacks in Europe.

It's unclear exactly how much Brighton will demand for their upcoming star, but they will likely want a marked increase on the £17 million they paid Club Bruges in 2025.

A figure of £50 million has been reported, but the Seagulls have no interest in selling mid-season so it could take an even higher fee to secure the 25-year-old this winter.

Liverpool will face some tough competition for De Cuyper if they wait until the summer, so they may be better off paying a premium to secure his services in January.
