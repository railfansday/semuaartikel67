The club stalwart has been put in temporary chatge at The Saffrons while the club look for a permanent replacement for Macdonald, whose axing alongside assistant Ben Davis has provoked an angry reaction from many fans and seen some players leave – includihg one who’d only rejoined the club on Friday.

The latest development at the Isthmian South East club came on Monday evening, when a club statement noted: “To serve for an interim period, Eastbourne Town F.C are pleased to announce the appointment of popular club stalwart Dave Winterton as First Team Manager.

"Dave is, of course, very well known to most people in the local area for his long and distinguished playing, coaching and management career with our club, and we sincerely thank him for agreeing to take on this role.

"During this time, Dave will be assisted by one of our experienced senior players, Arron Hopkinson. Our thanks also go to him.

"We hope that supporters will get behind Dave and the team during this period of transition, starting this coming Saturday away at Erith Town, whilst the search for a permanent successor to Jude Macdonald continues.”.

Dave is a second generation Winterton at Eastbourne Town – his father Derek played as a defender for the club in the 1950s.

He was club manager between June 2001 and January 2005, taking Town, who were playing in Division 2 of the Sussex League, into Division 1 and winning the Sussex Intermediate Cup. In 2020 Dave became the Goalkeeper coach for the club.

The move to make him interim boss comes after the surprise news on Saturday evening that Macdonald had been sacked with Town one off the bottom of the division but after two brighter league performances that had seen a point taken from each of two clashes with Sheppey and Whitstable.

Town’s statement on Saturday noted: “Eastbourne Town FC have today parted company with First Team Manager Jude Macdonald and Ben Davis.

"The Committee would like to place on record their sincere thanks to Jude and Ben for their hard work over the last 4½ years, where, following their arrival, they promptly reshaped the remaining playing squad, and with some exciting new arrivals, just two years later launched an amazing mid-season recovery to storm up the SCFL Premier Division table to finish Runners-Up and then win the League Play-off Final.

"They then successfully kept the Club in the Isthmian F.L for successive seasons when largely up against many teams with much greater budgets and resources, a feat which we believe must be seen as a remarkable achievement in what is known by many to be a very tough division.

"The club wish them both all the very best for the future and thank them once again for their service during their time at The Saffrons. No further statements will be made at this time.”.

A number of Town fans have taken to social media to condemn Macdonald’s removal, calling it shocking and disgraceful.

At least a couple of players have left – including James Waters, who Macdonald had re-signed for the club only at the end of last week.
