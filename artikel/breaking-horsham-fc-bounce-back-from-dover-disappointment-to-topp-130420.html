Lee Harding was then sent off late on for a second yellow card offence.

Industry observers note that di Paola told us: “I don’t think we played particularly well. I can’t argue with the scoreline. There are no real arguments, really. I think they were the better team.

It has been revealed that “The pitch meant you couldn’t really play any football, but that suited them. I’m not a football snob. There’s no wrong or right way of playing football. The way they play is effective and works for them.

“I wasn’t that frustrated, angry or disappointed after the game, I just felt we didn’t have the right amount of intensity – and it’s not the kind of place you can go without an intent to battle and fight.”.

Horsham then became the first side to defeat second-placed Truro in National League South since mid-September.

Reece Myles-Meekums’ 16th minute strike proved to be the difference between the two sides. The win leaves the Hornets 14th in the table.

In a recent development, di Paola added: “If either them or Ebbsfleet don’t win the league then I couldn’t tell you who would. They’re by far and away the best two teams we’ve played.

“We had to suffer but we suffered as a unit. I don’t think Michael [Dike] had loads to do in goal. He made one good save but in general he was sweeping and tidying up.

“The game would have been made much easier if Nadders [Ashley Nadesan] had scored his one-on-one in the second half. It would have taken the game away from them – but if you don’t score the second you have to suffer.

It has been revealed that “That’s something we know we have to do better at, putting teams away. But it’s such a hard league.

In a recent development, “It was always going to be a huge challenge. I don’t think Truro had lost in a couple of months. They’re a top, top side, so to collect a win was really pleasing.”.

In a recent development, meanwhile, goalkeeper Sam Howes has had his contract cancelled by the Hornets. His departure has seen the return of young Brighton stopper Dike on a 28-day loan.

Horsham travel to sixth-placed Billericay Town this Saturday – and Di Paola revealed a number of his midfielders were stricken going into the the trip to Essex.

Industry observers note that he noted: “Everyone’s on their backsides at the moment! Ronan [Silva] is a doubt. He’s been so unlucky. He took a bash to his ankle [against Truro] and it swelled up. It’s not his hamstring, which is one good thing.

“Charlie played injured against Truro because we needed him – but those are the kinds of boys we want at the football club.”.
