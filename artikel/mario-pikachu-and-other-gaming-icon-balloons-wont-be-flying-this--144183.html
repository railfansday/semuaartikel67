In a recent development, new York City is no stranger to bad weather, be it blazing-hot summers, snow-packed winters, or fierce windstorms. Unfortunately for countless families who have made it a holiday tradition to turn on the TV and watch giant totems of pop culture sail in the streets of the great city, the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade has been officially delayed due to a forecast of rain and windstorms--a first in its 100-year history.

It has been revealed that in recent years, gaming icons have become more and more prominent during the parade. Beginning in 1993, a balloon of Sonic the Hedgehog marked the first game character to float among the procession. Since then, more characters have joined the festivities, including Mario, Pac-Man, Pikachu, and even Red the Angry Bird, among others.

However, those who have followed the history of Macy's balloons know that the mix of bad weather and massive, floating objects can result in genuine danger for balloon handlers, bystanders, and objects on the streets. Rather infamously, Sonic's 1993 appearance was marred by an incident where the balloon got stuck on a lamppost, popping the balloon and injuring two bystanders.

Other balloon bursts have proved even more catastrophic, with a series of mishaps in 1997 leading to a Cat in the Hat balloon causing injuries that left a woman comatose for days. Since then, more emphasis has rightfully been put on safety.

While it might be disappointing to not be able to point at the TV and go "Mario!" with your younger cousins around the TV tomorrow, the balloons will get a chance to fly on Saturday starting at 8:30 AM ET, when the weather is likely to be significantly less awful. You can find other ways to fill the time--but maybe ask your relatives first if it's okay to let the kiddos watch you play GTA 6.
