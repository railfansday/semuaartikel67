NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

According to recent reports, coreWeave (CRWV +22.32%), a specialized cloud infrastructure provider for generative AI workloads, closed at $48.33, up 22.32%. Monday's advance followed renewed AI-sector optimism after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, and investors are now watching AI demand and capital spending.Trading volume reached 93.2 million shares, coming in about 91% above its three-month average of 48.9 million shares. CoreWeave IPO'd in 2025 and has grown 21% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.73%) closed at 7,237, up 4.73%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.51%) finished at 24,090, up 6.51%. In specialized cloud infrastructure and AI computing services, Nebius Group rose 20.28% to $97.80, and Iren climbed 23.86% to $14.64 as AI cloud names advanced together.

The Nvidia news clearly drove today's move, and for good reason. Nvidia not only owns almost 11% of CoreWeave, but it has also committed over $6 billion in business to CoreWeave. CoreWeave disclosed the order in late 2025, and it requires Nvidia to buy any cloud capacity CoreWeave doesn't sell to customers through April 2032.

CoreWeave is also dependent on the overall financial strength and capital spending of AI labs, hyperscalers, and other businesses. In its third-quarter report earlier this month, CoreWeave raised 2026 guidance, and now expects revenue growth to reach up to 160%, citing AI infrastructure demand strength. Nvidia is the backbone of that strength in many ways, and investors in CoreWeave will also need to believe that Nvidia remains on track despite the Jensen Huang scandal.
