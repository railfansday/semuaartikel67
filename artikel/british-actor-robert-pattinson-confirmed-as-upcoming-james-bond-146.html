Robert Pattinson will be cast as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig after he retired from the ​long-running ‌spy series.

Amazon MGM Studios made the announcement on social media on Monday.

James Bond 007, which follows the saga of a secret agent known for ‌his charm, is among the longest-running and highest-grossing movie franchises in Hollywood's ​history, debuting in 1962.

The role has been played by critically acclaimed actors such as Sean Connery, Pierce Brosnan, and Roger Moore, among others.

The British actor is the seventh James Bond in history, and rumors of his casting only emerged recently when it was revealed he had auditioned for the role.

"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson remarked in a statement. "To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.".

That team includes Oscar-nominated Denis Villeneuve, who will direct the next Bond movie from a script by "Peaky Blinders" creator Steven Knight.

Pattinson's first outing will come in the 26th Bond film, and the first since "No Time To Die" in 2021.
