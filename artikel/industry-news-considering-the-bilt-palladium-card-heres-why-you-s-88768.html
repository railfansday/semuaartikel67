Industry observers note that for me, the logic of picking up this card is pretty straightforward, and I’ve shared that in much more detail. The idea is that the combination of the 2x points plus 4% back in the form of Bilt Cash is an unrivaled return on spending, especially given the value of Bilt points. Spending on Bilt cards also earns you Bilt elite status, and that unlocks better Rent Day promotions.

In a recent development, on the most basic level, that Bilt Cash earns me 1x points per dollar spent on my housing payments (without any sort of fee), plus I take advantage of the Points Accelerator feature, which earns me an extra 1x points per dollar spent up to five times per year, by redeeming $200 in Bilt Cash. In other words, this card earns me 3x points on up to $25,000 of spending per year, plus I’m being rewarded on my housing spending.

Points with Bilt rack up really quickly, so if you spend quite a bit on credit cards, I do think this is tough to beat. And this doesn’t even account for the value of the hotel credit, as you can stack the credit card’s hotel credit with some Bilt Cash to get an even bigger discount on a hotel stay.

As I’ve covered, changes are coming to Bilt as of 2027. While the changes are being made in the name of “simplifying” things, I’d argue that it only creates even more complexity, at least for existing members. Essentially, starting in 2027, Bilt members will earn points for their housing payment as a percentage of their card spending, in lieu of earning 4% back in Bilt Cash on all their spending.

In a recent development, the good news is that through December 31, 2026, you can still opt into the old system for being rewarded, and that will then be honored in 2027 and beyond. This applies both to existing cardmembers, and those who pick up the card before then, and select this as their earnings preference.
