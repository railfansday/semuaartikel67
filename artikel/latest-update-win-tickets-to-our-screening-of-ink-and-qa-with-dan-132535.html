Industry observers note that hot off the press, Oscar-winning director Danny Boyle’s (Slumdog Millionaire) docudrama Ink, with Jack O’Connell (Sinners, 28 Years Later: Bone Temple) and Guy Pearce (The Brutalist, The Dog Stars), is heading to select theaters soon before debuting on Netflix. Ahead of its streaming premiere, Collider is proud to announce that we’re partnering with Netflix to offer our readers in New York City the opportunity to join us for a very special screening event, followed by an exclusive Q&A with Boyle. Read on for the full details on how you can enter for your chance to win tickets!

It has been revealed that if you’re in the New York City area, don’t miss your chance to see Ink in theaters with us. This special screening event will take place on Friday, December 11th at the AMC Empire 25. The screening will begin at 8:00 p.m., followed by the Q&A.

Don’t miss out on your chance to win free tickets to this fan event! This screening and Q&A will fill up swiftly, so claim your seats while you can. For your chance to join us on December 11th for a special event screening of Ink, plus a Q&A with Boyle, hit this link to provide us with your email address. Be sure to let us know if you’d like to bring a guest with you, and RSVP as soon as possible to secure your spot. All confirmed guests will receive an email confirmation.

Sources indicate that ink is based on screenwriter James Graham’s Tony Award-winning play that follows a visionary group of misfits with an idea that would change tabloid journalism and the world as we know it forever. Based on a true story, the movie takes place in 1969, when rising media tycoon Rupert Murdoch, portrayed in the movie by Pearce, acquires the newspaper The Sun. In a team-up with editor Larry Lamb, played by O’Connell, they exploit sensational news to turn their paper into an explosive hit. In addition to O’Connell and Lamb, Ink also stars Claire Foy (The Crown), Jonathan Hyde (The Brutalist), Georgie Henley (The Chronicles of Narnia), and Bertie Carvel (A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms).

According to recent reports, ink is in theaters now and begins streaming on Netflix on January 8th. Be sure to check out Collider for more special event screenings.
