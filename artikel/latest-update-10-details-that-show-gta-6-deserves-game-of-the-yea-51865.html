Grand Theft Auto 6 is now available to the public, but the controversial part doesn’t have anything to do with the game itself, but rather, what the game has been nominated for.

In a recent development, the nominees for Game of the Year at The Game Awards have officially been announced, and before GTA 6 was available to the public, it was placed as a nominee. In fact, it was nominated for more than just Game of the Year.

This has obviously bothered many gamers, but there is a reason why GTA 6 is on this list of nominees. Let’s break down why this long-awaited title deserves Game of the Year.

Rockstar is known for adding some great music to games. GTA 5 has a pretty great soundtrack, with it featuring numerous artists. Because of that, it was no surprise that Rockstar started announcing various artists for GTA 6.

Sources indicate that the radio is stacked with some amazing musicians who took the time to write fresh songs specifically for the game. With so much talent, it is difficult not to get lost in the beat, so long as you are someone who enjoys each artist’s music.

In the end, users recognize that this talent isn’t just for show, and the music in GTA 6 fits the vibe of the crime-forward series, making it feel more alive.

People have been waiting 13 years to get their hands on GTA 6. Ever since Grand Theft Auto 5 was released, people have been asking for the next game in the series. Well, over the course of those 13 years, Rockstar has been working hard.

The team has been busting their butts to develop GTA 6 and make it as massive as possible. They have talked about how they created new systems, enhanced older ones, and added a ton of detail into their newest game.

With this much effort put in, there is no denying that the team deserves some recognition, and just being nominated reveals that people care.

Recent findings suggest that for a long time, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time held the record for being the highest rated game of all time with a 99 on MetaCritic. Now that reviews for GTA 6 are out, it shares the same score as Ocarina of Time.

With that being noted, there were 22 professional reviews for Ocarina of Time, and over 150 for GTA 6, so GTA 6 has a more accurate representation from multiple parties, putting it above Ocarina of Time.

That isn’t a fluke. You can look at a few review scores, see a 10/10 and write it off, but when there are over 100 different people giving it the same score or something similar, that isn’t a coincidence.

Industry observers note that there are many people, who, when they purchase a game, are in it for the visual aspect. There is nothing wrong with this, and with the advancement of graphics engines, it has become commonplace for games to try and look lifelike.

Grand Theft Auto 6 has some of the best visuals of any game that has been released this year, and even in the last decade. The developers at Rockstar spend a lot of time trying to make every shot look as cinematic as possible.

The result of this, is one of the most beautiful games to come out in a very long time, and it will hold up for many years to come. The only slight downside is the 30fps, but that is a small price to pay for this level of beauty.

Rockstar has spent ages working on GTA 6, and a major part of that work has gone into making the world feel more alive rather than a sandbox that you can run and gun in like it was in GTA 5. Previous titles didn’t respect you being a character in the world, but rather let you do whatever you wanted.

NPCs have received a major overhaul in GTA 6, reacting more to you being in the world, and turning the game into a major life sim where people will get angry with you if you pull a gun, run away in fear, and much more.

In addition to this, there are tons of activities to take part in, so much so that you can ignore the main quest for ages and still have plenty to do. You definitely won’t get bored with this game.

Instead of focusing on fleshing out three individual characters, this time around Rockstar decided to focus on creating a couple. Their names are Jason and Lucia, and their story is powerful, and they are far more fleshed out than characters in the previous game.

According to recent reports, jason and Lucia are madly in love, and they will do anything to stand by one another. The Bonnie and Clyde dynamic fits the location of Vice City very well, and it is a story that many didn’t think would work at first.

This type of emphasis on building a relationship is something that we haven’t seen in a while, and Rockstar made it work very well, meaning it is a game that will be remembered for a long time.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar is known for making some incredible characters, and GTA 6 is no exception to this. From the beginning to the end, there are numerous characters that you meet that have a lasting effect on the story and some that are only there for a moment but leave an impression.

According to recent reports, think about your time with GTA 5 and how it was fun interacting with the game’s characters. They made you laugh, and they certainly are remembered.

Recent findings suggest that well, GTA 6 has that and then some, with a cast of side characters that each have a standout personality that will make you remember them for some time to come. It shows that the devs knew what they were doing when creating the world of Leonida.

Recent findings suggest that if there is something that the Rockstar devs understand it is comedy. There was so much of it baked into GTA 5 that it was difficult to avoid. Now that same energy has been carried over to GTA 6 and it is fantastic.

If you are a fan of satire then be prepared to see it everywhere in GTA 6 because nearly everything is a satirical take on a company, person, or event.

Sources indicate that rockstar devs are masters of comedic timing and satire, and GTA 6 proves it. It is a game that will have you chuckling at the references and laughing at the absurdity. It hits the funny bone and there is no denying that that plays a major role in why it is so good.

Something that I like in games is when you can edit your character in some way. I like to make my own character or dress up existing characters in fun outfits, and GTA 6 has that and then some.

Industry observers note that similar to previous games in the series, there are numerous outfits that you can purchase for your characters, which is a dream for those who like to customize their look. On top of that, you can edit your cars, and you can also edit your appearance through working out.

Recent findings suggest that that’s right. The game is detailed enough that Lucia and Jason will gain weight, their hair will grow long, and more. You can spend time truly focusing on your character’s appearance, and that is something I want out of my game of the year.

In a recent development, let’s be honest, there are many games that are released that are buggy messes. For instance, massive games like Crimson Desert and Assassin’s Creed Shadows had some massive glitches that hurt the games when they were first released.

Industry observers note that it took some rather large patches to fix them, and even then there were still problems. GTA 6 is one of the few games that have been released that has little to no issues with it, and in 2026, that is a massive feat, especially for a game with the the scope of GTA 6.

The lack of issues with the game at launch with how massive it is makes it a much more enticing pick for Game of the Year.
