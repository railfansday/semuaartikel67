The chaotic saga of litigation which followed the 2025 AFCON final will finally be resolved this week, as the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) prepares to release its final decision.

The champion of AFCON 2025 remains in dispute after the scandalous events of January 18th, where Senegal were stripped of a 1-0 victory after their squad briefly left the pitch in the second half.

Recent findings suggest that an unprecedented and unsavoury series of events followed. Morocco appealed the result and won, before Senegal escalated the affair to CAS in Lausanne, the world’s premier body for the resolution of sporting disputes.

Now, ten months after the full-time whistle was blown in Rabat, AFCON 2025 will finally have an undisputed champion, though the animosity will surely remain, regardless of the verdict.

Here is everything you need to know about the situation ahead of the final verdict being released...

An official press release from CAS confirms the decision will be released on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

It has been revealed that no specific time of day has been set. The press release simply says the decision will be released “in the course of the afternoon.”.

Morocco and Senegal met in the AFCON 2025 final, but the match was beset by unprecedented controversy.

Late in regular time, Morocco were awarded a penalty, which Senegal protested by leaving the pitch.

Industry observers note that players and staff, led by head coach Pape Thiaw, alike returned to the changing rooms, and the match was paused for 14 minutes. Some Senegal players remained on the pitch and returned to the pitch only at the behest of captain Sadio Mane.

Crucially, the referee never abandoned the match, and play resumed once the entire Senegal squad had returned to the pitch.

Brahim Diaz then missed the vital penalty and Senegal went on to win in extra time, with Villarreal’s Pape Gueye scoring the decider.

In March 2026, two months after the final, CAF announced the shock decision to reverse the result, saying Senegal had forfeited the match by leaving the pitch.

The result was thus changed to a 3-0 win for Morocco, who are now considered the AFCON 2025 champions.

The Senegalese Football Federation (FSF) immediately hit back at CAF’s decision to strip them of the AFCON title, and have since refused to return the trophy, which they have regularly paraded in public.

The FSF immediately appealed CAF’s decision and the case fell to CAS. CAF president Patrice Motsepe acknowledged Senegal’s “right” to do so and added that he will “respect the decision that’s taken at the highest level.”.

The government of Senegal has also criticised CAF’s move, expressing “profound dismay” and questioning the governing body’s credibility.

The Moroccan football federation, meanwhile, has called for tournament rules to be “respected” as they maintain their status as champions.
