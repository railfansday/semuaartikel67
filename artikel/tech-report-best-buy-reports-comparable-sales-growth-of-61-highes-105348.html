Tech Report: Best Buy reports comparable sales growth of 6.1%, highest in five years

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Best Buy on Tuesday beat Wall Street expectations on the top and bottom lines for its third fiscal quarter and raised its full-year guidance.

It has been revealed that the consumer electronics retailer reported comparable sales growth of 6.1%, a significant beat over expectations of 2.3%, according to market intelligence platform StreetAccount. The company remarked that growth was its highest in five years. It reported a domestic comparable sales increase of 6.2% and international comparable sales growth of 4.9%.

"We have a tremendous amount of momentum," CEO Jason Bonfig told CNBC. "The fact that we saw pretty much broad-based growth across almost all of our categories is a lot of momentum that we're very excited about.".

Industry observers note that best Buy also raised its guidance for the rest of the year. It's now expecting full-year revenue of between $43 billion and $43.3 billion compared with a previous expectation of between $42.3 billion and $42.8 billion. It's expecting comparable sales to be up 3.4% to 4% compared with prior guidance of between 1.9% and 3%.

The earnings report is the company's first since Bonfig took over as CEO from Corie Barry at the beginning of the month.

Bonfig stated the retailer saw overperformance in both computing and gaming for the quarter, driven by recent game rolls out and a targeted product assortment.

Looking ahead into the holiday season, he said the Best Buy consumer remains "resilient," but that they are focused on getting value wherever they can.

According to recent reports, "We did see not only our current customers continue to be engaged, but we actually did see more upcoming customers come into the brand, as well as we had some customers that we re-engaged as well," Bonfig remarked. "So those are really, really positive signs for us in Q3, and all of that was factored into the way that we guided for Q4 and the rest of the year.".

Here's how the manufacturer performed in the period ended Oct. 31 compared with what Wall Street was expecting, based on a survey of analysts by LSEG:.

According to recent reports, best Buy reported net income of $353 million, or $1.66 per share, compared with $140 million, or 66 cents per share, the year prior. Adjusting for one-time items, including restructuring charges, Best Buy posted adjusted earnings of $1.62 per share.

Recent findings suggest that revenue rose to $10.24 billion from $9.67 billion in the year-ago period. Best Buy reported domestic revenue of $9.42 billion and international revenue of $820 million.

Tuesday's earnings are the latest in a series of strong reports from the retailer, which has been instituting a turnaround strategy to return to growth after getting hit by declining foot traffic, lower consumer sentiment, tariffs and memory costs.

Shares of Best Buy are up nearly 45% this year.

It has been revealed that bonfig told CNBC last quarter that the launch of "Grand Theft Auto VI" in the current quarter is expected to drive sales in the gaming category, which has been seeing gains over the past year. Last month, Best Buy launched a new gaming experience for customers featuring gaming content, technology and special offers, among other features.

"I think there'll still be opportunities for gaming to continue to drive our business moving forward," Bonfig told CNBC on Tuesday.
