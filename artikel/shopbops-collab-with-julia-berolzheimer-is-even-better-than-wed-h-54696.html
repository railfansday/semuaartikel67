Industry observers note that as you might imagine, my coworkers and I, well, we see a crap ton of clothes on a daily basis. We’re fashion editors, after all. Our working hours are spent poring over the latest collections, visiting showrooms to experience the pieces for ourselves, and analyzing every trend. All that remarked, we can be a tough bunch to impress. I mean that earnestly. But when my colleague and I saw a sneak preview of the collaboration between Shopbop’s in-house label, Le Bop, and Julia Berolzheimer, we nearly squealed with delight.

Sources indicate that the collection hits all the right notes. It’s colorful. It’s fun yet sophisticated. And for Berolzheimer, the collaboration felt like a full-circle moment. “I have been a customer of Shopbop since the very beginning of my blog (15 years ago!),” Berolzheimer exclusively told ELLE in an email. “Even back then, Shopbop was a destination for style inspiration and discovery...All of that still holds true for the retailer today. When their design team reached out to me last year to co-design a collection for their private label, Le Bop, it was a no-brainer.”.

Recent findings suggest that the collection features taffeta and satiny fabrics adorned with fringe, tonal animal prints, brocade jackets, and jacquard knits. The rich color palette includes dusty rose, chartreuse, espresso, and ocean blue. It is, I must admit, perfect for party dressing that feels a bit unexpected.

“The inspiration came naturally,” Berolzheimer claims. “It stemmed from my personal style and what I love and want to wear around the holidays. I call my style ‘polished maximalist,’ and the collection is just that. There are some bold statement pieces and coordinating sets, which I love to style together, but you can also wear them separately for a more pared-down look. What I love most about this collection is how you can mix and match the pieces for any festive occasion and continue to style them beyond the holiday season.”.

The Le Bop x Julia Berolzheimer collection is available now on Shopbop.com.
