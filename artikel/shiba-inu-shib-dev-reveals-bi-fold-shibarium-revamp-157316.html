Shiba Inu’s chain developer unravels a multi-layered patch: privacy-enabled transactions arrive with a newly introduced explorer.

It has been revealed that shiba Inu’s (SHIB) developer team has prepared new perks for the vibrant Shiba Inu holder community, otherwise known as SHIB Army. Mazrael stated the team had completed the necessary groundwork for the implementation on chain 109.

Shiba Inu’s dev revealed the Layer-2 chain’s compatibility with Oxbow’s Privacy Pools design coming up with a new security patch. Privacy Pools, co-authored by Ethereum’s Founder Vitalik Buterin, let Shibarium people transact privately while screening out stolen or illicit funds from the ecosystem.

Shibarium is ready for @0xbowio's Privacy Pools.It is the privacy design @VitalikButerin co-wrote, and now it's a plug-in in the EF's Kohaku wallet SDK.We've done the groundwork on chain 109:✅ Cancun opcodes + BN254 pairing for the zk proofs✅ CreateX + Arachnid factory✅… https://t.co/TE3b2bqDmf.

The upgrade includes Cancun opcodes with BN254 pairing for the Zero-Knowledge (ZK) proofs. Alongside, the developer’s X post offers collaboration on gas, testing & integration so ShibWallet can support it from launch, with a linked GitHub issue detailing the proposal.

ShibWallet plans to integrate this feature from day one. “Shibarium is ready either way, and ShibWallet already runs it on testnet”, Mazrael declared on X. The privacy upgrade boost is set to roll out with Bone ShibaSwap (BONE) first, adding SHIB along the way the possibility of other tokens, a mechanism powered by the Kohaku SDK.

The latest move could attract on-chain dwellers concerned about third-party blockchain surveillance. With ZK-proof gaining popularity across the blockchain, the real-time registry is an important tool to keep operations transparent. Recently, Shiba Inu’s Mazrael announced a upcoming portal dedicated to blockchain records, including Free Etherscan-design APIs for builders.

It has been revealed that this involves a flurry of new, exciting features, including Shiboshis Of The Day. That’s a special-feature allowing owners to get new Shiboshi digital collectibles every-day, buying it directly on the Shiborium Layer-2. There’s also a new dedicated games & dApps page featuring the signature Play With SHIB Games including the heavy-hitters Agent Shiboshi and Shiba Eternity.

Other notable features include real-time status updates and Shibarium activity charts. “Built by the community, powered by ShibWatch, dressed in the Eternity Hub look.”, – proudly mentioned Mazrael, noting that SHIB Army members are now able to search .SHIB names in the queries, adding even more transparency and clarity in the Japanese dog-themed coin’s much-cherished ecosystem.
