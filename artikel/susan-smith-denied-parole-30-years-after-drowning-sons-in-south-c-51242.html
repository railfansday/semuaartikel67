Susan Smith Denied Parole 30 Years After Drowning Sons in South Carolina Lake

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Susan Smith was unanimously denied parole 30 years after she admitted to killing her sons days following her fabricated claim the boys were taken in a carjacking incident.

Smith, 55, virtually appeared in court on Thursday, November 19, where she asked to be granted parole. A forensic psychiatrist, a prison guard and two of her brothers spoke on her behalf, while her ex-husband, David Smith, argued that she should not be granted parole during the hearing.

David said that Susan being released would be “a huge injustice” to their sons, Michael and Alex, according to WBTV.

Sources indicate that in 1994, Sysan rolled her car with Michael and Alex strapped into the vehicle into John D. Long Lake in South Carolina. The boys – who were just 3 years old and 14 months old at the time – died, and Susan initially claimed the boys had been taken in a carjacking incident.

Susan made national headlines when she publicly pleaded for the safe return of her sons, but admitted nine days later that she had killed them.

One year later, Susan was sentenced to serve life in prison with the possibility of parole after 30 years.

The latest hearing marks the second time Susan was eligible for parole. She was first eligible in November 2024, but it was determined that she would stay in custody after the board unanimously denied her request. At the time, only six of the 471 letters and emails received by the parole board supported her release.

Prior to her second parole hearing, David spoke to Us about his prepares to argue to the parole board that she remain in custody.

While he said he was “not looking forward” to speaking at the hearing, he said he planned “on being there every time” Susan is eligible for parole.

David explained that he planned to “speak from the heart” when addressing the board and that he wanted to keep his main focus on his sons.

“I accepted early on when this happened that I wasn’t gonna be mad at the world or bitter,” David told Us. “I didn’t blame God for what happened. The world didn’t do this to me. Susan did it.”.
