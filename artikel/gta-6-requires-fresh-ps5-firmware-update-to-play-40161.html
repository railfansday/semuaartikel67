📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that as we reported early yesterday, PS5 has a latest firmware enhancement, numbered v14.23.00. It’s now confirmed you’ll need to have this installed to play GTA 6.

Notifying players on social media, a PlayStation Support page stated: “Sony Interactive has issued a PS5 system software upgrade that is required before playing GTA 6. Players should download this update prior to midnight on 19th November so that they’re ready to play on launch day, 19th November.”.

Sony usually gives customers a period of grace to play their games before a new firmware update is made mandatory, so this is unprecedented behaviour.

But the stakes have never been higher either: Rockstar’s open world is the most anticipated game of all time, and it’s coming at a time when the PS5 has been cracked wide open.

Sony’s right now in a cat-and-mouse battle with hackers, as it tries to shore up the security of its console.

That all comes at the cost of inconvenience to the rest of us, as we’re forced to install firmware updates which have been coming more and more frequently of late.

This latest version is just 1.3GB so it’s not a huge lift, but this is your 24 hour warning that you’ll need to have it installed to play GTA 6.
