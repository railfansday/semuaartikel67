Roma, fresh from a 2-2 draw at PSG in the Champions League, are looking for a striker to bolster Gasperini’s attack.

Among the most closely watched prospects is Real Madrid’s Endrick.

Wesley, his compatriot, admitted to TNT Sports that he wants to play with him in the capital.

According to recent reports, “I told Endrick to come and play for Roma! He’ll decide his future; obviously, it’s his career… I hope that wherever he goes, or if he stays at Real Madrid, he’ll be very happy and successful, because he’s a good guy who deserves it.”.

“But if he comes, he’ll be very well received by the Roma fans and by us, of course.”.

De Roon praises team effort: “We put in a great performance.”.

Joao Neves: “Roma are one of the best teams we’ve faced. They press really hard and have lots of quality.”.

Marquinhos greets Roma fans in away section.

Luis Enrique: “This was by far the hardest game so far.”.

Gasperini after PSG draw: “I will rewatch it, we were great. Soule is becoming a top player.”.

Gasperini: “I’m happy and proud of my team. Castro deserved a chance, Hermoso was brilliant.”.
