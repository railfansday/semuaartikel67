According to recent reports, earlier this week, Apple revealed that Siri AI usage limits will be implemented in iOS 27.3 and explained how iCloud+ increases those limits. Now we have a real-world example of what it looks like when limits are hit, and details on what counts toward those limits.

Siri AI and select other AI features will begin having usage limits in iOS 27.3 for advanced tasks.

This is standard practice with other AI services like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude because these tools rely on server-based models rather than running entirely on-device.

In a recent development, here’s an example from the iOS 27.3 beta showing what it looks like when you hit your Siri AI daily usage limit. Per Ben in a post on X:.

In a follow-up post, Ben clarifies that complex questions did stop working shortly after posting. As expected though, basic requests continued to work as usual—they don’t have a usage limit.

Recent findings suggest that one question that Apple hasn’t made clear is what qualifies as a complex or “advanced” Siri AI request. The organization shares just two examples of something advanced:.

The only things in that list which should ever be leaving my device are “deep research” (clearly asking online repositories) and "summarizing photo content.” Everything else has no reason not to be done on device, and as such can’t have any limits.
