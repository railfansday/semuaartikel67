The league is due to announce fixture changes for January by the end of this month, but Palace's involvement in the Europa League means there is some early certainty over the game in East Yorkshire.

Palace are away to Red Bull Salzburg the previous Thursday, meaning their weekend game will now kick off on Sunday, January 31, kicking off at 2pm.

The match will be shown live on Sky Sports.

Sources indicate that the sides meet at Selhurst Park on November 29, another game pushed a Sunday by Palace's Europa League schedule. That match will be new coach Thomas Frank's first Premier League home game in charge of the South London club.

Fortunately for both clubs, January's game will not be played on transfer deadline day, with the final day of transfer business pushed back 24 hours to 11pm on Monday February 1.
