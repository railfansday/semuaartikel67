Vitalii Mykolenko played the full 90 minutes as Ukraine secured a 1-0 victory over Georgia in their latest UEFA Nations League outing.

The Everton defender made his second successive start of this international window as his nation claimed all three points, while fellow Blues Brennan Johnson and Jake O’Brien were also in action for their respective countries.

Johnson was introduced as a 63rd-minute substitute for Wales, who suffered a narrow 3-2 defeat against Portugal.

Meanwhile, O’Brien came off the bench in the second half as the Republic of Ireland recorded an impressive 3-0 victory over Kosovo, who had been reduced to 10 men before half-time.

Elsewhere, Everton youngster Braiden Graham was an unused substitute as Northern Ireland played out a 1-1 draw with Hungary.

While Wales Under-19s duo Luis Gardner and Shea Pita both played the full contest as their side overcame Iceland 3-1 on penalties.

Gardner captained the young Dragons and converted the decisive spot-kick to seal the victory.

Scotland Under-17s striker Kai Hutchison was also involved, entering the action as a second-half substitute in an entertaining 6-3 victory over Sweden.
