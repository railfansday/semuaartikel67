📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that succession may be long over (and may she rest in power), but the phrase “ludicrously capacious bag” has proven its cultural staying power. While last year was all about major, face-obscuring sunglasses, 2026 belonged to the supersized bags.

Industry observers note that yes, this year was for those of us who need a purse that fits everything you could ever need and more: a book or two, a water bottle, perhaps a sweater, and, of course, a bottom lined with emotional support receipts. Micro bags had their moment; now, the pendulum has swung back. This year was for the real schleppers.

Celebrities—many of whom even bought into the ludicrously minuscule bag trend just a few years ago—were very eager for the return of the oversized purse. Perhaps nobody was more excited to haul their belongings around than Rosalía, who recently strutted through Upcoming York in a pair of lucite-heeled platform pointe shoes, carrying a massive, three-foot-long custom Dana Toder bag like a balance bar.

While hers was the most extreme, Rosalía was far from the only celeb schlepper this year. Many were fond of the gargantuan carry-on: Dua Lipa touched down in Latest York toting an enormous raffia Chanel shopper, while Kendall Jenner arrived in Venice with a Chanel weekender, and Pedro Pascal also joined in on the trend, carrying a bottle-green Chanel tote in London.

Are you sensing a theme? Yes, Matthieu Blazy is pro-schlep. But while he’s creating the most covetable accessories du jour, he’s not the only one here for a ginormous purse revival. Below, revisit 2026 in outsized bags, from Future’s Hermès HAC to Bella Hadid’s Prada.
