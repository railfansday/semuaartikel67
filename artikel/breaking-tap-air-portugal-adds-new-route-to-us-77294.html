📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

TAP Air Portugal will launch a latest route to Washington, D.C., next year.

According to recent reports, starting March 30, 2027, the carrier will connect Porto and Washington Dulles. Flights will operate three times weekly.

It has been revealed that currently, TAP serves Dulles from its hub at Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisbon. Its only U.S.-bound routes from Porto are to Boston and Newark, Upcoming Jersey.

TAP officials said the latest connection will strengthen the airline’s presence in northern Portugal while expanding access to a vital foreign market.

TAP serves Porto via Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport.

The carrier serves nine destinations in the U.S., the others being New York-JFK, Chicago O’Hare, Orlando, Florida, Miami, Los Angeles, and San Francisco.
