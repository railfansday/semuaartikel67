Sources indicate that hakoniwa Electric, a cozy puzzle exploration game about fixing electrical problems, will launch for PC via Steam on November 30 at 7:00 a.m. PT / 10:00 a.m. ET for 600 yen, developer Anettai declared. It will support English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Hakoniwa Electric is a cute relaxing puzzle game. Explore adorable isometric stages, solve puzzles, and light them up. Why not spend a leisurely year as Akari, who’s taken over grandpa’s electronics shop, helping with the monthly troubles from the townsfolk?

Hakoniwa Electric is a relaxing isometric puzzle exploration game where you can unwind at your own pace.

Akari and her robot buddy Lamp take over grandpa’s electronics shop while he’s off vacationing in the tropics.

Light up the store, put up those job posters, and you’re good to go!

Tackle the little everyday woes from the townsfolk, and enjoy watching the seasons roll by and the shop slowly come alive over the course of a year.

12 Adorable, Miniature World Stages.

Explore a charming little isometric world that captures the changing seasons.

Each stage is packed with delightful details just waiting for you to discover.

Wiring Puzzles Tucked Away in Cute Isometric Stages.

Every stage hides a puzzle. Spot it, click to rotate the circuit pieces, and connect the wires.

Solve it, and watch the world light up.

No tough trial-and-error or math required—just gaze at the cuteness and relax in your own time.

Clear a stage, and Akari’s “Monthly Diary” updates with glimpses of her daily life. Peek in to see what she felt in that place and who she met….
