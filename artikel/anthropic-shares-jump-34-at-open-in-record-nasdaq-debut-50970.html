📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above the $83 IPO price, valuing it at more than $2T, per Business Insider. The company raised $107.3B with its IPO and a private placement, the largest listing on record.

According to recent reports, anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above their $83 IPO price, Business Insider reported. The jump, bigger than the 27% grey markets had pointed to, valued the Claude maker at more than $2T. The stock trades under the ticker ANT.

Dario and Daniela Amodei rang the opening bell with staff earlier in the day. By midday in Newly introduced York, the shares had eased to about $107, still up nearly 29%, according to Bloomberg data.

The open beat SpaceX’s debut in June. SpaceX, which priced its IPO at $135 a share, opened 11% higher and closed its first day up 19%.

It has been revealed that anthropic raised about $81B by selling shares at $83 on Wednesday, above its $70 to $78 range. It is also raising $26B from a private placement at the same price, with Alphabet, Microsoft and Nvidia among the buyers. Together, that makes $107.3B, the biggest IPO on record.

Sources indicate that the debut lifted the wider market, with the Nasdaq Composite up more than 1% after the open. As the first significant pure-play AI stock, Anthropic is likely to be watched as a test of investors’ appetite for AI.

In a recent development, not everyone is convinced. In October, research firm Upcoming Constructs noted Anthropic might be worth as little as $144B. On Wednesday, a group of scientists signed a statement calling it “not a safe AI firm”.
