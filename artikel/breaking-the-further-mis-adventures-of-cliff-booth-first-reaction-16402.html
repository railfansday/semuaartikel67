It has been revealed that few films on the 2026 calendar felt like bigger question marks than “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.” The prospect of Brad Pitt reprising his Oscar-winning role from “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood” in a Netflix pseudo-sequel written by Quentin Tarantino but directed by David Fincher was not on many people’s bingo cards when the project was first confirmed. The fact that the news originally broke on April Fool’s Day even led some people to wonder if the whole thing was an elaborate joke.

But the crowd who made their way to the TCL Chinese Theater on Monday, November 16 can attest that “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” is very much real. The film was introduced by Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos, who joked that he instantly greenlit the film after receiving an 11-word text from Fincher that simply read “Written by Quentin Tarantino. Directed by David Fincher. Starring Brad Pitt.” As Sarandos tells it, he simply responded “Yes.” Related Stories ‘Hershey’ Review: An Unbelievably Flavorless Biopic About America’s Most Famous Chocolatier ‘Spider-Man: Brand Latest Day’ Returning to Theaters with a New Rosario Dawson Cameo — and Box Office Records and Oscar Noms in Mind.

Sources indicate that when the lights went down, the crowd was treated to a rollicking hangout movie filled with elaborate recreations of ’70s Los Angeles, classic rock and soul needle drops, and a meandering plot that blends crime and comedy into one elaborate excuse for Pitt’s Cliff Booth to wander around the city exuding charisma. It wasn’t long before the social media reactions began to flow in, many of which were quite positive.

The online buzz following the premiere praised Pitt’s willingness to do seemingly anything (a few scenes seem destined to go very viral), his romantic chemistry with Elizabeth Debicki, and the film’s unapologetic commitment to capturing both the Los Angeles of yesteryear that Booth inhabited and the style of movie that he loved to watch. And of course, there are plenty “Once Upon a Time in Hollywood” Easter Eggs to go around.

According to recent reports, “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” is set to debut for a two-week exclusive engagement in IMAX Theaters globally on November 25, 2026, before debuting on Netflix on Wednesday, December 23.
