Dunesday gets an official reaction from Denis Villeneuve.

Two major cinematic releases facing off at the box office is not unusual. In 2023, there was the cultural event Barbie vs. Oppenheimer, affectionately called Barbenheimer, which saw the battle between Greta Gerwig's Barbie and Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer opening on the same weekend. Now, two new movies will be having an epic battle in theaters as the world prepares for a major box office tug of war against two titans.

Sources indicate that dunesday is the new name for the battle between Dune: Part Three and Avengers: Doomsday, being released on the same day, December 18, making it a major holiday box office showdown. Villeneuve spoke with Time about his upcoming release, Dune: Part Three, and addressed Disney ahead of the major box office battle between his new movie and Avengers: Doomsday. In what could easily be one of the biggest box office battles of all time, Villeneuve is seemingly not threatened by the competition. The filmmaker states:.

"Disney put the biggest franchise of all time against us. I'm no fool. So I was deeply disappointed. But we had committed to this date, to IMAX. It was impossible to move. So I accepted my fate, praying to the gods of cinema. Dune is not a mammoth like the Avengers, but it’s a sexy little animal that has a lot of firepower, and I hope we will survive.

Industry observers note that it could be one of the biggest box-office weekends of all time because of both movies. Am I in competition with the Avengers? Absolutely not. It’s a T. Rex. But I stand proud because I think the film delivers.
