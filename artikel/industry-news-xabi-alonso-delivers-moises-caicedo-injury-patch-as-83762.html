Xabi Alonso claims Chelsea will not know the severity of Moises Caicedo’s injury until later this week.

The Ecuador international went down off the ball just past the 20-minute mark of Chelsea’s grim loss to Leeds on Saturday, and received treatment before being replaced by Jordan Henderson.

Caicedo has struggled for fitness all season and asked to be substituted at half-time during Ecuador’s 2-0 friendly win over Bolivia this week while on international duty.

The 25-year-old was deemed fit enough to start against Leeds, but was on the end of a number of heavy challenges early on at Stamford Bridge, including an incident which saw him require treatment after landing awkwardly on his back.

Speaking at full-time, Alonso delivered an update on his condition, saying: “Xabi Alonso delivers Moises Caicedo injury update as Chelsea star hit with fresh setback.”.

The match was goalless when Caicedo was withdrawn, but Chelsea soon took the lead through Emmanuel Emegha before being quickly pegged back by Noah Okafor’s leveller.

It swiftly unravelled after the break, though, as Noah Okafor’s brace and Dominic Calvert-Lewin’s penalty put the Blues to the sword.

According to recent reports, “It went wrong after the equaliser, after the change (with) Moi, we completely lost the momentum,” Alonso mused. “Until then, we were doing okay, I think that we were creating, playing a lot on the pitch, we were coming out of the pressure.

“But from that moment, they started believing and we started not believing.

“And after that, we never got the good feeling mentality in any moment and we were not able to come for the pressure and we didn't get close to their goal, so it completely changed.”.

Chelsea are already managing several injuries to key players, with Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix and Reece James all sidelined.

Alonso offered an upgrade on James and Lacroix in his pre-match press conference, stating that the latter was further ahead in his recovery from a hamstring injury.

Sources indicate that “I think that Max has a better chance than Reece,” he revealed. “It’s not definitely that Reece won’t be back before then, but Max has a better chance.”.

Alonso is also expected to provide an enhancement on Caicedo in his post-match press conference later this afternoon.
