Despite the ultimate failure of Chelsea loanee Axel Disasi to enable West Ham’s hapless defence to secure Premier League survival, most fans look back with a certain fondness at that brief period of optimism that coincided with the solid, committed defender’s arrival and the transformative effect he had on the Hammers’ fortunes.

In a recent development, twelve months on and unbelievably, West Ham’s defence is in need of rescuing once again. Week in, week out (apart from a brief, one game ‘Morato’ interlude which Parker has inexplicably ditched), Kilman and Mavropanos prove they’re not fit for purpose as a solid, reliable centre back pairing meaning West Ham must again look to shore up the leaky back line in January – another loan on the cards then:.

For West Ham, Heaven represents an opportunity to add a highly rated young defender to a squad looking to strengthen after a difficult campaign…For West Ham, a loan agreement could offer an opportunity to give Heaven regular senior football without requiring a major permanent investment immediately.

Is Ayden Heaven a better bet than Morato? Only if Scott Parker has the cojones to use him instead of his £40m flop.

His development at the club could then determine whether a longer-term deal becomes realistic.”.

That’s according to thehardtackle.com.

Although different in age and career trajectory, the left footed centre back could revitalise West Ham’s defence in the same way that Axel Disasi managed last year, replacing the hesitant Maximilian Kilman who still has to earn about £39 million of his £40 million transfer fee.

Is Heaven the answer to West Ham’s woes? Who can tell: He’d 100% be an improvement though.

Industry observers note that at 20, is he a significant upgrade on Morato or Golambeckis, either of whom would deprive more resilient than Kilman alongside Mavropanos? You’d think ‘not’.

Somehow each successive manager has continued making the same mistake and pairing the hapless duo together with predictably, the same results. If a loan of Ayden Heaven means West Ham are prepared to drop Kilman and split the centre back pairing of panic, then please, please let it happen, Koppen.
