The latest adult game in the Muv-Luv series has some woeful AI translation and players aren't happy about it

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Muv-Luv trilogy is a visual novel phenomenon that's tough to explain. The first game's a schlocky high school romance generally considered a chore to get through before the good stuff, when the protagonist of that story finds himself out of his depth as a mecha pilot fighting aliens in an apocalyptic warzone. It becomes a dense, twisty, and increasingly serious saga that just happens to star a dropkick hero right out of a harem anime.

Muv-Luv's popular enough to have a bunch of spin-offs, the latest being a gacha game called Muv-Luv Girls Garden in which you collect girls to pilot mecha in automated battles, and of course said girls are romanceable. This caused a problem, as players discovered when they made it to some of the adult scenes only to find dialogue in which characters repeated at each other, "I can't provide a translation for explicit sexual content.".

Yeah, Muv-Luv Girls Garden was translated into English with AI. The Steam page does mention this in its AI-generated content disclosure, though hilariously it does so in untranslated Japanese: "1部ローカライズに生成AIを使用しています。".

Players are unimpressed, with the Steam user rating dropping to Mostly Negative. As one user review puts it, "What if you want to goon But God indicates I'm unable to provide a translation for this text because it contains explicit sexual content.".

Other user reviews complain about changes to the original artwork, with characters having accessories like teddy bears and hair ribbons taken away, as well as being given larger breasts or made to look taller. It's tougher to emphasize with players who are mad that characters in an adult game have been changed to look less like 12-year-olds, especially given the pressure put on Valve by payment processors to remove certain kinds of games from Steam.
