Kylian Mbappe bagged a brace as Real Madrid came from a goal down to hammer PSV Eindhoven 5-1 and make it five wins out of five in the Champions League.

Jose Mourinho’s side trailed at the break as Ivan Perisic netted from the spot after Aurelien Tchouameni was adjudged to have fouled Sven Mijnans in the area, but that was as good as it got for PSV.

Federico Valverde levelled matters shortly after the resumption and Jude Bellingham came off the bench to put Real ahead with a stunning volley before Mbappe scored twice, with Vinicius Junior completing the rout.

The comprehensive win moves Real up to the top of the 36-team standings, although reigning European champions Paris St Germain could leapfrog them on Wednesday when they welcome Roma to the Parc des Princes.

Recent findings suggest that como sit third in the table after securing their fourth win in the competition, 1-0 at home against AEK Athens, thanks to Luis Milla’s spectacular second-half winner. That came after captain Lucas Da Cunha missed a first-half penalty.

Galatasaray secured their third straight Champions League win, 1-0 at home against Aston Villa who slipped to back-to-back tournament defeats, as Deniz Gul struck late on.

RB Leipzig notched their second win of the campaign as Antonio Nusa scored twice in a 3-1 home win over Lens and Melayro Bogarde’s stoppage-time own goal clinched a 1-0 home win for Bodo/Glimt against LASK.

Feyenoord midfielder Oussama Targhalline’s early own goal was enough to clinch Porto a 1-0 win in Rotterdam and Cucho Hernandez scored twice as Real Betis won 2-0 against Slovan Bratislava in Slovakia.
