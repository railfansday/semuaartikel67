Oceania has unveiled its Black Friday Sale, offering up to 50 percent savings across all 2027 voyages.

Available to book from November 18 through December 1, 2026, the offer includes over 200 voyages, giving travelers a chance to plan a year of worldwide discovery, the organization stated in a press release.

The collection spans the entire fleet, from destinations to lesser-explored gems, with highlight sailings to the Eastern Mediterranean.

Oceania remarked in a press release that guests can uncover cities like Dubrovnik, Athens and Venice, as well as islands and coastlines, including Kusadasi, Santorini and Kotor.

Oceania Cruises’ itineraries venture to 600 ports worldwide, meaning 2027 sailings also offer sojourns through the rugged wilderness of Alaska, crystal waters of the Caribbean, the far reaches of Asia and beyond.
