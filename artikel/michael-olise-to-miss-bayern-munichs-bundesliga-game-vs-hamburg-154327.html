Michael Olise to miss Bayern Munich’s Bundesliga game vs. Hamburg

Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany has confirmed that they will be without Michael Olise for their Bundesliga game against Hamburg on Saturday.

The Frenchman was injured in last weekend’s 1-1 draw against Cologne and subsequently missed Bayern’s UEFA Champions League defeat against Lille on Wednesday night. Olise, who left the Allianz Arena on crutches after a challenge from Cologne’s Tom Krauß at the edge of the box, has been wearing a VACOped brace to help with the recovery process after suffering bruising on his left foot.

He is, though, ruled out of Bayern’s game this weekend. “Olise won’t be there,” Kompany mentioned at Friday’s press conference. While Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari and Harry Kane are all sidelined, Luis Díaz returned against Lille – in a game where Lennart Karl led the attack – after sustaining a thigh injury during their 2-2 draw against Arsenal in October.

An attacking trio of Konrad Laimer, Tom Bischof and Nathaniel Brown started on Wednesday, but Kompany remained tight-lipped on who will start against HSV. “We have to make the most of the hand we’ve been dealt,” he added. “So everything remains open, but our expectations and standards remain exactly the same.”.
