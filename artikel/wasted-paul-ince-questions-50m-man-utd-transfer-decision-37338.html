Paul Ince still cannot wrap his head around Manchester United’s decision to spend £50million on Andrey Santos in the summer transfer window.

Santos completed a £50m move from Chelsea over the summer, arriving alongside Youri Tielemans and Carlos Baleba as the Red Devils sought to bolster their depth in central midfield.

Santos enjoyed an encouraging pre-season, but started only four of United’s first ten Premier League matches under Michael Carrick before his sacking.

Mauricio Pochettino’s arrival may represent a fresh start for the £50m midfielder, but Ince believes United would have been better served spending that money elsewhere over the summer.

‘Why did they spend £50m on Santos?’ Ince told The Sun. ‘That’s £50m wasted. They could have put that into the defence or bought a striker.

Recent findings suggest that ‘They have [Kobbie] Mainoo, who needs to develop. He needs to be playing, not Santos.

‘Tielemans, again, fantastic at Villa but it’s a different conversation when you come to United.

‘Baleba didn’t play a lot last year. He comes in for £70m, plays a couple of games and he’s injured again.

It has been revealed that ‘The game’s changing. It’s not all pass, pass, pass now. You’ve got to win your duels. You used to hear Pep Guardiola say every time, “We didn’t win our duels, we didn’t win our tackles”.
