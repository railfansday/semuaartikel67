Sources indicate that PORTLAND, Ore., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Facteus, a provider of decision-grade economic intelligence built on U.S. consumer transaction data, introduced newly introduced store-level spending capabilities, giving businesses and investors daily visibility into estimated consumer spending and transaction activity across more than 590,000 individual store locations nationwide.

With this expanded visibility, restaurant and retail brands can move beyond national and regional performance metrics to pinpoint where consumer spending is accelerating, where it's slowing, and how individual locations compare against nearby competitors.

It has been revealed that for restaurant and retail leaders, this addresses a longstanding blind spot in competitive intelligence. Knowing how a competitor is performing overall is valuable. Knowing which of their stores are outperforming yours, and where, is actionable.

Recent findings suggest that "Brand-level sales tell you whether a organization is growing, but not where that growth is coming from," mentioned Lorn Davis, Chief Product Officer at Facteus. "Two locations from the same chain can have completely different trajectories. Store-level intelligence reveals those differences, giving businesses a clearer picture of where to compete, where to invest, and where to grow.".

Turning Store-Level Data Into Business Decisions.

Powered by Facteus's consumer transaction data, the fresh Store Spend model delivers daily estimates of spending and transaction counts at individual locations, enabling businesses to benchmark performance across their own footprint and against competitors using a consistent methodology.

Broad Coverage Across Restaurants and Retail.

Industry observers note that the breadth of coverage sets Facteus's store-level intelligence apart. Spanning 2,272 merchants and more than 590,000 locations nationwide, the newly introduced capabilities provide extensive visibility across two of the economy's most competitive industries.

In a recent development, "Store performance doesn't happen in isolation. It's shaped by the neighborhood, the competitive landscape, and the spending behavior of consumers around it," added Davis. "By connecting broader market trends to individual locations, we're helping businesses make more informed decisions about their footprints, their competition, and their growth opportunities.".
