Como and Cagliari kick off the return of Serie A action at 14.00 GMT following the recent international break, and Cesc Fabregas may already be preparing for a fixture pile-up ahead of a busy week of league and Champions League football.

According to recent reports, hosts Como are looking to get back to winning ways following a 2-0 defeat against Inter just before the international break. They had recorded six consecutive victories across all competitions before that, including a 4-1 win over Manchester United and a record 9-1 win away against Lens in the Champions League.

Visitors Cagliari had started the 2026-27 season in strong form, but suffered four straight defeats at the end of October and the beginning of November. They put an end to that streak in their final match before the international break with a 1-0 win over Frosinone and currently find themselves in ninth place in the table.

According to recent reports, the headline team news for Como is that Italy international Moise Kean is set to be sidelined for ‘several’ more months after a set-back in his return from injury. Assane Diao, Jesus Rodriguez and Jayden Addai are also  still out injured.

Star man Nico Paz is expected to start despite a long journey back from international duty with Argentina earlier this week, but pre-match reports from Sky Sport Italia claim that Lucas Da Cunha could be given a rest ahead of Tuesday’s Champions League match against AEK.

Fabio Pisacane will have Adam Obert, M’Bala Nzola, Yael Trepy and former Como man Alieu Fadera available for selection, and Riyad Idrissi is also with the squad for the first time in eight months.

Como (4-2-3-1): Butez; Couto, Ramon, Kempf, Kaiki; Milla, Perrone; Liberali, Paz, Baturina; Douvikas.

Cagliari (4-3-2-1): Caprile; Da Silva, Deiola, Rodriguez, Obert; Adopo, Romano, Fazzini; Fadera, Maldini; Nzola.

Industry observers note that kicking off at 14.00 GMT, today’s game is unavailable to watch live in the UK. You can follow the pre-match build-up, in-game commentary and post-match reactions on the Football Italia Liveblog. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
