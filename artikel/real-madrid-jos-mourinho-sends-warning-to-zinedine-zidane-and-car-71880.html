According to recent reports, the dynamic is excellent for Real Madrid. On a series of eight victories in a row, the Merengue club is performing well in the championship as well as in the Champions League with second places behind FC Barcelona in La Liga and PSG in C1. Casa Blanca seems to have found its cruising speed but unfortunately there will be no matches until Saturday November 21 and the reception of Celta de Vigo. Indeed, the international break begins this Monday. And that offers its share of worry.

Industry observers note that josé Mourinho is expecting the worst and will examine the situation of his foes over the coming days. He took the opportunity to send a message to the various coaches including Zinedine Zidane who has four players from the Merengue club in his ranks with Ibrahima Konaté, Aurélien Tchouaméni, Eduardo Camavinga and Kylian Mbappé. Which makes France the selection which has the greatest number of Madrilenians in its ranks. “I’m telling you straight away, I’m worried about the international break, because if they stay with me and my staff, we could give them special attention for their recovery, but they will be with their national teams“, he declared in particular after the 3-0 victory against Valencia on Saturday.

For the Portuguese coach, this truce does not come at the best of times and that long trips will not provide adequate recovery time for his players: “I have never coached a team, but I imagine that their coaches want to work, and these players cannot work now, they need to rest to be able to play later with their teams. These players need a lot of attention during their recovery.».

Sources indicate that if he admitted that he would have done the same as the coaches if he were in their place, José Mourinho took the example of Carlo Ancelotti and the two Brazilians who were called up, Endrick and Vinicius Junior. “Regarding Carlo, look carefully who is with him: Vinícius, who should play, Endrick, and if Carlo plays him, I am grateful to him. It’s not Carlo’s entourage that worries me, but I don’t think they appreciate seeing a player get injured due to overwork either. They owe it to the players to be professional when it comes to their recovery“, he noted. José Mourinho has put the pressure on, it remains to be seen whether this will be effective.
