Yunus Musah is expected to continue at left-back as AC Milan will be back in action tomorrow evening, taking on Frosinone at San Siro.

Industry observers note that in the game against Genoa, due to injuries making life difficult at the back, Ruben Amorim opted to field Musah as a left-back of sorts. He did have Davide Bartesaghi at his disposal too, but chose the American and the ‘experiment’, if you like, worked.

Therefore, as also highlighted by Gazzetta dello Sport this morning, Amorim is expected to run his experiment one more time tomorrow evening. Now that the international break is over, Milan can get back to making it three wins in three games.

As the pink newspaper reports, the formation should indeed be a 4-4-1-1 or a 4-2-3-1 yet again, with Sankhoun Diawara taking the left centre-back spot and Musah starting on the flank. In midfield, meanwhile, Luka Modric and Adrien Rabiot should run the show.

It has been revealed that diego Moreira and Alphadjo Cisse will be the men out wide, looking to support Christian Pulisic and Goncalo Ramos with some service. In short, we are talking about an attacking-minded starting eleven here, which means getting the right balance will be key to winning.

Predicted Milan XI (4-2-3-1): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Musah; Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Pulisic, Cisse; Ramos.

Predicted bench: Torriani, P. Terracciano, F. Terracciano, Bartesaghi, Pavlovic, Vladimirov, Estupinan, Jashari, Comotto, Hutchinson, Camarda.

Predicted Frosinone XI (4-2-3-1): Palmisani; Oyono, Calvani, Monterisi, Terzic; Calo, Masini; Ghedjemis; Schmid, Kvernadze; Birigilea.

Predicted bench: Desplanches, Pisseri, Cittadini, Tchato, Amey, Akpoguma, Fayed, El Azzouzi, Cichella, Hasa, Fini, Zerbin, Bobcek.
