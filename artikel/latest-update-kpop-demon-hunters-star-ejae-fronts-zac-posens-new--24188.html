Sources indicate that EJAE is bringing her star power to Gap. The singer-songwriter behind Netflix’s blockbuster animated film “KPop Demon Hunters” is the face of GapStudio’s Winter 2026 campaign, showcasing a new 12-piece collection designed by Zac Posen.

Sources indicate that launching Nov. 20, the capsule marks the latest release from GapStudio, the retailer’s elevated fashion line overseen by Posen, Gap Inc.’s executive vice president and creative director. The fresh collection takes a more dressed-up approach to the brand’s signature American sportswear. Centered on a black palette, with accents of royal blue, deep red, winter white and neutral tones, you’ll find in the capsule denim, poplin and knitwear — this time with with sharper tailoring and exaggerated proportions. Standout pieces include a contoured rib maxi dress ($268), a structured denim jacket ($168), a denim double-breasted blazer ($198) and high-rise seamed stirrup jeans ($148).

One of the collection’s more playful designs is the Brannan Bear Sherpa Jacket ($268), a faux-fur outerwear piece complete with bear ears inspired by Gap’s longtime mascot.

Industry observers note that “For Winter 2026, we wanted to move beyond traditional occasion wear with versatile pieces designed to be worn from day to night,” Posen said in a statement. “There’s still that level of drama with thoughtful tailoring and exaggerated lines, but it’s balanced by a focused palette that feels elegant and wearable.”.

In a recent development, EJAE rose to international prominence as the singing voice of Rumi, the lead character in Netflix’s “KPop Demon Hunters,” and co-writer of the film’s breakout hit “Golden.” The song became a global chart phenomenon and earned the singer-songwriter a Golden Globe for best original song. Her appearance in the GapStudio campaign represents another crossover into fashion for the musician, who previously worked behind the scenes writing songs for K-pop acts including Red Velvet, aespa and Twice.

The release continues Posen’s efforts to elevate Gap’s fashion credentials through GapStudio, which launched as a more design-focused extension of the retailer’s everyday wardrobe. Earlier this fall, the brand unveiled a 20-piece collection modeled by Esther McGregor and Alton Mason.

It has been revealed that the GapStudio Winter 2026 collection will be available beginning Nov. 20 on Gap.com and at select Gap stores, with prices ranging from $54 to $268.
