Centrica boss memo to 400 workers at gas plant off Yorkshire coast says utility doing all it can ‘to save your jobs’.

The boss of the British Gas owner Centrica has told hundreds of staff at its largest gas storage facility that he was seeking urgent talks with Andy Burnham and would do everything he could to save jobs.

Chris O’Shea, the chief executive of Centrica, reportedly told staff at the Rough gas storage facility in east Yorkshire that the government had rebuffed the firm’s plea for short-term support to run the store over the winter.
