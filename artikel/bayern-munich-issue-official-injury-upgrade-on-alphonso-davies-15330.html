After Alphonso Davies suffered an injury setback while playing for Canada in a game against Guatemala last week, Bayern Munich have now provided an enhancement on the left-back's condition.

As confirmed by the German champions on Monday, examinations carried out by the club's medical team revealed that Davies has suffered a minor muscle tear in his left hamstring.

Bayern added that the 26-year-old is therefore “set for a spell on the sidelines”, without providing a specific timeframe for his recovery.

According to Munich-based outlet TZ, Davies is expected to be sidelined for around 7-10 days, with Bayern hopeful of having him fit in time for next week's Champions League clash against Lille.
