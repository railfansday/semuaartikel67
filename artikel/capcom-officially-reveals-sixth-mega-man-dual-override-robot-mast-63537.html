Capcom is bringing Mega Man: Dual Override to the Switch 1 & 2 in 2027 and to build some excitement, it's been revealing the Robot Masters you'll be facing in the game.

In a recent development, this week it's officially announced the sixth Robot Master, 'Boat Man'. Here's the official description, along with a look (via Rockman Corner):.

In a recent development, A coastal fishing robot with a timid nature, Boat Man drops anchor in Mega Man: Dual Override! Teased for being hesitant to venture the high seas, this nautical nemesis unleashes a typhoon of tyranny against all who doubt him!

As our sixth Robot Master, Boat Man casts a wide net against those that ridiculed him. Will he bait you into his tidal trap? Or will you take the plunge into his aquatic arena to battle him willingly?".

This follows Capcom announcing the winner of the Mega Man: Dual Override Robot Master design contest earlier this week. It's "Cleanser Man"!

These Robot Masters will be joined by Cook Man, Tank Man, Twinkle Girl and Jet Man, with more Robot Master Reveals to come.
