YouTuber Brandon Buckingham has canceled all upcoming filming trips and collaborations after revealing his health is deteriorating, warning that continuing to push himself could cost him his life.

The content creator, known for his documentary-style videos and interviews, has been battling serious medical problems since 2025, when he was hospitalized with multiple organ failure.

Although Buckingham returned to making YouTube videos earlier this year, he revealed in a November 16 Instagram Story that his condition has worsened and he has been hospitalized again.

“I regret to inform you I have cancelled all video trips and collaborations for the foreseeable future,” he wrote.

It has been revealed that “My health is bad and getting worse. I fear if I continue pushing myself I will lose my life.”.

Buckingham went on to detail the frightening symptoms he is experiencing, including purple hands, swollen feet, and persistent vomiting.

“I was just hospitalized AGAIN. My hands are purple, my feet are swollen, I am constantly vomiting. I can’t let this go on any longer,” he explained.

Despite the concerning upgrade, the YouTuber insisted he is taking steps to improve his condition and remains determined to recover.

“But don’t worry, I am being very proactive, and I WILL bounce back,” he added.

The announcement comes just days after Buckingham uploaded a new documentary filmed in India on November 12, titled India’s City of Trash | Inside the Most Polluted Town on Earth.

The video follows Buckingham and fellow creator Turkey Tom as they explore life around a massive landfill in Delhi.

Buckingham’s latest health scare comes nearly a year after he was rushed to intensive care with multiple organ failure in November 2025.

At the time, he revealed that his heart, lungs, liver, and kidneys were failing, with doctors treating him for sepsis, pneumonia, and other serious complications.

His condition prompted an outpouring of support, with MrBeast and thousands of fans helping raise more than $300,000 toward his medical expenses.

Sources indicate that in January 2026, Buckingham shared an encouraging recovery revision, revealing that his liver, kidneys, and pancreas had recovered. However, he mentioned he was still experiencing heart failure and permanent lung damage.

Buckingham has not disclosed the cause of his latest deterioration or indicated when he expects to resume filming.
