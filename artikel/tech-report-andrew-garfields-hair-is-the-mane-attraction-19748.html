📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that andrew Garfield’s hair is very long—and oh so glorious. When the 43-year-old actor sat down with GQ for his 2026 Men of the Year interview, he told us he’d grown his current shoulder-length mane for his role as Roy Horn (of Siegfried & Roy tiger-wrangling fame) in the upcoming series Wild Things. Production ultimately put him in a wig, but Garfield kept the length regardless. And thank goodness he did. The actor brought his enviable mane to the red carpet at our annual Men of the Year party at the Chateau Marmont in Los Angeles on Thursday night, where it held its own among Hollywood’s finest.

In a recent development, the Artificial star arrived in an Edward Sexton suit, accessorized with a Breguet watch. But, as has been the case lately, his hair was the real headliner. The neck-grazing length was in fine form, with a few stray strands tucked neatly behind his ears. And then there was the forehead curl: a particularly on-trend flourish that’s found its place atop some of this year’s best-dressed stars (including Ayo Edebiri).

We first caught a glimpse of Garfield’s longer locks at Wimbledon this summer in London. At the time, his longtime hairstylist, Lucy Halperin, let us in on the secret to the look: a little sea salt spray, styling cream, and a diffuser to bring out his natural curls and waves. The combination enhances the hair’s volume while keeping frizz at bay.

That mentioned, Halperin also credits his good hair to a bit of genetic luck. “Sometimes we just give it a good little wet down, and it kind of just works with him,” she told us at the time.

Whatever transformations Garfield has in store for his next on-screen role, we hope this particular look sticks around. Because if Thursday night was any indication, he’s not quite ready to part with it—and frankly, neither are we.
