Warning: SPOILERS ahead for NCIS: Origins season 3, episode 6!A dead, original NCIS character just made a surprise return.

The return of a deceased character in NCIS: Origins has been addressed by a series star after they made a history-changing return in the latest episode. NCIS: Origins season 3, episode 6 saw Mark Harmon's older Gibbs fall unconscious, only to have a vision of an older Mike Franks, with Muse Watson returning for the role following his previous stint on the original NCIS. The moment was the first time Watson had appeared in the prequel series, with Kyle Schmid playing a younger version of Franks as a main character on the prequel show.

Speaking with TVLine about his return, Watson explained what it was like to share the screen with Harmon once more for NCIS: Origins. He unveiled how the pair hadn't worked together for the last nine years, but, when they finally did share the screen again, it felt very familiar. In addition to being able to interact with him onscreen once more, Watson and Schmid both teased what to expect from the rest of season 3. This includes some high-octane action, comparative to a movie, with high stakes that made filming a massive undertaking.

Check out what Watson and Schmid had to say below:.

In a recent development, TVLine: What was it like sharing the screen with Mark Harmon again, and calling him "probie" again? Is it like riding a bicycle for you?

Sources indicate that muse Watson: It is. You know, Mark and I know each other, and even if we haven't seen each other in nine years, it's like we never miss a day if we get back together. So we understand each other and communicate with each other, and it's a great thing to see him again in person. But the love is always there.

TVLine: We only have a few episodes left in Season 3. Can you tease anything that’s coming up? Muse, will we see you again in Gibbs’ mind?

Muse Watson: I have no idea. They have a lot of footage. [Laughs].

According to recent reports, kyle Schmid: It’s action-packed. Shooting the last few episodes of the show was like shooting an action film. It was grueling and wearing and incredibly satisfying at the same time, and we get to see these characters really work as a team and kind of bond in a way that I don’t think we’ve seen before.

In a recent development, watson's character died in NCIS season 8, but has continued to return for flashbacks across the series. However, his joining the cast of NCIS: Origins for season 3 marks the first time he's ever appeared in an NCIS spinoff show. The moment happened after Gibbs' infected gunshot wound caused him to pass out. This is part of the series' present-day storyline running parallel to the 1990s main setting, after the older Gibbs is targeted while trying to retire peacefully in Alaska. This is where Watson's return comes into play.

There are only four episodes left in NCIS: Origins season 3, with the show having a shorter season than usual this time around. This is to make room for CBS' ever-growing lineup of original series, with NCIS: Sydney taking its place once 2027 rolls around. As both Watson and Schmid tease, the final episodes of the prequel spinoff are going to have a heavy action focus, seemingly in the past timeline. Whether this carries over to the present day remains to be seen.

It has been revealed that watson isn't sure if his version of Franks will return in later episodes of NCIS: Origins, but does say there's plenty of footage that could be used should they decide to bring him back. As for Schmid's younger rendition, it seems like he'll be caught up in the core conflicts of season 3's final episodes, with plenty of wild twists and turns as the show takes a high-octane action route in its approach. The teamwork on display can also inform the flashback by adding more layers depending on how past events unfold.

Recent findings suggest that seeing Watson return as Franks was a fun cameo that weaves the past and present storylines together in important ways. It helps bolster the show while also acting as a nostalgic trip for those who remember Watson from the original series. Since NCIS: Origins doesn't have much left for season 3, though, it remains to be seen if the actor will make another cameo before the show bows out.
