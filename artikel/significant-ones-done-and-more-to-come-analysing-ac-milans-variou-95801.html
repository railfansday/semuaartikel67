AC Milan’s negotiating table has remained busy between the summer and winter windows, with a clear emphasis on contracts.

Recent findings suggest that in the past month alone the club have announced new long-term deals for Strahinja Pavlovic and Christian Pulisic, and it believed that a couple more are not far from being announced. Extending the discussion to the youth teams, a lot of young talents have signed their first pro deals this year too.

Having been stung in the past by not preserving leverage when it comes to contracts, it marks a clear change in gear from the management to ensure that the prized assets for the present and future remain under control and do not slip away for nothing.

In the latest Bonus Podcast, Oli and Ben talk through the renewals Milan have completed recently, the ones that are close as per reports, as well as the situations that must be addressed soon as the clock ticks towards expiry.
