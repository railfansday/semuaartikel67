The City chief said: "Everyone can see that (his quality) but it's a risk every time. Hopefully he gets through this because people are going, ‘what are you playing him in that game for, he's a dead rubber (versus York).’.

“He needs to hit certain metrics and certain things during the week and what is the best environment for him to do that in.

“That’s the thing with Nick, he's in a good place at the minute, he's enjoying his football, he's fit.

“So, we need to harness that and keep him fit for as long as possible because when you put him on the pitch, he's going to score goals.”.

City, who return to action next Saturday with a Yorkshire derby at home to Doncaster Rovers, have emphatically answered the charge regarding their perceived lack of goals with a 12-goal haul in two big Valley Parade wins over Marine and York City respectively.
