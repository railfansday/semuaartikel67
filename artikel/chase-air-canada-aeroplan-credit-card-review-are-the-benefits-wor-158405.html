However, even if you have a different valuation of these points than I do, you can at least use the “Pay Yourself Back” feature to redeem these points for 1.25 cents each toward a travel purchase, so that’s a pretty high “cash out” value.

According to recent reports, those with the Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card can get a one-tier status upgrade to Aeroplan 35K status when they spend $75,000 on the card in a calendar year. You’ll get status for the remainder of the calendar year you qualify and the entire following calendar year.

Industry observers note that if you ask me, the value here is really for someone who is actually loyal to Air Canada. According to recent reports, the Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card offers a variety of valuable benefits and credits, beyond the elite status perks covered above. Some of these benefits are offered just for being a cardmember, while others can be earned with spending. The perks are pretty comprehensive, and you’ll find almost everything you could want short of lounge access. So let’s go over those.

The Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card offers up to $100 in Air Canada statement credits when you make qualifying Air Canada purchases. The credits apply to paid airfare, taxes and fees on award flights, and inflight food and drinks, though onboard Wi-Fi is excluded.

This breaks down as two credits of up to $50 each, one from January 1 through June 30 and one from July 1 through December 31. If you book cash fares on Air Canada twice a year, this is an easy way to put $100 back in your pocket, and this benefit alone can cover about half of the card’s annual fee.

Recent findings suggest that as an Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card holder, you can receive a 15% discount on the Aeroplan points required to book an award flight on Air Canada, Air Canada Express, or Air Canada Rouge. In practice, this also seems to work on partner award flights, as long as at least one segment is on Air Canada. The upside here can be absolutely huge, and it’s a great reason to have this card.

Recent findings suggest that the Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card offers a free first checked bag on Air Canada flights for the primary cardmember and up to eight others traveling on the same itinerary. This applies to itineraries with Air Canada, Air Canada Rouge, or Air Canada Express. The value of that could add up pretty quickly. There’s no need to actually pay for the ticket with the card, as long as you’re the primary cardmember and have your Aeroplan number linked to the reservation.

Recent findings suggest that once every four years, the Air Canada Aeroplan Credit Card offers a credit that’s valid toward a membership with Global Entry, TSA PreCheck, or NEXUS, worth up to $120. These are all programs that can save travelers time at the airport, and potentially even at the border.

According to recent reports, I specifically love that the card also offers a NEXUS credit, since that’s historically something we haven’t seen card issuers offer. NEXUS is arguably the best of the three programs, since it gets you access to Global Entry, TSA PreCheck, and expedited immigration in Canada.

Sources indicate that admittedly this is probably more interesting in theory than in practice, but I still like where Aeroplan is going with this. Those with the Air Canada Aeroplan Card will automatically have carbon offsets purchased for their flights when redeeming Aeroplan points for an Air Canada flight.
