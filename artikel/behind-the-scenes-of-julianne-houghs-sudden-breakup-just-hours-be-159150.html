📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that julianne Hough has truly had a week. On Monday (November 23), E! News revealed that the Dancing With the Stars co-host was dating Peloton instructor Adrian Williams after the pair had soft released their relationship on Instagram. But then one day later—just hours before the Dancing With the Stars live finale—TMZ reported that the “quite serious” relationship had “ended abruptly.”.

Despite all the drama, Julianne highlighted up to the Dancing With the Stars finale and didn’t miss a beat (shoutout to her semi-sheer revenge dress), and now a source is dishing on how she handled things behind the scenes.

^ A moment for the revenge dress.

Speaking to The Sun, the insider said that Julianne “seemed totally fine filming” and “wasn’t distracted or upset. She didn’t seem emotional or anything like that.”.

Sources indicate that “She was focused on the job but she did mention it was a ‘whirlwind’ of a week with the romance becoming public,” they added. “She didn’t get into the details about why things ended. The feeling I got is that it was extremely abrupt and it hadn’t even hit her yet that her relationship just crumbled because she was so focused on the finale and doing a good job.”.

The source didn’t have details on why the relationship ended so suddenly or on who called time, but did say that “now that the season is over, maybe it will hit her harder.” They also pointed out that she’s a total pro, which, true!
