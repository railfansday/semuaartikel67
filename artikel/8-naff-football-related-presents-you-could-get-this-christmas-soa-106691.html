It’s getting close to that time of year when your relatives know you like football and so try their best to get a gift, but they don’t always quite get it right.

Liking football means you often get just about anything as a gift provided it has your club’s badge on it (if you’re lucky) and let’s all hope that none of us open any of these under the tree this year.

Here are eight wonderfully awful football-related Christmas gifts for this year.

When we heard Bristol City were selling grass we thought that’s one way to raise the transfer budget but turns out it is of the turf variety.

The Robins are releasing soap that has been “scientifically” made to replicate the smell of the pitch at Ashton Gate so you can shower with the smell of a pitch that is 18th in the Championship right now.

We will give them credit for the excellent slogan of “because you’re turf it” but it has naturally opened them to such jokes as it smelling like the s**t football that gets played on it.

Profits will at least go to the club’s foundation.

Introducing the Ashton Gate-scented soap to raise funds for the Robins Foundation’s work in communities across Bristol.

Recent findings suggest that available online and in-store. https://t.co/kbDyZ1XbTC.

— Bristol City FC (@BristolCity) November 24, 2026.

This is worth it for the pun alone.

Ipswich are selling a Christmas candle called Ipswicks which they describe as a Luxury Scented Christmas Candle smelling of Pine, Eucalyptus & Cedarwood.

We don’t know what the Ipswich marketing department thinks of their fans though as there is a warning to not touch the tin as it may get hot.

Club-branded playing cards is not that unusual but we are including these because Arsenal have decided to charge their fans £70 for three packs of cards.

For that money, you get a commemorative box that has the names of the players that won the title last season and the cards are in the colours of the kits used last campaign but we hope no one buys that because that is an incredible waste of money.

Just imagine, you’ve had your Christmas dinner and someone suggests some card games and then pulls those out. Awful.

If you think £70 is too much, then don’t worry, you can get a single pack of cards for £18.

Sources indicate that honestly, there are so many things from the United store that could have made the list. Firstly, they are selling quite literally a cardboard box for £10. They also have a sack so the jokes write themselves.

But the one item we have picked out is the United-branded Monopoly board they are selling for £50.

Recent findings suggest that realising that there are not 28 United-related places to go on a Monopoly board, the club decided to name each square after moments in their history, fair enough. What isn’t fair enough is that the final square, which is the most expensive slot on the board, comes with the title of “2016/17 WON THE LOT.”.

Sorry? We’ve checked the league table and can’t help but see you were sixth? And surely the FA have made a mistake because they seem to think that Arsenal won the cup that year? While we are at it, you will want to have a word with UEFA because they seem to think that Real Madrid won the Champions League that season.

“The lot” United are referring to is actually the League Cup, Europa League and Community Shield. Did Jose Mourinho commission this?

Well they’ve got to pay their massive wages somehow.

It has been revealed that man Utd truly shocking in 2026-27 Premier League wage bill vs. position comparison.

It may have slipped your attention but did you know that League Two side Northampton Town are now owned by a Brazilian consortium fronted by Alexandre Pato?

The Cobblers are doing their best to spread the word by releasing a number of Brazil-inspired pieces of merchandise and so we are picking the half and half scarf that has Northampton in the middle and the Brazil flag on the ends.

It feels like the kind of thing that will end up in the National Football Museum in 100 years’ time.

According to recent reports, the problem with football is that it is fast-moving and so one product that is good one year can quickly become out of date. Step forward Arne Slot figure.

Liverpool commissioned a set of Soccerstarz of the 2025/26 squad but, unfortunately for Slot, is still hanging around in a storage cupboard somewhere.

They are trying their best to shift it, reducing it down to £1 or 90p if you are a club member, but even he looks annoyed at still being there.

Do you even love your club if you don’t wear socks promoting the club’s owners?

Any Leicester fans afraid of being called a plastic ought to buy themselves a pair of these fetching purple socks that do not display really anything to do with the club but instead the brand of King Power.

There is a Leicester badge but it is on the sole of the foot meaning no one will see it but at least you show off your favourite Bangkok-based travel retail conglomerate.

Bonus points to the Foxes for the Sonic the Hedgehog scarf. We don’t know why either.

It’s late on Christmas Day. You’re full to the brim but excited for the day’s football tomorrow.

You fancy a little tipple to round off the festivities and so what better way to scratch that itch and get prepared for tomorrow than with a Sheffield United shot glass with a picture of a chip butty on the side of it?

For just £3.50, or you can pay in four interest-free payments of 87p, you can have a shot glass which is “a nod to the no-fuss, unpretentious spirit that runs through every corner of S2 [where Bramall Lane is].

Apparently it “carries the kind of quiet pride that doesn’t need to shout” but it just makes us feel rather queasy.
