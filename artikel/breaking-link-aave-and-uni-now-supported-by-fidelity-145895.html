Fidelity Investments now supports three new cryptocurrencies on its retail crypto trading platform: Chainlink (LINK), Aave (AAVE) and Uniswap (UNI).

It has been revealed that the firm expanded from five available cryptocurrencies to eight on Nov. 24. In just one day, Fidelity added the same number of digital assets that it had added in the four years since it first made Bitcoin and Ether available for customer trading.

Recent findings suggest that eligible U.S. customers can trade these three tokens on the Fidelity website and app. Some states may have limited access to Fidelity's services.

Fidelity's three fresh listings include tokens that represent different areas of decentralized finance. Aave is a lending protocol where people can deposit crypto assets to earn interest or use them as collateral for loans. AAVE token holders can vote on changes to the protocol's lending markets and other aspects of its operation.

According to recent reports, uniswap is a decentralized trading platform where users can trade different tokens using shared pools of assets provided by other users. UNI is the Uniswap governance token.

In a recent development, chainlink provides the data that blockchain apps need to operate. For example, a lending protocol might use a Chainlink price feed to assess the value of collateral for a borrower's loan. Chainlink (LINK) tokens are used to pay for services provided on its network.

Fidelity Crypto started accepting customers from its waitlist in November 2022. At the time, the only two cryptocurrencies it offered were Bitcoin (BTC) and Ether (ETH). By March 2023, Fidelity was offering its service to more eligible U.S. investors, but these were still the only cryptocurrencies it allowed customers to trade.

According to recent reports, on July 17, 2024, Fidelity added Litecoin (LTC) to its list of cryptocurrencies, bringing the total to three. Over a year later, on Oct. 23, 2025, the manufacturer introduced Solana (SOL). In addition to individual retail accounts, Solana became available for crypto IRAs, wealth managers and institutional clients.

Recent findings suggest that fidelity added its fifth cryptocurrency on Feb. 4, 2026, when it launched Fidelity Digital Dollar (FIDD) as its first stablecoin available to retail and institutional investors. Eligible users can exchange or redeem each FIDD token for $1. Fidelity waives its usual crypto trading fee when users exchange FIDD for dollars.
