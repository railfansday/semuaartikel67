It has been revealed that the best part about any Grand Theft Auto game is the cheats, and Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers on that front. Rockstar's latest includes a whole mess of password-protected secrets that will really let you have some fun in Vice City. You'll have to work for them, though. GTA 6's cheats are deeply hidden, and will require you to jump through a few hoops to start using them.

Here's everything you need to know about cheats in GTA 6, from how to unlock them, to the list itself.

[Ed. note: We're still working to hunt down every cheat code, but until we find them all, this will give you an idea of what you'll need to do to unlock them all.].

Update (Nov. 21): We've updated this guide with inputs for three cheat codes.

Sources indicate that if you're looking for a way to enter cheat codes the moment you start GTA 6, you're going to be disappointed. You won't be able to use them until you have fully cleared the story. Once you finish the game and return to your save file, you'll find a fresh Cheat tab. Open your Map, tab over to Progression, and scroll to the end to find the Cheats menu.

You won't get the actual cheat codes immediately. Each one is locked behind a requirement. That can be as simple as finding a specific car to earn the ability to summon it, or as demanding as having Jason and Lucia hold hands for four hours. Once you clear a condition, a pop-up message will tell you that you've unlocked a new Cheat code, and the unlock requirement in the Cheats menu will be replaced with the code.

Notably, your progress throughout the game, even before beating it, does contribute to these requirements. By the time you roll credits and unlock the Cheats menu, you'll likely already have a few of them unlocked.

To activate a cheat code, first pull out your phone by pressing Up on your D-Pad. Then hold down L1 and R1 on the home screen. (We have only been able to test the PS5 version of the game so far, but our assumption is that you'll need to hold LB and RB on Xbox Series X.) While holding those buttons, you'll have to enter the unlocked code with the D-Pad. Based on our testing, you won't be able to activate a code until you've actually beaten the game and unlocked the Cheats menu.

Note that activating a cheat code will disable saving for the rest of your play session. You also won't be able to earn achievements while cheats are on. Always remember to save before activating a cheat so you don't lose any progress.
