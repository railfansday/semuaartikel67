📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that bitcoin tumbled 8.9% over the past 24 hours to $55,693.30 on Saturday, according to CoinGecko, extending its weekly loss to 16.2%. The total crypto market fell 5.2% to $2.095 trillion, while trading volume jumped 46.5% to $117.36 billion as investors rushed to exit. Ether sank 10.1% to $1,543.23, Solana dropped to $65.64 and Hyperliquid slid 14.1%. Bitcoin has now lost roughly $20,000 in a month and is far below its October 2025 record above $126,000. The selloff reflects a broad retreat from risk. The Dow and S&P 500 fell into correction territory on Friday, and even gold, now down more than 35% from its early 2026 peak, is being sold. With cash accounts paying about 5%, assets that offer no yield have lost appeal. Juan Xavier Sanchez of Activest Wealth Management told CNBC that Bitcoin has historically performed best when interest rates are low. Its rising link to the Nasdaq 100 since late September has also undercut its image as a safe haven. A run of security scares, from the Binance hack to bank and air traffic outages, has further dented confidence in digital systems.
