Dirty ice around young star Beta Pictoris suggests frequent collisions and sheds light on the system's formation

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (92 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent study of infrared light collected by the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST), a group of astronomers detected ice for the first time around the young star Beta Pictoris. Researchers had expected ice around Beta Pictoris, but previous searches had not directly detected it. The study, published in Science, found that the ice is mostly noncrystalline and mixed with carbon dioxide. The team says these newly introduced details provide clues about the early formation of the Beta Pictoris system and collisions that may still be occurring.
