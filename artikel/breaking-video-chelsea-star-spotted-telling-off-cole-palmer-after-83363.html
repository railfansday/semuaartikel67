Chelsea star Cole Palmer seemed to get a telling-off from teammate Joao Pedro for not acknowledging the fans after today’s defeat against Leeds United.

The Blues suffered a shock defeat at the hands of Leeds, with Daniel Farke’s side coming away with a stunning 4-1 victory and their first three points at Stamford Bridge since 1999.

Palmer had another pretty poor game, and it seems he was not at all happy as he looked to leave the pitch swiftly at full time, without showing his appreciation to the home support.
