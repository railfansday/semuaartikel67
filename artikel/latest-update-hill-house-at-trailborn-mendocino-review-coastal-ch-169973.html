Latest Update: Hill House at Trailborn Mendocino review: Coastal charm meets Marriott Bonvoy

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (178 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Mendocino now features its first major hotel loyalty program property with the renovated Hill House at Trailborn Mendocino, part of a nature lodge collection.
  • The hotel offers 44 rooms and suites, nature-inspired design, and amenities like a complimentary breakfast (for now), daily Après Hour, fire pits, and a lobby convenience store.
  • Guests can enjoy on-site activities such as yoga, wine sampling, live music, and a detective-themed library, with curated outdoor experiences planned for the future.
  • The property is accessible, located a short walk from downtown Mendocino, and award nights are available through the associated hotel loyalty program.
  • Complimentary breakfast will shift to a voucher system for a nearby restaurant once a second property opens in 2027.
  • Room rates vary seasonally, with higher prices on summer weekends and lower rates in the off-season.
  • Award night redemptions start at 48,000 points per night as of June 2026, per TPG's valuations.
