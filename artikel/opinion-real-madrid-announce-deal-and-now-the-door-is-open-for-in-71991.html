Recent findings suggest that the door is undoubtedly open for Liverpool to strike an incredible move. Real Madrid opened that door with a deal announcement.

In a recent development, liverpool need to boost their midfield. That's apparent to all, even after striking two massive-money contract deals with Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai this year.

Sources indicate that but do either actually get into Liverpool's first-XI right now? Virtually everyone would pick Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister at the pairing and that absolutely was not the blueprint for the campaign.

Nyoni is an interesting one, though. He's proven that Liverpool look better with a proper defensive, controlling midfielder in there - something most people figured would be the case before the summer.

In a recent development, liverpool, however, did not believe that. They didn't sign any midfielders and instead sold Curtis Jones without replacing him, leaving the squad short and over-reliant on attack-first options in the middle.

Industry observers note that nyoni has shown that Liverpool were wrong. Of course, it looks like they'd already figured that out by the end of August, with moves for midfielders quickly heading to the top of their agenda for 2027 after just a few games this season.

And we don't expect Liverpool to rely so heavily on Nyoni for the entirety of the campaign. He does need some degree of help and the midfield could use a reshuffle.

Our Value Adder model suggests Camavinga is very, very underrated and the reason for that is largely Aurelien Tchouameni. Tchouameni might be the world's best defensive midfielder, honestly, and casts an enormous shadow over his compatriot.

That's why Camavinga hasn't entirely established himself at Real Madrid and why certain clubs were pushing to sign him in the summer. He decided to stay put, however, and we imagine that was to try and force his way in under Jose Mourinho.

In a recent development, that hasn't happened. Camavinga has only started two La Liga fixtures and reports suggest they hold interest in signing Martin Zubimendi. That's a signing that would usurp Camavinga.

According to recent reports, more than that, Real Madrid have doubled-down on Tchouameni, committing to a big newly introduced contract this week. There's essentially no way for Camavinga to get into that starting XI regularly - and the new contract confirms that.

Which is why we feel the door is now open pretty wide for Liverpool. Camavinga is a fantastic, high-energy player who could be exactly what the Reds need to improve their midfield. The opportunity looks to be there and they should try to take it.
