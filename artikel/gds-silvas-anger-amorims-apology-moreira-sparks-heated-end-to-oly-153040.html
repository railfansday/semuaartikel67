There was a flash point towards the end of AC Milan’s win against Olympiacos due to Diego Moreira’s celebration.

Recent findings suggest that as La Gazzetta dello Sport recall, Moreira got into an altercation with half of the Olympiacos team in the final seconds of their Europa League match. The reason was his celebration after scoring the 4-2 goal: Moreira stood in front of the Greek fans and stared at them for a few seconds, motionless.

It was a gesture of defiance, as if to say: ‘Game over, I won’. He was also seen mimicking someone knocking on a door and then closing it, again to remind the home fans that the three points were coming safely back to Italy.

It is a reflection of the Belgian’s lively and brazen character. The Olympiacos players weren’t happy with the celebration as they deemed it to be provocative. That being noted, they mostly seemed to be feeding off the anger of the supporters in that section of the stadium.

According to recent reports, saliakas and Jota Silva didn’t take it well and went head-to-head with Moreira. Silva waved him off, as if to say ‘don’t do that in our house’. With Saliakas, however, there was a tense face-to-face exchange that lasted a second or so.

At the end of the press conference, Ruben Amorim was asked how he would have felt if the roles were reversed and a player celebrated like that after sealing a win against Milan.

It has been revealed that “It won’t happen again. Moreira is a young man, he needs to grow up. We know we wouldn’t like it if they did it to our fans. It’s my responsibility, but he didn’t do it in a malicious way. I apologise,” he said.

The referee blew the whistle after some pushing and shoving, bringing an end to a dramatic night in which Milan came out on top in a six-goal thriller. Moreira and Olympiacos probably won’t be together for dinner tonight, but that’s fine.
