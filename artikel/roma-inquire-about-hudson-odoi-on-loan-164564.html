📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roma continues to look to the Premier League to strengthen Gian Piero Gasperini’s attacking lineup in the winter transfer window.

Among the players being monitored by the Giallorossi management is Callum Hudson-Odoi, a 2000-born winger from Nottingham Forest.

According to Football Insider, the English player is ready to ask for a January transfer if he doesn’t find more playing time in the coming weeks.

In a recent development, the former Chelsea player has been marginalized by Oliver Glasner’s coaching project, partly due to the nature of the manager’s playing system. This season, Hudson-Odoi has played just 45 minutes in the Premier League, without ever starting in any competition.

Roma would like to secure the winger’s services on loan, guaranteeing him the prospect of a starting spot.

Sources indicate that hudson-Odoi’s contract with Nottingham Forest expires in 2028, and a loan move could represent a solution ahead of a possible permanent move next summer. Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens remains among the Giallorossi’s targets to strengthen their attack.
