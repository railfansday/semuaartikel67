The compact USB-C charger for Google’s latest Pixel Watch generations has hit the lowest price we’ve ever seen for Black Friday at just $18.99.

It has been revealed that compatible with Google’s upcoming charging system on Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5, the Wasserstein “On-the-Go” charger ditches the cable and just lets you use an existing USB-C cable to top off your smartwatch. It’s officially approved by Google and works with both sizes of the Pixel Watch 4 and the Pixel Watch 5.

Normally, the adapter is sold for $25, but is down to $18.99 for Black Friday, though it does cost a dollar more at Amazon.

The simple adapter is more compact than Google’s official charging cable, making it easier to bring along for travel. It also comes with a keychain so it can easily be stored away securely in a bag, and only needs USB-C input of 5V/1.5A.
