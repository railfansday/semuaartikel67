Jack Blues Bieber is all grown up. Today, Hailey Bieber gave us the clearest glimpse at her little son with Justin Bieber, and it came in the form of a sweet video promoting her latest Rhode products, specifically her pocket blushes, peptide lip boost, and peptide under-eye patches.

Industry observers note that in the video, shared on the brand’s Instagram, Hailey is seen having a cute, silly mom moment with Jack as she gets her makeup done. The little Bieber plays with her beauty blender, sits on his mom’s lap, and at one point hides under her shirt, where she suggests he stay forever.

While the clips show only the back and sides of Jack’s face, we do see the two-year-old’s spiky blond hair, which looks just like his famous dad’s when he was younger.

The video also sees Hailey talk about motherhood, and how having a baby has completely changed her as a person, “for the better.”.

Hailey and Justin, who married in 2018, welcomed Jack in August 2024. Since then, they have given peeks at their son on social media, with Justin even including him in his music video for “Yukon” (though his face is kept mostly hidden) and in the artwork for his albums Swag and Swag II.

Still, they tend to keep their little boy out of the spotlight, and have yet to introduce him to the flashy world of red carpets and Hollywood parties that they frequent.
