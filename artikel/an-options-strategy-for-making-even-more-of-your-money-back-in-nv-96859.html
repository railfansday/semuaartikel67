Sources indicate that nvidia's board has officially reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, igniting a roughly 28% relief rally into the low-$160s after last week's brutal sell-off drove shares down to $125.53. While Huang's return removes a major leadership vacuum, the stock still sits far below its $243.37 52-week high and autumn trading ranges above $200.

It has been revealed that trapped shareholders now face a classic post-crash hurdle: heavy overhead supply. Investors who bought near prior peaks often use sharp rebounds to exit at breakeven, creating stubborn technical resistance between $175 and $210 — especially with congressional testimony and export scrutiny still lingering.

Rather than waiting passively for a full V-shaped surge to all-time highs, underwater holders can deploy a stock repair strategy geared toward a partial recovery.
