Nvidia release hotfix for latest graphics card driver battering SSDs with excessive writes, but it looks like reverting may still be the better option

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Late last week, reports emerged of some folks having Nvidia's latest GeForce driver, 621.24, needlessly write vast amounts of data to their PC's SSD, a bug that could affect the component's lifespan at a time when replacing SSDs certainly isn't cheap. The immediate short-term solution for affected owners was to roll back to a previous driver, 621.01. Sadly it appears that might still be the best way to go despite Nvidia having released a hotfix.

According to recent reports, the hotfix, driver version 621.35, was released on November 20th and as of writing is only available to download directly from the Nvidia website. It's designed to address one issue, this being that "excessive writes to file nvlog.nvlgstg may occur in background".

It has been revealed that "Since we have so many changes being checked in, we usually try to align driver releases with significant game or product releases," Nvidia wrote in their announcement of the hotfix. "This process has served us pretty well over the years but it has one significant weakness. Sometimes a change that is crucial to many people might end up sitting and waiting until we are able to release the driver. The fixes that make it in are based in part on your feedback in the Driver Feedback threads and partly on how realistic it is for us to swiftly address them. These fixes (and many more) will be incorporated into the next official driver release, at which time the hotfix driver will be taken down.

It has been revealed that "To be sure, these Hotfix drivers are beta, optional and provided as-is. They are run through a much abbreviated QA process. The sole reason they exist is to get fixes out to you more rapidly. The safest option is to wait for the next WHQL certified driver. But we know that many of you are willing to try these out.".

In a recent development, unfortunately, it seems that based on some testing by customers on r/Nvidia, the hotfix makes the situation with excessive SSD writing less bad than it is with 621.24, but doesn't eliminate the problem entirely. According to one of these users, 621.35 stops the excessive writing from starting right after you boot up and reduces the amount that's being written when the glitch's at its peak, but doesn't prevent some excessive writing creeping in if hardware is left running for extended periods or left idling overnight.

According to recent reports, given that, rolling back to 621.01 still appears to be the most effective way of keeping the writes at an acceptable level while waiting for Nvidia to release a new driver than can permanently do away with the problem. One would hope it won't take too long, especially given the 621.24 driver still doesn't look to have been pulled offline so no one else grabs it prior to a fix arriving.

In a recent development, if you want to check if you're suffering from the excessive writing you'll need to open task manager, head to the 'details' tab, right click on the column header, click 'select columns', select 'I/O write bytes' from the menu of check boxes that brings up, then look for 'NVDisplay.Container.exe'. The number next that exe is in bytes, so you'll need to do some maths to work out if it's only writing a matter of MB or chunks of GB.
