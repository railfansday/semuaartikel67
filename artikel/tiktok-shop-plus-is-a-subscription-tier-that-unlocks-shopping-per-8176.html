TikTok Shop is delivering a perk for shoppers this holiday season: a fresh subscription service.

Initially, Shop launched as an offshoot of TikTok, but in recent months, the app’s commercial wing has been positioned as a standalone product. That transformation is driven by research that identifies TikTok Shop as a common reason why users open the app in the first place.

Recent findings suggest that in Southeast Asia, where TikTok has made deep inroads, a standalone version of TikTok Shop has quietly introduced. Consumers in Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, and The Philippines can use that app to claim exclusive deals and take advantage of free shipping.

For that price, owners will enjoy a lot of the same perks that can be found on Asia’s standalone TikTok Shop app. According to Modern Retail, TikTok Shop Plus features include free shipping on items marked with a “Plus” badge, instant refunds on qualifying products, and three different types of coupons. One discount will take $5 off any order, another will save $10 on orders of at least $59, and a third will knock $30 off orders of at least $119.

According to recent reports, “TikTok Shop Plus is currently being piloted with an expanding group of eligible U.S. owners,” mentioned TikTok President of Strategic Initiatives Patrick Nommensen in a statement. “As we gradually scale access to the pilot, we’ll continue to gather feedback and refine the experience, with the goal of delivering more value and convenience to shoppers.”.

In a recent development, tikTok Shop has received a lot of upgrades as its execs look to capitalize on the momentum they generated during the 2025 holiday season. During Black Friday weekend alone, the platform did $500 million of U.S. sales.

Recent findings suggest that to keep the good times rolling, TikTok Shop has integrated UPS’ Happy Returns service to cut down on those feelings of buyer’s remorse. And through partnerships with brands like Disney, TikTok Shop is stocking its virtual shelves with items that are on millions of wish lists.
