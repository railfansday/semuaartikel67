It has been revealed that here’s a fun fact: in the lore of the Fallout series, dogs weren’t allowed into the Vault-Tec Vaults, with all the dogs we see throughout the games being those that survived and evolved into wolf-like forms after the Great War. Cats, on the other hand, were permitted into the Vaults within reason thanks to their applications as therapy pets and efficacy as pest-hunters. In spite of that, cats didn’t start appearing in the Fallout games until Fallout 4 and Fallout 76. Apparently, the reason for that is that they all just sorta got lost somewhere.

Sources indicate that A newly-discovered document from the Australian Classification Board has leaked the existence of a newly introduced Fallout spin-off game titled A Wasteland Full of Cats. This game is apparently being created in cooperation between Bethesda and Devcats, an indie developer specializing in hidden object games about cats like A Park Full of Cats, A Building Full of Cats, and An Arcade Full of Cats.

Is it the Fallout game we’ve all been waiting for in the near-decade since the launch of Fallout 76? No, definitely not, but, well, it’s something, I suppose. Every bit of Fallout-adjacent content we get can continue to tide us over until Fallout 5, whenever that ends up being, or at least the next season of the Fallout television series.

In a recent development, according to the description of the game on the Australian Classification Board website, A Wasteland Full of Cats will cast you in the role of one of the various cats living in one of the Vaults that dot the Wasteland. Following some manner of accident, all of the other cats living in the Vault alongside you are let loose out into the Wasteland, and you’ll need to go out there to round them all up.

In the same vein as Devcats’s other games, this will be a jaunt through 14 locations and 33 rooms where you’ll need to point out the errant cats hidden within the scenery, Where’s Waldo-style.

A Wasteland Full of Cats will cast you in the role of one of the various cats living in one of the Vaults.

You’ll be helped along the way by various NPC characters, hopefully including some familiar faces, with many of the locations you visit apparently being classic spots from across the Fallout franchise. Some of these locations will have mild puzzle elements, allowing you to uncover non-critical secret levels. According to the description, the game will culminate in a final battle against a horde of Radroaches, though I’m admittedly not quite sure how finding cats translates into battling giant mutant roaches, but I guess we’ll find out.

It has been an exceptionally hot minute since the last mainline Fallout game, specifically the release of Fallout 4 in 2015. Since then, it’s been pretty much exclusively spin-offs, such as the mobile title Fallout Shelter, its gacha-added version, Fallout Shelter Online, the MMO stylings of Fallout 76, and I guess Zen Pinball’s Fallout table for the Pinball FX games, if you want to count that.

Recent findings suggest that back in 2022, Todd Howard said in an interview with IGN that development on Fallout 5 would begin in earnest once The Elder Scrolls VI was done and dusted. Of course, considering we only just learned that game’s full title recently, I have a sinking feeling the timeline has shifted on that a bit. For what it’s worth, Bethesda did say in an X, formerly Twitter post this past July that Fallout 5 has officially begun pre-production, but again, that doesn’t give us much to go on.

Sources indicate that as of now, we don’t have any particular release dates or even release windows for upcoming Fallout projects, whether it’s mainline games, the TV show, or even this newly introduced cat thing. Just gotta keep wading through the Wasteland patiently, I guess….
