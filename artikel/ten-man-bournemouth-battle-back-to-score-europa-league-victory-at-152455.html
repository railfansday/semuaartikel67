Bournemouth moved a step closer to automatic qualification for the Europa League last 16 after coming from behind with 10 men to win 2-1 at Celta Vigo.

It was a remarkable 10th victory in a row in all competitions for Marco Rose’s side and it might have been the most satisfying of the lot for the manager, coming as it did after his team had found themselves a goal down and a man down at half-time.

The visitors looked in trouble when, minutes after Iago Aspas had curled Celta in front, Bafode Diakite was red-carded for a poorly timed tackle.

Bournemouth might have folded then, but instead they came roaring back, Alvaro Rodriguez heading them level almost immediately after the start of the second half.

Sources indicate that A draw would have been an excellent result in the circumstances. Substitute Rayan had other ideas, waiting at the end of a rapid breakaway to sweep in the winner 12 minutes from time.

Victory against Viktoria Plzen in December will almost certainly now be enough to earn direct passage to the last 16.

Pablo Duran had the ball in the Bournemouth net after 10 minutes after it spilled back to him off Michele Di Gregorio, the goalkeeper relieved to see the offside flag go up against the Celta striker.

Industry observers note that the stand-in keeper redeemed himself minutes later with a solid save from an outrageous bicycle kick by Aspas. It would have been a remarkable goal but the Celta great would not be denied. Bournemouth failed fully to get the ball away, and from the edge of the box Aspas curled a beauty in off the far post.

Recent findings suggest that bournemouth had gone behind in the first half of their last game in this competition at Sparta Prague. That night, the balance was tipped almost immediately in their favour by a red card for the Czech side, but here it was Rose’s team who found themselves down to 10 men.

Diakite made an honest attempt to win the ball but was late and his studs landed on the ankle of Couhaib Driouech. An initial yellow card was overturned following a VAR check and Bournemouth were on the ropes.

It had been the most challenging half of their debut European campaign, but within minutes of the start of the second period, they were level.

Recent findings suggest that amine Adli was left in space outside the box to send over a wonderful cross to the far post. Arriving there was Rodriguez, sneaking ahead of his defender to get a head on the ball and nod in the equaliser.

A wonderful counter-attack brought the Premier League side their winner. Adrien Truffert revealed good strength out on left to outmuscle Sergio Carreira, and he fed the ball up to Marcus Tavernier. He might have shot himself but instead moved it on to Rayan who swept home.
