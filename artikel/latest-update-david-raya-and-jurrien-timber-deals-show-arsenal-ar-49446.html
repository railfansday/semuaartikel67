Arsenal are continuing their excellent work in retaining their key players as David Raya and Jurrien Timber have both been approached about newly introduced contracts.

Industry observers note that this comes after Declan Rice signed a five-year contract extension. The midfielder will now stay at the Emirates until 2031, but Mikel Arteta is now close to locking down more defenders in his squad.

Timber has reportedly agreed better terms, and Raya is also on the verge of a newly introduced deal. Although both deals are yet to be officially unveiled, it shows how notable Arsenal are trying to keep them at the top.

The importance of retaining Raya and Timber is evident.

Industry observers note that since arriving at the club from Brentford, Raya has become Arteta’s first choice in goal. He has become an key part of Arsenal’s attacking play due to his distribution, decision-making and game under pressure.

Recent findings suggest that his performances have also earned him honours outside of North London as he was named in the 2026 FIFPRO World 11. Only the second Arsenal player to win the award after Thierry Henry in 2006.

Raya has a contract in place until 2028, although he was given better financial terms last year. However, a latest long-term deal will offer more security to one of Arteta’s most trusted players.

It has been revealed that since joining from Ajax in 2023, Timber has become a key part of Arsenal’s backline. Despite injury problems, he has proven to be one of the most dependable defenders in the squad.

He can play as a right back, left back and centre back which provides Arteta with great versatility. He has the physicality and technical ability to help Arsenal at both ends of the pitch.

It has been reported that Timber has agreed to better terms. How long his contract is for is unknown.

There is a pattern beginning to emerge from Arsenal’s recent contract news.

Rice has been signed until 2031. Timber is also expected to sign, committing his peak years to the club. Raya is also expected to sign soon, with talks said to be at an advanced stage.

Arteta recently signed a contract extension until 2030. They aren’t just trying to keep their best players for another year but are looking to create consistency within the squad and management.

This is an issue for any club competing within the domestic and European leagues, as it can cause problems with team cohesion and development due to constant change of key players.

While there is still work to be done, this is positive news for fans.

Industry observers note that signing Raya and Timber will ensure that two key components of Arteta’s backline are safe. It will also show that with Rice’s fresh contract, they’re looking to build on their recent success and not let their best players near the end of their contracts.
