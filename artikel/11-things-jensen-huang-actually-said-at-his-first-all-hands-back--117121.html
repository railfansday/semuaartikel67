📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that jensen Huang is going to therapy for 90 minutes a day and rethinking his management style, and he states that violating Nvidia’s code of conduct isn’t necessarily a fireable offense. That’s just some of what the newly returned Nvidia CEO told staff during his first all-hands meeting on Tuesday.

It has been revealed that here are some of the most striking things Huang remarked during the meeting, according to remarks reviewed by WIRED. Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Sources indicate that on self-improvement: “I go to therapy every day for an hour and a half. And it’s been helping me a lot.”.

Sources indicate that on accountability: “There are rumors on the internet that apparently I’m a tech bro, and therefore I’m back. Apparently, I’m a tech bro, and because I wear a super nice jacket, that I’m above the law, and I’m above the code of conduct. That is not true. It won’t be tolerated, and it wasn’t tolerated here.”.

Recent findings suggest that “If one of you were to violate a code of conduct, what does that mean? If you were ever to violate a code of conduct, it is not appropriate. I just wanted you to know, it is not right, it's not appropriate, it won’t be tolerated. But ‘it won’t be tolerated’ does not mean the moment you violate a code of conduct, we walk you out the door. Facts, circumstances, a lot of assessments. And your management team will still make the decision. That is what is in the best interest of the company and its employees, with all the facts and circumstances.”.

On his management style: “The intensity I bring elevates everybody else. But I can go too far. When I go too far, it hurts people, it belittles them, it disempowers them. And that’s not right. And so I need to go figure out how I’m going to bring the intensity, the crazy intensity, to the field.”.

Industry observers note that on women at Nvidia: “I believe we need to create and continue to sustain a safe environment for all of our women. And if I did anything that put that in jeopardy, I cannot tell you how sad I am, and I’ll do everything I can to fix it. We will not tolerate that.”.

In a recent development, on succession: “All day long, this company can run autonomously, and you saw it. I promise you, whether I’m here or not, this company is wired up to execute, execute, and execute, and deliver on financial results.”.

Industry observers note that “I also didn’t believe in succession planning. Let that sink in. I don’t believe in succession planning. I think it’s flawed. The idea of succession planning is not flawed. What people think succession planning means is flawed.”.

Recent findings suggest that “There’s a reason why we don’t have succession planning; it’s not because we are unaware of the word. I’m the longest-running tech CEO in history.”.

In a recent development, on politics: “There [were] also rumors that apparently I’m back because the highest levels of our government wanted me back. That is also completely untrue. Our board of directors performed their duty as they should have.”.

It has been revealed that “It is absolutely true that President Trump really, really loves our company,” Huang told employees. “He gloats about Nvidia. You know he does. But there’s a question about politics. Are we now political? Are we partisan? I wanna be very clear with you. President Trump is the United States president. He is my president. And I’ll do everything I can to help this president be informed about how he could make decisions that are in the best interest of our country … We have to do it, and whoever the next president is will be my president, and I will do exactly the same.”.

“Don’t think for a second that politics ever plays a role in this company, not even a little bit. We are the most apolitical company. I don’t love that we talk about politics here. Take it home. I love that we talk about work here.”.
