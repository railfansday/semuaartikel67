Speaking at Monday’s press conference ahead of Arsenal’s UEFA Champions League against Borussia Dortmund, Gunners boss Mikel Arteta has given credit to the parents of Jobe Bellingham.

It has been revealed that “I think you have to congratulate mum and dad for what they have done because it is incredible. When you look at the two of them [Jobe and Jude Bellingham], they are two amazing footballers – what they are doing is remarkable. Let’s give credit to the parents,” Arteta told reporters ahead of Tuesday’s game at The Emirates.

Jude spent three years in Dortmund from 2020 to 2023 before moving to Real Madrid, where he’s established himself as an notable player. Jobe followed his brother’s footsteps by following him to the Signal Iduna Park when he joined from Sunderland in the summer of 2025.

Although struggling to initially find his feet in 2025/26, Jobe has been on an excellent run of form of late, which has in turn seen him called up to the England national team by Thomas Tuchel during the most recent international break.

With both Bellingham brothers on top of their game at the moment, it is understandable that Arteta chose to praise the parents who have been key to the success of both players.
