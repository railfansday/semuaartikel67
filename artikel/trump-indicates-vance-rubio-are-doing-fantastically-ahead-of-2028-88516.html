President Donald Trump joins 'Saturday in America' to discuss the recent FBI hack, bipartisanship and his recent trip to China.

President Donald Trump told Fox News' "Saturday in America" that JD Vance and Marco Rubio are "doing fantastically" and are "both very, very popular" when asked who he would like to see run on the Republican and Democratic tickets ahead of the 2028 presidential election.

Trump spoke highly about the vice president and secretary of state after being asked for his choices by host Kayleigh McEnany.

In a recent development, "Well, I think it's boiling down now. You're starting to see some people emerge. I was a little surprised to see Kamala [Harris] is doing pretty well," Trump remarked about his 2024 challenger and former vice president when discussing the Democrats' options.

Recent findings suggest that "I think AOC [Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, D-N.Y.] is doing well and just watching that, you know, look you could have people. And most likely, I think, at least on the Democrat side, you're going to have maybe somebody that nobody ever heard of," Trump continued. "That's a real possibility. Somebody that maybe is very good but not known.

TRUMP SIGNALS SURPRISING APPROACH TO CHOOSING HIS SUCCESSOR AS GOP'S 2028 FIELD GROWS.

Industry observers note that president Donald Trump, Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio hold a press conference in the James S. Brady Press Briefing Room of the White House in Washington, D.C., on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. (Sipa USA via Reuters Connect).

Sources indicate that "On the Republican side, you know, you could have — there are quite a few people that could be very good. Obviously, JD's vice president, Marco is right up there. The two of them are doing fantastically," Trump added. "They're both very, very popular. But you could have some surprises. We have some very capable people.".

In addition to Vance and Rubio, other Republicans whose names have been mentioned as possible 2028 candidates include Sen. Ted Cruz of Texas, Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis, former Vice President Mike Pence, former Georgia Gov. Brian Kemp and former Virginia Gov. Glenn Youngkin, among others.

When Trump was asked by McEnany if he thinks it "should be an open primary with lots of robust debates, or there should be some sort of unity ticket that the party coalesces around," the president noted, "could be either.".

"There could be some great unity tickets. I'll tell you what, the unity tickets are fantastic. You save all that money, you use it against the Democrats. I think it could be either," Trump added. "It's going to be a very interesting political season, and it's now just about, in another few weeks, it's going to be starting hot and heavy.".
