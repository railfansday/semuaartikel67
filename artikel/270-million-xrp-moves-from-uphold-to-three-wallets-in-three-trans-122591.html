XRP has printed a substantial exchange outflow, with 270 million XRP moving out of Uphold in 24 hours. That's three transfers of 90 million XRP each. The transfers raise more questions about what large holders are doing while XRP struggles below $1.

According to recent reports, the three transfers went to rP2Sxx…jzmV, rhDoUm…LVNk and rKB4EP…YUs3. In total, 78 transfers crossed the 1 million XRP threshold, moving 523 million XRP. Of the transfers tracked, 31 worth a combined 75.8 million XRP were movements between wallets belonging to the same owners. Taking those out gives us a clearer picture of transfers to outside wallets.

In a recent development, more XRP was removed from known exchange wallets than added to them. Outflows reached 456.2 million XRP, while inflows sat at 181.7 million XRP. Uphold led with a net outflow of 269.1 million XRP. Robinhood saw the largest net inflow at 13.5 million XRP.

Inflows and outflows can reflect usual trading and investment activities, including investors moving funds into self-custody. It's important to keep in mind that if those tokens stay off exchanges, there could be less XRP readily available for sale. It's not clear whether there's any accumulation here. The next thing to watch is whether the funds stay in those wallets or move back to an exchange.

XRP was trading at $0.9376, up 1.15% over 24 hours. It traded as low as $0.9124 and as high as $1.024 in that window. XRP is still below all the major moving averages on the daily chart. The downtrend that took hold in November is still in place. RSI is borderline oversold, so selling pressure might be fading. A sustained recovery is yet to come.

On November 22, the XRP Ledger recorded 2.8 million transactions and 3,833 newly introduced wallets. Payment volume reached 997.2 million XRP, while active accounts sat at 41,800. The network activity is still non-zero, though the transaction count is declining. The performance of XRP leaves a lot to be desired. The confirmation of a market recovery would only come if we witness stronger buying in the foreseeable future.
