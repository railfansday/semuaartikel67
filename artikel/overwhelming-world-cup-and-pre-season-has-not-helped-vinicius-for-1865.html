Real Madrid star Vinicius has yet to hit his best form this season, contributing to four goals (one goal, three assists) in his 12 appearances so far.

Vinicius was a high-profile transfer target for Arsenal in the summer, as he delayed signing a latest contract at Madrid.

The winger eventually penned a long-term deal with Los Blancos, though his performance levels have not been up to par.

Indeed, he has been suspended for four games after a sending-off against Villarreal, with Yan Diomande coming in to impress in Vinicius' absence.

However, Vinicius feels his busy summer, which included a last-16 exit for Brazil at the World Cup, is at least partly responsible for his dip in form.
