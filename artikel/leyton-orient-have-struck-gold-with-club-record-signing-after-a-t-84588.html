Leyton Orient have struck gold with club-record signing after a tough summer task: Opinion

Departed Leyton Orient favourite Dom Ballard notched 23 goals in 40 appearances as he deservedly picked up the League One Player of the Season award – even more impressive given Richie Wellens’ side only narrowly avoided the drop.

Ballard was the driving force behind avoiding relegation to League Two. The influential striker signed for Bristol City after just one season in East London, but his replacement is filling the void impressively.

Fresh from a superb campaign which yielded 24 goals in all competitions, Kabia flourished at Grimsby Town after arriving from non-league Truro City in the summer of 2025.

His arrival at Brisbane Road was initially met with scepticism following a slow start. But, patience in the 26-year-old frontman has paid off, netting on five occasions and registering three assists in the league.

Kabia’s rise to prominence has been a gradual one, with a prolific campaign at Truro City during the 2024/25 season finally kickstarting his career after spells in Scotland and Ireland.

He wasn’t expected to be an instant hit when he signed for Grimsby Town last summer as he made the two-division jump, but the Irish-born striker soon established himself as the go-to man up top.

Netting crucial goals in their run to the play-off semi-finals, Kabia’s explosive pace in behind and work out of possession for David Artell’s side made him a constant handful with his composure in front of goal another string to his bow.

Leyton Orient meanwhile were enduring a tough campaign, with Ballard one of their only shining lights as they survived relegation by the skin of their teeth. A big-money move felt inevitable after his prolific campaign in a struggling side, and Kabia was swiftly sounded out as an ideal replacement.

In a recent development, given Ballard’s exploits last term, it was hoped Kabia would hit the ground running. But, a solitary goal in his first six appearances made him an early target for scrutiny. Four goals in as many matches at the turn of September turned doubters into believers, but it is his off-the-ball stats which have been particularly eye-catching.

Averaging 33 defensive contributions and 16 tackles per 90 (FotMob), Kabia ranks as one of the best strikers in the third tier for his defensive work which is mandatory in Richie Wellens’ defensively oriented system.

Leyton Orient were never going to be able to attract a player of Ballard’s calibre to replace him, but Kabia brings bags of potential and gives the O’s a different dimension.

His predecessor was more of a poacher who was always in the right place at the right time, while Kabia is more direct and thrives by making runs in behind and using his stature to dominate aerially.

Wellens has also experimented with Kabia in a deeper, attacking midfield position but he has particularly thrived when the O’s have deployed three at the back with creative presences playing directly behind him.

According to recent reports, ultimately though, Kabia will be judged come the end of the season by his goal-scoring return. He may not be close to equaling Ballard’s remarkable return yet with six goals and three assists in 18 games across all competitions, his influence extends far beyond finding the back of the net.
