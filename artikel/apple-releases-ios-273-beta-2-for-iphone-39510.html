Recent findings suggest that apple has released the second iOS 27.3 developer beta for iPhone. The upcoming software update includes the redesigned Health app, new Safari features, expanded Siri AI capabilities, and more.

The second beta follows the first developer release on November 3 and first public beta on November 5.

One of the biggest changes introduced in iOS 27.3 so far is the redesigned Health app, which briefly appeared during iOS 27.2 testing before being removed ahead of release.

Sources indicate that on supported iPhones, Health’s newly introduced Insights tab brings together trends from sleep, activity, and more. A new Longevity tab includes Health Age, which uses Apple Watch data to compare your health metrics with what’s typical for your age.
