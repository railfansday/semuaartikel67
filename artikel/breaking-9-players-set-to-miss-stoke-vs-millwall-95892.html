Stoke City could have the chance to go to the top of the Championship on Wednesday night, depending on how results on Tuesday go, when they host Millwall and former boss Alex Neil at the bet365 Stadium.

Stoke have continued their scintillating run of form under Mark Robins with a 1-0 home victory over Birmingham City at the weekend, leapfrogging the Blues in the Championship table, and now sit only behind Middlesbrough on goal difference as things stand.

Depending on how Boro get on against Swansea City on Tuesday night, Stoke could go to the top of the league with another three points in this fixture, though returning boss Neil is bound to make it as difficult as possible for the Potters.

Millwall haven't been at their best as of late, with just one win in their last seven league games, but with just two defeats in their last 16 games against Stoke, they have more often than not had the upper hand in this encounter in recent memory.

For Stoke, Djibril Soumare and George Hirst picked up minor knocks against Birmingham and will need to be assessed; Ethan Galbraith could come back into the side after illness, whilst Millwall wait on decisions on Luke Cundle, Josh Coburn, and Romain Esse, as Football League World takes a look at each player set to miss Wednesday's clash in the Potteries.

French striker Lamine Cisse has shown sparks of his talent this campaign, particularly in the games against Bolton Wanderers and Watford, but has been out of action since being forced off in Stoke's goalless draw with Bristol City a month ago.

It has since been confirmed that the 23-year-old will likely be out of action until Christmas, providing George Hirst, Robert Bozenik, and Sam Gallagher with chances to impress Robins up top.

Veteran full-back Aaron Cresswell has yet to make an appearance this season after extending his stay in North Staffordshire for another year over the summer, having picked up an injury during pre-season.

Robins confirmed that the 36-year-old was back on the grass training last month, but he still remains some way short of fitness, and isn't expected to make a return to the pitch just yet.

Luca Bombino has found minutes at left wing-back recently following an injury to Eric Bocat and impressed, with the American looking to make his mark in the Championship following his potential $5 million (£3.7 million) move from MLS side San Diego late in the summer window.

After picking up a yellow card in the first half against Birmingham, Bombino mistimed his challenge around the hour mark on Patrick Roberts, receiving his marching orders, and as a result, will be suspended for this game, as Bocat or Justin Devenny will likely take his place on the left flank for the Potters against Millwall.

Ato Ampah has endured a torrid time with injuries ever since joining Stoke from Chelsea's academy for a potential £3 million in January 2026, with the young winger still yet to make a senior appearance for the Potters nearly a year later.

The England youth international was on the cusp of a first-team return in October before a further injury setback means he will have to wait even longer for his Potters debut, as the youngster will be praying 2027 is his year.

Onto Millwall now, as left-back Zak Sturge is set to miss the trip to North Staffordshire after receiving an early red card in his side's narrow 1-0 home defeat to Queens Park Rangers at the weekend.

This means Alfie Doughty could come back into the fold to face his former side on Wednesday, or Jordi Osei-Tutu could be preferred in that role.

Mathis Servais joined Millwall in the summer from Belgian outfit KV Mechelen to much excitement, but with just nine minutes of Championship football to shout about nearly six months later, it hasn't exactly been the dream start to life at The Den he would've hoped for.

A hamstring injury in pre-season forced him to wait to make his debut, and another injury sustained in the following international break has kept him out of action since, and it doesn't look like he will be ready for this one.

Arguably the biggest absence for Millwall is French defender Tristan Crama, who, after an incredible full debut year for the Lions, has struggled with injury woes this campaign.

The 25-year-old has been limited to just five appearances so far this season, injuring both of his ankles, and has been absent from the side since Halloween's 4-1 victory over Derby County, where he was forced to come off just after the hour mark.

Filip Marschall joined Millwall in the summer from League One Stevenage to provide an alternative option between the sticks to Lukas Jensen and Max Crocombe, but after four appearances, he suffered ankle ligament damage against Lincoln City.

Jensen has since returned to the side and is expected to start between the sticks as the former Aston Villa youth player recovers.

Ryan Leonard, like Crama, has been missing from Millwall's squad since the 4-1 home victory over Derby, and Neil issued an patch on the 34-year-old in early November.

Neil stated: "About eight days ago, Ryan Leonard contacted his knee on the left-hand side at our place. It’s been sore since, but it’s getting to the stage where the pain’s not dying down. We got it scanned, and unfortunately, Lenny’s got an glitch just above his knee. He won’t be back within the next month.".
