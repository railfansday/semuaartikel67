Recent findings suggest that it’s no secret that electric cars are more affordable to maintain than their gasoline-powered counterparts. While they may have a higher purchase price than a comparable gas-powered vehicle, EVs can save owners money over time by eliminating the need for regular engine oil changes, emissions tests, and other traditional maintenance tasks. Affordable electric vehicles are also becoming increasingly common, with even more options expected to arrive in the coming years.

In a recent development, however, that doesn't mean electric cars are completely maintenance-free. Like any other vehicle, EVs require regular care and attention to remain reliable and perform at their best for many years.

Although they don't have conventional combustion engines or traditional automatic transmissions, electric cars still feature components that require periodic maintenance. These include reduction gearboxes, brake fluid, brake pads, coolant systems, and tires among other things. Keeping these components in good condition is essential to ensuring an EV's long-term reliability.
