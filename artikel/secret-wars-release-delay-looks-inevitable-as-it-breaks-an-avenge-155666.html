Recent findings suggest that avengers: Secret Wars is currently set for release in 2027, only one year after Avengers: Doomsday, but the movie being pushed back is beginning to look more and more likely. Avengers: Secret Wars was originally set for release in November 2025, but delays and creative changes have since shuffled release dates, setting the franchise up for back-to-back December releases.

Industry observers note that while originally set to follow Avengers: The Kang Dynasty, with a focus on Jonathan Majors' multiversal villain who menaced Scott Lanf and firm in Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania, Secret Wars is now being set up with Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday. Bringing Robert Downey Jr. back to the franchise as the iconic villain is a compelling concept that will be given its first major examination in the upcoming Doomsday.

Industry observers note that secret Wars will shift genres for the MCU next year, and go a long way in setting up the universe's future. With all kinds of compelling films set to follow in 2028, there appear to be enormous intends for the future of the MCU, with new characters set to be introduced as well as a highly anticipated Black Panther sequel.

It has been revealed that to get there, however, Marvel will need to provide one of its most thrilling and memorable experiences yet with the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars. Considering the history of the franchise to date, there is one issue with the movie that Marvel may not be able to solve before its planned release date on December 17, 2027.

In a recent development, avengers: Secret Wars is still on the calendar for December 2027, but it has not yet begun principal photography. Following Doomsday's production, which shot from April to September 2025, its cast only reunited for reshoots throughout 2025 and 2026, laying the groundwork for the sequel's success.

This contrast makes Secret Wars' production that much more worrying. With only 13 months until it is set to debut in cinemas, Secret Wars has not begun filming on its enormous, high-stakes story, which is expected to see the stories of characters from multiple different universes put together in Battleworld.

While some movies are able to come together rapidly, the extensive size and scale of Secret Wars has called the release of the upcoming film into question. Especially considering the trajectory of the franchise to date, there are a few valid concerns to address concerning Secret Wars and its planned release date.

Avengers: Secret Wars is at the moment rumored to begin filming in January 2027, and if this occurs, it will have the smallest gap between production start and theatrical release of any installment in the Avengers franchise to date. The first Avengers currently holds this record with 13 months, beginning production in April 2011 for a May 2012 release.

Avengers: Age of Ultron extended this window slightly, beginning filming in February 2014 for a May 2015 wide theatrical release.

Avengers: Infinity War had a similar gap, filming in January 2017 for a theatrical release in late April, while Avengers: Endgame extended the production time significantly, beginning its production in August 2017 for release in April 2019.

Almost every Marvel movie has begun filming more than a year before release. With Secret Wars looking likely to begin photography in January, and setting a new record for the shortest time between production and release, this has naturally led to concerns about the film being done in time for its release date.

Sources indicate that avengers: Secret Wars is sure to be one of the biggest movies of 2027, packed full of all kinds of characters in all sorts of thrilling environments. A project like that takes time, and the thought of turning a satisfying film around in such a tight window is concerning.

Most significant-budget movies take a long time in post-production. Once movies are shot, they are put through a rigorous process of editing, sound design, visual effects, and rendering. These efforts are time-consuming, especially in the case of visual spectacles like the Avengers movies, which require a staggering amount of effects.

Sources indicate that beyond even the Avengers films, looking at 2026's biggest hits highlights this difficulty for Secret Wars. Project Hail Mary began filming in 2024 for its 2026 release, as did Michael. This long runway for production gives the filmmakers room to ensure the final film lives up to expectations.

Industry observers note that with all of this considered, Secret Wars is supposed to be the biggest Avengers entry yet. Giving it the least amount of time to be executed to its fullest potential is worrying, but the prospect of delaying it might be an even greater difficulty for Marvel.

Industry observers note that marvel has cleared the calendar for its Avengers films, which between Doomsday and Secret Wars, promise to reshape the entire universe. Still, there are events that are planned to come afterward, with three enormous releases set for 2028, beginning with Marvel's new reboot of the X-Men in May.

If Secret Wars is delayed, this will not only mean that the franchise will not have any films released during 2027's calendar year, but it will also likely have to push back Marvel's enormous slate. While delaying Secret Wars to February could mitigate this difficulty, that release window is not nearly as historically successful as December or summer.

Sources indicate that if Secret Wars is delayed, it will more likely be pushed back to May. If this is the case, a whole cascade of changes will likely follow. X-Men will certainly need to be pushed back from its May releaseand will likely take Ghost Rider's July slot. From there, Ghost Rider could likely find a suitable spot in the fall, allowing Black Panther 3 to still release that December.

According to recent reports, still, releasing four films in a year is a lot to ask of audiences who have already shown fatigue in some of Marvel's most jam-packed years. The ideal situation for all would be for Secret Wars to retain its release date. And while this looks increasingly less assured, it is still possible.

With all of this considered, it is still possible for Secret Wars to make its release date. If the film begins shooting in January, scheduling would be tight, but it is notable that this year's box office juggernaut Spider-Man: Brand Fresh Day had a similar gap. That film began production in early August 2025 for a late July 2026 release.

In addition, much of Secret Wars has likely already been done behind the scenes. The next Multiversal Saga film will largely be put together digitally, with many of the environments and characters rendered in visual effects throughout some of the film's action-packed events.

Filming is important, but it is also essential to note that Joe and Anthony Russo have been working on Doomsday and Secret Wars concurrently since 2024. Deep development and key visuals on both projects have been in process since that time, and certain scenes for Secret Wars could even have been captured during Doomsday's production.

Sources indicate that yes, this tight window for filming and release is a reason for concern. However, few filmmakers know how to navigate the machinations of the Marvel Cinematic Universe as well as the Russos do. Until Marvel indicates otherwise, Avengers: Secret Wars is still set for release in December 2027.
