📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Get Peacock Premium at $12.99 a month if you follow the Premier League or the NBA, since it streams about 175 matches a season and the Monday NBA games that NBC doesn’t broadcast. For NBC and Bravo highlights alone, the $8.99 Select blueprint carries most of them, and NBC’s own broadcasts are free with an antenna.

According to recent reports, peacock is NBCUniversal’s streaming service, carrying NBC and Bravo series, Universal and DreamWorks movies, Peacock originals such as The Traitors, and the live games NBC Sports covers. It’s built for sports fans and for viewers who follow NBC’s and Bravo’s shows.

Industry observers note that premium is worth $12.99 a month to Premier League and NBA fans, who get the matches and Monday games NBC doesn’t broadcast, while NBC and Bravo viewers who skip sports pay $8.99 for Select. NBC’s own broadcasts, Sunday Night Football included, are free with an antenna that picks up your local NBC station, and Instacart+ and Walmart+ members can get Premium at no extra cost.
