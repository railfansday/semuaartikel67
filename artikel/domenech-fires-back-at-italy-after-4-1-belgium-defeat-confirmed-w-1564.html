Raymond Domenech has fired back at the Italy national team, suggesting that the Azzurri ‘confirmed what we thought of them’ with their 4-1 defeat away against Belgium in the Nations League on Sunday evening.

A largely encouraging Nations League campaign ended in disappointment for Italy, who had started the weekend at the top of their group, but finished in third and missed out on a spot in the quarter-finals after a 4-1 defeat against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday night. Pio Esposito, Maxim De Cuyper, Nathan Ngoy, Jeremie Doku and Diego Moreira provided the goals.

The Italian press has responded with mixed verdicts, although many feel there is still room for optimism given the way that the Azzurri started the calendar year: From missing out on World Cup qualification to picking up 10 points from their last five matches, four of which came against the third-highest-ranked team in the world, France, and with a core of youngsters at the heart of Roberto Mancini’s project.

Old rivalries die hard, however, and former France head coach Domenech has not looked too kindly upon Italy during the November international break.

Shortly after Pio Esposito had scored to equalise in Italy’s eventual 3-1 victory over France, Domenech took to social media and posted the following message: “It’s beyond the Italians’ wildest dreams. So weak in every department, but what’s most surprising is their technical level. So many mistakes from easy passes, it’s alarming.”.

Domenech was widely mocked by jubilant Azzurri supporters in the comments after full-time, pointing to the full-time score, and of course, the result of the 2006 World Cup final, which Domenech lost against Marcello Lippi’s Italy.

Domenech has since fired back, however, following Italy’s 4-1 defeat against Belgium, which ended their Nations League campaign at the group stage.

According to recent reports, “France qualify as expected. Belgium crush the Italians, who confirmed what we thought of them,” he wrote on Sunday night.
