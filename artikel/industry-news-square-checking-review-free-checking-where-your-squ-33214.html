📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Open Square Checking if you take card payments with Square, since your sales are usually available within 15 minutes and it has no monthly fee. Add Square Savings to set aside a share of every card sale for taxes. If clients mostly pay by bank transfer, pick an account that sets aside part of every deposit.

According to recent reports, sutton Bank provides Square Checking, a business account you open and use in Square’s apps. Without it, a free payout of your sales to an outside bank takes until the next business day, while an instant one costs 1.95%, which works out to $19.50 on a $1,000 day of sales.

According to recent reports, sole proprietors who take most of their payments through Square should open it, since it costs $0 a month and usually makes each card sale spendable within minutes. Square’s automatic set-aside works only on card sales, so freelancers paid mostly by bank transfer do better with Novo Business Checking, which can route a share of every deposit to taxes for no monthly fee.
