It has been revealed that it was a big week for tech startup exits, headlined by Anthropic pulling off its massive IPO. On the down side, however, tech stocks took a huge dive in the past few days. Should that negative sentiment and downward pressure persist, we can expect it to spill over into private markets.

Sources indicate that for this past week, however, U.S. startups continued to secure sizable rounds, led by brain-computer interface organization Science landing a fresh $400 million. Another seven American companies revealed venture or growth funding of $100 million or more, and across the Atlantic, UK-based AI biotech startup Isomorphic Labs landed $5 billion, the largest financing of the week.

1. Science, $400M, neurotech: Science, a startup focused on brain-computer interface technology, and neural engineering, secured $400 million in Series D funding. The round brings total funding to date for the Alameda, California-based company to more than $900 million.

Recent findings suggest that 2. Model ML, $250M, AI and fintech: Model ML, an AI platform for financial and professional services, closed on $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. General Atlantic and QED Investors led the round for the three-year-old company, which is based in Upcoming York and London.

3. Galvanize Therapeutics, $130M, biotech: Redwood City, California-based Galvanize Therapeutics, a developer of pulsed electric field ablation technologies for oncology and chronic disease, picked up $130 million in Series D funding led by HealthQuest Capital.

Industry observers note that 4. General Flash, $128M, critical metals: General Flash, a developer of a new method to produce critical metals, revealed that it has raised $128 million across seed and Series A rounds from a long list of investors.

In a recent development, 5. Ramon.Space, $124M, space tech: Denver-based Ramon.Space, a developer of space computing infrastructure, pulled in $124M in Series B funding led by PSG.

6. Marrow Therapeutics, $120M, biotech: Watertown, Massachusetts-based Marrow Therapeutics, a developer of bone marrow-targeted therapies to treat severe blood disorders, confirmed its launch with a combined $173 million in Series A and B funding. Orbimed led its $120 million Series B round while Scion Life Sciences and venBio led the $53 million Series A.

Sources indicate that 7. Noeri, $110M, enterprise AI: Noeri, a developer of self-improving AI for enterprises and researchers, launched with $110 million in initial funding. Menlo Ventures led the round for the Palo Alto-based startup, which was founded by Stanford University computer science professors.

Recent findings suggest that 8. Seawolf Therapeutics, $100M, biotech: San Diego-based Seawolf Therapeutics, a biotech startup developing a novel DNA delivery platform, emerged from stealth and declared that it has raised $100 million in funding to date, with Versant Ventures, MPM BioImpact and Novartis Venture Fund as lead investors.

9. BrightAI, $97M, physical AI: BrightAI, a startup developing a sensor, drone and robotics-driven platform to bring continuous observability to infrastructure-heavy industries like power, water, and energy, secured $97 million in latest funding. Smith Point Capital led the financing for the Silicon Valley-based organization.

Recent findings suggest that 10. Vincue, $93M, vehicle intelligence platform: Kansas City-based Vincue, developer of a vehicle lifecycle intelligence platform, closed on $93 million in growth capital led by Wavecrest Growth Partners.

Sources indicate that we tracked the largest confirmed rounds in the Crunchbase database that were raised by U.S.-based companies for the period of Nov. 14-20, 2026. Although most announced rounds are in the database, there may be a small time lag, as some rounds are reported late in the week.
