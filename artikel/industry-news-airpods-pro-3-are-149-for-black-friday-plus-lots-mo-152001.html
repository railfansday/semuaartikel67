The Apple AirPods Pro 3 are on sale for $149, which is insane. That's $100 off their $249 list price, and we've never seen them drop this low before, not even during Prime Day.

These are the buds to get if you want the best noise cancellation Apple has to offer. Our reviewer called the ANC "outstanding" and gave them a 4.75 out of 5 overall. You also get excellent battery life, seamless iPhone pairing, and a built-in heart rate sensor for tracking workouts. They aren't perfect: there's no custom EQ, and transparency mode took a small step back from the AirPods Pro 2. But at this price, those are small trade-offs.
