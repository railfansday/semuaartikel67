📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

TAP Air Portugal will launch a newly introduced route to Washington, D.C., next year.

Starting March 30, 2027, the carrier will connect Porto and Washington Dulles. Flights will operate three times weekly.

In a recent development, currently, TAP serves Dulles from its hub at Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisbon. Its only U.S.-bound routes from Porto are to Boston and Newark, Fresh Jersey.

TAP officials said the new connection will strengthen the airline’s presence in northern Portugal while expanding access to a vital foreign market.

TAP serves Porto via Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport.

The carrier serves nine destinations in the U.S., the others being Upcoming York-JFK, Chicago O’Hare, Orlando, Florida, Miami, Los Angeles, and San Francisco.
