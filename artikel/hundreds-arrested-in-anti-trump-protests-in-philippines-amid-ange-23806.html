Hundreds arrested in anti-Trump protests in Philippines amid anger over Iran war and AI pact

Asean and Apec summits – as it happened.

Hundreds of people have been arrested in heated demonstrations against Donald Trump in Manila, as the US president arrived in the city for a leaders’ summit.

Organisers claimed as many as 10,000 people would be joining protests against Trump in the capital of the Philippines on Tuesday, where he is attending the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (Asean) summit. The crowds were expected to try to march to the summit site.

Authorities have arrested nearly 300 people in widespread protests held in the days leading up to the summit, with large parts of the capital closed off and tight security in place.

In a recent development, some burned effigies of Trump and faced off with police officers armed with batons and shields. Slogans on placards called the American leader the world’s “No 1 terrorist” in protests held on Monday.

Demonstrators angry at the cost of living and energy shock caused by the Iran war, and the new US-led Pax Silica initiative on AI and critical minerals, tried to reach the summit at the Philippine International Convention Center on Monday, but were blocked by police.

Pax Silica has sparked tensions in the Philippines over plans to build a tech hub outside Manila that critics say will affect water and energy supplies, and displace members of the Ayta Indigenous community.

Large sections of central Manila were closed to the public as part of the security cordon and public holidays put in place for the Asean summit.

After departing from Fairbanks in Alaska, Trump emerged from Air Force One at Ninoy Aquino International airport at 5.54pm local time (0954 GMT), live video demonstrated.

Industry observers note that trump will meet with the leaders of Australia, Japan and India on Tuesday night before heading to Shenzhen in China on Wednesday. There he will attend the Asia Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) summit and hold trilateral talks with China’s president, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin of Russia.

The backlash against Trump follows the ousting of the boss of Nvidia, Jensen Huang, and allegations members of the US administration, including the commerce secretary, Howard Lutnick, knew about his affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

Trump’s delegation was expected to include Elon Musk.

Protest leader Renato Reyes blamed the arrests in Manila on the heavy handed response of the government on the president, Ferdinand Marcos Jr, calling Monday the largest single day of police arrests since Marcos came to office in 2022.

According to recent reports, “It is really crazy that the Philippine government is rolling out the red carpet for someone who has been so flatly rejected by the American people,” Reyes mentioned. “Trump started a war that devastated the global economy and pushed oil prices in the Philippines to historic highs. It has caused untold suffering on the Filipino people.

“So, yes, Trump will have to pay for his crimes and he will have to be answerable, not just to the American people, but to the people of the world.”.

The Philippine national police mentioned some officers were injured during confrontations with demonstrators opposed to the summit, including when objects were allegedly thrown at officers.

Pax Silica will include a technology hub planned at New Clark City in Tarlac, about 100km from Manila.

Intended to bolster western stocks of AI technology and reduce “excessive dependencies” on single source supply chains, including from China, Pax Silica will provide chips and critical minerals to 26 participating signatory governments.

Amnesty International stated demonstrations should not be forcibly or violently dispersed.

Sources indicate that “Reports of arrests during the protests demand immediate scrutiny,” Amnesty’s director for the Philippines, Ritz Lee Santos III, stated. “Authorities must explain the specific grounds for each arrest and any continued detention. Anyone detained solely for peacefully exercising their rights must be released immediately.”.
