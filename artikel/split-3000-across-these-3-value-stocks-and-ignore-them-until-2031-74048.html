Value stocks have dominated growth stocks over the past year, an about-face from the previous three years of this market.

The Vanguard Morningstar Value ETF (VTV -1.62%) is up 17% over the past 12 months, while its large-cap counterpart, the Vanguard Morningstar Growth ETF (VUG -5.32%) is up just 1%.

And Vanguard strategists are not alone in thinking that value stocks will outperform growth stocks over the next 10 years, due to several factors, including overvalued large-cap growth stocks and the broadening out of AI, among other reasons.

If you are looking for value stocks to set and forget for the next five years, consider these three.

Target (TGT -0.97%) has had a resurgence over the past year or so after years of underperformance. The big-box retail stock has returned a staggering 71% year to date and 89% over the past year. At the same time, it is still pretty cheap, trading at 18 times forward earnings after being beaten down for several years.

The turnaround has been fueled by its upcoming CEO, Michael Fiddelke, who has boosted revenue and traffic through a combination of price cuts, increased digital sales, improved customer experience and inventory management, increased membership, and digital advertising through its Roundel business. He has been able to increase the gross margin, despite cutting prices, by streamlining and leaning into higher-margin growth areas.

Target also benefited from a low valuation and favorable year-over-year comps.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

The comps won't be as favorable next year, and the valuation won’t be as low, but Target has been able to reset to better compete with Walmart (WMT +1.51%) and Amazon (AMZN -4.10%) while leaning into its strengths and not trying to be everything to everyone.

It also has one of the best, most reliable dividends, which it has increased for 54 straight years.

Dollar General (DG -2.23%) is another retail stock that has embarked on a turnaround plan.

It introduced two store-remodeling plans in recent years: Project Renovate and Project Elevate. The blueprint is to overhaul or update some 4,250 stores this year, and those projects are expected to be complete by the end of fiscal 2026.

Dollar General has poured some $1.4 billion to $1.5 billion in capital expenditures into these and other projects this year, and about $1.2 billion in 2025.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Industry observers note that but it is already starting to reap the benefits. In the second quarter, net sales rose 5.2%, same-store sales increased 3.5%, operating profit rose 29%, and earnings jumped 33% to $2.48 per share. When all remodels are finished, Dollar General anticipates a 6% sales boost from fully remodeled stores and a 3% increase from upgraded stores.

Along with these renovations, Dollar General has taken steps to improve inventory management and focus on higher-margin sales items by eliminating much of the inventory that either wasn't selling or had lower margins.

It positions Dollar General to defend its stake as the premier deep-discount retailer. The stock is pretty cheap with a P/E ratio of 17, a forward P/E of 16, and a five-year PEG ratio of 0.41, which indicates that it is a long-term value relative to its earnings expectations.

Recent findings suggest that micron (MU -13.89%) is one of the most explosive growth stocks on the market, yet it is trading in value territory, so it is basically a value stock. Shares are up 141% year to date and 200% over the past 12 months as the high bandwidth memory AI chipmaker is one of three major players in its booming industry.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Micron is at the center of a memory and storage supercycle in which the supply of memory chips for AI computing cannot keep up with demand. This has given Micron incredible pricing power and a massive backlog with more than 75% of the firm's fiscal 2027 shipments already under contract. It is already booking out into fiscal 2028. Chief Operating Officer Manish Bhatia mentioned on the Q4 earnings call that Micron is seeing "stronger demand drivers than we've seen before" for 2027 and 2028.

It also has long-term strategic customer agreements with 26 customers that will account for more than 35% of the capacity Micron will supply through 2030 — and those agreements are likely to continue to increase over time.

The expected growth is outpacing price projections, as Micron stock is trading at just 10 times earnings and 4 times forward earnings. Further, it has a PEG ratio of just 0.15. It is the best value of these three stocks right now.

All three of these stocks are cheap, with growth catalysts that should make them stocks to buy and hold for the next several years.
