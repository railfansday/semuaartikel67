📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

An upsetting revelation about Grey’s Anatomy season 23 follows its spinoff’s already discouraging delay. Returning for a record 23rd season as part of ABC’s Fall 2026 lineup, Grey’s Anatomy was supposed to be replaced by its Texas-based spinoff during midseason, as the TV series would have had to take over Grey’s Anatomy’s Thursday night timeslot. However, massive delays made such a plan unfeasible, pushing back the Grey’s Anatomy spinoff from a 2026-2027 premiere to a 2027-2028 one. ABC nevertheless revealed even more dismal news regarding the long-running medical drama.

It has been revealed that grey’s Anatomy’s third spinoff is set to be based in rural West Texas and follow doctors at a medical center in the last town for miles. Such a setting pushes the Grey’s Anatomy spinoff to be completely different from the parent series, not only for the setting, but also for the type of storylines and traumas that reach the medical center in question.

Every Grey’s Anatomy spinoff that ever existed had been filmed in Los Angeles, which prompted Shonda Rhimes and Meg Marinis’s fresh brainchild to attempt to secure a tax credit so that it could do the same. However, California refused to award Grey’s Anatomy’s spinoff a tax credit twice, resulting in the production having to be moved to Albuquerque, New Mexico.

In a recent development, this inevitably delayed production enough that the Grey’s Anatomy spinoff couldn’t debut during the 2026-2027 midseason. However, another enhancement regarding the popular medical drama is also upsetting, as Grey’s Anatomy season 23 will air its winter finale this week.

In a recent development, after airing only five episodes of Grey’s Anatomy season 23, the medical drama is set to have its winter finale on Thursday, November 19, 2026. It is the first ABC TV show to do so, accompanied only by 911: Nashville, as all the other TV series that were part of ABC’s Fall 2026 lineup are set to return after a small hiatus, before taking another one for the end-of-the-year holidays.

Grey’s Anatomy season 23 already having a winter finale date set up makes the current time particularly difficult for the medical drama, especially given that its Texas-based spinoff will be delayed for at least half a year if not more. Grey’s Anatomy season 23, episode 6’s trailer nevertheless promises an action-packed episode of medical chaos, hierarchical concerns, and personal ones too, as Richard Webber’s wish to move the patient overflow to the VIP floor seemingly puts him at odds with both Grey Sloan Memorial’s latest chief, Seth Norton, and his own wife, Catherine Fox.

While the news of Grey’s Anatomy season 23 already having a midseason finale is undoubtedly disappointing, its return has also been set up, and it’s the best possible outcome for the ABC medical drama. Unlike Grey’s Anatomy season 21, which had its midseason finale during the third week of November only to return in early March, season 23 will come back sooner.

According to recent reports, grey’s Anatomy season 23’s winter premiere is set for Thursday, January 7, 2027. This means the hiatus is only seven weeks long, which isn’t short per se, but it cannot be considered a long one either. Such a schedule mirrors Grey’s Anatomy season 22’s one from last year, when the ABC medical drama aired its midseason finale in mid-November only to return in January 2026.

Grey’s Anatomy season 23’s return schedule would have inevitably looked differently had its Texas-based spinoff begun production earlier. However, the disappointment of a longer break for the record-breaking show would have been offset by another medical drama coming from the same minds that made Grey’s Anatomy the compelling drama that it is.

With the Texas-based Grey’s Anatomy spinoff’s debut delayed, having Grey’s Anatomy return in early 2027 is the best possible news for fans of the medical drama, particularly considering how Grey’s Anatomy alternated its return date between January and March consistently in the past ten years.
