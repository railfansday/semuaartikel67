Angelina Jolie is ushering in a latest era after quitting Los Angeles.

In the wake of selling the $24.75 million California estate she snapped up less than a year after filing for divorce from Brad Pitt in 2017, the actress has revealed plans to enjoy more time overseas and less time tied down to a singular location.

Recent findings suggest that jolie recently visited Cambodia, where local outlets reported she met with religious leaders. The “Maleficent” star reportedly “paid her respects and presented religious offerings” to Samdech Preah Maha Sumeatheathibodi Am Limheng, supreme patriarch of the Mohanikay Buddhist order.

She also visited a traditional crafts village on Cambodia's Koh Dach, which is known as Silk Island, to observe local artisans.

The mom of six detailed her special connection to the country during earlier travels to the Philippines, where she spoke on a Nov. 11 panel alongside Cambodian filmmaker Rithy Panh.

It has been revealed that “I found this place and the people to be so warm and so open, and I couldn’t believe their spirits were so strong after all they have suffered,” Jolie stated of Cambodia. “You go to certain places, and you just feel at home.”.

According to People magazine, the actress aims to expand her creative collective, Atelier Jolie, with a residency in Cambodia in 2017.

The effort will be a partnership between Panh’s Bophana Center and the Maddox Foundation, which Jolie founded in 2003 and named for her eldest child, Maddox Jolie, whom she adopted from Battambang, Cambodia, in 2002.

According to the Atelier Jolie website, the business is focused on “the discovery of art, craft and design work from cultures around the world.” She founded the certified B Corporation business in 2023, with a headquarters in Manhattan’s NoHo neighborhood, which serves as a retail store, art space, and cultural center.

Jolie spoke about her plans for the future in a September 2026 interview with L’Officiel, noting that she plans to spend more time “out of the States.”.

Recent findings suggest that “The hope for the atelier was always to be global,” she remarked. “The idea would be to visit the different locations around the world and meet local artists and makers.”.

She noted a desire to expand the atelier to Brazil, as well as “get back to doing more hands-on work” with the Maddox Foundation in Cambodia.

She also expressed a desire to live a life that’s less tied down to one specific locale.

“I don’t really initiative to have a base,” Jolie admitted. “I’d like to have a home base for my family, a place where some of the mom stuff and the family photos are. And then I’d like to be a bit nomadic for a while.”.

The star also noted that she has a home in Cambodia, which “will also be a residency a few months out of the year for the Khmer culture.”.

Jolie’s appreciation for the Southeast Asian country began when she first visited to film the 2001 movie “Lara Croft: Tomb Raider.” She returned a year later to adopt her son and has since spent lots of time traveling back and forth with her family.

In 2017, she directed “First They Killed My Father,” a Khmer-language biographical thriller based on a memoir of the same name by Cambodian human rights activist Loung Ung.

In a People magazine interview, Jolie revealed Maddox—who was 15 at the time—was deeply involved in the creation of the film and helped with research.

Jole revealed her son spent “over four months” in the country at that time, “really reading, listening, learning, and absorbing all things about his culture and his country, including the very, very dark parts.”.

Although Jolie was born and raised in Los Angeles, she has long expressed her desire to leave the city.

“I grew up in this town. I am here because I have to be here from a divorce,” she told The Hollywood Reporter in 2024. She explained that once her youngest children—twins Knox Leon and Vivienne Marcheline—turned 18, she’d be “able to leave.”.

The milestone birthday passed this July, paving the way for a new chapter for Jolie.

“It’s the end of a certain time for me,” she told L’Officiel weeks later.

Jolie made moves quickly, putting her idyllic Los Angeles compound on the market for $29.9 million in May, seemingly in anticipation of Knox and Vivienne’s 18th.

The six-bedroom, 10-bathroom abode found a buyer in September, selling for $24.75 million. Jolie had originally purchased the home for $24.5 million in 2017, and it served as her primary residence for nearly a decade.

While Jolie aims to spend the bulk of her time jet-setting, she hasn’t stated goodbye to the United States for good, and has reportedly purchased a Connecticut mansion, according to Page Six Hollywood.
