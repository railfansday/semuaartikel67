Caring for life: this was the motto of the XIV edition of the Ibero-American Architecture Biennial (BIAU) held in Brasília from November 3 to 6, 2026. The choice of care as a theme is significant, as is its presentation as an infinitive verb—lacking tense or mood—serving as an urgent call to action. Structured around the thematic axes of water, vegetation, housing, communities, and upcoming territories, the discussions made it clear that these elements are not mere resources or means for caring for life. Rather, they must be understood as subjects of care, demanding an attentive and respectful consideration of their natural and social cycles within architectural practice.
