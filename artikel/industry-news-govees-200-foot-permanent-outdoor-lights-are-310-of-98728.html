A smart power strip that needs no hub.

Industry observers note that the Kasa Smart Plug Power Strip HS300 is $37.99, down from $49.99, and it turns one wall outlet into six smart ones. Each outlet can be controlled on its own, there are three USB ports for chargers, and it works with Alexa and Google Home without a hub.

200 feet of permanent RGBIC lights for under $450.

Hanging holiday lights every November gets old. The Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights Pro, 200 feet stay up all year, with 120 RGBIC lights rated IP67 for weather and 75 preset scenes. They work with Alexa, Google Assistant and Matter, and they’re $449.99 against a list price of $759.99.

A Wi-Fi smart lock for under $240.

The Oil Rubbed Bronze Yale Assure Lock 2 touchscreen deadbolt with Wi-Fi is $237.97 (list price $279.99). It connects over Wi-Fi so you can lock up from your phone, and Yale’s DoorSense tells the lock when the door is closed so it can relock on its own. Yale rates the exterior IPX5 for weather.
