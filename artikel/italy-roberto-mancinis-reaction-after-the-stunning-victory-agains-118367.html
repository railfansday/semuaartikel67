According to recent reports, this Thursday evening, the France team lost to Italy (3-1) in the Nations League. Despite Michael Olise’s opener, Zinédine Zidane’s men completely lost the thread of the match against an attractive Squadra Azzurra. At the microphone of TF1 after the match, Roberto Mancini savored this great victory against the Blues.

In a recent development, “It wasn’t easy to beat France, because they have an incredible team with incredible players. They are one of the best teams in the world. We made a mistake after the first ten minutes with this goal, we suffered for another ten minutes and then we started playing and we were pretty good. It’s important to win, we are a young team and we have to prove. It’s important, especially when you play like that, it gives you confidence.”explained the Italian coach.
