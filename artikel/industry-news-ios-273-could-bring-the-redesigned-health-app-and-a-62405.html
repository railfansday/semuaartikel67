Industry News: iOS 27.3 Could Bring the Redesigned Health App and a More Capable Siri AI to Your Device Soon

🌐 Source: Cnet • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 5 min read (1059 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Apple launched the second public beta of iOS 27.3 on Thursday, Nov.
  • Apple historically releases an iOS update in December, so I expect iOS 27.3 to land on iPhones later this year.
  • In a recent development, since this is a beta, I recommend downloading it on something other than your primary device.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Apple launched the second public beta of iOS 27.3 on Thursday, Nov. 19, more than two months after the launch of the iPhone 18 Pro lineup and iOS 27. The headliner here is the return of the fresh Health app: After briefly testing the redesigned Health app in an early iOS 27.2 beta, Apple has put the revamped app back in the recent beta cycle.

Apple historically releases an iOS update in December, so I expect iOS 27.3 to land on iPhones later this year.

In a recent development, since this is a beta, I recommend downloading it on something other than your primary device. This isn’t the final version of iOS 27.3, so the update might be buggy and battery life may be short, so it’s best to keep those troubles on a secondary device.

Also, since this isn’t the final version, there could be more features to land on your iPhone when Apple releases 27.3.

Here are some features developers and beta testers can try now in the second iOS 27.3 beta.

Industry observers note that the redesigned Health app returns in the iOS 27.3 beta after Apple tested the feature in the iOS 27.2 beta process. Developers and beta testers can explore the refreshed app and see things like a upcoming Longevity tab and your Health Age.

Apple wrote online that we can expect the redesigned Health app to land on devices later this year.

If developers and beta testers have an Apple Watch that is running the watchOS 27.3 beta as well as an iPhone running the iOS 27.3 beta, those folks can test out Apple’s Live Rewind and Siri Recap in Audio Intelligence.

Sources indicate that live Rewind allows you to pull up a text transcript of a conversation in the past 15 seconds on your Apple Watch, and Siri Recap can automatically give you a summary of meetings, conversations and other events. Live Rewind snippets are replaced by upcoming audio and then deleted, and Siri Recaps delete themselves after seven days if you don’t manually delete them sooner. However, you can also save both of these in the Siri app and export them to the Notes or Journal apps on your iPhone.

While both Live Rewind and Siri Recap are automatically deleted over time, I’m uncomfortable with the ability to save snippets or recaps, which raises concerns about people recording my conversations without my knowledge. Even if Audio Intelligence just summarizes what I mentioned, I still find both features creepy.

And while I recognize the utility for certain groups of people, I also think it’s better to just, y’know… talk to someone instead of asking your watch what they just noted.

Industry observers note that siri AI gets a huge update in the iOS 27.3 beta. The feature can now do more actions in supported apps, like Apple Music and Messages. For example, if you go into Apple Music, you can ask Siri to create a playlist for you, and in Messages, you can ask Siri to schedule, edit or unsend messages. If you open an app and ask Siri what it can do, the digital assistant should give you some examples of its capabilities.

Recent findings suggest that you can also use Siri in Safari to scan an entire webpage — even parts of the page that aren’t in your direct view — to give you helpful information. So if you are looking at a menu online, you can ask Siri to find the gluten-free or vegetarian options.

The beta also brings Siri AI to traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Turkish and Norwegian.

In the latest iOS 27.3 beta, developers and beta testers can find video podcasts in the Apple TV app, as well as in the Podcasts app. You can find video podcasts by going into the app, tapping Home across the bottom of your screen and scrolling down to the Video Podcasts section.

This means you can watch a video podcast in the Apple TV app on your iPhone while commuting, and then you can finish that video later on an Apple TV 4K or another device with access to Apple TV.

Each workout recommends exercises, reps, sets and weights in order to help you meet your goals. And if you’re unfamiliar with an exercise, you can also watch video tutorials to see how to perform that exercise.

Sources indicate that standBy mode also gets an upgrade in the iOS 27.3 beta. In the beta, you can add full weather and calendar slides to the mode and choose from more colors.

In a recent development, apple introduced StandBy mode alongside iOS 17, and it was one of my favorite iPhone features. I’m happy Apple is finally showing it some love and adding more features and capabilities so it feels more like a home or desk hub.

Industry observers note that if you’re a fan of dark mode, the iOS 27.3 beta has a treat for you. The beta gives you a upcoming setting in Safari called Adaptive Dark Mode. When enabled, it forces dark mode onto webpages in Safari that may not have a dark mode option otherwise.

Here’s where to find the option.

1. Tap Settings.2. Tap Apps.3. Tap Safari.4. Tap Adaptive Dark Mode.5. Tap On.

Recent findings suggest that if you have an iPhone Duo and are running the iOS 27.3 beta, you have fresh options to lock and unlock your device. In the beta, you can enable your device to lock simply by closing the Duo. You can find this option in Settings and within the Display & Brightness menu.

In a recent development, next, people with the Duo can unlock the device using that phone’s front camera and without opening the foldable. You will have had to recently unlocked your device with Touch ID or your passcode. You can find this option in Settings and in the Touch ID & Passcode menu.

Those are a few of the fresh features developers can try now with the second beta of iOS 27.3. Apple has not mentioned when exactly it will release iOS 27.3 to the general public, but we expect it to land on devices before the end of the year.
