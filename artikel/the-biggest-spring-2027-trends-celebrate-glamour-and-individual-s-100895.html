The Biggest Spring 2027 Trends Celebrate Glamour and Individual Style

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The breakout trends from the Spring 2027 demonstrates were all about personalization and styling, humanizing the process of getting dressed at a time when AI can generate nearly anything for you. From the clashing of statement pieces at Chanel, Dries Van Noten, and Valentino to the coming-out party of the bra at Dior and Altuzarra, the spring runways celebrated self-expression.

There was also a touch of glamour almost everywhere. Dressing for daytime took on a newly introduced sheen via the cummerbunds and opera pumps at Kallmeyer, the metallic gold at Saint Laurent, and the boas at Tory Burch. At Michael Rider’s Celine, utilitarian staples were reimagined in sophisticated silhouettes, while brands like C and Bode took classic straight-leg jeans and added embellishment in the form of sparkle and embroidery. This season was all about celebrating the joy of getting dressed, a principle we think should be applied year-round.

Recent findings suggest that and lest we forget, this was the season of the skirt—so much so that Miuccia Prada and Raf Simons sent 63 skirt looks down the Prada runway in Milan. Some were embellished, some had sheer details, and almost all of them fell right below the knee.

Now that fashion month has wrapped and we’ve had some time to digest what we saw on the runways, we’re rounding up our nine favorite trends from the spring 2027 collections. And because March is still months away, we’ve shopped out some ways to get a jump on the looks now.

It has been revealed that mixed-and-matched looks were all over the runways. What felt new here was the against-the-grain pattern play, which felt like a message about personal style—an exhortation from designers to stop worrying about following the rules and lean into whatever makes you feel good.

Recent findings suggest that this approach allows you to play with things that are already in your wardrobe. Invest in a sparkly skirt to pair with a checkered shirt you already own, or pair a new jacquard jacket with some animal-print boots already in your rotation. The key to this trend is making it your own.

Staud Samira Faux-Fur-Trimmed Jacket.

Bode Asymmetrical Sequin-Embellished Fringed Skirt.

Herbert Levine Two-Faced 80 Boot.

Stripped-back had a whole new meaning this season! Designers quite literally loosened up a few buttons, exposing delicate bras worn underneath shirting. (Granted, some looks like those at Prada and Tom Ford had just a bra as the top, but we’re going to save that one for warmer months.) What’s so appealing about this look is the contrast between the sexy underpinnings and the polished separates. There’s nothing like a little lace to spice things up, and we are here for it!

Citizens of Humanity Wool Shirt.

Gucci Pleated Silk Twill Midi Skirt.

In a recent development, borrowing from the boys isn’t newly introduced in fashion. Surely I’m not the only one who loves a Winchester banker shirt with monogrammed initials? But the tuxedo dressing trend—which started on the fall 2026 runways and will be going strong all spring—feels fresh. Designers placed a huge emphasis on the waist (I call it “waistmaxxing”), with the focal point of many looks being the cummerbund. A tuxedo jacket, tailored pants, and opera pumps would create a chic head-to-toe approach, but these pieces look just as cool individually. I love tuxedo pants dressed down with a white T-shirt, for example.

Zw Collection Stripped Cummerbund Shirt.

Toccin High-Waisted Tuxedo Trouser.

We’ve seen neckerchiefs and bandanas trending in previous seasons, but in spring 2027, neckwear takes a decidedly glam turn with the rise of the feather boa. I loved how Tory Burch paired her boas with car coats and silky shirts with trousers, while Dior’s models wore them with extravagant blazers. Both made the case that these three-dimensional scarves should be worn during the day, which feels cool, unexpected, and very much in keeping with the season’s dressed-up turn.

The Upcoming Arrivals Ilkyaz Ozel Feather Scarf.

Designers really did their duty this season for the uniform dressers out there. If you love a tonal neutral, there were plenty to choose from in updated silhouettes, from balloon-style cargo pants to work shirts with peplums. What made them feel extra special was the way they were accessorized—with bold, colorful heeled boots and a bright satin bag at Celine, or with neon sandal heels at Balenciaga.

Co Camo Cotton-Blend Military Jacket.

JW Anderson Parachute Drawstring Barrel-Leg Track Cargo Pants.

In a recent development, the standout jeans this spring are covered in decadent sequins, embroidery, and beading. How fun is that? The key when it comes to styling is keeping your favorite basics on deck and building a look around the jeans. Whether you pair your statement denim with a classic white shirt for work (as seen at Dolce & Gabbana and Bode) or take them for a spin around the town with an equally dazzling top (like at Conner Ives), these are always a good idea—full stop.

Stella McCartney Crystal Embellished Straight-Leg Jeans.

Polo Ralph Lauren Embroidered Mid-Rise Straight-Leg Jeans.

Hellessy Embellished Straight-Leg Jeans.

Ramy Brook Lea Beaded High-Rise Wide-Leg Crop Jeans.

With the price of gold at an all-time high and the jewelry market turning to silver, it’s been a refreshing change of pace to see glowing golden hues on the runways. We saw some gold looks sprinkled throughout shows in New York like Ralph Lauren and Fforme, but the collection that really took the medal was Anthony Vaccarello’s final Saint Laurent show, where almost every look had a golden element. From gold jacquard to silky liquid-looking gold tops, these separates can easily spruce up your favorite black trousers for the office or white jeans for a weekend lunch. Or, if you want to take home the trophy, try going head-to-toe gilded for an evening event.

Dries Van Noten Jacquard Linen, Cotton, and Silk-Blend Coat.

According to recent reports, it’s fair to say that it’s the year of the skirt. Prada may have leaned in hardest, but this classic bottom was all over the runways in every city. The key silhouette was a hybrid pencil/A-line hitting slightly below the knee. (And let me tell you, as a trouser girl, this still sits within my tailored wheelhouse.) There’s a wide range of skirts on the market and an equal range of freedom when it comes to the styling. Whether you pair your skirt with a sweater and loafers for the office or wear it with a sleeveless top and heels for a night out, it’s a piece that can take you just about anywhere.

Massimo Dutti Pleated Bubble Hem Midi Skirt.

Polo Ralph Lauren Belted Crinkled Shell-Trimmed Crepe Midi Skirt.

Marni Black Crinkled Midi Skirt With Daisy Details.

Simone Rocha Pleated Sheer Satin Midi Skirt.

It has been revealed that coats aren’t necessarily what most people get excited about in spring—who wants to hide a cute outfit when it’s finally warm enough not to bundle up?—but spring 2027 will be an exception. The coats on the runway this season were much more directional, especially the ones we saw covered in embellishments and jingling and jangling down the catwalk. Next March and April, prepare to layer in pieces that feel equal parts charming and elegant.

Dries Van Noten Embellished Wool Longline Coat.

Chan Luu Midnight Studio Coat Black.

Cinq à Sept Mariella Embellished Faux-Fur-Cuff Coat.

Prabal Gurung Floral Jacquard Single-Breasted Car Coat.
