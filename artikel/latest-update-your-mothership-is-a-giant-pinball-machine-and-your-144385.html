Pinball remains such an intoxicating game because it's one where you have to constantly be on your toes. As the tiny metal sphere bats about the place, it's down to you to hit it at just the right time so as to keep racking up points upon points. It's a game of skill as much as it is luck. It is also incredibly silly, which is why I'm so tickled by Appulse, a sci-fi pinball roguelite top-down shooter that launched today wherein your ball is actually the main defence system on your spaceship.

In a recent development, yes! This is also silly! Especially considering the semi-realistic sci-fi vibe of it all. It encourages me to wonder who in this world looked at pinball and thought, "yes, this is the optimum method of defending our ship from invading robots." And I love this fictional character for such a thought!

According to recent reports, at its most basic function, Appulse works exactly as you'd expect from a pinball game, in that you shoot your ball, or ship in this case, and hit those flappers to keep it in the air, so to speak. Accept the key difference is your ball/ship has guns that you use to shoot the aforementioned invading robots, while also doubling up as an extra method for control. Need to right a bad hit? Shoot your shot and you'll fly off in the opposite direction.

I've seen this in other digital pinball games before, but that's not a knock, I can't imagine having to manage all this shooting without some semblance of control. The roguelite mechanics sound pretty nifty too. You can get perks like extra recoil when firing, which obviously will aid you in staying in control. Or you may unlock the ability to slow down time. Builds sound pretty flexible! And overall it just looks like a neat little time. If Appulse takes your fancy, then as mentioned it's available to pick up on Steam now.
