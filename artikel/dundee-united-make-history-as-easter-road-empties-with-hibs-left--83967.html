Jim Goodwin was full of pride after watching Dundee United make history with a landmark 4-0 demolition of Hibs.

The visitors recorded their biggest ever win at Easter Road, with Zac Sapsford’s first-half double paving the way for a stunning victory before Lyall Cameron and Jesse Randall piled on the misery.

It was a humbling afternoon for Hibs, who thought they had turned a corner under new head coach Marink Reedijk, and the home support deserted the stadium long before the full-time whistle.

Goodwin has experienced some low moments in Leith, losing his job as Aberdeen boss following a 6-0 thrashing to Hibs in January 2023, so was naturally delighted by the margin of this win.

He said: “It’s not been a venue that’s been all too kind to me over the years, so on a personal note I’m very pleased. But, of course, it’s all about Dundee United. I wasn’t aware of that stat but it is something that we can be proud of.

“It’s very rare that you come to a venue like Easter Road against good opposition, especially off the back of their recent results, and have the kind of convincing victory that we’ve just experienced.

“We scored four good goals. Even the build-up for the first, the penalty, was good.”.

Sapsford got United off the mark after eight minutes, recovering from having his spot-kick saved by Rafa Sallinger to slot home the rebound after Jason Kerr had fouled him.

The Australian doubled his tally six minutes before half-time, guiding Kevin Ciubotaru’s cross into the net at the near post.

United maintained their momentum after the break, with Cameron firing home off the underside of the bar after Daniel Bennie’s effort had struck the post.

Randall completed the rout, lashing a left-footed shot past Sallinger from 15 yards after being teed up by Tait.

Meanwhile, Hibs head coach Reedijk was left struggling to explain Hibs’ sorry collapse after his side had beaten Hearts in the Premier Sports Cup semi-final and thumped Aberdeen 4-0 before the international break.

The Dutchman stated: “What has happened in these two weeks? I haven’t seen these glimpses that it would happen during the week, if you know what I mean.

“And it is not that we didn’t create chances or whatever, it is more this basic behaviour which brings you into matches and continues spells and makes the opponent doubt. We had too many of these moments that didn’t fall our way.

“That is something we need to be very sharp with because we know how nice it is to win, but we also now experience how nice it is not to win and especially in this way. I hate losing but in this way I really hate losing.”.
