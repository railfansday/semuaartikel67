Singapore-based Sea Limited (SE -1.72%) operates the largest e-commerce platform in Southeast Asia, in addition to a booming digital financial services business and a mobile game development studio that owns some of the world's most popular titles.

But Sea just announced a brand-newly introduced business called Sea Cloud, which will offer a range of artificial intelligence (AI) services to enterprise customers. The firm is uniquely positioned to make this venture a success, and it could result in significant long-term returns for investors.

Sea stock is currently the cheapest it has ever been by one widely used valuation metric, and in fact, it's trading at a steep discount to most other AI cloud stocks. Read on.

Shopee is Sea's hybrid business-to-consumer and consumer-to-consumer e-commerce platform, and it has a dominant presence in Southeast Asian countries like Indonesia, Malaysia, and Singapore. Shopee processed 4.5 billion orders worth a combined $39.7 billion during the recent third quarter of 2026 (ended Sept. 30) alone, with those figures growing by 25% and 23% year over year, respectively.

Industry observers note that then there is Monee, Sea's digital financial services platform. It offers buy now, pay later loans to consumers to boost their purchasing power, and it also lends money to Shopee merchants to help them grow their businesses. It had 44 million active borrowers during the third quarter, and its loan book grew by a whopping 60% to $12.6 billion.

Over the last two years, Sea has invested heavily in AI capabilities to make those platforms more efficient. The manufacturer is now running AI software at scale in areas like fraud detection, customer service, and content generation, and it has mastered the art of squeezing the most value out of data center computing hardware.

In a recent development, developing those capabilities cost Sea a lot of money, but it will start profiting off them by offering them to enterprise customers through Sea Cloud. Unlike most other cloud providers, Sea's strategy won't involve building expensive data centers unless specifically requested by customers, with prepayments attached.

Industry observers note that in fact, Sea is entering the AI cloud race with a rock-solid balance sheet. It boasts a whopping $9.5 billion in cash (and cash equivalents) with under $1 billion in debt, whereas many other companies in this space have borrowed truckloads of money with an uncertain path to profitability.

NYSE · As of 9:52 AM ET 11/17/26.

Recent findings suggest that the Sea Cloud business is still in the formation stage, so it hasn't generated any revenue just yet. But even without it, Sea delivered $8.6 billion in revenue from its existing businesses during the third quarter, up by an eye-popping 43.4% from the year-ago period. It blew away Wall Street's average forecast of $8.1 billion.

Sea also delivered a great result at the bottom line, so its rapid revenue growth wasn't a result of burning through significant amounts of cash. It generated $931.3 million in adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA), up 28.6% from the year-ago period.

Industry observers note that despite Sea's incredible operating results, its stock is actually down 40% this year. It's mainly because management floated the idea of spinning Monee off into a separate company, and investors are still unsure what the structure might look like. However, the deal will have to satisfy regulators in multiple countries, and Sea's chief executive Forrest Li claims the process could take up to three years, so nothing is set in stone just yet.

Industry observers note that following its sharp decline, Sea stock is trading at a price-to-sales (P/S) ratio of just 1.5 as I write this on Nov. 15, which is the cheapest level in its history. Moreover, Sea is significantly cheaper than other AI cloud providers like CoreWeave, IREN, and Nebius Group.

With a portfolio of highly successful existing businesses, a rock-solid balance sheet, and an incoming AI cloud platform, I think Sea stock could be a great long-term buy at the current level.
