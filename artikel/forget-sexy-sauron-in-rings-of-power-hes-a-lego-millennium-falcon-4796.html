The Dark Lord has been upgraded from 'giant eyeball' (or sexy Sauron, if we're talking Rings of Power) to 'the galaxy's fastest hunk of junk', because now he's a Lego Millennium Falcon.

It has been revealed that posted to reddit by fan builder Minute_Food_2881, this alternate design takes the Lego Sauron's Helmet set (widely available at the usual suspects like Amazon) and reforges it into the most iconic ship in Star Wars. There's no cheating here; no extra pieces are used for this Lego Millennium Falcon beyond what's in the box. Considering how accurate and convincing the final product is, that makes this spin on one of the best Lego sets even more impressive - a bit like the fan-made Lego Navi set that's also doing the rounds this week.

In terms of the kit used for this build, the Dark Lord's signature helmet is a pretty manageable build; it's only 13 inches tall and 538 pieces. Because it's $69.97 via Amazon (a fraction of the price many Lord of the Rings sets reach), this is amongst the cheapest Lego options for fans of Middle-earth right now.

Sources indicate that thanks to the gray of Sauron's helmet along with its blend of sharp lines and curves, it translates surprisingly well to being a Millennium Falcon. Minute_Food_2881's design is instantly recognizable as Han Solo's ship as a result, and it's reminiscent of the official midi-scale Lego Millennium Falcon.

It has been revealed that the idea of turning something into Star Wars' most memorable bag of bolts is a long-running joke at this point (lovingly named "Can it Falcon?"), and there have been numerous amusing examples. It turns out that you can indeed turn a Lego pumpkin into the Millennium Falcon, for instance, and just last month someone made the ship from a chicken kit.

Industry observers note that not that the only cool sets from a galaxy far, far away are unofficial, of course. The new 50th anniversary Lego Star Wars sets are great, but I’m most interested in their weird collectible minifigures based on original concept art. Indeed, there are quite a few launching in January so it feels like 2027 could be a big one for fans of the franchise….

Because the franchise is celebrating a big birthday next year, there's plenty to get excited about; Hasbro goes back to the beginning with nostalgic Star Wars 50th anniversary action figures, for example.
