According to recent reports, kinetic typography gets a lot more interesting when the words feel like objects. In this tutorial we build a Story where four pages of text sit on the four faces of a cube, and every row of text turns on its own, one after another, like a split-flap board.

The trick is Drum wrap, a Transform setting in ascente.design that wraps a layer or a group around a cylinder or a four-sided cube. You spin it with the Tilt Y value you already know, and you animate it like any other property. No 3D software, no plugins.
