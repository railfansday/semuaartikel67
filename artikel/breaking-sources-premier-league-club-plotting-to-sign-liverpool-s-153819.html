Liverpool sensation Lewis Koumas is attracting loan interest ahead of the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old has emerged as a regular feature in Andoni Iraola's side so far this season, with the Reds' lack of attacking depth pushing him into the manager's thoughts.

He has scored just one goal from his 16 appearances across all competitons this term, but his displays have made him a valuable asset to aid with rotation at Anfield.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Koumas' performances have caught the eye of watching suitors, inculdiong some in the Premier League.

The Wales international is set to be pushed down the pecking order in the near future, with Hugo Ekitike nearing a return from a long-term injury in the latest year.

As a result, clubs could explore the possibility of taking Koumas on loan from Liverpool, although there are doubts about whether Iraola would be willing to let him go.

Football Insider reported on 26 November that Koumas looks set to continue in a key role, with Iraola placing his trust in young players after the 3-0 win against Club Brugge.

The forward has played an increased role after earning the manager's faith in pre-season, and that may make it difficult for interested clubs to prise him away from Anfield.

However, Premier League sides are said to be keeping a close eye on Koumas' situation, and if his involvement at Liverpool slows down, they could swoop for a deal.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There are clubs would be interested in Koumas if he did become available on loan, in the Premier League and elsewhere.

"He has played a lot of games this season, maybe not always as a starter but he has regularly came off the bench in the Premier League and in the Champions League as well.

"He's been a real breakthrough player for Liverpool this season under Andoni Iraola, so I'm sure that has caught the eye in case he does become available.

"He's providing back up to Alexander Isak right now while Ekitike is out injured, and I still feel he will have a role to play for Liverpool between now and the rest of season.

"I'm not sure if they would be willing to let him go, it might be a difficult deal to do, especially if Liverpool don't want to see him leave.".

While it is unclear which specific clubs have been keeping tabs on Koumas' situation at Liverpool, a number of Premier League sides are in need of a striker in January.

The Wales international is not usually an out-and-out striker, and has largely played on the wing until now, but has played a transformed role under Iraola.

Leeds United and Newcastle are the most high-profile clubs in need of a newly introduced striker right now, with both clubs struggling for depth and reliable options to lead the line.

Whether Koumas would be enough of a high-profile for those clubs would be questioned, however, given some of the other names who have been linked with the moves.
