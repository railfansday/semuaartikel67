The NFL is addressing new inflammatory comments.

After sports commentators Mike Florio and Chris Simms mocked coach Mike Vrabel's appearance on their Pro Football Talk Live podcast, making allusions to Nazi Germany and Adolf Hitler's mustache, the league condemned the comments.

In a recent development, the NFL's executive vice president Jeff Miller told Front Office Sports in a Nov. 18 statement, "We found the remarks inappropriate and offensive.".

Recent findings suggest that while discussing the Upcoming England Patriots' Nov. 15 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany, Simms and Florio—who did not explicitly name Hitler—made remarks about the Patriots head coach's looks.

Sources indicate that "He got the German mustache to go along with that right now, too," Simms—a former Denver Broncos quarterback—said during their Nov. 17 episode. "So, he was hitting all themes there over in Germany.".

Meanwhile, Florio, 61, joined in, adding, "If you're in Germany, you gotta be careful trimming the sides of that thing.".

Sources indicate that as he laughed, Simms, 46, noted that Vrabel's appearance in Germany could make some pedestrians do a double-take. He continued, "Who's that on the street over there? He put on a few pounds. Hold on, he's substantial, holy crap.".

But others on the PFL team weren't impressed with the jokes, with Florio noting that their show producer Gary Carter was heard groaning during the segment.

"It's funny, Gary," he stated. "It was 80 years ago. Let us have some fun, for crying out loud.".

Florio—who began the radio show in 2015—and Simms addressed the interaction following the incident.

Recent findings suggest that "On yesterday's (Nov. 17) PFT Live show," they said in a joint statement on NBC Sports' website, "we made comments that some found offensive and we apologize.".

NBC Sports—which distributes the podcast, though Florio retains 100 percent equity—also admonished the hosts for the comments.

Industry observers note that "On yesterday's PFT Live show, Mike and Chris made statements that were inappropriate," a spokesperson for the sports news company told The Athletic. "They have apologized.".

Sources indicate that E! News has reached out to Vrabel's reps for comment and has not yet heard back.
