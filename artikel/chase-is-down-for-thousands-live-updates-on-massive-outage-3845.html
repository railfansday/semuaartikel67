Chase is down for thousands — live updates on massive outage

🌐 Source: Tomsguide • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 4 min read (786 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Chase bank appears to be suffering an online banking outage.
  • The bank has not officially remarked anything about the outage online, but comments have claimed that tellers are telling customers they're having online banking challenges.
  • Finance is tracking statements from different banks.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Chase bank appears to be suffering an online banking outage. On the outage tracking site, Down Detector, reports slowly starting rolling in this morning before suddenly spiking around 10 am Pacific.

The bank has not officially remarked anything about the outage online, but comments have claimed that tellers are telling customers they're having online banking challenges.

One support tweet on X seems to confirm this.

In a recent development, yahoo! Finance is tracking statements from different banks. Wells Fargo and Bank of America have received some outage reports, but not as many as Chase, which again suggests that it's an issue between Chase and other banks.

"You can still access your accounts through our ATMs, banking locations, and customer service," the Wells Fargo website reportedly reads.

I have a Wells Fargo account and could not find that statement, but I've also had no issues logging in or using my accounts online, so my experience may not be the same.

Bank of America has not released a statement.

A Chase spokesperson has responded to our inquiries with a similar statement as the one received by the San Francisco Chronicle.

Recent findings suggest that “Due to a technical concern, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete but customers still have access to their accounts and funds. We are working to resolve this as swiftly as possible.".

However, our statement had one extra line saying that the concern was not cyber-related, meaning it is something in the Chase systems and not a malicious attack.

"This is a technical issue, not cyber related.”.

The spokesperson did not indicate how long it would take to resolve the glitch or any potential causes.

Reports are starting to go Down Detector, though as of this posting they are still around 4,000 after a peak of 5,000 reports about 30 minutes ago.

Analyst Bruce Schneier (Security Technologist; Fellow at Harvard Kennedy School) is an internationally renowned security technologist.

We asked him why companies don't have redundancies to prevent outages:.

According to recent reports, "Redundancies reduce profitability, and it is the goal of shareholders these days to wring all excess profits out of any and every company. Thatreduces resilience in general, and redundancy in particular.”.

With the Outlook outage last week, and more every day, Schneier expects more will come.

Sources indicate that "I guess I’m not surprised. Our systems are very fragile these days. Fragile systems are the most profitable in the near term. And there is no incentive to focus on the long term.”.

Chase has not responded to Tom's Guide as of this posting; however, a spokesperson did give a statement to the San Francisco Chronicle.

Recent findings suggest that “Due to a technical matter, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete, but customers still have access to their accounts and funds,” a Chase spokesperson told the paper. “We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible.”.

I'm seeing reports and comments that people are having trouble logging into other banks including Bank of America and Wells Fargo.

I've been able to login into Wells, but that may not be universal.

Lets u know, are you having problems with your bank?

According to posters on Reddit, people have said that after attempting online transactions they ended up going to their location branch.

Multiple people stated that the Chase tellers told them all their systems were down and they couldn't do anything.

"Then went to branch to ask about it they stated they can’t do anything because their system is down smh," said one representative poster.

Tom's Guide has reached out to Chase for comment.

However, if you're trying to send or receive money from or to a Chase account, you will likely have problems.
