Recent findings suggest that norse’s efforts to diversify beyond low-cost, long-haul flying ultimately proved insufficient to keep its planes in the air. The focus now shifts to what, if anything, can be salvaged from the business.

Norse Atlantic Airways has canceled all flights and discontinued ticket sales after its Norwegian operating subsidiary noted it is filing for bankruptcy.

The collapse comes a day after the low-cost, long-haul carrier suspended flights and two days after it described its financial situation as “critical.”.

In a stock exchange filing on Friday, Norse confirmed that the board of its Norwegian operating subsidiary, Norse Atlantic Airways AS, had concluded there was no longer a basis for continued operations. The board resolved to file a bankruptcy petition with a local court, which will appoint a trustee to oversee the process.

The filing does not extend to the listed parent organization, Norse Atlantic ASA, or its UK operating subsidiary. Norse mentioned efforts are continuing to find a solution that would allow the remaining parts of the business to continue operating.

However, flights operated by both the Norwegian and British airlines have been cance.
