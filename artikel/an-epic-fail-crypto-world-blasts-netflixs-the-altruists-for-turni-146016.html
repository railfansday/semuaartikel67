‘An epic fail’: Crypto world blasts Netflix’s ‘The Altruists’ for turning SBF’s crimes into a love story

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that A fresh Netflix series that tells the story of crypto crook Sam Bankman-Fried is winning decent reviews from TV critics and the public. But not everyone is a fan. People familiar with the crypto industry are taking to social media to blast the makers of “The Altruists” for distorting the nature of Bankman-Fried’s relationship with one-time girlfriend, Caroline Ellison, and for depicting the convicted felons as misguided idealists. The series, which dropped last week, is also taking fire for suggesting without evidence that Binance founder Changpeng Zhao is the real villain of the story—a depiction some claim is rooted in anti-Chinese racism.

The 8-part series, which lists Barack and Michelle Obama as executive producers, is based on real-world events that led to the 2022 collapse of Bankman-Fried’s crypto exchange, FTX. A subsequent FBI investigation resulted in Ellison pleading guilty and testifying against her former boyfriend, who is at the moment serving 25 years in federal prison for defrauding customers of billions of dollars.

Sources indicate that filmmakers regularly take liberties in order to increase dramatic impact. In the case of “The Altruists”, though, critics say the series engages in outright falsehoods by minimizing Bankman-Fried’s crimes, and depicting him as a victim who acted under duress from Binance’s Zhao. Don Wilson, the CEO of the crypto market-making firm DRW, posted a widely-shared clip from The Altruists showing Zhao and Bankman-Fried on X, describing it as “preposterous” and “an epic fail.”.

In a recent development, this is an epic fail by Netflix. Recasting this as something @SBF_FTX and Caroline were forced to do in order to placate @cz_binance – instead of highlighting the effective altruist ethos that drove them to commit yet another crime is preposterous. https://t.co/bV6IsoG6mE.

The clip itself demonstrates Zhao’s character telling Ellison and Bankman-Fried that FTX is failing to grow fast enough to justify an early investment he made in the exchange, and declaring that he will financially destroy the exchange if the pair fail to collude with him in a criminal trading scheme.

While Zhao was indeed an early investor in FTX, the problem of coercion never came up at Bankman-Fried’s criminal trial, and there is no evidence the exchange depicted in the clip ever took place. Another crypto industry vet on X likewise objected to another scene for “making SBF and Caroline look like innocent victims who were bullied” by Zhao.

In a recent development, netflix portrayed @cz_binance as a complete moron in its latest FTX movie while making SBF and Caroline look like innocent victims who were bullied.Who the hell wrote this script? pic.twitter.com/HjAwQ3N3W9.

Others objected to the heavy Cantonese accent affected by Zhao’s character, played by Terry Chen, complaining it promoted stereotypes. Zhao himself wrote on X, “hmm… I grew up in Canada, worked in the New York, Tokyo, many places. I almost never felt being targeted by racism. But something to think about…”. Veteran crypto journalist Laura Shin expressed a similar sentiment, stating “I’m just going to say it: Racism has to have played a role in how they portrayed [Zhao]. There’s no other explanation.”.

Sources indicate that aside from the depictions of Zhao, other viewers complained that “The Altruists” wrongly portrayed Ellison as the mastermind behind a series of accounting frauds used to conceal holes in the balance sheet of FTX and of Bankman-Fried’s hedge fund, Alameda Research. This contradicts contemporary news accounts and evidence from the criminal trial, which unambiguously pointed to Bankman-Fried as the one responsible for a series of frauds.

Those weighing in to complain about the depictions included FTX’s former COO, Constance Wang, who wrote, “Maybe you’ll enjoy The Altruists as a romantic comedy. For those of us who watched it together, it felt like someone else’s story with our names attached.”.

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, some critics of “The Altruists” suggested that the numerous inaccuracies could be explained in part for political reasons. Lawyer Hailey Lennon pointed to the production’s ties to the Obamas and Bankman-Fried’s hefty donations to the Democratic party, writing “The framing of the movie as some love story and to make SBF look more innocent is even more calculated than I initially realized. Always follow the money.”.

The Altruists was produced by Michelle and Barak Obama SBF publicly donated $39.8 million to Democratic candidates and aligned outside groups during the 2021–2022 election cycleThe framing of the movie as some love story and to make SBF look more innocent is even more… pic.twitter.com/pzvWJP7tUH.

Others on social media likewise pointed to the Obamas’ involvement, and Bankman-Fried’s donations to the Democratic party—including $5.2 million to former President Joe Biden’s campaign—as an explanation for the film’s depictions.

Bankman-Fried, who remains incarcerated in federal prison in California, has failed to express remorse for stealing over $8 billion from FTX customers and investors. He is right now slated to be released around 2047.
