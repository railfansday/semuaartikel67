The market seems to have mixed feelings about the departure of former Nvidia (NVDA -4.50%) CEO Jensen Huang.

An independent online petition requesting that Huang be reinstated now has over 180,000 signatures, including those from prominent business leaders like Elon Musk. President Donald Trump has also reportedly stated he wouldn't mind if Huang returned as CEO.

However, not everyone is as forgiving.

Sources indicate that during an interview with Fox News over the weekend, U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent expressed significant concerns about the situation and called for a "dramatic shakeup" at Nvidia. Make no mistake, Bessent just issued a blistering warning to the artificial intelligence (AI) chip giant.

Huang's resignation came after media outlets reported that the Nvidia co-founder had a relationship with a woman named Alice Huang (no relation), who previously was a controlling shareholder in a Singaporean data center tech giant called MegaSpeed.

Other media outlets, such as The Latest York Times, reported that Huang also had alleged extramarital affairs with Nvidia employees. But the relationship with Alice raises national security concerns.

In a recent development, the Information reported that Alice created a network that illegally diverted $7.5 billion worth of Nvidia chips to China. The U.S. at the moment has export restrictions on the sale of certain Nvidia chips to China. Nvidia has claimed that it has had virtually zero Chinese chip sales since April 2025.

"I want to keep coming back to what was the culture at Nvidia, and were they pressing these seemingly illicit sales to goose their revenues and their earnings?" Bessent mentioned during the Fox interview. "If this were a bank and they hadn't complied like this, the OCC (Office of the Comptroller of the Currency) would be in there right now, and a lot of the executives would be removed.".

Bessent didn't hold back his views on the scandal, either, saying it was clear that some companies buying chips from Nvidia were shell companies working to advance AI interests in China.

NASDAQ · As of 3:42 PM ET 11/16/26.

Additionally, Bessent clearly does not want to see Huang return as CEO and believes Nvidia needs to get its ducks in a row quickly.

Industry observers note that "This makes me think that as the board conducts the search for a fresh leader, that they need to go outside the firm," he mentioned. "I think we need answers in the next 10 days from the board and from the outside counsel.".

Industry observers note that bessent has been a close confidant of Trump during his second term. The question is, how much weight does Bessent's warning carry within the administration?

Not only did Trump recently say he wouldn't mind seeing Huang return to Nvidia, but Bessent's questions also suggest divisions between Bessent and Howard Lutnick, another close confidant of Trump and the Secretary of the U.S. Commerce Department.

In a recent development, bessent told CNBC that some federal agencies wanted to put MegaSpeed on an export blacklist. "But Commerce has the pen," Bessent remarked.

A White House spokesperson told Reuters last week that Trump has "complete confidence" in Lutnick. U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche noted on Nov. 12 that there is no confirmation yet that Alice was in fact a Chinese spy.

But questions remain about the extent to which Nvidia's chip sales, particularly in the Asia-Pacific region, have been diverted to China, if at all.

In a recent development, as Treasury Secretary, Bessent doesn't have direct authority to punish Nvidia, although historically he has been considered part of Trump's inner circle and has largely been in lockstep with the president. But with Trump seemingly supporting Huang, it's hard to know how much sway Bessent's words carry.

Still, it's clear that Bessent just issued a blistering warning to Nvidia, and I doubt he is alone among officials in the administration with concerns, so Bessent's warning shouldn't fall on deaf ears.
