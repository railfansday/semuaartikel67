RB Leipzig secured a 3-1 win against RC Lens in the Champions League on Tuesday night. Speaking after the game, head coach Martin Demichelis mentioned the team still has complications to address despite the strong start.

“We got off to a really good start. After ten minutes, we’d scored twice and had plenty of shots and corners. We played very well right up until half-time. We weren’t as good in the second half as we had been in the first,” he stated, further adding that the team lacked confidence on the ball and made a lot of mistakes: “Lens changed their approach and were the better side. There are things we need to address.”.

Industry observers note that demichelis also discussed the importance of enjoying the win: “We need to learn to enjoy moments like these. I congratulated the boys and told them that tonight wasn’t the time to be disappointed or have doubts. We can enjoy this win tonight,” he added.

In a recent development, regarding possible fatigue, he said they will try to utilize rotation going forward, even though that is not the issue at the moment: “That wasn’t our problem today, but fatigue will start to become a factor over the next few games. We’ll try to manage that by rotating the squad and sharing out the playing time. We need everyone in the squad.”.

Leipzig will travel to Old Trafford to face Manchester United in their next Champions League game but before that, they have important Bundesliga games against Freiburg and Mainz, while they will face Bayer Leverkusen in the DFB Pokal.
