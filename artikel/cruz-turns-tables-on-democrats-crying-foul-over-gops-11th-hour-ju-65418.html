According to recent reports, sen. Ted Cruz, R-Texas, responds to Sen. Cory Booker, D-N.J., during a Senate Judiciary Committee nominations hearing on Capitol Hill on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026.

It has been revealed that sen. Ted Cruz, R-Texas, criticized the "hypocrisy" of his Democratic counterparts over their selective outrage stemming from Republican senators' push to confirm President Donald Trump's judicial appointees before Democrats take control of the Senate in January.

Industry observers note that "And what did the same Senate Democrats who just gave sanctimonious speeches do? They rammed through 22 federal judges in November and December before leaving," Cruz stated of Democrats, referring to their actions following the 2024 elections.

In a recent development, cruz's remarks came during a Senate Judiciary Committee nomination hearing on Wednesday, where seven judicial nominees were introduced to committee. The Texas senator was responding to accusations from Sen. Cory Booker, D-N. J., who noted Republicans were "rushing" the judicial hearings and in his 13 years in office, he has never seen the committee hold "back-to-back" hearings without the minority's consent.

"You know, you don't need a particularly long memory to recognize the hypocrisy in what Senator Booker mentioned," Cruz said, comparing Booker's "lecture" of Republicans to the theatrics of Captain Renault in the movie "Casablanca.".

"He is shocked, shocked to find gambling in this institution," Cruz said.

Cruz pointed out that after President Joe Biden lost the White House and Democrats lost control of the Senate in the 2024 election, Senate Democrats "rammed through" 22 federal judges before Republicans took control in January 2025.

"You know, there's an old adage that hypocrisy is the tribute that vice plays to virtue," Cruz said.

Booker argued that Republicans were disregarding the election results and suggested that the committee wait until the next term to continue with the nomination process.

"This is not a left or right thing because I've watched both parties do this in the 13 years that I've been here," Booker stated. "I'm just simply saying that this is yet another step at a time where we could have paused and done this a right way.".
