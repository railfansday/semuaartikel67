Sources indicate that while Grand Theft Auto 5 had you juggle three people through a campaign, GTA 6 saddles you with a couple: Jason and Lucia. Although some fans will miss the hectic nature of three hotheads, Jason and Lucia bring a completely different vibe to GTA, and can go on missions together.

According to recent reports, initially, you'll start the game as Jason, with Lucia currently serving time in Leonida Penitentiary. Eventually, the two will cross paths, and you can play the game with both protagonists. Here's how long you'll need to wait until Lucia not only enters the picture, but is fully absorbed into the story.

Spoiling as little as possible, you'll get a short intro scene as Lucia in Leonida Penitentiary, then the perspective will immediately shift to Jason for quite some time. After progressing through the campaign a bit, Lucia will eventually start a work release program and will be allowed to return home and reunite with Jason.

In a recent development, the thing is, it will take some time until Lucia is actually brought on board. The opening hours of the game are firmly rooted in Jason's story, with periodic updates on Lucia's situation, bookended by phone calls about plans for when she gets out.

Recent findings suggest that since story completion is the same for everyone, this is one of the most accurate stats for when Lucia joins the game in earnest. The time it takes to get to this point varies (since you don't always need to progress with the campaign), but the percentage of story completion is a good barometer for where you're at.

Industry observers note that the ability to freely swap between characters arrives later in the game, in Chapter 2 (I hit this around the 15-hour mark). You'll take full control of Lucia for a while, eventually coming across a mission called "Breakfast Run." This core mission will teach you how to use the "protagonist swapping" mechanic from GTA 5, and will then let players swap between Jason and Lucia freely for most of the game (minus some mission-specific circumstances).

To do this, hold down the right D-pad button to swap between Jason and Lucia with L1 and R1. Alternatively, you can also invite Jason or Lucia along on most events, including side missions or roaming around the open world causing havoc. If there's a restriction, the game will let you know in advance, like "Jason Only" or "Lucia Only" for specific story missions.

Industry observers note that for the most part, story missions will bring Jason and Lucia into the fold automatically, so there's no reason to have to micromanage where any character is at any time. In GTA 6, quality of life and convenience are at the forefront, and you can even choose to play mostly as one character if you want.
