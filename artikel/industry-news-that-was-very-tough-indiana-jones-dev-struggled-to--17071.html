Industry News: "That Was Very Tough" - Indiana Jones Dev Struggled To Transition From Gunplay

🌐 Source: Nintendolife • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (335 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is one of the biggest surprises in recent years.
  • Yet transitioning from Wolfenstein wasn't exactly easy for the studio.
  • “It was one of those features that we were so afraid of.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is one of the biggest surprises in recent years. Developer MachineGames, well known for its ability to make gunning down Nazis immensely satisfying in its Wolfenstein franchise, created what is arguably the best Indy adventure since The Last Crusade.

Yet transitioning from Wolfenstein wasn't exactly easy for the studio. In a upcoming interview with the FRVR podcast (thanks, VGC), design director Jens Andersson explains how the developers found it "very tough" to focus on melee combat, given its expertise in gunplay.

“It was one of those features that we were so afraid of. Especially since MachineGames’ pedigree of Wolfenstein, coming into making Indiana Jones with a team primed to make more gore with heavy weapons, running and gunning… and then ‘oh, we’re going to make a family-friendly adventure’. That was very tough.”.

Granted, 'family-friendly' might be a bit of a stretch given the game's 16+ PEGI rating, but we understand the sentiment. To make matters worse, MachineGames had to nail the balance between making guns a last resort for Indy while ensuring they still felt good to use.

According to recent reports, “That was non-negotiable because we have a lot of people at Machine that love guns. They love everything about guns. They have people that professionally compete shooting. Gun Nuts. Obviously, you know, from Wolfenstein games that interest has been satiated. And at the game time, you have so many fans of Quake and other shooters out there. So we can’t make gunplay bad, that wouldn’t have been acceptable.”.

Industry observers note that to ensure that fans of guns still enjoyed the game, MachineGames strived to make melee combat "interesting, fun, and easy". This, combined with making reloading a fairly slow process, encourages you to experiment with your environment and locate more melee weapons.
