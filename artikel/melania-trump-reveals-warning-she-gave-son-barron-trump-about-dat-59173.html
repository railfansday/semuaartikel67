Melania Trump is a special adviser for her son’s dating life.

The First Lady recalled the cautionary advice she gave her and President Donald Trump’s 20-year-old son Barron Trump when it came to his romantic life.

“I always say it’s a dangerous world out there,” Melania remarked in the Nov. 20 episode of her new Prime Video docuseries MELANIA, “and be careful.”.

And beyond his dating life, the 56-year-old noted she’ll be there for Barron “anytime he wants to have my advice or input.”.

It has been revealed that “We have very open communication regarding life. I will always be there for him,” she continued. “I am, and I will always be. And he knows that he’s my priority, and he will always be my priority.”.

Plus, as Melania quipped, “I'm a very cool mom to Barron, and I remind him all the time.".

But while the former model has prioritized motherhood, she has been vocal about not strutting the path of parenthood alone. That’s why she was grateful to have her late mom Amalija Knavs, who died in 2024, to help raise Barron.

In a recent development, “My mother was the epitome of love and warmth, and for Barron, a grandmother who filled every moment with joy and wisdom,” Melania told USA Today in May. “Every story she read to Barron sparked his imagination, and every game they played strengthened their bond.”.

Now, the Melania author is passing down the hard-earned advice she gained from raising Barron.

According to recent reports, “To all the young mothers out there: Shower your children with love and support and always communicate openly,” she continued. “See them as their own unique individuals, not merely as extensions of yourself.”.

And when it comes to ensuring a bright future for the next generation, Melania added, “Guide them with wisdom but establish a foundation of freedom where they can carve their own life path.”.

Ivana Trump was the first wife of President-elect Donald Trump. Born in Gottwaldov, Czechoslovakia—now Zlín of the Czech Republic—in 1949, she rose to fame as a competitive skier. According to NBC News, the New York City Medical Examiner said she died of “blunt impact injuries” and that her manner of death was an accident. A senior NYC official with direct knowledge added the circumstances were consistent with a fall on the stairs in her New York apartment.

Melania Trump is Donald’s third wife and current First Lady of the United States. In 2024, she released her memoir Melania and she's set to appear in a documentary. Melania has also spoken about where she will spend her time during her husband's second presidency.
