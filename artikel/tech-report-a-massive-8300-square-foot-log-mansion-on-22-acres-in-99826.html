A sprawling 8,300-square-foot hand-hewn log home on 22 wooded acres in Greenacres, WA has hit the market for under $3 million after a $320,000 price drop.

Built with full timber walls throughout and soaring views of the Pacific Northwest skyline, the seven-bedroom estate offers total seclusion just 15 minutes from the highway.

Sources indicate that “It's huge. It's a 8,300-square-foot log cabin and the architecture on this one is just stunning. It's hand-hewn logs,” says listing agent Denay Hornberger with Realty One Group Eclipse. “It’s a one-of-a-kind property and the owners added a bunch of beautiful features to it.”.

Completed in 2008, the sellers bought the place before it was completed and finished the construction.

It has been revealed that “When they first bought it, the builder had spent too much money building it and ended up having to sell it when it was half done. They came in and finished it and added hundreds of thousands of dollars to this property to make it what it is today.” Hornberger explains, adding she lives on an adjacent property. “With the money that's been put into it, [the sellers have] an eye for detail. I mean, everything about this home has been well thought out.”.

Now the owners are listing it for $2.875 million.

Recent findings suggest that “The thing about a property like this is trying to figure out exactly how to price it because there are no comps to really help you decide where the price is right,” she notes, adding the initial asking price was a bit high. “I think we're now right in the sweet spot of where we need to be.”.

The home has seven bedrooms and seven bathrooms with real logs visible almost everywhere.

Industry observers note that “There are similar log cabins but they're not fully log cabins the way that [this home] is. Sometimes you have little bits of logs and then you have drywall,” Hornberger notes. “Here, the logs are everywhere throughout the house and that is extremely unique to have that. You feel like you are in a log cabin in every almost every single room you're in.”.

The fact the sellers spared no expense is evident immediately.

“You walk in and your breath is just taken away. It is so beautiful and it's so grand that when you walk in, you just have to take a moment and appreciate it because the windows are huge, the logs are huge,” she states. “The fireplace is absolutely stunning, and then you look out the window to just woods and skyline. It's just stunning.”.

The home sits on 22-acres of land and while it seems far away from civilization, it isn’t.

Recent findings suggest that “You're actually 15 minutes from the freeway and you're about 20 minutes from the mall,” Hornberger notes. “So even though you feel like you're in the middle of nowhere, you just drive a little bit and you're back in the mix of things. So, you get the best of both worlds.”.

It also epitomizes the feel of the Pacific Northwest.

“We have that woodsy feel. People like to wear their flannel here and we're kind of a laid-back community,” she explains. “So [the house] is taking the best of the Pacific Northwest and having that cabin-in-the-woodsy feel, which is what we're known for.”.

The amount of land makes it perfect for outdoors activities like hiking and hunting, but the house is also luxurious, meaning two types of perfect buyers.

“If you just want to be outside, it’s a perfect home for that,” Hornberger says. “I think the second perfect buyer is somebody who wants a second home... who's in a hustle and bustle all the time but really wants that getaway that they can go to several times a year, have that peace and quiet from the city, get back to nature, and then go back to their regular lives at the end of the day.”.
