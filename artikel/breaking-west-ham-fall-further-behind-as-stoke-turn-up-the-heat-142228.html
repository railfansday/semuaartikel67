📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you thought things couldn’t get any worse for West Ham, they certainly did last night, with the club dropping down to 13th in the EFL Championship. Four games took place on Wednesday evening, and the results made pretty grim reading for Hammers fans.

Sources indicate that blackburn Rovers leapfrogged West Ham courtesy of a 1-0 win away at Watford, with Todd Cantwell scoring from the penalty spot. Meanwhile, Birmingham City won 2-0 at Burnley, and West Bromwich Albion secured a 3-1 victory away at Bolton Wanderers.

According to recent reports, perhaps most worryingly of all, given West Ham’s next opponents, Stoke City recorded their fifth successive Championship victory by beating Millwall 2-1. As reported by the EFL’s official website, George Hirst scored both goals for the Potters.

There now appears to be something of a breakaway group of five clubs at the top of the division, with four points separating fifth-placed West Brom from sixth-placed Southampton.

The Hammers are already 11 points adrift of the automatic promotion places and eight behind West Brom.

Sources indicate that with rampant Stoke City next up for Scott Parker’s team at the London Stadium on Saturday, things aren’t getting any easier. We’re going to have to start winning games just to prevent what is already becoming quite a sizeable gap from getting any bigger.
