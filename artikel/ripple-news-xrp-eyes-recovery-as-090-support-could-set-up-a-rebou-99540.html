Sources indicate that in Ripple news today, XRP is showing signs of a potential rebound in this brutal November, with price bouncing from under $0.9 low to $0.93 as bulls return to the crypto market. Bitcoin and Ethereum have started recovering, but Ripple’s token has yet to catch the same speed and momentum. That could change if XRP manages to defend the $0.90 region.

Industry observers note that XRP has fallen by 25% this month as the same correction wiped out much of its recent momentum. Despite the sluggish recovery, XRP is far from an isolated disaster. The crypto market has endured heavy liquidations and risk-off selling, while money rotates toward AI stocks and other opportunities.

The November sell-off has taken XRP well away from its July 2025 peak near $3.65. Yet the decline has also pushed its daily Relative Strength Index below 30, placing the token in oversold territory.

In a recent development, that reading comes as sentiment across crypto has turned deeply pessimistic. Leveraged traders have been forced out of positions, ETF investors have withdrawn capital, and altcoins have struggled to attract fresh liquidity. XRP has been caught in that rotation, rather than facing a sudden deterioration in its own ecosystem. U.S. spot XRP ETFs have recorded roughly $30 million in November outflows, while combined assets have slipped below $1 billion.

Meanwhile, news on Ripple business and the XRP Ledger continues to move forward. RLUSD activity, network development, latest lending infrastructure, and ongoing amendment votes are adding to the ecosystem. Those developments haven’t prevented the correction, but they give investors something beyond price action to revisit when risk appetite returns.

XRP is also approaching a potentially attractive zone for longer-term buyers. Prices between $0.70 and $0.90 have become a key area of interest after the token briefly touched the lower end of that range. A sustained recovery in the wider market could make current prices look less intimidating.

Industry observers note that XRP’s immediate challenge is to turn its weekend recovery into something more convincing. The token needs to reclaim $0.95 before $1 becomes a realistic short-term target. A clean move above the psychological barrier could open the way toward $1.05 and $1.10 as traders look for momentum to return.

The downside remains clear. Another break below $0.90 might send XRP back toward $0.86, where buyers stepped in during the latest flush. That level is vital because another rejection could undermine the recovery attempt and extend the correction.

But XRP doesn’t need a spectacular catalyst to bounce. The market has already priced in a painful November, momentum is stretched, and sentiment is heavily tilted toward caution. If Bitcoin stabilizes and ETF outflows slow, XRP could have room for a sharp recovery.

For now, $0.90 is the level to watch. Holding it could give XRP the breathing room it needs to reclaim $1 and put November’s sell-off behind it.
