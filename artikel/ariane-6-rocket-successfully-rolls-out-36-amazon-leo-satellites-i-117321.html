📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Amazon's first satellite launch since a rocket failure that doomed 40 of its spacecraft went off without a hitch Tuesday morning (Nov. 24).

An Ariane 6 rocket carrying 36 satellites for the Amazon Leo constellation lifted off at 6:14 a.m. EST (1114 GMT) from Europe's Spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana, on Tuesday (Nov. 23), right at the end of the 29-minute window that opened at 6:45 a.m. EST (1045 GMT; 7:45 a.m. local time in Kourou).

It has been revealed that the launch was originally supposed to take place on Monday (Nov. 23), but that attempt was scrubbed due to a technical issue affecting the rocket's ground support systems, according to Arianespace.

Amazon Leo, so named because it will operate in low Earth orbit, will compete with SpaceX's Starlink internet constellation, which presently consists of more than 11,000 operational spacecraft.

In a recent development, amazon's network will eventually have more than 3,200 satellites, but just 400 or so have reached orbit to date. Another 40 were supposed to be delivered on Nov. 15, but they were lost after their rocket ride, a United Launch Alliance (ULA) Vulcan Centaur, suffered a problem shortly after launch.

According to recent reports, ariane 6 and Vulcan Centaur aren't the only rockets Amazon is depending on to build its broadband constellation. Jeff Bezos' organization has also booked rides on ULA's Atlas V rocket, Blue Origin's Newly introduced Glenn and SpaceX's Falcon 9.

Sources indicate that vulcan will be grounded for a while as ULA investigates the Nov. 15 failure and works to fix whatever caused it. And New Glenn hasn't flown since April, when the rocket failed to deliver AST SpaceMobile's BlueBird 7 satellite to the proper orbit. New Glenn's return to flight was further complicated on May 28, when the rocket exploded during a routine prelaunch test.

The Ariane 6, the successor to Arianespace's Ariane 5 heavy lifter, debuted in July 2024 and has nine launches under its belt to date. All of them have been successful except that maiden mission, which was a partial failure.

This morning's launch was the fourth Amazon Leo flight for the Ariane 6, following launches in February, April and June of this year.
