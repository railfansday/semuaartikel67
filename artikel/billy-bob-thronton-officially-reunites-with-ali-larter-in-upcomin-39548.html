According to recent reports, taylor Sheridan is as busy as ever expanding the Yellowstone universe with newly introduced demonstrates such as Marshals and Dutton Ranch, but he also has plenty of other new releases on the horizon. Currently dominating streaming right now is the long-awaited fourth season of Tulsa King, but fans don’t have to worry about the show going anywhere anytime soon. Paramount has renewed the show for Season 5, and Sylvester Stallone shared with Collider that he’d like to see the show run for six seasons, so it could easily be around for a few more years. The first official Tulsa King spin-off, Frisco King (starring Samuel L. Jackson), is coming to Paramount+ in December before Tulsa King Season 5 moves to New York — it will be the first season of the show not set in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Paramount has clearly found a creator worth investing in with Taylor Sheridan, and although he may be leaving the studio at the end of 2028 for NBCUniversal, he still has plenty left to give before his departure. When Yellowstone came to a close in 2024, it didn’t take Sheridan long to find his next hit series with Landman, which stars Billy Bob Thornton. Paramount has already released two full seasons of Landman in 2024 and 2025, and while it was once thought that the show would be back before the end of 2026, it’s now been confirmed that it won’t be back until sometime in 2027. Production is now underway on Season 3, though, and leading star Ali Larter has shared a huge batch of images from behind the scenes, seemingly confirming that there will be at least one episode set at TCU, where Ainsley is a cheerleader.

In a recent development, one of the images posted by Ali Larter over the weekend featured breakout Hollywood star Glen Powell, which sparked speculation that he could appear in an episode of Landman Season 3. It’s vital to note that it has been confirmed Powell is dating Landman star Michelle Randolph, who plays Ainsley Norris in the hit Taylor Sheridan series, so it’s possible he was simply visiting his girlfriend on a day off.
