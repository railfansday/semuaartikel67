A night out with Gwyneth Paltrow and Chris Martin's kids looks something just like this.

The former couple's daughter Apple Martin, 22, and son Moses Martin, 20, stepped out together for a rare siblings outing to Burberry's holiday party at The Mark Hotel in Newly introduced York City.

It has been revealed that for the Nov. 16 occasion, Apple looked practically like her mom's twin in a black duster jacket with a white crop top and gray trousers. Meanwhile, Moses was a spitting image of his dad, donning a gray coat paired with a black polo tee and matching jeans.

The pair were joined by Moses' People I've Met bandmates Orlando Wiltshire and Andrew Suster, who performed a short set for the star-studded crowd.

But while Apple and Moses are striking out on their own, many people are often "surprised at how lovely and unassuming and down-to-earth they are," according to Gwyneth.

Industry observers note that “Look, they’re the children of two super-famous people, and so they understand what comes with that," the Shakespeare in Love actress told Vanity Fair last year. "They’ve grown up in it.".

It has been revealed that “There’s this deep inherent pain in letting go because we’ve paired it with protecting them, and we can’t always protect them,” she continued. “We have to deeply let go and let them have their own experience and be the kind of person that they want to boomerang back to.”.

That's why Gwyneth—who broke up with Chris in 2014 after 10 years of marriage—was thrilled that both her children opted to enroll in college while also exploring careers in modeling and music.

"College is a great equalizer," she explained, calling Apple "so smart" and revealing that Moses "is incredible at math.".

According to recent reports, still, Gwyneth noted that her children—especially Apple—had "trepidation" about being in the spotlight. As Apple explained in an April 2025 profile with Interview, "I was really discouraged from doing anything in the public eye.".

"Also, I was like, 'I don’t think we need another celebrity child in the world,'" she continued. "I just try to do what feels right and block out anything regarding me in the news to the best of my ability. And I’m getting a lot better at being like, 'F--k it.'”.

Recent findings suggest that she added, "I’m not going to be scared. I just want to do what seems fun and figure my life out.".
