Princess Kate Middleton is set to open up about her ongoing cancer recovery in a forthcoming documentary.

“Cancer does change you, it changes your body, it changes you physically,” Kate, 44, began in a trailer for her upcoming Heal documentary released Saturday, November 21. “There’s a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you.”.

The Princess of Wales was diagnosed with an undisclosed form of cancer in 2024, subsequently undergoing a preventative round of chemotherapy as treatment. Kate confirmed in January 2025 that she is in remission, nearly one year before tackling the Three Peaks Challenge mountaineering event in June to raise awareness for cancer treatment and recovery. While completing her trek, Kate secretly filmed Heal.

Sources indicate that “Introducing Heal, a deeply personal latest documentary from The Princess of Wales, exploring life beyond diagnosis and the many ways people find comfort, strength and hope in the face of serious illness,” an Instagram statement read. “Through this documentary, The Princess hopes to help others navigate diagnosis, recovery and life beyond cancer and transform access to holistic cancer care which supports wellbeing, resilience and quality of life during and after diagnosis.”.

Kate revealed that all proceeds from Heal will be donated to the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, which is where she received treatment.

“Is it true when you leave the Royal Marsden, they let you ring the bell?” Kate’s brother, James Middleton, asked in a separate Heal scene mid-hike.

While Kate confirmed that Royal Marsden does allow patients to ring a bell to signify the end of treatment, she “didn’t ring the bell.”.

“Maybe this is your ringing of the bell,” James, 39, mused about their Three Peaks endeavor.

Kate further opened up about her experience on the trail, where participants hike across three mountains in England, Wales and Scotland.

It has been revealed that “You know healing is a journey, how to reconnect with the things you once knew and how to find a sense of hope and joy alongside uncertainty,” the Duchess of Cambridge added in a voiceover. “It’s clear we’re not meant to carry life’s challenges alone. Things can renew and grow. Often it is through pain that we realize there’s a greater beauty that lies beyond that.”.

At the end of the trailer, Kate completed the Three Peaks Challenge and reunited with husband Prince William and their three children: Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, 7.

Kate has been taking her ongoing cancer recovery one step at a time.

“I was told in hospital that my recovery would not be linear, there will be good days and bad days, that it will take time,” she told Country Life in a profile published earlier this month. “This is the same in nature. It shows us that periods of rest, reflection and renewal are equally as vital as moments of growth.”.
