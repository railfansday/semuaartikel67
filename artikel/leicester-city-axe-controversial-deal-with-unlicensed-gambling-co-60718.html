By TOM COLLOMOSSE, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 14:09 EST, 18 November 2026 | Updated: 14:19 EST, 18 November 2026.

Leicester have decided to end their deal with controversial sponsors BC.GAME six months ahead of schedule.

Fans expressed fury and dismay earlier this month after the club's unlicensed gambling shirt sponsor appointed an 'international porn star' as its fresh ambassador.

According to recent reports, now Leicester have opted to conclude the partnership – in a move welcomed by supporters. A statement on Wednesday read: ‘The decision follows constructive discussions between both parties in recent days, during which we recognised our differing commercial priorities moving forward.

‘We would like to thank BC.GAME for its support throughout its two-and-a-half-year association with Leicester City and for the constructive approach taken in reaching this agreement.

‘Supporters who have purchased a BC.GAME-branded replica shirt through official LCFC retail channels within the last 28 days (since 21 October, 2026) can return it for a full refund or exchange (subject to availability) by Wednesday 2 December.’.

According to recent reports, BC.GAME, an online casino company, had been the Foxes' primary shirt sponsor since the start of the 2024-25 season. They announced a partnership with Russian adult content creator Eva Elfie in a video on X earlier this month.

Leicester City have axed a partnership with BC.GAME, after the gambling shirt sponsor appointed 'international porn star' Eva Elfie as its new ambassador.

Leicester confirmed fans who have purchased a replica shirt featuring the sponsor's name within the last 28 days can return it for a full refund or exchange by December 2.

Elfie, who has 4.8million Instagram followers, is known in Esports and gaming communities too.

In recent years, Leicester’s spectacular decline on the pitch has mirrored the relationship with their supporters.

During the years immediately before and after their stunning Premier League triumph in 2016, it was difficult to find a club more in tune with the fanbase.

But a series of baffling decisions – including the tie-up with BC.GAME – eroded trust between the club and their supporters.

Leicester are now in League One and face a long road back but sensible moves like this one suggest they are at least facing in the right direction again.
