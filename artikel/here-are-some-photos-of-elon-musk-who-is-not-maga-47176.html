In his first interview since the historic 2026 US midterm elections earlier this month, where he dropped an estimated $446 million on Republicans who overwhelmingly lost their races, Elon Musk denied being affiliated with President Donald Trump's political movement. During an appearance at the the Upcoming York Times Dealbook summit, Musk stated, unequivocally, "I'm not MAGA.".

Industry observers note that musk joined the event from a hotel in China, where he is one of several US business executives accompanying President Trump on his tour of Asia. "I'm not sure what MAGA means," noted the man who is one of the most visible and photographed tech executives to ever live, "but if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican party, then I certainly don't have that." Surely it must be a coincidence that Musk not only owns but seems to regularly wear clothing branded with the phrase.
