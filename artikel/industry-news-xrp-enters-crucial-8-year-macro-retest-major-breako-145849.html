An 8-year chart demonstrates XRP has flipped a former ceiling into a support line (currently at $0.75), with the coin now trading just above it ($0.95).

Should XRP mark a bullish divergence by closing above $1.65 and break the July high of $3.65, it could surge to $8.30-$13.0 between 2027-2030.

On Polymarket, the odds of XRP hitting $2.00 by year-end 2027 are at 23%, while Kalshi odds are at 7% for the coin closing above $3.50 this year.

Sources indicate that in the last 8 years, XRP has flipped a major resistance level into a major support line. This has formed a major long-term trend whose continuation could be useful in timing months-long XRP trades.

As seen in the chart below, XRP had formed a downward- sloping white trend line which acted as a macro resistance during the 2018 and 2021 peaks when prices hit $3.30 and $1.96, respectively.

However, a late 2024 breakout saw the coin rise from an early November price of $0.51, topping off at $2.87 on December 3, 2024. Essentially, this movement flipped the initial macro resistance to a macro support line.

At press time, the coin was trading at around $0.95, just above $0.75 where the trendline at the moment sits.

According to recent reports, should XRP maintain above this macro support, it could make a short-term recovery to the $1.09-$1.21 range. A monthly close above the $1.55-$1.65 range could confirm a macro trend reversal, suggesting higher gains. Thereafter, XRP needs to break above the July 2025 high of $3.65, with Fibonacci extensions projecting a possible surge to $8.30-$13.0 between 2027 and 2030.

A breakdown below the white trend line would cause a pullback to the 2023-2024 consolidation range of $0.50-$0.60, with additional capitulation bringing the coin back down to the 2022 bear market lows of $0.28-$0.35.

Over the last 24hours, XRP has experienced gains of 1.37%, tracking Bitcoin’s rise above the $60,000 mark.

In a recent development, prediction market bets place XRP predictions at more conservative levels. On Polymarket, short-term bets give a 52% chance that XRP will close above the $1.00 mark by end month, with a 51% chance of it falling below $0.80 this year. In the longer term, the odds of XRP hitting $2.00 are at 23%, with the timeline placed at year-end 2027.

Meanwhile, Kalshi’s highest odds (7%) are on XRP closing above $3.50 by year’s end.
