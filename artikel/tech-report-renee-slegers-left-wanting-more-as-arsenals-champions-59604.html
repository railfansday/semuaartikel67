Arsenal head coach Renee Slegers admitted her side had been outmuscled by a clinical Lyon side after a 2-0 defeat at Borehamwood dented hopes of direct qualification for the Champions League quarter-finals.

Lyon were yet to concede a goal in Europe this season and had won all of their nine domestic league matches so far.

The French side made the most of a bright start to take a 24th-minute lead through Jule Brand, and should have gone into half-time with a bigger advantaged but Marie-Antoinette Katoto – top scorer in the Champions League this season with seven goals – blazed over from six yards.

Although Arsenal were improved early in the second half, with Caitlin Foord stretching Lyon down the flanks, clear chances were still hard to come by.

Lyon added a second against the run of play in the 57th minute when Melchie Dumornay drilled a shot into the top corner following another blistering counter attack.

“It was a tough game, it was a challenge,” Slegers said, quoted on the Arsenal club website.

In a recent development, “Especially the first half, also parts of the second half, the game becomes what they are good at, it was physical, it was fast. They are clinical with their moments, you see the quality they have.

“The second half, we dominated the game better, in possession, spent more time in their half, (but) still didn’t create clear cut chances.”.

Arsenal – who had battled to a 1-1 draw at holders Barcelona last time out – slipped to a first European defeat this season and ended the night in eighth place.

Lyon sit joint top with Barcelona on 13 points and already assured of a place in the quarter-finals ahead of the final round of league stage games.

Arsenal will be away to Inter Milan and could yet still avoid having to go into the play-off round if other results also go their way.

“It is the three points in Milan, that is everything we can control,” Slegers said. “That is what we go for, and then we will see what the other teams do.”.
