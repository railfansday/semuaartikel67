The only thing that feels more like Thanksgiving than an exquisitely burnished roast turkey is the (sometimes chaotic) assemblage of loved ones seated around it. For your tablescape this year, take inspiration from the beautifully unique cast of characters that make a family a family.

According to recent reports, she just stepped off a red eye from the substantial city, but she looks like she came from a gallery opening—and her place setting is as bold and eclectic as her fashion sense. Bulbous postmodern ceramic plates, '90s Art Deco revival flatware from Japan, and a hand-beaded placemat are exactly the things she’d be into. The button-shaped aluminum coaster is seldom occupied, since she prefers to keep the martini glass right where it belongs: in her hand.

Piano Cocktail Glass (Set of 2).

She’s 25 going on 70. In between baking all of the pies for the major dinner, she learned lace-making from a TikTok tutorial. She sews, she antiques, and she self-publishes Jane Austen fanfic online. The cheeky cotton bottle apron and pearl-tipped doily on her table might be brand latest from Gohar World, but they pair exquisitely with the pewter flatware she scored at an estate sale. Quilted floral placemats and plates adorned with dainty, hand-painted bows from The Six Bells, a store leading the revival of country cottage decor, evoke the pastoral, bucolic lifestyle she yearns for (as long as she can keep her 10-step skin-care routine).

Herzerlmuster Scalloped Placemats (Set of 2).

The combo of unbridled energy and still-developing motor skills means the Gen-Alpha kids are best served by a spread of disposable and shatterproof items. And, seeing that it’ll take some clever coaxing to pull these little gremlins away from their screens, their table must match the intense digital stimuli they’re accustomed to. Loop-de-loop silly straws, melamine plates, and acrylic forks and cups in as many colors as possible are the bare minimum to keep minds forged in a Cocomelon crucible engaged for the total duration of a Thanksgiving meal. Despite the visual chaos, their table might be more tranquil than the adults’.

Prue Leith Melamine Dinnerware Set and Tumblers.

Mammut Children's Chair and Table.

Sources indicate that thanksgiving might be the only time you see this elusive individual, as he otherwise spends the year fussing around his DIY Earthship in the desert outside of Santa Fe. His place setting is filled with natural materials, like these Shigaraki-style earthenware plates and zero-waste Belgian linen placemats. If he starts defending his dedication to composting toilets a little too enthusiastically, fill his hand-carved wooden mug with some herbal tea and he’ll settle down fine.

Jasper Linen Placements (Set of 4).

A matriarch par excellence, she’s been holding the family together for decades. Her demeanor might be as breezy as a Major Sur afternoon, but she means business when it comes to hosting Thanksgiving. Let it be known: She had a Nancy Meyers kitchen long before Nancy Meyers picked up a camera. To steal her vibe, invest in an Italian table cloth festooned with vines, hand-painted ceramic wicker plates, and bamboo-stemmed wine glasses. Place cards, arranged for maximum harmony, should bear each name in handwritten cursive. Miss Manners would be so proud.
