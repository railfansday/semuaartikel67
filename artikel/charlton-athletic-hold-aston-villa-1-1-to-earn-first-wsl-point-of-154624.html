It has been revealed that charlton Athletic drew 1-1 with Aston Villa in the Women’s Super League on Sunday 22 November, at The Oakwood in Crayford, ending a run of 10 straight league defeats to start the season. Flannery put the hosts ahead from the penalty spot in the 17th minute before Parker levelled in the 71st.

Industry observers note that the draw gives Karen Hills’ side their first point of the campaign after 11 matches. It came despite Charlton having only 13% of the ball.

Lined up in a 5-4-1, Charlton struck first. Flannery converted from 12 yards to give them a 1-0 half-time lead, and the hosts were the more threatening side in front of goal across the afternoon. They had 12 shots to Villa’s 8 and built expected goals of 1.74 against 0.9.

According to recent reports, accuracy was the concern. Six of Charlton’s attempts went wide, compared with one for Villa, and each side managed 3 shots on target. Flannery, who was booked for a foul in the 56th minute, was replaced after 82 minutes.

Villa controlled the ball with 87% possession and completed 438 passes from 549, against 205 from 306 for Charlton. They also won 9 corners to the hosts’ 2. The breakthrough finally arrived in the 71st minute through Parker.

That goal earned Parker a rating of 7, the highest on the pitch, in a 72-minute outing. Oriane Jean-François, rated 6.91, was cautioned for a foul in the first minute of stoppage time. Referee Megan Wilson highlighted one yellow card to each side.

Recent findings suggest that the result is a modest improvement on the last meeting between the clubs. Villa won 4-1 in the WSL Cup on 11 December 2024.

In a recent development, charlton are 14th with 1 point and a goal difference of -25. Their 1-0 win over Ipswich Town is the only victory in their last six matches in all competitions. Before Sunday they had lost 2-0 to West Ham, and earlier conceded six against Chelsea.

Recent findings suggest that aston Villa are 8th with 14 points and a goal difference of -5. They have now gone two games without defeat, following a 1-0 win over Birmingham City with this draw.

Both sides are back in league action on Sunday 13 December. Charlton travel to Tottenham Hotspur, while Villa visit West Ham.
