Leeds United defender James Justin has warned Chelsea have talent “spilling out” as the sides prepare to meet in the Premier League.

The old rivals did battle in the Carabao Cup in September and Chelsea emerged as 6-3 victors at Stamford Bridge.

Xabi Alonso’s men are due to host Leeds again tomorrow (November 21) and Justin has made it clear a stern test lies ahead.

He told Premier League Studios, as quoted by the official Leeds website: “I feel like it will be a bit of a different side than what we faced in the League Cup tie, and we know they are a team that likes to play in the small spaces.

“They have got talent spilling out and you have to keep control of that but also try and take advantage of somewhere they might not be as tight, obviously.

“They lost their last match against Sunderland so hopefully we can look at that match and learn a few things.”.

Leeds returned to winning ways prior to the international break, defeating Chelsea’s London rivals Tottenham Hotspur 1-0.

Daniel Farke’s men may well be the underdogs but just two points separate the fifth-placed Blues and ninth-placed Whites.

Justin said: “It has been nice to rest and recharge a little bit, and work on a few things on the training pitch. We are ready to go and pick things up again.

“I would say from last season we have come on leaps and bounds and the confidence that everyone has in the squad is a lot more compared to this time last season. We have still got a way to go but we have had a solid start.”.

Leeds showed ambition with their summer transfer window recruitment, splashing out a club-record £40m on England international goalkeeper James Trafford.

They also bolstered in the centre of defence by recruiting Tarik Muharemović and Nico Elvedi, who have been partnering Justin in a back three.

Sources indicate that justin said: “We obviously added some quality players in the summer. We are looking at this season and first and foremost to cement our place in the Premier League. Who knows what can happen after that.
