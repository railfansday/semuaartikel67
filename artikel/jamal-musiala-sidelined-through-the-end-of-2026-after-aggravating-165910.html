Jamal Musiala sidelined through the end of 2026 after aggravating muscle injury

According to recent reports, bayern Munich attacking midfielder Jamal Musiala has suffered another injury setback just days after returning to competitive football. According to Kicker, the 23-year-old appears to have aggravated the muscle injury that previously sidelined him for nearly two months. The German international will consequently miss Saturday’s Bundesliga away fixture against Hamburger SV.

Musiala suffered a muscle-fibre tear while warming up for Germany’s Nations League encounter against the Netherlands in September. After gradually working his way back to fitness, the Bayern star finally returned to competitive action in last Friday’s 1-1 draw against Köln. He also made a brief appearance in Wednesday’s 2-1 Champions League defeat against Lille.

Sources indicate that kicker reports that the original muscle injury has apparently flared up again, although the full extent of the latest setback and the length of Musiala’s forthcoming absence remain unclear. Fußballtransfers claims Musiala’s absence will stretch out for several more weeks. Sky Germany’s Florian Plettenberg is reporting that Musiala will miss 4-6 weeks.

Recent findings suggest that the latest development comes during an exceptionally difficult period for Musiala, who has also been dealing with a neurological condition. Kicker and Sport Bild both report that the 23-year-old experienced another absence episode on Monday, November 23, his second reported recurrence since collapsing during August’s preparation campaign. Musiala has consequently made just six competitive appearances this season, completing the full 90 minutes on only one occasion.

His ongoing health difficulties have also prompted considerable discussion surrounding his future at Bayern. Honorary president Uli Hoeneß and former CEO Karl-Heinz Rummenigge recently expressed concerns about the midfielder’s ability to regain his previous performance levels. Earlier this week, Bayern also confirmed that constructive discussions had taken place with Musiala’s representatives. The club emphasized that the player’s health remained the priority and that both parties intended to continue their dialogue confidentially.

For Kompany, Musiala’s latest absence also creates another selection headache amid a mounting injury crisis in Bayern’s attacking department. Michael Olise has also been ruled out of Saturday’s encounter with Hamburg. The French international continues to recover from bruising to his left foot sustained against Köln. Harry Kane, Serge Gnabry, and Ismael Saibari remain sidelined as well, leaving Bayern seriously depleted in attack. Luis Díaz at least returned to action against Lille following his own injury layoff.
