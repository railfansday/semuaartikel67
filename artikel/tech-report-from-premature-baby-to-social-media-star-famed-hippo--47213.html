The Cincinnati Zoo in Ohio says its beloved hippopotamus Fiona has died just days after swallowing a knitted hat that slipped into her enclosure.

Recent findings suggest that fiona was born premature at the Cincinnati Zoo in 2017 and at first struggled to survive. At birth, she weighed 13 kg (29 lbs), which the zoo said was about half the previous lowest recorded birth weight for her species. She reached 1,000 lbs in 2018.

Industry observers note that over the years, Fiona became a favourite among visitors and a star on social media. This video captures moments from her life, from her early days as a calf to celebrating her fifth birthday and receiving treatment just a few days ago.
