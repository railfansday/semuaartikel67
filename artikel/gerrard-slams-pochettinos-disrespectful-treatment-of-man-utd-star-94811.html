Gerrard Slams Pochettino's 'Disrespectful' Treatment of Man Utd Star

Things couldn't have gone much worse for Mauricio Pochettino in his first outing as Manchester United manager. He watched on as his new side were thumped 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield.

In a recent development, the Argentine's pre-game aims promptly went out the window when Lenny Yoro picked up a red card inside the first 10 minutes. He was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak as the last defender, and Dominik Szoboszlai smashed home the resulting free-kick.

Sources indicate that down to 10 men, the away team buckled completely, with Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz scoring to deliver all three points for Liverpool. Pochettino's 4-0 loss is the joint-worst debut result any Man Utd manager has suffered in club history.

Clearly, Pochettino has a lot of work to do if he is to improve Man Utd. Beyond the result, he's perhaps already made an early misstep with his treatment of one senior star. Steven Gerrard certainly thinks so.

Speaking on The Overlap, Gerrard claimed that the decision to drop Harry Maguire in favour of Matthijs de Ligt was wrong. He began:.

According to recent reports, "I thought he got it wrong. I thought he got it wrong in the back line. I think to start someone in a game of that magnitude, it's the biggest game in the country. All eyes are on it all over the world.".

Gerrard then claimed Pochettino's call demonstrated a lack of respect for Maguire:.

"Forget the youngsters that are maybe not ready at the moment. Matthijs de Ligt's been out for a year. Harry Maguire's their best defender at the moment. He should have started the game.

In a recent development, "He's one who is rolling his sleeves up and having a go. Okay, he hasn't been perfect, but to not play him and put De Ligt in before him, I thought it was a bit disrespectful.".

In a recent development, due to the red card, Maguire came on in the first half and Gerrard praised the Englishman's efforts, suggesting they were enough to prove he should have started: "And he made a difference when he came on. And he was one who was putting his body on the line.

In a recent development, "He made a great block, I think it was around the 38th, 39th minute, which stopped a certain goal. And I've watched Manchester United a lot this season, and he's one who's having a go.".

Pochettino may well have his eye on the January transfer market as a way to improve his struggling squad. After all, defenders Maguire, Lisandro Martinez and Luke Shaw will all enter the final six months of their contracts this winter.

Sources indicate that however, Gerrard has questioned the club's chances of landing an upgrade in the short term. He explained: "Who's going to let world-class centre-halves go in January? Unless someone's prepared to pay astronomical money. That's what's going to have to happen.".

According to recent reports, as such, Pochettino may well have to make do for the rest of the season with what he has available to him already. If that's the case, he'd best hope Maguire didn't find his treatment as 'disrespectful' as Gerrard suggests.
