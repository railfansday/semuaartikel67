Breaking: Starburst adds context support to give AI agents consistent access to business data definitions

UPDATED 08:00 EST / NOVEMBER 18 2026.

Starburst Data Inc. is expanding its data federation platform into business context management with the introduction of Context Studio, a latest capability designed to help artificial intelligence agents produce more accurate and trustworthy answers from information distributed across enterprise systems.

Context Studio collects business definitions from existing data catalogs, business intelligence tools, metric stores and documents, making them available alongside data when AI agents respond to prompts. The system is intended to eliminate conflicting definitions that can cause AI agents to produce incorrect results.

The latest capability addresses a problem the company encountered as customers began deploying its Aida natural-language data assistant in production environments, stated Starburst co-founder and Chief Executive Justin Borgman.

“We’ve found that as you get into production, the biggest glitch is context because you need to trust the answers the agent gives back,” he mentioned.

It has been revealed that the challenge is particularly acute in large organizations where different departments may use different definitions of common business metrics, such as revenue or active customers. Without a consistent understanding of those terms, AI models can produce plausible but inaccurate answers.

“When those definitions conflict, the [large language model] doesn’t know any better; it’s just going to guess,” Borgman noted. “That’s what leads to bad answers and an erosion of trust.”.

In a recent development, context Studio builds on Starburst’s existing data products architecture, which allows organizations to create curated views spanning multiple data sources. Those products incorporate business definitions, metadata and access controls that can be applied consistently when agents retrieve information.

Unlike conventional semantic layers that require organizations to consolidate definitions within a single platform, Context Studio federates existing definitions without requiring customers to move or rebuild their underlying data.

It has been revealed that built-in approval workflows resolve conflicting definitions, and a feedback mechanism is provided through which agents can propose improvements. Human reviewers approve changes before regression testing checks their impact on other queries.

Borgman characterized the expansion as an evolution of Starburst’s longstanding strategy of querying distributed data without centralizing it.

“We think context is going to live everywhere, just like we think data will live in multiple places,” he said. “Our approach treats [context] as a first-class principle in our design.”.

Starburst hasn’t yet disclosed quantitative evidence of Context Studio’s impact on AI accuracy or productivity. The company remarked it is collecting benchmark data from private preview customers.

According to recent reports, the company is also emphasizing traceability to increase confidence in AI-generated answers. Context Studio records which data sources agents access, the SQL queries they generate and the information used to produce responses. Access controls remain attached to underlying data products.

“Context is going to increase the probability of a good answer, but there’s also a need to trace how it got to that answer,” Borgman said.

It has been revealed that an agent can interview domain experts to capture business knowledge and convert it into draft data products. Starburst is also extending its Aida data assistant to answer questions across multiple data products and introducing an agent that automatically generates dashboards.

It has been revealed that other platform enhancements include Mission Control for monitoring usage and costs, automated Apache Iceberg maintenance, a bring-your-own-cloud deployment option and graphics processing unit-accelerated querying. Starburst claims its recently introduced Torin query engine delivers up to sevenfold performance improvements in tech giant benchmarks.

According to recent reports, context Studio is available in private preview for Starburst Galaxy and Starburst Enterprise. A public preview is planned for January, with general availability expected in the first quarter of 2027. Pricing hasn’t been finalized.
