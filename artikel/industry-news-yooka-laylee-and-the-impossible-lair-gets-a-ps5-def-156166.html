Playtonic has revealed Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair is being treated to a newly introduced Definitive Edition on PS5, and it's right around the corner.

Sources indicate that the upcoming version launches on 15th December 2026, and anyone who owns a digital PS4 version of the game gets a free upgrade. It appears anyone with a physical copy won't get the upgrade.

On base PS5 and PS5 Pro, the latest edition will support a 4K resolution at 120fps, better lighting and ambient occlusion effects, higher-fidelity shadows, and two orchestrated soundtracks. Also, if you don't already own it, the Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack is automatically bundled in with the PS5 version.

Industry observers note that one of the best platforming adventures of its generation is back, enhanced for PlayStation 5. Yooka & Laylee return for a full-scale 2.5D adventure, with a 3D Overworld that's an adventure in its own right, levels you can alter to create newly introduced ways to play, and Capital B's Impossible Lair waiting to test your skills.

A 2D and top-down side game that accompanies the main Yooka-Laylee game, the Impossible Lair went down really well upon its original launch. It's got an 82 rating on Metacritic, and in our own review, we called it an "improvement" on the original adventure.
