Virgil van Dijk has been one of Liverpool's best players for a while now.

The Dutchman has been the Reds' leader ever since Jordan Henderson left for pastures latest.

Since then, he's led the club to a Premier League win and has cemented himself as one of the Premier League's best-ever centre-backs.

However, now that he's 35 years old, it's clear that he's now coming to the end of his career.

This wouldn't really be all that much of a problem for Liverpool, that is, if his contract wasn't going to expire in June 2027.

But, because it is, they have a huge decision to make with the Reds' stance on the situation now being made more than clear.

Liverpool don't want to offer Virgil van Dijk another two-year deal.

Van Dijk is still one of the first names on Andoni Iraola's team sheet.

Most people thought that the Dutchman would have dropped off by now and may have potentially made way for a younger player.

But, so far, he's performed extremely well and has brought Jeremy Jacquet's game on leaps and bounds.

At the moment, he's Liverpool's highest-paid player and, since he's going to be 36 by the start of next season, a decision needs to be made on how long the Reds think the defender has left at this level.

That's why this latest update from Sport Italia is rather concerning.

"Gerry Cardinale is following the Reds captain's situation and dreams of a big signing this summer. The sticking point is a renewal: the Dutchman is asking for a two-year contract, while Liverpool still seem unconvinced," they wrote.

"Virgil van Dijk's future could become one of the most interesting situations of the upcoming summer transfer window. AC Milan is in the background, with Gerry Cardinale eyeing the possibility of bringing one of the most authoritative defenders in the international scene to San Siro.

"But before imagining the Dutch centre-back in a Rossoneri shirt, Liverpool must be taken into account.

"This is precisely where the crux of the matter lies: Van Dijk wants a two-year extension, while Liverpool do not appear willing, at least for now, to fully grant his request.".

Liverpool can't just offer Virgil van Dijk a one-year deal.

Van Dijk may be getting older now but, there's no way that he should only be getting a one-year deal.

The Dutchman has helped shore up Liverpool's defence massively since he arrived and, even though Mohamed Salah was let go for free last summer, Van Dijk shouldn't be given that same fate.

It's clear that another major rebuild is on its way to Anfield soon so, keeping Van Dijk, who Liverpool know can do a job, as one of their main centre-backs makes a lot of sense.

Obviously, if he asks for a pay rise then the Reds can't accept but, for a lower weekly wage, it feels like Liverpool would be silly to not offer him a deal.

Another two-years added onto his contract feels like it makes a lot of sense, especially since Giovanni Leoni, Ifeanyi Ndukwe and Mor Talla Ndiaye still haven't proven that they can plug the gap left by their captain yet.

Here's hoping that Liverpool wise up and give Van Dijk a fresh two-year deal.
