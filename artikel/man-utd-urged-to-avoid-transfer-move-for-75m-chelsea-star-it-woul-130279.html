Manchester United have been urged to avoid pursuing a move for wantaway Chelsea full-back Malo Gusto in the January transfer window.

United made a disappointing start to life under their new head coach Mauricio Pochettino, shipping four goals as they fell to a dispiriting defeat against Liverpool.

The scale of the task at hand will now be all the more apparent to Pochettino, with his side now winless in nine Premier League matches and just one point off bottom-of-the-table Crystal Palace.

The upcoming January transfer window may provide a precious opportunity for United to add some much-needed defensive depth to their squad.

Calvin Bassey is among the rumoured targets at centre-back, while Chelsea full-back Gusto has also been linked with a move to Old Trafford.

But Mikael Silvestre urged his former club against a move for Gusto, insisting Pochettino instead needs players who are in form and can hit the ground running straight away.

‘Manchester United should definitely not be looking to sign Malo Gusto,’ Silvestre told BestBettingBonuses.

‘He is not the right player for this current United defence, especially with the confidence so low.

‘Gusto is in a difficult place mentally. He had a torrid game with France during the last international window, and he’s not in the Chelsea starting eleven.
