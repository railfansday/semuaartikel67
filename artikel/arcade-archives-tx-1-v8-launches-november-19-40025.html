Industry observers note that arcade Archives 2 TX-1 V.8 will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and Switch 2, and Arcade Archives TX-1 V.8 for PlayStation 4 and Switch, on November 19, developer Hamster announced. The Arcade Archives 2 version will cost $9.99 / 1,100 yen, while Arcade Archives version will cost $7.99 / 837 yen. An upgrade from Arcade Archives to Arcade Archives 2 will be available for $2.99 / 330 yen.

Here is an overview of the game, via Hamster:.

TX-1 V.8 is a racing game released by TATSUMI in 1984.

The popular TX-1 has returned, featuring a variety of upgrades to make the game even easier to play!

You can now recover if your car rolls over or spins out, and it’s even possible to jump right over opponents ahead of you.

In a recent development, the eight countries featured in the Grand Prix stages are all latest! Enjoy the fresh scenery of each location as you race toward the finish line!

The Arcade Archives series is developed with the concept of faithfully reproducing classic arcade games. It also includes convenient features such as difficulty and screen settings, rewind, rapid-fire, and multiple save slots. In addition, players can compete for the best scores with players around the world through online rankings.

According to recent reports, arcade Archives 2 series introduces a brand-newly introduced mode called Time Attack Mode, in addition to the Original Mode, Hi Score Mode, and Caravan Mode from Arcade Archives. Time Attack Mode focuses on how swiftly you can complete the game, regardless of the score you achieve.

What’s more, with VRR (Variable Refresh Rate) support, the gameplay can be reproduced even more accurately, closer to the original arcade experience.

Please enjoy these iconic arcade games that defined an era with the Arcade Archives series!
