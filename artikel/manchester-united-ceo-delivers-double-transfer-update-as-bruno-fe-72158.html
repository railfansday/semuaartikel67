Manchester United CEO Omar Berrada has confirmed the club is in active negotiations with Bruno Fernandes over a upcoming contract.

He also admitted United did “absolutely everything” to attempt to convince JJ Gabriel to stay at the club.

United captain Fernandes’ current contract expires at the end of this season, and it has been reported that he has already rejected two offers of a new deal.

As the club has struggled in recent years, Fernandes has regularly been able to nick results with inspiring performances. Last season alone, he was responsible for nine goals and 21 assists in the Premier League.

As such, Berrada is desperate to tie him down to a new deal, but kept his cards close to his chest when discussing a potential new contract.

Speaking in a rare interview with Sky Sports, he stated: “We have a tremendous amount of respect for Bruno. We're very fortunate to have him as a captain.

Sources indicate that “What he's done for this team over the last six, seven years is absolutely incredible. Last year he won, I think, all the individual awards you can win.

“And we're very proud to have him as captain. It's true that we're having conversations about his upcoming contract, but I prefer to keep that private. I think it's something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club.”.

It has been revealed that one player United have failed to convince to stay, meanwhile, is JJ Gabriel. The 16-year-old winger is widely considered to be one of the game’s brightest talents, but left United stunned earlier this year when he lodged a surprise transfer request.

Sources indicate that he has since signed with a fresh agency and remarked his goodbyes to United, with Real Madrid expected to win the race to take him on. Gabriel will leave United without having made a first-team appearance.
