Porsche won’t unveil its electric 718 Boxster until 2027, but we were able to slide behind the wheel of a camouflaged prototype for a quick track spin.

Whereas most EVs shove their batteries under the floor, the Boxster EV puts its battery pack behind the seats, where the gas engine used to be.

This gives the electric Boxster a 60 percent rear weight distribution, not far off the gas-fed 718’s 55 percent rear bias.

After a few laps at the Porsche Experience Center Los Angeles, we can confirm this EV handles like a Boxster should.

We don’t know any other details, really: Porsche won’t say anything about range, power, price—any of that.
