Ahoy folks, and welcome to another edition of Box Art Brawl!

Looking back on last week, we checked out Dr. Mario & Puzzle League for the GBA to celebrate its recent addition to the NSO library. Neither design in this instance was particularly enthralling, and this is perhaps evident in the final result. Japan just about managed to claim a win with 51% of the vote, with North America / Europe making up the remaining 49%. Oof, so close!

According to recent reports, we're sticking with the GBA this week to peruse the box art designs for Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga. Released in 2003, this was the debut title in the spin-off RPG series, and arguably still its best. It was remade for the 3DS (and we did a Box Art Brawl for that one, too), but man, there's something about those GBA visuals that just make it, y'know?

North America and Europe share the same design for this one, so they'll be teaming up against Japan. Let's do this!

It has been revealed that the western design for Superstar Saga is so good, Nintendo essentially did it again with the 3DS version. It's busy, but we love the way Cackletta sort of looms over everything in the background, while the bros themselves strike some pretty sweet action poses. A great composition, overall.

Japan went weird with this one. We've got a completely white background here, broken by the large logo and the bros. That's not quite all though. We do love those silhouettes showing how to use 'A' and 'B' for the protagonists, while the placement of Peach and Cackletta at the top is just so random, we can't help but giggle at it.
