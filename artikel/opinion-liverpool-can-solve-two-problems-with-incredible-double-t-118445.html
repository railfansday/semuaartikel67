Liverpool have many problems that they need to solve among their ranks.

Andoni Iraola's men are fourth in the Premier League table at the moment with seven points separating them and first-placed Arsenal.

Manchester City and Brighton are the two other teams ahead of them with one point between all three clubs.

Liverpool's rocky start to the season really didn't help matters much and is one of the main reasons why they are where they are right now.

However, with the January transfer window now just around the corner, Liverpool could take advantage of it and force their way back up the table.

Barcelona want to sell Gavi and Alejandro Balde in January.

Two of the main positions that the Reds need to upgrade this winter are their left-back and centre-midfield.

Both positions have players among them that haven't met expectations at all this term.

This has led to a lot of underperformance from Liverpool and has forced them to continually experiment with the players that they have at their disposal.

In some cases, players such as Trey Nyoni and Lewis Koumas have massively benefitted from this but, in others, questions remain.

Many different players are on the Reds' shortlist but now, an patch from journalist Juan Jesus may have just confirmed who the Reds' main targets could end up being.

"In addition to Alejandro Balde, Barça are now pushing Gavi out this very winter to fund the signing of a centre-back, seen as the club’s absolute priority," he wrote.

"The main target to bolster the defence is Nico Schlotterbeck, but the current wage bill leaves no room to pull off a move of that magnitude in January.

"To comply with financial fair play rules and try to sign him in this winter window, the board is pushing for two key sales: Balde on one hand, and the bombshell departure of Gavi on the other.".

Gavi and Alejandro Balde would be two perfect signings for Liverpool.

Most Liverpool fans will know that Balde has been linked with the Reds for a while now.

The Spaniard has been left out of Hansi Flick's intends this term with the La Liga giants making it clear since the summer window slammed shut that the 23-year-old had no future left at the Camp Nou.

His natural position is left-back but, he's also been used as a right-back in the past.

Gavi is a completely different kettle of fish altogether. The 22-year-old has played in 179 games throughout his career so far with his best position being a central midfielder.

During his time in Barcelona's first-team squad, he's helped Pedri boss many different midfields and has contributed to 28 goals across all competitions.

Right now, it's unclear how much either of them would cost but, since the Reds' relationship with Barcelona is strong, a deal could definitely be agreed.
