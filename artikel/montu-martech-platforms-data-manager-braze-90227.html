Industry observers note that montu is Australia's largest healthtech business with a focus on alternative healthcare. Founded in 2019, it supports patients, doctors and pharmacies through its Alternaleaf clinic, offers accredited healthcare education to clinicians via its SAGED platform, and provides dispensing solutions to pharmacies across Australia through Leafio. Montu is also the founding member of Cannabis Council Australia, a not-for-profit advocacy body that advances legislative change across the healthcare landscape. Guided by our Culture of Care, our mission is to make alternative healthcare more affordable and accessible for the millions who could benefit. This commitment was recently recognised at the Cannabiz Awards, where Montu took home Best Use of Technology for Alternaleaf's IDC and Distributor of the Year for Leafio.

It has been revealed that sitting at the heart of CRM architecture and data engineering, you will support the technical platform function, directing the design, scaling, and integration of internal martech infrastructure across Montu Group’s ecosystems. Your remit spans platform architecture, data flow integrity, technical roadmaps, and the delivery of complex integrations to minimise engineering reliance.

In this role, you will build the robust technical foundation that powers scalable, automated, and hyper-personalised communications. You will support the configuration and governance of the martech stack, ensuring operational resilience and enabling advanced segmentation through sophisticated event and attribute schemas and seamless data orchestration. Hands on experience with Braze is essential.
