By MICHAEL PAVITT, SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 17:58 EST, 20 November 2026 | Updated: 10:47 EST, 21 November 2026.

Bayern Munich fear Harry Kane will be ruled out for months after suffering an ankle injury in their Bundesliga draw against Cologne - with the striker revealing he will only return for 'the business end of the season'.

Kane was forced off just 20 minutes into the match at the Allianz Arena following a challenge from Joel Schmied.

The defender had won the ball but caught Kane with his follow through on his ankle.

Kane had initially tried to play on after treatment, before eventually needing to be replaced.

The Ballon d'Or winner was upbeat on Saturday morning however, sharing a message with his fans that stressed his readiness to come back better than ever.

'The highs and lows of life!' Kane captioned a picture of himself wearing a protective boot. 'Obviously very disappointed to get this injury but as always it's an opportunity to show resilience and improvement going in 2027.

Harry Kane was forced off during Bayern Munich's draw against Cologne on Friday night.

Bayern confirmed the England captain is set to be out for months following the injury.

But despite his injury, Kane was upbeat as he spoke to fans for the first time on Saturday (Source: Harry Kane/X).

'I'll be back fresh and ready to go for the business end of the season!

'"Tough times don't last, tough people do" - my guy (Julian Edelman) told me this one!' Kane added, quoting the NFL icon.

'Thank you for all the messages! Alles gut (red heart emoji, strong arm emoji).'.

Speaking post-match, Bayern sporting director Max Eberl revealed that Kane has suffered ligament damage.

Industry observers note that A Bayern club statement confirmed the blow, reading: 'Harry Kane suffered a tear of the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle in Bayern Munich’s Bundesliga home match against 1. FC Köln on Friday evening.

'This was confirmed by an examination by Bayern Munich’s medical department.

In a recent development, 'The 33-year-old will therefore be out for the next few months. Get well soon Harry.'.

Kane's absence will be a major blow for Bayern, with the Ballon d'Or winner having scored ten goals in 16 matches so far this season.

Bayern, who are already without Luis Diaz, Saibari and Serge Gnabry, also saw Michael Olise come off at half-time.

The German giants later confirmed Olise had suffered a 'contusion of the forefoot', with the Frenchman set to undergo treatment in the coming days.

It remains to be seen whether Kane - who became England's most-capped player last week when making his 126th appearance in the 2-1 win over Croatia - is at risk of missing the Nations League quarter-final against Norway in March.

Vincent Kompany, who was back in the dugout for the first time since his collapse last month, admitted other players will have to step up for Bayern in the absence of star names such as Kane.

'These are world-class players, you can't replace them, but we have the mentality and we have to bring that now,' Kompany said, as per Bild.

'We need incredible performances from players who may not yet be used to this pressure at FC Bayern.

Bayern were held to a 1-1 draw by Cologne on Vincent Kompany's return to the touchline.

It has been revealed that 'We will all try together to make this situation a positive one for ourselves. FC Bayern does this time and time again, and that's what we want to do now too.'.

Bayern were ultimately held to a 1-1 draw by Cologne, with Tottenham loanee Mikey Moore giving the visitors a shock first half lead at the Allianz Arena.

Nathaniel Brown drew Bayern level before half-time, but the hosts were unable to find a winner on Kompany's return.

The draw gives Borussia Dortmund the chance to move to the top of the Bundesliga table on Saturday.
