The holidays are a time for families to come together, but for Guy Fieri, they’re also a reminder of the loved ones no longer with us.

It has been revealed that the chef and television personality posted a photo of himself and his late father, Jim, on Instagram to commemorate what would have been his 84th birthday. It’s been nearly three years since his father passed away following a long battle with pancreatic cancer.

It has been revealed that in the caption, Fieri described Jim as his hero and expressed how much he still misses him. Fans swiftly filled the comments with condolences and support.

Industry observers note that “Sending love on this special day,” one user wrote. On Facebook, another commented, “I hope your memories of your dad comfort you today. He must have been so proud of you.”.

Others shared their own happy memories of Jim. “I miss the stories and conversations with this man,” one person wrote. “He was the best!!” Another recalled, “Jim was a true romantic. I'll never forget when he made the rose petal trail for your mom.”.

In a recent development, fieri has been open about how deeply the loss affected him. “I wanna call him all the time and ask questions, help me solve issues or celebrate the wins,” he stated in a series of Instagram Stories last year. “Wouldn’t be here if it weren’t for him and it's hard to be here without him.”.

In a recent development, birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays are still hard for Fieri, but he’s also found some comfort in his family. In a 2024 interview with Graham Bensinger, he mentioned, “The funny thing is, out of everything that's happened, it has made me probably 10 times closer to my mom.”.

And judging by the outpouring of love from fans, friends, and loved ones in the comments, the Fieri’s can take comfort knowing Jim left a lasting impression on more people than just his family.
