It has been revealed that fondazione Milan will be the Match Charity Partner for AC Milan vs. Frosinone on Sunday, and Matteo Gabbia has spoken as part of the initiative.

Industry observers note that since 2003, the Foundation has used sport as a tool for change, reaching more than 200,000 young people through 288 projects in Italy and 25 other countries. More than €13m has been invested over the years on various projects to help those that are less fortunate.

Before kick-off on Sunday, the beneficiaries of the various schemes will take a photo with the starting lineup. They will also take part in pitch-side activities alongside Massimo Ambrosini, Daniele Massaro and Matteo Gabbia, who recently visited the Calcio Seduto project. Gabbia spoke at the event.

It has been revealed that “It’s not easy to sum up in just a few minutes what Franco (Baresi) was like as a person and as a player. He was a key figure for the entire AC Milan club, for us kids who are trying to live up to him, and above all for all those people who always told me about his games.

“It’s always exciting for me to hear people talk about Franco and what he achieved with these colours, as a player and as a man.

“I think Franco is a legend. Fondazione Milan embodies all the beauty and importance that Baresi had. I was moved when I met and got to know the kids from the sitting football team last week.

“I’m sure that if Franco had been there, he would have been proud of everything he organized and created for this foundation.”.

A post shared by Fondazione Milan (@fondazionemilan).

“I’m feeling better. It was a strange start to the season, and I was a bit disappointed personally. Hopefully, from the moment I return, it can be a positive season for me too.”.

Industry observers note that the pregame will also honour the memory of Franco Baresi and his bond with Fondazione Milan. He was a dedicated supporter of its projects, including as a member of its Board of Directors. Club employees will take part in the tribute, along with his longtime teammate and friend Daniele Massaro.

At Sunday’s match, fans can continue to support the Foundation’s projects through donations and auctions of players’ shirts, helping create new opportunities for other young people.
