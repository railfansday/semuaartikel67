The record has been scratched amid Aaron Rodgers' "Last Dance.".

It has been revealed that for the first half of the 2026 season, the Pittsburgh Steelers seemed well on their way to making it back to the postseason and giving Rodgers, who is playing in his final season in the NFL, one last kick at the can. The club entered its Week 9 bye at 6-2 on the season and riding a four-game winning streak. Upon their return to action, however, their season has gone sideways.

Recent findings suggest that pittsburgh has now dropped two straight games, including a 19-16 defeat to the Joe Milton-led Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday. Whatever the complications that have led to this skid are, the Steelers need to get them corrected in a relatively quick fashion, as it's not hyperbole to say their season is teetering.

The Week 11 loss dropped them to 6-4 on the season and has them falling from the No. 6 seed down to the No. 7 seed. Despite being on the bye, the Cleveland Browns leapt over them thanks to a tiebreaker. After splitting the regular-season series, Cleveland presently possesses the tiebreaker over Pittsburgh due to its superior record within the AFC North (4-3 vs. 3-3).

On top of jockeying for position with the Browns, the Steelers now need to concern themselves with a few AFC teams just outside the current playoff picture. While their season has been turnt upside down following the fractured fibula to Drake Maye, the Patriots (5-5) are a game behind Pittsburgh and own the head-to-head tiebreaker thanks to a win back in Week 2.

Sources indicate that speaking of tiebreakers, the Steelers' main competition for a wild card spot could end up being the surging Texans. Houston is 4-6 after Week 11, but has been lurking, especially as C.J. Stroud has been enjoying a career year under center. These two AFC clubs will square off in just a couple of weeks from Pittsburgh in Week 13.

In a recent development, before then, the Steelers will host the Denver Broncos, while the Houston Texans will host the Baltimore Ravens. At DraftKings Sportsbook, Pittsburgh is a 2.5-point underdog at home next week, and the Texans are a 6.5-point favorite. If those games go according to the spread, this Week 13 contest sets up Houston with an opportunity to move to 6-6 and gain the head-to-head tiebreaker with a win over the Steelers, who'd tumble down the standings and possibly out of the playoff picture entirely.

Sources indicate that pittsburgh would then finish out the season with games against the Jaguars (road), Ravens (twice), Panthers, and Titans (road). While that's not a gauntlet, no game is guaranteed given the way the Steelers have been playing post-bye.

Right now, Mike McCarthy's club sits at +138 to miss the playoffs. That feels like one of the best bets you can make in the futures market coming out of Week 11.

As J.J. Watt noted on the broadcast, that's not how this is supposed to work.
