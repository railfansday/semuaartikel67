GTA 6 Mamba GT locations: How to restore the third car for Wyman

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tracking down the GTA 6 Mamba GT locations is how you restore the third classic car Wyman asks you to help with. After completing the Transgressor and then the Riata, he'll send you a text with details about fixing this classic 60s roadster.

There are four Mambas to scan with the WAiNK It app in the counties around Port Gellhorn, so this one is a little more involved than the previous cars.

The first Mamba GT is in the garage of a farmhouse to the north of Leonida State Prison, which is southeast of Gellhorn. Head north up the main road to the east of the prison until you spot a private property gate on the left side of the road, opposite a building on the other side with Gruppe6 written on it.

In a recent development, smash the gate and head up the track to the house where you'll find the wreck in the garage to the left of it (you can enter via the backdoor). There's also a little cash in a box here, too.

Industry observers note that the second wrecked car is in the town of Ambrosia, east of Port Gellhorn, near the Xero Gas - Ambrosia station and the purchasable Wyatt Motors garage. Head right down the side of the gas station, turn right into the alley, and continue a little way until you spot a white garage door to your right, next to a big blue garage door. Interact to open the white one and find your Mamba GT to scan.

Sources indicate that the third Mamba GT is sunk in a pool at the Thrillbilly Mud Club—you will have visited here in the story with Wyman, when you met Billy and had a race, east of Port Gellhorn in Frogswater. Travel to the Mud Club - Dirt Track Races nav point, then head to the small pool just east of it to find the car partially submerged but still scannable from the shore.

Recent findings suggest that this last one is by far the hardest to get to. It's located on the north coast of Leonida in Lummox County, in the wreckage of a collapsed wooden bridge, to the east along the coast from the little town of Bonanza. It's also northwest of the Buck Creek Country Store.

Chances are, you won't have been to this area yet, but you can just set a nav marker in Moreau or Bonanza on the map and then work it out when you get there. The broken bridge is easy to spot since it's on the northernmost coastal road. Drop down to the ravine below the bridge to scan the car in the wreckage.

As with the previous cars, once you've found all four, Wyman will take a little time and then call asking you to Buck him $1,500 for his labor. Accept the payment request in the text or do it manually, after which a nav marker will appear to collect the Mamba GT from his scrapyard, same as with the other cars.
