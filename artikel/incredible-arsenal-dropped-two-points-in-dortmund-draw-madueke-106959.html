Arsenal dropped two points in their 1-1 Champions League draw against Borussia Dortmund, so claims Noni Madueke.

The Gunners were dominant in the first half at Emirates Stadium, where Viktor Gyokeres' stunner put them into a 10th-minute lead.

Yet Arsenal could not add to their lead, and Dortmund stepped up in the second half, equalising in the 55th minute through Maximilian Beier.

Jobe Bellingham saw a goal disallowed, but Arsenal came on strong late on, with Gyokeres squandering a golden chance, while the Gunners hit the post twice.

Arsenal's 30 shots are their most on record in a Champions League game (since 2003-04), and the Gunners' most in a game that they failed to win across all competitions since December 2023 v West Ham.

And while Madueke felt some of Arsenal's play was "incredible", he acknowledged Mikel Arteta's team are frustrated.

He told Amazon Prime: "I feel like the performance from us up until the last shot was incredible at times.

Industry observers note that "Definitely feels like two points dropped. A bit of frustration; need to get all three points, I think.".

Gyokeres echoed Madueke's sentiment, though he is confident Arsenal will get better results if they keep on creating so many chances.

"We could have scored in the second half. We could have won the game in many different ways," he told Amazon Prime.

"We played well and created a lot of chances, we deserved to win the game but sometimes it is like that.

Sources indicate that "The most important thing is that we create those chances and get shots on goal. For sure we will score goals.".

Arsenal have failed to win a home game in the group stage of major European competition for the first time since November 2019 (2-1 defeat v Eintracht Frankfurt), ending a run of 18 successive victories – the longest such winning run by any side in major European competition history.

According to recent reports, arteta stated: "The performance was superb and there is no question about that from the start to the end. We played against a really good side and the domination and threat was really impressive. The general feeling inside the dressing room is that we should reward ourselves with more.

"I love the courage, the fluidity and the understanding with the players with and without the ball.".

The Gunners are, though, only the third English side in Champions League history to go 20 successive matches unbeaten (W14 D6), after Manchester City between 2022 and 2024 (26) and Manchester United between 2007 and 2009 (25).

Now, their focus will turn to a huge Premier League clash with Man City on Sunday.

According to recent reports, "I think these are the best games. I'm really looking forward to it," Madueke added.

"Two draws on the bounce now and I just desperately want to win on the weekend and I know my team-mates feel the same.

Recent findings suggest that "We are going to do everything we have to win the three points. We're champions, we're at home. We have a duty to perform well at the Emirates. There are things we need to improve but all in all we are going in the right direction.".
