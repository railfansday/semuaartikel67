Steven Gerrard admits he is struggling to understand Xabi Alonso’s refusal to utilise one of Chelsea’s key summer signings.

After some encouraging early signs, the Blues have fallen off the title pace being set by Arsenal and have suffered several damaging defeats in recent weeks.

A rare home loss against Tottenham was compounded by Saturday’s drubbing at the hands of Leeds United at Stamford Bridge when defensive shortcomings were to the fore once again.

Alonso has refused to use injuries as an excuse for an alarming dip in form but the absences of Reece James and Joao Pedro are being keenly felt.

The Brazil international striker is particularly key component of Chelsea’s system, but cover was sourced during the summer window in the shape of the experienced Danny Welbeck.

Alonso, however, has appeared strangely reluctant to start the 35-year-old in Pedro’s absence, with Pedro Neto preferred on occasion while against Leeds he chose to hand a starting opportunity to the largely untried Emmanuel Emegha.

‘Especially with Joao Pedro out, you’re thinking it’s perfect for Danny Welbeck, and he’s an experienced player out there,’ Gerrard told The Overlap with Sky Bet when asked about his former England teammate.

‘He’s someone who can help a lot of youngsters that are playing, a lot of inexperienced players at Chelsea.

‘Same reason why they brought in Jordan Henderson, and you would say that Jordan’s done a really good job, what Xabi Alonso’s wanted him to do, but with no Joao Pedro, to not see hardly anything of Welbeck is a big surprise.
