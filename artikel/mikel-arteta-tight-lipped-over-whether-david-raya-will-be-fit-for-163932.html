Mikel Arteta opted against giving a definitive answer over whether David Raya will be fit for Arsenal’s mammoth clash with Manchester City this weekend - but indicated there was hope of the No 1’s return.

Raya hurt his shoulder in a seemingly innocuous collision with Newcastle striker William Osula in Arsenal’s 2-2 draw at St James’ Park last weekend, but the Spaniard escaped serious injury with Arteta confirming earlier in the week that he would not require surgery.

Having initially feared the Spaniard would have been “out for months”, Raya has already returned to training and is in the picture for a return to the starting line-up.

He nevertheless was not fit for the Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund in midweek and Arteta could not confirm if Sunday’s visit of City would come too soon for the 31-year-old.

Questioned on his involvement, Arteta replied: “I don't know... we have a few more hours. We're trying to get everybody back.”.

Arteta was then asked if there was a “ray of hope” if Raya might play, to which he said: “Yeah.”.

Raya was instrumental in Arsenal ending a 22-year wait for a Premier League title last season has carried his stellar form into the current campaign, boasting seven clean sheets in 10 matches prior to Arsenal’s trip to Newcastle.

He was deputised by Kepa Arrizabalaga for the Dortmund clash and Arteta backed his goalkeeping understudy as a more than adequate alternative.

“The experience that Kepa has is incredible, and for the teams he's been involved, the trophies that he's won,” he added.
