Liverpool need to make some transfers in January.

If they don't, they'll be left behind by their rivals and will probably end up falling even further down the Premier League table than they at the moment find themselves.

Of course, being fourth in the league isn't the end of the world but, considering the fact that they've spent over £600 million on latest players across the last couple of seasons, you'd have expected a lot more from them.

The nine points that separate first-place Arsenal and Liverpool needs to get chopped down so that they can start their charge for the Premier League title.

The easiest way for them to do this is going to be adding newly introduced players to their ranks and, with the next transfer window now just a month and a half away, deals need to start being put in place.

Liverpool could sign Alejandro Balde on loan with an option to buy.

Left-back has recently become a major problem for Liverpool.

Most fans thought that, when Milos Kerkez was brought in, the left-back would quickly become Liverpool's first-choice.

And, while Arne Slot tried to give him that, it was clear that he wasn't quite ready for the step up from Bournemouth.

This led to Andy Robertson being given a more prominent role again and, since he's now gone, questions have been asked about their left-back.

Kostas Tsimikas is another option that Liverpool have at their disposal but, the fact that TEAMtalk are now reporting that Alejandro Balde could be on his way to Anfield is excellent news.

"Liverpool and Manchester United have been informed of the deal Barcelona are prepared to accept for Alejandro Balde, with the Spanish giants ready to sanction his departure in January," they wrote.

"TEAMtalk can confirm that Balde’s agent, Jorge Mendes, has held discussions with both Liverpool and Manchester United, with the two Premier League giants having been made aware of the conditions surrounding a potential deal.

"Both clubs are actively looking at left-back additions ahead of January and view the Barcelona defender as an attractive option.

"TEAMtalk understands that Mendes has communicated a valuation of €40million (£33m) to interested clubs.

"That figure would apply whether the deal is structured as a permanent transfer or a loan arrangement with an option to buy.".

Alejandro Balde would help solve two of Liverpool's problems.

As mentioned, left-back is one of Liverpool's main problems right now.

However, there are other areas among their ranks which are also struggling.

Midfield is probably the main area that fans will feel like they need to upgrade.

Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni are their two best options right now and, even though both Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch can also cover those positions, each of them have proven that they can barely play in a double pivot.

According to recent reports, however, alongside both of these positions, right back is also an issue. Ronald Araujo may be leaving and, with the injury history that Conor Bradley and Jeremie Frimpong have, it's unclear whether they'll be reliable enough for Liverpool to trust.

Right now, the £33 million for Balde may feel like a lot of money but, if Araujo isn't signed, Tsimkas leaves and both Frimpong and Bradley end up side-lined, Liverpool are going to be so weak at the back.

Here's hoping that the changes they need to make get done.
