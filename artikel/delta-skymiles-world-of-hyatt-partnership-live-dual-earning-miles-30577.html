In September 2026, Delta SkyMiles and World of Hyatt unveiled plans to launch a fresh, long term strategic partnership. This announcement came just shortly after American AAdvantage and World of Hyatt revealed that they will be ending their partnership.

Recent findings suggest that at the time, we didn’t actually have any details as to what the partnership would entail, but that has now changed, as additional information has been revealed. Parts of the partnership are even now live, and members can already link their accounts.

Delta and Hyatt are launching a fresh loyalty program collaboration, which comes with a “shared ambition to create additional value, recognition and engagement opportunities across more of the travel journey.” This includes reciprocal opportunities to earn rewards, and also reciprocal opportunities to select perks with the partner brand.

Here’s how Laurie Blair, Hyatt’s SVP of Global Marketing and Loyalty, describes this:.

Sources indicate that “World of Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles were each built to make travel more meaningful and rewarding, which makes this collaboration feel meant to be. By connecting the Delta flight and Hyatt stay, we are helping members get more from the journeys they are already taking while opening the door to new experiences, destinations and memories. It is an exciting next step in our commitment to caring for members throughout the full travel journey, from takeoff to check out.”.

Here’s how Dwight James, Delta’s SVP of Customer Engagement & Loyalty, describes this:.

In a recent development, “At Delta, we know loyalty is something we earn by delivering meaningful value and experiences that make our Members want to keep choosing us. Our relationship with World of Hyatt gives us another way to do that by recognizing and rewarding Members across more of their travel, while creating fresh ways to earn, engage and experience both brands.”.

It has been revealed that effective immediately (as of November 17, 2026), elite members of both loyalty programs can earn simultaneously with the fresh “Dual Earn” benefit. Members must link their accounts, and can then earn additional rewards.
