📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

With the wellness industry still booming, we’re inundated with products and devices that promise a shortcut to total health. Now, there’s a long list of gadgets, apps, topical creams, and ingestible supplements designed to clear your mind, calm your nervous system, boost energy, and improve sleep.

One supplement class that continues to reign in the mainstream is greens powders, more than just green juice in powder form. Below, the experts unpack everything you really need to know about the latest generation of greens supplements.

Industry observers note that these supercharged green food powders aren’t just specialty items at vitamin shops; you can now find them everywhere—from drugstores to Amazon. But according to Philippa May, co-founder of women-first greens powder brand Naere, the significant players have one glaring problem: they’re created by men, for men.

Industry observers note that “We created Nectar 01 as an antidote to the male-dominated market,” she indicates. “The highly successful green powders already in the category were built around male-focused language: performance, optimization, stronger. And supplements aimed at women often leaned into insecurities: de-bloat, anti-aging, fixing your skin, helping you ‘bounce back.’ We felt there was nothing truly speaking to women as they are, beyond problems to be solved, and instead as modern individuals navigating the natural flux of daily life, in need of support rather than correction.”.

Industry observers note that this is where the next generation of greens powders comes in. Sitting alongside Naere is the much-hyped LymaID²: the biohacking superbrand’s latest launch (and its most accessibly priced offering, too). Founder Lucy Goff enlists the most revered specialists in a field to power her revolutionary debuts, so she created ID² in collaboration with Professor Paul Clayton, a clinical pharmacologist and senior scientific adviser to the U.K. government.

Similarly, Catherine Hurley has focused on organic powdered fruits and vegetables, rather than a long list of trendy ingredients that may appeal to the world’s biohacking bros. Her Newroad 30 powder contains a megadose of polyphenol-rich fruits and berries, alongside vegetables and pulses to support the gut microbiome. In short, it’s not a hack for superhuman fitness, but a real-food supplement to boost your existing diet.

Most of these powders contain greens—from common produce like spinach and kale to specialty greens like alfalfa grass and chlorophyll. These powders often also include fiber-rich foods, probiotic sources, and digestive enzymes to help further reduce bloating.

Industry observers note that but what do the experts really think? Nutritionists largely agree that greens powders can support wellness, but only when paired with a healthy, varied diet. (If your core food groups are Pret sandwiches and cortisol, mainlining a glass of greens each morning won’t save you.).

“The starting point for me is always food,” says Jessica Shand, a naturopathic nutritionist and author of The Hormone Balance Handbook. “No powder will ever replace the benefits of eating a wide variety of vegetables, fruit, legumes, nuts, and seeds because whole foods provide so much more than individual vitamins and minerals alone. They contain fiber, polyphenols, phytonutrients, and thousands of interactions that we simply cannot fully replicate in a supplement,” she says.

Industry observers note that that said, we also have to be realistic about how people live. “Busy schedules, frequent travel, limited access to fresh produce, or simply not consuming enough plant variety are all situations where a greens or plant powder may be helpful,” Shand adds. “However, I would only ever recommend a high-quality product, and, in my opinion, those are relatively few and far between.”.

Sources indicate that so, which greens powders are really worth investing in? Below, see the Bazaar breakdown of the supplements to stick to this year, all editor-tested.

Lyma is a brand known for moving the goalposts with its innovative (albeit investment-level) products, and ID²—the brand’s probiotic powdered supplement—is no different.

In a recent development, “The modern diet leaves most of us less than what we could be,” says Paul Clayton, a professor who worked with the brand on the launch. “Lyma ID² was built to put those elements back into place—the ones that the science of the longest-living people in human history told us matter most.”.

Based on the philosophy that longevity begins with a healthy gut, ID² contains a diverse range of prebiotic fibers, since no single strain benefits every section of the gut, along with bioavailable omega-3s and a range of minerals and vitamins.

Personally, I saw a huge difference after drinking the formula religiously for six weeks, especially with bloating. By the one-month mark, my midafternoon brain fog had all but subsided. And thanks to the genuinely pleasant grapefruit taste, I’ve stuck with it for several months now.

Newroad 30 is a gut supplement containing 30 organic powdered fruits and vegetables. Rather than focusing on greens, which can be an acquired taste, founder Catherine Hurley has placed red fruits and berries at the center of her formula. “They provide a rich source of polyphenols and antioxidants that support gut health, skin health, and healthy aging,” she explains. “We still include vegetables and pulses, but their flavors sit behind the berry profile. The result is a product that delivers the benefits while tasting genuinely great, making it much easier to enjoy every day.” Indeed, if you’ve struggled to commit to an earthy greens powder before, this black-currant-tinged powder is the one to choose, delivering vitamins and minerals to support a healthy gut microbiome without the unpleasant taste.

Another benefit is how Newroad 30 is made. “We freeze-dry every ingredient. Most powders are spray-dried, and this method doesn’t preserve nutrients, fiber, and polyphenols as well as freeze-drying, which also preserves flavor,” indicates Hurley. Everything is organic here, too—an important consideration because, as Hurley explains, “the process of drying the ingredients concentrates any pesticides present.”.

Lastly, what’s not in this plant-powered powder is as notable as what is. “I would not want to take a powder with additives, such as sweeteners, flavorings, colors, emulsifiers, and binders,” Hurley adds, and Newroad 30 is free of them.

Sources indicate that one of the longstanding powdered supplements on the market, Elle Macpherson’s The Super Elixir Daily Greens is particularly beneficial for those navigating hormonal shifts, such as perimenopause. Alongside prebiotics, four probiotic strains, adaptogenic botanicals, and powdered greens, it includes a cocktail of vitamins and minerals—from ginseng and glutamine to B vitamins—designed to boost energy and reduce brain fog. For the skin challenges that often come at this time (think: loss of laxity and dryness), it also includes biotin and zinc.

While this power cocktail won’t directly balance your hormones, it will provide a strong nutritional foundation to support the body through such a seismic shift. The taste is distinctly “green” (if you’re picky about flavor, expect a challenge), but the energy-boosting potential is real.

It has been revealed that the original green powder, AG1, was made with athletes in mind but is for anyone who needs extra physical energy and mental clarity. Essentially, it’s a potent, well-rounded multivitamin that provides 100 percent of the RDA for key nutrients, including vitamins C and E, energy-providing B vitamins, two strains of gut-boosting probiotics, plant superfoods, and stress-reducing adaptogens.

Sources indicate that anecdotal praise for this one isn’t hard to find: Chances are, you already know one loyal devotee. However, the formula notably lacks iron, which, since women generally need more iron than men, points to the brand’s male-leaning marketing. Also, keep in mind the formula is a proprietary blend, so the brand doesn’t disclose the exact amount of each ingredient.

Built by women, for women, Naere brings ingredient transparency to the supplements space with its online Integrity Index™—a digital service that analyzes any supplement, from any brand, and delivers a detailed breakdown of its safety and efficacy based on your personal needs.

In a recent development, this uncompromising focus on quality over quantity naturally follows through into its debut launch, Nectar 01. This powdered supplement contains 30 ingredients—four of which are patented—significantly fewer than many competitors.

You can delve into every ingredient on the brand’s website, but the headliners are marine-derived hydrolyzed collagen peptides and sodium hyaluronate to support skin and joints; algae-derived astaxanthin for antioxidant protection; selenium for immune function; and a patented black pepper extract to improve the bioavailability of the other ingredients.

“Ultimately, the best supplements aren’t about including everything, but about choosing well-dosed, bioavailable ingredients that genuinely deliver,” notes co-founder Philippa May. “And most importantly, consistency always wins. One supplement you take daily will always beat the supplement stack you forget.”.

It has been revealed that this is another one I found easy to stick to. The slightly sweet, green taste is peculiar but not unpleasant, and after finishing my first package, I saw real improvements in my bloating and digestion, plus steadier energy levels.
