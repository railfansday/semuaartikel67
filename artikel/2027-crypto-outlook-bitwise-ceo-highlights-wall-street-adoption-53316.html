The Modern Wealth Survey is an online study conducted for Charles Schwab by Logica Research among a national sample of Americans from August 24 to September 17, 2026.

Industry observers note that charles Schwab’s survey results show that cryptocurrency investors are planning to invest more in the coming months, which will boost adoption. More than half of all respondents (60%) expect to add to their holdings in the next 12 months, compared with ETF owners (56%) and stock owners (52%).

According to recent reports, the interest of the participants is driven by the long-term potential. Growth potential and portfolio diversification are the most commonly cited benefits by owners and those interested in crypto. According to the survey, more than half of Americans familiar with cryptocurrency say it is vital or essential to building wealth.

Half of those who own cryptocurrency see it as a complement to traditional investments, while more than two in five Americans familiar with cryptocurrency expect it to become a more popular investment; Millennials are the most likely generation to own and be interested in cryptocurrency.

The survey also found that Americans familiar with cryptocurrency are considering a range of ways to participate.
