December is often a very quiet month for fresh game releases, and that's particularly going to be the case in 2026 due to GTA 6's arrival and the desire from publishers to get as far away from it as possible, but we've still got the likes of Rayman Legends Retold and Stage Tour to look forward to, along with a couple of first-party Xbox expansions.

In terms of first-party stuff, Xbox is initially releasing a paid DLC pack for Minecraft Dungeons 2 on December 1st called The Humblers Awaken. This DLC is included with the Deluxe Edition of the game if you bought it, and it'll be available separately as well.

You can expect "new quests, unexplored environments, colorful cosmetics, and an expanded arsenal of powerful gear" in The Humblers Awaken, while base game players will also be treated to a free revision on the same day, adding in new challenges, weapons and armour.

Recent findings suggest that then, just a day later, Xbox and Obsidian are releasing a first expansion for The Outer Worlds 2, known as Side Effects Guaranteed. This DLC includes a upcoming narrative story (that'll be concluded in the second part next year), a new region of Elysium Valley to explore, new threats, new perks and an increased level cap.

There's also new weapons, armours, outfits, flaws, a new radio station - you get the picture!

Side Effects Guaranteed will cost $14.99 USD when it arrives on December 2nd, or you'll get it included if you bought either the Premium Edition or Premium Upgrade Edition of The Outer Worlds 2.

Perhaps there's a chance we might get even more from Xbox's first-party studios in December, but as mentioned, it's typically a month where the releases die down. In any case, it's nice to see two expansions on the way very soon!

The Sift is in danger! Something ancient is stirring beneath the ground of the latest dimension – a threat that has slumbered for ages and is now beginning to wake. Gather your trusty allies, venture deep into unexplored reaches, take on the not-so-happy humblers, and confront the foes behind their rude awakening!

Side Effects Guaranteed is the first chapter of a two-part expansion for The Outer Worlds 2. A distress signal beckons you and your crew of the Incognito to a derelict ship, where you discover the ship’s crew in suspended animation to keep themselves alive, as they’ve been infected with something that causes explosive cell death. The hunt for a cure leads to Elysium Valley, a brand-newly introduced region on Eden.
