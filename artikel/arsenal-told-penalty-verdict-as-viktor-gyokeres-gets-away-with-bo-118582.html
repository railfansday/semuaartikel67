Arsenal were fortunate not to concede a penalty against Borussia Dortmund during their Champions League draw.

The Premier League champions and Bundesliga leaders held each other to a 1-1 stalemate at the Emirates Stadium, as Viktor Gyokeres’ rifled opener was cancelled out in the second half by Maximilian Beier.

It was the Gunners’ second draw on the spin after a 2-2 encounter at St James’ Park, where Noni Madueke rescued a point against Newcastle after Malick Thiaw and Harvey Barnes had put the Magpies ahead following Eberechi Eze’s opening goal.

But it was the goalscorer Gyokeres who almost turned from hero to villain, as he allegedly handled a Dortmund corner in the second period.

It led Amazon Prime Video pundits Alan Shearer and Mark Clattenberg to question referee Maurizio Mariani’s decision not to award a spot kick to the visitors.

“I think Arsenal were fortunate to have gotten away with that one,” Shearer said on co-commentary.

"I know it might have been Fabio Silva in front of him trying to flick it on, but he didn't, and his arm is actually out.".

Clattenberg, said alongside the record Premier League goalscorer in the north London gantry, concurred.

"We all know UEFA have a stricter handball rule than we see in the Premier League, but what doesn't save Gyokeres in this situation is, the ball comes in from a distance and he makes a little movement of his arm towards the ball so that makes it look more like a penalty kick for me,” the former official noted.

Former Arsenal forward Theo Walcott spoke of his relief at the penalty not being given.

"As soon as it hit his hand, straight away my heart is in my mouth, I think it's a penalty," he said.

"I think he's got away with that. I'm pleased it wasn't given because it's not a penalty, but in Europe it probably should be.".

Gyokeres asserted his innocence in his post-match interview.

"I think the arm was towards my body," the Swede mentioned.

"I don't think it was outside, so I don't think it was anything.".
