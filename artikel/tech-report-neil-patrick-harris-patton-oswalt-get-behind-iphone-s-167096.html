EXCLUSIVE: Neil Patrick Harris and Patton Oswalt have signed on as executive producers of Will Mayo and Ian Scott McGregor’s short film, Dropping Off.

Shot almost entirely on security cameras and iPhones in Hollywood, the short “follows Thom (newcomer Tanner Agpoon-Steinbock), an aspiring musician gigging as a rideshare driver who is asked by a grieving passenger (actress and comedian Alyssa Limperis) to spread her grandmother’s ashes.” Rebecca Metz (Better Things) also stars.

Industry observers note that the film was inspired by an incident outside the filmmakers’ apartment in Hollywood when a man hurriedly dumped cremated remains behind their building and vanished. The act was caught on security cameras, but the identities of both the man, and the cremated, remain unknown. The film dramatizes the moment, and was shot in the same location, using the same security cameras. Industry observers note that the film premiered at Dances With Films: LA in 2026 and has been selected for Palm Springs Shortfest and Encounters. It won the audience award at the inaugural Long Beach Film Festival, among other prizes. The filmmakers are hoping for consideration in the Live Action Short Film category at the Oscars. Director Mayo’s short-form work has been Oscar-shortlisted, Emmy-nominated, and screened at Sundance, Venice, Berlinale, Telluride, and Tribeca.

In a recent development, “Will Mayo and Ian Scott McGregor have created something truly unique and inspired here,” mentioned Glee and How I Met Your Mother star Harris. “You think you’re watching one kind of film, then it unexpectedly reveals a subversive structure and an underlying cinematic intention that lands with an impressive emotional gut-punch. It’s funny, and different, and makes you think about film in a whole fresh way – it’s a substantial swing, I got goosebumps. I’m so excited to make movies with these guys.”.

“I love upcoming filmmakers,” added Ratatouille and The King Of Queens star Oswalt. “This [film] is really special. [Will and Ian] deal with grief, connection, loss of connection, how to rebuild it. Genuinely funny stuff, genuinely tense stuff – all in this efficient, perfect capsule. If [Will and Ian] build on this as filmmakers, then we’ve got a very good future ahead of us.”.
