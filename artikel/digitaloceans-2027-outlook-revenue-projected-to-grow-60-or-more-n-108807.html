DigitalOcean is poised for strong growth ahead, thanks to the artificial intelligence boom. It started by providing developers, start-ups, and small businesses a simple, affordable cloud computing platform without enterprise complexity. Now, the organization has successfully expanded into AI cloud infrastructure.

Sources indicate that this has resulted in DigitalOcean's fastest growth in a decade. The cloud giant raised its Q4 revenue growth projection to between 44% and 45% year over year, while also forecasting 2027 sales to jump at least 60% over 2026. To capitalize on the huge customer demand for AI, in September, DigitalOcean secured a $725 million equipment finance facility to fund AI infrastructure expansion.

In addition, while neoclouds purely rent AI cloud compute capacity, DigitalOcean pairs its AI hardware with software and other services. This differentiates and strengthens the company's offerings from competitors.

Recent findings suggest that more importantly, while the firm's AI-specific ARR rose 288% year over year in Q3, customers with ARR of $1 million or more grew 273%. Gaining client spend among large customers is key because they tend to churn less often than small businesses and individual developers. This adds greater stability to the company's revenue base, and helps to set DigitalOcean up for long-term growth.
