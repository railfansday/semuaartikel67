A new Avengers: Doomsday TV spot has hit the internet, and it appears to show Steve Rogers in Latveria.

Sources indicate that in the fresh footage, which you can see below, we briefly see Steve on a circular structure in Latveria. He uses Mjolnir to summon lightning, though what he's doing and why he needs the hammer is a mystery for now.

New doomsday TV spot, pretty sure nobody posted this yet https://t.co/GZ87JyMkguNovember 27, 2026.

The TV spot also pokes fun at Chris Evans's past in the Fantastic Four, when he played Johnny Storm in two of the Fox-era movies. Steve's son asks him if he can shoot lasers out of his eyes, catch fire, or fly, since he's a superhero. Johnny Storm, of course, can engulf his body in flames and fly.

Avengers: Doomsday sees the Avengers, Thunderbolts, Fantastic Four, X-Men, and Wakandans go up against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. The final trailer for the movie confirmed that Steve's decision to go back in time and live with Peggy Carter at the end of Avengers: Endgame triggered the incursions now threatening the multiverse.

According to recent reports, "We love this film. We've poured a lot into it. We've had seven years to think about it. We've got Robert back, greatest actor in the world," co-director Joe Russo said recently. "I think it is unlike any movie that's been made in the Marvel universe up to this point. It's tonally very different. It's very surprising, and I think the audience is going to be very surprised when they see the movie.".

According to recent reports, doomsday arrives this December 18. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the upcoming Marvel movies and TV indicates for everything else that's on the way.
