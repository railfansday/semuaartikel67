GTA 6 Lets You Stop And Even Derail The Train

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It might've been impossible, but for 13 years, that didn't stop GTA 5 players from throwing everything they could at the freight train to try and stop it dead in its tracks. What began with ramming a dump truck into it swiftly spiraled into spawning 100 rhino tanks, placing a UFO on the tracks, or even flooding Los Santos with a tsunami. Nothing worked. So, when GTA 6 finally hit store shelves, the number one question people raced to answer was: can you stop the train?

Industry observers note that not only can you stop the train in GTA 6, but you can even derail it. As shown by Twitter user jax_caine (video embedded below), the train isn't an unstoppable force of nature anymore. Hitting it from the side causes it to swerve off the rails, and if you wait around for another train, the two will collide.

Trains are much more dynamic this time around, reacting to their environment rather than pushing on full steam ahead without any thought to what's in front of them.

According to recent reports, if you stand on the tracks, a train will brake before hitting you. Likewise, when the train sees another train blocking the tracks, rather than ramming into it at full speed and causing a massive pile-up, it'll slow down. Hell, if you put a body on the tracks, they stop and even sound their horn at you.

It has been revealed that GTA 5's trains worked a little differently. They're static objects and their position is updated through coordinates, independent of physics interactions, which means that instead of reacting to the game's physics engine, they follow hard-coded tracks and override anything in their way. That's why they turn into submersibles when you flood Los Santos, and easily tear through 100 tanks. They literally can't be stopped.

Sources indicate that we don't know exactly how they work in GTA 6 yet, but it's clear they aren't static objects anymore. They behave like other vehicles, reacting dynamically to the world. You could fly a plane into the train and it'd be thrown off course, rather than bravely continuing ahead without a scratch.

According to recent reports, still, even with GTA 6 in hand and a train that we can derail, I doubt that'll stop the community from finding increasingly elaborate ways to stop the freight train. Even if they never do. With that mystery out of the way, maybe we can turn our attention to those strange symbols near Manifest Canyon, eh?
