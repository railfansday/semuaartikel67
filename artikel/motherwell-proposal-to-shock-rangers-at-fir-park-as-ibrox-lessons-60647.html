Motherwell boss Alfred Johansson notes the Steelmen will take key lessons from their Ibrox battle with Rangers into Saturday night’s rematch at Fir Park.

The 8pm kick-off live on Sky Sports is set to bring an added edge to proceedings with those in claret and amber improving after six straight Premiership losses. They are unbeaten in their last three games while Rangers failed to win in North Lanarkshire last season, drawing under Russell Martin and then Danny Rohl.

Now it’s current manager Derek McInnes’ turn to try and mastermind an away win at Motherwell, but last time out in Govan, the away side dominated the ball with almost 60% possession. Rangers still created several chances though during the 1-0 win with manager Johansson keen to see a focused approach this weekend.

He mentioned: “We know we are a good team and we can do good stuff. That was good, but we don’t use our positive play to score goals. That is something we are working hard on and know we have to improve.

It has been revealed that “We want to create more and we are. We are in the top five in the league for chances created and so on. We are on the right path there. In the last game against Rangers, I also learned that you have to take care of defensive transitions against teams like that. This is a fantastic challenge against a good side.

Sources indicate that “We are also in a moment of progression and we have some continuity in the team. We feel like we can compete and we are looking forward to the game. They are good in second-ball play and in transitions. We need to be a good version of ourselves if we are going to get points. There has to be a focus on how we attack and defend.”.

In a recent development, johansson added: “It will be a significant occasion and I am looking forward to it a lot. The guys have been fantastic in training and we love to play these games. I came to Motherwell for many reasons, but these fixtures were part of it. To play here on a Saturday evening when it’s dark outside the lights go on is fantastic.

Recent findings suggest that “There will be a lot of people in the stands and it’s going to be electric. It will be much better than playing the bounce game here last week when there was nobody in the stadium. It’s a massive difference to that! I see the progression of my team and development leads to results. We are moving in the way we want to do things.”.
