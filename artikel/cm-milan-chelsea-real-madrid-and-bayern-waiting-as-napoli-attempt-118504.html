AC Milan are eagerly watching the renewal situation at Napoli, as they attempt to extend Scott McTominay’s stay at the club.

It has been revealed that A potential move for McTominay has come a little out of the blue. The Scotsman has been one of the better players in Serie A since his arrival, and with renewal talks stalling, Milan have tried to get themselves involved in the discussion for his next club.

With suggestions that Adidas would also help to fund a move, it seems things have developed slightly to the point where it wouldn’t be a distant dream….

Sources indicate that according to Calciomercato.com, the Rossoneri are among a whole host of clubs interested in the McTominay situation. It’s believed that they are the only Italian team in the ‘race’, if you can call it that, whilst Chelsea, Real Madrid and Bayern Munich are also keeping an eye on the ongoing attempts to secure a renewal.

The latest update comes from his former home of Manchester, where Giovanni Manna has met with his agent, Colin Murdock, to try ‘accelerate their efforts’ to secure his future in Naples.

It has been revealed that his renewal is the priority for the Partenopei – who ‘want to keep him for a long time’ – and a salary raise from the current €4 million net per season is expected. Whether this will be enough, though, is another thing entirely.
