So, here’s everything coming to Paramount+ this month.

One of the first big releases coming to Paramount+ is “By Any Means,” a historical crime thriller inspired by real events surrounding organized crime and the civil rights movement in 1960s America. Mark Wahlberg plays a notorious Colombo crime family hitman who also spent years working as an FBI informant. The film takes some creative liberties with its historical characters and events, but there’s plenty of fascinating real-world material behind the story. It also has striking visuals and strong performances, which help bring this unsettling chapter of American history to the screen.

Inspired by true events, “By Any Means” is set in 1960s Mississippi, where a wave of brutal killings targeting civil rights leaders prompts the FBI to launch an investigation. Young agent Wayne Strider (Yahya Abdul-Mateen II) is assigned to uncover those responsible, but rapidly encounters resistance as local authorities appear reluctant to help. With witnesses too frightened to come forward and key suspects seemingly protected, Strider is forced into an unlikely partnership with notorious New York Mafia hitman Greg Scarpa (Mark Wahlberg). As the pair travel across Mississippi gathering information and confronting suspected killers, their vastly different methods create a volatile working relationship.

Premieres December 3 on Paramount+.

Recent findings suggest that taylor Sheridan is expanding the “Tulsa King” universe with “Frisco King,” a crime comedy-drama led by Samuel L. Jackson. The series follows Jackson’s character after his introduction in a two-episode arc during “Tulsa King” season 3, giving fans another chance to enjoy his wonderfully dry humor and effortless screen presence. Sylvester Stallone is also set to make a crossover appearance, adding another familiar face to the mix. Sheridan serves as creator, showrunner and sole writer across all eight episodes.

“Frisco King” follows Russell Lee Washington Jr. (Samuel L. Jackson), a seasoned Upcoming Orleans gangster who relocates to Frisco, Texas, looking to establish a new criminal operation. After discovering a thriving underground network of hustlers operating beneath the town’s respectable suburban exterior, Lee begins recruiting a team to help expand his business. His new associates include Teddy (Asa Germann), an ambitious college dropout who becomes his protégé, and Keith (Kai Caster), a local hotel valet who joins his operation. Alongside two local cheerleaders, Lee turns small-time schemes into increasingly lucrative ventures.

Premieres December 18 on Paramount+.

Another series worth adding to your watchlist is “The Day,” a British crime thriller adapted from the 2018 Belgian series. The bank heist setup is intriguing enough, but it’s interesting how the story unfolds from two different perspectives, shifting between the police negotiating outside and the people trapped inside the bank. Each episode reveals more about what happened, gradually changing how you view earlier events. The series also boasts a strong cast, including Minnie Driver, making it a good choice if you’re after a gripping crime drama with a slightly different storytelling approach.

The series centers around a bank robbery that takes an unexpected turn when police arrive in the middle of the heist, prompting the criminals to barricade themselves inside with several hostages. Minnie Driver stars as Sylvia “Vox” Voxley, a hostage negotiator tasked with bringing the situation under control before anyone gets hurt. However, as the siege unfolds, the story rewinds to revisit the events from inside the bank, revealing the perspectives of the robbers and their captives. Across eight episodes, the series switches between the police operation and the unfolding hostage crisis.
