Industry observers note that “Let me slip into something a little more comfortable,” Connor Storrie claims in Vogue’s latest Now Serving episode, trading his Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello ribbed beige V-neck sweater and belted gray trousers—he deadpans it’s his “Sunday walking around the house look”—for a gleaming gold metallic trench coat and aviator sunglasses. (Storrie eventually switches back to cook, though the energy remains decidedly high-fashion.).

Industry observers note that first up, the Heated Rivalry actor—and Vogue’s 2026 Best Dressed cover star—prepares his go-to order on a night out: a “Mezcal Mandy.” Skipping the jigger in favor of an eyeball pour of roughly two shots of mezcal over ice, topped with lime and soda water, Storrie takes a sip—despite the shoot taking place at 10 a.m. “This drink says that I don’t want any sugar in my system, but I want a little funk,” he explains.

From there, Storrie moves on to his everyday high-protein staple: a deconstructed burger. Acknowledging that his ingredient list is so minimal that the producers asked him to chop an onion just to get extra knife footage, Storrie karate-chops a pound of ground beef into portions with his bare hands (“So that I don’t get salmonella comments or whatever, I’m going to wash my hands for you people,” he quips) and cooks the patties in grass-fed butter, before topping it with provolone, arugula, thick slices of beefsteak tomato, and a generous squeeze of Dijon mustard. (Another pro tip: he saves the leftover pan grease to cook his six morning eggs the next day.).

Between flipping patties, Storrie reflects on his style evolution, from growing up in Odessa, Texas (“a lot of long cargo shorts, really messed-up tennis shoes, band tees”) and moving to California during the 2016 drop-crotch jogger era, to sitting front row alongside Madonna and Kate Moss at Saint Laurent. He also addresses the internet’s reaction to his recent buzzcut. "I feel like every six months, I’m a completely different person, and I try to honor that,” he notes. ‘I like the hair, but people were pissed off. People were not happy. Whatever, screw it. I’m doing my thing.”.

Sources indicate that while Now Serving guests typically pull out all the stops to impress the crew, Storrie notes that no one is rushing to sample his ultra-minimalist spread. “I don’t care. I like it,” he says with a shrug, before happily polishing off the plate himself. (His next stop? A workout, of course.).
