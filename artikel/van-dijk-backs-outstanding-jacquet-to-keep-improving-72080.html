Virgil van Dijk believes Jeremy Jacquet is only "going to get so much better" after his "outstanding" start to life at Liverpool.

Jacquet agreed to join Liverpool last winter in a pre-contract deal ahead of the summer, for a reported £55m transfer fee.

The Frenchman has impressed since moving to Anfield, and he is just one of two players to feature in all 16 matches this season, alongside Alexis Mac Allister.

Jacquet has helped to keep four clean sheets across all competitions, and leads Liverpool players for interceptions (25), blocks (12), tackles (35), tackles won (20), and duels won (81).

Van Dijk (1,114) is the only Liverpool player to have more touches than the 21-year-old (968).

And the Liverpool captain is excited by the connection he has with his fresh centre-back partner and is confident they will continue to improve, with a clash against old rivals Manchester United next up.

"He's been outstanding so far, I must say," Van Dijk told Sky Sports when asked about Jacquet.

"Very young, but he plays like a very mature player. He's still 21; he's still going to get so much better, and that's a good thing.

"Obviously, I speak to him a lot during the game, but after the game and training and certain moments as well, where I feel like I can help him.

It has been revealed that "As a centre-back partnership, you have to be able to understand what your qualities and what your flaws are at times and then complement each other on those things. You only get there by playing games.

"It's been good because he's helping me, I help him, and that's how it should be.

Sources indicate that "I must say I'm impressed with how we started, and now it's down to him to keep that level, keep that consistency, stay calm, because you can easily get carried away because there's a lot of plaudits coming his way and rightly so. But I know how it can work when you have maybe one lesser game or not up to the standard that you have.

"He just has to keep going, keep staying important and be fit, and then things will be working out pretty well.".

Should he start against United, Van Dijk will be starting a 50th consecutive Premier League game for Liverpool, the fourth time he has had a run of 50+ unbroken starts in the competition – the joint-most by an outfield player along with Sylvain Distin.

Aged 35 years and 137 days, he would also become the oldest outfielder player to start 50+ consecutive Premier League games for a single club, overtaking Kevin Davies for Bolton (67 in a row, aged 34y 175d).
