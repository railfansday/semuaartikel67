Latest Update: COD: Modern Warfare 4 gets a surprisingly sudden free trial, so you can see what the game's extraction mode DMZ is like

🌐 Source: Eurogamer • 📅 09 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (314 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • Sources indicate that the free trial only includes access to DMZ and no other part of Modern Warfare 4, such as multiplayer or the campaign.
  • DMZ released days before Modern Warfare 4's 23rd October release date, meaning this first trial has arrived just two weeks after the game's full launch.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that activision has released the first trial for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4's extraction mode DMZ. The trial is available across PC (Steam / Battlenet), PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S until Wednesday, 11th November.

Sources indicate that the free trial only includes access to DMZ and no other part of Modern Warfare 4, such as multiplayer or the campaign. And while it's not uncommon for Call of Duty titles to make some of their modes available for free for a limited time, the timing of this DMZ taster is notable.

DMZ released days before Modern Warfare 4's 23rd October release date, meaning this first trial has arrived just two weeks after the game's full launch. By comparison, Black Ops 7's Endgame mode had its first free trial months after launch, in the game's third season. That mentioned, Endgame continued to be freely available until Season 6.

Sources indicate that the DMZ trial also coincides with the launch of Modern Warfare 4's iteration of free-to-play Call of Duty battle royale mode Warzone, and Season 1. Indeed, both DMZ and the latest Warzone take place on the same Hajin map, so even if you don't decide to buy the full game to continue playing DMZ, some of that map knowledge will certainly transfer to Warzone.

Recent findings suggest that the DMZ trial does not appear to have any limits, meaning you can climb the ranks, access Story Missions - the only way to play PvE, with caveats - and complete Orders to your heart's content. That includes X01, the new endgame dungeon-like area available to experienced and fully-equipped players.

DMZ players who've been asking Infinity Ward to offer a PvE-only playlist of the PvPvE mode will certainly be happy with the influx of newly introduced players, as they could potentially amplify their calls for a PvP-free playlist.
