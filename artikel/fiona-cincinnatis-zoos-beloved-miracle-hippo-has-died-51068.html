Fiona, Cincinnati’s Zoo’s Beloved Miracle Hippo, Has Died

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a turn of fate somehow sadder than Punch the Monkey’s isolation and crueler than the loss of Harambe, the Cincinnati Zoo announced Thursday that the beloved, internet famous Nile hippopotamus, Fiona, has died at the age of 9.

“It is with extreme sadness that we share with you the devastating news that Fiona did not make it after multiple attempts from our amazing vets and keeper team to help her through what ended up being a medical emergency,” the zoo said in a tribute Thursday. “We know everyone in the community will be crushed by this news, as we all are.”.

It has been revealed that last week, vets reported that Fiona swallowed a knit hat that was accidentally thrown into her enclosure. Several days after the incident, Fiona’s appetite declined and zookeepers started a variety of tests and 24-hour care, in the hopes that Fiona could pass the hat naturally. (Surgery is a rarely recommended suggestion for hippos due to its high risk.) But Fiona continued to decline, losing energy and fluids.

Sources indicate that “Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome. The team performed necessary supportive care today in an attempt to save her, but it was not successful and she did not recover,” Thursday’s statement added. “Please keep her keepers and everyone who tried to help her in your thoughts as they try to navigate and come to terms with this shocking news. Fiona made history when she was born in 2017 as the first hippo born at the Cincinnati Zoo in over 53 years. (She was named after the Shrek character, princess Fiona, for the unique shape and size of her ears). Her mother, Bibi, was 17 at the time and went into labor early, delivering Fiona six weeks premature. Fiona was only 29 pounds and could not stand or nurse on her own.

Industry observers note that keepers and staff at the Cincinnati Zoo began documenting her growth on social media, drawing in millions of well-wishers and international fans as they watched the wobbling baby hippo fight to live and then begin to thrive. Even when Fiona reached adulthood, she remained a beloved internet icon known for her strength and resilience through seemingly insurmountable odds. Fiona was a fighter — even until the end.
