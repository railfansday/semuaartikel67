📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that it’s no secret, since leaders of Bayern Munich have themselves confirmed it: the Bavarian club is in negotiations with the Olise clan to extend the player’s contract. The French international’s lease expires in 2029 – so there is no urgency either – but the German club’s decision-makers feel the very hot breath of several clubs on their necks. This is particularly the case of Real Madrid, already confirmed as a suitor for the 24-year-old winger during the last summer transfer window.

It has been revealed that and everything indicates that the Madrilenians will go on the attack again next summer. Even if it is true that Yan Diomandé explodes, the doubts around Vinicius Jr could push Florentino Pérez to make a galactic move, and we know that he really appreciates the Frenchman. Several media have recently reported a sort of lobbying by the French internationals of Real Madrid to convince him to join them during the Blues training courses.

Recent findings suggest that in any case, José Félix Diaz has just provided some elements which cast a little chill on this problem. Indeed, the daily journalist A.S.who voted in the last Ballon d’Or, indicates that Madrid management has received somewhat worrying reports about the player. The latter would have a somewhat special character, with certain attitudes and certain gestures which would not always be understood by those around him. And on the Madrid side, locker room management has been tricky in recent years. This is clearly not a subject taken lightly.

According to recent reports, if the extra-sporting side therefore cools the Merengues a little, Real Madrid however has no doubts concerning the footballing qualities of the player, already considered one of the best on the planet, nor about his possible integration in the event of a transfer. The ball therefore risks being in the court of number 11 of the French team, who has a very juicy offer from Bayern on the table, with a salary estimated at 25 million euros per season.
