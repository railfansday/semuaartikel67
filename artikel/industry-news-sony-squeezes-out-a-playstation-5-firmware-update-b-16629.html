📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sony has managed to squeeze out another PlayStation 5 firmware upgrade before the highly-anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto 6.

Sony has been rolling firmware updates out on a fairly regular basis, and like many of those updates, this latest one - version 26.07-14.23.00 - doesn't bring any very meaningful changes. Sony repeated the same "we've made some security fixes to the system software" patch-note that we've seen many times before.

It has been revealed that and like all those firmware updates, 26.07-14.23.00 is mandatory for all PS5 and PS5 Pro consoles, assuming you want to go online or play the latest games. That, of course, includes GTA 6, which requires the console's firmware to be updated to its latest version before you can play.

It has been revealed that the revision should download automatically the next time you turn on your console, but you can also force the download through Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings. This is also where you can tick the option for your console to automatically download and install those updates in the future.

Although Sony hasn't directly stated so, the high frequency of those security firmware updates is likely related to the impending release of GTA 6, especially in light of recent gen-AI-driven PS5 jailbreaks.

Sources indicate that any firmware released before GTA 6's release now becomes required to play the game, which delays potential security exploits, seeing as hackers would need to find vulnerabilities in that firmware in order to play. While this may never happen, Sony is at least trying to minimise the risk as much as possible.

Industry observers note that GTA 6 arrives 19th November on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. It is now available to pre-load for anyone who pre-ordered the digital version, and those with the code-in-box physical version.
