It has been revealed that I’ve been tracking prices on Apple’s most coveted devices for years, and Black Friday remains one of the best times to shop tech deals. Now that Amazon’s 12-day Black Friday shopping event is officially in full swing, I’m impressed to see so many discounts across the board—everything from 28% off the AirPods Pro 3 to $250 off the MacBook Air.

SHOP AMAZON’S BLACK FRIDAY SALE.

Industry observers note that there’s a slew of snag-worthy Apple deals right now, but one stands out from the rest: The second-generation AirTag is just $23 right now, which is its lowest price of all time. I don’t expect this deal to drop much lower day-of, so I’d add it to cart ASAP before it sells out. I have a few AirTags myself, and they’re handy for holiday travel—and happen to make great gifts.

Sources indicate that expert tip: At $179, the AirPods Pro 3 are already cheaper than last year’s Black Friday deal, though they dropped as low as $169 during June Prime Day. Similarly, AirPods Max 2 headphones are a steep 22% off, which is just $30 above their record-low price from Prime Day. That said, I’m delusionally holding out hope that the AirPods 5 will go on sale at some point next week.

Expert tip: The best iPad deal up for grabs is $100 off the iPad Mini—though it dropped down to a record-low $349 during last year’s Black Friday sale. And even though you can score $150 off the 13-inch iPad Pro and $50 off the 11-inch iPad right now, both deals were significantly steeper last year. So, it’s possible that deals will be even better day-of.

According to recent reports, expert tip: Great news: the Apple Watch SE 3 returned to its record-low price of $199 and there’s a new lowest-ever deal on the Watch Series 12. I also saw a record-low deal on the Watch Ultra 3 earlier this month, but it’s at the moment sold out—so I’m keeping an eye out for a restock.

Expert tip: I found MacBook deals across several of Apple’s most popular models, including $500 off the 2026 MacBook Pro, $250 off the 13-inch and 15-inch 2026 MacBook Air, and the first-ever sale on the budget-friendly MacBook Neo. That noted, none of these laptops have hit record-low prices quite yet, so you stand to save more money if you wait for day-of deals.
