With V-8 power and a standard third row, the 2027 Audi SQ7 is a performance SUV that can fit the whole family.

The SQ7 is back for its second generation as the performance-oriented variant of Audi’s three-row Q7 SUV.

Fresh styling gives the SQ7 an updated look that’s very similar to the Q7, but racier elements such as the quad exhaust pipes are your clue this SUV is a different animal.

An air-spring suspension is standard and can be upgraded to a sport-tuned version for even sharper handling.

The SQ7 is made to be hustled on a back road, and a rear-wheel steering system helps you feel like you’re piloting a smaller SUV.

Audi has brought its digital matrix LED headlamps to the U.S. market. They adapt to road conditions and traffic to maximize visibility for the driver as well as other motorists.

The taillights are OLED panels and have several different light signatures.

Under the hood is a twin-turbo V-8 engine that makes 591 horsepower.

Acceleration is brisk, and Audi expects a 3.7-second 60 mph time.

The SQ7 is offered in one, nearly fully loaded trim, but there are a few option packages that include more performance-enhancing items.

According to recent reports, the price for all of this performance and luxury? A cool $100,095.
