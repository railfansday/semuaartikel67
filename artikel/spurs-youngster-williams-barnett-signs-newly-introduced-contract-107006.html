Tottenham youngster Luca Williams-Barnett has signed a new contract with the club.

The 18-year-old's previous deal was set to expire in June 2028, but he has now reportedly extended his stay at the club by five years.

Williams-Barnett made his Premier League debut for Spurs last month in their win over Crystal Palace, while also featuring off the bench against Leeds United before starting against Ipswich Town.

Against Ipswich on Saturday, Williams-Barnett (18 years 51 days) became the second-youngest player ever to both score and assist in a Premier League game, older only than Michael Owen in 1997 (17y 364d).

He was also the youngest player to score for Spurs in the Premier League since Gareth Bale against Fulham in September 2007 (18y 47d), and the fourth-youngest player to score on his first Premier League start behind Daniel Jebbison in May 2021 (17y 276d), Danny Cadamarteri in September 1997 (17y 343d), and Mark Cullen in May 2010 (18y 12d).

Williams-Barnett, who joined Spurs at the age of 10, has made five senior appearances in total, and could play an important role under Roberto De Zerbi.

"This is a special moment for me and my family, and one I have worked really hard to reach. But this is only the start," the teenager told the Spurs website.
