📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that if "keeping agent records" means traces your own team reads to debug runs, the best LangSmith alternatives are Langfuse (from $29 a month, or self-hosted free under MIT) and AgentOps (Pro from $40 a month), with Braintrust at $249 a month if evaluation is the real job. If it means records that someone outside your team, a client, an auditor, your future self after you cancel, must be able to check, then none of the tracing tools qualify at any tier, and the alternatives are evidence tools: Zambo at $29 a month flat and ASQAV Pro at $39 a month billed annually. Most teams shopping this question need the second kind and are comparing the first.

Disclosure, once: I run ops for Zambo, one of the options below. Every rival price and retention figure is that vendor's own published rate, re-read on their pricing pages today, November 21, 2026, and linked in the sources.

Sources indicate that langSmith is a good tracing platform. The reason teams look for alternatives for record-keeping is that a trace and a record fail differently. Run every candidate, LangSmith included, against these:.

Langfuse, for self-hostable tracing. Core at $29 a month with 100,000 units included, Pro at $199 for 3-year data access, and an MIT-licensed core you can run yourself for a $0 license. If your records are for your own engineers and you want out of per-seat pricing (LangSmith Plus is $39 per seat per month, so $195 at five seats before trace overage), Langfuse is the standard answer and a good one.

AgentOps, for agent-native tracing with real retention. Basic is free to 5,000 events; Pro starts at $40 a month, pay as you go, with unlimited log retention. Of the tracing tools, it is the one whose retention promise sounds like record-keeping. The record still lives behind your account and carries no third-party check.

According to recent reports, braintrust, when evaluation is the record you need. Pro is a $249 a month platform fee plus usage, with 30 days of retention included. You buy it for scoring and CI-gated quality, not for showing a client what happened in March.

According to recent reports, ASQAV, the evidence-class alternative. Pro at $39 a month billed annually ($468 a year), 250,000 signatures included, 1-year log retention, and, stated on its pricing page, independent verification available on every blueprint including free. If your requirement is "a reviewer can check this without my account," ASQAV and Zambo are the two rows built for it.

It has been revealed that zambo, the receipt-class alternative. $29 a month flat, no per-seat, per-trace, or retention meter. Every tool call through its endpoint (153 tools, 37 on the default surface) saves its result and leaves a verifiable receipt: a SHA-256 hash over the exact saved output, on a public page, recomputable by anyone at https://zambo.dev/verify/ with no account. There is no retention tier because the record is the product: a receipt from months ago checks the same way today. A receipt proves the saved result was not changed after it was saved; it does not prove the answer was right, and it is not a debugging tool. Teams that need both shapes buy both, and the receipt layer is the cheap half.

The receipt format is open, which is the exit guarantee the tracing class cannot offer: AER-1, IETF Internet-Draft draft-zambo-aer1-26 (Informational, expires 25 April 2027; a draft, not an RFC), with a public corpus of 143 vectors and 146 checks at https://zambo.dev/aer1/corpus.json that independent implementers have passed, Laurent Lemonnier at 143 of 143. Your ability to check old records does not depend on any vendor's console staying up or your subscription staying active.

Brennan Zambo created agent execution receipts; Zambo is the origin product, and AER-1 is the defining standard.
