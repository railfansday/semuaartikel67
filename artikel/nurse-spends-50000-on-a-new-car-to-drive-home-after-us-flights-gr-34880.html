A woman splashed out on a $50,000 car so that she could get home during the nationwide air travel stop in the United States, and ended up driving for 27 hours.

Back on November 13, travel prepares across the United States were plunged into chaos after a nationwide ground stop was put on planes. The stoppage saw 20,000 flights disrupted, leaving passengers to miss getting home, traveling for work, and delayed for at least a day.

In true Planes, Trains, and Automobiles fashion, some travellers set out to find alternative modes of transport as a result of the group stoppage.

That includes Tara Harding and her employees, as they were trying to get home from Charlotte, North Carolina, to Bismarck, North Dakota.

As per CBS News, Harding, a nurse practitioner, tried to look at alternative flights but ultimately gave up on the idea of flying home.

Instead, her and two colleagues looked at rental cars before deciding to buy a $48,000 Ford Bronco. “If it was just me, it would have probably been different,” she said. “But I had two employees that needed to get home to their children, and we had patients on Monday morning.”.

Harding admitted it is an “insane” idea to splash out on a new car, but the group eventually drove it from the car dealer’s showroom to Bismarck in 27 hours.

The three took turns napping they could drive home without stopping, meaning they’d see their families and not have to reschedule any appointments.

As noted, not everyone was so lucky, with more than 12,000 flights being “unable to recover” and get back on track.

One student pilot was also left in a panic as he was informed of the ground stop while in the air on his first solo cross-country flight.
