📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Following last week’s Gemini 4 Hydrogen launch, a small update to AI Mode today lets you start a “temporary thread” that won’t be saved to your Google history.

Like in the Gemini app, anything you discuss in a temporary thread isn’t saved to your account. It won’t appear in conversation history or influence future recommendations.

These AI Mode responses won’t be personalized with past preferences. After you close a temporary thread, there’s no way to access it again.

Sources indicate that to launch, tap the new pen button with a dotted border in the top-right corner, which is the same icon used in the Gemini app. The personalized greeting at the top of the prompt box switches to “Welcome, stranger.”.

It has been revealed that this is an alternative to opening google.com in Incognito Mode, while still being able to use the model picker. Meanwhile, Google’s use of “threads” to describe AI Mode conversations versus “chats” in the Gemini app is interesting.

Temporary threads are rolling out globally starting today on mobile and desktop browsers across all AI Mode languages.

It has been revealed that this joins other changes that Google is still testing for AI Mode. With the latest Google app beta on Android, you can now rapidly upload from Google Drive, which joins Gallery, Camera, and Files as attachment options. You can also now access Canvas on Android.
