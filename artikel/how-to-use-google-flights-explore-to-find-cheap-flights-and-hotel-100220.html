How to use Google Flights Explore to find cheap flights and hotel deals

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (167 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Google Flights Explore is an interactive tool that helps travelers find the cheapest flights from their chosen home airport to destinations worldwide.
  • Users can search with flexible dates, specific regions or open-ended destinations, making it ideal for those with flexible travel plans.
  • The platform allows filtering by number of stops, class of service, airline alliance, and more, and also displays hotel options alongside flight results.
  • Google Flights Explore is accessible both on desktop and mobile, offering similar features across devices.
  • Default searches show one-week trips within a six-month window, but users can adjust dates, trip length and passenger details.
  • Individual airline selection is not available in Explore, but can be managed by starting a search in the main Google Flights tool.
  • Hotel prices shown are the lowest found by Google at the time of search and may vary.
