Even if you keep up with the Kardashians, you might not recognize this Beverly Hills house, which was one of Kim Kardashian’s first real estate purchases. The reality star bought the gated home 16 years ago for $3.4 million, and it served as the setting when basketball player Kris Humphries proposed to her in 2011. But the residence has gotten a major overhaul in the years since Kardashian sold it following the short-lived marriage, and now the Tuscan-style villa is on the market for nearly $8.5 million.

It has been revealed that other listings on our radar this week include another reality TV–linked estate: A former Real Housewife of Beverly Hills is parting with her 13,200-square-foot Hidden Hills house. On the East Coast, a striking Connecticut home by a Frank Lloyd Wright protégé is also on the market. And across the pond, the memorable blue-door townhouse from ‘90s rom-com Notting Hill in London is also for sale. Check them all out below.

One of Kim Kardashian’s earliest real estate purchases was this gated property in Beverly Hills, which she acquired in 2010 for $3.4 million. The Tuscan-style villa quickly became a familiar backdrop on her family’s E! reality show, Keeping Up with the Kardashians, and was even the site of Kris Humphries’s (televised) 2011 proposal to her. In the aftermath of their 72-day marriage, Kardashian sold the half-acre estate, and its upcoming owners tapped fast-rising designer Sebastian Zuchowicki to oversee a complete overhaul. Now on the market for just under $8.5 million with Jack Harris and Michael Fahimian of Beverly Hills Estates, the five-bedroom home has a double-height entry foyer, custom kitchen cabinetry, and a primary suite with a fireplace, two walk-in closets, and a soaking tub.

Entrepreneur Diana Jenkins spent much of her time on The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills nesting in her sprawling Hidden Hills mansion with her newborn daughter, Eliyanah. Now, four years since leaving the Bravo reality series, the native Bosnian is returning to her home country, per Realtor.com—and listing the 13,200-square-foot mansion for $23 million with Sandeep Dhariwal of Sotheby’s International Realty—Beverly Hills Brokerage. Set on 4.4 acres, the seven-bedroom house was built in 2019 and comes with a plethora of perks, among them an infinity-edge pool, barbecue, fitness studio, and home cinema.

According to recent reports, it’s perhaps the most famous house in one of London’s most charming neighborhoods, so beloved that crowds are known to gather around it like the English version of Carrie Bradshaw’s West Village apartment. The blue-doored townhouse where Hugh Grant’s fictional bookseller lived and wooed Julia Roberts’s paparazzi-plagued Hollywood actress in 1999’s Notting Hill is now on the market. And the 2,750-square-foot house can be yours for roughly $10 million.

It has been revealed that with its organic silhouette, cantilevered roofline, and clever use of space, Leuthold House by Allan Gelbin bears the hallmarks of his mentor, legendary architect Frank Lloyd Wright. Completed in 1966 on five rolling acres in Newly introduced Canaan, the five-bedroom house spans roughly 5,700 square feet and underwent a restoration by Hobbs Inc. and JMK Architects in 2014. The interiors feature long expanses of glass and mahogany, while the grounds come with a spring-fed pond, a pool, a one-bedroom guest house, and 150 specimen trees. Listed for $6.2 million with John Engel and Melissa Engel of Douglas Elliman, the two-story house is on the market for the first time in 29 years.

It has been revealed that one of the art world’s most famous couples once lived and worked in a gated townhouse a block from Washington Square Park. Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner didn’t own the MacDougal Alley home—a brick-faced carriage house built in the 1870s—but rather stayed there in the winter of 1950 due to the generosity of its owner, a fellow celebrated artist and their friend Alfonso Ossorio. Now, Ossorio’s 1,500-square-foot residence is on the market for the first time since 1988, per the New York Post. The asking price for the two-bedroom, two-bath home is nearly $8 million.
