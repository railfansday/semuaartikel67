Technology has changed rapidly over the last few decades. While we've got hardware that's so much more powerful than what came 10 or 20 years ago, there's still a lot of enjoyment to be had in the classics. That's why there's a huge market for retro hardware, from game controllers designed to look like classic gamepads from old consoles, to full re-releases of old machines, with plenty of fun (and well-priced) accessories in between.

If you're buying for a retro aficionado, you're going to want to pick up something that's fun to use and has a serious nostalgic edge. There's a whole host of gear out there for those who prefer an '80s or '90s aesthetic, rather than the products we live with today.

In a recent development, you can relive the glory days of yesteryear today with these retro gift ideas.
