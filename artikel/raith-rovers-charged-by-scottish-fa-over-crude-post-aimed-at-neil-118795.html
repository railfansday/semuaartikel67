Raith Rovers have been charged by the Scottish Football Association over a social media post mocking Dunfermline Athletic manager Neil Lennon in the aftermath of the Fife derby.

Following the 0-0 draw at Stark's Park on November 6, Lennon made a pinching gesture with his thumb and forefinger towards rival supporters which was widely interpreted as a jibe calling Raith Rovers a "small club".

Recent findings suggest that shortly after the final whistle, Raith's official X account shared a photo of Lennon making the gesture alongside the caption, "How big is your penis, Neil?". The club deleted the post 52 minutes later after it went viral.

The SFA compliance officer has now issued the Kirkcaldy outfit with a notice of complaint relating to a breach of Disciplinary Rule 73, which reads: "No recognised football body, club, official, Team Official or other member of Team Staff, player, match official or other person under the jurisdiction of the Scottish FA shall in an interview, a ‘blog’ on the internet, on a social networking or micro-blogging site, or in any other manner calculated or likely to lead to publicity, make comment(s) of a discriminatory or offensive nature based on, but not limited to, race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation or disability, or that endorse or encourage foul play or violent Conduct or are otherwise offensive.

"There shall be a presumption that any material published in such a manner was published in the name of and/or with the authority of the person or body bearing to have published the material.".

Sanctions typically start at a £500 lower-end fine and rise up to a £5,000 top-end fine.

Speaking about the incident last week, Lennon described the episode as "embarrassing for them", referring to Raith, adding; "“You know, that’s like an official club Twitter account, which is embarrassing. I’ll say no more than that.”.

Industry observers note that raith are scheduled to face a Hampden disciplinary hearing on December 17. This falls just over a week before the two sides meet again at East End Park on Boxing Day.
