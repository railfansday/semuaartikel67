According to recent reports, some of the best off-duty celebrity looks happen post-workout. Think: Brittany Murphy smoking a cigarette in leg warmers, Madonna in an Ed Hardy tank and capri pants, Alexa Demie leaving Pilates in a Prada headband. The latest to join their ranks? Pop royalty Lady Gaga, who reminded us that your gym wardrobe needn’t be confined to Alo trainers.

Sources indicate that photographed leaving a yoga studio in Mill Valley, California, clutching a jar of what I can only assume is some sort of bone broth, Gaga’s all-black look—a batwing T-shirt, tracksuit bottoms, Balenciaga baseball cap and Gentle Monster sunglasses—might, at first glance, pass for standard workout attire. That is, until you get to the Saint Laurent slingbacks. Proof that there’s still room for a little pop-star theatrics, even when sweating your way through a Warrior One pose.

Of course, Gaga is hardly a newcomer to the impractical shoe. After welcoming her firstborn, Rose Bean Polansky, this autumn, she was spotted leaving a hair salon in a cape and ripped jeans, anchored by a pair of Balenciaga’s vertiginous Camden platform heels. An appropriate take on postpartum dressing from the woman who once told Jonathan Ross, “I would rather die than have my fans see me without a pair of heels on.” Yoga no exception.
