Artem Dovbyk, so far, hasn’t found the redemption he hoped for in Bologna.

It has been revealed that the Ukrainian striker joined the Rossoblu on loan this summer with an option to buy, hoping to rediscover his best self. Nothing of the sort.

The 1997-born striker has played just 105 minutes, scoring a goal against Sassuolo.

The Felsinei are far from satisfied with the former Girona player. Dovbyk hasn’t found any playing time under either Domenico Tedesco or Raffaele Palladino; the player isn’t convincing.

Bologna, as reported by Tuttosport, are dissatisfied with Artem Dovbyk’s performance, so much so that they are considering terminating his loan six months early. Roma, for their part, have no intention of reinstating him, so they will need to find a upcoming club if they decide to terminate his loan early.

According to Il Corriere di Bologna, the Ukrainian striker could return to La Liga, with Valencia reportedly pushing to have him available in January.

The Rossoblu club already has a replacement in mind: Lorenzo Lucca, who is leaving Napoli.

According to recent reports, the former Udinese player is a target for Palladino, who wants a striker more suited to his style of play than Dovbyk. The situation will need to be monitored, pending concrete developments. Roma is aware that there will be little room for the 1997-born striker at Bologna.
