📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Internal IT Manager owns the Service Desk and the Corporate Systems that run the business, and builds the AI driven workflows that connect them. You run the team and you also build: you design the automations, ship the integrations, and show the team how it is done. You set priorities, coach each person, and build alongside them.

You lead a small, hands on team that keeps the company running and builds toward AI Native. The team shares one queue, reviews metrics, and brings automation ideas to the company.

ServiceDesk and ticketing• Run the ServiceDesk across onboarding, offboarding, access requests, hardware and software support.• Own the ticketing platform: queues, categories, SLAs, escalation paths, and reporting.• Cut ticket volume through self service, knowledge base content, and AI assisted triage and resolution.Asset management• Maintain one accurate inventory of hardware, software licenses, and SaaS subscriptions.• Manage the full asset lifecycle: procurement, provisioning, tracking, refresh, and secure disposal.• Reconcile licenses against real usage to remove waste before each renewal.Corporate systems• Administer the core business stack: identity and SSO (Okta), device management (Iru), collaboration tools, and the systems used by People, Sales, Legal, and Finance.• Keep systems secure, documented, and available, with clear owners and change control.• Evaluate upcoming tools and retire those that no longer earn their cost.Corporate contracts and vendors• Manage IT vendor relationships, renewals, and negotiations.• Maintain a contract register with owners, terms, renewal dates, and spend.• Work with Legal and Finance on reviews, security questionnaires, and budget forecasts.

AI and enterprise workflowsYou treat AI as a core tool for running IT, and you build with it yourself.• Design and ship automated workflows for onboarding, offboarding, access provisioning, approvals, and renewals.• Integrate systems through APIs, webhooks, and iPaaS tools so data moves between ticketing, identity, HR, CRM, finance, and contract platforms without manual steps.• Deploy AI agents and assistants for ticket triage, knowledge search, request fulfillment, and employee support.• Write scripts and light code (Python, JavaScript, PowerShell) when a ready made tool falls short.• Set guardrails for AI use: data handling, access scope, review steps, and audit trails, agreed with Legal and Security.• Measure each automation by hours saved, errors avoided, and adoption, then improve or retire it.

• 5+ years in IT operations, with 2+ years leading a IT Operations or internal IT team.• Hands on experience administering corporate systems: identity and SSO (Okta), MDM, Google Workspace, and SaaS management.• Working knowledge of vendor contracts, renewals, and procurement.• Demonstrated delivery of automations and integrations using APIs, webhooks, and automation tools such as Zapier, or n8n.• Practical experience applying AI tools to IT work: agents, assistants, and workflow automation.• Scripting ability in Python, Bash, or PowerShell.• Strong written and spoken communication, with the ability to work with Legal, People, Sales, and Finance.• Sound judgment on security, privacy, and compliance basics.
