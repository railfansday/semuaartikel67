If you’ve been curious about smart rings but have been put off by their high price tags, Black Friday is a good time to consider giving one a try. That’s because the Oura Ring 5, which we consider the best smart ring you can buy, has dropped to an all-time low of $299 ($100 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Oura’s online storefront for Black Friday.

Compared to the Ring 4, the Oura Ring 5 offers a thinner, lighter, and more durable design that’s more comfortable to wear throughout the day and while sleeping. Like its predecessor, it can track your sleep, activity, heart rate, and other health metrics, giving you insights into your overall well-being without requiring you to constantly check a screen. It also offers roughly the same battery life and sensors as the prior model, though its latest charging case supports wireless charging. (Unfortunately, the case is currently sold separately for $99.).

Oura has also introduced several newly introduced software features, including Health Radar, which looks for potentially significant changes in your health. You can also import medical lab results, track GLP-1 medications, and access AI-assisted medical guidance through Oura’s partnership with Counsel Health. My colleague Victoria Song appreciated how the latter makes it easier to connect with an actual doctor, though she found it better suited for quick medical questions than more serious health concerns.

However, none of these software features are exclusive to the Ring 5, and you’ll still need a $5.99 monthly Oura membership to access most of its health insights. Given how similar the two generations are, we wouldn’t recommend upgrading if you already own a Ring 4. But if you’re fresh to smart rings, the current discount makes Oura’s latest model much more attractive, especially since it’s only $50 more than the Ring 4’s starting price.
