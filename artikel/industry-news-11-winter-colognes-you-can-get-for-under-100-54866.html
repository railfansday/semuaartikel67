Industry News: 11 Winter Colognes You Can Get for Under $100

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that there’s never been a better time to get into personal fragrance. The cologne market is booming with frequent newly introduced rolls out from luxury fashion houses and perfumeries, yes—but it’s also filled with tons of great scents from more accessible and affordable brands. And with the temperatures finally dropping below 50°F in New York and Thanksgiving coming up next week, we’re officially in winter mode. That means getting our puffer coats out of storage and changing up our seasonal fragrance rotation.

In a recent development, so out with the fresh, aquatic, citrusy fragrances that belong on the beach and in with the warm, spicy, smoky scents that make the wintertime feel cozy. We’re talking cardamom and cloves, smoky tobacco and incense, sweet vanilla and smooth amber. Want to capture the scent of sitting by the fireplace and sipping a hot cocoa while wearing a cashmere sweater? These scents will do that—and at a fraction of the cost of a designer fragrance. We rounded up our picks for the winter that all come in at under $100, so you can save the substantial bucks for holiday shopping.

If you’ve checked out any of our other affordable-cologne roundups, you won’t be surprised to see Cremo at the top of our list here. We talk about the brand a lot, but it’s for a good reason: Cremo’s colognes are some of our favorites out there, and they literally cost under $25 each. Spice & Black Vanilla is our favorite spicy scent from the lineup, and the warm blend of cardamom, vanilla, tobacco, and woods is perfect for winter. Wear it for a cold-weather date night, a fancy dinner, or really any occasion.

It has been revealed that want a decent cologne mist you can easily buy on Amazon for just $13? Old Spice has been around for almost 90 years—yes, it may remind you of your dad, but it’s been around for so long because it’s a classic that just works. But shaking things up a bit, the brand released a new collection of cologne mists earlier this year, which includes this Cashmere & Vanilla Wood scent bursting with spices and powdery iris. For $13, you might as well order a few.

One of our favorite boozy colognes (and all-time favorite colognes in general) is Jazz Club from Maison Margiela’s Replica line. It’s one you’ve probably heard of and read about many times in our fragrance roundups, and since the 1 oz bottle is only $89, I just couldn’t leave it off this list. With rum, tobacco, and vanilla bean at the center, Jazz Club is the olfactory equivalent of sipping a cocktail in a smoke-filled bar. In other words, it’s the perfect sexy winter scent.

According to recent reports, gap recently introduced a fresh addition to its revitalized fragrance line (originally from the ’90s): Bliss. The gourmand EDP adds some creamy sweetness to the otherwise mostly fresh and earthy collection, and it came just in time for winter. It delights the senses with notes of butterscotch, cocoa bean, coconut milk, and vanilla bourbon, and it’s practically making my mouth water just thinking about it.

It has been revealed that huron has only five different scents, but it’s a true example of a brand that puts quality over quantity. I love each and every one of the brand’s EDPs, and while the marine Sea Salt + Driftwood and Original Citrus + Eucalyptus are both fresh and perfect for the summer, the Sandalwood + Black Pepper fragrance is definitely our winter choice. It’s warm, woody, and spicy, with some airy florals at the heart and rich amber and more woods at the base.

If you like the scent of Tom Ford’s Oud Wood but can’t get past that $300 price tag, we hear you. That’s why House of Twist made Wood No. 28; it’s not exactly Oud Wood, but it’s close enough. And for just $48, we’ll take it. The fragrance is a nice blend of nutty cardamom, oud wood, vanilla, and creamy sandalwood—a combination pretty much anyone can get behind.

Making the cut at $99 is Phlur’s newest fragrance: Heavy Cream. It’s a rich gourmand that’s balanced out by some bright citrus and airy florals at the top and heart of the fragrance, so it doesn’t get too overwhelmingly heavy. It definitely has the notes you’d expect of a fragrance inspired by heavy cream, like vanilla, amber, and creamy musks.

Fine’ry is another one of our go-to brands when it comes to affordable colognes we actually like. And this winter, our top choice from the lineup is Smoke & Leather, which smells like—you guessed it—smoky tobacco and rich leather with some woody undertones. It definitely brings the heat on a cold day.

Boy Smells is known for pairing traditionally masculine and feminine scent notes for truly unisex fragrances that anyone can enjoy. Hinoki Fantôme is a fan favorite thanks to its blend of spices, woods, and smoke, which are underscored by earthy oakmoss, orris, and patchouli. You really can’t put this fragrance in one category, because it spans so many. It’s a complex fragrance that really works, especially for the winter.

According to recent reports, earthy scents are chameleons that can work in any season, so it really just matters what they’re paired with. In the instance of Stetson’s Free Rein from its new Untamed collection, the pairing of earthy notes of wild verbena, sweetgrass, prairie moss, and patchouli with vetiver smoke and red amber makes it a warm, rich fragrance that adds some heat to your cold-weather outfit.

Recent findings suggest that florals are, generally speaking, made for spring and summer—but sometimes you get a gorgeous blend of florals with woods, musks, or amber, and it becomes perfect for wintertime. That’s exactly what Lafco has done with its Golden Morning scent: It’s got light floral notes of apple blossom, lavender, violet leaf, and rose, which all play nicely with a rich, sweet base of woods, honey, amber, and musks.
