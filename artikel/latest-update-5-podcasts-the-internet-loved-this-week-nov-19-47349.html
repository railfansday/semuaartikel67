Whether you're into deadpan showdowns, niche hot takes, or simply wholesome interviews between creative minds, here's what we reckon is worth your time (and of course, we'd never leave out Mashable's own Say More, what are you, nuts?).

Sources indicate that do Alex Cooper and Trixie Mattel know what a floppy disk is for? No! Are they both pink-adorned powerhouses at the top of their damn game? Absolutely. This week's Call Her Daddy is a must-watch, a deep chat between natural chatters, shrouded in blush.

How to watch/listen: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, TikTok, Instagram.

It has been revealed that if you haven't joined Amy Poehler for her delightful chat with Andrew Scott, take a seat. In what might be the giggliest Good Hang yet, the Elsinore star joins the host in a chat about all things mischievous, from his early days of Shakespeare to his iconic roles in Sherlock and Fleabag and making everyone cry in All of Us Strangers — and there's a special guest question from his Fleabag and Elsinore co-star Olivia Colman.

How to watch/listen: YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Instagram.

According to recent reports, for Mashable's Say More this week, entertainment reporter Belen Edwards sat down with The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power cast to choose the ultimate Middle-earth squad. Charlie Vickers, Cynthia Addai-Robinson, Jamie Campbell Bower, and Daniel Weyman stopped by the studio for a game of Choose Your Squad, in which they rally the ultimate team to take down Sauron.

How to watch/listen: Mashable, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok.

It has been revealed that ziwe Fumudoh can rattle a guest, but Kathy Griffin is hard to shake. On this week's Iconic Guest, the unapologetic comedy icon joined the unflinching host for a NSFW interview to remember. "Kathy, is that the truth or do you have to add an 'allegedly?'" is a question Ziwe could ask multiple times.

How to watch/listen: YouTube, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, TikTok, Instagram.
