Non-stimulants are noncontrolled prescription medications used to treat the core ADHD symptoms, inattention, hyperactivity, and/or impulsivity.

With consistent daily dosing, non-stimulant medications can provide continuous medication exposure over a 24-hour period.

Most non-stimulant medications offer a distinct and targeted approach to help manage daily ADHD symptoms, working gradually over time to improve symptom control.

Every non-stimulant ADHD medication has a different safety profile, so side effects can vary. Having an open conversation with your provider before starting treatment is the best way to ensure your plan fits your needs and daily routine.
