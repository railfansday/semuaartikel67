The deal also comes at an ideal time for holiday and winter streaming. Paramount+ carries a huge library of hit movies and TV shows, and it offers livestreams of sports, including certain NFL games, all UFC fights, and soccer matches.

Paramount+ offers two subscription tiers: Essential and Premium. Essential normally costs $8.99 a month and includes ads on all content. This proposal also offers access to select Showtime series and lets you watch some NFL and UEFA Champions League games online.

It has been revealed that the Premium initiative (normally $13.99 a month) removes ads from on-demand content and gets you access to all of Showtime. Plus, you can watch CBS live for tons of sports content, and you can download content for offline viewing. 4K UHD, Dolby Vision, or HDR10 are also supported with the Premium plan.

But the best part about Paramount+’s Black Friday deal is that both aims are discounted to the same price of $2.99 a month for the first two months.
