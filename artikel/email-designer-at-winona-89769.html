📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Winona is one of the leading telemedicine companies providing HRT for women in menopause. We’ve built all of our technology in-house including an EHR, patient care admin system, patient portal, website, marketing technology etc. Winona has two compounding pharmacies and a team of in-house physicians providing world-class care via a fully vertically integrated business model.

It has been revealed that we’re looking for an experienced Email Designer with a strong background in email design, HTML email development, responsive design principles, and email platform limitations. The ideal candidate is highly proficient in Figma and experienced designing emails for platforms such as Iterable, Klaviyo, and similar email marketing tools, with a strong command of visual hierarchy, typography, brand consistency, and conversion-focused design. You’ll translate creative briefs into polished, high-performing emails across desktop and mobile devices, build and maintain reusable templates and modular design systems, and ensure every design is optimized for accessibility, usability, and compatibility across major email clients.

Translate creative briefs into polished, engaging email designs.

Build reusable templates and modular components, and maintain email design systems.

Ensure designs are optimized for accessibility, usability, and compatibility across major email clients.

Design for both desktop and mobile, applying responsive and mobile-first principles.

Apply email marketing best practices, including CTA placement, personalization, A/B testing, and performance optimization.

Collaborate with lifecycle marketing, copywriting, and development teams, incorporating feedback throughout the design process.

Manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously.

Maintain a high standard of quality through thorough design QA.

According to recent reports, demonstrated experience specifically in email design, with a portfolio that includes email campaigns, templates, and responsive email designs. General graphic or web design experience alone is not sufficient.

Experience with email marketing platforms such as Iterable, Klaviyo, or Mailchimp.

Recent findings suggest that familiarity with HTML/CSS for email development. (Experience developing HTML emails is a plus, but not required.).

Strong understanding of email design best practices, responsive and mobile-first design, HTML email limitations, and email client compatibility (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.).

Strong understanding of visual hierarchy, typography, accessibility standards, and conversion-focused design.

Experience with modular email design systems, reusable components, dynamic content, personalization, A/B testing, and email performance optimization.

Familiarity with lifecycle marketing strategies, customer journeys, and designing for different campaign types, including promotional, transactional, and automated email flows.

Proficient and comfortable using AI-powered design and productivity tools to streamline workflows and improve efficiency, with a willingness to continuously explore new AI capabilities.

Familiarity with project management tools (ClickUp, Asana, or similar).

Must be available to start as soon as possible, with no extended planned PTO between January and March.
