Sources indicate that the 4K Select was only released last year, but the 4K has officially replaced it. It features the Vega 2.0 operating system for even speedier navigation and a more robust app store. Amazon's Black Friday sale is a 12-day event, lasting from Nov. 19 to Nov. 30. Cyber Monday falls on Nov. 30, so the end of Amazon's sale marks the end of the Black Friday/Cyber Monday programming from the mega-retailer. That noted, historically we've seen some deals last into the days following, and even return ahead of Christmas in rarer cases.

In a recent development, most items from Amazon from Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 can be returned until Jan. 31, 2027, with some exceptions. Apple products, for instance, must be returned by Jan. 15, 2027.
