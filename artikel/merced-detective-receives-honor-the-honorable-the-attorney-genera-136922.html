Merced detective receives Honor the Honorable: The Attorney General’s Award for Distinguished Service in Policing

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Detective Jennifer Dabney of the Merced Police Department has received the Honor the Honorable: The Attorney General’s Award for Distinguished Service in Policing, one of the Department of Justice's highest honors for state and local law enforcement officers. U.S. Attorney Eric Grant attended the ceremony where Attorney General Todd Blanche unveiled the award recipients. This award represents the Department of Justice’s reaffirmed commitment to uplifting the valor and exceptional contributions to policing of our great nation’s law enforcement personnel.
