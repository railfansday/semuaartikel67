📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

American Airlines will add upcoming connections and cut others as it works to optimize its network at Los Angeles.

The carrier confirmed Friday that it will launch year-round service from LAX to Reno, Nevada, and seasonal service to Bozeman, Montana, and Grand Junction, Colorado.

LA-Reno will operate three times weekly, starting May 27, 2027, using Embraer E175 aircraft.

Recent findings suggest that the routes to Bozeman and Grand Junction will operate from May 27 to Aug. 9, with Bozeman service using an E175 and Grand Junction flights using a Bombardier CRJ700.

An American official remarked Grand Junction is a “seasonal market addition” while service to Aspen/Pitkin County, Colorado, is temporarily suspended due to construction work there.

American will also add frequencies on some existing routes out of LAX next summer:.

The carrier also confirmed that it will drop service from Los Angeles to Cleveland and Des Moines, Iowa, on February 11.
