Goncalo Inacio’s name is back in the headlines today and AC Milan are very hopeful of securing him in the upcoming year.

Although talk of a striker has dominated headlines recently, neither Gerry Cardinale nor Ruben Amorim have forgotten about the need to sign a defender. In fact, the pair have never stopped considering a January signing in the backline.

A late attempt was made for Inacio in the summer, and Milan have effectively left the deal on hold with the intention of picking it back up at the first possible opportunity.

Sources indicate that according to Gazzetta dello Sport, via MilanNews, the Portuguese defender is not seen as a replacement. Instead, he’ll be a starter, alongside Strahinja Pavlovic and Mario Gila, and someone who can raise the bar.

According to recent reports, indeed, he’s someone the club wants to bring in as a pillar for the future, and this is why Amorim and Cardinale are desperate to sign him in January. So much so that they’re not put off by the €40-45 million sought by Sporting.

Recent findings suggest that it’s undeniably a high asking price, but it’s consistent with the cost of the profile Amorim seeks. So, they’d be inclined to pay as much to secure their – importantly – shared top target.
