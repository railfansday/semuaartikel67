Solana has remained one of the major cryptocurrencies that have been heavily affected by the persistent crypto market volatility, stalling demand from both retail and institutional investors.

While Solana has continued to struggle amid the growing market uncertainty, it appears that traders are increasingly exiting their positions even across its DeFi market.

Recent data showcased by blockchain monitoring platform Lookonchain highlights that two Solana whales unstaked a total of 2.31 million SOL on November 19.

While the total amount of SOL unstaked was worth about $169.4 million, further data demonstrated that the whales proceeded to transfer the tokens to Coinbase, a leading cryptocurrency exchange in the U.S.

It is important to note that the act of withdrawing SOL previously committed to the network's staking mechanism is regarded as unstaking.

While this move automatically gives holders the ability to transfer, sell, or use the unstaked tokens, the massive unstaking of 2.31 million SOL from large holders has drawn attention within the crypto community regarding the intention behind the move.

With curiosity on the rise about the whales' decision to move such a large amount of SOL to Coinbase Prime, the timing of the move has triggered speculation that the whales may be intending to sell the tokens.

Notably, the move arrived when Solana was trading around $74, briefly shifting into green territory just a few days after it retested $67, the lowest level seen since June 2026.

Since the prolonged price correction has continued to weaken investor confidence, it appears that the whales have acted upon the growing fear and withdrawn the assets they had initially committed to the Solana network to hedge against further losses.
