"Here We Go Again" - After Delaying For Silksong, Indie Dev Braces For A Sea Of Sorrow Clash

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There were a good few indies set for launch in September last year that ended up being delayed to escape the blast radius of Hollow Knight: Silksong. Aeternum Game Studios' metroidvania, Aeterna Lucis, was one such title, pushing its release date to 3rd December 2026 to ensure it was way out of the way of whatever Team Cherry was cooking up. Heh, well...

According to recent reports, yesterday, the Silksong dev announced an upcoming 'Cherry Time' special presentation, promising news and announcements from the studio, and an update on the hotly-anticipated Sea of Sorrow DLC. The showcase is tomorrow (27th November), so there's every chance that, in dodging the initial launch, Aeterna Lucis has wound up right in the way of the DLC (thanks, Steam Hype).

It has been revealed that this time, however, the dev is standing firm. "A lot of people have been waiting for the Queen of Light for a long time now, and we don't want to make them wait any longer," the dev wrote in a message on social media, neatly captioned "Here we go again".

Industry observers note that it's all very jokey and light-hearted, with Aeternum even proposing a Silksong bundle if the dates do happen to clash. Still, there's room for a little cursing of fate: "With all the love in the world: is it just us, or does the universe have it in for us?".

You'll find the full statement below, followed by the post from Bluesky:.

A little over a year ago, just as we were about to launch our game, you announced Silksong. We stepped aside, pushed the whole project back a year and, honestly, it was one of the best decisions we've ever made: the genre had an incredible year, and we got to enjoy Silksong as fans and polish Aeterna Lucis into the game we always dreamed it would be.

Now here we are again, just days from launch. Aeterna Lucis, our latest metroidvania, comes out on December 3... and you announce Cherry Time. This time we're staying put. Not to go against you, but because a lot of people have been waiting for the Queen of Light for a long time, and we don't want to make them wait any longer.

In a recent development, whatever happens on Friday, we'll be watching like any other fan. And if Sea of Sorrow ends up launching on the same day, the real winners will be metroidvania fans.

That remarked, we have to ask, with all the love in the world: is it just us, or does the universe have it in for us? Hahaha. And since you're about to wreck our lives all over again, the least we can do is make a bundle together.

With all our love (and a little bit of fear), The Aeternum Game Studios team.

While we can't imagine Sea of Sorrow will have quite the industry-shaking impact that the base game did, it sure would be unfortunate to see an indie like Aeterna Lucis caught in the line of fire again. Hey, at least the dev is able to have a laugh with it.
