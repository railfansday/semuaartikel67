Having completed a search and rescue operation that saved all guests and crew of the American Heritage, the United States Coast Guard is now working on salvaging the riverboat.

According to a recent upgrade, officers have shifted their focus to removing the American Cruise Lines (ACL) vessel from the incident site at the Port of New Orleans.

“We are working closely with ACL and our local partners to remove any remaining pollution and review salvage aims,” U.S. Coast Guard Heartland stated.

“Our top priority during this process is the safety of the public, responders and the marine environment,” it added in a social media statement.

The Coast Guard also stated it is convening a Marine Investigation Board to fully investigate the cause behind the incident.

“In the upcoming days we will work closely with the National Transportation Safety Board to gather information that will be used during the investigation process.”.

The Coast Guard noted that the Mississippi River is at the moment open to limited two-way traffic, with a safety zone remaining in effect covering 500 feet from the American Heritage.

Vessels sailing in the area are asked to use a limited safe speed to ensure the safety of salvage operations, the Coast Guard added.

The American Heritage capsized and sank last Friday after being struck by the tanker American Asphalt while docked in Newly introduced Orleans.

According to local authorities, about 129 passengers were onboard during the incident, with all being safely rescued.

Thirty-two people were taken to local hospitals, and none had critical injuries, including 30 from the ship and two first responders.

In an official statement, American Cruise Lines thanked authorities and officials for their support in evacuating the ship.

“We are also grateful to the U.S. Coast Guard, the Port of Fresh Orleans, and the New Orleans Police, Fire, and EMS Departments for their ongoing help, and we will continue working in partnership with them throughout the entire investigation and salvage efforts,” the company added.
