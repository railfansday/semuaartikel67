Sunderland boss Regis le Bris has revealed that at least five Sunderland players could miss the derby match against Newcastle United at St James’ Park.

That game is in 17 days time on Sunday 6 December with a 2pm kick-off.

Regis le Bris announcing on Thursday that all five of these Sunderland players will miss the Premier League away game at Villa Park on Saturday.

The quintet that definitely miss the weekend’s Aston Villa away game are Brian Brobbey, Dan Ballard, Granit Xhaka, Romaine Mundle and Habib Diarra.

Two of these five Sunderland players will for sure miss the Newcastle United game, Regis le Bris saying today: “Daniel Ballard and Romaine Mundle will be back training in mid-December.”.

The way the Sunderland manager was speaking, it appears very unlikely that Brian Brobbey or Granit Xhaka will be available for the derby.

In a recent development, on Brian Brobbey, Regis le Bris saying today: “It is a significant injury for Brian, which we will have to assess weekly. It is a fresh injury for us but it is too early to know the timeline. He is sad and disappointed because he has worked so hard. He was a monster against Chelsea, so I think it is important for us and him to reset. I hope that he will use this period to get better.”.

As for Granit Xhaka, Regis le Bris stating he won’t be back until after Brian Brobbey: “For Granit it is a bit longer, it is a calf injury.”.

The only one of the five Sunderland players who appears to have any kind of realistic chance of featuring in the Newcastle United match is Habib Diarra.

With Regis le Bris explaining: “For Habib Diarra it is going well and we will see in the next two weeks or so.”.
