Wesley Fofana has laughed off his penalty miss that sent Chelsea out of the Carabao Cup in an interview that could complicate his relationship with an already disappointed fanbase.

Fofana was part of a Chelsea defence that has lost 1-0 to Sunderland and 4-1 at home to Leeds in the Blues’ last two games as Xabi Alonso’s side struggle for form in the Premier League.

The 25-year-old Fofana has conducted a two-and-a-half-hour interview with Zack Nani TV in which he admitted he almost quit football during Enzo Maresca’s time in charge of Chelsea due to regular long-term injury setbacks.

In a recent development, but in the interview, which it is understood was not arranged by the club, Fofana also spoke about missing the decisive penalty in the shootout as Chelsea were eliminated by Liverpool from the Carabao Cup last month at Anfield. The manner in which he discussed his miss threatens to irk some supporters.

“The Liverpool match was tough to take, but I moved on quickly because that’s just part of football,” Fofana noted.

“I took responsibility for the penalty - I missed it - and, well, I knew I wasn’t a good penalty taker to begin with, you know [laughs]?”.

Industry observers note that fofana continued: “So, I just thought, ‘Okay, I’ll own it.’ But I ended up conceding a penalty during the match - it was a handball - but that’s just part of the game. I could have avoided it; it’s the kind of thing we talk about - you have to be consistent, and you have to keep improving in those areas.

“But after the penalty, I stepped up, because I’m one of the most experienced players. Out on the field, there were a lot of young guys. And, well, you had to get out there, you know?

“You’ve got to go for it. And the way I look at it, if I fail, well, I’ve been through things. I’ve lived through a lot. If I fail, it’s not that it’s not a significant deal - sure, it sucks, obviously - but as for me, if I’m the one being attacked, it’s no big deal.

“It’s not the first time I’ve been attacked. It’s fine; it doesn’t matter. I couldn’t care less. I know there’s another match in three days.

“In three days’ time, you win, and people forget. They’ve already forgotten about your penalty. That’s just how football works. I stepped up, took it, and missed.
