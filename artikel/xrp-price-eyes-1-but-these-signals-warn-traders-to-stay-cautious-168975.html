📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that ripple [XRP] was up 3.46% in the past 24 hours, with a daily trading volume uptick of 34%. The price bounce followed a Bitcoin [BTC] move back above $61k, which was likely a result of a short squeeze.

This price bounce was reflected across the altcoin market, which has gained 1.72% in its cumulative market cap in a day, reaching $2.07 trillion at the time of writing.

Industry observers note that XRP was about to test the $1 resistance zone, a place it had faced rejection from earlier this week. Can the bulls succeed in reclaiming it as support this time?

In September and October, XRP was oscillating between the $1.30 support zone and the $1.50-$1.55 supply zone. This consolidation gave way to a downtrend that reached a low below $0.90.

Over the past two weeks, the $0.893 and $1.00 levels have emerged as key levels on the daily timeframe for swing traders. A daily session close outside these boundaries will likely decide the direction of the next swing price move.

It has been revealed that at press time, the CMF was at -0.12, signaling heavy selling pressure and capital outflows. The MACD was also moving below the zero line, a sign that downward momentum was prevalent. The MACD line made a bullish crossover above the signal line, an indication of short-term bounce potential.

Despite encouraging ETF flows over the past year, the altcoin’s performance had been lacklustre.

Recent findings suggest that the trend was now firmly bearish, the Directional Movement Index asserted. This indicator’s -DI and ADX readings above 20 confirmed the downtrend, alongside the price structure.

Sources indicate that like the 1-day chart indicated a sweep above $1 on Monday, XRP recently made a sweep of the $0.961 local peak in recent hours. Despite the short-term upward momentum, there is a chance of a pullback.

Potential buyers can wait to see the price reaction from the $0.92 and $0.86-$0.88 demand zones.

A bullish reaction on the hourly chart, in the form of something like an engulfing candle, would be a sign that bulls were ready to drive a move toward $1.00.

Sources indicate that traders should be wary of short-term volatility and wait for a positive reaction before buying. Risk-averse traders can remain sidelined until the higher timeframe trend gives a newly introduced direction with a move beyond $1.00 or $0.893.
