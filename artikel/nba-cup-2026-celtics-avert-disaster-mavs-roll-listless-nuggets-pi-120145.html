The Boston Celtics entered halftime of Tuesday's NBA Cup game apparently well on their way to snapping a four-game losing streak.

In a recent development, they eventually got there. But it wasn't easy as they had to fend off a furious comeback effort from the Washington Wizards.

The Celtics opened an 80-49 lead at halftime that the Wizards cut to 117-112 in the final four minutes. But the Celtics held on from there to avert disaster via a 131-116 victory.

A Jayson Tatum 3-pointer and two more by Baylor Scheierman in the game's final minutes helped ensure that the Celtics got back into the win column.

Industry observers note that with the win, the Celtics improved to 3-0 in NBA Cup play to maintain control of East Group C. The Wizards dropped to 2-2 in the NBA Cup.

Recent findings suggest that tatum led the effort with 41 points, 8 assists and 6 rebounds. He shot 6 of 15 from 3, but made all of them before halftime as cold second-half 3-point shooting helped the Wizards mount their rally.

According to recent reports, the Celtics were red-hot in the first half while shooting 15 of 26 (57.7%) from long distance. They shot 7 of 25 (28%) after halftime and needed three of those in the final minutes after the Wizards cut their deficit to five points.

Ultimately, their 43.1% success rate from 3 was a cornerstone of their win, but questions will surely surface about the wisdom of continuing to hoist up 3s after building a 31-point lead.

Sources indicate that paul George had no such struggles with a 28-point, 9-assist effort in which he shot 6 of 9 from 3. Anthony Davis led the Wizards with 33 points, 13 rebounds, 2 blocks and a steal.

Cooper Flagg was the best player on the floor Tuesday night in a Mavericks win over the Nuggets.

In a matchup of a former NBA champion and four-time MVP against a lineup featuring a rising NBA start, youth prevailed in Denver.

Cooper Flagg and the Dallas Mavericks rolled a listless Nuggets team in Denver to the tune of a a 120-101 win.

The Mavericks limited the Nuggets to 17 first-quarter points, then closed the second quarter with a 15-0 run to take a 58-39 lead into the break.

According to recent reports, the Nuggets answered with a 38-point third quarter and cut it as close as 80-77. But the Mavericks answered the run and closed the game strong to silence Ball Arena down the stretch with an overwhelming win.

Flagg led seven Mavericks players in double figures with 24 points, a career-high 12 assists and 6 rebounds.

Sources indicate that jamal Murray countered with 20 points and three rebounds on a 6-of-17 shooting effort. Nikola Jokić had a relatively quiet scoring night with 18 points, 11 rebounds, 5 assists and 5 turnovers.

Sources indicate that with the win, the Mavericks (10-7) improved to 3-0 in Cup play to take control of West Group A. The Nuggets (10-5) dropped to 2-1 in the NBA Cup to fall behind the Mavs.

Cade Cunningham was a late scratch and watched Tuesday's Pistons win in street clothes.

The Detroit Pistons secured an NBA Cup win over the Orlando Magic, but it was overshadowed by concern for Cade Cunningham, who was a late scratch due to a lingering knee injury.

The Pistons played without their All-NBA guard and cruised to a 104-86 win to hand the Magic their first loss in NBA Cup play. The Pistons (11-6) are now 2-1 in Cup play, while the Magic (7-10) drop to 3-1. The loss blocked the Magic from from a chance to clinch East Group A.

Cunningham was listed as probable prior to the game due to lingering knee soreness. But he was a late scratch before an NBA Cup game, raising concern that his injury is one that could further linger.

Head coach J.B. Bickerstaff declined to offer details on Cunningham's condition in his pregame news conference. He also didn't say much to tamp down concern.

According to recent reports, "He's battling through a lot, and I'll leave it at that," Bickerstaff stated. "He's going to go out and compete and give us what he's got. But he's fighting through a lot.".

Sources indicate that cunningham had previously missed one game this season, last Wednesday's 122-109 win over the Upcoming Orleans Pelicans. He struggled in his previous outing with a season-low five points in Sunday's 107-97 loss to the Atlanta Hawks.

Recent findings suggest that detroit struggled to get its offense going early without Cunningham as the Magic jumped out to a 22-16 first-quarter lead on their home court. But the Pistons seized control with a 32-14 advantage in the second quarter and kept the Magic at bay from there.

Sources indicate that isaiah Joe took Cunningham's place in the starting lineup and finished the game with a team-high 21 points alongside 4 rebounds and a steal. Daniss Jenkins was the primary playmaker in Cunningham's absence with 15 points and 10 assists. Desmond Bane led the Magic with 21 points and 5 assists.
