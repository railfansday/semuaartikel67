The series adaptation of Percy Jackson and the Olympians, based on Rick Riordan’s books, is back for the third season. Don’t fall behind, because this season is going to get pretty juicy. Plus, the world is expanding to include new Gods like Artemis (Daphne Keen) and Atlas (Holt McCallany), new demigods, and a Manticore. Here’s your guide to following the Percy Jackson season 3 episode release schedule.

Sources indicate that we also have all of the Percy Jackson season 3 episode titles in advance, which is a treasure trove for book readers because they’re also chapter titles. Season 3’s are drawn primarily from The Titan’s Curse, the third book in the series. You can basically figure out what’s going to happen in the season and in each episode.

The first two episodes dropped on November 20, 2026.

Two fresh episodes will drop weekly on Disney+ every Friday.

Typically, the episodes drop on the platform at midnight... if you live in California. Figure out what 12 a.m. PT is in your time zone, and you’re good to go!

Sources indicate that there are eight total episodes this season, same as season 1 and 2. We’ll get two newly introduced episodes every week, meaning that the season will wrap up on December 11.
