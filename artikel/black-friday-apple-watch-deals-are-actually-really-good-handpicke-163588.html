It has been revealed that in looking for the best Black Friday Apple Watch deals, we found great savings on both the Apple Watch Ultra 3 and Ultra 4. Apple's Ultra watches are designed for rugged outdoor use and performance athletes. They have astounding battery life, lasting up to three days in low-power mode. They also offer both GPS and cellular connectivity.

The older Apple Watch Ultra 3, usually $799, is now $250 off at Best Buy, and we've never seen this previous-gen smartwatch priced so low. Over at Amazon, the Apple Watch Ultra 4 is now on sale for $649.99, a $149.01 discount from the list price. The newest Apple Ultra Watch has even better battery life and more advanced health tracking; like the mainstream Series 12, it offers improved heart rate sensors and a Readiness score.

To be honest, the Ultra 3 offers a lot of the same features thanks to the recent watchOS 27 update, but if you want the more future-proofed option, then opt for the latest and greatest version, i.e., the Ultra 4.
