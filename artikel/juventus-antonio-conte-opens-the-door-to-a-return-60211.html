Juventus: Antonio Conte opens the door to a return

According to recent reports, antonio Conte has not definitively turned the page on Juventus. In an interview given to Cronache di Spogliatoiothe Italian coach raised the possibility of one day returning to the Turin bench, which he occupied between 2011 and 2014. I think that sooner or later, the circle with Juventus will have to close. I know I left something there “, he confided. The technician also revealed that he had been close to a comeback twice in recent years. “ The first time, when Juventus chose Maurizio Sarri: I was free and I finally joined Inter. The second time, when Giuntoli arrived, who decided to bet on Thiago Motta: I then found myself in Naples “, he explained. Despite his attachment to the Bianconeri, Conte recalls that a possible return will depend on several conditions. “ I must be free and Juventus must be looking for a coach “, he clarified, without giving further indications on his future.

In a recent development, antonio Conte returned to another missed opportunity to join Juventus, even before his first appointment in 2011. After his time at Bari, the former midfielder had started advanced discussions with the Turin leaders, but a tactical disagreement ultimately prevented his arrival. “ There were a lot of attackers and I remarked they needed wingers, but they took Diego even though they knew full well I wanted a 4-2-4 “, he said. During a meeting organized in Rome on the sidelines of the 2009 Champions League final, Conte refused to adapt his system to the Brazilian player’s preferences. “ If a player had to decide how to play, then I was not the coach for them “, he explained. Finally appointed two years later, the Italian made his mark in the club’s history by winning three consecutive Italian championship titles and two national Super Cups. His last season ended with a record 102 points in Serie A, before his departure in July 2014.
