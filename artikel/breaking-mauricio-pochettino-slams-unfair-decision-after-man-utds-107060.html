Mauricio Pochettino remarked Manchester United were on the wrong end of an ‘unfair’ decision during their heavy Premier League defeat to Liverpool.

Pochettino’s reign as United manager began in dismal fashion at Anfield as his side were thumped 4-0 by arch-rivals Liverpool.

The defining moment of the match came after just eight minutes as Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak as he raced through on goal.

Isak’s touch was heavy and United goalkeeper Senne Lammens quickly retrieved the ball but referee Samuel Barrott still deemed a red card to be the suitable punishment.

To make matters worse for Manchester United, Dominik Szoboszlai blasted home the subsequent free-kick to put the hosts ahead.

Liverpool scored again just five minutes later – Florian Wirtz volleying home a cross from the lively Rio Ngumoha.

England winger Ngumoha, 18, scored Liverpool’s third 20 minutes into the second half before Víctor Munoz added a fourth late on.

Pochettino described his first match as Manchester United manager as ‘painful and disappointing’ but criticised the early red card that paved the way for the heavy defeat.

Sources indicate that ‘So painful. So disappointed,’ Pochettino said in his post-match interview. ‘It’s not ok to start a game in the Premier League with this situation.

Manchester United dropped into the relegation zone prior to the November international break, when Carrick was relieved of his duties.
