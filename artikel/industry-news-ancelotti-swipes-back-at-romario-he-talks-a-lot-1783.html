Carlo Ancelotti took a swipe back at Romario after the latter called for the Brazil coach to step down.

Industry observers note that brazil suffered a 3-2 defeat to Japan on Friday. Vinicius Junior scored twice, but Raphinha was sent off, with Ancelotti also seeing red at full-time.

Recent findings suggest that in the wake of that defeat, Romario – who earned 70 caps for Brazil – called for Ancelotti to quit, posting on social media: "Ancelotti, it's done! Take your things and go.".

When asked about Romario's comments ahead of Brazil's friendly against Singapore, Ancelotti said: "In a democratic country with freedom of speech, everyone is allowed to express their opinion.

"He can tell lies, he can tell the truth or talk nonsense.

"It's not up to me to say whether the things he indicates are correct, wrong or just stupid. The truth is that Romario talks a lot. A lot. It's his right.".
