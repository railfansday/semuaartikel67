TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box is on sale for $115.96 at Walmart, down from its previous $237.98 price.

If you had no luck getting an invitation to buy one of the cheaper Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box listings at Amazon before stock ran out, Walmart's latest batch of Black Friday deals has something for you.

It has been revealed that as of Nov. 25, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box is available for just $115.96 at Walmart, with a hefty $122.02 saving (51% off). Walmart is now barely above TCGplayer's $114.58 market price — with just $1.38 separating the listings. Plus, unlike Amazon’s invite-only listings, these ETBs are readily available without delay.

Factoring in the nine titular 30th Celebration booster packs of trading cards, you're spending roughly $12.88 per Pokémon TCG anniversary pack before accounting for the extras.

These include the exclusive full-art foil Nidorina promo card, 16 foil Basic Energy cards, 65 sleeves, dice, a coin, six dividers, a player's guide, and a Pokémon TCG Live code card.
