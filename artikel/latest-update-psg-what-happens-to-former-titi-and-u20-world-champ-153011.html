Latest Update: PSG: what happens to former titi and U20 world champion Jean-Christophe Bahebeck?

Sources indicate that former great hope of Paris Saint-Germain, Jean-Christophe Bahebeck was part of this golden generation U20 world champion in 2013 alongside Paul Pogba, Mario Lemina, Lucas Digne, Geoffrey Kondogbia, Alphonse Areola, Florian Thauvin, Jordan Veretout and Kurt Zouma. The player born in 1993 experienced all the youth categories of the French team, from the U16s to the Espoirs, before making numerous stops across the four corners of the globe: Troyes, Valenciennes and Saint-Étienne in France, Pescara in Italy, Utrecht in the Netherlands, Partizan Belgrade in Serbia, and finally Palmaflor in Bolivia in 2022. His signing in Bolivia at just 28 years old was surprising at the time.

According to recent reports, “The former great hope of Paris Saint-Germain is the newly introduced recruit of the Fieras de Quillacollo. The club notes he will be its star player for this year. A huge blow »wrote the Bolivian media Red Uno in 2022. Even the Spanish daily A.S. expressed his surprise: “Palmaflor hits hard on the transfer market: the club recruits Bahebeck, formerly of PSG and French striker who was world champion in 2013. He even had the opportunity to share the locker room with Zlatan Ibrahimović, one of the best scorers in the history of the Parisian club. ».

Sources indicate that as for the player, he had justified his decision by the desire to live a new experience, far from everything he had known until then. The words of Zulay Pogba, Paul’s wife from Bolivia, had also dispelled his doubts according to what he confided in February 2022: “I made this decision after a bad experience at Partizan Belgrade. It was a radical change and a new beginning. I had good feedback about the country thanks to Pogba’s wife, and I find Latin American football magnificent, I noticed it with Gignac. I like challenges and new adventures, and I like to travel”explained Bahebeck to the Bolivian media El Diario.

The adventure will last only one season, Bahebeck will score two small goals in 15 matches, and his club will miss its objective of qualifying for the Copa Libertadores after an elimination in the semi-final of the championship against the club of The Strongest. Since this experience, the former Parisian made the decision to retire, without cutting off football. For several months, the native of Saint-Denis has been playing at Olympique Adamois in Val d’Oise in R2, and until recently he was coaching the Persan U10s, his very first club (Val-d’Oise, 95).

Sources indicate that “I would like to see those childish smiles again. Today everything has become professionalized too early, at ages when you shouldn’t even think about it. Of course, it takes rigor to progress, but football is first and foremost a game, a pleasure. This is what I lost throughout my careerhe recently confided to Amateur Football. I train three or four times a week, it’s a special feeling: teaching them what I was taught, seeing them progress. When they apply a lesson the following week, I have a stupid smile on the bench. I don’t shout during matches. Even if they struggle, I let them experiment for the next day. At 9-10 years old, no instructions from adults: I want them to think together, to help each other”. After chasing a career, he is now happy to help others grow.
