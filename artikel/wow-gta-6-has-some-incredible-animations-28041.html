In a recent development, grand Theft Auto 6, out now on Xbox and PS5, is a very big game. But don’t assume it’s just a blandly massive world with no tiny details to discover. Rockstar Games has packed GTA 6 with more detail than any of its previous games, including some truly impressive animations.

Recent findings suggest that let’s start with one of the earliest animations I spotted that truly made me go “Whhhooooaaa…” I parked too close to a car, and Jason was unable to fully open his door. In past GTA games, this would lead to the character teleporting out of the car or the door clipping through the other vehicle. In GTA 6, that’s not the case. Instead, Rockstar has added a dedicated animation for Jason and Lucia where they squeeze in and out of the car.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is filled with tiny animations like this that often made me shake my head and think, “I guess I get why this took so long to make.” For example, there are so many animations connected to eating and drinking in this game. I mean, you can crack open a cold one with one hand while holding and shooting an assault rifle in the other.

It has been revealed that I also discovered that Jason and Lucia can eat a burger while holding a soda at the same time and even switch between them whenever. And when your drink is empty, you can toss it in a nearby trash can with a procedural animation that doesn’t always work. I’ve missed the trash before, and I felt like an asshole. Jason and Lucia might be murderers who rob and steal, but they aren’t litterbugs!

It has been revealed that one animation that really caught me off-guard happened when I was testing how much damage cars in GTA 6 can take before they stop working. I found an empty parking lot, lined up my car with a nearby wall, and floored the accelerator. I expected to slam into the wall and total the front of my vehicle. Instead….

In a recent development, again, GTA 6 has so many surprising animations and details. I can’t list them all here because I know I’ve not found them all yet. Maybe I never will? Regardless, I highly recommend you poke around GTA 6 and try stuff out because you’re likely to find some wild detail or tiny animation you’ve never seen before.
