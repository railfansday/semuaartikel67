Kylie Jenner and boyfriend Timothée Chalamet stepped out hand in hand at the 2026 Governors Awards.

Per Instagram footage shared by journalist Clayton Davis on Sunday, November 15, the loved-up couple were seen walking through the foyer of Ovation Hollywood together just prior to entering the Ray Dolby Ballroom to enjoy the night’s annual event.

In the footage, Chalamet, 30, was seen briefly chatting with Tom Holland before the Spider-Man actor, 30, walked off and the Marty Supreme star slipped his hand back into Jenner’s.

It has been revealed that earlier in the night, both Chalamet and Jenner, 29, shared close-up looks at their individual ensembles via their Instagram accounts. While the actor posted two photos of himself looking dapper in a tuxedo — in a car alongside Jenner and also on his own — the cosmetics queen shared a full-length Instagram Stories snap of her 2006 Roberto Cavalli runway gown ahead of getting ready.

The vintage cream and gold design featured crystal encrusted straps across the back, which Jenner revealed in her teaser photo. She paired the gown with tousled waves worn out and cascading around her shoulders.

While it was Jenner’s turn to support her boyfriend at the event, the actor was recently seen cheering on Jenner as she made her Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show debut. Pictured front row at the October 18 event, Chalamet was spotted clapping alongside Kylie’s mom, Kris Jenner, and the momager’s partner, Corey Gamble, as Kylie rocked a $50 million “Fantasy” bra.

According to Vogue, the garment featured 4,410 natural diamonds and a 75.74-carat radiant-cut Fancy Vivid yellow diamond worth $22 million alone.

Kylie and Chalamet were first linked in April 2023, with Us reporting earlier this year that the couple are now “very serious and committed to each other.”.

A source exclusively shared with Us in March, “Their relationship is very strong and they have a good rhythm going.” The insider went on to reveal that they have “definitely had a lot of real conversations about their future and what they both want” after Chalamet publicly declared his love for Kylie while onstage at January’s 31st annual Critics Choice Awards.

In a recent development, while accepting the trophy for Best Actor, he thanked Kylie for the “foundation” they had built together. “I couldn’t do this without you. Thank you from the bottom of my heart,” he gushed at the time.

The camera then panned to Kylie in the audience as she mouthed, “I love you” to him in response.
