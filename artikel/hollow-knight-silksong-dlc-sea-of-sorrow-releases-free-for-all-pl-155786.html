It has been revealed that after an extended period of silence regarding its much anticipated expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, Team Cherry is back with a big enhancement on its upcoming DLC, Sea of Sorrow. The new content drops on December 11, 2026. Even better, it’s free for all players.

The Sea of Sorrow launch trailer shows off what appear to be several upcoming biomes and lots of other features, including what appears to be a new crest based on how Hornet is moving int he new gameplay footage. The announcement was part of a Team Cherry showcase that promoted three other very cool-looking indie games that the studio will be publishing called Radio Rain, Untangle: Plant Problems, Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City.

Sources indicate that it looks like a pretty major expansion. Fans are ecstatic. And yes, it looks like there’s a newly introduced fishing minigame coming to the Soulslike Metroidvania. Team Cherry also called Sea of Sorrow Silksong‘s “first major expansion,” suggesting there could be others as well.
