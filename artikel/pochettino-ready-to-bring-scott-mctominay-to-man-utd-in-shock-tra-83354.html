Manchester United are open to bringing Scott McTominay back to the club in a shock move, according to a report, with their chances of actually re-signing the Scotland international revealed.

Man United chiefs are ready to back latest manager Mauricio Pochettino in the transfer market, and as soon as the January transfer window, in order to help him turn the club's fortunes around.

The Red Devils go into the Argentine head coach's first match in charge against Liverpool on Sunday 19th in the standings, after Sunderland leapfrogged them on Saturday with their draw against Aston Villa.

Pochettino knows that a draw at Anfield will be enough to take them out of the bottom three, and that must be the minimum aim as he looks to lead the club up the table.

The 54-year-old must work with the current squad of players for the next six weeks before the January window opens, but it is expected that new players will be brought to Old Trafford in the New Year.

GIVEMESPORT sources have revealed that the club are looking at some key positions ahead of January, including the centre-back and striker positions, as well as both full-back positions.

The former two appear to be the priority, with GMS understanding that Fulham's Calvin Bassey is appreciated by the club, and he is able to play as both a left-sided centre-half and a left-back.

United are also keen on his Cottagers' team-mate Antonee Robinson, while Folarin Balogun is another United States international linked with a move to Old Trafford, given the success he has had under Pochettino at international level, and the fact that Monaco are willing to sell.

A move for Chelsea right-back Malo Gusto could also be 'one to watch', according to GMS sources.

Fabrizio Romano expects the club to look at a new centre-back and striker in January too, but it would be a shock if they looked at a new midfielder given they signed three at a cost of more than £150m in the summer.

Still, TEAMtalk claim that the club has been offered the chance to re-sign McTominay from Napoli, and they are refusing to rule out sanctioning a deal.

Despite winning the Serie A title in his first season at the club, and being named as the MVP of the league at the same time, the Scotland international's future in Naples is in doubt.

There are just 18 months left on the 29-year-old's contract at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona, and with no breakthrough in negotiations, an exit is looking increasingly likely.

McTominay made the decision to leave Old Trafford in 2024, but the report claims that he is now ready to explore a return to England and the Premier League, should he fail to reach an agreement to stay at Napoli.

The club want him to stay, but they are currently apart in terms of negotiations, meaning that intermediaries are now working on potential next club options for the Scot.

And, even though they have just rebuilt their midfield, TEAMtalk claim that midfield is an area that will be targeted by the club's recruitment team, especially given there is uncertainty over the future of captain Bruno Fernandes.

Pochettino would be attracted by McTominay's familiarity with being a United player, his Premier League experience and how he has come on leaps and bounds since leaving the Theatre of Dreams.

Despite the claim by the report, midfield surely will be one of United's lowest priorities given the clear shortcomings in other areas of the team.

If they did choose to focus in on a move for McTominay it would be a shock, but it would also be one that many United fans would surely say yes to given their current situation.
