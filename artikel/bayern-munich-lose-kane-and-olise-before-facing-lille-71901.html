Recent findings suggest that big blow in prospect for Bayern Munich? During the draw conceded at home against Cologne (1-1), this Friday evening in the Bundesliga, the Bavarian club saw two of its major players leave prematurely the match against Cologne, this Friday evening. Harry Kane and Michael Olise were both hit.

The first had to give up his place in the 20th minute. After a duel with Joël Schmied in the area, the English striker remained on the ground, visibly injured in the left ankle. He attempted to continue the meeting for a few minutes, before finally requesting the change. Tom Bischof replaced him.

Industry observers note that the scenario repeated itself with Michael Olise. The Frenchman was hit following contact with Tom Krauss in the 41st minute. Despite the treatment received on the pitch, the French winger limped to the locker room and did not return to the field after the break. Young Tim Binder took his place.

Sources indicate that these two injuries come at the worst time for Vincent Kompany. The Bavarian coach already has to deal with several notable absences, notably those of Serge Gnabry, Luis Diaz, Manuel Neuer, Alphonso Davies and Ismaël Saibari.

Industry observers note that very bad news for the German champions before the trip to Lille, scheduled for Wednesday in the Champions League. It remains to be seen the exact nature of Kane and Olise’s injuries, as well as their possible unavailability for this European meeting.
