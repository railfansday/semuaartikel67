Industry observers note that today's matter seems to be mostly a login problem, rather than a playback issue. 70% of those affected say gaining access to the platform is what's gone wrong for them. Are you able to access Spotify? Let us know in the comments.

Reports are dwindling away to very little right now, with plenty of folks posting that today's login problems have been solved for them and they've regained access to their account.
