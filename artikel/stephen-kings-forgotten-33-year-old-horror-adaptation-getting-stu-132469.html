Stephen King's horror adaptation gets a fresh life.

King's career began with his first novel, Carrie, published in 1974, which allowed him to become a full-time writer and later be named the King of Horror. King has a career spanning six decades and has published over 50 books and around 200 short stories, selling over 350 million copies of his work worldwide. Over the years, his novels have received many adaptations in movies, TV series, radio highlights, stage plays, and more. Now, a forgotten adaptation is returning in a brand-fresh way.

The adaptation of King's novel from 1991, titled Needful Things, is getting a stunning upcoming release. The 1993 movie Needful Things is set to get a 4K UHD and Blu-ray limited edition SteelBook release, with pre-orders open from December 1 ahead of its release on January 20, 2027. ViaVision Entertainment is releasing Needful Things in a whole new way with only 1,500 copies available. The movie has been restored from the original 35mm camera negative with both the 121-minute theatrical cut and the 191-minute television cut.

Needful Things, released in 1994, starred Ed Harris, Max Von Sydow, Bonnie Bedelia, Amanda Plummer, Ray McKinnon, and J.T. Walsh.
