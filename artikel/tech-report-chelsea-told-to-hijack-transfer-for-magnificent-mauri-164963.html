Chelsea icon Pat Nevin has urged his former club to move for Manchester United target Calvin Bassey in the January transfer window.

United’s creaking defence shipped another four goals in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge against Liverpool on Sunday.

The new manager will be backed in the January transfer window with a defender and a striker top of his wish-list with United rapidly being pulled into a relegation scrap.

Fulham defender Bassey has emerged as a leading target to strengthen at the back. The 26-year-old impressed again in Saturday’s 1-1 draw away to Manchester City with the Cottagers eager to tie him down to a new contract.

Chelsea meanwhile also conceded four goals at the weekend, beaten 4-1 at home to Leeds United. Only four teams have conceded more Premier League goals than the Blues this term with defence an area of real concern for club great Nevin.

The former Scotland international is a huge admirer of Bassey having also watched his rise with Rangers and believes the Nigeria international ‘walks into’ United’s starting XI.

But Nevin would also like to see Chelsea beat United to the punch.

‘He’s brilliant,’ Nevin said of Bassey, speaking to Casinolyze. ‘Of course he can walk into these substantial teams. I think he could walk into Man United, that’s not saying a lot, let’s be fair, but I think he can play at the top level anywhere.

‘Had Arsenal come in for him during the summer, I wouldn’t have been surprised at all. Because remember, Calvin can move about a bit as well, he can play different positions.
