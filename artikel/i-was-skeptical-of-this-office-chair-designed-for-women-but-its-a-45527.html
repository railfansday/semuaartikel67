So that’s the positives for design. The minus points go to some of the other material aspects. At this price point, I can see they’ve put the money into the other materials, which is understandable.

Industry observers note that let’s discuss seat height. Until I lowered this chair all the way down, I had NO IDEA how I could be sitting.
