📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Just how happy is Rockstar Games with the sales performance of its long-awaited Grand Theft Auto VI? Here's a clue: it didn't even wait 48 hours before releasing the first-day sales figures. According to its official statement, GTA VI's "day one retail sales reached $3.79 billion, setting a upcoming record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.".

Included in that tally is revenue from an astonishing 32.5 million preorders, making it the most pre-ordered game in history on both Xbox and PlayStation, according to Microsoft, makers of the Xbox game console and Sony, makers of the Playstation, the only two systems that can right now play the game.

But it's when you put those numbers into perspective that you get a sense of how astonishingly successful Rockstar's launch truly was. Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the highest-grossing movie of 2026, according to Box Office Mojo, with worldwide ticket sales exceeding $2.5 billion. That film was released at the end of July, more than three months ago, but Grand Theft Auto VI has already exceeded its worldwide box office total by more than a billion dollars.

In a recent development, but what if we widened our search? On Box Office Mojo's ranking of the highest-grossing movies of all time, 2019's Avengers: Endgame ranks near the top with worldwide ticket sales of over $2.9 billion — still well short of Rockstar's GTA VI score.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar's co-founder and current president Sam Houser was understandably elated by the reception to his firm's magnum opus: "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a fresh chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.".

But the good news likely doesn't end there. Rockstar Games and parent manufacturer Take-Two Interactive have at least two more major paydays to look forward to: the release of Grand Theft Auto VI on the multiplayer platform GTA Online and the future PC port, which would give PC players a chance to buy the game.
