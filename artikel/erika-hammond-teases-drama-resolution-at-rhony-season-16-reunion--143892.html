Erika Hammond is teasing The Real Housewives of Latest York City Season 16 reunion, revealing there will be “drama” and “resolution.”.

In a new interview, Hammond hinted that although some problems among the group will be resolved, she doesn’t see a path forward with the new “friend of” Devyn Simone.

“I will say it is a must-watch. There’s a lot going on,” Hammond told Extra. “There’s a lot more drama, but there is a lot of resolution. I think most of us got to resolve things that need to be resolved, and we walked away. Hammond and Toye clashed on the Bravo reality series, with the makeup artist criticizing the former’s Mosquito Island home and questioning everything about her. After filming the reunion, the cast went out to celebrate the end of production. However, Simone, who joined the series midseason, was notably missing, which raised red flags among fans about her standing with the group. Watch on Deadline.

“For me, I really was excited to welcome and meet Devyn into the group. You’ll see this as the show goes on. Not giving too much away,” Hammond noted. “But I just think it was really hurtful, some of the things that she was doing. It was really disappointing to see how easily she could make things up, and especially things that hurt people, and for me, that’s not a friend I want, and that’s not someone I want to be having dinner with.”.

When asked if she wanted to create distance with Simone, Hammond said, “For forever,” and added that she felt there was no coming back from it.

“I think when people falsely accuse you of something or lie, that is hurtful,” Hammond remarked. “Those are untrue things, and I don’t like being falsely accused of something.”.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, Simone took to social media to share a reaction to Hammond’s interview, posting, “You needed me for a storyline. Too bad you waited ’til the reunion.”.

In a set of posts, Simone added, “Just because you bullied people in grade school (allegedly) doesn’t mean you can bully me. I was telling the truth then. I’m telling the truth now. Time reveals all. You noted it.”.
