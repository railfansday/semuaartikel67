Norse’s cash crisis extends far beyond its own operations, forcing international airline and cruise partners to reassess their flying plans.

Industry observers note that norse Atlantic Airways formally suspended all of its flights Thursday and warned that its board intends to file for bankruptcy unless it can urgently secure fresh funding. The development comes a day after the Norway-based low-cost, long-haul carrier described its financial situation as "critical.".

In a stock exchange filing on Thursday, Norse confirmed it was in discussions with investors over a possible takeover that would include a financing solution. The manufacturer also held a meeting with holders of its $52 million senior secured bonds on Wednesday. “If a short-term financing solution is not secured urgently, it is the Board's intention to file for bankruptcy,” Norse mentioned.

The firm cautioned that there was no guarantee a viable solution would be found. Norse apologized to passengers for the disruption but gave no details on refunds or alternative travel arrangements.
