In a year full of niche Christmas aesthetics and newly introduced decorating trends, it’s easy to feel pulled in a dozen directions. But there’s a reason we keep coming back to a traditional holiday style: It lasts because it feels collected, not decorated. Think plaids and woven textures, vintage finds, and gilded accents—a Christmas that feels timeless, yet a little opulent. However, that look can come with a hefty price tag.

Recent findings suggest that I’m all for investing in quality pieces you’ll bring out year after year, and thankfully I discovered that Ballard Designs delivers that designer-inspired feel—without the heirloom price tag. Its collection is full of traditional accents and distinctive pieces that blend contemporary glamour with old-world charm. Plus, during its Black Friday sale, you can save 40 percent on all holiday decor.
