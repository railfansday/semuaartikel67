While this rather exciting week gave us the next James Bond, and it’s also the start of Objective by the Sea, the largest (and, well, pretty much only) Apple security conference in the world, held in Hawaii.

Recent findings suggest that now in its ninth year, OBTS is put on by friends of Security Bite Andy Rozenberg, Patrick Wardle and the Objective-See Foundation. Trainings are already underway, and the talks officially start Wednesday and run through Friday. This year’s lineup includes names like Csaba Fitzl, Sarah Edwards, Cat Self, and Jaron Bradley, plus Patrick himself, who teased more AI agent bugs after his Meta Muse zero-day back in September.

Patrick came on the podcast recently where we discussed the changes to Apple’s bug bounty program and the OBTS v9 conference.

Recent findings suggest that if you can’t make it out to Hawaii (same), there’s good news. Objective-See tells me that all of the talks will be livestreamed for free on its YouTube channel, starting Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 9:30 a.m. HST (2:30 p.m. ET). Follow Objective-See on socials for updates.

A lot of the Mac malware and vulnerability research I cover in this column gets its start on that stage, so expect to see updates here in the weeks ahead. In the meantime, I’ll be following and watching closely from much colder Ohio.
