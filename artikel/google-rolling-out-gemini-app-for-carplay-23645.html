In a recent development, like on Android Auto and Automotive, Google is bringing the Gemini app to CarPlay. This allows iOS users to converse with Gemini Live while driving.

There’s a simple waveform UI with Gemini indicating when it’s listening, thinking, and responding. There are buttons to “End” and “Mute” in the top-left corner.

According to recent reports, like on phones, you can interrupt Gemini Live’s response to add a detail, change subjects, or pause. After launching the app, you can “pick up where you left off with a recent chat or start a fresh conversation.” All CarPlay conversations sync with the Gemini app on your iPhone.
