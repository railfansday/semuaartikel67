AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim believes that the comeback win over Olympiacos is something that could ‘change the future’.

After 50 minutes of the game on the outskirts of Athens, it looked like Milan were heading for a fourth defeat in five Europa League games. Lorenzo Pirola crashed a header in off the bar via Mike Maignan, then Jota Silva scored a second early in the second half.

Recent findings suggest that then, out of almost nowhere, Omari Hutchinson inspired a run of three goals in 15 minutes. Firstly he scored a goal of his own with a brilliant solo run, then he set up Alphadjo Cisse for his 10th goal of the season, then he crossed for a Christian Pulisic volley to make it 3-2.

Diego Moreira added a fourth late on to make sure of the points. Amorim spoke to Sky after the final whistle to give his thoughts on what was a rollercoaster night, but one that thankfully ended well. His words are via MilanNews.

Will this second half change the Europa League season?

“It definitely changes our future. We played well in the second half, but we made a lot of mistakes again and we need to improve. But we got a lot of players back, it was a positive night.”.

It has been revealed that “It’s better to finish games like this, winning obviously. We have a lot of work to do to be a very strong team, but the spirit is there. It’s the start of something.”.

“I think this is the biggest problem: focusing on the small details like set pieces, goalkeeping, and the plays we need to make. It comes with time and experience.

Sources indicate that “The technical aspect is fine, but concentration is the most important thing. It’s tough to play physically every three days, but it’s even tougher to constantly have this pressure of having to win.”.

“I always expect this. He has the potential to do much more. He’s talented, like other players on the team. It’s no surprise, he’s very talented. It’s the mentality that counts.”.

How do you explain the build-up errors?

“We lost the ball without being pressured, trying to play the ball. We left Jashari alone to set up a team with four of our players behind their midfielders. When we had the ball, we gave it away without pressure.

“Koni sometimes put the ball in areas where there was pressure, but instead he was supposed to carry the ball. He’s responsible for pushing the team forward and guiding us forward. We went to get into their pressing zones.”.

It’s your first comeback win of the season….

“I think it’s a great feeling. If you look at the Benfica match, we had several opportunities. We created more than today. You have to see it to believe it, and today we saw that we came from two goals behind and won 4-2. Our confidence will grow.”.
