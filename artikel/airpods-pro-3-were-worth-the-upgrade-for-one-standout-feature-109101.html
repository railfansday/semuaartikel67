AirPods Pro 3 (now only $149, down from $249) offer many advantages over AirPods Pro 2, but after a year of use, the improvements to Active Noise Cancellation alone have proven worth the upgrade.

Sources indicate that I’ve been using AirPods Pro 3 for over a year, and they had a lot to live up to because I already loved AirPods Pro 2 so much. Initially I found the upcoming ear tips less comfortable, but thankfully that problem went away after a little extra time.

Some highlights of AirPods Pro 3 include the longer battery life, with 8 hours per charge compared to 6 on the previous model. The charging case’s fresh Ultra Wideband chip also makes it easier to find when it’s lost. Some customers will appreciate the heart rate sensor, but I wear an Apple Watch all the time so it doesn’t benefit me much.

It has been revealed that these newly introduced features are nice to have, but Active Noise Cancellation is where AirPods Pro 3 become a major upgrade. Apple says it’s “the world’s best in-ear Active Noise Cancellation.” Here’s how the new ANC compares to previous models:.

Sources indicate that I spend nearly my entire workday with AirPods Pro 3 in my ears. Whether I’m working from home with young kids around or at a busy coffee shop, I rely on ANC to block out background noise and help me focus.

Industry observers note that one reason I’ve been especially impressed with the ANC upgrades is that I didn’t have any real complaints about ANC on AirPods Pro 2. But Apple managed to deliver improvements in AirPods Pro 3 I had no idea I needed, and when I’ve tried going back to AirPods Pro 2 I couldn’t believe how much noise was let in.

If Active Noise Cancellation is crucial to you, I highly recommend AirPods Pro 3.
