Industry News: Etsy will revise its AI training terms after sellers close their shops

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (408 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Recent findings suggest that the latest terms take effect on 16 December.
  • Etsy and its “third party partners and vendors” may use sellers’ content to “develop, train, test and improve artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies.”.
  • Several sellers who had run shops for years noted they were leaving.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that the latest terms take effect on 16 December. They let Etsy and its partners use sellers’ content to build and train AI. The previous version had no clause about AI, the Times reported. The key line reads:.

Etsy and its “third party partners and vendors” may use sellers’ content to “develop, train, test and improve artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies.”.

Several sellers who had run shops for years noted they were leaving. Illustrator Aaron Millard said on Instagram he was deleting the shop he had run for nearly a decade. He wrote that sellers had no way to opt out.

Kristen and Geoff Zephyrus deleted their shop too. They have sold home goods and stationery on Etsy for 13 years as Cognitive Surplus.

“For Etsy to be helping to train algorithms with our human content is just unimaginably awful,” Kristen Zephyrus told the Times.

James Ossman, Etsy’s vice president for customer operations, answered sellers in a post on Etsy’s website this week.

Etsy will not “use your content to train A.I. models designed to copy your original work or replace your creativity,” Ossman wrote.

According to recent reports, he remarked third parties get access to sellers’ content for uses such as fraud detection and advertising. In a statement to the Times, he said Etsy would send sellers the revised terms soon. Sellers’ creative work belongs to them, he added.

Some critics doubt the reassurance. Tyler Wilde, PC Gamer’s US editor-in-chief, wrote an opinion piece on the clause. To him, it reads like blanket permission to train AI on user content for any purpose. He argued that Etsy should leave every seller out of AI training by default.

Industry observers note that etsy has faced seller protests before. In 2022, more than 20,000 sellers went on strike over a fee rise. Etsy had raised its basic sales fee from 5% to 6.5%. In October, it tightened its rules on low-quality AI content after users complained, the Times reported.

The firm cut 12% of its staff in August. The dispute also lands amid wider pushback against AI. Donald Trump Jr told Axios this week that investors had underestimated the AI backlash. Elton John urged Australia to protect artists from AI at the Aria awards.
