Arsenal fans have had plenty to say about Newcastle United and Bruno Guimaraes.

This comes ahead of Saturday’s match on Tyneside.

Arsenal fans had seen their team lose at St James’ Park three times in a row and not even score a single goal.

At 1-0, a cowardly challenge left Tino Livramento in agony and stretchered off, Arsenal taking advantage with two late goals, the winner in the very final seconds of added time.

Bruno Guimaraes now returning to St James’ Park for a first time, to give the match extra edge, as though it needed it….
