Industry observers note that buying a gift for the gardener on your list seems easy enough. Head to the garden center, find something cutesy with flowers on it, maybe a packet of seeds, add a few assorted tools, and call it a day. They’ll appreciate the thought behind any gift, of course, but if you want to make a gardener really happy, skip the cute and go for something useful.

After years of working with gardeners as a garden center manager and an avid gardener myself, I can tell you the gardening gifts they really want: boots that can handle mud, pruners that cut without a fight, and tools that solve a problem, hold up over time, or make the job easier.
