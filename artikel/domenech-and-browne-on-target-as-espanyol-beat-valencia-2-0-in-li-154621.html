Domenech and Browne on target as Espanyol beat Valencia 2-0 in Liga F

  • The breakthrough came in the 54th minute.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Espanyol returned to winning ways in Liga F on Monday evening. Their matchday 12 victory over Valencia ended a run of two straight defeats. The first half produced no goals.

The breakthrough came in the 54th minute. Domenech scored, with Bicho providing the assist. Bicho had been booked for a foul just before the half-hour, and Domenech picked up a yellow card of her own in the 65th minute.

Valencia had disciplinary problems too. Tamarit was cautioned in the 51st minute and Pauleta in the 78th, both for fouls.

Valencia’s best chance to draw level arrived in the 72nd minute, when they were awarded a penalty. Mrabet failed to convert it. Twelve minutes later her evening was over, sent off for unsportsmanlike conduct.

Sources indicate that down to ten, Valencia conceded again in the 89th minute. Browne scored from Gutiérrez’s assist to put the game beyond them. Torras was booked for a foul in the tenth minute of stoppage time.

Valencia’s last victory over Espanyol came on 2 May 2021, a 2-1 home win. Espanyol won the corresponding fixture 1-0 on 8 December 2024, and the most recent meeting, on 13 April 2025, finished 1-1.

It has been revealed that espanyol are sixth with 18 points from 12 games, having won five, drawn three and lost four, with a goal difference of 3. Madrid CFF in fourth are two points ahead of them, and Athletic Club in fifth are one point better off.

Valencia are 16th on 7 points. Nine of their 12 league matches have ended in defeat, and their goal difference stands at -13. Sevilla, in 15th, are three points clear of them. The loss also ended a four-match unbeaten run in all competitions, which had included a 3-0 win over CD Samper last time out.

Espanyol’s next assignment is a trip to Barcelona, who top the table with 31 points, on Tuesday 8 December. Valencia visit FC Levante Badalona on the same day.
