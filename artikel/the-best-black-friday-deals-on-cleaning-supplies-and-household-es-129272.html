Black Friday means different things to different people. For some, it means a discounted OLED TV, while for others, it means Christmas shopping is taken care of. But if you’re like me (or Monica Geller), it means there’s a chance your fave cleaning supplies and other household essentials are on sale, which is honestly pretty exciting.

It has been revealed that I know, laundry detergent and trash bags aren’t exactly fun purchases, but you have to buy them anyway. And I’d much rather stock up while they’re discounted than pay full price when I’m down to my last dishwasher tablet. (Preferably before then, but you get it.).
