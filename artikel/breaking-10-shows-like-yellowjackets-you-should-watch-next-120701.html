It has been revealed that yellowjackets, the time-hopping survival drama about a group of teenage girls who are stranded in the wilderness in 1996 and do terrible things to survive, is coming to an end. Whether or not it sticks the landing, it's been one helluva ride, and we'll soon have room in the streaming schedule for other other indicates about young people having a pretty rough go of it while just trying to survive.

In a recent development, situated between flashbacks and flash-forwards (not entirely unlike Yellowjackets), mystery/thriller The Wilds sees an airplane full of teenage girls crash on the way to an empowerment program in Hawaii. It quickly becomes clear that the accident was engineered and that the whole thing is some sort of social experiment, and the survivors are forced to compete against each other if they want to live. The show understands the ways in which young women are exploited and expected to compete against each other in the real world, not just a show about girlfights. Stream The Wilds on Prime Video.

It has been revealed that A bit more grounded...but only at first glance, The Stranded takes place on a Thai island in the midst of a tsunami. The island was formerly home to a prestigious high school, and a bunch of students have snuck back for an ill-timed party on the last day of class. A bad idea, clearly, after the storm hits. Kraam (Papangkorn Lerkchaleampote), one of the few students who actually lives on the island, is trapped there along with a couple dozen classmates. Kraam knows the island, but Anan (Chutavuth Pattarakampol) assumes leadership while other students either take on necessary roles or fall in line. As the miniseries progresses, the new social order breaks down, even as the island's mysteries reveal themselves and things get weirder and more complex. Stream The Stranded on Netflix.

Eden is more concerned with the survival horror than with some of the deeper themes and mysteries of Yellowjackets, but we're still playing with shifting power dynamics in this Spanish import. It has been revealed that the premise of The Leftovers is brilliantly subdued: Around two percent of the world's population disappears without explanation, and it's enough to upend just about everything. Politics have adapted to the upcoming normal, religions have collapsed and reformed, and families have had to make peace with the inexplicable loss of loved ones. The first season revolves around the Garvey family, led by Kevin (Justin Theroux), a sheriff whose wife (Amy Brenneman) left him to join a cult, while subsequent seasons broaden the scope to bring in other characters in other locations. It's more philosophical than Yellowjackets, and quieter, but the shows share an interest in the lingering effects of trauma, and in the stuff we get up to under threat and in the dark. Stream The Leftovers on HBO Max.

Recent findings suggest that the 100 tells the story of the descendants of refugees of nuclear devastation who return to Earth from their habitat in space to encounter the remnants of humanity who have survived on Earth. Naturally, the first people sent to scope things out are the juvenile delinquents (better them than me), and they discover that three civilizations have risen up in the aftermath of the apocalypse, and they are all pretty scary (including one populated by cannibals, naturally). More sci-fi than horror, but the survivalist themes and mysteries here make it more than a decent match. The show builds an impressive mythology over the course of its run, leading to a conclusion that’s borderline metaphysical. Stream The 100 on Pluto TV or buy it from Prime Video.

When pretty, popular girl Kate Wallis (Olivia Holt) goes missing without a trace in 1993, awkward Jeanette Turner (Chiara Aurelia) steps in to fill the gap, modeling herself after Kate by working her way into the missing girl's social circle and getting close with her boyfriend. That's all going great until Kate reappears two years later, accusing Jeanette of having witnessed the events around her disappearance and keeping quiet. Whoops. There's even more going on, as the show shifts back and forth between 1993, 1994, and 1995 to slowly reveal the missing pieces. Season two takes on a different story set in 1999 and 2000, and the forthcoming (surprise) third season will shake things up again. The emphasis here is on mystery, but it's a show about the aftereffects of trauma and of the dark secrets that can tear families and communities apart. Stream Cruel Summer on Hulu.

It has been revealed that A remote, charming town with some deep, dark secrets (the biggest of which, and one that it shares with Yellowjackets, is rather strongly hinted in the title), Gannibal stars Yuya Yagira as Daigo Agawa, a police officer from the substantial city who takes a job in the super cute (and, of course, remote) village of Kuge following some personal trouble. The perfectly charming Goto family runs the town, more or less, and welcomes Daigo and his family. Just a couple of reasons why I'm more of a city person. Stream Gannibal on Disney+ and Hulu.
