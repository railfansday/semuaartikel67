David Moyes' future as Everton boss always seems to be a talking point at the Hill Dickinson Stadium this season.

That's not typically unusual when a club is searching for new owners, with the current ones being criticised for a lack of investment on the pitch.

Moyes has done anything but back down, and when asked about the Janaury transfer window, he gave a very frank and very disappointing answer.

Despite all of the club turmoil over the summer, the Toffees are sitting sixth in the Premier League table during the international break.

The team are on a three-game unbeaten run and will be hoping to extend that against Brentford once the 2026-27 campaign resumes.

However, the lingering feeling that Moyes could step down at any time remains, and concerns over the situation are only going to get bigger.

Moyes continues to be linked with the Celtic job, as Martin O'Neill's tenure comes under further pressure at Parkhead this term.

Whispers have quietened down after the Hoops' dramatic win over Besiktas in the Europa League, but they've not disappeared.

It's not the Celtic job that Everton fans have to be worried about, though.

No, instead it's international football. During the summer, Moyes became the odds-on favourite to succeed Steve Clarke in the position.

The Scots eventually went with Sebastien Pocognoli, but so far, that has been an absolute nightmare at Hampden Park and away from it.

Pocognoli has only been in charge for six games, but the most recent one saw the Scots thrashed 4-0 by a second-string Switzerland team.

His side have only beaten North Macedonia since his appointment, and have now missed out on a Nations League promotion play-off.

The fans are already at their wits' end, just a handful of matches into his spell, and attention could soon turn to Moyes once again.

It has already been turned down, the idea of Moyes leaving Everton halfway through the season, but there might not be many more opportunities for him to manage his nation.
