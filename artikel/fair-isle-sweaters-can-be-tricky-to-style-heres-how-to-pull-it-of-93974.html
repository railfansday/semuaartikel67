Some might say the Fair Isle sweaters only look good during Thanksgiving week. We are brave enough to disagree with this warm (and cozy) take. In our book, we need a little alpine charm in our life every single day this winter. How else could we keep ourselves from freezing during the coldest temperatures?

Fair Isle sweaters are a traditional knitting style that features a distinctive pattern with multiple colors—either bold or neutral shades. The most trendy pattern we're seeing right now is geometric styles around the neckline or the center of the sweater. Still, even though it's adorable and cozy, this type of sweater might seem difficult to style.

Luckily, we've got you covered. No need to freeze in front of your closet when getting dressed in the morning! And the best part? Carve Designs has the chic Fair Isle sweaters and the styles to pair it with on sale for 25% off, just in time for Black Friday.

So, keep scrolling to see how we'd recommend pairing this (apparently) controversial and traditional sweater.

Sources indicate that 1. The most popular way to style the Fair Isle sweaters is with jeans. It's a no-brainer. You can style it more casually with relaxed boyfriend jeans or light-wash barrel jeans, paired with boots or sneakers. It can also feel dressier with a dark wide-leg jean, styled with gold jewelry, heels, and a chic shoulder bag—while keeping that cozy comfort with the trendy pattern.

Recent findings suggest that 2. If you want to make your outfit a little more interesting (but still casual), the Fair Isle sweater can also be styled with this pair of corduroy pants. This pairing adds an extra layer of textural and color difference to pop against the cozy pattern of the sweater. Add a pair of brown boots or cozy clogs to keep the working vibe going.

Recent findings suggest that 3. Skirts are an easy way to put a classy outfit together, especially during the fall season (but even during the winter, skirts can be layered over fleece tights to keep you warm). We recommend opting for a maxi skirt and pairing it with knee-high boots—either a silky one in a light color or a dark-wash denim one.

4. Wearing sweaters by themselves can look a little too plain for some personal styles out there, even with the Fair Isle pattern. An easy way to create some interest on the top half of your outfit is to layer a button-down with a collar under your sweater—pick between a silkier texture, like this Angela Kindsilk Top, or a more structured one, like this western denim style one.
