📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

All available 3D movies are included with your Disney+ subscription and come with extras you can only experience in VR.

While 3D movies on a VR headset are uniquely amazing (much more convincing than 3D TV ever was), the most exciting part of the Meta Quest experience is the immersive environments. Disney and Meta worked together to create a fully unique movie-watching experience that gives movies more than just the depth that 3D movies typically have; you’ll also be watching the movies in an environment that’s unique and changes throughout the movie.

Meta states you can “Catch Star Wars: A Fresh Hope (Episode IV) from a comfy spot on Tatooine, see the Avengers battle Loki from atop Avengers Tower, settle in for a Pixar movie night on the Monsters, Inc. Scare Floor, and more.” Some of these immersive environments were shown off at Meta Connect in September when Meta VR Glasses were revealed, but it’s a bit surprising to see so many of them launch months ahead of VR Glasses’ retail debut.

Industry observers note that disney also highlights IMAX experiences, which are also available in the Disney+ app on Meta Quest headsets. These movies mimic the full IMAX experience in VR by putting you in a virtual theater with a massive screen, all at the correct IMAX aspect ratio. Rumor also has it that Meta will include other 3D movies with its Meta VR+ subscription service.
