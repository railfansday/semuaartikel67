Arsenal welcome Borussia Dortmund to the Emirates tonight looking to keep their unbeaten Champions League record intact (kick-off 8pm, live on Amazon Prime).

They sit third in the table two points behind Paris Saint-Germain and Real Madrid, who both have a 100 per cent record, while the Gunners were held to a 2-2 draw by German giants Bayern Munich in match week three.

Tonight’s opponents have taken nine points from their four matches, with wins over Villarreal, Sabah and Real Betis, but they suffered a shock 1-0 defeat at the hands of Bodo Glimt, to leave them 12th in the table.

The two sides have met eight times before, but their last meeting was back during the 2014/15 season when they were drawn together in the group stages.

The German side won 2-0 at home in the September, thanks to goals from Ciro Immobile and Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang, who went on to play for the Gunners.

Arsenal won their home leg by the same scoreline two months later when Yaya Sanogo and Alexis Sanchez scored either side of half-time.

There’s not too much to choose between them with Mikel Arteta’s side leading the head-to-head 4-3, while the other game, back in September 2011 ended level.

Dortmund have won just once at the Emirates, a 2-1 win in 2013, when Henrikh Mkhitaryan and Robert Lewandowski were both on target, while Olivier Giroud scored for the home side.

Despite Arteta’s side slipping up in the Premier League at the weekend, when they were held to a 2-2 draw by Newcastle at St James’ Park, football betting sites are backing them for the win tonight at odds of 3/7 while you can get 7/1 on a win for Dortmund.

Both sides sit top of their respective leagues with Arsenal six points clear of second placed Brighton, while Niko Kovac’s side lead Bayern by two points.

In a recent development, but the Gunners are unbeaten in their last 16 group stage/league phase matches, winning 15 and drawing the other. They have also won their last 12 home games at this stage which is a club record and if they can win one more they would equal Liverpool's English record of 13, achieved between 2020 and 2025.

They have scored at least twice in 21 of their last 22 group stage/league phase home matches and scored the first goal in each of their last 12, so it’s very tough to see the German side getting anything from this one.

Dortmund coach Kovac has won only one of his five Champions League matches against English clubs, which was a 7-2 victory over Tottenham, while he was in charge of Bayern Munich back in 2019.

But they have scored 33 goals already this season and they’ve only failed to score twice in their 17 matches so far so both teams to score at 7/8, with the betting sites, is a pretty good option.

German Kai Havertz has scored four goals so far this season but three of those came in the opening four games of the season.

Since then, he has scored just one in 15 games for club and country and that was against Liverpool back in November, so surely he’s due another?

He scored the decisive goal for Chelsea against Dortmund in the 2022/23 season to reach the last eight of the competition.

Despite playing four years in Germany for Bayer Leverkusen that goal for Chelsea is his only one against tonight’s opponents in nine attempts.
