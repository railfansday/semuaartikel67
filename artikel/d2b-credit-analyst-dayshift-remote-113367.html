Position: Credit Analyst - Dayshift Remote.

Salary: Php 65,000 to Php 90,000/ monthly (negotiable based on experience).

Working Hours & Conditions: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM AEST/AEDT, Melbourne Time and Australian Holidays.

Employment: Full Time - Independent Contractor.

The organization is an Australian mortgage broking business dedicated to helping clients secure tailored lending solutions with clarity and confidence. The firm partners closely with an extensive panel of major and non-major lenders to support clients across residential home loans, refinancing, and specialized debt structures. Driven by rigorous compliance standards, operational efficiency, and high-touch service, the company delivers a seamless, transparent borrowing experience from initial discovery through to settlement and ongoing portfolio management.

The firm is seeking an exceptional, highly analytical Credit Analyst to take operational ownership of the credit assessment and deal structuring lifecycle. Partnering directly with the Principal Broker and collaborating alongside our in-house Loan Processor, you will step in immediately following the client interview via a warm handover to drive applications from initial analysis through to formal approval and settlement.

This role requires deep expertise across complex residential lending scenarios (PAYG, self-employed, trust, and SMSF), rigorous hands-on proficiency in Connective Mercury and Quickli, and a robust compliance mindset to safeguard quality assurance across every file.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
