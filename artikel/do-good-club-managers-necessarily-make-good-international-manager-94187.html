Recent findings suggest that who should replace Mauricio Pochettino as the USMNT manager? – Tom S.

In a recent development, although the salary I’m sure will be excellent, I think the US job is going to be a really difficult sell to a top-level coach. The 2030 World Cup is a long way away and I’m not sure the Concacaf Nations League, the Gold Cup or even the 2028 Copa América carry much appeal for a manager with European experience or aspirations. But I’m not entirely sure that sort of coach is necessarily a great idea. The last two World Cups, after all, have been won by low-key coaches who have come from within the federation and have worked with many of the players at youth level. That’s the positive from appointing from within; appointing from without is almost certainly more expensive and there’s the danger the next foreign manager does just what Mauricio Pochettino does, and has his head turned by the first vaguely attractive European job that comes calling.
