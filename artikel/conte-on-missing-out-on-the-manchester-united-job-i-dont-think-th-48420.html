Antonio Conte doesn’t believe Manchester United passed him over because they were worried about his methods.

Interview by Cronache di Spogliatoio, via CalcioMercato, the former Napoli coach spoke for the first time since United sacked Michael Carrick and appointed Mauricio Pochettino as his replacement last week.

Conte had been heavily linked with the job, which he’s been out of work since leaving Napoli in the summer, and he admits the club’s history appealed to him.

“Manchester United have such an important history that, if anyone was afraid of my methods, I don’t think the Red Devils would have been afraid of bringing in someone who can build something important and take them back to where their history belongs,” he stated.

Sources indicate that united haven’t won the Premier League since Sir Alex Ferguson’s final season in 2013, and they were third from bottom when Carrick was dismissed. Conte, who won the title with Chelsea in 2017, also spoke warmly about the legendary Scot.

Sources indicate that “Alex is someone I have enormous respect for. I’ve read his book, and when I was coaching Chelsea, I had the honour of going to his house.”.
