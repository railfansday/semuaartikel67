The decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) marked a turning point in the series of the final of the 2025 African Cup of Nations, the final result of which was confirmed (victory for Senegal 1-0 after extra time). But the story is not quite over, since Morocco still has a certain number of recourses to activate.

On another level, a strange rumor has been circulating in recent days. It would appear that Morocco intends to leave the Africa Zone to join Europe, by requesting membership in UEFA. The Atlas Lions are reportedly unhappy with the treatment they receive from the authorities.

In a recent development, this is obviously quite absurd, especially since we recall that CAF initially gave victory to Morocco. But since several accounts (more or less serious) relayed this “information”, a Nigerian journalist wanted to investigate. Osasu Obayiuwana contacted the Moroccan federation FRMF, which assured him that everything was false.

Sources indicate that A selection which is linked to a continent other than its own, this remains possible in football. From January 1, 2006, Australia thus obtained authorization to join the Asia Zone, and to leave Oceania, for sporting reasons. The gap in level was considered too great with the teams from his continent.

It has been revealed that another special case is that of Israel, which is part of UEFA, unlike its neighbors, such as Jordan, Lebanon or Syria, all members of the Asian Confederation. This time, it was geopolitical criteria that led to this decision, taken in the 1990s. Difficult to compare with the situation in Morocco….
