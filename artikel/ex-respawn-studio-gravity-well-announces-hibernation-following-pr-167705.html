📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Following a round of layoffs last month that resulted in the loss of 40 jobs, which itself came after a previous source of funding pulled out in the Spring, independent studio Gravity Well has now revealed that it is going into "hibernation" for the foreseeable future. The team was largely made up of devs who'd left Titanfall and Apex Legends studio Respawn Entertainment, and under the Gravity Well banner had been working on a upcoming, still-unannounced game for around six years.

A statement posted to the main page on Gravity Well's site reads:.

"We don’t exactly know what is next for us or Gravity Well, but we do know we still believe in the IP and game we were building. Even moreso, the team we managed to cultivate was truly world class and we’re going to miss getting to work with them every day. If you’re a hiring manager or recruiter and a Gravity Well resume hits your desk they come with our highest recommendation.

If you’re a publisher or investor and are interested in talking, let's connect!".

The message is signed off by its founders Drew McCoy, Jon 'Slothy' Shiring and Mon Saetern. Besides being the second project led by former Respawn developers to be doomed this year - see also Wildlight's Highguard - it's unfortunately another case of a studio made up of developers with incredible pedigrees being left at the whim of funding accruement in the current state of the industry. That said, it is noteworthy that the team have not shown anything publicly in the six years since their formation, minus a few pieces of concept art which can now be seen on its site. It's still unknown exactly what Gravity Well was working on, but it's likely to have been some kind of movement-based shooter, if the teams' experience on Titanfall, Apex Legends and Call of Duty is anything to go by. Solidarity to all affected, who I hope can find work elsewhere in the industry.
