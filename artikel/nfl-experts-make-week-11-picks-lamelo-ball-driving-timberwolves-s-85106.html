Sources indicate that when Anthony Edwards went down with injury just four games into the season, it seemed like the Timberwolves might be cooked. Instead, Minnesota is sitting at 11-4, fourth in the West and up to No. 3 in our latest NBA Power Rankings.

Recent findings suggest that much of that success is owed to LaMelo Ball, who is shining in his first season with the Wolves. He's averaging 17.7 points, 5.5 assists and 1.9 steals. For more evidence of Ball's impact, just look at how the team he left is doing without him. Brad Botkin explains:.

Botkin: "The LaMelo effect is being felt in two places: Minnesota, of course, but also Charlotte, where the offense has plummeted from a 123.3 rating with Ball on the court last year to a league-worst 103.1 this season. Being down Kon Knueppel doesn't help, but it's night and day watching this unit try to generate clean looks without Ball's creation and passing.".
