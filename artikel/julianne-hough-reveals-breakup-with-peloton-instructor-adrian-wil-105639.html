Julianne Hough is moving to her own rhythm.

Just one day after it was confirmed the Dancing With the Stars co-host was dating Peloton instruction Adrian Williams, she revealed the two have broken up.

Hough told TMZ that she and Williams briefly dated and are no longer together.

The news of the split came just two days after the fitness instructor, 42, soft-released his romance with the 38-year-old on social media, sharing a photo of Hough holding her dog Sunny to his Instagram Stories Nov. 22, which she’d then reshared to her own account. A source confirmed the relationship to E! News the following day.

While the dance pro has done her best to keep her love life out of the spotlight since her divorce from Brooks Laich was finalized in 2022, Hough teased earlier this year that there was a new man who’d waltzed into her life.

Industry observers note that "I might not be single," she admitted to E! News at the 2026 Emmys in September. "There's a lot to discuss off-camera.".

More recently, the Emmy winner reshared a cryptic post to her Instagram Stories Nov. 23 that read, “The safer you feel, the wilder it gets.”.

Amid the breakup, Hough has been in rehearsals for the DWTS season 35 finale on Nov. 24 as well as supporting her brother Derek Hough on the release of his newly introduced documentary The Symphony of Dance, which chronicled his wife Hayley Erbert’s recovery from a life-threatening brain injury in 2023.

“Wow, brought up so many memories, gut wrenching emotions and full body recalling of the fear and hope we were simultaneously experiencing,” Hough posted to her Nov. 23 Stories alongside a video of herself crying after watching the documentary for the first time. “But most of all so much gratitude in remembering all the love everyone poured into the two of you.”.

As for Williams, while the fitness pro has not spoken publicly about his brief romance with Hough, he appears to be keeping his focus on his own journey since the split.

The Peloton instructor reshared a post from his motivational account to his Instagram Stories Nov. 24 that included a picture of him in his car with a text-only photo that read, “Your reset doesn’t have to make sense to anyone else. Mine has three pedals.”.
