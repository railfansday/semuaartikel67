ESA's Hera probe uses 'superhuman' vision to inspect NASA's asteroid crash site

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (81 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, around four years ago, humanity intentionally hit an asteroid. The Double Asteroid Redirection Test (DART) was by pretty much all measures a resounding success when it struck the asteroid moonlet Dimorphos going more than 22,000 kilometers per hour (13,700 miles per hour). However, we still don't know just how much of a success it actually was—DART was a one-way mission with no real way to track the results of the impact close up.
