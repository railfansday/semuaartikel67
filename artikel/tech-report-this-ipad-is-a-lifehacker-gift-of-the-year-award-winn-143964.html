Sources indicate that if you're shopping for an iPad this holiday season, you might be a bit intimidated by Apple's price tags. The iPad mini starts at $599; the base iPad Air is $749; and the entry-level iPad Pro is a whopping $1,199. Most of Apple's iPad line certainly lives up to its expensive reputation.

It has been revealed that that's where the iPad A16 comes in. This is Apple's least expensive tablet, with an MSRP of $449. But despite being Apple's "budget" option, the iPad A16 shouldn't be ignored by customers looking for the best all-around device. In fact, it's really the best iPad for most people, which is why I named it the best tablet gift of 2026. What's more, retailers are giving the iPad A16 a $50 discount ahead of Black Friday, bringing the entry-level price down to $399.

In a recent development, the iPad A16 isn't Apple's best iPad (that would be the iPad Pro). It doesn't have the best display, the best camera, or the best processor. But it doesn't need those things: This iPad has a display, camera, and processor that are good enough for most use cases, without unnecessarily driving up the price. Simply put, it's one of the best tablets you can buy right now.

The iPad A16 has a large, 11-inch (technically 10.86 inches, according to Apple's fine print) IPS display with a brightness of 500 nits. As the name implies, it has Apple's A16 chip, the same processor as the iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 15. That chip is about four years old at this point, and while that means it won't be as fast as Apple's newer SoCs, it still holds its own with iOS 27. It comes with two cameras: One 12MP wide camera on the back, with support for 4K video recording, and a 12MP Center Stage camera on the front.

Industry observers note that apple notes this iPad can run for 10 hours while browsing the internet on wifi or watching video. You charge the tablet with USB-C, which supports data transfer up to USB 2.0 (which nets you 480Mb/s transfer speeds). It also comes with other perks you might expect from Apple's more "pro" tablets. You can connect the iPad to an external display, up to 4K resolution at 60Hz. It also supports two Apple Pencil models: The USB-C Apple Pencil and the first-gen Apple Pencil.

The only real consideration here is storage: This model comes with 128GB of storage, which can run out fast. It's particularly usable for consumers who rely on cloud storage or mostly stream their entertainment. But if your giftee aims to store photos, videos, or music on their iPad, it's worth considering upgrading. The $50 discount applies to most storage models, as well as the cellular iPad options, except for the 512GB models: The wifi-only model has a smaller discount, while the cellular option has a $100 discount.

Industry observers note that sure, the iPad A16 doesn't have the fastest processor, an OLED display, support for newer accessories, or Apple's sharpest cameras. But all of the specs it does have are enough to run the things you need on iPadOS. If someone on your list is looking for an iPad for web browsing, streaming, messaging, and light gaming—and they don't need something for professional work—this device is likely more than enough iPad for them.
