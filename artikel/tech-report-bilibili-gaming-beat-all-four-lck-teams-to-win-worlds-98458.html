Tech Report: Bilibili Gaming beat all four LCK teams to win Worlds 2026 and end Korean title run

🌐 Source: Esportsinsider • 📅 15 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (501 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • Sources indicate that bilibili Gaming are world champions.
  • Recent findings suggest that BLG took the hardest possible route.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bilibili Gaming beat Gen.G in Brooklyn to win the 2026 League of Legends World Championship, the LPL's first world title since 2021. Added to their First Stand trophy, the result gives a Chinese side a double that no team had managed since 2016, just as Riot takes the 2027 season opener to China.

Sources indicate that bilibili Gaming are world champions. The LPL side needed four games to beat Gen.G in Latest York and win the club’s first Worlds title. The result ends a run of four straight LCK world titles. It also gives the LPL its first world crown since EDward Gaming won in 2021.

Recent findings suggest that BLG took the hardest possible route. They knocked out all four LCK teams at the event: Hanwha Life Esports, Dplus KIA, T1 and Gen.G. Every Korean side that travelled to the tournament went home after facing them.

In a recent development, the final turned early. BLG levelled the series at 1-1 by winning a 53-minute second game. It was the longest game of the whole tournament. Gen.G had reached the final with two 3-0 knockout wins, over LYON on 4 November and Invictus Gaming on 7 November.

Sources indicate that for Knight, the MVP award closes a loop. The BLG mid laner lost the 2024 final to T1. Two years later, he finished on the winning side.

It has been revealed that the runners-up looked inward after the defeat. At Gen.G’s post-final press conference, Chovy said the team had broken its own rules. Duro, playing his first Worlds, regretted that they had dropped their drafting initiative.

Industry observers note that gen.G still leave with US$800,000 as runners-up. Semi-finalists T1 and Invictus Gaming received US$400,000 each. Riot ran the event from 15 October to 14 November with 19 teams.

Sources indicate that gen.G’s admission points to the draft as a weak spot. The figures support a narrower reading too. Gen.G swept both knockout opponents before the final. Against BLG, they could not hold the lead after taking the opening game.

It has been revealed that worlds was BLG’s second international trophy of the year. In March they won the First Stand Tournament in São Paulo. They beat G2 Esports 3-1 in that final and collected the US$250,000 top prize.

Winning both events makes BLG the first team since SK Telecom T1 in 2016 to take more than one major international title in a year.

The calendar also tilts towards China. First Stand will open the 2027 season as First Stand: All In, a one-off expanded event. Riot calls it the largest tournament it has ever produced. The event was first revealed for Southeast Asia. That region will now host First Stand in 2028 instead.

The 2027 Mid-Season Invitational will be held in Valencia.
