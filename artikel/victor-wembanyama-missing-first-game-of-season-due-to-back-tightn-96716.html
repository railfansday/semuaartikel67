The San Antonio Spurs were without both Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox on Monday night.

Recent findings suggest that wembanyama was ruled out of the Spurs' game against the Sacramento Kings due to back tightness, the team declared shortly before the game was set to tip off at the Golden 1 Center. Fox is dealing with a shin contusion, and was ruled out at the same time.

According to recent reports, while losing two star players for any contest is undoubtedly concerning, the two injuries were described as simply "bumps and bruises" by San Antonio Express-News' Jeff McDonald. Spurs coach Mitch Johnson stated both players are not expected to be sidelined long, but would not yet declare they would be ready to return for their NBA Cup matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers on Wednesday.

According to recent reports, the game against the Kings is the first that Wembanyama has missed all season. He's averaged 29.9 points, 12.3 rebounds and 3.9 blocks through the first 17 games of the season, all of which are career highs for the 22-year-old. He's fresh off a wild 50-point outing on Saturday night. Wembanyama outscored the Fresh Orleans Pelicans starters by himself in the first half before rolling to a dominant win. It was his second 50-point game already this season, following a 52-point performance against the Dallas Mavericks earlier this fall.

Fox has averaged 12.4 points and 5.8 assists per game this season. The 28-year-old is in his second full season with the Spurs, who landed him in a deal with the Kings during the 2024-25 campaign. Johnson mentioned Fox was injured when he was intentionally tripped by a Pelicans player.

Industry observers note that the Spurs were 14-3 entering Monday's contest in Sacramento, which is tied for the best record in the league. The Kings, meanwhile, are just 2-14 and have won only one of their 10 home games so far this season.
