After 13 long years, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally out and breaking records left, right, and centre. While folks who only own a Switch or Switch 2 can't get in on the action, we can at least appreciate a good Easter Egg when we see one.

Industry observers note that case in point, the game contains a pretty blatant nod to Mario and Luigi if you know where to look. As highlighted by TheGamingRevolution on YouTube, heading to Lummox County in the north of Leonida and locating a ruined site in the countryside will yield a pair of hats that look remarkably similar to the attire made famous by Nintendo's brotherly mascots.

Recent findings suggest that essentially, at the top of the ruins, you'll notice a near-invisible block floating just above the main structure. Reaching the top and then jumping into the block will cause it to burst, revealing two hats: one is red with the letter 'J' adorned for Jason, and the other green with the letter 'L' for Lucia.

According to recent reports, it's a lovely little nod to Nintendo, and one that we suspect most people will never see. It clearly showcases Rockstar Games' appreciation for Nintendo and its games, and it's a stark contrast to the sheer stupidity we've seen recently with so-called 'fans' review-bombing games over on Metacritic.

In case you missed it, GTA 6 has found itself being review-bombed by folks claiming it to be 'woke' while lamenting the lack of 60fps. Some people, however, seemingly believe that Nintendo fans are to blame for the low scores, and so have taken it upon themselves to do the same to Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2, lowering its once record-breaking user score of 9.6 down to 8.3.
