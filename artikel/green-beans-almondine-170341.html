Green beans are a must-have on Thanksgiving, but maybe you aren't particularly craving the classic green bean casserole. If that's the case, this is the recipe for you! This French-inspired green bean dish is much easier to make than it sounds and a great way to switch things up this holiday season. Sautéed with shallots and garlic, tossed with buttery, toasted almonds, and finished with lemon, these green beans are quick enough to serve for a weeknight dinner while also being fancy enough to have a spot at your holiday dinner.

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Meanwhile, prep an ice bath by filling a large bowl with ice and water. Cook the green beans until crisp-tender, 2 to 3 minutes (you want them to be slightly undercooked as they will continue to cook later).

Using a slotted spoon, immediately transfer beans to ice bath then drain.

In a recent development, in large skillet over medium-low heat, melt butter until its lightly bubbling. Add in the almonds and cook, stirring frequently, until they are golden brown, which should take about 3 to 4 minutes. Reduce heat to low and add shallots and garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the whole mixture is fragrant, another 2 to 3 minutes. Add in the green beans and toss until combined and beans are tender.

According to recent reports, stir in lemon zest and juice, tossing to coat. Season with salt and pepper and enjoy!

Store leftover green beans almondine in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
