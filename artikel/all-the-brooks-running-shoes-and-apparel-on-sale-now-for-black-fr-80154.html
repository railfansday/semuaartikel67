According to recent reports, the stability counterpart to the Ghost 18, the Adrenaline GTS is the shoe many of us ran in when we fell in love with running. The 25th iteration is the lightest yet, with a foamy midsole, the brand’s DNA Loft v3, to absorb shock and provide soft landings. The guide rails system is Brooks’s unobtrusive stability tech for neutral runners and overpronators alike. Dense foam on the medial and lateral sides acts as bumpers to help align your knee and ankle.

“This is a fantastic shoe,” remarked tester Chris Garges, an overpronator and heel striker. “I’ve run in every model Adrenaline dating back to the Adrenaline 3, and this is by far the best shoe in the series. I loved everything from the fit to the ride to the cushioning.”.

Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 Review.

Brooks wanted to change what we should expect when running in a max-cushioned trainer. “We noticed that for some people, they would start running in a neutral shoe and then their body would work extra. So the internal cost was actually increasing,” mentioned Brooks Footwear PLM and BlueLine Director Nikhil Jain.

The team set out to create a shoe that would cut that internal cost. The Glycerin Flex has a decoupled heel and Flex Zone; these components allow your foot to flex naturally instead of working against a stiff, beefy platform.

Recent findings suggest that “Testing the Brooks Glycerin Flex was a novel experience for me on multiple fronts,” said Editorial Assistant Michael McDonough. “Not only was this the first Brooks running shoe I’ve ever run in, but it was also the first hyperflexible shoe that I have ever seen—and overall, it was a positive experience. The Glycerin Flex combines the benefits of a daily trainer that allows your feet to work and get stronger with the comfort and protection of a maximalist shoe.”.

Sources indicate that we like the Glycerin Max 2 for run/walking and recovery days. The shoe has 45 mm of DNA Tuned foam in the heel and a 6 mm offset. Its beefiness was off-putting for some, especially for runners hoping to get in some tempo work on their long runs. But testers remarked the cushioning was extremely comfy on slow days.

“I loved how comfortable the sneaker was. I instantly felt supported and it felt like I was walking on a bouncy pillow,” stated wear-tester Steph Norman. “This sneaker would be great for easy/long miles where you do not have to worry about your feet hurting.”.

Sources indicate that the popular Brooks Ghost hits the trails. The shoe is outfitted for going off-road with 3 mm lugs on the TrailTack rubber outsole.

Sources indicate that our tester, David Hinkl, appreciated how the shoe protected his feet on gnarly surfaces. “The best part of the shoe for me was the sole,” he said. “It was beefy enough to prevent sharp rocks and pointy things from getting to my feet. Training is more fun when I don’t have to tiptoe across the trail.”.

The Canopy is iconic. Despite the curtains of rain, monstrous winds, and cold temps, Des Linden was the first American woman to win the 2018 Boston Marathon after 33 years. And she wore the Canopy Jacket. This version of the Canopy (the original is also on sale with 30 percent off) can be packed into a little pouch that’s easy to store in the depths of an overpacked duffel.

According to recent reports, the High Point keeps out the rain without overheating. Venting in the back allows airflow, while waterproof fabric and sealed inner seams keep you from getting soaked during a downpour. The jacket also packs down into a pocket.

In a recent development, these joggers are the softest joggers we’ve tested. They’re great for warmups, cooldowns, and traveling. I should know—I trekked Japan after running the Tokyo Marathon in these.

Industry observers note that the Run Visible not only ensures you’re seen with reflective detail, but it also keeps you warm thanks to Thermolite insulation. Three pockets provide storage. That hi-vis orange color also helps you pop out in low light.

The Chaser is for runners who love a soft compression short with ample storage. The high-rise Chaser has side pockets for stashing your phone and leg-opening grippers to prevent them from riding up.
