Nicole Richie has a simple solution for her beauty needs.

The Simple Life alum revealed that her and husband Joel Madden’s 18-year-old daughter Harlow Kate Madden has become her go-to makeup artist, as the teen is even more skilled than she is in that particular area.

“I would say that she has surpassed me,” Richie told People in an interview published Nov. 19. “She’s so good. I actually am not very good at doing my own makeup. She is a whole different animal.”.

And the 45-year-old knows exactly how her daughter—who chooses to go by her middle name Kate—got so good at glowing up her family.

“She grew up watching YouTube tutorials,” Richie explained, “and she’s so well-versed in makeup and contour and highlighting and just all the things.”.

“If I ever need my makeup done at home, I’m going to her,” the House of Harlow founder added. “It’s not me doing it ... She kills a cat-eye.”.

Of course, Kate will soon be showing off more of her talents as she officially builds the foundation for a modeling career after signing with The Lions Management.

Industry observers note that “I’ve always dreamed of modeling, so getting to start this next chapter with The Lions is incredibly exciting,” Kate shared in a Nov. 20 statement to Women’s Wear Daily. “Fashion has always been a way for me to express myself, and I can’t wait to explore that in a whole upcoming way.”.

She added, “I’m looking forward to learning, growing, meeting upcoming people, and seeing where this journey takes me.”.

Kate made her modeling debut alongside her mom for Balenciaga’s recent campaign celebrating the 25th anniversary of their Le City handbag, a style Richie was particularly fond of in the early 2000s.

And Madden—who also shares son Sparrow James Midnight, 17, with Richie—was particularly thrilled to see his daughter achieving her dream.

The Good Charlotte frontman reposted Kate’s modeling announcement to his Instagram Stories Nov. 20, captioning it with three red heart emojis and writing in all caps, “KATE!”.
