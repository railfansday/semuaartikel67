According to recent reports, the bond between Paris Saint-Germain and its supporters has never seemed so strong. Present at a press conference with a T-shirt celebrating the 35th anniversary of Virage Auteuil, Luis Enrique was keen to underline the special relationship he has had with the Parisian public since his arrival in the capital. “My relationship with the supporters is almost perfect. In my experience here in Paris for three seasons, it has been incredible. When we have problems, they are always there, I hope they will be there for years to come.”confided the Spanish technician, visibly grateful to a public who continued to show him their support.

In a recent development, A feeling widely shared by Warren Zaire-Emery, who also paid tribute to the loyalty of the red and blue supporters. The young Parisian midfielder particularly welcomed their constant presence, both at the Parc des Princes and during travels. “It’s magnificent, they’ve been there for 35 years, even more. It’s their 35th anniversary, but they support us, they are always there, home, away, no matter the matches and they push us. We are proud to make them proud”he declared in the mixed zone. Two testimonies which perfectly illustrate the strong relationship between the Parisian club and its faithful, the real driving force of the Rouge et Bleu in recent seasons.
