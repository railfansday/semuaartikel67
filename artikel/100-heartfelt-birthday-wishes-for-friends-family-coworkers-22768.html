To start writing a thoughtful birthday wish, think about what this person brings to your life. Are they the person who makes you laugh when things get heavy? Do they show up when others flake? Start there, and be as specific as possible.

According to recent reports, you could even include a shared memory, a personal trait you admire, or something they’ve done recently that made you proud. It doesn’t matter if it’s short or long — just that it’s real and meaningful to you.

If you don't know the person well, start your message by being warm and casual. Something like, “Wishing you a joyful day and a year filled with good things” works perfectly.

If it’s for someone you know in a professional context, try, “Hope your day is filled with appreciation and celebration — you deserve it.”.
