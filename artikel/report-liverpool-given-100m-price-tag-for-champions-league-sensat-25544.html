Liverpool have so much work to do on their first-team squad.

The Anfield outfit have been left in the dust by their title rivals with Arsenal now nine points clear of them in the Premier League.

Indeed, the fact that the Reds are fourth in the table is good news as, it means that they're still in the running for the Champions League.

However, after Arne Slot was sacked for finishing fifth last season, you'd have thought that the Anfield outfit would be trying to do much better than they did then, especially if they want to keep Andoni Iraola as their boss.

Obviously, transfers can help with this and now, the Reds have been told exactly how much they'll have to bid if they want to sign one of their latest transfer targets.

Galatasaray want to bump Aleksey Batrakov's release clause up to €100m.

Liverpool have been lacking a creative spark among their ranks for a while now.

When Trent Alexander-Arnold was still around, he was their main source of chances.

However, since he chose to leave Anfield for the Bernabeu a couple of years ago, the Reds have been trying to fill the void he left behind.

That obviously hasn't worked out all that well, even with the £116 million purchase of Florian Wirtz.

Since then, many different players have been linked with Liverpool and, one of the more recent and intriguing options has been Aleksey Batrakov.

The Russian at the moment plays for Galatasaray in the Turkish Super Lig and, so far this season he's bagged six goals for the club with four of them coming in the Champions League.

This form hasn't gone unnoticed with Turkish outlet Takvim now sharing how much Galatasaray seem to want for their 21-year-old star.

"Following interest from European clubs in the transfer, Galatasaray management is reportedly preparing to amend Batrakov's contract," they wrote.

"Galatasaray is planning to meet with the player's agent in the coming days.

"The Russian footballer, who has a contract with Galatasaray until 2031, has a €55 million release clause in his current contract.

"The management aims to increase this figure to 100 million euros with the newly introduced agreement.".

Aleksey Batrakov could be exactly what Liverpool need.

From the 13 games that Batrakov has played for Galatasaray this season, he's swiftly become one of their best players.

During those games, he's contributed to 10 goals across all competitions and has been used as a number 10 and a number eight.

His performances in both positions have been impeccable but, it's on the attack where he really shines.

Batrakov's ability to create a chance out of nothing and even get in on the goals himself is exactly what Liverpool are missing at the moment.

Obviously, nothing is set in stone yet and if Galatasaray do bump his release clause up to €100 million from €55 million, there's probably no chance of the Reds bringing him in any time soon.

But, for now, it's nice to know that Julian Ward and Iraola are keeping their eyes open for any possible deals that could benefit Liverpool.
