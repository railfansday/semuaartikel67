Welcome to the Turkey edition of the Week 12 grades!

Sources indicate that playing on Thanksgiving is one of the NFL's longest running traditions, but this year, it's not just about Thanksgiving. The league is trying to take over nearly the entire week.

In a recent development, the holiday slate of games kicked off on Wednesday night with the Rams beating the Packers 26-13 in the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game. Hopefully you enjoyed the NFL's newest tradition, because it will be sticking around. The league is planning on making this game an annual event.

The game between the Rams and Packers served as the appetizer for possibly the craziest Thanksgiving slate ever. All three matchups were decided by a game-winning score in the final five seconds, marking the first time that's every happened on Thanksgiving in NFL history.

The Turkey Day action started in Detroit, where the Bears pulled off a dramatic 39-35 win in a game where Caleb Williams threw a touchdown pass to Rome Odunze with just three seconds left to help Chicago stun Detroit. The two teams combined for 1,007 yards of offense, which is the second-most ever in a Thanksgiving game.

It has been revealed that the game in Detroit was crazy, but the second game of the day in Dallas might have topped it, with the Cowboys blowing a 31-10 lead in a 39-38 loss to the Eagles. It was the largest blown lead on Thanksgiving in NFL history. It also made the Cowboys the first team in NFL history to blow a lead of 14 points or more in the fourth quarter TWICE at home in a single season.

It has been revealed that the Turkey Day nightcap took place in Buffalo, where the weather shut down the offensive shootout that we were all expecting to see. With temperatures under 40 degrees and a steady rain falling for the entire game, the Josh Allen vs. Patrick Mahomes showdown turned into a defensive struggle with the Bills coming out on top in a 13-10 thriller. The Bills might want to ask the NFL if they can play on Thanksgiving every year, because Josh Allen is now 4-0 on the holiday.

There will be one more game coming on Friday (Broncos at Steelers), but before we get to that, let's hand out some grades for the four games that have been played so far.
