Gabby Bryan has released a new crowd-work comedy special, “Gabby Bryan Is My Therapist.”.

Industry observers note that out now on YouTube, the hour will stream on Amazon Prime Video beginning Dec. 4.

Bryan filmed the special on April 10 during a sold-out performance at Zanies Comedy Club in Nashville, where Bryan headlined as part of the Nashville Comedy Festival. According to recent reports, blurring the lines between stand-up comedy and group therapy, “Gabby Bryan Is My Therapist” features audience members unpacking their personal problems, relationship struggles and emotional baggage in front of a live crowd as Bryan inhabits the role of an entirely unqualified — but laugh-out-loud funny — therapist. The idea for the special came from a real-life encounter in which a therapist once told Bryan she should consider becoming a shrink.

According to recent reports, the tagline for the special warns: “Gabby Bryan is your therapist for one night only. She does not take your insurance and there will be a copay.”.

Sources indicate that bryan is currently on her “Gabby Bryan Is My Best Friend” tour. She recently brought her Edinburgh Fringe show “Billy and the Situation” to Brooklyn. And her previous crowd-work special, “Girl Town Hall,” is currently available on YouTube.

Bryan also co-hosts the comedy podcasts “I’ll Go if You Go” and “Let Me Ask My Dad.” Her on-screen credits include Prime Video’s “Life Itself” and HBO’s “Comedy on Earth.”.

She is represented by Brillstein Entertainment Partners, WME and Jackoway Austen.

“Gabby Bryan Is My Therapist” premieres on Nov. 19 on YouTube and on Prime Video on Dec. 4.
