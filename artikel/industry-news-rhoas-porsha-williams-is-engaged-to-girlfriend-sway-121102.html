The Real Housewives of Atlanta’s Porsha Williams is set to walk down the aisle again.

“I remarked YES. Engaged 11/21/26 ❤️ I love you forever @sway_thepro,” the reality TV star, 45, wrote via Instagram on Tuesday, November 24, in a shared post with her wife-to-be.

According to recent reports, the happy news was accompanied by a video of Porsha with Patrice “Sway” McKinney that saw both ladies walking towards one another dressed to the nines. At the bottom of a staircase, the couple embraced before sharing a passionate kiss for the camera.

The clip then transitioned into the moment Porsha, who went Instagram official with Sway, 41, on November 28, 2025, said yes and a giant diamond ring was slipped onto her engagement finger. The duo then placed their hands within each other’s, showing off Porsha’s bling further, before more kisses were recorded and cursive script confirmed the date of their engagement.

In a recent development, RHOA fans were quick to share happy wishes for the couple via Instagram once the announcement went live. “Screaming,” wrote one fan, while another fan cheekily commented on an Instagram account that shared the news, “Hey Siri, play “7 Rings” by Ariana Grande.”.

Porsha publicly confirmed the romance last year via an Instagram carousel that celebrated Sway’s birthday. “Happiest Birthday @sway_thepro Hope this year continues to bring you everything you want and more! 🥂🍾 Can’t wait to act up Saturday!” Porsha captioned the post at the time.

A string of photos revealed Sway hugging Porsha from behind as the reality star threw her head back in a laugh, while another snap showed the pair hugging as Sway kissed Porsha on the cheek. In a third photo, Sway and Porsha posed for a selfie.

Porsha has been married twice before: to former NFL player Kordell Stewart from 2011 to 2013, and to businessman Simon Guobadia from 2022 to 2024. She also shares a daughter, Pilar Jhena McKinley, with her ex-fiancé Dennis McKinley, whom Porsha welcomed in March 2019 after dating McKinley for just over one year.

Porsha first revealed that she was involved with a woman in October 2025 at Latest York City’s CultureCon. At the time, she told the audience that she was “talking to” two people, a man and a woman.

She teased at the time, “The people who I’m talking to now … it’s just two, I narrowed it down to two, and he is nice and she is nice.”.

Recent findings suggest that providing further insight, she continued, “I really just wanted to make sure that these were normal people. And what I talked to my therapist the other day was, I’m having a hard time opening up because they’re not narcissists and guess what narcissists do? They … mirror you, and these people are not, they’re challenging you, they’re asking me questions, they’re not letting me walk over them.”.
