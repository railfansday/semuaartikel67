Italy have been placed in a group with Spain, Albania and Finland for the European Under-21 Championships, to be played in Albania and Serbia next summer.

The tournament will run from June 16 to July 3, with Albania and Serbia co-hosting the competition.

They are joined by the nine qualifying group winners, including the Azzurrini, the runners-up with the best records, and four teams who came through November’s play-offs.

The draw for the European Under-21 Championships was held in Belgrade this evening.

The Nazionale were placed in Group B along with Spain, Finland, and co-hosts Albania.

Silvio Baldini’s men were second seeds along with France, the Netherlands and Croatia for the draw.

Nobody has won the European Under-21 Championship more than the Italians, who secured the trophy five times, the same number as Spain.

However, their most recent success was all the way back in 2004.
