📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, open GreenFi if keeping your deposits out of fossil fuel lending matters to you, and use its debit card at partner brands for 3% to 6% cash back. Keep savings you hold purely for interest elsewhere, since its top 3.25% rate needs the $71.88-a-year Plus plan and still pays less than the best free high-yield savings accounts.

Even on the full $25,000 that rate covers, and with a qualifying direct deposit or $500 in debit card purchases every month, Plus earns about 2.96% once you subtract its yearly fee, by our math. In April 2025, GreenFi took over the Aspiration Spend & Save accounts our old review covered, and it says the fraud case against Aspiration’s co-founder has no connection to them.

Industry observers note that greenFi suits savers who want their deposits kept out of fossil fuel lending and will shop its partner brands. Savings held for yield alone earn more at EverBank Performance Savings, which pays newly introduced accounts 4.20% with no fee or activity rule, above Plus’s 3.25% before its fee, though without GreenFi’s fossil fuel pledge.
