The 86 trillion threshold has been a sinister cloud over Shiba Inu. For now, it might take a step back, thanks to decreasing selling pressure on the market according to CryptoQuant. The token's technical state isn't great, unfortunately, as it's struggling to find fuel for a recovery.

Shiba Inu printed only 67 billion in exchange inflows, which is a 67% decrease from the local peak of 250 billion. The decreasing exchange inflows are a signal of decreasing selling pressure, even though the available supply on exchanges is still enormous.

Industry observers note that according to CryptoQuant, exchange reserves presently sit at around 85.74 trillion SHIB, worth approximately $332.68 million. They're still up by 0.04% over the last 24 hours.

The real question here is obviously the recovery potential: a decrease in selling pressure quite often precedes recoveries, but it's important to keep in mind that SHIB has barely found the fuel to decelerate the bearish trend, let alone retrace in price.

SHIB's price is stagnant at around $0.000004 and is unlikely to find a footing for a move up anytime soon.

According to recent reports, the ascending trendline that held the price since July was broken in mid-October, which opened the way toward more losses. Shiba Inu mostly followed the general downtrend but tumbled quickly in one daily candle in the middle of November, and it has been moving sideways since then. Volume on the day of the drop was lower than the spikes in August and September.

All five moving averages on the chart stay above the price, and the closest one sits around $0.0000044. The June and July lows near $0.0000041 are already behind us, so there's no clear support on the chart below, unfortunately.

The RSI dropped into the oversold zone on the day of that drop and hasn't climbed far from it since. Momentum remains bearish: SHIB's RSI is at 25, a clear oversold signal, even though there's barely any buying volume on the market.
