There was a time when satin seemed reserved for summer evenings or special occasions, but that has changed. Nowadays, the silky fabric is being paired with richer textures for everyday fall and winter outfits, not just for parties.

It all comes down to styling, and it’s a lesson in contrasts. Fall and winter are all about texture, so teaming a satin skirt with a soft cashmere knit keeps the look sleek and sophisticated while bringing a cozier feel to daytime. For evening events, silk on silk is a natural choice, and you can always add a faux-fur scarf or stole to embrace the cold-weather spirit.

According to recent reports, color plays its part, too. Swap summer’s pastels for deeper, moodier shades such as chocolate brown, bottle green, burgundy, and navy, which lend satin a richer, more seasonal feel. Champagne and ivory remain timeless, especially when worn head to toe beneath a tonal wool coat.

Whether you’re dressing for a holiday party, a workday, or a weekend brunch, below we’ve rounded up some inspo for how to style satin this season.

We love the contrast of silky, sheer balloon pants with a sporty neoprene jacket. Finished with Tabi ballet flats, a woven tote, and sleek sunglasses, the look feels effortlessly chic, even on a day of errands.
