Grand Theft Auto VI Made $3.79 Billion In One Day. Taylor Swift’s Entire Eras Tour Made About Half That.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that taylor Swift spent 21 months on the road to gross roughly $2 billion with the Eras Tour, a music industry record. Then Grand Theft Auto VI rolled into town and nearly doubled that in a single day, PEOPLE reports.

Rockstar Games and parent manufacturer Take-Two Interactive say GTA VI sales reached $3.79 billion worldwide on its release date. That’s based on company estimates. Rockstar calls it “the biggest entertainment launch in history” in a press release. The game also sold a record 32.5 million units before it even came out. (It costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.).

Recent findings suggest that for context, the previous installment, GTA V, debuted in 2013 and made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours. That mark previously held the Guinness World Record for the most a video game has earned in a day.

Sources indicate that GTA VI has a long way to travel to catch its predecessor, though. Take-Two indicates GTA V has sold nearly 235 million units.
