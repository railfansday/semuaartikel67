World of Warcraft: Forever introduces several fresh features, including a camping system that provides useful buffs to help you explore Azeroth. One of the first quests you'll encounter that uses this system is The Great Outdoors, a short introduction to camping. If you've landed on this guide, I'm sure you may be looking for some extra help here.

Although the quest seems straightforward, I found that one objective doesn't always register properly. If you're having the same problem, here's how to complete the quest and get around the glitch. We detail everything you need to know, below.

Industry observers note that you can start The Great Outdoors early in your adventure, regardless of your race. The quest giver and location vary by starting area, but the objectives remain the same.

Here's how to complete The Great Outdoors:.

While resting, you'll see the Welcoming Campfire effect counting down. Once the timer finishes, you'll receive Boosted Rest and complete the main objective.

When completing The Great Outdoors, I found that the Sit near campfire objective didn't always register, even when I was sitting beside the correct fire.

Recent findings suggest that to fix this, press X to sit next to the fire. Then, type /sit into the chat while already seated. This should help the game recognize that you are sitting. Look for the Welcoming Campfire icon in the top-right corner, where your normal buffs appear, to confirm it was triggered.

Once you've completed The Great Outdoors, you'll be introduced to additional camping quests, including ones that teach you about various professions like mining, cooking, and herbalism. Within these professions, you can craft new items that provide various buffs when added to a camp.

The camping system becomes more useful as you level, so be sure to use the bonuses and meet friends along the way.
