📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Choose US Mobile if you want a cheap prepaid proposal and the freedom to pick, and later switch, whichever of Verizon’s, AT&T’s, or T-Mobile’s networks works best where you live. Its main aims include taxes and fees in the price. Skip it if you want help in a store.

On annual plans, a light user pays $96 for 2 GB a month, a heavy user $390 for unlimited data with priority on busy networks, and a family $1,080 for four Unlimited Starter lines. Four lines on the three massive carriers’ cheapest unlimited plans cost $1,200 to $1,440 a year even after discounts, before taxes and fees, $120 to $360 more than that family’s $1,080.

Recent findings suggest that US Mobile suits anyone who wants a cheap plan and the freedom to move to whichever network works best where they live or travel. Anyone who wants a store should go to a big carrier, and anyone who wants one simple plan can choose a prepaid carrier on one network, such as Visible on Verizon’s, at $275 a year.
