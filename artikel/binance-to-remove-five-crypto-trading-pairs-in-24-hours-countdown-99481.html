Industry observers note that binance will delist five crypto spot trading pairs within the next 24 hours on Nov. 20, 2026, following a review.

To protect its customers and to ensure a high-quality trading market, Binance conducts regular reviews of all listed spot trading pairs; it may delist certain spot trading pairs for various reasons, including low liquidity and trading volume.

Binance will delist GAS/TRY, HBAR/BNB, HIVE/TRY, KSM/TRY and ZAMA/TRY pairs on November 20, 2026 at 03:00 UTC, according to an official announcement published on November 18.

Sources indicate that four of the five affected pairs are related to the Turkish lira (TRY), while the last one is HBAR/BNB. The decision affects trading between these specific assets and does not mean the cryptocurrencies themselves are being completely delisted from Binance.

The exchange clarified that users will still be able to trade the affected tokens through other available spot trading pairs on its platform.

Recent findings suggest that binance will terminate Spot Trading Bots services for the spot trading pairs simultaneously where applicable. Users are strongly advised to revision and/or cancel their Spot Trading Bots prior to the cessation of services for Spot Trading Bots to avoid any potential losses.

Sources indicate that in related news, Binance Margin will initiate a routine cleanup of Cross Margin accounts with collateral valued at less than 1 USD (or equivalent in other assets), effective from November 30, 2026 at 00:00 (UTC). The cleanup will be executed on a daily basis as an ongoing operational process.

Affected accounts include Cross Margin Accounts with a collateral value of less than 1 USD equivalent across all assets and no outstanding liabilities, as well as Margin Accounts with no capital activity in the past 6 months. Capital activity refers to any action that generates a capital flow.

For each eligible Small-Asset Account, the system will automatically transfer the collateral in users’ Margin Accounts to their Spot Accounts while the Margin Account functions remain normal, allowing users to transfer, borrow, or trade at any time after the cleanup.

Accounts with collateral equal to or exceeding 1 USD equivalent are not affected by this cleanup.
