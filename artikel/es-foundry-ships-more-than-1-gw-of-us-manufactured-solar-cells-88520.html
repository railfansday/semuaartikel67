ES Foundry, a U.S. manufacturer of high-efficiency solar cells, is at RE+ 2026 this week to showcase its American-made solar cells and announce a milestone in its operating capacity.

According to the tech giant, it is the first and only crystalline silicon solar cell manufacturer to ship more than 1 gigawatt (GW) of domestically manufactured solar cells in the United States.

As one of the few U.S. solar cell manufacturers with a fully operational facility producing and shipping cells to customers, ES Foundry is helping move American solar manufacturing to commercial reality.

It has been revealed that in July, the company revealed it reached its full 3-GW manufacturing capacity. The company began production on its first 1-GW line last year and has now completed its 2-GW expansion line.

Solar Power World attended the opening of its Greenwood, South Carolina facility last year, reporting on its manufacturing outfit and its unique approach to supply deals that include working with large-scale project developers and utilities to guarantee domestic content supplies.

At RE+, attendees can see its U.S.-manufactured high-efficiency monocrystalline bifacial PERC solar cells and meet with organization representatives to discuss domestic sourcing, manufacturing capabilities, and the evolving needs of the U.S. solar market.

“There’s an important difference between talking about building a domestic solar supply chain and actually making it happen,” noted ES Foundry CEO Alex Zhu in a statement. “At ES Foundry, we’re manufacturing at commercial scale, shipping cells to customers and navigating the realities of U.S. production every day. That experience gives us a unique perspective on what it will take to build a competitive, sustainable American solar industry. Success requires more than manufacturing capacity. It requires the right economics, technology investments, customer demand and policies to support long-term growth. We’re proud to be delivering on the promise of American manufacturing today while helping shape the industry’s future.”.

Visit ES Foundry at RE+ 2026: Booth C7434, November 16–19, Las Vegas, Nevada.
