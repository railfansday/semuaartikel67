According to recent reports, the Samsung S90H OLED TV is a winning 2026 release from Samsung, delivering impressively vibrant yet natural colors and striking contrast. An upgraded version of the S90F, with better glare protection and brighter SDR brightness, it has been praised as one of the most versatile OLED TVs, especially for daytime viewing. Right now, a 55-inch Samsung S90H OLED TV is $400 off on Amazon at a record low of $1,197.99 (originally $1,597.99) for Black Friday. It’s worth noting that the other sizes are also discounted, with the 77-inch model 29% off.

In a recent development, while it has identical pixel-level contrast and wide viewing angles compared to the S90F, the major upgrade here is a matte-like screen, previously only seen on Samsung’s pricier models like the S95 series, bringing an extra perk and better reflection control for those with sunny media rooms. Contrast and color accuracy are highlights on this model, as is shadow detail.

The S90H offers four HDMI 2.1 inputs and supports refresh rates up to 165Hz with a compatible PC, up from the S90F’s 144Hz. It also supports VRR, FreeSync Premium Pro, G-SYNC, and ALLM. Like its predecessor, it’s powered by the NQ4 AI Gen3 processor for picture and audio processing. Its 40W, 2.1-channel speaker system supports Dolby Atmos, although neither TV supports Dolby Vision.

If you’re looking for a well-equipped OLED at a mid-range price, the discounted 55-inch Samsung S90H is worth considering, especially if you’re a PC gamer or watch in a room prone to glare. It offers a stronger feature set than the S85H without requiring the jump to the more expensive S95H. Just compare the S90F’s current price before buying, particularly if movie nights in a dark room are your priority.
