📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that use Honeydue if you and your partner want one free app that shows both of your balances, bills, and spending, where each of you decides how much of your own accounts the other sees. Its budget is just a monthly spending limit for each category, so pay for a fuller app if you want a more detailed budget.

Each of you links your own bank and card accounts through Plaid, a bank-connection service, and Honeydue pulls in latest purchases several times a day, already sorted into categories. It suits couples who keep some money separate but want to blueprint from the same numbers.

Couples whose banks stay connected get a shared view of balances, bills, and spending from Honeydue at no cost. Couples who want savings goals and investments tracked beside their budget should pay $99.99 a year for Monarch Money, which a partner joins at no extra cost.
