📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Pretty quiet week in the world of video games, eh? Nothing particularly groundbreaking released that we can think of...

Oh wait! Yes, that Grand Theft Auto 6 rolled out, didn't it? And it was a rather significant deal. The game shot to the top of Metacritic's all-time best games list (tied with the original Ocarina of Time), and Digital Foundry weighed in on whether it'll ever come to Switch 2.

On a more Nintendo-related note, the Wii celebrated its 20th anniversary, Nintendo was named the most-nominated publisher at this year's The Game Awards, and a brand-fresh event got underway in Pokémon Pokopia.

All of this meant that it was a slightly quieter one in review land, but we still shared our thoughts on TANUKI: Pon's Summer (7/10).

So, is anyone playing something other than GTA this weekend?

In a recent development, it's been Well Dweller that has been gobbling up most of my time. I think I'm about halfway through now, and it really is delightful. Hopefully I'll make a bit more progress with that this weekend, and maybe finally dip my toe into Pragmata too. Who has time for GTA, eh?

According to recent reports, I’ve been absolutely barrelling through Ocarina with the kids, ticking off the Forest, Water, Spirit, and Fire Temples (in that order - a first for me!) in quick succession. A playthrough of the original over the summer definitely helped, so we’re one temple away from the big G, with about 70 Skulltulas bagged and five heart slots left to fill.

Beyond that, Queen’s Domain has me in its grasp, and I’ve also dipped into Well Dweller. Leonida is waiting to be explored on another console, too. Video games, huh? They’re per-ritty good. Have a good one, folks.

Recent findings suggest that to many people’s surprise, I joined the GTA VI bandwagon late last night and was only able to play for an hour. So I’ll likely be following Vice City traffic laws like the previous games and be an upstanding citizen.

I’m also planning on getting some more OOT time in with my main save, getting collectables so I can finally start my “Mai-ster Mode” run. I have a pretty busy weekend regardless, so I hope I can get some gaming sessions in!

In a recent development, I am up to mission 65 in Earth Defense Force 5, and the game continues to throw newly introduced enemies (and weapons) my way! Finally, Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 dropped on the eShop, but I must not neglect my The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time progress while serving the Emperor (surely no heresy here). I will also spend this Saturday at Lisboa Games Week, our annual video game celebration expo, and catch up with old friends while making new ones, playing fancy new and upcoming games.

My game of the week is Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader. With the GameCube’s recent 25th American release anniversary, this incredible 2001 slice of Star Wars spaceship combat action turned a quarter of a century. I am still waiting for something that surpasses it. Time to chase those secret hidden upgrades and try to go for those gold medals again. Should be “fun” in my old age and diminishing reflexes. I will have to trust the Force on this one...

Those are our plans, but what about yours? Let us know what you'll be slapping in your Switch (2) in the poll below.
