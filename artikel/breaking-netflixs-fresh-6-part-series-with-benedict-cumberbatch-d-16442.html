Netflix's latest comedy series laughs its way to positive reviews.

Netflix's roster of TV demonstrates is filled with exciting comedy series, and among 2026's biggest releases on the streamer so far is one of them, the Kristen Bell and Adam Brody rom-com show Nobody Wants This. The streaming service also features hit comedy shows in both the live-action and animated departments, including multi-season series like the coming-of-age Never Have I Ever, Lily Collins' global smash hit Emily in Paris, the complex BoJack Horseman, and more. In fact, one of Netflix's longest shows, with eight seasons, is a comedy, Major Mouth, and a new show has joined these releases with a hit status.

On Rotten Tomatoes, Netflix's Cunk on Cinema has debuted with a high 83% critics' score based on six reviews at the time of writing. That is a solid start for the latest comedy series, though it is key to note that with not that many reviews so far, the score is expected to fluctuate. There is also the fact that Cunk on Cinema was released worldwide on Netflix — after debuting on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer in the United Kingdom on November 12 — on November 15, which means that more reviews should come throughout its first week of availability.

Given how the show has only been out on Netflix for a couple of days, Cunk on Cinema's audience score on Rotten Tomatoes has yet to be unveiled, with fewer than 50 ratings logged so far. At the time of writing, the only negative review on the platform comes from The Wall Street Journal's John Anderson, who is blunt about how the show tackles Philomena Cunk's obliviousness when it comes to movies everyone knows, saying, "The jokes have been made." Cunk on Cinema is the latest release to follow Diane Morgan as the title investigative reporter after Cunk on Britain, Cunk on Earth, and more.

Morgan can also be seen in one of the biggest TV reveals on streaming right now, though not on Netflix, but Disney+. The actress gives life to one of the many AIs who appear in Marvel Studios' VisionQuest. That MCU connection continues in a way on Netflix's Cunk on Cinema, as one of the many guests who Philomena Cunk interviews on the comedy series in order to understand film-making is none other than Marvel's Doctor Strange star, Benedict Cumberbatch. The upcoming Netflix release also features interviews with director Michael Bay, composer Daniel Pemberton, and many other experts.

In a recent development, among the positive Cunk on Cinema reviews on Rotten Tomatoes is one from Daily Mail UK's Christopher Stevens, who believes the new Netflix comedy series "displayed flashes of genuine knowledge of how film evolved" and that made it even funnier. Cunk originated on Charlie Brooker's Weekly Wipe, with Brooker being the creator of a massive Netflix sci-fi series, the anthology smash hit Black Mirror, which has been renewed for season 7 on the streaming service. Now, it remains to be seen if Cunk on Cinema season 2 will happen, as Brooker and Morgan usually use Cunk for standalone miniseries, not multi-season shows.
