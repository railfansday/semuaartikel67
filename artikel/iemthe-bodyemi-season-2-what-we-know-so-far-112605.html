📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that the Body is Netflix’s latest buzzy teen psychodrama, and it’s not surprising why. The show, a cross between The Craft, Jennifer’s Body, and Cruel Intentions, follows a group of Catholic high school cheerleaders who sacrifice a newcomer in a pact with Satan to secure a spot in regionals. But when their sacrifice works too well, all of the girls on the team—and the town at large—are left with religious hysteria.

It has been revealed that the show quickly caught fire on social media following its arrival on November 11, leaving fans praying for a second installment, but will it return? Here’s everything we know about season 2 of The Body.

According to recent reports, the Body has not been renewed for a second season yet by Netflix. The streamer typically waits a few weeks after a show’s premiere before sharing a season renewal announcement.

No cast members have been confirmed for season 2 yet, but fans are hoping that Kristina Bogic, Sara Boustany, Geena Meszaros, Nnamdi Asomugha, Louisa Krause, Shirley Chen, Jackson Kelly, Sofia Wylie, Kyra Pierce, Phallon Pierce, Marcel Ruiz, Orlando Norman, and Gabby Windey all return to their roles.

Season 1 ends with a few cliffhangers that leave the window open for a potential season 2. In the final episode, Anya finally confesses to head priest Father Franklin (Nnamdi Asomugha), and Grace finally wakes up, with her first words being “I’m God.”.

Recent findings suggest that some fans have interpreted Anya’s confession as being the reason why Grace finally wakes up. Gammell didn’t confirm the theory, but she told Deadline, “That’s a fire take...that is Maddie’s prophecy, and if there’s a season 2 we’ll be all over that.”.

Elsewhere in Gammell’s Deadline interview, she opened up about Tyler and Lisbeth potentially sleeping together, which could possibly become its own season 2 plot. “I think if they sleep together, it’s like another great mystery of the show. None of us were there, so none of us really know what happened in that room,” she added. “But I do think Geena has a joke that Lisbeth’s first kiss was the mouth-to-mouth with Grace when she was trying to resuscitate her. So, I don’t know how fast she’s going. She’s just a baby.”.

In an interview with Swooon, Gammell noted that the ending of The Body was “a tease and a setup of everywhere that we wish to go next season, if we’re so lucky to get one.”.

Some of the cast members already have ideas on what they’d like to see happen should The Body receive a season 2 renewal. “We love to pitch stuff to Quinn [Shephard] and Gina [Gammell], like probably too much. They’re so receptive,” Boustany told Swooon, while Bogic added, “We’re all so close, and that was also the tone on set. It was very welcomed [to pitch ideas].”.

The Body season 2 has no release date yet, so fans will have to stay tuned for updates.
