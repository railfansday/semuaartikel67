If it’s up to Scott Caan and Casey Affleck, the Malloy brothers, aka “Mormon twins,” will be returning in Ocean’s 14.

While recently chatting with The Hollywood Reporter about his fresh movie The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, Caan was happy to share he and Affleck are “100 percent” open to reprising Turk and Virgil Malloy, respectively, in the follow-up that’s in development at Warner Bros.

Recent findings suggest that “If the movie gets made, I’m 100 percent in. I actually have not read the script yet, but I hear the script is great,” he said, adding, “I just saw Casey the other night too, and I asked him and he was like, ‘Yeah, let’s go.’ So I can speak for Casey. In a recent development, THR has reached out to Warner Bros. for comment.

Industry observers note that caan and Affleck first appeared in 2001’s Ocean’s Eleven, playing two bickering brothers that Danny Ocean (George Clooney) and Rusty Ryan (Brad Pitt) recruited from Utah to serve as the crew’s getaway drivers and mechanics. The pair reprised their roles in 2004’s Ocean’s Twelve and 2007’s Ocean’s Thirteen.

David Leitch (John Wick and The Fall Guy) is attached to direct the latest installment in the heist movie franchise that is also expected to see Clooney, Pitt, Matt Damon, Julia Roberts and Don Cheadle return.

Recent findings suggest that “Working with those guys, I got so spoiled,” Caan recalled of previously working with the stacked cast and filmmaker Steven Soderbergh. “I was young and I’m like, ‘Oh, this is what making big movies is,’ and it isn’t always like that. I remember walking into a room with all those guys thinking like, ‘All right, where’s the bad guy going to be or the guy who’s no fun?’ And I learned very rapidly in my early 20s [that] the most talented people are always the coolest people and the nicest people.”.

Ocean’s 14 is expected to begin filming in late 2027.

Leitch recently told People that “everybody’s excited about moving it forward,” but that “getting all those people together, and their schedules are always the concern.”.
