Square Enix has released newly introduced information and screenshots for Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United introducing traveling companions Fullbright, Ward, Undine, Herman, and Boston, two business minigames, the Zweig Tournament, and more battle elements.

■ Characters: Traveling Companions.

Fullbright (voiced by Hunter Peterson in English, Daiki Hamano in Japanese).

Sources indicate that A strapping young man and the heir to a prestigious merchant family based in Wilmington. He is a direct descendant of Fullbright XII, one of the twelve sacred knights of the Matriarch.

—Rexastrum <--> The Monarch’s Star.

Ward (voiced by Bill Butts in English, Kenji Hamada in Japanese).

Industry observers note that A dapper man with a very forward fashion sense hailing from Justerm who makes his living as a hunter. He is a descendant of Ferdinand, one of the twelve sacred knights of the Matriarch.

—Bellastrum <--> The Warrior’s Star.

Undine (voiced by Eurie Nam in English, Sayaka Kinoshita in Japanese).

In a recent development, an accomplished Genbu mage who oversees operations in the northern half of Mahzoz. She is a scholar who dedicates herself to researching magic with a burning passion alongside her young disciples.

—Aequastrum <--> The Merchant’s Star.

Herman (voiced by Cory Yee in English, Daichi Endo in Japanese).

Industry observers note that an old man with a prosthetic leg who can often be seen looking out to the seas from the Great Arch. He seems to be strangely knowledgeable about all manner of pirating affairs.

—Bellastrum <--> The Warrior’s Star.

Boston (voiced by Joshua David King in English, Masashi Yamane in Japanese).

A member of the Lobster Risk that lives on the island at the edge of the world. Quite the curious and erudite fellow who holds great admiration for his ancestors who felled Forneus alongside the Matriarch.

—Aequastrum <--> The Merchant’s Star.

In the Business minigame, you will engage in bidding wars between companies to purchase properties and strengthen your own corporation. It can be unlocked by speaking with Fullbright, the young heir to the renowned merchant family of the same name. Locate Agents all over the world to learn information about local properties and strategically buy them to increase your ranking!

Your owned properties and overall ranking can be viewed from the Business menu. Select Property Acquisition to see a list of available properties and start bidding!

—(1) Request funds from owned properties.

In a recent development, properties belong to one of five industries. The funds provided by each property or group is affected by the economic forecast, which can fluctuate at any time, so plan your requests wisely!

—(2) Outbid rivals to attract property owners to your side!

Each successfully purchased property increases your total assets.

It has been revealed that negotiations and Group Actions can be used to create advantageous conditions for yourself, or disadvantageous conditions for your rivals. The more properties you own, the more skills and rewards will be unlocked. Use them wisely to grow your tech giant to evergreater heights!

—Create favorable conditions using Negotiations.

—Use Group Actions to gain a huge funding boost once the Overdrive Gauge is full!

According to recent reports, —Some property acquisitions also grant rewards! Example Reward—Silent Sea Wine: Restores 200 BP.

Obtaining the property reward Alyosha Gramophone will unlock the Orchestrion in Pidona’s Bent Manor. It can be used to listen to both the original and remake versions of the soundtrack!

The Business minigame difficulty can be selected from one of three settings: Casual, Normal, and Classic. The difficulty can be changed from the Options menu at any time.

The northern bastion of Zweig is host to the Zweig Tournament, a grand showcase of power and wits! Though it was a one-time event in the original, it has been expanded in the remake to multiple rounds and categories. Each tournament cup will feature different foes and conditions. Strengthening your retinue and strategically placing participants will be key to winning the championship title!

Successfully completing the base tournaments will unlock more stages. Each stage has different rules and varying difficulties. Winning tournaments will grant rewards such as aurums and / or items.

It has been revealed that characters can glimmer new techniques and evasions in battle. The tech they use determines what they can glimmer next. Check the Glimmer Chart at any time to see which techs each character can glimmer. The chart indicates “????” for techs you haven’t glimmered yet.

—Glimmer Multishotfrom Instant Shot!

—Glimmer Disarm from Incorporeal Stance!

Combining spells of two different schools can create powerful spells known as Synthesized Arts. They can be learned by obtaining the requisite Treatises. If at least one of the synthesized spell’s elements is effective against a foe, the attack will count as hitting an enemy’s weakness. Synthesized Arts gain Technique Points for both elements it covers, making them an efficient method of leveling spell schools!

In the original, multiple retinue members were required to use Synthesized Arts. In the remake, characters can now use them individually. Treatises can be used to instantly teach characters Synthesized Arts.

In a recent development, mercilessly ambush your foes in a flash, occasionally inflicting Stun. Deals critical damage to Undead.

In a recent development, drown your foes in toxic rain. Occasionally inflicts Doom.

It has been revealed that conjure a barrier of light to protect all allies. Halves all damage for its duration.

There are weapons which allow characters to use or glimmer unique techniques. Weapon-specific techs can be used by any retinue member as long as the weapon is equipped to them.

—“Staff of Vivacity” Power Heal.

Release the powers of the Staff of Vivacity, restoring the target’s HP.

Recent findings suggest that unleash the full power of the stars contained within the blade to deal damage to all foes. The blade reverts to its normal form after use.

Industry observers note that jab elegantly with your spear and spin about using the weapon as a fulcrum. The number of hits increases with spear weapon level.

Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on February 4, 2027.
