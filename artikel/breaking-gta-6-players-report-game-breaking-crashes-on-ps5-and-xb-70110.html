GTA 6 players are reporting crashes on PS5 and Xbox, with some even unable to get past the opening minutes of the game.

While many GTA 6 fans across PlayStation and Xbox are enjoying their time in Leonida, it’s not all sunshine and rainbows for everyone trying to explore Rockstar’s latest installment.

Since its November 19 release, players have reported game-breaking crashes that prevent them from progressing through the main story and even playing altogether.

In a Reddit post, one PS5 player said the game had worked flawlessly until they reached Pipework, one of the game’s early missions, where you are introduced to Mandy.

“It crashes every time I use the plunger or stand around the sink with it,” they wrote, adding that they can no longer progress through the story. “You would think the plane flying was intensive, but it crashes every time I use the plunger or stand around the sink with it. I just paid $125 for my PS5 to be repaired and $100 for the game, this is ridiculous.”.

Others are running into problems much earlier, with a PS5 Pro owner revealing the game crashes as the opening prologue begins.

According to recent reports, “Game keeps crashing at the exact same point during the opening prologue. I’m using a PS5 Pro and have been unable to play since launch. The game crashes immediately as the startup prologue begins, either at the exact same point or a couple of seconds into it, displaying error code CE-108255-1.”.

Recent findings suggest that when checking the PlayStation Support page, error CE-108255-1 prompts players to simply “try updating your game”. However, GTA 6 fans who have encountered this issue on PS5 have reported that clearing the console’s cache, rebuilding its database, factory resetting the PS5, and downloading the game three times has done nothing to alleviate the problem.

GTA 6 forces you to take your time with the main story instead of speedrunning it.

Industry observers note that “Despite all this, the matter persists. It crashes at the same point every single time,” they wrote. “I’m honestly extremely disappointed and frustrated at this point. I’ve exhausted almost every troubleshooting step available online and still haven’t been able to play.”.

Recent findings suggest that the reports are not limited to PlayStation, either, as some Xbox Series X players have also reported crashes within minutes of play. “The game sometimes even crashes after only 15 minutes. The first crash happened about 2 hours after I started playing, and ever since that it kept crashing,” added another player.

Rockstar’s GTA 6 troubleshooting page currently directs players experiencing console errors to PlayStation or Xbox support, but hopefully a patch will fix this issue sooner rather than later.
