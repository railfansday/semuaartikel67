📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Several decades after World War II ended, this harrowing historical occurrence has remained an key part of modern global discourse. Thanks to the media, we're constantly given a front-row seat to bits and pieces of the event that arguably had the most influence in shaping our world as we know it today. The year so far has delivered its fair share of acclaimed WWII projects, from Tom Hanks' World War II with Tom Hanks docu-series to Brendan Fraser's Pressure and, more recently, Netflix's The Mosquito Bowl, starring Bill Skarsgård. A couple more titles are still expected before the year winds down, but unfortunately, the most anticipated one is missing from that list.

Recent findings suggest that back in 2022, the BBC brought its audiences what would instantly be deemed one of the greatest WWII series since Steven Spielberg and Hanks' miniseries, Band of Brothers. Band of Brothers was a masterclass in TV-making that drew comparisons to another legendary work, Saving Private Ryan, thanks to its high production value and visceral depiction of wartime horrors. Several follow-up efforts to recapture its essence were released to mixed to average reception until the BBC premiered SAS: Rogue Heroes. Created by Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders), SAS: Rogue Heroes has earned universal acclaim, with a rare 100% critic score on Rotten Tomatoes for Seasons 1 and 2.

Season 3 had been in production for some time, and reports of an early 2026 wrap had fans wishing for a late 2026 return. However, it's now official that the acclaimed war series will follow its usual Upcoming Year's release window, with the BBC officially reporting that SAS: Rogue Heroes Season 3 will be released in 2027. Alongside the announcement came first-look stills at what to expect from Season 3. The new photos, which can be seen below, feature a host of returning characters including Paddy Mayne (Jack O’Connell), Reg Seekings (Theo Barklem-Biggs), Jim Almonds (Corin Silva), Pat Riley (Jacob Ifan), Bill Fraser (Stuart Campbell) and John Tonkin (Jack Barton).

It has been revealed that season 3 of the series, adapted from Ben Macintyre's best-selling non-fiction book, will pick up from the events of the Season 2 finale, which saw the gallant SAS heroes parachuting towards Nazi-occupied France. The fresh chapter opens in the summer of 1994 in France, where they have now landed deep behind enemy lines to destroy the German forces and repel Allied advances. The brave men will yet again confront insurmountable challenges in their mission to free Western Europe.

Sources indicate that they will team with the French resistance, as well as members of the Maquis, to be played by a fresh cast including Bérangère McNeese (Miles & Juliette) and Hélie-Rose Dalmay (The Intruder). Other newcomers arriving in Season 3 include the characters Reverend Fraser McLuskey (Lorne MacFadyen) and an American soldier named Silus Roe, to be played by Nick Hargrove (American Primeval); all of whom can be spotted in the gallery above.

SAS: Rogue Heroes Season 3 will premiere on the BBC and BBC iPlayer on a yet-to-be-declared 2027 date, likely in January, and will stream in the US on MGM+. Stay tuned for future updates.
