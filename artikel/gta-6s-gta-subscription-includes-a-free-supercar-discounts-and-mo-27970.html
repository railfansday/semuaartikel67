Grand Theft Auto 6 is rolling out worldwide, and if you have a GTA+ subscription, you can pick up extra goodies for your trip to Vice City. Developer Rockstar Games has revealed that it'll be evolving its subscription service to cover both GTA Online and GTA 6, and for $8 a month, players can unlock several in-game benefits, including store discounts, more public transit options, and better haggling power. If you've preordered GTA 6, you'll automatically get a month of GTA+ for free. One thing to note: the services below are only available while you're subscribed, so if your membership expires or you cancel, those perks will no longer be available. Here's a breakdown of what you'll get if you stay subscribed. Venturas Blvd Elegy supercar and garage Looks do matter in Vice City, and nothing looks better than a '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy supercar when you hit the streets. This high-speed vehicle can be safely stored in a single-car garage in Crosstown, which also includes a weapon locker and the ability to fence stolen items directly. You'll gain access to it after you complete Chapter 3 in GTA 6. Transit Service Bonuses If you'd prefer to rely on public transit, you can grab an upgrade for these facilities. This includes Scooter Bros Premium, premium membership on the RydeMe rideshare app, free scooter, bike, and Seashark rentals, and free rides on Leonida public transportation. Store discounts Select businesses across the city will also offer you a 10% discount on their services. Weapons and clothes shops, hairdressers, tattooists, vehicle mod shops, and more are included. Increased sell value Selling an eligible vehicle will also net you an extra 10% in cash. Meanwhile, GTA Online players are also getting plenty of bang for their buck with GTA+ this month, as they can unlock a free car, in-game discounts, the Vinewood Car Club features, bonuses, and more.
