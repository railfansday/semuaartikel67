Birmingham City supporters have plenty of good reasons to be excited right now, with Chris Davies' side topping the Championship for the first time in many years, coupled with the well-documented prepares set out by Knighthead.

Blues' American investors have completely changed the mood around St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, and in a matter of years, such ambition is leading to a change of environment.

Having been mooted in April 2024, Birmingham unveiled the name and design of their planned 62,000-seater stadium a year ago this week.

The Powerhouse will pay tribute to the Second City's industrious past whilst being the centrepiece of the Sports Quarter regeneration project.

During the planning stages, Blues' owners have got many high-profile names on board regarding key elements such as transport, the creation of jobs and how the stadium will look on the outside and inside.

One figure constantly associated with the Sports Quarter development beyond Tom Wagner is Nick Smith, who became Birmingham's head of infrastructure in 2023.

Smith has issued several updates surrounding the planned development, which is set to be completed in the summer of 2031.

He, along with other Blues chiefs, have been in constant dialogue with supporters over the stadium move, as seen in April where he and Heatherwick Studio's Eliot Postma explained its scale at an event named 'Built by Us'.

With Thursday November 20th being the one-year anniversary of the Powerhouse's unveiling, Smith unveiled last week that another fan-led consultation will go live, titled 'Shaped by You'.

The concept will see Smith send out one question regarding the Powerhouse, to which Bluenoses have five days to respond, before feedback is detailed amongst relevant staff to help shape the experience into one which will be enjoyed by the entire fanbase.

Whilst the 12-chimney design has led to mixed reviews, it is one which will certainly be staying, but that doesn't mean there haven't been changes made in recent times to the overall Sports Quarter.

Indeed, it was recently confirmed that Birmingham are looking for a latest patch of land to build training facilities which will house all of the club's men's, women's and academy sides under one roof, as well as a separate stadium for the women's team.

The Sports Quarter is set to take up 135 acres of land, with reports detailing that the planned training facilities were set to take up over a half of such land, making it unfeasible.

This idea was heavily backed by Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore.

Compared to previous ownership regimes at St. Andrew's, few can fault the lengths at which Knighthead are going to in order to make supporters feel engaged with all that comes along with supporting Blues.

There have been several 'Open House' forums which have detailed goings-on on the pitch as well as administratively, and 'Shaped by You' isn't the only campaign which has been introduced this month.

According to recent reports, blues fans, what needs to be considered the most ahead of the Powerhouse stadium move? Comment below!

Blues confirmed a upcoming brand campaign ahead of the 3-0 win against Millwall on November 3rd titled 'Unreasonable', fronted by supporter and actor, Jayden Healey, who explained the concept.

Industry observers note that "Is it unreasonable to believe that we can have a 62,000-seater stadium, be the biggest club in the world and play Champions League football? Yeah, that's the point of it. But, I believe we're going to get there," he mentioned.
