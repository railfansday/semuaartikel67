📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nvidia shares rose 2.57% in premarket trading on Wednesday to $159.45, up $3.99 from Tuesday’s close of $155.46, after the chipmaker topped forecasts in its first earnings report since Jensen Huang’s exit. The stock was up as much as 3.8% earlier in the session. Third-quarter revenue reached $115.8 billion, more than double a year ago and 20% higher than the previous quarter, beating estimates of $109.1 billion. Adjusted earnings of $2.68 a share topped the $2.48 forecast. Data centre sales, which make up about 93% of revenue, jumped 110% to $107.6 billion. Nvidia guided for fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, ahead of the $125.1 billion estimate, though the period includes an extra week and assumes no data centre sales to China. The company also said it has started shipping its Vera Rubin NVL72 systems. The results lifted AI cloud stocks, with Nebius up 8.2%, Iren 7.4% and CoreWeave 6.6% before the open.
