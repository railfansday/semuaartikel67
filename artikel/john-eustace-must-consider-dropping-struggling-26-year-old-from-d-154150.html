John Eustace must consider dropping struggling 26-year-old from Derby County starting XI vs Watford: Opinion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Derby County are rooted to the bottom of the Championship table coming into this weekend’s games, and there is huge pressure on head coach John Eustace. However, he remains in post and will lead DCFC for their home game against fellow strugglers Watford on Saturday.

It feels like a must-win match for Eustace, who has been under growing pressure from supporters. The season has been a tough one all-round at Pride Park, but with 35 goals conceded in 17 games, they have the joint-worst defence in the league.

Recent findings suggest that one man who has been near ever-present in the Rams’ struggling backline is Dion Sanderson. Against Watford, Eustace has to consider pulling the 26-year-old from the starting lineup.

Recent findings suggest that sanderson has made 15 Championship appearances this season, starting all of them. He has played the full 90 minutes on all but two occasions, being taken off at half-time against West Brom and seeing red after 66 minutes in the 1-1 draw against Sheffield United.

Sources indicate that sanderson was a hit with Derby County last season and he has proven his ability in the Championship across 169 appearances at the level, so Eustace has good reason to believe the defender can deliver. However, the facts are that he hasn’t this season.

The former Birmingham City man has won only 51.3% of his duels, ranking him in the bottom 17% of Championship defenders. DCFC have conceded 28.13 xG with Sanderson on the pitch (bottom 2%) as well, although this is more of an indication of how bad the backline has been as a unit (FotMob).

Eustace has been limited in his ability to make defensive changes, but that is not the case anymore.

Matt Clarke returned to the team against Wolves, and Eiran Cashin was back on the bench. Those two, alongside free transfer signings Shane Duffy and Jack Robinson, mean that Derby County have the depth to make alterations in a bid to find form.

Sources indicate that duffy has impressed since joining, so it could be him and Clarke started in a centre-back pairing. If Eustace goes for a three at the back, it should be the fit-again Cashin or Robinson to join them in the side, not Sanderson.

According to recent reports, derby County desperately need some hope. The 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers gave them a boost, but consecutive defeats to Southampton and Wolves have dampened spirits again.

As rival clubs make changes in the dugout, DCFC have stuck by Eustace. However, that could change with a home defeat to Watford.

This is a winnable game, and you get the feeling that three points are a must. If not, Eustace could be sacked.

That could prompt changes from the boss, who will do all he can to drive a performance from his struggling side as pressure and relegation fears grow.
