Liverpool are reportedly keeping a close eye on Monza striker Gustavo Varela as they consider strengthening their attacking options, with the Portuguese youngster emerging as one of the most exciting prospects in Italian football.

The Reds are looking to improve their attacking depth, and the 21-year-old has attracted considerable attention following his impressive performances in Serie A this season.

It has been revealed that according to a TEAMtalk report, Liverpool have been sending scouts to monitor Varela’s performances weekly. The forward has made an excellent start to his career in Italy, registering nine goals and an assist in his first ten league appearances.

Industry observers note that his remarkable goalscoring form has attracted interest from several clubs, with Tottenham, Brentford and Sunderland also enquiring about his availability. Italian giants Juventus and Napoli are also monitoring the situation.

The Merseyside outfit have struggled to produce consistent attacking numbers this season, and bringing in another goalscorer could help address that concern.

Alexander Isak remains an vital figure in their forward line, while Hugo Ekitike is another option when fully fit. However, Liverpool could benefit from more competition in the final third, particularly if they want to challenge for major honours.
