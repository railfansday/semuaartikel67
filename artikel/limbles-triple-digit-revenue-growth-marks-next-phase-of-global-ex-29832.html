Momentum and industry recognition accelerate investment in EMEA, professional services and partner ecosystem.

In a recent development, LEHI, Utah, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Limble, the modern maintenance and asset management platform, today announced 339% revenue growth over the past three years, reflecting growing demand for its technology as the organization expands internationally and invests in professional services. The milestone earned Limble recognition in the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500â¢ ranking of the fastest-growing technology companies in North America. Limble now enters its next phase of global expansion, building on its recent acquisition of FM360's professional services business and its growing presence in the United Kingdom and across EMEA.

Limble CEO Gary Specter attributes the company's growth to a customer-first approach and a platform shaped by ongoing feedback from maintenance and operations teams, with an ongoing focus on helping organizations build the reliable maintenance data foundation needed for AI.

It has been revealed that "Maintenance has become a strategic function, and organizations that treat it that way are pulling ahead," Specter said. "We listen closely to our customers, and their feedback shapes how we build Limble. Our growth highlights how many leaders are ready to replace disconnected processes with a clear, consistent view of their assets. This next phase is about meeting them where they are, with the regional presence and implementation expertise to help them scale what works across every location.".

According to recent reports, industry research also highlights why maintenance technology is becoming central to organizations' AI strategy. In Limble's inaugural State of Maintenance Report, 92% of surveyed U.S. maintenance and operations professionals agree AI requires complete, reliable maintenance data to be effective. The finding points to a broader priority for organizations: ensuring the information captured through everyday maintenance activities is accurate, accessible and actionable.

Scaling Global Operations and Enterprise Customer Support.

To support growing demand for maintenance technology and enterprise implementation expertise, Limble is expanding its international presence, with a focus on the United Kingdom and broader EMEA region.

In a recent development, simon Link has been named Vice President of Sales, EMEA, to lead regional growth and support organizations modernizing maintenance and asset management across distributed operations. Limble is also growing its global partner network, with MCGlobal Solutions becoming a certified reseller and implementation partner in the UK and Australia.

Industry observers note that alongside its international expansion, Limble is scaling its implementation and advisory capabilities through both acquisitions and strategic partnerships. The firm's recent acquisition of FM360's professional services business strengthens its ability to support complex enterprise deployments and maintenance programs across multiple facilities, bringing additional expertise in program assessments, implementation, tailored training, custom integrations, and ongoing optimization.

Together, these investments will help enterprise customers standardize maintenance practices, improve technician adoption, integrate systems and scale maintenance programs across locations while accommodating the needs of each facility.

Industry observers note that "Maintenance technology is becoming essential to how organizations operate, and we believe this is just the beginning," Specter said. "We're building for the long term, investing in our platform, our people, our professional services capabilities, and our global reach so customers everywhere can manage their assets more effectively and get more value from their maintenance operations.".
