Pokemon Go‘s Daydream Destinations season puts the Alola region in the spotlight, introducing fresh Mega Evolutions, Dynamax debuts, and one of the game’s biggest annual events.

Mega Froslass and Mega Drampa will headline their own Raid Days, while Dynamax Alolan Sandshrew, Slowpoke, and Corphish join the Max Battle lineup ahead of February’s Go Tour: Alola.

With fresh Go Battle League competitions, Community Days, updated Egg pools, and seasonal rewards also on the way, here’s everything you need to know about Daydream Destinations.

The Daydream Destinations season will run from Tuesday, December 1, 2026, at 10:00 AM until Tuesday, March 1, 2027, at 10:00 AM local time.

This follows directly from the Twilight Trails season, which brought Mega Chandelure and Mega Staraptor to the game, along with Pokemon debuts, including Maschiff and its evolution, Mabosstiff.

Daydream Destinations wastes no time expanding the Pokedex, with Pyukumuku making its Pokemon Go debut during the Tropical Paradise event from December 1 to December 7.

Meanwhile, two new Minior forms will arrive during Minior Showers: Geminids, running from December 11 to December 16.

More Pokemon debuts are expected to be confirmed as the season unfolds and additional events are revealed.

Mega Froslass and Mega Drampa will expand Pokemon Go’s Mega Evolution roster during Daydream Destinations, with both making their debuts through dedicated Raid Days.

The newcomers will give trainers fresh opportunities to stock up on Mega Energy and unlock two more powerful options for their Raid teams.

Power Spots will have more to offer during Daydream Destinations, with Dynamax Alolan Sandshrew, Slowpoke, and Corphish joining the Max Battle rotation along with other creatures yet to be revealed.

Gigantamax Pokemon are also taking center stage this season, with Gigantamax Inteleon and Gigantamax Toxtricity headlining dedicated Max Battle Days.

Inteleon will kick things off on December 5, introducing its Shiny form, while details surrounding Toxtricity’s event have yet to be announced.

This latest season will introduce a fresh pool of Research Breakthrough encounters, rewarding players who collect seven stamps by completing Field Research tasks.
