The construction process for AS Roma’s new stadium in Pietralata continues, with the Giallorossi club working on the final adjustments to the detailed design, expected by Christmas.

As Il Romanista reports, excavations for the archaeological surveys have been completed, and the relevant documentation has already been submitted to the Superintendency, from which approval is awaited.

Industry observers note that this marks another step toward the start of construction, with the spring 2027 target confirmed. Massimiliano Umberti, president of the 4th Municipality of Rome, spoke to Radio Romanista.

When asked about the completion of the excavations, Umberti replied: “Yes, from what I know, yes. We are waiting for the PES from AS Roma, then there will be the tender for the areas. And then, finally, I think April could be the right month for the first stone.”.

Industry observers note that there’s also news regarding the proposal to name the current Quintiliani Metro B stop, located near the area designated for the new facility, after Dino Viola. The motion, presented by Councilor Riccardo Corbucci and co-signed by Ubaldo Righetti and Federico Rocca, has been tabled and awaits discussion in the Capitoline Assembly. Umberti reiterated his desire to link the neighborhood to the Giallorossi’s history: “There’s no point in hiding it: the IV Municipio will be branded Rome for obvious reasons.”.
