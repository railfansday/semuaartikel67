Google Maps is rolling out an revision on Android Auto that provides better navigation routes specifically for motorcycles.

Owners in India have recently noticed that Google Maps for Android Auto is showing a new message asking them to restart the app to “switch to two-wheeler directions.” Once enabled, a motorcycle icon appears on the ETA card.

Recent findings suggest that on a support page, Google details this mode, explaining that the mode tailors directions to skip routes where two-wheelers are not allowed, while also included narrow roads that may be faster. Google notes that the feature is only available in “limited locations.”.

Recent findings suggest that these directions show you routes away from roads where two-wheelers aren’t allowed. You may be directed towards shortcuts along narrower side roads.

The page also explains that it is only available for mobile devices, with no mention of Android Auto and CarPlay. Yet, a number of users are seeing the adjustment on their devices within the past two weeks, so it seems Google is expanding it out to Android Auto. That makes sense, as Android Auto has been working to better accommodate motorcycles for a while, and some motorcycles even now have Android Auto support built-in.
