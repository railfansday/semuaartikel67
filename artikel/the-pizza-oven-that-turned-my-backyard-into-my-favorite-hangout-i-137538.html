I wrote about my Gozney Arc XL pizza oven two summers ago, when it became the pie de resistance for using my backyard to get outside more (and for a suspicious number of new friends showing up at my house). More than a year later, I’m happy to report that the novelty hasn’t worn off. If anything, I love it more.

One of my favorite ways to spend a Sunday afternoon, or unwind after a mountain bike ride, is inviting friends over for homemade pizzas. Everyone brings their favorite toppings, we experiment with different combinations, and dinner becomes an activity in itself. It’s dinner and a show, and everyone gets fed. And because we keep ours on a covered porch with adequate ventilation, pizza night doesn’t end when winter arrives. Après-ski pizza parties might actually be my favorite use for it.

In a recent development, the Gozney Arc XL makes it ridiculously easy. It runs on propane (I use the same tank from my grill), reaches temperatures up to 950°F, and can cook a 16-inch pizza in as little as 60 seconds. Its lateral rolling flame distributes heat across the oven, so you don’t have to constantly rotate your pizza. The result is that perfectly blistered, chewy crust I usually associate with my favorite wood-fired pizza joints now in my own backyard.

Gozney makes several ovens, from more portable models to larger, more elaborate setups (many are also on sale right now). The Arc XL hits the sweet spot for me because it’s compact enough for our back patio, still with plenty of room to maneuver a full-size pizza.

According to recent reports, you can make your own dough, buy Gozney’s mixes, or do what we do: keep Trader Joe’s premade pizza dough in the freezer so we’re always ready for an impromptu pizza night. One tip: a little semolina flour on your pizza peel helps the dough slide into the oven without sticking.

My favorite toppings? Peaches, burrata, prosciutto, basil, and hot honey. Comment yours!

Learn from my mistake. When I first bought my Arc XL, I skipped the matching stand for a cheaper rolling cart from Amazon. It was a nightmare to assemble, felt flimsy, and looked terrible. I recently upgraded to the actual Gozney stand, and the difference is night and day. It’s sturdy, streamlined, easy to move, and designed to hold the oven securely. I should have trusted Gozney from the start.

It has been revealed that the stand has a 4.8-out-of-5-star rating from 204 customer reviews, so apparently plenty of people knew something I didn’t. Worth the upgrade, especially while it’s on sale.

If you’re shopping for someone who already owns a Gozney, the brand’s pizza peels, dough trays, and other accessories make excellent gifts and are also on sale for Black Friday.
