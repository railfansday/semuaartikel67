It's that time of year again: Your last chance to initiative your final flights or mattress runs of 2026 in order to snag last-minute elite status for next year.

Sources indicate that if you hold Marriott Bonvoy elite status and aren't going to requalify for your status tier this year, there's good news: The program is extending its “soft landing” policy into 2027. With this policy, if you fail to requalify for your current status this year, you will only drop one tier from March 2027 until February 2028.

Sources indicate that based on the program's elite-status policies, the Marriott Bonvoy status you earned in 2025 will expire at the end of February 2027 unless you requalify for your current level or higher in 2026. But what if you don't meet the threshold for the same or higher status?

Sources indicate that that's where Marriott's generous soft landing policy comes in. Whether you qualify for a level far below your current status or don't qualify for status at all in 2026, you'll only drop one status tier in 2027.

In a recent development, according to Marriott's website, members who don't requalify for status will see their new, lower tier take effect in early March 2027, and they can enjoy this status through February 2028. Meanwhile, if you requalify for your existing tier or a higher tier, you will also hold this status through February 2028.

This soft landing policy, which Marriott has offered for years, is a win-win: It provides valuable benefits to elite members who weren't able to requalify for status, while giving them a reason to stay loyal to Marriott and perhaps try again for status next year.
