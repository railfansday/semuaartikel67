📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that it’s hard to believe it’s been nearly a decade and a half since Jennifer Lawrence first stepped into the career-defining role of Katniss Everdeen—bringing Suzanne Collins’s Hunger Games trilogy to life with a 2012 film adaptation. Alongside costars Josh Hutcherson (who played Peeta Mellark) and Liam Hemsworth (Gale Hawthorne), the actor spent her early twenties on and off the dystopian set, subsequently filming the 2013 sequel, Hunger Games: Catching Fire, and the two-part Mockingjay finale, released in 2014 and 2015. And with a press tour every year, the red carpet saw Lawrence evolve from a fresh-faced actor on the precipice of global fame to a bold, bleached-hair Academy Award-winning star.

Fourteen years after it all began, Lawrence reunited with her Hunger Games family at the Fresh York City premiere of the franchise’s second prequel, Sunrise on the Reaping, last night. In addition to the forthcoming film’s cast (Elle Fanning, Whitney Peak, Mckenna Grace) and that of 2023’s Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes (Rachel Zegler, Tom Blyth), Lawrence was joined by the likes of OG costars Hutcherson, Woody Harrelson, and Elizabeth Banks. The Die My Love actor walked the purple carpet in a fur-trimmed black Dior coatdress pulled from Jonathan Anderson’s Pre-Fall 2026 collection and embroidered with red and pink flowers.
