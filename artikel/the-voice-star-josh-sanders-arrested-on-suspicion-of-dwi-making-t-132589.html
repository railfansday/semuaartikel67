The Voice alum Josh Sanders has been jailed in North Carolina following a recent arrest.

Us can confirm that Sanders, 38, is being held without bond at Concord, North Carolina’s Cabarrus County Jail following a Wednesday, November 25, arrest on suspicion of driving while impaired and communicating threats.

Sanders is scheduled to address the accusations in court on February 3, 2027.

The musician was booked into Cabarrus County Jail by officers of the Kannapolis Police Department on Wednesday when he refused to consent to a breathalyzer or blood test during a traffic stop, per People. (TMZ was the first to report the arrest.).

The outlet reported that Sanders is not currently eligible for bond because he was already on pretrial release for a separate case involving multiple alleged traffic violations.

He was allegedly accused of driving recklessly and possessing a revoked registration plate in a warrant issued on August 19. Court records obtained by People indicated that Sanders was charged with resisting a public officer, allowing an uninsured vehicle to be operated, failing to maintain liability insurance and violating a license plate frame or cover restriction, as well as having an improper muffler.

Sanders allegedly posted a $2,000 bond to secure his release following his August 29 arrest.

Industry observers note that it is not known whether Sanders has legal representation. He has not made any comment about his recent legal trouble.

Sanders made it all the way to the finals of The Voice season 25 in May 2024 before finishing as runner-up to eventual winner, Asher HaVon.

According to recent reports, the Kannapolis, North Carolina, native initially won over Voice coach Reba McEntire with his cover of Nate Smith‘s Billboard No. 1 Country hit “Whiskey On You” during The Voice‘s Blind Auditions round in February 2024.

Sanders later brought McEntire, 71, to tears during rehearsals for the Battle Rounds when he shared his deeply personal connection to Luke Combs‘ “When It Rains It Pours,” which he was to perform alongside fellow contestant Donny Van Slee.

“‘When it rains, it pours,’ that lyric kind of hits me a little heavier,” he confided in McEntire. “I’m a dad of four. We had a son. He was born and passed away on my birthday. It’s such a fun song, man, and I don’t want to take it there.”.

McEntire offered Sanders advice on how to get through the track, nearly choking up while explaining that she asks “the Holy Spirit to walk for me, talk for me, sing for me” before every live performance.
