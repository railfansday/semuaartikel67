Max Dowman has given Mikel Arteta a serious decision to make as the Arsenal star impressed once more on international duty.

The winger netted his first goal for England’s Under-21 side on Monday, as Lee Carsley’s side ran out 3-2 victors against France at Ashton Gate in Bristol.

His strike wrote him into the history books for his country, as he became the youngest ever goalscorer for the Under-21 team.

Dowman, 16, started off the right flank for the Young Lions, and he scored the third goal for the home side on a wet Monday evening.

He had his Arsenal team-mate Ethan Nwaneri inside him as the No10, with Tottenham’s Mikey Moore, on loan at FC Koln in Germany, on the left wing.

Divin Mubama, the Southampton striker, led the line while young Gunner Tommy Setford, who is spending the campaign on loan at Stevenage in League One, was between the sticks.

Dowman produced a stellar showing, capping off a well-crafted England goal as Harrison Armstrong seized upon a late mistake in the French backline, before squaring to the young forward to slot home into an unguarded net.

It follows on from his impressive display in the 1-0 defeat by Portugal last time out, where he was introduced for Amario Cozier-Duberry on the hour by Carsley.

While he could not find that elusive equaliser, he was bright in his substitute cameo on Friday.

It remains to be seen what role he could play in Arsenal’s trip to Newcastle United this weekend, before they host Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City at the Emirates Stadium next week.
