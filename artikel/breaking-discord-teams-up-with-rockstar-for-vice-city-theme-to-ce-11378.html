Discord has officially teamed up with Rockstar Games to introduce a limited-time GTA 6 Vice City theme, allowing consumers to customize the app’s appearance with colors and designs inspired by the upcoming game.

The collaboration arrives just days before Grand Theft Auto 6’s scheduled November 19 release, which will take players back to Vice City. With anticipation building around Rockstar’s next installment, Discord consumers can now bring the game’s neon-inspired aesthetic to their accounts.

Discord declared the new customization options in a post on X, writing: “Bring Vice City to your Discord.”.

“Starting today, you can apply the Vice City color theme, app icon, and Display Name Style,” the firm explained.

Bring Vice City to your Discord.​Starting today, you can apply the Vice City color theme, app icon, and Display Name Style. ​The Vice City theme is available to everyone through December 13 and to Nitro members (excluding Nitro Basic) through February 20. pic.twitter.com/QKsT1m8Ikq.

The theme is available on both desktop and mobile, allowing users to customize Discord with colors inspired by Vice City’s signature neon aesthetic.

From November 16 through November 22, users can activate the theme directly through Discord’s Quest Home. After that, the color theme and app icon can be found under User Settings > Appearance on desktop or Settings > App Settings on mobile.

The Display Name Style is available separately through Discord’s profile customization settings, allowing users to change how their name appears alongside the latest app design.

The official promotion also includes a Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Video Quest, giving eligible customers an opportunity to earn Discord Orbs by completing the activity before November 18.

Discord is also directing fans to Rockstar Games’ official Discord server, where they can follow updates and connect with other players ahead of GTA 6’s launch.
