Industry observers note that this is a ranking that is quite revealing of the low density among goalkeepers in France which has just been revealed. In its weekly letter, the CIES Football Observatory establishes its top 100 goalkeepers with the highest market value. And Mike Maignan, maintained in his position as No. 1 by Zinédine Zidane in blue, is not even in the world top 15.

In a recent development, the last bastion of AC Milan (31 years old), under contract until 2031 with the Lombard club, only climbs to 19th place with an estimate estimated at 43.8 million euros. And the Rossonero is not the most expensive French goalkeeper since he is ahead of a short head by one of his understudies in the selection, the Lensois Robin Risser, valued at 45.1 million euros.

No other French representative appears in the CIES top 40. You have to go down to 49e and 51e ranks to fall respectively on Lucas Chevalier, who has not played for almost a year for PSG (€20.2 million), and Guillaume Restes, who recently honored his first two selections with the Blues under the orders of “Zizou” in the League of Nations (€19.9 million).

Sources indicate that it is Joan Garcia (25 years old), linked to FC Barcelona until 2031, who dominates this ranking with a market value estimated at 80.6 million euros. Gianluigi Donnarumma (Manchester City, €79.9 million) and Senne Lammens (Manchester United, €64.2 million) complete the podium. Note that Matvey Safonov, the Paris Saint-Germain goalkeeper, is ninth (€50.8 million).
