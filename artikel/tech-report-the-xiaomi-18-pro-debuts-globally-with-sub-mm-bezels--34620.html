Xiaomi is pushing the limits of modern smartphone specs with a killer design, flush camera lenses, and a next-gen Snapdragon SoC.

Sources indicate that if you’re looking for a phone with top-tier specs, Xiaomi has a upcoming phone for you. The Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max are the company’s latest flagships, available starting today, November 17. When looking at the spec list for the Xiaomi 18 series, it’s difficult to find anything that was left out.

During the launch presentation, Xiaomi emphasized that its newly introduced privacy display wasn’t just a copy of “other privacy displays.” Rather, they created an entirely new subpixel layout called HyperRGB 2.0 that delivers far higher resolution and brightness output than the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra. You can also enable and disable the privacy display feature on the Xiaomi 18 Pro series with a click of the new AI button on the left side of the phone. Functionally, Xiaomi’s privacy display looks nearly identical to Samsung’s, including the ability to have specific apps use it.

According to recent reports, both models feature identical specs, including the privacy display and cameras, with the exception of the screen size, battery capacity, and charging speed. Here are the differences:.

In a recent development, the 120Hz LTPO display is bordered by a 0.99mm bezel, the smallest in the industry so far, 4,000-nit peak brightness, and TÜV Rheinland Triple Eye Care Certification, including a 2160Hz PWM dimming rate. Inside the phone is a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 processor with a 3D IceLoop cooling system, and each model’s silicon-carbon battery includes 28% silicon content.

Industry observers note that on the back is a pair of 200MP cameras, including a 1/1.28-inch main sensor and a 1/1.56-inch periscope telephoto sensor. These are massive sensors, especially compared to the 1/3.94″ and 1/2.52″ telephoto sensors in the Galaxy S26 Ultra. Despite their size, Xiaomi packs them onto the back in an island with fully flat glass — no protruding camera lenses!

That island on the back isn’t just for cameras, though. It also packs in a 120Hz LTPO M11 OLED panel with 4,000-nit peak brightness for ultra-high-quality selfies and group shots, plus support for notifications and apps. Xiaomi created a new GenAI Apps feature that lets you create your own apps on the back display, plus official support for several popular apps like Google Maps, Spotify, and more.

It has been revealed that the Xiaomi 18 Pro series ships with HyperOS 4 (Android 17) and includes 3 months of Google AI Pro, plus 5TB of Google Cloud storage. The Xiaomi 18 Pro retails for €1399 for the model with 12GB RAM and 256GB storage, or €1599 for 12GB/512GB. The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max starts at €1599 for 12GB/256GB, €1799 for 12GB/512GB, and a 16GB/1TB model will be available soon for €2099.

Industry observers note that some retailers also sell a bundle that includes the Official silicone magnetic case, which usually retails for €39, as well as the Magnetic Remote Stand, which retails for €69. That remote stand functions as a MagSafe-style stand and remote, giving you camera shutter functionality and a scrolling feature.
