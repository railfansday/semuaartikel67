Milan’s new lucrative kit deal with Adidas will allow the Rossoneri to make a significant-money signing, and according to QN, one of their top target is Napoli’s Scott McTominay.

It has been revealed that napoli star McTominay has reportedly attracted Milan’s interest. Italian newspaper Quotidiano Nazionale notes that the Rossoneri are keeping a close eye on the Scottish international, especially after agreeing to a fresh kit deal with Adidas.

Their agreement, which will begin in 2028, will last ten years and bring €50m per season to the Serie A giants.

This future financial boost isn’t the only reason Adidas might help Milan sign the Napoli star, who is one of the German company’s testimonials.

Furthermore, McTominay’s contract with Napoli expires in June 2028, and the two parties have yet to reach an agreement despite ongoing negotiations.

QN confirms that McTominay has requested a fresh €7m-a-year contract to extend his stay at the Stadio Maradona.

TMW reported the same fee just a few days ago, saying the midfielder’s demands are unlikely to be matched by any Serie A club.

Yet, this latest report suggests that Milan’s new agreement with Adidas might put the Rossoneri in a privileged position in the race for the Scottish star.
