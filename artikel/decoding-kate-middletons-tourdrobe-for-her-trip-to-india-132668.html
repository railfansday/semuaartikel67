Decoding Kate Middleton’s “Tourdrobe” for Her Trip to India

When Kate Middleton stepped out on the green carpet at the Earthshot Awards ceremony in Mumbai this week, it was “an absolute princess moment,” says British designer Amanda Wakeley.

Recent findings suggest that “Catherine has really come into her own. She has found her stride and a new confidence, and you see that in this dazzling but also practical ‘tourdrobe,’’’ Wakeley, who has previously dressed the Princess of Wales and used to dress Princess Diana, tells Vanity Fair.

It has been 10 years since Princess Kate last visited India. Her fashion choices are more sophisticated now than on her visit in 2016, and the speech she delivered at the Earthshot Prize ceremony on November 17 demonstrates a upcoming confidence. With her elevated look, there’s no trace of High Street labels. Kate’s flared floral dresses and the wedge shoes she used to love (but the late Queen Elizabeth apparently disliked) have been replaced by Manolo Blahnik pumps, couture power suits, and clothes by some of her favorite designers, such as Emilia Wickstead.

Prince William and Princess Kate attend The Earthshot Prize Awards Ceremony at the Jio World Convention Centre on November 17, 2026 in Mumbai, India.

Kate’s decision to wear a striking vermilion dress made from handwoven silk by New Delhi–based designer Mahima Mahajan to the Earthshot Prize ceremony spoke volumes. The eye-catching dress, which she paired with a burgundy Prada velvet clutch, was chosen after Kate had selected a special suite of emeralds from the royal gem collection. The green stones on the dress perfectly matched her jewels, which served as an homage to both Queen Elizabeth II and India, as the green of the Indian flag represents growth and prosperity.

Sources indicate that the dazzling stones known as the “emerald tassel suite” were worn frequently by the late Queen Elizabeth II, and while Kate has previously worn the earrings, this marked the first time she wore the set. The effect was dramatic.

“Never has Catherine looked more like a queen in waiting,” Wakeley says of the look.

It has been revealed that wearing the suite ensured that Kate, who has free rein of the royal jewels, looked more regal than ever. “What was fascinating was seeing Catherine wear these incredible family heirlooms along with more relaxed collections from some of her favorite modern jewelry designers like Kiki McDonough and Astley Clarke,” remarks Wakeley.

Kate’s carefully curated wardrobe for the trip wasn’t just about power dressing—it was also about showing she can be sustainable as well as on trend.

Industry observers note that “It was good to see Catherine making a proper nod to sustainability by rewearing her Roksanda dress,” Wakeley notes. “It’s clear a lot of thought has gone into her outfit choices. Her wardrobe has to be practical, and her clothes have to work harder than most people consider for their clothing; she needs weight in the hem of the floaty dresses. She has learned lessons the hard way. She is often seen bending down, talking to children, so she’s conscious of how low a neckline is, how short a skirt is.”.

Princess Kate visits the Nowrosjee Wadia Maternity Hospital, on November 18, 2026 in Mumbai, India.

For her visit to Mumbai’s Nowrosjee Wadia Maternity Hospital on November 18, Catherine chose a striking red tailored Emilia Wickstead skirt suit with Manolo Blahnik pumps. On November 16, she opted for a pistachio blazer by designer Veronica Beard, paired with white Alexander McQueen trousers and pearl-buckled Camilla Elphick flats, a relaxed and accessible look for when she picked up a cricket bat.

Princess Kate plays cricket on November 16, 2026 in Mumbai, India.

Recent findings suggest that as Princess of Wales, Catherine is often compared to Princess Diana. “The main difference is Diana was a fashion icon. Catherine has become a style icon,” Wakeley claims. “There’s a significant difference. Diana played into the fashion of the day and was finding her style. During the time she resigned from public life, she worked out her style DNA. Catherine has had to do that on the job. Very wisely, she has worked out her style and doesn’t get too impacted by fashion and trends. She goes for what she likes. What works for her rather than trends. She really knows now what works for her.”.
