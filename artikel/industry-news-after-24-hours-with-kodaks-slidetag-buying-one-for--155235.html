In a recent development, we don’t usually see companies announcing brand-new products just before Black Friday sales kick off, but after spending a day playing with Kodak’s new SlideTag, I’m sure glad it’s available now, because this is what most of my family is getting as a gift this year. Strictly speaking, it’s not even discounted, but its already cheap $35 price tag means this is just too good to pass up.

According to recent reports, the SlideTag is a tiny digital photo frame that sits on a keychain, but it’s also a tracker compatible with both Apple’s Find My and Google’s Find Hub. You can load it up with photos and have the six-color E-Ink display cycle through them at set intervals, or you can put something more practical on the screen.

The Kodak app makes it easy to generate a QR code, display a to-do list, countdown to a special event or just show you the current date in a nice calendar display. It’s incredibly easy to use, the color E-Ink display is surprisingly good and setting up the tracking feature is super easy. This thing does exactly what it notes it does, and with very little learning curve.

So what’s the catch? You don’t really get to choose what the outside of your SlideTag looks like. These clever little photo frames are only available as a blind box, showcasing eight designs across the vibrant history of Kodak’s packaging and something Kodak calls a secret edition, which is unique to this set.

Recent findings suggest that I ordered two, and while both designs I found in the packaging are great, there are other designs in the lineup I’d have chosen first. Some retailers are selling cases of eight SlideTags with promotional packaging, like you’d see at the checkout counter of a grocery store, but this case doesn’t guarantee you’re going to get all eight designs. There’s no real way to know what you’re going to get.

Industry observers note that that level of risk may not be your thing, but if you’re looking for a way to add a stealthy little tracker with no shortage of personality to your bag, backpack or purse, I think this is a great option. It also makes a great little gift, perfect for a stocking or white elephant party. And if this level of cutesy fun isn’t your thing, Apple’s second-gen AirTags are on sale for $18 this week.
