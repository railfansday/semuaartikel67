[$] Julia 1.13 brings REPL improvements and more

🌐 Source: Lwn • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Version 1.13 of Julia was released on September 10, 2026. Julia is a general-purpose language especially well suited to high-performance numerical computing. Its expressiveness, speed, and composability have made it increasingly popular with scientists and mathematicians. We last looked at Julia one year ago, in a review of the 1.12 release. The newest version features an enhanced read-eval-print loop (REPL), a GUI for installation, a smoother developer experience, and an assortment of performance improvements.
