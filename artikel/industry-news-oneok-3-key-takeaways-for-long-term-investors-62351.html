On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, ONEOK (OKE -2.48%) raised its full-year financial guidance for the third time this year after reporting record natural gas processing volumes in the Permian Basin region of West Texas and Newly introduced Mexico. A debt paydown and an integrated pipeline footprint give the company room to fund growth projects while returning capital to shareholders.

Three things management said stand out.

Industry observers note that the firm paid off nearly $5.5 billion of debt during the third quarter after completing a $9 billion equity transaction. Management is using that balance sheet relief to shift its leverage target to 3.25 times debt-to-EBITDA or lower, where EBITDA represents earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, a metric measuring core operating profitability.

Sources indicate that total raw feed throughput for natural gas liquids (NGLs), which are liquid hydrocarbons such as propane and ethane, grew 6% year over year. The manufacturer stored raw feed while waiting for its Medford fractionator facility to restart in mid-December, pushing those associated earnings into early 2027.

According to recent reports, surging power demand from electric utilities and technology data centers is driving long-term commitments for natural gas pipeline capacity. ONEOK recently locked in a 10-year contract to move 250 million cubic feet per day of natural gas to power a 1-gigawatt plant.

Producer drilling activity is building across the Bakken and Mid-Continent regions, giving management direct momentum into year-end. ONEOK's integrated network connects wellhead gathering to Gulf Coast export facilities, protecting cash flow through long-term fee contracts and active commodity hedging while management tracks drilling discipline and regional pricing differentials.

With debt paydowns opening up balance sheet room and recent acquisitions adding Permian scale, ONEOK is positioning its system to capture expanding power generation and export demand.
