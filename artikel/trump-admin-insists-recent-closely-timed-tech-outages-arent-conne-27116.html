📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There is no indication that a recent wave of high-profile technology outages is the result of a cyberattack or any form of coordinated action, a senior Trump administration official said Wednesday.

"We have a great deal of confidence in our assessment that these incidents are the result of upgrades of the systems, systems being patched, hardware being applied" or other "disparate" events, the official noted on condition of anonymity to discuss the government's intelligence assessments.

The official briefed news outlets by phone in the wake of numerous major disruptions at large corporations and federal agencies in recent days, including a nationwide ground stop, a protracted Microsoft Outlook outage and a reported blackout of the National Security Agency's classified communications network.

Other incidents include software concerns at JPMorgan Chase, an outage at United Airlines that caused some flight delays and product issues at Apple.
