In a recent development, an easy way to identify the best gifts? The ones that are still in use long after the decorations come down. That’s the appeal of a good tool. Not because it’s flashy, but because it becomes part of someone’s everyday routine.

It has been revealed that that’s what makes a Leatherman such an easy gift to give. With options ranging from minimalist everyday-carry knives to fully featured multitools, there’s a model for every personality on your list.

According to recent reports, better yet, select Leatherman favorites are 20 percent off through December 31, exclusively at Leatherman.com. During the brand’s Black Friday and Cyber Monday promotion (November 19-30), shoppers can also receive a free lanyard bead with orders over $125 or a free Bit Kit with orders over $225.

The only question is which one fits the person you’re shopping for.

Industry observers note that for the person who values versatility but travels light, the Skeletool CX is an everyday-carry upgrade they’ll actually use. Think travelers, hikers, cyclists, and anyone who likes being prepared without dedicating half a pocket to a tool. At just five ounces, it combines a knife, pliers, wire cutters, bit driver, and bottle opener in one streamlined package, striking an impressive balance between capability and portability. The customizable bit driver even stores an extra bit in the handle, making quick gear adjustments easy when you’re on the move. Available in a range of colors and customizable options, it’s a practical gift that feels personal, too.

Industry observers note that not everyone needs a multitool. Some people can get by with a really good knife. The Skeletool KB delivers exactly that, pairing a lightweight folding blade with a bottle opener built into the pocket clip. Weighing only 1.3 ounces, it’s a thoughtful upgrade for the person who always likes to have a knife within reach. The one everyone turns to when a box needs opening, a tag needs cutting, or ribbons need snipping off a holiday gift. Practical, affordable, and available in a range of colors, the Skeletool KB proves that sometimes the simplest gifts get the most use.

The Skeletool KBX takes the simplicity of the KB and adds a little grit. Its combo blade pairs a straight edge with a serrated section, making it a natural fit for people who put their gear to work. It’s the kind of knife that is just as likely to live in a tackle box or hunting pack as it is in a pocket. Durable and customizable, it’s a thoughtful gift for someone who believes good gear should be used, not babied.

According to recent reports, some people can’t help themselves. Give them a loose hinge, a wobbly chair, or any project that needs finishing, and they’ll start looking for a tool. The Rebar was built for exactly that kind of person. One of Leatherman’s longtime bestsellers, it packs 17 tools into a compact design, including pliers, wire cutters, screwdrivers, a file, a saw, and both straight and serrated blades. Whether they’re restoring an old piece of furniture, tying custom flies, or bringing a long-neglected project across the finish line, the Rebar is ready to help. Give the Rebar to the person who would rather figure it out themselves than wait for someone else to do it.

In a recent development, some people have a reputation for being prepared. Get that person on your list the Wave Plus. Considered one of Leatherman’s most iconic multitools, the Wave Plus packs 18 tools into a durable, easy-to-carry design. What sets it apart is accessibility: many of its most-used tools can be opened with one hand and accessed without unfolding the entire tool, making quick adjustments and repairs that much easier. More capable than a pocket knife but compact enough to carry regularly, it’s the multitool for people who like having an answer ready before the question is even asked. And like all Leatherman tools, it’s backed by a 25-year warranty.

Recent findings suggest that founded in 1983 by Tim Leatherman, Oregon-based Leatherman Tool Group is the world’s largest manufacturer of high-quality multipurpose products with distribution in more than 86 countries. Built upon three principles, unwavering perseverance, ingenious design, and the ability to save the day, Leatherman has empowered people around the world to solve problems, substantial and small. Proudly based and manufactured in Portland, Oregon, the brand’s factory is located in the same building as the manufacturer headquarters. For more information visit leatherman.com.
