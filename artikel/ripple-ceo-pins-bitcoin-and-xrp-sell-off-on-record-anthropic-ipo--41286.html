According to recent reports, ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse noted on Nov. 19 that crypto markets could rally after Anthropic's IPO, as they did after SpaceX went public. He spoke to CNBC on the day Anthropic's shares started trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

According to Garlinghouse, investors sold Bitcoin and XRP before the listing to free up cash for shares of the Claude developer.

In a recent development, "We talked earlier about the most recent sell-off in the crypto markets — we also saw a sell-off very similar when SpaceX went public. What happens is you've got risk-on assets and people are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included — Bitcoin, XRP — and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Anthropic IPO. What we saw after the SpaceX IPO was a rally in the crypto markets. I think you'll see that happen here as well," the Ripple CEO remarked.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the initial range of $70 to $78. The firm and its seven co-founders sold 980 million shares, up from the 825 million shares they planned to sell earlier. The offering raised $81.3 billion, and a $26 billion private placement brought the total to $107.3 billion.

It has been revealed that at a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, Anthropic became the world's 12th-biggest firm, just behind Tesla. SpaceX raised $75 billion in its market debut in June.

Sources indicate that dan Ives of Yorkville Ives remarked that "Anthropic's IPO is a big near-term win for the tech sector." University of Florida finance professor Jay Ritter compared AI with the early internet. "Thirty years ago, the internet was the next big thing. It has changed our lives. Today, artificial intelligence is the next big thing. It will change our lives, too," Ritter said.

Sources indicate that asked about the listing itself, Garlinghouse noted that Anthropic's shares would trade well. "I think it's gonna be extremely well received, and we obviously saw a price above the range they had. I think it's gonna trade very well," he said.

In the same interview, Garlinghouse called blockchain-based transactions "a wave of emerging technology." "I think 10 years from now you'll see blockchain implemented across our financial infrastructure," he mentioned.

It has been revealed that after the SpaceX IPO in June, Bitcoin took almost three weeks to turn higher. It started to rise after the U.S. Treasury revealed that it would buy back government bonds.
