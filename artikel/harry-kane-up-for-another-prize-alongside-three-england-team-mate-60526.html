Harry Kane is one of the main contenders for the prestigious Best Men's Player prize at the Globe Soccer Awards - and you can vote for the winner.

The Ballon d'Or king is joined by England team-mates Jude Bellingham, Declan Rice, and Bukayo Saka in the category.

Meanwhile, Lionesses star Alessia Russo is the sole English representative in contention for the Best Women's Player honour.

Voting is now open across 12 categories, with the awards to be handed out at a glitzy ceremony in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, on December 28.

The Globe Soccer Awards began in 2010 and have since become popular among fans worldwide, with more than 100 million votes cast in some years.

Last year's winner Ousmane Dembele is also in the running for Best Men's Player, with Lionel Messi, Lamine Yamal, and Kylian Mbappe joining him in the 26-strong shortlist.

Harry Kane, pictured with the Ballon d'Or, is among the nominees for Best Men's PLayer at the Globe Soccer Awards.

Voting is now open across 12 award categories ahead of a ceremony in Dubai in December.

Erling Haaland won the Best Men's Player prize for 2023 and is in contention again.

Premier League stars Erling Haaland, Bruno Fernandes, William Saliba, and Dominik Szoboszlai are also hoping to get their hands on the prize.

Mikel Arteta and Unai Emery are in the running for Best Coach, while Myles Lewis-Skelly and Nico O'Reilly are competing for the Emerging Player gong.

There are 14 categories, with a 'Career and Special Awards' section yet to see its talent unveiled.

Arsenal are hopefuls in the Best Club category for both men and women, with Aston Villa joining them in the former and Manchester City in the latter.

There will also be prizes for the best forward, midfielder, defender, and goalkeeper, and Fans' Historic Club, Best Sporting Director, and Best Agent.

It has been revealed that ronaldo, a serial winner at the ceremony, is defending his Best Middle East Player crown after winning his first Saudi Pro League title with Al-Nassr. Ivan Toney is among his rivals for that.

Fans are able to cast their votes until November 30, after which a jury including the likes of Antonio Conte and Luis Figo will also have their say.

It is an event of truly global interest, with supporters from more than 225 countries having cast votes in previous editions.

'We are incredibly proud to return for our third consecutive year as title partner of the GLOBE SOCCER Awards,' noted Adil Taqi, CEO of title sponsor BEYOND Developments.

'Our continued partnership reflects BEYOND Developments’ commitment to connecting with an international audience through shared passions and experiences.

Sources indicate that 'Football brings people together across cultures and borders, resonating with our belief in pushing boundaries and aiming higher, a mindset shared by the players, coaches and clubs who will be honoured this December. We are proud to stand alongside an event that unites the global football family.'.

Fans are able to vote via the Globe Soccer App.
