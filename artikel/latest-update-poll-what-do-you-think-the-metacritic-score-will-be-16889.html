Considering we're just a few days away from the release of GTA 6 on November 19th, we're bound to start getting reviews at some point between now and launch, and it'll be fascinating to see what the critics think of it!

Recent findings suggest that this is a series that has consistently dominated on Metacritic over the years - a website that takes loads of professional reviews and creates an average score based around them. Grand Theft Auto V has a 97 average rating on there, for example, while GTA 4 goes even higher with a 98 rating.

Recent findings suggest that in fact, every mainline release since GTA 3 (not including stuff like Chinatown Wars and Liberty City Stories) has reached at least a 95 on the platform. To put that into context, the best-rated Xbox Series X game of the year on Metacritic right now is Hades 2, and that game has a 95 average rating.

If we're going off history alone, GTA 6 has every chance of becoming the highest-reviewed game of 2026 on Xbox and PS5. The question is whether it'll manage to hit those highs once again, or whether it'll struggle to meet the hype.

Let's not forget there's never been a wait like this for a GTA game before, and while absence definitely does make the heart grow fonder, it also creates a sense of expectation that's extremely difficult to live up to.

It has been revealed that we'll have to wait and see what happens! You'll find a review roundup on Pure Xbox ASAP, and it seems the smart bet is at least a 95 rating on Metacritic, although that's much easier mentioned than achieved.
