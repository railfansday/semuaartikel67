Let me explain ...When you go for recreational travel in a group, the group could be:- (extended) family- social/family friends- old classmates/schoolmates (and their families)Reason I am asking is because I have never travelled in a "group of families" setting (even while growing up) - it has always been with my family or with my school buddies or alone.When I go by myself, of course, I can do whatever I want. When I've gone with buddies (w/o spouses), there are organizers who decide where to go, where to stay and the itinerary. If the setting is somewhere I would like to visit (and my family doesn't), I join the group. But I have never gone with my family in a group of families (that I can remember offhand).But I just got this question overhearing the struggles DW is having getting her extended family to join her for a couple of days at a vacation destination (I am not in the trip). No, it is not a 'destination wedding' kind of destination, just a beach town.When you go with a group of friends/families, do you guys - or organizer - just decide on a specific hotel and crack the proverbial whip to get everyone to join? Or is there a more nuanced way to do it? Do you pick the hotel with lowest/medium budget? Or do you just let everyone get their own hotel and just meet at a common location (every day) when you guys are at the location?
