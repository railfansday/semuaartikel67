Latest Update: First Photos Capture the Poles of a Rod-Shaped Bacterium

🌐 Source: Flipso • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (54 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that scientists have captured detailed images of the poles of rod-shaped bacteria for the first time by keeping live E. coli still long enough for atomic force microscopy. The technique could help reveal how bacteria grow, divide, and defend themselves, and may point to new antibiotic targets.
