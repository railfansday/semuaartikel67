Paulo Dybala felt personally responsible for leading Roma back to the Champions League and is relishing the opportunity to test himself against Europe's best teams.

Roma are in the Champions League for the first time since 2018-19 and have taken four points from their opening four matches.

But Gian Piero Gasperini's side, who have suffered dramatic defeats to Real Madrid and Manchester United already, face their toughest test on Wednesday, with a trip to the Parc des Princes to face European champions Paris Saint-Germain up next.

Roma have won four of their six games against French sides in major European competition (L2), but face a PSG team that have won all four of their matches so far.

PSG have also scored 20 goals in their four Champions League games this season, netting at least four each time. They are the first team to score 4+ goals in four consecutive games in the competition, while only in 2017-18 (25 in six games) and 2025-26 (21 in eight games) have they scored more in a single group stage.

But Dybala is looking forward to squaring off against Luis Enrique's side after spending his first four seasons at Roma trying to get them back into Europe's premier competition.

Industry observers note that "In my first years here, it was obviously a disappointment not to be able to bring Roma back to the Champions League. I also felt responsible," Dybala stated.

"In my first year, we came close to reaching the Europa League final, but in the years that followed we didn’t do enough to deserve it.

Recent findings suggest that "Last year, I saw a determined team. We showed that by keeping clean sheets in the last few games, because we knew qualification didn't depend on us until the final matchday.

"It was a moment of great pride for me, because I could see how happy the fans were to be back in the Champions League, just like we were.

"We want to test ourselves against the best teams in Europe, and now we can.".

PSG have faced seven different Italian opponents in European competition, but this will be their first ever meeting with Roma.

They have had at least 10 shots on target in all four of their Champions League games so far this season – no team on record (since 2003-04) has ever done so in five in a row.

Dybala and Donyell Malen have been Roma's goal threats, with the latter being directly involved in six goals in the Champions League this season (four goals, two assists). The only Roma player with more goal involvements in a single edition is Edin Dzeko (10 in 2017-18 and eight in 2018-19).

The Argentine has registered four assists in nine appearances across all competitions in 2026-27 after struggling with a calf injury.

Dybala signed a one-year contract extension with Roma during the summer, and he hopes to end his playing career in Italy.

Sources indicate that "I never imagined I'd have a career like this, let alone spend 15 years in Italy. I've lived in the south, the north, and now the centre of the country," Dybala added.

Sources indicate that "I've been here for almost half my life, and I'm very happy. Italy welcomed me with open arms. My daughter was born here, so she's Italian.

"I hope to stay in Italy for the rest of my career. It would be special for me to dedicate an entire career to Serie A, as well as the year I spent in Serie B, which was very important.".
