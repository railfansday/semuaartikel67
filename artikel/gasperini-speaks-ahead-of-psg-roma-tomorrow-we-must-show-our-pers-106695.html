After finishing their training at Trigoria, Roma arrived in Paris on the eve of the highly anticipated match against Luis Enrique’s PSG.

Gian Piero Gasperini presented the match at a press conference, addressing issues related to his possible options and more.

According to recent reports, the Giallorossi coach commented on the matchup against a giant of modern football: “It’s the first time we’ve faced PSG. We’ll have to understand their parameters, hopefully reducing their effectiveness.”.

Regarding the early comebacks in the competition, the coach mentioned: “The Champions League is the most crucial competition in the world. You can put in great performances, but there are also incidents, and the best players around.”.

Sources indicate that gasp recalled the match against Atalanta: “The match against Atalanta was tough. We were down to 10 men, but this is a different game. Roma is approaching this competition after years, and for us it’s a measure of what we’re doing.”.

Recent findings suggest that then the general from Grugliasco discussed the solutions he will adopt to limit his opponents: “Clearly, in these types of matches, more solutions are needed; we don’t have any previous parameters. Beyond tactics, personality will be important, as will the ability to counter their play.”.

The Parisians will have several injuries, but that won’t be a problem for them: “There’s no scientific answer to injuries. There’s been a great improvement in diagnoses, and PSG still has a squad that allows them to make up for absences. As I remarked before, PSG is a team that knows how to compensate for the absences of very strong players with organization and with substitutes, who are absolutely valuable.”.

Sources indicate that the matches against Real Madrid and Manchester United can teach us something. “This is the Champions League, a competition of incidents. You have to be excellent in every situation, there are some great players and sometimes it’s not even enough to put in strong performances, even though I think that’s the best way to get results. You have to be careful about mistakes because they often pay the price.”.
