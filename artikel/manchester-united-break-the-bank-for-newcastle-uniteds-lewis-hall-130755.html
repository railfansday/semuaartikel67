Mikael Silvestre has been talking about Manchester United and Lewis Hall.

The former Manchester United left-back (and central defender) speaking ahead of the next transfer window.

Just over five weeks now until that January 2027 window opens.

The Manchester United defence continuing to be a total shambles and Mikael Silvestre discussing players that the media have speculated as being of interest to Man U.

The four names put to Mikael Silvestre are Chelsea’s Malo Gusto, Fulham’s Antonee Robinson and Calvin Bassey, as well as Newcastle United’s Lewis Hall.

The former Man U defender making clear to BestBettingBonuses what he thinks about the Chelsea player: “Manchester United should definitely not be looking to sign Malo Gusto…I think it would be crazy to bring in a player like Gusto into a United defence that is so unsettled. You don’t want to add more problems.”.

Sources indicate that mikael Silvestre then considering the Fulham pair: “I have to be honest, neither Robinson or Bassey are players that excite me or who I think would upgrade the Man U defence. Manchester United need to sign players that are going to raise the level, and those players wouldn’t do that.”.

What about the Newcastle United star?

Mikael Silvestre: “Lewis Hall? That is different. I think he is the type of player that would absolutely raise the level and add value to the squad. He signed a new contract, so that means that Newcastle will command a bigger fee for him, but it doesn’t mean he isn’t available. If he is the player that Manchester United have identified to add quality to the defence, then you have to go and be ambitious and break the bank to get him. It would be a huge fee, but it would also secure the future of the left back position for the next decade because Hall is a young guy. I only want to see Man U sign players that raise the level. Hall would be one of them. It’s not a luxury signing, I think it’s an essential piece of business if he is the guy that you want to get.”.

Mikael Silvestre of course missing the most crucial factor.

If Lewis Hall did ever decide to leave Newcastle United, why on earth would he choose Manchester United?

The lifelong United fan would have his pick of numerous successful clubs, so why join the shambles at Old Trafford?
