The Honor Watch 6 Pro has a 5,500-nit OLED display, ceramic bezel, 18-day battery life, and costs less than it looks.

Sources indicate that in the world of athletic smartwatches, it isn’t often that you could mistake one for a luxury watch, but Honor seems to want to flip all that on its head with the Honor Watch 6 Pro. Just take a look at this excerpt from the company’s press release:.

Framing this brilliant display is a Precision Nanocrystalline Ceramic bezel crafted through a 1400°C high-temperature integrated sintering process, delivering a delicate texture and rich visual depth.

Inspired by modernist architecture, its diamond-cut octagonal rotatable crown features a dual-layer horizontal and vertical cut, complemented by a side button for intuitive navigation.

That sounds pretty wild to me, and it’s backed up by specs that sound just as legit, too. On top is a 1.5-inch LTPO Tandem AMOLED display that outputs 5,500-nit peak brightness. Underneath is a bevy of fresh sensors called the Intelligent Sense System, which Honor claims combines “multi-wavelength monitoring, multi-optical path patrol technology, and anti-interference glass technology to reach deep skin layers.”.

Runners will be pleased to know that the Honor Watch 6 Pro not only incorporates a dual-band GPS, but combines it with a “star-pointing 3-antenna design” and an AI fusion algorithm, which should deliver ultra-accurate tracking in any location and no matter how you hold your wrist. Despite its luxury looks, this one tracks over 120 sports modes, including Honor’s Professional Running Coach feature, which tracks running posture, lactate threshold, heart rate targets, and keeps you on pace with real-time voice coaching.

It has been revealed that during sports modes like Badminton, the watch can identify more than six types of swings, and Honor says other sports modes can deliver similarly detailed statistics. The watch is 5ATM, IP68, IP69, and IP69K water-and-dust-resistant rated, plus it integrates directly with Strava so you can pull all your fitness data together.

According to recent reports, honor is incorporating a newly introduced wrist twist gesture system this time around, letting you use gestures to perform actions hands-free. The watch is rated at 10 days of battery life on a single charge in regular usage mode, while endurance mode will stretch that to 18 days. Pre-sales begin today, November 19, and run until December 31, when the watch is expected to launch, starting at €279/£229 for early bird pricing. After launch, the retail MSRP starts at €379/£329.
