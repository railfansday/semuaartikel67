Jennifer Lawrence isn't letting speculation cloud her vision.

The Hunger Games star poked fun at theories that she'd recently undergone an eyelid surgery commonly referred to as an upper bleph, which have gained traction online since her return to the big screen for Sunrise on the Reaping.

It has been revealed that responding to her makeup artist Shayna Gold sharing a special delivery of Lawrence's new haircare line, Mes, the actress wrote on Instagram Stories Nov. 23, "iykyk she's the one who did my bleph.".

Industry observers note that the 36-year-old's one-liner referred to an upper blepharoplasty, a procedure that reconstructs the upper eyelid area, including the skin, socket and fat surrounding the eyelid. According to the National Institutes of Health, it's one of the most common operations done to the face.

According to recent reports, her comment appeared to shut down fan rumors—which were fueled by Gold's recent post of a photoshoot where she styled Lawrence's makeup—that she had elective plastic surgery to reduce her hooded lids. While the Red Sparrow actress didn't deny receiving the procedure outright, her cheeky response hinted that her upcoming appearance is all thanks to makeup instead.

And when it comes to cosmetic alterations, the Oscar winner has previously divulged her latest beauty upgrades, including her decision to receive breast implants this year.

Industry observers note that "They’re newly introduced," she told Andy Cohen on WWHL, adding of her cup size, "It’s a full C, I think. It’s the same size that they were.".

But the beauty founder is prioritizing the balance between sipping from the fountain of youth and protecting her ability to perform her craft.

"I can’t really get very much done," Lawrence told Vogue in September. "I need to have movement in my face—I get baby Botox, but I can't do a lot. If my skin is going to be a little saggier than I would like, at least it looks dewy and pretty.".

As such, Lawrence—mom to two kids with husband of seven years Cooke Maroney—explained she is hesitant to partake in facial fat transfers, though she did name what procedure interested her most.

"I would love to have a buccal fat removal. I can’t because everybody would know, but just know that I want that," she noted, noting, "I want to continue to watch and see what happens, but I don’t know about that.".
