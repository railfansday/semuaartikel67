The Film Committee at Media City Qatar has signed a memorandum of understanding with UAE-based regional streaming service Yango Play to explore the development, co-production and distribution of scripted and unscripted Arabic-language originals.

The accord, declared on the sidelines of the Film Committee at Media City Qatar’s Doha Industry Days 2026, will also cover content acquisition and licensing opportunities, kicking off with eight-part Qatari documentary series Elzam.

Produced by Doha-based Katara Studios, the series explores the Gulf region tradition of falconry through a group of contemporary falconers. It with premiere with the screening of a special episode at the Doha Film Festival, running alongside the Industry Days. Sources indicate that yango Play will seek to bring the series to audiences across the region and beyond the Middle East and North Africa. Watch on Deadline.

“Our ambition is to build a sustainable screen industry in Qatar that creates lasting opportunities for Arabic-language productions and Qatari and regional talent,” said Hassan Al Thawadi, Chairman of the Film Committee at Media City Qatar.

“Via our partnership with Yango Play, we will explore ways to develop original Arabic-language productions, build expertise within Qatar’s production sector and bring stories from across the Arab world to international audiences.”.

Yango Play is an entertainment App offering video streaming, music, and mini-games and integrated with the Yasmina AI assistant. It was launched in 2024 and has a special focus on Arabic-language content, with its biggest audiences in the Gulf and Egypt.

“Our partnership with the Film Committee at Media City Qatar represents an vital opportunity to champion Arabic storytelling and support the creative talent shaping the region’s entertainment industry,” stated Roman Shimanskiy, Chief Executive of Yango Play and Yasmina.

“Together, we look forward to exploring fresh collaborations that bring compelling local stories to life, create opportunities for regional talent and connect Qatari and regional productions with audiences across MENA and beyond.”.
