Rita Wilson is making her mark.

Two months debuting a massive cross on her back, sparking speculation that she had gotten a tattoo to match ink sported by her and Tom Hanks' son Chet Hanks, the "Stranger With Age" singer clarified if she had actually gotten some body art.

“It was a prank that I did for my son’s birthday, who has a lot of tattoos,” Rita emphasized during a Nov. 23 appearance on CBS Mornings. “I used to threaten him with getting a tattoo, and I would say, ‘You better watch out.'".

And while the temporary body art was "definitely a different look," the 70-year-old explained that she won't be going under the needle for real any time soon.

It has been revealed that as Rita—who also shares son Truman Hanks, 30, with Tom and is stepmom to the Oscar winner's kids Colin Hanks, 48, and E.A. Hanks, 40, from his first marriage—put it, “My daughter noted, ‘The fresh tattoo is not having a tattoo.’”.

Industry observers note that still, Chet appreciated the sentiment. After all, the 36-year-old and his mom share the same sense of humor.

Sources indicate that "He’s a character," Rita raved in an April interview with The Independent. "He’s so eccentric.”.

In fact, the Runaway Bride actress believed Chet "is the person I could never have been at that age.".

Recent findings suggest that "He’s just out there being himself, take it or leave it," she continued. "I love that.".

That's also why Rita is proud that Chet—who previously performed under the stage name Chet Haze—is exploring his passions by pivoting his music career from rap to country in recent years.

Industry observers note that "He's got such a beautiful tone in his voice, and it's so tender," she told People in May. "I love his voice I would do something with him in a second.".

So, what genre of music will their collaboration fall under? Rita, who teamed up with Chet on her 2018 track "Heart He Handed Down," has a few ideas.

She jokingly added, "Maybe we could do a cover of Eminem's 'Cleanin' Out My Closet.'".
