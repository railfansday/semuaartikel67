In a recent development, three of the four Dutch clubs playing in Europe on Thursday night won. Only AZ Alkmaar lost its match.

AZ lost 2-1 at home to Juventus in the Europa League.

In a recent development, NEC Nijmegen beat Omonia Nicosia 3-1, also in the Europa League. The Cypriot champions scored first, but NEC scored twice to make their first home win since August.

In the Conference League, Ajax thrashed Midtjylland 6-1 in Denmark.

It has been revealed that FC Twente beat Pafos FC 3-1 in Enschede. They have now won all three of their games in the competition.
