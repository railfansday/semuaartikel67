Federico Chiesa’s future could bring him back to Italy after a stint at Liverpool that fell far short of expectations.

In three seasons with the Reds, the attacking winger has failed to regain the consistency and performance that made him a star with Fiorentina, Juventus, and the national team.

This situation, on the cusp of turning thirty, could push him to seek a latest opportunity to relaunch his career. The most likely option would be a loan deal on advantageous terms, a solution that would satisfy both Liverpool and the player.

The English club would have the opportunity to develop Chiesa, potentially increasing his attractiveness on the transfer market, while the striker could return to playing more regularly. Also in the background is his goal of joining the national team, which he has been absent from for over two years.

With Roberto Mancini as manager, a return to the Italian league could offer him a chance to reclaim the Azzurri jersey, provided he can demonstrate on the pitch that he has regained his peak condition.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, the most interesting prospects for the former Juventus player’s future lie in Rome, where both clubs in the capital could be evaluating his potential arrival. On the Giallorossi front, Gian Piero Gasperini is seeking additional attacking options and would be interested in adding a player with Chiesa’s qualities, especially in a low-cost move. Much will depend, however, on the financial terms of the potential transfer and the player’s fitness.

Gennaro Gattuso’s Lazio could also enter the race, considering the possibility of strengthening their attack with an experienced player. The competition between the two Roman clubs does not, however, rule out interest from other Italian clubs, especially if Liverpool decides to facilitate the winger’s departure.
