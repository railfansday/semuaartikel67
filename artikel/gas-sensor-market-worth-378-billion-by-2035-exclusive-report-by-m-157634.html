The increasing adoption of gas sensors across industries such as oil & gas, chemicals, mining, automotive, power generation, and building automation is driving market growth. Gas sensors play a critical role in detecting hazardous gases, ensuring workplace safety, monitoring emissions, optimizing industrial processes, and enabling real-time air quality assessment across indoor and outdoor environments. Market growth is primarily driven by stringent environmental regulations, increasing emphasis on occupational health and safety, and growing demand for intelligent heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning (HVAC) and smart building systems. Additionally, rapid urbanization, rising concerns regarding air pollution, and the expansion of smart cities are boosting the deployment of connected gas sensing solutions. The growing adoption of Internet of Things (IoT)-enabled monitoring systems, portable air quality monitors, and advanced gas detection technologies is further creating opportunities for gas sensor manufacturers across industrial, commercial, and residential applications.

Volatile organic compounds segment to register highest CAGR in gas sensor market, by type, during forecast period.

The volatile organic compounds (VOCs) segment is expected to register the highest CAGR in the gas sensor market, by type, driven by increasing demand for VOC detection across consumer electronics, automotive, and building automation industries. VOC sensors provide reliable detection of harmful chemical compounds and are widely used in applications requiring continuous indoor air quality monitoring and pollution control. The growing adoption of VOC sensors in smart homes, connected heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems, and air purifiers is supporting demand for compact and low-power sensing solutions. In addition, increasing emphasis on environmental protection, workplace safety, and emission monitoring across manufacturing, automotive, chemicals, and packaging industries is further contributing to the segment's growth.

Smart cities and building automation segment to record highest CAGR during forecast period.

The smart cities and building automation segment is expected to register the highest CAGR in the gas sensor market, driven by the increasing adoption of intelligent building management systems and connected environmental monitoring solutions. Gas sensors provide continuous air quality monitoring and are widely used in applications requiring automated ventilation control, hazardous gas detection, and occupant safety. The growing integration of gas sensors with Internet of Things (IoT)-enabled systems is supporting real-time monitoring, remote data transmission, and automated responses to changes in gas concentration. In addition, increasing investments in smart city infrastructure, energy-efficient buildings, and advanced HVAC systems are further contributing to the segment's growth.
