Forget a “Million Reasons”—we’re looking at $7 million. The New York City penthouse where Lady Gaga once lived just hit the market for nearly $7 million—and she’s not the home’s only celebrity connection. The 3,700-square-foot duplex was also once home to former NBA star Carmelo Anthony and 30 Rock actor Tracy Morgan, per People.

Located in the Hit Factory Condominium, the unit is perched atop a residential complex that once housed the famous recording studio of the same name. The 1928 building on West 54th Street was the second home of the Hit Factory, where Grammy-winning artists like Michael Jackson, Madonna, U2, and Mariah Carey recorded songs.

In 2005, the music studio moved locations again, and the building was converted into loft-style luxury condos.

Industry observers note that on the market for the first time since 2018, Gaga’s former unit comes with three bedrooms—two on the lower level and a primary suite upstairs. The top-floor space includes its own walk-in closet, bath, and wrap-around terrace that measures over 1,000 square feet and features sweeping city views.

Industry observers note that the listing notes that the unit—once listed as a rental for $24,500 a month—has undergone a “gut renovation” and includes a double-height entry foyer and chef’s kitchen outfitted with appliances from Bosch, Sub-Zero, and Viking. Featuring white-oak flooring throughout, the residence also includes a home office, in-unit laundry, and an array of built-in storage solutions.

For her part, Gaga recently welcomed her first child with fiancé Michael Polansky. While the family is keeping a low profile, they are believed to reside primarily at the artist’s vast Malibu estate, which the native Newly introduced Yorker acquired in 2014.

According to recent reports, “They have an amazing biodome of love,” a source recently told Page Six. “Seems like a really safe place for her.”.

421 West 54th Street, #PHC is listed for $6.995 million with Douglas Elliman.
