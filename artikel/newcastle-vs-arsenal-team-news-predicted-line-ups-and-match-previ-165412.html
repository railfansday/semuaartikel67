Arsenal will have a tough task on hand as they visit St James’ Park.

Recent findings suggest that mikel Arteta’s side are five points clear at the top of the Premier League after their 1-0 victory over Hull City ahead of the international break. The Gunners have now gone nine games unbeaten in all competitions.

Despite only one win in their last six games, Newcastle will be a dangerous side at home.

It has been revealed that although Arsenal have won the last three encounters with Newcastle, recent games at Tyneside have not gone entirely smoothly for Mikel Arteta. He will also have to make a few key decisions in the squad with players injured and a busy run of fixtures.

In a recent development, bruno Guimaraes will certainly be a topic of discussion for this game. The midfielder joined Newcastle four-and-a-half years ago, playing an crucial role in the development of the side, before moving to Arsenal on a reported 75 million pound move in the summer.

Whilst he has struggled to make himself an automatic starter in Arteta’s side, he could have a bigger impact with the absence of Martin Zubimendi.

Recent findings suggest that zubimendi is likely to be out for several weeks. Declan Rice should start, and Guimaraes is also likely to be involved, to form a double pivot.

In a recent development, newcastle will try to play a gritty game to unsettle Arsenal. Despite Guimaraes’ knowledge of the opposition, he will have to assist the visitors to maintain control of the midfield battle.

Saliba is now back in contention after recovering from a back injury. The Frenchman has not played in the first team this season, but has made two appearances as he recovers. He played 45 minutes for the U21s.

Arteta confirmed that Saliba is in good shape and will begin to play more games, though he is unlikely to be in the starting line-up for the game against Newcastle.

According to recent reports, konsa should be in the starting line-up alongside Gabriel. Saliba could come on from the bench, as he looks to make his first competitive appearance of the season after the Champions League final last time out.

In a recent development, saka is still unavailable due to quad trouble, whilst Dowman is suspended. Madueke should start on the right and Havertz will spearhead the attack.

Industry observers note that newcastle have leaked goals at home this season. However, with Madueke and Tzolis providing pace in wide areas, this should not worry Arsenal too much.

Sources indicate that nevertheless, this game should be challenging for the Gunners. Newcastle are dangerous in attack and can capitalise on errors promptly in possession.

Despite Arsenal’s solid defensive record, they have failed to keep a clean sheet in their 4 visits to St James’ Park.

Sources indicate that overall this should be a good watch with goals scored by both sides. However, Arsenal should be able to pick up some vital 3 points.

Predicted score: Newcastle United 2-3 Arsenal.
