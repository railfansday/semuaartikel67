President Donald Trump on Friday noted he has not lost confidence in Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, pushing back on growing pressure from bipartisan lawmakers and even members of his own administration.

Sources indicate that trump, at a wide-ranging White House press conference, was asked about Sen. Tom Cotton, R-Ark., telling The Wall Street Journal that, if Lutnick is to be ousted, it should happen soon enough for the Republican-led Senate to confirm his replacement before Democrats take over the chamber.

Sources indicate that "Well, Tom's right," Trump replied, saying he would agree with taking swift action if he had indeed lost confidence in Lutnick. "But I have not lost confidence," Trump stated in response to a question from CNBC's Eamon Javers.

"He's a different kind of a guy, very good businessman. He's not traditional," Trump said, praising Lutnick's involvement in the U.S. taking an equity stake in Intel.
