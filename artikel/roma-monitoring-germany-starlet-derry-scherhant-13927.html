Derry Scherhant, born in 2002 and owned by Freiburg, is winning over Germany coach Jurgen Klopp.

In the 5-0 win over Malen’s Netherlands, Scherhant started and scored the opening goal.

A great long-range goal and a brilliant performance. Scherhant is a right-footed attacking winger who arrived at Freiburg in the summer of 2025 for just €2 million from Hertha Berlin.

A real bargain, considering his value has now significantly increased.

As reported by calciomercato.com, Roma have long been interested in Scherhant.

In a recent development, ahead of the winter transfer window, the Giallorossi are looking for a right-footed left winger with the same characteristics as the German: built (185 cm), speed, and finishing ability. This season with Freiburg, he scored two goals and four assists in nine Bundesliga appearances.
