Marco Rose says Bournemouth's fans should dare to dream of Europa League glory, after the Cherries fought back to overcome Celta Vigo and earn their 10th straight victory.

A 23rd-minute red card for Bafode Diakite, which came hot on the heels of Iago Aspas opening the scoring, appeared to put Bournemouth's long winning run at risk on Thursday.

However, Alvaro Rodriguez and Rayan struck in the second half as Bournemouth became the first Premier League side to win a game – in any competition – after being behind and down a man at half-time since August 2023, when Liverpool beat Newcastle United.

The Cherries have now won 10 straight games since losing 5-1 to Chelsea in early October, extending what was already a club-record winning streak, while they have also won their first five Europa League matches.

Bournemouth are only the third team to ever win their first five matches in major European competitions, after Nuremberg in 1961-62 and Ararat in 1972-73.

They were already one of the favourites to lift the trophy before Thursday's win, with the Opta supercomputer assigning them a 26% chance of doing so.

And Rose is not looking to dampen anyone's expectations, telling TNT Sports: "You're always allowed to dream.

"Now it's about getting in the top eight, to avoid even more games for us, as a Premier League club, and then we can see, step by step.

"Staying humble is good in our situation, and working hard.".
