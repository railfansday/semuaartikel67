Sources indicate that if you've spent any time on food TikTok, you've probably come across Glace's famous S'mores Hot Chocolate. The New York City dessert shop has become a social media sensation thanks to its ridiculously rich hot chocolate, complete with a thick ring of toasted marshmallow fluff around the cup and hefty scoop of whipped cream to top it off. And now you won't have to book a trip to Manhattan and stand in line just to try it.

Glace has officially rolled out its first-ever at-home hot chocolate mix, giving fans across the country the opportunity to recreate its signature drink without leaving their kitchens. And given how popular the in-store sensation has become, I'm guessing plenty of people will add this easy-to-duplicate version to their holiday wish lists.

Founded by Sasha Zabar in 2023, Glace first opened on Upcoming York City's Upper East Side, where its over-the-top S'mores Hot Chocolate swiftly became the star of the menu. The drink, which features rich drinking chocolate surrounded by a toasted marshmallow rim, has attracted plenty of attention online.

Since then, the brand has continued to dominate social media with other over-the-top menu items like its à la mode Warm Brownie Pudding Cake and Matcha Fudge Sundae, and has also expanded with a seasonal holiday truck at Rockefeller Center and Glace Candy, a neighboring shop specializing in gummies, chocolates, and other sweets.

But its newest launch means you don't have to live anywhere near New York to get a taste of at least one of its viral treats.

The upcoming Signature Hot Chocolate Mix costs $19.99 and comes with six individual sachets, each containing the same hot chocolate recipe served at Glace's shops.

Recent findings suggest that according to the brand, the mix is made with ground chocolate and A2 milk powder, without the cornstarch or powdered fillers commonly used in conventional hot chocolate mixes. The result is designed to deliver the same rich, glossy consistency that made the drink famous.

Preparing it is pretty straightforward: Simply whisk the mix over medium-low heat on the stovetop until it transforms into a thick, decadent drinking chocolate. Sadly, the at-home version doesn’t come with the shop’s signature marshmallow rim...at least, not yet.

In a recent development, glace says it's planning to release its signature marshmallow fluff separately, allowing customers to eventually recreate the full experience at home, toasted marshmallow rim and all. The brand hasn't revealed an official release date for the fluff, but we're keeping an eye out.

For now, the hot chocolate mix is available for pre-order on Glace's website, with shipments expected to begin in early December.
