Food, so much food! (Day 166)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Follow Gerjan "Hungry" Hertgers.

In a recent development, it’s resupply day today! We have 10 miles to go to Jo Mary road where Shaw’s will deliver our food drop. We have to be there before 2 pm, so let’s get going!

Industry observers note that we can never pass up a private pond beach though. Even though the view is simply amazing, our attention went to an exploring ladybug. Climbing on top of a fire ring and us creating stick bridges to help her find the grass again. What more do you need for entertainment?

Recent findings suggest that I’ve talked about us rationing food and being hungry more often lately, but today I hit a fresh low. Just before the road crossing I got hit with a crazy dizzy spell and I had to sit down for a second. So this is what Dreamcatcher is usually experiencing, huh.

I’m hallucinating too. I swear I can smell a grill in the distance. My mind is playing tricks on me. Luckily, we hit the road and got our resupply! We can’t start eating like crazy though, since this resupply is just as tight as the last one. But luckily for us, my dad is here too! With sandwiches!! Score! I basically inhaled it before I could say any words. It’s good to see my dad again! But he didn’t look ready to hike. His knee is still bothering him too much. He made the hard decision to definitively get off trail and give up on reaching Katahdin. That’s a hard pill to swallow, for us both to be honest. But it’s the right decision. It’s just not worth it to cause your body irreparable harm for ego. I’m super proud of him. For getting this far, but also for making the hard but smart decision. He got a ride back to Shaw’s and will go to Millinocket soon. We’ll see him there in a couple of days.

Sources indicate that in the meantime we saw a sign: “Hot grill cold drinks across the bridge – Chilly P”. My nose wasn’t fooling me! There’s actually a trail angel in the middle of the 100-mile having a grill going!

According to recent reports, chilly P is awesome. He truly is super chill and grilled us an insane amount of burgers. I had four, on top of the sandwich I already ate. I would be lying if I said I couldn’t eat more. There’s cold drinks, beers, fruit, snacks; basically everything a group of underfed hikers could ever need. It’s simply the best. We spent a good amount of time here, chilling with Chilly P.

It has been revealed that after continuing, we came across a day hiker giving us some snacks. I guess our trail magic meter must have been full again! We’re so grateful.

According to recent reports, at the end of the day, we reached a beautiful tent site on a peninsula on another lake, with mesmerizing skies. What a day.
