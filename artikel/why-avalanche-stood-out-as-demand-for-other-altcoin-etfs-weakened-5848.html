Why Avalanche stood out as demand for other altcoin ETFs weakened

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that avalanche [AVAX] is an outlier among other altcoins when comparing their ETF demand. Last week, it was the only altcoin that recorded positive net inflows among the 15 tracked US spot crypto ETF groups.

On the 13th of November, AVAX ETFs issued by Bitwise, VanEck, and Grayscale recorded net inflows of $227K. This brought the cumulative net inflows to $30.59 million, with the total assets across the ETFs at $46.28 million.

This reading implies that institutions are banking on AVAX to outperform other altcoins.

Despite that, the price of Avalanche crypto was falling. This could be attributed to the fact that the inflows were very small compared to what was recorded in early October. For instance, over $2.52 million in AVAX ETFs were bought on the 8th of October.

Over the past week, the price action has plunged from $11.63 to $8.66 but has pulled back to $9.14.

Sources indicate that the Bollinger Bands were opening up while the altcoin was in a downtrend but have since started to squeeze as AVAX traded above $9. This indicates either bulls or bears are preparing for a move.

The MACD, which is green, highlights that bulls have lost strength, with the size of the bars decreasing and becoming faint.

According to recent reports, therefore, there is a likelihood that the downtrend may be extended, considering the general crypto market is weak. Thus, the consolidation around $9 could be a new lower high (LH) before bears attempt to challenge the previous lower low at $8.59.

Industry observers note that but there is also a possibility that bulls could start a recovery at this point, considering AVAX ETFs were seeing positive net inflows. That would place $10 as the next bullish target.

Meanwhile, other altcoin ETFs were seeing net outflows, including Chainlink [LINK], Polkadot [DOT], Ethereum [ETH], and XRP, among others.

For instance, the Chainlink ETF issued by Grayscale recorded $705.59K in outflows on the 13th of November. These outflows by LINK follow a two-month record of strong inflows between August and October.

Similarly, the DOT ETF, which is issued by 21Shares, saw an outflow of $99.35K, reflecting the weakening demand. The price of DOT has declined below $1 over the past two weeks, at the moment trading at $0.72.

Even so, it remains unclear if AVAX ETF demand can withstand continued market weakness.
