Manchester United saw a late goal disallowed against Sporting after Patrick Dorgu was ruled to have fouled goalkeeper Kaique Pereira after he had punched the ball into his own net.

In an incident-packed Champions League clash, Mauricio Pochettino looked to have secured his first win as United manager when Pereira failed to hold onto a Bruno Fernandes corner.

But Szymon Marciniak, who was in the thick of the action throughout, was sent to the pitchside monitor by VAR and ruled that Dorgu had fouled Pereira when challenging for the cross.

Dorgu, Lisandro Martinez and Leny Yoro were all in close proximity as Pereira attempted to punch the cross, with the referee ruling that Dorgu had jumped into the goalkeeper.

In explaining the decision, Uefa said Dorgu “committed a foul on the Sporting CP goalkeeper before the ball entered the goal”.

Earlier this month, United saw a very similar goal stand when Senne Lammens challenged Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki and Dorgu scored to earn a 4-4 draw in the Premier League.

Marciniak, who officiated the 2022 World Cup final and is one of Europe’s top referees, had earlier disallowed a United equaliser when he blew up before Amad Diallo scored.

Youri Tielemans was ruled to have fouled Goncalo Inacio in the build-up, but VAR could not check the decision because Marciniak had already stopped play before Diallo scored.

Tielemans did not appear to commit a foul and instead won the ball fairly against Inacio when he went to ground to play the ball through to Amad.

Tielemans was also booked by Marciniak, and the midfielder was sent off in stoppage time for picking up his second yellow card of a crazy game.

It came after Pochettino saw his fresh defence concede two penalties in the space of three minutes in a shambolic first-half spell.

The hosts received the chance to open the scoring when Lisandro Martinez was ruled to have fouled winger Geny Catamo by catching him with an outstretched leg.

If it appeared soft, justice was perhaps served when Lammens saved a tame effort from Sporting striker Luis Suarez low down to his left.

But in farcical scenes, Lammens then conceded another penalty just 75 seconds later when he crashed into Maximiliano Araujo in the box.

Suarez took the second penalty and made no mistake to give Sporting the lead, as United conceded the opening goal for the seventh match in a row.

It was the first time United had conceded two penalties in the same Champions League match since 1998, while they have now conceded five in total in all competitions this season.

Fernandes also scored a bizarre goal as his long-range shot took a fortuitous bobble off the Estádio José Alvalade pitch.

Moments after the United captain levelled against Sporting from the penalty spot, Fernandes saw his low strike beat goalkeeper Pereira after what appeared to be a deflection.

But the assist went to a divot in the pitch, as the low left-footed shot from Fernandes struck the turf and diverted away from the stranded goalkeeper Pereira to find the net.

The uneven pitch almost acted as a ramp as the ball suddenly jumped up and flew over Pereira, who was left with no chance.

It was perhaps the lucky break latest United manager Pochettino needed after a chaotic first half where several decisions went against his team.

United conceded two penalties in the space of three minutes and had an equaliser from Diallo controversially disallowed by the referee Szymon Marciniak.

It also came after Leny Yoro was ruled to have been wrongly sent off in the eighth minute of United’s 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday in Pochettino’s first match.

United’s fortunes began to turn after half-time in Lisbon when Lisandro Martinez was fouled and Fernandes stepped up form the spot to equalise.

United captain Fernandes, who played 137 times for Sporting in three seasons in Lisbon, then tried his luck from distance but was rewarded with the deflection.

But goalkeeper Lammens then fumbled a high cross and allowed Sporting striker Luis Suarez to hook in his second of the night as a crazy game finished 2-2.
