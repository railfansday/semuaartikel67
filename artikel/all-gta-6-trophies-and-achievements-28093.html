It has been revealed that the full GTA 6 Trophy and Achievement list has been revealed, and there is plenty to keep you busy during your time in Leonida. There are a total of 55 virtual awards to unlock, and while some of them will be earned naturally by progressing through the story or carrying out certain activities, others will take a significant time investment to tick off – including the return of an award for attaining 100% completion of the game!

Naturally, there may be some mild spoilers in the names and descriptions of certain Secret Trophies and Hidden Achievements relating to story progress through GTA 6 and other activities you can take part in, but there's nothing too detailed or revealing in there to worry about. To find out more, here's the complete list of Achievements and Trophies in GTA 6.

You'll be working through plenty of GTA 6 side activities while progressing towards these awards, as well as tracking down various GTA 6 cars.

Below you'll find the complete GTA 6 Trophy and Achievement list, showing all of the virtual awards you can earn alongside their Trophy and Gamerscore value. Those marked in italics are Hidden Trophies or Secret Achievements, and if there are a specific number of things you need to do to unlock an award which isn't clear from the description, I've added the total in square brackets after:.
