Recent findings suggest that kevin Parker’s psychedelic pop outfit snagged the biggest honors on the night, including album of the year for Tame Impala’s fifth studio album, Deadbeat; best solo artist; and song of the year for “Dracula.” On winning solo artist, the Western Australian singer, songwriter and multi-instrumentalist used the moment to unfurl the joke that was on everyone’s mind. ARIA has now put the argument to bed that, yes, Tame Impala is a one-man band. Actually, it’s not so clear-cut. Tame Impala has, in fact won for best group at the ARIAs on three occasions, in 2013, 2015, and 2020. Industry observers note that by snagging album of the year for Deadbeat, a record that went to No. 2 on the ARIA Albums Chart in 2025, but missed out on the crown and got a lukewarm review from some armchair critics, Parker got the last laugh. “I’m so grateful,” he remarked. “There are so many great f—ing Australian artists and so many great nominees.” Deadbeat “got off to a rocky start, to be honest…but I’m so proud of it. It’s getting the respect that it deserves, I think.”.

As previously reported, Tame Impala collected the second annual Global Impact Award presented by Spotify. Savage Garden’s Darren Hayes was on hand to present the honor. “I’m so proud to be an Aussie artist, always,” Parker remarked. “Whenever I go overseas, I feel like I wear a badge on my shirt.” And, he noted, he’s “one of the lucky ones,” an artist who’s had an international career and just this week collected four Grammy Award nominations.

According to recent reports, another artist enjoying a global career is Ecca Vandal. As the Glastonbury Festival 2027 lineup dropped with her name on it, the Australian punk rock artist was converting four of her seven ARIA Award nominations, including the Michael Gudinski breakthrough artist, best independent release and best alternative release, in respect to her sophomore album Looking For People To Unfollow. “Not sure what breakthrough means,” she mentioned after snagging the award named after Michael Gudinski, the late, great Mushroom Group founder, but “the biggest breakthrough for me was realizing I didn’t need to be anybody but myself.” The rising star also gave a shout out to those people who said “no” along the way, a burn which forced her to “dig deeper.” The naysayers, “you did me a really major favor.”.

After collecting their first-ever ARIA No. 1 in August with their self-titled fifth album, DMA’S won a brace of ARIA Awards, including best group, while G Flip was also a two-time winner on the night (best video and best Australian live act).

K-pop superstars BTS won for most popular international artist, beating the likes of Taylor Swift, Olivia Rodrigo and Olivia Dean. Other winners included Bluey (best children’s album), Ninajirachi (Porter Robinson, for best dance/electronic release), Donny Benét (best jazz album), Emma Donovan (best blues & roots album) and Karnivool (best hard rock/heavy metal album).

According to recent reports, if music was the glue that connected the night, artificial intelligence — or rather, the threat of it to the creative community — was the theme. On each industry table, reminder of the “Not AI’s to Take” campaign, while presenters including Spiderbait’s Kram, Tim Minchin, Tash Sultana and Elton John shared their thoughts on the matter. Sultana, the singer, songwriter and guitarist, offered a poem as she presented the independent category: “roses are red, violets are blue, if you support AI music, f— you.”.

In a recent development, ARIA doesn’t support it. Earlier this year, the trade body announced it would ban from its official charts those recordings that featured mostly AI-generated music.

Performers at the 2026 ARIA Awards included Genesis Owusu; Blusher; Ecca Vandal; 3%, Scribe and Fred Leone; BOY SODA; and 2026 ARIA Hall of Fame inductees Spiderbait, which closed out the show with a medley of their hits including “Calypso,” “Buy Me A Pony” and “Black Betty.”.

Among record labels, it was an especially solid outing for Sony Music which had its fingerprints on 18 of the 30 awards.

Industry observers note that “What a night,” remarks ARIA CEO Annabelle Herd. “I am absolutely in awe of the incredible power of human artistry and Australian music. Tame Impala capping a sensational year on the world stage by sweeping the night on home soil, Ecca Vandal making her ARIA performance debut and walking away with four awards… then Sir Elton! I couldn’t be prouder to be the CEO of ARIA. Congratulations to all of our winners and a huge thank you to every artist who performed, you blew us away.”.

It has been revealed that the ARIAs, the Australian recorded music industry’s flagship awards ceremony, is supported by Destination NSW, in partnership with Spotify, and beamed out live on Paramount+ and broadcast on 10. Performances and moments will be available on the @ARIA.official YouTube channel.
