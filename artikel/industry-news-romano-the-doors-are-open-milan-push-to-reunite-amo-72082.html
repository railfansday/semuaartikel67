Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that AC Milan are maintaining contacts with Goncalo Inacio’s entourage over a January move.

In a recent development, when Ruben Amorim arrived in the summer as the new head coach, Inacio was linked almost immediately. The pair know each other well as Amorim brought him into the side while at Sporting CP, making him a mainstay of the three-man defence.

Heading into January, Milan need reinforcements and especially at the back. Matteo Gabbia and Fikayo Tomori’s injuries have depleted the depth, which has led to Sankhoun Diawara being a starter perhaps a bit earlier than planned.

Sources indicate that earlier this week, Record in Portugal stated that Sporting are willing to part with Inacio in the winter window but only for the right price, which could be north of €40m. Still, it marks a step forward from the summer, when a late attempt failed.

In a video on his YouTube channel, Fabrizio Romano gave an patch on Milan’s pursuit of a fresh central defender and he confirmed that Inacio seems to be the man in pole position.

Recent findings suggest that “Let’s keep Gonçalo Inacio’s name there. Confirmation has also come from Portugal about what we’ve been telling you, that Inacio could be a key player in the January transfer window,” he said.

According to recent reports, “I’m reiterating what I’ve been telling you for months, actually, about the relationships, because agent Miguel Pino is the same as Ruben Amorim’s agent. They’re all Portuguese, Amorim coached Inacio at Sporting. Arriving in January isn’t easy, though: it means being ready to be thrown into the fire.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s not always easy. Signing Gonçalo Inacio would mean Amorim signing a player he knows, with whom he speaks the same language, not just verbally but also on the pitch. There’s a different feeling, he wouldn’t have to explain anything to the boy.

“Milan are keeping in touch with the player, his departure in January is possible, the doors are open if necessary. Then we need to understand the conditions, the prices, it’s clear that Sporting isn’t just interested in giving away a player like that for any price.

“They’ll have to negotiate, and last summer, Milan – amidst the ups and downs of this negotiation – at a certain point hoped to be able to secure a deal that could include a very expensive loan with an option to buy, to make everything easier from a financial standpoint. Milan have several names on the list, but Inacio’s is definitely one to consider.”.
