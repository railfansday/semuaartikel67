'Very insecure' - Emile Heskey reveals Leicester City dressing-room truth about Robbie Savage

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Peterborough United appointed Robbie Savage as their new manager on October 2nd, having been impressed by the work he'd done with Forest Green Rovers. His ex-Leicester City teammate, Emile Heskey, has now offered a peak behind the curtain to reveal exactly what the Posh boss is like.

Savage is still juggling his life as a pundit and media personality, alongside the full-time gig with Peterborough in League One.

Naturally, there's been some opposition to that arrangement, and a run of seven games in all competitions without a win heading into this weekend isn't exactly helping his cause.

Ironically, Savage's first game as Peterborough boss came against Leicester on October 10th, with his side racing into a 3-0 lead before seeing the game out to win 3-1, giving the former Fox the ideal start to life in the dugout.

After coming through at Man United and cutting his teeth with Crewe, it had been at Leicester where Savage had made a name for himself in the Premier League.

Heskey has been appearing on the 'Jamie Vardy's Having A Party' podcast, with the two former Leicester strikers trading stories from their time at the club.

In one part, Heskey is quizzed on any 'funny stories' he might have from his time at Filbert Street, where he responds to talk about Savage turning up in a purple Porsche, which Vardy finds easy to believe.

Industry observers note that vardy says: "Stuff like that brings the team together. He’s set himself up, but it’s good for the group.".

Heskey totally agrees, saying: "He was really good in taking it (the banter he got)," before revealing to a surprised Vardy: "He actually is quite sensitive and very insecure is Robbie.

Recent findings suggest that "He’s one of those players where, before a game, we’d have to say, ‘We need you, Robbie, come on. You’re the best’.

Recent findings suggest that "He’d be nervous and like he didn’t want to play, pulling out of games. But this is this happy-go-lucky guy that you see all bubbly and that, but he is quite insecure.".

Heskey played with Savage at Leicester, before also linking up with him at Birmingham City later in the pair's careers.

Sources indicate that as Heskey reveals, Savage was good at taking things on the chin when it came to the banter he got in the changing rooms. He was also pretty good at doing the same thing from rival fans as a player.

Savage finished his Leicester career with 204 appearances for the Foxes, along with 137 at Derby County, 100 at Blackburn Rovers and a further 88 for Birmingham. 346 of his career outings came in the Premier League, and you can tell by the tone of Heskey's voice that he picked up a fair bit of admiration for that.

Moving into coaching isn't easy, but Savage has had stints with Macclesfield and Forest Green, cutting his teeth at the bottom of the game to earn this chance at Peterborough.

It's a slightly different persona he will have to give off as a manager compared to a player, but every inch of his experience will be key to turning things around for Posh.
