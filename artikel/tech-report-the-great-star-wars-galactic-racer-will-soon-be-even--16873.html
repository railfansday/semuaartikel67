While Star Wars: Galactic Racer is an excellent arcade racing game in many respects, it did launch with a few missing features that left some speed freaks a little disappointed.

However, it seems developer Fuse Games is keen to address those concerns, as it's just unveiled a couple of much-requested modes are officially in the works.

Declared via social posts, Galactic Racer is getting local split-screen multiplayer as well as Free Play mode:.

Fuse Games claims these new features are planned to arrive in "early 2027" and are currently being worked on within a "dedicated team.".

As the work-in-progress screenshot indicates, Galactic Racer will support four-player local multiplayer; there's no footage yet, so it's unknown how that's going to perform, but hopefully it can uphold the game's silky smooth frame rate.

Free Play, meanwhile, will be the home for split-screen, as well as other race types. "This new mode will allow you to choose your vehicle, character, planet, and race track to race alone or with up to 3 other players in local split-screen action," the post reads.

I'm really glad the studio is implementing these features in the newly introduced year. While Galactic Racer has plenty to enjoy already, I think the ability to make your own custom races is a logical next step.

To be honest, I wasn't expecting any split-screen support at all, but I'm pleased to be proven wrong there.
