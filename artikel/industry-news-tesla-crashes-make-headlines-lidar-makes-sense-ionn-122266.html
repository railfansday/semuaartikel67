Go back the several years that Musk told us about the limitations of LiDAR. Tesla was having it's own problems with Cameras and RADAR at the time. Musk objected to LiDAR, pointing out its limitations. At the time it appeared to make sense. LiDAR was expensive and the performance was at a certain level. But the occasionally visionary Elon wasn't particularly visionary on that occasion or every time he repeated his stance. Truth is, Cameras were cheaper, and computer performance wasn't wasn't going to get there if all the added complexity was loading it up. So Tesla ditched RADAR and only briefly considered LiDAR. I could say that Musk never considered it at all, but then he truly would have been a fool to base an opinion on nothing but his untested perception. But LiDAR got better and it got a lot, lot cheaper, and anyway, Tesla's HW3 wasn't going to cut it either. HW4 looks like it may have a tough time for 99.999% safety. Thing is, eyesight can't deal with every single situation, and neither can cameras. So if you want to get as close to 100% safety as possible, these days it seems a bit silly to leave aside LiDAR. But Elon has dug his heels in, and won't be going back on that decision from years ago. Maybe FSD might have got there quicker with LiDAR. Maybe not. And what of HW3.?
