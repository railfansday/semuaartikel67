Grand Theft Auto 6 will feature a playable female character for the first time since the 2D games.

Grand Theft Auto fans have waited 13 long years for the latest edition of the game to be released - and going to extreme lengths to be ready for its launch.

GTA 6 will be released at midnight on Thursday - and Victoria Evans, from West Sussex, is not leaving anything to chance.

Sources indicate that she has her alarm set for 05:00 GMT, and has a day off booked. She notes she put the game's release on her leave request form which was approved, she told BBC News, despite the fact her boss "doesn't know what GTA 6 is".

In a recent development, she's far from the only one. For the game's launch day, HR software company BrightHR, external looked at absence data from more than 1.2 million employees from 70,000 businesses across the UK.

Recent findings suggest that it found GTA was mentioned in 4.6% of time-off requests by employees. Throughout November and December, it noted an estimated 83,000 days had been booked off for the game.

According to recent reports, and booking leave is only one of the ways fans are making sure for the big day. Others are buying a new console to play it on or even pretending they're halfway around the world to get early access.

Market intelligence firm Sensor Tower estimates, external GTA 6 has already generated $1.18bn (Â£894m) from 12 million pre-orders - although Rockstar has not confirmed this.

Despite the option to preload the 212GB game, Jennifer Holmes, chief executive of the London Internet Exchange, remarked several UK internet providers including BT and Virgin Media had "increased capacity in anticipation of higher demand" ahead of launch.

GTA 6 - all you need to know about Rockstar's blockbuster game.

Gamers have long been known to book time off work for major releases.

When Red Dead Redemption 2 introduced in October 2018, social media people were sharing plans to take the day off, with one analytics firm reportedly finding, external more people talking about calling in sick for it than any other game that year.

Similar conversations took place ahead of Cyberpunk 2077's release in December 2020, with fans discussing, external taking several days off to play.

GTA 6 is set in a fictional state inspired by Florida, which has featured heavily in the game's marketing, including this promotional blimp.

Some places have decided to lean into the trend for GTA 6 by offering incentives around the game - including the US Army's 3rd Infantry Division based at Fort Stewart, Georgia.

It gave some active-duty soldiers a four-day pass to play the game when it is released in November, as long as they sign a reenlistment contract for a minimum of two years between 1 August and 12 November.

"The idea was to have a unique incentives programme that connects to what soldiers are interested in," public affairs officer for the division, Lieutenant Colonel Angel Tomko confirmed to the BBC in August.

"There are 130 soldiers eligible within the battalion, and 20 have selected this incentive as part of their reenlistment," she stated at the time.

Aside from taking time off, some gamers have also been discussing a technical loophole which could give them valuable extra hours of play before it officially unlocks in their country.

Because GTA 6 will release at midnight local time in all regions, New Zealand will become one of the first places where it is available, due to it reaching midnight earliest because of its position near the international date line.

This has led to some investigating ways to "trick" their consoles into thinking they're based in the island country when they are in fact thousands of miles away.

According to Google Trends, searches for the "GTA 6 Newly introduced Zealand trick" have surged by more than 5,000% in the UK over the past month.

Games journalist Vic Hood stated many gamers would be looking to play earlier to "get in on the action as soon as possible", as well as to prevent spoilers.

"As Newly introduced Zealand is 13 hours ahead of the UK, that's 13 hours of gameplay that has time to spread across the internet and potentially spoil some key story beats and in-game surprises," she said.

"Took an extra day of paid time off when I found this out," one fan posted on Reddit on the trick.

It also means gamers would be playing the game at the much nicer time of 11:00 GMT, rather than having to stay up all night.

But another Xbox user shared caution on Reddit, noting that while the loophole had been exploited for many years for other game releases, GTA 6 "might be the game where Microsoft finally does something about this 'exploit'" - which could include banning players.

GTA 6's developers Rockstar and publishers Take Two Interactive have not commented on the loophole, and neither have Sony nor Microsoft.

Louise Wooldridge from Ampere Analysis remarked both Microsoft and Sony had "rules governing regional accounts and purchases" for the Xbox and PlayStation, adding that Sony's were "slightly more restrictive".

Players who choose to use the workaround could therefore risk breaching those terms and conditions and potentially face restrictions on their accounts or lose access to their digital games.

The hype for the game has taken over locations across the world, including Selfridges in London.

Some gamers will have to wait even longer than their console-playing counterparts to get their hands on GTA 6, as the game will not be available on PC at launch, similar to previous GTA releases.

"Spare a thought for the PC users who will have to live vicariously through others' YouTube videos and Twitch streams," gamer Stephen Finn told the BBC.
