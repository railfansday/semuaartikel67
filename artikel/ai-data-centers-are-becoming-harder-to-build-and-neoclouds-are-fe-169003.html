📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that ⁠Nvidia has made clear⁠ it won’t backstop an AI data center until it’s nearly ready to run, and that’s landing just as neoclouds report construction delays and more expensive debt. The message to developers is that land, power, and a finished building matter more than a pitch deck.

⁠Anita Verma-Lallian⁠ has been selling exactly that. Her organization, Arizona Land Consulting, assembles data center sites for developers, hyperscalers, AI labs, and neoclouds, giving her a view into who’s buying, what makes a site viable, and where projects are running into trouble. She also partners with investor ⁠Chamath Palihapitiya,⁠ who has been predicting an industry shakeout.

On this episode of TechCrunch’s ⁠Equity⁠ podcast, Rebecca Bellan talks with Verma-Lallian about who bears the risk when Nvidia gets pickier, why speculative projects could be the first to fall apart, and what a 2027 shakeout might look like.
