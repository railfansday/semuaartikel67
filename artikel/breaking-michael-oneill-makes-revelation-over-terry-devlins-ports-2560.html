Breaking: Michael O’Neill makes revelation over Terry Devlin’s Portsmouth future which will leave Championship rivals ‘disappointed’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Portsmouth have enjoyed a fine season to date, and versatile star Terry Devlin has been a standout player for John Mousinho’s side. Mainly playing at right-back, the 23-year-old has chipped in with four goals in 14 Championship games, including a brace in the 3-1 win over Swansea City at the start of November.

It has been revealed that devlin’s progression has brought interest from elsewhere, but Pompey have been working to secure his future at Fratton Park. Now, Northern Ireland boss Michael O’Neill has indicated that a positive conclusion is close. Speaking on the player’s situation with BBC Sport over the international break, he mentioned: “He’s a very valuable player for Portsmouth.

“I think he’s in the process of signing a new contract there, and I imagine a lot of teams in the Championship will be disappointed by that.”.

Devlin last extended his contract in February 2025 and he is tied down until 2028. He is in his fourth season with the South Coast outfit, having joined from Glentoran in the summer of 2023.

Tying Devlin down to a new deal marks a key move from Portsmouth. It secures his future before he enters the final 18 months of his current deal, but it also serves as a statement of intent to lock down one of their standout players and most valuable assets.

Industry observers note that the 23-year-old has ups and downs over the course of his Pompey career but as a true utility player, he is a hugely valuable figure in Mousinho’s squad. His athleticism, attitude and well-rounded skillset means he can be trusted to deliver all over the pitch.

Furthermore, the deal will allow Devlin to stay focused on his own game. It will quieten noise over his future and a potential move elsewhere as ambitious sides like Wrexham have previously looked to prise him away from Fratton Park.

At 23, Devlin has time to grow on and off the pitch. On his current trajectory, his value will only grow, so Pompey will be glad to have him tied down to a secure and longer contract.

Sources indicate that as touched on before, Devlin has been the subject of interest from other clubs previously. Those admirers will have been keeping a close eye on his situation with 18 months left on his deal.

However, on a new long-term deal, Portsmouth will be in a better place to stand firm on their valuation. If someone wants to buy him, they will have to pay a handsome fee to do so.

It doesn’t rule out interest or bids, especially if he keeps progressing as he has done, but Devlin’s future at Portsmouth will be far more certain if a newly introduced long-term contract is in place.
