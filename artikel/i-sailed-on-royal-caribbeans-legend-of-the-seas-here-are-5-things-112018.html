I sailed on Royal Caribbean's Legend of the Seas. Here are 5 things that surprised me

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (189 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Legend of the Seas, one of the world's largest cruise ships, offers a wide range of dining, entertainment and relaxation options that can fill an entire vacation without leaving the ship.
  • Despite its capacity for thousands of passengers, the ship rarely felt crowded, with ample lounge chairs, manageable lines and easy navigation throughout public spaces.
  • Entertainment exceeded expectations, featuring high-caliber productions like acrobatic shows, ice skating performances and immersive experiences such as escape rooms.
  • Dining options included both casual and upscale venues, with standout experiences like an eight-course tasting menu at a specialty restaurant, while cabins were more spacious and comfortable than anticipated.
  • Popular events and pool areas can become busy, so planning ahead is recommended for specific activities or performances.
  • The ship's layout, clear signage and helpful crew make it easy to navigate, even for first-time cruisers.
  • Complimentary trams and strong Wi-Fi enhance the experience at the private island destination, making it simple to explore and plan activities.
