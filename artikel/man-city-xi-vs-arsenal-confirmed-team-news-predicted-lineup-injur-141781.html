Manchester City head to Arsenal this weekend in desperate need of a morale-boosting victory.

Enzo Maresca’s side have picked up just one point from the last 12 available in the Premier League, with a disappointing home draw with Fulham following successive defeats at the hands of Aston Villa, Brighton and Nottingham Forest.

Sitting third and already six points behind leaders Arsenal after 11 games, many pundits predict that City’s title chances will effectively be over if they lose in north London on Sunday.

It has been revealed that city are also still waiting for their appeal to be heard against the guilty verdict returned by an independent panel back in September on their charges of breaching the Premier League’s financial rules across a nine-season period between 2009/10 and 2017/18. They deny any wrongdoing.

Head coach Enzo Maresca has to find the right team to beat an Arsenal side that have lost just twice at home in 2026.

Gianluigi Donnarumma will start in goal as usual, with the back four in front of him likely consisting of Matheus Nunes, Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi and Josko Gvardiol.

Dias was rested for the home draw with Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday, with the City captain set to be restored to the starting XI here.

Nico O’Reilly has emerged as the first-choice option at left-back in recent weeks, but the defensive ability of Gvardiol could be key in a cagey match.

Maresca has experimented with the construction of his midfield in the last few matches. Enzo Fernandez was deployed as a No10 against Napoli, but a return to the preferred paring of the Argentine and Elliot Anderson at the base of City’s midfield feels likely at Arsenal.

Recent findings suggest that similarly, Iliman Ndiaye has impressed on the right wing in recent weeks. Rayan Cherki was deployed out wide against Napoli, but has been most effective as a No10.

Jeremy Doku has become trusted on the left, while Erling Haaland cannot be left out - even if he is enduring a goalscoring drought by his usual lofty standards.

Predicted Man City XI vs Arsenal (4-2-3-1): Donnarumma; Nunes, Dias, Guehi, Gvardiol; Fernandez, Anderson; Ndiaye, Cherki, Doku; Haaland.
