Whether or not Scott Parker has yet made up his mind about replacing his goalkeeper ahead of Saturday’s resumption of the Championship battle, the highlights reel from last night’s international tie between Denmark and Norway will prove compelling watching for West Ham’s new manager.

Quite possibly, if Parker hasn’t yet decided on a switch, seeing the latest error from his number one ‘keeper may well the tip the scales towards change: Hermansen dwelling too long on the ball and being dispossessed by Martin Odegaard after attempting a step-over inside his own ‘box turned out to be the pivotal moment in the game after Denmark had raced into a two goal lead against their rivals.

Hermansen’s howler proved to be the catalyst for a Norway recovery, scoring three and winning both the game and qualification for the last eight of the UEFA Nations League at the expense of the Danes, finishing second in the group thanks to their superior head-to-head record.

All too familiar expression of Hermansen’s anguish: the Dane needs a period of ‘reflection’ away from the firing line.

An expensive error indeed and one which cannot have escaped the attention of the coaching staff at Rush Green.

Industry observers note that it was the last thing Hermansen needed after his confidence-building performance against Portugal of three nights earlier, when the Dane completed seven saves to keep out his opponents – as West Ham’s web site gleefully told us. I’ll bet they’re silent about his blunder this morning.

The pressure for change between the sticks will only increase now and Areola – or Fin Herrick, frankly –  had better be fit and ready to roll on Saturday afternoon.
