Rangers travel to Motherwell on Saturday night as the Scottish Premiership resumes following the international break.

The Ibrox side will look to continue where they left off after embarking on a 12-match unbeaten run featuring 10 victories stretching back until August.

The Light Blues claimed a 2-0 win at St Mirren in their most recent fixture and also reached the Premier Sports Cup final with a win over Aberdeen in the Hampden semi-final.

Derek McInnes’ side could be seven points behind Celtic at the top of the table by the time the game starts, but will have two games in hand.

Motherwell recovered from a six-match losing streak to go into the break three games unbeaten thanks to away draws at St Johnstone and Dundee United either side of a home victory over St Mirren.

Industry observers note that the Steelmen finished fourth last season under Jens Berthel Askou, who left for Toulouse in the summer. but are currently 10th in the table with 13 points from 13 matches as they strive for consistent form under his successor Alfred Johanssen.

Sources indicate that the Scottish Premiership match between Motherwell and Rangers takes place at Fir Park, Motherwell on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Kick-off is 8pm.

The match will be broadcast live on Sky Sports Football with coverage starting at 7.30pm. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene on BBC One Scotland at 10.20pm.

Motherwell will assess Alex Lowry as he pushes to return from a knee injury, but Dylan Williams (hamstring) and Johnny Koutroumbis (muscle) remain out.

Ross McCrorie is back in contention for Rangers, while Daisuke Yokota will return to full training next week. Cammy Devlin and Lawrence Shankland are back running after hamstring injuries, while Youssef Chermiti (knee) remains out for the long term.

Don Robertson has been appointed as the match referee. He will be joined on the pitch by assistant referees Calum Spence and Scott Anderson, with Greg Soutar serving as the fourth official. Overseeing the video assistant referee duties from Clydesdale House is Chris Graham as the VAR, alongside Dan McFarlane as the Assistant VAR.
