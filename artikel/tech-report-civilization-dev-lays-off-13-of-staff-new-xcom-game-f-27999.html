Tech Report: Civilization Dev Lays Off 1/3 Of Staff, New XCOM Game Facing “Development Struggles” – Report

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The video game industry's woes continue as Take-Two's 2K division has confirmed that one third of the staff at Civilization and XCOM developer Firaxis Games is being laid off. Bloomberg confirmed the cuts, which will impact around 60 people. As part of the downsizing, Firaxis studio head Heather Hazen is leaving. A spokesperson for Firaxis confirmed the restructuring to Bloomberg, saying the cuts are part of an effort to "focus on design, technology, and player experience." "These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis," the spokesperson said. According to Bloomberg's sources, Firaxis is working on a fresh XCOM game, but it has faced "development struggles" and missed a major milestone this past summer. The game is still going ahead, though the sources said it "may face a shakeup" due to the layoffs. The latest game from Firaxis, Sid Meier's Civilization VII, was released in February 2025 and had numerous technical challenges upon release, though the experience has been improved since then with updates. The entire Civilization series has sold more than 80 million units. As for the XCOM series, the latest entry from Firaxis was 2020's XCOM: Chimera Squad, and before that was 2016's XCOM 2. Firaxis was co-founded by Sid Meier in 1996, so 2026 is the studio's 30th anniversary. Take-Two bought the organization in 2005 and put the studio and the franchise under the 2K banner.
