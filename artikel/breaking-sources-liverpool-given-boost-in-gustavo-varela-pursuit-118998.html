Liverpool are well placed to "tempt" Monza into selling star striker Gustavo Varela next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The Reds are keeping tabs on the 21-year-old at the moment following his electric start to the season in Serie A.

Monza have the Portuguese tied down to a long-term contract until the summer of 2030, and are not prepared to sell him mid-season.

Sources say that they could entertain a sale next summer, but are likely to hike Varela's price tag up further if he continues scoring goals.

The youngster already has 11 goals to his name from just 11 league games so far this term, despite it being his first taste of Italian football.

That form has prompted Liverpool to sit up and take notice, and they may potentially swoop in for him next summer.

Monza have enjoyed a bright start to the season upon their return to Serie A, and Varela's form in front of goal has been a big part of that.

The starlet has promptly established himself as one of the hottest young prospects in Italian football, despite only joining from Gil Vicente in the summer.

Varela is currently second in the Serie A scoring charts, with only Donyell Malen outperforming him in front of goal at the moment.

That means he has netted more goals this season than Inter Milan sensation Francesco Pio Esposito, while his current tally is over double that of Lautaro Martinez.

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Liverpool view Varela as more of a long-term target right now, but they could be forced into action next summer if his form.

However, that could see them pay a much more lofty fee for the Monza ace, with his club having clear security over his future.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness recently told Football Insider that he expects Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen to join Real Madrid ahead of Liverpool next summer.

The 24-year-old is out of contract in 2028, and Wyness expects the Seagulls to cash in on him at a lofty fee next summer.

The boardroom expert outlined that Brighton will turn it into a straight auction for Verbruggen, and he expects Madrid to win out in that situation.

Liverpool view the Dutchman as an ideal long-term replacement for Alisson, but they may ultimately have to look elsewhere.
