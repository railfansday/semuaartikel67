Travelers now pass through roughly 65 touchpoints before booking a trip, up from 45 in 2018, according to Skift Research and McKinsey. Travel brands own only a handful of them. Even within those, sales, booking, loyalty, and service run on separate systems, so the traveler’s history rarely follows them from one to the next.

Recent findings suggest that that gap is getting more expensive. Personal AI assistants remember what travelers prefer, and they increasingly shape where trips begin. Agentic AI gives travel brands a way to compete: their own agents, carrying a traveler’s context from first search to the moment a trip is disrupted. Phil Wang and Ronen Kadosh of ElevenLabs join Skift to break down how customer experience leaders can build a relationship that remembers the traveler.
