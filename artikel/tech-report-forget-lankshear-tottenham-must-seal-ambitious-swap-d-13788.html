Tottenham Hotspur signed 10 newly introduced players in the summer transfer window as they spent more than £300m to back Roberto De Zerbi in the market, but they still remain in the bottom half of the Premier League table after 10 matches.

That is ultimately an improvement for Tottenham when you consider that they finished 17th in the past two top flight campaigns, including only escaping relegation to the Championship on the final day of last season.

However, more is expected from a Spurs squad that has plenty of talent in it, and is starting to welcome back the majority of their injured players with the likes of James Maddison and Dejan Kulusevski now back and available.

Despite the summer spend, there is a feeling that the north London outfit are still lacking in the centre-forward position, with that being one of the positions that the club didn't significantly strengthen in the summer.

De Zerbi had Richarlison, Dominic Solanke and Will Lankshear available to him going into the summer window, but the former informed the Italian coach that he wanted to leave and fell out of favour, while Solanke is struggling to find the goalscoring form that would be expected from a £60m player.

Tottenham also decided to cash in on Lankshear after Championship side Middlesbrough arrived with an offer worth £10m, plus £10m if they win promotion to the Premier League, and it might be a decision they already have some regrets about, despite the money brought in for an academy graduate who only made six first-team appearances.

According to the Sun, Spurs are already keeping an eye on Lankshear's performances for Boro this term, with the player beginning the campaign by scoring 15 goals in 16 games in all competitions for the Teesside outfit.

The Championship high-flyers are remarked to be ready to demand £70m already if any Premier League club comes calling in January, as they look to secure promotion to the Premier League.

While Lankshear has the attributes to become a top Premier League striker, Tottenham should forget about him and move for Junior Kroupi instead.

De Zerbi has used Omar Marmoush in the centre-forward role this season at times when Dominic Solanke hasn't started, although it is another experiment that has so far failed to work out with the Egyptian yet to show that clinical instinct in front of goal.

TEAMtalk reported earlier this month that Spurs are keen on France international Junior Kroupi, and are even ready to offer players to Bournemouth in exchange in order to get a head-start on their Premier League rivals for a deal.

There is a common feeling that the 20-year-old may have been on the move in the summer had he not suffered an injury, and he has only just returned from that problem, scoring his first goal for his nation during the current international break.

It is the latest milestone in a rapid rise for the forward, who was in stunning form in his first season in English football last term, scoring the most goals in a season for any teenager in Premier League history.

Junior Kroupi has a huge future ahead of him if he continues his meteoric rise, and it would be no surprise if he becomes the next big-money transfer away from Bournemouth in the not-too-distant future.

The 20-year-old has already been labelled as 'generational' and 'world-class' by teammate Marcus Tavernier, and the fact he is already playing under Zinedine Zidane for the senior France side says it all.

Spurs are said to be willing to offer the likes of Mikey Moore, Luca Williams-Barnett and Wilson Odobert to the Cherries in order to try and agree a deal, and it would be a genius move based on what we've seen from Junior Kroupi since he landed in English football.

The Times reported in the summer that Bournemouth valued him at least £100m, and it's difficult to see them accepting less if he returns from this injury and starts scoring goals at the regularity that he did last term.
