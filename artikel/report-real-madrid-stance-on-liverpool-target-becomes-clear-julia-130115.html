Liverpool have already been linked with a lot of players this season.

The Anfield outfit seem to have many different areas of their first-team squad that need upgrading despite them spending well obver £600 million on new players across the last couple of years.

These transfers were expected to set the Reds up for a while but, in reality, they just highlighted other problems that Andoni Iraola has among his ranks.

They're presently fourth in the Premier League table which, while a decent result, is far away from where they need to be.

With players such as Alexander Isak, Bradley Barcola and Florian Wirtz among their ranks, they should be one of the best teams in the world but, instead, they just about scraping qualification for the Champions League.

This needs to change and, with the January transfer window now just around the corner, it could be about to.

Liverpool are 'keeping a close eye' on Nico Paz.

Over the last couple of years, Liverpool's attack has been their main focus of interest in the transfer market.

Mohamed Salah, Sadio Mane and Roberto Firmino have all left now and, with Iraola at the helm, a upcoming style of football has also made its way to Anfield this term.

His heavy-metal style of play has some similarities to Jurgen Klopp's and, after two season of Arne Slot, it was a welcome re-jig.

This summer alone, over £150 million was spent with the Reds' attack taking most of the money.

But, that hasn't stopped Liverpool from being linked with even more attackers.

In fact, a fresh update from journalist Ben Fernandes Santos has just shared that the Anfield outfit are one of the many clubs who are keeping a 'close eye' on Como's Nico Paz.

"Liverpool, Arsenal and Tottenham are keeping a close eye on Nico Paz," he wrote.

In a recent development, "The Argentine is focused on Como, but the summer will be crucial for his future. The trend is that he won't return to Real Madrid, despite being well liked at Valdebebas.

Industry observers note that "Arda Güler's contract renewal has been unveiled, and Bellingham's will come soon. Nico knows this, and it will influence his decision.

It has been revealed that "If Real Madrid don't win any titles, they'll look to sign a creative midfielder. Mourinho is delighted with the Tchouaméni - Bernardo Silva double pivot.

"The priority remains a centre-back, with Jacobo Ramón at the top of the list.".

Nico Paz would be an unreal addition for Liverpool.

Nobody knows what Liverpool's first-team squad is going to look like in 2027.

The January transfer window is just over a month away and, with the number of players who have been linked with a switch, a huge rebuild could be on its way.

And then, in the summer, another rebuild could take place.

Paz is a highly sought-after attacker that has long been linked with a Liverpool transfer.

He's Como's best player and, the fact that he's contributed to 14 goals in the 16 games he's played this season prove how good he actually is.

Whether he's used as a number 10, a right winger or a striker, Liverpool would have an incredible player on their hands if they manage to get a deal over the line.

Here's hoping they do and the Reds add even more firepower to their already stacked attack.
