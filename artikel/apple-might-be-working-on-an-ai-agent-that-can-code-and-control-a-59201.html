Apple wants to up its AI game, and the proof is in the organization's job listings.

In a recent development, bloomberg recently noticed Apple job listings related to AI that paint a very clear picture of what the tech giant is looking for. One of the ads is for a senior engineering manager for "a proactive, agentic AI assistant.".

Recent findings suggest that the job posting describes the goal as follows: "Imagine an AI agent that can use a computer the way a capable person does. It browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code, and calls services, and it chooses the right tool for each step. It does all this privately and with the polish people expect from Apple.".

In a recent development, the advertised role has since been pulled or hidden. One job ad does not reveal an entire strategy, but it ties in neatly with what we've previously heard on the topic.

Bloomberg recently reported that Apple is looking to turn Siri AI into an agent as early as next year. According to the report, latest Apple CEO John Ternus is pushing to evolve the AI assistant as an AI agent that can take actions autonomously on behalf of the user.

Agentic AI is trendy right now, with OpenAI launching Dots, Meta launching Muse, Google adding upcoming agentic capabilities to Gemini, and Anthropic beta-launching an agent called Duet – all in the past couple of months.

These agents can all act autonomously and perform a variety of actions on behalf of the user, including tracking your emails, setting reminders, and building and publishing social media posts. For Apple, which controls its hardware and software, it's a natural course of action, though the firm has so far been slow to push its AI efforts in the way that AI-native competitors have done.
