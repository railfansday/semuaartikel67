A $29.5 million residence spanning an entire floor of the Ritz-Carlton Hotel has hit the market in Latest York City.

Measuring 5,724 square feet—which is nearly three times the median in its ZIP code—Residence 29 at 50 Central Park South is one of the city’s “most prestigious addresses,” according to listing agent Zina Raslan of the Gambino Group at Compass.

The five-bedroom, five-plus-bathroom dwelling is one of only a dozen permanent residences exclusive to the hotel. And while it’s accessible through a private elevator vestibule, it offers a unique perk that’s also available to Ritz-Carlton guests.

“You can order room service from your very own home,” Raslan confirmed.

Industry observers note that with ample east- and west-facing windows found throughout the home, the skyline and Central Park views are absolutely spectacular. There are even two private terraces that look over the park—one for dining and one for lounging.

“These spaces give you a reason to really pause and enjoy the setting of Upcoming York City,” Raslan mentioned, conjuring ideas of “morning coffee overlooking the greenery of Central Park” or “dinner outside as the lights change.”.

The building originally opened in 1930 as the Hotel St. Moritz, designed by Emery Roth, the architect behind The San Remo and the Beresford. In 2002, it was reimagined as the Ritz-Carlton, with an exclusive collection of private residences above the hotel.

The residence is an excellent spot for hosting, with three dining areas—a formal dining room, a casual breakfast nook, and the aforementioned outdoor space—all connected by a seamless flow from the gorgeous Calacatta marble kitchen.

A unique solarium serves as a light-flooded space to relax, separate from the rest of the home.

“What’s really rare about this residence is that its layout has been thoughtfully divided into two separate bedroom wings,” explained Raslan.

The one wing has two bedrooms, and the other is home to the primary suite and two additional bedrooms.

Sources indicate that “It creates a really nice sense of privacy and separation,” she added. “Whether it’s your family staying here or hosting guests, it’s the perfect way to lay it out.”.

The sprawling primary suite—which Raslan accurately described as of “the moment”—is a true retreat from the bustle of the city, which you’d barely notice is just steps away if not for the sweeping city views.

It has been revealed that the bedroom is accompanied by a “massive primary suite” with a double sink and more stunning Calacatta marble. There are two added spaces in the suite. The first is a “zen and serene” private dressing room with artfully designed wardrobes and a coordinating vanity space, while the second is an office.

The bedroom also provides access to one of the home’s terraces.

In a recent development, “You are truly in the heart of Upcoming York City. You have Central Park at your doorstep, Fifth Avenue moments away, and everything that makes New York extraordinary around you,” Raslan said.

Sources indicate that “Yet here, on the 29th floor, you get this sense of calm and privacy, and that’s really what makes residence 29 so special to me. An entire floor to call your own, two private terraces overlooking the park, and a home that’s ready to move into.”.
