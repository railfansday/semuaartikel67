Brad Garlinghouse delivers a bullish take on Anthropic shares freshly trading on, but warns it’s pulling cash away from crypto.

Ripple’s CEO Brad Garlinghouse stated artificial intelligence (AI) is winning the capital competition against crypto currencies, draining much of the liquidity originally ascribed to the digital asset market. Garlinghouse names Anthropic’s $81 billion IPO as a key test for crypto’s resilience.

He clearly pins the recent crypto market downturn to the record Initial Public Offering (IPO) by Anthropic, but predicts a crypto bull rally to come similar to the SpaceX-tied one back in June. The Claude AI developer started trading this week on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

“What happens is you’ve got risk-on assets and people are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market, crypto included”. That kind of liquidity rotation took its toll on Bitcoin & XRP, as well as multiple other major-cap digital assets. The global market cap incurred $200 billion in weekly deficits, with a few days of cascading long position liquidations across Derivatives markets.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, way above the initial $70 range. At a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, Anthropic became the globe’s 12th-biggest tech giant, right behind Tesla. Ripple Labs has been in a league of its own when it comes to regulatory progress and vouching for clear crypto legislation, but the fintech giant doesn’t intend to go public in the near future.

Presently, Ripple Lab’s main focus remains around XRP’s utility in cross-border transactions, the internet of value (IoV) concept that moves information like money & the implementation of native RLUSD stablecoins in federal level payment corridors.

Recently, Ripple inked a National Trust Bank license in the USA to lay down the brickwork for RLUSD’s use in everyday payments.

Meanwhile, XRP’s price performance has been rather lackluster, pushing the altcoin towards the quarterly lows of $0.96 a few days ago. The $1 psychological resistance is crucial in the near-term.

The trading volume has been sliced in half from $3 billion a day regularly last month to $1.55 billion today: less speculative appetite could hint at prices nearing consolidation.

The broader XRP community has also argued whether Ripple’s native RLUSD stablecoin doesn’t steal XRP’s shine. The San Francisco-based giant’s stablecoin grew into a $2.5 billion market cap asset half a year into existence, while XRP’s 45% price downturn has raised some eyebrows among long-term HODLers.

Sources indicate that however, XRP’s usage on DEX trades is significant. The roll-over of RLUSD trades on XRPL’s native DEX consists of roughly 92% XRP-involved pairs, debunking the native coin replacement theory.
