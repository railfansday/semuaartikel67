Recent findings suggest that having not played at the weekend Roffey went into this game knowing a win would take them back into the play-off places. Callum Chesworth was serving a suspension, but Pat Gibbs was back in the starting line up and Dan Pearse on the bench after injury.

Roffey started well with Bailey Moyo firing over in the first minute. Two minutes later Charlie Gibson burst down the left and set up Moyo but Leather in the visitor’s goal pulled off a smart save. Roffey continued to press and after 15 minutes a well-judged pass from Pat Gibbs put Moyo in on goal, but he decided to square it and unfortunately no-one could get the final touch. Five minutes later Jordan Mase must have thought he had scored when put clear in the box, but Leather manages to tip his effort over the bar.

After weathering the early storm Down started to grow into the game with their dangerous front two of Harley and Leslie. Leslie latched on to a long pass and poked it past Boar’s keeper Charlie Burton, but Gibbs raced back to clear the danger. In their next attack the visitors had the ball in the net, but the assistant’s flag was already up.

Recent findings suggest that down were seeing much more of the ball now and it took a great tackle from Byron Napper to stop Harley getting through on goal. Strong appeals for a penalty from the men in red were waved away by the referee. To add salt to Down’s wounds, a minute later Roffey took the lead. A fast attack resulted in a free kick that top scorer Mase curled round the wall and in for the opener. The game looked well balanced at the interval, with Roffey having seen enough of their visitor’s threat to know that one goal was probably not going to be enough.

Three minutes after the break and one became two with another cracking finish. Stan Berry got to the byline then cut back and curled the ball inside the far post to double the lead and give the Boars some breathing space. With that extra cushion Roffey began to exert more control and Down old boy Callum Kilkenny raced down the left, but his shot hit the outside of the post. The hosts continued to look for a third and Luke Harrison put in a teasing cross that managed to evade the three on rushing Boars.

Industry observers note that evan Hoarty and Luca Allen replaced Moyo and Berry with 25 minutes remaining then in the seventy second minute Kilkenny looked like he had sealed the game for Roffey. His first shot was blocked but he reacted fastest to bury the rebound. It could have been four five minutes later when a blast from Mase was brilliantly saved by Leather.

Approaching the last Ten minutes and Dan Pearse, Hayden Skerry and Teddy Wood replaced Gibson, Napper and Kilkenny. A good illustration of the quality of the Boars squad. Skerry came close as his and Mase’s efforts were cleared off the line as the Boars still looked hungry. However, it was the visitors who completed the scoring when after a foul from a cross resulted in a penalty from which Will Harley sent Burton the wrong way for a consolation goal.

So, a tricky looking fixture had been negotiated and the three goals and three points took Roffey back up into fourth place in what is becoming a rather congested pack of teams behind leaders Haywards Heath and it is the Blues from Heath that will be visiting this Saturday for what should be an exciting encounter.

Roffey: Burton, Harrison, Gibson(Pearse), Napper (Wood), Gibbs, Meeres, Caplin, Mase, Berry(Allen), Moyo(Hoarty), Kilkenny(Skerry).
