Martin Zubimendi sends fresh Arsenal message as January transfer speculation grows

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, martin Zubimendi has raised questions among Arsenal fans over his future. This follows comments from the midfielder that he is keen to succeed in north London despite struggling for game time this season.

The Spain international has been linked with a move to Chelsea, Atletico Madrid and Real Madrid ahead of the January transfer window. Reports from Spain have noted that Zubimendi’s situation at the club is becoming more complex, with the player possibly being available.

Zubimendi has also spoken about the speculation. In his interview during the international break with Marca, he said he did not feel like he was near to a move to Atletico, and he is still delighted to be in London.

In a recent development, he added the 27-year-old has had no contact with any other clubs. His priority is to get into Arteta’s squad.

This is an interesting response to the transfer speculation that has been building. Arsenal may well attract interest for one of their midfielders. However, there does not appear to be any evidence that Zubimendi is actively looking to leave.

Clearly, Zubimendi’s role has shifted.

Recent findings suggest that in his first season, he played 38 times in the Premier League and became an crucial member of Arteta’s midfield. However, he has not started a league game this season.

Recent findings suggest that opta Analyst shows that he has only played 100 minutes in the Premier League in seven games. He has also faced increased competition for a spot in the team with the arrival of Bruno Guimarães.

Myles Lewis-Skelly is also an option in the middle of the park. Zubimendi will have to fight for his place back.

It is understood Arteta had a chat with Zubimendi ahead of the campaign and made it clear he would face more competition from the newly introduced signings and would have to earn his place.

Zubimendi has accepted the challenge and has mentioned he could see it as a chance to learn and improve due to his reduced workload.

Recent findings suggest that the interest from Spain and Chelsea will make it a tough choice if the right offers come in. However, losing Zubimendi will mean Arteta won’t have as many options to call upon in the busy second half of the season.

But he proved his worth in the game against Slavia Prague earlier this month. He started the Champions League match and played the full 90 minutes.

Sources indicate that this highlights that his contribution shouldn’t be judged purely on how much he plays in the Premier League. Arsenal will have a busy schedule in Europe and domestically, and he may provide some much-needed quality in the midfield.

In a recent development, with Zubimendi contracted until 2030, there is no immediate reason for Arsenal to look to sell him. He will have time to fight for his spot in the starting XI.

Despite not making a commitment to remain at the club beyond January, his remarks indicate that he is not on the lookout for alternatives.

According to recent reports, the priority for Arsenal will be trying to find a way to get the best out of Zubimendi. He was a regular last season and seems happy to wait for his chance.
