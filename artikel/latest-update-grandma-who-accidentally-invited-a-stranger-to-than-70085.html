The viral story of a grandmother who accidentally invited a stranger to Thanksgiving dinner is being turned into a Lifetime movie, nearly 10 years after their unlikely friendship began.

Wanda Dench and Jamal Hinton first made headlines in 2016 when a mistaken text message led to the pair celebrating Thanksgiving together. What started as an accidental invitation has since become an annual tradition, with the two continuing to reunite for the holiday.

Now, their friendship is heading to television after the pair signed a deal with Lifetime to bring their story to the screen, with a movie expected to arrive in November 2027.

Dench confirmed to The Arizona Republic on November 16 that she and Hinton recently signed over the rights to their story for a Lifetime movie right now titled ‘The Thanksgiving Grandma.’.

The film is expected to begin production in early 2027, with producer Jack Leslie targeting a November release that same year.

“They did tell us right up front that it’s based on our story,” Dench explained.

Hinton also unveiled the news on Instagram, sharing a photo alongside Dench and celebrating their partnership with Lifetime.

“Very excited to officially be in business with Lifetime!” he wrote. “They believe in our story, and it truly feels like we’re in the best hands as we take this next step.”.

He also thanked Dench for being a constant source of support throughout their friendship, telling followers that their journey was only getting started.

Recent findings suggest that the pair’s story began in November 2016 when Dench accidentally texted Hinton while attempting to invite her grandson to Thanksgiving dinner. Unbeknownst to her, her grandson had changed his phone number, and Hinton received the invitation instead.

Industry observers note that after exchanging messages and photos, the two realized they were strangers. However, Hinton jokingly asked whether he could still attend dinner.

“Of course you can, that’s what grandmas do … feed everyone,” Dench responded.

Their exchange quickly went viral on Twitter, and Hinton ultimately joined Dench and her family for Thanksgiving at her home in Arizona.

The tradition has continued every year since, with the pair sharing updates about their friendship on social media.

The upcoming Lifetime movie has been in development in various forms for years. Leslie initially approached Dench about adapting the story in 2018 while working at Netflix, but the project was eventually abandoned following downsizing at the tech giant.

Screenwriter Glen Dolman is now working on the Lifetime adaptation and recently spent a weekend at Dench’s home to learn more about their relationship.

“It’s going to be more of how we’ve supported each other throughout the 10 years,” Dench explained.
