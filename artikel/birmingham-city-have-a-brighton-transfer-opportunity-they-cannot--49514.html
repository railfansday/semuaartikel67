Birmingham City have a Brighton transfer opportunity they cannot afford to ignore

Birmingham City have had a brilliant Championship season so far, with Chris Davies’ side right now leading the way.

Whilst the Blues chief won’t be getting carried away, he will be delighted at how his team are performing, and the boss also deserves credit as he has tweaked the style of play this season, which is paying off spectacularly.

As is always the case, the January window will be pivotal in the promotion race, and Davies will hope for more support from Birmingham’s ambitious US owners.

Industry observers note that pleasingly, he won’t go into the market needing to overhaul the squad. Instead, it will be about adding a few quality players that can help Blues last the distance.

Brighton might looks at Birmingham's situation, too, and see the perfect opportunity to strike another deal for Ibrahim Osman.

Sources indicate that many Birmingham fans will feel the starting XI has shown enough to suggest that they can compete with anyone in this league. However, there will be doubts about whether Davies has the depth behind his first-team.

So, raising the level will be the priority, and most would agree that attacking reinforcements are needed - and Ibrahim Osman would be a dream signing for Blues.

Recent findings suggest that the winger spent the previous campaign at St. Andrew’s, and he impressed with his pace, dribbling ability and willingness to take players on.

He is capable down either flank, but he particularly excelled on the left, and he would add that star quality that could help Birmingham win tight games during the run-in.

Of course, Birmingham know all about Osman’s ability, as they got to see it first-hand last season, when he was one of a few positives in the second half of the campaign.

There are always difficulties that come with signing players in January, as you don’t know how an individual will adapt, whether they will settle into the club, and how they will be received by their teammates and the supporters.

Sources indicate that yet, in Osman’s case, the six months he spent at Birmingham would be a massive help, as he understands exactly what Davies would want from him on the pitch, and he has already played with the bulk of the Blues squad. Plus, he knows the fans appreciate his talent.

So, it would be a relatively straightforward transition, which would help Osman hit the ground running if he did return to Birmingham.

Osman joined Brighton for a fee of around £16m in 2024, but he is one of few transfers involving the Premier League side that probably hasn’t worked out.

The winger has failed to truly establish himself as a regular at the Seagulls, and any hopes he could do that this season appear to be over.

The Ghana international has managed just three Premier League appearances so far - all as a sub, with his last top flight outing coming in September.

He has featured in the Conference League, but it’s not the regular football that Osman will feel he needs at this stage of his career as he looks to deliver on his undoubted potential.

It’s also worth pointing out that this is at a time when Kaoru Mitoma and Femi Azeez have been out injured, whilst Yankuba Minteh has missed most of the season as well. So, when the trio are fit and firing, it’s hard to see where Osman fits in at all, and there's an opportunity for Brighton to send him back out on loan early next year.

Therefore, Osman will surely recognise that he must depart in the New Year, whether it’s temporarily or permanently, and he will see Blues as an attractive destination.

As outlined above, they are a club that he knows, and there is a very real chance that Blues could be playing in the Premier League next season, so Osman will see the potential at St. Andrew’s.

Ultimately, the 21-year-old will want to prove himself in the best league in the world, but he may need to take a small step back right now before reaching that level.

After difficult loan spells at Feyenoord and Auxerre, combined with his struggles at Brighton, Osman rediscovered his best form at Birmingham, and a return to the club in January could be ideal for both the club and the player.
