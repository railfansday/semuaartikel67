Industry observers note that bloomberg reported on November 23, 2026, in an account carried by The Verge, that Nothing CEO Carl Pei had demonstrated Ear Note earbuds with a microphone-equipped charging case for recording and AI transcription ahead of a launch planned for December 8, 2026. The system can capture audio through either the earbuds or the case, while a connected app manages recordings, transcripts, and AI-generated summaries.
