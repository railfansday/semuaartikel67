Perfect apps have to be easy, fast, and above all, delicious. Puff pastry dough makes hitting those benchmarks a cinch. Though making it from scratch is a lengthy process that includes folding tons of butter into dough (kind of like making croissants), store-bought frozen puff pastry is just as good (maybe better, considering the convenience!).
