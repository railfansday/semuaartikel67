Xbox has teamed up with DJ Khaled to give gamers an excuse to skip work and spend the day playing video games, complete with personalized doctor’s notes.

According to recent reports, the rapper and producer has taken on the role of “Dr. XBOX” as part of a new promotional campaign encouraging players to take time off during a major week for gaming.

Revealed on November 16, the campaign allows fans to generate their own doctor’s notes online, although Xbox has made it clear that the documents aren’t medically certified and won’t necessarily convince employers to approve a day off.

Players interested in getting a personalized doctor’s note can visit the website, where Xbox has rolled out a dedicated website for the promotion.

The notes are presented as prescriptions from DJ Khaled, who claims to have discovered a widespread condition called “Vitamin G deficiency” after observing symptoms including controller cramps, late-night gaming dreams, and excitement surrounding new releases.

Recent findings suggest that “After extensive research (and a whole lot of gaming), I’ve come to one conclusion: there’s a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency around the world. And I’m here to tell you that the cure is simple,” Khaled explained.
