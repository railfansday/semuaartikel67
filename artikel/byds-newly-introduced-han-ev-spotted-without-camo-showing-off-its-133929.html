Industry observers note that who doesn’t love a good fastback? BYD’s new Han was spotted completely undisguised, giving us an early look at its sleek design and interior ahead of its expected debut next week.

After launching the flagship Great Han last month, or the “Da Han,” as it’s called in China, BYD is preparing to introduce another fresh member to the lineup.

BYD already sells the smaller Han, but the electric sedan is due for an overhaul. The newly introduced version appeared in a regulatory filing by China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) last month.

According to the filing, the next-generation BYD Han will be available in three battery electric (BEV) and one plug-in hybrid (PHEV) variants.

The Han EV will offer two single-motor rear-wheel-drive (RWD) options, with an output of 328 hp (245 kW) and 402 hp (300 kW). A dual-motor all-wheel-drive (AWD) version will also be offered, delivering a combined 697 hp (520 kW).

All variants use BYD’s Blade LFP Battery and are expected to be equipped with its five-minute Flash Charging technology.

The PHEV version, on the other hand, uses a 1.5-liter engine and an electric motor with up to 268 hp (200 kW).

Ahead of its expected debut at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27, BYD’s new Han was captured in public without any disguise.

The images sent to Autohome show a design similar to the Great Han and other upcoming Dynasty series vehicles, with its latest “Dragon Face” signature front-end look.

A few things have changed from the current model, including new headlights and a full-length light bar, while the Chinese symbol moved to the hood.

From the side, the Han features a sleek, fastback silhouette. The vehicle also features a roof-mounted LiDAR to support advanced driver-assistance features. The newly introduced Han is expected to feature BYD’s God’s Eye B ADAS system.

According to recent reports, inside, the latest Han adopts BYD’s updated interior design. The new setup includes a floating center infotainment screen and a digital instrument panel behind a three-spoke steering wheel.

The center console is also updated with two wireless phone chargers and cupholders without covers.

BYD’s fresh Han is 5,156 mm long, 1,969 mm wide, and 1,507 mm tall, with a wheelbase of 3,030 mm, which is 161 mm longer than the current version.

The flagship Great Han is the largest of the series, measuring 5,256 mm long. After launching the Great Han on October 13, BYD sold 4,000 units by November 1.

In a recent development, since the flagship sedan starts at 235,900 yuan, or about $35,000, the smaller Han will likely be closer to 200,000 yuan ($30,000) or less. The outgoing Han starts at 168,800 yuan ($25,000).
