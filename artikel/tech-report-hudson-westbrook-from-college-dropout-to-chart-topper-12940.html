With a co-sign from Miranda Lambert, a performance at the Grand Ole Opry and a Billboard charting career already under his belt, 22-year-old Hudson Westbrook is one of country music’s fastest-rising stars. The singer/songwriter joins Billboard Up & Coming host Kyle Denis to discuss his rise on the country charts, his decision to leave college to pursue music and how he built an audience on TikTok. With his sophomore album Made You Look out now, Westbrook also opens up about his musical influences, why he chose to go without outside collaborators on the project, and how he stays true to his storytelling as an artist.

Welcome to Billboard’s Up & Coming Podcast, your go-to stop for the key moments and stories behind music’s hottest fresh stars. I’m your host Kyle Denis, and our guest today is one of the fastest rising stars in country music. He’s a Texas-born singer-songwriter who turned a viral TikTok into a Billboard charting career, earned a coveted co-sign from Miranda Lambert, and graced the Grand Old Opry. From the heart-wrenching house again to his brand new album ‘Made You Look,’ Hudson Westbrook has taken his honest songwriting and penchant for traditional country to the top of the radio charts and the hearts of young fans across the country. Nominated for New Male Artist of the Year at the 2026 Academy of Country Music Awards, this guy is well on his way to country superstardom, and his latest stop is Billboard Up & Coming. Midway through 2023, Hudson started posting clips of himself performing covers of contemporary country hits from Zach Bryan to the Red Clay Strays, steadily establishing a fan base drawn to his husky, earnest voice, and piercing blue eyes. By 2024, the Stevenville born and raised artist enrolled as a freshman at Texas Tech University, diving into its energy commerce major with looks to become a landman like his grandfather and uncle, but then he started sharing his original music, beginning with “Take It Slow.” In the weeks leading up to the song’s April 2025 release, Hudson garnered millions of views with clips of himself playing it on his acoustic guitar in his truck bed. To date, “Take It Slow” has earned 63.5 official on-demand U.S. streams, according to Luminate, and he followed that up with “House Again,” his debut single at Country Radio, where it hit No. 6 on Billboard’s Country Airplay chart. Soon after, he sang a No. 10 debut on the Country Albums chart with his debut set, “Texas Forever,” which he supported on opening runs for fellow country stars Bailey Zimmerman, George Strait and Morgan Wallen. At the moment on his headlining Hits Me tour, Hudson is also celebrating the release of his sophomore studio album ‘Made You Look.’ As country music continues to terrain at the forefront of mainstream music, we’re here to chop it up with one of its most exciting new voices. All right, Hudson Westbrook, welcome to Up & Coming.
