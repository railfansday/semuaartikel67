It has been revealed that after a 13-year drought, Grand Theft Auto VI has finally hit the masses. Predictably, a large swath of guys have called off work to give themselves just enough time to explore the immersive fictional land of Leonida, where players can do just about everything they can in real life—from driving cars and renovating homes to playing video games within a video game. In Grand Theft Auto VI, that now also includes hitting the gym. And if you want to progress through the story as efficiently as possible, becoming an in-game gym rat might be the best thing you can do.

In a recent development, expanding on a gameplay mechanic introduced in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, working out in GTA levels up your character to help with progressing through the story. The fitter you become in the game, the more stamina you have to sprint, the more health you have before you’re Wasted, and the more focus you have to take down enemies. Just like in real life, pumping iron makes daily life a whole lot easier.

To get fit, you can use the weights in Jason’s outdoor gym, which features the big three full-body lifts: squats, bench presses, and deadlifts. While your character handles the heavy lifting, all you have to do is carefully time your button presses to complete reps without causing an injury. Once you have enough money, you can purchase memberships at gyms across the city, giving you access to treadmills, free weights, and workout machines. The exercise options are so extensive (you can even do zone 2 cardio) that gamers have already started trading tips to the best GTA 6 workouts for maximizing your character’s gains.

As your character gets stronger, their avatar transforms accordingly, developing more defined muscles, a six-pack, and even a chiseled face. Neglecting your fitness and overeating (a real thing you can do in the game) has an alternate effect as your player visibly gains weight and adopts a “dad bod.”.

Inevitably there will be many players that skip the gym this weekend so they can pump a little virtual iron with their thumbs. There’s something undeniably ironic about looksmaxxing your GTA character while spending hours sitting on the couch playing a video game. But don’t think that just because it’s all on a screen that you won’t walk away from a session with a little back pain, so maybe schedule a few breaks in between. You could even build a home gym of your own like Jason’s—it’s already got one fitness expert’s approval.
