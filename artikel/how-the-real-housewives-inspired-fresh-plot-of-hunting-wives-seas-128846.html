Just call her the Real Housewife of Maple Brook, Texas.

Hunting Wives star Jaime Ray Newman revealed that when it comes to her character Callie Baskin, she was inspired by The Real Housewives of Dallas' fan favorite Momma Dee Simmons, mother of Housewife D'Andra Simmons.

"When I was auditioning for Callie, I became obsessed with Real Housewives of Dallas," Newman exclusively told E! News at the season two premiere. "I had Momma Dee in my ears for about a week, just listening to her over and over and over again.".

Even though Newman confessed that she wasn't previously "a reality TV person," the matriarch's larger-than-life personality helped her tap into a Texas socialite's persona. That’s especially true for Hunting Wives season two—hitting Netflix Nov. 26—because Callie and the other wives of Maple Brook become stars of their own over-the-top reality TV show, Married Justice.

As the 48-year-old explained, "When I read the first scripts, I'm like, 'Oh my god, I gotta go into full reality TV show mode.'".

As such, her creative process included "thinking, 'What would Callie pick and choose?' Like, little things, little ways to pose, little 'isms' that Callie would lean into," adding that it was fascinating to deep dive "into something I didn't know that much about.".

And returning to set with costars Brittany Snow and Malin Akerman after season one only encouraged Newman to make her performance even more dramatic.

"Seeing the show fully realized, now we got to really let loose," the Eastwick alum remarked. "I feel like season two was just much more wild.".

Sources indicate that that said, Newman's work is already resonating with fans, including the Real Housewives of Potomac's Karen Huger. While the Bravo star said reuniting with her real-life friend group this year has been "wonderful," she also volunteered herself for any future openings on the Hunting Wives cast, quipping, "all things are possible.".

Recent findings suggest that for now, the series has added the WNBA star Angel Reese for its second season. As for what the 24-year-old—who has acted in GOAT and A House of Dynamite—remarked viewers will take away from her role?

"Barbie," Reese mused, referencing her character's name, Trainer Barbie. "I'm just the Barbie, being myself.".
