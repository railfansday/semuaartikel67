📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that this past weekend, the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix returned to Paradise, Nevada, from November 19 to November 21, where Max Verstappen ended up on top, winning Saturday’s finals with a total of 25 points. This marked his third victory in Vegas and his 75th top-spot finish at a Grand Prix, and he bested Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, who came in second and third, respectively.

Recent findings suggest that each year, numerous F1 Grand Prix races are held around the world, with 22 events set to take place in 2026, ending with the circuit in Abu Dhabi in December. (There were originally 24 on the calendar, but the Bahrain and Saudi Arabian Grands Prix were canceled back in April.).

But, while there are quite a few Grand Prix to enjoy all year long, the Las Vegas races always bring out some of the starriest names, and this year was no exception.

It has been revealed that from the stands, F1 regulars like Keanu Reeves and Kim Kardashian took it all in, with the latter there to cheer on her boyfriend, Lewis Hamilton (who finished 7th for Ferrari). Meanwhile, other A-listers in attendance included Lisa, Teyana Taylor, Venus Williams, Sarah Michelle Gellar, Nina Dobrev, Rachel Brosnahan, and Eiza González, who moved between qualifiers, races, parties, and more.
