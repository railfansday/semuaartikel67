By LEWIS STEELE, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 17:09 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 18:48 EST, 25 November 2026.

Andoni Iraola tried to stifle a smirk on Tuesday when it was put to him that he had a fair chance of completing a perfect week by thrashing Manchester United, winning away in Europe and then winning his first-ever Merseyside Derby.

The usual responses came back of remaining level-headed and not getting carried away, of course – but Iraola has ticked off two of those three tasks and now goes to Everton with his much-criticised, big-money signings in the form of their Liverpool careers.

That is Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz, of course, who both scored here to further quash any narratives over them being expensive flops, a debate which had followed them for much of their first year or so on Merseyside.

Isak scored a brace to take his season tally to double-figures and make it six away games in a row where he has struck, while Wirtz followed up his best performance of the season against United with a fine strike from distance.

Wirtz especially has never been far away from criticism during his time at the club but is silencing his doubters every week and noted this week that Iraola had helped him rediscover the player Liverpool thought they were buying for £116million two summers ago.

The sentiment spreads beyond Wirtz. After a season of strife, Iraola has brought the feel-good factor back at Anfield and supporters are enjoying watching their team again after a year in which it felt like a chore to do so some weeks.

Alexander Isak scored twice as Liverpool beat Club Brugge 3-0 in the Champions League.

That made it three wins in a row for the Reds as they start to really click under Andoni Iraola.

Sources indicate that it is making the Liverpool fans fall for Iraola a little bit more each week. How that feeling would be turbocharged with Derby delight at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Sunday.

In a recent development, ‘I was very pleased with the result and the performance overall,’ noted Iraola. ‘At 3-0, you can think it was an easy game and I don’t think it was. We were facing a really good team.’.

As the 1,000 or so travelling Kop enjoyed their strong Belgian beers and chocolate waffles in this quaint, picturesque city, they were still laughing about poor old Manchester United and Sunday’s 4-0 demolition job.

This was a much tougher test against an opposition that had started well in the Champions League with three wins from four, so Liverpool’s performance was never going to be as convincing – but the victory was still important.

It helped them take a step closer to cementing a top eight place in this long-winded league phase. That would ease the pressure on a congested calendar by seeing them not only be able to rest players in the latter games of the league stage but also skip a play-off round.

Club Brugge are no mugs and they revealed that, with energetic winger Carlos Forbs the pick of the bunch. The former Manchester City academy starlet has inherited a certain Cristiano Ronaldo’s No 7 shirt at Portugal and gave Milos Kerkez a testing night here.

According to recent reports, the Belgian side, like Liverpool, had won three of their first four European games including victory away to Inter. They had won five of the last seven league titles with a brave playing style that Iraola admitted was a pleasure to analyse – which he added was not always the case.

Sources indicate that their bold approach was their undoing here, though, with possession tossed away far too easily for Liverpool’s opener after 13 minutes. Yann Sommer, in the winter of his career at 37, passed straight to Bradley Barcola who, via stand-in skipper Dominik Szoboszlai, found Isak.

The Swede needed no second invitation to fire home his ninth goal of the season. Isak had been rested for most of Liverpool’s early European outings but was the difference here and loves playing away: he has now netted on his last six games away from Anfield.

Liverpool have 12 points from five games and will almost certainly be in the knockout stages.

Florian Wirtz continued his fine form as he curled in a delightful effort to put Liverpool 2-0 up.

Iraola will hope to make it the perfect week with victory in Sunday's Merseyside Derby.

Only Mohamed Salah has done so in seven or more in recent history for Liverpool. Everton would be a fine place to match that record.

According to recent reports, despite the scoreline flattering Liverpool, things were far from easy after Isak’s opener. Alisson was needed to deny Felix Lemarechal both sides at half-time and Virgil van Dijk – rested from the start – was called on as a second-half substitute to steady the ship.

Industry observers note that winger Victor Munoz limped off in agony after going down before half-time holding his knee. Iraola feared the worst when a stretcher was called but the Spaniard walked off and was feeling ‘optimistic’ after the match, according to the boss.

Szoboszlai should have put the game to bed after a sublime team move led to the Hungarian being thwarted by Sommer at point-blank range, while Barcola was also guilty of wastefulness after getting his feet tangled up when in a dangerous position.

But Wirtz, in the best form of his Liverpool career, made it three goals in his last three with a fine strike from distance that clipped in off the post.

Then Isak raced through on goal and dinked a shot over Sommer for goal No 10 of the season for him. It completed a memorable night for Iraola and his big-money boys. Now to make it a perfect week at Everton on Sunday.
