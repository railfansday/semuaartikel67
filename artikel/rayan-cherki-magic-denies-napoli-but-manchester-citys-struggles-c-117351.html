For reasons best known to those eyeing the pound signs, Manchester City gave a debut to a special rust-coloured kit in front of a nonplussed crowd. It was of a piece with their performance for most of a familiarly frustrating night that at least concluded with a shot of energy, the home side belatedly shaking off their torpor and ending the prospect of a triumphant return to the Etihad for Kevin De Bruyne.

Despite the relief sparked by a fine equaliser by Rayan Cherki, who was rewarded for an insistent but hitherto fruitless evening’s endeavours when his angled shot flashed past Vanja Milinkovic-Savic, it was hard to see this as anything but a perfect snapshot of their autumn difficulties. Nobody could pretend City were up against crack opposition in a one-paced Napoli, who are eighth in Serie A and lack the brio of old, but they simply cannot keep the door shut and could not have quibbled about departing with a defeat.
