Filip Stanković and Łukasz Skorupski were the stars at the Penzo as bottom-of-the-table Venezia held Bologna to a goalless draw, with Redouane Halhal having an early opener ruled out by VAR.

Venezia came into the game bottom of Serie A with six points from 12 games, after a 4-0 thrashing at Juventus on Sunday. Davide Nicola was without the injured Franjić, Šverko and Thierry Correia, but handed a start to Gianluca Busio in midfield.

In a recent development, bologna, ninth on 18 points, had drawn 1-1 with Udinese last week. Raffaele Palladino left Riccardo Orsolini and Artem Dovbyk on the bench, while Lorenzo De Silvestri, Emil Holm, Juan Miranda, Oussama El Azzouzi and Ukko Happonen were all injured.

Venezia made the brighter start, and Skorupski had to dive low to his right to keep out John Yeboah’s effort after a defence-splitting pass from Akor Adams.

The hosts thought they had taken the lead in the 14th minute. Halhal burst into the box to meet Antoine Hainaut’s whipped cross and finish into the bottom corner. However, the goal was ruled out for offside after a lengthy VAR check.

Tempers flared in the aftermath, with Juan Jesus and Nadir Zortea both booked for dissent, and Martin Vitík and Hainaut soon joined them in the book.

In a recent development, skorupski was the busier goalkeeper in the first half. He produced a sharp save to deny Kike Pérez’s low volley on the half-hour, then thwarted Yeboah again from inside the box shortly before the break. At the other end, Jens Odgaard fired over from a good position and Arthur Theate headed straight at Stanković.

Recent findings suggest that bologna improved after the restart, with Matteo Darmian on for Vitík. Stanković pulled off a superb reflex save to keep out Nicolò Casale’s header, and Federico Bernardeschi curled just wide from a set piece moments later.

Palladino turned to Orsolini and Nicolò Cambiaghi just before the hour, while Nicola sent on Thórir Jóhann Helgason for Busio.

Sources indicate that helgason almost had an immediate impact. His set-piece delivery found Juan Jesus, whose header from the edge of the six-yard box came back off the post.

Sources indicate that bologna brought on Roberto Piccoli for Odgaard, and he blazed over from Rahim Alhassane’s cross. Venezia refreshed their attack with Albion Rrahmani and Lion Lauberbach, and the latter forced a strong save from Skorupski at his near post.

It has been revealed that the chances kept coming in the closing stages. Stanković denied Lewis Ferguson’s header and then made his best save of the night to stop Tommaso Pobega from inside the six-yard box. Skorupski rushed out to smother Helgason’s effort at the other end, and Alhassane fired high and wide.
