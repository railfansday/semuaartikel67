Volkswagen will reveal the upcoming GTI hot hatch on Friday, promising it will be “something really spicy.”.

To celebrate the GTI’s 50th anniversary, Volkswagen is “starting a new chapter,” with its first all-electric model to bear the iconic performance badge.

The ID. Polo GTI “brings the legendary GTI driving pleasure into the electric future,” VW said after opening pre-orders last month.

Industry observers note that volkswagen previewed a mysterious new hot hatch draped under a cloth that appears to be a souped-up version of the ID. Polo GTI. Despite its attempt to “hide” it, you can see the massive rear wing and wide wheel arches under the cloth.

The company promised that “something really spicy” will be served this Friday, November 20, with the tags #Volkswagen, #GTI, and #GTI50.

Although no other details were revealed, the upcoming hot hatch is thought to be the more powerful “Clubsport” variant.

A post shared by Volkswagen (@volkswagen).

Recent findings suggest that volkswagen’s head of driving dynamics, Florian Umbach, told Autocar earlier this year that the manufacturer is planning to launch the ID. Polo GTI Clubsport, a more powerful variant that will deliver close to 300 hp, up from 223 hp in the standard model.

Umbach said that Volkswagen is developing “a similar kind of paddleshift power delivery that the Hyundai N cars have” for the upcoming electric hot hatch.

Hyundai’s performance EVs, the IONIQ 5 N and IONIQ 6 N, feature a simulated gear-shifting system called N e-Shift that uses paddle shifters on the steering wheel to replicate the feel of an 8-speed dual-clutch transmission. It works with the N Active Sound+ system to make it feel like you’re in a traditional gas-powered sports car.

It has yet to be confirmed whether the hot hatch debuting on Friday is, in fact, the newly introduced ID. Polo Clubsport, but the design clues give us a good idea of what’s coming.

Volkswagen opened pre-orders for the ID. Polo GTI last month, starting at 39,995 euros (about $45,000).

Like the first Golf GTI from 1976, the electric ID. Polo GTI uses a front-wheel-drive powertrain. The front-mounted electric motor delivers 223 hp (166 kW) and 213 lb-ft (290 Nm) of instant torque, enabling it to sprint from 0 to 62 mph (0 to 100 km/h) in 6.8 seconds.

In early 2027, Volkswagen will open orders for the ID.3 GTI, the second EV to wear the badge, and its first rear-wheel-drive (RWD) GTI.
