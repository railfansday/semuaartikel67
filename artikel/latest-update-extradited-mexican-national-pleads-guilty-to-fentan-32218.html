Latest Update: Extradited Mexican National Pleads Guilty to Fentanyl Trafficking in Homeland Security Task Force Investigation

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (116 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

SAN DIEGO – An extradited Mexican national pleaded guilty in federal court today to conspiring to import massive quantities of fentanyl into the United States. According to court documents, Fausto Ramirez, 71, was a leader of a network that imported over 450,000 counterfeit, fentanyl-laced “M-30s” into the United States through the San Ysidro and Nogales Ports of Entry. Ramirez’s network of couriers brought in these pills which were then supplied to distributors across San Diego County and the United States. This long-term investigation linked Ramirez and his network to distributors who sold thousands of fentanyl pills on “Dream Market,” a dark web marketplace where owners buy and sell illegal substances and services.
