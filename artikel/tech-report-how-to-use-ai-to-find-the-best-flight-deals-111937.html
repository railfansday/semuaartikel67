Tech Report: How to use AI to find the best flight deals

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (168 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Artificial intelligence tools can now assist travelers in finding flight deals, comparing prices and deciphering fare rules, especially during major sales like Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
  • AI can help suggest alternative airports, flexible travel dates and analyze whether cash or points provide better value for booking flights.
  • Despite improvements, AI still struggles with finding real-time award availability and may present outdated or unbookable fares.
  • Combining AI with established search engines and deal-alert services yields the best results for travelers seeking savings.
  • AI-generated fares may not always reflect live prices or include all taxes and fees, so always verify before booking.
  • Award space and special promotions can vanish quickly, and AI may not capture dynamic changes or blackout dates.
  • AI is best used as a supplement to flight search tools and deal-alert platforms, not as a sole resource.
