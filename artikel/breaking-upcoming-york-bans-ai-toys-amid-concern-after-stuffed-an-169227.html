UPDATED 20:46 EST / NOVEMBER 25 2026.

Fresh York Governor Kathy Hochul today signed legislation that will impose a five-year ban on children’s toys powered by artificial intelligence chatbots, amid fears that dolls, robots and stuffed animals might be leading children astray or discussing X-rated themes.

“It’s my job to put New York families first,” noted Hochul, who explained as a mother and grandmother the matter is “deeply personal.” Hochul added that some of the wayward toys on the market at the moment have engaged children in “inappropriate conversations,” while some may also manipulate a child’s feelings or put their privacy at risk.

Sources indicate that “This is a warning that there are toys out there that could be harmful to your children,” the governor said. “When tech companies and unregulated AI pose a threat to our kids and our babies, you bet I’m going to have something to say about it.”.

Sources indicate that though dangerous dolls manipulating innocent children may sound like the plot of Hollywood movie, recent tests of certain chatbot-powered toys have become a real cause for concern. When journalists at NBC News tested the Pixie Paw, an AI-powered plush cat toy marketed to children aged three and older, it reportedly shared stories of massacres, gang rape and firing squads in what was described as graphic detail.

Other tests have shown some of the toys can become very convincing when trying to persuade the young user that they are human. “Parents have enough to worry about,” said Hochul, who was also aware some of the smart toys have encouraged kids to jump out of windows.

In a recent development, state senator Andrew Gounardes applauded the move, saying that modern life seems to be mimicking science fiction of days gone by. He invoked an episode of the “Twilight Zone that featured the Talky Tina doll, an insidious, possessed toy that tried to persuade a young girl to murder her strict stepfather.

New York Attorney General Letitia James explained that the toys being put on the market is reckless when they clearly expose “young people to inappropriate content and put “their mental health at risk.” She added, “We won’t allow our children’s health to become the collateral damage of big tech.”.

Hochul has been vocal about the dangers of AI for some time, recently imposing the first statewide moratorium on fresh data centers and last month signing a bill titled “AI Chatbot Restrictions for Minors,” which will prohibit firms from developing chatbots that feature elements that could be harmful to minors.
