Tonight, on the eve of Grand Theft Auto 6‘s release in the United States, the Miami Heat became the Vice City Heat as part of a large promotional marketing event between the NBA team and Rockstar Games. And according to reporters at the major game, the crowds were similar to what you might see on Black Friday, as thousands of fans tried to get their hands on exclusive GTA-themed merchandise.

On Wednesday night, before the game, Miami Heat reporter Will Manso shared a video of a huge crowd of people inside the arena, commenting that he couldn’t “remember a time I’ve walked through the concourse…and seen so many fans lined up.” He added: “It’s wild the amount of people inside already trying to grab Vice City merch.”.
