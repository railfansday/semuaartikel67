Beyond their attack, Roma are also looking to strengthen their midfield in January.

Gian Piero Gasperini has had to contend with injuries to Koné and Cristante this start to the season, so while the attack remains the priority, a possible midfield reinforcement cannot be ruled out.

As reported by Turkish journalist Ekrem Konur, the Giallorossi are interested in Ardon Jashari.

The Swiss midfielder, owned by AC Milan, is not finding much playing time in Serie A. He has made only three league starts, and has come off the bench on six other occasions.

Industry observers note that it’s not just Roma, as other major Italian clubs are reportedly eyeing the 2002-born player: Juventus, Atalanta, and Como are reportedly eyeing the former Brugge player. Aston Villa and Nottingham Forest are also reportedly interested in the Premier League.
