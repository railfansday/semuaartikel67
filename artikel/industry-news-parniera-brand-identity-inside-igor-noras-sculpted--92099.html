Igor Nora builds the Parniera brand identity from silk and sculpted gold, with a bespoke typeface and a silk moth mark in pale yellow.

According to recent reports, the idea is a collision the project calls Sculpted Duality. On one side, fine silk in fluid motion, pale yellow, soft and light. On the other, solid sculpted gold forms with architectural weight. The identity holds both at once: garments described as vulnerability and armor, built for a woman who commands the room with quiet power. Nothing shouts. The yellow field does the talking, and the black line work sits on it with total control.

Industry observers note that the wordmark is a bespoke typeface drawn for the house. Thin, delicate strokes carry the lightness, while high contrast serifs give the letters their structure. The detail that holds it together is the ligatures: internal connective strokes, clearest in the N, I, E and R, link the characters the way woven silk holds a thread, so the name reads as one continuous piece. A construction frame shows the letters PA on black, with guide bars and notes on the straight serifs and the joins between letters.

Industry observers note that the moth mark works the same way. It is built from four overlapping geometric forms, a line drawing of the Bombyx mori silk moth, the living link between the Persian name Parnia, a canvas of fine silk, and the sculpted gold the house stands for.

Sources indicate that the system stays physical wherever it lands. The moth mark sits alone on a pale yellow tile, then repeats as a woven petal pattern in yellow, gold and tan. The brand book opens in a black ring binder: a yellow page carries the wordmark embossed into the paper, facing a white page headed "Essence of allure." The same yellow carries the shopping bag, the wordmark pressed into the paper along the bottom edge, with black ribbon handles.

It has been revealed that at street level the system scales up without changing its voice. A tall yellow banner hangs on a red brick building beside a panel with the moth mark, and the closing collage pulls the whole world together: moth tile, beaded gown detail, patterned envelope, shopping bag, the wordmark on yellow, and the line "Presence without the noise." It is a quiet luxury, stated once and left alone.
