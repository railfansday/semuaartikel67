Actor Michael C. Hall has reacted to how GTA 6 features references to his Miami-set TV show Dexter, in which he famously played the serial killer Dexter.

In a recent development, appearing on the Barstool Movies show, Hall noted he was "informed" of the Easter eggs in GTA 6 about his character. "I wasn't consulted," he said with a laugh. "It's pretty cool to feel like [Dexter] has folded into the zeitgeist.".
