enSights brings AI-powered business management to solar and storage operations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that as solar and energy storage portfolios grow in both size and complexity, managing performance is just one part of the equation. Owners and operators must also keep track of power purchase agreements (PPAs), warranties, service contracts, billing, compliance requirements and project-level profitability.

Recent findings suggest that enSights is looking to bring those responsibilities together with the launch of its AI Studio, a upcoming addition to its Energy Business Management (EBM) platform. The organization announced the launch Nov. 10.

The AI Studio is designed to give solar and storage owners, operators, and asset managers access to a customizable team of AI specialists that can support business and operational workflows. Through no-code tools, customers can create AI dashboards and reports using natural-language instructions, while still establishing their own workflows, routines, data-access permissions and approval requirements.

The approach here reflects a broader shift toward renewable energy software, it’s moving beyond monitoring individual assets toward managing the financial and operational performance of entire portfolios.

Industry observers note that according to enSights, its EBM platform brings together energy management systems (EMS), production data, billing, PPA information, operations and maintenance, contractual obligations and compliance on a unified data foundation. That integration is central to the firm’s AI strategy.

Rather than limiting AI applications to identifying equipment complications or answering generation-related questions, the platform is designed to connect energy production with the contracts and financial obligations associated with each project.

In a recent development, the AI Studio includes an orchestrator that coordinates specialized AI agents focused on areas like monitoring, operations, revenue, warranties, permits, lenders, insurers, and asset management. For portfolio managers, those capabilities could help reduce the time spent reviewing disconnected systems and manually reconciling information across departments. Automated reviews of contractual obligations, revenue performance and operational alerts could also help teams identify issues earlier and direct attention to matters that still require human intervention.

The enSights AI Studio dashboard provides solar and energy storage operators with AI-powered tools for asset management, operational workflows, and financial oversight. Credit: enSights.

It has been revealed that enSights emphasized that people remain in control of the AI Team. People determine which workflows the agents handle and where approvals are required. Physical asset controls remain governed by established EMS and site-level systems.

It has been revealed that the AI Studio also builds on an expanded financial management offering within the EBM platform. Its Financial Asset Management Module includes revenue assurance, contract, and obligation management, and profit-and-loss reporting.

The module is intended to provide project-level financial visibility, helping companies identify and prevent revenue losses while generating reports for investors.

The platform also includes real-time EMS, weather and tariff forecasting, along with dispatch planning intended to optimize revenue while maintaining grid safety and local compliance.

enSights indicates its platform is deployed globally across more than 100,000 sites, representing over 5 GW of assets. The company looks to demonstrate the AI Studio and its broader EBM platform at RE+ 2026.

As AI gains traction across the clean energy industry, the next competitive advantage could come from connecting operational intelligence with the financial and contractual decisions that determine how successfully renewable energy portfolios perform.
