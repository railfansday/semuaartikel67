Sixteen-year-old JJ Gabriel has declared his departure from Manchester United, two months on from stunning club officials with his desire to leave.

Gabriel and his representatives made a bombshell request to have his academy contract torn up in September. That request was immediately refused by United and, while there was faint hope that a compromise could have been found to keep him at Old Trafford, he has stayed away ever since.

He has been training in Madrid in recent days ahead of a move to Real Madrid, and has taken to social media to bid farewell to the Red Devils after six years at the club.

Recent findings suggest that gabriel’s announcement is thought to have caught United by surprise, with the club first learning about it when the video appeared on social media. It’s understood that United haven’t heard from the player’s family since he was last seen at Carrington in September.

Despite his clear intention to sign for Madrid, United’s stance hasn’t changed and they still believe it will require FIFA’s approval for any move to go through.

Industry observers note that 'Here I grew as a player and as a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award. You gave me moments I’ll always treasure.

'To every coach, member of staff, team-mate, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow. As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything. JJ.'.

Industry observers note that daily Mail Sport exclusively revealed Madrid's growing confidence in securing the free-scoring starlet back in September. Gabriel has now secured a European Union passport through his mother's Cypriot heritage, and sources familiar with the situation believe a switch is imminent.

It is understood Gabriel will play for Madrid's Castilla team, which plays in Spain's third division, as well as in the Youth League.

He has also played – and scored – for the Under-21 side.

Gabriel, who turned 16 last month, is one of the brightest young talents in the world and his exit represents a huge loss to United.
