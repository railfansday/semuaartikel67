EXCLUSIVE: Hot off their highly buzzed awards title, Wicker, which is approaching $40M at the global box office, Black Bear has grabbed North American in a competitive situation on They Know, the new psychological horror written, directed and starring 3x Emmy, WGA and DGA winner Bill Hader.

The movie reps Hader’s feature directorial debut.

They Know also stars Linda Cardellini, who recently won an Emmy in supporting actress limited series for HBO’s DTF St. Louis.

Footage for the pic was shown off by global sales agent FilmNation Entertainment at the new TIFF film market with word that buyers were already circling. They Know is based off an idea from Hader and his Barry collaborator Duffy Boudreau, and follows a divorced dad who becomes increasingly suspicious and paranoid that his ex-wife’s new mysterious partner is exerting a strange, unsettling influence on their children. Additional details about the plot are being kept under wraps. Watch on Deadline.

Bob Graf and Hader produced under the latter’s Hanarply banner, with Alyssa Donovan co-producing. MRC fully bankrolled the film, which is at the moment in post-production, making this the second collaboration between Black Bear and MRC, following the studio’s recent acquisition of Honeymoon/Funeral out of the Toronto International Film Festival.

According to recent reports, “Bill Hader is a singular talent both in front of and behind the camera, and he has delivered a film in They Know unlike anything we’ve ever seen,” remarked Michael Heimler, President of Production & Operations, Black Bear. “They Know is authentic, original and bold filmmaking at the highest level, and we couldn’t be more excited to partner with Bill and re-team with our great friends at MRC, who consistently deliver boundary-pushing films that help define their genre.”.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to Wicker, Black Bear has a Guy Ritchie’s Wife & Dog releasing in February, starring Academy Award nominees Benedict Cumberbatch and Rosamund Pike and Academy Award winner Anthony Hopkins. There’s also David Leitch’s Jason Statham Stole My Bike in August, starring Jason Statham and Hannah Waddingham; Nahnatchka Khan’s Honeymoon / Funeral, starring Rebecca Ferguson and Greta Lee; A Woman in the Sun, starring Academy Award winners Renée Zellweger and Sissy Spacek in the directing debut of WGA Award nominee Julia Cox, who also wrote the screenplay; Jonah, starring Greta Lee and Cooper Hoffman in the feature directorial debut of Tony winner Danya Taymor; and Atmosphere, written and directed by Anna Boden & Ryan Fleck, based on the bestselling book by Taylor Jenkins Reid.

MRC is behind the recently released The Only Living Pickpocket in New York which is right now in theaters via Sony Pictures Classics, The Love Hypothesis, and Warner Bros’ Wuthering Heights ($242M at the global B.O.), as well as American Fiction, Knives Out, Saltburn, Fair Play, All of You , and G20. Its upcoming films include The Gallerist, A Place in Hell, The Best Is Yet to Come, and Eloise. MRC is also known for hit TV series such as Poker Face, Terminal List, Ted, Ozark, and House of Cards.

Hader is represented by UTA and Toczek Suddleson Law group.
