Otherway reworks The Whisky Exchange brand identity around a lighter twin-lion crest, vibrant purple, and layered print full of discovery.

The lions come first. Otherway kept the twin-lion crest at the heart of the brand and redrew it lighter and more fluid, so a heraldic mark that could have felt ceremonial now feels loose and alive. Around it, the whole system runs on one bold decision: a vibrant purple that holds everything together, a clear break from the dark greens and browns fine spirits usually hide behind. A substantial flared serif carries the line WHERE CURIOSITY KNOWS NO BOUNDS across the posters, while tasting cards stack in layers of orange, pink, blue and deep purple, borrowing the stamped edges and layered print of the labels themselves.

It has been revealed that type does the talking in three distinct voices. The headline serif is set huge, its flared strokes and sharp terminals giving even a single line the weight of a poster. The tasting cards switch to a small mono face, all even capitals, for the practical information: dates, prices, and flavour notes that read GINGER, APRICOT, ROSEMARY, COFFEE, GRAPE down the card. The manifesto panel drops to a plain sans on periwinkle blue, set as one calm block of copy. The grid behind it all is the stack itself. Cards overlap like printed labels, their scalloped edges and offset corners doing the work a border would do, and the Staff Picks poster splits its wordmark top and bottom around a single bottle, dead center.

The system proves itself on real surfaces. The lions leave the page as a gold blade sign, hung on the stone shopfront, and the same mark sits in deep purple at the top of the window vinyl. FINE SPIRITS from EVERY CORNER of the GLOBE runs down the glass in the same flared serif, with the shop's own line beneath it. Stamps and label textures layer across the cards and posters, and the identity carries through from bags to store, every detail considered, as rich and varied as the spirits themselves.
