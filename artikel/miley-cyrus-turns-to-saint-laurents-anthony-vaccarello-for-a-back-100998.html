Miley Cyrus Turns to Saint Laurent’s Anthony Vaccarello for a Backless Wedding Dress

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that miley Cyrus is a bride—a Saint Laurent bride, that is. Yesterday, the “Wrecking Ball” singer married longtime partner Maxx Morando at an intimate, flower-covered Palm Springs ceremony.

According to recent reports, in new photos shared directly by Cyrus, the woman of the hour was seen wearing a timeless white gown, designed by Anthony Vaccarello for Saint Laurent. The whole dress was crafted from silk, and featured long sleeves, an open plunging back, and a train that pooled at her feet. Styled by Bradley Kenneth, it was paired with a matching set of silk slingback heels.

It has been revealed that the decision to work with Saint Laurent was not unexpected, given Cyrus’s long-standing partnership with the house. Most recently, the star was seen in Vaccarello’s designs at the 2026 Golden Globes and during her Hollywood Bowl shows in October. The wedding dress, however, was likely one of the creative director’s final big moments with the label, as his departure was announced last month.

For glam, Cyrus went bold, styling her hair in an updo and giving her eyes a smokey shadow. Her stack of diamonds was the perfect blend of classic glamour and rockstar edge (the best of both worlds, if you will), with drop Chopard earrings, studs, and a crawler that curved upward along the ear.

According to recent reports, morando, too, wore Saint Laurent. Sitting next to his wife on a leather chair, he was seen in a black single-breasted tuxedo with a silk-satin tie. He added in his own rockstar edge with a pair of grained-leather sneakers and sunglasses.

A first look at the ceremony, obtained exclusively by DeuxMoi, revealed the couple—who first started dating back in 2021, after being set up on a blind date—standing amongst white roses. The nuptials took place at Frank Sinatra’s former home, with Flaming Lips frontman Wayne Coyne reportedly serving as officiant. All of the guests appeared to be wearing black, including Cyrus’s parents, Tish and Billy Ray, and their respective partners, Dominic Purcell and Elizabeth Hurley.
