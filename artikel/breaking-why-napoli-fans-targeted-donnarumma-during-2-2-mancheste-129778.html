Gianluigi Donnarumma was the subject of insulting chants from the travelling supporters during Napoli’s 2-2 draw away against Manchester City in the Champions League earlier this week in a continuation of a feud that has been going on since 2018.

Donnarumma was in goal for Man City in their 2-2 draw at home against Napoli and told reporters after full-time that he was not best pleased at chants directed towards his mother from the travelling Partenopei supporters inside The Etihad.

According to recent reports, donnarumma was born in Naples and several of his family members are Napoli supporters. This is part of the reason why there is a long-standing feud between the Italy national team goalkeeper and the Napoli supporters.

According to recent reports, back in 2018, Donnarumma was in goal for Milan when they held Napoli to a 0-0 draw in San Siro during the 2017-18 season run-in. Donnarumma made a crucial late save to deny Arkadiusz Milik to keep the scores at 0-0, which was costly for Maurizio Sarri’s Napoli side, who missed out on the Serie A title by four points that season.

After that game, Donnarumma took to social media to mock his uncle, a Napoli supporter, ‘apologising’ for his late intervention before letting off an expletive in his native Neapolitan dialect.

That video did not go down well with the Napoli supporters, who have never forgiven the Italy national team captain and still hold a grudge over eight-and-a-half years later.
