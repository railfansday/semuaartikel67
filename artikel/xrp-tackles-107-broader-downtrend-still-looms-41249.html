XRP could rebound toward $1.06–$1.07 as tentative signs of a bottom emerge, according to Cilinix.

Recent findings suggest that XRP may have room to recover toward $1.06 and $1.07, according to the host of Cilinix Crypto, whose latest XRP analysis points to tentative signs of a bottom. The forecast is conditional: renewed weakness in equities could instead send the token back toward $0.90 and $0.85.

The distinction matters. The host sees enough improvement to support a short-term rebound, but not enough to call an end to the larger downtrend. “The higher timeframe structure is still very bad,” the analyst remarked.

It has been revealed that the technical argument rests partly on XRP reclaiming its seven-day rolling volume-weighted average price, or VWAP, after a period of declines. The host noted that earlier recoveries above that measure had preceded upward moves, while cautioning that the indicator does not always carry much significance.

XRP had also moved back into the developing monthly value area, with an initially favorable reaction. Together, those developments gave the analyst more confidence that the market could stabilize and gradually push higher.

It has been revealed that there is a weakness in that setup, however. XRP broke above a previous swing high but swiftly fell back below it and had not reclaimed it at the time of the analysis. The host described that part of the structure as “slightly bearish.”.

Funding rates offered another supporting signal. They turned sharply negative around the recent lows before becoming positive again, which the analyst viewed as consistent with a potentially defended bottom, not confirmation of a lasting reversal.

Industry observers note that the broader backdrop kept the host cautious. Oil prices remained low and the volatility index had opened lower, but the S&P 500 was still near its lows.

Cilinix also cited a lack of ETF inflows as one reason not to adopt an XRP outlook that ran against the wider market.

Sources indicate that the $1.06–$1.07 area is therefore a potential recovery zone, not a confirmed breakout target. Cilinix would become more conservative there because of the weak higher-timeframe structure.
