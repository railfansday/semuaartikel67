After 15 games of the League Two season, the Millers are in the relegation zone.

But they have a win and a draw from their last two matches, and a two-goal victory at home to the Latics on Saturday would see the pair swap places, and Rotherham potentially leapfrogging others.

"I think the last two performances have been something to build on – the Bromley away performance (an FA Cup win to end a ten-match winless streak) and then Grimsby," commented Millers coach Bruce.

Sources indicate that "We've drawn too many games (seven in the league). A lot of those draws we know very easily could have been wins but it's easy for me to sit here and say that.

"Try to stay positive with it is the message to the players.

"I speak to managers all the time after games and they say, 'You've been a bit unlucky here.'.

Recent findings suggest that "But I don't want to make that excuse. I want to start getting results and I think the players do as well. My message is stay positive and the results will turn.".

Asked what he see which gives him that confidence, the former Leeds United and Hull City defender replied: "Togetherness, a hunger to get out of the situation we're in. We know we need a good week to ten days, which can easily happen over the Christmas period with the amount of fixtures.

"It's just about creating positivity, picking out the things we need to improve on, the things we need to keep doing, and just keep hammering home that message.

It has been revealed that "When you want to be successful at any football club you need everyone pulling in the right direction. That means players digging each other out, demanding better standards.

"If it gets to that point and we're all on the same page with that, that will be good.".

Kian Spence has served a suspension and is one of a number of players back ahead of a busy time in the English footballing calendar, but Bruce will be mindful of how he juggles his resources.

"Mulls (top-scorer Paul Mullin) has had a bit of a back issue but he should be okay," he remarked.

"I've got players coming back from injury and suspension and we've got Saturday-Tuesday, with Barnet away, so I will pick a team for both games and look to try and freshen things up to keep a level of freshness about the starting XI.".
