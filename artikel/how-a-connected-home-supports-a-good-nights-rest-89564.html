How a Connected Home Supports a Good Night’s Rest

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As a certified holistic health coach and wellness content creator, Carolina Salazar knows the importance of a peaceful wind-down routine before bed. She trusts Hubspace from The Home Depot that powers many of the smart devices in her home, to help ease her into a good night’s sleep.

Hubspace, The Home Depot’s exclusive smart home platform, powers more than 250 products across select private brands—like Hampton Bay, Husky, and Vissani—as well as national brand partners. It effortlessly connects everyday essentials, including smart security, indoor and outdoor lighting, appliances, switches, watering controls, and more.

Industry observers note that some of the Hubspace app’s most practical features help users personalize lighting and improve home security. Hubspace Connect, for example, lets you control compatible lighting from a single switch, while Vacation Mode is a security feature that turns lights and smart plugs on and off while you’re away. One of Salazar’s favorite features is Circadian Rhythm, which automatically transitions lights from cooler, whiter tones to warmer ones as the day goes on.

“I can set my lights to follow my circadian rhythm, so they shift automatically throughout the day,” says Salazar. “And when they start to turn warm in the evening, that’s my cue to slow it down.” That can mean cozying up with a book, brewing a cup of hot tea, or, in her case, lighting a candle at her bedside and journaling. When Salazar is finished putting her thoughts to paper, she uses the Hubspace app to adjust the color, temperature, and brightness of her lights, dimming them as she gets closer to sleep. This can even be programmed automatically in order to reduce screen time before bed.

“Colder rooms bring better sleep, so I also have Hubspace turn on my AC at night,” she notes.

Sources indicate that the Hubspace app is free to download and easily syncs with your home’s smart devices. You can enable voice control for screen-free commands—also a plus if you’re trying to reduce your pre-sleep screen time. And the app runs on both WiFi and Bluetooth—so even if the WiFi is down, your nighttime routine can still run seamlessly.
