Star Wars: Galactic Racer debuted on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC last month, and since then, players have taken a liking to Biddy Blas, completed hundreds of podraces, and more. More content is on the way, according to developer Fuse Games, which has revealed an upcoming "Speed Legends" patch coming sometime next month.

Recent findings suggest that more specifically, the free Speed Legends patch will include private lobbies, input re-binding, two new podracers, eight podracing Scenarios, and more. This update also marks the first premium content: the M-68 Landspeeder Icon vehicle from Solo: A Star Wars Story and three premium speeders from Star Wars: The Mandalorian.

Industry observers note that unfortunately, there's no release date for the patch yet; just word that it's coming in December. Fortunately, that's not too long of a wait.
