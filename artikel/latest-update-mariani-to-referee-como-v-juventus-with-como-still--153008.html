Latest Update: Mariani to referee Como v Juventus, with Como still without a win under him

It has been revealed that maurizio Mariani of the Aprilia section will take charge of Como v Juventus at the Sinigaglia, a direct fight for the Champions League places. Como have never won with him in charge, and his recent record has brought friction with Inter and with Francisco Conceição.

Maurizio Mariani of the Aprilia section will referee Como v Juventus at the Sinigaglia on Sunday 29 November at 20:45. The AIA named Peretti and Perrotti as his assistants. Marinelli will be fourth official. Pezzuto is on VAR, with Meraviglia as AVAR.

Como have never won with Mariani in charge. For Juventus, the appointment comes in a direct fight for the Champions League places. Spalletti’s side are sixth and Como fifth, so a win at the Sinigaglia would close the gap on a direct rival.

Mariani’s name came up last season in Milan prosecutors’ wiretaps in the so-called ‘Arbitropoli’ case, Tuttosport reported. Those conversations discussed referees stated to be welcome or unwelcome to Inter. Inter president Beppe Marotta also strongly criticised a penalty Mariani gave Napoli against Inter on 25 October 2025. He called it a ‘rigorino’, meaning a soft penalty.

The Juventus link is more direct. Francisco Conceição has had a goal disallowed by Mariani. The paper calls the referee a nightmare for the Portuguese winger. That disallowed goal was not the only troubled meeting between the two.

Both video officials will also work the night before. Pezzuto is assistant VAR for Torino v Lazio on Saturday 28 November at 18:00. Meraviglia runs the VAR for Inter v Genoa later that evening.

After 12 rounds, Como have won eight, drawn two and lost two. They have scored 20 goals and conceded 10. Juventus have won six, drawn three and lost three, with a 19-12 goal record. Spalletti has called the Como game a key moment after the win over Omonia.

Como will come into Sunday’s game with two more days of rest than Juventus. Spalletti countered that players returning from injury let him initiative for the Como game as well. Before the Omonia game, Nico González and Kalulu had not recovered, along with the long-term absentees.
