Recent findings suggest that some nail colors feel synonymous with a certain time of year; others are timeless enough to transcend the seasons. When it comes to winter, we just can’t get enough of deep burgundies, chocolate browns, and hues of cherry red, which are dominating our timelines—and our manicures—right now.

And you only have to look to Jennifer Lopez to know we’re on the right path. The 57-year-old is great at signaling the latest colors for your fingertips, from her “chai latte” nails back in September to her recent frosted tips. But she really has hit the winter nail on the red with a recent appearance, which saw the singer and actor dressed in head-to-toe burgundy.

According to recent reports, during a recent outing, Lopez paired a sheer pussy-bow blouse with satin mini shorts and lace tights, all in complementary shades of the deep red color. She finished the look with a leather trench coat—and nails in the perfect so-deep-they’re-almost-purple burgundy to match.

The good news is, for anyone wondering exactly what shade she’s wearing, her manicurist Tom Bachik has since shared an Instagram post with credits.

Bachik shared that he used the Shellac shade “Black Cherry” by CND to create Lopez’s dark red nails. Lopez isn’t the only one to try the burgundy nail trend—Anne Hathaway also stepped out in “cherry noir” nails recently. It couldn’t be a better shade to try ahead of the holidays. Opt for it at your next gel nail appointment, or try a similar color in regular nail polish below.
