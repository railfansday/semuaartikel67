When it comes to the iPhone Pro's frame, Apple has historically gone back and forth between aluminum and titanium.

In a recent development, the iPhone 15 Pro models, introduced in 2023, as well as iPhone 16 Pro, had a titanium frame, but Apple went back to aluminum unibody in the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro. However, the iPhone Air and foldable iPhone Duo still have titanium frames, likely due to the material's lighter weight and better structural strength when compared to aluminum.

The company also uses ceramic parts on some of its devices, notably the Apple Watch Ultra and some variants of the Apple Watch Series 12.

According to recent reports, now, two rumors claim Apple is going to use both these materials in the upcoming iPhone 20 Pro. A leaker called Fixed Focus Digital (via MacRumors) claims that Apple intends to manufacture parts of the phone's mid-frame using 3D-printed titanium. This does not necessarily mean the entire frame will be made of titanium, but it could improve the frame's strength and durability.
