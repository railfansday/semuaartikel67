Why molecular cage catalysts show enormous differences in performance

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, chemistry researchers have developed a new way to identify and understand highly effective artificial enzyme-like catalysts using powerful "molecular cages" that mimic the behavior of nature's enzymes. These tiny, hollow molecular structures act like miniature reaction chambers to trap molecules and accelerate chemical reactions. The cages highlighted enormous differences in performance, with the best catalysts more than 10,000 times more active than the least effective ones.
