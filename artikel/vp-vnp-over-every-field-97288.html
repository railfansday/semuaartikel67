📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, for every field, circuits for the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial, and for the permanent outside characteristic 2, need more than m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) gates for all large m. The same holds for approximating circuits. So VP is a proper subset of VNP, and VNP is not contained in the closure of VP. This does not settle P vs NP.

Sources indicate that the result was discovered by an internal research Claude model. This folder holds the paper and its Lean 4 formalization. Both were written by AI models, and no human mathematician has checked the proof.

Research artifact. Not maintained and not accepting contributions.

It has been revealed that paper.pdf: VP ≠ VNP over every field, by Claude (Anthropic). The full proof is its appendix.

Industry observers note that 1. Read the statement. Statement.lean states all of the above and imports only Mathlib. Lean checks the proofs, but no machine checks that this file states what is claimed. Appendix Q of paper.pdf lists the Lean counterparts of the numbered statements (Table 3) and says where they differ.
