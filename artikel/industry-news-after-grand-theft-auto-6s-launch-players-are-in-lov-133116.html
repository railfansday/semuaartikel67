It has been revealed that with Grand Theft Auto 6 launching late last night (unless you're in Fresh Zealand, in-person or otherwise), players have been flocking to online spaces to share their favourite moments. Industry observers note that the game, a vast open world in which crime and violence may in fact pay, obviously allows players to get into brawls with the denizens of Vice City. While this can of course be brutal, many of these scuffles have a capacity for grand comedy.
