Search for "pet affiliate program" and you find dozens of articles repeating the same line: "commissions up to 40%". None of them takes you to the document that actually defines the rate.

Industry observers note that I went to the document. The Amazon Associates Standard Commission Rules are public and list, category by category, what the associate earns per qualified purchase. Read on 16/11/2026:.

Besides percentages, there are fixed-value reward events: a Prime free trial pays R$ 9, Kindle Unlimited R$ 15, Amazon Music Unlimited R$ 10, and an Amazon Card subscription R$ 30.

Recent findings suggest that none of this is an estimate. It is a table, with a reading date.

Industry observers note that pet Shop at 11% means: a R$ 200 sale of food and accessories returns R$ 22. A R$ 500 bed returns R$ 55. To reach one thousand reais of commission in a month, you need to move roughly R$ 9,000 in sales in that category.

That is the calculation nobody does before writing the guide. Forty percent sounds like ten times more, and the gap between 11% and 40% is not an optimization, it is a broken expectation.

According to recent reports, the problem is not the commission, the link or the offer. It is at the top: not enough people arrive. A latest guide on a site with 58 human visits a month is text almost nobody reads. And 11% of zero sales is still zero.

That reorders the priorities of any affiliate project: first who reads, then what sells, and only then how much it pays.

Recent findings suggest that pages carrying affiliate links did not point to a product: they pointed to a search page (/s?k=term). Attribution still works, but search is a weak-intent page: the reader lands on a list with varying prices and picks something else.

A product link has a single price, reviews and a buy button on the same screen. For a comparison guide, that is the difference between "check the prices" and "buy this one".

It has been revealed that none of these four conclusions required a paid tool. They required reading the source, measuring our own funnel and accepting the number that showed up.
