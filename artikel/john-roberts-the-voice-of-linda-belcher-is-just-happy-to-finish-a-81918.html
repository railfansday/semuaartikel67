According to recent reports, you probably know John Roberts best as the voice of Linda Belcher on Bob’s Burgers. The show just wrapped up its 16th season and shows no signs of slowing down anytime soon. The show remains a reliable source of laughs, top-notch puns, and frankly wholesome fun.

According to recent reports, comedy is just part of Roberts’ life, however. In addition to his appearances on Gravity Falls and Comedy Bang! Bang!, he’s also a musician with deep roots in New York City’s queer nightlife scene. He just released his latest record, I Am, on Friday, and it dabbles in ’80s pop, ’90s house, and even dashes of ’00s rave. But while the record sounds made for the dance floor, it actually tackles some pretty heavy subject matter, like mortality, aging, and grief following the loss of his mother.

Despite that, Roberts is still having fun, as you can see in the video for “Complicated,” which revels in the queer cult camp of Scorpio Rising, William Friedkin’s Cruising, and The Wolf Man.
