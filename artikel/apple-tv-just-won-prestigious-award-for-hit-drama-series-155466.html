Apple TV (now $6.99/month for Black Friday) won significant at this year’s Emmy Awards thanks to Widow’s Bay, and this week at the International Emmys, the streamer triumphed again as Carême won the top Drama Series prize.

Apple TV had a record-breaking year at the Emmy Awards this fall, with 29 wins overall—beating HBO as the most-awarded network.

Its success was largely driven by Widow’s Bay, though the excellent Pluribus and Margo’s Got Money Troubles added wins too. But it seems the streamer’s Emmy success isn’t over.

Earlier this week at the International Emmy Awards in New York City, Apple TV won another prestigious prize: Carême claimed the award for top Drama Series.

Apple TV’s acclaimed and provocative French hit “Carême” won Drama Series, marking the second time Apple has landed the coveted award following a win in 2024 for “Drops of God.” Recently renewed for a second season and currently filming in Paris, “Carême” stars César Award winner Benjamin Voisin and is directed by Martin Bourboulon.

In a recent development, carême is based on the book “Carême: The First Celebrity Chef” by Ian Kelly. It premiered on Apple TV last spring and was acclaimed by critics, garnering a 95% Rotten Tomatoes score.

The show follows the story of Antonin Carême, who “rose from humble beginnings in Paris to the height of culinary stardom in Napoleon’s Europe.” Per Apple: “While he dreams only of becoming the most famous chef in the world, his talent and ambitions attract the attention of renowned and powerful politicians who use him as a spy for France.”.

Season 2 of Carême was announced earlier this fall, and is expected to premiere in 2027.

I loved Carême’s first season and definitely recommend checking it out if you’re up for a French-language series.

Industry observers note that apple TV is available for just $6.99/ month (over 50% off) for Black Friday. The discounted price lasts for up to six months.
