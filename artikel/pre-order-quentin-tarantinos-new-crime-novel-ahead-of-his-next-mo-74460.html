A prolific screenwriter, director, and author, Quentin Tarantino's exciting latest book release is available for pre-order now, and fans of the filmmaker won't want to miss this one.

Sources indicate that quentin Tarantino's movies are known for their fast pace, bloody violence, and references to the best pulp films of the 20th century, generating a dedicated audience base that demonstrates up for his projects. In the coming weeks, the release of his next film will coincide with the publication of his newest book, and we can't wait to read and watch the action sequences and Easter Eggs sure to be included in both works.

In the past, Tarantino has authored plenty of companions to movies, and his next release is no exception. Leaning into the crime and mystery genres with the compelling backdrop of Hollywood in the 20th century, there's little question that Tarantino's book will be a great crime thriller novel to dive into during the holiday season.

It has been revealed that after the stunning success of Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, it didn't take long for a sequel to be planned and further explorations of the atmospheric world to hit not only our screens, but our bookstores. The companion novel The Adventures of Cliff Booth, penned by Tarantino, will be released on December 8th, just a few weeks after the premiere of the movie, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.

It's not the first time that Tarantino has authored a book within the world of his most popular films, and we can't wait to see what he has in store for fans.

Set in 1977, The Adventures of Cliff Booth brings Cliff and the reader right back into the clash between the glamorous sheen of Hollywood and the criminal underbelly that he finds himself drawn back into. Following the cobbled-together odd jobs and clean-up operations that Cliff takes on to make ends meet, The Adventures of Cliff Booth will take the reader to hidden corners of '70s Los Angeles, transporting the audience to Cliff's world.

The Adventures of Cliff Booth: A Novel is available for pre-order here.

Starring Brad Pitt, who will reprise his role in Once Upon a Time in Hollywood as the titular Cliff Booth, it won't be long before audiences can watch the sequel film. The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth will have a two-week run exclusively in select IMAX movie theaters beginning on November 25th, and then will be available to watch on Netflix on December 23rd.

Not every actor who appeared in Once Upon a Time in Hollywood joins the cast of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, as Leonardo DiCaprio will be noticeably absent. However, there are some exciting developments surrounding the film, along with the positive early reviews. Written by Tarantino and directed by David Fincher, the movie will explore the world established by the first film while distinguishing itself as a worthy successor.

Though Quentin Tarantino didn't direct the project, the movie is in safe hands with an accomplished director like Fincher. It will be interesting to see how The Further Mid-Adventures of Cliff Booth connects to and differs from the book, The Adventures of Cliff Booth: A Novel. As always, it will be worth watching and reading both projects to get the full picture of Cliff's story.
