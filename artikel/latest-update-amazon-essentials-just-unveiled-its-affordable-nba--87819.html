Recent findings suggest that the NBA season is in full swing—and so is the holiday season. NBA swag always makes for a solid, low-effort gift for fans of the Association, and we’re always on top of the latest collabs. The National Basketball Association’s latest venture, though, might be its most accessible to date, and it’s one we didn’t really see coming: Amazon Essentials x NBA.

Shop the Amazon Essentials x NBA Collection.

While it is an unexpected collab to some degree, it technically isn’t all that shocking; Amazon Prime has a contract with the NBA to stream over 60 NBA games this season, including the playoffs. The collection features affordably priced T-shirts, sweatshirts, long-sleeved shirts, and even pajamas. It’s a steep departure from some of the high-priced collabs rolled out over the last couple of years (we won’t name names). It’s so budget-friendly, in fact, that it's literally impossible to spend over $30 on a single item. I spent more than double that on a Knicks championship hat over the summer.

The collection isn’t gigantic (at least yet), but it covers all of the essentials—pun not intended. Not every NBA team is part of the collaboration early on, so we’re mostly looking at massive-market teams here (except, randomly, the Timberwolves). And yes, that includes the Knicks.

Its retro-inspired heavyweight tees give off 90s vibes, and for under $25, an absolute steal. You can nab it in a long-sleeve style for the exact same price—a rarity these days. There’s also this Oversized Crewneck Sweatshirt that’s a little simpler, and a Family Set of Pajamas that make a solid (and wallet-friendly) holiday gift. If you’re looking to get something for her, the styles come in women’s, too.

Sources indicate that we also highlighted a couple of pieces for the little ones, including a French Terry Crewneck Sweatshirt and a Soft Fleece Zip-Up Hoodie. All things considered, color us impressed.
