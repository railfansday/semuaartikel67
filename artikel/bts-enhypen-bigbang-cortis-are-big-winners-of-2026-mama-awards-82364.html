BTS, ENHYPEN, BIGBANG & CORTIS Are Big Winners of 2026 MAMA Awards

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, BTS, ENHYPEN, BIGBANG and CORTIS were the major winners of the 2026 MAMA Awards, which were held Friday and Saturday (Nov. 20-21) at the Kyocera Dome Osaka, in Japan. The awards streamed live worldwide on CJ ENM’s Mnet Plus platform.

BTS won artist of the year, best male group and album of the year for ARIRANG. They also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male and song of the year for “SWIM.”.

ENHYPEN won fans’ choice of the year, favorite MCD Stage and also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.

Industry observers note that both groups have topped the all-genre Billboard 200 album chart this year. BTS led for three weeks in April with ARIRANG. ENHYPEN was on top for one week in September with The Sin: Bliss.

According to recent reports, BIGBANG won music visionary of the year and best music video, and also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male. Taeyang of BIGBANG also won best dance performance male solo with “LIVE FAST DIE SLOW.”.

CORTIS won song of the year and best dance performance male group with “REDRED.” CORTIS also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.

Yeonjun of TOMORROW X TOGETHER won best male artist. The group also made the top 10 for fans’ choice male.

It has been revealed that soyeon of i-dle won best female artist. That group also made the top 10 for fans’ choice female.

ALPHA DRIVE ONE and TUIDE were co-winners for best newly introduced artist.

In a recent development, music released between Oct. 1, 2025, and Sept. 30, 2026 was eligible for this year’s awards.

Performers on night 1 included Street World Fighter: Directors’ War, ILLIT, Panic, ALLDAY PROJECT, ALPHA DRIVE ONE, BABYMONSTER, CL, ENHYPEN, Hearts2Hearts, INI, LNGSHOT, MODYSSEY, NCT WISH, RESCENE, TREASURE and TUIDE.

The following performers took the stage on night 2: &TEAM, NAYEON (TWICE), HWASA, BIGBANG, CORTIS, IVE, izna, KiiiKiii, KO1KEYZ, LE SSERAFIM, WOODZ and ZEROBASEONE.

Key winners from night 1 include:.

Fans’ Choice of the Year with Visa: ENHYPEN.

Worldwide KConers’ Choice: ZeroBaseOne.

Best Breakthrough Artist: NCT Wish.

Favorite MCD Stage with Mnet Plus: ENYPEN.

Visa Super Stage Artist: Treasure.

Q100 Favorite Mega Artist: NCT Wish.

Key winners from night 2 include:.

Music Visionary of the Year: BIGBANG.

Song of the Year: CORTIS, “REDRED”.

Best Song Top 10: ILLI – “It’s Me”; CORTIS – “REDRED”; HWASA, “Good Goodbye”; LE SSERAFIM – “SPAGHETTI (feat. j-hope of BTS)”; I.O.I – “Suddenly”; IVE – “BANG BANG”; aespa – “LEMONADE”; BTS – “SWIM”; Hearts2Hearts – “RUDE!”; NMIXX “Blue Valentine”.

Favorite Dance & Vocal Performance Group: ZEROBASEONE.
