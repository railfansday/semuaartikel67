A newly introduced report has listed the most valuable goalkeepers in world football.

The report comes from the CIES Football Observatory.

Only one of their eight most valuable goalkeepers doesn’t play for a Premier League club.

That is Joan García, the Barcelona keeper, who is at the very top of the list with an estimated value of €80.6 million (approx £67.4m).

CIES Football Observatory explain that they have looked at ‘the goalkeepers worldwide who right now have the highest estimated transfer value according to our statistical model.’ Their model taking also taking into account the age of the goalkeepers and how long left on their contracts, when establishing their valuations.

Sources indicate that they report: ‘Italy’s Gianluigi Donnarumma (€79.9 million) and Belgium’s Senne Lammers (€64.2 million) round off the top three, with a rising value in fourth place : Aston Villa’s Japanese Zion Suzuki (€59.5 million). Paris Saint-Germain’s and Russia’s Matvey Safonov is the second highest-valued goalkeeper outside the English Premier League (€50.8 million), ahead of Jonas Urbig (Bayern Munich, €50.0 million).’.

Interestingly, amongst the seven Premier League goalkeepers that feature in the top eight, they include Newcastle United and Sunderland goalkeepers….
