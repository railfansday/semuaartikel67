Willie Brown, a longtime California Democrat and a power broker in the state party, said former Vice President Kamala Harris lacks the communication skills needed to run for president again in 2028.

Although Harris remains one of the country’s most recognizable Democratic figures, Brown’s skepticism comes from a longtime political ally and casts doubt on her potential candidacy.
