West Ham had hoped things would get better under Scott Parker, but the newly introduced boss is yet to oversee his first win since replacing Nuno Espirito Santo. Things got worse for Parker and WHUFC on Tuesday night as they lost 1-0 to Portsmouth, making it six Championship matches without a win to leave them down in 13th.

The Hammers are the league’s top scorers, and whilst Jarrod Bowen is leading the way with seven goals and seven assists, one surprise goal source has been Konstantinos Mavropanos on six. Strikers are struggling to step up, and Parker was a fresh lead talisman in January.

Sources indicate that on the flip side, the story is different for Mark Robins‘ Stoke City. After a tough start, the Potters look like one of the most complete teams in the Championship. After winning with 10 men against Birmingham City last week, City backed it up with a 2-1 win over Millwall, thanks to a George Hirst brace.

There’s a lot to like about Stoke City, but what really catches the eye is just how well they hold on to a lead. In their last five games, they have taken the lead on four occasions and not dropped a single point.

Recent findings suggest that “West Ham look a side made up of individuals rather than a team. That’s the problem that Scott Parker has to solve if he is to mould the Hammers into a side like his other previous promotion-winning outfits. Doing that against high-flying Stoke City does not look like the place to start.

In a recent development, “Mark Robins has his Potters team flying and looking good value for their 2nd-place position. Stoke City are so hard to score against, and if they take the lead, it’s almost game over. This team look the real deal and are only going in one direction.

Industry observers note that “WHUFC have the quality to win this game, but not the togetherness. It will all come down to who scores first, and Stoke usually do.”.

Industry observers note that “Most people would have thought West Ham would be the side sat in 2nd, while Stoke City languish in 13th. However, with 17 games of the season down, WHUFC are struggling for form, while the Potters just can’t stop winning.

“Given the talent on offer, it does feel like a matter of when the Hammers get going, rather than if. With his pedigree in the Championship and stars like Jarrod Bowen on the books, Scott Parker will find a winning formula. That remarked, four defeats in a six-game winless run is understandably worrying supporters.

According to recent reports, “The current situation at Stoke could not be much more different. They are absolutely up on cloud nine at the moment, with five Championship wins on the trot lifting them into 2nd. The numbers might suggest a dip in form is coming, but when you’re tight at the back and have goals coming from multiple sources, you’re going to win games.

“The London Stadium is not a happy place at the moment, and with SCFC coming into the game in such confidence, you have to think the visitors win again with more misery ahead for Parker and co.”.
