Industry News: Texas reveals proposal for US Space Academy in Houston

It has been revealed that the Texas Space Commission has published a proposal for the newly created United States Space Academy. It would be located in Houston on a site alongside NASA‘s Johnson Space Center, which was formerly home to Mission Control.

Recent findings suggest that revealed earlier this week, the proposals show clusters of buildings arranged in a flower-like shape, with petals surrounded by water. The renders place the facility on a currently vacant site alongside the NASA facility.

The proposal was created as a response to a request for information from the American space agency, which is looking to find a permanent home for the academy.

It follows an executive order issued earlier this year by president Donald Trump that established a presidential commission to determine how the proposed NASA-led institution could be formed.

In the order, he stated that the United States Space Academy would be established with the aim of training the “next generation of astronauts, scientists, engineers, operators, entrepreneurs, civil servants, and warfighters”.

The proposed academy would contain facilities for academic and more hands-on training.

It has received bipartisan support in Texas, with Republican senator Ted Cruz and Democrat senator-elect James Talarico issuing a joint statement in support of the bid.

“Texas has maintained a leading role in America’s human spaceflight program for decades, from training astronauts to directing missions at Johnson Space Center,” noted the pair in the statement.

In a recent development, “The Lone Star State has developed an extensive space industry, infrastructure, and expertise. Texas is best suited to train the next generation of American space leaders and is the natural home for the U.S. Space Academy.”.

According to the Texas Space Commission, the scheme was designed to align with NASA’s proposed timeline, which envisions groundbreaking commencing next year.

NASA recently announced plans for a permanent moon base, with a multi-phased blueprint culminating in “semi-permanent habitation models”.
