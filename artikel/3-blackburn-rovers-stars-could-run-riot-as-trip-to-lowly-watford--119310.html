3 Blackburn Rovers stars could run riot as trip to lowly Watford awaits

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Blackburn Rovers head to managerless Watford tonight as they look to move into the Championship’s top half with a win. Under the temporary lead of Damien Johnson, the East Lancashire outfit come into the game in 13th after a 2-2 draw with rivals Burnley on Sunday.

The Hornets had hoped Edward Iordanescu would be in charge for the game, but with no confirmation of his appointment as of Wednesday morning, they look set to be led by an interim team fronted by Adrian Mariappa. It marks a tough test for the hosts, as Rovers have a string of dangerous attackers in their arsenal.

Rovers’ impressing attackers and Watford’s struggles at the back could see the away side take all three points back to Ewood Park with them.

First of all, we’ll look at the numbers behind Watford’s struggling defence.

Industry observers note that the Hornets have conceded 28.5 xG, ranking them 19th in the Championship (FotMob). Their actual goals-conceded tally sits at 27, and the consensus at Vicarage Road is that they would be a lot worse off if it weren’t for impressing goalkeeper Federico Ravaglia.

They have faced 256 shots this season, which averages out at 16 per game. This, combined with their xG conceded, goes to show just how many shooting opportunities Watford afford their opposition, and it will set Blackburn Rovers tongues wagging.

In Andri Gudjohnsen, the visitors have one of the most clinical strikers in Europe. His 11 Championship goals from 24 shots equate to a 45.83% conversion rate, and in the continent’s top five leagues, the only strikers who can better that are Stade Rennais’ Boulaye Dia (60%) and Carlos Espi of Real Madrid (50% – via FBref).

If Watford concede chances like they have been this season, Gudjohnsen will get on the scoresheet.

Second-top scorer Oladapo Afolayan will be one to watch because of this too. Not only has he scored five league goals, but he’s contributed two assists as well. Captain and creative maestro Todd Cantwell will be at the centre of all attacks, and he should hope to add to his assist tally.

Depending on how much he is able to play after his recent return from injury, Ryoya Morishita is one to watch for the away side as well.

Sitting in 13th compared to Watford in 20th, Blackburn Rovers are favourites to win here. They’ve got the attacking talent to expose a poor Hornets defence.

However, there are caveats. The departure of Alessio Dionisi could bring the cliched ‘upcoming manager bounce’ for Watford, and under the interim lead of Mariappa, expect them to be motivated. His past as a defender and his previously leadership role in the dressing room at Vicarage Road could help tighten them up at the back.

But, with the attacking stars at their disposal, Rovers can win here and win comfortably if they’re on their game.
