It has been revealed that bermudAir, the unusual boutique carrier that debuted operations a few years back, keeps getting stranger. The company has just confirmed a massive expansion for the summer of 2027, and with this, we’ll see the airline add all kinds of seemingly random routes to and from Bermuda, plus all kinds of routes that don’t touch Bermuda.

Whether you want to fly from Nashville to Ocho Rios, or from Fredericton to Bermuda, the airline now has you covered!

It has been revealed that for a little bit of background, in mid-2023 we saw the launch of BermudAir, a quirky niche airline with the goal of operating Embraer jets between Bermuda (BDA) and the United States and Canada. Initially the airline was going to operate all-business class planes, but it scrapped (“delayed”) that concept before launch, instead introducing a two-cabin concept (though business class just has blocked adjacent seats and better service).

As I mentioned from the beginning, the whole thing struck me as an odd venture, essentially trying to serve Bermuda year-round profitably, while going up against the major carriers that serve the airport.

Sources indicate that to my surprise, the airline is still in business over three years after launch, though it hasn’t necessarily been that smooth. As is pretty common for an airlines startup, the first years have been rocky, with several routes being cut after launch. For that matter, the airline faced a lawsuit over millions in unpaid airport fees at its hub, which isn’t exactly a great sign.

Several months back, BermudAir announced new winter 2026-2027 seasonal flying to places other than Bermuda. Now the airline has announced summer seasonal flying for 2027, and it’s arguably even stranger. The routes are all over the place.

Sources indicate that what’s interesting here is how the airline unveiled seems to be taking a similar strategy both for this winter and for next summer. So based on the fact that the airline is moving forward with its current winter schedule, maybe this is working? I don’t know….

Industry observers note that it’s also interesting how the airline actually got permission to operate these routes. As a reminder, Bermuda, Anguilla, and Turks & Caicos, are all British Overseas Territories, and Belize and Jamaica are former British Overseas Territory. How does the airline get permission to serve places like Honduras, though? I guess the airline is somehow getting permission to operate this as fifth freedom service, or something.

Recent findings suggest that bermudAir is expanding rapidly, as the airline has declared latest routes for the summer of 2027. Not only does this include several new routes to Bermuda (including out of Fredericton!), but the airline is also adding flying between the United States and Grand Cayman, Honduras, Jamaica, etc.

I think BermudAir deserves an award for the most unusual route network of any “real” airline. I certainly can’t think of any other airline in this “league.”.
