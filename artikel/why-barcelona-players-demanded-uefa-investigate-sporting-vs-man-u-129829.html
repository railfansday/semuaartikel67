Why Barcelona Players Demanded UEFA Investigate Sporting vs Man Utd Referee

Barcelona players called for UEFA to investigate Polish referee Szymon Marciniak, who oversaw Manchester United's incident-packed Champions League encounter with Sporting CP in Lisbon on Wednesday night, back in 2025.

The official had a number of major calls to make during United's game at the Jose Alvalade Stadium. In the first half alone, Marciniak awarded Sporting two penalties, while he also disallowed a Red Devils goal from Amad Diallo.

In the second period, he handed Mauricio Pochettino's side a spot-kick before later disallowing the away team a late winner, controversially ruling out goalkeeper Kaique Pereira's own goal.

This decision was deemed correct by rules expert Christina Unkel, although many felt United were hard done by.

After the game, former Watford striker Troy Deeney described Marciniak's performance as 'terrible', claiming the official made several mistakes throughout.

This isn't the first time the FIFA-licensed referee has found himself in the limelight for the wrong reasons.

Back in 2025, Marciniak oversaw Barcelona's Champions League semi-final second-leg clash with Inter Milan at the San Siro.

The Catalan giants lost 4-3 in northern Italy, meaning they crashed out of Europe's elite competition 7-6 on aggregate, as Inter progressed to the final.

Hansi Flick's players were incensed by a number of Marciniak's decisions during the chaotic affair, most notably when the Pole refused to award Barcelona a penalty when Inter defender Francesco Acerbi appeared to handle the ball in the box.

Acerbi struck the ball against his own hand whilst trying to clear, and VAR agreed with the referee that this couldn't be considered a punishable handling offence.

This didn't prevent Barcelona's players from feeling aggrieved, while they were also frustrated by the awarding of Inter's penalty in the first half, when Pau Cubarsi was adjudged to have fouled Lautaro Martinez.

On top of these contentious calls, Marciniak also overturned a penalty appeal when Henrikh Mkhitaryan tripped Lamine Yamal, while Barcelona also felt that there was a foul by Denzel Dumfries on Gerrard Martin in the lead-up to Acerbi's 93rd-minute equaliser.

This succession of dubious decisions prompted many Blaugrana players to take aim at the referee.

It has been revealed that pedri called for UEFA to investigate the official (via the Telegraph): “It’s not the first time that this has happened to us with this referee; UEFA should look at it. There are things that I don’t understand, and they are complicated to explain.

It has been revealed that "All 50-50s are for them. In the penalty to Lamine [Yamal], which was then a foul, he did not give Mkhitaryan a yellow card, which would have been the second.”.

Inigo Martinez chimed in: "Everyone has seen today that the decisions fell on the same side, but I don’t want this to be talked about. In the end, he [the referee] had to make decisions and he made them that way. It’s a shame.".

"We all know what happened with this refereeing team the last time we came to play in the Champions League against Inter at San Siro," Eric Garcia added.

"We have to be proud of how we competed, especially against external factors.”.

According to recent reports, barcelona's players weren't the only ones infuriated by Marciniak's performance that night at the San Siro. Spanish media were outraged, with newspaper Mundo Deportivo accusing the 45-year-old of being a 'Madridista'.

“Flick’s team overcame Inter’s initial 2-0 and the scandalous refereeing of the ‘Madridista’, who deprived Barca of a penalty for hands and whistled a very rigorous one against," - Mundo Deportivo wrote after the game.

Meanwhile, Flick was also scathing of Marciniak's display.

“Every decision that was 50-50 went [Inter’s] way," the German said.

"I am disappointed, but not with my team. I don’t like talking about the referee... I’ve told him what I think, but I’m not going to say here what I’ve said.".
