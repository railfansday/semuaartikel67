Oh Infinity Nikki, how I wish I had more time for you!

I loved this game when it came out, but there are only so many hours in the day, aren’t there? Even I can’t play everything.

This fresh v3.0 update, due out on 26th November, looks absolutely gorgeous, though.

The headline is the addition of a cute little pink car for the eponymous character to drive, but she’ll also be visiting an entirely new city as part of the story, named Cicia.

There’s so much cool stuff here: Nikki’s getting muskets as a new weapon (freakin’ awesome!), as well as a magic staff. In fact, there’s a touch of Hogwarts to some of the new locations and gameplay on display.

Obviously I’m absolutely adoring GTA 6 at the moment, but this is such a huge contrast, with its feel-good locales and emphasis on pretty dresses.

I’m probably going to cave and revive my save in this over the weekend, because it looks like everyone who logs in will be getting free outfits for Nikki’s birthday on 1st December.

Sources indicate that are you guys still playing this? I know there were a fair few fans among our community.
