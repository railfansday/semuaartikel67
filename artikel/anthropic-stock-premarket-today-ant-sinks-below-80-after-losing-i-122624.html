📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic was trading at $78.16 before Wednesday’s opening bell, a fall of $3.74, or 4.57%, from Tuesday’s finish at $81.90. Tuesday marked the first session in which the AI developer ended below the $83 at which it sold shares to investors last week. During that day, the stock swung from a low of $75.61 to a high of $83.49, with roughly 311.5 million shares changing hands. Few recent mega-listings have lost their offer price this swiftly. Yahoo Finance data on the dozen largest US IPOs since 2008 shows only Uber, on day one, and Facebook, on day two, crossed below sooner. Arm and SK hynix lasted six sessions, and SpaceX lasted 22. Everyday investors have not stepped back, buying a net $100 million or so on each of the past two trading days, Vanda Research figures show. Broader pressure has outweighed that demand. Nvidia lost 5.86% on Tuesday and the Nasdaq shed 1.4%, as questions grew over whether companies building advanced AI models can turn reliable profits.
