Jennifer Lopez Pours Us a Straight Dose of Holiday Cheer With Her Parade of Festive Fashion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jennifer Lopez will never enter a room quietly. The “Proof of Love” singer is one for maximalist luxury—with her collection of Hermès bags, sky-high stilettos, shimmery catsuits, and fur coats—and now that the air has turned crisp, she’s doing holiday dressing the J.Lo way.

Recent findings suggest that this week, the superstar has been in Fresh York City promoting her concert special “The JLo Show: Live in Las Vegas” which premieres on November 26, as well as her new holiday collection of JLo Beauty Beso Balms. And if anyone knows how to dress for a promotional tour, it’s Lopez.

On Sunday, she kicked things off strong with a head-to-toe crimson look. A sheer candy apple-red pussy-bow blouse was tucked into silky matching shorts and layered under a cherry leather trench. For even more drama, Lopez slipped into a pair of red flower-print tights and rouge snakeskin pumps with golden stilettos. A Tom Ford alligator clutch in a deep apple red added the final monochrome touch, while diamond jewelry brought the shimmer to the Christmas-core ensemble.

It has been revealed that also on Sunday, Lopez changed into a sandy suede Retrofête minidress and chocolate pumps for an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. And she did several festive quick changes while on stage for the “Wig Swap Holiday Lounge Singers” bit.

According to recent reports, today, to attend Sirius XM’s Front Row, J.Lo wore a very different, but equally holiday-appropriate look. Leaning intro the traditional colors of the Christmas season—red and green—Lopez wore a sheer green top tucked into sheer burgundy tights and layered it all with mixed-metal chain belts.

An embellished cinnamon-brown jacket pulled the look together. And for a dose of jewel tones, Lopez popped on a pair of suede grape-colored heels (which matched her underwear) and carried a satin turquoise bag.

A shaggy black faux-fur coat from Auter kept her warm as she stepped outside the studio.

Most recently, on Instagram, the Office Romance star debuted what is maybe her most festive look of the week. A fluffy white fur coat from Valentino that was belted at the waist with a black leather bow and paired with black accessories: tights, pointy heels, a clutch, and oversize sunglasses. Oh, and don’t forget the extravagant diamond jewelry from the Back Vault x David Webb.

It seems it’s time to get out that holiday wardrobe, and we all better step it up.
