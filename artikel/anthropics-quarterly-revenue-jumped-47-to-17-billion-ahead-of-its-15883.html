📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic is set to price its IPO Wednesday and begin trading Thursday. The stage is set for what could be one of this year's most notable stock market events.

The highly anticipated IPO isn't just an vital moment for the organization and its shareholders -- it also represents a test of the health of the broader artificial intelligence (AI) trade.

It has been revealed that AI stocks have been volatile recently due to macroeconomic dynamics, funding concerns, and news related to the resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang -- and the market's appetite for Anthropic's IPO could provide a valuable indicator of what investors can expect in valuation moves within the category. While it's impossible to say with certainty exactly where AI stocks will go in the near term, it's clear that there is strong appetite for Anthropic's technologies and services.

According to recent reports, in the lead-up to its IPO, Anthropic filed an amended S-1 form with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) that added a breakdown of its business performance to supplement its previously disclosed results across the first half of the year. Strikingly, Anthropic's revenue rose from $11.6 billion in the second quarter to roughly $17 billion in the third quarter -- representing approximately 47% sequential growth.

Sources indicate that the Q3 revenue numbers show rapid scaling for service sales from the tech giant's Claude software suite. For context, the company recorded roughly $16.3 billion in revenue across this year's first two quarters. Sales in the third quarter alone exceeded the combined revenue from the first and second quarters.

The performance suggests that demand for the tech giant's tech continues to surge -- and that Anthropic is pulling monetization levers that are driving greater spending from its clients. Along those lines, the company's most recent enhancement stated that the artificial intelligence business now had more than 11,900 customers generating at least $100,000 in revenue over the past 12 months.

NASDAQ · As of 9:55 AM ET 11/17/26.

Industry observers note that with the rapid scaling of Anthropic's Claude models, there are clear winners in the artificial intelligence hardware and services space. The most obvious and direct example is Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +1.78%), which has a massive contract to provide compute to the frontier AI model leader. According to Anthropic's S-1 filing, the firm has a deal in place to purchase up to $120.5 billion in compute from SpaceX through 2029. Either side can cancel on 90 days' notice after early 2027, but it's a substantial agreement.

It has been revealed that leading hardware providers that source the key components that power SpaceX's compute and other data centers are also poised to win business as Anthropic scales. These companies include Nvidia, Micron, and Broadcom. So while Anthropic's IPO will likely have important near-term implications for valuations in the AI space, the extent of the manufacturer's long-term scaling will ultimately be far more important for buy-and-hold investors.
