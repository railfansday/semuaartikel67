With Zcash (ZEC -2.76%) falling to $922 during its first big drop below $1,000 since September, it's natural for investors to wonder whether it's time to buy the dip, especially considering that the coin is still up 73% this year so far.

Price action alone doesn't make for a good investment thesis, so let's take a closer look at this privacy coin and determine whether it's worth buying right now, despite the turbulence and uncertainty in the crypto market and the world.

Zcash's recent decline from its highs is to be expected, given the bumps in the road it has faced.

Recent findings suggest that for instance, on Oct. 29, hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance, the largest crypto exchange, marking the largest-ever crypto theft and worsening the climate of fear that has been intensifying since a spate of other crypto hacks earlier in the year. Similarly, when October's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data came in above market forecasts, it sparked a flight from risky assets like crypto amid expectations that the Federal Reserve will hike interest rates at its next meeting in December. Separately, investors pulled $46 million from Grayscale's Zcash exchange-traded fund (ETF) in October, followed by another $15 million in early November.

CRYPTO · As of 8:51 AM ET 11/21/26.

According to recent reports, none of those issues were caused by anything related to Zcash itself. Its main investment thesis, namely that investors will continue to seek to conceal their transactions and account balances from prying eyes, is still valid.

Now, let's turn to whether it makes sense to buy this coin right now.

According to recent reports, zcash just launched its latest network upgrade, NU7, which makes the chain significantly faster. It cuts the target block time, the gap between each batch of its confirmed transactions, from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. It also sequesters 60% of the network's transaction fees into a reserve that will disburse its funds to pay miners starting in around 2031. Those changes are well and good, but they don't imply any need to rush with a purchase.

Industry observers note that one interesting factor that might make it a better buy is the spread of privacy in crypto. On Nov. 10, Helius, a Solana (SOL -0.72%) infrastructure company, rolled out a privacy protocol for use on Solana. Its CEO signaled the design was inspired by Zcash's. This imitation validates Zcash, but it also means more competition in the future.

Industry observers note that overall, I believe in Zcash's long-term investment potential, but the market is a bit too fragile for me to buy. A coin that lost over 41% in just eight weeks can do it again, and I do not want to be taking risks like that in the current environment of uncertainty.

But for those with a very high risk tolerance and a five-year-plus holding horizon, not to mention already having a diversified portfolio, buying a small amount of Zcash right now could still pay off.
