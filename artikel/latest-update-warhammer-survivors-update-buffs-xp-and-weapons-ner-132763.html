If you've played over a hundred hours of Vampire Survivors you've probably forgotten how slow its early progression feels. It certainly seemed that way based on the reaction to Warhammer Survivors from Vampire Survivors veterans, who complained about its grindiness compared to their beloved bullet heaven originator, despite the arc of both games being quite similar. Developers Auroch Digital made Warhammer Survivors in partnership with Vampire Survivors creator Poncle, so of course the two games have a lot in common.

In a recent development, other complaints were more valid. The Beasts of Nurgle did have too many hit points, and their ability to regenerate only exacerbated that. The treasure chest-opening animation did take a bit too long. And some of the weapons were undertuned. Fortunately, those issues and more have been addressed in hotfix v2.18. Here are the highlights:.

The change to XP is an interesting one. It's a standard part of the genre's formula to have random XP drops—killing some enemies gives you nothing, while others drop XP gems and tempt you to rush into a mob to grab them. Making every enemy drop at least a low-value XP gem will speed up leveling a bit, but hopefully when you see green or red gems you'll still want to risk a mob of orks to yoink.

Industry observers note that auroch Digital recently revealed a roadmap of upcoming additions to Warhammer Survivors, with the first big enhancement in December promising more characters, weapons, and weapon evolutions. January's updates will add more quality-of-life and balance improvements, as well as cross-save, with another update in the first quarter of 2027 bringing another round of characters, weapons, and weapon evolutions.

Industry observers note that the final item on the roadmap is a tease that simply indicates, "ERROR… Details classified." It's probably not the addition of a whole extra universe to its current Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar settings, but I'd be stoked to see them add the other popular world of Warhammer. By which I mean Blood Bowl, of course.
