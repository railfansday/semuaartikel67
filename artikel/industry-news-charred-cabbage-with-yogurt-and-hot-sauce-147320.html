In a recent development, if you think cabbage is boring, this dish will absolutely change your mind. Charred aggressively, it takes on a smoky, almost meaty quality—it’s the main event. A drizzle of hot sauce, crispy breadcrumbs, and a cooling yogurt sauce round it out. Treat cabbage like a protein, and it will reward you.

Recent findings suggest that in a blender, combine 9 pickled Fresnos, stems removed and roughly chopped, plus 2 Tbsp. brine, 1 small shallot, roughly chopped, 3 garlic cloves, 3 Tbsp. red wine vinegar, juice of 1 navel orange, and 1 tsp. kosher salt. Blend on high, taking a pause to scrape the sides, until smooth. With the blender running on low, slowly pour in 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil until the sauce is silky and runny. Transfer the sauce to a bottle or jar and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

Sources indicate that preheat the oven to 425°F and set racks in the center and top of the oven. Line a sheet tray with parchment paper.

Make the Cabbage: Cut 1 large head green cabbage in half, then slice each half into four wedges. Arrange on the prepared sheet tray, drizzle generously with extra-virgin olive oil and kosher salt, and massage it into all those craggy layers. Roast on the center rack for 20–25 minutes, until a knife slides through easily. Set the broiler to high and slide the cabbage to the top rack. Broil for 2–4 minutes, until the edges are deeply charred and irresistibly crispy.

It has been revealed that while the Cabbage Roasts, Make the Breadcrumbs: In a small skillet over medium heat, melt 1 Tbsp. unsalted butter with 1 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil. Stir in ½ cup panko breadcrumbs, 1 garlic clove, finely grated, and ½ tsp. kosher salt, then toast, stirring constantly, until golden and fragrant. Remove from the heat and mix in ¼ preserved lemon, store-bought or homemade, peel only, finely diced, 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh dill, and 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley. Set aside to cool.

Industry observers note that make the Yogurt: In a small bowl, whisk together 1 cup full-fat Greek yogurt or labne, zest and juice ½ lemon, 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh dill, 1 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley, and 1 tsp. kosher salt until smooth and creamy.

When the cabbage is ready, swoop the yogurt across a serving plate. Arrange the cabbage wedges at dramatic angles. Drizzle generously with hot sauce, letting it seep into the leaves. Finish with the golden breadcrumbs, fresh dill fronds, and a few extra grates of lemon zest. Serve immediately.
