Spanish economist de Cos to succeed Lagarde as head of ECB

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (54 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Spanish monetary economist Pablo Hernandez de Cos on Thursday was set to replace France's Christine Lagarde as the new head of the European Central Bank (ECB). His nomination comes as the Europe's most powerful financial institution grapples with an Iran war-driven resurgence in inflation and a cost of living crisis.
