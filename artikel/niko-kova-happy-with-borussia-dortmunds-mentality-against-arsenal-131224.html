Niko Kovač ‘happy’ with Borussia Dortmund’s mentality against Arsenal

Niko Kovač has instilled somewhat of a winning mentality into his Borussia Dortmund side this season. With three 1-0 wins in the Bundesliga, his BVB team faced arguably their biggest challenge of the season on Tuesday night when they faced off against Premier League champions and last season’s UEFA Champions League finalists: Arsenal.

Although going behind to a superb Viktor Gyökeres wondergoal in the tenth minute, Maximilian Beier equalised for the Bundesliga leaders ten minutes into the second period, before Jobe Bellingham saw his strike ruled out for an offside. Evidently happy with a 1-1 draw in London, Kovač praised his team’s performance.

According to recent reports, “It’s a massive point for us,” Dortmund’s head coach send in his post-match press conference, attended by Get German Football News. “A big moment for all of us, for the team and for the fans who turned out in such large numbers today.

“In the end, we’re happy to take the point.”.

Although Arsenal registered 28 shots, only four were on target. With a pragmatic approach and steely defence, Dortmund are proving difficult to break down. “We’re here in London, so naturally you have to whether the storm.

“I think the team highlighted a really good side of themselves today. You can’t accuse them of hiding or not giving it their all.

“We fought as a team, kept fighting back, and showed that we can hold our own against a strong opponent.”.

GGFN | Daniel Pinder – reporting from the Emirates Stadium.
