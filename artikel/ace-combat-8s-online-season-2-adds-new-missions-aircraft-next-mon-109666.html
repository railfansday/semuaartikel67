The next season of content is coming soon to Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve's online multiplayer mode.

Publisher Bandai Namco has released the below trailer detailing all the latest bits and bobs:.

Season 2 of Ace Combat Online begins on 1st December, 2026, and there's plenty of newly introduced stuff for frequent flyers to enjoy.

There are fresh aircraft, including the Su-30SM -Mihaly- and the X-40 Nightmare.

You can also deck out your F-15C with a upcoming 'Climb King' skin, while the Mirage 2000-5 gets the 'Experimental' skin.

It's not just planes getting fresh looks; you can kit out your pilot with new clothing and emotes.

Speaking of pilots, a latest Pilot Rank, Captain, has been introduced; reach it and you'll be able to purchase the aforementioned X-40 Nightmare.

In a recent development, season 2 brings some fresh missions to Ace Combat Online, too. Co-Op Warfare gets the Kaninfot Valley mission, while Anchorhead Bay has been introduced to Co-Op Assault mode.

On top of all that, there are of course new premium and free Ace Passes to progress through, unlocking further cosmetic rewards.
