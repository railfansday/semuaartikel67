An upcoming Steam game is offering up to $5,000 towards a wedding or divorce for the player who can remove 100,000 padlocks from a bridge in Paris faster than anyone else.

Love Locks is a cleanup sim from Collision Games that is based on the tradition of couples fastening padlocks to a bridge and throwing the keys into the river. Your job is to undo all that work, starting with a magnet to fish those discarded keys out of the Seine River.

In a recent development, every lock you remove adds to your scrap pile, which unlocks better tools as it grows. You can start by hunting down the right key for an individual lock, then work your way up to bolt cutters, blowtorches, and lasers that can clear larger sections of the bridge.

And there’s an extra incentive to clear the bridge rapidly, as the devs are willing to give the person who completes the game fastest up to $5,000 towards their wedding…or divorce.

According to recent reports, the contest opens when the game launches on December 3 and runs through December 10. You’ll need to register and clear all 100,000 locks on a fresh save before the deadline.

According to recent reports, you can try as many times as you like during the week, with your fastest valid run counting. The winner is decided by verified in-game time, so someone who finishes later in the week can still take the prize.

Collision Games plans to announce the winner on December 17 once the results have been checked.
