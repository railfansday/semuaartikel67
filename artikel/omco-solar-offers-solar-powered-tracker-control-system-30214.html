OMCO Solar offers solar-powered tracker control system

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

OMCO Solar now offers a version of its OMCO Star Tracker Control System powered entirely by solar energy, allowing tracker controls to operate without AC power at the site.

The system, first rolled out last year, is designed for OMCO Origin Factory-Direct Trackers. Its tracker controllers, array controllers and sensor bridges use all-weather battery packs charged by solar modules.

Sources indicate that according to OMCO, the technology eliminates the need for AC power to tracker control components and an uninterruptible power supply. It also allows tracker controls to operate and protects modules against wind, snow and hail before AC power is available.

According to recent reports, “Our industry has solved so many problems as we have grown – we’re now the leading source for latest electric energy capacity in the US. This addition to the OMCO Star product line solves one more – how to protect modules if there’s no AC power to the site even for a short time. We’re excited to offer this solution to our existing customers and to new partners,” noted Matt Kesler, vice president of solar technology at OMCO Solar, in a statement.

It has been revealed that the OMCO Star system uses LoRa, or long-range, wireless technology with a communication range greater than 1 mile. According to OMCO, this reduces the number of network controllers required and allows direct communication with every tracker without relying on a mesh network.

The firm claims LoRa is 100 times less susceptible to electromagnetic noise than Zigbee and better able to connect to distant trackers despite obstacles.

According to recent reports, OMCO claims its all-weather battery packs allow the solar-powered controls to operate reliably in cold climates. The system includes field-replaceable batteries, a phone app that eliminates the need for buttons on controllers and a remote monitoring and control portal.

According to recent reports, the portal allows operators to move trackers for activities such as mowing, snow plowing and module cleaning. The system is developed, manufactured and supported by OMCO Solar in the United States.

“We are excited about bringing this innovation to the Utility Scale and DG tracker markets. Over the past few years, OMCO has expanded its capabilities beyond just being a US steel manufacturer to now being a true technology leader in our space. We look forward to supporting our customers/partners with the OMCO Star Tracker Control System, improving reliability, and reducing the labor and materials needed to build their projects,” remarked Eric Goodwin, vice president of business development at OMCO Solar.

OMCO Solar will display the all-DC tracker control system at RE+ in Las Vegas, November 17–19, in the North Hall, booth N516.
