FRESH YORK, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Globant (NYSE: GLOB), a global company focused on driving enterprise reinvention through AI, today announced Globant Converge 2026, its flagship virtual event, which will take place on December 10. Under the theme "Reinventing Companies for the AI-Native Era," Converge will bring together business and technology leaders from around the world who are transforming their companies and rethinking the way they build, deliver, and operate as AI becomes a foundational part of the enterprise.

It has been revealed that during the event, Globant will present Glob.AI, Globant's AI-Native Services Platform. Glob.AI combines human-supervised AI Pods with enterprise-grade governance to help organizations move beyond experimentation and deliver measurable business outcomes, under an outcome- and consumption-based model.
