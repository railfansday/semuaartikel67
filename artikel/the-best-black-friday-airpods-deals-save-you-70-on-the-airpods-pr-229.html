Black Friday is just under two weeks away, and headphones deals — including on AirPods — are starting to show out ahead of the year's biggest shopping holiday.

Black Friday sales start much earlier than the day itself, but it's in the immediate lead up (the period we're in now) that the deals start to really get strong. Case in point: the AirPods Pro 3 dropped to $179, slashing $79 off their list price.

Recent findings suggest that most major retailers Black Friday sales, including Amazon, have either started already in some iteration or kick off this week. This may mean further price dips on AirPods (the Maxes and AirPods 4 are also on sale), or first time major deals (as in the case of the AirPods 5). However, in years past we've seen retailers will wait until Thanksgiving or Black Friday to bring out any new record lows, and then AirPods can sell out quickly. All this to say, these early price dips are relatively substantial and not likely to get too much cheaper in the run up to Black Friday proper.
