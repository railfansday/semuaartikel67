The bill's author, Assemblymember Lori Wilson, cited figures showing that child injuries and fatalities in crashes have risen since the pandemic, with children ages 8 to 12 disproportionately affected.

"Extending the required age for booster use and implementing straightforward testing can significantly enhance child safety," CalMatters reported.

"So the best thing to do to keep your child safe while riding in a vehicle matches what our law is," Wilson mentioned of the bill in April 2025. "It really is to take into consideration all the varying types of seats that we sit on in the car. This is about safety.

"This is about making sure that if something happens, you are safe, and I think that’s the key thing that we’re trying to drive home is that it is not about treating people like babies.".
