Max Dowman became the youngest goalscorer in the history of England Under-21s after netting a late winner in a 3-2 victory over France at Ashton Gate.

Lee Carsley’s side continued their preparations for next year’s European Championship, bouncing back from Friday's 1-0 defeat by Portugal despite surrendering a two-goal lead on Monday.

Having produced a lacklustre performance three days ago, England made a lightning start this time. Mikey Moore gave them an early lead on his first under-21s start before Divin Mubama doubled their advantage.

According to recent reports, the Young Lions were comfortable, but Tommy Setford made a second mistake in as many games. He let Giovani Versini’s cross slip through his fingers to hand France a goal back, before the Frenchman equalised in style moments later.

Carsley handed a first start to promising Arsenal youngster Dowman, who proved to be the match-winner by tapping home at only 16 years old.

England got off to the perfect start following great work from Ethan Nwaneri down the left. His pass fed Moore, who tucked expertly past Mathieu Patouillet.

The Young Lions doubled their lead in the 29th minute as Nwaneri again provided the assist, lifting a high ball onto the head of Mubama, who nodded back across goal for 2-0.

France did not threaten in the first half but began the second period much more menacingly. Dayann Methalie found space inside the box but fired wide with their best chance to that point.

England almost scored a third down the other end when Nwaneri was denied by Patouillet’s diving save. The ball fell back to the Borussia Dortmund loanee, who saw his follow-up effort cleared off the line by Nathan Zeze.

Setford’s mistake had given Portugal victory on Friday, and he was again at fault when Versini’s untroubling cross slipped through his fingers to make it 2-1.

There was nothing Setford could do about the equaliser, however, as Versini let fly from distance with an impressive strike into the net.

However, England re-established their lead four minutes later when Harrison Armstrong fed Dowman, who was left unmarked inside the box to tap into an empty net for a historic goal.
