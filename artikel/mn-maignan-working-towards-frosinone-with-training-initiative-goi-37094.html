Mike Maignan will hopefully be back in goal for AC Milan this weekend, after initial fears that he could be out with an injury.

When Maignan went off in France’s game against Turkey, there was immediately a bit of panic regarding their options moving forward. Lorenzo Torriani was the only fit back-up and they’d not exactly been planning to invest in a goalkeeper.
