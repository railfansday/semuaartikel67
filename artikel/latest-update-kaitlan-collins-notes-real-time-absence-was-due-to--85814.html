The Season 24 finale of Real Time with Bill Maher featured a last-minute guest change as Kaitlan Collins was held up in Washington DC.

Although she was scheduled to appear on the HBO talk show alongside newly elected Texas Democratic congressman Bobby Pulido, the CNN anchor explained she was covering a White House press conference with Donald Trump amid a report that she was “quietly dropped” from the show’s lineup.

According to recent reports, “Yes, because I was….at the White House for a press conference with the president,” she wrote on X in response. “But Maher’s team was gracious and understanding and I can’t wait to watch the season finale!”.

Sources indicate that the reporter then shared a clip from the press conference of herself grilling Trump about his friendship with Vladimir Putin and whether he considers the Russian president an ally of the United States. “Anyways here’s my Q&A,” she wrote following Trump’s previous attempts to ban her and CNN from White House press briefings. In Collins’ place, NBC News and MS NOW’s Jacob Soboroff appeared on the panel for Friday’s Real Time season finale, with mentalist Oz Pearlman as Maher’s interview guest.
