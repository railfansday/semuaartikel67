Stephan El Shaarawy, right now at Genoa, gave an interview to La Gazzetta dello Sport, in which he discussed various topics, including the 2023 Europa League final he lost in Budapest while playing for Roma.

El Shaarawy on his decision to join Genoa and the impact De Rossi’s presence had on his former teammate in the capital: “We spoke a lot this summer. Things dragged on a bit due to some transfer market dynamics, but I continued training and we discussed our prepares. I kept him updated on everything. Daniele’s presence had a significant impact. I had a substantial offer from Saudi Arabia, but I needed to feel alive again.”.

According to recent reports, the former Roma winger on DDR’s changes, from teammate to coach. “I found him just as I left him in Rome: a man of great character, a leader, that’s how I always recognized him in the locker room. He knows how to motivate, motivate, and tell the team what to say, always in the right way and at the right time. A great person and a well-prepared coach.”.

Il Faraone and his rebirth in Rome after a difficult period in Monaco: “I discussed with my family whether it was the right choice, but for the last month and a half I trained alone, frustrated and disappointed again. Luckily, Roma arrived: a clear change, a rebirth with a back-heeled goal under the Sud on his debut.”.

El Shaarawy spoke about the move to China and his new experience with the Giallorossi, which was marred by the defeat in the Europa League final: “I can’t deny that the financial aspect played a significant role in my decision. The experience wasn’t bad, but it was very difficult: Covid broke out, the league was suspended for seven months, and I couldn’t return to Shanghai due to quarantine. So I decided to end my relationship and return to Roma. Everything was perfect, except for that Europa League final that would have changed the club’s history… Someone didn’t let us.”.
