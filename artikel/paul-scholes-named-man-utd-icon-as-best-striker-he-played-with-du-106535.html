Paul Scholes Named Man Utd Icon as Best Striker He Played With During His Career

Paul Scholes has revealed that Ruud van Nistelrooy was the best striker he shared the pitch with during his career. Scholes spent all 19 seasons of his senior career under Sir Alex Ferguson at Manchester United, where he made 714 appearances, scored 153 goals and provided another 83 assists. Those achievements have helped cement his reputation as one of the greatest players to have ever put on the red jersey of Man United.

During his time at Old Trafford, Scholes helped the Red Devils win 26 trophies, including 11 Premier League titles and two Champions League crowns, a haul that ranks him among the most decorated players in football history. He became renowned for his exceptional vision and range of passing, regularly delivering the ball with remarkable accuracy, timing and precision, traits that place him among the greatest midfielders of the Premier League era.

It has been revealed that scholes had the opportunity to play with some of the greatest strikers Man United have ever had throughout his career, from Andy Cole and Dwight Yorke to Eric Cantona and the club's record goalscorer, Wayne Rooney. Even so, none developed the same on-field understanding with him as Dutch striker van Nistelrooy.

Although he played alongside a number of Manchester United icons, Scholes explained to BBC Sounds during an episode of 606 Savage Social that one player stood out as the teammate he had a much stronger on-pitch connection with above all others.

In a recent development, speaking about van Nistelrooy, Scholes described a natural understanding between the pair. He knew where the striker would make his runs and precisely when to play the pass to give his Dutch teammate the clearest chance of scoring.

Sources indicate that "That's an easy one. I don't want to disrespect any of the other forwards - because I've played with some amazing players, especially at United. Andy Cole, Dwight Yorke, Ole Gunnar [Solskjaer] at the time, but I always thought I had the best connection with Ruud van Nistelrooy. I played the season with him, and I think I scored 20 goals playing as a ten. I knew what he wanted, I knew the runs he'd make and where he wanted the ball. What a finisher. That man was just hungry. His life depended on scoring goals. He was the one I felt I had a great connection with.".

In a recent development, scholes and van Nistelrooy played together 165 times and combined for 16 goal contributions, during the Dutchman's five seasons at Old Trafford. Nevertheless, he made a significant mark on both the club and English football, scoring 95 goals and adding 14 assists across 150 Premier League appearances. He also claimed the Golden Boot and Player of the Season awards in 2002/03.

His record is one reason he is so often regarded as one of the greatest strikers in Premier League history. Few forwards seemed so completely devoted to finding the net, making his relatively short spell in English football a disappointment for fans who wanted to see more.
