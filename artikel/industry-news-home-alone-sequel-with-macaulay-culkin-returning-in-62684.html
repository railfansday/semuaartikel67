📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Macaulay Culkin’s icy relationship with Home Alone has thawed in recent years as he’s once again embraced the Kevin McAllister role in live screenings and TV commercials. Now, this rekindling might result in Culkin’s character returning to the franchise for the first time in 35 years.

According to recent reports, variety reports that Disney is developing a sequel to the Home Alone franchise, with Culkin collaborating on the script with Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg. No other details were revealed, but the sequel — if green-lit — would presumably mark Culkin’s first Home Alone film since 1993’s Lost in Upcoming York.

In November 2025, Culkin said he was open to reprising the Kevin McAllister role, albeit with a twist: In Culkin’s idea for the sequel, he would now be a father who’s locked out of his own home by his son, who instead torments Kevin in many of the same ways that he tormented the Wet Bandits decades earlier.

According to recent reports, it’s unclear if Culkin’s idea is the one moving forward at Disney or if Rogen and Goldberg plotted a different story. Either way, any sequel would be arriving as the franchise is at its nadir: The sixth film in the franchise, Home Sweet Home Alone, was critically panned and largely ignored upon its Disney+ release in 2021. However, a potential lump of coal in the Home Alone sequel report until more details emerge: Rogen reportedly has used the real-life, long-gestating Madonna biopic starring Julia Garner as fodder for his fictional showbiz series The Studio, so don’t be too surprised if Home Alone 7 pops up as a subplot in an upcoming season and not as a genuine film.
