The Thanksgiving holiday is allowing Brooke Hogan to reflect on the loss of her father, wrestling icon Hulk Hogan.

Recent findings suggest that “I’ve realized that so much of my grief exists in what we will miss together, rather than even what we’ve lost…,” Brooke, 38, wrote via Instagram on Wednesday, November 25. “It’s a strange thing. Nostalgia in reverse. Longing for moments somewhere in the future that feel almost as familiar as memories.”.

The founder of BB Designs by Brooke continued, “I miss him in the thought of him falling asleep on my couch, after too much pecan pie, surrounded by the beautiful little family that is an extension of his own. I imagine the evolution of our relationship as I grow older.”.

Hulk died in July 2025 at the age of 71. His cause of death was acute myocardial infarction, most commonly known as a heart attack, according to documents obtained by Us.

Although her relationship with Hulk was complicated, Brooke — who shared a throwback photo with her father for her latest post — has been open about her grieving journey and processing different emotions.

“Sometimes the circumstances surrounding a relationship don’t accurately reflect the love that exists within it,” she wrote. “Life and dishonest influence can create distance between two hearts that were never meant to be far apart. I’ll die on that hill. No daughter doesn’t want her daddy.”.

“How strange it is to miss someone in a place you’ve yet to visit, at an age you’ve yet to reach, in a moment that’s still somewhere in your future,” she added.

Recent findings suggest that married to Steven Oleksy, Brooke has two children and presently lives in Florida. She continues to have a fractured relationship with her mom, Linda, 67, and brother, Nick, 36.

It has been revealed that “Grief stretches in both directions. It lives in the memories behind you, but also in the life unfolding ahead of you,” Brooke wrote in her latest post. “There’s an entirely different dimension of grief that exists within the beauty of living. The more life there is to celebrate, the more places there are to miss someone.”.

While Brooke acknowledged that she has “so much life ahead of me,” it could be why she feels her dad’s absence “so deeply.”.

It has been revealed that “I’ll miss him in chapters of my life that haven’t even been written yet,” she said. “It’s a longing born entirely out of so much love with nowhere to go. It’s nostalgia in reverse. Being homesick for a future with someone you needed and loved the most.”.
