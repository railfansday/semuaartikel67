Sheffield Wednesday landed the attacker on a temporary basis in September on a short-term deal. He has since made a positive impression at Hillsborough and has become one of SWFC’s main players so far this season in League One.

According to recent reports, keillor-Dunn, 29, has made 12 appearances for the Owls in all competitions to date, 10 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with six goals and one assist. The South Yorkshire outfit need to pursue a permanent move for him this winter and he can flourish under the guidance of newly introduced head coach Dave Challinor over the coming months.

The Sunderland-born man is a proven performer at third tier level and was a shrewd addition. Wrexham gave him the green light to head out the exit door to get some more game time under his belt and he hasn’t looked back.

According to recent reports, sheffield Wednesday won 3-0 at home against Plymouth Argyle last time after goals by Keillor-Dunn, Billy Mitchell and Di’Shon Bernard and they have risen back into the play-offs. The Owls are back in action on Friday night with an away trip to Barnsley.

Sheffield Wednesday’s decision to bring in Keillor-Dunn has worked out well for them. He injects valuable quality into SWFC’s starting XI.

He knows where the back of the net is and causes problems for opposition defenders. When everyone is available, he battles it out with the likes of Mason Burstow, Jamal Lowe and Ali Al-Hamadi for a place in the Owls’ team.

According to recent reports, keillor-Dunn re-signed for Wrexham back last February after six years away and penned a contract until June 2029 in North Wales. However, he has since found it hard for minutes at the Racecourse Ground, hence why they let him leave.

Industry observers note that he is unlikely to ever break into the Red Dragons’ team due to strong competition for places in his position. Therefore, they will surely be tempted to cash in on him if Sheffield Wednesday can match his valuation.

According to recent reports, sheffield Wednesday manager Challinor will be eager to put his own stamp on his newly inherited squad in January with some additions. SWFC already have a player in Keillor-Dunn who is performing so securing his future has to be a no-brainer.

Recent findings suggest that the Owls are eyeing promotion to the Championship at the first time of asking this term. They were relegated earlier this year after finishing in the bottom three along with Leicester City and Oxford United.
