Melania Trump has given another look into her life as first lady with the premiere of “Melania: The Series,” a two-episode follow-up to the previously released "Melania" documentary.

Both the film and the series detail the weeks leading up to President Donald Trump’s January 2025 inauguration.

Sources indicate that in the first episode, which was released on Nov. 20, Melania prepares to resume her White House duties for a second term.

Industry observers note that “The role of the first lady is managing of the White House,” she explained. “And I think people don’t realize that the first lady is in charge of the residence.”.

She spoke at length about her approach to the role, noting the pride she takes in carrying out duties such as decorating the Washington, DC, landmark and planning events.

“I’m proud to elevate America through every detail brought to life within the White House,” said Melania. “Each event is carefully planned for months with the design, decor, cuisine, music, and atmosphere, all arranged with precision to showcase our nation’s beauty, strength, and grace.”.

Melania explained that her hope is for “the White House to showcase the greatness of American culture,” and that she wanted to elevate it “to the highest standard.”.

In a recent development, “We preserve. We restore. We build new stuff,” she noted. “The White House should be the best of the best. It represents our country.”.

Recent findings suggest that on Nov. 6, the president declared the Oval Office a national monument in an attempt to prevent future presidents from making changes to the renovations that have been made in his second term.

Industry observers note that “We’ve had tremendous victory in this office,” he stated. “So we’re making it a national monument, and it will not be allowed to be touched by anybody, destroyed, ripped down by stupid people, in order to build something in a slightly different shape.”.

The White House has undergone extensive work since Trump has reentered office, including an overhaul of the iconic Rose Garden and a destruction of the East Wing to accommodate proposed aims for a 90,000-square-foot ballroom.

The first lady explained that she’s always had an interest in interior design, and when asked what kind of career she might have pursued had she not began working as a model before meeting her now-husband.

Recent findings suggest that “I would continue studying architecture. I think I would do something with design,” she said. Melania had briefly attended the University of Ljubljana in Slovenia, where she studied the design discipline before leaving after a few months to pursue modeling.

She also revealed that she had aims to bring some of her own pieces back to the White House.

Sources indicate that “I will make it a home again,” she noted. “Last time was very beautiful, very comfortable. We all loved it, and we will do it again.”.

At one point, an off-screen interviewer notes that “so much thought” goes into each minute decision and asks Melania, “Does your husband recognize the detail?”.

It has been revealed that “He takes the whole experience in. He’s busy with his own job, and his mind’s going nonstop,” she said. “He has so much to take care of. So maybe he doesn’t realize how much work it is.”.

At another moment in the documentary, Melania hinted at the scope of her role, saying, “I know what my husband is doing and what my son is doing, but I don’t think they know what I’m doing.”.

Ahead of the inauguration, CNN reported that Melania would most likely not reside in the White House full time, which she appeared to confirm in the docuseries.

Recent findings suggest that “My home is in three cities. Each has purpose and serves varying parts of my life,” she remarked. “To balance this lifestyle, I’m very intentional and detail-oriented.”.

Aside from the White House, the Melania splits her time between Trump Tower in New York City and Mar-a-Lago in Palm Beach, FL.

She also expressed how much she values privacy, noting the importance of getting enough sleep and seeking “solitude.”.

“I don’t need to be all the time with people,” said Melania. “I’m very happy in my own company. I always was.”.
