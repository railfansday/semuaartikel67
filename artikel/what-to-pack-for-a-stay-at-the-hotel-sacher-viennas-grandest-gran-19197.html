What to Pack for a Stay at the Hotel Sacher, Vienna’s Grandest Grand Dame

🌐 Source: Cntraveler • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (135 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

This grande dame has long held the title of society hot spot: Everyone from King Charles to Grace Kelly has passed through its damask suites. In honor of its 150th anniversary, however, Hotel Sacher Vienna is celebrating its rich tradition of fostering the arts by commissioning Austrian artist Erwin Wurm to create two surreal anthropomorphic sculptures of bright pink handbags to flank its doors. Earlier this year American artist Kaws also designed a limited edition box for its famed Sacher torte cake, as did tastemaker Aerin Lauder on another occasion, adorning hers with golden flowers inspired by Viennese decorative arts. Join the festivities by channeling the aesthetics of former regular Gustave Klimt—think jewel tones and sequins that will shimmer below the chandeliers of the ruby-red Rote Bar.
