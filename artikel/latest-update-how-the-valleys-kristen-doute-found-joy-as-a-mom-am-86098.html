The Valley star Kristen Doute is not going to sugarcoat the realities of the often difficult postpartum experience — but she’ll also be the first to tell you there’s light at the end of the latest mom, diaper-wearing, sleep-deprived tunnel.

“Just going through postpartum in itself was a really difficult time for me personally — and then filming on top of that? I was kind of not in my right head,” Kristen, 43, exclusively told Us on Thursday, November 19, while hosting her James Mae pop-up shopping event held at the Zero Lounge in West Hollywood, California.

In a recent development, “It was like an out-of-body experience,” Kristen, who also hosts the “Run It Back” podcast alongside Katie DiMento, which debuted on Friday, November 20, continued of her post-birth experience. “But as time moved on, I have created such a n amazing community of moms — like, outside of even my mom friends [and] on social media from my podcast. I’ve kept in touch with moms through email that have said, ‘Thank you for showing the honest truth of what postpartum can look like so it really normalizes that.’”.

She added, “It took me a while — it was about 10 months [before] I stopped feeling the postpartum depression, so it took me a little bit longer. But now I just find the joy in every single thing that [her daughter Kaia] does. My little girl loves to play with rocks, sticks and dirt, and it’s like I never realized how cool a rock or a leaf was. You’re just reliving your own childhood all over again.”.

Us exclusively confirmed back in June 2025 that the former Vanderpump Rules bad-girl-turned-voice-of-reason and her fiancé, Luke Broderick, were officially parents after welcoming baby girl Kaia.

In a recent development, “She’s here, and we still can’t believe it. Luke is even hotter as a dad, and I just feel so lucky,” Doute told Us at the time. “And even though I haven’t slept or showered in two days, seeing our daughter and finally being able to hold her makes it all worth it. We’re completely overwhelmed with love and so grateful for her— our lives are already changed completely.”.

Doute later opened up to Us about life as a latest mom, admitting that while she felt “really lucky” and “really fortunate” she was also exhausted and still adjusting to parenthood.

In a recent development, “I’m good. I mean, I’m exhausted. The tired thing is no joke. Maybe a day and a half ago, Luke and I had this system down: We’re like, OK, she’s napping; when she gets up, the bottle, the diapers are ready, all good to go. We were like, we got this, and we high-fived,” she told Us in July 2025. “Then last night I woke up, my boobs were exploding, I’m covered in milk, she’s screaming and crying, and we’re both like, ‘Oh my God, what do we do? What the hell do we do? Oh my God!’ Don’t count the chickens before they hatch. It’s frustrating because you don’t want her to be upset and you don’t know how to fix it fast enough. We’re all figuring things out together.”.

The pair’s postpartum struggles and relationship woes as new parents were heavily documented on season 3 of The Valley — a steep learning curve that was often painful for Kristen but, thanks to her daughter, one that was also healing, she told Us.

“My daughter has so much power, and I think there’s something about being a millennial too,” the reality TV star explained on Thursday. “We’re kind of trying to reparent ourselves a little bit, and take everything that our hardworking parents did but then make it a little bit gentler, a little bit softer, and also in talks about having confidence.”.

She added, “Like today, she saw me getting glam at home, and she thinks it’s the funniest, coolest thing ever. But we call it ‘fancy face’ at home, because I never want her to think that me getting a face full of makeup or having my hair curled by someone else is what makes me pretty or beautiful because it has a lot more to do on the inside than it does the out.”.
