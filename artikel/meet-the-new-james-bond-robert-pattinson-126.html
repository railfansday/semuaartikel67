Meet the new James Bond: Robert Pattinson

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, the Internet has been abuzz for months about which actor would replace Daniel Craig as British agent 007 James Bond in the franchise. We finally have the answer: Robert Pattinson, who built on his 2005 role as the doomed Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire into an illustrious career mixing roles in franchises and Hollywood blockbusters with daring arthouse fare.

Recent findings suggest that “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson stated in a statement. “To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”.

The choice will reunite Pattinson with Denis Villeneuve, who recently worked with him on the forthcoming Dune: Part III and will direct the next (as yet untitled) Bond installment. “I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey,” Villeneuve stated in a statement. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”.

According to recent reports, not everyone is happy with the casting choice; some have never forgiven Pattinson for the Twilight franchise, despite the actor’s impressive recent films. Pattinson has played darker roles in recent years, often as a villain. And at 40, Pattinson is a bit older than the actors he beat out, most notably Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden (Slow Horses), Josh O’Connor (Wake Up, Dead Man), and Callum Turner (Fantastic Beasts).

Recent findings suggest that but Pattinson is a logical choice for Bond; heck, he’s already played a secret agent in Christopher Nolan’s Tenet (2020). In fact, he originally topped the list of longtime Bond franchise steward Barbara Broccoli’s and Michael G. Wilson’s choices to take over from Craig. They shelved that idea when Pattinson was cast in Matt Reeves’ The Batman. But Broccoli and Wilson no longer have creative control over Bond, having sold the rights to Amazon MGM for $20 million.

Recent findings suggest that pattinson’s box office record this year probably didn’t hurt either: He played central roles in The Drama, Primetime, and The Odyssey, all of which made back their production costs and then some, with Dune: Part III still to come. It’s unclear how his Bond commitments will affect Pattinson’s future as Batman; The Batman: Part 2 has faced production delays (and a director change) and is slated for a 2028 release. Should there be a third installment—not assured in the wake of James Gunn and Peter Safran’s departure from DC Studios—Reeves will likely work around Pattinson’s Bond schedule.

“James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies,” producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman mentioned in a statement. “The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times.”.
