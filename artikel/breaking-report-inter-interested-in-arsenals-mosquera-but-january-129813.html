Reports in Italy claim that Inter are interested in Arsenal centre-back Cristhian Mosquera, who has previously been linked with a move to San Siro, but pulling off a transfer for the Premier League winner in January will be a difficult task.

Inter, along with city rivals Milan, were among the clubs reported to be interested in Mosquera while he was still at Valencia, before joining Arsenal in the summer of 2025.

The Gunners paid a fee reported to be around €15m for the Colombia international, but after playing an vital role in Arsenal’s 2025-26 season, which ended with a Premier League title and a run to the Champions League final, reports from Calciomercato.com claim that he is now valued in the region of €40m.

Calciomercato adds that Inter are looking to rejuvenate their central defensive department in the near future and that they are interested in a potential deal for Mosquera, whose minutes have been limited following Arsenal’s signing of England international Ezri Konsa over the summer.

Mosquera has made five total appearances for Arsenal this season: Two in the Premier League, one in the Champions League, one in the EFL Cup and one in the Community Shield. He has not featured in the Premier League since August.

As per Calciomercato, Mosquera remains an ‘idea’ and a transfer in January will be difficult to pull off, but he is a player who remains of interest and could become a more viable option for the summer transfer window in 2027.
