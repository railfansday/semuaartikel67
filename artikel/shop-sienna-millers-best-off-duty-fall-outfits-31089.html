📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sienna Miller has never been one to shy away from a statement look, but lately, the actress has taken a noticeably more understated approach to getting dressed. As we await the gripping conclusion of HBO's War this Thursday, we’re taking a closer look at the actress’s off-duty style over the past few weeks—and we noticed one thing in particular: each look was anchored by a great piece of outerwear.

Industry observers note that rather than the flowing silhouettes and eclectic layering the perennial boho girl has been known for in the past, she’s leaning into wardrobe foundations with a minimalist sensibility this season—perhaps taking a cue from her War character Carla Duval’s pared-back closet. Her recent roster includes bomber jackets, tailored coats, and leather motos, among other staples—but don’t mistake these for predictable basics. There’s a peplum-waist leather jacket with a more sculptural shape, a zip-up blouson in a bold shade of red, and even a trench coat updated in brown corduroy. Each bring something unexpected to the otherwise classic fall lineup.

Her styling approach is equally unfussy, keeping the focus on the outerwear itself and pairing each piece with everyday essentials. Think black pleated trousers, lived-in denim, simple T-shirts, and cozy knitwear. Accessories follow the same less-is-more philosophy, from a strappy heel she's worn on repeat in two different colorways to a timeless designer tote and understated jewelry. It’s proof that the best fall outfits don’t have to be complicated—but they should always start with a really good jacket.

Ahead, find easy fall outfit formulas inspired by Sienna Miller’s style.

It has been revealed that sienna Miller leaned into the peplum leather jacket trend in Balmain's sculptural take—a feminine twist on the classic moto shape. She kept the rest of the look simple with a black top, tailored trousers, and strappy sandals—which can easily be swapped for pointy ankle boots as temperatures continue to drop. Consider this a masterclass in giving the all-black outfit a little more personality.

A pop of red is the enduring color trend that the fashion crowd can’t quit—and a zip-up jacket is the easiest way to wear it. Miller styled hers effortlessly over a navy crewneck sweater and frayed white jeans, finishing the look with her favorite high-vamp black flats, Gucci’s Ophidia tote, and a printed scarf around the neck. It’s especially on-point for transitional dressing or a polished travel outfit.

Recent findings suggest that minimalist ‘90s-style coats are everywhere—perhaps a byproduct of the Love Story effect that swept the fashion conversation this year. Miller put her own spin on the classic piece, wearing it with a crisp blue button-down and pleated trousers, then adding strappy red Jude heels—a style she’s been wearing on repeat lately. Add a structured top-handle bag and gold chain necklace to complete the everyday look, perfect for the office and beyond.

In a recent development, trench coats are something of a British wardrobe institution, but Miller gave the heritage silhouette a fresh spin in brown corduroy. Underneath, she kept things understated, styling a simple black top with a pleated plaid skirt and knee-high boots. Add a waist belt and shoulder bag to carry the look through the day—a timeless fall outfit you’ll return to over and over.

It has been revealed that transitional dressing is all about the layers—and Sienna Miller has the off-duty formula down. A khaki bomber jacket, white T-shirt, and relaxed denim make up the foundation, while a navy knit tossed over the shoulders adds an extra layer for cooler mornings or evenings. Finish with her go-to red strappy heels for a little contrast, or swap in ballet flats for an equally easy daytime option.
