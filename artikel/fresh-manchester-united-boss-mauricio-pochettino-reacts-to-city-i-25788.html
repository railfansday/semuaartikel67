Mauricio Pochettino has said to “give him time” to make Manchester United the dominant club in the city again as he prepares to start work.

Pochettino will take charge of Man United for the first time against Liverpool on Sunday after being appointed to succeed Michael Carrick, who was sacked last week with the club sitting 18th in the Premier League table.

The Argentine’s return to club management has been delayed after being allowed to finish up his commitments with the United States national team.

His last game ended in a 2-0 win for USA against Haiti in Cincinnati during the early hours on Wednesday (UK time) to wrap up an 8-0 aggregate victory in their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final.

AC Milan forward Christian Pulisic opened the scoring with a spectacular overhead kick and teenager Julian Hall doubled the hosts’ lead before half-time.

After bidding an emotional farewell to the USA supporters inside the stadium, Pochettino immediately turned his attentions to the task at hand with Man United and the rivalry with Manchester City.

According to recent reports, told by former Man City loanee DaMarcus Beasley that “Manchester is blue, it will always be blue” in the TNT Sports US studio after the game, Pochettino responded: “You will see. Give me time.

“The upcoming challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenalin you want to feel.

“We need to add our common sense… we know the situation is not a situation that deserves to be a club like Manchester United, the fan base is amazing.”.

Pochettino heads to Old Trafford tasked with steering Man United out of the relegation zone before he can target winning titles and eventually usurping Man City as the dominant team in the city.

United have not been crowned Premier League champions since Sir Alex Ferguson retired, with the legendary Scotsman final season being the last time the club finished above their neighbours in the table.

Additional reporting by Press Association.
