When it comes to Thanksgiving and Christmas, hosting people can be overwhelming. And while Markle and Prince Harry are still trying to find their forever home, that is not stopping them from being good hosts. The Suits alum told People that she picked up a thing or two while working in the restaurant business before hitting it massive that she applies to her holiday hosting, and this piece of advice might just be the most vital one:.

Being a great host is all about anticipating your guests’ needs — something I learned working in restaurants and now love bringing into my own home. With a little thoughtful planning, you can make everyone feel comfortable and cared for.

According to recent reports, markle is no stranger to making sure her guests feel comfortable, as fans have seen on her lifestyle series, With Love, Meghan (which you can stream with a Netflix subscription). Additionally, she worked at the coat check of a Chicago bar while attending Northwestern University, so she knows what it takes to make a good environment. Speaking of environment, setting the mood is a very important part of creating a good one, and according to Markle, candles are a key part of that:.

Light a few candles — choose scented ones to set the mood in the living areas and unscented for the kitchen (in those spaces, let the fragrance of the food shine).

Meanwhile, hosting does not mean doing things alone, especially for a holiday like Thanksgiving when so much prep has to be done. On that note, Markle stressed the importance of doing a bit of extra work ahead of time. Even with kids, they’re never too young to assist, no matter how massive or small the work is:.

Recent findings suggest that chop your veggies and herbs in advance to save time on the day, and also for those finishing touches, whether you’re serving homemade or store-bought dishes. Your kids can join in too! They can proudly say they helped.

According to recent reports, markle and Prince Harry are parents to Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5, and I can’t imagine how chaotic the holidays are with two young children running around the house. Plus, it keeps the children busy and makes them feel included, so I definitely will keep that tip in mind.

Sources indicate that including the kids and working on the veggies early is not the only advice Markle had when it came to food prep. The former actress had plenty to share about the most notable Thanksgiving tradition, the food, and even confessed to one aspect that her own husband is a fan of, so much so that he would not let her live it down if she hadn’t brought it up:.

Served with creamy mashed potatoes, a green salad speckled with flower sprinkles and rich gravy (my husband would never forgive me if I didn’t add that to the table), this is sure to please your entire gathering.
