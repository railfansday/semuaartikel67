📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As part of The Witcher and Cyberpunk studio CD Projekt Red's recent Q3 earnings report, the developer offered some updates on upcoming game The Witcher 4.

During an earnings call yesterday, the studio revealed it had signed a upcoming partnership deal with Sony regarding marketing rights to The Witcher 4, as well as to extend "the presence" of CD Projekt Red's other games across the PlayStation Store and PlayStation Plus.

Industry observers note that "Just today, we signed fresh partnership agreements with Sony Interactive Entertainment covering the presence of our games on PlayStation and in PlayStation Plus subscriptions, as well as our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign," mentioned studio CFO Piotr Nielubowicz. "This cooperation will support our business and financial outlook in this and future years.".

Sources indicate that nielubowicz didn't elaborate on what exactly that means for The Witcher 4's marketing, but perhaps it could enable Sony to advertise the action-RPG as a PlayStation game, similar to the Grand Theft Auto 6 'plays best on PlayStation' campaign. But seeing as The Witcher 4 is currently targeting a 2028 release, we may not find out what form that agreement takes for some time.

Sources indicate that that mentioned, looking at similar marketing agreements from Sony's recent history, we can probably expect The Witcher 4 to show up during Sony State of Play presentations or even have dedicated PlayStation-focused showcases. The Witcher 4 footage may also be specified as being captured on PlayStation hardware - something we already saw with The Witcher 4 tech demo last year. Perhaps we'll see Witcher-themed accessories and PlayStation hardware, too.

Industry observers note that this is a notable shift for CDPR, which has historically had close ties to Xbox. Cyberpunk 2077's big Keanu Reeves reveal (as Johnny Silverhand) took place on Xbox's E3 2019 stage, and The Witcher 3's reveal happened on Microsoft's E3 2013 stage. Going further back, Xbox 360 was the only console The Witcher 2 was released on.

During the same earnings call, CDPR joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski said that we should expect the leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4 to be similar to that between GTA 5 and GTA 6, while also praising Rockstar's approach to marketing its record-breaking newly introduced game.
