In a recent development, norse Atlantic Airways is fighting for its survival after warning on Wednesday that it needs fresh funding to continue operating. In a short stock exchange filing, the Norwegian low-cost, long-haul carrier described its financial situation as “critical.”.

The organization stated it had explored various strategic alternatives with its advisors and was open to discussions over a potential takeover.

More immediately, Norse is in talks with selected parties to secure short-term financing. However, the airline warned that “there can be no assurances that such discussions will result in a viable solution.”.

Sources indicate that shares in Norse plunged to an all-time low following the announcement, down more than 70% as of 1 p.m. Oslo-time on Wednesday. The organization did not disclose how much money it needs or how long it can continue operating without additional funding.
