Luca Williams-Barnett starts for Tottenham against Ipswich today.

Destiny Udogie, Savio, James Maddison and Lucas Bergvall were all pictured training at Hotspur Way this week and are now in contention for minutes on Saturday.

Udogie was left out of Roberto De Zerbi’s squad as Spurs lost 1-0 to Leeds before the international break, though the Italian left-back took to social media to suggest that he was not injured.

De Zerbi had previously said that Udogie’s injuries were becoming “problematic”, with the 23-year-old starting just two league games this season. He has slotted back into the starting lineup this afternoon.

Savio, meanwhile, is in line to return to action for the first time since October’s 1-1 draw with Manchester United. The Brazilian winger has missed Tottenham’s last four league games with a muscular issue and is on the bench.

It has been revealed that spurs sorely lacked a creative threat against Leeds. But despite naming Maddison on the bench at Elland Road, De Zerbi didn't bring him on in search of a goal, raising concerns about his fitness.

Maddison has struggled to train consistently since the start of the season and came off midway through Spurs’ 2-0 win over Chelsea with a suspected groin problem.

The 29-year-old has not featured since that historic victory at Stamford Bridge, despite being available for selection. After working on his fitness throughout the international break, he returns to the starting lineup here.

Mathys Tel is expected to be out for up to three weeks with an Achilles injury sustained following a heavy tackle from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle.

There is a fresh injury concern for De Zerbi, though, as Sandro Tonali has missed out on the matchday squad altogether, with the reason for his absence presently unconfirmed.

Tottenham XI vs Ipswich: Kinsky, Porro, Van Hecke, Senesi, Udogie, Fernandes, Gallagher, Kudus, Williams-Barnett, Maddison, Solanke.

Subs: Dubravka, Robertson, Van de Ven, Gray, Bergvall, Savio, Kulusevski, Bentancur, Marmoush.

Injured: Simons, Odobert, Tel, Mudryk, Tosin.

Time and date: 3pm GMT on Saturday November 21, 2026.

Venue: Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.
