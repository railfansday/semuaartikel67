When the January transfer window opens up, West Brom will be hoping they are still in the promotion mix to attract some desired targets.

The Baggies have been excellent under James Morrison so far this season, going into the latest international break unbeaten in their last six league games and securing four victories in that time.

Their latest 3-0 romping of Millwall last weekend leaves Albion fifth in the second-tier standings, sitting just one point off the summit in such a compact top eight.

It’s been a remarkable turnaround under Morrison, particularly when you factor in that the Black Country outfit underwent somewhat of a revamp this summer, recruiting 10 new players, many of whom had never played a minute in the Championship before their moves to The Hawthorns.

And with the Baggies setting their sights on returning to the Premier League, the January transfer window could be a pivotal time that helps the team get over the line in their quest.

There’s been much talk of West Brom adding to their forward department in 2027, and the Baggies could be bringing further youth at the top end of the pitch, with the club previously being linked with a move for Liverpool starlet Jayden Danns.

With this in mind, FLW’s West Brom fan pundit has issued his thoughts over whether the Baggies should add the 20-year-old to the group in January.

Across the 2023-24 season and into the first half of 2024-25, young Liverpool forward Jayden Danns looked to be on the verge of forcing his way into the first team picture at Anfield.

However, a back injury early in 2025 saw his development stutter and then in 2025-26 his minutes were quite limited.

And after being unable to play a major role in pre-season for the Reds in the summer, it was expected that the 20-year-old would be heading out on loan, with some ‘ITK’ sources connected to West Brom linking him with a move to The Hawthorns.

However, a deal wasn’t able to be completed and Danns has remained at Liverpool leading into January, unable to get an opportunity to make his mark in the Premier League.

The Baggies also held an interest in Danns during the winter transfer window of 2025, with Danns reportedly snubbing Albion in favour of a loan move to Sunderland, although he would not make an appearance for the Black Cats due to an injury glitch.

Speaking to Football League World, West Brom fan pundit Callum Burgess has warned against Albion trying to make it third time lucky in their pursuit of Jayden Danns, believing he is too similar in profile of a player to Jimmy-Jay Morgan, and that the Black Country outfit need to recruit a striker of a similar ilk to Cameron Archer, who the Baggies failed to bring to The Hawthorns in the summer.

Callum told FLW: “I think the issue you’ve got with Jayden Danns is that he could be too similar to Jimmy-Jay Morgan.

It has been revealed that “When you look at our forward options, we’ve got Morgan, who I just mentioned, who can operate as a second striker. You’ve got Heggebo as someone who can hold a ball up and bring others to play, then you’ve got Barney Stewart who operates as a target man or poacher.

“If Albion are to bring in another striker in January, it would probably be in a similar mould to Cameron Archer, who we tried to bring in, of course, but obviously won’t be coming here, someone similar to him who can stretch defences, who has speed and can add an extra dimension to Albion’s attack.”.

When it comes to the addition of a striker this January, West Brom must eye up a more recognised and proven Championship goalscorer that can help cement them in the promotion conversation.

Albion haven’t seen a rich number of goals from their forward options so far this season, so it would seem puzzling for the club to add yet another unknown quantity into the mix who has no previous experience of playing in the Championship.

Baggies fans, would you like to see West Brom make a move for Jayden Danns in January, or should the club look at alternative targets? Comment below!

As well as that, Danns’ injury record is a cause for concern too, so it would seem to be such a waste of vital financial resources that could be better spent on a striker with more Championship pedigree that could make an immediate impact upon signing.

It would be surprising to see Albion make a move for Danns this January, with the Baggies needing to show much more ambition if they are serious about ending their six-year exodus from the Premier League.
