Over the next several months leading up to the 2027 MLB season, hundreds of players will switch teams and sign new contracts -- and some will stay right where they were and sign new contracts. Hundreds of millions of dollars will change hands and new merchandise will flood the shops.

Tarik Skubal is the clear best player available on the market, an ace among aces who will play the entire season at age 30. Behind him are lesser but still solid options in guys like Jazz Chisholm Jr., Tyler Rogers, Randy Arozarena and Freddy Peralta.

The Atlanta Braves signed the first top 50 player on our list, adding reliever Evan Phillips on a three-year, $35 million contract on Nov. 12. A day later, the Boston Red Sox reached an agreement with first baseman Jake Bauers, the No. 12 player in our top 50. The latest deal for Bauers, who just won a World Series ring with the Milwaukee Brewers, spans four seasons and is worth $58 million, per ESPN. Dustin May, meanwhile, is headed to the White Sox on a three-year, $52.5 million deal.

It has been revealed that where will everyone land? CBS Sports will be keeping track of the top 50 free agents below.
