Latest Update: United Airlines resolves system outage that affected departing flights

🌐 Source: CNBC • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (246 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • United Airlines on Monday mentioned it had resolved a "widespread system outage" that briefly affected departing flights.
  • "We have resolved a technology glitch that delayed flight departures," the airline mentioned in a statement.
  • A United spokesperson said there was no indication that the outage was a cyberattack.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

United Airlines on Monday mentioned it had resolved a "widespread system outage" that briefly affected departing flights.

"We have resolved a technology glitch that delayed flight departures," the airline mentioned in a statement. "While some flights were delayed, there was no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights.

A United spokesperson said there was no indication that the outage was a cyberattack.

The Air Line Pilots Association, which represents pilots for United and other carriers, said in an earlier message to members seen by CNBC that the operational outage was "affecting multiple pilot facing applications" and that United could not "provide outbound flights with final weights.".

United's outage came three days after a nationwide ground stop halted departures across the country for more than four hours, snarling travel and leading to widespread delays and cancellations over the weekend.

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed the problems that led to Friday's ground stop on a router in Arkansas that overwhelmed systems that feed radar and other data for air traffic control. Federal Aviation Administration Administrator Bryan Bedford noted the upgrades were part of "routine" maintenance to switch over from copper wires to fiber optic.

There were significantly fewer flight delays and cancellations Monday than had been reported Sunday, according to FlightAware data.

There was no indication that United's outage Monday was related to the challenges that caused Friday's ground stop.

— CNBC's Leslie Josephs and Phil LeBeau contributed to this report.
