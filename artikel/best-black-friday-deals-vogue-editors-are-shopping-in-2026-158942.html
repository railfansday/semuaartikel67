📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Black Friday is officially here—and whether or not you made it through the roster of early sales that broke last week, it’s time to reap the benefits of waiting for the main event. With fresh discounts, deeper markdowns, and plenty of coveted finds now up for grabs, there’s no shortage of pieces worth adding to your cart today. With so much to sift through, I always like to start with recommendations from my fellow Vogue editors for inspiration.

Industry observers note that this year, the deeper discounts are calling—and Vogue shopping editors are finally pulling the trigger on the pieces they’ve been eyeing all season. From luxe outerwear and cozy cold-weather accessories to covetable footwear (think elevated mules, sleek leather boots, and everyday loafers), plus loads of chic home and beauty finds. These wish lists are filled with all manner of luxuries, both significant and small, at the best prices of the season.

I myself am grabbing a few fall wardrobe heroes—from Arma’s sumptuous suede blazer to Khaite’s deep indigo denim and A.Emery’s little black sock boots that go with just about everything. Why wait when some of the best pieces are finally within reach?

Consider this your inside look at what’s actually worth adding to your cart this Black Friday, as our team continues to scout the standout deals and wish-list-worthy pieces worth shopping through the Cyber sale weekend.

“I discovered heaps of floaty nightgowns at Dôen—they may not be seasonally appropriate, but they are on sale. I wear my wool mockneck from 440me nearly weekly at home. I have it in the camel color but am eyeing the white now that it’s on sale, as well as their black boatneck wool-silk shirt. In the home department, I’m slightly desperate for a new pair of slippers—plus anything jasmine-scented, I’m a fan.” —Elly Leavitt, lifestyle shopping editor.

Recent findings suggest that “For this year’s sale, I’m looking for accessories, accessories, accessories! Whenever I find myself in a wardrobe slump, the best way to make my essentials feel fresh again is to introduce newness in small doses. From bags to jewelry and shoes, the more the merrier!” —Minty Mellon, shopping market editor.

“This Black Friday, I am looking to buy some items I have had on my wishlist from fashion to beauty. I have had my eye on this pair of Róhe trousers, heeled booties by Jude, and an easy woven bag by Dragon Diffusion to wear all year round. On the self care end, the Dyson Airwrap and Therabody depuffer are two things I have been wanting to adt to my everyday routine. Of course, can’t forget to restock on bedsheets!” —Andrea Zendejas, shopping market editor.

“This Black Friday, I’m checking off items I’ve had on my wishlist all fall: a novelty coat, colorful mules, and animal-print accessories. I’m also picking up some layering basics and stocking up on my favorite nail polish!” —Fred Sahai, associate commerce producer.

Recent findings suggest that “Table lamps, floor lamps, portable lamps… I love lamp and always have room for one more, even if space suggests otherwise. The Lumens sale is where I’ll be spending most of my time, mulling over Gubis and Poulsens and whether or not I need to replace my current set of stools with these from Artek… I think the answer is yes.” —Talia Abbas, shopping director.

It has been revealed that “I have had my eye on a few pieces from both Deiji Studio and Belle Anna, and think I will finally pull the trigger during their Black Friday sales. My boyfriend also wants to restock our kitchen cookware, so I think we will be hitting Made In Cookware’s selection of stainless steel pots and pans.” —Dalya Benor, senior shopping writer.

Industry observers note that “I’ve been holding out on early sales for even more savings, as Black Friday officially kicks off. I was thrilled to find this suede Arma blazer within Net-a-Porter’s markdowns in my size, along with Khaite’s indigo denim and A.Emery’s little black sock boot—essentially the components of my everyday fall uniform. I can never have enough black cashmere turtlenecks in the winter, and I’m investing now in Lisa Yang’s perfectly slouchy silhouette at Shopbop—especially since it’s still full price elsewhere.” —Kristina Rutkowski, contributor.

Recent findings suggest that “This Black Friday, I’m prioritizing shopping for my baby boy, due in early 2027, but I can’t help sneaking in a little something for myself, too. This Shrimps striped cardigan is simply too cute to pass up, while the Doona stroller is a newborn must-have I’m ready to splurge on. Nour Hammour makes some of the most stunning, timeless outerwear, and I imagine this roomy shearling coat will be especially bump-friendly for my winter travels. Lastly, I’ve been loving the zebra-print trend taking over fashion right now, with my eye especially on these striped flats from Toteme.” —Laura Jackson, contributor.

“From all the wear and tear throughout the year, I use the end of year sales to restock on the best footwear to carry me through the months ahead. I usually stick to neutral colors to make sure I have options with every outfit. On my way to check out I’m usually tempted by a few spontaneous accessories, too…” —Kasey Busiel, marketing & production manager, shopping.
