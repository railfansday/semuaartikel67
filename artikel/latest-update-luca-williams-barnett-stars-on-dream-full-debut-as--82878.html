Luca Williams-Barnett lived up to the hype with a sparking display on his full debut for Tottenham to inspire a 2-0 win over Ipswich.

Roberto De Zerbi turned to the 18-year-old after two bright cameos and the highly-rated Spurs attacker delivered with aplomb, scoring on his first Premier League start after 15 minutes before a mazy run set up Dominic Solanke at the start of the second half.

After Williams-Barnett made his senior debut at the age of 16 last season, the clamour for the teenager to play has only increased during a difficult last 12 months and with injuries to Mathys Tel and Mykhailo Mudryk, the England Under-19 international grasped his chance to show why he has been hailed as a “special talent” by De Zerbi.

It was not a completely positive afternoon for De Zerbi with Savio forced off late on with a problem on his comeback after a seven-week lay-off.

However, this victory made it three home wins in a row for Tottenham in the Premier League for the first time in two years in further evidence a corner may have been turned in N17.

The excitement was palpable when it was confirmed Williams-Barnett would earn a first start for Tottenham with De Zerbi making six changes from the defeat at Leeds.

Gary O’Neil also shuffled his pack and went to a back five, but Ipswich started strongly and Antonin Kinsky was forced to deny Abdoul Ouattara and Zian Flemming in quick succession.

With De Zerbi in the stand due to a touchline ban, Spurs slowly started to get on top with Solanke curling wide before Williams-Barnett had the crowd on their feet with two dangerous crosses after lightning-quick step-overs.

Solanke went close again with a header across goal before Christian Walton denied the Tottenham forward from another set-piece, but the breakthrough arrived in 15th minute and via teenage sensation Williams-Barnett.

Pedro Porro was the architect with a wonderful cross from the right-hand side and Williams-Barnett revealed excellent hunger to get ahead of Ouattara before he bundled in with his left knee.

It made Williams-Barnett the youngest Tottenham scorer in the Premier League since Gareth Bale in 2007.

Spurs almost made it 2-0 by the half hour mark but Mohammed Kudus curled a fine free-kick against a post.

Ipswich threatened again soon after; the busy Conor Gallagher blocking an effort by Exequiel Palacios before Kinsky claimed a corner and James Maddison received a booking for a pull back on Julio Enciso.

There was still time for Williams-Barnett to have a header saved and Walton thwarted a Maddison curler before the break.

With the game still in the balance, Spurs required a second goal and Williams-Barnett stepped up to make the difference again in the 52nd minute.

After Williams-Barnett picked up the ball inside his own half, the 18-year-old raced past Darnell Furlong and sent across an inch-perfect cross for Solanke to slide home his third goal this season.

Williams-Barnett accepted the acclaim before he left to a standing ovation with 26 minutes left.

After Walton prevented an own-goal by Ipswich captain Dara O’Shea, a minute’s applause in memory of Danny Kelly followed in the 69th minute and whilst Savio was forced off not long after his cross hit a post, Tottenham made it four wins in five.
