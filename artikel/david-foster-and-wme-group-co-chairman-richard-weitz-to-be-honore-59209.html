David Foster and WME Group Co-Chairman Richard Weitz to be Honored by the New York Pops

According to recent reports, producer/composer David Foster and Richard Weitz, co-chairman of WME Group, will be honored by the Upcoming York Pops at the orchestra’s 44th Birthday Gala, to be held on Monday, April 26. Foster will be the music honoree; Weitz will be the inaugural visionary honoree.

According to recent reports, the event will be held at 7 p.m. in Stern Auditorium/Perelman Stage at Carnegie Hall. The concert will feature Foster’s songs performed by the New York Pops, led by its music director and conductor Steven Reineke, with guest artists to be confirmed.

Sources indicate that foster, who has won 16 Grammys (including three for producer of the year, non-classical), is set to receive the Kennedy Center Honors next month. Foster has also won a Primetime Emmy Award and a Golden Globe and has received three Oscar nominations for best original song. A native of Canada, Foster has also played a key role in the discovery and career introduces of such artists as Celine Dion, Josh Groban and Michael Bublé.

Weitz serves alongside Christian Muirhead as co-chairman of WME Group, which comprises WME, IMG Licensing and non-scripted content firm Pantheon Media Group.

Weitz has co-chaired the Upcoming York Pops Gala for the past four years. “I am so grateful to The New York Pops for this honor,” Weitz said in a statement. “I believe wholeheartedly in the importance of music education being available to all. The New York Pops provides essential music education programs to students that otherwise would not have this type of access and the organization’s annual Birthday Gala raises much-needed funds to support these programs.”.

Proceeds from the gala support the Fresh York Pops orchestra and the organization’s PopsEd music education programs. The concert is followed by a black-tie optional dinner and dance at the Mandarin Oriental New York.

Sources indicate that tickets to the general public go on sale on Monday, Nov. 23 at 11 a.m. via the Carnegie Hall’s website, at 212-247-7800 or the Carnegie Hall box office.
