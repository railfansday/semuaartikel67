📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jensen Huang is scheduled to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3, according to a notice on the committee’s website.

The development comes just hours after Huang was reinstated as Nvidia chief executive, less than two weeks following his resignation after it was revealed that he engaged in at least two extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected by the U.S. government of working as a Chinese spy.

It has been revealed that the Senate Banking Committee has jurisdiction over export control policies that are designed to prevent sensitive U.S. technologies, such as chips capable of powering AI models, from falling into the hands of foreign governments and militaries.

The public hearing follows bipartisan calls for Huang to testify about his affair with the suspected Chinese spy and his potential involvement in chip-smuggling operations.

It’s worth noting that before his reinstatement as CEO, Huang had already agreed to appear before the House Foreign Affairs Committee, the Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, and the House Intelligence Committee on December 1.
