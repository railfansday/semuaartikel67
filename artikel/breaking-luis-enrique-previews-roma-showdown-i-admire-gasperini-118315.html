Breaking: Luis Enrique previews Roma showdown: “I admire Gasperini.”

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Four wins in as many Champions League matches.

According to recent reports, twenty goals scored and an average of five goals per game. PSG are a steamroller, and tomorrow at the Parc des Princes, Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma will have to put in a legendary performance to emerge unscathed from a match that inevitably sees them as underdogs.

On the eve of the match, Luis Enrique spoke at a press conference and outlined tomorrow’s battle against the Roman side.

On his memories from his brief spell in Rome, Enrique said, “It’s been 15 years since then, and it was a very special experience for me. I have fond memories of my year in Rome. I get a wonderful feeling every time I have the chance to visit Rome. It’s a magnificent city, and I’m happy. Of course, things can go better or worse, but that’s life, and you have to accept it. My memories of Rome are very positive.”.

“I think Gasperini is a very experienced coach. I really like him because his teams have a very clear identity and style of play, very different from what we’re used to. I remember when I was in Rome he was at Inter. It was his first year at Inter, and it didn’t end well for him because Inter didn’t have the confidence to keep a coach like Gasperini.”.

Sources indicate that “He’s proven his worth and is a top-notch coach. I like watching this type of coach, and as a coach, it’s a motivation for me to observe Gasperini’s teams. Tomorrow we’ll face some difficulties; we’ll see what kind of team they are and their particular way of defending. It’s very different from what we’re used to. It’ll be a great challenge to face.”.

Industry observers note that “I think Gasperini is clearly a phoenix of Italian football. He has a very different way of looking at football, which I like because he tries to constantly attack and be better than the opponent. His teams constantly press, and it’s unusual to see that in Italian football. He’s remained true to his way of understanding football. His team has a recognizable identity and very different characteristics from the others. They’re very physical, but at the same time they have quality players. If, as a coach, you have the ability to convince your quality players to do the defensive work, it means you’re a top-notch coach. It’s wonderful to have coaches like him, and, I repeat, I’m an admirer of Gasperini.”.
