Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are officially married! Nearly one year after their engagement was confirmed, the couple tied the knot on Saturday, November 21, in Palm Springs, California.

The singer, who noted “I do” in Saint Laurent, shared black-and-white photos from the wedding on Instagram, simply captioning the post, “M+M 🤍 11.21.26.” In photos, the couple could be seen exchanging vows in a poolside ceremony featuring a stunning display of sweeping white flowers and candles.

Recent findings suggest that in December 2025, Cyrus confirmed her engagement to musician Maxx Morando—a relationship she has largely kept out of the spotlight following her highly publicized marriage to Liam Hemsworth. The Bass Persuades singer was first linked to Morando in November 2021, when the two sparked romance rumors with a joint appearance at the Gucci Love Parade fashion show. (Cyrus later shared a photo of herself with Morando from the event on Instagram.).

Rumors escalated the following month, when Cyrus danced and held hands with Morando in Miami during her Latest Year’s Eve Party special for NBC. By April 2022, they confirmed their romance during an outing in West Hollywood, where they went all out with the PDA.

Fast forward to the 2024 Grammys and Morando supported Cyrus at the show—where she won her first Grammy for “Flowers”—and he gave her a passionate kiss.

It has been revealed that since then, the two have kept their relationship low-key aside from a few red carpet appearances together. But while Morando has mostly kept his personal life out of the public spotlight, he, like Cyrus, has had a long career in music.

Ahead, everything we know about Cyrus’s upcoming husband, Maxx Morando.

While best known as the drummer for Liily (and for the Regrettes, from 2015 to 2018), Morando also plays guitar. Additionally, he has also collaborated with Cyrus on several of her albums, including her most recent release, Bass Persuades. For the project, he co-produced the album’s title track alongside Cyrus, as well as several others, including “Aftermath” and “REDLIGHTS.” The musician also has songwriting credits, co-writing “Innocent Ways” with Cyrus and being credited on “Aftermath.”.

Morando also produced two songs, “Handstand” and “Violet Chemistry,” from the singer’s Endless Summer Vacation LP.

He also collaborated with Cyrus on her 2025 album, Something Beautiful, contributing as both a producer and songwriter on several tracks. Morando also played drums on the record and made a cameo in the “End of the World” music video.

In a recent development, the drummer, born in 1998, is six years Cyrus’s junior. (His birthday also happens to fall exactly a week ahead of the singer’s, on November 16.).

According to recent reports, cyrus opened up about their slight age gap in a 2024 interview with Harper’s Bazaar: “He grew up with a laptop. I had a desktop computer that I shared with my brothers and sisters. … Honestly, he’s raised our dog off Reddit. I’m like, Are you sure we’re supposed to be doing this? And he’s like, On Reddit it indicates blah, blah, blah.”.

Along with his musical endeavors, the drummer has dabbled in fashion design too, with Cyrus as his muse.

Cyrus revealed in September 2021 that Morando contributed cartoon designs to a custom vegan leather set by designer Shane Kastl, which Cyrus wore for a performance that month.

Recent findings suggest that “This look is a one-of-a-kind collaboration between two of my favorite emerging artists, Maxx Morando and Shane Kastl,” Cyrus mentioned of the special look in an interview. “Their collaboration is the perfect example of sustainability becoming the next fashion phase, and it proves that anything can be reinvented, which resonates with me deeply.”.

According to recent reports, in an interview for Bazaar’s December 2024 cover story, Cyrus opened up about her relationship with Morando, saying, “He’s very similar to me. We just don’t take life too seriously.”.

It has been revealed that she also shared how working with Morando on musical projects helped them bond: “I worked with my dad forever. That’s how me and my ex-husband met each other. I’ve always worked with the people that I love,” Cyrus notes. “And Maxx just inspires me so much.”.

The couple’s engagement was confirmed in December 2025, after Cyrus sparked rumors by wearing a diamond sparkler on her left-hand ring finger at the premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash in LA.

Sources indicate that morando keeps an extremely private, low profile in both his personal and professional life. While the musician does have an Instagram, he rarely posts, aside from sharing upcoming music that he works on with both his band and Cyrus.

In March 2023, a source told People that Morando is a “cool guy” who is drama-free, a trait that Cyrus finds extremely attractive.

In a recent development, “He has no interest in being a huge celebrity; he’s very low-key. Miley loves these qualities,” the source said. “Miley can be herself. They are supportive of each other’s careers. Miley is enjoying life and things are great.”.

Following a year-long engagement, the Morando and Cyrus officially wed in an intimate ceremony held in Palm Springs, California. The couple shared photos from the wedding on Instagram, which included black-and-white portraits of the couple with their dog, as well as a sweet photo of them kissing after saying “I do.”.

For the ceremony, the bride wore a long-sleeve, backless Saint Laurent wedding gown, while Morando kept things classic in a black suit.

The poolside ceremony took place at Frank Sinatra’s former home, which was transformed with hundreds of white roses for the occasion. In photos obtained by DeuxMoi, the couple could be seen exchanging vows by the pool, in front of an intimate crowd of guests dressed in all-black attire.
