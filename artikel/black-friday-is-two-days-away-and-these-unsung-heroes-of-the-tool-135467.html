Every toolbox has its stars. The cordless drill, the circular saw, the impact driver. Big, powerful tools that get most of the attention. And when I do—Wow—I sure am glad they’re there.

I’m talking about the clever tools specifically designed for certain situations that make everyday jobs easier. A Klein Voltage Tester so I don’t mistakenly zap myself as I replace a wall receptacle. An Olight Flashlight, small enough to carry in my shirt pocket yet bright enough to light up the night. Or a Japanese-style pull saw that makes clean, precise cuts the old-school way—by hand. These might not be the flashiest tools in my shop, but I’ve come to dedicate a singular drawer to them as I use them so often.
