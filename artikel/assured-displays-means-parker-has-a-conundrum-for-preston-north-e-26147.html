West Ham return to championship football on Saturday with the visit of Preston North End and must do so without one of their ever-presents this season, Maximilian Kilman, who is suspended for the home game.

Which produces something of a dilemma over who should replace Kilman, withWest Ham of course being short of a player ever since defender Jean-Clair Todibo was loaned out in the summer.

Fortunately, both West Ham defenders in action last night for their respective national sides came through unscathed and on the winning sides, meaning the West Ham manager now has to pick two defenders from three available for the Saturday home tie:.

Dinos Mavropanos put the seven goal horror show of last week behind him with a more or less solid performance as Greece kept a clean sheet and defeated Serbia 1-0.

Airidas Golambeckis will be hoping his England performance in the u20’s has helped his selection chances.

Teenager Airidas Golambeckis was part of the England u20 side who defeated Portugal 2 – 1 and apparently produced an ‘assured display‘ – so says his own club web site – thus strengthening his own case for selection.

However Loanee Morato will be hoping to revert to his more natural centre-back position and partner Mavropanos himself.

Scott Parker has to pick two from that threesome in his attempt to shore up West Ham’s back line, which has been weak in recent weeks, conceding eleven times in the last four outings.

The more cautious, logical approach would seem to favour the Brazilian partnering Mavropanos but the heart would favour Golambeckis who, for a teenager, has performed well whenever West Ham have called on him.

Industry observers note that tricky choice for West Ham’s newly introduced manager: Good news at least that both came through last night’s ties unscathed with a massive game coming up. Scott Parker needs a win and West Ham badly need a clean sheet in front of their own, highly critical supporters at London Stadium.
