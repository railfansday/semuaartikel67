It's been a significant week of outages, from the Federal Aviation Authority (FAA) grounding flights across America to Microsoft services going down worldwide— but Donald Trump wants you to know it's no big deal.

Industry observers note that in the Daily Show clip above, host Michael Kosta plays a clip of the U.S. president saying "it's life" and "it's fine" in response to press questions about the outages.

Sources indicate that "It's fine?" states host Michael Kosta. "Maybe for Trump it's fine. He doesn't fly commercial, he doesn't know how to use email, his bank is a guy from Qatar. For the rest of us, though, it's not fine. So your message to the American people is that things like this happen? I mean, do they? Because I remember airplanes took off, and my bank account was accessible, and that was actually, like, a really cool time.".
