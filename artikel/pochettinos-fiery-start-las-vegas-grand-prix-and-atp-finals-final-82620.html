To the background buzz of Australian cricket ripping itself apart over sandpapergate, England kick off their T20 series at the same venue they became world champions four years ago. The Melbourne Cricket Ground has not always been a happy hunting ground for touring England sides but Harry Brook’s last two visits as captain have been triumphant. A two-day win at the 90,000-capacity venue in last year’s Boxing Day Test was not only the high point of a traumatic Ashes campaign; it was their first red-ball victory on Australian soil since 2011. MCG also hosted England’s T20 World Cup triumph in 2022, where Brook was part of the side that defeated Pakistan to lift the trophy behind enemy lines. Rob Smyth has over-by-over coverage with Ali Martin reporting from Melbourne.

Wins over Hull and Coventry came before draws with Aston Villa and Newcastle. Barry Glendenning helms the blog as Will Unwin reports from the Etihad Stadium.

England are have been transformed since stumbling through the Six Nations and then falling to South Africa at Ellis Park, winning four consecutive Tests, amassing 36 tries and addressing their disciplinary lapses. Only South Africa are higher in the rankings and a year out from the 2027 World Cup final, it is the opportunity for Steve Borthwick’s men to make a statement.
