Almost €800m was spent among Bundesliga clubs on signings in the summer, per Transfermarkt, but while Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund have splashed big on Ismael Saibari and Konstantinos Karetsas, there’s been some excellent signings throughout the 18 teams.

Flourishing at RB Leipzig since returning to the club on loan in the summer, Christopher Nkunku has returned five goals and provided two assists since returning to the Saxony club. The Frenchman provided an assist against Borussia Mönchengladbach on his second debut before opening his account at the Red Bull Arena in Leipzig’s 5-0 victory over Hamburg. The 29-year-old then struck a hat-trick against Eintracht Frankfurt before adding a goal and assist to his 2026/27 tally against Augsburg prior to the international break. Often starting in a three-pronged attack alongside Romulo and Antonio Nusa/Tidiam Gomis, Nkunku is given the freedom to roam by Martin Demichelis, which makes his a nuisance for opposition defenders. Ranked in the 95th percentile for xGoals on target (4.14), Nkunku has proved clinical in front of goal, scoring five of his eight attempts on target.

Recent findings suggest that the Spaniard has big boots to fill following the departure of Alejandro Grimaldo, but the former Napoli man is settling into life at Bayer Leverkusen just fine. The 25-year-old has scored one goal and created a further four in his nine Bundesliga starts since swapping Italy for Germany. Equally adept at playing as the left-sided centre-back in a back three, or as the left-back or left-wing back in a back four/five, no defender ranks higher in the Bundesliga than Gutiérrez for assists and big chances created (eight) in Germany’s top flight this season.

Industry observers note that hennes Behrens at €3.5m is proving to be a steal for Augsburg, who are proving to be one of the Bundesliga’s best recruiters. Joining from Hoffenheim after spending last season on loan at Heidenheim, the 21-year-old is proving a useful weapon from left-back, where he’s registered three assists in nine Bundesliga outings this term. Possessing explosive pace and pinpoint crossing, Behrens is fast becoming one of the league’s most useful full-backs in the final third.

Industry observers note that re-joining Werder Bremen on loan after a failed spell abroad, Niclas Füllkrug is firing on all cylinders back at the Weserstadion. The 33-year-old sits at the top of the Bundesliga’s scoring charts this term having scored nine goals in as many appearances – seven of those have come from set-piece situations. Playing with a smile on his face once again, it’s great to see Füllkrug back to his best, which has also seen him return to the German national team. Carry on, and Bremen could well be competing for a place in Europe if the veteran goal-getter remains injury free and keeps up his output.

In a recent development, joining newly promoted Elversberg from Bayern Munich having spent last season on loan at 2. Bundesliga side Hertha Berlin, Maurice Krattenmacher has scored four goals and provided one assist in nine Bundesliga games this season – of which he’s only started four. The 21-year-old came off the bench to score a brace against Gladbach to secure a dramatic 4-3 victory for Vincent Wagner’s side, before netting against Bayern the following matchday with a superb curling effort. The attacking-midfielder also registered an assist and scored in wins of Augsburg and Mainz as the Bundesliga newcomers enjoy a magnificent start to their Bundesliga tenure.
