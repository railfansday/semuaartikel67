It has been revealed that zinédine Zidane and his players are set. Winners of Turkey (2-1) last Sunday, the Blues finished at the top of their Nations League group and will face a runner-up from League A in the quarter-finals (return) next March. With the Final Four in sight at the beginning of June. The France team now knows its three possible opponents after the last two matches of the first round this Tuesday.

Portugal won against Wales (2-3) and will be among the top seeds alongside the Blues. After a lively first period, with goals from Bruno Fernandes and Pedro Neto while the British led 2-1, it was Bruges winger Carlos Forbs who gave victory to his team 20 minutes from time (70th).

Meanwhile, Denmark-Norway was crazy! The Danes led 2-0 thanks to Rasmus Hojlund and Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg, but Erling Haaland and his teammates made a superb comeback in Copenhagen, with a first goal from the Norwegian cyborg, an equalizer from Martin Odegaard in the 83rd minute and a winning penalty from Haaland at the end of the suspense (96th).

Norway therefore finishes second in this group and is one of the three possible opponents of the France team, with the Netherlands and Spain, in the quarter-finals of the League of Nations (Belgium is the 4th runner-up in the group). The draw is scheduled for Friday noon.
