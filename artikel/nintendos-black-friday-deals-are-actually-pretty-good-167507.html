Industry observers note that black Friday is nearly upon us. From now through Cyber Monday, retailers and companies alike are offering deals and discounts on their wares. While I always keep an eye out for Nintendo deals offered by stores like Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart, it seems the manufacturer is offering its own discounts this year—and, surprisingly, they're actually pretty solid.

According to recent reports, last Thursday, Nintendo revealed its Black Friday discounts. We've already seen Switch 2 sales ahead of the Black Friday season, but now, Nintendo has confirmed an official discount for the console.

It has been revealed that starting Nov. 22 through Dec. 5, you can pick up Nintendo's Switch 2 Mario Kart bundle for $499.00, down $50 from $549.99. That includes the console, a download of Mario Kart World, and a 90-day Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership.

In a recent development, if you already have a Switch 2 console and/or Mario Kart World, you're not left out of the Black Friday deals here. From Nov. 22 through Nov. 29, Nintendo is also running discounts on several high-profile Switch and Switch 2 games:.

Donkey Kong Bananza was one of Nintendo's launch titles for the Switch 2. And while it's been out for over a year, Donkey Kong's first 3D game is still among the best-reviewed titles on the console. Nintendo is taking $20 off the game's MSRP for this promotion.

According to recent reports, kirby Air Riders on Switch 2 is only the second installment in the series. The original debuted all the way back on the GameCube, and while the title typically runs for $69.99, Nintendo is letting it go for $49.99.

Metroid Prime 4 arrived last year after an 18-year wait from Metroid Prime 3: Corruption. The game typically retails for $69.99 for a physical copy on Switch 2, but Nintendo is selling it for $49 during its early Black Friday promotion.

Super Mario Galaxy (both the original and the sequel) are beloved by fans. These titles originally introduced on the Wii, and Nintendo rereleased both for the Switch. (The company offers a free upgrade to the Switch 2 edition.) Nintendo asks $69.99 for this combo, and while you can find it for under $60, you'll officially be able to pick it up for $48.97 before Black Friday.

Industry observers note that nintendo's latest Mario Party, Jamboree, has a Switch 2 edition with a rather staggering MSRP of $79.99. While it's the version of the game with the most features, it's also the most expensive. You'll be able to buy it for $39.99 for Black Friday, saving up to $40.

Nintendo rarely offers official discounts on some of its biggest titles, especially in this current era. In addition, Nintendo revealed a sale on its digital store that runs from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2, though it didn't say which titles would be on sale. Just keep an eye on all these dates: The Switch 2 console promotion runs longer than the physical and digital games discounts, while the digital sale runs longer than the physical sale. Nintendo notes that these discounts will be available "at select retailers," as well as its brick-and-mortar locations in Upcoming York and San Francisco. You can also buy the Switch 2 bundle through Nintendo's website.

It has been revealed that it depends! Its Nintendo Switch 2 bundle deal runs from Nov. 22 through Dec. 5; the select games listed above will be on sale from Nov. 22 through Nov. 29; and digital titles will have discounts from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2.

While we don't know which digital titles will be discounted yet, we do know that physical copies of Donkey Kong Bananza, Kirby Air Riders, Metroid Prime 4: Beyond, Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2, and Super Mario Party: Jamboree will be on sale for $39.99 each.

Yes! The Switch 2 alone costs $499.99, so you essentially get the game for free. Mario Kart World is also one of the console's best multiplayer titles right now, so it makes sense to pick it up in this bundle.
