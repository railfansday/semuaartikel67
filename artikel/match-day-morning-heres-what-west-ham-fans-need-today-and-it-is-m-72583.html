Match day morning : Here’s what West Ham fans need today – and it is more than just ‘hope’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that there have been two weeks of no football and only fine words by which to judge Scott Parker’s fresh era at West Ham. Well received, the new manager has expressed himself eloquently in terms that supporters will love. However, this is the fifth manager in three years to come through the doors at London Stadium and each one has spoken with well-judged words.

This is the first game in which Parker’s work to date will be judged by West Ham’s home supporters.

Fans have ‘newly introduced manager fatigue’ – if there is such a thing. Those players who have been at the club for three years probably the same.

There’ll be an expectancy that those ‘fine words’ of Parker and Noble are turned into action on the pitch. As Mark Carlaw, writing on thewesthamway.com put it “It feels like our club is in good hands again” ..But there have been so many false dawns and fine words with the appointments of Lopetegui, Potter and Nuno all failing in their own unique ways to deliver the club from its tailspin towards mediocrity that judgement will be suspended until well after 5pm this afternoon.

“Pure grit, determination that I feel West Ham should have……….

it’s about effort, aggressiveness, a way of playing, and a real identity in a team that the fans can relate to.”.

Mark Noble’s own honest summary nailed it, so it’s time today time to see how our new boss is getting on in instilling this into the same bunch of players that just let in fourteen goals in the previous five games. So forgive us, Scotty P, if we hope for the best but prepare for the worst, just in case. Because this is West Ham after all. We know how this can go.

As John Cleese’s character famously put it in ‘Clockwise’:.

Sources indicate that “Its not the despair.. I can take the despair.. it’s the hope I can’t stand”.
