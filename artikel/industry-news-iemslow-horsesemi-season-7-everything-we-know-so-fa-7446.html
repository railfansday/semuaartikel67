In a recent development, after delivering a cliffhanger ending in season 6, fans of Slow Horses are wondering how the story could continue once season 7 rolls around. The fans are in luck—the MI5 agents of Slough House are clocking back in for another season, and the franchise doesn’t seem to be headed for an end anytime soon.

“I know that Gary [Oldman] has expressed a desire to play Lamb forever and that Mick [Herron] is still writing the books, so I hope it will go for as long as people want it,” the show’s executive producer, Will Smith, remarked in an October 2024 interview with TV Insider.

Here’s everything we know so far about Slow Horses season 7.

Yes! In July 2025, Deadline confirmed that Slow Horses would come back for a seventh season. The streaming platform greenlit the show’s seventh season before the fifth season of the show aired.

Gary Oldman, Jack Lowden, Kristin Scott Thomas, Jonathan Pryce, Hugo Weaving, Saskia Reeves, Rosalind Eleazar, Christopher Chung, Aimee-Ffion Edwards, Joanna Scanlan, Tom Brooke, Lucian Msamati, and Olivia Cooke are confirmed to return for season 7 of Slow Horses.

On October 19, Apple TV revealed that Varada Sethu (Andor, Doctor Who), Ethan Moorhouse (The Moment, Ludwig), Tuppence Middleton (Downton Abbey: A Upcoming Era), Merab Ninidze (Homeland), Joshua McGuire (Anatomy of a Scandal), and James Callis (Bridget Jones’s Diary)—who is returning—will join the season 7 cast.

According to an official press release, the season “is adapted from the Bad Actors novel in the Mick Herron Slough House book series, and sees Lamb and his Slow Horses on the hunt to find and neutralize a mole at the heart of the British Government before they can bring down the state.”.
