Mexico captures drug cartel boss ‘El Chapo Isidro’ after deadly firefight

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, mexico's military forces captured ​one of the most wanted suspected drug lords, Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro," the military announced on Saturday, after a deadly firefight that killed seven suspected attackers and four soldiers. Flores, one of the FBI’s 10 most wanted fugitives, was flown to Mexico City after his capture.
