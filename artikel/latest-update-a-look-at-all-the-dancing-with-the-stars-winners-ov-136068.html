Industry observers note that at this point, Dancing With the Stars has been on for a frightening amount of time—meaning a truly shocking number of celebrities have wrapped themselves in stretchy glitter onesies and pirouetted their way around that hallowed ballroom while regretting all their life choices, deep in contemplation about firing their agent. In fact, 35 stars have already taken home the coveted Mirrorball Trophy—ranging from people only your mom could pick out of an early ’90s lineup to some pretty famous faces, including several Olympians/Paralympians and Bachelor/Bachelorette stars.

In honor of DWTS season 35 coming to a close (no, I’m not okay, but thanks for asking), let’s take a look back at allllllllll the Dancing With the Stars winners throughout history.

Runners-up: John O’Hurley & Charlotte Jørgensen.

Sources indicate that about Kelly: She’s a model and actress best known for playing Sam McCall on General Hospital. For more info, ask my grandma, who’s been watching that soap for years.

Industry observers note that about Drew: This dude is Nick Lachey’s younger brother who was also in 98 Degrees. You just fully forgot he existed, it’s fine.

Runners-up: Mario Lopez & Karina Smirnoff.

About Emmitt: He’s a running back who played for the Dallas Cowboys! But obviously, this DWTS win is also important.

Runners-up: Joey Fatone & Kym Johnson.

About Apolo: He’s a not-at-all casual eight-time Olympic track speed skating medalist. I mean...his talent.

Hélio Castroneves & Julianne Hough.

Runners-up: Mel B & Maksim Chmerkovskiy.

About Hélio: He’s a Brazilian race car driver who won the Indy 500 three full times, no bigs.

Runners-up: Evan Lysacek & Anna Trebunskaya.
