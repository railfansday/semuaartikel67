NBA star Tyrese Haliburton and his wife, Jade Jones, are expecting their first child.

“Baby Hali 🧸Spring 2027,” Jones, 28, wrote via Instagram on Tuesday, November 24, alongside a carousel of photos that showed the couple posing with their dog in a garden. The snaps also included a print of Jones’ ultrasound that included four images of their baby.

Haliburton, 26, and Jones’ furry pooch, a mini goldendoodle called Ames, also sported a scarf that read “Major Brother.” In another adorable shot, the athlete cradled his wife’s growing belly from behind as he leant down and kissed the side of her face.

A string of high-profile sports stars congratulated the couple in the post’s comments section, including Simone Biles, who wrote, “OMGGGG CONGRATS” with two white heart emojis, and Caitlin Clark, who wrote, “I CAN’T WAIT, love you both!!!”.

Recent findings suggest that the official Indiana Pacers Instagram account also jumped in, writing “Congratulations” before adding yellow and blue heart emojis. Haliburton’s Pacers teammates also indicated their support, including Jay Huff, who wrote “Congrats y’all!” and Andrew Nembhard, who wrote, “Holayy.”.

The duo’s baby news comes after they tied the knot in July in a small courthouse ceremony. The intimate affair was followed with a much larger celebration the following month, which included a performance by rapper 50 Cent.

Wedding bells rang for Haliburton and Jones less than two months after one of Jones’ friends died during the bride’s bachelorette party in St. Barths. According to TMZ, Makenzi Kern died “unexpectedly” due to “health complications” and her family did not suspect any foul play, drugs or alcohol involvement.

Recent findings suggest that jones acknowledged the tragedy via Instagram on June 17, paying tribute to her former friend. “Kenzi was the funniest, goofiest person I’ve ever met,” she wrote at the time. “She was always making me belly laugh. She had such a gift of bringing happiness to every room she was in. Some of my favorite memories with Kenzi are the simple ones — spending a summer living in her city, leaving our mark on the capital district, running around our cars at stop lights, cheering on the bandshell together, obsessing over The Office, dancing on countertops to Hannah Montana, and watching her light up anytime she got to be on a boat.”.

It has been revealed that haliburton also shared an Instagram Story on the same day, honoring their late friend. “We will always love and miss you Kenz,” he wrote at the time. “Forever thankful for you.”.
