We’re only a couple of months away from our next Galaxy Unpacked, and while the expanded Galaxy S27 series is practically guaranteed to show up, the real fun tends to lie in whatever other gadgets show up alongside Samsung’s next flagship smartphone release. Based on some latest leaks, it’s sure looking like the next mobile accessory on the firm’s docket is a return to over-ear wireless headphones, and so far, they’re sounding pretty promising.

Samsung hasn’t produced over-ear headphones in about a decade — under its own name, at least — but it looks like that’s about to change. We hadn’t heard much in the way of details on the company’s next audio release, rumored to be named ‘Galaxy Studio,’ but a fresh leak courtesy of SamMobile is finally changing that. We’ll have to keep waiting for full renders, but these leaked graphics are giving us a general idea of what they might look like, plus a couple of key features.

It has been revealed that unsurprisingly, Galaxy Studio will include ANC, complete with a dedicated button to toggle the setting between its usual modes. Volume controls also could come in the shape of physical buttons, but playback controls appear to be relegated to a touch-sensitive area on its earpads, something that’s become relatively standard among the flagship headphone competition. SamMobile also reports it’ll come in “at least” black and red colorways, while a leak from earlier this month suggested rotational earcups.

Again, I don’t want to read too much into these leaked graphics, as it’s always possible the final release has a handful of changes. Based on these images, though, I’m pleasantly surprised to see Samsung avoiding an attempt at copying Apple’s AirPods Max design. The larger cups, combined with their ability to twist and turn for easy storage, sounds much closer to most (but not all) of Sony’s over-ear efforts across the last decade or so, and when it comes to headphones, there are much, much worse choices you could look to for inspiration. If Samsung can hit the right price on these — or bundle them with one of their ever-more-expensive smartphones — they could be a real winner.
