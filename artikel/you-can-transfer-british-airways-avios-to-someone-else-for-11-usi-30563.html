You Can Transfer British Airways Avios To Someone Else For $11 Using The Finnair Loophole

💡 Key Takeaways
  • British Airways charges most members £50 per transaction to give Avios to someone else, and each transfer tops out at 60,000 points.
  • You can move British Airways Avios into a Finnair account, and Finnair charges €10 to transfer Avios to another member.
  • According to recent reports, I’ve written about moving Avios between airline programs to get the best redemption deal.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

British Airways charges most members £50 per transaction to give Avios to someone else, and each transfer tops out at 60,000 points. Move the same points through Finnair and you can give someone 100,000 Avios for a single €10 fee. You can move British Airways Avios into a Finnair account, and Finnair charges €10 to transfer Avios to another member. I’ve written about moving Avios between airline programs to get the best redemption deal. The same flexibility lets you choose which program’s rules to use when giving points to another person. For two people with British Airways balances, the process is: Each person links their own British Airways and Finnair accounts. Names and email addresses need to match within each person’s accounts, and both programs need two-factor authentication enabled. The person giving the points moves their Avios into their own Finnair account. That transfer is free and 1:1. They use Finnair Shop’s transfer function to send the Avios to the other person’s Finnair account, paying €10. The recipient can use the points through Finnair or move them into their own linked British Airways account, again free at 1:1. Under the British Airways transfer terms, sending 100,000 Avios takes two transactions at £50 apiece, or the equivalent charged in your home currency (Gold members are exempt from that fee). Senders and recipients are each limited to 200,000 Avios per calendar year. Finnair doesn’t cap the amount or frequency of member transfers beyond your available balance. However, moving the points between airlines has its own limits. Open the accounts in advance. Finnair requires an account to be at least 30 days old to link with another Avios program. The sender must have been a member for at least 90 days to transfer to another person online. Finnair allows newer members to transfer via customer care at a higher €20 transfer fee. This route is for adults. Linking requires members to be at least 18, and you can’t transfer Avios into a Finnair Plus Junior account. Transfer ceiling. Finnair allows 1–999,999 Avios per cross-program transaction. Basic members have a shared 1 million Avios limit over 12 months, counting all linked programs and both directions. Higher tiers don’t have that 12-month cap. You may not need to pay anything. British Airways’ free household account pools balances for redemptions, and you can often simply book an award for someone else. But this lets you do it without restricting redemptions from your account to members of your household account. Don’t leave the points sitting in a dormant Finnair account. Transfers don’t restart their 18-month expiration clock. One Reddit member reported losing newly transferred Avios less than two weeks later because the Finnair account was already inactive.
