McKennie claims USA players could follow Pochettino to Manchester United

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that weston McKennie has remarked USA players could follow Mauricio Pochettino to Manchester United, days after his national team coach took the job there. Juventus, already without Thuram and Locatelli, now face pressure to rule out a mid-season sale.

Sources indicate that the question put to Weston McKennie was whether USA players might follow their coach to Old Trafford. His answer was that it could potentially happen.

In a recent development, mcKennie called the speculation inevitable. He said many USA players are due to change clubs within the next year, calciomercato.com reported.

Sources indicate that pochettino was asked about the same subject. He said now was not the right time to discuss it. He added that United are among the very top clubs and a dream for many players.

Pochettino and his staff remain in charge of the USA for two games against Haiti, on 14 and 17 November. United agreed with the federation that the staff would be released once those national-team duties were over. Pochettino became USA head coach in September 2024. His record with the national team stands at 21 wins, 12 defeats and two draws from 35 matches.

Recent findings suggest that the comments land awkwardly in Turin. Reports had already linked United with a January move for the American. Juventus regard him as central to Luciano Spalletti’s prepares and are reluctant to let him leave mid-season, ESPN reported.

His contract, which runs until June 2030, offers the club some protection. It was signed only months before Pochettino’s switch to English football.

McKennie arrived from Schalke 04 in August 2020. His value to Spalletti lies in his flexibility.

The club noted in its renewal announcement that he had been used on both flanks under Spalletti. He had also recently played as a striker.

In a recent development, the timing makes McKennie harder to replace. He is one of the few senior midfielders Spalletti still has available.

Thuram is out until 2027 after knee surgery. Captain Manuel Locatelli also faces a long absence after twisting his right knee against Milan. He had surgery in Lyon on 8 September to repair the external meniscus, and Corriere dello Sport put his lay-off at five to six months.

Losing him in January would thin the squad further at a delicate point of the season. Juventus are chasing a Champions League place. Qualification is key to the club’s finances.

That is why the club’s stance matters as much as the player’s words.

Industry observers note that mcKennie’s own answer was measured. He stated nobody knows which players a coach really wants.

In a recent development, for now, the pressure falls on Juventus. A clear statement ruling out a January sale would end the debate. Silence would leave it open until the window.

The next step comes at Old Trafford. Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United from 18 November, and his first squad decisions will show whether the interest is real.
