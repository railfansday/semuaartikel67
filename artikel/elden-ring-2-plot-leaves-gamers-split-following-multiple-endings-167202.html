Elden Ring 2 Plot Leaves Gamers Split Following Multiple Endings

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that the potential plot for Elden Ring 2 has left gamers split due to the original Elden Ring’s multiple endings. Depending on the choices you make during the game and the questlines you follow during the campaign, you may unlock different endings for the game. In total, there are six different endings for Elden Ring, each of which focuses on a certain aspect of lore. The regular one, for example, is centered around the Tarnished becoming an Elden Lord, even though the fate of the Lands Between isn’t really changed by that.

The other endings can drastically impact what it would mean for the future of the Lands Between, and this has become a serious topic of discussion among fans who are thinking about a potential sequel to the legendary RPG by FromSoftware. User backwards_watch on Reddit initiated a conversation thread showcasing Melina in the Frenzied Flame ending, talking about how they want to play Elden Ring 2 to “know how she’s going to kill me.” This is because this is the only ending in which Melina can be saved, but at the cost of burning the Lands Between in eternal flames.

That thread, however, invoked tons of opinions from fans, who argued in favor of or against some of the best and worst endings in Elden Ring. User marry_me_jane, for example, mentions that the fact that if the Lord of the Frenzied Flame ending is considered canon, then there is no possibility for a sequel, since everything will have been destroyed. In response to that argument, user Distinct-Office-609 mentions that FromSoftware is going to make the sequel set a thousand years after the original game, mentioning that “time is convoluted.” This wouldn’t be completely surprising.

FromSoftware has done something similar with Dark Souls and its sequels in the past, where sequels’ plots take place millennia after the previous iterations. If the official release of Elden Ring 2 does ever happen, it could be that it never acknowledges a canon ending for the original, simply taking place in crossover locations in the Lands Between, eons after the Elden Lords and gods of the original have fallen for any number of reasons. User Tofu_Gundam mentions that “Miyazaki will never ever give you anything resembling closure,” and that lines up with the idea of a partially disconnected sequel.

It has been revealed that nevertheless, there is something enticing about a sequel where the Frenzied Flame ending is considered canon. It’s the only one in which Melina survives. While it is described as the end of everything because the Frenzied Flame consumes everyone and everything, like Midra in Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree, life can persevere in other species, like Farum Azula and the Shadow Realm, as mentioned by user Cw3538cw. In such a scenario, Melina could, indeed, exact her revenge by choosing a new Tarnished to overthrow the Lord of the Frenzied Flame from that specific Elden Ring ending.

It has been revealed that overall, the conclusion is that there are many different possibilities for a sequel. It’s unlikely that FromSoftware will acknowledge one single ending to the game as canon, and as a soulslike fan, the best guess is that the developers would make the sequel set thousands of years after the events of the original. For now, however, there is no official talk about a true sequel to Elden Ring, as FromSoftware is working on The Duskbloods, which has been delayed to 2027.
