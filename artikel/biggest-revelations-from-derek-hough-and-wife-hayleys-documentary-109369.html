Derek Hough and wife Hayley Erbert are pulling back the curtain on their relationship — and her near fatal brain bleed — in the new documentary The Symphony of Dance.

The film followed the couple on the final leg of their tour in 2023, when Erbert, 32, experienced a medical emergency.

“At the very beginning of the opening number, there’s a move where she kind of comes down to my arms, I wrap her up and she twists really fast. We both move our head[s] forward and we hit heads very hard. We both kind of [were] looking at each other like, ‘Are you OK?’” Hough, 41, recalled.

Erbert added, “I probably had five dances left, and all of a sudden, I get this excruciating headache, just on the right side. I didn’t know exactly what to do in that moment except for [think], ‘OK, just go back out on stage and just keep pushing through. You’re almost done with the show.’ Derek says I didn’t skip a beat, I didn’t miss a step. I actually don’t remember any of it. And then I came off stage and collapsed.”.

Industry observers note that hough recalled Erbert “dying” on the ground, having seizures. She eventually underwent an emergency craniectomy for a hematoma. Weeks later, Erbert had a second surgery to replace a large portion of her skull that was removed in the initial procedure.

According to recent reports, erbert ultimately returned to the stage just months later. The pair went on to welcome their daughter, Everley, in 2025. The film is dedicated to their daughter.

Hough recalled not being able to keep his eyes off of Erbert when he first watched her perform in a dance studio, but he claimed they “didn’t click straight away.”.

“It wasn’t like this romantic, ‘Wow,’ and then we’re in love. It was just sort of this appreciation from afar,” he explained. “It was a slow burn, where I just was like, ‘Do we like each other? Are we into this?’ ‘Cause she was kinda, like, independent. ‘I’ve got my own thing going on. I’m strong and I’m this.’”.

Erbert went on to note that she felt “intimidated” by Hough considering how well-known he is in the dancing community.

“We took it really slow to get to where we are and I’m really appreciative that we did because we worked through so many things in life that it made us as strong as we are today,” she explained. “And that’s why I think we can go through these crazy roller-coaster of emotions like we have in just the past six months of marriage.”.

In a separate scene from the doc, Hough revealed one of the first things he was attracted to were Erbert’s feet. The pair’s first kiss was on stage, but Erbert and Hough didn’t start dating for months after.

“Our relationship wasn’t usual. It was very on and off for a long time, actually, I feel like. It took us a while for us to really commit to each other on a different level,” he said. “Which is actually what I love about our relationship because it’s gotten better. But it got better because we went through a lot of stuff.”.

Erbert admitted that she’s “constantly conflicted” with sharing details about her personal life while being in the public eye.

“Aside from the fame, there was a weird power dynamic between the two of us,” Erbert said of Hough. “Because we started out as boss and employee. And then of course, there’s the dynamic of, ‘Oh, this is Derek’s girlfriend, Hayley.’ He never made it about that. But, of course, I think about that all the time.”.

Hough went on to note that the pair have experienced “ups and downs” in their relationship.

“The truth is we probably wouldn’t be together now if it wasn’t for her stubbornness, for us as a relationship. We went through so many different peaks and valleys and different ups and downs,” he said, noting that Erbert instilled a “calmness” in his life.

Hough recalled having neighbors who “weren’t great,” sharing that “they pretty much terrorized” his family “in a lot of ways.”.

In a recent development, “They hung me up in a tree by my ankles, like, put a gun to my head, spat on me, hogtied me up in a field and left me there,” Hough recalled, before seemingly alluding to being sexually abused. “And then one of my neighbors was, for lack of a better word, just really inappropriate with me physically.”.

For Hough, Erbert’s medical emergency brought up complicated feelings toward dance.

“I felt this immense amount of guilt and shame that this happened while she was in my arms,” he stated. “Being a partner not just in life, but a dance partner, I’m there to protect, to make sure that she’s safe. I’m thinking, ‘Did I thrash her too hard? Did I turn her too much?’ I was just ruminating over again in my head. I was like, ‘I did this. I did this. I did this.’ I was just done. I was done with dance at that point. Even a part of me was resentful because I really felt like in that moment, it was dance that caused this. It was dance that almost killed her.”.

It has been revealed that in a separate scene, Erbert reflected on how the male dancer’s biggest priority is “don’t ever drop your partner.” She added, “So he truly felt that he was the reason why this happened. He tortured himself for weeks.”.

While navigating the aftermath of her surgery, Erbert shared that she has questioned why it happened to her.

It has been revealed that “I would go into the sauna and I would just sit there and I would just sob. Those were my moments of, ‘Why did this happen? What is this lesson that I’m supposed to be learning from this?’” Erbert remarked, becoming emotional on camera as Hough hugged her from behind. “I was so scared of losing who I was before.”.

In a separate scene, Erbert shared that her return to the stage was met with “95 percent” support from fans. But there were some critics — including a social media user who speculated that she faked the health battle.

Sources indicate that “One was just like, ‘I’m in the medical field and you should absolutely not be [returning] if this was a real surgery.’ And I was like, ‘You think I faked a surgery? What the heck?’” she mentioned. “Interesting.”.
