All three were namechecked post-match by Farke, who has warmly welcomed contributions from players not considered regular starters.

He told The Yorkshire Post: “[I am] very pleased because Brenden had perhaps a period when he was not playing with this clarity in this game, and for that he had to wait a few games for his chance.

Recent findings suggest that “But I have to say, I watched him carefully during this international break. He looked really sharp and much better in possession and training, with more clarity in his game.

“I had a good feeling when I brought him on, and this is why he also deserves to be in the spotlight today, because it was a well-taken goal, but also his touches before, it was more belief and more bravery and clarity than in the games before, and he has shown this in training.

“Hard work on the training pitch is always the answer. I'm a significant believer in you play how you train. He has trained really well during the international break, and for that I was also confident that he could make an impact today. We need this.

“Sean Longstaff has chipped in, after he had not too many minutes in the beginning of the season, right now also really excellent performances.

“Harry Wilson today again with another key pass, also before the goal, so it's important that he makes an impact.”.
