Sources indicate that hey, Space Fans! Our Space.com Community team has launched a fresh giveaway exclusively for our Space+ members, and you can join for free now for a chance to win one of two Dwarflab mini smart telescopes, each worth $419.

Already a Space+ member (hey, thanks!), you can still enter by entering the email associated with your membership on the entry page.

Sources indicate that while this Dwarflab smart telescope giveaway is our first contest, it won't be the last. Keep in mind, your entry is for a chance to win one of the two telescopes and there will be two U.S. winners in all.

If you're curious about why we picked Dwarflab's portable telescopes as a prize, check out our Dwarflab Mini smart telescope review to learn more.

We found the ultra-compact 30-mm refractor telescope to be one of the most portable smart telescopes around, and an affordable entry-level option for stargazers hoping to break into smart telescope astrophotography on a budget.

So if you're newly introduced to our Space+ crew, welcome aboard, and if you're a returning member entering the Dwarflab drawing, good luck!

We'll announce the winners in January 2027 via our contest page.
