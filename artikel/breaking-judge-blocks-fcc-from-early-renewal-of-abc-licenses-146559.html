Breaking: Judge blocks FCC from early renewal of ABC licenses

🌐 Source: Thehill • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A federal judge blocked the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) from its early renewal of ABC’s broadcast licenses. U.S. District Judge Loren AliKhan, an appointee of former President Biden, granted a preliminary injunction Wednesday night that prohibited the FCC from taking any more actions on ABC’s early license review, dismissing the agency’s lawsuit on jurisdictional grounds.….
