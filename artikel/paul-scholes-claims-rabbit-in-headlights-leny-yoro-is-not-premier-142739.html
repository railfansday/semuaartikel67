By LEWIS BROWNING, SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 16:03 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 16:05 EST, 23 November 2026.

Former Manchester United midfielder Paul Scholes has become the latest ex-Red Devil to tear into the club's defence, criticising Leny Yoro following his red card in his side's 4-0 defeat by Liverpool.

Yoro was sent off less than eight minutes into United's clash with their rivals at Anfield on Sunday, tripping Alexander Isak as he ran in on goal after an error from Matthijs de Ligt.

He has had a mixed time of things since his £52million move to Old Trafford in 2024, though has been playing in a leaky United defence that has conceded 23 goals in 11 Premier League matches this season – with only Crystal Palace conceding more.

Several different members of United's defence have come under fire this season, and, on Monday, it was the turn of Yoro.

'The Premier League at this minute just looks a little bit too much for him,' Scholes said on The Good, The Bad and The Football, sponsored by Midnite.

He added: 'You know what, he’s come in to a really bad setup defensively now. I think they also made the point yesterday if he went in next to (Virgil) Van Dijk he might look a little bit better but he doesn’t look Premier League level. He looks like a rabbit in the headlights.'.

Paul Scholes (pictured) has suggested Leny Yoro is not yet ready for the Premier League.

Yoro was sent off in the opening minutes of Manchester United's 4-0 defeat by Liverpool.

Industry observers note that fellow former United midfielder Nicky Butt, who played alongside Scholes for United between 1994 and 2004, added: 'If that’s Man United in our day, he’s on loan like a Jonny Evans or a John O’Shea. He’s a great talent that will come good.'.

Scholes, though, wasn't having any of it.

He replied: 'I’m not sure how much of a great talent he is. I think he has shown a little bit of a sign in the France squad but every time I’ve seen him he doesn’t look at the races. Everything’s happening around him and he’s just a bit, oh, what do I do?'.

United have also faced criticism for their apparent lack of running this season, with eyebrows raised when Youri Tielemans stated in the summer that they didn't do much prior to the campaign getting under way.

The club rank 17th for distance covered in the top flight this season, with Liverpool, Brentford and Everton below them. The latter two will almost certainly pass them though after their game on Monday.

That, according to Butt, is down to the players.

'We’ve had our say about the players and they’re just not good enough,' he said. 'It is the most unathletic team I’ve ever seen in Man United.

'I’m going to go through a team and I’m trying to be as honest as I can. Forget the quality for right now. For me, physicality-wise, if you go through a team, the right back, he’s physically OK, but he’s not amazing. De Ligt or Harry, whoever he’s going to play in there, physically not great. Martinez for me is physically not great. You got Yoro who will physically come good.

Nicky Butt (pictured), meanwhile, has criticised the physical ability of many of United's players.

'Then I go on to the midfield, none of them are physically good enough. Kobbie can get about the pitch 15-20 yards, but he’s not box to box. Said that numerous times. He’s got a lot of talent. He’s got a lot of things going for him. I think he’s a top player. He ain’t got the legs. He needs to have legs around him but his legs around him at the minute are Bruno and Tielemans - and they can’t run around.

In a recent development, 'Look at Mbeumo, I think he’s OK but he’s not going to blow anybody away. I think Cunha can run. So then you look at the whole squad and you’re saying three players can run.

'What I’m getting at there, and it sounds like I’m being a draconian but what you have to do first and foremost when you go to Anfield is to be able to compete physically and they can’t so they have no chance.'.
