Influencer Christie Miller has unveiled her own death at the age of 60 in a pre-recorded video following a four-year battle with breast cancer.

Miller was diagnosed with stage 4 HER2-positive breast cancer in June 2022 and amassed over 50,000 followers on Instagram by documenting her experiences with the disease and encouraging others to find joy in their lives.

The influencer also suffered a devastating personal loss in June 2025 when her husband of 36 years, Mark Miller, died from pancreatic cancer.

On November 23, a video was uploaded to Miller’s Instagram account in which she revealed that she had died and expressed her happiness at being reunited with her late husband.

“If you are seeing this video, it means that I am dead,” she said, before a montage of photos of the couple played alongside Peaches & Herb’s song ‘Reunited.’.

A post shared by Christie Miller | Chief Joy Officer (@iamchristiemiller).

Despite the circumstances surrounding the video, Miller was smiling and dressed in a colorful costume as she reflected on her life.

“Don’t be sad because it’s over, be happy because it happened,” she mentioned, quoting Dr. Seuss.

She encouraged her followers to allow themselves to grieve while also cherishing the memories they had made together.

“I have lived this beautiful life with no regrets,” she remarked.

In the days leading up to her death, Miller continued to share videos of herself enjoying some of her favorite activities.

Miller visited the beach with her dog on November 15 and spent what she believed would be her final week enjoying some of her favorite childhood foods, including a root beer float.

A post shared by Christie Miller | Chief Joy Officer (@iamchristiemiller).

Miller ended her farewell video by encouraging viewers not to let fear or other people’s opinions dictate how they live.

“Go out there and find joy every single day,” she remarked.

The caption accompanying the video confirmed that Miller died peacefully on the morning of November 23, surrounded by love.

Miller is one of several influencers to have recorded their own death announcements. In August 2026, TikToker Andreethegirl shared a final message with her followers after dying from cancer at 39.

Similarly, TikTok stars Tanner Martin and Bella Bradford unveiled their own deaths through pre-recorded videos following their respective battles with cancer.
