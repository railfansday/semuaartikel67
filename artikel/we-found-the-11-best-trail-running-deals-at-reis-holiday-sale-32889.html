Love it or hate it, the holiday season is apparently here. Sure, that means it’s time to panic-shop for gifts. But as trail runners, it’s also a once-in-a-year opportunity to score the biggest deals on the gear we need. (Yes, need.) That’s why I spent way too long pouring through REI’s Holiday Sale to find the best deals on the trail running shoes, apparel, and accoutrement that is genuinely used and loved by me and the Trail Runner team.

From November 13-23, REI is running its biggest sale of the season. Also during this window REI members get 20% off one full-price item—such as the Salomon Adv Skin 12 Hydration Vest, aka my favorite hydration vest. REI members also get another 20% off one item at REI.com/outlet. (Use the code Holiday2026 for both.).

Recent findings suggest that below, you’ll find my hand-picked items from the thousands on site. You can also go wild and check out the full sale for yourself.

The gloves themselves provide excellent energy transfer, and the carbon poles are super light but also plenty sturdy. I used them for full 110-mile a lap of Mount Blanc this summer and they still look and feel as good as new.

The III has an extra breathable upper and side panels that increase airflow but keep the rocks out. If you’re running on a lot of technical trails, this shoe is hard to beat.

With 2.5 layers of waterproof yet breathable laminate and fully taped seams, the Janji Rainrunner Pack Jacket looks up for the task. But what’s really catching my eye is the venting around the torso—no point in staying dry if you’re going to get super sweaty. Plus, it’s just a pretty sleek looking jacket.

It’s cute, it’s functional, and it comes in tons of both subtle and fun colors. I love the Janji Groundwork Longbra for warm runs, gym time, and even everyday wear. I have worn the women’s version of the Mountain Hardwear Jor Alloy Tights and can attest they keep your legs warm and free on the coldest winter runs. That’s thanks to strategically-placed fleece where you want to stay warm and stretch where you want to move. The waistband is, crucially, very comfortable and fully adjustable thanks to the drawstring.

Whether you’re running, ski mountaineering, or curling up on the couch, sometimes you just want to be cozy. (The hood actually stays up! Cold air doesn’t fly up your torso!) And that kangaroo pocket is just begging to keep your hands warm.

The Icebreaker Merino 200 Oasis Long-Sleeve Crewe Thermal Top is sturdy but soft, warm but breathable, doesn’t stink, has a UPF of 20, and transitions beautifully from the trails to daily life.

Recent findings suggest that for trail runners, there is one watch to rule them all. And it’s the Garmin Fenix 8 Pro. With built-in InReach tech, it’s a fully-functioning SOS device—no phone required. If you’ve been eyeing this bad boy now is the time to send it.
