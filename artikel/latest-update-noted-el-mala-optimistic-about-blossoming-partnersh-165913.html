Germany international Noted El Mala believes his developing partnership with Tottenham Hotspur loanee Mikey Moore still has considerable room for improvement. The 20-year-old Cologne attacker has spoken enthusiastically about his understanding with the English youngster following their successful attacking combination in last weekend’s 1-1 Bundesliga draw against Bayern Munich.

It has been revealed that moore, 19, scored his first Bundesliga goal in the 12th minute at the Allianz Arena, rounding Bayern goalkeeper Jonas Urbig after receiving a return pass from El Mala. The well-worked goal provided a welcome breakthrough for the Englishman following a difficult start to his loan spell in Germany.

As Get German Football News reported following the Bayern encounter, Moore expressed relief at finally opening his Bundesliga account and discussed the growing understanding between himself and El Mala.

The partnership has been a topic of considerable interest ever since Moore arrived from Tottenham in August. Cologne head coach René Wagner spoke of his enthusiasm for the attacking combination back in September, emphasizing that the two youngsters were not competing for one exclusive place in the team. Moore himself subsequently expressed his desire to develop a stronger understanding with El Mala ahead of October’s Rhein derby against Borussia Mönchengladbach.

According to recent reports, the friendship also extends beyond the training ground, with El Mala revealing that his older brother Malek has spent time with Moore as well. Speaking in an extensive interview with Kicker, the 20-year-old described their growing bond.

“Malek and I get on really well with Mikey,” El Mala told Kicker. “The three of us recently went out for a meal together. We get along very well. Now he’s scored his first goal after a great attacking move, which gives us confidence for the coming matches that we can play together like that. I also have a very good relationship with Linton Maina, for example, and you can see that on the pitch.”.

In a recent development, cologne have struggled to translate promising performances into victories this season, but the development of their young attacking players offers encouragement. With Moore finally opening his Bundesliga account and El Mala increasingly comfortable operating in central attacking positions, Wagner will hope their growing understanding produces further rewards.
