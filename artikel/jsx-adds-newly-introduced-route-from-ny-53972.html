JSX is launching a fresh nonstop route between the New York metro area and North Texas.

Industry observers note that starting Feb. 17, the public charter service will connect Teterboro Airport in Latest Jersey with Dallas Love Field. Flights will operate daily.

The service will be available only to members of the carrier’s free loyalty program, Club JSX.

It has been revealed that “Newly introduced York City and Dallas are two of America’s most influential business and cultural centers, and we believe traveling between them should be as seamless as the opportunities that connect them,” JSX CEO Alex Wilcox said in a news release. “As we continue expanding our network, our focus remains on giving customers something increasingly rare in air travel: more of their time back. With this new daily service, we’re bringing the simplicity, comfort, and convenience of JSX to one of the country’s most important travel corridors.”.

In a recent development, JSX added service to Teterboro, which it markets as New York City, in 2025. It also serves Westchester County Airport, located just north of New York.
