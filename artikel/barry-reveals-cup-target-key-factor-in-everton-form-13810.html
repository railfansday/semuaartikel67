Thierno Barry has praised Everton’s mentality – and their impressive Carabao Cup run – ahead of the Blues’ return to Premier League action.

David Moyes’ side are back under the lights on Monday evening (8pm GMT), facing an away trip to take on Brentford.

Before the international break, Everton secured three consecutive victories after wins over Newcastle United in the Carabao Cup, as well as beating Newcastle and Coventry City in the Premier League.

Sitting sixth in the top-flight table and heading into the Carabao Cup quarter-finals next month, Barry claims there’s a feel-good factor around the Club at the moment.

“For the Cup, I’m very happy to be in the quarter-final because it’s my first time," he told evertontv.

“I hope we can go into the final because it would be an amazing thing for this season.

Sources indicate that “I think the team is really good at the moment. We have a really good mentality and we know more [about] each other, so it’s perfect now.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m working a lot to do my best. Things are better now because the fans know me more, so I’m very happy.”.
