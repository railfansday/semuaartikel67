Andy Robertson issued a warning that Scotland do not have time to get things right under Sebastien Pocognoli after his record-breaking night was spoiled by a 4-0 defeat in Switzerland.

The Tottenham left-back became Scotland’s most-capped player by making his 103rd appearance in St Gallen to eclipse the previous milestone of 102 caps set by Kenny Dalglish 40 years ago.

However, the captain’s personal achievement was overshadowed by a dismal performance and result that left Scotland with a record of seven points from a possible 18 and a third place finish in Nations League Group B1.

Switzerland were gifted a first-half opener when Lewis Ferguson turned a harmless cross into his own net before Scotland allowed the roof to cave in by conceding twice in the opening six minutes of the second half.

Strikes from Winsley Boteli and Alvyn Sanches put the hosts 3-0 up before Zeki Amdouni added a fourth in the final minute to increase scrutiny on Pocognoli after a less than impressive start to his tenure.

It has been revealed that "I thought first half we were in the game," Robertson mentioned. "We lost a stupid goal but I thought we had chances and could easily have been one-each at half-time.

"We talk about it at half-time to say keep going and by 50 minutes we're 3-0 down. That cannot happen in international football.

According to recent reports, "We were playing against a good team but we were in the game in the first half. We have to do it for 90 minutes. Then you're 3-0 down at a tough place. A really tough night for us.".

Pocognoli has yet to convince the Tartan Army that he is the right appointment after succeeding Steve Clarke, who resigned after seven years in charge following Scotland's World Cup exit.

In a recent development, asked if it will take time for the 39-year-old Belgian's methods to bear fruit, Robertson warned: "I don't think you've got time in international football. You have to do it.

It has been revealed that "Are those supporters who have travelled everywhere going to give us time? No. Are the people inside Hampden going to give us time? No. Are the people outside Hampden that are going to be critical, and rightly so, going to give us time? No. We've not got time. The players don't have time. We have to step up.

"We now have a long break until March which is not a good thing. It's going to be a tough one to swallow.".
