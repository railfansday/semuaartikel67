Manchester United have been dealt a fresh blow in their pursuit of a left-back.

The Red Devils are worryingly short in that position, especially after injuries to both Luke Shaw and Patrick Dorgu this season.

It left now-sacked manager Michael Carrick to field right-backs Diogo Dalot and Noussair Mazraoui in that role in the absence of his two natural options.

It was one of a number of problems facing the former midfielder, who was relieved of his duties last Thursday after just one win in 11 games.

Mauricio Pochettino will take over the reins from him at Old Trafford, and the Argentine will look to stamp his mark on the United squad when it comes to recruitment.

Newcastle United’s Lewis Hall was a top target during the summer transfer window, but United did not act on that interest, with the former Chelsea defender penning a new deal on Tyneside instead.

Now, the Press Association reports that the Magpies will not entertain any offers for the England international in the upcoming January transfer window.

He has established himself as Thomas Tuchel’s first-choice left-back in recent months, and he impressed against Lamine Yamal in the Three Lions’ shock 3-2 win over Spain in the Nations League on Sunday.

It is thought that Newcastle are braced for more transfer interest in their 22-year-old, who signed for the north-east club in 2023, costing them £35million.

It is believed that they have no intention of selling him halfway through the campaign, having lost Sandro Tonali, Bruno Guimaraes, Anthony Gordon and Alexander Isak in the space of two summer transfer windows.
