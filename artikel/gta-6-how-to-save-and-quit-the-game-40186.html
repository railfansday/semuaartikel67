It has been revealed that GTA 6 features a manual save system. You can do this at practically any time, giving you the freedom to create your checkpoint in Leonida and come back to the game at a later date. Given that there are close to 100 GTA 6 Missions, this is going to come in very handy – particularly as there is no way to replay missions in GTA 6.

However, there is one significant limitation. There are only 15 manual save slots in GTA 6. It would be wise to keep one at the beginning and end of each major chapter, giving you the freedom to jump around if you want or need to. You can overwrite and load into any of these save slots in GTA 6. It's also worth mentioning that there is a single, secret 16th slot that is reserved for an auto-save which triggers after missions, if you're wasted, busted, or have some sort of major encounter in the open world.

The save system will also disable if you activate any GTA 6 cheats, forcing you to quit out of the game and reload a save that wasn't revelling in pure chaos.

Recent findings suggest that GTA 6 should let you save at any time, but if you're encountering a problem there's likely a good reason for it. You can't save in GTA 6 while you're on a mission. Instead you'll need to rely on the auto-save functionality, although you can restart missions from the beginning or from checkpoints by pressing the start button.

Recent findings suggest that if you're in free-roam and you can't save in GTA 6, it may be because Jason or Lucia just finished a phone conversation or objective. Try leaving the building or vehicle that you're in and try again.

In a recent development, there is no in-game option to quit out of GTA 6. If you want to end a session, you'll need to head back to the home screen of your PS5 or Xbox Series X and close the application manually. If you do this, just remember to save first – otherwise you'll be relying on the last auto-save GTA 6 made.
