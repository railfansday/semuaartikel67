While the Nye Camp boss concedes the Rocks will respect Elliot Powell’s side, he will ask his charges to concentrate on their own game initiative in the Isthmian South Central division clash between the second-placed Channel Islanders and Bognor Regis Town, who are third.

Hollands’ men go into the keenly anticipated showdown on the back of a hard-fought 2-1 home win over an in-form Hayes & Yeading United outfit last time out.

Goals from Luke Roberts and Henry McDonald secured the victory and a vital three points that keep the home side in the reckoning at the top end of the standings — and Hollands believes a win is in his side’s grasp at the Springfield Stadium.

Recent findings suggest that he told the club’s official YouTube channel: “We’ve got to worry about ourselves more so than we do Jersey. We will obviously pay them respect; we're going to their home ground and they're doing well so you have to pay the respect.

“But we must make sure that we have a plan suitable for us to get three points away from home, which will be a tough ask — but it's something that I feel we can achieve if we play to the heights that we have done this season.

“We have to have belief because we have shown that on our day we can compete with some of the best teams in this division.

Industry observers note that “I've got belief in them. It's all about what we can do and how we can influence games. I feel if we turn up, then we can compete with anyone, but we have to show that and have to do so on a more consistent basis.”.

The most recent triumph — which came after a home win over Littlehampton Town — further addressed a mini-slump in the league and the gaffer claims his charges should take the positives from getting back on track.

He added: “We must take the positives from two league wins on the spin and make sure we go there (Jersey) with the confidence that provides.”.

*The club estimates the number of travelling Rocks fans attending the game will be around 400, an incredible following for step 4 football.
