Liverpool may have found their next great Senegalese talent. The Premier League club have reportedly reached a verbal agreement to sign Sidy Barhama Ndiaye, a 16-year-old winger who currently plays for Diambars.

However, Liverpool fans will have to be patient, as the young Senegalese cannot move to England until January 2028.

The news was revealed by Fabrizio Romano, who confirmed that a verbal agreement exists between the two parties.

Having already been given a trial by Liverpool in the summer of 2025, Ndiaye reportedly convinced the club's scouts, who have now secured an option on his arrival. Born on 31 December 2009, he will turn 18 at the end of 2027, which is a requirement for a move to England.

A product of Diambars, one of the most renowned academies in Senegal, Sidy Barhama Ndiaye plays mainly on the right but can also operate on the left. He first caught the eye at the 2025 Under-17 Africa Cup of Nations, played when he was only 15, before continuing to progress with Senegal's youth teams.

Recent findings suggest that his main qualities are his pace, his explosiveness and his ability to beat an opponent one on one. Very direct when receiving the ball, he likes to provoke, accelerate and create openings. He also has a degree of efficiency in the final ball, as he again demonstrated at the Under-17 World Cup.

Industry observers note that during Senegal's 9-0 thrashing of Cuba, the young winger scored one goal and provided two assists. The performance helped to boost his reputation among European observers and scouts.

The comparison with Sadio Mane comes naturally around the young Senegalese.

Recent findings suggest that besides his origins, Ndiaye shares with his illustrious elder an attacking profile built on pace, penetration and the ability to make a difference on the wings. And if Liverpool, who benefited from Mane's talent for six seasons, complete the deal, that comparison will be unavoidable.

However, there is still a long way to go before he can be expected to play at Anfield. Between now and January 2028, Ndiaye will have to continue to progress at Diambars and confirm the promise shown with Senegal's youth teams.

Liverpool, for their part, have already taken a position on a player they want to integrate into their long-term project.
