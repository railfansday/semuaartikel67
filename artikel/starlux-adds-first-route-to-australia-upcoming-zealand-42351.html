Starlux Adds First Route to Australia, Upcoming Zealand

🌐 Source: Airlinegeeks • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (152 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Taiwanese carrier Starlux is branching out to Australia and Newly introduced Zealand with a new, one-stop connection.
  • Starting March 23, 2027, the airline will fly from Taipei to Sydney to Auckland.
  • Taipei-Sydney-Auckland will be the airline’s first one-stop or through route; all other flights are nonstop from Taiwan.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Taiwanese carrier Starlux is branching out to Australia and Newly introduced Zealand with a new, one-stop connection.

Starting March 23, 2027, the airline will fly from Taipei to Sydney to Auckland. Flights will initially operate four times weekly, then switch to daily on May 1. Starlux will operate the route using Airbus A330-900 aircraft.

Taipei-Sydney-Auckland will be the airline’s first one-stop or through route; all other flights are nonstop from Taiwan. Officials stated the one-stop approach will give travelers access to “two incredible destinations in one journey.”.

Starlux remarked it is working with Qantas to arrange connecting flights to other destinations in Australia, New Zealand, and Fiji.

According to recent reports, starlux is headquartered in Taipei and mainly flies within East and Southeast Asia, though it also offers service to North America and Europe. In the U.S., it serves Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, Phoenix, and Ontario, California.
