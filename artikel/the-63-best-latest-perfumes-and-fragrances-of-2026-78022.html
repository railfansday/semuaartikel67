📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Everything smells like vanilla. At least, that’s what perfume naysayers will tell you. And it’s somewhat true: If you’re judging by what’s popular, ubiquitous, or cool with the youth, then sure—vanilla scents and sweet gourmands are still trending.

Recent findings suggest that but hundreds of other fragrance releases in 2026 deserve a spot on your vanity and a spritz on your skin, and many are far less sweet. Gourmands took a turn for the weird and fruity this year, with notes like lychee and rice milk popping up in otherwise innocuous formulas. Woods and ambers continued to reign, as did spice-forward and citrus scents with unexpected depth. Perfumers are meeting our demand for newness in increasingly interesting, artistic ways, and we smelled hundreds of their creations to choose the 63 winners of our annual fragrance awards.

See all the winners of the 2026 Harper’s Bazaar Fragrance Awards, plus quotes from our beauty editors on what makes these formulas worthy of your next blind buy.

Chanel Coco Mademoiselle Crush Absolu.

“Perfumer Olivier Polge reinvented the beloved “Coco Mad” scent as Crush Absolu, a new juice centered around patchouli, amber, rose, and a lychee-grapefruit accord. ‘There’s something voluptuous, something very prideful, something very addictive,’ Polge says about the new scent.”.

According to recent reports, “The newly introduced iteration of J’adore was inspired by Rihanna herself. As such, it’s a sweeter, bolder take, with notes of honeyed jasmine, rose, ylang-ylang, and vanilla.” —JR.

Guerlain L'Heure Bleue Eau de Parfum.

"Bazaar’s Beauty Commerce Editor, Tiffany Dodson Davis, describes Guerlain’s L’Heure Bleue Eau de Parfum as the “scent of sunset,” thanks to a blue-sky accord that evokes the moment the sun goes down and the air turns crisp, clean, and fresh." —KI.

Burberry Goddess Amber Vanilla Eau de Parfum Intense.

“Lavender, maple syrup, four vanilla notes—I feel like a dog hearing all my favorite words. The result is a warm, ambery gourmand perfume that’s perfect for the cooler months.” —JR.

Armani Beauty Emporio Armani Power of You Eau de Parfum.

Recent findings suggest that "Passionfruit adds an unexpected zing to the vanilla base here, making Power of You one of our favorite tropical-leaning gourmands of the year. It's pure summertime sex appeal." —JR.

“Henry Rose Ripe captures fruits—from lychee to watermelon—at their juiciest, ripest point, like a delectable first bite, before packing a punch with black pepper, cardamom, and powdery florals.” —Katie Intner, beauty market and commerce editor.

Influxious Magic Hour Eau de Parfum.

“One of the most unexpected fruity florals on the list, Influxious Magic Hour melds watermelon and dragon fruit with spicy chili pepper for a burst of juicy energy.” —KI.

Balmain Paris Destin De Balmain Eau de Parfum.

In a recent development, "The opening note of Destin is strawberry, a modern and sweet fruity note that quickly dries down to a patchouli-forward, gorgeous floral musk. It will make you smell expensive." —JR.

"Neroli, bergamot, and yuzu combine with notes of orange blossom, jasmine, and tonka bean for a well-rounded fragrance that stands out from the pack. Parfum de Marly's Athénaïs eau de parfum has a zesty composition upon first spritz before drying down to a powdery finish." —Tiffany Dodson Davis, beauty commerce editor.

Sources indicate that “Gap recently brought back its iconic fragrance line from the ’90s, including the nostalgic Grass Eau de Parfum. It now features matcha, vetiver, green mandarin, and cedar for a fresh take on its outdoorsy, earthy scent.” —KI.

Maison Francis Kurkdjian Oud Velvet Mood Extrait de Parfum.

Industry observers note that “For those who are oud-averse, Maison Francis Kurkdjian’s Oud Velvet Mood is a lighter iteration of the category. The oud has a woody quality, and when paired with spicy cinnamon, rich saffron, and juicy peach, the result is light and warm like the sun.” —KI.

“This stunning perfume is all gardenia and vanilla, and in its simplicity, it happens to be one of our most complimented scents of the year.” —JR.

Balenciaga Pink Oud Extrait de Parfum.

"This is a bold, long-wearing scent for those who live and die for the sensual pairing of rose and oud. We're obsessed with the way it blooms and evolves on skin throughout the night." —JR.

Ex Nihilo Demon Dancer Eau de Parfum.

“Ex Nihilo Demon Dancer feels like moving through the crisp air on a spring day, with peppermint providing a breezy contrast to juicy rhubarb, bright bergamot, and warm tonka bean.” —KI.

“We don’t know what we love more: the magnetic floral perfume that begs to be worn every day, the sculptural bottle, or the bookish packaging that made us a fan before we even took our first whiff.” —JR.

“You can’t go wrong with Beyoncé’s sultry and sophisticated hair perfume, which leaves hair with a long-wearing trail of bergamot, jasmine, oud, sandalwood, and Haitian vetiver.” —JR.

Creed Iris Debonair Eau de Parfum.

Industry observers note that “Delightfully unisex, this aromatic scent is a masterclass in balance. The top opens with bergamot, cardamom, pink pepper, and wormwood. In the middle, you’ll find fruity fig, lavender, and violet leaf. The base is iris, amberwood, and ambrette.” —JR.

It has been revealed that D.S. & Durga Resurrected Corpse Flower.

“Earthy and green, with an olive top note and banana base note—this is the perfume to buy this year if you want to smell wholly original and unabashedly cool.” —JR.

“Creamy and warm with notes of chocolate and coffee, this is the must-have gourmand perfume of the winter. In a word, it makes us feel happy.” —JR.

Discothèque Next to Me or Nothing Eau De Parfum.

“More of a feeling than a fragrance, this scent is airy and easy to wear, with breezy notes of milk, jasmine, and woods.” —JR.

Phlur Heavy Cream Eau de Parfum.

"Gourmand lovers will want to overindulge in this elevated eau de parfum, which combines two types of creams—whipped and coconut—along with jasmine blossom and vanilla mousse notes for a sophisticated yet playful olfactory blend." —TDD.

“An olfactory version of a sound bath, Liis Forest Sounds boasts notes of bright yuzu, damp rain (earthier than plain water), green leaves, and woods.” —KI.

Dolce&Gabbana Dolce&Gabbana Your Devotion Eau de Parfum Intense.

Recent findings suggest that "The notes in this fragrance sound good enough to eat: Sicilian lemon, orange blossom, and vanilla milk. This is a date-night, delicious gourmand for those who usually prefer a lighter citrus scent." —JR.

Dries Van Noten Velvet Violet Eau de Parfum.

“This woody-gourmand scent captures the lush texture of velvet with a mix of unusual notes: violet leaf, clary sage essence, toasted sesame, barley roasted, and grilled tonka beans.” —JR.

Ellis Brooklyn Vanilla Rice Eau de Parfum.

“Rather than wrapping yourself in a cashmere blanket on a cold day, try wearing Ellis Brooklyn’s Vanilla Rice. It combines the warm notes of toasted rice, cherry blossom, sweet vanilla, and creamy coconut milk for a soft, slightly floral scent.” —KI.

Estée Lauder Glimmer Eau de Parfum Spray.

“Estée Lauder’s Glimmer gives crisp orange flower and rose a warm finish with vanilla bean, tonka bean, amber, and two types of wood.” —KI.

Glossier You Soie Eau de Parfum.

“The latest addition to Glossier’s You fragrance lineup, Soie is an aquatic and solar take on a floral scent, with tropical Tiare flower water and creamy rice in place of powdery florals.” —KI.

Industry observers note that “Not quite a fragrance, Future Society’s Base Scent is like the no-makeup makeup version of perfume. Infused with prebiotics and a patent-pending technology, the scent is designed to enhance and balance any fragrance layered beneath it—whether that’s lotion, deodorant, or your natural scent—by working with the skin’s microbiome.” —KI.

Harlem Perfume Co. Sensual Muse Eau de Parfum.

“A fruity floral that reads as more sophisticated, Harlem Fragrance Co.’s Sensual Muse gives juicy apricot, bright citrus, and creamy gardenia a warm, compelling character once whipped vanilla, sandalwood, and amber are added into the mix.” —KI.

“If you prefer your cocktails in fragrance form, look no further than By Kilian, which has mastered the art of turning a nutty, warm, slightly sweet espresso martini into a wearable scent that feels especially seductive at night.” —KI.
