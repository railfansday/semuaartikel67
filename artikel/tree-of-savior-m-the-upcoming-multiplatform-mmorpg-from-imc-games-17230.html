It has been revealed that back in the day, the MMORPG was purely the domain of PC players, but now everyone is getting in on the action. And a dark horse candidate that's stood out for many of us is none other than Tree of Savior, IMC Games' upcoming multiplatform MMORPG that's just released pre-registration!

Tree of Savior M is set in a fantasy world where its patron goddess has vanished into the ether. Guided by divine visions, it's up to you to help safeguard the world and its people. During the course of your adventures you'll be assisted not only by the usual suite of upgrades and equipment, but also assisted by recruitable Fellows to help you out, and the pet-like Kupoles!

According to recent reports, I guess I'd compare it to something like Don't Starve, weirdly enough. And that, combined with the hack 'n slash elements, as well as your standard suite of flashy abilities and bright colours, does make Tree of Savior M a surprisingly engaging-looking MMORPG.

Industry observers note that whether it'll live up to that hype, however, is something you'll have to judge for yourself. With Tree of Savior M having already launched in South Korea, Taiwan and beyond, it's had plenty of time to work out the kinks. So, if you think it'll be for you, pre-register for Tree of Savior M now on your platform of choice!
