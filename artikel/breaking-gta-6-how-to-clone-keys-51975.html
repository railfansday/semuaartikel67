📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This wouldn’t be a Grand Theft Auto game without a little bit of … well, Grand Theft Auto. One of the first things you will likely be encouraged to do in Grand Theft Auto 6 is steal a car, and for the most part, it can be relatively easy to do. Break a window and hotwire it. Nothing more to it. Unfortunately, later in the game, especially in higher end neighborhoods, there will be vehicles with electric locks that require a bit of finesse to ensure the alarm doesn’t go off.

At the beginning of the game, Jason will only have the ability to smash open a window to unlock the car’s door. This is something that has been apart of the series since its creation. In chapter 2, Lucia will learn how to Slim Jim a car to ensure that the alarm system won’t go off and you’ll have a cleaner getaway.

Unfortunately, some of the later cars won’t give you this option because they are keyless. This is what a key cloner is for. This will allow you to essentially hack into a car’s system and not only open the door but hotwire it in the process.

According to recent reports, unfortunately, early in the story you won’t be able to clone keys. You will have your trusty elbow and Slim Jim for the first two chapters, but once you hit Chapter 3, things change a little bit.

Industry observers note that you will meet a man by the name of Randy. He is a car dealer who deals in hot cars (stolen) and has employed both Lucia and Jason to get him some of the fastest and most expensive cars possible. Don’t worry about smashing them up; so long as they can pull into his garage, he can fix it.

Recent findings suggest that you will get a mission from Randy where he will want a Zorrusso located all the way Vice Beach (east). Head down and start this mission and Randy will call you to explain the situation.

It has been revealed that by starting this mission, you will unlock the key cloner app on your phone, allowing you to finally be able to unlock some of the higher-grade vehicles. Be cautious, though, as cars that require a key cloner generally come with at least a standard tracker, which can alert police to your location if you’re not driving fast enough.

Sources indicate that regardless, after completing this mission, the car world is your oyster. Go out and have some fun.
