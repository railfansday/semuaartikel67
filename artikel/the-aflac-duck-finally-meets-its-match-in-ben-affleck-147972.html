Ben Affleck speaks fluent Aflac.

The insurance brand partnered with Artists Equity, the production firm founded by Affleck and Matt Damon, on a campaign that plays like a scene from a feature film. Titled “High Stakes,” the ad was directed by and stars Affleck.

The spot plays on the Aflac Duck’s signature move of exclaiming its own name, which sounds nearly identical to Affleck’s last name.

According to recent reports, in it, a distressed team struggles to communicate with the insurer’s mascot. “I speak Aflac,” Affleck reveals as he enters the room.

Affleck relays information to the group, including an explanation of supplemental insurance.
