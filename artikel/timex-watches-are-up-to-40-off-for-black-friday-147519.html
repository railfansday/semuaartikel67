Do you want to know what’s better than an expensive watch? A cheap watch that looks expensive. Okay, yes, I understand that’s not a rule of thumb all Esquire editors follow, but for me a $25 Casio or a $50 Timex with a leather strap gets the job done fine. If you’re on the same page as me (or are working with a similar budget), there is one surprising place to look for deals on affordable watch brands this Black Friday. Timex, specifically on Amazon, is one of the best places to get a fresh watch during the shopping holiday. From Q Timex reissues to modern workhorses, there’s plenty to dig into.

Timex is all over Amazon for Black Friday, with discounts on some of its most popular men’s watch styles. Unpretentious favorites like the Weekender and Easy Reader are seeing discounts of up to 40 percent off, depending on the size and strap style you need. If you’re not looking for anything showy and just want a digital watch for training, you’ll find ample discounts on Timex’s tried-and-true Ironman classic watch.

Recent findings suggest that all of those men’s watch deals and more can be found on Timex’s Amazon store through the end of the week. Or, if you’re shopping for a gift for that special lady, you can pick up a timepiece for her too.
