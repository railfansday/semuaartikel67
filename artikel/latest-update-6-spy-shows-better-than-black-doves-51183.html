Killing Eve stands out because it turns traditional male-dominated espionage formulas on their head, trading stoic, invincible heroes for messy, complex human dynamics.

While both shows celebrate women in the field, Killing Eve's tone and perspective is far more unique and unpredictable compared to the more generic Black Doves. The Night Manager carves out a unique space between the high-fantasy glamour of espionage and the gritty cynicism of spycraft, balancing tension, luxury, and danger.
