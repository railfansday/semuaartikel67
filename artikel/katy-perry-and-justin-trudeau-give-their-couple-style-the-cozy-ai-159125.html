A good vacation wardrobe starts with what you wear to the airport. Mere days ahead of Thanksgiving, Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau were seen landing in Venice for a little fall getaway. And their outfits were, in many ways, exactly what you’d expect them to be.

In a recent development, for Perry, the flight called for a cozy camel knit set. Towing the line between loungewear and everyday style, the two-piece featured a V-neck sweater with a cropped, boxy silhouette. Drop shoulders gave it an oversize feel, while a pocket at the chest served to elevate the look. The singer wore it with matching pants, designed with a high-waisted fit and loose, relaxed leg. She wrapped herself in a cream scarf for an extra layer and, on her shoulder, carried a black tote with gold hardware.

To further accessorize, the “Teenage Dream” star added narrow tortoiseshell sunglasses, a chocolate-brown Ralph Lauren baseball cap, and some chunky headphones.

Next to her, her boyfriend, the former prime minister of Canada, went for darker hues. He made a statement in a deep-gray crewneck T-shirt that declared he “will yap about books,” and layered up with an eggplant-purple button-up jacket with a pointed collar and chest flap pockets. His dark-khaki trousers finished things off, and he grabbed a trusty backpack for the flight.

Prior to Venice, Perry and Trudeau, who have been romantically linked since the summer of 2025, were seen in Paris and, before that, in Barcelona. While in Spain, the two attended the Metafuturo conference, for which Trudeau spoke about the impact of artificial intelligence alongside Spanish cabinet ministers, business executives, and Olympic athletes.
