Micron (MU -11.77%) stock just might be the cheapest stock ever... that investors think is overpriced.

In a recent development, shares of the DRAM and NAND flash memory manufacturer sell for just 8x trailing earnings and less than 4x forward earnings. Investors slashed Micron's share price by 36% in November. At $566 this morning, Micron stock is down another 10% as of 10:20 a.m.

And once again, I blame TrendForce for that.

Industry observers note that five days ago, if you recall, a report from TrendForce sparked a sell-off in Micron stock. According to TrendForce, Chinese companies were cutting back on memory purchases, causing NAND spot prices to fall 20%.

And so Micron stock started falling.

Five days later, Micron's still falling as TrendForce publishes a fresh report: "Chinese [cloud service providers] have reduced their 2027 memory orders." And more NAND supply is coming to market, creating an "oversupplied market" and "a downward pricing cycle" in NAND.

NASDAQ · As of 11:16 AM ET 11/25/26.

According to recent reports, the good news is that not all of Micron's sales are NAND -- only about a quarter. The better news is that the reason NAND sales are down appears to be that Chinese companies can't right now get all the GPUs they want, such that they need less NAND to pair with GPUs. This implies that if China does get access to more GPUs in the future, the NAND situation may fix itself.

But the best news of all is the situation with DRAM:.

Recent findings suggest that although prices may "moderate," TrendForce states the DRAM market "is expected to remain undersupplied" in 2027. When Chinese buyers cut DRAM orders, the supply is "promptly absorbed" by American buyers. As a result, TrendForce expects DRAM supply to remain tight in 2027 -- and total DRAM sales could grow 69%!

I don't know about you, but to me, that still sounds incredibly bullish for Micron stock.
