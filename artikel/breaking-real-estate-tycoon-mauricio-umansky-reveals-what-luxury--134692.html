Breaking: Real Estate Tycoon Mauricio Umansky Reveals What Luxury Buyers Want Most—From Secluded Estates to Private Spas

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Mauricio Umansky has a read on what makes a multimillion-dollar home irresistible.

Sources indicate that for some of his buyers, it’s enough acreage to put real distance between them and the neighbors. For others, it’s the luxury of stepping into a sauna or taking a cold plunge without leaving home.

“Privacy is becoming a bigger priority for luxury buyers,” The Agency founder revealed while speaking to Mansion Global.

He noted buyers are drawn to properties where they can retreat from view, feel secure, and build a wellness routine around amenities such as a private gym or spa.

Umansky is putting his read on the market to work in Miami’s Edgewater neighborhood, where he is leading sales for HQ Residences Miami.

The project brings together singer Marc Anthony, hospitality entrepreneur Sam Nazarian, and SBE. Its pitch borrows from the hotel world, pairing private residences with wellness amenities, places for neighbors to gather, and an emphasis on service.

Even at the top of the market, buyers are watching what they pay. Umansky remarked there’s room to negotiate in some areas, while a well-priced home in a coveted location can still draw strong interest.

He recommended finding an agent who knows the neighborhood and being ready to act when a property comes along that you can see yourself living in for years.

Umansky’s own homes span Los Angeles, La Quinta, CA, and Aspen, CO.

Industry observers note that los Angeles gives him access to city life, while La Quinta offers the appeal of a slower pace, sunny days, and a round of golf. In Aspen, the draw extends from ski season to summers in the mountains and the sense of community he finds there.

If he were to add another address, Umansky said he could picture himself in Europe.

In a recent development, he singled out the South of France, Italy, and Spain, with particular affection for Madrid. Miami also appeals to him as a place to own a home, especially after spending more time in the city through his work on HQ Residences.

Industry observers note that the broker also reflected on what TV has done for his business. Appearances on “Buying Beverly Hills,” “Dancing With the Stars,” and “Special Forces” have introduced him to people he might never have met through real estate alone, he said, and brought more attention to The Agency.

Industry observers note that the trade-off is that some people form an impression of him from what they see on camera. He remarked he wants his reputation to rest on his work as an agent and the trust he earns from clients.

As for the prospect of running for mayor, Umansky spoke about what he would focus on if he pursued the job.

Industry observers note that “Los Angeles is my home, and I care deeply about its future,” he told Mansion Global. He said he’d look at how the city handles housing approvals and development, with the goal of making it easier to build and attracting more investment and jobs.

In a recent development, the candid glimpse into what wealthy buyers want comes one week after his stepdaughter, Farrah Aldjufrie—who is also Kyle Richards' daughter—gave birth to a baby boy. The arrival came three months after Aldjufrie revealed the baby's father is Zach Goldsmith, her "Buying Beverly Hills" co-star and fellow The Agency agent.

The 37-year-old property mogul, who is the daughter of Richards and her ex-husband, Guraish Aldjufrie, shared the news in July that she was expecting her first child.

Then, one month later, she hard-released her relationship with Goldsmith while sharing a video of the two of them celebrating their gender reveal.

A source told Page Six that the TV alum has welcomed her son, though Aldjufrie has yet to confirm the news herself.

The pregnancy marked a joyful personal milestone for the real estate mogul, whose professional achievements have often played out in front of TV audiences.

Aldjufrie had previously revealed how Umansky helped to cultivate her love of real estate. In a 2016 interview with The Daily Dish, she said that as a child, she used to drive around L.A. with her stepfather to tour listings, fascinated by everything that went into selling them.

“I always have been interested in real estate since I was a little kid. I’d drive around to open houses with my mom and Mauricio when I was 6, 7, 8 years old. I’d love to see all the houses,” she noted.

“I know L.A. geographically really, really well, having grown up here. I was an only child for the first eight years, so I spent a lot of time with them and was very interested in everything Mauricio was doing. But I have my own love for property and being in L.A. There's some of the most amazing properties and lifestyles in the world.”.

According to her profile on The Agency's website, Aldjufrie became an agent in 2013—and has since helped the manufacturer to achieve "over $5 billion in real estate sales.".

Interestingly, while she continues her professional and personal relationship with The Agency's founder, Umansky, her mother made clear just a few months ago that she believes she is entitled to a significant chunk of her estranged husband's empire—telling her Bravo co-stars that she will fight to claim her half of the business that she states she helped to build should the couple end up filing for divorce.

According to recent reports, umansky founded The Agency alongside Billy Rose and Blair Chang in 2011, 15 years after he wed Richards. By May 2022, the business had facilitated more than $57 billion in real estate sales, according to Forbes.

According to The Agency's website, the company operates more than 145 offices in 14 countries, while Umansky's bio notes that he has "achieved nearly $5 billion in real estate sales and holds the distinction of selling the most homes in the country priced above $20 million.

According to recent reports, "He has represented some of the world's most noteworthy properties, including the Playboy Mansion—the first house in L.A. to sell above the $100 million mark—the Walt Disney Estate, and residences owned by Michael Jackson, Michael Jordan and Prince.".

Indeed, in the 15 years since it was founded, The Agency has built up a wealth of clients, including Lady Gaga, LeBron James, and Ben Affleck.

While it's unclear whether Richards would seek to be involved in the day-to-day running of the business that she hopes to claim a 50% share in, it's likely that she could be in line for a hefty payout if Umansky wishes to keep The Agency to himself.

However, Richards noted that she isn't quite ready to pull the plug on their marriage once and for all.
