Tech Report: Capital One business credit cards for upcoming LLCs: Which card to get based on your credit history

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (187 words, 📖 Standard)
  • New LLCs can qualify for business cards even without established business credit, as personal credit history is a key factor in approval decisions.
  • Business cards are available for a range of credit profiles, from fair to excellent, with options for both cash-back and travel rewards.
  • Cards offer features such as free employee cards, virtual card numbers for vendor payments and tools to simplify expense management.
  • Personal guarantees are generally required, making the applicant personally responsible for the account even when applying under an LLC.
  • Preapproval tools are available and use a soft credit inquiry, so checking eligibility won't impact your credit score, but formal applications will result in a hard inquiry.
  • Some cards are designed for fair credit, while others require excellent credit and may offer higher rewards or premium travel benefits.
  • Annual fees and spending requirements vary, so it's important to match card selection to your business's spending patterns and reward preferences.
