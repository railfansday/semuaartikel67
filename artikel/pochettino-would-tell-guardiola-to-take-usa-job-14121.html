Mauricio Pochettino would tell Pep Guardiola to take the job as the United States head coach if he is offered the chance.

Despite signing a new four-year contract with the USA after leading them to the World Cup quarter-finals in July, Pochettino last week agreed to take over at Manchester United following the dismissal of Michael Carrick.

Guardiola is reportedly US Soccer's favoured candidate to replace Pochettino having left Manchester City at the end of last season.

Asked what he would tell Guardiola if the USA made an official approach, Pochettino replied: "Take it.

"Why? Because it’s a perfect moment to arrive at the federation. Everything is on the right path, in the right direction. Of course, take it.

"Of course, you are going to find really good players, good people – the players can fit his style.".

Pochettino was seen as a coup for USA when he took over and he now believes the role has evolved into one of the most desirable jobs in football.

"The vision from Europe, I promise it is completely different after the World Cup," mentioned Pochettino.

"Now, being head coach of the USA is more prestigious.

"If Manchester United or Real Madrid or Barcelona is coming for the US coach, you start to think about that.

"If you are another coach – maybe [Antonio] Conte, someone else, you say ‘Oh, this is a great opportunity.’ [In my case], ‘Oh, Pochettino, from Chelsea he goes to the US and now to Manchester United.’.

"I think that is, for me, the beginning of the change for the future: that idea that the American player can be very good, can be very competitive.

"The talent is here; it’s so powerful in this country. I think that is why I feel proud. Maybe that was our job, because I don’t believe in coincidence. That has happened because something needed to happen.
