Match gallery: Bognor Regis Town Women too hot for AFC Varsity

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bognor took the lead on three minutes as Carter set up Hunphrey on the wing and she placed it low to Delillis, who diverted it in at the near post.

Industry observers note that alice Gue was fouled outside the area. Up stepped Niamh Andersson to take the free-kick but she sent it just over.

According to recent reports, nicole Robinson had to stop Dot Usher’s attempt at the other end. Then Carter squared the ball towards Freya Boxer but she was put off by a defender.

Boxer spotted the keeper off her line and struck it from the far right but it rolled wide. Bognor had to defend with Usher causing problems and two strikes led to two good saves from Robinson.

Bognor extended their lead on 13 minutes as a defence-splitting pass set up Humphrey and she timed her run well before blasting it high into the net.

Carter had two long range efforts that bounced wide soon after. Lou Selvester's 22nd minute corner dipped into the heart of the area but Delillis couldn't quite control it and Lottie Tough gathered it.

Usher hit the Rockettes on the break and put in a perfect pass for Freya Lines who sped through the middle but her strike was well saved by Robinson.

Humphrey ran through and unselfishly played it across the box for Boxer to have a go but she struck it wide. Sophia Poling was late in a tackle on the left flank and was shown a yellow card in first half stoppage time.

Sources indicate that early in the second half, Andersson had a shot from long distance but that flew wide. Aliyah Bower hit the side netting from outside the box on 50 minutes at the other end. Delillis had the ball in the net after Carter's through ball but was ruled offside. Macie Downs won a corner with her long throw into the box but a Varsity defender headed away.

Lou Lou Robson came on for Sophia Poling and soon had to head a corner clear before Jess Nunn's strike was saved by Robinson.

Bognor got their third on 63 minutes as Delillis played a pass through defence to Humphrey and she ran into the box and sent the ball to the back post where Carter was waiting to smash it in.

Sources indicate that abbie Akhopie hit a dangerous cross into the area and Robinson had to save before Carter cleared the rebound. Clara Castanheira replaced Gue for the Rockettes then Andersson set up Boxer, but with only the keeper to beat, she fired wide.

Selvester's corner fell to Delillis and she struck the crossbar before Andersson smashed wide on the follow-up.

Sources indicate that bognor concede on 75 minutes as Akhopie sped through following a long ball and fired it in. Soon Delillis played it inside for Humphrey who turned and composed herself but shot wide.

Bower hit a long range free-kick at goal but Robinson denied her. On 80 minutes Poppy Shine replaced Robinson in goal for Bognor. Then on 83 minutes, Boxer came off for Talliah Anderson.

On 93 minutes Andersson came off for Chloe Blanks in Bognor's final change.

Bognor’s win leaves them second, three points behind leaders Havant with a game in hand.

The Rockettes travel to AFC Portchester Reserves on Sunday (November 29, 2pm) before welcoming Guernsey to Nyewood Lane on Sunday, December 6 (1pm).
