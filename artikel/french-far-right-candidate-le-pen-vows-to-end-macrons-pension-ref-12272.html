French far-right candidate Le Pen vows to end Macron's pension reform if elected

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Far-right French presidential contender Marine Le Pen said Tuesday she would eliminate President Emmanuel Macron's pension reform, allowing hundreds of thousands of French workers to retire earlier, and create a fresh €900 billion retirement fund. The frontrunner in polls to win the presidential race is seeking to convince investors she can balance one of her most popular pledges with promises ​to restore order to ‌the public finances.
