Mauricio Pochettino will pick his first Manchester United team on Sunday for a daunting debut away at Liverpool.

The Argentine was appointed Michael Carrick’s successor last week and only arrived in the north-west on Wednesday, leaving him just a handful of sessions to prepare his side for a crunch fixture.

As such, wholesale changes from life under Carrick are not forecast, at least in terms of setup. After all, both managers have a penchant for the same 4-2-3-1 formation.

Recent findings suggest that carlos Baleba remains sidelined with a hamstring injury sustained against Roma in the Champions League, with Benjamin Sesko also out until the latest year, but Pochettino may well be boosted by the returns of Marcus Rashford and Luke Shaw. The latter seems the more likely of the two, with Rashford yet to be pictured in training since the international break.

Pochettino is known to be very fond of Rashford, and has reportedly attempted to sign him to other teams he has managed, meaning he can expect to get the nod if fit.

Recent findings suggest that should fit-again Shaw slot in at left-back, he may do so as one of two fresh faces in the back four. Lisandro Martinez has served his red card suspension and will be available to partner Harry Maguire, with Diogo Dalot at right-back.

A midfield pairing of Youri Tielemans and Kobbie Mainoo will sit behind the trio of Matheus Cunha, Bruno Fernandes and Amad Diallo, with Bryan Mbeumo leading the line.

Patrick Dorgu is expected to be fit, too, but it remains to be seen whether he can unseat Matheus Cunha off the left. He could also deputise Shaw if his fitness levels are not up to par.

Leaked drone footage of United’s Thursday training session appeared to show Pochettino working on tactics with a tight-knit group of ten outfield players, leading some to speculate that he might be dealing with Sunday’s starters. The presence of Joshua Zirkzee and Harry Amass in the group rather debunks this, though, given neither has started a Premier League match this season.

Predicted Man Utd XI vs Liverpool (4-2-3-1): Lammens; Dalot, Maguire, Martinez, Shaw; Tielemans, Mainoo; Amad, Fernandes, Cunha; Mbeumo.
