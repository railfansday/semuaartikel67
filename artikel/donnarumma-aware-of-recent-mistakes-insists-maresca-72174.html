Enzo Maresca insisted Manchester City goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma is "aware" of his recent mistakes and revealed he will start against Fulham.

City lost each of their last three Premier League matches before the international break, a run which started with a 2-1 defeat at Aston Villa.

Donnarumma's mistake in that fixture led to Jackson's winner; only Bart Verbruggen (five), Jan Paul van Hecke and Robin Roefs (both four) have made more errors leading to goals than Donnarumma in the Premier League since the start of 2025-26.

And among goalkeepers, only Robin Roefs (eight) has committed more errors leading to goals in the competitions than Donnarumma (seven) in that time.

Donnarumma also made an error in Italy's Nations League win over France, before conceding four in a defeat to Belgium.

Maresca admitted City must stop their mistakes in defence, but believes Donnarumma will learn from his errors.

In a recent development, "He has been good. With Italy he made the mistake in the build-up, but then I read that he was man of the match in the [next] game, he made many good saves," said Maresca.

City have won their last 20 games against Fulham in all competitions, the longest winning streak one team has had against another in English football.

However, City have lost each of their last three Premier League games, as many as in their previous 36 (W25 D8); since the start of 2007-08, they have only lost four in a row twice, doing so in August 2008 and December 2024.
