Mikel Arteta mentioned David Raya’s shoulder injury isn’t ‘too serious’, sparking hope that the goalkeeper could return within weeks.

Raya was forced off in the 70th minute of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle on Saturday at St James’ Park, hurting his left shoulder in a collision with William Osula. He looked in agony as he left the pitch.

His No 2 Kepa Arrizabalaga is instead expected to play for the north London club in their Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

Though Arteta confirmed Raya doesn’t require surgery and didn’t yet rule him out for Sunday’s Premier League clash against Manchester City.

‘He’s (Raya) not fit for tomorrow, but it doesn’t look too serious,’ remarked Arteta. ‘We need some more tests tomorrow, but it’s quite encouraging. It seems to be better than expected.

‘We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain he had. The first test shows something much more encouraging.

Arsenal's No 1 David Raya underwent an MRI scan the day after going off against Newcastle.

‘There is no need for surgery, which was the main fear because that would have ruled him out for months.’.

Asked whether Raya could play at home to Man City, he added: ‘Tomorrow we’ll have more tests and we will know. I think we’ve seen what David brings to the team as well.

‘That’s why we are very protective with Kepa and (third-choice) Illan (Meslier). Everybody needs to be ready because these things unfortunately happen.’.

Raya has been sensational for the Premier League champions and his absence is likely to hurt the best defensive unit.

'That's a massive worry,' Ian Wright noted on Sky Sports when the stopper went down. 'I don't want to say I'm petrified simply because Kepa is in goal, he's the guy who has got to try and keep the goals out for us now.

It has been revealed that 'But yeah, that is a massive blow. Someone like him, Declan Rice, Gabriel, it is very tough to replace those guys.'.

Meanwhile, Bruno Guimaraes stated Arteta is ‘crazy’ about the small details that will take him to ‘another level’.

Since his summer move from Newcastle, the 29-year-old has scored vital goals against Sunderland and Leeds — but is yet to complete a full 90 minutes in the Premier League.

‘I now definitely understand my role in the team and the way Mikel wants me to play but I can still play much better,’ said the Brazilian.

Raya hit the turf in pain and went off the pitch clutching his arm to indicate that something serious had happened.

It has been revealed that ‘I think Mikel (Arteta) is unbelievable. He as a manager is so focused on small details, the way you control the ball, the way that you cannot be aligned with your opposite side.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Some details I have never heard before, I just found out with him here. He is crazy a little bit about these small details and I try and take (in) as much as I can because he is so passionate about them.

In a recent development, ‘He revealed me some clips (from when I was) in Newcastle and some clips here, where he thinks I can do things differently. He's trying to take me to another level.’.
