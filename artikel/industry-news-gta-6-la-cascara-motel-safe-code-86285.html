It has been revealed that you'll need the GTA 6 La Cascara safe code if you decide to rob this tempting-looking ruined motel in Port Gellhorn. This is probably the most lucrative robbery in the town with an LMG, grenade launcher, and a unique weapon, plus a unique vehicle and a whole lot of drugs.

Thankfully, though, the safe code, which is 6969 (nice) is fixed for this robbery. As with the Brooks Boatworks safe code and the Bowling Alley safe code, you can actually find all three of these on a board in the Brooks Boatworks robbery in northwest Port Gellhorn. To find it:.

Recent findings suggest that make sure to bring Jason to La Cascara when you can, since there's more than two stash bags worth of loot. Also, while you're on the top floor of La Cascara, head into the room next to the safe to find the Scrapped VF5C on the table—this silenced SMG is perhaps the best early gun I've found, simply because you can sneakily take out enemies. There's also a grenade launcher on the lower floor.

As well as those, make sure to scour the top floor, the floor below it, and the first floor, for weed, pills, sticky bombs, and lots of cash. Finally, steal the Vapid Firebug Dominator (the red and black muscle car) out front to clear all the key valuables.
