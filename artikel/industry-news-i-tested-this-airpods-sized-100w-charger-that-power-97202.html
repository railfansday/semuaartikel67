Many of the best phones and best laptops often claim to reach speedy charging levels — like how my Motorola Razr Fold is advertised to have 80W charging — so the screen on the charger shows the exact real-time wattage going through each of its three USB-C ports. With a quick tap, I can also cycle through different display readouts, including the charging protocol, internal temperature, and how much time is remaining until a full charge.
