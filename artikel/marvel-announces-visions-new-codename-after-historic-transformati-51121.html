Marvel's Vision has a newly introduced codename, as Marvel announces an upcoming transformation into an even more powerful robotic form. Here's the new codename, and what it means for the fate of everyone's favorite synthezoid.

Industry observers note that debuting in 1968's Avengers #57, Vision was named for a classic Marvel character - the Golden Age hero known as Aarkus, an alien who fought alongside Captain America in WWII. In Marvel canon, it was coined by Janet van Dyne's Wasp (much like the 'Avengers' name itself) on seeing the mysterious figure for the first time, when Vision was sent to attack the Avengers.

Vision has undergone numerous transformations since then, from taking on his emotionless white form to being semi-recreated as the Young Avengers hero Jonas. Now, Vision undergoes his darkest transformation yet and a latest name change.

According to recent reports, marvel recently revealed latest details for the upcoming Hulk War: Infernal Rage #1, officially revealing the names of each of the 'Infernal Avengers' commanded by the titular villain. These monsters are all heroes corrupted by the Infernal Hulk's magic and possessed by the souls of ancient monsters.

Industry observers note that the press release confirmed what fans had guessed - that the robotic being by Hulk's side is a transformed Vision. Patterned after a biblically-accurate angel, this latest Vision is a floating metallic skull with a single eye and four wings, a gigantic, uncovered brain emerging from the top.

Marvel's upcoming images name the being "The Diaboli Ex Machina," meaning "the devil from the machine." The reference is to an ancient Greek theatrical convention where a supernatural being would emerge to conclude the story, often appearing by a literal mechanism. "Diaboli Ex Machina" therefore speaks to a devil literally emerging from a mechanism, and also a supernatural being appearing suddenly to defeat Marvel's heroes.

Vision hasn't yet taken this form in Marvel Comics, though fans did glimpse it in an Amazing Spider-Man 1000/Queen in Black CGD 2026 #1 flash forward to the upcoming events of Hulk War.

The transformation is likely to happen in December 2's Infernal Hulk vs. Avengers #1, which will see Captain America, Iron Man, Captain Marvel, Vision and Scarlet Witch take on the Infernal Hulk, aka the demonic Eldest.

It has been revealed that because everyone but Wanda has a confirmed monster form in Hulk War - and due to their past romantic and familial bond - fans have speculated that The Diaboli Ex Machina is a combination of Scarlet Witch and Vision. This would go some way to explaining the religious iconography of Visions' upcoming design, but others characters have undergone radical transformations without this kind of explanation.

Industry observers note that it's more likely that the angelic elements of VIsion's transformation are connected to the demon soul implanted in him by the Infernal Hulk. It may even be linked to the android sent to defeat the Infernal Hulk by the One-Above-All (image above), which was destroyed but could be repurposed in transforming the Vision.

Industry observers note that marvel has been building to 2027's Hulk War since 2025, when the demonic Eldest defeated Bruce Banner and stole his gamma powers. Now, after resurrecting an army of monsters and corrupting heroes including Spider-Man, the Fantastic Four and iconic X-Men including Jubilee, the Hulk War is nearly upon us.

Before that point, the Avengers will embark on one last, desperate mission to stop the Infernal Hulk in his tracks, leading to the creation of the Iron Thing, the Ebon Sphinx, the War Dead and Vision's horrific Diaboli Ex Machina.

Hulk War: Infernal Rage #1 is coming February 3 from Marvel Comics.
