Combine 1 large red or yellow onion, finely chopped, 3 celery stalks, finely chopped, 1 large carrot, peeled, finely chopped, 1 tsp. freshly ground pepper, and ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Reduce heat to medium and cook, stirring often and scraping up any browned bits stuck to bottom of pan, until vegetables are softened, 6–8 minutes. Add 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced, and cook, stirring, just until fragrant, about 1 minute.

It has been revealed that add 2 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, 1½ cups apple cider, 1½ cups French green lentils, picked through, rinsed, ½ cup finely chopped parsley, 3 Tbsp. Dijon or whole grain mustard, and remaining ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt to pan. Increase heat to high and bring to a boil. Cook until liquid is slightly reduced, about 5 minutes. Cover pan, reduce heat to medium-low, and cook until lentils are tender and most of the liquid is absorbed but mixture is still saucy, 30–35 minutes.

Uncover pan and stir in 1 Tbsp. apple cider vinegar or red wine vinegar. Nestle sausages into lentil mixture and add any juices pooled on plate. Simmer until sausages are warmed through, about 5 minutes. Top with parsley leaves and reserved celery leaves to serve.
