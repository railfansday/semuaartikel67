The Danny Drinkwater Chelsea story still carries a sting. Five years at Stamford Bridge gave him plenty of time to think about how it all turned out, and his own verdict is not a happy one.

Drinkwater opened up in a Truth or Padel appearance alongside David Ornstein. He noted the move was one he could never have turned down, even with the benefit of hindsight. “No, I don’t regret moving. I regret, in hindsight, how it’s turned out. I think I could never turn it down,” he said.
