Spain: the tears of a referee during the tribute to Jesús de Miguel

A moving scene took place this Saturday before the Spanish second division meeting between Cadiz and Real Oviedo. While a minute of silence was organized in memory of Jesús de Miguel, the Tenerife striker who died on Tuesday after a shock during the match, referee Marta Huerta de Aza was unable to hold back her tears. Very touched by the disappearance of the player, the Spanish official burst into tears during the meditation, before the eyes of the two teams. A few moments earlier, she had placed a bouquet of flowers on the lawn, where the fatal impact had occurred. Originally from Palencia, but affiliated with the Tenerife arbitration committee, Marta Huerta de Aza thus paid a personal tribute to the missing player, in a heavy atmosphere.

The disappearance of Jesús de Miguel has had a profound impact on Spanish football, which is paying tribute to him throughout the weekend. The Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF), LaLiga and Liga F have decided to organize a joint tribute in all stadiums from November 6 to 9. Several meetings began with a minute of silence, while applause also accompanied these moments of contemplation. Some matches were even interrupted in the ninth minute, in reference to the number 9 worn by the Tenerife striker. These different initiatives bear witness to the emotion aroused by the death of the player, whose death shocked his teammates, his opponents and the whole of Spanish football.
