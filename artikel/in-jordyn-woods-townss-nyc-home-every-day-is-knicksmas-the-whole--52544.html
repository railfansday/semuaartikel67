In Jordyn Woods-Towns’s NYC Home, Every Day is Knicksmas: “The Whole House Is Knicks-ed Out Already!”

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The most wonderful time of the year holds a special place in Jordyn Woods-Towns’s heart—and no, we’re not talking about the start of basketball season. The fashion designer got engaged to Knicks phenom Karl-Anthony Towns on Christmas Eve 2025, and after a whirlwind 2026 including an NBA championship win for the Latest York NBA team (possibly courtesy of her viral accessory-turned-amulet) and a clifftop wedding replete with stunning details, the couple can’t wait to create festive memories as husband and wife.

“This is my first married Christmas,” Woods-Town tells AD. “I’ve only been married three months, but it’s our first holiday season, so I definitely need to try to figure out what our fresh traditions are.”.

The newlywed reveals that she created an entire PowerPoint presentation to prep attendees for her bachelorette party, so you’d best believe she appreciates a little organization when it comes to getting in the spirit for massive days. Planner that she is, Woods-Towns linked up with design marketplace Minted for her first family Christmas card and a curated suite of picks to welcome one particularly merry and bright yuletide season of life. Below, AD gets her take on holiday colors, basketball-themed decor, and more.

In anticipation of the holiday season, Woods-Towns teamed up with Minted to curate a selection of festive stationery and decor.

Architectural Digest: Have you always been a big holiday person?

Jordyn Woods-Towns: Every single season, I decorate. On Valentine’s Day I have the girls over to do a charcuterie board with salami roses and chocolate covered strawberries. Karl and I went crazy for Halloween; we turned the entire front yard into a whole different space with a graveyard and a 20-foot-tall skeleton. On Christmas, I love a theme.

For a couple years I went with a Candy Cane Lane vibe and now I’m in my chic Newly introduced York era. I love a Ralph Lauren Christmas. I love the natural linen and natural materials. I’ve been really into natural florals, textural things. I love feathers, incorporating some ostrich feathers throughout—I do a lot of that with my brand, and I feel like feathers are really having a moment right now.

In a recent development, in Upcoming York it’s been hard because I don’t drive and I don’t have my car. I can’t just run down to HomeGoods, grab something, and come back!

What’s your decor looking like this year?

I’ve been really into all one shade. There were a lot of greens in our wedding and green is my color of the season.

Woods-Towns is leaning toward florals and green for holiday decorating this year.

So it’s a green Christmas this year?

In a recent development, the tree’s already green. It could be kind of cool, full green-on-green. Now I’m the Grinch. [laughs].

With your Christmas Eve engagement last year, the holiday is a sweet part of your love story. What sorts of traditions are you looking forward to starting with Karl?

According to recent reports, I started with the holiday card with Minted. This is our first holiday card as a family. Obviously after you have kids, it kind of changes, but right now we’re at a time where life is just… he has a [Christmas Day] game and so we’re like, “Okay, we’re living the young, fast life.” But as time goes on, as our marriage goes on, we’re going to build different traditions. We love to be with our family and I always cook every holiday, so I’ll definitely be cooking after the game.

Is there a signature dish you like to serve?

It has been revealed that karl’s a picky eater. He loves chicken, so some version of chicken. He’s never had beef or pork or anything in his whole entire life. It’s just us, so some type of chicken and sides. Pretty simple.

What was the vibe of your holiday card?

According to recent reports, I wanted to use a photo from our wedding that really felt like us, something that not everyone has seen, to tie in our whole year. It was a green marbled border design with gold foil border by Jenna Holcomb. There are those shades of green, since there’s a lot of green incorporated into our wedding. Olive is very timeless, and it felt chic and like us, like something that people could keep. I don’t want to say everyone’s going to want to put a photo of us in their house, but I know our family [will].

It has been revealed that the Towns family’s holiday card. The design is “Marbled” by Jenna Holcomb.

Are you hosting bigger parties this season or just keeping it more intimate with the celebrations?

No huge parties, just because I don’t have the capacity to do that right now, but definitely getting the family together. We don’t have a huge family, so it should be pretty intimate, hopefully doing something with the team getting together. I think one of the girls was talking about doing a holiday party—even though I love to be the host, I don’t mind just showing up.

I would say it’s all in the details. I think through everything. But overall, it’s about the vibe that you’re bringing, the energy, and what you’re putting out. People remember the music, the food, and how they felt.

According to recent reports, it’s been a busy 2026 for Woods-Towns between then-fiancé Karl-Anthony Towns’s successful championship run with the Fresh York Knicks and her August wedding, but she’s keeping her energy high heading into holiday party season. “I feel like there’s an event every other day. But it’s not going to be like this forever. I had to change my mindset from, ‘Wow, I’m so tired,’ to, ‘Wow, I’m so lucky to be able to do this.’”.

Are you planning on sprinkling any Knicks Easter eggs throughout your home decor or in your gifting this year?

Industry observers note that there’s so many Knicks things in my house that it’s hard to escape. It’s already everywhere, the whole house is Knicks-ed out already! But I don’t mind Knicks-themed gifting—people love the Knicks.

Karl’s very hard to gift for, and his birthday’s actually right before the holidays. So it’s hard to figure out two really good gifts in a short amount of time. Gift giving is one of my love languages, and I love getting people things that they really can utilize in their everyday life. I need to think about that.

How else do you get in the holiday spirit at home?

I already bought Christmas pajamas and I already watched The Grinch twice with my niece and nephew two days ago. So I’m ready!
