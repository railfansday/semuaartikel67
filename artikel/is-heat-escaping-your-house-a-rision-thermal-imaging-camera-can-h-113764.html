Recent findings suggest that A thermal imaging camera detects infrared radiation emitted by objects and turns this into a visual representation of surface temperatures. Warmer (red, orange) and cooler (blue, purple) areas appear in contrasting colors, allowing you to see temperature differences that you can’t see with your own eyes.

This opens up some useful possibilities for homeowners. Imagine scanning around a window and spotting a cooler section along the frame. That doesn’t automatically mean there’s an air leak, but it’s a good reason to check the weatherstripping, caulking or surrounding insulation.
