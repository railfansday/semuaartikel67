Limitless started the brighter, dominating possession in the opening 10 minutes of this Sussex Junior Cup third round tie, before Goring gradually took control of the game with the midfield trio of Todor Bankov, Nathan Madgwick and Bambo carving out several chances for a front three of Connor Pomeroy, Ben Rubano and Luke Tate. Limitless kept Goring at bay until the 31st minute when Bambo fed Rubano whose clever flick found man of the match Connor Pomeroy who drove home from the edge of the box to give Goring a 1-0 lead. Four minutes later Bambo slipped in Pomeroy and his one two with Rubano saw him drive home for 2-0.

On 43 minutes a fine pass from Bankov found Pomeroy and his shot from just inside the box was very well saved by the home keeper. From the resultant corner, Luke Tate found Rubano who curled a delightful shot into the top corner to give Goring a well deserved 3-0 lead at half time.

Goring started the second half strongly with a Pomeroy header hitting the post on 48 minutes and a fine half volley soon after from Connor Harvey striking the crossbar. Debutant Josh Etherington went close on 63 minutes with a header from a Jacob Pippen cross before Limitless reduced the deficit from a free kick on 80 minutes, but the Goring defence of Ben Crack, Alex Staines, Charlie Sparks and Hayden Briggs were in no mood to concede again and the game ended with a well deserved 3-1 win for Goring who now go to Bosham Reserves for a fourth round tie on the 5th December.
