📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It’s a substantial day for the song “Runway,” which was written for the film The Devil Wears Prada 2 by a team that included Lady Gaga, Doeechi and Bruno Mars and performed on the film’s soundtrack by Gaga and Doechii.

First, the ballroom-influenced house-pop tune, which reached No. 50 on the Billboard Hot 100 and No. 25 on the US Spotify chart, received two Grammy Award nominations: best pop duo/group performance, for Gaga and Doechii; and best song written for visual media, for Gaga (real name Stefani Germanotta), Doechii (as Jaylah Hickmon), Henry Walter, Dernst “D’Mile” Emile II, Jayda Love, Bruno Mars and Andrew Watt. Now, The Hollywood Reporter can exclusively report that ahead of last Thursday’s submission deadline — and contrary to widespread speculation, including our own — “Runway” was 20th Century’s sole entry from the film for the best original song Oscar, not the other song that Gaga co-wrote for the film and performed on its soundtrack, “Shape of a Woman.”.

The Devil Wears Prada 2, released in May, was a strong performer at the box office, opening to $77 million domestically and $233.6 million globally en route to a worldwide gross of nearly $700 million.

Gaga, who also plays a small role in the film, has been nominated for the best original song Oscar three times: for “Til It Happens to You,” from The Hunting Ground, in 2016; “Shallow,” from A Star Is Born, in 2019; and “Hold My Hand,” from Top Gun: Maverick, in 2023. Doechii has never been Oscar-nominated.

The “Runway” music video, which was directed by Parris Goebel, became the #1 trending music video worldwide on YouTube.
