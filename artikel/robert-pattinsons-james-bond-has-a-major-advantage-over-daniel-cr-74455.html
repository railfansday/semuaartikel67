Robert Pattinson's James Bond Has A Major Advantage Over Daniel Craig's 007

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that auditions for the latest James Bond have been ongoing for months. Many A-list actors threw their names into the hat for the iconic role, with Josh O’Connor, Callum Turner, and Jack Lowden all proving to be finalists to be the next British spy. Then, just a few weeks ago, a twist came — both Robert Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult had reportedly entered the fray late on.

According to recent reports, now, Robert Pattinson has been officially unveiled to play Bond after impressing producers during his audition. The actor, who is still currently also playing Batman, is set to join yet another huge movie series, once again in a leading role. Pattinson had made a name for himself (post-Twilight) by appearing in smaller, independent projects, but in the last few years, has made a return to major blockbuster films with the likes of The Batman, The Odyssey, and now James Bond.

In a recent development, pattinson stated during the official press release for the news that it’s “the honor of a lifetime” to be chosen as the next 007. Director Denis Villeneuve had great praise for Pattinson, saying that “there could only be one 007,” but that the English actor stood out among the rest of the applicants (who he also had plenty of praise for, too). This will be the second time that Villeneuve and Pattinson work together, after next month’s highly anticipated Dune: Part Three.

In fact, it’s this already established partnership that may prove to be hugely important to the success of the next James Bond film. Daniel Craig, for example, had never worked with any of his Bond directors before, which meant that they had to find their creative partnership while working. Pattinson and Villeneuve, meanwhile, already have an established working relationship, which already puts them at a huge advantage, and is a good sign for the next movie in this iconic and long-running franchise.

As stated, Robert Pattinson and Denis Villeneuve have already worked once together on the upcoming Dune: Part Three. In the new film, which arrives on December 18 (the same day as Avengers: Doomsday), Pattinson plays Scytale in the new film, and serves as an antagonist to Timothee Chalamet’s Paul Atreides. The two are set to butt heads when the film arrives next month.

It has been revealed that scytale is described as a genetically engineered shapeshifter who was created by the Bene Tleilax. Now, he looks to disrupt Paul’s reign, not through outright warfare, but via espionage and manipulation of those around him. This makes Scytale a very different kind of villain for Paul to face, and a different kind of character for Pattinson, too. Pattinson has mostly played heroes in his career, from Edward Cullen in Twilight to Bruce Wayne in The Batman.

Yet, Villeneuve clearly was impressed by Pattinson so much while filming Dune: Part Three that he felt confident that the actor could play another significant role in his career. Not only that, but Villeneuve had only worked with Pattinson when the actor plays a villain, so the Canadian director must have seen enough in filming (and through his other roles) to convince him that this was the right man for James Bond.

Sources indicate that once again, it’s this pre-established relationship between Pattinson and Villeneuve that could prove to be crucial when the next James Bond movie starts filming. After all, the two are clearly used to working with one another, and are currently spending virtually every day with each other as they embark on the massive press tour for Dune: Part Three.

Sources indicate that ultimately, Pattinson is one of the very best actors working today and Villeneuve is, similarly, one of the very best directors working today. As a result, their collaboration on Dune: Part Three simply made a lot of sense, as does their upcoming work together on the still untitled James Bond film. Anticipation is high for the next 007 installment, even more so with Pattinson’s casting announcement, and his creative partnership with Villeneuve proves to be another huge element as the film gets closer to production.

Dune: Part Three releases in theaters on December 18.
