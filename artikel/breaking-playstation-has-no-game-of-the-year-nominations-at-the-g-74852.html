Last night, The Game Awards 2026 nominations were officially unveiled, and fans immediately noticed the prestigious Game of the Year category - previously won by the likes of Astro Bot, Clair Obscur, and Elden Ring - does not feature a PlayStation-published game this year. It's a rare sight for PlayStation Studios, which has secured a Game of the Year nomination every year since 2015.

PlayStation has published several games this year that might usually be shoe-ins for a Game of the Year nomination. Marvel's Wolverine is the tech giant's highest profile first-party release in 2026, but it's also responsible for this year's Saros, as well as the externally-developed but PlayStation-published Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls.

Recent findings suggest that despite missing out on the Game of the Year slot, however, PlayStation still features in other categories. All three of the aforementioned games are up for various, non-GOTY awards, while Marathon is nominated for Best Multiplayer, and the upcoming God of War Laufey for Most Anticipated.
