Matthias Jaissle has been talking about Bruno Guimaraes.

The Newcastle United Head Coach meeting the media at his press conference ahead of the Arsenal match on Saturday.

This is set to be the first return of Bruno Guimaraes to St James’ Park since he left in the summer.

Matthias Jaissle was officially appointed as the new Newcastle United Head Coach on 5 August 20206.

Only three days later, Bruno Guimaraes signed for Arsenal.

According to recent reports, today Matthias Jaissle making public that he tried to persuade Bruno Guimaraes to remain at Newcastle United for this 2026-27 season: “His decision was made already to join Arsenal, but I tried my best to convince him [to stay], I told him that we counted on him as a leader, I tried to explain the role he would have with us. It was my job to do my best and at least then I can look in the mirror and say that I tried my best.”.

In a recent development, matthias Jaissle talking about the challenge of facing Arsenal: “If you face the champions, that is motivation enough. We are playing at home in front of our fans; they can be the twelfth man. The fans can give us so much power and support. This is a great opportunity, a major challenge.”.

In a recent development, matthias Jaissle very happy to talk about the quality of Lewis Hall: “He is a marvellous player. I said that at the start when he committed his long-term future to the club. We are really pleased to have him here; he is a young lad with huge potential. He is doing a great job, not only for us, but also for his national team.”.
