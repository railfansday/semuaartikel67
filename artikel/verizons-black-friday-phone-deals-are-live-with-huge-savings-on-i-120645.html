Industry observers note that black Friday phone deals are everywhere right now, and Verizon has officially kicked off its own Black Friday sale in earnest. That means you can save on some of the latest flagship phones, claim some free gifts in the process, or grab yourself a discounted phone strategy for your whole family.

Here are the best Verizon Black Friday phone deals available now.

In a recent development, the Ultra is also eligible for a deal that gets you a free Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Buds 4. However, you will need to activate that watch with a Verizon service proposal if you want to claim those free buds.

Industry observers note that if you prefer the passport-shaped design in a foldable, then the Galaxy Z Fold 8 might be the one for you. Verizon has knocked the monthly payment down to $39.99, which saves you $460 on the price of the phone.

Verizon is also offering a free Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Buds 4 with an eligible Simplicity initiative and a Watch service plan.

This phone is also eligible for free accessories, with Verizon throwing in a Moto Watch Ultra, a Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G and a pair of Meta Glasses. A service initiative is needed for the watch and tablet, though.

There's no discount on the iPhone 18 Pro at Verizon, but signing up for a three year Simplicity Plus or Simplicity Pro plan will get you a free Apple Watch series 12 and the iPad 11.

That's quite a nice deal, since those two accessories would cost you $898 if you purchased them on their own at full price. You will need a Verizon service initiative to claim them both for free, though.

It has been revealed that if the S26 Ultra has too much for you, then the Galaxy S26 Plus might be more up your street. It lacks premium features like the S Pen and fourth camera lens, but it still offers a bright 6.,7-inch display, good battery life, fast charging, great cameras and the best Galaxy AI has to offer.

According to recent reports, save $184 on the Samsung Galaxy S26, with Verizon dropping monthly payments down to $22.66 instead of the usual $27.77. All you need is that three-year Simplicity strategy to qualify.

That way you get the flagship Galaxy experience with good cameras, battery life, and all the Galaxy AI features you can hope for right now.

It has been revealed that if you'd rather get a foldable with a clamshell design, then the Razr+ 2026 is a solid option for you. This deal at Verizon drops your monthly price from $30.55 to just $20 for your three-year Simplicity Blueprint.

Sources indicate that verizon will also give you a free Moto Watch Ultra, Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G and a pair of Meta Glasses. Though the watch and tablet do need a Verizon service proposal.

Save over 50% on the cost of a Pixel 11 Pro XL at Verizon, when you sign on for a Simplicity plan. Normally you'd be paying $36.11 a month for three years, but right now that figure has dropped to $19.99.

In a recent development, the Pixel 11 Pro is also available with a free Pixel Watch 5 a Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G tablets, and a pair of Meta Glasses. Again, the watch and tablet will require a Verizon service blueprint.

The same deal is available on the Pixel 11 Pro, with the monthly payment dropping from $30.55 to $13.33 on a three-year Simplicity strategy.

The Pixel 11 Pro can be had with a free Pixel Watch 5 and Google Pixel Buds 2a. Though, like the iPhone deal, getting the buds means activating your watch with a Verizon service proposal first.

In a recent development, samsung flagships are more expensive now, but Verizon is letting slash the cost of the Galaxy S26 FE — which is already pretty good value. That gets you a solid selection of flagship features, but without the inflated retail prices Samsung has decided we need to pay.

This is easily the cheapest foldable phone you can get right now, even if it is last year's model.
