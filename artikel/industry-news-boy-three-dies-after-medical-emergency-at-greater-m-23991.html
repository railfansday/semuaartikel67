Police are investigating death of child three days after emergency services were called to Cosy Toes nursery in Middleton.

A three-year-old boy has died after a “medical emergency” at a nursery in Greater Manchester.

Emergency services were called to Cosy Toes nursery in Middleton, near Manchester on the afternoon of 2 November after “reports of a concern for a child”.

Industry observers note that greater Manchester police noted at the time that a child had been taken to hospital after a “medical emergency” and was in a critical condition. An air ambulance landed close to the building, while three police vehicles, an ambulance and a rapid response paramedic car were also dispatched.

According to recent reports, police confirmed on Wednesday that the child, who has not been named, died three days later. “Sadly, the boy passed away on Thursday 5 November,” GMP mentioned in a statement. “His family is being supported by specialist officers during this incredibly difficult time.”.

An investigation had been opened, GMP said, adding that “no arrests have made in that time”. The force said the investigation was in line with usual procedure, “as the circumstances leading up to the boy’s death occurred in a care setting”.

Det Ch Insp Claire Brennan said: “This is an incredibly tragic development in a deeply upsetting incident and our thoughts remain with the boy’s family.

It has been revealed that “We are in the very early stages of our involvement and are carefully considering events and the evidence available to us. For now, we ask that the family’s privacy is respected as they begin to navigate the unimaginable loss of their little boy.”.

Recent findings suggest that cosy Toes runs four nurseries in Middleton, providing childcare for children up to five years old. GMP has asked anyone with information that could help the investigation to make a report online, or call 0161 856 3635.

Details can also be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or by filling out a form on their website.

Cosy Toes has been approached for comment.
