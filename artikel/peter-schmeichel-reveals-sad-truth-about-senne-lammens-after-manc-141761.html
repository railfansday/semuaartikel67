Peter Schmeichel believes Senne Lammens has not recovered from his mistake for Belgium at the World Cup but feels the goalkeeper is still being let down by his defence at Manchester United.

Lammens saved a penalty from Luis Suarez in the first half of United’s 2-2 draw with Sporting CP in the Champions League but then gave away a spot-kick shortly after which the Colombian striker converted.

The Belgian goalkeeper then gifted Sporting their equaliser in the second half as he dropped a high cross which allowed Suarez to acrobatically pounce on the loose ball.

Lammens impressed in his debut season at United following his £18.1 million move from Royal Antwerp last year but so far this term the 24-year-old has looked unconvincing.

United signed Karl Darlow in the summer but the former Leeds United and Newcastle goalkeeper is yet to make an appearance in the Premier League this season.

When asked at what point he believes that Lammens should be dropped, Schmeichel told CBS Sports: ‘I don’t know where that point is.

‘Mauricio Pochettino has come in, he’s brought his own staff, that means he’s got a new goalkeeping coach and maybe that is something that can change it for him.

‘I don’t know if you remember in the World Cup, he came against Spain and made a bit of a mistake, [Mikel] Merino scored and they [Belgium] got knocked out, and I feel like he’s lost a little bit of confidence from that mistake, hasn’t really recovered.

‘As a goalkeeper when you’re not on your best form you need your defenders in front of you to help you out and the sad truth is that they haven’t been able to do that and of course, that makes it so much worse.

‘I like him a lot but today was not a great day for him.’.

Lammens was also backed by United captain Bruno Fernandes after the draw in Lisbon.

‘It’s part of football, mistakes are part of football, and from that, you get goals,’ Fernandes said.

‘But, obviously, it’s not an individual sport, so everyone has to take the blame for everything we do good and everything we do bad.

‘I think the main thing and the most key thing is to understand we need to be together, in the good and in the bad moments, and it’s not going to change the belief we have in each other.’.
