📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

WUHU, China — Following the 2026 Chery International User Summit, Chery Auto is pressing ahead with its upcoming energy ecosystem strategy. Entering the Globalization 3.0 phase, the tech giant is deploying a mix of energy solutions to meet mobility energy needs around the world. As electric vehicles integrate with power grids, Chery Auto is developing a smart energy ecosystem that transforms new energy vehicles (NEVs) into dispatchable distributed mobile energy nodes, supporting grid management, sustainable urban development, and local energy resource utilization.

Unlike conventional charging, which can increase peak demand, V2G offers a flexible alternative. Vehicle-to-grid (V2G) enables bidirectional flow, allowing vehicles to store off-peak electricity and return power during peak demand. For users, this creates opportunities to participate in energy management and potentially generate value. With participation depending on commercial feasibility and user confidence, Chery Group’s global user base exceeding 21 million provides a foundation for V2G deployment.

In a recent development, chery Auto is validating its energy ecosystem across homes, communities and industrial parks. Its household Energy Cube integrates solar, storage and bidirectional charging to support electricity supply during exceptional conditions. In Wuhu, a large-scale AC V2G demonstration station enables private vehicles to support grid management through onboard chargers with limited modifications. In Spain, an industrial park microgrid uses green electricity to reduce energy costs, fossil fuel dependence, and carbon emissions.

Chery Auto’s Volt-dragon Ultra-Fast Charging technology targets 500 km of range in five minutes. Its hybrid engine has achieved 48.57% thermal efficiency, while the planned Rhino S solid-state battery targets 400 Wh/kg energy density. Chery’s Rhino Battery prioritizes fast charging, longevity and safety, addressing battery degradation and thermal management concerns associated with V2G charging cycles.

Chery Auto is developing modular solar, storage, charging, and V2G solutions tailored to regional infrastructure. In Europe, electricity markets provide opportunities for distributed energy participation. Mature markets integrate vehicles into grid management, while regions with weaker infrastructure can prioritize microgrids before introducing V2G.

In a recent development, through applications spanning households, communities, and industrial parks, Chery Auto is extending automotive-grade engineering into energy infrastructure. Its approach supports distributed energy networks using locally generated electricity and managing energy flows, positioning Chery to participate in future smart cities.
