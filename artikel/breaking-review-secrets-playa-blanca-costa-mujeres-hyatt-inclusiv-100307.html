It has been revealed that of note, I was in Cancun, Mexico for business meetings at another resort but wanted to evaluate both this experience for our readers as well as the overall position of Hyatt in its ever-growing Inclusives collection. My stay was almost exactly 24 hours and solo which is uncharacteristic at this destination resort. My experience will likely differ from other guests due to those factors.

Secrets Playa Blanca Costa Mujeres is an adults-only resort in the Hyatt Inclusive Collection, and I toured it on a site visit in November 2026. It sits on a stretch of white-sand beach in Costa Mujeres, 12 miles north of Cancun. Cancun International Airport is the arrival airport. I could not confirm a single official transfer time from the property page. Tour listings commonly put the drive in a 30 to 45 minute range.

Address: Carretera Punta Sam – Isla Blanca, Mz 2, Lt 10, Costa Mujeres, Zona Continental Isla Mujeres, Quintana Roo 77400, Mexico.

Google Maps: Secrets Playa Blanca Costa Mujeres.

According to recent reports, hyatt is the best in the business at high end resort arrivals. I had given my name at the security gate and by the time I reached the lobby, the door to the vehicle was opened for me, I was greeted by name and a “welcome home”, a constant refrain throughout my stay. It’s a small thing and maybe cliché but it was effective. I was given a Globalist upgrade even though rules at all-inclusives differ from the rest of the chain. As my upgrade was in a Preferred room, I was brought into a private room at the front of the lobby where check-in was done for me.

The lobby is an open-air room under an arched woven ceiling, with a direct view out to the ocean.

Industry observers note that the resort is spread across 10 buildings with 505 keys. Of those, 221 are Preferred Club keys with an elevated experience. My building (and others like it) had just five rooms on each floor and just five floors which made it feel boutique rather than massive. Every oceanfront room is Preferred Club, and so are the first two buildings on either side.

In a recent development, the room views follow a simple rule by floor. Every first floor room is a swim-out, floors two through four are tropical view, and the fifth floor is ocean view or ocean front. That makes booking easy to reason through: if you want to step off your patio into the water, go low, and if you want the sea, go high.

Recent findings suggest that the walkways give a sense of grandeur and space. All of the flora is local, and in a touch I loved, the service rooms are named after the plants.

According to recent reports, there are four pools, and each one has a unique characteristic. The Jungle pool is drinks only and hidden in the middle of the resort by trees. The Social pool runs activities throughout the day at this adults-only resort for those who want to participate. There is also an infinity pool on the edge of the beach, and then the Preferred Club pool for those staying in that category. These are separate from the first floor swim-outs.

The spa (hydrotherapy is included for Preferred guests) featured an outdoor circuit that included both hot and cold plunge pools, amongst other therapeutic services.

The Preferred Club pool was the most frequented, though some images below look more vacant due to rain that rolled through during my opportunity to collect media on my short stay.

The gym is open to every guest from 6 AM to 8 PM, and it is outfitted with Technogym equipment.

According to recent reports, the spa is open 8 AM to 8 PM with 17 treatment cabins and a beauty salon, and hydrotherapy is included. That last part is the headline. The hydrotherapy space is ridiculous, easily the standout of the entire property for me, and it is the kind of amenity that would make me pick this resort over a competitor down the beach. There are indoor and outdoor showers as well.

The men’s changing room featured private showers with an option for indoor or outdoor experiences.

The theater seats 264 people and hosts cabaret and Mayan shows. There are also pickleball courts, which I am starting to think should be required equipment at any resort that wants a younger crowd as well as padel for the European set (and growing American interest.).

Secrets is an adults-only brand, and every room here comes with what the tech giant calls Unlimited-Luxury. For a standard guest that means unlimited dining and top-shelf drinks, Wi-Fi, daytime activities and nightly entertainment, with tips and taxes included. Add 24-hour room service, a mini-bar refreshed daily (you can ask for just about anything you want in there), and pool and beach wait service for all guests.

Recent findings suggest that the Preferred Club is an upgrade, and at this resort it is a significant one because it owns the oceanfront. Secrets lists personalized check-in and check-out, afternoon hot and cold hors d’oeuvres, desserts and premium liquors, access to the Preferred Club lounge and beach bar, an upgraded mini-bar, upgraded bath amenities, butler service, Bali beds at the pool and beach reserved for club guests, and exclusive dining at the Preferred Club beach restaurant. Preferred also gives club guests exclusive access to an infinity pool and puts an oversized soaking tub on the terrace of the upgraded suites (at least with the oceanfronts).

In a recent development, I book a lot of Hyatt’s all-inclusives through Hyatt Privé rates, and this resort is one of 388 in the collection. Privé is Hyatt’s invitation-only program for travel advisors, so you only get the benefits when an approved advisor makes the booking, and the stay still earns World of Hyatt points and elite nights.

It has been revealed that the all-inclusive version of Privé looks a lot different from a Park Hyatt booking. As I wrote when Hyatt’s all-inclusive resorts joined Privé, there is no room upgrade and no breakfast benefit, since every meal is already included. At this property, Privé guests receive a $100 resort credit per stay, a welcome amenity and letter, hydrotherapy access, and early check-in and late check-out based on availability.

Industry observers note that elite status matters here more than at most all-inclusives. Under World of Hyatt’s terms, Globalists get the best room available at check-in, including rooms with club access, at the resort’s discretion. Explorists are eligible for upgrades too, but not to standard suites or club rooms. Travelers who have stayed at Secrets resorts as Globalists report being designated Preferred Club guests even when they did not land in a club building, which unlocks the lounge, pool and beach areas. This was my experience on all accounts at the resort. Late checkout is 2 PM for Explorists and 4 PM for Globalists, subject to availability at resorts. It was able to honor my request for a late check-out, I did not arrive early enough to request the early check-in. Hyatt waives resort fees on eligible paid rates (at least for now) and award nights for Globalists, and on award nights only for every other tier, and Globalists also get free parking on free nights though in 2028 Globalists will have to select this as a preference. One catch is that Suite Upgrade Awards cannot be used at Secrets resorts, so you cannot lock in a suite with one here.

According to recent reports, weddings are substantial business here. The courtyard outside the theater is the setting, with the sunset behind it, and the resort hosts lots of them. The resort disclosed its occupancy to me but it wasn’t clear if it was for public consumption. It was shockingly high especially for a Thursday in mid-November. Over the holidays or spring break all the hotels are full but this is a period where I would have expected a lull. Still, despite being extremely full, it never felt crowded.

For meetings and groups, the convention space measures 9,800 square feet and splits into five sections. The boardroom was occupied at the time so I couldn’t include images of that.

In a recent development, restaurants can be booked in advance through the Hyatt Inclusive Collection app, which I wish more all-inclusives offered. Nobody wants to find out at 7 PM that the restaurant they wanted is full and through the app this can be done well in advance of a guest’s arrival. The dress code in the restaurants is resort casual, though the patio does not require slacks and closed toed shoes for men, shorts are acceptable outside.

The restaurants include: Portofino (Italian), Bordeaux (French), Himitsu (pan-Asian, with sushi and teppanyaki), Oceana (seafood in an open-air setting), Barefoot Grill (light lunch), Market Café (the international buffet, which I noted on site as the Marketplace), Seaside Grill (a poolside grill), and Coco Café. The bars on that list include Rendezvous in the lobby, Barracuda on the beach, a swim-up bar, a beach bar, and the Preferred Club beach bar and lounge bar. There’s also a Sports Bar and Lounge next to the theater.

Oceana is the seafood restaurant, set in an open-air dining room with woven pendant lamps, macrame hangings and a pebble mosaic floor. I chose this for my one dinner on property during my brief stay.

In a recent development, I started with the lamb croquettes, which the menu serves with yogurt and sweet chili sauce, cashews, watercress and a pickled shallot salad, and the Iberian salad with fresh mozzarella, prosciutto, rucula, toasted pistachios, balsamic and roasted peach. A crusty loaf came with the meal.

For a main I had the coconut shrimp, served with parsley orzo pasta, coconut sauce and parmesan crispy, and finished with the mango cheesecake, which the menu plates with vanilla cream and a mango compote with honey and basil.

In a recent development, market Café, the international buffet I noted as the Marketplace, serves breakfast until 11:30 AM. [TO CONFIRM: lunch and dinner service at Market Café].

Industry observers note that seaside Grill hosts the Preferred Club à la carte breakfast. The official dining page says breakfast there is served for Preferred Club guests only. I tried a number of dishes for lunch to report to our fine audience.

Rendezvous sits at the heart of the property with 360-degree access, so you can walk up to it from any direction.

Preferred Club guests get their own bar at the beach, and the Preferred Club lounge also has an indoor lounge for when the sun gets to be too much.

My room was a Preferred Oceanfront King, a single open room with a king bed against a macrame headboard wall, rattan pendant lights, a ceiling fan, and a daybed sofa facing a marble bistro table. The balcony with jacuzzi looks straight over the beach. In the bathroom, a freestanding tub sits under a wood-slat ceiling beside a walk-in rain shower with a pebble floor and a bench. The double vanity has vessel sinks, round mirrors and a wave-textured wall, and the shower is stocked with Le Labo Bergamote 22 shampoo, conditioner and shower gel. Le Labo is a confirmed winner for me.

I also had the chance to tour a master suites, all of which are oceanfront, and they are also available as swim-outs.

Every first floor room is a swim-out, opening onto the pool.

Secrets Playa Blanca Costa Mujeres is an incredible resort that somehow felt small when you were in your building but substantial enough to have everything. For my stay, I used 45,000 points on a no-notice booking to offset a $1,300/night stay. I have seen rates from $560 for off-season which is without question worth it. I loved the property and I look forward to returning with more time and my wife, but the adults-only nature of the property will make it a harder stay for us to achieve.
