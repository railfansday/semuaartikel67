In a recent development, in our recently updated shoe guide, we called the Red Chili’s Voltage line a sleeper classic. This shoe is a tautly crafted, downturned, asymmetrical performance sport shoe with a solid crossover into bouldering. This latest version tweaks the Voltage in a few key ways: The higher toebox allows the knuckles to curl more naturally for steep climbing, and there’s an expanded toe-scumming patch and a reshaped heel cup with a narrower neck for a more secure fit. Paired with a stiff forefoot (courtesy of the half-length Pebax 2 mm midsole and the middling-rigid 4 mm XS Grip outsole), this is a built-up shoe that’s perfect for larger or heavier climbers pushing steep ground.
