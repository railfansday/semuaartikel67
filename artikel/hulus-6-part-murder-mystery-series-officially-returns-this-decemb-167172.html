Hulu is quietly building its reputation as the perfect destination for comedy fans. Many of its comedy indicates over the past several years have been positively received by fans and critics. From Deli Boys to The Great and, recently, Phony, the streamer is doing something right. However, one of its comedies towers over the others in critical reception and longevity. With five seasons and an average score of 95% on Rotten Tomatoes, the dramedy Only Murders in the Building is Hulu's undisputed flagship mystery series. Now, the show is set to return for a sixth season that shakes things up by trading the familiar halls of Manhattan's Arconia for a brand-upcoming setting.

Industry observers note that unofficially rebranded as Only Murders in London, the upcoming sixth season sends amateur sleuths Charles (Steve Martin), Oliver (Martin Short), and Mabel (Selena Gomez) to the British capital to untangle their latest homicide. For five seasons, the whodunnit thrived on the hyper-local charm of Upcoming York's Upper West Side and its eccentric building residents. But after five murders, the premise has started to strain believability, and the upcoming season aims to shake things up and possibly counter the slight decline in acclaim.

Sources indicate that relocating the trio to London injects fresh cultural friction, new suspects, and a new brand of humor into a formula that has consistently captivated audiences. Yet, regardless of the accents and architecture, the series' true secret weapon remains the effortless, intergenerational comedic chemistry between its three main characters, as captured in the Only Murders in the Building Season 6 trailer. The new season officially arrives with a two-episode premiere on Tuesday, December 8.
