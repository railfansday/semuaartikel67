Rockstar Games have released 38 upcoming screenshots of Grand Theft Auto 6, ahead of its release later this month.

These screenshots, found on a dedicated launch gallery website Rockstar has created, prompts viewers to scroll down and take in the sights of several high quality in-game screenshots snapshotting all kinds of people, places, and events.

Industry observers note that these include, but are not limited to, a DJ set in-action, a man and his pitbull looking tough to the camera, a tattooed man getting his hair done (machine gun on hand), and a daring motorbike escape. However, my personal favourite has got to be a lively shot of a dive bar. A band - seemingly called The Rusty Anchor is playing - and some older ladies appear to be having a friendly chat with an open-shirt-wearing geezer enjoying a beer.

It has been revealed that you can find all the new images in an image gallery below. So look through them at your pleasure:.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is due to release on the 19th November on PS5, and Xbox Series X/S. A few days prior, Rockstar employees are voting on whether or not they'd like to officially recognise a developer union, as the Rockstar vs IWBG union employment tribunal comes to a close on the eve of GTA 6's release date.
