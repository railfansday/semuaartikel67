It has been revealed that unity's next venture, amidst rumours of it being bought out by the Saudi Arabian Public Investment Fund, is the Unity Fellowship, a grant program that offers $50k in cash to Unity-using indie devs that initiative release a game within a year - without asking for recoup, revenue share, creative control or IP ownership in exchange. I don't need to tell you that all sounds too good to be true, even with the Unity engine requirement, but in fairness, the company has form for handing out cash. The Unity for Humanity grant, just earlier this year, spread $600K across ten different indie projects focused on societal and environmental issues.

Unity states that ten recipient studios will receive the Fellowship's cash grant, as well as direct partnership with Unity staff, industry connections, spots at Unity events and PR/marketing support. Even studios with an existing publisher can apply, if they think that "the Fellowship could still make a real difference for your game." It also clarifies that their policy regarding AI use is that "We don't require you to list the tools you used, but you must own or hold the rights to everything in your game, including any AI-generated content", which doesn't come as a huge surprise considering Unity's excitement around its Unity Spark game-generating AI tool.

Industry observers note that the grant is also not open to "countries or regions subject to U.S. sanctions", such as Russia, Iran and Cuba, as well as Brazil, Italy and Quebec due to "local regulations." For developers not in those areas, applications for the grant are open until December 18th, with the recipients to be notified by the end of February 2027. If you're a developer who's interested, you can apply here.
