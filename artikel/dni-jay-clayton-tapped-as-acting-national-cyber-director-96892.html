DNI Jay Clayton tapped as acting national cyber director

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton has been tapped to serve as acting national cyber director while the Trump administration's permanent nominee for the role awaits Senate confirmation, a White House official told CNBC on Monday.

Recent findings suggest that trump had already added artificial intelligence czar to Clayton's portfolio in October. The official asked not to be named because the pick hasn't been revealed.

The White House last week revealed that President Donald Trump had picked Kirsten Davies, the Pentagon's chief information officer, to lead the Office of the National Cyber Director.

According to recent reports, the U.S. cyber director advises the president on cybersecurity and coordinates U.S. cyber policy across federal agencies. Federal law states that a person may not serve as an acting officer if the president has submitted that person's nomination to the Senate for full appointment and if that person served as first assistant to the office for less than 90 days.

Davies would replace Sean Cairncross, who was confirmed to the role in August 2025 and whose departure was first reported by Axios last week.

Trump, at a White House press conference on Friday, stated he decided to replace Cairncross "because I didn't think that person was good enough.".
