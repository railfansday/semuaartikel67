ETF inflows and improving momentum support the recovery case, but XRP remains below key daily moving averages. For XRP to make a more sustainable move higher, it will need increased buying pressure in the spot market and a move higher through $1.13. For now, XRP can be found trading around $0.95 on Thursday.

In a recent development, according to a November 24 case study from the XRP Ledger (XRPL) team, over 15 million agentic payments have settled on XRPL over the past 12 months. Further, the 7-day average of agentic payments on XRPL sits at 134,618 per day and has been increasing week-over-week.

Agentic payments are made by autonomous AI agents for goods and services such as data and compute. These payments can be made in XRP or Ripple’s USD stablecoin (RLUSD). Further, XRPL charges a fee for these transactions, and the fee is paid in XRP. According to the case study, 99.37% of agentic payments have paid the minimum fee of 10 drops.

According to recent reports, further, 1,000 agentic calls cost approximately $0.014. Agentic payments are facilitated by infrastructure provider t54’s x402, and the AI services organization Heurist is using XRPL for agentic payments.

Despite the advancement in the agentic economy, it’s unclear how much of an impact this will have on XRP buying support, as payments can be made in RLUSD and transaction fees are relatively small, limiting support for foundational growth.

Industry observers note that on the daily chart, XRP is trading below its 50-day ($1.13), 100-day ($1.21), and 200-day ($1.29) Exponential Moving Averages (EMAs). Indicators are showing some improvement, with the Relative Strength Index (RSI) working its way back into the mid-30s and the Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) making a move into positive territory.

Recent findings suggest that for XRP to show sustainable improvement, it will need increased buying pressure in the spot market and a move higher through $1.13. From there, resistance would shift to $1.21 and $1.29. On the other hand, XRP would be exposed if buying pressure wanes, as there are no nearby moving averages for support.

It has been revealed that spot XRP exchange-traded funds (ETFs) saw $4 million in inflows on Wednesday, and open interest (OI) in perpetual futures has increased to 1.85 billion XRP from approximately 1.8 billion XRP the previous day. While this is positive for XRP, it’s still unclear whether this marks the start of a more sustainable move higher.
