Despite the fact that Moto Tag 2 has only been on the market for a few months at this point, Motorola is apparently getting Moto Tag 3 out in the not-too-distant future, and it’s already on sale (probably by mistake).

Recent findings suggest that an Amazon UK listing went up this week for “Moto Tag 3,” a newly introduced version of Motorola’s Android Find Hub tracker. The listing demonstrates the tracker in “Pantone Lucent White,” while a second image shared by Evan Blass shows the new model in a vibrant purple color.

There’s no indication of major changes in “Moto Tag 3,” with Motorola still claiming similar battery life, using a CR2032 battery, and supporting UWB in Android Find Hub.

The listing (thanks Matt!) explains:.

Motorola moto tag 3 helps you locate everyday items with precision and reliability. Using Ultra Wideband and Bluetooth technology, it works with the Android Find Hub network to track belongings both nearby and at distance. With more than 500 days of battery life powered by a replaceable CR2032 battery, it is designed for long-term use. Built-in privacy protections, encrypted location data, and unwanted tracking alerts support secure tracking in a compact, IP68-rated design suitable for daily carry.

Sources indicate that “Moto Tag 3” is on sale from Amazon UK, though it comes with a “1-4 month” shipping estimate. This is almost certainly a slip-up, especially given the timing.

This comes much earlier than expected. The original Moto Tag rolled out in 2024, with its sequel being confirmed in early 2026 before going on sale in the US less than six months ago in June 2026. Such an early move to “Moto Tag 3” is interesting, to say the least, so we’ll have to wait and see if there are any changes in tow beyond just the upcoming colors. That said, these are really nice colors. It’s also possible this is just the same model with a slip-up in branding – the model number on the Amazon listing is unchanged.

Industry observers note that we’ll have to wait and see what happens. Moto Tag 2 did debut at CES in January, and with Razr Flex also in the works, Motorola almost certainly would launch an updated tracker at the trade show if it truly does blueprint to do so soon.

This comes right as Moto Tag 2 has hit its lowest price ever at just $18.99 amid Black Friday sales.
