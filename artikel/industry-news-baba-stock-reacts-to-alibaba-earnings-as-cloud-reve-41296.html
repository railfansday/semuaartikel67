📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that alibaba reported strong fiscal Q2 2027 earnings, with revenue reaching RMB 271.5 billion, up 10% year over year and above analyst expectations. Income from operations surged 443% to RMB 29.1 billion, while non-GAAP net income increased 92% to RMB 19.9 billion. AI Cloud and Compute Services revenue jumped 53% to RMB 61 billion, while revenue from AI-related products recorded triple-digit growth for the 13th consecutive quarter. However, Alibaba’s growing AI investments increased financial pressure. Capital expenditure rose 60% to RMB 50.5 billion, contributing to a free cash flow outflow of RMB 34.5 billion. Adjusted earnings per ADS also missed analyst expectations despite strong revenue growth.
