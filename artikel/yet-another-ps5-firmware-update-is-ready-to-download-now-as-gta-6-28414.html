📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sony has issued another system software revision for PS5, and if you want access to any online features, you'll be forced to download and install it.

Yes, this is a required upgrade; it brings the firmware to version 26.07-14.23.00, and is a fairly small 1.3 GB.

In a recent development, what does it do, then? Well, as was the case with the last patch, it has one solitary patch note:.

According to recent reports, the folks at PlayStation have been really cracking down hard on the console's security. This enhancement comes about two weeks after the last, which was also about a fortnight removed from the previous one — an unusually speedy deployment.

Industry observers note that this is for a couple of reasons. The first is that hackers have been jailbreaking the PS5 in fresh and faster ways, utilising generative AI tech to find workarounds and weaknesses in the console's software infrastructure.

Secondly, GTA 6 is on the verge of release.

This is pertinent because, well, it's GTA 6; it's very possible it'll be the biggest game launch of all time. In other words, there's a heightened risk that people will want to hack their PS5 and gain access to the game without paying.

Industry observers note that sony has been making it so that your console must have the latest firmware installed to even boot up GTA 6, and it's highly likely today's revision will indeed be a necessity if you want to play the game. This theoretically minimises the risk of jailbreaking and piracy.

With the game now less than 48 hours away, Sony has sent out one more pre-launch security measure in its battle against hackers.
