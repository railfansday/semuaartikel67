Julianne Hough is tangoing with matters of the heart.

After the Dancing With the Stars co-host revealed that she and Peloton instructor Adrian Williams had broken up, she posted a series of photos and cryptic messages to social media, putting her feelings center stage.

In a recent development, “The highest currency is not attention," read one image reshared to Hough's Instagram Story Nov. 25, "it's trust.".

Earlier, the 38-year-old posted another text-only post to her Stories that mentioned, “May you attract someone who pours as much love and care into you as you pour into them.”.

It has been revealed that the Emmy winner also shared a photo of herself holding her dog Sonny among the pointed messages—the same photo Williams had shared to his Instagram Stories Nov. 22 when he’d soft rolled out their romance on social media. Hough had reshared the photo to her own account at the time, with a source confirming their relationship to E! News the following day.

Industry observers note that amid the breakup, Hough—who finalized her divorce from Brooks Laich in 2022—was also busy with the Nov. 24 finale of Dancing With the Stars, sharing a photo of herself and Sonny in a bathroom mirror to her Stories that same night with the caption, “Never have the words ‘The show must go on’ Rang more true than today! Grateful for all of you incredible humans that love and show up for your people.”.

As for Williams, while the fitness pro has yet to publicly comment on his brief romance with the dancer, he appears to be sticking to his own routine, keeping his focus on his fitness and his own pup, Frankie. The 42-year-old posted a photo of the dachshund during a walk to his Instagram Stories Nov. 25.

And when his split from Hough was confirmed, Williams shared his own cryptic message, reposting a photo from his motivational account that included a picture of him in his car with a text-only photo that read, “Your reset doesn’t have to make sense to anyone else. Mine has three pedals.”.
