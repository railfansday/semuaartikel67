Derek McInnes is not open to giving Oscar Cortes a path back to the Rangers first team this season, sources have told Football Insider.

Cortes, 22, has only made 21 senior appearances for Rangers since initially joining on loan from Lens in February 2024.

He has been deemed a flop since a £4.5million permanent switch in July 2025, undergoing loan spells at Sporting Gijon and Huracan in the last 12 months.

The pacy winger has impressed with the latter in 2026, registering nine goal contributions in 34 games to earn a surprise senior Colombia call-up in the recent international break.

Huracan can not afford to trigger his option to buy, but as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, McInnes is also not planning to welcome him back into the Gers' first team fold.

He will remain on the periphery of their squad upon returning in the upcoming year, with Rangers planning for a permanent sale.

Cortes' last Gers appearance came back in August 2025 in a cup win over Alloa Athletic, and it is still likely to be his last in blue and white.

The 22-year-old has impressed in Argentina but McInnes does not want to upset his existing wingers by adding him into the squad midway through the campaign.

Sources indicate that speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I can’t imagine him breaking into Derek McInnes’ prepares right now. McInnes has got a settled side, they’re quite well stocked in the wide position as well, so I can’t see where Cortes would break into the side anytime soon.

"With the player not figuring in those plans and being so far down the pecking order out wide, I think Rangers will look to try and see if they can move him on in January, ideally permanently so it could boost their transfer coffers.".

Huracan hold an option to purchase 50 per cent of Cortes' rights, with reports putting the latest figure at around £1.6million, but do not have the funds available to go through with the deal.

With a full Rangers return deemed highly unlikely, the 22-year-old will already be looking toward other options before January comes around.

According to reports in Bulgaria, relayed by the Scottish Sun, 31-time title winners CSKA Sofia are keen on loaning Cortes in for the second half of the campaign.

Sofia sit top of the Bulgarian First Professional League as it stands, but are winless from their opening three Conference League games.
