📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Woot has dropped the price of the JBL PartyBox 330 to $479.95 for Black Friday, bringing it to its lowest price to date. For comparison, the same speaker right now costs $499.95 on Amazon's Black Friday sale, though that purchase includes a 90-day free trial of Amazon Music. Woot, meanwhile, offers free shipping for Prime members ($6 otherwise), and covers the new unit with a 90-day limited warranty.

Beyond the discount, the PartyBox 330 has plenty to offer if you're looking for a speaker that can handle everything from backyard barbecues to larger gatherings—sitting between the smaller Club 120 and the larger PartyBox 520 in JBL's lineup, it delivers deep, punchy bass and enough volume to entertain a crowd, while its roughly 20-hour battery life means you can keep the music going all day without having to find an outlet. It's also rated IPX4 for water resistance, so a little rain or an accidental splash shouldn't be a problem.

According to recent reports, that protection has limits, though—the 330 isn’t dustproof or particularly impact-resistant, so you’ll still want to be careful where you set it down. Audio-wise, sound quality also changes depending on where you're standing, with music becoming noticeably more muffled from the sides and behind. Fortunately, JBL's graphic equalizer and sound presets give you some control over the tuning. Its two microphone inputs and a guitar input also make it suitable for karaoke nights or live performances. You also get Bluetooth, USB-C audio, and an optical input for connecting it to a TV, along with Auracast support if you want to link it with other compatible JBL speakers for a larger setup.
