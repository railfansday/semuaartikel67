France is dealing with water insecurity – a new study shows it's a global problem

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (64 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, tens of thousands of people in France are living under water restrictions as the country deals with a drought despite recent rains, according to the environment ministry. But this is a global problem, as a newly introduced study released Wednesday revealed "the largest global decline in surface water resources recorded" since 1991, when data collection began.
