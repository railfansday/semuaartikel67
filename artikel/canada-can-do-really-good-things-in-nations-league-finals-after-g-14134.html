Mauro Biello is confident that Canada can "do really good things" after they qualified for the CONCACAF Nations League Finals with a 4-1 victory over Guatemala on Monday.

Canada took a 3-2 lead into the second leg at BMO Field but were without Alphonso Davies, who suffered a muscular issue in the opening match and joined the likes of Cyle Larin, Stephen Eustaquio and Ismael Kone on the injury list.

Despite a strong start from the hosts, Oscar Santis drew Guatemala level on aggregate when he highlighted great composure on the edge of the six-yard box in the 34th minute.

But Jonathan David equalised from the spot five minutes later and Tani Oluwaseyi made it two on the stroke of half-time, putting Canada back in control of the tie.

Tajon Buchanan scored his first international goal since last year's Gold Cup after the restart and Oluwaseyi nodded in his second before the hour mark to complete a 7-3 aggregate victory.

Biello, who stood in for the suspended head coach Jesse Marsch, suggested Canada's injury problems could help spur them to a first trophy since winning the 2000 Gold Cup by increasing the competition in the squad.

Sources indicate that "These are tough games. It was a tough game also in Guatemala, and you saw it here: you're in total control of the game, and then it's 1-0 to them," said Biello.

It has been revealed that "Getting these injuries is not fun. You're losing key guys, but at the same time, we're expanding this pool of players.

"Guys are getting opportunities, and guys are taking their moments.

It has been revealed that "It's a good team, right? If we're able to get these guys all revving their engines, we're going to be able to do really good things.".
