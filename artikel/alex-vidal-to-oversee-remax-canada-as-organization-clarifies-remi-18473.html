Not only is Alex Vidal the new president of REMAX, but the company has clarified that his appointment to this position also encompasses REMAX Canada, making him the leader of REMAX’s Canadian operation.

Vidal was named president of REMAX in September, after The Real Brokerage completed its acquisition of REMAX, forming the Real REMAX Group.

In an emailed statement, a REMAX spokesperson told HousingWire that the president of REMAX has always overseen the manufacturer’s Canadian operation.

As Vidal moves into the Canadian leadership position, Don Kottick, who has served as the president of REMAX Canada since April 2025, has announced plans to leave the firm on Dec. 15. This information was conveyed to REMAX brokers and franchisees via an internal memo reviewed and confirmed by HousingWire.

“This move helps ensure every REMAX region around the world receives as much of the same focus, momentum and opportunities as possible,” the internal memo stated. “The next few months will be pivotal as we roll out new technology and invest in further strengthening the REMAX brand in Canada and around the world.”.

Prior to joining REMAX, Kottick was the president and CEO of Sotheby’s International Realty Canada.

In a recent development, before joining REMAX, Vidal served as the president of ERA Real Estate, helping lead the brand through Compass International Holdings’s acquisition of its parent firm Anywhere Real Estate. Compass and ERA are at the moment suing Vidal, claiming that he violated non-compete agreements through his move to REMAX. In the legal complaint filed in mid-October, Compass claims that the non-compete agreement prevents Vidal from serving as president or providing service to any competing business for a period of one year from his departure from ERA.
