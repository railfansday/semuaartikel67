Russian nuclear satellites strategy 'foolhardy', expert says

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (82 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, NATO chief Mark Rutte remarked Monday the alliance would defend against threats in space when asked about German media reports that Russia may be preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite. Speaking with FRANCE 24's Jennie Shin, Dafydd Townley, Senior Teaching Fellow in International Security at the University of Portsmouth, notes that the alleged plan would be "foolhardy because the impact would be indiscriminate, they wouldn't be able to choose only Western or non-Russian satellites".
