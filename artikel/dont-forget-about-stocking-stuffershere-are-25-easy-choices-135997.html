It’s true that sometimes the appetizer is just as good as (or even better than) the entrée, and that certainly applies to holiday gifts. What the heck am I talking about? Stocking stuffers. Those bite-size delights you open up before the main course (those bigger boxes under the tree), if you will. And if you do it right, a good stocking stuffer can be the best surprise of the holiday season, so put in a little effort. Don’t just throw in a keychain and magnet and call it a day.

What makes a good stocking stuffer, you ask? Well, considering these gifts should be small enough to, you know, fit in a stocking (whether that’s what you’re really using them for or not), anything bigger than a water bottle is out of the question. But that leaves you with tons of fun options—think tiny tech gadgets, stylish accessories, skincare upgrades, and little luxuries that punch well above their weight. We rounded up the 25 best small gifts with big appeal that everyone will be excited to find in their stocking this holiday season.
