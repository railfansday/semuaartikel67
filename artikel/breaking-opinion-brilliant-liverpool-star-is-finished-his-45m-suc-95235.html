Andoni Iraola still has several positions among his ranks that he needs to upgrade.

This summer, the Spaniard and Richard Hughes spent around £150 million trying to bolster the Reds' ranks as much as they could.

Obviously, this didn't work out how they'd have wanted it to with some fans left questioning whether the moves that they've made were worthwhile or not.

Some players have more than made their mark but others have failed.

The ones who failed are likely to be replaced with Milos Kerkez now one of the many players on Iraola's chopping block.

Daniel Svensson is coming to take Milos Kerkez's role.

Left-back has been a sore spot for Liverpool recently.

The Anfield outfit have failed to have one of their players put in a decent performance at left-back with fans demanding that a new signing is made.

Alejandro Balde is the most likely candidate at the moment with some murmurings of Lewis Hall also being considered by Liverpool.

At the moment, Kerkez is sharing the responsibility with Kostas Tsimikas and, even though the Greek scouser is supposed to be the more experienced player of the two, he's somehow performed even worse than the Hungarian.

Each time Kerkez or Tsimikas features for the Reds, fans demand that the other is brought on due to how poorly they've been performing.

That's why these recent links with Daniel Svensson are something that the Reds' fans should take seriously.

"According to information from fussball-news.de, Liverpool and Arsenal from the Premier League are showing the greatest interest, while Manchester United and Leeds United are also keeping a close eye on the player," they wrote.

"It is remarked that the scouting team of the 'Red Devils' observed him in the Champions League game against Real Betis and produced a report with the rating 'transferable/signable'".

"According to information from fussball-news.de, the management of Borussia Dortmund is still committed to keeping Svensson – who has a long-term contract running until June 2029 – and does not want to let him leave.

"Whether the club completely closes its doors depends on the conditions put on the table. While the club doesn't consider the player untouchable, it is reluctant to let him go unless suitable conditions are met and plans to strengthen its commitment through a salary increase.

Sources indicate that "Borussia Dortmund are hoping for at least €45 million if a sale goes through. Should competition intensify, the total package, including add-ons, could potentially reach €50 million, with performance-related bonuses and sell-on clauses likely to be decisive factors in any deal.

Industry observers note that "Premier League clubs are reportedly prepared to test his situation with a €30 million offer for the Swedish player. However, the player is noted to be happy in Dortmund and not planning a hasty move.".

Milos Kerkez needs to get back to his best soon.

Kerkez may have been in "brilliant" form for Hungary recently but, that has not been replicated at Anfield.

Every time he features, it feels like he makes a mistake that ends up costing Liverpool the game.

Whether it's a misplaced pass, a mis-timed tackle or even something just as simple as a poor clearance, he's always doing something to mess things up for the Anfield outfit.

That's why, even though he may feel untouchable in his current role, he needs to step things back up.

If he doesn't manage to do this, it's clear that he's going to be finished at Anfield.

Indeed, he's still only 23 years old so there's still a lot of his career left to go but, right now, it doesn't really feel like his future is all that bright, despite the £40 million fee that Liverpool paid for him last summer.
