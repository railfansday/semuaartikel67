Recent findings suggest that russell Crowe is one of the biggest movie stars in the world, and while many would argue that his A-list days are behind him, he still has plenty to give the industry. Last year, Crowe finally brought his passion project, Nuremberg, to the massive screen before it was added to Netflix at the start of 2026. Crowe is many years removed from his years as an Oscar contender, but back at the start of the 2000s, one could make the case that he was the hottest actor on the planet. In 2000, Crowe earned his first-ever Oscar nomination for his performance opposite Al Pacino in The Insider, and while he didn’t win the award, he followed it with a win one year later for his earth-shattering performance as Maximus in Gladiator. He was also nominated the following year for A Beautiful Mind.

According to recent reports, in the lead-up to Nuremberg last year, when Russell Crowe was on the film festival tour promoting the film before its November release, there was buzz that he was going to be awarded his first Oscar nod in over 20 years. This ultimately didn’t work out, but he has another shot this year with a sleeper contender thanks to his commanding performance in The Weight, the breathtaking Western co-starring Ethan Hawke. After grossing only a few million at the box office, The Weight is now available to rent or purchase on VOD platforms like Prime Video, where it has quietly become one of the most-watched titles in the world. The film was directed by Padraic McKinley, who has also been tapped to direct the fresh Sicario movie that’s being written by Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders).

Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey was recently added to VOD on Prime Video after a record-breaking run at the box office, and it has swiftly risen to the #1 spot on streaming charts. Brad Pitt’s latest blockbuster directed by David Ayer, Heart of the Beast, is also among the most-watched titles on VOD. As for traditional streaming charts which don’t require an additional rental or purchase, the new season of The Chosen is the most popular show to watch on Prime Video. To reach the #1 spot, it had to overthrow The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power.

Check out The Weight on Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Russell Crowe’s future projects.
