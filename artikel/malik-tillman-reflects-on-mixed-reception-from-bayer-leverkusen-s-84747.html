Malik Tillman reflects on mixed reception from Bayer Leverkusen supporters

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that bayer Leverkusen midfielder Malik Tillman has admitted that recent criticism from the club’s supporters has diminished his appreciation of their applause. Speaking after Saturday’s 4-0 Bundesliga victory over SC Paderborn, the 24-year-old former Germany U21 and current USMNT international offered a remarkably candid assessment of his relationship with the Leverkusen faithful despite producing his best performance of the season.

Recent findings suggest that tillman assisted Afonso Moreira’s opening goal with an excellent pass and impressed with several further attacking contributions. When head coach Carles Martínez substituted the midfielder in the 67th minute, the BayArena crowd responded with enthusiastic applause and chants of his name.

Industry observers note that the reception contrasted sharply with Leverkusen’s recent 4-2 home defeat against VfB Stuttgart, when Tillman was greeted by boos upon entering the pitch as a substitute. Rather than welcome the change in attitude, however, the former Bayern Munich academy player suggested that such reactions no longer carried much significance for him.

Get German Football News previously reported on Tillman’s frustrations with Leverkusen supporters following a revealing interview with the Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger in August. The American acknowledged that criticism of his performances was sometimes justified, but emphasized that some reactions had become excessively personal. Tillman also discussed difficulties with former coach Kasper Hjulmand and expressed disappointment over the support he had received from the club’s management.

The midfielder arrived from PSV Eindhoven in the summer of 2025 for approximately €35 million. The considerable expectations accompanying his arrival have frequently proven difficult to fulfill. Our detailed preseason assessment of Leverkusen highlighted Tillman’s difficulties establishing himself in Martínez’s plans amid increased competition for attacking positions.

It has been revealed that saturday’s performance nevertheless offered fresh encouragement. Tillman demonstrated the technical quality and creativity that persuaded Leverkusen to invest heavily in his services. Whether the latest display will help repair his relationship with sections of the club’s support remains another matter.

According to recent reports, “I have confidence in myself, but playing a good game naturally strengthens that confidence even further,” Tillman explained after Saturday’s match, as reported by Kicker. “I have to keep going here, no matter what happens. But I’m happy with my performance.”.

Asked how he felt about receiving applause after previously being booed, the American offered a notably different response.

“It’s difficult to say at this point,” Tillman admitted. “I don’t really care anymore. I don’t know. Of course, it sucks when you get booed, and at the end of the day, it doesn’t really mean much to me anymore when people cheer or shout my name.”.
