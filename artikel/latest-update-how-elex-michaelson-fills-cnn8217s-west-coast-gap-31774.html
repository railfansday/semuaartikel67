📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Elex Michaelson got a firsthand look at CNN journalists working a breaking news situation during the White House Correspondents’ Dinner in April.

Michaelson was one of the network’s guests that evening at the Washington Hilton, in Washington, D.C. He described being there as a “wonderful experience,” but it took a sharp turn when shots rang out within the building, and the president was promptly evacuated.

“The shooting itself was a very strange experience, and to see everybody get to work in their tuxes and trying to figure out what happened was also something I’ll never forget,” stated Michaelson.

The Los Angeles-based anchor of CNN’s late-night program, The Story Is, airing from 12 to 2 a.m. ET, Michaelson is no stranger to handling breaking news situations. He broke two major international news stories this year: he was the first to report when the US undertook military strikes and captured Nicolás Maduro in Venezuela in January, and he was also the first to report on the Iran bombings in March, both of which, coincidentally, happened around the same time on a Friday night at around 10:30 p.m. PT/1:30 a.m. ET. This past Friday, he was on the air for four hours as part of CNN’s continuous coverage of the FAA issuing a ground stop for all the planes across the entire country.

Speaking to TVNewser, Michaelson noted, “CNN does a lot of things well, but I think it does breaking news best of all.”.

For the Venezuelan military strikes, Michaelson recalls how they were covering a pop culture story when a producer told him over his earpiece that there were explosions in Caracas. In that moment, he realized who he was working for.

“We stated goodbye to the people, to a quick commercial break reset, and then we were off, and it was a reminder of just the global power of CNN.”.

It has been revealed that michaelson joined CNN in September 2025, bringing his West Coast sensibilities to the network. Before that, he was an anchor for the LA-based Fox affiliate KTTV 11 and earlier worked as a reporter at KABC-TV in Los Angeles and as a weekend morning anchor and weeknight reporter for XETV in San Diego (when the Tijuana-based station had English-language operations and a news department there). This past October, he and his team celebrated the first anniversary of The Story Is, which he describes as serving a niche that didn’t exist before: an audience in the western part of the U.S. that didn’t have a live news program airing during primetime hours in that region.

“I think we’ve filled that gap,” he mentioned. Adding, “I think we’ve also brought some fresh voices that you don’t see on cable news all day long that have a lot to say and are a valuable addition to the conversation.”.

Recent findings suggest that in its first year, according to CNN, The Story Is with Elex Michaelson has seen its total viewer audience grow by 15% since launch, compared with the six-month pre-launch period. It also led cable news among adults 25–54 when news broke of U.S. strikes on Venezuela on January 2, 2026 (1:45 – 2 a.m. ET). The show now attracts nearly one in four viewers in the Adults 25–54 demo in its Pacific time slot, up from one in five before launch, and is No. 1 in cable news among Hispanic viewers in its time slot, both in total viewers and the demo.

It has been revealed that the Story Is goes beyond politics, covering the news with a West Coast, laid-back feel. Michaelson said CNN president Mark Thompson described the program as “the most upbeat show on CNN,” and the anchor pointed out that during those hours they have had thoughtful entertainment conversations, profiled athletes involved in their communities, and shared other community service stories.

“I love politics, but I also am particularly proud of some of these profile stories that we’ve done with celebrities,” Michaelson mentioned. “I really love the sit-down we did with William Shatner and his daughter, both of whom had just battled stage four cancer and survived it.”.

Recent findings suggest that he touted the advantage of anchoring out of CNN’s LA bureau, which lets him showcase those types of stories. “If I was in Washington, you’re competing with like a thousand other reporters doing the same thing, on Capitol Hill, for example, but in this space there is more room to do something unique, which has been fun.”.

It has been revealed that as for the show’s first anniversary, amid the busy news cycle, Michaelson brought in pizza and toasted with champagne after the show to mark the occasion. He hopes to have a proper celebration in the near future now that the midterm elections have concluded.
