Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are married! The couple made things official on Saturday, November 21, after around four years of dating, and People published photos of the intimate moment—featuring Miley’s beautiful gown. The Grammy winner wore a long-sleeved white dress with a boat neck, low back, and train for her Palm Springs ceremony. No word yet as to the designer, but Miley posted photos of a closer look and it’s stunning.

It has been revealed that these two got together back in November 2021 and have been going strong ever since. Maxx even collaborated with Miley on her album, Endless Summer Vacation. She opened up about their meet-cute to Vogue UK, saying, “We got put on a blind date. Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, The worst that can happen is I leave.”.

A couple years later, Miley told The Cut that her mom had previously always encouraged her “to stay with the wrong guy because they’re hot” and said “No, I ended up with a person who means a lot to me and treats me really well and respects me. I had to learn that the hard way because my mommy taught me the wrong way, and then I had to learn the right way by myself.”.

In a recent development, the pair got engaged last year in December. So happy for them!
