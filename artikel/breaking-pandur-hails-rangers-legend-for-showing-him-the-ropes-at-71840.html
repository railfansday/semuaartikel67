Rangers goalkeeper Ivor Pandur has revealed his 'unbelievable' experience of working under Allan McGregor.

The former Rangers, Hull and Scotland keeper was brought into the Ibrox backroom staff by Derek McInnes as lead goalkeeping coach in the summer.

Pandur is reaping the benefits of his mentor’s experience and their relationship having kept eight clean sheets during his team’s 12-match unbeaten run.

'It’s unbelievable,' said the Croatian ahead of the Premiership encounter at Motherwell on Saturday night.

'With the career that he had here, he obviously has a lot of experience.

Recent findings suggest that 'We have a great relationship where he is my coach, he is my friend. So he’ll tell me when I ask him if I can do something better.

Ivor Pandur has made an impressive start to life at Rangers after joining from Hull City.

Recent findings suggest that 'We are here to make each other better. If I can make him a better coach, that’s brilliant. But he’s making me a better goalkeeper and I’m really, really thankful for that.

'Training, first of all, is really, really enjoyable. And he always asks, "What do I want to do? What do I want to improve?". And he’ll give me feedback.

Sources indicate that 'There’s not a single thing, I would say, that made me better. Just in general, to be able to work in these kind of circumstances and conditions, it’s really enjoyable and it’s making me happy every day.

Sources indicate that 'It’s making me excited to come to work. And that’s because of him also.

'He’s a great guy and he was a great goalkeeper, and it’s enough for me to learn from.'.

Manager McInnes this week described Pandur as a key 'driver' of team culture off the pitch, citing the respect he has from his team-mates.

Pandur, who joined from Hull in the summer, mentioned: 'I always want to lead by example. The words are not so important if you’re not doing your job right.

'So if I can lead by example and show up every day, work as hard as I can, a lot of young players can see that.

Pandur insists he is only going to keep improving under the guidance of Allan McGregor.

Sources indicate that 'And obviously, I’m always there to help somebody. I was also 19 or 20 and, when I had somebody to learn from, it was much easier.

'Because you get experiences through life and through years of playing, I always try to help everybody else.

'So there are times where I can be more vocal if I feel a need for it, but it’s never my first objective. It’s always to lead by example.'.

Pandur is still awaiting his first international cap but was again away with Croatia this month and he feels meeting the demands of Rangers fans is getting the best out of him.

It has been revealed that 'It keeps you on your toes,” the former Rijeka, Hellas Verona and Fortuna Sittard player noted. 'But in my opinion, pressure is in your head.

Sources indicate that 'We all play the game we love and we try to do our best. Some days you’ll do good, some days you’ll do bad. We always try to do the right thing.

It has been revealed that 'Pressure is bigger here, there’s more followers. But it can only make you better because it gets everything out of you.'.
