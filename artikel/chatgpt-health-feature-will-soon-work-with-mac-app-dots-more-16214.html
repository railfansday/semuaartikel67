📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that openAI relaunched its ChatGPT Health feature over the summer after piloting earlier in the year. Next, ChatGPT Health is set to expand to more platforms, including the Mac app and more.

In a recent development, with user permission, ChatGPT Health connects to Apple Health and supported medical records to provide useful context to health-related questions. The feature currently works in the United States on iPhone, iPad, and the web.

However, a recent notice from OpenAI points to ChatGPT Health coming to many more platforms.

In a recent development, for instance, ChatGPT Health will soon work with the desktop version of ChatGPT for the first time. This would bring ChatGPT Health to the Mac app.

OpenAI also claims ChatGPT Health will work with Android, ChatGPT Sites, and ChatGPT Dots, the company’s proactive assistant feature.

The move follows a similar pattern with the rollout of ChatGPT Finances, which was originally limited to mobile and the web before recently reaching the Mac app as well.

No official timing yet for when ChatGPT Health will expand, but OpenAI notified users about the upcoming expansion earlier this month.
