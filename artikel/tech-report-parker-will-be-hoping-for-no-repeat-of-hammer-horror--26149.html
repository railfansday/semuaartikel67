In this, the second week of the second international break of the season, there’s an understandable eagerness amongst fans to get back to the serious business of league football again this coming weekend.

However there is still a crucial fixture tonight, before the autumn internationals are concluded, in which West Ham supporters should take an interest.

Greece against Serbia in the UEFA Nations League Group A hardly sets the pulse racing as a neutral: It’s normally a game I could happily ignore but it takes on a special meaning after West Ham’s Dinos Mavropanos performed so poorly in the crushing 7 – 0 defeat by the Netherlands last time out.

Consequently I will be taking a bit of an interest in this somewhat obscure game to see how West Ham’s number 15 is looking after such a demoralising result three days ago,.

West Ham need Mavropanos to return having put the 7 – 0 thrashing firmly behind him.

West Ham’s main man took a bit of a pasting in the Greek media after the game, in what was described as the ‘blackest night‘ for Greek football.

West Ham, already without Maximilian Kilman, remember, banned for the upcoming Saturday tie against Preston North End, will be keeping everything crossed that their one remaining senior centre-back has a solid evening facing up to the likes of Mitrovic, Jovic and co.

Scott Parker already has a selection dilemma over bringing in teenager Airidas Golambeckis or loanee Morato to replace Kilman.

The last thing he or West Ham need is another shaky Mavropanos performance tonight and a return to the ‘Mavropanic’ days of the past – which another heavy defeat could usher back in.
