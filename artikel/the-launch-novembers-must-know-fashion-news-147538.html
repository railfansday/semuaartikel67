The Launch: November’s Must-Know Fashion News

Fall is in full swing, and with it, so is the fashion industry. As the leaves continue to turn and preparations for the holiday season get serious, brands are finding fresh ways to celebrate with exciting new capsules, collaborations, and stocking-worthy releases. Look no further than J.J. Martin, whose new book, Table Energy, explores the art of Italian entertaining, and the Rimowa x Maison Margiela collab, which turns travel into something even more desirable. Plus, there’s plenty of reason to get out and shop, with the likes of L/Uniform, Danielle Frankel, and Bulgari all opening new storefronts. Below, all the fashion news to know this month.

What: Fall/winter 2026 collection.

Where: Online at moncler.com, rickowens.eu, select Moncler stores, and Rick Owens flagship stores.

Why: Moncler and Rick Owens return to their shared winter universe with a collection that makes outerwear the main event. Quilted jackets arrive in oversized shapes, punctuated by metal studs, utility pockets, and a strap that lets you carry them when the layers come off. The palette stays pared back—black, silver-toned Palladium, Cloud Grey, and Grease—with matching boots and sunglasses to finish the look. In a black-and-white campaign photographed by Owens, screen legend Joe Dallesandro, known for his turn in Andy Warhol’s Superstars, brings his own countercultural history to the collection. “Little Joe mythologized an indifferent beauty in Lou Reed’s hymn to queer subculture,” Owens stated in a statement. “‘Take a Walk on the Wild Side’ was a turning point for me and a lot of other suppressed kids of my generation. his inclusion in Avedon’s photos of Warhol's entourage illustrated the magic of finding one’s tribe, for better or worse.”.

Where: Online at gap.com and in select Gap stores.

It has been revealed that why: Zac Posen is keeping things simple this winter season—and the latest GapStudio wardrobe is edited to just 12 pieces. The designer’s capsule gives familiar staples a sharper feel through sleek silhouettes, precise tailoring, and touches of unexpected texture like a playful teddy bear coat rendered in a cozy caramel fabric. Other highlights include elevated essentials that Posen has prioritized at the brand: a turtleneck maxi dress, a sleek poplin shirt, and high-rise seamed stirrup jeans. “For winter 2026, we wanted to move beyond traditional occasion wear with versatile pieces designed to be worn from day to night,” Posen remarked of the collection, which came to life on singer EJAE in the accompanying campaign, in a statement. “There’s still that level of drama with thoughtful tailoring and exaggerated lines, but it’s balanced by a focused palette that feels elegant and wearable.”.

Where: 31 East 72nd Street, New York.

Why: Parisian leather goods label L/Uniform is making its Fresh York debut with a flagship on the Upper East Side. Following boutiques in Paris and Tokyo, the store marks the brand’s first American address with founder Jeanne Signoles working with architect Jakob Sprenger to create a space that balances the polish of a boutique with the practical spirit at the heart of the label. “Opening a store in New York was a priority for me from the very beginning,” Signoles says. “I always felt that L/Uniform naturally belonged here, with the city’s energy, its independence, and its very strong connection to everyday life. Our bags are made to be used—to work, to travel, to accompany daily life.”.

L/Uniform Nº146 The Cross Body Bag.

L/Uniform Nº131 The Mini Press Bag.

Where: 6 Harrison Street, New York.

Sources indicate that why: A historic Tribeca landmark is the setting for Danielle Frankel’s upcoming flagship. At 6 Harrison Street, the bridal house has created a 12,000-square-foot space where original architectural character meets a considered, contemporary interior by Augusta Hoffman Studio. Private rooms make space for a more personal appointment, while the wider setting invites visitors to explore the collection at ease.

Danielle Frankel Chantilly Lace Degradé Border Drop Veil.

What: High jewelry collection and latest boutique.

Where: 237 Worth Avenue, Palm Beach, Florida.

Why: A 1920s Palm Beach estate is a fitting setting for Bulgari’s first U.S. presentation of “Eclettica.” Designed by Addison Mizner, the waterfront home hosted a gala that also marked the opening of the house’s new boutique. Venus Williams, Chanel Iman, and Becca Bloom were among the guests, with Jewel performing. The collection includes 15 transformable designs—the most Bulgari has presented in a single collection—alongside more than 50 high jewelry pieces. With its Roman roots and sculptural approach to high jewelry, Bulgari’s “Eclettica” brings a distinctly Italian point of view to Palm Beach.

Who: Rimowa and Maison Margiela.

What: Limited-edition collection.

Where: Online at rimowa.com and maisonmargiela.com and select Rimowa and Maison Margiela stores.

It has been revealed that why: Some things look better after they’ve been lived in. That idea is at the heart of Rimowa and Maison Margiela’s fresh travel collaboration, which brings together two signatures: the German luggage house’s familiar grooves and Margiela’s hand-painted white Bianchetto finish. Designed to change with use, the suitcase and matching bag will take on marks and patina as they travel. A campaign by Dexter Lander brings that idea to life in a Haussmannian apartment, where the white paint seems to spill beyond the pieces themselves.

Rimowa x Maison Margiela 5AC XL Bag.

Rimowa x Maison Margiela Classic Cabin.

What: Bird on a Rock installation, window displays, and holiday décor.

Where: The Landmark; 727 5th Avenue, New York.

Why: Tiffany & Co. has switched on the holiday season at The Landmark, with Amanda Seyfried leading the way. The actress lit the flagship’s Bird on a Rock installation by placing an ornament on its Blue Box tree, marking the third year of the display and a full-circle moment. Seyfried stars in the house’s newly introduced holiday film, “You & Me,” which travels between present-day New York and a memory from the ’90s. Mikey Madison, Jordyn Woods, and Louisa Jacobson joined the celebration.

In a recent development, why: Just in time for the holidays, La DoubleJ founder J.J. Martin has released Table Energy, a newly introduced book about the art of Italian entertaining, with a foreword by fashion writer Leandra Medine Cohen. Its pages move from alfresco lunches to candlelit dinners, all in the colorful, convivial spirit of La DoubleJ. The book made its debut at the brand’s Upper East Side boutique, The Lighthouse, where Martin, ELLE Decor’s head of editorial content Elisa Lipsky-Karasz, and ELLE editor-in-chief Nina Garcia gathered to celebrate l’art de la table.

What: Limited-edition collection.

Where: Online at tecovas.com, aninebing.com, and select retail locations.

In a recent development, why: Anine Bing has a newly introduced way to kick up a look. The designer has teamed up with Tecovas on a capsule that reworks the label’s Loretta and Jamie boots with star embroidery and polished goat leather. Cowboy hats and belts round out the lineup, which launches alongside Anine Bing’s spring/summer 2027 collection.
