Tomorrow at 9:00 PM, PSG and Roma will battle it out at the Parc des Princes for Matchday 5 of the Champions League League Phase.

The two best attacks in Europe will face off, having scored just under 50 goals in just three months of the season between the league and the Champions League. In addition to Luis Enrique, right-back Achraf Hakimi also spoke at a pre-match press conference.

“Wesley? I think he’s a very attacking player who likes to attack. Roma’s qualities help him express his game. We’ll all have to be focused, not just on Wesley, but on all the Roma players, who are going through a good period.”.

“I think Roma are going through a good period, especially in Serie A. We have to stay very focused. We know Roma’s strengths and weaknesses, and as I said, we have to play our game without hesitation, maintaining the same mentality as in recent games: going out and winning and getting the three points.”.
