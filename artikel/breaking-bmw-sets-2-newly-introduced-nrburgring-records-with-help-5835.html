📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that pirelli’s P Zero Trofeo RS is one of its most extreme performance tires, one capable of helping performance cars extract the most out of their potential. And that’s especially true when the organization works with automakers directly—as the Italian rubbermaker did with BMW, developed a bespoke version specifically for the BMW M range. The fresh semi-slick tire remains street legal, but doesn’t sacrifice at-the-limit grip. And it helped BMW set two new ‘Ring lap records with its BMW M2 with the M Performance Track Kit and BMW M3 CS Touring.

Industry observers note that the BMW M2 with the M Performance Track Kit managed a lap time of 7:25.068minutes. This lowered the Nürburgring Nordschleife compact car record, previously held by the M2 CS, by 0.5 seconds. The long-roof BMW M3, however, made more significant history: BMW’s M3 CS Touring lapped the 12.9-mile racetrack in 7:29.49 minutes, setting a upcoming record for the fastest touring car ever by shaving off more than five seconds. The previous touring car lap record of 7:35.06 was held by the standard BMW M3 Touring. A combination of increased horsepower, stronger brakes, and custom rubber—as well as BMW M Development engineer Jörg Weidinger behind the wheel—pushed the wagon to success.

“The bespoke development program is the result of close collaboration between Pirelli and BMW M’s R&D teams. Thanks to advanced virtual development techniques, the two teams were able to adapt the tire’s characteristics to the different requirements from the earliest stages of the project, in line with Pirelli’s Perfect Fit approach, which aims to create products tailored to each specific model or application,” the tire maker said in a statement.

Industry observers note that pirelli initially tested the tires alongside BMW at the Nürburgring Nordschleife, and the tires were also later tested at the Nardò test circuit in Italy. BMW and Pirelli have collaborated on over 30 bespoke tire projects across the P Zero family over the years, and like many others, a keen eye can tell which ones are made for Bimmers; the manufacturer-specific tires feature a BMW star marking, as a nod to the integrated development process.
