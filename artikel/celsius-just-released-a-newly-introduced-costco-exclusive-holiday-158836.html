Recent findings suggest that between end-of-year deadlines, holiday shopping, and family obligations, caffeine is doing a lot of heavy lifting right now. If you’re relying on a little extra boost to make it through the next few weeks, fortunately, Celsius has paired two seasonal flavors together in a pack worth adding to your grocery list. The only problem is you might just need a Costco membership to get your hands on it.

It has been revealed that the energy drink brand has released Sparkling Cranberry Apple exclusively at Costco as part of its limited-edition Cheers Variety Pack. The 18-count box contains nine cans of the upcoming flavor and nine of the returning Spritz Vibe Winter Edition, which takes its inspiration from a Shirley Temple.

According to recent reports, according to Celsius, Cranberry Apple combines the tartness of cranberries with the crisp sweetness of apples, which sounds particularly appropriate for this time of year. Its partner-in-pack, Spritz Vibe Winter Edition, unites the cherry and citrus flavors of the fizzy, grenadine-spiked classic. Both come in 12-ounce, zero-sugar cans containing 200 milligrams of caffeine.

Celsius confirmed the exclusive on Instagram on Wednesday, November 25, with the caption, “making room for cran apple at the thanksgiving table 🙌🍂 shop exclusively at @costco,” and the comments promptly turned into a series of increasingly eager requests.

“I NEED THE SPARKLING CRAN APPLE IN MY MOUTH NOW,” one person declared. “Please release that cran apple EVERYWHERE!!!!!” another begged.

It has been revealed that the Costco exclusivity was proving especially painful for a few shoppers. “Please release on it's own , I'm a Sam's Club girl 😭,” one commenter wrote, while someone else summed up their predicament even more dramatically: “Never been so devastated to not have a Costco.”.

In a recent development, those lucky enough to have tried it already seem particularly interested in keeping it around. “Hey so do me a favor and don’t ever get rid of these, thanks!” one fan requested, with another sharing some particularly enthusiastic praise: “the cran Apple is the only thing that is getting me through night shifts I LOVE😍”.

It has been revealed that the excitement isn't limited to Cranberry Apple, either. The returning Spritz Vibe has its own supporters, with one person demanding, "MAKE SHIRLEY TEMPLE A STAPLE FLAVOR!!".

As for all those fans demanding Cranberry Apple by the case? I have a feeling nine cans aren’t going to cut it.
