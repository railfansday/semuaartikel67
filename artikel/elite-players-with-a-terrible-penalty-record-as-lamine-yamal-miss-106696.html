Lamine Yamal missed another penalty at the weekend, meaning he has missed more than he has scored this season, but he is not the only elite player with a pretty poor record.

Yamal failed to convert from the spot when Barcelona were trailing 2-0 to Villarreal and even if his blushes were spared thanks to his teammates, it still added to his mixed bag of a penalty record.

Yamal has scored 11 and missed three, giving him a success rate of 79% and here are 10 other elite players who are not so great from the penalty spot.

Messi used to be absolutely awful at penalties. He missed four in the 2014-15 season and did so again in both of the following campaigns. No wonder he tried passing the ball rather than shooting.

Typical of Messi, he clearly identified it as a weakness and worked to improve it. He missed just one a season for the following two campaigns and is now one of the best penalty takers.

His earlier career form does though mean he still makes this list.

Griezmann would choose to have missed 100 penalties if he scored that one in Milan.

The former Atletico man missed a penalty during the Champions League final against city rivals Madrid in the San Siro, a match his team would go on to lose.

Griezmann did at least score in the penalty shootout but could have given Atletico a great chance at victory in the 90 minutes.

That was one of 14 penalties missed in his career to date.

For a player who seemed able to do pretty much whatever he wanted with the ball, Mahrez was oddly poor when it came to penalties.

He’s missed 12 in total, including four for both Leicester and Manchester City.

If you are looking for a player to have missed a penalty for a lot of different clubs, then Edin Dzeko is your man.

He’s missed spot kicks for five clubs and another for his international team, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

He only ever took one for City and missed it before Sergio Aguero resumed duties and his stint at Fenerbahce saves him from a frankly embarrassing record.

He managed to score seven for the Turkish club, missing two, which rescues him somewhat.

Ribery took eight penalties in the 2007-08 season alone but managed to miss two of them.

After that, he was never really given a first-choice run at spot kicks and only took three more for Bayern.

He did have a 100% record for France, though.

The Pro Evo 6 cheat code may have been able to blast them at will from 30 yards but it was not so easy from 12.

He missed a third of the ones he took, of which the majority came whilst he was at Inter.

Had his career ended there, then his penalty record would have been awful but he did redeem himself somewhat with nine scored and one missed for Flamengo in his latter career.

Missing more penalties than you have scored definitely means you are worth including on this list.

Sterling has failed to score six of the 10 penalties he has scored, so no wonder he was taken off penalty duty.

He scored his first-ever penalty back in the 2017-18 season but then missed the next two he took.

Sterling left City with a record of three scored and four missed but was somehow even worse for Chelsea and Arsenal. He took one for each London club and missed both of them.

A 232-goal striker should make for a prolific penalty taker, right? Wrong.

Inzaghi was surprisingly bad from spot kicks, even if he was only given a limited opportunity to take them.

The former Juventus and Milan player only took 25 in his long career and perhaps that is because he missed eight of them.

Industry observers note that most of his misses came from Milan where he scuffed three and managed to score two. No wonder then he took his final penalty nine years before the end of his career.

At one point in his career, Isak was awful at penalties.

When he left Spain for Newcastle in 2022, he had scored six but missed four and since coming to England he has improved a lot.

He missed one in the 2023-24 Premier League season and another in a UEFA Nations League match for Sweden but has scored 13 in the same time period.

His earlier work does mean he has a bad record, for now.

Vinicius Junior took over Cristiano Ronaldo’s number seven shirt but clearly did not inherit his penalty-taking ability.

In a recent development, of the 20 he has taken, Vinicius has missed six. His most recent came in the 2025/26 Champions League and since then, Kylian Mbappe has been Madrid’s first-choice penalty taker.

Ronaldo, by comparison, has a conversion record of 83%.
