Recent findings suggest that tonight, GQ hosts its annual celebration of the biggest, most impactful, and superlatively stylish Men of the Year at the Chateau Marmont in Hollywood. In 2026, of course, no great outfit—and no great man—is complete without a wristwatch. Naturally, for the liveliest menswear party of the year, attendees are bringing out their very best timepieces.

Recent findings suggest that the dress code is “modern red carpet,” and attendees have found creative ways to interpret that on their wrists. Skyler Gisondo threw down with the ultimate-classic Rolex Submariner. Michael Rider, always attuned to what’s trending in the watch world, is in a dainty Cartier Tank.
