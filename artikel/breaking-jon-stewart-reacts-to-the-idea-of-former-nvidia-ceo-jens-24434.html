It's only been a matter of days since former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after news of three affairs – including one with an alleged Chinese spy – became public. But apparently, there are already rumours of a comeback.

"A comeback? It's been a long weekend, huh?" notes Jon Stewart in the Daily Show clip above. "What'd you do, one day of soul-searching per affair?".

Stewart goes on to play clips of Trump continuing to defend Huang, with the president saying he "would not mind" if Huang returned as CEO.

Sources indicate that "Folks, the entire Jensen Huang soap opera exposes how all of these tech titans have us normies by the short and curlies," Stewart claims. "Like, they're creating the most powerful technology that the world has ever seen — that even they say, in their filing to go public, could pose an existential threat to humanity. But we are not even allowed to regulate them.".
