📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, HBO has been in the business of making instant classics for a long time now, and one of their latest additions is on track to do just that. There's no doubt that it's a tumultuous time for TV at large, and with the massive changes in terms of organization from parent companies running the show around HBO, some things are bound to fall through the cracks.

Well, despite significant changes like the Paramount Skydance takeover of brands like Warner Bros., and the fact that the DCU's future is hanging in the balance with the removal of James Gunn and Peter Safran as the co-heads of DC Studios, the Lanterns series is poised to have a long and healthy reputation as one of the best entries in the DCU.

With the incredible Kyle Chandler and Aaron Pierre investing a great deal of time and effort into crafting and refining their iterations of Hal Jordan and John Stewart respectively, it's good to see that Lanterns is likely to age like fine wine, especially when many of the other DCU projects, such as Creature Commandos, Supergirl, and Peacemaker season 2 all left audiences with questions about what the future holds.

Sources indicate that one of the greatest strengths of the Lanterns series, and one that is sure to help it continue to resonate with audiences into the future, is the fact that it's a self-contained story that is complete after eight episodes. Sure, there are some threads that could still be picked up, but the heart of the story is resolved, and audiences leave satisfied with the resolution presented by the finale.

Where many of the other DCU stories were designed to help build the wider universe and establish characters and connections that would ultimately play an important role in the overarching story, Lanterns was concerned with telling Hal and John's story.

Sources indicate that and by the end of the series, the way this relationship developed, the mysteries established early in the series, and the antagonists who created unrest and distress, were all put to rest. Ultimately, it's a buddy cop drama with a core mystery and some superhero elements that lift the story from the typical to the extraordinary.

According to recent reports, unfortunately, with Gunn's exit as the architect of the DCU, there is a high degree of uncertainty about what is next for the world he helped to shape from its latest beginning with Creature Commandos. Jeremy Latcham now stands to guide the DC Universe forward, but whether he chooses to discard many of the characters and stories that have been set up remains to be seen.

Despite all of that, Lanterns doesn't really need to do anything else to prove itself. Creature Commandos was clearly setting up a bigger story in the DCU. Superman and Supergirl both worked to establish the most powerful heroes on planet Earth, and Peacemaker season 2 bridged the gaps between the DCEU and the DCU while also setting up a larger plot for Chapter 1: Gods and Monsters.

Lanterns was less focused on building the rest of the DCU, and more concerned with creating a strong standalone story around Hal and John, which it accomplished. The focus on a character study rather than placing a piece into the DCU puzzle paid off, and regardless of what's next, it works.

In a recent development, but, there is a strong possibility that the Lanterns story will continue despite James Gunn's exit. As mentioned above, Jeremy Latcham has been assigned to reinvigorate the DCU, and to infuse some of the MCU magic he learned as an executive at Marvel Studios into this world of stories.

It's likely that Latcham will try to hold onto whatever worked in the DCU, and enhance the best parts of this universe while quietly shelving the things that no longer fit. While there is a strong possibility that the narratives that rely most on Gunn's control, such as Creature Commandos and whatever story is born out of the Peacemaker season 2 finale, Lanterns is in a very different position.

Sources indicate that the creative team that put Lanterns together was not directly controlled by Gunn. And beyond that, Lanterns has proven to be one of the most well-received entries in the DCU to date. As a result, it's very likely that Lanterns and characters like John Stewart as played by Aaron Pierre will survive into whatever the next chapter of the DCU looks like.
