How to buy and store fresh cranberries.

Look for firm, glossy cranberries and discard any that are soft, shriveled, or bruised. A few pale pink berries are nothing to worry about.

Keep unwashed cranberries in their original bag in the refrigerator for up to four weeks, though they’re best used sooner. To stock up, freeze them in their original packaging or spread clean thoroughly dried berries on a baking sheet until frozen, then transfer to a freezer bag. They’ll keep their quality for up to a year. Many cooked cranberry recipes work with frozen berries straight from the freezer; check the recipe before thawing.
