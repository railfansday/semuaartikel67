Everyone loves a holiday deal, and this week is a good chance to take advantage of one: early bird pricing ends this Friday for tickets to the 2026 GeekWire Gala.

Seattle tech’s biggest holiday celebration takes place Dec. 10. We’ve got a new venue and fresh experiences, and it’s a great chance to rub shoulders with hundreds of the region’s top founders, investors, entrepreneurs and business leaders.

The party is moving this year to The Shop, an exclusive venue in Seattle’s SoDo neighborhood featuring an extraordinary collection of classic and exotic cars that should help rev up the festivities.

In a recent development, the GeekWire Gala features food and drink, signature cocktails, games, music, dancing and more. Part reunion and part networking extravaganza, it’s a chance to turn our holiday party into your holiday party. Bring your friends, colleagues, clients, or entire team.

We’re also excited to bring back our Uncommon Thinkers program, recognizing the creative minds and unconventional builders shaping the future of the Pacific Northwest. Presented in partnership with Greater Seattle Partners, the program shines a spotlight on innovators making a difference across our region.

In partnership with Microsoft, we’ll also be raising awareness this year for six amazing nonprofits that help make our region a better place to live.

The festivities take place Thursday, Dec. 10, with the main event running from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. at The Shop at 2233 6th Ave. S. in SoDo.

Dress to impress, break out your holiday flair, or keep it geek chic. Cocktail attire is welcome, but there are no fashion rules at this party.

We’re also grateful to Greater Seattle Partners for its continued partnership on Uncommon Thinkers.
