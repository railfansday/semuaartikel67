Not only is it the resumption of hostilities in the Championship after a two week break but also the Preston North End game represents Scott Parker’s home debut as head coach: This is the start of the fresh post-Nuno era which has been eagerly awaited.

The manager himself has sent a message direct to West Ham fans, as if looking to build new bridges in the years since he last represented the club.

Not that he needs to, there’s a pretty universal approval at his appointment despite the ‘hiccup’ of the Blackburn Rovers defeat which thankfully now seems a long way distant in the rear view mirror. That was Nuno’s team anyways.

Scott Parker’s message is full of clips from his playing career – setting the stage for a substantial team performance this afternoon……..

Taking to X/Twitter, superimposed on images of Parker’s playing days and the best bits of West Ham this season, the newly introduced Manager makes his message clear:.

“I’ve experienced this club as a player and I know exactly what it means to represent West Ham United. To now be back here as Head Coach is something I’m incredibly proud of. I understand the passion of our supporters, the expectations that come from the people who love this club.

Now we have our first opportunity to come together, put in a performance our supporters can be proud of.

I can’t wait to walk out there on Saturday.

In a recent development, plea for unity or simply the latest man confirming his passion for this club – we knew that anyway. It ramps up, rather than damping down expectations which is fine: Parker gives himself a lot to live upto. No hiding place then, go on and ‘make us proud!’.
