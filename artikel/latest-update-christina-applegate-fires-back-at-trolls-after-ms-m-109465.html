Christina Applegate is clapping back at trolls who criticized her for being disappointed that she was misdiagnosed with multiple sclerosis.

“I don’t want to sit here and be like, ‘Boo-hoo-hoo me’ all the time about this whole thing because now it’s about kind of getting boots on the ground now and getting myself together,” Applegate, 54, said during the Tuesday, November 24, episode of her and Jamie Lynn Sigler’s “MeSsy” podcast. “It really does feel good to feel understood because it’s a hard thing to come out and say that I’m upset that I don’t have MS, right?”.

Addressing her critics, the Dead to Me alum continued, “A lot of people had a lot to f***ing say about it after I came out. But, you know, it’s not about that. It’s about an identity thing. … It’s about a community that I love still, as we know.”.

Applegate also pointed out that having a diagnosis allowed her to “relax” into her illness rather than constantly searching for answers about what’s wrong with her health.

“It can be incredibly stressful,” she added.

According to recent reports, applegate first unveiled her battle with what she believed to be MS in 2021. After five years of detailing her health journey and advocating for MS patients, the actress revealed in October that she had been misdiagnosed with the autoimmune disorder.

“[My doctor] came into my room and remarked, ‘Congratulations, you don’t have MS,’” she said on her podcast at the time. “I was, like, not happy at all. Everybody else was so happy.”.

In a recent development, applegate became emotional as she explained, “You don’t get misdiagnosed and then all of a sudden I’m better. I haven’t gotten better, Jamie! If anything, I’ve gotten worse. So I’m back at square one where I don’t know what’s going on with me. And that’s going to be an online investigation that my doctors are going to be getting into just to find out what has been going on since 2021, why did this happen?”.

The Married… With Children alum added that she went through a “great depression” after the health revelation.

“I lost everything. I lost my job. I lost — I’m sorry. What do you say to someone who’s been living with this for six years — God, I’m so sorry — because it stopped my life,” she mentioned through tears. “I still have to use wheelchairs when I go to the doctor’s office. I can’t walk from the car to the room. I’m still in this. Nothing magical happened when she said, ‘You don’t have MS.’ Nothing magical happened.”.

Applegate later shared a theory about what may have caused her MS-like symptoms.

Industry observers note that “I had my double mastectomy in 2008, and I am way overdue for having them replaced,” she said during the November 10 episode of her podcast. (Applegate underwent a double mastectomy after her August 2008 breast cancer diagnosis, followed by breast reconstruction surgery using saline expanders and implants.).

“I swear over the last five years or whatever, I’ve thought, ‘What if they’re leaking and poisoning me?’ And then there was some study … about it presenting as MS,” she continued. “So that’s one that I will be looking into because it’s something I’ve thought about.”.
