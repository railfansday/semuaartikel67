Along with a left back, West Ham United’s  recruitment director will be tasked with bringing a proven Championship striker to east London in January. The failure of Taty and Pablo to offer more than a token goal threat is forcing the club to rely heavily on Jarrod Bowen: More of Nuno’s famous ‘rebalancing’ is needed in the January window.

Much coveted Swansea City striker Jan Vipotnik is a former golden boot winner in this division and whilst not being the fastest across the turf certainly has an impressive record.

At just 24 he fits the profile of this new West Ham, but surely Swansea City, in the mix for promotion, wouldn’t let him go to a rival for any money.

West Ham already tried to bring Vipotnik to east London: Time for another bid?

Sources indicate that not necessarily, claim footballleagueworld.co.uk today. Arguing that Vipotnik to West Ham is ‘doable’, the EFL specialist web site claims:.

Vipotnik, 24, finished as the Championship’s top goalscorer last season, with 23 goals and three assists in 44 league appearances for Swansea City, as midway through the season, the Hammers registered an interest in the forward.

“[Vipotnik] this season, he has found himself battling for minutes with Adam Idah and Ross Stewart, and a move this winter could be on the cards.

Recent findings suggest that he’s certainly got a record to impress, despite not troubling the scorers when West Ham visited the Swans last month. Another one who, much like Joel Piroe, probably wouldn’t excel in the top division,  but is purpose-built to hit the ground running for a new Championship club in January.

Vipotnik wouldn’t come cheap but then those 15 goals could be worth their weight in gold.
