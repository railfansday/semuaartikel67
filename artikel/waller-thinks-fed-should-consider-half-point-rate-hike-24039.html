Waller Thinks Fed Should Consider Half-Point Rate Hike

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (49 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller urged the central bank to consider a larger, half-percentage-point rate hike at its December meeting. Waller mentioned inflation remains headed in the wrong direction in comments Wednesday on CNBC. Michael McKee reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg).
