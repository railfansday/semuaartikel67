How To Get Tommy’s Shirt In GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a dense game filled with all types of secrets, collectibles, and unique encounters. While many are fairly easy to come across, the most complicated one involves activating various switches across twelve power stations throughout the whole map.

Recent findings suggest that discovered and completed first by Memeio (full credit to them), this is a time-consuming easter egg that will take quite a bit of work if you want to unlock Tommy Vercetti's shirt from the original Vice City along with his legendary motorcycle, American Glory. Here is how to complete this puzzle so you can earn these rewards and go back in time!

In a recent development, to start the puzzle, you will need to head to the LPL Tower in Key Lento. At the top of the tower will be a small room with an ld television set on a table and a switch you can throw by the ladder. Interact with the switch and then approach the large machine with all the red buttons and "KOCH" on it. Press the only button available to trigger the Vice City noise and turn the TV on.

Recent findings suggest that before we start hunting down switches, we need to lay down a few ground rules you'll need to follow. The first and most notable is that there is no fast travel allowed. You have to visit each location manually, so if you have access to something like a helicopter, this will speed up your time significantly.

It has been revealed that the second is that roaming around any LPL Power Station is considered trespassing. Because of this, you will want to either knock out or kill anyone who spots you while you're going for the different switches. Not all locations will have workers, so just be careful and keep an eye out for these people as you explore.

In a recent development, speaking of switches, each Power Station listed below must be visited in chronological order. You will be either pressing a substantial red button or throwing a lever. Every station has three to four you can hit, and the one you need to interact with is determined by the purple tape with blue writing over it. This tape will either say Press D, W, A, or a black dot symbol.

Sources indicate that make sure to read the tape before throwing the switch to ensure you hit the right one! Below is the list of all the Power Station locations, along with which switch you need to hit at each one.

Our first Power Station is located in south Vice City. Make your way just north of the Tequesta distrct and you will find the LPL Power Station under the highway. Enter through the main gate, go left through the door, then take another left once you're inside. This will bring you to the control room, where you need to press the button that has the "Press D" purple strip of tape right above it.

Power Station 2 is in the northwestern corner of Vice City, in the Balsero district. When you arrive, hop the wall and make your way to the main building. Go inside and enter the control room. Once again, look for a machine that you can interact with that has the "Press D" tape over the red button. Hit that and then leave.

Recent findings suggest that we're now heading all the way down to Key Lento, specifically the northern section of this island. Just north of where Kenton is, you will find a small power station along the road. Make sure you have a car nearby and hop the fence. The moment you break open the door, an alarm will go off, so work promptly and look for the machine with the "Press Dot" tape over the button and press it. Now quickly leave before the cops arrive.

According to recent reports, now we're going north to Vice Beach — specifically, you'll want to go to Penmarch Isle in the north section of this region. When you reach the island, go north of the hospital, and along the coast will be our fourth power station. Head inside and press the button with the "Press A" tape over it.

It has been revealed that back in Vice City, go to the northern portion of the city and on the northwestern border of the Bayside district, you'll find the power station next to a construction site. Instead of going inside, look along the left side of the building for three levers you can pull. The one on the far left with the "Press Dot" tape on it is the one you want to interact with. Do not go through the door to the interior; it will trigger an alarm, and there are no buttons you need to press inside.

We now need to head all the way west to Port Gellhorn. In the middle of this region, a few blocks from Delights, you will find the power station. Once again, don't go through the door into the building. Instead, along the wall, look for another group of levers you can interact with. This time, pull on the one that has the "Press A" tape.

Sources indicate that get ready for a bit of a drive, because we now need to head south to Hamlet, which is the southernmost point of Vice-Dale County. On the east side of Hamlet, along the river, will be another power station. This time, go inside the small building in the corner of the facility and press the button with the "Press A" tape over the top of it.

According to recent reports, now go to Gloriana Key, which is in the southeast corner of Vice Dale County. This power plant is massive, so you want to head to the eastern side and look for a small building. Inside, you will find a collection of buttons, so hit the one that has the Press A tape and swiftly leave before you're spotted.

Okay, we're going back to Hamlet. This time you want to go to the west side of this region. When you arrive at the LPL station, go around back to where all the picnic tables are. You will see a lever with the "Press Dot" tape over it to the right of a ladder. Throw the lever and then leave.

In a recent development, going back to Port Gellhorn, go to the southernmost point of this region, and on a small hill will be the power station. When you enter through the gates, look for a lever under the letters "ME" painted in blue. This should be the switch; the "Press W" tape on it, so throw the lever and leave.

According to recent reports, returning to Hamlet for a final time, this time head to the south side of this town and look for the power plant. Jump the gate and go along the left side of the building. There will be two switches right next to one another under the painted letter B. Be careful when throwing the Press D lever, as it's right next to another one. Once you've activated the Press D lever, you are free to move on to the final power station.

Our final switch is located in the North Mariana County region just northwest of Hamlet. On the east side of this region you will find the last power station, so make you're over the gate. Approach the small building and look along the exterior for a lever with the "Press W" tape on it. This will be right below the blue letter Y. If you've done all the steps correctly, you will immediately get a text from an Unknown Number, telling you to go to the reactor.

It has been revealed that with all the power stations active, we need to make our way to the T.H. Power Station that's east of Hamlet. You need to be at the second reactor (which is in the center of the base) between the hours of 7:00 P.M. and 6:00 A.M. Keep in mind this area is heavily guarded, so you will want to sneak in to ensure you aren't killed during the final stretch. If you set the alarm off or have the police chasing you, the portal won't open.

It has been revealed that I recommend looking for a helicopter outside the T.H. Power Station and using that to fly over Reactor 2. Then, from a high altitude, jump out of the helicopter and parachute down to the reactor. This won't set any alarms off, allowing you to safely reach the portal without any issues! Once you find the portal, throw the lever directly to the left to be sent through the portal to 2002's Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

In a recent development, you can now freely explore the club, dance a bit, and get a drink at the bar. When you're ready to leave, head up the stairs behind the bar to trigger a cutscene where you will encounter Kent Paul from the original game. After a small dialogue exchange, you'll be sucked through the portal and dropped off in Vice City.

Sources indicate that upon exiting, you will have permanently unlocked Tommy Vercetti's shirt for Jason and Lucia, along with the American Glory motorcycle! This will conclude this complex puzzle. I want to once again thank and credit Memeio for being the first person to discover this easter egg.
