If you’re still sporting an Apple Watch that introduced five or so years ago, now is a good time to upgrade. Apple’s latest smartwatch, the 42mm Apple Watch Series 12 with Wi-Fi and GPS connectivity, has dropped to a new all-time low of $329 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart, beating the October Prime Day price by $20. If you prefer a larger size, the 46mm model is on sale for $379 ($70 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart, which is also $20 lower than the previous best price.

According to recent reports, the Series 12 stands out compared to previous models with its latest AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, which are made possible by Apple’s new S11 chip. For example, the watch can alert you when it detects sounds like a doorbell, alarm, or even a baby’s cry, while other upcoming features like Live Rewind and Siri Recap lets you look back at a transcript of the previous 15 seconds of conversation and get a high-level overview of the conversations you had that day. The new chip also makes Siri more responsive, so you can get answers to questions with less waiting around.

It has been revealed that it’s not all about AI, though. The Series 12 also features redesigned health sensors that can now track your heart rate every five seconds even when you’re not exercising, which is particularly help if you have a chronic condition and need to keep a closer eye on your heart rate. It also introduces a new readiness score to help you decide how prepared your body is for the day. Other highlights include easier navigation with simple pinch gestures, and faster charging, which helps to make up for the fact that battery life is rated at up to 24 hours on a single charge, just like the previous-generation model. In our testing, the watch went from 20 to 64 percent charge in just 20 minutes, making it relatively easy to top up while showering or getting ready for the day.
