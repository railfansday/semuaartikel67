Dancing With the Stars pro Jan Ravnik has fanned the flames of his ongoing feud with Maks Chmerkovskiy.

After Ravnik, 31, was told during a live backstage interview about his Tuesday, November 17, performances with partner Tatyana Ali, that his waltz was “beautiful,” he replied, “say that to Maks,” and pointed a directive finger towards the camera.

The professional dancer was joined by fellow pro Alan Bersten for the interview, with Bersten, 32, flashing a shocked face to the camera as he took in Ravnik’s cheeky remark.

The livestream moment was shared via a fan’s X account that night with the comments, “Ahhhhh not @JAnRavnik throwing shade at Maks !!!!!” and “Thank you to the person who caught this on TikTok.”.

Ravnik’s comment comes after seemingly several barbs were recently sent back and forth between himself and Chmerkovskiy, 46, who have locked horns since October 2025 after Chmerkovskiy criticized Ravnik’s debut DWTS season.

Social media customers reported online ahead of the Tuesday, November 10, episode of DWTS that Chmerkovskiy and fellow pro Gleb Savchenko had allegedly placed bets via a sports betting platform that Ali, 47, and Ravnik would be eliminated at the end of the night’s episode.

During an interview with USA Today after the episode wrapped, Ravnik noted his anthem for this season of DWTS was Taylor Swift’s “The Smallest Man Who Ever Lived,” before raising his eyebrows for the camera.

The response sparked online fan speculation that he was referring to Chmerkovskiy.

According to recent reports, in addition to comments made by Ravnik during Tuesday’s livestream, a post-episode interview with USA Today also seemingly referred to the betting drama. When asked for a message for fans, Ravnik remarked, “I hope you lose all your money,” which stopped Ali in her tracks.

“Well, OK, that was more of a general question but we got very specific,” she noted as Ravnik fell back in a fit of giggles.

During the October 24, 2025 episode of Chmerkovskiy’s wife, Peta Murgatroyd’s “Penthouse With Peta” podcast, Chmerkovskiy said, “I’m sorry, Jan has absolutely no business being a pro on Dancing With the Stars,” adding that Ravnik had “zero foundation technique, quality [and] understanding of the partnership.”.
