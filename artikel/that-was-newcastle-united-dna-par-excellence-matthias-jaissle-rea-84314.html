Matthias Jaissle watched on as his Newcastle United team drew 2-2 with league leaders Arsenal.

Arsenal completely dominant the first half and Newcastle struggled to get out of their half.

United fortunate to be only one down at the break.

An excellent triple substitution from Matthias Jaissle saw his bravery and decisiveness rewarded with an astonishing second half where Newcastle United were by far the better and more threatening team.

Newcastle deserved at least a point by the final whistle and can be well pleased with the character they showed.

Matthias Jaissle speaking to BBC Match of the Day after the final whistle: “Mixed feelings. Like the first home match here against Liverpool where a little bit more was possible. We need to also be realistic and humble. The first half was not good, the second half was excellent, so if you combine these two then I think a point is fair. Second half, the momentum and the energy in the stadium, you get the feeling that a little bit more is possible.”.

Matthias Jaissle on his message at half-time: “We encouraged them to do the high press in the right way, with a front-footed approach, not hesitating, not doubting, just pure belief in the way we want to do it. You saw that in the second half,  and this is something we can build on.”.

Sources indicate that matthias Jaissle on the atmosphere: “That helps. If you find that synergy between the players and the fans, then our playing style, then you can build something successful. We’re at the stage of the season where we need to be humble and realistic. A point against the best team in the Premier League is something we can build on.”.
