📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Khloé Kardashian made a surprise claim about Travis Barker “never” seeing wife Kourtney Kardashian.

It has been revealed that “Yesterday, I’m at the Get Real event. Travis is there [too] because he is announcing his documentary. So he’s like, ‘Just so you know, I am not the reason why you don’t hang out with Kourtney,'” Khloé, 42, told Kim Kardashian during the Thursday, November 26, episode of The Kardashians.

Khloé recalled being taken aback by Barker’s revelation.

Industry observers note that “He’s like, ‘I don’t see her ever. I never see her. I don’t hang out [with her],'” she stated. “He’s like, ‘So I’m in your boat. You want to hang out with her? I want to hang out with her. So I’m on [your] side.'”.

In a recent development, kim, 45, and Khloé then questioned what Kourtney, 46, does “all day” that has kept her away from loved ones. After her conversation with Barker, 51, Khloé got a call from Kourtney, who clarified the insinuation about her marriage.

“He’s like, ‘So I just want you to know, this isn’t me.’ I leave and I’m like, ‘Well, that was awkward.’ Then later last night, Kourtney FaceTimes me and she’s with Travis and [their son] Rocky,” Khloé continued. “I never mentioned [my distance from Kourtney] is because of Travis. She goes, ‘And I had to tell him, it’s what the public thinks. It’s because of Travis.'”.

In a confessional, Khloé explained her hope that Kourtney would be able to balance being a mom and wife, adding, “I wish Kourtney was better at balancing multiple relationships and that’s fine. Some people are, some people aren’t.”.

Industry observers note that she continued: “But it was never me singling out one person. She’s married — and first of all — she should be spending time with her husband. And I’ve told her that. It’s just about learning how to balance more than just one relationship.”.

It has been revealed that viewers previously had a front-row seat to Kourtney’s personal life on Keeping Up With the Kardashians. Her nearly 10-year relationship with Scott Disick was documented on the E! series as they expanded their family three times before calling it quits in 2015.

In a recent development, after the breakup, Kourtney found love with Barker. They got engaged in October 2021 before tying the knot in May 2022. The couple, who candidly discussed their journey to have children together, surprised the public when Kourtney declared in June 2023 that she was expecting a baby. Kourtney and Travis welcomed their son, Rocky, in November 2023. (The reality star also shares kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, and Reign, 11, with Disick, 43.).
