Recent findings suggest that huge news for the Beyhive and anyone who wants good hair: Beyoncé’s award-winning haircare brand Cécred is having one of its biggest sales of the year right now. Through Sunday, November 29, Cécred is offering 30% off sitewide with code BF30, plus a whole bunch of free gifts with your purchase.

According to recent reports, if you’ve ever thought Beyoncé has good hair, Cécred is partially why. Co-created with her mother and longtime hairstylist Tina Knowles, Cécred is a full line for all hair types, but many of the products are strengthening and nourishing for any kind of dry, damaged hair. At initial launch, Cécred started with the Foundation Collection, featuring a Clarifying Shampoo & Scalp Scrub, Hydrating Shampoo, Moisturizing Deep Conditioner, Reconstructing Treatment Mask, Fermented Rice & Rose Protein Ritual, Moisture Sealing Lotion, and the Nourishing Hair Oil. But the hero was the Fermented Rice and Rose Protein Ritual: a two-step system that uses fermented rice, amino acids, and biotin to help fortify hair strands and repair damage.

But since its launch in 2024, Cécred has expanded with a whole line of stylers, the viral Restoring Hair and Edge Drops, and more.

Industry observers note that and it’s not just another cash-grab celebrity brand, by the way. Cécred’s won multiple Cosmo beauty awards, including best hair game changer in our 2024 Holy Grail Beauty Awards and best hair oil for the Nourishing Hair Oil. And it’s topped many of our best-of lists. We named the Moisturizing Deep Conditioner the best overall deep conditioner last year, and the brand is #6 best celebrity beauty brand (ahead of 30+ others).

It has been revealed that “I definitely reach for this when I’m looking to tap into some main character energy. It’s rich, creamy, and deeply hydrating in a way that sets my hair up to stay bouncy and beautiful.” —JULEE WILSON, BEAUTY EDITOR-AT-LARGE.

“Leave it to Beyoncé to dream up a fresh way of harnessing the power of rice water. This innovative treatment, which also includes the Silk Rinse, helped to boost my hair’s strength, shine, and overall dopeness in one use.” —JW.
