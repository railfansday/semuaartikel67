Recent findings suggest that have you grown your moustache out for November? It’s never too late to join in, and there's always time to check up on your teammates.

It has been revealed that if you play WARDOGS (see our preview here) or are about to check it out, here’s a nice reason to do so. WARDOGS and Movember have teamed up for the chilly month of November for a mental health collab to raise awareness about men’s health and mental health. It’s time to give a virtual hand to your teammates on and off the battlefield while you keep earning coin.

Until November 30th, you’ll be able to keep your upper lip warmer (over the ski mask) as you shoot your way out of hairy situations with the Movember Pack DLC.

In a recent development, just like it’s vital to check on your teammates, it’s a good time to check on your mates as well. Drop by, send a message, see how they’re doing with everything going on in the world; it’s better to join forces and fight missions together with maximum health possible, after all.

Just like a real stache, the WARDOGS DLC special starts small but keeps growing as the player uses it, so it becomes quite impressively curved after a few hours in the field.

Players who take part in this charity drive also get a badge called the November 2026 Scoreboard Badge, which is permanent to show that they participated in the men’s health event.

Unfortunately, the stache itself only is available to use during November, as does the Movember event only exist in November, but that doesn’t mean that one should only check on his pals during one month a year.

Many things can happen to one’s friends, and prevention remains the best weapon in the game, with both mental health and screenings for prostate and testicular cancer.

Men have a higher rate of suicide and are less likely to be vocal about mental health challenges due to social pressure and expectations, but thankfully we’ve been managing to move the needle in the right direction; there is still a lot of work to be done, not just by Movember but by men as a whole.

In a recent development, we all have different methods of how to ease up, and for many men, it is gaming. Multiplayer titles in particular are great to unite with buddies after a long day at work or during some downtime at the weekend.

Sometimes just talking to a friend while multitasking with a controller can do wonders for mental health, on top of pushing away the loneliness.

Recent findings suggest that beyond being a sick cosmetic, the WARDOGS Tactical 'Stache is a powerful conversation starter. Movember has become a cultural phenomenon, and it's only natural for it to makes waves in games.

Industry observers note that just like you heal a teammate or check if he’s got enough mags during a firefight, check if he’s got enough energy for the day ahead or after a round of WARDOGS. Rep your moustaches with your mates as another community cosmetic to show how handsome all you lads are.

100% of the net profits of Movember Tactical 'Stache DLC (after platform fees, sales tax and refunds) go to Movember, funding research in prostate cancer, testicular cancer, mental health and suicide prevention.

According to recent reports, WARDOGS is a first-person multiplayer shooter currently available for Windows in early access. The game is expected to be available on PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X/S in 2028.
