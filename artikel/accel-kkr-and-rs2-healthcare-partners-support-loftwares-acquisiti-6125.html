Long-term investors reaffirm commitment to supporting the combined company's growth, product innovation and customer service.

Recent findings suggest that MENLO PARK, Calif. and BOSTON, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Accel-KKR and RS2 Healthcare Partners (formerly Riverside Partners), investors in Loftware, today revealed their support for Loftware's acquisition of Seagull Software, developer of BarTender, bringing together two leading providers of product identification and labeling solutions.

The combination represents an crucial next step in the combined organization's growth, significantly expanding its global reach and ability to serve customers across their lifecycle - from emerging businesses to some of the world's largest enterprises. By bringing together Loftware's enterprise-grade platform and BarTender's broad global footprint and trusted labeling technology, the combined company will offer customers and partners a more comprehensive set of product identification solutions while creating additional opportunities to accelerate innovation.

For more than 40 years, Loftware has been at the forefront of an industry that began with labeling and has evolved into the much broader and more strategic field of product identification. As customer needs have expanded - from label design and management to traceability, compliance, product intelligence and supply chain collaboration - Loftware has continued to expand its capabilities and global footprint alongside them. The combination with Seagull Software represents the next step in that evolution.

"Loftware has a long history of leading and helping shape this market as it has evolved, and we see this combination as another notable step forward," said Park Durrett, Managing Director at Accel-KKR. "Loftware and Seagull are highly complementary businesses, and together they will be able to serve a broader set of customers, across more markets and throughout their growth journey. We see a significant opportunity for the combined manufacturer to accelerate product innovation and expand capabilities in areas such as traceability and trading partner connectivity. We remain committed to supporting the team in maintaining the best-in-class service and support that customers and partners of both companies have come to expect.".

RS2 Healthcare Partners first invested in Loftware in 2014 and has supported the company through more than a decade of growth and transformation. During that time, Loftware has expanded internationally, invested significantly in its cloud platform and broadened its capabilities through both organic product development and strategic acquisitions.

"We have had the privilege of partnering with Loftware for more than a decade and have seen the tech giant evolve into a global leader while retaining a deep commitment to its customers and partners," mentioned David Belluck, Co-Managing Partner at RS2 Healthcare Partners. "This combination builds on that foundation, adding meaningful scale, reach and capabilities. Together with Accel-KKR, we look forward to continuing to provide the resources to invest in the products, people and customer experience that will support the combined company's next phase of growth.".

Accel-KKR joined RS2 as an investor in Loftware in 2023 through a significant growth equity investment designed to support the company's continued global expansion and innovation.

It has been revealed that founded in 1989, RS2 Healthcare Partners (formerly Riverside Partners) is a Boston-based private equity firm with total capital commitments of $1.6 billion raised since inception. The Firm exclusively focuses on healthcare investing in the lower middle market. RS2 Healthcare Partners combines a disciplined investment strategy, specialized sector expertise, a highly experienced team of investment professionals and healthcare advisors, and a collaborative approach to partnering with founders and management teams.

Industry observers note that accel-KKR is a technology-focused investment firm with over $24 billion in cumulative capital commitments. The firm focuses on software and tech-enabled businesses, well-positioned for topline and bottom-line growth. At the core of Accel-KKR's investment strategy is a commitment to developing strong partnerships with the management teams of its portfolio companies and a focus on building value alongside management by leveraging the significant resources available through the Accel-KKR network. Accel-KKR focuses on middle-market companies and provides a broad range of capital solutions, including buyout capital, minority-growth investments, and credit alternatives. Accel-KKR also invests across various transaction types, including private company recapitalizations, divisional carve-outs, and going-private transactions. Accel-KKR's headquarters is in Menlo Park, with offices in Atlanta, Chicago, Newly introduced York and London. Visit accel-kkr.com to learn more.
