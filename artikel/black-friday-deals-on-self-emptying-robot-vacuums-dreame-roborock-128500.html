If you’re buying a robot vacuum, it should be self-emptying. It just wouldn’t make sense to buy one without that feature, given that the whole point is to make vacuuming as hands-off as possible.

It has been revealed that I have two beagles, and I vacuum twice a day. That’s why I have what I like to call my Dreame Team: the Dreame L60 Pro Ultra, the Aero Pro Steam, and the N20 Steam Portable Carpet Cleaner. I also have a lone Shark upright. Clearly, I have enough cleaning to do without emptying a robot’s dustbin, too.

Luckily, almost every robot vacuum at Amazon seems to be on sale for Black Friday, and Mashable has tested so many robot vacuums. But when everything has a flashing red "Black Friday deal" label, figuring out which deals are genuinely good takes a little more work. That’s what Mashable's shopping team is here for.

Sources indicate that throughout Black Friday, I’ll be double-checking MSRPs, comparing prices across retailers, and watching for new deals from our favorite robot vacuum brands. The Roborock Q10 S5+ is a Mashable fave. Not only is it an affordable option, but Leah Stodart, our former vacuum expert, found it reliably performed in her cleaning tests, including wiping up a variety of spills and splatters in her kitchen.

Industry observers note that it’s also super quiet, even when emptying its dustbin. Stodart liked its accurate mapping and sonic mopping, which actually scrubs rather than just dragging a damp pad across your floors. At $229.99, you’re getting more than a basic robot that picks up crumbs and calls it a day.

And on a personal level, if I’m buying a robot to mop my floors, I’d prefer not to be responsible for washing its dirty pads afterward.
