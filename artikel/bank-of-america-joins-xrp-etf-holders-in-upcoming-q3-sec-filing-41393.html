Sources indicate that bank of America has disclosed a position in an XRP exchange-traded fund in its latest quarterly 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The filing, dated Nov. 16, demonstrates that the bank held 13,260 shares of the futures-based Volatility Shares XRP ETF (XRPI), worth about $106,600, at the end of the third quarter.

Industry observers note that bank of America held 13,000 shares of the fund in the first quarter and 13,260 shares in the second quarter, according to its earlier 13F filings. The same filing lists more than $685 million in BlackRock's IBIT, $13.58 million in Bitwise and $950,000 in VanEck.

It has been revealed that seven major banks disclosed XRP fund holdings worth a total of $29.4 million in filings published between Nov. 9 and Nov. 16. Italy's Intesa Sanpaolo disclosed the largest stake among them, at $27.21 million. The bank held 712,319 shares of the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF, worth about $20.52 million, and it also opened a upcoming position of 402,750 shares in the Bitwise XRP ETF, worth about $6.7 million.

UBS and Morgan Stanley disclosed XRP fund holdings of $1.91 million and $116,000, respectively. Most of the UBS position was in the Volatility Shares XRP ETF, and Morgan Stanley held funds from Franklin, REX-Osprey and Bitwise.

JPMorgan Chase held 539 shares of the Bitwise fund, worth about $9,000, and National Bank of Canada held 330 shares of the same fund, worth about $5,500. Bank of Montreal held REX-Osprey and ProShares Ultra XRP shares worth $4,300.

Recent findings suggest that the crypto portfolio of Goldman Sachs grew to $2.64 billion by the end of September, according to its 13F filing. The bank held about $1.28 billion in Bitcoin ETFs, and BlackRock's IBIT and Fidelity's FBTC accounted for $778 million and $398 million of that amount, respectively. Goldman also held about $1.22 billion in Ether funds, and BlackRock's ETHA accounted for $603 million of that amount.

Goldman held $144 million in XRP ETFs at the end of the third quarter, up from $87 million at the end of the second quarter. The bank's 13F filing for the first quarter indicated no XRP or Solana ETF holdings, and Goldman still held no Solana funds at the end of September.

The bank held shares of five spot XRP funds from Franklin Templeton, Bitwise, Canary, Grayscale and 21Shares, and its Franklin Templeton position was worth almost $48 million.

Industry observers note that besides, trading firm Jane Street reported $23.5 million in XRP funds, and Simplex Trading disclosed a $14 million stake in the Grayscale fund. EP Wealth Advisors raised its Volatility Shares XRP ETF holding by 37% to 18,400 shares, worth about $148,000.

A 13F filing only reveals long positions as of Sept. 30. It doesn't show whether the banks hedge those holdings with shorts, or what they did with the shares in October and November. Since then, XRP has fallen below $1, and on Tuesday morning, XRP was trading at $0.966.
