Vincent Kompany reflects on health scare: “Perhaps I didn’t listen to my body enough.”

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany has spoken publicly about his recent health scare at his first press conference since returning to work. Appearing at Thursday’s pre-match press conference ahead of Friday’s Bundesliga encounter with Cologne, the 40-year-old reflected on the circumstances surrounding his collapse, his recovery, and the extraordinary support he received during his absence.

Kompany collapsed while observing a Bayern reserve-team training session on October 23 and was subsequently hospitalized. As Get German Football News reported at the time, sporting director Max Eberl later confirmed that doctors had diagnosed an irritation of the meninges.

It has been revealed that the Belgian also sustained a shoulder injury during the incident, subsequently requiring surgery. Kompany appeared at Thursday’s press conference wearing a sling on his left arm, but emphasized that extensive medical examinations had provided reassurance about his general health.

The Bayern trainer acknowledged that the experience had been frightening, particularly given the unexpected nature of his collapse. Looking back, Kompany suggested that he had continued working while ill rather than giving his body the rest it required. The 40-year-old nevertheless expressed considerable relief that his medical examinations had not revealed anything requiring more serious intervention.

Kompany also took the opportunity to thank Bayern’s medical personnel, club officials, and the many individuals who had contacted him during his absence.

Recent findings suggest that the coach’s collapse prompted an outpouring of sympathy throughout German football. As Get German Football News documented in the aftermath, several Bundesliga coaches publicly reflected on the demanding nature of their profession while offering Kompany their best wishes.

The Belgian was particularly struck by the extent of the support he received, remarking that more people had contacted him during his illness than after his previous sporting successes.

It has been revealed that “Actually, I have to say that I’m very healthy,” Kompany explained. “I’ve undergone plenty of tests. We tried to find something, but fortunately nothing was so serious that further measures were necessary.”.

It has been revealed that “I was simply ill,” Kompany continued. “Perhaps I didn’t listen to my body enough, and everything that happened afterwards was simply a consequence of that.”.

It has been revealed that “I received so much support,” the Bayern coach added. “More people contacted me than when I won titles.”.
