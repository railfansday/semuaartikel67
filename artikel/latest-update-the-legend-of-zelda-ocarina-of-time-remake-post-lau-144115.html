📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has been out for less than a month, and already fans are being treated to new releases associated with it. Of course, there's the heavily leaked Ocarina of Time DLC, which is undoubtedly around the corner, and many are suspecting that a Majora's Mask remake is also in the works. However, before any of that, an officially confirmed post-launch release is being dropped shortly for the Ocarina of Time remake.

This comes shortly after the recently released major Ocarina of Time revision, and serves to offer fans a upcoming way to engage with what could very well be the Game of the Year. While it may not be quite as exciting as a full-blown sequel remake, it will make collectors very happy and give newcomers to the game a fun way of interacting with it. The best part is that you won't have to wait very long until this new Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time release is made available for purchase.

Sources indicate that ocarina of Time is getting two brand-fresh The Legend of Zelda amiibo that will introduce additional masks into the game. These amiibo have been officially declared by Nintendo via its X account, showcasing the figures themselves as well as how their new in-game masks look. Of course, it isn't terribly surprising that one of the biggest Nintendo games of the year, especially one associated with The Legend of Zelda, is getting amiibo, but it is nevertheless good news for collectors and fans of the game itself.

The amiibo are of Child Link and Zelda. The former is holding Navi in one hand while sitting down, and the latter is sitting on a stone wall while playing her ocarina. Both figures, when tapped against the Switch 2 or controller, will add a brand-latest mask for Link to wear in-game.

​​​​​​​There were previously leaks of masks that led many to believe a Majora's Mask remake was on the way, so it isn't surprising to see more added. The Child Link amiibo adds a mask of the face of Link's N64 Ocarina of Time model. Similarly, the Zelda one adds her N64 version's face as a wearable mask.

Nintendo has not confirmed a specific release date for these amiibo. All we know so far is that they're targeting a 2027 release. Of course, it isn't out of the realm of possibility that they'll launch sometime in the next few months, although it is equally possible we don't see them until mid next year. However, there are numerous social media posts, including on Reddit, of the actual amiibo in person, showcasing just how detailed they are. Additionally, we do not know how much they will cost, although the average price for an amiibo is around $17 when sold separately.

It is exciting that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is getting post-launch support, even if it is locked behind amiibo. Hopefully, more masks and other content, including the long-awaited Master Quest DLC, will also be released soon. In the meantime, there's a massive adventure awaiting new players in Hyrule, and those who've beaten it can enjoy embracing the hype in the lead-up to the release of these Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time amiibo.
