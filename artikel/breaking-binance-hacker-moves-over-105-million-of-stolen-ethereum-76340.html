📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Just a week after Binance suffered the biggest cyberattack seen in the history of the crypto market, the onchain monitoring platform Whale Alert has spotted the hacker moving some of the funds in large quantities.

The transfer has raised concerns across the crypto community about what the hacker might be up to and about the possibility of Binance recovering the stolen funds, especially after they are moved to another wallet.

Although the source did not provide sufficient details about the highly concerning transfer, it demonstrated that the attacker behind the 2026 Binance hack moved 60,000 ETH on Wednesday in 2026.

The broader crypto market has continued to suffer extreme volatility since the attack happened in late October. Hence, the total portion of Ethereum tokens transferred from the stolen stash is now worth $108.78 million at ETH’s current market price.

While the reason behind the sudden move after a few weeks of silence remains uncertain, further data showed that the assets were moved in five separate transfers of 12,000 ETH each.

Notably, each transaction was worth between $21.75 million and $21.77 million, and they were identified as Binance’s stolen funds because they were all moved from a wallet labeled 'Binance hack' in 2026.

Considering the history of the transferred funds, the move has sparked speculation that the hacker might already be preparing to spend the stolen funds, further sabotaging Binance’s chances of full recovery.

However, it is important to note that there is no evidence that the attacker has sold the Ethereum, as the receiving wallet has not been identified.
