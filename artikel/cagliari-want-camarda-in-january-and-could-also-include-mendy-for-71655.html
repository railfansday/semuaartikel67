Saturday’s reports claim that Cagliari are interested in taking Francesco Camarda on loan with a potential option to buy from Milan and that Paul Mendy could also move in the opposite direction.

Camarda was expected to serve as the understudy to Milan’s club record signing Goncalo Ramos in 2026-27, but opportunities have been hard to come by, especially after the emergence of Alphadjo Cisse, who is also capable of covering at centre-forward.

Industry observers note that into November, and Camarda has featured for just 119 minutes at club level across six appearances. He has only started once, in the Europa League, and has only played for 36 minutes across four substitute appearances in Serie A.

He is now expected to be made available for loan during the January transfer window.

Saturday’s reports from Gianluca Di Marzio claim that Cagliari are interested in pursuing a deal with Milan for Camarda and that they are willing to offer an option to buy and a buy-back option in favour of the Rossoneri, similar to the deal that Lecce agreed for Camarda at the start of last season.

Milan are also on the lookout for a upcoming attacking addition in January, and Gianlca Di Marzio claims that Cagliari are willing to offer the services of 19-year-old Mendy while discussing a potential deal for Camarda.
