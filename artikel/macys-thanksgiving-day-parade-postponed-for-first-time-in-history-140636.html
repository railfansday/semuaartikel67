📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If your Turkey Day plans include watching the parade, be prepared for some deflating news.

The annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed for the first time in history, with the milestone 100th celebration being delayed two days until Nov. 28 due to a severe storm headed to New York City, an NBC spokesperson confirmed to E! News.

“Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of Latest York, Macy’s and NBC have decided,” the spokesperson shared in a Nov. 25 statement, “in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28. More details to follow.”.

Macy’s expressed gratitude for the many people who bring the parade to living rooms every Thanksgiving.

Recent findings suggest that “Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year,” a spokesperson told MSNOW in a statement. “We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”.

And while the announcement marks the first time the parade has been postponed since its debut in 1924, the Thanksgiving Day staple has been cancelled three times throughout its 100-year history—from 1942 to 1944—due to World War II.

This year, the monumental centennial celebration was set to take place on Thursday, when a coastal storm is predicted the Substantial Apple and eastern coastline, bringing rain and strong winds.

This poses a major safety concern not only for the millions of Upcoming Yorkers who watch the parade in person, but also for the thousands of performers who participate, alongside the nearly 30 floats and record-breaking 100 inflatables, which will include characters such as Bluey, Buckaroo Bob the Minion and Hello Kitty.

And while viewers at home will have to wait a couple of days to watch the 100th annual Turkey Day spectacular in all of its glory, families will still have some parade entertainment on Thanksgiving.

Sources indicate that as an NBC spokesperson shared, the network—which broadcasts the celebration—will telecast the 2025 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Nov. 26 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.
