Xbox Provides Customers With DJ Khaled Doctor's Notes To Get Time Off For GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Xbox has teamed up with DJ Khaled for a bizarre crossover in which the singer, who goes by Dr. Xbox for the sake of this collab, challenges console owners with a doctor’s note so they can take time off to play GTA 6. The thing is, the note doesn’t actually mention GTA 6 at all.

A newly introduced post on the Xbox website penned by Khaled - sorry, Dr. Xbox himself details how this coming week is a substantial one for video games, and that many Xbox owners are going to want, nay, need, at least one day off to deal with “controller cramps” and staying up late to play games.

To those Xbox users, Dr. Xbox is offering an (un)official doctor’s note. Head to the Dr. Xbox website, type in your name, and you’ll get a personalized doctor’s note excusing you from work due to a “vitamin G deficiency”. It’s all very strange, but the Khaled of it all isn’t the strangest part. No, the strangest part is that neither the article nor the note mentions GTA 6 even once.

Industry observers note that the marketing ploy is clearly in place to promote GTA 6 on Xbox. Khaled has strong connections to Miami, the G in vitamin G presumably stands for GTA, and it’s happening three days before GTA 6 is released. The article acknowledges that this is a significant week for the gaming industry and specifically offers users one day off courtesy of a fake doctor’s note.

According to recent reports, “I’m officially prescribing a day dedicated to doing what you love: staying home, picking up that controller and playing Xbox. No distractions. No interruptions. Just you, your favorite games and the freedom to experience the biggest week in gaming the way it was meant to be experienced,” the post reads. No mention of why it’s the biggest week in gaming, fueling speculation that for some reason, Xbox isn’t allowed to say mention GTA 6 in its marketing materials.

This latest GTA-fueled marketing stunt that doesn’t mention GTA whatsoever comes in the wake of Xbox giving away a one-of-a-kind Series X hidden inside of an alligator. The contest announcement was peppered with Floridian references and imagery and clearly a promotion for the new Rockstar game, but there was no mention of the game itself.

It will almost certainly never be officially confirmed, but the takeaway from all this is that Xbox has to promote that GTA 6 is coming to Series X|S without actually saying the game’s name. If that is the case, it’s likely some sort of agreement between PlayStation and Rockstar where the game is largely being promoted as a PS5 exclusive. It isn’t, nor has that ever been explicitly said; however, GTA 6 trailers have flaunted the fact that GTA 6 “plays best on PS5” throughout 2026.

The launch trailer didn’t even feature an Xbox logo when reminding people where they can play it.

In a recent development, xbox has been flaunting the fact that owners of GTA 6 will be able to stream the game via the cloud, but even that exclusive feature has been bogged down by confusing messaging. The initial reveal made it seem as if that would be the only way GTA 6 could be played on PC. Turns out that’s not the case at all, and there will be no way to play GTA 6 on PC at launch.
