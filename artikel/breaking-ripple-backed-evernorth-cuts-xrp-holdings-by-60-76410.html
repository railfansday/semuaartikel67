📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Ripple-backed XRP treasury firm Evernorth has suffered substantial losses on its XRP ventures amid the persistent volatility facing the broad crypto market since the third quarter of 2026.

Sources indicate that the firm has reported a massive write-down of its XRP holdings for Q3 2026 in a recent filing with the U.S. SEC, drawing attention from the broader crypto community.

On Tuesday, November 17, Evernorth reported a substantial decline in the value of its XRP holdings after the firm wrote down over $505 million in its latest financial filing with the SEC.

Notably, Evernorth issued a Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, disclosing that it holds around 346 million XRP, now valued at about $342 million.

This has drawn attention across the XRP community and sparked discussions about the growing impact of persistent volatility on investors’ funds, as Evernorth’s XRP report marks a sharp drop from its initial valuation of $847 million.

Apparently, the firm reported that the tokens are now valued at about 40 cents per dollar of their initial worth. This means that the leading XRP treasury firm has reduced the value of its XRP holdings by nearly 60%.

It is important to note that the massive drop in the value of Evernorth’s XRP holdings does not mean that the firm has sold off its XRP. Rather, it marks a write-down in its previously listed XRP value.

The move may have been influenced by the persistent market drawdown, especially after XRP lost support at the key $1 level, closing steadily around $0.90 over the past few days.

Furthermore, Evernorth has specified that it has no intention of reversing the write-downs, even if the price of XRP later recovers.
