Democrats Must Fight to Fix Draft Legislation.

Washington, DC — Following Monday’s vote to open debate on the legislation, a number of Senate Democrats called for an amendment process to fix the “Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026.” However, on November 11th, Republicans began the process to jam the bill through the Senate. The caucus is reportedly seeking to “maximize” their final days in power, including attempting to pass yet another reconciliation bill and confirming many more judicial and political nominees.

This comes on the heels of Donald Trump stating that he would not approve wind energy projects, despite the fact that the draft permitting legislation, which he has endorsed, seeks to speed up the process for energy projects, including wind.

Industry observers note that sierra Club has stated its appreciation for the improvements the manager’s amendment made to the draft permitting legislation, but it has highlighted the remaining significant flaws in the bill. The organization called for a robust amendment process for the necessary changes to be made.

In response, Sierra Club Executive Director Loren Blackford released the following statement:.

Recent findings suggest that “Sierra Club appreciates the work Senators Heinrich and Whitehouse have put into crafting this bill with the intent of unleashing the mass buildout of clean energy and cutting costs for the American people. But this rogue president is already admitting he will refuse to follow the law while Senate Republicans are trying to jam this bill through as quickly, and with as little input, as possible. Democrats just won a sweeping mandate; they must fight to fix this fundamentally flawed bill, rather than allowing Trump and Senate Republicans to force it on the American people who just rejected Trump’s corrupt and costly energy policy.
