Peter Schmeichel believes simply avoiding relegation should be Mauricio Pochettino’s main ‘target’ at Manchester United this season.

Pochettino took charge of United for the first time away to bitter rivals Liverpool on Sunday afternoon and it proved a nightmare start for the Argentine, with his side thumped 4-0 at Anfield.

The Red Devils were reduced to ten men in only the tenth minute after Leny Yoro brought down Alexander Isak, and the visitors’ woes were compounded as Dominik Szoboszlai scored with the resulting free-kick.

Man-of-the-match Florian Wirtz doubled Liverpool’s advantage with an audacious finish just three minutes later, before a pair of second-half goals from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz put the hosts out of sight.

The result ensured United remained firmly in the relegation zone at the end of the weekend’s drama, only one point better off than rock-bottom Crystal Palace.

The Old Trafford giants, who now lead Europe’s top five leagues for most goals conceded (34 in all competitions), will look to stop the rot when they switch their focus to Champions League matters at Sporting on Wednesday evening.

Three days later, Pochettino’s men return to Premier League action at home to Brentford.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Realistically, can Manchester United just finish tenth or 12th? It would be okay,’ former United goalkeeper Schmeichel said on Viaplay following the side’s heavy defeat on Merseyside.

‘I know it’s silly to say but we are in the relegation zone and this team looks like a team that could get relegated.

It was some calamitous defending from Matthijs de Ligt and Leny Yoro that led to Liverpool’s breakthrough against United, with the latter guilty of fouling Alexander Isak after failing to cover his centre-back partner.

And while it was De Ligt that made the initial mistake, Yoro was the main focus of Schmeichel’s post-match criticism.

‘I think Isak is doing really well,’ the Dane noted when watching back replays of the move.

‘When Yoro comes in, Isak cuts across him and yes, he brings him down.

Industry observers note that ‘It’s terrible defending, it is. Not for that on its own but why is he not reading the ball over the top, over De Ligt?

‘Yoro should have been supporting his co-defender and he’s not.’.

Schmeichel has long been an outspoken critic of Lisandro Martinez and branded the defender ‘disgraceful’ in the wake of United’s 2-0 loss to Chelsea last month.

It came just a fortnight after Martinez had introduced an explosive attack on United’s board, in which he suggested the squad had been repeatedly let down by the club’s recruitment team.

Recent findings suggest that ‘I’m fed up talking about him. Honestly, I’m fed up talking about him,’ Schmeichel said of Martinez following United’s latest setback.

‘He’s a guy that criticises people upstairs, criticises his team-mates… I think you have to perform better than that, I really do think that.

‘You can through not all of the goals, but you can go through a couple of the goals and he’s there, at the centre of that, and he could have done something to at least make it harder for Liverpool to score.’.
