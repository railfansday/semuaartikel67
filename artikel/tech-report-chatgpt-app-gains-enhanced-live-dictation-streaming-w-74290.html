📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

ChatGPT dictation is getting a boost today. OpenAI has updated the speech-to-text feature with Live Dictation that improves both speed and reliability. The enhanced dictation upgrade also removes the 10-minute limit.

“Dictation in ChatGPT is getting faster and more reliable,” OpenAI confirmed on X.

“With Live Dictation, your speech turns into text as you talk, so you spend less time waiting for your message to finish transcribing,” the firm continued.

Atty Eleti, who works on voice features at OpenAI, added that the 10-minute dictation limit has also been removed as part of the dictation streaming upgrade.

More details from today’s app enhancement release notes below:.

Dictation in ChatGPT is getting faster and more reliable.

With Live Dictation, your speech turns into text as you talk, so you spend less time waiting for your message to finish transcribing. Tap or click on the waveform to show or hide text as you go. We’ve also removed the 10-minute recording limit.

Live Dictation is rolling out globally to all ChatGPT intends on iOS, Android, web, and the desktop app.
