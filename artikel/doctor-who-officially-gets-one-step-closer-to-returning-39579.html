The future of Doctor Who just got one step closer. It's been a whirlwind couple of years for Whovians, who have had to sit and watch their favorite show go through its most difficult period since the indefinite hiatus of 1989. A disastrous partnership with Disney and the breakdown between the once-beloved showrunner Russell T. Davies and Doctor Who fans left the show in a tricky spot, with the BBC responding by putting it out to competitive tender earlier this year.

But fears of the show's permanent cancellation were quickly silenced by the BBC, as they revealed they were eyeing a 2028 or 2029 return and the process of finding a new production home was heating up. The aforementioned former showrunner, Davies, claimed that a decision on the show would arrive sooner rather than later. You’ll start to see production with a brand-new Doctor, a brand-new companion, summer next year," he said, and it seems he might just be right.

Deadline has exclusively revealed that the search for a fresh era of Doctor Who just took a massive step forward, as around half a dozen UK production houses have been told they have made the final shortlist. Line of Duty creator Jed Mercurio, through his production company, Hat Trick Mercurio, is the most notable of these, and was already reported to be the frontrunner. Others reported to be on the shortlist include World Productions, which most recently produced ITV's The Party; STV Studios, which hopes to take production on the series from Wales to Scotland; and one of BBC Studios' labels, which briefly lost production control of the series to Bad Wolf.
