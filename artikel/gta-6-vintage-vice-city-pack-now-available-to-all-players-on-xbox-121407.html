It has been revealed that ahead of the release of GTA 6, there were three types of content made available for it - the standard game, the Ultimate Edition content, and the Vintage Vice City Pack. The latter was intended as a pre-order bonus across Xbox and PS5.

It has been revealed that now that the game is out in the wild though, Rockstar Games has decided to make the Vintage Vice City Pack available to purchase on the Xbox Store, carrying a price tag of £4.49 / $4.99. That's obviously not as good as getting it for free, but it's pretty cheap all the same, and a nice option to have if you missed out on the pre-order period.
