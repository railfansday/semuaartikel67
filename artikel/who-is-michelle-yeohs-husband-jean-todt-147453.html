Michelle Yeoh has spent decades bringing stories to the silver screen—with roles in hit films like Crazy Rich Asians and Wicked to the upcoming Blade Runner 2099—but her love life has a pretty remarkable story of its own.

Recent findings suggest that the Oscar-winning actress married French businessman and former Ferrari CEO Jean Todt in 2023, nearly 19 years after he first proposed. The pair have been together since 2004, supporting each other through career milestones, international travels, and plenty of memorable moments along the way. But who is the man standing by one of Hollywood’s most accomplished actresses?

Ahead, everything you need to know about Yeoh’s husband, Jean Todt, including his career in motorsports, how the couple met, and their long-awaited wedding.

Industry observers note that todt was born on February 25, 1946, in Pierrefort, a commune in France’s South-Central region. He studied at École des Cadres School of Economics and Business in Paris. At 80 years old, he’s 16 years Yeoh’s senior.

In a recent development, todt is best known for his work in motorsports, but his career actually began behind the wheel—or, more accurately, in the passenger seat. He started as a rally co-driver, a role he held from 1966 to 1981, before moving into the business and management side of racing.

Sources indicate that from there, he went on to work with some of the biggest names in the industry. In 1981, he joined Peugeot as its director of racing before founding Peugeot Talbot Sport, where he helped the team secure multiple World Rally Championship titles. In 1993, he moved to Ferrari, eventually becoming the manufacturer’s general manager in 2004 and CEO in 2006, a position he held until 2008.

Industry observers note that but his career didn’t end with Ferrari. In 2009, Todt was elected president of the Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile (FIA), the governing body for international motorsports, including Formula One. He held the position for 12 years, until 2021, and has also served as the United Nations secretary general's special envoy for road safety since 2015.

Yeoh and Todt’s love story began in Shanghai, China, in June 2004. Yeoh attended a Ferrari event, where she met Todt, who was then the company’s general manager. According to the couple’s wedding program, they first crossed paths on June 4, 2004, and Todt proposed less than two months later, on July 26. Yeoh noted yes, beginning what would become a nearly two-decade engagement.

After nearly 19 years together, Yeoh and Todt finally tied the knot on July 27, 2023, in Geneva, Switzerland, surrounded by family and friends. Brazilian racing driver Felipe Massa first shared the news, posting photos from the celebration on social media. Todt later confirmed the marriage and called Yeoh the love of his life.

The couple’s wedding program included a sweet recap of their relationship, noting that they had met 6,992 days earlier. Yeoh also shared photos from the celebration on Instagram, writing, “19 years and YES!! we are married!!” The actress wore several looks for the occasion, including a champagne-colored gown, and celebrated with loved ones—including her newly introduced husband’s colleagues from the world of Formula One.

Todt has one son, Nicolas, from a previous relationship. Born on November 17, 1977, Nicolas has followed in his father’s footsteps, carving out his own career in motorsports. He is a racing manager and talent manager who has worked with some of the sport’s biggest names, including Charles Leclerc and Felipe Massa.
