Ofcom receives 887 complaints, while media figures accuse Frostrup of using ‘microaggression’ against Odudu.

Almost 900 people have complained to the UK’s media regulator about an episode of ITV’s I’m A Celebrity … Get Me Out of Here! after the broadcaster Mariella Frostrup called her fellow campmate and presenter AJ Odudu “aggressive”.

Ofcom mentioned 887 people had filed complaints in relation to “alleged bullying behaviour” by Frostrup, who has been accused by other black media figures of using a “microaggression” against Odudu.
