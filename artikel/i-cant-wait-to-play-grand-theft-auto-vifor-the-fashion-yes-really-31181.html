I’m never one to out myself on main, but I’m here to declare: I am a proud (but once-closeted) gaymer.

As someone who rarely watches the latest movies or TV shows, people always retort: “Well, how do you spend your free time?” The answer is, and always has been: By booting up my Xbox. I’ve long found joy in immersing myself in a virtual world. When I was younger, it was through Super Mario on the Nintendo 64. (Aging myself much. . . I need a new night cream.) In my teen years, things evolved to the Resident Evil and Grand Theft Auto franchises. Even now, well into my early thirties, I still find myself slipping on a headset after a stressful day and blocking out the real world—by recently playing video games like Dead by Daylight, where one teams up with three fellow survivors to escape a blood-thirsty killer. That’s my idea of fun.

It has been revealed that this week, however, my attention is shifting back to Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto series—mainly, because Grand Theft Auto VI is finally releasing on Thursday. Over a decade in the works, the buzzy new game is one of the most anticipated (and pre-ordered) games in history—so much so that it’s already surpassed the $1 billion mark before it’s even out.

There’s certainly a lot to be excited for: The latest installment introduces two new characters, Jason and Lucia, and promises its biggest map to date (twice the size of GTA V’s world), interactive features (how strong Jason and Lucia’s relationship is depends on you), and even a star-studded soundtrack (featuring tracks by Cardi B, Travis Scott, Yung Lean, and Tove Lo, not to mention in-game radio commentary from notable names like Alex Consani and Lana Del Rey).

It has been revealed that sure, all of these innovative elements may have been what coaxed me to shell out $99.99 for the game’s ultimate edition ahead of Friday—but most of all, I’m excited to play around with all of the customizable fashions. (Yes, really.).

Characters in Grand Theft Auto VI.

Some may choose to play Grand Theft Auto for the high-stakes speed racing or dangerous crime missions, but I personally play it to flex cute outfits while doing it. Customizing your character’s look, after all, has long been an integral part of the GTA fun: Over the years, having the ability to choose your character’s ensembles, hairstyles, tattoos, and more has been one of the best parts of the game.

I’ve spent countless hours perfecting my saucy in-game ’fits—running around and causing chaos in my sleek biker jackets or stiletto booties. I don’t spend hours completing missions or bank heists to save money for my (virtual) savings account: I rack up the cash to spend it on the lavish fashions, duh! And the good news for me is: GTA VI is rumored to have several clothing, shoe, and jewelry shops to explore across Leonida. Meaning, if I’m going to become the most ruthless crime lord in town, I’m going to be able to look good doing it. (And yes, I will be maining Lucia, who is bound to have the cuter options.).

Lucia in a “Vice City Style” outfit from Grand Theft Auto VI.

In a recent development, it hasn’t taken high-fashion brands long to catch onto this idea that gaming can be glamorous. In Grand Theft Auto V, for one, your character could sport pieces from real-life streetwear labels such as Civilist, MISBHV and Born x Raised. And it’s not just GTA that’s been getting more and more into the fashion space. Over the years, video games have slowly becoming a high-fashion playground in general—releasing purchasable skins that can make your character feel completely tailored to you. Balenciaga has collaborated with games like Fortnite, Louis Vuitton with League of Legends, and Burberry with Roblox. Makes sense: Why shouldn’t your avatar reflect your personal tastes and likes?

Grand Theft Auto VI’s Lucia and Jason.

Sources indicate that so yes, come Thursday, I will be crashing cars, shooting absurdly-violent weapons, and being overall mischievous—but those are all side quests, because come launch day, the very first thing I will be doing is securing cash (perhaps a quick corner store robbery?) to buy me some latest chic shoes. Priorities, people.

The real question is. . . Who do we think will be the first luxury designer to get on board and collaborate with Grand Theft Auto VI? Demna, for one, has proven he is a gaming fan: Can we expect a Gucci x GTA wardrobe sometime soon? I, for one, wouldn’t mind running the streets in this fab faux-fur coat from spring 2027.
