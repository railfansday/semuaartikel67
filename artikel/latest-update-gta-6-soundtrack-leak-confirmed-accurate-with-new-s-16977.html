Industry observers note that grand Theft Auto 6 is more leak than ship at this point, with the anonymous individual known as Cyberleek in particular responsible for feeding the hype machine in ways that Rockstar weren't big fans of. The latest leak came from elsewhere, however, via a video of someone handling advance copies of the vinyl soundtrack. The album's track listing is visible, and has been confirmed as accurate by Rolling Stone's sources as well as Billboard's.

Industry observers note that this is the soundtrack rather than the score, which will be by Justice—the French electronic duo responsible for the excellent 2007 electro house album Cross—though they do feature here a couple of times as well. Their songs Flower in a Hurricane (with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard) and Starting from Zero (with Michael McDonald of the Doobie Brothers and Steely Dan) are both on side A. Here's the full tracklist.

Industry observers note that while the soundtrack focuses on fresh songs recorded specifically for the game, of course the radio stations will be full of licensed songs so that old people who don't know, I dunno, Jennie from Blackpink or whoever can stick to the Phil Collins and Cliff Richards. (No shade on Phil Collins, obviously.).

We've got a playlist of songs we expect to show up on the radio stations in our compilation of every GTA 6 song and radio station confirmed so far so you can get a headstart on those. GTA 6 will be out on consoles on November 19, which is hardly any time at all, but who knows when it will be available on PC.
