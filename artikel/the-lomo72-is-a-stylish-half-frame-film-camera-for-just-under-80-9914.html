The Lomo72 Is a Stylish Half-Frame Film Camera for Just Under $80

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lomography has declared the Lomo72, a new 35mm half-frame analog camera that captures 72 frames per 36-shot roll.

Recent findings suggest that this is actually Lomography’s second half-frame camera of the year, following the Simple Use Reloadable Film Camera Half Frame the tech giant unveiled in July. Like that camera, the fresh Lomo72 records a vertical 17x24mm image on half of a standard 35mm frame, fitting two photos onto a single frame. Where a typical 35mm camera captures a horizontal 36x24mm shot, the Lomo72 captures two 17x24mm ones.

As Lomography indicates, photographers can get creative with this. They can capture 72 “normal” shots or create diptych compositions on a single frame, pairing two thematically related shots side by side. Lomography also notes that the vertical orientation of the Lomo72’s photos is “perfect for sharing on social media.”.

It has been revealed that the Lomo72 separates itself further from the Simple Use Reloadable Film Camera through its build quality and design. While both cameras sport a relatively similar squarish look, the Lomo72 opts for a more robust design and a glass lens. Although Lomography hasn’t revealed the complete optical formula, it confirmed that the 27mm lens features a fixed f/8 aperture. Thanks to its half-frame design, the 27mm lens offers a 40.5mm-equivalent field of view. The lens can focus as close as 0.4 meters (1.3 feet).

Recent findings suggest that the Lomo72 has a decidedly old-school design, like many of Lomography’s cameras. It’s quite stylish, though it is presumably made mostly of plastic given its $79 price tag. It has an optical viewfinder and, in good news for all the trendy shooters out there, a traditional flash. The built-in flash also works with colorful gel filters.

It has been revealed that the camera features a 1/100s shutter speed, though it also includes a bulb mode for long exposures. It also has a tripod thread, works with a cable release, and supports multiple exposures.

Analog photography can be quite pricey, so a big advantage of a half-frame camera like the upcoming Lomo72 is that photographers get twice as many shots per roll of film. While users give up a bit of resolution and image area with the format, it is certainly a more economical way to shoot film.

“The Lomo72 Half-frame 35 mm Film Camera is inspired by the golden era of half-frame photography, made for every unhurried, imperfectly perfect moment. It is the kind of camera that makes you smile before you’ve even taken a photo,” Lomography says. “This camera is made for letting the mind wander. It’s about finding creativity everywhere and capturing real moments, ones that you get to relive in nostalgic comfort when you develop the roll.”.

The Lomo72 comes in black and silver colorways, though both feature the same central silver area around the viewfinder and lens. Both versions are just $79.

For customers in Asia-Pacific, the Lomo72 begins shipping this month. For everyone else, including photographers in Europe and North America, shipments start in December. Lomography says that worldwide delivery will occur before December 24, making the Lomo72 a potential Christmas present for analog photographers.
