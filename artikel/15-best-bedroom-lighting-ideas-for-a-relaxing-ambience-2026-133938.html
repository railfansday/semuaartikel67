📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

When you’re trying to unwind at the end of a long day, turning on the “big light” is simply not the move. The glare of a way-too-bright overhead light is not only unflattering but oftentimes overstimulating. It can make your otherwise cozy bedroom feel more like a department-store dressing room, leaving relaxation a little bit harder to come by. The right bedroom lighting, on the other hand, can bring a sense of warmth, atmosphere, and visual interest to the space where you spend some of your most cherished downtime.

The secret lies in layered lighting, which involves a series of soft illumination sources working together in concert: A bedside lamp provides focused task lighting for reading, while wall sconces free up valuable nightstand space and introduce a soft diffused glow. Floor lamps can brighten an otherwise neglected corner, and a beautiful chandelier or pendant light doubles as a bedroom’s focal point, even when the light is switched off.

Recent findings suggest that dimmable fixtures and warm white light bulbs can be especially transformational in bedrooms that also function as an office or a hangout space. Ahead, we’ve rounded up our favorite bedroom lighting ideas, including sculptural statement pieces, portable table lamps, and wall-mounted fixtures that usher in a sense of calm.

For a reading nook, a work-from-home space, or just a little warm white lighting, a floor lamp can show off your taste without sacrificing functionality. We’ve pulled two designer-favorite must-haves for those in the world of interior design.

Recent findings suggest that this 1960s model from Danish designer Mads Caprani hasn’t gone out of style since its release. The pleated shade, bentwood spine, and U-shaped cast-iron base make it one of the most easily recognized and iconic designs in floor lamps to this day. If you’re looking to make a statement about your style, this piece does just that—landing somewhere between studious and stylish, as in polo champion Nacho Figueras’s Florida home.

Another lamp for tastemakers, this design from Isamu Noguchi has made the rounds in many celebrity home tours from a minimalist Brooklyn brownstone to Troye Sivan’s modern Melbourne home. Commerce director Rachel Fletcher has previously named a Noguchi lamp one of her bedroom essentials. The reputation of Noguchi’s work and the unique style of his Akari lamps make this a design staple for anyone hoping to cultivate a modern bedroom.

We love the look of dual table lamps to frame your bed and create a distinct visual space in your bedroom. It also allows your bed to feel like a conscious design choice instead of a strictly functional piece. We’re taking bedside lamp notes from this Portland home owned by the lead singer of The Shins and decorated by Jessica Helgerson. And, if you don’t want to do things matchy-matchy, any one of these lamps would look great solo too.

It has been revealed that we love this moody metallic option from Article for a midcentury look—and we named it one of our favorite bedside lamps after ample editor testing. At $149, the price is also right.

If you’re struggling to find a table lamp that plays well with others, this is a viable option from coastal core experts Serena & Lily. This comes in four beautiful shades, inspired by the Italian shoreline, and there’s even a touch of rattan for a beachy bedside table moment.

The Flos Bellhop table lamp is a great source of ambient light in this minimalist Barcelona bedroom.

A godsend for a bedroom with inconvenient outlets, these design-forward portable lamps bring both ambience and task lighting wherever you need them.

It has been revealed that another favorite of interior designers, the Flos Bellhop table lamp is a great source of ambient light in a minimalist setting. These super-portable LED lamps have four dimmer settings, are USB-compatible, and can last up to 24 hours on a single charge. And, if you love the simple glass shape, they’re also available as wall sconces for a stylish outdoor lighting moment.

You never know when you’ll need a little extra light, and the Poldina Pro can adapt to your ever-changing needs. The lamp pairs a petite silhouette with the clean lines of Italian minimalist design, making it a versatile fit for your nightstand, dresser, or reading nook. Without any unsightly wires to speak of, the lamp can appear as an intentional design choice just about anywhere. Its touch-sensitive dimmer lets you adjust the brightness and choose between three color temperatures.

It has been revealed that AD shopping writer Julia Harrison, who tested the Micro version, appreciates its versatility for desk work and kitchen prep alike. She notes that the lamp “blends shyly and seamlessly into all setups that require a little, but not too much, light.” With approximately 12 hours of battery life, it’s an especially convenient option for adding a little extra illumination wherever you need it.

It has been revealed that wall lighting brings a warm dimension to the design of your bedroom and provides great task lighting for a bedtime read. While we love table lamps on matching nightstands, wall sconces bring an added degree of intentionality in their design, as seen in Walton Goggins’s guest bedroom and Jennifer Garner’s kid’s bedroom.

It has been revealed that available in 24 colors and with flexible orientation, this wall sconce channels the fun throwback design sensibility of Portland-based Schoolhouse. Not relegated to the bedroom alone, these fixtures can bring vibrancy to any space—as is evident in the bathroom of this timeless, edgy Massachusetts home.

Sources indicate that A visible mending detail modernizes a traditional style, so the pleated lampshade remains classic without being too precious. We love to imagine this lining a long entryway, or on either side of a tall headboard—the perfect lighting for bedtime pages.

Linen shades are classic for a reason: They soften the appearance of a light fixture, giving even the most straightforward silhouettes a dash of warmth and character. Quince’s Contemporary Sconce pairs crisp white linen shades with a streamlined steel frame, available in brass or black for two distinctly different looks. The brass finish befits a more traditional bedroom, while the black feels especially sharp juxtaposed against pale walls and minimalist decor. Choose a single or double configuration depending on your space, or install a matching pair on either side of the bed for a polished alternative to bedside table lamps.

Industry observers note that if a flower could moonlight as a light fixture, it would probably look something like Soho Home’s Florie Wall Light. Inspired by the interiors of White City House, the members’ club in West London, the sculptural design features two gently curved layers of champagne-colored glass that resemble a tulip’s petals. A fluted, bubbled texture gives the glass an almost vintage quality, while the frosted finish diffuses the light into a soft, atmospheric glow. Even at just nine inches tall and nine inches wide, the fixture has enough personality to stand on its own, though opting for a pair to flank the bed would be an especially charming choice to imbue the space with a dash of romance.

Actress Laura Harrier hangs a bespoke pendant lamp in her bedroom.

The wrong kind of flush mount can be an eyesore, and the right kind of pendant light can transform a space from tragic to tasteful. These picks from IKEA and Lumens are both contemporary yet classic, proving them a satisfying finishing touch that makes all the difference.

In a recent development, IKEA brings an affordable yet still-elevated style to the lineup with this ubiquitous, rattan-like pendant (technically latticed bamboo) from Ilse Crawford. We love this style as much for dining room lighting as we do for bedroom lighting. Can’t decide which? Two will only cost you $140 total.

Recent findings suggest that danish lighting design has a knack for making even the most practical objects feel like works of art. Case in point: the PH 5 Hanging Lamp. Its distinctive arrangement of overlapping shades creates a sculptural silhouette, with each layer directing and diffusing light. The three-shade system sends most of the illumination downward while allowing a softer glow to escape around the sides for ambient lighting. The result is a striking pendant that commands attention, even when it’s not turned on. The light would look especially beautiful suspended above a bedside table in place of a traditional lamp.

Supermodel Elsa Hosk incorporated a pink Tronchi chandelier, made of Murano glass, into her New York City bedroom.

Might some consider a chandelier too much for a bedroom? Yes, someone who needs to live a little. A chandelier brings a sense of occasion to every space it’s in, and your bedroom deserves no less. These picks bring elegance without overkill.

We love how supermodel Elsa Hosk incorporated a pink Tronchi chandelier made of Murano glass into her Latest York City bedroom, and you can find a similar pick on 1stDibs if you want to sleep in rose-colored bliss.

Recent findings suggest that often referred to as the “boob light,” this type of flush mount can get a bad rap, but West Elm has figured out how to make them shine. This contemporary ceiling light fixture is simple, aesthetic, and subtle.

Industry observers note that serena & Lily’s Capiz Scalloped Chandelier has the delicate charm of a seaside find, with a little extra glamour courtesy of its antique-gold framing. Each translucent capiz shell is individually hand-set, creating a softly scalloped silhouette that feels like a piece of jewelry for the ceiling. The natural variations in the shells’ shapes and opacity give the fixture a beautiful sense of movement. Light filters through the overlapping layers to cast a warm diffused glow, making it an especially lovely choice for a bedroom where atmosphere matters just as much as illumination.

Recent findings suggest that if you’re looking for guidance when it comes to your bedroom lights, it’s crucial to keep a variety of factors in mind. Do you do a lot of reading in bed? Maybe table lamps should be your primary focus. Do you have enough fixtures to keep the space illuminated but not too bright? Perhaps a floor lamp or two will provide that glow for you. Are you spending most of your time winding down and heading to sleep? Try a night-light or string lights for ambience! And, of course, you’ll want multiple light sources to get the look (and the vibe) just right.
