Manchester United are looking to sign a striker for fresh manager Mauricio Pochettino in January, and they have already got the green light from one of the best in the world over a move to Old Trafford if the deal is right.

Man United's main concerns this season have been defensively, with no club in the top five European leagues conceding more than the 30 goals they have in all competitions this term.

The Red Devils were planning an overhaul of their backline next summer having carried out extensive work on the attack and midfield in the previous two transfer windows, but they may have to bring forward those looks given how poor the club has been defensively in 2026-27 so far.

United look to need upgrades all across the back four, while there is no guarantee that Luke Shaw, Lisandro Martinez and Harry Maguire will still be at Old Trafford beyond this season given their contracts expire at the end of June.

According to GIVEMESPORT sources, the Red Devils hold an interest in Fulham duo Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson, with the former able to play in two positions the club are looking at strengthening, centre-back and left-back.

Chelsea defender Malo Gusto is also 'one to watch' in terms of United, with the Blues willing to sell, although they valued the Frenchman at £75m as recently as the summer.

Centre-forward is the other big priority for club chiefs in the winter, with GMS revealing previously that there was interest in Dominic Calvert-Lewin at Leeds, although the England international is expected to sign a upcoming contract to stay at Elland Road.

It will be difficult for United to sign a new striker in the January transfer window, which is renowned as a seller's market rather than a buyer's one, but according to journalist Rahman Osman they may have found a top striker who's keen to join, Galatasaray's Victor Osimhen.

The report states that the Nigeria international is flattered by recent speculation linking him with a switch to Old Trafford, and he'd be ready to listen to offers from them.

There is absolutely no doubt that Pochettino would sign Harry Kane tomorrow if he had the opportunity to, but that isn't going to happen after he won the Ballon d'Or and extended his contract with Bayern Munich.

Kane is a happy spot right now that has led him to football's greatest individual prize, and he has no need to return to the Premier League, yet at least.

Kane, one of the greatest strikers in Premier League history, has made no secret of how influential Pochettino has been in his playing career, and his two best seasons in terms of Premier League goals scored came under the Argentine's leadership.

A proper goalscoring centre-forward has become more of a rarity in recent years, in a world where Kane and Erling Haaland have excelled.

There is another experienced striker putting up ridiculous numbers too in Osimhen though, one of the best strikers in world football right now, who has been outstanding for Galatasaray ever since leaving Napoli for Turkey, scoring 69 goals in 85 appearances.

The Nigeria international was one of the main reasons Napoli ended their 33-year Scudetto drought when they won the Serie A title in 2023, and he has carried on that influence in Istanbul.

Osimhen, described as a 'Top 3 striker in the world' by Alvaro Morata, is a talisman who has so many different skill sets as a striker, and he would be ready to make an instant impact at Old Trafford.

The report claims that while the soon-to-be 28-year-old would be willing to listen to United proposals, he would want to earn a similar number to the £13m-per-year he is on at Galatasaray, who could demand between £80-100m to sell their star player.

Those are two major hurdles to overcome for a club that is looking to run more sensibly, but Osimhen has shown at his previous two clubs that he could be a game-changing signing.
