Kate Middleton is seen joining a cancer support group in a latest clip for the BBC and Universal’s upcoming documentary Heal, which releases Sunday.

“I haven’t really explored this outside my family walls,” the Princess of Wales tells a group of people from Shine Cancer Support.

The documentary will explore her thoughts on her illness and her recovery, including charity work that included her scaling the three highest mountains in Scotland, England, and Wales.

In Heal, set against the backdrop of the three peaks challenge, Middleton reflects on her cancer experience alongside others who have faced major health problems, discussing the impact serious illness can have on the afflicted person and those around them as well as places and experiences that can help in the healing process while exploring life beyond diagnosis and recovery. The doc will also feature her children and husband, Prince William. Sources indicate that middleton has never disclosed what type of cancer she had, but she entered remission last year. Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that in the U.S., the documentary will run at 8:00 p.m. on NBC and 11:30 p.m. on Bravo on November 30, streaming on Peacock next day. In the UK, it will air on BBC at 6:15 p.m. GMT on November 29. Additionally, Heal is set for release via NBCUniversal on Channel 9 in Australia on December 1 and CTV in Canada on December 7.

In a recent development, produced by Detail Films, Heal is directed by Benji Jackson. All proceeds from the distribution of the documentary are going to the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity.
