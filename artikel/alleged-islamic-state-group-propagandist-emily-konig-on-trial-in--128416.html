Alleged Islamic State group propagandist Emily Konig on trial in France

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that accused of being one of the Islamic State group's most active French propagandists, Emily Konig went on trial in Paris on Thursday. Originally from Brittany, Konig spent a decade in Syria before returning to France in 2022. She is charged with criminal association with a terrorist organisation and faces up to 30 years in prison if convicted. FRANCE 24's Luke Shrago is following the trial in Paris and joins us to explain the defence's strategy.
