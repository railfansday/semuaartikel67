Flight delayed or canceled? Here are the 7 best credit cards with trip delay reimbursement

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (217 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Trip delay protection reimburses reasonable out-of-pocket expenses such as lodging, meals, toiletries and local transportation when a trip paid for with an eligible card is delayed beyond a set threshold for a covered reason.
  • Coverage typically requires you to pay upfront and then file a claim for reimbursement, and it is secondary to any compensation provided by the airline or other sources.
  • Policies differ in how soon coverage begins, the maximum reimbursement allowed, and whether limits apply per traveler or per trip, which can significantly impact families or groups.
  • Eligibility, covered reasons for delay, and claim limits vary by card, so reviewing your benefits guide and booking rules is essential before relying on this protection.
  • Coverage usually requires a minimum delay of six hours or an overnight stay, and only applies to specific causes like bad weather or equipment failure.
  • Some cards require round-trip travel or payment of the full trip cost with the card, while others allow coverage for taxes and fees on award travel.
  • Claim limits and eligible family members differ; some cards restrict the number of claims per year or define covered travelers more narrowly.
