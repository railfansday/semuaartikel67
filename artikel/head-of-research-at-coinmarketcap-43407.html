📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, coinMarketCap is hiring a Head of Research to turn CMC data into research that institutions, media and partners trust and pay for. This is a senior, hands-on role reporting to the CMO.

Sources indicate that CMC is the most referenced price and market data source in crypto. Our research already reaches millions each month through reports like our Monthly Exchange Report, Asset Watch and Market Pulse, and is cited by Bloomberg, WSJ, Forbes and Fortune. The next step is different. We are building CMC into a data platform for crypto, tokenized assets and broader markets, and research is how we prove the quality of that data to the people who buy it.

You will own our research agenda end to end: what we publish, how rigorous it is, and how it supports our enterprise data business. You will also be the senior voice on sensitive market analysis for CMC and its partners.

Set and run the research roadmap, from recurring flagships (monthly exchange report, weekly market and asset coverage) to deep dives on exchanges, tokenized assets, RWAs and market structure.

Build institutional grade research that showcases CMC data to banks, funds, data buyers and enterprise API clients, working closely with our API and BD teams on what buyers need.

Own methodology and data integrity: define how we measure volume, liquidity, flows and reserves, document it, and make sure every published number holds up to scrutiny.

Lead research on sensitive or fast moving market events, balancing speed, neutrality and accuracy, and handle review requests from partners with sound judgement.

Be a credible public face for CMC research with tier one media, on panels and in interviews.

Manage and develop a small research team, at the moment one research analyst, and use AI and automation to scale output without lowering quality.

Partner with marketing, editorial and product to turn research into formats people actually read and share, from long form reports to charts and short social posts.

7+ years in research or data analysis, with at least 3 in crypto or digital asset markets.

A track record of published, data heavy research that institutions or serious media rely on.

Deep understanding of market structure: exchanges, spot and derivatives, liquidity, onchain flows and proof of reserves.

Strong quantitative skills and hands on data work (SQL or Python, APIs, large datasets).

Excellent written English and the judgement to present sensitive findings neutrally and accurately.

Experience leading or mentoring analysts.

Background at a crypto data provider, an institutional research desk or a TradFi data business.

Understanding of how data is packaged and sold to institutions (licensing, enterprise feeds, research subscriptions).

Knowledge of tokenized assets, RWAs or traditional market data.

Existing relationships with tier one financial and crypto media.

Comfort using AI tools to automate data pulls and drafts.

Mandarin, given our close work with Chinese speaking partners and media.
