Patrick Mahomes and wife Brittany Mahomes have revealed the gender of their fourth child.

“Baby #4🥹🤭,” the Chiefs quarterback, 31, and Brittany, also 31, teased via a joint Instagram video shared on Tuesday, November 17. In the video, the couple were seen in a kitchen with a white cake topped with tiny pink and blue frosted flowers and the words “Oh baby.”.

Holding a champagne glass each, the couple, joined by their three children — daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 22 months, and son Bronze, 3 — shared a sweet kiss before dunking the tops of their glasses into the cake to see which color frosting would appear once the glassware was retrieved.

To their delight, blue sugary goodness coated the rims of each glass, indicating that they will be welcoming a second son next year.

While Patrick grinned widely over the reveal, Brittany shared her own joy by pounding her fists in the air and jumping up and down. In the meantime, the couple’s family popped confetti cannons that sprayed blue and gold.

The duo then shared another kiss before giving their children hugs, with Brittany raising an arm in the air and Patrick lifting Bronze up high for the cameras.

The clip concluded with further footage of the children making snow angels out of the fallen confetti on the ground before Patrick’s mom, Randi Mahomes, was seen hugging Brittany’s mom, Diana Lindeberg, and the entire clan posed for photos together.

Recent findings suggest that brittany and Patrick revealed on Tuesday, November 10, that they were expanding their brood once again. “Another blessing from God🥹🙏🏼,” they wrote in a joint Instagram post. “& then there were 6🤍.”.

Two days later, Patrick said during a Chiefs press conference that he was hoping for a boy. “Obviously, it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way. I’m excited for it,” he said at the time. “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score.”.
