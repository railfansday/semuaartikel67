In a recent development, it almost goes without saying that many of us are permanently glued to our phones these days. Beauty-tech staples like Rhode’s viral Lip Case and Lip Tint bundle have been coveted for years. But this holiday, a few upcoming attachments are more than just practical; they’re also extremely giftable. The next generation of beauty phone accessories from popular brands like Sol de Janeiro and Huda Beauty are excellent stocking stuffers alongside collectible beauty minis, and make it easy to glam on the go.

Industry observers note that here, we’ve curated a short list of the best beauty phone and keychain accessories to shop now ahead of the holiday rush for just about everyone on your list. And the best part? Most are available for under $40.

You’ve probably already seen Dior’s stylish Médaillon lip balm all over your For You Page since the coveted phone accessory—which sticks directly to the back of your case—has caused quite the internet frenzy. Although it’s presently out of stock, Bazaar editors are keeping their eyes peeled and fingers crossed for the Médaillon’s return for our own holiday wish lists.

Sources indicate that and speaking of lip balm, we couldn’t compile a list of beauty phone accessories without including this OG sensation from Rhode. Designed to protect iPhones 13 through 17 Pro, the silicone bubble case features a puffy back for a secure grip, plus a dedicated space for the brand’s Peptide Lip Treatments and tints. If your friend already has their fair share of Rhode balms, you can also buy the case separately.

If your best friend is a Sol de Janeiro superfan, surprise them with this customizable balm and keychain set for endless compliments on how great a gift-giver you are. Whether they’re loyal to Sol de Janeiro’s classic Cheirosa 62 or prefer a different scent, your friend will get plenty of use out of this handy duo.

It has been revealed that you don’t have to spend a ton of money on a great beauty accessory: Daise’s Lip Service Lip Balm Phone Pod is proof at under $10. Their dermatologist-tested, flower-shaped balm sticks right on the case of any phone for easy lip hydration and is available in coconut, strawberry, vanilla, or watermelon.

Anyone who loves accessorizing and spritzing on Bella Hadid’s waterless Ôrebella fragrances will be thrilled to receive this scentable wristlet, designed by the supermodel and friends Devon and Sydney Carlson with Wildflower Cases. Inspired by traditional friendship bracelets, this limited-edition piece attaches to your phone case, camera, or keys. Its colorful ceramic beads infuse with your favorite Ôrebella scent, creating a fragrant keepsake.

Recent findings suggest that anytime your sister reaches for their phone to send a direct message, they can also reapply their lip balm, thanks to Lanolips’ strawberry-flavored 101 Ointment holder. It uses a sticker magnet and MagSafe attachment that’s compatible with all phone types and styles.

It has been revealed that forgot setting powder at home? Fear not, Huda Beauty’s Easy Bake Pressed Powder add-on makes it easy for the makeup lovers in your circle to keep their under-eye concealer intact wherever they are. Huda Beauty’s Phone Grip includes the pressed powder in up to eight shades, a compact mirror, and a mini sponge for all their setting needs, and it works best with MagSafe phone cases.

In a recent development, here’s a playful leather lipstick case charm from Marc Jacobs that clips directly to her keys, bag, or even a belt loop. The branded case has a zip-around closure to keep their favorite satin red or matte nude handy at a moment’s notice.

This isn’t an ordinary mirror; it’s a foldable high-tech mirror keychain. It's LED-lit with warm, neutral, and cool settings, USB-rechargeable, and features dual magnification. Plus, because the mirror neatly folds in half, it won’t take up much space next to their house or car keys.

It has been revealed that give your friend group's skincare minimalist no excuse not to wear sunscreen every day with Beauty of Joseon’s Day Dew Mini Keychain Set. The K-beauty favorite formula leaves no white cast and blends ingredients like hyaluronic acid and niacinamide to keep skin plump and glowy while minimizing irritation.
