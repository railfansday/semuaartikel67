Kim Kardashian shut down claims she was “rude” while visiting boyfriend Lewis Hamilton at a work event.

In a sneak peek at the Thursday, November 26, episode of The Kardashians, the reality star, 45, was asked about her time at the F1 Grand Prix over the summer, to which she replied, “I would never in a million years show up to my boyfriend’s job and make it all about me and start doing interviews.”.

Recent findings suggest that kim’s response to the rumors came as a screen grab from an article rehashed her being approached by Sky Sports reporter Martin Brundle at the event in Monaco. The journalist’s attempt to capture a pre-race interview took an awkward turn when Kim didn’t respond to his questions before the Formula 1 races.

Sources indicate that the interaction caused some critics to label Kim as “rude” while others defended her for keeping the focus on Hamilton, 41. Earlier in the season, Kim spoke about how she manifested her relationship after she collected over 50 attributes that she was looking for in a potential romantic partner.

Sources indicate that “Let me just say this, I am just really, really happy,” Kim gushed to the cameras in an October episode. “That’s all I’ll say for now.”.

Sources indicate that kim was seen answering a “private call” with Hamilton while filming the reality series. In another confessional, she opened up about finding someone who checked every box.

“Isn’t that crazy? Every single [box is checked]. 55 [of them],” she gushed. “To just look up one day and be like, ‘Wait, let me check the manifest list.’ And then be like, ‘Yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes.’ It is a crazy feeling.”.

Kim, who has been linked to the Formula 1 driver since February, previously discussed her attempt to manifest the perfect man.

Industry observers note that “[I need him to] protect me [and] fight for me. Good hygiene, and that is a given, so I should maybe take that off. [He should be] calm. No mom or dad issues. [I want someone who is] patient, supportive, genuinely happy for me [and] successful,” she shared in a June 2023 episode of the Hulu series. “[He should be] spontaneous, fun [and] my friends and family love him. [I want] someone that can be a role model for my kids — especially someone my boys can look up to.”.

Kim later elaborated on her search for The One.
