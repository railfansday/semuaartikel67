In a recent development, use Wefunder to back many startups and local businesses with small amounts, since it lists more crowdfunding raises than any other portal. Skip it for money you may need within several years, because a startup stake usually pays you back only when the company is sold or goes public.

Sources indicate that most companies on Wefunder accept investments as small as $100, and you add a 2% fee by bank transfer that’s never less than $8, so a $100 check costs $108. Wefunder says returns, when they come, take about seven years on average, though a loan to a local business pays you back every quarter or year.

Investors who can leave money alone for seven years or more and split it into checks of $400 or more, where the fee is no more than 2%, get the widest choice of raises here. Anyone who may need the money within several years should keep it in a high-yield savings account instead.
