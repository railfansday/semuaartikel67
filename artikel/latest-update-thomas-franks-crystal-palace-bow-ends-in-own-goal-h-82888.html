Thomas Frank suffered defeat in his first game as Crystal Palace boss as Dwight McNeil’s late own goal condemned them to a 3-2 loss at Coventry.

Frank, who was sacked by Tottenham in February, oversaw his first game for a third London club but was unable to stop them from losing for a fifth successive match in the Premier League.

Frank’s tenure got off to the worst start possible when Taiwo Awoniyi put the hosts in front with his second goal of the campaign, but Palace instantly responded through Yeremy Pino.

An all-action first half continued as Tatsuhiro Sakamoto’s sublime strike put Coventry back in front before Jorgen Strand Larsen’s first goal since September pulled Palace level again.

It looked like Palace would come away with a point until McNeil inadvertently bundled the ball into his own net to secure Coventry back-to-back home wins.

Frank Lampard’s side are growing in confidence in front of their own supporters and picked up a third win at the Coventry Building Society Arena.

Lampard was wary of the visitors starting strongly with a new boss, but it was his side who drew first blood when Jack Rudoni played the ball across for Awoniyi to stab a first-time effort into the far corner.

The hosts were left wondering how they were not two to the good when, seconds after Carl Rushworth’s save down one end, the Sky Blues broke through Awoniyi and he rounded Dean Henderson but saw his effort blocked heroically by Axel Disasi on the goal-line.

They were made to pay shortly afterwards as Palace levelled.

Daichi Kamada sent a cross in towards Strand Larsen who saw his failed scorpion attempt bounce kindly for Pino and he lashed home from close range.

Coventry regained their advantage on the half-hour mark courtesy of a sublime hit by Sakamoto.

Rudoni had assisted the first and was the creator again as he whipped a cross to Sakamoto who took a lovely touch with his chest before lashing a shot past Henderson from outside the box.

According to recent reports, palace came from behind for a second time on the stroke of half-time. Pino turned creator with a cross onto the head of Strand Larsen, who had sprung the offside the trap and nodded the ball beyond Rushworth.

Henderson was called into action to stop Coventry from going ahead for a third time as he dived to his right to palm Rudoni’s effort behind.

Palace were in the ascendancy and looking most likely to get their noses in front.

Strand Larsen was first onto a low cross by Pino but he was stopped by Rushworth and Bobby Thomas made a crucial block to stop substitute Evann Guessand from sweeping the ball home.

According to recent reports, it proved vital as Coventry snatched a winner. Brandon Thomas-Asante’s initial header was saved by Henderson but the ball bounced off McNeil and rolled over the line.
