🌐 Source: Physicianonfire • 📅 20 Oct 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (150 words, 📖 Standard)

According to recent reports, good, thoughtful article, Thanks! I have to agree about the ongoing obligations that the treadmill gets us into. I have to identify with Dr Net-Worth, but rather than having no purpose, I would say that one real, tangible benefit he has is security. Knowing he or his spouse can incur some life altering accident or illness and not affect the well being of the rest of the family. As an example my mother in law has dementia and has been in memory care for a decade. Her current bill is 200k per year. She and her husband planned very well and regardless of how long she lives she will be taken care of. The kids have not needed to contribute at all. My household has planned in a similar way. I know I will never need to be supported by my kids.
