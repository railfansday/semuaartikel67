📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Don’t expect Jennifer Lawrence to hop aboard the method dressing train anytime soon. The actor, who is set to reprise her role as Katniss Everdeen in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, arrived at the film’s Upcoming York premiere tonight dressed firmly as herself. By her side? Her former co-star Josh Hutcherson.

Josh Hutcherson and Jennifer Lawrence.

Lawrence hit the carpet in a look from Dior’s pre-fall 2026 collection: a sweeping black robe, cinched at the waist, pink embroidered flowers blooming from the hip all the way down the train, and a faux-fur white shawl collar draped over her shoulders. She accessorized with a pair of Fred Leighton onyx and diamond drop earrings that date back to the 1920s.

Jennifer Lawrence at the Hunger Games premiere.

It’s been a light year for Lawrence on the red carpet front: in January she kicked off 2026 at the Golden Globes, where she wore a mesh Givenchy by Sarah Burton gown with well-placed floral embroidery. It seems that she’s taking the motif to heart, bringing it back for her second carpet of the year. Sure, she may not be method dressing, but can you blame Jennifer Lawrence for loving a theme?
