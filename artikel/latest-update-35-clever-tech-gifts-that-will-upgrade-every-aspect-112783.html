Industry observers note that as much as we love a latest throw pillow or vase under the Christmas tree, we know that some of the most coveted presents are electronic. Cool tech gifts can genuinely change your giftee’s life for the better. They can help them travel better, upgrade their home, get them through the workday faster, or just make them smile. So whoever you’re shopping for, from the chronically online teen to the discerning coworker, will appreciate these genius gifts.

In a recent development, just don’t forget to personalize the item to your recipient. If they have interests such as photography and videography, consider getting them a tripod or GoPro camera so they can document all their action-packed adventures. The nature lover in your life will appreciate a smart bird feeder, and the chef will use the smart stand mixer all the time.

It has been revealed that below, we rounded up dozens of the coolest tech gifts on the market. Of course, we included tested and approved favorites from our very own staff. The technology that changed our lives will make for some super thoughtful gifts.

The Brick duo is kind of the anti-tech tech gift. If your loved one has been wanting to cut down their screen time, this is the smartest tool to help them do that. It works as a separation between you and your most distracting apps. One that can’t be dismissed without physical interaction. “I keep a Brick at my desk and in my kitchen as barriers to my doomscrolling habit,” Associate Shopping Editor Janae McKenzie indicates. “But the Brick Night is a real game-changer—it’s Bluetooth location-based, so the moment I cross the threshold into my bedroom, all my social apps are locked, so I actually have to go to sleep at a decent hour.”.

The easiest way to make your bathroom feel like a spa? Add a towel warmer. Stepping out of the shower, you can retrieve a fluffy towel from this heater to wrap up in, easing the harsh cold of tile floors. It comes with a timer, so you won’t have to worry about leaving it on overnight.
