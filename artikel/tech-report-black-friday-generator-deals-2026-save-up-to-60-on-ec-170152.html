If you’ve been waiting to find a great deal on emergency backup power for your home or portable power for a road trip, Black Friday and Cyber Monday are some of the best times to find the lowest prices on home generators, portable generators, power stations, and solar generators.

Recent findings suggest that this year, weather emergencies have produced a high demand for generators, especially battery-based power stations, which means some products may not ship for several weeks. Meanwhile, some companies like Jackery aren’t running any Black Friday deals at all.

According to recent reports, luckily, that’s not true for everyone. For Black Friday, we’re still seeing discounts as high as 40 percent or more. If you’ve been waiting for a good deal on backup power for your home or even just a few devices, this is a great time to buy.

According to recent reports, start by considering what you want to power during an emergency, like a refrigerator, a computer setup, or the entire house. Or, if you're camping or on a job site, consider the devices you want to power or recharge, such as power tools or camping gear like portable refrigerators.

According to recent reports, then consider the type of generator or power station you want. Fuel-based generators can run as long as you have enough fuel, but they’re loud and must be run outside because they emit potentially dangerous fumes. Power stations, on the other hand, run virtually silently and don’t emit fumes, so you can run them inside, making them great for city dwellers. They need to be recharged, but you can do that with AC power or a solar panel.

No matter what type you choose, it will cost several hundred dollars or more, so Black Friday and Cyber Monday are a good time to knock 20 to 40 percent off the regular price, including popular brands such as Jackery, Bluetti, EcoFlow, Generac, and Champion Power Equipment.

Tried and true, fuel-powered generators are popular because they can run indefinitely, as long as you have enough fuel on hand. Some run on just gasoline, while dual- or tri-fuel generators add propane or natural gas as alternatives. They can be loud, so an inverter generator reduces noise pollution and delivers cleaner power that’s safe for sensitive electronics like TVs.
