Black Friday is a great time to shop for a latest suitcase, but it might be an even better time to start planning where you’ll take it. With Thanksgiving behind us, travel companies are getting in on the seasonal savings with early promotions that give travelers a head start on one of the year’s biggest booking events: Travel Tuesday.

Sources indicate that held annually on the Tuesday after Thanksgiving, Travel Tuesday falls on December 1 this year and brings with it a wave of deals across flights, hotels, cruises, and vacation packages. It’s become a worthwhile date for travelers to have on their radar, as it’s a surefire way to spend less on mid-winter Caribbean escapes, European summer adventures, and long-haul cruises, from around-the-world voyages to Antarctic expeditions.

Sources indicate that while the official event is still a few days away, some of the steepest Travel Tuesday deals are already starting to surface—and there’s no reason to wait until December 1 to see what’s on offer. From early airfare promotions to discounts on some real bucket-list-worthy hotels and cruises, I’ve rounded up 15 of the best early Travel Tuesday deals to know about now, including the ones worth booking today and over the weekend.

Where the pool is ready and the palm trees sway at JW Marriott Orlando Bonnet Creek Resort & Spa.

The offer applies to stays taken between November 29, 2026, and May 2, 2027, so you’ll be able to take your pick from a fun winter weekend, spring break, or early-summer getaway.

Members can earn the extra points on eligible stays of at least two consecutive nights booked by January 3, 2027, for travel through the entire year ahead (with a minimum of $1,500 in qualifying charges).
