Sunderland have been dealt a massive blow amid growing concerns about the latest news surrounding Brian Brobbey's injuries.

The 24-year-old withdrew from his national team squad shortly after leaving the Stadium of Light, with reports in the Netherlands warning he may have suffered a muscle tear.

While there has been no official diagnosis from the Black Cats' side, his absence earlier this season caused problems for Regis Le Bris, and could be set to do so again.

Former Sunderland chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Brobbey's absence will be a serious blow to Sunderland's chances of turning their season around.

They at the moment sit inside the Premier League's bottom three, with growing fears they could be sucked into a relegation battle, and need all their star players fit and firing.

Given Brobbey's role in Le Bris' side, and the amount he is relied upon to lead the line, the latest reports surrounding his fitness have set alarm bells ringing in the North East.

Brown warned Football Insider last month that Sunderland's growing injury list will leave them in "substantial trouble" if star players are forced to spend long spells on the sidelines.

The last international break saw both Brobbey and Dan Ballard fall victim to fresh injury blows, and this one has brought no more relief, with more casualties suffered.

News surrounding the striker's injury from the Netherlands has now set alarm bells ringing, and Sunderland's miserable start to the season could be set to continue.

"Losing Brian Brobbey will be a massive blow for Sunderland if he's out for a long time," Brown told Football Insider.

"They'll assess him, do scans and everything at Sunderland to get a better idea of how long he will miss, but if it's a serious injury, that's very bad news for their chances.

"Brobbey has shown this season how important he is, not just with the goals he's scored by his general play, leading the line and providing an outlet for them.

"He occupies defenders, he's not afraid of a battle and a fight, he'll run in behind, he can drop short, and he scores goals which Sunderland have been lacking for a while.

"All of a sudden, for them to miss that player, it leaves them short of options and it's a huge change to the way they're able to play.

"I don't see anybody in that squad who can step in and play that same role at such a high level, so as far as Sunderland are concerned, they'll be hoping it's only a minor issue.".

Should Sunderland's form take another hit in Brobbey's absence, questions are certain to be asked of Le Bris, regardless of whether he is the one at fault.

With the Black Cats already in the bottom three and in danger of being caught in a relegation scrap, the powers that be may be forced into making a drastic decision.

Brown told Football Insider on 2 November that Sunderland are reluctant to sack Le Bris, but are determined to see results improve as they strive towards their ambitious plans.

While there are mitigating circumstances, including major injuries which have limited Le Bris' side, there is a growing feeling that results must improve immediately.
