Bellingham has scored seven goals this season but this will be one to remember.

Manager Jose Mourinho described Jude Bellingham as "without any doubt, the best midfielder in the world" following a wonder goal which inspired a brilliant second-half performance for Real Madrid in a 5-1 win against PSV.

Mourinho opted to leave the England midfielder out of the starting line-up for Tuesday's Champions League game at the Bernabeu - an understandable decision given the demands of Madrid's fixture schedule and the strength in depth at his disposal.

But after watching his side trail 1-0 at half-time against the Dutch side, Mourinho knew changes were needed.

Industry observers note that "He is an amazing player that has everything," the Portuguese manager noted. "He has physicality, he understands the game, he has absolutely everything. Today, he has changed how the game was evolving.".

Captain Fede Valverde equalised in the 50th minute and 10 minutes later Bellingham produced his moment of magic.

Receiving the ball just outside the penalty area, he took two touches to lift it into position before unleashing a magnificent volley with the outside of his right footr, leaving PSV keeper Matej Kovar with no chance.

It was a sensational finish, executed with extraordinary precision.

"I was a bit surprised, honestly," Bellingham mentioned about the goal. "I knew that I could control the ball well and get it into my grasp. But normally from there, I'm looking to see who's on as an option to play through.

"It popped up nicely and I thought I'm just going to hit it. So yeah, it was a nice goal and notable for the team, being the second goal, and gave us control.".

The 23-year-old celebrated in trademark fashion, standing with his arms outstretched in front of the home supporters as thousands serenaded him with chants of 'Hey Jude'.

It was his fourth goal in his past five appearances for Real.

Asked whether he should be compared to Real and France legend Zinedine Zidane, who won the Ballon d'Or and World Cup in 1998, Bellingham maintained his humility.

Industry observers note that "I think Zidane is much better than me. He's much more elegant and he's my idol," he noted.

'I'm just going to hit it!' - Bellingham reacts to stunning goal.

Kylian Mbappe scored two more goals and Vinicius Junior put away the fifth to complete a super turnaround from a team that had looked short of ideas in the first half.

At the suggestion that he is currently in the form of his life, Bellingham noted: "I think you're only as good as your next one [game]. Your last one doesn't really matter.

"But my focus has to change now to preparing for the next game. Thinking about my best form or being the best player in the world doesn't mean anything to me.".

For Bellingham, the ability to respond when things are not going according to plan is what separates the best teams from the rest.

"We're managing to show that mentality that good teams do, that even if you're not playing well, you can raise the level at any point," he mentioned.

His adaptation to life in Spain has also been an key part of that journey.

Asked about his increasingly impressive Spanish, Bellingham admitted he still gets nervous when speaking the language in public.

Industry observers note that "It's getting a little bit better. I get a little bit nervous speaking to you guys. It's much easier in the changing room," he joked to journalists.

"I'm loving living in Spain. I'm loving the club. I think for English players it's such an key thing to go and move abroad and learn new things and I've obviously benefited massively from that.".

And while his performances have inevitably brought renewed attention to individual awards, Bellingham said that personal recognition is not what motivates him.

Asked about the Ballon d'Or, he remarked his focus was only on the team.

"I don't care about the Ballon d'Or, honestly. I want to win a lot of titles with the team, the Champions League, the League.".

"The Ballon d'Or is something for you, journalists, to vote and create a bit of tension. But for me it's more notable that the team win.".
