Arsenal stars found themselves at the centre of a dispute as England and Spain players clashed at half-time of their Nations League showdown.

Gunners defensive duo Ben White and Ezri Konsa were on the scoresheet for the Three Lions, helping their side to a famous 3-2 win away in Spain.

England, however, had to see out the final 30 minutes with a man disadvantage as Manchester City midfielder Elliot Anderson was shown a red card for fouling Lamine Yamal inside the box.

Despite that, Thomas Tuchel’s side held on for three points that lifted them above Spain in their Nations League group.

The other major talking point in the Spanish capital was an incident that occurred at half-time, when White clashed with Paris Saint-Germain midfielder Fabian Ruiz as the pair walked off the pitch.

It has been revealed that this lead to a pouring of white and red shirts. David Raya, back-up to Unai Simon while on international duty, joined Arsenal team-mate Konsa in calming matters.

Martin Zubimendi also attempted to play peacemaker after initially getting involved, conversing with Konsa as the huddle moved towards the tunnel.

A sixth Arsenal player was close by, as Mikel Merino - moments away from being hauled off at half-time - watched on as the argument fizzled out.

It has been revealed that there were a total of eight Arsenal players involved in the matchday squads for both nations. Eberechi Eze was an unused sub on the England bench, with his club teammate Myles Lewis-Skelly introduced in the second half as Tuchel looked to see out victory for the Three Lions.

Arsenal wingers Noni Madueke and Bukayo Saka are England regulars, but missed out on the squad for the last international break of 2026.

The Gunners return to action this weekend, when they travel to face Newcastle on Saturday, November 21.
