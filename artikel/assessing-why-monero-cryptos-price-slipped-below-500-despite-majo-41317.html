📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, A week ago, MAGIC Grants, the non-profit cryptocurrency research, funding, and blockchain charity, contracted zkSecurity to review the generalized bulletproofs implementation in Monero [XMR] for the Full-Chain Membership Proofs (FCMP++). Monero unveiled that the audit had been successful, quoting MAGIC Grants’ published findings. It announced that,.

“It proves that the Generalized Bulletproofs protocol, in the form Monero implements it, is complete, zero-knowledge, and sound.”.

Industry observers note that zkSecurity reviewed the code alongside the cryptography for Generalized Proofs. Commissioned to examine the Generalized Proofs crate within the Monero Oxide project, the audit found no high or medium severity problems.

Four low-severity issues and three informational findings were reported, which the project developers have responded to since.

According to recent reports, generalized Proofs powers the FCMP++ upgrade, a major privacy upgrade for Monero. They are extensions of the standard Bulletproofs zero-knowledge protocol and are modified to support Pedersen vector commitments.

After the code review, zkSecurity wrote a separate proof of the protocol’s security in the Lean theorem prover, which builds machine-checkable mathematical proofs. This independent formalization proved the completeness of the Generalized Proofs protocol in the form Monero currently implements.

Recent findings suggest that the sweep of the late 2025 highs around $600 did not result in a bullish breakout. Instead, the previous week’s sell-off saw XMR close the weekly trading session below $500.

This was only a reset within the broader uptrend the privacy token has enjoyed since 2025. However, the reset could go deeper, below $500. The $390-$440 demand zone was the closest on the weekly chart.

In a recent development, zooming in, the 4-hour chart highlighted a short-term selling opportunity. The bearish impulse move to $455 was followed by a bounce to $518, the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level. XMR slid back towards $500 in the last 24 hours.

This reaction may be a sign that the sellers were back in control, with the DMI’s downtrend highlighting that this could continue.
