Grand Theft Auto 6 isn’t out in the U.S. until November 19, but if you want to play a day early, there’s a way. Here’s what you need to do.

According to recent reports, the global launch for GTA 6 starts at 12:00 a.m. in Upcoming Zealand on November 19, which is actually 6:00 a.m. ET on November 18 in the U.S. Yay, time zones! In order to start playing at that time, however, you’ll need to do different things depending on whether you’re playing on PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S.

Industry observers note that on Xbox Series X/S, the method for unlocking GTA 6 a day early is relatively straightforward. Just go to your settings menu, scroll down to “System,” Â and select “Language and Location.” From there, choose New Zealand and choose the option to restart your console. When it boots back up, your system will behave as if it’s in the New Zealand time zone, right down to the Microsoft store that decides when your games go live. Easy!

Recent findings suggest that A similar Latest Zealand trick is possible but not nearly as straightforward on PS5. On Sony’s console you need to create an entire new PSN account and select the country during the setup process. You then need to purchase GTA 6 through the New Zealand version of the PlayStation Store.

Assuming you don’t have a bank account or credit card registered to that country, you’ll need to purchase New Zealand PSN gift cards online for the total amount ($80 for the standard edition or $100 for the deluxe version). If you already purchased a U.S. version and pre-installed, sorry, that won’t be good enough. Is buying an entire second copy worth it to you to play roughly 18 hours early? Maybe!

According to recent reports, the good news is that you don’t have to play your Newly introduced Zealand version of GTA 6 only on your New Zealand PSN account. Instead, you can go to the settings menu on your NZ PSN account, open “Users and Accounts,” choose “Other,” and then select “Enable” in the “Console Sharing and Offline Play” section. This will let your regular U.S. PSN account access the game so all of your save data and trophy progress sticks with your main account.
