Russia's war on Ukraine: 'Americans want to have a deal whatever the cost for both Ukraine & Europe'

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (141 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that delano D'Souza is pleased to welcome Grégoire Roos, Director of the Europe and Russia and Eurasia Programmes at Chatham House. As European countries prepare to take part in the next round of Ukraine peace talks, Roos questions whether having five European representatives could weaken their negotiating position. "The other option is to realize that it dilutes the negotiating power of Europe. And I would lean towards that," he claims, proposing Finnish President Alexander Stubb as a possible representative. Roos also points to differences between Washington and European governments over negotiations with Russia. While "the Americans do absolutely want to have a deal whatever the cost for both Ukraine and Europe," he argues that European governments have little choice but to maintain pressure on Moscow, which has yet to abandon its "maximalist demands.".
