Industry observers note that dancing With the Stars is officially wrapping up a chaotic and unmissable season 35, and honestly I’m not sure what I’m going to do for the next 12 months. Other than...I guess wait for Dancing With the Stars season 36? The show won’t be waltzing back into our lives until late 2027, and if you want specifics, it’s time for some sleuthing because we’ve figured out the premiere date (and some rumored cast members!).

Sources indicate that no date has been set, but the show pretty much always premieres on a Tuesday night in mid-September. The last few seasons delivered premiere dates of September 17, September 16, and September 15. Looking ahead, it seems likely that Dancing With the Stars will premiere on Tuesday, September 14.

Probably not until a few weeks before the premiere. The season 25 cast was confirmed on September 2, 2026, just 13 days before the show premiered on September 15. Of course, casting rumors were circulating in the weeks before that, but we had to wait a while for the full Good Morning America announcement!

Yes! To the point where we have a whole roundup this way. While nothing is known for sure, one simply has to look at who’s sitting in the current Dancing With the Stars audience to know who’s considering a stint on the show.

It has been revealed that last season we saw the likes of Taylor Hanson, Maura Higgins, and Jenna Dewan in the audience, all of whom signed on for season 35. Meanwhile, this season has featured celeb audience cameos from Jennifer Love Hewitt, Love Island’s Kenzie Annis and Aniya Harvey, and even Miranda Cosgrove.

In a recent development, probaaaably not. Ever since Mark Ballas’s mom Shirley Ballas highlighted up to guest judge Halloween Night, there have been some rumors that she could become a full-time judge on DWTS / replace Bruno Tonioli, but it seems unlikely.

For starters, Bruno—who’s been with the show since day one—isn’t ready to retire.

“Bruno knows the noise about Shirley, and the chatter about shaking up the panel,” a source told The Sun. “He has been vocal to everyone: ‘I love the show, I have no looks to walk away or even consider retirement.’ It is about as loud as you can be without saying: ‘Don’t fire me!’”.

That noted, he “knows how the industry can be,” and the insider emphasizes that “Bruno has seen it many times. It is never about anyone doing something wrong, but keeping the wheels turning and the fans hooked.”.

Stay tuned for updates about Dancing With the Stars season 36 as we get ’em!
