Manchester United’s alternative targets to Mauricio Pochettino have been revealed.

The Red Devils were on the hunt for a latest manager after sacking Michael Carrick last week, with the former midfielder relieved of his duties after just one win in 11 matches.

It left the Premier League side mired in the top-flight relegation zone, and searching for their eighth permanent head coach since Sir Alex Ferguson’s retirement in 2013.

They turned to the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss after it became clear that he had a £7.5million release clause in his contract with US Soccer as head coach of the United States’ men’s side.

Now, The Athletic has reported the six other names that United’s hierarchy considered before landing the Argentine, who will take charge of his new club on Wednesday.

It is believed that the Red Devils explored Unai Emery’s availability, but the Aston Villa manager’s level of authority at his current club was understood to be too difficult to match.

Industry observers note that eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann, formerly of Newcastle and Bayern Munich respectively, were publicly-known candidates for United, but the former was decided against without any further consideration, while the latter’s lack of Premier League experience ultimately went against him. The same could be noted for Stuttgart’s Sebastian Hoeness.

The Athletic claims that United co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe held a personal interest in another ex-Chelsea and Tottenham figure, Antonio Conte, but the Italian’s desired level of control over club proceedings was believed to be ‘not the right fit’.

Como’s Cesc Fabregas, who masterminded the shock 4-1 victory over United in the Champions League earlier this term, was admired by chief executive Omar Berrada, but there were no further discussions over the former Arsenal and Chelsea midfielder.
