Google Maps has a pretty consistent style, but a latest option appears to be in the works with a darker “high-visibility” setting.

It has been revealed that the last major change to Google Maps styling came in 2023 (yes, time flies) when the platform rolled out a new color palette to all users. This was divisive among users, but ultimately worked out.

Now, Google appears to be working on another new shake-up.

The folks over at Android Authority spotted a fresh “high-visibility” toggle in the latest Google Maps update for Android, with the new style having darker colors for the map in both light and dark modes, as well as a purple line for your route compared to the blue we’re used to seeing.

The change was also spotted on Android Auto, with much darker colors (including a near-black dark mode) in both color settings for your car’s display. This is likely tied to this latest “High-visibility” setting, but it’s hard to say for certain – teardowns and enabling new features early can only tell us so much.

According to recent reports, this appears to all be controlled by a latest “Map style” setting in Google Maps, with “Standard” and “High-visibility” settings available. Functionally, everything remains the same, it’s just a new set of colors.

So, where the previous color changes were forced on all consumers, this one is at least optional, and the “High-visibility” naming seems like it’s targeted at those with difficulties in sight more so than just a general change for all users.
