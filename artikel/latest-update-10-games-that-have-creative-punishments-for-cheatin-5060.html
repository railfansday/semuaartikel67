Industry observers note that it’ll come as no surprise to any gamer that pretty much every game has a way to cheat, whether it's approved by the developers or otherwise. Over the years there have been some pretty creative and hilarious ways that developers have dealt with cheaters in all kinds of games from FPS games to the cozy ones.

While a lot of the punishments for cheating in more recent years are typical things like warnings or outright bans, there are some that take a more creative approach. Whether it’s a fun way to kill the player or an incredibly punishing turn of events for dark deeds, it’s always funny to see that happen.

This list consists of many games from all sorts of genres and ages, but they all have some interesting and hilarious punishments for what they perceive as cheating. It may not always be altering game files and could just be something players are able to abuse in game.

Sources indicate that way back in 1996 Crusader: No Regret came out following in the footsteps of its predecessor Crusader: No Remorse. The isometric gameplay combined with some great weapons and destructive environments made a very engaging combination.

The only problem was if you played the previous game and attempted to use any cheats from the last game Crusader: No Regret would have something to say about it. Players would be greeted with a message and teleported into a room with eight copies of the final boss.

In a recent development, heretic was created using the DOOM engine, but things were a bit mixed up and instead of the traditional DOOM world this one was steeped in fantasy elements. Everything revolved around the fantasy element as players could use magical weapons like the Elven Wand, and Phoenix Rod.

In a recent development, unfortunately for players who were well educated in the ways of DOOM, the developers had a surprise for anyone hoping to use DOOM cheats. Inserting the cheat for God Mode would instantly kill players, and the all weapons cheat would instead reduce you to using just a wooden staff.

With the recent remaster of Ocarina of Time there’s a lot of great details in the Legend of Zelda series coming to light. Some are just fun little tidbits or small things, but in Link’s Awakening the dev team decided to make things a little harder for anyone doing nefarious things with Link.

Link can steal some items from a shopkeeper by distracting him and then grabbing the items to take outside. Not only does doing this ruin Link’s reputation earning him the mark of thief by any other NPCs, that shopkeeper will also kill Link to settle the debt.

Traditional MMOs typically don’t play too well with cheaters, resulting in all kinds of punishments, but one MMO really stands out, EVE Online. This old school MMORPG is all about space battles and exploration in its entirely player driven world.

During a GM Week event there were public executions of cheaters who had been caught and the team opened up the destruction of these ships to any players who wanted to join. This turned it into an incredible spectacle that many will not soon forget.

The Sims series is probably one of the most prolific gaming franchises on the planet, being enjoyed by a huge demographic of people. Even in these cute games people try to cheat and The Sims devs were not going to let that slide even on the DS.

Industry observers note that if you attempted to use the internal system clock to skip time forward the game would notice and spawn an alien invasion that would cause a lot of problems. They become a persistent problem in the game and could require players to restart the game.

CD Projekt Red are known for their storytelling and incredible game design, but they don’t just stop being amazing there. While the devs respect when players are able to find something they hadn’t accounted for they didn’t stop them from doing it, they just got a bit creative.

Industry observers note that when The Witcher 3 originally came out there was an exploit that would let you kill cows in the starter area indefinitely giving you crazy amounts of money. To fix that they simply spawned a giant late game monster, a Chort, that would likely kill players as a punishment.

It has been revealed that there’s nothing quite like finding out a classic game has punishments for attempted cheating, and Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire is no exception. Despite being a game for mostly young people they weren’t going to stand by and let someone cheat in their game.

Industry observers note that pokemon, of course, has a way to mess with players who were able to manipulate game code although it's a bit different from others on the list. Instead of anything dangerous the game would spawn a Bad Egg in a box that cannot be moved creating an problem with inventory for the rest of the game.

Industry observers note that cheat codes in games have been around for longer than most gamers, so a lot of early games allowed players to put them in. Now just like some other games, they want to encourage you to still play the regular way and have to get a bit more creative about punishments.

Banjo-Kazooie took this to an extreme and offered players the ability to put in forbidden codes doing all kinds of silly things. If you put in too many Gruntilda will threaten to erase your save file, making this a very dangerous gamble if you aren’t careful.

Recent findings suggest that edmund can’t ever do something in half measures and Mewgenics is more than proof of that in almost every aspect. The game took a lot of things to extremes and dealing with cheaters is no exception.

Sources indicate that if you tried to save-scum, quitting out of something swiftly to reload a previous save, the game would know and you’d meet Steven. The first time you do this in a run he gives you a warning, but if you continue to do it you’ll no longer have control of the game and Steven will make all the choices.

Industry observers note that not many games have the sense of humor that The Stanley Parable has and this, of course, has to extend beyond the metaness of the game. This hilarious narrative game wants you to focus on the order of events and not have you screw that all up.

It has been revealed that if a player attempts to use console cheats the game takes notice and sends them to the Serious Room where the narrator has some choice words for them. Continuing to do it only makes the punishment longer and more ridiculous, much like everything in the game.
