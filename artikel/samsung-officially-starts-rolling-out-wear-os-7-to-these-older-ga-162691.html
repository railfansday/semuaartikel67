Following its debut on the Galaxy Watch 9 earlier this year, Samsung is finally rolling out Wear OS 7 to older Galaxy Watch models with its One UI 9 Watch update.

Samsung’s One UI 9 Watch update is largely focused on newly introduced health features including Vitals, Heart Health Score, Daily Cardio Load, Fitness Index, and improvements to both Sleep Coaching and Sleep Apnea alerts.

“Vitals” is perhaps the most useful day-to-day addition, with Samsung Health collecting data on key vital signs such as heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, skin temperature, and blood oxygen and establishing a range so you can see when your vitals are out of the usual range.

One UI 9 Watch is based on Wear OS 7, and also includes an improved Compass app with “Track Back,” a feature that helps people to follow their route back to a recorded point.

There’s also a upcoming “Portrait Watch Face” that can adjust the clock face around the subject of a photo.

Recent findings suggest that the Galaxy Watch 5 series and Watch FE should also be getting the update, but no beta is available for those devices. Samsung’s first Wear OS watches, the Galaxy Watch 4 series, will not get the update.
