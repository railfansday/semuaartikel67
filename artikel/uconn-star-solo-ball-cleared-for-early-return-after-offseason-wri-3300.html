It looks like Solo Ball will take the court again this season after all.

The UConn guard, who underwent wrist surgery this past offseason, has been cleared to return to full-contact practice, the school announced on Monday. While a specific timeline for his return isn't known just yet, Ball will "use the next few weeks as a ramp-up period." That would, barring any setbacks, likely mean that he'll be ready to go when Substantial East play gets going.

"We're excited to bring him back into the fold this season," head coach Dan Hurley noted in a statement. "When the decision was initially made for Solo to take a medical redshirt this season, the five-in-five rules weren't yet in place. After that significant development, along with tremendous progress in his rehab, we made the decision along with Solo and his family to bring him back for this season.".

Sources indicate that ball has been a key piece for the Huskies for years. He was a part-time starter as a freshman when they won the national championship in 2024, and was a huge part in their run to the national championship game in April, which they eventually lost to Michigan. Ball averaged 12.8 points and three rebounds per game last season.

In a recent development, ball injured his wrist in a game against Butler last December, though he missed minimal time and ended up returning to play through the rest of the year. But in April, after the season had ended, UConn announced that Ball was going to undergo surgery. That was supposed to knock him out for the entire 2026-27 campaign, and Ball was going to take a medical redshirt.

According to recent reports, but after the "tremendous progress" he's made after the surgery, both Hurley and Ball clearly feel that he's ready to go and make an impact again this season. He should provide a massive boost for the Huskies, too, especially after they've lost Nik Khamenia to a foot surgery.

Recent findings suggest that ball was a borderline NBA Draft prospect at the end of last season. A strong finish to the year would likely go a long way into getting him into the first round of next June's draft.

UConn, now No. 3 in the national rankings, holds a 4-0 record after big wins over Michigan and Ohio State in the first two weeks of the season. The Huskies will go up against No. 12 Arizona on Wednesday night, and then have No. 5 Duke waiting for them next week.
