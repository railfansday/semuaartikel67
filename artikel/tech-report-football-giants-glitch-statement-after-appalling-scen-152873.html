Fans have taken to social media and demanded an ‘enormous’ ban be handed out to one of Europe’s most well-known clubs.

On Thursday night, the latest round of fixtures in the Europa League and the Europa Conference League took place, with Premier League sides such as Brighton, Crystal Palace, Bournemouth and Sunderland all in action.

Although the English teams were not involved in any dramatic moments, the same can’t be stated for other teams around the continent.

For example, Besiktas fans were involved in an altercation with Georgian police during their match against Israeli club Hapoel Be’er-Sheva, due to a suspected pro-Palestinian banner.

There were crowd problems inside the Stade Velodrome, where Ligue 1 outfit Marseille were taking on Bulgarian club PFC Levski Sofia in the Europa League.

Marseille came away 1-0 winners thanks to Amine Gouiri’s 28th-minute penalty, but the match was marred by an ugly incident within the stands.

According to Goal, clashes broke out in the stands, resulting in PFC Levski Sofia suffering injuries. Footage on social media showed Marseille fans attacking the travelling Bulgarian supporters, with reports stating that the violence broke out prior to kick-off.

Goal stated: “The chaos escalated further when a fire extinguisher was discharged into the crowd, and projectiles were exchanged between the two sets of supporters near the visitor section.”.

In response to the incident, Marseille have since released a strongly worded statement condemning the violence that occurred on Thursday night.

It read: “Olympique de Marseille strongly condemns the violence that occurred on the fringes of the match against Levski Sofia.

Recent findings suggest that “The club is currently conducting a thorough analysis of the circumstances in which these incidents took place. OM is working in close collaboration with law enforcement authorities and remains fully at the disposal of the relevant authorities to ensure that full light is shed on these regrettable events.

“Olympique de Marseille wishes to emphasise that the safety of all spectators remains an absolute priority.”.

The president of France, Emmanuel Macron, also condemned the ‘appalling’ scenes inside the Stade Velodrome, saying that such ‘acts of violence have no place in sport nor in the public space’.

Taking to social media, fans have demanded Marseille be handed a ban; one said: “Absolutely appalling scenes before and throughout the game. Marseille should be punished severely for this!”.

Another commented: “None of the people being attacked are fighting back, must be a lifetime ban and a major fine for the club.”.

A third fan added: “Marseille banned from European football for 10 years minimum.”.

Someone else wrote: “Enough things have happened with Marseille now that this has to be a ban from European competition.”.

It has been revealed that A fifth said: “This has to be an enormous ban. It's simply not safe.”.
