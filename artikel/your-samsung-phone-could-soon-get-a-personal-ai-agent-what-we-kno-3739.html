Your Samsung phone could soon get a personal AI agent – what we know about Galaxy Claw

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Samsung’s Galaxy AI may be getting a cross-app intelligence upgrade, à la Google’s Gemini Intelligence and Apple’s Siri AI, according to an Android Authority report. Samsung is working on Galaxy Claw, which could make Galaxy AI more agentic and context-aware across your apps.

Also: Samsung mulls Galaxy AI usage limits and subscription tiers.

Industry observers note that right now, Galaxy AI lives inside your Galaxy devices and apps, but its smarts are limited to the apps it’s integrated with. Android Authority reports that Samsung’s Personal Agent Core is at the center of Galaxy Claw, connecting Galaxy AI to compatible apps and services, such as Calendar, Contacts, Messages, Gmail, Gallery, Phone, and Voice Recorder.

Sources indicate that android Authority found code that referred to Galaxy Claw in Samsung’s Personal Agent Core app, suggesting that Galaxy AI can complete cross-app tasks. For example, take the request: “Remind me to buy Mom a birthday card two days before her birthday.” An assistant without access to multiple apps will set a reminder to buy Mom a birthday card, but you might have to manually enter the date and time for the reminder to notify you.

With a more agentic assistant, it can access Mom’s contact card and her birthdate, calculate two days before her birthday, and remind you on that date. Like Gemini Intelligence and Siri AI, Personal Agent Core will need special permissions to access your sensitive information, such as messages, photos, and calls.

Also: 4 ways Claude Sheets beat using a Google Sheets connector.

Android Authority also reports that code strings in the app point to a recurring, scheduled tasks feature, which would allow owners to create custom automations and determine how often Galaxy AI should execute them, similar to Apple’s revamped Shortcuts.

Other code strings reveal a Daily Brief, Device Cleaner, Travel Planner, and a widget. An AI-powered daily brief is something I’ve been yearning for as an iPhone user. If I want a Siri-created brief, I’d have to build it via Shortcuts.

Recent findings suggest that gmail features a Gemini-powered daily brief, which compiles information from my email inbox and GChat messages to show me things I may have missed. I find it very useful for work, since my inbox is flooded and I often miss important emails.

Also: I’m a ChatGPT power user, but I won’t give it my wallet.

Samsung’s Galaxy ecosystem includes smartphones, smartwatches, smart rings, earbuds, and tablets, so there are plenty of opportunities for a more agentic Galaxy AI to bridge gaps across the devices in your personal ecosystem.

According to recent reports, additionally, Google Gemini integration is sweeping across Android (and iOS) apps, which might prompt Galaxy owners to choose the more robust AI assistant over Galaxy AI. If Samsung can find ways to make Galaxy AI more useful and exclusive to Galaxy users, it might win some people over. However, reports say Samsung is testing subscriptions for heavy Galaxy AI users, so more Galaxy AI integration might incur a fee.
