📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that I think we all knew it was going to be substantial, but this big? Rockstar has announced that GTA 6 sold a staggering 32.5 million copies during its preload period, setting a “fresh record across all platforms”.

In a recent development, that number is likely higher by now, because the press release’s wording implies it’s not counting launch day sales into the above total, which are guaranteed to be plentiful. (It’s only going to keep rising through Thanksgiving and Christmas, too.).

Rockstar says the game generated a frankly mind-boggling $3.79 billion on day one, making it the biggest entertainment launch in history. Estimates claim it cost about $1 billion to make.

It’s also eager to point out that the title is now the highest-rated game of all-time as well, with its near-perfect 99 Metacritic score nudging the N64 version of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time aside.

Recent findings suggest that “It is with immense gratitude that we enter a latest chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of GTA 6. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”.

The dev didn’t specify how sales were split between systems, but the expectation is the PS5 version sold best, with some estimates pegging it at around 75% of the total.

Sony was the primary marketing partner of the game, and promoted the fact it “plays best on PS5” (even though testing shows it’s broadly identical to the Xbox Series X version on base hardware).

In a recent development, this is just the beginning for GTA 6, of course, with no word on Rockstar’s prepares for the next iteration of GTA Online just yet. The game is also expected to launch on PC in 2027 as well.
