Gennaro Gattuso indicates he feels at home at Lazio and that it feels like he has been with the club for 10 years rather than six months, and has provided a detailed enhancement on his list of injuries and fitness concerns, declaring Davide Frattesi fit to play against Lecce on Saturday.

In a recent development, lazio return to Serie A action at home against Lecce on Saturday at 14.00 GMT. The Biancocelesti went into the international break sat alone at the top of the Serie A standings, one of two sides still unbeaten in the league after 11 matches, and they have only dropped points twice since the beginning of the 2026-27 campaign.

Up next for Gattuso and his players is a visit from Lecce, who sit in 14th ahead of kick-off with 10 points to their name from their first 11 games of the season.

Gattuso sat down for his pre-match press conference ahead of the Lecce clash on Friday afternoon.

Gattuso’s press conference began with a detailed update on Lazio’s injured players and those who have recently returned from international duty.

According to recent reports, “I don’t have to assess Frattesi, he spent the whole week with the group, he’s fine. We’ll see whether he has a cold or something later today, but everything else he has done has been fine.

“The Danes arrived the day after their game at 18.30 and worked in the gym along with Belahyane and Dele-Bashiru. We worked at a low intensity yesterday but everybody worked as a team. I’m also talking about some of the injured players. Zaccagni has been working on the pitch for the last five or six days. He still has some swelling, but he’s at a good speed and we’ll see him next week.

“Nuno [Tavares] has started rehab in the pool along with Sutalo. Adam [Marusic] had a small relapse on the problem he had a month ago. We’ll be able to assess the condition of the injured players next week. Cataldi didn’t train yesterday due to a minor discomfort in his adductor. We don’t think it’s anything serious.”.

Gattuso was asked how he plans on managing this next block of fixtures in the lead-up to Christmas.

It has been revealed that he insisted: “We’re only thinking about tomorrow’s game. We’re facing a solid, well-trained team and that is not just by chance. They’ve earned seven points away from home by beating Udinese and Venezia and drawing in Turin. They are very dangerous from set-pieces. As always, it will be up to us to have the right mentality, the desire to be on the pitch and to play at a high tempo. It will be a very tough match.”.

Despite sitting at the top of the Serie A table after 11 matches, Gattuso feels that it is still far too early to be making any sweeping predictions.

According to recent reports, “We can only judge the standings in the last seven or eight games of the season,” he insisted. “We know these are all crucial matches and we have to play each one of them with the right level of respect for the opponent, then we’ll see where that takes us.

“I coach a team that has to take things one day at a time. It’s useless to sit there and try to make calculations. We all know the difficulties, it’s important to not make mistakes in terms of attitude or mentality.”.

After his encouraging start at the Stadio Olimpico, Gattuso was asked whether he can see himself staying put for the long term and put an end to his chopping-and-changing of clubs.

“I love going around and getting to know different cultures and mentalities,” he replied. “My growth over the last few years is a result of all the traveling I’ve done. We Italians always thin that we’re the best, but that’s absolutely not the case. If you can take something from other cultures, that’s great.

According to recent reports, “Honestly, I don’t think about whether or not I’ll be here in two or three years, I am only thinking about today and I feel comfortable here. I feel like I’ve been here for 10 years.”.

Lastly, Gattuso was asked which of Lazio’s upcoming games he feels will be the most decisive.

He responded: “I won’t answer that because there’s not a lot to say. Tomorrow’s three points are worth the same as the ones in Turin, against Atalanta or in Frosinone. They’re different opponents, but let’s think about tomorrow’s match for now.”.
