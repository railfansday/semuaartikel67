Recent findings suggest that the Substantial Game is coming to the Big Screen. Technically the biggest screens.

Industry observers note that super Bowl LXI is set to play live on IMAX screens across North America, as well as in some international markets, specifically Australia, New Zealand, and Brazil. The game takes place on Sunday, February 14, 2027, or Valentine’s Day 2027, and now you can convince your partner that the Valentine’s Day date night is still alive because you can go out to the movie theater to watch the game instead of stay at home.

In a recent development, IMAX will screen everything from the pregame and postgame coverage, the full four-hour presentation of the game itself, and of course the Halftime Show headlined by Jay-Z, all beginning at 6 pm ET. ESPN’s Monday Night Football broadcast team of Joe Buck, Troy Aikman, Lisa Salters, and Laura Rutledge will be the ones you hear in the theater, so no luck if you’re a Peyton and Eli or Pat McAfee fan. The only thing we don’t know is who’s playing in the game just yet, but this Chicago Bears fan is feeling pretty good about his team’s chances. According to recent reports, tickets are expected to go on sale in January. Specific theater locations and ticketing information will be declared in the coming weeks.

This is the first time IMAX is screening something as massive as the Super Bowl, but it’s not the first time IMAX has played live sporting events in its theaters. IMAX also screened the opening ceremony for the Paris Olympics, it this year did a series of F1 races in partnership with Apple TV, and events like NHL games and even the NBA Finals have played internationally. This is on a much bigger scale as well, as this will be available in hundreds of IMAX screens across the country.

According to recent reports, ABC, Disney, and ESPN hadn’t been in the rotation for the Super Bowl for a long time, so they’ve been going all out for their first go around. Disney is making the game available via broadcast on ABC, as well as on ESPN and Univision, and also for streaming on Disney+. ESPN is also doing an alternate telecast on ESPN 2 with Peyton and Eli Manning as they do for “Monday Night Football” throughout the regular season. And it was previously unveiled that “The Pat McAfee Show” is getting in on the fun with its own Super Bowl show on ESPNU and “Playbook with Next Gen Stats” on NFL Network.

In a recent development, this is really a boon for theaters to do added business on a day and weekend when most theaters are pretty empty and the new movie options are few and far between. We’ll see though how the box office responds and how many people relocate their Super Bowl parties to their local multiplex.

“Watching the Super Bowl with your fellow fans is one of the great shared experiences in sports, and this first-of-its-kind collaboration with the NFL and IMAX takes it to a historic upcoming scale,” stated Ken Potrock, President, Major Event Integration for The Walt Disney Company. “With ESPN’s first Super Bowl, we’re reaching fans across an unprecedented range of platforms and meeting them wherever they want to be, with unique moments that only Disney and ESPN can deliver.”.

“The Super Bowl is a one-of-a-kind global cultural event imbued with spectacle, sound, drama, and heroes — all the makings of a great IMAX experience,” noted Rich Gelfond, CEO of IMAX. “This is a milestone for IMAX that further elevates our standing as a premier global platform for entertainment and events, and we’re excited to work in partnership with Disney, ESPN and the NFL to give football fans an unforgettable night.”.
