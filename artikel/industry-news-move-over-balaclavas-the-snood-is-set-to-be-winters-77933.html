📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It was just a few years ago that the balaclava was everywhere. Fashion people referred to it as the winter headpiece you absolutely needed. But now, I’ve noticed a latest star, and that’s the snood, alongside the hooded scarf. Most people over the years have blended these three headpieces, but I’m here to inform the masses: you’re probably wearing a snood or hooded scarf now, not a balaclava.

Balaclavas are a full-coverage piece of headgear that wraps around the entire head, neck, and face (leaving only the eyes, nose, or mouth exposed), whereas a snood is a continuous circular loop or tube of fabric designed primarily to insulate the neck and cover the top of the head.

According to recent reports, balaclavas tend to be closer to the face, similar in fit to a ski mask, while snoods are loose-fitting and have a sort of cowl design to them. According to the winter sports community, balaclavas are better for skiing and extreme cold, but for most of us, we’re just looking to warm up a little with our winter accessories.

If you’re ready to wear your own snood this winter season, I’ve gathered some stylish inspo below to help build out those mood boards.

According to recent reports, this brown snood moment with a light beige trench coat is peak winter outfit. The headpiece pulls the whole cozy vibes together and gives ultimate cool girl.

According to recent reports, as a Floridian girlie, shorts in the winter are my kind of speed. Throw on those uber warm tights I see scrolling down my TikTok ‘for you’ page and you’re in business. The hooded scarf just elevates the whole moment too and keeps your head warm, which is ultimately the goal.

Colorful snoods aren’t always top of the list, especially if you’re looking for a headpiece that can blend with all your outfits. But if you’re hoping to bring a bit more fun to your winter style, then grabbing a snood like this one above is the way to do it.

Sources indicate that monochromatic is always in, but getting super close to a full black look and throwing in a light-colored headpiece is my personal winter hack. It brightens up the whole outfit, but doesn’t a neutral color like the one above still keep the look uniform and stylish.
