Mauricio Pochettino said to “give him time” to make Manchester United the city’s dominant club again after taking charge of his last match in charge of the United States.

The US beat Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati to wrap up an 8-0 aggregate victory in their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final.

AC Milan forward Christian Pulisic opened the scoring with a spectacular overhead kick and teenager Julian Hall doubled the hosts’ lead before half-time.

Pochettino will take charge of Manchester United for the first time against Liverpool on Sunday after being appointed to succeed Michael Carrick, and arrived at the club’s Carrington training ground on Wednesday afternoon as he began work as their newly introduced head coach.

Pochettino, who has signed a deal until the summer of 2029, jetted into Manchester after the victory over Haiti and was whisked to the club’s training base to meet United staff and start preparations his first match.

His final message as USA manager was towards United fans, asking for patients as he looked to restore the footballing hierarchy in Manchester to what it used to be.

Told by former Manchester City loanee DaMarcus Beasley that “Manchester is blue, it will always be blue” in the TNT Sports studio after the game, Pochettino responded: “You will see. Give me time.

“The new challenge is a substantial challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenalin you want to feel.

“We need to add our common sense… we know the situation is not a situation that deserves to be a club like Manchester United, the fan base is amazing.”.

Pochettino heads to Old Trafford having led the US to the last 16 at this year’s World Cup on home soil.

Sources indicate that “The mentality is to learn from everyone,” Pochettino told reporters. “You need to have an open mind because I think the players can teach us everyday, we are much better coaching staff now.

“Football is about attitude, and it’s about the approach. It’s about connection, feelings and communication.”.
