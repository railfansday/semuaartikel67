In a recent development, it took me until I turned 30 to realize I was a vest guy. Turns out a fleece vest was the key to non-bulky winter layering. It’s what I was needing this whole time, a cornerstone piece. It gets me through the crisp fall mornings and it’s easy to shed for my afternoon walk in the sunshine. Now, I can’t get through a winter without adding another to my collection.

Versatility is the name of the game here. You can dress up any fleece vest with a button-up and breathable chinos, or keep it casual with, say, a long-sleeved tee and some jeans. And really, it’s the only thing that can fit between a bulky sweater and a winter jacket.

Industry observers note that there are tons of fleece vests on the market, so which one do you choose? Well, you don’t have to choose just one for starters. I like to have a couple around in different colors and weights, depending on the weather and my outfit choice. So regardless of if you’re on your first one, or it’s time for your fiftieth, you can shop these nine handpicked fleece vests that I and our other editors swear by.

Basics don’t get more durable than Patagonia. This is your timeless, basic option that'll last you many winters to come. Secure it in one of six versatile colors.

In addition to an adjustable waist, three pockets, and a full front zip, this vest is made from recycled fabrics, so you can have peace of mind knowing that you're doing good while looking good.

Why pay over $500 for a fleece vest from Canada Goose? Why indeed. Probably because you want the softest Italian-spun Poly Cotton Fleece and are actually able to feel the difference. It’s got everything you’d want, including a chest pocket.

Recent findings suggest that made from recycled scraps of Smartwool’s Merino wool, this is the softest fleece vest that won’t cost you $500. The rest of the materials are recycled and reprocessed too, so you get a dash of peace of mind with your comfort.

It has been revealed that when you look at vests like this, you think North Face. The Glacier Fleece is an especially light option, perfect for the days when you need more than three layers.
