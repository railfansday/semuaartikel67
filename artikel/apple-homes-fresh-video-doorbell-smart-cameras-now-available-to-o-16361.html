In a recent development, today Apple has launched two Home products: HomeView and a upcoming Apple TV 4K. Additionally, the first three accessories in the Apple Home Collection are now available to order: a video doorbell and two smart home security cameras.

Whether you’re interested in diving into the smart home market for the first time, or you simply want to upgrade your setup, Apple has several latest accessories worth considering.

Apple Home Collection is a curated set of accessories that Apple co-developed in partnership with LG and Schneider. They are designed for seamless setup in the Home app with no third-party apps or accounts required, and support all the latest Apple Home technologies.

In total, Apple has nearly 10 accessories coming—from smart thermostats to indoor sensors, a smart lock, security cameras, and more.
