Team Cherry has announced a livestream that is set to feature an patch on Hollow Knight: Silksong's Sea of Sorrow DLC.

GTA 6 may have released last week, but Team Cherry has returned to remind you that Rockstar Games isn't the only developer to have released a long-awaited title after a development cycle that became a meme.

It's still wild to think about, but the DLC-turned-full-sequel has been out for over 12 months after years of silkposting, and thankfully it looks like its DLC – which was announced shortly after launch with a 2026 release date, before striking fear into the hearts of many when that date vanished – won't take quite as long to arrive, as Team Cherry is set to give us an revision on it this week.

Industry observers note that join us in 48 hours for Cherry Time! Get ready for a bunch of exciting announcements, plus an patch on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow! Premiering here: youtu.be/M-QUgdcBpZU.

In a post on BlueSky, Team Cherry announced its own Nintendo Direct-equivalent, "Cherry Time!" The stream is set to kick off at 7am PT | 10am ET | 3pm GMT on November 27, 2026, with the post reading "Get ready for a bunch of exciting announcements, plus an enhancement on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow!".

In a recent development, it's worth noting that Team Cherry said during the initial announcement that we wouldn't know more about the DLC until "shortly before Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow releases," so there's a chance we could be looking at more Hollow Knight Silksong in a matter of days. When the Silksong reveal happened, it was two weeks before the game's release date, so hopefully it'll be no longer than that.

Of course, I do have to flag the terrifying idea that Team Cherry will announce that Sea of Sorrow will be turned into a full game….
