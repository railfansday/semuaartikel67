Wyze is today launching the Window Bird Cam, a new $70 camera that has a bird feeder and can identify bird species, keep a log, and more.

The Wyze Window Bird Cam comes in two parts. There’s a stick-up camera that mounts to the inside of your window with a shade to reduce reflections, similar to the Wyze Window Cam that rolled out last year. The other half mounts to the outside of your window with suction cups and includes a tray for birdseed.

The idea here is to attract birds direct to the camera’s view, with Wyze further adding a new “Bird Watch” feature to its app which can identify bird species and keep a log of what species have stopped by and how many birds are using the feeder.

It has been revealed that the camera’s lens is tuned for focus distances of around 4-6 inches and has a higher frame rate of 30fps. The feeder tray has drainage and there’s an add-on perch for $15 extra.

“Bird Watch” is free for 3 months, but costs $6.99/month (or $69.99/year) after that. Free users still get live view and bird detection, but no species ID, a limited log, and no cloud recordings of bird visits (there is a microSD card slot for local recording).
