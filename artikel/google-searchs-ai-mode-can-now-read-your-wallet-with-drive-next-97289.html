Google Search’s AI Mode can now read your Wallet, with Drive next

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google’s graphic for connecting Wallet, Drive and other apps to Personal Intelligence in Search.

Sources indicate that google is rolling out an opt-in connection between Google Wallet and Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode, with Drive and the Gemini app to follow in the coming weeks. The read-only Wallet link lets AI Mode suggest ways to cut spending and use card rewards.

It has been revealed that google Search’s AI Mode can now use your Google Wallet to give more personal answers, the company confirmed on Monday. Drive and the Gemini app will follow in the coming weeks. It is the latest step in Google’s AI overhaul of Search, unveiled at I/O in May.

The opt-in connections are part of Personal Intelligence, which already lets AI Mode draw on Gmail, Calendar and Photos. With Wallet linked, AI Mode can suggest ways to trim spending and make the most of cash-back and reward points on cards stored in Wallet. Google says the link is read-only, so AI Mode cannot move, manage or invest money.

Sources indicate that with Drive connected, AI Mode can use documents, spreadsheets and PDFs, for example to draw up a strategy for tax season. Linking the Gemini app will let ideas explored in one carry over to the other.

Industry observers note that google rolled out the same Wallet link in Gemini for US users ten days ago, and OpenAI has let some ChatGPT users link bank accounts since May. Users can switch each app on or off under Connected Apps in Search Personalization, and a new icon will show what personal context a response used.
