Data from XRPL explorer Bithomp reveals that the Batch and Ticket features on the XRP Ledger (XRPL) are increasingly being adopted, with both setting newly introduced daily records in November.

The data indicates an increasing use of two XRPL features intended to enhance transaction flexibility and efficiency on the network.

According to recent reports, batch activity reached a fresh daily high for the month at 1.962 million on November 3, 2026. Ticket activity came next at 1.553 million on November 7, 2026, also setting a new November record. The increase indicates that these features are being embraced in the XRPL ecosystem. Batch functionality allows multiple transactions to be grouped and executed together and was activated on the XRPL mainnet on October 9, 2026.

The growth in batch activity, which neared 2 million on November 3, 2026, comes less than one month after its launch.

It has been revealed that this is significant, as Batch is a prerequisite or accelerant for several high-value XRPL use cases already under contract or in the active pipeline, including atomic multi-party settlements. Institutional workflows (custody transfers, DvP settlement, cross-border payment finalization) require two or more accounts to act simultaneously.

On the other hand, tickets provide a way to reserve transaction sequence numbers, giving people more flexibility in managing transactions.

XRP Ledger metrics are in the green, with the transaction count up 75.9% to 4.3 million; active accounts jumped 128%, while XRP burned as fees rose 136%. Payments volume surged 137% in the last 24 hours to $1 billion, according to XRPScan data, while NFT mints rose 240% in the same timeframe.

Not only is the XRP Ledger seeing increased activity, but Ripple led stablecoin market cap growth by issuer over the past 24 hours, up $90.9 million, 4x the next issuer, multipli.fi, at $21.3 million.

It has been revealed that ripple's RLUSD market cap has grown 96% this year, across Ethereum and the XRP Ledger, to $2.7 billion from $1.4 billion in January. Most of that growth came in July, when Ripple debuted Ripple Mint. RLUSD market cap stood just under $1.5 billion that month and rose to $2.3 billion by August.

Recent findings suggest that in a milestone for the network, one year ago today, the first US ETF to hold only XRP began trading. Since then, US spot XRP ETFs have accumulated over 1.16 billion XRP.
