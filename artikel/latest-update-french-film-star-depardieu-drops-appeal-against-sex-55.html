Latest Update: French film star Depardieu drops appeal against sexual conviction

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (109 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that french film star Gérard Depardieu on Monday dropped his appeal against a conviction for sexually assaulting two women during the filming of a movie in 2021 and issued "sincere apologies" for his past behaviour on set. "For a long time on film sets, I hid behind provocation and ribald humour," Depardieu, 77, said in the statement, withdrawing his appeal on the day it was set to go to trial. "I believed my remarks and some of my gestures would be without consequences" for those involved, he said. "They were not, particularly coming from a powerful figure such as I once was.".
