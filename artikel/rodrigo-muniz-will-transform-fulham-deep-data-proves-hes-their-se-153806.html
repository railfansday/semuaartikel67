Rodrigo Muniz can help Fulham navigate their issues in front of goal.

The Cottagers are one of the worst in the Premier League when it comes to scoring, with only Sunderland (eight) and Nottingham Forest (nine) scoring fewer than Fulham's 11 goals.

Brazilian striker Muniz has not been favoured by Alvaro Arbeloa this season at all, with the 25-year-old managing just one start from nine appearances in the league.

However, his lack of opportunities is becoming increasingly difficult to justify given Fulham's struggles in the final third.

And his recent performances have only further added to the debate over Fulham's striker position.

With data showing he is the better option going forward for the London side.

Even in his limited minutes, Muniz has already proven to be better in front of goal compared to Gonzalo Garcia.

In 179 minutes, Muniz has racked up 0.99 xG, with an xGOT of 1.61 as per FotMob, showing that with his shot placement, he improves the quality of his chances.

From those chances, he has netted two goals in the Premier League, which is more than his Spanish counterpart has scored this campaign.

Garcia has been frustrating in front of goal, with his shooting erratic. In direct contrast to Muniz, he has drastically made his own chances worse.

From 4.07 xG, he has managed just 0.95 xGOT, suggesting his shot placement has significantly reduced the quality of the chances he has been given.

If Fulham are to get wins over the line, they cannot afford to continue trusting Garcia, who has started 10 of the 11 games so far.

It is time for Muniz to get a long run of games leading the line, and the London side may finally begin climbing the table.

Anytime a player's data is analysed despite limited minutes, there will be concerns over the sample size.

However, throughout his time in the Premier League, he has proven his clinical ability.

In 2023-24, he had an xG and xGOT difference of +1.01, while in 2024-25 that total stood at +2.41.

Last season was not quite there, as he had a negative total of -2.48.

But nonetheless, the trend is that he is often able to make more out of his chances than the original value suggests.
