Browser security events from Windows, macOS, iOS and Android now appear in the same Devicie console IT teams use to manage and secure their Microsoft Intune fleet.

Sources indicate that SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Devicie, a continuous operating layer for Microsoft Intune, today revealed that its Microsoft Edge for Business Reporting Connector now brings in security events from Edge for Business on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. Announced at Microsoft Ignite 2026, the update lets IT and security teams see browser risk across their whole fleet, laptops and phones included, in the same place they already manage device health and compliance.

It has been revealed that much of today's work, and much of its risk, happens in the browser. Edge for Business security connectors make that activity visible, and Devicie puts it next to the device. Devicie treats the browser as an endpoint: it deploys Edge policy and keeps it in place, and the security events Edge for Business reports show whether that policy is working. Until now, that view was limited to a single platform. It now extends to Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, with no extra agent on the device.

Built on Edge for Business security events, the Devicie Reporting Connector reports on five kinds of browser activity:.

Recent findings suggest that "Most IT teams can tell you whether a device is compliant. Far fewer can tell you what is happening in the browser on that device, especially across a mixed fleet," said Alex Hesterberg, CEO, Devicie. "Most of the systems consumers engage with today, including AI platforms, are through the browser. Bringing Edge for Business events from Windows, macOS, iOS and Android into Devicie gives teams one place to see whether their policies are working.".

Built for every team that manages endpoints.

Recent findings suggest that in-house IT teams, security teams and managed service providers (MSPs) use Devicie to run their Intune environments. Each tenant connects to Devicie with its own reporting key, so MSPs see every customer's browser events in one multi-tenant portal while each customer's data stays separate. Because Devicie also deploys Edge policy for extensions and security controls, teams can act on what they see in the same console, for example blocking an unsanctioned extension and confirming its removal.

Industry observers note that cross-platform reporting is available now to Devicie customers [confirm before release]. The connector requires a Devicie subscription, and Microsoft provides Edge for Business connectors at no additional cost. It is set up in the Edge management service in the Microsoft 365 admin center and requires a supported version of Edge on each platform; current requirements are listed on Microsoft Learn. Setup takes a few steps: copy the endpoint and reporting key from Devicie, enter them in the Edge management service, and choose which events to send.

It has been revealed that devicie's connector was one of the first in Microsoft's Edge for Business security connector framework, available in preview when the framework introduced in April 2025. Microsoft named the Devicie connector generally available at RSAC 2026, and Devicie released its full Edge for Business report to all customers in May 2026.
