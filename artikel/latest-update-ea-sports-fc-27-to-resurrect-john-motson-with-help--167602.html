Latest Update: EA Sports FC 27 to resurrect John Motson with help from generative AI, because apparently football games need someone yelling about goals from beyond the grave

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that in today's instance of a sentence I never thought I'd write, English football commentator John Motson has followed in the footsteps of Darth Vader. In the latest instance of a game featuring a dead celebrity's voice reanimated with help from generative AI, EA Sports FC 27 will soon have Motson offering words about passes and shots that clank off the bar despite him having died in 2023.

EA revealed Motson's resurrection for FC 27 to the BBC, with the deceased commentator's voice being made up of combination of old recording sessions and generative AI. The publishers got permission from Motson's widow and son to do this, and told the BBC they'd not have gone ahead if Motson's family - whom EA have paid an undisclosed sum for the rights to use Motson's likeness and recreate his voice - hadn't been on board.

In a recent development, "My dad was the least technological person in the world," Motson's son Fred said. "Till his dying day, he had a typewriter on his desk. So I don't think he would have quite understood what the AI was doing technologically - but I think, on the other hand, he was always really keen to get involved in these kinds of projects.".

According to recent reports, EA senior game design director Mat Prior said AI has been used for the likes of lines in which Motson talks about modern players who don't feature in the old excerpts of him calling games. You'd think that might've been the point at which EA began to question why it's necessary to resurrect Motson for the purpose of commentating on games featuring a number of players he might never have watched while he was alive, meaning we don't know what unique calls he might have made in response to seeing them strut their stuff. Alas, it seems that wasn't the case.

Industry observers note that "From a gaming standpoint, it's something we've used for many years - like machine learning to help drive animation systems and so on and so forth," Prior added. "It's not there to replace commentators in any way shape or form...there's certainly no future looks to replace anyone with AI." It's worth noting EA already use genAI to replicate the voices of some living commentators with their permission, with the justification given being that it helps cut down on the amount of work required to record hours of a real human saying different player names with different inflections.

In a recent development, motson served as a commentator on FIFA 96 through 05, but looks to have been absent from the series since that point aside from briefly popping up for a return in FIFA 19. So, aside from that late 2010s one-off, Motson hadn't been part of one of EA's football games for about two decades prior to this AI-enabled resurrection for FC 27. I'd argue that makes it a lot more difficult to make the case for this use of genAI. It's not like the tech's being used to help maintain continuity with a voice that's always been part of the series' furniture, as is the case with Witcher 3 devs CD Projekt Red's modification of Geralt's Polish voice via genAI in the Songs of the Past DLC following actor Jacek Rozenek's stroke. In that case, the actor was also alive to give his direct permission.

Whether families or estates should be able to permit the use of AI cloning on the voices of actors or performers who didn't give written permission for it while they were alive is a matter of much contention. Actor Peter Caulfield, who's been arguing for greater protections against AI voice cloning through a campaign called Save Our Voices Now, argued in the BBC piece that it should only be allowed in cases where living people have signed deals to allow it.

Sources indicate that personally, I think games should probably do without genAI-fuelled celebrity ressurections, especially in scenarios where there seems no pressing need to do so. Even Tupac's likeness being used without any AI bollocks in RGG Studio's upcoming historical beat 'em up Stranger Than Heaven feels unsettling. AI Motson will be added to FC 27 as an optional commentator on November 26th.
