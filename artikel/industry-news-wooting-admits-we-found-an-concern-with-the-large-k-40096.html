📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that so you may or may not have heard this, but Wooting has a Large Knob coming out soon. Think a traditional keyboard volume dial, but, yeah, large. And also available by itself without a keyboard. Well, Wooting opened up preorders for its $40/£34 Lekker Knob Large this year and the 80HE+, which was expected to launch in 2026, has now been pushed back to 2027. Significant news, I know.

In a video titled "We found an problem with the Large Knob…", Wooting co-founder and CEO Calder Limmen announced that the Large Knob has been delayed by at most four months (it has moved back to March, 2027).

Limmen says he wanted to ship in December but "at the same time, I also had to make a decision and delay the Large Knob so that we don't ship a Large Knob with issues".

Sources indicate that limmen notes 30% of the Large Knobs have a defect and they don't know why. They reportedly had a torque matter where larger production batches couldn't get the same level of resistance from each knob, which was then fixed by mounting the ball bearing inside the knob differently and changing some internals. But then, with the fresh batch, using a temperature chamber for testing, they spotted inaccurate testing in 30% of devices.

In a recent development, effectively, the representation of the wheel in the software and the wheel itself had different values. If you simply have to swivel the wheel to use it, that wouldn't be a problem, but the Large Knob reportedly uses very specific movements in its software, so it needs absolute clarity over where exactly the dial is pointing.

That all means the next few months will involve Wooting engineers looking inside the Large Knob, performing more temperature tests and trying to figure out why this defect is happening.

It has been revealed that limmen also clarifies that if they figure out the problem much sooner, they will ship it earlier than January. The March 2027 release date is effectively a worst-case scenario.

It has been revealed that as for those who have ordered the Large Knob, Wooting are handling the delays in different ways depending on how you ordered. If you picked up the Large Knob alongside a keyboard (other than the 80HE+, which comes with the knob built in), Wooting will ship the knob and keyboard separately, paying for the shipping. If you bought the keyboard with the built-in knob, it has also been delayed to March 2027, but will come with the travel case for free now.

If you bought the Large Knob by itself, you will get "some goodies [added] to your order." It's not clear yet what those goodies are, but a few extra is a nice way to make up for a delay.
