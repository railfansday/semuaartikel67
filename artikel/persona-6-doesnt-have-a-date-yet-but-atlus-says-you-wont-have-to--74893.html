According to recent reports, there's been a lot of talk around how long GTA fans have been waiting for the latest entry to launch, but Persona isn't actually that far behind. Persona 5 – in its original state – released over a decade ago on 15th September 2016, and although Persona 6 has now been officially announced, we're none the wiser as to when it will actually come out.

It's likely to finally land at some point in 2027 after the dust has settled from Persona 4 Revival, but according to a recent interview with general producer Kazuhisa Wada (thanks, Nintendo Everything), fans won't have to wait quite so long for the inevitable Persona 7.

"We will continue to cherish what makes Persona unique, while also embracing changes in the times and needs, and taking on latest challenges. We aim to be a brand that can grow for 5, 10 years, and beyond.

"Among those projects, the one we’re currently working on is Persona 6. While there’s still limited information I can share, we want to prove the results of all the challenges we’ve faced so far through the game itself. With that in mind, we’re creating something even better than before, and we’re feeling a real sense of accomplishment as we work on it.

"And Persona 6 is not the end. We are working on various things to ensure that you don’t have to wait as long for the next installment as we have in the past. And in order to make Persona an even bigger IP, we are constantly thinking about creative ideas and developments that will not disappoint your expectations. Please look forward to what’s to come from Persona.”.

In a recent development, we will look forward to it, Wada-san, thank you sir. Let's keep the focus on Persona 4 Revival and Persona 6 for now though, hm?

It's not a surprising statement, mind you. Game development is taking longer than ever these days, which also makes it more expensive than ever. So a lot of publishers are looking into potential solutions to cut down on dev time and increase the cadence of releases. As long as it doesn't involve handing everything off to AI, we're fine with that.

Persona 4 Revival is presently scheduled to launch on Switch 2 on 20th May 2027, a few months after other platforms. As for Persona 6, an imminent reveal has been rumoured recently thanks to a backend update on the official website. Hardly concrete, but it's a start.
