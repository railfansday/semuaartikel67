A bad bra definitely ruins an outfit—and possibly even your whole day. This might sound like an overly dramatic hot take, but any woman (and by that we mean every woman) who has ever had to corral her cleavage with unflattering undergarments will tell you: the struggle is real.

We’ve all been there. Maybe you found mint-condition, underpriced, vintage mini skirt at the local thrift, you’ve been eyeing that bodycon dress, or you bought a newly introduced suit that screams lady boss in time for a dream job interview. But then you can’t find a bra that works with the wardrobe so you settle for one that gets close. And when you pair that perfect ensemble with that imperfect undergarment, it throws you off your game. You see every lump, bump and misshapen cup—and you believe everyone else sees them too. You feel every quarter of an inch that the band rides up and the girls slide down. You fuss with painful straps and migrating cups. All of that will crush the confidence that the new clothes emboldened.

In a recent development, with the launch of the upcoming Flawlessly Smooth bra, Vanity Fair Lingerie promises it doesn’t have to be that way. Below, discover four ways these versatile full-coverage bras support you as the best base layer in the biz by bringing sexy to your back, Flawless Flex to your front, and supportive straps to your shoulders.

Created with Beauty Back four-way stretch technology, these bras take on every petulant protrusion and smooth over unwanted bumps so clothes can lie flat or flow uninterrupted across your back. Cups are lightly lined but have enough padding for the modest to maintain a smooth, elegant look.

Whether you’re a card-carrying member of the itty-bitty titty committee or are a classical Venus type, slight cup size fluctuations are totally normal—especially just before or during your period or in perimenopause. To combat that bit of biology, Vanity Fair Lingerie engineered built-in flex panels under the bust in the cups that shrink and expand as needed. They’re barely noticeable but hugely helpful when cups runneth over.
