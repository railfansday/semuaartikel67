Aston Villa and Newcastle are ready to battle for a January deal to sign a standout forward after watching him during the international break.

Villa had a busy summer window as they saw a number of key players depart, with Unai Emery making ten upcoming signings to plug the gaps and try and strengthen, including bringing in attackers Nicolas Jackson, Johan Manzambi, Alejandro Garnacho, and Ibrahim Mbaye,.

Newcastle, on the other hand, had a disappointing window after losing Eddie Howe, Anthony Gordon, Bruno Guimaraes, and Sandro Tonali, with a host of young players joining Matthias Jaissle's side, who have struggled to make an instant impact in the Premier League.

Both sides are keen to add more forward options to the squad in January as they look to climb up the table following mixed starts to their domestic campaign, and it has now emerged that both teams had representatives present for Portugal's 3-2 win over Wales in Cardiff this week.

TEAMtalk report that while at the game, both clubs were taking a very close look at winger Carlos Forbs, who presently plays his football in Belgium with Club Brugge.

The 22-year-old came off the substitutes' bench wearing the famous No.7 shirt, the first person to do so since Cristiano Ronaldo's retirement from international duty, and scored the winning goal with a fine solo effort, as he dribbled past defenders before crashing the ball into the bottom corner with his left foot.

A number of other Premier League clubs are also noted to be keen, but it's Villa and Newcastle who are most interested at this stage, and a move away from Club Brugge in January has not been ruled out as a possibility.

It's unclear how much any deal would cost, but there is confidence that an agreement could be reached. Forbs has Premier League experience, having spent a season on loan with Wolves in 2024, but he struggled for regular game time and that is not considered to be a fair reflection of his talent or fit for the division.

Forbs' winner for Portugal against Wales was his first international goal for the senior team, and the fact he was given the number 7 shirt shows the belief they have in him, but also the confidence he has in himself.

A direct and pacey winger, Forbs has the skillset to be a genuine gamechanger at the highest level, which is a sentiment shared by former international boss Roberto Martinez.

"He has a very special profile. He's a fast, vertical player, but he can play on both wings. I think that's very notable.".

Sources indicate that with Garnacho struggling at Villa, and Newcastle failing to truly replace Anthony Gordon in the summer, a move for Forbs makes a lot of sense for both sides. A deal is being considered for the winter window, and if an agreement can be found with Club Brugge, then it could be one of the best signings either team could make mid-season to improve their forward options.
