Arsenal lads were in action, and on the scoresheet, last night as England beat Spain 3-2 in Madrid. Ezri Konsa made it 2-0 with a close range header, Spain might wonder who was marking him (I think it was supposed to be Marc Cucurella) but the movement was really sharp. Then a couple of minute later, Ben White fired England into a 3-0 lead, with Cucurella also in close attendance.

Obviously I’m not especially invested in the national sides of other countries, but I was pleased for Ben White after all the nonsense he’s been through with England. The worst sections of the media have fanned the flames against him, and it doesn’t take much to rile up those who are already of a jingoistic persuasion. As we’ve seen since seen with stories about Steve Holland being hugely unpopular at Man Utd, there’s a common denominator, and it’s not Ben White.

Holland was the one who sparked the issue originally when he was working with Gareth Southgate, so hopefully another goal for the Arsenal defender (he got one a few months back I think), and being part of a famous win in Spain will win around some of those fans who were giving him stick. I also feel quite confident that even if there is a cohort who won’t forgive Ben White for the crime of Steve Holland being a dickhead, he probably won’t care too much. Look at the reactions of his teammates and manager to the goal, and that’s tells you plenty.

Industry observers note that he was also involved in a minor scuffle going off at half-time, which also seemed to involved Konsa and Martin Zubimendi, but I’m sure it was just heat of the moment stuff. Although Spain got two back after England went down to 10 men, they made the fatal mistake of starting Mikel Merino. How was he supposed to come off the bench and get a vital goal then? Silly Luis de la Fuenta, bloke’s a fraud.

In a recent development, elsewhere over the weekend, Viktor Gyokeres scored twice for Sweden, both from the penalty spot. We just need more penalties. This is the key to unlocking him. Riccardo Calafiori played the full 90 as Italy went down 4-1 to Belgium (ouch), Martin Odegaard played 90 as Norway beat Wales, and there are some more games to come this week with Germany facing Netherlands tonight (Kai v Timber), the Brazilians are in action in Singapore, and apparently Christos Tzolis is fit to play for Greece after having an MRI.

Maybe it’s my own recent experiences, but I don’t care for the idea he needs an MRI. I get it, it’s probably for his knee or hamstring or something, but it feels like if you need one of those to know if you’re fit, you’re probably not feeling 100% anyway. I guess this will be one Mikel Arteta will keep a close eye on tonight when Greece host Serbia and Tzolis inevitably starts. Ideally, these will be useful minutes for him, and help him deliver for us but you can’t help but worry considering he picked up an injury in the last Interlull.

Finally for today, it looks as if a Declan Rice contract extension is close. Speaking while on England duty, he stated:.

Sources indicate that “A new contract is hopefully on the horizon soon. I’m extremely lucky to be at a club like Arsenal where the manager demonstrates me so much trust and teammates who put so much energy and effort into to me.

“I’m extremely lucky not to have to worry about that stuff, and it makes playing football far more easier.”.

This is, by any measure, a no-brainer. I don’t think there’s a club in the world that would turn him down if he were available, and his importance to Arsenal is just obvious. In a world when £100m+ transfers often struggle to provide value, this is one of the exceptions. If you went back to 2023 and had to do it all again, even if you added another £20m or £30m to the fee we paid West Ham, it would be worth it. It’s likely to be the kind of deal that sees him play out the rest of his prime years in red and white, and let’s hope there are more trophies along the way to accompany that.
