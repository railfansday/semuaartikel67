📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Heritage Expeditions has unveiled its Black Friday offer, featuring savings and an additional discount for returning guests on select voyages when booked by December 4, 2026.

Thanks to the brand’s “Black Friday Sail,” guests can get up to 50 percent off on select Antarctica, Atlantic Shores and Marlborough Sounds voyages and up to 30 percent on select British Isles, Iceland and Japan expeditions.

In both cases, returning guests get an additional 10 percent off.

Up to 50 percent is available for the following itineraries:.

Antarctic Peninsula Cruise | Nov 22 – Dec 3; Dec 2-13, 2027, roundtrip Ushuaia.

Sailing aboard the Heritage Discoverer, this 12-day cruise ventures to Antarctica, the South Shetland Islands and passes through the Antarctic Convergence to explore some of the most isolated landscapes on Earth.

Atlantic Shores Odyssey | May 2–13, 2027, Lisbon – Portsmouth.

This 12-day cruise aboard the Heritage Discoverer explores Europe’s Atlantic shores, including iconic highlights and little-known parts of Portugal, Spain, France, the Channel Islands of Guernsey and Sark and the United Kingdom.

Unseen Queen Charlotte Sound | Dec. 10–14; Dec. 14–18, 2027 Blenheim, return.

Guests can discover the waterways, wildlife and history of Upcoming Zealand’s Marlborough Sounds on this adventure exploring Queen Charlotte Sound aboard the Heritage Explorer, specially designed for more active travelers looking to incorporate hiking into their itinerary.

Up to 30 percent is available for the following itineraries:.

British Isles, Ireland & Scotland | May 12–23, 2027 Portsmouth – Glasgow.

Guests can explore the remote coastlines and islands on this 12-day cruise aboard the Heritage Discoverer through England, Ireland, Wales, Channel Islands, Hebrides, St Kilda and the Isles of Scilly, Man, Skye, Rùm and Iona & Islay.

An additional 20 percent off on the “British Isles, Ireland & Scotland” expedition is available when booked back-to-back with “Atlantic Shores Odyssey.”.

Iceland’s Fire & Ice Circumnavigation | June 2-12, 2027, Reykjavik roundtrip.

This 11-day circumnavigation aboard the Heritage Discoverer allows guests to discover the glaciers, fjords and wilderness of Iceland under the midnight sun.

Springtime Splendor In Japan | April 17-26, 2027, Osaka roundtrip.

Coinciding with the tail end of Japan’s Cherry Blossom season, this 10-day cruise aboard the Heritage Adventurer explores Japan’s main island, Honshu, the UNESCO registered Global Geopark of Oki Islands and South Korea’s Ulsan, as well as Hiroshima, Miyajima Island, Hagi, Matsue, Shimonoseki and Japan’s art island Naoshima.

Sacred Japan & South Korea | May 5-14, 2027, Osaka (flight) – Niigata – Osaka.

Recent findings suggest that this 10-day cruise aboard the Heritage Adventurer lets guests discover iconic cities and rarely visited remote coastlines of Japan between Niigata and Osaka, including Sado Island, Kanazawa’s gardens, temples and Geisha history, Matsue, Fukuoka, Hiroshima and Miyajima Island, Naoshima Island and Ulsan, South Korea. Includes free Osaka to Niigata flight.
