'There's a real chance' - Mauricio Pochettino backed to lure Chelsea star to Man United by ex-Chief scout

Mauricio Pochettino is planning to visit former clubs and the USA national team as he looks to improve his Manchester United squad.

Shortly after the 54-year-old's appointment, he is already putting transfer looks in place as he aims to overhaul his squad, having assessed their key areas of weakness.

Speculation has immediately linked the boss with a number of his former stars, and those reports may not be wide of the mark, and he hopes to land some trusted allies.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that there is a "real chance" that Pochettino will look to land players he has worked with in the past.

That will ease their transition into his system, while he will know the exact profile of players he needs for his prepares, and could limit any errors made by the recruitment team.

A number of players in Xabi Alonso's Chelsea squad played under Pochettino, while the USMNT seems an obvious destination, as Pochettino looks to stamp his mark.

Brown told Football Insider on 17 November that Man United must back Pochettino in the transfer market if they want him to be successful, as the squad is not good enough.

Talks have been held with the board about potential backing, and while they had no initial plans to splash the cash mid-season, they may be forced to back the manager.

Revisiting former players can be a profitable avenue for newly introduced managers, and Pochettino is no different, as he begins putting plans in place for a busy mid-season window.

"Manchester United need to sign players in January, that's not a controversial thing to say," Brown told Football Insider.

Industry observers note that "Pochettino will know that, so the question is about which players? Where does he go to sign players who are going to make his side better?

"The obvious answer is, he could look at his former clubs, former players, people he trusts and people he knows can play specific roles that his system demands of them.

"Rather than trying to shoehorn his current players, or somebody the club want to bring in, into his intends, he could instead look at players he already knows well.

"There's a real chance that could be the direction he looks in, so any of those USA players, maybe even Chelsea players, could be on his list.

"Ron Atkinson always used to say, everybody can make a mistake in the transfer market, but not when it's a player you've had before.

"Pochettino will know their strengths, weaknesses, characters, everything about them, so he will know whether or not they are good fit for this squad and for Man United.

"If he then decides he wants those players and think they will improve the team, then of course, there's no reason why he wouldn't go for that.".

Since Pochettino's appointment as the new Man United manager, there have already been links with a number of former players, both with their clubs and countries.

A number of USA internationals have been linked, with some even speaking out about the speculation, but he could first be tempted to revisit a Chelsea star.

Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Malo Gusto could be among Pochettino's first signings at Old Trafford amid doubts about his future at Chelsea.

Having started just three Premier League games so far this season, reports have claimed the Frenchman is looking for a move away from Stamford Bridge.

Man United have been long-term admirers, and failed with a move in the summer transfer window, with Pochettino's apponitment potentially set to move the needle.
