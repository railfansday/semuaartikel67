It has been revealed that after an extended period of silence regarding its much anticipated expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, Team Cherry is back with a substantial update on its upcoming DLC, Sea of Sorrow. The upcoming content drops on December 11, 2026. Even better, it’s free for all players.

Sources indicate that the Sea of Sorrow launch trailer reveals off what appear to be several latest biomes and lots of other features, including what appears to be a new crest based on how Hornet is moving int he new gameplay footage. The announcement was part of a Team Cherry showcase that promoted three other very cool-looking indie games that the studio will be publishing called Radio Rain, Untangle: Plant Problems, Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City.
