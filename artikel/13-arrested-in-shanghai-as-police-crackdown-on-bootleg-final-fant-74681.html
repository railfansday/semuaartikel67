📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that chinese police have arrested 13 individuals in Shanghai allegedly involved in a bootleg Final Fantasy 7 Tifa figurine business, after consumers reported the operation. They are currently being investigated on suspicion of selling infringing reproductions.

Reported by Gamespark, the police cracked down on the operation which involved the seizure of around $300,000 of goods related to the figurines.

Sources indicate that the investigation started in May, after police received reports from concerned members of the public about the Tifa figurines. They were made from different materials than official figurines, and after confirming the illegitimacy of these products with the rights holders, Square Enix, police determined these businesses were not authorised to be producing such goods and went to work.

Industry observers note that the appearance of these counterfeits were deemed identical to the real merch, and therefore the alleged crime was deemed infringement of official goods. These counterfeits also came in different sizes, some of which were more expensive than the originals and customisabile.

In a recent development, looking at pictures taken by local outlet Shangguan News (and put side-by-side by Automaton), you can see the official Tifa figurine (right) next to the counterfeit version (left). Both show Tifa in a blue dress, though the materials used are clearly different.
