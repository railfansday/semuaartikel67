Alexander Isak is closing in on a record held by Mohamed Salah with his latest Liverpool goal.

The Swedish striker did not take long to open the scoring as Liverpool faced Club Brugge in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

Bradley Barcola won the ball high up the pitch to set the Reds on the attack and Dominik Szoboszlai teed Isak up to finish from close range.

It was a significant goal for Isak, who has now scored in six consecutive away matches for Liverpool.

Sources indicate that it is a feat that very few men have bettered. According to the stat gurus at Opta, no Liverpool player has ever scored in more than seven consecutive away appearances.

Sources indicate that salah is the current record holder, and should Isak find the back of the net in this weekend’s Merseyside derby at the Hill Dickinson Stadium, he would equal the Egyptian king’s record. Add another against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge on December 5, and he could surpass it.

Prior to the launch of the Premier League, Sam English, John Evans and John Aldridge all scored in seven consecutive away matches.

Salah called time on his Liverpool career at the end of last season, leaving with 257 goals and 123 assists across 442 appearances.

He now plies his trade in Turkey with Trabzonspor, where he has made a bright start to life, tallying 16 goal contributions in his first 17 appearances.
