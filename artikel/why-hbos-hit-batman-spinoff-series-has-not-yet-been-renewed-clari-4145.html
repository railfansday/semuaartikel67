One of the live-action DC properties still has some uncertainty about whether it is continuing with more stories.

Things are shaking up at DC Studios, as Peter Safran and James Gunn, who took on the roles of co-CEOs when Warner Bros. Discovery launched the fresh entity back in 2022, will be leaving their posts, with Jeremy Latcham taking over on December 1. While this has sparked a lot of questions about what will happen to the DC Universe franchise, the same can be stated about the Elseworlds banner.

In a recent development, in a breakdown from Deadline about the future of various DC Studios projects, one of the properties that was addressed was The Penguin TV show, as there are two reasons why a season 2 renewal hasn't happened yet. Lauren Lefranc, who was the showrunner on the Colin Farrell-starring drama, is right now developing a Poison Ivy series, which would not be set in the Matt Reeves universe if it goes forward.

Recent findings suggest that however, the second factor that is delaying any potential renewals for The Penguin is the fact that the focus is on Reeves' The Batman - Part II. The trade describes the 2028 sequel as "the most immediate priority" in the Elseworlds-based franchise. As of writing, DC Studios, HBO, and Warner Bros. TV have not commented on the future of the Oz Cobb-centric series after Deadline's report.

If Poison Ivy goes forward on HBO, with Lefranc being heavily involved as a producer, that could be why it is taking them longer to decide if they will renew The Penguin for another season. However, it needs to be pointed out that the original report about the potential DC project stated that she would not be the showrunner, as someone else would be tapped to shepherd it.

Sources indicate that the potential Pamela Isley-centric series would be the second DC title for Lefranc, who inked an overall deal with HBO and HBO Max back in August 2025, as she has launched her own production banner, Acid and Tender Productions. She shared the following statement at the time:.

In a recent development, lauren Lefranc: I am thrilled to continue my creative partnership with HBO. They have been unwavering in their support of my work on The Penguin, and beyond. My tech giant, Acid and Tender Productions, comes from a sincere desire to shepherd bold stories and perspectives from singular voices and artists I love. I came up in writers rooms and when they’re working, there’s nothing better. My hope is for Acid and Tender to become that kind of haven for writers and filmmakers who seek to tell authentic, off-kilter, character-driven stories, no matter the genre.

Industry observers note that while The Batman universe will continue on the major screen with the 2028 installment, audience members should not expect to see a lot of the DC supervillain. Farrell recently revealed on This Morning that he is only in two scenes in the upcoming continuation, as he stated, “So the Batman film, they're doing the sequel to The Batman, and I did two scenes in that. Just that. In and out. It was a bit tasty to do it. I was kind of moaning about the makeup again, but I do love the character and I'm very protective of the character. And there's definitely a conversation to be had about doing a second season.”.

In a recent development, even if the Irish star is game to do The Penguin season 2, it may also come down to how The Batman - Part II performs, and whether or not Reeves' continuity continues after the next film. With Robert Pattinson being officially cast as the new James Bond, time will tell if he will continue as the Dark Knight, especially if the 007 franchise will keep him busy for the coming years.

The Penguin season 1 is available to stream on HBO Max.
