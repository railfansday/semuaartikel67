Jude Bellingham sealed England's stunning 3-2 victory over world champions Spain with a kiss from girlfriend Ashlyn Castro as the couple made a rare public appearance together on Sunday.

England, who romped to a 3-0 lead with a superb display before the hour mark, were forced to weather a late onslaught from the Spanish after Elliot Anderson was shown a red card in the 63rd minute of the Nations League clash.

Although Mikel Oyarzabal and Nico Williams managed to claw two back for this year's World Cup winners, England held on with a backs-to-the-wall effort in the closing stages, courtesy of a few fine saves from Leeds goalkeeper James Trafford.

After England's victory, which called an end to Spain's historic 43-game unbeaten run, Bellingham was spotted celebrating with his model girlfriend Ashlyn inside the stadium.

Wearing a beaming smile, he went over to embrace both the 28-year-old American and his father Mark as the family soaked up the celebrations in the stands.

The Real Madrid superstar has largely kept his relationship with Ashlyn private since the pair were first linked in early 2025.

Jude Bellingham and his girlfriend Ashlyn Castro celebrate England's superb win over Spain.

The Real Madrid star then caught up with his dad Mark on a special night for the Bellinghams.

Ashlyn is a model and social media influencer from California (@instagram/ashlyncastro).

Ashlyn is a model and social media influencer from California who boasts over one million followers on Instagram, sharing fashion and beauty content online.

Spanish media waxed lyrical about Bellingham's display on the night, claiming he was 'determined to make a statement' at the stadium of Real Madrid's rivals Atletico.

It has been revealed that newspaper AS wrote: 'If anyone felt the match was crucial, it was Jude Bellingham. He stepped onto the Metropolitano pitch determined to make a statement, to impose his usual footballing and physical prowess.

'He exudes football in every movement, and his rise to the top is unstoppable.'.

It was a proud international break for the Bellinghams before a ball was kicked in Madrid, with Jude's 21-year-old younger brother Jobe also earning his first England call-up last week.

Jude and Jobe would have become the first brothers to play for England since Gary and Phil Neville 17 years ago, but the younger sibling stayed on Thomas Tuchel's bench as an unused substitute.

England, who topped Group C on goal difference over Spain, will now not play until the Nations League quarter-finals kick off next spring.

The final, should Tuchel's Three Lions make it that far, are scheduled for the summer.
