A drone operator has apologised for filming Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session, admitting that although the flight was legal, his actions were “unethical”.

The operator, behind the YouTube account CC Overview, filmed around 12 minutes of footage at Carrington on Thursday, showing Pochettino working with his upcoming squad ahead of Sunday’s Premier League trip to Liverpool.

It has led to suggestions on social media that Pochettino’s surprise starting line-up at Anfield has been leaked, with out-of-favour Joshua Zirkzee starting up top and Bryan Mbeumo playing in a wing-back role.

In an apology issued after deleting the video, the drone operator, who goes by ‘Colin’, mentioned: “I would like to speak about yesterday's incident. A total error of judgement on my part led to me filming some of the Manchester United training session at Carrington, whilst the flight was legal in terms of drone laws I fully accept it was unethical on my part. My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time.

"I had wrongly presumed that with it being close to the start of the session that no meaningful training sessions would have started so would have been some harmless warming up shown.

"I also wrongly assumed that the training session would have been open to various media outlets. I would like to apologise to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match.

Industry observers note that "I would also like to state that Liverpool Football Club or any other football club were not involved in my decision to film at Carrington yesterday. The video has been removed from my channel.
