Should you spare Arnold in GTA 6?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that in the mission 'Hey Arnold!' you face the story's first major moral dilemma: do you kill or spare Arnold in GTA 6? After Brian's shady business associate, Arnold, goes dark during GTA 6 Chapter 1, Jason, Brian, and Cal hunt him down to the radar station near the Aerostat. It turns out Arnold isn't very happy with Brian's drug-dealing tactics and, after some heated words, Arnold and his goons jump into a pickup truck and speed off.

It has been revealed that but no one disrespects Brian like that, not on Jason's watch, and a high speed car chase immediately ensues. When the chase ends, you must choose whether to let Arnold live or execute him, with your choice impacting your GTA 6 criminal profile.

It has been revealed that if you continue chasing Arnold to the Key Lento Marina, he abandons his vehicle and Brian tells Jason to "shut him up." Though, he doesn't specify how. He simply indicates: "Do whatever you have to do." You then need to chase after Arnold on foot (hopefully you've been working on your fitness) until you get close enough to tackle him or he reaches the end of the pier and has nowhere else to run. He then begins to beg Jason for his life.

To spare Arnold, hold down L2/LT to interact with him and press X/A to select the option to 'Spare' him. Instead of killing Arnold, Jason will threaten him and let him go. In return, you receive a contribution toward the Restrained criminal profile and Arnold scuttles off, with Brian none the wiser.

Sources indicate that there are two ways you can kill Arnold in this GTA 6 mission. The first is to complete the car chase to the Marina and then follow Arnold on foot until he reaches the end of the pier or you're able tackle him to the ground. However, when he starts begging for his life, you can instead execute him with your weapon. If you do this, you receive a warning that "Indiscriminate violence will contribute toward a violent criminal profile.".

Recent findings suggest that if you'd rather not deal with Arnold's begging – or a pesky foot chase – you can kill Arnold by shooting at his vehicle and/or him during the car chase. Killing Arnold in this way is quicker, but contributes toward a Ruthless criminal profile.

Whichever choice you make, the following cutscene will be the same.
