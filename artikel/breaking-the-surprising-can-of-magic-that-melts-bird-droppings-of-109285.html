WD-40 has been around since the 1950s. While my creaky joints don’t quite go back that far, I clearly remember it being a staple in my dad’s garage. The second anything needed lubricating, out came that familiar can of spray.

Originally invented as a rust-prevention solvent for the aerospace industry, its usefulness has since expanded into the domestic market, making it a well-known household staple. Instead of sitting on your tool shelf, you’re now just as likely to find it lurking in a cleaning cupboard.

Sources indicate that WD-40 is an amazing household cleaning product to keep in your kitchen.
