It has been revealed that in Paris, we are in no hurry to turn the page on Marquinhos. While several extensions are progressing and would even be almost completed (Dembélé and Kvaratskhelia in particular), the Parisian leaders are also looking into the case of their captain, under contract until 2028. According to The Parisianthey would consider offering him a new lease. Very satisfied with the Brazilian, the club has already discussed it with those around him in recent weeks.

According to recent reports, thirteen years after his arrival in Paris, Marquinhos remains an executive on the pitch as well as in the locker room. The Brazilian will, of course, be 34 years old at the end of his contract, but the Parisian management obviously takes this parameter into account. For the moment, discussions are only in their early stages. According to The Parisian, PSG would also work on the extensions of Quentin Ndjantou and David Boly.
