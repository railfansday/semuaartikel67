Tebas lists Real Madrid’s attacks on LaLiga a month after club demanded his resignation

In a recent development, laLiga president Javier Tebas has published a dated list of attacks on the competition by Florentino Pérez, Real Madrid's board and Real Madrid TV. The post comes a month after Madrid demanded his resignation and six days before Pérez reports to the club's members.

Javier Tebas has turned Real Madrid’s charge sheet back on the club. In a long thread on X titled ‘Who attacks whom?’, the LaLiga president set out years of attacks on the competition by Florentino Pérez, the club’s board and Real Madrid TV. A newspaper column by journalist Tomás González-Martín prompted the thread. The columnist had argued that LaLiga’s president should not spend his time attacking one of the league’s member clubs.

The list closes with the Real Madrid–Villarreal match. Tebas claims Real Madrid TV called LaLiga “adulterated and corrupt” that day. The channel also spoke of systemic corruption and used the phrase “filthy Negreira league”, in his account.

According to recent reports, earlier entries go back more than a year. According to Iusport, the list includes a statement approved by Real Madrid’s board on 3 February 2025. It claimed the system was structurally corrupt and that the competition had been manipulated and adulterated. Tebas also quotes a press conference on 12 May 2026, in which Pérez said Real Madrid’s enemy is always LaLiga. Another entry is Real Madrid TV director Jesús Alcaide. On 4 August 2025, Alcaide said the league was adulterated from the start. He pointed to the fixture calendar and the refusal to postpone Madrid’s opening game against Osasuna.

Industry observers note that tebas presented the post as part of his remit. His job, he wrote, extends to defending the clubs even when the person questioning them is Real Madrid’s president. He also rejected the idea that Pérez and Real Madrid should always be treated as one and the same. That distinction leaves him room to target the club’s president without casting it as an attack on the club.

Industry observers note that the thread follows Madrid’s official statement of 16 October. The club said Tebas’s repeated conduct made him institutionally unfit for his posts and called him a blight on Spanish football. It wanted him gone from LaLiga and from the federation, the body that includes the Technical Committee of Referees.

Recent findings suggest that the same day, EFE reported that Tebas had compared Real Madrid TV’s methods with propaganda techniques used by Joseph Goebbels, the Nazi propaganda minister. Tebas stated the comparison was about technique, not ideology or Madrid fans. A day later, the club’s website ran a round-up of international coverage of the comparison.

Recent findings suggest that the dispute also runs through the courts. A separate appeal ruling upheld a February 2024 first-instance judgment in LaLiga’s favour. Real Madrid said it disagreed strongly and would take the case to the Supreme Court.

According to recent reports, pérez now faces his members. Their representatives will gather at Ciudad Real Madrid’s basketball venue for two assemblies called by the board, with remote attendance also possible. The only item at the extraordinary assembly is a proposal to ratify tennis player Rafael Nadal as a board member. The ordinary assembly opens at 9:00 on first call and 10:00 on second call, with Pérez’s report as its opening item.
