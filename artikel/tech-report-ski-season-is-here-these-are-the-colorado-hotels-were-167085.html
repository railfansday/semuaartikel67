As home to some of the best skiing and snowboarding in the country, Colorado is one of the most popular destinations for winter. And while the slopes themselves tend to get most of the attention, where you stay goes a long way in making the trip special. After all, there’s a reason après-ski is a whole lifestyle.

It has been revealed that hilton has a number of hotels that help make the most of ski season and offers to help travelers get more from their winter getaway. Right now, Hilton is running a 20% off discount on stays for Hilton Honors members. The promo runs from Nov. 18 to Dec. 22 on stays booked before April 2, 2027. Hilton Honors is Hilton’s free-to-join loyalty program, with benefits including member rates and points toward free nights. Members who book eligible stays directly through Hilton.com or the Hilton Honors app can earn Points and access Hilton Honors benefits.

Whether you’re hitting the slopes or just looking to get away, these are some of the best Hilton hotels to stay at during the ski season.

In a recent development, if you’re looking for a ski-in ski-out property, Hotel Alpenrock Breckenridge, Curio Collection by Hilton is situated at the base of Peak 9 in Breckenridge. Beyond easy access to the slopes, it offers a fitness center, outdoor hot tubs, and a variety of dining options. Book Here.

Recent findings suggest that highline Vail is minutes from Vail Mountain, one of the most iconic ski destinations in the country, with complimentary shuttle service to three lift-access points in Vail Village. After skiing, guests can unwind in the year-round heated outdoor pool and oversized hot tub or enjoy dining and après-ski offerings on property. Book Here.

For a Rocky Mountain getaway with its own distinct character, Steamboat Springs pairs renowned skiing with a welcoming mountain town atmosphere. The Homewood Suites by Hilton Steamboat Springs hotel features amenities like pet-friendly rooms, an indoor pool, a fitness center, and in-room kitchens. The hotel is situated two miles from the ski resort. Book Here.

Sources indicate that looking to experience Colorado beyond the slopes? Another Colorado option is Embassy Suites by Hilton Denver Downtown Convention Center, which features access to downtown Denver. Sports fans can catch a Broncos game at Empower Field at Mile High or head to Ball Arena to see the Colorado Avalanche and Denver Nuggets. The hotel also offers an indoor pool, a fitness center, and an on-site restaurant. Book Here.
