Industry observers note that apple only rolled out high-end ‘Pro’ and ‘Duo’ iPhones this fall, marking a departure from previous years when more affordable models debuted too. But iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 are still coming, and a latest report offers an update on release timing.

A newly introduced report from Kim Yeong-ho at ETNews provides a fresh update on timing for the iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2.

Rumors have indicated for well over a year that Apple planned to push these models out of the typical fall launch window and introduce them in early 2027 instead.

Sources indicate that per ETNews, Samsung and LG are providing the display panels for the new models, with Samsung the sole supplier for iPhone Air 2 and both companies splitting orders for iPhone 18. And their mass production intends signal a March release for the products.

Kim Yeong-ho, writing at ETNews:.

Samsung Display plans to begin mass production in January, while LG Display will start late this year…The Korean panel makers appear to be aligning their production plans with an expected March launch.

A March launch window lines up well with when Apple tends to ramp up product introductions following a slower winter. Earlier this year, iPhone 17e debuted in March alongside upcoming Macs and the iPad Air.

The year before, however, the iPhone 16e arrived in February instead.

In a recent development, with Apple shaking up its normal iPhone rollout schedule, it was anyone’s guess how soon the upcoming models would debut. But a March launch makes sense and gives Apple a trio of new iPhones every six months—three unveiled in September, and another three in March.
