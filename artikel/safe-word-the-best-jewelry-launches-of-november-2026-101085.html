Safe Word is a series highlighting the latest trends in the jewelry market, keeping you up to date on all things that sparkle and shine.

It has been revealed that november’s jewelry rolls out are looking both backward and forward, with designers revisiting their roots, reworking archival ideas, and stepping into entirely new territory. First up, Quentin Jones is taking her surrealist art off the wall and onto the body with “Nocturne,” her first jewelry collection; Pomellato reaches back to 1997 to revive “Moneta Orientale” as a colorful modern talisman; David Yurman celebrates the magic of a New York holiday with Michael B. Jordan, Yerin Ha, and Inde Navarrette; Vhernier revisits its iconic “Trottola” design with new proportions and contrasting materials; Di Volpe reinterprets the classic tennis bracelet with customizable gemstones in “DiSegno”; Brent Neale transforms the classic bow into sculptural fine jewelry with “Wrap It Up”; and Kendra Scott launches the new “Kendra Scott Collection,” reimagining her signature Spike motif as a personal talisman. From archival revivals and meaningful motifs to innovative designs and new creative directions, November’s launches prove that sometimes the best way forward starts with looking back. For the rest of the month, we’ve got you covered on all the jewelry-related happenings worth knowing about.

Quentin Jones Turns Her Art Into Jewelry.

Recent findings suggest that quentin Jones is taking her art off the wall and onto the body with “Nocturne,” the first jewelry collection from House of Quentin Jones. Following the multidisciplinary artist’s debut of surrealist ceramics and tapestries last year, the fresh collection transforms recurring gestures and symbols from her work into sculptural collars, cuffs, armbands, pendants, chains, and earrings. Eyes, hands, stars, moons, tears, and knots take shape in silver and gold vermeil alongside freshwater pearls, onyx, opals, jade, glass, and silk tassels, giving the pieces a dreamlike, talismanic quality. Beginning as drawings and hand collages in Jones’s Brooklyn studio, each design carries her surrealist visual language into wearable form, further blurring the line between art, design, and fashion.

Piotrek Panszczyk Makes His Debut at Ippolita.

Ippolita is entering a latest chapter under creative director Piotrek Panszczyk, who makes his debut at the house with “Ancient Bohemia,” a collection inspired by its archives and the organic forms of nature. Taking a sculptural approach to jewelry, Panszczyk reimagines familiar signatures with an emphasis on texture, craftsmanship, and unconventional layering. The house’s iconic Wavy Disk becomes the Wavy Coin, featuring Hippolyta, Queen of the Amazons and the brand’s namesake, as a symbol of feminine strength. Elsewhere, hand-sculpted Stardust pavé is set entirely with diamonds, pearls make their first appearance, and lightweight, hand-hammered silver Roma links offer multiple ways to wear, from necklaces to belts. Lapis lazuli, mother-of-pearl, and quartz introduce color through Ippolita’s signature layered doublets and triplets, mixed rather than matched for an eclectic finish. The result is a fresh interpretation of the house’s heritage that feels both ancient and distinctly modern.

Jade Trau and Luis Morais Combine Gemstones and Diamonds.

According to recent reports, jade Trau and Luis Morais are joining forces for a limited-edition collection that brings together their different approaches to fine jewelry. Comprised of 16 one-of-a-kind pieces, including 12 bracelets and four necklaces, the collaboration pairs Morais’s colorful gemstone beads with Trau’s signature 18-karat gold and diamond elements. The designs feature a vibrant mix of stones, from chrysoprase and amazonite to lapis lazuli, turquoise, carnelian, and tiger’s-eye, accented by recognizable Jade Trau details like the Frankie rondelle, Sophisticate station, and Georgie diamond lock. Designed with everyday wear in mind, the bracelets lend themselves to layering, while the necklaces incorporate Trau’s diamond-set closures. The result is a playful meeting of color and craftsmanship that brings a fresh perspective to both designers’ signatures.

Lagos Looks to the Stars With Its Latest Collection.

According to recent reports, lagos references the night sky in its latest fine jewelry collection, taking inspiration from the North Star and its centuries-old symbolism of guidance and possibility. Designed to celebrate milestones, new beginnings, and meaningful connections, the “North Star” collection features sculptural star motifs crafted in 18-karat gold and illuminated by natural diamonds. Cascading silhouettes and light-catching details bring a sense of movement to the celestial designs, balancing statement-making shapes with delicate craftsmanship. Both symbolic and sophisticated, the assortment offers a modern interpretation of a timeless emblem, making each piece a reminder of the people and moments that guide us.

Kendra Scott Launches an Eponymous Collection.

Kendra Scott is offering a more personal look into her design world with the launch of the “Kendra Scott Collection,” a new line spanning fashion and fine jewelry. Its first edition, “The Spike,” revisits a silhouette the designer has worn throughout her career as a personal symbol of strength, confidence, and conviction. Inspired by nature and the radiance of the night sky, the collection translates the pointed motif into sculptural necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and charms, balancing bold proportions with intricate craftsmanship. Fashion jewelry incorporates tiger’s eye, malachite, and turquoise, while fine jewelry introduces lab-grown diamonds, pavé detailing, labradorite, and lapis. From statement-making links to delicate diamond charms, the collection offers a new interpretation of Scott’s longtime signature, inviting wearers to make the symbol their own.

Alison Lou’s ‘Fresh Wave’ Collection Experiments With New Materials.

Sources indicate that alison Lou is entering a upcoming chapter with “New Wave,” a fine jewelry collection that introduces unexpected materials and a fresh approach to the designer’s signature playful aesthetic. Exploring the relationship between movement and form, the collection pairs lightweight titanium and ceramic with lab-grown diamonds, creating fluid, sculptural silhouettes that curve and catch the light. Available in Fig and Denim Blue colorways, the designs include wavy rings, diamond-accented signets, delicate thread necklaces, and statement drop earrings. With its unconventional materials and easy-to-wear designs, “New Wave” brings a new sense of movement to Alison Lou’s everyday jewelry.

Jenny Bird and Emese Gormley Partner on the Anyu Ring.

Jenny Bird is expanding her JB FINE collection with a newly introduced collaboration featuring lifestyle creator and Lipstick on the Rim podcast host Emese Gormley. Named in honor of Gormley’s mother, the Anyu Ring offers a personal take on the classic signet, featuring three lab-grown baguette diamonds set in 14-karat yellow or white gold. The understated design combines the timeless appeal of a signet ring with a sentimental tribute to family, bringing a new perspective to Bird’s growing fine jewelry offering. Available exclusively on Jenny Bird’s website, the collaboration makes a case for jewelry that feels as meaningful as it looks.

Pomellato Revives Its 1997 Moneta Orientale Pendant as a Colorful Talisman.

According to recent reports, pomellato is looking to its archives for its latest good-luck charm. For the newest chapter of “Memorie d’Archivio,” the maison revives Moneta Orientale, a 1997 pendant inspired by the ancient symbolism of a circle surrounding a square, representing the harmony between heaven and earth. Made in 33 stone-and-pavé combinations, the design pairs colorful hard stones including black jade, amazonite, sodalite, rhodochrosite, and labradorite with Pomellato’s signature irregular pavé of diamonds, sapphires, rubies, and more. Offered in two proportions, each pendant transforms the archival motif into a modern talisman for prosperity and protection.

Bibi van der Velden Makes Her High Jewelry Debut With ‘Eclipse’.

According to recent reports, bibi van der Velden is entering a fresh era with “Eclipse,” the designer’s first high jewelry collection and most refined exploration of diamonds to date. Inspired by the fleeting moment when light gives way to darkness, the collection includes two six-piece suites: one in white diamonds and another defined by an ombré of diamonds into gray spinels deepening into black. Van der Velden’s signature alligator motif is abstracted into fluid, sculptural silhouettes, with scales engraved into the white gold on the reverse of each piece. “With Eclipse, I wanted to take those familiar elements and transform them into something more abstract and sculptural,” she says. “It’s a collection about transformation, which has always been at the heart of my work.”.

Aflalo and Leandra Medine Cohen Turn Everyday Indulgences Into Fine Jewelry.

In a recent development, aflalo is finding the humor in our obsession with wellness with “The Vanities,” a upcoming two-piece fine jewelry capsule designed exclusively by Leandra Medine Cohen. Playing with the contradiction between longevity and life’s little indulgences, the collection transforms two unlikely everyday accessories into precious objects. The Memento Mori is a 14-karat yellow gold cigar band set with more than four carats of diamonds, designed to slip over an Oura Ring, while The Libertine is a sterling silver pendant case adorned with diamonds and a wild-haired woman outlined in gold, designed to hold nicotine pouches or other small essentials. Equal parts extravagant and irreverent, the collection makes a playful case for enjoying life’s pleasures without sacrificing personal style.

Julia Berolzheimer’s Hart Jewelry Collection Draws on Childhood Memories.

In a recent development, hart is teaming up with Julia Berolzheimer for a latest jewelry collection that celebrates the art of the stack. Inspired by Berolzheimer’s childhood memories of her grandmother’s California ranch and her collection of Art Deco minaudières, the collaboration brings together whimsical charms, colorful gemstones, and meaningful talismans designed to be mixed, layered, and restyled. Botanical motifs take shape in Tree of Life coins, daisies, bees, and fruit-inspired charms, while carnelian, turquoise, and pink opal add playful pops of color. One standout is the Perfume Vesta Case, a modern interpretation of a Victorian-era accessory that can hold a sample-sized fragrance and hang alongside other charms. With interchangeable chains, cords, and pendants, the collection makes a case for jewelry that feels just as personal as the memories behind it.

David Yurman Captures the Magic of a Latest York Holiday.

David Yurman is celebrating the magic of the holidays in Newly introduced York City with a new campaign featuring global brand ambassador Michael B. Jordan alongside actresses Yerin Ha and Inde Navarrette. Set against a snow-covered Washington Square Park, the campaign captures a series of quintessential New York moments, from Jordan and Ha facing off in a spirited card game to Navarrette hailing a cab in search of the perfect holiday gift. Throughout the campaign, the cast wears some of the house’s most recognizable designs, including 18-karat gold Cable bracelets and pieces from the “Starburst” and “Armory” collections. Directed by Bradford Young and photographed by Drew Vickers, the campaign brings together the spirit of the city, the joy of togetherness, and the timeless appeal of David Yurman’s jewelry.

Vhernier Reintroduces Its Iconic ‘Trottola’ Design With New Proportions.

Vhernier is revisiting one of its most recognizable designs with a upcoming interpretation of “Trottola,” the sculptural collection originally introduced in 2003. Inspired by the continuous motion of a spinning top, the latest designs play with proportions, contrasting materials, and asymmetrical shapes across rings, bracelets, necklaces, and earrings. Polished rose gold meets glossy black jet, with select pieces featuring diamond pavé for an added contrast of light and dark. The collection also focuses on versatility, from rings designed for stacking to convertible earclips that can be worn as studs or dangling earrings. With its fluid forms and unexpected material combinations, “Trottola” brings a fresh perspective to Vhernier’s signature sculptural approach to jewelry.

Di Volpe Reinvents the Tennis Bracelet.

Di Volpe is rethinking the classic tennis bracelet with “DiSegno,” a latest customizable fine jewelry system designed to change with its wearer. Using concealed, patent-pending connections, individual cultured diamonds and gemstones, called Elements, can be removed, rearranged, or added by hand, allowing wearers to transform bracelets into necklaces or earrings without compromising the seamless look of traditional tennis jewelry. The collection rolls out with 28 stone-and-cut combinations, including rubies, emeralds, sapphires, and diamonds, alongside a limited-edition collaboration with Lebanese artist Ali Cha’aban featuring five sculptural designs. With new cuts, colors, and artist collaborations planned for future releases, “DiSegno” makes the case for fine jewelry that’s never quite finished.

Brent Neale Riffs on the Bow in Fine Jewelry.

Recent findings suggest that brent Neale is putting a bow on the holiday season with “Wrap It Up,” a new fine jewelry collection that transforms the familiar ribbon motif into sculptural works of art. Drawing inspiration from Old Hollywood glamour and the femininity of the 1960s, the designer plays with the contrast between rigid precious metals and the soft movement of fabric, crafting bows that twist, curl, and unfurl in 18-karat gold and platinum. Colored gemstones sit nestled within the ribbon-like forms, while pavé diamonds accentuate every curve and fold. Equal parts playful and precious, the collection brings a little gift-wrapped glamour to the jewelry box.

Grace Winfrey’s ‘Poppy Fortuna’ Collection Reimagines Good-Luck Charms.

Industry observers note that grace Winfrey is bringing a little extra luck to the jewelry box with “Poppy Fortuna,” a fresh fine jewelry collection inspired by the natural beauty of the Italian landscape and symbols of good fortune. Scarlet poppies, sunflowers, bees, four-leaf clovers, horseshoes, and ladybugs come to life through vibrant gemstones and intricate pavé, creating miniature mosaics of color and texture. Highlights include a Lucky Bangle combining three good-luck charms, sculptural sunflower earrings with tiny ladybugs tucked into their petals, and layered charm necklaces designed to feel like modern keepsakes. Handcrafted in New York City, each piece transforms familiar symbols of prosperity, protection, and happiness into playful, colorful works of art.

Robinson Pelham Channels ’90s Glamour for Its Anniversary.

It has been revealed that robinson Pelham is closing out its 30th anniversary celebrations with “Suite 96,” a fresh campaign and collection launch inspired by the glamour of late-’90s London. Set inside a private suite at St. James’s Hotel & Club in Mayfair, the campaign considers jewelry the ultimate room service, with diamonds arriving on trays, gold spilling out of wardrobes, and plenty of pieces to layer before a night out. At the center is “Studio 30,” a four-piece anniversary collection featuring diamond necklaces and statement earrings, including the sculptural Via and Hira necklaces, cascading diamond drops, and the bold Solstice Supreme. Alongside the anniversary designs, the house introduces new collections and additions to existing favorites, from chunky gold links and graphic heart rings to colorful enamel and gemstone combinations. Together, the launches celebrate three decades of Robinson Pelham’s bold, playful approach to fine jewelry.

Le Vian and Jared Revive Georgian-Era Romance.

Le Vian is bringing a little old-world romance to Jared Jewelers with its new exclusive “Georgian Chocolate & Nude Diamonds” collection. Inspired by the candlelit jewels of the Georgian era, when jewelry was designed to capture and reflect the warmth of natural light, the collection rethinks historic buttercup settings in polished Honey Gold, with eight rounded prongs framing each natural diamond. The designs pair Le Vian’s signature Chocolate and Nude Diamonds with warm gold detailing across solitaire rings, anniversary bands, tennis bracelets, necklaces, and earrings. Select styles introduce colorful gemstones including Chocolate Quartz, Green Apple Peridot, and Grape Amethyst, giving the vintage-inspired silhouettes a contemporary enhancement. With its sculptural settings and warm-toned stones, the collection brings the romance of antique jewelry into the present.

Wempe’s ‘Au:thentic’ Celebrates the Art of Goldsmithing.

It has been revealed that wempe’s new gender-inclusive fine jewelry collection, “Au:thentic,” puts craftsmanship front and center, finding inspiration in the foundational skills taught to aspiring goldsmiths. The collection takes its shape from the jump ring, one of the first techniques Patrick Sieroslawski learned as an apprentice more than 22 years ago, translating its form to necklaces, bracelets, rings, earrings, and pendants. Handcrafted at Wempe’s atelier in Germany, the pieces come in 18-karat yellow, white, and rose gold, with select styles set with diamonds. “Au:thentic” also marks the beginning of a multiyear rollout, with three additional chapters set to expand on its design language through twists, wraps, and interlocking links.
