According to recent reports, andré Dragosch indicates Fed language is already as hawkish as it was in 2022, and “we are probably close to peak hawkishness.” With the 2s10s spread under 30 basis points, a 50 basis point hike in December could invert the yield curve and bring back recession calls. He says what usually follows an inversion is a pause, then cuts, and “every time we reached this kind of maximum tightening in macro, Bitcoin also bottomed.”.
