Reds supporters have been calling out for communication from Raphael Khalili and co following the poor start to the season, the sacking of Colin Kazim-Richards and the embarrassing FA Cup exit to Wimborne Town.

But following the club confirming Kevin Blackwell’s appointment as head coach until the end of the season, the ownership group published an open letter to fans.

In the letter they ‘take full responsibility for the direction of this football club’ and recognised ‘significant shift in direction was necessary’ and that ‘difficult decisions have now been made’.

They also gave updates on strengthening the club’s leadership and the the ownership group.

Here is the open letter in full:.

From the Ownership Group and Board of Directors of Crawley Town Football Club.

First and foremost, we want to thank every Crawley Town supporter for their continued patience, loyalty and commitment to this Football Club during what has undoubtedly been a difficult period.

According to recent reports, we recognise that our start to the season has fallen well below the standards we expect, and more importantly, the standards that you, our supporters, deserve. We understand the frustration, the disappointment and the questions being asked. We also recognise that our communication in recent weeks has not been what it should be.

Over the past month, we have been working tirelessly behind the scenes to assess every aspect of the club and put in place the new structures and processes required to take us forward into a positive new chapter.

As owners, we take full responsibility for the direction of this football club. We have never underestimated the privilege of being entrusted with Crawley Town, nor the responsibilities and expectations that come with it.

Industry observers note that it became clear that a significant shift in direction was necessary. Some difficult decisions have now been made, but we firmly believe they are the best decisions for protecting the long-term interests of Crawley Town Football Club.

Today, we want to share with you some of the changes that we are making in order to demonstrate our commitment to changing the direction of the Club for the better.

First Team Management: Following the recent changes to our football management structure, we undertook an extensive search to identify the right person to lead Crawley Town going forward.

Over the past several weeks, we have spoken with and interviewed numerous experienced and highly regarded managers. Throughout this process, our priority has always been to make the right appointment for the Club, rather than rushing into a decision.

At the same time, we have been extremely encouraged by the impact Kevin Blackwell has made since taking charge. His experience, leadership and understanding of the challenges we face have been evident, and we have seen a marked improvement in the players' performances, engagement and attitude.

With the team remaining unbeaten in the league under Kevin's leadership, there have already been encouraging signs of greater stability, resilience and togetherness within the squad.

Having carefully considered all of our options and building upon the positive impact that we have already seen from Kevin, we firmly believe that he is the right man to lead Crawley Town for the remainder of the season, and we are pleased to confirm his appointment as First Team Manager.

Kevin has our full confidence and support, and we will be further strengthening his coaching team with the addition of another assistant coach in due course.

Our immediate focus is now on giving Kevin, his staff and the players every opportunity to succeed, restoring confidence and building the consistency required to climb the league table.

Strengthening the Club's Leadership: We are presently in the process of strengthening our senior executive structure to provide greater expertise and support across both our football and off-field operations.

We expect to provide further details subject to the necessary approvals from the Independent Football Regulator (IFR) and the EFL.

We believe that establishing a strong, experienced and accountable executive structure is fundamental to building a successful and sustainable football club.

Strengthening Our Ownership Group: We are delighted to announce and welcome Jonathan Meller to our Ownership Group. Jonathan brings extensive experience and knowledge from more than a decade of involvement in football and has a long-standing relationship with members of our Ownership Group. He has experience in helping clubs progress and sees Crawley Town as the perfect opportunity to help a club grow and move up the leagues.

We believe Jonathan's insight, relationships and understanding of the industry will be another valuable addition as we continue to develop the Club.

Industry observers note that recruitment, Data and Football Operations: Our review has also identified the need to modernise and strengthen the way the Club operates, particularly in the areas of player recruitment, performance analysis and internal processes. We will be able to announce the appointment of a fresh Head of Recruitment in the days/coming weeks, who will play an vital role in contributing to our future football strategy. In parallel, we are developing a comprehensive new data-led and eyes-on recruitment strategy, alongside improved systems and processes throughout the Club.

Our ambition is to build a more intelligent, efficient and structured football operation, where recruitment decisions are supported by robust analysis, clear football principles and a long-term vision and culture.

These changes will not happen overnight, but they are essential if we are to establish the foundations for lasting success.

Industry observers note that commercial Growth and Upcoming Opportunities: Off the pitch, we are equally focused on developing the Club's commercial potential and creating opportunities that extend beyond the traditional boundaries of a League Two football club. We are in the final stages of an exciting new commercial partnership, which we expect to announce shortly.

We believe this relationship has the potential to introduce Crawley Town to a significantly wider, global audience, creating newly introduced opportunities for the Club, our partners and our supporters.

Our ambition remains to build a club that is competitive on the pitch, commercially progressive off it and firmly connected to its community.

We appreciate that announcements, words and promises ultimately mean very little without results on the pitch.

Industry observers note that we will be judged by our actions, and rightly so: We are not asking anyone to ignore the difficulties of recent months or pretend that everything has been good enough. It hasn't. But we do ask that we now look forward together rather than backwards and divided.

Football is never a straight line. Every club experiences difficult periods, setbacks and moments when its character is tested. It is during these moments, more than any other, that the strength of a football club's community becomes so important.

Recent findings suggest that the supporters are, and always will be, the heartbeat of Crawley Town. Your loyalty, your passion and your support are what give this Club its identity and purpose.

We understand that support must be earned through performances and results, and we accept our responsibility to deliver both. However, we also believe that the strongest football clubs are those that stand together through the difficult moments, not just the successful ones.

And that brings us to Fleetwood.

Our upcoming fixture against Fleetwood is an important moment for this football club. We want to use it as an opportunity to draw a line under the difficult start to our season and begin a upcoming chapter, together. Fill the stands, make your voices heard and get behind Kevin and the players from the very first whistle.

We know the difference a united crowd can make, particularly when a team needs confidence and belief.

Let Fleetwood be the moment we come together, turn the page and start building the momentum that will define the rest of our season: There is still a long way to go, plenty of football to be played and everything to fight for. We have absolute belief in our ability to turn our fortunes around.

In a recent development, we remain as committed and determined today as we were on the day we took ownership of this Football Club. Our ambition has not changed, and neither has our belief in what Crawley Town can become. There will undoubtedly be further challenges along the way, but we are determined to face them with honesty, resilience and a shared sense of purpose.

Industry observers note that to every supporter who has continued to turn up, travel, sing and stand by the team during this difficult period, thank you. Your commitment does not go unnoticed or unappreciated.

For and on behalf of the Ownership Group & Board of Directors Crawley Town Football Club​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​.
