GTA 6 is the highest-rated game of all time

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto VI is officially out and critics love it.

Industry observers note that unleashed on Wednesday 13 years after its predecessor, Rockstar Games' wildly anticipated game has landed the highest ever rating on Metacritic. Essentially, it's instantly the best-reviewed game of all time, according to the aggregator site.

GTA 6 has a 99 percent rating on Metacritic, based on 141 critic reviews. This is a weighted average of individual critic scores calculated by Metacritic staff through a complex system involving review detail, publication prestige, and whether the critic has provided a score in their review.

Metacritic predicted GTA 6 would average 96 percent, an already staggering number it's blown out of the water.

In a dramatic twist, GTA 6 also accelerated past the previous #1 ranked game of all time, 1998 classic The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, which has just dominated headlines with Nintendo's new remastered version.

Critics have dubbed GTA 6 everything from "the single most impressive video game I have ever played" to "a masterpiece of open-world design." Meanwhile, players are spending their first few hours with the game and reacting in real-time.
