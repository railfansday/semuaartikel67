The second round of award winners of the 2026 World Architecture Festival (WAF) have been revealed, following Day Two of the world's largest international live-judged architectural event, being held at Broward County Convention Center, Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

The second round of award winners of the 2026 World Architecture Festival (WAF) have been confirmed, following Day Two of the world's largest international live-judged architectural event, being held at Broward County Convention Center, Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

Industry observers note that in total, the three-day festival will see over 400 live pitches from the 2026 finalists in front of over 130 international judges. Today has seen shortlisted projects from around the world compete for 19 award categories within Completed Buildings, Future Projects, Interiors and Landscape. Award winners include Sanjay Puri Architects, OHLAB, Snøhetta and Terreform ONE.
