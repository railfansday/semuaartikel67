Apple TV (now $6.99/month for limited time) has just announced that Ted Lasso will return for season 5. Here are the details.

This summer Apple TV aired the highly anticipated return of Ted Lasso.

Season 4 debuted three full years after the last season ended, and brought a fresh start to one of the streamer’s all-time most beloved series.

But after the season 4 finale aired, Apple provided no indication whether the show would continue or not.

Now, uncertainty around the show’s fate is over.

Apple has revealed that Ted Lasso season 5 is officially coming.

Following its hit fourth season, Apple’s global phenomenon “Ted Lasso” is officially returning back to the pitch for season five. Starring and executive produced by Emmy Award winner Jason Sudeikis, the fourth season of “Ted Lasso” introduced the Lady Greyhounds, with critics hailing it as “a perfect setup for a fresh return” and “one of the most beloved comedy demonstrates of the decade.”.

The announcement is light on further details. However, there should be a much shorter wait for season 5 than there was for season 4.

In a recent development, before Ted Lasso returned this year, Jason Sudeikis shared that he had a new three-season arc in mind. Apple’s Eddy Cue also suggested the company would be happy to continue making more seasons for years to come.

But until today’s announcement, the lack of official news still put Lasso fans’ hopes on pause.

Whether it returns in 2027 or, perhaps more likely 2028, Ted Lasso isn’t going away again anytime soon.
