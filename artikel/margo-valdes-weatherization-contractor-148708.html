📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After years as a building-performance consultant, Margo Valdes grew frustrated of seeing the problems that arise when contractors ignore fundamental building science. Three years ago, she founded Athena Building Performance, a weatherization company built on the premise that getting the details right starts with understanding why a building behaves the way it does.

Margo graduated from college with degrees in economics and physics, but her favorite course was “Castles,” taken during a semester abroad in England. The class explored how to determine the age of a castle, identify the type of stone used to build it, and understand the reasoning behind its design. Margo loved it.

According to recent reports, after working an unfulfilling office job, Margo saw an ad for a HERS rater at Conservation Services Group (now CLEAResult). The job sounded like a good fit, so she applied, and her career in building performance began.

Margo spent about six years at CSG and credits the company with giving her a strong foundation in building science. Though her work as a HERS rater focused on testing and evaluating, she received hands-on training in air-sealing and choosing and installing insulation. After leaving CSG, Margo’s focus shifted from latest construction to existing buildings. She joined a consulting firm, where she spent seven years as director of the Existing Buildings team.

It has been revealed that over the years, Margo became increasingly aware of how much the quality of weatherization work depends on understanding the building as a system—adding insulation affects moisture, and sealing air leaks changes ventilation. She decided the best way to improve the work was to get closer to it. That led her to the contractor side of the business, and to founding Athena.

Today, Athena operates as a specialized weatherization contractor, tackling residential air-sealing and insulation upgrades. While Margo spends most of her time managing the business, knowledgeable practice remains at its heart: Every new hire spends weeks doing hands-on field training and diagnostic walk-throughs to understand not just how the work is done, but why.

Margo envisions eventually expanding Athena into a full-service building-enclosure firm. For now, she’s focused on centering the manufacturer around what started it all: thoughtful, educated work.
