Leaving Priceline out of the B2B consolidation is a blow to the goals of Booking Partner Division.

Booking Holdings initially planned to consolidate three of its B2B brands into its fresh Booking Partner Division, but decided to leave Priceline out because of a potential U.S. Federal Trade Commission complaint against the brand.

The investigation created "operational complexities," Omri Morgenshtern, the head of the upcoming division and the CEO of Agoda, told Skift Wednesday.
