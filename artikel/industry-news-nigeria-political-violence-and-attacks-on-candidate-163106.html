Industry News: Nigeria: Political violence and attacks on candidates raise fears ahead of January elections

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Political violence is escalating in Nigeria ahead of the general elections scheduled for January. Candidates and their supporters have increasingly come under attack, with armed groups disrupting campaign rallies and some assaults resulting in deaths. Despite the growing number of incidents, security forces have struggled to contain the violence. With less than two months to go before the vote, fears are mounting that intimidation and deadly attacks could prevent many Nigerians from freely taking part in the democratic process.
