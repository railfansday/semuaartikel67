Another week without Kenneth Walker III? It's looking that way for the Kansas City Chiefs.

After missing Week 10 due to groin tightness, the Chiefs running back did not practice this week and has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals. Rookie Emmett Johnson, who rushed for 101 yards and caught two receiving touchdowns last week, will once again take on the starter's role.

In a recent development, los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua underwent surgery to repair his MCL this week, head coach Sean McVay confirmed on Friday. The procedure will rule him out for the next few months, which could result in him missing the rest of the 2026 NFL season.

According to recent reports, "We always thought [it] was a possibility," McVay remarked regarding Nacua's surgery. "It's going to be a couple months and we're going to have to go on without him.".

Industry observers note that the 6-4 Rams, who are on a bye in Week 11 and play Thanksgiving Eve against the Green Bay Packers, are currently tied for second in the NFC West and have seven more games left in the regular season. McVay did not have a definitive timeline for a return. The team is currently scheduled to play its Week 18 game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Jan. 3, a game that could be flexed depending on what the playoff picture looks like at the time.

McVay did not rule out a Nacua return should the Rams make the postseason, but said, "you're planning on not having him.".

Other Week 11 injuries to monitor:.

Luther Burden III (concussion) has cleared protocol after taking a helmet-to-helmet hit in Week 8 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. The Chicago Bears wide receiver logged three full practices this week and carries no injury designation. Same goes for defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, who was limited this week with a knee glitch. "Like 99 problems, but this ain't one," he mentioned.

Injuries that kept him limited in Wednesday's practice did not keep Las Vegas Raiders tight end Brock Bowers (ankle/quad) away from being on the field Thursday and Friday. The Raiders' leading receiver exited Sunday's loss to the Seattle Seahawks after just 11 snaps with an ankle issue. He is currently listed as questionable.

According to recent reports, the Cardinals are preparing to start 40-year-old Josh Johnson against the Chiefs as Carson Beck (beck) sat out Friday's practice. Johnson, who is on his 15th NFL team, would end up being the team's fourth different starter at quarterback in the past four weeks following Beck, Gardner Minshew and Jacoby Brissett.

DeVonta Smith (hamstring) has missed the past three games for the Philadelphia Eagles, but indicates he's 100% and has felt that way for a while. Smith stated he was able to play last week against the Carolina Panthers, but some things are out of his control. He carries no injury designation for Week 11.
