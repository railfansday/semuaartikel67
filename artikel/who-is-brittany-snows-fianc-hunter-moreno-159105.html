In a recent development, if you’re a fan of The Hunting Wives, chances are you’re probably invested in the on-screen affair between Brittany Snow and Malin Akerman’s characters, Sophie and Margo. But off screen, both actresses are happily in relationships—Akerman with her husband of eight years, Jack Donnelly, and Snow with new fiancé Hunter Moreno.

According to recent reports, snow unveiled the engagement news on a November 19 appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show, when Barrymore asked her directly, “Are you recently engaged?” The Hunting Wives star replied, “You know what? For the first time, I’m gonna tell you, I’m gonna tell you. Yes, we’re engaged, yeah. I haven’t noted it before. It’s okay. It’s not that big of a secret. I mean, like, who cares?”.

In talking about her husband-to-be, Snow revealed that the two began dating during the filming of the first season of The Hunting Wives. “He’s the best,” she grinned, adding that Akerman was one of the people to encourage the relationship early on.

It has been revealed that akerman, who was also a guest on the show, added, “We all met him. We got to be, like, the first ones to judge him. And the second I met him, I was like, ‘Oh, Brittany, he’s the one.’” She continued, “His smile right away. It’s just, you know, when you meet a person, and you can just feel their energy? Like, ‘This is the right energy for you.’”.

The costars then playfully admitted they jokingly refer to the cast’s boyfriends and husbands as “The Hunting Husbands.”.

According to recent reports, so, who is Snow’s fiancé, Hunter Moreno? Ahead, everything to know about the creative and their relationship.

Recent findings suggest that based in Los Angeles, Moreno began his career directing and filming YouTube videos for influencers before expanding into music, directing videos for artists including Dasha, Charlie Puth, Ava Max, and Machine Gun Kelly. Along with his video credentials, he’s also photographed artists from Selena Gomez to Lizzo, Renee Rapp, and Cara Delevingne. Moreno has also shot fashion editorials and campaigns for publications and brands including Vogue and Madewell.

In 2018, Moreno founded Make Sure Your Friends Are Okay (MSYFAO), a mental health awareness organization that sells clothing and merch, with a portion of its proceeds benefiting various mental health charities.

The initiative is rooted in Moreno’s own experiences with mental health. In an Instagram post shared in May, he opened up about his struggles with depression and the personal reason he started the organization. “In 2018 I started Make Sure Your Friends Are Okay for a selfish reason I didn’t say out loud: I hoped someone would check on me while I was struggling with my own mental health problems and depression,” he wrote. He went on to encourage others to check in on the people they care about and make conversations about mental health more normalized.

Recent findings suggest that mental health advocacy is also important to Snow, who co-founded September Letters in 2020. The letter-writing platform, which has partnered with the Mental Health Coalition, aims to help end the stigma surrounding mental health.

Moreno and Snow were first publicly linked in October 2024, when they were photographed kissing while walking through New York City. During a 2026 appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show, Snow confirmed that the two began dating during the filming of The Hunting Wives’ first season, which began in 2024.

It has been revealed that on March 28, 2026, Snow made their relationship Instagram official—though it was slyly packed into a massive carousel post. Amongst the photos from her birthday celebration in the desert were two photos of Snow and Moreno, a mirror selfie and a photo of the two posing with her dog.

Though Moreno’s Instagram is mostly dedicated to his professional work, in September, he uploaded a carousel of photos taken of Snow while traveling from Italy to France.

Rumors of an engagement first sparked between Snow and Moreno in November 2025, when a video shared by DeuxMoi highlighted Claire Danes chatting with Snow on a red carpet and seemingly congratulating her on her engagement. “You’re engaged?!” Danes exclaimed during the exchange. Nearly a year later, Snow finally confirmed the news during an appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show.

“You know what? For the first time, I’m gonna tell you, I’m gonna tell you. Yes, we’re engaged, yeah,” Snow remarked when Barrymore asked her directly. “I haven’t said it before. It’s okay. It’s not that massive of a secret. I mean, like, who cares?”.

While gushing about her fiancé alongside Barrymore and her Hunting Wives costar Akerman, Snow called Moreno “the best.” She also revealed that Akerman played a role in their love story, encouraging Snow to pursue the relationship early on.
