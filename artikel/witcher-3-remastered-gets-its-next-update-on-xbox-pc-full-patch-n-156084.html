Here comes another newly introduced patch for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered. The latest upgrade arrived today (November 26) for Xbox Series X|S, PC, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, focusing on "Photo Mode improvements" alongside a hearty batch of fixes.

"Patch 5.02 for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered is now available across PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2! From gameplay and balance and visual adjustments to Photo Mode improvements, to plenty fixes across the Continent and more, this update brings a host of changes to make your adventures on the Path even better.".

Most of the fixes revolve around problems with lighting, New Game+, the UI, quests, and Photo Mode. There aren't too many Xbox-specific fixes, but a couple are worth mentioning. These include a fix for achievements not unlocking after transferring PC saves via Cross Progression, as well as intermittent freezing on Xbox Series X|S.

In a recent development, in addition to CD Projekt Red's official fixes, cross-platform mods have just gone live for the Witcher 3 Remastered too! These feature more than 40 mods from community modders, such as boat races, glowing eyes for Geralt, hairstyles and more.
