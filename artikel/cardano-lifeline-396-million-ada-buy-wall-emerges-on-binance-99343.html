Cardano fell to $0.133, a five-month low on Saturday as the crypto market sell-off deepened. At the time of writing, Cardano had slightly rebounded, up 1.82% in the last 24 hours to $0.144, as a substantial buy wall of 3.96 million ADA emerged on Binance, offering a layer of support as sellers pressured the cryptocurrency.

Industry observers note that A buy wall occurs when a large volume of buy orders is concentrated around a particular price level on an exchange's order book. Such an order cluster can indicate that traders are prepared to purchase an asset at that price, potentially slowing further declines if selling pressure meets sufficient demand.

In Cardano's case, the 3.96 million ADA order cluster that occurred below the $0.14 level revealed a notable level of buying interest. As the orders at this level were filled, they helped absorb selling pressure for Cardano and provided short-term support for the price.

Current data from CoinGlass shows that open interest is down 1.98%, mostly due to shorts closing, although funding remains negative at −0.0099%, with shorts paying longs.

The next key levels to watch for ADA, according to on-chain data provided by CoinGlass, are $0.1462, which has a short liquidation cluster, and $0.1375, which has a long liquidation cluster.

In about two to three weeks, the Cardano Node 11.3 pre-release is anticipated, while an update to the Constitution governance action on-chain is expected in about one month.

Industry observers note that dijkstra requires both constitutional and technical changes in the era of on-chain governance. These include Constitutional guardrails (Enhancement Constitution) and Plutus V4 (Parameter Update).
