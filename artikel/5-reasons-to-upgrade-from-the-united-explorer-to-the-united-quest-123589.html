5 reasons to upgrade from the United Explorer to the United Quest

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (211 words, 📖 Standard)
  • A mid-tier airline card offers enhanced travel perks compared to a lower-tier option, including two free checked bags for the cardholder and a companion, annual airline credits and increased opportunities to earn elite status points.
  • The higher-tier card provides automatic annual airline credits and more generous statement credits for ride-hailing, grocery delivery and hotel stays, which may offset its higher ongoing fee for frequent travelers.
  • Both cards share core benefits such as priority boarding and credits for expedited airport security programs, but differ in the level and frequency of travel-related perks.
  • Upgrading may be worthwhile for those who regularly check multiple bags, use airline credits or pursue elite status, while occasional travelers may find the lower-tier card sufficient.
  • To receive checked bag benefits, your loyalty program number must be included in the reservation and tickets must be purchased with the eligible card.
  • Statement credits for services like ride-hailing and grocery delivery require activation and may have specific expiration dates or booking requirements.
  • Upgrading does not typically provide a new cardholder bonus, so applying for a new account may be preferable if eligible.
