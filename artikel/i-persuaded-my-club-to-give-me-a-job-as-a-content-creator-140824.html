Making videos with fans and players at his local football club is all in a day's work for Jacob.

He's a content creator for Bolton Wanderers FC, he comes up with new ideas for social media, filming videos and posts them onto official pages. The aim is to encourage people to follow the club.

Industry observers note that there are many different ways to develop a career in football content creation, from apprenticeships to freelance work and full-time employment. It isn't just about making videos - content creators need to understanding their audience, respond to analytics and adapt material for different platforms.

Jacob works as a content creator for Bolton Wanderers and his career progression has followed this path:.

Voluntary work at Bolton --> Professional content creator.

According to recent reports, while it might look simple, he's had to be resilient - and one of the biggest obstacles he faced was convincing Bolton to create his role. "Don't wait for an opportunity to come to you," he says. "If you're good at it, it does then sometimes lead into getting a role. If it doesn't, you've created a portfolio and it's really good for your CV and future opportunities.".
