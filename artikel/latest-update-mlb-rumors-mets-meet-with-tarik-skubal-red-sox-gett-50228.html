Latest Update: MLB rumors: Mets meet with Tarik Skubal, Red Sox getting calls for multiple players, plus Cubs, Red Sox, more

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The annual GM Meetings came and went last week with two significant transactions. The Braves signed reliever Evan Phillips (three years and $35 million) and the Red Sox signed slugger Jake Bauers (four years and $58 million). Bauers will give Boston some desperately needed home run power. Phillips will pair with Robert Suarez in the late innings for Atlanta.

In a recent development, otherwise, we're still waiting on the winter's first true blockbuster, and the clock is ticking with a lockout expected upon the collective bargaining agreement's expiration at 11:59 p.m. ET on Dec. 1. Here are our top 50 free agents and top 25 trade candidates, and the important offseason dates and deadlines you need to know. Here now are Tuesday's hot stove happenings.

Industry observers note that teams are calling the Rangers about left-hander MacKenzie Gore, who is a year away from free agency, according to The Athletic. There is some reluctance to trade for players with only one year of control because games could be lost to a work stoppage next year, but pitching is so in-demand that at least some clubs are exploring a deal for Gore.

According to recent reports, now 27, Gore is one of only six pitchers to make at least 30 starts in each of the last three years. His under-the-hood numbers (3.87 FIP and 4.19 expected ERA) were much better than his 4.58 actual ERA, plus he has long been considered to have untapped upside. Gore will give you innings, at minimum, and there's a chance his best days are ahead of him.

The Red Sox are exploring the trade market for righties Kutter Crawford, Tanner Houck, and Johan Oviedo, according to WEEI. They are also believed to be preparing to trade Jarren Duran, reports MassLive.com. Duran is a potential non-tender candidate if the Red Sox are unable to find a trade partner prior to Friday's deadline. The Mariners and Phillies are among the teams that are "poking around.".

It has been revealed that crawford, Houck, and Ovideo all missed significant time with injuries in 2026 (Crawford has not appeared in an MLB game since 2024) and are far down Boston's rotation depth chart. Duran has been pushed out of the everyday lineup by the recent Jake Bauers signing. Exploring trades for these four players in a seller's market is a sensible approach.

The Mets had an in-person meeting with top-ranked free agent Tarik Skubal on Monday, reports the Latest York Post. Owner Steve Cohen and president of baseball operations David Stearns were among those in attendance, according to SNY. Skubal and agent Scott Boras will meet with the Blue Jays, Dodgers, Padres, and potentially two other teams in the coming days.

According to recent reports, skubal is expected to sign before the lockout and is therefore working on a tight schedule, much tighter than usual for top Boras clients. Nolan McLean and Christian Scott give the Mets two promising young arms to build around, but they need to improve the rotation around them, and Skubal would give the club a no-doubt ace to lead the pitching staff.

Recent findings suggest that the pitching-needy Cubs have shown interest in free agent lefty Trevor Rogers, reports The Athletic. Chicago needs multiple starters this offseason and has cast a wide net in their search. Justin Steele (UCL) and Ben Brown (neck), ostensibly the Cubs' No. 1 and 2 starters, are both recovering from surgeries, and Brown is expected to miss the start of 2027.

We ranked Rogers as the third-best free agent available this offseason because he is very good as is, and because he'll make analytical models hum with the quality of his arsenal and strike-throwing. Nine pitchers made at least nine starts for the Cubs in 2026 and only two -- Javier Assad and Edward Cabrera -- currently remain under contract/team control.

Industry observers note that the AL East rival Orioles and Yankees are among the teams with interest in free agent slugger Randy Arozarena, according to the Baltimore Banner and New York Post. The O's also have some interest in re-signing Luis Robert Jr. Other than Dylan Beavers and maybe Tyler O'Neill, Baltimore's outfield is wide open right now. They have DH at-bats available too.

Industry observers note that it is likely the Yankees checked in on Arozarena out of due diligence more than serious interest. His righty bat would help their lefty-heavy lineup, though he would be a clunky roster fit as a corner outfielder who really needs to DH on a team that already has plenty of corner outfielders who really need to DH. If Arozarena's market is slow to develop, perhaps New York would pounce.

Teams around the league believe the Rockies are more open to trading All-Star catcher Hunter Goodman now than they were at the trade deadline, reports MLB Network. Colorado is said to have set a very high asking price for Goodman, who in 2026 became just the tenth primary catcher with a 40-homer season. He hit more homers on the road (25) than at Coors Field (16) too.

It has been revealed that ryan Jeffers and Carson Kelly represent viable free agent alternatives to Goodman as right-handed offense-first catchers, though Goodman is several years younger than both and will have a lower salary the next three years through arbitration. Teams will likely feel more confident in Goodman holding his production for another few years than Jeffers and Kelly.
