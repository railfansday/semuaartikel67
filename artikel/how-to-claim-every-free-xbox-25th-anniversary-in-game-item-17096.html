Xbox has had one of the most tumultuous years in its history. Ongoing mass layoffs, a leadership overhaul, and studio closures have plagued what was supposed to be a year celebrating its 25th anniversary. The brand has attempted to try and mark that occasion in good ways too, the latest of which is by giving those who continue to use its consoles and play its game some free stuff.

In a recent development, the anniversary celebrations have been ramped up this week as Sunday, November 15 marked exactly 25 years since the launch of the original Xbox console. To celebrate, Xbox revealed seven rewards you can claim across its biggest games, from unique upcoming armor to green guns, and we’re going to run you through how to claim all of them.

Halo seems like a good place to start since, along with the original console, it’s the series that started it all for Xbox. Halo: Campaign Evolved has a new armor set that makes Master Chief look like he’s wearing pieces of the new translucent green Series X25 consoles.

Sources indicate that if you would rather just have your gun coated in Xbox green rather than your entire protagonist, then you’ll want the rewards up for grabs in Gears of War: E-Day and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 instead. To get E-Day’s Anniversary Weapons Skin set, all you need to do is go to the in-game store and claim it.

Modern Warfare 4’s reward is a little different and, unfortunately, the only one of the six that requires you having to spend some money, at least if you don’t already own the game’s Vault Edition. If you have the edition, you should get the blueprint automatically. If you don’t, you’ll have to purchase the Vault Upgrade to get it.

Industry observers note that back to the completely free rewards, you can celebrate Xbox’s birthday in Minecraft by claiming the OG Green Character Creator Item through the Minecraft dressing room. Hop over to Forza Horizon 6, and you’ll find the GR GT Anniversary Livery. Another one that’s easy to claim, as it should be waiting for you in the game’s Gifts tab.
