As Avengers: Doomsday gets closer, Marvel fans have noticed that the marketing seems to be leaving out a huge chunk of the movie.

The final trailer has arrived, and while it includes plenty of action, it certainly seems that the final act of the movie is being kept in the shadows.

It has been revealed that "Unless I'm wildly misinterpreting the events of the film the 3rd act is still completely unknown right? Oh Godvin Feige…" claims one fan on Twitter.

There is one major team-up shot in the film featuring the Avengers and the X-Men – however, fans seem to think this moment comes closer to the middle of the film, while the true final fight is still under wraps.

"The only shots from the 3rd act (if I were to make an educated guess) are Thor praying and possibly the shot of Kate/Cassie," claims another fan.

"The 360 shot could genuinely be in the first hour man the Russos are crazy with this shit," notes someone else.

"Like others have stated, the Russos have put shots into trailers that ended up being completely fake or real but a severe misdirection in the trailer compared to what actually happens in the film, so we could be flying blind about the 3rd act, or it's secretly right in our face," points out another person.

There may be brief moments from the ending hidden in the footage released so far, but it really does seem that anything significant from the last part of the movie has been concealed.

Avengers: Doomsday arrives this December 18. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the upcoming Marvel movies and TV shows for everything else that's in store.
