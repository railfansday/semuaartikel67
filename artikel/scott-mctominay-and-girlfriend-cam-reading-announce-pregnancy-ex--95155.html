Napoli star Scott McTominay and his girlfriend Cam Reading have unveiled they're expecting their first child together.

The couple shared a joint post on Instagram with one picture of them holding up an ultrasound image, another of Reading reclining in a bikini and a third of the pregnancy test itself.

They revealed the news on the same day McTominay broke Napoli's three-game losing streak, scoring the winner in a 1-0 victory over Torino.

The couple wrote: 'The most precious gift, a lifetime of love.' McTominay, 29, and Reading have settled into life in Italy after he joined from Man United and revived his career, winning the Scudetto and earning Player of the Year honours.

It remains to be seen whether the baby will be born in Italy or back in the UK but the couple were overjoyed at sharing their happy news.

It is not exactly clear when McTominay and Reading first crossed paths but they have at least been together since the summer of 2023, when they were pictured on a boat trip in Greece.

Cam Reading and Scott McTominay shared the news that they're expecting a baby.

Reading posted this picture of her bump on her Instagram page and wrote of a 'precious gift'.

Reading, a director at asset management firm Fortress Capital partners, has seen her personal celebrity skyrocket in that time, zooming up from 10,000 Instagram followers to over 90,000.

According to La Repubblica, they have moved into a house by Lago Patria, a coastal lake near the suburb of Giugliano.

There, McTominay can be by the seaside promptly after popping out of his door and is only around a 20-minute drive from Napoli's training centre.

The couple regularly share photos from their new life together in Italy and McTominay is adored by fans having repeatedly delivered in big moments, none more so than on the final day of the 2024-25 season when an acrobatic strike put Napoli ahead as they clinched the title over Inter Milan.

Industry observers note that this season has been less straightforward for McTominay. The Scotland international underwent heart surgery after being diagnosed with 'a mild benign arrhythmia', which is an abnormal or irregular heart rhythm deemed non-serious and posing no significant risk to overall health.

Before the surgery, the British Heart Foundation explained that the procedure McTominay was having would see heat or cold energy used to create tiny scars in the heart to block faulty signals and restore a typical heartbeat.

The procedure took place at the start of September in Manchester, and he was initially supposed to be on the sidelines for two weeks before returning to training.

But he was ruled out of Scotland action during the international break and is now back on the grass with Napoli, scoring the winner against Torino on Saturday.

McTominay and Reading also posted this snap of the ultrasound and positive test.

The midfielder scored the winner on Saturday for Napoli against Torino.

He has now scored 33 goals in 92 matches across all competitions with the Partenopei and remains one of Serie A's leading lights.

The Italian press gushed over his contribution on Saturday, with Corriere dello Sport writing: 'He played with fire in his belly.

'Three shots in the opening 16 minutes; seven in total—three on target— and a decisive goal (his fourth against Torino, his favourite Serie A opponent, along with Inter), plus the charisma of a leader.'.
