QuickBooks Online Review – The Accounting Software Your Accountant Knows, at a Higher Price

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that choose QuickBooks Online if your accountant already works in it or your business tracks inventory or projects. Prepares with accountant access cost $38 to $340 a month after three discounted months, so if you mostly send invoices, a cheaper plan or app does that for less.

It has been revealed that picking accounting software is mostly a decision about who else will work in your books. If a bookkeeper or accountant closes your books each month or files your business return, using the program they already know saves time on both sides. If nobody else logs in, you pay for collaboration you never use.

Industry observers note that quickBooks Online is the cloud accounting software Intuit sells to small businesses. Intuit, founded in 1983 and based in Mountain View, California, also makes TurboTax and notes about 100 million customers use its products. It says 650,000 accountants worldwide use its platform, and every proposal from Simple Start up includes free logins for two or three of yours. QuickBooks customers are also paying more. Intuit’s fiscal 2026 results, for the year that ended July 31, 2026, show QuickBooks Online Accounting revenue up 23%. Intuit said the fourth quarter’s 20% gain came from customers paying more on average, more customers, and changes in which plans they buy.

QuickBooks Online comes in six plans. QuickBooks Free and QuickBooks Lite are for one person working alone, while the four business plans, Simple Start through Advanced, add accountant access and, from Essentials up, more users. Each business strategy includes everything in the one below it, so your choice comes down to how many people need logins and whether you track inventory or projects.

Fresh customers on a monthly proposal choose between 50% off for 3 months and a 30-day free trial, then pay the plan’s current price each month until they cancel, with no annual contract. By our math, the discount saves $70 in year one. Over the first 12 months, Plus costs $1,470 with it (three months at $70, then nine at $140) and $1,540 with the trial (11 paid months at $140).

Not every business needs Simple Start. If you mostly send invoices, QuickBooks Lite handles them for $20 a month without accountant access, and Wave’s free Starter blueprint covers unlimited invoices, estimates, bills, and bookkeeping records. If your team needs more logins but no inventory tracking, compare Xero, which charges no per-user license fees.

It has been revealed that every strategy pulls in transactions from the bank and card accounts you connect, one bank on Free and two on Lite. From Simple Start up, QuickBooks copies the categories you choose and applies them to newly introduced transactions automatically. Intuit Intelligence, Intuit’s name for its AI tools, does more at each level:.

Intuit still calls Intuit Intelligence a beta release, so its features and limits can change. You choose which tasks run on their own and can undo what the AI did. Only admin users get the chat, and each initiative caps how much they can use it each month.

Industry observers note that quickBooks Free caps invoices at two a month unless you qualify for and turn on QuickBooks Payments, which lets customers pay an invoice by card or bank transfer. Payments has no monthly fee, but each transaction costs:.

Recent findings suggest that bill pay comes with every business proposal. Intuit’s plan pages list five free standard ACH bill payments a month, an allotment they say may vary, and $0.50 for each one after that. Its Bill Pay page calls standard ACH free on every plan, so confirm the fee with Intuit before you pay more than five vendors a month that way. Checks cost $1.50, and Faster ACH, which usually arrives the next business day, costs $10. For $15 a month, Bill Pay Premium adds AI bill entry and unlimited e-filing of the 1099 forms you send contractors. Bill Pay Elite, at $45 a month, adds approval workflows and user roles, and it has come free with Advanced since Aug. 1, 2026.

Inventory and job costing start at Plus. Plus updates quantities on hand automatically, alerts you when an item needs reordering, drafts purchase orders, and turns them into bills when the goods arrive. Its project tools track labor, payroll, and expenses for each job, and it adds budgets and up to 40 classes and locations for sorting income and expenses. Advanced lifts that limit and adds custom reports and dashboards, two-way syncing with Excel, and an estimates-versus-actuals report for projects.

In a recent development, no QuickBooks Online proposal includes payroll. Intuit sells it separately as QuickBooks Workforce, at a monthly base fee plus a fee for each employee:.

Latest customers pay half the base fee for three months, but per-employee fees stay at full price. At full price, a five-person team on Workforce Payroll pays $85 a month, $50 plus $35 in employee fees.

Expert Assisted, at $59 a month after a 30-day free trial, gives you a bookkeeping expert to answer questions but not to work in your books. Full-Service Bookkeeping, with a dedicated bookkeeper doing the monthly work, starts at $300 a month after a one-time cleanup fee, and a cleanup alone starts at $600. Neither service prepares or files your taxes.

Phone support comes with every business initiative and is free during the trial, while QuickBooks Free gets chat. Advanced adds Priority Circle, a premium support line with on-demand training. The Better Business Bureau (BBB) gives Intuit an F, citing complaints it failed to answer or resolve. It logged more than 4,600 complaints against Intuit in the last three years, nearly 2,000 of them in the past 12 months. Those counts cover all of Intuit’s products, TurboTax and Mailchimp included, not QuickBooks alone.

Sources indicate that leaving QuickBooks takes planning. The Export data tool saves your reports and lists to a single .zip of Excel files. Estimates, purchase orders, attachments, and your chart of accounts (the list of categories your books use) export separately, and Intuit’s help page names two ways out, Excel or QuickBooks Desktop. After you cancel a paid initiative, you keep read-only access for a year to finish exporting. Cancel during or after a free trial, and Intuit keeps your data for 90 days.

QuickBooks Online is the safe pick when someone besides you works in the books. Your accountant gets in free, Plus handles inventory and job costing, and the account grows to 25 users on Advanced without a move. On the higher intends, its AI now does real cleanup and reconciliation work.

It’s poor value for a one-person business that mostly sends invoices. Simple Start’s $38 a month buys accountant access and reports that business won’t use, while QuickBooks Lite handles invoicing for $20 and Wave for nothing. The discounted price also doubles after the third month, and payroll costs extra. If your accountant already works in QuickBooks, pick the proposal that covers your headcount and budget for its full price from the fourth month.
