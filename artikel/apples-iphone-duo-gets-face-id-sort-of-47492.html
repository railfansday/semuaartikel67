📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

One of iPhone Duo's biggest disadvantages is the lack of Face ID. Apple's convenient way of unlocking the phone just by presenting your face simply did not fit on the thin foldable device, so Apple went with Touch ID on the power button instead.

Sources indicate that but Touch ID, as quick as it is, is just a tad more of a hassle than simply picking the Duo up and having it unlock with a glance. Luckily, Apple figured out a way to fix this.

In the latest developer beta of iOS 27.3, the Duo has a latest option, located under Settings - Touch ID & Passcode, called "Unlock with Front Camera." Turn it on, go through a quick onboarding process (similar to the one you do with the regular Face Unlock on other iPhones), and you'll be able to unlock the foldable just by looking at its front screen.

I've tried it out on my Duo, and it works as expected, easily unlocking the phone every time, even when my face was a little off to the side. There are caveats here, though. Face ID includes a 3D scanning mechanism making it a lot more secure, while this newly introduced feature on the Duo uses the front camera alone for face recognition. This is why Apple put a few additional safeguards in place. First, this system only works for unlocking the phone; for Apple Pay or accessing your password, you'll still need to use Touch ID. Also, face unlock won't work after 45 minutes of inactivity; you'll have to use Touch ID or passcode after that much time has passed. Finally, the system only works on the outer display, which has a better camera. Unlocking the phone via the FaceTime camera on the inner display will not work.

Apple's own warning below the option in the settings confirms this. "Use the front camera, along with a recent Touch ID or passcode authentication, to swiftly unlock iPhone Duo when it's closed. Face recognition data never leaves your device. To use Apple Pay or to access your password, Touch ID is still required.".

If you don't mind the weakened security, this is a pretty handy option, even given its limitations. I've already set up to unlock the iPhone Duo with my Apple Watch Ultra, so this doesn't change much, but users who don't have the Watch or don't wear it all the time might appreciate the face unlock feature.
