📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This past week resulted in one of the biggest correction of 2026. Bitcoin drop to a two-year low. Billions of positions were liquidated across crypto exchanges on November 22.

Ripple has achieved an important milestone, with RLUSD reaching top five stablecoins by market cap.

On the contrary, XRP dropped below $1.00, a significant psychological level that XRP held since November 2024.

Bitcoin trading volume has surged to its highest level since February as the world’s largest cryptocurrency stages a dramatic recovery from two-year lows, triggering hundreds of millions of dollars in liquidations.

Industry observers note that the massive spike in trading volume comes after Bitcoin tanked to two-year lows on Nov. 21 before bouncing back sharply to regain ground toward the psychologically crucial $60,000 level.

It has been revealed that the massive spike in trading volume comes in the wake of one of the biggest sell-offs in BTC this year. On Nov. 21, the world’s largest cryptocurrency dropped as low as around $55,100, a level last seen in September 2024.

Sources indicate that the sharp decline in Bitcoin prices came as risk assets tumbled amid rising concerns over the U.S. stock market and the artificial intelligence (AI) sector, which weighed on investor sentiment.

Ripple’s dollar-pegged stablecoin, RLUSD, has now entered the top five stablecoins by market cap, reaching a upcoming all-time high in its circulating supply, a major milestone for the stablecoin less than two years after its launch. RLUSD went live in December 2024.

RLUSD has surpassed World Liberty Financial USD (USD1), Global Dollar (USDG) and PayPal USD (PYUSD) to become the fifth-largest stablecoin in the world. Only Tether’s USDT, Circle’s USDC, Ethena’s USDe, and Dai (DAI) are ahead of it.

RLUSD’s total circulating supply across the XRP Ledger and Ethereum has reached a upcoming high, with $2.85 billion in circulation, according to Ripple’s stablecoin tracker.

Sources indicate that shiba Inu's exchange activity keeps on bringing more to the table. This time, 372.39 billion tokens flowed into exchanges.

Inflows recorded an increase of 0.36% over 24 hours, which once again raises the chances of a surge in market pressure, data from CryptoQuant suggests.

While the inflow number to exchanges is quite high, the total netflow doesn't hit the value of 100 billion, suggesting that the amount of inflows after withdrawals doesn't really disrupt the overall picture for Shiba Inu.

Outflows accounted for 280.82 billion SHIB, leaving a net inflow of 91.57 billion. About three quarters of the deposits were offset by withdrawals.

Sources indicate that U.S. and Canadian authorities have launched a massive cross-border crackdown on cryptocurrency fraud, bringing together the U.S.

Secret Service, Canadian law enforcement agencies and financial regulators to dismantle scam networks.

According to the Canadian securities regulator, the operation involves investigators, securities specialists and financial crime experts from both countries.

The initiative will use advanced technology and enhanced investigative capabilities to identify fraudulent activity and potential victims in near real time.
