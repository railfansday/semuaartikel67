If there’s one thing I take almost as seriously as chasing some good snow, it’s having the right wardrobe to go with it. I’m a lifelong snowboarder, a fairly recent convert to skiing, and someone who has spent plenty of winters bouncing between mountains on the east coast and out west. I’ve learned that the best of the best ski gear needs to do a lot more than look good in a lodge photo. It has to hold up through long lift rides, changing conditions, unexpected snowfall, and the occasional unglamorous tumble.

But that doesn’t mean style needs to take a backseat. In fact, I’d argue that the two should go hand in hand. That's a big part of why the popular ski brand, Halfdays, first caught my attention three years ago—and why I keep coming back season after season. Founded in 2020 by former Olympic freestyle aerial skier Kiley McKinnon and Ariana Ferweda, Halfdays came onto the ski scene with a refreshing take: women's ski apparel that actually considers women. Not just smaller versions of men’s gear or the usual boxy, so-called unisex silhouettes, but (finally) thoughtfully designed outerwear that takes fit, function, and fashion all very seriously.

Commerce writer Jessica Chapel is wearing the Halfdays Ashton jacket and Alessandra pant in Stowe, Vermont.

In a recent development, the outdoor apparel industry has come a long way, but finding technical pieces that feel just as considered aesthetically as they are functional on the mountain can still be a bit of a treasure hunt. So as someone who has accumulated her fair share of ski and snowboard gear over the years, I immediately fell in love with the brand’s mission, and eventually, the gear itself.

While I’ve come to adore its ski suits, bibs, and winter jackets, Halfdays' assortment has expanded beyond the big-ticket ski uniform. The brand offers sweat-wicking baselayers, cozy fleeces, everyday outdoor staples, and chic accessories that make sense well beyond your stay at a ski resort. My favorite pieces from Halfdays keep me feeling comfortable and put together on harsh winter days in New York City too, from knit headbands that cover my ears to winter leggings and warm socks that aren’t too thick.

In a recent development, of course, Halfdays isn’t the same brand it was when I first discovered it. What began as a ski-focused label has evolved into an outdoor brand with spring and summer collections spanning hiking, running, and more warm-weather pursuits. Its arrival at major retailers like Nordstrom, REI, and Backcountry, the opening of its Denver flagship (along with plenty of other storefronts), and a string of collaborations have helped solidify it as a functional brand for every outdoor adventurer.

Chapel is wearing the Parks Projects x Halfdays set on the slopes in Park City, Utah.

According to recent reports, that said, the brand has been having some fun with its fashion credentials, most recently teaming up with Gigi Hadid’s Guest in Residence on an eight-piece winter capsule. The result is a nostalgic take on mountainwear, bringing Guest in Residence’s signature knitwear looks to Halfdays' mountain-ready vibes, with playful retro details that feel just as at home at après-ski as they do on the slopes.

Recent findings suggest that on the performance side, though, I’m excited about the brand’s newest launch: Halfdays has officially joined forces with Gore-Tex for its most technical outerwear collection yet. From down jackets and shells to bibs and mittens, the colorful collection brings together trusted waterproof, windproof, and breathable technology with the fit and design that initially made me fall for Halfdays in the first place.

As another winter of snow chasing commences, I’m giving my cold-weather wardrobe yet another Halfdays refresh. Below, my tried-and-true ski season favorites from Halfdays, along with a few winter staples I’ve got my eye on.

According to recent reports, the Lawrence jacket is designed with Primaloft insulation, keeping you warm on the coldest winter days. It features underarm vents and an exterior chest pocket with an attached phone leash. Plus, the hood is adjustable, so you’ll get a perfect fit over your helmet every time. The jacket is best for bluebird days given its cropped length, but it’s also available in a longer version too.

I like to pair the Lawrence jacket with these matching shell pants. With a slim fit, an adjustable waistband, a water-resistant exterior, and zippered hems for snow boots, this pair is just as practical as it is stylish. Pro tip: Try these bad boys on with a pair of your favorite winter leggings underneath before hitting the mountain, just so you know that you’ll be comfortable and warm all day long.

In a recent development, don’t underestimate the importance of a solid baselayer. The Johnson top features mesh underarms and a sweat-wicking finish, so you don’t have to worry about overheating in your ski gear. The long-sleeve layer also has a silicon grip on the inside hem, preventing it from sliding up as you move.

Pair the Johnson top with these leggings for a stylish, mountain-ready set. They’re sweat-wicking and feature a layer of mesh on the calves for added breathability. Even better, they’re versatile enough to wear off the mountain too, whether you’re shopping at a holiday market or strolling through the park.

In a recent development, made with 100% merino wool, this chic Jacquard headband will keep your head and ears warm on the slopes—and it tastefully reps the brand too. I have (and love) it in the color Blue Fog, but it’s also available in black and red.

Industry observers note that I’m obsessed with these Gore-Tex x Halfdays mittens. Along with the insulated, breathable, and waterproof design, there’s also a leather palm construction for added grip and durability.

I can’t wait to put this three-layer shell jacket to the test on my trip to Stowe, Vermont, this December. It has all the classic Gore-Tex technologies that make the collaboration so enticing in the first place: engineered with durable water- and wind-proof material, and breathable protection without added insulation.

It has been revealed that for a matching set, grab this three-layer shell bib constructed with the same Gore-Tex build. With adjustable shoulder straps, a drop-seat construction, thigh vents, and gusseted hems, you’ll be covered and ready for anything, from head to toe.

According to recent reports, ah, one of the early Halfday classics. The tried-and-true Ashton jacket does it all, from ski trips to cabin getaways. With an adjustable (and removable) belt buckle enclosure, a helmet hood, and its many pockets for your phone and ski pass, it offers every bit of function you need for a full day on the mountain.

According to recent reports, for those who favor a puffy yet packable one-piece, the Murphy ski suit is another one of Halfdays' original bestsellers. I own this bad boy in red, and wear it when I need extra warmth on the mountain. One thing to note: I’m 5'3", and it’s just a bit long on my legs when I’m not in snow boots—but it’s not very often that I wear the suit without them.

For another vintage-inspired piece from the Guest in Residence collection, this cable knit inspired down jacket is all about staying warm in style. (And I’ve already added it to my cart.).

Get your hands on one of the Parks Project x Halfdays ski jackets while you still can. This gorgeous print is inspired by the Rocky Mountains—and I always receive compliments from other shredders when I wear it out on the slopes. Not to mention, it keeps me warm and comfy while doing so!
