The Rooks were held to a goalless draw at The Dripping Pan last Saturday by a stubborn Stanway Rovers side who started the day bottom of the Isthmian Premier Division table.

However, they responded well in midweek by avoiding a Sussex Senior Cup upset at 21st-placed Isthmian South East Division side Eastbourne Town, winning 2-1 at The Saffrons.

After both games, manager Craig Nelson admitted there were areas for his side to improve.

Following the Stanway game, which was Nelson’s 100th match in charge of the Rooks, the 40-year-old said: “For me, we had too many players [who] it felt like [were] going through the motions.

In a recent development, “The intensity felt a bit low, and the ball speed was low. We breached their backline so many times, but the quality beyond that, for me, was a little bit too low to come away with a victory.

Sources indicate that “Other than the first chance in the early minutes from Noel [Fisher], where for me he should score, I don't remember us having a clear-cut chance. So, not a great day at the office, in my opinion.”.

Despite being more fluent in attack at Eastbourne Town, with an impressive performance from debutant Kwesi Ntim playing a big part, Lewes still lacked some ruthlessness after going 2-0 up.

Afterwards, Nelson stated: “I think we had moments. They're always going to make the game difficult for us, and the conditions obviously don't help.

“We had some good moments in the second half. Probably more opportunities to score. On another day, you probably leave here with a bit more.

Industry observers note that “I think sometimes in cup games, the performance is secondary to actually progressing. We wanted to make sure that we put a strong outfit out because we want to progress in the cup, and we've done that, so happy days.”.

Lewes can celebrate a first appearance in the Senior Cup’s Quarter-Finals since the 2019-20 season as a result, and will learn their opponents when the draw is expected to take place in the next fortnight.

In the meantime, the Rooks make the 171-mile round trip to Ramsgate FC this Saturday, with the Rams sitting just three points behind Nelson’s side in 6th place.

The Kent side are the third-highest goalscorers in the league so far with 38 goals from 17 games, while Callum Jones’ 10 league strikes make him the division’s second-best individual goalscorer.

Nelson said: “Ramsgate is a really hostile place to go. They're going to be quick and physical, so we have to make sure that we bring that work ethic and match exactly what the game demands of us.”.
