A year ago, Raul Gonzalez Blanco left the fold. Legend of Real Madrid with whom he won 3 Champions Leagues and 6 Spanish championships, the 49-year-old had started a coaching career. Having moved to the youth teams of Casa Blanca, he held this role for 6 years, notably winning a Youth League during the 2019/2020 season. After several years where he had results, he applied to replace Carlo Ancelotti in the summer of 2025: “I’m happy to be here. I am where I want to be. When the time comes to assess the situation and the club makes decisions, if it ever makes them, which I don’t know, we’ll see.».

In a recent development, but in the summer of 2025, he left the Merengue club following the appointment of Xabi Alonso. Considered too soft by the Madrid management, Raul then slammed the door while waiting to find a first real challenge as number one coach. It was ultimately the Real Oviedo club which took the gamble by signing him a contract until June 2028. And while he has just led his very first training session with his newly introduced team, Raul arrives in a very complicated context for a coach who is launching his career.

As revealed Marcahis lack of experience is particularly worrying as the Azules are going through a difficult period. Relegated from La Liga last season, Real Oviedo is in ninth place, one point behind the play-off places. From a sporting point of view, there is nothing insurmountable about getting back into the race for promotion to La Liga. The problem mainly comes from the internal situation within the Asturias club.

According to recent reports, arriving within a squad whose contours he was unable to construct, Raul will have to quickly convince since Mexican businessman Jesús Martínez Patiño is quickly expecting a marked style of play from the Spanish technician. This was one of the reasons that pushed the leader to part ways with Julián Calero. In 4 years, 6 coaches have succeeded one another with Álvaro Cervera, Luis Carrión, Javi Calleja, Veljko Paunovic, Guillermo Almada and Julián Calero. An instability which has widened a gap between leaders and supporters.

The departure of Veljko Paunovic to bring back Luis Carrión last season in full operation to maintain La Liga was a moment of rift between the fans and the management. Additionally, Real Oviedo fans are often fans of FC Barcelona and Real Madrid. Through his attachment to the Merengue club, Raul represents a point of disagreement in the stands and will have to convince with the results. He will have to revitalize an offensive sector in difficulty with only 19 goals scored in 13 matches and big absentees like Carlos Fernández and Chris Ramos. A context far from easy for Raul who does not arrive on conquered territory.
