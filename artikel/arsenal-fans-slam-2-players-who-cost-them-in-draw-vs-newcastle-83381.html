Arsenal had to settle for a 2-2 draw away to Newcastle United in a chaotic game that has raised question marks about two of Mikel Arteta's players.

Just when it looked likely the Gunners were running away with the Premier League title, the Magpies came along. Matthias Jaissle came into the game under pressure amid a disappointing start to his reign, and his side were expected to suffer a fifth league defeat.

Sources indicate that when Eberechi Eze's 33rd-minute opener went in, it looked set to be a long night for Jaissle and his team. They were outplayed throughout the first half, but came out a different side after the break, taking the game to the defending champions.

Harvey Barnes equalised in the 49th minute to lift the mood at St James' Park before a pinball-like scenario in the visitors' box led to pandemonium in the 62nd minute. Malick Thiaw pounced to poke the ball into the back of the net and give his side a shock lead.

Sources indicate that that lead lasted until the 78th minute when Noni Madueke did superbly well to find the bottom corner. Mikel Arteta couldn't hide his delight on the touchline, though he wouldn't have been pleased with dropping two points.

In a recent development, one of the major talking points heading into tonight's game centred on Bruno Guimaraes' return to Tyneside. The manner of his departure in the summer left a sour taste in Newcastle fans' mouths, and they let him know it every time he touched the ball.

Recent findings suggest that guimaraes didn't have anywhere near the impact he'd have hoped for, and Arteta's midfield overall wasn't quite up to the high standards we've come to expect from them. Once the Gunners took the lead, they should have kicked on and secured all three points, but instead they allowed the hosts back into the game.

A frustrating night for Arteta was made even worse when David Raya suffered an injury and had to be replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga in the second half. Coming out of November's international break, he probably viewed this fixture favourably, yet a strange affair has thrown up a fair few concerns.

Sources indicate that arsenal fans haven't had much to complain about so far this season, certainly not the performances of Martin Odegaard. The Norwegian endured a frustrating previous campaign due to injury issues, unable to play a star role in his side's title win.

Odegaard has been back to his best this term, but his display at St James' Park didn't live up to expectations. Likewise, Guimaraes' subdued return as an opponent led to frustration among his current club's fanbase.

Supporters have largely blamed the pair for the dropped points, with one writing: "ODEGAARD AND BRUNO GUIMARAES OFF ASAP! They are off the pace in this game. Killing us.".

Another fan added: "Odegaard and Guimaraes are too weak in midfield.".

One supporter bemoaned a perceived lack of effort: "Odegaard and Bruno Guimaraes simply don't deserve to play with some of these very special players we have. How about they bloody contribute?".

Another expressed doubts over the summer fee: "Still yet to be convinced that spending £75m on Guimaraes was wise.".

One fan added: "Bruno Guimaraes and Martin Odegaard midfield would never work. Both of them seem to be running away from any form of contact. It’s the main reason we ain’t winning this game.".

Another took issue with Arteta's skipper and his leadership: "Odegaard is no captain quality.".

Arteta has plenty of midfield options in his squad, including Myles Lewis-Skelly, who came on for Guimaraes, as well as Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino. He'll be hoping for an improvement from his team when they host Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday.
