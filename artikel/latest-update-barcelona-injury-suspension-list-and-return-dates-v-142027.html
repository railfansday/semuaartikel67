Barcelona will continue their 2026-27 La Liga campaign against Deportivo La Coruna on Sunday afternoon.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up all of Barcelona's injury and suspension news ahead of their contest with Deportivo, who are 12th in the division.

Espart is out for another three weeks or so due to an ankle injury; the youngster faces a battle to be unavailable before the winter break in Spain.
