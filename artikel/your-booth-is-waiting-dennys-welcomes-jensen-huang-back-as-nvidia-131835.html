📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Jensen Huang is back at the helm of Nvidia — and Denny's has a booth waiting for him.

Sources indicate that the American diner chain welcomed the billionaire's return as Nvidia CEO by revealing that it had reinstated a commemorative plaque marking the birthplace of the artificial intelligence chip giant after learning of its removal on Nov. 13.

"Some things you just don't take off the menu. Jensen Huang is one of them," Denny's remarked in a post on X.

The plaque marks the booth at a Denny's restaurant in Silicon Valley, California, where Huang and his co-founders discussed the idea for Nvidia, which was founded in 1993.

Industry observers note that the restaurant chain said it learned on Nov. 13, a day after Huang's resignation, that the plaque had been removed.

"We put the plaque right back up where it belongs," Denny's mentioned, without specifying who had removed it or why.

Industry observers note that huang's ties to the diner chain, however, stretch back well before Nvidia's founding. He worked at Denny's as a teenager, washing dishes and serving customers before going on to build one of the world's most valuable companies.

In a recent development, the restaurant chain also disclosed that it had contacted Nvidia's communications team on Nov. 10 to express its pride in their shared history.

Huang's return came less than two weeks after his sudden resignation rattled investors and raised questions about corporate governance and succession planning at the chipmaker.

While his return has put those problems in the spotlight, Denny's offered a simpler message to its former employee.

"Jensen, your booth is waiting and the coffee's on. We're always open, 24/7," the restaurant chain mentioned.
