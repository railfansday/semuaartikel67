If you’ve been putting off replacing your running shoes until after your last race of the year, consider this your sign. Between Amazon’s Black Friday sale and Saucony’s own holiday sale, some of our favorite trainers and racing shoes are going for considerably less than we’d normally pay.

It has been revealed that what’s particularly great about this sale is that you don’t necessarily have to settle for a shoe that’s two or three generations old to get a good deal. The Ride 19, our pick for the best Saucony daily trainer, is included in the brand’s sale, as is the Endorphin Pro 5, one of the standouts from our 2026 Shoe Awards. That said, if you don’t mind running in last season’s shoes, Amazon has some seriously good deals on older Endorphin models.

Through Cyber Monday, Saucony is offering 25 percent off eligible footwear and apparel with the code CHEER. Amazon has even steeper markdowns, though prices vary by size and preferred colorway.

Whether you’re getting a head start on spring marathon training or just looking for something comfortable to log your winter miles, here are the Saucony deals worth shopping this Black Friday.

Sources indicate that carbon-plated racing shoes rarely come cheap, which is why we’re particularly excited about this deal. The Endorphin Elite 2 has an exceptionally soft, springy midsole that makes picking up the pace a whole lot of fun. With the Elite 3 now available, you can get its predecessor for less than half its original retail price. That’s a pretty incredible deal for a shoe of this caliber.

If you’re only buying one pair of running shoes during Black Friday, the Ride 19 is a good place to start. It’s the kind of no-fuss daily trainer you can reach for regardless of what’s on your training schedule, whether that’s a few easy miles around the neighborhood or a longer weekend run. It strikes a nice balance between cushioning and responsiveness without feeling excessive in either department.

Sources indicate that the Endorphin Speed 5 is a particularly good buy for runners who want a little more excitement from their training shoes without investing in a dedicated carbon-plated racer. Its nylon plate and Pwrrun PB cushioning make it well suited for intervals and tempo runs, but it’s versatile enough to wear for longer efforts, too. And now that the Speed 6 is out, the previous model is considerably more affordable.

The Endorphin Pro 4 has been a favorite among our editors for its responsive ride and relatively accommodating fit. It combines two types of performance foam with a carbon-fiber plate, giving runners plenty of cushioning for longer races without sacrificing that snappy sensation you want from a racing shoe. At nearly half off, it’s worth considering if you have a marathon on the calendar for 2027.

Industry observers note that stability shoes have come a long way from the stiff, bulky trainers they once were. The Guide 19 uses a broad platform and supportive geometry to help guide your stride while still offering plenty of cushioning underfoot. It's a worthwhile option for runners who want a little extra support, especially for everyday mileage.

If you’d rather invest in a newer racing shoe, the Endorphin Pro 5 is also discounted. Its combination of performance foam and a carbon plate delivers the responsive ride we look for in a race-day shoe, earning it a spot in our 2026 Shoe Awards. Getting $60 off a current-generation racing shoe is hard to ignore.

The Guide 18 is still a solid option for runners who want a comfortable, supportive daily trainer without paying full price for its successor. Our testers appreciated its cushioning and accommodating fit, making it an especially appealing shoe if you typically find stability trainers too restrictive.

For those who prefer spending their miles off the pavement, the Peregrine 16 offers the traction you need to tackle uneven terrain. Its grippy outsole makes it a practical addition to your winter running rotation, especially if your usual routes take you through muddy trails or loose gravel.
