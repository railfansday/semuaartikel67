📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin’s network activity continues to show signs of resilience despite mounting price pressure.

According to leading on-chain analytics provider Santiment, Bitcoin is creating about 50,000 new addresses per day, showing accelerating network growth despite the market downturn.

Tether’s USDT on Ethereum is also experiencing increased adoption, adding roughly 25,000 newly introduced addresses over the past month.

Therefore, this simultaneous growth is encouraging given the broader market weakness.

Why is this the case? Well, Bitcoin’s expanding address count points to rising network participation, while USDT’s growth illustrates continued activity in trading, payments, and capital transfers across the crypto ecosystem.

If both networks maintain this momentum through the final weeks of 2026, there is light at the end of the tunnel since it could strengthen the foundation for a more sustainable market recovery heading into 2027.

In a concerning twist, Santiment also revealed that wallets holding between 10 and 10,000 BTC have collectively offloaded approximately 150,000 BTC, representing a 1.1% reduction in their holdings since November 2.

Over the same period, Bitcoin dropped by 24.5%, sliding from $76,700 to $57,900.

Why is this development worth a keen eye? Well, sharks and whales still control 67.55% of Bitcoin’s circulating supply, making their investment decisions particularly influential.

Therefore, continued distribution from this group could increase selling pressure and undermine attempts at a sustained recovery.

According to Santiment, a stabilization in their collective holdings around 13.58 million BTC, followed by renewed accumulation, would improve the prospects of a more convincing relief rally.

From the price action front, the top cryptocurrency recently rebounded to the $60,000 zone, thanks to renewed market optimism following Nvidia’s announcement concerning Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as CEO and president, with the current price being $59,805 per CoinGecko data.

In a recent development, nevertheless, Bitcoin is not out of the woods yet since its next four-year cycle bottom could form around $41,000. Therefore, time will tell how the apex cryptocurrency plays out in the short term.
