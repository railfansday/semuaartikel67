The number of texts, DMs, WhatsApp messages, and Slack chats I receive asking for beauty recommendations only skyrockets around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. For better or worse, I’ve become the resident beauty expert among my friends and family, and it seems everywhere I turn, someone has an inquiry about what to buy (especially when the best Black Friday beauty deals are on the line).

According to recent reports, gone are the days of lining up outside mega retailers right after your turkey dinner to rush in store and snag holiday gifts. As an introvert, I’d never endure a line or consumerism chaos in pursuit of any deal, ever. But for those who are sale-insatiable, online shopping requires no change out of your post-Thanksgiving sweats (or leaving the couch). Somehow, Thanksgiving has come and gone—and yes, the deals are here. Of course, holiday shopping season is also good time to stock up the best deals for skincare products, coveted gift sets, and more from your favorite beauty brands. Below is a tight curation of the best Black Friday deals, so far.

Remember: These products rarely go on sale. Known for its thoughtful, skin-enhancing formulas and weighty, sculptural packaging, the brand is rolling out 20% off sitewide, with up to 50% off select items. What to buy? Baby Cheeks blush stick imparts a natural flush, while Eye Want You mascara leaves lashes feathery and soft to the touch. Meanwhile, the brand’s tinted liquid highlight leaves skin with a lit-from-within glow.

Everything from those buzzy direct-to-consumer brands to K-beauty favorites is in one place, with no tab-hopping. What to buy? Take Beauty of Joseon’s brightening face mask, or Merit’s non-sticky lip oil—you can add both to cart right now.

There’s prestige skin-care brand Joanna Czech (from the mastermind of the famed aesthetician), with their hydrating duo that Vogue’s Kiana Murden swears by on sale for a steal. Plus, tried-and-true makeup brushes from Sigma, for those looking to szoosh up the makeup tools.

Credo—with its meticulously vetted products that marry safety, sustainability, and performance—is the go-to destination for the clean beauty buff. Take Sakara’s probiotic and collagen supplements, designed to improve your skin health from the inside out. Then there’s Three Ships’ hydrating serum, which uses tremella mushroom as a botanically derived substitute for hyaluronic acid.

While you’re filling your cart with trending clothing and accessories at Revolve, it’s well worth taking a peek at the beauty deals, too. The selection might surprise you. Those aiming to make their living space cozier might like Vitruvi’s essential oils kit—add a few drops to your diffuser. Plus, there are plenty of hair accessories, like Lele Sadoughi’s major claw clip for those looking to jazz up their next updo.

For the minimalist beauty lover, Merit’s annual cyber sale is a can’t-miss moment—it’s quite literally the brand’s only sale of the year. Speaking of clean beauty powerhouses, Sasha Plavsic launched Ilia 14 years ago with a mission to create skin-first formulas that perform.
