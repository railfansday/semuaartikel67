Turkish champions had to move game on Saturday.

Unai Emery has insisted he will not alter his aims for Aston Villa’s Champions League game at ­Galatasaray on Tuesday night on account of concerns about the state of the pitch. The Turkish champions hastily moved Saturday’s home fixture from Rams Park after starting emergency repairs to the surface this month. The Villa defender Ian Maatsen said he and his teammates were aware the pitch was in a “bad condition”.

In a recent development, villa were informed by Uefa last week that they would be unable to train at the venue the day before the Champions League game in order to give the pitch maximum time to recover after heavy maintenance work. Galatasaray played Saturday’s victory over Samsunspor at Basaksehir Fatih Terim stadium, a significantly smaller ground named after the former Turkey manager, who played for and managed Galatasaray.
