Fiona, the beloved hippo at Cincinnati Zoo, has died after swallowing a visitor's knit hat.

Fiona the hippo received a formal tribute from the U. S. Senate on Friday, one day after the beloved Cincinnati Zoo resident died following a medical emergency.

Industry observers note that sen. Bernie Moreno, R-Ohio, introduced the resolution honoring Fiona's life and legacy while expressing the chamber's gratitude to the zoo's staff for their care and dedication. The measure passed by unanimous consent.

Speaking from the Senate floor next to a photo of the hippopotamus, Moreno described Fiona's death as a "devastating loss" and a tragic accident.

"The people who come to our city, our state, that now don’t have Fiona there," Moreno said. "It’s going to leave a hole in the zoo and a hole in their heart.".

In a recent development, the tribute came one day after the Cincinnati Zoo revealed that Fiona died after ingesting a knit hat that accidentally landed in her enclosure last week. According to the zoo, a visitor tossed the hat into the air outside the exhibit, where it caught on a ceiling fan and was released over the viewing windows into her habitat.

"The vet staff tried everything they could to help her after she ingested a hat that ended up in her habitat by accident last week," the zoo remarked in a Thursday statement. "Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome.".

Industry observers note that fiona, a Nile hippopotamus, rose to international fame in January 2017 after she was born six weeks premature, weighing just 29 pounds. Her survival drew millions of followers online and inspired books, clothing, beer and an ice cream flavor.

In a recent development, tributes poured in across Ohio and Capitol Hill following her death, with Sen. Rand Paul, R-Ky., praising Fiona on X as a "beloved presence" and the Cincinnati Bengals remembering her as "The queen of the Queen City.".

A representative for Moreno's office and the Cincinnati Zoo did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's requests for comment.
