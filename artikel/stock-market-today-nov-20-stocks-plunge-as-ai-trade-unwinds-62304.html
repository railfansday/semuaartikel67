By Emma Newbery – Nov 20, 2026 at 5:15PM EST.

Falling 3.70% to 6,910, the S&P 500 (^GSPC -3.70%) entered a technical correction after a seven-day losing streak, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -5.28%) dropped 5.28% to 22,618, erasing its annual gains. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -1.93%) fell 1.93% to 48,147.

According to recent reports, gold prices fell 3.02% to $3,517.24 as of U.S. market close, and the 10-Year Treasury yield slipped 0.10% to 4.91%. Sector-wise, energy and utility stocks gained, while technology and industrials tumbled.

Nvidia Corp (NVDA -14.01%) plummeted 14.01% following reports of a potential SEC accounting probe that accelerated a broader unwind of artificial intelligence trades. Broadcom Inc (AVGO -12.19%) tumbled 12.26% on unconfirmed reports that Alphabet's (GOOG -2.59%) Google would cut next year's chip shipments. Conversely, UnitedHealth Group Inc (UNH +2.83%)rose after TD Cowen upgraded the stock.

In a recent development, the sell-off in artificial intelligence (AI) stocks accelerated today, as simmering nerves boiled over into widespread loss of confidence. Nvidia's deepening woes only magnified the fallout. The Nasdaq Composite has fallen more than 10% this week, and the S&P 500 officially entered a technical correction after dropping 10% below its record high.

According to recent reports, institutional capital is rotating into defensive sectors, including retail and consumer staples. Walmart Inc (WMT +1.51%)gained after raising its full-year forecast in yesterday's earnings. Investors will be watching holiday grocery spending for signs of consumer resilience.

For investors, these dramatic shifts can be scary. It is vital to remember that markets have always recovered, even from serious sell-offs, and that in the long term, what matters is staying invested.
