According to James Pearce of The Athletic, both Federico Chiesa and Wataru Endo are expected to leave Liverpool by the end of next summer’s transfer window. Neither the Italian winger nor the Japanese midfielder have a place in the long-term aims of head coach Andoni Iraola, and the former Bournemouth boss has already informed them about that.

Chiesa missed the first five Premier League games this season through a back injury, and then he made a two-minute appearance in Liverpool’s home defeat to Manchester City. Endo, meanwhile, hasn’t played at all in any competition, and neither of them is a part of the Champions League squad.

Chiesa is likely to be sold, perhaps in January already, having 18 months left on his contract, while Endo is expected to leave Anfield as a free agent when his deal expires at the end of the season.

It has been revealed that captain Virgil van Dijk, goalkeeper Alisson Becker, left-back Kostas Tsimikas and versatile defender Joe Gomez, are all out of contract in the summer as well. Van Dijk and Alisson are likely to get sign newly introduced deals as they both wish to stay put and remain extremely vital for Liverpool.
