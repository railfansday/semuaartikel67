In a quite bizarre clip, Drew McIntyre, one of the best Superstars in WWE right now, has claimed that one current Rangers ace needs to lose some weight.

Industry observers note that although appreciated for his achievements as a wrestler in the United States, McIntyre was born in Ayr, Scotland, and is known to be a significant fan of the Ibrox outfit. The die-hard Rangers fan showed how invested he is in the team right now, as he urged one player to work on his fitness.

The WWE superstar was featured on the Away Days Shirt Shopping series by Classic Football Shirts on YouTube, where he directly spoke about club captain Lawrence Shankland.

Industry observers note that while talking about the football team he loves, McIntyre took the opportunity to tell Shankland to 'cut back on the burgers' if he wants to become a great like Ally McCoist or Mark Hateley. He explained:.

"I promise if you get off them when you get back from injury and score a bunch of goals, I will bring you In-N-Out burgers on a private jet to ScotlanPd. Lose that belly mate, and score some goals for Rangers! No offence but seriously, we need you!".

There were high expectations when Shankland joined Rangers in the summer, signing from Hearts and instantly becoming club captain. Three league goals and five games – four in 10 across all competitions – was a great start before a hamstring tear ruled the Scotland international out of action until early 2027.

Sources indicate that amid that misfortune, McIntyre has rather bluntly urged the 31-year-old forward to get back to full fitness. Rangers are currently second in the Scottish Premiership, four points behind Celtic, who have played an extra fixture.

McIntyre's passion for the Scottish giants was evident as he also recalled a conversation with former club striker Mark Hateley, involving Graeme Souness and Ally McCoist, which he felt relevant to Shankland. The WWE star explained:.

"When I did the tour of Ibrox, (Mark) Hateley told me a story that is relevant right now – Shankland I am talking to you.

"Hateley told me that when he came in (to Rangers), Souness gave him the number nine to upset Ally (McCoist).

"And the reason he did it was to make him (McCoist) work harder and get himself in better shape and score more goals.

"McCoist said 'Well I am scoring all these goals already' but Souness was like 'he can be better'.

"So Ally trained harder, and in the next season he won the Golden Boot. That could be you, Shankland. Put the work in mate, lose the carbs and you'll win the Golden Boot next year.".

Perhaps McIntyre's comments will light a similar fire in Shankland's belly.
