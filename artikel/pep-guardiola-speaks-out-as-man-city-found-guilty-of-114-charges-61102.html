According to recent reports, manchester City have been found guilty of 114 financial charges, the Premier League has confirmed. The verdict follows an eight-week hearing that opened almost two years ago. It closes one of the most significant disciplinary cases in the competition’s history.

Former head coach Pep Guardiola has already broken his silence. Reaction to the Pep Guardiola Man City charges verdict is spreading fast across the division, with Chelsea among the clubs watching closely.

Guardiola joined Manchester City in 2016 and turned them into the dominant force in English football. He left his post earlier this summer, with the club having already worked through prepares to replace Pep Guardiola at City.

According to recent reports, his exit means he holds no formal role at the Etihad. Yet he moved quickly to make his loyalty clear once the ruling landed. According to the football.london report that first carried his statement, Guardiola posted directly to his supporters.

Recent findings suggest that “I want every single @ManCity fan to know I am here; more than ever. I am, always have been, always will, be behind my club, my owner, my chairman [Khaldoon Al Mubarak], Ferran [Soriano], the players, staff, Enzo [Maresca] and you, our unique supporters. Let me be clear: we will get through this together. I love you all.”.

Manchester City chief executive Ferran Soriano has confirmed that the club intends to appeal the verdict. The statement from Guardiola comes to City’s defence despite him leaving the role this summer. He is still viewed as the man who built this era of success, even though he no longer sits in the dugout at the Etihad.

It has been revealed that the Pep Guardiola Man City charges case matters just as much to Chelsea as it does to Manchester City themselves. Chelsea, along with Arsenal and Tottenham, are among the Premier League clubs directly affected by the outcome. Every point City may have gained through breaches has a knock on effect further down the table.

Most top flight clubs including Chelsea are now pushing for City to face relegation as a consequence of the verdict. Some are reported to be weighing up legal action of their own. Guardiola has spoken about Chelsea before, including Guardiola’s previous comments on Chelsea. This latest statement fits a pattern of public backing for Manchester City, despite the criticism now directed at the club.

For Chelsea supporters, the case is not just about points on a table. It touches on the integrity of seasons they competed in directly. That is why so many rivals are refusing to let the matter rest with a guilty verdict alone.

Industry observers note that manchester City’s appeal process is now the next stage in a case that has already run for nearly two years. Ferran Soriano has made clear the club will contest the findings. No date has been confirmed for when that appeal will be heard.

Until then, no points deductions, penalties or changes to past titles have been confirmed by the Premier League. Chelsea and their rivals will be watching the appeal timeline just as closely as they watched the original hearing. The final outcome could still be a long way off.
