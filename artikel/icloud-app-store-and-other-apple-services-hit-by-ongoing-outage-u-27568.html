iCloud, App Store, and other Apple services hit by ongoing outage [U]

🌐 Source: 9to5mac • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (420 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • Downdetector is showing reports across multiple Apple services, even as Apple’s System Status page continues to list them as available.
  • Affected services include the App Store, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple Support, iPhone Activation, and more.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that apple services are having a rough Tuesday. iCloud, the App Store, and other services are experiencing an apparent outage, with users reporting connection failures and trouble signing in.

Downdetector is showing reports across multiple Apple services, even as Apple’s System Status page continues to list them as available.

Affected services include the App Store, iCloud, Apple Pay, Apple Support, iPhone Activation, and more.

Apple confirms a number of services experiencing issues on its general and developer status pages.

Apple retail stores and corporate offices are also reportedly experiencing outages.

Users are reporting unexpected iCloud sign-outs on iPhone and Mac, followed by verification failures or unknown errors when trying to sign back in.

Others say the App Store won’t load or download apps, while Apple Music and device activation are also running into problems. Developers have separately reported trouble accessing App Store Connect.

Recent findings suggest that the number of "coincidental" system failure happening these days make it difficult to believe it's not cyberattacks, AI failures, or both. Even more suspicious is that they all seem to be reciting the same script for the cause: "an internal update".

The disruption follows last week’s widespread Microsoft Outlook outage.

Yesterday, banking services also experienced problems after a technical glitch at JPMorgan Chase disrupted online access and delayed some transactions involving other banks.

In a recent development, are Apple services working for you? Let us know what you’re seeing in the comments.

Upgrade: Apple now states an internal update caused the outage, ruling out a cyber attack, and many services are coming back online. Consumer-facing services are back online. Developer-focused services are still experiencing challenges.

It has been revealed that apple: “Many of our systems are back up and running after an internal update caused some outages this morning. We’re working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible." Company notes this wasn’t a cyberattack.
