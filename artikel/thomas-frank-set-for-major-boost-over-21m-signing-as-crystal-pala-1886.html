Anan Khalaili is expected to be fit for Crystal Palace’s crucial Premier League clash away to Coventry this weekend.

The 22-year-old right-back was initially called up by Israel for this month’s Nations League fixtures but was not named in the matchday squad ahead of Saturday’s 0-0 draw with Kosovo and will not feature against Austria on Tuesday evening.

Khalaili, who joined Palace from Belgian side Union Saint-Gilloise for around £21million in the summer, has been struggling with inflammation in recent weeks.

Having recently returned from a hamstring injury, and Standard Sport understands Palace and the Israel Football Association have decided he should return to London to recover ahead of Saturday’s trip to Coventry.

Khalaili will rest over the next week to give himself the best possible chance of playing this weekend, but is expected to be available for selection.

As Standard Sport reported last week, Jorgen Strand Larsen could also be in contention for minutes despite withdrawing from Norway’s squad through injury.

Strand Larsen returned to London for further treatment over the weekend as he continues to manage an ankle injury sustained during Palace’s 2-2 draw with Besiktas last month.

The concern, however, is not believed to be serious, and the 26-year-old could lead the line against Coventry in the absence of the suspended Jean-Philippe Mateta.

Palace are bottom of the Premier League table and without a manager after parting ways with Pierre Sage last week, after the Frenchman took just five wins from 16 games in all competitions.

They are, though, closing in on the appointment of former Tottenham and Brentford boss Thomas Frank on a two-and-a-half-year deal, with the Dane set to be in the dugout against Coventry.
