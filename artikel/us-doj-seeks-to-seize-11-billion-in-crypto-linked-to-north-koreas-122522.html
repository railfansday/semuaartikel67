📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, the U.S Department of Justice (DOJ) has taken legal action to recover more than $1.1 billion in cryptos stolen from Binance on 29th October 2026. With this move, the government has now moved beyond simply freezing the stolen funds and is now seeking their legal forfeiture.

According to the DOJ, the stolen funds had already been placed under a restraining order, which was the reason why they were prevented from being transferred or accessed while authorities pursued recovery efforts.

Sources indicate that remarking on the same, Attorney General for National Security M. Scott Proctor remarked,.

The Department of Justice has long prioritized victims and their rights, and the commencement of this forfeiture action so soon after the restraint of these same assets reflects our commitment to ensuring that victims remain at the forefront of our work.

Binance suffered a cryptocurrency theft worth more than $6.3 billion, which the U.S authorities linked to North Korean cyber hackers.

Following the attack, the hackers allegedly used sophisticated laundering techniques to move the stolen cryptocurrency across different wallets and potentially convert it.

However, the FBI, U.S Secret Service, and the IRS has worked alongside private-sector companies to track the transactions and identify wallets holding the stolen funds.

It has been revealed that as its stands, the U.S. government has frozen and seized nearly $3 billion worth of cryptocurrencies from multiple sources since the Binance theft. The latest $1.1 billion forfeiture action is part of this broader recovery effort and does not represent an additional $1.1 billion on top of the nearly $3 billion already recovered or restrained.

If this forfeiture is successful, the action could help return the recovered assets to their rightful owners.
