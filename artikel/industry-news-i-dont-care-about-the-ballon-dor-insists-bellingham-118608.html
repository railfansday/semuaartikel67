Jude Bellingham insisted he does not "care about the Ballon d'Or" and would rather "win a lot of titles" with Real Madrid instead.

The England international stole the headlines in Madrid's 5-1 win over PSV in the Champions League on Tuesday after scoring a stunning goal.

Bellingham came on at half-time, with Los Blancos a goal down, and netted a sensational volley before also assisting Vinicius Junior late on at Estadio Bernabeu.

No side has won more Champions League games having been trailing at the break than Madrid, with this their 13th such victory (level with Barcelona).

Bellingham created three chances in the second half, won three of his four duels and recorded the joint-most final third entries (seven).

He finished 11th at this year's Ballon d'Or, but the 23-year-old is not focused on individual awards.

"I don't care about the Ballon d'Or, honestly," said Bellingham.

"I want to win a lot of titles with the team, the Champions League, the league.

"The Ballon d'Or is something for you, journalists, to vote and create a bit of tension. But for me it's more vital that the team win.".
