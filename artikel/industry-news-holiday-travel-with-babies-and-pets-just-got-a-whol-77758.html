It has been revealed that seasonal travel is exciting, but getting everyone from point A to point B can be a production—especially when babies and pets are involved. Between navigating crowded airports, packing the car with what feels like half your house, and keeping your four-legged family members calm amid all the chaos, there’s a ton to think about. And while you can’t control flight delays, holiday traffic, or the timing of a much-needed diaper change, having the right travel gear can make a world of difference.

In a recent development, enter Nuna and Tavo, two brands that bring thoughtful design, innovative safety features, and a beautiful aesthetic to (often hectic) family travel. Nuna’s premium baby gear helps take some of the stress out of everything from airport security to car-seat installations, while Tavo focuses the same level of attention on the safety and comfort of travel essentials designed specifically for pets. Think lightweight strollers that fold with one hand (because your other one is probably occupied), car seats that don’t require an online tutorial to install, and comfortable carriers that give your furry companions a safe spot of their own.

Whether you’re flying across the country to visit the grandparents, packing up for a holiday road trip, or exploring a new destination together, these six Nuna and Tavo essentials, available at Bloomingdale’s, can help make the journey a little more joyful for everyone involved.

Industry observers note that given crowded security lines, blowouts at the worst possible moment, and the sheer amount of stuff you have to tote, a lightweight stroller is a genuine lifesaver at the airport. At just 16.3 pounds, the Nuna VIAA cabn is designed to meet IATA cabin-size standards when folded with the armbar removed—though you’ll still want to double-check your airline’s requirements. One-handed steering makes navigating crowded terminals easier (especially when you’re juggling a much-needed iced coffee), while a roomy storage basket holds up to 22 pounds of diaper bags, coats, backup outfits, bottles, snacks, and approximately 47 things to keep your baby entertained. It even comes with a carrier bag for when it’s time to board.

In a recent development, this Nuna duo pairs the TRIV lx stroller, which folds with one hand and stands on its own, with the ultralight PIPA urbn infant car seat. And if you’ve ever broken a sweat or found yourself YouTubing car seat installation instructions for the 100th time, you’ll appreciate that this one clicks into place without a base, using its built-in pipaFIX rigid latch installation. Large rear wheels and all-wheel suspension pair with a spacious storage basket and handy tracking-device pocket to make the stroller ready for everyday outings.

The Nuna SWIV’s standout feature is button-activated 360-degree rotation on all four wheels, allowing it to move sideways and pivot through tight spaces without the struggle of squeezing through crowded airport lines and store aisles that clearly weren’t designed with strollers in mind. A one-handed recline with three positions, including a near-flat option, makes adjustments easy, and the stroller is travel-system-ready with any Nuna PIPA series infant car seat, allowing you to move baby from the car to the stroller.

The Tavo Crispin gives your pet a roomy, comfortable spot of their own, with mesh panels for ventilation and visibility so they can still keep eyes on their humans. It’s impact-tested and secures to your vehicle using lower anchors and a top tether, with a protective memory-foam guard for added peace of mind. And when the holiday travels are over, it collapses for easy storage without taking up precious space at home.

According to recent reports, this Tavo travel system might be your newly introduced favorite on-the-road companion. The Maeve car seat attaches to the Roscoe Trek stroller frame, so you can go from the car to a festive outing without having to transfer your pup to a different carrier. All-terrain rear wheels and suspension make bumpy sidewalks easier to navigate, while one-touch brakes and a compact stroller fold come in handy when it’s time for a coffee stop or to load everything back into the car.

There’s a very particular type of heart-rate spike that comes with flying with a pet. You have to stand in the airline’s special-assistance line with your (probably nervous) dog or cat, only to finally reach the counter and hold your breath as the agent (and sometimes a supervisor) decides whether their carrier fulfills the rules. The Tavo Dupree II has a chic carry-on design that’s also crash-tested, complete with built-in vehicle connectors and a protective memory-foam guard. Mesh panels keep air circulating, while a trolley sleeve lets you slip it over your suitcase handle instead of carrying everything separately. You’ll still want to double-check your airline’s requirements before flying.
