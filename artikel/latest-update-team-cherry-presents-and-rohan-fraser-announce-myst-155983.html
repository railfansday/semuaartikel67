Team Cherry Presents, a fresh publishing label under Hollow Knight developer Team Cherry, and developer Rohan Fraser have announced Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City, a mystery puzzle adventure game set atop a gigantic walking city. It will be available for PC via Steam. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

A grand mystery in a world of robots.

According to recent reports, famous investigator Abe Lightfoot and his eager assistant Momo arrive to solve a case with dramatic implications for a wondrous walking city and its inhabitants. Gather clues, interview witnesses and interrogate suspects as you follow leads through the city streets and into the belly of the walking city itself.
