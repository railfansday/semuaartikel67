When Gratitude Feels Like One More Thing You’re Failing At

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Every year as the holiday season approaches, gratitude advice arrives on cue. Count your blessings, states a friend. Write down three things you’re grateful for, urges a social media post. “Focus on the positive,” declares an advertisement, pitching the extra indulgence you oh-so deserve.

Sources indicate that there’s plenty of wisdom in appreciating what’s good in our lives. But what happens when life feels difficult, and gratitude is the last thing on your mind?

When life is hard, being told to feel grateful can sound like the opposite of good advice. Not only are you struggling, but apparently you’re not appreciating your life properly either.

As a mindfulness teacher, I sometimes hear people share their difficulties after our morning meditation. They’re navigating loneliness, relationship problems, health issues, uncertainty about the future, or the loss of someone they love. At times like these, being told to feel grateful can sound like the opposite of good advice. Not only are you struggling, but apparently you’re not appreciating your life properly either.
