Panache Digital Games will release the first major content update for the Early Access version of dark third-person narrative action adventure game 1666: Amsterdam in December, the developer declared. It will expand the game’s world, story, and supernatural abilities.

The December update marks the first major expansion of the Early Access experience, bringing newly introduced areas to explore, new supernatural abilities, and another Original for players to confront.

A New Region, a New Original, and More Witchcraft.

Arriving during the final moon of the year, the first major upgrade marks the beginning of a series of planned expansions for 1666: Amsterdam.

Industry observers note that players will be able to explore Ouderkerk, a latest region slightly larger than the game’s currently available area, effectively doubling the world players can explore. Alongside this new location comes another Esbat and a new Original, bringing the next chapter of Noa’s journey to Early Access.

The upgrade will also introduce additional Lux and Nux spells and combos, along with upcoming jewels and potions, giving players more ways to develop their abilities and approach encounters.

Panache Digital Games will share more details about these additions in the weeks leading up to the December release.

■ Early Access Price Remains Unchanged.

Panache Digital Games has also confirmed that 1666: Amsterdam will remain at its current Early Access price when the December patch launches, with no price increase for the newly introduced content.

This first major update is part of the team’s ongoing Early Access roadmap, with further expansions planned as development continues.

1666: Amsterdam is available now in Early Access for PC via Steam and Epic Games Store.
