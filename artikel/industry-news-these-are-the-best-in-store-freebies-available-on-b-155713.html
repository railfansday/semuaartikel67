Recent findings suggest that black Friday deals start showing up long before Black Friday actually arrives, and it's easy to shop most sales online without ever having to set foot in a store. But if you are willing to go early, stand in line, and navigate the crowds, you may be able to get some free stuff for your effort. Here are the freebies on offer this Black Friday.

Industry observers note that the first 100 shoppers in line at Target on Black Friday will receive a gift card worth $25, $50, or $100, and one shopper at every store will get a $1,000 gift card. Stores open at 6 a.m., and tickets redeemable for free gift cards will be handed out starting approximately an hour before. Shoppers must exchange their tickets for their gift card within the first hour after doors open.

Industry observers note that the first 200 shoppers at every Kohl's store on Black Friday will get a voucher for Kohl's Cash worth $5 to $100. Doors open at 5 a.m. If you show up on Saturday, Nov. 28 instead, you're eligible for a Sephora at Kohl's coupon (also worth $5 to $100).

If you're a MyLowe's Rewards member and you show up early on Black Friday, you could get one of several free buckets filled with products and coupons. The first 50 loyalty program members in line will receive a Black Friday Mystery Bucket, and four of those individuals will win one of the following grand prizes:.

A major appliance (under $2,000).

According to recent reports, the next 100 members get a Golden Mini Bucket, which contains $5 in MyLowe's Monday and a 20% off coupon, among other things. The loyalty program is free to join, and doors open at 6 a.m.

The first 250 shoppers at Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's locations on Black Friday will receive a promo card worth between $10 and $500. The cards are valid Dec. 4 through 26, so you can't use them to shop that day.

Sources indicate that JCPenney is running scheduled giveaways in stores throughout the day on Black Friday, so you could get free products even if you aren't there when doors open. If you line up at 5 a.m., you will get a peel-away card that is good for $10 off, $50 off, or $500 off your purchase, as well as a free Harry Potter mini snow globe. Additional giveaways will take place at 9 a.m., 1 p.m., and 5 p.m. for those in line.

In a recent development, michaels is giving away 500 Countdown to Craftsmas advent calendars in stores on Black Friday while supplies last. The calendars come with store coupons, and some include other freebies and cash prizes up to a $100 gift card. One winner will receive a $10,000 nationwide grand prize.

Academy Sports is distributing bonus cards worth $20, $50, or $100 to the first 100 customers in line on Black Friday between 5 a.m. and 6 a.m. CT. Prize amounts will be randomly awarded among entrants. Academy Sports has locations across more than 20 states.

Best Buy's Black Friday promo isn't strictly free, but members of the My Best Buy rewards program (including the free tier) can purchase a $100 gift card for $60. There is a limit of one per person while supplies last, and cards can be used immediately, so you can take advantage of sales.

The first 30 shoppers at Golf Galaxy locations on Black Friday are eligible for instant prizes, including products, gift cards, and in-store services. One person per location will win a swag bag worth more than $500. Doors open at 7 a.m.

In a recent development, if you're among the first 500 shoppers at Fleet Farm when doors open at 5 a.m. on Black Friday, you'll receive a plush eagle toy. The tech giant has also been running an "Orange Friday" event with in-store giveaways, though these have passed at most locations.

Sources indicate that in short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.

Sources indicate that follow our Black Friday coverage for the latest and best deals our team finds. Another good tip is to only buy what you need. It sounds simple, but making a list of the things you want before jumping into a sale with millions of products helps you focus and make smarter decisions.
