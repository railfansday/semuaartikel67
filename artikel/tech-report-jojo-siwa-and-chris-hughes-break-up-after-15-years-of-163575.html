JoJo Siwa is dancing to a solo number.

The "Karma" singer has split from former Love Island UK star Chris Hughes after a year and a half together, multiple outlets confirmed.

Recent findings suggest that E! News reached out to reps for the pair for comment but has not yet heard back.

Siwa, 23, and Hughes, 33, first met in 2025 on the set of Celebrity Big Brother UK. While the Dance Moms alum was dating podcaster Kath Ebbs at the time, she formed a close bond with Hughes during the reality competition series.

It has been revealed that "I've always told myself I'm a lesbian and I think being here, I’ve realized: ‘Oh, I’m not a lesbian, I’m queer,'" she told housemate Danny Beard at the time. "And I think that’s really cool.".

Siwa broke up from Ebbs shortly after the show's finale in May, with her and Hughes going public with their romance the following month.

“It’s not platonic anymore,” Siwa admitted in a June 2025 interview with The Guardian, “and it’s been a beautiful development, a beautiful connection, and I’m absolutely head over heels for him and he’s the same way.”.

The "Boomerang" artist also clarified her sexuality, emphasizing that she still considers herself to be a proud member of the LGBTQ+ community.

"I think the most beautiful term now is queer,” Siwa told E! News that same month. "There's loads of beautiful, different sexualities. Sexuality and queerness is a lovely rainbow that has all different places, and it also has places on this rainbow that we don't even know exist yet.”.

In a recent development, she continued, "What I realized with Christopher is I never have second guessed my feelings for him. Therefore, I've never second guessed my happiness, I've never second guessed what I'm feeling. And I think that's the most beautiful thing.”.

By July, Hughes declared Siwa to be "literally the love of my life.".

According to recent reports, "She's also my best friend," he noted to E! in a separate interview. "Like, we sit and do nothing together.".

At the time, the Chris & Kem musician shared he was ready to take the next steps with Hughes during their first Christmas together, when he gifted her a $2,600 Cartier ring. However, he noted that it wasn't necessarily a marriage proposal because "you always get people who get engaged on Christmas.".

As Hughes explained to The Sun, “I like the idea of it not being at a time where it could be expected.".
