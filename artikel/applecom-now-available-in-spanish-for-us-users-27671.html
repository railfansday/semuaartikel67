📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that apple is making a notable change to Apple․com. Starting today, owners in the United States can now browse the Apple website in Spanish.

In a recent development, the newly introduced Spanish version of the Apple website is available at apple.com/us-es. Users with their device language set to Spanish will see a prompt to switch. It’s also accessible by choosing Spanish from the country and language menu at the bottom.

According to recent reports, the Apple Online Store and Financial Services remain available in English only. When a user moves to the Apple Online Store, they’ll see a message reading (translated):.

Presently, the page you wish to visit is only available in English. If you prefer to shop in Spanish, please call 1-800-692-7753 and receive personalized assistance from a Specialist.

This is a great change for Spanish speakers in the United States.
