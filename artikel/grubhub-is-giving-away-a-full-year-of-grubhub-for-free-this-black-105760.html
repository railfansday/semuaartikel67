In a recent development, TL;DR: Through Dec. 7, first-time Grubhub customers (plus invited existing users) can get one year of Grubhub+ for free — with no promo code required.

Delivery fees can easily turn a $15 takeout order into a $28 regret, so getting a whole year of free delivery on the house is tough to beat. Through Dec. 7, this Grubhub+ Black Friday deal gives eligible diners a full 12 months of Grubhub+ for free — with no promo code required.

According to recent reports, normally, Grubhub+ runs $9.99 per month (which adds up to nearly $120 over a year) or $90 if you pay annually. That saves you up to $120 in membership fees alone.

Once your free year is active, your account unlocks the full lineup of Grubhub+ perks across both Grubhub and Seamless:.

$0 delivery fees on eligible standard orders from participating Grubhub+ restaurants and stores (including standard delivery on eligible Instacart-powered grocery orders), as long as your order meets the merchant's minimum subtotal before tax, tip, and fees.

Lower service fees and discounted priority delivery (at the reduced rate shown at checkout) on eligible restaurant orders that meet the subtotal minimum.

5% back in Grubhub+ Credit on eligible restaurant pickup orders, calculated on your food subtotal before tax, tip, and fees.

Exclusive member-only offers on qualifying restaurant orders (and your perks also carry over to Seamless).

Industry observers note that the offer is strictly for first-time Grubhub customers (limited to one promotional trial per account and household) or existing Grubhub customers who received a direct invite from Grubhub via email or inside the app. Corporate accounts and anyone who previously had a Grubhub+ membership terminated are not eligible.

Sources indicate that grubhub+ is an auto-renewing subscription. Once your free 12-month trial wraps up, your strategy will automatically convert to a paid membership at the then-current rate — at the moment $9.99 per month if you choose monthly billing at sign-up, or $90 per year if you pick annual billing, plus tax.

Even with $0 delivery fees, additional charges such as reduced service fees, driver benefits fees, small order fees, or distance and weather fees may still apply depending on the order.

If you are latest to Grubhub — or spotted an invite sitting in your inbox — signing up before Dec. 7 is an easy way to dodge delivery fees all year long. Just remember to cancel before month 13 hits.
