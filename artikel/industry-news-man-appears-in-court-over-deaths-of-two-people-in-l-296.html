A man has appeared in court charged with causing the deaths of two people in a car crash in Leeds.

Leigh Ross, 39, was charged in connection with an incident in which Cameron Bell, 22, and Jade Langton, 23, were killed in the early hours of Sunday.

Ross, of Harrogate, wept in the dock as he made his first appearance in court on Monday, and his voice broke as he was asked to confirm his date of birth and address.

He is charged with two counts of causing death by dangerous driving, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while uninsured, two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while disqualified and two counts of causing death having taken a vehicle without consent.

Recent findings suggest that he was remanded in custody until his next appearance at Leeds crown court. As he was leaving the dock, he looked towards the public gallery and said: “I’m sorry.”.

West Yorkshire police stated a chase began at 12.44am on Sunday after a blue BMW 123D failed to stop for officers in Gelderd Road.

The vehicle then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco that was turning into Gelderd Road from Lowfields Road.

Sources indicate that bell, from York, who was driving the Volkswagen, died at the scene. His passenger, Langton, also from York, was pronounced dead in hospital a short time later.

In a recent development, two boys aged 16 and 17 and a girl, 16, were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving. They have been released on bail pending further inquiries.

The West Yorkshire force has referred itself to the police watchdog, the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which has introduced an investigation into the incident.

Detectives are appealing to anyone who saw the collision, or has dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, to contact West Yorkshire police via 101 or online.
