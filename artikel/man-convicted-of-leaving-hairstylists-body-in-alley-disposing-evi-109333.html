A man has been found guilty of leaving a Colorado woman’s body in an alley for nearly two months and disposing of her belongings in an attempt to conceal her death.

Brandon David Mumma was convicted of tampering with a deceased human body and tampering with evidence on Thursday, November 19, in connection with his actions following Jax Gratton’s death, according to Colorado’s First Judicial District Attorney’s Office.

Recent findings suggest that mumma, 44, is believed to be the last person who was with Gratton before she went missing. She was later discovered dead in an alley in between two buildings, one of which housed Mumma’s office in Lakewood, on July 6, 2025, prosecutors remarked.

Prosecutors accused Mumma of tossing her body out of his office window after she died of a drug overdose on April 16, 2025, according to The Denver Post, which reports Gratton, 34, had worked as a hairstylist in Denver.

Sources indicate that “Jax’s loved ones have endured the anguish of searching for her, the devastation of losing her, and the pain of unanswered questions about her death,” First Judicial Circuit District Attorney Alexis King noted in a statement included in a Thursday news release. “Today’s verdicts hold Mr. Mumma accountable for his actions after her death and his efforts to conceal what happened.”.

Mumma and Gratton were in a “consensual intimate relationship” ahead of her death, prosecutors remarked in a March news release announcing his indictment in the case.

The evening of April 15, 2025, Gratton and Mumma both went to Mumma’s office space before a male friend later joined them inside the unit shortly after midnight, according to prosecutors.

Mumma and the friend are accused of stepping out of the office around 1:43 a.m. on April 16, 2025, leaving Gratton by herself, sleeping, after she had consumed drugs.

No one visited the office space until Mumma returned a few hours later, according to security camera footage, prosecutors said. After about an hour, Mumma left the office to move his car, then returned to the building with trash bags before throwing the bags into a dumpster.

On June 6, 2025, Gratton’s body was discovered in a “narrow, four-foot-wide space that is inaccessible to the public,” next to Mumma’s office building, the district attorney’s office said in March.

She was still wearing the clothes she wore the night she saw Gratton on April 15, 2025, according to prosecutors.

Investigators never located Gratton’s cell phone, which was last active the morning of April 16, 2025, near Mumma’s office, or her other personal belongings, prosecutors noted.

Mumma claimed to investigators that he last saw Gratton when he left her in his office unconscious that morning, after she consumed what prosecutors suspect was a sedative and had vomited, the Denver Post reported.

He reportedly said when he returned to the office, she was no longer there.

At trial, Mumma’s attorney acknowledged to jurors that his client made a “mistake” when he left her alone, per CourtTV.

The attorney, however, stated “that doesn’t make him responsible” for her death.

While speaking with 9NEWS, Jax’s mother Cherilynne Gratton expressed she’s glad Mumma has been convicted, but remarked, “There will never be justice.”.
