Exploratory talks have been opened between newly introduced Liverpool sporting director Julian Ward and Alexis Mac Allister over the Argentine signing a contract extension at Anfield, GIVEMESPORT understands.

Mac Allister's future has been up in the air for some time now, with his current contract due to expire in 2028, meaning he has little more than a year-and-a-half remaining on his deal.

The 27-year-old has been linked with a move away from the Reds, with Manchester City one of a number of clubs credited with an interest after some reports surfaced that Liverpool would not be offering him a new deal.

However, Ward, who replaced Richard Hughes as Liverpool's sporting director, has already opened exploratory talks with Mac Allister and his father over extending his stay in the north west.

Mac Allister has started the season extremely well and has proved to be an important player for new boss Andoni Iraola. However, Liverpool are in no rush to make a definitive decision and were not inclined to make a formal offer over the summer.

The door was never totally shut to a Mac Allister renewal as Liverpool never completely closed off dialogue, but now conversations are more framed around the specifics of a renewal.

Recent findings suggest that speaking about his start to the season under the former Bournemouth manager, Mac Allister mentioned: "Last year was a really difficult year for all of us. It was clear that I was not at the level. I am the first one to say, I have no problem.

Sources indicate that "Something wasn't right in the group. This manager changed it, and that's really important. The way we play is quite different. I don't like to compare.

"I feel good, that's what I want and that's what I enjoy.".

The Argentine has been at Liverpool since 2023, arriving in a £35million deal from Brighton & Hove Albion. He has five goals and one assist to his name under Iraola so far this season, in 16 appearances.

Liverpool take on old rivals Manchester United at Anfield on Sunday afternoon, in what will be upcoming United boss Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge as he looks to revolutionise Man Utd.
