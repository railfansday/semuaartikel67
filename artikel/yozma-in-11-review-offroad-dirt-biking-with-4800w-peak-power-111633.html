It has been revealed that as a teen, I loved riding — anything, my skateboard, my bike, whatever had wheels and I could get moving on, I was there for it. Then I spent a few years racing mountain bikes in the NORBA circuit when I was in my mid-20’s. I was never very good — always pretty mediocre, in fact, but I’ll be damned if it wasn’t some of the funnest experiences I’ve ever had. The feel of a trail, the woods, cruising through nature … it’s intoxicating. It always came with a lot of work — mountain biking pre e-bikes was a frikkin workout. Pedaling a bike up a combined 3,000 feet worth of climbing during a race would knock me out for days. If only I was in my teen years or 20s now — LOL.

Recent findings suggest that with bikes like the Yozma IN 11, you can cruise mountain trails at speeds up to 45 mph, and do it completely powered by solar energy, to boot. Compared with noisy, smelly gas bikes, the Yozma can cruise on sunshine and offers the quintessential instant torque of the all-electric motor, making racing competitions with gas bikes an absolute joke — the Yozma will crush them.

It also has a number of other features, including wheelie assist, reverse gear and high visibility brake lights.

So does this all-electric dirt bike capable of going 44 mph and helping me do assisted wheelies to impress the ladies. I mean … wtf.
