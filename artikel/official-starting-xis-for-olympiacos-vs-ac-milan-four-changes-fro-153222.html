AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim has named his starting line-up for tonight’s crucial Europa League game away against Olympiacos.

Milan have lost three of their opening four games in the competition and they are running out of runway to put things right. With 12 points up for grabs, the feeling is that the Rossoneri need at least a couple of wins to even get a play-off spot, given they sit outside the top 24 at present.

In a recent development, they face an Olympiacos side that have 10 points on the board already thanks to three wins and a draw, while they have conceded only once. Not helping matters for Amorim is the fact that there are nine absentees due to injury.

In a recent development, on paper, Milan will line up with a four-man defence of Mario Gila, Koni De Winter, Sankhoun Diawara and Pervis Estupinan in front of the goalkeeper and captain Mike Maignan. Davide Bartesaghi was the latest injury, making the change at left-back a forced one.

Industry observers note that luka Modric’s muscle strain means that Ardon Jashari will get another chance in the double pivot, alongside Adrien Rabiot who will play a bit deeper tonight. Omari Hutchinson, Alphadjo Cisse and Diego Moreira will then the centre-forward Goncalo Ramos in a 4-2-3-1 formation.

Matteo Pittarella, Valeri Vladimirov, Christian Comotto and Lorenzo Ossola all feature on a youthful bench, as well as Christian Pulisic and usual starting midfielder Yunus Musah.

Official Milan XI (4-2-3-1): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Estupiñán; Jashari, Rabiot; Hutchinson, Cisse, Moreira; Ramos.

Bench: Pittarella, Torriani, Tomori, Comotto, V. Vladimirov, Ossola, Terracciano, Musah, Pulisic.

Official Olympiacos XI (4-2-3-1):Ortega; Saliakas, Retsos, Pirola, Bruno; Freuler Gustavo Sà; Gelson Martins, Jota Silva, Fortounis; Gonzalez.

Bench: Stournaras, Georgakopoulos, Saliakas, David Carmo, Fortounis, Yaremchuk, Bakoulas, Tiknizyan, Filis, Toufakis.
