Recent findings suggest that sauron is blazing a path of destruction in The Rings of Power season 3, which sees him continuing to wage war against the Elves—and the stakes are even higher as he gets closer and closer to forging the One Ring to rule them all. But does that mean the show will return for a fourth season?

Here’s everything we know so far about The Rings of Power season 4.

According to recent reports, yes! On November 24, Amazon Prime Video declared that The Rings of Power will return for season 4. According to The Wrap, “pre-production is underway on the fourth installment, with filming slated to begin this spring Shepperton Studios in the U.K.”.

There are no details about what will happen in the next season of The Rings of Power, but the finale hints that the mounting war between Sauron and the Elves could continue into Season 4.

According to recent reports, in the final moments of the finale, Sauron enters a battle with Pharazôn without his ring on. Gil-Galad remarks that he’s in the presence of the “doom of men” the moment that Sauron surrenders with his head bowed before Pharazôn. The King and his soldiers laugh, but Sauron smiles to himself, hinting that he might be one step ahead.

It has been revealed that until more details emerge, the season 3 logline reads, “All the peoples of Middle-Earth are engaged in the heroic struggle against Sauron and his army of Orcs. His terrifying Ring-servants, the Nazgnagôl, roam Middle-Earth clad in black armor as tensions rise among the Elves trying desperately to hold the front lines; familial ties are strained among the Dwarves in Khazad-dûm; political factions push the island kingdom of Númenor to the brink of civil war; and in the eastern lands of Rhûn, Gandalf enlists his full powers of magic and persuasion as he fights to turn the tide of impending darkness.”.

No returning cast members have been confirmed for The Rings of Power season 4 yet, but fans will be expecting Morfydd Clark, Charlie Vickers, Robert Aramayo, Owain Arthur, Sophia Nomvete, Lloyd Owen, Benjamin Walker, Markella Kavenagh, Maxim Baldry, Daniel Weyman, Andrew Richardson, Zubin Varla, Adam Young, Simon Pegg, and Jamie Campbell Bower to return to their roles.
