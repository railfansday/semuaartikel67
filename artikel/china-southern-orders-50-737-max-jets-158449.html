📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A subsidiary of China Southern Airlines has agreed to buy 50 Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft, Reuters reported Tuesday.

The airplanes, which have a total list price of $7.4 billion, will be used by Xiamen Airlines, which is based in Fujian. Deliveries are expected from 2031 to 2034.

Recent findings suggest that the deal is Boeing’s largest with a Chinese operator in nearly a decade. The country largely shifted toward Airbus aircraft as relations with the U.S. frayed.

President Donald Trump hoped to reverse that trend during his state visit to China in May. Trump discussed a potential purchase directly with Chinese President Xi Jinping, and even brought along Boeing CEO Kelly Ortberg for the summit. Ultimately, Beijing agreed to buy 200 aircraft from Boeing, fewer than analysts expected, but still notable considering Boeing’s drought in the country.

It has been revealed that trump said earlier this week that he thinks China will buy 250 more aircraft from Boeing. He did not provide details or say where that information came from.

China Southern said the latest aircraft will help Xiamen expand after it relocates to the new Xiamen Xiang’an International Airport. The facility will replace Xiamen Gaoqi International Airport as Xiamen’s main gateway.
