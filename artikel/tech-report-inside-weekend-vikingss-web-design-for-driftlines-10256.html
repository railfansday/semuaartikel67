Driftlines by Weekend Vikings is an interactive WebGPU web design experience where nine jazz tracks are drawn in glowing light strands.

Industry observers note that built for Budapest jazz duo GÃ¡bor Szunyogh (keys) and Viktor SzathmÃ¡ry (drums), the Driftlines site turns a nine-track album into a real-time WebGPU canvas. There is no static tracklist table on entry. Everything begins over an obsidian background with a single pulsing speaker outline made of light and a prompt to put on headphones.

It has been revealed that once clicked, the intro animation pulls horizontal gold and amber filaments across the screen. Those threads gather into the angular Weekend Vikings monogram rune, inviting visitors to trace the geometry and pluck individual strings before the strands unspool into the word PRESENT and the scanline-striped DRIFTLINES title. Moving the cursor or dragging across the viewport bends the driftlines like taut wires. They part around the pointer and snap back into formation with spring tension.

Sources indicate that after the title sequence settles, the web design shifts into an interactive nine-song menu where all nine track titles, from It Clicked and Skipper to Suspended Summer and Pin Tweaks, are woven directly from the glowing lines. Hovering or stepping through the list with arrow keys sends current through the selected title while the remaining tracks stay etched as faint wireframes. Selecting a song transitions through a custom sigil-drawing canvas into full-screen visualizers where audio frequencies drive the light trails.

To keep the canvas uncluttered, secondary navigation lives inside a vertical About tab pinned to the right edge. Pulling that tab slides out a frosted-glass drawer with a portrait of Szunyogh and SzathmÃ¡ry, the Driftlines cover art, direct links to Spotify and Apple Music, and a warm gold button leading back to the main band page, where a nine-chapter preview card and rotating album notes complete the web design system.
