Industry observers note that A fresh murder mystery sent the characters from Hulu‘s Only Murders in the Building to London for Season 6, and it’s where the show hosted its premiere this evening, with the cast, producers, and creatives teasing what’s ahead. The series will premiere on December 8 via Hulu and on Disney+ internationally.

On the red carpet at London’s Leicester Square, John Hoffman, who co-created the series with star Steve Martin, told Deadline that although they had fun across the pond, the show will return to Upcoming York, should it get renewed for a seventh season.

According to recent reports, deadline previously reported that the show’s writers room moved to Fresh York after being located in Los Angeles for the past six seasons. “I love Newly introduced York like nobody else, and the show is so steeped in New York. So, going back to New York is going to be a thrill for Season 7,” Hoffman mentioned. “I do hope that might happen. And then, beyond that, we’ve learned a lesson from London, which is that the show can travel, and it really excites everybody to imagine where it might be able to go next.” Watch on Deadline.

‘Only Murders In The Building’ showrunner John Hoffman confirms return to Newly introduced York for Season 7: “I love New York like nobody else and the show is so steeped in New York. So, going back to New York is going to be a thrill for Season 7.” pic.twitter.com/c4Ir9pBslH.

Recent findings suggest that speaking of Martin, he shared his feelings about returning to the Big Apple alongside Martin Short. The usual trio was one down, with Selena Gomez unable to attend the celebration due to a scheduling conflict.

“We’re always open to it [filming in latest cities] but right now, our goal is to head back to New York City,” stated Martin.

Steve Martin and Martin Short on returning to Latest York for ‘Only Murders In The Building’ Season 7: “We’re always open to it [filming in new cities] but right now, our goal is to head back to New York City.” pic.twitter.com/U4tPMMpXVJ.

The Season 6 cast also on the red carpet included Nicola Coughlan, Simone Ashley, David Tennant, Martin Freeman, Jodie Whittaker, and Sean Teale.

In Season 6 of Only Murders in the Building, after successfully bringing the guilty to justice once again, Charles (Martin), Oliver (Short), and Mabel (Gomez) embark on their hardest case yet when the next victim — Cinda Canning (Tina Fey) — arrives at their doorstep, summoning them to London to catch her killer. Our beloved trio of true crime-solving podcasters must now navigate their way through the home of the cozy murder mystery, replete with a whole new collection of suspicious and particularly English-eccentric characters. But can they follow in the footsteps of the woman whose podcast started it all for them … without having their story end the same as hers?

In addition to those previously mentioned, the fresh season will also feature Olivia Colman, Meryl Streep, Jim Broadbent, Peter Capaldi, Richard Ayoade, Jennifer Saunders, Rhea Norwood, Matthew Beard, Sharon Horgan, Richard E. Grant, Geri Halliwell-Horner, Lesley Nichols and Jane Horrocks.
