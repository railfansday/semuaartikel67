Jordan Pickford should still get the nod as England's number one goalkeeper over James Trafford, so says Ashley Young.

Thomas Tuchel has used this Nations League campaign to split time evenly between Pickford and Trafford.

Pickford has been England's undisputed number one since 2017, with the 32-year-old making 94 appearances and keeping 49 clean sheets.

Peter Shilton (125) is the only goalkeeper to have won more caps for England than Pickford, though he now has fierce competition from Leeds United shot-stopper Trafford, who made a string of fine saves to help the Three Lions beat Spain on Sunday.

Pickford has now not started four of England's last seven competitive internationals, having only not been in the XI for two of their previous 41 beforehand.

However, in their last nine competitive matches without Jordan Pickford, England have only kept two clean sheets and conceded an average of two goals per game (18 in total).

And even though Trafford's performance saw plenty of calls for the 24-year-old to be installed as England's first-choice goalkeeper, Young has backed his former Everton club-mate Pickford.

"You've always got to give goalkeepers the chance to show what they're about," Young said on The Overlap Daily.

"Playing as a defender, you want to know who's behind you as a goalkeeper. Right, that's it, this is who's going to be our number one. That's who’s going to be in goal. And I think, rightly so, Jordan Pickford has earned 94 caps for a reason.

According to recent reports, "He has been at three World Cups, two Euros; he’s got 49 clean sheets. It's understandable, but for me, Pickford is England's number one.

"Of course, Trafford has come in and done well, made some great saves at the end of the Spain game, but I think Pickford is a goalkeeper who demonstrates himself more with his feet when playing for England, because Thomas Tuchel wants that, more passing out from the back and whatnot.

Sources indicate that "I'm more confident in Pickford than Trafford with his feet. But when you've got different managers, it's different. At Everton, they play out from the back at times but not as much as England do. For me, Pickford's England's number one.".

Trafford has impressed for Leeds this season, although he did make an error leading directly to a goal when his mistake was pounced on by Evanilson in Leeds' recent defeat to Bournemouth.

Nevertheless, Trafford has a goals prevented figure of 4.4, which is the joint-best in the Premier League, along with Brighton's Bart Verbruggen.

Trafford's 82.2% save ratio also tops the charts in the division.

Pickford, meanwhile, has registered a 73.7% save ratio, and 1.1 goals prevented.
