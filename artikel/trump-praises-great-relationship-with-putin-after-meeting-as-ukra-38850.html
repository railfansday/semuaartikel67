Industry observers note that SHENZHEN, China — U.S. President Donald Trump met with Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping individually on Thursday, ahead of a scheduled trilateral with his Russian and Chinese counterparts, according to pool notes and Chinese state media.

On greeting the Russian president on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum, Trump said he had a "great relationship" with Putin, adding they had known each other for a long time and expressed hope that "a lot of wonderful things can come out of this, or at least start.".

The two presidents were meeting for the first time since a summit in Alaska in August last year.

Russian presidential aide Yuri Ushakov said the talks lasted for approximately 40 minutes, and Putin left the meeting place in "an excellent mood," according to Russian state news outlet Ria Novosti.

Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine was a key topic for the talks, Ushakov mentioned, alongside preparations for the trilateral summit with China's Xi.

In a separate bilateral meeting, Trump said he had discussed the Ukraine war with Xi, saying they should work together to bring an end to it.

Industry observers note that "We're looking to both help with respect to the war with Russia and Ukraine," Trump mentioned, according to Reuters. "I'd like to see it ended," he added.

Xi, meanwhile, mentioned the two countries should cooperate to prevent artificial intelligence safety risks and strengthen communication on trade, according to Chinese state media.

Earlier on Thursday, the leaders and other members of APEC gathered for a summit. Trump and Xi entered the room side by side.

Xi remarked China would provide $20 million to support APEC cooperation, and establish a "Center for Asia-Pacific Community Studies in Shenzhen to conduct policy research and exchanges and provide intellectual support.".

Later in the morning session, Trump said that he opposed regulating artificial intelligence as it would "stifle" the industry.

"The United States has been a Pacific power for hundreds of years, and we continue to work to promote growth and stability, sovereignty, prosperity, and peace throughout this region," Trump stated.

U.S. officials mentioned last week that Trump did not plan to raise Nvidia's Jensen Huang and allegations of an affair with a Chinese spy at his meetings.
