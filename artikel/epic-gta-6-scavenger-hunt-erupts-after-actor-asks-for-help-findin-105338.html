Just imagine you're an actor who booked an unspecified gig years ago and then found out nearly half a decade later that you were performing a role in the biggest video game of all time, Grand Theft Auto VI?

Recent findings suggest that pretty cool, huh? That's exactly what happened to 63-year-old Guy A. Fortt.

Sources indicate that and if you were Fortt, you'd probably want to proudly show off your scenes to your whole family and all of your friends. Right? There's one massive problem for Fortt, though: He has no idea who he played in GTA 6.

Sources indicate that and there's another matter for Fortt, who, according to IGN, previously had roles in both Jessica Jones and The Sopranos. He doesn't have an Xbox or PlayStation 5, so he can't even play the game and try to find his character(s).

However, Fortt does have a Facebook and Instagram account. And he posted a video on both detailing his GTA 6 dilemma. Now, according to Fortt in a follow-up video, millions of gamers are essentially on an in-game scavenger hunt to find the character or characters that Fortt played.

It has been revealed that "I was a motion capture actor on GTA 6, but I don't know who the hell I am,” Fortt explained in his viral Instagram video. "I auditioned about four or five years ago; they put the balls all over my body and face. And then I forgot all about it." Fortt says he received an email recently informing him that he was in GTA 6.

"This is where I need your help," he remarked.

As of publication, it does not seem like anyone has found Fortt's GTA 6 character yet, so the hunt is still on.

Want to help find Fortt in GTA 6? While the actor doesn't know much about the role he played, he has provided some potentially helpful information. According to Fortt, he recalls that his performance involved standing behind a counter. He also interacted with Jason and Lucia, the game's protagonists.

As IGN explains, while this information could help narrow down the potential characters to say, a store clerk or cop sitting at a desk, there's another variable that can make the hunt for Fortt more difficult. Rockstar Games hired dozens of actors to play various NPC roles in GTA 6 in order to make up the diverse population of Leonida. So, tracking down a more minor, essentially background, character in the game might prove to be difficult.

Here's hoping maybe Rockstar Games can help Guy A. Fortt out.
