World of Warcraft: Forever players will now be able to get even more benefit from the game's strongest buffs, as developer Blizzard's latest hotfix has made World Buffs persist across loading screens as intended. World Buffs are powerful stat-boosting effects granted to players in a particular area for certain events. The most common World Buff players see is Warchief's Blessing on the Horde and the latest Might of Stormwind for the Alliance, which are granted to all players in Orgrimmar, Stormwind, and their surrounding low-level zones when players on their respective faction turn in a specific high-level quest. Now that players are reaching endgame and turning in the required quest, these buffs are popping off constantly. They grant a whopping 300 hit points, 15% melee haste, and 10 mana regen per five seconds for one hour. At lower levels, those are some serious stats, particularly the 300 HP. Unfortunately, players haven't been able to take full advantage of the buffs, as a bug was causing them to be lost when hitting a loading screen. That meant any time a group stepped into a dungeon or took a boat or zeppelin to a new location, their World Buffs would be removed. That has finally been fixed in the latest upgrade, so players can now enter dungeons with World Buffs to help maximize their damage and health. This should be particularly useful for doing dungeons at a slightly lower level than is intended, as the extra HP and melee haste is definitely noticable. Blizzard clarified in the patch notes that World Buffs will still be removed when entering raids, so players won't be able to stack as many World Buffs as possible before raid night, as was the case in previous versions of classic WoW. Other changes found in the patch notes include a fix for an Uldaman boss that players were exploiting to make fast gold. Another is a fix for "a few spells and abilities" that were causing more weapon-proc effects than intended, likely nerfing the DPS of whatever classes were taking advantage of the bug. You can read the full patch notes below. Blizzard recently outlined in the latest episode of the WoW: Forever podcast how it wants to get rid of the idea of "best-in-slot gear," as well as how the game's has overhauled old-school WoW's player-vs.-player systems. In other Forever news, the game's launch has seen more than 15 million characters created so far, with the highest weekly player numbers for WoW in 15 years.
