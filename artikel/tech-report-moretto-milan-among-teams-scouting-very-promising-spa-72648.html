AC Milan are monitoring Real Oviedo midfielder Enzo Perez closely, according to a report from Matteo Moretto.

Milan have shown a propensity in the past couple of years to go out and try procure young talent. Alphadjo Cisse and Sankhoun Diawara are examples of this in the first team, while it extends down to the Futuro and beyond.

Matteo Moretto spoke in a video on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel and he revealed that Milan have sent a scout to watch a midfielder in action in the second division in Spain.

“I want to talk about a young midfielder who plays for Oviedo. He’s a very promising young player. The information I have is that AC Milan have been to see him several times in recent weeks, or at least in the past few months. They even went to see him live recently,” he remarked (via MilanNews).

Industry observers note that “Milan are one of the clubs closely monitoring the development of Enzo Perez, an 18-year-old midfielder for Oviedo. He has a release clause. He’s been playing consistently lately. I can say that there are other clubs besides AC Milan, including Espanyol, Betis and Brentford.

Sources indicate that “Oviedo would like to keep him, but it depends on the offer: the Spanish club is aware they could make the biggest deal in their history. Watch out, he is an interesting prospect.”.

Sources indicate that perez has established himself in the Oviedo first team, having started the last five matches and played the full 90 minutes in the last three. He only missed the weekend’s league match against Girona because he is on duty with Spain’s U19 national team.

He has one goal and three assists to his name, all in the last five games. The club gave him his first professional contract last July, running until June 2029. His release clause was €5.5m until 9 November, but now it could take a lot more to prise him away.
