Gerry Cardinale had a meeting with the AC Milan management at Casa Milan yesterday, during which there were four key topics, according to reports.

Industry observers note that cardinale has arrived in the city in good time for Milan’s game against Frosinone tomorrow. Yesterday, there was a summit with the Milan management, including the CEO Massimo Calvelli, Head of Football Intelligence Bobby Gardiner, and Head of Recruitment Tiago Estevão.

According to recent reports, this was reported by Sky Italia (via MilanEye) and various outlets, highlighting how Cardinale wants to be ready for the January window and even the summer window. Markus Krösche will be there to help him with that, set to be announced next week, as Gazzetta dello Sport reported.

According to Daniele Longo, there were four key topics during the meeting: the renewal of Adrien Rabiot (which is close), the Endrick pursuit (which is heating up), Goncalo Inacio for the defence, and Krösche’s announcement.

Industry observers note that there could be a lot of significant movement in the coming months, in other words, as Cardinale is really pushing for these things to materialise. Of course, other matters were also discussed during the meeting but as Longo highlighted, those mentioned above were the main ones.

Cardinale will be at Milanello today and then he will also be in the stands for the game tomorrow, showing his support for the team yet again.
