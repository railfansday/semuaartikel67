Leverkusen hold further talks to extend Maza deal as club set sale timeframe

Bayer Leverkusen have made further progress in their efforts to extend Ibrahim Maza's contract, with talks now in the final stages, according to Sky Germany.

The Algeria international is set to extend his current deal by a further year, taking it from 2030 until 2031, with no release clause to be included in the agreement.

The move would put Die Werkself in an even stronger negotiating position amid growing interest in the 20-year-old, with Sky Germany adding that the Bundesliga club expect to sell him by the summer of 2028 at the latest.

Leverkusen reportedly view Maza as their next potential €100 million-plus sale.
