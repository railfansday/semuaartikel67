Ambassador Cruise Line has launched its 2026 Black Friday campaign, offering guests complimentary drinks on selected sailings.

The offers are available on a range of adult-only cruises departing from February 2027 up to June 2028 for all newly introduced bookings made from 09:00 AM on November 24 through 11:59 on December 2, 2026.

According to recent reports, the Classic Drinks Package covers house wines, selected beers and ciders, house-brand spirits, eligible house-brand cocktails and a range of soft drinks. Guests can also benefit from a 50 percent discount on premium drinks from the bar.

In addition to the Free Classic Drinks offer, the campaign also includes an upgrade to the all-inclusive Ambassador Fare from £19 per person per night.

Guests traveling alone can enjoy great deals across all four solo cabin categories as part of the promotion, while groups of 15 or more can benefit from a 5 percent discount on selected sailings, with additional complimentary places available for larger bookings.

For groups of 25 guests, Ambassador will offer one additional free place (26th place), and two free places for groups of 50 guests (51st and 52nd places).

The company mentioned in a press release that its campaign offers are available across a range of no-fly sailings onboard the Ambience and Ambition.

The sailings featured in the promotion depart between February 2027 and June 2028 to destinations including Spain, the Nordics, France, Morocco, Portugal and the Canary Islands.

The firm stated that all first-time bookings on the Ambition and Ambience will benefit from the Ambassador Guarantee, which promises to reimburse any new-to-cruise guests who fail to enjoy the entertainment, cuisine and service.

The guarantee gives travelers additional confidence when booking an Ambassador cruise for the first time and applies to upcoming-to-brand guests booking an itinerary of four nights or more.
