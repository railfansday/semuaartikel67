Liverpool have had quite a lot of luck when it comes to signing South America players.

Right now, the Reds have Alisson, Ronald Araujo and Alexis Mac Allister among their ranks and, so far this season, all three of them have played fairly regular roles for Liverpool.

Obviously, the same can't be remarked for all South American's with Darwin Nunez's poor performances probably the most recent flop in that regard.

However, more often than not, the Reds' eye for talent in South America has been top notch and, with Luis Diaz and Nunez being sold last summer, Liverpool lost a decent chunk of them.

But, fortunately for the Reds, it now looks like they're trying to rectify that with the latest patch to emerge from Anfield being rather exciting.

Liverpool have cast their eyes on Athletico Paranaense's Arthur Dias.

One of the main things Liverpool have tried to do with their squad over the summer is get them prepared for the future.

The Anfield outfit have always had one of the best academies in the world but, signing young talent from other clubs has also been a key part to their recruitment strategy.

Trey Nyoni and Rio Ngumoha are the best examples of this right now but, after the upcoming transfer windows, that could be about to change.

That is, if an update from FussballEuropa is to be believed.

It has been revealed that "Liverpool FC has its eyes everywhere – including on the Brazilian market. The Reds' scouts are keeping a close eye on Arthur Dias. The race for the talented center-back is already on," they wrote.

Athletico Paranaense could have made a significant sum from Arthur Dias last summer. Tottenham Hotspur revealed concrete interest and reportedly submitted an offer of just over €20 million. However, this was too low for Paranaense, and consequently, no deal was reached.

In a recent development, dias is one of the most exciting defensive talents in Brazilian football and, at just 19 years old, has developed into an indispensable defensive leader at Paranaense. And not only that: Dias has even earned two caps for the Brazilian national team.

"This automatically makes the rising star a target for other clubs. According to information from Fussballeuropa.com, Premier League side Liverpool are keeping a close eye on Dias. The Reds are regularly monitoring the young defender and keeping a close eye on his development.".

Arthur Dias could plug the gap in Liverpool's defence.

Obviously, the future of Liverpool's defence is up in the air at the moment.

Nobody knows what the Reds' intends for their players who's contracts expire are and, that is a tad concerning.

Three of their defenders are set to leave L4 in June 2027 and, if a deal isn't reached before then, they'll be able to exit for nothing.

This has now happened two years in a row with Trent Alexander-Arnold and Ibrahima Konate both leaving for free.

Liverpool's lack of knowing is probably why so many defenders are still being linked.

Dias is only 19 years old and, he may have only featured in 55 professional games across his career thus far but, this season alone, he's played 31 times in the Brazilian Serie A and has even scored a goal.

Obviously, he's still very young so he has a lot of developing to do but, from the fact that he's played all of those games in the Brazilian Serie A already, it's clear that he's one to watch.

And, with Virgil van Dijk, Kostas Tsimikas, Joe Gomez and Ronald Araujo all potentially leaving, Dias could definitely be on Liverpool's shortlist.
