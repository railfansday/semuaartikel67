Industry observers note that miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are married—and she opted for a very timeless wedding dress. The singer posted photos from her ceremony, unveiling the long-sleeve, backless gown she wore. She accessorized with earrings and slingback heels.

The couple revealed they were engaged in December 2025. For the most part, they have kept their wedding plans under wraps.

In March, a source told People that Cyrus was “not planning some huge, traditional event. Maxx is extremely private, and that’s just not who he is. When they do get married, it will be something smaller and meaningful to them. Not a significant Hollywood production.”.

In a recent development, cyrus has preferred to keep their relationship largely out of the spotlight. When asked about their engagement, she told People on December 2, “The detail that I can share is that for us, our privacy and us kind of keeping it small has been something that I’ve been astounded that I’ve actually been able to have and being able to kind of have more choice. I think it’s also being older, being more protective of what I’m open to sharing.”.

Sources indicate that morando ultimately revealed how he proposed to Cyrus. In July, he told Wonderland that they had been in Kyoto, Japan. “For some reason—you’ll find this as crazy as I did—she’d never been to Japan before,” he stated. “Neither had I, and we’d both wanted to go for a really long time. There were talks before we went about me popping the question but I was trying to keep it secret. She was not trying to keep it a secret, if you know what I mean. So I had to be really sneaky about it. We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could.”.

“We took a walk around the hotel grounds,” he continued. “There was this little outer garden that you had to cross a plank to get over. There was a sign that remarked you couldn’t cross; I was like, ‘That’s crazy, let’s go over there.’ She’s like, ‘No, it’s blocked.’ Eventually I persuaded her and we walked in and it was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos, and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”.
