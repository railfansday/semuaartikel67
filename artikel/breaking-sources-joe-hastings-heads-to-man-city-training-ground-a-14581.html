Falkirk sensation Joe Hastings is training with Manchester City with a view to a January move, sources have told Football Insider.

The 17-year-old has been identified as one for the future by Man City scouts, and they have given him the opportunity to prove himself at their training ground.

Hastings, a Scotland Under-17 international, has yet to make his senior debut for Falkirk but has been involved in a number of matchday squads with the first team.

He penned his first professional contract with the Premiership outfit just over one year ago, but could already be tempted away to the bright lights of the Premier League.

The goalkeeper is seen as one of the biggest talents to come out of Scotland in recent years, and there is a feeling he will play at the highest level later down the line.

For now, Man City are believed to be assessing his performances in training and are keen to land a permanent deal, which could be completed in the January window.

Hastings has been widely tipped as a future top-level goalkeeper, and is extremely highly regarded not just with Falkirk, but also by the Scotland national team.

His development through the academy ranks has inspired interest from a number of top clubs, both in Scotland, in England and across Europe.

Man City have now moved firmly to the front of the queue by bringing him in to training, and are progressing towards reaching an agreement for him to join in January.

Hastings could be the latest young star to join the Man City academy, with work ongoing to land the brightest domestic talents and bolster their youth ranks for the future.

Hastings would not be the only young Falkirk star to make the switch to English football after impressing in Scotland, with their recent success placing clubs on alert.

During the last summer transfer window, Barney Stewart was pursued by a number of English clubs who had scouts keeping tabs on his performances in Scotland.

Football Insider revealed in May that West Brom had made a move to sign Stewart and hoped to add him to their squad, before they eventually reached an agreement.

Since joining the Championship club, the striker has made 12 league appearances and scored one goal, but remains part of their long-term looks.
