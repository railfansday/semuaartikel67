Sources indicate that jonathan Pearce and Ally Yahuff's scene with her parents kicking her out after revealing her pregnancy was heartbreaking, but as her parents have allegedly stated on social media, it was scripted. As 90 Day Fiancé: The Other Way cut to Ally crying in bed, her parents hopped on social media to give the alleged truth behind this storyline.

According to recent reports, for those who missed the latest episode on the 2026 TV schedule, Jonathan and Ally met with her parents and sister and broke the news about the pregnancy. The episode then cut to a scene of the city, with text explaining that her parents refused to participate in filming after they revealed the news. Now we're learning that's allegedly not entirely true, as they have become the latest subjects to accuse the TLC series of faking scenes.

Sources indicate that ally's parents replied to a comment on a social media post made by 90 Day Fiancé fan @NathanTemp2 and laid out what they allege happened after Jonathan dropped the news. As it turns out, their story is completely different than what was shown in the episode. Check it out below (via @KikiandKibbitz):.

It has been revealed that this is not true at all. We took the pregnancy very well. We are very happy. We have been spending quality time with Ally, Jon, and the baby for almost 7 months now. What disappointed us was the fact that the whole storyline is revolving around us, the parents, not knowing about the pregnancy, when we already knew! We just played along.

Recent findings suggest that the comment went on to state that they were under the impression the storyline was solely about Jonathan and Ally, but agreed to filming despite being uncomfortable on camera. They did ask TLC to omit the footage, but it had nothing to do with Ally's pregnancy and more their discomfort replaying that moment for television.

Now, it's always vital to note that this information is still alleged, and Ally's parents could be stating this to save face after what was originally shown in 90 Day Fiancé: The Other Way. Conversely, I did wonder why Jonathan and Ally would need to tell them the news when she was clearly showing.

Recent findings suggest that the newly introduced information presents conflicting accounts of what happened with Jonathan and Ally, and makes me question why she was so upset afterward. If that scene was also scripted, then I'm also wondering if all the red flags that we saw were also just for the show.
