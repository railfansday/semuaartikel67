This is the best price we've ever seen on a single AirTag.

For Black Friday, the price of a single AirTag (second generation) dropped to just $18 on Amazon. The retail price for one AirTag is $29, so this is a nice 38% off.

You could potentially lower the price even further by combining additional ways to save on Amazon purchases with this sale, such as this recent targeted Amazon deal we've seen that can save you as much as 50% by using some Amex points.

And if you need more than one AirTag, the price for a four-pack is right now $79.99 on Amazon (down from a retail price of $99.99), but that is actually a bit pricier than buying exactly the number of AirTags you need individually.

If you aren't already an AirTags convert, they are a reliable way to keep tabs on your bags, even if the airline can't tell you where it is.

If you've had your AirTag for a while and think you need to replace it because the battery is getting low, there's good news: You don't actually need to buy a new one and instead you can change the battery on an AirTag.
