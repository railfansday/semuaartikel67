It is the penultimate week of the college football regular season.

Next week is a highly anticipated rivalry week, so many teams are playing tune-up games this week to prepare for their rival.

Other teams face tough tests as they try to improve their standing with the College Football Playoff committee, get the sixth win that will help them become bowl eligible, or at the very least, look like a program recruits or potential transfers will want to join next year.

Even if the overall slate looks a little lackluster, most players have only two weeks left to make a name for themselves in the individual award races.

With that in mind, here is a look at the players to watch in college football's Week 12 slate.

No. 11 Wake Forest at Georgia Tech (Noon ET, DIRECTV/ESPN).

According to recent reports, the Demon Deacons are win-and-in … at least for the ACC title game. Wake Forest has two games left: Georgia Tech and Duke. A win this weekend would secure the Demon Deacons' spot in the ACC championship game, with an automatic CFP bid hanging in the balance.

Hernandez heads into this weekend's game against the Yellow Jackets with the fourth-most receiving yards in the FBS. He has scored at least one touchdown in four straight games.

Hernandez was also named one of 13 finalists for this year's Biletnikoff Award, which goes to the nation's top wide receiver.

Industry observers note that san Diego State at No. 17 Boise State (9:30 p.m. ET, DIRECTV/USA).

Madsen is human. The Broncos quarterback finally threw his first interception of the entire season two games ago in the 46-45 win over Texas State.

According to recent reports, how did he respond? Only with his best game of the season so far. In an absolute slugfest against Oregon State, Madsen completed 23 of his 32 passes for 371 yards and four touchdowns.

The Boise State QB is now up to 2,051 yards, 19 touchdowns and one interception as he tries to keep the Broncos in the CFP hunt as the Group of Six representative.

Sources indicate that iowa State at UCF (Friday, 6 p.m. ET, DIRECTV/FS1).

Recent findings suggest that there is a new FBS leader in rushing yards. Flora ran all over the Cincinnati defense for 216 yards and three touchdowns in Iowa State's dominant 41-13 win last weekend.

The Cyclones running back now has 1,335 rushing yards, the most in the FBS, and he has added 12 touchdowns.

It has been revealed that flora still has a long way to go to break the school's single-season record, but he could post the third-best rushing season in Iowa State history with 238 yards over the final two games to move in front of Breece Hall. He has run for at least 182 yards in three consecutive games, so he is certainly on the right pace for that.

Memphis at Navy (Noon ET, DIRECTV/ESPN2).

Industry observers note that when the AP poll was released this week, many may have been surprised to see the Memphis Tigers sneak into the ranking at No. 24, ahead of No. 25 Ole Miss.

Sources indicate that memphis is 8-2 and tied for first in the American Conference alongside UTSA. The Tigers have won three straight and six of their last seven games. One substantial piece of this win streak has been junior wide receiver Jemyri Davis.

Recent findings suggest that he leads the team in yards (561) and touchdowns (7) and has done it on just 28 receptions. Davis has scored a touchdown in four consecutive games.

With the Tigers having a chance to be the G6 rep in the CFP, Davis will be leaned on even more for Memphis' final two regular-season games.

Colorado at Cincinnati (Noon ET, DIRECTV/ESPN+).

The Bearcats lost to Flora and Iowa State last week, but Tabb still put up substantial numbers with 165 yards on the ground.

Tabb has tallied at least 131 rushing yards in four of his last five games. While his total yards and 10 touchdowns aren't close to leading the FBS, his impressive 9.1 yards per carry is.

Bowling Green at Toledo (Friday, 7:30 p.m. ET, DIRECTV/ESPN2).

You may not know Bradley, but it's worth keeping an eye on him for Bowling Green's final two games of the year.

While the Falcons are 3-7, Bradley has been the team's highlight and has recorded an FBS-high 14 sacks.

According to recent reports, he recorded five sacks last week in Bowling Green's win over Kent State. Bradley is five sacks behind Kevin O'Brien, who presently holds the school's single-season record.

Industry observers note that the Falcons and Bradley will take on Toledo this week. The Rockets' Andrew Zock is tied for the second-most sacks in the FBS (11.5), so both defenses have a player to watch.
