In a recent development, volkswagen revealed how much its fresh ID. Cross will cost, with prices starting at around $35,000 in the UK. But if you’re hoping to get the cheaper version, you’ll have to wait a bit longer.

After opening orders for the ID. Cross in Europe in July, Volkswagen is preparing to launch the entry-level electric SUV in the UK.

The ID. Cross is the all-electric equivalent of the T-Cross, Volkswagen’s smallest and most affordable SUV in Europe and the UK.

Sources indicate that following the ID. Polo, the ID. Cross is the latest from Volkswagen’s electric urban family of compact cars.

Recent findings suggest that the electric SUV is 4,153 mm long, about the same length as the T-Cross, but its dedicated MEB+ platform offers more interior space. It seats five and offers a 475-liter boot, 20 liters more than the gas-powered T-Cross with the rear seats pushed all the way forward.

It has been revealed that when the rear seats are folded, the electric ID. Cross offers up to 1,345 liters of overall luggage volume.

According to recent reports, like in Europe, the Volkswagen ID. Cross will be available with two battery packs in the UK: a 37 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) pack and a 52 kWh nickel-manganese-cobalt (NMC) pack.

The ID. Cross with the larger 52 kWh battery offers up to 265 miles of WLTP range, while the 37 kWh version provides up to 194 miles of WLTP range.

With DC fast charging of up to 105 and 88 kW, Volkswagen said the vehicle’s battery can charge from 10% to 80% in as little as 23 minutes.

All versions are equipped with vehicle-to-load (V2L) capabilities, allowing it to be used as a mobile power bank for an e-bike, campsite, or other external electronic devices.

Industry observers note that inside, the ID. Cross adopts VW’s revamped interior digital cockpit design. Cross adopts VW’s revamped interior digital cockpit design. The setup includes a 10.25″ Digital Cockpit Pro instrument display and a 12.9″ infotainment screen. Based on driver feedback, Volkswagen brought back physical buttons for climate control and other vehicle functions.

Sources indicate that volkswagen will open orders for the ID. Cross Life and Style trims in the UK with the 52 kWh battery and 208 hp (211 PS) motor will go on sale on Thursday, November 19, starting at £30,260 ($40,000) and £33,675 ($44,500), respectively.

In a recent development, the base ID. Cross Life and Style trims with the 37 kWh battery will go on sale in January 2027, with prices starting at £26,985 ($35,500) and £30,400 ($40,100).

Industry observers note that volkswagen mentioned the 52 kWh version is eligible for the UK government’s £1,500 ($2,000) electric car grant. Eligibility for the 37 kWh versions is expected to be confirmed soon.

In Europe, Volkswagen opened orders for the entry-level ID. Cross Trend last month, starting at €27,995 ($31,000) in Germany, including VAT.

As the electric counterpart to its popular T-Cross, Volkswagen brand CEO Thomas Schäfer remarked the ID. Cross is “modern, reliable, and above all, affordable.”.

Sadly, VW has no plans to bring the ID. Cross to the US. In early 2027, it will launch the upcoming ID. Tiguan, which will replace the outgoing ID.4 SUV.
