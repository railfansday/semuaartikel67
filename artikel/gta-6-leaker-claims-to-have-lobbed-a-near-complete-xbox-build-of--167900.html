📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that cyberleek, the GTA 6 leaker or leakers who shared a bunch of alleged gameplay earlier this year, are now claiming to have released a nearly complete in-development build of the crimsim's Xbox version. Continuing their established grift, the leaker or leakers are threatening to release the main executable file they've withheld unless people pump up the value of their cryptocurrency.

In a recent development, "It is done," a statement posted on Cyberleek’s website claimed. "CL holds the full November 2026 build of Grand Theft Auto VI, and it is going out by torrent. Now. Every file except one - the main executable - held on a countdown. Honest terms, as always: it is an Xbox build this time, not a PC build. CL does not care. This community will have it emulated inside a week, running far past 30fps. CL told you not to preorder for a reason. This is one of the reasons.".

So, more anime villain chatter from Cyberleek, who claim the executable file is on a timer and will be released immediately if their crypto coin reaches a $15 million market cap. It looks like the build is yet to be confirmed as genuine.

If any build leak were to result in GTA 6 being emulated on PC in rapid fashion, that would threaten to undercut an official port. Rockstar have yet to confirm that such a port is on the way and when it'll drop, but an October report from GamesBeat claimed the first three months of 2027 as the window in which Rockstar initiative to deliver the game to PC. Former Rockstar North technical director Obbe Vermeij recently told our Edwin he reckons that concerns about unofficial ports based on the console version of GTA 6, likely made with generative AI, might lead Rockstar not to leave too big a gap before bringing the crimsim to PC officially.

Any alleged playable unofficial port of GTA 6 made with help from genAI and chucked online could, of course, just be a great big pile of malware.

Rockstar, who previously called the initial barrage of leaks laid claim to by Cyberleek "heartbreaking", as well as parent organization Take-Two, looked to have begun a hunt for the identity of the leakers back in August.
