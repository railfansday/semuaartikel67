On the eve of GTA 6 finally making its debut, it seems a lucky few have been given a special way to play it.

Several NBA players, all belonging to the Miami Heat team, have received a crazy care package from Rockstar Games that includes a special edition PS5 Pro console that's not publicly available.

The console essentially matches the GTA 6 DualSense controllers, which are available to purchase. The machine sports the VI logo in one corner, has a black-on-black, embossed palm tree motif, and the blades across the console are that telltale neon pink and blue.

According to recent reports, it looks great, and the package also comes with two of the matching controllers and a copy of GTA 6 itself. It's all delivered in a suitcase adorned in the same stylings.

Recent findings suggest that this is all part of the massive marketing spin that's taking place in Miami. A special, one-night-only basketball game sees Miami Heat be rebranded as Vice City, not to mention other initiatives and stunts spotted in the area.

This move has attracted a bit of criticism from onlookers, though; with the console not accessible to regular fans, it feels to them unfair that athletes and celebrities are simply gifted exclusive goods like this.

It has been revealed that still, while it's a nice looking PS5 Pro, it doesn't do anything special. Whichever PS5 you have, GTA 6 will soon be playable, and it turns out the game is absolutely stellar.
