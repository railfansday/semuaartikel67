Wendy’s is getting nostalgic this holiday season, and as someone who grew up eating in one of the chain’s iconic glass sunrooms, I’m particularly excited about this one.

The fast food chain just introduced a limited-time Holiday Box complete with retro packaging, collectible knit hats, and a choice of some familiar menu favorites. And of course, two new peppermint-inspired Frosty desserts.

Obviously, I had to try everything for myself.

Available beginning November 16 at participating Wendy’s locations nationwide, the Holiday Box starts at $11.99 and includes your choice of a Dave’s Double or 10-piece crispy or spicy chicken nuggets, small fries, a small soft drink, and one collectible knit hat.

The packaging is designed to resemble Wendy’s old-school Solarium restaurants, complete with the recognizable glass-walled dining area that became a staple of the chain beginning in the 1980s.

And I have to admit, this one hit me right in the childhood memories.

It has been revealed that growing up, I remember going to Wendy’s with my mom and sitting in that yellow, glass-enclosed sunroom. Seeing the design recreated as a miniature holiday box immediately brought me back to those visits.

Each Holiday Box includes one of four collectible knit hats featuring a retro Wendy’s design: Frosty, Biggie, the classic Wendy’s logo, or the famous “Where’s the Beef?” slogan.

The hats come in sealed mystery bags, meaning you won’t know which one you’re getting until you open it.

Thankfully, I ended up with the blue Frosty beanie, which was exactly the design I was hoping for.

Had I opened the bag to find a hat with “Biggie” written across my forehead, though, I think we would’ve had a problem.

The Holiday Box is available for a limited time while supplies last, and customers can’t select which hat they receive.

Alongside the Holiday Box, Wendy’s is introducing two limited-time desserts: the Peppermint Frosty Swirl and Peppermint Brownie Frosty Fusion.

Both are available with either a vanilla or chocolate Frosty base, so I decided to try one of each.

This was easily my favorite of the two.

The Peppermint Brownie Frosty Fusion combines Wendy’s classic Frosty with peppermint sauce and brownie pieces, creating a dessert that feels especially appropriate for the holiday season.

I went with the vanilla base, and I absolutely loved it.

Sources indicate that the peppermint flavor was strong and immediately reminded me of a candy cane or a York Peppermint Pattie. It had that recognizable minty sweetness I look for in a holiday dessert, without getting lost in the vanilla Frosty.

The brownie pieces were another highlight, adding a nice texture and a layer of chocolate flavor to the mix. I particularly liked how the chocolate complemented the peppermint rather than competing with it.

In a recent development, for me, this is exactly what a seasonal Frosty should taste like. It’s festive, flavorful, and different enough from the regular menu to make it worth trying.

For my second dessert, I went with the chocolate Peppermint Frosty Swirl, which combines Wendy’s classic Chocolate Frosty with peppermint sauce.

My biggest piece of advice is to give this one a good stir before digging in.

Sources indicate that when I first tried it, most of the peppermint syrup was concentrated toward the top of the dessert. Mixing everything together helped distribute the flavor more evenly throughout the Frosty.

Once I did that, I really enjoyed it.

The combination of chocolate and peppermint works well, although I didn’t find the mint flavor nearly as strong as it was in the vanilla Brownie Frosty Fusion.

Industry observers note that still, I’m happy to report that Wendy’s peppermint sauce works with both Frosty flavors. If you prefer a more straightforward chocolate-mint combination without brownie pieces, this is a solid option.

Personally, though, I’d go back for the Fusion.
