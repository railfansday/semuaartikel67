Hold On, Did Grace Die on ‘The Body’?

Spoilers ahead for The Body season 1.

Talk about divine intervention. Now that you’ve finished the first season of The Body, let’s talk about that ending. Specifically, what happens to Grace? Is she alive? Is she somehow more than that? How?!

At the beginning of the first season, Sofia Wylie’s character Grace is initiated into the dance team and given drugs at a house party as part of a “Satanic ritual.” The drug, ecstasy, causes Grace to pass out, seize, and seemingly die that night. The party took place at a building called Devil’s House... what did they expect? The girls try to cover up what they did by driving her home. But then, unexpectedly, Grace starts breathing again at the end of the episode. Is Grace just in a coma? That’s what they go with until things get weirder. It kind of becomes a whole thing.

Well, it was 2004. The Passion of the Christ was one of the highest-grossing films of the year. Everyone was doing the Numa Numa dance and just starting to get into Lost. If a teenage girl came back from the dead, it would be a huge deal. People would definitely try to cast her as a savior, which is what happens to Grace.

Lisbeth starts claiming she tried to resuscitate her, and that her survival is a miracle she performed. Meanwhile, Grace starts haunting some of the girls in their dreams. So, naturally, they start to ascribe meaning to her. Word spreads, and before long the whole town starts to worship.

In the finale, immediately after Anya confesses to what they did, Grace wakes up from her coma and says “I’m God.” She is risen? Literally, that’s what she says. (It’s also, for the theory-brained, the same thing Lisbeth says when she indicates up at the infamous Halloween party because she dressed up as “slutty God,” complete with a toga and a giant crucifix. You know, just before she talks about being anointed.).

What did she mean by that? Does she even realize that she noted it? Not only are Grace’s words bizarre, but doctors also conclude that she wasn’t even in a coma that whole time. All they’re able to conclude is that she was unconscious. Where was she?! What happened to her?!

It has been revealed that according to Wylie, creator Quinn Shepherd has an explanation that the two of them are keeping to themselves for potential future seasons of The Body. “Quinn’s explanation is something I’d love to keep under wraps,” the actress told Tudum, “because I think the beauty of the show is that it stays within the mystery, whether it’s medical or spiritual.”.

So, ultimately, it’s all a big mystery. We just have to have faith that The Body will get renewed for season 2 and continue this story.
