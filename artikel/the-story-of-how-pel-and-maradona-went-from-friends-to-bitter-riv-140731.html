A new book charts the complicated relationship between the two legends, starting on a sunny afternoon in Rio in 1979.

In a recent development, pelé and Diego Maradona first met in April 1979, four months before the Argentinian led his country’s under-20 side to victory at the World Youth Cup. Maradona told a journalist friend that his dream was to meet the Brazilian, and after a few weeks of aborted attempts to bring them together – mostly because Diego did not want to travel on a Monday, the one day he had free for his girlfriend Claudia – the meeting finally took place in Rio.

Reporter Guillermo Blanco, who was writing about the meeting for Argentinian sports magazine El Gráfico, got the OK from Pelé’s people and was so intent on making it happen that he checked Maradona onto a flight one Sunday night without telling him. He collared the young man after Argentinos Juniors played Huracán and then raced to the airport and put him on a plane with his father, his agent and a photographer.
