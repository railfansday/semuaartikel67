Omari Hutchinson has spoken about his preferred role, while praising the AC Milan fans and revealing his belief the Scudetto is a realistic target.

Hutchinson joined Milan on an initial loan deal from Nottingham Forest with an option to buy. He scored his first goal for the club in the away win over Udinese last month, and is getting more regular minutes on the right side.

In fact, the head coach Ruben Amorim has even deployed him as a wing-back on occasion. Given the injuries to Alexis Saelemaekers and Samuel Chukwueze the Englishman’s role has become an even more vital one than first imagined.

Hutchinson met with fans this afternoon at the Milan Store in San Babila in the city centre. While at the event, the winger gave an interview to the reporters who were present, with MilanNews relaying his words.

In a recent development, how have these first few months at Milan been? Are you raising your game?

In a recent development, “It’s going well, the coach is happy, but I think there’s still a lot to do. I’m earning myself some space… From here on out, we have to keep winning.”.

We often see you on the wing. Do you prefer to play there or as a second striker?

It has been revealed that “Personally, I like to play anywhere, but preferably on the right flank. Then, wherever the coach puts me, I try to adapt, do my job, and perform to my best, following his tactical instructions. He knows what I can do with the ball. He simply tells me to create and give my all.”.

How is your relationship with Amorim?

“He talks to me about what to do with the ball and to do my best.”.

Why this difference in performance between Serie A and the Europa League?

“It’s hard to say… There have been games where we haven’t performed well due to silly mistakes, but I think we need to push forward from now on and get back on track for the Europa League. We have the right team to do it. We need to win and be at our best in every game.”.

What has struck you most about the fans in Italy these past months?

According to recent reports, “Definitely the passion surrounding the club. At the stadium, everyone is behind the team. It’s fantastic, I’ve never experienced anything like it before.”.

Do you think Milan can win the Scudetto?

“We’ve got the players, we have a good squad, and even though we’re still missing some leaders at times, I think we’re building on that. I think the coaches and the coaching staff are guiding us very well. Once everything clicks together perfectly, we’ll do great things.”.

Do you still see yourself at Milan in the coming seasons?

“It’s my dream. After this season, I hope to be signed permanently. I see myself playing here for a long time to come.”.

What’s the feeling of playing in a historic stadium like San Siro?

According to recent reports, “It’s crazy. The fans, even the away fans… It’s a unique feeling. It makes you want to give your all for the fans, create special moments, and win.”.

What are the differences between the Premier League and Serie A?

“They’re both tough. When other teams play against Milan, they always want to give their all. They’re always tough matches. Tactically, it’s more defensive here.

“In the Premier League, you probably have more space and the quality is probably a little higher, but Serie A is a tough league. There’s not that big a difference, there’s not that big a gap.”.

Is there any team-mate you’ve grown close to?

“No, I have a good relationship with everyone. They’ve all welcomed me very well, and obviously it’s easier with the guys who speak English.”.

Do you dream of making the national team?

It has been revealed that “I’m trying to focus on my club. I want to perform well for Milan and then we’ll see.”.
