📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Join Shell Fuel Rewards if most of your gas comes from Shell, since the free program takes 5 cents a gallon off every fill-up for drivers who buy 10 gallons or more there six times a quarter. When another station on your route undercuts your Shell by more than your discount, fill up there instead.

For an average car or SUV buying all its gas at Shell, those 5 cents a gallon come to about $21 a year on the 423 gallons it burns, by the Federal Highway Administration’s count. Paying with Shell S Pay from a checking account raises the discount to 10 cents, about $42, in place of whatever a credit card would earn on that gas.

Industry observers note that drivers who already buy 10 gallons or more at Shell six times a quarter get the program’s 5 cents a gallon for nothing. Drivers without a Shell on their routes should check a gas price app and fill up at the cheapest station instead.
