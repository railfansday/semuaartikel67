Liverpool have performed much better since they were beaten by Fenerbahce in the Champions League a few weeks ago.

The Reds were torn apart by the Turkish Super Lig outfit with former Liverpool star Dirk Kuyt leading his team to a historic win.

Indeed, they are one of the better teams in Turkey but, compared to how much talent the Reds have among their ranks, they shouldn't have even had a chance to beat them.

That's why Liverpool's defeat was so hard to take for fans.

However, as mentioned, since then, things have gone well for Liverpool with the club even beating arch-rivals Manchester United 4-0.

Even though transfers are expected to occur in the January transfer window, Andoni Iraola still has a few players among his ranks who fans haven't seen the best of yet.

That's why Wednesday's Champions League tie against Club Brugge could be incredibly crucial for one of them.

Victor Munoz has to start against Club Brugge.

Sunday's game was Liverpool's highlight of the season so far.

Their win against the Red Devils meant that they were thrusted back into fourth position and now find themselves seven points adrift of first-placed Arsenal.

This is still a decent gap to cut down but, it's a lot less daunting than the nine points that separated them over the international break.

During Sunday's victory against Man United, Victor Munoz was left on the bench to start with.

And, even though Liverpool were winning their arch-rivals 3-0 when the last 20 minutes of the game came upon them, Iraola still chose to bring the Spaniard on.

Most people expected that transfer to have been done to give those who started a rest but, Munoz had other aims.

In the 18 minutes of football that he played, his lightning quick speed and his ability to turn nothing chances into goals paid off as the 23-year-old bagged the Reds' fourth and last goal of the game.

He also maintained a 92 per cent pass accuracy and he even made one key pass.

This performance alone proves why he should be starting against Club Brugge in Liverpool's next Champions League tie.

Victor Munoz could become a key player for Liverpool in the next couple of years.

Munoz was Liverpool's bargain signing of the summer transfer window.

The Spaniard was brought in by the Reds for a fee worth around £35 million.

At the time, Newcastle were also trying to sign him but, in the end, he chose Liverpool.

Most fans thought that he'd be more of a back-up than anything else but, in reality, he's become a key member of Iraola's first-team squad.

His performances have been second to none and, even though he's often been used in his less preferred position of right-wing, he's still contributed to four goals in the 13 games he's featured in.

Here's hoping that, if Iraola does start the "outstanding" attacker on Wednesday, that he can put on another stellar performance.
