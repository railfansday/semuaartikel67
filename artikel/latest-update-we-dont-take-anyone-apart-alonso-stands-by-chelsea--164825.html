Xabi Alonso insisted Chelsea "don't take anyone apart" after Wesley Fofana came under more criticism in their defeat to Nottingham Forest.

Chelsea lost 3-2 at the City Ground on Friday after Igor Jesus' volley and a brace from Morgan Gibbs-White secured victory for Forest, despite Emmanuel Emegha's double for the Blues.

Since the start of March, Chelsea have lost 14 of their 23 Premier League games (W7 D2) – the most of any side in the competition in this period.

Chelsea have conceded 23 goals in the Premier League this season – only bottom side Crystal Palace (27) have shipped more.

In fact, it is the Blues' joint-most in their opening 12 matches of a campaign in the competition (also 23 in 2015-16).

Fofana, who was booed by his own fans after a recent interview in which he appeared to joke about missing the decisive penalty in an EFL Cup defeat to Liverpool, gave away a penalty for Forest's second goal after a clumsy challenge on Liam Delap.

The Frenchman was then replaced by Levi Colwill, who was benched for makeshift centre-back Moises Caicedo, but Alonso has called for togetherness during a difficult spell.

"We need to support everyone. Everyone that is in the team we need to support," Alonso told Sky Sports when asked about Fofana.

"We know we have to go through difficult moments and we don't take apart anyone and we want to help and stick together.

"It's a difficult moment for everyone. It's disappointing again to lose again but we need to keep trying to find solutions, we can't stop.

"We are low in confidence, the team is struggling to hold a result. After getting to 2-1 we have conceded and go to 3-1. Everything is not going our way, we are responsible for that and we are responsible to find solutions.".

Alonso has now lost as many of his 15 games as Chelsea manager in all competitions as he did in 34 games in charge of previous club Real Madrid (six).
