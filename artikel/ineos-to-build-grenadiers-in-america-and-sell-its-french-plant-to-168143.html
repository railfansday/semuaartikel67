Ineos to Build Grenadiers in America and Sell Its French Plant to BYD

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Ineos Grenadier SUV and Quartermaster pickup truck could be trading the French-flag badges that signal their Hambach production for ones that feature America’s stars and stripes. That’s because the fledgling British automaker has now confirmed plans to begin building its vehicles in the United States, with the first American-made Ineos models scheduled for 2028.

Industry observers note that ineos Automotive is still determining the exact location of its planned American production facility, but it states that it has developed a shortlist of potential sites. The move will bring the brand’s production to its largest market, with the United States making up around 70 percent of Ineos’s global sales.

Since Ineos already sells most of its vehicles in the U.S., the move will help make the brand more efficient. By building its vehicles in America, Ineos will also be able to avoid paying many of the lofty import tariffs that it’s right now subjected to. Plus, along with announcing intends to move to the United States, Ineos confirmed it’s in the process of selling its current Hambach facility to Chinese automotive giant BYD.

At this point, Ineos hasn’t revealed whether it plans to buy an existing manufacturing facility, build a upcoming one, or use another automaker’s excess capacity. One thing Ineos has been clear about, though, is that the move will cover its entire global production. A final decision on the facility is expected within the next few months to keep the move on schedule.
