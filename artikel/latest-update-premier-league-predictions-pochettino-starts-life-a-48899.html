The Opta supercomputer has delivered its match predictions ahead of the latest weekend of the 2026-27 Premier League season following the latest international break.

During the two-week hiatus, there were two managerial changes, with Mauricio Pochettino replacing Michael Carrick at Manchester United, who sit in the relegation zone, and Thomas Frank succeeding Pierre Sage at Crystal Palace, who prop up the table.

At the other end of the standings, Arsenal hold a five-point lead, having won each of their last five matches.

They sit above high-flying Brighton, while Manchester City slipped down to third following three straight losses.

Matchday 11 sees fixtures take place on Saturday, Sunday and Monday, and ahead of what should be another enticing group of Premier League fixtures, we check out the Opta supercomputer's match predictions.

Manchester City's strong start to the season came crashing down before the break, and they are looking to get back to winning ways when they host Fulham on Saturday.

City have lost three straight league games, as many as in their previous 36 (W25 D8); since the start of 2007-08, they have only lost four in a row twice, doing so in August 2008 and December 2024.

However, City have won their last 17 Premier League games against Fulham and have won their last 20 against the Cottagers in all competitions.

And our predictive model makes them the favourites to triumph at 66.1%, with no team having a higher win probability across the fixtures this weekend. Fulham's hopes of a win sit at 15.5%.

Fulham struggled at the start of the season, but they are unbeaten in their last four league games (W2 D2) and have lost just one of their last seven (W2 D4).

Onto the 15:00 kick-offs, Aston Villa are looking to bounce back after throwing away a late lead in their thrilling 4-4 draw with Man Utd against Sunderland.

Villa have lost just one of their last 12 top-flight matches against Sunderland (W5 D6), going down 3-1 at the Stadium of Light in January 2016.

And Villa are unbeaten in their last six league games (W3 D3), though they dropped points from winning positions in all three draws.

Sunderland, meanwhile, are looking to win successive Premier League games for the first time this season following their 1-0 victory over Chelsea last time out.

It is Villa who are the favourites to triumph, winning 59.3% of the supercomputer's pre-match simulations for Unai Emery's 150th league game in charge, to Sunderland's 19.4%.

BOURNEMOUTH V NOTTINGHAM FOREST.

A rampant Bournemouth (55.3%) are looking to continue their push up the table against Nottingham Forest (21.6%), who ended their slump against Man City before the break.

The Cherries, who have won each of their last eight matches in all competitions, are unbeaten in all eight of their Premier League games against Forest (W4 D4).

Bournemouth have pushed their way into the top seven after a slow start to the campaign, and they are now aiming to win five straight games in the top-flight for the very first time.

As for Forest, they are winless in six league away games against Bournemouth (D2 L4), though they are looking to win successive top-flight matches for the second time this calendar year (a run of three in April/May).

And a victory would be the perfect result for Oliver Glasner, who is set to take charge of his 100th Premier League match (W33 D32 L34), though he is yet to beat Bournemouth in the competition.

Chelsea went into the international break after a disappointing loss to Sunderland, while Leeds United ended their five-game winless streak with a win over Tottenham.

The Blues are expected to come out on top at Stamford Bridge, with a 51.1% win probability, given they have won 10 points in five top-flight home games this term (W3 D1 L1).

Sources indicate that they have also won eight of their last 10 home games against Leeds in all competitions (D2), since a 2-0 loss in December 1999. Chelsea have already beaten the Whites at home in the EFL Cup this season, winning 6-3.

But Leeds, who won 24% of the pre-match simulations, avoided defeat in both league meetings with Chelsea in 2025-26 (3-1 at home, 2-2 away).

They have not gone three straight without defeat against them since between November 2000 and October 2001, however.

At 26.8%, there is a healthy chance of a draw between Coventry City and Crystal Palace, as Frank makes his bow in charge of the Eagles.

Coventry are the favourites, at 45.7%, while Palace have a 27.5% win probability.

This is the first meeting between Coventry and Palace in any competition since March 2012, a 1-1 draw in the Championship.

Palace won their first four visits to the Coventry Building Society Arena between 2005 and 2008 but are winless in their last four in all competitions between 2009 and 2012 (D2 L2).

Coventry have lost just one of their six Premier League games against Crystal Palace (W2 D3).

Frank's former club Tottenham are also in action on Saturday, as they host Ipswich Town.

Spurs' strong run was snapped in a 1-0 loss to Leeds before the international break, but our model is backing them to bounce back, with Roberto De Zerbi's team given a 55.2% win probability to Ipswich's 21.7%, with the chance of a draw coming in at 23.1%.

However, Spurs have lost four of their last five Premier League games against Ipswich, though the exception was a 4-1 win in their last meeting in February 2025.

Tottenham are also winless in four when playing their first Premier League match after an international break (D1 L3), drawing 1-1 with Man Utd following this season's first break.

But Spurs should be expected to score, as Ipswich have conceded in each of their last 29 Premier League games, and only four teams have ever gone 30+ consecutive matches in the competition without a shutout.

Sources indicate that saturday's action rounds out at St. James' Park, as Newcastle United host Arsenal.

Newcastle have won just one of their last 29 Premier League games against sides starting the day top of the table (D8 L20), and none of their last 18 (D4 L14) since a 2-1 victory over Chelsea in December 2014.

And the supercomputer anticipates that run to continue, with Arsenal (56.4%) the most likely away victors this weekend.

None of Newcastle's last 13 Premier League home games against Arsenal have been drawn (W4 L9), with the Magpies winning three of the last five, but they are handed just a 21.7% win probability here.

Arsenal have only won more Premier League games against Everton (41) and West Ham (39) than they have against Newcastle (38), while their 32 clean sheets against the Magpies are the most one side has against another in the competition.

Hull City won their first two Premier League games this season but are winless in all eight since then (D5 L3).

It is the fourth time a side has won their first two games and then none of their next eight in the competition, along with Wolves in 2011-12, Hull in 2016-17, and West Brom in 2017-18, all of whom were relegated.

However, ahead of their first top-flight meeting with Brighton, the Tigers are still unbeaten at home in the Premier League this term.

Brighton are winless in all six of their visits to MKM Stadium in all competitions (D2 L4). Their last away win against Hull came at Boothferry Park in the fourth tier in October 2000.

But the Seagulls are flying high and are considered massive favourites, at 50.1%. There is a 23.6% probability of a draw, with Hull's chances coming in at 26.3%.

Sunday's headline fixture sees Manchester United, under the tutelage of Pochettino, head to Anfield to face rivals, Liverpool.

Just one of the Red Devils' last nine bosses (including caretakers) has lost his first Premier League game of a spell in charge of the club (W4 D4), with Erik ten Hag losing 1-2 to Brighton in August 2022.

United won this fixture 2-1 last season, ending a nine-game winless run at Anfield in the Premier League (D5 L4).

In fact, Liverpool lost both Premier League meetings with United last season, as many as they had in their previous 18 (W7 D9). They last lost more consecutively between December 2014 and January 2016 (4).

But the supercomputer is backing Liverpool, who have a 57.3% win probability. United's chances are rated at just 20.5%, with a 22.2% draw threat.

MD11 rounds out with Brentford hosting Everton on Monday.

In-form Everton are the underdogs, with a 28.8% chance of winning. Brentford, meanwhile, are handed a 44.3% chance of victory. There is a 26.9% likelihood of a draw, making this game the most likely to finish all level.

Everton have won back-to-back Premier League games for the first time since February/March, and are looking to win three in a row in the competition for the first time since May 2025.

Brentford have won four of their last seven Premier League games at the Gtech Community Stadium (D2 L1), and are looking for back-to-back home league wins for the first time in 2026, having last done so in November 2025, when they won three on the spin.
