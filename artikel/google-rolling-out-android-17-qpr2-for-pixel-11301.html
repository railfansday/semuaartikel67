Recent findings suggest that long-pressing on your homescreen reveals a tweaked design for the menu pop-up where the carousel appears first. Going to Wallpaper & style > Color reveals a new paint palette icon that gives you the “Select a color” slider to choose the main accent after previously being limited to presets. The corner pencil icon on the main page lets you choose between Neutral, Soft, Bright, and Bold themes.

Sources indicate that on the Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold, HiLight now supports message notifications — blinking twice — after launching with just calls and Gemini status. Go to Settings > System > HiLight for the new “Messages from favorites: Glows when your favorite contacts send you messages.” When you go to Google Contacts, the HiLight sheet demonstrates individual options for Phone calls and Messages.

In a recent development, when you’re charging or Battery Saver is enabled, there’s now an outline around the battery icon in Quick Settings. Additionally, it’s now Settings > Battery & charging for a more unified menu as introduced on the Pixel 11 series.

In Quick Settings, there are new Tiles for Audio streams and Voice broadcast. A bigger revision that lets you customize the arrangement of the brightness slider and media player is not launching with this release.

The Android Beta Program will remain open and continue testing QPRs and feature drops.

Sources indicate that to get the stable version, those running the Android 17 QPR2 Beta have to opt out first. Devices will not be wiped by the stable OTA once that becomes widely available.
