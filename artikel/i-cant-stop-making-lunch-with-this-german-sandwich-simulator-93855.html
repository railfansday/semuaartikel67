The art of making a sandwich has been revered since John Montagu, the fourth Earl of Sandwich, was so engrossed in a game of cards in 1762 he demanded meat between slices of bread to fuel his playing — it was a deeply espresso martini-coded move which saw all of his jealous mates also ask for one.

Over 250 years later, the subtle craft of 'wich is championed once again in an online sandwich-making simulator that threatens to eat into my GTA 6 time.

Created by German developer Gregor Weichbrodt with music by Scott Hühnerkrisp, a latest sandwich simulator is exactly that. "You make a sandwich. That's all," reads the simulator's about page. Notably, it's not the same as FiftyEight Studios' 2026 game Sandwich Simulator or Sandwich Squad's 2024 Sandwich Sim.

Sources indicate that each sandwich gets a serial number and a permalink, so you can revisit your creation later for reasons I can't quite conjure here beyond lunch inspo. To date, 42,375 unique sandwiches have been made on the simulator since it launched in Oct. 2026. For example, my tower of power sandwich will probably live on the internet after I shuffle off this mortal coil.

But there's more going on here, with the developer promising "realistic cucumber behavior" (if you put a slice too far on the edge it'll fall off) and "soft body physics" (slices of cheese, salmon, or ham will drape themselves over what's already on the sandwich).

To make a sandwich, all you need to do is add toppings from the array provided in the bottom menu. To add spreads, you tap the pot and a virtual knife will appear; holding your mouse down will drag that butter across the bread. For other toppings like cheese, radishes, crisps, eggs, tomatoes, and whatnot, you just drag your pick from the menu onto your bread.

Sources indicate that if you want to get rid of anything, because you've realised you hate radish (you monster), you can just drag it onto the board. And once you're happy with your creation, you can tap the photo button to save it and generate your permalink — a page which gives you a delightful description of all the ingredients. You can also see every sandwich that has ever been created on the game.

Yes, this might be the era of GTA 6, but between missions I'll be making salad sandwiches and drawing my own aquarium. Bit of balance, right?
