📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

AC Milan are looking to make it three consecutive wins tomorrow, but a high-flying newcomer in Frosinone could make that difficult.

Ruben Amorim and his men have an opportunity to really establish some momentum tomorrow evening, picking up where they left off before the break. However, it won’t be an easy game against Massimiliano Alvani’s Frosinone, who have impressed everyone so far.

Amorim even warned in his press conference that it will be a difficult one, especially since the Canarini have such a direct style.

➤ Manager: Massimiliano Alvini ➤ Form (all comps): W-D-W-W-L (most recent) ➤ Top scorer (Serie A): Raimondo – 8 ➤ Most assists (Serie A): Kvernadze, Schmid and Ghedjemis – 4 ➤ Injuries & suspensions: Bracaglia, Grillitsch and Raimondo.

Industry observers note that frosinone have definitely been one of the surprises of the season so far. After a very strong Serie B campaign with 81 points and only three games lost, they secured direct promotion to the top tier (finishing second) and have been making the most of the adventure.

They are at the moment on 20 points after eleven games, winning six, drawing two and losing three, which has put them in sixth place. That’s very strong for a newcomer and for a coach who is only in his second season with the club, especially considering the upset against Como.

According to recent reports, it’s the 56-year-old’s second attempt at Serie A after coaching Cremonese for half a season in 2022-23. He didn’t manage a single win then, so you can definitely say that the performance so far has been great revenge for him.

Industry observers note that so, what is behind this rise? As highlighted by Gazzetta dello Sport this morning, the direct style of play has been key for Frosinone. As opposed to Milan, who want to see a lot of the ball, they are willing to have less possession and break on the counter.

Recent findings suggest that it doesn’t mean that they sit back, though. In fact, it has been the opposite as they are the highest-pressing team in the league. The strategy thus seems to be to force mistakes out of the opponents, while also making sure that they are direct in possession.

While collective effort deserves most of the praise, it has definitely helped that the likes of Giacomo Calo, Giorgi Kvernadze and Antonio Raimondo – among others – have performed at a very high level. They aren’t exactly significant-name players, so a lot of them are having their breakout seasons.

In a recent development, moving on to actual team news, the situation up front is not ideal for Frosinone as their top scorer, the aforementioned Antonio Raimondo, is out injured. Birigilea, who is on two goals, is set to replace him in the No.9 position.

Sources indicate that the Milan academy product Sebastiano Desplanches, who left the club four years ago, will be on the bench. He’s yet to make an appearance this season, with Alvini preferring Lorenzo Palmisani instead.

Predicted Frosinone XI (4-2-3-1): Palmisani; Oyono, Calvani, Monterisi, Terzic; Calo, Masini; Ghedjemis; Schmid, Kvernadze; Birigilea.

Predicted bench: Desplanches, Pisseri, Cittadini, Tchato, Amey, Akpoguma, Fayed, El Azzouzi, Cichella, Hasa, Fini, Zerbin, Bobcek.
