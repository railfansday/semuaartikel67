Rihanna always knows how to kiss it better.

After EJ Johnson raised eyebrows with a video detailing Rihanna’s tardiness to a Paris Fashion Week show—and when her apparent clap back raised eyebrows even higher—the “Umbrella” singer took a moment to confirm there’s no lingering bad blood.

“There’s no beef, you know there’s no beef,” she said in a video shared by TMZ. “I just felt bad for him, you know?”.

In fact, she’s willing to put her best foot forward with EJ—or rather, allow them to.

When asked whether she’d ever let EJ walk in a Savage x Fenty show, the 38-year-old affirmed, “He deserves to.”.

But while Rihanna proved it’s all loveeeeeee with EJ these days, there was a period when fans thought the pair had everything but love on the brain.

ICYMI, during Paris Fashion Week back in September, EJ shared a video recapping their experience at Dior’s Ready to Wear Spring/Summer 2027 collection.

“We just left the Dior show, getting out of there was crazy but the show itself was beautiful,” the Rich Kids of Beverly Hills alum said in the vlog. “It did start an hour late because Rihanna was late and, you know, like, we didn’t care to wait for her, but the show was beautiful nonetheless.”.

Then, just a few days later, a video circulated on social media in which EJ could be seen posing for paparazzi in Paris, before making their way into their hotel. EJ’s way was soon blocked, however, by hotel staff, creating a kind of standstill witnessed by a crowd of fans and photographers.

And while Rihanna’s one-word response to the video (“Damn”) was originally interpreted as shade, garnering thousands of likes, her new comments reveal she just felt bad for the awkward moment EJ found themself in.

Besides, these days, Rihanna—mom to sons RZA, 4, Riot, 3, and daughter Rocki, 14 months, with A$AP Rocky—has been busy since she's been back in the studio, working on her first album in a decade.

It was A$AP who pulled back the proverbial curtain, revealing on The Jason Lee Show in August, “She in the studio right now.”.

But even in the moments he remarked it, the rapper knew he’d just opened a massive can of worms.

“Yeah, I remarked it. Sorry, babe,” he joked. “Like, bro, she working. She cooking, no funny s--t.”.

He then quipped, “Damn, I’ma get in trouble for this.”.
