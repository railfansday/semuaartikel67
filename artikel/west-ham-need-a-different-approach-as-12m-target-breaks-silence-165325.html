FC Midtjylland winger Mikel Gogorza has confirmed that West Ham United submitted a concrete transfer offer for him during the summer window. The 20-year-old has also revealed that playing in England remains his dream, despite rejecting the opportunity to join the Hammers.

Speaking to Danish publication Bold following Midtjylland’s 2-0 Conference League victory over Pafos last night, Gogorza explained that the offer arrived whilst he was recovering from a groin injury. According to an English translation of the interview published by Sport Witness, West Ham’s bid was worth approximately £12 million.

Recent findings suggest that gogorza revealed that both he and his club felt it was crucial for him to spend another season in Denmark following his injury. With the January transfer window approaching and Scott Parker’s team struggling for attacking inspiration, it’ll be interesting to see whether the Hammers revisit any of their summer transfer targets.

I’d certainly be hoping that West Ham wouldn’t be revisiting their interest in Gogorza, primarily because I don’t believe him to be an upgrade on what we have. He certainly doesn’t appear to be worth a £18 million gamble which is the number presently being quoted.

In a recent development, look, the jury is still out on Edvin Austbø, who appears to be a promising player. But I’m firmly of the opinion that West Ham do need another winger, ideally one who’s versatile enough to play on both sides.

It would be my preference to have somebody with experience of English football, and I’m intrigued to find out where Nils Koppen has been focusing his interest.

It’s worth remembering that last summer, as Claret & Hugh exclusively revealed at the time, the Hammers hierarchy hired Ben Chorley to assist with identifying Championship-based players.
