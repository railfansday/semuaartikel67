Mikel Arteta has clamped down on Arsenal stars' Fantasy Premier League (FPL) exploits after 'Tzatziki FC', Christos Tzolis' team, 'leaked' team news.

The Arsenal manager admitted he has 'taken measures' to ensure opponents are not given an advantage by his players' selections on the popular game.

Tzolis picked himself as captain of his FPL side prior to the Gunners' 1-0 victory over Liverpool, a clear indication that he would start after recently coming back from injury.

Arteta then caught wind of the situation and Tzolis removed all Arsenal players from his fantasy team for their next game against Hull.

X account FPL Focal identified 'Tzatziki FC' as the Greek winger's team and screenshots of his teams from both game weeks showed how his picks changed.

Recent findings suggest that on Friday, speaking about this glitch, Arteta remarked: 'With Christos, with the squad and with my kids as well. Constantly they are asking me about line-ups as well, because they want to know.

Christos Tzolis picked himself in Fantasy Premier League before starting the Liverpool game.

Tzolis' team, seen here before the Liverpool clash, is called 'Tzatziki FC' and was identified as his side by X account FPL Focal.

Sources indicate that 'It’s a topic, you know. These are the kind of things that are happening in our society right now and it’s something upcoming.

'We haven’t dealt with it [before] and we need to put newly introduced things in place. Obviously confidentiality is something critical because it’s about trust and about what you are trying to do and that should be kept as a huge secret. We’ll take measures to make sure it doesn’t happen.'.

Arteta then confirmed that those measures had already been taken.

Tzolis is a known obsessive of the game, which puts him among 13 million other players who participate every year.

He even quipped after providing an assist against Coventry earlier in the season: 'I will take the three points', referring to the FPL score for doing so.

Chelsea had a similar matter this season, with their players indicating that Joao Pedro was out injured, removing the Brazilian from their FPL teams before his absence was confirmed by the club.

Teams are taking the matter seriously and are keen to ensure any possible advantage their opponents might have regarding team news is nipped in the bud.

Five years ago, Aston Villa stopped their players from participating in FPL after a number of them took Jack Grealish out of their line-ups, notifying eagle-eyed competitors that he was injured.

Mikel Arteta has cracked down on FPL use and 'taken measures' to ensure there are no leaks.

The potential impact of players joining the global GTA 6 mania is another concern for Arteta, with a number of top-flight players already sharing how gripped they will be by the newly introduced game.

Bryan Mbeumo posted the loading screen at midnight on Thursday, indicating that he was having a late-night gaming session just days before the Liverpool clash, while Alexander Isak has noted he will be hunkering down to play the game.

Declan Rice has previously spoken about how he often games into the early hours after matches when adrenaline is still coursing through him.

And his manager noted: 'We’ll try to find out how much exposure we have to these kinds of games and make sure it’s the right one to be able to perform and focus on what we have to do.'.
