AC Milan defender Strahinja Pavlovic will partake in further medical tests tomorrow, according to a report, to assess the problem with his kidney stone.

It’s been a tough season for Pavlovic where injuries are concerned. He struggled through the first international break with fatigue and he’s since had a month-long issue with his kidney, which has kept him out of action.

Barring that, Ruben Amorim has looked to him whenever possible. So the Serbian’s absence is a major loss for the Portuguese, who has also taken to a liking to him, as Massimiliano Allegri did before him.

According to Gazzetta dello Sport, via MilanXtra on X, the Serbian defender will head for further medical tests tomorrow at La Madonnina – where medicals for newly introduced signings are normally held – to assess the issues with his kidney stones.

The problem has sidelined him throughout the past month, so it’s something that must be looked at further to avoid it becoming a recurring issue. Any outcome could also be key for the next few months, too.

Last night, Amorim mentioned Pavlovic when questioned about Goncalo Inacio. So, if the defender is to be out for much longer, he may have to revisit his own comments….
