📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lamina1 CEO and co-founder Rebecca Barkin has exited her post leading the AI and blockchain-based IP management platform alongside author Neal Stephenson to join live experiences manufacturer Tait as chief executive of product and platform technology.

In this fresh role, Barkin will lead the strategy, portfolio direction, and investment priorities for Tait’s technology products and platforms, per the company. She will focus on connecting hardware and software capabilities, developing technologies that can scale across projects and markets, and expanding what clients can imagine and achieve. Most recently, co-founder and CEO of Lamina1, an AI and blockchain-based platform developed in partnership with Stephenson to offer creators a new way to manage IP, Barkin previously held leadership roles at Madison Square Garden Sphere and Magic Leap.

“TAIT has built its reputation on solving complex challenges and delivering experiences that few otherscan,” Tait CEO Adam Davis said. “Our teams have developed extraordinary technical capabilities through years of innovation and problem-solving for our clients. Rebecca brings the product leadership, technology expertise, and commercial perspective to help us build on that foundation, develop more connected and scalable platforms, and bring the value of TAIT technology to more clients and markets.”.

In a recent development, “If you work in immersive or live entertainment, you know TAIT as the firm you call toachieve the impossible; from design to engineering and production, the combination of their deep experience, credibility, and broad capabilities is unmatched,” Barkin said. “In the past, I’ve had the pleasure of partnering with TAIT, and now I have the privilege of steering the vision for our suite of technology products as they support the greatest storytellers of our time.”.
