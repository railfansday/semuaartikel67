Inzaghi: ‘One of the greatest nights of my career in Athens, didn’t sleep for 10 days’

Milan are in Athens ahead of their crucial Europa League match against Olympiacos on Thursday evening, and Pippo Inzaghi has looked back on his special memories from Milan’s 2007 Champions League final victory in the Greek capital: ‘One of the greatest nights of my career’.

Milan have picked up just three points from their first four matches in the Europa League this season and are now in need of points heading into their match against Olympiacos, who are unbeaten on 10 points in the competition thus far.

Inzaghi, speaking in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport, has urged the Rossoneri players to go about their game with courage and personality, and also recalled some of his memories from Milan’s 2-1 victory over Liverpool in the Champions League final in Athens back in 2007, where he scored both of his side’s goals.

Industry observers note that “I had one of the greatest nights of my career in Athens. It’s a city I associate with special memories and for that reason, it will always hold a special place in my heart,” Inzaghi told La Gazzetta dello Sport.

Recalling his memories around that final, Inzaghi said: “I remember the wait for the game, I knew how important that final was and I felt that it could be a special evening.”.

During  the game, he remembers: “The second goal the best, the moment I realised the trophy was really close.”.

And after the final whistle, he mentioned: “I didn’t sleep for 10 days, I woke up in the middle of the night thinking that the final and my goals had been a dream.”.

Inzaghi says that it is impossible to compare the feeling of winning the World Cup and the Champions League, though.

“Those are two different memories, because both enriched my football career. A World Cup victory is the highest honour for any footballer. Athens, however, had a special meaning for Milan’s history. We came off a disappointing final, which I watched from the stands, but this time around I was on the pitch and able to contribute to the victory.

It has been revealed that “Fate gave me a second chance and it went the way we now know it did. However, I can’t rank those two glorious nights because those achievements each mean something different to me.”.

Inzaghi still enjoys a special relationship with the Milan supporters.

In a recent development, he explained: “I spent some extraordinary years at Milan: The bond with the fans was born out of goals and victories, but also from the commitment I always tried to give. Knowing that all this has survived the test of time means an awful lot to me.”.

According to recent reports, lastly, turning his attention to Thursday night’s game against Olympiacos, Inzaghi stated: “I hope they face the match with a lot of personality, and return to home enjoying a great European evening. I know how much that shirt matters and how much the fans expect in a game like this. I hope the team can bring them joy and a victory.”.
