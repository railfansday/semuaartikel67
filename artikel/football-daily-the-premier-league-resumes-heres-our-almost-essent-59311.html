Just like taking the Manchester United job or listening in on a conversation with Stephen Warnock, the men’s international break can be a long, hard slog, a domestic hiatus so long that we can forget both basic hygiene and how the clubs are faring in the Best League in the World™: the Championship the Premier League. So after desperately trying to break into either tears or just a smile at Harry Kane’s appearance record – no dice, unfortunately – here is a rundown on the state of play for each club, as we prepare for a weekend of football that might actually make us feel something.

Industry observers note that 1 Arsenal There are some genuinely likable things about the Gunners – Bukayo Saka, Paul Merson being a bit giddy, Ben White’s apathy to it all – but Arsenal fans were a lot easier to manage when they weren’t this good. It’s theirs to lose, etc.

2 Brighton Turns out all you need to turn your club into genuine title contenders is a world-class recruitment programme and Pascal Gross to accidentally realise that he’s the second coming aged 35.

3 Manchester City Enjoy it while it lasts, lads.

4 Liverpool These lot haven’t been title contenders since Mohamed Salah started playing like Robert Snodgrass and haven’t looked like vastly improving since replacing the right-winger with various different, expensive left-wingers.

5 Chelsea Anything could and probably will happen with the Blues, and that’s not a compliment.

6 Everton The Toffees should be worse than last season – Iliman Ndiaye has left, Jack Grealish issued a grovelling apology after going awol, their only striker is Thierno Barry – but instead they are much, much better. David Moyes, the genius grandpa of the league at 63, will set a newly introduced Everton record for games managed this weekend (593).

It has been revealed that 7 Bournemouth The Dorset Hydra from Greek mythology. Every time you cut off its head, or sell a defender to PSG or Real Madrid, they come back stronger. Since Alex Scott was injured on 3 October, Bournemouth lost at Chelsea and then won eight on the bounce. Make it make sense.

8 Brentford This underdog narrative is wearing a little thin now. Brentford are just a well-oiled football machine but they have no mates and worse, no enemies. What a sad little life. Enjoy Tin Pot qualification. We hope it makes you very happy.

In a recent development, 9 Leeds The Yorkshire club might be 78% Dominic Calvert-Lewin at this point, so only Lucas Radebe knows where they will be when the striker gets knacked, but Daniel Farke is doing a fine job with Leeds in the top half. For now.

10 Aston Villa It would be hard for any club to lose Morgan Rogers, Ezri Konsa, Ollie Watkins, Youri Tieleman and Emi Martínez in one transfer window and improve. Not much more to say. Why do we like Zion Suzuki so much? Genuinely not sure.

11 Tottenham Whisper it, but are Spurs back (to being a mid-table team again)?

12 Newcastle One win in six and a kit that looks like a barcode means it’s been a poor campaign thus far, which Matthias Jaissle and co will hope to resurrect this weekend against – checks notes – the champions. Ah.

13 Hull City A remarkable achievement that the promoted side are not anywhere near the bottom three considering there is absolutely nobody, including Sergej Jakirovic, who can name 11 current Hull players off the top of their heads.

14 Ipswich Town Shining bright like Gary O’Neil’s teeth. Keep this up and they might even survive.

Industry observers note that 15 Fulham Quick tip for Álvaro Arbeloa: when you noted on Friday that “Fulham is not a jail – everyone that is here is because they want to be here”, it really makes it sound like Fulham is actually a jail, which nobody wants to be in. Roll on Manchester City (A)!

16 Nottingham Forest It’s entirely believable that Forest will follow up their 3-1 win over Manchester City with victory at Bournemouth this weekend. It’s also entirely believable that Oliver Glasner will be sacked by Christmas and Forest will be playing in the Championship next season. It’s all part of the Evangelos Marinakis Experience, baby.

Recent findings suggest that 17 Coventry City Genuinely quite nice to see that sky blue kit back in the top flight. All Cov fans would snap your hand off for 17th come the end of the campaign.

18 Manchester United Mauricio Pochettino has the chance to do something very, very funny over the next few months.

19 Sunderland Turns out qualifying for Europe isn’t all it’s made up to be.

20 Crystal Palace The Eagles are surely too good to go down? Their trip to Coventry this weekend absolutely stinks of a six-pointer. Hard not to believe that Thomas Frank’s lovely locks will sort out the mess in south London. Imagine if Adam Wharton had Thomas Frank’s hair? OK, that’s enough Football Daily for today.
