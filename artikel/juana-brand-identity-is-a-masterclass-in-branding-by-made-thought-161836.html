Made Thought builds the Juana brand identity on acid lime and amber, a CBD skincare system where dense label type meets macro botanical light.

Juana is a CBD-powered skincare brand, and Made Thought frames it as half family tradition, half skincare innovation. Founder Yann Moujawaz comes from a line of surgeons and Syrian olive grove stewards, and his formulations aim to switch on the skin's own renewal with expert precision. The design answers a market full of noise and false claims with something that looks like proof: warm amber glass, clinical label density, and botanical photography shot like evidence. The palette does the arguing. Acid lime cuts against deep olive and burnt orange, and the amber packs glow against it all like samples held up to the light.

The wordmark is a compact grotesque JUANA in capitals, set small and quiet on every pack, letting the label copy do the talking. On the lime tubes, ingredient lists run down the barrel in tight bilingual columns, English beside French, with the CBD strength called out large: 100mg on the cleanser, 300mg on the face cream, plus vitamin E, jojoba oil, and hyaluronic acid in the same breath.

At poster scale the same type goes loud. Nurture Facial Cleanser, 100mg CBD fills the frame in acid lime, underlined, with the pack leaning into the shot. The web panel pairs a flat lime block reading Full Spectrum Cannabinoids with a hand dipping into a jar, and a line-drawn diagram of the whole range sits on olive like a spec sheet. The system holds together because the grid never changes, only the scale does.

Industry observers note that the materials carry the science half of the story. Amber glass keeps the formulas away from the light, frosted lime plastic softens the tubes, and matte black caps close every silhouette. White outer cartons stack in a stepped row with a single lime band along the top edge, the only colour the box allows itself. Macro shots push closer still: a pipette suspended in fortifying face oil, bubbles rising through amber, and a leaf photographed so tight its veins turn into a map of yellow, green, and blue.

Recent findings suggest that founder Yann Moujawaz calls the partnership magic, saying passion was generously poured into it, and the photography backs him up. Nothing here is styled to look expensive for its own sake. The glow comes from the product itself, amber oil against orange light, and the identity lets it show.
