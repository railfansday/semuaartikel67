Amazon's Black Friday sale has officially kicked off, which can only mean one thing — Kindles are finally on sale.

Industry observers note that although Amazon just dropped a whole newly introduced collection of redesigned Kindles, the best savings are on 2024 Kindle models. But don't be put off by the previous generation; we like the 2024 models just as much as the 2026 models, and for the Kindle Paperwhite, we even prefer the 2024 model.

It has been revealed that right now, you can save up to $200 on Kindles, including the 2025 Scribe models. Not to mention, the Kindle Paperwhite just got a very good price cut — even better than its Prime Big Deal Days price.

So if you're ready for a new Kindle, you can start shopping before Black Friday with these deals.

The very best deal on Kindles is on the 2024 Amazon Kindle Paperwhite. Ahead of Black Friday, it's down to $129.99, saving you $70 off its list price of $199.99. This sale price also brings it below its previous list price before Amazon raised prices earlier this year. It's also one of the few Kindles to be cheaper on Black Friday than on October Prime Day.
