The Game Awards 2026 nominees have been revealed, and Grand Theft Auto 6 leads the pack with eight nominations, two days before it even launches.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar's crime epic is up for Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, and Best Action/Adventure Game. Its eighth nomination is Manni L. Perez's Best Performance nod for her role as Lucia, which is how we found out who plays her in the first place.

For Game of the Year, GTA 6 is up against 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Nintendo is the most-nominated publisher, with 12 nominations across its games, followed by Capcom with 11, and Annapurna Interactive and PlayStation with nine each.

In a recent development, the nominees were picked by a jury of more than 160 media and creator outlets, who may get advance or pre-release access to games - which explains how GTA 6 made the cut before launch. The winners will be revealed on Thursday, 10th December, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. Fans can vote on The Game Awards' website until 6pm PT on 9th December.
