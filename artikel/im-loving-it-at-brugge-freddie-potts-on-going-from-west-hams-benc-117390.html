Midfielder is preparing to face Liverpool after leaving the club he loves and overcoming a tough start in Belgium.

According to recent reports, in April Freddie Potts was absorbing the disappointment of being substituted at half-time during West Ham’s FA Cup quarter-final defeat by Leeds. The midfielder was out of favour under Nuno Espírito Santo and could not have imagined that he would be preparing to face Liverpool in the Champions League seven months later.

It is the 23-year-old’s reward for taking the difficult decision to leave his boyhood club last summer. Potts noticed his minutes were drying up in pre-season, even though West Ham were not exactly stocked with talent after relegation, and he was ready for a fresh challenge when Club Brugge offered about £10m to sign him.
