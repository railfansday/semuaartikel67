The Damallsvenskan once mastered Europe and attracted the best players, but many are now worried for its future.

Industry observers note that on 12 October 2003, Sweden stood still. The women’s football team were playing Germany in the World Cup final. TV4 set a new viewing record as almost half the nation watched the game go into extra time, where Nia Künzer scored the golden goal winner after 98 minutes.

Recent findings suggest that it was a bitter pill to swallow but there was still the belief that the tournament would mark the start of women’s football’s commercial breakthrough in Sweden. After all, how could sponsors and broadcasters ignore such undeniable interest?
