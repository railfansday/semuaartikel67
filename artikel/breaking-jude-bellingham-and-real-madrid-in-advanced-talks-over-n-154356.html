Jude Bellingham and Real Madrid are advancing in talks over a new contract that will make him one of the best paid players in Europe.

Madrid have been eager to tie down the in-form England midfielder beyond his current contract, understood to be worth around £12.5million a year after tax, which runs until 2029, in recognition of his performances.

Talks were initiated in September over an extension until 2031 and have been progressing well since with sources at Madrid confident they are close to reaching a conclusion.

Bellingham is expected to be handed improved terms to put him on par with team-mate Vinicius Jr, who agreed a new deal last summer reputedly on around £500,000 a week after tax.

Sources indicate that kylian Mbappe remains the best paid player at the club while talks are also advancing over a new contract for Fede Valverde. Real have also recently renewed the contracts of Turkish winger Arda Guler and France international Aurelien Tchouameni on deals until 2032.

The contract for Bellingham will be just reward for the 23-year-old who has flourished under coach Jose Mourinho who recently described the former Birmingham City academy graduate as ‘one of a kind’.
