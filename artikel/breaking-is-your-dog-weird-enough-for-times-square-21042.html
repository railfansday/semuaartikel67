According to recent reports, genetics can contribute, too. Inherited tendencies may influence a dog's motivation to chase, retrieve, explore, or seek food. But a predisposition doesn't guarantee a particular behavior, nor does it explain every individual habit. A dog's behavior comes from a combination of biology and learned experience, which is often less convenient than blaming everything on their breed, but it is much closer to reality.

A 2022 study published in Science looked at the behavior and DNA of thousands of dogs. Researchers found that a dog's breed explained only about 9% of the differences in behavior between individual dogs. In other words, dogs of the same breed can have very different personalities and behaviors. Genetics plays a role, but it doesn't tell the whole story.

In a recent development, that doesn't mean genetics is irrelevant. The researchers found evidence that many behavioral traits do have a heritable component. Rather, the findings show why breed alone is a poor crystal ball for predicting how an individual dog will behave.
