Generative media platform each::labs has raised a $7M seed round, the firm confirmed today, with Revo Capital and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) co-leading the investment.

Sources indicate that the San Francisco startup, founded in June 2024 by engineers from Türkiye, notes monthly revenue grew about fivefold between August and October, to roughly $2M. That puts its annualised run rate above $20mn, built on less than $2mn of earlier funding and a team of about 20 people.

According to recent reports, its platform puts over 600 outside models for images, video, and audio behind one API, next to models each::labs trains itself. Customers range from production studios and online retailers to consumer AI apps and ad platforms. More than 750 paid customers used the service in October, according to the company.

The growth followed a July rebuild aimed at reliability, and the firm states that when it replayed old customer traffic through the new system, the success rate went from 56% to 100%. Core requests also got up to 25 times faster, and a failed generation now falls back to another model on its own.

Usage then surged in September, as consumer AI apps moved into video, and the platform handled around a million daily runs in October, each::labs remarked. Character swapping became one of its heaviest workloads, prompting the team to build Lockstep, a model that keeps a clip’s motion while replacing who appears in it.

Lockstep debuted on 28 October at $0.09 per second of 720p video. The company indicates that is about a quarter of what Higgsfield’s rival Genjutsu Motion Transfer costs for the same resolution on its own marketplace. Ad firm Wask, meanwhile, uses the platform for its AI creative feature, generating over 1,000 ad creatives a minute at peak.

“Before generative media, if you couldn’t draw, you couldn’t show anyone what was in your head. Now you can, but only after fighting the tool for it,” stated Eftal Yurtseven, co-founder and CEO of each::labs. “We exist to close that gap. We build the layer above the models, and when the model our customers need doesn’t exist, we build it.”.

It has been revealed that yurtseven ran the data platform team at grocery delivery firm Getir before starting the tech giant with a fellow ex-Insider engineer. In October, each::labs also opened each::studio, a San Francisco space where film, VFX, and marketing teams use generative media without writing code.

Revo Capital, Türkiye’s largest early-stage fund, first invested in the startup in September 2025, and the EBRD anchors Revo’s $100mn third fund.

Industry observers note that the new capital will go towards more in-house models, sales and marketing hires in San Francisco, and a bigger each::studio. Its first outside round, in February 2025, was led by Right Side Capital.
