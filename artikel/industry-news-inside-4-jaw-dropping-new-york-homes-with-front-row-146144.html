This Thanksgiving marks the 100th anniversary of the annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, and while the weather forced the parade to be postponed for the first time in its history, thousands are still expected to cram the streets of Manhattan to watch the iconic event on its rescheduled date, Saturday.

But imagine having a front-row seat without ever leaving home?

There are plenty of apartments, condos, and penthouses that line the parade route that runs along Central Park, and we found four active listings that would allow you to watch the parade year after year without ever stepping outside.

Why it’s here: Residence 7C sits along Central Park West on West 75th Street, putting the parade right outside its windows.

“With over 45 feet of frontage along the parade route, balloons floating right past the windows, and sweeping southeast views, it's the ultimate vantage point for the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade,” explained listing agent Jessica Svensson of Douglas Elliman.

The 3,000-square-foot unit is distinguished by its grand prewar proportions, including a nearly 30-foot great room, a 21-foot formal dining room, and 10-foot ceilings.

Its gracious floor strategy separates the entertaining spaces from the private bedroom wing, while the corner positioning offers sweeping views east across Central Park and south toward the Manhattan skyline and Billionaires' Row.

Other noteworthy features include a primary suite with western exposure, a flexible library or home office, an eat-in kitchen, central AC, a built-in bar with refrigeration, and a separate laundry room.

Jessica Svensson of Douglas Elliman is the listing agent.

Why it’s here: Thanks to the building’s 1931 column-free corner design, Units 5Q and 4P offer unobstructed northern views up Central Park West, ideal for watching the Thanksgiving Day Parade.

“For years three generations have gathered to watch the parade in this special home,” remarked listing agent Nealy Nusbaum Erber of Christie's International Real Estate Fresh York.

The 3,000+ square foot apartment offers a truly magical sense of living among the treetops, with beautiful park views from all of the major entertaining rooms. The flexible floor plan allows for a wonderful separation between the living and sleeping quarters, offering both gracious entertaining spaces and privacy.

Standout building amenities include a state-of-the-art fitness center, golf simulator, Pilates room, children’s playroom, residents’ lounge, private work suites, and a pet washing station.

Nealy Nusbaum Erber of Christie's International Real Estate New York is the listing agent.

My husband, an amateur yet published photographer, has shot pictures of the parade on many occasions from those front windows,” explained listing agent Barbara Fox of Brown Harris.

This unique combination of two apartments comprises approximately 4,000 square feet in a top Central Park West building. Located between 65th and 66th Streets in the bustling Lincoln Center neighborhood, there’s no shortage of fine dining, gourmet food shops, wonderful shopping, and the serenity of Central Park just across the street.

Barbara Fox of Brown Harris Stevens is the listing agent.
