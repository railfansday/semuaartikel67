📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you’re looking for a bag so versatile it can be your go-to personal item one day and your grocery shopping companion the next, the renowned tote bag is the only option. From early morning flights to daily office commutes, or even a packed Saturday afternoon at the farmers market, our editors are always relying on their favorite tote. The best tote bags hold everything from our laptops, headphones, and water bottles to sleep masks and our most-used beauty products. Whether you reach for a structured leather version to pack your essential items in or an oversized, canvas carryall for a day at the park, the functions of the right tote are infinite.

It has been revealed that though the design of a tote bag is inherently simple, our favorite brands have curated precise variations to suit every kind of traveler. My favorite tote has been my everyday work bag for years, but it has also accompanied me on countless flights, weekend getaways, and last-minute happy hours. A reliable tote bag really can do it all. Keep reading to discover 15 of our editors' favorite tote bags and why we can’t live without them.

In a recent development, coach has been a favorite brand of mine ever since I got my first little black bag as a teenager. Though I still cherish that tiny bag, my most used Coach item is my leather tote. For years, the Nina Tote bag has been my reliable sidekick. I’ve had it for so long that Coach no longer produces it. On the bright side, the Maggie Tote bag is almost identical to mine and is just as practical. I have put my bag through anything and everything over the years, and there is very little wear and tear. The leather is structured and durable, yet flexible enough to fit everything I need without toppling over. With plenty of interior pockets, my necessities all have a place, so I never have to go digging through the bottom of my bag to find the right lip gloss. Not only do I love traveling with this tote, but it’s also my everyday work bag and my go-to for almost every kind of activity.

I've never really had a work bag that I truly loved until I tried this one from Cuyana. What I appreciate about the System Tote are the additional organizational accessories that you can purchase to add a bit more structure to the interior. Aside from lugging it to and from the office each day, I use this tote as a personal item on the plane and brought it with me on a road trip to Acadia National Park, and have found it to be the ultimate travel companion. The built-in interior pocket is perfect for storing essentials you need to reach easily, like your passport, earbuds, or some petty cash, and since the bag can be snapped closed, you don't have to worry about anything flying out. —Jamie Spain, travel bookings editor.

This bag is all about the pockets. No tote has ever kept me quite as organized as this one from MZ Wallace, a brand known for its signature quilted bags. It offers six interior pockets, five exterior pockets, and a detachable pouch, offering me a dedicated space for all of my essentials plus a whole lot more. I’m always impressed at how much this thing fits; an umbrella, my 13-inch laptop, a full change of clothes, and my lunch. The Medium Metro tote features ultra-soft shoulder straps, making it a go-to for commutes to the office. My favorite thing about this bag is how lightweight it feels even when full—it never digs into my shoulders or hurts my arms. More features that make this roomy tote a smart choice for travelers: a luggage sleeve, protective feet, and a detachable and adjustable crossbody strap. —Meaghan Kenny, commerce editor.

Monos’s Metro Tote has been with me longer than most New Yorkers are in relationships. I’ve had this faux-leather cutie for nearly five years now, and it’s traveled with me through new jobs, new apartments, and plenty of new adventures. I actually bring it to work with me every day—the laptop sleeve and spacious interior keep me organized on my way to and from the office. —Hannah Chubb, senior commerce editor.

Recent findings suggest that I've received so many compliments on this gorgeous structured bag from Cuyana—it's been a great work bag, with plenty of space for my laptop, planner, Kindle, chargers, and other day-to-day essentials. I wasn't sure how well it would function as a personal item as there's not a zipper to fully close the bag, but on a flight to Aruba, I made use of the leather ties to keep items contained, and found it well-sized to slip under the seat in front of me, even with my toiletry bag and a sweatshirt stuffed inside. I'm also a significant fan of the snap-to-close key leash, and the interior slips pockets and zipper pocket. —Madison Flager, commerce director.

It has been revealed that the bag is perfect in the way a red apple sitting on a teacher’s desk is perfect. It’s classic, without ornament, the kind of thing that expresses its utility in its design. It’s both ubiquitous and aspirational, stirring up images of a dreamy East Coast: a white-sand, Nantucket beach; a shabby, wood-planked boathouse on the coast of Maine; a Gothic-inspired Ivy League campus. I've used it as a carry-on item, a beach tote, and as a weekender. Simply having it out on display in your home has the power to imbue your space with a charming, outdoorsy vibe. —Jessica Sulima, commerce writer.

Baggu’s Nylon shoulder bag is more durable than its canvas tote bags but equally as stylish. It’s slightly slouchy, slightly structured, and comes in the signature Baggu colors and patterns. The adjustable quilted shoulder strap allows for a crossbody fit, a must-have for me when I’m commuting into the office or walking longer distances (nobody needs their laptop sliding off their shoulder onto the sidewalk). The zip closure and interior pockets are handy for organizing miscellaneous goodies and preventing spillage. Plus, the lightweight material can easily be flattened to fit into my suitcase. I find myself continuously reaching for this multipurpose tote on my way to the gym, office, farmers market, and airport. —Hannah Towey, associate editor.

Just when I think I’ve run out of room, I haven’t. The expandable bottom of this Calpak laptop tote adds an extra four inches, allowing me to add last-minute essentials or souvenirs mid-trip. Like all products in the brand’s Luka collection, it’s made of a lightweight, ultra-soft, puffy exterior that makes carrying its handle easy. The material is also easy to clean—simply wipe away dirt or spills without a worry. Inside, there’s a padded 15" laptop sleeve, multiple side pockets, and a zippered section to keep smaller items organized. Another feature I love: There’s a zippered pocket on the outside that keeps my phone or passport secure, while still being easy to access. It’s a great tote for commuters and thanks to its luggage trolley sleeve, an ideal carry-on bag for travelers. —M.K.

As a shopping writer who is constantly running between the office, press previews, catch ups with friends and, of course, the airport, I need a bag with space for all the day’s exploits and then some. July’s Juliette Soft Work Tote is my perfect do-it-all companion. It’s beautiful—I’ve received multiple compliments on this bag as I’ve been whizzing around with it, and the divided interior is really clever. There’s a 14" laptop sleeve to keep tech protected—all the more crucial when you’re headed on a flight— and there’s also a dedicated water bottle holder too. It has a wide bottom so your lunchbox can go inside flat, plus a key leash to stop the rummage for your house keys. Brushed gold hardware and a buttery-soft faux leather exterior mean it looks effortlessly chic at after-work drinks, and when I'm off to the airport, the removable trolley sleeve slides over my suitcase handle so I can breeze through the terminal. It is my favorite desk-to-departure tote bag. —Charley Ward, commerce writer, Condé Nast Traveller UK.

My Strathberry Kite is my most-used travel accessory. Not only do I use it for day-to-day tasks, it comes with me on almost every trip (the exception being adventure travel), and most recently carried me through trips to Lisbon and Istanbul, where I spent weekends catching up with friends and sightseeing. It's an ideal size for a laptop, should you wish to take it, but fits in plenty of other necessary items, too (like flip-flops or ballet flats after hours of strolling through cobbled streets). I love the walnut color and the soft, supple leather. I'm sure this will be a favorite of mine for years to come. —Abigail Malbon, global audience development manager.

I never travel without animal print, and there was something about this colorway that felt perfectly understated yet maximalist. I found it online about a year ago, and it sold like hot cakes—and has been (devastatingly) sold out since. I received it as a sweet gift from my partner a few months ago, who had pre-ordered it to arrive right when it was back in stock. I've been using it as my go-to work bag ever since. It has an incredible amount of space and strength to carry all of my everyday essentials and then some—from my laptop and a book to makeup and toiletries. Because of its exterior fabric as hair-on cowhide, she just needs to be shielded from the rain a bit more than your average bag. If the design is calling your name on this bad boy, it’s well worth the splurge. —Jessica Chapel, commerce writer.

Recent findings suggest that I love anything Longchamp, and this gorgeous beach tote from the brand is my cherry on top of any vacation outfit. It’s large enough to hold a full day’s worth of goodies (sunscreen! sweatshirt! beach read!), but fits on the shoulder with ease. Plus, the toggle on the front keeps things contained in the most Longchamp-esque way. —H.C.

After passing the gauntlet of a last-minute trip to the British Virgin Islands, I always keep the Foldie Travel Bag on me, whether it’s riding in my backpack during my daily commute or stuffed into my suitcase on longer hauls. The front pocket easily held my thick reading list and two Moleskine classic notebooks; the full 40 liters of space in the main compartment (with the expansion unzipped) fit a week’s worth of clothes, chargers for four different devices, and another bag filled with toiletry bottles, plus room to spare for souvenirs. Another perk: the two inner pockets and wet pocket proved useful in sorting out the bag’s increasingly chaotic contents. The luggage sleeve was a relieving bonus that allowed me to transfer the hefty weight of my zealous packing to my suitcase. —Kat Chen, editorial assistant, destinations.

According to recent reports, I absolutely love this tote. Whether I’m wearing it to the office or while traveling, it fits everything I need. Plus, it comes with a separate removable pouch that I use even when I’m not wearing the bag. This slouchy bag is super durable and matches any outfit. —Pooja Nayyar, associate social media manager.

Sentimentality may cloud my vision, for the tote in my possession belongs to my late grandmother. And when I tell you I was complimented frequently for it while in Italy, I mean I felt I needed to use the bag to beat back admirers waxing: “Wow, is Vera Bradley back?”; “Is it reversible?!”; “The textiles are so beautiful.” I often caught myself fantasizing about dumping it upside down to perform a “What’s in my bag” for nobody in particular—so capacious is the tote that I was needlessly carrying multiple novels within and rarely remembered to leave my toiletry kit on the hotel bathroom counter where it belonged. Handy pockets ringing the interior are just icing, and would have kept my passport safely inside had I ever been moved to act on the aforementioned dumping. —Charlie Hobbs, associate editor.
