According to recent reports, when it comes to 'classy' party games, I'd be hard-pressed to think of any. By their very nature, most party games are lighthearted and fun, but that's where Suits & Stallions comes in: a new entry in the genre available now on iOS and Android that leans into the classier side of the equation.

Recent findings suggest that what's the classiest thing you can imagine? Horse racing, of course! And that's what Suits & Stallions is all about, as you race your porcelain model horses across a board in a classily lit museum setting. Each card you draw can move either you, your opponent or both, depending on the corresponding suit, but you can try to nudge the results or whip your chosen stallion into shape for a better chance at success.

According to recent reports, there are also other optional modes, such as Troika, which sees you racing an entire tower of horses rather than one. And the best part is that if you stay playing against the PC, then all of this is free!

Industry observers note that of course, the main appeal of any party game is playing with your friends, and in that regard Suits & Stallions is an interesting example of the genre. Less likely to cause pure chaos, but I can see a devious card draw and decision to send your friend back causing plenty of lighthearted arguments.
