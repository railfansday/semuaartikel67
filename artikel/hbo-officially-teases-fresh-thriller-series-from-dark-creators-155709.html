📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the 2017 German sci-fi mystery thriller series Dark aired for three seasons on Netflix, gaining critical acclaim and a global cult following. Its complex plot explored the paradoxical nature of time travel through strange mysteries in the fictional town of Winden, Germany.

Six years after its finale, series creators Baran bo Odar and Jantje Friese are producing a upcoming mystery thriller, Sonnental. HBO Max Germany released a 30-second teaser for the series, introducing the plot and the protagonist played by Dark star Lisa Vicari.

According to recent reports, sonnental shares Dark's spooky atmosphere and eerie woodland setting, but the new series is a crime mystery rather than sci-fi. The plot follows a series of murders based on Der Struwwelpeter, a German book that features 10 stories about misbehaving children whose actions have terrible consequences. In the teaser, we hear the voice of a child singing the first verse of "The Story of Wicked Frederick," the second story in Der Struwwelpeter.

"Der Friederich, der Friederich,.

This translates to "Frederick, Frederick, he was a terrible tyrant." The tale is about a boy who abuses and tortures animals. When a dog Frederick whips bites him, the child becomes ill and bedridden and the dog eats all his food. HBO indicates the book's twisted tales will become "deadly nightmares" in Sonnental, so Frederick's karmic punishment may be even more sinister. All six episodes of Sonnental will premiere on HBO Max in March 2027.
