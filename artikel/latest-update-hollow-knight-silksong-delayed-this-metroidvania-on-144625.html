Well, after avoiding the sequel's release date, one manufacturer is saying no more, and refuses to shift even at the risk of competing with DLC.

Team Cherry, the outfit behind Hollow Knight and Silksong, has announced Cherry Time, an upcoming livestream that'll contain details on Sea of Sorrow. This has fans incredibly excited, and fellow devs wary of when Sea of Sorrows may come out.

Sources indicate that aeternum Game Studios has had enough, though. "A little over a year ago, just as we were about to launch our game, you announced Silksong," the studio writes in an open letter to Team Cherry.
