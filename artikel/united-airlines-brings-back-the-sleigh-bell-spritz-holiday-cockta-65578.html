📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

United Airlines is bringing back its Sleigh Bell Spritz for the holidays, combining vodka with seasonal cranberry Sprite and a cranberry garnish. It is a simple addition to the beverage cart, but it’s nice to see a bit of seasonal variety onboard.

According to a recent memo to flight attendants, United is gradually reintroducing Sprite Winter Spiced Cranberry during November, with availability continuing through December.

The Sleigh Bell Spritz features:.

The cocktail costs $10 in United Economy and is complimentary in premium cabins. United also mentioned the returning drink in its recent Thanksgiving travel announcement, suggesting a squeeze of lime alongside the vodka, seasonal Sprite, and fresh cranberries.

In a recent development, the internal memo states hub catering locations will supply cranberries for both United First and economy service. Since the rollout is gradual, do not expect every flight to have the ingredients immediately, though I saw recently that the drink is being advertised in the this month’s seatback buy-on-board pamphlet.

While, hardly an elaborate cocktail–an airplane beverage cart has its limitations–and a seasonal mixer is an easy way to offer something different without requiring a flight attendant to become a bartender. The cranberry garnish is a nice touch too…presentation matters, even in economy class, and it’s just a low-hanging fruit, if you’ll pardon the pun, that leave passengers smiling.

I’m not a fan of Sprite (far too sweet), but may try one…for the blog of course.

According to recent reports, the Sleigh Bell Spritz is returning to United Airlines through December, with a gradual rollout during November and a $10 price in economy. If you are flying United over the Christmas holidays and looking for something a bit different to drink, there’s your opportunity.
