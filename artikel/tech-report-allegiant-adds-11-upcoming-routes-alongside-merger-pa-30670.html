Tech Report: Allegiant adds 11 upcoming routes alongside merger partner Sun Country, swaps 5 others

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (153 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Allegiant and Sun Country announced 11 new routes as part of their ongoing integration following a recent merger.
  • Gulf Shores, Alabama, is a major beneficiary, gaining multiple new nonstop flights and expanded service from both airlines.
  • Five routes will switch operating carriers between Allegiant and Sun Country, further blending their networks.
  • Both airlines will continue to operate under separate brands until at least 2028, with a single brand planned for the future.
  • New routes begin launching in April and May 2027, with specific start dates varying by destination and carrier.
  • Some destinations, such as Gulf Shores and Minneapolis-Saint Paul, will see service from both airlines for the first time.
  • Travelers should review which airline operates each route, as carriers are exchanging certain routes starting in April 2027.
