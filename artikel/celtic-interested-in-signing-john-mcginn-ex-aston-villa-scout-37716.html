Celtic 'interested' in signing John McGinn - ex-Aston Villa scout

Celtic would be interested in making a move for John McGinn if he decides to leave Aston Villa and return to Scotland in the coming years.

That is according to former senior Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 32-year-old's current terms expire in 2028, with it yet unclear if he will sign a further extension at Villa Park in the coming months.

McGinn has looked to be in some of the best form of his career over the last two seasons, already having scored three goals and provided five assists so far in the upcoming campaign.

He has also been superb for Scotland, scoring twice in last week's 2-0 win over North Macedonia at Hampden Park.

The midfielder remains Villa's captain and a key part of Unai Emery's team, but will be quietly considering his options ahead of the final years of his career.

McGinn was born in Glasgow and his career began with St Mirren, rather than Celtic or Rangers.

He joined Hibernian in 2015, and would be signed by Villa three years later in a deal worth £2.75million.

McGinn has since revealed that he was desperate to join Celtic, with his grandad having served as the club's chairman from 1986 to 1991.

Sources indicate that "I was at Villa when they signed him in 2018. It was a little bit of a surprise signing from Hibs, to be honest," King told Football Insider.

It has been revealed that "I think he's done very well since he's been at Aston Villa. He's been there eight years. He's 32 now, but he's got a contract until the end of the 27-28 season.

It has been revealed that "Obviously, Everton were interested in him in the summer. Celtic would be interested in him, if he thought about going back to Scotland. He's still got a good couple of years in him, I think.".

Celtic reportedly made three bids in July 2018 to sign McGinn, seeing their final £2m offer turned down before Villa came in.

"If he wasn't my grandpa and I wasn't a Celtic fan, I probably would be a Celtic player," the Villa captain admitted in August this year.

Industry observers note that "They [Celtic] felt because I was a Celtic fan, it would just happen. Nobody else would come in and Hibs would crumble. Hibs were amazing.

"They stated: 'We're not going to stand in your way, but they need to pay us what you're worth.' I gave them my word that I'd wait until they pay what I worth and it never got there.

It has been revealed that "It was two or three million. They actually phoned my grandpa to try to influence me, but he had a good relationship with both sides. He trusted me and my agent and he mentioned: 'It's nothing to do with me, if you've got the money, pay the money.'.

Recent findings suggest that "It went on for months. I met Brendan [Rodgers]. I was desperate to go to Celtic.".
