Recent findings suggest that every day, your faithful, over-caffeinated GQ Recommends editors cover everything from wardrobe essentials to step by step guides on how to find your office fragrance so that you know exactly what to purchase when you're shopping for your everyday items and big-ticket splurges. But, in-between bouts of menswear proselytizing and shilling for Big Gloop we do a lot of shopping for ourselves. And—while we like to think of ourselves as thoughtful consumers—even we smash that add to cart button on a whim a few times a month. That's why we put together this non-exhaustive list of what GQ Recommends editors impulse-bought this month. Here's everything we tossed in our virtual bags against our better judgement.

Sources indicate that am I the only one deeply intrigued by this jacket's above-the-waist crop, capacious pockets, and just-right collar? Someone please investigate and report back. —Avidan Grossman.

I already have a couple of pairs of Oni jeans, which means I don't need any more. That said, I could not help myself when I saw these beauties. While most other jeans use a handful of machines to achieve various kinds of stitches that make the process much faster, the 900HM jeans are made using only a single-needle straight stitch machine, essentially doubling the amount of time it takes to make a single pair. —Gerald Ortiz.

In a recent development, everyone's playing GTA but that doesn't mean I can't reminisce about Red Dead Redemption 2. —Jordan Bowman.

No one breaches the defenses of my wallet as swiftly as MarieBelle. Look at it! It's beautiful! And it tastes like autumn! —Mari Herrema.

In a recent development, expanding my man's color palette, one accessory at a time. —Mari Herrema.

Full disclosure: I didn't impulse buy this recently. I did, however, impulse buy it roughly five years ago, a spur-of-the-moment decision I pat myself on the back for around this time every fall. —Avidan Grossman.

It has been revealed that as long as the temperature refuses to sink below an arbitrary, say, 50-ish degrees, this is the only lightweight layer you need. —Avidan Grossman.

Just some damn good pants. That's all it takes to hit add to cart, sometimes you just know it when you see it. —Jordan Bowman.

Personally, I don't think my dad needs any more socks, but it is a guaranteed home-run of a gift for him. —Mari Herrema.

I'm on a personal quest to find the ideal everyday derbys, which has led me here. —Jordan Bowman.

Recent findings suggest that if you're the type of sicko who swears by shorts year-round, you could do a lot worse than sticking with these. (Also, like, hire me at your startup or whatever.) —Avidan Grossman.

It has been revealed that it's that time of the year when my notebooks start to reach their last leaves. So I went for a very pristine, unadorned notebook made by one of the best notebook manufacturers on the market. —Gerald Ortiz.

The vintage guy in me was pounding through my chest when I saw these rare pants. I've been on the hunt for a good pair of vintage cargos, and the M-43s have always been on my watch list for their unique pockets and herringbone twill fabric, though the right pair never found their way to me. So when this black overdyed pair was listed on eBay, I knew I'd finally found the one. —Gerald Ortiz.

Sources indicate that sometimes I feel like telling my dad that giving his Museum Classic to my Garmin-pilled brother is the silliest thing in the world and that I would actually wear the watch. Other times I remember my wrists are not Man Sized and that I make my own money, too. —Mari Herrema.
