Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Europa League and Conference League, with Milan visiting Olympiacos, Juventus hosting Omonia Nicosia, and Atalanta away to Borac.

We begin at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET) with Milan in Greece hoping to turn their European fortunes around with Olympiacos.

At 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET), Juventus welcome Omonia Nicosia to stay in the top spots, while Atalanta are in Bosnia and Herzegovina against borac.

Maurizio Sarri warns Atalanta ‘cannot always expect to resolve games with these comebacks’ after a 3-2 away to Borac in the Conference League, while Gianluca Scamacca is ‘lacking confidence.’.

Luciano Spalletti admits Andrea Cambiaso’s stunning goal ‘got Juventus back on track’ for a 4-1 Europa League win over Omonia Nicosia, and Jeff Ekhator provides different solutions in attack.

Atalanta are certainly never dull….

Atalanta just do not know when they’re beaten.

Has to be stated that a) they were against 9 men, but b) had missed at least 3 open goals before that.

Atalanta wasted so many chances in Bosnia, but after Borac went down to nine men, completed another incredible comeback to win 3-2 with Nikola Krstovic, Sead Kolasinac and Davide Zappacosta.

Juventus recovered from a Neto howler to beat Omonia Nicosia 4-1 with Jeff Ekhator, Andrea Cambiaso, Jeremie Boga and Francisco Conceicao on target.
