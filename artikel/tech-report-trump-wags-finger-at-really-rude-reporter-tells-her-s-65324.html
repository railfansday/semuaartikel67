President Donald Trump rebuked a female reporter at the end of a White House news conference Friday, wagging his finger at her, calling her "really a rude person" and telling her she belonged in the "back row.".

The exchange unfolded as Trump wrapped up a lengthy news conference following his return from an Asia trip, just after inviting White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller to the podium and praising Miller's work on immigration and deregulation.

Before addressing the press corps, Trump defended Miller's temperament, noting that critics often view him as overly strict on immigration.

After the reporter replied, "Who are you?" Trump sharply criticized both her demeanor and her seating placement in the room.
