Liverpool have been linked with a lot of transfers this season.

Nobody knows exactly what's going to happen in the January window or even the 2027-28 summer window but, change is almost certainly afoot.

Without that, Liverpool will fall even further behind their title rivals and cause themselves more harm than good.

January is a notoriously hard time for clubs to agree deals with teams from across the globe demanding more money than usual due to the restricted time available to replace their players.

However, that doesn't mean it's impossible to get some over the line with one club now trying their luck with a Liverpool star.

Trabzonspor have opened up contract talks with Virgil van Dijk.

One of the main players to have been linked with a Liverpool exit this summer is Virgil van Dijk.

The Dutchman's contract is set to expire in June 2027 and, since Liverpool don't know whether he's going to be able to keep his performance levels this high for another two years, they're waiting to decide whether he's worth another deal or not.

Most fans believe that he is because, on the market right now, there are little to no options available for Liverpool to explore, not ones that wouldn't cost a fortune, anyway.

But, because another two years will obviously be a lot of money for the Reds to commit to, they need to be 100% sure about their decision.

So, thanks to their stalling, media outlets have started to link Van Dijk with a move away from Liverpool with A Spor now sharing an revision on the 35 year old's future.

"Trabzonspor, which caused a stir with the signings of Mohamed Salah , Andre Onana , and Fabinho, is showing no signs of slowing down in the transfer market," they wrote.

"It has been claimed that the Black Sea representative has begun official negotiations for 35-year-old veteran defender Virgil van Dijk, who is in the final year of his contract with Premier League giant Liverpool .

"While it is not yet clear whether the transfer will be finalized during the mid-season break or at the end of the season.".

Liverpool can only let Virgil van Dijk go if they sign a replacement or have one lined up.

Van Dijk has been Liverpool's rock at the back for as long as anyone can remember now.

The Dutchman has been their leader and is one of the main reasons why they've been so successful.

Without signing him in 2017, it feels like the Reds would have been nowhere near as dominant as they were.

Right now, they're trying to get back to that era and, while that's going to be difficult, it's certainly something that they can do.

But, if they sell Van Dijk and don't sign a replacement or at least have one lined up for the summer, they're going to dig themselves into a massive hole.

Nico Schlotterbeck, Jacobo Ramon and even Murillo are all solid options for Liverpool to explore but, if they don't get a deal for at least one newly introduced centre-back done before they allow Van Dijk to leave, some serious questions will need to be asked.

Here's hoping that Julian Ward and Iraola have a blueprint in mind.
