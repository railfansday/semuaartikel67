Season 35 of Dancing With the Stars is about to be over, but first, a new winner must be crowned!

The finale episode of DWTS will see couples compete in three rounds of competition as Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood battle it out for a chance to win the season 35 Len Goodman mirrorball trophy.

Recent findings suggest that first, contestants will dance compete in the judges’ choice round, as each judge selected a dance style for one of the contestants to perform. Second will be the instant dance challenge, which will task them to perform a number without knowing the dance style or the song until only minutes before performing it live. The final performance is the beloved freestyle round, where all rules are out the window, leaving contestants with one final dance to wow the judges and fans.

Industry observers note that for the first time, the DWTS finale is airing live coast-to-coast, allowing fans in any time zone in the U.S., its territories and Canada to vote for the season 35 winner. (The show typically only airs live for the East coast, with voting taking place only during the East coast airing of each show, leaving West coast and Mountain time viewers unable to vote live.).

The couple with the lowest combined total of judges’ scores and viewer votes is typically eliminated at the end of each DWTS episode; the winner is determined by combining the judges’ scores and viewer votes in the finale episode.

Sources indicate that each viewer is allowed up to 10 votes per couple, per voting method (via ABC’s website and by text). Live voting takes place during the live airing of each show and ends shortly after the final performance.
