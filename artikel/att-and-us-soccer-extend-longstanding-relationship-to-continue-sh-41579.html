Industry observers note that as soccer continues to grow across America, AT&T and U.S. Soccer are extending their longstanding partnership, which began in 2009. Through the multiyear renewal, AT&T will continue championing U.S. Soccer and its National Teams through athlete storytelling, fan engagement and long-term investment in the sport.

Industry observers note that the renewal comes as soccer continues to gain momentum across the U.S. As more people play, watch and follow the sport across the country, AT&T and U.S. Soccer will continue creating new ways for fans to connect with the teams, athletes and moments that make soccer meaningful.

In a recent development, AT&T's support of U.S. Soccer also extends to the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center, the new home for all 27 U.S. Soccer National Teams. The facility will bring the Men's and Women's National Teams together at one training location for the first time. As a Founding Partner, AT&T will provide advanced connectivity across the campus, including AT&T Connected Fields, to support how players and coaches prepare, analyze performance and improve their game. Looking ahead, AT&T and U.S. Soccer will explore new ways to connect players with technology and data that can enhance training and performance.

Recent findings suggest that the renewal also gives fans more ways to engage with U.S. Soccer's teams and athletes through content, matchday activations and other programming. Through She's Connected by AT&T, the tech giant will continue putting women athletes in the spotlight, telling their stories and showcasing their impact well beyond the field.

"We've been proud to support U.S. Soccer since 2009, and we believe the sport's greatest opportunities are still ahead," said Sabina Ahmed, assistant vice president of media and sponsorships, AT&T. "As soccer continues to grow, so does its ability to inspire, unite and open doors for people on and off the field. Through this partnership, we're helping more fans connect with the sport they love while supporting the athletes, programs and facilities moving the game forward.".

"Soccer is reaching more people across the country, and partners like AT&T help us turn that interest into lasting fandom," remarked David Wright, Chief Business Officer at U.S. Soccer. "AT&T has been part of U.S. Soccer since 2009, and as a Founding Partner of the Arthur M. Blank U.S. Soccer National Training Center, it is helping create a home for all 27 of our National Teams. We're proud to continue working together to support our athletes, give fans more ways to engage with our teams and grow the game for the next generation.".

"It's an exciting time for U.S. Soccer, and it means a lot to see AT&T continue investing in the sport and our National Teams," said Christian Pulisic, U.S. Men's National Team player. "The newly introduced National Training Center is a great example of what that commitment can enable, bringing our teams together in one place with the connectivity and technology to help us prepare and perform at our best.".
