Florian Wirtz admitted he has been unhappy with some of his performances for Liverpool but he is now enjoying being in his best run of form since joining the club.

The Germany playmaker’s performances have been intensely scrutinised since his £116m move from Bayer Leverkusen two summers ago and, after an underwhelming first season in a campaign where the whole team struggled, he has started to kick into gear.

He has scored five times and contributed two assists in his last four appearances for club and country and the smile is back on his face.

“In the last few games I got a lot of goal contributions and I think you could say that I am in the best form since I arrived,” he said ahead of the Champions League game against Club Brugge in Belgium.

According to recent reports, “To be honest, I cannot really say that I have changed much. I think that I have become a bit better on a physical level, that is the only reason I could explain it.

“But I also started to know better English football and the league and I am feeling very good. I am enjoying my football at Liverpool. I am happy it is going like this at the moment.

According to recent reports, “I have heard a lot of people talking about me smiling or that I don’t smile that much. Maybe that is only because the people don’t really know me. Normally I am a very positive guy.

“It’s just when I am on the pitch I try and bring out my best performances and be as focused as I can be. Maybe that is sometimes why it looks like I don’t smile so much, but I don’t think that is a thing you have to be concerned about.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s mainly a thing that goes on in my own head. It’s not about the people who talk about me. It’s the goals I set myself and the performances I want to show for myself.

Recent findings suggest that “I always believed in myself. I knew my qualities and I know them now.

“Of course, there were days where I wasn’t happy with myself but I still believe it makes no sense to put too much pressure on yourself as that doesn’t help to get to where you want.

“I just believed in myself so I wasn’t too annoyed. But now I can only speak about the time I am having now and I’m really enjoying it.”.

After a run one goal in 17 club appearances stretching back to May, the 23-year-old has scored in back-to-back matches and he created 10 chances in Sunday’s win against Manchester United, including an assist for Victor Munoz’s goal.

Wirtz added: “I would say my priority is that the club and the team is successful, that’s the main thing in my head.

“I know that offensive players get checked by numbers and I’m always trying to use my opportunities when I have them but, in the end, for me it’s just about the success of the team.

According to recent reports, “I’m not going into a game and trying to be the player who has scored the most goals or something. If I see a team-mate in a better position I give them the ball and I hope they also do that with me.”.
