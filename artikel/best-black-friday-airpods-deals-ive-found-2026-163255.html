Whether you want pocketable earbuds for everyday use, premium noise-canceling headphones, or seamless Apple integration, these are some of the best prices I've seen recently. The flagship AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s best AirPods yet. Upgrades include stronger noise-canceling, a upcoming acoustic architecture for deeper bass, and redesigned ear tips for a more secure fit. The ear tips also keep my AirPods from slipping during longer runs and sweatier workouts. New tools include a built-in heart rate sensor for fitness tracking, live translation, and a camera remote to snap photos or videos on your iPhone. These earbuds are also the first AirPods to be IP57 rated against dust, sweat, and rain.

Recent findings suggest that at the time of writing, they are 40 percent off at Amazon, Best Buy, Target, and Walmart. This is the lowest price I’ve ever seen them drop to. In other words, I’d grab a pair now if they’re on your wish list.

It has been revealed that the AirPods Max 2’s newer H2 chip improves active noise cancellation and transparency mode and enables a suite of intelligent features, including conversation awareness, live translation, and improved Siri interactions. The AirPods Max 2 are designed with a latest high-dynamic-range amplifier for deeper bass, more natural vocals, and cleaner highs. The ear cuffs, while plush and comfortable for longer listening sessions, do trap some heat, but I have yet to find a pair of over-ear headphones that don’t.

At $549, the AirPods Max 2 can be a tough sell, especially with so many excellent, more affordable alternatives on the market. But at $130 off, they’re a lot easier to justify, especially if you want the seamless integration that comes with Apple’s ecosystem.
