In the summer, AC Milan included a buy option in the loan deal to bring Omari Hutchinson to San Siro, but what would be the cost?

According to recent reports, speaking in recent days, Hutchinson has admitted that a move to San Siro, on a permanent basis, would be a dream and it’s entirely possible that it’s fulfilled. Milan are looking to raise the bar of the squad and whilst maybe not a starter level player, he has the potential to grow substantially.

Industry observers note that glimpses of an impressive player have been seen in recent months, so it’s not an overlookable investment. However, the price must be right, especially with other significant investments planned.

With the recent comments, Calciomercato.com have looked into the loan structure and it’s unfortunately not promising. The loan fee in itself was about €4 million, which isn’t a high cost.

Recent findings suggest that however, to redeem him on a permanent basis, the Rossoneri would be looking at a €50m clause, which is simply unfeasible at this moment. Though it’s worth noting that this figure could potentially be renegotiated.

Right now, it’s unlikely that the Diavolo would take that step, even with a ‘positive season so far’ as the report states. Ruben Amorim believes in him, seeing him as a reliable ally, but more is needed if he’s to achieve his dream.
