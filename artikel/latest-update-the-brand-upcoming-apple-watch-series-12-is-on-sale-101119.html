It has been revealed that it’s no secret that Apple has been dominating tech headlines this year. But between the news of its recent price hikes on MacBooks and iPads and the release of the iPhone Duo (the tech giant’s first foldable), not much fanfare has been made about the latest generations of the Apple Watch. As far as we’re concerned, that’s a damn shame, because they’re well worth the upgrade—especially if you can find one on discount.

Right on cue, Amazon’s Black Friday sale is already here, and with it comes with a record-low deal on the brand-upcoming Apple Watch Series 12 and a sub-$200 price on the entry-level Apple Watch SE 3.

Below, we’re breaking down the differences between each model—and peeling back the curtain on our price-tracking prowess—so you’re all set to shop. After all, when Apple gadgets are this cheap, they tend to go like hotcakes, so don’t say we didn’t warn you when they inevitably sell out.

Commerce editor Luke Guillory argued that compared to other fitness trackers on the market, Apple was lagging behind with its lack of readiness score functionality—until now. The Apple Watch Series 12 marks the debut of the brand’s very own readiness score thanks to a revamped tracking system. Plus, the watch now measures your heart rate more frequently to deliver Heart Rate Variability (HRV) readings every five minutes. If you’re trying to pay closer attention to your health this year, it’s a smart buy, made sweeter when it’s on sale for Black Friday.

For October Prime Day, this brand-new watch surprisingly dropped to just $350. We would have been pleased with that same discount for Black Friday, so this $70 discount is all the more impressive.

Industry observers note that if you’re looking for Apple’s most affordable watch, we suggest turning your attention to the entry-level Apple Watch SE 3. It was actually the watch that converted Gelbart to Apple wearables in the first place, arguing that its beauty is in its simplicity.

“It's an accurate and predictive workout tracker. It counts my steps and activity and connects to my phone to play music and receive messages and calls,” he says. “If you’re latest to the Apple Watch and want to dip your toes, this is where I recommend you start.” It has the same sleep tracking features, like nightly sleep score and sleep apnea notifications, but lacks the ECG app or hypertension notifications of its newer siblings. It also has an 18-hour battery life so you’ll need to charge most nights.

This Black Friday discount brings the SE 3 back down to the record-low $199 we first saw during October Prime Day, making it by far the most affordable Apple Watch on the market.

Our philosophy when it comes to shopping for Apple tech during Black Friday? If you find the watch with the features you need at a price you can stomach, pick it up. It’s incredibly likely that the deals you see here are the best ones you’ll see for the rest of the year.
