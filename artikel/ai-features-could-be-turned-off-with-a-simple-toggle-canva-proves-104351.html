Industry observers note that AI has taken over everything in the past few years, with AI features bombarding virtually every app or service available. If you’ve ever wanted to just turn off AI features, Canva proves that it could be a simple toggle, if companies actually wanted to give you a choice.

Canva today unveiled “AI Controls,” a newly introduced toggle that completely turns off generative AI features within the editing app.

Generative AI can be quite helpful in working on posters, editing images, and more, but it’s not everyone’s preference. Canva puts this well:.

Industry observers note that for some people, AI is the most exciting creative tool in a generation, while others prefer bringing their ideas to life without it. We believe the future of creativity should give everyone the freedom to choose their own path, whether that’s creating with AI, without it, or somewhere in between.

To give its consumers the choice, Canva’s Permissions menu now has a simple toggle for “Enable Generative AI features.” Toggling it off disables features such as Magic Write and Magic Edit. There’s also an option to limit how much AI-generated imagery appears in the Canva library.

Put simply, this is great to see.

AI has its place, but everyone should have a choice like this, especially when it comes to apps and services used for artistry in the same vein as Canva. Yet, the tech industry continues to go in the opposite direction, shoving AI into every corner whether it’s actually useful or not. Canva proves, though, that it doesn’t have to be that way.
