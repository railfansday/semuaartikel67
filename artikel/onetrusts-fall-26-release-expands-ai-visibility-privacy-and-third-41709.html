UPDATED 09:00 EST / NOVEMBER 18 2026.

Governance platform provider OneTrust LLC today announced its Fall ’26 Release, a set of platform updates mentioned to bring governance into the workflows where business decisions are made.

Building on OneTrust CORIE, a shared intelligence layer for governing artificial intelligence agents introduced at the tech giant’s TrustWeek conference in September, the release covers AI visibility, privacy, consent and third-party risk management.

Sources indicate that leading the list of updates is a revised AI inventory, which is designed to reflect changes across a customer’s environment on a continuous basis. According to OneTrust, the change is needed because many organizations running complex networks of AI systems and agents have only limited visibility into how those systems operate. Model associations, potential matches and deprecation indicators from providers are now pulled into the inventory from a curated library of foundation models.

In a recent development, relationships among AI systems, models, agents, projects, risks, controls and workflows can be explored through an interactive AI lineage view. AI performance and operational dashboards, added in the release, bring usage, associated risk, evaluation coverage, outbound personally identifiable information discovery and token consumption into one summary.

Ryan Karlin, vice president of product management at OneTrust, noted knowing which AI systems are in the business “is just the starting point.” According to Karlin, teams also need visibility into the activity and dependencies of those systems “alongside the privacy and third-party risks they already manage.”.

In a recent development, on the third-party risk side, a workflow rule that debuts a risk assessment can now trigger OneTrust’s Deep Research AI skill. The in-depth research report that the skill compiles from public data sources is then used to answer the assessment questionnaire. OneTrust claims the integration can cut the time needed to complete a third-party risk assessment from weeks to minutes.

Drawing on previous assessments and supplier documentation, the company’s expanded AI Document Scanning and Inventory Response Analysis features can now also answer questions about the business engagement tied to a third party.

Sources indicate that for privacy teams, the release adds protections against automated abuse of data subject request web forms. Growing volumes of those automated requests are straining team workloads, according to OneTrust. To filter out the abuse, enhanced CAPTCHA options now work alongside cross-site request forgery token controls to block suspicious submissions.

In the company’s consent management platform, navigation, configuration, scanning, publishing and geolocation management have also been brought into a single workflow under a modernized interface.

It has been revealed that rounding out the release are reporting updates. Enhanced assessment reporting and application programming interfaces are stated to give privacy teams more insight into what drives risk scores. Upcoming risk hierarchy visualizations, which show how individual risks across business units aggregate into enterprise risk categories, are aimed at helping teams prioritize risk treatment.

OneTrust is sharing availability details for the Fall ’26 Release through an overview webinar.

OneTrust has raised $1.13 billion to date, including $150 million in 2023 at a valuation of $4.5 billion. Investors in the manufacturer include Insight Partners LP, Coatue Management, Technology Crossover Ventures LP, SoftBank Vision Fund 2 LP, Franklin Resources Inc., Generation Investment Management LLP and Sands Capital Management.
