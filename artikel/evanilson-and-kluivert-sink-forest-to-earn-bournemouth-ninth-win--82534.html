Marco Rose saluted the in-form Evanilson after the Brazilian scored for a sixth Premier League game in a row to lift Bournemouth up to fourth place with a 2-0 win over Nottingham Forest.

Evanilson’s brilliantly taken 19th-minute opener set his side on course for a club record ninth straight win in all competitions, while the defeat means Forest are only above the relegation places on goal difference having failed to build on the momentum of their 3-1 win against Manchester City before the international break.

Rose noted: “We defend together as a team, and we attack together as a team, but the forward guys were excellent today. They did a great job scoring the goals and the other guys support them. Evanilson creates space, carries the ball, provides assists and now he scores goals as a No 9, really beautiful goals so I am happy.”.

Both teams started the game on the front foot and Forest had the first sight of goal. Neco Williams nodded dangerously across the face of goal at the far post, but Liam Delap was beaten to the ball by home defender Antonio Silva.

Evanilson was denied by a brilliant last-ditch tackle from Forest centre-back Jair Cunha before Marcus Tavernier’s 20-yard curler was well saved by Matz Sels.

Bournemouth took the lead in the 19th minute. James Hill cushioned a header into the path of Evanilson, and the Brazilian took one touch before blasting a left-foot shot into the roof of the net.

Justin Kluivert almost doubled the home side’s lead with an effort from 20 yards which sailed narrowly over the crossbar. Evanilson then failed to keep his shot down after a long throw from Hill caused chaos in the Forest penalty area.

Sources indicate that oliver Glasner’s side had a penalty appeal waved away when Adam Smith blocked Williams’s effort into the arm of teammate Tyler Adams. But Kluivert did make it 2-0 on the stroke of half-time with a simple far-post finish after a superb run and cross from Tavernier.

Industry observers note that forest were much improved in the second half, adopting a more direct approach in a bid to find a way back into the game. Delap turned provider by whipping in a cross for Morgan Gibbs-White but the captain could not properly connect with his header.

• Bournemouth have won five successive games in the top-flight of English football for the first time. Only Arsenal (67, before Newcastle game) and Manchester City (60) have collected more Premier League points in 2026 than the Cherries (52 – W13 D13 L4).

• Bournemouth have won each of their last nine games in all competitions, their longest ever such run, overtaking two separate runs of eight in 1998-99 and 2012-13.

• Nottingham Forest failed to score in this game despite registering 2.14 expected goals, the highest xG tally by any team not to score in a Premier League match this season. Opta.

In a recent development, james McAtee ballooned an effort over the bar as the visitors continued to misfire in front of goal. The substitute Chris Wood also miscued a header from a corner when he should have at least tested the underworked Bournemouth goalkeeper, Djordje Petrovic.

Sources indicate that bournemouth always looked a threat on the counter and Kluivert came close to his second and Bournemouth’s third when he stung the gloves of Sels from 18 yards out. Kluivert should also have been more clinical when he found himself within finishing range but unselfishly laid the ball off to Rayan and the chance went begging.

Glasner, whose side had 19 shots but only one on target, mentioned: “Of course when you lose it’s always a tough 90 minutes, especially when you make too many mistakes like we did in the first half.

In a recent development, “The second goal straight before half-time was a little bit of a killer but credit to the lads in the second half for finding a filter back into the game. We had some good situations, but we couldn’t convert them.

“We are not able to score in this game, and this is what we have to look at and where we have to improve. We took too many touches, and were not so aggressive.”.
