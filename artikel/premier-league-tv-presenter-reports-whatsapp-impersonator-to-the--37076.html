By TAMARA PRENN, SENIOR SPORTS REPORTER.

Published: 04:43 EST, 17 November 2026 | Updated: 09:33 EST, 17 November 2026.

Premier League presenter Ellie Collins issued a warning to her followers after learning that she had been being impersonated on social media.

The 26-year-old has over 58,000 followers on Instagram having built up a loyal fanbase via her work on broadcasters covering the top flight and NFL in the US.

But Collins was forced to make a statement after an imposter was found to have been reaching out to people in her name over a number of months.

'I want to make people aware that someone has been impersonating me on WhatsApp, messaging high-profile athletes, pretending to be me with their username set to my name and a picture of me,' she told her followers on the social media app.

'This has been happening on and off for several months and seems to be upping in frequency more recently.

'I have reported this to the police and they’ve asked that I share it on here to make people aware, particularly anyone I have worked with or connected with in the football/sports world.

Premier League presenter Ellie Collins has warned fans of an impersonator pretending to be her on social media (All photos source: Instagram/Ellie Collins).

The 26-year-old maintains an active presence on the site and has over 58,000 followers.

After previously working as a waitress, Collins transitioned into broadcasting four years ago.

Since starting her career in the media, Collins has had the opportunity to interview a number of sports stars including Frank Lampard.

Sources indicate that 'If you receive a message from someone claiming to be me... it’s most likely not. If you don’t have my personal number and are unsure, just DM me on Instagram.

'If you receive anything suspicious, please send it to me directly so I can keep a record of it.

'Thank youuu (purple heart emoji, winking emoji),' she signed off.

Collins has worked in the media since joining IMG Media as an assistant five years ago.

Since 2022, the former waitress has served as a presenter for Premier League productions, which has led to opportunities to interview sports stars including Andy Robertson, John Terry, Bruno Fernandes, Conor Gallagher and Cody Gakpo.

Collins has also worked a number of events beyond the domestic league, such as Wimbledon and the 2025 Champions League final.

This year, she also covered the Open for Sky Sports from Royal Birkdale, and racing at York's Ebor Festival with ITV.
