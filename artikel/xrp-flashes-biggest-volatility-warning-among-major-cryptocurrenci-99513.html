XRP Flashes Biggest Volatility Warning Among Major Cryptocurrencies

🌐 Source: U • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (438 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • XRP stands out as the cryptocurrency with the most alarming volatility warning among major digital assets, according to recent Coinbase data.
  • It has been revealed that XRP's seven-day options-implied price move stands at 1.94 times its historical median.
  • XRP exhibits the highest premium above its own historical volatility.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

XRP stands out as the cryptocurrency with the most alarming volatility warning among major digital assets, according to recent Coinbase data. The options market indicates that traders are now preparing for price swings that could be nearly double XRP's historical seven-day price move.

It has been revealed that XRP's seven-day options-implied price move stands at 1.94 times its historical median. This figure far exceeds similar metrics for major cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and Solana (SOL).

XRP exhibits the highest premium above its own historical volatility. Solana demonstrates the lowest premium among these four cryptocurrencies, Coinbase remarked.

Sources indicate that as the chart below indicates, the options-implied seven-day price move for XRP is around 9.6%, according to Glassnode and Coinbase data as of Nov. 17. This compares to its historical median absolute price move of about 4.9%.

Basically, this means that the options market is signaling a price swing nearly twice as large as XRP's typical seven-day price movement.

According to recent reports, interestingly, Solana's implied price move is also close to 9.5%, almost the same as XRP's. Yet its historical median weekly price move is much higher at about 6.5%.

The key here is that although both cryptocurrencies exhibit similarly large potential price moves, the magnitude of the expected volatility surprise is significantly greater for XRP relative to its usual trading range.

It has been revealed that for comparison, Bitcoin shows an options-implied seven-day move of about 6%, versus its historical median of about 3.5%. For Ethereum, these numbers are about 8% and 5.1%, respectively.

Obviously, this latest figure doesn't necessarily imply that XRP is going to crash. It's a sign that options traders expect a major price move regardless of its direction.

Implied volatility is a forward-looking measure. It's based on options prices. The higher the implied volatility, the more traders pay for exposure to potential price movements or protection against them.

The chart from Coinbase is based on a one-standard-deviation estimate. It's derived by converting annualized at-the-money implied volatility into a seven-day timeframe.

It has been revealed that for XRP, the roughly 9.6% implied move represents a statistical range. It's not a guaranteed price target. A one-standard-deviation range suggests about a 68% chance of the price staying within this range under typical modeling assumptions.

Thus, the latest figure of 1.94x implies that XRP's volatility premium remains high despite a slight decrease from the previous reading.

According to recent reports, from XRP traders' perspective, this can be a very challenging market environment. For traders using leverage, there could be a high risk of liquidation due to potentially sharp price moves.
