Latest Update: 10 Great Games That Defined The Xbox Series X|S

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It’s rare for gaming brands to last through three console generations, let alone four. Microsoft’s gaming brand, Xbox, has managed to do just that, reaching the momentous milestone of creating gaming memories for 25 years.

However, Xbox’s fourth and current console generation, the Xbox Series X|S, has been impacted by an ongoing identity crisis for the brand, with Xbox initially focusing on acquiring studios, Game Pass, and Play Anywhere, only to lay off hundreds of employees and refocus on developing exclusive titles.

Despite that rocky history, there were still many great exclusive and Microsoft-supported games that launched on the Xbox Series X|S and showcased Xbox’s ever-changing priorities.

In 2014, Microsoft acquired Mojang and the greater Minecraft franchise. Since then, Microsoft has been striving to expand the Minecraft franchise through spinoffs like Minecraft: Story Mode, Minecraft Dungeons, and, for the Series consoles, Minecraft Legends.

Developed by Mojang and Blackbird Interactive, Minecraft Legends is a cross between traditional action games and RTS titles, having players acquire resources and mobs like Golems to defend the world against invading Nether forces.

Like many Minecraft spinoffs, Legends’ reception was overall mixed between critics and gamers, but its unique gameplay style goes to show just how much endless potential the Minecraft franchise has beyond its humble survival origins.

The British video game developer Ninja Theory soared into gaming prominence with the release of the ambitious action-adventure game Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice, but that game’s success wound up being a blessing and a curse: NT was acquired by Microsoft shortly thereafter in 2018.

Since then, Microsoft helped fund and publish the MOBA Bleeding Edge as well as Hellblade’s sequel, Senua's Saga: Hellblade II, which followed series protagonist Senua once again as she sets out to free her people from captivity in Iceland during the ninth century.

While Hellblade II would receive critical praise for its story and gameplay just like its predecessor, its success was apparently not enough for Microsoft to continue investing in NT, with the developer now facing closure and a third Hellblade game facing cancellation.

Recent findings suggest that double Fine is well-known for creating some of the most creative and hilarious games of the 21st century. They also have a long history with Xbox, having released Psychonauts as a timed original Xbox exclusive and Iron Brigade as an Xbox 360 exclusive.

However, Double Fine briefly joined Xbox Game Studios in 2019 to release Psychonauts 2 and its first Series X|S exclusive, Keeper, a very unique adventure game following a living lighthouse as it sets out to help a bird called Twig reunite with its flock.

Despite its rather bizarre, Dr. Seuss-like nature, Keeper wound up being a very fun, visually wondrous, and heartwarming game that every Double Fine fan and Series X|S owner should give a try, even if Double Fine is no longer a part of Xbox Game Studios.

Bethesda and Microsoft have often worked together to release some of the best RPGs as timed exclusives on Xbox consoles, such as The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind, but their relationship solidified in 2020 when Microsoft officially acquired ZeniMax Media and its studios including Bethesda.

Recent findings suggest that this led Microsoft to make Bethesda’s first brand-newly introduced RPG in years, Starfield, a timed Series X|S exclusive. The game takes place in the early 24th century in Alpha Centauri human colony worlds and sees players join Constellation, the last group of space explorers.

While Starfield turned out to be not as impressive as similar space-based games like No Man’s Sky and The Outer Worlds, it still managed to deliver engaging FPS combat and third-person spaceship piloting, as well as a wide variety of customization options for the Spacefarer, ship, and bases.

In 2023, Microsoft made the record-breaking acquisition of Activision Blizzard for $75.4 billion. Since then, most Activision Blizzard games have been optimized and intended for Series X|S play, with one of Activision's best games released under Microsoft ownership being Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4.

The fourth and final entry in the 2019 Call of Duty: Modern Warfare reboot series, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 features a campaign split between Captain John Price hunting down Vladimir Makarov and South Korean soldiers fighting a North Korean invasion.

MW4 is easily the best Call of Duty campaign released in years, ending the new MW series in an arguably far better way than the original MW3 and hopefully setting the stage for more great COD campaigns to come in the near future.

Obsidian Entertainment is famous for creating some of the best RPGs ever made, such as Fallout: Newly introduced Vegas and South Park: The Stick of Truth, but recently they’ve increasingly become known for creating some of the most fun survival multiplayer games, such as Grounded 2.

Set two years after Grounded, Grounded 2 sees the same kids from the first game once again shrink down to the size of insects as they struggle to survive in the public Brookhollow Park, where grass now appears like a vast jungle and spiders are monolithic predators.

Grounded 2 is still in early access, but that doesn’t stop it from being one of the most fun multiplayer games to play on Series X|S consoles, with players being able to explore and craft in over eight distinct biomes on foot or by tamed bug mounts.

As a part of ZeniMax’s acquisition, Microsoft also acquired the Sweden-based MachineGames, famous for developing the newly introduced Wolfenstein series of games. In 2024, it released one of the most surprising hits of the current console generation, with Indiana Jones and the Great Circle.

Released as a timed Series X|S exclusive, Indiana Jones and the Great Circle is a primarily first-person adventure game, following the famed archaeologist in between the first and third movies. He sets out to discover the secret behind the global Great Circle before his enemies do.

While Indiana Jones and the Great Circle doesn’t have Harrison Ford reprise his iconic role, it’s easily the best Indiana Jones story to appear since 1989 and is a pure fun, pulp adventure that any fan of Steven Spielberg and George Lucas’ movies will enjoy.

The Xbox One era was a rough time to be a Halo fan, and sadly, the Series X|S era wasn’t much different. Halo Studios, formerly 343 Industries, only released one brand-fresh Halo game on the platform: Halo Infinite.

In a recent development, the sixth mainline entry in the Halo series, Halo Infinite sees Master Chief attempt to defeat the now tyrannical Cortana on Zeta Halo, only for the Banished from Halo Wars 2 to ambush the UNSC. Master Chief, a Pelican pilot, and a new AI called The Weapon must attempt to defeat the Banished alone.

In a recent development, infinite may have launched without Forge, campaign co-op, and a lack of several multiplayer modes, but it still wound up featuring a fun multiplayer that aimed to return the series to its more classic roots. An emotional campaign story set in the series' first true open-world setting on Zeta Halo, it's not to be missed.

Recent findings suggest that for 44 years, Microsoft has stood as an innovative pioneer in the realm of flight simulation games, through its long-running Microsoft Flight Simulator series. In 2024, Asobo Studio and Microsoft released the most realistic and visually impressive entry yet, with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

A direct follow-up to 2020’s Microsoft Flight Simulator reboot, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 features much of the same core gameplay as its predecessor, but with a ton of new additions such as an in-depth career mode, dynamic environmental events and wildlife, and a plethora of new aircraft to fly such as airships and gliders.

Industry observers note that microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 is easily one of the most relaxing and awe-inspiring games to play in the Series X|S era, as people can fly practically any aircraft they want in first or third-person all over the globe. Iconic locations including Zion National Park, Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park, Rio de Janeiro, and more are available as awe-inspiring sights.

Without a doubt, the best game that truly defines the Xbox Series X|S era and showcases the power of the console, as well as the potential future of the Xbox brand, is The Coalition's Gears of War: E-Day.

The sixth mainline entry in the Gears of War series and prequel to the first Gears game, Gears of War: E-Day has players once again embody the iconic duo of COG soldiers Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago, as they witness the Locust’s first attack on humanity in the city of Kalona on Emergence Day.

Industry observers note that returning to the franchise’s gritty, more horrifying roots, E-Day is to many Xbox fans the best Gears game since Gears of War 3, as it not only features an epic yet somber campaign but features a highly addictive multiplayer spearheaded by the newly introduced PvE mode Horde Siege. Every element of the series at its best.
