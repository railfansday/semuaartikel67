It has been revealed that anthropic (ANT +0.00%), the tech giant behind the Claude AI models, is set to begin trading on the Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT. It's one of the most anticipated IPOs in history, and the final pricing showed just how much demand there is.

I've called Anthropic arguably the most impressive pre-IPO company I've ever seen, and I plan to own it. But I'm not planning to buy shares the minute they start trading. Here's why, and how I plan to approach adding the AI giant to my portfolio.

It has been revealed that to say that there was a lot of demand for Anthropic's IPO would be an understatement. Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above its original range of $70 to $78.

In a recent development, it also increased the size of the deal by about 19%, selling 980 million shares, about 956 million from the manufacturer and the rest from its co-founders. At an $83 IPO price, Anthropic raised $81.3 billion. Including a separate $26 billion private placement, the total deal size is $107.3 billion, valuing Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion and making it the largest IPO on record, surpassing SpaceX (SPCX +1.60%).

Speaking of SpaceX, that is easily the closest comparison. It was a similarly-sized IPO that priced at $135 in June and closed its first day at $160.95, up 19%. Just days later, it hit an intraday high of $225.64. Since then, it has fallen about 35% from that peak to around $145.

Recent findings suggest that of course, there's no guarantee Anthropic stock follows the same path. For one thing, its public float is larger. SpaceX sold only about 4% of its shares, while Anthropic's 980 million shares are more than 7% of its total. Anthropic's lock-up also releases insider shares in stages, starting with up to 316 million on the first day of trading. More supply could mean a smaller pop, and possibly a smaller drop once the initial hype dies down. But the point is that nobody, myself included, has a crystal ball that can tell us whether Anthropic's stock will be higher or lower than its IPO price in a day, a week, a month, or a year.

I plan to wait at least a few days for the excitement around these IPO stocks to settle before I buy anything. If the price has come back toward the IPO price, I'll open a normal starter position. If the hype has pushed it far above $83, I'll take a small nibble and build the position gradually.

Either way, I'm thinking in years, not days. Anthropic's growth is remarkable, but it also faces enormous computing bills and stiff competition, so I want room to add if the stock gets cheaper. Having said that, I'd rather own a small piece of this business at a fair price than wait for a perfect price that may never come.
