It has been revealed that join Upromise if you already save in a 529 blueprint or repay student loans and shop online, since it’s free and sends the cash back to those accounts. Expect about $1 back on a $100 order at big stores, so start the orders you’d place anyway there and never buy more to earn it.

Industry observers note that online stores pay Upromise for sending them shoppers, and it passes you a share as cash back, which it moves each month into the 529 college savings plan, student loan, or bank account you link. It suits college savers and graduates repaying loans, and it no longer mails checks under the rewards tech giant Prodege, which bought it from Sallie Mae in 2020.

Recent findings suggest that college savers and student loan borrowers who shop online anyway get a free top-up by starting those orders at Upromise. Nobody should buy more to earn it, since an extra $100 order at 1% back still costs you $99.
