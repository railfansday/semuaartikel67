Graham Alexander set for Preston North End January overhaul with loan deals poised to be cancelled

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Preston North End have had a disastrous season so far which has left them rock bottom of the Championship after 15 games. PNE are already seven points adrift of safety, with a managerial change to Graham Alexander so far not working out.

Sources indicate that that leaves them in need of major changes in January, and Alexander is looking to make his mark on the squad. According to Pete O’Rourke, the Preston boss is hoping to terminate all his sides loan deals to replace them with his own targets in the winter.

Industry observers note that north End presently have four loanees in their squad; Caleb Wiley, Liam Gibbs, Delano Burgzorg and Jusef Erabi. PNE have already brought in Greg Docherty under Alexander but he is clearly far from done with his Preston squad overhaul.

In a recent development, so far this season all of the PNE loanees have underperformed, which is evident in the Championship table. Erabi has managed just two goals in 15 appearances, while Burgzorg is yet to score despite contributing a handful of assists.

Wiley has shown promise, scoring on his Deepdale debut against Bristol City, but injury has restricted him to five appearances. Gibbs has also struggled for availability, making five appearances before suffering a quad injury that is expected to sideline him for several months.

Returning the four players in January would free up squad places and potentially allow Alexander to recruit players better suited to his preferred system. With the relegation battle intensifying, the manager needs greater reliability and quality from his available options.

Industry observers note that preston North End need to revamp their squad in January. They need something special to turn their season around after what has been a horrendous opening third of the campaign and returning those loanees is a simple way to switch things up.

However, with that said, PNE still need those loanees for another 10 games at least. With the busy festive period coming up, for those players to know that they are not wanted by Alexander might not be ideal.

It has been revealed that he needs those players to give them all over the next months or so. Sometimes that can be a wake-up call for some players, who will see this as a time to prove themselves but it can also go in the other way.

PNE have a massive few weeks ahead which include the current loanees, which starts at West Ham this weekend. If Preston cant pickup some points in over December, they are going to be in major trouble no matter what happens in January.
