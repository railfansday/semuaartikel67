This Isn’t the First Time Bond Has Had to Choose Between Omega and JLC

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that just a few months ago, Robert Pattinson signed on with Jaeger-LeCoultre as a brand ambassador, appearing in La Grande Maison’s ads wearing the new Master Control Chronometre Perpetual Calendar. However, that role has become complicated since the actor was announced as James Bond last week, given that the iconic character has been an Omega man for going on 31 years. This feels like uncharted waters, but it turns out this isn’t the first time two watch brands have competed for 007’s affections.

How this will play out is anyone’s guess, but there are clues we can find in the past. Bizarrely enough, this exact horological love triangle has played out before, when Pierce Brosnan was MI6’s top agent. The Irish actor taking up the mantle of 007 coincided with Omega coming on as Bond’s official watch supplier. In 1995’s GoldenEye, Brosnan wears the Seamaster Diver 300M with a blue-wave dial. The watch has become synonymous with Bond.

Recent findings suggest that however, just a few years into life as a superspy, Brosnan produced a remake of the classic menswear flick The Thomas Crown Affair. Due to his Bondbligations, dressing became extremely complicated. In the 1999 movie, Brosnan is forced to wear an open shirt and an untied white bow tie draped around his neck during a black-tie ball because his Bond contract reportedly forbade him from wearing a tuxedo in other movies. And because of the actor’s contract with Omega, Thomas Crown’s watch had to be handled just as delicately.

Recent findings suggest that nick Gould, watch spotter extraordinaire, pointed out to us that Pierce Brosnan, still in the midst of his run as 007, strapped on a JLC Reverso for The Thomas Crown Affair remake. The reversible watch was a perfect symbol of the titular character’s double life. But because Brosnan was still entwined with Bond, the watch’s dial was scrubbed to remove both the brand and model name. The coincidence that a Bond would have to select between a JLC and an Omega is even stranger because, unlike Pattinson, Brosnan had no ties to JLC. And there wasn’t some unbreakable tie between Thomas Crown and the brand either. In the original version of The Thomas Crown Affair, Steve McQueen wears a Patek Philippe pocket watch, a Cartier Tank Cintrée, and a Jaeger-LeCoultre Memovox.

According to recent reports, now, Pattinson and Brosnan’s situations aren’t perfectly analogous. The latter’s affair involved a one-off movie placement that conflicted with Bond’s requirement to wear Omega. Pattinson’s predicament involves a freshly inked brand ambassadorship butting up against 007’s long-held partnership. However, it is strange to see the exact pair of brands tied up in Bond-related drama.

This time around, neither brand appears to be backing down. In the wake of Pattinson’s announcement, JLC noted in a statement: “Jaeger-LeCoultre warmly congratulates Robert Pattinson on his appointment to the iconic role of James Bond, a tremendous milestone in an already distinguished cinematic career.” And a representative confirmed, “Robert Pattinson continues in his role as a Global Ambassador for Jaeger-LeCoultre under our existing agreement.” Meanwhile, Omega has posted its Bond watches several times on Instagram. Pattinson, of course, is caught in the middle. As watch lovers first and foremost, we personally love the idea of him continuing to wear JLC on his own time and a Seamaster 300M on Bond time. After all, if anyone can lead a double life while keeping two competing loyalties straight, surely it’s 007.
