📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Josh Hutcherson has a fresh sci-fi series on the way, and it will make even his successful The Hunger Games franchise look small. 2026 has been a great year for sci-fi movies, as the genre has found enormous success both in theaters and on streaming. Additionally, the year's two biggest cinematic events are still to come, and they go all in on the sci-fi.

Sources indicate that avengers: Doomsday is all about the multiverse, while Denis Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three will conclude the best modern sci-fi movie franchise. Recently, before those two anticipated movies, The Hunger Games franchise returned to the massive screen. Hutcherson made history alongside Jennifer Lawrence in the four original movies in the beloved dystopian franchise. The actors have now returned as Peeta Mellark and Katniss Everdeen, respectively.

According to recent reports, they reprised their fan-favorite roles in the epilogue of the new prequel movie The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which was released in theaters on November 20 and is already making waves at the box office. The excitement around Hutcherson's return to the genre franchise and the longevity of The Hunger Games universe as a whole are notable.

That stated, the actor has another sci-fi role on the way, and not only is it part of an even bigger franchise, but it lives in a pocket of a shared universe that by itself is enough to make The Hunger Games feel small. The upcoming sci-fi series in question is Mister Miracle, which is set in the ongoing DC Universe started by James Gunn.

First, I'm not saying that The Hunger Games franchise is small, as we all know just how rich its lore is, with the vast majority of the Hunger Games editions not having made their way to the significant screen. There have also been two The Hunger Games prequels in addition to the original four movies, so the franchise keeps expanding. That said, the DCU pulls from an even bigger history thanks to the vast source material that comes from DC Comics, which predates The Hunger Games franchise by several decades.

This is a universe that includes major heroes like Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, The Flash, and many more. While Hutcherson's Mister Miracle is not one of the most popular characters from DC Comics, his upcoming animated series will delve into a corner of the franchise that has some of DC's most intricate lore, as it came from the mind of Jack Kirby himself, with the Fourth World and the Upcoming Gods. Mister Miracle is the adopted son of Darkseid, one of DC's biggest villains, and he was essentially raised in hell, the planet Apokolips.

Sources indicate that hutcherson's new sci-fi series will explore that upbringing in the cosmos and how Scott Free's mind has been impacted by all he has endured, his years as a superhero, Mister Miracle's anxiety over his personal life, and more. The show is also being developed by DC Comics writer Tom King, who famously penned the award-winning Mister Miracle comic book series, which was all about mental health and exploring the character and the New Gods' vast history. As such, The Hunger Games franchise, while expansive, will end up looking small next to it.

Additionally, there is also the fact that beyond his DCU debut in the upcoming adult animated series, Hutcherson is also expected to reprise his role as Mister Miracle in live-action in the future. Given how the character has been connected to the Justice League in the comics, as well as the actor's popularity, there is a chance that he could be part of the team whenever it returns to the big screen. As such, with a guaranteed animated debut and the potential for a live-action future in a rich shared universe, Hutcherson's Mister Miracle is a sci-fi series that will make The Hunger Games feel small.
