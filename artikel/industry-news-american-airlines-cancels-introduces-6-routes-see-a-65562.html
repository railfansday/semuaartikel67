It has been revealed that in the past week, American Airlines revealed more changes to its huge network. They all involve Los Angeles International Airport, at which the oneworld member is the third-largest operator for passengers, flights, seats for sale, and available seat miles.

According to recent reports, american's schedule submission to OAG shows that Los Angeles is the carrier's ninth-busiest hub for departures this year. It rises to eighth for available seats and seventh for ASMs. And for passenger traffic, which is obviously retrospective, it was eighth through August. While Los Angeles is not a huge hub for American, the route changes are notable.

It has been revealed that in the latest schedule upgrade, two of the following three routes will now be removed in early 2027. The initiative was for them to be served daily through 2027, although Cleveland was to have only a handful of services in April and May before being ramped up to daily again for the peak summer.
