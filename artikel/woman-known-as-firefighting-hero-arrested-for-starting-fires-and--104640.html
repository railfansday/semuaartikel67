A woman who was praised as being a hero for fighting fires in Singapore has now been arrested for arson and is accused of starting those same fires.

It has been revealed that if a fire hits, your first port of call should really be the emergency services so that they can dispatch some professionals. However, there are times when you have to fight the fire yourself, at least until the fire crew manages to get there.

Well, over in Singapore, 33-year-old Ong Siew Hoon took things into her own hands, fighting a set of fires that had been started in a trash chute in the building that she lives in.

It has been revealed that as a result, she was nominated for a community award that rewards such acts of bravery. However, as it turns out, the 33-year-old has been arrested on suspicion of starting the fires in the first place.

According to Asia One, Ong Siew Hoon was arrested on November 20 and was charged with one count of mischief by fire.

In a recent development, as per reports, the 33-year-old was charged in court, with prosecutors asking for her to be remanded by police for a week while they carry out the investigation. Ong was also asked to help police revisit the scene and aid in the investigation as well.

She was also described as a “kind-hearted female resident” of the building, who mentioned she had learned to use fire hoses in a bid to battle the constant flames in the building’s trash compartments and would check every hour for flames.

According to Mothership, if found guilty, the 33-year-old could face up to seven years in prison.

Ong was also expected to receive her community hero award on November 27, which is now the day she should be released from police custody.
