📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that walk dogs through Wag if you live in a busy city and want paid walks on your own schedule, since walk requests reach you without a client list. Set your rates knowing Wag keeps about 40% of each booking’s price, and take walks close together, because travel between dogs is unpaid.

Pet parents pay 1.67 times the rate you set, Wag’s help center says, so the $24 Wag lists for a half-hour walk pays you $14.37 plus tips. Self-employment tax for your Social Security and Medicare takes about 14% of that, leaving $12.34, and with 15 minutes between walks to reach the next dog, four walks fill three hours, about $16.45 an hour after that tax.

Wag suits dog lovers in busy cities who can take walks a few minutes apart and want paid walks before they have clients of their own. Walkers ready to build repeat clients keep more on Rover, which takes 20% of each booking in most of the U.S., half of Wag’s roughly 40%.
