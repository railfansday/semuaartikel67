Sources indicate that young men in relationships around the world have spent the last few weeks warning their significant others about Nov. 19. On that day, and for their forseeable future, the men in their lives would be MIA, they noted. They wouldn't answer texts. They weren't going to hang out. Many women were told to accept that men would put little to no effort into their relationships for a while as they focused entirely on Grand Theft Auto 6.

Recent findings suggest that these men, who were planning on abandoning their girlfriends for an undetermined amount of time, logged in to Rockstar's game on Thursday night to find another expectant woman waiting on the other side. Her name was Lucia, and she couldn't fully be brushed off — not if the player wanted to have a positive experience with the game's main story, anyway.

“What is this cuck simulator?” said one popular streamer whose clips of discomfort with Jason and Lucia's relationship have gone viral online. Although actively recoiling is an extreme reaction to GTA 6, the men doing so are emblematic of a wider trend of confusion and disgust with the game's relationship-driven experience.

"This game is woke slop," another male player complained after discovering a mission that tasked them with buying flowers for Lucia. "It's obvious it was made by women.".

"I'm just going to kill myself, what the fuck," a player wrote in a viral video that called GTA 6 a "latina dating simulator." The footage highlights the couple moving furniture and watching TV together.

Industry observers note that rockstar Games has made no secret that the relationship between Jason and Lucia was a central element of their upcoming game. The studio has likened the pair to a modern Bonnie and Clyde. Much of the marketing has focused on the mechanics Rockstar built to help players develop that relationship, like having small check-ins or going on dates.

For a good while, the men on the other side of those advertisements were fully on board. Lucia is hot, so what's not to like? On popular online communities like Reddit, most of the top posts about the game leading up to release were men sharing AI-generated images of Lucia naked, or in revealing clothing. The men were so into Lucia as an idea that when an apparent hacker proposed leaking nude gameplay of Lucia, the rogue was able to raise millions of real-world dollars.

According to recent reports, men couldn't get enough of Lucia ... until they actually got to experience her. To be clear, GTA 6 doesn't position Lucia as a nagging woman with annoying demands. Lucia is in prison at the start of the game, and players take control right before she is released. Jason's first story missions focus on classic GTA action, like driving, drugs, and making money. The game ambiently tells fans that they can do specific things to help prepare for Lucia's arrival, like painting the walls or patching her home's leaky roof. These activities are so subtle that some players probably miss them. Certainly, they could be disregarded altogether.

GTA 6's release is an event for women and men alike, but the game’s cultural identity is an unusually gendered one. Mainstream publications covering the game often do so by parsing it through the lens of heterosexual relationships. Here, people indulged in gendered thought experiments like “Is dating Lucia considered cheating for men?” One of the most pervasive GTA 6 memes frameworks dealt with men's anxieties about juggling GTA 6's release with the demands of their existing relationships.

In a recent development, GTA 6 is a video game, though, and gamers have a compulsion to do everything they can. Games position players to be the good guys, but the fantasy is easier to uphold when it's an abstract tale of good versus evil. When playing a game ostensibly means upkeep like showering your crusty character because your girlfriend thinks you reek, any semblance of a traditional power fantasy is shattered.

"10 hrs in and the game is just a chore simulator," one fan complained in a thread with dozens of replies debating whether Rockstar's latest could truly be considered a GTA game. In it, fans recognize GTA 6 still technically allows them to do most of the signature activities the franchise is known for. The big difference is that this time, Rockstar places empathy at the forefront. This change of direction is present in Jason and Lucia's relationship, but it's also evident throughout the rest of the game. You can absolutely start a rampage on a crowded beach — you just have to stomach killing some of the most realistic humans ever depicted in a video game.

Recent findings suggest that similarly, players can decide against nurturing their relationship with Lucia. The central story still revolves around the duo, and the two are fundamentally linked in a way I won't spoil. There's a version of GTA 6 that doesn't impose nonstop chores like buying groceries. Surprisingly, the game even allows you to forego picking Lucia up from prison. That playthrough comes with in-game arguments with Lucia that can escalate to a point where the two won't talk with one another much anymore.

Playing the story in GTA has always been optional, though. Historically, many fans haven't bothered with the narrative and approach the series as a free-roaming sandbox. That ethos is largely still tenable in GTA 6. The real question is whether players, particularly men, can stand being confronted with the disappointment of their actions.It may not be a lost cause. The same streamer who has accrued millions of views while recoiling from the screen, unable to watch Jason and Lucia's relationship play out, has readily called Jason a "simp" and GTA 6 a "female fantasy.".

"I'm enjoying the game," he stated in the same breath.
