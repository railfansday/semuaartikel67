Will Sweet put Miles Rutherford’s team ahead on 41 minutes, shortly after Town’s Kristian Shevchenko was sent off.

Jeremiah Okine-Peters levelled five minutes into the second half but Emmett Dunn’s superb header restored City’s lead just before the hour.

Okine-Peters had the final say, making it 2-2 13 minutes from time.

The point means City are still in the Southern League Premier South play-off places – they’re seventh,10 points behind leaders Uxbridge – and that’s who they play next, at Oaklands Park next Saturday.
