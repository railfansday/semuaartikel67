It has been revealed that while traditional stuffing is technically supposed to be stuffed inside the turkey to cook, that isn’t actually the ideal or even recommended way to cook your Thanksgiving turkey (or stuffing). I've found that you’ll achieve much better results by cooking them separately. By cooking your stuffing in a baking dish, you get crunchy, buttery pieces of bread on top and soft, bread pudding-esque pieces underneath. It's my perfect version of the holiday side—I tested it again and again, so it was worthy of your Thanksgiving table.

What People Are Saying:"[It's] an excellent recipe you can tweak it for taste. I like to up the onion by half again and use a lot more fresh sage." - GreenClover.

"Using a [mandoline] made the final preparation fast and easy. It was a big hit over a lovely meal. The dressing brought high praise from two trained chefs." - RedFish.

According to recent reports, as I mentioned above, sourdough bread is my go-to for stuffing. The first step is to bake it! You'll need 2 sheets, or do this in batches. You want to dry it out, so bake for 15 to 20 minutes.

While that's toasting, it's time to cook the aromatics. Melt some butter, then cook the onions, leeks, and garlic until your kitchen smells amazing and everything is softened.

Sources indicate that time for the liquids: Simply whisk eggs, broth, milk, and some fresh herbs in a large measuring cup. You can use a touch of red pepper for a hint of spice, which I highly recommend, but if you have a spice-averse crowd, you can skip it.

Industry observers note that when the bread is done toasting, turn the oven temp up to 375°. Prepare a 13" x 9" baking dish by coating it with a thin layer of butter.

Industry observers note that time to combine it all together: In a large bowl, mix the bread, aromatics, and liquid ingredients and toss until combined. You can let this sit at room temperature for up to 1 hour if you're planning ahead. Dot with even more butter, then bake until crispy. You can serve this stuffing warm or at room temperature.
