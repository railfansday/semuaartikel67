Hotelbeds is accepting a lower take rate to gain market share, while investing in latest sources of revenue.

Recent findings suggest that in August, Skift examined why Hotelbeds was earning less from every dollar of travel it sold. Three months later, its annual results show that the pressure has intensified — and its financial filings reveal how much the organization is investing in other ways to make money.

Industry observers note that since 2024, parent company HBX Group's investments and estimated acquisition consideration across five businesses total €102 million ($112 million), according to a Skift analysis of its accounts. About €65 million ($71 million) of that depends on how the acquired businesses perform, so the final bill could be higher or lower. Nearly a decade after using acquisitions to consolidate global hotel wholesaling, HBX is buying its way into holiday packaging, experiences, luxury travel and hotel software.
