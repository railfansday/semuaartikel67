According to recent reports, join AARP if you’ll use a discount or two a year, since one can cover the $20 fee and another adult in your home gets a membership at no extra cost. Buy an AARP-branded insurance policy only if it beats other companies’ quotes.

AARP’s claim that a single benefit may cover the whole fee holds for anyone paying the full $98 a year for Walmart+, since the member price of $58 saves $40, twice the fee. Smaller discounts need more spending to cover it, and at the 10% off food and nonalcoholic drinks that many chain restaurants give members, you’d spend $200 to save $20.

Sources indicate that it suits people who’d use one large discount a year, such as Walmart+’s, or several smaller ones, and a couple shares a single $20 fee. If the magazine is all you’d use, skip the membership and keep your $20.
