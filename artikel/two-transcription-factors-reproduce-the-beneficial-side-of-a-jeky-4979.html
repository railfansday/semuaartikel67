Two transcription factors reproduce the beneficial side of a 'Jekyll and Hyde' viral protein

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (82 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

LMP1 is an Epstein-Barr virus protein that has a "Mr. Hyde" side that can promote cancer and a "Dr. Jekyll" side that can spur antitumor immune responses. Scientists at Hiroshima University have now traced key antitumor immune effects of LMP1 to two transcription factors, Spi-B and IRF1, and found that expressing the two together made tumor cells easier for the immune system to recognize and better able to activate CD8+ T cells without using the viral protein itself.
