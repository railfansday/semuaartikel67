An old leak claiming Rockstar’s next game would be an LA Noire sequel about the Zodiac Killer is back in the spotlight after GTA 6 players discovered that the same source described the game’s ending correctly.

The claims were shared on GTAForums in 2023 by user Shahanshah, who relayed a post from an anonymous 4chan user that also covered several GTA 6 story details.

Now that GTA 6 is out on PlayStation and Xbox, players on the GamingLeaksAndRumours subreddit have gone back through the post and say its ending details match the final game.

The same source had already been credited with details Rockstar confirmed before launch, including Jason and Lucia’s relationship and the game’s Criminal Profile system.

According to the post, the sequel moves the series from 1940s Los Angeles to San Francisco, with a story built around the unidentified Zodiac Killer.

In a recent development, “You’re not gonna believe me, but Noire 2. Set in San Fran. Zodiac killer,” the post reads.

It added that the game was “still in the very early stages of development, though, it’ll probably be 2030 when it comes out.”.

The source also claimed Rockstar wants to bring back the facial-scanning tech the original LA Noire was known for, and push it much further than seen previously.

“The higher-ups are talking about doing the face scanning stuff again, but making it insane. They want it to be the best looking game ever made,” the post said.

In a recent development, the rumor first did the rounds in September 2026, before the GTA 6 details could be checked against the finished game. None of the LA Noire 2 claims have been confirmed.

Rockstar does already have a studio with close ties to the original, which was developed by Team Bondi and published by Rockstar in 2011.

Team Bondi founder Brendan McNamara went on to start Video Games Deluxe, which worked on the 2017 LA Noire re-release before Rockstar bought it and renamed it Rockstar Australia in 2025.

Rockstar hasn’t revealed what Rockstar Australia is working on, and has yet to announce a newly introduced LA Noire game.
