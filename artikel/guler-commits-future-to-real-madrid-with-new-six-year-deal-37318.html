Arda Guler's strong start to the 2026-27 campaign has been rewarded with a newly introduced six-year contract at Real Madrid.

Guler, who joined the Spanish giants from Fenerbahce three years ago, has agreed to fresh terms that will keep him at Madrid until 2032.

The 21-year-old has impressed under the guidance of Jose Mourinho this season, registering 10 goal contributions (four goals, six assists) in all competitions.

Indeed, only Kylian Mbappe (20) has more goal involvements than Guler, while the Turkish international's tally of 47 chances created is more than any other player for Madrid.

His most impressive display came in Madrid's dramatic 4-3 win over Roma in the Champions League, as he came off the bench to provide two assists late in the day.

Guler also impressed during the international break, netting a stunning free-kick against Belgium, but it was not enough to stop Turkiye falling to a 2-1 defeat.

Turkiye finished bottom of Group A1 after losing all six of their Nations League matches, but Guler will be hoping to shine once again for Madrid this weekend.

Madrid welcome Celta Vigo to the Santiago Bernabeu on Saturday as they aim to keep the pressure on LaLiga leaders Barcelona.

Los Blancos have won their last eight games in all competitions, their best winning run since a 10-game streak between May and September 2022 under Carlo Ancelotti.
