As Anthropic executives gathered inside the Nasdaq MarketSite in Times Square on Thursday morning to ring the opening bell for a historic $81 billion initial public offering, a tightly orchestrated coalition of far-left, socialist, environmental, anti-Israel and Democratic grassroots groups executed a staged street action outside, complete with linked-arm sit-ins, pre-printed banners and rapid-fire social media drops timed to hijack the news cycle.

"This IPO has got to go! Hey, hey, ho, ho," the staged protesters chanted to a small cadre of photographers as the audience.

Industry observers note that "Shame! Shame!" a protester yelled to a few responding "Shame!" in response.

Protesters against rapid artificial intelligence growth demonstrate outside Anthropic headquarters on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, in San Francisco. (Noah Berger/AP Photo).

A close look at Thursday’s demonstration reveals the coordinated protest infrastructure that lawmakers have long warned is engineered to "sow discord" rather than produce meaningful policy solutions.

According to Fox News Digital's analysis, the protest was no organic, grassroots effort. Rather, 14 groups, several of them part of well-funded organizations with estimated combined annual revenues of $43 million, coordinated the protest with a careful run-of-show and media choreography.

A Fox News Digital tracker of the Anthropic protest organizers reveals a well-orchestrated effort across multiple organizations. They represent a spectrum of views from socialists to far-left environmental groups and anti-Israel activists.

CHAOS INC.: COMMUNISTS, ANTIFA, AGITATORS IN THE RED-GREEN ALLIANCE.

A series of socialist, far-left, anti-Israel and radical environmental organizations announce a protest against the Anthropic IPO on Nov. 19, 2026. (Climate Defiance).

Sources indicate that roughly 21 hours before the market opened, around 5:45 p.m. EST on Wednesday, the activist network laid the groundwork online, complete with an "Anthropic IPO Day of Action Toolkit" for actions in New York City and the company's San Francisco headquarters, providing ready-made organizing tips to activists with pre-produced graphics, directions to "comment-storm Anthropic's official pages" and "some ideas for signage.".

Recent findings suggest that in a joint Instagram announcement, posted by Climate Defenders Action and amplified by Climate Defiance and other partner accounts, organizers summoned demonstrators to 43rd Street and Broadway in New York City at 9 a.m. "sharp" on Thursday. Last week, activists from Climate Defiance heckled New York Democratic Rep. Hakeem Jeffries, calling him a "traitor.".

The digital flyer, emblazoned with "STOP AI TECH OLIGARCHS: RALLY AGAINST THE ANTHROPIC IPO," framed the listing as an existential threat to working Americans. Organizers claimed Anthropic was rushing to cash in before an "AI bubble" bursts, arguing that fast-track index inclusion rules would force workers’ 401(k)s and municipal pension funds to buy inflated shares while tech billionaires walk away with billions.

The "Day of Action Toolkit" included sign slogan suggestions like "FREEZE AI," the precise language that landed on one sign in New York City.

On Thursday morning, activists arrived outside Nasdaq’s glass-walled studio at 9 a.m., positioning themselves directly beneath glowing Times Square billboards bearing the ANTHROPIC logo.

One masked demonstrator — captioned on screen with his speech about "an IPO" — argued to onlookers and social media cameras that corporations were rushing public offerings to "receive assets" at the expense of everyday workers .

A review of the organizers' social media highlights the coalition staggered its content releases to dominate algorithmic feeds right as Anthropic shares began live trading around midday:.

The video featured the police van's sirens, fast-cut edits and the staged "perp walk.".

From the "Action Toolkit," the organizers wrote their own narrative: "Every newspaper and talk show in the world will cover this IPO," the organizers at "Quit GPT" wrote.

"If we take action publicly, in the streets, on social media and in Anthropic’s own comments, then this will be swept up in the press story. It could be enough to change the headlines from 'Anthropic delivers best IPO in history' to 'Anthropic IPOs amidst safety outcry.'".

They noted hopefully, "This will be one of our best chances to raise the alarm on Anthropic’s out of control AI development.".
