Recent findings suggest that it’s a Black Friday miracle. After NBC angered fans by not showing the entire halftime performance by the Goo Goo Dolls during the Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills game on Thursday night, the Bills dropped the band’s complete set online.

For their 10-plus-minute performance at Highmark Stadium in Buffalo, the Goo Goo Dolls played “Iris,” “Broadway,” and “Slide.” Instead of airing the entire thing, NBC opted to let Mike Tomlin and Mike Tirico chew the fat about Aaron Rodgers, much to the annoyance of many viewers.

Recent findings suggest that fans in attendance at the game praised the rainy performance, with some going so far as to call it “one of the greatest indicates ever.” NBC ended up running only about 30 seconds of the band’s set on their broadcast. One fan wrote, “Aaron Rodgers ruins one last thing as NBC airs Tomlin interview instead of Goo Goo Dolls concert.”.

According to recent reports, steven Van Zandt wrote on X, “They are a great band. That was badly handled and disrespectful.”.
