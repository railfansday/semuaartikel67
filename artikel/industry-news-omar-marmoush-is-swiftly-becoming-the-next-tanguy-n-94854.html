Luca Williams-Barnett is the talk of Tottenham Hotspur following his stunning first competitive start against Ipswich Town over the weekend.

The 18-year-old was handed his full debut by Roberto De Zerbi at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and he took full advantage by scoring after 15 minutes, before playing a big part in the second goal scored by Dominic Solanke in the second half.

The teenager is clearly a huge talent, and he showed up some of his more senior teammates with his stunning display against the Tractor Boys, which will leave some fearing for their spots in De Zerbi's XI and squad.

Competition for places in the frontline is already fierce and that's even with Savio, Mykhailo Mudryk, Wilson Odobert and Xavi Simons right now out of action through injury, and Richarlison cast aside after failing to secure a summer move away from north London.

James Maddison, Dominic Solanke and Mohammed Kudus were the other players named in the front four alongside Williams-Barnett on Saturday, which left the likes of Dejan Kulusevski and Omar Marmoush on the substitutes' bench.

Marmoush hasn't quite made the impact some were hoping for when he signed for Tottenham in August.

The 27-year-old joined Spurs from Manchester City on loan with an obligation to make the deal permanent for £60million next summer, as Roberto De Zerbi strengthened his attacking department.

In a recent development, but it's fair to suggest that Marmoush hasn't hit the ground running in north London. Indeed, the Egyptian has scored just once in 11 appearances for Spurs, and that came in the 3-1 win against Coventry City last month.

Marmoush has had his fair share of game time as well. Before Saturday's visit of Ipswich Town, he had started in eight of Tottenham's previous nine matches, becoming a regular member in De Zerbi's starting line-up.

In a recent development, but he was dropped down to the bench for the game against Ipswich with Dominic Solanke replacing him up front. Teenager Luca Williams-Barnett was another to start, as he came in for the injured Mathys Tel.

Although Spurs do still look a little toothless at times, Solanke and Williams-Barnett both scored in a 2-0 win against Ipswich, proving De Zerbi's decision to be the right one, and they both deserve to keep their place for the trip to Sunderland.

Sources indicate that where does this leave Marmoush? Right now, he is going to have to fight for his place in De Zerbi's first-choice XI. With Tottenham boasting a strong squad and expected to dip into the market for a new No.9 in January, he has no other choice.

But with Marmoush struggling and not having the desired impact some Tottenham fans wanted, there are fears he could turn out to be the next Tanguy Ndombele, who joined Spurs for a substantial fee and with such huge hopes but didn't perform at the required level.

In a recent development, ndombele arrived at Tottenham for £63m from Lyon in 2019, a then club-record deal. However, the Frenchman flattered to deceive. Prior to his move to Spurs, he was considered as one of the most exciting up-and-coming midfielders in Europe.

He had this impressive swagger about him and his technical attributes were eye-catching, but he did not deliver on a regular basis and flopped in north London, leaving the club in 2024 to sign for Nice on a permanent deal.

Yes, it's early days, but Marmoush could end up like Ndombele if he doesn't improve, given the huge money spent on the ex-Eintracht Frankfurt forward and the expectations Tottenham fans had when he was signed.

In a recent development, marmoush has often been used in the centre forward role by De Zerbi this season, but he may have to use him elsewhere to get the best out of them. That includes shifting him back into the No.10 position when James Maddison is rotated. He can also be used on the left, but with Williams-Barnett, Savinho, Tel and Mykhalio Mudryk all competing for that spot, he is needed centrally.

Tottenham need more from Marmoush, and fast, starting with the trip to Sunderland this weekend.
