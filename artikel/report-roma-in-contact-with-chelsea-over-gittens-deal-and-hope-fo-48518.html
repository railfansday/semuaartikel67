Reports in Italy claim that Roma are interested in a January deal for Chelsea winger Jamie Bynoe-Gittens, that the Giallorossi have been in contact with the Premier League club for over a year, and that Donyell Malen could play a key role in convincing the Englishman to move to the Stadio Olimpico.

Industry observers note that despite scoring more goals than any other side in Serie A so far this season (31), Roma are hoping to strengthen their attacking unit during the January transfer window. Gian Piero Gasperini has been after a new left winger ever since he joined Roma last summer and the latest reports in Italy claim that Gittens is one of their current leading options.

Gittens impressed during his time with Borussia Dortmund, where he scored 12 goals and provided 11 assists in the Bundesliga before completing a move to Chelsea in the summer of 2025 at a cost of around €61m with bonuses included.

Since then, the youngster has found it difficult to cement his place in the Chelsea first team, and as things stand, he is without a Premier League appearance yet in 2026-27 and has only played 55 competitive minutes, which have come in two EFL Cup appearances.

According to reports from the Corriere dello Sport, Roma hope that Gittens will be considered surplus to requirements during the January transfer window.

In a recent development, thursday’s report claims that Roma are interested in picking up Gittens in a similar package to the one that saw them sign Donyell Malen from Aston Villa last January: That would be on loan with an option to buy, potentially an obligation under certain conditions. Napoli are also said to be interested in a January deal for Gittens.

Roma are also hoping that Malen, who spent three-and-a-half years at Borussia Dortmund with Gittens, will help to convince the 22-year-old to join the Giallorossi during the upcoming transfer window.

The Corriere dello Sport claims that Roma have been interested in Gittens for some time, and that they have spoken to Chelesa on several occasions since the summer.

Thursday’s report adds that Nottingham Forest’s Callum Hudson-Odoi could also be an option if a deal for Gittens does not come off.
