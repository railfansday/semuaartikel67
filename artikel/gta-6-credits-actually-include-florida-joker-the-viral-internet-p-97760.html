📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lawrence Sullivan, better known to the online world as the 'Florida Joker', is in GTA 6. After accusing Rockstar Games of using his likeness without consent and then demanding a $10m payout, the two parties have seemingly settled their differences.

Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto 6's earliest controversy came from its very first official trailer, when Sullivan alleged one of the teaser's similarly tattooed characters was modeled after him. The former tattoo model, who first went viral because of a mugshot that shared an uncanny resemblance to Batman's Joker, soon called on Rockstar to pay him $2m. His demand rapidly shot up to $10m, before he dropped all legal threats and politely asked to officially work with the developer.

Rockstar seems to have relented to that last request - the vast GTA 6 credits include none other than one Lawrence Sullivan, listed under the 'Local Population' cast, alongside dozens of other names.

In a recent development, GTA 6 players wasted no time, and promptly found the suspect first teased way back in that original 2023 trailer. In its quest to parody all parts of modern life under capitalism, GTA 6 lets you doomscroll through a social media app called Snapmatic, which flings an endless number of short-form clips and thirst traps at you. If you're lucky enough, or if you scroll for long enough, you'll find a video of Sullivan's in-game counterpart beating up a fake lookalike. He certainly sounds like Sullivan, too.

"Two years y'all been talking crazy about me," he wrote under a recent Instagram post. "Who got the last laugh now?!".

According to recent reports, the GTA 6 credits were illuminating for other reasons, too. We finally know who voices the game's loveable duo, Jason and Lucia, for one. All 34 of the infamously fired Rockstar workers also appear in GTA 6's credits. The controversial move eventually led to accusations of union busting on Rockstar's part, which is presently being investigated as part of an employment tribunal in Scotland.
