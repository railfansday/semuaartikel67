By Joe Tenebruso – Nov 24, 2026 at 7:21PM EST.

Many of your stocks are likely to disappoint you.

It's a sobering statement, but one that's borne out by decades of stock market returns.

In a recent development, research from J.P. Morgan going back to 1980 indicates that two-thirds of the stocks in the Russell 3000, which is designed to track the performance of the largest 3,000 publicly traded U.S. companies, tend to underperform the index.

Worse still, roughly 40% of stocks had negative absolute returns.

Moreover, a report from Trivariate Research suggests that an even larger percentage of companies underperformed in recent years, with just 23% of stocks beating the S&P 500 over the past decade.

In a recent development, at first glance, this data is pretty disheartening. Investing may not be easy, but this research makes it seem nearly impossible to be successful.

The truth, fortunately, is that it's not.

There's a beautiful asymmetry to investing.

It's true that a stock could lose 100% of its value. Enduring such a loss is not pleasant. But a well-chosen stock can gain 200%, 500%, or even 1,000% or more.

This capped downside and uncapped upside is part of the magic of investing. As long as you don't use leverage (such as buying stocks on margin), a few big winners can more than make up for a host of losers.

While the odds may be against you, it's still possible to find stocks that go on to outperform the market averages.

The most valuable suggestion I can give you is to buy shares in the best businesses you can find.

In a recent development, search for companies with the strongest competitive advantages and largest growth opportunities. This includes those with proven business models, beloved brands, superior products and services, and excellent management. Even better if they also possess powerful network effects, high barriers to entry, and sizable switching costs.

Look for the innovators, disruptors, and best of breed. I call these businesses Tier 1 enterprises, and finding just a handful of them can set you up for a lifetime of investment success.

It has been revealed that to achieve those life-changing returns, you'll need to keep them long enough to maximize their impact on your wealth. This is one reason why Warren Buffett claims his "favorite holding period is forever.".

Strive to purchase only shares of businesses you strategy to hold for many years. This will lead you to focus on companies with the strongest competitive positions and widest economic moats, or those you believe will eventually build them.

A stock you thought was a sure winner will fail. Recessions will slow the greatest of growth stories. Bear markets will bring fear, uncertainty, and doubt. Even your own emotions may betray you.

Sources indicate that come what may, don't quit. Steel yourself with the knowledge that the hard times will pass and you will get through them.

Recent findings suggest that learn from your mistakes. Apply the lessons to your future stock selections.

Continuously refine your investing process until you obtain the results you need to provide for yourself and your family and achieve your financial freedom.
