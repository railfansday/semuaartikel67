Marten De Roon, after PSG-Roma (2-2), commented with satisfaction on the team’s performance to Prime Video.

“I’m quite tired. I said I shouldn’t have stood up too long in the locker room or I’d have fallen over (laughs). They made us run. They’re a very strong team that moves a lot, but we were especially good in the second half. We were aggressive and won some balls back.”.

The Giallorossi midfielder spoke about the team’s solidity after going behind: “After the 2-1, we could have loosened up, but we gave it more, we defended in open spaces, and we did very well. Congratulations to the team; it was a great performance.”.

De Roon on the improvements shown after tonight’s match: “A step forward. This is the strongest team in the world, especially after the performances against Real Madrid and Manchester United. It was crucial to get the result. This gives us a lot of confidence.”.

The Giallorossi midfielder concluded on Roma’s merits: “Despite the performance, we need the points to move forward, and today we deserved to do so, and it wasn’t easy against this team.”.

Joao Neves: “Roma are one of the best teams we’ve faced. They press really hard and have lots of quality.”.
