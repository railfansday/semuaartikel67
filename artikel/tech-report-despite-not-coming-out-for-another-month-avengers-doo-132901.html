It might not be out for another month, but Avengers: Doomsday is already on an Oscars finalist list.

Sources indicate that per Variety, Doomsday has made the cut for the visual effects top 20. While the Academy doesn't confirm its top 20, Variety says it has verified the list with sources.

Industry observers note that judging by the trailers released so far, Doomsday is indeed a VFX heavy movie, so it's not a huge surprise that it might make it to the list. The full roundup is below:.

Avengers: Doomsday arrives this December, and it sees Earth's Mightiest Heroes go up against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. How and why Doom and Tony Stark shares the same face is still a mystery for now.

In a recent development, "People wondered, 'Is Doom just a variant of another character? Is this just a shortcut to bringing back another character?' The answer is no. He’s Victor Von Doom," Kevin Feige stated recently, only confusing us further.

The latest trailer for the movie, however, is focused on Steve Rogers, who is returning after retiring in the past in Avengers: Endgame. However, with some timeline confusion, it looks like his situation is far more complicated than it seems.

Sources indicate that doomsday arrives on December 18, while Oscars nominations are unveiled on January 21, 2027. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the upcoming Marvel movies and TV shows.
