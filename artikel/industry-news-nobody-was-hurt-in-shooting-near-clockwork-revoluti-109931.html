📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that "Nobody was hurt" in a shooting last week near the California office of Clockwork Revolution developers InXile, both the studio and local police have confirmed. The incident, which took place on November 11th, is presently being investigated and police have reportedly taken a suspect into custody.

"Officers got there [on November 11th]," Tustin police sergeant Christopher Peterson noted in statement to Aftermath. "They responded to a report of a drive-by shooting there. Located multiple shell casings. Nobody was hurt. Processed the crime scene, and then sometime [Friday] morning, a little before 11 [AM], they took a suspect into custody. And the investigation is still kind of ongoing as to where and why.".

"We’re relieved that no one was hurt at our inXile office," a spokesperson for InXile said in a separate statement. "Our focus is on supporting our team, and we are working closely with law enforcement as they investigate.".

It has been revealed that inXile are set to release steampunk RPG Clockwork Revolution next year. The studio, developers of the likes of Wasteland 3 and The Bard's Tale IV, are one of the few Xbox-owned studios not to have been affected - at least as of writing - by Microsoft's ongoing mass layoffs this year.

Industry observers note that since being announced in 2023, Clockwork Revolution's dripped with Bioshock-ish vibes and popped up semi-regularly at Xbox showcases. "Wield the power of an incredible device to travel back in time, changing the past and rewriting the future of the city you call home: the vibrant steampunk metropolis of Avalon," reads its Steam synopsis. "In this immersive RPG, you'll step into the shoes of Morgan Vanette, a fully customizable player-character and member of the Rotten Row Hooligans. Travel between past and present as you battle the ruthless Lady Ironwood, who has been manipulating time to keep the working class struggling while amassing immense wealth and power.".

Best wishes to the staff at InXile, here's hoping that going forwards they're able to get with making what's looking to be an intriguingly funky game without enduring any more concerning incidents like this one.
