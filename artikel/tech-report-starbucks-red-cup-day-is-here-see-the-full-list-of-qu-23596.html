Industry observers note that according to Starbucks, it’s time to get in the holiday giving spirit. Today (Tuesday, November 17, 2026) is Starbucks Red Cup Day. The annual tradition sees the coffee chain give out free reusable red holiday cups with the purchase of any fall or holiday beverage. This year, the Starbucks Creative Studio decorated the limited-edition 16-ounce cup with a coffee tree branch tied in a red bow. A green and white ribbon wraps around the outside of the cup. How to get the Starbucks Red Cup for free? The 2026 holiday red cup is available at participating Starbucks locations while supplies last. You can also get one while using a Starbucks drive-thru or when ordering delivery on DoorDash, Grubhub or Uber Eats. You can use the red cup (or any of your own drinkware) in the future for 10 cents off your drink. What Starbucks beverages are included in the Red Cup Day deal? The free red cup is only available when purchasing any size of a fall or holiday drink—other than the Starbucks Christmas Blend and Starbucks Reserve Christmas brewed coffee.
