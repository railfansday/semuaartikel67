Recent findings suggest that wolves travel to Lincoln City this weekend looking to extend their unbeaten run to nine games after thumping Derby County 3-0 on Tuesday. Adam Armstrong struck twice against the Rams, while Mateus Mane continued his impressive recent form by opening his account for the season.

It has been revealed that ahead of the trip to Lincoln, Cesar Peixoto has provided the injury latest for his squad. As reported by Inside Wolves, Peixoto was positive about his squad situation, and even revealed how Raul Jimenez is progressing.

Recent findings suggest that “Raul came yesterday to see the doctor, the specialist, and the recuperation is going very, very well; that’s what the specialist noted,” Peixoto said. “I don’t know if the medical department lets me say this, but it’s true! I think it’s not a problem.

“He’s on track; he’s taken his brace off and is one step closer in his recovery.” Jean-Ricner Bellegarde has also recovered from his knock and could be selected in the squad.

Sources indicate that “Everyone is OK,” Peixoto added. “Bellegarde is OK, he has trained with us and he needs time to get to the right fitness, but not injured at the moment. We have only (Ghislain) Konan and Raul (out), the other players trained with us.”.

Wolves are looking very strong in the Championship at the moment. With Armstrong starting to find the net, the problems Peixoto seemed to have in his squad seem to be disappearing.

In a recent development, the striker role had been a major concern coming into the January window. However, the return of Jimenez will feel like a new signing in that regard, especially with Sean Neave and Armstrong finding form.

In a recent development, jimenez had a decent start to this season before his injury and his experience at the top end of the pitch will be useful. If he can find the form that all Wolves fans know he has, then all of a sudden they have some seriously strong options in attack.

According to recent reports, wolves had a slow start to the season this term, but have turned things around in recent weeks. Performances are improving week on week as well results and more importantly, they are becoming a very difficult side to beat.

For side coming in January, they need to show they are an appealing side to join, and as a club pushing for promotion, Wolves are definitely an appealing option.

Peixoto has really made his mark of the Wolves squad this season. With their injury concerns now lightening up, they are in very strong spot to push on in the second half of the campaign.
