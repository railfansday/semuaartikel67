Wiper blades aren’t the most exciting thing to find under the tree, but they’re a lot more useful when a storm rolls in. For Black Friday, a two-pack of Bosch ICON beam wiper blades is down to $45 from $53 on Amazon, a savings of 18 percent. (This deal is for a 22A and 22B pair, so check Amazon’s vehicle fitment tool before ordering.).

The Bosch ICON was named the Top Pick in our windshield wiper blade test. After installing and evaluating the blades for fit, wiping performance, noise, and streaking, we called them “the total package,” praising their quiet operation and even pressure across the windshield.

The ICON’s beam-style design has a low profile that sits flush against the glass, helping maintain consistent contact across the blade. That’s useful in heavy rain, when a streaky or skipping wiper can turn a routine drive into a squinting exercise.

It has been revealed that our testing also found that the blades handled light mist and highway grime well, while their rubber resisted hardening better than budget options. Bosch’s universal adapters made installation straightforward on our test vehicle, too.

Prices can change, but at about $45, the ICONs are a solid opportunity to replace worn blades with a set Road & Track rated highly.
