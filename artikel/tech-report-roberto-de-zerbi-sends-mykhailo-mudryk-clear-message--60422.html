Roberto De Zerbi has noted that Mykhailo Mudryk has his full confidence, but must work hard to change the trajectory of his loan spell at Tottenham after a difficult start to life in north London.

Mudryk joined Spurs on a season-long loan from Chelsea on the final day of the summer transfer window, with the club having a £75million option to buy the Ukrainian international at the end of the season.

Mudryk was suspended from competitive football from November 2024 until July 2026 after testing positive for the banned substance meldonium, but it had been hoped he could resurrect his career under De Zerbi.

The pair worked together during Mudryk’s formative years at Shakhtar Donetsk, with the 25-year-old calling De Zerbi his “football father”.

Mudryk, though, has not got going in a Spurs shirt after suffering an ankle ligament injury in training in September.

He had been targeting a return in Spurs’ next two games, but now appears to have suffered a setback after De Zerbi confirmed he would not be available against Ipswich this weekend.

“No, because the plan was to play Ipswich or Sunderland, or any way to be available,” De Zerbi stated when asked if he would be back to face Ipswich.

“He felt no pain, but not comfortable last week. And they take two weeks more, and step by step we’re going to see the situation.”.

To make matters worse, Mudryk appeared on the Ukrainian podcast ‘Guest of Gordon’ this week and stated that before joining Chelsea in January 2023, he had wanted to join Spurs’ arch-rivals Arsenal before adding that he was using his loan at Spurs to build fitness ahead of a return to Chelsea next summer.

Asked about the comments, De Zerbi mentioned he understood why Spurs fans would be frustrated and asked Mudryk to show his qualities on the pitch.

“Yes, I can understand the fans, but Mudryk is a good guy, he’s a good player,” De Zerbi continued.

Industry observers note that “He suffered a lot for the situation in the last couple of years. He has just one thing to do, show on the pitch his qualities, show on the pitch that now he’s playing for Tottenham. Tottenham is a substantial club, like the other clubs, not less at all.”.

Mudryk has played just 16 minutes in all competitions since signing for Spurs, but he retains De Zerbi’s trust, with the Italian sympathetic to the mental distress the winger suffered during his doping ban.

According to recent reports, “He has a substantial chance to play with us because Tottenham is a big club. He knows very well the coach, he knows very well the style of play. He knows I give him total confidence to show what he is. So to move the focus in the right direction, in a good direction. But I think he’s working hard to recover and to play earlier.”.
