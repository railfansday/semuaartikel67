Tenerife have returned to action for the first time since Spanish footballer Jesus de Miguel was tragically killed in a collision during a game with FC Andorra earlier this month.

Goalkeeper Jesus Owono came flying out of his goal and wiped out De Miguel with a high challenge, which resulted in the striker being admitted to intensive care, where he later died.

Thirteen days on from the incident, Tenerife played their first game since the player's death as they took on AD Ceuta CF in LaLiga Hypermotion action – with the game filled with tributes to the star.

Players gave a floral tribute as they arrived for the match, and gathered outside gate nine of the Heliodoro Rodríguez Lopez Stadium – the shirt number he wore.

Grief-stricken Tenerife stars, meanwhile, took to the field carrying a bouquet of flowers, and placed it at the scene of the incident.

Both sets of players and fans then observed a minute's silence in memory of De Miguel before the game kicked off.

CF Tenerife returned to action for the first time since the death of Jesus de Miguel in a game flooded with tributes.

Both sets of players gathered around the centre circle before the game to pay tribute.

The forward (pictured in 2025) passed away after being on the receiving end of a brutal tackle.

The game was then brought to a pause in the ninth minute, so the players could pay another tribute.

Fans inside the stadium unveiled a tifo which read: 'Jesus de Miguel, 9, eternal blanquiazul', which translates to: 'Jesus de Miguel, 9, eternally white and blue.'.

Other fans held up white and blue cards beside the tifo, with others in the ground applauding. When Tenerife scored, De Miguel's shirt was held in the air.

After the game, which Tenerife won 2-0, both teams gathered in the centre circle to place De Miguel's shirt on a bouquet of flowers, and the fans applauded to bring an end to an emotional day.

The tragedy occurred as the game against Andorra entered the seventh minute of stoppage time, when De Miguel looked set to race away on the counter-attack for Tenerife in search of a winner.

As he watched a long ball drop into his path, the striker was struck in the chest by Owono's flying knee as he attempted to clear the ball near the halfway line.

After initially rolling around on the floor, De Miguel became motionless, and medical staff rushed onto the pitch to attend to him.

Recent findings suggest that owono was initially given a red card for the challenge before the decision was soon taken by the referee to abandon the fixture. Fans have chanted 'murderer' in his direction.
