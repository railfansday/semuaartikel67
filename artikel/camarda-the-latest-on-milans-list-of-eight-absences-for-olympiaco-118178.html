Francesco Camarda is the latest Milan player to be ruled out of Milan’s upcoming Europa League clash against Olympiacos after picking up an ankle problem in training according to Wednesday’s reports.

Milan are due to take on Olympiacos in Athens on Thursday evening and will have to do so without eight of their first-team players.

Luka Modric is expected to be unavailable with a muscular issue, as are Strahinja Pavlovic, Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Alexis Saelemaekers and Ruben Loftus-Cheek.

Camarda is the latest player to be added to the Milan injury list after suffering an ankle issue and is unlikely to be called up for the Olympiacos clash as a result.

The Rossoneri are in for a tough test away against the Greek side on Thursday night: The hosts are in sixth place ahead of kick-off and remain unbeaten after their first four matches in the competition, with three wins and one draw totalling 10 points.

Milan, meanwhile, are presently outside of the knock-out round qualification spots, all the way down in 28th place after one win and three defeats, against Benfica, Bournemouth and Ferencvaros, from their first four matches.
