According to recent reports, TL;DR: Lego and F1 have announced a 2027 Lego Speed Champions Formula 1 collection featuring brick-built replicas of all 11 team cars and all 22 F1 drivers recreated as minifigures. Check out the full range.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is always a special experience, but Lego is adding some brick-built magic this year.

Lego and Formula 1 have confirmed the next phase of their partnership: a 2027 Lego Speed Champions Formula 1 collection featuring brick-built replicas of all 11 team cars and 22 official F1 drivers recreated as Lego minifigures.

The full collection hits shelves globally on March 1, 2027, with preorders live from Nov. 19 in select regions.

Recent findings suggest that to celebrate the launch, Lego is staging its inaugural Lego Race on the Las Vegas Strip circuit on Nov. 21, putting a mystery lineup of drivers behind the wheel of life-size, drivable 28,000-brick F1 minicars ahead of the main Grand Prix. The Lego Races were absolutely wild in Miami and Silverstone. Here's hoping for more of the same in Vegas.

It has been revealed that the biggest pull of this newly introduced launch is the minifigures. Previously, Lego's F1 Speed Champions collections nailed the cars, but the cockpits were always staffed by unnamed helmeted drivers. Turning the actual grid into collectible minifigures fixes the single biggest complaint from F1 fans and collectors.

It has been revealed that while preorders are open in select regions, none of these sets will arrive in time for the 2026 holiday gifting window — the collection doesn't officially launch until March 1, 2027. That's a tough wait, but at least we can all check out this weekend's Lego Race before the Las Vegas GP.
