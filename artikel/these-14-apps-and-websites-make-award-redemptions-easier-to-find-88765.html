These 14 apps and websites make award redemptions easier to find

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (189 words, 📖 Standard)
  • A range of apps and websites now streamline the process of finding hotel and flight award availability, helping travelers maximize points and miles for trips in 2027 and beyond.
  • These tools offer features such as real-time award searches, alerts for seat and room availability, side-by-side cash and points pricing, and recommendations for optimal redemptions.
  • Options include both free and paid memberships, with premium plans unlocking advanced search capabilities, more frequent alerts, and customizable valuations.
  • Many platforms support multiple airline and hotel loyalty programs, allowing users to compare options and plan redemptions efficiently.
  • Most tools offer basic features for free, but advanced functions like multiple alerts or broader search windows often require a paid subscription.
  • Some platforms provide exclusive discounts or trial periods for new users, and certain features may be limited to specific loyalty programs or travel brands.
  • Always confirm award availability directly with the airline or hotel before transferring points, as availability can change rapidly.
