It has been revealed that JACKSONVILLE, Fla., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Newfold Digital, a leading web and commerce technology organization serving millions of small businesses worldwide, today declared the appointment of David Eblen as Chief Product Officer of Network Solutions Group.

Eblen joins Sarah Hartline Morrison, who was appointed Chief Marketing Officer of Network Solutions Group in October. Together, they will help advance a clear strategy for two established brands with distinct roles. Network Solutions focuses on digital identity, trust and protection, helping businesses establish and safeguard their presence online. Domain.com makes it simple for entrepreneurs to claim their name and establish an online presence. As AI transforms how customers discover and engage with businesses, both brands are evolving to help entrepreneurs build trusted digital identities and grow.

Industry observers note that "We're entering a latest era where a business's digital identity and the trust behind it matter more than ever," noted Sachin Puri, Chief Executive Officer of Newfold Digital. "Network Solutions has spent decades helping businesses establish that identity, and now we have an opportunity to redefine what it means in an AI-powered world. David brings deep product expertise and a track record of building and scaling technology platforms. Together with Sarah, he'll help us accelerate innovation, simplify the customer experience, and turn our vision for Network Solutions Group into reality.".

David Eblen, Chief Product Officer, Network Solutions Group.

Eblen brings more than 20 years of product leadership in hosting, cloud and online software and services. Most recently, he served as Chief Product Officer at Extra Duty Solutions, a SaaS platform provider for public safety agencies. Before that, he spent six years at Liquid Web as Chief Product Officer and Senior Vice President, Product Innovation and Delivery, where he led product and UX across the company's cloud business.

It has been revealed that earlier, Eblen spent nearly a decade at Rackspace Technology in San Antonio, where he oversaw a portfolio spanning ERP, eCommerce, productivity tools and CRM platforms, including Office 365 offerings. He holds multiple bachelor's and master's degrees from Texas A&M University and The University of Texas at San Antonio and is based in San Antonio.

As Chief Product Officer, Eblen will lead product for Network Solutions Group, including the domains, websites, email and online tools small businesses rely on to get online and grow. He will shape the group's product vision, roadmap and user experience and work closely with Morrison and the organization's technology teams to bring Network Solutions' products, brand and customer experience together.

I'm excited to work with this team to build intuitive products that make it easier for businesses to establish their identity, earn customer trust, and grow.".
