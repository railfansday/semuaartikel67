Last night, Taylor Swift made a glamorous appearance at the CMAs, stunning us not only with a performance that mashed up “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song,” but also with her red-carpet look. Her hair was pulled back into an effortless updo, she traded her beloved bold red lip for a warmer-toned shade, and she topped it all off with a glossy burgundy manicure.

The deep red is pretty much the perfect late-fall nail color. It feels festive enough for Thanksgiving and will carry you straight through the holiday season. Plus, it's also an easy shade to wear, no matter your nail shape and length. Swift opted for short, softly rounded square nails, but the color would look just as elevated on a longer almond or oval shape, too. She even carried the hue through to her toes, opting for a matching pedicure to complete the look.

Recent findings suggest that it's a shade that goes with practically anything in your closet. Swift wore hers with a bright yellow gown, but on the off chance that you don’t have a red-carpet event coming up (stars, they’re just like us!) you can easily wear the glossy burgundy with a cozy knit sweater or your favorite winter coat. It's a fall classic for a reason.

And while Swift tends to keep her go-to beauty products under wraps (we can only speculate about her favorite cat-eye liner or the exact red lipstick tubes in her rotation), we don't know which polish she wore.
