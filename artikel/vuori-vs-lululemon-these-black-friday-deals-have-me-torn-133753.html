WHEN IT COMES to athleisure, I’m usually torn between two brands: Vuori and Lululemon. Both have become staples in my wardrobe, whether I’m heading to the gym or catching a flight. And I don’t care what anyone states about dressing up for the airport—I’m always going to travel in sweats. Comfort is nonnegotiable when I’m sitting on a plane for hours, and both brands make some of the best joggers and sweatshirts I’ve put on my body. But what I appreciate most is that neither brand is limited to workout clothes. They’ve got plenty of everyday pieces, from sleek trousers and versatile jackets to tees and button-downs, that look just as fly outside the gym.

According to recent reports, now that the Black Friday deals have arrived, there’s even more reason to shop both brands. Vuori and Lululemon are rolling out almost 50 percent off deals on everything from performance gear to casual wardrobe staples, so it’s a great time to refresh your workout rotation or grab something comfortable for your next trip. But which brand has the better Black Friday deals? I’ve rounded up the styles worth shopping from both to help you decide where to spend your money.
