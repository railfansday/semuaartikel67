Industry observers note that it’s almost here: Anthropic is expected to go public on Thursday, November 19, and its IPO share price has been set well above the company’s target range, a sign that demand for the stock is strong. The AI giant, best known for its LLM Claude, is in the middle of the biggest—or at least the most-hyped—initial public offering since SpaceX went public in a record stock listing earlier this year. Late Wednesday, Anthropic confirmed that it priced shares at $83, above earlier estimates of $70 to $78 per share. Anthropic’s offering consists of 980 million shares of Class A common stock, with the company selling roughly 956.4 million shares itself and certain stockholders selling about 23.6 million. The Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, and other media outlets reported the $83 share price earlier in the day. A lot is hinging on how well Anthropic’s stock performs in the public markets on Thursday as AI-related stocks have seen a significant pullback in the wake of escalating “AI bubble” fears compounded by the recent ouster of Nvidia’s Jensen Huang. Here’s what to know as Anthropic’s stock is about to commence trading. About Anthropic Anthropic, PBC (Nasdaq: ANT) is an artificial intelligence public benefit corporation based in San Francisco. It was established in January 2021 by a group of seven cofounders who left now-rival OpenAI over safety and direction concerns and disagreements. That includes Dario Amodei, CEO; Daniela Amodei, president; Tom Brown, chief compute officer; Jack Clark, head of public benefit; Jared Kaplan, chief science officer; Sam McCandlish, chief architect; and Chris Olah, interpretability research lead. Anthropic’s primary product is its Claude LLM, which itself has multiple tiers, and other features such as its AI assistant, its coding tool Claude Code, and more. What’s particularly notable about Anthropic’s IPO is that it may be considered one of the first and most valuable pure-play AI IPOs—meaning that Anthropic’s primary function and market is AI, while many other companies in the space (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, etc.) have broader business operations that include AI. Also notable is the company’s prospectus, which includes more than 90 pages of risk factors that investors should take into consideration. That list, it’s worth pointing out, also includes “existential risks” to humanity. When is Anthropic’s IPO? Anthropic shares are expected to start trading on November 19, when the stock market opens at 9:30 a.m. ET. The stock will trade under the ticker “ANT,” which was already at the top of Yahoo’s trending stock tickers list on Wednesday, despite not actively trading yet. A countdown clock on the ANT ticker page alerts customers to when trading is expected to begin—down to the second (although the actual time may differ). Anthropic shares will trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. What is the IPO share price of ANT? The initial public offering price for ANT shares is $83, higher than Anthropic’s projected range of $70 to $78 per share. How many ANT shares are available in its IPO? Anthropic’s initial public offering will include the sale of 980 million shares of its Class A common stock, above an earlier estimate of 825 million. The lead underwriters for Anthropic’s IPO are Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, per its prospectus. How much is Anthropic expected to raise in its IPO? Anthropic’s valuation is now as high as $1.5 trillion, Bloomberg estimates. That puts it on the cusp of the top 10 largest companies in the world by market capitalization; it could be worth more than Walmart and Berkshire Hathaway, for instance, and roughly on par with Tesla and Meta. Assuming it does reach a valuation of $1.5 trillion, Anthropic would be one of 15 public companies worth at least $1 trillion. What are analysts saying? As noted, Anthropic is the biggest pure-play AI IPO to come along, which has investors chomping at the bit. But it’s also rife with risks, as outlined in its prospectus and filing statements. The company is definitely driving revenue: Its S-1 filing highlights that through the first nine months of 2026, Anthropic saw revenue of $33.2 billion, up more than 1,200% year over year. Analysts generally have a rosy view of the stock ahead of the IPO. New Street Research rated it a “buy,” and analyst Pierre Ferragu called it a “generational IPO,” according to CNBC. KGI Securities likewise rated the stock as “buy,” and gave it a price target of $105, significantly higher than the pre-IPO range. Analysts at Morningstar set a target of $92 per share, also seeing the stock as “undervalued” ahead of trading. Disclosure: Morningstar was founded by Fast Company’s owner, Joe Mansueto.
