Better Than Araujo: Man Utd Already Know Priority First Signing For Pochettino

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that manchester United are in dire need of a newly introduced left-back in January. Lisandro Martinez was used in that position for the 2-2 draw at Sporting CP on Wednesday, after Diogo Dalot was dropped following another dismal performance in the 4-0 thrashing at Liverpool.

In a recent development, luke Shaw is nearing a return to full fitness, however, and is expected to take back that spot soon, after missing the last seven weeks with a muscle problem. Despite that, Shaw has struggled even when available and United should look to upgrade in the transfer window.

According to recent reports, according to Sport Witness, United are showing interest in Sporting left-back Maxi Araujo. It's understood the Reds have been monitoring the Uruguay international for a number of months now, and, of course, saw him up close at the Estadio Jose Alvalade on Wednesday.

Recent findings suggest that araujo has a £67million release clause in his contract, but United could get him for less in January. As well as that, he is believed to be 'very open' about making the move to Old Trafford and has previously expressed his desire to play in the Premier League.

Industry observers note that it could be a smart signing, but United should avoid Araujo and instead target someone else. While the 26-year-old did win Sporting's second spot-kick as he was fouled by Senne Lammens and was an attacking threat, he has some big flaws to his game.

Indeed, in the draw against United, Araujo lost possession NINE times, completing just 86 per cent of his passes (38/46) and having one out of four successful dribbles. He didn't make a single accurate pass into the box and completed just one successful cross.

In a recent development, alongside that, Araujo's defending flattered to deceive. He won just 42 per cent (8/19) of his duels, one tackle and committed three fouls. The ex-Puebla and Toluca man started as a left wing-back and often got caught out with Amad Diallo having joy on that side.

While he can be strong going forward, Araujo does struggle defensively and has proven that on a number of occasions this season. None more so than in the 3-2 defeat to LASK in the Champions League last month, where he could have done better for all three goals. He didn't stop the cross for LASK's first, while both the second and third came down his side.

Considering that and the fact United have conceded the most goals (36) in Europe's top five leagues across all competitions this season, the Reds need a more defensive-minded left-back and Newcastle United's Lewis Hall would be perfect.

It has been revealed that the Athletic have reported that United remain interested in Hall, and although it could be difficult for them to persuade Newcastle to sell in January, the Reds need to do all they can to sign him. The England international is one of the best left-sided defenders in the Premier League right now and would be an exceptional addition, even if he is expensive.

Industry observers note that defensively, there are not too many better than Hall about. He produced an impressive performance to keep Lamine Yamal quiet in England's famous 3-2 win against Spain earlier this month, and has been a nemesis for many a right-winger in recent times.

He can attack as well. Indeed, in 113 appearances for Newcastle, Hall has directly contributed to 13 goals. Earlier this season, Hall found the net in a 2-1 win at Hull City and grabbed an assist in a 2-2 draw at home against Bournemouth.

Sources indicate that united should go all out to bring Hall to Old Trafford. He should be their first-choice target with Araujo and Antonee Robinson behind. Despite their league position, the Reds should be targeting the best in class - and Hall is just that.

According to recent reports, former Newcastle boss Eddie Howe heaped praise on the "outstanding" Hall last season, despite leaving him out of the starting line-up to face Arsenal. “Lewis is an outstanding player and you never for any second doubt his qualities and what he can bring to the team," Howe said, when asked the reason behind Dan Burn's inclusion in the team at left-back.

Meanwhile, Pochettino worked with Hall for a brief period at Chelsea and wished him well as he moved to Newcastle. Acknowledging his potential, the Argentine said: “He was unlucky that he could not play [more], but he has a good future and I hope he can settle well there.”.

Thomas Tuchel hailed Hall's progress during the recent international break, saying he made a "massive impression" on the England team.
