Why Potts swapped West Ham for Club Brugge after 16 years

Freddie Potts has started all of Club Brugge's Champions League games.

When Liverpool visit Club Brugge on Wednesday they are likely to come up against English midfielder Freddie Potts.

The 23-year-old ended a 16-year association with West Ham in the summer to join the Belgian champions for an undisclosed fee.

And he has started every game, bar one missed through suspension, in all competitions.

After losing to Aston Villa in the Champions League, they have defeated Inter Milan, Lens and PSV.

"It really is a dream because you hear the music and then you see your family in the stands and you remember all the times that you watched Champions League on TV," Potts told BBC Radio 5 Live's Euro Leagues Show.

"You think of all the memories that you make watching it and to be playing in it now is something that every kid dreams of.".

In a recent development, potts' whole life had been based around West Ham. He had been with the club since the age of six and his father Steve, who made 506 appearances for the Hammers, is a first-team coach.

Potts Jr played 28 times for West Ham, with 25 of them coming last season as they were relegated from the Premier League.

He also had loan spells with Wycombe in League One and Portsmouth in the Championship.

"In pre-season my minutes with West Ham were limited, they weren't as much as I wanted and then I spoke with my agent and stated 'let's explore options because this season I'm 22, turning 23'," Potts said.

In a recent development, "It's a massive part of my career and I wanted to play. The opportunity of Club Brugge came along with Champions League football, the best club in Belgium and it was an opportunity I couldn't really turn down with how my minutes were going down with West Ham.

It has been revealed that "I grew up in the area, going to the stadium watching the games. It was always going to be a tough decision but it was something that I had to do for my career.

"It was an emotional decision because it had to be made quite quickly. It's been the right decision for me and so far I'm enjoying it, I'm loving life here.".

Club Brugge won the Belgian title last season and are second, just one point behind leaders Union Saint-Gilloise this campaign.

It has been revealed that "It's a physical league," said Potts. "My running and physical stats have been higher than they've ever been. It's a demanding league in that way.

Sources indicate that "Club Brugge demands a lot, from winning the ball back and going man for man in the press. And your responsibility off the ball is something that you have to take really seriously.".

Freddie Potts on leaving West Ham, life in Belgium and beating Inter Milan.

Potts says he has not started to learn French or Flemish and that team talks are done in English, with the side managed by Croat Ivan Leko.

And he revealed he has a lifelong history with the city.

"The whole move itself is funny because we used to visit Brugge when I was a kid," he said.

"We used to come around Christmas, so my family all know the city. And now I'm here they come out as much as they can.

Recent findings suggest that "It's a great city with a lot of culture. It's brilliant. You won't find many places like it.".

Next up for the side is the visit of Liverpool this week - with both clubs on nine points after four games.

The Reds are eighth and Club Brugge are ninth in the table.

"Everyone knows Liverpool as a club, the size and the Premier League teams," noted Potts, who started for West Ham at home to the Reds last season.

Industry observers note that "It's going to be a major game. We're level on points in the Champions League and we've put big performances in this competition and we're hoping that we're going to do the same.".
