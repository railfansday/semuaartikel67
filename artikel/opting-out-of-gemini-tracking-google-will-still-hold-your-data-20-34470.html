Opting out of Gemini tracking? Google will still hold your data 20x longer

From 72 hours to 2 months in a single quiet announcement. Careful what you ask Google's AI, because it's storing it even if you ask it not to.

Opting out of Gemini chat retention is about to mean a lot less thanks to a quietly revealed Google patch.

Google on Monday remarked that it was making changes to Gemini chat history that, at first glance, makes it seem like consumers are simply getting more control over their discussions with the Chocolate Factory’s chatbot.

At the core of the announcement is Google's splitting of Gemini chat history settings from AI model training permissions. What that means is that users will now be able to store their chat histories with Gemini without also having to opt in to allow their conversations to be used to train those same models, as was previously the case.

So far so good: Being able to have Gemini remember more of your past interactions to contextualize newer discussions is nice, to be sure. The second part of the announcement, however, might raise one's privacy hackles.

According to recent reports, "Now, if you turn your fresh History setting off, future chats will be saved for around two months," Google notes. "They're used to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public.".

Google said it is making that change in the name of making Gemini safer and more secure, but it fails to mention that the chat retention period for users who turned their history off had been just 72 hours before this enhancement.

That's right: Prior to Monday's change, Gemini users who turned off the "Keep Activity" option only had their chats retained for three days, according to an archived copy of the same page that now states that choosing the same please-don't-store-my-AI-chats option means Google keeps them "for around two months.".

It's also worth noting that, even if users have the option to not store Gemini chat records enabled, submitting a feedback request on any Gemini post means that temporary retention period gets extended to three years. That was the case both before and after Monday's change, mind you, but for the privacy-conscious it's definitely key to note.

Sending feedback to Google about a Gemini response also means that humans will be reviewing that particular conversation, as well as any associated files and conversations that can be associated with the one in question. Submitting feedback also means that Google assumes the right to use the conversation and its associated content to improve its services and improve its AI models.

It has been revealed that in other words, even if you opt out of having your conversations stored, your Gemini history could still end up being used to feed the Google machine. The manufacturer didn't respond to questions for this story. ®.
