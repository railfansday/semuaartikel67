Fulham manager Alvaro Arbeloa has no concerns over Calvin Bassey leaving the club in the January transfer window despite interest from Manchester United.

Mauricio Pochettino has arrived at Old Trafford tasked with lifting the club from their latest malaise and will be backed in January with a defender and a striker needed.

Bassey has emerged as a target at the back with United’s defence in disarray this season, conceding 19 goals in 10 appearances.

According to recent reports, bassey’s current contract at Craven Cottage expires in the summer with Fulham holding the option to extend that by a further 12 months. The Cottagers are also in active talks with their captain over a newly introduced contract which would effectively put an end to United’s interest.

Fulham have no intention of selling the former Rangers star with Arbeloa describing him as one of the best defenders ‘in the world’.

Sources indicate that ‘For me, I am going to be really clear. He’s very, very key,’ the Fulham boss said.

‘He is one of my captains. From the first days when I arrived I saw how good he is.

‘I think he is one of the best centre backs in the world, so it’s normal if there are so many teams that wants Calvin, that for me is normal.

‘I’m not worried about that because I am really happy with Calvin. I think Calvin is really happy here. He is so important for us. He is one of our leaders.’.
