What is Palisociety? Your guide to one of Marriott’s newest boutique collections

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (184 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Palihotel Hollywood is a boutique property in Los Angeles that emphasizes unique design, neighborhood character and a personalized guest experience.
  • The hotel is part of the Palisociety collection, which recently partnered with a major hotel loyalty program, allowing members to earn and redeem points at these properties.
  • Each Palisociety hotel features distinct decor and amenities reflecting its local community, with Palihotel Hollywood showcasing vintage Hollywood charm and thoughtful in-room touches.
  • While guests can use loyalty points for stays and receive some elite benefits, certain standard perks from the loyalty program are not available at these locations.
  • Room upgrades and late checkout are subject to availability and not guaranteed for elite members at these properties.
  • Benefits such as lounge access, nightly upgrade awards, discounted member rates and mobile check-in are excluded for these hotels.
  • Nightly rates at Palihotel Hollywood start around $185 or 43,000 points per night as of the article's publication.
