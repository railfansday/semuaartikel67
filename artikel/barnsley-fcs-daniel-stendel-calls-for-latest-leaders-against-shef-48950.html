The Reds were in a run of nine League One games without a win, so to get one over the Owls in front of the live television cameras would be a huge boost, but with Cameron McGeehan suspended and Luca Connell one of those laid low by illness, they will have to do it the hard way.

Coach Daniel Stendel is calling for new leaders to emerge.

In the absence of captain McGeehan, Connell will be the next up to lead the team but Stendel stated the midfielder is "one of the players we have to look at" after sickness in the camp.

"We have some people with a bit of illness this week. We have to see how fit they are for Friday," explained Stendel.

"We will not just decide after training today (Thursday) but also the feeling in the night and tomorrow morning before we can really say which players will start tomorrow.

"Cameron was ill this week, he's not in. It is what it is.

"PK (Patrick Kelly) was away with the (Northern Irish) international team.

"It's also a big chance to build a newly introduced responsibility in the squad. Some players have to take responsibility, it doesn't matter their ages or whether they've played 500 games.

According to recent reports, "You have the character to take responsibility or not. I can see we have some players in the squad who have it.

"It's part of my work to look at which players can take the team to another level, especially when you think in the long-term to improve and develop a team that has a chance to play in the top of the league.

"We have to look at which players are ready for that and are able to take more responsibility.

"It's a big chance to make a big step in this direction.".

Ivorian striker Ben Hamed Toure has trained this week, although Stendel was cautious about whether he might throw him back in, having not played since September because of a dislocated shoulder.
