Dsquared2 Pre-Fall 2027

🌐 Source: Vogue • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (247 words, 📖 Standard)

Dean and Dan Caten set out to design a rebel aristocrat’s wardrobe for pre-fall. Rigorous military uniforms and tailored pieces clashed with punk and glam rock references. Often the designers used “callbacks from previous Dsquared2 collections” as a starting point.

In a recent development, for menswear that meant a leather-effect coated biker jacket and ripped denim pants with rocker vibes. Workwear proposals with paint splatters were juxtaposed against pinstriped pants and blazers hybridized with components of other garments. Uniforms had a moment as well. A peacoat with gold buttons, a blue Napoleon jacket with contrasting red piping, and a red tuxedo jacket with black lapels were all in the mix, their severity softened by double shirts held together with safety pins or distressed knitwear. Brooches created from logo-ed keys and crystals completed the picture.

A “Kate Moss attitude” characterized the women’s looks, with outerwear taking center stage. Distressed denim jackets were reworked with couture-like balloon-shaped backs, while a red leather jacket became more sculptural thanks to a metal wire strategically placed inside the hems and the collar. The same effect was used on a peacoat with a fur hood. Napoleon jackets were turned out in denim, giving them an everyday aura that was undercut by the Victorian lingerie-inspired garments paired with them. The mashups could be spotted within women’s as well; see the tweed inserts applied to a canvas parka or the workwear blouson accented with the hems of a red puffer jacket.
