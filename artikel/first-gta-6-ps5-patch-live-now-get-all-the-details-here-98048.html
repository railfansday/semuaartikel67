Original Story: Rockstar Games has today published the first GTA 6 post-launch update on PS5, bringing its open worlder up to version number 01.002.002.

It's a small 603.8MB patch on PS5, and a 1.6GB download on Xbox Series X|S consoles. However, you might not be able to download it right away on PS5. It appears the developer is utilising a rollout system where owners of the game receive the patch in waves.

Industry observers note that according to Rockstar dataminer Tex2, the upgrade is live right now for 33% of GTA 6 owners on PS5. Another third of the title's userbase gets the patch in another few hours, and then everyone will have it by the end of the day. This is apparently to avoid server overload.

No patch notes have been shared by Rockstar Games yet, but when they are, I'll revision this article with further details.

There have been some small technical issues GTA 6 players have encountered since launch, like a PS5 error code, and now a upcoming glitch on Xbox Series X|S which runs gameplay at double the speed.
