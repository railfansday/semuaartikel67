Apple this week revealed the additional Siri AI usage you’ll get if you have an iCloud+ subscription. This ranges from doubled limits for a 200GB proposal to 20 times for a 12TB plan.

However, the company’s support document reveals that some countries will not get access to these increased usage allowances ….

Apple hasn’t yet specified the standard limits applicable to Siri AI usage, instead saying that these will vary depending on anything from the feature used to the overall level of demand at the time. However, it did list the proportional increases you get with paid iCloud aims.

Industry observers note that however, tucked away in the small print in the manufacturer’s support document are some exclusions. One of them is no surprise: EU countries.

According to recent reports, siri AI isn’t available at all in the EU due to Apple’s perception of potential antitrust concerns. Essentially, Apple believes that if it launches Siri AI there, it will be required to give competing AI services the same level of access to its devices.
