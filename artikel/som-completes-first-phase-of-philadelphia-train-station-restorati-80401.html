SOM completes first phase of Philadelphia train station restoration

Architecture studio SOM has completed the first phase of the renovation of the William H Gray III 30th Street Station in Philadelphia, USA, “breathing new life” into the 1930s beaux arts main train hall.

According to recent reports, the project saw the renovation of the main train hall, a beaux arts Graham, Anderson, Probst & White building completed in 1931 with a grand limestone facade and Corinthian columns. It serves as a major transit hub for the Northeastern United States.

The hall is on the National Register of Historic Places, greatly restricting the changes that can be made to the structure and its interiors.

Keeping to the historic details, including the art deco chandeliers that hang from the tall ceilings, SOM restored the space with builder Gilbane through renovation and a series of specific interventions.

Chief among these was the moving of ticketing, bag drop-off, and other facilities that had been placed in the main train hall into a little-used northern wing of the building.

“Our restoration and repositioning presents an extraordinary opportunity to leverage the station’s strengths, while breathing latest life into this beloved building,” remarked SOM Partner Colin Koop.

This move freed up more room for seating and through traffic in the main hall.

Working with retail partner Nelson Worldwide, SOM also revamped the concessions in the south concourse.

SOM worked closely with US rail system operator Amtrak on the project, which includes offices for Amtrak in the upper levels of the building.

In a recent development, it is part of a wider renovation SOM declared for the station and surrounding areas in Philadelphia in 2016, which included a number of tall buildings. The status of these upcoming structures is unclear.

SOM noted the efforts in preserving the character of the structure.

“We are preserving its historic character while positioning the station for the next generation of travelers to come,” said SOM Principal Phil Wessell.

“As part of our broader work along Amtrak’s Northeast Corridor, this revitalization reinforces the station’s role as both a vital transportation hub and a defining civic space for Philadelphia.”.

SOM also led the renovation of Daniel Patrick Moynihan Train Hall in Newly introduced York in 2021, with plans for a Penn Station renovation released earlier this year by PAU.
