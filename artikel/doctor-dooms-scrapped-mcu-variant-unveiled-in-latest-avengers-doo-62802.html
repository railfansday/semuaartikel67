A scrapped version of Doctor Doom has surfaced online ahead of Avengers: Doomsday's release.

Sources indicate that robert Downey Jr. is set to make his MCU return as Victor von Doom in Avengers: Doomsday. This newly introduced role is a complete 180 for the actor as he goes from the heroic Tony Stark, also known as Iron Man, to the infamous villain Doctor Doom. His casting was unveiled at San Diego Comic-Con in 2024, exciting fans of the actor who are excited to see him bring this classic comic book villain to life on the substantial screen.

Sources indicate that A newly introduced image of Doctor Doom has emerged ahead of Avengers: Doomsday, offering Marvel Cinematic Universe fans a look at an alternate costume that was reportedly dropped. Although Downey Jr.'s iteration of the villain has already appeared in promo material for the highly anticipated crossover, one of the original designs offers a completely different take on the character.

A new series of images features an upcoming Lord Doom collectible figure, which is based on concept art created for Avengers: Doomsday. Instead of depicting the villain's official design in the upcoming superhero film, the figure was inspired by an alternative variant that really hones in on his role as the ruler of Latveria.

In a recent development, the collectible features Doom wearing a dark green cloak with his iconic hood. The costume also has gold buttons, chains, and a huge belt. His jacket includes a Latverian logo. However, despite the different outfit, the action figure of the character is still wearing his infamous metallic mask.

Although the collectible is officially associated with Avengers: Doomsday, its appearance should not be seen as Doctor Doom's final costume in the movie. The action figure is specifically based on concept art instead of what the character will actually look like in the upcoming film.

It has been revealed that this is not the first time concept art of Lord Doom has appeared in merchandise for Avengers: Doomsday. Hasbro previously released a Marvel Legends action figure featuring a similar design, as well as Funko! Pop's version of the character. Both toys were also described as being inspired by concept art from the film.

According to recent reports, directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, Avengers: Doomsday brings together a star-studded lineup of some of Marvel's most iconic actors, even from films that predate the MCU, such as the original X-Men. The massive ensemble cast includes Patrick Stewart (Professor X), Ian McKellen (Magneto), James Marsden (Cyclops), Rebecca Romijn (Mystique), Kelsey Grammer (Beast), Alan Cumming (Nightcrawler), Chris Evans (Steve Rogers), Hayley Atwell (Peggy Carter), Chris Hemsworth (Thor), Anthony Mackie (Sam Wilson), Paul Rudd (Ant-Man), Simu Liu (Shang-Chi), Tom Hiddleston (Loki), Winston Duke (M'Baku), and more.

Avengers: Doomsday will be released in theaters on December 18, 2026.
