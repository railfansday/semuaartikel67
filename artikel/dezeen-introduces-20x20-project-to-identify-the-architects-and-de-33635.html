Today is Dezeen’s 20th birthday, and to celebrate we launch our 20×20 project in partnership with Kohler.

For the series we’ve asked 20 architects and designers who have had a major impact over the past 20 years to each select an architect or designer that they believe will have the biggest impact over the next 20 years.

Called 20×20, the project aims to celebrate the past 20 years of Dezeen while looking forward to the names and faces we’ll be seeing much more of in the next 20 years.

We’ll be revealing the selected architects and designers on Dezeen over the next four weeks, getting to know them and their work in a series of interviews.

The series launches today with a profile of Russian-born, Paris-based designer Harry Nuriev, who was selected by Danish architect Bjarke Ingels.

He will be followed by the selections of Es Devlin, Faye Toogood, Francine Houbine, Frida Escobedo, Huberto Campana, Iris van herpen, Jeanne Gang, Kelly Wearstler, Lesley Lokko, Ma Yansong, Marc Newson, Mariam Kamara, Marina Tabassum, Nendo, Neri&Hu, Patricia Urquiola, Sabine Marcelis, Sou Fujimoto and Yasmeen Lari.
