Pay Less for Amazon Prime – Get a Half-Price Blueprint or Share One Membership

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Amazon Prime costs about half as much for anyone 18 to 24 or in college and for people on SNAP, Medicaid, or other qualifying aid, so check those looks before you pay $139 a year. If another adult in your home also pays full price, cancel one membership and share the one you keep through Amazon Family.

Both discounted intends carry the same core benefits as regular Prime, so paying the full $14.99 a month instead of $7.49 or $6.99 costs $90 to $96 more a year.

Industry observers note that prime Access costs $6.99 a month after a 30-day free trial for newly introduced members. It’s open to people on SNAP, Medicaid, Social Security disability, or one of 10 other assistance programs, including public housing and energy aid, and to anyone who passes Amazon’s income check. You upload proof, such as your benefits card or eligibility letter, and reverify every 12 months.

On regular Prime or Prime for Young Adults, pay monthly only if you’ll keep the membership nine months or fewer, such as just for the holidays. Ten months of regular Prime at $14.99 cost $149.90, more than the $139 yearly price, so switch to yearly billing once you know you’ll keep it.

In a recent development, amazon Family, which replaced Amazon Household, lets a Prime member share free delivery, Prime Video, Amazon Music, and member deals with one other adult in the household, who keeps a separate Amazon account. Two adults paying $139 each can cancel one membership and save $139 a year.

Prime for Young Adults and free trials can’t be shared, so the shared membership has to be regular Prime or Prime Access. And the member who shares Prime must also share a payment method, which the other adult can use for purchases, so set this up only with someone you’d trust with it.

In a home where either adult qualifies for Prime Access, keep that membership, since it covers both of you for $83.88 a year, $55.12 less than one regular annual membership.
