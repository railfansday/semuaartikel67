Fresh offers on Atmos Rewards credit cards: Earn up to 90,000 points, companion fares and more

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (181 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Atmos Rewards is a loyalty program for Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, enabling members to earn and redeem points across both carriers and select partners.
  • Three cobranded cards—one business and two personal—currently feature strong introductory offers, appealing to frequent and occasional travelers alike.
  • Benefits across these cards include free checked bags, preferred or priority boarding, lounge passes, and companion ticket options, with some cards offering statement credits for travel programs like TSA PreCheck or Global Entry.
  • The business card version stands out for its high earning potential on eligible flights and perks that extend to employee cardholders.
  • Welcome bonuses vary by card and may require meeting specific spending thresholds within the first 90 days.
  • Certain benefits, such as lounge access, companion fares, or statement credits, are subject to terms and may differ between business and personal cards.
  • Point values are based on TPG's November 2026 valuations and may fluctuate over time.
