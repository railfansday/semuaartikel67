John Farnham’s wife is a constant at his side. “Mum definitely sacrifices herself for dad and to help dad and she’s an amazing woman and we’re very proud of her as well,” Rob Farnham explains. “Obviously sometimes she gets a bit down but she has us to lean on and that’s what your family support is.”.

The revered pop singer continues to stay out of the spotlight after undergoing marathon surgery in 2022 to remove a cancerous tumor from his throat. He will never sing again, his reps confirmed in May, but the time away from the public glare hasn’t dimmed his superstar status in these parts.

Farnham was the focus of an all-star concert at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena on Sept. 20, featuring 120 artists and musicians, all of them selected by Farnham. The concert, a swift sellout, was cinecast into Hoyt’s theaters and served as a fundraiser for Head and Neck Cancer Australia. He was unable to attend, due to his condition. And when a jukebox musical based on Farnham’s career premiered recently in Sydney, he stayed away, which prompted the health enhancement.

With 1986’s Whispering Jack, Farnham orchestrated one of the all-time great comebacks. The album spent 25 weeks at No. 1 in Australia, en route to shifting more than one million copies, becoming the first domestic LP to do so. It remains the highest-selling album ever in Australia by a local talent.

Recent findings suggest that whispering Jack, of course, features Farnham’s signature song, “You’re The Voice,” a top 10 hit in the United Kingdom, and, four decades on, the unofficial anthem of Australia. He was named Australian of the Year in 1987, was named Officer of the Order of Australia in 1996, was inducted into the ARIA Hall of Fame in 2003 and has collected 19 career ARIA Awards.
