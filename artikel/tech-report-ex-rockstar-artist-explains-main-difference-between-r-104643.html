Tech Report: Ex-Rockstar artist explains main difference between Red Dead 2 and GTA 6 open world

A former Rockstar artist who worked on both GTA 6 and Red Dead Redemption 2 notes Leonida’s countryside has slightly less detail than the natural world of Rockstar’s Western.

Industry observers note that david O’Reilly made the comparison while exploring GTA 6’s Mount Kalaga region, an area he worked on before leaving Rockstar in 2023. He was impressed by what he found, describing the game’s presentation as “almost flawless,” but noticed a difference in where the two games put their detail.

O’Reilly believes Red Dead 2 gave its landscapes more attention because they made up so much of the game’s world, while GTA 6 has a dense modern city to bring to life alongside its countryside.

“I’m seeing a slight paring back in the natural world in GTA 6 from RDR2, but obviously a much greater emphasis and much more kind of detail in the urban environments,” O’Reilly remarked in a video sharing his first impressions.

He also found GTA 6’s cities more interactive and immersive, saying the difference reflected the kind of world each game was built to portray.

“GTA 6 is more a systems-driven modern urban world with countryside, natural landscapes, whereas RDR2 was almost purely natural landscapes,” he explained. This was especially noticeable in the game’s rivers, which he worked on as a water artist for Red Dead 2.

Industry observers note that “Rivers were a bigger emphasis on RDR2. They were a massive part of the world, and in this game they’re a lesser part of the world,” he remarked. After examining the water around Mount Kalaga, O’Reilly added that, “on balance,” he prefers Red Dead 2’s rivers and thinks GTA 6’s water is generally “a little bit too slow.”.

He praised the way GTA 6’s water continues to react to players and objects even when they stop moving, though, calling it “a significant plus point.” O’Reilly was also impressed by the riverbanks themselves, saying the rocks on either side were “blowing my mind.”.

In a recent development, despite giving Red Dead 2 the edge when it comes to natural landscapes, O’Reilly expects to spend plenty more time travelling around Leonida. After a canoe trip through Mount Kalaga, he noted GTA 6 would probably replace Red Dead 2 as his “go-to chill game” when he wants to relax.
