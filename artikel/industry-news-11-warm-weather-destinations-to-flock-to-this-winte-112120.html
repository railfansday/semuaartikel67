Arguably the best way to overcome winter is to pretend like it’s not happening—i.e., jetting off to a warm-weather destination, where seasons are not taken very seriously and an outdoor adventure is always on the table. The best winter vacation destinations offer comfortable temperatures, off-peak advantages, and festive happenings. On this list, you’ll find some of the usual suspects, from Punta Cana to the US Virgin Islands, as well as locales you might not have considered (far too often, travelers forget vibrant Marrakech is a shorter-haul Mediterranean option).

Industry observers note that whether you’re interested in hiking to the wild and wondrous waterfalls of Puerto Rico’s El Yunque National Rainforest, snorkeling through the kaleidoscopic waters of Belize’s barrier reef, or simply want to kick back and relax with a dip in Costa Rica’s hot springs, these sunny locations are bound to cure those cold-weather blues. So, trade your snow boots for flip-flops and get to plotting your next escape.

When to go: JanuaryAverage winter temperature: 80–85 degrees.

According to recent reports, if you’ve been looking for an excuse to finally book that trip to Hawaii, shoot for after the holiday rush, when flights get significantly cheaper. January might be the start of rainy season in Maui, but the showers are often short and localized; for the most part, you’ll enjoy sunshiny days with temperatures that hover in the low-80s. Humpback whales make their way from Alaska around this time, and you can spot the giant beauties from Papawai Point on Honoapiilani Highway, an overlook facing the ʻAuʻau Channel. If you’re not afraid of massive waves, heavy north swells at this time of year make for excellent surfing, especially at Ho‘okipa Beach Park. The Road to Hana, a scenic and beautiful drive at any time of year, becomes especially lush in the winter after the rain has fallen, and you’ll likely avoid the crowds too. Along the way, hit up sites like the Twin Falls or the black sand beach at Waianapanapa State Park.

Recent findings suggest that where to stay: A Condé Nast Traveler Triple Crown winner, Hāna-Maui Resort, a luxurious off-grid escape, is the pot of gold at the end of the Road to Hana, while Hotel Wailea offers an adults-only vibe on the southwestern coast. Though much humbler, Jimi Hendrix’s former gingerbread-style home, now available for rent on Airbnb, is also a fair contender for your home away from home, surrounded by all kinds of fruit trees and perfectly positioned for daily rainbows.

When to go: December–JanuaryAverage winter temperature: 65 degrees.

It has been revealed that while you won’t be breaking a sweat in San Diego come December, it’s an excellent place to escape to around the holidays for festive fun in mild, mid-60-degree temperatures. The Parade of Lights is a classic seasonal event, when over 100 private boats get decked out in twinkling lights and sail around San Diego Bay (watch from spots like Shelter Island or Seaport Village on select nights in December). Between November and January, you can also enjoy ice skating without the chill at the Hotel del Coronado, where you’ll glide alongside the Pacific. They say San Diego sunsets are best in the winter, when the sun cuts a path closer to the horizon and there’s not as much coastal marine fog—one of the best places to catch one is Sunset Cliffs Natural Park on the Point Loma peninsula.

Sources indicate that where to stay: La Valencia, otherwise known as the “Pink Lady” of La Jolla, is one of San Diego’s most iconic stays, and you might hear a seal or two barking in the distance. For unparalleled mountain views, consider this glass house overlooking Sweetwater Regional Park.

When to go: December–MarchAverage winter temperature: 70–85 degrees.

For a Caribbean escape with outdoor adventure, cultural festivals, and farm-to-table splendor, head to Puerto Rico. From December to March, you can take advantage of the dry season on the island, which means your days won’t be interrupted by sudden showers. Holiday festivities continue well beyond Christmas, from Three Kings Day on January 6—the oldest of these celebrations takes place in Juana Díaz, with a reenactment of the Magi on horseback—to the San Sebastian Street Festival, when the namesake street in Old San Juan is filled with art vendors, live music, and plenty of mofongo (January 21–24 this year). Winter is also a great time to explore El Yunque National Forest when it’s not shrouded in unbearable humidity—hike to La Coca Waterfall, take in the green canopy from Yokahú Tower, and keep an ear out for singing coquí frogs.

Sources indicate that where to stay: For luxe accommodations just a 25-minute drive from El Yunque, look no further than Four Seasons Resort and Residences Puerto Rico, which coordinates guided treks with a strong emphasis on Indigenous history. Condado Ocean Club offers direct access to the beach, while this lakefront retreat near Cueva Ventana and Arecibo Observatory is ideal for couples seeking verdant views.

When to go: December–MarchAverage winter temperature: 85 degrees.

In a recent development, another destination where US travelers can leave their passports at home, the US Virgin Islands are a quintessential Caribbean paradise. December through March is peak season, and there are plenty of reasons why—no rain, calm seas, and temperatures that hover between 77 and 85 degrees, to name a few. St. Croix, the largest of the USVI’s three main islands, is defined by the white sands of Rainbow Beach, as well as its Danish ancestry, which you can explore at colonial-era sites like Fort Frederik and Fort Christiansvaern. St. John is filled with all kinds of natural wonders; take the Underwater Snorkel Trail at Trunk Bay to peer at parrotfish, or set off on the three-mile Reef Bay Trail to hike among pre-Columbian petroglyphs painted by Taíno Indians. St. Thomas is a bustling port with cosmopolitan charm, and you’d be well advised to sample a few conch fritters at Gladys' Café. Whichever island you choose, clear skies and turquoise waters await.

Where to stay: One of Traveler’s favorite private island resorts (and the only one in the US Virgin Islands), Lovango Resort and Beach Club is located across St. John’s Caneel Bay and offers all kinds of eco-chic accommodations, from wooden treehouses to explorer-style tents. The Westin St. Thomas is set right in front of the harbor, with a stellar spa worth traveling for. For something a bit more intimate, this light-filled, two-bedroom cottage is just a five-minute walk away from Cruz Beach.

When to go: December–FebruaryAverage winter temperature: 65 degrees.

While temperatures do drop in Marrakech during the winter—the peaks of the Atlas Mountains will be blanketed in snow—you’ll see far more sun than in certain parts of the US; plus, it’s all the more reason to enjoy a steamy hammam. Winter is a great time to explore the city's popular sites with thinner crowds: comb through the medina’s bustling souks for fragrant spices and a pair of babouche slippers, take in the cobalt blue buildings at the Jardin Majorelle, or learn about one of France’s most legendary fashion designers at the Yves Saint Laurent Museum. Keep an eye out for the dates of the Marrakech International Film Festival, which takes place between the months of November and December—this year it falls from November 20 to 28, and features 76 films from 39 countries.

Where to stay: Traveler contributor Jordan Lippe-McGraw is a big fan of this family-friendly villa, with a heated pool, hammam, and cinema room. A Triple Crown winner, La Mamounia is the city’s grand dame. As global features director Erin Florio puts it, “Every inch of this palatial property has been hyper-considered to make the guest feel as though they have arrived in some regal estate of an Arabian kingdom.” At the Royal Mansour, all riads come with 24/7 access to a private butler.

When to go: December–AprilAverage winter temperature: 80–90 degrees.

Industry observers note that the Arenal Volcano looms over Costa Rica’s La Fortuna region, heating its natural springs, and there’s a greater chance you’ll see it without cloud cover in the winter. For travelers seeking a combo of adventure and relaxation, La Fortuna (about a three-hour drive from San José) is well-suited for both. Hike through cooled lava fields at Arenal Volcano National Park or swim around La Fortuna Waterfall, then take advantage of nature’s healing powers at Tabacón Thermal Resort and Spa. For a truly memorable experience, book a tour with Arenal Horse Tours and ride your steed straight into the Arenal River for a refreshing dip.

Industry observers note that where to stay: While Tabacón is perhaps the most iconic resort in the area, The Springs Resort & Spa is another great option, boasting two dozen hot springs, pools, and waterfalls spread across the 65-acre property. If you’re looking for something more intimate, this forest cabin features a private spring-fed pool, rain shower, and marked hiking trails—a one-of-a-kind off-grid retreat.

When to go: December–AprilAverage winter temperature: 80–85 degrees.

Sources indicate that though small, this Central American country offers a diversity of experiences, whether you want to take it slow in a charming fishing village or ascend newly introduced heights in the jungle. Perhaps the most popular destination on Belize’s tourism map, Ambergris Caye is a departure point for the country’s barrier reef—though just south of Ambergris Caye, Caye Caulker provides a nice alternative. While on the car-free island, book a tour with Ragga Sail Adventures for a half-day snorkel through Hol Chan Marine Reserve, the oldest marine reserve in the country. Further south in Punta Gorda, you’ll find rugged adventures and a strong sense of Mayan culture. Here, you’ll have access to several offshore cayes (Belize has hundreds of these small islands, brimming with marine life), from Snake Cayes to Sapodilla Cayes Marine Reserve; if you prefer jungle, take a boat tour through the Monkey River, or hike to the waterfall at Rio Blanco National Park. And be sure to explore the surrounding Toledo District for Mayan ruins, like the Lubaantun, which dates back to 700–900 AD and is also an excellent site for birdwatching.

It has been revealed that where to stay: Nestled within the Belizean rainforest, Copal Tree Lodge’s jungle suites will make you feel as though you’re suspended among the trees. Wake up to the sounds of monkeys every morning, then take a farm tour around the surrounding property, where you’ll discover how cacao, vanilla, and sugar cane grow. Over in San Pedro, Alaia Belize is your luxury option, just 600 yards away from the barrier reef, while this tiny home will make you feel as though you’re vacationing in a fairy tale.

When to go: December–AprilAverage winter temperature: 80 degrees.

Sources indicate that december through April is peak vacation time in Punta Cana, when the rainy season has subsided and there’s not a cloud in the sky. With average highs in the 80s and average lows in the 70s, you’re likely to experience perfect weather during your stay. Come February, you’ve got a good chance of spotting some humpback whales if you cast your eyes off the coast of the Península de Samaná, where they typically pass through until April. The country also celebrates Independence Day in February—on the 27th, to be exact. Visit around that time, and you’re bound to run into a Carnaval parade or bachata dance circle. With the Cordillera Oriental mountain range looming in the background and a selection of cenotes around town—from Hoyo Azul to Hoyo Claro—mountain buggy excursions and diving adventures await even in the winter.

Where to stay: The W Punta Cana redefines the all-inclusive resort as we know it with top-tier dining and some of the largest rooms in the country. Hyatt Vivid Punta Cana, which just opened its doors in August 2026, is another upscale all-inclusive in the area, boasting swim-out suites and a chic speakeasy. If you’ve got kids in tow but still want to experience the height of luxury, Eden Roc Cap Cana is a consistent Readers' Choice Awards winner. For a villa the whole family will enjoy, this eight-bedroom Airbnb in Tortuga Bay comes with a private pool.

When to go: December–AprilAverage winter temperature: 70 degrees.

According to recent reports, another prime location for whale watching, Baja California’s southern tip sees the migration of humpback and gray whales between the months of December and April (you might even elect to swim with whale sharks, too, which roam around the nearby Bay of La Paz around this time). You’ll have just missed the rainy season in November, making way for sunny, 70-degree beach days and not-too-chilly evenings. The creative town of Todos Santos boasts a number of art galleries to pop into, like Galeria de Todos Santos and La Sonrisa de la Muerte, while the Sierra de la Laguna mountains are home to stunning trails and swimming holes. If you’re a surfer, Playa Los Cerritos and Playa San Pedro offer some of Mexico’s best waves.

Where to stay: Todos Santos is home to a number of cutting-edge boutique hotels. Hotel San Cristobal, with its vibrant interiors and delectably fresh seafood, perfectly embodies the bohemian vibes of this tiny surf town. Paradero Todos Santos, located on a five-and-a-half-acre farm, is designed around outdoor adventure: Pick the “Unbound” option, and surfing, hiking, and biking experiences will all be included in the nightly fare. If you prefer to go the rental route, this little bungalow is set among farmland in Pescadero, with its own private rooftop.

It has been revealed that miami is a good idea any time of year, but December through February is considered the dry season, promising temperatures in the mid-70s. Two important events pop up this time of year: Art Basel Miami, in December (4–6, this year), and the South Beach Wine & Food Festival, in February (running the 25–28 in 2027). But there’s always something to do in the Magic City, whether that’s taking a peek into the Pérez Art Museum, spending money in the Design District, or having a stroll through the Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden.

Recent findings suggest that where to stay: To embrace the happy-go-lucky spirit of the locals, check in to the Goodtime Hotel and sip on a Purple Reign cocktail (co-owner Pharrell’s favorite drink) at the rooftop pool club. If you’d like to tap into the city’s Art Deco architectural history, stay at The Moore, where you’ll be close to Wynwood and Downtown Miami. This dreamy loft in Collins Park, just a short walk to the beach, is a good bet too.

When to go: December–FebruaryAverage winter temperature: 85 degrees.

Industry observers note that depending on where you’re coming from, Cape Town might be a little out of the way. But the US's winter is their summer, and with that comes sunny days and a dry, Mediterranean-like climate. Around mid-January, tourists start to flee and kids return to school, making popular attractions more accessible. Take a cable car (or hike, if you’re feeling bold) up to the famous Table Mountain, or drive to Boulders Beach, which boasts one of the last colonies of African penguins in the world. There are plenty of opportunities to engage with some art, as well, from the contemporary galleries at Zeitz MOCAA to the rainbow houses of the Bo-Kaap Museum. And if you’re feeling especially adventurous, you can treat Cape Town as a jumping-off point for the famed Garden Route road trip.

Sources indicate that where to stay: For views of both Table Mountain and the V&A Waterfront, stay at the Silo Hotel, housed in a former grain silo and famous for its maximalist interior design. For something a little more off-the-beaten-path, Ellerman House is an Edwardian mansion turned boutique hotel situated in the exclusive Bantry Bay area. And if it's privacy you're after, this three-bedroom bungalow in St. James features a private hot tub and covered deck with gorgeous sea views.
