📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

MINTROCKET, Pugstorm, and Fireshine Games have announced a two-way crossover for both DAVE THE DIVER and Core Keeper. Starting November 30, players will be able to unlock the crossover content through a series of missions in DAVE THE DIVER, while Core Keeper players will simply need to revision the game. The crossover content is free to owners of each game and will be available permanently once activated.

In DAVE THE DIVER, trouble comes to Otto and Sammy’s farm when Dave plants a mysterious clump of moss from deep underground. Overnight, the moss spreads across the fields, unleashing an invasion of Cavelings from Core Keeper. Armed with a pickaxe, Dave must defend the farm in a newly introduced Core Keeper-inspired combat minigame, battling pickaxe-wielding Cavelings and fireball-hurling Caveling Shamans while clearing away the moss to stop more enemies from appearing.

Industry observers note that players who complete the crossover mission can earn special rewards, including the Spicy Lure Charm, which speeds up crab trap catches, and collectible Caveling and Caveling Shaman figurines to display at the sushi bar. The mission becomes available by redeeming a special coupon code after unlocking the farm following Sammy’s VIP event.

It has been revealed that in Core Keeper, Cobra joins the underground adventure as a new merchant, bringing a collection of familiar faces and items from the Blue Hole. Players can adopt Momo and Mini Dave as pets, decorate their bases with sushi bar furniture and a custom Blue Hole-inspired scene, and equip Bancho’s signature sushi chef armor. Other additions include a new harpoon gun, green tea, and the Reef Shark as a catchable fish. The crossover also features original DAVE THE DIVER sound effects, including the voices of Dave and Cobra.

The free crossover revision arrives November 30, 2026, with DAVE THE DIVER content launching first on Steam and console platforms to follow. Core Keeper players can access the upcoming content on all platforms from November 30 simply by updating their game. The crossover content will be available perpetually in both games once players activate it.

DAVE THE DIVER is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store, iOS, and Android.

Core Keeper is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam.
