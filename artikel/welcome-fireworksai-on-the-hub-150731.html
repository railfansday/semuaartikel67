Industry observers note that for direct requests, i.e. when you use a Fireworks key, you are billed directly on your Fireworks account.

Sources indicate that for routed requests, i.e. when you authenticate via the hub, you'll only pay the standard Fireworks API rates. There's no additional markup from us, we just pass through the provider costs directly. (In the future, we may establish revenue-sharing agreements with our provider partners.).
