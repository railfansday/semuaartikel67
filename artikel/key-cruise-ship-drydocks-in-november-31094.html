Cruise Industry News looks into key drydock projects taking place in November, from major refurbishments to modernization projects.

Celebrity Beyond Cruise Line: Celebrity Cruises Capacity: 3,260 guests Tonnage: 141,200 Year built: 2022 Drydock Period: November 1 to November 18, 2026 Shipyard: Grand Bahama Shipyard (Freeport, the Bahamas).

The Celebrity Beyond recently underwent a drydock at the Grand Bahama Shipyard in Freeport.

In addition to undergoing technical maintenance, the vessel introduced latest features during the shipyard stay, including a refreshed pool deck and new dining venues.

The Edge-class ship welcomed guests back on November 18, kicking off a winter season sailing to the Caribbean from Port Everglades.

Carnival Luminosa Cruise Line: Carnival Cruise Line Capacity: 2,260 guests Tonnage: 92,720 Year built: 2009 Drydock Period: October to November, 2026 Shipyard: Seatrium Tuas Boulevard Yard (Singapore).

The Carnival Luminosa recently resumed service after undergoing an 18-day drydock at the Seatrium Tuas Boulevard Yard in Singapore.

As reported by Cruise Industry News, the 2009-built vessel welcomed guests back with a series of enhancements, including newly introduced gaming machines in its casino, a brand-new Effy Jewelry store and a revitalized video arcade.

The ship also underwent technical maintenance and class inspections before kicking off a repositioning voyage ahead of its winter season in Australia and the South Pacific.

AIDAmar Cruise Line: AIDA Cruises Capacity: 2,174 guests Tonnage: 88,000 Year built: 2012 Drydock Period: November 1 to December 20, 2026 Shipyard: Chantier Naval de Marseille (Marseille, France).

The AIDAmar is undergoing a major refurbishment as part of the AIDA Evolution fleet modernization program.

As the third ship to be revitalized as part of the initiative, the 2012-built vessel entered a drydock at the Chantier Naval de Marseille on November 1, 2026.

Before welcoming guests back in late December, the ship will introduce updated technical equipment, as well as refreshed interiors, upgraded cabins and fresh venues.

Crystal Serenity Cruise Line: Crystal Cruises Capacity: 740 guests Tonnage: 68,870 Year built: 2003 Drydock Period: October 19 to November 9, 2026 Shipyard: Lisnave (Setúbal, Portugal).

The Crystal Serenity recently underwent a three-week drydock at the Lisnave shipyard in Setúbal, Portugal.

The project focused on refining the onboard experience and included updates to the vessel’s Atrium and Crystal Cove, which were refreshed with a modern layout and additional seating.

Other areas undergoing updates included the pool deck, the Waterside Restaurant, the Stardust Club, the Seahorse Pool and the Marketplace.

Amadea Cruise Line: Phoenix Reisen Capacity: 600 guests Tonnage: 29,000 Year built: 1991 Drydock Period: November 6 to November 20, 2026 Shipyard: Lloyd Werft (Bremerhaven, Germany).

The Amadea is undergoing a modernization project at the Lloyd Werft shipyard in Bremerhaven, Germany.

According to Phoenix Reisen, the project is highlighted by technical enhancements, with initiatives aimed at improving operational reliability and energy efficiency.

The 29,000-ton vessel is also undergoing guest-facing changes, including the extension of the Copernicus Bar, an extensive renovation of its show lounge and a redesign of the Vista Lounge.
