Google Wallet is preparing another form of digital ID storage, with support for your Social Security card apparently on the way.

Sources indicate that you can already store your passport or select State driver’s licenses in Google Wallet in the US, and it seems Google isn’t stopping there. Wallet now appears to be preparing some level of support for storing a digital copy of your Social Security card, as code in the latest app updates suggest.

Recent findings suggest that there’s zero reason to store your SS card like this. Unnecessary exposure.

The folks over at Android Authority spotted explicit references to “Social Security Number” in Google Play Services v26.46.30 in connection with Google Wallet.

Recent findings suggest that while exact functionality is unclear at this time, this appears to include support for seeing your Social Security benefits, or at least logging those benefits, in a digital form. There are mentions of Retirement, Survivors, and/or Disability Insurance (RSDI), Supplemental Security Income, Medicare, and more, as well as qualifiers for deductions and effective dates.
