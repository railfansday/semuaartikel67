Apple only launched high-end ‘Pro’ and ‘Duo’ iPhones this fall, marking a departure from previous years when more affordable models debuted too. But iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 are still coming, and a newly introduced report offers an update on release timing.

A fresh report from Kim Yeong-ho at ETNews provides a fresh update on timing for the iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2.

Rumors have indicated for well over a year that Apple planned to push these models out of the typical fall launch window and introduce them in early 2027 instead.

Per ETNews, Samsung and LG are providing the display panels for the latest models, with Samsung the sole supplier for iPhone Air 2 and both companies splitting orders for iPhone 18. And their mass production prepares signal a March release for the products.

Kim Yeong-ho, writing at ETNews:.

Samsung Display prepares to begin mass production in January, while LG Display will start late this year…The Korean panel makers appear to be aligning their production plans with an expected March launch.

A March launch window lines up well with when Apple tends to ramp up product introductions following a slower winter. Earlier this year, iPhone 17e debuted in March alongside newly introduced Macs and the iPad Air.

The year before, however, the iPhone 16e arrived in February instead.

Industry observers note that with Apple shaking up its normal iPhone rollout schedule, it was anyone’s guess how soon the latest models would debut. But a March launch makes sense and gives Apple a trio of new iPhones every six months—three unveiled in September, and another three in March.
