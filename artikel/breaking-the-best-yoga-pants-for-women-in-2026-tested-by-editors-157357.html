Sources indicate that “When you’re shopping for yoga pants, you want more than just a cute pair—you want leggings that actually work for your practice,” indicates Melody Zoller, NASM, a personal trainer and senior director at obé Fitness. “The right pair should feel good against your skin, move with you through every twist, fold, and lunge, and give you the level of support you need without constant tugging or adjusting.” She recommends looking for blends of the following materials:.

No matter what fabric composition a pair of yoga pants is made with, Zoller notes to always do the squat test in natural light to make sure they’re not see-through!

Recent findings suggest that all the options on this list are high-rise. Why? During yoga, you need something that stays up, holds your stomach in, and covers your midriff, especially during twisting and upside-down poses, Zoller says. A high-rise pair will help keep your lower body locked in while bending and changing positions. Opt for a slim or tapered cut to prevent extra fabric from bunching up during certain poses. That’s not to say a flare can’t work (especially for a more chill practice or day-to-day wear), but a slimmer hem or bootcut is more functional.

Look for a light to medium level of compression for your yoga pants, Zoller claims. Compression is useful because it can help enhance circulation, which may improve performance and reduce muscle fatigue, says Ainee Dehradunwala, NASM, a personal trainer. “However, it's important to ensure they are not too tight, as excessive compression can restrict movement and cause discomfort,” she says. Choose a pair that makes you feel secure without digging into your waist or legs—which could majorly get in the way of your flow.
