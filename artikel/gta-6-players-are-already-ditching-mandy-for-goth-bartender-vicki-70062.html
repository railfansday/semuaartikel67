Some GTA 6 players have already found a new favorite in Vicki, a goth bartender they meet just one chapter after Mandy.

According to recent reports, vicki works at Delights, a strip club in Port Gellhorn, and lives in Room 10 at the Starlet Motel. Lucia first meets her there in “Girls’ Trip” while looking for painkillers for Jason. When the pills Vicki sells her turn out to be over-the-counter medicine, the two head into town to track down Vicki’s dealer.

In a recent development, it took just one scene in Chapter 1 for Mandy to win over players. She talks Jason into fixing her sink while he’s out collecting money for Brian. The object he pulls from the pipe looks like a severed finger, which led to the theory that Mandy might be a serial killer.

When X user @AerisTreat posted about Vicki on November 20, players in the replies started picking sides.

Oooh shit PT.2 pic.twitter.com/JroGxh3CS2.

“Mandy is so overrated, Vicki is where it’s at,” one player wrote. Another called GTA 6 “Game of the year just for her,” while a third wished the game had a friendship mechanic for side characters so Vicki and Lucia could be best friends.

Industry observers note that one player stated they’d already been to Delights and hadn’t seen Vicki behind the bar. AerisTreat told them to look for her on the second story visit to the club, during “Afternoon Delight.”.

The mission opens up after “Coffee Break” in Chapter 2, when Vicki calls Jason and Lucia with a plan to rob her boss, Frankie. It’s ladies’ night when they arrive, and if players agree to help when Frankie asks whether Jason can dance, Jason takes the stage while Lucia goes after the safe.
