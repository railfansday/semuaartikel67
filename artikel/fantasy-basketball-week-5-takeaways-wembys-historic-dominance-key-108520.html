A quick fantasy basketball breakdown on all 30 teams after Week 5 of the 2026-27 NBA season.

Industry observers note that spurs: Victor Wembanyama dominated Saturday: 50 points, 19 rebounds, 5 blocks and 4 steals in 32 minutes of a 114-91 win over the Pelicans. It got him the High Score of the week, with 97 fantasy points. He's the first player since 1973-74 with a 50/15/5/4 game (since steals and blocks became official stats). It's his second 50 piece this month (52 on Nov. 4), and San Antonio is 15-3 on the season with Wemby leading the way as the top player in fantasy.

Bulls: Josh Giddey posted 23 points, 14 rebounds and a career-high 22 assists in Sunday's 151-145 OT loss to Denver, the league's first 20-20 game this season. Giddey followed up that epic game with a 2-for-11 performance with 4 points, 11 assists, 2 stocks and 3 turnovers on Monday. He was benched in crunch time (again). If you have Giddey, I'd sell. The game-to-game variance is too erratic.

Heat: Bam Adebayo posted 24 points, 15 rebounds and a career-high 12 assists with zero turnovers in Sunday's 123-113 win over OKC with Giannis Antetokounmpo (groin) out. Miami has won seven straight to reach 10-6. Shout-out to Klay Thompson, who averaged 20.7 points and 5.1 3s over a recent seven-game stretch and is closing in on Ray Allen for third all time in 3s. Keep holding Klay in 12s.

Bucks: Tyler Herro set a new career high in Philly on Saturday, dropping 50 points, then added 32 with 8 assists in Monday's win in Toronto to snap a 13-game skid. At 2-16, start prepping for some shakeups in the rotation — which could impact vets like Herro, Kyle Kuzma and Myles Turner. Rookie Brayden Burries (40% rostered) had 14 points, 10 boards, and 4 assists off the bench Monday, and should be rostered in most leagues.

According to recent reports, cavaliers: James Harden dropped 51 in Friday's 128-119 win over Indiana, passing Kobe Bryant for the third-most 50-point games ever (26). Evan Mobley (toe) has missed six straight and is due for re-evaluation this week. Kenny Atkinson has started Tyrese Proctor and Sam Merrill over the past week, but rookie Meleek Thomas (13.3 points in 24 minutes) is still the best streamer here.

Nets: Nolan Traore (right hamstring) will be re-evaluated in 8-10 weeks. Mikel Brown Jr. (19% rostered) got the first crack, starting Monday in Minnesota with his minutes restriction lifted: 17 points, a career-high 7 assists and 5 boards in 29 minutes. Egor Demin shot 3-for-13 off the bench. Brown is the add over Demin. The No. 6 pick has a path to 30 minutes a night.

Lakers: Austin Reaves (left heel) practiced fully on Sunday and is targeting a return on Friday in San Antonio. Quentin Grimes averaged 9.2 points through 12 games, then moved into the starting lineup over the last five games, averaging 24.8 points, 4.6 rebounds, and 3.4 assists. He ranks 31st in 9-cat over that span, with 35.58 fantasy points per game.

Enjoy it now, because he won't see anywhere near 37 minutes a night when Reaves returns. Matisse Thybulle is the keeper. His 39 steals rank second in the league, and he and Wemby are the only players this year with 15+ blocks, 20+ steals and 30+ 3s.

Hawks: Atlanta has won four straight without CJ McCollum, whose left calf strain will be re-evaluated in about four weeks. No. 8 pick Kingston Flemings got next, but don't rush to get him because the Hawks won't play until Friday.

It has been revealed that dyson Daniels, though? He's such a unique fantasy talent. He's posting 9.5 points, 7.4 boards, 5.6 assists and a league-leading 2.9 steals per night. He can't shoot beyond 10 feet, but he's touching nearly every major counting-stats category except blocks and 3s.

Grizzlies: No. 3 pick Cameron Boozer (ankle) will be re-evaluated in four weeks. That hurts. Kris Murray started in his absence on Monday, playing 32 minutes and got 5 stocks. Add him to the watchlist, even though Memphis will likely form a committee between Murray, Taylor Hendricks and Jerami Grant (when he gets back). Make sure Cam Spencer (45%) isn't on waivers.

Industry observers note that clippers: Brandon Ingram started in his debut Monday in Houston and posted 16 points, 7 boards, and 4 assists in 27 minutes. The shooting was unsurprisingly rough (6-for-18, 0-for-6 from three), but 18 shots in his first game since May is a great sign. Marc Stein reported that LA is showcasing vets like Rui Hachimura and Kris Dunn before the trade deadline.

Jazz: Svi Mykhailiuk went OFF Sunday for 39 points and 10 threes, both career highs. I'd rather pick up Ace Bailey, though.

76ers: Philly's five-game win streak ended Monday against Portland with Joel Embiid (illness) out. Embiid's been better, and fantasy managers could take the opportunity to float offers because health is always the biggest question mark with Embiid.

Celtics: Four straight losses. Derrick White is shooting 40.1% from the field and 28.8% from 3, well off his 45.4/38.7 peak. I'm holding in 9-cat, but it's been a disappointing start.

According to recent reports, mavericks: Just two games this week, and two scheduled before the NBA Cup next week. Cut fringe Mavs for players who play more games over the next two weeks.

Recent findings suggest that knicks: Josh Hart is averaging 6.6 points on 34/27/60 shooting. Woof.

Industry observers note that pistons: Over the past five games, Ausar Thompson ranks 16th in 9-cat. He's making 87.5% of his free throws (albeit low volume, but still!).

Magic: Anthony Black continues to lap Jalen Suggs. Suggs is averaging 9-5-4 with 35/29/73 shooting splits. He's down to 73% rostered, but fantasy managers should stay patient as he ramps up from a bone bruise injury that cost him eight games.

Industry observers note that wizards: The Wizards want Trae Young to focus less on scoring and more on passing. Per Kevin O'Connor, Young had 8 minutes of possession time per game over his first 6 games. It's down to 5 minutes over his last 5 games.

Industry observers note that raptors: Toronto lost to the 2-16 Bucks and sits at 6-11 overall. Kawhi Leonard (knee) is shooting at practice but is still a couple of weeks away from returning. Jakob Poeltl is back on the radar after averaging 11.0 points, 9.3 rebounds, 3.7 assists, 1.3 steals, and 1.3 blocks in 29.7 minutes per game in his last three games.

Pacers: At 5-12 without Tyrese Haliburton, Indiana is reportedly looking to make a move. In the meantime, Kelly Oubre Jr. (36%) should be rostered in all leagues.

According to recent reports, hornets: Brandon Miller and Coby White sat all of OT in a four-point loss to the Lakers last week. Minutes restrictions are costing teams Ws.

Timberwolves: Rudy Gobert ranks 114th in points leagues and 290th in 9-cat. His efficiency near the rim is pacing to his worst since his rookie season — he's 17-for-53 on layups through Monday. Plus, his rebounding and block rates are tumbling. Keep an eye on Joan Beringer.

Rockets: At 13-5, Houston owns the NBA's best offensive rating (121.1) and a plus-8.2 net rating (5th in the league).

In a recent development, nuggets: Nikola Jokić logged triple-double No. 202 on Sunday. He's seven behind Russell Westbrook's record.

Trail Blazers: Donovan Clingan is finishing 74% at the rim, up from 66% last season.

Thunder: Nearly 80% of Ajay Mitchell's shots are 2s and he's hitting 28.3% from 3. He'll need to extend that range if the Thunder want to right the ship.

According to recent reports, suns: Four straight wins after Monday's OT victory in Upcoming Orleans. If Jalen Green was dropped in a points league, pick him up.

Warriors: Stephen Curry (oblique) is due for re-evaluation this week.

According to recent reports, pelicans: Fresh Orleans sits at 5-14, so it's a team to watch by the deadline. Keep holding the young guys, Jeremiah Fears and Derik Queen. Everyone else could be on the block.

In a recent development, kings: Keegan Murray has 12-plus points and seven-plus boards in four of his last five. Rounding into form? Maybe.
