Most of the deals we outline below can be found at Backcountry, at REI, and at specific manufacturers like Black Diamond.

Industry observers note that backcountry launched its Black Friday Sale early on November 20, with deals up to 60% off. We found some of the best deals on climbing shoes there. However, if you’re shopping in person, keep in mind that the co-op is closed on Black Friday itself for its annual #OptOutside campaign.

On the brand side, Black Diamond has been running an outlet sale more or less all month with up to 50% off past styles (mostly apparel, but some hardgoods). As you head into the discount madness, here are a couple Black Friday/Cyber Monday shopping tips for climbers:.

Looking to build out your climbing shoe quiver? Is your go-to pair beyond the point of resole or repair? Ready to graduate to a more performance-oriented design? This is your moment. You may be thinking: But I’m not going to be climbing much for the next few months. I’ll just wait.

Sources indicate that real ones know that would be a grave error. Come February or March, you will still need new shoes. But chance are, they’ll be full price. Plus, if you strategy ahead and invest now, you can spend the winter breaking them in around the gym. Here are a few standout Black Friday climbing deals on rock and approach shoes to consider below.

If your footwork repertoire involves pockets and micro edging, the La Sportiva Miura VS Climbing Shoe is among the best. Last year, we chose the Miura VS as the best tech shoe out there. With an aggressive downturn and semi-stiff, low-profile toe box, this shoe is perfect for precision climbing up slab and narrow cracks. Note that VS means this shoe uses a Velcro closure.

In a recent development, la Sportiva’s Katana is an awesome technical shoe for edging and beyond. Note that the men’s version is stiffer than the women’s version. This shoe is great for precision placements on slab, narrow cracks, and steep routes. After a buddy gifted me his slightly too small Katanas, I had them resoled and have been loving them indoors and out. You can also check out our review of the Katana here.

It has been revealed that with great grip and breathability, these approach shoes strike a near-perfect balance between nimble hiking and climbing performance—without the weight. That’s why we selected the TX2 Evo as our top pick for a lightweight approach shoe last year. We also love that La Sportiva crafts this pair with recycled materials and a sole designed for easy repairs. This is one of the best Black Friday climbing deals out there if you need a good approach shoe.

Industry observers note that beyond shoes, there’s a lot more climbing gear on sale out there during Black Friday. Ropes, hardware, and more are all marked down 20% or more. Here are our top editor-tested picks.

Most climbers care about the planet. We can do our part in a lot of ways, one of which is buying more planet-friendly gear. Edelrid manufactured this 70m rope with over 40% renewable materials, leveraging a bio-based polyamide in part made from castor oil. This is the first synthetic rope of its kind that relies less heavily on fossil fuels. Of course, the rope has to work, too, and in tests, we found it held up well. It feeds well and proved kink-resistant in our tests. We dubbed it the best eco-friendly cord in our 2025 rope guide.

We tested Mammut’s Alpine Core Protect Rope and called it one of the safest ropes we’d ever used. It also held up remarkably well after significant abuse in the field. By weaving burly aramid fibers into its core, this innovative rope from Mammut helps resist cutting. And right now, you can snag it at 20% off.

Using the same cam-assisted blocking that makes the Grigri the safer choice over an ATC or other devices, the NEOX innovates with an integrated wheel that makes feeding out slack when lead belaying faster and smoother. Petzl also engineered the NEOX with recycled materials to reduce its environmental impact. We’ve used it for rock, gym, and ice climbing and we’re big fans.

Sources indicate that I have been loving the Black Diamond Solution Hoody as a light fall belay jacket and even for cold rock climbing when I’m too chilly to shed my jacket. The Solution manages to be super lightweight, movement-friendly, and breathable, yet warm. This is largely because the specific PrimaLoft insulation used in this jacket is made with technology developed by NASA for astronauts. Here on earth, it also works great. It honestly does feel like being wrapped in warm air. The pocket real estate is also generous, and it’s nice that the whole thing packs into the left hand pocket so you can easily stash it in your pack.

Recent findings suggest that A chance to save on cams? We’ll take it. Snag one (or a few) of this Black Diamond #.5 to enhance your rack for fast and light missions.

Our top all-around harness of Fall 2026, the Solution uses what Black Diamond dubs Contour Edge Technology for the waist and leg loops, meaning they’re more rigid in the middle, but flexible and soft at the edges for increased comfort. One of our testers found that this made for a climbing harness with “fantastic” hip support. It didn’t cause any uncomfortable pressure or distracting pain points on his body, steadfastly supporting him during 10-hour days on the wall.

According to recent reports, one of our editors’ favorite gifts for this holiday season, Trango’s REAL Chalk is better for humans and the planet. This seawater-sourced chalk comes in 50g, 250g, or 500g bags. Makes for a great stocking stuffer—or stock up for yourself for 2027.

I bought a BD Momentum Full-Body Harness in 2018 or 2019 and am still using it today. It’s been through years of indoor and outdoor climbing with both of my kids. It’s user-friendly, too—a four-year-old can handle putting it on themselves. When my son finally outgrows his next spring, it’s in good enough shape that we will pass it down to another climbing family. This is a great time to save on a kids’ harness that will last until your kid outgrows it.

According to recent reports, perhaps the most hyped carabiner of 2026 (if there is such a thing as carabiner hype), the Magnetron returned by popular demand a few months ago. After swiftly going out of stock, what’s been billed as “Alex Honnold’s favorite carabiner” is now back in inventory—and on sale. The biggest boon of this cult-favorite gear is that you can never forget to lock it, thanks to an automatic magnetic locking mechanism. However, it’s not great for ice/alpine climbing and can be a tad difficult to operate for smaller hand sizes. I personally have a small hand and find it easy to use and love it. But you can read our full thoughts here.

One of our top ice tool picks this winter, the Black Diamond Hydras offer an impeccably balanced swing, ultra-precise pick, and a high degree of modularity make the Black Diamond Hydra our tool of choice for moderate and vertical ice. Interchangeable screw-on head weights let us dial in balance for any terrain, and the razor-thin picks—2.5 millimeters at the tip versus a 3- to 3.5-millimeter industry average—place like a dream. Read more about the Hydras in our Winter Gear Guide.

Our best all-around helmet of Fall 2026, the Black Diamond Vision offers the best of both worlds when it comes to comfort and weight. Made from mostly foam, with a plastic, ABS hardshell covering a portion of the top of the helmet, the Vision is impressively light at 215 grams.

After quickly burning through Black Diamond Crack Gloves (that broke within a few months while climbing an indoor crack), I ordered a pair of these Ocún gloves a couple years ago and have loved them ever since. They are easy to put on, simple to clip to a back gear loop of your harness (I keep them clipped under my chalkbag), comfortable, and super durable on sandstone, granite, and human-made cracks alike.
