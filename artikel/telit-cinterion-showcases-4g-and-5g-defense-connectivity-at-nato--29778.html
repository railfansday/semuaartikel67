BOCA RATON, Fla., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Telit Cinterion, an end-to-end Internet of Things (IoT) solutions enabler, today declared it is exhibiting at NATO Edge 2026, Nov. 17â19 in Izmir, Turkey. At Booth L86, the company is presenting its 4G and 5G solutions for defense systems and demonstrating how its modules can support multiple communications and positioning technologies within a single defense-focused system.

More information about the event is available on the NATO Edge 2026 website.

In a recent development, the booth features a technology display designed to demonstrate multiple connectivity options at the edge. Two Telit Cinterion modules within the display provide 5G cellular connectivity. Three Wi-Fi 6 modules provide local wireless connectivity. A global navigation satellite system (GNSS) module supports positioning, while a narrowband IoT (NB-IoT) module supports connectivity for both terrestrial networks (TN) and non-terrestrial networks (NTN).

The display illustrates how multiple wireless technologies can be incorporated into defense and mission-critical systems with different communications and positioning requirements. Telit Cinterion's portfolio includes cellular modules and custom communication board designs for original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) serving the defense industry. The firm also provides system integration, industrialization and manufacturing support.

In a recent development, headquartered in the United States, Telit Cinterion has U.S. manufacturing capabilities and a global engineering and operations footprint. The company offers 4G and 5G technologies for autonomous systems, mobile platforms and tactical networks.

It has been revealed that "Connected systems are becoming a foundational element of defense modernization initiatives around the world," mentioned Neset Yalcinkaya, president of IoT hardware at Telit Cinterion. "Defense customers need resilient communications and a trusted technology partner that can support them from design through deployment. At NATO Edge, we'll show how our 4G and 5G portfolio, engineering expertise and U.S. manufacturing capabilities can help customers develop the next generation of connected mission-critical systems.".

In a recent development, telit Cinterion's defense strategy focuses on working with leading defense and technology companies to deliver resilient, multi-network communications solutions. By combining proven cellular technology with engineering, integration and manufacturing support, the company aims to help defense customers accelerate development and deployment across land, air, sea and autonomous platforms.

Attendees can visit Telit Cinterion at Booth L86 to discuss connectivity requirements for defense and mission-critical applications.
