By KIERAN GILL, MAIL SPORT REPORTER.

Published: 08:00 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 09:55 EST, 24 November 2026.

Wesley Fofana has risked the ire of Chelsea fans with an ill-timed interview in which he laughed off his penalty-shootout miss at Liverpool while claiming he considered quitting football while injured until he was convinced otherwise.

Industry observers note that the 25-year-old Frenchman, one half of a defence partnership alongside Levi Colwill that has struggled this season, conducted a two-hour chat with a French YouTube channel. It is understood the interview was not arranged by Chelsea, who have lost three of their last four league fixtures and are in an uncomfortable moment under newly introduced manager, Xabi Alonso.

In the discussion, Fofana was asked about the spot-kick he skied at Liverpool which confirmed Chelsea’s exit from the Carabao Cup last month.

Sources indicate that ‘I took responsibility for the penalty. I missed it. I knew I wasn't a good shot from penalties. I (gave away) the penalty in the game. It's a penalty, it's a hand. I could have avoided it.

‘You have to go because I'm one of the oldest. On the pitch, there were a lot of young players. You had to take responsibility. ‘If I miss, I've lived through things. It's not that it doesn't matter. For sure, it sucks. But if they attack me, it doesn't matter. It's not the first time they're going to attack me, I don't care. I know that in three days there's another game, they forget you missed a penalty.

‘I went, I took it, I missed. I put a nice shot on it. Did you see my shot? I remember a fan really high up caught the ball but yeah, I missed, now I can laugh about it because football is like this. The next game, we beat Man United and that is it.’.

Wesley Fofana risked winding up Chelsea fans with his interview on French YouTube channel.

Fofana smashed his penalty over the bar against Liverpool in October but saw the funny side.

Previously plagued by injuries, Fofana revealed how he considered retirement until he was seemingly advised to honour his contract with Chelsea by his representative.

After taking the points on his driving licence to 50 in January of this year, his lawyer disclosed his salary in a statement to the court, revealing that his client is paid more than £200,000 per week.

He is contracted to Chelsea until 2029.

‘I really wanted to stop, because, for the first time in my life, I wasn’t happy anymore. I thought to myself, “I’m too fragile, I’m the Abou Diaby of our generation. There has to be someone like that, and it happened to me. It’s my destiny, I accept it.”.

‘Of course, I was very sad and didn’t want to stop, because I was living my dream.

Recent findings suggest that ‘I sat down with my agents. “I’m not going to play football anymore.” Then they told me about the contract, et cetera.

‘They told me, “It’s not that simple. There are years on your contract to take, there is money to take. Even if you're going to stop playing football, you're going to smile. Even if you get hurt. But you have a contract. You have to finish it. Even for your future.” But I was like, “I don't want it anymore.” In fact, I was like, “Football makes me more unhappy than happy. Why should I continue?”’.

As well as Fofana, Mykhailo Mudryk also conducted an unsolicited interview last week which neither his parent club, Chelsea, nor his loan club, Tottenham Hotspur, knew was happening.

Chelsea’s next match is away at Nottingham Forest this Friday, when Fofana and Colwill, if selected, will need to try to stop their former team-mate Liam Delap from scoring.
