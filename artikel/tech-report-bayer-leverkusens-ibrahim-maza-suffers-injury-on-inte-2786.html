Bayer Leverkusen playmaker Ibrahim Maza has sustained an ankle injury while on international duty, the Algerian Football Federation (FAF) has confirmed.

Industry observers note that the 20-year-old suffered an ankle strain during Saturday’s Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Togo and, following medical examinations, will not travel to Togo for the second-leg on Tuesday. The FAF did not provide further details on Maza’s absence.

This, of course, is a concern for Bayer Leverkusen ahead of Saturday’s Bundesliga game against Paderborn but, per Bild, Bayer hope that Maza will be available.

Under Carles Martínez, Maza (20) is shining. With three goals and three assists in nine Bundesliga games in 2026/27, the Berlin-born Algeria international is close to surpassing last season’s tally of three goals and five assists in 28 Bundesliga appearances.

A key player for die Werkself, Leverkusen managing director for sport Simon Rolfes confirmed that the club are in talks to extend Maza’s contract beyond 2030.
