Perez Hilton Returns to Miami Residence—After Hospitalization for At-Home Health Incident

Perez Hilton has returned to his Miami residence after a health incident left him hospitalized.

The 48-year-old was hospitalized in August following a distressing livestream that prompted several calls to police and requests for a welfare check.

Officers ultimately transported Hilton to a nearby hospital, while mental health professionals and the Crisis Response Unit offered support to his friends and family.

Sources indicate that hilton received treatment for multiple broken bones and significant blood loss. After undergoing surgery, he was transferred to an inpatient medical facility.

Now, his family has revealed that Hilton is “back with his family while continuing his recovery through an intensive outpatient program.”.

They noted that he is getting "ongoing therapy" and "mental health support.".

Recent findings suggest that “Over the past few weeks, Perez has enjoyed several successful weekend visits with his mom and children. Being reunited with his loved ones has meant so much to everyone, especially after these past few difficult months. With Thanksgiving approaching, we’re looking forward to spending some much-needed quality time together as a family and enjoying a little peace and quiet during the holiday," the statement continued.

In September, his family shared an enhancement on the situation as they begged fans to vacate his property.

The message revealed that before the livestream event, Hilton's "children, niece, and sister were inside the home.".

"When it became clear that Perez was experiencing a severe mental health crisis and harming himself, they immediately fled to protect the children from witnessing any further trauma," the message continued.

The patch noted the family's priority was "helping the children begin to heal from what they experienced," while explaining that allowing them to return to the privacy and familiarity of their home is an vital part of that process.

Hilton's family previously revealed that he was receiving medical care and was "able to communicate.".

The update came after Hilton opened about his move to Realtor.com®, reflecting on what he'll miss most about his property and his fond memories of Las Vegas.

“It is a beautiful home, and it served me well personally and professionally," he noted. "I filmed tons of television segments and news interviews from this home, and produced two documentaries.

Recent findings suggest that "I would film every day for my socials, and livestream as well, and I do a lot of talking head commentary work. I filmed tons of specials for different networks and streamers in my home and outdoors. It's just so beautiful,".

In a recent development, "All three of my favorite spaces in the home have one thing in common—they are all very healing for me. The primary bath has the biggest, most luxurious shower that I've ever had, and the closet is huge and has so much space. Also, I never had my own, dedicated office before, and I really loved that. It's just the perfect home for a family. Everybody has space," he added.

Speaking about the property's location, Hilton told Realtor.com, "It was important for me to be close to the Strip, but it’s equal distance to Summerlin and everywhere we needed to be. My children’s schools, doctors, and dentists were all just a few minutes away.”.

Sources indicate that hilton was especially pleased with the fact that his mother had her own private living space. One of home designer Blue Heron's signature features is a "NexGen Suite," and, because he bought the home before it was finished, he got to customize elements of the layout to ensure they were perfect for his mom.

Sources indicate that “This was perfect for me, because my mother is a huge part of my family,” remarked Hilton. “She’s both grandmother and co-parent to my children. To be able to give her the space that she deserves was crucial. She had her own entrance, her own living room and kitchenette area.".

However, despite his love for Nevada, Hilton noted that he is confident in his decision.

"It just feels like the right move, the only move, to make at this time. I've loved Las Vegas, but it's time to be closer to family," he added.

Sources indicate that the property is being sold with Kathryn Winterton of Las Vegas Sotheby’s real estate agency, who told Realtor.com, "This home was designed for the way people want to live and entertain today. The natural light, open spaces, and seamless connection to the backyard create an atmosphere that feels both sophisticated and welcoming.".

The home spans 7,000 square feet and boasts several modern living spaces, as well as a resort-style pool.

As in so many family dwellings, Hilton notes the kitchen is the heart of the home, with its premium appliances, sleek custom cabinetry and a massive dining and prep island.

The kitchen opens up to the high-ceiling living and dining areas, which feel expansive, welcoming and luxurious. Generous walls of glass provide abundant natural light create a light, airy atmosphere.

Sources indicate that outdoors on the acre lot there's a pool, spa, lush landscaping and multiple outdoor gathering spaces. Hilton said he did a lot of his production work outside, as there are a variety of luxurious backdrops ideal for his commentary.

Set in a chic, private gated neighborhood known as Prado, Hilton enjoyed the fact that the home is on a corner lot in a small community with fewer than 20 homes.

Hilton and his family enjoyed the home, the community and Las Vegas in general so much he says he feels a bit of guilt moving his family away. But "kids are adaptable," he says. He knows this from experience.

A little over three years ago, Hilton packed up his three children and his mother and moved to Las Vegas from Los Angeles, the city where he had infinite Hollywood connections and a luxe, six bedroom, five bath classic Spanish Colonial built in 1926.

Prior to that, Hilton and his family lived in Fresh York City for several years.

Hilton took to Instagram to explain the reasoning behind his move from Las Vegas, where he has been living for the past 3.5 years.

It has been revealed that "We’re moving permanently to Miami. I am so excited, not nervous. I know this is the right move for me and my children and my mother," he noted at the beginning of the video.

Although Hilton insisted he has "nothing but wonderful memories" while calling Nevada home, he claimed that his decision to relocate to the Sunshine State came from a higher power.

Industry observers note that “I need to move to Miami for many reasons. First, this is heaven-sent. God told me to move to Miami. ... The holy spirit made these words come out of my mouth: ‘Let’s move to Miami,'" he said.

The influencer noted that his "trauma" from growing up in The Magic City has since been "released.".

“My entire family is in Miami. I still have friends from high school. Though I have not lived there in 30 years, it has always been my home. Once we move there, any second now, it will feel like home right away," he added.

Hilton, who has three children via surrogacy, said a large part of his decision to move was due to his difficult year, which was filled with several health scares, including a 21-day hospital stay and a blood clot.

It has been revealed that "Even though it was really painful being in the hospital and so sick, I reconnected with my faith, and this move to Miami feels divine. I was just listening to God. I used to not have a great relationship with Miami when I was younger, but now things are different and Miami is different, and I'm excited to get to rediscover it as an adult," he told Realtor.com.

In a recent development, speaking about his health woes, he stated, "I know this move will be great for my family. Nearly dying this year twice and the experience here—you never think it’s going to happen, but it was just so hard not only for me but my mom, for the kids, for my sister who is in Los Angeles. Everybody else is in Miami. To a newly introduced beginning and thank you, God, for guiding me back to Florida where my family needs to be.”.
