FBI criminal database hacked

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (69 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that the FBI is investigating a reported major cyberattack targeting its national law enforcement database, the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), which holds sensitive criminal justice records, including information on fugitives, missing persons, criminal histories and stolen property. President Donald Trump mentioned he had just been briefed on the incident, describing it as “not good”. FRANCE 24’s Fraser Jackson reports from Washington DC.
