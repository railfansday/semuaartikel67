In a recent development, sen. Elissa Slotkin, D-Mich., stated Thursday she will back Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., as he challenges current Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer for the majority leader slot.

Van Hollen announced a bid for the role last week.

In a recent development, "I've been clear as well that I don't think that Senator Schumer is best positioned to be our next leader. I'm encouraged that people like Chris Van Hollen got in the race, and because I've been clear that I wasn't supporting Schumer, I will be supporting Chris Van Hollen," Slotkin stated in a video posted to social media.
