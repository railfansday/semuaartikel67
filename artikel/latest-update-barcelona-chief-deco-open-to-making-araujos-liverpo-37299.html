Barcelona sporting director Deco is open to the idea of letting Ronald Araujo join Liverpool permanently after a strong start to his loan spell at Anfield.

Araujo, a centre-back by trade, joined Liverpool on a season-long loan and has become an instant fan favourite on Merseyside with his combative displays at right-back.

The 27-year-old has made 15 appearances across all competitions, while also proving he is a threat in the final third after scoring twice and registering an assist.

It was reported that the deal includes an option to buy the Uruguay international for £47.14m (€55m), though it remains to be seen whether Liverpool are keen to activate it.

Sources indicate that jeremy Jacquet has impressed since joining from Rennes, with Virgil van Dijk also out of contract at the end of the 2026-27 season. Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley have returned to team training after long-term injuries.

Industry observers note that "I am happy for him," Deco told Catalunya Radio when asked about Araujo's positive start at Anfield. "We work for Barcelona and want what's best for the club – to win, to build the best possible squad – but we are also humans, and we also want to build relationships.

Recent findings suggest that "Ronald's been one of the club captains in recent years. He has had difficult moments, but also moments of great happiness, and he has been one of the leaders of this team.

"Coming back to football, things weren't going especially well for him on the pitch. He took the decision [to join Liverpool] and we respect it.

"The most key thing for us now is seeing Ronald recovered, happy, enjoying himself and in a positive place.

Recent findings suggest that "If he decides to stay, Liverpool has an option to make the deal permanent. They would also have to negotiate with him – it's not just about paying the clause – but we would be happy for several reasons [if the deal goes through].

"Firstly, because the move has worked out and because Ronald is happy and is able to continue his career successfully. We want players to succeed. Also, the money would not hurt, either.".

Araujo's move was reportedly seen as a one-off by Liverpool, who were short in defence ahead of Andoni Iraola's first season in charge.

According to recent reports, he was restricted to 16 starts in all competitions last season, having only made 17 in 2024-25. In each of Barcelona's previous three campaigns, Araujo started at least 30 games.

The defender was also granted a mental health break of just over a month midway through 2025-26, but he has wasted little time in settling into the Premier League.

In the top flight this season, only Jacquet (68) has won more duels than Araujo's 49, while he also ranks second for aerial duels won (30) and clearances (40) in Liverpool's ranks.

Deco confirmed there is a deadline for Liverpool to decide if they are willing to pay the fee to bring in Araujo, though he mentioned exceptions could be made to strike a deal.

Sources indicate that "In football, you can always negotiate," Deco added. "There is a deadline for exercising the option, just as there is when we bring players in on loan.

According to recent reports, "After that, you can always try to negotiate and work things out. It will depend on the circumstances at the time, on us, on the player and on various other factors.".
