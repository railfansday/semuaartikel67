“I love the romance of the past,” notes Ken Fulk, mastermind of the most significant updates to the Beverly Hills Hotel since the ’40s, when Paul R. Williams created many of its beloved spaces and its pink and green palette. Fulk has refreshed terraces and devised what he calls “the prettiest dining room in all of L.A., a very 1920s library bar, and an over-the-top Copacabana-style supper club,” opening early next year.

An unabashed Hollywood fanboy, Fulk aimed for old-school glamour with contemporary panache. “In my mind,” he claims, “Marlene Dietrich and Cary Grant could walk through the lot.”.
