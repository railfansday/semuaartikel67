In a recent development, BMW issued a “Park Outside” warning as part of a recall that covers nearly three-quarters of a million vehicles and includes a fire risk warning. The recall covers 720,796 vehicles ranging from the 2012 through the 2018 model years. The recall population includes some 3-series, 5-series, Z4, X1, X3, X4, X5 xDrive40e, 4-series Gran Coupe, 2-series convertible, 328xi Gran Turismo, 2-series coupe, 4-series convertible, 3-series coupe, and 3-series Sportswagon vehicles.

It has been revealed that the problem is centered on a connector for the water pump, which, over time, could deteriorate and allow moisture to enter the pump. If moisture enters the connector, it could result in a short circuit, which in turn could cause a vehicle fire.

The recall follows a similar recall issued on August 13, 2024, which also centered on the water pump. The remedy for that recall involved a protective shield to divert any residual fluid from the engine’s positive crankcase ventilation system’s air intake hose to keep it from entering the water pump’s electrical connector. Unfortunately, after that recall was performed, BMW was made aware of several instances of pump failures on vehicles that had the remedy performed. At the time of this newer recall, the organization is aware of 10 field instances in the United States believed to be related to the issue.

It has been revealed that the automaker is still working on a permanent fix for the affected vehicles. Dealer notification letters have already been sent, with interim owner notification letters scheduled to be sent on December 25. Owners concerned that their vehicle may be included in the recall population will have to wait until that day to search for their VIN.
