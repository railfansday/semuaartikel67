Rio Ngumoha is one of the most exciting young players in world football, and the teenager is already one of Liverpool's best players.

Mohamed Salah's departure left a clear gap in the Reds' forward line, and Sunday's win over Manchester United highlighted exactly why the club were right to trust Ngumoha to fill it.

The England international started on the left wing, with Bradley Barcola on the right and Alexander Isak through the middle.

Being the only one of the front three to cost less than £120 million, expectations on the youngster going into the massive clash were understandably lower, but Rio showed that he is every bit as dangerous as Isak and Barcola.

Ngumoha scoring Liverpool's third topped off the phenomenal performance, even leading Reds' legend Jamie Carragher to say the teenager was the "best player on the pitch" just after the goal.

Sunday's game against United was only Rio's second start in the Premier League this season, but it is very hard to argue the winger doesn't deserve to start more often from here on out.
