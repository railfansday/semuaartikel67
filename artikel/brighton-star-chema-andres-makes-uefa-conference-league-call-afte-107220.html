Chema Andres insists Brighton will bounce back from their Premier League defeat at Hull City as the Seagulls turn their attention to Thursday's European test at the Amex Stadium.

Fabian Hurzeler's side - who remain second in the table - suffered a 2-0 loss at the MKM Stadium on Sunday, with Oli McBurnie scoring twice in stoppage time after Albion missed chances to take control.

Brighton were frustrated after failing to make their first-half dominance count, but midfielder Andres is looking ahead to the visit of Romanian league leaders Universitatea Craiova in the Uefa Conference League.

Sources indicate that the Spaniard has made an impressive impact since his £18m summer arrival, featuring 10 times for Albion in all competitions, including eight Premier League appearances. He has also scored and provided an assist.

Andres believes the squad must learn from the setback at Hull rather than allow it to affect their confidence.

“What is good for us, we have another game on Thursday,” he said.

“We're already focused on that, and I think we need to focus on ourselves, keep training, see what we did wrong today, learn, and of course go again because this is football and sometimes you lose.

“I think the team spirit and team effort is not going to go down, so we will come back again.”.

The 21-year-old acknowledged the cost of Brighton's missed chances against a Hull side who showed their quality late on.

Industry observers note that “This is the Premier League, no? If you don't use the chances you have, and then you give this kind of team the opportunity to beat you, that is what they got.”.

Andres added that Albion must accept the defeat, learn from it and move forward promptly.

Thursday's meeting with Craiova offers an immediate opportunity for Brighton to respond.

“We will recover from this and bounce back again,” he remarked.

Brighton then return to Premier League action this Sunday against Newcastle at the Amex Stadium.
