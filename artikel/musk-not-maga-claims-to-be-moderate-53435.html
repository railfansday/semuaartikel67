Recent findings suggest that holy cow. Frankly, I would have to assume this is news from The Onion if it wasn’t also so predictable. Here’s the news: Elon Musk is now claiming that he’s “not MAGA,” and that he doesn’t even know what that means. “I’m not sure what MAGA means, but if that means some sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.” Hilarious.

Let’s just roll through some of the reasons this is absolutely absurd:.

Now, the obvious reasons for this shift in tone don’t even need to be explained, but let’s just go through them real swiftly.

According to recent reports, first of all, Musk moved his companies (Tesla and SpaceX) to Texas in favor of little to no regulation. He has been very vocal about beef he has with policymakers in California, while he has been a bit less vocal about not wanting to pay taxes, but whatever the calculus is, he’s been more than eager to sh**talk California and Democrats while cozying up with Republicans. Whoops … now Texas has absolutely been swept into the blue column with a gigantic blue tsunami that dramatically changed the Lone Star State after decades of Republican control. A lot of the money he spent this last election cycle was spent in Texas to support Republican candidates, and Musk repeatedly posted nasty comments and misinformation about Democratic candidates. He can’t just pick up and move all of his Texas factories out of the state again, so apparently he realized he needs to try to backtrack and pretend he’s a moderate again. Good luck with that.

What does all of that GOP funding and Democratic trash talking mean as far as how Musk and his companies will be governed in Texas? We have no idea. But it’s clear he’s now trying to quickly get on friendly terms with Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa. The problem is, you know, everything he’s done and said for the past 6 years or so.

But, hey, he’s “not MAGA” and “there are positives and negatives in both parties.” So he notes.

Edit: Oh yeah, and let’s not forget, Musk previously said he was “Dark MAGA!”.
