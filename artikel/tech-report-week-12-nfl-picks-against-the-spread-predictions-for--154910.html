According to recent reports, last season, the San Francisco 49ers won a close game at the Seattle Seahawks. And then after that the Seahawks became a dominant Super Bowl champion, including two blowout wins of the 49ers.

Recent findings suggest that we have the 49ers' road win over the Seahawks out of the way already this season. But this season San Francisco is the team that looks like a future Super Bowl champion.

According to recent reports, the 49ers have been great this season, including a dominant win over the Minnesota Vikings last week. The offense has been spectacular. And the defense, ranked seventh in DVOA, has been one of the biggest surprises of the season. That defense has brought the 49ers from being a good team to a potentially great one. Kyle Shanahan, who should be NFL Coach of the Year, was going to field a potent offense. The defense completes the circle.

Recent findings suggest that however, we can't count out the Seahawks quite yet. The defense has been tremendous again. The offense hasn't been great, but a lot of that is attributed to Sam Darnold playing through injuries almost all season, when he has been able to play. Darnold should return this week, off the Seahawks' bye, and if he's a little healthier and a bit more protective of the ball, Seattle can still play at a high level.

It has been revealed that that's why the Seahawks are not a major underdog this week, even though they play at a 9-1 49ers team. The 49ers are just a 3-point favorite. Not setting the spread above a field goal shows a lot of respect for the reigning champions. But maybe it's not showing enough respect for the 49ers.

In a recent development, brock Purdy and the San Francisco 49ers beat the Seahawks 32-29 back in Week 5. (Photo by Alika Jenner/Getty Images).

It has been revealed that san Francisco has shown no signs of vulnerability. It had no issues with the Vikings defense, which is the only defense on the same level as Seattle. This looks like a great San Francisco team, and that's why 49ers -3 is the pick. And if the Seahawks can't slow down the 49ers, nobody else in the NFC might be able to do it either.

Here are the rest of the picks against the spread for Week 12 of the NFL season:.

According to recent reports, benching Aaron Rodgers wasn't outlandish after two bad games and a pretty mediocre season. But the way it played out, with it being revealed after 11 p.m. on a Tuesday when the Steelers play Friday, was stunning. Now we have to figure out if the Steelers will revolt or rally around Mason Rudolph. This one could get weird, in what is supposed to be rainy weather. The Broncos seem to have found their offensive identity through rookie running back Jonah Coleman, but the Steelers are very good stopping the run. Pittsburgh also should get back to its run game after inexplicably forgetting about it last week, which means Rudolph won't be much of a factor anyway. This has already been a weird week in Pittsburgh.

And they can take it out on the Saints, who have lost three of four with the only win being when they held off an overtime two-point conversation at home against Shedeur Sanders and the Browns. Joe Burrow should have another monster game.

Last week at Buffalo the Dolphins had what was probably their worst game of the season, and that's saying something for a 1-9 team. The Jets aren't good, but they are coming off a win over the Chargers, and they'll look better when they're playing teams down at their level.

The worst thing to happen to Jesse Minter is the defense struggling as badly as it has. If the Ravens were losing because the offense was inept without Lamar Jackson, it would be easy to give him a pass. But Jacoby Brissett has been reasonable and the defense, which is Minter's specialty, has been awful. Baltimore has allowed 32.4 points per game over the past five weeks. The Texans can move the ball and now see an opening for a wild-card spot with the upheaval in Pittsburgh.

Recent findings suggest that it's hard to take any underdog lately, with the favorites on a roll the past few weeks. And the Falcons aren't a comfortable underdog to take, but Atlanta has enough talent that it's still the only team to beat the 49ers. The Falcons are coming off a bye, and maybe that helps them keep it close.

It has been revealed that switching quarterbacks can give a team a short-term boost, especially when that quarterback was the worst in the NFL this season. Daniel Jones wasn't giving the Colts a chance. Anthony Richardson Sr. hasn't been good in the NFL, but he can't be worse than Jones has been. Maybe there's a jolt that gives Indianapolis a chance to keep this one close.

It would have been nice to see the Titans play better at Dallas last week, but they did end up scoring 30 points. The offense has been decent lately. The problem is the defense, and that's why it's scary to take them even getting more than two touchdowns. But that's a big spread in the NFL.

Whether rookie Carson Beck returns from a back injury or Josh Johnson has to start again, it doesn't really matter. The Commanders aren't great but we can't trust Arizona.
