Chelsea secured a deserved 1-0 victory over Manchester City at Stamford Bridge to leapfrog their rivals and reclaim top spot in the WSL table.

Sandy Baltimore's first-half goal was enough to ensure Chelsea inflicted City's first defeat of the season and blow the title race wide open.

After keeping the reigning WSL champions at bay following an early barrage, Chelsea grew in confidence but still lacked the cutting edge that had seen them held to back-to-back draws in the past week.

A moment of magic from Baltimore in the fourth minute of stoppage time ensured history would not repeat itself.

City goalkeeper Ayaka Yamashita was sent off in the 75th minute for a rash challenge on Alyssa Thompson, a move which appeared to invigorate the visitors, but Chelsea held firm to pick up all three points.
