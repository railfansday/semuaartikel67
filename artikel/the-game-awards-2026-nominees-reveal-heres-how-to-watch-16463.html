The Game Awards 2026 Nominees Reveal: Here's How to Watch

🌐 Source: Polygon • 📅 13 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (409 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • According to recent reports, fans will be able to watch the TGAs Nomination Special on Tuesday via YouTube and Twitch.
  • The Game Awards 2026 Nomination Special will be livestreamed on Tuesday, Nov.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Game Awards 2026 is fast approaching, and this year's award nominees are set to be unveiled on Tuesday, Nov. 17. The nominees will be revealed during a dedicated livestream — that just happens to be coming two days ahead of the year's most anticipated game, Grand Theft Auto 6.

According to recent reports, fans will be able to watch the TGAs Nomination Special on Tuesday via YouTube and Twitch. Host and executive producer Geoff Keighley will announce the games, studios, and talent nominated for this year's awards, which will include Game of the Year.

The Game Awards 2026 Nomination Special will be livestreamed on Tuesday, Nov. 17, starting at 9 a.m. PST/12 p.m. EST. In past years, Keighley has made the special pretty efficient; the event usually takes around 10 minutes. Nominees for Game of the Year are typically declared at the very end of the special.

Voting for fan-based awards will go live immediately after The Game Awards 2026 Nomination Special concludes.

Sources indicate that yes. According to The Game Awards' official website, games eligible for this year's main awards "must be made commercially available to customers on or before Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 p.m. PT." Grand Theft Auto 6 will squeak in ahead of that; the game is officially being released on Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.

Sources indicate that for the TGA's Players’ Voice category, games must ship on or before Nov. 30, 2026, organizers say. So if you're thinking about voting for GTA 6, you have ample time to spend with the game to make up your mind.

It has been revealed that the Game Awards 2026 will be held on Thursday, Dec. 10. The show starts at 4:30 p.m. PST/7:30 p.m. EST, including the traditional 30-minute pre-show.

Industry observers note that this year's The Game Awards will stream on more platforms than ever, with Disney Plus, Live TV on PS5, and Roblox joining the long list of platforms on which to watch. The show will also be available on YouTube, Twitch, Steam, TikTok Live, X, Kick, Facebook, and Instagram Live. In China, the show will be available on more than 20 platforms, including Baidu, Bilibili, Douyin, DouYu, Huya, WeChat, Weibo and Xiaohongshu. Additional partners include CHZZK and SOOP in South Korea; NicoNico in Japan; Vidio in Indonesia; Now TV in Hong Kong; mewatch in Singapore; and BIGO LIVE and Likee across Southeast Asia.
