EXCLUSIVE: Hollywood vet Stephen Herek, whose credits include Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure and 101 Dalmatians, is attached to direct rom-com Emma’s Dilemmas, which is due to shoot in Italy and Fresh York City late next year.

It has been revealed that the movie follows Emma Tate, “a young Brooklyn artist navigating the unpredictable world of modern romance, and her free-spirited expat mother, Georgina, whose complicated love life in Florence proves that romantic dilemmas don’t necessarily get easier with age. As their adventures unfold in Italy, both women discover that the path to love, self-discovery and happiness is rarely a straight line.” Related Stories News UK Culture Minister Delivers Emotional Plea To YouTube To "Set An Example" & Remove Russell Brand's Channel News Senate And House Lawmakers Added As Cosponsors To New Federal Film & TV Incentive Legislation.

The feature is being produced by Phil Viardo for Former Prodigy Media (The Inventor), alongside the film’s writer Anya Migdal and Drew Moerlein of Moerlein Entertainment, with Former Prodigy Media co-founder and former Brady Bunch actor Christopher Knight executive-producing. Watch on Deadline.

It has been revealed that also joining is Panorama Films, one of Italy’s most established production service companies, whose credits include Eat Pray Love, The White Lotus, Mission: Impossible and Jason Bourne. The organization most recently worked on both of Mel Gibson’s Resurrection Of The Christ films. Panorama principals Ute Leonhardt and Marco Valerio Pugini will serve as executive producers.

The project, based on Migdal’s short film of the same name, was brought to Herek by actor, producer and businessman Knight, a longtime friend.

According to recent reports, herek, also known for Critters, the Oscar-nominated Mr. Holland’s Opus, Rock Star, Mighty Ducks, The Three Musketeers and Holy Man, most recently directed Netflix rom-com Our Little Secret, starring Lindsay Lohan and Kristin Chenoweth, and the streamer’s Dog Gone, with Rob Lowe.

In a recent development, “Stephen has an extraordinary gift for finding the humanity and humor in stories that connect with audiences across generations and around the globe,” said Viardo. “From his iconic comedies to his recent Netflix success, his work demonstrates an ability to deliver films that are entertaining, emotionally resonant and evergreen. We’re thrilled to work alongside him to bring Emma’s Dilemmas to life through his distinguished lens.”.

Former Prodigy Media is also collaborating with writer Migdal on two additional projects: the comedy All Bets Are Off and coming-of-age feature The Heart Will Break.

Herek is represented by David Gersh at the Gersh Agency.
