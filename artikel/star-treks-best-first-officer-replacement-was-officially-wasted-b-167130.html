Star Trek's Best First Officer Replacement Was Officially Wasted By Paramount+

Star Trek: Discovery introduced the best First Officer replacement for Captain Saru (Doug Jones), but audiences only got one season with him because Paramount+ canceled the series. Star Trek: Discovery premiered in 2017, and its success sparked multiple upcoming Star Trek series and a streaming movie executive produced by Alex Kurtzman and Secret Hideout. However, Paramount+ ended the series with season 5 in 2024.

Industry observers note that the USS Discovery was unique in several ways, starting with its spore displacement hub drive allowing it to jump to a new location instantaneously. Discovery's bridge, however, saw a game of musical chairs, with multiple Starfleet Officers (and a couple pretending to be) taking command of the starship before Captain Michael Burnham (Sonequa Martin-Green) became Disco's permanent master at the end of season 3.

Naturally, the USS Discovery's captaincy in flux meant the First Officer would be, as well. Michael Burnham, Saru (Doug Jones), and even Lieutenant Sylvia Tilly (Mary Wiseman) took turns as Discovery's Number One. Saru surprisingly served as Burnham's XO while also holding the rank of Captain. Star Trek: Discovery season 5 ended this turnover with a fantastic replacement for Saru who only lasted that lone season.

In a recent development, star Trek: Discovery season 5 introduced Commander Rayner (Callum Keith Rennie), the best replacement First Officer the series produced. The former Captain of the USS Antares, Rayner was a Kellerun who had a distinguished 30-year Starfleet career before he was forced into retirement for his extreme methods in hunting down Moll (Eve Harlow) and L'ak (Elias Toufexis).

Sources indicate that surprisingly, Captain Burnham personally requested Rayner as her upcoming First Officer despite their animosity. Rayner accepted a demotion to Commander and became Burnham's Number One, and that's when the magic happened. Gruff, no-nonsense, and not keen on the USS Discovery crew's overly-familial culture, Rayner was the ideal foil for the passionate and action-oriented Captain Burnham.

Rayner was also a stark contrast to Saru, who became a Federation Ambassador and married T'Rina (Tara Rosling), the President of Ni'Var. Burnham and Saru shared a close-knit bond, but Rayner challenged Michael and kept Discovery's crew on business differently than Saru. In Star Trek: Discovery's series finale, Rayner becomes acting Captain, finally taking the center seat, and leads the saucer-separated USS Discovery to defeat the Breen.

Sadly, Star Trek: Discovery fans only enjoyed a brief 10 episodes with Commander Rayner and the formidable presence of Callum Keith Rennie. The Canadian actor who memorably played the Cylon Leoben Conroy brought some of the welcome grit of Battlestar Galactica into Star Trek: Discovery. Rayner emerged as a fan favorite, but his story was cut short when it was just getting started.

Star Trek: Discovery was expected to run for seven seasons, just as the series set in Star Trek: The Next Generation's 24th century did under executive producer Rick Berman. Discovery wrapped production on season 5, and showrunner Michelle Paradise hadn't yet begun to map out season 6 when Paramount+ delivered an unexpected axe to Disco.

Star Trek: Picard ended in 2023 after its planned three seasons, but Paramount+ ignored fan outcry and never put its proposed spinoff, Star Trek: Legacy, into development.

In 2023, Skydance Media began purchasing Paramount Global, which was completed in 2025. The rapid expansion of Paramount+'s Star Trek franchise under executive producer Alex Kurtzman suddenly stopped, and the streamer began canceling its existing Star Trek series. Star Trek: Discovery was the second show Paramount+ ended after it canceled and purged all episodes of Star Trek: Prodigy from its service.

It has been revealed that star Trek: Discovery's fifth and final season premiered on Paramount+ in March 2024 and was over in late May. Star Trek: Lower Decks ended with season 5 in December of that year. Star Trek: Strange Latest Worlds ultimately received five seasons, ending in 2027, while the newest series, Star Trek: Starfleet Academy, only got two seasons before it also ends in 2027. With that, the decade-long Kurtzman era that began with Star Trek: Discovery comes to a close.

While small fry in terms of the massive picture of Star Trek's streaming era, Commander Rayner was an unfortunate casualty of corporate acquisitions and mergers. Star Trek: Discovery was intended to go on to season 6 and continue Commander Rayner's story, likely to culminate in a seventh and final season. In a better timeline, that's what happened.

Star Trek: Discovery is streaming on Paramount+.
