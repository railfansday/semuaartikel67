SPONSORED POST: As cyberattacks become more sophisticated, organizations need to look beyond prevention and ensure they can recover critical data swiftly and reliably.

Cybersecurity has long been a contest between attackers finding latest ways in and defenders trying to keep them out. Artificial intelligence is making that contest more complicated, giving cybercriminals new ways to develop and adapt their attacks while putting additional pressure on conventional cyber defences.

For enterprise IT leaders, the challenge extends beyond preventing a breach. What happens when an attacker gets through? Can compromised data be identified, clean copies recovered, and essential systems brought back online without prolonged disruption?

These questions are becoming increasingly important as organizations confront both traditional threats and emerging AI-powered cyberattacks. A successful defense strategy must, therefore, account for what happens after an incident, not just the measures designed to prevent one.

This is where cyber-resilient enterprise storage has an key role to play. Rather than relying solely on traditional backup processes, organizations need ways to protect critical data, detect potential compromise,  establish which copies can safely be recovered, and restore those copies as quickly as possible.

Infinidat addresses this challenge through its InfiniBox and InfiniBox SSA enterprise storage platforms, which incorporate the organization’s InfiniSafe cyber resilience and recovery software suite.

InfiniSafe combines immutable snapshots, which cannot be altered or deleted during their protected retention period, with logical air-gapping to isolate the management plane from the data plane. Fenced forensic environments provide a separate space for validating data before recovery.

According to recent reports, the technology also supports automated cyber protection, allowing security monitoring data center wide cyber security software applications or a Security Operations Center (SOC) to automatically talk to InfiniBoxes and trigger immutable snapshots when suspicious activity is detected. This helps reduce the window in which critical data might be exposed.

For organizations concerned about recovering compromised information, optional InfiniSafe Cyber Detection uses AI-based analysis to help identify cyber affected data and locate the last known good copy of the data for recovery.

Speed is another consideration. Infinidat guarantees recovery of immutable snapshots in one minute or less for InfiniBox and InfiniBox SSA, subject to the guarantee’s terms and conditions.

Ultimately, cyber resilience isn’t about assuming every cyberattack can be stopped. It’s about ensuring that when defences are breached, organizations have the tools and processes to recover trusted data and limit the operational consequences.

For enterprise IT teams, that makes recovery readiness an essential part of the cybersecurity conversation.
