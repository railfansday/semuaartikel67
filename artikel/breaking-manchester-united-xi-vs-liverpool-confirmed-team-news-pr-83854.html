Manchester United will be managed by Mauricio Pochettino for the first time as they face fierce rivals Liverpool this weekend.

The Argentine was appointed as Michael Carrick’s successor last week and only arrived in the UK on Wednesday, leaving him just a handful of sessions to prepare his Man Utd side for a crunch fixture at Anfield.

Industry observers note that as such, wholesale changes from life under Carrick are not forecast, at least in terms of setup. After all, both managers have a penchant for the same 4-2-3-1 formation.

Carlos Baleba remains sidelined with a hamstring injury sustained against Roma in the Champions League, with Benjamin Sesko also out until the Fresh Year, but Pochettino may well be boosted by the returns of Marcus Rashford and Luke Shaw.

The latter seems the more likely of the two to be available on Sunday, with Rashford yet to be pictured in training since the international break.

Pochettino is known to be fond of Rashford, and has reportedly attempted to sign him for other clubs he has managed, meaning he can expect to get the nod if fit.

Should Shaw slot in at left-back, he may do so as one of two fresh faces in the defence. Lisandro Martinez has served his red card suspension and will be available to partner Harry Maguire, with Diogo Dalot likely at right-back.

A midfield pairing of Youri Tielemans and Kobbie Mainoo should sit behind the trio of Matheus Cunha, Bruno Fernandes and Amad Diallo, with Bryan Mbeumo leading the line.

Patrick Dorgu is expected to be available, but it remains to be seen whether he can unseat Cunha on the left flank. He could also deputise for Shaw if the latter is not fit enough to start.

Leaked drone footage of United’s Thursday training session appeared to show Pochettino working on tactics with a tight-knit group of 10 outfield players, leading some to speculate that he might be dealing with Sunday’s starters.

The presence of Joshua Zirkzee and Harry Amass in the group rather debunks that theory, though, given that neither player has started a Premier League match so far this season.
