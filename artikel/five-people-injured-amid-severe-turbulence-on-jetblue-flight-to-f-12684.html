📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Some passengers were taken to hospital after flight safely landed at Fort-Lauderdale-Hollywood airport.

Five people were injured when severe turbulence struck a JetBlue flight landing in Florida on Tuesday morning.

The aircraft was coming in from Puerto Rico when the turbulence hit, and it landed safely at its destination of Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood international airport. Five people reported having suffered injuries with some requiring hospitalisation.

“The aircraft landed safely at approximately 6:58 a.m. and proceeded to Terminal 3, where Broward Sheriff Fire Rescue personnel were standing by to evaluate those reporting injuries. Fire rescue personnel identified five individuals reporting injuries or requiring evaluation,” Broward sheriff’s office fire rescue said in a statement to television news station WRAL.

Turbulence has been a risk of air travel since flight began – but experts believe it is likely to get worse as the climate crisis plays out.

An analysis of atmospheric data collected between 1979 and 2020 found severe clear-air turbulence increased by 55% – and moderate and light turbulence had increased 37% and 17%. Scientists at the University of Reading in the UK conducted that analysis.
