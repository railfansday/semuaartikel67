Jensen Huang, who resigned in disgrace from the world’s most valuable firm, Nvidia, less than two weeks ago, has been reinstated as CEO and president.

The board of directors announced today (Monday) that it “voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA.”.

Huang resigned after it was revealed he engaged in a series of extramarital affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy.

The relationship with Alice Huang (no relation) raised national security concerns; Washington restricts exports of advanced AI chips to China. Nvidia has spent years lobbying to loosen those restrictions.

It has been revealed that “In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk,” the statement adds.

According to recent reports, chair of the Board, Harvey C. Jones, notes, “The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers.

“We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Firm. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as NVIDIA’s Chief Financial Officer.”.

The Guardian notes that the speed of Huang’s return is surprising, given the investigation into his conduct has yet to be completed.

Nvidia commissioned a law firm to conduct a “thorough and independent” investigation into Huang’s relationship with Alice Huang. The attorney general of California is also examining whether Nvidia contravened U.S. export restrictions on its semiconductor chips.

President Trump has welcomed the news, calling Huang “a Great Patriot and National Treasure.”.

“The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO. The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at NVIDIA in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy,” Trump writes on Truth Social.

Recent findings suggest that “The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security. The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.”.

Nvidia shares are expected to surge when Wall Street opens imminently.
