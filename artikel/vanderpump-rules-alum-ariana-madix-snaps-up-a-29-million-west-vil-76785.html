“Vanderpump Rules” alum Ariana Madix and boyfriend Daniel Wai have taken an exciting new step together after snapping up a $2.9 million loft in New York City.

The couple—who have been in a long-distance relationship since 2023—purchased a recently-renovated condominium in the Printing House building in the West Village, according to Curbed.

Although Madix still holds ties to Los Angeles—with a $1.6 million home she purchased in 2024 and her sandwich shop, Something About Her—she’s been spending much more time in the Massive Apple in recent years. It’s where Wai, a personal trainer, lives full time.

It has been revealed that of course, there’s also her Broadway career. The “Love Island USA” host made her on-stage debut as Roxie Hart in “Chicago” in January 2024 and is currently starring as Connie Francis in the musical, “Just in Time” at the Circle in the Square Theatre. Her run has been extended through Nov. 28.

Recent findings suggest that the 1,763-square-foot residence boasts 15-foot ceilings and spans two levels. According to the listing, the home has been configured into a three-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom layout, and includes “top-of-the-line finishes” throughout.

According to recent reports, the entrance of the unit opens into the “modern” kitchen, which has a dedicated dining space and leads way to the double-height living room. This floor of the home also has a powder room and a “massive primary bedroom” with a “luxurious five-piece bathroom and double walk-in closets.”.

A mezzanine level—accessible by a statement-making steel staircase—hosts the second and third bedrooms, as well as a white marble-finished second full bathroom.

“The space is made complete by abundant storage options throughout, intelligent home pre-wiring, remotely operable central HVAC, and automatic blinds,” the listing adds.

Aside from its prime location in the West Village, the building itself offers quite a few amenities.

“The Printing House is an iconic, fully-staffed, industrial building that anchors the West Village and was reimagined into luxury loft style residences,” the listing explains.

The security was likely a draw for Madix, whose California home was broken into just last month. Burglars shattered the home’s back door, stealing jewelry and other valuable possessions on Oct. 17.

The former printing factory was first built in 1910, and was later converted into residential condos in the 1980s. Today, there’s an Equinox on-site, a rooftop pool, and a beautifully-landscaped sun deck that offers breathtaking views of the Manhattan skyline.

While fans may associate the reality TV star with the West Coast, New York City is actually where she got her start.

It has been revealed that before she placed roots in L.A., the Melbourne, FL, native moved to New York after college in hopes of pursuing a career in sketch comedy. She became a bartender at Butter, a famed Midtown Manhattan restaurant located within the Cassa Hotel.

Recent findings suggest that “Some parts of it are like riding a bike,” Madix told Extra in 2023, when she had returned to Fresh York for the first time. “Taking the train and walking everywhere, things like that. I was like, ‘Ok, I remember how to do this.’”.

The actress noted that in some ways, it feels like “a different city” than the one she had lived in as a recent college graduate.

Industry observers note that “I was here pre-Instagram, pre-iPhone, pre-social media. It is very different in a lot of ways, but I think that’s just, you know, the world at large,” she said.

The fresh pad isn’t the only real estate move Madix has made this year.

Following a yearslong legal battle with her ex-boyfriend, Tom Sandoval, the former couple finally cut ties with their Valley Village abode in an off-market transaction this spring, pulling in $3.1 million.

In April 2026, TMZ reported that Madix and Sandoval’s listing agent had been preparing to put the five-bedroom home on the market, but an anonymous buyer swooped in to make an offer the former couple simply couldn’t refuse.

"We’ve been keeping a secret! We had this beautiful home in Valley Village that we were prepping for market and before we could post about it or list it, we got it under contract for full-price off-market!" listing agent Ashley Fultz with DMF Realty wrote in an Instagram caption.

Industry observers note that the pair purchased the six-bedroom modern farmhouse-style dwelling in 2019 for $2.08 million. Before their highly publicized split in 2023, they completely transformed the home with a massive renovation which included a live greenery wall and a dedicated room for content creation and social media.
