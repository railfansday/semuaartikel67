If the Galaxy S26 Ultra is too expensive, and you can go without the Privacy Display, pick this instead. The S25 Ultra features many of the same features, including a large and bright display, an S Pen, significant battery and a 200MP main camera.

Industry observers note that samsung's take on the passport-shaped foldable is easily one of the best phones of the year. But it's also a few hundred dollars cheaper.

In a recent development, if the latest Galaxy S26 is up your alley, Walmart has knocked it back down to $799, reversing the recent price hike. You get the latest Galaxy AI features, a 6.3-inch OLED display, a 4,300 mAh battery, 50MP main camera, 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage standard.

In a recent development, if you want a bigger screen and less-flashy hardware, the S25 Plus has your back — and a price to match. It has a 6.7-inch display, 45W charging, 50MP rear cameras, a 4,900 mAh battery, and all the latest Galaxy AI features.

The super thin Galaxy S25 Edge is still available, and it's been given a nice Black Friday discount. So if you want a skinny phone with two solid cameras running Android, now's your chance.

It has been revealed that if Samsung's price hikes have you down, the Galaxy S26 FE is the perfect solution — offering flagship level specs at a newly-discounted price. For your money you get solid camera and battery performance, including a telephoto lens, and a large 6.7-inch display and the latest Galaxy AI software.

It may not be the most exciting foldable around, nor is this the biggest discount, but if you want a Pixel Fold device this is the best discount around right now. Just be aware that you need to pay an extra $180 for Mint's Unlimited strategy to be eligible.

According to recent reports, one of the best cheap phones you can buy, the Nothing 4a Pro offers great cameras and battery life, including a 50MP telephoto lens — which is a rarity at this price point. According to recent reports, if you want a cheap compact foldable without the added data blueprint, the 2025 Motorola Razr is the one to pick.
