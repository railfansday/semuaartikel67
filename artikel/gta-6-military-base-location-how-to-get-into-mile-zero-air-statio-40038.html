📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It's a bit of a drive (or boat, or flight) to get all the way out to the Mile Zero military base on the big bridges to the southern keys but it's actually not that far away from the first house you've got access to at the start of the game in Key Lento.

You'll get a wanted level if you get spotted on base (and wind up in a pretty immediate gunfight for it) but this is still the easiest way to steal a tank or get your hands on a combat jet.

Mile Zero Air Station Southeast.

The easiest way to get into the military base without a full gunfight is to use the stunt jump Mile Zero Air Station Southeast. The name states it all: this jump is on the southeast corner of the base along the southern access road. You'll have to weave through a few slightly-manned concrete barricade checkpoints and take a bit of gunfire to reach it where there's a major dirt mound leading to the barbed wire fence around the base. Take your vehicle over that at speed to get credit for the stunt jump and land yourself inside the base.
