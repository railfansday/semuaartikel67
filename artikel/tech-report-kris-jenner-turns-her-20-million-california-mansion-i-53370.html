Kris Jenner is welcoming the holiday season with a glowing display at her California mansion, where warm white lights outline the gates, hedges, roofline, and doorways.

The reality TV star, 71, shared a look at the decorations in a video posted to Instagram on Nov. 13, giving followers a view of the home’s exterior as the entrance gates opened.

The clip is set to “Somewhere in My Memory,” the instrumental piece associated with the film "Home Alone.".

As the gates move apart, the lights come into view across the landscaping and around the house, creating a bright welcome along the property’s entrance.

In a recent development, “It’s my favorite time of the year!” Jenner wrote in the caption. She described the setup as “the chicest Christmas lights in all the land” and “just magical! I could stare at them all night.”.

According to recent reports, the display extends beyond the immediate entrance. Trees, bushes, and other landscaping are also illuminated, making the lights visible throughout the grounds.

The video focuses on the home’s exterior, where the warm white lighting brings attention to the landscaping and the architectural outlines of the mansion.

Jenner’s post arrives as her family begins sharing its holiday decorating for the year.

Her daughter Khloé Kardashian indicated off the lights at her own Los Angeles home earlier in November.

Thousands of white lights were arranged around the trees, windows, and doors, giving the exterior a gingerbread house-inspired look. Kardashian credited lighting installation manufacturer The Christmas Brothers with the work.

The holiday displays have become a recurring part of the Kardashian-Jenner family’s seasonal social media posts.

Last year, Jenner’s daughters Kim and Kourtney Kardashian gave followers a peek at another festive detail at their mother’s home: a bunk room prepared for the grandchildren.

The sleeping area featured several sets of bunk beds dressed with red checked bedspreads, Christmas wreaths, and twinkling lights.

Kourtney described the setup as “magical” in an Instagram Stories post, while Kim shared her own reaction to the room, asking how cute her mother’s bunk bed room was for the children.

Jenner also shared glimpses of the family’s interior holiday decorations in photos from their Christmas Eve celebration in 2025.

The images highlighted a fireplace dressed with garland and lights, with stockings hanging from the mantel.

A collection of Christmas trees stood beneath the staircase, each dripping in ornaments. The dining table was arranged with red linens, plaid dishes, greenery, and reindeer-inspired candleholders.

This year’s video turns the attention back outside, offering a quick look at the lights welcoming guests to Jenner’s home.

With the gates opening onto illuminated landscaping and the house outlined in white lights, the display gives the family’s holiday season a festive start.
