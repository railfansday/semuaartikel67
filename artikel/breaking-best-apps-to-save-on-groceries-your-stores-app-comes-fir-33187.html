In a recent development, to save on groceries, start with your store’s own app if it has digital coupons, which cost nothing and load in a few taps before each trip. Then add Ibotta for the name brands you buy, and Flipp if you shop more than one store’s sales. Stop there, since every extra app asks for its own taps each trip.

Industry observers note that grocery prices rose 1.8% over the 12 months through September 2026, the Bureau of Labor Statistics’ Consumer Price Index demonstrates. Its Consumer Expenditure Survey puts the average household’s grocery spending at $6,491 for 2025, which works out to about $540 a month. The apps here cut that bill in different ways, and we weighed each by what it saves, how many taps it asks of you, and how soon the savings reach you.

Receipt scanning apps that pay points on any receipt you photograph, whatever you bought, are a separate kind from these.

Each app’s digital coupons work only at its own company’s stores, and they come off at checkout once you load them to your account and enter your phone number or scan your card.

Buy the average household’s $540 a month of groceries at Kroger or one of its other chains, and the points come to about $5 off groceries or $7.50 off a 15-gallon fill-up, by our math.

If you buy most groceries at Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, or a sister chain, the points from the average household’s $540 a month reach $15 off groceries about every two months, by our math.

For more information, read our full Safeway forU Review.
