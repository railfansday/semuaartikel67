A cheese tray is hardly the most exciting thing you can order on an airplane, but American Airlines does this one very well. I recently enjoyed its buy-on-board fruit and cheese plate in economy class and would choose it over the comparable offerings on Delta or United. Let’s take a closer look in this week’s Meal of the Week.

Industry observers note that american sells its fruit and cheese plate for $13 or 1,300 AAdvantage miles (please folks, don’t use miles to pay for it). The current selection includes:.

I loved all three varieties of cheese (I’m not fan of bleu or brie, so very happy not to have to waste anything).

The crackers were excellent too. That might sound like a minor detail, but good cheese deserves good crackers, and these complemented the cheese very well…oh how I loathe soda crackers.

The apples were sweet and crisp, I enjoyed the single fig, and I loved finishing with the Toblerone. A little chocolate at the end was just right…that’s often my dessert at home too.

I have enjoyed cheese plates on Delta and United too, but American offers the superior economy class version, in my opinion. I really liked the blueberry artigiano and smoked black pepper cheddar.

This was a late evening flight where I had planned to eat in the lounge before my flight but due to a flight delay jumped onto an earlier flight. It was 8:00 pm, I had not eaten, and had I been flying on United I would have been out of luck since you have to pre-order any food onboard beyond snack boxes.

I figured I would have to make a Taco Bell run in LA when I landed, but this proved enough..and my body thanks me.

It has been revealed that american Airlines has a very good buy-on-board cheese plate. I enjoyed all three cheeses, the crackers were excellent, the apples were sweet, and the Toblerone was a nice dessert.

In a recent development, simple food can be very satisfying when the ingredients are good. This is something American does better than Delta or United in economy class, and I would gladly order it again.

p.s. If you’re still feeling peckish, you might also enjoy a bag of salted, mixed nuts from Nuts & Clark…$11 or 1,100 miles. Also recommended!
