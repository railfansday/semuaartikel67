According to recent reports, publisher H2 Interactive and MOSS development subsidiary Kaminari Games have delayed shoot ’em up sequel Caladrius 2/Dark Element from its previously planned December 10, 2026 release date to January 27, 2027 worldwide. It will be available for PlayStation 5, Switch, and PC via Steam.

Additionally, the following changes will be applied to the PlayStation 5 version in order to comply with platform guidelines:.

“Malice” is a visual effect that obscures parts of the character artwork. Its appearance and removal options differ slightly in the PlayStation 5 version.

Recent findings suggest that these differences do not affect gameplay or balance. The character illustrations themselves are identical across platforms.

Here is an overview of the game, via H2 Interactive:.

The Caladrius series returns. Thirteen years after the original title made its debut, the series is reborn with a brand-fresh installment that further evolves the shocking experience fans remember. Set in a dark gothic world where beauty and darkness intertwine, Caladrius 2/Dark Element delivers exhilarating 2D bullet-hell shooting action. Fight your way across battlefields engulfed in darkness alongside a cast of unique characters brought to life by talented new illustrators.
