There is no longer any doubt, Pierre Sage will return his apron as coach of Crystal Palace. This weekend’s defeat at Selhurst Park against Liverpool (3-1) finally convinced his management to oust him. He will have managed 16 matches in all competitions for 5 small victories. The Eagles are above all the red bottom of the Premier League and the time is now for urgency. To replace the Frenchman, a few names have already come out of the hat like Thomas Frank, Vitor Pereira, Frank Lampard or Sean Dyche.

In a recent development, two fresh coaches are cited by Sky Sports. First there is Ole Werner, free since his departure from RB Leipzig last June, and Jens Berthel Askou. The 44-year-old Dane joined Toulouse this summer with whom he indicates rather attractive things in the game, without really being rewarded from an accounting point of view (TFC is 13th). Before arriving in France, he led Motherwell to 4th place in the Scottish Premier League last season.
