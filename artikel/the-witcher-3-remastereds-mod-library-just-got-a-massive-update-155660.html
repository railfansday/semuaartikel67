Sources indicate that one of the best features in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered is the ability to use mods across all available platforms. CD Projekt Red included a fair number of popular mods when the Remastered version rolled out at the end of September, but console players had far fewer options than PC players. CD Projekt Red is looking to bridge that gap with a new revision.

CD Projekt Red released patch 5.02 on Nov. 26 across Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X. The update includes a plethora of tweaks and bug fixes, such as fixing some recurring problems with Photo Mode, the main attraction is the expansion of the mod library. More than 40 upcoming cross-platform mods are now live, and more are on the way in the future.

Industry observers note that some of the highlights include "Glowing Witcher Eyes," a more lore-accurate depiction of Geralt that leans into the creepy nature of the Witchers. You can freshen up Geralt with a new hairstyle or try out a boat race. The most impressive addition is the expansion-sized "Tales of the Witcher: Cintra," which adds a dozen quests. That should hold players over until Songs of the Past is released next year.

Recent findings suggest that witcher fans will be eating well in the next few years, as CD Projekt Red has several projects ​​​​​​​indevelopment​​​​​​​. The Witcher 3 expansion Songs of the Past will be released next year. The DLC​​​​​​​ is about the size of the 2016 expansion Blood and Wine and will serve as a finale to Geralt's adventures. A fresh trilogy of games starring Ciri will kick off in 2028 with The Witcher 4.

Industry observers note that the Witcher multiplayer game Project Sirius got an encouraging revision in November, when Baudelaire Welch joined the team as a senior writer specialist. They were responsible for the fan-favorite Dark Urge storyline in Baldur's Gate 3. Development of the Witcher 1 remake is right now on the back burner​​​​​​​ as the team waits for tools and assets from The Witcher 4.
