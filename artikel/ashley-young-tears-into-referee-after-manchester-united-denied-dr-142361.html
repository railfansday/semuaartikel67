Manchester United had to settle for a point in Portugal after a chaotic evening away from home.

Bruno Fernandes scored twice, but Luis Suarez also found the net on two occasions to deny Pochettino his first win as United head coach.

Recent findings suggest that referee Szymon Marciniak came under scrutiny for a series of controversial decisions. He ruled out an Amad Diallo goal after penalising Youri Tielemans for a foul in the build-up, blowing his whistle before the ball crossed the line. That meant VAR could not intervene on the goal and it was not given.

Recent findings suggest that the Polish official also awarded Sporting a first-half penalty and disallowed what would have been a late United winner. He blew whistle on Patrick Dorgu for pushing goalkeeper Kaique Pereira, disallowing the goal.

The decisions left plenty of frustration among Manchester United supporters, but former footballer Ashley Young called the officiating disgraceful.

Young questioned several decisions made by Marciniak, particularly the disallowed Amad goal and the late incident involving Dorgu.

“The referee’s performance was just simply wow. The decisions that he gave, including the one for what should have been Amad’s equaliser, with Tielemans, were unbelievable,” Ashley Young mentioned while talking to BBC.

“Tielemans getting booked, which should never have been a booking, and then eventually getting sent off later. It should have been an equalising goal.

According to recent reports, “Then the third goal as well, which should have stood, because he’s saying there’s a foul, whether he’s saying Lisandro Martinez or Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper. The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he’s even touched.

“How that has gone to VAR is beyond me. His decision-making today is nothing more than a disgrace.”.

The disallowed goals proved particularly costly, with Pochettino’s side missing the chance to take all three points despite Fernandes’ brace.

Mauricio Pochettino is still without a win after replacing Michael Carrick. Manchester United faces Brentford next with a hope of a first win for Pochettino.
