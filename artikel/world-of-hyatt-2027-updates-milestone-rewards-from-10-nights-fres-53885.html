According to recent reports, world of Hyatt members can expect more flexibility and choice from January 1, 2027. Highlights of the update include earning Milestone Rewards sooner, a bunch of new Milestone Rewards, and more flexibility when redeeming rewards and paying for stays. There’s actually no huge catch, and the changes I learned about last night on an intimate call with the Hyatt team are genuinely member-focused.

The update follows the latest Hyatt-Delta relationship, which also rolled out this week.

According to recent reports, starting January 1, 2027, World of Hyatt members will start enjoying Milestone Rewards after 10 nights stayed or 20,000 base points. That’s great news for relatively infrequent Hyatt guests (like myself), who will no longer have to stay 20 nights or earn 35,000 base points to earn their first Milestone Reward.

Milestone Rewards will be unlocked at every ten nights, up to 150 nights.

The downside of free parking becoming a Milestone Reward means that World of Hyatt members who earn Globalist from 2027 onwards will no longer enjoy it as a permanent status perk on award stays. This will be a notable downgrade to Globalists who value their parking perk.

Hyatt has published a handy new infographic with all the details.

There are also two more key Milestone Reward changes to note, both of which aren’t such positive news:.

In a recent development, for the first time, World of Hyatt members will be able to stack multiple awards on a single stay. For example, you can use a Suite Upgrade Voucher, a Guest of Honor Award, and claim free parking, all on the same stay.

It has been revealed that all awards will also expire after 365 of being issued. That’s great for awards that used to only last 180 days.

I think this could be my favorite patch. Hyatt is enhancing its Pay My Way option to allow guests to choose how to pay for each night of a stay. As someone who struggles to rack up Hyatt points (Hyatt isn’t a transfer partner of any cards in the U.K.), this should certainly streamline booking stays for me.

The booking page will display each award applied to each individual night and provide guests with a drop-down menu to select how they’d like to pay for each night.

According to recent reports, at a time when we’re plagued with loyalty program devaluations, these changes coming to World of Hyatt appear to be a genuinely positive slew of updates. Even when you consider some of the less favorable updates, like Globalists losing free parking and some award expiration dates being limited to 365 days, the changes will be positive overall for the vast majority of World of Hyatt members.
