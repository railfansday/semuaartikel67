Sources indicate that call me Buddy the Elf, but IMO, there’s no such thing as getting too in the holiday spirit. I’ve been known to display multiple Christmas trees, I love putting together holiday cards, and cooking Christmas dinner for friends and family is one of my great joys in life. And of course, all of this is happening while I’m blasting Mariah Carey and all my other favorite holiday songs.

It has been revealed that but don’t think I’ve forgotten about the beauty side of things! As a faithful beauty editor, I also get excited to change up my fragrance this time of year and embrace some holiday scents. Do I want to smell just like a Christmas tree? No, not really. But I do want to spritz something that’ll help get me in the holiday spirit. Think about all the best scents of the season—gingerbread, pine, cinnamon spice, vanilla... These make for perfect holiday perfumes. So, in the name of very notable holiday beauty research, I’ve rounded up the best festive fragrances worth adding to your rotation this season. From cozy gourmand perfumes to fresh woodsy notes and everything in between, one spritz of any of these will instantly get you in the holiday spirit.

Industry observers note that nest’s famous holiday candle is a staple in my home, and now, I can wear the scent on my skin too. It bottles up that festive feeling with fir balsam, winter cranberry, and grapefruit for a perfume that’s equal parts fresh, fruity, and woodsy. Think wandering through a Christmas tree farm with a little tart cranberry cocktail. It’s festive without smelling like you’re hiding pine branches up your sleeves.

Holiday parties call for a little spiked eggnog, and Dior Spice Blend brings that same boozy, spicy energy to your fragrance wardrobe. Rich rum notes, fiery black pepper, and zesty ginger blend together for a warm, intoxicating, and spirited scent. I love that it’s bold, sophisticated, and much less sugary than a typical holiday gourmand.

You don’t actually have to know how to ski to appreciate a good après ski moment (bring on the hot chocolate and jacuzzi, please). Ellis Brooklyn’s wintry scent captures that exact winter getaway feeling, thanks to seasonal notes of juniper berries, cardamom, soft suede, and musk. It’s woodsy, a little romantic, and pretty much begging to be worn at a chic mountain lodge.

Hot chocolate is a holiday must-have for me, but let’s be real: The best part is the mountain of whipped cream on top. Philosophy Fresh Cream bottles up that sweet, fluffy deliciousness with notes of whipped cream, tonka bean, heliotrope, and sugar. It’s soft, milky, and comforting without any heaviness or spice. And at under $60, it’s a pretty sweet addition to your holiday fragrance wardrobe.

In a recent development, dreaming of a white Christmas? Future Society Snow Beginnings can make that happen. It smells like the magic of a fresh snowfall, but with a surprisingly fruity, floral twist. Cool Japanese mint and sparkling pink grapefruit deliver that feeling of crisp winter air, while lychee, crystallized rose, and sake add a sweet warmth. It’s a winter wonderland in a bottle, no freezing temperatures or shoveling required.

Who says you have to spend a fortune to smell festive? Dove’s Holiday Treats Frosted Plum Berry Body Mist delivers a juicy, fruity take on holiday fragrance for just about the same price as your seasonal latte. The sweet, bright, and surprising plum feels playful and is a refreshing departure from the usual dessert-heavy holiday scents. Plus, it’s easy to layer with your favorite body lotion, scrub, or perfume for a little extra festive flair.
