It has been revealed that terrible start to the match for Crystal Palace on the Tottenham pitch! While the Eagles are away to their neighbor London in the Premier League, Jean-Philippe Mateta completely ruined his team in the 13th minute of play. The French striker was sent off after a dangerous tackle on the Achilles heel of Dutch defender Micky van de Ven. An intervention as excessive as it was avoidable, since the Tottenham player had arrived first on the ball and no immediate danger threatened Pierre Sage’s men. Initially, referee Michael Oliver decided to sanction Mateta with a simple yellow card. But after being invited to review the images by the VAR, the English official finally reversed his decision to send him a direct red card.

According to recent reports, an exclusion which seriously complicates the task of Crystal Palace, already in great difficulty since the start of the season. Before this meeting, the Eagles occupied a worrying 17th place in the Premier League, with only two victories on the clock. Now forced to play ten against eleven for more than 70 minutes, the Londoners will have to redouble their efforts to hope to obtain a result against Spurs. A frustrating scenario for Pierre Sage, whose position on the bench is already weakened by the poor results of his team. While the French technician had recently insisted on the need to make the right decisions to rectify the situation, this mistake from his center forward disrupted his intends from the first quarter of an hour.
