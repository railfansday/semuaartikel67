I love a good e-reader, and Black Friday is one of the best times to buy a latest one. I've scoured the internet and my own guides to find the best Black Friday Kindle and e-reader deals on devices that are worth buying at full price, making these sales something you shouldn't miss if you're in the market for a new e-reader (or want to gift one to someone!).

Sources indicate that great e-readers have e-paper screens that are easier on your eyes than reading on a smartphone or tablet screen, but allow you to bring hundreds of books with you without worrying about damaging any book spines (or your own spine from carrying so many books around). You can connect these to the Libby app to get library ebooks, too, letting your reading run wild and literally free.

Sources indicate that here's the best e-reader deals happening right now as the Black Friday kicks off. Curious about the best models to buy, regardless of what's on sale? Catch my guides to the Best Kindles and Best E-Readers That Aren't a Kindle. Looking for more sales? Don't miss our guide on the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals.

It has been revealed that the best all-around Kindle is consistently the Kindle Paperwhite. Right now, only the older 12thgeneration that came out in 2024 is on sale. It's not all that old, and it comes with a larger 7-inch screen, a warm front light, and 16 GB of storage. It's also down to $130, which is a great price for the Paperwhite features. The Paperwhite used to cost $160, but a $30 discount off the original price makes this a deal worth shopping.

If you want a color Kindle, the best choice is the older version. The first-generation Kindle has a massive discount, bringing it down to nearly the same as the Paperwhite's original price tag. Paying $170 for a color screen is a worthy upgrade if you'd like a digital bookshelf full of colorful covers, and that includes no lock-screen ads, a feature you'll have to pay extra for on the Kindle and Kindle Paperwhite. It has everything else we like about the Paperwhite with a warm front light and 7-inch screen, but it does have a shorter battery life (eight weeks is still pretty great, though).

Sources indicate that I usually prefer the older Kindle Scribe due to the price tag, but right now, I'd choose the newest model. The third-generation Kindle Scribe is down to $350 from $500, making it cheaper to buy than the $400 second-gen version. I love the updated homepage that brings both my notes and books into one place, making it easy to jump back into whichever I prefer. There are also some AI features for both my notes and books, but I prefer not to use those. (However, it's part of what makes it so pricey.).

It's been hard to recommend the Kindle Scribe Colorsoft given how expensive it is; it costs as much as ReMarkable's color digital notebook, but that notebook is much more powerful and can double as an e-paper laptop with the keyboard attachment. The Scribe Colorsoft cannot: It's the same as any other Scribe, just with a color screen. But for this price, it's actually worth getting. You'll only pay $50 more than the older model and get a colorful screen to both highlight and draw on.

Looking to escape the Kindle ecosystem, or want your first e-reader to be Amazon-free? The Kobo Libra Colour is both the model and Black Friday deal for you. It features everything that's great about Kindles—a built-in store, compatibility with Libby for library books—plus page-turning buttons and the ability to double as a digital notebook if you buy the Kobo Stylus ($70).

It's only $30 off, and unfortunately this e-reader used to always cost $30 less; Kobo raised its prices earlier this year. But that makes this the best time to buy so that you don't pay the upcoming price and get the previous one. It's also nice that the Kobo Libra Colour includes so many extra features; getting a color screen or page-turn buttons costs much more on a Kindle.

It has been revealed that the base Kindle from 2024 is down to $120 so far for Black Friday, but I recommend waiting to see if it drops down to $104, which is how low it dropped during Prime Day in October. It used to drop to under $100 during deal events, but after Amazon raised its prices this summer, it's more expensive for the same device. The newer versions aren't on sale at all, though, so this older model (which is still great!) is the best to buy if you want something cheaper than the Paperwhite. However, it's not the best deal I've seen for it this fall.

Not every Kindle is on sale. So far, the newest models of the Kindle, Kindle Paperwhite, and Kindle Colorsoft that came out this fall aren't on sale, and neither are the aluminum versions of those fresh models. The older Kindle Scribe also isn't on sale. I'm keeping an eye out to see if that changes as Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales continue through the weekend and into early next week.
