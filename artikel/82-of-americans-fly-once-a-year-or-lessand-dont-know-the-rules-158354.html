Who’s Actually In The Seat Next To You? In 2015, then-American Airlines president Scott Kirby said 87% of American’s customers flew them just once in the previous year and generated about half their revenue. Kirby was explaining why American needed basic economy. For every 100 American adults who flew in 2017, 24 took just one trip, and all 100 averaged 5.3 trips apiece. In the 1970s and 1980s, roughly half the air trips Americans took were for business. Personal trips already accounted for about 70% before the pandemic and 74% in 2025.
