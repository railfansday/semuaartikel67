Gordon Ramsay is officially a grandpa after his daughter Holly Ramsay-Peaty gave birth to her first baby.

“Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, Welcome to the World 🩷 25/11/2026 🤍⭐️,” Holly, 26, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 26. “You’ll forever be our beautiful baby girl and George’s baby sister. Thank you to all the Doctors, Nurses and Midwives who made sure we were safe, healthy and happy — we will forever be grateful 🤍.”.

Gordon, 60, later commented, “Welcome Miss Daphne, congratulations to Adam & Holly wow wow wow 🤩 ❤️❤️❤️ @hollyramsaypeaty @adamramsaypeaty.”.

Holly confirmed her pregnancy in June.

It has been revealed that “Baby Ramsay-Peaty coming December 2026 🐣,” she wrote via Instagram in a joint post with her husband, Adam Ramsay-Peaty. “We can’t wait to meet our baby girl.”.

Gordon subsequently shared his excitement in the comments section.

Industry observers note that “Congratulations to you both. Sending lots of love, Dad ❤️,” he replied at the time. “I’m going to be a very over excited grandad, especially this Christmas.”.

Industry observers note that holly is one of Gordon’s six children with his wife, Tana Ramsay. (In addition to Holly, Gordon and Tana, 52, share daughters Megan and Matilda as well as sons, Jack, Oscar and Jesse.).

The influencer married Olympic swimmer Adam in December 2025 after four years together, where Gordon walked her down the aisle at England’s Bath Abbey.

“I’m truly so lucky being able to walk this beautiful bride down the aisle and gaining an incredible son-in-law @adam_peaty!” Gordon wrote via Instagram after the couple’s nuptials. “I love you so much @hollyramsayy and couldn’t be a prouder dad.”.

In addition to his ceremony duties, Gordon delivered an emotional speech during the reception.

“It’s tough, honestly. Being the dad with three daughters, you dream of that moment, and that moment arrives, and you’re just like a hot mess,” the celebrity chef joked on the U.K.’s This Morning in January. “But the speech was perfect, it went well. … You’ve got to be warm, emotional. It’s a tough gig.”.

Holly and Adam have been together since 2021 before Adam popped the question to Holly in 2024.

Sources indicate that “I am marrying my best friend. I truly cannot put into words how I am feeling right now,” Holly captioned Instagram photos. “I still [remember] how big my smile was the morning I got home from my first date with you. “Thank you for letting the little girl inside of me feel loved, seen and happier than ever.”.

According to recent reports, she added at the time, “I love you, and I cannot wait to be your wife. I promise to always be there with you and George, I’m so [grateful] to be in his life and I cannot wait for more. Everything is better with you. Everything has been better since you. Here’s to forever.”.

According to recent reports, adam and Holly’s new arrival is the pro athlete’s second child. He previously welcomed son George with a past partner in 2020.

“I knew everything was going to change from there, but it’s been amazing,” Adam told the BBC in June 2020 months after becoming a first-time dad. “Everyone’s thinking it was an accident but that’s not the case. It’s one of the most beautiful things you can do together and we’ve already sorted the nursery.”.
