Breaking: Houthi attack on Saudi Arabian sovereign wealth fund PIF's tower injures 11

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis hit Saudi Arabian sovereign wealth fund Public Investment Fund Tower on Tuesday, injuring 11.

According to recent reports, the kingdom's civil defense directorate did not name the tower, but posted an image showing damage to the PIF tower. Eyewitness reports by Al Jazeera also indicated that the building that was hit was the PIF Tower in Riyadh.

The injured included two Saudi citizens, two Indians, three Nepalis, and four Egyptians.

The attack comes as tensions have flared up again between Saudi Arabia, which is leading an international coalition to restore the internationally recognized government of Yemen after it was forced to flee in 2015, and the Houthis.

Turki al-Maliki, a spokesperson for the coalition, said one of the towers of the financial center was targeted in a "cowardly terrorist attack," referring to the financial center in the Saudi capital of Riyadh.

He added that authorities were conducting an on-site investigation at the scene of the attack.

This has been the second attack by the Iran-backed group in six days on the tower, although the previous attack did not cause any casualties.

On Wednesday, Houthi brigadier general Yahya Saree warned on X that the port of Jeddah and all Saudi ports along the Red Sea coast will become "targets for our forces," if Riyadh attacks the Yemeni Red Sea port of Hodeidah.

Recent findings suggest that the coalition had asked civilians to evacuate the port "for their safety," on Nov. 20.

Attacks by the rebel Houthis have been escalating on Saudi Arabia, with the group targeting one of Saudi Arabia's oil refineries on Sunday, according to Reuters. The Houthis also rolled out drone and ballistic missiles against the country's airports, most recently targeting the King Khalid International Airport on Oct. 25, according to reports.

In a recent development, the fresh attacks come amid wider strikes by Tehran and Iran-linked forces in the Gulf, with Bahrain also reporting that it was attacked by Iran over the weekend. Bahrain is home to the U.S. Navy's Fifth Fleet.

"As a result of the heinous Iranian aggression, a civilian industrial facility was targeted, causing a fire at the site," Bahrain's Ministry of Interior remarked on X.

The ministry later said two people, a Bahraini citizen and a Pakistani resident, were injured in the attack.

— CNBC's Azhar Sukri contributed to this report.
