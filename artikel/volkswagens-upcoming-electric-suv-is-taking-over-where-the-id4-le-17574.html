According to recent reports, volkswagen is turning one of its best-selling nameplates into an electric SUV. The upcoming ID. Tiguan is now rolling off the assembly line and will replace the ID.4 in 2027.

In a recent development, the ID.4 and ID.5 are on the way out, and the latest ID. Tiguan will replace them in Volkswagen’s EV lineup next year.

Industry observers note that following the ID. Polo and ID. Cross, which are now on sale in Europe, the ID. Tiguan is the next model to go on sale as a “True Volkswagen,” as the company calls it.

The newly introduced electric SUV adopts Volkswagen’s best-selling nameplate, the Tiguan, as part of its shift away from the numerical system used on the outgoing ID.4 and ID.5.

Recent findings suggest that volkswagen unveiled on Thursday that series production of the electric ID. Tiguan has now begun at its Embden Plant in Germany.

“The Tiguan is our best-selling model worldwide, and the ID.4 and ID.5 are among our most successful electric cars,” VW Brand CEO Thomas Shafer noted last month after unveiling the latest SUV. He added that “The ID. Tiguan brings these two success stories together.”.

In a recent development, the ID. Tiguan will be a step change from its predecessors, the ID.4 and ID.5, featuring Volkswagen’s “Pure Positive” design language, which includes an overhauled interior and exterior design and updated tech.

Industry observers note that volkswagen remarked the ID. Tiguan’s “hallmark feature” is the completely redesigned exterior. The new design remains closer to the gas-powered Tiguan, with a boxy, traditional SUV look.

Sources indicate that unlike the ID.4’s aerodynamic design, the newly introduced ID. Tiguan features a wide, chunky front end and traditional pull-style door handles instead of the flush ones.

It has been revealed that in Volkswagen’s words, the new ID. Tiguan electric SUV “looks like a vehicle from the premium segment.”.

Inside, the updates are just as evident. The overhauled interior features Volkswagen’s upcoming infotainment setup, including a 10″ Digital Cockpit and a 15″ center display. There’s also an optional 12″ passenger screen available.

According to recent reports, another highlight is the return of physical buttons. After listening to customer feedback from current drivers, Volkswagen added physical buttons under the center display and on the steering wheel for climate control and other vehicle functions.

To spark a bit of nostalgia, VW added a fun upcoming Retro Mode feature that transforms the Digital Cockpit and center display to mimic the style of iconic models like the first-generation Golf.

Drivers can also choose from six classic VW models to display in a virtual “garage” on the infotainment screen: Golf I, Golf II Country, Golf IV, Tiguan I, Race Touareg, and Beetle.

Sources indicate that the newly introduced Volkswagen ID. Tiguan will be available with two battery packs and three performance levels:.

In a recent development, depending on the configuration, the ID. Tiguan offers a “new benchmark” for Volkswagen’s electric SUVs in Europe with a WLTP range of up to 377 miles (607 km).

It has been revealed that volkswagen opened orders for the ID. Tiguan in Germany and a few other European markets in October, starting at €42,695 ($47,800). It’s available in three trims: Trend, Life, and Style.

Sources indicate that after it launches in Europe through early 2027, Volkswagen said the ID. Tiguan will go on sale in North America to replace the ID.4.

Recent findings suggest that apparently, the new ID Tiguan has a slightly better Cx (Drag Coefficient) at 0.26 versus 0.28 on first gen ID4 and 0.27 on 2nd gen. According to VW, it’s achieved using front air curtains, a minimal air intake, an enclosed underbody, and a longer roof spoiler.

Volkswagen said prices and other details will come in “due course.” The current VW ID.4 uses an 82 kWh battery that provides an EPA-estimated range of up to 291 miles in the RWD version. The AWD model offers around 263 miles.

Since the European model offers close to 25 miles (40 km) more WLTP range than the outgoing ID.4, the ID. Tiguan is expected to offer over 300 miles on the EPA scale.

Official US prices have not yet been declared. The outgoing ID.4 has a starting MSRP of about $45,000, so the ID. Tiguan will likely be priced similarly.
