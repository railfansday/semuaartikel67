Published: 12:00 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 12:04 EST, 25 November 2026.

Newcastle chiefs are inching closer to confirming a new stadium as their direction of travel.

We reported in last week’s Newcastle Confidential how advanced talks with Upcoming York investment bankers Goldman Sachs over financing the £1.3billion project had indicated significant progress on the decision to leave St James’ Park, with a 65,000-capacity stadium on nearby Leazes Park the intention.

Fellow Wall Street firm JP Morgan have now been appointed as advisers in selling a stake in the club, and this would be to inject fresh equity to help fund infrastructural developments, including a stadium.

Sources have stressed this is not a case of Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund looking to sell the club, but rather by creating upcoming shares – potentially worth up to 25 per cent of the club – it raises capital for such a significant outlay.

It has been revealed that with Newcastle said to be valued at £1.5billion, this could equate to a £375million cash injection. PIF would retain their majority ownership, even if diluted to around 63 per cent from its current 85 per cent holding.

As one source mentioned: ‘This is not about taking cash off the table, it is about funding growth.'.

Yasir Al-Rummayan and Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund are not looking to sell Newcastle despite their plans to sell a stake in the club.

According to recent reports, we understand Goldman Sachs are still involved, and their role will be to provide a loan or other investment to help finance the stadium. As we reported last week, the cost of a fresh home would not be exclusively funded by PIF and co-owner Jamie Reuben, with loans, share problems and investments all part of the package.

Industry observers note that the expectation is that a new stadium, rather than a St James’ renovation, will soon be recommended to the club’s owners by the executive team. It will then be up to PIF to sign off on the looks and for a public announcement to be prepared.

REVEALED: What Dedic was doing in bow and arrow fiasco!

Amar Dedic was firing arrows at a tree during his infamous countryside escapade, Confidential can now reveal.

The details of exactly what the Newcastle defender was doing with a bow and arrows in a Northumberland beauty spot can be shared for the first time after police closed the case, including the deployment of a helicopter to track him down.

The chopper was part of a major search operation in which Dedic and his friend were stopped by more than half a dozen patrol cars.

According to recent reports, investigators are now satisfied the 24-year-old posed no threat to the public after a 999 caller reported seeing two men walking by a reservoir with what they described as a ‘crossbow’. It was, in fact, a bow and arrows.

I can reveal that Dedic was not hunting animals (or the public) during his Harlow Hill adventure, but was instead using a tree for target practice. He and his pal were firing beyond a fence with the aim of landing their arrows in the trunk.

Recent findings suggest that however, while the recreational archers were walking back to Dedic’s car, the emergency call was made by a concerned member of the public. Police cars, including armed response, arrived on the scene but the Bosnian had left.

Amar Dedic was firing arrows at a tree during his infamous countryside escapade, which saw police despatch a helicopter to track him down.

Dedic has taken the misunderstanding in good spirits, and we can expect him to copy Harvey Barnes' archer celebration if he scores in the coming weeks.

This prompted controllers at Northumbria Police HQ to scramble a helicopter to join the hunt and, seven miles away in the exclusive Darras Hall estate, Dedic was pulled over amid a blaze of blue lights.

It has been revealed that adding to the incredulity of the situation was the sudden appearance of goalkeeper Mark Gillespie and former defender Paul Dummett, who helped Dedic and police during questioning. They were in the area by chance.

Dedic has taken the misunderstanding in good spirits and, if he does score his first goal for Newcastle in the coming weeks, we should expect a Harvey Barnes-style archer celebration.

Joelinton is set to return to action next week – in time for the Tyne-Wear derby.

Confidential reported last week that the prognosis was not as bad as first feared after the Brazilian suffered a muscle injury during the defeat by Everton on November 2. However, I’m now told Joelinton is expected to return to full training next week, with Manchester United the visitors on Wednesday followed by the clash with Sunderland four days later.

The 30-year-old started three league games after a previous injury absence before he broke down again, raising serious questions over his ability to withstand a Premier League season. Nonetheless, Joelinton’s involvement for the derby would be a major boost to Matthias Jaissle in his first taste of the fixture.

First images of Toon's luxury private members' club.

Newcastle have taken inspiration from NFL giants Dallas Cowboys as they explore aims for a luxury private members' club at their £190million training headquarters – and I can reveal stunning images of how the exclusive facility would look.

Confidential understands club chiefs have been studying some of the world's leading sporting complexes, including The Star, the Cowboys' spectacular training base in Texas, as they look to transform their own 260-acre site into a world-class destination.

And their ambitions could extend well beyond football. Newcastle have now approached existing hospitality clients and members of their business club to gauge interest in a private members' retreat within the proposed training complex at Woolsington, which is set to open in the summer of 2029.

We can reveal the first concept images of a private members' club at Newcastle's proposed £190m training base.

The Dallas Cowboys' HQ in Frisco, Texas is one source of inspiration for the envisaged facility at Woolsington (pictured).

The market research includes questions about whether prospective members would be interested in business meeting spaces, a licensed bar and leisure club facilities. The idea is to create an exclusive social and business hub alongside the upcoming training ground, which will house the men's first team and academy, with further prepares to integrate the women's team.

The Dallas Cowboys' headquarters in Frisco, Texas, has provided one source of inspiration. Known as The Star, the sprawling development combines elite training facilities with hospitality, entertainment, restaurants and commercial spaces. Club officials have visited NFL facilities as part of extensive research into how the world's leading sports organisations operate away from matchdays.

The striking concept images that we are able to bring you offer a glimpse of the scale of Newcastle's ambition as they seek to build a complex that will rank as one of the best in world football.

Wilson's familiar fresh neighbour.

Sporting director Ross Wilson has a new neighbour – Matthias Jaissle!

The pair are now living in the same complex in the Jesmond area of Newcastle, after Jaissle spent an initial period staying in the Dakota Hotel on the city’s Quayside.

This should prove handy ahead of the January window, and the club’s transfer war room might well become Wilson’s living room!

The car park also offers a clue as to what time Jaissle is starting work every day, and given Wilson rarely sees the manager’s vehicle there in the morning, it is assumed he is at the club’s Benton base before the milkman!

Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle is out of the Dakota Hotel, having moved into the same complex as the club's sporting director Ross Wilson.

Finally, Newcastle have beaten Sunderland on Tyneside, 16 years after their last league victory!

Two teams of legends played a game of walking football at Newcastle’s Foundation building close to St James’ Park on Wednesday.

According to recent reports, and we can reveal that Newcastle won 14-3! The Toon team included Shay Given, Olivier Bernard, Steve Howey, Shola Ameobi, Dummett, John Anderson and Kenny Wharton. The top scorers, surprisingly, were said to be former left backs Bernard and Dummett!

The get-together was aimed at raising awareness over walking football opportunities, with the Newcastle Foundation and Sunderland’s Foundation of Light both supporting the sport through various initiatives.
