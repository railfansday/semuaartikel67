In case you need another reminder of how old you are, the Wii has just turned 20 years old in North America (those in Europe have until 8th December to hit the milestone).

To celebrate, Circana's Mat Piscatella has posted a breakdown of the top 20 best-selling Wii games of all time. For anyone clued in, the list won't be particularly surprising, but it's definitely a stark reminder of how weird the Wii generation was. It was chock full of simple, casual games that you just don't see these days – at least not to the same degree, Just Dance is still very much a thing, of course.

Industry observers note that it was a simpler time, and just thinking about it now is making us miss it dearly. But, one thing... Twilight Princess is below Link's Crossbow Training?! Holy moly.

Recent findings suggest that 1. Wii Play 2. Mario Kart Wii 3. Latest Super Mario Bros. Wii 4. Wii Fit 5. Wii Fit Plus 6. Wii Sports Resort 7. Super Smash Bros. Brawl 8. Super Mario Galaxy 9. Just Dance 2 10. Just Dance 3 11. Link’s Crossbow Training 12. Mario Party 8 13. The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess 14. Donkey Kong Country Returns 15. LEGO Star Wars Complete Saga 16. Just Dance 4 17. Super Mario Galaxy 2 18. Just Dance 19. Guitar Hero 3 20. Carnival Games.
