📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, chainlink [LINK] fell 3.24% over the past 24 hours, declining more than Bitcoin and the broader crypto market. The slump appeared tied to market wide selling amid Bitcoin ETF Outflows and rate concerns, rather than a coin-specific setback.

Over the same period, Bitcoin fell 2.2% to $59,218, while the broader crypto market declined 1.87%.

Even news of Fidelity listing LINK on the 24th of November failed to halt the decline. Combined with the token’s weak technical structure, that muted response suggested selling pressure remained firm.

According to recent reports, at the time of writing, LINK was trading at $8.75, between the 0% and 23.6% Fibonacci levels on its daily timeframe. The token’s inability to gain the momentum it needed to reclaim the 23.6% level during its recent recovery attempts suggests that buyers have struggled to regain control.

Moreover, with RSI at 34.07 and OBV still falling, the chart suggests that momentum remains weak with little evidence that buyers are stepping in with conviction.

Recent findings suggest that on the H4 chart, LINK has begun retesting the 50% Fibonacci support level, which it reclaimed on November 22. A failure to defend this level would weaken the short-term recovery and increase the risk of a move toward the 61.8% level.

The MACD also neutralised but remained below its signal line, suggesting the token’s recent bullish momentum has officially started to fade.

Meanwhile, declining OBV points to weakening buying pressure, increasing the risk of a deeper pullback.

Even if LINK holds the 50% level and recovers, the broader daily structure remains bearish.
