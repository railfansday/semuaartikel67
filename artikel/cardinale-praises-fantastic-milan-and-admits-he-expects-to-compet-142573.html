Gerry Cardinale gave his thoughts after watching his AC Milan side run riot against Frosinone at San Siro this evening.

It has been yet another few days where the Milan owner has made his presence felt. After landing in Italy, he was at Milanello and Casa Milan on Saturday for a series of important meetings with the management and the squad, and then he watched first hand as the Rossoneri won tonight.

An own-goal from Gabriele Calvani got the scoring going, before Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic all scored in the 5-1 rout of Frosinone. It could have been more in truth, but it was another display of the level this team can play at.

Cardinale was interviewed by Michael Cuomo of TeleLombardia after the final whistle having made his way down from the stands, and his comments were relayed by MilanNews.

Industry observers note that how happy is he? Is he satisfied?

Industry observers note that “Well, I’m never satisfied, but the results speak for themselves. The boys were fantastic tonight. I hope you’re happy. I hope the fans are happy. I think the team is coming together really well.

“It’s a game, let’s keep it under control, but I’m really proud of the boys. This is a team. It’s a team that wants to win until the end.”.

Can you dream of winning the Scudetto?

Sources indicate that “Look, it’s November. We still have a long way to go. But we want to see improvements in every match. This weekend was very interesting if you look at the kind of results we’ve had. You can see that something is changing in Serie A.

According to recent reports, “I was very happy to see us not just win, but win by that margin. And so, we have to build on that, but as Amorim says, every match is something to build on, and we have a long way to go. I personally still expect us to compete for the Scudetto, but it’s a long way off. One match at a time.

“Now the focus is on the Europa League. Yes, and as Ruben said, we have to do both. European competitions are important. Everything is a test. If you want to win a Scudetto, you have to do it all the way, and that’s what we’ll do.”.

Could signings arrive in January?

Sources indicate that “Again: January is just another stage. As you know, the January transfer window is complicated. You have to be disciplined. You can’t just jump into something randomly. The only thing that will happen under my ownership is that every time we play in the transfer market, we’ll play to win.

“That’s the theme. That’s the theme I said at the beginning of this year. So, if we make a move in any transfer window, January or June, it will be a move that will count.”.
