Juventus could have a low-cost boost for their attack in January by bringing Lois Openda back early from his Olympique Lyonnais loan or finding him a different club.

The Bianconeri are struggling to get goals, not helped by the injury crisis ruling out Kenan Yildiz, Francisco Conceicao, Nico Gonzalez and Arkadiusz Milik for weeks or even months at a time.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, there is an intriguing solution to bolster the options for coach Luciano Spalletti without spending any money.

The idea is to interrupt Openda’s loan experience at Olympique Lyonnais after just six months and bring him back to Turin during the January transfer window.

The 26-year-old was sent out on loan for €3.5m, and has made 10 competitive appearances for the French side, scoring two goals with one assist.

However, his last game was on October 9, when sustaining a fracture against RC Lens, and is expected back on the field next month.

In his absence, Lyon have parted organization with coach Paulo Fonseca and brought in Carlos Vicens, whose idea of a striker is someone with very different characteristics to Openda.

If the Belgian is not getting regular playing time in Lyon, then it is likely he will either try to find another club to join on loan for the rest of the season, or even return to base and try his hand at Juve again.

He was considered a very expensive mistake from RB Leipzig for €46m between the loan and obligation to buy, managing only two goals in 34 games for the Bianconeri.
