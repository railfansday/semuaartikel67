Cranberry Mojitos

🌐 Source: Delish • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (110 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, mojitos are light, refreshing, and always hit the spot in the summertime, but we love them so much that we feel they should be celebrated all year long. To bring them fully into the holiday season, we decided to give them a red-and-green upgrade courtesy of cranberry juice. The tart, sweet juice brings a seasonal spin that also lends a welcome fruitiness to the sparkling cocktail. Plus, the mint leaves and cranberries bring an almost holly-like look that really solidifies this drink as a new Christmas staple.
