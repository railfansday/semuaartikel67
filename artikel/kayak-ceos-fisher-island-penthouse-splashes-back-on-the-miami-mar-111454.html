An opulent five-bedroom penthouse atop Palazzo Della Luna on Miami's Fisher Island, listed for an astonishing $85 million, has just surfed to the top spot on the Realtor.com® weekly list of the most expensive homes.

It has been revealed that the sky-high estate was purchased by Kayak CEO Steve Hafner and his wife, Staci, for $40 million in 2021. Originally listed in March 2025 for $85 million, the opulent condo has returned to the market at the same price. The Hafners could more than double their money, if they get anything near their eye-watering asking price.

Spanning the entire 10th floor of the boutique waterfront building, the 10,194-square-foot residence has 15-foot ceilings, oversized windows, and floor-to-ceiling glass walls that open to bayfront terraces.

The open floor plan features a water-facing living room, a billiard room, a built-in bar, and a modern kitchen. A private fitness center could be transformed into a sixth bedroom.

A 7,300-square-foot rooftop has a zero-edge heated pool, a hot tub, a summer kitchen, two wet bars, and multiple lounges for entertaining.

More spectacular mansions to make the week's most expensive homes list include Kylie Jenner's Holmby Hills concrete fortress, a recently remodeled Aspen, CO, mountain retreat, and a waterfront estate in the Hamptons.

Beachfront retreat: This five-bedroom estate, built in 1969, features 3,495 square feet of modernized living space on 55 feet of Pacific Ocean frontage.

It has been revealed that styled by interior designer Michael S. Smith, the coastal sanctuary has wood paneling, wide-plank floors, and expansive windows and disappearing glass walls. The chef's kitchen has professional-grade appliances, a waterfall breakfast bar, and a dining area that leads to an outdoor patio. A sculptural spiral staircase leads upstairs to the primary suite, which has a vaulted ceiling, a sitting area, and a private terrace.

There is also a three-car garage and a detached one-bedroom guesthouse.

Chris Cortazzo of Compass is the listing agent.

Concrete fortress: Kylie Jenner is once again looking to part ways with her Holmby Hills mansion, which she purchased for $36.5 million in 2020.

The 15,227-square-foot estate first hit the market in 2025 for a $48 million, followed by several price cuts. Highlights of the seven-bedroom sanctuary, designed for "five-star resort-style living," include a primary wing with dual bathrooms, multiple fireplaces, a private den, and an outdoor lounge. Two guest suites have separate living areas, kitchenettes, and an expansive deck.

Outdoor entertaining areas include a geometric zero-edge pool, professional pickleball and basketball courts, a commercial-grade kitchen, and a private gym. The nearly 1-acre lot also has a guardhouse, a four-car garage, and five covered parking spaces.

In a recent development, joshua Altman of Douglas Elliman of California Inc. is the listing agent.

The Gallery House: Built in 2006, this mountain retreat was recently remodeled and "exquisitely furnished" to offer a modern 7,870-square-foot interior.

Industry observers note that designed for entertaining, the elegant kitchen has dual convection ovens, two dishwashers, a wet bar, and a coffee bar. Each of the six en suite bedrooms has a patio or walkout access and features amenities such as a fireplace, a steam shower, and a soaking tub. Other highlights include a wine cellar, an executive office, a mudroom with ski lockers, a bunk room, a private gym, a massage room, and a primary suite with oxygen supplementation.

Located adjacent to the Tiehack Chairlift and minutes from downtown Aspen, the 1.15-acre property also has multiple outdoor entertaining areas, a hot tub, a cold plunge, a pizza oven, a wooden playset, and a four-car garage with a lift system.

Lex Tarumianz of Aspen Snowmass Sotheby's International Realty is the listing agent.

The Madison Club: This flagship sanctuary developed by Gala Asher might be the "final spec estate he will ever build in the community.".

In a recent development, the 14,000-square-foot open floor plan has six bedrooms, 10 bathrooms, a secondary professional chef's kitchen, and a "world-class" cinema. A lavish 3,000-square-foot primary "penthouse" has "explosive" mountain views. Other highlights include an expansive motor court, a seven-car garage, and a "rare" zero-edge circular pool and spa.

The 1-acre property also has a pickleball court and a fitness center that open to the pool deck.

Ginger Glass of Compass is the listing agent.

Waterfront Hamptons: The sellers bought this 2.63-acre property for $12.5 million in 2012 and built an 8,300-square-foot mansion in 2016.

The eight-bedroom shingled residence has sweeping views of the Atlantic Ocean and Shinnecock Bay. The timeless interior features custom millwork, built-in seating, four fireplaces, oversized windows, and French doors that open to covered porches. The grand kitchen has an island with dual sinks, a breakfast bar, and glass doors that lead to a water-facing deck. Two bunk rooms are ideal for hosting guests.

The property also has a tennis court and swimming pool. A wooden bridge leading to the beach provides direct access to the water.

Danielle Grossi of The Agency Hamptons is the listing agent.

Cocoloba: This 5.07-acre compound is stated to be owned by Al-Fe Heat Treating founder Kurt Westman and his wife, Kathi, who purchased it for $3 million in 2011 and then built a tropical paradise in 2017.

The 9,843-square-foot estate, tucked away on a private peninsula, features a six-bedroom primary residence and a detached guest residence connected by a walking bridge. Designed for grand-scale entertaining, the light-filled open floor initiative includes a stunning two-story great room with floor-to-ceiling windows capturing the oceanfront views; a chef's kitchen with a butler's pantry, catering area, and wine room; and a sun-soaked primary suite with a sitting room, two vanities, dual water closets, a walk-in shower, and a soaking tub.

Garden pathways wind through the lushly landscaped lot, which also has a reflective saltwater pond, a heated swimming pool with a waterfall grotto, a private beach, and multiple waterfront entertainment spaces.

Offering direct access to the Atlantic Ocean, the property has approximately 450 feet of dockage extending into a protected basin, along with an air-conditioned boathouse, a tackle room, and a workshop.

Eric Rizzo of Ocean Sotheby's International Realty is the listing agent.

The Atlantic Anchor: This seven-bedroom coastal retreat is noted to be owned by real estate developer Daniel E. Edwards, who purchased the 1.02-acre property for $22 million in 2023.

Industry observers note that built in 2020 in the private enclave of Harbor Court, which comprises just two oceanfront estates, this 15,150-square-foot mansion has 150 feet of frontage along the Atlantic Ocean. The elegant interior features an elevator, a private office, a third-floor clubroom with a bar and home theater, a 500-bottle wine cellar, and a primary suite with dual spa-inspired bathrooms and a wraparound terrace with Atlantic-facing views. Covered patios overlook the resort-inspired free-form pool with waterfalls, a waterslide, and a spa.

A tennis court, a temperature-controlled garage with RV-height doors, a workshop, a professional-grade golf simulator, and a two-bedroom guesthouse with an elevator are also on the property.

The Matt and Nick Team of Serhant is the listing agent.

Carla House: This seven-bedroom Trousdale Estate, built in 2019 and designed by architect Noah Walker, was formerly owned by convicted real estate Ponzi schemer Robert Shapiro.

According to The Real Deal, a liquidation entity for Shapiro's Woodbridge Group of Companies sold the 20,541-square-foot mansion for $35 million in 2019 to the current owner.

It has been revealed that the open floor strategy features retractable glass walls, custom millwork, wood ceilings, concrete accents, and two fireplaces. The show kitchen has dual marble islands, a wine cellar, and a secondary catering kitchen. The primary suite has a leather wall, a private lounge, a soaking tub, and dual closets. Other amenities include a home theater with stadium seating, a fitness studio, a wellness center, a home office, service quarters, and a roof deck.

The 1.14-acre lot also has an 80-foot pool and a four-car garage.

Tomer Fridman of The Beverly Hills Estates Inc. is the listing agent.

Bighorn Golf Club: This Guy Dreier–designed curvilinear estate, perched atop a summit on 7.64 acres, offers views spanning the Coachella Valley to the Santa Rosa Mountains.

Recent findings suggest that built in 2007 for publishing executive Duane Hagadone, the seven-bedroom "architectural masterpiece" is "organically sculpted" into desert bedrock with titanium, copper, and glass. It was last sold for $42 million in 2022, then hit the market in March 2025 for $59 million. It has now returned with a substantial price increase.

Eye-popping amenities include interlocking sculptural pools, three spas, an open-air glass elevator, a 10-car garage, a private view-top pickleball court, an aquatic walkway flanked by exotic marine life, and a detached four-suite guest wing. The primary wing has book-matched Golden onyx, a spa bath, a plunge pool, and a cantilevered cliffside office. A commercial kitchen was designed for entertaining. The home is being offered fully furnished.

A $1,812 monthly association fee provides access to championship golf, a 13,000-square-foot wellness center, and fine dining.

Ginger Glass of Compass is the listing agent.

Palazzo Della Luna: This full-floor penthouse owned by Kayak CEO Steve Hafner last changed hands for $40 million in 2021.

Spanning the entire 10th floor, the five-bedroom condo was designed to feature breathtaking views from nearly every room. Built in 2019, the 10,194-square-foot open floor plan features 15-foot ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass walls that capture vistas stretching from the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay to Government Cut, Fisher Island, and the Miami skyline.

It also has a private 7,322-square-foot rooftop terrace, along with nearly 2,000 square feet of covered wraparound terraces to soak in the ocean and city scenery.

Jill Eber of Coldwell Banker Realty is the listing agent.
