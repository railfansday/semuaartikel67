As GTA 6 finally arrives, increasingly outspoken games industry legend Warren Spector - known for seminal immersive sims including Deus Ex - has offered both praise for the game's achievements and lambasted its "billion dollar" development model, writing, "I just hope GTA 6 is the last of its kind even if it’s the best we have to offer".

Spector revealed his retirement from games development in August following struggles at his OtherSide Entertainment studio, and has been vocal about the state of the games industry in the months since, notably saying anyone at EA responsible for the "travesty" of BioWare's closure should be "ashamed". And now Spector has taken on GTA 6.

Industry observers note that "First, congratulations to everyone who worked on GTA 6. It looks like it will be a high point of sorts for our medium and our business," he wrote in a post on LinkedIn. "However… its billion dollar development cost and an undoubtedly crazy marketing budget don't represent a business model anyone else can or probably should emulate.

In a recent development, "That's especially true," Spector continued, "when thousands of developers are out of work. A billion dollars pays for a lot of games and a lot of salaries. Personally I'd rather have hundreds of games and full employment than one game no matter how amazing it is.".

The "sure success" of GTA 6 proves a point he's made "for years", he remarked: "That no game that matters ships on time and/or on budget". Highlighting GTA 6's year-long delay and reported billion dollar budget, Spector suggested, "Give a game the time and money to prove its potential (key word, potential) and you have a chance of making something great. Underfund a game or force it out the door before it's ready [is] guaranteed failure no matter how much potential it has.

"So, again, congratulations," Spector concluded. "Ambition and innovation are necessary for the medium to reach its potential. I just hope GTA 6 is the last of its kind even if it's the best we have to offer.".

Despite Spector's concerns, GTA 6's arrival is, as expected, already causing shockwaves across the industry. It has broken pre-order records for Xbox and PlayStation stores, smashed the all-time livestream viewer record, and apparently briefly broke Reddit too. And is its success deserved? Eurogamer's Chris Tapsell thinks so, saying it "pushes back the horizon of video games once again" in his five star GTA 6 review.
