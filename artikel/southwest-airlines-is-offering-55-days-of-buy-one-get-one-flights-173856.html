Southwest Airlines is offering 55 days of buy-one-get-one flights. Here’s how to get them

🌐 Source: Fastcompany • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 5 min read (1089 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Southwest Airlines is taking a slightly different approach to Black Friday this year. Rather than simply discounting flights, the airline is giving travelers an opportunity to earn one of its most coveted perks: the Companion Pass. On Friday, Southwest announced a three-day promotion that allows Rapid Rewards members to earn a temporary Companion Pass by booking and completing just one round-trip flight or two one-way flights. The offer comes just weeks before Southwest is set to overhaul its Rapid Rewards loyalty program, significantly changing how travelers qualify for the benefit. How to earn Southwest’s promotional Companion Pass If you’re already planning to fly over the holidays, earning the pass could be relatively easy. To take advantage of the promotion, travelers will need to: Register for the promotion through Southwest. Book eligible flights between November 27 and November 29. Complete either a single round-trip flight or two one-way flights between November 27 and January 5, 2027. Travelers who score the pass will be able to bring a designated companion on eligible flights between January 8 and March 3, 2027 for free, with the exception of taxes and fees, which start at $5.60 each way. There’s also no limit to the number of eligible flights travelers can use the pass for during the promotional period, so you and your companion could, in theory, go on a 2-month adventure only paying for one of your flights. Southwest is changing how travelers earn a Companion Pass The promotion comes at an interesting time for Southwest. In October, the airline unveiled changes to its Rapid Rewards loyalty program that will take effect on January 1, 2027. Under the existing program, travelers can earn a Companion Pass by completing 100 qualifying one-way flights or accumulating 135,000 Companion Pass qualifying points in a calendar year. Starting in January, Southwest will eliminate Companion Pass qualifying points and instead use Tier Qualifying Points to determine eligibility for elite status and Companion Pass benefits. The airline is also introducing a upcoming milestone reward that gives members three Companion Pass trips, while travelers who reach its new Platinum elite status will receive unlimited companion trips. One of the biggest changes is how credit card rewards factor into the equation. Historically, Southwest credit card welcome bonuses have offered a particularly lucrative shortcut to earning a Companion Pass. Starting in 2027, those bonuses will no longer count toward qualification. The same goes for referral bonuses, anniversary points, and points earned through hotel and rental car partners. Everyday spending on Southwest credit cards will still help travelers qualify, with cardholders earning one Tier Qualifying Point per dollar spent. There’s still time to earn a Companion Pass under the old rules Southwest is giving prospective credit card customers one final opportunity to take advantage of the existing qualification structure. Travelers who apply for a Southwest Rapid Rewards credit card by December 14, are approved, and meet the spending requirements for its welcome bonus can still have those bonus points count toward earning a Companion Pass under the 2026 rules, even if they complete the spending requirement in early 2027. Those who meet the overall qualification requirements can enjoy unlimited companion flights through December 31, 2027. For travelers who don’t fly Southwest often enough to qualify for the traditional Companion Pass, however, this weekend’s Black Friday promotion offers a much easier way to experience the perk, even if only for a couple of months.
