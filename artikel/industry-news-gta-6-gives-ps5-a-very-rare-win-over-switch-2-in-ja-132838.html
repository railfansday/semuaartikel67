📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

At this point, it’s well known that Switch 2 is by far the market leader in Japan.

While the PS5 still commands some marketshare, it gets dominated by the Nintendo machine on a weekly basis.

GTA 6’s launch is significant, then, because for the first time in a while it’s pushed Sony’s console in front of the Switch 2 – albeit only by a few thousand units.

According to Famitsu’s latest report, the PS5 sold 41,472 units through the week ending 22nd November, while the Switch 2 managed 36,817 units.

This is an incredibly rare scenario, and likely won’t be repeated again (if ever). But it demonstrates that with the right software, Sony can put up a fight domestically against Nintendo.

The firm’s been taking the region a lot more seriously under Japanese CEO Hideaki Nishino, launching more affordable region locked units to try and stimulate interest.

This week saw the release of the new Midnight Black model, which certainly helped contribute to the sales.

But PS5’s success was primarily motivated by GTA 6, which sold a very respectable 200,759 units at retail for the week.

That’s slightly less than GTA 5’s 304,752 units opening in 2013, although remember digital buying habits have changed dramatically in the last 13 years, and GTA 6’s physical release was not particularly compelling as it doesn’t include a disc.

A good week for Sony in its home region, then – one that’s unlikely to be replicated, but should be taken as a sign it can compete with the right lineup and pricing model.

[source famitsu.com, via gematsu.com].
