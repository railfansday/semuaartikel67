Poseidon Expeditions has offered an early glimpse into the Ocean Spirit, its purpose-built new expedition ship.

Debuting in late 2028, the PC6 class Ocean Spirit is an expedition vessel developed specifically for the cruise line thanks to a deal inked between Poseidon and United Waterways and its ocean division Ocean Advice this summer.

Poseidon has now shared that the 120-meter-long ship will be able to accommodate 160 guests in suites across seven categories: Window, Balcony, Promenade, Deluxe, Captain’s, Panorama and Owner’s.

The suites range in size from 19 to 40 square meters and select categories will feature balconies.

Beyond suites, the ship will feature public spaces designed to complement the journey, including lounges, dining spaces, observation decks and wellness facilities.

Noting that this marks a newly introduced chapter for the tech giant, the cruise line said that the Ocean Spirit carries the ethos of Poseidon Expeditions forward as a new expression of the spirit of exploration that has guided it from the beginning.
