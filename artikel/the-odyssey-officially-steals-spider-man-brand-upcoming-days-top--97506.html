It has been revealed that 2026 has been an interesting year for movies with captivating stories and intriguing characters gracing the big and small screen and getting equal love and praise from the audiences. The year was full of visionary directors enchanting the audience with stunning visuals and heartfelt stories; Phil Lord and Christopher Miller brought Project Hail Mary starring Ryan Gosling, which was a brilliant exploration of deep space, isolation, and friendship; Steven Spielberg brought Disclosure Day, a meditation on what happens to faith, humanity, and science when we start to accept that extraterrestrial life exists. Andrew Stanton brought the fifth installment of Toy Story, a timely story that touched parents and kids alike.

Industry observers note that among many notable films, this year is Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey, a passion project for the director, which wowed millions of fans worldwide. The feature is a grounded, R-rated adaptation of Homer’s ancient Greek poem. After the fall of Troy, King Odysseus (Matt Damon) of Ithaca endures a brutal 10-year journey across the Mediterranean, confronting storms and monsters like Cyclops, the witch Circe, the Sirens, and the nymph Calypso. The 94% Rotten Tomatoes-rated movie broke out at the box office and has earned $1.8 billion globally against a roughly $250–$300 million budget, making it Nolan's highest-grossing film.

Industry observers note that another movie that did a billion-dollar business worldwide is Tom Holland’s Spider-Man: Brand Latest Day. Director Destin Daniel Cretton brought a grittier iteration of the character, as Peter grows lonely after the events of No Where Home. The 90% Rotten Tomatoes-rated feature gave way to The Odyssey during the theatrical run, which seems like a good decision given the time gap between the two movies benefited both. But a streaming clash is here, and there’s a clear winner.

Recent findings suggest that spider-Man: Brand New Day dropped on PVOD in October and has since claimed the top spot on streamers like Prime Video. The Odyssey came out on November 17 and instantly dethroned the other Holland-starrer. Presently, The Odyssey is the top movie on Prime Video’s global top 10 charts, followed by Spider-Man: Brand New Day in a list that also includes Brad Pitt’s Heart of the Beast, Anne Hathaway’s Verity, Curry Baker’s Obsession, The Hunger Games franchise, and more. The feature is also at the top spot on the Apple TV Store.

Check out The Odyssey and Brand New Day on Amazon Prime and stay tuned to Collider for more such updates.
