GTA 6 is out for everyone everywhere (except on PC), so Rockstar's gone and posted a mega big resolution version of the full map of Vice City and the greater Leonida state area.

It's handy to have in your pocket even if you are already playing the game because, you'll have noticed, GTA 6's map has a fog of war you need to clear as you play. Even after you do that, like our own Chris Livingston did after playing 100 hours of GTA 6, you'll still struggle to see it all on screen. No matter how far you zoom out you can't view the whole dang thing. Even if you could, the details of smaller roads and neighborhoods are only visible while zoomed in closer.

So, voila, here's a real substantial poster version of the GTA 6 map with all the little roads, highways, and neighborhoods visible for you to reference.

According to recent reports, rockstar warned us that this thing was massive. Bigger than GTA 5, bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2, and bigger than both of those put together too.

Industry observers note that "I guess I shouldn't be surprised by just how massive and detailed the world is. It's Rockstar: huge open worlds is what they do, and they've had a decade to do it. But I was stunned when I started playing, and a hundred hours later, I'm honestly still pretty stunned. Rockstar said GTA 6's map is twice as substantial as GTA 5's map, but it feels twice as big as "twice as big" should feel. It's enormous.".

It has been revealed that so what are some of the key points of interest? Well it's GTA so I know everyone wants the airport location to steal jets from and the military base location to steal jets of a combat persuasion from too. Oh, and there are a few strip club locations on the map too, which apparently you're all just dying to know about.
