📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Dancing With the Stars has officially wrapped season 35, and the show is already preparing for season 36 next fall. And apparently producers are making a huge change to the voting process to prevent high-scoring couples (think: Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy) from being eliminated too early in the competition.

Recent findings suggest that “Producers have already discussed bringing back the judges’ save for next season. Again, it’s still early but that is more than likely going to be brought back,” a source tells The Sun. “They’ve already made it clear they want to shake it up next season, drastically.”.

Industry observers note that the judges’ save (which was previously in effect from Season 28 to Season 32) lets the judges step in and decide which of the bottom two pairings goes home after fans have already voted. Ultimately, it’s a way to counter fan favoritism overriding high scores.

DWTS abandoned the judges’ save because it ultimately gave the experts more power than viewers at home. As executive producer Deena Katz told TV Line, “I think it should be about America. And at the end of the day, the judges' save sometimes isn't what America wanted. And [eliminations are based on] half the judges and half America voting, and so that's kind of the reason we did it.”.

FYI, Dancing With the Stars’ UK version, Strictly Come Dancing, still uses the judges’ save, and judge Shirley Ballas explained to Extra, “I think it's good that the audience choose. They put two in the bottom. That was their choice. And then, at the end of the day, we get to save the dancer that we think is the best. Because sometimes, on your kind of show... they could send all the good dancers home. And then, you know, you're left with a different final. So it's notable that there's something for everybody.”.
