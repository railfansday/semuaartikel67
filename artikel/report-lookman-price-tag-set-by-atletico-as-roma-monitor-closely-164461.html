📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Atlético Madrid are prepared to sell Ademola Lookman in January, less than a year after signing him from Atalanta.

Industry observers note that according to Spanish outlet Fichajes, via CalcioMercato, the Nigerian forward is no longer considered untouchable by Diego Simeone, and the club would listen to offers. They won’t let him go cheaply, though. Atlético want a permanent sale worth at least the €35-40m they paid Atalanta last February.

It has been revealed that lookman is presently sidelined with a knee sprain picked up on international duty with Nigeria. The Super Eagles’ staff initially thought it was minor, but tests in Madrid demonstrated a more serious problem, although the ligaments don’t appear to be involved. He is expected to be out until early 2027, which means he should return just as the January window opens.

In a recent development, inter were previously strong admirers, but they now proposal to spend their January budget on defence. That leaves Roma as the most likely Serie A destination.

It has been revealed that gian Piero Gasperini turned Lookman into one of Europe’s most dangerous forwards at Atalanta, despite their well-documented clashes over transfers. The Roma coach has been looking for a player with his profile since the summer.

Roma also have a good relationship with Atlético, having already done business with them over Nahuel Molina.
