Former Manchester United star Ashley Young has urged Mauricio Pochettino to make a major stance and sell Bruno Fernandes in January.

Fernandes has been a phenomenal servant to United over the years, sticking with them through thick and thin after a difficult period.

His contract, though, is set to expire next summer, though United do have the option to extend that by a further 12 months.

Reports have suggested that he has rejected two contract offers from United, but discussions have been put on hold over a fresh deal.

Fernandes clearly isn't ready to fully commit to United as they sit in the relegation zone in the Premier League.

The Portuguese international hasn't hit his usual heights so far this season, providing a goal or an assist in just two Premier League matches.

He's now looking to impress upcoming manager Mauricio Pochettino, but his future remains up in the air.

Former United player Ashley Young has suggested that Manchester United should be looking to sell Fernandes during the January transfer window.

"If Manchester United can cash in on him in January, I'd do that. I wouldn't be looking to extend his contract at all, or keep him there if he'd be unhappy. He seems to be a player for whom personal accolades are more crucial than the team, and that's what he's about. He'll be the one saying, "Yeah, I won Player of the Year. I won Player of the Year. It was me, not my other teammates." That's how he looks, and he looks that way on the pitch.".

Former Liverpool defender Stephen Warnock has suggested that it would be a 'massive stance' from Pochettino to give the green light for Fernandes to leave the club in January.

Young later tipped United to replace Fernandes with someone like Morgan Gibbs-White.

Although Fernandes has been struggling this season, he is still United's captain, which could throw a spanner in the works.

It would be a decision that wouldn't be looked at fondly by many United supporters, and we're also yet to truly see what he's like under Pochettino.

Selling Fernandes during the January transfer window would be a hugely controversial move from United.

Although many would look at the season from Fernandes and suggest he's not been at his best, he still provided 11 goals and assists in 16 games.

Last season, he hit 31 goals and assists in 37 games, so it's safe to say he's carried United on his back at times.

In fact, since joining the club, Fernandes has hit at least 20 goals and assists combined in every single season.

United won't want to lose Fernandes for free, but given they have the option to extend his contract, selling him in the middle of the campaign would certainly be a controversial move.

It'll be interesting to see how Fernandes and Pochettino work together over the coming months and whether they can drag United out of this mess.
