Dozens of Palestinians have been killed in Gaza in near daily air strikes over the past month.

A Hamas political official and his wife were among eight Palestinians killed in three Israeli air strikes across the Gaza Strip on Thursday.

Local sources told the BBC a residential apartment in Deir al-Balah was targeted at dawn, killing Khaled Abu Nada - the deputy education minister - and his wife, Abeer Mohammed Ismail Abu Nada.

A Hamas Civil Defence spokesman stated that rescue teams and medical personnel had transferred eight bodies to Al-Aqsa Hospital in central Gaza and Al-Shifa Hospital in Gaza City.

In a joint statement, Israel's prime minister and defence minister stated the operation was "part of a series of targeted killings" against Hamas officials.

Thursday's strikes also reportedly killed three Palestinians on al-Nafaq Street, east of Gaza City. Their identities have not yet been established, eyewitnesses remarked.

In a separate strike on Shati refugee camp, west of Gaza City, three Palestinians working for Hamas's security services were killed. They were identified as Yasser al-Lahham, Iyad Ahmed and Ibrahim al-Mashal.

Khaled Abu Nada and his wife Abeer Mohammed Ismail Abu Nada were killed in the air strike.

In a post X, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed the death of Khaled Abu Nada and noted about 80 Hamas officials have been killed in the last month.

"No one in Hamas's leadership will succeed in evading us," he remarked, adding that the military would "continue to pursue our enemies and protect the security of Israeli civilians".

The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) remarked it had carried out a strike on a "senior" Hamas operative, and would provide further details later.

The attacks came a day after Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, 47, the head of Hamas's internal security service in northern Gaza, his wife and their two teenage daughters were killed in Israeli air strike on their apartment in Shati refugee camp in Gaza City.

On Tuesday, the overall commander of Hamas's internal security service was among 10 Palestinians reportedly killed in Israeli strikes.

Violence has continued in Gaza since Israel and Hamas agreed to a US-brokered ceasefire in October last year. Israeli strikes take place almost daily in areas under Hamas's control, which are home to 2.1 million people, most of them displaced.

At least 1,705 people have been killed in Gaza over the past 13 months, according to the territory's Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures are considered reliable by the UN.

Israel states six of its soldiers have been killed in Gaza during the same period.

The Gaza war was triggered by the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023, when about 1,200 people were killed and 251 others were taken hostage.

Since then, more than 74,772 people have been killed in Israeli attacks in Gaza, the health ministry states.
