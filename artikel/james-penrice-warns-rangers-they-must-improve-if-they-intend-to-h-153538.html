By JOHN MCGARRY, SCOTTISH CHIEF SPORTS FEATURE WRITER.

Published: 10:26 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 10:37 EST, 25 November 2026.

James Penrice has warned that Rangers can forget about going top of the table on Saturday if they play like they did for the first 45 minutes against Aberdeen.

Derek McInnes’ side were a goal down at half-time and heading to their first defeat in 14 games after failing to get going against the Dons.

With the manager making three changes at half-time, the Ibrox men scored three times in an incredible 11-minute burst through Ryan Naderi, Djeidi Gassama and Nico Raskin.

And while Aberdeen netted again via Findlay Marshall, Rangers hung on for the win which put them within a point of Celtic.

In a recent development, with the leaders facing Hearts at Tynecastle on Sunday, McInnes’ men can move to the summit if they defeat high-flying Falkirk the day before. But Penrice states that will only happen if they deliver a more complete display than they highlighted on Tuesday.

It has been revealed that ‘It’s a massive incentive for us, but the league isn’t won in November,’ said the defender. ‘Falkirk are playing well. They’ve travelled well away from home too.

James Penrice has warned Rangers they will need to improve if they are to take top spot.

‘We’ll need to be on it. We can’t go into that game like we did on Tuesday night. I wouldn’t say we were lucky, but we can’t concede two goals at home. We can’t do that on Saturday.

Sources indicate that ‘Is it crucial we grab that chance (to go top)? Definitely. We’ll just concentrate on ourselves. It’s another game to tick off and we’ll turn up hoping to get the three points.’.

McInnes introduced Tuur Rommens, Findlay Curtis and Kim Min-su at the break with the changes leading to a much-improved display.

Despite chalking up the win which maintained the unbeaten run, Penrice insists the overall show left considerable room for improvement.

It has been revealed that ‘It was very mixed,’ he reflected. ‘We concede in the first half, but then fly out the traps in the second.

‘I thought we were really good for 20 minutes. We go and score a third and look comfortable. But then we switch off. We turned our back at a free-kick and they end up scoring. We made it difficult for ourselves.

‘But we got three goals. Ryan got his usual one where he’s alive in the box. Gas was brilliant and he got his goal then Nico got his. But again, we made it hard for ourselves. The main thing is we got three points.

‘We made a few changes at half-time and I thought the boys who came on were brilliant. They had that real spark and energy. We started the second half really well but, ultimately, we need to score more.

Rangers goalscorers Djeidi Gassama, Nico Raskin and Ryan Naderi celebrate against Aberdeen.

‘We could have done that and made it easy for ourselves.’.

Rangers haven’t now tasted defeat since losing away to Czech side Jablonec on August 27. And while not every display in that run has been outstanding, Penrice is encouraged by the fact that the team is showing plenty of character.

Industry observers note that ‘We’ve shown we’ve got a bit of resilience,’ he mentioned. ‘It’s not a bad trait to be winning games and scoring late on. We’re doing that. But as players we want to make the game as easy as possible.

‘We’re on a run just now and hopefully it continues.

‘The positives on Tuesday night were that, in spells, we were really good. We were flying in the second half. But we can’t concede two goals at home and expect to always come away with a win.’.

Meanwhile, Penrice states he’s got no fears that Min-su will become a goal threat for the team.

The South Korean, an £8.5million summer signing from Girona, has yet to find the net in 14 appearances with his sole assist coming against Motherwell last Saturday.

The forward thought he’d broken his duck with a spectacular late effort against the Dons only for the ball to sail over.

‘I stated to Min-su at full-time that me and significant Ryan were ready to run to him at 100mph if he’d scored that goal,’ Penrice smiled.

It has been revealed that ‘You can see he wants it so bad. He probably just needs to calm down in front of goal.

It has been revealed that ‘I thought that was the one when he chopped inside. I thought he was scoring, but he put it into row Z.

Kim Min-su has impressed for Rangers but is still waiting to score his first goal for the club.

‘It wasn’t ideal, but he was still happy afterward because we won the game.

Recent findings suggest that ‘You can see in his game that, at every opportunity, he’s trying to get a shot away. It will come and hopefully it comes at a perfect time.

It has been revealed that ‘There’s a reason these players are at Ibrox — it’s because they’re extremely talented. It’s just that last bit but they’re very young.

According to recent reports, ‘Min-su’s game isn’t just goals and assists. He’s a really talented footballer who creates problems for teams. He’s been a massive part of why we’re on this run. You can all see it.

‘It’s about more than goals and assists, but if he adds that to his game, he’s only going to go on and have a very successful career.

It has been revealed that ‘That’s what the boys want for him. We want our wide players and strikers to score as many as possible.’.
