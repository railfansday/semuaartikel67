Hollow Knight: Silksong developer Team Cherry debuted a special 'Cherry Time Special Presentation' today (27th November), with the aim of bringing more information to light about the game's first big DLC offering, Sea of Sorrows, but also provide a nice little springboard for some other indie titles.

Industry observers note that but first, the reason we're all here: Sea of Sorrow is "an expansion like we've never done before", indicates Team Cherry, before showing off a trailer for the content - which will be free for all players, in case you've forgetten. The trailer confirms the release date for the DLC, 11th December, and teases some upcoming environments, enemies, bosses and - right at the end - what looks like a fishing mini-game. But of course, this is a nautical-themed expansion, after all.

It has been revealed that team Cherry has hinted at the future of Silksong before. Recently, and more than a year after its initial release, physical versions of Hollow Knight: Silksong finally began arriving on players' doorsteps. These editions seemed to tease previously unconfirmed "free content packs" (note the plural). "The world of Hollow Knight: Silksong will expand with free content packs," the booklet read in the physical editions. "Prepare for even more adventures filled with new friends, new abilities, and new ferocious foes!" We have no hint on what will happen next for Silksong, though, off the back of this presentation.
