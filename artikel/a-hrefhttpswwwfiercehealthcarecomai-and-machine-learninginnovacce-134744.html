Autonomous artificial intelligence healthcare operations platform Innovaccer is expanding its Gravity Voice AI capabilities with a latest integration of Grok Voice.

According to recent reports, through the integration, the platform’s healthcare intelligence data, workflow infrastructure and voice AI will be combined with Grok Voice at scale. Trained on real phone audio, Grok Voice is HIPAA-eligible and is available in more than 25 languages.

CEO and co-founder Abhinav Shashank said in a statement that healthcare voice AI is moving from "answering questions to doing work.".

"We scaled from hundreds of thousands of interactions to more than five million and revealed measurable results in appointments, referrals and engagement," Shashank noted. "By leveraging Grok Voice, we are bringing frontier intelligence into that foundation and extending where these agents work across the healthcare journey.".

According to recent reports, specifically, Innovaccer notes patient voice interactions have grown from roughly 250,000 to more than five million since its launch six months ago. And it has tripled referral intakes, helped organizations book "significantly more" appointments and reduce documentation time by 90% per case.

Sources indicate that founded in 2014, the manufacturer builds software solutions to unify enterprise data and applies AI to automate manual tasks and streamline workflows for payers and providers. As of May, it states it serves more than 200 health systems and payers, 80 million patients across the U.S. and 95% of community pharmacies.

The Grok integration is now live for customers.

The platform prepares to integrate other Grok models via API in the future, executives say, including Grok Build and Grok Bot.
