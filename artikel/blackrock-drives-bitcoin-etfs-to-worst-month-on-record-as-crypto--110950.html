BlackRock drives Bitcoin ETFs to worst month on record as crypto fund withdrawals near $6 billion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds (ETFs) have recorded their worst month since launching in January 2024, led by unprecedented withdrawals from BlackRock.

Data from Farside Investors indicates that the funds registered $569.69 million in net outflows on Nov. 23, pushing November's withdrawals to $4.71 billion. This surpasses the previous monthly record of $4.51 billion set in June by approximately $200 million.

Meanwhile, US spot Ethereum ETFs recorded $84.92 million in withdrawals on Monday, bringing their monthly outflows to $1.15 billion.

Together, the investment products have registered $5.86 billion in net withdrawals this month, reflecting deteriorating demand for crypto exposure through traditional financial markets.

The latest withdrawals came despite Monday's crypto market rally after Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia's chief executive officer briefly lifted Bitcoin above $64,000. The top crypto has pared back these gains and is trading at $60.065 as of press time, per CryptoSlate's data.

Sources indicate that according to Farside Investors, BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) led Monday's withdrawals with $509.3 million, accounting for approximately 89% of the day's net outflows. Grayscale's Bitcoin Mini Trust and Bitwise's BITB followed with withdrawals of $61.08 million and $12.6 million, respectively.

Fidelity's FBTC was the only Bitcoin fund to attract fresh capital, registering $13.29 million in net inflows.

BlackRock has emerged as the epicenter of November's crypto ETF withdrawals, with IBIT recording nearly $4 billion in monthly net outflows, its highest since inception.

In a recent development, the withdrawals represent approximately 85% of the combined outflows from US spot Bitcoin ETFs this month. IBIT has recorded withdrawals in 15 of November's 16 trading sessions.

Notably, the pace of redemptions has accelerated considerably over the past three trading sessions.

IBIT registered a then-record $541.1 million outflow on Nov. 19 before surpassing that figure with another $544.7 million in withdrawals on Nov. 20.

Combined with Monday's outflows, investors have pulled nearly $1.6 billion from the BlackRock fund over three consecutive trading sessions, representing about 40% of its monthly withdrawals.

Similar redemption pressure has extended to BlackRock's Ethereum investment products.

According to Farside Investors, the asset manager's iShares Ethereum Trust (ETHA) registered $90.5 million in net outflows on Monday, bringing its monthly outflows to around $977 million. Notably, BlackRock’s staked Ether fund (ETHB) has also registered more than $90 million in outflows this month.

Consequently, BlackRock's flagship Bitcoin and Ethereum funds have recorded about $4.9 billion in combined outflows this month.

Despite the recent redemptions, IBIT alone retains more than $62 billion in cumulative net inflows since its launch, while ETHA and ETHB hold over $10 billion in combined inflows.

In a recent development, meanwhile, four trading sessions remain before November ends, including a shortened session on Nov. 27 following the Thanksgiving holiday.

With combined Bitcoin and Ethereum ETF withdrawals already approaching $6 billion, investors would need to reverse the prevailing redemption trend during the shortened trading week to prevent November's losses from deepening further.
