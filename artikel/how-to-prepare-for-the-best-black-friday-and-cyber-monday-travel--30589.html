According to recent reports, black Friday is no longer just one day; it's practically an entire month. Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday deals are coming from every direction, and it can be tough to keep up with them all.

It has been revealed that if you're feeling overwhelmed, we're here to help. Whether you're looking to save on your next massive trip, hoping to score some amazing travel gear or want to buy holiday gifts, there's a deal for everyone — and we're here to help you navigate this year's sea of Black Friday sales and maximize your savings.

Although many deals are already live, the official 2026 sale days are as follows:.

Traditionally, Black Friday is best for in-store deals. However, we've seen an increasing number of online deals emerge each year, so we recommend checking both in-store and online pricing before making a purchase.

According to recent reports, cyber Monday (the Monday after Black Friday) is when most companies release deals online, but it’s not uncommon for deals to launch even earlier. A good example is Amazon, which is already offering numerous discounts in advance of the official Nov. 19 launch of its Black Friday Week.

Industry observers note that meanwhile, Travel Tuesday (the day after Cyber Monday) is when airlines, hotels, cruise operators and other travel companies typically release deals, though some sales have already begun. You should always be looking for ways to double or triple dip on your purchases, and Black Friday is no exception. Here are our top tips for saving even more money and maximizing your rewards this season.

If your desired merchant is available on a shopping portal, you can earn bonus rewards, such as points, miles, or cash back, on your purchase.

Sources indicate that for instance, Rakuten is currently offering 8% cash back (or 8 American Express Membership Rewards points or Bilt Rewards Points per dollar spent) on accommodations at Booking.com. If this earning rate stays the same through Booking.com's Black Friday sale, you could potentially get 8% back on top of the platform's up-to-40% discount on hotels.

Payouts often vary from day to day, so we recommend using a shopping portal aggregator like Cashback Monitor to find the best portal for your purchase.

Related: How to maximize shopping portals for Black Friday and holiday deals.

In a recent development, amex Offers and Chase Offers are among our favorite ways to save money on everyday purchases, and they're easy to stack with Black Friday sales. Check your offers on all your credit card accounts (each card may be different) before making a purchase, and if any apply to your merchant, be sure to activate them on your card and pay with the same card.

If you collect Amex Membership Rewards points, check your Amazon account to see if you're targeted for one of these offers to save up to 40% when you redeem at least 714 points at checkout.

Industry observers note that lastly, remember to use the correct card to maximize your rewards on Black Friday purchases. Our lists of the top travel cards and best cards for online shopping are good places to start.

When booking travel, be sure to use a card that comes with built-in trip protections. For other purchases, consider a card that offers extended warranties.

As Black Friday sales continue to proliferate, it's helpful to strategy ahead. Consider what you'd like to purchase (or book), and mark your calendars accordingly.

Recent findings suggest that don't forget that many travel bookings are refundable within 24 hours of making your reservation (and sometimes longer). If you spot a can't-miss deal, consider booking right away and double-checking your dates afterward.
