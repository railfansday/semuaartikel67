On the Munich side, the case of Jamal Musiala crystallizes tensions. Indeed, between the leaders who question his contract until 2030 in the face of his neurological problems and the clan which defends him in the middle of this media and sporting storm, Rio Ferdinand reacted strongly on X. Obviously annoyed by the attitude of the Bavarian management and the way in which the case is presented publicly, the former English international defender delivered a scathing statement: “Bayern Munich is better than that… If they want to negotiate more reasonable terms for the club in the future due to Jamal’s health problems, then surely this should be discussed with him and his entourage rather than the media. ».

For the former Manchester United player, this media exposure only increases the burden of a young player already faced with a delicate situation on a medical and sporting level. “This situation must already be difficult enough for Jamal to deal with without the media and club management talking about it publicly. Bayern Munich is one of the clubs that, from the outside, has always behaved with a class and a level of demand that most have admired. Unfortunately, we simply do not find this Bayern Munich in this situation”also wrote Rio Ferdinand.
