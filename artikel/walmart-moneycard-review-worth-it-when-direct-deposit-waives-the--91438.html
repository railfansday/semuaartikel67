In a recent development, get the Walmart MoneyCard if you shop at Walmart and can send it $500 a month in direct deposits, which waives its $5.94 monthly fee. Then the cash back, up to $75 a year, is yours to keep. Without those deposits, the fee costs nearly as much as the most cash back it pays.

The MoneyCard is a Visa or Mastercard debit account from Green Dot Bank for people who shop at Walmart, the only place it pays cash back. A shopper who spends $300 a month in Walmart’s stores earns 1% there, or $36 a year, about half of the $71.28 the fee costs over 12 months without direct deposit.

It has been revealed that walmart shoppers who send it $500 or more of their pay each month skip the monthly fee and keep up to $75 a year in cash back. Without those deposits, anyone who can open a free checking account should keep their pay there instead of paying $71.28 a year for this one.
