Black Friday may still be a few weeks away, but furniture retailers aren’t waiting for the official festivities to begin. Some of our favorite brands are already rolling out discounts on living room furniture, bedroom sets, and statement pieces that have absolutely no business being in our shopping cart but somehow end up there anyway. Considering how swiftly foundational pieces like a upcoming sofa, bed frame, or dresser can gobble up a decorating budget, we’re certainly not complaining.

Industry observers note that of course, not every early November sale is technically a Black Friday sale. Some brands are soft launching their holiday shopping season while others are tempting us with fall clearance events and limited-time promotions. Either way, there is an abundance of furniture deals worth shopping well before November 27. We’ve rounded up early Black Friday furniture deals from 14 retailers that should be on your home decor radar, including AD-tested favorites, designer collaborations, and plenty of pieces that might finally convince you to update that room you’ve been meaning to refresh.

In a recent development, A good night’s sleep is an invaluable investment, but that doesn't mean you have to pay full price for it. Saatva is at the moment offering up to $625 off orders, giving you a head start on upgrading your sleep set up if a luxurious mattress is on your wishlist. Note: You need a mattress in your cart for the discount to apply. The Loom & Leaf is particularly attractive for memory foam devotees, which contributor Bailey Berg previously described as the “most breathable, comfortable mattress” she’d ever tried. And if a bedroom overhaul is in the cards, Saatva’s upholstered Halle storage platform bed offers a discreet place to stash extra bedding totally out of sight.

A little coastal influence can go a long way, even if the nearest beach is blanketed in snow during the off-season. Serena & Lily is currently hosting The Sale of the Year, with up to 40% off furniture, mirrors, and lighting, plus discounts across nearly every other product category. It’s a chance to bring the brand’s breezy, tailored aesthetic home in time for the holidays. We’re particularly fond of its outdoor lineup, including the Pacifica Sofa, which AD contributor Blake Bakkila put to the test for more than three years at her Newport Beach home. Despite its exposure to coastal moisture, she reported no fading, warping, or damage, and gave the sofa an overall A rating. For an indoor refresh, the Carter counter stool brings the brand’s signature natural texture into the kitchen.

It has been revealed that there’s a reason Wayfair is a go-to destination when a room needs furnishing without a designer-size budget. The retailer’s Holiday Flash Sale is underway, with discounts of up to 70% sitewide. Its family of brands includes AllModern, a favorite for contemporary silhouettes at relatively approachable prices. AD contributor Elise Portale tested the AllModern Breanna chaise sectional, praising its soft upholstery. “At the end of the day, the best way I can describe it is ‘luxurious,’” she remarked. Take your living room refresh a step further with a ladder bookcase from Madison Park, or get a head start on decking the halls with festive finds at wallet-friendly prices.

According to recent reports, for those with a penchant for clean-lined furniture, Article’s vast catalog of ready-to-ship mid-century-inspired pieces is a treasure trove. A sale section filled with last-chance pieces priced at up to 40% off only sweetens the deal. Don’t sleep on the Tessu bed frame, either, especially if you’re looking to soften up your bedroom with its low profile and ivory wool boucle upholstery.

In a recent development, A design classic rarely comes cheap, which makes Design Within Reach’s current Herman Miller promotion particularly noteworthy. Right now, you can score 20% off the brand’s iconic modern desk chairs and 25% off task and gaming chairs. Think of this as your sign to incorporate a little bit of legendary mid-century design into your workday for a fraction of the price. And don’t overlook Herman Miller’s lighting, tables, and home decor pieces, which are also included in this rare sale.

We’ve been drawn to the collection created by actor Laura Harrier and interior designer Tiffany Howell, which debuted in April and pairs richly layered materials and vintage influences to contemporary furniture. Joybird’s extensive upholstery catalog and seemingly endless configuration options make up for every sad millennial-gray starter sofa that’s seen one move too many. The brand’s early Black Friday sale offers up to 45% off select furniture, including its distinctive, customizable seating. AD senior entertainment editor Rachel Wallace chose the Sebastian sofa in a vibrant chartreuse velvet and was won over by its rounded silhouette and generous proportions. “This is by far the most supreme nap sofa I have ever experienced,” she said. The Sebastian is just one of many seating options worth considering, along with the Amelia grand swivel chair, a cozy contender for reading nooks and movie nights.

Amazon’s furniture selection can require some digging, but there are worthwhile finds among the seemingly endless listings, particularly for bedroom basics. Allewie is among our favorite furniture brands on Amazon, and early deals on its already affordable pieces can save you up to 33%. The brand’s lift-up hydraulic storage bed frame is an especially practical pick for bedrooms short on closet space, offering concealed storage beneath the mattress. “I think the best part of the frame is that it takes up a good amount of space without being excessive, allowing for a good variety of bedroom setup,” says contributor Will Porter, who tested the bed frame at home. The brand’s black oak wooden bed frame takes a more understated approach with a sleek, modern headboard and footboard, but don’t stop there. Amazon has plenty of discounted furniture for the rest of your home, too.

These contemporary heirlooms are more likely to be passed down from one generation to the next than they are to go out of style, so these prices are practically a steal.

The brand’s midcentury-inspired dressers and nightstands are perennial favorites, but we’re particularly interested in the Shelter Sleeper sofa for anyone expecting out-of-town guests this holiday season. Contributor Yelena Moroz Alpert tested the queen-size version in dark olive performance velvet and called it “the softest, cushiest couch in my house.” Another standout from the sale is the midcentury five-drawer dresser, a handsome storage solution narrow enough for smaller bedrooms and cozy apartments. Equally impressive deals can be found on accent chairs, dining tables, and some of the brand’s bestsellers.

Pottery Barn’s extensive selection of living room, dining room, and bedroom furniture has earned it a reputation as a one-stop shop for furnishing an entire space. Its current Fall Sale includes up to 70% of select furniture, with additional discounts on certain styles. The brand is known for its comfortable, traditional seating, including the York Slope Arm Deep Seat Slipcovered Side Sleeper, currently 20% off. Contributor Kate McGregor tested the sofa and praised its plush cushions, washable slipcover, and easy-to-use sleeper mechanism. “This is one of the most comfortable sofas I’ve had the pleasure of sitting on,” she wrote. If your living room doubles as your guest room, this is one deal you won’t want to skip.

The direct-to-consumer furniture brand is already hosting a Black Friday Preview with up to 40% off.
