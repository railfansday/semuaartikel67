📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, black Friday lands on Nov. 27 this year, with Cyber Monday following on Nov. 30. Many astronomy companies aren’t waiting, though: Several sales are already running, and a few extend through the end of December.

You’ll find our roundup of sales below. Manufacturers selling directly from their own sites come first. Retailers follow, along with the brand promotions they’re carrying.

Recent findings suggest that celestron — Holiday sale (“Season of Savings”)The NexStar SE and NexStar Evolution lines are discounted by as much as $500, with the promotion running through Dec. 31. The sale also includes reduced prices on the collection’s binoculars, eyepieces, and accessories.

Recent findings suggest that explore Scientific — Black Friday saleEverything in stock is 20% off, with select telescopes, eyepieces, and binoculars dropped by up to 30%, through Nov. 30 or while supplies last. Sale prices are already listed, so no code is required. The newly introduced Sidara Series telescopes and the 72° and 92° ED Series eyepieces are excluded.

Industry observers note that pegasus Astro — Black Friday saleFrom Nov. 23 through Dec. 4, Pegasus Astro is offering two bundles. The NYX-88 bundle pairs the NYX-88 harmonic drive mount with its matching tripod and hand controller. The Ultimate Powerbox v3 bundle also includes a free 16- to 17-volt, 10-amp power supply (bundle SKU PEG-DEAL2-2611). The bundles are sold through Pegasus Astro’s authorized dealers.

Seestar — Black Friday saleZWO’s smart telescope brand has taken 5% off the S30 Pro, bringing it to $664 from $699, through Nov. 30. Select tripods and accessories are also discounted.

Sky-Watcher — Holiday sale (“Ho Ho Holidays”)Through Dec. 31, Sky-Watcher is cutting up to $1,401 off its mounts and Dobsonians. The sale started Nov. 1 and covers manual, tabletop, and computerized Dobs, including the Virtuoso GTi line. On the mount side, it includes EQ, AZ, and AZ-EQ models such as the Star Adventurer trackers, the EQ6 and EQ8 series, and the CQ350.

Industry observers note that SVBONY — Black Friday saleSelected SVBONY telescopes, astro cameras, binoculars, and spotting scopes are up to 50% off, and discounts are already live. The main Black Friday sale runs from Nov. 16 to 29 with a Cyber Monday sale from Nov. 30 to Dec. 6.

Unistellar — Black Friday saleEvery Unistellar telescope is 20% off from Nov. 1 through Dec. 1, with the discount applied automatically at checkout. Accessories such as backpacks and solar filters carry the same discount.

According to recent reports, ZWO — Black Friday saleFrom Nov. 10 to 30, most ZWO cameras and accessories are up to 10% off. Mounts are also 10% off and come with a free tripod.

Agena AstroProducts — Black Friday and holiday brand promotions.

In a recent development, astronomics — Holiday saleThe Sky-Watcher Holiday Dobsonian Sale covers 15 Dobsonians carried by Astronomics, from tabletop models to 16-inch go-to Flextubes. Discounts run roughly 11% to 13%, with the largest savings, $701, on the 16-inch Flextube SynScan 400P. No end date is posted.

Sources indicate that B&H Photo — Holiday sale (“Holiday Deals on Cameras & Lenses”)B&H is discounting cameras and lenses from more than 20 brands. The camera makers include Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, OM System, and Pentax. On the lens side are names popular with astrophotographers, such as Sigma, Tamron, Rokinon, Samyang, Venus Optics’ Laowa line, and AstrHori. B&H’s Holiday Deal Zone also posts short-lived offers that rotate every day or two, including tripods, camera bags, and other gear that’s useful under the night sky.

Recent findings suggest that high Point Scientific — Holiday brand promotions running now; Black Friday sale coming soonHigh Point’s dedicated Black Friday page claims deals are coming soon. In the meantime, the retailer is carrying several brand promotions:.

Industry observers note that telescopes.net — Black Friday saleFrom Nov. 23 through Nov. 30, code HOLIYAY takes 5% off in-stock, regularly priced items.

Did we miss your sale? Retailers and manufacturers can send details to astronomyeditorial@astronomy.com, and we’ll add it to this list.
