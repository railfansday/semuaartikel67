Industry observers note that “Dirty Dirty Disco” was released Sept. 10 as the first collaboration between the Melbourne-formed dance-rock group and Minogue, pairing her with Rogue Traders vocalist Natalie Bassingthwaighte.

“When I heard the original version of ‘Dirty Dirty Disco’ I was instantly in love with the record,” Mashd N Kutcher’s Matt James noted. “What an awesome link up between two icons. The remix came together super quickly and was all about the vibes, a cut to spin late nights.”.

Rogue Traders’ James Ash said the Minogue collaboration was unusual for the group. “Getting to work with the immortal Dannii Minogue, and combining her magic with what we do in Rogue Traders has led to something special,” he said. “Then when Matt reached out and basically sent me his mix completely out of the blue — I was absolutely floored.”.

According to recent reports, minogue noted she had wanted to work with the group for some time. “I couldn’t be more happy that he was digging this track and wanted to remix it and spin it in his sets,” she said.

According to recent reports, minogue, the younger sister of Kylie Minogue, charted on Billboard’s Top Dance Albums with 2003’s Neon Nights, which peaked at No. 17. Rogue Traders were formed by Ash and Steve Davis, with Bassingthwaighte joining in 2005; their biggest hit, “Voodoo Child,” led their second album, Here Come the Drums.

Mashd N Kutcher have had an expansive 2026, releasing collaborations with Tujamo and Darude and performing at the inaugural NFL game in Melbourne, the post-AFL Grand Final entertainment and the Sydney Marathon. They also played main stages at Handpicked, Sunny Side Up Festival and Snow Machine in Japan.

According to recent reports, the group have logged more than 2,000 live performances, hold multiple platinum certifications in Australia and reached No. 12 in triple j’s Hottest 100 of 2020 with “Get on the Beers,” their remix of a speech by then-Victorian Premier Daniel Andrews.

According to recent reports, mashd N Kutcher begin a 12-week Friday-night takeover across the NOVA Network nationally on Nov. 27.
