According to recent reports, A $100 budget opens up plenty of possibilities for thoughtful gifting, especially when you know where to look. From sculptural objects for the home to everyday essentials with unexpected details, there’s no shortage of well-designed finds that won’t break the bank.

Photography courtesy of Williams Sonoma.

Le Creuset brings a little holiday magic to its classic cocotte with an embossed Christmas tree lid and a gold-finished star knob. Crafted from durable glazed stoneware, the 24-ounce vessel moves easily from oven to table, making it ideal for individual pot pies, baked pasta, or warm desserts. Available in six colors, it’s a festive take on a kitchen staple that’s as functional as it is charming.

The Noel Mini Cocotte is available at williams-sonoma.com for $62.

Inspired by the pleated designs of Issey Miyake, Wooj’s Kinoko Lamp borrows the delicate look of folded paper without its fragility. Available in Amber, Smoke, Olive Oil, and Natural, its softly glowing silhouette brings warmth and a little whimsy to a desk, bookshelf, or bedside table.

The Kinoko Lamp is available at wooj.design for $95.

With its puffy, quilted silhouette and compact proportions, Calpak’s Luka Mini Duffel brings a playful edge to an everyday essential. The lightweight, water-resistant bag packs plenty of functionality into its design, including padded shoulder straps, dedicated bottle pockets, and a luggage sleeve with a hidden security compartment. Available in 18 colors, from understated neutrals to shimmering metallics, it makes a stylish companion for weekend getaways or trips to the gym.

The Luka Mini Duffel is available in select colors at calpaktravel.com for $98.
