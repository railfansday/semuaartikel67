Seeking redemption after failing at Spurs, newly installed Crystal Palace manager has a tough challenge ahead.

Recent findings suggest that thomas Frank promised his wife, Nanna, he would take a year’s sabbatical after being sacked by Tottenham in February, but he couldn’t wait that long. Having received a reported £8m payoff from Spurs, he kept an extremely low profile in the first few months after an experience at the club that sources close to the 53‑year‑old have described as “traumatic”.

Sources indicate that frank broke his silence at the start of June with a statement released by the League Managers Association. It said he would cover the World Cup for the BBC (thereby going head-to-head with Ange Postecoglou, his predecessor at Spurs, who worked for ITV) before “experiencing the Tour de France”, a lifelong passion. Frank usually preferred to run to Brentford’s training ground and home matches from his home in East Sheen during a nine‑year spell as assistant and then manager, but he occasionally cycled because it helped to clear his head.
