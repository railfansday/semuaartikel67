Breaking: TSA PreCheck Touchless ID: Every airport with the speedy, new security lanes

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (178 words, 📖 Standard)
  • The TSA PreCheck Touchless ID program now enables eligible travelers to pass through security checkpoints at over 60 major U.S. airports without presenting a physical ID or boarding pass.
  • This system uses facial comparison technology to match travelers' faces with their passport photos on file, streamlining the security process.
  • To use Touchless ID, travelers must opt in through their airline frequent flyer profile by uploading a passport photo and consenting to facial identification.
  • Availability varies by airline and terminal, so travelers should confirm participation at their departure airport before relying on this service.
  • Only TSA PreCheck members or those with access through Global Entry who opt in with their airline can use Touchless ID lanes.
  • Not all airlines or terminals at a given airport may offer Touchless ID, so check availability in advance.
  • Travelers should still bring a physical ID, as technical issues or officer requests may require traditional identification.
