GTA 6 has no shortage of forces trying to put a damper on your crime spree, but the long arm of the law might be the least of your worries if you're planning a successful caper in Vice City. As players have discovered, the greatest threat to a clean getaway might just be an impressive mural, as they've smashed their vehicles right into an unforgiving wall that looks like it was ripped straight out of a Road Runner cartoon. Located in Vice City's Decor District, this piece of street art resembles a tunnel going under the nearby overpass, and at high speed, it's pretty convincing--something I discovered to my dismay, totaling my pretty sweet muscle car in the process. Does anyone have an industrial-strength magnet so that I can start working out these dents? I'm sure it'll buff out. It's a delicious bit of trolling from Rockstar Games: the kind of design choice that would make Looney Tunes legend Tex Avery proud with how effective it is. Numerous players have been caught out, and this is just one of many little secrets and Easter eggs hidden in the game.
