Multiple sources in Italy say Moise Kean’s return to action with Como has been delayed due to a upcoming tibia glitch.

Italy and Como striker Kean will be out longer than expected.

The 26-year-old last played in October, featuring in Italy’s 1-1 draw against France, and was expected to miss five weeks.

However, La Provincia di Como and Sky Sport Italia claim that Kean will need more time to recover.

According to La Provincia di Como, Kean recently saw a newly introduced specialist, while Sky claims the striker has developed new problems in his tibia.

Sky confirms Kean’s recent specialist visit, adding that it took place in Barcelona in the past few days.

Although the nature of his latest problem remains unclear, Kean won’t be back anytime soon, as both media outlets say the ex-Fiorentina and Juventus star needs more time to return to action.

Kean joined Como from Fiorentina in a €40m deal last summer. The Italy international scored one goal and provided one assist in six appearances before his injury.
