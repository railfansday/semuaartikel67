Some Kingdom Hearts fans are convinced that they’ve found a reference to the series in Grand Theft Auto 6, but not everyone is so sure it's intentional.

As you no doubt know by now, GTA 6 is absolutely chock-a-block with references to other video games. We’ve seen Mario, Final Fantasy, the original Vice City, Clair Obscur, a surprisingly sharp The Last of Us jab, and oh so much more. In fact, it’s probably easier to say the games that don’t have some kind of easter egg in GTA 6.

So far, one such series is Kingdom Hearts, which is a little bit of a shame since it’s my all-time favourite. You’d think a Keyblade ripoff would be hiding somewhere in Leonida, but that doesn’t appear to be the case just yet. Even if the series’ signature weapon isn't lurking, some fans are sure there’s a nod in there to one of its iconic symbols.

Sources indicate that as pointed out by Redditor Vogcoycoycut on the Kingdom Hearts subreddit, fans have found what they believe to be a reference to the series within GTA 6. It can be found during one of the game’s story missions, where Jason and Dre’Quan go to a watch store that’s clearly meant to be parodying Rolex.

On the floor of that shop is a crown symbol that’s meant to be riffing on the one that Rolex has. Only, in this case, it’s much closer to the crown featured on Sora’s necklace and serves as one of Kingdom Hearts' main symbols. It’s not one-to-one or anything, but it’s incredibly close, and much more so than a generic crown logo.

According to recent reports, while the riff on Rolex and the symbol being featured in a jewellery store (which makes me think of Sora’s necklace) do make it seem like a nod to Kingdom Hearts, there’s also a chance that it’s just a generic crown symbol that’s close to KH and that fans are seeing what they want to see. The thread is clearly very split on whether it’s intentional or not, and I can't blame them.

You could even really stretch by saying that time is a reference to Young Xehanort. Like I say, you’d REALLY be stretching.

In a recent development, that being mentioned, this is Rockstar that we’re talking about, so we know that no detail is included in GTA 6 without at least some thought behind it. It’s still certainly not definitive, but there’s a chance someone at the studio is a massive fan of Kingdom Hearts and wanted to include at least one nod to the series.
