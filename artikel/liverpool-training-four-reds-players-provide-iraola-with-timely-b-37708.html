Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola has been provided with a timely team news boost ahead of the Premier League showdown against Manchester United on Sunday.

With the international break now concluded, the Reds players who were away with their countries have now reconvened on Merseyside and were put through their paces at the AXA Training Centre on Thursday.

There were no fresh injury concerns arising from national team duties, and there had already been good news earlier in the week when Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni resumed first-team training as they both close in on long-awaited returns from lengthy absences.

As per Liverpool Echo, a number of Reds players who had been carrying slight knocks or been absent through injury prior to the international window are now on course to be available for the Man United fixture.

The Dutch trio of Virgil van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong all trained, with the captain skipping Netherlands duty due to a minor issue, the midfielder missing our last three games with a heel problem, and the right-back overcoming a muscle injury he sustained in mid-October.

(Photo by Kate McShane/Getty Images).

Dominik Szoboszlai had played through a slight knee problem for Hungary in recent days, but he was involved in the session in Kirkby today, albeit with strapping on the affected area.

Two other notable inclusions were Alexis Mac Allister and Ronald Araujo, both of whom partook in training despite being just back from representing their countries in South America, although Bradley and Leoni weren’t involved in today’s session as their recoveries are being managed carefully.

Iraola will be delighted to get through the international window with no fresh injury absentees (unlike the damaging extended break earlier in the autumn), and also to see those who had been carrying knocks all involved in the session this morning.

The returns of Gravenberch and Frimpong give the head coach greater scope for selection in midfield and at right-back respectively, and Van Dijk and Szoboszlai could both be cleared to start as they seem to have shaken off their minor issues.

Barring any new setbacks between now and Sunday, it looks as though the only definite absentees for the Man United game will be Bradley, Leoni and Hugo Ekitike, each of whom continue to nurse long-term injuries but are on course to return in the coming weeks.

Iraola will inevitably be asked for updates on each of those players when he holds his pre-match press conference on Friday afternoon, and his squad seems to be nicely stocked at the beginning of what’ll be a punishing fixture schedule in the lead-up to the festive period. Long may it stay that way!
