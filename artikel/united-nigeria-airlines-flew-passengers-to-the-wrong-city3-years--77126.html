According to recent reports, united Nigeria Airlines flew passengers to the wrong city and blamed the weather. Three years later, they’re admitting it was all a lie. I covered this incident on November 28, 2023, including the conflict between the pilot reportedly acknowledging a wrong flight plan and the airline insisting on a weather diversion. Now, Live and Let’s Fly flags the airline chairman’s admission that the passengers’ explanation was right. Executive Chairman Obiora Okonkwo revisited the episode at the World Public Relations Forum in Abuja on November 17. A dispatcher had entered the wrong destination in the paperwork. The pilots flew there. The flight attendants apparently believed they’d reached the city everyone bought tickets to. He described the weather explanation as “just a lie” and offered this advice: “The best way of public relations is to tell the truth, but tell it responsibly.” Passengers Were Welcomed To Abuja While Sitting In Asaba On November 26, 2023, flight 0504 left Lagos with passengers booked to Abuja, Nigeria’s capital. It landed in Asaba instead, about 200 miles away. This was United Nigeria Airlines, not United Airlines. Passengers reported that the flight attendants welcomed everyone to Abuja but then everyone discovered where they actually were.
