According to recent reports, google designed TPUs from the ground up to be exceptionally efficient for machine learning workloads, delivering top-tier performance per total cost of ownership (TCO). Because TPUs trade general-purpose hardware scheduling for wide systolic arrays, dedicated SparseCore engines, and custom interconnect fabrics, adhering to specific architectural best practices is essential to achieve maximum performance. To help developers get the most out of TPUs, we are launching the open-source AI-Hypercomputer/tpu-codesign guide. This post highlights its key principles—spanning shape alignment, architectural choices like LatentMoE, and parallelism strategies—to accelerate LLMs, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, and other hardware-intensive workloads on TPUs.

Performance tuning starts with model definition. Architectural choices made before the first training step set permanent ceilings on MFU (Model FLOP Utilization), cost, and serving latency. The general rules hold for any large-matmul architecture (dense LLMs, multimodal, and world models), and the companion AI-Hypercomputer/tpu-codesign repository grows model- and workload-specific sections over time.

In a recent development, eighth-generation TPUs. These principles carry forward to TPU 8t and TPU 8i and to the newly revealed TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max. The reasoning stays the same and the thresholds move with the hardware. TPU 8t and TPU 8i both add native FP4. For training, TPU 8t also brings more balanced Vector Processing Unit (VPU) scaling. For serving, TPU 8i adds the Boardfly topology, a Collectives Acceleration Engine (CAE), and 3x more on-chip SRAM. TPUs are purpose-built custom ML ASICs designed from the ground up to deliver state-of-the-art performance per total cost of ownership (TCO). While their architectural choices—such as wide 256×256 systolic arrays, software-managed VMEM, dedicated SparseCore engines, and direct multi-dimensional ICI fabrics—enable exceptional efficiency, they inherently create specific structural constraints and programming patterns that must be carefully followed to unlock maximal performance.
