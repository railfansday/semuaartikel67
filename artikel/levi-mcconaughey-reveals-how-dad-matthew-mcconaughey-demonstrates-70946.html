Levi McConaughey is never dazed nor confused about Matthew McConaughey’s guidance.

In fact, as the 18-year-old—whose role in the Apple TV movie Way of the Warrior Kid marks his second onscreen appearance—explained, his dad shows up for him in ways that’s more than just alright.

“He's in my corner as my dad and as my friend,” Levi told E! News in an exclusive interview at the movie Way of the Warrior Kid premiere in Los Angeles Nov.19, “and I couldn't be more happy and blessed and thankful for him being a dad to me, which means a lot.”.

And as Matthew watches Levi navigate his own career, the Dazed and Confused alum—who is also dad to Vida McConaughey, 16, and Livingston McConaughey, 13, with Camila Alves McConaughey—has shared very candid advice with his oldest son.

In a recent development, “No bulls--t in your bones,” Levi admitted. “100 percent effort. If not 100 percent, 110 percent effort.”.

In Way of the Warrior Kid, Levi appears alongside Chris Pratt, Linda Cardellini, Jude Hill, Ava Torres and more—an experience he’s simply grateful to have under his belt.

“I’m just thankful to be in an environment with G [director McG] and Chris and Jude and Ava,” he noted, “and everyone to do with this movie that made it a really collaborative, creative environment. It was great.”.

Matthew’s words of wisdom echoes what he’s previously shared about what he’s emphasized to Levi as he steps in front of the camera.

In a recent development, “You’ve got to be able to be able to detail with rejection, that’s part of it,” the Oscar winner told E! News in August. “You’ve got to hopefully love doing it and love the process so much that enough ‘nos’ don’t matter. If not, it’s not for you, because I was taught, there’s no ‘help wanted’ signs in Hollywood.”.

As Matthew noted, you miss 100 percent of the shots you don’t take.

Industry observers note that “I just try to push, to say, ‘It’s better to make a fool of yourself and find out’ then to leave an audition and go, ‘Oh I had that moment and I hesitated,’” he continued. “Make a fool of yourself. Fail more. Try and fail more. No one gives as much of a damn as you think they do.”.

Industry observers note that way of the Warrior Kid will premiere globally on Apple TV Nov. 25.
