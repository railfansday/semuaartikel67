Pictures of the rumoured Milan home shirts for the 2027-28 season, which will be the final year of the club’s partnership with kit manufacturer Puma, have recently emerged online. Take a look below!

According to kit experts Footy Headlines, the Rossoneri strip for the 2027-28 season will feature much narrower stripes compared to the current 2026-27 strip, and will also feature a polo collar, as opposed to the round-neck collar on the most recent home shirt.

The 2027-28 season will be the final year of the club’s partnership with German manufacturer Puma, who have designed the Rossoneri shirts since 2018. Milan will make the switch to Adidas for the 2028-29 season onwards.
