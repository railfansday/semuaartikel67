Cheats in GTA 6 are weirdly restrictive, and I'm counting the minutes until the PC version fixes them

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, using cheats in GTA 6 is a given. GTA games are substantial, beautiful sandboxes of destruction, and sometimes I want to forget about story missions and just magically fill my pockets with cash, spawn a tank or a jet, and go on a rampage with infinite incendiary ammo.

Recent findings suggest that naturally, you can do that with GTA 6 cheats. But only eventually. If you were hoping to try out some cheats the moment you step into the game, I've got some bad news for you—and then some worse news. Here are the problems:.

Maybe that's fair—Rockstar devs don't want you busting out cheats and skipping through the game they spent 10 years crafting. But then again, if you shell out potentially $100 for a game, shouldn't you be able to play it however the heck you want? I think so.

Industry observers note that GTA 6 is a big, long game, with a five chapter main story that contains 91 missions. It took me about 90 hours to finish, at which point I could finally access the cheats menu.

That's where I found the next bit of bad news...

Congrats on playing those 90 hours: are you ready for 90 more? Most cheats on the menu are locked because there are requirements you need to meet to use them. Want a cheat that removes fall damage? First you'll need to survive 20 falls of over more than 40 feet. Want to spawn a parachute? First complete 50 skydives and basejumps.

Recent findings suggest that using an infinite ammo cheat means first winning 25 medals on shooting ranges. To fast-forward time you'll need to find 10 collectible watches—and in 90 hours I found exactly one of them. Spawning a jet means first completing every single challenge in the game, which is gonna take, ooh, let me check… forever.

I don't love this cheat unlock system at all, but there's even more bad news:.

Want to use a cheat to give yourself an extra $250,000 so you can go on a car buying spree? Cool, go ahead. But you won't be able to save your progress once you enable a cheat.

Recent findings suggest that whatever fun you have cheating is purely of the sandbox variety. You can buy cars and clothes and guns with cheated money, but you'll lose it all when you stop playing because you're not allowed to save your game once you use a single cheat. Booo.

And there's one final bummer for ya:.

I tried it. I opened an older save where I hadn't yet completed the main story, and tried to input the cheats I'd learned from my complete game save. No dice! The cheats don't work unless you're using a save file that's story complete. So, starting over at the beginning and using cheats? Not gonna happen.

Industry observers note that I'm really not a fan of this. With so many restrictions in place, Rockstar has really taken the fun out of being a huge GTA cheater! When GTA 6 finally comes to PC, I hope modders make cheats their top priority.
