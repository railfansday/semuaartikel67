MSC Cruises has celebrated the achievements of its shore excursion partners at the second “Shorexcellence Awards,” which was held in Genoa as part of BEST 2026, the Blue Economy Summit and Trade Show.

The awards recognize excellence in innovation, operational performance, sustainability and collaboration.

Marialuisa Iaccarino, vice president, shore excursions, MSC Cruises, remarked: “Every shore excursion is an opportunity to create lasting memories and meaningful connections between our guests and the destinations they visit.”.

“The awards celebrate partners who continuously exceed expectations, deliver exceptional service and embrace innovation while helping us showcase the very best of each destination,” added Iaccarino.

“Their passion, commitment and expertise are instrumental to the success of our shore excursion program and the experiences we offer our guests every day.”.

Bringing together tour operators, destination experts and industry stakeholders from around the world, the awards recognize the important role that shore excursion partners play in creating memorable experiences for guests and connecting them with the destinations and communities they visit.

In a press release, the firm mentioned guest feedback plays a significant role in the awards, with winning tours and operators achieving exceptional satisfaction scores and consistently demonstrating a strong commitment to quality throughout the guest journey.

The 2026 awards highlighted responsible tourism and the importance of strong destination partnerships, MSC added.
