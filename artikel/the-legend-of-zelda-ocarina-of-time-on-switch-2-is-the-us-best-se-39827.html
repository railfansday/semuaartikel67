The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on Switch 2 is the US' best-selling physical game so far this year

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the very popular and critically-acclaimed The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake has set another record. After selling 5.2m copies in four days and becoming the Switch 2's fastest-selling game, Ocarina of Time has now been revealed to also be a major seller at retail.

Sources indicate that the remake has sold more than 1m physical copies in the US alone in its launch week (ending 7th November). That's according to Circana analyst Mat Piscatella, who revealed on Bluesky that the record also makes it the first title to surpass 1m physical sales since 2023's Tears of the Kingdom. That is, no other game - from any publisher - has managed to sell over 1m copies in the US within its first week in three years.

According to recent reports, while Nintendo games tend to perform better at retail than those from Sony, Xbox or third-party publishers, physical sales in general have been declining in the US. In July, Piscatella revealed that just seven PlayStation games have sold more than 100,000 physical copies up to that point in 2026, and that was after all the backlash to Sony's decision to end physical disc manufacturing by January 2028.

Ocarina of Time's lead of this particular segment likely won't continue, however. That's because, as Piscatella pointed out in a follow-up post, Circana's tracking considers disc-less, code-in-box versions to be physical editions. This means Grand Theft Auto 6 will likely overtake the remake in short order, just as it's expected to break records elsewhere.

In Eurogamer's glowing review of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, Christian Donlan argued that Nintendo has done just enough to modernise a timeless classic while maintaining what made the original so special for many players.
