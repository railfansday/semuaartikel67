The biggest country music stars came together on Wednesday, November 18, to honor those nominated at the 2026 CMA Awards.

The 60th annual ceremony was held at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville — with Lainey Wilson returning to host for the third consecutive year.

Ahead of the big night, the Country Music Association declared its nominees, which included Ella Langley leading with nine while Miranda Lambert followed up with seven nominations and Kacey Musgraves and producer Ben West earned five each.

Scroll down to see the complete list of nominees for the 2026 CMA Awards — and look for the bolded names to find out who won, which Us will update live:.
