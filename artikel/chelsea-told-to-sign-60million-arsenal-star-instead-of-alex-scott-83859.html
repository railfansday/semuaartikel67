Chelsea should drop their interest in Bournemouth star Alex Scott and turn their attention to a more experienced alternative, according to Jimmy Floyd Hasselbaink.

The Blues invested heavily in their squad again during the summer transfer window, but remain light in central midfield where they are heavily reliant on Moises Caicedo following the departures of Enzo Fernandez and Andrey Santos.

Romeo Lavia’s return to form and fitness has represented a welcome boost, but Xabi Alonso is keen to reinforce his engine room further come January.

Scott, who Chelsea attempted to sign in the summer, could well be on the move given his delicate contract situation at Bournemouth.

Arsenal and Liverpool have both been linked with the England international and although Hasselbaink is a keen admirer of the 23-year-old, he feels his old club would be better suited by targeting the Gunners’ out of favour Spain international Martin Zubimendi.

The World Cup winner is yet to start a Premier League game this season amid increased competition as a result of Bruno Guimaraes’ arrival from Newcastle.

Atletico Madrid are understood to have registered their interest in the former Real Sociedad star who is determined to stay and fight for his place at Arsenal, but Hasselbaink believes Chelsea should test the waters.

‘Alex Scott is obviously a top player,’ Hasselbaink told Canada Sports Betting.’He is a player that is getting better with every game that he plays, and he fits with the transfer policy and philosophy of Chelsea because he has serious potential to develop into one of the top midfielders in the world.

Sources indicate that ‘I would prefer somebody with a little bit more experience because we have so many young players in the squad. I think what Xabi Alonso needs is somebody with more experience who is also a top international.
