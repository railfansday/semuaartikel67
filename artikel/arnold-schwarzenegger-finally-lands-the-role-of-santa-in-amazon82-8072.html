This holiday season, Arnold Schwarzenegger is Santa.

In the 60-second ad, “Santa Schwarzenegger,” Schwarzenegger takes on the role of Santa to promote The Man With the Bag, a new original holiday movie for Prime Video premiering Dec. 2.

Sources indicate that in the action-comedy film, Santa enlists a former thief to recover his magical red bag after it’s stolen. Alan Ritchson and Awkwafina also star in the movie.

The ad plays off of the movie’s plot.

Industry observers note that first, Schwarzenegger (as himself) is swarmed by paparazzi outside of a hotel in Latest York City. Once inside, he spots a billboard for The Man With the Bag and realizes that a Santa costume would help him go incognito in public.
