The game is finally here, and there’s plenty to discuss.

It has been revealed that it’s hard to believe, but Grand Theft Auto VI is now a real thing that we all can play. Thirteen years after GTA V, and eight years since Red Dead Redemption 2, Rockstar is back with its latest sprawling, extravagant open world. There’s a lot to experience — and a lot to talk about.

It has been revealed that perhaps the most impressive thing about GTA VI is how flexible it is, letting you mold the experience to your own play style. You can still be a maniacal criminal, but you can also focus on the fresh love story that includes an entire button dedicated to holding hands. Or you can spend the hours fishing or driving around listening to the radio. Maybe you just want to doomscroll through a fake version of Instagram. As GTA VI producer Rob Nelson told me just ahead of launch, “It’s a risk. It’s a real change for Grand Theft Auto. You can still play it the old way, you can reject all of this stuff. But for people who do engage, it’s meaningful — you’re earning it, you’re working at it, just like in a normal relationship.”.

Now that the game is out and people are finding their own way through Leonida, it’s a great time to start talking about it. I’ve played through the entire main story — you can read my review right here — but I’m nowhere close to experiencing everything in the game. But I’m happy to answer any questions you have, though I’ll be careful around some of the massive story spoilers. I’ll also be joined by senior reporter Jay Peters, who is still early in the game and can offer a different perspective. There’s a good chance some other Verge staff will stop by as well, since we’re basically all playing it.

Industry observers note that so drop all of your questions, theories, and stray thoughts about GTA VI here. I’ll be checking in throughout the day to join the conversation in the comments.
