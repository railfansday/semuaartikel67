Elite university Harvard maintains significant Bitcoin exposure as it holds over 3 million shares of a Bitcoin ETF, a recent regulatory filing has revealed.

Harvard University remains highly exposed to Bitcoin (BTC) as the elite institution continues to hold over 3 million shares of a Bitcoin exchange-traded fund (ETF).

As per Bitcoin Magazine’s Nov. 16 report, Harvard Management Tech giant, which manages Harvard University’s endowment, held 3,044,612 shares of BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) as of Sept. 30, 2026.

Industry observers note that the Form 13F filing by Harvard University with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) revealed that its investment in the Bitcoin ETF was worth roughly $144.1 million at the end of the third quarter.

Notably, Harvard did not change its Bitcoin ETF share count during the third quarter. The university held the same number of shares as reported at the end of June.

Harvard first disclosed its investment in BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF during the second quarter of 2025.

In a recent development, as per Harvard's Form 13F filing submitted on Aug. 8, 2025, it held 1,906,000 IBIT shares at the end of the second quarter. The investment was worth roughly $116.7 million as of June 30.

The university subsequently increased its Bitcoin exposure significantly.

For instance, at the end of September 2025, Harvard held 6,813,612 IBIT shares, worth roughly $442.9 million. The Bitcoin ETF was the biggest investment that Harvard publicly reported at the time.

Later, however, the university started trimming its Bitcoin exposure. For example, during the fourth quarter of 2025, Harvard reduced its IBIT holdings by about 21.4%. Its total share holdings fell to 5.35 million.

In a recent development, during the first quarter of 2026, Harvard again reduced its Bitcoin exposure by 43.1%. This brought down the number of IBIT shares to roughly 3.04 million.

Industry observers note that since then, the university has maintained this number of shares. Harvard's quarter-end share count has remained unchanged in recent quarters.

Harvard University is not the only top-tier American university that has gained exposure to the biggest cryptocurrency by market capitalization.

According to recent reports, in October 2024, Emory University became the first U.S. university endowment to publicly disclose its holdings in a Bitcoin ETF.

It held roughly 2.7 million shares of Grayscale’s Bitcoin Mini Trust, worth over $15 million at the time.

Meanwhile, in May 2025, Brown University disclosed its holdings of 105,000 shares of BlackRock's IBIT, worth roughly $4.9 million as of March 31 of that year.

Later, Brown doubled its stake, bringing its holdings to 212,500 shares.

Meanwhile, Dartmouth College has also joined the list of universities with exposure to cryptocurrency ETFs.

In its recent filing, Dartmouth College disclosed that it held 201,531 IBIT shares. The investment was worth roughly $9.5 million as of the end of September 2026.
