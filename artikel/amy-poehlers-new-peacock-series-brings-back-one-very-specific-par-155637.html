Sources indicate that amy Poehler has a new comedy series, and all is right in the world. Trading Indiana for Greece, Poehler stars in Dig as archaeologist Elise DeBellnick, who's spent much of her career as right-hand to Dr. Neville Wycombe (Hugh Laurie), a charming yet arrogant professor who keeps teasing retirement but never actually following through. When she uncovers an artifact at a previous dig site, she decides to finally take credit for herself and not report it to Wycombe. However, she finds out that comes with its own chaos.

In a recent development, A great sitcom is only as good as its ensemble, and the latest Mike Schur series doesn't disappoint. With Poehler as the lead, she surrounds herself with the likes of Antonia Thomas, Fina Strazza, and Geraldine Viswanathan; together, this quartet of women embarks on a journey that "digs" into the past and alters their future forever... with plenty of laughs along the way. Their unforgettable summer also includes Hugh Laurie, Kayvan Novak, and Jason Mantzoukas.

Ahead of Dig's release, Collider sat down with Antonia Thomas, Fina Strazza, and Geraldine Viswanathan to discuss the latest Peacock series. The three actresses praise the leadership of Poehler as well as the brilliantly written scripts. Plus, they speculate what a second season could look like, from next season's location to a new character vibe.

In a recent development, COLLIDER: Congratulations on Dig. The three of you and Amy have such great onscreen chemistry. How did you bond together in order to translate that chemistry onscreen?

GERALDINE VISWANATHAN: Thank God it was just there.

VISWANATHAN: We just dove right in. My first week of shooting was all my scenes with Amy in the pit when we got stuck, and we had to get out. We just had the best time in the pit. [Laughs] We were just having so much fun, and I couldn't believe that I was wrestling around with Amy Poehler in a hole in the ground. [Laughs] It was definitely a pinch me moment. But it was just so easy. I think this script is so brilliantly written, and it's so sharp. Those characters and dynamics are so clear. That's Mike Schur's specialty, I think, and then with these brilliant actors and women, it all just fell into place.

What did it mean for each of you to have Amy as a leader, not just as a co-star but also as one of the co-creators?

In a recent development, THOMAS: We all grew up watching Parks and Recreation, and she is an absolute queen, and she is a complete inspiration. I listen to "Good Hang" as well. She's just such an incredibly kind, brilliant, wonderful person. First of all, having her as a creator, her mind is so brilliant; so you're just like "brilliant." But then actually getting to share a screen with her too, [it was] just sort of like a dream come true. It meant an incredible amount and feels like a pinch me moment, right? Just amazing.

Seeing you in the pit also was like, "Close enough, welcome back to 'Parks and Rec.'".
