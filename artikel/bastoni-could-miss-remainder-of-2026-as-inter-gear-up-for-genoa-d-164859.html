Inter centre-back Alessandro Bastoni has suffered a left quadriceps strain, with reports indicating he will miss Saturday's meeting with Genoa and may not return until 2027.

The Nerazzurri confirmed on Friday that Bastoni had undergone tests in the wake of Wednesday's 2-0 Champions League victory over Stuttgart, in which Bastoni only lasted 41 minutes before being replaced by Federico Dimarco.

Sources indicate that bastoni has made 15 appearances for Inter across all competitions, with all 15 of those being starts. The only outfielder to better his 1,199 minutes for Cristian Chivu's side in 2026-27 is midfielder Nicolo Barella (1,298).

Bastoni has won 45 of the 79 duels he has contested this term, including 14 of 20 in the air, helping Inter win 29 points from the first 12 games of their Scudetto defence.

A club statement remarked: "Alessandro Bastoni underwent clinical and diagnostic tests this morning at the Humanitas Institute in Rozzano.

"The tests revealed a strain to his left quadriceps.".

Inter did not offer a timeline for Bastoni's recovery, but with widespread reports indicating he could miss at least three weeks, he may not return until the newly introduced year.

Inter have seven games to play before Newly introduced Year's Eve, the last of which is a Supercoppa Italiana final showdown with Lazio on December 22, a little over three weeks away.

Despite Inter's strong points haul, they sit third in Serie A ahead of Genoa's visit to San Siro, behind Lazio and Roma.

"We didn't lose clarity of mind after the game in Bergamo: we played well for 75 minutes, but the final stages don't define the good things we produced in that match," he stated.

"To tell the truth, you have to remain clear-headed and analyse what happened from a technical and mental point of view.".
