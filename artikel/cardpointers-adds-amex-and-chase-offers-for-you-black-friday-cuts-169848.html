On a $250 hotel stay booked directly with IHG, my Chase Sapphire Reserve earns 1,000 points. But some American Express cardmembers can get $50 back on that same bill at participating hotels if they pay with the enrolled card by December 31, plus Membership Rewards points. I’d take the Amex deal. I’d just like to know which Amex to pack, given that my wife and I have about 26 credit cards in a drawer.

CardPointers+ can add the offer to an eligible card and tell you which one to bring. It’s 50% off through Cyber Monday, November 30. A year costs $49.99 (usually $99.99), and lifetime access is $150 (usually $300).

The free version of CardPointers helps you choose a card for groceries or gas. Turn on automatic enrollment in the paid version and the browser extension adds eligible offers when you sign in to American Express, Chase, Bank of America, Citi, Wells Fargo or U.S. Bank. You can search “IHG” before a trip, or the extension can flag a discount while you’re on a store’s website.

In a recent development, on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, you can schedule an overnight check for newly introduced offers. If you save your bank logins, your device stores them in its Keychain and signs in directly with the bank. U.S. Bank generally still asks for a verification code each time.

Since August, adding an Amex Offer to one card generally makes it disappear from the others under the same login. CardPointers can pick the card with the highest category bonus (say, Gold for a restaurant offer). You can also give first dibs to a latest card where you’re working on an initial bonus.

If I already know to use that Amex at IHG, the app saves me a few clicks. If I’d otherwise pay with my Chase card on a stay I was booking anyway, I’d count the $50 less the value of any extra points I’d earn on Chase. At these prices, lifetime access starts saving money in year four.
