Struggling Blackpool sack Evatt on his birthday

Ian Evatt won 23 of his 57 games in charge of Blackpool.

Blackpool have sacked head coach Ian Evatt on his 45th birthday after a year in charge following a run of one win and six defeats in their past 10 league games.

Evatt's final match was Saturday's 1-0 away defeat by fellow strugglers AFC Wimbledon, leaving the Tangerines immediately above the League One relegation zone.

That defeat, however, came after the he said his squad had been decimated by a sickness bug during their trip to the capital.

Evatt departs the Seasiders with the announcement made a matter of hours after the club wished him a happy birthday, external on social media.

In a statement, the club thanked Evatt for his efforts and said Steve Thompson will take charge for Saturday's game against Wigan Athletic.

Blackpool announced Ian Evatt's dismissal less than seven hours after wishing him a happy birthday.

Blackpool appointed Evatt, who won promotion from League One and the Championship during seven years as a centre-back for the club, as Steve Bruce's successor in October 2025.

During a six-year playing career at Bloomfield Road, Evatt made more than 250 appearances for the Seasiders.

On arrival as a manager, a tally of 13 points from a possible 15 in December 2025 lifted them out of the relegation zone, although they lost 10 and won two of their next 15 matches in all competitions.

They finished the campaign strongly, with six wins in seven games as part of a sequence of one defeat in 13 between mid-March and the end of August, only to struggle again as this season continued.

Speaking after their defeat by Mansfield in October, Evatt noted he had "a huge amount of confidence" in his ability because of his record.

Evatt guided Barrow to the 2019-20 National League title in his first managerial role, then won promotion to League One with Bolton Wanderers the following season.

Wanderers went on to lift the 2022-23 EFL Trophy and reached the third-tier play-offs in consecutive seasons under Evatt, losing in the final in his final full campaign in 2023-24.

After an encouraging start to the season, Blackpool's form has been alarming since the beginning of September, and owner-chairman Simon Sadler has decided a change was needed with 33 league matches still to play.

There have been mitigating circumstances but everyone in the hot seat is judged on their points return.

His final game in charge will undoubtedly go down as one of those factors. Illness within the squad made the defeat at Wimbledon somewhat understandable, but the performance should have warranted more.

Throughout Evatt's tenure, he was forced to constantly juggle an injury list that never seemed to ease.

The centre-back position was a recurring problem and is unlikely to be solved overnight. Steve Bruce signings Fraser Horsfall and Michael Ihiekwe have yet to make a league appearance this season, despite being expected to play a significant role after injuries at the back end of the last campaign.

Supporters wanted greater tactical flexibility and a system that would allow mercurial talents such as Josh Bowler to have a greater impact.

However, Evatt largely stuck with a wing-back formation, believing he did not have the personnel to play effectively in other ways.

He made an immediate impact after taking charge, but that was followed by a significant downturn in form before a recovery that secured League One survival last season.

This term, there had been hope that those struggles were behind Blackpool.

Evatt will retain legendary status for his achievements as a player, having won two promotions and played in the Premier League.

However, Blackpool are now searching for their eighth permanent manager or head coach since Sadler took over the club in the summer of 2019.
