Sources indicate that after a phenomenal first season that broke viewership records, Apple TV's best sci-fi series will return for another run, but sadly, not this year. The acclaimed drama premiered on November 7, 2025, with a perfect 100% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, while setting a new Apple TV record for a drama series premiere. Before Season 1 ended, it had become the platform's most-watched series in history, surpassing both Severance and Ted Lasso. It also received 18 Emmy Award nominations and took home six, including Outstanding Lead Actress and Outstanding Writing for a Drama Series.

According to recent reports, now sitting at 99% on Rotten Tomatoes, Pluribus remains one of Apple TV's standout sci-fi achievements. Set in post-apocalyptic Albuquerque, New Mexico, the nine-episode first season follows the most miserable person on Earth, who must save the world from happiness. Vince Gilligan created the sci-fi masterpiece after finding success with Breaking Bad, its spin-off Better Call Saul, and The X-Files.

It has been revealed that rhea Seehorn leads Pluribus as Carol Sturka, a romantasy author and one of only 13 people in the world immune to the hive-mind virus that kills a large fraction of the human population and infects the rest with artificial happiness. Season 2 will continue the brilliant plot, now closer to our screens as filming is underway; however, no premiere date has been unveiled. In the meantime, the series has made a major change ahead of its return, promoting an crucial recurring character, as Deadline reports.

Samba Schutte has officially been promoted to series regular after masterfully portraying Koumba Diabaté in Pluribus Season 1. Mr Diabaté is one of the immune characters, a Mauritanian who chooses to see the bright side of the situation. Rather than go into panic mode and try to save the world like Carol, he embraces a hedonistic, luxurious lifestyle in the penthouse suite at The Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino. For his performance, Schutte received a Saturn Award nomination for Best Guest Star in a Television Series.

Sources indicate that in addition to Seehorn and Schutte, Pluribus Season 2 will see Karolina Wydra and Carlos-Manuel Vesga reprise their roles as Zosia, Carol's artificially happy chaperone, and Manousos Oviedo, the extremely paranoid Colombian immune, respectively. Further casting details, including new and returning cast members, have not yet been revealed. Sony Pictures Television produces Pluribus for Apple TV, with executive producers Gilligan, Gordon Smith, Alison Tatlock, Diane Mercer, Jeff Frost, Jenn Carroll, and Trina Siopy.

Pluribus streams on Apple TV. Follow Collider for more updates about the sci-fi show's return.
