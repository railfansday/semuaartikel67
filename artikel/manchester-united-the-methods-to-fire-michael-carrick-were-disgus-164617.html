Manchester United: the methods to fire Michael Carrick were disgusting…

It has been revealed that for several weeks already, his fate had been sealed. Michael Carrick was finally dismissed from his position this Thursday, after meeting his now ex-leaders the same morning. Omar Berrada, the general manager, and Jason Wilcox, the football director, informed him of the end of the adventure, three days after the announcement of his imminent departure. It is this method that is being singled out today by the press and observers, more than the dismissal of the Manchester United coach. The situation was becoming inevitable.

According to recent reports, while he knew he was doomed since Monday morning, the day after the draw at Old Trafford against Aston Villa (4-4), the technician continued his mission, biding his time. His representatives had been contacted by management to announce their intentions to separate. Except that in the meantime, Berrada and Wilcox hopped on a plane to Monaco to meet the club’s co-owner, Sir Jim Ratcliffe, and finalize the details together. Only returning to Manchester on Wednesday evening, they then called Carrick to arrange a meeting the following morning.

According to The Telegraphthis way of doing things and this very long wait upset the former midfielder. He did not understand why he had to organize the sessions on Tuesday and Wednesday, without most of the management players, who had left for selection, but with his assistants: Steve Holland, Jonathan Woodgate, Jonny Evans and Travis Binnion, all fired since yesterday. At the same time, MU was working on the arrival of Mauricio Pochettino. In this tense context, made of mutual reproaches and delaying tactics, the information inevitably leaked.

It has been revealed that “Over the past 24 to 48 hours, the situation has been catastrophic. While the manager, a club legend, directs training on the field, the management and owner are in Monaco, and simultaneously contact the American Football Federation to approach Mauricio Pochettino», jumps Gary Neville in the still very popular podcast, Stick to Football by The Overlap. “It’s simply sickening. This is well below the standards expected in any other tech giant, in any other industry. This would be unfair dismissal and you would be prosecuted», Continues the former Mancunian defender.

“Being in Monaco while the team, the staff and the people who manage the club on a daily basis are in Manchester is very frowned upon, and it casts a negative light on the management. I don’t think anyone will come out of this unscathed.» Strong words which echo those made the day before by Peter Schmeichel, another legend of the Red Devils of the 90s. «If they look for someone else, let them relieve him of his duties! Don’t let him come and lead the training. He did it again yesterday (Wednesday). How disrespectful is that?», asked the former goalkeeper.

According to recent reports, faced with Carrick’s anger, management tried to defend itself, again according to The Telegraph. She understands the frustration of the 45-year-old technician and regrets that their separation happened like this. She also denies having carried out all the checks (notably legal) prior to and necessary for dismissal, before communicating her decision to the person concerned. The club believes it has demonstrated all possible transparency. These explanations will not convince many people as Manchester United is used to these procedures. The dismissal of Amorim, for example, is less than a year old….
