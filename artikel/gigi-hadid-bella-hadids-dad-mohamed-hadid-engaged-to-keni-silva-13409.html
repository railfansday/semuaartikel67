Gigi Hadid and Bella Hadid have reason to celebrate with their family.

After all, their dad Mohamed Hadid announced his engagement to Keni Silva, his girlfriend of three years, during a special outing at the inaugural Vogue Arabia Ball.

At the Nov. 13 event in Dubai, Mohamed, 78, looked the part of the groom in a crisp black suit, while Keni was a dream in an all-white Krikor Jabotian look, complete with a sculptural corset piece and intricately embroidered sheer gown.

Plus, the 43-year-old wore the diamond engagement ring that Mohamed gave her when he popped the question at the Polo Lounge in Beverly Hills.

In a recent development, “It was a beautiful, intimate evening, exactly the kind of moment I cherish,” Keni told Vogue Arabia at the event. “I’ve never been someone who needs a grand production or extravagant gestures to feel loved. For me, it’s always been about the sincerity behind them.”.

She added, “Sometimes the most beautiful moments in life are the ones you didn’t even know you were wishing for.”.

For his part, Mohamed—dad to Gigi, 31, Bella, 30, and son Anwar, 27, with ex-wife Yolanda Hadid, as well as three other daughters from previous relationships—reflected on his favorite qualities of his "incredibly caring, loving, and loyal" fiancée.

"What I love about Keni is her simplicity, her love for life, and her love for her children," he mentioned. "I loved her almost instantly once I understood her heart—how beautiful she is not only on the outside, but in her heart and in her soul.”.

Likewise, Keni couldn’t help but rave over her husband-to-be. "What I love most about him, beyond his beautiful heart, extraordinary passion, generosity,” she said, “and the dreams he continues to pursue, is that he always makes me laugh.".

Sources indicate that but for the author—who published her book Divorce As an Opportunity in January 2025 after her 2022 divorce—her relationship isn’t just about the joy she finds with Mohamed. In fact, their romance also serves as an example that “love doesn’t have an expiration date.”.

“You can start over, you can find love again, and yes, you can still have your fairy tale and your happily ever after,” she shared, before noting that their engagement is “also about reminding women that their story doesn’t end with divorce.”.

As for when Keni and Mohamed are planning to walk down the aisle? They’re not rushing their nuptials.

In a recent development, “We’re allowing ourselves to enjoy this beautiful moment,” she said. “There’s something wonderful about not rushing into the next thing and simply appreciating where life has brought us.”.
