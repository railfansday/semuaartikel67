Recent findings suggest that back in June, Apple hiked prices for all of its entire line-up of MacBooks and iPads, with the MacBook Air rising by $200. Thanks to Amazon, you can pick up the 2026 MacBook Air with an M5 chip with that $200 price hike removed (and an extra $50 discount added), bringing the price down to just $1,049 for its 16GB RAM and 512GB SSD model. It's also dropped the 16GB / 1TB model to $1,299, with the 24GB / 1TB model costing $1,549.

Industry observers note that few can rival the MacBook for build quality and design: that's why they're so popular. The Air is its lightest laptop design, with this base-edition 13.6-inch model shipping with 16GB of memory and a 512GB SSD for storage. This latest M5 chip from Apple uses a 10-core CPU and 10-core GPU, giving you incremental gains in performance and speed compared to the last-gen M4.

Recent findings suggest that these ultra-light and slim-line laptops get the N1 wireless chip for Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 connectivity, along with a 13.6-inch Liquid Retina display, giving you stunning, clear images. You also have a 12MP Center Stage camera for video calls, along with Touch ID for biometric security.

Recent findings suggest that other specs are available, including the 16GB/1TB model that we mentioned for $1,299 and the 24GB/1TB model for $1,549. They're identical in design to the $1,049 model, with the same price available for the Sky Blue, Starlight (yellow), classic Silver, and Sky Blue colorways, all featuring the same M5 chip. You're just paying more for extra storage or memory, depending on whether you need it or not.

This 13.6-Inch MacBook Air is complete with Apple's Liquid Retina display, 16GB of unified memory, a 512GB SSD, 12MP center stage camera, touch ID, and Wi-Fi 7 connectivity.

Recent findings suggest that this model is the same shape and design as the one above, but you get a boost in storage. This MacBook Air comes with a 1TB SSD instead.

Amazon does like to tempt buyers with small discounts on Apple products from time to time, but the up to $300 discounts on the latest MacBook Air models are a good sign. Apple only raised its prices back in June, with Amazon's pricing following shortly afterward. You can't get them at these prices from Apple directly, either.

Whether you go for the $1,099 16GB/512GB MacBook Air, the $1,299 16GB/1TB upgrade, or the higher-spec 24GB/1TB edition for $1,599, these are great deals for a new Apple laptop in the current market. Don't expect them to be around for long, though.
