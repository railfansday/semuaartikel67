📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that there comes a time when every bleached blonde must ask themselves: is it really worth the damage, the time, the cash? Jessie Buckley has the answer.

Industry observers note that often, as a blonde, you’re not having so much more fun that the upkeep is justified. If this is you, why not take a page from Buckley’s book ahead of party season, and go back to brunette?

In a recent development, for her hair color homecoming—unveiled at the Ivors Composer Awards in London on Tuesday night—the Irish actor (who was blonde when she collected her Best Actress Oscar in scarlet and pink Chanel earlier this year) opted for a milk-chocolate shade, flush with notes of caramel and cinnamon. Her chin-length bob was styled sleek and straight with a deep side part by Fabio Petri—long a go-to for Buckley, as well as other bob-lovers like Gracie Abrams and Saoirse Ronan. Makeup, gleaming with a peachy blush and cool-toned taupe lip, was by Naoko Sofia Patrizia Scintu.

A variation on the mocha hair trend, Buckley’s new color is the latest in an ongoing question dividing celebs: to join team glossy mocha or winter blonde? Happily, as Jessie Buckley proves, it seems there’s no wrong choice.

At the Academy Awards in March.

Buckley at the Ivors Composer Awards this week.

According to recent reports, lily James’s luxe brunette is every bit as fab as Nicola Coughlan’s cowboy beige refresh or Rachel Sennott’s apricot blonde. It seems it boils down to maintenance—with blondes requiring more salon time—and personal preference. As a self-professed hair colour chameleon, (“My hair’s my way of hiding,” she told Vogue earlier this year about her frequent hair swaps), Buckley can certainly attest.
