It has been revealed that finding an insulated water bottle that fits in a standard cup holder without leaking all over your bag is harder than it sounds, which is why Owala's FreeSip lineup has taken over everywhere from gym bags to office desks. Amazon's Owala Black Friday deals just knocked up to 24% off the brand's most popular bottles and tumblers, giving you an easy excuse to upgrade your daily hydration or check off a few holiday gifts.

The sale covers everything from the 16-ounce kids bottle and the flagship 24-ounce FreeSip up to the 30-ounce Sway and massive 40-ounce Tumbler — plus the SmoothSip Slider coffee tumbler for daily caffeine runs.

Here are some of the standouts from the drop:.

It has been revealed that the Owala FreeSip Stainless Steel Water Bottle (24-Ounce) has earned its viral hype thanks to a smart two-in-one spout that lets you sip upright through a built-in straw or tilt the bottle back to take a big gulp. In Mashable's hands-on testing, Samantha Mangino called it "the water bottle to end all water bottles," noting how the flip-top lid keeps the mouthpiece clean while the carry loop locks it shut so it never leaks in your bag.

Triple-wall insulation keeps ice water cold for up to 24 hours — Mangino even found full ice cubes still clinking around a day later — and the slim base fits easily into standard cup holders and comes apart cleanly for washing.

The Owala SmoothSip Slider Insulated Coffee Tumbler (20-Ounce) is built to keep hot drinks steaming for up to 6 hours and iced coffees chilled for up to 24 hours without spilling in your bag. Its smooth slider spout delivers a comfortable sip while sealing shut to prevent splashes and leaks on bumpy morning commutes. It is also BPA- and phthalate-free and features a dishwasher-safe lid for quick cleanup.

Sources indicate that the Owala FreeSip Sway Stainless Steel Water Bottle (30-Ounce) gives you more capacity without forcing you to give up your car's cup holder, thanks to a tapered base that fits in most standard cup holders. Like the classic FreeSip, its push-button lid flips open to reveal a two-in-one spout for sipping upright through a built-in straw or tilting back for a bigger gulp, while a sliding lock keeps it 100 percent leakproof in your bag.

It also swaps the standard carry loop for a comfortable top bucket handle, keeps cold drinks chilled for up to 24 hours with triple-wall insulation, and features a wide mouth for easy ice loading and cleaning.

Retire that dented, sticker-covered bottle sitting on your desk and grab a fresh Owala while the Black Friday markdowns are still flowing.
