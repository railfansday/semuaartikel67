Turkey’s top diplomat has spoken with his Georgian counterpart after incidents at Besiktas’s Europa League match against Israeli club Hapoel Be’er-Sheva in Batumi, a foreign ministry source confirmed.

Turkey’s Hakan Fidan “has spoken by phone” with his Georgian counterpart, Maka Botchorishvili, the source stated, adding: “Officials from the Turkish embassy in Tbilisi and the consulate general in Batumi are at the stadium.
