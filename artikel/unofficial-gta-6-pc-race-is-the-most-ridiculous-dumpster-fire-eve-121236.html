As vibe coders desperately hurl commands at AI in an attempt to get GTA 6 running on PC, one former Destiny 2 dev claims it's led to the "most ridiculous dumpster fire ever" – one that, like a car crash, is hard to look away from.

Since notorious GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek dropped an Xbox build of the game last week – and more recently followed up with its executable file – multiple groups have been racing to get Rockstar's latest blockbuster up and running on PC. After all, it only rolled out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so there's a gap there for people without a console that folks have been desperate to fill – though I can't help but think that some hoping for their success might just be keen for a way to play GTA 6 for free.

In a recent development, anyway, it was always inevitable that people would try this, but the way they've been going about it has been messy, to say the least. We've already observed vibe coders begging their AI models of choice to do the GTA 6 port for them, "make no mistakes," and do it "faster," and former gameplay capture artist at Bungie, Amy Hendricks, has also been watching on in dumbfounded awe, documenting what she calls "the most ridiculous dumpster fire ever" in a thread on Bluesky.

She first looks at "the slopfest" she calls Project Leonida – one of the frontrunning decompilation projects that yesterday saw its Discord server completely disappear after Rockstar apparently got in touch, and its 15-year-old lead quit (though they've since told followers to "ignore" those posts, so who knows what's going on there). Before that, Hendricks documented: "One group feels like the chatbot developers are not moving fast enough (One reason being the main dev is still at school), so they're going to split the main GTA 6 recomp team into two via forking.".

It has been revealed that not long after that, fears began that Rockstar and Take-Two were on the way. A screenshot Hendricks shares from the Discord server before its apparent takedown reveals customers copy and pasting the same message they seemingly hoped would clear their name should any official investigation come about.

"In case of an investigation by any federal entity or similar, I do not have any involvement with this group or with the people in it, I do not know how I am here, probably added by a thrid party, I do not support any actions by the member of this group," they declare in unison. Yes, they all copied the same typos, too.

Despite all this, though, the vibe coders pressed on. An amusing highlight reveals one asking the AI why its task was taking so long, to be patiently told by the robot, "Each rebuild takes a long time, and the hang fix changes the shaders too, so it would need another rebuild.".

Hendricks speculates that all these attempts are costing those involved some serious cash, too: "Fun fact: This is most likely using API Billing (Subs give you a small quota, API billing lets you go as far as your wallet allows), so everyone involved has most likely spent hundreds if not thousands of dollars already.".

Recent findings suggest that A follow-up post further explains that one of the people streaming their vibe coding efforts was using Fable 5.2, and "API pricing is built around the price of 1 Million tokens. Fable charges $8 for input (what it does) and $40 output (what it puts out) per 1M. If he's been doing this day and night for hours, easily thousands of dollars.".

It has been revealed that oh dear. Needless to say, it's not looking like a smooth operation. As Hendricks puts it, "I have always wanted to see the supposed 'expert programmers with AI making recomps' in action and it turns out it's just a bunch of kids hitting their head on a wall so im gonna keep watching out of morbid [curiosity].".

According to recent reports, despite it all, it does seem like progress is being made, though. One website – which Hendricks refers to as a "slop leaderboard" – is keeping track of the progress made across various unofficial GTA 6 PC projects, with the top one at the time of writing apparently 77.4% playable. I'm sure both prospective players and Rockstar alike will be watching this space with bated breath.
