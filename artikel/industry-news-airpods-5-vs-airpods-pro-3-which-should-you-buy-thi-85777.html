The holiday shopping season is here, and Apple’s AirPods are sure to be one of the most popular gifts. If you’re shopping for AirPods, Apple sells three different models: AirPods 5, AirPods Pro 3, and AirPods Max 2. Here’s how they compare.

Recent findings suggest that airPods 5 were introduced in September and come in two different versions. Unlike their predecessors, both versions of AirPods 5 offer Active Noise Cancellation. They differ in the charging case, battery life, and volume control. The base model AirPods 5 ($129) come with a case that can only charge via USB-C.

Sources indicate that the case for the higher-end AirPods 5 ($149) supports wireless charging and Find My. You also get one extra hour of battery life. The higher-end model also adds on-stem volume control, so you can adjust the volume just by swiping up or down on one of the earbud stems.

Sources indicate that the key thing about AirPods 5 is their open-ear design, unlike the in-ear design of the AirPods Pro 3 (more on those later). Even with that open-ear design, AirPods 5 offer incredible Active Noise Cancellation capabilities.

If you are shopping for AirPods 5, I do recommend spending the extra $20 to get the higher-end model. The better charging case alone is worth the extra money. With Find My integration and a built-in speaker, you can track your AirPods and play a sound from the case if you lose them.

If the person you’re shopping for doesn’t like in-ear earbuds, AirPods 5 are an excellent choice.

For most people, though, AirPods Pro 3 are probably the better choice ….

AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s most capable earbuds, even with the introduction of AirPods 5 this year. They offer an in-ear fit and come with five foam-infused silicone ear tip sizes. With that in-ear design, AirPods Pro 3 can offer up to 3x stronger noise cancellation than AirPods 5. In my testing, this difference is notable.

AirPods Pro 3 also offer several other features you won’t find on AirPods 5, including a heart rate sensor for workouts, plus Hearing Test, Hearing Aid, and Hearing Protection features.

Recent findings suggest that I still think AirPods Pro 3 are one of the best products in Apple’s lineup today. I wear mine for multiple hours every single day and can’t imagine life without them. I love the in-ear fit, and the ANC still impresses me, whether I’m at home or somewhere like a coffee shop.

Industry observers note that if you know the person you’re shopping for (whether it’s yourself or someone else) likes the in-ear earbud design, I think AirPods Pro 3 are the best option. This is especially true when AirPods Pro 3 are on sale, like they are right now at $179. The price difference between AirPods 5 and AirPods Pro 3 is very small, and you’re getting a much better product.

Finally, we have AirPods Max. These are Apple’s only option for people who want AirPods in an over-ear headphone form factor. Last year, they were pretty hard to recommend since they’d gone five years without a real upgrade.

In a recent development, this year, however, Apple officially introduced AirPods Max 2, bringing modern features to Apple’s high-end headphones. This includes features like Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, Personalized Volume, and more.

According to recent reports, airPods Max 2 are a far more compelling option than AirPods Max were at this time last year. They’re still pricey, but I love the aesthetics and find them very comfortable. There’s something about the premium aluminum design with an elegant Digital Crown that really stands out.

If you’re looking to splurge this holiday season, AirPods Max 2 are a great choice. For more realistic budgets, I think AirPods Pro 3 are the best option.
