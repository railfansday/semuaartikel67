Wayne Rooney has revealed there was a period in his Manchester United career where he contemplated suicide.

The former England striker mentioned that, even as he flourished on the pitch, his mental health was suffering off it, despite being newly married and a dad to his eldest child, Kai.

He would spend two days at a time drinking at home and, he told the ‘Life At The Midpoint with Gabby Logan’ podcast, at his lowest point considered taking his own life.

Recent findings suggest that “I can look back and say I was selfish, but I knew I was going through something which I was really struggling to deal with. Then I didn’t want Kai seeing me like that, so that’s why I’d hide away as well,” Rooney said.

“I got to a place where you’re contemplating suicide. You just didn’t want to be around anyone. I didn’t want to be see people in the streets. I didn’t want to go into shops.

“I was always comfortable on a football pitch, but everything else [I] really struggled with it.”.

Rooney, United’s all-time record goalscorer, remarked the challenges he faced occurred at the height of his Red Devils career.

“You don’t want to show any weakness, so you try to mask it,” he added.

“Even though I knew the position I was in, it was difficult to actually admit that I was in that in that position because I felt like you feel less of a man if you like for doing that.

“I was speaking to a therapist to try and figure out what was up with me because when I look back I was probably at the height of my career, I’ve just had a young son, I’ve just been married. It should be the happiest point in my life and it wasn’t.

“It was probably the lowest point because of the selfish acts I was taking.”.

Rooney, who is right now featuring in ITV reality programme ‘I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here’, credits his wife Coleen with helping him turn the tide.

According to recent reports, “Coleen helped me massively. She could have walked away, it was that bad, but she stuck by me and helped me get through it,” he stated.
