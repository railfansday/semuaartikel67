📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that there’s nothing like finding a perfect color match. Yesterday, Dua Lipa shared one of her famous photo dumps—and proved that her wardrobe is full of cobalt-blue statements.

Leading the Instagram post with a selfie in a sporty, high-low look, the singer was seen lounging in a black sports bra and leggings. Over the activewear, she layered up with a cozy North Face fleece puffer, pulled from the brand’s collaboration with New York City-based label Aimé Leon Dore. The jacket was crafted from a wool-blend fabric that had been dyed in a bold blue shade, putting a fashion spin on an outdoor classic.

It has been revealed that lipa paired the outerwear with a matching Chanel flap bag, reimagined for the Chanel Coco Neige 2026 season in quilted nylon. The slouchy, capacious accessory featured a chain strap and silver hardware detailing.

Recent findings suggest that also known as Yves Klein blue, named for the French artist who developed the intense shade in the 1950s, cobalt has seen a resurgence over the past few seasons—and became somewhat of a hero color during the Spring/Summer 2027 reveals. Whether drenching an entire look or adding a hint of color to an otherwise neutral ensemble, the vibrant shade appeared time and time again on the season’s runways, seen on everything from Givenchy peplum blazers to Ralph Lauren two-piece sets to Loewe tights.

In a recent development, lipa was, naturally, an early adopter of the trend. Back in March, she attended the Elton John AIDS Foundation Academy Awards viewing party in a shimmering fringe gown in the shade, designed by Demna for Gucci.
