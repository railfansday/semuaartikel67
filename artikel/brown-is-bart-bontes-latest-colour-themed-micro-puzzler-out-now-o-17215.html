📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

With so many big, flashy mobile games currently available to play, it can all seem a bit overwhelming. Fortunately, there's still room for fun, experimental stuff to flourish on mobile, as we've seen with solo developer Bart Bonte. Now, the latest in Bonte's series of colour-themed micro-puzzlers has just been released, with the launch of Brown for iOS and Android.

It has been revealed that if you're not familiar with Bart Bonte's previous entries such as Orange and Purple, then you'll be glad to know things are pretty straightforward. Because, rather than a core mechanic, each of the 50 levels in Bart Bonte's Brown is based around self-contained logic and puzzles, which you'll need to figure out to solve.

Bart Bonte is, much like Tepes Ovidiu, one of those developers we follow closely specifically because their smaller-scale releases present a breath of fresh air. We all like flashy, Unreal Engine-fuelled ARPGs, shooters or racers, but simple, straightforward puzzlers are arguably what mobile was built on.

Even better, Brown is also built on the same free-to-try model, meaning you can give it a go for yourself and find out whether it's for you before choosing whether to pay the premium entry. All in all, a good way to pass the hours on a long flight or journey.
