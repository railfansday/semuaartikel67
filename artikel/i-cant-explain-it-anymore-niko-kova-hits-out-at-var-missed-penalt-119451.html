Shortly before Maximilian Beier’s equaliser in Borussia Dortmund’s 1-1 draw against Arsenal in the UEFA Champions League on Tuesday night, the Bundesliga leaders believe they should have been awarded a penalty when Viktor Gyökeres handled the ball with an outstretched arm in the area.

Referee Maurizio Mariani, however, waved play on and his decision wasn’t overruled by the VAR.

It has been revealed that “I can’t explain it anymore,” said Niko Kovač. “I don’t know what constitutes as handball and what doesn’t.” BVB captain Nico Schlotterbeck also weighed in on the decision, adding: “I have a feeling we should have had a handball penalty following a corner.”.

Patrick Ittrich, who refereed in the Bundesliga between 2016 and 2026, also believes Dortmund should have been awarded a penalty. “For me, it was a clear penalty,” he told Prime Video.

Industry observers note that “I think the only argument is that one could think the player slightly pulled his arm back, but he only pulled it back when the ball was already on his hand. He actually wanted to head it, realised: ‘I can’t reach it,’ and then raised his elbow and clearly played the ball with his hand.”.

Recent findings suggest that “That’s an extended body surface, there’s a hint of intent involved. For me that’s a clear penalty.”.
