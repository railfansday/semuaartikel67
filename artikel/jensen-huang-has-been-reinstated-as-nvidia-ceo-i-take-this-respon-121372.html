Nvidia has reinstated its disgraced former CEO and leather jacket collector Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and director of its board, according to a press release today.

Recent findings suggest that huang, who co-founded Nvidia back in 1993, was forced to resign from his positions at the company earlier this month, after it came to light he had an affair with a Chinese businesswoman named Alice Huang, a former executive at Singaporean tech firm Megaspeed International who was suspected by US intelligence of being an asset of the Chinese Ministry of State Security. Huang also reportedly carried out two additional affairs with Nvidia employees.

Industry observers note that he was replaced by Nvidia CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. The Nvidia board initially responded to pressure from tech industry leaders to bring Huang back with a statement that he would not be reinstated. He has now been reinstated.

"Nvidia today revealed that effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board", confirmed the corporation in a press release. "The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead Nvidia.

"In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk". Huang has a close relationship with US president Donald Trump, and Trump has backed calls for Huang's reinstatement.

Markets were unhappy with Huang's dismissal earlier this month; when it was unveiled, Nvidia shed billions of dollars in market value as traders adjusted to the prospect of a future without his leadership. The announcement of his reinstatement has sent Nvidia share prices skyrocketing by over 30%.

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution", remarked Huang. Meanwhile, Nvidia has formed "a committee comprised solely of independent directors to investigate" Huang's affair with a possible Chinese intelligence asset and notes that "investigation will proceed to conclusion.".
