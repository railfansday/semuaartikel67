Breaking: Tokenized Equities Hit Another Record High of $3.8 Billion. Is This the Biggest Bull Market in Crypto Right Now?

Recent findings suggest that the momentum behind tokenized equities continues to grow. The total circulating supply recently hit another record high of $3.8 billion. For the year, growth in the tokenized equities market is a resounding 590%.

Industry observers note that and there's even better news to come. In the U.S., thanks to recent regulatory approval, retail investors will soon have access to an expanding array of platforms and exchanges for trading these tokenized equities 24/7. So where can they get started?

Recent findings suggest that tokenized equities are a form of real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. In short, they are digital tokens that trade on a blockchain. As a result, the traditional starting point for many investors has always been the Layer 1 blockchain networks that are powering RWA tokenization.

In a recent development, that typically means either Ethereum or Solana (SOL -0.91%). My personal pick here is Solana, which could see rapid increases in its tokenized stock trading activity after the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) stated it will connect its tokenization service to Solana in the second half of 2027. One sleeper here could be Avalanche (AVAX -2.19%), whose technology NYSE looks to use for a custom chain behind its tokenized-securities venue, pending regulatory approval.

Going one step further, investors could target Layer 2 blockchain networks that sit atop these foundational Layer 1 networks. A popular pick here is Arbitrum (ARB -2.37%), which Robinhood Markets (HOOD -7.65%) already embraced for its brand-upcoming Robinhood Chain.

Investors could also choose to invest in platforms and exchanges offering tokenized stock trading. That's where things get tricky, since the SEC only granted a temporary, conditional exemption for tokenized stock trading on Sept. 17. The lion's share of trading still happens overseas.

But that's about to change. Robinhood, for example, already offers tokenized stock trading to its European customers, and could offer it to its U.S. customers. And the same is true for other trading platforms, such as eToro, Kraken, and Coinbase Global.

It has been revealed that the major name to watch right now is OKXICE, a joint venture between the crypto exchange OKX and the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE -0.53%). Starting on Nov. 23, tokenized stock trading will become available in more than 70 different stocks and ETFs.

Industry observers note that finally, investors could go with fintech companies helping to make RWA tokenization a reality. One target here is Securitize (SECZ -8.35%), which has become a favorite of tech investor Cathie Wood. Her firm, ARK Invest, recently made headlines by partnering with Securitize to tokenize an ARK venture fund that holds stakes in both OpenAI and Anthropic.

Industry observers note that there are also crypto tokens that provide exposure to fintech companies active in tokenized equities. One option here is Ondo (ONDO -0.95%), which is the token of a New York City-based fintech organization that specializes in tokenizing real-world assets.

It's vital to point out that even though the tokenized equity market is growing by leaps and bounds, total trading volume is heavily concentrated on just the biggest names in tech, including Nvidia (NVDA -4.50%), Tesla (TSLA -3.43%), and Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +0.32%).

It has been revealed that one way to see which names are gaining traction is to check the leaderboard on RWA.xyz, which provides real-time data on tokenized assets. Right now, the most popular tokenized stocks are Securitize, SpaceX, Nvidia, Strategy, and Circle Internet Group.

So one way to gain exposure to tokenized stocks is simply to buy tech stocks with a built-in international audience.

Obviously, there are many options for crypto investors seeking exposure to tokenized equities. The market is exploding in popularity, to the point where one popular investment letter (The Kobeissi Letter) recently proclaimed it "the biggest bull market in crypto.".

Industry observers note that just keep in mind, though, that tokenized stock trading is not without its controversy. Notably, some of the biggest tech companies -- including Nvidia,  Microsoft and Alphabet -- have objected to having tokenized versions of their shares trade on OKXICE. As of Nov. 5, 67 companies had opted out. And, even though the new OKXICE trading platform will offer at least 70 different digital assets, 59 of them are thematic ETFs from the same company (Direxion).

That being said, the shift to 24/7 trading looks like a no-brainer. According to some estimates, between 60% and 70% of tokenized stock trading occurs outside U.S. trading hours. So there's clearly pent-up demand for this product from investors.

According to recent reports, it remains to be seen, however, who the biggest winners will be. Will it be the blockchain networks? The platforms and exchanges? Or the fintech companies helping to tokenize traditional stocks?
