In a recent development, TORONTO, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MDA Space Ltd. (TSX: MDA) (NYSE: MDA) (the "Firm"), a trusted mission partner to the rapidly expanding global defence and space industry, today announced that it has completed its previously announced acquisition of Blue Canyon Technologies (BCT) LLC, a U.S.-based spacecraft and satellite component manufacturer and mission services provider, formerly part of RTX.

It has been revealed that BCT brings 400 highly skilled employees in the Denver, Colorado space and aerospace hub, and more than 95 spacecraft rolled out and 4,300 products on orbit. Its addition to MDA Space provides the company with a strategic business and manufacturing footprint to capitalize on growing demand in the U.S. government market for defence space missions. With the close of the acquisition, BCT will be rebranded as MDA Space and operate as MDA Space USA.

In a recent development, "I want to welcome the Blue Canyon Technologies team to MDA Space. The addition of BCT to MDA Space adds a strong and established business and team with domestic manufacturing capability that is ideally positioned to accelerate our growth strategy in the expanding U.S. defence market," said Mike Greenley, CEO of MDA Space. "Securing these strategic benefits on an accretive basis with a profitable and cash-generating business makes this an ideal fit for MDA Space global expansion and continued shareholder value creation.".
