📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Strategy reportedly sold 32 BTC in June, followed by three larger sales in July and August.

Earlier sales coincided with its cost basis falling below the $75,400 average cost level.

Its latest reported purchase was 485 BTC on November 16, as the average cost line came under pressure again.

Sources indicate that strategy could be approaching another shift in its Bitcoin strategy. Historical data from BitcoinTreasuries shows several sell-offs when its average cost line was breached. Now, its raising questions about whether the latest dip could interrupt its buying streak.

It has been revealed that the manufacturer’s last reported purchase was 485 BTC on November 16. But with the average cost level back in focus during November, investors have a reason to watch its next move.

The historical figures show four reported sales: 32 BTC on June 1, 3,588 BTC on July 6, 1,637 BTC on August 3, and another 1,691 BTC on August 10.

According to the pattern identified in the BitcoinTreasuries data, these sales occurred when Strategy’s average cost line fell below its average cost of $75,400 marked green.

In a recent development, that’s a notable sequence. It suggests the average cost line may have coincided with previous changes in the company’s buying and selling activity.

It has been revealed that purchases reportedly resumed in September after the average cost level was regained. That buying continued into November, with the latest recorded purchase standing at 485 BTC on November 16.

Now the average cost line has reportedly been breached again. That puts the latest purchase under scrutiny. If the earlier pattern repeats, the November 16 acquisition could prove to be the last buy before another period of selling.

In a recent development, the possibility is worth watching, but history isn’t a guarantee. The data figures show past sales and a recurring relationship with the $75,400 average cost level; they don’t confirm a rule requiring Strategy to sell Bitcoin whenever that level is crossed.

Sources indicate that for now, Strategy BTC sales remain a possibility rather than a confirmed next move. The next purchase disclosure will matter. If no fresh buying appears, investors may start asking whether the November 16 purchase marked the end of this accumulation phase.
