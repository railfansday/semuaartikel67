Curtis “50 Cent” Jackson‘s reboot with Reebok is making a comeback, just in time for Christmas.

Recent findings suggest that the hip-hop veteran recently teamed up with the sportswear specialist for the G-Unit G6, a blast from the past sneaker which were unveiled in August in its original red, white and blue colorway and proved to be a hit with 50 Cent’s followers. Those shoes re-launch globally on Friday, Nov. 20.

The basketball and street fashion-inspired gear initially rolled out in 2003, the year when Fifty blew up on airwaves and charts around the globe. The one that got away, “In Da Club,” logged nine weeks at No. 1 on the Hot 100, and the New Yorker followed it up two months later with “21 Questions,” featuring the late Nate Dogg, which spent a full month atop the national singles survey. His smash album Get Rich or Die Tryin’ held the top spot on the Billboard 200 for six weeks, and lent its name to the 2005 semi-autobiographical film, which gave 50 Cent his first starring role. That partnership made Jackson one of the first musicians to lead a major footwear franchise. “Creating it the first time was special,” Jackson recounts in an interview with GQ. “I worked with Todd (Krinsky) and the team on everything and they really let me make it mine. It wasn’t just a sneaker—it became part of the culture.”.

Krinsky, who started with Reebok in 1993, working in the mailroom, is now CEO. “Our relationship with 50 has always gone beyond sneakers,” he remarks in a statement. “He helped define an important chapter in Reebok’s history, and together we created something that had a lasting impact on sport style and culture. Bringing the G-Unit G6 back is an opportunity to celebrate that legacy while introducing it to a newly introduced generation.”.

In a recent development, tastes changed and by 2006 that original partnership had moved, with over four million pairs sold in those three years. Nostalgia and licensed kit, however, make for a powerful combination. “We’ve been talking about it for a while,” 50 continues. “Reebok is back, the G-Unit fans are still here, and there’s a whole latest generation discovering us. The timing just felt right.”.

Sources indicate that the G-Unit G6 will be available from $130 at Reebok’s U.S. site, and for A$210 in Australia. “The takeover goes global,” reads 50 Cent’s social post. Check it out below.
