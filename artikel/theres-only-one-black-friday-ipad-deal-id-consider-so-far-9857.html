Black Friday iPad deals are already coming in, with early discounts on most models as the holidays approach. But, having tracked iPad Black Friday prices for close to a decade, I'd hold off buying for now in all but one case: Amazon's $100 off the M4 iPad Air.

With iPad prices inevitably more expensive than during Black Friday last year because of RAM and SSD shortages, this could be as good a deal as we get on the latest Air, and it offers pretty good value. If you do need more power, up to $150 off the M5 iPad Pro at B&H Photo is also tempting. But I think the lower-spec iPads could still fall in price as Black Friday gets closer.

This is the iPad to go for as a potential laptop replacement capable of video editing and heavy graphics work. The OLED screen provides up to 1600 nits peak brightness for HDR work and a fast 120Hz refresh rate for zero lag.

The M4 iPad Air was only released in March of this year, so $100 off might be the best deal we see over Black Friday 2026, and it's not to be sniffed at. This is the iPad we recommend for most people as t's significantly more affordable than the pro, but still has Apple Pencil Pro support and enough power for digital drawing, photo editing and light video editing in Final Cut. There's also $100 off its larger 13-inch sibling.

The latest generation of the basic iPad is a good a choice as ever for anyone looking for a general, everyday tablet for browsing, entertainment and casual sketching and notetaking. This tablet was also released in March 2025, and Amazon had $70 off the original price during Black Friday last year, so this deal could (I would say should) get better.

It's more powerful than the standard iPad and supports Apple Pencil Pro, making it a small but mighty option for sketching and notetaking on the go.

iPad Black Friday deals in the UK aren't great so far, but any saving is welcome on Apple's best tablet – we've not seen any discount at all since Apple hiked prices in April because of the RAM crisis.

Apple hiked the price of all of its current iPad models around the middle of 2026 because of the increase in the cost of components, particularly memory (RAM) and internal storage (SSDs). The whole consumer tech sector has been impacted by these price rises, which have been caused by a shortage of component due to the demand from companies building AI data centres.

Sources indicate that this means that this year's iPad Black Friday deals will inevitably be more expensive than last year's. There's no sign that starting prices will fall again any time soon.

Keeping in mind the size of discounts seen during previous iPad Black Friday sales is a good way to gauge whether it's the right time to buy or if prices could fall further. The pattern we've seen is that the best deals on the latest generation of iPads tend to be up to $70 off the standard iPad and $100 off the iPad Air and up to $200 off higher-spec iPad Pros.

In a recent development, in 14 years of tracking prices, our experience is that there is rarely any difference between Black Friday and Cyber Monday iPad prices. They are usually the same deals under a different name. On a very few occasions, we have seen fresh discounts appear on Cyber Monday.

The best iPad for you will depend on what you want to use it for. For browsing, watching movies, social media and just occasional note taking and sketching, this year's standard 11-inch iPad with the A16 chip will do just fine, and it doesn't make sense to spend more. Aim to spend no more than $399 on this,.

It has been revealed that if you're an artist and want a more efficient drawing experience, I'd recommend stepping up to the iPad Air. A newly introduced M4-chipped generation of this tablet was released in March, and Amazon's already slashed the price by $100 in a Black Friday deal. This tablet comes in two choices of size: 11 inches or 13 inches.

Industry observers note that if you're a professional artist and you have the budget for it, the iPad Pro is the top-of-the-range option. Also coming in 11- and 13-inch models, it has a superior screen with a bright OLED panel for improved contrast along along with a faster 120Hz refresh rate for a seamless drawing experience.

Coming with the M5 chip, this is the iPad to choose if you want to replace you're laptop when working on the go, and it's cut out to handle more demanding creative software, including video editing.

Remember that all iPads come in various configurations, with the option of WiFi and cellular connectivity or just WiFi alone, plus two size options for the Pro and Air and at least two choices of internal storage capacity. When quoting prices above, we've generally quoted the price of the base model with WiFi and the smallest SSD. This might not necessarily be the option that meets your needs, but retailers will usually list other configurations under the same product listing.

If you use a tablet for work and want to store large files on the device, you may need a higher capacity. The iPad Pro, for example, is available with 256GB, 512GB, 1TB or 2TB. the price rises notably, but the higher-spec models sometimes come in for bigger discounts.

Sources indicate that we'll be linking directly to the best Black Friday iPad deals across all four models here on this page. The best thing you can do to prepare to choose the best deal for you is to think ahead about which iPad you want and in which configuration.

Once you've decided which iPad you want (the Pro, Air, mini or standard iPad), choose the size (there's a choice of 11in or 13in with the iPad Pro and iPad Air), whether you want cellular connectivity or WiFi alone and which storage capacity you'll think you need. Whether you can make do with the starting capacity or you need to hike it up to 512GB, or even 1TB or 2TB on the iPad Pro will depend on how you expect to use the device and whether you'll also be using cloud storage or will want to store large design or video files on the tablet itself.

Apple is unlikely to run any direct iPad Black Friday discounts itself. Instead, it's likely yo offer gift vouchers worth up to $100/£80 with the purchase of certain iPads. This might be appealing if you know that you're going to want to buy another Apple product in the future, but most people would be better off going for deals at third-party sites.

In previous years, we have seen good Black Friday iPad deals at most of the major retailers in the US and UK. In the US, Amazon and B&H Photo tend to have the best prices, while Walmart and Target can also be worth checking.

In the UK, Amazon and Very tend to have the biggest discounts, while Laptops Direct is worth checking for discounts on older models. We track prices at all of these retailers and more.

Traditionally, Black Friday was the Friday after Thanksgiving in the US. It's since extended to cover almost all of November at some retailers.
