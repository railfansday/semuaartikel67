GTA 6 reportedly sets massive livestreaming record, watched more in its first week than Elden Ring, Diablo 4, Valorant, and Lost Ark combined

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 has broken another livestreaming record. After attracting more viewers across livestreaming platforms than any game ever on its launch day, Rockstar's open-world crime sim has reportedly continued to dominate on platforms like Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.

It has been revealed that over its first seven days on sale, GTA 6 has amassed over 420m hours watched, according to Streams Charts. That's nearly three times more than the previous record, held by Valorant's closed beta in April 2020. In fact, that 420m hours tally is more than the first-week watch time achieved by Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo 4, and Lost Ark combined.

Sources indicate that as for the livestreaming platforms that saw the largest audiences for GTA 6, YouTube reportedly had the lead in average audience numbers during the first six hours of launch, but Twitch took over in content volume in the days that followed. In fact, Twitch's GTA 6 watch time was higher at launch than any other game in its history.

In a recent development, streams Charts also noted 1,056 channels reached new all-time peak viewership numbers while streaming GTA 6, in some cases multiplying their previous record tenfold. Major content creators like IShowSpeed, IlloJuan, and TheBurntPeanut stole the spotlight, but GTA 6 also helped many other channels set new viewership records.

Recent findings suggest that livestreaming, of course, isn't the only area where GTA 6 has set new records. Publisher Take-Two called it the biggest entertainment launch in history, generating $3.79bn (£2.89bn) in revenue from 32.5m copies sold on day one. Other parts of the industry have benefited, too. Anticipation for GTA 6 boosted US hardware sales for both PlayStation and Xbox, just as GTA 6 became each console brand's most pre-ordered title ever. At launch, more than 39 million people played GTA 6 on PS5, according to Sony. The game also had the highest number of concurrent players ever on any PlayStation console, beating even the most popular free-to-play titles.
