Liverpool are one of four clubs interested in making a move for Atalanta centre-back Giorgio Scalvini, according to TEAMtalk, as details emerge about their level of interest in the Italian.

The 22-year-old is rated extremely highly in Italy and has been linked with a move to the Premier League for some time now.

Scalvini has endured his fair share of injury issues in recent seasons, including an ACL injury on the eve of Euro 2024 and a shoulder glitch the following season, but he is now featuring again more regularly for Atalanta.

TEAMtalk report that a number of Premier League clubs are now carrying out detailed work on the defender ahead of potential moves.

As well as interest from Andoni Iraola's Liverpool team, Manchester United and Chelsea have been keeping a close eye on Scalvini.

Newcastle United are also long-term admirers of the Chiari-born player.

Liverpool did add to their defensive options back in the summer with highly-rated Jeremy Jacquet joining in a £55m deal from Rennes and youngster Ifeanyi Ndukwe moving from Austria Wien for £2.6m.

The potential addition of Scalvini, who is valued at €38m (£32m) by Transfermarkt, would be an outstanding acquisition for Liverpool and Iraola as they proposal for the years ahead.

Giorgio Scalvini's 2026/27 season in numbers.

Liverpool would first and foremost be getting an excellent defender in Scalvini.

A strong defender with technical quality and composure on the ball, the youngster is also capable of playing in a high defensive line and he can also chip in with a goal after notching three last term.

He has already been described as being 'elite' by Football Talent Scout Jacek Kulig on X this year, such is his ability on and off the ball.

Former Arsenal defender Sead Kolasinac is a teammate of Scalvini at Atalanta and he previously revealed how shocked he was at the player's quality when he was still a teenager.

"I am struck by the maturity of his 19 years," noted the Bosnian. "He reads the game well, he knows what to do with the ball, defend one-on-one, how to use his body in marking: it's nice to have him alongside in defence.

"But first of all he is a good guy, even humble: if he doesn't lose this character, he will have a great career.".

Scalvini also came in for praise from former Atalanta boss Gian Piero Gasperini, who stated that the centre-back is "destined for greatness" when speaking three years ago.

“He is already a great player at 19,” said Gasperini, as reported by Foot Italia.

According to recent reports, “He is a leader on and off the pitch. He’s destined for greatness. Wherever you put him he has quality and class.”.
