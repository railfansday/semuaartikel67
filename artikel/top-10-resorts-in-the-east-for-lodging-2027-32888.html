Weekend ski days are great, but there’s something to be said for settling in for a few nights. Waking up slopeside, sipping coffee while watching the lifts spin, and taking your time getting to first chair? That’s a different kind of ski vacation.

Recent findings suggest that whether you’re looking for a spacious condo for the whole crew, a cozy inn, or a full-service hotel with all the amenities, these are the Eastern resorts SKI readers say do lodging best. So ditch the day-trip routine, pack an extra bag, and make a weekend (or longer) of it.

Sources indicate that “The past two years are when I really started going to Waterville Valley for skiing, and I truly enjoy the family feeling of things. Staff is awesome and always available. You can always find someone to help and answer any questions that you have, and it is very walkable.
