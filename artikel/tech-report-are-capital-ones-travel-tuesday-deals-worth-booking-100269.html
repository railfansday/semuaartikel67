Tech Report: Are Capital One’s Travel Tuesday deals worth booking?

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (199 words, 📖 Standard)
  • A range of limited-time travel deals are available starting Dec. 1, including discounts on hotels, rental cars, vacation rentals and flight-and-stay packages through a major travel portal.
  • Offers include a complimentary fourth night at select properties, hotel savings in Washington, D.C. and Florida, vacation rental discounts, and 15% off qualifying car rentals.
  • Eligibility varies by card type, with some deals targeted to specific cardholders and others more broadly available.
  • Some promotions, such as the complimentary fourth night and certain vacation rental discounts, may already be accessible to eligible cardholders outside of Travel Tuesday.
  • Booking and travel windows, as well as supply limits, apply to each offer—hotel deals in D.C. end Dec. 7 or when supplies run out, and car rental discounts end Dec. 15.
  • Not all cardholders will see every promotion; premium vacation rental and curated hotel offers are targeted to select accounts.
  • Discounts may be less valuable if your travel plans do not align with the specific destinations or brands featured in these deals.
