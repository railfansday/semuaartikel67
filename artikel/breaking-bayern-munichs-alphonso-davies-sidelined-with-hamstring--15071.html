📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bayern Munich have announced that Alphonso Davies suffered a hamstring injury during the international break.

The 26-year-old lasted just six minutes of Canada’s 3-2 win in Guatemala on Saturday.

Sources indicate that “Following a medical scan [Sunday] in Toronto, it has been confirmed that Alphonso Davies sustained a minor lower-body muscle strain. He is travelling back to Germany, where he will continue to receive ongoing care,” a Canada Soccer spokesperson informed Canadian Soccer Daily on Sunday evening.

Bayern have since confirmed that Davies will face time on the sidelines, even though a clear duration was not mentioned.

According to Bayern Munich reporter for TZ, Philipp Kessler, Davies will be out for 7-10 days. The initiative is for him to return to the squad for the UEFA Champions League game in Lille next week, indicating that the injury isn’t serious.

Davies has struggled for consistent playing time in the last couple of seasons because of muscular injuries, and has also sustained hamstring injuries back in April and March, keeping him out of crucial games.
