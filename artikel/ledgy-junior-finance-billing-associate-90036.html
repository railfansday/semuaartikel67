Industry observers note that at Ledgy, we’re on a mission to make Europe a powerhouse of entrepreneurship by building a modern, tech-driven equity management and financial reporting platform for private and public companies. In 2026, we aim to be the leading provider for European IPOs and reporting for share-based payments. We are a value-based tech giant with a core focus on being humble, transparent, ambitious and impactful, all in order to delivery the best experience for our customers and end people.

We are proud to partner with some of the world’s leading investors. Fresh Enterprise Associates led our $22m Series B round in 2022, with Philip Chopin joining Sequoia’s Luciana Lixandru on our board.

In a recent development, we were founded in Switzerland in 2017 and today we operate globally from offices in Zurich and London. We encourage diversity and are an international team coming from 26 different countries and speaking 25 different languages.

As Junior Finance and Billing Associate you own billing operations, pre-accounting, and day-to-day finance tasks for our four entities across Ledgy.

According to recent reports, you report to the Finance Lead and work closely with Revenue Operations, Sales, Customer Success and our external accounting partners. This is a role with real ownership: billing is yours from day one and you will grow to own key work streams in international accounting and finance operations.

Pay within the range reflects your skills, experience and the scope of the role, and is set using objective criteria applied consistently across the team. Certain roles are also eligible for additional rewards including variable pay under the applicable strategy. Ledgy offers equity through our employee option plan. Benefits offered vary by country and type of employment.
