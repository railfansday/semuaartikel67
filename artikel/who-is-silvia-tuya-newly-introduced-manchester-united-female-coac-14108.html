Man Utd have already agreed a £9m compensation package with the US Soccer Federation to extract Pochettino from his contract as manager of the United States Men’s National Team (USMNT).

Following the Argentine to Old Trafford will be long-serving lieutenants, including assistant manager Jesus Perez and goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez.

But Pochettino will also bring newer additions to his coaching team, including strength and conditioning coach Tuya in what will be a first for Man United.

Silvia Tuya - full name Silvia Tuya Vinas - is a strength and conditioning coach, right now employed by the USMNT.

Tuya will become the first female coach of the men’s first team squad at Old Trafford, creating history ahead of a new chapter for Manchester United.

The strength and conditioning coach will be familiar with creating such history, having previously become the first female coach in the history of the USMNT’s senior squad when she joined the backroom staff in October 2024.

Tuya holds a doctorate of science in high performance and is fluent in English, Spanish, and Catalan.

She has previously lectured at the University of Barcelona and National Institute of Physical Education of Catalonia, before joining Pochettino’s USA set up in October 2024.

Prior to the move to the States, the Spaniard briefly worked with FC Badalona in her native Catalonia, the team competing in Liga F - the top-flight of the women’s game in Spain.

Tuya also worked with Levante’s women's team, and has experience in strength and conditioning athletes from other disciplines, such as tennis, rugby, basketball, and ironman competitors.

The specialist in strength and conditioning was in charge of physical preparation at Levante, and will likely work closely with fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino - son of Mauricio - at Man United.

A PhD fellow, Tuya has contributed to different scientific papers, including research into reactions in football.

While certain members of Pochettino’s backroom staff have already travelled to England, the Guardian claim Tuya, analyst Alec Scott and Miguel D’Agostino have remained with the USA camp ahead of the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final second leg with Haiti.

As such, Tuya will be expected to start work at Carrington from November 18 - the same day as Pochettino.

Man United agreed that Pochettino could take charge of the USA’s games against Haiti before starting work at Carrington and Old Trafford.
