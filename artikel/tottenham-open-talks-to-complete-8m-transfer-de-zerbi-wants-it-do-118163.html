Tottenham are already in talks over their first January transfer window deal, according to reports in Turkey.

Spurs have been in a rich vein of form of late, turning their fortunes from the start of the 2026/27 season around with four wins in their last five games to move into the top half of the Premier League table.

Roberto De Zerbi's methods and philosophy are starting to permeate through the playing squad, and while the Italian's teams are normally known for their free-flowing, high-press, attacking football, his Tottenham team is set up with defensive solidity first and foremost.

Tottenham have the second-best Expected Goals Conceded statistics in the top flight so far, only just behind league leaders Arsenal, showing their defensive nous.

It is at the other end of the pitch where they have so far struggled, with just 12 goals in 11 games so far, the joint fourth-worst in the division.

It has been revealed that dominic Solanke has two goals to his name, scoring his second of the campaign on Saturday, with centre-back Jan Paul van Hecke also on two as the club's joint top scorers in the league. Omar Marmoush is the only other recognised striker in the squad, although he can play in a variety of positions across the front line.

That is because Richarlison, the club's top scorer in all competitions in 2025/26, was left out of the 25-man Premier League squad entirely, following a falling out with De Zerbi over his future. De Zerbi initially described Richarlison as having an "unbelievable" attitude, but admitted that he had been left confused by the striker changing his mind over whether or not he wanted to stay at the club.

The Brazilian had been in talks over a move to Everton and Turkish side Trabzonspor in the late stages of the summer transfer window, but a move away did not come to fruition and the 29-year-old is now in the wilderness at Hotspur Way, training and playing with the U21s.

Sources indicate that richarlison only has seven months left on his current deal at Spurs and will be a free agent in the summer. He can also strike up a pre-contract agreement with foreign clubs in the January transfer window, ahead of a move in the summer.

But talks are already underway between Tottenham and Trabzonspor, over a potential move in the January transfer window.

Turkish journalist Yagiz Sabuncuoglu has reported that talks have already taken place between the two clubs and that Spurs are demanding a €10million (£8million).

De Zerbi will be keen to see the Brazilian leave the club in the winter, rather than having someone at the club who is not part of his aims for the second half of the season.

Spurs are also targeting a striker in the winter window, with the Italian keen to add another source of goals to his squad. Eli Junior Kroupi, Igor Mantanovic and Ivan Toney are names who have been linked with a move to N17.
