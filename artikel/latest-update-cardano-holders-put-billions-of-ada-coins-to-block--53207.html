Cardano DReps have rejected a proposal by Toto Finance to spend 22 million ADA from the Cardano treasury on a "Strategic Copper Reserve.".

The vote closed on Nov. 15, and DReps holding 2.86 billion ADA voted against the proposal, according to Cardano governance data. DReps with 20.02 million ADA voted in favor of it.

DRep John MacPherson was among those who voted "No," and he explained his decision in a post on X.

Sources indicate that macPherson wrote that he is not against real-world assets on Cardano. Still, he remarked the proposal asks the treasury "to take on both the copper price and the ada price," and Toto Finance would collect "up to about 3.4M ada in fees.".

At the same time, MacPherson noted that the proposal is “more honest about its risks than most.”.

Toto Finance, a company led by Monty Metzger, submitted the proposal on Oct. 15. ADA is trading at $0.152 on Monday, and the requested 22 million ADA is now worth around $3.3 million.

The strategy was to buy copper with 14.72 million ADA and store it in a warehouse near Fresh Orleans. Toto Finance wanted to issue one CuTT token for each pound of copper. The metal would be sold in 18 to 24 months.

The Cardano Foundation, which votes as a DRep, unveiled its "No" vote in October.

"We recognize the opportunity in real-world asset tokenization; however, this proposal places 22,000,000 ada under the recipient's sole control to fund a fee-heavy concentrated commodity position whose safeguards are not yet in place," the foundation wrote.

Three members of the Constitutional Committee voted "No," and the other four members did not vote.

According to recent reports, crypto commentator Ingeniero Cripto agreed with MacPherson in the replies under his post. He called the "double exposure" to the prices of copper and ADA the key problem of the proposal, as the Cardano treasury already holds ADA.

According to him, RWAs on Cardano make sense, but “funding the asset itself is a different question than funding the rails.”.
