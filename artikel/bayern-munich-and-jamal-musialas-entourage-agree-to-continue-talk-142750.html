📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bayern Munich have revealed that constructive talks have been held with Jamal Musiala’s management following the player’s health condition, which has been a hot topic of late.

As widely reported, Musiala has symptoms resembling absence seizures linked to a neurological disorder, with Bayern further adding that the player’s health is the top priority for both parties and that Musiala will receive full support throughout his recovery.

The statement further clarified that Musiala was first examined for symptoms on 22 April 2025, which took place in consultation with Bayern and its medical department, immediately after the neurological symptoms first appeared.

Germany’s record champions were heavily criticized for how they handled the situation following statements from Uli Hoeneß and Karl-Heinz Rummenigge.

Hoeneß said: “We’re in a dilemma with him. He has an illness that cannot simply be cured, but can be managed to some extent with medication. However, whether he will ever regain the level of performance he once had remains uncertain. He is still receiving his salary, of course. But just imagine if Bayern were now to question his contract and say: ‘What he is capable of delivering now is no longer what was stipulated in the contract.'”.

Industry observers note that rummenigge echoed this statement, commenting about a potential pay cut for Musiala. “We would have to speak with his family, the player himself and his agency. Because, in that situation, those figures would simply no longer be financially justifiable, with all due respect,” he added.

In response to this, FIFPRO strongly condemned Bayern Munich’s leadership, accusing the club of a “coordinated strategy” to publicly pressure Musiala into renegotiating his contract.

Now Bayern have said that the club and Musiala’s management have agreed to continue their discussions “in a spirit of partnership and confidentiality.”.
