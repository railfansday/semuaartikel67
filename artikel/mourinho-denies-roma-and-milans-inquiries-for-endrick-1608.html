📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Endrick, despite playing very little this early in the season, is a player who has attracted the attention of Roma and AC Milan.

It has been revealed that both Italian clubs have shown interest in the 2006-born player, owned by Real Madrid. He’s a young, strong player with plenty of room for growth, something Mourinho is counting on for the future.

Sources indicate that the Special One doesn’t want to let go of the Brazilian striker and continues to put up a defensive stance. There are no convincing offers, and Endrick isn’t moving from Madrid. This is the Portuguese coach’s message.

AC Milan are serious about Endrick. As reported by Corriere dello Sport, the Rossoneri are willing to offer €60 million with the option of adding another €10 million and an €80 million buyback clause to allow Real Madrid to maintain control of the player. An ambitious proposal that met with a wall of resistance from Mourinho.

The same message was sent to Roma, who would like to bring the Brazilian striker to the capital.

Endrick is in Mourinho’s prepares; he is a resource worth developing and a talent to build a future around. This eloquent thought also undermines the excellent relationship AC Milan has with the agency managing the interests of the 2006-born player.
