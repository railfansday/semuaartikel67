It has been revealed that I doubt many runners look forward to running in wintry weather (at least not for months on end), but winter does have a silver lining: lots of latest gear to shop. Montreal-based running apparel brand Ciele certainly knows a thing or two about running in the cold, and the brand just released its second-ever Woolmark-certified collection of merino wool running apparel and accessories.

The collection highlights Ciele’s two merino-based fabrics: Sorino WS, a merino-silk blend used in base layers, and Sorino W, a 100% merino fabric used in headwear. The collection includes several hats, two facemasks, and base layers for men and women. Woolmark, an independent wool authority, certifies all gear to ensure wool textiles meet industry standards for fiber content, colorfastness, and durability.

Check out some highlights from the collection below, and shop the full lineup at Ciele.com.

According to recent reports, the beauty of this hat is its simplicity: a straightforward cap that lets merino wool's natural performance shine. Made from pure merino, it fits snugly, so it won’t shift while running, and the knit fabric naturally traps heat, vents excess moisture, and resists odors. Added bonus: This hat is machine washable (on the cold setting), and the “speedway” pattern gives it a snazzy look, too.

Prefer a more classic “watch cap” style beanie? Check out the VNTBeanie, also made from 100 percent merino fabric. You’ll get all the performance benefits of merino, plus the hat’s ribbed structure boosts its breathability (and looks great, too).

The FRDMask will help you power through icy winds and single-digit temperatures. It’s way more than a face mask: Made from a plush merino-cashmere blend, it keeps your entire head, neck, and face warm. The key is its all-encompassing design, which layers neatly under a jacket and seals cold spots that outerwear and a hat would normally leave exposed.

Industry observers note that the HLSLongsleeve really demonstrates the value of merino as a performance fabric. Made from a comfy merino-silk blend, it regulates your temperature, breathes well, resists odors, and feels great (Ciele states the silk gives it a smooth texture and boosts its strength). The snug fit and thumbholes make it easy to layer under a jacket and gloves, but it’s also great in warmer weather—the fabric is rated UPF 30, so it protects you from sunburn when worn on its own.
