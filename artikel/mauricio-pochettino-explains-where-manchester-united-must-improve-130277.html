Mauricio Pochettino has called on his Manchester United squad to work on their focus after individual errors saw them drop points at Sporting.

The Argentine’s chaotic start to life with United continued in his second match as United were held to an entertaining 2-2 draw by Sporting.

Industry observers note that united gave up two penalties in the first half, one of which was saved, while Senne Lammens dropped the ball to gift the hosts their late equaliser. Youri Tielemans also found himself sent off in second-half injury-time as he picked up a brace of yellow cards.

As he continues his pursuit of his first win with United, Pochettino said United needed to cut out their silly errors.

Asked how to go about stemming the flow of such mistakes, he told TNT Sports: “With this type of situation, in different clubs, we need to be better. We need to concentrate more, focus more, try to recover better, all the efforts.”.

To their credit, United were much improved in Lisbon, and Pochettino stated he is looking forward to hosting Brentford at the weekend.

Recent findings suggest that “We start to fix. With a performance like today it's the best medicine to prepare the next game.

In a recent development, "We demonstrated the way we want to play. We've only been working together one week.”.

Focus now turns to recovery for United, as Pochettino bemoaned the hectic fixture schedule which will limit his time on the training ground with his fresh squad.

Sources indicate that he continued: “The priority is next one. We need to believe more and more and more. Second half we recovered a lot of balls in the opposition half. That's the way we want to play.

“We need to go to Old Trafford and show the commitment and play the way we want to play today.”.
