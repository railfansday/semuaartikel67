📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sorenson Capital-led funding will accelerate AI-powered activation of first-party video and website engagement data, connecting human interactions with intelligent go-to-market strategies.

In a recent development, SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sequel.io, a video-driven audience intelligence and activation platform, today announced $15 million in funding led by Sorenson Capital, bringing total funding to approximately $26 million. Upcoming investors in the round include High Alpha, Album VC and Webflow Ventures, with additional participation from existing investors Javelin Venture Partners, Struck Capital, Alumni Ventures and Social Leverage. The investment will support product innovation and enterprise growth as Sequel helps marketers turn human video engagement and website behavior into unique first-party intelligence that AI agents can act on across GTM.

Sources indicate that from Human Engagement to First-Party Audience IntelligenceMost platforms still treat webinars as a one-off event. The experience often lives off-site, separate from the rest of the buyer journey and the tools marketing teams use every day. That leaves teams doing more manual work to run programs and makes it harder to carry what they learn from the event into the rest of GTM.

Industry observers note that sequel takes a different approach. Its video is hosted directly on a brand's website and connects what people watch, ask and respond to during live, simulive and on-demand video with the other content they explore across the site. It's the only provider in its category combining these signals at both the individual contact and account level. This gives marketers a clearer view of audience interests, emerging purchase intent and engagement across buying groups, and the context to make outreach and experiences more relevant.

"Video on its own can't build buyer trust at scale, and AI on its own can't create the context brands need to grow pipeline," remarked Oana Manolache, co-founder and CEO of Sequel.io. "The opportunity is in bringing human conversation, first-party audience intelligence and AI agents together. That gives brands a better way to understand buyers, build trust and turn those relationships into growth. This funding gives us the opportunity to push that vision further and help shape what modern go-to-market looks like in the AI era.".

Recent findings suggest that as AI-generated communication becomes more common, Sequel sees human-led video as a way for brands to build trust while learning what audiences actually care about. Its AI agents can use that first-party context to inform program planning, content creation, personalization and follow-up. The platform can personalize website experiences, publish video summaries and recaps to landing pages, and make original expertise discoverable by future visitors, search engines and AI answer engines. Customers can also build custom agents within Sequel or connect through its MCP server.

According to recent reports, "The next era of go-to-market will be won by the companies that understand their buyers best, and that understanding starts with first-party data," said Eric Hilton, partner at Sorenson Capital. "AI agents are only as good as the data they act on, and few signals are richer than the first-party data around how a real buyer engages with a firm's video and website. Sequel owns that moment, and we believe it's well positioned to define this emerging category.".

Customer Momentum and Measurable ResultsSequel customers include ElevenLabs, Clay, Carta, Extreme Networks, Morningstar DBRS, Cato Networks, MaintainX and symplr. The company has grown revenue 3x for three consecutive years, now serves 6 million owners globally and has grown its enterprise customer base 5x since January 2026. Sequel's net revenue retention is rising significantly, as customers renew and expand their usage on the platform.

The funding will help Sequel expand its enterprise business and develop more ways to activate audience intelligence across the customer journey, including personalized website experiences, reusable video content and custom agent workflows.

"Sequel makes interactive video native to a brand's domain, turning passive web pages into high-intent conversion engines," said Dan Brown, head of Webflow Ventures. "As an agentic web marketing platform, Webflow helps marketing teams turn their websites into their most valuable growth channel, and Sequel brings that same belief to video.".

About Sequel.ioSequel helps B2B marketing teams run live and on-demand video directly on their own websites, connect video engagement with first-party website behavior and use that context to create better content, more relevant experiences and more opportunities for growth.

By connecting human video, first-party audience context and AI agents, Sequel helps marketers turn webinars and video into a go-to-market engine.

The future of GTM is more human than you think.
