The Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago has received a promised gift of 65 artworks from longtime supporters Jack and Sandra Guthman to mark its 60th anniversary.

Sources indicate that the gift from the Chicago-based collectors spans paintings, photography, works on paper, and sculptures, and includes works by Pope.L, Gary Simmons, Andrea Bowers, Christian Marclay, Elle Pérez, and Shinique Smith. The museum mentioned in a release that the selected works were chosen in tandem with the curatorial staff, “taking the MCA’s collecting priorities into serious consideration.” Related Articles Bluhm Family Gives $5 M. to MCA Chicago for Gender Equity Fund, Named After Outgoing Director Outgoing MCA Chicago Director Madeleine Grynsztejn Offers the Consummate Insider's Guide to the Windy City.

Industry observers note that in its announcement, the MCA Chicago reported that, of the 54 artists represented in the gift, 23 (or 43 percent) are people of color, 19 (or 35 percente) are women, and 13 are entering the collection for the first time, among them Rashid Johnson, Christopher Wool, Dawoud Bey, and Amanda Williams. Johnson’s traveling survey, which opened at the Guggenheim Museum last year, is currently on view at the MCA Chicago; Williams will have a solo show at the museum next year.

Sources indicate that “Together, Jack and Sandy have helped shape Chicago’s cultural life through their collecting, advocacy, and longstanding engagement with artists and institutions,” MCA Chicago director Madeleine Grynsztejn remarked in a statement. “To be entrusted with this tremendous gift is a great honor.”.

The Guthmans first donated an artwork to the museum in 1988, and Jack joined the board of trustees the year after. To mark its 50th anniversary in 2016, they donated work by 50 women-identifying photographers, which was the subject of the exhibition “Woman With a Camera.” With the newly donated 65 works, the Guthmans have given a total of 130 works over the past nearly four decades, the largest amount by a single collector in the MCA Chicago’s history.

Jack is an attorney who has worked on several mega-developments in Chicago. He is also the founder and past board chair of the Chicago Architecture Biennial. Sandra worked at IBM from 1965 to 1993, rising from systems engineer to director of marketing for the Midwest. She also served as chief executive and chair of the Chicago-based Polk Bros. Foundation; she is now chair emerita. In addition to the MCA Chicago, they have supported the Art Institute of Chicago, the Hyde Park Art Center, Theaster Gates’s Art Bank, and the Harris Theater for Music and Dance. At the Harris, they sponsored the creation of the Guthman Gallery.

Recent findings suggest that last month, the MCA Chicago received a $5 million gift from the Bluhm family to endow the Women Artists Initiative, which will provide funds in perpetuity to help the museum achieve its gender equity goals in its programming and collection. Both gifts are meant to honor Grynsztejn, who is stepping down at the end of the year.

According to recent reports, “We are fortunate to have the opportunity to donate these works as the MCA marks its 60th anniversary and as Madeleine Grynsztejn steps down after a remarkable tenure as its Pritzker Director,” the Guthmans stated in a statement. “We celebrate both the institution and the woman who has lifted it to a where it is today—a Chicago institution now acknowledged as a leader in the world of contemporary art.”.
