TUCSON, Ariz., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Marketing for the Future, LLC, a marketing agency serving home service companies across the United States and Canada, today revealed the launch of a free Marketing Budget Calculator. The tool helps business owners determine exactly how much they should spend on marketing to hit their revenue goals, based on their industry, current revenue, and desired growth pace. Available now at mktg4thefuture.com/home-service-marketing-budget-calculator, the calculator is also free for ad agencies and marketing firms to embed directly on their own websites.

Instead of basing recommendations on trailing revenue, the calculator works backward from a manufacturer's revenue goal. Customers select their industry, enter their trailing-twelve-month revenue, set a target for the next twelve months, and choose a growth pace of Light, Moderate, or Aggressive. The tool then generates a recommended annual and monthly marketing budget, broken down by quarter and week, along with a suggested split across marketing channels. Results update instantly, with no email signup required.

Sources indicate that "Business owners need clear, data-driven insight into what it truly takes to scale in today's digital landscape. This tool was built to provide exactly that. Having spent two decades in digital marketing, I can say with confidence that the level of competition we are seeing right now is unprecedented," said Caleb Tennenbaum, Founder & CEO of Marketing for the Future.

Home service businessesâincluding those in pest control, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, garage doors, cleaning, pool service, painting, and remodelingâoften struggle to know whether they're underspending or overspending on marketing relative to their growth goals. The calculator addresses this gap by calibrating budget recommendations to typical investment levels within each trade, accounting for factors like competition and customer acquisition patterns specific to that industry.

In a recent development, ad agencies and marketing firms can embed the calculator on their own websites at no cost. Every embed includes the Marketing for the Future logo and a link back to mktg4thefuture.com, giving agencies a useful client tool while extending the company's brand reach. Embedding requires only downloading the calculator file, uploading it to a website, and pasting a short code snippet onto the desired page.

Industry observers note that marketing for the Future positions the calculator as a starting point for a broader conversation about marketing investment. The company encourages business owners and agencies alike to use the tool to benchmark their current spend, blueprint for growth, and avoid the common pitfalls of underfunding or overfunding their marketing efforts.

The Marketing Budget Calculator is available now, free of charge, at https://mktg4thefuture.com/home-service-marketing-budget-calculator/.
