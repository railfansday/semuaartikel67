Paolo Vanoli claims Cher Ndour and Alieu Njie are both fit and Arthur Atta is back in contention as Fiorentina prepare to face Monza on Monday night.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Fiorentina coach gave his pre-match press conference at the Viola Park ahead of the trip to the U-Power Stadium, where kick-off is at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT].

Industry observers note that “Ndour has been training separately and will join the group tomorrow. Njie took a knock on international duty but has come back well and recovered,” Vanoli said. “Oulaï has come back well too, and his morale is high after his goal. Fagioli is in a different frame of mind, but he’s fired up. Atta has been training with the group, and I think he’ll be involved in Monza.

“He’s an crucial player and I’m happy to have him back, but he’s been out for a long time, so his minutes will increase gradually.”.

Vanoli also responded to reports that he might free Franco Mastantuono from defensive duties.

Sources indicate that “I’m sorry you’re spreading the idea that an attacker has to stay close to goal. That’s your speculation. Kvara said that if he didn’t defend, he didn’t play. Football today is about both phases. We need to get better so he does less defending by improving our attacking play, but a player has to be complete if he wants to stay at the very highest level. I’ve never seen a coach who doesn’t make a player do defensive work.”.

In a recent development, fiorentina are unbeaten in three after beating Sassuolo 4-2 and drawing 2-2 with Juventus. They’re 15th on nine points, two above Monza, who are 18th and have Serie A’s top scorer, Gustavo Varela.

“They’re well organised, with a very good coach. They’re a direct team who go man-to-man, so we’ll need to move the ball rapidly without holding on to it too much. They’re top in almost every physical category, so it’ll be a complicated game. Every team that’s played there has suffered.”.

Fiorentina have conceded 25 goals in 11 games, and Vanoli was asked about it.

Industry observers note that “It’s not the same defence as last year. Apart from De Gea, all the others are new. Plenty of teams in Italian football concede more. We’re being brave, as we were against Juventus, but we need to work better on individual tactics at the back. That’s something that’s been lost a bit in Italy.”.

Asked what he’d like for Christmas from the transfer market, he replied: “I’d choose a win against Monza.”.
