Mauricio Pochettino has been pictured arriving in the UK for the first time as Manchester United manager.

The Argentine flew straight from the United States in the early hours of Wednesday morning and the Gulfstream G700 he was believed to be on was spotted landing at Manchester airport shortly before midday.

It was unveiled last week that the former Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea boss was making a return to the Premier League, succeeding Michael Carrick at Old Trafford.

However, the 54-year-old’s start to his fourth spell in English football was delayed as he finished up commitments with the United States Men’s National Team (USMNT), overseeing an 8-0 aggregate win over Haiti in the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-finals in his last game in charge.

Now after touching down in the UK, Pochettino can now take the next step in his career.

“I am ready [to return],” he told the Telegraph. “This is what I miss a little bit. I miss to be, every day, with something here.

“Guardiola asked me when I was at the game, he said, ‘Oh, you don’t want to come back to the Premier League?’ I said, ‘No, I’m happy here.’.

“[He said] ‘Yeah, but you don’t miss, every day, to…’ I say, ‘A little bit, yes, because you think now today, and then we have December camp against Jamaica for the MLS [players in the pool].

It has been revealed that “But after, you need to wait [until] March. What do you do? You need to watch games, you need to travel – administrative things. That is nice, it’s good.

“But every day, to be involved: press conference, training, everything … this type of possibility, like with United, you say yes. I want that. I want to prove myself again.”.

Pochettino is expected to head to Carrington immediately as he settles into his new role as Man United head coach, with the focus already shifting to his first game in charge against Liverpool on Sunday.
