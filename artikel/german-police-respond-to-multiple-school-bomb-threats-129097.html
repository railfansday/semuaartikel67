📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Police in several locations across Germany reported large numbers of bomb threats targeting schools.

They remarked that while they were investigating the claims, they did not initially have reason to treat them as credible threats.

More than 400 schools in the southwestern state of Baden Württemberg received threats, and more than 250 in Saxony-Anhalt received the emails, with several other regions also targeted on Monday morning.

Police in Erfurt in Thuringia were among the first to matter a statement on the threats, saying they were responding to threats at "multiple schools" after morning warnings, including in Jena, Erfurt, Mühlhausen, Zella-Mehlis, Nordhausen, Gotha, Rudolstadt and Greiz.

Industry observers note that "The same word-for-word threats were received this morning at the various eductional institutes," police wrote in a press release. "Based on preliminary findings other federal states are also affected by the threatening messages.".

Police noted they had no information yet on the potential author or sender of the emails. They were investigating breach of the peace-based crimes via the threat of criminal activity.

"At present the police is not working on the assumption of the bomb threats being serious and recommends that schools do not implement evacuation measures and continue their normal operations," they said.

Police in Freiburg in the southwest and authorities to the north in Hanover also mentioned they were responding to emailed bomb threats directed against schools.

Similar reports are emerging to the north of Germany in Denmark.

As in Germany, Danish police remarked they did not believe the threats were credible, "but they understandably create insecurity locally at the schools.".

This follows other recent cases of coordinated bomb threats against multiple schools that turned out to be hoaxes in Italy earlier in the month.

It also comes amid various apparent bids to create disruption within Europe in recent weeks, such as multiple bomb threats targeting commercial airlines, or hacking into supermarket refrigeration systems to spoil produce en masse.
