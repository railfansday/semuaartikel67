Despite falling behind early on, Common responded in excellent fashion, scoring twice before the break and adding two further goals in the second half to record a deserved victory.

Common started brightly and could have taken an early lead when Sam Ellis met a delivery into the box, only to see his header superbly saved by the visiting goalkeeper. However, it was Pagham who broke the deadlock in the 10th minute when Common failed to clear a free kick and the loose ball was scrambled home from close range.

The hosts looked for an immediate response and Ollie Hull went close to restoring parity, but his near-post effort was once again kept out by the busy Pagham goalkeeper.

Common's persistence was rewarded in spectacular fashion in the 23rd minute when Ollie MacDonald collected possession some 30 yards from goal before unleashing a thunderous strike that flew beyond the helpless goalkeeper and into the roof of the net.

The equaliser sparked Common into life and they soon completed the turnaround. Lewis Unwin delivered an inviting free kick into the penalty area, where Kieran Ludlow rose highest to guide a perfectly placed header into the far corner of the net.

Ludlow then demonstrated his importance at the opposite end, producing a vital goal-line clearance as Pagham searched for an equaliser.

Common had a golden opportunity to extend their advantage on the stroke of half-time when they were awarded a penalty. However, Hull saw his spot kick saved and appeared to be pulled down as he attempted to reach the rebound, only for the referee to wave away the appeals.

The hosts continued to look dangerous after the interval and extended their lead in the 57th minute. Excellent work from Hull down the left flank saw him deliver the ball across goal for Freddie Warren, who was on hand to tap home from close range.

Recent findings suggest that warren almost doubled his tally shortly afterwards when his effort crashed against the crossbar, while substitute Sonny Tyrell also went close after bursting into the penalty area, only to be denied by another fine save from the visiting goalkeeper. At the other end, Matt Cruttwell enjoyed a relatively quiet afternoon as Common defended resolutely and restricted Pagham to very few clear-cut opportunities.

Common put the icing on the cake in the 90th minute when Hull, who had been a constant threat throughout, once again turned provider. His pass found Tyrell on the edge of the penalty area and the substitute made no mistake, firing a powerful effort into the roof of the net to complete an emphatic victory.

Recent findings suggest that boss Russ Eldridge mentioned: “It was a massive three points for us and I felt we thoroughly deserved the win. Going behind early on was disappointing, particularly from a set piece, but the lads showed great character to respond in the way they did.

Sources indicate that “We scored some excellent goals, particularly Ollie MacDonald’s strike, a fantastic effort. We looked dangerous going forward and, in truth, could have scored a few more.

Recent findings suggest that “What pleased me most was the attitude and work rate. We’ve put in some really spirited performances against sides near the top of the table recently without getting rewards, so it was key we backed those up with three points.

According to recent reports, “We know there’s still plenty of hard work ahead of us, but hopefully this result will give everyone a bit of confidence and something to build on. The challenge now is to put a run of results together.”.
