We have been married a year and looking to start a family. Our current home I bought in 2018 for 190k (3 bed 2 bath). Are thee other unstated reasons you and your spouse are looking for a bigger house? 3 bedrooms and 2 baths seems like enough space for two people. I would wait until the children arrive before you start looking. Who knows what could happen in the meantime. Perhaps you have a child born with disabilities that require a different house layout? What happens if you find you are unable to have children? Unless there are other reasons left out, you are putting your cart in front of the horse.Statistics: Posted by lthenderson — Wed Nov 18, 2026 8:38 am.
