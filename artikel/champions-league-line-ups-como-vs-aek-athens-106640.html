Como can make a push to qualify in the Champions League top eight as they host Luka Jovic’s AEK Athens with Nico Paz, Martin Baturina and Tasos Douvikas upfront.

It kicks off at the Stadio Sinigaglia at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

Recent findings suggest that you can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Manchester City vs. Napoli on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Lariani have been a true revelation in their first ever Champions League campaign, going into Matchday Five in fourth place after three victories and a lone defeat away to Feyenoord.

Injuries remain a concern, as Moise Kean is out until next year with that recurring shin glitch, and Assane Diao is still sidelined, with Maxence Caqueret not at 100 per cent.

Douvikas is the only available centre-forward, but has been on spectacular form and is the current Champions League top scorer on six goals.

Cesc Fabregas chooses from game to game between goalkeepers Jean Butez and Robert Sanchez.

Trevoh Chalobah makes his comeback in defence after sustaining a muscular injury on October 18.

Lucas Da Cunha, Nico Paz and Baturina form the trio in support of Douvikas.

AEK Athens are in 29th place after winning their Champions League opener against LASK, then losing three on the bounce to Shakhtar Donetsk, Manchester City and Real Madrid.

However, they fell only 3-2 at the 92nd minute against Real Madrid, having been in front twice.

Aboubakary Koita was sent off for dissent at the final whistle of that match and is therefore suspended, with midfielder Kervin Arriaga out injured.

Former Lazio goalkeeper Thomas Strakosha is given the gloves for the first time in the Champions League this season after recovering from a wrist injury and making his domestic comeback at the weekend.

Other familiar faces for Serie A include ex-Bologna full-back Charalampos Lykogiannis, former Cagliari midfielder Razvan Marin, Inter flop Joao Mario and ex-Milan and Fiorentina striker Jovic.

Martin Georgiev, Hakim Sahabo, Dereck Kutesa, Frantzdy Pierrot and Christos Alexiou are cup-tied.

Como: Sanchez; Couto, Ramon, Chalobah, Valle; Perrone, Milla; Da Cunha, Nico Paz, Baturina; Douvikas.

Como bench: Butez, Ginelli, Kempf, Dossena, Kaiki, Ricci, Smolcic, Liberali, Papaccioli, Mazzara.

AEK Athens: Strakosha; Rota, Moukoudi, Relvas, Pilos; Majer, R Marin, Vitalis, Kutesa; Jovic, Gacinovic.

AEK bench: Balamotis, Brignoli, Kairinen, Varga, Mantalos, Vida, Lykogiannis, Joao Mario, Zini.
