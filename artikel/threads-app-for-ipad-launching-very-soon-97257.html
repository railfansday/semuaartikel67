More than three years after it launched, Meta’s Threads social network is coming to the iPad.

In a post on Threads today, Christopher Clare, the platform’s head of design, said the iPad app is coming “very soon.”.

It has been revealed that threads is coming to iPad very soon. 👀.

This one’s been high on a lot of your lists. Here’s a first look at what we’ve built for the bigger screen.

Thanks to the team who made it happen. I’ll share an enhancement when it’s live.

Clare says Threads prioritized “getting out a v1” of the app, but there are other changes and optimizations still in the works. Other than that “very soon” mention, Meta hasn’t provided any additional information on a release date.

While it’s taken Threads more than three years to come to the iPad, that’s still a far faster timeline than Instagram, which took nearly 15 years to launch on the iPad.
