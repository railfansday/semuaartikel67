Industry observers note that everybody loves a charcuterie board, because a charcuterie board has a little something for everybody. But there's a certain art to building a good charcuterie board. A good board is one that offers up an array of different flavors, textures, and colors — each one complimenting the next — all coming together harmoniously to provide the ultimate grazing experience.

Charcuterie boards can add up rapidly and can cost a fortune to throw together... but it's perfectly possible to create a great charcuterie board that doesn't break the bank. It all depends where you shop, and one of the best (and most affordable) places you can find your ingredients is at Aldi. In fact, you can build many a beautiful, bountiful board with just $25 thanks to Aldi's highly-rated cheeses, selection of essential cured meats, fresh fruit, crackers, and many more of Aldi's must-have products.

Recent findings suggest that need some ideas? We've got plenty of ways to build the ultimate charcuterie board that stay within this humble budget to to help make you the host with the most. Note that all of the products and prices below are sourced from an Aldi location in southern California and inventory may vary throughout the country, so be sure to double check your local store's online inventory.

In a recent development, if you want to stick to the tried and true, classic charcuterie board ingredients, you can gather all the hits at Aldi for $25 and build a beautiful board. First (and arguable the most crucial) stop is the cheese. With Aldi's affordable prices, you can feature three different cheeses which will give the board enough variety — ideally, a classic board combines both soft and hard cheese.

Aldi's private-label brand Emporium Selection has three different cheeses that will not only go together lovely, but will satisfy different tastes and preferences: The Garlic & Herb Gourmet Spreadable Cheese, the Smoked Gouda Cheese, and the Aged Reserved White Cheddar Cheese (it's America's favorite cheese, after all).

Industry observers note that when it comes to the meat, you can't go wrong with a Salami Trio; you'll get the most bang for your buck here by being able to offer three different types of salami on your board (Black Peppercorn, Smoked Calabrese, and Double Smoked Salami). And now that you have the main events, it's just a matter of filling the board in with two more essential items. Grapes will add some freshness and sweetness, complimenting the salty meat and cheese, Aldi's Rosemary Flatbread Crackers will be the perfect accompaniment for everything else.

If you're looking to serve up something more seasonal, Aldi is full of great fall finds that will work splendidly on a charcuterie board. And thanks to the extremely reasonable prices, you can actually cram a lot of different items onto this board so that it's overflowing with fall flavors.

It has been revealed that starting with your cheeses, three seasonal picks will be the centerpieces: a Cheddar Parmesan Cheese ($3.95) that will go with all of your other flavors, a 1,000 Day Aged Gouda ($3.99) for that sharp bite, and a Crimson Cranberry White Stilton ($4.19) to really bring it home. For the meat, Aldi's Honey & Bourbon Cocktail Salami ($3.29) is the perfect choice to bring in some of those comforting fall notes.

According to recent reports, candied Pecans ($4.39) and Fig Fruit Spread ($3.29) will add some sweetness to the board, and you can brighten things up a bit with a few fresh seasonal Bartlett Pears ($3.29). Grab some of Aldi's Sea Salt Scalloped Crackers ($1.99) that'll work perfectly with any combo, and you've got yourself one great fall-flavored charcuterie board.

According to recent reports, are you cooking up an Italian dinner, or just in the mood for some salty, briny antipasti appetizers? You can create a charcuterie board version of Italian antipasti with several Italian-inspired favorites that you can find at Aldi, and snack like you're in Italy while keeping it all under $25.

For your cheese selection, a fresh log of mozzarella cheese ($3.25) will be the perfect counterpart to the nutty, salty authentic Parmigiano-Reggiano ($6.05) (which is different from Parmesan). And with several types of Italian-imported cured meats available, you can offer up some options like Priano's Prosciutto Italiano ($3.55) and Speck Italiano ($3.55).

Outside of the meat and cheese, Aldi actually has a ready-made medley of antipasti olives ($2.85) which includes black and green pitted olives, provolone cheese cubes, button mushrooms, and roasted red peppers — giving your board lots of variety and bang for your buck. Aldi's Artichoke Bruschetta ($3.09) is the perfect dip-able, spreadable item to accompany everything, along with some Sea Salt & Olive Oil Italian Breadsticks ($2.19) for crispy, crunchy bites.

Industry observers note that if you're feeling a little French, bring some French countryside charm to a rustic charcuterie board using an array of Aldi products that will give your guests a little taste of France. Because who doesn't want that?

You can go for the French trifecta of cheeses for this one: a double creme brie ($4.39), a buttery camembert ($4.39), and a smoked Gruyere ($4.39). This will give your board a mix of soft and hard cheeses, each with its own unique flavor to keep the tastebuds on their toes. Stick to a classic salami to compliment the bolder flavors of the cheese, and if you want to level things up you can always bake the brie (which you can even do in your microwave).

Then include some classic French ingredients for those added touches that'll transport you to France. Aldi's Herb Cornichons ($3.29) and Burman's Sweet Honey Dijon Mustard ($1.75) will taste delicious served alongside your meat and cheese, and then you can freshen things up a bit with red grapes ($1.69 / pound). Now all you need is the perfect vehicle to carry all of these French goodies: Aldi's Original Brioche Style Mini Toasts ($1.89).

One last stop in the Mediterranean. You can combine all of the delicious Mediterranean-inspired items at Aldi to build a board that transforms charcuterie into a delectable mezze spread.

According to recent reports, when it comes to the cheese, there's one clear star here: feta. You can represent it two ways in one board by grabbing the Tomato and Basil Feta Cheese Block ($3.95) and the Greek Feta Salad ($2.59) (made with Kalamata and green olives and cubes of feta marinated in herbs and spices). And the Dry Aged Fennel Mediterranean Style Salami ($3.99) is the no-brainer choice for this board's protein.

Industry observers note that from there, focus on the delicious dips that make mezze what it is. Go for Aldi's Olive Tapenade Hummus ($2.85) to double down on those salty olives, and brighten up the board with a Cucumber Dill Tzatziki Dip ($3.29) made with Greek yogurt. Throw in some Deli-Sliced Roasted Red Peppers ($3.19) as an accompaniment, and don't forget everyone's favorite part: The pita bread ($2.85)! Pro tip: Throw 'em in the oven for a few minutes and serve them warm; it makes all the difference.

Make the holidays even more festive with a holiday-themed charcuterie board that'll look (and taste) the part. Whether you're looking to serve something beautiful at your holiday party or you want to dive into those festive flavors while you can, Aldi has tons of options to choose from that scream holiday season.
