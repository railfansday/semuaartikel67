Sources indicate that if you’ve been thinking about getting an Apple Card, now might be a good time. Users who apply for an Apple Card through December 10 are eligible to earn double Daily Cash for their first year. Here are the details.

As spotted by MacRumors, Apple is offering a limited-time promotion that doubles Daily Cash rewards for upcoming Apple Card customers, which in practice means eligible owners can earn up to 6% cash back on purchases during their first year.

Sources indicate that if you’re not familiar with the Apple Card’s Daily Cash, Apple describes it as a “no points, no gimmicks” cash back program. Here’s how the organization explains it:.

Get Daily Cash back from a percentage of your purchases. Not a month from now. Every day. There’s no limit to how much you can get.

With the promo, which is available exclusively through this link, upcoming Apple Card owners will see these values double to 6% on purchases from Apple and select merchants using Apple Pay, 4% on purchases made with Apple Pay, and 2% on purchases made with the physical or virtual card.

Sources indicate that apple notes that there is a $1,000 limit on the additional Bonus Daily Cash earned through the promotion. Once that limit is reached, rewards return to the standard 1%, 2%, and 3% rates, even if the first year hasn’t ended. The usual “merchant offers may change at any time” disclaimer also applies to the higher cash back rates.

Still, this looks like a pretty compelling offer for anyone who was already considering applying for an Apple Card.
