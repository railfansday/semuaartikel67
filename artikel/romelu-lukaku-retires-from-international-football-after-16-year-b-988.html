Belgium’s all-time record goalscorer Romelu Lukaku has retired from international football at the age of 33.

The forward made the announcement on Monday, a day after being an unused substitute as Belgium beat Italy 4-1 in the Nations League.

Lukaku had been named in the Red Devils' squad for this month's Nations League fixtures, but missed last Thursday’s 2-1 win over Turkey with a hip injury and then failed to make it off the bench against Italy.

He scored 94 goals in 135 appearances for Belgium over a run that began with his debut in 2010.

The former Chelsea and Manchester United striker revealed his decision in a statement on his official Instagram page.

“Over these 16 years, I gave everything for the national team,” Lukaku remarked. “I experienced every emotion imaginable. I was lucky enough to play alongside extraordinary teammates, to whom I will always be grateful. I want to thank them all.

In a recent development, “I also want to thank all the coaches who pushed me to give my best and trusted me to help the team. It was a wonderful journey, and I cherished every moment.

“To the Belgian fans, thank you for all the support you have given our team. I am proud to have represented my country at four World Cups and three European Championships. I will never forget those moments.

“I am at peace with my decision to stop here. It was incredible. I enjoyed it immensely. I am grateful. Thank you.”.

His final Belgium appearance came in a 4-1 defeat by France last month.

He leaves the international stage six goals short of a century, having struggled with a number of injuries in recent years.

The Fenerbahce attacker was still a central figure at this summer's World Cup, finding the net against Latest Zealand, Senegal and the United States.

Those goals made him Belgium's outright leading scorer at World Cups with eight, moving him past the previous record-holder, Marc Wilmots.

Across a lengthy international career, Lukaku helped Belgium finish third at the 2018 World Cup. He also reached back-to-back European Championship quarter-finals with his country in 2016 and 2021.
