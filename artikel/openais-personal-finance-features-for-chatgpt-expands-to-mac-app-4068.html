📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that earlier this year, OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Finances. The feature started on the mobile app and website, but it’s been missing from ChatGPT’s desktop app. Starting today. ChatGPT Finances is now available on the Mac as well.

ChatGPT Finances originally launched in beta as a preview feature for paid accounts. It originally required a $100/month plan before coming to the $20/month tier over the summer.

Sources indicate that since October, ChatGPT Finances has been available for all prepares, including free users. The only requirement is that ChatGPT Finances is limited to users in the United States.

I do not think I will give all my financial data to ChatGPT. Thanks, but hard pass on this one.

OpenIA states users “can securely connect your financial accounts to find ways to save, understand your spending, and make sense of your investments—with answers grounded in your finances.”.
