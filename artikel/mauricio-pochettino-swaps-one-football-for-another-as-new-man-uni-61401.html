Mauricio Pochettino's United States farewell tour continued on Sunday night with a pitstop at an NFL showdown.

The manager has one game left in charge of the United States before parting ways with the national team to join Manchester United after agreeing a deal with the Premier League club earlier this week.

The Argentine will replace Michael Carrick at Old Trafford on a two-and-a-half year contract after United agreed to pay more than £10million ($13m) to buy him and his backroom staff out of their contracts with the US Soccer Federation.

It has been revealed that before his return to England, the 54-year-old is enjoying his swansong with the US. In between overseeing his final games in charge of the national team, Pochettino was spotted soaking in the action of the other variety of football.

The former Tottenham Hotspur boss attended the primetime NFL showdown between the Cincinnati Bengals and Pittsburgh Steelers at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, Ohio on Sunday night.

The head coach, sporting his blue USSF coat, was captured standing on the sidelines with members of his staff ahead of the Sunday Night Football matchup.

Mauricio Pochettino's United States farewell tour continued at the NFL on Sunday night.

The fresh Manchester United boss attended the Cincinnati Bengals' game at Paycor Stadium.

He was greeted by kicker Evan McPherson, long snapper William Wagner and punter Ryan Rehkow - members of the Bengals' special teams - on the turf before kickoff.

The US is set to face Haiti in the second-leg of their CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal at TQL Stadium in Cincinnati on Tuesday.

The game, which will take place around a mile north of the Bengals' Paycor Stadium, will mark Pochettino's final match in charge of the US national team.

He also took charge of the USA's 6-0 first-leg win against Haiti on Tuesday, which marked the first stop on his farewell tour.

Pochettino will begin his new role with Manchester United on Wednesday, immediately following Tuesday's second-leg. His first game will be against Liverpool at Anfield next Sunday.

He emotionally addressed his departure from the US setup and impending latest job in a press conference on Friday.

Flanked by US Soccer Federation bosses at the team’s headquarters in Georgia, an emotional Pochettino wiped away tears as he thanked the players and staff.

In a recent development, ‘I haven't prepared a speech, I'm talking with my heart,’ he noted. ‘It's difficult for me to talk now because I am a very emotional guy.

The Argentine was captured on the sidelines ahead of the game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

It has been revealed that ‘When these moments arrive, it is always tough. Normally it is the opposite – you have been sacked! You cry because you want to stay.

‘I think it was one of the most difficult decisions of our career, but you have to move forward.

According to recent reports, ‘Not too many opportunities can happen in your career with a side like Manchester United. That is difficult to turn down when it arrives.

Sources indicate that ‘In some part it’s a very sad decision, in another part we are professional and we are trying to make our dreams come true. We are excited for that step forward.'.
