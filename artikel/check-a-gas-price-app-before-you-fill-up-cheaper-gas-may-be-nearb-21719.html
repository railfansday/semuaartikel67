Check a Gas Price App Before You Fill Up – Cheaper Gas May Be Nearby

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, gas stations a few blocks apart can charge different prices for the same grade of gas, so compare them in a gas price app before your next fill-up. Pick the cheapest station on a route you already drive, since a detour of a few miles can cost more in gas than you save on a partial tank.

Industry observers note that regular gas averaged $4.01 a gallon nationally on Nov. 9, up from $3.07 a year earlier, according to AAA. Thanksgiving road trips add fill-ups away from home, at stations whose prices you don’t know.

Sources indicate that where a station sits affects what it charges. In a UC Santa Barbara study of stations in Santa Barbara County, the ones nearest freeway ramps tended to charge more than those on streets inside town, and so did stations near shopping centers and major roads. So at a highway exit, check prices a few blocks away from the ramp before you settle for the station beside it.

GasBuddy and Waze show prices their users report, and Google Maps lists prices when you search for gas stations. A user-reported price can be out of date, so check the sign before you pull in. Costco members can also see their warehouse’s current gas price in Costco’s app.

Say you fill a 15-gallon tank every week, and a station on your usual route charges 30 cents a gallon less than where you stop now. That’s $4.50 a fill-up, about $234 a year. A detour cuts into that. At $4 a gallon in a car that gets 25 miles per gallon, each mile costs 16 cents, so a station 2 miles off your route adds a 4-mile round trip, or 64 cents. On 15 gallons at 30 cents off, you still come out $3.86 ahead. For a station only 10 cents cheaper, though, a 5-gallon fill saves 50 cents, less than the 64-cent drive.

Recent findings suggest that timing helps a little. GasBuddy’s 2026 analysis found that in most states Sunday was the most reliably cheap day to fill up and Wednesday through Friday tended to cost more. Filling up on your state’s cheapest day instead of its priciest can save 4 to 9 cents a gallon.

Open the app when your gauge hits a quarter tank, while you still have the range to pass up the station in front of you.
