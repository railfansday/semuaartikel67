Latest Update: BMW M2 Driver Clocked at 170 MPH Tells Police He Wanted One Last Drive Before Trading It In: Cops

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Your typical 2023 BMW M2 has an electronically limited top speed of 155 miles per hour. However, the optional M Driver's Package raises that limit to 177 mph. According to the Arizona Department of Public Safety (AZDPS) Highway Patrol, one M2 driver was apparently determined to explore the upper limits of his car's performance before trading it in; a state trooper clocked him traveling 170 mph on the highway, and criminal charges followed.

The incident happened on November 9, 2026, on Arizona State Route 202 near Sky Harbor Boulevard in Tempe, Arizona. According to authorities, an AZDPS Highway Patrol State Trooper spotted the BMW traveling at a high rate of speed and passing other vehicles. Using a LIDAR device, the trooper reportedly recorded the vehicle traveling 170 mph in a posted 65-mph zone—an astonishing 105 mph over the limit.

Police were not going to engage in a pursuit at that speed, however, and said they decided to use the Highway Patrol Traffic Operations Center to track the vehicle's whereabouts. Eventually the BMW exited the highway, and police were then able to stop the car. At this time, the police reportedly noticed that the BMW didn’t have a license plate.

It has been revealed that police say the officer asked the driver how fast he thought he was going, and the man estimated his speed at approximately 110 to 115 mph. That's still far above the posted speed limit, but nowhere near the 170 mph that had him approaching Daytona 500 qualifying territory. The driver later claimed he hadn't realized just how fast he was traveling, according to authorities.

Why was he driving so fast? He reportedly told the trooper he was planning to trade in the BMW and wanted to take it for one last drive. Hopefully his new car will be slower than the M2, because as police pointed out in their social media post, at 170 mph, the BMW was traveling approximately 250 feet every second. A crash at that speed would be devastating.

The driver was, unsurprisingly, arrested for reckless driving and endangerment, due to his excessive speed and unsafe driving behavior.
