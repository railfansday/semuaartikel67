Qatar Airways Airbus A321LRs With Flat Bed Business Class Launching Soon

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that in the coming weeks, Qatar Airways is expected to take delivery of its first Airbus A321LR, which will be the carrier’s first narrow body aircraft featuring latest generation interiors, including flat beds. The airline will be introducing a “mini-Qsuite” type product on these planes, and we’ve now learned exactly when this new premium narrow body jet will enter service.

Industry observers note that qatar Airways currently has a total of 50 factory fresh Airbus A321-family aircraft on order. This includes 10 A321LRs and 40 A321neos — both of those are latest generation aircraft, with the A321LR featuring additional range.

The expectation is that the first A321LR will be delivered in the coming weeks, before the end of 2026. The 10 A321LRs should join the fleet pretty rapidly, and will then be followed by the A321neos. The A321LRs will be configured with 160 seats, including 16 business class seats and 144 economy class seats.

The A321LR has a maximum range of 4,000 nautical miles, so it can be used for many destinations in Europe, Africa, and Asia. Below is a map showing the rough range from Doha (though in reality it won’t be quite as far, accounting for winds, fuel reserves, etc.).

Now, for those paying close attention, I should acknowledge that Qatar Airways briefly flew six leased, all-economy A321neos, but those were just intended as a temporary solution, and are now flying for Sun PhuQuoc Airways, which is running an interesting operation.

It has been revealed that qatar Airways’ A321neo-family aircraft order goes back a long time, and involved a lot of drama. The order was first revealed in 2011, then the airline reconfirmed and upsized it in 2017. However, then former Qatar Airways CEO Akbar Al Baker got into a fight with Airbus over A350 production quality, and refused to take delivery of any Airbus planes.

It has been revealed that that turned into a massive court battle, which was eventually resolved. But in the process, Qatar Airways lost all of its Airbus delivery slots, which is why these planes are coming way later than initially expected.

Industry observers note that qatar Airways has been promising all along that it would introduce some sort of a next generation flat bed business class product on its Airbus A321LRs. As you’d expect, business class seats on narrow bodies sometimes differ from those on wide bodies, due to differing cabin constraints.

What can we expect onboard these Qatar Airways A321LRs in business class? Executive Traveller quotes Qatar Airways CEO Hamad Ali Al-Khater as saying that the fresh business class product will be based on the STELIA Aerospace OPERA platform.

It has been revealed that these planes will boast 16 business class seats, in a 1-1 configuration. This product offers reverse herringbone seats, and Qatar Airways plans to have doors at each seat as well, of course with its customized finishes.

The launch customer for the STELIA Aerospace OPERA platform was Air France on its A350s. However, that’s the wide body version of the seat, which is modified from the narrow body version.

When it comes to the narrow body version of the STELIA Aerospace OPERA platform, ITA Airways was the launch customer, on its A321neos.

Industry observers note that this is also the product that Etihad is installing on its A321LRs. ITA Airways and Etihad don’t have doors in business class, but Qatar Airways does initiative to offer it. Etihad also markets the first row of the cabin as first class (and that has doors), but I wouldn’t expect Qatar Airways to do something similar.

This is about as good as narrow body business class seats get, as it’s generally preferred to the herringbone seats you’ll find on other carriers, like JetBlue A321LRs, American A321XLRs, etc.

Qatar Airways now seems pretty certain about the delivery of its upcoming Airbus A321LRs, as the airline has officially put the first routes on sale, as flagged by AeroRoutes:.

When you pull up the above flights, you’ll now see the seat map for the A321LR, which confirms the 16-seat business class cabin.

In the coming weeks, Qatar Airways is expected to take delivery of its first of 10 Airbus A321LRs. This will be the first custom designed next generation narrow body that the airline takes delivery of. We knew the airline would offer flat bed seats on these planes, and now we have the details of that.

It has been revealed that the airline has gone with the STELIA Aerospace OPERA platform, the same product you’ll find on similar planes of Etihad and ITA Airways. However, unlike those airlines, Qatar Airways will have doors in business class. The planes will enter service by very early 2027, flying from Doha to both Vienna and Zurich.
