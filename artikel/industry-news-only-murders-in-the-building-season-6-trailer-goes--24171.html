Only Murders in the Building's Season 6 trailer is here, and the podcasters have well and truly left the building. Renamed Only Murders in London, Hulu's comedy mystery is headed across the pond for its sixth season, with Charles (Steve Martin), Oliver (Martin Short), and Mabel (Selena Gomez) hoping to discover what happened to their latest true crime podcast subject.

It has been revealed that season 5 ended with the death of fellow podcaster Cinda Canning (Tina Fey), who was found at the gate of the titular Arconia apartment building. Determined to investigate, Only Murders in the Building's intrepid crime-solving trio are now headed to London, the location of Canning's last podcast episode.

Sources indicate that judging from the trailer, it seems as though they'll swiftly encounter more than one culture clash —  as well as a smorgasbord of very European characters. The star-studded lineup of actors joining Season 6 includes Nicola Coughlan, Olivia Colman, Martin Freeman, Richard E. Grant, Jim Broadbent, former Spice Girl Geri Halliwell-Horner, and even Doctor Who's Tenth, Twelfth, and Thirteenth Doctors David Tennant, Peter Capaldi, and Jodie Whittaker.

Only Murders in London lands Dec. 8 on Hulu.
