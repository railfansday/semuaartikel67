It has been revealed that yep, he's back. Less than two weeks after Jensen Huang resigned from being CEO of Nvidia following reports of multiple affairs, the company's board have reinstated him "effective immediately".

Sources indicate that "The board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr Huang, after which the board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candour and ability to lead Nvidia," the company revealed. "In the process of reaching its decision, the board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.".

"The board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," added recently appointed chairman Harvey Jones, who'll remain in the post following Huang's return. Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO during Huang's short break, will go back to just being chief financial officer.

"I am honoured to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a upcoming industrial revolution," Huang declared. "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.".

It has been revealed that oh, and Nvidia's board added that the independent investigation they set in motion "in light of recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort" will "proceed to conclusion". Huang "fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it", they noted.

Since resigning amid the scandal, Huang had made it clear he'd be open to returning to his post if the Nvidia board okayed it and had several powerful people either call for his reinstatement or declare they'd consider hiring him. "The country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the success and prosperity of not only the company, but the American economy," one such advocate, US president Donald Trump, has reportedly posted to cursed hellcape Truth social. Huang is a "great patriot and national treasure", the Trumpeter added.
