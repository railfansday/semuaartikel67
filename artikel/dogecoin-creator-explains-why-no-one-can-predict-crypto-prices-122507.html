Dogecoin co-founder Billy Markus, who is known on X as Shibetoshi Nakamoto, has explained why, in his opinion, no one can predict the prices of cryptocurrencies.

Markus shared his opinion on Wednesday in a reply to an X post with a prediction that the price of Zcash (ZEC) would fall to $100.

"my opinion is that every single cryptocurrency has an arbitrary value mostly unrelated to anything except that people believe they can make more money if others buy it from them in the future and thus all future projections are pissing in the wind," the Dogecoin co-founder wrote.

Markus did not mention Zcash or any other token by name in his reply, and his post was about every single cryptocurrency on the market, including the one he created himself.

At the time of the post, ZEC was trading at around $728, up by 41.3% over the past year, according to price data on X.

In a recent development, the latest comment from Markus comes a week after Bitwise filed an S-1 registration statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a spot Zcash ETF. According to the filing, the fund is set to trade on the Texas Stock Exchange under the ticker ZEC, and it will not stake ZEC or use shielded transactions.

Industry observers note that markus created Dogecoin together with Jackson Palmer in December 2013. The two developers based the cryptocurrency on the Doge meme with a Shiba Inu dog, and they rolled out it as a joke about the crypto market of that time.

Industry observers note that markus has stated in the past that it took him a couple of hours to write the code of Dogecoin. In 2021, Markus also said on X that he had sold all of his DOGE in 2015 after he lost his job, and that he got about the price of a used Honda Civic for it.

Since then, Markus has stayed active on X under the Shibetoshi Nakamoto name, and he often replies to posts about crypto prices, forecasts and fresh tokens.

In June 2022, Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates spoke about crypto and NFTs at the TechCrunch Sessions: Climate conference.

"As an asset class, it's 100% based on the greater fool theory — that somebody's going to pay more for it than I do," Gates noted.

Earlier, at the Berkshire Hathaway annual meeting in April 2022, Warren Buffett said that he would not buy all of the Bitcoin in the world even for $25.

"If you told me you own all of the bitcoin in the world and you offered it to me for $25, I wouldn't take it because what would I do with it?" Buffett remarked.

According to recent reports, according to Buffett, Bitcoin does not produce anything. At the same meeting, Buffett compared it with farmland and apartment buildings, and he said that he would pay for a share of them because farmland produces food and apartments produce rent.
