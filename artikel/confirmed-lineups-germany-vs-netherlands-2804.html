With top spot in UEFA Nations League Group A2 up for grabs, Jürgen Klopp's Germany prepare for battle with neighbouring Netherlands at the Olympiastadion this evening.

Roughly an hour ahead of the scheduled 20:45 CET kick-off in Berlin, both head coaches have revealed their starting lineups for the game – you can view them in full below.

Germany (4-2-3-1): Ter Stegen – Treu, Tah, Schlotterbeck, Brown – Kimmich, Pavlović – Gruda, Wirtz, Scherhant – Havertz.

Netherlands (4-3-3): Verbruggen – Dumfries, Van Hecke, Van de Ven, J.Timber – Veerman, Reijnders, Q.Timber – Summerville, Malen, Gakpo.
