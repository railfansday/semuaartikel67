Industry observers note that nicolas Cage is a man with a wonderful reputation for excellent films. The most unique man in Hollywood won the Academy Award for Best Actor in 1996 for his role in Leaving Las Vegas, earned another nomination for 2002's Adaptation, and has appeared in several critically acclaimed masterpieces, such as 1987's Raising Arizona, 1997's Face/Off, and, more recently, 2022's The Unbearable Weight of Massive Talent, where he perfectly played an alternate version of himself.

Sources indicate that but Cage is also no stranger to negative headlines among his many hits. Recently, he was caught up in controversy after speaking out against modern superhero projects, following the cancellation of his hit series Spider-Noir, an offshoot of the animated Spider-Verse films (Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse and Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse). "When I see these 50-plus and otherwise intelligent and remarkably gifted thespians putting on these ridiculous outfits and getting ready for the fight," he noted, "it’s hard for me not to be reminded that the MCU is becoming a well-funded and glorified WWE.".

In a recent development, following these comments, Cage is back with his latest performance and is once again facing negative attention. The sports biopic, Madden, which stars Cage as the titular voice of football alongside fellow Oscar winner Christian Bale as Oakland Raiders owner Al Davis, is being torn apart by critics. The Prime Video release, which is also in limited theaters, has earned just 13% on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes at the time of writing. This is Cage's second-lowest score for a film on the review site this decade. The film is directed by five-time Academy Award nominee David O. Russell, based on a script he co-wrote with Cambron Clark.

"Madden is so desperate to be loved that, unlike Madden himself, it can’t be respected," wrote one critic in a particularly scathing review, with others calling the film "vapid, unfunny, and clunky," "pure sawdust," and "led by the kind of stunt acting from both principals that never lets you forget they are giving a performance." For ScreenRant, Matthew Rudoy was more positive, awarding a 6/10 score and calling the film "a surprisingly heartfelt biopic.".

Madden is available to stream on Prime Video. Stay tuned to Collider for all the latest movie news.
