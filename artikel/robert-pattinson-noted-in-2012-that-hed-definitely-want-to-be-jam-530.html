Robert Pattinson Noted in 2012 That He’d ‘Definitely’ Want to Be James Bond in ‘Like 20 Years’ When He’s Not So ‘Fresh-Faced’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Fourteen years before his official casting as James Bond, Robert Pattinson expressed interest in playing cinema’s most famous secret agent. The actor was speaking to the U.K. online publication Shortlist (via People) in the summer of 2012, a few months ahead of both his swan song as Edward Cullen in “The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2″ and the release of Daniel Craig’s third Bond movie “Skyfall,” when he was asked about becoming the next 007 actor.

“Yeah definitely, but more like 20 years,” Pattinson answered at the time. “I think there’d be nothing worse than, like, ‘Let’s get a fresh-faced Bond!’ That would be the worst idea in the world. After Daniel Craig, you have to have some baggage. It would be ridiculous to reinvent [him] as some young posh kid. Or, if [he] was, I’d have to get so tortured in the first few scenes. I’d have to do the first film with one arm or something.” Related Stories Keira Knightley Says ‘It Means Nothing' When Fans Quote ‘Love Actually’ to Her Because She Hasn’t Watched It Since the Premiere: ‘I Don’t Remember It’ Paul Bettany, Asha Banks, Gugu Mbatha-Raw and Colin Firth Team for Feel-Good Comedy 'It's Alright,' Protagonist Launches Sales at AFM (EXCLUSIVE).

In a recent development, it turns out Pattinson only needed 14 years to agree to sign on as the upcoming James Bond, taking over the franchise after Daniel Craig’s exit in “No Time to Die.” Pattinson will be the first 007 under the franchise’s new owner, Amazon MGM Studios. Denis Villeneuve, who directed Pattinson in “Dune: Part Three,” is helming the movie. Pattinson reportedly beat out contenders such as Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor and Nicholas Hoult in the final rounds of auditions.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson said in a statement about his casting as Bond. “To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”.

According to recent reports, villeneuve added in his own statement: “Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our fresh James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”.

According to recent reports, amy Pascal, who has produced the “Spider-Man” franchise and several 007 films, and David Heyman, the producer behind the “Harry Potter” series, are producing the new Bond film, with Tanya Lapointe executive producing. The script is by “Peaky Blinders” creator Steven Knight.
