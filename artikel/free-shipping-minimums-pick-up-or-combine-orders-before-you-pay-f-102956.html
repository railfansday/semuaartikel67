Skip the shipping fee by choosing store pickup where it’s free or by holding your cart until things you need reach the free-shipping minimum. Buy a shipping membership only if it costs less than a year of shipping fees on small orders. Add filler only when it costs less than the fee or you’d buy it anyway.

Say your cart is $12 short of a $35 minimum and shipping costs $6. A $12 filler item saves the fee but costs $12, so you pay $6 more than the fee alone.

The cart is a few dollars short and a cheap item closes the gap for less than the fee, or the item is something you’d buy within a few weeks anyway, like paper towels you’re running low on. Walmart+ costs $98 a year and waives Walmart’s $6.99 fee on orders under $35, so it takes 15 such orders a year to come out ahead. Target Circle 360 costs $99 a year, but paying with a Target Circle Card gets you free shipping on most Target items without it. For Amazon Prime, at $139 a year, divide that fee by the shipping charge Amazon reveals on a small order. Count its video and other perks only if you’d use them.

Before you pay for a membership, open the store’s order history and count last year’s orders that came in under its minimum.
