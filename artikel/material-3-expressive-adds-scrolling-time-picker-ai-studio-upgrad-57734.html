In a recent development, google has given the time picker component a Material 3 Expressive redesign, while adding a new scrolling variant. Meanwhile, Google AI Studio is boosting its Android app development experience.

According to recent reports, compared to Material 3, the updated M3 Expressive input and dial time pickers drop the “Select time” hint in the top-right corner, while the AM and PM buttons now benefit from expressive shape, color, and behavior. The hour and minute fields, as well as the dial selector, feature higher contrast.

The big addition today is the scroll time picker. Instead of using the keyboard or analog clock to pick the time, you can just vertically scroll when setting an alarm or scheduling an event.

Nooo, the scrolling variant is what I don't like about iOS — it takes forever to dial anything in it! Why would Google copy it?

The Material Design team indicates this “compact way to select time” is “best placed inline with other content.” A keyboard icon lets you switch to the input time picker.

Use scroll time pickers instead of dial pickers when you want people to see surrounding content.
