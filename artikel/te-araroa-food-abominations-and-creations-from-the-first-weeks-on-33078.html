This was Sven’s invention, the first one he let me try was made in a garlic flavoured wrap. I’m sorry to say that it was delicious and I ended up eating peanut butter cheese wraps for the next week of lunches. You do have to eat this with some candy or something high in sugar otherwise you’ll find yourself halfway up a major hill wanting to throw up because your body has been unable to process the fat in the cheese and peanut butter and you will have run out of energy. Or if you’re lucky you’ve just been through a town and can have a side of fresh fruit and vege.

The best blueberries I’ve ever had.

A concoction involving a rotating seasoning sachet du jour (this day it was carbonara) mixed in with a packet of tuna and some pasta. Honestly, once it was all mixed together it didn’t look bad at all. He claims he hasn’t gotten tired of it yet. He has it with a side of vanilla protein. This day he did forget to let the pasta soak so it was a little crunchy.

This was my invention at the Puhoi tea rooms. To be honest I didn’t think it was weird or heinous but literally everyone else did. I’d say I was the real winner here because Sinèad ended up ordering the same thing after me, and guess what?? It was GOOD. The man at the tea room called me weird about six times but still brought me my bacon and it only cost $12 total.

Bacon not pictured because he wouldn’t bring it out at the same time as the scones.

What was I supposed to do, NOT order bacon?

It has been revealed that mash, dehydrated peas, beef jerky and polenta. Mash, peas, TVP. Polenta, instant noodles, peanut butter. Just bits and pieces from various ziploc bags with boiled water and a stock cube and some salt. Again, this is my formula. And I love it. Your goo your way. Delicious. It just looks like nothing anyone would eat in normal life.

When you don’t feel like figuring out what to eat and you only have scraps left in your food bag so you shove a packet of beef jerky in a wrap and call it a day.

This night we hit a supermarket and all good over-zealous in the produce section. The result was an extremely filling but unfortunately low calorie dinner of hot chips and salad in wraps. My body was so happy to eat all the vegetables so it was worth the need to eat extra the next day.

Once in a while you’re blessed with knowledge of something you didn’t know you even needed to know. That’s how felt when I learned about single serve sachets of pizza sauce. So naturally I bought chorizo and Parmesan and ate trail pizzas for lunch for the next several days.

Not much to say about this one other than it SLAPS and you can swap out your yogurt flavours and your oat flavours to keep this fresh. Best consumed in your tent while packing. It also doesn’t look that nice and some days you don’t nail the amount of water so you end up with a drink instead of actual oats. Oh well!!!
