According to recent reports, care.com’s 2026 Cost of Care Report puts the average cost of a nanny for one child at $870 for a 40-hour week, or $21.75 an hour. At two-thirds of that, each family in a share pays about $580 a week instead of $870, roughly $15,000 less over a year.

It has been revealed that the nanny comes out ahead too. Two families paying $14.50 an hour each give the nanny $29 an hour, a third more than the $21.75 one family pays, for watching both families’ children at once.

At about $580 a week, a share still costs more than a day care center, which averages $332 a week in the same report. The extra $248 a week buys care in a home with one other family’s children instead of a center’s classroom.
