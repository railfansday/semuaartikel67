If you hadn’t heard of Dan Burn and somebody detailed his journey in football, there is no way you would believe it.

Recent findings suggest that santiago Munez and Goal! sounds like a very believable factual documentary in comparison.

Dan Burn one of countless kids taken on by the Newcastle United Academy but don’t make it.

In his case, it was at the age of 11 when told his dream ended there.

It has been revealed that the rest is history. Though you would be forgiven if you double checked certain facts because they sound like the work of somebody’s very active imagination.

Dan Burn who entered the world of men’s football with Darlington at non-league level, whilst working part-time collecting shopping trolleys at ASDA.

Through grit, determination and of course ability, Dan Burn developed a very decent career for himself, playing for Fulham, Yeovil, Wigan, Birmingham and Brighton.

This career not standing out as a stellar one, but certainly a very decent life in football, with a fair few Premier League appearances amongst his 300+ games across the years.

Then just over three months ahead of his 30th birthday, Dan Burn got the call that was going to change his life forever.

Inheriting a desperate mid-season relegation fight when taking the Newcastle United job in November 2021, two months later Eddie Howe was looking to bring in ready to play Premier League experienced players to avoid instant disaster for the then upcoming Newcastle United owners.

It has been revealed that kieran Trippier, Chris Wood and Matt Targett arrived at St James’ Park in January 2022, with Brighton reluctantly allowing Dan Burn to join that trio for a reported £13m transfer fee. Eddie Howe and the Newcastle United owners were ridiculed by many journalists, pundits and fans of other clubs. Tagged as the ‘richest’ club owners in world football and yet they and their manager thought Dan Burn was the kind of signing they needed. Hilarious. Bruno Guimaraes also arrived in that same transfer window but he was aimed at the longer-term, new to the Premier League it would be March 2022 before he started a game for NUFC, by which point United were well on their way to safety with Burn, Targett and Wood playing key roles in that, Kieran Trippier also started well but very quickly picked up a significant injury and only returned at the very end of that season.

With relegation avoided, Eddie Howe brought in the exciting young Dutch left-footed central defender Sven Botman, who when he was 20 turning 21 played a key role in a stunning Ligue 1 title success for Lille.

With the summer 2022 signing of Botman, I don’t think any Newcastle fan would have been surprised if Dan Burn had then ended up becoming a squad player. Instead he became a player that Eddie Howe picked every match with very few exceptions. Matt Targett picked up an injury very early that 2022-23 season and Dan Burn was moved to left-back.

The back five that played almost every Premier League match that 2022-23 season was Pope, Trippier, Schar, Botman Burn.

The ‘experts’ couldn’t believe it, Eddie Howe and Newcastle United finished fourth in the Premier League and qualified for the Champions League, plus NUFC got to their first Wembley final in 24 years.

That success was massively built on an astonishing defensive record.

Manchester City won the 2022-23 Premier League title only conceding 33 goals in the 38 league matches, this Newcastle United five matched that low goals conceded total. In the six Carabao Cup matches that got United to Wembley, only two goals were conceded across the half dozen matches.

Eddie Howe had created something very special at Newcastle United and Dan Burn was one of the first names on the team sheet, whether playing in the centre of defence or left-back.

Dan Burn finally living the Newcastle United dream in his 30s.

In a recent development, he was somebody the fans could really relate to, many of us kidding ourselves that if only things had worked out a little differently, that could have been you or me instead of Dan Burn in a Newcastle United shirt. That wasn’t the reality of course but that is how Dan Burn makes you feel. That he is a Newcastle United fan who by that sheer grit and determination is playing for NUFC, rather than watching them.

We have had many other homegrown heroes, many in my lifetime, but Alan Shearer had this incredible talent for scoring goals that elevated him above all others in the Premier League, whilst Peter Beardsley and Paul Gascoigne just had incredible talent that for me matches any player I have ever seen play football for any club or country.

As for Dan Burn, he has continued to be phenomenal in the colours of Newcastle United.

The 2023-24 was one of the worst ever for Newcastle United injuries, all but mission impossible for Eddie Howe and his small squad to lose so many players so often, plus the disastrous Sandro Tonali suspension. With the squad hit by so many injuries, when Dan Burn picked up a serious one to his back, he came back far earlier than the medical staff wanted him to because the manager, team and fans needed him. Newcastle United still managed 60 EPL points that 2023-24 season and came so close to qualifying from their Champions League group which was as tough as it could possibly be. One opponent reached the final and another the semi-final. United were cheated in Paris out of a famous win that would have led to them qualifying instead of PSG. In the stunning 4-1 home win over PSG, Dan Burn was immense at the back and scored THAT goal.

The finances meant Newcastle United didn’t buy any new first team players in summer 2024 and indeed a PSR crisis that had to be dealt with in June 2024 meant Elliot Anderson and Yankuba Minteh were sold. Dan Burn and his Newcastle United team-mates reacted by producing the most successful season in living memory for most of us. Another Champions League qualification. Oh..and a trophy. The unforgettable sight of Dan Burn scoring the best headed goal Wembley (new or old) has ever seen. Burn one of United’s very best players that magical day, a trophy finally lifted on 16 March 2025.

Industry observers note that the 2025-26 season proved far too much for a still too small Newcastle United squad to cope with a 58 game season, especially as injuries piled up once again. Including Dan Burn the victim of a cowardly challenge during the away derby mix, the Geordie warrior wanted to try and play on, even though it was later discovered he’d punctured a lung and broken a rib. As always, his commitment was immense during such a challenging season.

It has been revealed that the perfect choice to be the fresh Newcastle United captain, injury delayed his 2026-27 season kicking off. No surprise that things significantly improved at the back when Dan Burn was able to start in the Premier League. After conceding 14 goals in the opening seven Premier League matches, the next three saw only two conceded (against Palace, Everton and Fulham). Then Dan Burn leading Newcastle United to Saturday’s comeback draw against an Arsenal side that had won nine of their opening ten EPL matches.

Dan Burn getting absolutely appalling abuse from Newcastle United fans.

In recent days a video has circulated online, Dan Burn stopped and asked for one of those one minute video interviews, where random questions are fired and only need a name as the answer.

I’m not sure when the video interview happened but my guess is that Dan Burn was stopped as he went into the Newcastle United Foundation dinner last week, which was to raise funds for the charity and to celebrate local achievers.

So just in case you were still wondering which answer has prompted all of this abuse, it is Dan Burn saying Alexander Isak is the best player he has played with.

That this then justifies saying what a useless football player Dan Burn is AND far far worse, these fans aiming abuse at him as a person, his character.

Everybody is of course entitled to their own opinion BUT I can’t even attempt to get my head around why anyone would have this opinion AND even worse, be desperate to tell everyone else.

The worst thing about social media is that everybody can now have their say.

It is like those fans who now want us to believe that Eddie Howe simply got lucky with the success he brought to NUFC, that he just stumbled along and it as all down to others why Newcastle United were suddenly winning football matches, getting to Wembley, playing in the Champions League and….winning a trophy.
