Sources indicate that the United Video Game Workers-CWA union has released a report with details on the hardship fund it started in collaboration with Necrosoft Games earlier this year benefiting laid-off developers. The UVW reports that through its itch.io bundle and from monies received via union dues and outside donations, it raised $290,000 over two weeks. All that cash has been dispersed to over 150 devs from across the industry to help with expenses like rent, medical bills, and other debt.

"This came at a time when I deeply, deeply needed a hand to pull me up while I'm drowning.".
