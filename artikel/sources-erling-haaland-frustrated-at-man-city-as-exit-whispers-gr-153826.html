Erling Haaland has grown frustrated at Manchester City as a result of their recent run of poor form, sources have told Football Insider.

The 26-year-old sets extremely high standards, both for his team and for himself, and is not happy with the way those standards have dropped on the pitch in recent weeks.

This comes amid speculation about his long-term future at the Etihad, with the likes of Real Madrid keeping a close eye on his situation in case he becomes available.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Haaland's frustrations are not a major concern for Man City, but they have grown nonetheless.

The Norway international has scored nine goals in 11 Premier League games this season, but has not found the net in four games, adding to his recent disillusionment.

Haaland is believed to be just as frustrated with his own displays as with Man City's, but against a backdrop of speculation about his future, doubts have started to creep in.

Football Insider revealed last month that Man City have no intends to entertain offers for Haaland in the near future, as he remains central to their long-term plans.

It has been suggested previously that the striker's future may hinge on whether Man City's appeal against their 114 charges is successful, and what punishment may follow.

However, with Haaland now growing frustrated at Man City, new questions have been asked about his future, with clubs keeping a close eye on his situation.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There's always going to be interest in Erling Haaland because such a brilliant player that he is, world-class, guaranteed goals.

Recent findings suggest that "I think maybe it's just a bit of frustration from Haaland on City's disjointed run right now. Obviously, they've not picked up many wins. They've dropped points in the Premier League and in the Champions League as well.

In a recent development, "Personally it's a bit of frustration as well. Haaland has not scored in his last three games now, which is pretty much almost unheard of during his time at the Etihad.

"So it's probably just more frustration on City's run of results and maybe his lack of goals as well in recent weeks as well that's led to maybe some of those question marks over him.".

Should Haaland decide he wants to leave Man City at any stage in the near future, there will be no shortage of suitors, given his status as one of the world's best players.

Spanish giants Real Madrid and Barcelona have both been long-term admirers of the striker and are frequently linked with moves to lure him away from English football.

Meanwhile, former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider on 3 November that Arsenal and Paris Saint-Germain could join their Spanish rivals in their pursuit.

The Gunners are seen as the only English club who could lure the striker away from the Etihad, both financially and in terms of their success on the pitch.

However, it is expected that if Haaland was to leave Man City, his most likely move would be to Real Madrid.
