Latest Update: tvOS 27.2.1 launching soon with Siri AI for older Apple TV 4K models

Today the newly introduced Apple TV 4K has arrived (order here) with Siri AI as a major tentpole feature. But older Apple TV 4K models are about to get Siri AI too via a tvOS 27.2.1 software update.

Earlier this week, iOS 27.2.1 debuted alongside companion updates like macOS 27.2.1, watchOS 27.2.1, and more.

According to recent reports, notably missing was tvOS 27.2.1. But the update’s absence now makes more sense, as Apple has confirmed that tvOS 27.2.1 introduces Siri AI support for select Apple TV 4K models.

Apple’s website has a newly updated support document that lists requirements for Siri AI on Apple TV. It says you need:.

tvOS 27.2.1 on Apple TV 4K (2nd generation and later) and a Siri AI-enabled, paired device nearby.

Although Apple has not yet specified launch timing for tvOS 27.2.1, it could launch as soon as today, or else early next week.

Because the latest Apple TV 4K just now debuted, it’s understandable that Apple wouldn’t want to ship Siri AI for older models before the new one arrived.

As Apple’s website claims, you’ll need a 2nd generation Apple TV 4K or later plus another paired Siri AI-enabled device, like a recent iPhone, to get the new Siri experience in tvOS.
