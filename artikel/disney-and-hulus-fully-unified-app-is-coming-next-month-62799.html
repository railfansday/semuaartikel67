Disney has unveiled a major milestone coming next month in its prepares to merge the Disney+ and Hulu apps, which will be “fully unified” into Disney+ soon.

Today Disney reported its quarterly earnings and gave an patch on plans to merge Disney+ and Hulu into one app.

Live TV and premium add-ons were the final Hulu features that hadn’t yet been imported to the Disney+ app. But by adding those capabilities into Disney+, the app unification will be “complete.”.

Sources indicate that the branding is going to get messed up. People look at Disney+ and Hulu as two different unique things. If you make the name just Disney+ people will think what Hulu historically provided is gone.

I find the current integration of Disney+ and Hulu cumbersome. I always find myself going back to my Hulu app to watch content. This may end up being a terrible move for them.

The ultimate goal of unifying everything in Disney+, though, will be accomplished soon.

Industry observers note that also in the earnings report, Disney noted that creator content will start appearing in the Disney+ app in December. This is related to the company’s deal with TikTok that debuted after its previous OpenAI deal fell apart when Sora was killed off. The Disney+ app will also get “more vertical video, live sports, and podcasts” soon too, Disney said.
