Mormon Wives’ Bobbi Althoff and Boyfriend Tyler Hawkins Break Up

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bobbi Althoff and Tyler Hawkins are benching their relationship.

The basketball star revealed that he and the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star split two months ago, almost two years after they started dating in November 2024.

It has been revealed that “It wasn't a bad breakup. There wasn't one thing that led to us splitting,” Hawkins assured fans in a Nov. 16 TikTok. “I just think that we were two very different people, and I think that over time we realized we weren't as compatible as we thought we were. And that's not a knock on her. I think she's amazing.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I have nothing but love for her and her family. They were great to me,” he added. “I just think that we realize we might be better as friends rather than partners.”.

Sources indicate that the 27-year-old explained that he wanted to set the record straight for fans because he started getting questions about his and Althoff’s relationship following the Nov. 12 premiere of SLOMWOC.

As for where he and the 29-year-old stand now? Hawkins shared that he wouldn’t “change anything” about the two years they had together.

“I'm forever grateful that we got the chance to experience life together for that time period,” he said. “I truly just wish her the best. I hope she finds her person. I hope she's successful in whatever it is that she chooses to do. But it's really all love.”.

Still, it was “crazy" for him to watch back his scenes on Mormon Wives: OC, which gave a glimpse into their romance.

Sources indicate that “Actually seeing us as a couple, knowing that we aren’t together, was tough,” he told People in an interview published Nov. 16. “I miss her and everything and the life that we had.”.

As for Althoff, while the Not This Again podcast host—who shares two daughters with ex-husband Cory Althoff—has not publicly spoken about the split, she was previously candid about the hurtful comments she received when she would post about Hawkins online.

“We've been together for a year,” Bobbi stated through tears in a November 2025 TikTok video, “but without fail, every single time I post him, all the comments are about how I'm ‘ran through’ and, ‘Oh, look, another one,’ or, ‘He probably doesn't know how ran through she is.’ Just mean, mean things.”.

“As much as I wish that these comments didn't get to me, I'm human,” she continued. “If you got this many negative things noted about you every day, it would get to you, too.”.
