Atlanta-based investment adviser Arkadios Wealth Advisors, with $8.79 billion in client assets, has disclosed a holding in an XRP exchange-traded fund (ETF) in its most recent quarterly filing, according to BankXRP.

The firm joins a growing list of known XRP fund holders, including Bank of America, National Bank of Canada, and trading firm Susquehanna International Group.

Arkadios submitted its Form 13F filing to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on Nov. 23. The filing highlighted securities worth roughly $7.38 billion across 1,880 holdings as of Sept. 30.

Another regulatory filing reveals $8.79 billion in total assets under management.

Recent findings suggest that however, the Nov. 24 post from BankXRP does not reveal the XRP fund's name, the number of shares held or the dollar value of the investment.

Susquehanna reported one of the largest increases in holdings of the Bitwise XRP ETF during the quarter.

In a recent development, the trading firm held 878,786 shares valued at $14.6 million as of Sept. 30, compared to 53,116 shares at the end of June.

Susquehanna added 825,670 shares of the Bitwise XRP ETF, making it one of the fund's bigger reported holders.

The next-largest reported holding was Goldman Sachs, with 2.31 million shares worth $38.4 million.

It has been revealed that susquehanna also increased its holdings in the Franklin XRP ETF by adding 399,571 shares. This brought its total to 421,700 shares worth $6.82 million.

The firm also reported 199,374 shares in the Grayscale XRP fund, worth $5.74 million.

Susquehanna's combined holdings in Bitwise, Franklin and Grayscale XRP products were valued at around $27.2 million at the end of the quarter.

Industry observers note that national Bank of Canada also appeared in the filings, but with a much smaller position. It held 330 shares of the Bitwise XRP ETF, worth roughly $5,500 as of Sept. 30.

The Bitwise fund holds XRP directly. This means investors can gain exposure to the token through a security they can buy in their brokerage accounts.

Two other investment managers reported holdings in the Volatility Shares XRP ETF, which trades under the ticker XRPI.

The fund uses futures contracts to gain exposure to XRP's price movements.

EP Wealth Advisors increased its XRPI holdings by 4,975 shares during the third quarter, a 37% increase from the previous quarter.

Industry observers note that the firm held 18,400 shares as of Sept. 30, valued at $147,936.

Bank of America reported a holding of 13,260 shares of the same ETF, worth roughly $106,600.
