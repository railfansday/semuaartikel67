No one feels the electric touch of Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss’ reunion quite like Jennifer Lawrence.

Industry observers note that after the Grammy winner and supermodel came together to cheer on Swift’s husband Travis Kelce as his Kansas City Chiefs took on the Fresh York Jets at Arrowhead Stadium on Nov. 8, the Hunger Games alum shared her theory as to why the pair finally reached a state of grace after years of feud rumors.

In a recent development, “That’s what happens when you get old,” Lawrence quipped to Andy Cohen during her Nov. 19 appearance on Watch What Happens Live. “The things that you fought about, like it just seem silly.”.

As she put it, “It just seems like elder abuse to me.”.

Though Swift, 36, and Kloss, 34, proved there’s no bad blood while cheering on the “Opalite” hitmaker’s guy on the Chiefs earlier this month, that wasn’t always the case.

In fact, speculation surrounding their friendship began after the former Victoria’s Secret Angel’s name was noticeably left off a T-shirt that Swift wore in her 2017 music video for “Look What You Made Me Do”, which featured a list of her girl squad.

And though Kloss attempted to calm down the speculation by sharing a photo with Swift backstage at the Reputation tour in 2018, the “I Knew It, I Knew You” singer did not attend Kloss’ wedding to Kushner—with whom she shares kids Levi, 5, Elijah, 3, and Rae, 13 months—a few months later.

At the time, no one was more concerned with the significant reputation of their friendship than Lawrence.

"I'd like to know what's going on with Karlie Kloss and Taylor Swift, that's the honest to God truth,” the Silver Linings Playbook actress stated in a 2018 interview with The New York Times. "Is nobody else curious? It's keeping me up at night. What happened?”.

But it seems their friendship is safe and sound these days. After all, Kloss was even in attendance for the latest chapter in Swift and Kelce’s love story as they tied the knot at Madison Square Garden July 3.

And while Kloss was hush-hush about the ins and outs of the ceremony, she made it all too clear that she and Swift’s friendship has seen daylight once again.

Sources indicate that “Listen, I try to protect the privacy of my friends’ personal lives,” Kloss said on Today July 28. “But what I will say is that they’re an amazing couple and I’m so happy for them.”.

May the odds be ever in your favor as you keep reading for more of Lawrence’s best quotes….

When she was nominated for her first Best Actress Oscar at 20, for the 2010 indie drama Winter's Bone, Jennifer Lawrence made quite the impression right out of the gate, setting the tone for endless interviews to come.

"I just ate a Philly cheese steak and I was kinda worried about it," the star, clad in her instant-classic red Calvin Klein dress, shared with Entertainment Tonight on her first Oscars red carpet when told she looked incredible.

"You know, I think that people are built the way that they're built," she continued. "You know, there's that quote, the Kate Moss quote, that's like 'Nothing tastes as good as skinny feels'? "And I'm like, I can name a lot of things that taste better than skinny feels: potatoes, bread...a Philly cheese steak and fries!".

This is not the outfit Lawrence was wearing when, in her words, she stalked John Stamos at a party.

"He was at a party, and I turned into a perverted guy," she said on Conan in 2013. "I was like following him into rooms and staring at his ass.".

According to recent reports, lawrence explained, "I lost my mind. And I got so upset—I didn't want to meet him because when I meet people it's never good, and I was avoiding him. But then my friends brought him over because my friends are jerks, and I started really freaking out. And he asked me if I was on mushrooms and I noted, 'No. I'm dead sober. This is just me...'.

"I just kept smiling and being like, 'Dude... Uncle Jesse.'".

Nick Jonas alerted Stamos to the interview by tweeting at him, "I envy you," to which Stamos replied, "I envy myself!" The Full House star added, "For the first time, I was totally oblivious.".

"You guys are just standing up because you feel bad that I fell—and that's really embarrassing, but thank you," the Silver Linings Playbook star mentioned upon finally reaching the stage to accept her Oscar for Best Performance by an Actress in a Leading Role in 2013, making the 22-year-old the second-youngest best actress winner ever.

Though the incident did indeed establish her reputation as a stumbler, the standing ovation was for a role well played.

And maybe a little bit because she fell.

According to recent reports, "Was that on purpose? Absolutely," Lawrence joked backstage, Oscar in hand. "What do you mean, 'what happened?' Look at my dress," she added, pointing to the massive skirt of her Dior ballgown. "I tried to walk upstairs in this dress. That's what happened...Yeah, I think I just stepped on the fabric, and they wax the stairs.".

Asked what went through her mind in that moment, she replied, "A bad word that I can't say, that starts with 'F.'".

Recalling the moment a year later to W, Lawrence explained, "I was at the Oscars, waiting to hear if my name was called, and I kept thinking, Cakewalk, cakewalk, cakewalk. I thought, Why is 'cakewalk' stuck in my head? And then, as I started to walk up the stairs and the fabric from my dress tucked under my feet, I realized my stylist had told me, 'Kick, walk, kick, walk.' You are supposed to kick the dress out while you walk, and I totally forgot because I was thinking about cake! And that's why I fell.'".

Lawrence later wondered if the fall was the beginning of a descent.

She already felt that her perch at the top of the Hollywood food chain was "precarious," noting on the Newly introduced York Times' The Interview podcast in 2025, "That’s just the nature of things.".

"And then I fell getting my Oscar," she continued, "and the next year I was waving to fans and I tripped on a cone and I remember being like: “[Expletive], that’s it. Nobody’s gonna believe that I fell two years in a row.”.

So, when she was accused of staging a fall, "Everybody thought that meant everything that I did was fake and it was all a shtick. This is how it felt to me. That I got found out as this fraud.".

Why She Identifies as a "Stay-at-Home Mom".

“I identify as a stay-at-home mom," Lawrence, who shares two young sons with husband Cooke Maroney, said on a January 2026 episode of SmartLess. "But I am obviously working.".

Industry observers note that she explained, “I’m not used to being busy like this. And then, for two weeks, you promote. It is hectic.”.

"Fortunately," she added, "my husband is the greatest father in the entire world, so when I’m working, I don’t have any more guilt than the usual every day, all-day parent guilt.".
