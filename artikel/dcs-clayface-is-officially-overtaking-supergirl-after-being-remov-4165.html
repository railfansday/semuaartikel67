According to recent reports, it's been a complicated few days over at Warner Bros. subsidiary DC Studios, which saw a major leadership change following the removal of co-CEOs James Gunn and Peter Safran. The studio's slate remains up in the air, even though Gunn has a newly introduced Superman movie locked and loaded for a 2027 release. Gunn and Safran's tenure began with the promise of making movies that don't rely on expanding shared universes and are green-lit only if their scripts are up to the mark. While the inaugural film, Superman, received mostly positive reviews, things came tumbling down in a matter of months.

The DC Universe franchise's second major-screen offering, Supergirl, emerged as one of the biggest flops of 2026. The movie performed so poorly that it's set to be overtaken by the franchise's latest feature, Clayface. Not many would have chosen a standalone anti-hero spin-off as the third installment of one of the most lucrative film franchises in history, and this is perhaps what led to Gunn and Safran's departure. The understanding is that the studio wants the most popular DC characters to pave the way for a Justice League film.

It has been revealed that unlike Supergirl, which received mixed reviews from critics earlier this year, Clayface has received mostly positive reviews. Directed by James Watkins, the body horror film is now sitting at a "Certified Fresh" 78% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, where the consensus reads, "Carving out a unique identity for itself in the DCU while closely resembling a litany of body horror classics, Clayface takes time to get going until it eventually morphs into an icky and satisfying revenge thriller." Even more impressive is the film's "Verified Hot" 94% audience score on the aggregator website.

Meanwhile, Supergirl now holds a 52% score on Rotten Tomatoes. Directed by Craig Gillespie and starring Milly Alcock, the film grossed $126 million worldwide against a reported budget of more than $180 million. Of this total, $72 million came from domestic theaters. Clayface has so far grossed $63 million domestically and is just $5 million shy of surpassing Supergirl worldwide. It has already beaten Supergirl in terms of international box-office revenue.

Produced on a reported budget of $40 million, Clayface stars Tom Rhys Harries, Naomi Ackie, and Max Minghella. The movie opened in 3,468 theaters nationwide and is now playing in 2,284 locations. The movie will debut on the PVOD market on December 8. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
