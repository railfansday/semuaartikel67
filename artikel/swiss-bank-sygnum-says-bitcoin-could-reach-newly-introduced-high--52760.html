Swiss Bank Sygnum Says Bitcoin Could Reach Newly introduced High Near $128,000

🌐 Source: Bitcoinmagazine • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (680 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Industry observers note that sygnum Bank estimates that bitcoin could reach a new all-time high near $128,000.
  • The report also frames the current downturn as the shallowest bear market for bitcoin to date.
  • The outlook arrives as bitcoin trades near $63,000 after six straight weekly losses and a drop below its 200-week moving average.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that sygnum Bank estimates that bitcoin could reach a new all-time high near $128,000. The Swiss digital asset bank laid out the scenario in its 2027 Sygnal outlook on Thursday, alongside a call that bitcoin and ether will both set new highs while the broader market stays narrow.

The report also frames the current downturn as the shallowest bear market for bitcoin to date. Sygnum puts the drawdown at around 54%, compared with declines of more than 70% in previous cycles, and claims digital assets enter 2027 with lower structural volatility and stronger fundamentals.

The outlook arrives as bitcoin trades near $63,000 after six straight weekly losses and a drop below its 200-week moving average. Sygnum’s case is that the market has changed since earlier cycles, not that the slide is over.

Sygnum describes a more mature market. Volatility is increasingly driven by broader dollar liquidity and market structure, it mentioned, and institutions have added upcoming channels through ETFs, leveraged trading and crypto-backed credit. By the end of October, stablecoin supply stood near $298 billion and tokenized real-world assets exceeded $39 billion.

It has been revealed that the estimate rests on a rotation out of technology stocks. Digital assets are worth about 6% of the Nasdaq’s market value, and Sygnum calculates that if 0.75% of Nasdaq value, roughly $360 billion, moved into crypto, total market capitalization could rise by more than 60%.

In a recent development, in that scenario, bitcoin would reach a latest high near $128,000. The report does not put a date on it and describes the figure as an estimate built on Sygnum’s own calculations, not a price target. It also notes that a sell-off in AI stocks could spill into crypto. Right now, Anthropic’s record $81 billion IPO is set to open about 25% to 30% above its $83 offering price, which has lifted AI and chip stocks, but worries about higher interest rates have pulled the Nasdaq back from an early 2% gain to about 0.3%.

Recent findings suggest that behind the price call is a shift in how institutions treat bitcoin. Sygnum predicts it will move out of the tactical sleeve of portfolios and into the “strategic reserve bucket,” competing with gold for capital that wants protection against currency debasement. BlackRock, Morgan Stanley and Bank of America each recommend allocations of 1% to 4%.

Sources indicate that the report states 2027 should reward selection over broad market exposure. Sygnum expects adoption to concentrate in stablecoins, tokenized assets, bitcoin as an institutional macro asset and tokens with proven usage, and it believes investors will be rewarded for choosing assets with real usage and revenue.

The math is simple, according to the report. Bitcoin is worth about 6% of gold’s market value, so moving just 1% of gold’s value into it would lift its market capitalization by at least 17%.

Large holders have already shown that appetite. BlackRock has mentioned security concerns are pushing bitcoin whales into its spot ETF, even as miners such as MARA have sold coins to raise cash.

Recent findings suggest that sygnum also expects tokens that return revenue to holders to outperform, stablecoin settlement at Visa to triple to $60 billion by the fourth quarter of 2027, and carry trades, which pair long spot with short futures, to beat Treasury bills. That comes after the Federal Reserve’s September rate rise to 3.75%–4.00% and 67 straight days of negative bitcoin funding rates in 2026. It describes regulation as clearer but still incomplete, with Europe’s MiCA regime now fully operational and U.S. agencies still advancing rules for crypto markets.

Fabian Dori, Sygnum’s chief investment officer and lead author, remarked volatility “has not disappeared; it has just migrated from the asset itself to its plumbing,” and that institutional adoption “has kept on building.”.

“To understand the shape of 2027 markets, investors must look past price to fundamentals,” Dori mentioned.
