Gary Neville has urged Mauricio Pochettino to sell five Manchester United players at the end of the season.

Pochettino has been brought in as Michael Carrick’s replacement and will arrive just three days before United’s next Premier League fixture away to Liverpool on November 22.

After a poor start to the season, United sit 18th in the Premier League with just one win in 10 games and are out of the Carabao Cup after a defeat at home to Brighton in September.

United’s players have been booed by supporters at Old Trafford but Neville believes Diogo Dalot has been made an ‘easy target’ and has defended the Portuguese full-back because of his work-rate.

Neville also feels Pochettino will look to move on Noussair Mazraoui, Matthijs de Ligt, Mason Mount, Marcus Rashford and Joshua Zirkzee at the end of the campaign, and expects Lisandro Martinez to leave when his contract expires next summer.

‘Do I think Dalot is Denis Irwin? No. Let’s be really clear, Denis Irwin is the greatest full-back Manchester United have ever had, I shouldn’t be mentioning him in the same breath, but I would get rid of a few before Dalot,’ Neville noted on Stick to United.

According to recent reports, ‘Dalot got booed last week at Old Trafford and I was really surprised at that. Dalot’s been shoved onto that left-hand side constantly. Amorim had him at left-wing back dying, it was awful to watch. If you put me at left wing-back or left-back, and I’m a right-back, by the way, and he’s a right-back, I’d be like, ‘not a chance, what are you putting me there for?’.

It has been revealed that ‘So he’s literally sticking his hand up and saying, ‘go on, I’ll play there. I’ll do anything for the team.’ I never see him shirk anything, right? He’s not an amazing player, let’s be clear. He never shirks anything, him. I never look at him and think he could work harder. Ever.

‘I actually think, to be fair, if he was in a decent back four, he’d be all right. I actually do. But he’s got a shocking back four alongside him. Positionally he’s not great, he could be better on the ball. I just think he’s such an easy target, and I know that will be unpopular with the fanbase.

In a recent development, ‘I’d be getting rid of Mazraoui well before him. I’d be getting rid of Martinez well before him. I’d be getting rid of De Ligt well before him.

‘De Ligt has got one big problem, he doesn’t play football. If a player is injury-prone and doesn’t play football, just because he hasn’t played it doesn’t mean he should go above a player who’s played every single week and is throwing his hand up.

‘You want someone who’s actually reliable and available to play. So De Ligt has to go.

‘I think he [Pochettino] will want to bring a new goalkeeper in. I think he’ll want someone to compete with [Senne] Lammens, I’m not saying he changes Lammens, I think it’s too early, I think we saw enough last season to carry on with him, hopefully build his confidence back up, I like his composure.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Out of the defenders he’ll keep [Luke] Shaw because he knows him and he’ll probably get the best out of him. The problem with Luke is injuries and you’ll have to manage him.

‘He’ll keep [Harry] Maguire because he’ll trust him and think he’s basically a soldier, someone who can do a job for him.

‘He’ll keep [Leny] Yoro because he’ll think there’s something to work with. [Ayden] Heaven, I’d loan him out, get some experience in the Championship. He’s nowhere near it, it’s unfair to put him in the situation he’s in.

Industry observers note that ‘I’d keep Bruno [Fernandes], I’d keep Kobbie [Mainoo], I’d keep [Youri] Tielemans, Mount would have to go, [Andrey] Santos… Mauricio may like him because he’s young, quite mobile, he’s aggressive, he might get something out of him. Him and [Carlos] Baleba will be in that middle bit. Mount’s the one that goes definitely, in that midfield section.

In a recent development, ‘Zirkzee definitely to go, I would think. I think Marcus 100 per cent to go because of the fact he’s on massive money and that story has been well told. They can’t afford to pay him that money, he’s in that section where you have to get rid of that wage at the earliest opportunity.

‘He’ll work with Amad, he’ll work with Mbeumo, he’ll work with Cunha and he’ll work with Sesko and think, ‘okay, I’ll make you into a centre-forward’.

Industry observers note that ‘He’ll use Dorgu in the short term but he’ll be one who will go in the next couple of years. Ugarte will go, 100 per cent.

According to recent reports, ‘If you look through it now I’d say Martinez would go, De Ligt would go, Mazraoui would go, Mount would go, first wave next summer. Rashford if you can, and Zirkzee.

‘And then the year after you’d be looking at Shaw, Maguire, Dalot, Bruno, that’s your second wave and maybe then you’ve made your mind up on Baleba and Santos.

Recent findings suggest that ‘But the earliest Mauricio Pochettino can unravel this squad is two seasons if he does well. It could take three to get this squad unravelled.’.
