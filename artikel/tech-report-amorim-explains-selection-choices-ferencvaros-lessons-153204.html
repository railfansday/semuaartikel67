AC Milan head coach Ruben Amorim knows that results are needed in Europe, but once again stressed that he foresees a ‘long-term project’.

Milan have racked up just three out of 12 points in the first half of the league phase, meaning they really need to change gear if they are to progress into the next round. Tonight’s game is another very tough one on paper, given Olympiacos have 10 points on the board and are in the top eight.

Amorim has named a strong starting line-up featuring the likes of Mario Gila, Koni De Winter, Adrien Rabiot, Diego Moreira, Goncalo Ramos and the top scorer Alphadjo Cisse. However, what doesn’t help the cause at all are the nine injury absentees, which is noticeable when looking at the bench.

According to recent reports, the head coach spoke to Sky in the build-up to kick-off in Greece to shed light on his selections and the importance of learning lessons in Europe. MilanNews relayed his words.

You’ve gone with an attacking side tonight….

It has been revealed that “In football, you have to defend and attack. We have to defend with 11 men and attack with 11 men. The players know what positions they have to occupy and are ready.

“We have to be more compact, and even with these attacking players on the pitch, we can defend better than in other Europa League matches.”.

Did the players learn their lesson after Ferencvaros?

“I think they understand. We’ve seen videos, we’re working. Against Ferencvaros, we were sluggish because we weren’t compact and focused on the details. In Europe, we need to raise our game and do what we’ve done several times in Serie A.”.

Are you ready to prove you can play three good games in a row in a week?

According to recent reports, “I think we have to be a great team. To be that, you have to win both in Serie A and in Europe. We have that responsibility. Results matter a lot, obviously, but we’re on a long-term project. We need to understand if we can play during the week, and today will be an additional test.”.
