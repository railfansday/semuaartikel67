Jarrod Bowen marked his 300th appearance for West Ham at Portsmouth tonight, hoping against hope that the anniversary would be marked by a West Ham win. The Hammers captain hit the post in the opening minutes – and West Ham went behind yet again through their own terrible defending.

West Ham sat eleventh at half time, apparently, so I read, their lowest-ever league position in 131 years*. West Ham came close in the second half as their desperation increased: Shots rattled the bar but the ball stayed out.. and Parker lost again.

Poor defending for Portsmouth’s goal, Herrick saved the first cross but couldn’t save the shot that led to Portsmouth’s goal. Slightly dodgy fluffed volleyed clearance that the youngster somehow got away with on 40 minutes. Shaky tonight.
