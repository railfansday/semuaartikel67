Bankman-Fried began serving his penalty on December 12, 2022. He is imprisoned at Federal Correctional Institution, Lompoc I in California. Fun (?) fact: Changpeng Zhao aka CZ, Bankman-Fried’s former investor who also appears on The Altruists, served four months at the same prison in 2024.

Ellison was sentenced to 24 months in federal prison and released after 14 months at the beginning of 2026. She was held at Federal Correctional Institution, Danbury in Connecticut. That particular prison has a whole history of fun facts. It’s where Piper Kerman, the author of Orange Is the Fresh Black, served time. That’s right, this prison is the “real” Litchfield. It has also appeared in popular culture multiple times and other infamous inmates IRL include Steve Bannon, Lauryn Hill, and Teresa Giudice.

Over the summer, Ellison was discreetly hired at Manifund, a nonprofit for matching donors with charity organizations. The tech giant’s CEO, Austin Chen, pledged to give her a second chance, and praised Ellison’s “relatable” personal writing on his own Substack. He noted that FTX gave him seed money, and that he was one of the victims of Bankman-Fried and Ellison’s crimes. (And just flagging for work-life balance reasons that he also remarked she gets along great with his young daughter.).

Industry observers note that “I understand that some of our users, donors, and partners may now be skittish about working with Manifund, given this announcement,” Chen concludes. “I appreciate any trust you are willing to extend, and ask that you judge us by our historical and future outputs.
