📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that democrats seized control of Congress, in part by focusing on affordability and the economy. An AP-NORC survey conducted just before the election found that both were ranked top issues for registered voters, outpacing the next most important matter, health care, by about 15 percentage points.

The new Democratic Congress will presumably pursue policies that are advertised as ways to reduce the cost of living. One idea they should reject is universal basic income (UBI). Leftists have long pushed for UBI, and the idea is gaining stream among tech leaders. It’s a policy that would backfire by reigniting inflation and ultimately harming those it attempts to help.

Joshua Bandoch is a research fellow with the Competitive Enterprise Institute, a free-market public-policy organization based in Washington, D.C.
