Attention C-suiters: Selena Gomez just found your company holiday party outfit.

The actor and Rare Beauty founder joined Only Murders in the Building co-star Martin Short for a spot on Jimmy Kimmel Live! last night and kept things businesslike yet festive in a black velvet skirt suit from Khaite: a cropped jacket with ultra-padded power shoulders and a matching mid-length pencil skirt with a front slit.

Selena Gomez arrives at Jimmy Kimmel Live!

It has been revealed that gomez, styled by Erin Walsh, paired her rich power suiting with a pair of pointy-toed patent leather slingbacks and an East-West JW Pei clutch. She finished off the look with a slick, low, center-parted bun; a fire-engine red manicure, and a liberal dusting of rosy blush on her cheekbones.

According to recent reports, the look seems particularly well-suited for any festive CEO (or any kind of manager, really) hoping to make a splash at the annual holiday party while still telegraphing their authority. With a business-like silhouette and a luxe fabric, Selena Gomez looks ready to deliver an all-time year-end toast. Here’s to a great Q1 in 2027!
