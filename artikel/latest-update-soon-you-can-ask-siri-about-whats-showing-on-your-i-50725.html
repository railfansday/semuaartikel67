According to recent reports, hey Siri, what’s the name of this blue plant on my screen? You couldn’t ask your iPad that question before, but you’ll be able to soon.

It has been revealed that in its upcoming iPadOS 27.3 release, Apple is adding several new features for iPads — Siri mode in the camera app, window resizing by tapping and more tools in the Freeform whiteboard app. The features are being tested in beta and will be released to the general public sometime in December. The new OS will be available on all iPads that support iPadOS 27.

The release notes for iPadOS 27.3 are listed here.

According to recent reports, on an iPhone, you can ask Siri about what’s in your camera frame. Siri can identify the object and perform a web search on it. You can also take action items, such as pointing the camera at a restaurant bill and asking Siri to split it if you want to split it with people you’re with.

That same functionality will soon be available on iPads, with the iPadOS 27.3 release. Maybe you want to identify a plant out in nature, or an historic building during a walking tour, or figure out how to replicate a tasty dish you just ordered at a restaurant.

According to recent reports, another latest feature in iPadOS 27.3 will make it easier to make windows bigger. Let’s say you’ve got a few windows open on your iPad, but there’s one you want to enlarge. You’ll be able to double-tap the top of the window to enlarge it, then double-tap again to revert it to its original size.

Prior to the iPadOS 27.3 release, you had to drag a corner of the window to make it bigger or smaller.

According to recent reports, freeform is an iPad app that serves as a digital whiteboard for sketching and writing out ideas and collaborating. You can add photos, videos, text, PDFs and other items to the whiteboard — using your mouse, finger or Apple Pencil — and sync it across other Apple devices such as iPhones and Macs.
