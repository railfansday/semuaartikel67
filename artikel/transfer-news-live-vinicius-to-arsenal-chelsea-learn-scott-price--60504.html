The January transfer window is fast approaching, with Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham all looking to strengthen their squads ahead of the second half of the season. Arsenal continue to be linked with Real Madrid’s Vinicius Junior despite the Brazilian signing a fresh deal with the La Liga giants in the summer. The winger featured heavily on the Premier League champions’ wishlist in the previous window and could be on the move again here. The Gunners also like the look of Bournemouth’s Eli Junior Kroupi and Antonio Nusa.

Mauricio Pochettino’s Manchester United are plotting to raid Fulham for Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson as the Argentine’s first two signings.

Latest transfer news and rumours.

Crystal Palace have confirmed the arrival of Bernardo Cueva from Chelsea.

The Mexican was the Blues’ set-piece coach, but he has now moved to Selhurst Park to become a more general first-team coach under latest boss Thomas Frank.

Mykhailo Mudryk has revealed that he wanted to join Arsenal.

The Tottenham winger, on loan from Chelsea, admitted that before he signed for the Blues in January 2023, he had wanted to move to north London.

Before he made the move to Stamford Bridge, he had been heavily linked with a move to Mikel Arteta’s side, but ultimately, he decided to join Graham Potter’s Blues instead.

Now, he has declared that his real desire was a move to Arsenal, which never materialised.

Arsenal are searching for a new left winger, and a surprise target of theirs is Brentford’s Kevin Schade.

Teamtalk reports that the Gunners have registered an interest in the German, liking in particular his versatility across the frontline.

Arsenal are not giving up in their pursuit of Vinicius Junior.

Fichjaes claims that the Gunners are still monitoring the Brazilian, despite the forward signing a upcoming contract at Real Madrid in the summer to stave off any further interest from the Premier League champions.

Chelsea have been told it will take over £100million to sign Alex Scott from Bournemouth.

That comes from Teamtalk, who claim that the Cherries are fully expectant of bids around that figure come the January window.

The Blues lead the race for the England midfielder, who is slowly making his anticipated return from injury.

The i Paper reports that Manchester United are plotting to raid Fulham for Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson.

Mauricio Pochettino is looking to strengthen his backline and has reportedly identified the Cottagers’ pair as the two to do so in the January window.

Standard Sport understands, however, that Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a upcoming contract in a bid to keep him at the club.

Fulham are in talks with captain Calvin Bassey over a upcoming contract as they look to fend off interest from Manchester United, writes Standard Sport’s Sam Tabuteau.

The 26-year-old centre-back, whose contract expires next summer, is understood to be on United’s shortlist as they look to sign defensive reinforcements in January.

Fulham hold the option to extend Bassey’s current deal by a further year.

The Cottagers are keen to keep hold of Bassey, especially after Alvaro Arbeloa promoted him to captain in the summer.

Nothing has been agreed yet, but Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a new contract and do not intend to sell him in January.

Thomas Frank has confirmed Crystal Palace are working to appoint Chelsea set-piece coach Bernardo Cueva, with a deal “very close.”.

Frank was appointed the Eagles’ head coach this week, replacing the Frenchman Pierre Sage, who lasted just 16 matches in the job and left the club dead last in the Premier League.

In a recent development, as he looks to establish his coaching staff, though, Frank is looking into a reunion with Cueva. The pair were colleagues at Brentford between 2020 and 2024, when Cueva was poached by the Blues, who reportedly spent £1m to secure his services.

But Chelsea have upgraded on Cueva this season, signing Aston Villa’s Austin MacPhee in a similarly pricey move.

Palace have reportedly been in talks with Chelsea over Cueva’s transfer for some time, and Frank, speaking in his first press conference since taking over, has now confirmed the widespread reports that a deal is imminent.

Club Brugge winger Carlos Forbs is attracting a growing number of suiters, including Arsenal.

Fichajes reports that an impressive run of form in Belgium has alerted a number of Premier League clubs.

The 22-year-old has revitalised his career after difficult spells at Ajax and Wolves, producing five goals and eight assists in 15 appearances across all competitions this season.

Arsenal are continuing their search for a marquee forward ahead of the January transfer window.

Sky Sports report that the Premier League champions are tracking RB Leipzig’s Antonio Nusa and Brentford’s Kevin Schade as potential options, with a preference for a left-sided attacker capable of playing across the frontline.

Arsenal are also monitoring Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott and could sell a midfielder to compete with Chelsea and Liverpool for the 23-year-old’s signature.
