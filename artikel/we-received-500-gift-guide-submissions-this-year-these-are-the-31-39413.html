It has been revealed that I’ll let you in on a not-so-well-kept secret about commerce journalism: Every year, we’re sent hundreds of sample products to test for our roundups and reviews. It’s one of the best parts of the job because a) who doesn’t love free stuff, and b) come the holidays, we know the best Christmas gifts almost as well as the guy in the North Pole.

The two most vital qualities to consider when deciding on a gift are usability and uniqueness. The best Christmas gifts can be heavy on one or the other or a mix of both. At one end of the spectrum, you have highly usable presents, like a great pair of earbuds or a streaming subscription. In the middle are useful items that your giftee might not buy for themselves, like a coffee membership or a latest jacket. And on the other far end of the spectrum are things that might not be “useful,” but they’re fun and unique enough to brighten a loved one’s holidays (like this little guy).

Recent findings suggest that for our list of the best Christmas gifts below, we included products across that whole spectrum at all price points, which means there should be something for everyone on your list from acquaintances to close friends and family members. We included our favorite stuff, but also consulted search data throughout the year to predict popular products that your loved ones might be thinking about.

It has been revealed that one of our must-buys for anyone seeing live music is a pair of Loop earplugs. They reduce the noise of concerts down to a safe level, and they look great doing it.

Trade Coffee ensures your loved one gets a new bag of beans or grounds delivered regularly. This means their coffee quality will almost certainly go up (Trade sources some great stuff), but it’s also a fun way to explore new brands they might not normally seek out.

Recent findings suggest that sweaters are a Christmas classic for good reason. But this cashmere quarter-zip from Nordstrom is one they’ll actually wear, whether to the office or around the house.

Cozy Earth’s buttery-soft pajama sets have earned them a cult following that we’re certainly part of. If you’re looking to upgrade a loved one’s sleepwear, it’s an easy present.

It has been revealed that our writer Sophie Dunne gifted her mom a MasterClass membership years ago, and it has made Christmas a breeze: Every year, her mom just wants her subscription renewed. But Sophie’s mom isn’t alone in loving MasterClass. The online learning platform has a course for everyone, whether they’re looking to improve a specific skill or check out something new.

In a recent development, greener pastures await those who diverge from AirPods. One of the best pairs of noise-canceling earbuds on the market are these Momentum 5s from Sennheiser. The premium buds deliver exceptionally immersive, clear sound and top-of-the-line noise-cancellation power for working, traveling, or commuting.

For something personal and unique, pick up one of these custom photo frames.

These Maui Jim sunglasses do double duty. They’re extra lightweight and comfortable for all-day outdoor adventures, and they showcase a Y2K look that would play in the city.

Industry observers note that watches are one of the most reliable, meaningful present ideas, but they don’t have to break the bank. Case in point: this elegant Bulova Surveyor ladies’ timepiece.

It has been revealed that for a men’s watch, consider this Timex Automatic E Line. It takes inspiration from the iconic (but extremely expensive) Patek Philippe Aquanaut, delivering a balanced look between sporty and dressy that your giftee can wear anywhere.

Sources indicate that an Audible membership unlocks a massive library of audiobooks, and it costs just $0.99 a month for the first three months. But your giftee doesn’t need to know how affordable it was.

If you’re shopping for a cook or a young person setting up their first kitchen, they’re sure to make use of a great fry pan. We like this made-in-America option from Eater and Heritage Steel.

For any festivities around Christmas, pick up these festive crackers. Friends and family can break them open to find paper hats, jokes, bingo game components, and other tchotchkes to keep the fun going.

Sources indicate that for the green thumb on your shopping list, check out this indoor hydroponic gardening system. It’s sleek and approachable for anyone, helping them grow fresh herbs, veggies, and greens in even the smallest spaces.

We think this 14K gold bracelet from Gorjana hits a sweet spot of price, quality, and customizability for gifting. You get to pick from 12 birthstones, adding a personal touch to an already great piece of jewelry.

Birdfy brought bird feeders into the 21st century with their smart, camera-equipped offering. They’ve continued to improve on the product, now offering 2K video, accurate bird identification, and a host of other fun features that help your giftee get to know their winged neighbors.
