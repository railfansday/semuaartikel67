📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that we don’t know about you, but our intrigue surrounding Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau’s relationship is still very much high. It’s the romantic link-up that simply no one could have ever predicted—a former Canadian prime minister falling in love with a global pop star. It’s giving Hallmark movie!

In many ways, they do seem like perfect opposites: His career as a former buttoned-up politician juxtaposed with Perry’s penchant for campy outfits—see: her many Met Gala wins—not to mention a string of total pop bangers. You could say she is the yin to his yang. Just take their latest couple spotting this weekend: Perry and Trudeau were seen arriving at Venice’s Marco Polo Airport together, and their distinct approaches to travel style only further cemented their individuality.

The singer’s fashion approach to being on the go? She opted for a quiet luxury travel look: a luxe, cashmere-looking camel two-piece set, plus a beige scarf overtop. Her accessories—like the brown Polo Ralph Lauren cap and oversized leather bag—were classic pop-star-off-duty chic. Clearly, she has mastered the art of boarding a plane in style (or, in this Venice case, a gondola).

Trudeau, however, went a little more kitsch-meets-dadcore with his choice of travel look: He donned an unbuttoned shirt, chinos, and a cheeky slogan tee that read “Will yap about books,” proving to the world that he is, in fact, an avid reader. (Fun fact: In 2017, he shared some of his essential reading list at the time, which included books by David Hackett Fischer and Stephen King.).

It has been revealed that now, if this were any other coordinated celebrity couple, perhaps Perry and Trudeau could have gone more matchy-matchy for their joint outing. But we frankly love the more opposites-attract approach. Why should one give up their unique fashion tendencies the minute they get into a relationship? Let Perry have her stealth-wealth cozy essentials, and Trudeau his quirky T-shirts. Which isn’t to say that Perry is a stranger to an eye-catching tee herself. Remember her “Nasty Woman” top circa 2016?
