GTA 6 Review Score Discrepancy Has Fans Debating Its Metacritic

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto 6 set a record earlier this week by launching with the highest game score of all time on Metacritic, but it hasn't all been sunshine and rainbows. Once user scores became available, it quickly became clear that there was a big divide between critics and players, at least when it came to those leaving reviews on the site.

According to recent reports, as of this article's writing, the critic score for Grand Theft Auto 6 stands at a 99 on Metacritic, while the user score (from over 70,000 ratings) is a significantly lower 5.6. This might seem to imply that the average fan isn't all that impressed with the game, but many of those discussing the Metacritic results believe that the user score still doesn't paint an entirely accurate picture.

One Reddit post about the Metacritic scores (shared when the user score was sitting at only 3.7) sparked plenty of debate, with one particular sentiment standing out as the most popular. The top comment from Apprehensive_Spend_7 calls the user score a result of "Insane review bombing," a term generally used to refer to massive waves of 0/10 ratings intended to lower the average rather than properly reflect a game's positive and negative qualities.

Industry observers note that user Katastroferrr comes to the same consensus, noting that they're "not a Rockstar glazer" but suggesting that, whether the game is a 10/10, it certainly isn't a 3.7. ToyzillaRawr suggests that both the critic and audience scores feel off the mark, giving it a personal 8/10 based on their experience so far. Dalvic2468 posits that the 99 average is the result of its status as "a technical masterpiece," arguing that the game ultimately "deserves its rating.".

At any rate, it looks like the critic score is here to stay, and even some last-minute negative reviews from Metacritic-eligible publications would struggle to tank the average too much. The audience score, meanwhile, appears to be steadily rising as more ratings come in, leaving a possibility that it will work its way up to a favorable rating in time.

According to recent reports, A better metric for audience reception might be the PlayStation Store, where players can only rate the game if they've actually purchased it. There, it has a 4.82/5 with 217,000 ratings, representing widespread acclaim despite failing to reach the astronomical heights of the critic scores.

The simplest explanation for the review discrepancy is likely a discrepancy in prerogative. Despite various reviews citing shortcomings with the game, critics seem to favor awarding it a 10 for its generally incomparable status, as it offers an experience that, for better or worse, is ultimately unlike anything that's come before it.

In a recent development, negative audience reviews, meanwhile, come from a variety of impulses. Some — especially the middle-of-the-road ratings — focus on many of the same shortcomings that critics have acknowledged, while others stem from more culturally loaded opinions or a desire to counteract its perceived overrating.

Sources indicate that regardless, there's no denying that Grand Theft Auto 6 has made a splash, and being at the center of controversy is nothing new for the series. This time, it just isn't any of the extreme content that's causing the ruckus.
