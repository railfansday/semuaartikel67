I didn’t wake up hot or sweaty when sleeping on the Saatva Cloud Memory Foam Pillow, and that’s all thanks to Saatva’s temperature regulation. Memory foam is a far more heat-retaining material than natural latex, so to mitigate any overheating complications, Saatva utilized organic cotton for the cover — which is breathable — and shredded the memory foam and latex interior fill so air could weave its way through and keep my temperature neutral at night.

Industry observers note that during the beginning of testing, I was sleeping without the air-conditioner and just a fan, so it was about 75 degrees in my house when I fell asleep at night and around 70 degrees when I woke up in the morning.
