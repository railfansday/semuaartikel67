With three wins in their last four matches, Sheffield United have found some form heading in to the busy December period.

And one of Chris Wilder's stars of the campaign has been Sydie Peck, who remained at Bramall Lane despite transfer attention from elsewhere, is operating on another level.

Peck went through the entire 2024-25 Championship season without scoring, but after notching five goals last season, he's already matched that tally from midfield in 16 outings in 2026-27 - adding three assists to that tally too.

Peck - who joined United from Arsenal's youth setup in 2021 - rarely plays together with fellow midfield youngster Oliver Arblaster, who came through the Blades academy, but they started alongside each other in the engine room in the 4-0 rout of Watford this past weekend.

That pairing has been a source of contention for many United supporters in recent times - Peck's run in the first-team on a regular basis only really began when Arblaster was sidelined with a knee injury two seasons ago, and the latter was left sidelined more often than not when he was fit and ready to return last year.

They worked pretty well together against an abject Hornets side, but Wilder seemed to object to a question posed to him about whether or not they can work as a good pairing on a regular basis.

Wilder seemed utterly bemused, noting they've only played together six times in midfield, winning three and losing two, and he believes that fans are waiting for Peck and Arblaster to 'fail' as a partnership for whatever reason - you can watch the full rant below.

Arblaster's emergence in Sheffield United's relegated Premier League team in 2024, following a stint on loan at Port Vale, was a genuinely exciting moment - despite the drop back to the Championship, United had a player that was ready to start week in, week out in the Championship.

Sadly, his knee injury set him back a year, but it allowed Peck to emerge from the shadows and become the player he has now developed into - whilst still a raw talent, there's a reason why Premier League clubs have been circling in recent times from the likes of Newcastle United and Sunderland.

If Peck continues to seriously impress, then Sheffield United will have a big decision to make in the January window should bids arrive at their door - selling Andre Brooks, Callum O'Hare and Gus Hamer for decent money in the summer though could mean the club are in a stronger financial position than many think.

Every player has a price though, and with January fast approaching, there's a chance that the Peck and Arblaster partnership may not have that much time to flourish.
