Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso will be sweating over the availability of midfield lynchpin Moises Caicedo for Saturday’s Premier League clash with rivals Leeds.

The former Brighton man has featured on international duty with Ecuador, playing 81 minutes and scoring during a 2-0 win over Costa Rica last week.

Caicedo was handed another start in Ecuador’s next friendly with Bolivia earlier this week, only for the midfielder to ask to come off at half-time due to feeling unwell.

After the game Caicedo admitted that he had taken precautions in order to give himself the best chance of being available for Chelsea.

Caicedo explained to reporters: "I didn't feel very well, so I asked to come off instead, because I know I have another commitment with my club.

"So, I'll try to get there as soon as possible and recover.".

A £115million signing from Brighton in the summer of 2023, Caicedo’s current campaign has already been heavily impacted by injury.

A knock kept the holding midfielder out of Chelsea’s Premier League opener with Fulham, before Caicedo sustained a calf issue just 14 minutes into an appearance off the bench against former employers Brighton.

Industry observers note that that calf injury kept Caicedo out for over a month, leading to the 25-year-old missing seven games for club and country. Fit enough to play the Blues’ last five Premier League fixtures, Caicedo was a doubt due to fitness concerns for the 1-0 loss away at Sunderland.

As such, travelling to South America to play for Ecuador may have exacerbated those fitness complications.

"[I'm] a little tired, with some minor issues, but I'm here because it's my national team and what matters most to me is being here; with my people, with my Ecuador," Caicedo said earlier in the international window.

"I'm making a massive effort to be here, and I'm happy about that.".

In total Caicedo has made seven competitive appearances under Alonso this season.
