Apple has officially kicked off its Black Friday Shopping Event in the United States and other countries. You can score an Apple gift card of up to $300 with an eligible product.

Apple’s offer comes as Black Friday deals at Amazon continue, with more lucrative cash savings than Apple’s gift card offer. You can score $100 off AirPods Pro 3, $100 off MacBook Neo, up to $400 off MacBook Air, and much more. Here are a few highlights, thanks to our friends over at 9to5Toys.

Industry observers note that as in past years, Apple’s Black Friday Shopping Event offers you a gift card when you buy an eligible product. The company isn’t offering cash discounts on any of its products. The max gift card you can earn is $300, which comes with the purchase of a maxed-out MacBook Pro.

Sources indicate that apple’s newest products are excluded from the Black Friday Shopping Event. This includes the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Duo, MacBook Pro with PROLED, HomeView, and Apple TV 4K (4th generation).

Recent findings suggest that the gift card you receive with a purchase can’t be applied to the product’s purchase price. Instead, you can use it toward a future purchase at Apple, including hardware, services like Apple Music and Apple TV, and Apple Store purchases.

The Apple Black Friday Event runs from today through November 30 at 11:59 p.m. PT. It’s available on Apple’s website and in Apple Stores. You cannot combine it with any other discounts, such as Apple’s Education Store or Veterans and Military Store. Apple’s holiday return window is also presently active, so you have until January 8, 2027, to return any purchases made.
