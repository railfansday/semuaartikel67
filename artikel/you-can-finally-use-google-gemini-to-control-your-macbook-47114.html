📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google and Apple might be competitors, but they can play nice sometimes.

For example, Google just unveiled that you can now use Gemini AI to control a Mac computer. With this fresh feature, you can give Gemini commands and it will root through your files, access your apps, and browse the web to complete whatever tasks you've asked it to perform. All of this is facilitated by the Gemini app for macOS 27 Golden Gate, per Google's press release.

How can people take advantage of Gemini computer use for MacBooks, Mac Mini, and iMac devices?

In a recent development, google provided several examples in an announcement blog post, such as asking Gemini to take a child's sketch of a butterfly and render it polygonally using the 3D modeling program Blender. Once the request is processed, Gemini performs the task in an isolated background window with a preview display right in the Gemini app, allowing you to watch it perform the task in real-time. However, as Google noted, you also retain full use of your Mac while this is happening, allowing people to continue browsing, typing, and completing other types of work.

Recent findings suggest that while this may be a boon to Gemini power people who own devices like the fresh MacBook Pro OLED, it's worth noting that giving an AI agent full access to all your local files and apps carries inherent risks. Google gives users the ability to freely choose which apps Gemini has access to, alongside the ability to watch the process as it unfolds, in case something goes wrong. So, you can pause Gemini if it's doing something you don't like, and approve decisions before they're made. Industry observers note that it should also be noted that Google is playing catch-up with this announcement. Meta, OpenAI, and Anthropic already offer this type of agentic feature, so Google's just a tad late to the party.

Google also just upgraded Gemini with even more task-completing power this week, introducing Gemini Tasks, a feature in the AI chatbot that lets it complete tasks for browser people (a feature not to be confused with Google Tasks).

Per Google, the new computer use feature is rolling out immediately for English users in the Gemini app, though it requires a Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription. Users will need a Mac device with Apple Silicon and macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later.

To get started, visit gemini.google/mac.
