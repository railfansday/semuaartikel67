It was another topsy-turvy week in college football as multiple teams ranked inside the College Football Playoff field went down. One of those teams was UCLA, which suffered its first loss of the season to Michigan in triple overtime.

As a result, the Bruins are down to No. 12 in our latest CFB Power Rankings, but they have far bigger problems than that. Quarterback Ty Dieffenbach went down with a hip injury, and he may not be back in time to play USC this weekend. Even with Dieffenbach in the game, the UCLA offense wasn't exactly firing on all cylinders. Our own Brandon Marcello has more.

Michigan, the team that beat UCLA, finally has some positive vibes going into its rivalry game against Ohio State. The Wolverines are up 13 spots in this week's CBS Sports 138. Speaking of the Buckeyes, there's a small chance they could still sneak into the Significant Ten Championship Game.

According to recent reports, in case you didn't hear, ace Tarik Skubal signed a record-shattering $450 million deal with the Mets over the weekend. That got MLB expert Mike Axisa thinking about the players who could top Skubal's deal in the coming years.

It should come as no surprise that Pirates superstar Paul Skenes was on that list. Even after a "down" year by his standards, Skenes is perhaps the best young pitcher in the game. If he can return to the heights of his 2025 campaign — a 1.97 ERA and 216 strikeouts en route to a Cy Young Award — he will absolutely break Skubal's record.

With Skubal off the market thanks to that lucrative deal from the Mets, teams are looking for other options on the mound. The Blue Jays have turned their attention to Rays stud Freddy Peralta.
