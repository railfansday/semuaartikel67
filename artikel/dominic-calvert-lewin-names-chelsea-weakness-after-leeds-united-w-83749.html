Dominic Calvert-Lewin believes Chelsea’s players could not handle the ‘intensity’ during Leeds United’s 4-1 win at Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

Chelsea took the lead in the first half through an excellent finish from Emmanuel Emegha but Xabi Alonso’s side were in front for just three minutes as Noah Okafor pulled Leeds level.

Leeds then came out flying in the second half and Leeds were given a penalty after Jayden Bogle’s thumping strike hit Rogers on the top of his arm.

Calvert-Lewin converted the spot-kick to put Leeds ahead and Daniel Farke’s side continued their dominance in the second half as Okafor netted his second of the game before Brenden Aaronson completed the deserved victory with a goal in injury time.

Chelsea have now been beaten in three out of their last four Premier League games, while only Coventry City, Ipswich Town and Crystal Palace have conceded more goals than Xabi Alonso’s side this season.

During his post-match interview with Sky Sports, Calvert-Lewin was asked what the message was from Farke during the half-time break with Chelsea already showing signs of fragility.

‘The message was just more of the same. I think we were going well, we just weren’t putting our chances away and we knew the next chance was coming and we’d have to take it,’ Calvert-Lewin replied.

‘To be honest, I expected Chelsea to come out with a bit more oomph in the second half but I think our intensity, the way that we played and the way we set up against them, they couldn’t handle it, and I think that reflected in the scoreline.’.

Asked what the message was ahead of the game against Chelsea, Calvert-Lewin replied: ‘Intensity, bring the intensity from the start.
