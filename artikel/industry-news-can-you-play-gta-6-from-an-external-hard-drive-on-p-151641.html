Rockstar’s behemoth takes up a lot of storage space.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is the biggest game of the year by some distance, having racked up $3.79 billion in pre-orders and day-one sales. On PlayStation 5 alone, more than 39 million unique players took control of dual protagonists Jason and Lucia on opening weekend, making it the biggest game debut in the 32-year history of PlayStation.

In a recent development, there will be many other folks who want to play the game and haven't had a chance to jump in yet. One thing they should note is that, with a hefty install size, GTA 6 will take up a significant chunk of their PS5 or Xbox Series X/S storage space.

You might be wondering whether you can install GTA 6 on an external hard drive to avoid having to delete other games and data from your console. You can move GTA 6 to your external storage when you're not playing it. However, to run the game locally, you'll need to have it installed on your system's built-in storage, an SDD you've installed in your PS5 or a compatible NVMe expansion card that's plugged into your Xbox.

In a recent development, as with many other recent games, GTA 6 needs to take advantage of the fast-loading capabilities of the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. The consoles are unable to load data for these titles swiftly enough from an external hard drive over a USB connection. For the same reason, disc-based PS5 and Xbox Series X games are installed onto system storage before you can play them. The PS5 can run PS4 games from external storage, though. Likewise, an Xbox Series X/S can run older Xbox One and Xbox 360 titles directly from an external hard drive.
