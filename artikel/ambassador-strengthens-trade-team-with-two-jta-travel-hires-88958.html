Ambassador Cruise Line has appointed Louise Winter as national commercial manager and Chris Cundall as divisional sales manager – North.

Both join Ambassador on January 4, 2027, from JTA Travel Group, taking up their new positions within the trade team led by Nicola Harper, Ambassador’s head of distribution.

“With Ambassador continuing to expand its offering both in the UK and across Europe, we remain fully committed to working closely with our trade partners and ensuring they have everything they need to succeed,” commented Harper.

“With this in mind, I’m delighted to welcome Louise and Chris to the Ambassador family. Both bring a wealth of experience, considerable industry knowledge and a proven track record of building strong commercial relationships, which will be invaluable as we continue to grow and our trade offering develops.”.

In her new role as national commercial manager, Winter will support Ambassador’s national trade relationships, identify new business opportunities and help drive sales growth across the UK.

She brings more than 25 years’ experience across the travel and retail sectors, most recently as group head of operations at JTA Travel Group, where she has overseen commercial and operational performance across JTA Travel since February 2024.

“I’m thrilled to be joining Ambassador Cruise Line at such an exciting time for the business. Ambassador has a fantastic proposition, strong ambitions for growth and, importantly, a real commitment to the travel trade – something I’m extremely passionate about,” noted Winter.

As divisional sales manager – North, Cundall will be responsible for developing existing commercial relationships across the North of England, Scotland and Northern Ireland to maximize sales opportunities and drive day-to-day trading, while identifying new agent and trade partnerships.

He joins Ambassador from JTA Travel Group, where he oversees sales strategy and commercial relationships across the UK travel trade.

Having spent over 30 years working within the travel industry, I understand the importance of building strong relationships with travel agent partners and working collaboratively to deliver success,” noted Cundall.
