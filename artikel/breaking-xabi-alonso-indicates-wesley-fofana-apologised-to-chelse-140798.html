Xabi Alonso notes Wesley Fofana has apologised to Chelsea after giving an explosive interview without the club’s permission.

A two-hour conversation with Fofana was published by French YouTuber Zach Nani on Friday, during which the 25-year-old appeared to joke about missing a penalty in Chelsea’s shootout defeat to Liverpool which knocked them out of the Carabao Cup.

He has also received a torrent of backlash for stating that he considered retiring from football during his lengthy injury lay-off, only to be discouraged by his agent because “you’ve still got so many years left on your contract, and so much money to earn” - insinuating he was motivated to sit on his current lucrative deal at Stamford Bridge.

Alonso was pressed about the interview before Friday’s trip to Nottingham Forest, revealing in his news conference that Fofana had tried to make amends with the club.

“Absolutely, we have addressed that with him and for sure it was unfortunate,” Alonso noted.

“He apologised, he knows it was not right the moment, it was not right the content, we are here to take things seriously and he said that it's not going to be repeated and we know that it's something that we don't want, these kind of things and they are not going to be repeated for sure.”.

Asked whether Fofana would face any further sanction, such as be dropped from the squad, or if the case was closed, Alonso replied: “Closed.”.

Fofana has struggled for form as part of Chelsea’s porous defence in recent weeks and, alongside Levi Colwill, came under criticism for his part in the club’s damning 4-1 defeat to Leeds last weekend.

Chelsea have been hurt by the absence of summer signing Maxence Lacroix with a hamstring injury and Alonso confirmed he is still not yet fit to make a return.

“It's one less week than the last game so we are closer to recovery but not for this game,” Alonso said.

Joao Pedro also remains sidelined as he nurses a thigh problem, alongside winger Jamie Gittens and defender Jorrel Hato, though Alonso did confirm Moises Caicedo will be involved after enduring an injury scare against Leeds.
