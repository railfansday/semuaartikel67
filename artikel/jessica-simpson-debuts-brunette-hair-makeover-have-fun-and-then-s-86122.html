Jessica Simpson has parted ways with her signature blonde tresses in favor of a darker — and more winter-like — shade.

According to recent reports, “They say blondes have more fun,” celebrity hairstylist Ken Paves wrote via his Instagram Stories on Friday, November 20, alongside a selfie featuring Simpson, 46. “She says brunettes have fun and then some.”.

According to recent reports, simpson, who reposted the snap onto her account, dressed casually in all-black. As for her glam, the singer’s newly darkened mane was styled in loose, face-framing waves.

While Simpson is often synonymous with her honey-colored hair, she made a major statement late last year when she went brunette.

According to recent reports, “It took a few hours to color because we kept making it darker and darker,” her colorist Rita Hazan told People in December 2025. “I didn’t want to go too dark at once, so we gradually increased the hue to ensure that we got the most perfect tone.”.

She continued, “For years we kept different variations of either blonde or very blonde. Just recently I took her dirty blonde, and the shade continues to get deeper and more brunette.”.

According to Hazan, Simpson’s darker ‘do even matched her “very moody” music return. The performer released her long-awaited follow-up EP, Nashville Canyon Part 1, earlier that year.

Always inviting God into the room where music creates itself, with a voice that communicates on a spiritual level of grace without fear and allowing the music to be my main voice.”.
