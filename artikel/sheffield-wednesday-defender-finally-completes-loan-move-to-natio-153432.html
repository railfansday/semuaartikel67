The young defender was handed his first league start in almost 13 months at the end of October - just a few weeks after speculation had strongly linked him with a loan move to the fifth tier to broaden his football education.

Then Owls head coach Henrik Pedersen had confirmed that Weaver was set to go out on loan to boost his game-time levels following a long-term injury.

The move has now finally been signed off.

Sporting director Simon Wilson said: “Ernie has been keen to get more regular game time in this important stage of his development.

"We have had to hold him back recently to cover the defensive positions, but as players have returned to fitness, we are now in a position to facilitate this loan.

"The National League is an excellent level for young players, offering flexibility through short-term loans and a competitive environment that will challenge players, including Ernie.”.

Weaver signed his first professional contract with the Owls in July 2025.

The 20-year-old made his first team debut one month later against Bolton Wanderers in the Carabao Cup, and has played 15 appearances for Wednesday in total.
