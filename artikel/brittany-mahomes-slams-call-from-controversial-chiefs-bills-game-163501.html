Brittany Mahomes is throwing a red flag at this call.

Recent findings suggest that the wife of Patrick Mahomes made no secret of her thoughts on the Kansas City Chiefs’ loss to the Buffalo Bills during the Nov. 26 Thanksgiving game, sharing her frustration over an overturned call involving player Rashee Rice’s catch in the fourth quarter.

“HAHAHAHAHAHA WHAT A JOKE,” Brittany posted to her Instagram Stories Nov. 26. “Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us.”.

The 31-year-old—who is expecting her fourth child with the Chiefs quarterback—added, “Not sure what an actual catch is after that | guess? I don't wanna hear s--t anymore from anyone.”.

She then shared a video of the moment in question, showing wide receiver Rashee catching the ball before being tackled by Bills player Amani Hooker. As Rashee went down, the ball made contact with the turf, and the pass was ruled incomplete. Brittany’s posts made clear she thought the officials had fumbled the call.

Brittany wasn’t ready to let the ruling slide and kept the pressure on in several more Instagram Stories about the call, which ultimately contributed to the Chiefs’ 13-10 loss to the Bills.

Of course, Brittany—who is mom to kids Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months—has always had Patrick’s back, on and off the field.

"She has her head on her shoulders the right way," Patrick told E! News in July 2023. "She's my rock. She's the one that, through tough times, can get me through.".

The 31-year-old added, “Having someone that supports you every single day like I do is the reason that I am in the position that I am today.".

And that love and support go both ways, as Brittany has credited the Super Bowl champion with helping her shake off any hate she’s received online.

It has been revealed that "I think social media used to get to me a lot and now, honestly, I don't give a f--k what people have to say about me anymore," Brittany shared on a 2024 episode of the Whoop podcast. "I think he's helped me get to that point to where I'm strong in who I am, I'm confident in who I am, I'm confident in mine and his relationship. I'm confident in our life, so why does what other people say have to matter at all?".
