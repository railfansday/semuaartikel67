Bannan started Challinor's first league game as Owls manager in the hole, but the latter states it was a horses for courses decision.

Industry observers note that "We've not got a load of midfielders but they do bring different things," he argued ahead of Friday's League One game at Barnsley. "I think Barry can play in different roles.

"For how we want to play at times and have control, him playing centrally and at times a little bit deeper is something for him because of his technical ability.

"I think it will work really well and will probably extend his career depending on how long he wants to play for because there'll be less demand on him influencing the game higher up.

"His strength is his weakness because he wants to influence games and win games at times by himself and put himself in areas where he thinks he can do that.

"Sometimes that can be a detriment of the team.

"I suppose the luxury we have at the minute is we're able to at times ask him to be a little bit more disciplined but on other occasions let him be a little bit of a free spirit.

"When he's higher up he can roll into wide areas, he can come deeper if he wants.

"But we're going to have to find different things at the minute because we don't have the players specifically fit in the positions that we'd like to have.

"We've just got to be careful we don't leave Davo (centre-forward Davis Keillor-Dunn) isolated.

"Something I looked at before I came into the club was a lot of people wanting to come towards the ball.

"The responsibility of the back players and the midfield players is to give the ball in the areas where they can hurt teams.".
