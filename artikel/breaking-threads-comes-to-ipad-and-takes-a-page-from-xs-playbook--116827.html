Breaking: Threads comes to iPad and takes a page from X’s playbook with cashtags

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It may have taken 15 years for Instagram to launch on the iPad, but Instagram’s other app, Threads, has taken a slightly faster path to the Apple tablet. In an overnight revision, the company rolled out a massive-screen version of the X competitor. It’s also added another latest feature, borrowed from X: the stock-tracking feature known as cashtags.

In a recent development, while the iPad version of the app works well in portrait orientation, some users are disappointed to find that the landscape layout doesn’t work as they’d expect. Instead of offering a multi-column interface as on Threads’ web app, the iPad app focuses on the user’s main feed in a single-column layout. There are also options to swipe right and left from the main screen to access the search screen and the feeds menu, respectively.

The iPad app isn’t the organization’s only update in the latest release. According to a post from Threads engineer Guy Dickinson, Threads will now support cashtags — a feature popularized by Twitter years ago for tracking live stock information. These specialized tags start with the dollar sign (“$”) followed by a ticker symbol. When you click on a cashtag, you can follow the discussions around the stock.

It has been revealed that in addition, Threads is adding live stock quotes, charts, and other context at the top of each cashtag’s page. As you search for a particular cashtag, the autocomplete search results will display the stock’s current price and the percentage it’s up or down. Dickinson says the feature will currently support NYSE and Nasdaq stocks, ETFs, and crypto. Cashtags will be supported on the web, iOS, and Android. However, they’re just now rolling out, so you may not immediately see them.

Sources indicate that the update points to Threads’ broader ambition to compete with X, not only as a destination for discussions about various topics, but also as a place where people can track real-time news and information. Cashtags in particular could attract traders and investors to Threads who typically rely on X.

Despite facing stiff competition from X, Meta confirmed earlier this year that Threads topped 500 million monthly users, and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg indicates the company believes the app is on track to reach a billion users over time.
