Andy Serkis has brought out his Gollum voice once more as his new Lord of the Rings film The Hunt for Gollum marks the end of filming.

The wrap video posted by Warner Bros. gives fans a taste of Serkis' final speech on-set, as well as apparent confirmation that Hobbiton will once again be shown on the major screen.

Industry observers note that then, the clip takes a surreal turn: the foul-mouthed "diva" 'Gollum' is trying to be coaxed out of his trailer by the crew. But instead of packing up, the production team grows tired and ends up towing the trailer and shipping him off to places unknown.

Industry observers note that that’s a wrap! Well, almost. Production on The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum has officially ended. Only in theaters December 2027. https://t.co/mBb1Rmw70JNovember 25, 2026.

The Hunt for Gollum, directed by Serkis, will see the creature formerly known as Smeagol return, finding himself firmly in the crosshairs of Strider/Aragorn (now played by Jamie Dornan, replacing Viggo Mortensen) after gaining possession of The One Ring.

Speaking about playing Aragorn, Dornan remarked he hadn't spoken "directly" to Mortensen, but was thrilled to hear he gave the casting his blessing.

According to recent reports, the cast also includes Sir Ian McKellen as Gandalf and Elijah Wood as Frodo, with the story expected to take place just prior or in the early moments of The Fellowship of the Ring. Kate Winslet and Anya Taylor-Joy will also be making their first tentative steps into Middle-earth.

Serkis had already mentioned in a previous interview that production had come close to finishing, meaning its 2027 release date – the same day as Avengers: Secret Wars, no less – is feeling pretty secure.
