Fifty three newly elected members of Congress told Harvard University on Tuesday that they will boycott an informal orientation program at Harvard’s Kennedy School next month in support of the school’s striking graduate students union.

In a letter to the university’s administrators, the incoming House Democrats expressed thanks for their invitations to the Bipartisan Program for Newly Elected Members of Congress, in which more than 800 members have participated in since 1972.
