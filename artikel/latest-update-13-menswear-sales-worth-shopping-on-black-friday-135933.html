If you’re looking hard enough for sales on menswear, you can almost always find a discount on something decent, a pair of shoes, a shirt maybe, but you have to scrounge. Every other week of the year, that’s just how it is. But this week, the week of Black Friday, the opposite is true. Every store and their mother is having a sale and suddenly, it’s not easy to tell which markdowns are gonna go fast and which ones will still be there when you log on again in December. Luckily, you have me. Year-round, it’s my job to keep tabs on deals so I know which ones to pounce on this Black Friday.

Sources indicate that on Amazon, there’s my favorite Lacoste polo, which is actually on an outrageous sale going up to 40 percent off if you’re the right size. Filson jackets are 25 percent off. Lululemon isn’t just for the gals, it has a ton of great stuff for men and its Black Friday sale is absolutely stacked. And REI is doing its classic “We hate Black Friday but are doing a sale anyway” thing, and as always we are here for it.

Sources indicate that let’s start with the personal favorites; after that we’ll just list deals alphabetically by brand. First, Amazon. Yeah, yeah, everything is Amazon now. But when Bezos is offering significant discounts on Lacoste polos and Levi’s, we listen.

Nothing is ever going to beat the cool of a beat-to-hell Filson jacket. If you’ve been holding off on buying one, I can’t recommend it enough. Plus, Black Friday is the perfect time to get one for 25 percent off.

I tell regular—meaning normal guys, not fashion-y dorks like me—that Huckberry is really the answer to 90 percent of what they want. All of a man’s casual wardrobe can be bought here; the only thing it doesn’t stock well is suits and tailoring. I’m seeing up to 40 percent off on seasonal shirts, and up to 30 percent off jackets, sweaters, and pants; that’s as good as it gets, folks.

My hunt is for a really good jacket—sport coat, blazer, suit separate, or whatever you want to call it. That’s what I’m showing, but you need to do some of your own hunting on this one.

According to recent reports, del Toro, which has for a long time just been a really good rich guy made-in-Italy shoe brand, has started putting out some really lovely elevated casual clothing—all vintage Italian-inspired stuff—and a majority of it is 20 percent off. And J. Crew has its usual sale going on, but an extra 40 percent off sale styles thrown in with the code FRIDAY on top of that.
