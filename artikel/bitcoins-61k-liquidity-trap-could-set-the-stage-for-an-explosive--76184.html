📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Market analyst Crypto with Sarmad warns that Bitcoin’s current sell-off hasn’t ended yet, as one final bearish leg could unfold before a major recovery.

With mounting selling pressure weighing on the top cryptocurrency’s sentiment, the analyst believes another liquidity sweep could set the stage for Bitcoin’s next major rally.

According to Crypto with Sarmad, Bitcoin could first rebound toward the $68,000 to $70,000 zone before reversing sharply and sweeping liquidity below $61,000.

Based on his analysis, this potential decline could be instrumental in trapping bearish traders before triggering a powerful recovery toward $87,000.

Industry observers note that what’s Bitcoin’s broader market roadmap? Well, Crypto with Sarmad outlines a move from $64,000 to $70,000, followed by a deeper correction toward $58,000. This will prompt a rebound to $87,000, with the long-term target of $135,000 in the picture.

Per CoinGecko data, BTC is trading at $63,057, down 23.9% over the past month, with this decline showing the mounting pressure facing the crypto market as questions get raised whether the worst of the sell-off is already factored in.

Adding to the bearish outlook, market analyst Ali Martinez has revealed that large Bitcoin holders are continuing to reduce their exposure.

According to Martinez, large entities have sold or redistributed approximately $3.40 billion worth of Bitcoin over the past two weeks, representing 48,618 BTC.

In a recent development, what does this trend suggest? Sustained distribution suggests that major holders are not yet showing convincing signs of renewed accumulation, which could complicate Bitcoin’s recovery if bulls don’t enter the market in full swing.

Nevertheless, all hope is not lost because comparisons between Bitcoin’s current drawdown and market movements surrounding major technology listings, including the anticipated Anthropic IPO and SpaceX listing, have raised speculation about a potential $90,000 recovery by February next year.
