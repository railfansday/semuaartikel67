Luca Williams-Barnett has hailed his new contract as “a special moment” after committing his future to Tottenham.

The young star has impressed in north London since his breakthrough into the first-team fold under Roberto De Zerbi this term.

The winger notched his first goal for the club at senior level against Ipswich Town after the international break, having made his first senior start at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

He made his Premier League debut off the bench in the win over Crystal Palace, and stepped off the bench in the defeat by Leeds United.

“This is a special moment for me and my family, and one I have worked really hard to reach, but this is only the start,” the 18-year-old said.

“Spurs is a family to me. I've been here for so long – it's a second home, where I trust the people and coaches who have pushed me to become the player I am today.

“The club has trusted and believed in me, now I'm ready to repay that.”.

De Zerbi remarked that the Spurs academy product was a player that he “thought the world of”.

“I'm so happy that he has signed a new contract,” the Italian said.

“He has benefitted from being around the first team every day, growing in stature and consistency, and has deserved his place in the matchday squad over recent weeks.

“Everyone can see how technically gifted Luca is on the pitch but I also love his calmness and decision-making under pressure.

“The best players have confidence on the pitch and humility off it. There is still plenty of room for Luca to improve, and I know he will continue to work hard every day to be the best version of himself.”.
