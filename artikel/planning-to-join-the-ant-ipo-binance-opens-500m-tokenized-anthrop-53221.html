According to recent reports, planning to join Anthropic’s ANT IPO? Binance is giving crypto investors another way to gain exposure to one of the biggest technology listings of 2026. Binance Wallet has opened an eight-hour subscription campaign for Anthropic tokenized stocks through its xStocks partnership. The offering carries a $500 million USDC subscription cap, with indicative prices ranging from $70 to $78 per token.

It has been revealed that the campaign opened on November 18 at 06:00 UTC and closes at 14:00 UTC. Eligible participants can apply through Binance Wallet using USDC, with a minimum subscription of 100 USDC. Successful applicants will receive tokenized Anthropic exposure following the allocation process.

The ANT IPO campaign uses an allocation system based on Binance Alpha Points. Owners with more than zero Alpha Points receive a subscription quota of $20,000. Higher balances unlock substantially larger limits. Users holding more than 40 points qualify for $100,000, while those exceeding 100 points receive $200,000.

The highest tier requires more than 200 Alpha Points and unlocks a $500,000 subscription quota. Binance also offers an additional $50,000 quota to eligible customers holding its Trenchers Badge.

These figures are the maximum subscription limits, not guaranteed allocations. The campaign uses an oversubscription model, meaning successful applicants could receive full or partial allocations.

Participants must commit USDC through a non-custodial smart contract. Unused funds will return to their Binance Wallet after allocation calculations finish. The subscription also carries a 5% partner service fee charged by xStocks. Binance Wallet does not charge an additional participation fee.

Sources indicate that binance has set an indicative subscription price between $70 and $78 per token. This matches Anthropic’s proposed IPO pricing range in its registration filing. Anthropic aims to offer 825 million Class A shares under the proposed listing. The organization has applied to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

Sources indicate that at the upper end of its proposed price range, the offering implies a valuation of approximately $1.41 trillion. At $70 per share, the implied valuation falls to $1.26 trillion.

The final offering price will depend on demand, valuation conditions, and arrangements between the issuer and underwriters.

The distribution process begins after the application window closes. Successful participants will receive their tokenized exposure after the underlying issuance is completed.
