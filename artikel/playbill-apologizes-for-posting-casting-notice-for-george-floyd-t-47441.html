Playbill is apologizing for running an ad for singers for a cast album called “George Floyd the Musical” from the Busted Broadway manufacturer.

Recent findings suggest that the project drew numerous negative comments from Broadway stars and many others. Busted Broadway responded on its social media Thursday that “The project ‘George Floyd the Musical’ is NOT making light of, condoning, or mocking the horrific 2020 murder of George Floyd. It’s a satire on the CULTURE, rather than the person. Never judge a show by its title, as many who have never seen the script or score appear to be doing now.” Related Stories How Netflix‘s Animated 'Steps’ Reimagines the Cinderella Story Through the Eyes of Her Asian Stepsisters and Finds a New Kind of Happily Ever After ‘Russell Brand: Open Secret’: Netflix Doc Reveals His ‘Jokes’ About Rape, Making a Girlfriend ‘Brush Her Teeth Until They Bled’ and More.

The theater publishing manufacturer Playbill wrote in a public apology, “Late yesterday afternoon, Playbill Jobs incorrectly approved a job listing that had no place on our site. Once leadership was alerted, the listing was immediately removed, and we began reviewing and revising our approval processes. We apologize unequivocally for giving this hateful listing any further air, and we promise to do better going forward. We at Playbill aim to lift up this community with empathy, consideration, and respect. We hope those who read and follow us do the same.

“We know this listing has upset and offended many, and the fact that it made it onto our site has disappointed those who trust us as an outlet,” the post continued.

“For that, we are sorry, and are committed to regaining your trust,” the apology concluded.

Busted Broadway “brings you musical cast recordings that are too ridiculous, too raunchy and too absurd to see the stage,” according to its website. “Busted Broadway is not for the mollycoddled or politically correct listener,” says the firm which has produced projects for “Human Centipede: The Musical” and “Genghis Khan: The Musical.” The company lists that “George Floyd: The Musical” is coming soon, according to its website, with the tagline “The $20 bill that changed the world.”.

Playbill noted in a statement that going forward, it would ban Busted Broadway from posting ads and would not write about their projects, according to Deadline.
