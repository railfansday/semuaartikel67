In a recent development, mcDonald’s has officially unveiled the Home Alone Meal, a nostalgia-heavy collab with Home Alone. Launching at participating U.S. restaurants nationwide on Dec. 1 (while supplies last), the limited-time promotion pairs breakfast, lunch, and dinner combos with latest pizza-inspired sides and one of four pairs of collectible Home Alone socks.

According to recent reports, truthfully, we could take or leave the menu items. But the socks? We want them bad.

There's a lot to like about this latest collab:.

Collectible Sock Designs: Every Home Alone Meal comes packed with one of four limited-edition holiday sock pairs featuring the Golden Arches and the McCallister house alongside character designs of Kevin McCallister, Grimace, the Hamburglar, or Birdie the Early Bird.

Little Nero’s Pizza Menu Hacks: You can order the meal at breakfast, lunch, or dinner. Afternoon and evening meals swap standard fries for Little Nero’s Pizza McShaker Fries — fries served with a packet of tangy tomato, garlic, Italian herb, and cheese seasoning that you shake up in the bag. You can also pair your order with the latest Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce (creamy tomato blended with aged Parmesan, herbs, and garlic).

It has been revealed that "The best McDonald's moments are the ones our fans make their own," remarked Morgan Flatley, Global Chief Marketing Officer and Head of New Business Ventures at McDonald's and interim U.S. Chief Creative Officer. "Bringing together two icons people already love, McDonald's and Home Alone, we're giving fans across the globe a holiday moment to share and celebrate, through nostalgic flavors, collectible keepsakes and the kind of playful experience only McDonald's can deliver.”.

In a recent development, we should note that the sock designs are distributed at random while supplies last. If you're determined to collect all four pairs without buying a whole lot of McDonald's, expect resellers to flood the market within 48 hours of the launch.
