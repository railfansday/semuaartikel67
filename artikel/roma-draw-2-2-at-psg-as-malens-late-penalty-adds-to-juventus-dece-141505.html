Roma draw 2-2 at PSG as Malen’s late penalty adds to Juventus’ December challenge

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roma came from behind to draw 2-2 with holders Paris Saint-Germain, with substitute Donyell Malen scoring a 78th-minute penalty. The leaders' rivals above Juventus indicated the depth of their squad less than a month before they host Spalletti's side.

Sources indicate that roma left the Parc des Princes with a point and with their reputation enhanced. Mario Hermoso gave them the lead against the European champions. Paris Saint-Germain turned the game around through an Illia Zabarnyi header and an Ousmane Dembélé penalty.

In a recent development, in Europe, Malen’s spot-kick was his fifth goal. Across all competitions, it marked his 17th strike of the campaign, from 16 appearances. It came days after he scored four times in Roma’s 5-1 win at Parma. Yet he did not start in Paris, and neither did Paulo Dybala. Gian Piero Gasperini mentioned he left Malen out because of the number of games he had played for Roma and the Netherlands.

That a reshuffled Roma could take a point in Paris says plenty about their squad. Luis Enrique’s side had not dropped points since a home defeat to Monaco on 4 September.

Recent findings suggest that the result does not fix Roma’s European position. The club lists the game as Matchday 5 of the league phase. Roma have one win, two draws and two defeats so far. Under the format, clubs finishing 9th to 24th enter the play-offs, while 25th to 36th are knocked out. The top eight go straight into the round of 16.

Industry observers note that that leaves Roma with work to do in a crowded December. Their next Champions League match is at home to Sporting CP on 8 December, kicking off at 21:00. They then travel to AEK Athens on 19 January and host Lille on 27 January 2027.

According to recent reports, domestically, Roma’s form has been strong. Their league record demonstrates nine wins and three draws, with 36 goals scored and 11 conceded, Corriere dello Sport reported.

In a recent development, for Spalletti’s side, the schedule offers some contrast. After that European fixture, Roma play the derby away to Lazio on Sunday 13 December at 12:30. That leaves them six days between the derby and the visit of Juventus. Before travelling to Rome, Juventus are away to Como on 29 November. Udinese and Monza then come to Turin in Serie A, on 6 December and 14 December respectively.

Whether Roma’s European commitments take a toll remains to be seen. The evidence from Paris suggests Gasperini can rotate without a sharp drop in quality. Malen’s form, from the bench or from the start, will be a central concern for Juventus.

The two sides meet in round 16 of Serie A on Saturday 19 December 2026, with kick-off at 20:45.
