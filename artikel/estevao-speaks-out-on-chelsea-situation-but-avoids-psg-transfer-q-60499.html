Estevao has refused to address reports linking him with a move to Paris Saint-Germain but says he has received ‘a lot of motivation’ during the international break having struggled for playing time at Chelsea this season.

The 19-year-old has started just one game in the Premier League this season and delivered an impressive performance in Chelsea’s 2-0 win at home to Manchester United on October 31.

However, Estevao was back on the bench for Chelsea’s 1-0 defeat to Sunderland on November 8, although he was brought on as a half-time substitute during the loss at the Stadium of Light.

Xabi Alonso praised Estevao’s response after he was left as an unused substitute during Chelsea’s penalty shootout defeat to Liverpool in the Carabao Cup last month and has insisted that the Brazilian winger ‘will have his moment’ this season.

However, reports have claimed that Estevao has been unhappy with his lack of playing time under Alonso and is considering his future at Chelsea.

Estevao started in both of Brazil’s friendlies during the international break and delivered a lively display during the 7-0 win over Singapore on Tuesday as he scored a goal and registered two assists.

‘I’m very happy to have received the minutes in the friendlies,’ Estevao told ESPN Brasil after his national side’s win at the National Stadium in Singapore.

‘I thank Ancelotti for his confidence because every player wants to play and at Chelsea I’ve had few minutes, but coming here and knowing that the manager trusts me gives me a lot of motivation.
