Shiba Inu (SHIB) kept leaving crypto exchanges this week as the price of the meme cryptocurrency fell to levels last seen in the spring of 2021, and on Wednesday, the outflow picked up sharply.

Sources indicate that according to CoinGlass data, 520.83 billion SHIB left exchanges over the past 24 hours. That amount made up most of the 757.51 billion SHIB that exchanges lost over the past seven days, and over the past 30 days, the outflow reached 2.88 trillion SHIB.

Recent findings suggest that on Wednesday evening, SHIB was trading at $0.00000344, according to CoinGlass. So far this week, the token was down 4.78%, and its market capitalization went down to $2.02 billion, according to TradingView.

In a recent development, according to the weekly chart on TradingView, SHIB last traded at these levels in the spring of 2021, months before the token reached its all-time high of $0.00008845 on Oct. 28, 2021.

From early 2026 until mid-November, the price of SHIB held above $0.000004, and the yearly low stood at $0.00000411 at the July 17 close. In November, SHIB fell to $0.00000373, and in late November, the token dropped as low as $0.00000337. On Jan. 1, the token closed the day at $0.00000692, and since the start of the year, SHIB has lost 50.3% of its value, according to CoinGlass. As of Nov. 22, SHIB had lost 22.7% in November so far, according to CryptoRank.

The latest drop in SHIB came after Bitcoin fell back below $60,000 this week. On Saturday, Nov. 21, Bitcoin dropped as low as $55,150, its lowest price since September 2024. Over the past 24 hours, $57,590 worth of SHIB futures positions were liquidated, and $54,750 of that amount came from long positions on OKX, according to CoinGlass.

The Wednesday outflow was not the first large one this month. On Nov. 17, about 312 billion SHIB were withdrawn from exchanges, according to CryptoQuant. On the same day, the number of SHIB holders on Ethereum passed 1.7 million, according to Etherscan.

CoinGlass data also shows that since late 2024, the total SHIB balance on exchanges has gone down by 19.18 trillion tokens, or 20.54%, to 74.19 trillion, and over the same period, the price of SHIB fell by 88.39%. The remaining 74.19 trillion SHIB on exchanges make up 12.59% of the circulating supply and are worth about $254.75 million.

Most of the 24-hour outflow came from exchanges that CoinGlass groups as "Others," as their combined SHIB balance fell by 88.5% to 70.93 billion tokens. Over the same period, the SHIB balances of Binance and OKX barely changed, and Coinbase, Bitfinex and Bitstamp added SHIB.

As a result, in the week when the price of SHIB fell to levels last seen in 2021, exchanges lost more SHIB in a single day than over the previous six days combined, according to CoinGlass data.
