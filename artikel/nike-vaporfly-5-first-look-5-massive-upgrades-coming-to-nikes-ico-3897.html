📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

I vividly remember the launch of the Nike Vaporfly 4% in 2017 — the bright blue shoe with a carbon plate that promised to help runners get 4% faster on race day. It changed how we think about race-day shoes for good. To celebrate the shoe’s 10th anniversary, Nike has unveiled the Vaporfly 5, the lightest, most aggressive version of the Vaporfly to date. Let’s put aside how old this all makes me feel and dive into the details of the redesigned racer.

In a recent development, nike has mentioned this is its lightest, fastest, most aggressive Vaporfly yet. The shoe has a redesigned Wave Flyplate, which contours around the foot to improve stability and increase energy return.

There’s a newly introduced midsole foam: ZoomX LT, which is 17% lighter and offers 8% more energy return than standard ZoomX. Plus, the shoe looks different from the Vaporfly we know and love, with a sizeable cutout on the side of the shoe to allow the Wave Flyplate to do its thing.

Tom’s Guide was invited to see a sneak peek of the shoe, and here’s what we learned:.

You can see from inital images of the super shoe that theres a sizeable cutout on the side of the shoe. This is to allow the wave-shaped plate to perform as intended, and offer the spring sensation under the arch of the foot.

It has been revealed that the shoe will cost $225, but it won’t be hitting the market until fall 2027, so we’ve got a while to wait. We will bring you our review once we’ve got the shoes on our feet, so stay tuned.
