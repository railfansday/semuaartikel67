Aston Villa are open to cutting short Alejandro Garnacho's loan deal from Chelsea in January after he fell down the pecking order, according to The Athletic.

The Argentina winger made the move to Villa Park in the summer on an initial season-long loan deal, with an obligation to make the move permanent next summer based on a select number of appearances being made.

But so far Garnacho has made just seven appearances for the club, and fresh speculation around his future emerged this week after he was left out of the matchday squad for the Champions League clash with Galatasaray, despite Unai Emery naming two goalkeepers on the bench and filling just 11 of his 12 substitute spots.

Now it has emerged that Villa are exploring the possibility of letting Garnacho leave the club in January, according to Jacob Tanswell.

It has been revealed that the deal with Chelsea includes no January break clause, and the Blues have no intention of taking him back at this stage. However, there is hope from Villa that Garnacho and Chelsea could strike an agreement for him to go elsewhere, and they would then be happy to see him move on as he does not presently feature in their plans.

Any exit would hinge on another side finding a solution for the 22-year-old, who has previously played for Manchester United and Chelsea in the last two seasons.

Speaking after the defeat to Galatasaray in midweek, Emery admitted that Garnacho was not someone he was counting on at this stage of the campaign.

"Today the players who were here are who I think are the best players to be here with us.".

Villa cancelling the loan deal would also end any chances of a permanent deal going through in the summer, which is reportedly expected to cost £42.5m should the obligation be triggered.

In a recent development, the Midlands side had a similar situation with Liverpool and Harvey Elliott last season, which ultimately ended with the England youth international sitting on the sidelines for much of the campaign before returning to Anfield in the summer. It now seems that they are looking to avoid that situation from happening again by finding a way to cut this arrangement short.

When Garnacho arrived during the summer, there was hope that Emery could wave his magic wand and get the winger back to the form which saw him being a regular starter for Man Utd under Erik ten Hag.

But it's clear that there are problems beyond what is happening on the pitch with him, having struggled to assert himself anywhere since falling out of favour at Old Trafford.

According to recent reports, villa are not in the best position financially, so they don't want to waste money on players that won't help the team. With Emery having full control at the club, it's clear that unless there is a major U-turn on Garnacho, he has no future at the club, and therefore signing him permanently would make no sense.

We have seen with the Elliott situation last season that the manager has no concern with leaving him out for the rest of the season and not using him, so it's best for everyone involved that a solution is found in January for him to go elsewhere and play.
