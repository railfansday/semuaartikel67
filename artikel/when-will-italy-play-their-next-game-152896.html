Italy have been knocked out of the Nations League group phase, so when is their next match?

The last international games of the year were played during the November break, so Italy, like most other countries worldwide, will return to action next year, precisely in March.

The Azzurri finished third in their Nations League group, seeing winners France and runners-up Belgium progress to the quarter-finals.

Despite the disappointment of the elimination, which experts at BetPanda had widely predicted, it is fair to say the Azzurri must see the glass half full.

Their Nations League campaign featured victories over Turkiye and France, and only their two losses to Belgium sealed the Nazionale’s elimination.

Italy, however, still managed to secure a spot among the seeded teams in the Euro 2028 qualifying draw thanks to their Nations League results.

It has been revealed that as previously mentioned, the next international break is in March 2027, running from Monday 22 to Tuesday 30. As the Azzurri won’t be playing in the Nations League quarter-finals, they’ll start their Euro 2028 qualifying campaign straight away.

Industry observers note that italy will learn their opponents on December 6, when the qualifying draw takes place. The first match will be played on March 26, 2027 and the second on March 29. The Azzurri will likely be placed in one of the four-team qualifying groups after their elimination from the Nations League.

Regardless of their opponents, the Azzurri will look to start their qualification campaign strongly and bounce back after a 4-1 loss to Belgium in their most recent match.

“These nights can happen in the growth process; you need to learn from these experiences. Considering the way we got here, we had the chance to qualify, but we knew it would be tough, as Belgium are a strong team,” Mancini said at full time in Belgium.

“We are sad, but this night will help us going forward.”.

Sources indicate that mancini returned for a second spell as Italy’s head coach in July. He has grabbed three wins, one draw and two defeats in his first six games in charge in his second spell. The Euro 2020 winner has signed a contract until 2030, when, hopefully, he’ll bring the Nazionale back to the World Cup.

Italy already know that their captain Gianluigi Donnarumma will miss the next international match after being booked in the most recent match against Belgium.

Gigio was one yellow card away from suspension prior to the game in Belgium, so he’ll miss the next one.
