Xabi Alonso claims he takes the “whole responsibility” for Chelsea’s dismal defending but disagrees with his friend Jamie Carragher that Wesley Fofana and Levi Colwill will never be a good enough centre-back pairing for the Blues.

Fofana and Colwill were heavily criticised for their performances last Saturday as the west Londoners were thrashed 4-1 at home to Leeds in the Premier League.

The defeat exposed further weaknesses in Alonso’s defence and proved the worst possible response to their disappointing previous outing, the 1-0 defeat to Sunderland before the international break.

Chelsea head to the City Ground to face Nottingham Forest on Friday night eager to get back to winning ways.

Analysing the Leeds loss, Carragher mentioned on Sky Sports: “Chelsea have got a massive problem at centre-back and if they don’t change it in January, they’re going to have a massive problem in terms of where they feel they should be this season with the Champions League.”.

Speaking about Fofana and Colwill, he added: “As a partnership, this will never work because they’re not physical enough and there’s no real leader there.”.

Asked whether he had seen these comments from his former Liverpool team-mate, Alonso stated he had not, before adding: “I must disagree. I disagree with Carra. For sure, you can have bad games. We were not good [against Leeds]. But I think I’ve seen them [Colwill and Fofana] play well, and I’ve seen them show good levels.

“This is the goal and the challenge – to be consistent in the performance and to be consistent in the basics and not to drop in certain games when we are facing a tough opponent.”.

According to recent reports, alonso, speaking more broadly about Chelsea’s poor defending, added: “There are no excuses not to do better. We are in the process. Taking steps, taking a step forward but until it is totally in the DNA of the team it takes time.

It has been revealed that “I take the whole responsibility of the team and how they perform. We need to show [improvement against Forest], not just talk. Talk is cheap. It’s about performing on the pitch.

According to recent reports, “We want to be back on that more solid feeling that we had. This was hopefully one-[off], and we don’t want to repeat it.”.
