Recent findings suggest that REI has never believed in Black Friday. A cynic might say it’s smug in that fact—with its long-running OptOutside campaign. We, however, still find it charming, even during this day and age when there’s so much to be cynical about.

It has been revealed that that mentioned, the co-op has started to do holiday discounts, usually running through the back half of November and into December. For those quick on the draw, there’s no difference. It happens, same-ish time and same-ish place, every year. These are the best deals you’ll ever find at REI, but they are not doorbuster, Amazon, oh my god deals. It’s a lot more tame than that. Still, if you’re looking for jackets, thermal layers, or outdoor gear (like sleeping bags, backpacks, and camping chairs), the time to shop is now.

Fjallraven Vardag G-1000 Pile Jacket.

It has been revealed that the three best deals, for us, are above. If you’re looking for real performance wear, it’s the Arc’teryx Cerium. If you’re getting a down jacket, a Cerium is the one you want—packs well, keeps you warm, plenty durable. For something more fashion-y, go Fjällräven fleece-lined. And, if you are an actual outdoorsman—going out hunting, hiking, or boating—you need a Garmin inReach. The Mini 3 Plus is the best in the lineup at the moment, and a 20-percent-off deal is a solid score.
