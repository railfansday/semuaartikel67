It has been revealed that HOUSTON, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cambrian Innovation has commissioned and is operating a wastewater treatment and resource recovery system at a dairy processing plant in Wisconsin. The system treats approximately 280,000 gallons per day, or about 102 million gallons annually, and converts the plant's organic waste into renewable natural gas (RNG). Cambrian designed, built, owns, and operates the system under a 20-year, pay-per-gallon agreement, with no upfront capital cost to the plant.

The system replaces lagoon-based treatment and the spray application of roughly 100 million gallons of partially treated water per year. The plant's objectives were long-term regulatory compliance and lower operating costs. Treated water is discharged to a local tributary under the site's National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit, and RNG is prepared for injection into the local pipeline.

"The engineering of this particular system was instrumental in protecting the environment and generating a valuable resource of natural gas," remarked Joshua Hanlon, VP of Operations at Cambrian. "The advanced treatment of ethylene effluent meets stringent NPDES requirements for discharge to a local small creek. High quality renewable natural gas is produced from the waste and sold back to the local community.".

In a recent development, the project reflects Cambrian's resource-recovery approach: treated water returns to the watershed, biosolids return to land as beneficial reuse, and organic waste is converted to pipeline-grade renewable energy. Cambrian's own operators run the system day to day, including solids processing, which removes the operation of the wastewater system from the plant's scope. Cambrian offers the same pay-per-gallon model across manufacturing and commercial industries.

According to recent reports, founded in 2011, Cambrian offers wastewater treatment and reuse to a wide range of industries: food and beverage, data center, pulp and paper, pharmaceutical, oil and gas, steel, and more. Cambrian's vision is industry as an ecosystem. Biological systems are inherently sustainable, and Cambrian applies them to industrial wastewater treatment and resource recovery. Cambrian designs, builds, owns, and operates these systems on-site as a service, with no upfront capital cost to the client. Cambrian-owned systems operate at more than 35 sites and have treated nearly 5 billion gallons of water.
