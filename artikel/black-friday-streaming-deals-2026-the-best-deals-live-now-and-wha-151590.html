The majority of our favorite streamers raised subscription prices, including Peacock, Apple TV, Disney+, Hulu, Netflix, ESPN, and Paramount+. Viewers everywhere are looking to save a little cash — particularly with Christmas coming up — so the raft of Black Friday streaming deals are very welcome indeed.

It has been revealed that most streaming deals have been live since early this week, but a few more have joined in for Black Friday proper. The majority of them expire on Nov. 30, aka Cyber Monday, unless otherwise noted. So don't hesitate to secure these offers as soon as possible — you really don't want to miss out.

I have to be honest here: most streaming deals aren't going to turn your finances around in any life-changing way. The Black Friday streaming price cuts have become steadily less tempting over the past few years. They're just not as impressive as they used to be. It's also worth noting that most deals apply only to the ad-supported tiers (with just a few exceptions). Still, any and every discount is welcome in a time when prices keep rising. Every cent counts, and it's easy to lose track of just how much you spend on streaming — especially if you're signed up for more than one service.

Sources indicate that most streamers announced their Black Friday deals early this week, but you may be wondering if there are still more to come. With just a few days until Cyber Monday, I'd say the best streaming deals have already arrived. I can't imagine that many more will pop up at this stage.

Recent findings suggest that I'm shocked that Peacock's Black Friday deal is as good as it is for 2026. Last year, the streamer didn't offer a deal at all, so this is light-years better than I was expecting. If you've been holding off on subscribing, now is likely the only time you'll get a decent discount. The Paramount+, HBO Max, and Disney+/Hulu Black Friday offers are pretty much on par with their deals of years past. I would lock in HBO Max and the Disney+/Hulu bundle for a full year, as more price increases could pop up in 2027. Paramount+ tends to offer discounts throughout the year more than any other streamer, so if you miss out on this one, it's no biggie.

Sources indicate that it's not worth holding on for a Netflix Black Friday deal, unfortunately. Netflix has never offered a Black Friday discount, or any kind of discount for that matter. Netflix is the top name in streaming and obviously feels that it doesn't need to offer Black Friday deals. I don't expect this year to be any different, but I'm always willing to be proven wrong. It would be a nice and welcome surprise.

We'll be updating this list of the best Black Friday streaming deals on the off chance any more turn up, so be sure to check back for updates.
