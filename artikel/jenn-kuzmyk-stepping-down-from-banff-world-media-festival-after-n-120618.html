It’s the end of an era for the Banff World Media Festival with its long-serving boss Jenn Kuzmyk stepping down.

In a recent development, kuzmyk will leave her role as Executive Director of Banff and publisher of Canadian trade title Playback on December 11. Both brands are part of Toronto-based Brunico Communications, the media publishing and events business.

Industry observers note that on an interim basis, Brunico President and CEO Russell Goldstein will lead the commercial elements of the 2027 Banff festival alongside Mary Maddever, Brunico’s Chief Content Officer. Kuzmyk will move into a short-term consulting role to support the transition. It has been revealed that kuzmyk is considered the driving force behind establishing Banff was a major event on the global entertainment industry calendar each summer in Canada. She has focused on internationalizing the fest with execs from more than 50 countries participating, and building out the conference program and Rockie Awards. Speakers at the conference have included Ted Sarandos, Jeffrey Katzenberg, Channing Dungey and Pearlena Igbokwe. Watch on Deadline.

According to recent reports, “Jenn’s vision and passion have left an indelible mark on both Banff and Playback,” said Goldstein. “Under her watch, she has elevated these iconic brands with a consummate blend of strategic leadership and innovation. Building on this exceptional foundation, Banff 2027 is poised for growth under the stewardship of our remarkably talented and dedicated team. We are deeply grateful for Jenn’s contributions and wish her every success in her next chapter.”.

Sean Cohan, President of Bell Media and chair of the Banff directors board, added that she had “steered Banff through an era of rapid industry evolution with grace, foresight, and integrity, expanding its international reach while creating vital, career-defining opportunities for industry participants at every level.”.

It has been revealed that “Leading Banff and Playback has been the privilege of my career,” mentioned Kuzmyk. “I want to thank Sean and the Banff board of directors for their trust, guidance and commitment to the festival, and our supporters, partners and sponsors, whose belief in Banff makes everything we do possible.

“To the Banff and Playback teams: you are the reason the festival and publication are as strong as they have ever been, and it has been a joy to work alongside you. I’m especially grateful to Russell Goldstein for his years of support and partnership. I leave knowing both Banff and Playback have never been in better shape, and I’ll share more about what’s next in the Fresh Year for me.”.
