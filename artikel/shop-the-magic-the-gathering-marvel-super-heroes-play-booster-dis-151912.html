TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Play Booster Display Box is on sale for $104.90 at Amazon, down from its $147.95 list price.

Of all the Amazon discounts we've seen on Magic: The Gathering's Marvel Super Heroes expansion since its June debut, stocking up on Play Boosters has never been so cheap. That's thanks to this new Black Friday deal for trading card collectors.

As of Nov. 27, the Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Play Booster Display Box is now available for only $104.90 on Amazon. With $43.05 taken off its current $147.95 list price (29% off), you're now paying only $3.50 per pack.

What’s more, this new $104.90 deal is only $9.96 above TCGplayer market price of $98.94 — closer than we’ve ever seen before.

It has been revealed that beating every Amazon deal we've previously covered for the box, this deal’s actually sold and shipped by Amazon instead of a third-party seller — with Prime members also getting free delivery by Nov. 28 at the time of writing.

Sources indicate that across the display box’s 30 14-card boosters, Marvel comic book lovers can hunt for everything from Infinity Stones to Classic Comic Card treatments. Alongside a guaranteed Traditional Foil, each booster houses up to four rare-or-better cards, with a foil land appearing in 20% of packs.
