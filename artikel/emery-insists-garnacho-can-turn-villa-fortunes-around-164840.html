Unai Emery insists Alejandro Garnacho can turn things around at Aston Villa, despite a difficult start to his loan tenure.

The Argentine joined Villa on a season-long loan from Chelsea, with the Villans reportedly having an obligation to buy him if he reaches a certain number of appearances.

Garnacho has struggled so far under Emery, failing to score or assist in seven matches this season, with all of his appearances coming off the bench.

Among players to have made at least seven appearances across Europe's top five leagues this season, only Charly Alcaraz (Everton) and Carlos Espi (Real Madrid) have played fewer minutes than Garnacho (95).

Garnacho was left out of the Villa squad for their trip to Galatasaray in midweek, with Emery instead choosing two goalkeepers and only filling 11 of his 12 substitute spots.

However, the Spaniard has encouraged Garnacho to keep improving and pushing for a place in the squad.

"When he is not involved in the squad, it is because I have to choose from other players as well," Emery said.
