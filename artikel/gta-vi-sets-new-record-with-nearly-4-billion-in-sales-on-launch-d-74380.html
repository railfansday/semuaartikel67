The highly anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto VI shattered video game sales records, earning $3.79 billion in the first 24 hours of its launch.

It has been revealed that already, the 13-year wait for the latest installment of the crime spree series had racked up more than 32.5 million pre-orders, according to Rockstar Games and GTA parent firm Take-Two. After Thursday’s numbers came in, the companies dubbed the franchise the “highest-grossing entertainment property” and the “biggest entertainment launch in history.”.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar, said in a statement, per The Hollywood Reporter. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world, and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.” Trending Stories Patrick Stewart Cause of Death Revealed ‘Yes, Dear’ Star Anthony Clark's Cause of Death Revealed Selena Gomez Shares Her Reaction to Martin Short Ruining Her Wedding Cake Marlon Jackson Reveals Stage 4 Prostate Cancer Diagnosis.

Sources indicate that GTA VI was already competing against itself for the highest release-day sales. Its predecessor, GTA V, earned $815.7 million within 24 hours of its September 2013 release, while Call of Duty: Black Ops II pulled in $500 million in its first day on shelves in November 2012.

It has been revealed that the video game has earned rave reviews, including an aggregated score of 99 on Metacritic and perfect scores from gaming critics. “It raises the bar at every level that matters,” Gene Park wrote for The Washington Post. “Grand Theft Auto VI is the most extraordinary game I’ve ever reviewed.”.
