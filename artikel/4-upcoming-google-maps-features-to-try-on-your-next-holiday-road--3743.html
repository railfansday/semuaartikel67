Recent findings suggest that even as gas prices dampen some travel plans, you’ll still have plenty of tech giant on the roads this holiday season. AAA predicts that 73 million Americans will travel by car for Thanksgiving between Nov. 24 and Nov. 30. Google Maps is ready to accompany travelers on their journeys with four new features just in time for holiday travel near and far.

Sources indicate that in a press release, Google said it’s rolling out four latest Ask Maps features that make it easy to create itineraries, find available appointments, and search your saved lists. Here’s what to know about the latest features coming to Google Maps.

Google has made it easy to build step-by-step itineraries and get real-time route details when you pull up directions. You can use Ask Maps to blueprint simple tasks, like a day of holiday shopping, or more complex plans, like a multi-day road trip with stops along the way.

You can get a day-by-day schedule with tips and numbered map pins for each stop, which makes it easy to visualize your plan. You can also share your itinerary with friends and family, or export it to Google Docs. It’s like being your own trip planner without outsourcing the work to a travel agent. To access your itinerary, ask Maps to pull it up, or look for the “itinerary” badge in your chat history. This way, you can see real-time details like live traffic conditions, whether a place is open, and more.

Maps can help you stay ahead. You can now pull up directions with helpful tips before you go, tailored to your route and proposal. These include tips on where to park or charge your car, as well as real-time updates on conditions such as weather, potential delays, tolls, and more.

We’re heading into the most wonderful (and busiest) time of year for travel and shopping, and Google wants to reduce your stress. Thanks to new agentic capabilities, you can have Ask Maps find beauty, pet care, auto, and home repair appointments online and call local businesses for you. Ask, “Find a 4 p.m. hair appointment near me,” and Ask Maps will show you open slots at nearby businesses so you can book and go. It’s that easy.

If you’re playing host for the holidays, Google will help you show off all your town’s favorite spots. Now you can use Ask Maps to find saved places on your lists. Ask things like, “Which spots saved in my lists have availability for 6 people in the next half-hour?” Ask Maps scans across your lists and checks live availability so you can pick a place and just go.

Sources indicate that updates begin rolling out today on Android and iOS, and Ask Maps is also expanding to 30 countries across Europe, including Spain, Germany, and the UK. Ask Maps will now be available in more than 200 countries and territories. Travel itineraries and list search will be available everywhere Ask Maps is offered, while route tips and appointment locating will first launch in the US, with more regions to follow soon, according to Google.
