Grand Theft Auto 6 makes it more challenging than the previous game to make a lot of money quickly, but that doesn't mean there aren't ways to do it. Some jobs pay better than others, while others let you earn money faster. It largely comes down to your play style, but there are some simple options.

In a recent development, whether you want to avoid the cops more often is entirely up to you, and if you do, you'll have a much better time with certain moneymaking methods than others. Whatever you choose, consistency is the most notable thing. It can be a grind out there in Vice City.

It has been revealed that of all the ways to make money fast, especially early on, things like boosting cars or winning races can really speed up the process. You can also stick up stores, but the convenience stores are less of a way to go. Focus on the bigger takes to make a real financial impact.

Boosting cars becomes an option while Jason and Lucia are hiding out in Port Gellerhorn. When you meet Moses, you can start selling stolen cars, although his prices aren't nearly as good as what you can get from other characters as you progress through the game.

Industry observers note that regardless, it's a quick-and-dirty way to make decent money, especially once you've gotten the hang of which cars he can sell and which ones to avoid. Use the Waink app often to get a feel for which cars have trackers and which ones don't, and you can boost plenty of cars pretty swiftly.

If you want to steer clear of the cops, aim for cars that are already unlocked. While most cars being actively driven won't be locked and alarmed, robbing them will alert the cops almost every time, so it's not the safest bet. Instead, look for parked cars that aren't alarmed, then stick to using the Slim Jim.

Be aware that you'll have to unlock the key cloner before you can steal certain cars that require it, otherwise you'll just sit still in the passenger seat. The Waink app will also tell you this before you break in.

It has been revealed that as you get further into the game, you'll meet more people like Randy who can sell higher-value cars, regardless of the trackers they've got installed. This will earn you even more money per car, but the process remains pretty much the same.

Sources indicate that winning races is one of the best ways to make money fast once you've unlocked them. They pay well into the thousands per win, but that number drops the worse you perform. Depending on the race, it might be a bit challenging to get the hang of, but it's worth it.

You usually have to buy into many types of races, and if you don't have a compatible vehicle, then you also have to pay to rent one for the race. However, those costs are drastically outweighed by the amount you bring in when you win.

To avoid losing those fees every time you don't win a race, you can play the system a bit. Before the race ends, you can pause the game and restart it, which doesn't charge you the buy-in or rental fees a second time. That way, you can essentially keep trying over and over until you get the result you're looking for.

Another one of the best options for making money promptly is to stick up some of the right locations. Not every store is the best to stick up, such as the stores that don't have a cash register. These won't yield much, but neither will just a standard convenience store.

Instead, you want to focus on some of the more lucrative store options, like pawn shops. These carry much more valuable items that you can fence elsewhere, rather than just a few stacks of cash that go straight into your pocket.

These locations are much more difficult to rob than a regular convenience store.

Industry observers note that jewelry stores are another great option for sticking up because the takes are so much higher than other stores that require a similar amount of effort. These are jobs best done by both Jason and Lucia, allowing one of them to cover the crowd while the other grabs the goods.

It has been revealed that regardless of what method you choose, the best way to maximize the money you make is to keep playing through the main story missions. These will get you to better points in the game with more opportunities, and soon, you'll have more money than you know what to do with.
