Recent findings suggest that if you want to spend a little less time doomscrolling and a more time reading stuff you’ll actually remember, an e-reader you can actually slip into a pocket is a good place to start. Right now, the XTEINK X4 Pro is down to $84, taking $15 off its usual $99 price. The offer runs through November 30.

The appeal here is pretty straightforward: this is a dedicated E Ink reader that doesn’t need its own spot in your bag. The X4 Pro weighs just 72g and measures 5.95mm thick, with built-in magnets that let it attach to a compatible phone or magnetic case. It’s designed for the handful of pages you can fit into a train ride, a lunch break, or the wait before an appointment.

Sources indicate that its 4.3-inch display is smaller than what you’d find on a typical Kindle, but that’s the point. You’re trading some screen space for something easier to keep with you. There’s a touchscreen for tapping and swiping through menus, plus physical buttons for turning pages one-handed.

According to recent reports, the feature that makes the Pro worth a closer look is its adjustable front light. Unlike the X3 and X4 Classic, it lets you keep reading when the room gets dark, with brightness and warm-to-cool adjustments for settling into a few chapters before bed. That’s a useful upgrade on a device small enough to take from the morning commute to the nightstand.

You also get a 16GB microSD card, support for cards up to 256GB, and EPUB and TXT document support.

For someone who already has a larger reader at home, the X4 Pro makes sense as the smaller one that comes along for the day. It’s also a gift under $100 that doesn’t feel like another phone accessory, even if it can ride on the back of a Pixel or iPhone (or Galaxy with a compatible case).
