A major issue for Watford this campaign has been the midfield. The summer departure of Imran Louza was a major blow, with his technical qualities, set-piece delivery and ability to dictate games sorely missed.

It has been revealed that similarly, Giorgi Chakvetadze’s transfer to Udinese has left the Hornets devoid of a creative spark, with his tendency to drift centrally and unlock defences. The club have simply not recruited adequate replacements, and they are suffering for it.

Industry observers note that the current midfield options at Vicarage Road consist of Hector Kyprianou, Edo Kayembe, Edoardo Bove, and recent signing Martin Payero. None of these options are particularly offensive or astute going forward, lacking the ball-carrying and forward-thinking impetus of Chakvetadze, and the game-changing potential of Louza.
