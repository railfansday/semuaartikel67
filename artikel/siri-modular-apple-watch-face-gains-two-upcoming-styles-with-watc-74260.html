Siri Modular, the new Apple Watch face in watchOS 27, is gaining new style options. watchOS 27.3, at the moment in beta, adds two more style options to the existing set. This gives people five options for adjusting what type of information appears on Siri Modular.

Industry observers note that siri Modular originally shipped with three style options. Each supports a small line complication above the time.

Additionally, the original three styles include these options:.

In watchOS 27.3, Apple introduces two fresh Siri features that work with Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4.

Industry observers note that first, there’s Siri Recap which uses the Apple Watch to turn audio into high-level text summaries. Second, there’s Live Rewind which lets you view a transcript of the last 15 seconds of spoken word.

The two latest Siri Modular Apple Watch face styles help surface three things: invoking Siri, viewing conversations with Siri, and viewing Siri Recaps on the Apple Watch.

The fresh styles include a pill-shaped module that includes icon shortcuts that go directly to Siri conversations, invoke Siri AI, or go directly to Siri Recaps in the Siri app.

Industry observers note that one style features a large digital clock with the newly introduced three-button Siri module. Another includes a compact digital clock with a Smart Stack tile and the new three-button Siri module.

This brings the style options to five. If you use the three circular complications configuration, you can also assign a Siri icon that invokes Siri AI.

watchOS 27.3 is at the moment in beta and expected to be released officially in December.
