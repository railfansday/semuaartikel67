Windstar Cruises has confirmed its Thankful for Travel 2026 promotion, featuring added value on more than 600 voyages through March 2028.

For bookings made from November 18 to December 2, 2026, on select voyages departing through March 31, 2028, guests get a complimentary All-Inclusive package valued at $99 per guest, $300 onboard credit per stateroom and reduced deposits of $150 per person.

Featured destinations include Europe, Greece, Iceland and Tahiti, as well as voyages aboard the new Star Explorer, debuting in December 2026.

Select specialty voyages and Star Collector itineraries are excluded from the offer.

“The holidays are a natural time for travelers to start thinking about where they want to go next, and travel advisors play such an crucial role in turning those ideas into memorable experiences,” said Janet Bava, chief commercial officer of Windstar Cruises.

“With Thankful for Travel, we’re giving our partners a compelling opportunity to help clients plan ahead, with meaningful added value and exciting options across Europe, Tahiti and beyond.”.

For a particularly memorable start to 2027, guests can ring in the latest year aboard the 224-guest Star Explorer on its nine-night maiden voyage roundtrip from Barcelona on December 28.

Other holiday sailings include Windstar’s 2026 Christmas cruises sailing either French Polynesia or Panama and Costa Rica.

French Polynesia itineraries will be operated by two ships in 2027 as the Wind Star is set to join the Star Breeze in March.

Apart from this, guests can opt for Windstar’s year-round Mediterranean voyages to discover the Greek Isles or go farther north and explore Iceland and Northern Europe.
