Latest Update: Wendy's Is Making Major Changes To Its Menu—Including A Creamier Frosty

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that wendy's is betting that better bacon and a creamier Frosty could help its sales slump. After reporting another decline in U.S. sales, the fast food chain is testing changes to several of its best-known menu items. This refresh will appear on the menu at some point in 2027, according to Fox News.

According to recent reports, the most interesting update might be the Frosty, which Wendy's is working to make richer and creamier. The chain hasn't revealed the exact recipe changes, but given how recognizable the dessert has become, I'm curious how much the fresh version will differ. Whether or not it wins over fans who already know and love the original consistency is also up in the air.

It has been revealed that then there's the bacon. Wendy's is testing thicker, crispier bacon that could give sandwiches like the Baconator a more substantial bite. Personally, I think this is a pretty smart move. There's nothing more disappointing than ordering a bacon cheeseburger and barely noticing the bacon.

The chain is also experimenting with whole-leaf lettuce on sandwiches and upgraded salads, all part of an effort to improve the quality of its existing menu rather than relying entirely on fresh products.

Recent findings suggest that these changes aren’t coming out of nowhere. Wendy's reported a 5.7% decline in U.S. same-restaurant sales during the third quarter of 2026, marking another difficult period for the chain. In response, CEO Bob Wright is introducing a multi-year turnaround strategy called “Dave's Way,” named after Wendy's founder Dave Thomas.

Industry observers note that the strategy focuses on improving the menu, marketing, restaurant operations, and digital ordering experience while giving customers better value for their money. But depending on your nearest location, it might come at a cost: breakfast.

Wendy's is allowing franchise operators to decide whether offering breakfast makes financial sense for their individual restaurants. That means some locations could stop serving breakfast altogether, although the manufacturer isn't eliminating the morning menu nationwide.

Industry observers note that wendy's also plans to simplify its offerings by removing lower-selling items that make restaurant kitchens more complicated to operate. However, the chain hasn't provided a complete list of products that could disappear.

The chain is still testing upgraded ingredients, so we won’t know the extent of the menu refresh or when it’ll roll out until some point in 2027. But if you’re anything like me, you’ll be waiting with bated breath to see how Wendy’s changes one of the most iconic fast food desserts.
