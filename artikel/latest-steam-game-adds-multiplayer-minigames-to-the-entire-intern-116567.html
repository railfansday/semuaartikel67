A upcoming Steam game that lets players explore the internet and play minigames together in a single web browser could become your group’s next obsession.

From viral browser games that allow you to stroke virtual cats to gacha games that let players battle it out in Pokemon card-style battles with Wikipedia pages, 2026 has been a fantastic year for weird and wonderful games.

Joining this list is INTERNET4, an upcoming Steam game that lets you and your friends fish, compete in general knowledge quizzes, draw, and browse the internet together.

It has been revealed that browsing the significant World Wide Web is usually a solitary thing, but the developers behind INTERNET4 have decided this no longer needs to be the case. Instead, you and eight buddies will be able to team up and explore websites together.

It has been revealed that the main highlight is Wikipedia Challenge, which creates minigames based on information from the popular website and those across the web. You’ll need to follow information between articles to reach the target page, while Research Battle has you investigating a random topic.

The latter requires you to show off your answer and even vote amongst your pals to choose the winner. There’s also a more relaxed fishing minigame (who doesn’t like a bit of video game fishing?) where you can catch, record, and compare your bites while surfing the net.

Tuning into your favorite livestreams and watching videos together is also an option for those who would rather spend their time doomscrolling memes. INTERNET4 also lets you initiative trips by exploring destinations or pull up a game map to discuss strategies between adrenaline-filled gaming sessions.
