Tech platforms would have to pay even when they surface news link previews or story snippets in their products, including AI search products. The law would also force platforms to compensate publishers when their journalism is used to train AI models.

According to recent reports, any compensation would be paid through a state-run management organization, which would then distribute the funds to publishers. Platforms that don’t comply could face sanctions, including the loss of their local business permits.

Social media and search engine companies have come out in force against the bill, with Google leading the campaign.
