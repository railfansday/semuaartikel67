What if your brand’s biggest opportunity was completely hidden within your existing assets?

In this episode of Marketing Vanguard, Jenny Rooney sits down with AARP CMO Anna Banks to unpack the challenge of repositioning a brand with more than 125 million Americans aged 50 and over in its sights.

Anna gets into why the second half of life deserves a different story, why celebrity partnerships need to lead somewhere meaningful, and why AARP’s next chapter depends on connecting the many touchpoints it already owns.

Anna also shares how performance marketing translates into a mission-driven organization, what makes a partnership genuinely valuable, and why good leadership starts with listening.

Anna Banks is the Chief Marketing Officer at AARP with over 30 years of marketing experience across brand strategy, digital, ecommerce, and performance marketing under her belt.

Her career has taken her from Lotus Development Corporation and 15 years on the agency side to Walmart eCommerce, Fairtrade USA, and Sephora. At AARP, she’s bringing those experiences together to help reposition a legacy brand and connect more meaningfully with the 125 million Americans aged 50 and over.

Recent findings suggest that her approach brings together consumer insight, brand engagement and performance-driven conversion. In this conversation, she shares how AARP is thinking about relevance, partnerships, technology and the opportunity to change how people think about aging.

10:31 AARP Has a Relevance Challenge — AARP is a legacy powerhouse, but keeping membership growth in step with the changing population means making sure more people understand how relevant the organization is to their lives.

It has been revealed that 12:28 The Second Half of Life Is the Most Creative Act — What if people stopped thinking about the second half of life as a period of decline? Anna shares how AARP wants people to see it as a chance to figure out what really matters and what drives them.

13:49 Celebrity Gets Attention. The Challenges Keep It. — From Eva Longoria to George Clooney, celebrity partnerships help AARP catch people’s attention. But the stories around caregiving, representation and aging are what make those partnerships matter.

15:51 Your Brand’s Assets Need to Work Together — Anna talks about the opportunity to connect AARP’s magazine, website and other channels, and why the organization needs to show up wherever people are already consuming media.

17:11 Stop Underestimating the 50+ Audience — Think people over 50 aren’t using mobile search or smart TVs? Anna challenges those assumptions and points out that the average 50+ consumer uses seven tech devices.

26:22 Performance Marketing Has a Place at AARP — Anna explains how her experience at Sephora translates to AARP’s membership acquisition and advertising model, and why performance marketing is just as relevant in a mission-driven organization.

Jenny Rooney is Chief Brand and Community Officer, leading strategy for the overall ADWEEK brand as well as the ways in which we serve and support our audiences with high-value content, products, partnerships and experiences, notably through our community programs such as Marketing Vanguard.
