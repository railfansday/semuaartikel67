📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, U2’s latest studio album Carnaval de Luz makes a splash on the Billboard charts as the set debuts in the top 10 on seven rankings (dated Nov. 28) – including No. 1 on Top Rock Albums, Vinyl Albums and Indie Store Album Sales.

It also stakes a claim in the top 10 on the Billboard 200 (No. 4, the band’s 14th top 10 on the chart), Top Album Sales (No. 2), Top Alternative Albums (No. 2) and Top Rock & Alternative Albums (No. 2).

U2 captured their first top 10 album on any Billboard chart with The Joshua Tree in 1987, when it debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard 200 dated April 4 of that year, on its way to a nine-week run at No. 1.

According to recent reports, carnaval de Luz launches with 49,000 equivalent album units earned in the United States in the week ending Nov. 19, according to Luminate. Of that sum, album sales comprise 42,500 (22,000 from vinyl sales — the band’s best week on vinyl since Luminate began tracking data in 1991), SEA units comprise 5,500 (equaling 5.77 million on-demand official streams of the set’s songs) and TEA units comprise 1,000.

Industry observers note that the upcoming album was preceded by its lead single “Silencio,” which marked the group’s 45th entry on the Alternative Airplay chart earlier in November (tying Foo Fighters for the most in the chart’s history). On Wednesday night (Nov. 25), U2 will headline the halftime show of the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve NFL game, which kicks off at 8 p.m. ET on Netflix. The veteran band also revealed North American dates for The Greatest Show on Earth World Tour this week, launching with an August concert in Miami.

Recent findings suggest that elsewhere on the latest Top Album Sales chart, ATEEZ notches its eighth No. 1 with the chart-topping debut of GOLDEN HOUR : Part.6, while The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack (No. 4) and LNGSHOT’s S.W.I.S.H. (No. 7) also debut.
