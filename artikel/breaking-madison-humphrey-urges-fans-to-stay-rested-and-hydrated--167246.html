Madison Humphrey is sharing a message of self-care after returning home from the hospital in time for Thanksgiving.

“I just wanted to say thank you so much for all the kind messages checking in,” Humphrey, 27, wrote via her Instagram Story on Thursday, November 26, one day after she was rushed to the emergency room.

The influencer — who is best known for her “Divorced Mom” and “Leasing Manager” sketches — added that she was “super thankful to be home resting and on the mend!”.

Humphrey posted a photo of herself holding what appeared to be get-well-soon flowers in the kitchen, as she reminded her followers to take care of themselves amid cold and flu season.

“There’s a lot going around this year so a good reminder to stay mindful, rested and hydrated,” she wrote, but didn’t give any more details about her condition.

According to recent reports, the TikToker did, however, joke, “And yes I did (finally) get to finish DWTS! Congratulations to Erza [Frech] and Daniella [Karagach] on their beautiful win!!” referring to the Tuesday, November 24, season 35 finale of the dance competition.

“Wishing everyone a wonderful Thanksgiving today,” she concluded.

Humphrey’s upbeat message came one day after she informed her followers that she was in the hospital.

“One minute you’re watching the Dancing With the Stars finale and the next, being rushed to the ER,” Humphrey wrote via her Instagram Story on Wednesday, November 25.

She posted a picture lying in a hospital bed, wearing sweatpants, a T-shirt and Ugg boots while being hooked up to a monitor.

While Humphrey didn’t give any more information about what happened, she thanked the “incredible medical staff who work holidays, nights, days and long hours to keep us all safe and healthy.”.

Humphrey is a content creator who is known for her comedic sketches and parody videos on TikTok, many of which have been pegged to Dancing With the Stars.

She has amassed five million followers on TikTok and was named one of the 10 creators in the Icon category for the platform’s 2026 The Discover List.

Last month, Humphrey declared that her leasing manager character was getting made into a TV show with Tubi, taking her comedic chops to the next level.
