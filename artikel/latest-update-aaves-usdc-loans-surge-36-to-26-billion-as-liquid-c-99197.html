It has been revealed that aave DAO service provider TokenLogic has proposed on Nov. 20 raising the USDC stake target for Umbrella, the protocol’s bad-debt backstop on Ethereum, from 38.84 million to 45.8 million USDC.

The proposal would also cut the extra reward APY at that target from 3.14% to 2.5%.

Industry observers note that if holders withdrew from the Nov. 17 cooldown queue at the historical rate with no other changes to stake, about 46 million USDC would remain staked, roughly 240,000 USDC above the proposed target. Under the same test, USDT would fall short.

Recent findings suggest that the proposal would extend Umbrella rewards for USDC, USDT and WETH past their Dec. 2 expiry through April 2, 2027. TokenLogic plans to gather forum feedback before advancing the renewal to a formal Aave Improvement Proposal.

Aave’s ten latest governance proposals showed no corresponding vote or execution as of Nov. 23.

Industry observers note that umbrella stakers put their interest-bearing Aave deposits at risk to cover bad debt. To leave, they first start a 20-day cooldown, then have two days to withdraw.

Industry observers note that across cooldown windows closed through Oct. 26, TokenLogic found that holders withdrew 44% of the USDC and 55% of the USDT they had queued.

On Nov. 17, Holders had staked 53.75 million USDC, with 32.6% in cooldown. At that balance, withdrawals of about 45% from the queue would bring USDC stake down to the proposed target, only slightly above the 44% historically withdrawn.

Sources indicate that the USDT vault held nearly 59 million staked, with 55.4% queued. Applying its historical exit share leaves about 41 million USDT, or 91% of its proposed 45.2 million target.

It has been revealed that at historical withdrawal rates, the Nov. 17 model leaves USDC 240,000 above target and USDT nearly 4.2 million below.

On Nov. 23, CryptoKeyhole valued the USDC and USDT stakes at about $51.3 million and $46.8 million, respectively, using readings from the Ethereum contracts. Its dashboard reported stake totals without cooldown balances, so withdrawal estimates are tied to Nov. 17.

It has been revealed that active USDC loans on Aave V3 Ethereum Core rose about 36%, from $1.9 billion on June 3 to $2.6 billion on Nov. 17, according to TokenLogic. USDe and sUSDe together rose from 4% to 12% of the collateral backing those loans.

Meanwhile, the share of collateral in the group of highly liquid assets TokenLogic tracks fell from 88.5% to 77.6%.

According to recent reports, against that November loan book, the existing target amounts to 1.5% of USDC borrowing. The proposal would lift that share to 1.77%, below the roughly 2% used in June. TokenLogic also proposed cutting USDT’s extra APY at target from 3.17% to 2.5%.

In a recent development, the 2.50% applies to the additional Umbrella reward at the target stake level. At the higher stake recorded on Nov. 17, the proposed rules would pay about 1.76% in annualized extra rewards.

According to recent reports, tokenLogic put the total annualized yield, including deposit interest, at 7% on average over the seven days through Nov. 17.

The Aave DAO takes the first loss up to 1.3 million USDC. Beyond that offset, Umbrella can slash the USDC stake designated for the same Ethereum Core reserve and send the slashed assets to the Aave Collector to cover the deficit.

The Aave DAO’s first-loss offset sits ahead of the matching Umbrella vault on Ethereum Core.
