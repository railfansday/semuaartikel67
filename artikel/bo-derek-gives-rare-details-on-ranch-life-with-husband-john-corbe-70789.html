Bo Derek couldn't help but wonder what John Corbett would do for her 70th birthday.

Industry observers note that “He’s a great gift giver," Derek told People, recalling one such special gift from her husband. “I have a friend with a organization. He’s very successful, and he’s starting a newly introduced company. And John went and got me shares in his company. It’s so much fun. I am not a wheeler dealer or a trader by any means. It’s all just algebra to me.".

But more than any present, Derek appreciates the gift of Corbett's companionship.

In a recent development, “He keeps me laughing," she shared of the Sex and the City alum. "He’s very youthful, enthusiastic.".

So much so that the model doesn't need a massive party with dozens of friends.

“If you have 20 or 30 people, you’re just running around—‘Hi, what do you need? Can I get you a drink?’” she said. “I always felt a little empty after a major party.”.

That's why Corbett, 65, came up with an idea that would have Carrie Bradshaw quaking in her Manolo Blahnik boots.

“He stated, ‘I don’t care how many weeks it takes. Why don’t we invite a friend or two at a time and have meaningful dinners with them?’" Derek said. “That’s probably what I’ll do this year, because I love it.".

It has been revealed that limiting their social engagements isn't the only way the pair—who met in 2002 and married in 2020—maintain a quiet life on their coastal farm. As she explained, they also prefer to stay home to spend time with their two German shepherds.

“When we first met, he didn’t want to have a girlfriend with a dog because they just require too much time. You’re just off going off having fun, and you got to rush back to the dog,” she told the outlet. “And now I can’t get him to go anywhere with me because he doesn’t want to leave the dogs.”.

But fortunately, that take hasn't landed him in the doghouse, as Derek revealed she's also fond of her man's best friend, adding, “It’s not fun for him unless the dogs are with us and it’s great that we share that.”.
