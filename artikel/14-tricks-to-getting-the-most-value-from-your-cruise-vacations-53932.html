14 tricks to getting the most value from your cruise vacations

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (179 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Cruises offer significant built-in value, with meals, entertainment and transportation included, plus many additional perks if you know where to look.
  • Booking strategies such as monitoring upgrade offers, choosing less popular cabins or ships and leveraging loyalty programs can lead to substantial savings and extra benefits.
  • Free and low-cost activities abound, from onboard entertainment and specialty dining to private island experiences and included beverages.
  • Careful planning and attention to pre-cruise communications can unlock discounts on packages for drinks, dining, Wi-Fi and shore excursions.
  • Upgrade and package offers may arrive via email or be available in cruise line portals, so regular monitoring is advised.
  • Loyalty program perks and status matches can provide valuable extras, but eligibility and benefits vary by line and sometimes require proactive enrollment.
  • Some activities, specialty dining and VIP packages incur additional costs, and certain perks may require advance reservations or specific eligibility.
