Industry observers note that in a recent announcement, Binance Wallet stated it will discontinue support for the Blast chain effective November 30, 2026, at 10:00 (UTC). After this date, the crypto exchange noted that users will no longer be able to view, send, or receive Blast assets within the Binance Wallet.

Ethereum layer-2 network Blast announced in October 2026 that it was shutting down, a little over two years after its launch, as fading activity left it unable to cover operating costs.

Sources indicate that binance noted that this change only affects the display and management of Blast assets within Binance Wallet, as people can still claim their assets on the official Blast site. In light of this, users are strongly advised to manage or transfer their Blast assets before November 30, 2026, at 10:00 (UTC).

This upgrade only affects the Blast network and does not impact other blockchains supported by Binance Wallet.

Binance has resumed its cryptocurrency listing activity after a brief pause, introducing upcoming perpetual contracts for Hyperliquid (HYPE) and TRON (TRX).

The late-October hack had interrupted the exchange's listings; its last addition was on October 29, when it revealed it was listing the Predict token.

In the last 24 hours, Binance has made two listing announcements: one for HYPE and TRX perpetual contracts, and one for TradFi perpetual contracts on Nu Holdings Ltd. Class A Ordinary Shares and Freedom Holding Corp. Common Stock.

Sources indicate that binance Futures will launch HYPEUSDC and TRXUSDC Perpetual Contracts with up to 75x leverage on November 16, 2026, at 08:30 (UTC) and 08:35 (UTC), respectively. The move is intended to expand the list of trading choices offered on Binance Futures and enhance people’ trading experience.

To expand the list of trading choices offered on Binance Futures and enhance consumers’ trading experience, the NUUSDT USDT-Priced Perpetual Contract and the FRHCUSDT USDT-Priced Perpetual Contract have been admitted to trading on Binance RIE and to clearing and settlement by Binance RCH today, November 12, 2026.
