Industry observers note that gigabyte's Gaming A16 laptop with the RTX 5070 and 16GB DDR5 RAM is one of the cheapest that comes equipped with that particular GPU. At 1200p, even the 85W RTX 5070 gets plenty of room to stretch its legs, and 1TB of storage is pretty sweet - it lets you go nuts during quite a few Steam sales.

Key specs: RTX 5070 (85 W) | Ryzen 7 260 | 16-inch | 1200p | 165 Hz | 16 GB DDR5 | 1 TB SSD.

Price check: Walmart $1,549.99 (Core Ultra 9 185H) | Amazon $1,448 (Core i7-13620H).

How are we all doing, fellow Black Friday shoppers? Feeling the evil clutches of the memory crisis closing in? Crying over $2,000 GPUs? Having nightmares about laptop prices?

Well, I can't help you with the clutches and whatnot, but I can certainly help you with a great Black Friday gaming laptop deal today. I just spotted this Gigabyte Gaming A16 laptop at Best Buy, and when I saw those specs with that price tag, I felt a (brief, fleeting, but much-needed) sense of relief. Now there is a decent laptop at a reasonable price. So they do exist.

Gigabyte's Gaming A16 has a bunch of things worth mentioning, but the best part is certainly the Nvidia RTX 5070 GPU. That's a midrange GPU at what is now an almost budget-level price, so it's genuinely a good deal. The caveat here is that it's the 85 W version, so it's not the most powerful iteration of the RTX 5070 you'll ever get to see, but at this price? I'll take it.

Apart from the RTX 5070, which, with the help of DLSS 5, should be able to carry most games at reasonable settings, you're also getting the AMD Ryzen 7 260 laptop CPU, a 1 TB SSD, and 16 GB DDR5 RAM. Display-wise, we're looking at a 1920 x 1200 resolution with a 165 Hz refresh rate. All in all, very respectable.

Recent findings suggest that shopping now, during Black Friday sales, gives you $300 off the regular list price. That's a whole lot of laptop for just $1,300, so I'd jump on this deal while it's still up.

Recent findings suggest that but wait... 16 GB DDR5 RAM sounds a little bit restrictive, right? It's what we're used to in this hellscape, what with the memory shortages, but it's not ideal. Well, good news, Best Buy also has a 32 GB DDR5 version of that same-ish laptop. It's not the exact same, but it's pretty close, and the best part is that it's still affordable.

Recent findings suggest that gigabyte's Gaming A16 laptop is the cheapest way to bag yourself an RTX 5070 mobile GPU, even if it is a restricted version at 85 W. But with this slight extra expense you also get a full 32 GB of DDR5 memory. And that is worth way more than the ~$80 extra this costs over the 16 GB machine.

Key specs: RTX 5070 (85 W) | Core i7-13620H | 16-inch | 1200p | 165 Hz | 32 GB DDR5 | 1 TB SSD.

Price check: Walmart $1,618 | Amazon $1,618.

In a recent development, this model brings you over to Intel with the Core i7-13620H mobile chip, but it keeps the RTX 5070 (85W) and the 1200p 165Hz screen. It fixes my main issue with the cheaper model without ramping up the price too much. And for a premium that is considerably lower than it would be if you were to try and upgrade the $1,300 version yourself.

The 32 GB version of the Gigabyte Gaming A16 is now $1,379, down from $1,849.99, at Best Buy. Scoop it up while it's still there and enjoy having a solid GPU at what's now a reasonable price.
