In a recent development, following a 1993 fire in Malibu, the water district, which is managed by the county, installed emergency backup generators capable of pushing water uphill in the case of a power outage. As a result, the plaintiffs say, the lines melted during the 2025 blaze, rendering the recent improvements all but worthless.
