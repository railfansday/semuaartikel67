Princess Cruises has announced that it welcomed guests to Celebration Key with the inaugural call of the Star Princess.

The event features a festive Bahamian welcome and special ceremonies marking the arrival at the south side of Grand Bahama Island.

The inaugural visit marks the beginning of 69 scheduled calls during the 2026-27 season, followed by 32 planned calls in 2028, across six ships.

“For nearly 60 years, Princess has welcomed guests to the Bahamas, an essential part of our global portfolio,” noted Gus Antorcha, Princess Cruises’ president.

Recent findings suggest that “This milestone also reflects the strength of our partnership with The Bahamas and our shared commitment to supporting tourism in Grand Bahama. With 69 calls planned this season, we look forward to welcoming thousands of guests to this extraordinary destination and creating meaningful experiences that become part of their own Princess stories long after their vacations end.”.

The manufacturer stated in a press release that the inaugural celebration brought together Honorable Ginger Moxey, minister for Grand Bahama and member of Parliament for Pineridge, and other government, tourism and port officials for a ceremony featuring the Star Princess’ Captain Paolo Arrigo and President Gus Antorcha.

A traditional Bahamian Junkanoo band performed a rendition of “The Love Boat” theme song, while beach rake art and a custom Princess Cruises sand sculpture beneath Celebration Key’s Sun Portal provided tributes to the occasion.

The press release noted that the destination is designed to offer something for every type of traveler.

It added that Celebration Key invites Princess guests to experience white-sand beaches, freshwater lagoons, family friendly activities, private cabanas, shopping and Bahamian flavors.
