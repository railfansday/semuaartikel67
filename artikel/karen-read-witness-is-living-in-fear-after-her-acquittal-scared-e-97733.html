Witnesses in Karen Read’s case looked back on the death of her boyfriend John O’Keefe more than one year after she was acquitted of his murder, with one person saying he’s “scared every single day” due to the conspiracy theories associated with the high-profile case.

Several witnesses of the case have now spoken out in Netflix’s three-part docuseries The Trials of Karen Read, which premiered on Monday, November 23.

“I am scared every single day about somebody coming up to me, whether they try to attack me, whether they try to do something horrible. I’m scared every day of my life for that,” witness Colin Albert remarked of the theories regarding what happened to O’Keefe.

According to recent reports, boston police officer O’Keefe, 46, died in January 2022, and Read, 46, was arrested for his murder after she allegedly hit him with her car. Prosecutors argued that Read backed her SUV into O’Keefe and left him in the snow after a night of drinking. Read’s defense claimed he was killed inside the house and that she was framed.

According to recent reports, read’s first trial ended in a mistrial in 2024, and she was eventually acquitted of second-degree murder, manslaughter while operating under the influence and leaving the scene of a collision resulting in death charged during her retrial in June 2025. However, she was found guilty of operating a motor vehicle under the influence of liquor.

After she was acquitted, theories circulated that his friends and loved ones were involved in covering up his death.

In a recent development, both Colin and his uncle, Boston police officer Brian Albert, were central figures in the prosecutors’ cases against Read. Brian, who was O’Keefe’s friend, mentioned it wasn’t possible that the late officer was attacked in the home.

“That night, there’s a lot of people in my house,” Brian recalled. “If John had come in either door, he would’ve been seen by everybody in the middle of the kitchen. John O’Keefe never came into my house.”.

O’Keefe’s family and other friends also reflected on the killing in the docuseries, saying that the public interest in Read has overshadowed what happened to the victim.

“The victim and his family, they lost,” Jen McCabe, O’Keefe’s friend who served as a key witness for the prosecution, remarked in the docuseries. “And people should remember John, and the amazing guy he was, and not Karen Read.”.

Sources indicate that while several witnesses in the case appeared in the docuseries, Read opted not to participate. However, she has always maintained her innocence.
