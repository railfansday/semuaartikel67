Napoli’s injury problems got even worse on Saturday night, as Antonio Vergara left the pitch in tears early in the game against Torino and is expected to miss Manchester City.

After a sprint, Vergara felt something in the back of his left thigh. He immediately clutched the area, raising fears of a muscle injury. He left the pitch in tears and in a lot of pain in just the 13th minute. Kevin De Bruyne came on in his place.

In a recent development, it was a double blow for Massimiliano Allegri. He’d left De Bruyne on the bench to rest him after two Nations League starts for Belgium, with Tuesday’s Champions League trip to Manchester City in mind, but the Belgian ended up playing almost the whole game.

It has been revealed that vergara had been given a new role in Allegri’s 3-5-2, playing in support of Rasmus Hojlund. Before kick-off, Allegri had praised his ability to find space between the lines and get into the box.

Napoli will have to wait for tests to find out how serious the problem is, but Vergara looks very doubtful for the trip to Manchester.

He joins a long list of absentees. Alex Meret is out for around two months with a back fracture, while Benoit Badiashile, Alisson Santos, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo and Alessandro Buongiorno are all sidelined.

Allegri’s side came into the game without a win since 10 October, when they beat Frosinone 3-0.
