According to recent reports, greeicy has forged a music and acting career since the age of 15. In 2018, she was a Billboard Latin on the Rise artist and the following year she nabbed a best fresh artist nomination at the Latin Grammys. Sources indicate that “I love it, because for me, it’s impossible not to feel that this is my best year—to feel that wonderful things have happened to me—but I also love the thought that what is happening to me today is the result of the work put in over the years. This didn’t happen overnight,” the Colombian artist said in the latest episode of Billboard’s “Cafecito Con” video series. In a recent development, this year, Greeicy achieved her first-ever No. 1 on a Billboard chart when “Yapaque,” her collaboration with Farruko and Steve Aoki, reached the top of the Latin Airplay in July. In the summer, she formed part of Karol G’s Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour on select dates, where they performed their collaboration “Amiga Mía” live for the first time. And earlier this month, her album Candela was nominated for best contemporary tropical album at the Latin Grammys.

“I think I’ve come to an understanding this year—it’s not that I’m not a dreamer, but because I grew up in an environment that was economically limited in many ways, I ended up limiting my own dreams, too. I used to say, ‘How wonderful it would be to be a singer,’ but I saw it more as a game than as a real possibility,” she shared. “This year, looking at the reality I’m living—a life I never even imagined for myself—I realized that being where I am now shows me it’s possible to dream even bigger.”.
