After years of anticipation, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here, and millions of people are experiencing their first hours in Vice City the same way: through someone else's livestream.

Across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick, creators are racing through the game's opening missions (which apparently include a little home renovation), exploring its sprawling open world, and inviting their audiences along for the ride. For some viewers, it's a chance to see Rockstar Games' long-awaited sequel before they can play it themselves. For others, watching their favorite streamer discover the game in real-time is part of the experience.

Sources indicate that and the numbers are staggering. According to preliminary data from streaming analytics platform Streams Charts, GTA VI reached 6.38 million concurrent viewers across livestreaming platforms as of 12:30 UTC on Nov. 18. That's roughly 3.7 times the previous game-launch record held by Valorant, whose 2020 closed beta attracted 1.74 million concurrent viewers.

YouTube accounted for 2.66 million viewers, followed by Twitch with 2.50 million and Kick with 1.31 million. On Twitch alone, GTA VI has already surpassed every previous game launch tracked by Streams Charts, becoming the platform's sixth-biggest game category in history.

Of course, with thousands of channels broadcasting the game, figuring out whom to watch can be almost as overwhelming as deciding what to do first in Vice City.

Whether you're looking for chaotic reactions, familiar gaming personalities, or someone who knows the Grand Theft Auto franchise inside and out, here are nine creators worth tuning into.

Few creators can turn a video game into an unpredictable spectacle quite like iShowSpeed. The YouTube superstar has built his enormous audience around his explosive reactions, spontaneous antics, and ability to make just about anything entertaining. Case in point: A clip of Speed getting arrested in GTA VI after attempting to make his getaway on a mobility scooter, which he hilariously refers to as a "slow-ass car," has already gone viral. It's exactly the kind of absurd, unscripted chaos viewers have come to expect from his streams.

Kai Cenat has made marathon livestreams into major internet events, and GTA VI is another opportunity to bring his massive community together. Known for his elaborate productions, celebrity collaborations, and larger-than-life personality, Cenat is approaching the launch with a particularly ambitious goal of becoming one of the first creators to finish the game.

It has been revealed that félix "xQc" Lengyel is no stranger to Grand Theft Auto. The Canadian streamer has spent countless hours in the world of GTA V roleplay, where his unpredictable behavior and interactions with other players have produced plenty of viral moments. Now he's bringing that familiarity with Rockstar's open-world chaos to the latest game.

Sources indicate that australian gaming creator Kathleen "Loserfruit" Belsten has built a massive following on Twitch and YouTube with her colorful personality, quick wit, and entertaining gameplay. Best known for her Fortnite content, she's now trading the battle royale for the streets of Vice City. With more than 3 million Twitch followers, Loserfruit is one of the platform's most recognizable women in gaming, making her first adventures in GTA VI worth tuning into.

Recent findings suggest that australian streamer Dinah isn't just playing GTA VI; she's attempting to conquer it. The creator has committed to six straight days of streaming Rockstar's latest open-world adventure, with the ambitious goal of reaching 100 percent completion. It's a marathon undertaking for a game packed with missions, side quests, and plenty of distractions. For viewers who want to experience every corner of Vice City alongside a creator, Dinah's stream might be the place to start.

It has been revealed that spanish creator Rubén "Rubius" Doblas is one of the most recognizable gaming personalities in the Spanish-speaking world. With a career spanning more than a decade, he's built a loyal audience around his humor, gaming commentary, and energetic livestreams. His GTA VI broadcast is also a reminder that this launch is truly a global event.

Industry observers note that andre "Typical Gamer" Rebelo has spent years building an audience around open-world games, including GTA V. His channel is a natural destination for viewers who want to follow the latest game's missions, explore its mechanics, and discover what Rockstar has packed into Vice City.

Watch: Typical Gamer on YouTube.

For viewers interested in the finer details of Grand Theft Auto, Australian creator DarkViperAU is a particularly compelling choice. Known for his GTA V speedruns, challenge videos, and extensive knowledge of the franchise, he brings a different perspective to the launch. Expect a closer look at how the game actually works, not just the spectacle of playing it for the first time.

Brazilian streamer Alan "alanzoka" Ferreira is another major name drawing audiences to GTA VI. Known for his entertaining playthroughs and expressive reactions, he's among Brazil's most established gaming creators. His inclusion also reflects the enormous international audience following Rockstar's latest release.

TheBurntPeanut has developed a distinctive online identity through his animated peanut avatar and gaming streams. For viewers who enjoy personality-driven broadcasts and a healthy dose of absurdity, his GTA VI coverage offers something a little different from the usual face-cam experience.

Watch: TheBurntPeanut on Twitch.

According to recent reports, TGG has spent years creating Grand Theft Auto guides, tutorials, and videos that explore the franchise's many secrets. His familiarity with GTA V makes him a useful creator to follow for players interested in the sequel's details, from gameplay systems to the possibilities of its fresh open world.
