📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Alvin and the Chipmunks is at the centre of controversy, with fans furious about the franchise’s use of artificial intelligence across its YouTube content.

A new Alvin and the Chipmunks movie is slated to release in 2028. To promote the film, a photo of Alvin circulated on social media. Immediately, some fans suspected it may be AI-generated and were unhappy.

In a video posted by Munk Media, a YouTube account discussing news of the franchise, they shared that the account had begun a practice of using AI to generate its channel banners and thumbnails.

While some fans were frustrated with this practice, it was argued by others that thumbnails are merely a marketing tactic, and the use of AI to generate them is something you could “look past and move on from.”.

However, a new video of nursery rhyme Wheels on the Bus, featuring “Lil Alvin and the Chipmunks”, appears to be entirely AI-generated.

Munk Media’s video highlights the telling signs that the video is AI-generated, including inconsistent character and vehicle designs that change from shot to shot, as well as glitched motion and layering.

Despite fans being promised that AI would not be used in the franchise, this appears to be the first time an official release carrying the Alvin and the Chipmunks name has been generated using AI, leaving fans nervous for its future.
