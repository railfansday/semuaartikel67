Nintendo Promises To Fix The Long Load Times On Pikmin 4 - Switch 2 Edition

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nintendo has issued a statement acknowledging load time challenges on Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy on Switch 2.

Sources indicate that the company has promised to released a patch addressing the concerns, citing the long load times that primarily occur when travelling to surface areas. It's also identified an matter in which long loads occur if your network connection is unstable.

While Nintendo works to resolve the issues, it has advised to play in an environment with a stable network connection.

Recent findings suggest that "In Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy, we have identified an glitch where loading times are longer than expected when traveling to surface areas, including the Rescue Camp. We are currently preparing an revision to address this. We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.

According to recent reports, "Additionally, we have identified an problem where launching the game or accessing the "Dandori Challenge" (Dandori Test) may take an extended amount of time if the network connection is unstable. We are currently investigating the cause of this issue.

"If you encounter these problems, playing in an environment with a stable network connection may help resolve them.".

Pikmin 4 on Switch 2 garnered decent reviews upon release, though it's safe to say that it's not the most beloved revision Nintendo has provided for its latest console. Players were immediately put off by the load times, a lack of 60fps, and seemingly slight latest content based on existing levels.

Indeed, Nintendo's latest statement has been met with comments from consumers demanding that 60fps be added to the game, but the manufacturer has not acknowledged this, so it seems unlikely to be included at this time.
