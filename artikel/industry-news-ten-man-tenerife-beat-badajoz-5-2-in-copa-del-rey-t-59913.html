Industry News: Ten-man Tenerife beat Badajoz 5-2 in Copa del Rey tie postponed after De Miguel’s death

In a recent development, tenerife won 5-2 at Badajoz in the Copa del Rey despite finishing the match with ten men. The victory keeps their cup run alive ahead of the second-round draw.

In a recent development, at the Estadio Nuevo Vivero on Wednesday 18 November, Álvaro Cervera’s side booked a place in the second round of the Copa del Rey. The tie had been postponed twice, first after the train accident in Toledo province and then after the death of Tenerife forward Jesús de Miguel on 3 November, who was remembered with a minute’s silence.

Industry observers note that cervera started a line-up made up largely of reserves. It still produced a scoreline Tenerife had never recorded in 260 Copa del Rey matches, according to elDiario.es (Canarias Ahora). About 3,000 spectators watched, around 20 per cent of the stadium’s capacity.

Sources indicate that grant-Leon Ranos opened the scoring in the sixth minute from a Marc Mateu assist. Juanjo Sánchez added a second a minute later. Ranos struck again in the 38th minute, set up by Gerard Valentín.

Carlos Beitia pulled one back for Badajoz in the 49th minute. Roko Jureškin was then sent off in the 58th minute. Ten-man Tenerife still extended the lead through substitute Iván Chapela in the 69th minute, assisted by Martin Pečar.

It has been revealed that jorge Barba replied for the hosts in the 74th minute from a Manuel Hernández assist. Víctor Moreno completed the scoring deep into stoppage time, with Álvarez providing the pass.

The Segunda Federación side went into the tie third in their group. They were also unbeaten in seven league matches in a row.

Tenerife have now come through their opening Copa del Rey tie in eight seasons in a row. The win followed a 2-0 victory over AD Ceuta FC. Before that came a run of draws against FC Andorra, Sabadell, Celta de Vigo II and Girona.

Industry observers note that this was the first meeting between the clubs in the cup. They had played each other 22 times in Segunda División. Tenerife won ten of those games, Badajoz eight, and four ended level. Tenerife’s previous visit to Badajoz, on 30 March 2003, ended in a 2-1 defeat under David Amaral.

Sources indicate that cervera’s side return to league duty at home to Mallorca in Segunda División on Saturday 21 November. Badajoz travel to Recreativo Huelva in Segunda División RFEF Group 4 on Sunday 22 November.

The second round will feature 56 teams in 28 one-off ties. The lower-division side hosts if its ground meets federation standards. The draw was put back because of the postponed first-round matches. The Ciudad del Fútbol in Las Rozas, Madrid, home of the federation, is the venue for it.
