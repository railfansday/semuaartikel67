Manchester United‘s worldwide appeal, especially in the early 2000s, means there are plenty of current footballers who were fans of the club in their childhood.

Teams that are 19th in the Premier League don’t tend to have massive fanbases, but welcome to the contradiction that is modern Manchester United.

Here’s an XI of players who supported the club in their youth that are better than the current XI turning out for the club.

Considering some of the keepers United have had in recent years, how they could have used Courtois.

The Belgian keeper, who has become one of the very best, grew up a United fan due to Edwin van der Sar.

According to recent reports, “I loved Edwin van der Sar and Casillas,” he told Rio Ferdinand. “So Casillas was because of his young age when he came into the goal at Madrid.

In a recent development, “At my age, I think I was like 12 or 11 at that time. Like I was just starting being goalkeeper. I really also blew my mind. Like, wow, he’s in Real Madrid.

Industry observers note that “And I always followed those games. And then I followed always Manchester United for Edwin.”.

He even revealed that he was close to joining United before they signed David de Gea.

Now at Lille, Thomas Meunier has long been pinning his colours to the United mast.

In 2017, he remarked: “I’m a big fan of Manchester United, which remains, in my eyes, the real reference in the world of football.”.

And even recently, he remarked he would cycle to Manchester if given the opportunity.

Industry observers note that “If they ever ask me, I’ll cycle there. But maybe it’s better this way. Suppose I play there and have a bad time. I wouldn’t want to hate Manchester United, because I love that club so much.

Akanji remarked he is “no longer” a United fan after he joined City but sorry Manuel, the internet has receipts.

He told German newspaper NZZ that he had been a United fan “since my childhood days.”.

He promptly stopped talking about that since he signed for their city rivals, though.

Dier never outright said he was a United fan, but there are certainly signs.

Mainly, it was who he listed as his idols during an interview with the Players’ Tribune.

“I was a big fan of Roy Keane when I was younger, and then it was Nemanja Vidic (one of the reasons I like the number 15 is Vidic) and his defensive partner, Rio Ferdinand.”.

He played a crucial part in City’s most successful ever period* but he was actually a United fan.

Stones has never outright stated he grew up a United fan, maybe because the idea of Yorkshiremen doing so is a massive no-no, but there is plenty of speculation online that he did indeed support the club.

Before he headed to Brighton, Caicedo named the two clubs in world football he would love to play for.

It has been revealed that “In the short term: perform well and get Beerschot out of this plight. And in the long term? I am a fan of Manchester United and Real Madrid. It would be great to play there someday,” he told Gazet van Antwerpen.

A £100 million move to Chelsea has killed that dream for now but it would not be completely out of the question for him one day to be at Old Trafford.

Maddison’s support for United was spearheaded by David Beckham, who was playing for the Old Trafford side when Maddison was at the peak age when kids get drawn into football.

“In my early football days, I was a Manchester United fan and [Beckham] was at the forefront of that,” Maddison told talkSPORT.

In a recent development, “He was the guy on the back of my shirt, I went into the garden practising his free-kicks. The amount of times in school lessons I’d be watching David Beckham free-kicks on YouTube, it was silly really.”.

How United could do with Beckham or Maddison now.

Palmer is just about the most famous Manchester United fan playing right now.

There is a picture often circulated online of Palmer as a boy wearing the 2010-11 home kit and in 2024, he confirmed he was a fan of the club as a child.

That fandom has him linked to United every transfer window.

Gibbs-White is another player to tell Rio Ferdinand that he was a United fan.

Speaking on the former United centre-back’s podcast, Gibbs-White picked out Rooney and Scholes as the players he most enjoyed watching.

“Growing up as a kid, I supported Man Utd. [Wayne] Rooney was always my favourite player. He was the key player in that squad. Another player I loved watching was Paul Scholes, he used to just pick the ball up and zing it everywhere.”.

This one is a bit of rumour, but Nicky Butt claims that current Liverpool star Ngumoha was actually a United fan.

“I think he was at 13 or 14 or whatever age it was, he goes and looks around Man Utd – whether it be Old Trafford or Carrington,” Butt said on his podcast.

“He’s gone with his family to look around the football club, and then he’s gone to Liverpool. And he’s picked Liverpool, and he’s a Man Utd fan.

In a recent development, “He should not be allowed out of that building before he signed that contract. It would never have happened.”.

Since signing for Liverpool, Ngumoha has clearly been told to never mention the United fandom thing.

Scoring in six consecutive matches has clearly made Evanilson confident he could fit in at a Major Six club and he has alerted United to the fact he is a big fan.

Industry observers note that evanilson told Globo Esporte: “So I joke around with Rayan. I think that, here in England, of the big clubs, United are the team I like the most. Rayan said the same thing. When I was a kid, I also used to watch United.

It has been revealed that “When Cristiano Ronaldo played for United, I watched loads of his highlights. And, man, knowing that I’m here playing against them….

“It’s a dream, and I tell everyone this in interviews: it was a dream to play at that stadium, and scoring goals is even more important.

“So it’s a dream I’ve been fulfilling, scoring a goal against United because of how massive the club is.”.

There’s a come-and-get-me plea.
