Three Years After His Death, EA Is Using AI To Bring Back A Famed Commentator In FC 27

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

EA Sports FC 27 is adding an AI-generated commentator for the first time in the series' history, with famed British commentator John Motson having his voice synthesized for the football title. Motson, who passed way in 2023, previously provided commentary for EA's FIFA series, appearing in the game's first iteration in 1996 and featuring until 2005. According to the BBC, EA approached Motson's wife and son for permission to regenerate his voice and use his likeness for this year's entry in the series. Both agreed to the idea, but neither party has disclosed how much EA has paid for the use. Fred Motson, John's son, stated that he believed his father would've approved of this use of AI. "My dad was the least technological person in the world," Fred stated. "Till his dying day, he had a typewriter on his desk. So I don't think he would have quite understood what the AI was doing technologically, but I think, on the other hand, he was always really keen to get involved in these kinds of projects." Peter Caulfield, founder of Save Our Voices Now, which campaigns against this exact use of AI, questions whether a family should be able to provide consent for someone who can no longer give it themselves. "If someone has signed a deal allowing their likeness to be used after they die, I have no concern with that," he explained. "But in life or in death, unless you have specifically agreed to it, AI cloning of any kind should not be allowed. Being a family member shouldn't automatically give you that right either." This isn't new to video games, either. Epic Games used a similar method to bring back the late James Earl Jones' voice for its Star Wars event in Fortnite, with the actor's estate agreeing to the use. It allowed players to converse with the AI bot while playing, and was very promptly removed after some people managed to get the bot to spew out racist or vulgar phrases, all delivered with the voice of Jones. It also drew the ire of SAG-AFTRA, which claimed that Epic had violated labor practices. Derek Rae, one of the at the moment employed commentators on EA Sports FC 27, mentioned he was excited by the inclusion of Motson's voice, and that he didn't believe AI was yet going to replace his own work on the game. When asked whether he would make the same decision to have his work endure for years to come through AI, he replied, "I'm hopefully many years away from having to make a decision like that.".
