Liverpool youngster Trey Nyoni has picked out Arsenal captain Martin Odegaard as the ‘toughest opponent’ he has faced at the start of his career.

Odegaard has returned to his sparkling best this season, starting all ten of Arsenal’s opening matches in the Premier League with the reigning champions sitting five points clear at the summit.

Having managed just one goal across the Gunners’ title-winning campaign last term, the 27-year-old appears to have rediscovered his scoring boots, finding the net on five occasions in all competitions this term.

It was Odegaard’s 72nd-minute strike that proved the difference before the international break, when Mikel Arteta’s men were made to work for their victory at home to newly promoted Hull City.

The Norway international will no doubt be desperate to pick up where he left off when the north London giants return to top-flight against at Newcastle on Saturday evening.

Nyoni has himself enjoyed an impressive start to the season at Liverpool and locked horns with Odegaard when the pair did battle at Anfield at the start of the month.

And while Nyoni was taken off before the hour-mark on Merseyside, it’s clear Odegaard left a big impression on the 19-year-old in the 58 minutes his opposite number was on the pitch.

Asked to name the ‘toughest’ player he has come up against, Nyoni told The Inside Scoop, ‘For me personally, I would say Odegaard,’ as he looked back on that afternoon.

‘The way he plays, it’s very busy, moving around and always looking for an option.

‘I think what he brings to the team benefits the team. Just because he’s not scoring or assisting, doesn’t mean he’s not effective.

‘Nowadays, the idea that if a player isn’t getting goals or assists, they’re useless, but every player has their own role in the team.

‘Odegaard and people like Flo, if they’re not getting goals and assists, people that don’t watch the game automatically think they haven’t played well but that’s not how football works.’.

In the aftermath of Arsenal’s nervy win over Hull, club legend Paul Merson mentioned Odegaard had been ‘like a new signing’ for Arteta this season.

‘He’s been outstanding. He’s the only one really that moves it rapidly,’ Merson said of Odegaard on Sky Sports.

‘He moves it quickly, he moves, he gets into positions. I just thought too many players took too many touches and they slowed it down and gave Hull half a chance.

‘I think he’s been outstanding this season, he’s like a new signing for them and he’s been the man so far.
