Two headers from Cucho Hernández gave Real Betis victory over Slovan Bratislava in the Champions League. The result strengthens Manuel Pellegrini's side's position in the league phase with three rounds left.

In a recent development, for Hernández, Tuesday night was his first start after more than a month out injured, and he marked it with a double in Bratislava. The win came in the fifth round of the Champions League league stage.

According to recent reports, ángel Ortiz supplied the cross for the opener, delivering it from the right for Hernández to meet at the back post. Isco then supplied the assist for his second after the break. That goal was his third of Betis’s Champions League campaign.

Sources indicate that hernández went off after 63 minutes, and Troy Parrott took his place. Marc Roca replaced Facundo Bernal, who had been booked in the seventh minute. Álvaro Fidalgo came on for Isco.

Industry observers note that pellegrini made six changes from the side that drew 1-1 with Sevilla in the derby. Isco, Abde and Antony kept their places.

Recent findings suggest that the visitors dominated the game in every department. Betis completed 605 of their 670 passes, an accuracy of 90%. Slovan managed 309 passes in total. Betis put six shots on target, while Slovan managed two. The expected goals figure was 1.52 for Betis and 0.43 for the hosts.

In a recent development, slovan goalkeeper Dominik Takáč made four saves. At the other end, Álvaro Vallés made a sharp save to stop Andraž Šporar, a moment UEFA’s match summary named as one of the key moments of the game. French referee Jérôme Brisard showed one yellow card to each side.

The game also brought Slovan coach Yaya Touré up against Pellegrini, his old manager at Manchester City. Touré’s side stay bottom of the table after five defeats. They have conceded 19 goals and scored only one.

Industry observers note that betis now have four wins and one defeat from five matches. They are well placed to fight for a top-eight finish over the last three rounds, according to AFP. A place in the top eight would send them straight into the last 16 without a play-off.

Recent findings suggest that the remaining fixtures are demanding. Betis host Como on 9 December 2026 and Arsenal on 20 January 2027. They finish the league phase away to Bayern Munich on 27 January 2027.
