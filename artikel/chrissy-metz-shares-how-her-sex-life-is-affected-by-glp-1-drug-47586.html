Chrissy Metz doesn't have to hunt for a spark in the bedroom.

As the Hunting Wives star shared, taking a GLP-1 to help her lose weight fortunately hasn't caused the potential side effect of a lowered sex drive.

“Some people are not really excited about anything. They are sort of just homeostasis baseline," Metz, who is in a private relationship of two years, told Page Six in an interview published Nov. 19. "Thank goodness I haven’t had that.".

Still, the This Is Us alum, who finds it crucial "to reiterate that not every medication is for everybody," knows her experience using Ro can be different from others taking weight loss drugs and experiencing a lower libido.

"I don’t know if studies have shown it’s because of weight loss or who knows," Metz continued. "I’m not sure where the root is, but some people have found that it has increased their sex life or increased their libido.".

As such, the 46-year-old is grateful for how the drug helped her successfully lose 100 pounds, a change Metz felt as she performed eight indicates a week in Broadway musical & Juliet while also managing perimenopause.

"I could have started it, and I wasn’t successful, or I wasn’t seeing any improvements, but that’s not been my case and my story," she acknowledged. "It doesn’t matter what I do. There’s always going to be naysayers and haters, and that’s fine. People are entitled to their opinion, but it’s my life.".

In fact, Metz has a clear response to outsiders who interject their point of view in decisions made between her and her doctor.

In her words, "They’re not in my body every day. They’re not in the doctor’s office with me reading my blood results. They’re not here when I used to binge eat because the food noise was so loud.".

And as the "Actress" singer learned to pay no mind to the unnecessary scrutiny, she's appreciated the support of her unnamed boyfriend, whom she praised as "a wonderful partner as well who is loving and supportive and all those things that you actually love.".
