TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Jumpstart Booster Box is on sale for $104.90 at Amazon, down from its $119.99 list price.

With Black Friday steadily approaching, Amazon has already beaten its previous record-low price for one of Magic: The Gathering's most accessible trading card releases — with the Super Heroes Jumpstart Booster Box finally falling below $105.

Recent findings suggest that as of Nov. 18, the 24-pack Jumpstart display is going for just $104.90 at Amazon, shaving $15.09 off its usual $119.99 price. That's only $13.25 above its TCGplayer market price. It also beats the latest Amazon record of $117.21 by another $12.31.

In a recent development, this new bargain is the lowest price ever seen on Amazon. Plus, no third-party seller involved — with Amazon shipping and selling the box itself, and free non-Prime delivery shown for Nov. 23 at the time of writing. Just be aware that it's a final sale with no returns.

It has been revealed that each of the 24 packs contains 20 Marvel-themed cards, including Lands, and choosing any two gives you a playable deck. There are 51 different themes to mix among heroes and villains.
