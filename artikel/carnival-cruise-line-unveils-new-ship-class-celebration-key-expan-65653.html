Carnival Cruise Line Unveils New Ship Class, Celebration Key Expansion

🌐 Source: Cruiseindustrynews • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (602 words, 📚 Deep Dive)

  • It has been revealed that the company said the latest ships were intentionally designed on a smaller scale.
  • “With a 29-ship fleet, we know our guests appreciate choices,” remarked Christine Duffy, president of Carnival Cruise Line.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, carnival Cruise Line plans to introduce a latest class of ships to its fleet. Two LNG-powered ships of approximately 130,000 gross tons each will be built by Fincantieri for delivery in 2033 and 2035, according to a press release.

It has been revealed that the company said the latest ships were intentionally designed on a smaller scale. That will give them access to more ports and widen the brand’s destination and itinerary options.

“With a 29-ship fleet, we know our guests appreciate choices,” remarked Christine Duffy, president of Carnival Cruise Line.

“They love the incredible experiences and amenities on our Excel Class ships, the largest in our fleet, but they also love the intimacy our smaller ships offer. This newly introduced class gives us the best of both worlds, helping future-proof our fleet with the flexibility to take America’s Cruise Line to even more places in the years ahead.”.

According to recent reports, at the Newly introduced York event on Thursday, Duffy also gave a first look at the upcoming Carnival Destiny. She outlined expansions at Celebration Key and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, and unveiled aims to extend popular onboard benefits ashore.

It has been revealed that the Carnival Destiny will debut in 2029 as the first of three Ace Class ships. It will have more outdoor pools than any other Carnival ship: eight in total, including six infinity pools, with more than 11,200 square feet of water surface. They include the brand’s first outward-facing main pool zone.

Sources indicate that other features include an ocean-front lanai with several pools and dining and bar venues near the sea. There will also be new premium venues with multideck ocean views, including an exclusive pool on Deck 18 Aft. The ship will offer 25 new dining and bar experiences and a reimagined Limelight Lounge.

In a recent development, compared with Carnival’s newest ships, the Carnival Destiny will have nearly twice the space for family venues and attractions. They will be grouped in a single family zone with mini golf, arcades and dining. A upcoming Dr. Seuss Splash Station will build on Carnival’s 13-year partnership with Dr. Seuss Enterprises.

According to recent reports, for adults, the ship will introduce Carnival’s first adult-only zone. It will span four decks and include the brand’s first adult-only staterooms, an expanded Serenity retreat and an exclusive rooftop pool deck.

Carnival is also planning two expansion phases at Celebration Key, each adding 3,000 guests of land-side capacity, for 6,000 in total.

Sources indicate that the first phase is due in 2028. It will add a new portal with a heated beach-entry pool and swim-up bar, a social waterslide for four to five riders and an aqua play area for younger children.

It has been revealed that the second phase, in 2030, will bring a major water park with 18 waterslides. It will also have what the company called the cruise industry’s longest lazy river, plus an exclusive area with an infinity pool, more beach access and fresh dining and bar concepts.

In spring 2027, Carnival will open Serenity Beach at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay. It will be a complimentary retreat for guests 21 and older, with daybeds and cabanas available for an extra charge.

Industry observers note that starting in February 2027, Carnival’s CHEERS!, CHEERS! Zero Proof, Bottomless Bubbles and CHEERS! Social drink packages will also apply at Celebration Key and RelaxAway.

“Guests are seeking more choice, more flexibility and more ways to make the most of their time together,” Duffy said.
