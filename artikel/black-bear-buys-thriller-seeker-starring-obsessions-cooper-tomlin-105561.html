📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Black Bear has acquired “Seeker,” a thriller featuring “Obsession” actor Cooper Tomlinson, “Off Campus” breakout Mika Abdalla and “Happy Death Day” star Jessica Rothe.

Sources indicate that the manufacturer has North American and U.K. distribution rights to the movie, which was introduced to buyers at this year’s AFM. Directed by Adam Carter Rehmeier and written by Michael Learner, “Seeker” follows Grant (Tomlinson), whose fiancée Caroline (Abdalla) mysteriously disappeared five years earlier. Now a ride-share driver, his life is upended when he picks up a passenger who he is convinced is responsible for her absence. According to recent reports, “’Seeker’ is exactly the type of bold and propulsive storytelling we look for at Black Bear,” said Katie Anderson, Black Bear’s executive VP of acquisitions. “Michael’s script had us all on the edge of our seats, and with Adam’s singular voice and this cast’s incredible talent, we couldn’t be more excited to bring this film to theaters.”.

Recent findings suggest that alex Lebovici and Learner will produce for Hammerstone Studios, with Adam McKay and Todd Schulman for HyperObject Industries, and Noah Centineo and Enzo Marc of Arkhum Productions. Samuel Hall and Warren Goz will executive-produce for North Five Six, alongside Ibrahim Mohammed, Yasmyne Mohammed, Chase Vergari, Karina Manashil, David Goldman and Andria Spring.

“Seeker” will begin production in January in Washington. The film is fully financed by Hammerstone Studios and Script 2 Screen. North Five Six are representing international distribution rights, with CAA Media Finance having represented U.S. rights.

Black Bear was founded in 2011 as independent production studio and recently introduced a domestic distribution arm. The company’s latest theatrical release, the offbeat romantic dramedy “Wicker,” has been a box office winner with $40 million globally to date. Black Bear’s upcoming slate includes Guy Ritchie’s “Wife & Dog,” starring Benedict Cumberbatch and Rosamund Pike; and David Leitch’s “Jason Statham Stole My Bike” led by Statham and Hannah Waddingham. It also recently nabbed Bill Hader’s feature directing debut, “They Know,” a psychological horror film starring Hader and Linda Cardellini.
