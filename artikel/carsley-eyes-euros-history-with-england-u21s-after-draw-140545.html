England Under-21s won Euro 2025 with an extra-time win in Slovakia against Germany.

Lee Carsley has urged his England Under-21s to create a legacy as they aim for a third straight European title.

The Young Lions - who have won the past two European Championships in 2023 and 2025 under Carsley - have been drawn in Group A against France, Poland and co-hosts Serbia at Euro 2027.

Only Italy have won three successive European Under-21 tournaments - between 1992 and 1996 - but that was when just eight teams competed at the finals and not the current 16.

Head coach Carsley told BBC Sport: "The players I am privileged to coach and work with have a great opportunity to make history with three in a row in this format and create their own legacy within the under-21s.".

He was speaking after Thursday's draw in Belgrade, with Serbia hosting the tournament with Albania.

"This group of players are in a really unique position and it will be a while before another group will be in this position," remarked Carsley.

"Let's go for it. Why not? Go all out to try to win it. That's what we're going to do. We have the players who can do that.

Recent findings suggest that "They are highly motivated. We have seen that in the last two tournaments we have had, the players were so determined to win every game and it makes it a lot easier from a coaching point of view.".

England will open their campaign against France - who they beat 3-2 in Bristol last week - on 16 June in Leskovac before facing Serbia in Belgrade on 19 June.

The Young Lions stay in the Serbian capital for their final group game against Poland on Tuesday, 22 June.
