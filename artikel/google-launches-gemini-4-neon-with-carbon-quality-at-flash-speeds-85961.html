Neon has the same 1 million-token context window with four thinking levels, like Hydrogen: Low, Medium, High, and Extra High. Officially, the upcoming model sits between Hydrogen and Carbon in the Gemini 4 family.

Sources indicate that neon is built for complex coding, analytical reasoning, and production-grade enterprise workflows. While Carbon tackles your hardest, long-horizon projects and Hydrogen handles fast everyday execution, Neon balances depth and efficiency.

According to recent reports, neon is priced at $3/1M input tokens and $15/1M output tokens, or twice as expensive as Hydrogen. Argon and Carbon cost $2/1M input and $10/1M output until November 30. After the promotional pricing, it will be $4/1M input and $20/1M output, with Neon 25% cheaper as a result.
