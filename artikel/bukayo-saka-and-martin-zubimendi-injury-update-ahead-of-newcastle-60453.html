Bukayo Saka will be sidelined for another four weeks while Arsenal will also be without Martin Zubimendi for their Premier League game against Newcastle United on Saturday.

Saka has missed Arsenal’s last five games after suffering a thigh injury during Arsenal’s 2-2 draw with Bayern Munich in the Champions League on October 21.

When asked about the progress of Saka’s recovery, Arteta said on Friday: ‘We had another test. He’s evolving really well.

It has been revealed that ‘It looks like it’s going to be an eight week injury more or less, so I think it’s four at the moment. It’s a while for him to be back with the team.’.

Meanwhile, Zubimendi was forced off during Spain’s 3-2 defeat to England on Sunday and will miss Arsenal’s trip to St James’ Park this weekend.

‘He’s out,’ Arteta mentioned of Zubimendi.
