Breaking: As digital-only GTA 6 smashes records, GameStop CEO states move from physical copies "hurt consumers"

GTA 6 is finally here, but it's digital-only – a reality that GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen likely isn't too keen on, judging by his take regarding publishers' move away from physical games.

It's no secret that we're gradually moving toward a digital world, with Sony's decision to end physical disc production immediately springing to mind (and GTA 6 coming as a code in a box, of course) – and, it seems, Cohen isn't a major fan of this move, much like many collectors and gamers themselves aren't. He explains as much in a upcoming interview with Bloomberg, stating that folks should be in control of the media they pay for.

According to recent reports, "When you buy a game, you should be able to sell it, trade it or lend it to a friend," the chief executive officer argues. It's not only a matter of players being unhappy, either – Cohen indicates it's harming brands like GameStop, which rely on physical purchases, trade-ins, and the like. "Unquestionably," he admits, "the decision to move to all digital has hurt consumers, and it's also hurt GameStop." That does make sense, I suppose.

In a recent development, he describes how he's been in talks with "the obvious" publishers, detailing why they're not a fan of trade-ins: they lose money. "Of course they don't like [trade-ins] because removing a used copy eliminates an alternative to buying fresh," in Cohen's own words. "They're taking away competition." That checks out, too, I suppose, as I can see why used copies and such could cost companies cash they'd otherwise be receiving themselves.

Industry observers note that as for GTA 6 itself, Cohen doesn't explicitly comment on it – but when it comes to presales, it sounds like GameStop has done well enough. "It's a code in a box," he indicates, noting that, yes, Grand Theft Auto 6 is exclusively digital. But, "I mean, we're selling a lot of it." Still, I do wonder how different things would look if it were truly physical… although I suppose there wouldn't be much variation at this point.
