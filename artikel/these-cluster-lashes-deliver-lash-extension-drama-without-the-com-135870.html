In a recent development, there’s a moment, usually somewhere between the third coat of mascara and the fourth clump, when you realize you want more than a tube can give. Cluster lashes are the answer a lot of us didn’t know we needed. Small, feathery sections of lash that sit exactly where you want drama, with none of the commitment of full extensions or the fuss of a traditional strip, cluster lashes deliver custom volume with a lightweight, natural-looking finish that blends seamlessly with your own lashes.

Cluster lashes give you the dimension of a lash-studio set in about five minutes, and they’re forgiving enough for beginners (yay!). Want a wispy cat-eye flick at the outer corners? Done. A fuller, doll-like fringe across the lid? You got it. Because you control the placement, the result looks like your lashes on their best day, and nothing like falsies (which is perfectly cool if that’s your vibe).
