Recent findings suggest that photoVogue is proud to unveil the 100 photographers and video makers selected for our latest Global Open Call, Brave Newly introduced Visions: Creativity as Rebellion. Chosen from an extraordinary 95,758 submissions by 9,160 artists across 155 regions, this digital exhibition celebrates the power of visual storytelling to challenge the status quo and imagine alternative futures.

According to recent reports, in a world saturated with images, true creative rebellion lies in refusing to give us what we already know. Rebellion doesn't always announce itself loudly—while some selected works confront urgent social and political realities, others explore identity, memory, tenderness, and desire through intimate, poetic, or playful lenses. What unites these 100 artists is not a single aesthetic, but a fearless willingness to experiment and question what we have been taught to accept.

Each of these 100 selected artists brings a distinct, urgent perspective to contemporary visual culture—spanning photography, video, and multimedia. In order to see the work of all of the selected artists, please click here to enter the full digital exhibition and experience how they are reshaping the way we see the world.

Sources indicate that this shortlist is just the beginning. In the coming weeks, we will announce the final artists selected to exhibit at the upcoming PhotoVogue Festival, reveal the three PhotoVogue Grant recipients, and share the festival's official dates and location. Follow PhotoVogue on Instagram and check back in the gallery for updates—and thank you to every artist who trusted us with their vision.
