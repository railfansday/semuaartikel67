It has been revealed that on a walk through Vice City, you might pass tourists taking vacation photos, someone playing Frisbee with their dog, or a bouncer working the door at a club. A little farther down the street, you could see a carjacking, a police chase, or someone dancing on the roof of a car. And all of that can happen while you're just on your way to buy a T-shirt or pair of shoes.

Recent findings suggest that ahead of Grand Theft Auto VI’s Nov. 19 release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, early reviews, gameplay clips, and livestreamers are giving us a better sense of who we'll meet in Vice City and across Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. The game is packed full of NPCs, short for non-player characters, and stopping to talk to one can lead to anything from a quick hello to a whole new side quest.

Industry observers note that the detail that went into many of these characters is clearly impressive, or as one X user remarked, "nothing short of magic," which makes sense considering Rockstar put together an entire team in Los Angeles to give the people you pass on the street more personality. The studio created roughly 600,000 animations for its NPCs, Edge reported last month.

Players are already posting their strangest NPC encounters, and we're learning a lot about how they work. Now of course, a short video can be hard to authenticate, especially when AI can mimic gameplay footage, so note that we haven't independently verified every clip making the rounds. Nevertheless, here's what early reviews and gameplay clips suggest can happen when you run into some of these characters.

Industry observers note that in GTA 6, you can greet or taunt NPCs as you pass, and they will all react in different ways depending in part on their individual temperament, what they've seen, and how you've treated them, according to Edge. Some are especially hostile and can pick a fight, slap you, and even follow you onto the road. In a gameplay clip posted by the GTA 6 Updates account, another driver chases Jason after he cuts her off. One streamer Paisley found NPCs in will "hunt you down and try to murder you if you hit their car.".

It has been revealed that "Even the grandmas in Vice City are dangerous," according to one X user, who shared a clip of an elderly woman in a scooter robbing another NPC at gunpoint. When the player intervened, she turned her weapon on them. "GTA 6 NPCs are absolutely unhinged," the user added.

In a recent development, NPCs are often causing chaos in the background. In one clip, a man was filling up his gas tank when he lit himself on fire. Another saunters the beach with a pet alligator on a leash. One NPC "asked Jason for a cigarette, spat it into the air, caught it in his mouth, and then high-fived himself when Jason left him hanging".

There is a wide mix of people in the game, and their jobs vary by district. Rockstar built nearly 500 repeatable scenes across 12,500 locations. Even overheard dialogue can be worth sharing — in a clip from streamer N3ON's playthrough, he is speechless after hearing an NPC say "clip that." An NPC's job shapes the options you get when approaching them. For example, Rockstar's extended gameplay footage shows Jason choosing how to get a bouncer's attention. Shopkeepers can also jump into action when trouble walks through the door. In The Verge's review, gang members followed the reviewer into a clothing store. The shopkeeper called the police, forcing the reviewer to empty the register before making a getaway. And that's just one example — we've seen other clips of NPCs calling the cops on Jason throughout the game.

You can also come across police who are busy with someone else. During one demo, Jason passed two officers who were already making an arrest. Streamer BlueThunderReal described getting close enough to a police perimeter that officers told them to "clear out.".

Some of the people working across Leonida are also part of Jason and Lucia's story. In the Keys, Jason works for smuggler Brian Heder and knows one of his associates, Cal Hampton. On the music side, nightclub owner Boobie Ike runs a record label with Dre'Quan Priest, and the rap duo Real Dimez are signed to it. Then there's Raul Bautista, a bank robber who’s always looking for people to bring in on a job.

Rockstar worked out some of those connections far beyond the named cast. Senior design director Steve Messinger remarked that the team mapped how drugs move through gang compounds and reach buyers on the street, even though players might only see a small part of that operation.

Other people come into the story because you decide to stop. Rockstar told Destructoid it built more than 1,000 encounters whose timing can depend on how much attention you give them. Drive past and you might hear the first line of dialogue and get another chance to stop later. Stick around and you might finish the encounter then and there, or meet someone who has more to say the next time you cross paths.

It has been revealed that side missions can also grow out of relationships Jason already has. His old Army friend Bret Vogel, for instance, teaches him how to track deer and gives him more animals to hunt, as Mashable reported.

And sometimes the person you help has a pretty specific plan for what comes next. In Jason Schreier's Bloomberg review, Jason stops to help someone who was hit by a corporate delivery driver. The person decides to sue the company and offers Jason a cut if he'll serve as a witness.
