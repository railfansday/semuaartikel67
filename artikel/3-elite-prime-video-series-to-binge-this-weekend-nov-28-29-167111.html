In fact, the series has been renewed for a fourth installment, such is its streaming success. However, Season 3 has come to an end, leaving a hole in people's watchlists. In a recent development, this December, the hilarious Ghosts will return to CBS for two festive hour-long specials before the season officially resumes in late January next year. Ahead of the show's return, you can begin a binge-watch of every episode right now on Prime Video. The series follows Sam (Rose McIver) and Jay Arondekar (Utkarsh Ambudkar), who inherit Woodstone Manor and aim to turn it into a B&B. However, after Sam has a fall, she discovers the building is already inhabited by some quirky spirits.

There's little scary about this ghost story, with showrunners Joe Port and Joe Wiseman instead offering one of the coziest demonstrates on streaming. The series is an adaptation of the British show of the same name, starring Charlotte Ritchie and Kiell Smith-Bynoe, and has managed to carve its own identity out of its hugely successful older sibling.

Next week begins the final month of 2026, so it's time to catch up on all the best Prime Video reveals that might've passed you by. With that in mind, don't miss out on a binge of all eight episodes in the opening season of Neagley. Released following an explosive Reacher Season 4 finale, this spin-off stars Maria Sten as she begins a upcoming life as a Private Investigator in Chicago, "drawing from her experience with Jack Reacher and the 110 Special Investigators.".

Action-packed and full of mystery, this pitch-perfect actioner manages to build on the style and tone of the Reacher series, twisting in a new direction with Sten's worthy lead performance. Called "a show that delivers exactly what it promises," featuring "an incredibly intriguing lead and greatly elevated by a likable cast," by Collider's David Caballero in his review, the show was a significant hit with critics and audiences and was renewed for a second season in October.

Tom Holland's return as Peter Parker in Spider-Man: Brand Fresh Day might've stolen the superhero spotlight this year, but ahead of Avengers: Doomsday, don't miss out on this Prime Video superhero gem that was cruelly canceled after a successful first season. A spin-off of the animated Spider-Verse films, Spider-Noir stars Oscar winner Nicolas Cage as 1930s New York Private Investigator Ben Reilly, as the weight of being the city's only superhero begins to take its toll.

Lamorne Morris, Li Jun Li, Karen Rodriguez, Abraham Popoola, Jack Huston, and Brendan Gleeson also star in this stylish, action-packed series that boasts arguably Cage's best performance of the decade so far. The series earned plenty of praise from critics, being dubbed "a heck of a lot of fun" by Collider's Aidan Kelley.
