Arion Kurtaj, who pleaded guilty last week to hacking Grand Theft Auto VI developer Rockstar Games and other companies, including Uber and Revolut, was sentenced to seven years in prison but released on license, the BBC reports. After hacking Rockstar, Kurtaj leaked 90 videos of GTA VI in 2022, years before the game’s public release last week.

Prior to his sentencing, Kurtaj had already spent time in a hospital because of his autism and in prison.

According to recent reports, “You are obviously a very talented young man,” the judge in the case mentioned, according to Sky News. “Don’t waste those talents by spending the rest of your life in and out of prison.” Kurtaj cannot use platforms Telegram and Discord as part of a five-year Serious Crime Prevention Order, the BBC claims.

Sources indicate that rockstar debuted GTA VI last week to positive reviews. The studio said the game made $3.79 billion in its first day of sales.
