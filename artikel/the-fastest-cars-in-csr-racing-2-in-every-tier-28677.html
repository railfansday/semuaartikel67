Updated with the very best cars to race in 2026.

Updated on November 18th, 2026 - Version: 6.7.2.

Recent findings suggest that want to swiftly compare stats on all the cars in CSR Racing 2? Then this is the guide for you.

Below, we've listed all the base stats for every car and highlighted which wins in each category. R stands for Rep Bonus, and F is for Fusion Slots.

According to recent reports, obviously, every car can be completely changed through upgrades and tuning, but these starting numbers give you a good indication of that car's overall abilities. It provides a quick glimpse at the stats, which might just be enough to help you pick out which car to choose to purchase next.

We've also included some thoughts on which are the best cars in CSR Racing 2 - both rare imports and those you can buy with in-game cash.

Just click through below to find out the fastest cars in every tier.

Recent findings suggest that many great choices in this category. At first glance, it seems like the Golf GTI MK1 is a clear winner, especially as it also has a 6% RP bonus and eight fusion slots. However, it must be won in the Rare Imports lot, which means it's a lot harder to come by.

On top of that, the fastest car so far is the Ford Mustang Boss 302 with e.c level 50, which means, even though it's not as fast early on, it can make it up in the later stages.

The Veloster Turbo R Spec is also a decent choice, but can only be purchased with (150) Gold.

Recent findings suggest that the M235i Coupe is a fantastic car for Tier 2, especially since you can buy it for just $75,000. It's light and has high power, but you'll need to upgrade your tyres to bring the grip up.

Recent findings suggest that potentially, the Mercedes-Benz 300 SL Coupe can be the fastest in this tier and by quite a significant margin. Not early on, though. The same goes for the Ferrari 250 GTO and Aston Martin DB5.

If you want Tier 2's best vehicle, you'll need to earn the F-150 from the Rare Imports lot.

The Ferrari California 30 is obviously still one of the best Tier 3 cars in class - no wonder it's being kept back as a prize.

It has been revealed that so if you can't win that, the SRT Hellcat at 825 G is a good choice with its high power, low shift time, and strong grip. If you only have dollars to spend, get the RS 5 Coupe.

Sources indicate that at level 50, the Pontiac GTO "The Judge" is the fastest car in this category. The BMW M2 (G87) Coupe "M Performance" is right behind it in second place. As you would expect, any variant of the Civic Type R is going to be fast! This is one of my favourite categories, as there are several good choices.

Industry observers note that the GT-R (R35) Premium looks like a pretty good vehicle for this tier. Not only is it affordable, but it has an amazing grip (too much, perhaps?!) and a low shift time. You'll need to work on power and lowering the weight with a lighter body.

The Viper ACR is another good option: it has great power for the tier, a low shift time, and 23 Fusion Slots.

Right now, the Rocket Bunny is looking more attractive than ever, but the big Grand Cherokee Trackhawk is tempting too.

Industry observers note that at this point, the game has some amazing cars to use, and the latest releases push performance to the limits. These are by far the fastest cars in CSR Racing 2. The Bugatti Chiron is a fantastic choice, as is the FXX K, but launch cars such as the Bugatti Veyron and One:1 are still great.

If you don't want to rely on Rare Import lot wins, the Aventador LP 700-4 is a good bet with its high power, strong grip, and its relatively lightweight to boot.

Regardless, though, there's no doubt you'll have fun with vehicles like the 'Cuda "TorC" in CSR2.
