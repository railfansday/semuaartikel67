Above all we prize our mission-driven, flat, life-celebrating, no-corporate culture, and we look forward to meeting you and learning more about what you can bring to the team.

Sources indicate that the Cloud Engineer role is critical to scaling Chess.com's infrastructure to support millions of daily chess games and serve global people. This position combines site reliability engineering expertise with cloud infrastructure management, directly impacting system performance, reliability, and scalability across Chess.com's multi-regional distributed architecture. The role requires deep technical expertise in both infrastructure automation and application optimization to maintain Chess.com's position as a high-traffic gaming platform while enabling rapid feature development and deployment.
