Frosinone midfielder Giacomo Calò has fired a warning to AC Milan ahead of Sunday’s game, insisting they do not feel any pressure.

In many ways, Frosinone have been the surprise package of Serie A so far this season. Having been promoted from Serie B in 2025-26, they have racked up six wins in 11 games, scoring 24 goals in the process and allowing 15.

Recent findings suggest that they are 13 points above the relegation zone, which is very impressive given that the mission before a ball was kicked will likely have been just to get above the dotted line by any means. Now, they face a trip to San Siro where they can really test how far they have come.

In a recent development, milan may be four points ahead, but Frosinone will come to try and cause a bit of a stir. Giacomo Calò gave an interview to Il Messaggero (via MilanPress) in which he discussed the upcoming games for the Lazio-based side.

What are Frosinone’s aims for the season?

It has been revealed that “At first it was to stay up. Then we’ll see during the year, because we’ve only played eleven games and there are still a lot to go. We know it’s a tough league, that we could have some very difficult moments, with the schedule that Milan, Inter, Roma, and Lazio now have in store for us.

What do you like about the head coach Alvini?
