These stocks to buy right now all come from companies that lead their main businesses and grew both sales and operating profit in their latest quarterly reports. Buy a few through a low-cost brokerage account, as a small slice of your money beside an index fund, and hold them for years.

It has been revealed that this list is for investors who want to own a few companies for years beside an index fund. Each firm leads the business it’s known for and grew both its sales and its operating profit, the profit from the business itself, from a year earlier in its latest quarterly report. None faces a crisis that has already happened, such as a chief executive forced out or a court judgment massive enough to change its results.

Technology companies come first, then companies from the rest of the economy.

Amazon runs an online store and Amazon Web Services (AWS), which rents out computing power to other companies. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, net sales grew 12% from a year earlier, operating profit came to 13.9% of sales, its highest share ever, and AWS sales grew 39%. Its main risk is the Federal Trade Commission’s lawsuit claiming it illegally keeps a monopoly, set for trial on March 29, 2027.

Alphabet owns Google, which sells ads on its search engine and YouTube, and Google Cloud, which sells computing and AI services to businesses. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue grew 25% and operating profit 30% from a year earlier, and Google Cloud’s revenue more than doubled. Its main risk is court rulings that Google broke antitrust law in search and in ad technology, which Google is appealing or plans to appeal.

Apple makes the iPhone, Mac, and iPad and sells services such as the App Store and iCloud. In its fiscal fourth quarter, ended September 26, 2026, revenue and net income each grew 12% from a year earlier, both records for a September quarter, and services revenue set a record for any quarter. Its main risk is its reliance on the iPhone, which brought in about half its sales that quarter.

According to recent reports, microsoft sells Microsoft 365 office software, Windows, and cloud computing through Azure. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue grew 19% and operating profit 21% from a year earlier, and Azure revenue grew 48%. Its main risk is the cost of the data centers it is building for AI, the main reason it spent $45.2 billion on buildings and equipment in that quarter.

ASML, a Dutch firm, makes the machines chipmakers use to print circuit patterns onto silicon, and it is the only maker of the extreme ultraviolet (EUV) machines the most advanced chips need. Net sales reached a record 11.7 billion euros in the third quarter of 2026, up from 7.5 billion a year earlier, and operating profit more than doubled. Its main risk is export controls, which already keep its EUV machines out of China and could tighten further.

According to recent reports, costco runs warehouse clubs, substantial stores for shoppers who pay a membership fee. In the 16 weeks ended August 30, 2026, net sales grew 11.2% and net income reached $2.998 billion, against $2.610 billion a year earlier, helped by a one-time tariff refund. Its main risk is a thin margin, about 4 cents of operating profit on each dollar of revenue that quarter, so a dip in membership renewals would cut into its profit.

Recent findings suggest that JPMorgan Chase runs the largest U.S. bank by assets, serving households through Chase and large companies through its investment bank. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, net revenue grew 8% and net income 6% from a year earlier. Its main risk is a recession, when more borrowers fall behind and its loan losses rise.

Recent findings suggest that eli Lilly sells one medicine, tirzepatide, under two names, Mounjaro for type 2 diabetes and Zepbound for obesity. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue grew 39% from a year earlier and net income reached $6.5 billion, against $5.6 billion. Its main risk is its reliance on those two brands, about two-thirds of its revenue, while the prices it collects are falling, 15% lower on average than a year earlier.

In a recent development, visa runs the network that moves payments made with Visa cards between shoppers’ banks and stores’ banks in over 200 countries and territories. In its fiscal fourth quarter, ended September 30, 2026, net revenue grew 13% and net income 18% from a year earlier. Its main risk is a Justice Department lawsuit, filed in 2024 and not yet tried, that accuses it of monopolizing U.S. debit card payments.

Recent findings suggest that duke Energy sells electricity to 8.7 million customers in six states and natural gas to 1.6 million. In the quarter ended September 30, 2026, operating revenue grew 6.6% from a year earlier and net income reached $1.55 billion, against $1.41 billion, as its rates recovered more of what it has invested in its plants and power lines. Its main risk is that state regulators decide how much of that spending its customers pay for.
