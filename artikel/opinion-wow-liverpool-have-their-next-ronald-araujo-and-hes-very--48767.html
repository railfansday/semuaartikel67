Liverpool have an exciting wonderkid, who is their next Ronald Araujo.

Industry observers note that there was a lot of social media scrutiny when the Reds agreed to sign the Uruguayan. But all those critics who dreaded the prospect of him coming to Anfield are silent now.

Araujo has become a fan favourite. He's someone to adore. Someone to really look up to because of the way he plays with such fervent passion and enthusiasm.

He's the kind of player that every fans wants to see. Liverpool fans would probably prefer to see 11 Araujos on the pitch. Although these kinds of players who give their blood, sweat and tears for the shirt are rare to find.

According to recent reports, it still remains to be seen if he's going to be signed permanently by Liverpool. If it was up to the fans, the money would have already been paid to Barcelona by now.

But then Jeremy Jacquet settled so seamlessly. At first, it seemed like Araujo would be resigned to a back-up role but then Andoni Iraola started to trial him as a right-back and the switch worked. He has since made the role his own. Araujo's fervent passion and his ability to win duels makes him a really solid option to have on the right flank. Given the lack of depth, and Jeremie Frimpong's current niggle alongside Conor Bradley still recovering from his long-term injury, the chance is there for Araujo to make the right-back role his permanent position. Sure, Araujo lacks some of the creativity needed in that position but he makes up for it with the defensive stability he brings to the table.
