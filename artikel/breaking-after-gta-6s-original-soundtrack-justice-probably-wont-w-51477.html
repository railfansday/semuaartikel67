You've probably heard at least a little bit of the Grand Theft Auto 6 soundtrack from French electronic duo, Justice: a single taken from the act's original soundtrack has been released as a standalone track, and was the accompaniment to GTA 6's launch trailer. Featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and the fantastic King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, it's clear that the duo - and Rockstar - have spared no expense on the production of music for the game.

Industry observers note that from what we've heard so far, it's all very good: synth-drenched and poppy, a perfect mix of modern electronica and nostalgic throwback - ideal for the Miami-based setting of Vice City. You ought to enjoy the music while you can, though, because it seems Justice won't be coming back to video games any time soon. If ever.

Recent findings suggest that via a new interview with French publication Rockyrama, Justice's two members - Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay - shed a little light about their work with Rockstar, and their inspiration for joining the project. "When [the offer to work with Rockstar] arrived on the table, we thought 'if there’s one video game we have to do, it's this one.' As soon as we accepted and started working [on the OST], we thought it would probably be the first and last score we’ll ever do for a video game.".

Interesting. The duo, from the clip we can see on Rockyrama's posts, don't seem to give more information about this decision - did they have a bad time working with Rockstar? Was it intense? Or is it rather down to the fact that, if you're going to work on a game, you may as well make it what is largely expected to be the biggest game of all time.

Justice, for what it's worth, consider the work they've done on GTA 6 to be "a real album" of music - it's not seen as lesser or different to the band, just because it's being released as part of a video game. "GTA 6 gave us the excuse and direction to do it. The music always had to be listenable on its own, even if each track is tailored for [a specific] scene," the band explains.

Sources indicate that the band's album isn't the only music connected to the game, either. There's also a 34-song album launching for the game, composed of original songs from some of the biggest artists in the world. The album features Travis Scott, Rina Sawayama, Cardi B, Pinkpantheress, SZA, Keith Richards, Dua Lipa, Jennie, Lana Del Rey, Zara Larsson and many more. The full tracklist leaked at the weekend.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 debuts on Thursday, 19th November for PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. Rockstar has been embroiled in legal action after it was accused of union busting following the firing of over 30 employees. The case's conclusion will be determined this week.
