Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso has no choice but to reinstate Emiliano Martinez to their starting eleven against Leeds despite Mike Penders' impressive recent form.

The Blues finally looked to have solved their long-standing goalkeeping complications when Martinez arrived from Aston Villa in the summer.

He has been among the best stoppers on the planet in recent years, winning a wealth of individual prizes for club and country.

Martinez's form since joining Chelsea has not been particularly exemplary, and the broken jaw he suffered at Everton last month allowed 21-year-old back-up Penders to come into the team in his absence.

Penders impressed massively with Martinez sidelined, but now that the Argentine stopper is back fit, Alonso has a big decision to make for Saturday's visit of Leeds.

One stat in particular proves why the more experienced man should be back between the sticks at Stamford Bridge.

Martinez has only managed to keep one clean sheet in his first six Chelsea appearances, which was in the Everton clash where Penders came on for the final 15 minutes.

The Belgian back-up has kept two clean sheets in his seven starts so far this term, and boasts fewer goals conceded per 90 (0.95 to 1.89) and a better goals prevented per 90 (0.31 to 0.28) than Martinez in the top flight this season.

Those stats point to Penders being the obvious choice, especially after he won the October Premier League Save of the Month award for superbly denying Man United's Bryan Mbeumo.

However, Chelsea face a Leeds side who love to load the box and utilise their height and power to dominate opponents on Saturday, with the third-most set-piece goals scored (5) and joint second-fewest set-piece goals conceded (1) this season.

Martinez has greatly outperformed Penders for high claims per 90 in 2026-27, with 2.23 to his 0.63.

He is also better at finding a teammate from a distance, with 7.03 accurate long balls per 90 to Penders' 4.74.

The 21-year-old's sole mistake in his time starting for the Blues came as he punched the ball into his own goal from a cross in their 2-0 loss to Spurs.

Martinez would clearly be a better tactical fit for Chelsea against Leeds, and these stats in particular prove why.

Alonso spoke highly of Penders in pre-season but did insist that he would value experience in the form of Robert Sanchez, prior to Martinez's arrival in August.

Recent findings suggest that speaking to the press at that point of the summer, he remarked: "Mike is a young goalkeeper that has done a great season at Strasbourg. Rob, he has played many years in the club, in Chelsea, as well as the Premier League, he has experience. So to have this security in the goal is key.”.

It is clear that Alonso likes his keepers to have nous and know-how over anything, and so fans can surely expect Martinez to return to the team this weekend.
