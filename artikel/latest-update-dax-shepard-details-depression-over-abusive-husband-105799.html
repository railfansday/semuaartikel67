Dax Shepard is getting candid about his mental health amid controversy.

The Armchair Expert host shared he's experiencing depression over allegations that he's an "abusive husband" to his wife of 13 years Kristen Bell.

"I generally don't have depression, but I've had it," Shepard shared on the Nov. 23 episode of his podcast. "I'm so tired and I've been taking so many naps, and I got to say there that is a silver lining.".

The 51-year-old went on to describe the "continuing narrative" that he allegedly mistreats his wife as "very offensive," noting how the accusations first started last year when some social media users misconstrued an inside joke the Frozen star had made in an Instagram post celebrating their wedding anniversary.

"We were watching Dateline one time, and everyone kills their rich spouse," Shepard recounted to guest Zosia Mamet, explaining how he was "complimenting Kristen" by telling her at the time, "'You know, I’m heavily incentivized to kill you, but I’m not going to.’".

Though Bell had told him that the quip was "one of the most romantic things anyone stated to her," Shepard said a "cottage industry of women" online didn't think so and accused him of being a "dangerous man.".

And when Bell recently revealed that her husband unconventionally flirts with her by complimenting other people while in her presence, Shepard noted the allegations that he's a "controlling" spouse only snowballed.

"Of course, the controlling husband would try to control the narrative," Shepard sarcastically remarked, before agreeing to Mamet's assessment that he's "f--ked.".

In fact, Shepard has emphatically denied claims that he mistreats Bell, with whom he shares two daughters.

According to recent reports, "I don't ever yell at my children. I don't swear at them. I don't yell at my wife," he said on Armchair Expert's Nov. 9 episode. "To have given it my all to be the opposite of what, now, some group of people is saying I am is hard.".

"I find it deeply insulting to my wife, additionally," Shepard added. "She's sitting in a house with her two daughters and modeling some abusive relationship? It's very offensive to her.".
