It’s easy to get lost in the Apple release hype (we do it too, and were just as excited for the iPhone Duo), but the newest, fastest, shiniest iteration of tech products is often overkill for most people. Sometimes the older model is still the most capable and least fussy option. Case in point—The M5 MacBook Air.

It’s a best-of-all-worlds MacBook with a slim design, fast processing, and a reasonable price, making it the ideal personal laptop for most people. It’s also at the moment on sale for Black Friday, saving you $250.

We’ll admit the Apple MacBook M6 is the best laptop we tested this year. It has the powerful specs and compute you need for creative projects like image or video editing, audio production, or local AI workflows. However, it’s more computer than most people need for basic email, Google Docs, web browsing, casual gaming, and streaming video. Priced at $2,000, it’s also not cheap.

The MacBook Air with the M5 chip is for the everyman. It was the first in Apple’s Air series to include the powerful M5 processor, finally bridging the gap between the Pro and the Air. It offers 18 hours of battery life, more AI capabilities, and plenty of connectivity options, including two Thunderbolt 4 ports, a headphone jack (for the old-school types), a MagSafe charging port, and an Apple N1 wireless chip for Wi-Fi 7* and Bluetooth 6.

In a recent development, the vivid Liquid Retina display and four-speaker sound system with Spatial Audio and Dolby Atmos support handle your streaming and gaming needs with clarity and ease. For work, a 2MP Center Stage camera and three-mic array will handle Zoom or Teams calls.
