Brazil loss in Singapore marred by unruly end to friendly.

Argentina beaten 2-1 by Uruguay in Buenos Aires.

Raphinha was sent off as Brazil crashed to a 3-2 defeat by Japan in their international friendly in Singapore. The Barcelona captain had been substituted after 71 minutes, but was shown a red card after protesting against Tatsuhiro Sakamoto’s stoppage-time winner on the touchline. The Brazil head coach, Carlo Ancelotti, was also sent off in a fractious ending to the match.

Japan went 2-0 up inside 20 minutes through Takefusa Kubo and Keito Nakamura. Vinícius Júnior reduced the deficit in the 33rd minute and scored again to drag them level on 75 minutes, before Sakamoto’s controversial late winner.

Argentina’s post-Messi era began with a 2-1 defeat at home against Uruguay in Buenos Aires. The loss marked the World Cup finalists’ first match since Lionel Messi’s international retirement in early October.

Leonardo Balerdi opened the scoring with an own goal in the 48th minute, before Maximiliano Araújo doubled Uruguay’s lead in the 53rd minute under their newly introduced coach, Diego Forlán. Julián Álvarez pulled one back for the hosts in the 73rd minute.

This clásico rioplatense on Friday also kicked off a new chapter for Argentina under Lionel Scaloni, who extended his contract to 2030 this week.

According to recent reports, messi’s retirement remains a fresh wound for local fans; notably, the La Bombonera stadium fell short of a sellout, a rare departure from the norm. While most supporters in the stands wore the albiceleste jersey with Messi’s No 10, Scaloni chose not to assign the number for the match. The coach explained he has yet to select a successor and as it is not mandatory he prefers to leave the jersey vacant for now.

On the pitch, Argentina lacked the explosive, incisive spark Messi typically produced in the final third. Neither Nicolás Paz nor Thiago Almada was able to fill that void. Argentina will hope to bounce back against Costa Rica in Córdoba on Tuesday, while Uruguay host the Dominican Republic on the same day.
