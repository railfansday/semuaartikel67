Street trucks, real ones, are more than a flashy appearance package. Thankfully, Ford is committed to legit performance, as we can see with the new SV300T package developed by Ford Custom Garage for the Maverick pickup truck. Sure, the kit includes some prominent styling changes, like black or bronze body graphics and matching 20-by-8-inch wheels, but it also makes the engine more powerful and upgrades the chassis to be more playful.

The SV300T treatment is available on 2025–2026 Maverick XLT, Lariat, and Lobo models, with the former two requiring the turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder and all-wheel-drive system that’s standard on the Lobo. That setup is normally good for 250 horsepower and 280 pound-feet of torque, but those figures rise to 300 horses and 350 pound-feet. That’s attributed to the Maverick adopting a turbocharger from the EcoBoost Mustang that brings a larger compressor wheel (59 versus 53 mm), and it’s paired with a Ford Performance intercooler. Naturally, this setup benefits from a custom calibration. The soundtrack is provided by a Borla exhaust.

Recent findings suggest that along with more power, the XLT and Lariat versions of the Maverick SV300T receive an upgraded chassis that turns them into true street trucks. The list includes lowering springs, bigger anti-roll bars, and dampers from the Lobo, which already includes these parts as well as larger front brake rotors clamped by two-piston calipers. The latter is optional on the XLT and Lariat versions. If you’re wondering whether Lobo customers get a discount for not needing the extra add-ons, don’t worry, they do.

Industry observers note that for those who order the SV300T package directly from Ford, it can be installed on the XLT and Lariat for an extra $15,595; the Lobo version costs $2000 less. For reference, the ‘26 version of the Lobo starts at $37,775, while the turbocharged XLT and Lariat start at $32,490 and $37,715, respectively. You can do the math if you want the totals for the SV300T-equipped Mavericks, which Ford claims include installation, recommended tires, and transportation. Everything also comes with a three-year or 36,000-mile warranty.

If you already own an eligible Maverick and prefer to find your own ASE-certified technician to do the install, the price of the SV300T package drops considerably to $4500 for the Lobo and $6075 for the XLT and Lariat.
