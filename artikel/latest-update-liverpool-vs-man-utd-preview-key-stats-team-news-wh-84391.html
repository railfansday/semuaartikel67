Mauricio Pochettino begins his Manchester United reign with a huge test at Anfield on Sunday, with Manchester United desperate for a result after making their worst start to a top-flight season in 40 years.

In a recent development, manchester United have collected just eight points from their opening 10 Premier League matches, with one win and five draws leaving them 18th in the table. They have also conceded in every league game this season and are at the moment on a run of 11 consecutive matches without keeping a clean sheet in all competitions.

Mauricio Pochettino only arrived to the club on Wednesday after replacing Michael Carrick, leaving him with just a few training sessions to prepare for one of the biggest fixtures in English football.

There is some encouragement for the Argentine, however. Eight of United’s last nine permanent or caretaker managers have avoided defeat in their first match in charge. That may be some encouragement to the Argentine.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, Liverpool are not exactly enjoying a comfortable season at home either. They sit fourth with 18 points but have won just once in their five Premier League matches at Anfield so far, giving them the third-worst home record in the Premier League.

Recent findings suggest that their recent form across all competitions stands at three wins, one draw and two defeats in their last six matches. Liverpool most recently beat Crystal Palace 3-1, but they will know that United’s poor league position does not make this fixture any less complicated.

Liverpool have received positive news ahead of Sunday’s meeting. Dominik Szoboszlai and Virgil van Dijk are both expected to feature, while Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong could also be available.

Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni have returned to team training following knee injuries but are unlikely to make the squad. Hugo Ekitike remains a long-term absentee with an Achilles problem.

Manchester United could welcome back Patrick Dorgu and Harry Maguire after both trained following fitness concerns. They were seen in that leaked drone footage as well. Lisandro Martínez is also available again after serving his suspension.

Industry observers note that luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford could return, although their availability remains uncertain. Carlos Baleba, Manuel Ugarte and Benjamin Sesko are still sidelined.

The new United boss has a difficult personal record against Liverpool. Pochettino has faced the Reds 19 times, winning three, drawing five and losing 11. He has also failed to win any of his last eight meetings with them in all competitions. That might be a concern, but everything has a first time.

In a recent development, pochettino will be encouraged by United’s overall record against Liverpool. United have an edge with 85 wins compared to Liverpool’s 72 across 218 meetings. Their most recent encounter also went their way, with a 3-2 victory on May 3.

However, United arrive on a disappointing run of seven consecutive away matches without a win and have managed just one victory in their last 11 games.
