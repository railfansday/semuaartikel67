Mario Kart World is getting more free content, though not exactly the type many fans have been hoping for. A slew of upcoming courses and two new Knockout Tour routes are leaving players cold as they wait for something more substantial.

Ver. 1.9.0 patch went live on November 24 and adds eight courses, each a “remix” of an existing one. There are also a series of minor gameplay tweaks and bug fixes. Here are the highlights:.

You might think players would be happy for a surprise free revision adding newly introduced versions of courses heading into the holiday stretch, but a lot of the reactions so far have ranged from disappointed to confused. “Okay, now what about new tracks and DLC characters/content?” one person wrote on X. “No new courses but we need 9 million versions of SNES Mario circuit,” wrote another.

Not everyone’s upset, mostly because getting update 1.9.0 means Mario Kart World is that much closer to version 2.0.0. Fans have been speculating about the Switch 2 launch exclusive getting paid DLC since last December, and knowing Nintendo’s reputation for having content that’s ready to go and holding it back until it deems the time is right, I wouldn’t be surprised if the more extensive expansion people have been waiting for gets revealed early in 2027.

Sources indicate that some are banking on it appearing during the normal February Direct. In the meantime, you can race all your cousins in a latest version of Shy Guy Bazaar 2.
