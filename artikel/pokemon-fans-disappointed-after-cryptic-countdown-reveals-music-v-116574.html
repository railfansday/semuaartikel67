Pokemon fans have been left disappointed after a cryptic countdown from The Pokemon Company turned out to be a 30th-anniversary music video rather than upcoming information about Gen 10.

Since October, Pokemon’s Japanese X account had been posting anniversary images featuring different Pokemon and the franchise’s 30th anniversary logo. The National Pokedex number for the Pokemon featured in each image appeared to represent the hours remaining until November 30, with the countdown eventually pointing to a reveal at 2 p.m. UTC.

It has been revealed that the posts offered no explanation for what would happen when the timer reached zero. That led fans to speculate that Pokemon could finally reveal more about Gen 10, including another look at Winds and Waves, a fresh Pokemon Presents, or long-requested announcements such as Ruby and Sapphire ports.

Industry observers note that confirmation. We have a third data point676 hours from now is 14:00 UTC on November 30th 2026.As I predicted, they are counting down to then. What will arrive at that point, it remains a mystery but I am excited pic.twitter.com/68gFMSOy9M.

The Pokemon Manufacturer revealed that the countdown was leading to Pokemon Special Music Video “Evergreen,” a fresh animated project celebrating the franchise’s 30th anniversary.

Sources indicate that the video will feature a song by Official HIGE DANdism, with animation produced by MADHOUSE and direction from Naoko Yamada. It will premiere on the official Pokemon YouTube channel on November 30 at 11 p.m. JST, or 2 p.m. UTC. The “Evergreen” song will then be released digitally on December 1.

It has been revealed that pokemon described the project as the conclusion to its year-long “Pokemon to Tsuzuku,” or “Continuing with Pokemon,” anniversary campaign. The announcement was met with mixed reactions from fans who had spent weeks trying to work out what the countdown meant.

CentroLeaks stated the video would mark the finale of Pokemon’s 30th anniversary, while one fan posted, “I got excited for nothing.” Another questioned the point of counting down to “a f***ing music video no one on the planet cares about.”.

According to The Pokémon Firm this video will mark the finale of Pokémon’s 30th anniversary.

Other fans were more open to the idea, especially because the project could resemble 2020’s popular “GOTCHA!” music video, which was set to Bump of Chicken’s “Acacia.” One fan argued that if “Evergreen” reaches the same standard, it could still be “some good s***” for the anniversary’s animation lineup.

According to recent reports, however, the reveal still frustrated fans hoping for Gen 10 news. “We could’ve gotten Winds/Waves but nope,” one post read, calling the countdown “lame and pathetic” and saying it was not worth the buildup.

The music video may be the finale for Pokemon’s 30th Anniversary celebration, so fans expecting a Gen 10 announcement may have to wait for Pokemon Day in February 2027.
