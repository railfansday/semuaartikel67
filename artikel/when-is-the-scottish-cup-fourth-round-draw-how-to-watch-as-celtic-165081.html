It is that time of year when the Scottish Cup starts to come to prominence.

Twenty third round ties take place this weekend, culminating in the draw for the fourth round, which is when holders Celtic and the 11 other Premiership clubs enter the competition.

Here are all the details for the Scottish Cup fourth round draw:.

The Scottish Cup fourth round draw takes place on Sunday, November 29, starting at 7.15pm.

Recent findings suggest that the draw will be screened live on the BBC Scotland channel and the BBC Sport website. Former Hearts, Rangers and Motherwell midfielder Andy Halliday will make it.

Aberdeen, Celtic, Dundee, Dundee United, Falkirk, Hearts, Hibs, Kilmarnock, Motherwell, Rangers, St Johnstone, St Mirren.

Friday, November 27, 7.30pm: Largs Thistle v Ayr United.
