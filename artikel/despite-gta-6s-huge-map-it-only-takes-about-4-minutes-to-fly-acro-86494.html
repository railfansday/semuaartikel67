📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6's map is so major it's going to take months for people to comb through it and find everything. Rockstar has already posted the full map of Leonida online, but it doesn't answer one simple question that I know we've all been wondering: How long would it take to fly across this entire thing in a plane?

It has been revealed that the answer is a lot less time than it would take to go by car, but still long enough to give you an idea of the sheer scale we're working with here. Using the closest plane to Jason's house, my trip across Leonida took around 4 minutes and 18 seconds, which is a little under an hour of in-game time. You can watch the entire flight from start to finish below.

Industry observers note that keep in mind that the Mammoth Dodo seaplane that carried me from the southern tip of the archipelago to the northern tip of the map is just one type of plane in GTA 6. I would bet there are faster options if you really want to zoom.

Even though I'm still very early in the GTA 6's story, I didn't have to go too far out of my way to test this. Jason's house is conveniently located pretty close to a spot with perfectly unattended planes to borrow. The Sunbelt Highway that runs south of it will lead you right to the earliest planes and helicopters you can find. Just don't fly over the nearby military base (trust me, it's bad news) and you'll be fine.

I learned a few things on the way:.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar compared GTA 6's map to its previous games, describing it as about the size of GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. Despite knowing this beforehand, I was still shocked to see how the map kept going when I first opened it up. Four minutes might not sound like a lot, but the enormity of it all sinks in as you glide above densely populated cities and neighborhoods that could be the setting of their own standalone videogames.

Industry observers note that my experiment has left the fog of war on my map split into two halves. My flight path carved a line of partially revealed locations straight through it. I don't expect to clear the rest out for dozens of hours, but now I'm tempted to find out how long of a roadtrip it would be to do the same journey by car.
