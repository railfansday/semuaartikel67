Newly introduced Jersey congresswoman, charged with interfering with immigration arrest, set to face trial over May 2025 incident.

An appeals court decided on Thursday that the Democratic Newly introduced Jersey representative LaMonica McIver will continue to face charges related to a skirmish outside an immigration detention center last year when she attempted to conduct an oversight visit there.

The court’s decision to decline McIver’s request to overturn the court’s earlier ruling comes just a day after she pressed the homeland security secretary for the Trump administration’s aggressive immigration enforcement operations, telling the secretary: “You are killing people.”.

Now, with the court’s decision, she is facing a likely trial for the May 2025 incident.

“Because of a weaponized Department of Justice, and because I dared to do my job and conduct oversight of ICE, I am facing 17 years in prison,” McIver remarked in a statement on Thursday. “Now, due to the disappointing decision by the third circuit, we are all one step closer to a world in which the president of the United States can try to imprison people simply because he does not like what they stand for.”.

McIver was charged last year with assaulting, resisting, impeding and interfering with federal officers. The prosecution led to a lengthy legal battle, with McIver claiming the prosecution represented a political attack by the Trump administration.

McIver has attempted to have the case dismissed on various grounds, including by arguing the constitution protects her from prosecution. The third circuit court of appeals rejected her arguments in August and on Thursday declined her request to have an “en banc” hearing, meaning the full bench of active judges refused to reconsider the three-judge panel’s decision.

“This result is frustrating, but my lawyers and I are looking at every possible option for what comes next,” McIver added.

The case stems from a heated oversight visit to a contentious immigration detention center in her state.

Last May, McIver arrived at the Delaney Hall detention center, a large Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) facility in Latest Jersey, for a congressional oversight visit. She arrived with two other members of Congress and Newark’s mayor, Ras Baraka.

Recent findings suggest that mcIver and the other officials were barred access into the detention center. While standing outside Delaney Hall, a federal agent attempted to arrest Baraka for refusing to depart the premises. According to a homeland security agent’s declaration, McIver intervened, attempting to prevent Baraka from being arrested.

Federal officials claim that during the pandemonium, McIver “slammed her forearm” into the homeland security agent and “forcibly” struck another deportation officer with her forearms.

“I hope that everyone who feels that way will stand with me in my fight against these unjust charges, because while I am the first lawmaker they have tried to jail, I will surely not be the last,” McIver mentioned.

The court’s decision came a day after McIver got into a heated exchange with the homeland security secretary, Markwayne Mullin, for leading the agencies in charge of the Trump administration’s aggressive immigration crackdown.

The Delaney Hall facility has fallen under repeated scrutiny since it began detaining immigrants arrested by ICE again in 2025. At least three people detained at Delaney Hall have died. And the facility was the site of major clashes with ICE earlier this year during protests outside the facility in support of a hunger strike by detained immigrants.

McIver brought up conditions inside Delaney Hall and the number of who have died in ICE custody during Wednesday’s House committee hearing with Mullin.

“As you know, I am facing 17 years in prison, from this Trump administration, for my work at Delaney,” McIver said on Wednesday, then asked Mullin: “Do you even understand why I have been sounding the alarm about Delaney for so long?”.

Mullin quipped that he had visited Delaney and mentioned the facility was in “very good condition”.

McIver challenged Mullin’s claims, telling him: “History is going to judge you for what you are doing.”.

In a recent development, “You are killing people. You are killing people,” McIver added during the exchange with Mullin, who told her those claims were false.

McIver is the only elected representative facing charges for last year’s Delaney Hall scuffle. The charges against Baraka were dropped.
