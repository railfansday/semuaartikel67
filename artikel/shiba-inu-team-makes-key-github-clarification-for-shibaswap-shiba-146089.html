"Shib.io is home to both Shibarium and ShibaSwap," Shiba Inu developer and long-time community member Mazrael remarked, citing the project’s public Git repository as evidence of the longstanding relationship.

Mazrael recently spoke about the link between the official web presence of the Shiba Inu ecosystem and its decentralized platforms in a post, stating that the information has been consistent since the launch of ShibaSwap.

@Heisenbergiqr3 @tacitfi @randhindi @LCSHIB @Shibtoken https://t.co/RP8CH8r1zW is home of Shibarium and Shibaswap as indicated in our public git that has not changed since Shibaswap and is the same git for shibarium.

Mazrael indicates the public Git repository has not changed since the launch of ShibaSwap and is the same repository that is used for Shibarium. The clarification seems intended to quell confusion on Shiba Inu's official channels as legacy domains become decommissioned. He pointed to the project’s public Git as proof that Shib.io is the home of Shibarium and ShibaSwap.

The clarification strengthens the link among Shib.io, ShibaSwap and Shibarium, two key components of the Shiba Inu ecosystem.

Mazrael's reference to the unchanged public Git repository also highlights the team's position that this connection is not a recent development but part of the project's established infrastructure.

In the latest development for Shibarium, Mazrael unveiled that CreateX is now live on the Shiba Inu Layer 2.

Shiba Inu has climbed back above $0.0000035, recovering from Saturday's low of $0.00000337, its weakest level in years. It’s trading near $0.0000036, up 3.23% in the last 24 hours.

The broader crypto market is seeing a short-term rebound, but it does not fully establish that the decline has ended. According to Santiment, “Stablecoin Whale Dry Powder Continues Shrinking Across Crypto.”.

The largest tracked stablecoin whales are holding far less dry powder than before, Santiment stated, noting that since the start of the year, USDT balances on Ethereum held by the top 100 whales have fallen by $15.14 billion (-21.1%) and USDC (on Ethereum) balances are down $6.72 billion (-23.7%).

Sources indicate that stablecoins are crypto’s ready-to-deploy cash, so large whale balances can represent potential buying power for BTC and altcoins. Falling balances can mean funds were deployed, redeemed, transferred elsewhere, or moved outside these tracked wallets. Either way, having less USDT and USDC concentrated among major holders means less obvious dry powder waiting on the sidelines.
