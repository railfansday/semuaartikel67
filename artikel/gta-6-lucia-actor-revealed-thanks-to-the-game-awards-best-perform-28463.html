We finally know who is playing Lucia in GTA 6 thanks to Geoff Keighley, as the actor has been revealed by their nomination for best performance at The Game Awards.

Rockstar has been pretty quiet about who will be acting in GTA 6. King of the Hill actor Stephen Root revealed he was in the game after everyone already figured it out by virtue of Brian sounding like a number of his characters, which reportedly prompted Rockstar to send reminders to the voice cast that they are still under NDA. But with GTA 6 being eligible for The Game Awards 2026, it won't exactly match up with Rockstar's aims.

In a recent development, and now with the announcement of The Game Awards 2026 nominees which unsurprisingly features GTA 6 up for the top award, Best Performance has shed light on one of our two protagonists. Manni L. Perez – who has been rumored to be the lead actress for years now – as Lucia.

We still don't know who Jason is though, with actor Dylan Rourke rumored heavily for the role. We'll likely find out later this week.

Elsewhere in the category we have Patrick Gibson as James Bond in 007 First Light, Rahul Kohli as Arjun in Saros, Angela Sant'Albano as Grace in Resident Evil Requiem, and Liam McIntyre as Logan in Marvel's Wolverine.
