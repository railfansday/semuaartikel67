Recent findings suggest that is it really even Thanksgiving if a giant, monocled peanut in a top hat doesn’t strut down the streets of Manhattan? This year, for Planters, the answer is no.

The snacking brand is bringing Mr. Peanut back to Macy’s annual Thanksgiving Day Parade for the first time in a decade, and it’s not the only brand set to make a splash or debut as the tradition marks its 100th year.

With last year’s event the most-watched American entertainment program of the year, drawing a record 35.7 million viewers, brands like Levi’s, DoorDash, and Geico are now joining the celebrations for the first time, along with around 100 other balloons.
