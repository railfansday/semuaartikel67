Mercato: OM, three recruits (already identified) expected this winter

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Olympique de Marseille is already preparing its recruitment for the next winter transfer window. Despite the threat of sanctions from the National Directorate of Management Control (DNCG), Marseille leaders have identified three positions to strengthen, with several dozen players already targeted.

Recent findings suggest that the financial situation of OM does not seem to slow down the ambitions of its recruitment unit. According to information from ProvenceGrégory Lorenzi and his teams are already working on the next transfer market. Three profiles would be sought: a central defender, a box-to-box midfielder and a center forward.

In a recent development, the Marseille leaders have even taken a lead on certain concerns. Advanced discussions have already taken place with several dozen players. Priority would be given in particular to profiles with knowledge of Ligue 1 and likely to be recruited in the form of a loan. A track which would allow residents of the Vélodrome to strengthen their workforce without necessarily incurring excessive expenses.

Sources indicate that it remains to be seen whether this work can come to fruition. OM must in fact return to the DNCG at the beginning of December. With its payroll still exceeding the set threshold, the Marseille team expects sanctions. Its president, Stéphane Richard, also recognized that a sanction was likely, while ensuring that he wanted to limit the consequences.

Industry observers note that the challenge will therefore be to convince the supervisory body to leave more room for maneuver. In the meantime, Grégory Lorenzi continues his prospecting work with the aim of preparing as best as possible for the month of January. But between sporting ambitions and financial constraints, the next Olympian transfer window promises to be tricky.
