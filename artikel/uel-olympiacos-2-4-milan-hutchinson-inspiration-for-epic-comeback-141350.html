📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Omari Hutchinson kept Milan’s Europa League hopes alive by inspiring the 4-2 comeback win away to Olympiacos with a goal and two assists for Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic, then Diego Moreira late on.

It has been revealed that the Rossoneri had a terrible record in the Europa League, losing three of their four matches without finding the net, with a 4-0 victory away to RB Salzburg the outlier. They made the trip to Greece without Luka Modric, Francesco Camarda, Davide Bartesaghi, Strahinja Pavlovic, Samuel Chukwueze, Matteo Gabbia, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers and Pietro Terracciano, so squad rotation was kept to a minimum.

Olympiacos were still unbeaten with three wins and a draw, but missed Leon Bailey, Chiquinho, Gustavo Puerta and Santiago Hezze are out, plus long-term absentees and cup-tied Yusuf Yazici, Balsa Popovic, Ayoub El Kaabi, Marius Mouandilmadji, Clayton, Joel Roca and Lorenzo Scipioni.

Diego Moreira was valuable in defence during the opening minutes when charging down the Martins effort after a Panagiotis Retsos cross from the right.

Gelson Martins had a couple of pops from distance, but the first real chance was on 22 minutes with a curler that Maignan flew to palm around the upright.

It has been revealed that olympiacos were increasing the pressure and broke the deadlock with former Inter defender and Italy Under-21 international Pirola’s header, in off the underside of the crossbar from a Gustavo Sa corner. UEFA credited it as a Maignan own goal because the ball hadn’t completely crossed the line until it hit the goalkeeper on the way off the bar.

Pirola was every bit as decisive four minutes later, because Alphadjo Cisse had sprinted away on the counter, was tackled by the on-rushing goalkeeper, but still managed to prod it towards the empty net, only for the Italian defender to rush back for a desperate goal-line clearance.

It should’ve been 2-0 in added time, as Bruno Onyemaechi pulled back from the left for Pirola to fire over undisturbed from 10 yards.

It has been revealed that milan introduced Christian Pulisic and Yunus Musah for the restart, but instead went 2-0 down. Maignan made the save on the Joao Silva angled drive, but as the move continued, the same player ran onto the Freuler through ball to blast into the roof of the net from just inside the area.

It has been revealed that just as Olympiacos were pouring forward for a third, they were caught by a rapid counter-attack. Hutchinson started the move from inside his own half, rode two tackles and cut inside to drill low into the far bottom corner to give Milan some hope.

Hutchinson contributed to the equaliser too as the comeback was completed, his cross from the right finding Cisse to squirm away from his marker to nod in at the back post.

It has been revealed that hutchinson was simply unstoppable in the second half when used out wide on the right, and he provided his second assist to turn the game around, met by the fantastic Pulisic volley from 12 yards with the right boot. It was the American’s fifth goal in his last six appearances.

Cisse’s acrobatic bicycle kick didn’t quite come off from yet another Hutchinson cross, while Pirola’s risky interception prevented Adrien Rabiot meeting the Moreira assist.

In the final minute, penalty appeals were rejected because a header was charged down by Fikayo Tomori’s shoulder, but then Milan wasted the chance to add a fourth on the counter with Pulisic ballooning over.

With the last kick of the game, Hutchinson broke away on the counter from an Olympiacos corner, sent Pulisic down the left, he rolled across for Moreira to take a touch, then sweep in from 12 yards for 4-2.

The Rossoneri had never been able to beat a Greek team away from home before, managing only four draws and two defeats, including a 3-1 loss to Olympiacos in December 2018.
