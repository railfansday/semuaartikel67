Getting everything set up was surprisingly easy, too. The step-by-step instruction booklet was handy, but after just a quick glance, I realized I was probably overthinking things. This was going to be much easier than my first microgreens-growing experiment!

First, I placed the water reservoir on the table, popped in the planting trays, and added the bamboo fiber mats, making sure the little wicking tabs were tucked underneath so they could reach the water below. Then came the fun part: sprinkling the seeds over the mats!

Once everything was in place, I filled the reservoir to the maximum fill line and gave the seeds a light misting to help settle them.

I popped the humidity dome on top, closed the vent to keep the moisture in, and placed the whole setup by my kitchen window alongside my other houseplants. It took about three days before I started seeing little sprouts appear, and in 8 days, I had leaves and a thick mass of roots. It's such a fun process to watch them grow. Microgreens really do seem to spring up before your eyes! The instructions recommend opening the dome vent as soon as you see upcoming growth, so that's exactly what I did.

One other thing to keep an eye on is the water level. You want to make sure the little wicking tabs on the bamboo mats stay in contact with the water in the reservoir below. That way, your growing microgreens have a steady supply of moisture without you having to fuss over them too much.
