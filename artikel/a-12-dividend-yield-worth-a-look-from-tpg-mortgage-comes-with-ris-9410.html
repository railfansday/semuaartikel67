Everyone would like a 12% yield that comes with little to no risk. With MITT-B (MITT.PR.B), investors can get that 12% yield.

Unfortunately, the risk decided to come along.

MITT-B is a preferred share from TPG Mortgage Investment Trust (MITT). Shares are right now trading around $16.94 and offer a stripped yield of approximately 12.10%.

That's a massive yield for a preferred share. However, it does come with our highest risk rating. MITT-B carries a risk rating of 5 on our scale from 1 to 5. Of all the preferred shares we cover, only two companies have preferred shares carrying that rating. MITT is one of them.
