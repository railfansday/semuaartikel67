📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Rockstar Games just launched Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19 to rave reviews and huge sales. Everyone wants to know what's coming next, and surely there will eventually be another GTA game, but not soon. Appearing on OpenAI's technology and business show TBPN recently, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick was asked how a fresh GTA game could possibly up the ante. TBPN's Jordi Hays theorized that GTA 7 could "be a live version of the entire world." Zelnick, for his part, remarked people should remember that the interview was recorded on November 19, the day that GTA 6 released, so it's not time to talk about GTA 7. "I can't help myself. We at Take-Two just went live GTA 6 on November 19. Let me check my watch. Oh, it's still November 19. And you're asking a question about GTA 7," he said. While Rockstar isn't talking about GTA 7 yet--and no one should expect that to happen anytime soon--Take-Two has already signaled that the next GTA game could come out sooner. He said the “nature and velocity” of the firm’s content releases in its major franchises, including GTA, will look “dramatically different over the next decade.” GTA 6 released more than 13 years after GTA 5 came out in 2013. However, development didn't begin in earnest on GTA 6 until after Red Dead Redemption II came out in 2018, according to reporter Jason Schreier. This is because Rockstar's network of studios around the world work together on one project at a time, though smaller teams are also cooking up new things simultaneously.
