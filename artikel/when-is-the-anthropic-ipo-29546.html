Sources indicate that anthropic is scheduled to finalise its IPO pricing on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with shares expected to start trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market the following day, November 19, under the ticker symbol ANT. The firm has set an indicative price range of $70 to $78 per share, with its fully diluted valuation projected between $1.26 trillion and $1.41 trillion. The offering is attracting major technology investors, with Nvidia, Google and Microsoft among seven companies participating in a separate $26 billion private placement at the IPO price. Anthropic, the developer of Claude AI, has reported an annualised revenue run rate of approximately $81 billion as of October 2026. However, its expansion aims also involve substantial spending on computing infrastructure, making the listing an important test of investor appetite for large AI businesses.
