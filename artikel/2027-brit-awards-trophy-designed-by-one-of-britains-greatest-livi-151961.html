Recent findings suggest that the 2027 BRIT Awards trophy has been designed by former Turner Prize-winning visual artist Dame Rachel Whiteread (Nov. 27).

Each year a different designer shapes a distinct creative vision for their trophy, modeled on the image of Britannia, traditionally represented by a female warrior with a shield and helmet.

It has been revealed that in recent years, the trophy has been designed by iconic names such as Dame Vivienne Westwood, Sir Peter Blake, Tracey Emin, Dame Zaha Hadid, and last year’s designer, Matthew Williamson. Sources indicate that whiteread took on 2027’s assignment, and her trophy will be presented to winners at the ceremony on Feb. 27 at the Co-op Live in Manchester.

Sources indicate that in 1993, Whiteread became the first woman to win the Turner Prize, an accolade presented annually to a British visual artist; previous victors include Damien Hirst, Gilbert & George, and Anish Kapoor. Four years after her win, in 1997, she was the first woman to represent Britain at the Venice Biennale.

Her work primarily focuses on casting, and her Turner Prize-winning exhibit House (1993) featured a life-sized concrete cast of a Victorian terraced house in east London. Whiteread’s work has been exhibited in the Tate Museum, Paris’ Centre Pompidou and Upcoming York’s Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Museum of Modern Art.

Recent findings suggest that whiteread’s vision for the 2027 trophy features a steely, silver frame with holes punctuated throughout a silver frame alongside holes. “I used the motif of the BRIT Award and tried to change it in some way,” she said in a statement. “I exposed the interior of the statue – as an artist would do with their music.”.

Recent findings suggest that speaking on the appointment, Glyn Aikins, co-president of RCA Records at Sony Music U.K., called Whiteread one of “Britain’s greatest living artists.” He added, “Rachel has created something that is unmistakably her own and is a fitting tribute to the limitless imagination of the artists we celebrate each year and that also captures what the BRITs stands for in celebrating the very best of British creativity. The artists who lift this trophy will be holding a genuine work of art.”.

Nominations for the 2027 BRIT Awards will be announced in the coming months. No performers have yet been confirmed.
