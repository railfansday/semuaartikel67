Delta fans, take note: You can get some serious bonus miles in the coming weeks by booking a cruise.

As of this week, Delta Cruises is offering customers 5 Delta miles for every eligible dollar spent on a cruise. That's up sharply from the standard offer of 2 miles per dollar spent. Terms apply.*.

Indeed, Delta Cruises notes this is the richest accelerated mileage offer it has run all year.

Meanwhile, you can earn even more Delta miles by taking advantage of this promotion and paying for your cruise with a Delta SkyMiles-branded credit card. More on that below.

Recent findings suggest that note that you must act fast to get the bonus miles. The accelerated Delta Cruises mileage offer is only available through Dec. 15. It applies to new bookings for cruises scheduled between now and Dec. 31, 2027.

The Delta Cruises bonus miles offer is available to customers who book any cabin on any of the cruise brands available through Delta Cruises.

These include all the major cruise brands, such as Royal Caribbean, Carnival Cruise Line, Norwegian Cruise Line, MSC Cruises and Princess Cruises, as well as many smaller ocean, expedition and river cruise lines.

Among the caveats: Bonus miles are earned per cabin and are awarded to only one passenger per cruise booking. In addition, to qualify, customers must book through the Delta Cruises program at cruises.delta.com.

Also, the number of miles that customers earn as part of the promotion is based on the total cruise purchase price excluding port charges, port expenses, travel protection, taxes and fees charged at the time of booking.

There is no limit to the number of miles a customer can earn under the promotion.

Miles will post to Delta SkyMiles accounts six to eight weeks after the cruise is completed, according to Delta Cruises.

You can maximize your purchase by using a Delta SkyMiles credit card, such as the , which earns 1 mile per dollar spent on cruises. With this card, you can find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 80,000 bonus miles after you spend $2,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. Welcome offers vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer. Terms apply.*.
