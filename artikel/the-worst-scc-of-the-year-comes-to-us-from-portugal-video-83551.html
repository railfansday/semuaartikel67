It should have been an uneventful evening for FC Porto, who faced a Liga 3 team in the Portuguese Cup on Friday. But if the Dragoes avoided humiliation by qualifying on penalties (1-1, 3-1 tab), Francisco Moura will keep terrible memories of this meeting.

The 27-year-old defender scored one of the most incredible CSCs of the year. After a duel lost by Seko Fofana (who opened the scoring in the first period), and a ball pushed back by his goalkeeper, Francisco Moura pushed the leather into his own net, with a simple pass from the inside of his foot, very poorly assured.

According to recent reports, even if he was not really attacked, the former Famalicao player, with his back to goal, undoubtedly wanted to push the ball for a corner to avoid taking any risks. The least we can say is that he missed his gesture.

Immediately, his teammates and his goalkeeper came to comfort him. The FC Porto supporters, understanding, also applauded him.

In a recent development, “Anything can happen in the Cup, reacted coach Francesco Farioli on Sport TV. There have been several examples recently, it’s always difficult. Situations can arise that complicate everything. It’s part of football. As you saw, we are all behind Francisco (Moura). It’s unfortunate. The support from FC Porto players and even Paredes has been great, these are very positive gestures. ».
