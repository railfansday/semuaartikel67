Recent findings suggest that A man has developed a condition that makes him see the face of his cat on humans and vice versa after having surgery. Seriously.

According to MedicalXpress, a 28-year-old man by the name of Karl from the northeastern United States developed a form of palinopsia after having brain surgery back in 2025.

According to recent reports, his form of palinopsia, prosopometamorphopsia, means that there are times when Karl has seen something earlier in the day, it can persist or reappear later on. As MedicalXpress notes, the 28-year-old has suffered this when he sees his cat, Luna, before anything else that day.

It has been revealed that in those cases, Karl will see features of his grey tabby cat in the faces of other human beings. The main of those being his cat’s eyes, pupils, and stripes.

It isn’t always the cat that he sees, however. The report notes that the 28-year-old can see things the other way round, meaning he sees human eyes on the face of his cat. Which is pretty freaky.

“This case of Karl seeing his cat’s features in human faces and vice versa demonstrates that individual features can be pulled from memory and influence our ongoing perception,” noted Brad Duchaine, a professor and chair of psychological and brain sciences and principal investigator of the Social Perception Lab at Dartmouth.

Duchaine noted that Karl’s situation is different to other cases of prosopometamorphopsia – PMO – that have been documented in the past, due to the fact he only sees features, not the full face.

The Newly introduced York Post noted that PMO is sometimes often referred to as “demon face syndrome” because of how it can distort the perception of someone else’s face.

As for treatment, the 28-year-old is continuing to work with Duchaine about the condition.
