Willie Geist is making a change to his morning routine today.

The television personality announced that he'll be leaving Morning Joe at the end of the year after nearly 20 years on the MSNOW show cohosted by Joe Scarborough and Mika Brzezinski as he expands his role at Today.

Sources indicate that "I am so grateful to both NBC and MSNOW for the unique opportunity to do what I love at both networks," Geist remarked in a statement Nov. 16. "This is a bittersweet goodbye to Joe, Mika, and the place that made possible everything else in my career over the last two decades, but I am thrilled to settle in with my family at Today and dedicate all of my time to that special place.".

Industry observers note that by no longer being the only on-air talent to work for both NBC News and MSNOW since last year's spinoff of cable networks from NBCUniversal, the Sunday Sitdown podcast host—who first joined Today as the cohost of its 9 a.m. hour back in 2012—will refocus his efforts on building out his presence on Today.

"Over the past 10 years, Willie has transformed Sunday Today from a broadcast into a distinctive, multi-platform brand with a voice and identity all its own,” Executive Vice President of Today and Lifestyle Libby Leist explained in a statement. “We’re thrilled he’ll now be able to focus full-time on building the brand in latest ways and bringing his exceptional journalism to all of our platforms.".

Geist, 51—who has interviewed numerous celebs including Dolly Parton, Jennifer Lopez, David Letterman, Al Pacino and Hillary Clinton on Sunday Today with Willie Geist—had previously shared his desire to facilitate "deeper storytelling" on the series that he hopes to be "uplifting, but not cheesy.".

"The more time you can spend with somebody, the more of a relationship you develop with the person if you don't already have one, the more trust you build with the person or the more interesting the interview becomes," he told E! News back in 2021. "We ask for an hour. Sometimes we go longer, sometimes it's 45 minutes based on somebody's schedule, but if I don't know the person already, that gives me that trust and shows them I'm prepared and I respect their time and I respect their work.”.
