It has been revealed that anne Hathaway is officially a mom of three, and her smile says she couldn’t be happier. Yesterday afternoon, the Oscar winner revealed to the world that she had given birth to her little one when she stepped out for a stroll in Latest York City, smiling widely as she carried her newborn—a daughter—swaddled to her chest in a brown velvet baby carrier.

The only part of Hathaway’s youngest that was visible was her head, snuggled in a plum-colored beanie decorated with a tiny pink pom-pom, which peeked out from the top of the carrier.

In these first public photos with her newborn, Hathaway sported not only a big grin, but also a luxury gray overcoat from Brunello Cucinelli, which she layered over white sweats and a lavender knit sweater. She completed the cozy fit with brown suede Rothy’s clogs and a black baseball cap.

According to TMZ, who first broke the photos, the Verity star was seen walking around with a friend, who even stopped to capture snaps of Hathaway with her newborn.

It has been revealed that the Devil Wears Prada 2 star and her husband, Adam Schulman, are also parents to sons Jonathan and Jack, who were born in 2016 and 2019, respectively. Hathaway has yet to reveal when exactly she gave birth to their third child, or the name of their daughter, but the sex was revealed in a congratulatory Instagram Story by her costume designer, Molly Rogers.

After months of witnessing Hathaway’s incredible maternity style as she promoted her many projects of the year, we’re ready to be inspired by her mom fashion.
