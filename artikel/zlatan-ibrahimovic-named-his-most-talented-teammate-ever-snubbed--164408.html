Zlatan Ibrahimovic Named His ‘Most Talented’ Teammate Ever - Snubbed Messi and Rooney

Truth be told, if Zlatan Ibrahimovic could name himself as his most talented ever teammate, then he probably would. Unfortunately for him, though, that's not how it tends to work.

Whether the Swede will ever admit it or not, he had the chance to play with some generational stars throughout an illustrious career. He spent time at AC Milan, Paris Saint-Germain, Barcelona, Ajax, Inter Milan and Manchester United - making an impact wherever he went.

By the end of his career, Ibrahimavic had more than 500 goals to his name. Wherever he went, he was the star of the show. But every show needs co-stars and the towering striker had those in abundance.

It's not often that an inflated ego is justifiable, but when you've had the career that Ibrahimovic had, then perhaps it's fair to refer to yourself in third person.

Industry observers note that the former Barcelona man linked up with some undeniable legends throughout his time on the pitch, too. At the Nou Camp, he was flanked by Lionel Messi - argaubly the greatest player of all time. Even he didn't deny Messi's GOAT status during punditry duty at the 2026 World Cup.

"We spend years comparing him to everyone else, but even the greats before him can’t match the complete body of work. Pele, [Diego] Maradona, [Johan] Cruyff, whoever you want to name, they were all brilliant, but Messi’s numbers, longevity, and trophies put him in a category of his own.".

It has been revealed that in the latter stages of his career, Ibrahimovic had the chance to play with Wayne Rooney at Man United. It wasn't exactly the Red Devils at the peak of their powers, but the duo still gave Old Trafford a taste of what life may have looked like with both in the prime of their careers.

Recent findings suggest that at AC Milan, the iconic forward also played with Andrea Pirlo, who was so often a striker's dream throughout his career. The list could go on and on, but none of Ibrahimovic's former teammates are more talented than one special South American, according to the Swede himself.

Industry observers note that it takes a lot to snub the likes of Messi and Rooney, but to Ibrahimovic's defence, he has quite the list of teammates to pick from. When he was asked to narrow that list down to one name, he selected Brazilian star Adriano.

Industry observers note that having played with the Brazilian at Inter from 2006 to 2009, Adriano and Ibrahimovic had the chance to form quite the partnership. Their best season came in the 08/09 campaign when the duo scored a combined 55 goals as Inter picked up a fourth-straight Serie A title.

While mental health battles prevented the Brazilian from ever truly reaching his full potential, Adriano remains the "most talented" teammate that Ibrahimovic has ever had.

Speaking to reporters, Ibrahimovic spoke highly of the former Inter man, saying: "I played with great champions. I played with players that were already...wow. I played with players that I saw were a talent and became...wow.

In a recent development, "But the one I felt could [play at the top for] longer, and he didn't do it, was Adriano when I was at Inter. When I came to Inter the first thing I mentioned to the president was 'I demand [Adriano] to stay because he is the player I want to play with'.

Industry observers note that "Because, the way he was, he was an animal. He could shoot from every angle. Nobody could tackle him, nobody could take the ball, he was a pure animal. But, it last for a short time.".
