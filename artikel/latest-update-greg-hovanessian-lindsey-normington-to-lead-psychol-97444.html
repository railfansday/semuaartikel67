Industry observers note that EXCLUSIVE: Greg Hovanessian (The Mist, Cardinal) and Lindsey Normington (Margo’s Got Money Troubles, Anora) are set to lead the upcoming psychological thriller Nightwaves, from writer and director Kristian McKay (Do Not Open, Kill Trip) and Obscura Film. Cherie Currie (Foxes, Twilight Zone: The Movie) and Shane Coffey (Pretty Little Liars, Good Girls) will also star.

Set against the dark, desolate highways of Texas, Nightwaves unfolds over the course of a single night, following Mason (Hovanessian), a young man driving alone whose unexpected phone conversation with a mysterious woman (Normington) offers an initially welcome escape. As their exchange shifts from flirtation to increasingly unsettling questions, the woman on the other end of the line begins to threaten everything Mason is desperately trying to leave behind. With each passing mile, he finds himself moving closer to a confrontation he cannot simply hang up to avoid. Industry observers note that currie and Coffey’s roles are under wraps. Watch on Deadline.

Production is presently underway in San Antonio and the surrounding Texas landscape.

Recent findings suggest that “There’s something incredibly intimate about talking to someone in the dark when you can’t see their face. You start filling in the blanks, and that can be seductive or terrifying,” shared McKay. “With Nightwaves, I want the audience to feel that connection tightening until it becomes a trap. Greg and Lindsey bring the vulnerability and unpredictability these roles demand, and having Cherie and Shane in this world gives the film such a distinct personality.”.

According to recent reports, obscura Film touts in a release how “McKay will bring a distinctive visual approach to the thriller, allowing tension and performances to unfold in extended, uninterrupted moments. Using the empty stretches of highway, passing headlights, and the confined interior of a moving car, the film transforms an ordinary phone conversation into an increasingly claustrophobic psychological experience, where what remains unseen may be more terrifying than anything in view.”.

The film is produced by Obscura Film partners Daunesh Alcott and McKay, alongside Jose Corres. Executive produced by Gustavo Oliveira.

Recent findings suggest that nightwaves expands Obscura Film’s growing slate of independently produced genre features. The project follows Kill Trip, acquired by Quiver Distribution and released in 2026, and Do Not Open, McKay’s supernatural thriller starring Freddy Carter, Lulu Wilson, Mark McKenna, John Ford Coley and Stelio Savante, which is currently in post-production.

It has been revealed that hovanessian is repped by Robert Stein Management and Clear Talent Group. Normington is repped by Innovative Artists and Authentic Talent & Literary Management. Coffey is repped by Industry Entertainment and Pakula/ King.
