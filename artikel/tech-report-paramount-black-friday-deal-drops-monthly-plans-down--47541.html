At a Glance: Paramount+ Black Friday Offer.

– Paramount+ Essential includes: Ad-supported streaming, 40,000+ episodes and movies, three simultaneous streams, NFL on CBS, UEFA Champions League, live UFC events and select Showtime series. – Paramount+ Premium includes: Everything in Essential, an ad-free on-demand experience, 4K UHD and select HDR formats, downloads, live CBS streaming and the full Showtime library.– This offer does not apply to annual subscriptions.

The most compelling aspect of this promotion is the opportunity to get Paramount+ Premium for the same introductory price as its Paramount+ Essential tier. At just $2.99 per month for the first two months, the offer also stands out among this year’s Black Friday streaming deals.

While competing platforms have rolled out their own seasonal discounts, many still come in above Paramount+’s introductory price, making this one of the more affordable opportunities to add a major streaming service to your lineup.

For viewers looking to stretch their entertainment budget without sacrificing access to premium programming, live sports and a substantial movie and television library, it’s a particularly appealing deal. With the promotion running for just 11 days and ending Nov. 30, there’s a very limited window to take advantage.
