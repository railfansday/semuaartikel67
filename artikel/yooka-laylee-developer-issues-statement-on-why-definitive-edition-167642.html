Digital Foundry's John Linneman has also weighed in on this:.

Original Story: Yesterday, we published an article about how Xbox fans were making their voices heard regarding a lack of Definitive Edition upgrade for Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair, and we've now got an official statement about it.

In a post on social media, developer Playtonic Games explained that "the cost of bringing it to more platforms just isn't viable for us at this time", and that they've had to "prioritise our efforts given the technical challenges and costs".

Here's the full statement that was shared by the studio:.

"Hi folks, just wanted to say a massive thanks to everyone on here for the support for Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition. The response has BEE-n incredible, and we can't wait for you to experience the game all over again on December 15th. We are aware of feedback regarding the lack of Xbox Series S/X support, and we just wanted to provide some clarity on the reasoning behind this decision.

In a recent development, bringing Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition to PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 has been an exciting adventure, and we can't wait for latest and returning players alike to experience the Impossible Lair on these platforms for the first time. Unfortunately, we've had to prioritise our efforts given the technical challenges and costs presented in the creation of this updated version of the game and as a result, we are unable to commit to any additional platforms for the foreseeable future.

Sources indicate that we understand the frustration felt by those of you who won't be able to access the Definitive Edition at launch, but unfortunately the cost of bringing it to more platforms just isn't viable for us at this time. As a small team, we hope you guys can understand the challenges we face. Please know that this wasn't an easy decision to make, and we apologise to any of you affected by this. We only wish things were different, and we value every single one of you and your feedback.".

Based on this, it only sounds like there's a slim chance of getting the Definitive Edition upgrade of Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair on Xbox, but who knows whether it could end up being added down the line.

For what it's worth, the game already runs and plays well on Xbox One, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, but it's obviously a bummer when other platforms are getting enhanced visuals and up to 120FPS as part of this upgrade.

It is what it is! We'll just keep enjoying the 2019 version instead.
