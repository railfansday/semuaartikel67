Steven Spielberg has directed musicals, sci-fi epics, adventure films, and biopics, but one genre has eluded him over the course of his decades-long career. He has never directed a Western, despite having openly longed to put his spin on the type of movies that turned one of his idols, John Ford, into a legend.

Industry observers note that well, the wait is over. Spielberg is developing an untitled film for Focus Features about two fugitives charting a course across the American West, and he’s assembled a crack team to help him. The ensemble will include Jessie Buckley, fresh off her Oscar win for “Hamnet,” Cillian Murphy, the Oscar-winning star of “Oppenheimer,” and Mark Rylance, a frequent Spielberg collaborator who won, you guessed it, an Oscar for “Bridge of Spies.” Related Stories ‘Focker In-Law’ Review: An Eager Ariana Grande Isn’t Enough to Save a Comedy Sequel That Can’t Remember What People Liked About This Franchise Jane Fonda on Documentary ‘Gaslit,’ Trump, and What the Democrats’ Midterm Victory Means for Climate Change.

It has been revealed that but there’s more to get cinephiles’ hearts racing! Paul Thomas Anderson, the Oscar-winning filmmaker behind “Boogie Nights,” “There Will Be Blood,” and “One Battle After Another,” is writing the script. It’s based on Kevin Barry’s novel, “The Heart in Winter.” Though Anderson usually directs his screenplays, he’s done script doctoring for Ridley Scott (“Napoleon”) and Martin Scorsese (“Killers of the Flower Moon” and the upcoming “What Happens at Night”). He also did some work on “Corky Romano” — hey, they can’t all be winners! So why not join Spielberg’s merry band?

It has been revealed that spielberg is still one of the all-time greats, but he could use a hit. “Disclosure Day,” which marked his return to summer movies, was a box office disappointment, and his most recent films, like “The Fabelmans” and “West Side Story,” struggled to connect with audiences despite earning excellent reviews. Maybe saddling up will be just what the movie gods ordered.

Kristie Macosko Krieger, Sara Murphy, Anderson, and Spielberg will produce the film, which is expected to hit theaters in 2028. Tim Headington, Neil Shah, Ryan Zacarias, and Ana Leocha will executive produce it.
