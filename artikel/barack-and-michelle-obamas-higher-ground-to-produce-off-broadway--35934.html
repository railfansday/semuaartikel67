Barack and Michelle Obama are returning to the New York theater scene with some Real Housewives in tow.

Sources indicate that higher Ground, the media manufacturer founded by the former president and first lady, will serve as co-producers on the upcoming Off-Broadway revival of Neil Simon’s farce “Rumors.” This production features several stars from Bravo’s “Real Housewives” franchise, including Lisa Rinna, Phaedra Parks, Dolores Catania, Stacey Rusch, Cynthia Bailey, Eileen Davidson, Carole Radziwill and Candiace Dillard-Bassett. This will be the second theatrical venture for Higher Ground following this year’s revival of “Proof,” featuring Ayo Edebiri and Don Cheadle.

In a recent development, “Rumors,” which will be directed by Zhailon Levingston, a recent Tony Award winner for “Cats: The Jellicle Ball,” revolves around a swanky soirée that spirals out of control. Here’s the official logline: “Reality was just rehearsal. When four couples gather for a party in a wealthy New York suburb, they expect champagne, revelry, and a night of good fun. Instead, they find their host in an alarming state, his wife missing, and the police on their way. Talk about a dinner party from hell!”.

Sources indicate that “Zhailon Levingston’s reimagining of this beloved Neil Simon classic will be a dream come true for fans of reality TV and great theatre alike,” said Michelle Obama. “As someone who’s seen her share of ‘Housewives’ drama, I can’t imagine a better cast to serve up its secrets, gossip, and spiraling chaos. Our team at Higher Ground is thrilled to help bring this sharp, visionary production to life.”.

The revival will have a limited run at the Newman Mills Theater at the Robert W. Wilson MCC Theater Space. Performances will begin on Jan. 9 ahead of opening night on Jan. 25, 2027. It debuts nearly four decades after the original production of “Rumors,” which premiered on Broadway in 1988 and starred Christine Baranski, Jessica Walter, Joyce Van Patten and Lisa Banes.

“It is the greatest honor to welcome President Barack Obama, fellow ‘Real Housewives’ superfan Michelle Obama, and the entire Higher Ground team to our revival of Neil Simon’s ‘Rumors’ as a co-producer,” mentioned producer George Strus. “Their enthusiasm for Zhailon’s vision is both inspiring and exciting, and I am thrilled to collaborate with them to bring this masterful comedy back to the Latest York stage for the first time since its original Tony Award-winning 1988 Broadway bow. We are overjoyed to welcome Higher Ground to the team and cannot wait to welcome audiences to the world of ‘Rumors’ in early January.”.
