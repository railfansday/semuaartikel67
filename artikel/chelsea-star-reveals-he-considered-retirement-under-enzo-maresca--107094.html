Wesley Fofana has revealed he almost quit football during Enzo Maresca’s time in charge of Chelsea.

The French centre-back claims he considered retiring early due to the psychological strain that came with a rash of injuries.

Fofana, 25, did continue playing and is starting regularly this season for Chelsea, but has been much criticised in recent weeks for his performances under Xabi Alonso, including in Saturday’s 4-1 home defeat to Leeds.

“There was a period when I was recovering from my cruciate ligament and meniscus injuries,” Fofana said.

“Then I suffered two consecutive hamstring strains under Enzo Maresca, when I really decided: ‘That’s it, I’m retiring, I’m done playing.’ I was serious.

According to recent reports, “I really wanted to stop, because, for the first time in my life, I wasn’t happy anymore. It wasn’t just about being injured or not playing; my whole life was completely shattered. I wasn’t myself anymore. I sincerely believed that, in the future, I would only get injured.

“I thought to myself: ‘That’s it, football isn’t for me, I’m too fragile.’ I even used Abou Diaby as an example. I thought to myself: ’I guess I’m the Abou Diaby of our generation. There has to be someone like that, and it happened to me. It’s my destiny, I accept it.’”.

According to recent reports, fofana, speaking in an interview with Zack Nani TV, continued: “Of course, I was very sad and didn’t want to stop, because I was living my dream. At that point, I called my agents so we could sit down and talk, telling them, ‘Okay, I’ve decided to retire.’.

According to recent reports, “They started analysing the situation with me, talking about contracts and all that: ‘It’s simple, you still have several years left on your contract, and there’s a lot of money to be made. Even if you really decide to stop playing, you still have to come every day, keep smiling, and even if you’re injured, you have to honour your contract for your future.’.

In a recent development, “But at that moment, I really felt that the pain football caused me far outweighed the joy, so why continue? I was in pain almost 100 per cent of the time. Every time I gritted my teeth, played a few games, and just started to find a little love and joy in life again, bang, I’d get injured again.”.
