Management reported that while the company met the upper end of its adjusted EBITDA guidance, underlying operational performance was impacted by softer demand in the death care market and project delays within the industrial engineering segment. Leadership stated that immediate priorities involve improving cash generation and simplifying the organizational structure to gain better purchasing leverage across its remaining business units.
