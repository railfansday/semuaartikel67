Take-Two Interactive (TTWO +0.65%) and Anthropic (ANT -8.50%) are arguably the two stocks that generated the most excitement this week.

It has been revealed that anthropic introduced its IPO this week, as its Claude artificial intelligence (AI) assistant has generated investor excitement and driven massive revenue growth. That may have been overshadowed by gamers who have awaited Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6), possibly since Take-Two released GTA 5 in 2013.

Despite that anticipation and the likely boost to Take-Two stock, I believe Anthropic is the better buy, and here's why.

Admittedly, Take-Two might be a more obvious choice since the anticipation for GTA 6 likely predates Anthropic's 2021 founding.

It has been revealed that with that waiting game now over, Take-Two subsidiary Rockstar Games revealed that it sold 32.5 million copies of the game on day one, generating $3.79 billion in revenue. That is huge considering that the company reported $3 billion in the first six months of fiscal 2027 (ended Sept. 30). That was game-changing, figuratively and literally, for Take-Two stock, which also reported an 8% year-over-year revenue decline during the same period.

Industry observers note that furthermore, net losses have left it without a price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio. Still, according to analysts, this sales boost puts Take-Two on track for an estimated 27% yearly revenue gain in fiscal 2027 and a return to profitability. That recovery could make investors more willing to pay its 30 forward P/E ratio.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Unfortunately, game releases are one-off events, and such temporary sales bumps tend to run their course swiftly. Consequently, analysts believe the entertainment stock's revenue growth will slow to 6% in fiscal 2028, making the release unlikely to change the manufacturer's long-term value proposition.

In a recent development, in contrast, Anthropic's gains are on track to be more than one-time. Companies from nearly all sectors of the economy are scrambling to incorporate AI into their businesses. This has greatly benefited Claude, which has stood toe-to-toe with the competition from OpenAI's ChatGPT and Alphabet's (GOOGL -2.70%) (GOOG -2.59%) Google Gemini.

Industry observers note that its financials confirm its fast-growing popularity. The $33 billion it generated in the first nine months of 2026 far exceeded the $2.5 billion it reported during the same period in 2025.

Moreover, although it lost $104 billion during the first three quarters of 2026, $94 billion of that loss resulted from changes in the fair value of convertible notes. The operating losses were less than $1.3 billion of that total loss, suggesting it could turn an operating profit in the foreseeable future.

Additionally, even after giving back most of its IPO pop, its price-to-sales (P/S) ratio is around 45, far more than Take-Two's sales multiple of 6.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

In a recent development, for next year, analysts estimate 135% revenue growth. At current prices, that would likely reduce its sales multiple over time, potentially pushing Anthropic stock higher.

Between Take-Two and Anthropic, I recommend the latter.

Indeed, the GTA 6 release should put Take-Two on a firmer financial footing. Unfortunately, that sales bump is likely to be temporary, suggesting it cannot rely on one newly introduced game release to improve Take-Two's long-term value proposition.

It has been revealed that in contrast, Anthropic has benefited from tremendous AI demand, so much so that it will likely report triple-digit revenue growth for the foreseeable future. That probably means that even with an elevated valuation, it should deliver higher returns to investors over time.
