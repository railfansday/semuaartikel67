Arc Raiders finally reveals early 2026 roadmap, but we’re not getting a upcoming map for a while

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After weeks of waiting, Arc Raiders developer Embark has officially unveiled the first few months of the game’s 2026 roadmap. The blueprint is split into four months, covering January to April, collectively dubbed Escalation.

In a recent development, we’re expecting a content revision each month, with the majority of the biggest additions happening on the game’s existing maps. There is, however, at least one newly introduced map in the works, but it won’t be here until sometime in April.

While we wait for that, Arc Raiders is going to go through several events and other in-game changes, some more meaningful (and exciting) than others. January looks to be the lightest so far, which makes sense given that it’s just a week or so from ending.

January’s chapter is titled Headwinds, kicking off on January 27. This segment will see the addition of a latest matchmaking option for players above level 40 - at or near the game’s endgame. We’re also expecting a new Map Condition, and a new Player Project for everyone to contribute to.

Sources indicate that things start getting interesting in February, when Shrouded Sky will begin. This chapter brings three exciting additions to Arc Raiders. There will be a upcoming Map Condition, new Arc Threat, and the start of another Player Project.

In a recent development, interestingly, we’re going to see some sort of map enhancement during the month, but Embark didn’t share anymore details. The next Raider Deck will also deploy in February, alongside a new Expedition Window.

The Flashpoint chapter begins in March, when we’re expecting another upcoming Map Condition, one more new Arc Threat, as well as another Player Project. March’s most interesting addition is an update to everyone’s trusty rooster friend, Scrappy.

Finally, April is when the massive guns come out, because that’s when the first new map of 2026 will arrive in Arc Raiders. With it, comes a new large Arc, which is to be expected. A new Map Condition will debut then, too, likely starting off on the new map.

That’s pretty much it, but seeing as we’re just one day away from the start of the January stage, things are about to get exciting again in Arc Raiders.
