Pedro Neto has provided Xabi Alonso with a major selection dilemma for Chelsea’s next game, with the winger now at risk of missing the Premier League showdown against Liverpool.

The Blues' miserable form continued on Friday night, slumping to a 3-2 defeat away at Nottingham Forest.

An Igor Jesus volley in addition to a brace from Morgan Gibbs-White was enough to inflict a third straight defeat on Chelsea either side of the November international break.

While Emmanuel Emegha twice gave the visitors hope of rescuing at least a point with two goals off the bench, the Blues are now at risk of ending the weekend in the bottom half of the Premier League table.

A further negative from a nightmare trip to the City Ground was the yellow card shown to Neto in the 71st minute.

With that being his fourth booking in the Premier League this season, he is now walking the disciplinary tight-rope ahead of next Wednesday's London derby at home to Crystal Palace.

Rules state that players who pick up five yellow cards in the first 19 matches of the Premier League season are hit with a one-match ban.

According to recent reports, for Chelsea, the cut-off point in which suspensions will be handed out for a fifth yellow card is the home game against Newcastle on January 3, 2027. From there, players who are booked 10 times in the first 32 matches of the campaign are suspended for two games.

As a result, if Neto features against Palace and is booked, his punishment will be to sit out the visit of Liverpool to Stamford Bridge next Saturday.

To avoid a one-game Premier League suspension, the Portugal international has to watch his step in upcoming games against Palace, Liverpool, Manchester City, Aston Villa, Coventry, Ipswich and Newcastle.

Neto is already facing one ban in the upcoming year as he will be suspended for the first leg of Portugal’s Nations League quarter-final against the Netherlands after being sent-off in the win over Wales.
