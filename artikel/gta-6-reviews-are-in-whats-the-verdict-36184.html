GTA 6 reviews are in: Whats the verdict?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that A casual 13 years since its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here. But reviewers, who've already spent hours with the game, are actually allowed to talk about it.

On Wednesday, Rockstar Games released its wildly-anticipated game, with fans flocking to PlayStation 5 and Xbox consoles to hit the streets of the Florida-inspired city of Leonida. New Zealand fans might be the first in the world to get GTA 6 thanks to timezones, but reviewers have had the game for a while now, with time to properly digest its world. And now, with the embargo lifted, their thoughts are now live.

So, what's the verdict for GTA 6? Well, it's only the highest rated game of all time.

Here's a round-up of reviews for Rockstar's mega game.

"Exquisitely detailed, endlessly ripe for experimentation, and uncompromisingly engineered, GTA 6 has not stopped astonishing me since I started it. I regularly find myself in love with video games, but it’s extraordinarily rare that I find myself in complete awe of them. Put simply, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played." — Luke Reilly, IGN.

"Grand Theft Auto VI excels at just letting you go wherever the roads and waves may take you, be they shabby, seedy, or sensational. Its virtual experiences may lack touch or taste, but as a collection of richly drawn and intoxicatingly self-guided interactive adventures, it’s a video game I put above some far more expensive trips I’ve taken in real life. For seeking endless hedonism, something you can’t resist even if it kills you, where else would you go but Vice City?" — Jordan Minor, PCMag.

"Like its forebears, GTA 6 feels like two games — a core narrative about making a living through the underground in a rags-to-riches-to-rags-to-riches saga of a star-crossed couple, and a massive sandbox to do whatever you want." — David Lumb, CNET.

"With realism that defies belief, this vast game easily accommodates its trademark foulmouthed, OTT chaos – as well as new empathy for the fugitive couple at its centre." — Keza MacDonald, Guardian.

Sources indicate that "GTA 6 is a truly remarkable game. A masterpiece of open-world design that brings forward the best parts of all of Rockstar’s past games into one billion-dollar adventure that is unlike anything I’ve played before. It’s a game I’ll be playing for years, and I can’t wait to talk about it with others." — Zack Zwiezen, Kotaku.

"But where the dollars are most felt isn’t in the intricate skylines or the typically bombastic missions, which take liberal inspiration from decades of crime movies in the vein of Mann, Scorsese and Bay. It’s all impressively done and rendered with technological aplomb, but it isn’t anything we haven’t seen before. Instead, the game’s artistry is felt most in its Bonnie and Clyde duo." — Yussef Cole, New York Times.

"Expectations for the game are sky-high, but Grand Theft Auto VI is a masterpiece of game design. Rockstar has clearly put thought into every aspect, from character progression and money to the consequences of Jason and Lucia’s actions and the relationship they build together; the game pulls all of it off flawlessly." — Imani Jones, RPGamer.

It has been revealed that "Written, programmed and performed by a cast and crew of tens of thousands, “Grand Theft Auto VI″ is more than a typical video game, it is a great gathering of artforms and talent, an unruly, comprehensive cultural atlas of American life and manners. Its triumph lies in encounters I just described, where race, class, gender, money and attention shape our circumstances and what we decide to do about them." — Gene Park, Washington Post.

According to recent reports, "Jason and Lucia's relationship serves as the emotional engine of the story, and when paired with the game’s sandbox world, that’s where the magic happens. You see, GTA VI is actually a relationship simulator in disguise, and you get to turn Jason into the perfect boyfriend." — Zhiqing Wan, Retcon Magazine.

"This is, to me, the best illustration of what GTA 6 is: a game of staggering scope and scale obsessed with small details that could be lost in its vastness, a giant haystack full of needles that you’ll constantly find, creating delight in their improbable discovery." — Tamoor Hussain, GameSpot.

"Rockstar Games’ astonishing open-world adventure, the most impossibly grand and expensive video game that may ever exist, doesn’t just generate its morbid pleasure from cop-killing sprees and armed robberies; that comes from living in a reactive place governed by intricate rules that beg to be bent and busted." — Giovanni Colantonio, Polygon.

"Thirteen years later, Rockstar has crafted a worthy successor to GTA V and the new standard-bearer for open-world games," — Andrew Webster, The Verge.

Industry observers note that "The reimagined and expanded Vice City is still the grand, crude, satirical criminal playground you’d expect after 25 years of 3D Grand Theft Auto games. And that world is as full, detailed, technically impressive, and polished as you would expect after this latest instalment received a reported billion-dollar budget and roughly a decade of development work involving thousands of Rockstar staffers." — Kyle Orland, Ars Technica.

Industry observers note that few video games have cities this vast, this dense, this full of secrets and systems that build on one another to give you stories you’ll want to text your buddies about. Few games are so technically impressive, so close to photorealism when the sun is setting and the light hits the Vice City skyline just right. Few games are this consistently hilarious." — Jason Schreier, Bloomberg.

Sources indicate that "A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action – and a load more after that." — Chris Tapsell, Eurogamer.

Industry observers note that "At its core, GTA 6 is a pretty good game. There are large sections where it is an absolutely epic game, and large sections that are boring and too limited. However, after I pushed through the boring parts and it opened up enough to become a proper GTA title, it became worth it." — Alice Clarke, Screen Hub.

"GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation. Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality. There's a degree of sophistication to everything GTA 6 does, and there's no aspect of the series' gameplay that hasn't been improved over past instalments." — Josh West, Games Radar.
