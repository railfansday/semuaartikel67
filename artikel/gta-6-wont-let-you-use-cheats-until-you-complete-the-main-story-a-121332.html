Here's a twist: you can't use cheats in Grand Theft Auto 6 until you complete the entire game's main story.

In a recent development, that's what we discovered from our tests at least, after loading in saves at various points through the game to be sure, and it's also both a first for the series and a departure even from how developer Rockstar handled cheats in Red Dead Redemption 2. In that game,approximately half of the cheats were available from the off, and the other half required discovering specific newspapers from around the game's world to unlock.

In GTA 6, you'll again need to do some legwork. This time it appears that all of the game's cheats not only require you to finish the story, but also complete various tasks or challenges to unlock them.

The way it works is simple enough. Once you finish the main story, the "Cheats" tab - previously hidden - quietly appears at the far end of the Progress menu, accessible when you open the in-game map and scroll with the bumper buttons. From there, you can view various sub-sections of cheats, sorted by category, and a list of 10 or so cheats for each.

With each cheat there'll be a short explanation of what it does, and what's required to unlock it and, once unlocked, guidance on how to perform it. This is done via whipping out your phone, holding down the shoulder buttons and using various D-Pad inputs - much the same as GTA 5.

There's also another caveat to bear in mind: when you use a cheat, you'll see a notification warning you that the game can't be saved while it's active, and that Trophies and Achievements are also disabled until you end that session. This makes sense, once you understand the nature of GTA 6's story - there is a cheat for giving yourself a load of money, for instance; another departure from GTA 5 where that popular cheat was removed - but given you'll need certain amounts of cash at various points of the story, enabling you to shortcut that would disrupt the way the narrative unfolds.

We'll have a guide from yours truly to all the game's cheats live on Eurogamer in the very near future, as the game releases here in the UK. In the meantime, if you find yourself on the fence about this one, be sure to give our GTA 6 review a look. As I put it there, giving the game five stars: it is "the most believable and unbelievable video game world ever made".
