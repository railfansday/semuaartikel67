England produced a statement win over world champions Spain in Madrid in their Nations League decider to top Group A3.

The Three Lions had already confirmed their place in the quarter-finals in March with victory over Croatia earlier in this window.

But England have now given themselves the best chances of a favourable draw by beating Spain 3-2, while also proving they are one of the teams to beat.

Thomas Tuchel’s side will be glued to next Friday’s draw, with League A still to be completed across the international break.

But here’s everything you need to know about the next stages of the competition:.

The draw will take place at 11am GMT on Friday 20 November.

England will feature in the last eight of the Nations League 2026/27, with a two-legged tie to decide who will play in the semi-finals and final, both to be played across a ‘finals’ week.

According to recent reports, as well as the Nations League quarter-finals, the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs will be drawn, set to be held in March 2027. The final tournament is scheduled to take place across 9–13 June 2027.

England's potential opponents in the last eight of the Uefa Nations League, as things stand, are Belgium, Netherlands or Denmark.

Industry observers note that beating Spain ensured the Three Lions will face a second-placed team in their quarter-final; Belgium have finished second in their group, while Germany thrashed the Netherlands to condemn the Dutch to runners-up. Denmark need a point against Norway to secure second-place.

England topping the group also means they will play the second leg of their quarter-final at home.
