JetBlue Adds Newly introduced Destination in Latin America, Caribbean Expansion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

JetBlue is growing its presence in Central America and the Caribbean.

Industry observers note that for the first time ever, the carrier will serve the Honduran capital of Tegucigalpa. Starting Feb. 25, 2027, flights from Fort Lauderdale, Florida, will arrive at Palmerola International Airport in Comayagua, which serves Tegucigalpa.

Right now, JetBlue’s only destination in Honduras is San Pedro Sula, in the country’s northwest.

In a recent development, on Feb. 23, the carrier will launch service between Fort Lauderdale and George Town, Grand Cayman, in the Cayman Islands.

Both routes will operate daily, using a mix of Airbus A320 and A220 aircraft.

Industry observers note that jetBlue will also offer limited-time daily service from Fort Lauderdale to Port of Spain, Trinidad and Tobago, between Jan. 29 and Feb. 15. The connection will help travelers reach the islands during Carnival, the airline noted.

“We’re grateful for the way customers across South Florida continue to embrace JetBlue, and we’re committed to delivering even more of the destinations and travel options they’re looking for,” Daniel Shurz, JetBlue’s senior vice president of revenue, network, and enterprise planning, noted in a news release. “With new international service to Honduras and Grand Cayman, we’re strengthening Fort Lauderdale’s role as a key gateway to Latin America and the Caribbean, making it easier for customers to reconnect with loved ones, explore new places, and travel across our growing network.”.

In a recent development, american has prioritized Latin America as demand from U.S. markets, especially Miami, grows. The carrier just recently launched service to Barranquilla and Cali in Colombia and will add Barcelona, Venezuela, to its network in January.
