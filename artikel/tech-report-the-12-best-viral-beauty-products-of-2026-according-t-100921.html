I’ll be the first to tell you that half of the things were meh, but there were also several that completely blew team Cosmo away.

Sources indicate that I polled our beauty editors to pull together this list of the best viral beauty products of 2026 that are 100 percent worth your hard-earned cash. These innovative items wowed us, earned permanent spots in our vanities, and even changed our lives in one way or another (no joke). You may not be able to trust everything you see on TikTok, but you can have confidence in our top viral beauty picks. Keep reading to discover them all.

Sources indicate that one Cosmo editor says: “Few fragrances caught my—and the internet’s—attention quite like Cloud Reverie this year. The whole concept is incredible: Future Society wanted to bottle the scent of clouds, so they stuck a petri dish out of a plane to collect actual cloud microbes (!!), then viewed them under a microscope to create the most realistic cloud fragrance. The result is a soft, slightly powdery dream that swirls together fluffy vanilla, sparkling aldehydes, a touch of sugar and iris, and musk. This makes me want to smell like a cloud every day.”—MARY HONKUS, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY EDITOR.

I understand why it sold out multiple times, especially when new colors were released.”—MH.
