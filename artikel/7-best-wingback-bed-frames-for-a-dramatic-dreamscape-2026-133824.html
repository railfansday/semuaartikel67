Imagine if you could translate that cocooning feeling you get from your favorite reading chairs into your bedroom. Well, you can, thanks to wingback bed frames. The headboard shape has the same curved ‘wings’ as those high-back chairs. This time, they’re tucking you into bed rather than into your next read. The wing style dates back to the 1600s, serving the purpose of blocking drafts and providing extra warmth in those pre-central heating days.

In a recent development, when crafting your cozy bedroom, there are a few things to consider. One is upholstery. For people aiming to do everything from bed (we don’t judge), or those with little ones and pets, perhaps consider a performance fabric that can easily be treated with an upholstery cleaner, should sticky fingers or coffee stains leave their mark. Next is legs. Most wingback beds have a platform base, which helps to create a grounded look. But if you already have plenty of floor-sitting furniture, a slim leg could aid in giving your bedroom the levity it needs. Regardless, we’ve done the heavy lifting for you to spotlight seven of the best wingback bed frames for a dramatic bedscape.

Industry observers note that for those looking to go down the traditional route of a streamlined, upholstered wingback bed, Quince has you covered. The nubby bouclé adds a sense of warmth to your bedroom, while the tapered ‘wings’ cocoon you as you drift off to sleep. We particularly love the taller headboard, 42 inches to be exact, that shows off the curved edges no matter how plush your mattress may be. Quince offers white glove delivery for free, meaning that no assembly is required. A quick note, ivory upholstery may seem intimidating for those who prefer to drink their coffee in bed, but the performance fabric can easily be spot cleaned should the need arise.

Most wingback styles carry a sense of opulence and grandeur, but modern-minimalists can also embrace this look. The Emmy Bed from DWR has the signature “wings,” without the height of most tall wingback headboards. Even in the tall version, this frame is still shorter in height than any other competitor mentioned in this list. There are more than 50 upholstery fabrics up for grabs, but we’re partial to the linen-blend spruce colorway with slim, walnut-finished legs for an earthy, serene-looking sleepscape. Place two similarly stained wooden nightstands on either side to lay the foundation for your room.

In a recent development, since a small bedroom only offers so much square footage to work with, that’s where storage beds come in handy. Saatva’s Cassis bed frame has a hydraulic lift mechanism to reveal a storage cavity built-into the platform bed frame. Saatva’s bed frames are sturdy McGregor’s experience, so you won’t have to worry about creaking, shifting, or fraying upholstery here. The high-quality build is designed to last years. Choose between a linen upholstery for a more relaxed look, or a velvet for a moodier vibe.

Recent findings suggest that your bed frame is among the largest pieces you’ll welcome into your space, and the dimensions could compromise how you move through your bedroom. Interior Define’s bespoke offerings are great for this reason, as you can choose from dozens of upholstery options, two sizes (queen or king), and two headboard heights (45 or 55 inches). If you’re tight on storage space, you can also add extra drawers to your frame. “I loved the idea of having built-in drawers and bonus storage in addition to the coziness of a fully upholstered bed frame,” says Melissa Maria, senior visuals editor, who selected the mink colorway. “My bedroom at the moment could be described as eclectic. The bed frame works well with my other existing pieces, as it’s a classic shape and a neutral color.”.

In a recent development, castlery remains a top choice among our editors, especially for those of us furnishing contemporary spaces. Lisa Aiken, senior vice president of shopping at Condé Nast, added this bed frame to match the scale of other large items in her modern-minimalist, midcentury home. “Aesthetically, the wingback headboard is very eye-catching, providing a visually interesting form,” she says. “Practically, the storage that this bed frame provides is substantial, especially the large full-width drawer at the foot of the bed.”.

After moving from a midcentury-modern Florida home to a historic home in Ohio, contributor Yelena Moroz Alpert was searching for a substantial bed frame to coordinate with her newly introduced digs. She was drawn to the dusty lilac upholstery, and the subtle pull-out storage drawer to manage her self-described quilt addiction. “When I dress this bed to the nines with all my linens, it feels like I've snuck away to a Parisian boutique hotel,” Alpert indicates. “The shelter headboard creates a little alcove, which is lovely when I read in bed, and it feels like an embrace when I sleep.”.
