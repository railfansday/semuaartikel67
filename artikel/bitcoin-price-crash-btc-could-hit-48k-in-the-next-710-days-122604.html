Bitcoin Price Crash: BTC Could Hit $48K in the Next 7–10 Days

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that bitcoin could fall to $48,000 within the next seven to ten days if it fails to recover above key resistance levels. BTC price recently rebounded from around $57,000 to $63,000–$64,000 following a rally in NVIDIA shares after positive news surrounding CEO Jensen Huang. The recovery also lifted the Nasdaq, S&P 500, and broader crypto market.

However, BTC failed to break above $65,000–$66,000 and has started losing momentum. This is a warning that the market could face another round of selling.

According to recent reports, bitcoin recently broke below a long-term trendline that had supported its price since 2022. Although buyers pushed the price back above $58,000, they failed to reclaim the broken trendline.

Recent findings suggest that crypto analyst crypto banter, said the failure to move above $65,000–$66,000 is a major concern. A recovery above this range could signal a change in market direction. Otherwise, Bitcoin could continue falling.

The recent weekly candle also showed that buyers could not push prices higher despite attempts to recover from the decline.

The analyst examined previous Bitcoin sell-offs and found that the cryptocurrency often recorded another low within seven to eight days after its first recovery attempt.

Industry observers note that for example, BTC price previously fell from the $80,000 range to around $59,000 before recovering. However, it reached another low about eight days later.

Based on these past movements, the analyst believes Bitcoin could face another decline within the next seven to ten days if it fails to break above resistance.

A recovery that forms a higher low between $55,000 and $60,000 could instead indicate that buyers are returning.

The $48,000 target comes from the analyst’s long-term price analysis using historical trends and Fibonacci levels.

In a recent development, bitcoin’s six-month chart places an vital Fibonacci level near $48,000. The analyst also found that this price area aligns with historical support levels from previous market cycles.

Industry observers note that the Elliott Wave analysis also points to a possible fifth downward move. By comparing the size of previous declines, the analyst estimated that Bitcoin could fall toward $48,000 if the current selling continues.

However, this remains a possible scenario, not a confirmed price target.

The analyst also expects Bitcoin’s price movements to become more volatile, with a wider trading range between the upper-$40,000s and $100,000.

Despite the short-term bearish outlook, the analyst believes a recovery remains possible after the current decline.

Recent findings suggest that for now, traders are watching whether Bitcoin can break above $65,000–$66,000 or falls toward $55,000–$60,000. A failure to hold these lower levels could bring the $48,000 target into focus.
