‘NCIS: Upcoming York’ Officially Gets a Special Sunday Episode Release

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

NCIS has enjoyed a resurgent year on CBS, and part of that has to do with the return of Sam Hanna as a regular presence on Tuesday nights. The former NCIS: Los Angeles Senior Special Agent and partner of Grisha Callen (Chris O'Donnell), played memorably by LL COOL J, finally has his own show again after NCIS: New York came together quickly earlier this year. So far, the results speak for themselves, too. The latest procedural spin-off debuted to a strong 5.62 million average viewers earlier this fall, trailing only the flagship series as the most-watched show on Tuesday night that week, and it has since held steady with an average of 5.18 million viewers over the first three episodes. As part of a three-show night, the franchise's visit to the Significant Apple is fitting right in.

Industry observers note that with just two episodes left to go until the holiday break, Sam has reestablished himself well in his hometown. Latest York brings him back to the titular city after years of special operations on a personal mission to investigate the death of a friend and Navy reservist, only to link up with the local NCIS team and fit right in with the crew. Rolling with a new partner in the roguish Nick Schaeffer (Scott Caan) under the leadership of Assistant Special Agent-in-Charge Robyn Wells (Jennifer Beals), he's been busy solving crimes throughout familiar streets and neighborhoods involving everyone from corrupt politicians to jewelry store thieves. Next year, when the series returns, though, he'll be taking naval crime investigation to a new night for a special occasion.

It has been revealed that CBS unveiled its midseason premiere dates earlier this week and, with it, confirmed that NCIS: New York would be getting a special Sunday night broadcast at the end of January. Right after the network hosts the AFC Championship Game, a new episode will debut, giving Sam and the team a massive lead-in for one night only. Otherwise, episodes will continue to air in the normal 9 p.m. ET/PT time slot on Tuesdays. After wrapping up for the fall on December 8, the entire NCIS slate is slated to pick back up starting on January 12.

Industry observers note that along with the rest of the NCIS slate, Latest York is also about to go on break to celebrate Thanksgiving. Next week, no new episodes of the procedural franchise will air, with programming returning as usual on December 1. When it does return, viewers will be treated to "The River," which revolves around the disappearance of a Marine sergeant in New Jersey. The team will be called in to investigate why he suddenly vanished, though it may have something to do with his decision to flee Quantico the night before a high-stakes mission. Expect the whole team to get involved, including Addy Ross (Jacqueline Byers), Wyatt Sellers (Shane Harper), and Sean Sullivan (Devin Druid).

NCIS: New York airs Tuesday nights on CBS at 9 p.m. and will air its special Sunday night broadcast after the AFC Championship on January 31. Stay tuned here at Collider for more updates on the future of the latest NCIS spin-off.
