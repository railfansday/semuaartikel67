Tech Report: Amazon Adds a Newly introduced Prime Member Perk Ahead of Black Friday

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you’re already thinking about meals post-Thanksgiving, Amazon has a fresh option.

In a recent development, prime members now have Uber Eats with no delivery fee as a meal delivery perk. Plus, you’ll get $20 off the first two Uber Eats-eligible orders of $25 or more for the first 14 days. You can expect some Amazon-exclusive offers, and if you have a Prime Visa credit card, you’ll earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders until Dec. 5 with a Prime membership.

It has been revealed that the Amazon and Uber Eats partnership is just in time for the holidays if you want a convenient dinner option while you shop Black Friday deals and sales. But heads up: You won’t get the limited-time deal and free delivery if you order through the Uber Eats app. In order to get free delivery and $20 off your first two orders, go to the Uber Eats tab at the top of your Amazon app or Uber Eats on Amazon’s website. Place your order as usual with a participating restaurant, but make sure the $20 discount applies at checkout.

I tried out the fresh perk and bought myself dinner. Aside from the meal, tip, a nearly $10 service fee and tax all added up to the cost of another meal — which is worth paying attention to after your first two orders.

Recent findings suggest that the newly introduced Uber Eats perk is now available to all Prime members, including those with Prime Access and Prime for Young Adults. And don’t forget, our Amazon Prime membership still offers free same-day delivery on perishable grocery orders over $25 when you order from Amazon Grocery.

Previously, your Prime subscription included a GrubHub Plus membership at no additional cost. GrubHub Plus included no delivery fees, lower service fees and 5% back on pickup orders. An Amazon spokesperson said in an email to CNET that the GrubHub and Amazon partnership ended. There’s no telling how long Amazon will partner with Uber Eats.

Recent findings suggest that “We continuously evaluate our third-party offers to ensure they provide the best possible value for Prime members,” the spokesperson mentioned. “As part of that ongoing evaluation, we identified an opportunity to deliver an even better restaurant delivery experience.”.

Recent findings suggest that however, a GrubHub spokesperson stated in an email to CNET that if you have a Prime membership and activated GrubHub Plus, you’ll have the membership until Dec. 31, 2027.
