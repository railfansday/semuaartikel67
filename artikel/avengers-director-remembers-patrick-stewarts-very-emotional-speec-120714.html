Industry observers note that during the last day of principal photography on Avengers: Doomsday, revered actor Patrick Stewart, who reprised his role of Professor Charles Xavier for the film, stood up and gave a moving speech to the cast and crew. It would be his last day on a film set anywhere, according to his colleagues.

“We didn’t know necessarily it would be his last day on a set, but he was sort of indicating to us that he thought it was,” Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige remarked in an interview with Entertainment Weekly.

Anthony and Joe Russo, who helmed the last two Avengers films, 2018’s Infinity War and 2019’s Endgame, also felt the impact of the celebrated actor’s words that day. “I think it was in the back of all of our heads, we may never see him on set again,” Anthony told the outlet, calling the atmosphere that day as “very emotional.”.

In a recent development, when addressing those he had worked side-by-side with, Stewart highlighted his character Xavier, whose empathy for others offered a source of comfort and security. “It was clear that he was struggling with his health, and he worked so hard to deliver a performance and a presence — for us as directors, for his other actors, for the crew,” Anthony remarked. “It was someone who was really being challenged in a very deep way on a health level, showing up and doing that. For him to have muscled through and delivered the performance that he did in that movie, and the experience that he delivered for all of us as collaborators, it made it intensely emotional.”.

In a recent development, feige confirmed to EW that he and the Russos are working on planning a tribute to Stewart. “We’re long done filming that movie, and he did an amazing job for us,” he stated. “We’re looking now at how we can put something together that honors and respects our friend.”.

Stewart died at age 86 on Oct. 28 in his Los Angeles home. His prolific career spanned decades with work across more than five dozen films and over 50 television appearances, from X-Men and Star Trek to Dune and American Dad.
