With a trio of front men who are struggling to put the ball into the net, West Ham face an uphill battle to end their five game run without a win with a trip to Fratton Park tomorrow night.

Joel Piroe remains the best of a poor bunch of Hammers front men with four West Ham goals to his name – whilst Taty Castellanos and Pablo have a solitary strike each.

Recent findings suggest that matt’s article earlier highlighted the woeful statistics of shots per game and goals resulting – the Hammers simply have to do better. Reports suggest Castellanos will be on his way in January to accommodate a replacement.

Sources indicate that closer to home, West Ham already have one youngster who is making waves: Dan Cummings, just loaned out to Aldershot in the fifth tier, has already broken his duck with a strike on debut against Scunthorpe United. Scott Parker has already proven with Finn Herrick’s selection that he’s not afraid to select young talent where appropriate.

20 year-old Cummings had been on the pitch just a few minutes into his debut. According to West Ham’s own web site, he:.

“Chased down a long ball on the right edge of the Scunthorpe penalty area before beating his defender, cutting inside and sliding the ball under goalkeeper Maison Campbell with his right foot.

The goal came just six minutes after Cummings had been introduced from the substitutes’ bench“.

If Cummings carries on in that vein he’ll soon be on Scott Parker’s radar: As a third or fourth striker option who is young, fast, enthusiastic, match fit and prepared to chase everything, what is the worst that could happen?

Hitting the target at the moment appears to be a very rare skill at West Ham United: A couple of months’ more with half a dozen goals and Cummings could well have the opportunity of making a name for himself in front of a significantly larger crowd than Aldershot’s 2,252.
