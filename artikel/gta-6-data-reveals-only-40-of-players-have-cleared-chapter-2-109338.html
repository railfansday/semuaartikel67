If your feed is full of clips and streams from people who’ve already gone deep into Grand Theft Auto 6, here’s some reassurance: most players aren’t as far along as you probably think.

Recent findings suggest that according to PSNProfiles, only 66.22% of Grand Theft Auto 6 players on PlayStation 5 have earned the trophy for completing Chapter 1, which takes place in the Leonida Keys. The numbers on Xbox are similar, with 56% of players having beaten that part of the game, according to TrueAchievements. This completion percentage feels particularly significant because the first chapter of the game is a substantial departure from classic GTA, with Jason living out a relatively low-key existence interrupted by the occasional shootout with backwoods drug dealers. Still, it seems a solid chunk of players are more than happy fishing with Cal for now.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, only 40.55% of players have cleared Chapter 2 on PS5, and 31% on Xbox. Again, considering that Chapter 3 is arguably where the game becomes more of a traditional Grand Theft Auto entry, as you officially enter Vice City, it’s a bit of a surprise that the numbers are so low, even if it takes close to 20 hours to get there.

As for the rest of the game, few have gotten past Chapter 3: 6.45% have cleared that chapter on PS5, compared to 4% on Xbox; 1.26% have cleared Chapter 4 on PS5 against 1% on Xbox; and a tiny 0.88% have cleared the final mission on PlayStation against fewer than 1% on Xbox.

Recent findings suggest that although it’s tempting to assume these low completion numbers are simply because the game came out so recently, Sony released player engagement numbers that made it clear most have already put a ton of time into GTA 6. Mat Piscatella, senior director at market research firm Circana, revealed that players spent an average of more than 8 hours playing the game on launch day. As of Nov. 23, PS5 players had already spent an average of 17.4 hours playing. While users are playing a lot and sticking with the game, most are taking their time. Rockstar previously noted the main story takes around 50 hours to finish.

Shortly after GTA 6 was released, publisher Take-Two Interactive announced that the game had set a “fresh record for entertainment across any medium,” making it the biggest launch in entertainment history. It sold 32.5 million copies during its pre-release period, generating $3.79 billion in revenue. In the previously mentioned post that announced PlayStation engagement numbers, Sony also remarked GTA 6 was the biggest launch in PlayStation history and that 39 million people had played it on the PS5 as of Monday.

According to recent reports, as for its critical reception, the game has a staggering Metacritic score of 99, tying it with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — the Nintendo 64 version — for the highest-rated game of all time. By every metric, GTA 6 has been a cultural event.
