Catherine Zeta-Jones’s Daughter Carys Borrows Her Mom’s Lacy Gucci Top From the ’90s

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

When your mom is Catherine Zeta-Jones, there is hardly a ’90s or Y2K gem you can’t get your hands on. From turn-of-the-century Fendi baguettes to red-carpet gowns (that crimson Versace dress from the 2004 Oscars particularly comes to mind), the Academy Award-winning actor embraced all the best of the decades’ trends. And now, her daughter gets to as well.

Yesterday, Carys Douglas, the 23-year-old first-born child of Zeta-Jones and husband Michael Douglas, went through her mother’s archives for a night-out look perfectly suitable for a Gucci x Moda Operandi cocktail event in Upcoming York City. Her choice was a sheer long-sleeve lace top from the Italian fashion house, with an elongated, deep U neckline secured solely by a single clasp. The piece, with its raw hems, was a familiar pull—last seen on the Chicago star at the 1996 premiere of The Long Kiss Goodnight in Los Angeles.

According to recent reports, at the ’90s event, Zeta-Jones layered the top over a little black cami and paired it with slim trousers in the same sheer lace. She added a large cross necklace, chunky rings, and a pair of open-toe heels, and slung a large bag over her shoulder.

Industry observers note that three decades later, her daughter put her own spin on the piece, wearing it with a little black miniskirt and semi-sheer black tights. Douglas took a cue from her movie-star mom’s glam, wearing her hair down in slept-in waves and focusing her makeup on the lips, adding a pop of color. She grabbed a quilted Gucci bag to finish things off.

It has been revealed that this was not the first time Douglas borrowed something from her mom’s closet. In 2024, she celebrated her 21st birthday in a pink Emanuel Ungaro slip previously seen on Zeta-Jones at the 1999 MTV Movie Awards. After posting the look on Instagram, her famous mom lovingly commented: “You deserve it sweetheart.”.
