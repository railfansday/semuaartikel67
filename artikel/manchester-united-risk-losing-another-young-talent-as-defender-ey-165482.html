Manchester United could be facing another unwanted academy departure, with Ayden Heaven open to leaving Old Trafford on loan when the January transfer window opens.

The 20-year-old defender is concerned about his lack of regular football under Mauricio Pochettino and believes a temporary move could be the best option for his development, as reported by the BBC.

Ayden Heaven was an unused substitute during United’s 4-0 defeat against Liverpool on Sunday as Manchester United battle near the bottom of the Premier League table.

The England Under-20 international has already worked under three different managers since joining United from Arsenal in February 2025 in a much hyped move. He enjoyed a period of regular football under Ruben Amorim but lost his place under Michael Carrick, making occasional appearances.

He has made eight appearances this season including five in the Premier League but remains uncertain about his role under the upcoming manager.

It has been revealed that A loan move could make sense for Ayden Heaven if Pochettino cannot offer him regular minutes. However, Manchester United should be careful about allowing another promising young defender to leave permanently.

According to recent reports, united have lost a number of young prospects lately over lack of time. James Garner is a prime example. He was unable to make his way into the team before impressing away from Old Trafford with Everton.

Manchester United need to keep Ayden Heaven at the club especially with the situation surrounding the future and fitness concerns of various players. Lisandro Martinez has been linked with a potential departure, while De Ligt is a major injury concern. Harry Maguire is also approaching his contract and is an ageing option now. United still need to find some stability in their backline.

According to recent reports, heaven is contracted until 2029, with the club holding an option to extend his deal by another year. There is little reason to rush into a permanent sale when he still has considerable time to develop.

United’s immediate priority may be avoiding relegation and Pochettino understandably needs experienced players for that fight. But the club also have to think beyond the current crisis.

Losing another young defender because of limited opportunities would be a poor outcome, especially when Heaven could yet have an important role to play at Old Trafford.
