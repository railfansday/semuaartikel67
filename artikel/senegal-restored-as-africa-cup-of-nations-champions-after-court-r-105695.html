Senegal have been reinstated as the champions of Africa, and their controversial 1-0 victory against Morocco confirmed in the record books, after the scandal over January’s Africa Cup of Nations final was resolved in a Swiss courtroom.

Industry observers note that the court of arbitration for sport (Cas) ruled that a decision to award the 2025 Afcon title to Morocco on appeal should be set aside. Senegal celebrated with a social media post proclaiming the team to have won the title twice: first on the field and then at Cas. Morocco, who are due to co-host the World Cup in 2030, declared their intention to “pursue all legal avenues” to challenge the ruling.
