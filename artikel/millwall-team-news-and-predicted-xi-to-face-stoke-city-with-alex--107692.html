Millwall have struggled to replicate the form of last term which saw them finish in 3rd place. They at the moment languish in 16th place following three consecutive Championship defeats.

Injuries have plagued the Lions’ early-season endeavours, and a run of two victories in 10 outings, coming against lowly Preston North End and Derby County, has left supporters debating whether Alex Neil is the right man to take them forward.

Industry observers note that there is no shame in falling to Birmingham City and West Brom, despite poor performances against the two promotion contenders. However, the Lions’ loss to QPR escalated the cause for concern when they were played off the park, not helped by Zak Sturge’s early dismissal.

Recent findings suggest that stoke City meanwhile are 2nd in the table after four consecutive wins. They defeated Birmingham City on Saturday despite playing much of the second half with 10 men after Luca Bombino’s sending off.

Sturge will serve his one-game suspension against Stoke City after denying a clear goal-scoring opportunity by pulling down QPR forward Paul Smyth.

According to recent reports, the absences of Millwall duo Ryan Leonard and Tristan Crama have been felt in the backline, with the latter struggling with ongoing ankle problems while Leonard has been sidelined with a knee injury. Both are expected to make a return at the start of December.

A similar return timeframe is in the pipeline for Filip Marschall, who suffered an ankle ligament injury in their draw to Lincoln City in October.

Sources indicate that mathis Servais was among a number of Millwall’s summer arrivals who generated considerable excitement upon arrival, signing from Belgian outfit KV Mechelen. He has played just nine minutes of Championship football due to a hamstring injury and remains out here.

Jensen; Sykes, Taylor, Cooper, Doughty; De Norre, Metcalfe; Neghli, Arconte, Lisbie; Ivanovic.

Jensen started his first game in goal for the first time in two months against QPR, and the injury to Marschall means he has hold of the number one shirt ahead of Max Crocombe.

Caleb Taylor and Jake Cooper have been Neil’s go-to centre-back pairing this term, starting in all 16 Championship matches, and will likely continue their partnership with Mark Sykes set to start at right-back. The red card to Sturge means the left-back role is up for grabs, one which Alfie Doughty could fill against his former side with Jordi Osei-Tutu also another option.

Casper de Norre was withdrawn at half-time against QPR but should be fit to start alongside Jenson Metcalfe.

Promising wideman Kyrell Lisbie has been deployed on both flanks of the attack this term and will likely start on the left, with Camiel Neghli occupying the right as he looks to rediscover the form he revealed last season.

Kyrell Lisbie’s Championship stats.

Yet to score since drawing with Middlesbrough in September, Tairyk Arconte will look to rediscover his finishing touch as the Guadeloupe international looks set to line up in an advanced midfield role.

Sources indicate that the responsibility of leading the line has fallen to Lyndon Dykes this term, but he has been part of a misfiring Millwall attack. As such, Serbian striker Mihailo Ivanovic could come in his place for only his second league start of the season.

Stoke City vs Millwall kicks off at 20:00 at the Bet365 Stadium and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Football.
