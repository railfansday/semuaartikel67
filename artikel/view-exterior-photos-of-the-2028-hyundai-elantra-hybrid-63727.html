The new Hyundai Elantra lineup will again include a hybrid variant, and the next-gen version pictured here will be more powerful than before.

The hybrid version of the new Elantra will debut for the 2028 model year, but it will look very similar to its nonhybrid sibling.

Its updated gas-electric powertrain will pair a gas-fed 1.6-liter four-cylinder with electric motors that combine for 155 horsepower (up 16).

The new Elantra Hybrid is expected to earn up to 53 mpg combined, per Hyundai’s estimate.

With its new bodywork, every Elantra boasts an assertive appearance that’s highlighted by wide haunches.

Like the new nonhybrid Elantra, the hybrid compact sedan’s wheelbase has been stretched by 1.2 inches.

While we’re still waiting to find out how much the 2028 Hyundai Elantra Hybrid will cost, we expect it to land somewhere between $25K–$30K.
