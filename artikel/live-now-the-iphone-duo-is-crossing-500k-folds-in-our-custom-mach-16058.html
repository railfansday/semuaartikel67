📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As we enter our fourth week of folding Apple’s iPhone Duo in our custom Fold-A-Phone machine, the $1,999 foldable phone is about to pass half a million folds during today’s 1 p.m. ET livestream. While we began noticing the first signs of wear on the hinge at the 451,000-fold mark on Friday, the phone is still able to open and close.

Sources indicate that you can watch as we cross this milestone live on our YouTube channel, embedded above. At our CNET Labs location in Louisville, Kentucky, this robust custom-built machine has been opening and closing the foldable phone around 20,000 times per day to simulate years of wear and tear on the hinge.

The livestream isn’t just for watching a phone open and close — you’ll also be able to chat with CNET editors who will be available to answer your iPhone Duo questions live in the chat.

Will Apple’s first foldable live up to the likes of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold? Will its neat opening animation remain slick after half a million folds? Join us and find out.
