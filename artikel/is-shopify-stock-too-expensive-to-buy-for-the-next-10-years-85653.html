Sources indicate that shopify (SHOP -6.25%) is a fast-growing e-commerce powerhouse with tons of opportunity ahead. It looks like a fantastic opportunity for investors in every way except one, but that one is quite important: It has an extremely rich price tag.

Recent findings suggest that shopify has become a major player in e-commerce. It doesn't rank with the top e-commerce retailers because it doesn't sell anything online; it sells services, from full websites to point-of-sale devices for omnichannel clients, for e-commerce retailers.

But its gross merchandise volume, or the total sales of its clients, was $119 billion in the third quarter, which is more than Amazon's (AMZN -4.10%) online and third-party sales of $116 billion.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Revenue increased 32% year over year in the third quarter, impressive in this sticky-inflation environment, and it reported a 22% cash flow margin.

Shopify is a great tech giant, but it's expensive on just about every valuation metric.

Recent findings suggest that that makes me hesitant to recommend it as a strong buy. You might imagine that in 10 years it's bound to reward shareholders, but that's not necessarily the case.

Recent findings suggest that consider PayPal Holdings (PYPL -5.46%), a Shopify competitor in certain services. Ten years ago, it was the pioneer in digital payments, but it has gained a grand total of 53% over the past decade after losing ground.

I think Shopify is a great stock to hold for the next 10 years, but I'd wait for a better entry point.
