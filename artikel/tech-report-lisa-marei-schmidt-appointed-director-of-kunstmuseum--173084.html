Lisa Marei Schmidt, the director of the Brücke-Museum in Berlin, is leaving her post to take over as director of the Kunstmuseum Bern and Zentrum Paul Klee in Bern, Switzerland, starting August 1, 2027. She will also chair the management board of both institutions and be responsible for their artistic direction. The Renzo Piano-designed Zentrum Paul Klee holds the world’s largest collection of works by Klee, while the Kunstmuseum Bern is Switzerland’s oldest fine art museum.

Schmidt has been at the Brücke-Museum since 2017 and has orchestrated many exhibitions, including Escape into Art? The Brücke Painters in the Nazi Period (2019), Whose Expression? The Brücke Artists and Colonialism (2021–22), and Confiscated and Forgotten: The Female Artists Targeted by the Nazi ‘Degenerate Art’ Campaign, which opens April 30 to July 25, 2027. The Berlin museum is presently exhibiting Swedish Modernism until February 28, 2027, with a focus on the artist groups De unga (The Young Ones), De åtta (The Eight) and the Gothenburg Colorists. “To me, museums are places where art and people meet. They provide space for encounters, new perspectives and dialogue across society,” mentioned Schmidt, in a statement on Thursday. “I very much look forward to getting to know the two remarkable institutions, the teams and the people in the City and Canton of Bern. Sources indicate that schmidt was chosen out of 97 candidates who applied for the position. Jonathan Gimmel, president of the umbrella foundation of Kunstmuseum Bern – Zentrum Paul Klee, noted in a statement that Schmidt brings “remarkable professional expertise, international experience and a deep understanding of people” as she shapes the next “development phase of our museums.”.

Before joining Brücke-Museum, Schmidt was a research trainee at Kunstsammlung Nordrhein-Westfalen and a curator at the Hamburger Bahnhof from 2012 to 2017. She studied art history and modern German literature in Marburg, Amsterdam, and Berlin before graduating from the Royal College of Art.
