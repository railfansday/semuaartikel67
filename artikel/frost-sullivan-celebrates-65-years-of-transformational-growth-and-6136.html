Recent findings suggest that SINGAPORE, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Frost & Sullivan, the Transformational Growth Organization, will host an exclusive 65th Anniversary and 2026 Best Practices Recognition Gala Dinner on 18 November 2026 at Hilton Singapore Orchard, bringing together distinguished business leaders, industry innovators, and key stakeholders to celebrate a milestone in the company's history and spotlight organisations demonstrating excellence in innovation, leadership, and transformational growth.
