BYD is preparing to launch a new flagship MPV soon after previewing the large people mover for the first time.

We knew it was coming soon after images of the camouflaged multi-purpose vehicle (MPV) began flooding the internet last month.

Zhang Zhuo, general manager of BYD’s Ocean sales division, confirmed the new flagship MPV is coming soon.

“A latest creation for the times, born for the family,” Zhuo said after posting a preview of it for the first time on his Weibo account on Friday.

According to recent reports, the new flagship Ocean MPV is “coming soon,” according to Zhuo, but he didn’t reveal a specific debut date. He previously stated the firm expects to launch its first MPV under the Ocean lineup by the end of 2026, so local reports suggest it will make an official debut next week at the 2026 Guangzhou Auto show, which kicks off on November 27.

Although it’s blurred, the preview shows a similar shape and size to the recent spy images, with a roof-mounted LiDAR and traditional pull-style door handles.

It doesn’t appear to look much like the futuristic Ocean-V concept BYD unveiled at the 2026 Beijing Auto Show earlier this year, a preview of its first MPV under the Ocean series.

Sources indicate that the concept featured a sliding center display screen and two different modes: Camping and Movie. In camping mode, the seats either fold flat to create a bed or swivel to face each other.

In Movie mode, the vehicle transforms into a private mobile theater with a large display screen that flips out of the dashboard.

While it may not have some of the more extravagant elements from the concept, the Ocean MPV is expected to be “flagship-worthy,” if you will, with BYD’s latest tech and other premium features like zero-gravity seats and plenty of digital screens.

The Ocean-V’s innovations “will gradually make their way into mass-production vehicles,” BYD remarked earlier this year.

BYD has yet to announce any details, but the flagship Ocean MPV is expected to be about the size of the Xia, a large plug-in hybrid minivan sold under its Dynasty lineup.

The BYD Xia measures 5,145 mm long, 1,970 mm wide, and 1,805 mm tall, with a 3,045 mm wheelbase.

Like the Xia, the Ocean MPV is expected to use BYD’s latest-generation DM-i plug-in hybrid system with a 1.5L engine and a 200 kW electric motor.

Recent findings suggest that the Xia starts at 249,800 yuan ($37,500) in China. It’s available with two BYD Blade LFP battery packs: 20.4 kWh and 36.6 kWh, providing pure electric CLTC ranges of 100 km (62 miles) and 218 km (135 miles). On a full tank of gas and full charge, the plug-in people mover offers a combined CLTC range of 1,163 km (722 miles).

BYD also sells the flagship Denza D9 MPV under its luxury sub-brand. With the Denza D9 starting above 300,000 yuan and the Xia at around 250,000 yuan, the Ocean MPV is expected to fill the gap.

Zhuo mentioned to stay tuned for more coming soon, so check back for updates.
