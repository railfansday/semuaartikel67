Virgil van Dijk is unsure where he will be playing his football next season, with his Liverpool contract due to expire in the summer.

The Dutchman, 35, has plied his trade at Anfield since 2018 and is now a two-time Premier League champion.

Industry observers note that he will be out of contract in June, though, and has revealed in a new interview with Sky Sports that it remains unclear whether he will extend his stay. He is, however, clear that he expects to continue his playing career for several more years.

In a recent development, per reports from BBC Sport, talks regarding a fresh deal are underway but an agreement is not close, and no resolution is expected before the new year. From January, Van Dijk will be free to sign pre-contract agreements with clubs outside England.

“The main thing for me is that I feel good and I'm looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion,” said Van Dijk.

“Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see.

“At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I'm going to do next season, it's untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I've got no idea either.”.

He continued, explaining that he will remain fully focused on on-pitch matters as he awaits clarity on his contract situation.

Recent findings suggest that “But I'm relaxed with it because if you know me, I only focus on what I can control and that's at the moment the Manchester United game. Then we'll focus to the Champions League and whatever happens in the new year coming, we'll see then.

"But it's been an incredible journey, almost 10 years this summer and it's been a massive part of my life and I'll forever be grateful for anything that happened over those years.”.

Sources indicate that he added, matter-of-factly: “If it's getting extended, we'll see then. If not, then I'm going to say goodbyes in the summer.”.
