Opinion: Richard Hughes' bargain deal has saved Liverpool's season

Liverpool finished the 2025-26 season rather poorly.

They were so bad that Arne Slot was actually relieved of his duties.

Admittedly, this came as a bit of a surprise to most people as fans thought that Fenway Sports Group were going to try and stick with the Dutchman for at least one more season.

Obviously, that didn't end up happening and in the end, Andoni Iraola took over and is currently leading the Reds to a fourth-place finish.

Their performances may have improved but, that doesn't mean that they should settle for fourth, especially since they spent over £150 million on fresh players in the summer.

Despite this, it's good that things seem to be going well with one of Richard Hughes' last deals potentially saving Liverpool's campaign.

Jeremy Jacquet was a bargain buy from Richard Hughes.

When the £55 million fee for Jeremy Jacquet was first revealed, most people thought that Liverpool massively overspent on him.

The centre-back is only 20 years old and has had no experience of football outside of Ligue 1.

He was also playing for a Rennes team which weren't seen as one of France's best which led many to believe that he was just outperforming his teammates, rather than actually playing well.

However, since coming into Liverpool's starting XI, Jacquet has changed the minds of the doubters and has quickly entered the conversation as one of the Premier League's best-performing defenders.

The Frenchman has played an awful lot of football for Liverpool this term and has had a couple of different partners in the centre of defence.

But, every challenge he has faced has led to his skills improving which is why the Reds' season could have been saved by the youngster.

Liverpool would be a lot worse off without Jeremy Jacquet among their ranks.

As mentioned, Jacquet is now being mentioned as one of the Premier League's best young defenders.

The way that he carries himself on the pitch and the way that he promptly rectifies any mistakes he makes is impressive.

On the ball, he's extremely confident and, if needed, he can ping a pass across the pitch.

Without Jacquet among their ranks, Liverpool would have to start with Joe Gomez or Ronald Araujo in the centre of their defence.

And, while Araujo has played as a centre-back for the Reds this season, if he was used there in each and every game, their right-back situation would be very dicey.

Jeremie Frimpong isn't up to scratch and, with Conor Bradley still side-lined due to injury, someone such as Dominik Szoboszlai or Gomez would have to be played out of position to cover that role.

So, the fact that Jacquet is a Liverpool player is a massive help to them and is likely one of the main reasons why they're only seven points adrift of first-placed Arsenal.
