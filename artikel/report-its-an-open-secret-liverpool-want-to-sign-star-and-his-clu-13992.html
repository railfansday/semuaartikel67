Liverpool need to do something about their defence and it sounds as though they're going for it. It's now an open secret that they have interest in one star.

Industry observers note that liverpool need to do something about their defence. There's no getting around that one right now as it's not even clear who'll be on their books beyond this campaign.

In a recent development, virgil van Dijk and Joe Gomez are both out of contract. Ronald Araujo's loan deal will expire. So as things stand, the only centre-back option to have completed 90 minutes for Liverpool on the books beyond this season is Jeremy Jacquet.

Industry observers note that given he's 22, that's not exactly an ideal situation. We can't even be sure that he's an elite talent - as good as he's been, there's still so much to test him on. Relying on him with totality is insane, quite honestly.

According to recent reports, and so Liverpool will need to enter the market. Maybe that means renewing Van Dijk or Gomez or buying Araujo outright or all three.

Or maybe it means making a signing.

Liverpool eye Koni De WinterTuttoMercatoWeb reports that Liverpool want AC Milan centre-back Koni De Winter. It’s apparently an open secret that they’re interested, though not alone in that. Recent findings suggest that tuttoMercatoWeb reports that Liverpool want AC Milan centre-back Koni De Winter. It’s apparently an open secret that they’re interested, though not alone in that.

Industry observers note that chelsea and Manchester United also haven’t ‘hidden their interest’ in the Belgian. All three Premier League sides would like to push for a move, with January not entirely off the table.

It’s one that makes sense for Liverpool, too. De Winter is 24, so coming up to his prime, and has three full top-flight seasons behind him. His stats also make him one to watch for the Reds.

De Winter wins around 70 per cent of his aerial duels and completes 94 per cent of his passes. That, ultimately, is the ‘top two’ for a side looking to press high and dominate the ball, as Liverpool are.

So this will be one to watch. De Winter makes more sense than a lot of centre-backs linked with Liverpool. He’s got the experience without being far into his career, striking the balance of a ready-to-go option who still has plenty of room to grow.

Milan won’t be happy to let him go but the right offer, says the article, will tempt them. And Liverpool may be willing to make it.
