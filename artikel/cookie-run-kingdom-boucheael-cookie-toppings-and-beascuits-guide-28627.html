Cookie Run Kingdom: Boucheael Cookie Toppings and Beascuits guide

🌐 Source: Pocketgamer • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (100 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Depending on your Topping stats, you want to roll the Beascuit after you've found the ideal rolls on the Toppings.
  • Because his passive gives you a total of 30% Cooldown by maxing the Toppings ATK, having that extra 10-15% Cooldown will help you keep his Sanctuary up pretty much all the time.
  • The goal is for him to survive as long as possible.

Depending on your Topping stats, you want to roll the Beascuit after you've found the ideal rolls on the Toppings. His skill stats don't appear pre-battle, so by taking those into account, you want to get a total of about 60%+ ATK and 10-15% Cooldown. Any extra stats should go into DMG Resist.

Because his passive gives you a total of 30% Cooldown by maxing the Toppings ATK, having that extra 10-15% Cooldown will help you keep his Sanctuary up pretty much all the time.

The goal is for him to survive as long as possible.
