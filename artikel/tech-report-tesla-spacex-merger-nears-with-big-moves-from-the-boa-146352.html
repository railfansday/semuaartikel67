📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that there’s been the expectation for months, since SpaceX’s IPO at least, that Tesla and SpaceX will merge. The suspicions haven’t been built on a lot of evidence, more so just the sense that this is what Elon Musk would like to do — including because it would give him basically unchecked control of the combined company (more so than he already has).

Well, far beyond suspicions, reporting implies that this is really going to happen, and may even be on the verge of happening. The Financial Times reports that Tesla and SpaceX have appointed a total of five directors from their boards to explore the possibility — three directors from Tesla’s board and two from SpaceX’s board. In fact, we even know the people who have been appointed — Robyn Denholm (Tesla), Kathleen Wilson-Thompson (Tesla), Jack Hartung (Tesla), Roelof Botha (SpaceX), and Randy Glein (SpaceX).

“Musk said on SpaceX’s third-quarter earnings call last month ​that a merger unifying his AI, robotics, EV and aerospace ambitions ​under a single corporate roof would be ‘a good idea,'” Reuters also noted. “He confirmed that the companies had formed special committees of independent directors to discuss ​a union.”.

According to recent reports, when this idea first came up, the big concern that was raised is that Tesla is heavily dependent on China — for both sales and production — and SpaceX is a key military supplier. China could, therefore, put strong pressure on the firm to make it do what China wants. SpaceX would, in essence, be compromised. Whether or not there’s a way around that or it matters in this era of Trump’s massive corruption, I don’t know. Just look at what has happened and is happening with NVIDIA — the CEO (Jensen Huang) was having an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, NVIDIA then went ahead and did what China wanted it to do (with the support of President Trump, whom NVIDIA’s CEO heavily supported), Huang was forced out of NVIDIA after it was revealed he had been having an affair with this spy (as well as other people, including employees), and now he’s been welcomed back to NVIDIA after Trump noted a few things and the board had a long, heart-to-heart talk with Huang. With that story in mind, not to mention the insane levels of corruption Trump and his family have been engaging in, is the Trump administration going to have any issue with the national security concerns that would be raised from Tesla and SpaceX merging?

We’ll see what happens, but at the moment, it sure looks like a merger is on the horizon.
