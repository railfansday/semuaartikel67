GTA 6: How to Find the Parrot's Secret Stash

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that grand Theft Auto 6 is chock full of side quests that point you towards bonus hits or gang hideouts. All over Leonida, groups of ner’do wells are making cash and leaving it lying around, all yours for the taking if you’re quick enough on the draw and know where to look.

It has been revealed that while picking up Jason’s neighbor Shirley’s rum order from a nearby liquor store, you just might hear some squawking the attendant writes off as a loud TV. In reality, he’s hiding a pretty penny within an exotic animal ring run out the back, and a spiteful parrot is stuck back there ready to spill the beans. Let’s go over the quickest way to find it and run it for all its worth.

It has been revealed that to start, you’ll want to head to the Three Sheers Liquor store, marked on your map by Shirley when you accept her request to snag her rum order. Located just down the road from Jason’s place, enter for a small cutscene hinting there's more going on behind closed doors.

Once you secure Shirley’s rum in your trunk, head down the alley to the right of the store. You’ll hear a bunch of people arguing with what sounds like a parrot, insulting the crew and blabbing about hiding cash. Continue down the alley until you spot an opening in the barbed wire cover on the wall.

Hop over, and the gang will start a brawl. Be careful, as stepping too far away will prompt multiple members to draw their guns. If you want to get out of this quietly and professionally, make sure you handle the group hand-to-hand; that way, no one calls the cops.

Once you handle all the goons, the parrot will start praising you. By interacting with it, Jason will ask about the money it mentioned, and it will start dropping hints. The notable one is the generator, alluding to the small box generator in the upper left corner of the yard, behind the liquor store.

Recent findings suggest that go into focus mode, and you’ll notice a small marked crevice in the side of the store, right beside the generator. Interact with it to pocket a cool $1,382, a solid early-game sum that will more than pay for running Shirley’s errands. You can also pocket a number of melee weapons from the goons, including machetes and shovels if you’re looking for a hand-to-hand option.

The liquor store gang is one of many small, unaffiliated criminal groups that you can find throughout Leonida, acting as a great first taste of the kind of rewards that can be hiding right out of plain sight. Return Shirley’s rum, and you’ve had a very successful errand.
