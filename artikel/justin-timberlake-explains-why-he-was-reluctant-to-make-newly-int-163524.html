Justin Timberlake is opening up about what convinced him to share a more personal side of himself in his upcoming documentary.

During an appearance on The Tonight Show on Thursday (Nov. 26), the 45-year-old pop superstar told host Jimmy Fallon that he was initially hesitant to pull back the curtain on his private life for Timberlake, a four-part documentary series premiering on Apple TV on Dec. 11.

“I was really reluctant to do it,” Timberlake told Fallon, noting that he’s always been a pretty private person. But his relationship with director Fisher Stevens eventually helped change his mind, with their conversations encouraging him to open up about parts of his life he hadn’t previously discussed.

“When Fisher first approached me to do this, I was reluctant because I was like, ‘I don’t wanna talk about myself,’” Timberlake continued. But as they continued talking, he found himself “searching for something that was beneath the surface a little more.”.

The “Can’t Stop the Feeling!” singer also reflected on how his experiences over the past five years have shaped him.

Sources indicate that “You just never know what somebody’s going through, right?” Timberlake said. “And I think I kind of decided, maybe people see my life from the outside and it’s like, ‘Wow!’ But everybody’s going through something, and I think I’ve gone through a lot in the last five years that I feel like has made me a better human and a better father, a better husband, a better friend.”.

Timberlake noted making the docuseries proved “to be really cathartic,” and he hopes his willingness to be vulnerable will resonate with viewers who may be navigating similar experiences.

Recent findings suggest that “Maybe people see my life from the outside and it’s like, ‘Wow!’ But everybody’s going through something,” the singer remarked. “Maybe I can be a beacon of light for whoever’s watching.”.

The four-part series will feature never-before-seen archival footage, along with interviews with Timberlake’s friends, collaborators and family members. His wife, Jessica Biel, and his mother, Lynn Harless, both appear in a recent teaser for the project.

Recent findings suggest that the first two episodes of Timberlake will premiere Dec. 11 on Apple TV, followed by the third episode on Dec. 18 and the series finale on Dec. 23.

Elsewhere during his Tonight Show appearance, Timberlake celebrated the 20th anniversary of his sophomore solo album, FutureSex/LoveSounds, with a pair of musical medleys performed at New York City’s Rainbow Room. The performances featured some of the set’s biggest hits, including “SexyBack,” “My Love,” “Summer Love” and “What Goes Around…Comes Around.”.

Elsewhere during his Tonight Show takeover, Timberlake joined Fallon for a turkey-themed comedy sketch, revived their popular “Camp Winnipesaukee” bit, and teamed up with The Roots for a “History of Party Songs” medley featuring hits from the Spice Girls, Earth, Wind & Fire and others.
