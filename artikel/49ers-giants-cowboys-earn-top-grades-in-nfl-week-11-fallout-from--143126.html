Industry observers note that we fell for it again. Everyone underrated Houston. How could college basketball be so blind? Kelvin Sampson is as reliable as anyone in this sport and seems to own the nation's best team following the Cougars' run to the Players Era 8 title. At the very least, Sampson owns his most dangerous offense to date.

Our Cameron Salerno projects that Houston will move up to No. 1 in the AP Top 25 rankings when they are released later today.

Industry observers note that we also foresee Indiana moving into the top 25 for the first time in this young season thanks to a 2-0 week in which it beat No. 20 Arkansas and Kentucky. The 14-point win over Kentucky not only cemented the Hoosiers' case to crack the rankings but also emphasized the Wildcats' flaws. It also pointed the microscope on Mark Pope and his less-than-accurate explanation of his team's lack of experience.

Don't forget about Oregon. Three early-season losses dropped the Ducks out of the rankings and playoff picture, but with Dylan Raiola kicking the offense into gear, they trounced Michigan State and are squarely in the hunt for the Big Ten Championship Game and CFP. Losses from UCLA and Indiana helped their cause and made them one of the most fortunate teams of the week.

In a recent development, elsewhere in the Big Ten, Ohio State is trending sharply in the other direction and on Saturday tumbled right out of playoff consideration. It has to be a devastating feeling to be on the wrong end of Nebraska snapping a 31-game losing streak against ranked opponents. It is hardly a consolation for losing a fourth game for the first time since 2011, but at least Jeremiah Smith put up monster numbers on the Cornhuskers. Nebraska coach Matt Rhule is ready to declare the Heisman Trophy race over, and our Brandon Marcello says that is not an overreaction.

It has been revealed that while Ryan Day slogs through the worst year of his career, Josh Heupel is pulling off his best coaching job to date. His Tennessee squad downed LSU by two touchdowns for a revenge win over Lane Kiffin and earned a "B+" on our Week 12 report card for the victory.
