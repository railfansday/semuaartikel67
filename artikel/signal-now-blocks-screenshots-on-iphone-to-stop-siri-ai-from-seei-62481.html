Popular secure messaging app Signal has rolled out a new upgrade that automatically disables screenshots for the sake of blocking Siri AI from reading your chats. Here are the details.

Signal’s latest app revision on iPhone disables a key system feature: taking screenshots.

As Signal told Reuters, that’s because Apple doesn’t presently provide better tools for iOS developers to safeguard app content from Siri AI and third-party AI agents.

By default, consumers who update and try to take a screenshot will see a message that indicates: “Screenshot prevented by ‘Block Siri AI’ setting.”.

Fortunately, there’s an easy workaround. Users who want to re-enable screenshots can do so by visiting Signal’s privacy settings and turning off the latest toggle that’s labeled ‘Block Siri AI.’.
