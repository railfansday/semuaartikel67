Latest Update: Allegri: ‘Napoli needed to smile for a goal, McTominay and De Bruyne switched role’

Sources indicate that max Allegri explains the tactical tweaks he made for Scott McTominay and Kevin De Bruyne during Napoli’s 1-0 win over Torino, not to mention his advice. ‘Try smiling and we might get a goal.’.

The Partenopei struggled to get the better of Torino at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona, and were rescued by the woodwork when Rolando Mandragora’s screamer surprised Vanja Milinkovic-Savic just before half-time.

However, McTominay got the only goal of the game when Noa Lang turned a defensive run into an attacking one, setting up the Scotland international on the edge of the area.

It was noted on the touchline that after the goal, Allegri turned to his bench and noted this was because he had told the players to smile more.

“The mood wasn’t right because we had so many chances, I told them at half-time to try smiling and we might get a goal,” Allegri told Sky Sport Italia.

“They did really well to win this game, while suffering against a strong opponent. Perhaps it is positive that we won under pressure, but we also should’ve done better controlling the tempo, moving the ball from one side to the other.

“The lads did very well in defending on set plays and corner kicks.”.

It came at a cost, as Napoli’s injury crisis intensified further with Antonio Vergara limping off just 11 minutes into the game, meaning there was barely any rest for De Bruyne ahead of Tuesday’s Champions League trip to Manchester City.

At first, De Bruyne was in the more advanced role behind Rasmus Hojlund, but after a few minutes Allegri dropped the Belgium international deeper and put McTominay there instead.

“The fun thing about this job is finding solutions. I am starting to get to know these players, I thought De Bruyne could do better as a support for the striker, but in this situation, McTominay was more useful,” explained the coach.

In a recent development, “I just told McTominay not to be already in the position right behind Hojlund, but to run into that space, as it would make him harder to mark. If he is already there and not mobile, then obviously he’s going to be easier to control, but he started to move between the lines and did better.

Sources indicate that “I think Hojlund had a good game, he worked for the team. In football, sometimes things go well in one situation, then go badly in another. Today we finally managed to defend with more determination and grit, but then didn’t score as many as before.

“Hojlund scored on international duty last week, he is a very useful player for us.”.

Allegri has made history, becoming the first ever coach to amass 1,100 Serie A points, but now he faces a difficult Champions League trip to Manchester City on Tuesday.

“We need to be realistic, because as I said when the fixture list was first drawn up, our Champions League qualification would be decided in the final three matches,” said the coach.

“On Tuesday we need a good performance, try to get a result, but also know that we’ve got to sort our Serie A position first.”.
