Launching in time for Spark Season, the Soneil group's holiday gift to EV drivers.

BRAMPTON, ON, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Soneil Spark, the EV charging business of Soneil Electronics, today publicly launched Spark Cashback, a program that turns the Clean Fuel credits your home charging creates into cash in your pocket. It rolls out alongside Spark Season, the Soneil group's way of giving back to EV drivers this holiday season.

"We've spent more than 30 years building power electronics people can rely on, and Spark Cashback puts that to work for everyday drivers," noted Sonya Jain, CEO of Soneil Spark. "The charger is free with a deposit you get back, and every charge puts money back in your pocket. Spark Season is how we give back, with a gift that keeps giving and is even better shared with someone you care about.".

Sources indicate that under Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations, fuel suppliers must buy compliance credits, and charging an EV at home creates them. Your Spark charger tracks each session over Wi-Fi, Soneil Spark sells the credits, and you get a share as cash by Interac e-Transfer. The fuel industry helps offset your everyday charging, while you keep driving without tailpipe emissions.

Free, because your deposit comes back.

Sources indicate that the charger is free with a refundable deposit. Once you reach 1,250 kWh, the full deposit comes back, earlier than most comparable free-charger programs. You end up with a home charger that cost you nothing, apart from shipping, plus cash back on top. How soon depends on your driving.

Built on 30 years in power electronics.

Soneil Spark designs and builds a complete range of EV charging under one roof, including mobile charging trailers for fleets and enterprise, all on our own technology, made in Canada, rather than rebadged off-the-shelf hardware. Soneil Electronics has reliably supplied batteries and chargers globally across a wide range of industries and applications, from golf carts and cleaning appliances to material handling and emergency power. The firm has supplied more than 5 million chargers, and every Soneil Spark charger is backed by 24/7 North American-based support.
