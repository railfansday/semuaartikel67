Mario Kart World has a brand-fresh update available to download right now on Nintendo Switch 2, bringing eight new courses to race on, two new Knockout Tour routes, and more. This new racing content arrives as part of update version 1.9.0 for Mario Kart World.

Sources indicate that the big highlight of the enhancement is the eight new courses, which are actually remixed spins on classic Mario Kart tracks from across the series' history. They are: Mario Bros. Circuit 2, Desert Hill 2, Shy Guy Bazaar 2, Wario Stadium 2, Airship Fortress 2, DK Pass 2, Koopa Troopa Beach 2, and Salty Salty Speedway 2. You can get a look at each in the image below:.

Recent findings suggest that this update also adds two new Knockout Tour routes: Feather Rally and Lucky Cat Rally. Both are available to race through starting today.

Elsewhere in the patch, Nintendo has revamped the Mario Kart World "Replay" feature, letting players watch replays of past races and battles.
