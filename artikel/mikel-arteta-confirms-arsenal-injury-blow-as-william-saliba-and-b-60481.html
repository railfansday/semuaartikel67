William Saliba is “ready” to make his first-team return for Arsenal, but Martin Zubimendi faces “weeks” out with a hamstring injury, indicates Mikel Arteta.

Defender Saliba is yet to feature for the first team this season due to a long-term back injury, but played 45 minutes in an Under-21 match against Aston Villa just before the international break and then featured in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Watford.

He is now in contention to be in the matchday squad at Newcastle on Saturday evening.

In a recent development, arteta told reporters on Friday: “He is ready, he’s in a good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes.

Industry observers note that “He played two games, one behind-closed-doors and one more official. He did really well and he feels in good condition.

However, Zubimendi will be absent at St James’ Park and for the upcoming matches after being forced off for Spain against England last week.

Sources indicate that “[Zubimendi] is out,” Arteta added. “Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring.

“He wasn’t too concerned after the game but after the MRI scan indicated an injury there. he’s going to be out for a few weeks I think.”.

Saka will also be missing as he continues to recover from a quad injury, though Arteta confirmed he has not required surgery.
