What started as one artist’s response to being cut out of a music video has exploded, becoming a driving force in Nigeria’s cultural landscape.

As a student in the central Nigerian city of Ilorin, Lawal Habeeb Kola loved staying in his hostel room to produce beats rather than returning to his family house in Lagos, the more frenetic city of his birth.

Recent findings suggest that in 2022, one of his beats became popular on TikTok, and a version featuring ad-libbed vocals from a hypeman sent it viral. He was invited to the music video shoot in Lagos and thought his significant break had finally come, but when the video was released, he was absent.
