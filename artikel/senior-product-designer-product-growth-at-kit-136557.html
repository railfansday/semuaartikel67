It has been revealed that kit is the email marketing platform experts choose to grow their audience, deepen their relationships, and build a business around the work they love. For coaches, YouTubers, authors, podcasters, and other creatives, there isn't a better marketing hub to rely on to grow audiences, automate email marketing, and sell digital products — all within one platform.More importantly, there isn't a team more committed to helping creators earn a living. We're on a mission to help creators earn $1 billion using our creator marketing platform. We have always been 100% independent and 100% remote.We are proud to have built a product that our customers love, and we look for people who have enthusiasm and belief in our mission, vision, and values to join our team. We're also embracing AI thoughtfully — both in how we build and how we hire to ensure our team is adaptable, innovative, and ready for what's next.

We’re looking for a senior product designer to be the first dedicated designer on our product growth team, shaping how creators get set up, see value in their first days, and enjoy a seamless upgrade experience. Kit ships fast, and the product growth team builds the frameworks and systems that connect creators to the value in their strategy as the product keeps changing. We need a designer who can balance rapid experimentation with long-term value for our creators.

Product growth is a new team at Kit, formed earlier this year. In the six months since, we’ve found our rhythm and started to move the numbers, but we’re still shaping how we work, how we prioritise and where we focus in the long term. As the team’s designer, you’ll be the design voice for growth, with real influence over how the team works and what it builds.

You'll own the design of the moments that move our funnel: onboarding, activation, free trials, upgrade and upsell flows, pricing and checkout, and billing. You'll work in a team that learns by shipping, through A/B tests and ship-and-measure changes, and you'll use data to decide what to try next.

This is a full-time IC role for a designer who loves connecting design decisions to numbers, moves rapidly without sacrificing quality, and is excited about using AI to accelerate outcomes.

We need someone who thinks deeply about user behaviour, interaction design and the product experience, and who is proactive about finding the next opportunity rather than waiting for a spec.

You'll report to Dan Winer, Director of Product Design, and join a small, high-impact product design team currently consisting of four senior product designers and a senior design engineer. From day one, you’ll be part of the product growth team’s product trio alongside the product manager and engineering manager, shaping the team’s vision, priorities and how we work. You’ll also work closely with the team’s engineers.

Sources indicate that first week: Get to know Kit’s product and the product growth team’s goals. Get access to Figma, Linear, Mixpanel and our other reporting tools. Take part in get-to-know-you calls with the team.

First month: Get up to speed on how we track and measure creator behaviour in Mixpanel, Omni and Clarity, which experiments are running now, and what we’ve learnt from past ones. Join the product trio’s planning and prioritisation discussions, and ship a small experiment or two, with support from the Director of Product Design as you take ownership of the growth work.

First six months: Own design across the team’s work, which spans growth (onboarding, activation, trials and upgrades) and billing (checkout, strategy changes, downgrades and cancellation). This might include personalising onboarding, improving free-to-paid conversion or simplifying our payment flows. By six months, the team should rely on you for a clear picture of how creators move through these flows, where they get stuck and why. You’ll help the team run more and better experiments, from forming a hypothesis to reading the results. We don’t have dedicated researchers, so you’ll talk to creators, run usability tests, track the impact of your work and proactively suggest iterations.

In a recent development, first year: Help the team improve our activation, free-to-paid, trial conversion, and early retention rates, so that more creators upgrade, stay on Kit, and find real value. Turn our upsell, discount and plan change moments into one coherent experience instead of a set of one-off prompts.

Interaction design for complex web applications, with a strong eye for visual hierarchy and detail.

Grounding design decisions in the creator’s voice through interviews, usability testing and customer feedback, partnering with the PM so it shapes what we build as well as how.

Designing flows that change depending on who’s seeing them, like free, trial and paid accounts, or creators upgrading and downgrading.

Designing onboarding, activation, upgrade, pricing, checkout or billing flows.

Experiment design and reading results, including knowing when an A/B test is the right approach or when to just ship and track.

Defining what events to track so the team can measure what you shipped, using tools like Mixpanel.

Clear written and visual communication of design rationale, including async walkthroughs.

Around 5+ years as a product designer on SaaS or other complex web-based products.

Led design on growth, monetisation or onboarding work and can point to its impact on metrics, ideally including retention or quality of conversion, not just volume.

Worked in a cross-functional team with a product manager and engineers, shipping continuously.

Worked within and contributed to an established design system.

A portfolio that demonstrates how research or data informed your decisions and what happened as a result.

Bonus: designed pricing, billing or plan change experiences.

Curious about why people behave the way they do, and about what the numbers aren’t telling you.

Comfortable being wrong in public and sharing what you don’t know, not just what you do.

Proactive and self-directed, with a bias for shipping.

Excited about AI and pragmatic about how to use it.

Low ego and comfortable giving and receiving direct feedback.

Preferring to execute designs handed down from a product manager.

Treating a conversion win as the finish line, without checking whether those creators stay with Kit and find value.

Wanting long, highly-refined projects with little contact with data or results.

Discomfort with experiments that fail or with changing direction based on what we learn.

Focusing on visual polish without deeper thinking about user behaviour and business outcomes.

Being philosophically opposed to using AI in your work.

Needing well-established processes and a fully defined roadmap from day one.
