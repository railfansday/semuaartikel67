IShowSpeed broke down in tears after finishing GTA 6’s story at the end of a 52-hour livestream, during which the streamer didn’t sleep once.

It wasn’t how Speed had planned to play GTA 6 either, having told fans just days before launch that he had no intention of marathoning it.

“I just don’t wanna rush it, and I want you guys to play the game as well, not just watch me,” he noted. “It’s still gonna be a 12-hour stream. I don’t wanna rush.”.

🚨| BREAKING: Speed reveals he WON’T BE MARATHONING GTA 6 because he doesn’t want to rush through the game 🤯👀Speed: “I just don’t wanna rush it… and I want you guys to play the game as well, not just watch me. It’s still gonna be an 12 hour stream. I don’t wanna rush" pic.twitter.com/appYXWFnbh.

That plan quickly changed once he was hooked on the game, with Speed going live on YouTube and Twitch at 9pm ET on November 18 and not logging off for more than two days.

Around 40 hours in, he’d already worked through roughly 82% of the main story and was telling viewers he needed about 12 more hours to see it through.

That nearly went out the window three hours later, when his PS5 crashed in the middle of a cutscene.

“I don’t even think I saved, chat. There’s no way,” Speed told viewers as the game rebooted, convinced he’d lost his progress.

🚨| BREAKING: Speed LOSES IT after his GTA 6 suddenly CRASHES in the middle of a mission before he even had a chance to SAVE HIS PROGRESS 😱😬Speed: “Say WALLAHI… There’s NO WAY” 😭 pic.twitter.com/JhVdu1q7Wn.

To his relief, the autosave had kicked in, dropping him back at 88% on the “Team Building” mission so the marathon could carry on.

Sources indicate that just before the credits rolled, Speed stopped to reflect on how far he’d come. “After 52 hours of non-stop playing, I didn’t go to sleep once,” he told viewers. “I haven’t slept in three days.”.

The ending then left him visibly shocked, and he broke down in tears as the story wrapped up.

According to recent reports, “Bro, I just played this game for 52 hours,” he noted. “What was that, bro? What the hell was that?”.

Even so, he stood by his pre-launch promise. “I didn’t rush it. I just never got off the game. I just never slept,” he stated.

Before signing off, Speed teased a “huge announcement” coming soon, though he’s yet to reveal what it is.

🚨| BREAKING: Speed has OFFICIALLY ENDED HIS GTA 6 MARATHON after completing the game 100% in 52 HOURS STRAIGHT with NO SLEEP and declared he’ll be BACK SOON WITH A HUGE ANNOUNCEMENT 🤯👀🔥 pic.twitter.com/jJ1sB0xrWZ.

Across the three VODs it took to fit the whole stream on YouTube, the marathon has now passed 120 million views.
