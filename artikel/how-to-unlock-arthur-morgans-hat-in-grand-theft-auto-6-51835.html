As you'd expect from a Rockstar game, Grand Theft Auto 6 is absolutely packed with secrets and easter eggs that reference both pop culture and the wider world of video games.

It has been revealed that the best of these nods, though, are the ones that directly reference Rockstar's previous works. That includes a particularly snazzy Red Dead Redemption 2 easter egg that you'll want to see.

In a recent development, to find Arthur Morgan's hat, you'll need to head to a yard sale that's taking place on Orchard Avenue in the South of Port Gellhorn. Head to the location marked on the map, and you'll see a bunch of balloons in a driveway that will let you know you're in the right place to find Arthur's iconic hat.

According to recent reports, once here, look at the white sofa close to the house, and you'll see a hat sitting on top of a guitar case that you can interact with. The so-called "Genuine Cowboy Hat" is being sold for a steal at $5, and the owner will even make a comment about having no idea where it came from. Real ones will, of course, recognise the rope around the hat that makes it clear who it previously belonged to.

Funnily enough, this isn't the first time a Red Dead Redemption hat has shown up in a different Rockstar game. John's hat made a cameo in LA Noire, although you sadly couldn't wear it.
