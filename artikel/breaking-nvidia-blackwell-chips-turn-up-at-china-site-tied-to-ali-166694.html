Breaking: Nvidia Blackwell Chips Turn Up at China Site Tied to Alice Huang

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (124 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that A pair of Chinese firms installed Nvidia Corp.’s powerful Blackwell semiconductors in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, the woman at the heart of a controversy surrounding the US chipmaker. The gear installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project included a GB300 NVL72 rack, among Nvidia’s most powerful products, according to a serial number specified in documents submitted to Chinese regulators in November. Shanghai Shuoyao offered the hardware as collateral to obtain a 96 million yuan ($14 million) loan from Shanghai Innovation Bank, according to filings from the lender that were reviewed by Bloomberg News. Bloomberg's Tom Keene and Paul Sweeney get details from Olivia Levieux, Markets Strategist with Bloomberg News (Source: Bloomberg).
