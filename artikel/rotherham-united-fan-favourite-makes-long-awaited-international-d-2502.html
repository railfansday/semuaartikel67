Rotherham United fan favourite makes long-awaited international debut in big 3-2 win over Mexico

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Rotherham United man Denzel Hall replaced Kyle Ming in a right wing-back role at the break and helped see out a vital win. The Reggae Boyz put themselves in pole position to reach the CONCACAF Nations League semi-finals ahead of Wednesday’s second leg, by winning the first tie 3-2.

It could have been an even more memorable night for the hosts as they were three to the good by the hour-mark. But, Mexico staged a late fightback to reduce the deficit, courtesy of goals from Gilberto Mora and Julian Quinones.

Hall isn’t the only Rotherham United player to represent Jamaica, with Brandon Cover also featuring for the Caribbean nation. However, he missed out on a call-up this time around.

This was Hall’s first call-up having been granted a Jamaican passport in October 2025 and looked set to play a key role in the final stages of their FIFA World Cup qualifying campaign last season.

According to recent reports, hall did feature for the Netherlands at youth level, representing them from U15 to U17, having been born in Rotterdam. But, the wealth of talent ahead of him blocked his pathway into the senior side.

With Jamaican heritage through his father, the Rotherham United man was eligible to switch international allegiance and did so last year to get a taste of international football on the biggest stage.

Despite his impressive performances in a struggling Millers side last season, the 25-year-old was overlooked for his first two opportunities to join the squad as the Reggae Boyz suffered a defeat to DR Congo in March to deny them a place in the World Cup.

Taking the less-travelled route from Rotterdam to Rotherham last summer, Hall signed from Heerenveen but was expected to leave ahead of the new season following their relegation.

According to recent reports, cardiff City and Preston North End were reportedly interested in the versatile defender but neither deal materialised. Since, he has started in all of Rotherham United’s League Two games this term.

A rotten run of form has left them in 22nd place with their last league victory coming against Northampton Town nearly two months ago, although progressing past Bromley in the FA Cup last time out could help inspire an upturn in form.

Hall’s performance against Mexico means he could earn his first start in Wednesday’s second-leg, with his athleticism and work rate in a wing-back role making him a valuable option to call upon.

Also in contention for Rotherham’s trip to high-flying Grimsby Town this weekend, Hall is set to play a key role in trying to help the Millers avoid relegation to non-league for the first time in their history.
