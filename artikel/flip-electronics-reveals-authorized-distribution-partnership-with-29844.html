ALPHARETTA, Ga., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Flip Electronics, a global authorized distributor specializing in hard-to-find, discontinued LTB, EOL, aged and obsolete electronic components, today confirmed it has signed an authorized distribution partnership agreement with GSI Technology. Through this agreement, Flip Electronics will distribute GSI's portfolio of high performance SRAM devices focusing on Aged, End of Life and Obsolete devices supporting innovation across aerospace, defense, medical imaging and industrial systems.

"We are pleased to offer our EOL and Obsolete products through Flip Electronics, with their emphasis on customer service and strong commitment to solving customers' obsolescence supply chain challenges - a critical factor for our legacy and EOL customers," said Didier Lasserre, Vice President of Worldwide Sales and Investor Relations.

In a recent development, "For aerospace, defense, medical, and industrial customers, component obsolescence can create significant challenges for programs designed to operate for decades," stated Jason Murphy, CEO of Flip Electronics. "Our partnership with GSI Technology allows us to provide customers with authorized, traceable SRAM solutions that help address obsolescence challenges, extend the life of existing systems, and maintain continuity across long-term programs.".

According to recent reports, about Flip ElectronicsFlip Electronics is a global authorized distributor of electronic components, focused on delivering factory-direct, fully traceable components for high-reliability applications. Flip specializes in obsolescence mitigation, extended-life manufacturing, lifecycle extension, and obsolescence solutions for markets where quality and authenticity are critical. Flip Electronics supports a wide range of industries, including aerospace, defense, industrial, EMS, automotive, transportation, and medical, by providing authorized, traceable electronic components and innovative solutions to combat obsolescence and supply chain disruption.
