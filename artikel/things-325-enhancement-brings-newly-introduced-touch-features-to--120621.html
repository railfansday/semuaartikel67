📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that cultured Code is bringing swipe gestures to Things on Apple’s touchscreen MacBook Pro. Version 3.25 builds on the task manager’s basic touch support so you can schedule a to-do with a swipe or select several tasks with two fingers and drag them all at once.

It has been revealed that say you’re going through your Today list and want to put something off until tomorrow. Swipe right on the to-do to open the date picker. Open it by touch and you’ll see taller rows that make dates easier to tap. Custom menus get the same treatment.

Swiping left selects a to-do. Swipe left again to deselect it. For a longer list, swipe up or down with two fingers to select several items at once. Then touch and hold any selected item to pick up the whole group, even if some of those tasks are offscreen. You can also drag individual to-dos and headings.

A single tap selects a to-do, and a second tap expands it. Double-tapping works too. Inside a to-do, checklist items are easier to reorder by touch. You can also cancel a to-do or checklist item by pressing and holding its checkbox.

Cultured Code has also made the sidebar easier to resize with a finger and given buttons throughout the Mac app larger tap targets.

The upgrade follows Siri AI and Spotlight integration in Things 3.24 and more flexible scheduling for repeating to-dos in Things 3.23. You can find Things for Mac on the Mac App Store.
