A referee has hit back at claims he was “drunk” while officiating an AFC Champions League Two match.

Footage has been widely shared on social media of Syrian referee Hanna Hattab looking visibly disoriented during a match between Iran's Gol Gohar and Al Jazira of the United Arab Emirates.

One particular incident raised eyebrows when Hattab demonstrated a yellow card to Al Jazira’s goalkeeper for a foul that happened close to the halfway line, 40 yards away from him.

Hattab was then replaced at half-time with the fourth official taking over for the remainder of the game, which Al Jazira won 1-0.

Reports claimed Gol Gohar staff accused the referee of being drunk following erratic decision-making as similar accusations flooded in online.

But Hattab, who is part of the AFC's elite refereeing group, has refuted the allegations as “insult and defamation”, threatening legal action.

He wrote in a statement on Facebook: “I confirm that family and personal life are not the responsibility of others, and are not a game in your hands. Religion, reason, and morals do not tolerate the insult of people's privacy or defamation of them.

“I also confirm my full right to take all legal and judicial measures against anyone who published or made a statement or contributed to the insult and defamation of me, regardless of their position or status, whether they are an individual, an institution, or a sports entity.”.

He also said he would concern a further statement following contact with the Syrian Arab Football Federation (SFA) which will “clarify all the details and facts related to what happened ... with full transparency and clarity.”.

Omani journalist Issa Said, who covers Asian football, has since claimed on social media that what happened to Hattab was "due to a drop in his blood sugar, which caused him to lose concentration and focus".

The SFA have also put out a statement standing in support of Hattab, while stating it has requested his medical report from the match.

“The Main Referees Committee of the Syrian Arab Football Federation confirms that it stands in full support of international referee Mr Hana Khattab regarding the incident he was subjected to, following reassurance about his health,” it said.

“This comes after the committee was reassured of his well-being, following a number of discussions on social media concerning the incident. The committee emphasises its full support for him and its commitment to following up on the matter in all its details and developments.

Sources indicate that “In this regard, the committee has requested that the Syrian Football Referees Committee provide the medical report relating to the referee, the match observers' report and the referee assessor's report. This is in order to establish the circumstances of the incident accurately and comprehensively and to complete the necessary procedures in this matter.

“The committee also confirms that it will provide the relevant authorities and the public with the findings as soon as the requested reports are received, in order to ensure accuracy and to obtain information from official sources.”.
