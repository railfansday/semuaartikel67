Celtic have suffered a major injury setback following confirmation that Mika Baur faces a lengthy spell on the sidelines.

The £6million summer signing from Paderborn has become a crucial part of Celtic's midfield this season, starting 17 of the 19 matches played across all competitions thus far, and scoring four goals.

His form in Glasgow saw him break into the German national team in September, earning his first two caps under Jurgen Klopp, which was followed by his inclusion in the Germany Under-21 squad for the most recent November internationals.

However, he had to withdraw from international duty after picking up an ankle injury in a challenge with Kilmarnock’s Aaron Tshibola in the final match before the break, which Celtic won 1-0.

Celtic manager Martin O’Neill confirmed that the 22-year-old has suffered ligament damage and could be out of action until February, which would rule him out of the remainder of the Europa League campaign and the next Old Firm derby against Rangers at Ibrox on January 3.

“He’ll be out for some considerable time,” O’Neill said as he addressed the media ahead of the Saturday’s Premiership clash with St Mirren.

It has been revealed that “It’s a real shame, he was playing great for us. There’s talk about 10 weeks, but I spoke to Mika yesterday and he’s a quick healer.

In a recent development, “There are things he can’t do for a while, his foot’s in a boot. He was on fire, he was doing great for us. It is a blow and it seemed so innocuous as well.

“I thought he’d been kicked, but it was the other foot he landed on and he’s just gone over on it.”.

Celtic will also assess the fitness of Benjamin Nygren ahead of the game against St Mirren with the midfielder suffering from a heel matter that saw him miss Sweden’s 1-1 draw in Poland on Tuesday.

It has been revealed that “He didn’t train today,” O’Neill mentioned. “We’ll see how he is in the morning. He’s got a sore heel. He’s been feeling it for a couple of weeks.”.
