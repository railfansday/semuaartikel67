The hunting wives have some new victims.

For the residents of Maple Brook, Texas, no character is ever really safe, as evidenced by Hunting Wives' 11 shocking deaths in season two.

It has been revealed that the Netflix series starring Brittany Snow and Malin Akerman returned on a morbid note Nov. 26: another young girl was murdered by a predator in town. Elly was the granddaughter of gang leader Zelda Moffitt, played by Widow's Bay star Dale Dickey.

As Zelda and her biker crew, including her nephew Gentle John Moffitt (played by Cam Gigandet), investigated the killing to get payback, the trail led them to Margo's brother Kyle. To draw Kyle out, Zelda's gang kidnapped Sophie (Snow) and Margo (Akerman), during which Sophie eventually admits Kyle's been a goner since season one.

In a recent development, the revelation accidentally outed Gentle John as Elly's real killer, and with that mind, Zelda repaid the betrayal with an eye for an eye by fatally shooting Gigandet’s character. Unfortunately for the leader of the gang, she and four of her henchmen are offed during a tense shootout with the police when confronted during the kidnapping.

According to recent reports, but the body count doesn't stop there. After Brad—played by George Ferrier—confronted the abuse he endured from his late mother, Jill, the trauma release caused him to bring a violent end to his final abuser, a shady music manager portrayed by John Stamos.

In a recent development, after killing Stamos in his luxury mansion and then shooting his father, Reverend Clint, Brad sought out his former flame, Margo, over what really happened to his ex, Abby Jackson. After the athlete figured out that the housewife was Abby's killer all along, Margo made the snap decision to silence Brad for good.

That noted, hiding Margo's secret resulted in several more deaths. Her husband Jed's newly introduced campaign advisor, Lincoln Trout, orchestrated a hit on Jed's former political strategist, Jia-Yi, to protect his governorship campaign from further scandal. And in the season finale, Margo and Sophie teamed up one last time to murder television producer Nadia Kelly (Kim Matula) at the scene of a car crash after the reporter had put together that Jill was framed for Abby's murder.

Fortunately, some Maple Brook residents lived to see another day. Despite being shot by his son, Reverend Clint survived the attack, while Deputy Wanda Salazar (Karen Rodriguez) and Deputy Walter Flynn (Hunt Emery) carry on their romance thanks to Flynn coming out of his coma following the shootout with Zelda.
