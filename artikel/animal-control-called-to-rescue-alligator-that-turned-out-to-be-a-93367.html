According to recent reports, animal control was forced to respond to a call about an alligator on the loose, but it turned out to be just a pool floatie. Seriously.

Over the years, plenty of people have rushed to call emergency services to report something that, when investigated, turned out to be totally false.

It has been revealed that this was the case over in Sugar Land, Texas, on November 12 when animal control received a call about an alligator being on the loose. As per Sugar Land Animal Services, they were told that the gator appeared to be feeling a bit sick and in need of some assistance.

Yet, when they reached it, it wasn’t all it seemed. “When ASO Perez arrived on the scene to investigate, she was in for a big surprise. What looked very real from a distance turned out to be a runaway pool floatie!” they posted on Facebook.

“While this call definitely gave our team a good laugh, we always take every report seriously,” Animal Services added.

According to recent reports, in the photo, the inflatable gator had its mouth stuck open with teeth on show, so if you see it from a distance, you might be confused. However, up close, you can tell it’s meant to be lounged on, not hunted or scared of.

“Remember: if you ever spot wildlife that looks sick or injured, please keep a safe distance and call the experts to handle it. You never know if it’s the real deal!”.

Naturally, commenters saw the funny side of things too. “Well, that was a rather inflated story then,” one joked. “She is very brave! Thank you, Officer Perez, for all you do,” another added. “Wow. It looks so realistic! Crazy,” another replied.

As noted, it isn’t the first instance of floaties being reported as real, nor is it the wildest.
