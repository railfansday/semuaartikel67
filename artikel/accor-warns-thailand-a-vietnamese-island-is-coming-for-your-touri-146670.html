According to recent reports, accor's Asia development chief claims Vietnam's fast-growing tourism island could win over price-conscious tour groups that now go to Thailand. It means Thailand needs to rethink how it sells itself. Accor has a stake in both outcomes: 85 hotels in Thailand, and a deal for more than 1,400 rooms on Phu Quoc.

Speaking in Bangkok on Tuesday, Accor's top developer for Asia had a blunt message for his Thai audience: a Vietnamese island is about to start taking your tourists.

"This is a warning to us here in Thailand. Competition is coming," Andrew Langdon, Accor's chief development officer for Asia, stated at the NoVacancy Asia conference in Bangkok. "We need to have a look at how we market.".

Langdon gave the example of a Russian tour group planning its next holiday. Next year, he noted, that group could well pick Phu Quoc over Thailand "because it's going to be cheaper and just as easy to get to.".
