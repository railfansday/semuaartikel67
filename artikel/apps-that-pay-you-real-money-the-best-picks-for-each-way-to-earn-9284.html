It has been revealed that pick the money-making app that fits what you can put in: Survey Junkie for spare minutes, Rakuten for online shopping, DoorDash or Uber for a car, and Neighbor for an empty garage. Favor apps that pay cash to your bank or PayPal, and expect real pay only for hours of work, a car, or things you own.

According to recent reports, most apps that pay you hand over small sums for spare minutes or spending you’d do anyway. By Survey Junkie’s own figures, three surveys a day earn as much as $40 a month, which works out to under $2 an hour by our math, since its surveys average 15 minutes.

Industry observers note that pay grows with what you put in. Uber drivers in Chicago earned a median of $27.97 an hour in fares, tips, and certain promotions from Oct. 5 to Nov. 2, 2026, before gas and other car costs. A private garage on Neighbor in Seattle lists for $333 a month on average. The picks below are the best apps for each way to earn.
