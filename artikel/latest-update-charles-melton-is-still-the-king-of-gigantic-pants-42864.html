Latest Update: Charles Melton Is Still the King of Gigantic Pants

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Charles Melton has long loved big, baggy pants. Back in 2024, the actor landed on GQ’s annual list of best pants thanks to one extremely voluminous pair from the Japanese label Sillage. And if his fits this fall are any indication, Melton won’t be stepping away from the assertively wide bottoms anytime soon. In fact, he keeps wearing one fantastically huge pair after another.

Sources indicate that the Her Private Hell star’s latest giant-trouser run kicked off in mid-October at the star-studded Academy Gala in Los Angeles. While A-list dressers like A$AP Rocky and Jacob Elordi showed up in respectfully roomy pants, Melton upped the ante in an ERL tuxedo that left no doubt about his dedication to the slouch. His cropped-front tailcoat was neat as a pin and fit like a glove, but his slacks were dramatically roomy and artfully rumpled—parachute pants gone black tie.

Later that same month, he popped up in Texas for the F1 Grand Prix in a very Fashion Dude outfit: Louis Vuitton workwear jacket, sporty sunglasses, bucket hat, and some choice Wales Bonner x Adidas Sambas. And on his legs? A twisted-seam silhouette billowy enough to make a scrum of downtown stylists and creative directors blush. ​.

But Melton saved his biggest look of all for last week in New York. At a VIP dinner for the South Korean eyewear label Gentle Monster, he donned a matching set from Viviano, the Tokyo-based label from designer Viviano Sue. Cut from gabardine as dark as a moonless night, both his Harrington jacket and balloon trousers looked as though they’d been blown up to the size of a weather balloon and then deflated, leaving behind all the crinkles and creases. He contrasted the dramatic ensemble with a pair of tiny oval sunglasses, which capped the whole thing off just right.

Industry observers note that even as menswear’s ideal pant fit moves towards a relaxed straight leg, some guys who dove deep into the baggy waters aren’t quite ready to give their roomy fits up just yet. Melton clearly loves the look and wears it well. And when your gigantic trousers help you stand out in a crowd as effortlessly and stylishly as his do, why bother changing?
