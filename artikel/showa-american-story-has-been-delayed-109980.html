If you were feeling like today was a good day for killing some zombies in a Japanese-American setting as a teenage girl brought back from the dead, this might not be a happy day for you, as Showa American Story has been pushed back for a later release.

The game was originally revealed by NEKCOM back in January 2022, known as Project SAS. While it had no initial release date, the game was later unveiled (now with its proper name) for the PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Steam. As production went on, the team had to let go of the former version, and after some more delays, it’s now projected to come out officially in 2027.

The announcement was made through NEKCOM’s social media, where they confirmed the official launch for October 28, 2027. The announcement reaffirms the team’s focus on “polishing and optimizing the experience” for the title before its official release. This has been a frequent move by developers over the last few years, and probably for the best.

Notable titles that went through the same way include Assassin’s Creed Shadows, Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, and, of course, GTA 6, which all took some extra time to guarantee a perfectly-tuned experience for the players. And with so many Easter eggs and secrets to discover in some of those games, it’s hard to say this was a bad call.

It has been revealed that showa American Story had its release date first revealed back in 2024, projected for a 2025 window. However, the game was pushed back to 2026 to ensure its development would go smoothly. And while it’s now being pushed back once again, we can probably rest assured that this should be the last time, as it now has a fixed date for its release.

The announcement reaffirms the team’s focus on ‘polishing and optimizing the experience’ for the title before its official release.

In a recent development, the Chinese RPG game was first conceptualized back in 2016, but only entered official development during this decade. It follows an alternative timeline where the United States has been heavily influenced by Japan, bringing a chaotic amalgamation of cultures to life. Oh, and we have zombies too.

According to recent reports, we follow the story of Chigusa Choko, a 19-year-old stuntwoman who has been brought back to life alongside hordes of undead. After awakening years after her death, in the so-called Showa era 66, Choko is looking for answers to why she is back in this period, and what has transpired to make the world fall into such disorder. It’s just your average zombie movie, but with some interesting quirks.

A good example is how Choko has some kind of unique power. Her physical capacities are greatly enhanced, and she can easily make her way through the waves of mindlessly acting bodies. But with a lot of gore, of course. From melee weapons to heavy gunfire, she has tons of ways to make quick work of her fellow undead.

But the main attraction here is the amazing “Showa America” culture, combining both of the best and the worst aspects of the Showa Era in Japan and our ever-loving American Dream in one. The 80s and 90s culture of both countries is presented as one, so it’s not uncommon to catch one of the other survivors in this apocalyptic setting to gush over their favorite anime right after saluting their flag.

According to recent reports, with your usual amount of special moves coming straight out of a B-movie, plenty of dark humor, and a lot of love for both the represented cultures, Showa American Story is definitely a title I’m excited to hear more about. And as the industry grows ever so demanding, I’m glad to see it’ll have some extra time to cook. It's done wonders for GTA, so I can’t complain about it now.
