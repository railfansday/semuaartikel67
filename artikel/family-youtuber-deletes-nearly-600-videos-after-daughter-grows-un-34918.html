A Japanese family YouTuber has deleted nearly 600 videos from her channel after her eldest daughter grew uncomfortable with people she had just met being able to watch her childhood online.

Industry observers note that the family paused its YouTube and social media activity in December 2025 after Okan confirmed that her eldest daughter had been smoking while underage. When Okan confirmed a return in May, she said her daughter had decided to step back from the videos, though viewers might still hear her voice in some footage.

The channel then went nearly six months without another upload.

By early August, the channel’s public video count had fallen from about 590 to 175. By early November, only the May upgrade remained, according to Japanese YouTube news outlet Yutura.

Industry observers note that in her November 17 update, Okan remarked her eldest daughter had grown embarrassed by the idea of people she had just met seeing videos from when she was little. Okan also cited stricter YouTube policies as a factor in the decision. “We deleted the videos out of respect for my daughter’s feelings,” she said, according to Yutura’s account of the video.

Recent findings suggest that okan now expects her eldest daughter to stop appearing on social media altogether. She and Yota have also decided when they’ll stop showing their younger daughter’s face, though Okan kept that date private. She looks to keep making everyday vlogs after that point.
