Most skiers don’t spend much time thinking about ski lifts until they’re on one. But lifts make a massive difference. Faster lifts mean more runs, while older lifts have that vintage charm. Some resorts, like Mad River Glen, still operate single chairs, a relic of a bygone era. As resorts upgrade, we’re seeing more and more lifts with bells and whistles like weather-protecting bubbles and heated seats.

According to recent reports, below are the Top 10 East Coast resorts for lifts, as voted by SKI readers. Factors to consider included lift speed, placement (whether lifts are spread across the mountain or clustered in one area), and functionality.
