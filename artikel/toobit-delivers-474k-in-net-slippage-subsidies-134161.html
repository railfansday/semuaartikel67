Toobit, the award-winning global cryptocurrency exchange, today reports that its zero-slippage trading service delivered 474,904 USDT in net slippage subsidies between July 1 and October 27, 2026, with 3,766 trading accounts receiving positive subsidies during the period.

Industry observers note that slippage is an often-overlooked trading cost that occurs when an order is filled at a different price than expected. Unlike trading fees, slippage can be difficult to anticipate, particularly during rapid market movements. By reducing unfavorable price differences, traders can better manage their trading costs and keep more of their potential returns.

In a recent development, to help traders address this challenge, Toobit offers its guaranteed price (GTD price) feature for eligible perpetual futures orders. The feature supports take-profit (TP) and stop-loss (SL) settings, with GTD TP targeting fills at the trigger price or better and GTD SL helping protect against unfavorable slippage.

For futures traders, this protection can be particularly valuable during volatile conditions. A conventional stop-loss market order may fill at a price below its trigger when prices fall rapidly, increasing losses beyond the intended level. Guaranteed price helps address this risk by allowing eligible stop-loss orders to close at their preset prices.

Sources indicate that traders can access guaranteed price on supported perpetual futures pairs, subject to eligibility requirements, fees, and execution exceptions. Traders can explore Toobit Futures or learn more through the guaranteed price feature guide.
