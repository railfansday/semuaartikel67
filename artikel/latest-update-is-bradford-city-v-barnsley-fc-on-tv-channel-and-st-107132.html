Michael Duff’s Bantams will be looking to climb into a stronger play-off spot with a victory on their home turf, while Barnsley are going to be desperate to escape the foot of the League One table.

Previewing the game against his former club, Duff said: “Barnsley didn't look like a wounded animal on Friday, but a football team with loads of energy and good players.

It has been revealed that "I don't think we're at a point now where we can assume that, well, ‘we're just better than them'. Everything that's got us, this football club, the team, the squad, to where it has and the success it's had, is because it's had that underdog mentality. Here are the key TV and streaming details as Bradford and Barnsley prepare to lock horns.

Kick-off at Valley Parade is scheduled for 7:45pm tonight (Tuesday, November 24).

Recent findings suggest that yes - those without a ticket can watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions are available online.

Supporters can stream via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Leeds and BBC Radio Sheffield.
