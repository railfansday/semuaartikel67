Grand Theft Auto 6 has plenty of fun Easter eggs--many of which tie back to earlier games in the series--but you can also find a few nods to other megapopular game franchises hiding within the vast map of Leonida. If you take a detour along the north side of Lummox County, you'll come across an area in the woods that looks like it'd be right at home in a Super Mario Bros. game, and you can even collect two distinct caps for your troubles--which might suggest that the greater Mario multiverse theory is true after all. How to find the GTA 6 Super Mario Bros. Easter egg in GTA 6 You can mark this location on your map, as you can find the reference to the Nintendo mascot in Lummox County. It's up north from Mount Kalaga, at the end of a twisty road that seemingly goes nowhere: Hit the bong, dude Once you've arrived, scout the area for a purple tarp that has a bong sitting on it. Smoke it, get high, and climb the structure in front of you. You'll notice that there's a plume of smoke coming out of the one column, and if you stand in it, an invisible block will appear above your head. Smash the block Jump up, and you'll smash the block. You won't get any gold coins for your trouble, but you will get a nostalgic tune and two caps that reference Nintendo's favorite plumbers, Mario and Luigi. Collect the caps Each cap has an initial embroidered on it: "J" for Jason, "L" for Lucia. You can add them to your collection, or leave them to gather dust; it's up to you. As mentioned above, GTA 6 has references to a lot of pop culture. Someone at Rockstar Games is clearly a huge Dexter fan, Bigfoot is on the prowl, and I really wish I'd chosen the elevator without a killer clown inside it.
