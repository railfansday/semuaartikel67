Inter throw away three two-goal leads at Atalanta as Juventus stay eight points back

It has been revealed that inter drew 4-4 at Bergamo after leading 2-0, 3-1 and 4-2, and dropped to third behind Lazio and Roma. Juventus sit eight points behind them before Thursday's Europa League game with Omonia and Sunday's trip to Como.

It has been revealed that inter led 2-0, then 3-1, then 4-2 at the Newly introduced Balance Arena on Sunday night. They still left Bergamo with a single point. The 4-4 draw with Atalanta left Chivu’s side third, three points behind leaders Lazio on 32 and now overtaken by Roma on 30.

Recent findings suggest that behind them, Milan have 27 and Como 26. Inter’s advantage over Juventus stands at eight points. The top of the table is spreading out, and Inter’s slip leaves three clubs within three points of each other at the summit.

Esposito gave Inter a two-goal cushion with his first two goals of the night. Elmas pulled one back for Atalanta on 35 minutes. Esposito restored the margin seven minutes after half-time to complete his hat-trick.

Atalanta played with a late change in goal. Sportiello started because Carnesecchi had to pull out during the warm-up. De Ketelaere cut the gap on 72 minutes. Four minutes later Zappacosta handled a Thuram shot on the goal line. Inter’s advantage was back to two goals once Sučić beat Sportiello with the resulting penalty.

De Ketelaere struck for a second time on 81 minutes. Bastoni’s handball then gave Atalanta a stoppage-time penalty, and Kessié converted it.

Chivu stated his side had failed the exam in the final minutes. He called the result “two gifted points”. Esposito now has six goals this season, and no Inter player has scored more in Serie A.

Inter also lost Hakan Çalhanoğlu. He came off in the 33rd minute with a problem in the hamstring of his right thigh and is due to have tests on Tuesday 24 November, Sky Sport reported. He will miss Wednesday’s Champions League game against Stuttgart and Saturday’s league match with Genoa at San Siro. It is his second muscle injury of the season, after a layoff of about 20 days in September.

Recent findings suggest that juventus arrive at their own busy week in form. All four goals against Venezia came after half-time, from Woltemade, Koopmeiners, Cabal and a Kolo Muani penalty. The win came in Luciano Spalletti’s 600th Serie A game as a coach.

The Europa League comes first. Kick-off for Thursday’s league-phase meeting at the Allianz Stadium is at 21:00. Three days later they visit Como, one of the clubs above them, in round 13 on Sunday 29 November 2026 at 20:45.

It has been revealed that the direct test against Inter is further away. The league meeting between the two clubs is set for Sunday 10 January 2027 at 20:45.
