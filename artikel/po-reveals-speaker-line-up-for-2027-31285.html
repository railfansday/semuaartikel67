P&O Cruises has revealed that it will welcome a line-up of adventurers, broadcasters, sporting icons and storytellers on three of its longer cruises in 2027.

Guests sailing on the Arcadia’s world cruise, the Aurora’s grand tour and the Iona’s 35-night cruise between January and April will hear first-hand stories from remarkable guest speakers.

Carnival UK & P&O Cruises president Paul Ludlow noted: “Our longer holidays give guests the time to explore more of the world and these remarkable speakers will bring another dimension to that sense of discovery.”.

The company said in a press release that the Arcadia guests will hear from Dale Templar, the BAFTA and Emmy-winning wildlife producer and director behind the BBC’s Human Planet.

He will be joined by Rebecca Stephens MBE, the first British woman to climb Mount Everest and complete the Seven Summits and Zelda la Grange, who will share stories from her 19 years working alongside Nelson Mandela.

The line-up also includes Australian rugby icon David Campese; photography industry veteran and Getty Images executive Carmin Romanelli; best-selling author and travel writer Sue Williams and much-loved gardening presenter Charlie Dimmock.

On the Aurora’s grand tour, Royal Marine veteran and adventurer Mitchell Hutchcraft will share the story of his 9,000-mile human-powered journey from England to the summit of Everest.

TV producer, director and expedition organizer Russ Malkin will take guests behind the scenes of adventures filmed across more than 160 countries.

Recent findings suggest that also joining the Aurora will be engineer, doctor and astronaut Robert Thirsk; Indianapolis 500 racing driver Lyn St. James; Olympic gold medalist Dr Rochelle Stevens; and film critic, author and broadcaster James King.

Industry observers note that the Iona’s 35-night cruise will welcome Olympic, World and Commonwealth champion Christine Ohuruogu. She will be joined by long-serving royal correspondent Simon Vigar and former SAS soldier Colin Maclachlan, who will share insights from his career and experiences in the special forces.

The special guest program will run from January to April 2027, with individual speakers joining selected sections of Arcadia’s world cruise, Aurora’s grand tour and Iona’s 35-night cruise.
