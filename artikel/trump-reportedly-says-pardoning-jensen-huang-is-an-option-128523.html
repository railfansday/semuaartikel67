During an interview with the Newly introduced York Post on Tuesday, President Trump mentioned that pardoning Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who has been ensnared in a scandal involving an alleged Chinese spy, is “an option,” albeit one that he doesn’t think he will have to exercise.

“It’s always an option, I guess, but I don’t think it’ll be necessary,” the president reportedly told the outlet.

Industry observers note that huang, who was ousted from his own company less than two weeks ago, was reinstated Monday. He faces ongoing questions about his relationship with Alice Huang, an executive with alleged ties to Chinese intelligence who previously worked for a company that has been accused of illegally diverting Nvidia chips to China.

Huang is scheduled to testify at a public Senate Banking Committee hearing next week.

When asked about the hearing, Trump reportedly mentioned: “I think I want him to just tell the truth, and I mean, his lawyers are going to have to advise him, but I’ve always found him to be 100% aboveboard, and you know a very smart person.”.

According to recent reports, at another point in the interview, when asked what he knew about Huang’s other extramarital affairs, Trump reportedly remarked: “I really didn’t get into that. I got into the business of chips, much more than the business of sex.”.

TechCrunch has reached out to the White House for comment.
