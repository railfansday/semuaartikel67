The ultimate guide to Southwest Airlines credit card eligibility

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (194 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Southwest offers five co-branded cards—three personal and two business—each with distinct perks and eligibility rules.
  • Approval and welcome bonus eligibility are influenced by general application restrictions, including a limit on recent card openings and specific rules for Southwest cards.
  • Personal Southwest cards generally restrict you to one at a time, with bonus eligibility limited if you've received a bonus in the past 24 months.
  • Business cards have separate eligibility, allowing you to hold both types if you meet requirements.
  • Eligibility for personal cards requires being under the five-card limit for new accounts in 24 months and not currently holding or recently earning a bonus from another personal Southwest card.
  • Business card eligibility is separate, but you cannot hold or have recently earned a bonus from the same business card within 24 months; holding one business card does not affect eligibility for the other.
  • Welcome bonuses on these cards will no longer count toward earning a Companion Pass after Dec. 2, making timing crucial for those seeking this benefit.
