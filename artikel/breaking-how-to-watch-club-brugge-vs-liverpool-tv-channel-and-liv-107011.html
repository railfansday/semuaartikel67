Liverpool look to return to winning ways in the Champions League with a visit to Belgium to face Club Brugge on Wednesday night.

Andoni Iraola’s Reds lost 3-2 in Turkey last time out in Europe, ending up on the wrong side of a topsy-turvy game with Fenerbahce.

Defeat to Fenerbahce ended a run of three wins to start Liverpool’s Champions League campaign.

The Reds responded to that loss with Premier League victories over Crystal Palace and Manchester United either side of the international break, but Iraola’s Liverpool face a Club Brugge side that have already claimed major scalps in the Champions League this season.

Iraola will be without two key men for the match, though, with both Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch left out of the travelling squad.

After starting the league phase with a 3-2 loss to Aston Villa, Club Brugge stunned Inter Milan 4-3 at the San Siro - before wins over Lens and PSV Eindhoven.

Industry observers note that TV channel: In the UK, the game will be televised live on TNT Sports. Coverage starts at 7pm GMT on TNT Sports 2 ahead of kick-off at 8pm.
