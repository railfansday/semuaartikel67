Worthing enter the competition at the next stage along with all their National League Premier counterparts – and it is one of them, Forest Green Rovers, who they will welcome to Woodside Road for the tie.

The Rebels are at the moment nine places above Rovers in the table but lost 2-1 at home to them in Woodside Road’s first ever game at step one in August so weill be out to put that right – along with the memory of FGR’s FA Cup win at Worthing last October in the first game after Adam Hinshelwood returned to the dugout.

Industry observers note that horsham have been drawn at home to Hashtag United – a side who now play two divisions below the Hornets. Hashtag are plying their trade in the Isthmian North division this season after relegation from step three last season and are doing well – sitting third at present.

But after Dominic Di Paola’s side put out Torquay in round two on Saturday with an excellent 1-0 victory, they will start as favourites in the next round.

Only one of the surviving Sussex trio are on the road – Eastbourne Borough are off to Oxford City, who play one step above the sports in National League North.

Borough reached the next round with a last-gasp Tyano Wilson winner that saw them beat Chertsey 2-1 at Priory Lane on Saturday.

There’s plenty of incentive for all teams taking part in the third round – winners at that stage pick up £4,500 prize money, with losers receiving £1,250 each.

Victory also puts teams into the last 32 (fourth round) – when dreams of Wembley might start to take shape.

Ties in round three are due to take place on Saturday, December 12.

Missing out on a third round spot are Chichester City, Burgess Hill and Whitehawk, who all lost their second round ties at the weekend.
