Latest Update: Prediction: Crystal Palace vs Real Sociedad

Thomas Frank takes charge of his first home match as Crystal Palace manager on Thursday night when the Eagles welcome Real Sociedad to Selhurst Park in the Europa League.

The two sides are level on five points after four league-phase matches, having each recorded one win, two draws and one defeat. With Crystal Palace sitting 21st and Real Sociedad 20th in the standings, both clubs know a victory could provide a significant boost to their hopes of reaching the knockout rounds.

Crystal Palace are still searching for an upturn in fortunes after a difficult season that has left them bottom of the Premier League table.

According to recent reports, thomas Frank’s first game in charge ended in a 3-2 defeat away to Coventry City, extending Palace’s losing streak in the league to five matches. Although Frank praised the effort and passion shown by his players, the Eagles have now suffered eight defeats from their opening 11 league fixtures, with meagre seven points on the board.

Defensive problems have been a recurring theme throughout the campaign. Only Manchester United have conceded more goals across Europe’s top five leagues this season, with Palace shipping 33 goals in all competitions. They have also conceded at least twice in 11 of their 17 matches.

Recent findings suggest that european football has at least offered some encouragement. The reigning Conference League champions are unbeaten in their last eight major European home matches, winning five and drawing three. Frank also boasts a strong home record in Europe from his previous spells with Tottenham Hotspur and Brondby, suffering just one defeat in nine such games.

However, Palace have failed to win any of their last three Europa League matches. Defeats to Lyon and draws against Besiktas and Hoffenheim have left them outside the top places, although Daichi Kamada’s dramatic 91st-minute equaliser against Hoffenheim earned a valuable point three weeks ago.

With only two points separating them from the top eight, Thursday’s match could prove crucial in the race for automatic qualification to the last 16.

Crystal Palace remain without Chadi Riad, Ismaila Sarr and Takehiro Tomiyasu through injury, while Oscar Mingueza is a doubt after picking up a knock at the weekend.

Yeremy Pino and Jean-Philippe Mateta are suspended domestically and should therefore start in Europe, but midfielder Adam Wharton is unavailable after accumulating yellow cards in the competition.

Frank switched to a four-man defence against Coventry and could keep that system in place. Jaydee Canvot, Honest Ahanor and Axel Disasi are competing for a place alongside Chris Richards in central defence, while Ben Chilwell could replace Tyrick Mitchell at left-back.

Real Sociedad have experienced a mixed return to European football after missing out on continental competition last season.

Recent findings suggest that the Spanish side started their Europa League campaign with a 2-1 home defeat against Bournemouth before drawing with Union SG and Lyon. Their standout result came on matchday three when they thrashed Lillestrom 6-0 away from home.

In a recent development, domestically, Pellegrino Matarazzo’s side have also struggled for consistency. Over their last 10 matches in all competitions, they have recorded five wins, two draws and three defeats. Most recently, La Real suffered a 3-2 loss away to Racing Santander in La Liga.

Despite that setback, Real Sociedad remain fifth in La Liga and are still chasing a top-four place, although they right now sit five points adrift. Vital matches against Sevilla, Real Madrid and Villarreal are looming, but their immediate focus is on strengthening their Europa League position.

The visitors are aiming to reach the Europa League knockout rounds for the sixth time in six appearances since the competition was rebranded in 2009-10. Their record against English opposition, however, is not encouraging. Real Sociedad have won only one of their last 11 meetings with English clubs, a 1-0 victory away to Manchester United in the 2022-23 group stage. Matarazzo himself has managed only two wins from eight Europa League matches as a coach.

Alvaro Odriozola continues to recover from a long-term knee injury, while Ander Barrenetxea recently underwent surgery on a hamstring problem and faces an extended spell on the sidelines.

Jon Martin could come into central defence alongside Igor Zubeldia, replacing Chelsea loanee Mamadou Sarr. Hector Fort and Sergio Gomez are expected to feature as full-backs.

Recent findings suggest that captain Mikel Oyarzabal has yet to score in the Europa League this season, but he is expected to retain his place in attack ahead of Orri Oskarsson. He could be supported by Goncalo Guedes, Luka Sucic and Takefusa Kubo.

Both teams enter the contest looking to rebound from disappointing domestic defeats, and neither side can realistically afford another setback in Europe.

It has been revealed that crystal Palace’s defensive complications continue to undermine their performances, but their strong home record in continental competition offers encouragement. Real Sociedad have shown flashes of quality throughout the league phase, yet have struggled for consistency and historically have not enjoyed much success against English opponents.

With both teams carrying attacking threats and defensive vulnerabilities, this has the makings of an entertaining encounter that could produce goals at both ends.
