Industry observers note that it's promptly approaching 10 years since the FBI franchise first hit the air, and as the show continues to release new seasons, more and more fans are drawn into its intense storylines and endearing performances. The flagship FBI series first premiered in 2018, and now eight years later, it's still going strong in its ninth season. The first FBI spin-off, Most Wanted, debuted its first season in 2020, and the show was on the air for over 100 episodes before saying goodbye in 2025. FBI: International premiered in 2021, and while it didn't quite make it to 100 episodes, it also went off the air in 2025. This left only the original FBI series left standing on CBS, but the potential for more spin-offs — including the new hit CIA series — is endless.

Like many CBS shows, FBI went on hiatus last week following the premiere of its seventh episode, "Posterity," but the show is finally set to return on Monday with the eighth episode of Season 9, "22 Minutes." In next week's episode, when a DEA informant is murdered, the team must stop the local drug lord responsible for the heinous act before he takes out the only witness, who happens to be a 16-year-old girl. Meanwhile, Isobel pressures Maggie to pump the brakes on her relationship with OA following his return to 26 Fed.

Industry observers note that the latest episode is set to be released on Monday, November 30, at 8 p.m. EST. For those who don't want to wait that long, Collider is thrilled to partner with CBS to exclusively preview a new sneak peek at next week's episode, which captures one of the most intense moments from the episode, where the team decides that they can't wait for S.W.A.T. and must move in immediately.

According to recent reports, like all CBS highlights, upcoming episodes of FBI can be streamed the following day after they air on CBS on Paramount+. This means that this week's episode will be available to stream as soon as fans wake up on the morning of Tuesday, December 1. For those who enjoy FBI, there is no shortage of other shows like it to watch on Paramount+ and CBS as well, including Tracker (starring Justin Hartley) and NCIS: Origins (starring Austin Stowell).

In a recent development, check out next week's episode of FBI on Monday, November 30, at 8 p.m. EST, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of FBI Season 9.

FBI: A team of elite agents in the Upcoming York office of the Federal Bureau of Investigation uses their skills, intellect, and state-of-the-art technologies to keep the city and country safe from various threats, ranging from terrorism to organized crime. Each case challenges their expertise, dedication, and moral fortitude, as they navigate the complexities of law enforcement and justice.
