Dancing With the Stars pro Sharna Burgess shared how her beloved pet’s mystery illness has taken its toll on her family.

Sources indicate that “Knowing that our frenchie is terminal and his illness is a mystery,” Burgess, 41, wrote about her bulldog on Friday, November 20, when asked about her “pit” of the week in an Instagram Stories Q&A. “We are on borrowed time during everything we can.”.

In a recent development, she continued, “I’m just praying he holds out until Brian [Austin Green] gets home. He could also last a few months, we just don’t know. It’s up to him really.”.

Burgess, who has been engaged to Green, 53, since July 2023, has since tried to cope with her pet’s condition through self-care.

“[My] peak — getting a few hours of me time, reading in front of my bedroom fireplace with a cup of coffee and no one needing a single thing from me,” she noted on Friday of her weekly highlight. “Journaling and meditating for the first time in months.”.

Burgess is also in the throws of a busy week gearing up for the Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale.

Industry observers note that “For the pros competing, it’s wild and high stakes. You are the most exhausted, sad it’s coming to an end, depleted of everything you actually need to function as a human being but you have to do your best work of the season,” she noted in a follow-up Q&A slide about the chaotic finale prep. “Yet somehow, it’s also the time of your life. Eliminated pros? A million routines to learn and a crazy schedule.”.

She added, “We all just try and support each other through it in whatever way we can.”.

Burgess, who was eliminated from the competition with partner Tyler Cameron ahead of the semifinals, is gearing up to take the dance floor once more on the Tuesday, November 24, live show.

“Yes, but only one,” she teased of appearing in the finalists’ freestyles. “I have five performances for [the] finale.”.

According to recent reports, in addition to her dance schedule, Burgess and Green recently adopted another dog named Birdie. (Green, who shares four kids from previous relationships, and Burgess welcomed son Zane in 2022.).
