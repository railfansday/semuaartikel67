Mauricio Pochettino’s first home game as Manchester United boss sees them welcome Brentford to Old Trafford on Saturday, with the hosts desperate for three points.

Pochettino is still figuring out how best to set up his upcoming team, first opting for a back three in the 4-0 drubbing by Liverpool at Anfield last weekend before then switching to a four-man defence for the chaotic Champions League draw with Sporting on Wednesday.

Industry observers note that although Brentford will fancy their chances of victory based on the respective league positions of the two clubs - Man Utd 19th, the Bees in sixth - United will be urged by their fans to attack. As such, a more timid back three seems unlikely.

Sources indicate that it will be a case of balancing fitness levels with only two days of rest between games. Goalkeeper Senne Lammens has come under fire in recent weeks for his performances, but back-up Karl Darlow has struggled with injury since arriving in the summer.

According to recent reports, pochettino’s defensive options are fairly limited. Leny Yoro is available after his early red card against Liverpool was overturned, while Harry Maguire was restored to the starting XI for the Sporting game.

Brentford’s pace in behind may necessitate a quicker backline, meaning Maguire could be dropped once again here.

Lisandro Martinez has been at left-back, but Patrick Dorgu’s attacking qualities will likely be required on Saturday and Luke Shaw is also now back in training. By moving Martinez back to centre-half alongside Yoro, Pochettino can find the pace required.

Diogo Dalot may get the nod ahead of Noussair Mazraoui at right-back.

Pochettino has rapidly settled on a midfield partnership of Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans, who was controversially sent off late on against Sporting but is available in the Premier League.

It has been revealed that the attackers in front of them almost pick themselves - Matheus Cunha, Bryan Mbeumo and captain Bruno Fernandes will all be involved in some capacity. However, their exact positions are subject to change.

Fernandes was deployed on the left behind Cunha in the defeat by Liverpool, while Mbeumo was briefly used as the lone forward under Michael Carrick.

Here, we’re predicting Cunha to lead the line, with Fernandes in behind as the No10 and Mbeumo on the left wing.

Recent findings suggest that if Amad Diallo is fit enough to start after being forced off in the draw with Sporting, expect the Ivorian on the right flank. If not, youngster Shea Lacey could be a candidate to start.

Marcus Rashford and Benjamin Sesko remain injured, as do midfielders Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte.

Predicted Man Utd XI vs Brentford (4-2-3-1): Lammens; Dalot, Yoro, Martinez, Dorgu; Mainoo, Tielemans; Amad, Fernandes, Mbeumo; Cunha.

Injured: Rashford, Baleba, Ugarte, Sesko.
