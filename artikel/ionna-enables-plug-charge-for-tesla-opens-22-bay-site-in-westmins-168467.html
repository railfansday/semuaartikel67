IONNA, the DC charging operator backed by several major OEMs, has opened its largest DC charging site yet, with 22 bays of 400kW charging in Westminster, California.

Alongside the opening, IONNA is also enabling Plug & Charge operation for Tesla EVs, with Stellantis EVs “coming soon.”.

IONNA is a rapidly expanding DC charging network which is garnering good reviews from EV drivers, with clean, fast and easy to use charging stations (which it calls “Rechargeries”).

But until now, we haven’t had all that many chargers in the LA area. There are a couple in South OC and the Inland Empire, but nothing closer to the population center of the metro area (though more are opening soon).

That changes now with IONNA’s new flagship charging station in Westminster. It features 12 CCS connectors and 10 NACS ports, so you can charge just about any EV (no Chademo, nor can my AC-only Tesla Roadster charge here… ho hum).

It has been revealed that in addition to 22 chargers with peak 400kW capacity, the site features indoor and outdoor lounge space, restrooms, and other amenities like an In N Out and a taco shop across the street. (Not quite as full-featured as my favorite charging station, Rove, but IONNA has a much larger national footprint, and does have faster chargers).

Recent findings suggest that there’s also a small, convenience-store-like area that uses Amazon’s “just walk out” technology. You swipe a card to unlock the room, pick up any items you want, and just walk out. The many cameras on the ceiling track you (and whoever came in with you) and what you’re picking up, then charge your card for the items you took with you.

The store has snacks and drinks, though no ice cream, which is the main thing I’m looking for on a roadtrip stop… IONNA may convert one of the fridges to a freezer in the future, depending on how things go.

And, importantly, the site has trash cans and window wipers – just what you need on a roadtrip stop.

Speaking of roadtrips, the charger opening caps off a cross-country roadtrip by IONNA personnel, who set out in several cars from various parts of the country, all convening on the latest Rechargery in Westminster.

Industry observers note that over 20 EVs joined the roadtrip and drove over 40,000 combined miles on their trip to Westminster. The teams charged at approximately 30% of the company’s charging sites, showing just how easy it is to do roadtrips in an EV.

The Rechargery opens tomorrow in Westminster, at 6260 Westminster Blvd, just off the 405 freeway. Look for the big sign. For the first weeks, you can charge at a promotional rate of $0.20/kWh. Afterwards, IONNA will charge its standard rate of $0.39/kWh.

Perhaps more importantly than a new charger opening, though, is IONNA’s announcement today that Teslas can now use its network with Plug & Charge functionality (well, technically Autocharge, which is basically the same thing).

Window wipers and fluid are an underrated missing feature at EV stations, hope to see more of this.

What this means is that you can link your car and credit card ahead of time (via the Presto charging app), then simply plug in at any IONNA station and start charging, without having to tap or swipe or open an app. This is similar to the experience Teslas have always had on Superchargers, so should be familiar to Tesla owners.

The functionality is rolled out across all of IONNA’s network, starting today. So you should be able to test it out on your Tesla vehicle. IONNA already supports Plug & Charge for BMW, GM, Hyundai, Kia, Mercedes-Benz, Toyota, Rivian, Ford, Porsche and Volvo EVs.

Further, IONNA claims support is “coming soon” for Stellantis EVs (Jeep, Dodge, Ram, Fiat), stating that those cars will be able to Plug & Charge on its network in December. It now supports Autocharge for about two thirds of the EVs on American roads.
