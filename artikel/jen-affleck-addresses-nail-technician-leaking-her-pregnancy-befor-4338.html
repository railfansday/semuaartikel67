Jen Affleck‘s nail tech Kortney Poulson broke her silence about allegedly leaking her client’s pregnancy — and the reality star swiftly weighed in.

During season 1 of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, which is streaming now, Affleck, 27, learned on camera that Poulson had told others that the Hulu personality was pregnant with baby No. 4.

“I just wanna briefly address everything that’s being said online. Unfortunately, there’s a lot of context that is missing from the show,” Poulson said in an Instagram video on Saturday, November 14. “I am accepting that I’m going to have to live with people forming opinions about me without knowing the full story.”.

Poulson made it clear that she was on good terms with Affleck.

“What matters to me most is that Jen and I are good. I’ve worked incredibly hard to build my business and earn the trust of my clients, would never carelessly jeopardize that or everything I’ve built,” she continued. “The people and clients that know me personally know that I take client confidentiality extremely seriously. That’s all I’m going to say about the situation.”.

In a recent development, affleck shared Poulsen’s statement on her Instagram Story, adding, “Kortney has done my nails for years, and I have nothing but love for her. I think there’s a lot of context missing, and ultimately, this was a huge misunderstanding with no ill intent.”.

She concluded: “Anyone who truly knows Kortney knows the kind of person she is. She’s built the clientele she has for a reason, and the love and loyalty of her clients speak for themselves.”.

Season 1 of the Hulu series reintroduced Jen, who first starred on the original Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, and followed her as she tried to end her marriage to Zac Affleck. While Jen didn’t file for divorce on the show, news broke days before Mormon Wives: OC premiered that she had taken the steps to legally part ways with Zac.

“The same reason why I filed for divorce is the same reason why it was really hard,” Jen exclusively told Us.

Industry observers note that jen recalled reconsidering her marriage after she became pregnant with baby No. 4.

“It was this fourth child. I had to ask myself very hard questions like, ‘Is this the type of relationship I want this child to look up to?'” she explained. “When I realized it was a no, that’s when I realized that I needed to make a change. You’ll see a lot of that unfold in season [1 of the spinoff].”.

Despite attempts to fix their relationship, Jen and Zac continued to face problems.
