Juventus boss Luciano Spalletti will take charge of his 600th Serie A match as a manager against Venezia, and he is approaching it the same way he did his first.

Spalletti will become the sixth manager to reach this milestone in the competition's history, after Carlo Mazzone (792), Nereo Rocco (747), Giovanni Trapattoni (689), Gian Piero Gasperini (648) and Nils Liedholm (635).

Juventus have had a topsy-turvy season so far, but are unbeaten in their last two league matches, twice coming from behind to draw 2-2 with Fiorentina following a 2-0 win over Napoli.

And Spalletti will be keen to mark his landmark match with a victory.

"I am preparing with the same emotion as I did for my first in Serie A," he said about his landmark match.

"There were moments during this journey that marked my career as a coach. I'm very happy to have had this tour in football, because it was my life and what I always wished for.

"I learned to be happy even when things are not going in my favour, and I hope to keep going for as long as possible.".

Juventus sit seventh in the league with just 18 points after 11 games, with suggestions they could miss out on Champions League qualification for the second season running, but Spalletti is not yet worried.

"I do think we have that possibility, but our objective is to become something," Spalletti said when asked about their start to the season.

Sources indicate that "If we do that, then we can get good results and a strong position in the table. We have to rely on our work; we want to create habits for constant improvement.".

It is fitting that Spalletti's landmark match is against Venezia, whom he coached at the start of his career, though he was sacked twice in the space of one season in 1999.

Sunday's visitors to the Allianz Stadium sit 19th, with only six points so far, and they have lost each of their last six Serie A away games, but Spalletti had high praise for the team.

"I have fond memories of a melancholic but splendid city," Spalletti added of his time at Venezia. "Those were my first seasons. I had strong Presidential and directorial figures; the team was struggling, so I was fired and brought back.

"I want to congratulate Davide Nicola, as wherever he goes in his career, he brings a solution to problems and fuels enthusiasm. They'll try to strangle us and then send strong strikers on the attack like Akor Adams, so balance is essential.".

After an assist against Fiorentina and a goal and an assist against AZ, Randal Kolo Muani could be involved in a goal in three consecutive club appearances in all competitions for the first time since between May and June 2025.

That was also for Juventus when he got a goal against Venezia in Serie A, a brace against Al Ain and an assist against Wydad Casablanca in the Club World Cup.

Since Serie A returned to a 20-team format in 2004-05, Antoine Hainaut (four – two goals, two assists) is the first Venezia defender to have a goal involvement in more than three goals in a single top-flight season.

He set up Toma Basic's opener against Udinese before the international break, but also led that game in shots (four) and touches in the opposition box (six), alongside his two chances created.

Juventus are unbeaten in their last 14 Serie A matches against Venezia (W11 D3), with the Winged Lions' last victory against the Bianconeri dating back to April 1962 (3-0 at the Penzo).

In fact, Juventus have never lost in 14 home Serie A games against Venezia (W11 D3).

Among the teams currently in the top-flight, Venezia are one of only two sides against whom Juventus have always remained unbeaten in home league matches, along with Frosinone (W2 D1).

Following their recent success against Udinese, Venezia could win two consecutive Serie A matches for the first time since November 2021 (against Roma and Bologna on that occasion).

But Juventus have picked up 22 points from a possible 24 in their last eight Serie A matches against newly promoted sides (W7 D1), scoring 21 goals in the process, and conceding just one goal in their most recent six games against such teams.
