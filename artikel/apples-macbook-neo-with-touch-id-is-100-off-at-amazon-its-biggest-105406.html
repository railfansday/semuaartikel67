Sources indicate that we were on hand when Apple launched its first-ever budget laptop in New York City this spring, and we (rightfully) predicted it was going to become a bestseller. The Neo put the entire Windows laptop world on notice with its speedy iPhone chip and elite build quality. And — bonus — it comes in fun colors.

According to recent reports, an upgraded MacBook Neo with 512GB of storage, Touch ID, and a "blush" pink finish is now $100 off during Amazon's Black Friday Week sale. Not only does this deal completely erase the Neo's price hike from June, but it also beats the Apple Store's education pricing for the Neo — that's never happened before. (We expect this deal to sell out fast, as no other retailer is matching it.).

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, the base MacBook Neo with 256GB of storage is $50 off at Amazon in all four colors (blush, citrus, indigo, and silver). In a recent development, this price drop couldn't come at a better time. Mashable recently named the Neo a Gift of the Year winner. If a teen or younger student you know needs a newly introduced laptop, we think they'll be delighted to unwrap this one. It's just as well-made as other MacBooks, has an all-day battery life (it lasted exactly 14 hours and 50 minutes in our battery rundown test), and runs basic tasks faster than most premium Windows laptops we've tried.

As we said when it originally launched, the "MacBook Neo is a shot across the bow of the Windows laptop market." To quote our tech editor, "This colorful budget laptop brings the MacBook's premium design elements and user experience to a whole fresh customer base.".

"I was able to edit a simple, cheesy video in Final Cut Pro using the MacBook Neo without struggling against the spinning wheel of death.
