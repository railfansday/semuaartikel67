Industry observers note that you might have noticed, but we make kind of a significant deal of Black Friday. We run 100s of Black Friday pages, showcasing the best deals on everything from streaming services to Apple tech. We generally focus on online shopping, but believe it or not, some people still shop in store. Crazy, right?

There are many reasons to actually visit a store this week. Of course, you can find Black Friday deals on some of the items on your wishlist. But you can also walk away with store freebies offered as an incentive to get you off your phone and into a brick-and-mortar shop.

Recent findings suggest that we're seeing limited-time offers from popular spots like Lowe's and Kohl's. We've scanned the market to line up a selection of the best store freebies offered by leading retailers over Black Friday. Check out our favorites below:.

Recent findings suggest that shoppers visiting Bass Pro Shops or Cabela’s can score free spending money on Nov. 27. The first 250 guests (ages 16 and older) through the doors at each location will receive a free promo card valued between $10 and $500 on a first-come, first-served basis. These promo cards cannot be spent on Black Friday and become valid starting Dec. 6.

Select Fleet Farm locations are hosting annual Orange Friday doorbuster events, handing out a free 2026 Fleet Farm collector’s hat alongside mystery scratch-off tickets worth up to 50% off an in-store purchase. Check your local store’s schedule before making the drive.

JCPenney is spacing out its Black Friday in-store freebies across four separate windows on Nov. 27, starting with a free mini Hogwarts Express snow globe for early doorbusters. If you time your trip right, you also have a shot at four rotating prize giveaways throughout the day:.

5 a.m.: $500 off a $500 purchase coupon.

9 a.m.: Free Ninja Dual Basket Air Fryer.

1 p.m.: Free lab-grown diamond stud earrings.

5 p.m.: Free Samsonite 21-inch hardside luggage.

Sources indicate that on Nov. 27, the first 200 customers through the doors at participating Kohl’s stores will receive a free Kohl’s Cash voucher worth anywhere from $5 to $100. Kohl’s is then running a second wave on Nov. 28, giving the first 200 in-store shoppers a free coupon worth $5 to $100 off at Sephora at Kohl’s.

Lowe’s is officially bringing back its viral Black Friday bucket giveaway on Nov. 27 for MyLowe’s Rewards members (it's free to join). Last year, the first 50 loyalty members at each store received a five-gallon bucket packed with free tools and home goodies, with select winners also receiving a certificate for a free major appliance valued at up to $2,000. Lowe’s has confirmed the giveaway returns this year, with exact bucket quantities and contents scheduled to be revealed on Nov. 24.

In a recent development, on Nov. 27, Michaels is giving away 500 free Countdown to Craftsmas Advent Calendars per store with no purchase required while supplies last. Each calendar offers daily December crafts, decorating ideas, and store coupons. Select boxes pack major cash prizes, including one $100 Michaels gift card at every store plus one $10,000 grand prize nationwide.

On Nov. 27, the first 100 guests in line at participating stores will receive a giveaway ticket redeemable for a free Target Gift Card worth $25, $50, or $100 — plus one lucky shopper at every store will walk away with a $1,000 Target Gift Card.
