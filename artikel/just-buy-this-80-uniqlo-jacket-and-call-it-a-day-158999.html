Sources indicate that we know what you're thinking: You just told me ALL the Black Friday deals are here, GQ, so why the heck are you redirecting my attention to a single, solitary jacket? Here's why: Uniqlo’s cropped, collared jacket—or blouson, if you’re fancy—is not like all other Black Friday deals.

For one, the Japanese mega-retailer has the slightly annoying, slightly endearing habit of refusing to display the original prices of its discounted products, so we have no clue how staggering the deal is. For another, it’s not just a single, solitary jacket: it’s practically a workweek’s worth of ‘em, from the no-frills zip-up joint that first piqued our interest to its cozy, padded counterpart—similarly collared, similarly cropped—we’re eyeing wistfully as the mercury plunges. (There’s also a duo of handsome-as-hell options from the brand’s ongoing link-up with JW Anderson, one of them done up in an especially gentlemanly guncheck plaid.).

But back to that original jacket. It looks like something a brooding Harrison Ford might've worn to a red-carpet premiere in the gloriously unmediated pre-iPhone ‘90s. It’s on sale for $80. (A couple of its counterparts mentioned above are even $70.) And it’s available in a slew of can’t-fail autumnal hues, including a muddy shade of brown that’ll look killer with the corduroys you just bought, and an inky shade of blue that’ll look equally righteous with faded jeans.

It has been revealed that so don't miss your chance to cop this offensively affordable fall upgrade. We’re not suggesting you ignore the rest of the Black Friday hullabaloo entirely (though more power to you—and your tryptophan-addled psyche—if you do). We're just asking you to consider ignoring the rest of the Black Friday hullabaloo in favor of a sick jacket we guarantee you'll wear non-stop for the next month.

According to recent reports, the boxy, relaxed fit is dialed enough to sync up with your hardiest overshirts, baggiest pants, and heftiest loafers. The neutral colorways are simple enough to sync up with everything else already in your closet. Oh, and did we mention it's only $80?
