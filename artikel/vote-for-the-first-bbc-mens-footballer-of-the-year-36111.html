Voting is now open for the BBC Men's Footballer of the Year 2026 award, which will be presented at Sports Personality of the Year.

Fans from the UK can vote from the five-player shortlist of Jude Bellingham, Harry Kane, Kylian Mbappe, Rodri and Lamine Yamal.

The vote closes on Friday, 27 November at 09:00 GMT.

The winner of the award - new for this year - will be presented during the live Sports Personality of the Year show on Sunday, 13 December.

Alex Kay-Jelski, Director of BBC Sport, noted: "From the drama and excitement of the men's World Cup to the moments that have captivated fans across the domestic and international game, 2026 has given us so much to celebrate.

"Our inaugural BBC Men's Footballer of the Year award is about recognising the players who have made this year so special, inspired fans and left their mark on the game.

"It's a fantastic shortlist, and I can't wait to see who the public choose as our first-ever winner.".

The shortlist was selected by an expert panel comprising former international players and some of the BBC's most respected football voices, bringing together a wealth of experience and insight from across the game.

The panel included Cesar Azpilicueta (Spain), Scott Brown (Scotland), Olivier Giroud (France), Shay Given (Republic of Ireland), Hassan Kachloul (Morocco), Lucas Leiva (Brazil), Benni McCarthy (South Africa), Alan Shearer, Ellen White (both England) and Ashley Williams (Wales).

Meet the SPOTY Men's Footballer of the Year contenders.

Club: Real Madrid Position: Midfield.

Bellingham made history this summer as he became the first Englishman to score as many as seven goals at a major tournament.

In a superb World Cup for the midfielder, he memorably netted twice in back-to-back games to help the Three Lions beat Mexico and Norway.

That meant he joined exalted tech giant as the first player to score two or more goals in successive World Cup knockout games since Diego Maradona in 1986, and the second youngest in history behind Pele in 1958.

Bellingham also became the youngest European player to make an appearance at four different major tournaments, and also reached the milestone of 50 caps at a younger age than any other England men's player.

The goals continued to flow for Real Madrid, too, with Bellingham notching his 50th for the club to move above the legendary Zinedine Zidane in the Los Blancos' all-time scoring charts.

Bellingham was recently joined in the England squad by his brother Jobe.

Club: Bayern Munich Position: Forward.

In a stunning year of goalscoring, Kane become the first Englishman for 25 years (and only fifth in history) to win the Ballon d'Or - awarded to the world's best footballer.

His tally of 73 goals in 65 matches for club and country during a stellar 2025-26 season has only been surpassed in one of Europe's top five leagues by Lionel Messi's 82 for Barcelona and Argentina in 2011-12.

Kane scored 61 of those goals in just 51 appearances for Bayern Munich as he helped the Bavarians to a league and cup double. His total was 19 more than any other player in Europe's top five divisions.

Already England's record goalscorer, he surpassed Peter Shilton's mark of 125 appearances to become the most-capped Englishman in history.

Six goals at this summer's World Cup also helped him overtake Gary Lineker as the Three Lions' record scorer at the tournament, while he joined David Beckham as the only Englishmen to score in three separate World Cups.

Kane scored his first England goal 79 seconds into his debut in 2015.

Club: Real Madrid Position: Forward.

Mbappe came out on top of an enthralling tussle with Lionel Messi at the 2026 World Cup to become the leading goalscorer in the tournament's history.

The France forward, who is still only 27, took his tally to 22 from just three World Cups to finish one ahead of the great Argentine.

Mbappe's 10 goals from eight games this summer meant he won his second World Cup Golden Boot - a feat no player has managed before.

It also made him the first man to reach double figures at a World Cup since Gerd Muller in 1970, with only Just Fontaine (13) and Sandor Kocsis (11) having scored more goals in a single tournament.

Other achievements in a standout year included him finishing runner-up in the Ballon d'Or awards and overtaking Olivier Giroud as France's all-time leading goalscorer.

He scored 25 times to retain his title as La Liga's top scorer during a 2025-26 campaign in which he netted 42 goals in all competitions for Real Madrid.

Mbappe has won eight consecutive league golden boots.

Club: Barcelona Position: Midfield.

Midfield maestro Rodri earned yet another accolade in his incredible career when he was awarded the Golden Ball trophy for the World Cup's best player.

By captaining Spain to glory in the US, Rodri became only the fourth male player to win the World Cup, European Championship, Champions League and Ballon d'Or - following in the footsteps of legendary Germans Gerd Muller and Franz Beckenbauer and France's Zinedine Zidane.

His achievements were made all the more special given doubts remained over whether he would recapture his best form after a long recovery from a serious knee injury in 2024.

Industry observers note that then Manchester City boss Pep Guardiola predicted he would return to his best at the World Cup and he was right. Rodri completed 756 passes there - the most in the tournament since records began in 1966.

His final season at Manchester City before departing for Barcelona ended in familiar fashion with a domestic cup double taking his trophy haul with the club to 14.

Rodri scored the goal that won Manchester City the Champions League in 2023.

Club: Barcelona Position: Forward.

Lamine Yamal continues to rewrite the record books with his astonishing exploits for club and country.

It has been revealed that just days after his 19th birthday, the Spaniard became the youngest footballer to have played in - and won - both a World Cup and a European Championship. The only players younger than him to play in a World Cup final are Brazil's Pele in 1958 and Giuseppe Bergomi for Italy in 1982.

For the second year running, his performances led to a podium place at the Ballon d'Or and at the same award ceremony he became the first footballer in history to win the Young Talent of the Year three years in succession.

It has been revealed that at club level, he was named La Liga's Best Player courtesy of a starring role in Barcelona's title success. The teenager ended the season with an impressive 24 goals and 18 assists across all competitions.

He recently broke Kylian Mbappe's record for the most goals by a teenager in Champions League history.

Lamine Yamal was 16 years and 362 days when he scored at the European Championship in 2024.
