This Thanksgiving cauliflower is primed to steal the show as the star of your vegetarian Thanksgiving spread. If you’re craving all those classic fall flavors without the meat, this is the recipe you’ll find yourself coming back to year after year. Complete with a homemade vegetarian gravy, this roasted cauliflower is satisfying enough to be your main dish, but also easy enough to serve as a holiday side alongside your mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, and turkey tenderloin.

This recipe is a holiday favorite, but if you're looking for more ways to shake up a roasted cauliflower for your holiday dinner, checkout our brown sugar BBQ whole-roasted cauliflower or balsamic-glazed roasted cauliflower.
