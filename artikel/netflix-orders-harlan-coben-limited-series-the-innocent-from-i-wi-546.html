Netflix Orders Harlan Coben Limited Series ‘The Innocent’ From ‘I Will Find You’ Team

  • Netflix has ordered the limited series “The Innocent” based on the Harlan Coben novel of the same name.
  • In a recent development, the announcement came as part of Netflix’s inaugural appearance at the Texas Book Festival in celebration of the streamer’s many book-to-screen adaptations.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Showrunners Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt, and Steven Lilien have signed an overall deal at netflix.

Netflix has ordered the limited series “The Innocent” based on the Harlan Coben novel of the same name.

In a recent development, the announcement came as part of Netflix’s inaugural appearance at the Texas Book Festival in celebration of the streamer’s many book-to-screen adaptations. Coben is a massive part of that side of Netflix’s business, with “The Innocent” representing the fifteenth adaptation of his work with the company. It has been revealed that this now marks the second adaptation of “The Innocent” for Netflix. The book was previously adapted into the Spanish-language series “El inocente” in 2021.

Sources indicate that “The Innocent” hails from writer, showrunners, and executive producers Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt, and Steven Lilien, who previously collaborated on the creation of the Harlan Coben series “I Will Find You” for Netflix. With the series order, Hull, Wynbrandt, and Lilien have signed an overall deal with Netflix under their StoryMath production banner. Coben and his son, Ben Coben, are also executive producers via Final Twist Productions, which is also under a Netflix overall deal.

Recent findings suggest that “We’re thrilled to call Netflix our home and couldn’t be more grateful to our friends Jinny Howe, Cole Galvin, James Flannery, Mike Azzolino, and of course, the incredible Harlan Coben, for their continued partnership,” Hull, Wynbrandt, and Lilien stated in a joint statement. “Bringing ‘I Will Find You’ to life and seeing it connect with audiences around the world was an amazing experience. We’re excited to bring our signature brand of twisty and propulsive storytelling to The Innocent and the many fresh stories we look forward to telling at Netflix.”.

In a recent development, in the book, Matt Hunter tried to break up a fight but ended up accidentally killing a man. Now, nine years later, he and his pregnant wife Olivia have pieced their lives back together. But when mysterious deaths begin piling up with evidence pointing to Matt as the killer, the two must work outside the law to find the truth.

“Harlan Coben is one of the great creative partnerships at Netflix, and ‘The Innocent’ marks our 15th story together,” noted Cole Galvin, Netflix vice president of drama series. “Nobody builds a can’t-look-away thriller like Harlan, and Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien brought their own incredible creativity to I Will Find You, a global hit for us. We’re thrilled to welcome them to the Netflix family, and we can’t wait for the world to get hooked on their Innocent.”.

“I Will Find You” debuted in June 2026 and rapidly became one of the most popular Netflix indicates of all time. At the time of this publishing, the show ranks at number five on the all time Top 10 list of English language TV shows on the streamer with over 120 million views. Per Netflix, all of Coben’s shows have pulled in over 450 million views in total.

Up next, Netflix will release the Coben adaptations “Myron Bolitar” and “The Woods.” Coben’s other projects with Netflix are: “Fool Me Once,” “Run Away,” “Safe,” “The Woods” (Polish series), “El inocente,” “Gone for Good,” “Stay Close,” “Hold Tight,” “The Stranger,” “Missing You,” “Just One Look,” “Caught,” and “I Will Find You.”.

Hull, Wynbrand, and Lilien are repped by CAA and Range, with Lilien and Wynbrandt also repped by attorney Todd Rubenstein and Hull repped by attorney Dave Ryan.
