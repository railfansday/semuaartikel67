Theory explains how fusion plasmas enter a state of improved energy confinement, solving a 40-year puzzle

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (69 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, nuclear fusion promises a source of energy by combining light atomic nuclei, the same process that powers the sun. To achieve fusion on Earth, scientists must heat a gas to extreme temperatures, creating a charged state of matter called plasma. Keeping this plasma hot and stable long enough for fusion energy production is one of the main challenges facing the technology.
