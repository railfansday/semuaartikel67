The upcoming Hulu-Onyx Collective limited series “Victoria” has added four new cast members, Variety has learned.

Recent findings suggest that kyle MacLachlan (“Twin Peaks,” “Fallout”), Michael Hyatt (“The Wire,” “The Pitt”), Marwan Kenzari (“Aladdin,” “The Old Guard”), and J. Alphonse Nicholson (“Lanterns,” “P-Valley”) are all set to appear in the series, which was ordered at Hulu in September.

Previously declared cast members include Li Jun Li, Diane Lane, and Mark Duplass. The logline for the series states, “Set in the cutthroat world of San Francisco’s elite, ‘Victoria’ is an erotic thriller about a brilliant con artist (Li) who finds herself in a dark web of power, luxury and the crosshairs of a seductive killer who knows her secrets.”.

MacLachlan will play Howard Shannon, who is described as “a razor-sharp businessman and co-founder of one of Silicon Valley’s most prestigious VC firms. His relentless drive for success has no limits.” Hyatt will play Detective Thompson, noted to be “one of SFPD’s most-experienced detectives. She is a dogged investigator who leads with instinct.”.

Kenzari will play Glen Martin, “an ambitious venture capitalist with deep connections to the San Francisco elite. He will do whatever it takes to carve out the status he feels he deserves.” And Nicholson will play Jack, “a reckless loose cannon and Max’s (Li) partner in crime. He is willing to go to unimaginable lengths to pull off a grift.”.

Sources indicate that macLachlan is repped by UTA, Entertainment 360, and Jackoway Austen Tyerman. Hyatt is repped by Buchwald, Color Creative, and Frankfurt Kurnit Klein and Selz. Kenzari is repped by CAA and Anonymous Content. Nicholson is repped by AC Management, CESD and Jackoway Austen Tyerman.

Christy Hall is the creator, writer, executive producer, and showrunner on “Victoria.” Geeta Vasant Patel will direct the pilot and executive produce. Lilly Burns, Elise Henderson, and Tony Hernandez of Counterpart Studios executive produce, with Li co-producing in addition to starring.
