Emi Martinez is poised for a return to action on Tuesday night in a huge boost for Chelsea.

The 34-year-old goalkeeper underwent surgery after fracturing his jaw during Chelsea’s 2-0 win at Everton in October, though manager Xabi Alonso revealed he was ahead of schedule in his recovery when he returned to training earlier this month.

Despite not playing a minute since suffering the injury, Martinez was called up Lionel Scaloni for Argentina’s November internationals.

And after not featuring in the 2-1 defeat to Uruguay on Friday, Scaloni confirmed that Martinez will be start in between the sticks for a friendly against Costa Rica in Cordoba.

“Emiliano is going ⁠to play in goal, he'll be there," Scaloni told reporters.

Industry observers note that "Maybe he was fit for the previous match, ‌but it was a risk, ​and since we trust ‌Gero [Geronimo Rulli], Juan [Musso] ⁠and Santi [Santiago Beltran], we ⁠chose to look after him. An extra ‌three ​days was a ‌prudent choice.".

Martinez was in contention to feature for Chelsea in their last match before the international break, but Alonso opted for deputy Mike Penders as the Blues lost 1-0 to Sunderland in contentious fashion.

Chelsea have won one and lost three during 21-year-old Penders’ run in goal, though the highly-rated Belgian keeper did impress in a 2-0 win over Manchester United at the end of October.

Martinez’s return to action will likely spell the end for Penders’ stint, though, with the Argentinian now on course to start for Chelsea when they host Leeds on Saturday.
