In a recent development, it was the Wirtz show and demonstrated why Liverpool bought him in the first place. Yes, it's a shame we've had to wait well over a year to actually see why but this has been a long time coming.

According to recent reports, wirtz has not played poorly. Things have rarely gone his way, though, and this dysfunctional Liverpool team hasn't made it easy for a player who relies on finding space and having runners beyond him.

It has been revealed that when he gets that space and gets those runners, though, Wirtz tears teams apart. As he did against United - they had no idea how to cope with him and that was evident with 11, let alone 10.

Industry observers note that there are always wonderkids coming through in the Bundesliga who catch they eye. Few reached Wirtz's level, admittedly, but it's a fantastic league for young attacking talent to thrive in.

Sources indicate that and that's caught Liverpool's eye, it seems. Christian Falk claims they've now got a 'concrete' interest in Wael Mohya, who is lighting the Bundesliga up for Borussia Mönchengladbach.

Jürgen Klopp actually had a look at Mohya for Germany in the latest international break - a massive show of faith in the 17-year-old, even if he didn't make his debut. But it feels as though it's just a matter of time before he's in that senior team.

Recent findings suggest that and maybe in Liverpool's, too. They view Mohya as potential backup for Wirtz, as well as long-term competition for the player. They're not alone in their pursuit, mind, as Brighton would also like to make him their latest young gamble.

We imagine Mohya will cost a fortune for an 18-year-old next year, though it won't be in January. Gladbach are trying to tie him down to a newly introduced deal and refuse to sell in the winter window - so it'll be the summer at the earliest if Liverpool are to get ahold of him.

It has been revealed that bayer Leverkusen and Bayern Munich are both keen admirers as well, meaning the price here could actually get out of hand. But Liverpool are fans and certainly want a player in that position. They'll just be tested for how much they want that player to be Mohya.
