A 25-foot-wide townhouse with a carriage house has just hit the market for $11.95 million in Manhattan’s Carnegie Hill.

Built in 1899, the five-bedroom residence underwent a “no-expense-spared” reconstruction and features 13 newly renovated rooms, each with individually zoned heating and cooling.

Industry observers note that “It is certainly very rare for a listing like this townhouse with a carriage house to come on the market in uptown Manhattan,” said listing agent Daniella G. Schlisser of Brown Harris Stevens. “I don’t recall ever seeing something like this here.”.

The 7,700-square-foot interior was entirely gutted and rebuilt with fresh structural steel, plumbing, electrical, and commercial-grade mechanical systems.

Industry observers note that “It has incredible sun and light, which is incredibly unusual for a townhouse,” Schlisser stated. “The home is flooded with direct sunlight facing the back and it also gets sun to the north in the front.”.

The six-story dwelling features a garden level that leads to a limestone gallery with custom paneling. There is a separately accessed commercial space offering “continued retail or office use” that could also be transformed into additional living space. French doors open to a bluestone-decked garden with a built-in grill.

“There is a phenomenal amount of space and light, and being a 25-foot-wide townhouse is also rare,” she noted.

A parlor level boasts a living room with a fireplace and herringbone floors. The nearby formal dining room was designed for grand-scale entertaining. Also on this level is a garden-facing eat-in kitchen with a breakfast bar, granite countertops, custom cabinetry, and high-end appliances.

The third floor has two en-suite bedrooms and a den with coffered ceilings, a wood-burning fireplace, and access to a private terrace overlooking the carriage house.

Two king-sized bedroom suites with custom "study" areas are on the fourth floor.

A lavish primary suite spans the entire fifth floor. It features a south-facing sitting room that opens to a private terrace, a windowed dressing room, and a spa-inspired bathroom with a skylight, soaking tub, and a double vanity.

The sixth floor boasts a “skylit” fitness and wellness room along with a roof terrace “awaiting the next owner’s finishing touch.”.

Schlisser stated every prospective buyer she has shown the home to is interested in living there full-time.

It has been revealed that “Carnegie Hill is a wonderful neighborhood,” she noted. “Culturally and educationally, everything is there and the most frequented museums are there as well as some of the best private schools in Manhattan.”.

The detached carriage house has cathedral ceilings, vertical-grain Douglas fir paneling, and a sleeping loft that could be used for guest accommodations or additional recreation space.

Industry observers note that “I think the next buyer will be someone who is looking for space and proximity to schools,” Schlisser added. “The style is all fresh but very much a pre-war style. Everything has been done with incredible care. It’s really beautiful.”.
