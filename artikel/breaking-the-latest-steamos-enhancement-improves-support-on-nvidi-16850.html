The preview version of SteamOS 3.10.0 has just dropped, and with it comes better support for AMD and Nvidia hardware, and it's now based on Linux kernel 7.3.

According to recent reports, for AMD graphics, the latest SteamOS update adds support for HDMI 2.1 VRR (Variable Refresh Rate), FRL(Fixed Rate Link), and ALLM (Auto Low Latency Mode). This should all contribute to a smoother gaming experience with higher bandwidth video and less input lag.

As for Nvidia graphics, it has reportedly received increased stability and performance, alongside support for Mesh Shaders on Turing and newer architectures. The latest SteamOS updates also fixed rendering corruption problems in Shadow of the Tomb Raider and The Surge 2.

Then, of course, SteamOS now uses the Linux 7.3 kernel (which launched last month).

Industry observers note that those are the massive updates from SteamOS 3.10.0, but there are also a handful of fixes and changes. Game Mode has had the following changes:.

SteamOS Previews are experimental by nature. You have to swap your enhancement channel in Steam settings to get access, and there's always a chance upcoming updates come with bugs or stability problems. If you come across a problem, Valve has a form to track SteamOS bugs.

If you don't fancy downloading a preview, SteamOS 3.10.0 is likely to come to the public channel at some point in the future, based on feedback from the preview.
