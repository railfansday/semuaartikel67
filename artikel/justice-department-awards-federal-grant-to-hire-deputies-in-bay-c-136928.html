Justice Department Awards Federal Grant to Hire Deputies in Bay County

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (42 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Tallahassee, Florida — Through the Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office), the Department of Justice today revealed it has awarded nearly $150 million under the 2026 COPS Hiring Program (CHP), including $3.125 million to Bay County.
