According to recent reports, hybrid and remote work have changed what people expect from a modern workspace. No longer do people want fixed seating or single-purpose design. Instead, they are looking for modular, multi-use furniture that boosts productivity while maintaining comfort and style. When Chris and Jon Panichella set out to design their latest seating collection for Arcadia, they turned to everyday life for inspiration.

In a recent development, “The idea for Almeya began with a woman working from an inflatable raft at the beach,” the Panichellas explain. “She could recline and read in one position, then sit up and work in another—a simple moment that captured how much people now expect from their spaces: comfort, flexibility, and function. That observation became the very first concept sketch that eventually evolved into the Almeya offering we know today.”.

Photography courtesy of Arcadia.

According to recent reports, with a full family of products—including modular, lounge, work-lounge, benches, and occasional tables—the Almeya Collection offers a fluid experience that adapts to these modern shifts in productivity and collaboration. Across the series, soft, organic forms invite comfort and ease, while integrated tables, flexible configurations, and strategic power stations allow for both focus and sociability.

According to recent reports, almeya began with a single lounge chair and an integrated two-tiered side table. The idea was simple: the combination could function as a self-contained work island, with two separate surfaces, power access, and an optional rotating tablet. With this concept in mind, the Panichellas developed a standalone lounge chair, love seat, and sofa with the same flexible spirit. (Case in point: The swivel-base lounge chair allows for adaptability and easy collaboration). Finally, the complete collection came together, designed to support the various—and often unpredictable—ways people work and gather.

Photography courtesy of Arcadia.

To inspire interaction, designers are encouraged to group lounge seating intermittently to foster spontaneous connections. To create pathways or natural flow within a space, they can explore Almeya’s modular capabilities. The organic curves of the lounge models also create an inviting warmth, blurring the line between contract and residential. And for healthcare and education settings, optional clean-outs are offered between the seat and back.

Photography courtesy of Arcadia.

Completing the collection, occasional tables carry the same gentle, organic language for a cohesive experience. Available in multiple sizes and shapes, tables feature upholstered sides and are offered with ash or walnut veneer, laminate, Corian, and Fenix top surfaces.
