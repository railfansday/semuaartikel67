Bobbi Althoff means business when it comes to defending her friends.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: OC star is sticking up for costar McCall DaPron, who faced backlash for questioning Althoff's religious background as a non-Mormon and whether Aspyn Ovard had forged relationships in their inner circle for clout.

Recent findings suggest that "I'm going to need all you people to stop bullying McCall. It's really out of control," the reality star penned on an Instagram Story Nov. 15. "I understand she made some mistakes but your response to calling her a mean girl is being mean?".

Though Althoff and DaPron—the older sister of SLOMW's Mayci Neeley, who formed a friendship with Ovard during the show—bumped heads, the podcaster said the audience giving her castmate the villain treatment online is "not necessary," as she and DaPron, 36, made amends since filming some of those tense moments.

In a recent development, "People make mistakes. People learn from them & people grow," Althoff, 29, continued. "Imagine having your lowest most vulnerable moments out there for everyone to see.".

In a recent development, and fellow Mormon Wives star Avery Woods agreed that DaPron had grown from her missteps, praising her costar's "incredible come around moment at the end of the season" in a group interview with Entertainment Tonight shared Nov. 13. Their reconciliation followed a conflict in episode two, when DaPron tried to share a negative comment left by one of Woods' followers after the influencer had explained she avoids reading mean posts on social media.

As such, Althoff advised fans to remember to keep perspective as her new friend group finds their footing.

In other words, "Just be patient & kind and have grace because we are all human.".

For her part, DaPron denied her and Jordan McClanahan's status as the series' antagonists.

Sources indicate that "I don't feel like we're the villains. Maybe that's how we were portrayed," she told the outlet in the same joint interview. "So much is taken out of context or a lot gets cut.".

That noted, the jewelry designer—who shares four kids with husband Alex DaPron—said watching the season back was "all very confusing.".
