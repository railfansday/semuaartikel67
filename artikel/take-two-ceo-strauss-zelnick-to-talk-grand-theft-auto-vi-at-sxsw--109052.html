Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick, the man behind Grand Theft Auto VI, will speak at SXSW London 2027, organizers announced Tuesday.

Zelnick, who will talk “about building one of the world’s biggest entertainment franchises,” is among the first-wave of speakers for SXSW London, which returns to Shoreditch, east London, for its third year in May.

According to recent reports, built around the theme “The Power of Human Potential”, the 2027 festival will focus on people in a year dominated by what machines can do. Additional speakers include Jon Steinback, former DeepMind creative lead whose start-up Marker is building an AI writing tool designed to develop, rather than delete, writers. David Thornton, President of Genomics Limited, will explore how rapid analysis of large-scale genetic and genomic information will empower us to predict disease risks and improve medicine.

Rounding out the first group of speakers are Sir Martin Sorrell and Rory Sutherland and media personality GK Barry, who will discuss the future of advertising and the creator economy. Additionally, Refik Anadol, founder of the newly opened L.A. museum, Dataland, will share his artistic vision for AI and data-driven installations. Watch on Deadline.

The 2027 festival will run from May 24-29.
