French architecture studio Ateliers Jean Nouvel has shared photos revealing construction progress on the giant Shenzhen Opera House, due for completion in China in 2028.

Located on the Shekou Peninsula overlooking Shenzhen Bay, the opera house will eventually be defined by an elongated, wavy roofscape, nodding to the site’s connection to the sea.

Pritzker Prize-winning architect Jean Nouvel’s studio has designed it as a new cultural landmark for the city, with a 2,300-seat concert hall, an 1,800-seat theatre and an 800-seat venue among its facilities.

The construction photos help visualise the scale of the 250,000-square-metre opera house, with its various facilities sprawling out across its waterfront site.

They showcase its main concert hall with its horseshoe-shaped initiative, alongside the two smaller performance venues.

Ateliers Jean Nouvel’s design for Shenzhen Opera House was the winning entry of an invited international architecture competition, for which other entrants included SUBSTANTIAL, Snøhetta, MVRDV and Kengo Kuma.

Alongside the main performance halls, it will contain a multifunctional theatre with 400 seats, cultural spaces, backstage areas, apartments for performers and offices.

To help further connect the building to the surrounding water, the interior is expected to feature iridescent materials such as mother-of-pearl, also known as nacre.

“The sea will be both around you and over you,” explained Nouvel when the project was revealed in 2021.

“This explains more clearly why the main foyer has to be made of a noble, precious and luminous material that spells the meeting of sea and music and light,” Nouvel continued.

“Using the lustrous white reflections of a nacreous material will be a concrete poeticisation of the meeting of the sea and the auditorium and concert halls of the Opera House.”.

French architect Nouvel founded his eponymous studio in 1994. We recently reported on the news that the studio’s La Querola housing development in Andorra, designed with Spanish studio Ribas & Ribas Arquitectos, was damaged in a fire. The project won the housing category of this year’s Dezeen Awards, which took place last night.

Another landmark opera house recently featured on Dezeen is the Shanghai Grand Opera House, designed by Snøhetta with a giant spiral staircase.
