📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nintendo has announced a Pikmin 4 Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy crossover for its addictive online battle royale game Tetris 99.

Industry observers note that it's taking place between 26th November 2026 and 30th November 2026. During this time, you'll be able to unlock a special theme when you earn 100 event points. Here is Nintendo's official announcement, and you can see the new theme in action in the video above.

Industry observers note that "The Tetris® 99 58th MAXIMUS CUP event runs from 11pm PT on November 26 to 10:59pm PT on November 30. To participate, you’ll need to be a Nintendo Switch Online member* and play the Tetris 99 online mode during the event period. You’ll earn event points based on your placement in each match. Once you’ve accumulated a total of 100 event points, a latest theme will unlock, featuring art, music and Tetrimino designs inspired by the Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy game for the Nintendo Switch 2 system!".
