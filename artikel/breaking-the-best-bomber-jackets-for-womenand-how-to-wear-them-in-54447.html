Recent findings suggest that when it comes to layering for the cold-weather season, bomber jackets are always a go-to: The classic silhouette is as versatile as it is stylish, a forever favorite that withstands the trend cycle. From leather to suede, techy nylon to faux-fur trimmings, the bomber jacket exudes an understated cool factor.

As seen in the latest street style looks during Fashion Week in Paris, Milan, and Fresh York, it’s clear that the jacket has staying power. It makes complete sense: They’re an easy staple for dressing up or down. To style, pair them with jeans for a casual boyish look, or throw them over a dainty dress for an unexpected mix of hard and soft textures and layers.

Below, an edit of our favorite bomber jackets—with ideas on how to wear each.

You can’t go wrong with a classic leather bomber. City-slickers will love Róhe’s oxblood version, paired with Emily Dawn Long’s shawl-scarf and corduroys from The Row for a cozy yet stylish seasonal look.

Arma’s suede bomber covers all the bases: It’s versatile for day-to-night wear and the rib-trim adds the right amount of structure to this sweater-and-skirt look. The finishing touch? Paris Texas’s patent knee-highs, which share a name with the desert-set Wim Wenders film).

Recent findings suggest that the latest jacket trend has taken a stand, literally. The stand-up collar sends a strong statement with Balenciaga’s Thriller-inspired look. Pair with white barrel-leg pants and fanciful accessories like Savette’s bejeweled pouch and Khaite’s fringe loafer for a touch of whimsy.

According to recent reports, flights of fancy may take hold with Nour Hammour’s aviator-style, shearling collar bomber. Pair it with Maria McManus’s opera scarf and Rùadh’s oversized turtleneck for a winter look that doesn’t sacrifice style for warmth.

Channel Jean Luc-Godard’s film, Masculin Féminin, with this look that blends masculine toughness in Staud’s distressed leather bomber with the delicate femininity of If Only If’s dainty nightgown. Pair with Vibi Venezia’s girlish flats and Malene Birger’s lipstick necklace to complete the look.

According to recent reports, the canvas and wool bomber from LA-based brand Cobra S.C. is an easy throw-on style with an oversized fit and impeccable tailoring. Pair with a printed waffle shirt from Emily Dawn Long, perfect straight-leg Mother jeans and sporty-chic flats for an everyday, errand-ready look.

This draped, batwing style from Louis Vuitton serves as testament to the recent nylon bomber trend—a more fluid approach to the ’80s look that feels fresh for the season. Pair with barrel-leg military-style pants from Deiji Studios and deer-print ankle booties from Loulou de Saison for an outfit that plays with volumes and textures.
