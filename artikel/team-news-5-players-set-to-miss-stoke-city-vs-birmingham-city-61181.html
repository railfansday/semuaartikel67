The Championship is back after yet another international break, and we've now got a straight sprint until the Easter weekend with no interruptions.

The second-tier returns with a bang, as two of the sides sitting on 29 points atop the table go head-to-head at the bet365 Stadium on Saturday.

Stoke City, who have exceeded early-season expectations once more, will be keen not to fade away over the coming months like they did last year. A win at home against the current table-toppers, Birmingham City, would go a long way towards doing that.

The Blues were ruthless over the seven-game block between international breaks, winning six of seven, and three of those wins came by at least a three-goal margin. Their form away from St Andrew's is as big a contributor to their place atop the table, winning just one game fewer already this year than they did in the entirety of last season.

But only Swansea City and Middlesbrough — two fellow quick-starters — have come away from Stoke with all three points, and Kim Hellberg's side needed a late winner to do so. This won't be an easy matchup for either, and three points will give a massive boost to both sides' promotion hopes.

These five players, however, won't be able to feature.

French attacker Lamine Cisse had started six straight for Stoke before he went down inside 20 minutes against Bristol City last month and was forced off.

The extended run in the team, plus a goal in the win at Bolton Wanderers, gave hope that the £2m arrival last summer from Bastia was starting to find his feet in Mark Robins' side, so this setback is especially frustrating.

The ankle injury he picked up should see him out until Christmas.

Ato Ampah has had a terrible time with injuries ever since he arrived at Stoke from Chelsea's academy back in January.

He's yet to make his debut for the Potters due to a series of setbacks. Last season, it was an elongated recovery from a toe injury which kept him on the sidelines, and over pre-season he suffered a setback to that recovery.

While spending time with the Under-21s, though, the 20-year-old picked up an impact injury to his knee, which required surgery and will keep him out for the foreseeable future.

Industry observers note that it's still unconfirmed whether Eric Bocat will be able to return against Birmingham. He's missed the last four games with a knee injury, which forced him off against Derby County last month.

The hope after his diagnosis was that the left-back could make his return after the international break, but he remains a doubt, with Ben Johnson and Luca Bombino waiting in the wings to deputise in his absence.

Fellow left-back Aaron Cresswell is also a doubt for Saturday's clash with Birmingham, as he nears closer to a return to fitness from a knee injury he picked up in pre-season.

The 36-year-old remained a Stoke player over the summer, signing a one-year extension after he made 25 league appearances in his debut campaign following his departure from West Ham United.

According to recent reports, birmingham's bill of health is relatively clear heading into the weekend. James Beadle and August Priske are both available again after missing a couple before the break, and Kristoffer Lund, who missed six weeks, is also back training.

Marc Leonard looked to be on his way out of Birmingham after heading on loan to Hearts back in January, but an Achilles injury suffered weeks before the end of the campaign saw him return to St Andrew's for treatment.

He'll remain out until the Upcoming Year, and all eyes will be on what his role in Chris Davies' side will be after his return.
