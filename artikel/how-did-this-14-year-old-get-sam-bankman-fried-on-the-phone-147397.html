Journalists who’ve been anxious about losing their jobs to artificial intelligence would be wise to focus on a different, altogether less menacing but equally impressive threat: 14-year-old Eli Goldfine, the shoestring podcaster from Westchester County who last week conducted a 90-minute interview with Sam Bankman-Fried, who’s currently serving a 25-year sentence in federal prison for orchestrating a multi-billion-dollar cryptocurrency grift.

So how, exactly, does this precocious ninth grader with braces—and not a reporter from one of the scores of legacy publications who’d surely be gunning for access to the disgraced cryptocurrency king—score an interview so coveted? “I had written about Sam on Twitter a little bit, and it got picked up by the person who was at the time running Sam’s account,” Goldfine, who hosts a podcast about prediction markets called The Supercycle, told me earlier this week. “I think they were impressed with my knowledge of various parts of his criminal case.”.

According to recent reports, goldfine’s classmates are impressed, too, even though he’s been on the receiving end of some gentle ribbing about his on-air ad reads. But can any of them say they got Bankman-Fried to speculate on the likelihood that AI will bring about the extinction of the human race? ("Less than 50%, more than 10%," by the way). Before school’s out for Thanksgiving break, Goldfine joined GQ to talk about his media diet, dream interview subject, and the mechanics of interviewing a man behind bars.
