When most investors think of Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%), they think of Jensen Huang. That's why, when the famous chief executive officer stepped down and then came back to his post 11 days later, the stock reacted. Huang's departure resulted in declines, and his return resulted in significant gains -- the stock soared more than 30% in one trading session.

We all know that CEOs are key, but Huang's visionary role at Nvidia, as well as his prominence in the artificial intelligence (AI) space worldwide, make him a key player in this high-growth field. Without his leadership, Nvidia shareholders worried that the organization might not continue to expand at the same pace and in the same direction.

Nvidia has trailblazed in AI, with its early entry into the AI chip market and innovations that have kept it ahead of rivals. This has resulted in jaw-dropping earnings growth, and this deep involvement in AI has transformed Jensen Huang into an expert in the field. His knowledge and visibility about what's ahead have made him a valuable asset -- and one that investors weren't eager to replace.
