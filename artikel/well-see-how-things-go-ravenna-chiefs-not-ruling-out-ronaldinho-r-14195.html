Ravenna's club executives have left the door open for Ronaldinho to play for the Serie C side again after his remarkable comeback to professional football.

Ronaldinho appeared in his first match since 2015 on Sunday, playing the opening 45 minutes in Ravenna's 2-0 win over Forli at Stadio Comunale Bruno Benelli.

The 46-year-old joined Ravenna as an investor in August and worked his way up to fitness before making an appearance for the third-tier outfit.

He had been due to play for the first 30 minutes, but instead lasted until half-time before he was replaced by Matteo Maggio.

Ronaldinho was unable to score the 300th goal of his career, but he put in a solid display, completing 17 of his 21 passes while also creating a chance from a corner.

After 14 games of the 2026-27 season, Ravenna sit second in Group B of the Serie C standings, six points adrift of Spice.

But speaking after their win at the weekend, Ravenna president Ignazio Cipriani told Sky Italia that Ronaldinho could feature again for the team in the future.

"He didn't want to come off, he was having fun," Cipriani noted.

When asked about a possible return, Cipriani stated: "We'll see how things go.".

A post shared by Ravenna Football Club (@ravennafootballclub).

Cipriani's comments were echoed by Ravenna's vice president Ariedo Braida, who was at AC Milan when Ronaldinho played there between 2008 and 2011.

"A champion like him enchants young and old alike, everyone has fallen in love with Ronaldinho," he said.

"There are those who know how to play football and those who kick a ball. He knows how to play football. I hope to see him here again.".

Ravenna have won their last four league matches, and face eighth-place Livorno this weekend.
