Sources indicate that for my EGOHOME Black 12" Foam mattress review, I slept on the EGOHOME mattress for over seven months (for reference, we usually test mattresses at-home for three weeks), and I always felt supported no matter what position I slept in. However, the wider testing panel (along with our subjective tests) found that its body-hugging foam is great for side and back sleepers, but too soft for stomach sleepers.

Both the Siena Signature and the SweetNight CoolNest performed surprisingly well in our temperature regulation tests, where we used a thermal imaging camera to measure how well the mattresses absorb and dissipate heat.

As one of the best cooling mattresses on the market, the SweetNight has a rapid cooling rate of 2.1°F per minute. The Siena, meanwhile, displayed a slower cooling rate of 1.52°F per minute; however, it absorbed less heat in the first place.

Subjectively, too, the SweetNight wins, as my fellow testers and I were immediately impressed with its cool-to-the-touch feel. I also slept on it during summer heatwaves in a building with no AC, and I never felt overheated.

The Siena also kept its lead tester comfortable, but provided a temperature-neutral feel rather than an actively cooling feel — this may be because it's designed with a focus on breathability rather than featuring any specific cooling tech.

Industry observers note that in contrast, the EGOHOME Black didn't impress me with its cooling when I slept on it during hot weather. I often woke up feeling overheated and could feel patches of heat build up under my pressure points. Testing panelist Ruth Jones, agreed, reporting that she felt the mattress warm up when she was lying on her stomach.
