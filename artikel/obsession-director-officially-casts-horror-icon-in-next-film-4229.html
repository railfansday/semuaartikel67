Obsession Director Officially Casts Horror Icon in Next Film

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Curry Barker's Obsession is one of the year's best horror films, but his next movie might end up being even better. Barker is writing, directing, and starring in next year's Anything but Ghosts, and he'll be joined by a horror movie icon.

On Monday, Deadline reported Matthew Lillard has joined the cast for Anything but Ghosts during additional filming. (Principal photography wrapped earlier this year.) The specifics of his character aren't known yet.

Anything but Ghosts stars Barker, his longtime collaborator Cooper Tomlinson (who's also co-writing it), Bryce Dallas Howard, and Aaron Paul. Barker and Tomlinson play fake paranormal investigators who end up encountering real ghosts. Tomlinson described Anything but Ghosts as "if Key and Peele walked into Hereditary" on a podcast. It will be released on May 7, 2027, just about a year after Obsession took theaters by storm.

It has been revealed that lillard broke through with his role as Stu Macher in the original Scream and reprised the role in Scream 7, released earlier this year. He's also starred in Five Nights at Freddy's, its sequel, and the latest adaptation of Carrie.

In a recent development, aside from Scream, Lillard might be best known for his role as Shaggy in the early 2000s Scooby-Doo movies. He then went on to voice the character in several animated films and TV specials over the years.

It has been revealed that anything but Ghosts won't be Lillard's only big 2027 film. He's also joined the cast of James Gunn's Superman sequel Man of Tomorrow in an undisclosed role.
