Return made for Vincent Kompany. The Belgian coach of Bayern Munich took a forced rest following health problems on October 23. Back in training this Monday, the former Manchester City defender will lead the Rekordmeister’s match this Friday against Cologne.

This was revealed by Bayern Munich President Jan-Christian Dreesen in an interview with Abdendzeitung: “Yes. Vincent is in a very good mood. I saw him today. His meningeal irritation has healed. He’s already had shoulder surgery.”.
