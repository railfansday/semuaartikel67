Several AC Milan players have been highly praised by the Italian media after the 5-1 dismantling of Frosinone at San Siro.

Milan ended up with goals from five different sources as Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic all netted, in addition to the opening goal which was an own-goal by Calvani. Adrien Rabiot got three assists, while Diego Moreira shone too.

It was a statement result and a ruthless display of the weapons that the Rossoneri have in transition, and it moves Ruben Amorim’s side back into the top four, while they remain five points off leaders Lazio.

Starting with La Gazzetta dello Sport, they gave the team’s overall performance a score of 8 out of 10, which is the same mark that they gave Amorim for his selections and tactical handling of what could have been a tough game on paper.

According to recent reports, rabiot was the Serie A Man of the Match and he gets the crown from La Gazzetta too, scoring an 8. The worst performer was Davide Bartesaghi, who struggled in his battle with Ghedjemis and got a 5.5.

Moreira matched Rabiot with an 8, while Pulisic and Luka Modric were given a 7.5. Four players scored a 7: Yunus Musah, Ramos, Jashari and Cisse. Sankhoun Diawara (6.5) passed the test, with Koni De Winter, Strahinja Pavlovic and Pervis Estupinan being given a 6.

It has been revealed that corriere dello Sport were a tad harsher with their scoring, giving Rabiot a 7.5 as the highest score. Jashari, Moreira, Modric, Pulisic and Ramos were handed a 7. They gave Cisse a 6.5, and everyone else got a passing score. Amorim (7.5) was also praised.

Tuttosport meanwhile gave Rabiot an 8, distributing 7.5s instead to Moreira, Modric, Pulisic and the coach Amorim. Scores of 7 went to Jashari, Musah, Ramos and Cisse. Gila, De Winter and Diawara got given a 6.5, and everyone else a 6.
