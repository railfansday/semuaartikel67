Where To Find The Painted Tunnel In GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, driving in GTA 6 is pretty tricky at the best of times and far more realistic than it has ever been in the series before. The cops are also harder to shake than ever, which is why the last thing you want when racing away from them at high speed is Rockstar pulling a prank that will bring everything to a grinding halt, literally.

Industry observers note that the prank that multiple players have fallen foul of is a hyper-realistic mural that looks like a tunnel. More specifically, it looks like the road you’re on just continues straight ahead, taking you under a bridge.

The idea is that players, especially those in the middle of a police chase, either won’t notice that the through road is actually a painting until it’s too late, or, as many clips have demonstrated, don’t notice it at all and plough headfirst into the mural.

The more realistic driving in GTA 6 extends beyond how it feels to handle its vehicles. It also applies to what might happen if you were to, say, drive into a wall at 100 mph. Falling for the prank is going to seriously damage your car and cause serious injuries to Jason or Lucia in the process. Maybe even both of them if they’re both in the car.

Recent findings suggest that the tunnel prank is, naturally, being compared to when the gimmick was used in Looney Tunes cartoons. Most often used by Wile E. Coyote, the iconic cartoon character would often paint tunnels on cliff sides to try and fool Road Runner into trying to use them. Road Runner would usually defy the laws of physics and run right on through. Unfortunately, that’s not possible in GTA 6’s take on the decades-old joke.

If you want to go and check out the mural yourself, or make a note of where it is in Leonida so you know when to be on high alert, it can be found in the Vice Decor District of Vice City. If you do happen to be on the run and can’t keep an eye on where you are at any given moment, just keep an eye out for a part of the game where murals are popping up on buildings left and right. Odds are you’re in the Decor District, and one of those murals is trying to fool you into crashing headfirst into a solid brick wall.
