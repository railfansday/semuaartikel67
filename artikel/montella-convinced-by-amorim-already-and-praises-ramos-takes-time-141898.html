AC Milan are enjoying a strong first season under Ruben Amorim and whilst there is room for improvement, there are promising signs.

In a recent development, when Milan moved for Amorim, the reaction was mixed. His stock was low after his time in Manchester, and it could’ve been a difficult sell for San Siro.

However, the Portuguese impressed with his first words and was backed heavily in the summer – something which he arguably lacked at Old Trafford – and results have been largely positive.

Of course, there have been a few problems in certain games and the Rossoneri are not invincible. Yet, with an incomplete squad and strong results to look back on, the Portuguese looks like he could aid the Diavolo in their bid to return to the top.

Speaking to Gazzetta dello Sport – in the second part of his interview, which was initially released yesterday – Montella offered heavy praise to both Amorim and Goncalo Ramos, as MilanNews relays.

“Amorim’s style of play has convinced me, he should be left free to impose his ideas. I also like Gonçalo Ramos, but we need to give him time. Just because they paid you 70 million doesn’t mean you’ll automatically score twenty goals in the first half of the season.

“It takes time and confidence: he can perform well both as a lone striker and with another striker.”.
