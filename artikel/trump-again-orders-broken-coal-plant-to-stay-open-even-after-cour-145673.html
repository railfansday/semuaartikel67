Campbell coal plant in Michigan to remain open, 534 days after the plant’s planned retirement. The orders have cost ratepayers $303 million and counting, causing electricity prices to increase amid a global energy crisis.

One of the Department of Energy’s recent tactics has been to matter “emergency orders” forcing coal plants to stay open, even when nobody wants them open.

The DoE has used this on six coal plants (and one oil-and-gas plant) in six states, claiming that the plants need to be kept open for “reliability.”.

The problem, of course, is that coal is the least reliable form of energy we have available to us. It’s also expensive and polluting, which is why coal use has been plummeting in recent years, dropping from a high of around half of US electricity generation in the early 2000s, down to 17% now.

Recent findings suggest that so everyone is trying to get off coal, for good reason. And when I say “everyone,” I mean everyone – even the owners of the plants subject to these orders have made filings opposing the orders.

The only person trying to keep these coal plants open is fossil shill Chris Wright, posing as head of the Department of Energy, whose main goal is to make American energy supply more expensive and less secure, and ensure that Americans suffer as much harm as possible from it, both to their lungs and pocketbooks.

Each of these orders was obviously illegal from the start – there is no emergency that should force broken coal plants to be reopened, at great cost, in order to sit there idle, providing nothing for ratepayers in the region who are on the hook for the hundreds of millions of dollars that these orders will cost.

Thankfully, courts agreed – even if it took them a while. After more than a year of this nonsense, a federal appeals court blocked these “emergency orders,” specifically targeting the order for the J.H. Campbell coal plant in Michigan. The court unanimously found that there was no emergency keeping it open.

The orders for the other plants are all subject to similar lawsuits, and given the ruling for the Campbell plant, it is expected that courts will find similarly for each of them.

However, despite that order, the DoE has continued its illegal actions. Today, it issued another 90-day “emergency order” forcing the Campbell plant to stay open until mid-February. It does this as it requests a re-hearing on the previous decision, which was unanimously found to be illegal.

In the interim, Americans have dealt with skyrocketing energy prices throughout all aspects of life, largely driven by a now 8+ month long war of choice in Iran. The 90 day order will continue to balloon costs, keeping a coal plant open that nobody wants open, even its own operators who had already planned for its retirement.

Who knew that MAGA meant battling the well-documented improvements of modernity, like clean(er) and cheaper energy, vaccines, scientific R&D, etc.?

In a recent filing, the plants owners said “From the date the first emergency order became effective through September 30, 2026, the amount Consumers expects to seek recovery from FERC associated with the emergency orders, including amounts already requested, is $303 million.”.

This amount is in addition to the hundreds of millions of dollars of costs incurred by the other six plants subject to these false emergency orders, bringing costs up by over $600 million across the country just from these emergency orders alone. The Campbell plant is the worst of the bunch in terms of pollution – which isn’t accounted for in the $600 million number, meaning American health costs are rising even more in addition to the staffing costs for these broken plants.
