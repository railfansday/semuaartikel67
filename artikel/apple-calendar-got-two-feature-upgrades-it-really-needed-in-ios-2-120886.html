I’ve used Apple’s Calendar app for years, and there’s an elegant simplicity to it. But one key area the app has long lagged behind competitors is event creation.

In a recent development, when you need to create a latest event, the Calendar app historically has required manually filling in various fields for the title, date, time, invitees, etc. By contrast, the excellent third-party app Fantastical lets you type in natural language and it automatically fills in relevant parameters based on what you type.

Recent findings suggest that in iOS 27, Apple finally introduces its own natural language input for Calendar events. You can type out your event details and certain recognized parameters will be used to automatically fill in the detail fields.

While the feature still isn’t on par with what Fantastical offers, since it doesn’t support as many data points as the third-party alternative, it’s very much a step in the right direction.
