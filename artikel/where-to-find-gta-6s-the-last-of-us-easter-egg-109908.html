Recent findings suggest that there are more secrets, references, and easter eggs in GTA 6 than we can possibly know. We will almost certainly be stumbling on them for years to come. A lot have been discovered during these first few days, though, including what is almost certainly a friendly jab at Naughty Dog for its many remasters and remakes of The Last of Us games.

Industry observers note that there are electronics stores dotted all over Leonida, but on the shelves of one called Game Horde that can be found in the Port Gellhorn region of the map, there are video games. Not video games that exist in our world, or would even be compatible with our consoles, but all are references to real games and compatible with the JoySystem, the in-universe console you can play back at your house after Cal sets it up in the early stages of the game.

Sources indicate that there are a few games on the shelf, most of which appear to be GTA 6 versions of real-life titles, although the most obvious is the We Came Together collection. Not only do the name and the font confirm that it's the Vice City version of The Last of Us, but Rockstar has had a little fun at Naughty Dog’s expense with the easter egg.

Under the game name along the case’s spine, it reads “I, II, & III Remasters of the Remakes”. Clearly a little dig at Naughty Dog for repeatedly re-releasing the first two Last of Us games rather than making a new one. The Last of Us has a rematser and a remake, The Last of Us Part 2 has already been remastered, and both Part 1’s remake and Part 2’s remaster were re-released as a double-pack.

Sources indicate that you might argue that it’s pretty rich of Rockstar to make that joke when it released GTA 5 across three different consoles, it just never gave its GTA 6 predecessor the remaster label. Even when taking that into account, though, Naughty Dog has given its games more remasters and remakes, even though the original The Last of Us was released only three months before GTA 5.

According to recent reports, the GTA 6 equivalent of The Last of Us Part 3 is also already available in Leonida. We’ll be waiting another decade for the real thing, at least.

It has been revealed that the other JoySystem games on the shelf are Desolate Rim, which is Death Stranding based on the font, Righteous Slaughter is GTA’s Call of Duty, and Glory Souls 2. Clearly a FromSoftware game, but it’s unclear which one. The name suggests Demon’s Souls, but the font screams Elden Ring.

GTA 6 players have spent almost 80,000 years collectively in-game already, so it’s no wonder these tiny details are already being discovered. What’s even more remarkable, considering that timeframe, is how much more we still haven’t seen.
