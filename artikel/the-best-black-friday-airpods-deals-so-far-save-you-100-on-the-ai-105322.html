Black Friday sales are live across the web, and headphones deals — including deep discounts on AirPods — are starting to show out.

It has been revealed that so far, the main attraction is an unprecedented discount on the AirPods Pro 3, Apple's premium wireless earbuds. Now $149 at Amazon and Walmart, or a full $100 off, they've never been cheaper before this week.

Sources indicate that the AirPods 4 and AirPods Max 2 are also on sale for at least 24% off right now, but bear in mind that neither pair has returned to its lowest price on record just yet. If you're willing to wait a few more days, we expect both models to get an extra $20 cheaper on Thanksgiving or Black Friday proper (which fall on Nov. 26 and 27 in 2026, respectively).

Recent findings suggest that below, we've gathered the best Black Friday AirPods deals that are live so far. We've also made a prediction about the AirPods 5's Black Friday deal, as they're the only model that isn't on sale at all yet. (Spoiler: It probably won't be a huge markdown.).

The AirPods Pro 3 stand among the best noise-cancelling earbuds we've ever tested. Our reviewer called them "a remarkable upgrade in functionality over their predecessors, with better battery life and ANC, as well as a new heart rate sensor." They also last twice as long as the standard AirPods 5 on one charge.
