According to recent reports, postscript is the AI-powered, all-in-one retention platform trusted by 20,000+ Shopify brands, including Brooklinen, Ruggable, True Classic, and Dr. Squatch.

Our mission is to maximize the lifetime value of every shopper. We built the best-in-class SMS platform, expanded into email, and launched revenue-driving AI products you won’t find anywhere else. Now we’re building the most AI-forward team in ecommerce, and we’re just getting started.

Backed by Greylock and Y Combinator. Fully remote since 2018.

Postscript is hiring a Lead Product Counsel to own AI, privacy and data law for our platform. You will be the legal partner to the teams building AI-enabled conversations between brands and shoppers across 20,000 merchants and millions of consumers.

It has been revealed that the ideal candidate is a curious, effective and practical attorney who will work cross-functionally to provide counsel and compliance strategies to teams across Postscript on various AI, privacy and data protection matters. They are also interested in how AI is built and deployed in marketing and advertising, and in using AI in their own legal work. This is a great opportunity for someone who wants to be on the cutting edge of AI deployment and policy in marketing technology.

According to recent reports, postscript Legal covers messaging compliance, privacy and product law, commercial work, litigation and policy. We treat compliance as a product advantage: our approach to consent and AI eligibility is something merchants choose us for. We also help shape the rules as founders of the Ecommerce Innovation Alliance, a leading industry policy advocacy group.

It has been revealed that the team is small and senior, and we work AI-first. We have built our own AI tools for drafting, research and review, we try upcoming tools as they ship, and we teach each other what works. We also have dedicated internal AI operations support for design, development, integrations and larger projects. The firm sets aside time for you to develop AI skills, not just licenses, because we think this is the future of how in-house legal teams will work. The tools make us faster. They do not make the call: every piece of advice still carries the judgment of the lawyer who gave it.

Industry observers note that for information about how we use your personal data, please see our U.S. Job Applicant Privacy Notice.

Recent findings suggest that you are welcome here. Postscript is an ever-evolving place of equal employment for talented individuals.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.
