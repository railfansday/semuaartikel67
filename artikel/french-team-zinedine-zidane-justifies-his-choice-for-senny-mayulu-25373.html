According to recent reports, while several withdrawals have affected the French team in recent days, such as Pierre Kalulu, replaced by Saël Kumbedi, Marcus Thuram, replaced by Éli Junior Kroupi, or even Aurélien Tchouaméni, who was not to be replaced, the now confirmed withdrawal of Kylian Mbappé against Italy pushed Zinedine Zidane to call on new reinforcements. The Blues coach therefore decided to promote Senny Mayulu, initially called up with the Espoirs. The Paris Saint-Germain titi therefore joins the A group for the clash against Italy in the Nations League. Present at a press conference this Wednesday evening, on the eve of the meeting, Zinedine Zidane explained his choice.

“He can play in this position (in 9) and in the middle of the field, he does good things with Paris, he is a young player, I like his technique, what he does, he was on our lists. We do this consistently. On a list of players and Senny is with us, I’m happy with that”explained the ex-Real Madrid coach. The French technician also clarified his wishes regarding the positioning of his players: “what I want, I want to see the player, in the selection when I want to see a player, it’s in his position.
