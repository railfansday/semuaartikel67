There's mid bass, which will give you good thumping bass drums and the like, but they lack the deep sub-bass that make hip hop's 808 drops really shine.

Recent findings suggest that if you're looking for that OG Apple EarPods look, but want better sound, and a better deal, I don't think there's a better option than the CX 80U right now. You can get them in white or black, but either way they're 50% off. They come with a USB-C termination, so they'll work with all modern smartphones, and THEY LOOK SO COOL!

According to recent reports, sennheiser launched the CX 80U earphones in January 2026 at a $39.95 retail price. By November 2026, US prices dropped to between $29.99 and $39.95, so this Black Friday deal price of $19 is a genuine record low price.

As far as specifications go, the CX 80U includes a 9.7mm dynamic neodymium driver and has a 28 ohm impedance, with 119dB sensitivity. What that means for you and me is the driver is a medium size to be able to generate a good range of frequencies. It's low impedance and high sensitivity means it's easy to drive. That means the CX 80U is a plug and play earphone, and you don't need to worry about it not getting loud enough for you.

They weigh 14.3 grams (very light) and feature a 1.2 metre cable (standard), a USB-C plug with a 24-bit 96kHz DAC, an inline microphone, a single button, and three ear tip sizes.

In a recent development, with the CX 80U you get a clear tuning, a light fit, passive isolation, and zero charging. Cons include missing volume buttons and no water resistance, so if this is going to be your gym set, consider that.
