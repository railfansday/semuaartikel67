Industry observers note that top of the Ticker: After a nearly 20-year run, Willie Geist will be departing MS NOW for an expanded role at NBC News. Geist will continue to focus his efforts on Sunday Morning and broaden his presence across Today and other NBC News platforms.

In announcing his departure on Monday, during Morning Joe, Geist said, “I am so grateful to both NBC and MSNOW for the unique opportunity to do what I love at both networks. He added, “This is a bittersweet goodbye to Joe, Mika, and the place that made possible everything else in my career over the last two decades, but I am thrilled to settle in with my family at Today and dedicate all of my time to that special place.”.

Geist was the only remaining on-air talent still pulling double duty for MS NOW and NBC News after NBCUniversal spun off its cable network assets into a new media tech giant, Versant, in 2025.

Recent findings suggest that continued independence: Skydance CEO David Ellison, during The Latest York Times’ DealBook Summit held on Wednesday, reiterated that its two news outlets, CBS News and CNN, will continue to operate as separate entities and be editorially independent. He did say there might be “some news sharing in the future,” but was happy with how they are currently positioned.

Industry observers note that distribution agreement: The free streaming Newsmax channel, Newsmax2, will now be available over the air across 112 television stations owned by HC2 Brodcasting. This widens Newsmax2’s footprint, as it will now be available in all of the top 40 U.S. television markets and 57 of the top 60.

Industry observers note that milestone unlocked: ABC News’ Sunday public affairs program This Week celebrated its 45th anniversary on Sunday, November 15. Highest honor: Meanwhile, Martha Raddatz received the National Press Club’s Fourth Estate Award at a gala on Nov. 12 in Washington, D.C. Present to witness this occasion were Raddatz’s ABC News colleagues, including Mary Bruce, Rachel Scott, Mark Burstein, and daughter Greta Williams.

Industry observers note that in addition, CNBC announced a slew of appointments to its editorial team, including Francesca Levy who joins as director of podcasts and digital shows on Dec. 7, reporting to Matt Quayle, senior executive producer of program development.
