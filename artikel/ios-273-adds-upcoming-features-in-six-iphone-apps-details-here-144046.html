Recent findings suggest that iOS 27.3 is coming soon, and it’s a substantial update for fresh iPhone features, including additions that span six built-in Apple apps. Here’s what’s new.

Apple Music adds a pair of upgrades in iOS 27.3. Selfie Sing is an all-latest feature that lets you record short videos of yourself singing along to your favorite songs, aided by onscreen lyrics. It’s perfect for sharing on TikTok or other social platforms.

In a recent development, if you’re not the type to share karaoke videos online, another nice iOS 27.3 change is that the lyrics view for songs has gotten an iPhone Duo-inspired upgrade in landscape mode. Now, artwork for the song you’re listening to blends beautifully into the stream of lyrics.
