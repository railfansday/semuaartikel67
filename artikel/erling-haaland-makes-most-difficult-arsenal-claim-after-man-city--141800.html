Erling Haaland admits Manchester City are ‘not clicking fully’ and believes his side now face the ‘most difficult game of the season’ against Arsenal this weekend.

City have now registered just two wins in their last seven games in all competitions after they came from behind twice to draw 2-2 at home to Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday night.

Haaland, meanwhile, endured another frustrating game as he failed to find the net and the Norway international registered just one shot on target against Napoli.

‘We managed to come back two times but we demand more, we have to do better, we have to find some ways to make us control the game more and perform in a better way,’ Haaland told beIN Sports after City’s draw at the Etihad Stadium.

‘I have no idea what we were missing, but you could feel it in the last few games that there’s something that’s not clicking fully and we have to find out and we have to stay positive and we have to get the best out of every single player.’.

In a separate post-match interview, Haaland added: ‘Football is so easy and so difficult. When you win games everything is easy and you know what to do, and when you don’t win you start to question everything. The perfect thing to do is don’t think.
