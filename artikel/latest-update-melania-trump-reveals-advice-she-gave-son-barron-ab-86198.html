Melania Trump has a few tips for her son, Barron Trump, as he navigates his dating life.

Melania, who shares Barron, 20, with her husband, President Donald Trump, recalled the advice she provided her son in the Friday, November 20 episode of her newly introduced Prime Video docuseries, MELANIA.

“I always say it’s a dangerous world out there,” Melania, 56, noted in the docuseries, adding, “and be careful.”.

While Melania may have some words of wisdom when it comes to dating, she emphasized that Barron can always turn to her for advice on anything, not just romance.

According to recent reports, “I will always be there for him,” she noted. “We have very open communication regarding life. I will always be there for him. I am, and I will always be. And he knows that he’s my priority, and he will always be my priority.”.

The former model also joked about her parenting style, declaring, “I’m a very cool mom to Barron, and I remind him all the time.”.

When asked whether Barron has any intentions of following in his father’s political footsteps, Melania stated it was his call.

Recent findings suggest that “I think it’s very important that he lives the life that he wants and what makes him happy. I don’t like to be controlling over him. He has his own mind, his own character, his own beliefs, and I respect that,” she remarked.

The First Lady added, “He likes politics. He gave his father incredible advice. He told him how to connect with the young voters, his generation. Told him who he needs to talk to. He introduced him to podcasts, to streamers.”.

During a January appearance on Fox Business’ Mornings With Maria, Melania again noted that her son’s wellbeing was of the utmost importance to her.

“You need to be there for a child nonstop, especially when they need you, especially at that age that Barron is,” she said.
