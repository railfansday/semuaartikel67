📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin is facing mixed reactions as mounting selling pressure continues, with large holders steadily reducing their exposure.

Meanwhile, the leading cryptocurrency is approaching a critical demand zone that previously sparked a major rally, putting the market at a crucial turning point.

According to market analyst Ali Martinez, Bitcoin whales have sold more than 34,000 BTC, worth approximately $2.6 billion, over the past week.

More notably, this sell-off coincided with BTC’s decline from $79,000 to below $73,000, signaling growing distribution among large holders.

Bitcoin is not out of the woods yet because bearish momentum has intensified since the apex cryptocurrency has fallen below the psychological price of $70,000 to trade at $67,209 per CoinGecko data.

It has been revealed that adding to the bearish outlook, longtime Bitcoin critic Peter Schiff has warned that the cryptocurrency’s long-term downtrend is far from over. His comments came after Bitcoin’s recent drop toward $68,000, reinforcing concerns that the current correction could deepen.

Despite the selling pressure engulfing the BTC market, on-chain metrics provider WealthManager highlights a potential opportunity for bulls.

Why is this the case? Well, BTC has returned to the demand zone that triggered its August rally, where buyers previously stepped in and fueled a substantial recovery.

According to recent reports, with Bitcoin now testing the same area again, the big question remains whether bulls will dust themselves off and defend it once more, because a successful hold could attract fresh demand and provide the foundation for a rebound. Nevertheless, a breakdown could expose Bitcoin to further losses.
