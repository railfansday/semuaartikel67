Leeds United striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin has aimed a slight dig at Chelsea after today’s major win at Stamford Bridge.

Calvert-Lewin scored from the penalty spot for Leeds as they picked up a surprise 4-1 victory away to the Blues, and it seems even the England international himself expected more from his opponents.

Chelsea went 1-0 up in the first half, but the lead didn’t last long and Xabi Alonso’s side ended up falling apart as Leeds ran riot.

See below for Calvert-Lewin’s post-match interview as he admitted he expected more from Chelsea and that they just couldn’t handle Leeds on the day….

"The intensity and the way we played, they couldn't handle it".

Calvert-Lewin praised the intensity Leeds played at against Chelsea today, saying: “I think we were going well, we just weren’t putting our chances away (in the first half).

“To be honest I expected Chelsea to come out with a bit more umph in the second half, but I think our intensity and the way that we played and the way that we set up against them, they couldn’t handle it.”.
