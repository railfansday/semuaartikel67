European Union top officials and UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham announced deals on trade and youth mobility at Friday's Brussels summit.

This might be the first step to rebuild ties, a decade after Britain voted to leave the EU.

During the delegations' meeting, Burnham said the deal was a "landmark agreement," while European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called it a "turning point" in relations.

"This is a good day for Britain. This is a good day for Europe," Burnham noted.

Speaking at the joint press conference, European Council President Antonio Costa said the bloc respected the British decision to leave the EU and would respect its choice to strengthen ties in the post-Brexit era.

He mentioned jokingly that the EU and UK "may drive on the opposite sides of the road, but what matters is they are heading for the same direction.".

Burnham said that the deals were made with young people in mind, adding: "This deal is for you. This deal really is about lifting your life chances.".

He went further and said "Brexit took opportunities from young people," and that they have to "open up latest opportunities" for young people.

Von der Leyen said the youth experience project would "open horizons and create friendships that will last a lifetime.".

She mentioned the trade agreement is a "two way street" and it means "more choice for the customers.".

Von der Leyen also mentioned the agreement on the carbon market, saying that it would cut emissions and generate investments in cleaner industries.

She also stated Britain will rejoin the Erasmus+ program, adding, "We miss them so much.".

Burnham remarked the agreements reached Friday, however, represent the most the UK could achieve at the moment, "What we are announcing today is probably ‌the limit of what we can achieve in this ‌Parliament, given what needs ‌to be done to implement it.".

In September, Burnham noted he would seek a political consensus on the country's future relationship with the EU, even suggesting he could campaign to take Britain back into the bloc at the next election.

"Britain and Europe share values, interests and a global neighborhood. In a more dangerous world, we are stronger together. A closer partnership with Europe is good for working people, good for Britain and good for Europe," Burnham stated as he arrived in Brussels.

The three key agreements establish ways to:.

The "youth experience scheme" would allow 100,000 people under 30 from each side to reside for up to three years in each other's territory for work, study or travel without being subject to existing visa requirements.

Sources indicate that no deal was reached on tuition fees for EU students studying in Britain, a deal-breaker for the bloc. The EU has pressed for the UK to reduce university tuition fees for EU students to the same level paid by British students.

"There will be further discussions coming out of it on complications including on tuition fees," Burnham said.

Von der Leyen ‌mentioned ⁠she was confident negotiators would reach a ​solution ​on ‌tuition fees, "We will be working ‌on that in ‌the ​next month and as we have a ​track record of finding ⁠solutions,.".

On the food deal, the EU and UK have agreed to scrap certificates and border checks on food and animal products to ease post-Brexit trade. In return, Britain will align more closely with EU food safety and animal health rules.

They also agreed to link their emissions-trading systems, allowing them to recognize each other's carbon allowances and exempt traded goods from carbon border taxes.

According to the AFP news agency, London estimates the deal could boost the British economy by 3.8 billion pounds annually, and spare 6.5 billion pounds worth of steel, aluminum and other industrial exports from additional taxation.

Leaders noted they will establish a "structured dialogue on migration." Their joint summit statement said this would include "engagement with third countries" and "returns to third countries," among other issues.

The two sides agreed to launch formal talks on economic security and strengthen cooperation on export controls and supply chains, among other areas.
