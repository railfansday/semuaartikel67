Hyatt bought Mr & Mrs Smith to bring boutique hotels into its loyalty program, and that’s working: World of Hyatt members now make most of its bookings. But those bookings bring in less money each, the UK business posted a loss in 2025, and Hyatt has taken a goodwill charge in the segment that includes it. Whether the deal pays off depends on gains elsewhere in Hyatt, which the manufacturer doesn’t break out.

Mr & Mrs Smith handled far more bookings last year than before Hyatt bought it, but earned less on each one, and its UK business posted a loss, according to newly filed accounts.

It has been revealed that smith Global, the UK organization behind the boutique hotel booking site, made 209,565 bookings in 2025, up 41% from the year before, according to accounts filed with the UK’s Companies House business register. More than half of those bookings came from members of World of Hyatt, Hyatt’s free loyalty program.

Industry observers note that hyatt paid £58 million ($72 million) in cash for Mr & Mrs Smith in June 2023 to bring boutique and independent hotels into World of Hyatt, Skift reported. About half the collection — more than 1,250 properties — can now be booked on hyatt.com, Hyatt says.
