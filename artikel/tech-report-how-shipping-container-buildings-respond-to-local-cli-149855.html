Industry observers note that code compliance: Conversions must align with local zoning ordinances, building standards, and regional energy codes (e.g., California Title 24). Procurement: When sourcing shipping containers in the United States, choose a vetted local supplier to minimize transport costs and carbon footprint. Foundation/site access: Depending on soil stability, the container may need screw piles, pier footings, or a gravel pit foundation. There must also be enough clearance for positioning with a truck or crane.
