Lego’s New Formula 1 Speed Champions Collection Includes Tiny Versions of All 22 Current F1 Drivers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It’s been two years since Lego and Formula 1 first partnered up to translate the excitement of the grand prix grid into tiny, plastic, painful-to-step-on brick form. But while the race cars were a realistic low-polygon tribute to the life-size real things, the minifigures that accompanied them were fairly generic. Until now, that is, because the upcoming Lego Speed Champions lineup features all 11 teams on the 2026 grid and all 22 full-time drivers, rendered with realistic driving suits and customized hair.

Industry observers note that declared at the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix against a backdrop of several giant minifigures and a full complement of Lego-clad go-karts (now updated with neon LED light strips for the full Vegas experience), this next batch of Speed Champions sets will go on sale March 1, 2027. That makes for plenty of time to build all 11 before the first race of the season in Bahrain, although it’s also yet another thing to wait for during the off-season.

In a recent development, arvid Lindblad, driver for the Visa Cash App Racing Bulls Team, made at appearance at Lego’s press announcement, and posed with both his large and small Lego likenesses. Asked about his thoughts on the result, the British driver said: “Awesome, nailed it. They got the hair perfect,” adding that, “I’ve always loved Legos, though I never imagined having my own minifigure.”.

And speaking of hair, yes, the Valteri Bottas minifigure is rocking a mullet. Each plastic hairpiece was approved by the respective drivers. “When we pitched the the hairstyles to the teams and the drivers, they absolutely loved it,” said Will Thorogood, the Lego designer who led this effort. “They all thought it was as funny as we did.”.

Industry observers note that another key part of bringing out the driver’s personality is the helmets, which are “far more detailed than any helmet we've ever done before with the with the Lego production,” according to Thorogood. Lando Norris’s helmet, for example, “has all the blob details on it” as well as a minifigure Easter egg hidden in the design. Each driver also comes with two facial expressions on opposite sides of the minfigure head; Charles Leclerc’s visage is either relaxed in sunglasses or a more serious grimace, both with the Ferrari driver’s stubbled mustache and goatee.

The cars themselves reflect the new regulations and, notes Thorogood, “as you look closely, you'll see every single one is built completely differently to capture all the aero details and principles that each of the teams has employed.” These too feature several Easter eggs for Lego fans, such as suspension wishbones made from wizard wands and rear diffuser strakes made from minifigure meat cleavers.

In a recent development, measuring 7.5 inches long and 2.5 inches wide, the Speed Champions sets will be priced at $29.99 when they go on sale next year. More photos and details are viewable now on the Speed Champions section of the Lego Website.
