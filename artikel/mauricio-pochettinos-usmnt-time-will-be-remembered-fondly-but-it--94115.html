The incoming Manchester United manager improved the US team’s culture and competitiveness, but could not bring about sea change in two years.

Sources indicate that in a theater high up in a midtown Manhattan skyscraper built from lots of glass, a highlight reel played to deafening music. And then, on “a monumental day for US Soccer”, federation president, Cindy Parlow Cone, announced they had bagged their man, Mauricio Pochettino, to become the highest-profile United States manager in the men’s national team’s history.

Pochettino held court for three-quarters of an hour in his signature blend of Spanglish before the assembled media and a grab bag of sponsors. Then he recorded some TV hits. And after that, in a bit of accidental prescience, he went off to record a podcast with Gary Neville.
