Sources indicate that before you donate used books, sort them by who will read them: picture books for a classroom, novels for your library’s used-book sale, and paperbacks for a prison book program. Then check what each place accepts, because many turn away encyclopedia sets, old textbooks, and water-damaged copies.

It has been revealed that A box left at the library’s front desk isn’t sure to find a reader. Most public libraries accept donated books only if they’re free to keep each one, sell it at a book sale, or discard it, the American Library Association says.

Industry observers note that every place that takes used books has its own readers and its own limits, often on a book’s format, age, or condition. Encyclopedia sets and books with mold, water stains, or missing pages are the hardest to place, since few groups take them at all.
