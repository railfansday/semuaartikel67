Not content with being the biggest entertainment launch in history and the highest rated game of all time, GTA 6 has already broken another record.

The game’s official album, which features all-new tracks from the likes of Dua Lipa, set a new opening day record for a soundtrack on Spotify with a dizzying 29.85 million streams.

The compilation has 34 songs, including music from artists like Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, and Jennie.

I was listening to the album yesterday and, while I was already familiar with a lot of the songs from my time in Leonida writing the review, I do think it’s a good and eclectic mix. I’m not surprised to see it break records.
