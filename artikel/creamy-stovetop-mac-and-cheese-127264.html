We had a little welcome home party at the end of the bike ride, and I made this mac and cheese for 25 people at that party. Multiple friends came up and asked me for the recipe.

Since then, I’ve made this several more times, always when we have friends for dinner or I’m feeding a massive group of people.

So in my mind, this is a party mac and cheese. You can serve it to a lot of people, and mostly everyone will love it.

Industry observers note that if you want to prep it ahead (which I do quite a bit, being that it’s party mac and cheese) – I cook the noodles ahead, grate the cheese ahead, and even make the bechamel sauce with the flour, butter, milk, and cream. Then, right before serving, I re-warm the sauce (adding water and whisking to bring make it smooth again), gently melt the cheese in, and toss the noodles in the sauce.
