Tottenham Hotspur recorded three successive home Premier League wins for the first time in two years as they swept Ipswich Town aside on Saturday afternoon.

Tottenham were looking to bounce back from a disappointing 1-0 defeat to Leeds United at Elland Road before the international break, and Roberto De Zerbi made the substantial call to hand 18-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett his first competitive start for the club on the left wing.

Spurs fans have long been calling to see the teenager handed a start after what they had seen from the youngster in pre-season and in glimpses off the substitutes' bench this term, and his Italian manager decided to blood him in the absence of the injured Mathys Tel.

It was still a significant decision given Dejan Kulusevski, Omar Marmoush and Savio were named on the substitutes' bench, but he repaid his manager's faith with a stunning display that will surely see him retain his spot against Sunderland next Sunday.

Williams-Barnett took just 15 minutes to open his account for the club as he finished from close range following a fine cross from the right by Pedro Porro, before assisting Dominic Solanke's goal in the second half following a low ball in from the left flank.

The 18-year-old was a huge threat throughout his 64 minutes on the pitch, and mucked in from a defensive point of view as well to help the team keep a clean sheet.

Williams-Barnett for Tottenham vs Ipswich.

Williams-Barnett will take all the plaudits, and rightly so, but his teammate Porro was also influential in the victory against the Tractor Boys.

In a summer when Tottenham paid £100m to sign a player, and brought in nine more, arguably their best bit of summer business was tying Porro down to a new long-term contract at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Fresh on the back of winning the World Cup with Spain, there would have been concerns that the 27-year-old would attract interest from some of Europe's biggest clubs, especially after interest from Barcelona and Real Madrid previously.

However, Porro had already committed his future to Spurs by penning a five-year deal in June, extending his stay in North London even though the club only escaped relegation to the Championship on the final day of last season.

There have been a few ups and downs in terms of his performances this term, including a poor outing against Leeds last time out, but the Spaniard was back to his best and on top form against Ipswich.

As we mentioned previously, it was his cross that gave Williams-Barnett his first goal in a Tottenham shirt, and his display led to him actually being given a higher rating than the teenager on Fotmob (8.3 compared to 8.1).

He did everything he could to help Spurs in the final third, while he also completed his defensive duties to help the hosts keep a clean sheet.

Tottenham have made a significant improvement after a tough start to the season under De Zerbi's leadership, and there is no doubt that they look a much better team when Porro is on his game.
