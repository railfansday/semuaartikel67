AAWireless, the original wireless Android Auto adapter brand, is introducing fresh hardware this week with its compact Nexus and Nano adapters.

The upcoming AAWireless Nexus and Nano are both designed as smaller adapters compared to the main Two and Two+ models. Both sold for $69.99 ($74.99 on Amazon), these are both drastically smaller – something that we’ve seen pop up from other adventurous brands, but now we’re getting it with a known name in the space.

According to recent reports, AAWireless Nexus features a circular puck design with a USB-C plug that has a replaceable cable. AAWireless Nano, meanwhile, is an adapter on the end of a USB-A plug. It’s about as small as this concept can get.

Sources indicate that both adapters feature an upgraded chipset, Wi-Fi 6 support, and both Android Auto and CarPlay. They also feature physical buttons, just like the AAWireless Two series.

Industry observers note that pre-registration is open today, with early orders available starting on December 1. The actual release date is December 8.
