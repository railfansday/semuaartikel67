Latest Update: OKX’s 24/7 stock trading launch saw just 7% of first-day volume come from customers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

OKX opened round-the-clock trading in six stock tokens at noon ET on Nov. 23, allowing eligible US customers to use the USDC stablecoin to buy and sell the tokens in the OKX app after the stock market closes.

The opening lineup pairs Coinbase (COIN), Securitize (SECZ), Strategy (MSTR), BitGo (BTGO), Robinhood (HOOD) and Circle (CRCL) with USDC.

All six recorded trades on Nov. 23 and were still the only pairs in the venue’s public asset list when checked Nov. 24. OKX indicates it has confirmed 113 assets for a rolling rollout, including stocks, ETFs and exchange-traded products.

Customers must meet geographic eligibility rules, verify their identity, activate a self-custodial CeDeFi Wallet and complete onboarding.

According to recent reports, the venue screens wallets and checks their permission before a trade. OKXICE indicates Securitize Markets tokenized the six launch stocks and that underlying shares back the tokens one-for-one through the tokenizer’s brokerage arrangements.

OKXICE LLC operates the service. The company is a joint venture between affiliates of OKX and Intercontinental Exchange, the parent company of the Fresh York Stock Exchange.

OKXICE indicates it operates under temporary, conditional SEC relief from the statutory definition of an exchange, and OKX says the NYSE has no role in running or overseeing the venue.

The venue indicates it stops trading a token when the exchange that lists the underlying stock halts or suspends the shares.

According to recent reports, in that case, the token can resume trading only after the primary listing exchange resumes trading in the stock. The venue’s rules also provide for stoppages tied to volume limits and risk controls.

Recent findings suggest that trades execute in Uniswap v4 liquidity pools on OKX’s X Layer network. A buyer adds USDC to the pool and takes stock tokens out, pushing the pool price up, while a sale pushes it down.

The venue simulates the order against available liquidity before showing a quote. Liquidity providers can commit funds to specific price ranges, so order size and available funds near the current price affect what a customer gets.

USDC and stock tokens change hands through the same Uniswap v4 pool.

OKX states those trades can clear at a different price from the underlying share, especially outside regular market hours. At midnight ET on Nov. 24, OKX’s daily statistics showed about $1.22 million held across the six pools.

In a recent development, robinhood’s pool held about $353,000 and Securitize’s held about $31,000. Customer wallets accounted for $121,000 of the $1.64 million in trades tracked by Allium’s first-day analysis through 4 p.m. ET on Nov. 23.
