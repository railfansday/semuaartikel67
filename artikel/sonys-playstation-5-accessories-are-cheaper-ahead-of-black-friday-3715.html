According to recent reports, several retailers are offering discounts on PlayStation 5 games, including deep price cuts on recent console-exclusive hits like Marvel’s Wolverine, Ghost of Yotei, Saros, and hundreds more. We’ll cover a bunch of those in the coming days, but this post is focused on the great hardware discounts that are happening, including $20 off the stellar DualSense wireless controller and its many color schemes, as well as $30 off the customizable DualSense Edge. The substantial sale also extends to headsets, console covers, and even Sony’s 27-inch monitor that has a hook to charge your controller.

Sitting out from the discounts, at least for now, are fresh PlayStation 5 consoles. It’s not impossible that a deal will surface later this month, though it’s more unlikely to happen than in past years for a couple of reasons. For one, console deals are exceedingly rare due to the rise in costs for the consoles associated with RAMageddon. Perhaps the bigger reason is Grand Theft Auto VI, a system-selling game launching November 19th that will likely convince a lot of people to pay full price for a console. Not to worry, though.
