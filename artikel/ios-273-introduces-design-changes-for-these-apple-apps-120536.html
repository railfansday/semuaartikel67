iOS 27.3 includes many new features for Apple’s built-in apps, but it also introduces design changes on the iPhone across Calendar, Safari, Music, and more. Here’s what’s new.

Apple is always iterating on the design of its software. While major overhauls like iOS 26’s Liquid Glass don’t come along often, the company makes smaller design changes all the time via software updates. In iOS 27.3, which will launch to all consumers next month, Apple has design changes coming to four popular apps: Health, Calendar, Safari, and Music.

The Health app gets the biggest design changes.

As seen above, Health introduces brand fresh Insights, Longevity, and Browse tabs that replace the previous options and include a variety of fresh UI elements like a top navigation row, personalized prompts and coaching, educational videos, and a lot more.

As the side-by-side above indicates, the buttons for search and creating a latest event have moved out of the top-right corner and into the bottom of the screen.

Calendar also now highlights the year at the top of the screen next to the current month, and has updated the ‘Today’ button to show the current date instead.

In a recent development, safari introduces an optional latest design for tabs: Vertical Tabs. You can enable these inside Settings ⇾ Apps ⇾ Safari for a different approach to tabs across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Here’s an example of vertical tabs on iPhone Duo.

Vertical Tabs won’t be for everyone, but they’ve proven popular in other browsers, so it’s great to see Safari add the option.

It has been revealed that song artwork is now able to break the constraints of a square and blend seamlessly into the scrolling lyrics view. Here are examples from Beta Profiles on X:.

According to recent reports, new in iOS 27.3 Beta: Animated artwork blends into the Apple Music player in landscape mode, and it looks so good. pic.twitter.com/k8rgSEALGo.

This lyrics view was previously available on iPhone Duo, but now it’s coming to all iPhones.
