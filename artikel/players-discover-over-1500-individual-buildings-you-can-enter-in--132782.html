One GTA 6 player has put in the legwork and found that a shocking number of buildings across Leonida are freely accessible.

Industry observers note that per a Reddit post by Aileos, 1,563 of the game's 5,588 buildings can be entered, coming in at 27.97 percent of structures in the game. Some of those are ones you'd expect of course: stores, banks, and the like. But plenty of them may surprise you. The next time you're walking across a neighbourhood, pick a house and just walk inside… in-game, of course.

In a recent development, A funny quirk with this discovery is its relation to previous leaks for Grand Theft Auto 6. Back in 2025 it was claimed that over 700 buildings can be freely entered in GTA 6, something that received ample doubt from those anticipating the Rockstar release. Take this comment from user Macklaviel: "There's a difference between proven true and not proven wrong yet. 700 interiors would be astonishingly good but it sets the expectations too high and it realistically will be way less than that.".

It has been revealed that plenty of the best secrets in GTA 6 can be found off the beaten trail. Random drops of illicit goods can be found all over the place. So, if you're having a blast making your way through the game, take an hour or so just to wander around the place and take in the scenery.
