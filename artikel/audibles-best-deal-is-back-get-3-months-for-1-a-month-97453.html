In addition to a wide selection of books, Audible has exclusives and originals. Originals include the Andy Weir-produced Exoplanet, written by Benjamin Percy and read by Anthony Mackie, Ariana DeBose, and John Leguizamo. Audible features podcasts as well, including A Great Book With Barack Obama, a podcast hosted by the former president with guests including Tom Hanks and Ta-Nehisi Coates.

The top titles on Audible include Suzanne Collins’ Sunrise on the Reaping, narrated by Jefferson White, Matt Dinniman’s Dungeon Crawler Carl, narrated by Jeff Hays, and Exoplanet.

Sources indicate that with a standard monthly subscription, Audible members can choose one audiobook a month to listen to. The selected audiobook stays in your collection, meaning you can revisit favorite titles. Additional books can be purchased at full price.

The premium membership starts at $14.95/mo. In addition to one book credit a month, members get access to any of the titles in the all-you-can-listen-to catalogue and credits that roll over month to month.

Memberships can be cancelled at any time, and members can keep any title they purchased with a credit even if their membership is no longer active.

Recent findings suggest that as for the Audible app, it features adjustable playback speeds, and you can set a timer to pause playback if you’re listening before bed. Other features include car mode, which enlarges app icons for easier one-handed control. Users can also download titles for offline listening while traveling.
