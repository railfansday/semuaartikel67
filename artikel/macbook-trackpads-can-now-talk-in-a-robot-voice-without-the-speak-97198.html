MacBook trackpads can now talk in a robot voice without the speakers, thanks to a free tool

🌐 Source: Digitaltrends • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (50 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that nospeaker is a free open-source tool that drives a MacBook's Force Touch haptic motor up to 4,000 times a second so the trackpad can speak text you type. It is quieter on your lap and has only been tested on one Mac.
