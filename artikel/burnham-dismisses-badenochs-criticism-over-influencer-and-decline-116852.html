Diane Taylor is a Guardian reporter who covers migration.

Britons get many of the facts about migration wrong and Reform UK voters are the group who get these facts most wrong, according to newly introduced research published today by King’s College University and YouGov.

Among Reform UK supporters, 57% think net migration rose significantly in the year to June 2025, compared with 11% of Labour supporters and 8% of Greens. Eight in 10 Reform supporters think that asylum seekers are prioritised for social housing, compared with 21% of Green supporters.

It is wrong to believe that if we could only correct misperceptions, we would automatically reduce concerns about immigration or policy preferences – because that misdiagnoses a large part of the cause, that these divides are partly driven by deep differences in values and identity.
