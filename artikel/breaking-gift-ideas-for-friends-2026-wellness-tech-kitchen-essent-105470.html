It has been revealed that in writing this guide on gift ideas for friends, I learned that there really is no catchall gift that will please everyone. Choosing these gifts, I thought back to some of the best self-care products and tech I’ve tested recently. I included the gadgets I reach for regularly, as well as those I’ve bought for family and friends after testing and loving them. So please use this guide as a starting point, keeping your friend’s personality, needs, and hobbies in mind as you shop for the right gifts.

After testing hundreds of products during my years on the WIRED Reviews team, these are the items I’d recommend to the friends on my own list. I’ve included self-care gifts like massagers, cozy gifts like an ultrasoft throw blanket, kitchen staples like a chef’s knife, and everything in between. There are many gifts at different price points, and they’re also categorized by gift type below. Be sure to check back often, as I will be updating this guide with versatile new picks as we get closer to gifting season.
