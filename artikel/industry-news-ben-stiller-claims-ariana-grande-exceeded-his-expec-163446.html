Ariana Grande was the newest addition to the Focker family this Thanksgiving.

Following a 16-year gap since the last film in the Meet the Parents franchise, Little Fockers, hit theaters, a fourth installment, Focker-in-Law, was released this week by Universal and Paramount Pictures. Grande stars as Olivia, a former FBI hostage negotiator who accompanies her boyfriend Henry Focker (Skyler Gisondo), the son of Greg Focker (Ben Stiller), to a family reunion celebrating his grandfather Jack’s (Robert De Niro) 80th birthday.

Grande’s performance has received raves, including from The Hollywood Reporter, where critic Frank Scheck called her “the best thing in the film, going toe-to-toe with her more cinematically experienced co-stars and displaying both formidable steeliness and deadpan comic skills.” Stiller, who shares multiple scenes with Grande as his character attempts to sabotage Henry’s proposal to Olivia throughout the film, wasn’t surprised by the Grammy winner’s acting chops. “I thought she was incredible in Wicked. I was pleasantly surprised by how incredible an actor she is to work with, and I wasn’t not expecting that, but she really surpassed all my expectations in terms of her sense of timing, her character and being able to pull off a character like this. It’s very specific,” he told THR of Grande, who’s returned to acting, alongside her music career in recent years, earning an Oscar nomination for best supporting actress at the 97th Academy Awards for Wicked. “She was great to work with and she’s just a really nice person and fun to hang out with.”.

Stiller also recalled one scene that put him in an interesting position: performing in front of the pop star. One of Greg’s attempts to stop Henry from proposing comes when he serenades his father-in-law, Jack, in front of the whole family, singing John Legend’s “All of Me” — the song Henry intended to sing to Olivia before popping the question.

“She was on set watching the whole time and she was incredibly generous and sweet and very supportive. I knew if I tried my best, it probably would be good enough for the movie,” Stiller mentioned with a laugh. “But it was interesting because I had to do it a lot in front of her. It was an interesting day.”.

Beanie Feldstein, who plays Henry’s sister Samantha, also had high praise for Grande. “Ari is the most incredible person on planet Earth. I love her more than life itself, and she’s a tour de force in this movie,” she said.

As the Fockerverse has spanned 26 years, Stiller isn’t convinced that John Hamburg’s Focker-in-Law will be the end of the franchise, though he’s leaving that decision up to audiences. “I think if the audience wants one, there is probably another story there,” he said.

Recent findings suggest that and it were up to Stiller? “I would do it for sure,” he quipped.

Focker-in-Law is now playing in theaters.
