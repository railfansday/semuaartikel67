📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that if you’re an Advisory Opinions fan and live in or near Texas, we’ve got some good news: AO will be doing live tapings in Houston on Saturday, Dec. 5, and Austin on Sunday, Dec. 6. Tickets are still available for both indicates.

Industry observers note that on Wednesday, the battle over the Temporary Protected Status program returned to the Supreme Court. The Trump administration asked the justices to block a September 2025 ruling that kept the program in place for Venezuelan nationals. For more on the request, see the On Site section below.

Recent findings suggest that the December argument session will begin on Monday, Nov. 30.
