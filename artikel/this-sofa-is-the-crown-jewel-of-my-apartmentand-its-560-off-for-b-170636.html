As a shopping and design editor, I pride myself on being the go-to person for home shopping advice. And at a dinner party, the first question I usually get is where to shop for a good couch. The good news? Now is the best time to invest in a new, high-quality sofa. Black Friday deals are underway on nearly all of the furniture brands we trust. Including the sofa brand two editors have tested and loved: Albany Park. I test dozens of new furniture pieces a year, and my sofa has remained in my apartment the whole time. So believe me when I say that Albany Park’s sofa sale is not one you want to miss.

Shop Albany Park's Holiday Sale.

Here’s what you need to know to take advantage of the biggest savings: Albany Park's Holiday Sale is live from now through Sunday, December 6. For the next week, you can save up to 30 percent on the brand's cushiest seating—no code needed. Plus, you'll get free white-glove delivery and three free pillows with any sofa.

Industry observers note that albany Park is one of our favorite luxury furniture brands, and for good reason. They have a pretty curated selection, with three main lines that fit a range of aesthetics. The discounts depend on your line of choice: you'll save 25 percent on Lido & Barton seating, and 30 percent on the Kova collection.

According to recent reports, so which line is right for you? If you're a midcentury modern devotee, give the Lido collection a try. The tapered legs and sleek lines will blend into any room. But my personal favorite is the Barton line. I have the Barton sofa and storage ottoman in my apartment, both upholstered in a dreamy teal velvet. The jewel tone shines throughout the day, making my apartment feel extra luxe. I dream about coming home and collapsing into it, and the sturdy, low-profile frame keeps my home feeling modern. Even in a smaller space, it's deceptively spacious. And the velvet has only gotten softer over time, to the point where I look forward to mid-afternoon naps on it.

Don't just take my word for Albany Park's quality, though. The deals are even better on the cloud-like, modular Kova sofa, which Senior Commerce Editor Marina Liao swears by. “It's the perfect in-between of a super soft and very firm couch,” Liao notes. “The cushions don't need fluffing, and the performance linen is very low maintenance to care for.” Plus, you can rearrange it into the ultra-trendy pit sofa for the perfect family movie night seating. The brand offers stain-resistant and pet-friendly upholstery, so it'll withstand even the busiest of households.

If your sofa has seen better days, and you've been waiting for the right time to upgrade it, your time has come. Bonus: if you’re in the market for a larger sectional, the savings get into the thousands. You don't have long to get this editor-approved furniture brand on discount, so I suggest you act fast. Your purchase will last for years to come.
