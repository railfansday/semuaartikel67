📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that GTA 6's Ultimate Edition has proven enormously popular, despite its hefty $100 price tag. So much so that Strauss Zelnick, boss of Rockstar parent organization Take-Two, recently confirmed it's the edition most people are buying - and that's despite a lack of specific information about how its unlockable content works. With GTA 6 just hours away from release, however - and out now in certain parts of the work - details are beginning to emerge, and the answer is perhaps a little surprising.

As previously confirmed, the $100/£90 Ultimate Edition provides access to exclusive vehicles, weapons, shops, and more, with the full list as follows:.

Rockstar also previously revealed that "Ultimate Edition bonuses become available to Jason and Lucia across their story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter". The specifics of how - and when - all this stuff unlocks, though, remained unclear. So if you've opted for the Ultimate Edition, you might be wondering where your stuff is when you finally fire up the game. As reported by IGN, which has full unlock details, it turns out virtually all of GTA 6's Ultimate Edition content, as per details in the in-game shop, requires progressing through the main story - in some cases dozens and dozens of hours into the story - before it'll become available.

Recent findings suggest that ultimate Edition-exclusive shops, for instance, unlock during Chapter 1, and many of the other additions become available by the end of Chapter 2. That doesn't sound too difficult, but as Eurogamer's Chris Tapsell notes, reaching that point could take upward of 25 hours. As for some of the late-game unlocks, such as the Shitzu Squalo and the Vice City Style outfits and tattoos (available in Chapter 4) and Chapter 5's 95 Grotti Cheetah, you could be looking at closing in on 60 hours of playtime before they unlock.

"It'll vary depending how hard people focus on the main story," Chris explains, "but if you were mostly focused on the main story plus the optional character-based missions in largely chronological order, that's about how long it'd take.".

Sources indicate that it's also worth noting - perhaps surprisingly - that some Ultimate Edition content is only a temporary unlock, usable only during specific chapters. Jason's Dinka Enduro motorcycle and Crest Kayak, for instance, can only be accessed and enjoyed during Chapter 1. That's also true for his retro Vapid Ganado, available from the Coastal Auto Care mod shop in Key Lento. So basically, you'll want to make the most of these while you can!

Recent findings suggest that all this might be a little disappointing for players who were expecting to be showered with Ultimate Edition spoils from the off (and that they'd be permanent unlocks), but the good news is 60 hours in GTA 6 is far from a chore. As Chris explained in his five star GTA 6 review, Rockstar's latest is an "irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action" that "pushes back the horizon of video games once again".
