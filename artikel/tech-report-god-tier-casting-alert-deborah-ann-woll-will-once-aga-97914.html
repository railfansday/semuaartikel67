Faye will be in God of War's Amazon show – played by game actor Deborah Ann Woll.

As unveiled by Amazon, Woll is once more playing Faye, after making her first appearance as the character – the mother of Atreus and longtime partner of Kratos – in flashbacks and vision sequences during 2022's God of War Ragnarok.

Woll joins Dave Bautista's Kratos and Callum Vinson's Atreus in a first season that will likely mirror much of 2018's God of War narrative – namely, how Kratos and Atreus journey on a quest to spread Faye's ashes.

Bautista replaced original Kratos actor Ryan Hurst after he suffered an injury during production, forcing cameras to stop rolling.

Woll will also portray Faye once more in next year's standalone game God of War Laufey, which sees her fight through the mysterious Everywhen to enact her plans to save Kratos and her son, Atreus. She's even accompanied by a mysterious talking cube, voiced by The Boys actor Jack Quaid. Naturally.

"I've known about this for nearly ten years, and I haven't been able to talk about it." Woll, who also stated she wasn't put off by the negative response to the title, explained earlier this year at Game Con Canada (via CG Mag).

According to recent reports, she added, "When [director] Cory [Barlog] brought me in to talk about doing Ragnarok and show me the 2018 game, he already had a poster for the Laufey game with me and a cube. A cube that's been there since the beginning. It's deeply a part of the lore.".
