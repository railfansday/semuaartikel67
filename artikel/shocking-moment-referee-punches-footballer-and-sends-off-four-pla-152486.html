Published: 09:18 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 09:19 EST, 27 November 2026.

Bolivian referee Jordy Aleman was stunningly seen appearing to punch a player after being surrounded in the aftermath of the Bolivian Cup final.

Footage showed Aleman being surrounded by Real Potosi players following the final whistle of the match, the second leg of the final.

Real Potosi had proved unable to overturn a 3-1 first leg deficit against Always Ready in their home leg as they were held to a goalless draw.

Real Potosi had finished the match down to 10 men after Kevin Villegas was sent off in stoppage time for violent conduct.

Players had surrounded Aleman following the final whistle to contest refereeing decisions during the match, with the official then appearing to strike out at Real Potosi goalkeeper Christian Martinez.

According to Marca, the referee then demonstrated a further four red cards to players and dismissed a further three members of Real Potosi's coaching staff.

It has been revealed that martinez later admitted to insulting the referee, who was surrounded by security after the incident. Source: ESPN.

Footage then showed Aleman surrounded by security in the aftermath of the incident.

Martinez later revealed that he had told the official to 'go to hell' and that he 'hoped his mother dies', leading to the referee striking out at him.

The Argentine goalkeeper remarked he had been left with a bloodied mouth following the punch.

Martinez later issued a statement on social media to confirm he will not pursue legal action or make a complaint about the official following the extraordinary incident.

'After what happened at the end of the second leg of the Paceña Cup final against Always Ready, where I was punched by referee Jordy Alemán Peralta, I have decided not to initiate legal action or file complaints with the sports authorities,' Martinez wrote on social media.

'My intention is to put this episode behind me and prevent this situation from continuing to generate conflicts, while remaining fully available to the competent authorities for any clarification that may be necessary.

'Nothing should overshadow the extraordinary season we've had,.

'We competed until the very last minute and gave everything on the field for this jersey.'.

The Bolivian Football Federation are yet to confirm the sanction Aleman will receive following the incident.

The organisation's disciplinary rules give them the power to impose a ban of up to three years, as well as a fine equivalent to 20 times the national minimum wage.
