Recent findings suggest that I'm going to hold your hand when I say this: most streaming deals aren't going to blow you away. In fact, over the last few years, Black Friday streaming deals have gotten significantly less impressive. Still, any and every discount is welcome in a time where prices keep rising.

Last year, the Disney+ and Hulu bundle Black Friday deal was one of my favorites at $4.99/month for 12 months. I expect to see something similar this year, but likely a couple of dollars more. And before you ask, yes. It will be the ad-supported version.

Paramount+ will likely drop a streaming deal similar to its Prime Video add-on deal: a steep discount on its Premium ad-free subscription for a limited number of months.

According to recent reports, peacock used to offer the best Black Friday streaming deal around, but last year it gave us nothing. And that was after raising costs from $7.99 to $10.99 per month. This year, the price jumped up to $12.99 per month and I'm not holding out hope to see any kind of deal.

In a recent development, netflix has never offered a Black Friday discount, or any kind of discount for that matter. I don't expect this year to be any different.
