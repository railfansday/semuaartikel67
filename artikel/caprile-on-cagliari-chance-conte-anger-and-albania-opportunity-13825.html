Elia Caprile has been the most effective goalkeeper in Serie A this season at Cagliari, but reveals Antonio Conte ‘did not take it well’ when he left Napoli, is open to playing for Albania, and nearly gave up his career.

The 25-year-old has kept four clean sheets in 11 Serie A games so far this season, more than any other goalkeeper in the division, and also made more saves than anyone else.

Despite that, he knows that he’s very far behind in the ranking for Italy, where Gianluigi Donnarumma, Guglielmo Vicario, Marco Carnesecchi and Alex Meret are all ahead of him.

“I would love to silence all those who wouldn’t bet a cent on me when I was little, that fire inside me is still burning,” Caprile told the Corriere della Sera newspaper.

Sources indicate that “I thought of quitting so many times, especially when a few coaches were mean to me. I wasn’t playing, I was shorter than the other kids, I was fat.

According to recent reports, “I was made to feel bad about it and lost my way, but I got advice from two goalkeeping coaches Bressan and Squizzi at the moment I needed them the most. They showed me football is made of sacrifice, both before and after training.”.

Caprile has never played for Italy at senior level, and now has Albanian citizenship thanks to his partner.

According to recent reports, “I don’t know if something can come from it, so far no Federation has knocked at my door. If they do, then I will evaluate.”.

He already made the decision to walk away from Napoli in 2025 when frustrated at the lack of playing time, and took the opportunity to shine at Cagliari.

Recent findings suggest that “Conte told me to stay and fight for my place with Meret, so he was honest, and so was I. He chose Alex and won the Scudetto, so it was wise, but I was 23 and really wanted to play. I felt the physical need to be on the pitch,” explained Caprile.

“So in January, I told him I wanted to leave. Conte did not take it well, but he understood.”.
