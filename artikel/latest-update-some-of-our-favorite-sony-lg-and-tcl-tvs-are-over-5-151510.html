Most of us spend a lot of time with our TVs, be it watching movies, TV reveals, the news, or playing games. It’s part of being a fan of technology. And while they’re massive purchases, they can last for years. But eventually, the time comes when we need a upcoming one. Has that time come for you? Black Friday sales will make it easier to find something great for a lot less than it’d usually cost.

According to recent reports, with several different types of TVs out there, it can get confusing which ones are worth it. Are the fresh RGB LED TVs all they’re cracked up to be? (Sometimes.) Does OLED still deliver the best contrast available? (Yup.) Are tried-and-true LED TVs still worth it in 2026 for those on a budget? (Absolutely.) One thing you can be sure of, is any TV I recommend here is more than worth the price. If you need a TV, Black Friday is the time to buy.

TCL came out swinging early this year with its flagship X11L. It gets blazingly bright, has great color accuracy, and is now thousands of dollars cheaper than its original price. Read our review.

After a prolonged period of quiet, Vizio is back with a good Mini LED TV at a great price. The Quantum 4K offers solid brightness and contrast thanks to its small but mighty backlighting; however, its best feature is that it’s easy to make dumb and keep your viewing data private.

It has been revealed that despite being an entry-level variant of the B6 OLED, this model features four HDMI 2.1 ports, a 120Hz refresh rate, a capable processor, and pixel-perfect image quality and contrast for any content you want to watch. Lower brightness is likely the biggest compromise with getting a cheaper OLED.

The QM8L uses the same SQD technology as the X11L, which provides higher brightness and more colors, but at a fraction of the cost.

The Bravia 7 II is Sony’s first RGB LED TV. Its screen is a little reflective, but the picture is absolutely beautiful thanks to the color capability of the RGB LED backlight and Sony’s stellar processing. Read our review.

Sources indicate that hisense’s 2026 CanvasTV is a 4K QLED TV that doubles as wall art when you’re not watching. It features a matte, anti-glare display and runs on Google’s excellent TV software; it’s also a great pick for gamers thanks to its snappy 144Hz variable refresh rate and a pair of HDMI 2.1 ports.

Sources indicate that the U7SG was a little pricey when it came out, but now it’s a fantastic deal. It gets bright enough for rooms with lots of light and handles reflections well. Read our review.

Sources indicate that the Samsung S95H OLED is one of the best of the year (and won Value Electronics’ TV Shootout). It features a glare-free screen, incredible contrast, and excellent color accuracy right out of the box.

In a recent development, roku now makes some of the most affordable, yet feature-packed 4K OLED TVs available for gamers or movie lovers looking for a major TV upgrade under $1,000. The Pro Series LX with better brightness, faster refresh rate, and a polarizer to reduce reflections starts at $200 more.
