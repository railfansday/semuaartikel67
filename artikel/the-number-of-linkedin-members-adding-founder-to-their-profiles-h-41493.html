The Number of LinkedIn Members Adding ‘Founder’ to Their Profiles Has Almost Doubled Since 2022 — What’s Going On?

🌐 Source: Entrepreneur • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (381 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Solopreneurs are on a roll, thanks to AI tools that let one person cover jobs that once required a whole team.
  • Nearly half (46%) of professionals say AI makes it easier to start a business, according to new LinkedIn data.
  • It has been revealed that founders are also handling their own marketing.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Solopreneurs are on a roll, thanks to AI tools that let one person cover jobs that once required a whole team.

Nearly half (46%) of professionals say AI makes it easier to start a business, according to new LinkedIn data. Among small business owners who credit AI and digital tools, 35% say AI helped them launch faster (or launch at all), 28% say it let them start with less capital and 27% say it removed the need for a technical co-founder.

It has been revealed that founders are also handling their own marketing. About 80% of them now post content to build credibility, up from 61% just six months ago. Prompt engineering has more than doubled in a year and is now the fastest-growing skill among U.S. founders on the platform.

Mariana Antaya is one example. She left Microsoft in 2023 to run her AI education tech giant, quantifAI, full time and has landed partnerships with Mercedes-Benz and Airwallex without hiring a team. Going solo still carries risk, though. About 20% of fresh ventures fail within their first year, per the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
