Markus Krösche is already in Milan and hard at work, even though his appointment as the Rossoneri’s newly introduced Head of Football is yet to be made official.

According to transfer expert Matteo Moretto, speaking on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, the German executive has already got down to business ahead of the January window.

“Markus Krösche is already in Milan, already very operational, despite it not being official yet, but the announcement is expected shortly,” Moretto said, via MilanNews24.

Krösche has long been Gerry Cardinale’s first choice.

On 18 October, he was released from his duties at Eintracht Frankfurt with immediate effect at his own request.

Milan and Eintracht have now reportedly reached an agreement over compensation, exchanging documents yesterday, with the Rossoneri paying around €4m.

Krösche’s first job will be Adrien Rabiot’s contract renewal.

In a recent development, “There’s a lot of work going on over Rabiot’s renewal. Cardinale’s intention is to close it by the end of the year, because Rabiot is an indispensable player for Ruben Amorim,” Moretto explained.

“Then we’ll see how the market develops. Milan want to strengthen up front, and Endrick is one name.”.

It has been revealed that rabiot is under contract until June 2028, and Milan are reportedly working towards a fresh deal until 2030. Cardinale said on Sunday that any January signing will only come if it’s “a move that counts”.
