TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. This Black Friday deal is only available for a limited time.

Black Friday deals are coming in hot and heavy. We're inundated with fresh price drops on laptops, headphones, fitness trackers, and everything in between. And yes, we're seeing a huge number of price drops on Apple.

In a recent development, wading through the deals on iPads, MacBooks, and AirPods, it's tough to ignore T-Mobile's massive sale on iPhones. If you've been waiting until now to upgrade, you're in luck: For limited-time, the Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free at T-Mobile. Yes, you read that right.

Recent findings suggest that T-Mobile keeps things surprisingly flexible when it comes to qualifying for this Black Friday deal. You can port your existing phone number from an eligible rival carrier to a qualifying T-Mix strategy, and T-Mobile covers the full cost of the phone. Alternatively, existing customers can trade in an eligible phone in any condition on a qualifying T-Mix plan.

T-Mobile distributes the full discount as 36 monthly bill credits. If you leave T-Mobile or cancel your line before that three-year payment blueprint ends, the credits stop immediately, and you will owe the remaining balance on the device.

You are also still required to pay local sales tax on the phone's full $1,199 retail value upfront, plus T-Mobile's standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout. Unfortunately, you can't escape those upfront charges.

Score the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free with T-Mobile over Black Friday.
