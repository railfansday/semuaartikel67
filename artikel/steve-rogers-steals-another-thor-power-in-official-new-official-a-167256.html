📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that avengers: Doomsday trailers have played up Thor's simmering jealousy over Steve Rogers wielding an intact Mjölnir, Thor's enchanted hammer. Steve has summoned lightning with it, like in Avengers: Endgame, and a new TV spot for Doomsday shows him utilizing another of Thor's powers.

The 30-second clip demonstrates Steve in what is presumably Latveria. Kneeling in front of what looks like a von Doom family tomb or memorial, Steve summons Mjölnir and calls down riotous lightning, but it's not clear why. The clip ends with a shot of Steve Rogers using Mjölnir to fly — possibly through the Bifrost — as he continues to be worthy of the power of Thor.

It has been revealed that steve and Peggy Carter's teenage son was the emotional hook in the film's final full trailer, and he appears here as well. Their kid peppers Steve with questions about his powers, asking him if he can fly, turn into fire, or shoot lasers from his eyes, the latter two potentially referencing the Human Torch and Cyclops. It'll be interesting to see if Steve and Peggy's son sees the other heroes in action or if he's kept safely tucked away the entire film.

Recent findings suggest that the Steve-centric clip follows an earlier clip released on Wednesday. It shows Steve and Thor waltzing into Doctor Doom's poorly lit throne room. Magneto is later shown confronting Doom and trying to pull his mask off to no avail. It looks like Doom has some sort of magic spell making sure it doesn't leave his face.

Fans won't have to wait long to see Steve wield Mjölnir and all the other Avengers, Thunderbolts, Fantastic Four, and X-Men heroes duke it out with Doom as the MCU's next blockbuster is three weeks away.

Industry observers note that avengers: Doomsday flies into theaters Dec. 18.
