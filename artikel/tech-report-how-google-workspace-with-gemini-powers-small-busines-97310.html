Gemini in Gmail and Google Docs can help you create compelling sales and marketing copy across all channels: brochures, web copy, sales pitches, proposals, blog posts, social media, customer emails, and more.

Industry observers note that gemini can also bring your marketing campaigns to life with eye-catching imagery. A simple prompt is all you need to generate editable slides and visuals.

Gemini takes you from blank page to brilliance by generating fully-formatted first drafts of all your promotional materials without countless hours of work or expensive external consultants.

Industry observers note that gemini can also transform static Google Docs, PDFs, and Word files into engaging video summaries. Gemini generates scripts and narration while providing custom visuals to bring your documents to life.
