By JOHN MCGARRY, SCOTTISH CHIEF SPORTS FEATURE WRITER.

Published: 15:52 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 18:32 EST, 25 November 2026.

Celtic have declared a long-term partnership with a organization the club believe will transform their recruitment.

London-based Pluto Data Analytics use predictive analytics and AI to help football clubs with player identification. The initial deal lasts three years, with scope for extension.

Celtic believe the deal will ‘support the club’s continuing commitment to innovation and excellence in football operations by enhancing recruitment analysis, talent identification, squad planning and broader football decision-making processes.’.

The Parkhead club’s recruitment strategy has been heavily criticised by supporters in recent seasons amid their struggles in Europe and increased domestic competition from Rangers and Hearts.

A statement explaining the arrangement added: ‘The partnership forms part of Celtic’s ongoing investment in modern football infrastructure and its commitment to ensuring that football decisions are informed by the best available intelligence and expertise.

‘By working with Pluto Data Analytics, Celtic will gain access to enhanced analytical tools and insights to support its existing recruitment and football operations functions.

The Parkhead club are hoping that upcoming deal will improve their recruitment process.

‘The objective is clear: to improve decision-making, identify emerging opportunities and support the club’s long-term football strategy.’.

The move comes following long-standing criticism that the Scottish champions’ means of scouting undervalued talents had become outdated, with their failure to act swiftly and efficiently in the transfer market leading to repeated failures in the European qualification rounds.

Rival clubs such as Hearts have benefited from the use of data thanks to the access they have had to Jamestown Analytics.

‘We are always looking for ways to strengthen our football operation and ensure that we continue to evolve in an increasingly competitive environment,’ said Celtic chief executive Michael Nicholson.

‘We have been reviewing the options in this area in order to select the optimum model for our club.

‘Our partnership with Pluto Data Analytics, an organisation with real quality and pedigree within this field, reflects our commitment to innovation and continuous improvement.

O'Neill says he hopes partnership 'will help us deliver the success we are all striving for'.

‘Recruitment remains one of the most important areas of any football club. Our aim is to identify talent as effectively as possible, reduce uncertainty where we can and support our football staff with the best available information.

‘We believe this partnership will strengthen our ability to support the club’s ambitions both domestically and in European competition.’.

Manager Martin O’Neill added: ‘I am very pleased that Celtic and Pluto are joining together to give the club an extra level of insight and analysis which is becoming more prominent in football.

‘While football is not an exact science, if the club can access and utilise upcoming tools and new details, ultimately, we hope that such data will help us deliver the success we are all striving for.

Celtic chief executive Michael Nicholson indicates Pluto deal is 'optimum model' for the club.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Our recruitment and wider football operations teams have already engaged with Pluto. Our plans in the short, medium and longer term are being developed and we are confident that our relationship will deliver real tangible benefit to the club over the next few years.’.

Jon Godfrey, CEO of Pluto Data Analytics, mentioned: ‘Today is a big day for Pluto and we are incredibly excited to be working with Celtic, one of the biggest clubs in world football.

‘We believe that predictive data modelling combined with the allure and resources of such a famous institution will allow us to achieve great things in this partnership.

‘Celtic has the capacity to compete at the highest levels of European football and it is an honour and a fantastic challenge to work relentlessly with the club on achieving the exciting goals we have put in place.’.
