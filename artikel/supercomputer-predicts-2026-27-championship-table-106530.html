The final 2026/27 Championship table has been predicted in full by a Supercomputer following the opening months of the latest season.

That trio - which, for the first time in four years, was not made up of the same three clubs that had won promotion the season before - will all be desperate to bounce straight back at the first attempt.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, Lincoln City, Cardiff City and Bolton Wanderers would surely be more than content with consolidation after securing their Championship status on the back of impressive League One campaigns. For those clubs, simply keeping their heads above water and establishing themselves at a higher level may be viewed as success.

However, if the Supercomputer's projections, courtesy of Opta (as of 24/11/26), are to be believed, there could be plenty of twists and turns ahead. The Championship rarely follows a script and has a habit of throwing up surprises when least expected, and this predicted final table suggests several clubs may be in for a rollercoaster ride. In fact, the standings look markedly different from how many supporters and pundits would have expected the season to unfold.

It has been revealed that preston North End have been perennial mid-table finishers in the Championship for over a decade. However, after a dismal start to the 2026/27 campaign, Opta's Supercomputer thinks they will finish rock bottom this campaign and drop to League One alongside Derby County and Watford.

Wins against Bolton, Bristol City, Preston and Burnley helped put them up into a much safer predicted position initially, but a 4-0 thrashing at the hands of Charlton has them hovering just above the bottom three.

Industry observers note that burnley, who were relegated from the Premier League in 25/26, are expected to finish 20th with just 49 points. That would be a huge disappointment for the Lancashire side, who would have had aspirations of returning to England's top tier at the first time of asking.

They couldn't maintain that incredible run, though, plummeting down the table following an 11-match winless run. After beating Sheffield United and Lincoln, they now sit slightly higher up the table.

It has been revealed that the Robins have firmly established themselves in the English second tier, qualifying for the playoffs in the 2024/25 season. However, despite success in recent years, the Supercomputer thinks they will struggle and finish in the bottom half.

Millwall came so close to achieving promotion to England's top tier in 2025/26, eventually having to settle for third place and a spot in the playoffs. Opta's Supercomputer thinks they will just miss out on a playoff berth this time round.

Wrexham narrowly failed to make the playoffs last season as they finished seventh. After a very slow start to the season, they are again expected to miss out, with the Supercomputer predicting them to finish in a disappointing 13th with 64 points.

According to recent reports, portsmouth were at threat of relegation in the 2025/26 campaign, but they avoided the drop after improving in the final months. They've had an impressive start to the campaign and are being predicted to finish in the top half, having just beaten West Ham.

In a recent development, sheffield United endured a torrid campaign in 25/26. At one point, it looked as if they would have a battle on their hands to maintain their Championship status. They eventually finished mid-table, and they are predicted to end this campaign in a similar position.

Norwich City ended the 2025/26 campaign on fire and would have been expecting to start 26/27 in the same manner.
