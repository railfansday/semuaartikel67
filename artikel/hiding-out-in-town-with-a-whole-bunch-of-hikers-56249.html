Hiding Out in Town With a Whole Bunch of Hikers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that this morning I woke up pretty dang early at the room in Strawberry. Which was a little bit of a challenge, because there were 5 other hikers in the same room! I got up slowly and as quietly as I could and headed into the living room. I put my contacts in, combed through my hair, and brushed my teeth. Slide and I weren’t the only ones up though. Woods was walking around in the kitchen and starting up a pot of coffee. The rain was pouring down outside, and I’d spent much of the evening listening to the roar of thunder. What a perfect day to spend in town! It wasn’t long before hikers began waking up and heading into the living room. I think someone put a movie on first thing, because what else is there to do? Then we began to scheme about breakfast.

Sources indicate that the town of Strawberry is a smaller town just down the road from the town of Pine. There is a breakfast joint in Pine, but the breakfast place in Strawberry is unfortunately closed today. Apparently yesterday this larger group of hikers met a local guy named Al and his wife. He had mentioned that he would come by this morning around 9AM and pick people up to go to breakfast in town. Slide and I were both very intrigued, but we weren’t a part of that original strategy whatsoever. I figured since there were 10 hikers in the house, it was probably pretty unlikely that there would be enough room in the car for all of us to go to breakfast.

Recent findings suggest that kitty Hawk wound up calling Al to confirm the details and told Al that there were about 8 of us who would be interested in going to be breakfast. And what felt like a lost cause to me turned into yet another trail miracle. Al stated that it was no problem for him and his wife to drive separately to pick us up, that way there would be enough room for all 8 of us in the cars! It was a rainy-day miracle. I was absolutely ravenous at this point, so it was very good news. Since we got into town so late last night, we weren’t able to stop at the store or pick anything up. Which meant I woke up this morning wildly hungry.

The server gave our table two massive, hot cinnamon rolls for free before our food even came out.

According to recent reports, around 9AM the large group of us left the Airbnb and walked down the road to a parking area with a bunch of mailboxes. That was the easiest place for Al to pick us up, and we didn’t mind the short walk. Al and his wife were incredibly nice, and we hung out at the parking lot talking with them for a bit. Then they drove us into Pine and all of us sat to eat at the diner in town. The staff was incredibly nice and were able to seat all of us at one big table. They even brought us over hot, fresh cinnamon rolls to eat while we waited for our food, on the house! I wound up getting a breakfast burrito with chorizo and Dr. Pepper. I didn’t get my soda fix last night so I couldn’t resist the call, even at breakfast.

From left to right: Lazarus, Woods, Leaf, Al & his wife, Kitty Hawk, PI, Slide, me, and 9 Lives.

While we sat there another AZT hiker wound up coming into the restaurant and she joined us. Her name was Mel and she was also staying in town to wait out the storm. Apparently, there were at least a couple other hikers in town as well that she had seen. Everybody ate and then sat around talking at the table for a long time. It was really nice to catch up with 9 Lives, who I haven’t seen in person since we were hiking the CDT together in 2024. We hiked around 2,000 miles together that year and it doesn’t feel possible that it’s been so long. The other hikers at breakfast were Lazarus, PI, Kitty Hawk, Woods, Leaf, Slide, Al, his wife, and me. As we sat inside, we got to watch the rain pour down outside. That is always incredibly satisfying when you’re on trail. It feels like a huge win to make it to town and be able to avoid bad weather.

Free mini pies from the diner in Pine.

Recent findings suggest that after everyone finished eating, we got ready to head out. But not before the lovely staff at the diner gave us about 6 mini pies to take home with us. They were a day or so old but still looked incredible. Then once everyone paid, we decided to walk across the street to a Hippie Shop, just for fun. They had clothing, accessories, and all sorts of random stuff. I didn’t find anything that spoke to me but Slide wound up finding a button-down shirt that he really liked. It was just fun shopping around with no place to be. Then before we headed back to the Airbnb, Al drove us to the grocery store to grab a few things there. I grabbed some drinks and random things there but didn’t get too much stuff. We’re actually planning to check into a separate room later on today and I don’t want to have to carry a bunch of stuff around.

Industry observers note that the Airbnb that everyone is staying at is absolutely perfect, but it is filled to the brim with hikers! Last night it was completely full, and someone was sleeping on the couch. And while we were at the breakfast place we ran into Torin aka Espresso, another hiker that we last saw at the North Rim. He was looking for a place to stay tonight too and decided to come with all of us back to the Airbnb. Since we’re going to be in town for 2 more nights it just made more sense to book another place down the street and be a little more comfortable. I’m hoping to get a bunch of writing and editing done which will be easier with some more space. The blueprint for now is to just hang out with everyone at the Airbnb until around 3PM. Then Slide and I will go and check in to this other spot just down the street. That way we can still hang out with everyone but also enjoy a bit more space.

The AZT sobo hiker bubble waiting out a storm in Strawberry, AZ.

Once everyone finished grocery shopping, we got a ride back to the Airbnb with Al. And not long after we got inside it began to pour like crazy outside. It even hailed for a while! Everyone got comfortable back at the Airbnb and put on another movie. We all sat on the couch, talked, and enjoyed the show. I even got some editing done while we all hung out. Then just before 3PM I got a notification that we could go and check in at the room down the road. There was a break in the rain which felt like the perfect opportunity. I just wanted to drop everything off before the weather started up again. Then we would probably come back to the Airbnb to hang out with the gang later on.

In a recent development, the hippie shop in Pine was adorable and really fun to walk around. They even gave you these cute reusable bags with any purchase.

The place that I booked for the night was called the Strawberry Inn, and it was really adorable. The location had a bunch of tiny home style rentals which were perfect for us. Plus, I was even able to book a room that had its own private hot tub. The idea of soaking in a hot tub while it rained sounded amazing. The room was perfect for the two of us and had everything that we could need. We got comfortable there and I decided to get some of my electronics charged. Then I went through my food bag to access how much more food I might need for this next stretch. I had even seen online that there was a Walmart nearby which would deliver. So that was going to be my plan to get the rest of my resupply goodies at some point.

The big group of hikers hanging out outside of the hippie store in Pine.

It has been revealed that not long after we checked into the room, we were already hungry again. Luckily this location was right around the corner from the couple of different restaurants in town. At this point it was raining lightly but it wasn’t too terrible. So, we just carried our umbrellas and walked the short ways down the road to the bar and grill that was open. When we walked inside there was absolutely no indication that the place served food. The bar was full of guests, but nobody was eating. But they did indeed sell hot food! I wound up getting a chicken sandwich with a side of fried cheese curds. Those are really delicious. I also got a Dr. Pepper, but it had a really weird flavor likely due to coming from the bar soda gun. It was the strangest tasting Dr. Pepper that I’d ever had.

A chicken sandwich with a side of fried cheese curds!

Slide and I hung out there for a bit and enjoyed our food. Then retreated back to the Strawberry Inn to relax for a while. I got some more editing done and Slide went through his photos. Then later on we decided to walk back over to the Airbnb to hang out with all of the other hikers. They were still watching yet another movie when we arrived. Truly though, what else is there to do in this town? We joined them for another one and wound up hanging out until sometime after 9PM.

One of the only joints open in the town of Strawberry.

It has been revealed that eventually we decided to walk back down the road to our room for the night. There is actually a breakfast place right next to the Inn which is going to be open tomorrow morning. It was closed today and the menu looks really good, so I’m excited to try that out tomorrow. We’re going to be taking our second zero day in a row tomorrow to wait out worse weather. Apparently, the rain is supposed to be much worse tomorrow than it was today, and more than an inch and a half of rain is expected over the course of the day. I’m looking forward to another day of rest and having some more time to get my writing and video editing done before we hit the trail once more.
