A one-line prompt that makes ChatGPT leak its private opinion of you, in a pretty funny format: a WhatsApp group chat where the AI models gossip about their users.

Also worth trying on Claude, Gemini, or any assistant that's been accumulating your chat history.

Instead of a summary, the model commits to the bit. It invents a group chat, something like "Customers We Can't Figure Out", puts the other AI models in it, and lets them gossip. About you. Using everything it has quietly learned from your conversations.

It has been revealed that one person's actual output, shared with permission. Translated from Turkish; his name and a few identifying details have been swapped for generic ones. Everything else is exactly as the model wrote it.

Shared by its author, translated from Turkish, with his name and identifying details generalized. The "recursion, no base case" line is doing a lot of work here.

According to recent reports, run the prompt, read your leaked group chat, and notice two things: how hard you laugh, and how much of it is true. Both measurements are the point.

Sources indicate that I'm not affiliated with OpenAI and this may indeed be a viral marketing idea they're testing. But I had such a laugh, I figured others would have some fun with it as well and decided to share it.
