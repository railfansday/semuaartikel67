Futures data hints at bulls regaining control over Ether’s price, but seasoned analysts hint at a bigger correction.

In a recent development, chartist Peter Brandt highlighted a multi-year triangle form on Ethereum’s (ETH) weekly chart, with the cryptocurrency approaching the lower boundary of the pattern. The $1,383 level is crucial due to the long-term support floor holding the threshold, usually backed by both retail & crypto whales.

It has been revealed that peter Brandt’s cautious look on Ethereum’s (ETH) near term price matches with Peter Schiff’s recent ETH price prediction towards a $1,000 slump. He even predicted a further slump that could lead to a 70% deficit if the market doesn’t adjust to geopolitical challenges & stock chaos.

If $ETH cannot remain above 1383 I see no reason why price cannot go back to sub-$500 pic.twitter.com/OT9hQ3lBFU.

Recent findings suggest that with Bitcoin (BTC) backtracking to mid $57,000 levels, the rest of the market followed by plunging way below the crucial moving averages. Right now, Ethereum (ETH) is situated between the lower & mid envelopes of the Bollinger Band, putting the $1,699 level in focus.

Recent findings suggest that other analysts treat the $1,550 price region as a potential base rather than the beginning of a much deeper breakdown, while Futures data perfectly aligns with that theory. Short-sellers are dominating liquidations again, while the long versus short ratio hits above 1 on most exchanges.

Given that the largest altcoin maintains that level on the 4-hour charts, the $1,755.58 Red-Label Bollinger Band comes into play. That level will be hard to restore while the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) still dwells in red figures. A push above 0 could restore long-term holder faith in ETH.

Also, the Ethereum-based exchange-traded funds (ETFs) have been dropping millions of dollars a day, dropping $217.50 million since last week. The weekly ETH ETF flows are now on a 9-week losing streak. Last time the ETF flows turned positive on September 25, 2026.

Wall Street’s sell-off sentiment didn’t scare away some famous crypto whales: BitMine’s Tom Lee acquired a massive 7,819 ETH, taking the firm’s total holdings to 6.09 million ETH, summing up to 4.98% of Ethereum’s total supply as the altcoin hovers around $1,699.
