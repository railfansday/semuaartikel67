Competition: UEFA Champions League Date: Wednesday, November 25, 2026 Kickoff: 20:00 GMT / 21:00 CET Venue: San Siro, Milan.

Verdict: Inter to win Best odds: 11/25 Bookmaker: 1xBet.

According to recent reports, inter has little time to dwell on a remarkable evening in Bergamo. After surrendering a two-goal advantage late in Sunday’s 4-4 draw with Atalanta, Cristian Chivu’s side returns to San Siro with another vital European assignment. Stuttgart arrives a point ahead of the host in the Champions League standings, making this a useful opportunity for Inter to improve its position.

Four goals should usually be enough to win away from home. Inter discovered otherwise at Atalanta, where Pio Esposito’s hat-trick was followed by a Petar Sucic penalty that made it 4-2. Two late goals left the Italian champion with a single point and gave Chivu plenty to address before Wednesday.

There was encouragement in the attacking performance. Marcus Thuram repeatedly unsettled the home defense, Esposito finished with confidence and Lautaro Martinez returned from injury. Chivu has several credible combinations available up front, a useful advantage when a European game becomes stretched late on.

According to recent reports, inter’s continental campaign has also taken a healthier direction. Defeats against Real Madrid and Club Brugge were followed by a 1-0 victory over Shakhtar Donetsk and a 3-0 win at Feyenoord. Those consecutive clean sheets offer a reminder that the team can manage games far more effectively than it did on Sunday.

Hakan Calhanoglu and Piotr Zielinski are unavailable, with Chivu confirming his midfield limitations before the match. Ange-Yoan Bonny is also sidelined. John Stones is available, while Alessandro Bastoni returns from his Champions League suspension. Lautaro’s comeback gives the coach another option alongside Thuram and Esposito.

Stuttgart made an excellent start to the league phase, beating Viking 3-1 and Slovan Bratislava 3-0. The subsequent fixtures have been less rewarding: a 1-1 draw with Atletico Madrid was followed by a 2-0 defeat at Galatasaray, leaving Sebastian Hoeness’ side on seven points from four games.

It has been revealed that the Bundesliga picture has been uneven. A goalless draw with Schalke at the weekend left Stuttgart with four wins and five defeats from ten league matches. Its European performances deserve respect, although the trip to Milan demands a level of concentration that has been difficult to maintain domestically.

Stuttgart should still have opportunities. Inter’s missing midfielders could make the first pass out of defense less reliable, and an aggressive visiting press would test those replacements. The danger for Stuttgart comes when that pressure is bypassed, allowing Inter’s forwards to attack space.

Stuttgart has confirmed that every player registered for the Champions League is available. Angelo Stiller and Luca Jaquez return to the squad after injury, improving Hoeness’ options in midfield and defense.

Inter’s midfield absences and Sunday’s defensive lapses leave Stuttgart a route into the game. Even so, the home side’s attacking options and recent European recovery make it our choice. We expect a closer contest than the short match odds might suggest, with Inter doing enough to win 2-1.
