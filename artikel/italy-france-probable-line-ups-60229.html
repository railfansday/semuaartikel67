📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that first in the standings of Group 1 of League A in the Nations League, the French team has the opportunity to definitively validate its qualification for the quarter-finals in the event of victory this evening at San Siro, against Italy. For this classic of European football, Zinédine Zidane experienced the start of the rally marked by a cascade of packages (Pierre Kalulu, Marcus Thuram, Aurélien Tchouaméni), without forgetting the thorny case of Kylian Mbappé, absent against the Squadra Azzurra, but hoped for next Sunday against Turkey.

Sources indicate that this should not prevent the Blues coach from putting together an attractive team, largely capable of doing better than the draw conceded in the first leg (1-1). In a 4-3-3 which has once again become the preferred pattern of the Tricolores, the cages will be guarded by Mike Maignan. In front of the Rossonero, Malo Gusto, Jérémy Jacquet, Dayot Upamecano and Andy Diouf will form the rearguard. A notch higher, Adrien Rabiot should be preferred to Eduardo Camavinga. At his side, we would find Lucas Da Cunha and Rayan Cherki.

In attack, Mbappé’s loss should be compensated by the tenure of Désiré Doué up front. The Paris Saint-Germain player should be supported by Michael Olise on the right and Bradley Barcola on the left. Called in as reinforcements to compensate for the offensive defections, Éli Junior Kroupi and Senny Mayulu would start the match on the sidelines. Opposite, Roberto Mancini should line up a starting eleven close to the one who stood up to the Blues at the Stade de France.

Recent findings suggest that not unhappy with Mbappé’s package, the transalpine coach should also offer a team structured in 4-3-3, with Gianluigi Donnarumma in goal. The four defenders selected are expected to be Michael Kayode, Giorgio Scalvini, Alessandro Bastoni and Riccardo Calafiori. The midfield will also look good with Davide Frattesi, Sandro Tonali and Nicolò Barella (who would replace Nicolò Fagioli). Finally, the attacking trio would be composed of Sebastiano Esposito, Francesco Pio Esposito and another Nicolò, the Bologna striker, Nicolò Cambiaghi.
