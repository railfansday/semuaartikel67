In a recent development, we are now just four days away from the release of GTA 6 on Xbox Series X/S and PS5, but the proximity to the much-anticipated launch date isn't stemming the tide of leaks that has been plaguing the game for much of the past year. Yesterday, a fresh video - seemingly from a record store that received vinyl records of the soundtrack early - demonstrated off the full 34-song tracklist for the game.

According to recent reports, thanks to the listing printed on the back of Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album vinyl, we have a better idea of the licensed music that'll appear in-game. Travis Scott, Rina Sawayama, Cardi B, Pinkpantheress, SZA, Keith Richards, Dua Lipa, Jennie, Lana Del Rey, Zara Larsson are just some of the names that make up the impressive list. We know about a few of these tracks from September's announcement of the record, but only six tracks were revealed then, not the whole list.

It has been revealed that you can expect to hear these tracks on the radio - alongside many more, no doubt - and deployed at curated times during cutscenes or in missions, to really tie together the vibe of the city. This is something Vice City did remarkably well its roster of 80s tracks, and I can't wait to see how Rockstar has used more contemporary music in the same way here.

Of course, there have been a lot of Grand Theft Auto 6 leaks in the run-up to the game's launch. Another recent example is the full game's map which seemingly leaked online thanks to the £350 collector's edition on show in a UK department store. Then there were all the leaks from a group calling itself CyberLeek, which shared unofficial gameplay and map details over the course of a few weeks, before Rockstar had even revealed much gameplay itself.

It has been revealed that amongst all this, Rockstar has been embroiled in legal action after it was accused of union busting following the firing of over 30 employees. The case's conclusion will be determined this week, as the game goes on sale.
