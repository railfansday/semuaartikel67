Two SPFL postponements due to frozen pitch

🌐 Source: Co • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (76 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Dunfermline Athletic's Scottish Championship match against Greenock Morton and Spartans' League 2 game with Dumbarton have been postponed because of frozen pitches.
  • The fixtures were meant to be played at 15:00 GMT on Saturday but will be rearranged with latest details to be confirmed.
  • Queen's Park v Livingston will go ahead after a pitch inspection took place at Lesser Hampden, with the officials deeming the surface playable.
