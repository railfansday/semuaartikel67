Tech Report: Splatoon 3 version 12.0.0 upgrade debuts November 17

🌐 Source: Gematsu • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (621 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Nintendo will release the version 12.0.0 enhancement for Splatoon 3 on November 17, the company declared.
  • According to recent reports, sections marked “on Nintendo Switch 2″ apply only to Nintendo Switch 2.
  • Made it possible to participate with a friend in traditional X Battles as a group of two.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nintendo will release the version 12.0.0 enhancement for Splatoon 3 on November 17, the company declared.

According to recent reports, sections marked “on Nintendo Switch 2″ apply only to Nintendo Switch 2. All other changes apply to both Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch.

Changes to Anarchy Battle (Series).

Made it possible to participate with a friend in traditional X Battles as a group of two.

Changes to GameChat on Nintendo Switch 2.

Added a feature that allows players to open GameChat and invite friends from their group while playing modes with friends, such as battles or Salmon Run.

This patch focuses on making changes to matchmaking and multiplayer balance.

According to recent reports, for X Battles, we have added a feature to allow players to play as a pair with a friend. The goal of this feature is to expand the range of ways to play, and we will introduce it on a trial basis starting in Chill Season 2026. After we have introduced it, we will comprehensively analyze how these battles are actually played and how the gameplay situation shifts, including for players who are not playing with a friend. Then we will decide whether to continue providing this feature in future seasons.

For Anarchy Battle (Series), we have changed the matchmaking system such that, in exchange for removing the feature allowing players to continue with the same team, matchmaking will now complete more rapidly and with less imbalance of skill level and equipped weapons between players and their opponents.

Industry observers note that for Challenges, we have changed how Challenge Power is calculated to make it easier to raise Challenge Power the more wins a player has. With this change, between players who would have ended up with about the same Challenge Power under the previous system, the player who attained the greater number of wins will have the higher final Challenge Power.

For multiplayer balance, we made a variety of changes to main, sub, and special weapons, including changes to the flight distance of Blaster shots.

Recent findings suggest that with most weapons, when a player fires while moving, the player’s movement speed is added to the shot’s speed to a certain extent, resulting in a more natural trajectory. For Blasters, we have minimized the change in shot flight distance caused by this effect, making it easier to maintain an appropriate distance against an opponent’s Blaster.

Among the special weapons, we have changed the Reefslider because, while there is statistically little impact on victories in matches between extremely skilled players, it has a high impact on matches between less skilled players. The intention behind this change is to alleviate that striking difference. By narrowing the area affected by the explosion we have made it so that players cannot reap the maximum benefit without using it at the precise position. On the other hand, we have extended the invulnerability period to make it harder for opponents who used terrain to survive the explosion to counterattack and easier for the player to make follow-up attacks.

It has been revealed that for the Tenta Missiles, we have decreased the vulnerability while firing and expanded the area where it is possible to lock on to opponents. As a result, it will now be easier to use, even in situations where opponents are nearby the player. By decreasing the impact interval, it becomes easier to defeat opponents who are slow to dodge, but the duration for which they must keep dodging is reduced.

We have also added a feature for Nintendo Switch 2 that will make it easier to use GameChat.

The next update will focus on changes to multiplayer balance.

Splatoon 3 is available now for Switch.
