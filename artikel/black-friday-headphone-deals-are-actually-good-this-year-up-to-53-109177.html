Normally, I have to concentrate to find legitimate deals through all the crappy ones — especially this early in the week.

According to recent reports, pure. Class. That's how I'd describe the Bowers & Wilkins Px7 S3. And I'd know, because I own and use a set of them on a near daily basis at home.
