It has been revealed that google is announcing some significant changes to its smart home software. Starting next year, the company will shut down the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app and retire Google Assistant on its smart home devices.

In a recent development, the Nest app shutdown takes place on January 25th, 2027, meaning you’ll have to manage your Nest products, including Nest thermostats, cameras, and Nest Protect alarms, from the Google Home app instead. If you get the Google Home app set up, Google will move over your Nest devices, including cameras and video history, to the Home app on that January 25th date. The company will also automatically transfer Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions to Google Home Premium. Google says it will begin deleting Nest account data on March 25th, 2027.

The changes for Google Assistant will take place later: On August 2nd, 2027, the Gemini for Home voice assistant will replace Google Assistant on smart speakers and smart displays, the company says. Google argues that Gemini for Home allows users to ask more complex questions and speak naturally instead of needing to use dedicated commands or specific language.

According to recent reports, gemini for Home was announced last year, so it’s not exactly a shock that Google is going to move on from Google Assistant in the smart home (especially when the organization also killed Google Assistant on phones earlier this year). The company claims it prepares to share more updates about the changes ahead of the August 2027 transition.
