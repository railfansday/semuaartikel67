Understand How Satellite Links Shape IoT Device Design and Testing

🌐 Source: Wiley • 📅 12 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (230 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Cellular IoT has extended low-power connectivity to many remote places. Even so, about four-fifths of the earth’s surface remains outside the range of a base station. Non-terrestrial networks (NTN) address this gap by adding satellite access to the 5G standard. One modem can then connect through a satellite when no terrestrial cell is available.

Recent findings suggest that this shift changes how devices are designed. Satellites are in motion. Signals therefore face long propagation delays and changing Doppler shift. Link budgets are tight and device antennas are small. A device can pass static conformance tests and still fail on a real satellite link.

This White Paper explains these challenges. It also outlines the test conditions and measurements needed to evaluate synchronization, signaling, receiver performance, and link reliability.
