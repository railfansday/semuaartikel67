President Trump landed in Manila on Tuesday, beginning a trip to Asia in which he will attend an ASEAN summit, greet members of the Quad, then travel to China for an APEC meeting.

Here’s what those acronyms mean, as well as some highlights from previous summits — including gold crowns and awkward handshakes.

In a recent development, the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum is a group of 21 member economies, most of which are from that region. Its members also include Canada, Mexico, Australia, Russia and the United States. It promotes trade in Asia, but is not a free trade bloc. Its leaders meet annually, with the location rotating each year.

Sources indicate that meetings between world leaders on the sidelines of the summit can often overshadow the main proceedings. This year, in the Chinese tech hub of Shenzhen, Mr. Trump is expected to meet with President Xi Jinping of China and President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia, as well as with Mr. Xi separately.

It has been revealed that last year, when Mr. Trump visited the South Korean city of Gyeongju during the week of the APEC meeting, President Lee Jae Myung of South Korea gave him a replica of a gold crown that researchers believe was worn by an ancient Korean ruler. Mr. Trump also met with Mr. Xi in the nearby city of Busan, where the two struck a truce in their countries’ trade war.
