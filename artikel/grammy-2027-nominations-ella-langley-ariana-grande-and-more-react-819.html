Recording Academy voters will have a difficult time choosin’ the winners for the Grammys 2027, you can tell by the way these nominations are stacked.

Naturally, nominee Ella Langley received several first-time nominations for the biggest hit of the year, "Choosin' Texas"—which broke the record for the longest running chart topper on the Billboard Hot 100 in September—including Record of the Year and Song of the Year. She also scored nods for Album of the Year for Dandelion, as well as Best Latest Artist and more.

Industry observers note that sharing her seven total nominations to her Instagram Story Nov. 16, Langley proved she was at a loss for words over the honor, adding only a crying emoji and a white heart emoji.

It has been revealed that after quite the year for music, Langley as well as Grammy veterans like Taylor Swift, Beyoncé and more are up for awards at the upcoming ceremony, set to air Feb. 7, 2027, on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu. While Ariana Grande already has three Grammy wins under her belt, she proved the honor of nomination never gets old.

"I am so overwhelmed," Grande wrote in an Instagram post Nov. 16. "It is hard to find words at the moment. but for now, simply… thank you so, so much @recordingacademy for recognizing my sweet Petal and 'Hate That I Made You Love Me' in this way… i’m so irrevocably proud of this body of work and it is such an honor to receive this acknowledgment from you.".

This year’s nominations were announced by Grammys CEO Harvey Mason Jr. as well as Recording Academy Board of Trustees member Dr. Chelsey Green, as well as some of music's biggest stars including Swift, who walked away with five nominations for The Life of a Showgirl, as well as her Toy Stoy 5 track "I Knew It, I Knew You.".

It has been revealed that meanwhile, the stars behind some of the year’s biggest hits also participated in the nominations ceremony. Kehlani, Pharrell Williams and David Guetta also appeared in the broadcast to help announce the nominees.

Of course, this year’s nominees are far from the first to be floored by the possibility of accepting a golden gramophone come February—as the saying goes, it really is an honor to be nominated.

While Sombr did not end up taking home any hardware at the 2026 ceremony, the “12 to 12” singer emphasized how thrilling it was to hear his name called among Dean, as well as The Marías, KATSEYE, Addison Raye, Leon Thomas III, Alex Warren and Lola Young were announced last year as contenders for the Best New Artist prize.

“I am a Grammy nominated artist," Sombr (real name Shane Michael Boose) wrote in an emotional Instagram post last year. “I am in shock!! Thank you @recordingacademy and everyone who voted for me. I am so proud to be recognized among so many amazing artists that I love and respect.”.

For the now-21-year-old, the Grammy nod was a testament to how far he has come.

“This time last year, I was 19, depressed and not knowing where to turn,” he shared. “I wrote all of these songs on my own in my bedroom. Looking back, I am so thankful to everyone who encouraged me to keep going.”.

And while the accolade meant a lot, the “Back to Friends” singer highlighted that his fans meant the most.

It has been revealed that “You’re the reason I get up in the morning and keep doing this even when I’m at my lowest," he shared. "Seeing how the music impacts you means everything to me. I love you so much. Thank you from the bottom of my heart.”.

Raye broke down in tears over her 11 nominations, tying Beyoncé for the most nominations by a solo female artist in a single year.

It has been revealed that "WHAT IS HAPPENING WHAT IS HAPPENING," Raye wrote in an Instagram post. "ELEVEN GRAMMY NOMINATIONS INCLUDING ALBUM OF THE YEAR MY HEARTTTTT CANNOT EVEN FATHOM, MY HEARTTTTTTTT.".

After receiving her first-ever nomination for Best New Artist, the "Bottle" singer shared the news on Instagram quipping, "Oh s--t.".

Recent findings suggest that she doesn't hate that she made you love Petal. After scoring four nominations for her latest pop era, Grande shared a lengthy statement to Instagram.

According to recent reports, "hellow, good day, crying !!!!!!!!!!! petal !!!!!!!!! i am so overwhelmed, it is hard to find words at the moment," Grande wrote in a Nov. 16 Instagram post. "But for now, simply… thank you so, so much @grammys for recognizing my sweet Petal and 'Hate That I Made You Love Me' in this way i’m so irrevocably proud of this body of work and it is such an honor to receive this acknowledgment from you.".

Industry observers note that "I share this, of course, with my extraordinary co-producers and dear friends @ilya_music and max," she continued. "I have loved every moment of doing this with you both, growing in so many new directions and sprouting new leaves together over the past 12 years… thank you for supporting my curiosity as an artist and for championing me as a songwriter and producer the way that you do.".

Grande also championed her "Hate That I Made You Love Me" music video director Christian Breslauer for its Grammy nod, adding, "Thank you for caring the way you do and for taking the time to make sure that every single frame of each video we do together shines a light on the truth of each song.".
