📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, piracy has been the scourge of the anime and manga industries for decades. Now, the Japanese government is clamping down on those sailing into illegal seas on the internet with "AI agents.".

As per Yomiuri (H/T Automaton Media) Japan’s Agency for Cultural Affairs has trialled the AI detection system over the past year on both Twitter and across the wider internet.

In recent years, it has been confirmed that piracy to anime, manga and games in Japan costs the industries north of $35 billion dollars, a figure almost equivalent to what they bring in revenue each year.

It comes at a time where anime is on the precipice of a worldwide breakout – if it hasn't done so already. Demon Slayer Infinity Castle grossed almost $800 million worldwide, with a sequel on the way. Chainsaw Man season 2 is also likely to arrive next year alongside confirmed names such as Delicious in Dungeon season 2 and Frieren: Beyond Journey's End season 3.
