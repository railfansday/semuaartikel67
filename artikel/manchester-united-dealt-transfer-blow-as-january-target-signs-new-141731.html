Dominic Calvert-Lewin has shut down all rumours of a Leeds United exit by signing a latest contract at Elland Road.

The striker had been linked with a move away from the Yorkshire club - with several outlets identifying him as a potential target for Manchester United - but he will remain a White after penning a fresh two-and-a-half-year deal.

The No9 has spent the past two seasons at Leeds, joining Daniel Farke’s side upon their return to the Premier League last term.

He has established himself as the club’s first-choice striker since his free transfer move after his contract at Everton expired at the end of the 2024-25 campaign.

The England international has forced his way into Thomas Tuchel’s plans for the most recent Nations League games, and he could well have a chance to impress up top with Harry Kane sidelined after tearing his ankle ligaments.

Calvert-Lewin netted 15 goals in his first season in a Leeds shirt, and he has six strikes in 13 matches so far this term.

His hot run of goalscoring form has seen him linked with a move to Old Trafford, where latest manager Mauricio Pochettino is thought to be keen on reinforcing his attack after injuries to Benjamin Sesko and Marcus Rashford.

Calvert-Lewin was one of the names that was reportedly on the Red Devils’ shortlist ahead of the January transfer window, but his decision to sign a new contract at Elland Road will come as a major blow to Pochettino as he looks to shape United’s squad in his image.
