Industry observers note that A smoke break at the Signature Theatre ultimately resulted in one of Lois Smith‘s most fascinating performances. The Tony Award winner met Zane Pais while they were both grabbing a quick cigarette while rehearsing plays. Pais had been an admirer of Smith’s work in stage productions like “The Inheritance” and films such as “Marjorie Prime” and “Five Easy Pieces,” and longed to collaborate.

“She has the most amazing delivery of lines; she’s always able to do something so unexpected with the language,” Pais noted during a recent Zoom interview with Smith. “I’ve seen Lois my whole life in so many amazing shows, just be so unbelievably present on stage and be such a generous actor, and just give these heartfelt and unexpected performances. Industry observers note that so Pais wrote a short film, “Lightning Bug,” with Smith in mind. In it, she plays Lydia, a fiercely independent nonagenarian who is being pressured into selling the family home by her son and granddaughter. But things take an unexpected turn when a strange young man crashes their dinner, giving Lydia a chance to demonstrate she hasn’t missed a step despite her advanced age.

“It was all there in the script,” Smith, who recently turned 96, mentioned. “It wasn’t complex for me to pull it off even though the script is complex.”.

The role did require Smith to perform some fight choreography, but she seemed more nervous about singing a song on screen.

“I really worried: Am I going to be able to do this?” Smith stated. “They made me a tape, and I listened to it and listened to it and practiced. I was nervous, but it went all right.”.

“Lightning Bug” filmed over a weekend in the upstate Newly introduced York home of one of Pais’s relatives. It was true no-frills, all-hands effort. The cast and crew bunked in an Airbnb, and a friend of Pais’s cooked meals for everyone.

It has been revealed that “It felt like we were part of a theater troupe,” Pais remarked. “We’d work hard. Then in the evenings, we would have red wine and smoke cigarettes.”.

“I seldom smoke,” Smith interjected. “Occasionally, I’ll smoke other people’s cigarettes.”.
