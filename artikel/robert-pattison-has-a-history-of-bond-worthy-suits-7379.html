📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The name is Pattinson—Robert Pattinson.

After a much-talked-about worldwide hunt, the actor was confirmed as the next James Bond. As such, the British star will soon be slipping into 007’s signature slick suits in an upcoming Bond film to be directed by Denis Villeneuve. (Pattinson reportedly beat out other actors such as Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, and Josh O’Connor, all of whom screen-tested for the iconic role.).

While it will be a while before we see what stamp Pattinson puts on the beloved franchise, one thing is for certain: His red carpet history definitely proves he’s got the style DNA to play Bond.

For years now, the star has been sticking to wearing a sleek array of suits and tuxedos for his public appearances—a uniform that his new secret agent character almost wears exclusively in the films, too. (Looking good in a tux, after all, is half the job, if not more.) On past red carpets, Pattinson has favored classic black tuxedos and bow ties, but he’s also worn unlined styles with unbuttoned shirts—and no tie!—that feel just a little bit less stuffy.

Pattinson’s effortless approach to tailoring, then, could be a preview to a new era and mood for Bond—something a little more relaxed and contemporary, compared to the rigid, slim-fit suits that past Bonds have worn on-screen. Where Bond goes traditional, Pattinson has favored slouchier-fit silhouettes, undone ties, and chunkier shoes that add a more modern edge to dressing up. It’s all very Bond, but cooler Bond.

Of course, in Hollywood, it’s not uncommon for actors to dress for the part they want—so perhaps Pattinson has been method-dressing to play Bond all along. Or, maybe his innate penchant for suiting up is what got him cast over all of the other auditioning heartthrobs. Whatever the case may be, the actor’s style feels like the correct inspiration for guiding Bond’s wardrobe today. The film’s costume department should start pinning some of Pattinson’s best suiting hits to the mood board now: He is the perfect reference.
