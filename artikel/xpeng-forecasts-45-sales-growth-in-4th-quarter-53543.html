📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

XPENG just reported 3rd quarter financial details today, while also forecasting what will happen in the 4th quarter. Everything is looking quite rosy at the moment.

Recent findings suggest that as far as 3rd quarter financials go, on the back of strong sales, the firm logged 24.28 billion yuan ($3.643 billion) of revenue. That’s a fresh quarterly record for the company. It’s 19% more than in Q3 2025 and 23% more than Q2 2026.

That stated, despite strong revenue, the manufacturer still scored a net loss. It had a significant 870 million yuan ($130.5 million) net loss, in fact, which was up 128% from 380 million yuan in Q3 2025 and down 35% from 1.34 billion from Q2 2026. XPENG made a small profit in Q4 2025, and since then has again been trying to get its finances into the green again instead of in the red.

Recent findings suggest that good news going into the 4th quarter is the company expects even better. XPENG is forecasting 169,000 to 174,000 vehicle deliveries, which would mean year-over-year growth of 45% to 50%. Expected revenue is 30.4 billion yuan to 31.8 billion yuan, which would mean growth of 37% to 43%.

We’ll see. The organization is still not expected to get another net profit quarter, but perhaps the quarter could surprise and XPENG could log its second quarterly profit. One thing making that unlikely, though, is that XPENG’s R&D costs were up significantly, 17.5%. Also, general and administrative expenses were up 11.4% year over year. We’ll see what the 4th quarter brings.
