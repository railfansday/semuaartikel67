In a recent development, one of Liverpool's best youngsters looks destined for an incredible promotion. This kid is the real deal and he's going to go far - quick.

Industry observers note that liverpool have some absolutely incredible young talents at the club right now, the result of a deliberate project to sign the best they could find. Rio Ngumoha is arguably still the brightest of the stars - a player who looks set to command a first-team place before long.

In fact, Ngumoha creates an interesting problem for Liverpool. They've just paid enormous money to sign Bradley Barcola but he essentially plays the same role as the teenager. It's not entirely clear what the initiative is there.

Sources indicate that ngumoha was signed as a youth player, of course, but the Reds have gone in other directions, too. Giovanni Leoni joined for just shy of £30m from Parma in 2025. A serious knee injury sustained on his debut has kept him out since but there's reason to believe he'll be available again soon.

According to recent reports, ngumoha is the bright light in attack, then, while Leoni - along with Jeremy Jacquet - represents the future in defence. So what about midfield?

Trey Nyoni has been the story of Liverpool's season so far. He's stepped up not just to a regular first-team role but arguably the starting place - and he's done it in just a few months.

Virtually everyone would like the teenager to start at the base of midfield against Manchester United this Sunday and you can't really hype him more than that. The growth has been extraordinary and Nyoni looks like a world-beater talent who can dominate the Reds' midfield for years to come.

His rise has been so great, in fact, that we believe he's set for a promotion next March. That's the next international break and we'd be pretty surprised if Nyoni hasn't earned himself a senior England spot by then.

Industry observers note that this is a player who's grown from an untested teenager to a UEFA Champions League starter in three months. Give him another three or four months and he'll be considered a true star in the Premier League.

Nyoni has forced Liverpool into a difficult decision this season in regards to who starts in midfield. He's going to give them another headache in January as they weigh up the need for a new midfielder.

According to recent reports, and now we fully expect him to give Thomas Tuchel another headache next March. We're witnessing the rise of Nyoni right now. And it shows absolutely no signs of stopping soon.
