Revenant XSpark recover from a map down to beat GOC and set up Kryon Esports rematch

🌐 Source: Esportsinsider • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (444 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • The third upper-bracket quarter-final of the VCT Pacific Kickoff: South Asia – Qualifier 2027 needed a deciding third map.
  • Revenant XSpark levelled by winning Haven 13-5 in 35 minutes.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GOC Esports took the opening map, but Revenant XSpark won the next two to reach the upper semi-final of the VCT Pacific Kickoff South Asia qualifier playoffs. Kryon Esports are next, with two South Asian places in the Pacific Last Chance Qualifier at stake.

The third upper-bracket quarter-final of the VCT Pacific Kickoff: South Asia – Qualifier 2027 needed a deciding third map.

Sunset ran for 51 minutes. Revenant XSpark levelled by winning Haven 13-5 in 35 minutes. They then won the deciding map, which lasted 49 minutes.

The scoreline matches the two sides’ Predator League India semi-final, which Revenant XSpark also won 2-1. GOC lost the third-place match of that event to GodLike Esports. GOC now drop into the lower bracket and face RANKERS in the first lower round on Wednesday 25 November.

Industry observers note that kryon booked their place in the upper semi-final by beating RANKERS 2-1. On the other side of the upper bracket, GodLike Esports beat Trigger Point Esports 2-0 and SIGN Esports beat Zexar Esports by the same score.

Industry observers note that second place in that Predator League event earned Kryon 125,000 INR, while the winners collected 250,000 INR, VLR.gg reported. Both teams qualified for the APAC Predator League 2027, an offline event in Ho Chi Minh City.

Recent findings suggest that revenant XSpark played the Predator League with Lightningfast, hellff, Hoax, deecee and Venka. Rinsat, now listed on Revenant XSpark’s roster alongside Hoax, Techno, venka, k1ngkappa and Madelyn, appeared in Kryon’s lineup for that event.

In a recent development, riot Games and JioBLAST are running the playoffs online as an eight-team double-elimination bracket from 23 to 29 November. The field came through an online Open Qualifier of 152 teams, held from 17 to 20 November. Revenant XSpark finished in the top four there, while GOC placed in the 5th–8th group.

The South Asian qualifiers will be joined at the Last Chance Qualifier by one team from Oceania and the runner-up of the SEA Kickoff Playoffs. ONE Up is organising that event with Riot Games.

In a recent development, beyond it lies the 2027 Pacific Kickoff in Bangkok, from 11 to 31 January 2027. It will feature 12 teams: eight partner teams and four Open teams. The top three qualify for the first Masters event, Masters Seoul. Any Open team that reaches Kickoff receives a fixed US$100,000 per event on top of any prize money.

The winner of Thursday’s match plays in the upper final on 27 November, and the grand final is on 29 November.
