Rockstar Games has partnered with Panini to release real-life GTA 6 trading cards based on the collectibles players can find scattered throughout Leonida.

There’s no shortage of things to collect in Grand Theft Auto 6, with Rockstar taking a page out of Red Dead Redemption 2’s book by including hundreds of trading cards for players to hunt down.

There are 480 cards across 18 different sets, ranging from fictional sports stars to comic book characters and nightclub dancers, with players able to sell their rare finds at pawn shops for some extra cash.

Now, just days after GTA 6’s record-breaking launch, Rockstar is giving collectors a chance to rip open packs in real life.

Grand Theft Auto VI official trading cards, coming soon from @PaniniAmerica and @RockstarGames. pic.twitter.com/H1VrYVXTm5.

On November 24, Panini revealed its partnership with Rockstar Games to produce the first officially licensed Grand Theft Auto trading card collection, set to launch in December 2026.

Dubbed the GTA VI: Sports Trading Card Collection, the set will recreate cards featuring fictional athletes and teams from Rockstar’s universe.

According to Panini’s product listing, each hobby box will contain four packs of four cards, with collectors pulling an average of two Rookies, one Retro, one Illustrated, and four Special Silvers per box.

Much like real-world sports cards, there will also be rare color variants and numbered parallels, including Bronze Legend cards limited to 49 copies, Silver Legends limited to 25, and coveted one-of-one Gold cards.

It has been revealed that you just know all the down bad GTA 6 players who have fallen head over heels for goth stripper Vicki, will be clamoring for her card. However, the collection may be limited to sports cards for now.

The collaboration is the latest example of Rockstar bringing its fictional world to life, having already created a real-life Grotti Cheetah worth £1 million and teamed up with the Miami Heat for Vice City merchandise.

Panini has yet to announce pricing, an exact release date, or international availability, but considering how much money GTA 6 merchandise has already fetched on the resale market, those one-of-one cards could end up being worth a pretty penny.
