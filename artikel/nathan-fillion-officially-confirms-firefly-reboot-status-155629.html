Nathan Fillion may not be leading any giant blockbusters at the box office, but he’s been in plenty of projects in the last few years with large audiences. One of Fillion’s biggest hits that immediately comes to mind is The Rookie, the ABC cop drama in which he plays John Nolan and has for almost 10 years. The next season of The Rookie is set to kick off early next year on ABC, which leaves fans plenty of time to catch up on past seasons on Hulu. Fillion’s DCU role as Guy Gardner’s Green Lantern is now up in the air following the news that James Gunn and Peter Safran had been replaced as the CEOs of DC by Jeremy Latcham, who spent years working on the Marvel machine with Kevin Feige.

Nathan Fillion is also widely known and loved for his roles in older shows, such as Castle, another ABC series that was on the air for seven years in the late 2000s and early 2010s. However, even before Castle, Fillion starred in the first and only season of the hit sci-fi series that’s gone on to become a cult-classic in the last 20 years, Firefly. Other names involved in the show include Morena Baccarin, Alan Tudyk, and even Adam Baldwin, and the show was written and created for TV by Joss Whedon, who directed The Avengers and Avengers: Age of Ultron. Earlier this year, news broke that an animated Firefly reboot is officially in the works after 20 years, but it’s still unclear at this time when it will be released. However, Fillion recently went on the Revenge Of podcast with Patton Oswalt, and he shared an patch about how things are going on the project:.

According to recent reports, “Listen, things happened, albeit very slowly. But, things are still moving forward. Not in the way I thought it would, but in more ways, yes, I’m getting more yeses than I am nos, which is wonderful.”.

Recent findings suggest that the first and only season of Firefly, which consists of 14 episodes, can be streamed in its entirety on either Prime Video or Hulu in America. The first season of Firefly holds a solid 77% from critics on the aggregate site Rotten Tomatoes, but it’s on the audience-driven Popcornmeter where the show really shines, as it holds a near-perfect 96%. Even on IMDb, Firefly holds an 8.9 rating out of 10, which comes from over 300,000 reviews. It’s safe to say that expectations for the reboot are high.

Check out all episodes of Firefly on Hulu or Prime Video and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of the animated reboot series.
