Recent findings suggest that for Pamela Anderson, the holiday season is less about elaborate celebrations and more about appreciating life’s quieter moments. “I love the fall. I always say fall is the season of the soul,” she tells ELLE. Whether she’s tending to her garden on Vancouver Island, cooking vegetarian dishes for her family, or watching her sons establish traditions of their own, Anderson has found a new appreciation for the simple pleasures that make this time of year so special.

It’s a philosophy that extends to her approach to gift-giving. A self-described sentimentalist, Anderson prefers presents that carry a personal meaning, from family photographs to jewelry passed down through generations. And through her partnership with Pandora (Anderson is a face of the brand), she’s found a way to celebrate those connections with pieces that feel both personal and accessible.

I love the fall. I always say fall is the season of the soul. I think that’s a Virginia Woolf quote. Even in a garden, you focus more on the little things instead of being overwhelmed by so much abundance. It feels like a really special time of year. I love the fall weather and the leaves changing. It’s beautiful, especially here on the East Coast. Where I’m from on Vancouver Island, it actually looks very similar.

I absolutely love gardening. When I was home, I was cleaning up a bunch of leaves, and it feels like a workout, too. It’s nice to be outside, even in the rain. I don’t shy away from the rain, or nothing would ever get done where I’m from. I love being outside and getting my hands dirty. In this part of my life, I’m really gravitating toward that, but I’m also working a lot, so it’s a really good balance. I get to do really glamorous things, work on film and onstage, and then I go home, put my boots on, and get in the garden.

Recent findings suggest that now, the boys are usually with their wife and fiancée over the holidays. They’re building their lives and their own traditions, so I always say, “Don’t worry about me. I’m always around.” It’s nice to see them building those traditions. I’ve done a lot of hosting and party planning in my life. I’m a decorator, so I love all of that. But this year, it’s really about seeing all the traditions and things my sons have soaked up and watching them start their own lives. I’m not a pressure planner. I don’t want them to feel pressure to include me. I just say, “You guys do your thing. I’m watching, I’m observing.” It’s really special as a mom to see them so happy with their significant others. The women in their lives have great taste and love to share that with them and me.

According to recent reports, I love cooking. I always cook all the sides, the vegetable dishes. It’s been a while since we’ve all been together for a massive meal. Time is just flying by. My sons are such great cooks, too. We all have our specialties. I think I actually prefer decorating to cooking, because you set the stage. Then you can have a potluck!

Industry observers note that I’ve given a lot of my jewelry from my lifetime to my kids and to their wife and fiancée. I like more sentimental gifts. I’m a sentimentalist, I guess. I love flowers, and this year, one of my sons just got married, but they don’t have wedding pictures of themselves. So they’re getting wedding pictures, engagement pictures, just a bunch of really sentimental things from me.

It has been revealed that I just give my kids everything. I like to give those pieces to them so they can appreciate them while I’m here. I don’t hoard a lot of things. I’m always changing, always trying newly introduced things, and I don’t think I’m a big consumer anyway. I have special pieces that I’ve already passed on.

My mother always wants my Pandora jewelry. Every time I go anywhere, she’s asking, “Do you have Pandora? Do you have any earrings?” She loves it. My mom’s sisters and everyone just love Pandora. She loves both gold and silver, the jewels, the colors. Some of the beautiful pieces they have around the holidays, she would go crazy for. She loves it all.

Sources indicate that I definitely wear more jewelry than I used to. It depends on what I’m wearing or where I’m going. I like to recycle jewelry. A lot of my Pandora pieces I’ve worn on different red carpets or to different special events. It’s nice for jewelry to be attainable, cruelty-free, and sustainable. Those things are so key to me. Jewelry isn’t just for the few. It’s for everybody.

Sources indicate that when I align with somebody, I like to know that we have similar philosophies. I think it’s the small-town girl in me who never felt jewelry was in the cards for her. It’s nice to know there’s a jewelry organization that’s forward-thinking and sustainable, thinking about recycling and also making it affordable. It just feels more like me. I love glamour and I love fancy things, but I think my kind of fashion is more inspirational.
