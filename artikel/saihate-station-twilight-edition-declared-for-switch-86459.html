Publisher PLAYISM and developer viv have announced Saihate Station: Twilight Edition, a Switch version of the 2D exploration horror adventure game including the base game and downloadable content “Twilight Railway,” as well as two mini-games. It will launch on March 25, 2027 worldwide with English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language support. A physical edition will be available in Japan for 8,800 yen.

Saihate Station first introduced for PC via Steam on March 7, 2025. The downloadable content “Twilight Railway” launched on April 18, 2025.

Here is an overview of the game, via PLAYISM:.

Sources indicate that since its release in March 2025, this 2D exploration horror adventure game has recorded a cumulative series total of 1.6 million downloads, an exceptional achievement for an indie free game, and continues to receive passionate support from fans in Japan and abroad. Now it is finally coming to Nintendo Switch!

Saihate Station: Twilight Edition includes the main game, the downloadable content “Twilight Railway,” and two mini-games, all in one package.

“After falling asleep on the last train, I woke up in a bizarre place called Saihate Station…”.

Recent findings suggest that haru Akinashi is a self-effacing office worker who lacks confidence and fails at everything he tries. Having slept through his stop on the last train, he finds himself trapped in a bizarre place called Saihate Station, where gruesome supernatural phenomena occur.

Shion Tatsunami is his complete opposite: a cheerful, talented colleague and former friend—happens to be there as well, and the two promise to work together to return home.

Their interactions are awkward at first, given the estrangement, but with each problem they overcome together, Haru recalls their former friendship, and their bond grows stronger than before.

Then, the truth of the world closes in.

The main game features four multiple-ending story routes. The ending branches based on fluctuations in Haru’s “dependency level,” which changes according to the player’s actions.

Industry observers note that starting November 20, pre-orders for the physical edition will begin sequentially at retailers in Japan. As a first-print bonus, a “shaka-shaka acrylic stand keychain” featuring a train and character motif will be included. A limited edition featuring a special box with an “exclusive newly drawn mini-game book” and a “Haru-Shion handkerchief” is also planned. Bonuses and limited editions are available in limited quantities, so please place your pre-order early.
