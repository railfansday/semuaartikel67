📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Grand Theft Auto games are full of NPCs that make the world feel more real, or more alive, or more dangerous. But none of them accomplish what GTAVI’s Cool Dog has done in their small cameo in the game: Be both Cool and a Dog.

Sources indicate that what’s that, you say? You’ve been through most of the game at this point and haven’t met anyone named Cool Dog? Well, let’s fix that.

Rockstar seems like it was afraid the player was going to get themselves far out to sea in the ocean, as the fictional Leonida resembles an island more than the real-life analogue of Florida, and not have a way to boat back. It was seemingly so afraid, in fact, that it put together a mechanic to rescue you if you get lost.

Industry observers note that to make this happen purposely instead of organically, you must take a boat out into open waters and put yourself as far away from open islands or other boats as possible. I did this by opening fire on my own boat, and after the two or three times it exploded and killed me, I managed to just gently sink one instead. If you are far enough out with no land or ships for miles, you will know within seconds, as you should hear the sound of a jetski and barking around you.

And then you will then meet Cool Dog. If you target them with the dialogue button, their name will show as Cool Dog, and you can question them, and ultimately accept the free ride they are offering you as either Lucia or Jason.

Cool Dog drives the jetski near land every so often, so if you’re just looking to get back, you can hop off wherever and swim the rest of the way. If you let Cool Dog finish their journey, though, they will take you to a little island with a bunch of other still-cool-but-not-as-cool dogs. There, Lucia or Jason can play catch with them, pet the dogs, and get a cool pair of Wraparound Shades themselves from a treasure chest. Playing with the dogs for a while will also get them to dig up treasure for you on the island, which can range anywhere from disposable to useful.

Cool Dog leaves their jetski on the shore, so if you want to leave, you can drive it back. But I like to assume Cool Dog either finds a better one or just picks their jetski up wherever you leave it.

It has been revealed that cool Dog is likely based on Loosy, a Golden Retriever who went viral in May for riding a jet ski around Ft. Lauderdale, Florida. Maybe that’s why the game was delayed, so Rockstar could have time to implement this pivotal feature.
