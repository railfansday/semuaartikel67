Obviously, massive spoilers follow for GTA 6 endings!

There are multiple endings to GTA 6, and while we haven't experienced them all personally here at PC Gamer, the gaming community has been sharing them around for anyone curious about what they may have missed.

The ending you get depends on the choices you make during the final GTA 6 story mission, but also depends on how you handled Jason and Lucia's romance and criminal profiles throughout the game, so seeing every version of the ending would mean not just playing the ending multiple times, but having different playthroughs of the game altogether.

In a recent development, there are a couple of endings that I don't consider "different" from each other but do have some differences leading up to them, which I'll explain below. There may also be additional endings we haven't seen yet, so we may enhancement this guide in the future.

Recent findings suggest that in the bridge endings, Jason and Lucia have defeated Mendez and his squad and gotten the hard drive back from Georgio's penthouse and escaped. They drive to Cal's place in Key Lento, but discover he has been compromised. They drive to the bridge, but the cops have set up a roadblock and have the couple trapped. Lucia wants to keep going, but Jason suggests they jump off the bridge and swim for it.

A choice comes up on the screen: "Swim For it" or "No Surrender.".

It has been revealed that jason and Lucia try to run the roadblock and are shot to death by cops on the bridge. They die holding hands. In the credits, we see that their son Tomas lives happily with Lucia's aunt, her husband, and their children.

Jason and Lucia are shot to death by cops on the bridge. They die holding hands. In the credits, their lawyer, Abe, brings the money to Lucia's aunt. Tomas lives happily with Lucia's aunt, her husband, and their children in a much bigger house than the other No Surrender ending.

Choosing "Swim for it" presents an additional choice. When Jason gets out of the car, Lucia can choose to "Exit" the car with him, or change her mind by not choosing it.

Sources indicate that jason and Lucia decide to jump off the bridge and swim for it. They both exit the car without holding guns. Lucia jumps, but Jason doesn't jump and is arrested. Lucia signs a deal for immunity while Jason goes to prison. Lucia gets a job in a diner. Lucia takes Tomas to meet Jason in prison, but he can only see his son through a glass divider.

Jason and Lucia decide to jump off the bridge and swim for it. Jason exits the car with a gun, but Lucia changes her mind and stays in the car. The police open fire and Jason and Lucia are both shot to death. Tomas lives with Lucia's aunt, her husband, and their children.

According to recent reports, jason and Lucia decide to jump off the bridge and swim for it, Jason doesn't jump and is arrested. Two years later, Lucia uses the savings account to get Jason freed from prison. Lucia goes to the prison to pick up Jason. Jason meets Tomas and holds his hand.

If you don't have a strong relationship between Jason and Lucia, there is a different ending location: the base of Georgio's tower. In these endings, the couple never makes it to the bridge. You can also choose to break up the couple, either as Jason or as Lucia, as you begin the mission Protective Custody.

After the tower fight with Georgio's men, police confront the couple at the base of the tower. The police open fire and Jason pushes Lucia down. Jason is shot. Lucia has a choice to "Stay down" or "Defend Jason". She stays down and watches Jason die. Lucia is arrested, signs a deal, and visits Tomas at her aunt's house. Lucia then leaves Tomas with her aunt, takes out a gun, and continues her life of crime by preparing to rob a diner.

After the tower fight with Georgio's men, police confront them at the base of the tower. The police open fire and Jason pushes Lucia down. Jason is shot. Lucia chooses "Defend Jason" and fights the cops. They die together.

It has been revealed that on the way down to ground level at the tower, Jason decides to run. Lucia is arrested and signs the deal, then lives with her aunt and Tomas while working in a diner. One night there's a knock on the door and she finds a bag of cash on the porch. We see Jason driving away with... Rocco the drug-sniffing dog from earlier in the game. Sure, that might as well happen.

How to get this ending (sourced by GamingwithTZ on YouTube):.

Industry observers note that jason and Lucia make a deal with Mendez after defeating him. Once they have the hard drive, they're surrounded by cops at the bottom of the tower. Mendez arrives and takes them away in his car, then drives them to the swamp. Later in the credits, we see two skulls in the swamp, side-by-side, with a bullet hole in each.

Sources indicate that note: You may need a specific criminal profile for this ending. I tried to take the deal (as Lucia) in one of my ending playthroughs, but Jason shot Mendez anyway. I had both Jason and Lucia at "Professional" level criminal profiles. This ending was not available to me. Speculation, but this ending may only be available if you don't have "Professional" as your criminal profiles.

There are potentially (and almost certainly) more endings to GTA 6. There are still a few combinations of all the different variables, including your romance level, criminal profile level, amount of money saved, and the choices you make during the final mission, that we haven't seen yet.

We'll revision this guide if the community finds more endings.
