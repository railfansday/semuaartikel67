Industry observers note that blackburn Rovers head to Turf Moor off the back of their 2-1 win at home against West Ham last time out before the international break. Attacking pair Andri Gudjohnsen and Dapo Afolayan got their goals against the Hammers as they secured an eye-catching three points.

The Lancashire outfit are positioned in 13th place in the Championship table after their first 15 fixtures of the season. They are only three points off the play-offs behind both QPR and Southampton.

Dunn, who had two separate spells at Ewood Park during his playing career, has delivered his score prediction ahead of the weekend via The Red Rose Divide on YouTube: “I’m going to go with 2-1 Blackburn. First goal scorer it would be daft not to pick Gudjohnsen but I feel like it might be a set piece. I think there could be some set pieces.”.

It has been revealed that blackburn Rovers have seen head coach Tony Mowbray take a step back this week due to health concerns. Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds will take first-team duties for the time being.

It has been revealed that dunn believes Blackburn Rovers can hurt Burnley from set-pieces. Centre-backs Hayden Carter and Sean McLoughlin will head up the pitch for set pieces.

In striker Gudjohnsen, Rovers have one of the best attackers in the division at the moment. The 24-year-old, who is an Iceland international with 45 caps to his name, has 11 goals in 15 outings in all competitions in this campaign.

Todd Cantwell has flourished so far this term and he injects valuable quality into the team. He is out of contract next summer and his long-term situation needs addressing sooner rather than later.

It has been revealed that afolayan has also hit the ground running over recent times and can cause problems for opposition defenders. The ex-Bolton Wanderers man joined from St. Pauli last January and initially took a bit of time to find his feet.

In a recent development, blackburn Rovers will be in confident mood after seeing off West Ham. They can’t afford to be complacent against Burnley though and will need to be at their best to get something, especially in a derby game.
