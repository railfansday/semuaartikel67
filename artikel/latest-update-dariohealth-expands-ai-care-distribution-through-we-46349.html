📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

DarioHealth announced a partnership with WebMD Health Services on November 19, 2026, to distribute its AI-powered multi-condition care platform to the well-being organization’s network of more than 3,500 employers, health plans, health systems and public-sector organizations.

According to recent reports, webMD Health Services states its network covers 65 million lives. That figure represents potential distribution, not committed enrollment in DarioHealth’s programs.

Recent findings suggest that darioHealth expects annual recurring revenue from the agreement to begin in 2027. One anticipated route is distribution through the administrative services organization of an integrated healthcare system with a broad network of hospitals and healthcare services.

The integration pairs WebMD ONE, the WebMD engagement platform, with DarioHealth’s clinical programs and provider-backed care. WebMD Health Services contributes risk-identification, engagement and behavior-change capabilities. DarioIQ, DarioHealth’s AI-driven intelligence engine, uses proprietary longitudinal health data, meaning information collected over time, to personalize member engagement and interventions.

DarioHealth indicates its AI platform draws on more than 13 billion proprietary healthcare data points collected over more than a decade and analyzes biometric, clinical and behavioral data to tailor interventions to individual members.

Recent findings suggest that organizations already using WebMD Health Services will be able to connect members from WebMD ONE into personalized clinical interventions across multiple chronic conditions. By placing those services in one workflow, the companies are seeking to reduce the complexity of managing multiple point solutions.

WebMD Health Services will offer DarioHealth’s full suite: cardiometabolic care, behavioral health, musculoskeletal care with posture management, obstructive sleep apnea support, and women’s health, including perimenopause and menopause support. The offering also covers GLP-1 support programs and access to care through DarioHealth’s provider network.

“As clients choose to add Dario, our multi-condition platform creates the opportunity to deliver multiple solutions through each new customer relationship,” said DarioHealth CEO Erez Raphael. He also said DarioIQ supports personalized engagement at scale, while provider-backed care extends the organization’s role further into the care journey.

John Harrison, president and general manager of WebMD Health Services, said the agreement builds on WebMD ONE’s existing cost-containment services by adding more clinically intensive, personalized support intended to help clients address health risks and manage spending across a broader range of needs.

DarioHealth reported that a recent independent analysis of published peer-reviewed studies found potential savings of up to 10% in payers’ total healthcare costs during the year being measured, a platform-level estimate rather than a commitment to WebMD Health Services clients.

It has been revealed that the partnership joins engagement, AI-driven personalization and provider-backed care in one distribution channel. Converting that reach into recurring revenue depends on clients choosing to add DarioHealth’s services, making adoption the decisive commercial test for the agreement.
