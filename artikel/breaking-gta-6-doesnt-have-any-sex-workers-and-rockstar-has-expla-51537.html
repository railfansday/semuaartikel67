A mainstay of the GTA series has been the presence of sex workers, but Rockstar Games has dropped this aspect for the newly released GTA 6.

As reported by Shack News, unlike most other games in the long-running franchise, you cannot hire a sex worker in GTA 6.

The outlet asked Rockstar about this decision, and received the following response:.

"GTAVI is a game about a couple in a bind, lovers on the run. While we have taken a lot forward from previous GTA titles, everything we have implemented in VI has been done to support the spirit of the story we wanted to tell, and the gameplay systems we wanted to expand upon - things like wanted system, the radio, robberies and car theft. Many aspects of previous titles are not present in GTAVI, but the elements we have we believe are all necessary and complementary to the feeling and motivations we wanted to emphasize as you play through Jason and Lucia's story.".

So, in other words, sex workers aren't included this time around because it wouldn't make sense for Jason or Lucia to pursue that, as they're already in a relationship — and that's a major part of the story.

As mentioned, they have been present in the series since 2001's GTA 3, and this will be the first 3D game to exclude them.

Industry observers note that GTA 6 is also the first game that centres on a couple, however, with the relationship between Jason and Lucia interwoven in the gameplay as well as being a key tenet of the narrative. It seems Rockstar didn't think sex workers would be conducive to this goal.
