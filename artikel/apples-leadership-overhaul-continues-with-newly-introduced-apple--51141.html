Recent findings suggest that bloomberg reports that Apple lifer Corey Fugman will replace Jennifer Bailey as the head of the Apple Pay and Wallet division. Here are the details.

A few months ago, Bloomberg reported that Jennifer Bailey, Apple’s head of Apple Pay and Wallet, who had led Apple Pay since its launch in 2014, would retire in October.

At the time, Apple’s services boss Eddy Cue noted internally that the company would announce its succession proposal before her transition at the end of October, and that she would “continue to be available to support the transition in an advisory role.”.

October came and went without any public announcement on that front. Today, however, Bloomberg reported that Apple has tapped Corey Fugman, “a 26-year organization veteran as the new head of its payment service and digital wallet unit.”.

According to his LinkedIn profile, Fugman joined Apple in September 2000 as Merchandising Manager for Apple Retail and has held several leadership positions since then, including overseeing Apple Card and Apple Cash from 2019 to 2021.

Most recently, he served as Senior Director of Apple Pay and Wallet, a position he has held since November 2021.

With the latest job, Fugman inherits a crucial tap-to-pay business, which is estimated to generate billions of dollars a year. It handles tens of billions of transactions annually across more than 90 markets, with Apple taking a percentage.

In a recent development, the division also includes the Apple Card, which is transitioning from a partnership with Goldman Sachs Group Inc. to JPMorgan Chase & Co. Fugman has been highly involved with the shift to Chase, which is still ongoing, the people mentioned.

The report notes that Fugman will report to Cue, and reaffirms that Bailey “will remain a consultant in the coming months,” echoing Cue’s earlier comments about her role in the transition.

Finally, Bloomberg adds that while Apple has undergone several leadership changes in recent months, including John Ternus replacing Tim Cook as CEO, Cue’s responsibilities have expanded considerably.

In addition to overseeing services such as Apple TV, Apple Music, Maps, and iCloud, Cue has recently taken charge of the App Store and Health following Jeff Williams’ departure as Chief Operating Officer and Phil Schiller’s decision to step down from overseeing the App Store.
