United Quest Card vs. United Club Card: How premium do you want to go?

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (166 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Two premium options cater to frequent United travelers: one focuses on lounge access and faster status, the other on annual credits and award discounts.
  • The lounge-access card is ideal for those who value United Club membership and higher Premier qualifying point (PQP) earning potential.
  • The annual-credit card suits travelers seeking TravelBank cash, award discounts and core United perks without paying for lounge access.
  • Both cards provide free checked bags, priority boarding, inflight discounts and expanded award availability on United flights.
  • Eligibility for welcome offers is restricted if you currently hold the card or received its bonus in the past 24 months.
  • You can hold both cards, but overlapping benefits and combined fees may outweigh the incremental value for most travelers.
  • United miles are valued at 1.2 cents each as of November 2026 per TPG's valuations.
