Regional hosting lets Asia-Pacific enterprises keep AI agent security data in country while they scale AI agent adoption.

NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Noma, the agent security and governance platform for enterprises deploying AI agents at scale, today revealed the availability of its cloud hosting in Australia and Singapore, expanding data residency options for organizations across Asia-Pacific seeking to secure and govern their AI agents.

As enterprises integrate agents into critical business workflows, data residency is becoming an increasingly crucial consideration, particularly for organizations in regulated industries. With cloud hosting now available in Australia and Singapore, Noma enables organizations to choose where their environment is hosted, simplifying deployment while supporting their data residency requirements.

In-Country Hosting for AI Agent Security Data.

Sources indicate that organizations can keep their Noma tenant, AI agent security data, and management console hosted in their selected country, which supports their data residency requirements. Regional hosting also helps organizations align with local data protection regulations, internal security policies, and the expectations of regulators and auditors.

By expanding its regional hosting capabilities, Noma is removing another barrier to AI adoption and giving organizations the flexibility to secure and govern AI agents while meeting their business, security, and data residency requirements.

Secure Agent Adoption, Close to Home.

The expansion into Australia and Singapore reinforces Noma's commitment to supporting enterprises across Asia-Pacific to securely adopt agentic AI. Noma's mission remains to empower enterprises to embrace AI innovation with confidence, without compromising security or control.

It has been revealed that noma's Australia and Singapore cloud hosting regions are now available. New customers can select their preferred hosting region during setup while existing customers can contact their Noma account team to discuss regional hosting options.

To learn more, visit: https://noma.security/blog/control-your-agents-while-keeping-your-data-local-noma-expands-cloud-hosting-to-australia-and-singapore.

Noma is the Agent Security & Governance Platform, helping enterprises adopt AI agents safely and at scale. Its context-aware runtime controls protect agent actions without slowing innovation.
