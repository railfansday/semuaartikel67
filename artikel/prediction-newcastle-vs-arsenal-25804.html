It has been revealed that competition: Premier League Date: Saturday, November 21, 2026 Kickoff: 17:30 GMT Venue: St. James’ Park, Newcastle.

Verdict: Under 2.5 goals Best odds: 123/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet.

Arsenal resumes its title defense with a trip to Newcastle on Saturday, carrying a defensive record that gives Mikel Arteta every reason to approach another demanding away fixture with confidence. The champion has collected 27 points and seven clean sheets from its opening ten Premier League matches.

Newcastle has found consistency much harder to come by, although recent results suggest this could become a patient contest with relatively few goals.

According to recent reports, newcastle came close to entering the international break with a useful away victory. Jacob Murphy broke the deadlock at Fulham in the 80th minute, only for Rodrigo Muniz to equalize three minutes later. Harvey Barnes had also passed up a good opportunity, leaving Matthias Jaissle with some encouragement from the performance and frustration over the points that slipped away.

According to recent reports, that 1-1 draw followed a 2-0 league victory at Crystal Palace and a 1-0 home defeat against Everton. Each of those three matches finished below the 2.5-goal line, with Newcastle producing enough to remain competitive without consistently finding the finishing touch to settle games.

Against Arsenal, the balance between pressing and protecting midfield will matter. Newcastle’s best route appears to involve choosing its moments to attack, getting Barnes into space and making set pieces count. Giving the visitor repeated opportunities to attack an exposed back line would make an already difficult assignment considerably harder.

Joelinton remains an injury concern after missing the Fulham match, while goalkeeper Ewen Jaouen is unavailable with a hamstring problem. Jacob Ramsey’s one-match suspension applied to the Fulham fixture, leaving him eligible to return against Arsenal.

Arsenal has developed a useful habit of making a single goal sufficient. Its two most recent Premier League outings brought 1-0 victories over Liverpool and Hull City, with Martin Odegaard providing the breakthrough against Hull in the 72nd minute. That performance required patience, particularly while the visiting defense continued to frustrate Arsenal’s attempts to create clear chances.

In a recent development, the broader pattern strengthens the case for another restrained scoreline. Arsenal’s last four league victories have been achieved without conceding, beating Nottingham Forest, Everton, Liverpool and Hull while scoring five goals across those matches. Arteta’s side has been able to protect narrow advantages exceptionally well.

Sources indicate that for this trip, another controlled performance looks a reasonable expectation. Newcastle should provide a sterner physical examination than Hull, but Arsenal has already demonstrated at Anfield that it can leave a difficult ground with a clean sheet and three points.

Bukayo Saka remains sidelined, while Max Dowman is suspended. Martin Zubimendi needs assessment after suffering a muscle problem on international duty with Spain.

According to recent reports, william Saliba has taken another step toward returning by playing 45 minutes for the under-21 side. His involvement here remains a selection decision, rather than a confirmed return to the starting lineup.

Industry observers note that arsenal deserves favoritism, with its defensive discipline offering the strongest argument for another away victory. Newcastle should make the visitor work for opportunities, however, and the recent scoring patterns suggest this could remain close well into the second half.

Sources indicate that under 2.5 goals is the preferred selection. A 1-0 Arsenal victory is the predicted score, although a tight draw would also fit the reasoning behind the goals bet.
