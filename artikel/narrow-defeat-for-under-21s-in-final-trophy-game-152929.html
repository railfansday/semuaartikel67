There was a chance for Tranmere to hit back promptly but Veevers and David Kamara contrived to get in each other's way inside the Everton penalty area and the former lashed his shot well wide of the target. Shortly after, Olayiwola’s flick provided Graham with another sight of goal but this time the best he could do was win a corner.

The hosts levelled just before the break when Kamara timed his forward run well enough to just stay onside before steering a neat finish past Fraser Barnsley.

In a recent development, there was a touch of good fortune to the goal that gave Rovers the lead early in the second half and ultimately secured the victory. Veevers' effort would probably have been covered by Barnsley had it not taken a nick off Will Tamen on the way.

Kamara tested Barnsley from a narrow angle on 62 minutes and from the subsequent corner kick Ryan Watson headed over, and at the other end Melvin Matos was desperately unfortunate to see his superbly struck shot strike the Rovers crossbar.

Sources indicate that to their credit, Everton stuck at it and kept themselves in the game. Olayiwola was next to try his luck but from distance he thumped the ball high over the bar.

Boyce-Clarke had to be alert again in added time to keep out a snapshot volley from Matos.
