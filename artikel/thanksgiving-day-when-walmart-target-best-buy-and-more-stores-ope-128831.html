TL;DR: On Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26), Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, and most major retailers are closed all day, while select grocers (like Kroger, Wegmans, and Whole Foods) and drugstores (Walgreens and CVS) stay open with modified hours.

In a recent development, whether you realized halfway through prepping dinner that you forgot the cranberry sauce or need an emergency prescription refill, finding an open store on Thanksgiving Day is trickier than it used to be. For the seventh straight year, almost every major massive-box, warehouse, and department store — including Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco, Lowe's, and Kohl's — is keeping its doors locked all day on Thursday, Nov. 26.

That does not mean you are completely out of luck, though. A handful of grocery chains, drugstores, dollar stores, and outdoor retailers will stay open on Thanksgiving with modified holiday hours (mostly closing by mid-afternoon, often with their pharmacy counters shut).

We rounded up which stores are open and closed on Thanksgiving 2026 below so you don't waste a trip to an empty parking lot and can blueprint your Thursday errands accordingly.

According to recent reports, if you need last-minute ingredients on Thursday morning, several major supermarket chains are staying open with shortened hours. Keep in mind that most in-store grocery pharmacies are closed on Thanksgiving Day, and exact opening and closing times vary by store — so check your local store locator before leaving the house.
