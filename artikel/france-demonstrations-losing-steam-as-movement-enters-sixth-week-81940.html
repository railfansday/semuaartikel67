France: Demonstrations losing steam as movement enters sixth week

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (102 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Around 2,200 people took part in protests across France by 2pm local time, according to the Interior Ministry, which recorded 44 demonstrations nationwide and seven arrests, with the situation described as generally calm. Turnout was lower than on previous Saturdays. Reporting from Place de la République in Paris, FRANCE 24’s Antonia Kerrigan said rent rights organisations had also joined the sixth consecutive Saturday of protests, highlighting their growing pan-European dimension, with demonstrations taking place in seven European countries, including Spain, Portugal, Greece and Germany, where rising living costs and access to affordable housing are among the main concerns.
