Joshua Kimmich has criticised Bayern Munich over their handling of teammate Jamal Musiala, describing the club’s public conduct towards the midfielder — who suffers from a neurological condition that causes seizures — as "not Bayern-like".

In a recent development, the comments follow public criticism from senior figures at Bayern regarding Musiala’s performances and contract. The player experienced two seizures on the pitch during pre-season fixtures, prompting opposition from the international players' union, FIFPRO.

A key squad member has now spoken out, warning that the club risks damaging its traditional family values.

"What I don't understand is the way this is being communicated in public," Kimmich told reporters after Bayern's 2-1 Champions League loss at Lille on Wednesday. "It must never happen that the burden is placed on a player. The club is obviously the top priority and bigger than a player, but it's not bigger than a person. That's an absolute no-go and it is not Bayern-like either.".

Kimmich also questioned whether Musiala's ongoing struggles were more closely linked to physical injuries over the past two years, including a badly fractured leg at last year's Club World Cup, rather than his medical condition.

Sources indicate that "It's not the case that he has problems because he's unprofessional. He's there every day trying to get the maximum out of himself," Kimmich added.

Bayern met with Musiala's representatives on Tuesday to clarify the timeline of events, rejecting speculation that the midfielder concealed his health condition prior to signing his recent contract extension.

Both the club and Musiala's representatives accept that he was first examined for seizure symptoms in April 2025, two months after extending his contract.

"This took place in consultation with FC Bayern and its medical unit immediately after the neurological symptoms first appeared," Bayern said.

Musiala went public with his diagnosis in August after collapsing on the pitch during two pre-season matches, alarming fans and fellow professionals.

Bayern coach Vincent Kompany stated at the time that the episodes were triggered by a change in Musiala's medication. He was rested for the start of the campaign and has since missed games due to a muscle injury.

Recent findings suggest that musiala came off the bench in the 76th minute of Wednesday's defeat at Lille, capping a difficult week for Bayern. Ballon D'Or winner Harry Kane injured his ankle on Friday and faces months on the sidelines, while Bayern have lost the lead in the Bundesliga and fallen to 15th in the Champions League standings.
