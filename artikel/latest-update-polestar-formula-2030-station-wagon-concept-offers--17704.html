Latest Update: Polestar Formula 2030 Station Wagon Concept Offers a Futuristic Blueprint for Scandinavian Design

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Before Polestar’s public battle with the U.S. Department of Commerce earlier this year, the Swedish automaker was perhaps best known here for its avant-garde Scandinavian design language. Now, it seems like Polestar is simply teasing us after being banned from the United States starting in 2027, as the newly revealed Polestar Formula 2030 concept car is one of the coolest wagons we’ve ever seen.

According to recent reports, the Formula 2030 concept car is the creation of Polestar’s head of design, Philipp Römers, and opens a window into the company’s future direction. The brand’s “remove the unnecessary, improve what remains, and refuse to compromise” design philosophy led the project, and Römers said the model is a practical four-door sports car. At 184 inches long, 79.5 inches wide, and with a 109-inch wheelbase, the Polestar Formula 2030 is sized between the outgoing Polestar 2 sedan and the incoming Polestar 7 hatchback. Polestar declined to provide any mechanical details about the Formula 2030.

In a recent development, “Design should always reflect the values and mission of a brand, and that is exactly what we did with Polestar Formula 2030 concept,” Römers stated. “It is a physical embodiment of how we apply Polestar‘s core values of design, performance, and sustainability to the next production cars. A bold evolution of our already well-established design DNA to strengthen the brand‘s positioning with performance at its heart.”.

According to recent reports, A long hood, short overhangs, and 22-inch center-lock wheels exemplify the classic Polestar look, while also expressing “power and performance.” Dual-blade headlights mark the front end and serve as a preview of the updated Polestar 2, which will arrive in 2027. The shark nose and deep front splitter create the central spine of the Formula 2030 and give it a low and wide stance, adding to its performance credentials. A single large windscreen wiper pays homage to motorsport.

Polestar’s Single Blade rear lights highlight the rear end; instead of a connected light bar, the organization wanted to explore latest geometric shapes and angles. The rear glass also wraps around the side of the vehicle, opening a shallow side-view behind the C-pillar. A fixed rear wing sits on lit mounts, while the lower rear diffuser adds genuine downforce.

It has been revealed that “We wanted to evolve, while respecting, the core design values we demonstrated with previous models that define the Polestar brand in such a recognizable way. And with Formula 2030, with its clear Polestar design attributes like the Dual Blade headlights, wraparound visor-like windshield and powerful haunches, we have done just that,” Nahum Escobedo, head of exterior design at Polestar, noted. “But we’ve pushed perceptions, preconceptions, and the expected as well, with our considered choices in form language, amplification of the performance topic, and new detailing like the Single Blade rear lights.”.

Inside the Formula 2030, the automaker stuck with minimalism. A 12.2-inch Center Screen Display is paired with the 20-inch “HyperDIM”—a precisely curved Driver Information Monitor—to round out the digital offerings. Air-inflatable bolsters are fitted to each seat to balance cushioned comfort and performance-oriented ergonomics. When combined with Polestar’s three drive modes—Performance, Normal, and Autonomous—the center displays and adjustable seats will position themselves for the mood; for example, in Performance mode, the steering wheel, screens, and seats will lower and move toward the driver. Similarly, Polestar will offer a configurable mix of physical and digital controls in the center stack; users can choose which controls they want to be actioned by the physical buttons. (Attentive readers will also notice a steering yoke in place of the typical steering wheel.).

According to recent reports, finally, the Polestar Formula 2030 reveals off a fresh “Nitrogen” exterior color, which is specific to the concept. Variations of the new color in gloss, matte, and satin finishes are scattered around the car. Polestar’s characteristic Swedish Gold color adorns the Brembo brake calipers, valve caps, interior detailing, and seat belts. The interior finish of the doors is made from a fully recyclable material, which is contrasted by exposed structural interior parts such as the seat frame made from low-emission galvanized steel. All told, the Polestar Formula 2030 offers a compelling glimpse of what’s ahead for the Swedish automaker, and we’re sure sad to miss out on it.
