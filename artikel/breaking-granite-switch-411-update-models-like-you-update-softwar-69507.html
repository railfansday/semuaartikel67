Just over a week ago, we released Granite Switch 4.1, showing that our modular Switch architecture lets you configure a language model with specialized, cache-efficient adapters — adding fresh expertise on a single endpoint.

Industry observers note that granite Switch 4.1.1 is a patch release. The version number means what it means in software: fully backwards compatible with 4.1, no migration, incremental improvements under the hood.

According to recent reports, while we are excited about these newly introduced features, what is notable is the speed and efficiency with which our teams could release this enhancement. Apart from training the new ASR model and Decision adapter, nothing was recomputed: the base model is untouched. You compose the latest adapters you want and deploy.

This pattern can power far more than patch releases to an IBM-provided model. We're working on tutorials and an Adapter SDK so developers and third-party providers can build their own adapters — iterate on versions, test features, and ship updates the way you ship software. More soon.

Until then, test out our latest models, and try out the fresh multilingual and punctuation capabilities powering our Speech-to-Decisions demo.
