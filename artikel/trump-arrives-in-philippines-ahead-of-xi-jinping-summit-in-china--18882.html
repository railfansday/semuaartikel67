President Donald Trump touched down in the Philippines on Tuesday as part of an Asia trip that will take him to China for a historic summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

The trip marks Trump's first overseas visit since Republicans suffered bruising defeats in the U.S. midterm elections.

Trump noted during a gaggle on Monday that while in China this week he will meet with Chinese President Xi Jingping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

"And then we go to China. And we'll be with President Xi, he was here a month ago. And we're also gonna meet with President Putin. And we may meet with Kim Jong Un also," Trump noted, referring to North Korea's leader. "He may come. I don't know. That's up to President Xi," Trump later added.

Trump indicated that during the meeting with his Chinese and Russian counterparts, they would discuss challenges including denuclearization, Iran, Ukraine, economics and trade.

Asked on Tuesday if Kim Jong Un would come, Trump noted, "He would like to, yeah. We'll see what happens.

The trip also comes after tech giant Nvidia confirmed last week that President and CEO Jensen Huang had resigned from the company — the resignation announcement was made after Huang admitted to cheating on his wife amid reporting that he had a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy.

Trump met Tuesday in the Philippines with leaders of the Quad, which includes the U.S., Japan, Australia and India.

He signed a document with Thailand Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul on Tuesday as the two nations moved closer toward inking a trade deal.

"Today's deal lays the foundation for much stronger trading relationship that will bring the United States and Thailand closer than ever before," Trump stated.

"Thailand will eliminate all tariffs on U.S. exports ... and remove substantial non-tariff barriers on American vehicles, medical devices, pharmaceuticals, ethanol and poultry. Thailand will also be buying an estimated $2.6 billion in additional U.S. agricultural products," he stated.

Recent findings suggest that "This is an incredible victory for both countries, both the people of Thailand and the American people. And I look forward to finalizing the details of our formal agreement in the coming weeks," Trump stated, noting that today they were signing "a joint statement talking about the deal," which will eventually be finalized.

"Actually, it's a very major trade deal," Trump later noted.

The title on the document they signed reads, "Joint Statement on an Agreement Between the United States of America and the Kingdom of Thailand on Reciprocal Trade." The statement says that the two men "have reached substantial conclusion in their negotiations" toward a trade agreement.

Trump also addressed world leaders during an Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) gathering in the Philippines on Tuesday.

"I'm here today because America is deeply committed to the success, sovereignty and prosperity of our many friends and partners — many of whom are at this table right now — but partners in this region, and we always will be, we're with you always," he noted.
