In episode 764 of the Fine Homebuilding Podcast, Noah Kanter writes in with a question about ceiling insulation for a mid-1980s addition of a home he’s renovating in Rochester, Latest York. The roof is a vented assembly with a ridge vent and several soffit vents on each side and no rafter/insulation baffles. The roof sheathing is also in mediocre condition but isn’t leaking; Noah expects to replace this further down the road. For now, he’s curious about the best strategy for preserving the vented roof assembly. The podcast crew weighs in.
