Marks out of ten for how the Scotland players fared against Switzerland at Berit Sitterstadion in St. Gallen.

Scotland suffered a miserable 4-0 Nations League defeat against Switzerland in St Gallen as Andy Robertson’s record-breaking 103rd international appearance was overshadowed by a second-half collapse.

Sebastien Pocognoli’s side started brightly, pressing with greater intensity and creating some promising openings. However, their positive start was undone in the 22nd minute when Lewis Ferguson inadvertently turned Zachary Athekame’s cross into his own net.

The visitors responded well, with Aaron Hickey heading narrowly wide from Robertson’s excellent delivery, but the visitors were unable to find an equaliser before the break. Brentford right-back Hickey’s evening was also cut short by another hamstring injury, forcing him off after 36 minutes.

Any hopes of a comeback disappeared within six minutes of the restart. Winsley Boteli doubled Switzerland’s advantage in the 47th minute before Alvyn Sanches produced a superb finish to make it three.

Scotland struggled to recover, with Scott McTominay missing a glorious opportunity to pull one back from close range. The Swiss remained comfortable throughout and added a fourth in the 89th minute when Zeki Amdouni’s effort took a deflection off Luke Graham and beat Angus Gunn.
