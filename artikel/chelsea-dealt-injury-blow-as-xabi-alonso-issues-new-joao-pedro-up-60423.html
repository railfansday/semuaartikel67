Joao Pedro is still not fit to face Leeds United this weekend, Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has revealed.

The Blues have been unable to call on the striker since his shock omission from their starting lineup for last month’s 2-0 home defeat to Tottenham.

The Brazilian had picked up a thigh injury in training and has not represented the west Londoners since.

During that time, Chelsea’s form has dipped as a result and they have lost to both Spurs and Sunderland in the Premier League as well as being eliminated from the Carabao Cup by Liverpool on penalties.

Joao Pedro also sat out of Brazil duty during the international break.

Before the 1-0 defeat at the Stadium of Light on November 8 in their last game before the international break, Alonso had remarked there were “not really [any] updates” on the forward’s situation.

Chelsea’s proposal had always been to wait until the return of club football following the international window and then reassess the 25-year-old’s situation.

On Friday afternoon, Alonso provided a fresh update on Joao Pedro’s recovery as he previewed his side’s home league fixture against Daniel Farke’s Leeds on Saturday.

“Not yet,” the Spaniard replied when asked about his No9.

“He’s not ready to train yet for the team. Not for Leeds no. We need to see day by day how he is feeling.”.

Chelsea will also be without summer signing Maxence Lacroix, who picked up a hamstring injury in the loss at Anfield.

Standard Sport reported previously that the Blues fear he could be out until the turn of the year.

“He is not ready to play for tomorrow and is a similar situation to Joao,” Alonso noted of the French international.
