The Amex Membership Rewards program has just introduced a latest bonus for points transfers to Avianca Lifemiles. While this is hardly the biggest bonus, something is better than nothing, and the program is useful for Star Alliance redemptions.

Between November 16 and December 16, 2026, it’s possible to receive a 10% bonus when moving points from Amex Membership Rewards to Avianca Lifemiles. This is being offered on Amex’s end, so when you go the points transfer page, make sure that you see this offer available.

Industry observers note that ordinarily points transfer between programs at a 1:1 ratio (in increments of 1,000 points), while through this promotion you’d get 1,100 Lifemiles for every 1,000 Amex Membership Rewards points transferred. Points transfers from Amex to Avianca are typically instant, and the bonus miles will post the same time as the standard miles.
