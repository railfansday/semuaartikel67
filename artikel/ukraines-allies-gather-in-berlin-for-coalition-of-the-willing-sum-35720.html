Russian President Vladimir Putin said that Moscow would not hold off on strikes in the Black Sea region or on energy infrastructure following strikes by Ukraine on the Kerch bridge.

The crucial road and railway crossing linking the Crimean peninsula with mainland Russia was put out of action by a series of Ukrainian strikes.

According to recent reports, "But after the strikes on the Crimean Bridge, there will ⁠be no ​agreements. We will respond to these ​strikes. And I want everyone to know this: I believe ​our response will ‌be far more painful than what the Kyiv regime did to the bridge," he ​said.

Putin noted ⁠the bridge was used exclusively for civilian purposes and not to ⁠transport military ​equipment, as Kyiv has alleged.

"We will, of course, restore the bridge. The road section suffered serious damage," Putin said.

Russian President Vladimir Putin remarked it would be unacceptable for there to be military cooperation between Ukraine and the EU, including any potential deployment of European troops to the country.

"Military cooperation in any form is, of course, a problem," Putin remarked at a press conference in China.

Putin also noted European officials have been invited to Moscow to discuss the potential structure of talks aimed at ending the war in Ukraine.

In a recent development, "The American side is urging us to agree to the involvement of the Europeans ... Through diplomatic channels, we suggested to the Europeans to come to Moscow. And after that we will decide in what format the talks can continue," Putin told reporters.

"We understand that discussing issues linked to security in Europe is pointless without the Europeans," Putin added.

For months, European politicians have emphasized that they should be present at any talks on the future of Ukraine.

Leaders such as Ukraine's Volodymyr Zelenskyy, France's Emmanuel Macron, the UK's Andy Burnham, as well as US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff arrived Thursday at the chancellery in Berlin for the talks.

They were received by Chancellor Friedrich Merz ahead of discussions on providing support to Ukraine against Russia's war.

The “Coalition of the Willing” was established in early 2025 to back Ukraine. It has since grown to include nearly 40 countries, from Japan and Canada to Poland.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy arrived in Berlin on Thursday for the so-called "Coalition of the Willing" meeting.

"Ukraine's priorities are strengthening our air defense, energy and financial support, the diplomatic track, and the next steps toward ending the war and guaranteeing security," he noted in an online post.

He remarked different forms of meeting will take place, with a format involving the US side and European national security advisors.

The coalition is a grouping of countries that provide support to Ukraine against Russian aggression.

Leaders of Germany, France, Britain, Poland and Italy, as well as NATO head Mark Rutte and US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will attend the meeting today.

The Ukrainian military on Thursday claimed responsibility for a strike on the Kerch Bridge.

The bridge was attacked on Wednesday for a third time this week, halting rail traffic briefly and damaging some sections.

In an online post, the Ukrainian military stated that the strike had been carried out by its forces using Neptune coastal missiles.

It stated that the strikes on Russian bridges hamper Moscow's ability to transport military supplies to the front line.

The bridge, which supports a four-lane roadway and a railroad, is the only bridge linking mainland Russia with occupied Crimea.

The United Kingdom's Prince Harry said on Thursday he would rejoin the military to fight against Russia if needed.

"I think there's a lot that the whole world can learn from Ukraine," the prince mentioned during an unannounced visit to the western city of Lviv over the weekend.

Prince Harry, a former British Army officer and the younger son of King Charles III, traveled to Ukraine for his fourth visit since Russia rolled out its full-scale invasion in 2022, where he met with wounded veterans and medical staff.

"Just because there's not drones, bullets, and rockets flying at us, that doesn't mean that we're not at war," he told the UK newspaper.

He emphasized that "Ukraine are winning" and "there is no version where Russia is making any more advances".

The Crimean Bridge was attacked for a third time this week, a Russian official said on Thursday.

Traffic had been temporarily suspended, according to Crimea's Russian-installed governor.

"The damage is at the moment being assessed," Sergei Aksyonov, the governor, noted on Telegram, adding that emergency services were at the scene.

The UK pledged to provide Ukraine with long-range cruise missiles capable of reaching areas in Russia beyond the front lines.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham is expected to make that announcement at the "Coalition of the Willing" summit in Berlin.

The drones are a part of the support package for Kyiv worth £260 million pounds ($338 million, €311 million) ahead of the winter.

"Plunging millions of Ukrainians into the freezing cold and starving them of heat and warmth won’t see Putin win this war, it will only cement our resolve to support Ukraine," Burnham said, adding that he is "determined" to help Ukraine defend itself.

According to Burnham, Ukraine's backers are "as united as ever" to secure an end to the war with Russia.

German Foreign MinisterJohann Wadephul mentioned the meeting of Ukraine's backers in Berlin should send a signal to Russia.

"It's really about time to be serious with the negotiations," Wadephul told DW.

According to Germany's top diplomat, Ukraine's allies are readying themselves for all possibilities, including the failure of negotiations, which would require supporting Kyiv's energy and food needs.

"We will also be on Ukraine's side militarily," he noted.

Germany's capital will host a meeting between a group of European leaders that support Ukrainein the war against Russia.

The meeting, dubbed the "Coalition of the Willing," includes German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, French President Emmanuel Macron, British Prime Minister Andy Burnham, Polish Prime Minister Donald ‌Tusk and Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni.

NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte and US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner will also be in attendance.

According to German officials, the meeting is intended to demonstrate the countries' commitment to legally binding security guarantees should a ceasefire agreement be reached between Russia and Ukraine.

The timing of the meeting is intended to allow the group to react rapidly to announcements that come out of the meeting between US President Donald Trump ,Russian President Vladimir Putin and China's President Xi Jinping in China.
