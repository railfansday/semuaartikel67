Recent findings suggest that fox News' Madeline Rivera provides details on a reported cyberattack after a string of outages, with the FBI at the center of this attack. Rivera says this is the second breach in recent months.

Hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC), three sources familiar with the matter told Fox News, marking the second cyberattack against the bureau in months.

The FBI is investigating the hack to determine the unauthorized entry point, but for now, it is unclear what information was compromised, sources remarked.

The NCIC database is a critical information-sharing tool for federal, state and local authorities, helping them track fugitives, monitor registered sex offenders, check whether someone has failed a gun background check, among many other things.

In an interview with Fox News' Kayleigh McEnany on Saturday morning, President Donald Trump said he had "just heard" about the hack.

According to recent reports, "I will say that it’s not good. I don’t like that," Trump added.

The FBI did not immediately respond to a request for comment. CNN first reported the hack.

In September, a group called ShinyHunters hacked into the FBI's job portal website intending to steal sensitive employment records.

The mysterious group, known for extortion plots, claimed it stole sensitive information belonging to almost every FBI agent, including names, home addresses, Social Security numbers, assignments and, in some cases, the names of family members.

FBI STRIKES BACK AT HACKING GROUP WITH OVERSEAS ARREST OF ALLEGED LEADER.

Sources indicate that canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky was arrested on Oct. 8 and faces federal extortion conspiracy charges in connection with the FBI's investigation into ShinyHunters, according to court records and law enforcement sources that spoke to Reuters.

The details of Dubrovsky's alleged crimes are unknown as the court records in relation to his case remain sealed.

Industry observers note that FBI Director Kash Patel revealed the arrest of ShinyHunters "suspected co-conspirator" on Oct. 9, a day after Dubrovsky was taken into custody.
