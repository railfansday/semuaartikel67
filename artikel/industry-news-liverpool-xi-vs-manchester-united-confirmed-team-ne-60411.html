Andoni Iraola should have a relatively healthy squad to choose from when he selects Liverpool’s starting XI to face arch-rivals Manchester United at Anfield on Sunday.

Industry observers note that and the Basque had plenty of good news to deliver at his pre-match press conference, as he confirmed Virgil van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch. and Jeremie Frimpong - all previously considered doubts - would be fit to feature.

Both missed out on international duty with the Netherlands - van Dijk pulling out of the squad after concerns over a ‘little niggle’ he has been carrying.

Dominik Szoboszlai has struggled with fitness in recent weeks, but came through two games for Hungary unscathed. Iraola will have also have been pleased by the news that Alexander Isak was rested for Sweden’s second game of the international break, leaving the Reds’ top scorer in top condition for the weekend.

According to recent reports, in goal, veteran Alisson will start in between the sticks. Van Dijk’s decision to drop out of the Netherlands squad seemed precautionary, and it is hoped he will feature here.

The Dutchman is building a strong connection with Jeremy Jacquet at the back. Milos Kerkez is first choice at left back, but injuries at right-back have caused Iraola headaches all season.

Ronald Araujo has deputised on the right for the majority of the season, and he will likely start there again.

A fully fit Szoboszlai is Liverpool’s best midfielder, so the Hungarian is slated to start alongside Alexis Mac Allister in the heart of the Reds’ team.

Florian Wirtz saw his best run of form since signing for the club last summer interrupted slightly by injury, but the German should soon get back into a rhythm with another start at number 10.

On the wings, Bradley Barcola and Cody Gakpo are emerging as first choices - but it wouldn’t be a surprise to see Victor Munoz start ahead of Barcola.

It has been revealed that up top, Isak will once again lead the line. The Swede will hope to add to his seven Premier League goals in nine top-flight appearances.
