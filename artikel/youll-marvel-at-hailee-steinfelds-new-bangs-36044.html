Hailee Steinfeld is ending the year with a bang.

The Sinners alum showed off an impromptu set of bangs she had styled between stops on her press tour for her animated adventure movie, Hexed.

In a recent development, "I had one last thing to do before I went back to Buffalo…cut those bangs!" Steinfeld shared Nov. 18 on her Beau Society Substack, alongside a mirror selfie. "I love them so much.".

But while the Marvel actress is spellbound by her fresh fringe—especially as the young witch she plays in the coming-of-age tale also rocks bangs—she still admitted perfecting the new look has taken a little magic.

"I have a cowlick—not exactly a recipe for success with bangs, but here we are," the 29-year-old quipped, noting that a set of stylish gold hair clips "have been saving me.".

Before she committed to the chop, achieved with the help of her longtime stylist Renato Campora, Steinfeld recalled the timeless advice from the hair pro about how to make the infamously tricky decision to crop her hair or not.

Recent findings suggest that "I asked Renato if he could give me bangs, and he told me to sleep on it," the "Starving" singer recounted of their conversation before Hexed's L.A. premiere Nov. 12. "If I still wanted them the next time I saw him (the following day), we could do it.".

Recent findings suggest that but her upcoming 'do isn't the only patch Steinfeld has reveled in during her first international film tour since appearing in Ryan Coogler's vampire flick last year. The new mom, who welcomed daughter Harper, 7 months, with husband Josh Allen in April, has also taken quality time with her little one on the road.

"My little Harper Haize was with me while I was getting ready," she gushed over the mommy-daughter moment on premiere day. "She’s too tiny to come to a movie premiere with me—even for a Disney animation—but she’s been such a huge part of this whole Hexed experience.".

With the baby girl's arrival, Steinfeld has also found blending her life as a mom and a movie star "surreal.".

As she explained about Harper, "She was with me during my recording sessions (before she was born) and now she’s singing and babbling in my arms while I get my hair and makeup done for the premiere.".
