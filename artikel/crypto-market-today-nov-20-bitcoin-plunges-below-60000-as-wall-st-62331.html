By RJ Fulton – Nov 20, 2026 at 4:45PM EST.

Cryptocurrency markets suffered another brutal sell-off on Friday, November 20, as a wave of selling across Wall Street spilled into digital assets. Bitcoin broke below the psychologically vital $60,000 level, while Ethereum and Solana experienced even steeper declines as investors continued to retreat from riskier assets.

As of early evening, Bitcoin (BTC -4.94%) had plunged 6.01% to $58,186.39, while Ethereum (ETH -7.80%) tumbled 9.03% to $1,596.72 and Solana (SOL -5.39%) declined 8.02% to $67.21. The latest losses come as major cryptocurrencies struggle to establish a bottom, with weakening liquidity and persistent selling pressure raising the prospect of fresh bear market lows.

Deteriorating investor risk appetite continued to weigh on cryptocurrencies Friday as concerns over interest rates, elevated valuations, and tighter financial conditions fueled a broad sell-off across global markets. With the Nasdaq plunging more than 5%, investors appeared increasingly focused on raising cash and reducing exposure to riskier assets.

The simultaneous decline in stocks, gold, and cryptocurrencies suggests selling pressure is extending beyond individual markets. Rather than simply rotating capital between asset classes, investors appear to be reducing their overall risk exposure, leaving cryptocurrencies particularly vulnerable as liquidity conditions continue to deteriorate.

Spot cryptocurrency ETFs suffered steep losses on Friday, mirroring the broader sell-off in digital assets. The iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT -5.87%) fell 5.87%, while the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC -6.16%) declined 5.97%. Meanwhile, the iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA -9.16%) plunged 9.16%, reflecting Ethereum's particularly sharp decline.

Recent findings suggest that bitcoin's break below $60,000 marks another significant deterioration in the cryptocurrency market's technical outlook. With Ethereum approaching $1,500 and Solana falling below $70, several major cryptocurrencies are now trading near levels that could determine whether the current bear market extends into 2027. If selling pressure persists, investors could see another round of fresh lows before a sustainable recovery takes shape.

Beyond price action, the broader withdrawal of capital from digital assets presents a more fundamental challenge. Bitcoin ETF outflows have reportedly exceeded $2.5 billion in 2026, while the simultaneous sell-off across stocks, gold, and cryptocurrencies suggests investors are prioritizing liquidity over risk-taking. Without renewed capital inflows, even sharp relief rallies may struggle to develop into sustained recoveries.

In a recent development, looking ahead, investors should watch whether Bitcoin can reclaim $60,000, Ethereum can recover above $1,800, and Solana can establish support above $70. Improving ETF flows, stabilizing prices, and a recovery in broader financial markets would provide more convincing evidence that conditions are improving. Until then, the combination of weakening demand and unfavorable macroeconomic conditions leaves cryptocurrencies vulnerable to further declines heading into year-end.
