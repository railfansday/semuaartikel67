Well, it's finally here, and for many, I imagine that it all feels quite surreal.

GTA 6 is out now here in the UK, which is why this article's being published at midnight GMT.

Those of you in North America and other territories that are hours behind obviously have a little longer to wait — regional release timings be damned — but I'd still love to hear your initial thoughts on the game once you've had a chance to sit down and play it.

And that's really the point of this article — it's a place where the Push Square community can come together and discuss what is the biggest video game release in... well, probably forever.

Such an occasion deserves its own discussion; we're basically living through a cultural phenomenon right now, and even if you don't strategy on buying or playing GTA 6, it's still almost impossible to avoid all of the hype — and it'll be even harder to ignore the game's lasting influence over the years ahead.

At this juncture it's worth noting that GTA 6 is officially the highest rated video game of all time, which feels like a good talking point in itself. Over 100 reviews across PS5 and Xbox and the title currently sits at a staggering 99 (ninety-nine!) score on Metacritic. Utterly unprecedented and bordering on absurd.

Recent findings suggest that our esteemed editor Sammy is one of many reviewers who slapped a perfect 10/10 on the game. You can read all of his thoughts in our GTA 6 PS5 review.

I'm not asking you to give it a review score of your own just yet — that poll will likely arrive towards the end of next week — but it's possible that these insane levels of praise will have pushed you into buying the game at launch.

And if you are dipping straight in, I should really let you know that we've got a bunch of guides ready to go.

According to recent reports, as noted in Sammy's above review, GTA 6 is just stuffed with... er, stuff, to a truly obsessive degree — and you'll need to cross off a huge amount of that stuff if you want to nab the game's all-vital Platinum Trophy.

So be sure to take a look at our GTA 6 guide if you need some assistance — it's got spoiler-free breakdowns of the game's seemingly innumerable collectibles.

We've also got a couple of spoiler-free beginners guides. Tips & Tricks is where you'll find advice on how to get the most out of your gameplay, while our Ultimate Edition Guide breaks down everything you need to know about actually accessing the additional content you get with the more expensive version of the game.

Now then, that's enough nattering from me — it's your turn.

I've included a handful of polls below in the hope of getting the ball rolling — so be sure to cast your votes, and when you're done, feel free to share any and all of your GTA 6 opinions in the comments section below.
