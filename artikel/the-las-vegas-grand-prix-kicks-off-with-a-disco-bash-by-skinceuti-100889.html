📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Outside, the city of Las Vegas is readying its streets for this weekend’s Formula One Grand Prix, when the world’s fastest drivers in the world’s fastest cars will tear down the Strip at 200-plus miles per hour. But inside Sin City’s hot spots, the starry F1 events are just getting started—and everyone at the SkinCeuticals Las Vegas Grand Prix Party quite literally had their (glowing) game faces on.

On Wednesday night, SkinCeuticals, as the official skincare partner of Scuderia Ferrari HP, hosted an exclusive bash at Baby Disco, the intimate, moody lounge tucked away within Zero Bond at The Wynn Las Vegas.

According to recent reports, with the space aglow in Ferrari Rosso Scuderia red under a shiny mirrored disco ball and a checkered-flag DJ booth, guests snacked on wagyu bites and gourmet caviar-topped chicken nuggets and sipped on-theme cocktails. (Also served: SkinCeuticals’s top-shelf favorites, including the P-TIOX and C E Ferulic serums, passed around on silver trays.) The model Kelly Piquet, partner of F1 star Max Verstappen, posted up on the green velvet banquette, as other attendees played around with SkinCeuticals-branded poker chips and cards around the room.

On the venue’s outdoor terrace overlooking the glowing marvel that is Sphere, actor Chace Crawford and his dad, dermatologist Chris Wayne Crawford, took a moment to enjoy the view. The two were doing a little father-son bonding—a perfect setting since F1 is “a male, testosterone-fueled sport,” joked Chris. As a Texas native, Chace (who also came through GQ’s Men of the Year 2026 party in LA last week) has seen the F1 Grand Prix in Austin, but he’s excited to catch his first race under the megawatt Vegas lights. “It’s surreal out here,” noted the The Boys star, looking out over the Strip.

It has been revealed that inside, sportscaster and former Seattle Seahawk superstar Russell Wilson, a big F1 fan, couldn’t wait for lights-out-and-away-we-go on Sunday. He’s attended F1 races in Monaco and Montreal, but he really digs the Vegas experience. “I’m racing around the track tomorrow night,” he said, “so I’m fired up about it.”.

It has been revealed that at 9 p.m. the party kicked into high gear as Alex Pall and Drew Taggart of the Chainsmokers took to the DJ booth, easing into their set, kicking off a remix of thesynth-pop classic “Don't You Want Me” then incrementally dialing up the heat until Taggart took to the mic himself to perform “Paris,” the DJ duo’s 2017 hit. When the musician Sooin, of the K-pop group Meovv, hopped into the booth to chop it up with the Chainsmokers, the room lit up.

It has been revealed that they closed out their set with some on-theme bottle service, as the Baby Disco crew made their way through the crowd hoisting magnums of Champagne and a strobe-flashing disco ball shaped like SkinCeuticals’s iconic serum dropper bottle. Time for the cooldown lap.

“I just want to say, everyone has really nice skin in here,” Taggart told the room with a grin. “Shout out to SkinCeuticals.”.
