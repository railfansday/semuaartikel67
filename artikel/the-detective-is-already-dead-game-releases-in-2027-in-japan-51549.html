Mystery adventure game The Detective Is Already Dead will launch for Switch in 2027 in Japan, TAITO revealed.

Here is an overview of the game, via TAITO:.

The Detective Is Already Dead is an adventure game starring Kimihiko Kimizuka, featuring a completely original story supervised by author Nigoju. Enjoy multiple endings that branch based on your relationship with each heroine, including Siesta.

In a recent development, when he comes to, Kimizuka finds himself living a peaceful everyday life with everyone. Natsunagi, Saikawa, Charlotte, and even Siesta, who should have been dead, are all there with him.

According to recent reports, but then, an unsettling feeling falls upon him. Could this world be nothing more than an illusion?

As he unravels the mysteries, his everyday life begins to change. Adventures in another world, resort wars on a southern island, and even a career as an idol in the entertainment industry!?

The ending changes depending on the choices you make.

And so begins an adventure in an unfamiliar world.
