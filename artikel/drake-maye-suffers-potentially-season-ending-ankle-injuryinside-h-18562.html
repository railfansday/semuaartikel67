Newly introduced England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye could miss the rest of the 2026 season after injuring his ankle against the Detroit Lions in Munich.

Sources indicate that according to reports, the athlete may have fractured his ankle. Maye was hurt on the Patriots’ first possession of the Week 10 matchup when Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson sacked him on third-and-goal.

The third-year quarterback was evaluated on the sideline before walking to the locker room. Latest England later ruled him out with an ankle injury. Tommy DeVito is the Patriots' backup quarterback.

It has been revealed that patriots coach Mike Vrabel offered few details after the game. On Monday morning, he told WEEI’s “The Greg Hill Show” that Maye had stayed in Germany as a precaution and was expected to return to the U.S. soon.

“The revision that I have is just out of caution, Drake stayed back,” Vrabel noted, as quoted by the Boston Herald’s Doug Kyed. “He’ll be traveling back here shortly. But just wanted to make sure that everything was good for him to travel back and safely. The things that go around injuries, and just wanted to make sure everything was in line for him to travel, and it seems like he is. He’ll be returning here, I think, shortly.”.
