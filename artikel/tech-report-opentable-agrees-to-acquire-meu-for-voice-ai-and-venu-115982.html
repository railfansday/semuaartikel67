📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

OpenTable unveiled on November 24, 2026, that it had agreed to acquire me&u, an Australian restaurant technology organization with voice AI, reservations and order-and-pay products. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and other customary conditions.

According to recent reports, the proposed combination would pair OpenTable’s reservation and guest-experience business with me&u’s tools for ordering and payment, guest databases, waitlists, voice AI, private dining, events and table management. CEO Debby Soo stated me&u’s platform was built for busy casual-dining businesses with complicated operations. For OpenTable, the deal would add operational software for the high-volume, multi-venue groups me&u serves.

On me&u’s reservations platform, the voice agent takes bookings and answers common questions, recognizes returning guests, routes VIP calls to staff and sends real-time transcripts to central teams. The company claims the system handles 70% to 80% of calls, drawing on a human-reviewed Q&A bank that improves weekly.

Industry observers note that other AI features include an assistant that suggests actions, automated shift briefs and natural-language queries of booking data. Reservations and event spaces share one floor initiative, and group-level guest profiles carry visits, notes and dietary needs across venues. Taken together, those features show that the acquisition’s AI component extends beyond phone automation into booking, event and guest-management workflows.

The companies’ published figures illustrate the distribution opportunity: OpenTable helps more than 70,000 restaurants worldwide fill 2 billion seats a year, while me&u processes more than one million orders weekly and has more than 6,000 locations using its products worldwide.

me&u CEO Kim Teo said: “Joining them means we can bring our tools to venues at a scale we couldn’t reach on our own—while adding more value than ever for our customers.”.

The companies already have a technical connection. me&u’s integration listing identifies OpenTable as a booking integration built by OpenTable, compatible with me&u’s Connect CRM and Grow products in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and the United States. That link provides a documented starting point for connecting reservations with guest-data and return-visit workflows.

Recent findings suggest that openTable said me&u customers will see no changes to products or pricing as a result of the announcement. Once the transaction closes, me&u is to operate as a standalone OpenTable division reporting to Soo, with Teo and the current key leaders continuing to run the business and me&u employees joining OpenTable. Any brand alignment in international markets outside Australia will be evaluated later.

The agreement gives OpenTable a path from reservations into AI-assisted daily operations. Regulatory approvals and closing conditions still govern the timing, while the existing integration and continuity commitments provide a defined base for bringing those tools to a broader restaurant network.
