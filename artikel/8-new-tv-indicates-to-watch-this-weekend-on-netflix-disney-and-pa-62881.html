8 New TV Indicates to Watch This Weekend on Netflix, Disney, and Paramount (November 20-22)

  • And while those releases are still some time away, there’s plenty to tide you over this weekend, including fresh episodes of The Rings of Power, which we’ve discussed over the past week, and new TV highlights premiering or returning.
  • In a recent development, percy Jackson and the Olympians is back on Disney Plus.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Happy weekend! Genre fans have plenty to look forward to when it comes to TV. Earlier this week, Netflix revealed the return of 3 Body Project, the heady sci-fi series based on the Liu Cixin novels and adapted by the Game of Thrones showrunners. No big deal. Other series you can look forward to soon include Alien: Earth season 2 and Neuromancer. This month, Blade Runner 2099 is premiering on Prime Video.

And while those releases are still some time away, there’s plenty to tide you over this weekend, including fresh episodes of The Rings of Power, which we’ve discussed over the past week, and new TV highlights premiering or returning. Yellowjackets is back for a fourth and final season. The series picks up as the girls, who’ve been stranded in the wilderness for 19 months, are finally rescued. Meanwhile, in the adult timeline, their lives are falling apart.

In a recent development, percy Jackson and the Olympians is back on Disney Plus. This season follows Percy (Walker Scobell) and Grover (Aryan Simhadri) as they take on their most personal mission yet: rescuing Annabeth (Leah Jeffries) from Kronos.

Other highlights include the fresh Netflix series The Altruists, a biographical drama following the rise and fall of FTX, a cryptocurrency exchange company founded in 2019 that reached a $32 billion valuation. By 2022, the company collapsed after allegations of misusing billions of dollars.

Recent findings suggest that there’s also Dig, the latest TV show from Mike Schur and Amy Poehler. The series marks her first show since Parks and Recreation, and while she’s the lead, she’s also the co-creator and one of the writers. The cast is rounded out by Hugh Laurie and Geraldine Viswanathan, with appearances from Max Greenfield, Jason Mantzoukas, and others.

It has been revealed that we also have a couple of international recommendations: Gordon is an Argentine TV show based on the life and crimes of Anibal Gordon, one of the country's most notorious criminals during the ‘70s and ‘80s. There’s also The Blue Road, a South Korean series set during the 2002 World Cup, where investigators are looking into a string of connected murders in South Korea and Japan. Happy watching.

Yellowjackets is back for one last season. The series, following a team of female soccer players who get stranded in the wilderness after a plane crash, is set across different timelines, following the aftermath of the crash and the lives of the same women 25 years later. The final season finds the survivors finally returning home.

Sources indicate that shauna’s (Melanie Lynskey) life is upended. After reuniting with her ex Melissa (Hilary Swank) and almost killing her, Shauna’s husband Jeff (Warren Kole) and their daughter Callie (Sarah Desjardins) have decided to leave her. She’s going through a midlife crisis, buying a massive pickup truck on the family’s credit card and torturing Jeff in any way she can. Her romantic tensions take up a lot of her narrative, for better or worse. Lynskey is saddled with a lot of Shauna’s shenanigans, which she makes work for the most part. Whether she’s singing karaoke off-tune or stumbling confused into an orgy, Lynskey is hilarious and wounded, and remains a highlight of the series.

It has been revealed that percy Jackson and the Olympians is back with season 3. Based on the beloved Rick Riordan books, the third season adapts the franchise’s third novel, The Titan’s Curse, marking the first time that book has been adapted to the screen.

Based on the real story that was extensively reported on by Upcoming York Magazine, The Altruists follows the relationship between Sam Bankman-Fried (Anthony Boyle) and Caroline Ellison (Julia Garner), two ambitious trading partners who come in and out of their lives while working on FTX, a cryptocurrency exchange company that closed in 2022 after becoming the subject of a cryptocurrency scandal.

Industry observers note that marking Amy Poehler’s comeback to television and her latest collaboration with Mike Schur, Dig follows four women working on an archaeological dig site in Greece. When they find a precious artifact, the women get pulled into an international conspiracy.

Dig doesn't have the deep bench of supporting characters that made Parks and Rec so great, but there are a few decent recurring faces. Kayvan Novak (Nandor in What We Do in the Shadows) is charming as the very Greek mayor of the small town where the show takes place, who has a devastating crush on Elise. Meanwhile, Dropout's Brennan Lee Mulligan has a hilarious guest role as the bumbling fail-son of the rich old man who's been bankrolling the entire dig site. Mulligan's episode in particular, in which the archeologists have to placate an idiotic millionaire in the hopes of getting more funding for their work, feels like a vision of what the show could have been in another timeline where it wasn't bogged down by its serialized drama.

If you’re looking for a foreign crime drama, Gordon is an Argentine crime series following the real-life crimes of Anibal Gordon. The man was one of the most prominent criminals in Argentina during the ‘70s and ‘80s, involved in petty crimes and also in the Dirty War, where members who opposed the government were targeted.

South Korea's latest crime thriller is set against the backdrop of the 2002 FIFA World Cup and follows a string of murders that took place in South Korea and Japan, with the crime scenes having messages written in both languages and uncovering a larger conspiracy.

Industry observers note that ken Burns’ latest documentary explores the criminal justice system in America, diving deep into 250 years of policing, prisons, and more. Apart from being deeply researched, the documentary features voice work from some of the industry’s best actors, including Samuel L. Jackson, Laura Linney, Matthew Rhys, and more.

In a recent development, lastly, if you’re looking for something a little more relaxed, The Circle is back. The reality series, which initially aired on Netflix, has moved to Hulu. After a two-year break, the series is back with the original show’s premise, with contestants participating in a game where they’re all apart, fostering connections through social media, with the ability to catfish others or to play the game as themselves.
