Liverpool have held initial talks with Alexis Mac Allister’s representatives over a potential new deal at Anfield, according to a report from Ben Jacobs.

Jacobs suggested on Wednesday that newly introduced Liverpool sporting director Julian Ward has spoken with both Mac Allister’s agent and father a possible contract extension.

Watching Andoni Iraola get a newly introduced era underway at Anfield following his arrival over the summer, the Argentinian international has been one of the key performances in the red half of Merseyside this season.

Heading into November’s international break with a maiden Premier League strike of the season during Liverpool’s 3-1 victory away at Crystal Palace, Mac Allister has become a key figure in Iraola’s side.

However, with his current deal set to expire in 2028, the former Brighton star has made his frustrations clear in regards to finding a solution for his long-term future.

With the Anfield hierarchy previously in no rush to tie the 27-year-old down to a newly introduced deal, they passed on the opportunity to open official discussions during the summer.

Nevertheless, with the former Premier League champions making it clear that they door was never shut on Mac Allister signing a renewal, Jacobs reported on Wednesday that Liverpool have finally held initials talks with the 59-cap international.

🚨 BREAKING: Newly introduced Liverpool sporting director Julian Ward has had some initial contact with Alexis Mac Allister’s father and agent over a new deal.

Discussions are exploratory talks at this point.

Mac Allister has started the season extremely well and has proven an important player for Andoni Iraola.

Mac Allister’s contract runs to 2028. Talks are centred around whether a renewal is viable for both parties.

#LFC in no rush, and were not inclined to make a formal offer over the summer.

The door was never totally shut to a Mac Allister renewal as Liverpool had never ended dialogue, but now conversations are more framed around specifics.
