Atlas Ocean Voyages is asking guests for their opinions as it explores color concepts for its latest sailing yacht, the Atlas Adventurer.

In a social media update, the tech giant said that four liveries are at the moment being considered for the hull: wine red, a steel blue, a graphite gray or an emerald green, inviting guests to vote on their favorite options by commenting on posts on its Instagram and Facebook profile.

Set to enter service in late 2028 as the fourth ship in Atlas Ocean Voyages’ fleet, the 400-guest Atlas Adventurer is at the moment under construction at the China Merchants shipyard in Haimen, China.

In addition to being the brand’s largest, the 26,000-ton ship will be the first sailing vessel in Atlas Ocean’s fleet, which now comprises three expedition vessels.

Earlier this year, the tech giant built a miniature of the ship to study hydrodynamics and real-world performance, conducting trials at MARIN (Maritime Research Institute Netherlands) in Wageningen, the Netherlands.

The design is also highlighted by its green features, which include a 9-megawatt battery system that will enable near-silent operations when sailing under wind power.

With an ice-class rated hull, the Adventurer will offer both traditional and expedition itineraries, expanding Atlas Ocean’s deployment to fresh areas.

For its maiden season, the ship is scheduled to offer 15 departures across Asia, Africa and the Mediterranean, including latest-to-the-manufacturer areas, such as Southeast Asia and Eastern Africa.
