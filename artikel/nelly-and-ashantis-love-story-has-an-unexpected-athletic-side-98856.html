Recent findings suggest that husband-and-wife duo Nelly and Ashanti aren’t just hip-hop royalty—they’re reality TV stars too. The second season of their show Nelly & Ashanti: We Belong Together is streaming now on Peacock, and follows the couple as they juggle married life, hectic careers, and raising their toddler son Kareem Kenkaide (aka “KK”). Through all of their highs and lows, they have each other’s back—and display insane chemistry while doing so.

Sources indicate that women’s Health recently got a glimpse of their playful dynamic when the couple stopped by for a round of “In Fitness and In Health,” the game that puts their knowledge of each other to the test. Given that Nelly and Ashanti first met all the way back in the early 2000s (they dated for 10 years before breaking up in 2013, only to rekindle things in 2023), they pretty much passed with flying colors.

Yes, Nelly knows his bride well, even if he’s still learning the correct definition of “love language.”.

“Your love language...English?” he guesses, making Ashanti laugh before changing his answer: “Birkin?”.

“I'll take Birkin!” Ashanti indicates with a grin. “Thank you! One coming on the way.”.

Sources indicate that when the question turns to Nelly’s secret talent, Ashanti has an answer at the ready: “I don't know if it's too much of a secret, but you are a really good athlete,” she notes. “You play baseball very well; football very well. And you hoop.”.

“That’s right,” Nelly claims proudly.

According to recent reports, and he’s not the only athlete in the family. When Nelly is asked which Olympic sport Ashanti would partake in, he confidently answers “track.” And he’s right on the money.

“I was actually scouted by the Olympics when I was in high school,” Ashanti states. “I used to do the triple jump, and I made the Wall of Fame at my school.”.

Maybe the couple’s next show should be titled Karaoke Relay, and instead of passing a baton, they pass the mic! (You can have that idea for free, guys!) Either way, it’s clear these two dynamos really do belong together.
