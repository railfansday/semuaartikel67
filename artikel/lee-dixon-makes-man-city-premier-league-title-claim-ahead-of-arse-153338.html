Lee Dixon thinks Manchester City’s Premier League title chances are far from over even if they fall to defeat against Arsenal on Sunday.

City started the season in fine form after Enzo Maresca’s arrival, but have now slipped to third and are without a win in their last four league matches.

Last season’s runners-up spent lavishly over the summer, but now trail Arsenal by six points ahead of their pivotal meeting at the Emirates on Sunday.

Bubbling away in the background remains the possibility of a huge points deduction after the club were found guilty of all but one of the 115 charges for financial breaches.

And Dixon believes that such uncertainty might be playing on the minds of City’s players, telling Metro: ‘Nobody liked Arsenal under George Graham and we built up resilience because of that.

‘I think that could have been happening for City at the start of the season with the charges, but that bounce can die off a bit and then all of a sudden, you’ve had a couple of bad results and it gets into your head.’.

Arsenal could extend their lead over City to nine points with victory at the Emirates on Sunday, but Dixon believes that it would still be far too early to rule City out of this season’s title race.

‘As a fan, after seeing Arsenal get over the line last season, you now have something to fall back on and I do believe that, from a fan’s perspective, if you see Arsenal beat City on Sunday, you can think that’s it,’ he added.

‘However, as a player, you won’t see any of them coming out and going, “It’s done and dusted”, because City have so much quality. We all know that City could go on a ten-game winning streak, so they’ll always have that opportunity.

‘At the moment, there’s less pressure for Arsenal at the top from a points perspective and it’s pointing to it being their title to lose, but right now, it’s too early in the season to say it’s over if Arsenal win on Sunday.’.

Arsenal have not been at their best since returning from the international break, with a draw against Newcastle last weekend followed up by a stalemate against Borussia Dortmund in midweek.

But Dixon has still been encouraged by some aspects of Arsenal’s football, most notably the performances of Noni Madueke, who has featured more prominently since Bukayo Saka sustained a thigh injury last month.

‘I love his directness because when he comes on, his first touch is generally in the direction of the full-back which is what I hated as a full-back myself,’ Dixon stated of Madueke.

‘You know what you’re in for as a full-back coming up against somebody like him. I’ve been very impressed with him but I’ve always been worried by his end product, but he seems to be producing better numbers now.

It has been revealed that ‘You need to take your hat off to him for that because Saka’s boots are major boots to fill. He’s really had to knuckle down and he’s receiving the benefit of that.’.

Lee Dixon was speaking with Metro on behalf of Betfred.
