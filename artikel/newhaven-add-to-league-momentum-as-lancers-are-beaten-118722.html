In a recent development, the Dockers looked the more confident team from the outset, taking the game to their visitors from the first whistle. Their positive start was rewarded when, with just six minutes on the clock, they took the lead.

The Lancing defence failed to deal with a Ross Barclay long throw, Billy Oliver held up the ball before laying it into the path of Lee Robinson, and Newhaven’s goalscorer extraordinaire did the rest; lashing the ball into the net with his left foot.

According to recent reports, lancing, though, responded swiftly to the early setback and were level just five minutes later. A decent move culminated with Jay Popham finding space in the area, and he finished into the bottom corner to restore parity.

Sources indicate that the early goalscoring wasn’t finished there. Before 20 minutes had been played, the Dockers were back in front. Matt Gunn rising highest in a crowed penalty to area to nod the ball into the net from inside the six-yard box.

According to recent reports, newhaven should have reached the interval further ahead. Tyler Capon headed an Ezra Roeg cross over after a flowing move down the left flank, before Capon again went close from a teasing Ash Wadhams cross. Seconds later, Robinson appeared to be bundled over in the area, only for the ref to write the challenge off as a coming together.

Robinson then had a chance to add to the scoreline, denied only by a last-ditch tackle just as he was about to pull the trigger.

The second half played out in a similar vein to the first, with Newhaven very much controlling proceedings.

Industry observers note that chances continued to come and go for the home side. Robinson sent a header wide after fine link-up play between him and Wadhams, before the striker was denied by a decent save from the Lancing keeper.

With 25 minutes to play, the game should have been dead and buried. As it was, the Dockers entered the last quarter somewhat fortunate to still be ahead.

Antoine Sharpe beat Ethan Pike to a long ball forward, but with the goal gaping he could only strike against the post from a tight angle.

Sources indicate that it was a let-off for Newhaven, but one they soon capitalised on. With 14 minutes remaining, a moment of magic from Oliver wrapped up a much-deserved three-points for the Dockers.

Receiving the ball just outside the area, Oliver wriggled his way past numerous Lancing players, before keeping his composure and firing the ball into the roof of the net. A fine strike to cap another fine performance for the Dockers.

That victory makes if four wins in their last five games for Newhaven, and they will look to make it five wins from six when they visit Wick this weekend.
