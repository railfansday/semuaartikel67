According to recent reports, it depends on the fabric, but in most cases, heat is your friend. Washing sweatpants in hot water followed by a high-heat dry will help shrink your selected pair. Just proceed with thought—this method is effective, but not reversible!

It has been revealed that stretching depends largely on the material, and many pairs will naturally relax with wear. For a faster fix, lightly dampen the fabric and gently stretch it by hand into your desired silhouette, then let it air-dry. You may need to repeat the process a few times, but patience will pay off.
