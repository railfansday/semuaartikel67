Pochettino's Own Kane: Ivan Toney Keen to Join Man Utd if He Leaves Al-Ahli

Manchester United chiefs have conceded that they must back new manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window given how their season is currently panning out.

Hopes were high that Man United were going to push on following the permanent appointment of Michael Carrick in the summer, after a stunning run of form under the 45-year-old's leadership in the first half of 2026 saw them secure a third-place finish in the Premier League, and a return to Champions League football as a result.

It would be no exaggeration to say the 2026-27 campaign to date has been a disaster though, with Pochettino replacing Carrick in the Old Trafford dugout following the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa prior to the international break.

United went into Sunday's clash against Liverpool in 19th position in the Premier League table, having won just one of their top flight fixtures this term, at home to newly-promoted Ipswich Town.

Fans would have been hoping for a fresh manager bounce under the Argentine, but the chances of that happening were ultimately extinguished following Leny Yoro's eighth minute red card, which has since been overturned.

That decision doesn't help United though, given they were beaten 4-0 on the day, which has left them in dire straits in terms of their Premier League position.

The Red Devils have won two of their four matches in the Champions League though, and they have the opportunity to give themselves a confidence booster ahead of a big league game against Brentford at Old Trafford this weekend when they travel to face Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday night.

Pochettino has another five weeks working with this squad before he has the chance to add new faces to the squad, with the Times reporting that, despite their defensive issues this term, a striker will be the main priority.

Monaco striker Folarin Balogun has been linked, while TEAMtalk claim that intermediaries have been in touch with United over the potential availability of Al-Ahli striker Ivan Toney.

The 30-year-old has been in outstanding goalscoring form in Saudi Arabia after making the move from Brentford, but is stated to be open to a return to European football in 2027.

The report adds that a switch to Old Trafford would strongly appeal to the England international.

We all know that Harry Kane was arguably at his best in a Tottenham shirt when Pochettino was manager there, and the Ballon d'Or winner cannot speak highly enough of the Argentine following their time together.

Kane has just signed a latest contract at Bayern Munich and is currently injured, and there is no chance, in the short-term at least, that he will be returning to the Premier League.

In the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko, Pochettino would love to find a striker as close to Kane as possible, but that is never going to be easy in a January window.

But if the 54-year-old head coach wants a talismanic centre-forward with Premier League experience and a prolific goal record, there won't be too many better options than Toney.

The 30-year-old played a starring role for Brentford in the English top flight before making the switch to Saudi Arabia, where he has produced some ridiculous goal numbers.

His displays led to him being described as 'one of the best strikers in the world' by former Al-Ahli sporting director Rui Pedro, which reveals how highly he is rated, and the quality he has in front of goal.

If the deal is right in January and Toney is ready to join, he could be a no-brainer addition for a club looking to turn their fortunes around.
