Hila Klein has announced that she is stepping away from the H3 podcast amid her divorce from Ethan Klein, as he has been teasing a comeback.

Back on October 22, Ethan Klein confirmed that the H3 Podcast was going on an “indefinite hiatus,” before his wife, Hila Klein, revealed that he had been having an affair for over six months. Hila confirmed that she had filed for divorce from Ethan, adding: “I am living in a nightmare I can’t wake up from.”.

Ethan also revealed that he would be giving up any stake in the H3 podcast, as well as their other ventures, suggesting that the podcast crew would be making a return.

Now, Hila has announced that she has decided to stop working on any newly introduced show, saying she is “devastated” to be stepping away.

It has been revealed that “I’m so sorry to share that I’ve made the difficult decision to stop working on the new show. I truly tried with every ounce of strength I had to make this work, but after everything that has happened, I’ve come to realize that I can’t continue down this path while also giving myself the space I need to heal and protect my mental health,” she stated on November 23.

Hila added that she needs to “focus on myself, my healing, and what’s best for me and my kids,” before noting that she is “devastated” about ending the show.

“Walking away from something that has been such a huge part of my life is incredibly painful,” she continued. “I will miss you all more than I can put into words. Thank you for all the love and support over the years.”.
