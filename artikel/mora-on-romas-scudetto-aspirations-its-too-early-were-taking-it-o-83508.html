There was also joy for Rodrigo Mora, who scored his fourth consecutive goal in Serie A, his fifth in the league. After the match, the Giallorossi’s number 86 spoke to DAZN.

“From day one I knew what Malen would be capable of and what he represented, I’m very happy for him and he deserves it.”.

Industry observers note that “Yes, it’s an important moment for me too. But I’m happy for the team and for the three points.”.
