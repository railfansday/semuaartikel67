According to recent reports, this holiday season, we’re taking our decorating inspiration off the page—and into a Greenwich Village townhouse. ELLE Decor worked with designer Josh Greene to curate and design a festive space for Walmart’s Holiday Wonderland, a free, two-day event inside a restored 1850s townhouse in Greenwich Village. Find us at 16 West 10th Street on Friday, November 20, from noon to 6 p.m. ET and Saturday, November 21, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET. You can find more information on the Walmart’s Holiday Wonderland event page.

Inside the ELLE Decor Living Room, a festive fireplace sets the scene for complimentary digital holiday portraits, sent straight to your phone. Santa will be there Friday from 2 to 6 p.m. and Saturday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. And if something in the room catches your eye, you can shop it right then and there by scanning a QR code.
