Google Wallet just turned your Android phone into a digital house key – how it works

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, google is adding digital home keys to Google Wallet, letting consumers tap their phones to unlock compatible smart locks. The home key functionality, which will integrate with Google Home, will roll out this month.

Also: This tiny box gives your older tech smart home superpowers.

Apple introduced Home Keys in 2021 with iOS 15, allowing users to store digital keys in the Apple Wallet. This lets you tap your iPhone or Apple Watch on the lock to unlock it. More recently, the addition of Express Mode to Apple Home lets users unlock smart locks without first unlocking their iPhones with Face ID.

Sources indicate that google’s digital keys use tap-to-unlock via NFC on smart locks. Like the Apple Home Key, you don’t need to unlock your phone to use your digital key, though you can change your verification settings as you wish.

In a recent development, google’s announcement confirms that the manufacturer is embracing Aliro and UWB unlocking technologies as well. UWB unlocking for smart locks is supported on Google Pixel 10 Pro and newer models, as well as select UWB-enabled Samsung Galaxy phones. Aliro is a common standard for compatible smart locks and devices.

Before Google’s announcement, compatible smart locks could be controlled on Android through their accompanying app and Google Home. Smart lock companies also offered their own digital access methods, like NFC cards, Bluetooth, and geolocation.

Recent findings suggest that one digital key will be created for each Google Home, so it can unlock all compatible smart locks in that home. You won’t need to search through digital keys in Google Wallet to find the one for your front door, back door, or bedroom door — just tap your phone and Google Home will recognize the lock as one in your smart home ecosystem.

At launch, Google Wallet will support digital keys for the Aqara U400 with UWB and NFC, and NFC on the Schlage Sense Pro and Nuki Keypad 2.

Google Wallet already supports digital car keys for compatible vehicles.
