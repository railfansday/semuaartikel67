Ayden Heaven has opened the door to a move away from Manchester United.

That is according to BBC Sport, who claim that the young defender could explore an Old Trafford exit over a lack of playing time.

It is believed that he would prefer a loan move, but he could also leave the club permanently, with Fulham and Barcelona credited with interest.

The 20-year-old has played a bit-part role this term for the Red Devils, often filling in at either centre-back - his preferred position - or left full-back amid an injury crisis in the defensive third.

He was an unused substitute for United’s 4-0 drubbing by Liverpool at Anfield last weekend, as new manager Mauricio Pochettino endured a nightmare start to his tenure in the north west.

United sit 19th in the Premier League table, second from bottom, and mired in the relegation zone after a run of 12 matches with just one victory.

This term, Heaven, who arrived at Old Trafford from Arsenal’s youth setup in February 2025, has played eight times in all competitions.

He replaced Harry Maguire in the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa before the international break, in what proved to be Michael Carrick’s final game in charge before he was sacked.

The young central defender was brought on at half time against Unai Emery’s Villans, with the score at 2-2 after Villa’s quickfire double inside the first four minutes thanks to goals from Alysson and Nicolas Jackson.

Heaven’s most recent start came in the 3-2 win over Roma in the Champions League, partnering Maguire before being substituted for Lisandro Martinez in the 75th minute, with United trailing 2-1.
