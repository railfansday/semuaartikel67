📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is out, players are discovering that Rockstar Games' latest feels more like Red Dead Redemption 2 than GTA 5. Rockstar Games builds on the work it did in its open-world western and applies it to the context of a modern city. That’s a major part of why it's so successful, but that might make some players nervous. While GTA 6 moves at a quicker pace, movement can still feel a little slower than you might expect​​​​​​​.

If you’re in that boat, don't worry: GTA 6 includes a few vital settings that you can toggle on to speed up the pace.

Recent findings suggest that first, you have to understand the nuances of movement in GTA 6. Jason and Lucia have three movement states: walk, jog, and run. By default, they’ll trot at a slow pace if you just push the left joystick forward. Press it in, and they’ll break into a light jog that doesn’t use any stamina. Press it again or hold the left stick, and they’ll go into a full run that burns stamina, but also counts as cardio. Trying to swap between a jog and a run can wind up feeling a little clumsy if you hate pressing a stick as a button.

You can change that by going into Settings, tabbing over to Gameplay, and scrolling down to Movement. Here, you’ll find three settings that will help control how fast you move. The most important one is Movement Speed. This changes what max speed you reach when moving the joystick. It’s set to walk by default, but you can change that to Fast Walk, Jog, or Run. Note that Run doesn’t change default to your fastest possible speed, but rather your top speed before you’ll start burning stamina. Don’t be afraid to experiment in that menu and find the default speed that feels most natural to you.

Industry observers note that you can also change your Combat Movement Speed separately, which determines how you’ll move in a shootout. By default, it’s set to Fast Walk, but you can similarly set it to Walk, Jog, and Run as well.

If you want to speed things along just a little more, you can also toggle Stand & Sprint on. When crouching in GTA 6, clicking the left stick will make you hustle forward a little faster in that crouched state. This option will instead snap you into a standing position out of a crouch when you run. So if stealth isn’t really your style, this will make sure you can get the jump on enemies faster.

Industry observers note that there are plenty more handy options to check out in GTA 6’s Settings menu, including a host of accessibility features that will let you change minigame controls, button holding prompts, and more. Make sure to poke around and fine tune the game to your liking if anything feels off.
