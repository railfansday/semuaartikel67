It has been revealed that as I was driving around in Grand Theft Auto 6, I heard a voice on the radio. It sounded very familiar. And silly. Even a bit…weird, you might say. It sounded a lot like Weird Al. And after confirming it with Rockstar, I was delighted to learn that the comic musician and parody master is indeed a radio DJ in GTA 6.Â.

As you cruise around Vice City and the greater state of Leonida, you can tune into the radio station Vice City FM. This is a station that plays hit songs from the 1980s. Yes, this is the station all you aging gamers, myself included, will be jamming out to as you recapture those memories of Vice City on PS2. Anyway, one of the two hosts on this station is none other than Mr. Weird Al Yankovic himself! Take a listen via the clip below:.

Weird Al’s wild persona is dialed up quite a bit in GTA 6, with him sometimes screaming about a song or a past life experience. My wife, who is a major Weird Al fan, has said she’s more interested in playing GTA 6 knowing he’s in the game.

Earlier this week, Weird Al hinted at playing some big game soon, which now seems like he was teasing his appearance in GTA 6.Â.

Recent findings suggest that sadly, what’s not in the game, as far as I can tell, are any of his original songs or parodies. I’m very, very sad that “Dare to Be Stupid” isn’t on the in-game radio. PC players: Once GTA 6 is ported to that platform, we need to fix that and add some Weird Al to the station.

In a recent development, he also isn’t in the game physically, so you can’t kill Weird Al. And if that was something you wanted to do, well, you should go take a moment and think about how terrible you are for even pondering such an idea. Weird Al deserves to live a long and happy life. And I hope he got a nice paycheck for this surprising role in GTA 6.Â.
