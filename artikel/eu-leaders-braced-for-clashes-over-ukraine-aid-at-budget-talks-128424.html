Europe live – latest updates.

EU leaders are bracing for a clash over long-term financial aid for Ukraine amid an effort to finalise high-stakes budget talks by the end of the year.

According to recent reports, arriving in Brussels on Thursday, the German chancellor, Friedrich Merz, reiterated his view that all EU member states should support a €45bn (£38bn) loan for Ukraine for 2027. “When the European Union provides funding, it does so as the European Union – and that union consists of 27 member states, not 23 or 24,” he said.
