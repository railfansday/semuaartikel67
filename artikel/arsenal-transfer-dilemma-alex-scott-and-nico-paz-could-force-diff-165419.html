Potential signings of Alex Scott and Nico Paz may pose a challenge for Arteta.

While both players would add quality to the squad, their arrival would be a consideration for current players in the Emirates squad.

Scott would add quality in midfield, and Paz adds creativity and versatility going forward. Arteta must consider that adding these players may hinder the development of current players.

Arsenal are believed to be keen on bringing Alex Scott to the club.

After rejecting contract offers from Bournemouth, Scott could only have one year left on his contract at the club next summer. However, he is understood to be valued by Bournemouth at over £100m.

It has been revealed that he would be a great addition for Arteta, with the versatility and skill he has. He could give added competition in midfield.

Yet his potential arrival raises questions about Myles Lewis-Skelly’s future role.

According to recent reports, myles Lewis-Skelly was included in the Champions League final line-up last season. He showed that he is trusted by the club.He has also demonstrated his commitment by remaining at the club despite opportunities to gain regular playing time at other clubs.

In a recent development, signing another expensive midfielder may present difficulties. It is unclear at this point whether Scott will be a useful addition to the squad or simply overcrowd this area of the team.

Nico Paz presents another interesting option for Arsenal.

According to recent reports, paz joined Como from Real Madrid in the summer. He has caught the eye of a number of massive clubs and has increased his appeal with strong performances in international football. The 22-year-old has scored 5 times in his last 5 games for Argentina.

Paz is a versatile right-sided player and could be an appealing addition to Arteta’s strike force.

There are already plenty of players fighting for attacking positions at Arsenal.

Sources indicate that bukayo Saka is key in the team’s attack, and with Martin Ødegaard and Noni Madueke in the squad too, there are plenty of options. Max Dowman is a young player looking to break into the first team.

While Paz would add quality to the squad, Arsenal would need to find a specific role for him. There are complications with the move, though. Real Madrid have an option to re-sign him in 2027 for 80 million euros.

Although Arsenal are facing challenges in attracting quality players to the club. They now also face issues in managing a squad with experienced internationals and developing young players.

Although Scott and Paz would improve the depth of the squad, players must not be sacrificed for new players if they have already shown quality.

It has been revealed that players such as Lewis-Skelly and Dowman may be negatively affected by this. Opportunities may be taken away from them if the club continues to spend large amounts of money on latest players.

However, it is still very key for Arsenal to bring in quality players to compete for major titles.

Know how the additions will integrate within the playing style and how they affect the other players within the squad.

Industry observers note that the Gunners can strengthen further without overloading the squad. Good additions can be made to the first XI whilst also providing a realistic chance for young players to come through from within the academy.
