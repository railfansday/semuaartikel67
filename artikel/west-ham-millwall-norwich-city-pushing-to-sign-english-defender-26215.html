West Ham United, Millwall, and Norwich City will each have their sights firmly fixed on a place in the Championship top eight this season, and as the January window approaches, the trio are set to battle it out with Premier League clubs for the signature of a highly-rated defender.

Whilst neither West Ham, Millwall, nor Norwich occupy one of the top eight places in the Championship right now, this will not deter either of them from forcing their way into those places come May 2027.

It hasn't been a totally ideal start to the season for any of those clubs, and as the January window grows closer, attention will be swiftly turning to transfer business, where these three sides are set to go head-to-head.

According to TEAMtalk, West Ham, Millwall, and Norwich are keeping close tabs on Stevenage centre-back Jack Taylor, with the Englishman at the moment thriving out on loan with Exeter City in League Two.

However, the Championship trio face competition from Premier League sides such as Brighton and Hove Albion, Everton, Brentford, Crystal Palace, and Fulham, with Taylor's performances in the fourth tier not going unnoticed.

Taylor has never played above the fourth tier in his career, aged 21, and the jump to the Premier League or Championship at this stage would be an incredibly jarring one, but one he could be ready to make.

Taylor was on the youth books of Chelsea as a youngster, but decided to quit academy football and pursue his own route into the professional game, where he was eventually noticed by Sutton United whilst playing for his college.

A loan to the sixth tier in 2024/25 with Hampton and Richmond gave Taylor his first regular taste of first-team football, before breaking into the Sutton side after six months on loan.

His performances in the National League caught the attention of Stevenage, who signed Taylor in January 2026 before sending him out on loan to League Two Exeter City in the summer, where he has thrived in the fourth tier.

Yet to make a senior appearance for the Boro, Taylor is starring for the Grecians in Devon, as Stevenage may well have hit the jackpot on a player who may well never play a game for them if a January deal is agreed.

The Hammers lack depth and youth in the middle of their back line, with both Konstantinos Mavropanos and Max Kilman closing in on 30, and no real prospects coming through their academy.

Parker will be looking to make some additions in January, though whether Taylor will be one of them remains to be seen.
