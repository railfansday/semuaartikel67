iOS 27.3 launches next month and includes several exciting changes for iPhone Duo, here’s what’s new for Apple’s foldable model.

Apple is granting iPhone Duo owners one of their biggest feature requests: face unlock is coming with iOS 27.3.

Face unlock isn’t a full-fledged Face ID, and it doesn’t work for features like Apple Pay, but it is a convenient new option for unlocking your iPhone Duo just by looking at it. Per Apple:.

Sources indicate that use the front camera, along with a recent Touch ID or passcode authentication, to promptly unlock iPhone Duo when it’s closed. Face recognition data never leaves your device.

According to recent reports, if your iPhone Duo has gone unused for 45 minutes or more, face unlock won’t work and you’ll need to authenticate with Touch ID or your passcode. But for most users, that probably just means using Touch ID first thing in the morning, then relying on face unlock the rest of the day.

On a related note, you can also enable Attention Aware features in iOS 27.3. These are available whether you use the face unlock feature or not, but are limited to the outer display. Once enabled, they can automatically lower the volume of alarms and timers, keep the display from dimming, and more.

It has been revealed that reachability is available on other iPhone models to move onscreen content down vertically, closer to your thumb. But on iPhone Duo with iOS 27.3, Apple has a unique twist on the feature.

Instead of helping you reach content vertically, Reachability on iPhone Duo works horizontally.

If you’re using the outer screen and find that buttons or other UI elements are out of reach, you can swipe from the side to have onscreen content shift closer to your thumb.

You can always disable Reachability if you find yourself accidentally triggering it too often. But if one-handed iPhone Duo use gives you trouble, Reachability is a great latest option to have.

Read more: iOS 27.3 introduces upcoming iPhone Duo feature for one-handed use.

According to recent reports, what should happen when you close your iPhone Duo’s folding screen? Apple has found that owners have different preferences. Some want the iPhone to stay unlocked for continued use, while others prefer that it automatically locks when closed.

In iOS 27.3, Apple adds a latest setting so owners can enable whichever behavior they prefer.

Recent findings suggest that by default, iPhone Duo will work as it always has, staying unlocked after being closed. But inside Settings ⇾ Display & Brightness, iOS 27.3 provides the option of automatically locking upon being closed instead. You could achieve something similar before with Shortcuts, but now there’s a built-in setting too.

Industry observers note that it’s not exactly a “latest feature,” but iPhone Duo users will also find that the system keyboard has changed in iOS 27.3. Apple tweaked the outer display keyboard to push keys closer together and make one-handed use easier.
