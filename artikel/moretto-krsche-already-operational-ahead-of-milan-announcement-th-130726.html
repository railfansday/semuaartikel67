Matteo Moretto has confirmed that Markus Krösche’s arrival should be announced by AC Milan as early as next week.

Sources indicate that after their failed attempts to get Krösche out of Eintracht Frankfurt at the start of the summer, it seems as though Milan have finally got their man. Various sources have spoken of an agreement being in place after the director left the German club.

Recent findings suggest that la Gazzetta dello Sport claimed on Friday that Krösche’s appointment could actually be declared in the coming days. While waiting to see when his ‘official’ start date is, it is clear the former RB Leipzig executive will already be planning for the future.

Moretto spoke during a video on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel about the situation regarding Krösche’s arrival, and also whether he might be able to bring his right-hand man Timmo Hardung with him.

“There’s already a full agreement between all parties involved, and AC Milan are preparing the official announcement, which is expected before the end of November. This announcement will be made next week, according to what we’ve been told,” he said.

“I’ve been told that in recent days there have also been several meetings and contacts between Krosche and Cardinale to initiative the January transfer window. So, in reality, Krosche is already operational in some respects.

Recent findings suggest that “The official announcement is coming. We will have to understand between now and February what the future of Krosche’s right-hand man, Timmo Hardung, the current outgoing sporting director of Eintracht, will be. In short, he is Krosche’s right-hand man, who the director himself wants to bring to Milan.

Industry observers note that “He wants to bring people he trusts extremely into the Milan organisation and from what I’m told, Hardung’s name continues to be a strong one, perhaps more so from February onwards. Then, in short, we will understand the timing soon.”.
