I Tried Taco Bell’s New FLAMIN’ HOT Menu Items To See If They Really Bring The Heat

I'll try just about anything Taco Bell puts on its menu, and whatever I order usually ends up drenched in hot sauce. So when I heard the chain was adding a new FLAMIN' HOT offering (and bringing back a punchy fan favorite), I was curious whether they'd finally give me enough spice to leave those packets alone. I like my food hot enough to get a few beads of sweat forming on my nose, and with a name like FLAMIN' HOT, I don't think that's asking too much.

There were two contenders: the returning FLAMIN' HOT Grilled Cheese Burrito ($5.49), which I'd missed during its first run, and the brand-new FLAMIN' HOT Mini Taco Salad ($2.49). Both feature Fritos FLAMIN' HOT, so I figured I was in for some decent spice. Here's how that worked out.

In a recent development, I love a good taco salad, especially when the bowl itself is edible. But my first impression of this mini version was that it looked like a pretty ordinary Taco Bell salad. And after a few bites, I wasn't finding much to convince me otherwise.

In a recent development, you've got seasoned beef, refried beans, lettuce, tomatoes, cheddar, and Creamy Chipotle sauce tucked into a crispy flour tortilla bowl. Beneath everything was a layer of reddish rice, which Taco Bell claims is seasoned with FLAMIN' HOT Fritos. I could definitely see the color, but whatever was making that rice spicy wasn't doing much for my taste buds.

In a recent development, and speaking of Fritos, I'm still trying to figure out where mine went. They're supposed to be in there, but I genuinely couldn't tell whether they'd been included in my particular serving. I would've loved more of the chipotle sauce, too, because the other ingredients seemed to overpower whatever heat was hiding underneath.

The shell itself was fine, although a little bland and greasy. I’m still pro-taco salads, but I just wish the spice had been more noticeable—especially with a name that makes such a major promise.

The returning burrito is stuffed with seasoned beef, spicy rice, crunchy FLAMIN' HOT Fritos, nacho cheese sauce, sour cream, and Creamy Chipotle sauce. And because Taco Bell clearly believes there's no such thing as too much cheese, the entire thing gets a three-cheese blend grilled onto the outside of the tortilla.

I took a pretty substantial first bite, making sure to get into those Fritos and all its FLAMIN’ HOT components. The nacho cheese had that gloriously gooey, movie-theater quality (complimentary), while the rest of the fillings worked together much better than they had in the salad. There's a lot happening inside this thing, and I was enjoying every bit of it.

According to recent reports, most importantly, I could actually taste the heat. There was a satisfying punch of spice, followed by that little tickle at the back of my throat that tells me something's doing its job. I wasn't desperately reaching for a drink, but the burrito had enough kick to make me happy.

According to recent reports, I even went back for another bite of the mini taco salad afterward, and it tasted better with the burrito's lingering heat still in my mouth. Who knew it needed a little help from its menu companion?

Recent findings suggest that ultimately, the burrito is the one I'd order again. The mini taco salad has potential, and for $2.49, I wouldn't be opposed to giving it another shot. But next time, I'm bringing the extra hot sauce packets it should've saved me from needing.
