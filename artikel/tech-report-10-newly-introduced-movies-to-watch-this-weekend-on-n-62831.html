If you prefer to stay home, you have plenty of options. Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey is out on VOD and finally available to watch on your TV. The movie stars Matt Damon as Odysseus, following him and his crew of men as they try to return home in the aftermath of the devastating Trojan War.

According to recent reports, other major releases include Steps, Netflix’s latest animated movie, following Cinderella’s evil stepsisters, with one of them forced to team up with her in order to save their realm. It’s voiced by Amanda Seyfried, Ali Wong, Stephanie Tsu, Daniel Radcliffe, and more.

In a recent development, if you’re looking for horror or drama with a healthy dose of disturbing material, Netflix has two options: Insidious: Out of the Further follows a mom plagued by awful nightmares that might be missives from a parallel dimension; Gentle Monster follows a pianist and a mother who moves to the country with her family looking for a break from city life. Then her husband is accused by the police of serious crimes.

According to recent reports, other horror movies are available on VOD. There’s The Ice Cream Man, following an ice cream seller whose frozen desserts turn kids into killers, and Appofeniacs, a series of horror snippets exploring AI.

Industry observers note that other highlights include Nicolas Cage's Best Pancakes in the County, the Anthony Bourdain biopic Tony, and the Patricia Highsmith-inspired psychological thriller A Talent for Murder. There’s also The Beast, a newly introduced Samuel L. Jackson film where he stars as a President trying to survive a terrorist attack with only a wounded Secret Service Agent for protection. Happy watching.

Recent findings suggest that perhaps the biggest film of the year is finally available on streaming. Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey adapts the story of Odysseus (Matt Damon) to the screen in appropriately epic fashion and format, having him face giants, witches, sea monsters, mermaids, and much more as he tries to return home to his wife (Anne Hathaway) and son (Tom Holland).

It has been revealed that the Odyssey opens with a gentle warning: "A time of apparent magic," a gentle prod at audiences to both accept and question what they're about to see. Yet Ithaca, and the Palace of Odysseus, is all too devoid of magic. His wife Penelope (Anne Hathaway) has spent the last 20 years waiting for her husband (Matt Damon) to return home from the Trojan War, while a small army of suitors vies to replace him. Running out of time before lead suitor Antinous (Robert Pattinson) can seal the deal, Odysseus' son Telemachus (Tom Holland) sets out on a journey to either find his dad or prove he's dead and claim the crown (either option seems fine for the young lad).

Sources indicate that netflix’s latest animated film takes the well-trodden story of Cinderella and reinvents it through a fresh perspective. It tells the story from the side of her evil stepsisters. The story follows Lilith (Ali Wong), who accidentally turns her sister Margot (Stephanie Tsu) into a frog. To bring her sister back, she has to team up with her estranged stepsister Cinderella (Amanda Seyfried) to save the kingdom.

In a recent development, the star-studded cast of Steps works hard to give depth to characters who, for all intents and purposes, were completely shallow in their original tales. Wong is known for her sharpness and her comedy. She meshes well with this more modern take on the ugly stepsister, but she’s just as capable of hitting you where it hurts when the story calls for it. Her anger at being unaccepted and belittled by the people around her, while Cinderella only has to exist to be loved, is heartbreaking to watch.

In a recent development, insidious: Out of the Further is finally out on Netflix. The film expands on the successful Blumhouse franchise, focusing on a different family. It follows Gemma (Amelia Eve), a single mother traumatized by a childhood dominated by an absent mother and a terrifying incident. As she begins to have strange dreams, she starts to scare her daughter and mirror her mother’s erratic behavior.

In a recent development, written and directed by Mary Kreutzer, Gentle Monster follows a concert pianist (Lea Seydoux) who relocates to the countryside after her husband claims burnout. As the film progresses, we learn that her husband is facing some serious accusations from the police.

It has been revealed that based on a pivotal summer in Anthony Bourdain’s life, Tony is set in 1975, following a 19-year-old fictional version of Bourdain who relocates to Provincetown to follow his high school crush. After a series of slips and disasters, he ends up getting a job as a dishwasher in a restaurant.

Sources indicate that nicolas Cage stars in this thriller, which has drawn comparisons to Tarantino, playing a criminal on the run from a botched heist. He hides in a diner, triggering a face-off with all sorts of enemies, including federal agents, criminals, and more, putting a seemingly innocent waitress and line cook at risk.

Eli Roth’s latest film follows a strange and mysterious ice cream man (Ari Millen) who serves frozen desserts that turn kids into violent killers.

Set in 1995, A Talent for Murder follows an editor from New York (Alden Ehrenreich) who’s been sent to the Swiss Alps to convince Patricia Highsmith (Helen Mirren) to write a new Tom Ripley novel. There, she challenges him to help her craft the perfect murder.

According to recent reports, appofeniacs contains various stories, all centered on the impact of AI. It follows a troll wreaking chaos on people’s lives by creating AI deepfakes, including a smear campaign, putting a man's life and career at risk, and more.

The Beast is an action-thriller starring Samuel L. Jackson. The premise is wild: the US President (Jackson), a former U.S. veteran, gets stranded at an international summit, with his protective detail wiped out in an attack. He’s left alone with a wounded Secret Service agent (Joel Kinnaman) as the two try to survive.
