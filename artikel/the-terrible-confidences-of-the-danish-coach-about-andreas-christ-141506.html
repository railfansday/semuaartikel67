Central defender of FC Barcelona, ​​the Dane Andreas Christensen (30 years old) has played 8 matches this season. Always prone to injuries, the former Chelsea player notably missed 7 matches between the last international break and this weekend with the 2-1 victory against Atlético de Madrid. Only back on the field, he will not travel with Denmark for the international break in November.

Last night, he was in tears on the phone, he is so frustrated and upset by this situation. He felt he was not ready and therefore had to withdraw. Given his current physical condition, he does not feel fit to play international matches.
