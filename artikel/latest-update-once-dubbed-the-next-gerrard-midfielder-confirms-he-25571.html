A once exciting midfielder who was labelled as the next Steven Gerrard is back at Liverpool.

Recent findings suggest that the Reds' academy has done some astonishing work over the last decade. Liverpool were not always recognised as the best academy in England.

Traditionally the likes of Arsenal, Manchester United and even Chelsea have come to be known as the conveyor belt for the country's best prospects.

However, in the last decade Liverpool's reputation has changed.

Thanks to Jurgen Klopp, Alex Inglethorpe and a strategy to increase recruitment and scouting at youth level Liverpool are now the place to be for the country's leading talents.

Just look at the likes of Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni as well as Luca Eden, Erik Farkas and Joshua Abe coming through.
