GTA 6: Where to Find a Fighter Jet

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It’s not just wheeled vehicles that you can steal in Grand Theft Auto 6. Just as in previous games, you’ll be able to take control of some flying vehicles, be it fighter jets, personal planes or commercial planes. In this case, we’re talking about the former. These aren’t exactly useful in most scenarios, but just having one is something to be proud of, not to mention you can do some serious unnecessary damage with its missiles. This guide will help you track one down and steal it.

Recent findings suggest that there is only one place in the entirety of the map where you can find fighter jets, and it’s, surprisingly, very early on. When you start the story of Grand Theft Auto 6, you will be on the outskirts of Vice City. The small Key Lento, where small drug trafficking happens and everyone is either horny or incredibly violent. Regardless, there is an opportunity here.

While the game will be pointing you more north, with Lucia being held in Hamlet and Vice City even further north, most people wouldn’t even consider looking south. Well, if you do, you will be treated to a long road that heads into a small town called Southwind, and next to it, is the Mile Zero Air Station.

This is essentially the military base of Grand Theft Auto 6. While there are restricted areas elsewhere, this is definitely the most heavily guarded location you will encounter, and because of that, you will find some heavy firepower here.

There will be Air Force One planes, armored vehicles, tanks and, of course, fighter jets. Inside the Miel Zero Air Station sits a good number of fighter jets for stealth, but getting one isn’t exactly the easiest of tasks.

According to recent reports, in order to get to Mile Zero Air Station, you can take a couple of paths. It’s very difficult to remain undetected, almost impossible really, but if you’re able to avoid detection, you’ll be able to grab a jet. Unfortunately, when you get airborne, you’ll immediately be on their radar, in which they will send fighter jets to intercept you, which will go poorly very promptly.

There are a couple ways to get in. The first is through the main entrance near Southwind. This is heavily guarded, so if you’re doing this approach, sneak into the armored vehicle that’s generally parked next to the entrance and then drive in.

If you want to go the stealth approach, come in from the sea. The port off the western part of Mile Zero Air Station is still guarded, but there are no fences, so there’s a lot more room to move around.

It has been revealed that it’s not impossible to outrun the 5-star Wanted level when getting a fighter jet, but it’s hard. In our best attempt, we flew back into downtown. The fighter jets and choppers still chased us and damaged the jet beyond repair. What we did was, while Downtown, or in Tequesta (south of Downtown), go as high as possible.

The jets will follow, and as soon as you begin to level off, jump out. I hope you have a parachute as you’re going to fall down as far as possible until you’re level with some of the skyscrapers. Tequesta is full of helipads with at least two of them having helicopters on them.

Try to parachute over to them and land. It can be tricky, but it can be done. While the jets are still distracted high in the sky, you should be able to fly the helicopter out of the wanted zone.
