"Essentially Worthless" - Disgruntled Fans Are Review Bombing Pikmin 4 On Switch 2

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is out now, bringing 4K visuals to the 2023 title along with the new Dandori Academy mode and some neat little extras like voice commands.

We awarded it a score of 7/10 in our review. It's still good – hell, the main Pikmin 4 campaign is possibly the best of the bunch – yet we can't deny our disappointment at the lack of mouse controls and 60fps performance.

It has been revealed that but if you take a look on Metacritic, you'd think Nintendo just put out its worst game in years. Although the metascore is sitting at a commendable 83, the game's user score is much, much lower at 2.3. This essentially means its average is 'Generally Unfavourable'. Yeesh.

It has been revealed that most folks just seem disgruntled at the notion that Nintendo is charging for what they perceive to be a very minor upgrade over the original. It's true that a lot of games over the years have received free updates for newer hardware, but Nintendo has opted to charge £7.99 / $9.99 / €9.99.

"This switch 2 edition is essentially worthless. At worst it's a spit in the face of series fans. $20 AUD for a bunch of recycled content and 4k visuals. They couldn't even be bothered to implement 60fps even though the system could definitely handle it. Such a lazy patch, and yet they have the gall to charge $20 AUD.".

Meanwhile, another user provided a slightly more measured assessment, stating:.

"The Dandori Academy has reused content, half of the maps so far are taken directly from the base game and the other half share the same exact layout. Plus the new card on the title screen looks like an ad, ruins the nice scenery, fortunately it can be hidden.

Sources indicate that "The main game has also suffered, now somehow having longer loading times than it did on the original Switch. All other Switch 2 editions have made them shorter, so this is really weird. It's not a small difference either, some have literally doubled.".

The comment about the load times is particularly interesting.

According to recent reports, this has been highlighted elsewhere, with YouTuber 'Press A!' posting an analysis titled 'Pikmin 4's DLC is a disaster...' (flexing that signature YouTube nuance right there) in which he compares the load times of the Switch 2 Edition with the original. When exiting out of a cave, the original would take around 6 seconds or so to load the main environment, however the same moment took over 30 seconds in the Switch 2 version.

Sources indicate that A few people have mentioned the load times being quite long, so we tested it out ourselves. While loading into a cave takes about 6 seconds, loading out took around 14-15 seconds. Not the 30+ that others are citing, then, but probably long enough to raise an eyebrow or two.

According to recent reports, pikmin 4's user score on Switch 2 is a startling contrast to that of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Despite some concerns here and there regarding the game's overall difficulty and approachability compared to the original, Ocarina of Time commands the highest user score of all time at 9.6.

We'll be curious to see whether Nintendo takes any steps to improve the load times in Pikmin 4, but for what you actually get with the newly introduced content and visual enhancements on Switch 2, we'd say the upgrade price is probably appropriate. It'll tide us over until Pikmin 5, at least.
