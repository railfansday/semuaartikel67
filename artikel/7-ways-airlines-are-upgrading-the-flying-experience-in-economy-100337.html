7 ways airlines are upgrading the flying experience in economy

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (171 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Airlines are introducing notable upgrades in economy cabins, including improved Wi-Fi, innovative seating options and enhanced entertainment systems.
  • Free, faster onboard internet is becoming standard for loyalty program members as more carriers install advanced connectivity technology.
  • New comfort features such as adjustable headrests, walk-up snack bars and even couch-like seating or bunk beds are being rolled out on select long-haul routes.
  • Enhanced seatback entertainment now includes Bluetooth pairing and partnerships with major streaming services, offering passengers more personalized options.
  • Many of these upgrades are limited to specific aircraft or long-haul routes and may require an additional fee or be available only to loyalty program members.
  • Some features, like bunk beds or 'Relax Row' seating, are debuting in the coming years and may be subject to availability or booking restrictions.
  • Access to certain entertainment options or wellness zones may vary by airline and route.
