A Chinese influencer has sparked backlash after using AI to digitally recreate his late grandfather and continue posting videos to their popular fashion account, which has nearly six million followers.

The social media account, known as “Kang Kang and Grandpa,” became a viral sensation thanks to videos showing Grandpa Kang modeling stylish outfits, streetwear, and accessories alongside his grandson.

However, in September 2026, Kang Kang unveiled that his grandfather had died peacefully in his sleep, leaving fans devastated.

The account had built an audience of approximately 5.8 million followers, with viewers praising Grandpa Kang’s fashion sense and positive attitude toward aging.

In a recent development, in October, the account unexpectedly returned with new videos featuring the deceased influencer, but there was a catch. His grandson had used artificial intelligence to bring him back digitally.

According to the South China Morning Post, Kang Kang uploaded several AI-generated videos, including one showing his grandfather interacting with him as a baby.

Another depicted Grandpa Kang lining up to buy designer sneakers and asking fellow elderly shoppers whether they were fans of K-pop star G-Dragon.

One particularly unusual video demonstrated the pair together across different historical periods, including as cavemen and even prehistoric fish.

While some fans supported Kang Kang’s decision to keep his grandfather’s memory alive, others questioned whether it was appropriate to use AI to depict someone who could no longer consent.

As Chinese outlet The Paper reported, supporters argued that the technology offered grieving families a upcoming way to remain connected to their loved ones. Critics, however, accused the account of exploiting the deceased.

On November 8, Kang Kang addressed the controversy, explaining that he wanted to recreate moments with his grandfather that they never had the opportunity to film.

He said the videos were intended to share previously untold stories and allow his grandfather to continue bringing joy to viewers.

However, Kang Kang acknowledged the criticism and said he would reconsider using AI if the content made people uncomfortable.

By November 11, the account had removed the controversial AI videos, along with Kang Kang’s response. Its Xiaohongshu profile had also reportedly been cleared of posts.

The controversy comes as AI-generated personalities continue to divide audiences. In October, a Chinese AI K-pop group faced backlash after its creators announced that one of its virtual members was pregnant, later admitting they invented the storyline to write her out of the group.
