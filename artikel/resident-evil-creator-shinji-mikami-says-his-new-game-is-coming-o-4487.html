Resident Evil creator Shinji Mikami says his new game is coming out in 2027

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Resident Evil series creator Shinji Mikami says the debut title from his new studio Unbound is on track to release next year.

Recent findings suggest that mikami said as much during an appearance on a Japanese podcast last month, but his comments seem to have slipped under the radar until Twitter account Genki shared them. The interview is entirely in Japanese with no official translation, but according to machine translation, Mikami says the game will be out somewhere around the middle of 2027.

According to recent reports, that is unfortunately the only clear-cut aspect of this story, as everything else starts getting really confusing the deeper you dig. News of Unbound's formation didn't become widely circulated until March of this year, but the studio's official website says it was established in 2022, which is before Mikami left his previous studio, Tango Gameworks, the team behind The Evil Within, Ghostwire Tokyo, and Hi-Fi Rush. The website itself is also an overstimulating mess of cryptic, flashing imagery, Japanese and English-language text, and a broken countdown clock.

And then there's Kamuy, another studio Mikami apparently founded after his time at Tango. In 2024, Automaton reported on comments made by Mikami during a talk show indicating he was done with the survival horror genre and wanted to focus on games with shorter release cycles led by young talent. It's unclear if Kamuy has simply been renamed Unbound or if they're two distinct entities, but we haven't heard a word about Kamuy in years.

Recent findings suggest that separately, there's been some conflicting information about the nature of Unbound's debut title. Mikami's longtime collaborator Masato Kimura described the game as "a AAA title for high-end consumers," but in this upcoming(ish) interview, Mikami states it's a relatively small game. Again, it's machine-translated, but that definitely would conflict with the idea of a premium, big-budget AAA game.

It has been revealed that unbound was acquired by Stellar Blade developer Shift Up in 2026, so it stands to reason that funding isn't an issue in terms of overall scale, but the latest comments from Mikami seem to indicate a smaller release. All that is to say, we know virtually nothing about this game beyond the fact that it's apparently coming in 2027. Whether it'll be another survival horror game like Mikami is known for or something completely different, I suppose we'll find out next year.

Resident Evil creator Shinji Mikami believes devs need to "make games that leave people wanting to clear them with their own hands" rather than being content watching a playthrough.
