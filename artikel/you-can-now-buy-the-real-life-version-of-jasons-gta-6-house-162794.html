The real-life version of Jason’s Key Lento home in Grand Theft Auto 6 has gone up for sale, but it’s going to cost you a pretty penny.

It has been revealed that after 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto fans were finally able to jump into GTA 6 on November 19. While a few people have completed the game already, others are still making their way through Chapter 1.

The early part of the game has some pretty fun and very South Florida vibes to it, seeing as you’re set in Key Lento, working for Brian Heder. When you’re not doing jobs, you can visit the Rusty Anchor, go on some treasure hunts, or just kick back and relax in Jason’s waterfront house before getting a workout in.

Well, if you’ve got nearly $750,000 to your name, and fancy a move to Florida, then you can now buy a house that has a striking resemblance to Jason’s home.

That’s right, the home is based in Key Largo, Florida, and has been advertised by realtor Jordan Moc.

Unlike Jason’s house, this one actually has two bedrooms – instead of one – but it is on stilts and painted blue. That leaky roof on Jason’s house is nowhere to be seen here, thankfully, and there are concrete steps instead of wooden ones.

Funnily enough, it also has a boat in the yard – just like Jason’s home – and you do have access to the water as well.

The house they believe inspired Jason’s home in GTA 6 was just listed for $739,000 in Key Largo pic.twitter.com/HqwZs4Hu3Y.

The listing has been pounced on by Grand Theft Auto fans, with many asking if they can pay through BuckMe – the game’s banking service.

“Hey, I forgot something under the floorboards, I’ll call by at the weekend,” one joked. “Does it come with the neighbor who needs help with plumbing,” another mentioned, referring to Mandy, who lives nearby in-game.

“After GTA VI, these houses will appreciate in value more than anywhere else in America,” another added.

Sources indicate that there are plenty of real-life places that have inspired GTA 6 locations. It is a little freaky to be driving around Ocean Beach if you’ve actually been to Miami Beach.
