Gayle King and her family are mourning a tragic loss.

The CBS Mornings host’s son-in-law Virgil Miller—who married her daughter Kirby Bumpus in 2020 before welcoming two children together—died at the age of 45 following a battle with cancer, CBS Mornings executive producer Jon Tower told staff in a Nov. 27 internal memo obtained by multiple outlets.

Industry observers note that “Mornings Family—I’m sorry to be delivering this sad news,” Tower began, sharing that “over the Thanksgiving holiday,” Miller died after a “courageous battle with cancer.”“Our thoughts right now are with her daughter, and their two beautiful children,” Tower added. “We’re respecting the family and giving them privacy as they get through this difficult time.”.

According to recent reports, he added that King would be taking a week off from CBS Mornings “to help with family matters,” noting the 71-year-old would return the week of Dec. 7.

Tower did not share additional insight into Miller’s health battle.

Miller married King and ex-husband William Bumpus’ daughter Bumpus in December 2020, almost two years after the couple had announced their engagement.

It has been revealed that bumpus, 40, shared the news on Instagram in February 2019, posting a photo of herself and Miller as she showed off her engagement ring with the caption, “Here. WE. Go!”.

But the duo—who shared son Luca, 5, and daughter Grayson, 2—had quite a different wedding than they’d imagined, as they married at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic at her godmother Oprah Winfrey’s Santa Barbara home. As King shared, the guest list was very tiny, encompassing Winfrey, her partner Stedman Graham, King and her son William Bumpus Jr., who officiated the nuptials.

According to recent reports, “Strict covid rules I couldn’t even hug her on her wedding day that was haaaaard!” King wrote on Instagram in February 2021. “Not the wedding we planned in 2020 but my favorite son @willgb3 performed the ceremony... the day was gorgeous and so was kirby!”.

And through the years, the journalist had been effusive in her praise of Miller.

For his birthday in July 2022, King praised Miller as her “fav son-in-law,” and adding that he was a “great husband to fav daughter @kirbybump & best dad to fav grandson.”.
