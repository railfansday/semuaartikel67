As a VPN, Surfshark is very accomplished. It's fast – posting peak speeds of over 2,000 Mbps in our latest testing – and it's very effective at unblocking streaming sites, too.

Of course, none of that would matter if it wasn't secure. But, thankfully, it is. Then there's Surfshark's Scam Protection, which checks SMS messages for signs of fraud, uses AI to spot suspicious emails and phishing attempts, and blocks access to malicious or unwanted websites.
