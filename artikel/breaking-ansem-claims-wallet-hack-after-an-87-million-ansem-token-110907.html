Crypto trader Ansem noted his Pump wallet was hacked on Tuesday, roughly 51 minutes after a Solana wallet labeled “ansemconzimp” on Pump.fun finished selling about 87.49 million ANSEM, according to a Coindaro tally.

Industry observers note that in his post, Ansem said he suspected someone had accessed his Bullpen profile, a trading app where he said he had imported the wallet’s Solana private key. In a later reply, Ansem denied making the trades and said he was conducting a security audit.

Ansem also said he believed the activity was limited to Solana. He said Bullpen was the only place he had imported his private key and that he didn't think anyone had accessed his Ethereum-compatible Pump wallets.

Solscan’s wallet page lists the Pump.fun name “ansemconzimp” and identifies the wallet as the owner of the token account for The Black Bull, the token that trades as ANSEM.

It reported that the wallet began the period with about 87.48 million ANSEM and received a few thousand more while selling was underway, accounting for the slightly higher total sold.
