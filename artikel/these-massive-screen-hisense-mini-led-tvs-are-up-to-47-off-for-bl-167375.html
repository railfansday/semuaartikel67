Recent findings suggest that the entire Hisense U8 lineup is on sale for Black Friday, with prices starting at $749 for the 55-inch model (down from $1,424.99) and going up to $2,797.99 for the massive 100-inch version (originally $3,999.99). The discounts also extend to the 65-, 75-, and 85-inch models, with savings of up to $1,200 across the lineup. Regardless of which size you choose, Amazon throws in free delivery to the room of your choice, a service that normally costs $19.99.

According to recent reports, brightness is the U8QG’s main advantage, with the manufacturer estimating up to 5,000 nits of peak brightness, while PCMag measured around 3,200 nits when reviewing the 65-inch model. Actual performance will vary with the picture mode and content, but either figure points to strong highlights in Dolby Vision and HDR10 video. Its Mini-LED local dimming also produces deep blacks, although some light bloom can appear around bright objects on dark backgrounds. Audio-wise, its built-in 4.1.2-channel system, which includes surround channels, a subwoofer, and upward-firing speakers, gives movies more scale than typical TV speakers, though a separate sound system can still offer clearer separation and a broader soundstage.

The U8QG also has plenty to offer gamers using a current-generation console or a capable gaming PC. It supports refresh rates up to 165Hz, variable refresh rate, and AMD FreeSync Premium Pro, with a measured 4.3 milliseconds of input lag at 1080p and 120Hz and 10.1 milliseconds at 4K and 60Hz. Those results should make controls feel responsive, even during fast-paced games. There’s also a side-mounted USB-C port that accepts DisplayPort video, while Google TV handles streaming alongside Apple AirPlay and Google Cast. The biggest inconvenience is the limited selection of three HDMI ports, since a console, soundbar, and streaming device would occupy all of them.
