If you don't want to splash out on a fancy tablet this year, this iPad sets you up with the essentials. It comes with 128GB of storage, the A16 chip, a vibrant Liquid Retina display with True Tone (which adjusts the display's color temperature based on the lighting conditions), a 12MP Center Stage front camera, and a 12MP Wide back camera. Plus, it has an all-day battery life.

As for why it's our top budget iPad, Mashable's Samantha Mangino writes that "Even though its tech is basic compared to the advanced iPad Air and Pro, it's great for someone who wants a portable screen for streaming, playing games, and reading.".
