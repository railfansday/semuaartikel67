Aston Villa’s hopes of progressing to the Champions League knockout rounds were dented as they were beaten 1-0 at Galatasaray.

Unai Emery’s side won their first three games in the league phase but rather than bouncing back from a 3-0 defeat at Barcelona, they suffered another reverse in Istanbul.

Deniz Gul’s 77th minute header was the game’s first shot on target and was enough to seal all three points for the Turkish side.

With games against Paris St Germain and Borussia Dortmund to come either side of Christmas, Unai Emery’s men now have a challenge to finish in the top eight and earn a direct ticket into the knockout phase.

Sources indicate that even more worryingly, this latest loss made for six straight games without a win in all competitions, while Villa failed to register a single shot on target in the 90 minutes. their sixth consecutive defeat.

The pre-match talk was about the state of the pitch at RAMS Park, with Galatasaray playing the weekend’s league match away from the stadium in order for the surface to undergo emergency repairs.

As a result, Villa were unable to train on it ahead of the game and the pitch was plied with sand.

But the visitors initially acquitted themselves well and managed the cauldron of noise as they enjoyed a controlled start, with their best moment coming when they made a quick break down the right and the ball ended up with Johan Manzambi, but he could not get a shot away.

Galatasaray came into it as the first half progressed, but did not test Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, who will have welcomed a quiet opening after his recent troubles.

The intensity increased at the start of the second half and there were two flashpoints as tempers flared.

First, John McGinn and Aleksey Batrakov went into the book after McGinn flew into his counterpart as revenge for a heavy challenge on Alysson by former Tottenham defender Davinson Sanchez.

There was another coming together as Joao Gomes was booked for stopping Yunus Akgun’s counter-attack.

The hosts suddenly had the bit between their teeth and they pushed Villa back, with Aleksey Batrakov flashing a volley well over, before a loose ball from a corner fell to Gul, who showed bravery to head the ball home while taking a kick in the face from Nicolas Jackson.
