Cherry Time! broadcast set for November 27 featuring “exciting announcements” and Hollow Knight: Silksong free expansion ‘Sea of Sorrow’

🌐 Source: Gematsu • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (90 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Sources indicate that team Cherry will host a Cherry Time!
  • Hollow Knight: Silksong is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam, GOG, Humble Store, and Microsoft Store.

Sources indicate that team Cherry will host a Cherry Time! broadcast on November 27 at 7:00 a.m. PT / 10:00 a.m. ET featuring “a bunch of exciting announcements,” as well as an revision on Hollow Knight: Silksong free expansion “Sea of Sorrow.” You will be able to watch it on YouTube.

Hollow Knight: Silksong is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam, GOG, Humble Store, and Microsoft Store. It is also available via Game Pass.
