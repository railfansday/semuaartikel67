Liverpool have a wonderful chance to sign the perfect Arsenal star. It's a deal they've simply got to get over the line.

Liverpool have to improve their midfield and they have to do it promptly. It's difficult to see how they could challenge for honours this season without an improvement in the middle, honestly.

There will be opinions that waiting until the summer is sensible and maybe that's right. Liverpool don't need to go all-out for trophies in Andoni Iraola's first season, after all. Building gradually might be the safer option.

But it does feel as though there are plenty of good options in January. Options who could quickly improve this team and potentially at a reasonable cost, too. If we're right about that, then surely there's no need to really wait?

Which brings up a case with Arsenal and the potential for sending a big offer their way.

According to recent reports, AS are the latest to push the idea of Zubimendi leaving Arsenal and TeamTalk have claimed this month that Liverpool were contacted by intermediaries. It's a golden opportunity, then, for the Reds to snap up a player who would significantly help in midfield.

We're not sure Liverpool will find a better option, honestly. He's someone they ultimately came close to signing in 2024 and his level hasn't dropped since then. If anything, it's improved. And Zubimendi is exactly the kind of player that Liverpool are crying out for.

It's an opportunity that shouldn't be missed.
