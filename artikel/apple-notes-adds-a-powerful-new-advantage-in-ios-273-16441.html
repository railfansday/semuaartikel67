There’s no shortage of note-taking apps on the App Store, but Apple Notes has long been my favorite, and in iOS 27.3 it’s gaining a fresh advantage: extensive app controls via Siri AI.

Voice-driven computing is on the rise, with AI assistants—including Siri AI—now more capable and intelligent than ever.

Siri AI is already a substantial upgrade over the old Siri, but it’s getting even better in iOS 27.3.

As we reported last week, iOS 27.3 gives Siri AI many upcoming capabilities across built-in Apple apps on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and more.

Sources indicate that the one I’ve been testing the most is Apple Notes. Siri has offered basic Notes-related functionality for years, but it gains a whole new set of powers with the new update.

Sources indicate that I have nearly 3,000 notes saved in Apple Notes and use the app every day, so it’s been fun seeing what Siri can now do for me. One common example is that I’ve been able to have Siri save a link to the webpage I’m right now viewing inside a note, and even specify exactly where in the note I want it to go.

Industry observers note that in theory, third-party notes apps may be able to offer similar controls sometime next year when app skills officially launch. For now though, anyone who wants to control their notes app by voice—or via Spotlight, as I tend to do on my iPad Pro—have another reason to use Apple Notes.
