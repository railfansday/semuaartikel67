Manchester United’s first Champions League game under new head coach Mauricio Pochettino sees them travel to the Estadio Jose Alvalade to face Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday night.

The Red Devils are seeking to bounce back from a humbling 4-0 Premier League loss to Liverpool on Sunday, while Sporting eased to victory by the same scoreline against lower-league opposition in the Taca de Portugal last weekend.
