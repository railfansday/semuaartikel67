Nongshim RedForce sign Deft after military service, reuniting DRX’s 2022 world title trio

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that nongshim RedForce have brought 2022 world champion Deft back to the LCK and paired him again with Kingen and Pyosik. The move follows an eighth-place 2026 for the club and comes days after BLG ended Korea's four-year hold on the World Championship.

Kim ‘Deft’ Hyuk-kyu’s signing was confirmed on 19 November. The club used its official social media channels to make the announcement. Deft began his military service after the 2024 season ended.

The deal reunites him with two members of the DRX side that won the 2022 World Championship. Jungler Hong ‘Pyosik’ Chang-hyeon joined later the same day, alongside support Moon ‘Andil’ Gwan-bin. Top laner Hwang ‘Kingen’ Seong-hoon had re-signed a day earlier.

In a recent development, deft’s signing was announced at 7pm. The Pyosik and Andil additions that followed completed Nongshim’s five-man starting line-up, Inven Global reported. Mid laner Seon ‘Calix’ Hyeon-bin provides continuity. He has been with the organisation since 2021 and extended his deal on 18 November.

Deft brings a long winning record. He made his debut with MVP Blue in 2013. His titles include the LCK, the LPL, the Mid-Season Invitational and the 2022 World Championship.

Kingen first joined Nongshim for the 2025 season. Pyosik arrives after a difficult 2026 at DN SOOPers. That team lost a run of matches, and rookie Sharvel took his starting place.

The move had been signalled. About a month before the announcement, Sheep Esports reported from Korean sources that Nongshim planned to sign Deft. The same report stated rookie AD carry Diable would need a newly introduced team.

The signings mark a sharp change of direction after a poor year. Once the league campaign was over, the team lasted just a single match in the play-ins.

Recent findings suggest that the deal lands days after a setback for the whole league. Bilibili Gaming’s victory over Gen.G came at the Barclays Center in Newly introduced York. It gave the organisation its first Summoner’s Cup.

BLG won all four of its series against LCK teams at the tournament. Every LCK seed fell to the Chinese side on its way to the trophy. The title is also the LPL’s first since EDward Gaming won in 2021.

Industry observers note that spain gets its debut as a Mid-Season Invitational host in 2027, with Valencia’s Feria de Valencia as the venue. Before that, Riot plans to publish First Stand: All In’s format and qualification process in January.
