Janelle Brown opened up on Sister Wives about being “excommunicated” from the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints after choosing to marry polygamist Kody Brown.

Sources indicate that “I was excommunicated from the LDS church right about the time I was either pregnant with Logan or had just had Logan,” Janelle, 57, revealed during the Sunday, November 15, episode of the TLC series. (Janelle and Kody, 57, share six children: Logan, 32, Maddie, 31, Hunter, 29, Gabe, 25, Savanah, 21, and son Garrison, who died in March 2024 at the age of 25.).

She recalled that after marrying Kody in 1993 and becoming his second wife, the LDS church, which she was raised in, shunned her.

“I was, like, 24 and I remember the letter coming to our house,” Janelle shared. “Kody was not involved. It was just me. Yeah, they excommunicated me.”.

Industry observers note that the LDS church practices mainstream Mormonism. Kody and his wives initially practiced Mormon fundamentalism, and were specifically part of the Apostolic United Brethren (AUB), before they all slowly distanced themselves from the religion.

Janelle, for her part, revealed on Sunday’s episode that she still has a “spiritual life” but it’s not under a specific umbrella.

According to recent reports, “I have a relationship with God. I just don’t have a church I worship with,” she shared. “I’m kind of waiting for some sign that I need to go somewhere or I belong somewhere. I don’t know.”.

Janelle also noted that during her first marriage to ex-sister wife Meri Brown’s brother, she actually had her husband join the LDS church.

However, once that marriage ended, Janelle remained close with Meri’s family, who were polygamists, and that sparked her own interest in the practice.

It has been revealed that “I felt a spiritual calling. I made this decision. It wasn’t because I was — I thought I was less than. I didn’t think it was because I only deserved this,” Janelle told former sister wife and close friend Christine Brown. “I chose it because I felt like it was a calling and it wasn’t anything to do with my perception of what I was worth or what I was entitled to or what I deserved.”.

Christine, 54, pointed out, “You found the value in it. I think we were talking about this the other day, that there was value because you were believing in a community.”.

Janelle agreed, adding, “I thought that we would have some sort of really great communal family experience or something.”.

While all of Kody’s now-ex wives initially bought into the polygamist lifestyle, three of his wives have walked away in recent years.

Industry observers note that christine announced her split from Kody in 2021 after more than 25 years of a spiritual marriage. Janelle revealed in December 2022 that she and Kody had been separated for months.
