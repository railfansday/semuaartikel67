Gary Neville and Jamie Carragher have made their predictions for Sunday’s huge Premier League clash between Liverpool and Manchester United.

United head to Anfield to take on arch-rivals Liverpool in what will be Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge of the Red Devils.

The Argentine has signed a two-and-a-half-year deal at Old Trafford, replacing Michael Carrick after a dismal start to the season for United, which has them sitting 18th in the league table after ten matches.

Harry Maguire’s late header saw United claim a famous 2-1 victory when the sides met on Merseyside last season, but Pochettino will need to summon an immediate turnaround from his side to give them a fighting chance this weekend.

But former Red Devils Neville still believes that United can get a positive, telling the Overlap: ‘I just don’t think the defence is good enough, the defence will concede goals, I don’t care who the manager is.

‘But I think it’ll be a draw. Probably.’.

Carragher, though, disagreed with Neville’s sentiment and instead backed his former side to prevail at home on Sunday.

Recent findings suggest that asked if United could get a draw at Anfield, he joked: ‘They can, of course they can. They won’t, but of course they can.’.

‘I think Liverpool will concede as well, but I’m going to go 3-1 [to Liverpool],’ Carragher responded when asked for his prediction.

United will have Lisandro Martinez back available for selection after his red card against Chelsea, but Luke Shaw, Carlos Baleba, Manuel Ugarte, Marcus Rashford and Benjamin Sesko are all expected to miss out.

Liverpool, meanwhile, have been boosted by Jeremie Frimpong and Ryan Gravenberch’s return to training, with both in contention to feature on Sunday.

Virgil van Dijk and Dominik Szoboszlai are both also available after minor injury concerns over the international break.
