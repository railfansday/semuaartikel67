Hyatt to launch stackable awards, new Milestone Rewards and Delta perks in 2027

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (205 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Major updates to the World of Hyatt loyalty program will take effect Jan. 1, 2027, introducing more flexible Milestone Rewards and the ability to combine multiple awards on a single stay.
  • Award certificates, such as free nights and suite upgrades, will have a standardized 365-day validity from issuance, doubling the previous window for some awards but shortening it for others.
  • A new partnership with Delta will allow elite members to earn rewards across both hotel stays and flights, provided certain status and booking criteria are met.
  • The booking platform will offer enhanced "Pay My Way" functionality, enabling members to mix awards, points, cash or Points + Cash for each night.
  • Suite Upgrade Awards will have a shorter validity period for some members, depending on when the award is selected.
  • Free parking on award stays will no longer be a standard benefit for top-tier members after February 2028, becoming a Milestone Reward instead.
  • Some benefits, such as My Hyatt Concierge, will now require selection as a Milestone Reward rather than being automatic at certain status levels.
