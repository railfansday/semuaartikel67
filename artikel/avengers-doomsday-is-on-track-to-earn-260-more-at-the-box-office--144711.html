The true box office winner of Dunesday has been predicted by the Nielsen ratings, and it's none other than Avengers: Doomsday.

Per Deadline, NRG (National Research Group, owned by Nielsen) predicts that Doomsday will bring in $270 million at the global box office during its opening weekend. Dune 3 is predicted to bring in $75 million.

According to recent reports, this is the highest projection NRG has ever recorded, with Spider-Man: Brand Upcoming Day being the second-highest. To date, Avengers: Endgame now holds the record second-highest opening weekend for any movie ever at $357.1M, having been beat out by Spider-Man: Brand New Day's $360 million.

Avengers: Doomsday tickets went on sale back in July for the special Infinity Vision screenings. It's worth noting that 60,000 more tickets were sold for the early Thursday screenings following the VisionQuest finale, which teases the return of Wanda Maximoff aka the Scarlet Witch. Wanda will indeed return in Doomsday, but she won't be, uh, herself... if you catch my drift.

Dune 3 tickets have been a bit of a nightmare for fans as they sold out almost immediately back in April, and then crashed several movie theater apps when more tickets were released back in August. Oh, and, when tickets did sell out in April... people started reselling them for up to $1000. The people yearn for sand.
