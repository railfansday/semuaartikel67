Gérard Depardieu to Stand Trial for Rape Charges After Failed Appeal

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

French actor Gérard Depardieu will face a criminal rape trial after an appeal from his lawyers failed.

According to Reuters, on Thursday the Paris Court of Appeal ruled that a trial would go ahead regarding rape charges that stemmed from accusations made by actress Charlotte Arnould in 2018. She alleged that in August of that year, Depardieu raped her twice at his Paris home. He was indicted in December 2020 and has denied any wrongdoing. Last year, his lawyers appealed the prosecutors’ decision for the case to go to trial. Though the court rejected his appeal, it ruled that Depardieu will only be tried for the first alleged rape, and not the second. If convicted, the 77-year-old could face 15 to 20 years in prison.

Recent findings suggest that the news comes just 10 days after Depardieu’s withdrawal of a separate appeal regarding his conviction last year on sexual assault charges. He was given an 18-month suspended prison sentence for sexually assaulting two women on the set of his 2021 film “Les Volets Verts.”.

In a statement to French newspaper Le Monde, he stated: “Justice has spoken. I have been found guilty. I accept this and take responsibility for this conviction,” offering his “deep and sincere apologies” to those who “may have suffered as a result.”.

Recent findings suggest that last week, a new complaint alleged further rape and sexual assault allegations against Depardieu during the filming of Guillaume Nicloux’s “Thalasso” in 2018. His lawyers told French radio station RTL that they were unaware of the complaint, which was filed in May 2025, shortly after his first sexual assault trial.
