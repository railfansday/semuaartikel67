In a recent development, netflix’s dark-comedy anthology series Beef promptly captured viewers’ attention when its first season premiered on April 6, 2023. Now, less than a year after the second season finished airing, there’s an enhancement on the possibility of a third season of Beef.

The tragicomedy from showrunner Lee Sung Jin made a strong impression with its sharp humor and memorable storytelling, leaving fans eager to see what would come next. After all, it was rare to have a TV series where the biggest questions seemed to be: what if we followed people so adamant about destroying each other and everyone around them? Would that be entertaining?

Sources indicate that as it turned out, the answer was a resounding yes. The first season was well received, with 98% of critics' reviews on Rotten Tomatoes positive. Polygon’s take on the first season called it a “cataclysmic spiral of existential despair and self-destruction” and praised stars Steven Yeun and Ali Wong for their electric chemistry as bitter rivals, Danny Cho and Amy Lau.

Industry observers note that the second season moved on from Yeun and Wong, though both remained executive producers. The Netflix show’s upcoming beef centered on two very different couples and featured significant names like Oscar Isaac and Carey Mulligan. It also found critical success, raising one big question: Would Sung Jin have another Beef adventure in the future?

Industry observers note that it’s a question we’ve been wondering about too. At a press junket for Netflix’s upcoming family-fantasy movie, Steps, Polygon asked executive producer and Beef star Ali Wong whether there were any plans for Beef season 3.

“There are a lot of ongoing discussions, I'll say,” Wong teased, but couldn’t say more about whether the series would return for a third season.

It has been revealed that though Wong’s answer was far from definitive, it offers hope that Sung Jin’s vision for a third season could become a reality. Until we get firmer confirmation, fans will just have to wait and see.

Beef seasons 1 and 2 are streaming now exclusively on Netflix.
