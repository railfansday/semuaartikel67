📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Netflix has a packed slate to end the year, including returning favorites, newly introduced films, and live events. The platform will host two Christmas Day NFL games—Bills vs. Broncos and Packers vs. Bears—as well as the FIFA Women’s World Cup 2027™ Final Draw and Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua on Dec. 11 and the second installment of the Six Kings Slam men's tennis tournament on Dec. 27.

Recent findings suggest that on the film side, comedy Bad Day (Dec. 11) stars Cameron Diaz as a single mom having the worst day of her life in a series of comedic mishaps. THE FURTHER MIS-ADVENTURES OF CLIFF BOOTH (Dec. 23) is a crime comedy written by Quentin Tarantino, directed by David Fincher, and starring Brad Pitt as a stuntman working odd jobs in 1970s LA. The film is a spin-off (set eight years later) of the 2019 movie Once Upon a Time in Hollywood.

Recent findings suggest that the December documentary lineup is long and includes 14th (Dec. 4), an Ava DuVernay film—in the spirit of the earlier 13th—that examines the 14th Amendment principles of citizenship and due process.

Finally, familiar series with new seasons include Emily in Paris (Dec. 24), My Next Guest Needs No Introduction With David Letterman (Dec. 14), and a new installment of Hot Ones: Extra Heat (Dec. 2) featuring Rob Lowe.
