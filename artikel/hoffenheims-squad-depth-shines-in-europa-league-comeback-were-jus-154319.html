Hoffenheim’s squad depth shines in Europa League comeback: “We’re just a really awesome crew.”

Recent findings suggest that the strength of Hoffenheim’s bench proved decisive in Thursday evening’s 3-1 Europa League comeback victory over Sturm Graz. Christian Ilzer’s substitutes supplied two of the three goals as the Kraichgauer recovered from a disappointing first half to secure an important European victory against their head coach’s former club.

According to recent reports, hoffenheim entered the dressing room trailing 1-0 after Oumar Diakité’s 43rd-minute opener, with sections of the home crowd expressing their displeasure at the halftime whistle. The hosts nevertheless emerged with renewed energy and eventually turned the match around. Speaking to journalists in the mixed zone afterward, Austrian international Alexander Prass insisted that Hoffenheim’s response had not surprised him.

It has been revealed that “No it doesn’t surprise me,” Prass remarked in the mixed zone when questioned about the comeback. “Because we’re just a really awesome crew. We’re capable of banding together even after a total shit first half, or a not so good first half. Sorry. We can up the energy. Every individual can help us mutually bring what was lacking in the first half. We did everything better in the second half.”.

It has been revealed that ilzer’s decision to introduce Patrick Wimmer, Andrej Kramaric, and Bambasé Conté shortly after the hour mark proved particularly influential. Adam Hlozek equalized in the 72nd minute before Conté completed the turnaround eight minutes later, finishing off a Tim Lemperle cross. Substitute Max Moerstedt sealed the 3-1 victory with his first touch in the 90th minute. Wimmer initiated the attack by winning possession, with Kramaric subsequently setting up the young German striker.

Conté, himself one of the decisive substitutes, agreed when asked about the quality of Hoffenheim’s options from the bench.

“Yeah you could say that,” Conté responded in the mixed zone. “We made it difficult for ourselves in the first half. From the restart onwards we produced a very positive energy with the substitutions and we turned the game around.”.

In a recent development, hoffenheim have already benefited from influential substitute appearances this season. As GGFN examined following the club’s 3-0 Bundesliga victory over Bayer Leverkusen, Kramaric also transformed that encounter after entering in the second half, contributing a goal and an assist.

Industry observers note that despite Thursday’s successful turnaround, team captain Oliver Baumann was less inclined to celebrate the performance unreservedly. Speaking to RTL after the match, the goalkeeper criticized Hoffenheim’s first-half approach and stressed that the team must learn to sustain its improved football over 70 or 80 minutes rather than relying on one good half.

Baumann acknowledged that Hoffenheim remain in a developmental phase. The German international nevertheless welcomed the response after halftime, which helped lift Ilzer’s side to 15th place in the Europa League standings.

Thursday’s comeback thus highlighted both the shortcomings Hoffenheim must address and the considerable resources Ilzer has at his disposal. With the likes of Wimmer, Kramaric, Conté, and Moerstedt capable of influencing matches from the bench, the TSG trainer certainly has options when his starting XI struggles to find its rhythm.
