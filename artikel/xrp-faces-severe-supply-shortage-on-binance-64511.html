XRP Faces Severe Supply Shortage on Binance

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The price of XRP has continued to slump to deeper levels amid the prolonged market volatility. However, recent onchain data has shown an unexpected trend in exchange activity.

According to the latest data from crypto analytics platform Cryptoquant, XRP is right now facing a severe supply shortage on the world’s largest crypto exchange, Binance.

According to the data, XRP’s Supply Shortage Index on Binance has surged to 2.4 as of November 19 despite the asset’s extreme price weakness in recent days.

Notably, the data further reveals that the metric has surpassed the previous reading of 2.09 recorded on July 6, marking a five-month high in XRP’s supply shortage index.

It is notable to note that readings above +2 are a strong indication of severely tightened supply, which means that the amount of XRP available on the exchange has decreased significantly.

While negative readings often mean the opposite, this means that XRP has witnessed a sharp reversal from the supply level seen on September 27.

At the time, the index stood at -1.5 while XRP traded above $1.50, signaling an abundance of XRP on Binance. This means that more tokens were available for trading during the period, which potentially added to selling pressure.

While that is no longer the case, the latest data demonstrates that the XRP supply index has jumped by 3.9 points in just 53 days, moving from supply abundance to severe supply tightness in just about two months.

This typically suggests that XRP is being aggressively moved out of exchanges despite its prolonged price weakness, potentially driving a rebound in the demand for XRP, which could eventually trigger a price recovery.
