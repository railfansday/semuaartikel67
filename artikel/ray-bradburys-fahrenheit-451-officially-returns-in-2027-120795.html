Stories like Ray Bradbury's 1953 novel Fahrenheit 451 have remained timely throughout the years. Written as a critique of censorship and the act of book burning, the novel has been adapted into many different formats, and now a new take on it is heading our way through a new graphic novel from artist Victor Santos. Collider is thrilled to announce that Titan Comics' new graphic novel has the approval of the Bradbury estate and will be released in July 2027. To celebrate the new release of Fahrenheit 451, Collider is thrilled to bring you an exclusive new look at the artwork from Santos' stunning adaptation of Bradbury's timeless work.

Sources indicate that the upcoming images show imagery of the burning of books that became so connected to Bradbury's work. From ripped pages falling to the books themselves sitting in an ashy pile, the work that Santos is doing in his adaptation is truly something remarkable. Santos is an Eisner and Harvey Award-winning artist and has been the co-creator of such graphic novels as Filthy Rich, Rashomon, Bad Girls, Against Hope, and Violent Love.

Fahrenheit 451 has been adapted multiple times, with the most recent adaptation coming in 2018 from Ramin Bahrani, starring Michael B. Jordan as Guy Montag and Sofia Boutella as Clarisse. But the story, as a whole, is still timely in a way that even back in the 1950s, people couldn't have predicted. Bradbury's original work was looking at the way that television can inform and manipulate stories, influencing people who are not willing to read and do the research themselves. That is still just as topical today, and using a graphic novel to share that story adds a whole new layer to the narrative.

It has been revealed that you can get your copy of Fahrenheit 451 in July of next year for $24.99 from Titan Comics. Stay tuned at Collider for more.
