Vincent Kompany responds to Uli Hoeneß’s comments on Jamal Musiala: “We have different roles.”

Industry observers note that in his first press conference back from his illness-related absence, Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany offered an interesting perspective on Jamal Musiala’s impending return to competitive football. The Belgian suggested that the German international could benefit from the changing expectations surrounding him. Speaking at Thursday’s pre-match press conference ahead of Bayern’s Bundesliga encounter with Köln, Kompany also addressed recent remarks from honorary president Uli Hoeneß concerning the 23-year-old’s long-term prospects.

Musiala is expected to return to Bayern’s matchday squad on Friday, provided he encounters no setbacks during Thursday’s final training session. The attacking midfielder has been sidelined since suffering a significant thigh injury while warming up for Germany’s UEFA Nations League encounter with the Netherlands in September.

The latest setback interrupted an encouraging return to competitive football. Musiala has accumulated approximately 200 minutes this season, scoring in both of his starts. He opened the scoring in Bayern’s 5-0 Champions League victory over Bodø/Glimt and the 7-0 Bundesliga triumph over Union Berlin.

Musiala’s recovery has attracted additional attention following recent remarks from Uli Hoeneß questioning whether the midfielder can regain his previous performance level. Bayern CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen also raised questions about how the club might respond if Musiala’s performances were permanently affected. Sporting director Max Eberl sought to reassure supporters on Thursday, emphasizing the club’s continued belief in Musiala’s ability to rediscover his best form.

Kompany, meanwhile, adopted a different approach to the discussion. Rather than disagreeing with Bayern’s hierarchy, the Belgian emphasized that his responsibility as head coach requires him to maintain confidence in his players regardless of the concerns expressed elsewhere within the organization.

According to recent reports, the Bayern trainer also suggested that Musiala’s changed circumstances could provide valuable motivation. Having previously enjoyed the status of an almost untouchable young star, the midfielder now has an opportunity to demonstrate his abilities without carrying precisely the same expectations.

Industry observers note that kompany drew a parallel between Musiala’s comeback and his own return following an extended absence due to illness. The coach expressed hope that both experiences could provide renewed energy for Bayern during the remainder of the season. The 40-year-old also praised Musiala’s determination throughout his rehabilitation, emphasizing that the youngster must ultimately demonstrate his recovery through performances on the pitch.

“We don’t have differing opinions,” Kompany added when questioned about Hoeneß’ comments on Musiala. “What we have is different roles. My role is to believe in all the players. That’s my role. It would be a catastrophe if I came here and stated that, for example, five specific players didn’t matter.”.

It has been revealed that “The fact that we have different roles is one of the things that function well at Bayern,” Kompany continued. “I have 100 percent respect for the opinions of the [club] hierarchy. I don’t have a problem with that. One has to put it into perspective and we can also utilize this in a sporting sense.”.

“There’s nothing that can serve as a greater motivation for a player like Jamal,” Kompany concluded. “There’s something he can show without the same pressure as when he was a ‘golden child’. This is also a nice moment. Luckily, the head coach has had plenty of such moments in his career and I’m looking forward to it.”.
