On October 29, the Google Home release notes had a Device Firmware section that mentioned the patch for both the 4K and HD models. Compared to the original changelog, Google added “Connectivity, media streaming, and display performance improvements.”.

Original 10/12: According to Google’s release notes, UTTA.260813.002 is rolling out just for the 4K model. There is no new release for the HD variant today. Both streaming devices are usually updated at the same time.

This patch bumps the Android Security Patch Level to August 2026 (from October 2025). Google also notes the presence of “Other bug fixes and performance improvements.”.

On your Chromecast with Google TV, go to Settings > System > About > System patch to download and install.
