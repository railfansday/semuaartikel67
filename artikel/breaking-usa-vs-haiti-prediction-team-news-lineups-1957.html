On Tuesday, the USA aim to give their manager a fitting send-off when they host Haiti in the second leg of that quarter-final CONCACAF Nations League tie at TQL Stadium in Cincinnati.

It would require an epic collapse for the Yanks to be eliminated from this tournament on Tuesday, having beaten the Haitians 6-0 in leg one.

For the final time, Mauricio Pochettino will be on the US sidelines Tuesday, hoping to end his tenure on a positive note before heading to Old Trafford to join Manchester United.

While he leaves the national team program without a trophy, the Argentine coach has seen this team flourish since the World Cup concluded, and they are presently on a five-match winning run across all competitions.

Pochettino’s men have been near-flawless since the fall, posting three successive clean sheets while netting three or more goals in four of their last five encounters.

The US have only lost three Nations League knockout matches, boasting a 100% home record in quarter-final outings.

Tuesday will mark their fifth international at TQL Stadium, with the Yanks undefeated at the venue, including two wins.

They are unbeaten in their last 11 internationals against Haiti, winning those last four meetings by a combined margin of 10-1.

David Badia’s men have lost their last two matches by a combined margin of 8-0 after winning their first three Nations League group outings.

The Haitians have not won a quarter-final contest in a CONCACAF competition since the 2019 Gold Cup when they were down 2-0 and stunned Canada 3-2.

They have not lost a League A encounter in this tournament when netting the opening goal since the 2023 group stage when Jamaica beat them 3-2.

Les Grenadiers can claim their first victory over the US on American soil Tuesday since 1969, when they beat them 1-0 in a World Cup qualifier in San Diego.
