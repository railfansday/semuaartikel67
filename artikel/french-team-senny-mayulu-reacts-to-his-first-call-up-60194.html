Between Aurélien Tchouameni’s withdrawal for the entire gathering, and that of Kylian Mbappé for the match against Italy this Thursday evening (8:45 p.m.) in the Nations League, the France team found itself a little depopulated. Zinedine Zidane therefore took the opportunity to call Senny Mayulu, summoned for the first time to the Blues, who was then part of the Espoirs group at Clairefontaine. The Parisian has the advantage of versatility, as the coach highlighted yesterday.

“It’s a source of pride as a young player to be called up by the A’s of the French team, for the country in which I grew up. I was with the Espoirs in the building next door and the coach warned me that I had to go up with the A’s. Now I have to show why I’m here», Reacted the 20-year-old player in front of an FFF camera, not really impressed by the news. “I’ll fit in well, I already know a lot of people. Everything will be fine, then we have to do the work on the ground. We’re going to travel to Italy, and then I think there will be a little hazing.».
