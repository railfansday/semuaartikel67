It has been revealed that when it comes to open world games, there’s nothing quite as dense as Grand Theft Auto 6. Rockstar has created one of the most impressive open world games ever, with so much to see and do. It becomes incredibly immersive, to the point where sometimes you want a little more game in the game. One specific feature that is a bit unconventional is the fast travel system, but just how do you do it?

The obvious question a lot of people might have is “is there fast travel in Grand Theft Auto 6?” This is a massive game with a lot of things to see and do. It’s one of the most densely packed games we’ve ever played, and being an open world, one would expect there to be some sort of fast travel system.

You’ll probably go through a good portion of the game not realizing this, but there is indeed a fast travel system. This is because, even though it’s introduced to you early in the game, it’s something easily overlooked. With that mentioned, it does come with a bit of a caveat to the point, especially early on, it’s not really worth using.

Industry observers note that with all of that said, the way you can fast travel is through your phone. First, you will need to put a destination on your map. It can be a custom pin, or it can be a specific location such as a mission marker or clothing store.

Once you have this selected, bring up your phone and select the RydeMe app. This will bring up a standard menu showing how much it will cost to travel that distance you inputed (you can change the destination in this menu) along with ordering a Premium Ryde.

Select the ride and a car will appear shortly. Getting in, though, won’t immediately teleport you to that location. Instead, there will be dialogue, especially if you’re with your partner, and sometimes it is worth it to listen.

With that stated, if you want to actually teleport, you can pay the driver an extra $100 to skip the ride and get there immediately. The downside is obviously, early on, this is an expensive way of traveling. Also, it will pass time considerably because the car itself will take a long time to get to the destination without breaking any rules.

Whether or not you want to use the teleport system in GTA 6 will probably be determined how far a place is located, as anything under 4 or 5 miles generally isn’t worth paying for, but when it gets above that, it can be a bit of a stretch – unless you want to take a helicopter or plane.
