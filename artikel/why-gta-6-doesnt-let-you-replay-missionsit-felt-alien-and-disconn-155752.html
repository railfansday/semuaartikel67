One of the first things people noticed with Grand Theft Auto 6 is that you cannot replay missions, at least not in a simple, straightforward way. GTA 5 had a mission replay option, as did Red Dead Redemption II, so why doesn't GTA 6?

Senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku that Rockstar "certainly" considered a mission replay feature "early on in development." However, Butchard noted, "As the game came together, it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected.".

He went on to say replaying missions "really diminished the choices you had made.".

While GTA 6 doesn't make it easy to replay missions, you still can do so by reloading an earlier GTA 6 save. Something else to know is that if you're in the middle of a mission and it's not going to plan, you can open the game menu and select retry, quit, or restart.

According to recent reports, GTA 6 blasted off with 32.5 million sales via prepurchases alone and around $4 billion in revenue on day one. The game is only going to get bigger and sell more copies over time, and it's helping drive upcoming PlayStation and Xbox hardware sales.
