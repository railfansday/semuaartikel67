📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the Cardano [ADA] Treasury Foundation wants 25 million ADA to host 12 events in 2027. Its last $22 million proposal (to buy copper) barely got any votes at all.

Hence, the question — Will this one fare any better?

Sources indicate that on 16th November, the Cardano Foundation filed a proposal to pull 24,995,775 ADA from the network’s treasury. The money would pay for Cardano’s presence at 12 crypto events next year.

In a recent development, some of the biggest names on the circuit are on the list. Consensus in Miami, TOKEN2049 in both Singapore and Upcoming York, Web Summit in Lisbon, and India Blockchain Week in Bangalore.

The run kicks off in February with the Africa Tech Summit in Nairobi.

$2 million will go towards sponsorships and $1.06 million for booths and equipment. Travel is said to cost $702K, while side events will account for $695,000.

Industry observers note that according to the Foundation, none of it covers salaries or profit. Whatever isn’t spent goes back to the treasury.

Industry observers note that however, here’s what’s tricky. The Foundation priced ADA at $0.20 to reach its $5 million budget. ADA, however, was valued at $0.15 at press time. At that price, the request is worth about $3.85 million. That is a gap of over $1 million.

Industry observers note that for the budget to work as planned, ADA needs to climb by almost 30%. Not impossible, but far from guaranteed.

DReps, the delegates who vote on treasury spending, have until 20th December to decide.

In a recent development, will 25 million ADA move the price? Probably not. There are about 37.6 billion ADA in circulation. So, this withdrawal will merely be a drop in the ocean.

However, it’s worth pointing out that the chart had bigger problems at press time.

ADA fell from $0.25 in October to $0.155 following a price drop on the 14th.

Recent findings suggest that is a pause imminent? We might be in the early stages, yes. The RSI fell to 21 after that drop, with the same since climbing to 32, just crossing above its average. The OI has also recovered from about $145 million to $153.5 million. Finally, the funding rates were positive yesterday.

On the contrary, the Chaikin Money Flow was negative, implying that sellers might still have a slight edge.
