The fresh indictment builds on a series of criminal matters involving Campbell in multiple counties.

Sources indicate that in October 2024, Attorney General James and Bronx District Attorney Darcel Clark confirmed two separate indictments against Campbell. In the first, she and her co-conspirators — including her husband — were charged in an alleged deed theft scheme involving the childhood home of a Bronx woman. Prosecutors say the group gained the homeowner’s trust, then used fraudulent contracts bearing the homeowner’s forged signature to transfer the property to a shell firm they controlled and to obtain a $493,500 mortgage on the home. Campbell is scheduled to go to trial in that case on Feb. 22, 2027.

Those alleged scams took more than $250,000 from vulnerable Fresh Yorkers, including first-time homebuyers, immigrants and a personal friend, the OAG noted. Campbell was sentenced to time served in those matters.

It has been revealed that attorney General James alleges Campbell knowingly omitted her December 2025 felony convictions in Westchester County and the 18 Bronx County charges that were already pending at the time she filed the renewal.
