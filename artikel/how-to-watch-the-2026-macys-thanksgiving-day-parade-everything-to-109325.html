The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Makes Major Change to Broadway Lineup.

According to recent reports, the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is typically a platform for Broadway shows to perform standalone medleys, with three to five shows included throughout the event. This year, however, the parade opted to replace these individual performances with one 10-minute “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” medley with performers from nine different shows.

It has been revealed that the Broadway musicals included in the medley are The Book of Mormon, Chicago, Disney’s Aladdin, Hamilton, The Lost Boys, Rent, Richard O’Brien’s The Rocky Horror Show, Schmigadoon! and Wicked.

A coastal storm threatens to bring heavy rain and strong wind gusts of 30 to 45 mph to Upcoming York City on Thanksgiving Day, which has a “significant chance” of grounding the balloons, Dan DePodwin, AccuWeather vice president of forecasting, told The Post ahead of the parade.
