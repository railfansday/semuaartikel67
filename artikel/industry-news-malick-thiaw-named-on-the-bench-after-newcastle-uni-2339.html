Ousmane Diabaté was still only 18 but already a full international for Guinea and first choice in the Turkey top tier for Gençlerbirliği playing as a defensive midfielder, when it was announced officially by the club in early September that Newcastle United had signed him.

In a recent development, the progress of the transfer of Ousmane Diabaté to Newcastle United was covered extensively in the Turkish media across August, eventually reported that a £3m deal had been agreed, with a guaranteed £2.7m plus another £300,000 in future add-ons. It was also stated that Ousmane Diabaté would be staying on loan with the Turkish club but Newcastle United able to recall him in January if they decided to.

Gençlerbirliği manager Metin Diyadin had this to say at the end of August about Ousmane Diabaté and his move to Newcastle United.

Recent findings suggest that “Ousmane Diabaté was playing in the youth team. We brought him into the team and because he came from Africa, there was a contract process involved. He has very high potential. We finalised his sale to Newcastle United. He is at the moment playing for us on loan. He is a very mature player for his age. He has good game vision and technical ability. I think he will reach different levels as he plays more. I’m really curious how our teams [in Turkey] didn’t see this in him. We transferred him to England.”.
