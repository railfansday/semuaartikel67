William Saliba is fit enough to return to Arsenal’s matchday squad for this weekend’s trip to face Newcastle - but will he get the nod?

In a recent development, the imperious French defender has yet to feature for the Gunners this season. He played through a back injury throughout the latter stages of last term and for much of the World Cup.

But the pain eventually grew unmanageable, and he returned to Arsenal immediately after the summer tournament for assessment.

Arsenal decided he should not undergo surgery, instead opting for a lengthy period of rehabilitation at Colney.

Recent findings suggest that but he is now ready to return after months on the sidelines, Mikel Arteta has confirmed. Crucially, though, Saliba is not expected to usurp Ezri Konsa for Saturday’s trip to Newcastle.

“He is ready, he’s in a good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes,” said Arteta on Friday. “He played two games, one behind-closed-doors and one more official. He did really well and he feels in good condition.”.

Saliba featured for Arsenal’s Under-21s earlier this month, playing 45 minutes of a 5-2 defeat of Aston Villa. Watford were the reported opposition for the behind-closed-doors friendly to which Arteta referred.

According to recent reports, in any case, Arteta’s patch on Saliba’s condition implies he is not yet ready to start for the Gunners, implying Konsa will continue to partner Gabriel in the heart of defence. Saliba may be limited to a substitute appearance as he looks to pick up his first competitive Arsenal minutes since the Champions League final.
