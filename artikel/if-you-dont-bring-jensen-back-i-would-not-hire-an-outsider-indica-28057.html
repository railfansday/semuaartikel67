📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that after Jensen Huang's departure from the manufacturer last week, Nvidia is now on the hunt for its next permanent CEO. Speaking in the Q&A section of Nvidia's Q3 2027 earnings call, board chair Harvey Jones states the directors are taking the search "very seriously.".

"We're going to look into internal candidates as well as external candidates" claims Jones. "The board appreciates that we're living in a world where the advanced silicon technologies and national security dynamics are making major changes in our marketplace. Our new CEO will have to navigate those waters very carefully.".

Jones also notes the manufacturer "won't expedite the CEO before finding exactly the right person," but Amazon boss Jeff Bezos seems to think that hiring outside of Nvidia would be a mistake.

"If you don't bring Jensen back, I would not hire an outsider," states Bezos, speaking at the Silicon Valley & The World conference on Tuesday (via Yahoo Finance). "Nvidia is too complicated. I think outside CEOs in complex companies have a vanishingly small chance of succeeding, and so I would always think that's a last resort.".

Nvidia is presently being helmed by CFO Collette Kress as an interim chief, while recent reports have suggested that Huang may make a potential return to the company.

The ex-Nvidia chief said that he would come back "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the organization, and America" after a petition calling for his reinstatement, but Nvidia's board has poured cold water on this idea already.

"The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO," indicates Jones during the earnings call opening statements.
