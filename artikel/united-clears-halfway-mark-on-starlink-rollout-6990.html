United Clears Halfway Mark on Starlink Rollout

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Starlink satellite internet service is now available on more than half of United Airlines’ daily flights, the carrier announced Thursday.

The airline mentioned it accelerated Starlink installations ahead of the Thanksgiving travel period. It expects to fly an average of 500,000 people per day between Nov. 19 and Dec. 1.

Customers can use United’s Starlink Wi-Fi tracker to check whether their upcoming flight is equipped with the service up to three days before their departure.

It has been revealed that united selected Starlink as its onboard wireless internet provider in 2024. It first installed the technology on its regional jets and is now working on its long-haul widebody fleet.

Starlink Wi-Fi is free for MileagePlus members.

Recent findings suggest that united expects to have Starlink on nearly 64% of daily flights by the end of the year. Installations should be complete by the end of 2027, officials said.
