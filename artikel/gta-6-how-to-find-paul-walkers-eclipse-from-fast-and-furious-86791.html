📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that what a stupendous launch for GTA 6, right? The game just became one of the highest-grossing entertainment pieces in the entire industry, and not only are the sales high, but the game is just full of a ton of insane details, mechanics and easter eggs to gush about, especially if you’re a hardcore fan like me.

Speaking of fanboying, if you're a fan of cheesy Hollywood blockbusters, you may have enjoyed the original Fast and Furious movies. If so, I have a surprise for you. The madmen at Rockstar have added Brian’s (Paul Walker) Eclipse to GTA 6 as an Easter Egg, and it is an absolute thing of beauty.

So, if you want to know more about where you can quickly find this car and own it for yourself, then join me for this brief guide for GTA 6, where I’ll discuss the location of the iconic green Eclipse from the first Fast and Furious movie, as well as how you can own it.

Note: I strongly recommend being in Chapter 2 when you try to get this car; you’ll see why.

Industry observers note that more specifically, this is a modified Penumbra, which looks almost identical to the iconic '95 Eclipse from the Fast and Furious franchise. You can find this specialized car at the dead center of Vespucci Islands, located in the heart of Vice City.

Recent findings suggest that the location is shown above, so make your way over; the exact spot is a house with a locked fence gate you can climb over. Either just open the garage or go through the side door to find the modified Penumbra with the unique livery.

And much like the buster that got Dom out of handcuffs in the movie, you’ll have to break open and hotwire the car to steal it for yourself here. It will set off an alarm that will alert nearby residents and eventually the cops, so I strongly advise you to get the car out of there immediately.

Of course, if you’re in Chapter 1, you won’t have an option to own or register another car as Jason. However, once you make it to Chapter 2, you can register a car with Lucia after you first visit a guy named Moses at a garage. This is where I strongly recommend you get this car because the first one you register with Moses is free of charge, as long as the car you bring is low-key.

Industry observers note that the Green ’95 Eclipse Penumbra will work without any challenges, so you can bring it straight back to his garage and get this beauty owned under Lucia. But there’s a slight catch that may rub you the wrong way.

Sources indicate that registering this car as your first free one from Moses means you have to wipe the livery off and replace it with a standard paint job. Don’t panic; it may look like you can’t recover the iconic livery, but you actually can: just visit the Vice Vinyl Mod shop in Vice City, and this garage should give you the option to restore the car's original livery/paint job.
