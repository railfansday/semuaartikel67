Netflix is bringing a massive audience to yet another series that originated on a smaller streamer. MGM+ sci-fi horror series From raked in 1.69 billion minutes viewed from November 2 to 8, easily putting it atop Nielsen’s streaming charts for the week.

In a recent development, the series has generated 5 billion minutes viewed since the first three seasons landed on Netflix on October 19. The fourth season of the series debuted on MGM+ in April and has already been renewed for a fifth and final season, making the timing quite serendipitous for it to catch hold on Netflix.

From led streaming in the first week of November by an extremely wide margin, besting some of the most reliable acquired series, including second place’s Grey’s Anatomy, which racked up 857 million minutes viewed across its 471 episodes that same week. From‘s total is made even more impressive by the fact that there are only 30 episodes available on Netflix.
