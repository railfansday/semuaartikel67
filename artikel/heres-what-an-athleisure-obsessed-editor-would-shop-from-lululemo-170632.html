It has been revealed that as a lululemon stan since my tween years (I’ve been rocking my Aligns for over a decade), I know that the brand’s workout gear and loungewear are worth the money—but that doesn’t necessarily mean I want to pay full price. Thankfully, lululemon’s rare Black Friday sale is here, bringing savings on the brand’s most popular products.

Head Straight to lululemon’s Black Friday Sale.

Sources indicate that if you’re as lululemon-obsessed as I am, you know that the brand pretty much never hosts sales (aside from Black Friday and Latest Year’s)—meaning now is the time to finally invest in the viral Define jacket or stock up on Align leggings (a Cosmo editor favorite) while they’re as low as $59. I also found a handful of gift-worthy stocking stuffers for the lululemon fanatic on your list, like mini belt bags and clippable card pouches. Just be sure to act fast if you see something you like—stock is already selling out.
