Recent findings suggest that with Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launching, some fans are booking time off work, while others are making considerably bigger commitments to secure a few uninterrupted days in Vice City. According to reporting from Task & Purpose, 43 soldiers from the U.S. Army's 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion at Fort Stewart, Georgia, have signed up for a special reenlistment incentive offering a four-day weekend to play GTA 6 when it launched on Nov. 19.

It has been revealed that the catch? They had to commit to another two to six years of military service to get the time off.

The unusual offer has already attracted dozens of volunteers, with some soldiers getting additional financial incentives alongside their extended weekend. Sgt. Jamire Smith, a combat engineer who reenlisted for another six years, told Task & Purpose that he was particularly excited about the game's campaign.

"I'm all campaign. I'm all about the heist," Smith remarked. "I think I have a good 24 hours to waste on it," he joked.

According to recent reports, smith was already considering leaving the Army before his unit helped convince him to stay. His reenlistment also came with a $60,000 bonus, as the Army wanted to retain his specialized skills operating heavy breaching vehicles.

The four-day GTA 6 weekend was the brainchild of Staff Sgt. Brittany Wolfer, a career counselor who wanted to find a more creative way to connect with soldiers considering reenlistment.

Sources indicate that "There is always going to be a disconnect between your senior command and your lowest soldier," Wolfer told Task & Purpose. "So how do you bridge that gap to where that lower soldier still knows that their battalion commander or their senior leadership cares about them and sees what they're interested in.".

Industry observers note that with 43 soldiers now signed up, the 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion has found a pretty unconventional way to make reenlistment more appealing. Rockstar Games might want to take notice, too. Wolfer told Task & Purpose she'd love to see the 3rd Infantry Division's bulldog mascot, Rocky, make an appearance in the game.

"Put Rocky in 'Grand Theft Auto.' Absolutely, that'd be, like, peak in my career, honestly," she remarked.

The incentive comes as GTA 6 fever reaches a new level, with Rockstar's long-awaited sequel earning a 99 Metascore on Metacritic and tying The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest rating in the site's history. The game has also shattered streaming records, attracting more than 3.29 million concurrent viewers on Twitch alone as players and content creators get their first look at Vice City. With reviews praising its sprawling open world, impressive attention to detail, and emotionally driven story, it's safe to say those 43 soldiers have plenty to look forward to during their four-day weekend.
