As teased before the launch, Henry Cavill has built a video game on Googlebook, and it’s called Mustache Protocol.

Patch 11/11: “Mustache Protocol” is now available to play: googlebook.google/henry-cavill.

This 16-bit retro game has three levels and can be played on desktop or mobile. There are “hilarious power-ups and Easter eggs” throughout.

In a recent development, in honor of Movember, your gameplay supports men’s health. From November 11 to 30, for every game played, Google will donate $11 to Movember, up to $500,000. Every play helps drive awareness and funding for men’s mental health, suicide prevention, prostate cancer, and testicular cancer.
