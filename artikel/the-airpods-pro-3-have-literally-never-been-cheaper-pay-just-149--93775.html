TL;DR: As of Nov. 23, you can get the Apple AirPods Pro 3 for $149, down from $249, at Amazon. With a 40% discount, this is the lowest sale price we've ever seen for these popular earbuds.

In a recent development, amazon's Black Friday sale kicked off on Nov. 19, and the retailer isn't playing around. We're seeing major price drops across tons of categories, particularly on Apple products. The MacBook Air is finally back under $1,000, Apple Watch discounts have finally landed, and the AirPods Max 2 headphones are down to just $419. However, the best Black Friday Apple deal so far has to be on the noise-cancelling AirPods Pro 3 wireless earbuds.

Right now, you can get the Apple AirPods Pro 3 for $149, down from $249, at Amazon. That's a 40% discount, or $100 in savings, and the lowest price we've ever seen on these premium buds. In the past, we've seen Amazon discount the AirPods Pro 3 to $179 or even $169, but the newly introduced $149 price puts them on par with the regular AirPods 5. That's right, the AirPods Pro 3 are now the same price as the base-level AirPods 5, at least, at Amazon. (As of this writing, Walmart has price matched the $149 price at Amazon.) Now they're $100 less, which makes them an easy gift for the Apple person on your list (or for you, no judgment).
