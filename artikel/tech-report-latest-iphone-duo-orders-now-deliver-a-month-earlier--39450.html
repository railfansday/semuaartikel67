It has been revealed that interested in getting an iPhone Duo before Christmas? You might be in luck, as Apple’s online store delivery estimates just got pulled forward by a full month, with December delivery now expected.

Apple seems to be finding success in ramping up its iPhone Duo production.

Within minutes of iPhone Duo pre-orders opening on October 16, delivery estimates swiftly slipped into November, then December, and after about a half hour of orders, the earliest you could get an iPhone Duo was late January.

According to recent reports, apple did deliver some Duo units earlier than the initial estimates. But the late January timeframe remained in place for all newly introduced iPhone Duo orders until now.

But as spotted by Max Weinbach, latest iPhone Duo orders from Apple just got bumped up by about a month. Now, delivery estimates are showing December 16-23 for customers in the US and elsewhere.

Latest Duo orders seemed to have been pulled forward ~a month They must be scaling manufacturing rapidly pic.twitter.com/VFkCmCoRLF.

This is a great indicator that Apple has been able to accelerate production. It’s also likely that there was a slowdown in orders due to the late January estimate putting off prospective buyers, thus making it easier for the manufacturer to catch up on backorders.

According to recent reports, while we still don’t know sales numbers, the iPhone Duo by all appearances has been a hit already. And with iOS 27.3 adding a face unlock feature soon, one of the biggest critiques reviewers had is largely being mitigated.
