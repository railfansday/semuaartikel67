According to recent reports, WELLESLEY, Mass., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Park Edge Advisors, LLC ("Park Edge") today revealed intends to launch the Park Edge Adaptive Riskâ¢ ETF (NYSE Arca: KISS) (the "Fund") with Twin Oak ETF Tech giant ("Twin Oak"). The Fund is expected to begin trading the week of December 14, 2026. Before launch, eligible investors can contribute portfolios of appreciated securities in exchange for Fund shares through a Section 351 exchange. The exchange window is open now.

According to recent reports, the Fund is designed to sit at the core of a portfolio: the savings an investor is counting on for retirement, kept separate from more speculative holdings. It holds equity securities, gold-related investments and digital asset-related investments and raises or lowers each position as market conditions change.

Sources indicate that park Edge built the strategy around three mistakes it sees investors make with their core savings. They assume risk is constant across all macroeconomic conditions. It's not. Risk clusters. This leads to large losses during bear markets because of maintaining static allocations to risk. Then, as a result, investors spend much of the next bull market just getting back to breakeven. A disciplined process for adapting risk to changing conditions can separate the signal from the noise.

The Fund is designed to follow a set of rules instead. It seeks to choose exposures that suit the current economic backdrop. It aims to reduce overall risk when its market signals turn defensive. And it is designed to size each position up or down based on that asset's own trend and volatility.

According to recent reports, "Many investors have done well in stocks and are now focused on their go-forward returns yet are hampered by embedded gains when it comes to adjusting risk," said Richard Lowrie, Managing Partner and Chief Investment Officer of Park Edge Advisors. "This Fund gives them a disciplined way to stay invested to a process for adapting portfolio risk to conditions, and the 351 exchange lets eligible clients exchange appreciated holdings for shares of the fund.".

"Park Edge has built a disciplined, rules-based process, and that kind of strategy translates naturally to an ETF," said Zach Wainwright, Chief Executive Officer of Twin Oak ETF Tech giant. "We're glad to bring it to a wider set of investors through a transparent, exchange-listed vehicle.".
