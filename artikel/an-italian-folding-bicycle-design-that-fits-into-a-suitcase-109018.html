This T&C Pocket Bici is a folding bicycle made by Tresoldi & Casiraghi, an Italian metalworking firm, in 1963. Unlike the folding bicycles of today that split in half, the components here all fold into the racetrack-shaped frame. The whole thing then fits within its own suitcase. (Brompton bike in the photo for scale.).

Recent findings suggest that the bike's origin story is a bit murky; one auction site claims it was created specifically for the Chinese market, and that 2,500 units were sold. In contrast, BikeFlip says a Chinese group placed an initial order for 2,000 units, but that after T&C produced the first 1,000 units, payment never materialized and the project was scrapped.

Notably that channel is Chinese, so it appears at least some of the production units landed in the originally intended market.

Those wheels are even smaller than the small 16 inch Brompton wheels. Bike Friday managed to package 20 inch wheels into a suitcase. The physics of a larger wheel provide a better ride. I can't even ride 16 inch wheels. These look like scooter wheels.
