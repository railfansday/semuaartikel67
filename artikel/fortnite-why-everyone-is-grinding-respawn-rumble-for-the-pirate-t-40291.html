Players are opening an ungodly number of chests in Fortnite in the hopes of winning big with Pirate Shop Access tokens, which make everything in the Item Shop completely free for two weeks—once in 2026, and once in 2027.

Sources indicate that it's a complete roll of the dice... if the dice had 137,500 sides. But there is a way to make the grind a little less tedious. As reported by Fortnite Leaks, you can now get the token and all other rewards in Respawn Rumble.

In a recent development, it's unclear if this is intentional, so it may be patched out soon, but the reason the community is celebrating and jumping headfirst into the mode is because you respawn infinitely, allowing you to open chests again and again and again without worry. That means you can retreat into the zone and stomach the damage for even more loot.

There are no special animations, and there's a bug where rewards are displayed with a grey chest icon and the text 'PH' (placeholder), but once you return to the lobby, you'll see what exactly the reward was.

According to recent reports, while this does make farming chests a lot easier, as you no longer need to worry about dying, there is a slight catch. One of the big benefits of playing Respawn Rumble is that you can have a squad of up to eight players, in theory increasing your odds even further when opening chests. Typically, if one person in your squad gets a reward, everyone does. Not in Respawn Rumble.

Sources indicate that according to Twitter user @SoundOriented, "you *ONLY* get the treasure for your Respawn Rumble fills in YOUR squad. The other 4 people do NOT get the reward." In so many words, only members of the predetermined lobby squad get the rewards, meaning that your odds aren't strictly better than in standard battle royale. Still, pilfering through chests at a rapid pace means rolling the dice a whole lot more than you otherwise could.

Recent findings suggest that it should be stated, since there's a lot of confusion about how RNG works, that the token isn't guaranteed if you open 137,500 chests. Imagine trying to guess a random number between 1 and 137,500, but every time you make another guess, the other person has changed the number they're thinking of. So, while you can open a lot more chests through Respawn Rumble, that doesn't mean there's an invisible tracker bringing you closer and closer to finding the Pirate Shop Access.
