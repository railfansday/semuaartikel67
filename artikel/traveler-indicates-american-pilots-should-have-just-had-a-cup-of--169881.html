Recent findings suggest that there’s no denying that traveling by air can be stressful, and that it’s extremely frustrating when things go wrong. Furthermore, not everyone is a frequent traveler, and understands the ins and outs of how the airline industry functions.

Recent findings suggest that so that brings us to a complaint from last night from an American Airlines passenger. The complaint is so incredibly off base in the first place. But what’s even worse is that when this person is confronted with reality, he just doubles down and keeps going. This person deserves to be roasted, and if you ask me, should be banned from the airline.

Late last night, a social media user posted a picture of two airline pilots on X, with the following caption:.

I won’t be seeing my family this Thanksgiving because a couple pilots couldn’t drink a cup of coffee and get us home. They clocked out in two hours, but we had 3 hr flight. Thanks @AmericanAir.

In a recent development, I won't be seeing my family this Thanksgiving because a couple pilots couldn't drink a cup of coffee and get us home. They clocked out in two hours, but we had 3 hr flight. Thanks @AmericanAir pic.twitter.com/q1I8pjsM3w.

Industry observers note that I can totally appreciate how frustrating it is when a flight is canceled, but this is actually very simple — federal regulations dictate the maximum number of hours that pilots can fly, and that’s for good reason. The airline industry operates with the goal of putting safety above all else, and pilot fatigue has been a contributing factor to many accidents over the years.

I totally get it, you want to be home for Thanksgiving, and it sucks things went wrong, but those are the facts. This is federal law, and on top of that, we should want pilots to be well rested, rather than just saying “keep working, just have another cup of coffee.”.

According to recent reports, I’d like to point out what makes this even worse — this was a flight out of Miami last night, and the airport had shut down due to a ground stop, because a FedEx MD-11 ran off the runway. That was an absolute pain for all passengers out of the airport, and I can assure you that American also wasn’t happy about that happening, and the impact it had on its operations.

All would be forgiven if this traveler either just didn’t say anything further, or acknowledged “ah, I wasn’t aware of that.” Instead, he has had dozens and dozens of posts, just going on and on and on.

He posted about how American claimed the cancelation was the airport’s fault, but insisted “it’s actually these pilots fault,” and suggested “pilots will lie to save money for their manufacturer.”.
