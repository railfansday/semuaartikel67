Tech Report: Julia Child’s Purple-Hued Cambridge Home Sells Just Days After Listing

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that in today’s real estate market, price cuts are increasingly common. But Julia Child’s former Cambridge home just bucked the trend, selling mere days after listing—and fetching above the asking price. The legendary TV chef’s longtime Massachusetts residence hit the market on September 23 for $6.475 million. By October 2, it had found a buyer. The sale officially closed on October 29, per Zillow. In the end, the six-bedroom Cape Cod–style house traded hands for $6.525 million, $50,000 above asking.

Child and her husband, Paul, lived in the house for decades, starting in 1961, after Paul retired from his job in the federal government. The estate is also where Child shot several popular TV shows, among them In Julia’s Kitchen with Master Chefs and Julia and Jacques Cooking at Home.

It has been revealed that painted a muted shade of purple, the 6,300-square-foot home sits on a quarter-acre lot just a few blocks from Harvard Yard. Dating to 1889, the residence at one time featured a custom kitchen designed especially for Child, with pegboard walls, a six-burner industrial Garland range, and maple countertops a few inches taller than average to suit her 6’3” frame.

It has been revealed that in the wake of Paul’s 1994 death, Child continued to live in the residence until 2001, when she donated the property to Smith College, her alma mater. (The Massachusetts institution sold the home in 2002 for $2.35 million, per Zillow.) The famous kitchen, meanwhile, was meticulously deconstructed—down to its linoleum flooring—and rebuilt in D.C.’s National Museum of American History, where it remains on display today.

It has been revealed that since then, the fresh owners have revamped the three-story home’s kitchen, which now sports marble countertops, a metallic backsplash, Poggenpohl cabinetry, and a Sub-Zero refrigerator. The rest of the residence is equipped with light-filled interiors, hardwood floors, and eco-friendly additions like solar panels.

This summer, the Cambridge City Council passed a proposal to install a historical marker on the estate’s grounds, according to Boston.com.

By chance, another Child-linked property recently traded hands as well, albeit for a discount. In August, a Santa Barbara ranch where she filmed the 12-episode PBS series Dinner at Julia’s listed for just under $22 million. It sold on October 16 for $19 million.
