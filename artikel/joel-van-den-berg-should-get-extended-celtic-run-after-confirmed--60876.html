Joel van den Berg should finally be in line for a chance in Celtic's first team with Mika Baur now sidelined for the next 10 weeks.

Baur has been one of Celtic's best players so far this season since his £4.5million summer switch from Paderborn.

The 22-year-old earned a maiden Germany call-up last month and has bagged four goals in his first 19 Hoops appearances.

Sources indicate that celtic have been handed a huge blow ahead of their clash against St. Mirren, with official news that Baur is set to be sidelined with ankle ligament damage for the next two-and-a-half months.

Fellow summer signing, Van den Berg, must surely now be in with a chance of breaking into Martin O'Neill's side after a tough start to life at Parkhead.

The 19-year-old arrived as a highly-rated box-to-box midfielder from PSV at the end of the window, but is yet to even make his Celtic debut.

Celtic's flurry of late summer signings saw them fork out a reported fee of up to £3.4m on versatile midfielder Van den Berg.

The Netherlands youth international made six first-team appearances for PSV last season, while also registering 10 goal contributions in 26 Dutch second-tier games for their reserve team.

He joined Celtic with the hope of instantly breaking into O'Neill's side, but suffered an injury in training in September and first appeared in green and white for their B team earlier this month.

His Eerste Divisie stats from last season highlight clear similarities to Baur's playstyle, with impressive chances created (1.65), duels won (5.62) and ball recoveries (5.54) per 90 minutes.

Van den Berg is a similarly diminutive, attack-minded, deep-lying playmaker who should be able to complement Callum McGregor's aggressive, defensive nature, much like Baur has since joining.

The German's injury should clear the path for the teenager to finally become a regular under O'Neill in the next few weeks, potentially starting this weekend against St. Mirren.

O'Neill addressed the media ahead of Saturday's Premiership clash, offering encouragement that Van den Berg could finally make his senior debut for Celtic because of Baur's absence.

It has been revealed that he said: "Unfortunately he’s [Van den Berg] not in the European squad, but in the next couple of weeks, between now and Christmas time, I’d hope to see him make his mark. As I said to him yesterday, ‘When you get in, it’s really up to you.’”.

It has been revealed that "There might be some of the players, like young Van den Berg, who might come through and absolutely take off. That would obviously be great for us. And that’s, I suppose, really what we’re hoping for, that they can do.“It’s a matter of them getting in and having a chance. Now that Mika’s out of the team, it is a chance for someone to step up.”.

Van den Berg certainly looks in good stead to make his Celtic bow soon, and some strong performances could finally kickstart his career at the club following a slow start.
