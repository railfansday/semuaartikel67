All GTA 6 pre-orders come with a free month of GTA+, which nets you some in-game bonuses like a free vehicle, garage, and higher selling prices.

But before you excitedly claim your freebie, it’s essential you understand auto-renew will be enabled.

This means unless you disable it, you’ll be charged the monthly price of $7.99/£6.99/€7.99 as soon as your free month is over.

Once you’ve claimed your free trial, from the PS5’s home screen scroll left to the PS Store and select ‘...’ > Subscriptions:.

You’ll see all of your active subscriptions listed.

Select ‘GTA+’ and press ‘Cancel Subscription’:.

Don’t worry, you’ll still get to enjoy the entirety of your free month, this will just prevent you from being charged.

If you want to keep GTA+, then of course you don’t need to do this. Just be aware you will get charged every month. You can also re-activate your subscription if you choose to do so by repeating the steps above:.

Hopefully this heads-up helps anyway.

To be fair, the PS5 does tell you about this when activating your free month, but it’s GTA 6 release day and I suspect some of you will be giddy and not necessarily in the mood for the fine print.
