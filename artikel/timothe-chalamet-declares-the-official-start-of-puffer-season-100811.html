Timothée Chalamet Declares the Official Start of Puffer Season

Like any true Upcoming Yorker, Timothée Chalamet has built himself a deep bench of cold-weather staples. We’re talking puffers on puffers on puffers—hailing from The North Face, Supreme, Thug Club and beyond—paired with all manner of boots, including Visvim lace-ups, Red Wing moc toes, and multiple pairs of Timbs (of both the OG Wheat and custom Chrome Hearts varieties.) For all his fashion-guy flourishes, Chalamet’s winter outfits generally have the chops to handle a brick New York sidewalk come February. So yesterday, even though it was still a balmy 68 degrees in NYC, it was no surprise to see Chalamet cheering on his beloved Knicks at Madison Square Garden in yet another excellent down-filled jacket and blizzard-ready boots.

In a recent development, sitting courtside, the Dune star wore a combination familiar to just about anyone who’s endured winter on the East Coast. The jacket was a dusty pink number from Goldwin, the cult Japanese outdoors brand founded back in 1950. It cuts a sharp silhouette despite packing some serious down insulation, and the windproof, water-resistant nylon is designed to ward off rain, sleet, and snow. The muted exterior has a subtle sheen—not quite Cam’ron pink, but certainly not your average black puffer. Over the past couple years, pink has become one of Chalamet’s signature colors: Last January, he paired a bubblegum Telfar puffer with an Arc’teryx toque and a Chanel purse like it was no big deal; less than a month later, he popped up at the Berlin Film Festival in head-to-toe pastel Chrome Hearts. This time out, he left the handbag at home but topped things off with a Knicks championship hat.

According to recent reports, and then there were the boots. At first glance, they could pass for a pair of Uggs, thanks to the familiar shape and cozy suede uppers. (It wouldn’t have been the first time he wore a pair of the Aussie surfer classics courtside.) But these pull-ons were, in fact, from menswear favorite Merrell, built for some truly cold and ugly weather. The outer suede is water-resistant, the lining is insulated, and the rubber outsoles boast some serious traction. Plus, the very gorp-y utility cord at the heel lets you cinch them extra tight. Like the advanced-level dresser he is, Chalamet tucked his baggy black pants into the boots, making sure his footwear got a chance to really shine.

It has been revealed that once again, Chalamet has turned practical winter clothes into an A-list look. While a pink puffer might not be every guy’s idea of cold-weather dressing, there’s something to be mentioned for a winter coat with a little personality. Was celebrity row’s resident heartthrob a few weeks early with this ensemble? Sure, but when that first serious snowfall hits, he’ll be extra ready.
