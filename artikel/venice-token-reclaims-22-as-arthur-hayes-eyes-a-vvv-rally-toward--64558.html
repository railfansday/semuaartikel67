Venice Token reclaims $22 as Arthur Hayes eyes a VVV rally toward $40

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The utility and access token for the generative platform Venice AI, Venice Token [VVV], has rallied 7.22% in the past 24 hours. It has climbed 17.2% over the past week, putting in a strong performance at a time when most of the rest of crypto was facing selling pressure.

Its daily trading volume has also leapt by 54%, to $54.45 million, according to CoinMarketCap data.

In a post on X, crypto exchange BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes wrote that he expected a Venice Token rally. Three other altcoins, namely Ethereum, NEAR, and ZCash, were also mentioned.

He set a price target of $40 for VVV, as well as $2,800 for ETH, $1,800 for ZEC, and $6 for NEAR.

It has been revealed that in May, he had grouped ZEC with Hyperliquid and called for a sizeable rally for both tokens. ZEC nearly reached his $1,000 target shortly thereafter.

According to recent reports, on the weekly chart, Venice Token presented an attractive buying opportunity. After the strong rally from $9.81 to $34.61 earlier this year, the altcoin saw a correction, reaching as low as $16.73 a week ago.

Sources indicate that however, the weekly trading session did not close below $19.28, the 61.8% Fibonacci retracement level. The bullish reaction after the sweep of this retracement level suggested bulls were beginning to take control of the market.

Industry observers note that there was a long way to go, and the wider market sentiment could affect VVV negatively. Based on the evidence at hand, the defense of the $19.8 demand zone indicated bullishness.

The immediate target is the $34 highs, and $40 might be possible, but the route there is unclear.

In a recent development, the 4-hour chart displayed a series of lower highs and lower lows in recent weeks, a downtrend. The latest lower high at $19.72 was flipped to support on Monday.

Furthermore, the altcoin has managed to climb past $22.14 and breached the 50% higher timeframe retracement level, too.

Recent findings suggest that swing traders and long-term investors can look to buy Venice Token. A breakdown below $20.16 will flip the H4 bias bearish, while a drop below $16.74 will signal a deeper correction on the weekly timeframe.
