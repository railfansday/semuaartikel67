Queen's Domain's melee combat isn't for everyone: Luckily there's an in-game cheat book you can use to get a gun (and it doesn't bork your achievements cheevos or nothin').

First person King's Field/Metroid Prime-like Queen's Domain has rapidly become a top-two shortlister for my personal game of the year, but it can be a prickly, difficult-to-recommend game, particularly in its first 30 minutes or so before you get the game-defining Endu airdash sword.

I'm of a mind with news writer Josh Wolens that the contrast between pre- and post-Endu gameplay really helps sell the game's power curve, but our coworkers Andy Chalk and Morgan Park both bounced off Queen's Domain's admittedly sluggish melee combat.

Industry observers note that there isn't a difficulty selection in Queen's Domain, but there is something close to it: A "witch's grimoire" where you can input codes to receive unique items. Most of these are references to streamers, content creators, or other indie developers.

While referential, they mesh surprisingly well with Queen's Domain's aesthetic, and I don't think they threaten its overall very deliberate sense of progression. What's more, I say playing the game at all is better than avoiding this little cheat code book out of principle.

You can enter the code "hempie" (a reference to the vtuber of the same name) to receive a Soviet SVT-40 semi-automatic battle rifle. A bit incongruous for the medieval fantasy setting, perhaps, but I think it makes for an early game boon while not completely upending Queen's Domain's balance. Most of my preferred later game weapons have been ranged options⁠—the code just gets you one much earlier than you could otherwise. The hempie rifle has already proven an early favorite for Queen's Domain speedrunners like current world record holder Wilbun84, whose video also demonstrates where to find the grimoire.

Recent findings suggest that on the starting beach, you'll notice a dead tree to your right while facing inland. There is a secret compartment on the other side of the tree, facing away from you: Press the "use" button while close to the tree to reveal the secret compartment (similar to the secret doors scattered throughout Queen's Domain). If you'd like to get even crazier with the grimoire, Destructoid has a full list of all the codes you can input with their corresponding rewards. The grimoire has a limited number of uses, so you can't get everything right away, but you can unlock more uses as the game goes on.
