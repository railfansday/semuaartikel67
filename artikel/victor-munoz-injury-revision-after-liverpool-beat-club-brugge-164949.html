Andoni Iraola notes he remains optimistic over Victor Munoz’s injury after the winger was forced off during Liverpool’s 3-0 win over Club Brugge in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

Munoz, who was signed in a £34.5 million deal from Osasuna in the summer, injured his knee in the closing stages of the first half.

The 23-year-old looked in significant discomfort but was able to limp off the pitch without needing a stretcher.

Two goals from Alexander Isak, plus a strike from Florian Wirtz, sealed Liverpool’s victory against Club Brugge to make it four wins in five Champions League games this season.

However, Iraola could now be without Munoz for Liverpool’s next Premier League fixture away to Everton on Sunday.
