According to recent reports, NETANYA, Israel, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Tessera Defense and Homeland Security Inc. ("Tessera" or the "Firm") (OTC: HLSQ) today unveiled that on November 10, 2026 it received written notice from NYSE Regulation that the staff had determined to commence proceedings to delist the Company's common stock from NYSE American LLC ("NYSE American"), and that trading in the common stock on NYSE American was suspended immediately. NYSE Regulation determined that the Company is no longer suitable for listing pursuant to Section 1003(f)(v) of the NYSE American Company Guide due to the low selling price of the common stock.

Under NYSE American's procedures, the Manufacturer had a right to a review of the staff's determination by the Listings Qualifications Panel of the Committee for Review of the Board of Directors of NYSE American by submitting a written request on or before November 17, 2026. The Company did not request review. NYSE American has stated that it will apply to the Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") to delist the common stock upon completion of all applicable procedures.

In a recent development, the Company's common stock is quoted on the Pink Limited Market operated by OTC Markets Group Inc. under the symbol "HLSQ". The Company intends to apply for quotation of its common stock on the OTCQB Venture Market. There can be no assurance that the application will be approved or that an active trading market for the common stock will develop or be maintained.

The Manufacturer is consulting with outside counsel regarding an application to list its common stock on a national securities exchange if and when the Company meets the applicable initial listing standards. The Company does not currently meet the minimum share price requirements for initial listing on a national securities exchange. There can be no assurance that the Company will meet the applicable listing standards, that it will submit an application or that any application will be approved.

The suspension and delisting do not change the Firm's obligation to file periodic and current reports with the SEC, and the Company intends to continue to file those reports.

Further details are included in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed today with the SEC.
