Lowe’s Home Improvement grew the old-fashioned way, working its way up from a single, small, family-owned North Carolina general store founded in 1921. But the Lowe’s hardware store empire is plenty substantial these days. A tool-filled Lowe’s superstore is now about as big as a Upcoming York City block. The focus is mostly on appliances and tools for the home DIYer, rather than large building contractors. I like to pop in for grill and griddle tools or just some propane. But you can also buy a pre-finished door complete with the frame—or the lumber, sanders, routers, and saws you’d use to make your own door, including the hinges and the wood stain. It’s easy to get lost in the endless aisles if you love the smell of plaster and wood dust, but it’s easier to find the best Lowe’s deals on the website. This roundup includes Lowe’s daily deals, a promo code, and a Lowe's offer code for 40% off appliances, plus info on the Lowe’s rewards program and military discounts.

Industry observers note that with winter rapidly approaching and temperatures dropping, now is a great time to stay indoors and tackle the home improvement projects and upgrades you’ve been putting off. Shop the Lowe’s Black Friday sale to save on upcoming projects and home upgrades. You’ll find deals on appliances, tools, holiday decorations, grills, bathroom essentials, kitchen upgrades, and more.

It has been revealed that with Lowe’s Deals, you can get up to 35% off select major appliances. During this Lowe’s Deals sales period, you can save up to $2,000 when you bundle select KitchenAid appliances and an additional $100 on select Whirlpool and Maytag laundry pairs. Plus, there are hundreds of select and best-selling appliances like refrigerators, dishwashers, microwaves, ranges, freezers, and more.

It’s no secret that kitchen and bathroom remodels are the most costly home improvement projects any homeowner can take on. For those bigger (and much more costly) renovations, Lowe’s is helping you save more the more you spend. This means you can get up to an additional: 10% off when you spend $750-$1,999, 20% off when you spend $2,000-$4,999, and 30% off when you spend over $5,000. This Lowe’s savings on kitchen and bath program is ideal for homeowners doing a complete room reno, potentially saving thousands of dollars over the course of the project.

According to recent reports, if you’re planning major work this year or just like to putter around, it’s probably worth picking up a MyLowe’s Rewards Program membership. Each eligible purchase accrues points that pay out in $5 increments of in-store credit at Lowe’s. Like many loyalty programs, you get more benefits the more you spend at the store, including free shipping on all purchases, including small ones, member-only discounts, and point boosters for better discounts.

There are three membership tiers, depending on what you buy. One large appliance might already qualify you for the highest tier of rewards, at which point you might as well. These are the three tiers, based on the total cost of what you’ve bought at Lowe’s in the past year. Bronze ($0-$499): $35 minimum for free shipping; 1 point per $1 spent. Silver ($500-$1,999): Free shipping; 1.25 points per $1 spent. Gold ($2,000+): Free shipping; 1.5 points per $1 spent.

According to recent reports, for contractors, there’s a separate MyLowe’s Pro Rewards Program that offers Lowe’s in-store credit in $1 increments for every 100 points earned. (Points expire in half-yearly increments, on June 30 and December 31.) Benefits include member-only deals, volume discounts, free standard shipping in the contiguous United States, and a 20% paint discount after an annual spend of $3,000 or more. Other benefits include analytics and spend reports, online order quoting, and purchase authorization for specified crew members.

All verified active military, veterans, and military spouses are eligible for a 10% discount on full-price items—meaning items don’t have to be on sale to qualify. This is available through what Lowe’s calls the Everyday Military Discount program. When you enroll, you also get an immediate upgrade to Silver status in the MyLowe’s Rewards program, which means more rewards points per dollar and free shipping on all orders in the contiguous United States.

Get a free Silver status upgrade instantly when you enroll in our Military Discount program and validate through ID.me. MyLowe’s Rewards Silver status members receive free standard shipping (excludes AK and HI) and earn more points per dollar.

Another way to get 5% off items you need regularly is to set up a Lowe’s subscription. This is good for items ranging from cleaning products, air and water filters, lawn care, pet goods, plumbing, and batteries. Shipping is free, and delivery happens automatically.

Recent findings suggest that you may not realize that Lowe’s has a Lowest Price Guarantee policy, which means that if you find a lower price on an identical item, Lowe’s will match the competitor’s price. All you need to do is chat with a Lowe’s employee in-store or call 1-877-465-6937. Plus, the Lowest Price Guarantee includes the price of the item(s) plus shipping or delivery costs.
