📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A Wisconsin woman will spend the next several years in prison after she admitted to abusing a patient with disabilities by repeatedly hitting him with his favorite toy.

Toyo Perez pleaded guilty to four counts of patient abuse and five counts of abusing an individual at risk in August. Three months after Perez, 50, entered her plea, Rock County Circuit Judge Derrick Grubb sentenced her to serve 13 years in prison. Once she is no longer incarcerated, she will serve an additional nine years of extended supervision.

The investigation into Perez’s abuse began in January, when members of the Rock County Sheriff’s Office were shown a video that captured her hitting an adult patient with disabilities, according to WKOW’s courtroom report.

After authorities became aware of the abuse, detectives discovered more than a dozen videos documenting similar acts of abuse. The Rock County District Attorney’s Office said that the footage demonstrated Perez striking and pushing the man over and over, while she was also seen twisting the man’s ear and beating him with a hard plastic weighted baby doll — what prosecutors described as his “favorite” toy.

“She was there to care for him,” prosecutor Verity Altenberger told the judge in court. “There is no ‘why.’ This was not one bad day. This was multiple times per day, for multiple days, over weeks.”.

The prosecutor added that Perez’s abuse was “pure cruelty.”.

Meanwhile, Perez’s defense attorney argued that her treatment of the victim was a result of stress.

Law enforcement said that the victim is an adult male who has significant autism and developmental delays, with an approximate cognitive level similar to that of a 1-year-old child.

Family members mentioned they initially believed the victim remained calm around Perez because they shared a special connection, but prosecutors said they later learned that his subdued state was a result of the abuse and an instilled sense of fear.

After Perez pleaded guilty, the state requested a sentence of 24 years in prison while the defense requested she simply serve probation time.

Once the judge determined Perez’s sentence, he spoke to her about the gravity of her crimes.
