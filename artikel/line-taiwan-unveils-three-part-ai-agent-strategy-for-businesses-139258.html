📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

At LINE BIZ CONVERGE 2026 on November 26, 2026, LINE Taiwan mentioned it will pursue a three-part AI agent strategy spanning consumer assistance, brand service and merchant operations, building on more than 3.6 million official accounts to move customer interactions beyond answers toward completed tasks.

Sources indicate that the strategy connects consumer access, brand fulfillment and merchant decision-making. It also places customer-facing agents within a broader product framework covering advertising, commerce and data, tying service interactions to subsequent business activity.

Sources indicate that the planned Consumer Agent would handle everyday needs and connect users to services directly inside chats. Brand Agent, delivered through official accounts, would integrate brand service workflows and complete customer tasks. LINE Biz AI is the Business Agent, intended to combine data insights with operational recommendations and execution support for merchants.

In a recent development, for brands, the upcoming AI agent platform will sit inside OA Plus, the administrative backend for official accounts. Businesses will be able to choose appointment, sales or customer-service agent roles and describe their requirements in natural language, with AI guiding the setup. They will also be able to combine basic and advanced functions according to their operating needs.

LINE Biz AI is designed to use the customer context created by those interactions. Within OA Plus, it would combine appointment records, product or service preferences, official-account interactions and membership information, automatically tag customer profiles and identify audiences. For a nail salon, that could mean recognizing a customer who has not booked recently, recommending a return-visit offer and sending messages when the merchant directs it to do so.

Industry observers note that LINE first detailed the official-account platform in an October 13, 2026 announcement, which said brands could connect their own point-of-sale, customer relationship management, membership, reservation and scheduling systems alongside standard templates. LINE planned to begin brand cooperation and validation at the end of 2026 and targeted a formal launch in the first quarter of 2027.

LINE Biz AI has a separate, staged rollout. It became available on the LINE Biz-Solutions website in November 2026 to provide AI Q&A about products and services. LINE plans to bring it into OA Plus in the first half of 2027 for operational decision and action support, then gradually integrate AI capabilities across products in the second half of 2027 so it can assist across services.

In a recent development, the surrounding products are intended to connect agent interactions to transactions. The Chat Ad format in LINE’s LAP advertising system lets a consumer enter a brand conversation after clicking an ad and ask about a product or service before deciding whether to add the official account as a friend. A separate Cross Buy and LINE Gift test uses previous purchasing and gifting behavior to anticipate future gifting demand, generate ads from LINE Gift product assets and direct consumers back to LINE Gift to complete a purchase.

In a recent development, physical stores provide another entry point. With LINE Touch, a shopper can tap a phone to open a brand’s LINE MINI App, collect coupons and redeem them in-store without downloading a separate app. LINE reports that a FamilyMart trial combining the two products reached a maximum coupon redemption rate of 91%.

Industry observers note that for campaign planning, LINE Data Hub is designed to show audience profiles before ads run and use campaign results afterward to inform subsequent prepares. AI Persona uses semantic analysis across LINE cross-service behavior labels, official-account friend data and user research, allowing brands to explore audience characteristics in natural language and receive suggestions for messaging and ad creative. LINE also plans partnerships under its DMP Alliance data program with Invoice Monster operator Chun Shu Technology, ECPay DATA and uTagGo to add spending, payment, ecommerce and mobility data sources.

Industry observers note that together, those products describe an operating loop in which an ad starts a conversation, an agent completes a service task and the resulting customer information informs the next action. With the main brand and merchant capabilities scheduled in stages through 2027, the practical test is whether those releases form a shared operating loop across OA Plus and LINE’s commerce and data tools, rather than a set of isolated chat features.
