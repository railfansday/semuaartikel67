Warning: Chef Brooke Williamson is about to have you gobbling for more as she previews her delicious Thanksgiving feast with boyfriend Bobby Flay.

“This year, I said I wanted to make the turkey, which is not always the case,” Williamson, 48, exclusively shared with Us while discussing her recent partnership with luxury appliance brand Thermador. “We’re doing an Italian theme this year, and Bobby always does a themed Thanksgiving. Last year, we did Southeast Asian Thanksgiving, and everything had Southeast Asian flavors.”.

She continued, “I think that having a theme makes it kind of creative and fun, and turns those traditional dishes into something slightly more unexpected.”.

According to Williamson, Flay, 61, loves mashed potatoes while she savors stuffing and makes sure the traditional dish is on the table in November.

“I think we’re both really flexible,” she said. “But if I want to make a squash dish, then I do feel like some greens and some vegetables are necessary. Bobby’s more of a starch and protein kind of person, so I will probably add a couple of extra veggies to the table.”.

While Williamson joked that her boyfriend wakes up hungry every day, Thanksgiving is extra special for the pair as they both love food and creating special memories in the kitchen.

“Thanksgiving is the ultimate sort of fall, winter holiday in the sense that it’s all about the food. It’s about the family, but there’s less pressure than, like, say, Christmas or Hanukkah with gifts,” she said. “It’s about the food. It’s about the community.”.

In between cooking and eating, Williamson tries her best to make time to explore the latest and greatest in kitchen gadgets and appliances.

While visiting Thermador’s showroom in Irvine, California, this month, Williamson was able to check out the brand’s latest innovations, including its next-generation freedom columns refrigeration collection.

Recent findings suggest that “They’re very innovative in the way that they consider elevating your kitchen experience,” Williamson told Us about Thermador. “[This showroom] really showcases the best of these brands and what Thermador has to offer. … I get to cook on some beautiful ranges and out of some beautiful refrigerators that are all state-of-the- art.”.

Discovering new flavors, products and innovations are just some of the many reasons why Williamson is so grateful to have the life she has as a chef and best-selling author.

Recent findings suggest that “I think a lot of it is fresh and unexpected in the best way,” she stated about her life today. “My life looks very different than it did a few years ago. But then again, it looks different every year. That’s the funny thing about the restaurant industry is that any day moving forward is unexpected. So this is just sort of an amplified version of unexpected every day, and it’s exciting, and it’s fun, and it’s full of joy.”.
