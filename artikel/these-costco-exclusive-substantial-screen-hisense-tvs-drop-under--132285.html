Black Friday is bringing some substantial discounts on massive-screen TVs this year, particularly for Costco members looking to upgrade without spending well over $1,000. From November 26 through November 30, the retailer is dropping the 100-inch Hisense QD7 to $999 from $1,799, an $800 discount, while the 85-inch Hisense U65 Pro will also cost $999, down from $1,299. Both models are Costco exclusives, and come with a three-year Allstate protection initiative, bringing the total coverage to five years when combined with the manufacturer’s warranty.

Recent findings suggest that for anyone who puts sheer size first, the 100-inch QD7 offers an enormous viewing area for movies, sports, and gaming, with a 4K Hi-QLED display that uses Quantum Dot technology to produce richer, more vibrant colors. It's particularly appealing for a spacious living room or dedicated entertainment area, though you'll want to measure your wall and seating distance before committing to a screen this large. As for performance, the 85-inch QD7 offers a useful reference point, since CNET named it one of the best 85-inch TVs of 2026—and the two models share the same features apart from screen size.

The 85-inch U65 Pro, meanwhile, gives up 15 inches of screen space but adds Mini-LED backlighting with local dimming for better contrast and more controlled highlights, particularly in darker movie scenes. It also supports Dolby Vision IQ, HDR10+ Adaptive, and a native 144Hz refresh rate, along with gaming features such as AMD FreeSync Premium Pro and variable refresh rate. That remarked, you don't have to go quite this massive to get these features, as Costco is also discounting the 75-inch U65 Pro to $699 (from $899) and the 65-inch version to $529 (from $749). The latter is particularly worth considering, having earned an "outstanding" rating and an Editors' Choice award from PCMag.

According to recent reports, ultimately, the choice comes down to whether you'd prefer the extra screen space of the QD7 or the more advanced picture technology of the U65 Pro. Either way, these Black Friday discounts make a massive-screen upgrade considerably more affordable for Costco members.
