According to recent reports, welcome back to Beauty Marks: Vogue’s weekly edition of the best moments in celebrity beauty, from Vogue editors’ IG feeds, and all the glam of the fashion and pop culture landscapes. Each week, we curate the nail art to pin for your next nail appointment, newly introduced recruits to ‘Team Bob’, and major red carpet moments from the week’s most glamorous affairs. As always, it’s as much about celebrity beauty as it is about the makeup artists, hairstylists, and nail artists, as well as the creators crafting the trends you’re about to see everywhere.

Industry observers note that this week, beauty was all about the finer details. Sadie Soverall and Lola Petticrew adopted some statement bangs, while Jessie Buckley, Emilia Clarke, and Jessica Chastain offered three very different takes on the bob. Elsewhere, Simone Ashley and Eva Victor favored sleek, glossy lengths, Jennifer Lopez made the humble nude manicure feel fresh again, and Hailey Bieber delivered a masterclass in winter-ready glam. From Old Hollywood drama to playful, graphic flourishes, there was plenty to bookmark.

Scroll through the week’s best of celebrity beauty and pop culture’s glam below, and head to the app to vote for your favorite.

According to recent reports, A little soft glamour goes a long way. For the Governors Awards, Eva Victor chose long, glassy, center-parted brunette hair, paired with subtly smoky taupe eyes, delicately flushed cheeks, and a rosy lip. A balance of warmth and sharp definition. Hair by Justine Marjan, makeup by Yukari.

Jessie Buckley is a brunette once more, opting for a wintry, chocolate shade. At the Ivors Composer Awards, hairstylist Fabio Petri gave her chin-length cut a severe side part and sleek, tucked-under ends. Fresh peachy cheeks and a softly defined nude lip, courtesy of Naoko Scintu, offered a lovely counterpoint.

Winter beauty inspiration, courtesy of Hailey Bieber. A slicked-back bun, softly bronzed cheeks, fluttery lashes, and her signature glossy nude lip make an excellent match for that enormous, fluffy white collar. Makeup by Leah Darcy.

Lola Petticrew’s jet-black lengths and perfectly straight baby bangs, exaggerated, graphic winged eyeliner, and mauve-toned shadow lands somewhere between gothic heroine and sixties doll. Hair by Sami Knight, makeup by Nhinako.

Ella Rubin is captivating with a softly smudged eye and a great nude lip. Makeup artist Shayna Gold created this pretty, gently defined Chanel Beauty look using Les Beiges eyeshadow, Le Liner de Chanel in 512 Noir, and Rouge Coco lipstick in 124 Marie, paired with subtly flushed cheeks and luminous skin.

It has been revealed that trust Jennifer Lopez to make a classic nude manicure feel ultra luxurious. For the premiere of The JLo Show, manicurist Tom Bachik created a juicy peachy nude using shade FS-106 from his NEUDES collection. A lesson in understated nails for a woman who rarely does understated anything. Supermodel snatched hair by Chris Appleton, gleaming makeup by Ernesto Casillas.

Another day, another excellent bob. Emilia Clarke’s buttery blonde, chin-grazing cut is all about that clean, blunt line and a little natural movement through the ends—crafted by hairstylist Jacob Rozenberg. Makeup by Shayna Gold.

Recent findings suggest that those wispy, curled baby bangs! Sadie Soverall’s chocolate-strawberry lengths get a playful, almost vintage twist with sculpted little forehead curls and flipped-out face-framing pieces. Glowing skin and a soft, muted lip keep things modern. Hair by Emilie Bromley, makeup by Brooke Turnbull.

Old Hollywood with a darker edge. Jessica Chastain went full screen siren for the CMA Awards, with a dramatically side-parted, sculpted blonde bob and deep, vampish lipstick. Hair by Renato Campora, makeup by Hung Vanngo, and nails by Julie Kandalec.

In a recent development, golden girl! For the Earthshot Prize in Mumbai, Simone Ashley paired her dazzling Swadesh x Manish Malhotra lehenga with glossy, poker-straight lengths, a razor-sharp center part, softly smoked eyes, and warm bronze tones. Hair by Rio Sreedharan, makeup by Lake Sanu, and nails by Michelle Humphrey.
