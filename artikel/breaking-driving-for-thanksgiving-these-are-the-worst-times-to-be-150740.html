It has been revealed that another Thanksgiving week is upon us, which means tens of millions of Americans will once again travel by plane, train, and automobile to see their loved ones. However, this Thanksgiving period will be a little different than in recent years—at least when it comes to transportation. That’s because the American Automobile Association (AAA) indicates that, for the first time in several years, fewer Americans will be traveling. “After three years of record highs, the number of travelers is slightly lower partly because of higher travel costs this year,” says Stacey Barber, vice president of AAA travel. “But travelers won’t notice much of a difference on the roads and at the airports, since projected volumes remain near record levels.”  That means that despite fewer travelers, the roads are still expected to be packed at times. With that in mind, here are the best and worst times to travel by car over the 2026 Thanksgiving period. Best times to hit the roads for the Thanksgiving travel period For the 2026 Thanksgiving period, AAA expects 72.9 million people to travel to their holiday destinations by car. That’s down from last year’s 73.1 million automobile travelers, but still a significant amount. AAA defines the 2026 Thanksgiving period as the seven days lasting from Tuesday, November 24 to Monday, November 30, 2026. But despite the expected 72.9 million road travelers, how congested the streets will be depends heavily on when you travel during the period. AAA says these are the best times to be on the road (ie: the times with the expected lowest congestion) over the period, according to transportation data and insights provider INRIX data compiled by the auto organization: Tuesday, November 24: Before 1:00 PM Wednesday, November 25: Before 12:00 PM  Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving): Minimal Traffic Impact Expected  Friday, November 27: Before 12:00 PM  Saturday, November 28: Before 11:00 AM  Sunday, November 29: Before 11:00 AM  Monday, November 30: Before 1:00 PM  Worst times to hit the roads for the Thanksgiving travel period INRIX data indicates that the worst days to travel over the Thanksgiving period—that is, the days the congestion is expected to be the highest—are the Tuesday and Wednesday before Thanksgiving, and the Sunday and Monday after. Those are the days when most people choose to travel to and from their holiday destinations. As for the individual times that are expected to be the worst to travel by car, those are: Tuesday, November 24: 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM  Wednesday, November 25: 12:00 PM to 7:00 PM  Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving): Minimal Traffic Impact Expected  Friday, November 27: 1:00 PM to 7:00 PM  Saturday, November 28: 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM  Sunday, November 29: 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM  Monday, November 30: 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM  Fewer total travelers are expected over the Thanksgiving 2026 period When combining all modes of transportation—auto, air, and “other,” which includes bus, boat, and train—AAA says 81.48 million people in America are expected to travel over the 2026 Thanksgiving period. That’s about 121,000 fewer than the 81.61 million who traveled during the 2025 Thanksgiving period. Still, 2026’s expected numbers far surpass the 77.78 million people who traveled in 2019, the last year before the pandemic. The expected decline in travelers this year is mainly due to higher travel costs, especially gas prices, AAA says. Besides a decline of about 200,000 car travelers, AAA says there will also be a drop in air travelers by about 0.2% to 5.96 million (versus 5.97 million in 2025). However, more people will travel by “other” modes during this period than last year. “Other” includes travel by bus, train, and cruise. In the 2026 Thanksgiving period, the other travel segment is expected to reach 2.6 million travelers—up 4.1% from 2.49 million in 2025.
