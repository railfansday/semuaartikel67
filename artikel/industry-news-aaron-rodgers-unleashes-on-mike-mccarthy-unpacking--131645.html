Aaron Rodgers is sharing his side of the story as it relates to the Steelers' stunning decision to bench him ahead of Friday's game against the Broncos.

During a scorching appearance on The Pat McAfree show, Rodgers, who remarked that teammate Pat Freiermuth was the one who informed him of Pittsburgh's decision to make him inactive for Friday's game, accused Steelers coach Mike McCarthy of sleeping and being late to team meetings throughout the season. Rodgers leveled several other accusations against McCarthy, including that he arrived late this week and slept during an offensive meeting.

He also remarked that McCarthy did the same things in Green Bay but that he covered for him during their years together there.

"In my professional opinion, based on 22 years of experience, believed that the best interest of the team was to tell him that what's best for the team is for him not to call the plays, and if he wasn't up for that, then they should probably release me," he said. "And that's when the narrative got spun by them, and they posted things, and nobody called me. So, at this point, I'm not going back to Pittsburgh. That ship is definitely sailed. You know, I love those guys. I talked to so many of them. ... They know what's going on. Like I said, they all know. Everybody knows. Just like the guys in Green Bay knew. It's like the people in Dallas knew. Like they know what the deal is.".

Rodgers also noted that McCarthy was often late to watch film on upcoming opponents, including in preparation for this week's game against the Broncos. But Rodgers said his breaking point was when McCarthy said, "1-2-3 Packers!" at halftime of Sunday's loss to the Eagles. McCarthy coached in Green Bay from 2006-18.

In a recent development, "Last Wednesday, we were in a quarterback meeting," Rodgers remarked. "Mike came in late, sat down, and within 10 minutes began snoring. Woke himself up, was rattling his papers. We all looked around. Five minutes later, you hear the snoring again, and less than five minutes after that, he walks out of the room, like nothing happened. Thursday, he misses an offensive meeting.

Rodgers said McCarthy missed the team walkthrough on Friday, as well as the "protection walkthrough" on Thursday. He also added that the coach missed the Saturday morning meeting.

"And then [he] comes to Sunday when obviously we struggled, and the product wasn't great, and you know there were some guys early on that didn't like the way things were going, especially defensive guys that Mike didn't want to get another defense," Rodgers remarked.

It has been revealed that "So a lot of those guys, you know, he kind of already lost a lot of those guys. And I think the nail in the coffin for so many of us was at halftime.".

The Steelers had reportedly planned to keep Rodgers in the organization despite his benching, but they have since decided to release him since the interview.

McCarthy, Rodgers alleged, has also not done a good job connecting with players and hasn't spent enough time with the defense. Rodgers had defended McCarthy up to this point, telling his teammates to give him more time.

ESPN's Brooke Pryor reported shortly after the interview that "multiple league sources corroborated Rodgers' account" to the network. According to Pryor, "One source texted: 'It's all true.'" However, there has been no official response or statement from the Steelers organization at this point.

McCarthy responded to some of Rodgers' allegations Wednesday afternoon, but he declined to reveal any specifics about "private conversations.".

It has been revealed that "I know how to win, and that's the facts," McCarthy replied. "This is my third program. I blueprint on winning here because of the men and because of this organization.".

Rodgers stated his final straw came during halftime against the Eagles this past Sunday. Down 13-3, Rodgers said McCarthy spoke to the team in the locker room and then ended the meeting with "1-2-3, Packers.".

"That's what he broke it down on in the locker room," Rodgers said. "Nobody is even saying Steelers because it was so in the moment and one of those weird ramblings, something. Everybody across the entire locker room heard it, and I think we all looked around going, 'what the f---, what is going on. Where is his head at.'".

In a recent development, the Steelers ended up losing that game in Philadelphia, allowing the lowly Eagles to snap their seven-game losing streak. Rodgers mentioned on Monday he decided to confront McCarthy about the situation.

Rodgers confirmed during the Pat McAfee interview that his time with the Steelers was over after a season and a half. When asked about his future with the organization, he plainly remarked, "That ship has definitely sailed." This comes after he said he tried repeatedly to work things out with McCarthy.

Sources indicate that when Rodgers addressed McCarthy on Monday, he stated that McCarthy denied ever sleeping through and meeting late to meetings. Rodgers offered him an ultimatum: If changes weren't made (such as McCarthy relinquishing play-calling duties), he would rather not be the team's quarterback, which ultimately happened.

It has been revealed that as a leader on the team, Rodgers felt compelled to address McCarthy, not only with his concerns, but with his teammates' concerns, according to Rodgers. Rodgers stated that everyone inside the Steelers' facility knows what is happening, but — given his stature as a four-time MVP — he is the only one who can speak openly, since he has nothing to lose.

Rodgers said he met with McCarthy this week to discuss his concerns, but the two couldn't get on the same page.

In a recent development, "I'm telling you, you snored in the meeting twice, in a quarterback meeting; that's unacceptable," Rodgers remarked. "You're telling me you've never slept at a facility before. And I'm telling you, I have a hundred witnesses and collaborating people who would support what I'm saying. I'm not saying that to f---ing bury you. I'm saying that because this behavior and these habits are not healthy.".

Rodgers added: "I'm standing on the truth. Everybody there knows it.".

Sources indicate that during his scorched-earth interview, Rodgers mentioned he was on a phone call with Freiermuth when he learned the Steelers were benching him in favor of Mason Rudolph. Rodgers said Freiermuth mentioned that "I can't believe they're doing this." Rodgers responded, "Doing what?" and that is when he learned about the situation.

In a locker room media availability after the interview, Freiermuth didn't contradict Rodgers' statement, but he also didn't have much to say.

Industry observers note that "I was just checking in on a close friend [about] what I heard. That's all I have to say about that," Freiermuth said.

Instead of Rodgers, the Steelers are moving forward with Rudolph for Friday's game against the Broncos. Pittsburgh (6-4) is reeling after consecutive losses to the Bengals and Eagles.

Rodgers, however, will likely become a free agent sometime soon.
