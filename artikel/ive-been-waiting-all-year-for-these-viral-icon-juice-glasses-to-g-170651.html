Industry observers note that if you've ever stood in an Anthropologie holding one Icon Juice Glass in each hand, trying to choose between the bow and the bee, I have great news: This Black Friday, you can just get both! The hand-blown glasses with the tiny beaded icons (you know the ones, they're all over your feed) are 40% off right now, which brings each one from $16 down to $9.60.

Recent findings suggest that and as far as Black Friday deals go, this is a really good one. Anthropologie is taking 40% off its most-loved styles and 30% off almost everything else. (Just know that the product page will still say $16. The discount reveals up once the glass is in your cart.).

Anthropologie Boardwalk Icon Juice Glass.

Anthropologie City Icon Juice Glass.

Part of the charm is that each glass is mouth-blown and decorated by hand with glass-bead icons, so no two look exactly the same. They hold about 17 ounces, which is plenty for an iced coffee, a spritz, or (fine) actual juice. There are dozens of designs across the collection, and the original style alone includes a red heart, a black bow, a bee, and an engagement ring for the friend who just said yes. Shoppers are big fans too, with more than 7,400 reviews for the collection on Anthropologie's site. (P.S. They're hand-wash only and not made for hot drinks, so save your tea for a mug.).
