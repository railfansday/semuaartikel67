Recent findings suggest that four-plus years into the massive collaboration between Omega and Swatch, the partners are still finding ways to reimagine their blockbuster MoonSwatch. And despite nearly 40 iterations of the piece, they still generate massive excitement. The most recent version required interested collectors to fill out an “Electronic Swatch Timepiece Application” in hopes of being selected to purchase one. More than 1.2 million people happily played along. Now, Swatch and Omega are back and playing the hits with a Snoopy-inflected version of the MoonSwatch.

It has been revealed that meet the new Mission to Earthphase - Silver Snowflake. The blacked-out watch is reminiscent of the collector-favorite Speedmaster known as the “Dark Side of the Moon” from 2013. What that watch was lacking compared to this one is an adorable drawing of Snoopy and Woodstock dressed as astronauts and a special silver disc with a unique laser-engraved snowflake design. Safe to say, Omega and Swatch have another hit on their hands.

The black-on-black-on-black design works to great effect here. Everything is rendered in the dark shade: the case, bezel, dial, and matching rubber strap with Velcro closure. Like all MoonSwatch references, the Silver Snowflake model measures 42mm in diameter and features the asymmetric design familiar to Speedy lovers everywhere.

Recent findings suggest that but it’s the easter eggs on the dial that really make this MoonSwatch stand out. Naturally, there are several hidden details. For instance, check out the miniature skis standing in for the indices at 11 o'clock. That’s a special shoutout to the Apollo 11 50th Anniversary of the Speedmaster. This model also returns to a trick employed by the “Cold Moon” MoonSwatch released last November. That watch and this one feature a snowflake design generated at random, meaning each watch is functionally unique. Here, the snowflake appears on a silver-coated disc in the moonphase complication at 2 o’clock.

In a recent development, at 10 o’clock is Swatch and Omega’s signature MoonSwatch “Earth phase” indicator, which bears an image of the Earth with UV ink-coated oceans set against a starry background. Again, look closely: Switzerland is represented in white, its UV ink glowing green in the dark, while Snoopy and Woodstock, represented below, feature secret details in blue-emission UV ink. Protecting the dial is a box-shaped, bio-sourced crystal with an engraved “S” in the center—a detail familiar to fans of vintage Speedmasters, some of which bore “O”-engraved Hesalite crystals.

Industry observers note that like several other MoonSwatch models in the brand’s now-vast collection, buying the Mission to Earthphase - Silver Snowflake requires navigating certain arcane rules. The first part is simple: it’ll be available in stores worldwide this Black Friday, November 27th. However, following that, your only chance to nab one will be when it’s snowing in Switzerland. (And only until March 20th, 2027.) When it comes to the MoonSwatch, simply buying one has become half the fun.
