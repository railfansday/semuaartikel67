A club spokesperson stated: “Swindon Town Football Club can confirm that we have parted organization with first-team manager Ian Holloway.

According to recent reports, “Since joining the club towards the end of 2024, Ian has played an key role in stabilising our on-field position and helping the club make progress season upon season. We would like to place on record our sincere thanks to Ian for his hard work, commitment and endeavour throughout his time at the Nigel Eady County Ground. We wish him every success for the future.

“However, following careful consideration of recent results and performances, the club believes that now is the right time to take a different direction. Your continued support, particularly through challenging periods, demonstrates the strength and passion of this Football Club.

We ask supporters to continue standing together with us as we work towards achieving them.
