By MATT BARLOW, FOOTBALL WRITER.

Published: 17:30 EST, 20 November 2026 | Updated: 17:35 EST, 20 November 2026.

Mykhailo Mudryk is still two weeks away from a Tottenham return after a suffering setback to his recovery from an ankle injury.

Mudryk, who has played only 16 minutes since joining on loan from Chelsea, clashed with the Ukrainian FA this week when he blamed them for his positive drug test which ruled him out of football for almost two years.

He also risked the wrath of Spurs fans by admitting he only made the move across London to recover his fitness before returning to Stamford Bridge where Blues boss Xabi Alonso was 'counting on him'.

And he managed to annoy both Chelsea and Spurs fans by revealing, during the same YouTube interview, that he wanted to sign for Arsenal when he moved to the Premier League in January 2023 but his agent forced through the move with the best commission.

'I can understand the fans, but Mudryk is a good guy,' stated Spurs boss De Zerbi, who worked with the Ukraine international at Shakhtar Dontetsk and convinced him to resist interest from Brentford and Coventry and be reunited in North London.

In a recent development, 'He's a good player. He suffered a lot for the situation in the last couple of years. He has just one thing to do, show on the pitch his qualities, show on the pitch that now he's playing for Tottenham. Tottenham is a major club, like the other clubs, not less at all.'.

Mykhailo Mudryk admitted he joined Tottenham to regain fitness before returning to Chelsea, while also stating he had wanted to sign for Arsenal in 2023 before his transfer to the Blues.

Roberto De Zerbi acknowledged Tottenham fans' views on Mudryk's comments, but insisted the winger has 'suffered a lot' following his doping ban.

Chelsea paid £86million for Mudryk but he made little impression before he was hit with a four-year ban in 2024, which was wiped out this summer enabling him to return to competition.

In a recent development, 'He has no responsibility in this part,' noted De Zerbi. 'Now his focus is just on the ball or just on the football. He has a big chance to play with us because Tottenham is a big club. He knows very well the coach, he knows very well the style of play.

'He knows I give him total confidence to show what he is. So to move the focus in the right direction, in a good direction. But I think he's working hard to recover and to play earlier.'.

Recent findings suggest that mudryk made his Spurs debut as a sub at Nottingham Forest in September but injured an ankle in training a few days later. He was initially expected to be out for two months with Saturday's game against Ipswich his target to return.

'The strategy was to be available for Ipswich or Sunderland,' mentioned De Zerbi, who will serve a touchline ban against Ipswich for three yellow cards. 'Then he felt not pain but he was not comfortable last week. And they take two weeks more, and step by step we're going to see the situation.'.

James Maddison's limited involvement for Tottenham relates to a niggling hip injury dating back to pre-season.

Maddison has been an unused sub in two Spurs games since limping off when the problem flared up at Chelsea, on his second start of the season and his second start in six days.

Roberto De Zerbi confirmed ahead of Saturday's game against Ipswich that it had been the same problem which dogged Maddison through the summer as he fought back from a serious knee injury.

The Spurs boss revealed how his physical condition had suffered from not training regularly and reminded the 29-year-old England midfielder he could only make an impact in the Premier League in peak condition.

De Zerbi confirmed James Maddison is available to face Ipswich but stressed the midfielder needs to push himself to reach his peak physical condition.

'It is not a big injury, but he was not able to work,' explained De Zerbi. 'He had a problem with his hip. The same as preseason. It's not a big problem but if you don't work consistently you lose the physical condition and to play in this level of competition you have to stay in the top physical condition.'.

It has been revealed that maddison, who turns 30 on Monday, missed most of last season with a cruciate knee injury and has played only 195 minutes this season. His return was further complicated by a shoulder injury after coming on at Brentford on the opening game of the season.

'He is still young, 29 years is not old,' noted De Zerbi. 'He has to push and demand of himself the top in terms of physical condition, because if he is 80 or 90 per cent of his physical condition he makes the difference for us, he changes the games.'.

Maddison and Destiny Udogie have both trained fully through the international break and are available to face Ipswich.

In a recent development, 'He worked very well from the Wednesday after Leeds,' noted De Zerbi. 'From Chelsea to the Leeds game, he worked just three times on the pitch at the training ground.

'If you don't work during the week you risk injury and lose the normal physical condition you need to play Premier League. The same for Udogie. When they work and are fit to work, they have many chances to play, especially Maddison because a special player.'.

Recent findings suggest that maddison's first start of the season came against Coventry when he scored the opener and inspired Spurs to their first win. He barely trained but started again in the win at Chelsea five days later, where he broke down early in the second half and was replaced by Conor Gallagher.

De Zerbi made it clear there were no problems connected to Maddison being pictured out at a pub in Coventry after the defeat at Leeds. It was the last game before the international break and the first of three days off.

'I am angry because next time he has to invite me,' joked the Spurs boss and he added: 'I am happy. I know when my players work well. If they are serious on the pitch, off the pitch they can do what they want. I'm not a policeman.

Industry observers note that 'James is top professional, top guy, top player. The private life is not our problem if they work well and they are serious, and give their best for the club, the shirt and the fans, and he is perfect when it comes to that.'.
