It has been revealed that TL;DR: On Black Friday (Nov. 27), the first 100 guests in line at every Target store get a free $25, $50, or $100 Target gift card — plus one $1,000 winner per store (no purchase necessary).

It has been revealed that target is giving early birds a major incentive to set their alarms on Black Friday morning. On Friday, Nov. 27, the retailer is handing out free Target gift cards worth $25, $50, or $100 to the first 100 eligible guests in line at every store — plus one lucky shopper at each location will walk away with a $1,000 Target gift card.

Unlike online doorbusters that disappear in seconds, this giveaway happens strictly in person and comes with a tight arrival and redemption window.

Target stores reopen at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27. Showing up right at 6 a.m. will be too late if you want a free gift card. Target associates will start handing out prize redemption tickets to the first 100 eligible people in line roughly an hour before doors open (around 5 a.m. local time at most stores).

Tickets are handed out on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last, with a strict limit of one ticket per person.

Sources indicate that once doors open at 6 a.m. local time, you have to take your ticket to the Black Friday event cart inside that same Target store within one hour of store opening (by 7 a.m. local time at 6 a.m. locations). If you miss that one-hour redemption window, you forfeit your prize.

Recent findings suggest that your card's exact dollar value is revealed when you redeem your ticket at the event cart. Out of the 100 winners at every store, 60 people get a $25 gift card, 30 get a $50 gift card, nine get a $100 gift card, and one person gets the $1,000 grand prize.

The giveaway is open to shoppers ages 13 and older (minors need a parent or legal guardian's permission to participate), and no purchase is necessary to get a ticket or claim a card.

Once you claim your gift card, you can spend it right away on Target's Black Friday sale or save it for later in stores and online — just note that you cannot use it on prepaid or specialty gift cards. Anything you buy that's eligible also qualifies for Target's Holiday Price Match Guarantee through Dec. 24.

Double-check your local Target's exact hours before heading out — and if you are mapping out a multi-store morning route, check out our guide to Black Friday 2026 store hours to see when other major retailers open their doors.
