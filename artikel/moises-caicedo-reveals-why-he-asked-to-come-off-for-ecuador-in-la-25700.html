Moises Caicedo has revealed he asked to be substituted at half-time playing for Ecuador as Chelsea were handed a scare during the international break.

Ecuador played friendly matches against Costa Rica and Bolivia, picking up 2-0 victories in both games.

Caicedo played 84 minutes in Quito in what was Marcelo Gallardo’s first game in charge on Saturday, named in the starting XI again on Tuesday.

The former Brighton star was replaced halfway through that game, later revealing he was ‘not feeling well’ with Chelsea’s game against Leeds United on Saturday in his thoughts.

Recent findings suggest that ‘No, I didn’t feel very well, so I asked to come off instead, because I know I have another commitment with my club.,’ Caicedo told reporters. ‘So, I’ll try to get there as soon as possible and recover.’.

Caicedo missed Chelsea’s opening game of the season against Fulham, suffering a calf injury 14 minutes after coming off the bench in the following game against Brighton.

He was ruled out of the next three games but has started Chelsea’s last five in the Premier League.

He was also a doubt heading into the game against Sunderland before the international break.

During the international break, the 25-year-old opened up on his recent fitness issues.

‘[I’m] a little tired, with some minor complications, but I’m here because it’s my national team and what matters most to me is being here; with my people, with my Ecuador,’ he said.

Caicedo is not expected to return to Chelsea training until Friday and will likely have just one session to prove his fitness ahead of the visit of Leeds on Saturday afternoon.
