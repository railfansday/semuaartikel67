📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Spotify charges $12.99 a month for one person, $6.99 for a student, and $21.99 for a family. Dropping the individual plan for the free tier saves $155.88 a year, in exchange for ads and no music downloads.

Sources indicate that the free tier does let you search for any song and play it. Three other major music apps have free versions with ads, each with its own catch:.

Recent findings suggest that downloads matter most on a subway or a flight with no signal, where streaming stops. To get some without a subscription, check your library’s music apps.

Freegal Music, offered through participating libraries, streams songs ad-free and lets you download a few each week to keep. On hoopla, you can borrow a full album for seven days to stream or download, and each album uses one of your monthly borrows. While you’re signed in, check what else your library card covers.

For radio, iHeartRadio streams live stations from around the country for free, and public radio stations stream on their own websites.

On Spotify, cancel today rather than on the renewal date. You keep Premium through the period you’ve already paid for, and your playlists and saved music stay with the free account.
