Above: To celebrate Bulgari’s latest collection and boutique, guests roamed the grounds of a waterfront Palm Beach estate, designed by architect Addison Mizner.

In a recent development, few people shaped the look of South Florida as much as architect Addison Mizner. A pioneer of Spanish Colonial Revival architecture, his Mediterranean style quickly took Palm Beach by storm in the 1920s, as he designed and built more than 50 Florida mansions and villas. And his impact still lingers, as, on Wednesday night, stars like Venus Williams and Chanel Iman spent their evening seated under the tennis pavilion at one of Mizner’s famed waterfront Palm Beach estates.

The crowd was there to celebrate a far newer building in the neighborhood: Bulgari’s Palm Beach boutique, which opened earlier this week. In honor of the store’s debut, the brand brought its newest high jewelry collection, Eclettica, to the United States for the first time.

“Palm Beach embodies a distinctive spirit of effortless sophistication,” mentioned Bulgari CEO Laura Burdese.

Located in a heritage building in Palm Beach’s shopping district, the boutique is modeled after a traditional Italian piazza. Anchored by four travertine columns, the open space encourages clients to move through the maison’s most famous collections. Venini’s classic Serpenti chandelier, made of Murano glass and seen in Bvlgari boutiques worldwide, serves as a focal point in a shape reminiscent of the brand’s many symbolic snakes.

It has been revealed that elsewhere in the store, decorative panels by Milan-based Pictalab depict Floridian ocean and beach views, grounding the Italian maison in its surrounding landscape. Among the art is the Eclettica collection, which borrows its name from eclecticism and stays true to its moniker: mixing inspirations from painting, sculpture, and architecture, translating them into bold, colorful pieces.

It’s a fitting style for Palm Beach, where Mizner’s vibrant Mediterranean and Spanish-influenced designs borrow inspiration from entire eras and regions, giving the area its everlasting mix-master flair.

“We are delighted to bring Eclettica to this extraordinary destination,” Burdese said, “where its bold artistic vision and exceptional craftsmanship find a natural resonance with the city’s vibrant character.”.
