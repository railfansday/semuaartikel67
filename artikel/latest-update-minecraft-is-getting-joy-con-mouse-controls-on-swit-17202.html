If you've read our Minecraft Switch 2 review here on Nintendo Life, you'll know one thing at the moment missing from this version is mouse mode.

Industry observers note that fortunately, an enhancement is already in the works to add Joy-Con 2 mouse controls to this version of the game. These controls can be accessed right now in the closed beta build.

According to recent reports, this includes the ability to use mouse controls with either the left or right Joy-Con, and you can also customise settings, such as cursor and camera sensitivity. Additionally, you place blocks with the 'ZL' button, and break and select blocks with the 'ZR' button. Button layout can also be adjusted in the game's settings.

At the moment, there's no release date for the official rollout of mouse controls on Switch 2, but they'll be made available to every player at some point in the future.

In case you missed it, Minecraft players who own a digital Switch copy of the game can presently get a free Switch 2 upgrade until the end of January 2027. Owners of a physical copy on Switch can also get a discount on the Minecraft 'Ultimate Collection' upgrade for Switch 2.

Industry observers note that have you tried out Minecraft on the Switch 2 yet? Will you be using mouse controls? Let us know in the comments.
