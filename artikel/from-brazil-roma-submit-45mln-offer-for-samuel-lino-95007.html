📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, roma continues their pursuit of a winger. Tony D’Amico is working to please Gian Piero Gasperini, who has been calling for a reinforcement in that position for several months to add newly introduced options to the defense.

Several names are on the table, but in the last few hours a upcoming lead seems to be gaining traction: Samuel Lino.

According to Brazilian portal RTI Esporte, the Giallorossi are pursuing the player seriously, so much so that they have already initiated contact with his entourage and submitted an official €45 million offer to Flamengo.

The Brazilian club, recently crowned champions of the league, hasn’t closed the door on a possible transfer but would only be willing to part with one of their key players if they receive a particularly high offer.

Samuel Lino was one of Flamengo’s key players this season, finishing with 13 goals and 9 assists. Roma is waiting to see if there is enough room to move the deal forward.
