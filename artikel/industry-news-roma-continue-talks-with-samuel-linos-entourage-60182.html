Roma are back in the hunt for Samuel Lino, this time reportedly ready to offer €45 million.

According to RTI Esporte, the Giallorossi have initiated new contacts with the Brazilian winger’s representatives, expressing their willingness to invest a significant sum to bring him to the capital during the January transfer window.

The sum would be higher than the €25 million Flamengo had already rejected, according to rumors circulating in recent weeks.

The Rio club hasn’t ruled out a move away, but continues to demand high prices for the 1999-born player, so much so that ESPN Brasil had indicated a valuation close to €60 million, corresponding to the player’s release clause.

Flamengo’s financial situation could favor negotiations, especially after the suspension of their sponsorship agreement with Betano, which guaranteed the club up to 268.5 million reais per season.

The contract has been suspended until March 31, 2027, barring any further developments, and the Brazilian club is evaluating how to compensate for the shortfall, including through possible transfers.

Samuel Lino arrived at Flamengo in the summer of 2025 from Atlético Madrid in a deal worth approximately €22 million plus bonuses. This season, he has made 54 appearances, scoring 15 goals and providing 13 assists.
