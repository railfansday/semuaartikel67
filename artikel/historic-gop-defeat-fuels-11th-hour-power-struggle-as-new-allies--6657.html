According to recent reports, with Democrats poised to take control of the Texas governor's mansion for the first time in decades, Republican state Rep. Brian Harrison is rallying a coalition of conservative organizations behind a last-ditch push to rein in the sweeping emergency powers Gov. Greg Abbott has wielded for years.

Industry observers note that "The largest coalition of conservative organizations ever formed to restore separation of powers and rein in unconstitutional emergency powers joins the call for [Republican Texas Gov. Greg Abbott] to convene a special session before Democrats take over!!" Harrison wrote in a statement posted on X on Thursday.

"I'm proud to be leading this effort (something I've been working on for many years on a bipartisan basis), and am grateful to [Citizens for Renewing America] for helping organize this unprecedented coalition fighting to limit the damage that Democrats can inflict on Texas if we don't act now!" he added.

It has been revealed that the announcement comes after Harrison urged Abbott on Nov. 4 to call a special session to curb gubernatorial emergency powers in the state before Democratic Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa assumes office.

Sources indicate that "In her first national TV appearance after winning, Gina Hinojosa agreed with me that these powers can be abused," Harrison told Fox News Digital on Friday. "I renew my invitation for her to join my call to limit them immediately, as she’s told me she wants to for years.".

According to recent reports, "I will not sit silently and let Democrats turn Texas into a sanctuary state or enact socialist price controls without fighting back," the Texas Republican added. "I’m proud to provide the type of bold, pro-liberty leadership Texans deserve and honored to partner with an overwhelming and historic number of conservatives unifying in the call for action. Recent findings suggest that hinojosa told MS NOW in a Nov. 5 interview that "there's no question Greg Abbott has used his emergency powers in a way that is abusive.".

"My commitment is that I will only use emergency powers to deliver to the people of Texas at a time when Texans want relief, and I would hope no legislator would block that effort towards relief.".

Hinojosa's campaign and Abbott's office did not respond to Fox News Digital's request for comment.

Harrison said the "growing" list of groups supporting his calls for a special legislative session includes True Texas Project, Citizens for Renewing America, Texas Gun Rights and Gun Owners of America, among several others.

Citizens for Renewing America and Texas Gun Rights did not respond to Fox News Digital's request seeking comment.

"GOA has a proven track record of fighting executive overreach, including our successful efforts in 2021 to eliminate the governor’s authority to restrict firearms and ammunition.".
