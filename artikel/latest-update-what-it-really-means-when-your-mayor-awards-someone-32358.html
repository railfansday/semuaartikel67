A key to the city looks official, but it won't unlock any doors. Here's why cities give them out and what recipients actually receive.

Whether it’s in a movie or real life, everyone has seen or heard of someone receiving an oversized gold key that is a “key to the city.” The ceremony is usually accompanied with a photoshoot, a handshake, and applause. But what does the key actually do?

Recent findings suggest that cities across the world still hand out these awards, and the tradition goes back much further than most people realize. Learn what a key to the city represents, how the custom began, and what someone actually gets to do with it.

A key to the city is an award that cities give to people they want to honor.

“The Key to the City is presented as a symbol of civic recognition and Philadelphia’s appreciation for individuals whose achievements, service and influence have made a meaningful impact on the city and its people,” according to a release from the City of Philadelphia.

The key is usually decorative, with the city’s name or seal on it. Some come in a display box or with a certificate. Little Rock, Arkansas, considers its key the city’s highest ceremonial honor.

But the key won’t open City Hall or any other public building. It doesn’t give the recipient special privileges or any authority, either. In 2010, New York City tried something different with an art project that gave people working keys to certain locations. Those weren’t the same as the city’s usual honorary keys.

It has been revealed that the tradition goes back to when cities had walls and gates that closed at night. Giving someone a key meant they could come and go without asking permission.

It has been revealed that in medieval Europe, leaders also exchanged keys during official visits or military surrenders. Handing over the keys sometimes meant giving up control of a city. Over time, the custom became a way to welcome or honor someone.

Today, cities give keys to athletes, public figures, volunteers, and local residents. Each city has its own rules for who receives one.

It has been revealed that philadelphia, for example, honored a resident in 2026 after he walked across the country to support career opportunities for young people. Elk Grove, California, recognizes military accomplishments and acts of heroism, while Little Rock reserves its keys for people who live outside Arkansas.

According to recent reports, mayors usually present the keys, although some cities involve the city council or accept nominations from residents. Recipients get a keepsake and some public recognition, but no special privileges.
