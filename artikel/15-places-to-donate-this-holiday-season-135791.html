📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The act of gift-giving always seems incredibly fun before the holidays come around. But I doubt I’m the only one who has suddenly come to in a sweaty, consumerist panic after all the presents are unwrapped and there is wrapping paper and ribbon on the floor, wishing I’d put my money somewhere meaningful instead of showering my little cousins with books they are exceedingly polite about but clearly hate.

That’s why, in recent years, I’ve made the decision to donate to an important cause in a loved one’s name—and I’ve never, ever regretted it. Below, find a roundup of some of the Vogue staff’s favorite organizations doing hard and necessary work that could use a little love this holiday season:.

According to recent reports, “I always give to the Red Cross in the wake of natural disasters, but this year the cause was a little closer to home when the organization stepped in to provide relief to those displaced by the terrible building collapse that took place in Flatbush earlier this month. I live near the building site, and for several nights, the entire neighborhood was kept awake by hovering helicopters, a terrible reminder of the tragedy that had occurred. There have been many initiatives since, organized by friends and neighbors, to provide assistance. But for those who might like to bolster the resilient spirit of Latest York City this holiday season from farther afield, a donation to the Red Cross would be worthwhile.” —Chloe Schama.

“As someone who has always loved reading, I believe books have the power to open up new worlds, spark our imaginations, and inspire us to dream bigger. Every child deserves the opportunity to experience that same magic. Behind the Book brings diverse books and authors into underserved New York City public school classrooms, helping children discover the joy of reading and storytelling.” —Molly Barstein.

According to recent reports, one person is diagnosed with cancer every 1.6 seconds worldwide, and the Breakthrough Board is a women’s coalition that has been funding life-saving cancer research at the University of Chicago for nearly 80 years. Vogue Senior Digital Line Editor Jane Chun recommends donating and/or volunteering your time for this worthy cause.

Many of us would be at a loss without our furry companions, so a donation to this animal welfare organization—which operates the largest no-kill animal shelter in the US in Kanab, Utah, and partners with shelters and rescue groups nationwide—is a particularly nice way to honor the beloved pet in your life.

United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has detained over 8,000 children and increased family separations under the Trump administration. A donation to this California-based nonprofit (which will be matched through the end of December) helps to support those children and families with critical legal resources, medical and psychological treatment, and other forms of sorely needed support.

This nonprofit organization, which will have Vogue Head of Editorial Content Chloe Malle’s support this year, has been helping people affected by conflict and disaster around the world for the past 90 years. It is currently matching donations for families in Gaza (times three) through Giving Tuesday.

Over 10 million older adults live in poverty in the US, and this organization—which was founded by a Philadelphia social worker in 1954—works to mitigate that sad state of affairs by delivering healthy, culturally sensitive meals (and needed social contact) to seniors around the country.

It has been revealed that A donation to this organization attempting to find a cure for multiple myeloma (a type of blood cancer that forms in plasma cells) goes particularly far right now, notes Vogue Fashion Commerce Director Madeline Fass, as every dollar you give before December 31 will be doubled—up to $1 million—thanks to a special matching challenge from Dr. Elsey’s Fund to Cure Cancer.

With ICE raids and arrests ongoing all over the country, a donation to the Los Angeles-based National Day Laborer Organizing Network’s Immigrant Defense Fund helps to support legal defense and public advocacy for immigrant communities under attack.

Industry observers note that the right to a safe, accessible abortion has only become more imperiled since the Supreme Court overturned Roe v. Wade in 2022, and this nonprofit network of over 100 independent, grassroots abortion funds is working to increase access and make sure no pregnant person is forced to go without potentially lifesaving care.

Federal funding for NPR, PBS, and local public media stations was ended by President Donald Trump in 2025, but you can step up and help fill the gap by donating directly to your local station to support independent journalism. “I initiative to support my local NPR station, WNYC,” says Chloe Malle.

Nobody should be forced to navigate the aftermath of sexual assault or misconduct alone, and a donation to the Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network (RAINN) helps fund vital services like the organization’s national 24-hour sexual assault hotline to ensure that survivors always have somewhere to turn.

In a recent development, there are tons of great organizations working to end hunger in the US, from Feeding America to Feed the Children, but sometimes it’s nice to find one working locally. For Fresh Yorkers, that might just be this Harlem-based nonprofit that serves 900 free, chef-prepared meals a week to community members facing hunger.

It’s a scary time for trans, nonbinary, and gender-nonconforming youth in the US, which makes a donation to this mutual aid project serving unhoused and/or vulnerable trans youth (or a gift purchased directly from someone’s registry) particularly meaningful.

“I plan to adopt a couple of snowy owls for the children in my life through the World Wildlife Fund,” notes Malle—and you, too, can help prevent the vulnerable species’s extinction (while nabbing yourself a snowy owl plush toy) by donating to the WWF.
