According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6 is a very major game and includes almost every feature seen in a past GTA game, including the ability to drive tanks, customize cars with turbo, and rob stores. However, one feature that isn’t included in GTA 6 is the ability to hire a sex worker off the street, something players have been doing since 2001.

Sources indicate that in my over 150 hours playing GTA 6, which is a very good game in case you didn’t hear already, I never encountered a sex worker on the streets of Vice City. To be honest, I never really sought out prostitutes, as I was roleplaying Jason and Lucia as a loving couple who cared about each other deeply. But yeah, when I did look around for sex workers walking the street, I didn’t spot any. And that’s because Rockstar Games didn’t include them in GTA 6.

In an interview with Shack News, Rockstar noted that it only included features that felt necessary and complementary to Jason and Lucia’s criminal tale.

Turns out, the ability to hire prostitutes isn’t necessary to make a good GTA.
