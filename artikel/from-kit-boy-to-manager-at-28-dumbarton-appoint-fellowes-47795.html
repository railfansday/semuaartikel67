Dumbarton appoint 28-year-old manager.

Martin Fellowes has become the youngest manager in SPFL history, taking on the Dumbarton head coach job at the age of 28.

Leaving his position as assistant manager and head of football operations at Scottish League 2 leaders Stranraer, Fellowes inherits a team six places further down the ladder.

With experience on the Scotland Under-19s coaching team, Fellowes spent almost 10 years with East Kilbride, rising from teenage kit boy to a two-month spell as interim manager in 2023, winning the Lowland League Cup.

With a day job in office furniture sales, he holds a Uefa A licence.

"You have to earn people's respect as a person," Fellowes told BBC Scotland.

"When you are a young guy and you're leading a football club, I don't think you can come in and change everything and lead with an iron fist.

Recent findings suggest that "You have to get people's respect on and off the pitch. I know there will be plenty of criticism and there will be plenty of people sitting here today doubting that I'm capable of doing this job at 28.

"But now really the work for me has started on, by the end of the process, having gained those people onside to say, 'he was more than capable of doing this job and we're delighted to have him here'.

"I can only do that through winning games of football.".

Martin Fellowes (right) as a 17-year-old kit man at East Kilbride.

Jim Duffy was 29 when he began his long managerial career at Falkirk and Rhys McCabe was player/manager at Airdrieonians at the same age.

Ian Cathro, now leading French club Saint-Etienne, took charge of Hearts when he was just 30.

Fellowes replaces Frank McKeown, who resigned last month, while director of football Danny Lennon had been standing in on an interim basis.

The Sons are two points off the promotion places and visit Spartans on Saturday.

"We're not going to reinvent the wheel," Fellowes added.

"We're not going to do anything that anybody else hasn't been doing.

"But if we can add the 5% to every different department and win games of football, I think over time people will understand that sometimes the age isn't a barrier but it can actually be an advantage.".
