In a recent development, one of Apple’s most beloved products this year was undoubtedly the MacBook Neo. Unlike some of its other budget products, Apple seems interested in updating the MacBook Neo on an annual cycle, and we may get the next MacBook Neo around March.

According to Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman, Apple is targeting the iOS/macOS 27.4 release cycle for the release of the latest MacBook Neo, as well as the A19 budget iPad, iPhone 18, iPhone 18e, and OLED iPad Air.

While he notes that this cycle runs between February and March, it doesn’t necessarily mean the products will launch on the earlier end of that spectrum. Apple is also expected to release the iPhone Air 2, OLED iPad Air, and a upcoming iPad Pro within the first half of the year, but they’ll likely come a bit later than the aforementioned products.

As expected, the next MacBook Neo will bump up from the A18 Pro chip with 8GB of RAM to the A19 Pro chip with 12GB of RAM.

Sources indicate that apple will also introduce fresh colors on the MacBook Neo “frequently,” though it’s unclear if we’ll see that with the next iteration. From the Power On newsletter:.

In a recent development, the other thing I’ve heard is that Apple plans to refresh the MacBook Neo’s colors frequently. We’ll see if that trend starts with the first follow-up.

It has been revealed that I’m certainly excited to see the next generation MacBook Neo, especially with 12GB of unified memory. While 8GB is certainly fine, heavy multitaskers will notice it, especially with unoptimized software.

The bigger question is: will the next MacBook Neo have a price increase? With additional RAM included, it feels inevitable.

Hopefully backlit keyboard if they increase price.

Apple increased the price of MacBook Neo from $599 to $699 back in June, and in October the tech giant also silently decreased the education discount by $50, meaning the education price has increased from $499 to $649 since launch.

If there is a price increase, it likely won’t be too major, maybe $50 – leaving the computer at $749. Compared to the rest of the laptop landscape, MacBook Neo would still be a good value. We’ll see what happens.
