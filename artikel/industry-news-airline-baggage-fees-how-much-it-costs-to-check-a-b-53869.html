Industry News: Airline baggage fees: How much it costs to check a bag on major US airlines

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (196 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Airline baggage fees in the U.S. have increased in 2026, with first checked bags typically starting around $45 to $50 each way and higher costs for additional or overweight bags.
  • Carry-on allowances vary, but most major airlines permit one personal item and one carry-on bag, with some low-cost carriers charging extra or imposing stricter limits.
  • Paying for baggage online in advance can reduce costs, and certain fare types or elite status may include free checked bags or higher weight limits.
  • There are multiple strategies to avoid or reduce baggage fees, including holding elite status, booking premium cabin tickets, or using specific travel benefits.
  • Baggage fees differ by airline, fare type, travel date and whether payment is made online or at the airport.
  • Low-cost carriers often have the highest and most variable fees, and may enforce stricter weight limits (e.g., 40 pounds for checked bags on some airlines).
  • Specialty items and overweight or oversized bags typically incur additional charges, and policies for basic economy fares can be more restrictive.
