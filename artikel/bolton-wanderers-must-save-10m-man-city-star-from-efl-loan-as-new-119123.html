Bolton Wanderers have had a positive return to the Championship, as they look to establish themselves back at this level.

Steven Schumacher has done a fantastic job with the Trotters since his appointment, and whilst he will be pleased at how they’ve coped this season, they are struggling for consistency.

Therefore, there is plenty of room to improve, and the Bolton boss will know that the cushion to the bottom three can be eaten into very quickly if they don’t keep picking up results.

So, January could be crucial, and one player who is likely to be on the market is Jeremy Monga.

The winger joined Man City from Leicester for around £10m in the summer, and he was immediately loaned to Swansea, which seemed like a good fit for the player.

However, with Moussa Yeo, Elijah Just and Joseph Opoku also joining the Welsh club in the summer, it’s fair to say competition for places is fierce in the final third.

The past few months will be hugely frustrating for Monga, and video footage of the player wandering around the pitch on his own after the recent game against West Brom left many feeling sorry for the 17-year-old, who has seen his career stall.

As a result, it’s fair to say that Monga and his parent club will be counting down the days until the January window opens, so his season-long loan can be cut short.

Of course, Vitor Matos’ departure from Swansea could change things, but the Welsh club are stacked for talent in the final third.

Elijah Just, Moussa Yeo and Joseph Opoku were among the summer additions, so game time was always going to be difficult to come by for Monga, and it’s hard to envisage him forcing his way into the XI even under a latest head coach.

Following on from that, if City do send Monga somewhere else, they will be prioritising first-team football for the player, which could make Bolton the perfect fit.

Right now, the likes of Thierry Gale, Samuel Iling-Junior and Kylian Dong have all struggled for form, even if Gale has made some decent contributions earlier on in the campaign.

Nevertheless, attacking reinforcements will be the priority, and Monga could flourish in Schumacher’s setup.

Unlike many newly-promoted teams, Bolton are a front-footed, attacking team, and they haven’t been scared of taking the game to their opponents - as the likes of West Ham and Wolves found out, even if they did ultimately lose against the Hammers.

As part of his XI, Schumacher wants wingers who can hug the touchline, and he gives them freedom to carry the ball and express themselves.

That’s exactly what Monga needs right now, and, despite his age, the England youth international proved on occasions with Leicester last season that he can cause problems at this level.

We know that Bolton don’t have the biggest budget in the Championship, so they have to be smart in their recruitment, and they have made some good signings in the past few windows.

But, January is also difficult, and you don’t want to get sucked into paying over the odds for players, so it’s all about finding the balance.

In Monga, Bolton could get an exciting talent on loan, and, if it does work out, it will only enhance their reputation for developing players, which could be crucial moving forward.

So, it’s certainly one that they should pursue, and January will be a massive month for the Trotters if they want to ensure they can have a comfortable end to the campaign.
