Written in sand: How dunes reveal the secrets of planetary winds

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (53 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Stanford University researchers have shown that the median sand grain size in dunes on Earth and Mars reveals the speed of the winds that formed those dunes. The finding enables scientists to use sand as a wind speedometer for both modern and ancient dunes found across the solar system.
