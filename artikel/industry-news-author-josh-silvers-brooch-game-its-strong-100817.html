📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Author Josh Silver has been having quite the whirlwind year—thanks to his debut adult novel, Fruit Fly, which was released in August and is now a New York Times bestseller. Don’t call the book’s plot meta, though it does draw loose parallels to Silver’s own budding literary life: The dark satirical thriller follows a struggling author who “exploits a vulnerable young man’s life” to write his novel. “The whole thing has felt completely surreal, honestly,” Silver tells Vogue of releasing his buzzy breakout work. “Getting to travel and watch a story that started in my own head connect with readers all over the world is a feeling I don't think I’ll ever quite get used to.”.

Last week, Silver continued his months-long press tour for the book—this time, by attending the National Book Awards in New York City as a special guest. “It was an amazing way to cap off a busy year—getting to be in one room with so many of my personal heroes,” states Silver, who appreciated the opportunity to celebrate the art of writing. “Especially right now, it feels vital to celebrate the people fighting to keep diverse stories and voices on the shelves.” The cherry on top, meanwhile? He attended the event wearing a sleek and snazzy Celine tuxedo by Michael Rider.

In a recent development, styled by Christopher Rao, Silver’s polished look consisted of a black wool-and-mohair Celine tuxedo finished off with a white poplin dress shirt, black silk tie, leather belt, and lambskin jazz shoes. (Dance shoes are in, as you know.) “The tailoring is just razor-sharp,” notes Silver of his smart, modern look. “It feels classic, and effortlessly cool all at once.”.

Sources indicate that even more special were the thematic brooches that he punctuated the ensemble with. “My stylist Christopher paired it with these incredible 1940s Van Cleef & Arpels diamond fly brooches from FD Gallery,” Silver notes. “They’re a little nod to the book, and honestly, I feel very lucky to be wearing them. The whole look is really smart.” He also wore earrings and rings from Prounis Jewelry.

Recent findings suggest that you could say the dapper formal attire served as an extremely amped-up version of the author’s everyday style: He certainly isn’t rocking priceless jewels and bespoke tailoring while writing away at his desk every day. “Day-to-day, I’m quite low-key—mostly living in comfortable basics while I’m at my laptop,” he says. (What? Did you expect him to write in couture?) “That’s why nights like [the Book Awards] are so fun—I don’t get to clean up like this very often,” he says.

Through the end of the year, meanwhile, you’ll find Silver slipping back into his basics, toiling away at home: Fans will be pleased to know he’s working on his next highly-anticipated book. “It’s coming along nicely,” he teases, without giving anything away. “After all the travel this year, I’m actually really excited to just put my head down, disappear into that world, and finish it.” As are we.
