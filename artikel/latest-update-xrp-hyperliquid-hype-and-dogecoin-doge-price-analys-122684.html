XRP, Hyperliquid and Dogecoin are approaching notable price levels as selling pressure keeps rising. All three trade below major moving averages, leaving traders focused on nearby support and the strength of any short-term rebound. The sections below review the main price levels, market developments and conditions that could shape their next moves.

XRP remains under significant bearish pressure, trading near $0.9316 after a sharp decline from September highs around $1.65. The daily chart reveals a sustained downtrend, with successive lower highs and lower lows indicating that sellers continue to dominate price action.

It has been revealed that XRP has fallen below all its major moving averages, including the 200-day moving average near $1.30, reinforcing the bearish outlook. Meanwhile, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) remains around 30–35, suggesting weak momentum and approaching oversold conditions.

Immediate support stands at $0.90, followed by the critical $0.85 level. A breakdown below this region could accelerate losses toward $0.80. Conversely, buyers must reclaim $1.00 to initiate a meaningful recovery, with stronger resistance around $1.10 and $1.20.

According to recent reports, broader market developments add uncertainty. US spot XRP ETFs have experienced renewed selling pressure, with approximately $12.2 million in net withdrawals reported for November 13–20. These outflows highlight weakening institutional demand amid broader cryptocurrency market volatility.

Recent findings suggest that nevertheless, Ripple's expanding institutional ecosystem and ongoing XRP Ledger lending developments offer potential longer-term catalysts. Recent reports indicate growing validator support for proposed lending functionality, although implementation remains uncertain.

Hyperliquid continues to face significant selling pressure, trading near $62.43 after a steep decline from September highs around $97. The daily chart reveals a deteriorating technical structure, with lower highs and lower lows suggesting bears remain firmly in control.

HYPE has slipped below its major moving averages, including the 200-day moving average near $68, reinforcing the bearish outlook. Meanwhile, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) hovers around 35, indicating weakening momentum and approaching oversold conditions.

In a recent development, immediate support lies between $60 and $61. A decisive breakdown below this region could expose $57, followed by the crucial 52–55 demand zone. Conversely, buyers must reclaim 68–70 to establish a meaningful recovery, with stronger resistance around 76–78.

Recent findings suggest that market fundamentals present a mixed picture. Hyperliquid's expanding perpetual futures ecosystem and protocol-funded token buybacks continue to support its long-term investment narrative. However, increasing competition, token unlock concerns, and declining fee capture from builder-operated markets have complicated sentiment.

Hyperliquid's proposed expansion into regulated US perpetual futures markets could attract institutional participation, although implementation remains subject to regulatory approval. Meanwhile, scheduled token releases continue to raise questions about whether buyback demand can absorb additional supply.

Despite these developments, HYPE's immediate outlook remains bearish. Holding $60 could encourage a technical rebound toward $68, but sustained recovery requires stronger buying volume and renewed momentum.

In a recent development, dogecoin (DOGE) continues to face intense selling pressure, trading near $0.05558 after declining sharply from September highs above $0.10. The daily chart reveals a persistent bearish structure, with lower highs and lower lows reflecting sustained weakness across the meme coin market.

Industry observers note that DOGE trades below all major moving averages, including the 200-day moving average near $0.085, reinforcing the broader downtrend. Meanwhile, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) hovers around 25–30, indicating oversold conditions that could encourage a short-term rebound.

Immediate support stands at $0.054, followed by the psychological $0.05 level. A decisive breakdown below this zone could expose $0.045. Conversely, buyers must reclaim $0.06 to establish recovery momentum, with stronger resistance around $0.065 and $0.07.

Sources indicate that broader market conditions remain challenging. Bitcoin's November 20 decline below $60,000 intensified cryptocurrency market uncertainty, while weakening risk appetite continued to weigh on speculative assets. Dogecoin has been particularly vulnerable to the wider selloff.

Sources indicate that institutional demand also appears subdued. Recent reports indicate US spot Dogecoin ETFs recorded zero net flows between October 14 and November 16, highlighting limited upcoming investment activity. Meanwhile, declining futures positioning suggests traders are reducing leveraged exposure.

Sources indicate that despite oversold conditions, DOGE's technical outlook remains bearish. Holding $0.054 could trigger a relief rally toward $0.06, but sustained recovery requires stronger buying volume.

Until Dogecoin decisively reclaims $0.06, downside risks remain elevated, with $0.05 emerging as the next critical support.
