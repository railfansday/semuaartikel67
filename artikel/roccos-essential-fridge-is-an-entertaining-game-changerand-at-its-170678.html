Industry observers note that we rarely think of our fridge as the sexiest part of our home. Even less so a mini fridge, since it often reminds us of college party days. Rocco has been the brand to change that, with their viral Classic Fridge that’s in every cool-girl apartment (including Senior Commerce Editor Marina Liao’s). But the longstanding grievance people have with it? The price. So when I heard they launched a new fridge—The Essential—that’s even colder but $800 cheaper? I had to try it for myself.

It has been revealed that I’m a proud beverage girlie. I keep a triple coaster at my desk for when I switch between Diet Coke, iced coffee, and water throughout the day. A considerable amount of my fridge space was dedicated to canned drinks. The Rocco Essential fridge let me return my produce drawer to its original function. And then it became so much more. Scroll on to find out how it elevated my hosting game.

Best for: Hosting in small spaces (it doubles as a bar cart!).

Not ideal for: Storing perishable drinks like juices, shakes, or dairy.

Price at review: $995, discounted from $1,195.

Bottom Line: The Rocco Essential is the sleek younger sister to its viral Classic, and the Sight System takes the guesswork out of grocery shopping. You’ll always know how much wine you have at home.

Industry observers note that at its baseline, the Rocco Essential Fridge is a souped-up mini fridge, specifically designed to be the best home for your beverages. It can hold a grand total of 27 bottles of wine or 77 12-ounce cans, but an optional expansion pack increases that to 105 cans. It comes with six racks, each with indentations that keep your cans and bottles from rattling around. Every drink is stored on its side, and each shelf slides in and out easily without getting stuck. They can be removed and repositioned to accommodate bigger bottles, like two-liter sodas and Champagne.

The Rocco Essential right now comes in three colors: cream, sage, and graphite, each with a brown-tinted glass facade. The matte silhouette of the fridge is pretty sleek on its own, but there are optional add-ons to make it blend into your home a bit more. You can add a two-in-one oak serving tray and cutting board to the top, or swap the metal handle for a matching wooden one.

The most powerful feature is its compressor, made with the same technology as Sub-Zero fridges. It supports three cool-down modes: Smart, Quiet, and Party. Party mode offers rapid cool-down that’s great for high-traffic use (or cooling lukewarm sodas bought last-minute). Quiet mode uses a slower cool-down with less noise, and Smart switches between Party and Quiet as needed. And the compressor noise peaks at 39 decibels—library quiet.

It has been revealed that the Essential Fridge has many of the same benefits as the Classic—same powerful compressor, same app controls, and same beverage tracking database. One of the biggest changes is the look. The Essential has a more neutral color range than the pops of color in the Classic, and swaps a reeded glass facade for a warm tint.

The Essential’s main compromise is its single temperature zone, compared to the dual temperature zones of the Classic. In the Essential, if you have specialty wine that needs to be stored at a certain temperature. The rest of your drinks will have to be at that temperature too. That noted, the Essential is Rocco’s coldest fridge, getting down to 34ºF instead of the Classic’s 37ºF.

Both glasses are UV-rated, but the Essential has a dual-layer to the Classic’s triple. The Classic has slightly improved insulation because of this, but the tinted glass facade of the Essential keeps the state of your fridge’s interior more private. It also has a safety feature that the Classic doesn’t. The Essential comes with a childproof lock, which is extra important as the fridge is closer to the ground and easier for kids to access.

The Rocco Essential is my new best friend when I’m hosting. It’s so beautiful that I placed it just past my entryway, and without fail, guests coo over it when they see it. This also makes it easy for me to offer a cold beverage the second someone walks in the door, helping them settle in.

Recent findings suggest that the technology is stunningly easy to use—you can either control it with the iOS app or the slim control panel on the top of the fridge. But honestly? It’s a set-it-and-forget-it gadget, quietly humming along in the background. Sodas, wine, ready-to-drink cocktails, and cold brew are all kept ready to go. I’m impressed by how many cans or bottles can fit on one shelf, especially if you use mini cans. The bottom rack has a narrow clearance overhead, so I use that as my skincare drawer. I’m sure this confuses the app, but an ice-cold eye mask in the morning is too good to pass up.

What the Rocco Fridge is truly known for is its Sight System. The Rocco app controls the light and temperature, but it’s also connected to an ever-expanding beverage database. Its camera actually recognizes the drinks you place on each rack. Sometimes the drinks need to be rotated in order for the system to recognize them, but the app will let you know if this is the case. The fridge does a fresh scan every time you open or close the door. So, if you remove a soda, it will revision the quantity. You can check the app while you are away from home, say, picking up refreshments for a party, and you’ll know exactly what you’re working with. If the app doesn’t recognize your drink when you first add it, give it a few days, and it’s usually added to the database.

Industry observers note that the Rocco Essential is smaller than a typical full-size fridge, but it does take up considerable square footage. It functions as a high-tech bar cabinet would, so it’s the kind of piece you want to design around.

Industry observers note that the Rocco Essential fridge is the best gift you could ever give a frequent host. It’s eye-catching but minimalist. There’s no condensation on the fridge’s exterior, so you can store your favorite glasses or cocktail garnishes right on top. Even though I know I’ll be downsizing my home soon, I’m determined to keep it (or switch to their Mini Mini fridge). If you’re ready for a Rocco fridge of your own, the Essential is less than $1,000 for the first time EVER this Black Friday.
