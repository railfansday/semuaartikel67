Industry observers note that reminders of Him, one of the hit adaptations of Colleen Hoover novels, is the only film of that trio to be trending on Netflix nationwide. At the time of writing, Reminders of Him stands as the 7th most popular movie on Netflix in the United States. Last weekend's recommendations did not feature a single original film from the streamer, which I decided to change for our fresh options.

In a recent development, as for the only licensed film on our Netflix recommendations, that project happens to be one of the most popular movies of 2026, a true win for theaters. It is also one of the most engaging horror movies in recent history, birthing a couple of stars. These are the best movies to watch on Netflix this weekend:.

In a recent development, obsession is the only non-Netflix movie on the list, and it became a huge hit. With a budget of under $1 million and a box office total of $524.5 million, Obsession surpassed even the creative team's wildest expectations for the movie. All of that excitement is backed up by a solid concept with plenty of scary and unsettling moments, as well as a magnetic performance from Inde Navarrette.

Sources indicate that navarrette plays Nikki Freeman, who is changed by a supernatural event, as Michael Johnston's Bear makes a wish to a One Wish Willow, and it leads to Nikki becoming obsessed with him. That setup is perfect to give Navarrette a meaty role as Nikki slowly loses more and more of herself, only living to please Bear and going to extreme lengths to keep him only for her. Obsession is dark, topical, and comes as a breath of fresh air, birthing stars, with Navarrette cast as Rogue in Marvel's X-Men movie and Johnston starring in Brendan Fraser's The Mummy: Book of the Dead.

Staying in a similar dark tone to Obsession is the first of the Netflix Original movies on the list. Heartland stars Jessica Chastain as a former country singer on a mission. She comes back to Nashville to try to solve the mystery behind the sudden disappearance of her niece, Charlie Heart, a rising country star in her own right.

Heartland is a thriller with a sound lead performance from Chastain, who plays around with an intensity and swagger that elevate the overall story when it comes short. It also comes with a talented ensemble in the form of Garrett Hedlund (Tulsa King), Ross Lynch (Chilling Adventures of Sabrina), and more.

Finally, if you are looking for a delicious and fun romance to get lost in this weekend on Netflix, then the perfect call is the early holiday film Guarding Stars, which arrived on the streamer on November 18. Guarding Stars has two major actors in lead roles, with Supernatural's Jared Padalecki playing Jack Stapleton, an action movie star, and Gossip Girl's Leighton Meester as Hannah Brooks, the bodyguard hired to protect him.

Guarding Stars is the #1 movie on Netflix in the United States right now.

The characters' personalities could not be more different, but it is from their constant bickering and clashes that a romantic spark first manifests itself, and Padalecki has undeniable chemistry with Meester. Guarding Stars is based on Katherine Center's novel The Bodyguard. Guarding Stars should provide plenty of laughs and romance to set up Christmas season on Netflix this weekend.
