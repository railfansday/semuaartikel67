The Azamara Quest is right now wrapping up a drydock in Elefsis to become the first ship to undergo refurbishment as part of the Azamara Forward program.

After wrapping up its summer season in Piraeus, the vessel arrived at a Greek shipyard on October 31, 2026.

As part of the project, the 2000-built ship is getting a new deck with more staterooms, in addition to refreshed public rooms, as well as new venues, such as the Atlas Bar.

All staterooms are also being updated with upcoming finishes, improved lighting and design details aimed at enhancing comfort.

Sources indicate that the new Penthouse Deck adds 12 additional suites. The new premium staterooms will also introduce two new suite categories to the company’s fleet: Panorama and Grandview.

“We have designed these suites to have that calming, welcoming environment of a home away from home feeling for our guests,” said Martha Brabham, vice president of design and revitalization.

“So we would love our guests to enjoy the color scheme, the palette. We curated the elements in the room to really immerse you into that ocean experience.”.

Upon completion of the enhancements, the Azamara Quest is now scheduled to welcome guests back on November 26, 2026.

The ship is then set to offer itineraries in the Mediterranean, the Atlantic and the Caribbean before kicking off Azamara’s annual World Cruise in 2027.

“As the ship undergoes drydock, there’s a lot of work happening behind the scenes to bring Azamara Forward to life,” Azamara mentioned in a status patch.

“From refreshed spaces to thoughtful enhancements throughout the ship, every detail is coming together as the transformation takes shape,” the company added.

All ships in the company’s fleet are scheduled to undergo refurbishments as part of the Azamara Forward program.

The Azamara Onward will undergo the refit next, entering drydock in late 2027, followed by the Azamara Journey in late 2028.
