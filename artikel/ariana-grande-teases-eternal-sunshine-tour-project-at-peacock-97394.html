Ariana Grande is teasing a Peacock project centered on her Eternal Sunshine Tour that could be a documentary or concert film.

A clip of the singer was posted on social media from her tour, showing her standing in the middle of the stage and singing “Eternal Sunshine.” As the clip fades to black, text on screen reads, “Coming soon to… Peacock.”.

Sources indicate that “I don’t care what people say it’s true, say it’s true,” Grande is heard singing in the clip shared on Instagram by the NBCUniversal streaming platform and the singer. It has been revealed that arianators were already on the case and suspected the singer was working on something after Grande posted a photo on social media last week alongside director Christian Breslauer and editor Andrew Morrow. Breslauer worked with Grande on videos like “Yes, and?” and “We Can’t Be Friends (Wait for Your Love,” while Morrow is known for editing Beyoncé’s Renaissance: A Film by Beyoncé.

According to recent reports, grande toured Canada, England, and the U.S. with the Eternal Sunshine Tour earlier this year, kicking things off on June 6 at the Oakland Arena in Oakland. The singer would then travel to the Los Angeles area to perform six indicates between Crypto.com Arena and the Kia Forum. Other cities Grande stopped in included Austin, Atlanta, New York City, Chicago, Boston, and Montreal. Grande ended her tour at The O2 Arena in London, England, performing 10 shows, with her last performance on September 1st. Watch on Deadline.

Recent findings suggest that at the moment, Grande is in the middle of promoting her latest film, Focker-in-Law, in which she stars alongside Ben Stiller, Skyler Gisondo, Owen Wilson, Robert De Niro, and more. The comedy film opens in theaters on November 25.
