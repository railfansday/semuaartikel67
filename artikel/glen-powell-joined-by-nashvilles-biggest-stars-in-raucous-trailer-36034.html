In a recent development, glen Powell is surrounded by country royalty in the trailer for his upcoming musical comedy The Comeback King. The first trailer for the Judd Apatow-directed film dropped on Wednesday (Nov. 18) and it finds on-the-skids country star Aubrey King (Powell) returning to college to finish his degree after a very public infidelity scandal tanks his career.

“I’m on that stage every night doing a damn beer commercial,” Powell’s King laments at the beginning of the two-minute trailer as he takes a shot and sprays his audience with beer before bringing a fan on stage and chugging an entire can of suds from her cowboy boot as part of his gimmicky act. Cut to King puking his guts out backstage after the show as his manager (comedian Stavros Halkias) checks in on him.

“That lady wasn’t wearing socks, I just puked up a Band-Aid,” a disgusted King responds.

The film, co-written by Apatow and Powell, follows the country star as his career explodes and he gets a shout-out from his glitzy pop star girlfriend Missy (Madelyn Cline) at an awards show at a time when things could not be going better. Until he walks in on Missy cheating on him, which leads to a downward spiral of depression and confusion.

“I’m actually concerned for you,” Jelly Roll tells King during a studio session with the “Save Me” singer, Miranda Lambert and Brad Paisley. “You’re a mess,” Lambert agrees. “You smell like people think I smell,” Jelly adds. Then, when Missy releases a song claiming King was the one who cheated, his manager (Kumail Nanjiani) informs the country singer that the general online consensus is that he’s a scumbag, but also a “huge loser” and that, not for nothing, his eyes are too close together.

King’s life turns around when he falls in love with a professor at the college he enrolls in, Sam (Cristin Milioti) in the film that has gotten tons of headlines in the Nashville area amid sightings of Powell playing a country star around town, including scenes filmed on the closing night of the 2026 CMA Fest at Nissan Stadium. The cast also includes former SNL star Vanessa Bayer, as well as comedians Tig Notaro, Jin, Hao Li, Atsuko Okatsuka and Mike Birbiglia and a bevy of real country stars playing themselves, including Jelly, Paisley and Lambert, as well as Reba McEntire, Shaboozey, Lainey Wilson, Luke Bryan, Brandi Carlile, Christ Stapleton and Megan Moroney.

On the red carpet at Wednesday night’s 2026 CMA Awards, Powell told Billboard that the movie is a “love letter to country music,” noting that his sister, songwriter Leslie Powell, penned eight original songs for the film.

Sources indicate that the Comeback King is due out on Feb. 5, 2027.
