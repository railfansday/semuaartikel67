Throughout the years-long process of casting the upcoming James Bond, I tried to follow the watches to an answer. When Aaron Taylor-Johnson joined the Omega family in May 2025, it felt like watching the 007 Vatican spit white smoke from its steeple. Just this past weekend, fans were still pinning red strings on their bulletin boards between Jack Lowden and the MI6 superspy when images resurfaced of the Slow Horses star wearing Omega. Since 1995’s Goldeneye, Bond has been an Omega man—a relationship later canonized in one of cinema’s most iconic line readings, when Daniel Craig confirmed his brand of choice in three stretched-out syllables: Oh-Mee-Guh.

It has been revealed that if, like me, you were only basing your predictions on what the reported pool of potential Bonds wore on their wrists, Robert Pattinson would have been an easy name to cross off. The newly confirmed 007 only signed on as an ambassador for the Swiss luxury brand Jaeger-LeCoultre two short months ago! When I interviewed Pattinson about the appointment over the summer, he was effusive about his latest partner: “I very rarely even wear watches, but when I tried it on, I was like, ‘That’s my favorite watch.’ I wanted to just buy it.”.

Sources indicate that now, in addition to juggling his other iconic role as Batman, Pattinson will presumably need to balance his existing commitment to JLC with his latest gig’s long-held ties to Omega. (Reps for both brands did not immediately respond to requests for comment, though JLC conspicuously posted a photo of Pattinson on Instagram a few hours after the announcement.).

It has been revealed that during our conversation earlier this summer, Pattinson revealed what he looks for in a watch. He caught the collecting bug in March, when he wore JLC’s Master Control Chronometre Perpetual Calendar to the Oscars afterparties. “All these other actors with their fancy watches on asked me, ‘Hey, what are you wearing?’” Pattinson recalls. “It was one of those moments where I’m like, ‘It’s kind of badass having this thing on.’”.

Sources indicate that if it’s attention-grabbing badass watches Pattinson is after, he’s in good hands with Omega. The brand is best known for supplying the watches Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin wore when they landed on the moon in 1969. Omega has been happy to create special versions of the Speedmaster for its current partners to dial up its profile. And, of course, the Swiss house continues to expand its Bond-inspired offerings: The maker recently added a “Spectre” version of the Seamaster, 007’s preferred model, to the lineup. And whenever Omega needs to drum up buzz around soon-to-be-released models, they tend to show up on former Bond Craig’s wrists during public appearances.

Speculators trying to pin down the next Bond would have had better luck if they’d read some less recent watch tea leaves. Back in 2020, Christopher Nolan released Tenet, his inscrutable but enthralling time-warp thriller in which Pattinson plays a mysterious, dapper, and charming spy involved in a high-stakes mission to save the world—about as good a preview of what the 40-year-old actor will look like as 007 as you’ll find in his filmography. And the watch on his wrist in that flick? An Omega.
