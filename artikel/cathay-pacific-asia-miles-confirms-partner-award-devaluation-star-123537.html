📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that starting in January, it’ll take more Cathay Pacific miles to fly British Airways first class from Los Angeles to London than to fly Cathay first class all the way from Latest York to Hong Kong. The London flight goes from 135,000 to 162,000 Asia Miles each way. Hong Kong stays at 160,000. For two people booking London, that’s an extra 54,000 miles one way. A week after Chase added 1:1 transfers to Cathay Pacific, Cathay tells me its partner airlines are charging it more for award seats. It’s raising the mileage price of selected partner awards and dropping Companion Ticket Awards. The new rates kick in at midnight in Hong Kong on January 5, 2027. In the U.S., get your ticket issued before 11 a.m. Eastern on Monday, January 4 (10 a.m. Central, 8 a.m. in Los Angeles) for future travel to book at the old rates.
