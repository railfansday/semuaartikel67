After missing another practice on Friday, Brock Purdy (ankle) is out for Sunday's game against the Seattle Seahawks.

Recent findings suggest that the San Francisco 49ers quarterback is dealing with an ankle injury suffered last week against the Minnesota Vikings. It is not a high ankle sprain, head coach Kyle Shanahan noted this week, but Purdy is now set to sit out this week, leaving Mac Jones with the start. Jones won five of his eight starts last season as Purdy dealt with a toe injury.

It has been revealed that like Purdy, Justin Herbert (concussion) will also be out this week after not practicing. The Los Angeles Chargers quarterback was placed in protocol after Sunday's loss to the Newly introduced York Jets.

"This isn't my first rodeo with regard to starting quarterbacks having an injury you don't know at the beginning of the week when we start game planning," mentioned offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel, with DJ Uiagalelei set to start for the 1-9 Chargers after Herbert was ruled out.

Other Week 12 injuries to monitor:.

In a recent development, parker Washington has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Tennessee Titans. The Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver injured his shoulder in practice on Thursday and is considered week-to-week. Running back Bhayshul Tuten (foot/ankle) will also not play. Chris Rodriguez Jr. take over as the starter.

Las Vegas Raiders listed tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) has been listed as questionable after being limited early this week and missing Friday's practice. Bowers missed last week's loss to the Denver Broncos after exiting Week 10 early.

Recent findings suggest that kirk Cousins won't get one of his weapons back in Week 12 as wide receiver Jordan Addison (thumb) remains sidelined. Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O'Connell mentioned Addison is "progressing well," but he will not be back on the field in Week 12.

Tee Higgins (hamstring) woke up sore on Friday morning and did not practice for the Cincinnati Bengals. There is no worry there, head coach Zac Taylor mentioned, as he should be fine for the game against the Upcoming Orleans Saints despite the "questionable" designation.

Carson Beck (back) is ruled out for the second week in a row while dealing with back spasms. Josh Johnson, who started last week in a blowout loss to the Chiefs, will be starting under center for the Arizona Cardinals yet again.
