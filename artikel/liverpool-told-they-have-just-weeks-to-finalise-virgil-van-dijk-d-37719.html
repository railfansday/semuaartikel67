Liverpool are set to offer Virgil van Dijk a one-year deal with the option of an additional year.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 35-year-old's current terms are set to expire at the end of the season, with a decision on his future yet to be confirmed.

Van Dijk has been excellent under Andoni Iraola so far this season, but is on significant wages and will inevitably slow down at some point.

Liverpool have already begun to plan for life after the Netherlands international, with Jeremy Jacquet and Ronald Araujo both having arrived in the summer.

Giovanni Leoni is also thought to have a high ceiling, but is yet to make his return from injury after tearing his ACL almost 14 months ago.

Football Insider revealed this month that Liverpool are among the clubs tracking Ismaelo Ganiou, as they look to further prepare for Van Dijk's potential departure.

Both sides appear relaxed about the situation, with a decision expected to be made in the coming months.

The Reds are in a similar position with Alisson, with a decision also due to be made on his contract.

Industry observers note that "I think they won't mind him staying for a year. I would certainly expect a year with a year option because they haven't really replaced him," King told Football Insider.

"And he's a very good player to have playing alongside the young boy they've got there [Jacquet]. It helps him.

"But there again, his contract, June 2027. 30th of June, he could be a free agent. There's got to be an offer in January to not allow him to just walk away from the football club.

Sources indicate that "There again he's 35 but he looks pretty fit. The only thing I'd question on him is his pace.".

Should Van Dijk leave Liverpool next year, it is unlikely that he will retire.

The centre-back clearly has much left in the tank, and could well continue to play in the Premier League for another year.

In a recent development, "I feel good but it’s not about me at the moment. Let’s see. I’m not in any panic whatsoever. I want to keep playing and continue what I’m doing," he remarked earlier this month.

It has been revealed that "Let’s see what the season brings. I’ve had an amazing time already so far. If that will be extended, that’s the case.

According to recent reports, "If not, then I will go somewhere else. There’s nothing to report at the moment but that doesn’t mean that there’s any stress or anything.”.
