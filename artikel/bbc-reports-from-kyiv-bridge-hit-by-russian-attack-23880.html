Russian strikes hit two key bridges in Kyiv, with Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko saying the Ukrainian capital had suffered "damage to critical infrastructure systems" and disruption to heating and water supplies overnight.

Elsewhere, images shared by President Volodymyr Zelensky revealed a missile approaching Crimean Bridge. Zelensky said it was an "unprecedented operation" weeks in the making.

Built by Russia after it invaded and illegally annexed Ukraine's southern peninsula in 2014, the bridge was personally opened by Putin in 2018.
