Juan Mata, the former Chelsea and Manchester United playmaker, announced his retirement at the age of 38 on Wednesday, concluding a storied international and club career.

Industry observers note that A key part of Spain's most successful era in international football, Mata finished with 41 appearances and 10 goals. He lifted the World Cup in South Africa in 2010 and scored in the final of Euro 2012 two years later.

Sources indicate that "Sometimes, to love is to let go... Thank you, football," Mata captioned a video on his Instagram.

According to recent reports, the midfielder was widely praised for his technical ability and deft touch. In 2017, he co-rolled out the charity Common Goal, an initiative encouraging footballers to pledge one percent of their income to fund community projects and drive social change.

After starting out in Real Madrid's youth system, Mata emerged at Valencia before moving to Chelsea in 2011, where he won the Champions League, FA Cup and Europa League before departing for United in 2014.

During his time with the Red Devils, Mata won the Europa league, FA Cup and League cup before leaving the Premier League after a decade at the top level.

Leaving in 2022, Mata secured league titles in Turkey with Galatasaray and Japan with Vissel Kobe before wrapping up his career in Australia.

Mata left Melbourne Victory in September following a final season that produced five goals and 12 assists in 25 appearances.
