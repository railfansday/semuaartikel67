Charli XCX is ready to boom clap back at some criticism.

After American DJ John Summit clarified he wasn’t dissing the 2027 Grammy nominees when he said the nominations were a “f--king joke” for not respecting “real” electronic dance music, the Brat singer proved she did not love it.

Recent findings suggest that as Charli wrote on X Nov. 16 in response, “Not this guy again!”.

Summit (real name John Schuster) has previously praised Charli’s music on social media, even requesting to collaborate with her. However, he wrote on X in 2024, “Charli has left me on read but that’s ok i still stan.”.

Sources indicate that while Summit has never been nominated for a Grammy, the “Girl so confusing” singer won two trophies in the Dance/Electronic Music categories in 2025, including for Best Dance/Electronic Album for Brat. (She also won the 2025 Grammy for Best Recording Package.).

This year, Charli earned three nominations—one for Best Alternative Music Album for her album Music, Fashion, Film and two for Visual Media for her work on the Margot Robbie film Wuthering Heights.

After Summit was shut out of this year’s nominations—which saw artists like Madonna, Tinashe, Fred Again.., and Slayyyter among the Dance/Electronic nominees—he shared his frustration on social media.

Recent findings suggest that “Never submitting to the grammys again unless s--t changes,” he wrote in part on X. “The whole dance category is just music industry fueled pop stars.”.

Amid the backlash to his criticism, the “Where You Are” artist walked back his ire.

In a recent development, “Logging back on as i realized i didn’t do a good job of making my point earlier,” he wrote in a separate post to X. “My intent was not to drag other artists down and i seriously congratulate everyone who was nominated. They fully deserve the recognition.”.

Still, Summit added, “I just think there should be an extra category for more dance music djs & producers.”.

Of course, Summit wasn’t the only artist left out of the 2027 Grammy nominees. For all the shocking snubs and surprises from this year’s nominations, keep reading….

In a recent development, abrams failed to snag any nominations for her latest album Daughter From Hell, which she submitted for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album. Her single "Hit the Wall" was also absent from the Song of the Year, Record of the Year and Best Pop Solo Performance lists.

Abrams has previously received two nominations for Best Fresh Artist in 2024 and Best Pop Duo Performance for "Us" which featured Taylor Swift in 2025.

While Grande picked up some surprise nominations for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance for "For Good" alongside Cynthia Erivo, as well as Song of the Year and Best Music Video for "hate that I made you love me," and Best Pop Vocal Album for Petal, she was snubbed for Album of the Year.

Although Kahan scored one nomination for Best Contemporary Folk Album, he was not included in the major categories like Album of the Year and Song of the Year for his hit The Great Divide.

Beyoncé is famously the most decorated Grammy-winning artist—the 35-time winner scored her 100th nomination this year— but her Best R&B Solo Performance nod for "Morning Dew (Donk)" was somewhat of a surprise considering its delayed release in celebration of B'Day's 20th anniversary in July.

Sources indicate that this year was significant for Hobert, who released her debut album Who's the Clown last year, but she failed to score a nod for Best Fresh Artist. (Her brother Malcolm Todd was also left off the shortlist.).

Surprise: The Devil Wears Prada 2.

Recent findings suggest that that's actually not all for this much-anticipated sequel. The Devil Wears Prada 2 scored a surprise nomination for Best Compilation Soundtrack For Visual Media alongside Stranger Things season five, Wicked: For Good, The Wizard of Oz at the Sphere and Wuthering Heights.

It's been a long time coming for the first-time Grammy nominee, who scored her first nods for Best Dance/Electronic Recording for "Melatonin" as well as Best Dance Pop Recording for "Too Easy.".

Sources indicate that swift wasn't totally snubbed by the Recording Academy—she earned nods for Record of the Year, Album of the Year, Song of the Year, Best Pop Duo Performance alongside Sabrina Carpenter, Best Song Written for a Visual Media and more. But The Life of a Showgirl was not among Best Pop Vocal Album contenders, while "I Knew It, I Knew You" was snubbed for Best Country Song and Best Country Solo Performance.

Still, it was a surprise to see "I Knew It, I Knew You" score a nomination for Song of the Year, a category introduced by Swift herself during the nomination livestream, given Swift's other hits from The Life of a Showgirl.

The hype hasn't died down, Wicked is still oh so very popular. Wicked: For Good scored two nominations including Best Song Written For Visual Media and Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance.

Disco All The Time, Grammy Occasionally. The "Aperture" singer failed to score recognition for his latest album in any category. Styles has three Grammy wins under his belt, including Album of the Year for Harry's House in 2023.

Her comeback is far from over. The pop star received her first Grammy nominations in 16 years, racking up nods for Best Dance Pop Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Recording, Best Dance/Electronic Album and Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for "Bring Your Love" alongside Carpenter.

Although Lefty rose to massive acclaim this year for hits like "Boston," she failed to receive any recognition by The Recording Academy.
