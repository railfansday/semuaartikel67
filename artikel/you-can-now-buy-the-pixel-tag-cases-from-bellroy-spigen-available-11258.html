It has been revealed that A single Pixel Tag costs $29.99, while the 4-pack yields some savings at $99.99. The former is available from both the Google Store (arriving as early as Friday) and Amazon (Thursday delivery). We’re currently only seeing the 4-pack at Google’s storefront.
