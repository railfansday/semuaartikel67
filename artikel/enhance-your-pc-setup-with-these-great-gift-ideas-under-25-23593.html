But not everything has to cost the earth; with these great gift suggestions that all fall under $25, you can afford to treat yourself to an impulse purchase or perhaps stuff someone's stocking for the holidays.

According to recent reports, we've taken a selection of tech gifts from PC peripherals to speakers and even network connectivity. All available to buy now for $25 and under, and best of all, the majority of these products come from well-known brands such as Razer, TP-Link, Ugreen, JBL, and Redragon, so you're not taking a risk on purchasing a bunch of e-waste.

A low-cost desktop mic solution that offers noise-canceling recording and an easy-to-access mute function. Perfect for chatting with friends on Discord or with your work colleagues on Teams.

In a recent development, A full-size 104-key wired keyboard with bright LED lighting and membrane switches with a "Mechanical Feel". An excellent budget option for a gaming keyboard for either a PC or a game console.
