Grand Theft Auto 6 has been out in the hands of console players for a few days now, and a few of us at PC Gamer have been dusting off our PlayStations and Xboxes to dive into Leonida as we eagerly anticipate its eventual (but yet unannounced) PC release next year potentially.

I've been gaming it up myself and, as is always the case with GTA, I am absolutely loving driving around. Flying down the highway, exploring all the alleyways and retail areas, and taking out to the waters on a boat or jetski. All of it, of course, to the soundtrack of GTA 6's various radio stations.

Industry observers note that there are a huge number of stations in this game—19 of them, to be exact—with a ton of excellently-curated songs lingering within and famous cameo hosts. Usually I find myself just picking one station and sticking to it, but with GTA 6? I've been cycling between quite a few, each one offering something a little bit different.

Four stations have been grabbing most of my attention though. Pop station Flash FM has easily gained the most airtime in my playthrough so far. Am I listening to Ariana Grande in my everyday life? No. But am I bombing it down the highway while singing Break Free to myself every time it comes on? I absolutely am.

But I've also been peppering in a ton of 80s station Vice City FM. I mean, c'mon, you've got Weird Al Yankovic as a co-host, first of all. But you've also got straight up bangers like Lionel Richie's All Night Long, Simply Red's Money's Too Tight (To Mention), and Billy Ocean's Loverboy.

There's also Emotion 98.3, the radio station dedicated to songs about love (both being in love and being heartbroken, of course) and yes, I have stayed in the car waiting to hear Phil Collins' excellent drumming in In The Air Tonight. And finally, because I am a raging emo at heart, I've obviously had to whack on a bunch of The Chamber 106.6. Tool, System of a Down, and Metallica? Count me in.

But what about you? If any of you PC gamers are partaking on console, is only one radio station catching your attention, or have you found yourself channel hopping just like me? Or is there somehow not a single station that's caught your fancy, instead finding the podcasts a more entertaining use of your listening time? Be sure to drop a comment and let me know.
