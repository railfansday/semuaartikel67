Sometimes, the beautiful game can make Football Daily feel old. Even Monday’s ostensibly old-school fixture between Brentford and Everton felt like the future destroying the past. The analytics-coded hosts prevailed 4-0 and James Tarkowski was dismissed by the VAR overlords for a challenge that would have been considered “firm but fair” on the muddy pitches of our youth. “I don’t think you can tackle like that in this day and age,” a misty-eyed David Moyes sighed after the game, before heading to the team bus to rewatch the Battle of Santiago.

If Sunday was Gary Neville’s Sistine Chapel [Monday’s Football Daily], then his commentary on the Fernando Torres goal for Chelsea against Barcelona must be his David” – Ronnie McMahon.

Regarding Declan Hackett’s assertion [Monday’s letters] that Donald Trump will be helping his mate Mauricio Pochettino by ringing the FA to overturn Lenny Yoro’s red card, perhaps Poch should be careful what he wishes for: Trump may equally decide to get Michael Carrick reinstated, as he also has a bit of previous for that sort of thing” – Dexter O Riordan.

Recent findings suggest that in all the time that I have been watching football, I don’t think I have ever seen a player dismiss himself and be away down the tunnel before given a red card. Thanks James Tarkowski” – Nigel Sanders.

Brighton had only failed to win twice in the league since 5 September. A 1-1 draw with Leeds and a 2-0 defeat at Liverpool - their only opponents with double letters in their name.
