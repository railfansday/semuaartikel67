It’s a good day to be an investor in a tech stock, with shares of companies from Alphabet to Micron up in premarket trading. Concerns over an AI bubble, the Nvidia scandal, and global instability have led to some slumps recently. The tech-heavy Nasdaq is down 7.57% over the last month as of Wednesday’s close. Now it looks like a turnaround could be within reach as Anthropic, maker of the Claude AI chatbot, is set to make its IPO. No matter how far under a rock you are, it’s hard to avoid the news of Anthropic’s market debut and the impact it could have on both the tech industry and stock market. Anthropic is the first fully AI firm of this size to go public, and its high valuation sends a clear signal to investors that the demand for AI is there. As reported by the Financial Times, the company raised a record-breaking $107 billion in the offering (including a private placement). So, how are the biggest tech stocks shaking out ahead of Anthropic’s IPO? Let’s start with the Magnificent 7: Alphabet Inc. (Nasdaq: GOOG): up 4.15% Amazon.com, Inc. (Nasdaq: AMZN): up 2.14% Apple Inc. (Nasdaq: AAPL): up 0.41% Meta Platforms, Inc. (Nasdaq: META): up 1.47% Microsoft Corporation (Nasdaq: MSFT): up 1.22% Nvidia Corporation (Nasdaq: NVDA): up 1.58% Tesla, Inc. (Nasdaq: TSLA): up 1.93% Then there’s memory chip companies, which are seeing a significant boost across the board:  Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU): up 4.97% SanDisk Corporation (Nasdaq: SNDK): up 4.74% Seagate Technology Holdings plc (Nasdaq: STX): up 5.75% Western Digital Corporation (Nasdaq: WDC): up 5.56% Over on South Korea’s Kospi Index, SK Hynix and Samsung closed up 3.41% and 1.13%, respectively, on Thursday. What’s the latest with Anthropic’s IPO? On Thursday, Anthropic will make its stock market debut on Nasdaq with the ticker ANT. The company is listing its shares at $83, an increase from its earlier estimate of $70 to $78 per share, making for a valuation of up to $1.5 trillion. The offering will include 980 million shares of Class A common stock.
