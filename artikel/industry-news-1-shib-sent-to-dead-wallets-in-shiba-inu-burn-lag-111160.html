Shibburn’s page reveals that just $1 worth of SHIB has been burned over the past 24 hours, indicating a decrease in Shiba Inu’s token-burning activity.

The daily burn rate has dropped by 37.21% due to a burn of 303,294 SHIB worth just $1 over the last day.

The latest figure suggests that very little SHIB was destroyed during the reported period, although an increase in burn rate was seen on a weekly time frame.

157.31 million SHIB were burned in the last seven days, with the burn rate increasing by 113%; a different scenario presents itself on a 30-day basis, when the monthly burn rate fell 48.87% with 343.11 million SHIB burned.

Burns involve sending tokens to inaccessible wallet addresses, effectively taking them out of the available supply, with Shiba Inu advancing this course as 41.08% of its initial supply has now been erased.

A total of 410,845,365,512,059 SHIB has been burned in 21,978 transactions, according to Shibburn.

Shiba Inu fell to a fresh low of $0.00000337 as widespread selling picked up across the crypto market.

In a recent development, at the time of writing, SHIB remains down 5.73% over the last 24 hours to $0.00000362. Just below SHIB's current price, a large buy wall has emerged, with Binance's spot order book revealing 17.92 trillion SHIB positioned at $0.00000361.

SHIB is exhibiting a strong accumulation of buy orders, which could signal a key support level as traders monitor buyers’ ability to soak up selling pressure.

A buy wall occurs when a lot of limit buy orders are stacked up around some price level and can show strong buying interest, providing temporary support if market participants are willing to fill those orders.

Industry observers note that now, if the price of Shiba Inu goes lower, then the 17.92 trillion SHIB order concentration that was reported could be an important area to watch. If the orders get filled and remain there, they could help absorb some of the selling pressure.
