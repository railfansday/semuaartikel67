📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

On November 17, The Rx Assistant, an AI-powered service that helps patients streamline the prescriptions process by finding doctors, verifying insurance coverage, and enrolling patients in savings programs, unveiled the launch of Builder — a specialized platform for pharmaceutical brands to deliver AI experiences for patients.

The Rx Assistant Builder is a no-code platform that enables pharmaceutical brands to rapidly create, get regulatory approval for, and deploy compliant AI patient and provider support tools across web, voice, SMS, and email.

It has been revealed that the aim of Builder was to further simplify the prescription process for both pharmaceutical manufacturers and patients. From a pharmaceutical brand standpoint, Builder helps gather different tools and processes into one place. The flexibility of user experience, such as being able to switch from online to voice chat, allows manufacturers to cater to different needs depending on different patient populations.

“AI, when deployed correctly, can decrease time wasted in the ‘healthcare consideration cycle.’” said Jake Coniglio, co-founder and CEO, The Rx Assistant. “This counter balancing force can simultaneously allow patients and HCPs to to source information faster and go deeper.”.

In its early deployments, The Rx Assistant found that its AI assistant outperformed brands’ own websites on a 3 to 1 scale, on tasks such as finding doctors and completing symptom quizzes. Brands were also able to move from contract signing to approval in eight weeks.

In a recent development, “By deploying our platform to both patients and healthcare providers we can remove a lot of friction on both ends of the journey. With that in mind, we want to be where people are already going and help make that experience more meaningful.” mentioned Coniglio.

Sources indicate that “Brands spend millions on patient support programs that patients don’t end up using in large part because they are unaware. We can be the glue that links these disparate silos together.”.
