Publisher Devolver Digital is giving away free copies of its new game to players who delete GTA 6 from their consoles.

With Rockstar’s long-awaited sequel almost here at last, players can download GTA 6 ahead of time and have it ready to go as soon as it unlocks.

If you’ve already got it sitting on your console, Devolver wants you to give up that head start for a chance to get its own game for free.

The publisher announced the giveaway for Volvy’s Adventure: Reslimed, which also launches on November 19, calling it “November’s hottest release.” It’s a retro 2D platformer where you play as Devolver’s mascot Volvy, who is trying to rescue his friends from a group of warlocks.

To enter, record a video showing yourself deleting your GTA 6 preload, then quote-post Devolver’s giveaway announcement on X with the clip and the hashtag #MakeRoomForVolvy.

Once posted, you’ll have a chance of winning a Steam key for Volvy’s Adventure: Reslimed.

Industry observers note that you can enter using footage from your console, but you’ll need a PC to play the game if you win. Devolver explained in a follow-up post that Volvy’s Adventure: Reslimed is “launching exclusively on PC” and that “Steam keys are the only prize available for this giveaway.”.

According to recent reports, plenty of fans have already taken them up on the offer, too. The quote tweets show plenty of customers deleting Grand Theft Auto, while one was even willing to cancel their pre-order from a retailer.
