Customers on MEXC’s global exchange can’t use their platform-issued debit cards following an issuer problem.

It has been revealed that the exchange announced that its Global Card service ends today, November 18, 2026, at 15:30 UTC after its third-party card issuer made service adjustments. Existing cards will no longer process transactions and fresh applications are suspended.

The virtual Visa card introduced on August 31 with features that included up to 10% USDT cashback and high spending limits. It lasted under three months.

MEXC Global Card Service Update We regret to announce that, following service adjustments by our third-party card issuer, MEXC Global Card services will be discontinued on Nov 18, 2026, at 15:30 UTC. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause. We remain… pic.twitter.com/QXLFMeRvd7.

The shutdown follows the earlier discontinuation of MEXC’s APAC Card on November 11 over similar issuer complications, and it comes after a temporary maintenance pause that began on November 14.

Cardholders can still withdraw their remaining balances to their MEXC Fiat account before the cutoff.

Any funds left on the card will be snapshotted on November 21 at 15:30 UTC and credited to Spot accounts within three business days.

Refunds for prior transactions will go to Fiat accounts once received, pending cashback continues on schedule, and people are advised to cancel or switch any recurring payments tied to the card.

MEXC has not identified the card issuer or detailed the exact nature of the adjustments. Funds are not frozen; they are being returned to owners’ exchange accounts.
