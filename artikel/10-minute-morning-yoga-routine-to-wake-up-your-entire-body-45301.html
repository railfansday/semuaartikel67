And then go in between these two poses, getting into all parts of the spine, including the neck, upper back, mid back, lower back, all the way to your tailbone, trying to get that full range of motion.

In a recent development, still on your hands and knees, lift your right leg and trace some circles with it, as massive as you can, and then let’s reverse the direction of those circles. You’re simply trying to create movement through your right hip. You don’t need to overthink this one.

From Downward-Facing Dog, reach your right leg toward the sky, bend your right knee, open your hip, and take one more of those big hip circles from your Three-Legged Dog.
