Head coach Fabian Hurzeler admits it was not always easy for Brajan Gruda at Brighton.

Gruda joined Brighton in 2024 for £25m from Bundesliga club Mainz and revealed glimpses of his potential with the Seagulls.

The German playmaker made 43 appearances and claimed four goals with eight assists but struggled to nail down a regular starting position.

Right wing and the No 10 spot is Gruda's preferred role and he was regularly behind the likes of Yankuba Minteh, Diego Gomez and Georginio Rutter in the pecking order.

Gruda joined RB Leipzig on loan for the second part of last term and then agreed to a season-long loan last summer.

The 22-year-old has impressed in the Bundesliga and has five goals and eight assists in total for Martín Demichelis' Leipzig, who are fourth in the German top flight.

Gruda also dazzled for Germany last week as he scored a fine individual goal on his full debut against Serbia and then claimed two assists and netted a goal in Germany's 5-0 win against The Netherlands. Brighton's Bart Verbruggen was in goal for the Dutch.

It has been revealed that germany boss Jurgen Klopp heaped praise on Gruda. "He's a fantastic player who loves to take people on," remarked the former Liverpool manager. "And he hasn't yet fulfilled his potential.".

Gruda is contracted with Brighton until 2028 and his loan at Liepzig is believed to have an obligation to buy, with certain conditions.

“The obligation to buy for Brajan Gruda will be activated in 2027 due to accounting reasons," posted German transfer expert Florian Plettenberg. "The total package for RB Leipzig will amount to €30m.".

He also stated that Bayern Munich are keeping a close eye on the situation.

Gruda's development has left many Brighton fans hoping for a return - especially with the uncertainty surrounding Minteh, who almost joined Liverpool last summer.

"I'm also full of praise for Gruda," mentioned Fabian Hurzeler to Sussex World ahead Sunday's trip to Hull City.

Recent findings suggest that "Because when I see him playing, I see a great development. He's doing the basic things well. I think one thing that Jürgen always mentioned is to make him run back, to make him defend.

"I think this will always be the key for his game. Then in possession, he always has the quality to beat an opponent.".

Asked why it did not work out for him at Brighton, Hurzeler told Sussex World: "Again, he was very young with us. For us, it was always vital to give him more game minutes.

Industry observers note that "He had a lot of competition when Minteh was playing in this position, Diego Gomez was in this position. So it was not that easy for him to get those game minutes.

"Then he made the move to Leipzig, where he had more and more game minutes.

"Especially with these young players, I think you see brilliantly now with our young players, if they get game minutes, if they have this trust and confidence, then they are capable of doing really good things.

"I think that was the only issue here with him, that he didn't have this confidence and these game minutes, and now he can really express himself.".

It has been revealed that hurzeler is though delighted by his improvement and believes working with Klopp is a "good fit" for Gruda. The Brighton boss was however coy on whether Gruda will be seen in Brighton colours again.

Industry observers note that "It's a joy to watch him,” he remarked to Sussex World. “I'm really happy to see him playing like this. I think it's a good fit with Jürgen.

"I think that Jürgen really gets this belief into his players and therefore it was a joy to watch Germany playing this week.

"To see them chasing as a team, to see them doing counter-pressing, gegenpressing, and that's really good.

"I'm really happy for Brajan, so let's see how the future will be.".
