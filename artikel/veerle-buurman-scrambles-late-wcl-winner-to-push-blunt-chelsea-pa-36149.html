Chelsea were made to work hard for three Champions League points against a resilient Paris Saint-Germain side but their patience paid off, Veerle Buurman squeezing the ball in at the near post to put them, temporarily at least, at the top of the table in the league phase.

Buurman had stolen the show in Chelsea’s defeat of Manchester City at the weekend, coming on 12 minutes in and nullifying Khadija Shaw’s threat to be named player of the match, and she was the hero under the lights with Chelsea doing everything but score prior to her goal.

It has been revealed that “She is a top, young talent and is so mature on and off the pitch,” said Sonia Bompastor. “She is a confident player and a really complete player.”.

Bompastor’s side were frustrated by Häcken last week, held to a 0-0 draw by the Swedish side and falling out of the top four automatic qualification places. There was a desire to put things right at AFC Wimbledon’s stadium and they did just that, albeit in not the prettiest of encounters.

“Sometimes in football you need to find a way to win and the result tonight was really notable for us,” stated Bompastor.

Lauren Hemp and Khadija Shaw both scored off the bench to earn Manchester City a 2-0 Women’s Champions League victory over Häcken in Gothenburg.

The first half ended goalless with City's best chances coming just before the break. Aoba Fujino was put through on goal but she hit her effort straight at Häcken keeper Jennifer Falk, who made saves from Grace Clinton and Rebecca Knaak to keep the hosts on terms at the interval.

It has been revealed that hemp and Shaw were both introduced at half-time by Andrée Jeglertz, and the England winger opened the scoring after 55 minutes. It was a fortuitous breakthrough, with Emma Östlund's clearance ricocheting off Hemp's shin and into the net [pictured]. Mary Fowler appeared to have added a second soon after, but her strike was ruled out for offside against Yui Hasegawa.

Soon after Hemp was denied her second goal when her shot hit the post, she turned creator by passing to an onrushing Shaw, who tucked home for City's second in the 77th minute. The visitors could have had a third, but Falk saved Fowler's stoppage-time penalty after a VAR intervention.

Sources indicate that signe Bruun also made the difference off the bench on Wednesday, scoring two late goals as Real Madrid wore down 10-woman Internazionale to win 3-1. The Danish forward put Madrid ahead five minutes of time before adding a late penalty to secure all three points.

Lineth Beerensteyn got the hosts off the mark in Madrid after 28 minutes, and Inter faced an uphill battle after Karolina Vilhjalmsdottir was sent off three minutes before the break. The visitors got an unexpected equaliser when Michela Cambiaghi headed in from a corner, and held on until Bruun's late double.

It has been revealed that city and Real Madrid are both unbeaten after five league phase games, winning two and drawing three matches each as they bid to finish in the top four and avoid the playoffs. Juventus join them on nine points after late goals from Annamaria Serturini and Matilde Pavan earned a 2-0 home win over Leuven. PA and Guardian sport.

PSG had lost 2-1 at home to Bayern Munich after three draws and one win in the competition before their trip to London. Denied a point late on, they remain 10th and dangerously close to slipping out of the playoff places with one game to play.

In a recent development, chelsea had been thrillingly gutsy and diligent in their 1-0 win over City on Saturday and against PSG they were similarly solid at the back, but they lacked spark in attack. Ellie Carpenter became the focal point of their forward play from the right, with the striker Melvine Malard injured in the game against City.

PSG nullifying the threat of Carpenter blunted Chelsea, and despite their dominance of the ball, amassing 64% possession in the first half, Chelsea struggled to carve a way through the visitors’ backline. They had seven attempts on goal to PSG’s six at the break, despite 33 attacks to 10.

Chelsea have historically performed well against PSG, winning three of their four Champions League meetings prior to this penultimate fixture of their league phase campaign. In the 2018-19 quarter-finals they earned a 2-0 home win and, after a 2-1 away defeat, progressed to the semi-finals; in the 2022-23 season they won both group meetings with an overall score of 4-0.

Recent findings suggest that the visiting team’s first attempt on target arrived after the break, with Merveille Kanjinga forcing a near-post save from Hannah Hampton. While Chelsea still dominated, PSG had more forays forward in the second half.

Erin Cuthbert, Maika Hamano and Lucy Bronze arrived at the hour mark as the home team sought to deal the killer blow their dominance required. It increasingly felt like it would take something special to break the deadlock, akin to the sublime effort that Sandy Baltimore had delivered against City.

According to recent reports, aggie Beever-Jones was replaced by Sjoeke Nüsken late on and the German instantly made a difference, forcing a sublime save from Katarzyna Kiedrzynek. The breakthrough came from the resulting corner, and it was far less sublime than Saturday’s effort, Buurman somehow squeezing the ball in at the near post after PSG failed to clear Cuthbert’s ball in.

Industry observers note that “The main feeling is one of frustration,” mentioned the PSG manager, Paulo César. “We defended well, we just lacked a bit of clarity on the transition and I have mixed feelings. I’m proud of my team but it demonstrates that there are very fine margins that we need to rival a major team like Chelsea.”.

Ultimately, the goal was the bit of luck Chelsea needed and they look increasingly formidable.
