Industry observers note that save more by joining Booking .com’s loyalty program, Genius, which provides discounts and rewards for travel-related expenses. Members can enjoy up to 20% off on stays and 15% off on car rentals. The program has three tiers: Level 1 offers a 10% discount on select stays and cars; Level 2 offers up to 15% off after five bookings within two years; and Level 3 offers up to 20% off stays and 15% off rentals after 15 bookings in two years. Remember to sign in when browsing to access discounts of up to 50%, free cancellations, and exclusive secret deals from Booking .com.

According to recent reports, if you’re a frequent traveler, the Booking.com Genius Rewards Visa Signature Credit Card is a smart way to earn extra rewards on your everyday expenses. There’s no annual fee, and you can earn 6% travel credits on bookings made through the Booking.com app and 5% on other travel booked on Booking.com when you use this card. Additionally, you’ll receive $150 in travel credits after spending $1,500 within the first 90 days. Increased usage unlocks more benefits—reaching Genius Level 3 unlocks rewards such as 10-20% discounts on select stays and 10-15% off certain rental cars, along with priority support.

A cruise is one of the most affordable ways to enjoy an all-inclusive vacation while staying on the mainland and partying at sea. If you’ve ever been curious about taking a cruise (or are a returning sailor), now’s a great time to book for so much less. If you book a qualifying sailing departing on or before December 31, 2027, you’ll get up to $1,000 to spend onboard, which counts toward almost anything, including cocktails, specialty dining, spa treatments, and other onboard purchases.

It has been revealed that the amount you’ll get to spend varies based on the total cruise price and doesn’t include travel protection, port charges, port expenses, or taxes. It’s generally as follows: $25 per $1-$999 booking, $50 per $1,000-$1,499 booking, $75 per $1,500-$1,999 booking, $100 per $2,000-$2,999 booking, $125 per $3,000-$3,999 booking, $175 per $4,000-$5,999 booking, $250 per $6,000-$7,999 booking, $350 per $8,000-$9,999 booking, $500 per $10,000-$14,999 booking, $750 per $15,000-$19,999 booking, and $1,000 per $20,000 or more booking.

Plus, if you don’t have the full amount due for a cruise right now, you can still lock in for just $25. As long as you pay a small, non-refundable $25 fee, Booking will advance your cruise line deposit (up to $500). Your deposit will be automatically charged 10 days before your final payment is due, giving you peace of mind and more time to strategy (and save!).

In a recent development, if you have time off for your next vacation but don’t know where to go, I’d check out Booking’s seasonal and holiday deals page, which features rotating and flash deals based on the time of year, seasonality, and location. There are time-specific destinations, such as Lunar New Year deals, spring holiday deals focused on warm-weather travel destinations, and Carnival deals for places that celebrate in style, like the Big Easy.

Recent findings suggest that if you’re booking travel to a destination that requires a car, I’d highly recommend renting one through Booking. Booking partners with the most popular rental car companies, including Avis, Budget, Hertz, Enterprise, and more, so you can book with peace of mind. When you travel more, you’ll spend less—all you need to do is sign in, browse car rental options, and look for the blue ‘Genius’ label to save 10% on car rentals. Plus, if you spend with your Genius Rewards Visa, you can earn 5% in travel credits on car rentals.
