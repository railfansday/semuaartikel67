‘Do You Have What It Takes?’: KATSEYE Appears in Trailer for K-Pop Film Inspired by Their Experience

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that the upcoming movie K-Pop: The Debut centers on a televised competition series aiming to assemble a “truly global girl group” with contestants from all over the world. Sound familiar? It definitely does to KATSEYE.

In a recent development, that’s why it only makes sense that the real-life girl group makes a cameo in the film, as shown in a new trailer that dropped Monday (Nov. 23). “Hey, it’s KATSEYE!” members Daniela, Sophia, Megan, Lara and Yoonchae say in unison at the beginning of the preview, after which Yoonchae asks: “Do you have what it takes to debut as the next K-pop superstar?”.

The trailer then introduces viewers to leading lady Lizzy Lee — played by Ji-young Yoo — who is working toward becoming a doctor but dreams of being a star. After seeing KATSEYE’s callout, she sends in an audition tape for K-Pop Superstar, which leads her to travel to Korea to compete amongst dozens of other hopefuls with varying levels of dance skills, vocal abilities and humility.

“We want to create a truly global girl group with the best talent in the world,” an executive indicates at one point in the trailer over scenes of Lee and her competitors in intense rehearsals and performing together on stage.

Arriving in theaters on Feb. 26 next year, the Benson Lee-directed project will feature a star-studded soundtrack of songs by BTS, aespa, ENHYPEN, Twice, JISOO of BLACKPINK and, of course, KATSEYE. It’ll also star Eric Nam, Jitae Yoo, Tony Revolori, Gia Kim, Yim Si-wan, Ahin Lee, Aliyah Turner, Renata Vaca, Sung Jun, Kang Sora, Hyongchol Lee, Silia Kapsis, Shana Kim and Park Jubi.

According to recent reports, “A young Korean-American woman has spent her life following the path her family chose for her,” a synopsis reads. “But when she earns a spot in a globally televised competition to form the next K-pop girl group, she travels to Seoul, South Korea, to pursue the dream she must still keep hidden. Surrounded by extraordinary performers from around the world, all competing for just four coveted spots, Lizzy must push herself further than she ever imagined.”.

The Paramount Pictures film was produced in part by Hybe Americas, three years after Hybe x Geffen’s The Debut: Dream Academy experiment. The K-Pop: The Debut trailer does not directly cite KATSEYE’s experience as inspiration, but its plot does follow the Dream Academy premise to a tee; just like in the movie, the 2023 web series narrowed down a class of trainees through a combination of judge and fan voting to determine who would earn a spot in what the organization billed as the world’s first global girl group.

After KATSEYE was assembled, Netflix captured the process in more detail behind the scenes with its Pop Star Academy docuseries.
