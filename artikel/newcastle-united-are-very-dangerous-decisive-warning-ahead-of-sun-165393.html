Pascal Gross has been Brighton’s top player this season.

The midfielder is now 35 years of age but at the top of his form.

He is also still desperate to play as much football as possible.

Pascal Gross was one of only three first choice eleven players who started on Thursday in the Europa League after losing at Hull City last weekend in the Premier League.

The German international now looking forward to Newcastle United on Sunday but issuing a warning to his team-mates.

Pascal Gross: “They have a lot of fast players and a lot of physical players. Newcastle United are very dangerous. We need to be at our best if we want to win the game.”.

According to recent reports, brighton dominated last weekend but still lost 2-0 to the Tigers, Pascal Gross: “I think Hull was a big lesson, the Premier League is very tough. You need to be up to speed if you want to get the three points, but we are confident, we play at home, and we want to keep our form.”.

Pascal Gross appreciating that a very busy key part of the season is upcoming: “If you don’t win games very fast, it can turn around. Now it’s the winter period, so it’s a bit different – now every three days we play difficult games. We play Newcastle, Bournemouth, and Forest in six days, and you need to keep winning, which is really difficult. I’m interested myself to see how we handle it as a group, and I hope we will handle it well.”.

Pascal Gross on the Brighton fans… “We always need a good crowd. We need our fans to get behind us to make it difficult for the opponent and support us. They do it brilliantly and we try to give them something to jump on. We try to bring the intensity, play forward, and push, so I think it’s a combination.”.
