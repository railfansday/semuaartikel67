By now, anyone reading this has probably gotten themselves a PS5 or Xbox and played at least a handful of hours of Grand Theft Auto VI after it introduced last week. Next time you go to play the game on your console of choice, there will likely be a patch you have to download.

On Monday, Rockstar released the first official post-launch update for GTA 6. It's not a hugely seismic patch by any means, as it largely addresses a handful of in-game glitches that range from minor to major. Since GTA 6 is not a multiplayer game, you might be able to play it without downloading the patch, but based on the patch notes, you will probably want to wait for it to install first.

In a recent development, according to Rockstar's upgrade notes, the first GTA 6 patch is intended to fix seven common bugs players had encountered in the first few days after launch. Here are all the things Rockstar remarked it fixed on Monday:.

"Several concerns" that had caused the game to crash.

An glitch that caused weather to not change for extended periods of time.

An issue where the game would not auto-save correctly.

"Several complications" that caused in-game phone calls, including ones that push the story forward, to not trigger on time.

An glitch where players could not lose their wanted levels.

An problem where players could not deadlift at the gym.

An problem that caused partner characters to not hand bags of contraband to the player.

Industry observers note that as you can see, these range from minor irritations to nearly game-breaking glitches. Phone calls not triggering properly is especially bad, as they regularly unlock new story missions. Obviously, not being able to lose a wanted level would also be a pretty big bummer.
