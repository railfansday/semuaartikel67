If you've been waiting for the Black Friday deals to drop to upgrade your outdoor wear, I've got good news. REI deals have rolled in and I've hand-picked three Patagonia and Arc’teryx deals you absolutely shouldn't skip. These are jackets I own, and swear by.

Sources indicate that my top deal right now is the Patagonia Houdini Stash Half-Zip Pullover, which is discounted to $99 from $145 at REI. This tiny little jacket weighs less than your iPhone and can easily be stowed in your pocket.

Real talk: I own three versions of Patagonia's Houdini Jacket, including a full-zip hoodie, full-zip jacket, and the Patagonia Stash Houdini Half-Zip shown here. Weighing just 3.7 ounces, this little wonder crams into a back pocket with ease. Yet, when worn and zipped up, it provides a surprising amount of warmth, wind- and water-resistance. The women's jacket is also on sale for $99.

The Arc'teryx Squamish Jacket is similar in design to Patagonia's Houdini line of ultra-light packable wind- and rain-resistant shells. And like the Houdini, I'm a huge fan of the Squamish line (I own the hooded one, also on sale below). This deal is on the "Arctic Silk" style for a cool 44% off.

I've noted it before, and I'll say it again, the Arc’teryx Squamish Hoody is one of my all-time favorite featherweight, packable pieces of outerwear, and a staple for outdoor adventures and travel.
