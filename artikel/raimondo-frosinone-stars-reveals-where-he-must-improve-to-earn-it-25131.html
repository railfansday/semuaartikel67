Raimondo: Frosinone stars reveals where he must improve to earn Italy call-up

Frosinone striker Antonio Raimondo indicates he must improve in two areas before earning a senior Italy call-up and names Euro 2020 winner Ciro Immobile as one of his role models.

Raimondo is one of Serie A’s top scorers this season, with eight goals in ten league games. The 22-year-old hasn’t yet received an Italy call-up and, perhaps, only a recent injury has kept him out of Roberto Mancini’s squad for the November break.

“I’m not thinking about expectations placed on me. When things go well, you are always good; when they don’t, you become the worst. I think it’s notable to find balance,” Raimondo told Tuttosport.

When asked about his role models, the Frosinone star replied: “Cavani, Icardi and Immobile. Three fantastic strikers, and I’d like to take something from all of them. I still watch games of great players to study their movements and understand their mindset: being clinical in front of goal, how to attack space, or move into open field.”.

Raimondo already discussed a potential Italy call-up in previous interviews, so what does he need to do to finally fulfil his dream?

“I must keep doing what I’m doing – he replied – and try to improve in the areas I mentioned before: playing with my shoulders to the goal and linking up with my teammates.”.

In a recent development, frosinone are arguably the biggest surprise in Serie A this season, as the newly promoted side sits 6th in the standings, ahead of Juventus and Napoli. Can they aim for something more than survival?

“First, we think about survival, and once we’ve achieved that, we’ll think about something different,” Raimondo concluded.
