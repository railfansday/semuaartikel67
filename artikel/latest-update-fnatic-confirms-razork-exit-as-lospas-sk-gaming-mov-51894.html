Latest Update: Fnatic confirms Razork exit as Lospa’s SK Gaming move deepens 2027 rebuild

🌐 Source: Esportsinsider • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (449 words, 📖 Standard)

  • The club marked the departure with a farewell video titled “Thank You, Iván”.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Fnatic has confirmed the departure of its longest-serving player, jungler Razork, who is set to join Team Heretics. With support Lospa also set to leave, the London club faces a near-total LEC roster rebuild for 2027.

Recent findings suggest that fnatic has made its first confirmed exit official as it rebuilds its LEC roster for 2027. Razork was the London club’s longest-serving player and its main playmaker. He leaves after five seasons in the jungle.

The club marked the departure with a farewell video titled “Thank You, Iván”. In it, Fnatic recalls him arriving as a young player with huge ambition. He had entered the LEC with Misfits Gaming in 2020 before moving to Fnatic in 2022. The club later extended his contract to run through 2026.

Industry observers note that his next stop appears to be Team Heretics. Sheep Esports reported that the 26-year-old will play alongside midlaner Jackies and support Jun. Heretics had already tried to sign him a year earlier. On that occasion it agreed a buyout with Fnatic but could not agree terms with the player.

The rebuild runs deeper than the jungle. Every Fnatic team member except Lospa was due out of contract in 2026. The support was signed until the end of 2027.

It has been revealed that that deal will not keep him at Fnatic either. Lospa has reportedly agreed verbally to join SK Gaming for 2027. No buyout is involved in moving his contract between the two clubs. With that move, the one contract running beyond 2026 also leaves the club.

In the jungle, Yike is set to replace Razork in a 2027 line-up of Soboro, Yike, Vladi, Doggo and Labrov, according to Sheep Esports sources.

The overhaul follows a difficult year. Fnatic finished eighth in LEC 2026 Versus. It then placed seventh in Spring, which ended on 10 May. Summer brought the same finishing position.

According to recent reports, A loss to GIANTX on 30 August kept the team out of the Summer playoffs. That defeat left the club absent from Worlds for the first time since 2016.

According to recent reports, for the UK scene, the scale of change matters. Fnatic is one of the LEC’s best-supported clubs among British fans.

The club has also opened a search for a League of Legends General Manager. Its job advert states Fnatic goes into a rebuild in 2027. The fresh hire will run recruitment of players, coaches and staff, including transfers and benchings, and will manage the team’s budget.
