These two major-cap altcoin peers are hitting close to historically-undervalued territory: a highly-awaited bottom sign?

French analyst EGRAG compared the 4-hour charts of XRP, Ethereum, Hedera, Bitcoin, Solana, and Stellar to assess which assets are showing relative strength after recent lows.

The mainstream French crypto chart analyst’s review centers on price reaction, volume-profile location, and the distance remaining to each coin’s Value Area Low (VAL).

XRP is holding above its recent low without returning for a full retest. Solana is behaving in a similar fashion, maintaining its recovery structure. Stellar (XLM) is tracking XRP but appears slightly weaker. Ethereum (ETH) is retesting its lows, with a potential higher low developing.

Recent findings suggest that bitcoin (BTC) also retested and printed a slightly higher high. Hedera’s HBAR was retested with a lower high, the most shaky local structure among the six.

In a recent development, XRP and Stellar (XLM) are the nearest to re-entering their value areas. Bitcoin reveals the more constructive local recovery structure among the larger-cap assets, even though it remains farther from its VAL than the two payment-focused names.

A double bottom is not confirmed simply because price holds above a prior low. The analysis treats bottoming as a process rather than a single candle. Early strength is marked by reclaiming the VAL.

It has been revealed that structural confirmation requires a break of the local high. Stronger bullish conviction needs a reclaim of the Point of Control (POC) accompanied by volume.

The 4-hour volume profiles show each asset still trading below or near the lower boundary of its recent value area. Acceptance back inside the value area, followed by a move toward the POC, would shift the local bias.

Until that occurs, the charts remain in a recovery attempt rather than a confirmed reversal.

As of November 19, 2026, approximate prices stood near 63,700–64,000 for Bitcoin, $1,810 for Ethereum, $0.99 for XRP, $75 for Solana (SOL), $0.138 for Stellar (XLM) & $0.056 for Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR).

The broader market remains under moderate pressure, with Bitcoin dominance elevated and the Fear & Greed Index in Extreme Fear territory for consecutive days now.

According to recent reports, the XRP coin & Stellar Lumens (XLM) are the nearest to early strength signals. Bitcoin’s slightly higher high on the retest is the more constructive structural note among the majors.

In a recent development, confirmation across the group still requires a break of local highs and a return into the value area with volume. Until those conditions are met, the recovery remains unconfirmed.
