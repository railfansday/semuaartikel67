XRP reportedly traded at just 29 cents in Kraken’s Canadian-dollar market during a sharp, localized sell-off discussed by crypto commentator Crypto Sensei.

His video examines whether the trades reflected an exchange-specific liquidity problem rather than a broader XRP collapse, alongside falling ledger activity and disputed price forecasts.

According to the account presented in the video, 11 Canadian-dollar markets fell by at least 65%, with bitcoin trading below 18,000 and ether around 560.

The host cites November 20, 2026 as the incident date and reports that Kraken considered the orders correctly processed and would let the trades stand.

Sources indicate that the analyst didn’t include trade records or Kraken’s full statement. Those limits matter: the reported 29-cent execution should not be confused with XRP’s prevailing price across exchanges.

Sources indicate that crypto Sensei attributes the dislocation to heavy selling meeting insufficient liquidity. Buyers may have secured steep discounts, while sellers received prices far below those available elsewhere.

“Whoever sold and just got totally wiped out here, I feel for you,” he says. The video does not establish whether individual losses resulted from discretionary sales, automated orders or liquidations.

A separate headline claiming the XRP Ledger had lost 60% of its users also draws scrutiny.

The underlying figure, according to the clarification cited in the YouTube video, was roughly a 63% decline in daily activity, not evidence that a comparable share of holders permanently departed or sold their XRP.

The host challenges Jake Claver’s repeated $100 year-end XRP forecast, arguing that institutional adoption is a longer process than such targets suggest. He also distinguishes Ripple’s business expansion from confirmed institutional use of XRP itself.

According to recent reports, former Ripple executive and Evernorth CEO Ashish Birla offers a longer-term XRP development case in a clip: Evernorth intends to deploy XRP on-chain, pursue yield and support ecosystem features rather than simply hold the asset. The video leaves the execution details and eventual returns unresolved.

Sources indicate that for HODLers, these are separate questions: whether an exchange has enough liquidity, whether ledger usage is growing, and whether adoption creates demand for XRP. A dramatic trade print, or an ambitious treasury strategy, does not answer all three.
