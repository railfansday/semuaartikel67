Christie Miller wanted her last message on this Earth to be joyful.

The influencer who documented her battle with stage four breast cancer confirmed that she had died in a pre-recorded video shared to her Instagram Nov. 23. She was 60.

Recent findings suggest that “If you are seeing this video it means that I am dead,” Miller stated, going on to show a supercut of images of her and late husband Mark Miller, who died in 2025 after a battle with pancreatic cancer. “That’s right. I have crossed the veil. I am sitting with my sweetie.”.

Although it was a heartbreaking parting from the content creator, she emphasized she was feeling “absolute bliss” and “no regret” in how she lived her life.

It has been revealed that “If you are missing me, please be sad,” she continued. “It’s normal to be sad but also be happy that we got to spend time together whether it was in person friends or a chance meeting or we’ve known each other forever. What I know for sure is, life is great. Life is whatever we make it.”.

Miller—who was diagnosed with cancer in 2022—highlighted that she felt total enlightenment at the end of her life, saying she was “living at the top” of David Hawkins’ Map of Consciousness.

“What’s at the top? Love, peace, gratitude, joy,” she mused. “I do not let myself slip from those places. Yes, you can be in joy and still have grief. You can be at peace knowing you’re dying. It’s all good and it’s about choosing what emotion we want to have in the time. I have lived this beautiful life with no regrets.”.

The self-proclaimed Chief Joy Officer—who donned a colorful piñata costume in the video—assured her followers that she was only feeling happy to reunite with her late husband and ended her video with some parting words of wisdom.

“My last bit of advice for you is go out there and live your life,” she shared. “Feel the fear and do it anyway. Stop freaking caring about what anyone thinks. They’re too self-involved in their own life. They’re not worried about you.”.

As she put it, “Go out there and find joy every single day.”.
