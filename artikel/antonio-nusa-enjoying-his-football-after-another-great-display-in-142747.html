RB Leipzig’s man on form Antonio Nusa continued to put on another dazzling display in his team’s 3-1 win against French side RC Lens. Scoring a brace, the Norwegian takes his goal involvement tally to 17 in 18 games across all competitions this season.

In a recent development, speaking on his impressive form after the game, the 21-year-old said: “I’m just enjoying playing football and helping the team. I’m happy when I can make a difference and contribute to our success. Obviously, whenever I shoot, I’m trying to score. My shots are going in at the moment. I’ve been working hard on my finishing in training and want to help the team with my goals.”.

It has been revealed that nusa also discussed the importance of the win and how hard the team had to fight to get it. “We had to fight right until the end to get this win. It’s particularly important in games like these to give everything until the final whistle. Ultimately, it’s the result that counts – getting the three points was the most important thing,” he added.

Nusa’s superb form this season hasn’t gone unnoticed, with Arsenal showing an interest in the young attacker.
