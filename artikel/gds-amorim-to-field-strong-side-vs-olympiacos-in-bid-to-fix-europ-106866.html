AC Milan will head to Greece on Thursday with the need to overturn a negative trend, so Ruben Amorim will leave nothing to chance.

Sources indicate that in the past few weeks (barring the international break), Milan have alternated good performances at weekends with disastrous midweek results. The collapse against Bologna is one example, while losses to Bournemouth and Ferencvaros have complicated the Europa League mission.

It has been revealed that so, La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) write that Amorim won’t be opting for the usual excessive rotation against Olympiacos on Thursday night in Athens. There will be some changes, but the coach will field several regular starters.

The paper report that Amorim knows full well that victory is the only acceptable result in order for the Rossoneri to get back on track in their major to qualify for at least the Europa League play-offs, with the top eight now a distant mirage.

The Portuguese will rest Luka Modric in midfield. He will be replaced by Ardon Jashari, who scored his first goal for the Rossoneri in the win over Genoa. Adrien Rabiot – the Rossoneri’s driving force in this first part of the season – will remain in the team.

The defensive choices are almost forced, with Koni De Winter and Sankhoun Diawara still the centre-backs. Strahinja Pavlovic is not yet 100% fit and will undergo further tests on the kidney stone issues that have kept him out for the last month, even though he played 20 minutes on Sunday.

Up front, the same attack that played against Frosinone should be seen, namely Christian Pulisic and Diego Moreira supporting Gonçalo Ramos, who finally returned to scoring after more than a month without a goal.

Both Alphadjo Cisse and Omari Hutchison are raring to go, but both are expected to start on the bench in Athens, ready to come on as substitutes. Beyond the players, it will be crucial for Milan to take to the pitch with the right attitude.
