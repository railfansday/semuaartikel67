Latest Update: TurboTax Review – Worth It If You Want Guidance or an Expert

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Choose TurboTax if you want tax software that walks you through every question, or a tax expert to answer them, check your return, or file it for you. If you’d fill in the forms on your own, file with a free program that covers them instead, since every TurboTax tier beyond simple returns charges a fee.

Doing your own taxes saves a preparer’s fee, but it leaves you to judge whether every answer is right. TurboTax is built around that worry. Its interview turns tax forms into plain questions about your year, and each step up in price adds more help, from a tax expert who answers questions as you go to one who prepares and files the whole return.

TurboTax is made by Intuit, the Mountain View, California, organization that started in 1983 with the Quicken money manager and also makes Credit Karma and QuickBooks. TurboTax brought in $5.3 billion in Intuit’s fiscal year that ended July 31, 2026, and 53% of that came from TurboTax Live, its tax-expert services.

TurboTax Online comes in three levels of help, priced by how complex your return is and how much of the work you hand off: do it yourself, Expert Assist, and Expert Full Service. TurboTax hadn’t posted prices for 2026 returns when this review was written in November 2026, so the prices below are what it charges for 2025 returns. Last season, those prices covered returns filed by March 31, and TurboTax expected to charge more from April 1. You pay the price in effect the day you print or e-file.

In a recent development, the do-it-yourself and Expert Assist tiers share one interview. In place of form lines, it asks about your year, such as whether you own a home, have children, or gave to charity, and fills in the forms from your answers. Intuit Assist, its AI helper, answers questions along the way, and a tool called CompleteCheck reviews the whole return for errors before you file.

In a recent development, you can snap a photo of a tax form, upload it, or import it straight from your employer or financial institution. If you used TurboTax Online last year, signing in with the same user ID carries your information over. Switchers can transfer last year’s Form 1040 as a PDF from other providers, including H&R Block, TaxAct, and TaxSlayer.

The do-it-yourself tiers differ by which parts of a return they handle, and the two paid ones charge $64 for each state return.

Sources indicate that enlisted active-duty members and reservists, E-1 through E-9, file federal and state returns free in any of these tiers with a W-2 from the Defense Finance and Accounting Service. Officers, veterans, retirees, and National Guard members don’t qualify, and the discount doesn’t apply to the expert tiers.

Returns for an S corporation, a partnership, or a multi-member limited liability company (LLC) go through TurboTax’s business service, TurboTax Experts for Business.

It has been revealed that expert Assist is the same software with a tax expert on call. You can reach one by phone, live chat, or one-way video, where you see the expert but they can’t see you, and share your screen so the expert can guide you. Before you file, an expert gives the whole return a final review. If the return needs significant advice or actual preparation, the expert may have to sign as its preparer and take primary responsibility for it.

Last season, experts were available seven days a week from Jan. 5 through April 20, 5 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific time, and customers could keep asking questions through Dec. 31 of the year they filed.

In a recent development, full Service hands the whole return to a dedicated tax expert. You upload your documents, and the expert prepares the return, walks you through it, then signs and files it. TurboTax says its expert files only a return they believe is 100% correct.

According to recent reports, it starts at $129 for one federal return with one W-2, plus $69 per state, and the final price depends on the forms your return needs. You get an estimate before the expert starts. You can work with the expert by phone, one-way video, or messages, or in person where a local expert is offered, at one of more than 500 TurboTax Expert offices.

TurboTax’s calculation and refund guarantees last for what Intuit calls the life of a return, seven years from the day you file it with TurboTax.

Audit support is information only, so no one from TurboTax represents you before the IRS. For that, it sells Audit Defense, an add-on from a separate company, Tax Audit, for an extra fee.

Paying TurboTax out of your federal refund can add a $40 service fee. An early-refund option deposits your federal refund up to five days before the IRS would, for a fee taken from the refund, though TurboTax’s early-refund page doesn’t say how much that fee is. You aren’t charged if the money doesn’t arrive at least a day early.

According to recent reports, turboTax has also offered a Refund Advance, an interest-free loan from WebBank against your expected refund, with no loan fees. Last season it lent up to half the refund, to a maximum of $4,000, or up to the whole refund and $10,000 for Full Service customers. Filers needed an expected federal refund of at least $500, and the money went into a Credit Karma Money checking account.

You pay only when you print or e-file, and once you do, you give up the right to a refund of the price.

TurboTax is the tax software to pay for when you want the most guidance, or a tax expert, on your return. Its interview and imports suit filers who find tax forms confusing. Its expert tiers put a professional on the return to answer questions, review it, or prepare it, and its guarantees cover the expert’s mistakes as well as the software’s.

Industry observers note that its weakness is price. Every tier past Free Edition’s simple returns charges a fee, so anyone who’d fill in the forms without help pays for guidance they won’t use. Intuit’s own Credit Karma Tax indicates it will charge $0 for a 2026 federal return. Pay TurboTax’s prices for its interview if tax forms confuse you, or for an expert who checks or prepares your return.
