Dark Matter Technologies and ServiceLink have partnered to bring automated title, valuation and closing services to lenders using Dark Matter’s Empower loan origination system.

Recent findings suggest that the partnership integrates ServiceLink’s Accelerated Close platform, powered by its NowIntelligence technology, into Empower. The service automates title and valuation orders, as well as closing orders, and allows lenders or borrowers to schedule appraisals and closings.

According to recent reports, using loan data, Accelerated Close automatically determines whether a loan qualifies for an expedited workflow. NowIntelligence evaluates eligibility using real-time market intelligence, including public and proprietary data, vendor information and market analytics.

Industry observers note that for eligible loans, Empower automatically places title and valuation orders with ServiceLink through Exchange, Dark Matter’s partner marketplace. Once a loan reaches clear-to-close status, closing orders are also placed automatically through Empower.

The companies said the integration reduces manual work for lender operations teams and can shorten the time between underwriting approval and a scheduled closing.

Borrower expectations are also driving demand for faster and more flexible closing processes. According to ServiceLink’s 2026 State of Homebuying Report, 82% of borrowers believe a refinance should take less than a month to close, while 87% said the ability to schedule their own appraisal or closing would influence their choice of lender.

It has been revealed that “Borrowers judge their lender by how fast and frictionless the process feels, and knowing when their appraisal and closing will happen matters a great deal,” stated Vikas Rao, CEO of Dark Matter Technologies. “Accelerated Close takes routine ordering off lenders’ plates and, if desired, puts scheduling in borrowers’ hands, so loans close faster and borrowers feel in control of one of the biggest financial decisions of their lives.”.

ServiceLink CEO Chris Azur stated the integration is intended to help lenders shorten cycle times, improve pull-through rates and provide borrowers with a more streamlined experience.

It has been revealed that for lenders, shorter cycle times could also reduce costs associated with extended rate locks. Orders placed through ServiceLink will continue to use the existing Exchange workflow for order status updates, documents and messaging.

Lenders can enable Accelerated Close through their Dark Matter or ServiceLink account managers, through self-service configuration or as part of a newly introduced Empower implementation, the companies’ release stated.
