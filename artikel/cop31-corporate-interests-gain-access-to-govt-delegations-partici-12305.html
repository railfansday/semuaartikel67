COP31: Corporate interests gain access to govt delegations participating in climate negotiations

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (143 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, eve Irvine is pleased to welcome Siphesihle Mvundla, South African Climate and Energy Justice Campaigner at groundWork, a non-profit environmental justice organization. At COP31 in Turkey, representatives of major polluting industries have gained access to climate negotiations through government delegations, accredited organisations and other participation arrangements, according to research by the Kick Big Polluters Out coalition. Mvundla questions the extent of their involvement: "This year particularly, it's not just about the numbers, but it's about the influence that these private interests would have on the negotiations." While businesses have a role in implementing climate action, he questions their access to negotiations intended to regulate their industries and is calling for "an end to big polluter sponsorship and partnership at climate conferences" and insisted: "No more allowing big polluters to write the rules of climate action.".
