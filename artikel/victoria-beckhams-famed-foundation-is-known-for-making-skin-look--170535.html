There are very few beauty products that unite the entire ELLE beauty team. There is one recent launch, however, that we are all obsessed with, and we’re not the only ones. When Victoria Beckham Beauty’s The Foundation Drops launched last year, they were met first with curiosity (this is a foundation that costs $110, after all), and then adoration, as we all discovered that actually, yes, this little 30ml bottle is indeed worth its weight in gold. In fact, we loved these tinted drops so much that we wrote a dedicated review of them.

Victoria Beckham was never going to release a subpar complexion product, but this silky foundation is truly beyond expectations. The viral bestseller works beautifully on all skin types and textures, skimming over enlarged pores, thread veins, and acne scars with ease, and bringing luminous life back to more mature complexions too.

Recent findings suggest that the secret is all in the texture. As a serum foundation, it feels more like an oil than a viscous liquid and skims over the skin to leave behind a glowing (never greasy) veil of coverage. The coverage is light-medium and completely buildable, so you likely won’t need to team it with a concealer. Beckham called on German medical scientist and skin care kingpin Augustinus Bader to help create the formula, which is infused with his signature TFC8 blend of minerals and vitamins. So in short, your skin will look better with and without the foundation on.

In a recent development, why am I telling you all this? Well, if you were one of the many who snorted at the idea of a triple-figure foundation, today is your day: For Black Friday, Victoria Beckham Beauty is having a site-wide sale, including a 20 percent reduction on the only foundation you’ll ever want to use again. Our advice? Stock up and buy two: there is absolutely no chance you won’t love it.

In fact, this isn't the only VBB product deserving of a spot in your kit. Ahead of party season, the Satin Kajal Liner is the obvious winner: creamy, blendable, and available in a growing number of jewel tones, it's little wonder this note-perfect eye pencil is Beckham's bestselling product. Similarly, the Longwear Eyeshadow Sticks are excellent for festive nights. If you, like me, find shadows and palettes too much of a faff, they're bound to please: simply scribble over your lid and blend the edges for a perfect smoky eye.

Okay, one last recommendation before you go forth and shop: Victoria Beckham's fragrances are in a league of their own. Suite 302 is the one to gift yourself for Christmas: with tart cherry atop a lingering trail of tobacco and leather, it's perfectly wintry and incredibly sexy.
