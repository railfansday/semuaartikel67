📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that hardly a day goes by without a report dropping that someone’s cousin’s friend’s brother overheard someone else’s cousin’s friend’s brother at a bar say that [insert hot British actor here] is the next James Bond. And up until this morning, Amazon MGM and director Denis Villeneuve have not confirmed one. single. thing. But it’s official: Robert Pattinson is the fresh James Bond.

Sources indicate that the rumor mill has been churning for months. Several actors screen-tested for the iconic role, including Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, but Robert Pattinson was an eleventh hour surprise. Matthew Belloni wrote in Puck News last week that there was reason to think Robert is favored to become the next 007.

More sources explained that since Robert’s “test deal was so difficult to close,” people close to the situation were “wondering why the filmmakers would bother if they didn’t really want him.”.

It has been revealed that there’s been so much discourse about whether or not Bond should be an established household name (traditionally, he isn’t), with former casting director Debbie McWilliams—who cast the last three Bonds—telling The Independent, “It is absolutely essential that he retains a total enigma. I don’t want to see any of them as Bond because we now know so much about them. We want to know as little about them personally as possible, because that what spies are. We don’t need to know where he goes shopping or who his parents are, or where he lives. We never want to see him at home. And a vital element of the whole thing is his job description. He’s licensed to kill, and we have to believe that he can do that. If you don’t, then you’ve lost the audience.”.

Casting Robert Pattinson definitely challenges that approach, but we’ll see how it goes!
