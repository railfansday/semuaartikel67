Ian Wright has implored Arsenal to make significant additions in the January transfer window amid his growing concerns over Mikel Arteta’s misfiring attack.

The Gunners have set the early Premier League title pace, winning nine of their 11 games so far, and are handily placed to qualify for the next phase of the Champions League.

Consecutive draws against Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund have, however, sparked fresh fears that Mikel Arteta has left himself short of enough firepower.

The Gunners dominated large spells of their two most recent matches but wasted numerous chances to put both games to bed and were forced to settle for a point on each occasion.

After allowing Gabriel Martinelli and Leandro Trossard to depart in the summer, Arsenal were only able to recruit Greek international winger Christos Tzolis as a replacement.

The former Club Brugges winger impressed during pre-season but has struggled after a bright start and is still to score his first goal for his upcoming club.

Tzolis struggled badly against Dortmund in midweek and Wright is concerned the 24-year-old will struggle to hit the heights reached by the two players Arsenal were prepared to let go of following their title-winning campaign last season.

He told The Overlap: ‘It was a tough game to watch, a hard watch.

‘Defensively we are so solid and midfield, we are so great. But they’ve got to do something about that front four or front three.
