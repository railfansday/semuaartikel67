It has been revealed that the Casa da Arquitetura — Portuguese Centre for Architecture, in Matosinhos, will inaugurate the exhibition "Plataforma Cidade: no momento do arquivo" (City Platform: At the Moment of the Archive) on November 20 at 9:00 PM, as part of the institution's 9th-anniversary program. Curated by Inês Lobo and Manuel Salgado, with research by Rute Figueiredo and photography by Duarte Belo, the exhibition offers an interpretation of the transformations of the Portuguese territory over the last 50 years, from the dawn of democracy to the present day.

The Casa da Arquitetura — Portuguese Centre for Architecture, in Matosinhos, will inaugurate the exhibition "Plataforma Cidade: no momento do arquivo" (City Platform: At the Moment of the Archive) on November 20 at 9:00 PM, as part of the institution's 9th-anniversary program. Curated by Inês Lobo and Manuel Salgado, with research by Rute Figueiredo and photography by Duarte Belo, the exhibition offers an interpretation of the transformations of the Portuguese territory over the last 50 years, from the dawn of democracy to the present day.

According to recent reports, building on the celebration of 50 years of Local Government, the project examines how public policies, territorial intervention programs, and urban projects have contributed to the construction of contemporary Portuguese cities. Rather than simply showcasing works or celebrating their authors, the exhibition uses these projects as case studies to understand the processes, decisions, and strategies that have shaped the territory.

The exhibition integrates architecture, photography, cartography, chronologies, and archival documentation, organized around three complementary devices: a territorial inventory documenting 160 Portuguese cities; a chronological platform linking political, economic, social, and cultural events with urban evolution; and a set of actions gathering territorial intervention programs and urban projects.
