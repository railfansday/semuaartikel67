Celtic and Rangers are keeping a close eye on Zidane Iqbal's performances for Dundee United, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has been outstanding at Tannadice Park since his move from Dutch outfit Ultrecht during the summer, and has set tongues wagging across Scotland.

Just months after making the move to the Scottish Premiership, the midfielder is now being widely tipped to make a big move in the not-so-distant future.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, both Celtic and Rangers have taken note of Iqbal's performances so far this season.

The Iraq international has made just eight appearances since his move to Dundee United, and it has not taken long for Scotland's top clubs to be alerted to his situation.

Dundee United are understandably reluctant to sell Iqbal, and a January move has already been ruled out, but they may be forced to brace for offers next summer.

Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Martin O'Neill is determined to strengthen his Celtic squad, with the addition of a upcoming midfielder seen as a priority.

Iqbal's performances since his move to Scotland have placed him high on Celtic's shortlist, as well as that of their Old Firm rivals, as they assess their long-term targets.

A January move is not on the cards, but ahead of next summer's transfer window, both Celtic and Rangers will be keeping a close eye on Iqbal's progress.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "If he continues to impress for Dundee United, it would be no surprise that maybe Celtic or other clubs are looking at him.

"He's been a revalation for Dundee United since his arrival from Utrecht in the summer transfer window.

"It's a normal situation if a player outside the major two in Scotland, performs well, they normally do attract interest from the likes of Celtic and Rangers as well.

"We know Celtic's midfield problems, they are struggling in that area of the team and have never really replaced the players who left in the summer.

"If Zidane Iqbal continues to impress up at Tannadice, it'll be more than just Celtic will be looking at Iqbal there probably come the summer window.".

With the news that Iqbal will not be available to make a move in the January transfer window, Celtic could be forced to assess some of their other targets short-term.

O'Neill is determined to strengthen his midfield, with the January window being eyed as a crucial period if Celtic are to hold on to the Scottish Premiership title this season.

Football Insider revealed on 19 November that Celtic are plotting a move to sign Le Havre midfielder Simon Ebonog, who could become availble in the mid-season window.

Celtic believe the 22-year-old could be the answer to their lack of dynamism in the middle of the park, having impressed with his displays in Ligue 1.

Sources say that Celtic also want to bring in a young player as a long-term project, and Ebonog is viewed as a player with a "significant ceiling" in the game.
