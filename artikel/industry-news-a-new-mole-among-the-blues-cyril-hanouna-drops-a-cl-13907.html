Sixteen years after the Blues mole in Knysna, is the French team harboring a upcoming secret informant? This is what Cyril Hanouna has been clearly implying for several days, by making shattering revelations behind the scenes of the French selection in his show All Beautiful All N9ufon W9.

After ensuring that Kylian Mbappé and Zinédine Zidane were at odds, which the coach denied at a press conference before France-Turkey, and that the Real Madrid striker had alienated Ousmane Dembélé and a large part of the locker room, the host affirms that another conflict exists within the group.

Recent findings suggest that “There is a war between the captains and those who have just arrived and who are starting to piss off”he notes, while specifying that “Zizou” is “very close” new players he recently called up. Information that he describes as “safe”. Where does he get it from? “Since Thursday, they have been looking for the mole”he laughs.

Sources indicate that “Everyone is stressed. In the staff, they all look at each other: who is the mole? Who gives information to Hanouna? “, rejoices the presenter, before addressing the 1998 world champion: “Zinédine, you know, it’s not on your staff. And unfortunately, Zinédine, you won’t be able to fire the person who is the mole. ».

Industry observers note that and Cyril Hanouna continued: “He’s someone who’s invincible. That’s all I have to say.
