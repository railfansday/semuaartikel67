📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hunter Schafer took the mob wife aesthetic and gave it a daring, high-fashion spin. Last night, the model and actress attended the red-carpet premiere of Blade Runner 2099 in Los Angeles while in the teeniest of Prada dresses and the fluffiest of fur jackets.

Sources indicate that the minidress was a ruched leather number dyed in splashes of distressed brown for an antique look. The micro strapless piece had a sweetheart neckline and a hemline so high that it didn’t go past Schafer’s hips. Luckily, she wore matching leather panties underneath.

Styled by Dara, the supermodel made the mini work, slipping into sparkly black textured tights that acted as her bottoms and were met by pointy black heels. She topped things off with a luxurious brown fur jacket that wrapped Schafer in a cloud of glamour. The piece was cropped to match the minidress’ super short hemline.

Layered diamond earrings and a massive diamond ring upped the ante, while Schafer’s glam added an element of surprise.

Recent findings suggest that makeup artist Sandy Ganzer swiped a streak of shimmery champagne eyeshadow across Schafer’s eyes, creating a futuristic shield or mask-like effect that paid homage to Daryl Hannah’s replicant character, Pris, from the 1982 sci-fi film Blade Runner. A messy bun and a glossy pink lip, meanwhile, brought the otherworldly look back to the red carpet.

Industry observers note that schafer plays Cora, a human fugitive who has spent her life running from Blade Runners, in the action series. All eight episodes of the show will premiere on Amazon Prime Video on November 25.
