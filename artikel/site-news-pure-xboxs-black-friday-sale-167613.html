Tis the season for Black Friday deals and we've got a couple of our own to offer to you.

We hope you are already aware that Pure Xbox relies on advertising revenue to continue to operate. However, a few years ago, we introduced an alternative way to support us, in the form of a subscription fee which will allow you to view our content without any ads.

We've probably not promoted this feature enough, so we figured Black Friday would be a great time to highlight the subscription programme and ask for your support, directly.

A subscription also gets you ad-free access to our sister sites across the Hookshot Network: Nintendo Life, Push Square, Time Extension, and our most recent arrival, Steam Hype.

If you didn't already know, we partnered with FameHype to bring you our own eShop code store where you can purchase Xbox Store Credit and games (varies by region). These are official codes (no grey market here) and a small percentage of the sale comes back to us.

Now would be a great time to stock up on credit; for every $100 you purchase, you'll get a $5 discount and you'll be supporting us.

Recent findings suggest that please note that some external links on this page are affiliate links, which means if you click them and make a purchase we may receive a small percentage of the sale. Please read our FTC Disclosure for more information.

As we start looking forward to 2027, we're already planning some fresh components of the network-wide supporter programme and hope to add some new perks soon.

You will no doubt be spoilt for choice with Black Friday deals across your favourite retailers, but if you've been enjoying our website(s) over the past month, year, or decade(s), we'd love for you to make it official and become a supporter!

Let us know your ideas for what you would like us to include in the future version of the supporter programme.
