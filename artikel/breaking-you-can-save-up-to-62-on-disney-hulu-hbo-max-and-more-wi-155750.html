📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that subscriptions to streaming services can add up quickly, especially as companies continue to regularly raise prices. But most streamers also offer discounts for Black Friday, so if you've been holding off on signing up for a newly introduced service or returning to one you've canceled, now is the best time of year to audit your subscriptions and take advantage of deals.

According to recent reports, HBO's Black Friday deal drops the price of a Basic subscription to $5.49 per month for 12 months (normally $10.99 per month). This tier is ad-supported and doesn't include live sports, but the deal is available as a standalone subscription, a Prime Video add-on, and through Roku.

It has been revealed that peacock's ad-supported Select plan typically costs $8.99 per month or $89.99 per year, but the service's Black Friday promo brings it down to $2.99 per month (for three months) or $29.99 for an annual plan. The ad-free Premium subscription is currently discounted to $8.99 per month for three months or $89.99 for one year (normally $12.99 per month or $129.99 per year).

Beyond the major players, there are plenty of other streaming deals for Black Friday.

In a recent development, many services are heavily discounted as Prime Video add-ons, including MGM+, Starz, AMC+, and Crunchyroll. Roku has similar subscription deals.

Starz is also offering standalone discounts on its monthly plan ($2.99 per month for three months, down from $11.99 per month) and its annual subscription ($11.99 for year, down from $69.99).
