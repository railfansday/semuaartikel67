Premier League icon Michael Owen believes the signing of a forward should be the “next step” for Leeds United.

Recent findings suggest that leeds signed Dominic Calvert-Lewin on a free transfer from Everton in the summer of 2025 and he has since led the line to widespread acclaim. His form has pushed him back into the England fold and he recently picked up his 15th cap in a 3-2 Nations League win over Spain.

Lukas Nmecha was recruited in the same summer and is the second-choice frontman, with Joel Piroe now out on loan at West Ham United.

Leeds are not blessed with a lengthy list of alternatives to the pair and Owen believes depth will be key if the Whites are to keep climbing the Premier League table.

He told The Yorkshire Post: “Calvert-Lewin looks to have a new lease of life playing for Leeds and is doing a good job and playing well.

“But for Leeds to keep building and improving, having back-up is needed as they have started the season well and [they are] looking competitive in games I’ve seen this season for sure.

“They’ve recruited well with James Trafford, so yes, another striker would be the next step for sure.”.

The likes of Harry Wilson, Noah Okafor and Jean-Matteo Bahoya could deployed up front, but their presence in that role would require a system change.

Piroe was once Leeds’ chief marksman but does not appear to have a future at Elland Road, nor does Wilfried Gnonto, who is now on loan at Fiorentina.

Leeds boss Daniel Farke could well want another option in the ‘number nine’ department, but persuading a player to be a third-choice option is not an easy task.
