📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ella Langley was named Entertainer of the Year after a clean sweep at the 2026 Country Music Association Awards.

Recent findings suggest that langley, 27, took home the prestigious award at the Wednesday, November 18, ceremony at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, which aired live on ABC and Disney+. The singer-songwriter was nominated alongside Luke Combs, Cody Johnson, Chris Stapleton and Lainey Wilson. (Johnson, 39, was not in attendance at the 2026 CMAs as he is focusing on his mental and physical health.).

Recent findings suggest that the “I Can’t Love You Anymore” singer kept her acceptance speech light-hearted, as she already been on the CMAs stage multiple times after winning Single and Song of the Year Awards for “Choosin’ Texas,” plus Female Vocalist of the Year. Her sophomore record Dandelion was chosen as Album of the Year as well.

“At this point, I’m sick of me,” she joked. “I have watched these awards since I was a little kid and the award I cared most about was Song of the Year and Entertainer of the Year … I’ve run out of things to say. I just don’t know.”.

After thanking her band, Langley quipped, “This isn’t real life. I feel like this is fake and I’m up here accepting an award that’s fake. Thank you, Jesus. I’m at a loss for words. … Trust the people you have around you. Thank you, CMAs.”.

She was surprised by previous Entertainer of the Year winner Wilson, 34, who presented her with a bedazzled broom.

“I noted I was gonna bring you a bedazzled broom because I knew you was gonna sweep girl,” Wilson teased.

Langley, 27, came into Wednesday’s CMA Awards with a historic nine nominations, tying Miranda Lambert and Wilson for the most nods by a female artist in a single year.

Lambert, 43, trailed in second place at the 2026 CMAs with seven nominations, including Song of the Year for co-writing Langley’s hit “Choosin’ Texas.” She was recognized in the Female Vocalist of the Year and Songwriter of the Year categories as well. (Lambert’s 70 total career CMA Award nominations is the record for any female artist.).

Kacey Musgraves and producer Ben West notched five nods each this year, while Entertainer of the Year nominee Johnson was eligible in four categories.

Wilson won the 2025 Entertainer of the Year trophy, in addition to hosting last year’s ceremony just as she did again on Wednesday night.

“I was just so tickled to be here hosting this thing tonight,” she told the star-studded crowd in her 2025 acceptance speech. “I just came into here wanting to celebrate country music.”.

Industry observers note that wilson included a special shoutout for her then-fiancée, former NFL player Devlin “Duck” Hodges, saying, “Thank you, Duck! We get married next year. Come on, now! Thank you for loving me and supporting me.”.

“Thank you to the fans,” she concluded. “They just keep on making my dreams come true every single day. I love ya’all, God bless! We’re about to party at [my Nashville restaurant] Bell Bottoms Up!”.

It has been revealed that us reported in May that Wilson and Hodges, 30, tied the knot at Ruskin Cave in Dickson, Tennessee, after five years of dating. The outdoor ceremony was held with a view of a nearby waterfall as well as wildflowers as decorations at the altar to match Wilson’s bridal bouquet.

Sources indicate that “Our family and friends know how to have a good time, and the dance floor was packed all night,” Wilson told Vogue at the time. “To close out a perfect evening, Duck and I followed the band through a sendoff line of sparklers and cheering loved ones, climbed into an old white Ford truck and drove off!”.

Wilson hosted the 2026 CMA Awards on ABC and Disney+ Wednesday, live from Nashville.
