There's reason to believe that we'll get our first proper look Persona 6 next month, during The Game Awards.

This rumour stems from Reddit user ronakwya, who has, rather diligently, been keeping tabs on the game's Japanese website. The idea being that if the domain receives any backend updates, it could mean that an announcement is incoming.

And guess what? The site was altered just recently, on the 8th November — and it wasn't just some standard update. This was, apparently, the kind of renewal that happens when a company overhauls a domain — as in, the website is redesigned to fall in line with a big announcement, ready to be made public when the bomb drops.

In a recent development, but what really adds a lot of weight to this discovery is that Atlus followed this pattern almost exactly with Persona 4 Revival, before its own reveal earlier this year. Revival's teaser website received a behind-the-scenes overhaul roughly one month before appearing at the Xbox Games Showcase in the summer.

The Game Awards is obviously happening one month from this enhancement — on the 10th December — and so you can certainly argue that things are starting to add up.

But would Atlus opt for a Persona 6 blowout when Persona 4 Revival is just three months away from release? Admittedly, it does seem a bit counterintuitive from a marketing perspective — I've always been of a mind that the developer will kick the Persona 6 machine into gear once Revival is out.

Indeed, Persona Super Live 2027 takes place on 19th March, and to me, that felt like the ideal venue in terms of timing.

Having remarked that, it simply doesn't get bigger than The Game Awards — it's the most watched gaming event out there, and by a massive distance. All evidence points to Persona 6 launching at some point in 2027, and so Atlus won't be handed an opportunity like this again.
