Solo Leveling Sequel Series Officially Confirms Season 3 English Release Date

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that the wait for newly introduced chapters of Solo Leveling: Ragnarok is finally over for English-speaking readers. The sequel webtoon has returned for its third season, and Tappytoon has confirmed a regular release schedule for its official English edition. New installments are set to arrive every Saturday at 12 p.m. ET, giving readers a dependable time to follow Sung Suho’s next battles.

It has been revealed that this announcement via @Tappy_toon on X is particularly welcome after months of uncertainty surrounding the series’ future in English. Season 3 began in Korean on KakaoPage on November 4, 2026, but readers elsewhere had to wait for a platform and schedule confirmation. Tappytoon has now stepped forward with the latest episodes, while its animated trailer offers a first look at the season’s updated artwork and ongoing story.

Industry observers note that tappytoon began its English return on November 11, publishing the first two chapters of Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Season 3. Episode 71 is scheduled to follow on November 17, before Episode 72 arrives on Saturday, November 21. After that, the webtoon is expected to settle into its weekly Saturday slot at noon Eastern Time, making Tappytoon the place to follow the newly introduced English chapters.

Sources indicate that the English edition is right now behind the Korean release on KakaoPage, where Season 3 opened with Episodes 69, 70, and 71 together on November 4. Episode 72 followed on November 11, with Episode 73 scheduled for November 18. The gap should narrow as Tappytoon catches up; Episode 74, for example, is expected in English on December 5 after its Korean debut on November 25.

It has been revealed that the move also gives the sequel a clearer path forward as Tapas prepares to end its service. The platform confirmed the closure in September 2026 and says access to its content will end on March 31, 2027. Tappytoon’s confirmed schedule is crucial for readers who want to keep following the official English edition without relying on an uncertain release blueprint.

Sources indicate that the newly introduced season resumes with Sung Suho facing a threat that calls for more than his own growing strength. A massive Gate has appeared in South Korea, prompting Suho and the country’s S-Rank Hunters to join forces with American hunter Thomas Andre. Their mission is to close the Gate, but its destination makes the operation deeply personal for Suho.

According to recent reports, the Gate leads to the Land of the Snowfolk, where Suho’s mother, Cha Haein, has been trapped for years. That connection raises the stakes beyond another dangerous raid, tying the new conflict directly to Suho’s family and the legacy he is still trying to understand. The arc also brings familiar hunters back into the action, including Choi Jongin and Baek Yoonho, while the Frost Monarch’s presence looms over the threat.

According to recent reports, season 3 also marks a major creative change behind the scenes. The previous artist, JIN, stepped away for mandatory military service in South Korea, leaving the webtoon on hiatus for nearly 10 months. Sanjimseung has taken over the artwork and is credited as M.Beast on Tappytoon. The platform’s upcoming Solo Leveling: Ragnarok animated trailer previews scenes from the season’s opening chapters and introduces readers to the sequel’s next visual chapter.
