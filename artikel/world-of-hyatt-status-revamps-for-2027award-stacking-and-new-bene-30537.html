Industry observers note that hyatt will finally let members combine a free night certificate with a confirmed suite upgrade starting January 1, 2027. I’ve wanted that for years. They’re also extending milestone free night certificates from 180 days to a year, and adding rewards after just 10 qualifying nights.

Sources indicate that this isn’t an across the board improvement. Suite upgrades will expire sooner. The suite upgrade choice at 50 nights gets cut in half. And My Hyatt Concierge becomes something you select alongside other rewards, rather than something everyone automatically receives at 60 nights. Free parking will no longer be an automatic benefit on award stays for Globalists.

Sources indicate that when I covered Hyatt’s last major status benefit refresh in November 2023, I liked it overall. They made awards transferable, added choices, and let members try Globalist benefits before earning Globalist. Guest of Honor became limited, but the Milestone Rewards themselves mostly got better.

It has been revealed that this time there are new and better options. I genuinely like a lot of the choices, but there are real tradeoffs. Hyatt is pitching the changes as more choice and flexibility, based on listening to members. That’s actually fair for much of this, though I don’t think members told them what they really wanted was for suite upgrade to expire up to a year earlier.

It has been revealed that hyatt will allow members to combine eligible awards on the same reservation, including Suite Upgrade Awards, Guest of Honor Awards and the new Free Parking Awards. This fixes one of the problems I’ve been complaining about for years. A free night certificate is something you often want to use on a special leisure stay. That’s also when a suite may matter most, especially traveling with family. When I didn’t want to use a free night certificate was a work trip by myself. Free night awards had a lot less value to me because I had to give up a confirmed suite to use them. But being able to use the two awards together makes both more valuable. Combining Guest of Honor with a suite upgrade is useful, too. You can give someone Globalist treatment and confirm their suite ahead of time, rather than leaving the room upgrade to availability when they arrive. Hyatt also promises an enhanced Pay My Way experience, letting members mix awards, points, cash and Points + Cash by night. Online selection, gifting and redemption will be available for eligible awards. Pay My Way and stacking, by the way, really do all work together. Hyatt offers me this illustration, Let’s say a member is making a three night booking. Assuming both the Standard Suite was available for the duration, and there is award inventory available on the selected dates (just like today) – yes, a member could apply a Suite Upgrade Award to the whole stay, a Free Parking award to the whole stay, a Guest of Honor Award to the whole stay, and then pay for the reservation using points on one night, a promotional Free Night Award on another night, and cash on the third night.

The qualifying night choices add up to 10 nights: one at the first milestone, two at the next, two at 30 nights and five at 40 nights. These count toward both status and Milestone Rewards. That creates a path to Globalist with 50 qualifying nights from other sources, if you select all four night rewards. The requirement remains 60, but 10 can come from these choices. And you earn qualifying nights with the The World Of Hyatt Credit Card (See rates and fees) too – 5 qualifying night credits every year and 2 qualifying night every time you spend $5,000 on the card. The 30 night option to take 10,000 points instead of a Category 1–4 certificate is useful for someone who struggles to use the certificate. A good Category 4 redemption can be worth more, and the certificate will become easier to use with the longer expiration and ability to add a suite upgrade. One Fewer Suite Upgrade, And A Choice About Your Concierge The reduction at 50 nights is easy to miss. The current choice is two Suite Upgrade Awards. The new chart offers one. At 60 nights or 100,000 base points, members will receive: 2 Guest of Honor Awards. A choice of free nights: 2 Category 1–4 awards or 1 Category 1–7 award. Two selections from this menu: 7,500 bonus points, a Free Parking Award, a $100 Delta certificate, 2 Suite Upgrade Awards, or My Hyatt Concierge. You can still select the two suite upgrades and concierge access, keeping the existing 60 night package. But if you want points or parking, something else has to go. And you can’t make the same two choices at 60 nights – you can’t have four upgrades, 15,000 points or two parking awards. You don’t have o select all of your awards at once, though you do have to make these two selections at the same time. Hyatt explains to me that you’ll still have 90 days to make your choices, and: In milestones where there are separate choices, such as 30 nights, the member can make those choices at different points within the 90 day selection window (e.g., could choose the Free Night Award one day, and the HIC Spa credit on a different day.) At 60 nights, the member can choose their free night award choice on a different day from the other selection, but the two selections within “Choose Two” must be made together at the same time. I do plan to keep the concierge benefit, even though mine has never been very responsive. I won’t give up the ability to email someone to address concerns. I just wish I would hear back more quickly, and didn’t need to follow up so often. Maybe that’s actually coming? Hyatt tells me they do plan greater investment in the concierge program: [B]y shifting to a choice, we will be able to further invest in My Hyatt Concierge and deliver even more proactive and personalized care. The upcoming parking award covers a qualifying paid or award stay of up to seven nights, and can be gifted. It appears among the choices at 60, 70, 80 and 90 nights. However it’s no longer simply a benefit for Globalists redeeming points for free nights. I wrote in that Hyatt was surveying turning automatic benefits into awards you could choose and they’ve done that with this one. The benefit is no longer unlimited It comes with a tradeoff – the benefit is instead of something else And you have to strategy ahead, one more thing to track and manage, rather than something that’s just ‘taken care of’ in recognition of your loyalty. Hyatt explains the decision to me as, [T]his decision was made as 70% of Globalists did not use the free parking benefit in 2025; 95% used it 4 times or less, and moving this to a choice allowed the program to retain it for members who value it and introduce other exciting new awards like the $200 Thompson Hotels Credit and the $500 Premium Suite Credit. If the benefit wasn’t being used, it actually wasn’t that expensive to offer! And not using it regularly doesn’t mean the option isn’t valued. It may not have been the most valuable benefit of Globalist for most people, but I wouldn’t personally make the tradeoff (in fairness, I’m someone that did use and get value from it). Of course, a parking award will be eligible for use a paid stay while it previously was only available on points. However, at 70, 80 or 90 nights where you can claim it you’re passing up 10,000 points or a suite upgrade to select it. A Year To Use Every Award Is Both An Improvement And A Cut Hyatt’s release presents the new expiration policy as giving members extra time. That’s true for some awards, but the effect runs in both directions. Hyatt tells me, For all awards issued on January 1, 2027 or later, expiration dates will be 365 days from the date of issuance. Milestone free night awards that presently expire after 180 days will get substantially more time. But suite upgrades, ordinary Milestone Guest of Honor awards and club access awards currently have much longer lives, generally through the end of the year earned plus another 14 months under Hyatt’s existing rules. A suite upgrade selected January 1, 2026 remained valid through February 2028. Under a 365 day rule, the same issuance date would produce a January 1, 2027 expiration. That’s more than a year of usefulness gone. Shortening the window makes it harder to hold upgrades for a major trip, to book far ahead, or to save awards from a heavy travel year for a quieter one.
