Industry observers note that germany, who finished top of Group A2 following their 5-0 win over the Netherlands in Berlin on Monday are to face Belgium in the Nations League quarterfinals — after they finished second in Group A1. The Belgians recently came up with a statement result of their own, beating Italy 4-1. The match-up between the neighboring countries promises to be closely contested, with Belgium sitting at No. 8 in the men's rankings, three places above Germany.

The head coach of Belgium is former Bayern Munich player Mark van Bommel, who took over from Rudi Garcia following the World Cup, where they were knocked out by eventual champions Spain in the quarterfinals.

Germany head into the knockout phase on a high note, not only because they won their group, but also the way they performed in their last two matches, against Serbia and the Netherlands in the group stage.

Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp followed the draw, held in Nyon on Friday, from the German FA's (DFB) Frankfurt offices. He played down the notion that Germany enter the tie as the favorites.

It has been revealed that "I've practically never given it any thought. It's never interested me," he noted.

Industry observers note that "An awful lot can happen in that time. We're very happy with the final impression we left, yet we're also fully aware that we're only at the very beginning of a development process. We want to carry this positive momentum forward—there's still a great deal to be done.".

The matches are slated for March 25 and March 30, 2027, and having won their group, Germany will have home-field advantage for the second leg.

Germany and Belgium have faced each other 26 times to date, with West Germany and Germany having the upper hand with 20 wins, five defeats and one draw.

Should Germany advance to the semifinals, they will go on to face the winner of England and Norway, a World Cup quarterfinals rematch.

In the other two semifinals matchups, the Netherlands drew Portugal, while the top pairing sees Spain face France in a rematch of one of last summer's World Cup semifinals.

The "Final Four" mini tournament is to be held from June 9 to 13 but the host venue won't be determined until the semifinal lineup has been completed.
