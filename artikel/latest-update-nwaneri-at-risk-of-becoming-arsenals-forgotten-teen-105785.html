Youngest player to appear in Premier League is struggling to establish himself on loan at the Bundesliga club.

Ethan Nwaneri has one record Max Dowman can never break. It is more than four years since he became the youngest player to appear in the Premier League, with Dowman, his fellow Hale End academy graduate, older by 54 days when he made his highly anticipated debut. But after a week when Arsenal published an article dedicated to Dowman’s growing collection of milestones, Nwaneri risks becoming the club’s forgotten teenager.

Nwaneri joined Borussia Dortmund on loan in the summer and returns to the Emirates Stadium in the Champions League on Tuesday, although the 19-year-old will probably be on the bench, having not started for the German club yet in the competition. Despite making a good impression with an assist on his Bundesliga debut, against Hoffenheim, he has not started in the league since 17 October and has yet to complete more than 65 minutes in a Bundesliga match.
