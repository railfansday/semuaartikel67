The blackout originated in the 1960s when Burnley chairman Bob Lord encouraged the EFL to oppose television coverage of 15:00 Saturday matches.

Andy Burnham has urged caution on lifting the Saturday 3pm TV blackout in the UK, saying the health of the football pyramid should come first.

The rule was introduced in the 1960s to encourage fans to attend lower league games and prevents matches from being shown on UK television between 2:45pm and 5:15pm on Saturdays.

The blackout, which is enforced by Article 48 of the Uefa Statutes and is imposed by the English and Scottish football associations, comes into effect when 50% of fixtures in the top two divisions are scheduled to kick off at 3pm.
