GTA 6 is slowly rolling out across the globe. The crime epic's already available to play in countries such as South Korea and New Zealand, but Japanese players are finding out that male nudity is only partially obscured by a thin black thong that covers Jason and the other men in Leonida.

According to recent reports, last week, we reported that GTA 6 might have been censored in Japan since the region's age rating board lacked any mention of the "sexual content" we knew it would include elsewhere. The Japanese version's install size was also notably smaller than other places in the world.

Now that GTA 6 is finally in the hands of players, we have a better idea of what was left out and/or covered up in Japan. Multiple social media accounts playing the game in Japan have reported that when you take main man Jason into the shower, his butt is only kinda covered up by a thong. At least in the version I played, Jason is usually fully naked while washing up.

Sources indicate that another social media post spots a different male NPC wearing a very similar black thong during a cutscene that normally has him laid bare in international versions of the game. I'm guessing all instances of male nudity might be obscured similarly in GTA 6's Japanese version.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 is far from the only game that's dealt with regional censorship in Japan, however. Baldur's Gate 3 and The Witcher 3 had their most salacious scenes censored in some manner, for example. And even GTA 5 had to remove its infamous torture scene for approval in Japan. It's quite possible GTA 6 has had to cover more than just the backside of nude men to go on sale in the country, although those regional differences won't become entirely clear until players get further through the story.

Ahead of the game's full launch, GTA 6 managed to break Reddit and viewership records on platforms like YouTube and Twitch. Interesting tidbits are also flying out on release day, like the fact you can't replay missions or use cheats until finishing the story. Our Chris Tapsell still had a cracking time with the epic open worlder. "A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that," he wrote in Eurogamer's GTA 6 review.
