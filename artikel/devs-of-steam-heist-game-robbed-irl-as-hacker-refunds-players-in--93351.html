Devs of Steam heist game robbed IRL as hacker refunds players’ in-game purchases

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The developers of One-armed Robber are asking players to spend their surprise refunds in the free Steam heist game after a hacker sent the money back without the studio’s permission.

Duhndal released One-armed Robber in December 2023. The first-person game lets players rob banks and stores solo or with friends, with each robber limited to one arm. Crews can sneak into a vault or fight their way through, then haul out as much loot as they can carry. The game has roughly 54,000 Steam reviews, according to SteamDB.

Industry observers note that on November 20, Duhndal noted someone gained access to one of its Steamworks API keys and used it to ban players and issue refunds for in-game purchases. The studio said the incident affected a small share of its player base.

The refunds went to players who had never asked for their money back. Duhndal said the transactions caused “significant financial losses” for its small team. The studio gave no figure for the losses or how many refunds were issued.

Duhndal asked players who want to help it recover to put some of their refund toward another purchase in One-armed Robber or one of the studio’s other games. It also confirmed a sale on heist maps for players looking to pick up missions they don’t already own.

It has been revealed that “This is completely optional! You haven’t done anything wrong, and you don’t owe us anything,” the developer said.

Duhndal stated it revoked the compromised key and lifted all unauthorized bans. Players who were unfairly banned should now be able to get back into the game.
