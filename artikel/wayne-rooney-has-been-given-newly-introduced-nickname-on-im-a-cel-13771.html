Former Manchester United and England striker Wayne Rooney has already been given a nickname by ITV bosses, just one show into the latest series of I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!.

The 41-year-old has bagged the biggest jungle fee of the series and made his debut in dramatic style last night. He faced his fear of heights by skydiving over the Australian coast before tucking into a thick pink liquid, which Ant and Dec dubbed a prawn star martini. He then had to transfer it into another cup to reveal a word at the bottom of his drink and secure a meal for camp.

It's safe to say it's been one of the most action-packed starts to a fresh series, and the Scouser has already been voted to take on Tuesday night's Bushtucker Trial, aptly named 'Jungle Food Lab'.

'Wazza' will certainly have his work cut out if he's to go one better than his wife, Coleen, who finished as a runner-up during her appearance in 2024. But it seems he's already winning over those behind the scenes, with ITV bosses having apparently taken a shine to the former footballer.

According to The Sun, ITV bosses kept Rooney's signing under wraps by giving him a codename, months before the full lineup was declared.

That name has now emerged as 'Wonder', which is a nod to the nickname he used to have in the formative years of his Premier League career.

It has been revealed that pundits used to refer to Wazza as 'Wonder Boy' when he was just making a name for himself at Everton in the early-noughties. He famously became the youngest-ever goalscorer in Premier League history at 16, when he netted a last-minute winner for The Toffees against Arsenal in 2002.

After a career that saw him win five league titles, a Champions League as part of the 2007/08 treble-winning season, and briefly hold the title as England's record goalscorer, Rooney remains popular for his no-nonsense, often humourous punditry. There's many-a-reason he is now the shortest-ever priced celeb to win the show at 7/4.

It has also now come to light that ITV wanted Rooney to be part of last year's lineup that saw fellow Mancunians Aitch and Angry Ginge become among the two most popular celebrities.

Industry observers note that while Wayne would have had plenty of laughs with his mates, he didn’t feel physically ready to be a part of the show. Speaking to Gabby Logan on her Life At The Midpoint Podcast, Wayne admitted he has always been self-conscious about his weight and that played a major part in him declining.

He mentioned: “I wouldn’t have been able to do it, if I’m being honest. They asked me last year to go in and I spoke to Angry Ginge, who I know, and Aitch, who I know, and they were saying ‘come in’. Imagine the three of us in there. It would have been chaos. But I didn’t because physically I didn’t think I’d be able to.".

Since snubbing the show last year, Rooney has undergone an incredible body transformation, quitting alcohol and exercising more often to shave off three stone ahead of his appearance in the Australian jungle.
