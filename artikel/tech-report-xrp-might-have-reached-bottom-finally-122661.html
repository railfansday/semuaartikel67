XRP's sharp price decline might have finally stopped, as the asset has started to form a trading range that could provide a foundation for a recovery going forward. The most likely support level here is $0.9, as the price has already tested it three times.

It has been revealed that the rapid downfall of XRP began on November 5, essentially marking the beginning of a sharp decline for the asset. The failure to break above the 100 EMA effectively eliminated the possibility of an uptrend. The price failed to break the mid-term trend and declined rapidly.

Sources indicate that the declining trading volume is yet another signal. As the downtrend accelerated, investors reacted to the rapidly deteriorating market conditions by selling some of their holdings, further fueling the downward movement.

In a recent development, with the divergence of moving averages, volatility is decreasing, but a more established bearish trend is forming, unfortunately. The most likely outcome could be a deceleration of the trend and a halt to the current decline.

Fundamentally, the cryptocurrency market remains weak. Risk-on sentiment among investors has disappeared, while the crash in tech and AI stocks could further fuel outflows from Bitcoin and crypto. The recoveries we are witnessing right now remain largely speculative.

It has been revealed that it is notable to note that the $0.9 support level does not necessarily confirm that XRP has reached its bottom. The asset is still trading substantially below its major moving averages, with the 50 EMA and 100 EMA remaining out of reach for now.

Until XRP manages to reclaim at least the $1 psychological price level, any upward movement will most likely remain a short-term recovery rather than an actual trend reversal.

Recent findings suggest that at the same time, the formation of a narrow trading range around $0.93 could give buyers some breathing room. If selling pressure continues to decrease and volume stabilizes, XRP might try recovering and taking a chance at breaking the $1-$1.05 price level, where the first serious resistance is most likely.
