The Shiba Inu exchange activity has remained bearish as the broader crypto market continues to face persistent selling pressure, causing major assets to retest multi-year lows.

While other leading cryptocurrencies are still facing intense sell-offs from traders, the Shiba Inu ecosystem is beginning to signal a shift in market trend.

Although momentum still remains down and Shiba Inu exchange activity has stayed bearish, the latest data from the crypto analytics platform CryptoQuant shows that the intense selling pressure seen in the past day is finally easing.

The data reveals that the Shiba Inu exchange netflow has faded to just about 18 billion SHIB on November 21, a substantial decrease from the nearly half a trillion SHIB recorded the previous day.

It is key to note that the current SHIB netflow still reflects a positive balance, which indicates sustained dominance by bear traders.

However, the slowdown to about 18 billion SHIB reveals that SHIB selling has decreased significantly even though sellers are not yet fully wiped out, providing a relief signal amid the growing market fear.

Although the current positive balance in the SHIB netflow highlights that the number of SHIB tokens moved to exchanges for sell-off attempts is about 18 billion higher than the number purchased, its price movement is already turning bullish.

Latest market data shows that Shiba Inu has surged by 1.51% over the last 24 hours, bringing its price to $0.000003625 as of the time of writing.

Industry observers note that this suggests that demand is gradually returning to the SHIB ecosystem after multiple weeks of prolonged volatility. However, it remains uncertain whether the sudden price reversal is just a brief relief rally or a major recovery.
