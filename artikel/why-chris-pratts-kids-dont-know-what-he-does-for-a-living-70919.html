Chris Pratt doesn’t want to bring work home with him.

The Parks and Recreation alum recently shared why he has kept his and wife Katherine Schwarzenegger’s kids Lyla, 6, Eloise, 4, and Ford, 2, in the dark when it comes to his career in the spotlight.

Recent findings suggest that “I haven’t quite told my kids what I do for a living so it’s a little weird to show them my movies,” Pratt explained in an exclusive E! News interview at the premiere for his new Apple TV film The Way of the Warrior Kid Nov. 19. “They’ve started watching some movies and this is definitely appropriate for kids of my kids’ ages. I think a 4 or a 6-year-old could definitely watch this movie. However, I might not.”.

The 47-year-old—also dad to son Jack, 14, with ex Anna Faris—said that while his oldest is aware of his fame, he hasn’t felt ready to introduce his younger kids to his profession.

“They’ll be like, ‘Dad, why are you on the TV?’” he considered. “We haven’t figured that out yet.”.

In a recent development, of course, he wasn’t ruling out the concept of showing the film, which hits Apple TV Nov. 25, to at least his daughters down the line.

“I’ll have to check with Katherine,” he remarked, noting that Jack, “has already seen it and he loves it.”.

But Pratt emphasizes that his eldest son has certainly proved he can handle any pressure that comes his way.

According to recent reports, “Just recently my son Jack was studying for an A Gov test and it was really complicated stuff and they’re getting into some hard academics,” Pratt recalled, “and he was looking at an answer and I stated, ‘I don’t know if that’s the right answer or not. That’s kind of like, your teacher’s opinion,’ and he was like, ‘Yeah, but Dad, I want to get an A on this test so if that’s her opinion I want to know that that’s the right answer but I also understand that it might not be the right answer.’”.

Nodding toward the nuances of his teen’s thinking, Pratt emphasized, “I was like, ‘Oh this kid is really smart. Like, he’s going places.’”.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, Jack’s mom Faris has similarly felt floored by her son’s growth, telling E! News in March that he’s become a “very steady” kid.

“He's kind of a tugboat of a kid,” the 49-year-old—who is also stepmom to her husband Michael Barrett’s two kids—shared. “Emotionally he's not easily rattled, and he's seen a lot, as far as, like, his folks in this industry.”.
