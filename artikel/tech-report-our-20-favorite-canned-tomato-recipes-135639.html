There are certain foods that we wish we could package up and save forever, and tomatoes are at the top of that list. Canning is an excellent way to hold onto the fleeting abundance of summer, when these versatile fruits are at their ripest, juiciest, and most flavorful. Canning may never be able to re-create the feeling of picking a plump, juicy tomato fresh from the vine and slicing it while it’s still warm from the sun, but in the depths of winter, we’ll take what we can get. Whether they are using diced, puréed, crushed, or whole canned tomatoes, these recipes make the most of the ingredient, extending its life well beyond the growing season.
