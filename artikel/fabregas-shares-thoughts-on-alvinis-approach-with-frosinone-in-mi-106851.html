Cesc Fabregas has praised Massimiliano Alvini for the way he set Frosinone up against AC Milan, despite their heavy defeat.

Recent findings suggest that there has been a divided narrative after Milan put five goals past Frosinone at San Siro on Sunday night, the team that have been one of the surprise packages in Serie A so far this season. Some feel Alvini was too aggressive in his approach, while others praised his unwavering principles.

It has been revealed that it is not too dissimilar to some of the discourse that has followed Fabregas around early in his coaching career. The exchange between the Spaniard and Massimiliano Allegri made the headlines last season, after Milan’s more pragmatic approach yielded four points against the Lariani.

Speaking at a press conference on the eve of the Champions League match against AEK Athens, Fabregas also answered a question about his style of play and gave a nod to Alvini. His words are via MilanPress.

According to recent reports, “I want to continue to hear that Alvini is a great coach. He is. In the press conference he remarked he’ll never change, and for me that means identity, courage, and knowing what you want to do,” he began.

Industry observers note that “It’s very key, even when you win or lose 1-0 or not. You’re not bad when you lose, you’re not a phenomenon when you win. I’ll do the same as he did against Milan, I’ll stick with my ideas.

Industry observers note that “Obviously, there are games where you don’t play well, you have to know how to manage them when you’re not at your best. But I can’t change everything that’s good here.”.
