It has been revealed that TL;DR: Pokémon GO and Duolingo just teamed up for exclusive cross-app rewards. Score free Duolingo avatar suits, regional and shiny encounters, and more.

Pokémon GO and Duolingo just merged forces in one of the most delightfully chaotic app crossovers of the year.

It has been revealed that live from Nov. 17, the two mobile giants are running a cross-app event called Duo’s Pokémon Party. By keeping a seven-day language streak alive in Duolingo, players can unlock custom Pokémon outfits for their Duolingo avatars alongside a unique promo code for event-themed Timed Research in Pokémon GO.

It has been revealed that to join, trainers must opt into Duo’s Pokémon Party inside the Duolingo app by Dec. 21, and finish at least one lesson per day across seven separate days before Dec. 29. It's that easy.

Sources indicate that maybe we're cynical, but this feels like a rare opportunity. It's refreshing to see a free cross-promotion that rewards daily habits across both platforms:.

Four free Duolingo avatar suits: Completing daily lessons unlocks up to nine total rewards, headlined by four wearable Pokémon suits for your Duolingo profile: Pikachu, Chatot, a Poké Ball, and a Pokémon GO trainer outfit.

Industry observers note that regional and Shiny encounters in Pokémon GO: Finishing seven days of lessons generates a unique offer code redeemable on the Pokémon GO Web Store. That unlocks Timed Research featuring XP, Stardust, and guaranteed encounters with Pikachu, Meowth, Lucario, and regional-exclusive Chatot.

In a recent development, foreign-language gameplay and upcoming language tags: When you activate the Timed Research, you pick a target language, and your tasks actually appear in that language. Caught Pokémon also debut a new Language Tag on their summary screen, letting you tap to inspect their official names across different languages.

Industry observers note that we should note that while the Pokémon GO encounters are permanent additions to your Pokédex, the four Pokémon avatar suits are temporary rentals. All earned Pokémon suits will automatically expire and vanish from your Duolingo closet on March 31, 2027.
