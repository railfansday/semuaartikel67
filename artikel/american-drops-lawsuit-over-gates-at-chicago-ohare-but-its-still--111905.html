American has spent much of the year building back its Chicago presence, even earning another gate at O’Hare just from increasing capacity at the airport, which has been the backdrop of an intense turf war with United.

American Airlines dropped its lawsuit challenging Chicago’s 2025 decision to shift O’Hare International Airport gate space from American to United Airlines, days after losing an appeal in the case.

The reallocation, which took effect October 1, 2025, cost American more than 500 feet of gate space, about four narrow-body gates. American said it would operate out of seven fewer. United gained about four and a half.

American has since added three gates at O’Hare: two acquired from Spirit and one earned by growing at the airport.
