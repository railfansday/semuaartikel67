📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, over the years, NBC News’ Today has been a morning staple for many American households. As it prepares to celebrate its 75th anniversary, Today can also be viewed as the originator of influencer culture.

Sources indicate that the morning show’s unique New York location at 10 Rockefeller Plaza has proven to be a showcase for its talent and content offerings. Having streetside studios has made the show accessible to its many fans, turning Studio 1A into a city landmark and tourist attraction, with the Today Plaza recording nearly 65,000 visitors annually.

It has been revealed that with nearly 3 million total viewers as of the week of Nov. 2, 2026, Today has continued to cement itself as the premier morning viewing destination. It remains one of the few media environments where brands can move seamlessly from awareness to engagement to real-world action.

Daily engagement with viewers through television screens and fans outside the studio on the Plaza has turned Today into a natural integration point for brands and customers.

Industry observers note that “The power of the Today Plaza comes from its ability to turn audience connection into meaningful business results,” said Sari Feinberg, svp, Development, Advertising & Partnerships. “Few media environments can consistently bring together live experiences, trusted talent, premium content, and cross-platform storytelling at the scale that Today does.”.

Industry observers note that the network indicates products that have appeared on the morning show have led 78% of viewers to perceive brands advertised on Today as more trustworthy. It adds that businesses that have used the Today Plaza to promote their brands have seen triple-digit lifts in ad search engagement for brand partners compared to total TV and double-digit lifts across the full funnel.

Michaels, Lowe’s, and Citibank are some of the brands that have taken advantage of the Today Plaza while the broadcast is on air.

For Michaels, since launching its presence on the Plaza, it has expanded from a single Halloween-focused segment into six integrated Today segments over the past year, resulting in a 200% increase in ad recall, according to NBCU.

Recent findings suggest that lowes has used the Plaza to deliver a product-driven lifestyle segment showcasing outdoor furniture and seasonal home refresh ideas that inspire viewers to elevate their outdoor spaces. Its presence on Today has made the show’s viewers 25% more likely to recommend the retailer, according to NBCU.

In a recent development, finally, Citibank’s over 10-year sponsorship of the Citi Concert Series on Today has turned the Plaza into a place for live performances, creating cultural moments that unite artists, Plaza crowds, and audiences at home, while also delivering nearly 20 hours of brand exposure across linear, digital, and social. One of the most recent campaigns resulted in a 44% increase in brand favorability for Citibank among the morning show’s audience, according to NBCU.

Using Today and the Today Plaza creates a unique advertising experience, offering “authentic opportunities for brands to show up in culture, connect with consumers in meaningful ways, and drive measurable outcomes,” said Feinberg.

As it looks ahead to the next 75 years, Today aims to continue paving the way as a meaningful influencer by building strong marketing partnerships where audience trust meets action.
