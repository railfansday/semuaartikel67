West Ham United are reportedly preparing a January loan move for highly rated Arsenal youngster Ethan Nwaneri as Scott Parker looks to strengthen his squad for the second half of the Championship season.

According to an exclusive from Steve Kay at FootballTransfers, the Hammers have revived their interest in the 19-year-old attacking midfielder, having previously targeted him under Nuno Espírito Santo.

Sources indicate that nwaneri is presently on loan at Borussia Dortmund, having joined the German club on a season-long deal in September. However, the youngster has played just 29 minutes across Dortmund’s last seven matches, and Arsenal could consider recalling him before sending him elsewhere.

Having the ability to operate both centrally and on the right wing, Nwaneri could offer Parker some much-needed attacking options.

Ethan Nwaneri has been linked with a move to West Ham.

In a recent development, personally, I would absolutely welcome Nwaneri to West Ham. If nothing else, it could free up Jarrod Bowen to play more centrally.

Sources indicate that clearly, the West Ham captain has lost at least a yard of pace and is struggling stuck out on the right wing. I can’t quite shift from my mind his wonderful second-half performance against Cardiff City, when he was given something of a free role.

Unfortunately, Parker has since reverted to using Bowen as a conventional right winger, and we’re once again struggling to get the best out of our captain.

Whether anything comes of the Nwaneri link remains to be seen, particularly given the complications surrounding his existing loan agreement.

Personally, I think West Ham could do with improving both wings in the January transfer window. And that’s really just the tip of the iceberg for a team that needs serious surgery.
