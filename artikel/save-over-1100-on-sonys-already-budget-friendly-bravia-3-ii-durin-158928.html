We’re huge fans of Sony TVs here at Pop Mech, but there is an elephant in the room—they’re pretty damn expensive. While it’s sometimes hard to recommend some of its high-end models to our readers (at least from a sticker-shock perspective), Sony does make a budget-friendly option at a far more accessible price point: the Sony BRAVIA 3 II.

Better news: During Black Friday, you can save over $1,100 on the editor-tested 4K TV for a limited time. And it’s available on Amazon.

Recent findings suggest that the $1,100 in savings applies to the 100-inch Bravia 3 II, but a few sizes are on sale. You can save over $100 on the 55-inch (now $698) and the 65-inch (now $798). As you move up in size, the savings multiply. The 75-inch version is over $200 off, and the 85-inch unit is more than $400 off. The 100-inch version is the substantial kahuna, but there are plenty of savings to go around.

Recent findings suggest that the Bravia 3 II is Sony’s mid-tier, wallet-friendly option, but keep in mind that the BRAVIA line is high-end, so it’s essentially a mid-tier “plus.” In essence, it offers a cinema-like experience at an accessible price point. The device’s proprietary X-Balanced Speakers deliver deeper bass and clearer sound resolution, and it supports Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, plus integrated DTS:X surround sound for an immersive, theater-like experience.

While it’s ideal for movie buffs, it also features an enhanced 120Hz refresh rate for sports and live TV. The BRAVIA 3 II also includes a powerful XR Processor with AI scene recognition that customizes contrast, color, and brightness based on what you’re viewing. The AI functionality can also restore lost detail and reduce unwanted background noise. The unit’s XR Triluminos Pro can also reproduce more than a billion real-world colors with natural shades and hyper-realistic detail.

And like most BRAVIA TVs, it offers enhanced compatibility with Sony’s PlayStation 5, including Auto HDR Tone Mapping and Auto Genre Picture Mode to optimize picture quality for smooth, lag-free gameplay.

Industry observers note that this is easily one of our favorite Black Friday TV deals of 2026, and the editor-tested unit is a personal favorite of yours truly. If you’re looking for a larger screen, in particular, this is the perfect early Christmas gift for yourself. Or, if you’re feeling really Santa-like, it's perfect for someone special in your life. Happy shopping.
