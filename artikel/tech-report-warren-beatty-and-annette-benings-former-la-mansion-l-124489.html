📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the longtime Beverly Hills home of Warren Beatty and Annette Bening will soon hit the market, ELLE Decor can exclusively reveal. The married couple custom-built the gated residence in 1992 and raised their four kids there. The stars are one of Hollywood’s most famous power couples: Beatty has received 14 Academy Award nominations, winning for Best Director in 1982, and three Golden Globe Awards, while Bening has received five Oscar nominations, including Best Actress for 1999’s American Beauty, for which she won the Golden Globe.

In a recent development, the A-list actors parted with their longtime property in 2012, selling it for $6.7 million, per Zillow. Its latest asking price will be $14.995 million.

The nine-bedroom estate’s current owners—hotelier Jonathan Baker and his wife, Jenny—acquired the property directly from Beatty and Bening, then spearheaded a $5 million renovation that concluded in 2019. They modified the Mediterranean-style home to have a more contemporary feel, with minimal moldings, glossy surfaces, modern furnishings, and bold splashes of color.

However, they still paid homage to the estate’s movie-star pedigree, creating what they call the “Warren Beatty Theater,” a 17-seat screening room in what was once Beatty’s home office.

It has been revealed that other nods to the legendary actor—who starred in classics like Bonnie and Clyde, Dick Tracy, and Bulworth—included integrating his drop-down projection screen into the newly introduced living room. Additional perks include a wine room with a suspended-wire storage system, a wood-paneled dressing room with plenty of built-ins, and a network of terraces.

The home’s amenities continue on the manicured grounds, which feature an infinity-edge pool, a lighted tennis court, multiple al fresco entertaining areas, and a separate guest house.

The couple are parents to four children, among them Ella Beatty, who is following in her parents’ acting footsteps and recently played Lizzie Borden in the Netflix series Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story.

The Beverly Hills house will be listed for $14.995 million with Cory Weiss of Douglas Elliman and Jacob Greene of the Altman Brothers at Douglas Elliman.
