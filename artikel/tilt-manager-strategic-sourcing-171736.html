Industry observers note that apply practical commercial judgment: Work with business owners to identify essential needs rapidly, challenge unnecessary scope, and evaluate pricing, usage commitments, and total cost. Use targeted market checks or competitive bids where useful, and formal RFPs only when the complexity or value of the purchase warrants them.

According to recent reports, apply zero-based vendor budgeting: Partner with Finance and business owners to justify vendor spend from current needs, expected usage, and business value rather than carrying forward last year's budget. Challenge renewals and minimum commitments, and identify services to eliminate, consolidate, or right-size.

Industry observers note that build a simple sourcing and vendor management process: Partner with Legal, Compliance, IT, and Finance to design an easy-to-use workflow from vendor intake and onboarding through ongoing reviews, renewals, and offboarding. Build a practical negotiation playbook and coach teams on how to buy well.

Make negotiated savings real: Partner with Finance and Accounts Payable on contract-to-invoice reviews, checking billed rates, usage, discounts, credits, and price increases against agreed terms. Make procurement IPO-ready: Put in place public-tech giant controls, including purchase approvals, segregation of duties, contract records, and audit trails that support Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance.

Improve the tools and data: Contribute to Tilt's procure-to-pay (P2P) and contract lifecycle management (CLM) processes alongside Finance and Legal, keep contract and renewal records current, and use AI tools to speed up contract review and spend analysis.
