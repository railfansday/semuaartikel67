📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, peter Farrelly's "I Play Rocky" tells the story of the making of the very first "Rocky" movie. Anthony Ippolito plays "Rocky" star/writer Sylvester Stallone, and much of the film centers on his negotiations with "Rocky" producers Irwin Winkler (P.J. Byrne) and Robert Chartoff (Toby Kebbel). The movie also traces the early romantic days between Stallone and his first wife, Sasha Czack, who kept their home in order and offered support — while pregnant — throughout the production of "Rocky.".

Sasha is played by AnnaSophia Robb, who has been racking up screen credits since she was a kid. Her first major screen role was that of the title character in the 2004 TV movie "Samantha: An American Girl Holiday," and she quickly became one of the more ubiquitous child actors of her generation. Just a year later, she appeared in the dog-based film "Because of Winn-Dixie" and played the bratty Violet Beauregard in Tim Burton's "Charlie and the Chocolate Factory." Then, the year after that, she played a possibly evil child in "The Reaping" and starred opposite Josh Hutcherson in the heartbreaking book turned film "Bridge to Terabithia," which is easily one of the saddest movies Disney has ever made.

Sources indicate that robb has only continued to work steadily up to the present. She's popped up in low-profile straight-to-video movies like "Spy School," franchise starters like "Jumper," reboots like "Race to Witch Mountain," and soulful dramedies such as "The Way Way Back." Meanwhile, on the TV side of things, she's starred in the "Sex and the City" prequel show "The Carrie Diaries," PBS's "Mercy Street," and NBC's short-lived crime drama series "Grosse Pointe Garden Society." Her acting career is a healthy mix of projects big and small, and she comes across as wholly devoted in practically all of them, "I Play Rocky" included.

It has been revealed that those who might be concerned about AnnaSophia Robb's personal well-being as a former child actor (a profession that doesn't always treat kids well) should know that she has always endeavored to live a regular life beyond her career. As she told the Arapahoe Herald in 2009:.

According to recent reports, "Living in Colorado grounds me, it makes me keep perspective. [... When I fly to Los Angeles], all my problems in Colorado disappear, and I start to think about work. I change positions. I only think about working stuff, and all of my stuff at school seems so small [and vice versa].".

In a recent development, this seems healthy for a teenager traversing Hollywood, and she appears to have survived high school well enough. When CBS News caught up with her in 2014, she had enrolled at Newly introduced York University and explained that she never wanted to be a "star," per se, noting that she's rather devoted to her craft. Indeed, Robb has apparently succeeded in showbiz by retaining a healthy personal life and remaining focused on acting rather than stardom. And her career has offered her a wide variety of roles in which to ply her craft.

Case in point: Robb is excellent in "I Play Rocky" and plays Sasha Czack as a no-nonsense individual who speaks very frankly to Sylvester Stallone. To be sure, Robb possesses a natural confidence that continues to serve her very well. "I Play Rocky" itself may be "syrupy" (to quote /Film's review of the movie), but Anthony Ippolito is a standout as Stallone, and Robb does not vanish next to him, either.

"I Play Rocky" is currently playing in theaters.
