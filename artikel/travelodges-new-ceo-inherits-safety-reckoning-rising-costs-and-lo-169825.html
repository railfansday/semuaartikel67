According to recent reports, travelodge has a fresh chief executive, 38 safety recommendations to implement, and debt to refinance. Peter Krueger starts Tuesday.

Travelodge's new boss is a former investment banker, and he'll need to be. The UK budget hotel group named Peter Krueger CEO on Thursday alongside third-quarter results that revealed earnings falling. He inherits a safety scandal, rising costs and debt that starts coming due in 2027.

Krueger joins from TUI AG, where he was most recently CEO of the Holiday Experiences segment, overseeing the German package holiday group’s hotels, cruises, and excursions businesses. Before TUI, he spent 13 years in investment banking at Deutsche Bank.

Krueger succeeds Jo Boydell, who resigned in August as part of the fallout from a sexual assault at a Travelodge property in December 2022 and the tech giant’s handling of the victim’s subsequent complaint.
