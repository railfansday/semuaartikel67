It has been an excellent start to the season for Sheffield United in the Championship.

After a summer of off-field uncertainty that saw the departures of key players such as Andre Brooks, Callum O'Hare and Gus Hamer, it is fair to say Sheffield United supporters went into the season with low expectations, but Chris Wilder's men have certainly surprised many with their strong start.

After scoring a 90th-minute equaliser to salvage a point in a 1-1 draw with Southampton at Bramall Lane on Tuesday night, Sheffield United are ninth in the Championship table, just one point from the play-off places.

As the Blades continue their impressive form, hopes of a potential promotion push will be increasing in S2, but one man who may not be enjoying his current situation is striker Tom Cannon.

Cannon joined United from Leicester City in January 2025 for a reported fee of around £10 million, but he has scored just 11 goals in 73 appearances during what has been a frustrating near two-year spell at the club.

The 23-year-old has netted three times in 16 games this season, but just nine of those appearances have come from the start, and on what was just his second start in the past month against the Saints in midweek, he was replaced by Patrick Bamford at half time.

Cannon was linked with a return to two of his former loan clubs in the summer, Preston North End and Stoke City, but, speaking in September, the striker denied that he was keen to make a move elsewhere.

When asked which player the club should loan out in January, FLW's Sheffield United fan pundit Jimmy, from the YouTube channel Blades Ramble, claimed that Cannon could benefit from a temporary move, either to boost his confidence ahead of a return to Bramall Lane or increase his value before a potential sale.

"It's slightly controversial this one because I'm not sure how many would agree, but I think that Tom Cannon needs a loan away," Jimmy told FLW.

"Some Blades may have given up on him already and would like to get a fee for him because strikers obviously go for a premium, but I don't think his value would be that high at the moment.

"I think there's potential that he goes elsewhere and does well.

"He could be offered out on loan to the Championship, and then, if he does well, it certainly raises his price, but we would also have the option of bringing him back into the fold at United, where he's still got years to run on his contract.

"He could play a part for us or we could sell him on for a higher price than we'd get now.

"I would be looking at potentially getting Tom Cannon some confidence under his belt by sending him out on loan.".

Despite his underwhelming form throughout his time at the club, Wilder has always been publicly supportive of Cannon, and the Sheffield United boss may be reluctant to give up on a player he has devoted plenty of time to developing.

However, almost two years into his spell at Bramall Lane, Cannon is showing no sign of delivering the prolific form the Blades would have been expecting when they paid an eight-figure fee for his services, so Wilder may soon have a significant decision to make.

Cannon performed better during his loan stints at Preston and Stoke than he has after his permanent moves to Leicester and Sheffield United, so if the South Yorkshire outfit are keen to recoup some of their money, sending him out on loan could be the perfect way to put him in the shop window.
