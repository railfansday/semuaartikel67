Netflix Adds The 2026 Horror Movie That Made Over 500x Its Budget At The Box Office

Recent findings suggest that "Obsession" passed a box office milestone only five other horror films ever have when it crossed the $500 million mark. That would be an achievement regardless of its budget, but this movie only cost $600,000 to $750,000 to make. That means that when "Obsession" brought in $524.4 million by the end of its unprecedented box office run, it had made more than 500 times its budget.

In a recent development, for a while, that made "Obsession" the most profitable movie ever at the box office, until it was technically dethroned by the low-budget Chinese movie "Niu Lai." Still, it remains one of the most stunning horror success stories of recent years, which is especially impressive considering we've seen multiple low-budget horror movies become hits of late. But if you somehow managed to miss out on all of this, or just want to revisit one of the best horror movies of 2026, Netflix has you covered.

"Obsession" has a relatively simple premise, yet its execution and the lead performances elevate it beyond your typical low-budget horror fare. The film stars Michael Johnston as Baron "Bear" Bailey, a luckless music store worker who has a major crush on his friend/co-worker Nikki Freeman (Inde Navarrette). After buying a One Wish Willow toy, he wishes that Nikki loved him more than anyone in the world and gets everything he asked for and more. The only problem is, Nikki becomes absolutely obsessed with him, to the extent she's willing to hurt anyone who gets in her way.

Recent findings suggest that it's well worth doing so. "Obsession" is a confident crowd-pleaser with a killer lead performance from Navarette. In fact the actor's portrayal of Nikki proved to be her breakout turn, landing Navarrette a role as Rogue in the Marvel Cinematic Universe's upcoming "X-Men" movie reboot. No doubt, more high-profile roles await. As such, even if you're not too intrigued by the prospect of a twisted girlfriend causing violent chaos, you should at least check out "Obsession" to see one of the best performances of 2026 and the beginning of a star's ascendence. The film is streaming on Netflix as of November 17, 2026.
