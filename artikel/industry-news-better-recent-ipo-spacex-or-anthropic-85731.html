It has been revealed that this has been an active year for IPOs. The two largest have been Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -7.69%) and Anthropic (ANT -8.50%), which are among the leaders in their respective industries. Both companies sport eye-popping valuations, but considering the massive opportunities they have identified for their businesses, they might deliver outstanding returns even from current levels for investors willing to stay the course, if they can execute. But which one is the better buy today? Let's find out whether investors should invest in SpaceX or Anthropic right now.

Sources indicate that spaceX's third-quarter revenue increased by 161% year over year to $14.6 billion. It reported a net profit of $4.4 billion, versus a net loss of $1.1 billion in the prior-year quarter. Those are strong results, largely attributable to SpaceX's Super Intelligence (SI) and connectivity segments, both of which were profitable in the quarter. SI revenue of $7.6 billion accounted for 52% of the tech giant's top line.

In a recent development, spaceX's space launch segment is still operating at a loss, but the company's opportunities in SI and connectivity are much larger. SpaceX identified a $28.5 trillion total addressable market, $26.5 trillion of which is within the artificial intelligence (AI) industry. The company is investing heavily to tap into this market. SpaceX is actively looking to increase its compute capacity to meet the soaring demand for AI compute.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Within a few years, the rocket firm might generate an even larger share of its revenue from its SI business, given how promptly it is growing. In the meantime, the company's next-gen rocket, Starship, could unlock even more opportunities, including in AI and connectivity.

It has been revealed that starship is still being tested, but it may help significantly reduce launch costs, thereby cutting operating expenses in the tech giant's space segment and enabling it to launch more satellites into orbit, including its newer, better Starlink V3 satellites and potentially AI satellites. We aren't there yet, but SpaceX's ambitions look promising. If the company can realize them, its stock could soar over the long run.

In a recent development, anthropic is now the record holder for the largest IPO ever, and the company's recent financial results help explain why. In the third quarter, Anthropic's revenue grew 1,127% year over year to $17 billion. The company ended the period with a $71 billion annualized revenue run rate, and by the end of October, that had already grown to $81 billion.

Industry observers note that that highlights just how strong the demand for Anthropic's AI-powered tools is. Meanwhile, in the third quarter, the firm recorded an operating loss of $567 million, much better than the $3.1 billion loss in the third quarter of 2025. Regarding the bottom line, there is good news in bad news. Let's start with the latter.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Anthropic's Q3 net loss was a massive $23.3 billion, versus the net loss of $12.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025. But here's the good news: Most of that loss was due to non-cash charges resulting from increases in the fair value of various financial instruments. For long-term investors, that probably isn't the worst thing in the world, especially looking at Anthropic's pace of growth and the opportunities ahead.

The company wants to tap into the $40 trillion in economic value that knowledge workers create. Anthropic's AI tools help make these workers far more efficient and could unlock opportunities that were previously unattainable. And it has only begun to scratch the surface of the opportunities available to it. If all goes well, the stock may produce life-changing returns over the long run.

It has been revealed that anthropic is generating higher revenue and growing faster than SpaceX, and it is also less expensive, at least based on both companies' price-to-sales (P/S) ratios. Based on Anthropic's $1.76 trillion market cap (on a fully diluted basis as of this writing) and its trailing 12-month revenue as of Sept. 30, the stock trades at 49.9x sales.

In a recent development, spaceX's P/S is 57.2. However, while SpaceX was profitable in the third quarter, Anthropic wasn't. Both remain unprofitable over the trailing 12-month period, but SpaceX will likely be in the green on the bottom line next year. What about both companies' opportunities? Their projected addressable markets may be a bit overstated, but there is no question that huge opportunities lie ahead for both.

It has been revealed that so, which one is the better buy today? I'd pick SpaceX. It is closer to profitability, is the undisputed leader in space launch and satellite-based connectivity, and is more diversified than Anthropic. That said, both stocks look like high-risk, high-reward plays. At current levels, only investors comfortable with significant volatility should consider either.
