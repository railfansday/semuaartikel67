📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Two points in three matches for English clubs this Tuesday in the Champions League. After Aston Villa’s defeat at Galatasaray (1-0), at 6:45 p.m., Arsenal and Manchester City failed to win at 9 p.m.

Recent findings suggest that the Gunners were held by Dortmund (1-1), in a lively meeting, where Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring (10th). The BvB reacted well in the second half, with the equalizing goal from Maximilian Beier (55th), following a lot of work from young Frenchman Joane Gadou.

According to recent reports, A little further north, Manchester City conceded a draw against Naples (2-2). It was the visitors who led the score twice, thanks to Rasmus Hojlund (55th) and Noa Lang (76th), but the Skyblues managed to react thanks to two former Ligue 1 players: ex-Lille player Ayyoub Bouaddi (67th), whose first goal in C1, and former Lyonnais Rayan Cherki (86th).

In the standings, before Wednesday’s matches, Arsenal is provisionally 5th with 11 points, while Man City is in 6th place with 10 points on the clock.
