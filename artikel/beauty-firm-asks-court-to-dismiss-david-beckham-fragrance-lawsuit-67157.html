A German beauty group that makes and distributes former soccer player David Beckham’s fragrance line has kicked out at the soccer icon’s lawsuit against them, asking a Latest York court to dismiss the case.

The Associated Press reported Tuesday that German-based Coty has argued that the New York Supreme Court lacks jurisdiction, and that Beckham’s commercial entity DB Ventures is simply using the claim to “manufacture its early exit” from their deal to sign with another tech giant.

DB Ventures had originally filed suit in April, alleging that Beckham’s brand of luxury colognes and fragrances were being sold at gas stations and discount stores in violation of their agreement.

According to the AP, Beckham’s name is a famous global brand synonymous with luxury and elegance.

According to recent reports, “Imagine, then, the shock and dismay experienced when DB Ventures discovered that David Beckham products were being offered for sale in a gas station,” the complaint remarked. “How could this possibly have happened? Desperation and greed.”.

According to the AP, DB Ventures is seeking at least $41 million in damages for breach of its licensing agreement.

According to recent reports, coty told the AP that it strongly disputes the claims and would not comment further on ongoing litigation. Authentic Brands, DB Ventures’ parent organization, declined to comment to the AP.

Beckham, a former Manchester United, Real Madrid, AC Milan, Paris Saint-Germain and LA Galaxy player who captained the English national football team and was knighted in 2025, has built a business career that includes co-owning soccer team Inter Miami.

According to the Sunday Times, he is the first billionaire sports figure in the United Kingdom, having a combined net worth of about $1.58 billion with his wife, Victoria Beckham, a former member of the Spice Girls music group.
