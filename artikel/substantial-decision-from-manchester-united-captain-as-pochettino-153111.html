Recent findings suggest that manchester United transfer news: Red Devils captain Bruno Fernandes has turned down a second contract offer, according to a report from The Athletic covered by CaughtOffside. The 32-year-old is unhappy with both the proposed salary and the length of the deal, and the standoff lands with his current contract set to expire in June 2027.

According to recent reports, the calendar adds pressure. In 50 days, Fernandes can open talks with foreign clubs, and Galatasaray have already been linked. United hold a 12-month extension option that stops him leaving for free, but triggering it only postpones the argument and keeps an unhappy captain in the dressing room.

Industry observers note that the refusal follows a bad few weeks. After a 4-1 Champions League defeat by Como, Fernandes publicly criticised Michael Carrick’s tactics, and Carrick dropped him for the Bournemouth game. Carrick has since been sacked with United 18th in the Premier League, inside the relegation zone, so Mauricio Pochettino inherits the standoff alongside a team in trouble.

According to recent reports, his strengths are obvious. He set a Premier League record with 21 assists last season, delivers set pieces better than anyone at the club and opened this campaign with a hat-trick against Ipswich. His weaknesses are just as clear. At 32, the Portuguese international gives little protection when United lose the ball, and his shot selection can be wasteful.

Recent findings suggest that pochettino’s best sides pressed hard and ran through a creative number ten with two athletic midfielders behind him. Fernandes suits the creative role, but only if those two midfielders cover his space.

In a recent development, united should keep him, but on shorter terms than he wants. Nobody else in the squad creates chances at his rate, and a team sitting in the bottom three cannot replace that in January. A long, expensive deal for a player entering his mid-thirties would create a wage problem just as Pochettino tries to rebuild the midfield around younger runners.

Recent findings suggest that the sensible offer is a short contract with a raise tied to appearances and Champions League qualification. That respects his standing without committing United beyond the point where his legs start to fail.

It has been revealed that if Fernandes still refuses, the board should trigger the extension and sell next summer. A sale with a year left on his deal will still bring a meaningful fee, whereas losing him for nothing in 2027 would waste the one asset United can sell on their own terms.
