It has been revealed that you'll be hard-pressed to find a PlayStation 5 Pro in stock right now. Major retailers, including PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Amazon (including bundle offers), Target, and Walmart, are sold out. With the upcoming release of Grand Theft Auto VI, the reseller market has gotten a little ridiculous (as has everything else).

It has been revealed that beyond the obvious hype for Rockstar's next release, Sony is dealing with broader supply chain bottlenecks. The global AI boom is squeezing the semiconductor market, which means Sony can't build consoles fast enough. According to distributor reports, these supply constraints could keep stock tight well into 2027.

Naturally, resellers are having a field day. We've seen third-party listings on reseller sites (and even GameStop's official pre-owned page) gouging buyers for $1,300 to $1,500+ for a console that retails for $899. Sony is taking the issue so seriously that in Japan, they're forcing buyers to enter a lottery — and prove they've clocked at least 60 hours of PS4 or PS5 playtime over the last two years — just for a chance to buy a PS5 Pro. If you absolutely have to buy one today and have money to burn, here's what the third-party market currently looks like:.

Amazon: Used PS5 Pro consoles are selling for more than $1,700; no new versions are right now available.

GameStop: A pre-owned unit will currently run you $1,399.99.

Swappa: This is a legitimate, verified marketplace for used tech. However, verified PS5 Pro listings are right now sitting at $1,338.

StockX: The popular resale bidding site at the moment has the console hovering around $1,239.

Walmart: Don't be fooled by third-party sellers on major retail sites. A low-rated seller on Walmart is at the moment asking $1,669.99 (open-box units are already sold out).

Mercari: We previously found a listing here for $800. Since this is $100 below the official MSRP for a highly coveted, sold-out item, we think it was almost certainly a scam. Avoid deals that sound too good to be true.

If you're set on securing a Pro before or after launch day, here's how to play the restock game without burning a hole in your wallet:.

Industry observers note that bookmark and refresh product pages. Keep direct product pages saved for PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Amazon, Target, and Walmart. PlayStation Direct in particular occasionally drops invite-only or queue-based stock.

Recent findings suggest that check local inventory. Sometimes brick-and-mortar stores (like local GameStops or Target stores) receive small physical allocations that don't always show up online right away. It never hurts to ask an associate in person.

Industry observers note that preorder the game now. Don't hold your breath for a last-minute GTA 6 PS5 Pro console bundle. Secure your physical or digital preorder now so you're ready to go, no matter which console sits under your TV on day one.

Recent findings suggest that the official retail price for a PS5 Pro is $899. If you see it listed for $1,300, $1,500, or more, you're looking at a third-party markup. Don't feed the resellers.
