In a recent development, nvidia (NVDA -14.01%) has been a rough stock to own over the past few weeks. It has lost its co-founder and CEO, Jensen Huang, and some investors worry it may never recover. While the board searches for a upcoming leader to fill his shoes, investors are waiting to see what happens next.

Recent findings suggest that in the meantime, I think there are several great companies available to invest in, with Broadcom (AVGO -12.19%) being one of the best options. Broadcom is a direct competitor of Nvidia and has some advantages over Nvidia. That could lead to it stealing more market share during the current turmoil, making it a top stock to consider buying now.

In a recent development, nvidia's primary products are graphics processing units (GPUs), which are incredible computing units that can process multiple calculations in parallel. While these units are great at nearly any task, they don't specialize in any one task. Some of this potential is wasted when a GPU is used for one workload throughout its lifespan. A specific and purpose-built computing unit would do a better job in these scenarios, and that's where Broadcom steps in.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Broadcom has emerged as a great partner for designing custom AI chips, and many companies have already partnered with it and launched their own custom AI chips. However, there has been no larger partner than Alphabet (GOOG -2.59%) (GOOGL -2.70%). Alphabet and Broadcom collaborated to design and build its Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), which can outperform GPUs in many scenarios from a cost perspective. These units are fantastic, and companies like Anthropic have designed much of their computing infrastructure around them.

In a recent development, with Nvidia GPUs expensive and not purpose-built, many AI companies are turning to custom AI chips like the TPU to deliver the computing power they need at a lower price point. This is translating into huge growth for Broadcom, and it's growing even faster than Nvidia is.

Industry observers note that broadcom hasn't been shy about telling investors of the impressive growth that's coming down the pipeline. During its most recent conference call, management revealed guidance that AI semiconductor revenue will double in 2027 to $115 billion. Furthermore, Broadcom unveiled that that figure is expected to double again to $230 billion in 2028. It doesn't get much better than that, and investors are bound to be happy with returns if Broadcom can follow through on that projection.

Sources indicate that despite that projection, Broadcom's stock isn't valued at all that high. Broadcom is already in fiscal year 2027, and trades at just 17 times next year's earnings.

AVGO PE Ratio (Forward) data by YCharts.

That's a very cheap price tag to pay for a stalwart like Broadcom.

Broadcom also doesn't carry the same risks as a tech giant like Nvidia, where a questionable portion of sales is being diverted to China. Broadcom's customers are well-known, and there isn't much risk of its products falling into the wrong hands. This is a key factor that many investors ignore, along with the fact that Broadcom's products are starting to steal data center market share.

I think all of this adds up to Broadcom being one of the best investment options right now. It's cheap, growing quickly, doesn't have export risk, and has a stable leadership team. That is a recipe for success in the AI world, and I think Broadcom shareholders will be well rewarded over the long term.

Now is a perfect time to buy amid the turmoil in the market surrounding AI stocks. By this time next year, Broadcom's stock will likely be much higher.
