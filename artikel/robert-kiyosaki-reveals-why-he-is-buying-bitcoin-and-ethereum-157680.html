Renowned financial guru and Rich Dad Poor Dad author Robert Kiyosaki has offered compelling insight into the reasons behind his buying spree of Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) despite the current crypto market crash.

Kiyosaki, who is an esteemed investor, predicts that a financial crisis could be on the horizon, and it might be even worse than the 2008 market crash. He believes that crypto assets and other hard assets are an effective hedge against the repercussions of government-induced overborrowing and money printing.

The financial guru's latest X post explains his rationale behind investing in Bitcoin, Ethereum, gold, silver, real estate, and oil instead of traditional financial assets.

"Why I am buying real (no ETFs) gold, silver, Bitcoin, and Ethereum," Kiyosaki noted in his latest post.

He attributed his views to the Federal Reserve, the U.S. Treasury and the colossal debt obligations of the U.S.

According to Kiyosaki, the government's inability to curb spending will lead to massive money printing in the future. This might erode the purchasing power of the U.S. dollar.

"The BIG PRINT is about to begin," Kiyosaki warned.

Industry observers note that this is the core reason for his investments in Bitcoin and Ethereum. He believes that these assets will rise in value when the government is forced to aggressively print money in response to escalating financial complications.

Moreover, he believes that holding these crypto assets directly would be better than investing in exchange-traded funds (ETFs). However, he did not offer a reason for his opposition to ETFs in his post.

Furthermore, Kiyosaki stated that the next financial crisis could be much bigger than the global financial crisis of 2008.

"This crash we are just now sliding into is much bigger than 2008. Possibly a global depression," he commented.

He also mentioned that his gold, silver, Bitcoin and Ethereum holdings could make him one of the ultra-rich if his predictions come true.

Industry observers note that however, Kiyosaki mentioned some figures that were not entirely accurate. For instance, he stated that the U.S. national debt stood at $49 trillion. But according to Treasury data, the national debt was about $40.7 trillion in November 2026. Also, his claim that the U.S. is printing $1 trillion every 90 days conflates government debt with money printing.

Although Kiyosaki is a strong believer in Bitcoin, he recently advised investors not to buy Bitcoin immediately amid the digital asset's current price slump.

On Nov. 20, the Rich Dad Poor Dad author cautioned against catching a 'falling knife.'.

"Never grab a falling knife." This means that investors should wait for the market crash to end before buying.

"I started buying bitcoin around $20k after the market crashed last time," Kiyosaki recalled.

"Will wait for Bitcoin bottom again," he added.
