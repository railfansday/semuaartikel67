Lexie Shoaibi, the voice actor behind Mandy in Grand Theft Auto 6, has called her newly found fame “bittersweet” while hitting out at people trying to exploit her likeness.

After 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto fans have finally been able to jump into GTA 6, exploring Jason and Lucia’s story, as well as the wider state of Leonida.

One of the first side characters that players are introduced to is Mandy, one of Brian’s tenants in Key Lento, during the mission Pipework. This is the same mission that some fans reported problems with, forcing them to start latest saves.

Mandy’s appearance has got some fans thirsting over her, while others believe she’s got a serial killer vibe to her. Naturally, Lexie Shoaibi, who voices the character, has had the spotlight shone on her too.

The actress appeared on Hasan Piker’s November 22 stream, trying her hand at GTA and also helping him with a charity fundraiser.

Sources indicate that she told the Twitch star that her newly found fame was a bit “bittersweet” as it has spawned fake social media accounts. “I don’t know how that happened,” Shoaibi said. “People pretending to be me? It’s thrilling. Please stop, but it’s also so exciting. It’s sort of bittersweet.”.

Industry observers note that shoaibi also noted that she spent around 10 days on set for Grand Theft Auto 6, and plays other “little characters,” so she isn’t just Mandy. “It was awesome,” she said.

Outside of Piker’s stream, the actress also issued a warning on Instagram about the fake accounts.

“This is fake! I don’t have a Twitter! This isn’t me! Please, someone, make it go away, ew!” she remarked in an Instagram Story post.

Recent findings suggest that shoaibi isn’t the only actor to embrace their role in GTA 6. The actors behind Jason and Lucia have also confirmed their identities, responding to increased attention on social media as well.

They haven’t got any cheat codes to make the heat die down outside of Rockstar’s virtual world, and will now forever be known as their characters.
