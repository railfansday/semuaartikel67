Tech Report: The free game reward that asks for your 2FA code

🌐 Source: Dev • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (132 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Malwarebytes found a fake "FC27 x Prime Gaming" reward page aimed at EA Sports FC players.
  • It claims your reward is waiting and you need to connect your EA account.
  • It has been revealed that the page also records whether you came from Facebook, Instagram, Messenger or TikTok.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Malwarebytes found a fake "FC27 x Prime Gaming" reward page aimed at EA Sports FC players.

It claims your reward is waiting and you need to connect your EA account. The sign-in page looks like EA's, but it sits on the scammers' own domain. It takes your email, your password and then your 2FA code, and sends each one to their server. Then it drops you on the real EA site so nothing feels off.

It has been revealed that the page also records whether you came from Facebook, Instagram, Messenger or TikTok. That lets them track which platforms send visitors.

Type ea.com yourself. A sign-in code only goes into the real site, for a login you started.

Source: Malwarebytes, Nov 16, 2026.
