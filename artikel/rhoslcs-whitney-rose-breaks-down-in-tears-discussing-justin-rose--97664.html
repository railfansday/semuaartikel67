The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City’s Whitney Rose is opening up about the highs and lows of navigating her recent split from estranged husband Justin Rose.

Recent findings suggest that “So lately, through divorce process, the show airing, life, kids, all of the things, I find myself — I just cry at random times,” Whitney, 40, remarked in a Sunday, November 22, Instagram video. “I will be overcome with emotion and just start crying. Half the time I don’t know if I’m crying because I’m sad, if I’m angry, if I’m happy or I don’t know. I just let myself feel it and I just go through it and I just let it out. Half the time it catches me off guard. I’ll be walking down the aisle in the grocery store or I’ll be in a meeting or in the middle of the night. I don’t know. Divorce sucks. It sucks. It’s hard.”.

News broke in July that Whitney and Justin called it quits after more than a decade together. Whitney filed for divorce from Justin, with whom she shares daughter Bobbie, 16, and son Brooks, 13, in October.

“I’m just so grateful for all the things that I do have,” she mentioned, becoming emotional. “I woke up in my beautiful home that I love with my kids safe in their beds. I have a healthy body. And yes, times are hard right now but I’m just so grateful and proud of myself for pushing through it and teaching myself how resilient I really am. Even with all of the critics. So I just want to thank the universe and put it back out there that I’m so grateful for everything that I have good in my life right now.”.

Despite feeling “pain and heartache,” Whitney reflected on having “so many beautiful things happening” in her life.

According to recent reports, “I’m just grateful that I never gave up on myself, my career, my happiness,” she remarked. “I hope everyone can take a pause today and share in the gratitude because when we give it to the universe, it comes right back to us. So, love you all.”.
