In a recent development, mark Gurman reports that Apple hasn’t named a new vice president to replace McCormack, who reported to software chief Craig Federighi. “Instead, his former deputies are assuming his responsibilities under other executives,” the report says.

In his role, McCormack oversaw “close coordination between software engineers and Apple’s camera hardware and chip teams.” He’s credited with last year’s redesigned selfie camera and other projects:.

Industry observers note that his work included the redesigned selfie camera introduced with the iPhone 17 lineup last year. This year, Apple added a variable-aperture camera to the iPhone 18 Pro lineup, giving users more control over lighting and depth of field. The organization also introduced a customizable Camera app interface with professional controls.

Sources indicate that bloomberg describes McCormack as a “strong opponent of using generative AI to create or alter photographs,” a philosophy that created tension as Apple embraced AI tools in the Photos app. His retirement, however, “wasn’t related to a disagreement or clash with Apple over its AI strategy,” the report indicates.
