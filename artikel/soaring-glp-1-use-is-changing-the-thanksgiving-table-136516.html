The number of people using weight loss drugs such as Ozempic, Wegovy, and Mounjaro have increased drastically over the last two years, changing the grocery landscape and the Thanksgiving table.

The number of U.S. households that included a current GLP-1 user rose from 9% in January 2025 to 21% in May 2026, according to a PwC analysis of Numerator’s survey data. Separately, a survey from Gartner this year found that 10% of consumers said they had taken a GLP-1 medication in the last 12 months.

“Even if you’re not on one, [you are] thinking about the accommodations of other people in your group or family or Friendsgiving who might be,” Emily Weiss, senior principal analyst at Gartner, told ADWEEK.
