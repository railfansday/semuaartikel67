PQube will publish Village Studio-developed card-based hacking adventure game Hack ’95 when it rolls out for PC via Steam in 2027, the companies confirmed.

Hack ’95 is being developed by a team of industry veterans with experience at SEGA, Rovio, Disney, TikTok, and Adult Swim, with a script overseen by international bestselling author (A Boy Made of Blocks) and veteran games journalist (The Guardian) Keith Stuart.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Welcome to the dawn of the Internet. Your best friend, elite hacker mega_hurtz, is missing, presumed dead. All signs point to an assassination by the Federal Bureau of Cyber Security and the all-powerful Axis Megasystems. To uncover the truth, you’ll need illegal scripts and righteous hardware to hack your way through the Bureau’s high-security House of Cards network.

In a recent development, hack ’95 is a card-based hacking adventure set inside Navigate ’95, a ‘state-of-the-art’ operating system featuring multitasking and CD-quality sound. Chat with the freaks and geeks of cyberspace’s underbelly, who task you with defeating security systems and snooping through compromised desktops. All to unravel a conspiracy more sinister than you could ever imagine.

It has been revealed that no coding skills? No problem! All you need is a deck, a 56kbps modem, and a healthy disregard for authority.
