Arsenal are still sweating on the fitness of David Raya as they prepare for a crunch run of fixtures that begins with Sunday’s visit of Manchester City in the Premier League.

The Gunners were rocked by an injury to their influential first-choice goalkeeper during the latter stages of last weekend’s eventful 2-2 draw with Newcastle after he hurt his shoulder during an initially innocuous-looking collision with William Osula.

Raya sat out Tuesday’s lively home draw with Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League, with unconvincing deputy Kepa Arrizabalaga stepping in from the start ahead of No3 Illan Meslier.

Mikel Arteta will be desperate to have the reigning Golden Glove winner back for a crucial stretch of games that sees Arsenal also take on the likes of Tottenham, Real Madrid and Manchester United before Christmas.

With that massive showdown against title rivals City coming up at the Emirates Stadium, here is all the latest Arsenal injury news and return dates, with William Saliba now back and Max Dowman having served his suspension.

Raya was in considerable pain after colliding with Osula at St James’ Park and had to go off after receiving treatment, with his left arm placed in a protective sling before he was promptly sent for scans.

He was seen still wearing that sling while out in London the next day and was then absent from Arsenal’s open training session at London Colney on Monday morning.

Arteta stated that he would definitely miss the Dortmund game, but soothed fan fears by stating that the injury was “not too serious” following “encouraging” test results and did not require surgery, raising hopes that he would be in a position to make a quick return against City.

A new enhancement was expected following the draw with Dortmund, when Arteta was coy on if Raya would be involved at the weekend.

It has been revealed that “No, we have nothing to add today unfortunately,” Arteta noted. “We need to know first how bad it is and whether he has a chance or not [to face Man City].”.

Potential return date: Sunday November 29, vs Manchester City (H) or Wednesday December 2, vs Brentford (A).

Saka has not played for Arsenal since limping off early during the heavyweight 2-2 away draw with Bayern Munich in the Champions League on October 21.

The England winger sustained a thigh injury and initial reports claimed there were fears that he could be sidelined for up to eight weeks.

Saka has yet to resume training and when asked about his recovery before the Newcastle game, Arteta suggested that he was likely to miss another month.

According to recent reports, quizzed on if Saka would be available for Manchester United’s visit to north London just before Christmas, the manager mentioned: “I cannot be that specific. In four weeks’ time a lot of things can happen. Maybe it is three and then it is five.

It has been revealed that “At the end, there are a few elements. One is the healing of that tissue – and we cannot predict exactly when that tissue is going to be in the best condition to push it.

“Then there is the rehab plan that we have now, what he needs to do in the gym, what he needs to do in terms of treatment and on the field.”.

Potential return date: Sunday December 20, vs Manchester United (H).

Zubimendi started for Spain in their dramatic 3-2 defeat by England during the November international break, but was forced off injured in the 72nd minute.

It was later revealed to be a hamstring complaint, which Arteta expects to keep him out for a period of weeks after he played no part against either Newcastle or Dortmund.

"He's out," Arteta noted of Zubimendi last week. “Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring.

"He wasn't too concerned to be fair after the game, but after the MRI scans, it demonstrated that there is an injury there and he's going to be out for a few weeks.".

Potential return date: Sunday December 13, vs Bournemouth (H).
