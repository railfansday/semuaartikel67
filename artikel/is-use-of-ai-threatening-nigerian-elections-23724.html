Is use of AI threatening Nigerian elections?

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, with less than two months to Nigeria’s general elections, there are growing concerns about the possible use of artificial intelligence to influence the outcome of the election. Artificial intelligence is being used for various sinister purposes sometimes to influence voters' perceptions. There also appears an attempt to manipulate the voters register. It was recently discovered that the register was bloated with AI-created individuals. France 24's Sam Olukoya reports from Lagos.
