KLM Offloaded A Passenger Rather Than Use The Blocked Seat We Offered

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that I recently flew KLM from Amsterdam to Barcelona, and a passenger was taken off our full flight even though my wife and I offered him the empty seat between us. The crew followed a rule exactly, and as far as I can tell, nobody got anything out of it.

According to recent reports, business class inside Europe is basically a regular economy seat with the middle seat blocked. On some planes the middle seat is physically blocked with a small table. On this flight it was just empty, blocked online so nobody could book it, but technically a regular seat anyone could sit in.

I was on KL1519 on 2 September, in row 2 with my wife, with that empty middle seat between us.

After the doors closed, it turned out that two passengers had boarding passes for the same seat. Whoever got there first sat down. The other one was a fairly young guy, and now he had nowhere to sit. The flight was completely full, there wasn’t a single free seat in economy.

I heard the crew apologizing to him and saying they would have to take him off the plane and put him on the next flight.

I knew this would take a while, because the doors were already closed and he had boarded. Getting him off meant opening the doors again, and maybe taking his bag off too. Once on another flight someone got off and everyone had to hold up their bags.

And I felt sorry for the guy. Maybe he was in a hurry to get somewhere, and none of this was his fault. The airline gave two people boarding passes for one seat, and he was already on board.

Sources indicate that so I asked my wife if she minded giving up the empty seat between us. The flight is only about two hours. She was fine with it, so I went to the crew and offered it.

The crew was very grateful, but said seating someone there was against the rules and they couldn’t decide it themselves, they had to check with someone.

That took about 10 minutes. Then they came back, thanked me again, and mentioned that unfortunately it wasn’t possible under the rules.

So they opened the doors and offloaded the guy, I hope they rebooked him on the next one. In the end we pushed back about 40 minutes late and landed in Barcelona about half an hour late. After landing I think even the captain came over, shook my hand and thanked me.

According to recent reports, as I understand it, the middle seat is blocked to give the business class passengers more space. In this case that was us, and we were the ones offering to give that space up.

We didn’t need the space, and we’d have put up with one more person in the row for two hours.

Industry observers note that on a completely full KLM flight, two passengers got boarding passes for the same seat. My wife and I offered the empty blocked seat between us in business, so he wouldn’t have to get off. The crew checked with someone, the answer was no, and he was offloaded anyway, and we left about 40 minutes late.

Recent findings suggest that I get that the crew couldn’t make this call themselves. Still, we didn’t need that seat at all.

Is there a good reason for this rule that I’m missing?
