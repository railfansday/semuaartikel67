AC Milan are turning to youth for their next transfer window: in addition to Real Madrid’s Brazilian forward Endrick, the Serie A side are thinking of two young Ligue 1 talents.

The Lombard club have a initiative to take Endrick from Real Madrid in the January window. Despite the reluctance of Jose Mourinho, the Brazilian could indeed leave if the Spanish club’s management as a whole is won over by an offer of £42.5m (€50m) plus bonuses, along with a buy-back clause of £68m (€80m), on a model similar to the deal that sent Nico Paz to Como.

AC Milan also want to add a centre-back to their squad, as their majority shareholder, billionaire Gerry Cardinale, dreams of Virgil van Dijk, whose contract at Liverpool expires next June.

But the fourth-placed side in Serie A could also sign one or two talents from Ligue 1. First, according to Gazzetta dello Sport, AC Milan are taking a close interest in Ismaelo Ganiou. An already impressive list of 13 suitors exists for the RC Lens centre-back, who is also followed closely by a 14th suitor from the Premier League, Liverpool.

In a recent development, do the Italians have a chance? The Frenchman’s valuation is high, according to Gazzetta, which cites a figure of £34m (€40m). The Italian outlet says AC Milan can get involved from around £30m (€35m). Is that enough, given the very strong competition for the player?

Sources indicate that while Gazzetta also cites Sporting CP defender Goncalo Inacio (25) and Union Saint-Gilloise’s Senegalese Massire Sylla (21), Calciomercato.com mentions an interest from AC Milan in Nathan Ngoy. The 23-year-old Belgium international is a little older than Ganiou but less sought after, although Aston Villa and Newcastle are also in the race for the Belgian defender.

In a recent development, under contract with Lille until June 2029, Ngoy could leave for a fee of around £34m (€40m), according to an international agent quoted by Belgian outlet La Derniere Heure. Still a little too expensive for the Lombard club? In either case, RC Lens and Lille can rub their hands: the two northern clubs should receive handsome cheques for their player in 2027.
