In a recent development, A challenging first half for Billingshurst meant they fell to defeat against promotion challengers Hassocks. The away team were swiftly out of the traps & on 7 minutes only a great block from Jamie Smith prevented the away side taking the lead. It was a short term reprieve when 5 minutes later, ‘hurst gave the ball away and Omar Abdsami finished well across goalkeeper Fraser Trigwell. Worst was to follow when from a corner, a Hassocks header made it 2-0. At this point the officials intervened & decided that the ball hadn’t crossed the line and gave a penalty for a handball against Nat Guest. The penalty was duly despatched by Lewis Finney to make it 2-0 against now 10 man  Billingshurst. Within 2 minutes, it was 3-0 when Finney latched on to a free ball and scored from 25 yards. For the next 20 minutes, Hassocks continued to dominate play and it was little surprise that just prior to half-time, Dan Perry added to his tally against Billingshurst having scored a hat-trick for East Grinstead Town a few weeks earlier. 4-0 at half time.

Into the 2nd half, Billingshurst changed their shape & to their credit battled hard without creating a huge number of chances. Hassocks continued to dominate but did not make that dominance pay but still ran out comfortable 4-0 winners.

They scored a 2nd from a corner that wasn't even  a corner, the resulting header went over the line, but then the  officials decided it was a handball but our player was behind the goal  line.
