Former Wolves midfielder Rúben Neves has revealed he is looking forward to playing at the Cardiff City Stadium again, as he reflected on a ‘crazy’ 1-0 win over the Bluebirds earlier in his career.

The 29-year-old is currently playing for Al-Hilal, with the move to Saudi Arabia coming after six years at Wolves. Neves had a remarkable time at Molineux, initially joining the club from Porto when they were in the Championship.

Despite the risk, it was a move that worked out, as he starred for Nuno Espírito Santo’s side as they won the title in the second tier, before recording consecutive 7th-placed finishes in the Premier League.

Neves would ultimately make over 250 appearances in Old Gold, and his quality, leadership and loyalty means he is regarded as a modern-day legend at the club.

During that period, there were many high points for Wolves and Neves, and one of the standout moments came during the 2017/18 campaign, when Wanderers won the Championship title.

In April of that season, Nuno’s side made the trip to take on fellow promotion rivals Cardiff, and the two teams played out one of the most remarkable games in Championship history.

According to recent reports, neves had given the visitors the lead with a trademark free-kick, but Cardiff missed the chance to equalise in stoppage time when Gary Madine saw his effort saved by John Ruddy. Incredibly, the Bluebirds would be awarded another spot-kick, but Junior Hoilett hit the bar with the last kick of the game.

That prompted wild celebrations, with Cardiff boss Neil Warnock famously snubbing Nuno’s offer of a handshake after the game.

So, it’s a stadium that will hold many memories for all Wolves fans, and Neves is back in the Welsh capital with Portugal, as Jorge Jesus’ side prepare to take on the Dragons in the Nations League.

Inevitably, Neves was questioned about coming back to the Cardiff City Stadium, and he told BBC Wales that it remains one of the ‘craziest’ games he has ever been involved in.

In a recent development, “I remember (it) very well! It was a crazy match, one of the craziest matches in my life, and fortunately we won that match.

“I scored a free-kick, and then in the last minutes they had two penalties, they missed both penalties, and of course, after that there was a bit of confusion on the pitch because the emotions were high.

“But it was a fantastic match, and it’s a stadium that brings me good memories.”.

This is nice for Wolves fans to hear, as they still love Neves, and it will be great for them to see that he still looks back on his time at the club fondly, with all supporters hoping that he might one day return for a second spell at some point before he retires.

Obviously, the Cardiff game is one that will live long in the memory, and it’s no exaggeration to say that it was an ending to a match that you rarely ever see.

It was a huge disappointment for Cardiff at the time, but, to their credit, the Bluebirds did manage to put the defeat behind them, as they went up automatically with Wolves - and they did actually beat them at home in the top flight.
