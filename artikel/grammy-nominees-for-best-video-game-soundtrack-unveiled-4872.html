Grammy Nominees For Best Video Game Soundtrack Unveiled

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The nominees for this year's Grammy Awards have been announced, a ceremony that has handed out an award for best video game soundtrack since 2023. Five games have made the shortlist this year, with some of the biggest titles from the past 12 months making the shortlist for their respective scores.

Industry observers note that the five games with OSTs vying for a Grammy award this year are 007: First Light, Marvel's Wolverine, Ghost of Yotei, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora - From the Ashes, and Star Wars: Zero Company. The winner of the award won't be revealed until Sunday, February 7, 2027, when the annual Grammys ceremony takes place.

As is often the case when it comes to awards eligibility, it might seem a little odd to see a game like Ghost of Yotei on the list since it was released over a year ago. In fact, it will have been available for close to 18 months by the time the Grammys take place in February of next year.

Sources indicate that the 2027 Grammys eligibility period incorporates anything that was released between August 31, 2025, and August 28, 2026. Since Yotei was released in October 2025, it qualifies for a nomination. The same applies to Avatar's From The Ashes DLC, which was released in December 2025. It's actually odd that Wolverine has made the cut since that wasn't released until September 15, 2026, after this year's eligibility window had technically closed.

In a recent development, it's nominated nonetheless, which means four out of five of this year's Grammys video game OST nominees are from licensed games. In the five years that this award has existed, 14 out of 25 of the nominees have been licensed games, but only one licensed game has ever won it. That honor went to Star Wars Jedi: Survivor in 2024.

This will also be the third year in a row that Frontiers of Pandora has been nominated. Its base game was nominated in 2025, then its first DLC last year, and now its From The Ashes expansion.

Aside from Jedi: Survivor in 2024, other previous winners of the Grammy Award for best video game soundtrack include Sword of the Sea and Assassin's Creed Valhalla's Dawn of Ragnarok expansion. Those responsible for voting received heavy criticism last year after Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's soundtrack wasn't even nominated.
