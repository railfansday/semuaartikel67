According to recent reports, the fact that the characters of Netflix’s The Altruists feel like one significant family is what the computer science community would call a bug, not a feature. Based on real people and led by two true story mavens, Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle, the series documents how one of the many reasons the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange company FTX happened is that the employees were all friends, lovers, and emotionally tangled in ways that made it hard to do business ethically. That and the blatant fraud. As you get sucked into the series, and the many romances at the center of all that crime, let me provide you with a cheat sheet for the cast and characters of The Altruists.

Most of the actors on the Netflix series are young, because the people they are portraying were young when this all went down. The adult cast includes some pretty recognizable names, like Jennifer Grey (Dirty Dancing), as well as character actors like Robin Weigart (Deadwood), Paul Reiser (Mad About You), and William Mapother (Lost).
