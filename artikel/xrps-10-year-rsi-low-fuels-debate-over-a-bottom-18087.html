Analysts see possible recovery signals, but extreme oversold conditions don’t confirm a reversal: further deficit remains possible.

XRP’s daily relative strength index has fallen to its lowest reading since 2016, according to chart analysis cited by Matt, the cryptocurrency YouTuber presenting the upcoming Moon Lambo video episode.

His central argument: extreme selling pressure may signal an opportunity, even as XRP price trades below $1 and investors remain deeply fearful.

In the recording, dated November 17, 2026, Matt puts XRP just below $0.96 and Bitcoin at $64,571. He also cites a Crypto Fear and Greed Index reading of 12 out of 100, firmly in “extreme fear” territory. Those figures are presented in the video rather than independently verified market data.

The most striking figure comes from Crypto Insight UK: an XRP daily RSI of 16.17, described as its lowest in a decade. Matt acknowledges the limitation directly: “Just because we’re oversold doesn’t mean we can’t go lower.”.

The weekly chart offers a less straightforward signal. Citing analyst D Greg Crypto, Matt describes XRP’s weekly RSI falling below 20, recovering that level and then quickly testing it again—an unusually compressed sequence compared with previous recoveries.

Under that analysis, holding above 20 could support a potential double-bottom momentum structure. A sustained break below it would instead leave room for further capitulation. The distinction matters: an extreme indicator reading is not the same thing as a confirmed price bottom.

Matt also highlights Charting Guy’s view that XRP resembles a previous cycle’s bottoming process, with a weekly bullish divergence and a possible re-accumulation pattern. That analyst maintains a minimum xrp price target of $4.36, not a forecast for the eventual peak.

The timing is considerably less certain. Charting Guy suggests possible altcoin seasons in summer 2027 and late 2028, while Matt argues that Bitcoin’s direction will remain crucial for XRP and the wider market.

According to recent reports, matt’s own response is to keep holding rather than trade the volatility. He identifies himself as an enthusiast without a financial background, not a financial adviser.

For now, the consequential question is whether exhausted selling develops into sustained buying. Moon Lambo presents several arguments for recovery, but its own weekly-RSI discussion leaves a clear risk unresolved: XRP price could remain under pressure before any broader market rebound takes hold.
