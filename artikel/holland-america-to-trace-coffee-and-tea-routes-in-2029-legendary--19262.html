Holland America Line has unveiled a latest 27-day “Coffee, Tea & Spice Route” Legendary Voyage aboard the Eurodam in 2029.

The cruise exploring Asia’s historic coffee, tea and spice routes departs roundtrip from Singapore Jan. 3, 2029, exploring 13 ports across seven countries, each with a special connection to the theme of the voyage.

This ranges from Jakarta’s history of coffee commerce and the spice trade to Taiwan’s oolong teas, mountain-grown varieties and modern tea culture.

“For centuries, ships have sailed these storied routes, carrying flavors and traditions from Asia to the rest of the world. This voyage lets our guests follow that story back to its source and experience a part of the world that has influenced how people eat and drink for generations,” stated Paul Grigsby, vice president of itinerary planning and deployment for Holland America Line.

“In Manila, guests can discover Filipino coffee and culinary traditions alongside the city’s trading history, while Brunei adds Malay culinary traditions and regional spice influences to the journey. They can indulge in the flavors of the region while taking in the extraordinary cultures, landscapes and experiences that make Asia so fascinating to explore. That combination of a great story and an exceptional itinerary is what a Legendary Voyage is all about.”.

Working in with Art of Tea and Beukenhorst, producer of Holland America Line’s 1873 coffee, the cruise line is developing special programming that is set to include unique tea blends and select single-origin coffees.

The cruise will open for booking later this week.
