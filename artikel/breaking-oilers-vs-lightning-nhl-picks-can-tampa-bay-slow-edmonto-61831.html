Sources indicate that we get to see two of the great players of their generation face off tonight in Edmonton's Connor McDavid and Tampa Bay's Nikita Kucherov. McDavid has won just about every major NHL award in his career possible – even a Conn Smythe Trophy in 2024 as playoff MVP despite the Oilers losing in seven to the Florida Panthers in the Finals.

McDavid is the massive -400 favorite to win a fourth career Hart Trophy as regular-season MVP after getting two more points Tuesday in a 5-2 win at Florida. It was the Oilers' sixth straight win as they improved to an astonishing 10-0-1 on the road. That shootout loss came in New Jersey back on Nov. 3. Edmonton is only the fifth team in NHL history to record 10 road wins in a single season in 11 games or fewer. The last do to it was the 2023-24 Los Angeles Kings.

It's rare to see such a massive MVP favorite in November, but McDavid has a 23-game point streak to open the season, tied with Wayne Gretzky for the fourth-longest in NHL history. Gretzky (51 and 30), Mats Sundin (30) and Mario Lemieux (28) are the only players with longer streaks. McDavid leads the league by a mile with 55 points and has 28 in 11 road games. That 2.54-point-per-game road average has McDavid on pace for 106 road points, which would pass Gretzky (104 in 1985-86 and 1984-85) for the most in a single campaign.

Keep in mind that the NHL switched from an 82-game to 84-game regular season this year. Gretzky is the only player to have 200 points in a season and did it four times: 215 points in 1985-86, 212 in '81-82, 208 in '84-85 and 205 in '83-84. McDavid is on pace for 202.4 points. That he reaches 200, which I highly doubt, is +2200 and that he breaks Gretzky's mark of 215 is +10000.

Industry observers note that let's not forget about McDavid's unbelievable wingman Leon Draisaitl, who is second in the league with 44 points and tied for first with 21 goals. He had an assist Tuesday to extend a points streak to 10 games for the 12th time in his Hall of Fame career. That's tied with Peter Stastny for the second-most by a player born outside North America, behind Kucherov (13).

It has been revealed that mcDavid and Draisaitl can become the first set of teammates with 100 combined points through 24 games of a season since Penguins legends Lemieux and Jaromir Jagr (108) in 1995-96. McDavid is an absurd -425 for a point tonight, while Draisaitl is the game's +125 anytime goalscoring favorite.
