And while it might seem that these realistic simulacra would be poor fits for mobiles - requiring bags of computing power and complex input devices - they're actually proliferating on iOS.

Sources indicate that after all, the modern mobile packs a walloping great processor and a hugely versatile touchscreen. You just have to get the user interface right.

Thrifty Business is a cute and cosy simulator where you are running a thrift shop in a small town. You will spend your days purchasing small boxes of items from locals, stocking your shop, and hosting events inside your building. As you continue to manage this shop, you will also learn more about the townsfolk, what items they are looking for and why they are selling items. It's quite fun to manage the shop, get to know your community, and help them grow.

You will find many well-known football superstars who can be a part of your team. What separates this sim from others is attention to detail. You can spend hours looking for a player that will fit your squad perfectly, and then spend time trying to sign and train him. There are various team compositions and options, so with a bit of luck, your decisions will decide who wins the day.
