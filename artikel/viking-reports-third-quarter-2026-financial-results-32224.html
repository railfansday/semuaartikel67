Viking Holdings today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on bookings.

“We delivered strong third quarter revenue, supported by continued fleet expansion, the benefits of our diversified business model and growth in revenue per PCD,” remarked Leah Talactac, President and CEO of Viking.

“While prolonged periods of historically low water levels disrupted portions of our European river operations during the quarter, our nearly 30 years of experience operating on Europe’s waterways allowed us to adapt to changing conditions and minimize the impact on our guests. We are proud of and thankful for our crew and shoreside teams for their extraordinary efforts during this period. Importantly, we remain encouraged by the resilience of demand across our diverse portfolio of products and the long-term appeal of the one Viking brand.”.

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results.

During the third quarter of 2026, Capacity PCDs increased by 10.2% over the same period in 2025. This year-over-year increase was mainly driven by the growth of the Tech giant’s fleet. Occupancy for the third quarter of 2026 was 91.3% compared to 96.0% in 2025.

Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $2,184.2 million, an increase of $184.6 million, or 9.2%, compared to the same period in 2025 mainly driven by increased Capacity PCDs and higher revenue per PCD in 2026 compared to 2025, partially offset by lower Occupancy.

Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was $855.3 million, a decrease of $26.4 million, or 3.0%, compared to the same period in 2025, and Adjusted Gross Margin for the third quarter of 2026 was $1,388.1 million, an increase of $54.4 million, or 4.1%, over the same period in 2025. Net Yield was $612 for the third quarter of 2026, down 0.8% year-over-year.

For the third quarter of 2026, vessel operating expenses were $461.4 million and vessel operating expenses excluding fuel were $401.2 million. Compared to the same period in 2025, vessel operating expenses increased $69.2 million, or 17.6%, and vessel operating expenses excluding fuel increased $54.7 million, or 15.8%, mainly driven by the growth of the Company’s fleet in 2026 compared to 2025.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $498.4 million compared to $514.0 million for the same period in 2025. Adjusted Net Income attributable to Viking Holdings Ltd was $496.9 million compared to $533.8 million for the same period in 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA was $673.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $30.0 million, or 4.3%, over the same period in 2025.

Diluted EPS and Adjusted EPS were $1.11 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to Diluted EPS of $1.15 and Adjusted EPS of $1.20 for the same period in 2025.

Historically low water levels on certain European rivers impacted portions of the Company’s river operations during the third quarter of 2026. The Company implemented a range of mitigation measures to maintain operations and did not cancel any scheduled cruises. However, the low water conditions resulted in itinerary disruptions and increased guest cancellations, which reduced Occupancy compared to the third quarter of 2025. The low water disruptions reduced third quarter total revenue by approximately $100 million. The Company estimates that the impact on Adjusted Gross Margin was also approximately $100 million, reflecting the lost margin from guest cancellations as well as incremental direct costs incurred to mitigate the disruptions.

Enhancement on Operating Capacity and Bookings.

For our Core Products, operating capacity is 7% higher for the 2026 season compared to the 2025 season and 15% higher for the 2027 season compared to the 2026 season.

As of November 8, 2026, for our Core Products, we had sold 93% of our Capacity PCDs for the 2026 season and 68% for the 2027 season.

As of November 8, 2026, for our Core Products, we had $6,200 million of Advance Bookings for the 2026 season, 11% higher than the 2025 season at the same point in time. Since our last earnings release, Advance Bookings for the 2026 season declined primarily reflecting guest cancellations related to itineraries disrupted by low water levels. Advance Bookings per PCD for the 2026 season was $830, 6% higher than the 2025 season at the same point in time.

Recent findings suggest that as of November 8, 2026, for our Core Products, we had $5,829 million of Advance Bookings for the 2027 season, 17% higher than the 2026 season at the same point in time. Advance Bookings per PCD for the 2027 season was $929, 8% higher than the 2026 season at the same point in time.

The Company issued vouchers as a goodwill gesture to certain guests who traveled on itineraries impacted by low water levels. Advance Bookings include redemptions of vouchers. When a voucher is redeemed, the latest booking is reflected in Advance Bookings at net value.

Recent findings suggest that “Our Advance Bookings remain strong, with 68% of our capacity for the 2027 season already sold, providing meaningful visibility into future demand,” remarked Linh Banh, Chief Financial Officer of Viking. “The enduring appeal of the Viking brand and our products, combined with our diversified business model, differentiated marketing capabilities and strong guest loyalty, continue to support our growth and reinforce our confidence in the long-term opportunities across our business.”.

On September 10, 2026, the Manufacturer announced that its Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program of up to $1.0 billion. During the third quarter of 2026, the Company returned $350.0 million to shareholders through share repurchases.
