In a recent development, during a Panel Talk at Fan Expo Chicago in August 2026, Scott Snyder teased that Issues Absolute Batman #26 and Absolute Batman #27 would act as transitional stories. Snyder had further added that they will focus mainly on Barbara Gordon's “Year One”-style story about her life as the Bat and another story involving Joker using Clayface to corrupt Batman's public image. The story corrupting Batman’s public image now seems to be a bit more branched out after Snyder’s latest X (formerly Twitter) post.

In a recent development, the exact story of Absolute Batman #26-#27 is still not fully clear, and it will likely focus on what Snyder noted, including Joker using Clayface to corrupt Batman’s image. But now Snyder has posted a cover photo for The Demon Child Part One, which hints at a more diverse storyline in the future that goes beyond Joker trying to affect Batman’s image conventionally. The picture demonstrates a baby with Batman’s mask on its head and, considering that it is titled Absolute Batman: The Demon Child, there’s likely some other diss-Batman plot here.

Industry observers note that the explosive ending for Absolute Batman #25 confirms that Issue #27 will begin The Demon Child arc with Scott Snyder and regular artist Nick Dragotta reunited in February 2027. Snyder had already described #27 as the start of a “really, really crucial arc.” The other important part is that Snyder is clearly teasing that this won’t be an isolated issue. The Demon Child Part One framing is explicitly the beginning of a multi-issue storyline.

Four teams, four completely different reasons to put yourself in danger for strangers. This is the roster you actually belong on, what they would want from you, and what it would cost you to stay.

Recent findings suggest that you belong where the stakes are largest and the personalities are loudest. This is a team of people who are all used to being the most key person in the room, which is exactly why it works and exactly why it keeps nearly falling apart.

According to recent reports, you have never had the luxury of being thanked for this. The X-Men protect a world that has never once been comfortable with them existing, and something in you understands exactly why they keep doing it anyway.

You do not want colleagues, you want your people. The Titans are the team where the friendship came first and the world-saving turned up afterwards as an inconvenient side effect.

Sources indicate that you are not asking who to trust. You worked out the answer a while ago and it was nobody in a cape. This is where you go once you have decided the people in charge need watching, and that nobody else is going to do it.

According to recent reports, snyder’s teaser immediately sent readers toward the two darkest possibilities in the replies section. One fan pleaded, “Please don’t let it be Joker’s baby.” This is an understandable fear given the repeated “HAHA” imagery behind the child and Absolute Batman #25 positioning Selina’s pregnancy immediately after Joker lets Bruce escape. There is, however, currently no evidence that Joker is the father. At the same time, the framing strongly suggests he may have aims for the child.

In a recent development, another theory has considerably more continuity behind it: Bruce and Selina last slept together in Absolute Batman #13, when Bruce still had Venom coursing through his body after Ark M. That has fans wondering whether The Demon Child could somehow be Venom-affected, although the series has never established that Venom can alter a pregnancy or be inherited. For now, even the child’s identity remains deliberately unresolved.

Absolute Batman: The Demon Child Part One will arrive sometime in 2027. The exact release date is currently not yet clear. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
