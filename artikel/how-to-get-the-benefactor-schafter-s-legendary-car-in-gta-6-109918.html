How To Get The Benefactor Schafter S Legendary Car In GTA 6

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There are a lot of secrets and collectibles hidden throughout Grand Theft Auto 6. These can range from simple easter eggs to comically elaborate puzzles you'll need to solve. However, some just require a bit of criminal activity to earn some great rewards.

Two of these are the Guardia Brothers FRA legendary assault rifle and Benefactor sports car, both of which are obtainable at the same location! If you're looking to snag a few grand, a newly introduced car, and a weapon, here is where to find them and how to get these legendary items.

According to recent reports, if you want to get this legendary assault rifle and sports car, you will need to first head to Vice City and then go to the Little Cuba district. In the northeast corner of Little Cuba there will be a gated house that a black van will pull into if you get close. This doesn't always happen, and your ability to rob this area isn't determined by the van showing up, so don't fret.

While you can try to just bust through the front gate, you can actually sneak in over the wall. Head to the right of the gate along the spiked wall (shown above) until you reach a section near the back that can be climbed over. I strongly recommend doing this, as you can basically eliminate every gang member without raising an alarm so long as you have a silenced weapon.

Sources indicate that once you reach the backyard, ignore the legendary Benefactor car for now and head up the scaffolding along the side of the building to the roof. Climb through the open hatch and equip your silenced weapon. Below you will be four gang members sitting around a table on the ground floor. Additionally, there is one other criminal rummaging through a safe on the second floor.

Industry observers note that take out your silenced weapon, activate Focus mode, and eliminate all four criminals on the first floor before they can react. If you're fast enough, all of them can be killed without alerting anyone outside or even the guy looking in the safe! With the four gang members dead, use your Focus and look at the floor by the ladder.

This will reveal a secret stash area that has some cash you can loot. There is also a high-end watch on the table to your left along with some Sticky Bombs, which you will need to escape. When you're ready, head down to the second floor and knock out the guy putting money into the safe. Take all the cash and go to the bottom floor.

It has been revealed that there you will find a mix of money, marked bills, and jewelry. Stuff your bag full of valuables, loot the bodies, and look for a small armory to the right of the front entrance. In a weapon case, you will find the Guardian Brothers FMA assault rifle. Once you've thoroughly ransacked the place, take everything out the back door to where the Benefactor is.

Store your items in the truck of the car, take out your silenced weapon, and kill the guard who is standing along the driveway near the gate. With him dead, approach the gate at the end of the driveway, attach a sticky bomb to the side when prompted, and shoot the two guards on the other side of the gate. Blow open the gate, then run up to the switch to open the main gate so you can drive out (circled above).

This should allow you to escape without triggering a single gunfight or having the police called on you. Now you can take the Benefactor and get it registered at someone like Moses for a little over $3,800. This ensures you can call the car anytime you need it, making it yours for the rest of the game.
