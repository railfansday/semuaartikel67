Nvidia (NVDA -14.01%) has been in the news for the wrong reasons lately. However, the tech giant put any concerns about its growth trajectory to rest following the release of its fiscal third-quarter results (for the three months ended Oct. 25) on Nov. 17.

The semiconductor giant didn't just crush Wall Street's expectations, but it also delivered stronger-than-expected guidance. Let's take a closer look at Nvidia's results and guidance to see whether this semiconductor stock can regain its mojo and jump higher over the coming year.

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang recently resigned as CEO under unceremonious circumstances. CFO Colette Kress now heads the manufacturer in an interim capacity, while the search for a permanent CEO continues.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

The company's solid results should assuage investors' concerns about its future during this uncertain time as it undergoes a transition. Nvidia's revenue shot up by 103% from the year-ago period to $115.8 billion in fiscal Q3, exceeding the $109.1 billion consensus estimate. Its non-GAAP earnings per share increased by 111% year over year to $2.68, beating the $2.48 estimate.

Recent findings suggest that the guidance clearly suggests that the recent developments at Nvidia won't be hurting its dominance in the artificial intelligence (AI) chip market. The tech giant anticipates $130 billion in revenue for the current quarter, and this estimate excludes sales to Chinese customers. Wall Street would have settled for $125.1 billion in guidance.

According to recent reports, nvidia's guidance implies a 91% year-over-year increase in revenue. That's stronger than the 73% revenue increase the organization reported in the same quarter last year. What's more, Kress noted on the latest earnings call that Nvidia's revenue will increase by 75% in fiscal 2028 (which begins in late January next year).

The company had previously anticipated a 70% increase in fiscal 2028 revenue, though it noted that the outlook was supply-constrained. It now expects stronger growth owing to rising demand for its chips and the company's ability to secure additional supply.

Sources indicate that even better, Nvidia sees an improvement in its margin profile in fiscal 2028. Kress pointed out that the company's gross margin will bottom in the current quarter and improve to the 72%-73% range in the next fiscal year. The company expects a non-GAAP gross margin of 72% in the current quarter, indicating it can deliver healthy earnings growth next year on the back of a strong top-line jump.

It has been revealed that the fiscal 2028 guidance was the icing on the cake for Nvidia, as investor confidence would have taken a big hit had it scrapped the same. However, the better-than-expected guidance indicates that the company's growth story remains intact amid the ongoing transition. Also, a potential margin improvement should help Nvidia deliver strong earnings growth next year, which could send the stock soaring.

Nvidia stock has performed poorly on the market this year, and recent developments at the organization have cast a cloud of negativity over it. However, the latest results and guidance should bring the stock out of the rut it is in.

According to recent reports, nvidia has generated $293.7 billion in revenue in the first nine months of fiscal 2027. Its fiscal Q4 guidance indicates that it will end the year with almost $424 billion in revenue. That implies a 96% jump over its fiscal 2026 revenue. The company's top line could reach $742 billion next year, based on the 75% jump it is forecasting for fiscal 2028.

Recent findings suggest that nvidia trades at 10 times sales, a premium to the U.S. technology sector's price-to-sales ratio of 6. However, its outstanding growth justifies that premium. Assuming Nvidia trades at even 8 times sales at the end of fiscal 2028 and achieves $742 billion in revenue, its market cap could reach almost $6 trillion. That's 60% higher than its current market cap, suggesting that the stock could deliver respectable gains over the coming year.

So, buying this AI stock after its latest results could be a smart move, as 2027 could be a much better year for Nvidia.
