It has been revealed that shiba Inu (SHIB) met Thanksgiving Day with a "Bull" label on its four-hour price chart. The label appeared on the relative strength index (RSI) of the SHIB/USD chart on TradingView, and on Thursday afternoon SHIB was trading at around $0.00000364, up 1.4% on the day.

Sources indicate that on Saturday, SHIB fell as low as $0.00000337, according to TradingView. It was the lowest price of SHIB since 2021, and before that, the lowest price of SHIB in 2026 was $0.00000411 at the July 17 close. On Wednesday, the token dropped close to the Saturday low again. According to the chart, the RSI was around 23 on Saturday and around 32 on Wednesday, and the "Bull" label appeared next to the Wednesday reading.

The Crypto Fear and Greed Index on CoinMarketCap showed 13 on Wednesday, up from 10 on Tuesday.

It has been revealed that the four-hour SHIB chart also demonstrated the label on Oct. 27 and Nov. 5. It appeared twice more between Nov. 17 and Nov. 19, and two days later SHIB fell to its Saturday low.

The daily SHIB chart had the same label in late June and in July. On Thursday, there was no upcoming label on the daily chart.

SHIB has lost 22.7% in November so far, according to CryptoRank data as of Nov. 22. The token also lost 29.6% and 24.8% in November of 2021 and 2022, respectively. In November of 2023 and 2024, SHIB gained 6.62% and 48.8%, and last November it fell 16.2%.

SHIB's median December return is -17.6%, and its average December return is -11.4%, according to CryptoRank data. SHIB gained 24.6% in December 2023 and lost 21% and 17.6% in December of 2024 and 2025, respectively. In 2026, SHIB has lost value in six of 11 months so far. In July, August and September, the token gained 12.2%, 7.55% and 13.5%, respectively. SHIB lost 24% in June and 18.6% in October.

On Jan. 1, SHIB closed at $0.00000692, according to CoinGecko. SHIB set its record high of $0.00008845 on Oct. 28, 2021, and in December of that year it lost 29.5%, according to CryptoRank. In January 2022, the token lost another 36%.

The number of SHIB holders on Ethereum passed 1.7 million on Nov. 17, according to Etherscan. CryptoQuant data from that day showed about 312 billion SHIB leaving exchanges.

According to recent reports, on Thursday, SHIB was No. 34 on CoinMarketCap with a market capitalization of $2.14 billion. Its circulating supply stood at 589.23 trillion SHIB. Bittensor (TAO) was one place higher, and its market capitalization was also $2.14 billion. Hedera (HBAR) was No. 31 at $2.37 billion, and the PayPal USD (PYUSD) stablecoin held the 32nd spot between them.
