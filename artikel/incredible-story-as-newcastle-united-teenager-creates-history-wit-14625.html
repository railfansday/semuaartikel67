Newcastle United teenager Matheos Ferreira has made history in the colours of Brazil.

The exciting 18-year-old winger got his first ever call up for his country this month.

Matheos Ferreira named in the Brazil Under 20s squad for a double header with Peru.

He was an unused sub on Friday when the young Brazil stars won 2-0.

Then on Monday he made history, Matheos Ferreira becoming the first player developed at the Newcastle United Academy to go on to represent Brazil at international level.

The 18-year-old is from Berwick but is eligible to represent the country through his Brazilian father, Thiago.

Not only did Matheos Ferreira make his first ever start for the Brazil Under 20s on Monday, it only took him 14 minutes to score!

The United winger putting Brazil into the lead against Peru Under 20s in Belem.

Matheos Ferreira seeing his dream international debut get even better when he also supplied an assist as the Brazil youngsters routed the opposition and won 9-0 against Peru.

Sources indicate that the Newcastle United Academy star talked to official NUFC club media from Brazil ahead of his debut: “I had to start training as soon as I got there but I think it’s shown how far I have come as a player because I wasn’t too nervous or anxious… I am doing the thing I love the most, playing football, and it was nice to get on the pitch and train with the squad. It’s been busy! The culture is completely different here but I really like it. We’re having to train in the evening as it is usually over 30 degrees during the day. Since the first training session, I’ve felt very welcome by the lads and the coaches as well.”.

Matheos Ferreira also qualifies to play potentially for England and Italy (his mam’s Italian).

The winger joined the Newcastle United Academy in 2020 after playing for Berwick Juniors and Cramlington Juniors and recalled the moment he found out he’d got his first Brazil call up: “My first reaction was just pure joy. I was so happy. My parents wanted to tell me first before I checked my phone but it was still a great moment to share with them, who were both very emotional. I think it’s a reward for how I’ve been playing but also how I’ve managed to conduct myself off the pitch with my recovery, willingness to learn and reflecting on how I can improve on the matches I’ve played in. It’s an amazing achievement but I don’t want this to be the last. I will keep pushing on and try to get that next call-up or progress into the first-team. Once I reach one level, it’s about getting to the next one. It doesn’t stop there. We’ve got the South American (Youth Football) Championship in January and the Under-20 World Cup is next year so I’m pushing really hard to try and get into both squads. I’m doing everything I can to try and make my international debut. It would mean a lot. I haven’t got this substantial pressure of needing to do well – I just want to get on that pitch, show what I can do and do what I love the most.”.

Matheos Ferreira remembering: “My first memory of Brazilian football would be my dad showing videos of Ronaldinho, R9 and Neymar,” he added. “The first game I watched live was at Wembley between England and Brazil and Neymar played. I think it finished goalless though! The interest they revealed me and the project they have was a substantial reason [to accept the call-up]. Brazil is a big country so to be recognised by them is amazing. It’s a big opportunity and an honour to represent them.”.

It has been revealed that the 18-year-old rising Newcastle United star is buzzing with how the 2026-27 season has kicked off, for club and country: “I’ve really enjoyed the start to this season. The coaches trust me as I’ve been playing in different positions for the Under-21s; wing-back, as a winger or a number ten. I think they know what I can provide for the team. I think its demonstrates the qualities I have. Playing as a right wing-back shows I can defend, put my body on the line and then it’s about showing my strengths going forward. I’ve embraced the challenges of switching positions, thinking about the tactical side of the game.”.

A brilliant moment on Monday for Matheos Ferreira and here’s to many more appearances (and goals!) in the colours of Brazil AND Newcastle United.
