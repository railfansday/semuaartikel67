Reactions to ICJ ruling underline global divisions over court with China raising concerns about judicial overreach.

The US has greeted with caution the world’s highest court’s ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against its Rohingya minority, while China has raised concerns of judicial overreach.

Sources indicate that reactions to Thursday’s historic ruling underlined deep international division over the role of the court and similar institutions. The international court of justice (ICJ) had never previously found a state responsible for genocide and the decision could set a precedent for future genocide claims, notably a case against Israel.
