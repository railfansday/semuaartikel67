Recent findings suggest that who indicates international truce often means housekeeping… And on the Manchester United side, after a catastrophic start to the season, Michael Carrick left. After a particularly good second part of the 2025/2026 season, having taken over from Ruben Amorim and qualifying the Red Devils for the Champions League, the former midfielder obtained catastrophic results at the start of the year. The Mancuniens are thus in a sad eighteenth place in the Premier League standings and they quickly paid the price.

Sources indicate that very rapidly, the name of Mauricio Pochettino appeared as the main candidate to replace him, ahead of other names like Eddie Howe or Julian Nagelsmann. The North American coach maintains a superb rating across the Channel, where he had obtained excellent results with Southampton, then at Tottenham between 2014 and 2019, including a Champions League final. If his return to London with Chelsea during the 2023/2024 season did not necessarily go very well, the former PSG coach remains considered a top coach by our English neighbors.

It was therefore quite natural that Manchester United turned to him, while the Argentinian had also had the ambition, for a long time, to coach the Red Devils one day. The Mancunians confirmed his arrival until 2029 via an official press release this Friday: “Mauricio Pochettino has agreed to take charge of the Manchester United men’s team from November 18. The club has reached an agreement with the United States Soccer Federation so that Mauricio and his staff can be released from their duties once their assignments with the United States men’s national team are completed. ».

Note that they had to pay around nine million euros to snatch him from the United States selection, where he was under contract until 2030. The same for a good part of his staff. Pochettino will have a few days to discover his players – even if many are with their selection – and try to familiarize them with his methods before a huge meeting after the break: a shock against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday November 22. Baptism of fire for Pochettino therefore….
