President Donald Trump promised to put a “tremendous tax” on companies that build data centers outside of the US, even as states and cities around the country are placing restrictions on new applications to build them.

“We’re not going to allow our companies to build data centers outside of our country,” Trump said at a press conference Friday following his summit with China President Xi Jinping and Russia President Vladimir Putin. “They allowed that to happen with chips and everything else, and it was very destructive to our country. It’s where we started going behind.” Should the US allow data centers to be built outside of the country, Trump warned, “China will take them all.”.

In a recent development, trump didn’t share many details behind the initiative, and apparently was floating the proposal publicly for the first time. “I don’t know if that’s a breaking story. But I haven’t even remarked that to my people,” Trump said, flanked by Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio. “But I’m telling them now. They’re here. Many of them are here.”.

In a recent development, trump’s promise could be difficult to fulfill with a fresh Democratic majority Congress being sworn in within a couple of months. Even members of his own party recognize that the data center buildout has become an unpopular problem across the board. With states installing moratoriums and new restrictions on data center proposals, companies may be forced to look outside of the country, but Trump’s threat is an attempt to make that a less attractive alternative.
