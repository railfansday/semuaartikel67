Mauricio Pochettino could be backed to spend massive in January as he eyes a major overhaul of his Manchester United squad.

The 54-year-old was appointed as the new permanent manager last week, replacing Michael Carrick, and has a massive job on his hand to turn around a miserable start to the season.

There is now a belief that he will need backing from the board if he is to arrest this recent run of form, with key areas of the squad seen as major weaknesses by the new boss.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Pochettino has already run the rule over his squad to identify ares that need investment in January.

Talks have been held with the board about potential backing, and while they had no initial plans to splash the cash mid-season, they may be forced to back the manager.

Defensive issues have been the major talking point at Old Trafford, while there are doubts about their attacking depth and the quality of midfielders brought in during the summer.

Brown told Football Insider on 16 November that Pochettino is already being set up to fail at Man United with the current hierarchy unlikely to bring success.

However, they can give the manager the best possible chance if they back him in the January transfer window, with major changes needed in this Man United squad.

They are currently inside the Premier League's bottom three after ten games, and while the situation is not unsalvagable, investment will be needed in key areas.

"Mauricio Pochettino is going to want to spend major in January to improve his squad," Brown told Football Insider.

"It will have been a factor when he was appointed, he's looked at the squad, assessed what he might need and which areas he will want to improve in when he can.

"That will have been discussed with the board as well, and over the next few weeks when he takes charge, it will become even more clear where the weaknesses are.

"You can point the finger at previous managers all you like, but there are parts of this team that are not good enough, and that has to change if they want to be successful.

"Pochettino will have strong ideas of which players he wants to bring in and which players he wants out, but it's on the board now to support him to put those plans in place.

In a recent development, "They can't just sit back and hope he'll pull off a miracle, they need to be atively helping him to be a success. I have my doubts about whether they're willing to do that.".

Pochettino will already have an idea of which players he wants to bring in, and in which positions, with the weaknesses in his Man United squad having been made clear.

A defensive overhaul is widely accepted to be the priority at Old Trafford, as it was under Carrick, with the addition of new centre-backs and full-backs firmly on the cards.

As a result, Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Pochettino could revisit one of his old clubs, Chelsea, in pursuit of a mid-season deal to sign Malo Gusto.

The France defender was on United's radar during the summer transfer window when they missed out on a late deal as Chelsea opted to block his departure.

However, after a slow start to the season for Gusto, he could be allowed to leave Stamford Bridge and reuinite with his former manager, who is believed to be a big fan.
