Travis Barker is thankful for all the small things he shares with Rocky Barker.

Especially as the Blink-182 drummer revealed that he and wife Kourtney Kardashian’s 3-year-old son share the exact same taste in Vans sneakers.

“We have a version of my black Dues Paid and a version of my white ones that are all studded out and he has ‘em too,” Barker shared in a recent interview with Complex. “And it’s his go-tos.”.

And Rocky has made no secret of his penchant for his dad’s style of shoe.

“You ask him to pick any shoe in his closet—mind you, he’s 3—but he’ll say the spiked ones. ‘I want the spiked ones,’” Barker added. “And everywhere we go, people are like, ‘Oh look at his shoes.’”.

The 51-year-old—who is stepdad to Atiana de la Hoya, 27, and shares Landon Barker, 22, and Alabama Barker, 20, with ex Shanna Moakler—quipped that the studded shoes are definitely, “so hard. It’s so tough.”.

And the similarities between Barker and Rocky don’t stop at their shoes. Rocky also loves being the toddler at a rock show, as Kardashian shared a video of her son rocking out to Blink-182 during a February concert in Arizona.

In the clip posted to Instagram, Rocky could be seen bopping along backstage as Barker played the drums in the distance.

And it’s certainly a special time for Rocky, who celebrated his third birthday earlier this month with an epic Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles birthday party.

Kardashian—also mom to kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, and Reign, 11, with ex Scott Disick—gave fans a look at the most excellent celebrations, posting a video to her Instagram Stories as the family made their way through an elaborate “sewer” that had green slime oozing all around before opening up to the backyard.

It has been revealed that the Poosh founder also shared a heartwarming birthday tribute to Rocky on Instagram Nov. 12, posting two photos of the tiny tot as a newborn baby.

It has been revealed that she captioned the sweet snaps, “Heaven blew every trumpet and played every horn on the wonderful, marvelous night you were born. 11.1.23.”.
