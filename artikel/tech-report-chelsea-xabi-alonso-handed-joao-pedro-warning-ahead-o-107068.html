Xabi Alonso has been warned that Joao Pedro’s return from injury may not be the silver bullet needed to resurrect Chelsea’s form.

The Spaniard’s side have won just one of their last five matches in all competitions, slumping to a 4-1 home collapse against Leeds United last time out.

Injuries have hamstrung the Blues, with captain Reece James and £115m man Moises Caicedo among those sharing the treatment room with Pedro, but Alonso’s issues cut deeper.

Chelsea’s alarming dip in form appeared to coincide with the absences of Joao Pedro, but former Chelsea star Joe Cole said that the Brazilian’s return will not outweigh the Blues’ calamitous defending.

He said: “The team is not playing well, with Joao Pedro coming back in will help, but the whole of the set-up at Chelsea needs to be better.

“The players need to step up and perform, and they need to stop conceding goals. I think that's the crucial thing with Chelsea, they need to stop conceding goals.”.

The Brazilian striker has scored 20 Premier League goals since joining the club last season, but a thigh injury sustained in training last month has left him sidelined, and he is not expected to return until the new year.

In a record worse than all but four top-flight sides, the Blues’ backline has shipped 20 goals so far this term, almost double the tally conceded at this stage of last season.

Cole, who lifted three league titles with Chelsea during a seven-year stint in West London, also weighed in on rumours linking Bournemouth’s Alex Scott to a sensational move.

Standard Sport reported last month that Scott is available for sale for an offer in excess of £100m, with Alonso’s Chelsea weighing up a January move.

Cole, appearing on the Dressing Room podcast sponsored by BOYLE Sports, said: “Alex Scott is a great player, he's going to be linked. Chelsea are linked with players like that at the moment, we're buying players from Bournemouth rather than taking players from around Real Madrid or Barcelona.
