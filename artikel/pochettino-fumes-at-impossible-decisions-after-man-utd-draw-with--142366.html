Pochettino fumes at ‘impossible’ decisions after Man Utd draw with Sporting

Mauricio Pochettino was left frustrated by several refereeing decisions during Manchester United’s 2-2 draw with Sporting Lisbon, insisting his side were punished in key moments of the game.

It has been revealed that manchester United had to settle for a point in Portugal after a chaotic evening away from home. Bruno Fernandes scored twice, but Luis Suarez also found the net on two occasions to deny Pochettino his first win as United head coach.

According to recent reports, referee Szymon Marciniak came under scrutiny for a series of controversial decisions. He ruled out an Amad Diallo goal after penalising Youri Tielemans for a foul in the build-up, blowing his whistle before the ball crossed the line. That meant VAR could not intervene on the goal and it was not given.

It has been revealed that the Polish official also awarded Sporting a first-half penalty and disallowed what would have been a late United winner. He blew whistle on Patrick Dorgu for pushing goalkeeper Kaique Pereira, disallowing the goal.

Pochettino did not hide his frustration when asked about the decisions after the match.

“I think your first question is difficult to answer because I think everyone knows all that was, it was impossible,” he remarked after the game.

“You know, it’s difficult to understand why that happened on the field. It’s difficult to find a game where you get punished [so much] in one game. We were punished a lot in key actions.”.

United also conceded an 80th-minute equaliser after Senne Lammens failed to hold a cross, allowing Suarez to score from close range.

However, Pochettino was keen to defend his goalkeeper rather than blame him for the mistake.

“I think, please, it’s to blame us,” the United boss stated.

He acknowledged that his side needed to manage such situations better.

“But of course, we need to accept, we accept the challenge and we are here to try to improve, you know, the small details that for sure we will improve.”.

Pochettino’s frustration was understandable after two similar incidents of bad refereeing in his first two games at United. Leny Yoro’s red card against Liverpool was later overturned on appeal, while a series of errors against Sporting cost him yet another victory. But despite the errors, Pochettino also recognises that his players need to improve their decision-making during games.
