Soon, your alarms, timers, and phone calls will be less disruptive on iPhone Duo.

Sources indicate that with today’s iOS 27.3 developer beta 2, iPhone Duo gained a fresh feature: Unlock with Front Camera. This lets you unlock your iPhone Duo in a similar fashion to Face ID, but using the front selfie camera, rather than Face ID’s TrueDepth 3D camera system. But, Face ID is more than just unlocking: it also provides Attention Aware features.

Attention Aware features, which were first introduced with iPhone X, check for when you’re looking at the phone before performing certain actions:.

iPhone will check for attention before dimming the display, expanding a notification when locked, or lowering the volume of some alerts.

Recent findings suggest that previously, Attention Aware features required Face ID, but iOS 27.3 extends it to iPhone Duo. Like the new face unlock feature, Attention Aware features only work when using the outer display. However, the face unlock feature does not have to be set up to use the Attention Aware features.

In a recent development, after updating to iOS 27.3, the features should be automatically enabled. You can enable or disable the feature in Settings → Accessibility → Face Recognition & Attention. You can also enable Haptic on Successful Unlock there, just like an iPhone with Face ID.

In my experience, Attention Aware features will lower the volume of alarms, timers, and incoming calls when your iPhone is already unlocked. It also will prevent the display from dimming and locking while you are actively looking at it. Finally, it will expand and show notification content on the Lock Screen after unlocking with the newly introduced face unlock feature.
