By KIERAN GILL, MAIL SPORT REPORTER.

Published: 05:36 EST, 19 November 2026 | Updated: 05:52 EST, 19 November 2026.

A poll taken by Daily Mail Sport led to almost 2,000 votes from Chelsea fans, and 57.9 per cent were in favour of Mike Penders keeping his place over Emiliano Martinez.

One is a 21-year-old Belgian yet to appear for his country and still in the early stages of what will hopefully be a highly promising career. The other is a 34-year-old Argentinian who won the 2022 World Cup and has appeared 234 times in the Premier League.

We mention those brief backgrounds because when assessing who will give him the greater chance of stopping Leeds United from scoring on Saturday, Xabi Alonso takes them into account. Indeed, Alonso told us so while covering Chelsea’s pre-season tour.

One conversation with the manager was centred on his goalkeeper options. At that time, they were Penders and Robert Sanchez.

Alonso praised Penders’ potential but explained to us how highly he values ‘experience’ in his goalkeeper. ‘To have this security in goal is vital,’ he insisted when asked.

Sources indicate that sanchez was indeed nominated as his No 1 for their first fixture of the Premier League season at Fulham. The 29-year-old Spaniard had a stinker, which led to him being loaned to Como. It is a straightforward loan, and he has been second-choice to Jean Butez under Cesc Fabregas, so there is a chance he will be back at Chelsea next summer, but that is a problem for another day.

A Daily Mail Sport poll of almost 2,000 Chelsea fans resulted in 57.9 per cent voting in favour of Mike Penders (above) keeping his place over Emiliano Martinez.

Martinez is available for the first time since breaking his jaw at Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Martinez arrived from Aston Villa for £7.5million, was told he would be Chelsea’s first-choice goalkeeper, and is now available again. He is able to play without a protective mask, as he did while representing Argentina in their 7-0 friendly win over Costa Rica on Tuesday night.

It was his first appearance since breaking his jaw at Everton and having surgery exactly a month prior. He headed back to London after that victory to return to Chelsea, presumably under the impression that he will be playing against Leeds come Saturday.

It is Alonso who will have the ultimate say on who goes in goal, in conjunction with his head of goalkeeping, Ben Roberts, and assistant goalkeeping coach, James Russell. That decision will be communicated with his chosen goalkeeper in the build-up, though Alonso is unlikely to tell us when we ask him at his pre-match press conference on Friday afternoon.

One insider consulted this week mentioned his suspicion is the Argentinian will make an immediate return because it would create a headache for Alonso. Martinez is a big character, volatile, and, if reduced to second choice, would not be pleased.

Statistically, Leeds cross the ball more than most in the Premier League, which could be more challenging for Penders than Martinez.

Filling in for Martinez, Penders punched the ball into his own goal on his first league start for Chelsea in a 2-0 loss to Tottenham Hotspur.

In a recent development, however, he impressed against Liverpool in the Carabao Cup, then Manchester United and Sunderland in the Premier League. He won the Save of the Month award for October for denying Bryan Mbeumo at Stamford Bridge.

Penders won October's Save of the Month award for denying Man United's Bryan Mbeumo.

Penders is clearly a terrific talent, and it would be tempting to continue to show trust in him by having him face Leeds. Chelsea then face Nottingham Forest, Crystal Palace, Liverpool and Manchester City in a rush of fixtures as we head into December.

It is a substantial decision to be made by Alonso, and would take bravery for him to stick with his prodigy in Penders, when we know the experience of the proven Martinez weighs heavy in his mind.

Chelsea vice captain Moises Caicedo is to be checked over at Cobham after a small injury scare while the influential midfielder was away with Ecuador.

Industry observers note that caicedo would be expected to wear the armband at Leeds in Reece James’ ongoing absence. He is set to return to the training ground on Thursday to be assessed.

The 25-year-old played 84 minutes in Ecuador’s first fixture of the break, a 2-0 victory against Costa Rica in which he scored. However, he then asked to be substituted at half-time of their 2-0 win over Bolivia, which ended in the early hours of Wednesday morning. Captaining his country, it was believed to be due to a small physical complaint.

Meanwhile, Chelsea have been looking at the midfield market in the build-up to this January after they missed out on bolstering their options in the summer when Enzo Fernandez was sold to Manchester City. They retain an interest in Bournemouth's Alex Scott.
