Alex Bruce was “immensely proud” of his Rotherham United players after their goalless draw with Grimsby Town.

That pride, however, was mixed with anguish over certain refereeing decisions as the sides shared the points at Blundell Park.

Both goalkeepers made some crucial saves and the woodwork came to the Millers’ rescue in the first half as they picked up just their fifth away point of the campaign.

He said: “I was immensely proud of the team, proud of everyone involved, to be honest. Seeing the numbers we had behind the goal from up in the stands, and the connection between the players and supporters after a fighting display, made me proud.

“If we are going to start winning games and climbing that table, then we do need to first show the fight and the spirit that we showed today.

“I have to be careful [speaking about officiating] because I keep getting in trouble. I’ll let people make their own judgement but I’ve seen Paul Mullin head in a goal without even making contact with their goalkeeper.

“The contact comes after he has made the header, so that is a clear goal for us that should stand.

“I get a bit of stick for showing my grievances towards officials and decisions made, but I don’t want to have to point these things out, so I will let those who have watched make their own minds up.”.

Grimsby boss David Artell, meanwhile, noted: “I thought it was a competitive game, an entertaining one for a 0-0.

“Both teams could have easily come away with the win, but without speaking for Alex, he is probably a bit happier than we are with the result.

“We stay on an unbeaten run, but we don’t sugarcoat things because my job is to win, and we didn’t do quite enough today.

“There are mitigating factors, the number of injuries we have being one, but I always want to focus on the team I have out there.

“Against the ball, I thought we were very good against a tough team, a team that were in League One last season.

“They (Rotherham) are very competitive physically, with how many duels they win, but our lads stuck at it, and that’s how we got the clean sheet, without quite having the quality or right decisions in the final third to win.”.
