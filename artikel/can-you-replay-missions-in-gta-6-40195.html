Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that want to know something a little surprising about this long-awaited trip to Leonida? Rockstar hasn't put any options in the game to replay missions in GTA 6. That's right, there is no mission replay and it's unlikely that it'll be added in later either.

With over 90 main story missions in GTA 6, some of which are an absolute riot of a time while others feature pretty substantial branching paths that help shape your GTA 6 Criminal Profile system, it's no surprise that many of you are wondering how to replay your favorite moments from the campaign.

It has been revealed that sadly, after playing the game for over 100 hours for my GTA 6 review, I can confirm that there's simply no option to do so. It kind of makes sense when you think about it.

GTA 5 did let you replay missions easy enough, but that was a way to let you back into key moments to try and complete skill challenges – Rockstar has since moved all of the GTA 6 challenges into its own spot in the progress menu. They are now far broader in scope, and primarily used to unlock GTA 6 cheats once you've wrapped up the story.

According to recent reports, if you're worried about missing anything or wanting to play key moments from a chapter again, I would recommend that you learn the ins and outs of the GTA 6 save system – this lets you keep and load back into 15 different sessions. Otherwise, you'd have to start a whole fresh game just to replay a mission in GTA 6!
