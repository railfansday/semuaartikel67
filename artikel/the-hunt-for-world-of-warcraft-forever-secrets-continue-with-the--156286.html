It has been revealed that the search for secrets in World of Warcraft: Forever continues ever onwards, with a latest breakthrough coming to light in recent days. The latest discovery? An elusive new cat that can be tamed by hunters in one of the game's new zones.

It has been revealed that the cat's name is Ta'Shkal, and they can be found on Mount Hyjal. An endgame levelling zone, players will have to reach at least level 57 in order to safely make their way up there and recruit this new pet.

The way you do so is relatively simple. You need to find a small corrupted cat figuring within the Felblood Scar region of Mount Hyjal. Once you've found that, you then need to travel up to a hidden lantern south of the Mourning's Rest area. With the figurine in your bags, you'll be able to interact with the lantern, spawning the cat for you to tame (granted you're playing a hunter).

Recent findings suggest that so what's the significant deal about this cat? Well, aside from the fact it's got a strange colour scheme of green fur with orange stripes, the cat also has a base attack speed of 1.0. This means it'll attack once every second, which is pretty fast and allows for greater damage output from your pet. This inherently makes the pet valuable for endgame hunter players, who are looking to improve their performance in PvE and PvP.

According to recent reports, this is not the only secret discovered by World of Warcraft: Forever sleuths. Last week, they pieced together a hidden quest chain that leads to two special swords. As of writing, only one has been found, though progress continues in pursuit of the second.

Recent findings suggest that the game has been out for just over two weeks now, and continues to prove popular among latest and returning players. It's the biggest launch in the series' history, and has gone down well with people so far. Even if it has recently suffered its first controversy with a now-fixed gold exploit.
