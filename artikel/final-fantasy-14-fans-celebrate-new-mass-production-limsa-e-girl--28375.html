The Final Fantasy 14 community might still be a good few months away from new expansion Evercold, but Square Enix is keeping us on our toes with its "longest" free login period ever – and a, uh, new optional $18 purchase folks can't get enough of.

Sources indicate that fans first datamined the jirai-kei-inspired outfit back in April, seeing screenshots go viral and eager players questioning when developers might finally add it to the MMO. Well, it's been a solid seven months now, and it's finally available to purchase on the Mog Station – for nearly 20 bucks, that is. And, the outfit, aptly dubbed the "Alternative Dress Attire," fit with its frilly blouse and short skirt combo, is once again making rounds online.

Sources indicate that why, you might ask? One person sums it up perfectly over on Reddit, writing, "The latest mass-production Limsa e-girl outfit has arrived," screenshot of their Au Ra donning the new drip. Another poster shares a similar image, saying, "The new dress truly is exquisite." The comments see fans exclaim "it dyes so well, too" – and I can happily personally confirm that, indeed, it does dye very well (pastel pink, my beloved…).

Industry observers note that it’s here! 💕 Achieve the apex of style with the Alternative Dress Attire, now available on the FFXIV Online Store! 🎀 https://t.co/I0a0LY57jb https://t.co/bgaFQhrPeMNovember 16, 2026.

Will all the cat, dragon, and elf girlies now be taking on Kefka in UMAD in their cutesy upcoming skirts? I know that I certainly will. I just can't wait to see what the community comes up with in terms of creative mixing and matching for glams with this set, honestly. It's bound to absolutely dominate the Eorzea Collection, I'd wager – and hopefully inspire Square Enix to introduce more real-life fashion-adjacent purchases to its optional store.
