The AirPods Pro 3 are cheaper than ever. Despite receiving an early discount last week, Apple’s latest flagship wireless earbuds are still on sale for just $149 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart. They originally sold for $249, but are commonly available for $199 outside major deal events. The best price that we’ve previously seen was $169.

It’s a weird situation that, right now, the feature-packed AirPods Pro 3 are the same price as the newer (but less impressive) AirPods 5, which include a wireless charging case. The Pro 3 deliver considerably more bang for your buck.

In a recent development, this model is Apple’s best-sounding, longest-lasting AirPods, and they ship with five silicone ear tips. Compared to the second-gen AirPod Pro, these offer better water resistance (IP57 versus IP54), longer battery life per charge (eight hours versus six), improved noise cancellation, and heart rate sensing through built-in sensors. The case also has Apple’s second-gen Ultra Wideband chip in the case.
