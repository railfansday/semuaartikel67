Liverpool: why a former PSG imagines Mbappé joining the Reds

Industry observers note that what if Kylian Mbappé joined Liverpool after his adventure at Real Madrid? In any case, this is the conviction of Youssouf Mulumbu, former Paris Saint-Germain midfielder, who spoke about the future of the French striker in an interview with GOAL. While the Blues captain is still playing for Madrid, Mulumbu believes that a departure for the Premier League could be the next step in his career. “ I really think that after Real Madrid, if he wants to leave this club, and it’s a significant “if” because he will stay there for a long time, he will go to Liverpool, for sure “, he mentioned. The former Parisian therefore does not believe in a return of Mbappé to PSG, in particular because of the circumstances of his departure in 2024. “ It’s ironic because the way he left PSG wasn’t the best “, he recalled. To justify his prediction, Mulumbu relies above all on the links already established between the French international and the Reds. “ He spoke with Jürgen Klopp at the time and he is a real Liverpool fan. It would be the best option for him and for the club “, he added. A hypothesis which, however, remains very distant, with no departure from Real Madrid currently being announced.

This possibility did not come completely out of nowhere, since Kylian Mbappé himself admitted to having been close to joining Liverpool when he was still playing at AS Monaco. In an interview given to The Athletic last September, the French striker recounted the discussions initiated with Jürgen Klopp and his mother’s particular interest in the English club. “ Yes, I was close to Liverpool. My mother was a massive admirer of Jürgen Klopp and Liverpool was her favorite destination “, he confided. Fayza Lamari even went to Anfield to discover the facilities and meet the managers, before falling under the spell of the club. Mbappé also revealed that he met Klopp during a particularly original exchange, on board a plane, during which the German coach presented his project to him. “ I told him: ‘You don’t need to convince me. You are Jürgen Klopp, this is Liverpool, a great club’ “, mentioned the Frenchman. Despite these discussions, he ultimately favored PSG in order to win in his hometown. Now based at Real Madrid, where he is pursuing his goal of winning the Champions League and the Ballon d’Or, Mbappé therefore maintains a special history with Liverpool, even if there is no evidence today to confirm that he will one day join the Reds.
