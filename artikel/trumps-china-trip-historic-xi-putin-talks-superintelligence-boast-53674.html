President Donald Trump meets with Russian President Vladimir Putin ahead of trilateral talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

President Donald Trump's visit to China for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit featured an elaborate welcome, warm exchanges with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, and a declaration of American technological dominance on Chinese soil.

The gathering in Shenzhen culminated in the first-ever trilateral meeting between the leaders of the United States, China and Russia, with nuclear arms reductions and the war in Ukraine among the problems on the table.

Here are some of the standout moments from Trump's visit.

XI BREAKS PROTOCOL TO PERSONALLY WELCOME TRUMP AHEAD OF HISTORIC TRILATERAL SUMMIT AND MORE TOP HEADLINES.

Trump declared the United States the world leader in "superintelligence" during remarks at the summit Thursday, making the assertion in one of China's major technology hubs.

"The United States is also proud to be the world leader in superintelligence, the greatest technological transformation since the Industrial Revolution," Trump told APEC leaders.

The remarks came as Washington and Beijing compete for leadership in artificial intelligence and advanced semiconductors.

It has been revealed that trump joined Xi and Putin for the first-ever trilateral meeting between the leaders of the United States, China and Russia just after 3 a.m. ET, calling the gathering an opportunity to reduce nuclear weapons and address global conflicts.

"As the world's three largest nuclear powers by far, the United States, Russia and China are uniquely positioned to discuss how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons on Earth," Trump noted.

The president also stated he had held extensive discussions with Putin about ending the war in Ukraine.

Russian President Vladimir Putin shakes hands with Chinese President Xi Jinping during a meeting on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Leaders' Summit in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, China November 18, 2026. (Sputnik/Sergei Bobylyov/Pool via Reuters).

It has been revealed that "I think things are happening," Trump said. "I think that he and I hope the other side would like to see an end to that war.".

Trump also raised preventing Iran from obtaining nuclear weapons and said he would propose ways for the three countries to protect their sovereignty from the International Criminal Court, which he accused of exceeding its jurisdiction.

A Bloomberg reporter was barred from the trilateral meeting after asking Trump during his bilateral with Xi whether Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had been "disloyal to the United States," according to White House pool reports. Chinese officials warned that shouting questions was considered extremely rude and that reporters could be removed for doing so, despite White House efforts to secure the Bloomberg reporter's access.

Xi personally greeted Trump upon his arrival in Shenzhen Wednesday, meeting the president at the foot of the aircraft stairs in an elaborate welcome.

Recent findings suggest that the two exchanged greetings before standing together for the American and Chinese national anthems. A military flyover followed, with five jets leaving trails of colored smoke overhead.

Trump offered warm words for both Xi and Putin during the summit, praising his personal relationship with the Chinese leader and referring to the Russian president as a "friend.".

It has been revealed that "President Xi and I have formed an outstanding personal relationship," Trump said during their bilateral meeting. "Some people are unhappy when they hear that, but I'm very happy about it.".

Trump also told Xi, "You're my friend, and we have a very special relationship.".

"Some people are unhappy when they hear that, but I'm very happy about it," he went on, telling the Chinese leader: "And I think as long as you and I are in charge of things, we'll never have a problem.".

Recent findings suggest that during a separate encounter with Putin, Trump was heard saying, "Who is he? It's my friend. Nice to see you," according to video shared by Clash Report.
