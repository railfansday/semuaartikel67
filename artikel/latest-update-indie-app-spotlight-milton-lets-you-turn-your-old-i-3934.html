Industry observers note that apple finally released its upcoming HomeView this week, a smart display that serves at the center of your home. What if you don’t want to buy a new product though? Milton is an app for older iPads running iPadOS 17 and later that can serve a similar purpose.

Industry observers note that milton offers a customizable dashboard that allows you to put your most key tiles front and center. It can show your calendar, chores, shopping lists, weather, the clock, a photo frame, a to do list, and so much more. Everything you include will show on the family iPad, but it can also sync to iPhones if you want to check something remotely.

Sources indicate that there’s one neat feature that’ll be fun for families: a points system. If your kids complete chores, they can be awarded points and as a parent you can set goals for what kids can earn with enough points. This system can be used to encourage kids to complete chores or follow routines. Parents can also sign off on the chores to ensure kids aren’t just tapping the complete button.

In a recent development, it also serves as a nice recipe guide. If you take photos of cookbooks, Milton can parse through what you captured and provide a step by step guide for cooking. If you don’t have certain ingredients, it can swiftly be added to your shoping list.

It has been revealed that this only scratches the surface of what Milton can do. You can read their website for the rest.

Here’s why Josh, the developer, decided to build Milton:.

It has been revealed that I’m a software engineer, and my partner is an oncology pharmacist. She wanted one place that kept our household organised: the calendar, the chores, the shopping, what’s for dinner. Skylight is expensive, and the iPad apps that do this charge a subscription every year. We wanted something that fit our lives, connected us as the Apple family we already are, and didn’t cost families a fortune.

Sources indicate that like most families, we had an old iPad lying around. We put it in a case with a magnet, stuck it to the fridge, and that became Milton.

In a recent development, milton provides a very generous free offering on the App Store. The core organizer features are all available out of the box, but if you want to add more people to the app, sync across devices, or share the app with other family members (for their personal devices), you’ll need to purchase Milton Individual for $4.99 (one time purchase) or Milton Family for $14.99. Milton Family automatically shares with your iCloud family sharing group.
