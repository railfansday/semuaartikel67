5 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade tips for first-time watchers

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (150 words, 📖 Standard)
  • The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City is a major holiday event attracting over 3.5 million attendees each year.
  • Visitors can watch the balloon inflation the day before the parade for a unique, up-close experience.
  • Securing a good viewing spot requires arriving very early or booking special brunches or hotel rooms along the route.
  • Many hotels near the parade route offer special viewing options, but these often come at a premium and may require advance reservations.
  • Expect large crowds and long lines, especially for balloon inflation and prime parade viewing spots.
  • Weather in late November can be unpredictable, so bring warm clothing and rain gear.
  • Hotel rooms with parade views are often sold as part of premium packages and may have minimum stay requirements.
