European Commission president Ursula von der Leyen sets out 22nd package of sanctions.

Turning to Ukraine, Nato’s Rutte stresses the need to secure the flow of “crucial supplies” or air defence weapons and ammunition to Ukraine.

“It helps Ukraine to defend itself today, but it also strengthens Ukraine’s hands in any future negotiations with Russia. … But support needs to be concrete. We have to show Putin that his attempts to weaken our support will fail.”.

“Our citizens need to know that the lights will stay on, that their homes will stay warm, that hospitals, transport and communications will keep working.”.

According to recent reports, “One day it can be in the form of an arson attack against a factory or drones disrupt disrupting an airport. The next, it is sabotaged railways or cargo vessels targeted at sea and cyber-attacks.”.

According to recent reports, “It’s all happening now, and it won’t go away anytime soon. … The conclusion of all of this is that we are all targeted, that we are all affected – when it comes to our pipelines, our cables, our energy grids and our economies – and they are all interconnected. … That means that we are all in this for the long term, and therefore we must step up to the plate, to ensure that, we stay ahead of danger.”.

“But if we are serious about keeping our alliance strong and secure, then all allies must pull their weight not only in 2035, but starting now. At least, everybody needs to have the credible path to get there.”.

According to recent reports, “These investments are also a boon to prosperity. There are dividends to be gained from investing more in defence. This is about real jobs for real people.”.
