Framery's flexible pod subscription tackles office furniture waste concern

According to office pod brand Framery, when companies resize or move, desks and meeting-room furniture often aren’t deemed “worth the cost of moving” and are discarded during the process.

Framery explained that the EU and US discard 11 million tonnes of furniture a year.

Specifically, the brand points to a study for the European Environmental Bureau, which found that in the EU, 80 to 90 per cent of office furniture is burned or landfilled.

According to the Environmental Protection Agency, in the US, less than one per cent is recycled.

Framery aims to tackle this glitch with its office pod subscription service, presenting a more environmentally-friendly alternative to disposing of office furniture when a lease runs out, for example.

“The ‘fast furniture’ trend means 80 per cent of furniture ends up in landfills,” said Framery’s chief design officer, Joonas Vartola. “We wanted to create a pod that’s part of the solution, not the problem.”.

“Framery pods are built for heavy use, keeping them in the field for tens of thousands of sessions and out of landfills,” Vartola added.

For example, if there’s an increase in staff and more pods are needed, or if an office’s layout changes.

The scheme covers the full range of its signature line pods, from one-person phone booths to meeting pods.

According to Saija Serpola, managing director and founding partner at IR Partners, using the pods has enabled increased flexibility and reduced financial risk when investing in additional office furniture.

“We can terminate, increase, or change the solution as our organisation develops.”.

Framery pods are built from mono-materials, including steel, aluminium, glass and PET.

In a recent development, “Our pods with heavy-duty, high-quality materials engineered to withstand wear and tear,” noted Vartola. “It’s all about reducing waste, from design to decades of use.”.

As part of the scheme, when a change is required, the pods are dismantled, shipped and rebuilt, rather than discarded into landfill.

According to Framery, each pod is designed for disassembly, with all parts replaceable and recyclable. This includes everything from interior panels, glass, upholstery, carpet, ventilation units and seals.

During refurbishment, each pod is restored to as-fresh condition and delivered to its next office with a lifetime warranty.

“That is a manufacturing question before it is a commercial one, and it is settled years earlier, on the drawing board.”.

To learn more, visit Framery’s website.
