Practical experience setting up and troubleshooting conversion tracking with Google Tag Manager, GA4, and advertising platform pixels or conversion tags.

A strong understanding of core paid media metrics, including CPC, CPM, CTR, conversion rate, CPA, and ROAS, and experience analyzing campaign data and turning findings into actionable optimizations.

Familiarity with audience targeting, retargeting, and campaign testing methodologies, and an understanding of marketing funnels, attribution, and how paid media contributes to lead generation and customer acquisition.

Experience optimizing landing pages and collaborating on conversion rate optimization (CRO) initiatives.

Proficiency in other languages and experience managing non-English paid media campaigns.

Experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude to improve campaign workflows, analysis, and creative development.
