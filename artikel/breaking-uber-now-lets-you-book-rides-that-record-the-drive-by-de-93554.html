📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Uber is piloting a newly introduced feature in the U.S. that offers rides in which drivers have enabled the organization’s recording feature, Record My Ride.

The option, which uses the front camera of the Uber driver’s phone to record the entire drive, will show up as “Recorded Rides” in the app.

Industry observers note that the ride-hailing company is starting the pilot in Baltimore, Raleigh, Indianapolis, and St. Louis and will expand to more locations later.

According to recent reports, uber noted it is testing recorded rides specifically for rides booked at late night or those involving teens. Earlier this year, it introduced a live video streaming feature for rides booked for teenagers. In another test, the company had enabled audio recording for late-night trips in some cities.

The organization is also testing a feature that will turn on recording when the app detects the ride going off-course, or when the vehicle might have had an accident.

The company stated drivers can opt out of recording through the Settings panel. Similarly, riders can also decline permission to record their rides via an in-app notification.
