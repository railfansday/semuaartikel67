Lianlian DigiTech Releases XCard with Controls for AI Agent Payments

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lianlian DigiTech and Visa revealed on November 18, 2026 the launch of LianLian XCard and a live business purchase completed with a payment credential designed specifically for an authorised AI agent, extending their collaboration into business spending and expense management.

Sources indicate that the companies described the purchase as an Asia Pacific first for a live B2B transaction using an agent-specific tokenised payment credential. LianLian XWorker, an AI agent registered in Visa’s Agentic Directory, obtained the credential through Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect and completed an office-supply order. The directory includes AI agents and merchants that Visa has verified as legitimate participants in agentic commerce.

Unveiled at Singapore FinTech Festival 2026, XCard is powered by Visa Intelligent Commerce and designed for spending across teams and projects. Eligible clients can apply through XWorker, while customers can link a LianLian XPay wallet to let authorised AI agents pay with XCard within business-defined parameters.

It has been revealed that visa Intelligent Commerce brings together agent identification, tokenisation, authentication and transaction controls for AI-initiated payments. Intelligent Commerce Connect supplies a single integration through the Visa Acceptance Platform for permissioned transactions across payment schemes, token providers and agent ecosystems.

Industry observers note that the new credential adds an agent-specific payment capability to that framework. Together with agent identification and business-defined controls, it enables a manufacturer to determine what an authorised agent may purchase and how payment is made. The arrangement ties payment authority to a recognised agent and places the business’s purchasing and payment rules around the transaction.

The launch builds on a July 24, 2026 announcement featuring LoopXPay, Lianlian’s earlier purchasing agent. In that transaction, LoopXPay identified a purchasing requirement, recommended and compared suppliers, ordered a product sample and paid within a single workflow. It operated under predefined spending controls and approval parameters and was registered in Visa’s Agentic Directory.

The earlier journey demonstrated sourcing through settlement; the latest purchase adds a dedicated agent payment credential and uses XWorker for a separate office-supply order. Visa and Lianlian said the approach can reduce manual purchasing for small and medium-sized businesses while preserving oversight, and that they will continue exploring applications in procurement, supplier payments and other B2B workflows.

XCard’s controls include card limits and transaction thresholds. The festival’s launch-session record describes multi-currency payments, real-time spending controls and centralized expense management, as well as native support for autonomous payments by AI agents. Those features connect agent-initiated purchases to a broader system for managing team and project expenditure.

Lianlian DigiTech mentioned selected AI-related benefits are planned for eligible customers. They are future additions, not part of the functionality described as available at launch.

The launch links two requirements for business use of agentic commerce: verifying that an agent is authorised to transact and enforcing the tech giant’s spending policy when it does. XCard supplies the card and expense-management layer, while XWorker and Visa’s credential system handle the agent-initiated payment. Whether those permissions can be carried consistently into the broader procurement and supplier-payment workflows the companies are exploring is the central question for the next phase of their collaboration.
