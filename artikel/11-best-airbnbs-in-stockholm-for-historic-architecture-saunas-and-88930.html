Sources indicate that for a break that balances the buzz of discovering a fresh city with a good dose of fresh air, Stockholm’s mix of lush parks and eclectic architecture is an enduring choice. The city is formed of 14 islands, meaning you’re never too far from a waterfront to amble along. It's packed with historical charm (head to Gamla Stan to see one of Europe’s oldest medieval cobbled streets), as well as stylish boutiques and museums championing everything from Abba to the world’s best-preserved 17th-century warship. While the city is home to a slew of excellent hotels, from elegant converted townhouses to the former home of Astrid Lindgren (author of the beloved Pippi Longstocking book series), you’ll want to look to Airbnb for a real home-from-home vibe.

These 11 excellent rentals throughout the city and its outskirts balance top-notch amenities with the freedom that only comes with a private booking. True to Nordic form, there’s a clutch of homes with their own sauna and steam rooms, and all have either sea or skyline views. Below, the best Airbnbs in Stockholm to book for a weekend of weaving through medieval streets, gallery hopping, and lots of cinnamon buns.

We've vetted these listings based on Superhost status, ratings, amenities, location, previous guest reviews, and decor.

Number of guests: 4Bed and bath: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathsWhy we love it: Historic architecture, prime location, dedicated workspace.

Industry observers note that subverting traditional Scandi minimalist sensibilities, this cool retreat in the hip heart of Södermalm channels a moody, more underground kind of charm. On the top floor, light-flooded, open-plan living areas are packed with rich, creamy furnishings and crisp architectural lines—multiple previous guests say that this is one of the most stylish homes they’ve ever stayed in. Downstairs, two tranquil bedrooms are carved directly into the exposed natural rock, so you can sleep beneath historic vaulted ceilings after a long day skipping from sight to sight. On that note: Södermalm is an eclectic area of town, with plenty of vintage shops and wine bars, after which you can hole up in this cocooning, cave-like retreat, as though you’re stepping into your own secret—and very stylish—bunker.

Neighborhood: SödermalmThings to do nearby: Fotografiska, Riddarholm Church, Stockholm City Museum.

Number of guests: 2Bed and bath: 1 bedroom, 1 bathWhy we love it: Forest access, indoor fireplace, outdoor gas grill.

Blink, and you might miss this off-grid, A-frame cabin, hidden among a thick swathe of pine trees. It’s worth searching for: Once you make your way through the foliage, you’ll find a truly wonderful Swedish escape for two. The glass-fronted structure invites in all of the elements, while inside, it’s time to switch off—there’s a crackling open hearth where you can grill all of your meals, and precious little noise other than the rustle of wind through the canopy. Fair warning: There’s a very rustic toilet and seasonal shower about a 30-second walk down the forest path, so if you’re not a DIY fan, perhaps look elsewhere. Nonetheless, for more adventurous sorts, the trade-off means absolute tranquility surrounded by happily grazing sheep and twinkly starry night skies—all within a hop and a skip of the capital.

Neighborhood: VärmdöThings to do nearby: Lillsved Beach, Buddharama Temple, Stora Sandön.

Number of guests: 8Bed and bath: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathsWhy we love it: Historic warehouse, steps from the lake, wood-burning fireplace.

Wood-beamed ceilings, clattering floors, a muted color scheme, and a signature bright exterior welcome you in from the cold—this former warehouse just oozes 19th-century charm the moment you round the corner. Better yet, it’s totally massive, sprawling across three roomy floors, with a prime spot perched right on the peaceful banks of Åkers canal. A snazzy and social kitchen space opens onto a central island and a fireplace, where all the family will want to gather. When it’s time to bed down, there are cozy curtained sleeping nooks and an additional attic dormitory, adding a playful, rustic vibe. You can rent canoes from host Cecile to paddle down the canal, or jump on an e-bike to explore the surrounding nature reserves. When you return, fire up the outdoor grill and gather around the dining table for a long dinner with more endless views.

Neighborhood: Norrö-StavaThings to do nearby: Prästfjärden lake, Näsudden nature reserve, Åkersbro walking trails.

Number of guests: 3Bed and bath: 1 bedroom, 1.5 bathsWhy we love it: Sea views, sauna, hot tub.

In a recent development, this joyfully bobbing houseboat turns the gray blue waters of the Baltic into something akin to your own front lawn—and it does so just 15 minutes from the heady sights of central Stockholm. Panoramic floor-to-ceiling glass means you feel that you’re virtually out on the water—and, of course, you can dive straight into the lake from your bedroom deck. Dry off and hop into the private wood-fired sauna to warm up. Hop off the boat, and you can sunbathe on the jetty, cook up some local catches on the barbecue, or jump into the complimentary canoe. All in all, this is an idyllic waterside playground that active types will love.

Number of guests: 4Bed and bath: 2 bedrooms, 1 bathWhy we love it: Sauna, beach access, pet-friendly.

Expect some truly good seascape ASMR when you stay here—you’re so close to the water you can hear the waves rolling onto the shore right from the porch. Grab the kayak from the cupboard and head down to the beach to make the most of this beautiful location. It’s a short drive from the home to Stockholm’s city center, but you’re rewarded with much serenity and glorious views instead. For those seeking a secluded family stay, this is the place.

Neighborhood: TyresöThings to do nearby: Sandholmarna nature preserve, Tyresta nature reserve, Lilla beach.

Number of guests: 6Bed and bath: 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathsWhy we love it: Wood stove, sauna, beach access.

Sources indicate that this spacious waterside cottage is not in Stockholm’s city center, but by compromising on a 30-minute commute into town, you gain something really special. The owners took the original 130-year-old structure down piece by piece, rebuilding it on their own property. It was worth the effort. Now, it lies surrounded by pine trees in a nature reserve beside a sparkling, clear lake, with the sea just a few steps further in the other direction. A large porch provides the spot for barbecue dinners and sunbathing sessions in the summer, and there are two spas within walking distance for those looking for their Scandi sweat fix—the sea-facing Skepparholmen Spa, and Japanese onsen-style Yasuragi. But you don't have to head that far, as the property has its own sauna. Stay in as long as you can muster, then cool off by jumping in the lake—something all the more satisfactory in the winter months.

A previous guest notes this flat has a Latest York City vibe, and we can see why—the interior space is small but stylish, with an industrial bent and views all over the coolest part of Stockholm from the balcony. It’s on the top floor of this apartment block, so the vistas can’t be beat. It’s been a winner for those on work trips, thanks to superfast WiFi and a dedicated workspace, and, unusually for Airbnb stays, you can bring your dog too.

Number of guests: 4Bed and bath: 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroomWhy we love it: Steam room, in-unit washer/dryer, fully equipped kitchen.

For an Airbnb stay that’s as close to checking into a luxury hotel as possible, look to this plush apartment right in the city center. The whole place is brand latest, and no expense has been spared on the fittings—there are genuine oak floorboards, Italian travertine surfaces, underfloor heating, and a slew of top-notch furniture, plus little touches such as a shower seat and a gloriously powerful rainfall shower. The best bit is the steam room; there’s no better way to decompress after a long flight or a blustery autumnal walk by the river.

Sources indicate that if dopamine itself could be characterized as a building, it’d be this one. The place (all 300 square feet of it) is pure joy from top to bottom—with a pink-tiled kitchen, mint-green bathroom, and cozy bedroom nook with yellow walls and blue candy-striped bedding. Cheery artworks add a final flourish. Despite its bijou size, there’s room for two more guests on the pullout bed, and there’s a patio with twinkly fairy lights to sit out on sunny afternoons with a book, a glass of wine, or both.

Neighborhood: ÄlvsjöThings to do nearby: Älvsjö Forest Nature Preserve, Herrängens Gård, Långbro park.

Number of guests: 4Bed and bath: 1 bedroom, 1 bathWhy we love it: Beach access, sauna, kayak.

Sources indicate that forget your average homely fisherman’s cottage—this home here, on the cusp of the lake in the city’s swish Stocksund neighborhood, marks a newer, more modern breed of lake house. It’s chic as anything you’ll find in the city, with smooth, polished concrete floors and knotty pine walls finished with a moody, cocooning interior palette. Sweeping glass windows make the most of the beautiful views that stretch over the water and right to the city as it twinkles on the other side. Grab the kayak and spend an afternoon bobbing on the lake, then stop in for a well-deserved sweat session in the private sauna.

Neighborhood: StocksundThings to do nearby: Restaurang Hemma, Svanholmsparken park, Swedish Museum of Natural History.

Number of guests: 3Bed and bath: 1 bedroom, 1 bathWhy we love it: Wood-burning fireplace, Aesop toiletries, garden view.

This traditional Swedish apartment has had a modern facelift recently. Now it pairs gorgeous original features, including imposing 10-foot-high ceilings and whitewashed floorboards, with a sleek new kitchen and selection of cool designer furniture pieces. There's also a truly cloudlike sofa just made for cozy nights in. The surrounding streets are just as cool as the interiors; this home is smack dab in SoFo—by far Stockholm’s trendiest district—so you're just one street over from a clutch of the best of the city’s bar, restaurant, and café scene.
