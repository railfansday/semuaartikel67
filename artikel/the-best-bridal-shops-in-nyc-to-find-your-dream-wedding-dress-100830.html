From multi-brand salons to designer showrooms, the best bridal shops in NYC are worth making appointments at to find every piece of your wedding wardrobe.

It has been revealed that whether you live in the U.S. or beyond, Upcoming York City is the premier destination to shop for a wedding dress. The best bridal shops in NYC run the gamut from singular showrooms for locally based ateliers, flagship stores for international brands, and salons featuring curated selections from a number of top bridal designers. To-be-weds looking for their own wedding wardrobe can always benefit from a trip to multiple bridal shops around New York to help them discover some of the most beautifully made and innovative designs in the bridal industry.

Industry observers note that as a Fresh York local who tried over 100 dresses ahead of her own wedding, wore five looks over her wedding weekend, and regularly attends bridal fashion week both in New York and Barcelona, I am constantly asked where to shop in the city. I myself went to 15 different salons over the course of my search for the perfect wardrobe (and had repeat visits at multiple). While I always advise brides to do a little research to understand the styles they like and what brands they are drawn to, I usually give the same advice. First, try out one or two multi-brand salons with styles within your budget that carry designers you are interested in. This can give you an opportunity to try multiple silhouettes and see options from a variety of designers’ perspectives. Then, choose a few individual brand salons and showrooms to visit.

It’s crucial to take into consideration that many stores require an appointment fee—and that those can book out months in advance. Also, some stores might offer trunk shows showcasing larger, new collections from certain designers seasonally. So you should invest your time and money strategically into the shops that align with your style and will have the selection you are interested in. However, don’t be afraid to try something different—often, brides fall in love with a wedding dress they never expected.

It has been revealed that when trying on wedding gowns, it’s important to be prepared so you can have the best overall experience. It’s advised to wear nude undergarments that won’t show through more sheer styles and consider bringing a heel you might like to wear with a gown. You should also avoid wearing heavy makeup and fragrances to appointments. Sample gowns are reworn by many to-be-weds, and many stores ask you to go fresh-faced to avoid makeup transfer. While you can bring a few guests along like a bridesmaid, mom, or stylist, check with each salon’s policies on how big of a party you can bring.

Industry observers note that as most wedding dresses take six to eight months to make, it is advised to book your appointments about nine months to a year ahead of your wedding. On rare occasions, you can find designers who might be able to work on shorter timelines. Certain popular designers can book out their appointments months in advance, so as soon as you know you want to start the shopping process, take a look at availability.
