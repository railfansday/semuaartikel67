📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, pokémon Go is having a Pawniard Community Day event on Saturday, Nov. 21 from 2-5 p.m. in your local time. This event is indeed running alongside the "Dynamax Trails" event, and it begins right when Eternatus' stint in the morning ends.

As expected with a Community Day event, Pawniward will spawn in huge numbers with a high chance for it to appear shiny. There are also several other bonuses and perks, which we've listed out below.

As per old research by the now-defunct website The Silph Road (via Wayback Machine), shiny rates on Community Days are about 1 in 24, which means that if you keep playing throughout the three-hour window, you should find quite a few shiny Pokémon.

If you’re short on time or Poké Balls, you can pop an Incense, then rapidly tap each Pawniard to check for shiny ones, running from any that aren’t shiny. Notably, any Pawniard you’ve already tapped will face where your player is standing, so that should help identify which ones you may have already checked.

If you evolve Bisharp into Kingambit from 2-9 p.m. in your local time, it will learn the charged move Kowtow Cleave.

If you miss out on evolving it during this period, you will likely be able to evolve it during a Community Day weekend event in December to get Kowtow Cleave. If you don’t want to wait, you can use an Elite TM to get the move.

Usually, to evolve Bisharp into Kingambit, you'd need to set Bisharp as your buddy and beat 15 dark- or steel-type Pokémon in raids. However, for this Community Day, this requirement has been reduced heavily.

On Saturday, Nov. 21 from 2-9 p.m., you will only need to defeat 1 dark- or steel-type Pokémon in a raid with Bisharp as your buddy to evolve it into Kingambit. The evolution will still cost 100 Pawniard Candy, though. The Bisharp you are trying to evolve won't need to be used in the raid, it just needs to be set as your buddy Pokémon.

Industry observers note that with its Community Day move, Kingambit is actually a very strong dark-type attacker, only falling to Mega and shadow Pokémon. Definitely power one up and give it Snarl and Kowtow Cleave for success.

Its Community Day move also makes it a strong pick in PvP, specifically in the Great League. Run it with Low Kick, Iron Head, and Kowtow Cleave to make the most of it.

The following bonuses will be active during Pawniard Community Day:.

That said, you should definitely put your eggs in Incubators and pop an Incense and try to nab some powerful Pawniard.

If you can Mega Evolve Gyarados, Steelix, Scizor, Skarmory, Houndoom, Tyranitar, Sableye, Mawile, Aggron, Sharpedo, Absol, Metagross, Lucario, Greninja, or Malamar, you’ll score additional Pawniard Candy per catch.

In a recent development, just the same as previous Community Days this year, the Lure Module bonus will be active for this event, too. Pawniard will continue to spawn from Lure Modules placed until 9 p.m., retaining their Community Day shiny rate and their chance to spawn with a Special Background.
