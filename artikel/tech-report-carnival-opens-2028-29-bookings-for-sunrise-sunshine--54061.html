Tech Report: Carnival Opens 2028-29 Bookings for Sunrise, Sunshine and Valor

Carnival Cruise Line has opened bookings for the Carnival Sunrise, Sunshine and Valor’s 2028-29 season sailing from Upcoming Orleans, Galveston, Miami and Mobile.

The schedule includes more than 160 itineraries ranging from four-day getaways to 14-day Carnival Journeys cruises and offers opportunities to visit 21 destinations across The Bahamas, Caribbean, Mexico and Central America.

Highlights include the Sunrise expanding Miami operations, the Sunshine going year-round in Galveston and the Valor featuring extended Carnival Journeys cruises from Mobile.

More than 50 sailings across the three ships will visit Carnival’s exclusive destination Celebration Key, which the firm expects will welcome 3.5 million guests in 2027.

The Carnival Sunrise will open the deployment with a spring season from Miami beginning in April 2028, offering four- to eight-day sailings visiting The Bahamas and Caribbean and calling at Celebration Key, Nassau, Grand Turk, Ocho Rios, Grand Cayman, Cozumel, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

Starting June 2028, the ship will reposition to New Orleans, from where it will offer six-, seven-, eight- and nine-day cruises to The Bahamas and Western Caribbean year round, visiting destinations such as Celebration Key, Nassau, Key West, Cozumel, Belize, Grand Cayman, Montego Bay and Isla Tropicale.

The cruise line underlined a series of seven-day Bahamas cruises featuring Celebration Key, Nassau and Key West as a highlight.

The Sunrise will also operate a 14-day Carnival Journeys cruise to the Caribbean and Panama, visiting Aruba, Curaçao, Cartagena, Colón and Limón.

The Carnival Sunshine will operate year-round from Galveston for the first time, with 29 four-day cruises to Cozumel or Progreso, as well as 24 10-day voyages to the Western Caribbean, Eastern Caribbean and Caribbean and Panama.

Select Eastern Caribbean itineraries feature calls to Celebration Key, Grand Turk, Amber Cove and RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay.

The Carnival Valor will continue offering six-, seven- and eight-day itineraries from Mobile to The Bahamas and Western Caribbean, including sailings to Celebration Key, Nassau, Bimini, RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, Cozumel, Belize, Grand Cayman, Montego Bay and Ocho Rios.

There will also be two 14-day Carnival Journeys voyages: a Caribbean and Panama itinerary visiting Aruba, Curaçao, Cartagena, Colón and Limón, and a Southern Caribbean itinerary calling at San Juan, Aruba, Curaçao, Amber Cove, Grand Cayman and Ocho Rios.
