Survey of 1,000+ SMB respondents finds rising satisfaction and engagement amid increased AI use and in-office work.

DUBLIN, Calif., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- TriNet (NYSE: TNET), a leading provider of comprehensive human resources solutions for small and medium-size businesses (SMBs) today unveiled its 2027 State of the Workplace report, an annual look at employer and employee sentiment across the SMB landscape throughout the nation.

Based on a survey of more than 1,000 SMB respondents, including 575 full-time employees and 525 employers, the report examines some of the most pressing workplace challenges facing SMBs, including employee engagement and satisfaction, return-to-office trends, benefits and AI in the workplace.

Conducted by TriNet and administered by Qualtrics, the study also examines year-over-year shifts and differences across generations, offering a snapshot of how the SMB workplace continues to evolve.

"As businesses navigate an increasingly complex world of work, leaders have an opportunity to be intentional about the kind of workplace they want to create. That starts with listening to employees, understanding their evolving needs and recognizing that the employee experience plays an notable role in business success," said Catherine Wragg, Chief People Officer, TriNet. "The findings in this report offer valuable perspective for leaders looking to make informed decisions, strengthen their organizations and create an environment where both their people and their businesses can thrive.".
