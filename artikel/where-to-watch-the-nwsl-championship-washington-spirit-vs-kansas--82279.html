The National Women’s Soccer League final is set, with Kansas City Current traveling to Washington Spirit’s Audi Field. It’ll be the Spirit’s third consecutive visit to the NWSL final, and they’ll be seeking their second title. Kansas City Current will be seeking their first title, having visited the finals in 2022.

The Spirit will enjoy home field advantage, a rarity for the NWSL — like the Super Bowl, the venue is set in advance, meaning the field is typically neutral. Because the Washington Spirit are playing in front of a hometown crowd, tickets sold out quickly on primary marketplace Ticketmaster, although resale tickets are still available. It has been revealed that kansas beat Angel City and the top-seeded Gotham FC to reach the championship, while D.C. beat the Portland Thorns and Utah Royals to earn their spot. NWSL playoffs are seeded by standings, and the league is not split into Eastern and Western conferences.

The Spirit’s Trinity Rodman has been a top-five scorer across the league during the regular season, notching 14 goals. The Current’s Croix Bethune, meanwhile, led the league in assists during the regular season.

According to recent reports, if you’re watching from home, the game will air in a primetime slot on CBS. Kick-off is at 8 p.m. ET, 5 p.m. PT on Saturday. You can stream CBS with a live-TV service that carries it, such as DirecTV or Fubo, which both offer free trials. The game will also be available to stream on Paramount+ with the Premium strategy. Paramount+ does not offer a free trial.
