FIFA's World Rankings have been updated following the second international break of the 2026/27 season, and there have been changes aplenty.

England withstood a late comeback from 2026 World Champions Spain to win 3-2 at Riyadh Air Metropolitano Stadium. It was La Roja's first defeat in over two years, with their 43-match unbeaten run coming to an end.

Elsewhere, Mauricio Pochettino's reign as United States manager came to an end on a positive note as his side progressed to the Concacaf Nations League semi-finals with a resounding 8-0 victory against Haiti over two legs.

Argentina and Portugal also played their first matches following Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo's retirements from international football, while the likes of France, Germany and Brazil have also been in action.

But how has the international break affected FIFA's official rankings? Let's take a closer look at the top 50...

According to recent reports, venezuela just creep into the top 50, despite losing to Russia in their most recent outing. They are followed by Slovenia, who have moved up three place, and South Africa.

Slovakia have dropped two places to 47th, despite going unbeaten in their past eight matches. Serbia are now ranked the 46th best side in the world after a dismal set of results. They have been beaten in their last eight matches, with their last victory coming all the way back in March 2026 when they beat Saudi Arabia.

In a recent development, scotland have dropped to 44th after being thumped 4-0 by Switzerland. They are above Panama, but below Mali, who are up four places, and Cameroon.

Industry observers note that wales have also moved down two places and are now outside the top 40 after defeats to Norway and Portugal. Craig Bellamy's side have won just one of their last 10 matches.

In a recent development, greece are very much a side on the up. The European side have cracked the top 40 after beating Serbia in their most recent outing. DR Congo, Poland, Hungary and Ukraine are above them.

Sources indicate that russia are currently banned from FIFA competitions, but they are still able to compete in friendly matches. They have won five of their last seven matches, with their most recent outing being a 2-0 victory over Venezuela.

Graham Potter's Sweden, Turkey, Algeria and Paraguay are ranked 34th, 33rd, 32nd and 31st respectively.

It has been revealed that it has been a dismal few months for Turkey. Substantial things were expected of them at the 2026 World Cup, but they crashed out of the group stage. They are now outside the top 30 and falling rapidly after losing all six of their Nations League matches.

Sources indicate that nigeria are now the 29th best side in the world. The Super Eagles, who have several top players including Victor Osimhen and Ademola Lookman, beat Tanzania in their most recent outing. They are sandwiched in between South Korea and Ivory Coast.

According to recent reports, canada have risen to 25th after beating Guatemala home and away in their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final. They have moved above Australia and Egypt.

Ecuador, Iran, Austria and Denmark just miss out on a place in the top 20.

According to recent reports, uruguay make the top 20 despite failing spectacularly at the 2026 World Cup. Norway, who made the quarter-finals of this summer's major tournament, are ranked in 19th after defeating Wales and Denmark.

Industry observers note that japan were crowned Kirin Cup champions for a 12th time in October, defeating Upcoming Zealand in the final. They are ranked a place above African giants Senegal, who were officially reinstated as the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champions following a ruling from the Court of Arbitration for Sport.

The United States are presently on a six-game winning streak and are now ranked 16th in the world. They are now searching for a upcoming manager after Mauricio Pochettino left his post to join Manchester United. Italy, who failed to qualify for the 2026 World Cup, have gone above Croatia into 14th after defeating France and losing to Belgium.

Rafael Marquez has had a very poor start to his tenure as Mexico manager, winning just one of his opening six matches at the helm. As a result, El Tri have dropped out of the top 10 and into 13th.

It has been revealed that jurgen Klopp's Germany are now on the cusp of the top 10 after beating Serbia and then thrashing the Netherlands 5-0. They are now above Switzerland.

Colombia have moved into the top 10 following the latest round of international games, with the Netherlands and Belgium in ninth and eighth respectively.

According to recent reports, morocco climbed two places to fifth after recording back-to-back wins over Niger in November. The Atlas Lions have slipped back down to seventh, though, after Senegal were officially reinstated as the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champions.

Brazil and Portugal remain in sixth and fifth respectively. Portugal, now without Cristiano Ronaldo following his retirement from international football, were thumped 4-1 at home to Denmark in their first match without the greatest goalscorer ever. They bounced back with a hard-fought 3-2 victory over Wales.

Recent findings suggest that the top four remain unchanged. England are ranked fourth, behind France and Argentina. 2026 World Cup champions Spain are the only team to have over 2000 points and retain top spot, despite their remarkable unbeaten run coming to an end.
