Welcome to “What’s in My Bag,” a new series where TPG staffers open their carry-ons, personal items and dopp kits to share the travel essentials they never leave home without. First up: The Points Guy founder, Brian Kelly.

Recent findings suggest that as a travel expert who’s constantly on the go, I will say some of my favorite things that you’ll find in my bag revolve around staying organized and being comfortable — I always know where my essentials are. At this stage of my life, I’m willing to splurge a bit on handcrafted or well-made products that last. Quality is nonnegotiable.

This travel pouch is so chic, but definitely a splurge. It’s made with Saffiano leather and Re-Nylon, an innovative fabric that’s produced from recycled plastic recovered from the ocean. You can find it at Saks Fifth Avenue. I love it for keeping everything I might need on the go organized — with two top zipper pockets and one each on the front and back, I never run out of room.

Buy: Prada Re-Nylon and Saffiano leather travel pouch, $1,120.

According to recent reports, these Bose noise-canceling headphones are comfortable and seamlessly connect via Bluetooth. Plus, they are stylish and have great sound quality. The surround sound is awesome.

Buy: Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones, from $349 (normally $449).

As a content creator, I needed a good lav microphone with an iPhone receiver for when I’m out and about. The Rode wireless mic is super easy to use, holds a charge all day and slips right into my travel pouch.

Buy: Rode ultra-compact wireless microphone system with USB-C connector, $89 (normally $99).

My go-to quick snack is Quest protein peanut butter cups — indulgent yet packed with protein. I keep Grüns gummies in my bag for a healthy mix of essential vitamins and prebiotics. They keep me feeling energized and are more than just a multivitamin — they’re made with whole fruits and veggies too.

Buy: Grüns sugar-free gummies (pack of 28), $73.32; Quest protein peanut butter cups (pack of 12), $28.49.

I firmly believe in investing in pieces that will last, and this handcrafted leather wallet from Aspinal of London is no exception. It holds up to eight cards and can be personalized with your initials.

Buy: Aspinal of London 8-card billfold wallet in black woven leather, $180 (normally $355).

Industry observers note that I have a second phone for travel that’s connected to the T-Mobile network as a backup, and, depending on your service plan, T-Mobile also offers free Wi-Fi on U.S. airlines. I keep my main phone on Verizon, but you never want to be stuck without a phone when abroad.

Buy: iPhone 18 Pro, from $1,199.

Sources indicate that I’m a big tennis fan, whether it’s traveling for a match or catching the U.S. Open when it's in New York. This easy-to-adjust hat is perfect to keep in my bag, so I always have one on me for sun protection.

Buy: Wimbledon Mono-tone Herringbone Cap, $64.

Fashionable sun protection is a no-brainer. I love these Tom Ford sunglasses in black.

Buy: Tom Ford Snowdon sunglasses, $284 (normally $316).

This small device is super versatile — not only does it convert 220–240V AC input to 120V AC output, but it also includes five plug adapters so I can use it in nearly every country in the world.

Buy: Insignia international converter and plug adapters, $17.49 (normally $29.99).

This portable power bank gives me peace of mind knowing I can charge my phone on the go. Small and lightweight, it fits on the back of my iPhone without covering the camera and fits easily into my pocket or travel pouch.

Buy: UGreen 10000mAh 22.5W compact power bank, $19.99 (normally $29.99).

I never know when an idea will strike, or when I need to jot down a few meeting notes on the go. The Moleskine XL leather-bound hardcover notebook feels luxurious and has large pages that make writing easier.

Buy: Moleskine classic hardcover XL notebook, $24.75.

Dry air on planes can rapidly dry out your lips — hydration is key. I love that the Supergoop lip balm moisturizes without feeling sticky, plus it doubles as sun protection.

Sources indicate that buy: Supergoop! Play Lip Balm SPF 30, $9.60 (normally $12).
