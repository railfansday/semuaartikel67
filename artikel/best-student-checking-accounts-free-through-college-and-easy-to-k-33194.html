Pick Chase Secure Banking instead if you want a branch near campus, and route $250 a month of direct deposits there to keep it free after 24.

It has been revealed that at major banks, a student’s free checking often lasts only until a set age. Wells Fargo’s Everyday Checking, which the bank lists as best for college students, waives its $15 monthly fee for owners 17 to 24, but once you turn 25, your age no longer waives it. An account with no monthly fee at any age avoids that catch, so you won’t need to switch banks after graduation.
