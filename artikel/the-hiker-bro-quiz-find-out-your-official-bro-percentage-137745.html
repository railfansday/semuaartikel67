Eliza “Swerve” Sweeney’s Hiker Bro Quiz started with a list of habits she and a friend kept noticing among their fellow PCT hikers, from skipping lunch breaks to knowing their exact hiking pace.

It has been revealed that after spending roughly two months hiking with a group of five guys, including through the Sierra, Swerve met another hiker, Matza, in Northern California. As they compared stories, they realized they’d seen many of the same behaviors in different hiking partners.

They turned those observations into a list, then a quiz, which they shared with other hikers on the PCT.

Industry observers note that swerve brought the quiz to Backpacker Radio episode 379, where Elise and Badger took it on the show. Below, you can take it yourself and see how your hiking habits compare.
