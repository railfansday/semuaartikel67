Investment firm Roundhill has suddenly terminated plans to launch two fresh XRP- and Solana (SOL)-based exchange-traded funds (ETFs).

This decision comes after over a year of regulatory filings and multiple delays.

Recent findings suggest that as per the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filings on Nov. 18, Roundhill has officially withdrawn plans to launch the Roundhill XRP Covered Call Strategy ETF and Roundhill Solana Covered Call Strategy ETF.

Industry observers note that interestingly, it submitted the applications for these two funds on Oct. 17, 2025. Therefore, these two funds had been awaiting launch for over 13 months.

As per the latest regulatory filings, Roundhill has been consistently delaying the effective dates for these two funds.

However, instead of launching them, the investment firm has decided to drop both products.

In its requests to withdraw the filings for these funds, Roundhill revealed that it "has no further intention to pursue effectiveness of the Fund." The firm noted that it had not sold any shares under the registrations for these two funds.

Intriguingly, Roundhill failed to elaborate on the sudden decision to axe its plans to launch these two ETFs.

It has been revealed that roundhill was planning to launch income-generating XRP and Solana ETFs using a covered call strategy. In such a strategy, investors sell options to earn premiums while also maintaining exposure to the price movements of XRP and Solana.

Unlike spot-based ETFs, these funds would not have held the cryptocurrencies directly. Instead, they would have used options written against existing cryptocurrency ETFs.

A covered call strategy can be useful for generating regular income. However, it can also cap profits during strong market rallies.

In a recent development, notably, there has been relatively low demand for income-generating cryptocurrency investment products. This raises concerns about investors' interest in these ETFs.

Surprisingly, this decision came amid substantial net inflows into spot-based XRP and Solana ETFs.

Sources indicate that U.S. spot XRP and Solana ETFs recorded net inflows of roughly $969 million between May and October 2026, according to data from SoSoValue.

Overall, these spot ETFs held around $3.26 billion in assets under management as of the end of October.
