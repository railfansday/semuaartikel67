Early 2000s kids grew up with the dark comedy and bizarre horror of Invader Zim, so it’s no surprise that an entire generation of now-adults has developed a taste for a similarly unconventional brand of horror. Now, fans who enjoy stories that blend multiple subgenres, from occult horror to animal-centric protagonists and dark comedy, have a newly introduced four-part series to look forward to, complete with official artwork from none other than Invader Zim creator Jhonen Vasquez.

On October 21, 2026, Emmy Award-winning screenwriter Philip Gelatt (LOVE, DEATH & ROBOTS), acclaimed filmmaker David Yarovesky (BRIGHTBURN), and visionary artist Adam Pollina (X-FORCE) released their fresh four-part horror series, It Demands Sacrifice, and it’s truly unlike any horror series fans have experienced before.

Instead of featuring a typical human protagonist, the series introduces readers to Bronson, the youngest and smallest member of a close-knit family of goats living on a quiet farm. However, their peaceful lives are turned upside down when “a drug-fueled, sex-obsessed satanic hippie murder cult descends on the farm, slaughtering the family who raised and protected the goats before abducting Bronson and his loved ones.”.

It has been revealed that the official synopsis for the series continues, teasing: “Deep in the wilderness, beneath a blood-red moon, hundreds of cult followers gather for an ancient ritual. Their purpose: to sacrifice the goats and summon a ravenous demon from beyond. Now, the fate of his family rests on the shoulders of the one creature least prepared to save them.”.

While the first installment of this newly introduced horror series is already available to read, fans still have three more chapters to look forward to, with It Demands Sacrifice #2 set to arrive on December 23, 2026. However, to tide fans over, Bad Idea Comics has shared a brand-new cover for the upcoming installment from none other than celebrated comic artist, animator, and screenwriter Jhonen Vasquez, the creator of Invader Zim.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to being the legendary creator of Invader Zim, Vasquez is known for his hit comic book debut, Johnny the Homicidal Maniac, and its hit sequel, Squee!, which earned him two Eisner Award nominations. Notably, he also took home the International Horror Guild Award for the beloved follow-up, I Feel Sick. Now, Vasquez is joining the star-studded cast of A-list creators contributing to It Demands Sacrifice with a simply awesome variant cover for issue #2.

This variant cover, which can be seen above, features the series' protagonist, Bronson, curled up in the fetal position, surrounded by a pop-art-esque background, with the goat lying in a puddle of hot-pink blood. The cover captures the series' eerie yet somewhat whimsical vibe while putting Vasquez's iconic art style on full display. However, if the synopsis and Vasquez's cover art aren't enough to convince you that this is a must-read series, then a preview of Pollina and Dean White's interior artwork for problem #2 just might:.

According to recent reports, it Demands Sacrifice #1 is an oversized, 48-page glitch now available for $5.99. Issue #2, which will run 32 pages, will be available for the same price in December and will see this drugged-out-hippie-murder-cultist nightmare escalate. The synopsis for issue #2 teases that Bronson, desperate to find his family, finds himself in a house of horrors, where all he encounters are Satanic revelers lost in a depraved frenzy of drugs, blood, sex, and violence.

So, if you're in the mood for some seriously freaky horror, It Demands Sacrifice needs to be bumped up on your TBR list.

It Demands Sacrifice #2 from BAD IDEA COMICS will be available on December 23, 2026!
