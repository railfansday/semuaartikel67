As teased towards the end of last week, The Blood of Dawnwalker studio Rebel Wolves has updated its PS5 RPG with a latest game+ mode, available to download now.

It's a 4.157GB download, and the option to play the game again whilst carrying over your level, skills, and inventory is the main attraction.

Having finished the RPG's epilogue, you'll unlock newly introduced game+. The level cap is raised to 100 in your second playthrough, and the difficulty scaling tied to each region is removed. Enemy levels have been adjusted, and they shall have damage multipliers so combat still presents a challenge.

According to recent reports, "In NG+, armor, swords, and accessories will have new versions with updated stats while keeping their original names and icons," the blog post reveals. "You can easily recognize these items by their new frames and the NG+ label next to their names (and for NG+ legendary items – a unique red frame).".

According to recent reports, fresh Game+ becomes a new option on the main menu, and begins after selecting your base game save and difficulty level. You'll then get all your gear at the start of the Withering Away quest in the prologue.
