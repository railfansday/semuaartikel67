Tech Report: Coinbase Reveals COIN Buybacks as It Capitalizes on AI

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong remarked on X that the manufacturer is repurchasing stock in the fourth quarter because investors are overlooking the potential of the payments business for AI agents.

"Good to take advantage of market dislocations," said Armstrong.

It has been revealed that the company is buying back stock using funds previously authorized for repurchases. He didn't specify the number of COIN shares, the price or the amount spent.

At the start of the quarter, Coinbase had $1.96 billion left to repurchase stock. Over the prior three months, it repurchased 37,995 shares for a total cost of $5.3 million, at an average price of $139.91. All repurchases occurred in August, according to its third-quarter report.

Recent findings suggest that coinbase's board of directors initially authorized stock buybacks of up to $1 billion in October 2024. A year later, the buyback authorization was doubled. The company also received an option to repurchase some outstanding debt.

The board increased the buyback limit again in January 2026, bringing the total authorization to $4 billion.

Some buybacks have been carried out to cancel stock issued as employee compensation. By the end of September, the manufacturer had repurchased 10.2 million Class A shares and canceled more than 85% of the stock issued for employee compensation since the fourth quarter of 2024. This is according to an earnings presentation.

Armstrong envisions a scenario where crypto and AI are involved in different aspects of the same transaction.

It has been revealed that for example, AI agents might help consumers make financial decisions and follow their instructions. Then, a crypto network may be used to transfer funds.

Coinbase wants to provide accounts, payment services, and marketplaces for humans and AI agents to use.

According to recent reports, "With AI, everybody has a private banker in your pocket that can help you make better financial decisions," Armstrong posted on Nov. 25.

In a recent development, coinbase added its wallet and payment services to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Payments. This offers a way for developers to build agents on Amazon and allow the software to discover paid services. Agents can make small payments when necessary.

Now Armstrong is inviting developers to add their specialized AI models to Agentic.Market.

Recent findings suggest that according to Armstrong, an AI agent can choose which specialist to hire for a task based on its capabilities and price. Coinbase provides the platform where agents can find specialists and pay them.
