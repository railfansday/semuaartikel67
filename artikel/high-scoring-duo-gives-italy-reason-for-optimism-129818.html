Francesco Pio Esposito and Alphadjo Cisse have already scored seven and nine goals, respectively, this season, giving the Azzurri reason for hope ahead of the 2028 Euros and the 2030 World Cup.

They are not just young; they are also incredibly prolific, and their current form gives the Azzurri reason to be optimistic in the coming years.

Inter’s Esposito and Milan’s Cisse have been in fantastic form this season, netting seven and nine goals across all competitions, attracting increasing attention from betting sites with welcome bonus.

Ironically, neither is a regular starter for their clubs, yet Pio and Alpha, as the fans call them, find the net almost every time they take to the pitch.

Sources indicate that esposito, 21, has already become Italy’s starting striker and has scored ten goals in 15 international appearances. Cisse made his senior debut with the Azzurri in a 4-1 loss to Belgium in November, but is currently Italy’s top scorer across all competitions, with nine goals in 14 matches.

Both Esposito and Cisse scored during the past weekend and were included in Football Italia’s Italian TOTW.

Football Italia also attended Cisse’s first press conference as an Italy national team player during the latest international break.

“I can play in every position. I don’t know if I can say which one I prefer, but I can play anywhere in attacking areas,” he said.

Recent findings suggest that “Being here is wonderful and comes with great responsibility. We’ve only been here a few days, but the impact has been very positive. We’ll have the chance to get to know each other even better in the coming days.

“I’m very ambitious, and playing for the national team is a source of pride,” the 20-year-old added.

It has been revealed that “As I stated before, I think I have broad shoulders. Many players on the national team have been here a long time and have also won the European Championship. I respect them, and we have to take things step by step.”.

Surely, a potential strike partnership with Pio Esposito already excites Italy fans massively, especially now that Roberto Mancini’s upcoming project is starting to take shape.

Italy have been eliminated from the Nations League, but still earned ten points, finishing the group phase with one draw and one win against France, one of the world’s best teams.

In recent years, even after winning the Euros in 2021, La Nazionale has struggled to find a proven, prolific striker, but this promising attacking duo offers reason for optimism in the years to come.

Esposito and Pio’s next target will be to score enough goals to help Italy qualify for the Euros, before entering a crucial World Cup qualifying campaign that will need to send the Azzurri back to the World Cup for the first time since 2014.
