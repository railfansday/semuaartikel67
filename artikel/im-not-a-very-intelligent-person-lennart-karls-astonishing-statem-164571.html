Great hope of Bayern Munich, Lennart Karl is having a mixed start to the season despite 3 goals and 2 assists in 15 matches. Often a substitute, the 18-year-old attacking midfielder continues to learn. The German international (5 caps) spoke in a meeting with Bavarian fans in South Tyrol and admitted that he would not have known what to do with his life outside of football in a rather surprising way.

Sources indicate that “I didn’t have a plan B. I still wouldn’t know what to do. I’m not a very intelligent person. I have to be honest. For me, there was only one plan. My parents told me to finish my studies. I refused. I got up every morning with only one desire: to play football.“, he declared in comments collected by Sky Germany.
