📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

There are few things more anxiety-inducing than creating a well-crafted social media post and instantly finding it has a typo in it. Instagram Stories now lets you fix that.

Sources indicate that thanks to a recent update that includes several platform improvements, Instagram creators can edit their Stories up to 15 minutes after posting. You no longer have to start from scratch, and the edits won’t tank your engagement or sacrifice performance. To make changes to text, music, stickers, drawings or their placement or style, simply tap the menu at the bottom or the More button on your story to find the edit option. Instagram states that you won’t lose views, likes or replies as a result. After the 15-minute window closes, your Story can’t be changed without deleting it.

It has been revealed that the edit option is a minor enhancement but a welcome enhancement for those who regularly post on Instagram, said CNET’s Abrar Al-Heeti. It’s especially a plus if you always find something to nitpick after you set it live, she said.

Al-Heeti also pointed to a recent update that lets you change a song on a published post. “It’s great to see Instagram adding more features that let you tweak something after it’s reached your audience, without losing out on any existing views or engagement.”.

The Stories edit feature is slowly rolling out to all accounts, so first check whether your app is running the latest version.

In addition, Instagram is adding upcoming stickers with a new sticker sheet, photo dumps that let you choose multiple pics more easily and new backgrounds including blur, colors and gradients.

Earlier this week, Meta unveiled the Instagram-based chat app called Direct, which is presently in testing in Thailand and Turkey.
