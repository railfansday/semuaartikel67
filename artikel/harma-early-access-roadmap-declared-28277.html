Publisher Astrolabe Games and developer INDIRECT SHINE have announced the Early Access roadmap for Roguelike cooperative deckbuilder HARMA, which is now available for PC via Steam.

The Early Access release features more than 200 hours of content and day-one support for up to four players in online cooperative play. Over the course of Early Access, the game will be updated with ongoing balance adjustments and a steady stream of newly introduced content.

Recent findings suggest that to celebrate the Early Access launch, HARMA is also hosting a “World-First Highest Difficulty Clear” competition from November 17 to November 25. Players can compete solo or team up in groups of up to four to conquer the game’s highest difficulty, “Rift Level 15,” as soon as possible. The first completionists in each category will win “big prizes.”.
