Riccardo Calafiori admits he’s having the ‘best’ moment of his career at Arsenal, sees the Italy project as a ‘long-term journey’ and confirms he’d like to return to Roma one day.

Italy international Calafiori received an award from Rome Mayor Roberto Gualtieri on Tuesday. Gualtieri presented the Arsenal defender with a special plaque “for the outstanding results achieved” in 2026, including Arsenal’s Premier League title.

“I want to thank Mayor Gualtieri. I felt quite emotional walking in here today,” Calafiori told reporters via Gazzetta.

According to recent reports, “Thank you for this recognition, which fills me with pride. I always try to represent my Roman roots abroad. Whenever I come back, it’s always a great emotion.”.

Talking about his spell at Arsenal, the Italian defender added: “I think it’s the best moment of my career. I feel like a leader in the dressing room, and I try to help my teammates and the younger players.”.

Born in Rome and a product of the Roma academy, Calafiori already remarked in the past that he’d like to play for the Giallorossi again one day.

During today’s award ceremony, he confirmed: “I hope to win as much as possible over the coming years. Then, in the more distant future, as I’ve always stated, I would love to return to Roma.”.

Calafiori received a special plaque, but he also had a gift for Rome’s Mayor: a signed Arsenal away jersey.

“I spoke with the Mayor about the new stadium project, and I can’t wait to see it completed,” the defender mentioned.

In a recent development, calafiori was also asked his thoughts on Roma’s results this season: “Everyone speaks highly of Gasperini and his ability to get the best out of players. I hope he can achieve the best possible results by the end of the season,” he replied.

Calafiori started Italy’s last two games against France and Belgium, securing a 3-1 win against Les Bleus, but also suffering a 4-1 loss away in Belgium.

Recent findings suggest that “We are sorry about the elimination from the Nations League, but there’s a desire to start again from certain principles. It’s a long-term journey,” he concluded.
