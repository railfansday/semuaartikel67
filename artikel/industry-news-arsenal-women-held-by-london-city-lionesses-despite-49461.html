Arsenal Women missed the chance to end their frustrating run of draws at home as London City Lionesses came from behind to earn a 1-1 draw at the Emirates Stadium.

Recent findings suggest that frida Maanum gave the Gunners the lead in an exciting start to the game. But Isobel Goodwin equalised almost straight away. Despite creating plenty of chances, Renée Slegers’ side failed to find a second goal.

Industry observers note that arsenal have now lost their last five WSL games at the Emirates. The results were yet another frustrating afternoon for the defending champions, who put in a strong display against Barcelona in Europe earlier in the week.

Arsenal started on a high note, creating some dangerous chances with their quick play. Emily Fox and Lisa Baum worked really well together down the right, and Ona Batlle had a great chance from the edge of the box.

In a recent development, as the pressure mounted, Fox surged forward and played a perfectly weighted through ball to Alessia Russo. The striker turned and played the ball back to Maanum, who rounded off her impressive comeback from injury by finishing.

Recent findings suggest that arsenal had little time to rejoice. London City equalised just a minute later as Goodwin found herself in space to fire past Misa Rodríguez.

In a recent development, the visitors looked more confident after equalising, but Mary Earps was proving a tough ask for Arsenal to score past. The ex-Manchester United shot-stopper kept out Baum and made some key stops from Arsenal’s efforts.

Rodriguez also had to make a key stop before half-time as she got a hand to Daniela Arques Lazaro’s powerful strike to keep the scores level.

The Gunners continued their attacking play but were made to work hard by a high pressing side from London City.

According to recent reports, but Arsenal still managed to create some chances. Caitlin Foord put Earps under pressure and Maanum and Baum combined before the latter saw her curl just wide.

London City posed a threat too. With Kadidiatou Diani and Grace Geyoro causing defensive problems for Arsenal, Geyoro fired into the side netting.

Delphine Cascarino had the game’s defining moment of the second half, hitting the crossbar from outside the box. However, Arsenal were lucky to escape and Maanum came close to winning the game, hitting the woodwork from a dangerous counter.

In a recent development, slegers brought on Kyra Cooney-Cross and Olivia Smith in the 72nd minute to provide some new energy. Smith looked lively upon entering the game, but Earps denied her efforts and Batlle’s cross.

Emily Fox had to be substituted due to injury in the final moments of the game, but Slegers later indicated that she would be okay.

According to recent reports, the Gunners were unable to find a winning goal and will now turn their attention to their Champions League clash on Thursday with Lyon. They will then face West Ham at home in the WSL.
