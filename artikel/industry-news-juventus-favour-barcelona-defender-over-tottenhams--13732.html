Il Corriere dello Sport reports that Barcelona’s Alejandro Balde is Juventus’ top left-back target for January, but the Bianconeri are also looking to Lorenzo Bernasconi for 2027-28.

Juventus are expected to bring in some reinforcements during the January transfer window, including a new left-back.

Corriere dello Sport reports that Barcelona’s Balde is the Old Lady’s top priority in that position, with the Serie A giants hoping to sign the Spaniard on a six-month loan.

Gazzetta had already reported on Juventus’ interest in Balde, describing the Spaniard as a “dream target” for the Bianconeri.

According to CorSport, Juventus prefer Balde over Rayan Ait-Nouri (Man City), Juan Miranda (Bologna) and Destiny Udogie (Tottenham), while Bernasconi is their top target for the 2027-28 season.

Juventus already held talks with Atalanta last summer, and Bernasconi signed a contract extension with La Dea just a few hours ago; the club confirmed the new deal yesterday, Monday, November 16.

According to Corriere dello Sport, Atalanta already demanded a fee close to €30m for Bernasconi last summer, so if Juventus can’t afford him in January, they’ll try to get Balde on loan and then resume talks with La Dea at the end of the season.
