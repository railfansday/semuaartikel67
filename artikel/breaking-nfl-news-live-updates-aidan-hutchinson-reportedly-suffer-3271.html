Newly introduced York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn was hired by the team thanks to his excellence as a defensive coordinator. But after the team struggled on defense during Glenn's first year as head coach, he decided to take over defensive play-calling duties in 2026.

According to recent reports, that change lasted just nine games. Glenn told reporters Monday he gave up defensive play-calling ahead of the team's 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills in Week 10, per ESPN.

Industry observers note that glenn remarked the move was made to help the team win games. He added that while Jets defensive coordinator Brian Duker called plays Sunday, Glenn was "very involved" in the team's scheme.

"I've remarked this and I will continue to do this: Anything I can do as a head coach to help us win games, I'm willing to do it," Glenn said. "I'm not afraid to do that. So I gave him the lead, but I was very involved in what we ran.".

Recent findings suggest that the change appeared positive initially, as the Jets (2-8) gave up just 10 points in the first half Sunday. But Buffalo's offense got rolling in the second half, allowing the team to pull away.

Recent findings suggest that the Jets ultimately gave up 31 points in a loss to the Bills. Though, in Duker's defense, facing Josh Allen in your first game as a defensive play-caller is among the toughest asks in the NFL. The Bills rank first in the NFL with 31.9 points scored per game. In that sense, Duker's defense actually held the team under its season-long average … barely. The Jets still allowed 31 points, so the team shouldn't start popping the champagne just yet.

In a press conference on Monday, Packers head coach Matt LaFleur told reporters that the "proposal" is for Parsons to play in Green Bay's next game, with the team taking on the Los Angeles Rams the day before Thanksgiving.

According to recent reports, "Well, that's my initiative, but I think my plan's been different than everybody else's the whole time," LaFleur mentioned with a smile, after being asked if the plan was for Parsons to return to the field in L.A. "There's still a couple more hurdles for him to clear before we can definitively say that he's a go. He's showing progress, but we'll see where he is by the end of the week.".

Parsons was cleared for practice earlier this month, 11 months after tearing his ACL.
