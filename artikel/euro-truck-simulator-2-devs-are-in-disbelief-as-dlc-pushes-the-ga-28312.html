Euro Truck Simulator 2 devs are in disbelief as DLC pushes the game over 100,000 concurrent players

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Even as a longtime fan of SCS Software's line of truck simulators, I still have a little difficulty believing just how popular they've remained over the past decade. American Truck Simulator just hit a fresh concurrent player record in September thanks to a fresh bit of DLC that lets you escape the trucking grind, and now Euro Truck Simulator 2 has done the same on a much more impressive scale.

Recent findings suggest that on November 12, the Coaches DLC launched for Euro Truck Simulator 2. For my fellow Americans, you can translate "coaches" to "buses" – this is an expansion all about getting behind the wheel of a public transportation vehicle, picking up passengers, and taking them on long-distance trips across Europe. Like with American Truck Simulator's Road Trip DLC, it offers a whole newly introduced way to explore a now-massive map, and fans have been playing it in droves.

It has been revealed that euro Truck Simulator 2 reached a newly introduced all-time Steam concurrent player record of 101,783 on November 15, as SteamDB shows. That's a massive jump over the previous record of 73,887, set back in February, and genuinely incredible for a game that was first released in 2012.

According to recent reports, 14 years on Steam, with a dedicated community that's passionate, creative & has always supported us 🙏 We've smashed our record (again!) today with 101,000+ concurrent players on Steam! Thank you, everyone! ❤️ We're excited for the future of Euro Truck Simulator 2 🚛🚌 https://t.co/IYfE2x4XM5November 15, 2026.

It has been revealed that "14 years on Steam, with a dedicated community that's passionate, creative & has always supported us," community manager Alex Blackman enthuses in a tweet. "We've smashed our record (again!) today with 101,000+ concurrent players on Steam! Thank you, everyone!".

In a recent development, in fact, Blackman himself might offer a good example of just how dedicated that community is. He founded TruckersFM, an online radio station built for truck sim players and run by a dedicated team of volunteer DJs. While Blackman has moved to work for SCS itself, TruckersFM is still going strong over a decade after it was founded. Who knows what that community will look like in another decade's time?
