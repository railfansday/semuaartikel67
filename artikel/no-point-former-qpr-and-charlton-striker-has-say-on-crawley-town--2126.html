That’s the view of former QPR and Charlton striker Conor Washington as he has his say on Crawley Town’s next managerial appointment.

Reds owners have yet to name a permanent successor for Colin Kazim-Richards, who was sacked on October 21 following a poor start to the season which has sees them rock bottom of League Two.

Kevin Blackwell has been in charge on an interim basis but Reds fans are eagerly anticipating who will be the next permanent boss as they look to climb the table.

But Washington, in his latest exclusive EFL column for BestBettingBonuses.co.uk, claims there is a bigger picture at play.

“I think the bigger question for me is around the overall football strategy at the club,” he noted. “With the structure they've got, they either need to decide on a clear way they want the club to play, recruit players who fit that and then appoint managers who can implement it, or they need to appoint a manager with a really clear playing style and back him with recruitment that suits what he's trying to do. Both approaches can work, but you can't really keep moving between the two.

"Otherwise you end up with players recruited for one manager being asked to play a completely different way under the next one.

“From what Colin Kazim-Richards said in the summer, there was clearly an attempt to recruit players specifically for his ideas, but now they're potentially looking for another manager just a few months later. That's where the problems can start.”.

And he added that it’s a massive job for whoever comes in. He mentioned: “I think whoever comes in needs to be really clear about what they've inherited, what the players are actually capable of and how they can get the best out of them.

"There's no point appointing someone because you like their philosophy if the squad isn't equipped to carry it out, particularly when you're fighting to stay in the league.”.

Reds face Fleetwood Town at home on Saturday following their international break.
