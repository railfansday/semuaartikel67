Sources indicate that tarik Skubal is going to be a Fresh York Met. The two-time Cy Young winner is joining the Mets on a nine-year, $450 million contract that will keep him in Flushing through 2035. That contract breaks the record for both total value and AAV for a pitcher (excluding Shohei Ohtani).

A pre-Thanksgiving frenzy, meanwhile, landed Trevor Rogers and NPB slugger Teruaki Sato with the Cubs,. ALCS MVP Freddy Peralta with the Orioles and Kaima Taira with the Blue Jays.

Industry observers note that now, the rest of the free agent class, including solid options like Jazz Chisholm Jr., Randy Arozarena and Seiya Suzuki, are racing against the clock before the expected lockout after the CBA expires at 11:59 p.m. on Dec. 1.
