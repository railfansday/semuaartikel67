With the holiday season fast approaching, headphones worth hundreds of dollars are at their record-low prices of the season, but it's a pair of $59 Sony headphones that have sold out twice since their release in early November.

That's simply the retro appeal of the Sony MDR-3R headphones, Sony's modernized version of the classic Walkman style. They don't come with noise cancellation or a Bluetooth connection, but they do come with a striking look. And two different colored pairs of ear cushions (blue and orange) for added customization. As of Nov. 25, they're back in stock at Sony's website and Best Buy, though they remain completely sold out at Amazon. It has been revealed that based on the amount of compliments on the design I received while testing them, I'm not surprised these have been popular enough to sell out. They're undeniably cool, even when plugged into an iPhone. Plus, they make listening to classic '80s tracks feel just that much better. Soundscape-wise, this on-ear design isn't going to give you the best performance of what's available, but it's surprisingly clear (if a little on the flatter side). The USB-C connection makes them simple to connect to modern devices, and the in-line remote and built-in mic are both great for handling playback and calls.

At $59.99, they're certainly not the fanciest headphones, but they're some of the coolest. While I can't say whether or not they'll go on sale for Black Friday, what I can say is if you want to guarantee having a pair in hand for Christmas, grabbing them while they're in stock now isn't a bad idea.
