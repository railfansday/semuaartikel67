Testing for my Jabees PEACE Duo Pillow Speaker review came at the right time. After wearing too thick and too bulky earbuds to bed every night, my ear canals had become increasingly sore. The contact-free Peace Duo is designed to circumvent this matter, but I was a little skeptical. I'd never tested a pillow speaker before and worried it would feel uncomfortable.

However, I need soothing white noise and soundscapes to sleep, and the Peace Duo Pillow Speaker promises to deliver just that via bone-conduction technology, transmitting audio directly through your pillow as soon as you rest your head on it.

Sources indicate that from the first night of use, I was pleasantly surprised by the feel of this speaker. Or, more accurately, the lack thereof. I couldn't feel the ultra-thin, side sleeper-friendly speaker through my pillow at all.

I was also blown away by how private the audio was; when I take my head off my pillow, I can't hear a single sound from the speaker. This private listening makes it great for those sharing a room with a partner or roommate.

Sources indicate that its Bluetooth connectivity means I can listen to any soundscape or ambient noise I want. I've been enjoying this free hidden iPhone background noise feature in particular lately.

Industry observers note that however, back sleepers and anyone using a memory foam or latex foam pillow may find the volume muffled or too quiet. Also, the audio isn't clear enough to follow guided sleep meditations or sleep stories.
