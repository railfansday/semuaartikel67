Manchester United may still be reeling from a 4-0 shellacking at Anfield on Sunday, but Mauricio Pochettino’s side return to action on Wednesday as they travel to Sporting in the Champions League.

Unlike their Premier League form, one win in 11 games leaving Man Utd in the bottom three, the Red Devils have fared decently enough in Europe, with two wins from four league phase fixtures.

However, both of those victories came at Old Trafford - with Man Utd well beaten by both Atletico Madrid and Italian outfit Como on the continent.

Pochettino, meanwhile, does not have long to get his side on song and will hope to pick up a first win of his rein here.

Sporting are no mugs when it comes to the Champions League this term - winning all but one of their four games so far.

The only blemish on their record was a somewhat surprising defeat to Austrian outfit LASK at home, giving United hope they can get something on their travels on Wednesday night.

TV channel: In the UK, the game will be televised live on TNT Sports. Coverage starts at 7pm GMT on TNT Sports 1 ahead of kick-off at 8pm.
