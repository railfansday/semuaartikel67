How To Play GTA 6 On Your PC Or Phone Via Remote Play On Xbox

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 can be streamed via Xbox Cloud Gaming, and Microsoft has also now confirmed support for Remote Play on Xbox. This means you can play GTA 6 on your PC, smartphone, or a device like Xbox Rog Ally. Remote Play is an Xbox service that lets you stream games from your Xbox Series X|S to a device you might already have, and it's easy to get working. From your console, select Settings then Devices & connections then Remote features. From there, check the box for Enable remote features. Xbox Remote Play also has a "test" feature so you can preview the experience in your home environment before going further. From there, you can play GTA 6 from a mobile device by accessing the Xbox Remote Play website, selecting the console you want to connect to, and then the device. To play GTA 6 on your PC or Xbox Rog Ally, owners can open the Xbox app for PC, click on their profile, then select Accessories & Remote Play and then select Remote Play to choose the device to play on. Additionally, people have spotted that GTA 6's store page on the PlayStation Store now mentions that Remote Play is supported and that PlayStation 5 and PlayStation Portal owners can use cloud streaming if they have a Premium membership. Right now, you need a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S to stream GTA 6, but some are dreaming of the day when you can simply stream the game to your PC or phone without any expensive hardware. That day may eventually come for GTA 6, as Take-Two's own CEO has remarked the rising cost of gaming hardware is not a good thing and that streaming games could be a massive business very soon. “If you believe in streaming, and to be clear, we really do, and I’m happy to put a timeline on it. I think we’ll be in commercial streaming mode within three years, and by commercial, I mean low latency," Strauss Zelnick said. "That can 10x the effective installed base. Now, it doesn’t mean we’re going to 10x our revenue–and we wouldn’t project that we would–but because obviously your avid consumers already had access to video game machines. But I do think it creates great opportunity for titles that are broadly desirable, even outside of core markets.” GTA 6 is doing just fine without the potential expanded reach of streaming. After all, the game sold 32.5 million copies before launch and generated close to $4 billion in revenue by day one. Even if GTA 6 doesn't add support for full streaming without a console, the game is expected to expand its reach in a major way in 2027 with an expected PC release. Beyond that, GTA 6 is reportedly going to add an online mode in 2027 as well, and it could be bigger than Fortnite.
