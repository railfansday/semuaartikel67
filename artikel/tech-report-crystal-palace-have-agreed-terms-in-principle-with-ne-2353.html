On Thursday (16 November 2026), Crystal Palace sacked Pierre Sage.

The Selhurst Park club with official announcement on that Thursday afternoon.

Crystal Palace only though the second Premier League club to sack their manager during this 2026-27 season, as Manchester United beat them by a couple of hours by dismissing Michael Carrick.

We have since seen Man U announce Mauricio Pochettino as their new manager and now Crystal Palace set to name their replacement.

Monday has seen Sky Sports reveal that their information is that Crystal Palace ‘have agreed terms in principle’ with Thomas Frank.

This has been backed up by the likes of The Athletic, who report that Crystal Palace are finalising the deal and getting things in place whereby Frank’s preferred backroom team will also come into Selhurst Park.

Thomas Frank did a brilliant job for years at Brentford before becoming yet another manager to fail at Tottenham.

He will head into Crystal Palace to find a team rock bottom of the Premier League with only seven points after ten matches.

International breaks are always a popular time for sacking Premier League managers with a bit of time to appoint a replacement before the league games resume.

A defeat in the tenth round of EPL matches for any of the Premier League managers most at risk was sure to put them in immediate danger.

With Pierre Sage and Crystal Palace the only ones towards the bottom to lose that weekend, it seemingly sealed that Palace manager’s fate.
