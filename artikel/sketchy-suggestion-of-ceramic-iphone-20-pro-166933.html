A sketchy rumor is doing the rounds suggesting that Apple is working on a ceramic edition of the iPhone 20 Pro.

Ceramic? For a phone? C’mon. Sketchy is an understatement.

There are no details, and while one concept image highlights a ceramic back, this seems a rather unlikely possibility ….

Fixed Focus Digital is one of those pointing to the possibility, stating that they have seen ceramic components for next year’s flagship iPhone. An image has also been shared by Tech Overwatch, which is very clearly a render, simply imagining what it might look like.

Details are very vague, so it’s possible that the whole thing is a misunderstanding based on Apple’s Ceramic Shield protection for the display and glass back, as seen on the iPhone Air.

Recent findings suggest that given that the iPhone 20 Pro will itself be a very premium device and involve a completely upcoming production line setup, it seems rather unlikely Apple would want to either launch a super-premium version or experiment with two different versions at the same time.
