‘Fall now, rally later’ – Ripple CEO sees crypto rebound after Anthropic IPO

On the 19th of November, Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said in an interview with CNBC that the recent crypto market sell-off is a result of capital rotating into IPOs like Anthropic. However, he does expect the markets to rebound once the listing is complete.

Still on-chain data for XRP suggests that despite on-chain indicators looking bullish, the altcoin is struggling to move beyond the $1 psychological level.

Garlinghouse argued that the recent bloodbath across digital assets is less about a fundamental collapse in crypto and more about investors shifting funds to chase the AI boom.

He pointed to a previous sell-off in the market, acknowledging,.

It has been revealed that we also saw a sell-off very similar when SpaceX went public. What happens is… people are taking liquidity out of a very liquid market in crypto, including Bitcoin and XRP, and moving it into SpaceX. The exact same thing happened coming into the Athropic IPO… What we saw after the SpaceX IPO is a rally in the crypto market.

He believes that the same story will play out with the Anthropic IPO too.

Sources indicate that garlinghouse’s bullish thesis does have a solid footing. Most notably, CryptoQuant’s data indicates that the XRP Exchange Reserve on Binance has been declining over the last thirty days.

In late October 2026, Binance held roughly 2.7 billion XRP, but the figure dropped to 2.47 billion XRP as of the 19th of November.

When Exchange Reserve falls, it typically signals that investors are moving coins to self-custody, reducing the immediate supply available for selling.

Despite the tightening supply on exchanges, XRP’s price action remains volatile. At press time, XRP was trading at $0.9834, down 1.4% in the last 24 hours.

Industry observers note that the chart further revealed a market struggling to find firm footing. XRP managed to hold above $1 during November 12-14 before a steep drop dragged it down to the $0.90 region.

The last 24 hours saw the asset sail briefly above $1 but faced an immediate rejection, slumping back into the high $0.90s.

As XRP remains in a tug-of-war between falling exchange reserves and repeated price rejections at $1.00, investors are uncertain about its next direction.

If Garlinghouse is correct and digital assets do rally after the Anthropic IPO, the rotation will easily cause prices to soar beyond $1. However, until that macroeconomic shift occurs, XRP holders appear content to move their coins off exchanges and wait out the storm.
