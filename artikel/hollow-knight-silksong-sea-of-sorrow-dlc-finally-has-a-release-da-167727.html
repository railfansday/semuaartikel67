We had our fingers crossed that Team Cherry might reveal the release date for Hollow Knight: Silksong's first DLC in its special presentation today, and what do you know, it did!

It has been revealed that sea of Sorrow will officially launch on Switch and Switch 2 on 11th December. This is a free expansion, so all those with the base game will be able to download and dive right in from this date.

According to recent reports, team Cherry initially teased the DLC late last year, leaving us for 11 months with the vague '2026' release window. It sees Hornet dive into a brand newly introduced area of Pharloom, packed with new bosses, tools, enemies and... fishing?
