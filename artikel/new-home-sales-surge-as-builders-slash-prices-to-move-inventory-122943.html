New-Home Sales Surge as Builders Slash Prices to Move Inventory

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

New -home sales surged in October as prices dropped, according to the latest data from the U.S. Census Bureau.

Sales of fresh single-family homes hit 701,000 in October, 14.2% above what they were in September and 7.5% above October 2025. Meanwhile, the median sales price hit $391,300, 0.3% below September 2026 and 3.2% below October 2025.

Industry observers note that that was driven entirely by a surge in the South, which saw a 36.2% increase from last month and a 24.7% increase from October 2025. Northeast sales plummeted 40.0% month over month and 63.4% year over year, and the Midwest and West also saw declines.

In a recent development, this comes after new-home sales fell 0.6% month over month, 5.3% year over year in September, to 676,000. New-home sales were running 2.9% below 2025 numbers, Realtor.com® economists found. The median sales price in September for new homes fell 3.9% year over year to $400,400.

Realtor.com Senior Economist Joel Berner noted that latest home sales are still 3% below where they were in the first 10 months of 2025. And housing inventory remains low.

"Builders are lowering prices to meet demand, and the median newly introduced home sales price remains well below the median existing home sales price," Berner noted.

Industry observers note that the National Association of Home Builders found that newly introduced homes are nearly $40,000 less expensive than existing homes. Single-family housing starts rose 4% last month amid an uneven year. The latest Realtor.com quarterly construction insights report indicates that prices on new homes are holding steady, but builders are having to get creative to attract buyers.

Meanwhile, major homebuilders have reported large year-over-year declines in revenue. This week, the National Association of Home Builders/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index plunged to a 14-year low.

The tough environment has forced builders to get creative.

Sources indicate that "Builders are exercising caution, preferring to line up a buyer for their homes before starting work on them rather than building as many spec homes," Berner stated. "New construction remains a strong alternative to the existing home segment, offering savings on mortgage rates, sale prices, and total cost of ownership.".

Simon Boag, CEO of Aro Homes, says his company opted for across-the-board price cuts on its prefabricated homes, slashing about $12 million off the asking price of its overall housing stock. It ended up holding an auction to sell a batch of 10 homes, which seems to have worked. About eight of the homes found buyers.

Boag tells Realtor.com that he looks at past recessions when many buyers remained in the market, even if they weren't looking aggressively. Homebuilders have to accept lower margins, but there are buyers looking for homes.

According to recent reports, "You look at a decent homebuilder margin in the 20% range, but at 17%, you're still a viable entity," Boag says. "The business model is about, 'OK markets move all the time,' and how do you deal with that.".

For buyers, the market may have a higher barrier to entry in the high interest rate environment. That means preparation is essential.

Earlier this week, the S&P Cotality Case-Shiller Index revealed home values fell for the 16th straight month.

Jeffrey Ruben, president of home lending at WSFS Bank, expects the "tug of war" between high mortgage rates and affordability constraints limiting buyers in 2027.

"While this is a good sign for home builders, it doesn't indicate what the market reveals as a whole," Ruben said. "We are seeing the data signal movement toward a buyer's market. This is seen more clearly through existing home sales with time on market, overall sales, and total inventory.".

He stated buyers should be looking beyond standard resale listings and comparing the total monthly payment structure across both new and existing homes.

"Connecting with a lender early to evaluate builder incentives alongside traditional financing options can help buyers identify opportunities in the current market without stretching their household budget," he mentioned.
