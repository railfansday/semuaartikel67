Liverpool are presently fourth in the Premier League table.

If they finish in this position by the end of the term, they'll qualify for the Champions League again.

But, in reality, they should be aiming for a much higher finish.

They have spent over £600 million on latest players across these last couple of years and, with that amount of spending, you'd expect them to be one of the best teams in the world.

That clearly isn't the case as, around a month ago now, they were beaten 3-2 by Fenerbahce which is probably their low point of the season.

However, this latest news to emerge from Anfield could change everything.

Liverpool and Rio Ngumoha are getting closer to agreeing a latest deal.

One of the biggest stories to emerge from the 2026-27 season so far is about what's going to happen to Rio Ngumoha.

The Englishman was promoted to Liverpool's first-team squad by Arne Slot last term and, since then, he's never really looked back.

Talk of him extending his contract, which expires in 2028, has been rife since the 2026-27 season got fully underway with some rumours about him potentially switching Liverpool for Munich at some point.

Fortunately, this deal never came to fruition and now, the Reds are in the driving seat to lock him down.

And, thanks to a fresh update from TEAMtalk, that looks like exactly what's about to happen.

"Ngumoha is primed to put pen to paper on a latest and much improved contract at Anfield," they wrote.

"Bayern Munich are once again sniffing around the player amid a raft of injuries and challenges with their forward line.

"However, sources tell us Liverpool are close to finalising a new deal that will ensure Ngumoha continues to terrorise Premier League defences for years to come.".

Rio Ngumoha is the future of Liverpool's attack.

This season, Ngumoha has featured 15 times for Liverpool and has contributed to four goals.

Three of them have come as assists for his teammates and, whether he's been used on the left or right-wing, he's always put in plenty of effort.

As mentioned, Ngumoha's effort hasn't gone unnoticed with clubs such as Bayern Munich trying their hardest to strike a deal with him.

Obviously, Liverpool would never let that happen but it does prove how high the Englishman's ceiling actually is.

Right now, Ngumoha might not be a guaranteed starter but, in the future when certain players exit or Liverpool's tactics change, he's likely to be one of the first names on Liverpool's team sheet.

The former Chelsea man may only be 18 years old but, as long as Liverpool keep him around, their attack will be sorted for at least one attacker so, here's hoping these rumours of a deal being close to completion are true and that the Reds can lock their 'game-changer' down soon.
