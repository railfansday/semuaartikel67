Luis de la Fuente singles out ‘fantastic’ England star after Spain defeat

Luis de la Fuente reserved special praise for James Trafford following Spain’s 3-2 defeat to England, hailing the goalkeeper’s ‘fantastic’ display in Madrid.

England produced arguably their finest all-round performance under Thomas Tuchel to land a hefty psychological blow on the reigning world and European champions in Sunday’s Nations League classic.

The Three Lions flew out of the blocks from minute one and were duly rewarded for their efforts as captain Harry Kane converted from the spot to break the deadlock on the stroke of half-time.

Goals from Arsenal defenders Ezri Konsa and Ben White increased the deficit after the restart, with Spain all at sea before Elliot Anderson’s sending off handed the hosts a lifeline.

Mikel Oyarzabal scored with the resulting penalty before Nico Williams finished off a flowing Spanish move to set up a tense final 15 minutes at Atletico Madrid’s Metropolitano Stadium.

But ten-man England managed to keep La Roja at bay thanks largely to an inspired Trafford, who was selected over Jordan Pickford and repaid Tuchel’s faith with a series of stunning late stops to help preserve the visitors’ narrow advantage.

Trafford’s double save to keep out Fermin Lopez and Aymeric Laporte earned the Leeds United keeper immediate high fives and adulation from his team-mates.

The result brought Spain’s incredible 43-match unbeaten streak to a halt and ensured England finished top of their Nations League group having picked up 15 from a possible 18 points.

‘Positives? There are a lot of positives to find here. It’s very difficult to get here, to achieve the numbers that we’ve achieved,’ Spain boss De La Fuente said when asked about Spain’s run being brought to an end.

‘It’s something historical that we’ve done. We’ve been champions of the Nations League, the Euros, the World Cup, everything is positive.

‘But we know how to win and we know how to lose. And we have to say that we faced a rival that we managed to beat in the past but I have to say that they were the better team in the first 30 minutes.

‘I had a feeling we could get there when they went one man down but I’m really proud of this team, their hunger, their eagerness.

‘I’m convinced that the best is yet to come for this team.’.

De la Fuente made four second-half substitutions as they looked to claw their way back into the contest, with Fermin Lopez, Nico Williams, Pedri and Dean Huijsen all sent on in the Spanish capital.

But the home team were thwarted by Trafford’s heroics in the closing stages – and De la Fuente took the opportunity to applaud the ‘keeper’s outstanding showing.

‘What we wanted to do with the substitutions was try to go all out, try to generate more chances,’ he explained.

‘We did generate some chances but England’s goalkeeper has produced a fantastic performance and stopped two or three potential goals in the last ten or 15 minutes.

‘In any case, there were many alternatives but again, I want to say how fantastic England were on the night.’.

Asked what he and Tuchel had discussed shortly after the final whistle, De La Fuente replied: ‘We just spoke for a few seconds about what happened this week and we just wanted to clarify things.

‘I have no problem with him at all, all I have is admiration and respect.

‘As for the dressing room, when you are not used to losing, you look at eachother and ask, “What happened here?”, so what did I tell my players? I told them that we haven’t lost for three years and we’ve won all the vital tournaments.

‘All I wanted to do was say how proud I am of them because of this and it’s a shame we don’t have another game coming up, we have to wait until March.’.

The Spain head coach added: ‘We’ll surely come back in March with renewed energy, thinking about the Nations League final.

‘We’re going to try to get there, knowing difficult it is to get there and win a competition like this.

‘But the spirit, the intention, the ambition are all there.’.
