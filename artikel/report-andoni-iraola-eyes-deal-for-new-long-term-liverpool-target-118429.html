Julian Ward and Andoni Iraola have a lot of work to do on Liverpool's first-team squad this January.

Over £150 million was already spent on the club this summer but, since they're currently fourth in the table and find themselves seven points adrift of first-placed Arsenal, they have a bit of ground to make up.

Obviously, all of their problems can't be rectified in the winter window but, if they can fix some of their main ones, that would be spectacular.

Not only would bringing in another player or two give the Reds a boost but, it would also help them get back on track and start to get back to their best.

Liverpool see Gustavo Varela as a 'long-term target' rather than a short-term option.

Over the last few days, the transfer rumour mill has been going into overdrive.

Every single position among the Reds ranks is being scrutinised with fans seemingly rather happy with the recent performances of their club.

On Sunday, they managed to beat Manchester United 4-0 and, even though the Red Devils were down to 10 men for most of the day, Liverpool's performance was still impressive.

However, their problem areas were made clear once again with only two strikers available for Iraola to choose from.

And, of the two, one of them was Lewis Koumas who, even though he's good, he doesn't really jump out as a Premier League-winning attacker.

Because of this, rumours about the future of Liverpool's attack have emerged with Monza's Gustavo Varela now appearing on the Reds' shortlist.

However, a recent report from Football Insider has clarified just how serious Liverpool are about bringing the Portuguese striker in.

"Liverpool will not prioritise a move to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela in January, sources have told Football Insider," they wrote.

"The 21-year-old has emerged as a target for the Reds, having struck 11 goals from his opening 11 games in Serie A so far this term.

"The Reds are "keeping tabs" on the Portuguese youngster, with several top Italian clubs also having their "eyes fixed on him" at the moment.

"However, sources say that Varela is viewed as more of a long-term option at Anfield, and the club are not likely to make a move for him this winter.

"Monza are relaxed over their frontman's future for now, but they know that further interest from top clubs is inevitable if he continues to perform.".

Gustavo Varela is an interesting transfer for Liverpool to explore.

Varela is almost certainly a player that barely anyone outside of Italy will have heard of.

The Portuguese striker is the Serie A's current top goal scorer despite his team being in a relegation battle.

The strangest thing is that, he only just joined the Italian outfit during the 2026-27 summer transfer window for €2.5 million.

Before this term, he'd had a couple of poor seasons but now, he really seems to have come into his own despite only being 21 years old.

His age coupled with his keen eye for goal are two major boxes that Liverpool like to tick when looking for attackers and, if the fee that Monza want is reasonable, there's no reason why a deal couldn't be explored.

Obviously, there's no point going for him in January as, the Reds need to see how good Hugo Ekitike is first but, as a potential option for the future, Varela looks like a fairly solid option.
