After the better part of 7 years, Samsung’s mobile division has a new chief and it’s Choi Won-Joon, who is already off to a great start with the successful launch of Galaxy Z Fold 8.

In a recent development, in an announcement today, Samsung confirmed some shuffling of its executive team, most notably with the end of TM Roh’s tenure as the head of Samsung’s MX business unit. In other words, there’s a fresh captain at the helm for Samsung’s mobile division, and it’s Choi Won-Joon.

Won-Joon has been the Chief Operating Officer of Samsung’s MX unit since March of 2025, and more recently oversaw the launch of Galaxy S26 and Galaxy Z Fold 8, appearing on stage at both launch events and in behind-the-scenes meetings with the press.

It has been revealed that choi Won-joon, Head of the MX Business Unit at Samsung Electronics’ DX Division, has held positions such as Head of the Next-Generation Product Development Team, Head of the Strategic Product Development Team, and Head of Development at the MX Business Unit. Since being appointed President and Chief Operating Officer (COO) of the MX Business Unit in March 2025, he has led the successive successes of the Galaxy S26 and Z Fold 8. Based on the know-how accumulated while leading the development of the Galaxy series for a long time, he is expected to lead innovation in foldable phones, on-device AI, and wearables.

TM Roh isn’t leaving the manufacturer, but will drop his responsibilities in the mobile division. He remains one of two CEOs at Samsung alongside Jeon Young-hyun.
