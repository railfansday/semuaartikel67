According to recent reports, I think I’m convinced Starbucks is determined to take up every inch of cabinet space in our kitchens. Just when I think I’ve seen enough adorable drinkware to last me a lifetime, the coffee chain gives me another reason to reconsider.

Recent findings suggest that starbucks has teamed up with FriendsWithYou, the Los Angeles-based artistic duo of Samuel Borkson and Arturo Sandoval III, to launch a limited-edition merchandise collection celebrating joy and friendship. The colorful lineup features six drinkware designs starring two adorable characters: Little Cloud Boy and his upcoming barista friend, Fluffy.

It has been revealed that the collection officially launches November 24 at select Starbucks locations across the U.S. and Canada, just in time for holiday shopping.
