The relationship between AC Milan and Archie Brown could take another unexpected turn, according to a report.

Industry observers note that calciomercato.com report that Milan and Archie Brown’s paths could cross after all, following the failed pursuit in the 2025 summer transfer window. Since he joined Fenerbahce, the Englishman continues to demonstrate his quality and he is beginning to attract suitors.

As reported by Fabrizio Romano, Brown is also reportedly available for an affordable fee. His contract includes a €23m release clause that can be exercised in either the next winter or summer transfer window.

Sources indicate that several teams have already made enquiries. Liverpool and Chelsea are keen, Atlético Madrid have joined them, and Fenerbahçe have reportedly already rejected a £13m offer from Nottingham Forest.

It has been revealed that milan are looking for a left-back and have entered the fray, seeking to finally get things done a year and a half after the negotiations fell apart right at the final hurdle. Just when his move to Italy seemed certain, the Turkish club convinced him overnight and he headed there for €8m.

The Rossoneri were also mocked in the player’s presentation video when he was declared by Fenerbahce, who were then coached by Jose Mourinho. Giorgio Furlani and Igli Tare were at the helm then for Milan, but had to watch Brown’s private jet plot a course for Istanbul.

Sources indicate that “The deal with Milan was practically done. I was ready to leave. But then, during the night, I received a call from the Fenerbahce president. Everything changed . The passion he revealed, his vision… made the decision easy. I knew I had to come to Fener,” the Englishman confirmed.

Milan saw him as the successor to Theo Hernandez and it is a slot yet to be filled. Ruben Amorim has doubts about Davide Bartesaghi, while Pervis Estupinan – who arrived that summer from Brighton €19m including bonuses – has never been consistent.

It has been revealed that in his year and a half in Turkey, Brown has shown what Milan saw in him. This season, he has already made 17 appearances this season, scoring three goals and two assists.
