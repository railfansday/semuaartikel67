As the total number of burn transactions approaches the 22,000 mark, Shiba Inu’s token-burning activity is nearing another significant milestone.

The number of SHIB burn transactions at the moment stands at 21,991, with only nine transactions to go to reach the 22,000 milestone, according to Shibburn. In total, these transactions have burned 410,845,372,884,899 SHIB.

This is a testament to the continuing efforts by the SHIB community to reduce the cryptocurrency’s massive circulating supply by sending tokens to unreachable wallet addresses. The tokens are sent to these dead wallets and are then permanently out of circulation.

It has been revealed that shiba Inu began with an initial supply of approximately one quadrillion tokens. Since then, over 410.8 trillion SHIB tokens have been burned, meaning that 41.08% of the original supply of 1 quadrillion tokens has been destroyed. But individual burns have very different effects. Some transactions are for relatively small amounts, while others remove millions or even billions of SHIB from circulation.

The milestone is another sign of the community’s ongoing interest in Shiba Inu’s tokenomics as investors look for developments that could create stronger demand across the ecosystem.

The daily burn rate of Shiba Inu has seen a huge spike of 443%, as the SHIB community continues its attempt to shrink the circulating supply of the cryptocurrency.

A higher burn rate indicates more tokens have been destroyed than on the previous day. According to Shibburn, 4.40 million SHIB have been burned in the last 24 hours, with the daily burn rate jumping 443%. This contributes to a total of 8.42 million SHIB burned in the last seven days and 322.85 million SHIB sent to dead wallets in the last 30 days.

Despite these cumulative burns, SHIB’s price performance still depends on broader market conditions and investor demand. At the time of writing, SHIB was up 5.60% in the last 24 hours to $0.00000364 amid the Thanksgiving holiday and down 6.45% weekly.
