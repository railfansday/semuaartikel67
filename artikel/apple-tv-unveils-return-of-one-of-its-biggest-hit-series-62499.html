Recent findings suggest that apple TV (now $6.99/month for limited time) has just unveiled the return of one of its most popular series: Presumed Innocent. Here’s the release date and first trailer.

Presumed Innocent, the legal thriller starring Jake Gyllenhaal, was a major hit for Apple when it aired in 2024. Though it was originally conceived as a limited series, Apple gave it a surprise renewal for season 2.

The upcoming season won’t feature Gyllenhaal’s character, but instead tells an all-new story with a cast led by Rachel Brosnahan and Matthew Rhys (Widow’s Bay).

Presumed Innocent season 2 premieres Friday, February 19 with two episodes, followed by weekly releases through April 2.

Here’s the official plot summary, followed by the teaser trailer:.

Sources indicate that the sophomore season of “Presumed Innocent” is based on “Dissection of a Murder,” the debut legal thriller novel by Jo Murray, who also serves as a co-executive producer. Up-and-coming defense attorney Lelia Reynolds (Brosnahan) faces off against her district attorney husband (Rhys) in a murder case that threatens to destroy both their careers and their marriage.

You can watch Presumed Innocent season 1 on Apple TV. The eight-episode first season tells the story of “the horrific murder that upends the Chicago Prosecuting Attorney’s office when one of its own is suspected of a crime.”.

Apple has started making a habit of renewing reveals that were originally intended as limited series.

The streamer recently gave season 2 renewals to acclaimed comedy Margo’s Got Money Troubles and thriller Imperfect Women. But Presumed Innocent preceded them both, and it will be interesting to see if its return proves successful.

Apple TV is available for just $6.99 per month (over 50% off) through Monday, November 30.
