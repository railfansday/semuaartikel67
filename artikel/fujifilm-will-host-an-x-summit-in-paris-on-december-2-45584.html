Fujifilm Will Host an X Summit in Paris on December 2

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Fujifilm has unveiled that it will host an X Summit in Paris on December 2 at 4 AM EST. X Summits have traditionally been Fujifilm’s stage for announcing a new product, and given Fujifilm has not released an X-series camera this year, it could very much use one.

“2026 is stated to mark 200 years since the invention of photography in France,” Fujifilm notes. “To celebrate two centuries of photographic history, and to honor all the photographers and photography lovers who have created countless inspiring images along the way, the next X Summit comes to you from Paris, France.”.

Fujifilm has not hosted an X Summit in 2026, although it has hosted multiple Fujikina events around the world, including in Denmark and Poland this past spring and in Upcoming York City in October. The last X Summit was held in Shanghai in June 2025, which saw the announcement of the X-E5 and 23mm f/2.8 R. Before that, the X Summit in Prague launched the GFX100RF.

Going 18 months between X Summits is extremely unusual, but getting one in 2026 at all feels like a gift, given how quiet Fujifilm has been this year.

There is no official word on what to expect out of this X Summit (of course), although the perception has been that Fujifilm’s flagship cameras are in dire need of a refresh: the X-Pro, X-T, and X-H cameras are long in the tooth now.

In a recent development, A new camera launch would also be an opportunity to address the largest complaint Fujifilm has come up against: autofocus. To its credit, Fujifilm knows it’s a place it must improve.

“We’re trying,” Yuji Igarashi, General Manager of Professional Imaging Group, Imaging Solutions Division, Fujifilm Corporation, explained to PetaPixel at CP+ 2026 in Yokohama. “We still believe it’s necessary to just continue to improve by algorithm improvement. And it’s not that we’re not doing it. We are continuing to work on it.”.

Speaking of future devices, Igarashi said improved sensors and processors likely would help.

“In theory, future devices will be much more advanced, with the consideration of higher processing speed. That would help us as well, but that wouldn’t help existing people, so we’re trying to do both — algorithm improvement plus device improvement.”.

Fujifilm will broadcast the X Summit live on its YouTube channel on December 2 at 4 AM EST.
