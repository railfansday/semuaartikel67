Dino Toppmöller, who left his role at French Ligue 1 side RC Lens in September, has been linked with a possible move back to the Bundesliga according to Sport BILD.

Cologne who only gathered nine points from the first nine games of the Bundesliga are contemplating a managerial change and the former Eintracht Frankfurt coach is on top of the list.

Toppmöller was dismissed from his role at Lens not because of results on the pitch but because of his loyalty to his assistant coach, whom he felt was wrongly terminated.

The Billy Goats will face Bayern Munich on Friday, but the next game against Elversberg on November 28 is expected to determine the future of 38-year-old coach Wagner.

Industry observers note that toppmöller was under consideration in Cologne last summer, but an agreement wasn’t reached on his salary. According to Sport Bild, the 45-year-old would be open to renewed talks. In addition to Toppmöller, Christian Eichner (most recently at Karlsruhe), Horst Steffen (most recently at Werder Bremen) and David Hubert (Union Saint-Gilloise) are possible candidates for the role if Wagner departs.
