It has been revealed that liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola reportedly refused to strike a deal for a World Cup star over the summer. He got his wish - and was absolutely right.

In a recent development, andoni Iraola didn't have the easiest job when he took over as Liverpool head coach in the summer. He inherited a squad that was incredibly expensive - by far the most in the Reds' history - but wasn't playing well.

In a recent development, wasn't playing well at all, actually. Liverpool's points last season were generated through individual efforts rather than team play, surviving on the fact they had more talent than their opponents.

It has been revealed that the potential was always massive, then, but the pressure was high and it was completely unclear if the group could actually play together. Moreover, Liverpool intended to spend even more over the summer and would lost some incredibly important players.

Recent findings suggest that it's now November and we're still completely unclear whether this group can play together. But things are promising. And you've got to say that some of that promise is thanks to Iraola putting his foot down.

It has been revealed that alisson Becker's contract technically expired last summer, but Liverpool triggered an option in the deal that extended it for an extra year. Their proposal at one stage appeared to be that they'd extent it so they could sell Alisson for a bit of money before they lost him for free.

Juventus were heavily interested in signing Alisson. It certainly appeared that a deal would go through, too, only for things to fizzle out and for the goalkeeper to remain with Liverpool this season.

In a recent development, it appears that Iraola played a large role in that happening. Journalist Nicolo Schira reports that Iraola considered Alisson to be non-transferrable and wouldn't let him depart the club.

Recent findings suggest that what a decision that was. Alisson has had a tremendous start to the season, firmly re-establishing himself as no.1 and one of the best goalkeepers on the planet right now.

On top of that, Iraola kept one of the most experienced players at the club - something incredibly vital with all this turnover each summer. It was unquestionably the correct decision.

Industry observers note that and one that looks like it might result in committing to more years of Alisson. Schira states that Liverpool are in talks to get a upcoming contract signed, potentially extending things to 2029. And to say we're behind that decision would be an understatement.
