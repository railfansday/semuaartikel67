Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto’s reference pie isn’t focused solely on the crime fiction that gave it so much of its texture. The most recent Grand Theft Auto 6 owes plenty to its video game contemporaries, and isn’t afraid to toss some love to the biggest and brightest of the industry throughout the journey.

Amongst these references are two Mario and Luigi-styled caps for Jason and Lucia, found through use of an oddly placed bong and a little bit of platforming. If you want some goofy merch to match with your partner in crime, look no further!

To locate the hats, head towards the bottom left corner of Moreau, south of Bonanza. Follow the river until you find the ruins of a paper mill, burnt down in some kind of horrible fire. Towards the left side of the ruins, you’ll find a bong sitting on a table, with a prompt to use it.

It has been revealed that once you do, you’ll hear a poof, and a barely visible block will appear above the central pillar making up the ruins above you. Parkour up the table, and climb between the pillars until you have a clear shot at the block. Shoot it, and two caps will drop on the pillar, one for Jason and one for Lucia.

According to recent reports, make sure you bring both partners to pick them up, and don’t forget to hit the nearby Meditation spot for a nice little boost to your Focus, making this trek both functional and form-efficient. These hats work like any other, throwing cops off your trail if you switch to them before or after committing a crime. Even if wearing your initials on your forehead isn’t exactly the sneakiest option in the world.
