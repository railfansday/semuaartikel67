Vilcek Foundation Launches Open Call for 2027 Grants Offering $200,000 in Funding to US Nonprofits

🌐 Source: Hyperallergic • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (364 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, this week, the Vilcek Foundation declared its third annual open call for grant applications. The organization invites nonprofits whose work aligns with the foundation’s mission to elevate immigrant contributions in the United States and foster an appreciation for the arts and sciences to apply for funding now through March 31, 2027.

It has been revealed that the foundation will award a total of $200,000 to US-based nonprofits as a part of its 2027 grants cycle. Individual organizations may apply for a grant of up to $20,000.

Applicants should lead innovative initiatives that generate measurable impact within and beyond their immediate communities. Strong applications demonstrate both a unique vision and clear plan of action for uplifting immigrants, the arts, or the sciences. Qualifying organizations must be recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3).

The Vilcek Foundation is actively expanding the geographic reach of its grantmaking with the goal of supporting organizations across all US states and select territories. The 2027 grant cycle aims to support nonprofits based around the country, including those in the following locations not previously represented in the foundation’s grantmaking: Alabama, Arkansas, Indiana, Iowa, Mississippi, Nevada, South Carolina, South Dakota, West Virginia, Wisconsin, American Samoa, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, and the US Virgin Islands.

Since its founding in 2000, the Vilcek Foundation has remained steadfast in its philanthropic mission. To date, the foundation has provided more than $7 million in grants to nonprofits in 40 states, two US territories, and Washington, DC.

In a recent development, earlier this month, the foundation revealed the 12 organizations receiving funding as a part of its 2026 grants cycle. Situated across the country, the 2026 grantees include the Bemis Center for Contemporary Arts; Counterpublic, whose Triennial is at the moment on view in St. Louis; Delaware Contemporary Arts; and the Japanese American National Museum.

The 2027 Vilcek Foundation grant period will run from September 1, 2027 through August 31, 2028.

Organizations may apply for either project support or operating support.

Interested nonprofits should submit applications via the foundation’s Grants Portal by 11:59 pm (ET) on March 31, 2027.

For full eligibility requirements and application details, visit vilcek.org/grants.
