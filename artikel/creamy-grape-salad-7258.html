Creamy Grape Salad

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Creamy grape salad is a Southern side dish staple. To make it, toss fresh grapes (use both green and red grapes for a colorful dish) in a sweetened mixture of cream cheese and sour cream until they're evenly coated. Then, top with chopped pecans for the perfect crunch to balance out the creamy dressing. Trust me—if you haven’t tried this nostalgic dish yet, add it to your holiday list for the ultimate sweet side.

In a large bowl, using a whisk or spoon, mix cream cheese, sour cream, granulated sugar, and vanilla.

Using a spatula, gently fold in grapes. Transfer to a serving bowl.

According to recent reports, in a small bowl, stir pecans and brown sugar. Top salad with pecan mixture. Enjoy!

Recent findings suggest that store leftover grape salad in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Give it a gentle stir before serving.
