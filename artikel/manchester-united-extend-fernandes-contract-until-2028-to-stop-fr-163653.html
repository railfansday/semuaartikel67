Club activate option and plan further talks with captain.

‘I am not nice,’ notes Pochettino as he targets improvement.

Manchester United have triggered a clause to extend the captain Bruno Fernandes’ contract until the summer of 2028 on a day when Mauricio Pochettino warned his players they should not expect him to be “nice” in his attempts to turn around the club’s fortunes.

Industry observers note that fernandes’ deal was due to expire in June and by exercising the option United have avoided the risk from January that he could negotiate a pre-contract agreement with a club abroad to join on a free next summer. Fernandes said this month that talks over a longer extension were on hold “because of the context of the team and the club”. He has turned down two United contract offers.
