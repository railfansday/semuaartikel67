📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Turkey stock is a must-make if you had turkey as your Thanksgiving centerpiece. You don't want to let those scraps go to waste, do you? Throw in some of those wilting herbs and whatever carrots and celery you have on hand to make a great pantry staple. It takes almost zero effort and leaves you with a flavorful stock that you can store in the freezer until you're ready to put it to use.

In a recent development, you can make basically anything you'd make with chicken stock (which is a lot!) with this stock. One of the best uses of turkey stock is turkey gravy and that can be used to top something like poutine or stuffing. Or you can make a time-tested classic Turkey Tetrazzini. Whatever you do, don't let this stock go to waste.

Add all of the ingredients to a large stockpot. Cover turkey completely with water, about 10 to 12 cups.

Over medium heat, bring the stockpot to a boil, then reduce heat and let it simmer for 3 hours. Spoon fat off the top as it rises.

Use tongs to remove the turkey carcass.

It has been revealed that strain stock through a fine mesh strainer. Let the stock cool to room temperature.

Store in refrigerator until ready to use for up to 2 weeks or in the freezer for 3 months.

Stock is the perfect thing to store in your freezer! After letting your stock cool to room temp you can store in the refrigerator for 2 weeks or in the freezer for up to 3 months. Set it out in the fridge the night before you plan to use to defrost or place it in a pan of barely simmering water to thaw out promptly!
