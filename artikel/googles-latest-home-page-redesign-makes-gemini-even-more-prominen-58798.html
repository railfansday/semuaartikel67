Google's latest home page redesign makes Gemini even more prominent

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A upcoming collapsible sidebar in AI mode demonstrates your most recent searches.

Google is doubling down on Gemini AI in its desktop search, despite protestations from people and employees alike. The latest feature is a collapsible sidebar in AI mode that shows your most recent searches, in case you want to jump back to a previous query. "In testing, this sidebar made a major difference for making progress and continuing to explore the web," wrote VP of search Robby Stein on X.

The upcoming sidebar is only available in Google Search's AI mode. Clicking anywhere on the left of the AI search window opens up the sidebar, showing all recent searches, both AI and organic. Then, selecting any of the sidebar descriptions opens up an AI mode search window on the subject. You can close the AI search sidebar at any time by clicking on the "close sidebar" icon at the top right.

Fresh on the Google desktop homepage globally: we're rolling out a collapsible sidebar to easily find & jump back into your recent AI searches.

If you're like me and never use Google's dedicated AI Search mode, you won't see the latest sidebar. Still, it's bound to ruffle some feathers among users and publishers who think there's already too much Gemini in Google Search. (As an, er, sidebar, I'd add that the feature would be nice to have in regular Search, as a list of recent queries would save me from having a zillion tabs open.).

Sources indicate that google seems to value its AI search development more than actual money, given that its market cap recently dropped by $500 billion after it admitted that AI search isn't earning as much as organic search. However, the company is likely focused on maintaining its AI search lead over rivals like ChatGPT and Claude. And, as CEO Sundar Pichai put it in the company's last earnings call, Google's commitment to AI is "long-range planning process, not a short-term allocation exercise.".
