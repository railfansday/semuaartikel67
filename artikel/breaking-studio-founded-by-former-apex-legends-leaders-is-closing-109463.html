Breaking: Studio founded by former Apex Legends leaders is closing without releasing a game

Gravity Well, an "anti-crunch" studio founded in 2020 by former Apex Legends executive producer Drew McCoy and lead software engineer Jon Shiring, is entering "hibernation" after losing funding for its game.

Gravity Well's launch in 2020 came with high hopes. Founded by veterans of Call of Duty, Titanfall, and Apex Legends, it made the health of the team "an absolute top priority," McCoy mentioned at the time.

In a recent development, "That means we are anti-crunch. That means good compensation. That means everyone at Gravity Well has creative freedom, because when someone else makes all of the decisions, work isn't fun and the end product isn't as good.".

But like so many other ambitious new studios introduced around that time, it fell victim to the post-pandemic retrenchment and has effectively shuttered without releasing a game. The shutdown comes just a month after the studio laid off more than 40 people in what it stated was a "fight for survival" caused by the loss of funding. It also warned that "given the current state of the industry our future is uncertain.".

A message now posted on the Gravity Well website (via RPS) says the funding situation hasn't changed, and "sadly the time has come for Gravity Well to go into hibernation.".

In a recent development, "We don’t exactly know what is next for us or Gravity Well, but we do know we still believe in the IP and game we were building," the message states. "Even moreso, the team we managed to cultivate was truly world class and we're going to miss getting to work with them every day. If you're a hiring manager or recruiter and a Gravity Well resume hits your desk they come with our highest recommendation.".

The announcement concludes with an invitation to publishers and investors to reach out if they're interested in supporting the project.
