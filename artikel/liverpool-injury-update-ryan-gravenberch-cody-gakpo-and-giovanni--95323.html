Liverpool were without two key stars against Manchester United in a surprise double omission from Andoni Iraola.

Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch were left out of the squad for the 4-0 win at Anfield, while Giovanni Leoni remained sidelined alongside Hugo Ektike.

The Reds were easy victors against Mauricio Pochettino’s side, but will be hoping for a boost ahead of their Champions League clash at Club Brugge.

With that in mind, here is the latest Liverpool injury news and return dates….

Leoni has finally returned to training after over a year out of action.

The Italian ruptured his ACL against Southampton on-debut last year, and has suffered some setbacks in his recovery.

He was set to come back in September, but that was pushed back to December.

Andoni Iraola explained: “It’s taking more time than we wanted. The planning was to train with the team in this past international break [but] probably it’s going to be in the next international break, so we’ve lost one month in the process.

“Giovanni’s injury, I wasn’t here but talking to the medical department, it was not just an ACL – it was a bad injury, a lot of things around the knee were involved apart from the ACL.”.

Potential return date: Saturday December 12, vs Leeds (H).

Long-term absentee Bradley is also back in training after his serious knee bone and ligament injury he suffered against Arsenal in January.

His return will seriously bolster Iraola’s options at right-back, which were already boosted by the return of Jeremie Frimpong.

Potential return date: Wednesday, December 2 vs Sunderland (H).

Forward Gakpo was left out of the squad to face United with a calf problem, Iraola revealed on Sunday.

The winger had suffered the matter on international duty with the Netherlands.

“Gakpo got injured with the Netherlands, he left the game with a soleus injury,” Iraola said.

“He hasn’t been training with us, nothing since getting injured from the national team.

Potential return date: Wednesday, December 2 vs Sunderland (H).
