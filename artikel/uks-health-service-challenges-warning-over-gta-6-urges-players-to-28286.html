GTA 6 is so massive that even the UK's National Health Service — commonly known as the NHS — has gotten in on the act.

To be fair, the NHS raises a good point with regards to the open world game's imminent release. On Instagram, it's urging players to take regular breaks in order to avoid issues like repetitive strain injury — or RSI for short.

In a cruel twist, the official account's admin has actually had to undergo surgery to fix their problem, meaning that playing GTA 6 at launch is going to be difficult.

Industry observers note that "Thoughts and prayers for your admin. Bit of surgery to remove a lump. Great NHS service, bad timing," the post reads.

According to recent reports, "Planning on dedicating time to gaming later this week? Try and take regular breaks, obviously. And be mindful of RSI," it continues.

Recent findings suggest that in this case, RSI is caused by overuse of certain muscles in the hands and fingers, brought about by repeated actions. It's a pretty common injury amongst gamers, and without wanting to scare you off completely, it can lead to lasting damage if you refuse to properly rest the impacted area.

The NHS has got a whole page on RSI if you want more information.
