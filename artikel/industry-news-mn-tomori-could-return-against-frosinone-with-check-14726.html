Recent findings suggest that fikayo Tomori suffered a thigh tear against Bologna, and AC Milan are awaiting updates about his recovery. However, some good news has arrived.

The international break couldn’t have come at a better time for Tomori. It allows him to recover at Milanello without missing valuable training time with the team. Given 13 players have been called up this week, he’s hardly in a ‘proper’ environment right now.
