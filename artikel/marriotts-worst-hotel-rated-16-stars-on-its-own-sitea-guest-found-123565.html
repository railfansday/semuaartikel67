You didn’t need to investigate eeach property to find out whether it met basic standards. You knew what the brand stood for. Now I find Marriott overpromises and enforces too little, afraid of alienating owners by making them spend money. Net rooms growth has become the priority, even when the rooms undermine the reason customers choose Marriott in the first place. Here, the property isn’t even flying one of Marriott’s flags yet and presumably there will be some investment. But they aren’t making the owner do that first, before delivering their guests. They’re trading a reputation built over decades for current fee revenue. Owners pay Marriott to deliver customers. If travelers stop trusting the tech giant’s hotels, Marriott has fewer customers to send those owners. Then the owners have little reason to keep paying Marriott.
