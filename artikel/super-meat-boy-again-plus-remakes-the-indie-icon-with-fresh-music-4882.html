Team Meat has declared a newly introduced remake of its classic 2010 platformer: Super Meat Boy Again+, this time with a fresh coat of bloodsoaked paint and entirely new levels.

The brutally tough platformer became a cult hit more than a decade ago and spawned one sequel, one 3D spin-off and a whole speedrunning community dedicated to beating its masocore levels without dying. Super Meat Boy Again+ is more or less the same game, but with much more bolted on top.

"This version is just like the original Super Meat Boy game but with fresh art, music, cutscenes, levels, secrets and unlockables...new everything," the game's Steam page notes. "Since 2010, millions of people [have] played Super Meat Boy, all the secrets are known, all the levels are mastered, all the characters unlocked, so we have to give fans familiar with the game something to pull their hair out over. That's the "+" in Again+, and no, we won't spoil it. You'll go into it blind like you did the first time you played Super Meat Boy.".

Sources indicate that original composer Danny Baranowsky - better known as Danny B - is also back with an expanded soundtrack. The reveal trailer up above shows off how drastically different the game's updated art looks, too.

In a recent development, "For our old fans, we want to present the game as you remember it through the wonderful lens of nostalgia. For our new fans, we want to give you a great looking, easy to pick up but difficult to master platformer that you may have missed because it came out like 4 years after YouTube was invented," the studio explains.

There's no release date yet for Super Meat Boy Again+ and the only confirmed platform thus far is Steam.
