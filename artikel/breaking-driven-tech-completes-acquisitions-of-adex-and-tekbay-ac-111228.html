Recent findings suggest that NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Driven Tech, Inc. today unveiled the successful completion of its acquisitions of ADEX and TekBay, following receipt of all required government and regulatory approvals.

The acquisitions significantly expand Driven's expertise in enterprise AI, data engineering, agentic AI, and cloud-native development across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud.

Both teams will join Driven's Applied Intelligence Business Unit, led by Sanjeev Pant, further strengthening the company's three strategic platforms: Pervasive Security, Applied Intelligence, and AI-Driven Digital Foundations.

A key example of this collaboration is Lasius, Driven's agentic AI orchestration and security platform, developed by the ADEX engineering team based on Driven's strategic vision.

"These acquisitions represent a significant milestone in our evolution," noted Vinu Thomas, President and Chief Operating Officer of Driven Tech. "We're bringing together exceptional AI engineering talent with our expertise in cybersecurity and infrastructure to help enterprises move beyond AI experimentation and securely operationalize AI at scale.".

With the acquisitions complete, Driven will focus on integrating its expanded engineering organization, accelerating innovation, and bringing fresh AI-driven solutions to enterprise customers.

Driven Tech, Inc. is a technology solutions and services company delivering integrated capabilities across Pervasive Security, Applied Intelligence, and AI-Driven Digital Foundations.
