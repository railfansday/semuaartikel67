GTA 6 mania has paved the way to some pretty wild headlines over the last couple of years, but now, as we approach the finish line, KFC might have provided the craziest yet.

In a recent development, in Australia, the fast food chain has declared it's holding a fake funeral in order to provide people with a plausible reason to take the day off work. Yes, really.

The company teased the event on social media platforms:.

The funeral, for the dearly departed (and completely non-existent) Ann Alibi, will take place on 19th November, 11am AEST at the KFC in Robina, Queensland.

Attendees will be able to enjoy some free chicken at the wake.

Apparently, the service includes "a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, a book of condolences, an order of service and even a heartfelt eulogy delivered by a surprise guest.".

If you can't attend in person, a livestream will be broadcast starting at 12pm AEST, and you can still claim that free food via delivery instead.

So, if you're an Aussie looking for an excuse to take 19th November off work, KFC's marketing stunt may be just the ticket.

This isn't the only business reacting to the imminent launch of GTA 6; some, such as Palworld dev Pocketpair, are simply giving employees the time off.
