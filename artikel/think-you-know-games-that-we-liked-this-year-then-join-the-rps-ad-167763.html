📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, ho ho ho, the RPS Advent Calendar 2026 is... not ready yet. We haven’t written the words, and I keep forgetting to unblur that newly introduced picture of Horace (drawn by the reliably talented Amanda Flagg). But while drag our lumpen feet on the business of deciding our favourite games this year, RPS readers have begun plotting, and scheming, and apparently in some cases, spreadsheeting. That’s right: the Advent Calendar Guessing Game has returned, and is open right now for keen participants.

If you’re unfamiliar with this grand tradition – which is once again being run by extra-lovely reader Aerothorn on the RPS Discord – then here’s a quick rundown. You, the reader, take a guess at the 24 games we’ve chosen to hide behind Horace’s wall of advent doors this year, finalising your list before the calendar rolls out on December 1st. Once all games are revealed, those with the most accurate guesses shall bask forever in the glory of their triumph, and also bag some free access to the RPS Supporter programme. 3rd place wins a three-month subscription, 2nd place wins six months, and the 1st place Guesser Supreme wins a full year of Supp perks.

Industry observers note that be warned, though, that victory will demand a sharp mind and an impeccable memory. Or, at the very least, a willingness to go back through our reviews and What Are We Playing This Weekend posts, to remind yourself which games have taken our fancies these past twelve months. Anecdotally, I think we’ve been slightly better at actually writing about our favourites in 2026 than we were for last year’s calendar, which should hopefully make for an easier Guessing Game. If also, perhaps, a more competitive one?

You can read up on the full game eligibility rules, start discussing your picks, and submit your guesses by joining the #2026-advent-calendar-guessing-game channel in the RPS Discord linked above. Note that you don’t need to correctly guess the position of an included game to earn a point for it, only its presence in the list, though you should specify which game you think will earn the coveted Day 24 door – our overall top game of the year, essentially – as this detail will serve as one of the tiebreakers in the event of a draw.

According to recent reports, thanks again to Aerothorn for setting this up, and good luck to all who participate! Here’s one hint, for free: we didn’t have Sin, Oisin, and Jeremy P voting for the Advent Calendar after having them for the RPS 100, so don’t rely too much on the latter for inspiration. Sorry, Unbeatable.
