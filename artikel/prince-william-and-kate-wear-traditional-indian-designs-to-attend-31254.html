📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Prince and Princess of Wales paid homage to Indian culture as they attended the sixth annual Earthshot Prize Awards tonight in Mumbai.

Recent findings suggest that after a busy day of engagements (and outfit changes), the couple made it to the Jio World Convention Centre to celebrate global change-makers who are actively working to solve urgent environmental problems. It was the first time the awards were held in India, and the event brought together around 5,000 global guests, world leaders, and celebrities.

And for the momentous evening, the British royals wore traditional Indian attire.

In a recent development, prince William went for a rare all-black look, the highlight of which was a traditional velvet Bandhgala jacket by Indian designer Manish Malhotra. By his side, Princess Catherine—at her first Earthshot ceremony since 2022—was a vision in a vermilion-red anarkali gown custom-made for her by Indian designer Mahima.

The stunning long-sleeve piece had gold and green embroidery throughout, and Kate expertly accessorized it with jewelry from Queen Elizabeth II’s fabulous emerald tassel suite. Up top, she wore the late monarch’s luxurious diamond-and-emerald tassel drop earrings, while around her neck, the matching tassel necklace took the spotlight.

The suite is believed to have been gifted to Queen Elizabeth by Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan of Abu Dhabi during the 1989 United Arab Emirates state visit to Britain. And through the years, the monarch took it out for several vital occasions, including for the Return Dinner hosted in 2011 by President Barack Obama and first lady Michelle Obama at the end of their state visit to the United Kingdom.

In 2022, the queen loaned the suite to Kate, who wore the necklace and earrings to the state dinner hosted by the Governor General of Jamaica. Now, it’s become a shining standout in her royal jewelry collection.
