A fresh update has landed for Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Switch 2.

Industry observers note that it's not a massive one, all things considered, but it does add GameShare support for those who want to play online multiplayer via GameChat. We've also got a bunch of miscellaneous improvements to performance, stability, etc. Gotta love that stability.

Here are the official patch notes:.

- Added GameShare support for online multiplayer via GameChat - Various stability, performance, and quality of life fixes.

An enhancement on other systems adds a upcoming menu narration accessibility option, however this is not available for the Switch 2 and nor does it seem imminent.

Recent findings suggest that in our review for Legacy of the Dark Knight, we said that "with a frequently hilarious script, endless callbacks, and a cracking open world to explore, TT's love for the Caped Crusader always manages to shine through". We awarded it a score of 8/10.
