Redeem your points and miles: How to search flight award availability for the major airlines

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (165 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Finding award flight availability is often the most challenging part of redeeming points and miles effectively.
  • Third-party tools like Seats.aero and Point.me allow travelers to search multiple airline loyalty programs and routes simultaneously, streamlining the process.
  • Each tool offers unique features, such as live award searches, filters, and alerts, but neither can book tickets directly—final bookings must be made through the airline program.
  • Comparing award prices across different loyalty programs can save significant points or miles, as pricing and availability can vary widely.
  • Dynamic pricing means award costs fluctuate based on demand and date, so partner programs may not always offer better value.
  • Some premium cabin awards, especially on certain airlines, have additional restrictions or require elite status to book.
  • Phantom availability—when seats appear bookable but are not—remains a risk, so always confirm availability before transferring points.
