This week, DirecTV rolled out a bundle that combines Netflix, HBO Max and Apple TV at a discount while aiming to help streamline billing in one place. Customers with any DirecTV Signature or Genre Pack can add this bundle to their proposal, with two notable exceptions: the MyEntertainment genre plan or Premier package are not eligible, as both aims already include HBO Max.

There are two tiers to choose from with the latest bundles: a Value blueprint, which runs $28 per month (in addition to any monthly live TV subscription fees) and carries Netflix Standard with Ads, HBO Max’s ad-based plan and Apple TV, or the Deluxe plan, an ad-free option that costs $43.50 a month and includes Netflix Standard, HBO Max Standard and Apple TV. Either bundle can be added to or canceled from your existing plan at any time.

Right now, if you want to watch all the best shows, live sports and news programs, they’re scattered across multiple services, and this bundle offers you three of the most robust services out there, while skirting their latest price increases.
