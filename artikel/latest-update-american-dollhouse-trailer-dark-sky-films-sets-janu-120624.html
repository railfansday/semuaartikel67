EXCLUSIVE: Dark Shy Films has set a January 15 U.S. release for John Valley’s horror movie American Dollhouse and unveiled the official trailer.

The Austin-shot picture stars Hailley Laurén (1923) as a woman trying to get her life back on track who inherits her childhood home and decides to move there for a fresh start.

These aims take a deadly turn when she finds herself being stalked by a psychotic neighbor with an unsettling obsession, tipping the familiar comforts of home and family into the setting for an increasingly violent nightmare.

Laurén is joined in the cast by Kelsey Pribilski (Man Finds Tape), Tinus Seaux (The Pizzagate Massacre), Danielle Evon Ploeger (RATS!), and Richard C. Jones (HBO Max’s Love & Death). Watch on Deadline.

It is a second feature for Austin-based director Valley who previously made waves among horror fans with his 2020 debut The Pizzagate Massacre.

The film world premiered at SXSW this year and has since played at a raft of genre festivals including France’s L’Etrange Festival and the Brooklyn Horror Film Festival.

It is produced by David Axe, Samuel Butler, Shane Greb, and Roosevelt Film Lab’s Christian Sosa and Jon Wroblewski.
