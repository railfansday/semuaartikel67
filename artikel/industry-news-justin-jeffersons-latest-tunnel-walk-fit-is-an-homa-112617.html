Industry News: Justin Jefferson’s Latest Tunnel-Walk Fit Is an Homage to Mexican Fashion

It has been revealed that ever since the NFL season kicked off back in September, the Minnesota Vikings’ star receiver Justin Jefferson has been going full-tilt. Last night, his team squared off against the San Fransisco 49ers in Mexico City (the NFL has been scheduling more away-gamers outside the U.S. to try to expand their viewership) in one of the marquee matchups of the season, and while the 49ers ended up winning that one, Jefferson remains inspired by the Vikings’ 7-3 record so far. “I’ve been great,” Jefferson indicates. “Everything’s been going really well, and I’m feeling good.”.

According to recent reports, preparing for such a big game is no small feat, of course—even if a pro like Jefferson makes it look easy. Ahead of hitting the field, you can often find him getting ready both physically and mentally. “Preparation is everything: I want to make sure I'm confident, focused, and ready to perform at my best,” he says. How did he prepare for Mexico City? “A big part has been making sure I'm physically and mentally ready for the challenges of playing at a higher altitude,” Jefferson says. (Mexico City is about 1.4 miles above sea level—much more intense than even Denver’s Mile High Stadium.) “I've been using the altitude chamber to help my body acclimate. Mentally, it’s about studying the plays, understanding the different schemes, and making sure we're approaching the game with the right mindset.”.

A more unexpected way Jefferson gets game-day ready, however: With his intentional tunnel-walk outfits. Yesterday, before the athletes even hit the field, Jefferson wanted to rock a meaningful look that would put him in the right mindset for his Mexico City matchup—so he enlisted one of the city’s most well-known designers, Patricio Campillo, to craft a custom look for him. “I've always tried to make my tunnel looks reflect where I am in the world, especially when we're playing internationally,” says Jefferson. “For me, fashion is an opportunity to connect with different cultures and showcase designers who bring something unique to the table.”.

A very cool moment for me: Meet the designer—Patricio Campillo—behind the look.

In a recent development, wanting to rep a design that represented the local culture, Jefferson called up Campillo to start ideating his custom look. He was drawn to the designer for his unique craftsmanship, creativity, and attention to detail. “Choosing Campillo felt like a natural decision—I was genuinely impressed,” claims Jefferson. “You can tell how much time, passion, and hard work goes into creating these looks. Being able to represent that craftsmanship on an international stage is something I think is really special.”.

Together, they landed on doing a cool, modern take on tailoring. Campillo created a cropped, boxy-style jacket with brown leather trousers—a signature element of the label that the designer wanted to introduce to a whole new audience. “It’s exciting to see someone of Justin’s caliber embrace Campillo,” he notes. “I’ve always believed that fashion has the power to bring together people from different backgrounds, so seeing an athlete like Justin connect with our work is especially meaningful.”.

He accessorized with pieces such as a horsehair bag, Oakley shades, and his signature chains. When the wide receiver put on the final look for the first ime, he was immediately drawn to the finer details. “One of my favorite details was the horsehair incorporated into the bag,” says Jefferson. “It’s such a unique touch and gives the piece a lot of character.” He also appreciated the hand-craftsmanship. “Knowing how much time and effort goes into creating each piece makes you appreciate the look even more,” he says. “I love wearing things that have a story behind them, and this outfit definitely does.”.

The fit, custom made by Patricio Campillo.

Industry observers note that it’s not the first time that Jefferson has flexed a fashion statement for his tunnel walk. During past games, he has worn everything from Off-White to Louis Vuitton and Chrome Hearts. What he wears and experiments, of course, depends on the setting. “My tunnel looks are really an extension of my personality, and how I’m feeling that day,” he says. “There are a few things I consider when putting a look together: my mood, who we’re playing, and the brands or designers I want to showcase. Fashion has become another creative outlet for me, and I enjoy stepping outside of what people might expect.”.

This fluid approach to dressing applies to his off-duty wardrobe, too. Jefferson indicates he enjoys playing around with fashion even on his days off. “Right now, I'd describe my personal style as evolving,” he says. “I'm really focused on experimenting with different looks and exploring styles I might not have considered earlier in my career. I like stepping out of my comfort zone and being open to trying newly introduced things. Fashion is all about self-expression, and I think the more willing you are to take risks, the more you learn about yourself and what you like.”.

It has been revealed that what does this mean for the rest of Jefferson’s NFL season? You can expect tons more tunnel-walk outfits, for starters. “Every week is another opportunity to bring something exciting to the tunnel,” he indicates. “We love finding ways to surprise people, turn heads, and keep things interesting.” Start taking your bets—fashion bets, not sporting bets—on what runway outfit or custom creation he will don next. “We’ve already had a few outfits this season that have gotten a lot of attention, and that’s always fun to see,” he says. “But for me, it’s not just about getting people talking—it’s about continuing to discover fresh brands, experimenting with different styles, and finding creative ways to express myself through fashion.”.
