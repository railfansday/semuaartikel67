Chelsea have reportedly held transfer talks with Bournemouth over a January deal for Alex Scott, with things heading in a ‘positive’ direction.

That’s according to a report from Simon Phillips, with the Chelsea insider providing some interesting details on his Substack this morning.

Scott has long been strongly linked with Chelsea, and it seems things are moving in the right direction in terms of the Blues bringing the England international to Stamford Bridge in the middle of the season.

“I have had it confirmed by my top SPTC Source that Chelsea and Bournemouth did once again hold more talks around Alex Scott to Chelsea in January, over the international break. And this source describes the talks as ‘positive’,” Phillips wrote.

“Remember, another top SPTC Source told us about these talks, and that as a result of them, Chelsea are Bournemouth had actually got pretty close on a fee now – there is some work to do on add-ons and structuring of any deal.

“However, our top source also notes more clubs have started to sniff around for Scott now as well, even though he has verbally agreed to Chelsea’s terms. Nothing is done yet, it’s key to keep reiterating this.”.

Scott has shown himself to be a superb talent during his time at Bournemouth, and it seems inevitable that he’ll eventually end up moving to a bigger club.

The Cherries have done well to cash in on some star players for a profit in recent times, such as Antoine Semenyo, Dean Huijsen, and Milos Kerkez.

We’ve previously reported here on Chelsea pursuing Scott, with Bournemouth open to a sale and other clubs also mentioned as suitors.

Sky Sports have linked Arsenal with an interest in the 23-year-old, while you can also see below for Ben Jacobs posting on X about Liverpool also potentially being in the race for his signature….
