Recent findings suggest that amazon's early Black Friday sale is bringing some pretty significant discounts on Apple devices, including several of the organization's latest releases. The biggest savings are on the M5 MacBook Air, with up to $249 off, though there are plenty of smaller deals worth considering, too, from the AirPods Pro 3 at $179.00 to the Apple Pencil Pro at $99. You'll also find discounts on iPads, the Mac mini, and everyday accessories like the Magic Keyboard, Magic Trackpad, and AirTags. With Apple raising prices across its Mac and iPad lineups in June, citing higher memory chip costs, these sales offer a good opportunity to upgrade without paying full price.

The AirPods Pro 3 are down to $179 from $249, saving you $70 on Apple's latest premium wireless earbuds. They offer improved active noise cancellation over the previous generation, along with redesigned foam-infused ear tips and a heart-rate sensor that tracks workouts without an Apple Watch. They also support Live Translation with a compatible iPhone and carry an IP57 rating for sweat and water resistance. PCMag’s review goes into more detail on how they perform.

The AirPods Max 2, which launched in March, are already down to $449.99 from $549, saving you nearly $100 on Apple's premium over-ear headphones. The biggest upgrade over the original model is the H2 chip, which Apple says makes active noise cancellation up to 1.5 times more effective while introducing features like Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, and Live Translation. For more on its performance, check out this PCMag review.

The Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS, 42mm) is down to $329 from $399, saving you $70. It covers everything you'd expect from a premium smartwatch, including fitness tracking, health monitoring, notifications, and the ability to take calls when your iPhone is nearby. PCMag gave it an "outstanding" rating in its review and also named it its Gift of the Year for 2026.

Recent findings suggest that if you'd prefer to occasionally leave your iPhone behind, the Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS + Cellular, 42mm) is $70 off, bringing its price down to $429 from $499. It has all the same features as the GPS model but adds cellular connectivity, allowing you to make calls, send messages, and stream music even when your phone isn't nearby (provided you have an active cellular plan).

For a more affordable option, the Apple Watch SE 3 (GPS, 40mm) is down to $199 from $249, saving you $50. It covers the essentials, including workout tracking, daily activity monitoring, and notifications, making it a practical choice if you don't need the Series 12's more advanced health features. Check out PCMag's review for a closer look at what you get for the price.

In a recent development, the Apple Watch SE 3 (GPS + Cellular, 40mm) is also $50 off, bringing its price down to $249 from $299. For an extra $50 over the GPS-only model, you get the convenience of making calls, sending messages, and staying connected without keeping your iPhone nearby, provided you have an active cellular blueprint. It's worth considering if you regularly go for runs, head to the gym, or step out without your phone but still want to stay reachable.

You still get the M5 chip, faster SSD performance, and Wi-Fi 7 support, all in a smaller, lighter design.
