Liz Hurley’s Wedding Guest Dress Breaks All the Rules (In the Best Way)

There are assumed rules to wedding guest dressing. Don’t wear black; don’t wear anything too revealing; don’t wear anything that could, by any reasonable measure, “upstage” the bride. Liz Hurley had other ideas this weekend, arriving at the wedding of her boyfriend Billy Ray Cyrus’s daughter in an almost transparent Gaurav Gupta gown with a thigh-high slit.

Though Liz Hurley’s look might have the average congregation clutching their pearls, this was hardly the average wedding. For one thing, it was Miley Cyrus’s wedding to Maxx Morando, held at Frank Sinatra’s former home in Palm Springs, with an all-black dress code. For another, well, what else would you expect from the woman who shot to fame in a Versace dress held together with safety pins at the 1994 premiere of Four Weddings And A Funeral?

Industry observers note that she and Cyrus met while filming Christmas In Paradise in 2022, hard-launching their relationship on Instagram in April 2025. And while the sight of one of the original ’90s glamazons in plaid shirts and cowboy boots might once have seemed incongruous, Liz Hurley seems to have taken naturally to life on the country singer’s Tennessee ranch. These days, she looks every bit as at home in a pair of 501s as she does in a cut-out bodycon gown—whether or not the occasion calls for one.
