GTA 6 players have already found their fresh favorite character in the game, but there’s a strong chance she might be a serial killer.

Vice City is packed with memorable characters, from protagonists Jason and Lucia to the other misfits and criminals they meet throughout the story. But there’s one in particular that has captured the hearts of the internet — Mandy.

You meet Mandy early on in Chapter 1 while making collections for Brian, and, putting it mildly, she has a bit of a soft spot for Jason. She ropes him into fixing her plumbing before he can leave with the cash, only for her to try to seduce him.

It’s a relatively minor moment, and Jason ultimately shoots her down, but social media has been buzzing with fans falling head over heels for Mandy.

Mandy… I'm weak. What have you done Rockstar 😭 pic.twitter.com/mlCsR2NCaQ.

There is one small issue, though — many players are also convinced she’s actually a serial killer.

While fixing Mandy’s sink, Jason removes an object blocking a pipe, which she quickly snatches away from him. However, if you’re looking carefully, you’ll see that it was a severed finger that she’d clearly tried to get rid of.

This is weird enough by itself, but if you take the time to explore the rest of her home, there are even more clues that all is not what it seems.

The trunk of her car contains a number of suspicious items, including a meat cleaver and tape. Meanwhile, you can find containers of acid and saws scattered around her yard.

Most damning of all, there’s even what appears to be a grave at the side of her home, and it looks like it was dug recently, too.
