Riccardo Calafiori had an evening to forget for Italy, and it may harm his chances back at Arsenal.

The defender was part of the Italian side that lost 4-1 to Belgium, leaving them facing relegation play-offs for League A.

The 24-year-old has been a mainstay in Arsenal's squad this season, starting nine of the 10 Premier League games so far.

Mikel Arteta clearly trusts the talent the Italian has to offer, but ahead of facing Newcastle this weekend, he may have something to think about.

While he was away on international duty, Calafiori came up against a player that offers a similar threat to what Arsenal are set to face.

And he did not cover himself in glory for his nation.

Arteta will be well aware of the threat Elanga poses for Arsenal this weekend, with his selection of fullbacks all the more crucial against Newcastle.

Calafiori's performance against Belgium will do little to reassure the Arsenal boss that he is the right man to handle that challenge.

According to Italian outlet Eurosport, Calafiori's evening against Belgium was a "nightmare.".

The fullback was unable to deal with the speed and pace of winger Diego Moreria, which may prompt some fears back at the Emirates.

When domestic football returns, the left-back has a possible match-up against Anthony Elanga looming, with Newcastle awaiting.

Like Moreira, Elanga is a pacy winger who often looks to beat his man down the wing.

On Sunday evening, Calafiori did not deal with that well, with Sofascore showing he won just one of his three attempted tackles during the game - a 33 per cent success rate.

He also only won 50 per cent of his duels, showcasing that it was a mixed evening for him against the AC Milan star.

Moreira was also able to get himself on the scoresheet up against the Arsenal man.

With Calafiori's struggles evident, that could give Arteta something to ponder, as Elanga is likely to provide the same headache he has just faced and failed to deliver on.

Arsenal have plenty of options at left-back, with Piero Hincapie one of the choices that could be turned to.

Additionally, Jurrien Timber is also capable of playing on either side of defence, with Myles Lewis-Skelly also among Arteta's choices.

Of course, the Spaniard could view Calafiori's poor display as a one-off bad night, but his "nightmare" against Belgium should give his club manager something to think about.

Especially with Newcastle's direct and vertical football, which will look to exploit pace down the wings.
