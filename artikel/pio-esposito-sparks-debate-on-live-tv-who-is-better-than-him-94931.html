Francesco Pio Esposito scored a hat-trick in Inter’s dramatic 4-4 draw against Atalanta on Sunday, sparking a debate live on Italian TV between Andrea Stramaccioni and Valon Behrami: ‘You have to tell me who is better than him as a target striker.’.

Pio Esposito’s hat-trick against Atalanta, his first in Serie A, wasn’t enough for Inter to secure all three points in Bergamo, but it cemented the striker’s status as the best Italian centre-forward.

Even during the latest international break, Pio Esposito scored three goals in two Italy games, including a brace against France.

The 21-year-old now has seven goals and two assists in 14 games across all competitions with Inter this season.

His performance against Atalanta sparked a debate on Italian TV between ex-Inter coach Stramaccioni and former Serie A midfielder Behrami.

“You have to tell me who is better than Pio Esposito as a target man,” Stramaccioni remarked during DAZN’s post-match show on Sunday.

The ex-Inter coach hit back: “He’s not a target man. Osimhen runs behind. I’m talking about players who are 1.90 meters tall, hold the ball, and play with their backs to goal. Osimhen makes runs out wide so fast he scorches the grass; what are we even talking about?

Recent findings suggest that “So, [Harry] Kane,” argued Behrami, sparking another quick response from Stramaccioni: “Oh, come on. Kane is practically a nine-and-a-half. Sublime technique, drops out of the box, even plays as a No.10, roams all over the pitch. I’m talking about players like Toni, in the Bierhoff mould. Maybe there’s Samu Omorodion.”.

Behrami insisted: “Sorloth? Wood?”.

“Sorloth plays as a winger,” Strama replied.

“Right, so it’s a category of its own, and Esposito is the best in it, the only one,” insisted Behrami.

Industry observers note that “He’s the best in the world in a category with just one player. I’ve given you five names. They’re different because they offer something more.

“Esposito’s category is the category of players who are missing things from their game. He’s missing a lot of the pieces he needs to be a top player.”.

It has been revealed that “Oh, so you’d compare Toni to Osimhen or Kane? They’re different types of centre-forward. Do you remember Skuhravy? He was that kind of centre-forward. You don’t get them anymore,” concluded Stramaccioni.

“I’m saying the major, traditional centre-forward barely exists in world football anymore. Otherwise, tell me who fits that description.”.
