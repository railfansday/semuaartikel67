Physical storage and memory are only getting more expensive, but that doesn’t mean we need to store any less. The alternative solution is a secure cloud storage platform, and there’s really only one good option.

According to recent reports, based in Switzerland and operating out of data centers in the EU and US, pCloud is relied on by over 24 million users across the globe. Its standard for security comes naturally high, given where it’s based. Swiss law sets the standard for data privacy and consumer rights, and pCloud’s entire foundation sits on security and privacy, with a 13-year history to back it up.

Recent findings suggest that that extends throughout every platform it’s available on, including Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux. On just about any platform, customers get access to the pCloud Drive app for dedicated storage and backup. On Android and iOS, you have an additional option to automatically back up photos to free up space on your phone. pCloud Photos also acts as a smart photo gallery within the app, and sharing between other pCloud users and friends is made incredibly simple.

No matter where you access your pCloud Drive, all your files are there – safe and secure. You can store all kinds of files in pCloud, including documents, photos, videos, and even documents containing passwords.

Of course, you also get the option to take security a step further with pCloud Encryption. Your most sensitive files can be stored with an added layer of client-side, zero-knowledge encryption. The key is all yours, and no one else’s.

Even if you already have a hefty backup solution like an in-home NAS, pCloud is still a good option because it makes that NAS that much more useful. It has an official NAS backup feature with support for WebDAV, Rsync, SFTP, and SCP. pCloud simply provides another safety net for your existing backup.

Unlike a lot of cloud storage services, pCloud doesn’t charge on a monthly or even yearly basis. Just like buying physical storage, paying once for what you need is enough. But what makes it a better option than going the traditional route – beyond the skyrocketing storage prices – is the features and security that come with it.

Starting with the base 500GB lifetime storage plan, pCloud’s one-time price comes down to $159 – 43% off. The 2 TB plan is currently down to $379, while the massive 10GB lifetime plan is 61% off at $1,099. pCloud is also offering a bundled 3-in-1 option that includes 3TB of storage, a lifetime pCloud Pass subscription, and pCloud Encryption for $579.

Secure cloud storage has major advantages, and a one-time subscription for terabytes of storage is rare these days.
