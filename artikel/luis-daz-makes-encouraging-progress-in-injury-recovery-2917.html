As confirmed last week, Luis Díaz took a significant step towards returning from a muscle tear by partially rejoining team training.

The Colombia international has continued to make progress since then, and a return to action for Bayern Munich could soon be on the cards.

According to Sky Germany, a comeback against 1. FC Köln on Friday comes too soon, but Díaz could return before the end of the month.

The 29-year-old attacker has scored five goals in 11 appearances for Bayern Munich so far this season.

The Bavarians have three more games to play in November, taking on Köln at home before facing Lille and Hamburger SV away.
