A nest thermostat is the perfect example of someone selling you something you don't need, following the tech startup adage, "What is my mother no longer doing for me?".

Lets make adjusting the temperature in your house complicated, with possible privacy implications. Now we're going to control it with AI to make it worse.

Or you can just, you know, push buttons on a normal dumb thermostat when you want to change the temperature.

Recent findings suggest that there are lots of other options. There are local-only "smart" thermostats, both networked and not. Or there are relatively simple digital thermostats which allow complex programs, including a vacation mode. Those have been around since long before thermostats with color LCDs.

Nest was sold as having lifetime support. I don't know about you but I don't feel like 15-years qualifies as a lifetime. Sure it's a long time in the tech world but mortgage loans are typically 30 years.

People are supposed to be pushed out of the Nest, not the other way around...
