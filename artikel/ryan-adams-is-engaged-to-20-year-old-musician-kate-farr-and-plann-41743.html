Musician Ryan Adams is engaged to Kate Farr, a writer and producer.

The couple shared the news with TMZ, explaining that they got engaged in early October after meeting through mutual friends six months prior.

It has been revealed that “I’ve never been happier in my life,” the former Whiskeytown front man, 52, said. “Kate is the most fascinating, kindest nerd I’ve ever met. She’s my best friend, and I’ve never been happier. Here’s to love.”.

According to the publication, Farr, 22, recently “moved to Los Angeles to be with Ryan,” and the pair are at the moment living together.

However, they seem to have aims to leave the West Coast soon, with Adams noting they’re house-hunting in the Midwest so they can be closer to Farr’s family in Ohio.

Adams made headlines over the summer when he canceled his European tour in July. At the time, he stated he had fallen on “tough times” and had “been sick.”.

In a previous Instagram Story, he had revealed that he was selling some personal belongings—including guitars, musical equipment, and a vehicle—because he was “broke and sick and tired and defeated.”.

In 2019, seven women—including Adams’ ex-wife, actress Mandy Moore, 42, and singer Phoebe Bridgers, 32—publicly accused the singer of abuse.

In a New York Times piece, they alleged a “pattern of manipulative behavior,” in which he “dangled career opportunities while simultaneously pursuing female artists for sex.”.

In a recent development, bridgers reported “obsessive” behavior from Adams during the pair’s “whirlwind romance” in 2014, when she was 20 and he was nearly 40. She has claimed that he floated the idea of marriage in the first week of the relationship, and told Bridgers’ mother that his looking out for her daughter was a “once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.”.

In the aftermath, the singer-songwriter’s album release was canceled amid an FBI investigation surrounding sexually explicit communications with an underage fan. The bureau closed its investigation without charges that fall, according to Page Six.

Adams’ lawyer, Andrew B. Brettler, denied the claims, stating the musician never “engaged in inappropriate online sexual communications with someone he knew was underage.”.

“As someone who has always tried to spread joy through my music and my life, hearing that some people believe I caused them pain saddens me greatly,” Adams wrote in a social media post at the time of the reports, according to People magazine.

In a recent development, “I am resolved to work to be the best man I can be. And I wish everyone compassion, understanding and healing.”.

A year later, in a lengthy letter published in the Daily Mail, Adams made an additional statement.

It has been revealed that “All I can say is that I’m sorry. It’s that simple. This period of isolation and reflection made me realize that I needed to make significant changes in my life,” he wrote. “I’ve gotten past the point where I would be apologizing just for the sake of being let off the hook and I know full well that any apology from me probably won’t be accepted by those I’ve hurt.”.

Noting that he understood that “there’s no going back,” Adams claimed he was “fully accountable” for his “harmful behavior” and will continue to be for “actions moving forward.”.

Adams and Moore ended their six-year marriage in 2016. They were married in 2009 and separated in 2015. The former couple had previously lived in a Los Feliz home Moore purchased in 2002 for $1.7 million, splitting the mortgage. However, they offloaded the property for $3.2 million amid their split.

In a recent development, the “This Is Us” star has since married Dawes front man Taylor Goldsmith. After their 2018 wedding, the couple welcomed three children—August “Gus” Harrison, 5, Oscar “Ozzie” Bennett Goldsmith, 4, and Louise “Lou” Everett Goldsmith, 2.

Moore reflected on her first marriage in a 2024 episode of “Dinner’s on Me with Jesse Tyler Ferguson.”.

According to recent reports, “It’s so weird because I think back to that chapter, and it almost feels like it was someone else entirely that it happened to, because I’m in such a different place in my life, and I’m married and I have children,” she explained. “It’s almost like, how did I ever do that? How did I ever find myself in that place where I allowed myself to be treated that way, that I viewed myself that way?”.
