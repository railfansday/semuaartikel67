Hill Dickinson Stadium will be transformed into a festive destination this December as Everton unveils The Magic of Christmas – a packed programme of family experiences, Christmas parties, festive dining and entertainment at the Club’s waterfront home.

For the first time, the stadium will host a dedicated programme of Christmas events, giving Evertonians, families, friends, businesses and visitors to the city the chance to experience Hill Dickinson Stadium in a completely different way.

Running throughout December, The Magic of Christmas will feature the spectacular newly introduced Enchanted Lights experience, the return of Breakfast with Santa, two Après-Ski Christmas Party Nights and a special Christmas Bottomless Brunch at Domingo’s Bar & Grill.

Whether it’s a magical family day out, a Christmas celebration with friends or an end-of-year party with colleagues, there will be something for everyone at Hill Dickinson Stadium this festive season.

Supporters and visitors can explore the full programme and book their Christmas experience at hilldickinsonstadium.com/christmas.

ENCHANTED LIGHTS.

Friday 11 December - Wednesday 23 December.

At the heart of The Magic of Christmas will be Enchanted Lights, a brand-fresh family experience bringing Hill Dickinson Stadium to life with light, music and festive entertainment.

Guests will begin their Christmas journey on Everton Way before entering an illuminated trail filled with festive installations and photo opportunities.

From there, visitors will enjoy a unique opportunity to enter Hill Dickinson Stadium through the West Stand Players’ Entrance, following the same route used by Jordan Pickford, Jack Grealish, James Tarkowski and Everton’s First Team on matchdays.

The journey continues down the Players’ Tunnel and out into the stadium, where guests will take their seats pitchside for a dazzling Christmas light show inside Everton’s waterfront home.

The experience then continues at Christmas in The Yard, a specially-created festive village offering seasonal food and drink alongside live entertainment.

Families can make their visit even more memorable by adding a trip to Santa’s Grotto, giving younger visitors the opportunity to meet Father Christmas.

Guests booking by Sunday 1 November can take advantage of an Early Bird offer of 20%% off adult and junior tickets by using the code HDSLIGHTS at checkout.

Families can also add a visit to Santa’s Grotto for £10 per child, available for juniors and infants.

Guests with a valid event ticket will also benefit from free parking at Nelson Dock, while flexible booking allows visitors to move their booking up to 36 hours before their selected time.

*Free infant tickets do not include entry to Santa’s Grotto unless the optional Grotto experience is purchased.

BREAKFAST WITH SANTA.

Friday 18 December - Wednesday 23 December.

Father Christmas will also return to Hill Dickinson Stadium for the Club’s popular Breakfast with Santa experience.

Families can enjoy a festive breakfast and Christmas activities before meeting Santa himself, with every child receiving a special gift from Santa to take home.

The experience also includes an exclusive mini stadium tour, taking guests behind the scenes at Hill Dickinson Stadium, through the Players’ Tunnel and pitchside for a special festive photo opportunity.

APRÈS-SKI CHRISTMAS PARTY NIGHTS.

Friday 11 December and Saturday 12 December.

Trade the slopes for the stadium as Hill Dickinson Stadium transforms into an alpine-inspired party destination for two special Après-Ski Christmas Party Nights.

Guests can enjoy festive food and drinks alongside a live singer, DJ, saxophone and bongo performers, themed dancers and immersive alpine-style entertainment throughout the evening.

Tickets include two festive food vouchers and two drinks vouchers, making either night an ideal option for Christmas parties or celebrations with friends, family or colleagues.

Private Christmas party packages are also available for groups looking for their own exclusive festive celebration.
