📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

TAP Air Portugal will launch a latest route to Washington, D.C., next year.

In a recent development, starting March 30, 2027, the carrier will connect Porto and Washington Dulles. Flights will operate three times weekly.

Presently, TAP serves Dulles from its hub at Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisbon. Its only U.S.-bound routes from Porto are to Boston and Newark, Latest Jersey.

TAP officials noted the newly introduced connection will strengthen the airline’s presence in northern Portugal while expanding access to a vital foreign market.

TAP serves Porto via Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport.

The carrier serves nine destinations in the U.S., the others being Upcoming York-JFK, Chicago O’Hare, Orlando, Florida, Miami, Los Angeles, and San Francisco.
