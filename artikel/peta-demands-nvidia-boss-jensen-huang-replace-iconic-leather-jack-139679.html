Animal rights organization PETA is urging Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to ditch his iconic black leather jacket, even sending the billionaire an $875 vegan alternative made from olive waste.

Much like Steve Jobs with his black turtlenecks and Mark Zuckerberg with his gray T-shirts, Huang’s leather jackets have become synonymous with the Nvidia boss.

His signature look has even proven lucrative. In July 2026, one of Huang’s signed Tom Ford leather jackets sold for a staggering $960,000 at auction, fetching 16 times its estimated price.

However, following Huang’s return as Nvidia CEO amid an ongoing foreign intelligence investigation for cheating on his wife with an alleged Chinese spy, PETA wants the billionaire to give his wardrobe an upgrade.

In a November 18 letter addressed to Huang, PETA President Tracy Reiman argued that despite his reputation as a technological innovator, his fashion choices were outdated.

Recent findings suggest that “Tech doesn’t move forward by clinging to outdated systems, and neither does fashion,” Reiman said. “PETA is calling on everyone to embrace vegan leathers made from cactus, cork, apples, and other plant materials and leave animals out of their wardrobes.”.

To encourage Huang to make the switch, PETA sent him an $875 jacket from fashion brand Benedetti Life, according to the New York Post.

Sources indicate that the jacket is made using Oleatex, a leather alternative derived from olive harvesting waste. It features an organic cotton lining and an exterior consisting of 95% Biotex and 5% polyurethane.

PETA has also asked Huang to donate his old leather jacket to its planned Museum of Animal-Sourced Atrocities, offering the billionaire the prospect of a tax deduction in return.

According to recent reports, interestingly, Huang had already ditched his trademark look when he returned to Nvidia’s headquarters on November 23. However, rather than opting for PETA’s olive-based alternative, he appeared to be sporting a $3,300 Dior cotton jacket, according to GQ.

Whether Huang is willing to part ways with his extensive leather jacket collection remains to be seen. The Nvidia boss previously revealed that he owns between 30 and 40 of them.
