A father of two has defended his decision to play GTA 6 while his partner was in labor after footage of him gaming in the hospital sparked backlash on social media.

Sources indicate that since launching on November 19, Grand Theft Auto 6 has had quite an impact on people’s everyday lives. From schools sending out minibuses to catch absent students to players ditching real-life gyms in favor of in-game workouts, fans have been finding every opportunity to explore Leonida.

For 24-year-old Adrian Bryant from Georgia, that even meant bringing his Xbox to the hospital as his partner, Autumn, 25, prepared to give birth to their second child.

The dad shared footage of himself playing Rockstar’s latest game on the hospital room’s TV, captioning the video: “when your girl is about to give birth but Lucia wants to go on a date.”.

The footage amassed nearly 2M views and sparked outrage from viewers who accused Bryant of prioritizing a video game over the birth of his child.

“As a man come on bro, you seriously couldn’t wait till u home to play. yes the game is great but moments like this is far greater in life,” one viewer commented.

However, Bryant revealed that it was actually Autumn who asked him to bring the console, with the couple having done the same thing during the birth of their first child, according to Need To Know.

Even the hospital staff apparently got involved in the gaming session.

“They actually sat and watched me play to also while asking questions,” he remarked of the nurses.

Autumn also defended the arrangement in the comments, confirming that bringing the Xbox was her idea.

As criticism continued, Bryant uploaded a follow-up montage of photos with his children, insisting that the gaming session hadn’t interfered with his responsibilities as a father.

“Not that I had to prove it to anyone I’m actually a phenomenal dad/partner. I love both of my children dearly and would do anything for them,” he wrote.

“I played for about a hour and then me and mom watched movies and I played a playlist with her favorite songs to keep her relaxed when it was time to push.”.

Bryant also insisted that he would have immediately put down the controller if Autumn needed him.
