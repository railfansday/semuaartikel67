📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.What we're looking for.

We're looking for a Red Team Lead to establish our Red Team and strengthen the security culture at Supabase. As the team lead, you'll build the team and work alongside them to proposal and execute realistic attack simulations across the entire tech stack. You'll guide the team to turn findings into practical recommendations that drive meaningful security improvements.

Recent findings suggest that this role is ideal for someone who combines technical expertise with thoughtful, people-first leadership. Success in this role means our team brings security at Supabase to latest heights.

Develop our Red Team from the ground up.

Lead and mentor a team that values technical depth, curiosity, collaboration, and accountability, where people can challenge ideas and learn. Align their efforts with their career growth and organizational security goals.

Stay technically connected to the planning and execution of operations. Guide the team to make sound judgment calls and communicate operational impact to various audiences.

Define the team's strategic roadmap and operational practices for our environment and threat landscape. Use metrics and impact narratives to communicate the team's success.

Build strategic relationships with relevant teams, including Legal, Engineering, IT, HR, and Finance, for cross-functional influence and amplified security impact. Foster a collaborative relationship with defensive teams.

Advise leadership on strategic growth beyond the Red Team.

Establish Supabase as a thought leader in the security space. Share appropriate research and lessons publicly through writing or talks.

Have experience leading adversary emulation teams, especially balancing hands-on work and management as a player-coach.

Have experience with global asynchronous teams and can work independently.

Are familiar with cloud-native offensive operations, including cloud and CI/CD pipelines, and creating and securely deploying command-and-control (C2), reconnaissance tools, and infrastructure for adversary simulations.

Understand attack techniques targeting browsers, cloud infrastructure, databases, SaaS applications, supply chains, Linux and macOS devices, and identity and access systems.

Naturally build rapport, trust, and engagement within Security and across the organization.

Work collaboratively with kindness and respect.

Can clearly communicate complex security concepts to a wide range of audiences, including executive leadership.

In a recent development, we hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the manufacturer. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Industry observers note that supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

Once a year, the entire manufacturer gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

It has been revealed that we operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F).

Recent findings suggest that we move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.
