Sony has shared a selection of staggering statistics relating to the launch of GTA 6 on PS5.

From launch on 19th November through to the end of the title's opening weekend, Sony recorded over 39 million unique players.

The game set a newly introduced record on PlayStation for the highest number of concurrent players on any game.

PS5 players have logged 683 million hours of GTA 6 playtime, and on average, every owner on PlayStation has played it for 17.4 hours.

Outside of the console's launch and Christmas holidays, the launch of GTA 6 resulted in "the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day". GTA 6 was the first game played on 70% of those fresh PS5 systems.

"These are just a few ways we’ve seen our PlayStation community come together around this incredible moment in gaming, and we’re excited to see how you continue to build and share latest memories and experiences in the months ahead," said content communications manager Tim Turi on the PlayStation Blog.

Industry observers note that the day after release, Rockstar Games revealed GTA 6 had sold 32.5 million units across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. With these newly introduced stats from PlayStation, we know it's sold over 39 million copies on PS5 alone, as of yesterday.
