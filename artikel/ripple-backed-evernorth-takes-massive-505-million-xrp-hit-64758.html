Ripple-backed XRP treasury manufacturer Evernorth Holdings has disclosed a significant decrease in the book value of its crypto asset holdings, with total impairment charges amounting to around $505 million before going public on Nasdaq.

Per an SEC filing on Nov. 13, the 346.3 million XRP tokens that Evernorth Holdings owned had a book value of just $341.9 million as of Sept. 30, 2026.

This is compared to the firm's initial accounting of the crypto assets at roughly $846.6 million, with an average acquisition or contribution price of $2.44 per XRP. This represents a nearly 60% decline in book value.

Sources indicate that as per the latest filing, Evernorth recorded around $271 million in XRP impairment charges in the first nine months of 2026. This is in addition to the $233.7 million in impairment charges recorded in 2025.

Interestingly, only $6.9 million of the recent impairment charges occurred in the third quarter.

This also explains why Evernorth's XRP assets had a book value so far below their market value at the end of September.

Evernorth calculates impairments based on the lowest observable intraday price of XRP for each batch of tokens acquired. Once the assets are written down, the manufacturer is unable to reverse the previous losses if the price rises.

For reference, on Sept. 30, XRP closed the day trading at around $1.49 on Coinbase. At that price, Evernorth's 346.3 million XRP tokens would have been worth roughly $516 million, much higher than the disclosed book value of $341.9 million.

The recent disclosure comes around a month after Evernorth completed its business combination with Armada Acquisition Corp. II on Oct. 9. It started trading on Nasdaq under the symbol XRPN on Oct. 12.

Following the business combination, Evernorth held around 473 million XRP tokens and had also raised gross cash proceeds of around $300 million.

Its investors include Ripple, SBI Group, Arrington Capital, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR.

It has been revealed that on Oct. 14, Evernorth announced collaborations with Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo to implement institutional investment strategies starting in Q4 2026.

Meanwhile, its latest SEC filing did not include details on the amount of XRP deployed to generate yield or the returns generated from it.
