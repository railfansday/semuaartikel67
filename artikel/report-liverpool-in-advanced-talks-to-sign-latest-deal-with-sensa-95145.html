Liverpool are looking to complete an incredibly important move, and are in advanced talks to get it done.

After a mass exodus of homegrown players, the importance of the Reds keeping hold of their club-trained young stars has intensified immeasurably.

Liverpool lost Trent Alexander-Arnold, Tyler Morton, Jarell Quansah, Ben Doak and Caoimhin Kelleher all in the same summer.

Industry observers note that these were players who had become crucial first-team members and had represented exciting potential. Most of them have gone onto do very well at their next clubs - with the exception of Alexander-Arnold - who must now be ruing whether thinking the grass was greener on the other side was the right call.

But the less mentioned about Trent the better. The focus for Liverpool is on making sure situations like that never happen again.

Liverpool working hard to complete dealsJulian Ward made it his mission in order to ensure that Liverpool's future is stockpiled with enough homegrown talent this summer. In his previous role he played a key part of negotiations with Joshua Abe convincing him to reject lucrative offers including a deal that would have offered him £50k a week in order to remain at Liverpool. Ward was also an advocate and impressive negotiator to entice Erik Farkas, an England U18 international, to stay at the club despite interest from elsewhere. Now, his next mission is focused on tying Rio Ngumoha down to a new deal. Although Ward has his interest on another English playmaker, who he is now reportedly very close to agreeing a deal with.
