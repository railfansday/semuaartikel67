According to recent reports, november through New Year’s is marketed as the magical season, and for many people, it can be. But if you are struggling with infertility and/or pregnancy loss, it can represent two months of triggers, traumas, jealousy, and misery. Why? Lots of reasons.

In a recent development, many people save surprise pregnancy announcements for gatherings, which can be agonizing for anyone who wants nothing more than to make such an announcement themselves. Family and friend parties bring forced encounters. Intrusive questions are common: So when are you going to start a family? I hear you’ve been struggling. I think you should see my cousin’s doctor. You need to gain weight, exercise less, try my herbalist, or just adopt.

And it doesn't help when the focus of TV specials, commercials, and religious services is on babies and children. What can you do to make this season a bit less dread-worthy? Maybe even experience some joy?

Don’t assume that every pregnancy announcement or belly was achieved easily or through spontaneous sex (roughly 1 in every 37 babies are conceived through IVF). Many people never share their infertility struggles, and most of those who build their families through donor eggs, sperm, or embryo don’t tell anyone.

You are not alone. It is estimated that 1 in 6 individuals struggle with family-building.

According to recent reports, if you can’t get into the holiday mood whatsoever, don’t pressure yourself. The majority of those experiencing infertility report symptoms of anxiety and depression, and women experience the same level of psychological distress as do women facing cancer or heart disease.

Most importantly, put yourself on your own list; nurture yourself.

In a recent development, resolve is the National Infertility Organization. They provide patient education and support in every state.

Prelude patients also have access to emotional support information/resources on their patient portal.

Recent findings suggest that ASRM is the American Society for Reproductive Medicine. They provide up-to-date, accurate medical information.
