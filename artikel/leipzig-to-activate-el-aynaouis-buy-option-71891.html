Neil El Aynaoui has recovered from his injury and is ready to return to action for Leipzig.

The Moroccan midfielder, who left the national team’s training camp early due to leg problems, has received the green light from the German club’s medical staff.

According to BILD, the 2001-born player will be available to Martin Demichelis for the away match against Union Berlin, scheduled for Saturday, November 21st.

According to recent reports, el Aynaoui had already resumed partial training with his teammates earlier this week, while sporting director Sebastian Schuppan had ruled out any serious problems in recent days: “It’s not a serious injury. It’s more of a combination of the games he’s played and the many knocks he’s received. He’s feeling the strain of the last few weeks and months.”.

The international break has therefore allowed him to recover without having to miss further matches against Leipzig.

Industry observers note that the Moroccan has become a regular in Demichelis’s midfield, starting him in twelve of the thirteen matches played so far. His performances have also allowed him to leapfrog Nicolas Seiwald in the Argentine coach’s pecking order.

Recent findings suggest that according to BILD, if he continues at this level, a buyout at the end of the season could become a possibility. Leipzig paid Roma €4 million for the loan of El Aynaoui, with a buyout option set at €25 million.

Before deciding whether to acquire him permanently, however, the German club will also need to evaluate its own financial situation, given UEFA’s requirements regarding transfer revenue.
