You can never be too cautious when it comes to your online privacy. Between websites tracking your activity, advertisers collecting browsing data, and the ever-present risks of using public wifi, having an extra layer of protection is always a good idea.

In a recent development, proton VPN has built a reputation around privacy and security and offers fully encrypted connections that help protect your internet traffic. This is especially notable when you’re browsing on public wifi networks. The VPN service offers access to well over 20,000 servers across 149 countries, giving you plenty of options for choosing a connection location. This can come in handy if you want to access region-specific content while traveling or just want to get around geographical restrictions.

The Plus initiative is particularly appealing if you plan to stream content from other parts of the world, as it offers higher speeds, supports streaming services, and lets you connect as many as 10 devices simultaneously.

One of Proton VPN’s biggest draws is its focus on privacy. Based out of Switzerland, Proton operates under Swiss privacy laws. Additionally, the apps are open-source and independently audited, which adds another level of transparency to the service. Finally, you can expect to find quality-of-life additions like a kill switch, which cuts your internet connection if the VPN drops unexpectedly. This is critical to ensuring that your real IP address doesn’t get exposed on the internet.

If you’re looking to save some money, the 24-month subscription is the best way to take advantage of this offer. This Black Friday deal knocks 75% off the price, bringing it down to just $2.49 a month. Do keep in mind you’re signing up for a long-term commitment, though, and it makes most sense if you do not blueprint to switch VPN providers.
