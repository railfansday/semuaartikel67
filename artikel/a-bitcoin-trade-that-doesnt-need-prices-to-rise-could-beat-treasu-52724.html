Sygnum expects crypto carry trades to earn more than Treasury bills on average in 2027, according to its annual Sygnal outlook released Nov. 19.

The bank also pointed to a recovery in Bitcoin funding rates and futures premiums, citing 67 consecutive days of negative Bitcoin funding in 2026.

The bank expects renewed demand for leverage to keep funding rates and futures basis mostly positive in 2027, even after the Federal Reserve raised its policy-rate target range to 3.75%-4% in September.

The trade pairs a Bitcoin purchase with a short futures position. If the two positions are sized to offset each other, a rise in Bitcoin helps the purchase and hurts the short. The trader seeks to earn a premium on dated futures or collect funding payments on perpetual futures.

Recent findings suggest that with a dated futures contract, the gap between the futures price and the spot price is called the basis. When the contract trades above spot, the trader can buy Bitcoin at the lower price and sell futures at the higher price, and the two prices converge at expiry.

Industry observers note that perpetual futures have no expiry, so their funding payments recur while positions are open. When funding is positive, longs pay shorts, and shorts pay longs when it is negative. The 67-day streak Sygnum cites describes how long funding stayed negative, when traders shorting perpetuals would have faced a funding cost.

Recent findings suggest that buy Bitcoin and short futures. The source of the return depends on the type of contract.

Recent findings suggest that dated futures seek to capture an initial price premium. Perpetual futures pay or charge funding while the trade is open.

Trading and financing costs affect realized returns. Sygnum’s release gives no bill maturity or yield for the comparison.

Recent findings suggest that the Treasury-bill comparison asks what the same capital could earn in short-term US government debt. The Fed’s policy rate sets the context for short-term rates.

In Sygnal Six, the bank estimates that if about $360 billion, or 0.75% of Nasdaq market value, moved into crypto, total crypto market capitalization could rise more than 60% and Bitcoin could reach a newly introduced high near $128,000.

Those two forecasts sit alongside six others on token value accrual, Bitcoin in strategic reserves, stablecoins in correspondent banking, tokenization, prediction markets and AI demand.

Fabian Dori, the bank’s chief investment officer and the report’s lead author, remarked investors should look beyond price to fundamentals.

Mostly positive funding rates and futures premiums in 2027 would support Sygnum’s case for a carry recovery. The test is whether the resulting average return clears Treasury bills.
