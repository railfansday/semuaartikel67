Black Friday 2026 deals on Pixel devices are now live on the US Google Store. The phone deals are available until December 5 at 11:59 p.m. PT, while watch discounts end December 1.

It has been revealed that google is taking $200 off the Pixel 11, which is unchanged from the last sale in late October. The Pixel 11 Pro is $350 off (previously $300), while it’s $400 off (compared to $350) for the Pixel 11 Pro XL. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold sees only a $250 discount.

There are matching discounts at Amazon and Best Buy today.

There are also discounts on the refurbished Pixel 9 Pro ($75 off to $574), Pixel 9 ($50 off to $449), and Pixel 8 Pro ($50 off to $499).

Meanwhile, Google is once again discounting the Pixel Watch 5 Wi-Fi and LTE by $100. There’s a deal on the Fitbit Air that lets you get the tracker and upcoming Champagne Gold Metal Mesh band for $30 off at $119.99. Google will give you $25 toward a Fitbit Edge if you trade in a Charge 6.
