📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It’s infuriating that Google still hasn’t released a new Nest Hub, and it makes Amazon’s Black Friday discounts for Echo Show smart displays pretty darn tempting.

Google has always taken a pretty conservative approach to smart home hardware, but the gap in Nest Hub releases is getting more than a bit frustrating at this point. Nest Hub’s last hardware patch was in 2021, and the Nest Hub Max even further back in 2019. There’s some hope on the horizon, but the competition hasn’t slowed down. Even Apple has a smart display now, while we’re just sitting and waiting within Google’s ecosystem.

That context in mind, Amazon’s Echo Show series sure is tempting right now.

The latest Echo Show models come in 8.7-inch and 11-inch sizes with better-quality displays than anything Google offers, a much more modern design, and better speakers too. It’s tempting on its own, but Black Friday prices push that to a newly introduced level, with both around $100 off and starting at $99.99.

Recent findings suggest that this isn’t just a Black Friday deal post. It’s also me as someone who’s deeply invested in the Google Home ecosystem being annoyed there’s no Nest Hub sequel, nothing to replace the aging hardware in my home, and genuinely being tempted by the alternatives.
