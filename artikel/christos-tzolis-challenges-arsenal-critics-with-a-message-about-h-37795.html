Winger Christos Tzolis wants to prove he has a lot to offer Arsenal beyond his attacking prowess.

In a recent development, since moving to the club from Club Brugge in July, the 24-year-old has made a great start to life at the club after an impressive season in Belgium. The Greek attacker scored 17 goals and made 23 assists in 36 league games.

But doubts were raised about his ease of integration into the Premier League. In his first stint in England with Norwich City, he showed little sign of becoming the player he is now.

Recent findings suggest that since making his debut for the club, Tzolis has become a more rounded attacking threat. He has started all 7 Premier League games this season and provides a reliable option on the left for Arteta.

However, in recent comments, it seems that he is aware of the perceptions around his physicality and is keen to change them.

Tzolis spoke to Arsenal Media, saying that he doesn’t think the notion of him lacking the physicality to play at top level has validity.

Recent findings suggest that he claimed that he is not a player without muscle and cited his accomplishments. He also described his tendency to use aggression against his opponents.

In a recent development, this is a key part of Arteta’s system at the club. Tzolis is expected to press from the flanks if Arsenal lose the ball.

In a recent development, tzolis is comfortable with this role. He is able to track back well to support his full-back, adding an extra dimension to the left side.

In a recent development, the Greek international has proven his physical ability outside of match play. He was nicknamed Leonidas by the fitness team at the club due to a strong performance in pre-season.

It is reported that he completed the running challenge better than anyone in the squad.

Sources indicate that tzolis has joined Arsenal to add attacking flair to the squad, having left for Besiktas. He looks like he could be a formidable goalscorer and creator.

Industry observers note that in the league last season, Tzolis contributed 40 goals and put in a good performance in Europe. This led to Arsenal spending a reported £34 million to bring him to the club.

He has not had the best impact in the league, registering just one assist and no goals, but this should not be taken to mean he is not contributing.

Tzolis has shown an ability to fit into Arteta’s style of play. His defensive contribution, work rate and combative nature may prove very valuable in tough away games.

Despite there being plenty to improve on, Tzolis provides more than just an attacking option for Arsenal.

If Tzolis can change people’s view of him in terms of his physicality and continues to put his technical skills and determination together, then he can be a valuable asset in Arteta’s plans.
