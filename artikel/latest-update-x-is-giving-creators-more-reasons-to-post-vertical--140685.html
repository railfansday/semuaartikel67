Latest Update: X is giving creators more reasons to post vertical videos

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that A short video can make it from TikTok to Instagram Reels to the group chat before lunch. X wants more of those clips to land in its feed, too, and it's giving some creators a reason to post videos they've already made.

On Wednesday, Nov. 25, X laid out its plans to get more creators making, sharing, and earning money from videos on the platform. It showed off an upgraded editor that lets creators record, crop, and caption videos without leaving X, confirmed that its clipping tool is now available on the web, and highlighted an invite-only bonus it's been testing since October for creators who regularly post short, vertical videos.

Industry observers note that X states video is already its fastest-growing format. "Posts with video get 5x the reach and engagement, and 36% of all impressions now go to video," the organization’s creator team wrote on Nov. 20.

The editor lets creators record in segments, use a green screen, or turn horizontal footage into a vertical clip with one tap. They can also trim the footage, generate captions, add text, and pick a thumbnail before posting. A separate revision, announced Nov. 20, lets creators mix up to 20 photos and videos in a single swipeable post. Those tools join React with Video, which X introduced over the summer to let people respond on camera with the original post behind them.

X is also paying some creators to try vertical video through its Video Kickstart Bonus, an invite-only pilot it began testing in October. To earn the bonus, creators must post 15 qualifying videos on 15 different days within about a month. Each video must be their own work, in a vertical 9:16 format, and under three minutes. A TikTok, Reel, or YouTube Short can count as long as it’s free of another platform’s branding. Creators can see their bonus amount in Creator Studio, X's dashboard for creator tools and payouts. Participants who don’t already have Premium+ also get a free year.

Original Content Rewards also gives creators a way to earn when someone else shares a moment from their video. X's clipping tool, now available on the web as well as iOS and Android, lets people pull an excerpt, add their take, and post it with a link back to the original. Both the person who made the video and the person who posted the clip can earn if they're enrolled in the program.

As X creator product lead Allegra Jacchia put it Wednesday, the goal is to make sure that "when someone clips their video, they get the credit and a share of the earnings.".

According to recent reports, X first rolled out clipping on iOS and Android earlier this month, and Wednesday's announcement confirmed it’s now available on the web, too. To make a clip, open the repost menu on an eligible prerecorded video and select "Clip video." From there, you can trim the footage to between five and 60 seconds and add your commentary before posting. The original video must run for at least two minutes, and the clip can take up no more than 30 percent of it. (So if you're clipping a two-minute video, you get up to 36 seconds.).

The clip plays right in the feed. For everyone in the replies asking for the full video, there's a shortcut. Tap the "Clip from" label and X takes you straight to the moment in the original video where the excerpt starts. For creators enrolled in Original Content Rewards, earnings from clips show up in their regular payouts.

The video tools will feel familiar to anyone who's spent time on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts. Letting creators earn a bonus for videos they've already made gives them a reason to add X to the list of places they post.
