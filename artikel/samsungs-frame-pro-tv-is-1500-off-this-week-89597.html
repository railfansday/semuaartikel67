According to recent reports, back in the old days of Black Friday shopping, you had to fight other members of your community—your kids’ bus driver, the people that sat next to you in church, a friend’s personal trainer—to score a good TV on sale. You were all in tents, only able to use whatever porta-potty they had set up in the parking lot. You were, effectively, animals—full survival mode.

Sources indicate that thankfully, Black Friday—which Amazon is hellbent on making an even longer shopping holiday with it’s “Turkey 12,” 12-day event—is more dignified these days. You’re still a pawn in some executive’s scheme to show a big green number to the shareholders, but at least it’s a little more dignified. You can shop in your PJs, and you don’t even have to spend money on gas.

Our favorite deal of the day week is Samsung’s Frame TV, which is at its lowest price ever. Right now, the 65-inch size of the standard 2026 model is under $1,000.

As an Amazon skeptic, I did my due diligence and compared prices against Best Buy, Walmart, and Samsung’s website—they’re all the same, to be fair. But chances are you’ll be picking up other sale items on Amazon anyway, so you might as well do all your shopping at once.

The standard Frame TV is great, but if you only want the latest and greatest tech, it’s worth spending a bit extra on the 2026 Pro model, which offers upgraded picture quality and sound. “When you need a TV that decorates your wall like a work of art, The Frame Pro is everything,” writes associate commerce editor Bryn Gelbert. “A glare-proof screen, thin bezel, and partnerships with the Met, MoMA, and a few high-profile artist estates make it the art TV to beat.”.

If you’re after the steepest deal, the 2025 75-inch is 50 percent off right now.

Sources indicate that will these Frame TV prices drop further on Black Friday itself? Only time will tell—but based on price history during previous sales like Prime Day, we think it’s safe to shop now. It is incredibly unlikely that you’ll find a Frame TV for more than 50 percent off. This is one you should add to cart now.
