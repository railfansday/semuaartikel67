An influencer using AI prompts to score cheap upgrades on flights has reportedly ended up overpaying compared to normal prices.

Recent findings suggest that as artificial intelligence has become an everyday part of life for a lot of people, it has also helped travellers looking to plot out journeys. Many have used ChatGPT to scout out restaurant, hotels, and even score deals on flights. Though, others have fallen foul of fake AI ads for travel hotspots that don’t actually exist.

However, as per travel experts at One Mile at a Time, an influencer who has been advertising a ChatGPT script to get cheaper upgrades on flights, seemingly ended up overpaying.

MavGPT laid out three prompts for ChatGPT, including urging the AI to ask about what airmiles you have, as well as checking if a flight you’re booked on has any upgrade available. In his example, he claimed he was flying with American Airlines Los Angeles to London Heathrow, with the AI spending 52,000 miles and a $190 fee for the upgrade.

According to One Mile at a Time, MavGPT “got screwed” if that was the AI’s best idea, seeing as it took the instant upgrade offer that the airline offer anyway.

They added that it wasn’t an “actual trick or hack,” and the AI should have looked at the “systemwide upgrade” seeing as there were still empty seats available.

“While there are definitely some strong use cases for AI, I think this is actually a pretty poor showing of what it can do,” they added, noting that the miles could have easily been saved and spent on “something else” instead.
