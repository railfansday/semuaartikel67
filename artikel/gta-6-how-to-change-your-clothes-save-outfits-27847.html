Recent findings suggest that in GTA 6, style can be extremely crucial. While it's not paramount to gameplay, it can affect how the authorities recognize you; plus, Jason and Lucia constantly change gear to suit any given mission. For some players, the cosmetic aspect of any open-world game is the game itself.

According to recent reports, as an open-world game with a dense world, fans will immediately flock to the customization system, which runs very deep in GTA 6. Here's how to find new outfits, change into them, and keep a supply of clothes on hand for when things really go south out in the wild. More so than ever, clothes have a pretty important role in the game.

According to recent reports, changing clothes is unlocked shortly after you start the main game (following the chase with Brian, and meeting one of his associates at a pool party), when you only control Jason. Once you acquire Lucia, and she joins the fray as a main character, you'll be able to alter her style as well.

Changing your clothes in GTA 6 is primarily done in two ways:.

According to recent reports, stores are marked with a black T-shirt icon and sell clothes, but many other locations can provide outfits as well, like gun shops, which sell camo/tactical gear and the like. In other words, your options are vast, and once you start traveling past the original Chapter 1 Florida Keys location, you'll find more stores than you can handle.

Alternatively, you can visit a barber to change up Jason's facial hair, hair color, and style (the same goes for Lucia's hairstyle when she's available). Using all of these tools, you can utterly alter how Jason and Lucia look compared to their "canon/trailer" character models.

It has been revealed that once you're comfortable with an outfit, go to the outfit menu in the dresser interface, then press and hold Square on PlayStation (X on Xbox) to add it to the trunk. You can have five outfits saved in the trunk,.

It's vital to note that this needs to be a registered car. Initially, you're given access to Jason's car, which can be recovered no matter what using towing services, even if it drives off a cliff and explodes. That way, your outfits will always be safe and can't be destroyed because of a mission-based mishap.

It has been revealed that outfits stored away in your trunk can be extremely useful, as police scan for how the suspects were dressed at the time of the crime. So if you were wearing an orange jumpsuit for a robbery, and successfully escape, you can change outfits (and potentially vehicles) to make a clean getaway. Try to have at least two outfits stored away for this express purpose.

According to recent reports, with all the customization options available in GTA 6, odds are you're going to be using the outfit system quite a bit. Just be sure to change your look periodically on the go to avoid police detection.
