In a recent development, for free gift cards, start with Fetch, since almost any receipt you get earns points, and add Capital One Shopping if you shop online or Microsoft Rewards if you’ll search with Bing. Save survey and game apps for spare minutes, since they pay for your time rather than for what you’d do anyway.

Whichever you pick, a legit app never makes you pay a fee, buy a gift card, or share a card’s numbers to collect what you’ve earned. The Federal Trade Commission warns that no honest business asks you to buy a gift card as payment for a prize, and that anyone who gets a card’s number and PIN can spend the money on it.

Recent findings suggest that every app here pays out in gift cards, and some pay cash too, but expect pocket money rather than a wage from any of them. You can also buy gift cards below face value for stores you use, and sell any you won’t use.

Receipt scanning apps pay on purchases you’ve already made, so the work is getting your receipts into the app after each trip.

Almost any receipt counts, from groceries to online orders, so it’s the easiest start for most people, and receipts with the brands its offers feature earn the most points.
