📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This is odd to say, but latest releases rarely hit at the right time for me. What I mean is, new things typically get dropped early season. Or they are announced on a runway half a year before they ever come out. I never see a new clothing release—I guess it’s more common in home and tech—where I say: I need this right now.

Industry observers note that but it happened this week. The first two picks on this list are really calling after my wallet. First, Bennett Winch (a favorite around here) put out its newest suede, this classy navy. Though I’m more of a browns and earth tones, it’s got me really excited to get a travel bag packed for the holidays. And right after that, two more favorites, Filson and Baracuta, put together their collective calling cards for an incredible Mackinaw wool G9. After a cold and wet week here in New York, I’m getting real close to buying.

Elsewhere, it’s all leaning a bit gift-y. Fitting as we start to get through our Esquire gift guides: Luxury gifts, gifts for your wife, the tech that everyone’s talking about. It’s small collabs, colognes, and plenty of stuff that’ll look nice under the tree.

Bennett Winch makes the most gorgeous men’s bags you can find anywhere in this world. This navy suede is the latest in a long line of incredible releases. You could walk into the snobbiest first class cabin on a JFK to CDG, and this bag will be the apple of everyone’s eye. —Luke Guillory, commerce editor.

According to recent reports, our friends at Baracuta and Filson UK cooked up some insane heat. Like the loggers wearing the original Mackinaw or mechanics wearing the original Baracuta, I shall wear this to my job: Sitting in a coffee shop writing about bullshit. —L.G.
