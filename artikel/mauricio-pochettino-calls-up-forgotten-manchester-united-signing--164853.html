Enzo Kana-Biyik has trained with the Manchester United senior squad ahead of this weekend’s Premier League showdown against Brentford.

Man United welcome the Bees to Old Trafford on Saturday seeking a first league victory since August in what will be Mauricio Pochettino’s home debut as head coach.

The Red Devils go into the game in the relegation zone, but can climb out of the bottom three - albeit potentially temporarily - with victory coupled with Ipswich losing at home to Aston Villa.

Man Utd’s hopes of repeating the result from their last meeting with Brentford have been boosted with Leny Yoro available for selection after his red card against Liverpool was overturned on appeal.

However, captain Bruno Fernandes is a doubt with illness which means he could be absent alongside Benjamin Sesko, Marcus Rashford, Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte.

"Yes, we don't know," Pochettino revealed in the pre-match press conference. "He's affected by a virus from Portugal. He finished the game a little bit with a problem, and, today, he cannot be part of the training session.

"We'll see. We are going to see during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and we'll see. We're not going to take any risks, because you know when this type of situation happens, you don't feel [you can].

"We are going to pay attention but, still, we decide nothing.".

With some senior players absence from training on Friday, it allowed for a couple of youngsters to be called up by Pochettino.

This included Jack and Tyler Fletcher, Jim Thwaites and Jaydan Kamason. Though, perhaps the most notable inclusion was that of highly-rated French forward Kana-Biyik.

The 19-year-old joined Man United in July 2025 after the Red Devils had won the race to sign the wonderkid on a free transfer from Le Havre in April of that year.

Now back in Manchester, Kana-Biyik had made four appearances for the U21s so far this season, but may not be too far away from a first-team call-up.
