📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Austin Reaves will return to the Los Angeles Lakers' lineup for their NBA Cup matchup with the San Antonio Spurs on Friday. He is listed as available on the team's injury report.

According to recent reports, reaves, 28, has been sidelined since suffering a bruised heel in a Nov. 11 win over the Milwaukee Bucks. At the time, the Lakers believed Reaves' injury wasn't serious and didn't require imaging. Head coach JJ Redick said the guard was day-to-day. However, he missed the team's next five games.

During that five-game span, the Lakers went 4-1 while facing lesser teams including the Charlotte Hornets (4-13), Utah Jazz (3-16) and Sacramento Kings (2-16).

Recent findings suggest that in 12 games this season, Reaves is averaging 24.7 points per game, second behind Luka Dončić, who leads the league with an average of 36.6 points. He's also averaging 4.7 assists and 1.2 steals while shooting 41% on 66 3-point attempts.

Recent findings suggest that the Spurs also could get guards De'Aaron Fox and Dylan Harper back for Friday's game. Both are listed as questionable.

The Lakers go into Friday's matchup with the NBA's best record at 15-3, a half-game ahead of the 15-4 Spurs.

In the NBA Cup standings, both teams have a 2-1 record in the Western Conference's Group C. San Antonio would win the group with a win and the Portland Trail Blazers losing to the Golden State Warriors. However, with a +48 point differential, the Spurs could still advance on a tiebreaker.

According to recent reports, the Lakers would prevail with a victory and a Warriors loss. But a defeat would eliminate them from the in-season tournament.
