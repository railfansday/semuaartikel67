R11 Travel Suspends Upcoming Bookings for Corazul Sailings

R11 Travel confirmed it is temporarily suspending upcoming bookings for Corazul Cruceros’ departures in Brazil.

R11 Travel, which serves as Corazul’s commercial and marketing partner, mentioned the sales suspension will continue until the manufacturer “confirms the necessary details for the resumption of operations and the official start date of its season.”.

According to a statement, the sales interruption follows the tech giant’s recent decision to extend the operational pause of the Buenavista through November 30, 2026.

As previously reported by Cruise Industry News, Corazul launched service in late October, offering short cruises from Santos and Rio de Janeiro to other Brazilian destinations.

The company paused operations just a week later, citing efforts to eliminate technical and operational challenges identified during the first sailings.

The Brazilian travel operator stated this decision aims to ensure security and commercial clarity for travel agencies and passengers.

“In ten years of operation, R11 Travel has built its reputation on credibility, transparency and commitment to the tourism market,” mentioned Ricardo Amaral, CEO of R11 Travel, which also serves as GSA for Royal Caribbean brands in Brazil.

According to recent reports, “Our commercial role demands responsibility and respect for travel agencies and guests. Therefore, until we have concrete confirmation regarding the resumption of operations and the season’s schedule, we have opted to suspend upcoming sales,” he added.

R11 Travel noted that affected guests who opted for a refund will receive a reimbursement of the cruise fare through their original form of payment.

The company estimates that requests with all the necessary and accurate information will be completed within 15 days.

In a previously issued statement, R11 mentioned that affected passengers can also choose to reschedule their cruise, advising them to contact their travel agencies within 15 days.

Presently anchored off Santos, the Buenavista was originally built for P&O Cruises and entered service in 1995.

Before being acquired by Corazul, the 1,804-passenger vessel spent seven years in China as Astro Ocean Cruises’ Piano Land.
