It has been revealed that wolves have handed the winger plenty of game time over the past 12 months or so, despite his tender age. He has become a key member of WWFC’s first-team squad over recent times.

Mane, 19, is under contract at Molineux until the summer of 2029. He has made 18 appearances for the Midlands outfit in all competitions this season, 16 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with three assists.

Maxwell has delivered his verdict on the youngster on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “Mane we hold to very high standards because we think he’s a very, very, very good player. It is always worth remembering he only turned 19 two months ago. He’s such a young player still.

“If you look at Championship minutes this season, he’s the second youngest player behind Ryan Kavuma-McQueen to have played more than 600 minutes. He’s in a little bucket there with Kavuma-McQueen, who’s still 17, then Mane and (Divine) Mukasa, who has just turned 19, and then Romelle Donovan, who is just about to turn 20.”.

Wolves will be pleased that they managed to keep hold of Mane over the summer. WWFC were relegated from the Premier League earlier this year after they finished in the bottom three along with West Ham and Burnley.

Industry observers note that mane, who is an ex-England and Portugal youth international, was linked with an exit in the last transfer window. However, nothing materialised in the end and he stayed put beyond the deadline back in early September.

Many expected the teenager to take the second tier by storm but things haven’t exactly panned out like that. He still hasn’t scored and probably hasn’t made the impact in the EFL he would have hoped to before a ball was kicked this term.

Nevertheless, he is starting to find his feet now and is a promising talent. He will get better with experience and his game is still developing.

Industry observers note that mane looked good against Charlton Athletic for Wolves last time out. He caused problems for WWFC’s last opponents and helped them win 2-1 after goals by Adam Armstrong and Rafiki Stated.

Recent findings suggest that cesar Peixoto’s side are back in action on Tuesday night as they aim to keep their momentum going. They face another match on their own patch against Derby County.

It has been revealed that the Rams are in poor form and are in the drop zone along with Burnley and Preston North End. They have won only twice in the league and are five points from safety behind both Lincoln City and Watford after their 2-0 loss at Southampton over the weekend.

Wolves will fancy their chances of securing another win to boost their promotion push as they chase down the top two Middlesbrough and Stoke City. They are presently sat in 3rd place in the table after 16 fixtures and they are in the play-offs along with Birmingham City, West Brom, Southampton, QPR and Swansea City.
