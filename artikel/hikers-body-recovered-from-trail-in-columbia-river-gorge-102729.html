📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that search and rescue personnel recovered the body of a male hiker from the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area in Oregon over the weekend. Deputies reportedly found the man deceased at Wahkeena Falls, a popular waterfall hiking destination east of Portland, when they arrived to investigate a report of an unconscious hiker on the trail.

Authorities told OregonLive they believe the man likely died of a “sudden medical event.” His identity has not been released.

It has been revealed that the Columbia River Gorge is an 80-plus-mile river canyon on the Oregon-Washington border. It is a popular outdoor recreation destination with more than 200 miles of hiking trails, including a small section of the Pacific Crest Trail near Cascade Locks, Oregon.

Wahkeena Falls is located approximately 15 miles west of the PCT.
