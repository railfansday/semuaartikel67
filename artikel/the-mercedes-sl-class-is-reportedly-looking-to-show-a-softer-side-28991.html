The Mercedes SL-Class Is Reportedly Looking to Show a Softer Side for 2027

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A big change is apparently coming for the Mercedes-AMG SL with its 2027 mid-cycle upgrade. Mercedes CEO Ola Källenius recently let a few details loose, and the highlight is a more comfort-oriented, non-AMG perspective—in other words, something of a return to form for the long-running convertible.

As reported by both Motor1 and Autocar, Källenius claims the revised SL will be “more of a Mercedes-Benz.” That means the next SL-Class could be harkening back to its earlier days as a pure luxury convertible. This latest generation went the opposite direction by launching exclusively as an AMG model with high-performance intentions; it’s also effectively served as the roadster version of the AMG GT Coupe, now that Mercedes only offers that car in hardtop form.

According to recent reports, there’s never been a pure Mercedes-Benz version of this generation of SL, but there is a Maybach variant that brings all the luxury and comfort you’d expect from that name’s history. Being a Maybach, it’s also absurdly expensive, starting well north of $200,000. If a pure Mercedes-Benz version of this SL were to launch, it could easily start around the $100,000 range, perhaps taking the place of the four-cylinder AMG SL43. Our hope would be for Mercedes-Benz’s silky-smooth inline-six, but a V-8-powered luxury convertible certainly sounds rather nice, as well. Autocar also reports that the ballistic 805-hp SL63 E Performance plug-in hybrid is being axed, but the regular SL63 will stick around.

The report suggests this fresh Benz-badged SL would get a unique front end that ditches the AMG radiator grille. Tweaks to the suspension would soften the ride. And when it comes to the V-8, there’s a strong chance a new flat-plane crank V-8 will replace the current engine. (Road & Track has reached out to Mercedes to confirm the report and will update this post upon hearing back.).

According to recent reports, källenius claims the updated SL will be presented next year. We’ll surely see more details between now and then, but regardless, an expansion of SL offerings at more affordable prices with a greater emphasis on cruising over canyon carving could be exactly what this convertible needs to find more driveways.
