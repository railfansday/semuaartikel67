Amongst the criticism and gnashing of teeth over West Ham’s failure to beat bottom side Preston North End yesterday, there were quieter, less reactionary voices who were suggesting that perhaps all is not yet lost.

This evening we feature a fan comment by Claret and Hugh reader ‘D” to highlight the considered alternative that perhaps, all is not doom and gloom just yet and that the upcoming manager – whether or not he’s your own personal choice – deserves a fair chance to sort out his three predecessors’ combined cock ups.

I left that stadium yesterday thinking I must have watched a different game to many others, and then got back home about 7 to look online at various sites and saw exactly the same sort of rubbish.

Industry observers note that let me make it clear I did not want Scott Parker as our upcoming manager but you have to give him and the team a chance. Our last 2 managers were awful but they didn’t get booed at full time after 1 game. At least we were showing more endeavour and purpose.

It took a year before the boo boys came out in force against Nuno.

It has been revealed that the only thing I would say is that we need to try to get Kante into the starting line up but not at the expense of Alvarez as he was great. I just wish he was captain. Yes Piroe missed a sitter and we hit the woodwork a number of times and do lack a creative spark which I don’t think Engels is providing, okay he did a good through ball and crossed for our goal but I want to see more.

Most articles I read said we didn’t deserve to win and some even said we were lucky to draw. That is complete garbage we did deserve to win and if a few of those chances had of gone in then the mood would be the complete opposite and SP would be lauded as the newly introduced messiah which would have also been rubbish.

There are too many reactionary idiots out there and people who booed after that hang your heads in shame. Booing the players in the early stages of a latest managerial regime is ridiculous.

Sources indicate that at least give it till Christmas…. It was a bad result, not a terrible performance”.
