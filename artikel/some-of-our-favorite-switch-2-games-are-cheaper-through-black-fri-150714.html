In a recent development, black Friday is a great time to expand your game library because it’s a chance to catch deals that aren’t normally happening throughout the year. That’s true for Nintendo Switch 2 games as well. In addition to a $50 off sale on one of its console bundles, Nintendo is also knocking $20 off some of the Switch 2’s best titles. Some of the most notable games include Donkey Kong Bananza, which is $49.99 (usually $69.99) at Amazon, Target, and Best Buy, and Super Mario Party Jamboree Plus Jamboree TV for $59.99 (usually $79.99) at Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy.

Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and GameStop are offering discounts on several more Switch 2 games, though their respective end times and availability varies. Nintendo shared that some game sales will end on November 29th. Even if deals dry up for physical games on your wishlist, digital game deals are happening through Nintendo’s eShop until 11:59PM PT on Wednesday, December 2nd.
