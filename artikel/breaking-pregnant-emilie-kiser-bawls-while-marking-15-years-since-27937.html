Lifestyle influencer Emilie Kiser has shared her heartbreak as she prepares to mark one-and-a-half years since the death of her son, Trigg.

“Bawled the whole way home from my hair appointment, just missing my Triggy so so much,” Kiser, 27, wrote over a Tuesday, November 17, Instagram Story photo that revealed her sitting in her car as tears streamed down her face. “I’m just so f***ing sad … tomorrow if a year and a half since we’ve lost Trigg and I just, literally, can’t even believe it.”.

The social media personality, who announced in July that she is pregnant after the May 2025 death of Trigg, 3, following a fall into the family’s backyard pool, added that the reality of the milestone had hit her earlier in the week.

“It already has hit me, this entire week, but … it’s so painful, and just so s***ty,” she mentioned.

A subsequent Instagram Story featured a photo of Kiser’s reddened face as she stared blankly ahead. “I genuinely hate making others uncomfortable, like, sometimes that’s how I feel crying in front of people or it being noticeable that I’ve been crying,” she wrote over the top of it. “So now avoiding going into my home because I have contractors here.”.

An earlier Instagram Story had explained that despite the sunny disposition she often exhibits via her social media posts, “social media is fake sometimes.” She added that she wasn’t immune from “masking how I really feel” and explained that it’s how she survives “most days so my brain doesn’t completely shut down.”.

Sources indicate that kiser and her husband, Brady Kiser, lost their child at their Arizona home on May 12, 2025. Brady, 30, and Trigg’s brother Teddy, 19 months, were both home when Trigg was pulled unconscious from the pool.

Police officers arrived at the scene and began performing CPR before firefighters took over life-saving measures. Trigg was subsequently flown to Phoenix Children’s Hospital for further treatment and the Chandler, Arizona, Police Department confirmed to Us on May 18, 2025, that Trigg died.

Sources indicate that on August 28 that year, Emilie broke her silence on the tragedy. “Loss of this magnitude feels impossible to put into words. I’ve spent days, weeks, months trying to find them and also take the time I’ve needed to digest the loss of my baby,” she wrote via social media at the time.

According to recent reports, she confirmed her current pregnancy via Instagram on July 3. “We are so grateful and excited to share that we will be welcoming another baby into our family 🤍🪿. This news feels very surreal and has already been such a bright light to us during some of our darkest days,” Emilie wrote at the time. “As I stated last year, my family’s privacy will always be my priority. Because of that, I’m not sure how much I will be sharing about this pregnancy and next phase of life, but I appreciate your understanding as I navigate those boundaries.”.
