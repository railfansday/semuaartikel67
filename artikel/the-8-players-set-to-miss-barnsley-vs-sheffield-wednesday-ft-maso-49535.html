The November international break is over, but the first EFL matchup back takes place between two League One sides who weren't afforded a rest over the past week.

Barnsley host South Yorkshire rivals Sheffield Wednesday in a showdown between two sides at opposite ends of the table, and two fanbases who have contrasting feelings towards their higher-ups.

Sections of the Tykes fanbase are looking to stage a protest in front of the Sky cameras against their ownership and chairman, Neerav Parekh, with the club in a continued decline and facing the grim possibility of a first fourth-tier campaign in 47 years.

Their opponents know all about negative feelings towards their ownership, but this is a Wednesday side reborn under the stewardship of David Storch and on the hunt for an immediate promotion back to the Championship.

Dave Challinor's looking for his first win on the road as Wednesday boss, and three points could see them up to third before everyone else plays. Meanwhile, Barnsley need the points to avoid being cut adrift early in this relegation battle.

These eight players won't be available for either side in this significant Friday night clash under the lights at Oakwell.

Barnsley didn't have a great time at Wigan Athletic last Saturday, losing 2-1 to one of their bottom four neighbours before action began.

What was made worse during the game was the realisation that their captain, Cameron McGeehan, would be suspended for Friday night's clash with Sheffield Wednesday after he picked up his fifth yellow card of the season midway through the first half.

The Northern Irishman is Barnsley's leading goalscorer both in all competitions (9) and League One (4), including the equaliser at the Brick Community Stadium last weekend. He'll be a massive miss for Daniel Stendel's side.

Centre-back Jack Shepherd also remains on the sidelines, as he sees out the final game in his three-match ban after he saw red for a crunching challenge on Louie Sibley nine minutes into their 1-1 draw with Oxford United on Halloween.

Talented teenager Arley Kay has moved from full-back into the centre over the last couple of games, and will likely do so again against Wednesday.

Recent findings suggest that tobi Adeyemo started five straight for Barnsley after making the post-window free-agent arrival after leaving Watford in the summer. He spent part of last season on loan at Crawley Town, where he netted once in 12 League Two games.

He's missed the last two in all competitions, though, and Reds boss Daniel Stendel revealed he'd suffered a training injury which will keep him out for some weeks.

Shepherd's suspension has meant that Barnsley have been light on centre-backs in recent weeks, with Blackburn Rovers loanee Connor O'Riordan also sidelined.

In a recent development, the Irishman has been absent for a month now, since their 2-1 defeat at Luton Town. He was initially out through the concussion protocols, but faces time out now thanks to a shoulder injury in training, which may require surgery.

Sheffield Wednesday's availability list is easing up, with Dave Challinor possibly able to integrate six players who missed the win over Plymouth Argyle last weekend into the side on Friday night.

It has been revealed that left-back Max Lowe remains out, though, after he reaggravated a previous injury against Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup a couple of weeks ago. The 29-year-old should still be out for around a month.

Lowe's injury hit especially hard, given that his back-up at left-back, Reece James, is also missing with a groin injury, and shouldn't be back until around mid-December.

Sources indicate that that led Challinor to deploy Jamal Lowe at left wing-back against Plymouth, a rather apt replacement given he's Max's namesake, and the striker did quite well. We'll see if the Wednesday boss opts for the same at Oakwell.

Davis Keillor-Dunn has been the main man to lead the line so far for Challinor, but it would have been intriguing to see if that had been the case if Mason Burstow was fit and firing.
