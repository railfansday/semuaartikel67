This year, Black Friday falls on Friday, Nov. 27 (the day after Thanksgiving). However, the sales actually started a lot sooner. Amazon and Best Buy debuted their official Black Friday 2026 sales on Nov. 19, Target began its savings event on Nov. 22, and Walmart kicked things off on Nov. 23.

Cyber Monday falls on Monday, Nov. 30 this year. While some stores will have a one-day-only sale on that date, most online retailers have evolved into offering a full "Cyber Week" sales event.

This year, you can find discounts of up to 50% or more on Bose QuietComfort Headphones, Hisense and LG TVs, and Amazon Echo smart home devices. You can also find substantial discounts on virtually every Apple products, including record-low prices on the entire Apple Watch lineup, plus savings on MacBook and AirPods. For budget shoppers looking for the best tech deals under $50, we recommend checking out the JLab JBuds Lux ANC Headphones and Anker charging products.
