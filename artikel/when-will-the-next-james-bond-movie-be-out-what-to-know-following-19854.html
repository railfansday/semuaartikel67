Robert Pattinson has officially been unveiled as the newly introduced 007, and now we’re left wondering: Just when will we see him on our screens as the famous secret agent in movie history?

The 40-year-old actor was confirmed earlier this afternoon to be playing the role of James Bond by Amazon MGM Studios. He said of taking on the famous spy role, “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”.

Rob takes on the role following Daniel Craig’s departure as the spy in the most recent Bond movie No Time to Die in 2021.

Industry observers note that given it’s already been five years since we last had a James Bond movie, just how long will we have to wait for the next one? As of now, we don’t have an official release date for the new James Bond movie. However, given it’s November 2026 and Rob’s role has only just been revealed, it seems unlikely we can expect the movie to be released next year.

In a recent development, according to an April 2026 report from The Sun, it’s unlikely that a new movie would be coming out until at least 2028, due to director Denis Villeneuve wanting to take a break after the Dune franchise comes to an end. Dune: Part Three, which also stars Rob, comes out on December 18, 2026.

A source told the publication, “Bond won’t be back until 2028. The production mindset’s gone from ‘Let’s hurry this through’ to ‘Make sure we get this right.’ Denis wanted a long break after Dune 3 and has some broad ideas on the script. Steven will get on with drafts, before they map out the final version. That’ll take at least the whole summer.”.

They added, “Realistically, if they can make January 2027 work for pre-production, then that will be a success.”.

As for when we can expect the movie to come out in 2028, Digital Spy reports there are two dates booked out in Amazon’s release calendar—April 21 and August 4—for an “Untitled Amazon MGM Event Film.”.

According to recent reports, in 2022, Amazon acquired MGM Studios, and in 2025, they reached an agreement for a fresh joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, who controlled the creative rights to Bond. This new venture meant that “Amazon MGM Studios will gain creative control of the James Bond franchise following closing of the transaction.”.

Industry observers note that and since February 2025, the hunt has been on for the next leading actor to take on the role of James Bond. Also reportedly said to be in the running for the role of Bond were Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, and Josh O’Connor, among (many) others.
