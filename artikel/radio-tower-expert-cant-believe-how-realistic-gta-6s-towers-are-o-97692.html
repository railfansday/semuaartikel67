According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6 is a massive game, yes, but if you dig into everything inside of it, you’ll quickly discover that nearly every inch of this big world is filled with incredibly realistic details. Case in point: a content creator who has also worked on large communication towers was recently stunned by GTA 6′s attention to detail. Folks, we got Erathed tower legs.

Sources indicate that content creator MrMcg92, who primarily plays first-person shooters and military sims, is also someone who has, in real life, worked on and climbed large communication and radio towers. So whenever he comes across a radio tower in a video game, he takes some time to do a “Tower Inspection.” He’s looked at towers in Battlefield, Days Gone, and Far Cry. And while all of these towers had some nice details, they often made mistakes or were missing vital bits MrMcg92 would expect to find on a real comms tower. That wasn’t the case when he did a tower inspection in GTA 6. Instead, he was flabbergasted and impressed by the tower’s hyper-realistic details.

“Oh my fucking god, boys,” exclaimed MrMcg92 when walking up to a radio tower in GTA 6. “For the first time ever, we got the tower legs earthed. Earthed tower legs with saddles. And double locknuts on the HDs, lads. Double lock nuts everywhere!”.

He then spotted a red power cable running up the middle of the tower and claimed that if there was an aircraft warning light at the top, he would get a “stiffy.”Â As he climbed the tower, he continued to be impressed by how Rockstar’s artists included every tiny detail that should be found on a real-life tower like this.

Sources indicate that “Oh, boys, we got proper AA clamps on the mount points. Tails running out the bottom of the panels to the RRU,” pointed out the creator on his climb up the tower. I don’t really know what half of this means, but based on his past videos, expert knowledge, and excitement, I get the feeling this is all just very, very impressive.

And yes, at the top was indeed a red warning light.

I hope somewhere an artist at Rockstar who helped with this tower sees this video and gets to feel really proud of what they made.
