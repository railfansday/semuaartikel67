Jury still not out on 23-year-old’s Millwall future amid eventful League One loan

Sources indicate that millwall were a lot more selective in who was sent out on loan this campaign after failing to reap the rewards from the players who had spells away from The Den last season. The Lions sanctioned season-long exits for Ra’ees Bangura-Williams, Rafiq Lamptey, Ajay Matthews and Kamarl Grant.

It is the latter who is under greater pressure to succeed in his stint away from the South London outfit, despite signing a contract extension in 2025. Grant was loaned to Bromley in the summer to make it his third spell at the Ravens.

Industry observers note that the 23-year-old is in the fourth loan of his career. He is also yet to feature in the Championship for Millwall. Time is running out for him to prove himself to Alex Neil, and the defender has a tough task to do so in League One.

Grant has been a part of one of the worst defences in the third tier this campaign. The Ravens are averaging 2.54 goals conceded per game as they navigate their first season in League One.

Karl Robinson’s side have an xG against of 1.98. Although the 23-year-old has featured in 11 of their 13 games this term, the blame can’t entirely be put on him. The defender has made 46 recoveries for Bromley and blocked 10 shots.

According to recent reports, grant’s best display this campaign came against league leaders Huddersfield Town during a 2-1 victory. He made 11 defensive contributions and won 100% of his ground duels. The Millwall academy graduate also made seven clearances and wasn’t dribbled past during the 90 minutes.

Recent findings suggest that the Lions will be encouraged by that performance, in particular, given the Terriers’ quality. It does show that despite Bromley’s overall disappointment, the 23-year-old is standing out.

Sources indicate that with Grant having mixed success on loan at Bromley, Millwall’s decision regarding his future will be far from made up. One thing that points to the 23-year-old still having a chance to break through for Neil’s side is their lack of depth at centre-back.

Jake Cooper is in his final year of his contract and Danny Batth likely won’t be kept on for another campaign. Elkan Baggott and Caleb Taylor are the only other senior options, with the latter certain to take up one of the spots next season.

If Grant can help lower Bromley’s leaky record at the back, then he has every chance of playing a part for Millwall next term. That being said, should an offer come in for the 23-year-old next summer, the Lions would likely entertain a deal.

Recent findings suggest that the South London outfit agreed to Adam Mayor’s permanent exit after he struggled to get into the first team, and the defender is in a similar boat. This season will be telling as to whether the Lions’ academy graduate has a future at The Den or not.
