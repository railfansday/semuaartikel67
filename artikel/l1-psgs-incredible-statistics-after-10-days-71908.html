It has been revealed that at Paris Saint-Germain, the danger does not come from just one man, but from everywhere. This season, the capital club displays impressive offensive diversity with no less than 12 different players having already found the net, for a total of 22 goals. A collective strength which perfectly illustrates the richness of the Parisian squad and its ability to make a difference in all areas of the pitch. Whether through its attackers, its midfielders or even its defenders, PSG has numerous solutions to shake the opposing nets.

In this collective, Ferran Torres currently stands out with four goals on the clock. Behind him, Achraf Hakimi and Marquinhos follow with three goals each, while Mika Godts, Senny Mayulu and Illia Zabarnyi have two. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, Vitinha, Ousmane Dembélé, Désiré Doué, Maghnes Akliouche and Lucas Beraldo also participated in this offensive dynamic. A distribution of goals which states a lot about the Parisian strike force: in Paris, each player can become a danger and change a match.
