Industry observers note that are you wondering how connected the worlds of GTA 5 and GTA 6 are? Well, I've got some news for you. Keep on reading below if you'd like some very minor spoilers for the early game of GTA 6, spoilers for GTA 5, as well as some information about how the fresh game links to GTA 5 and other Rockstar games.

Right, if you're reading from this point onwards, you're clearly here for the tea. In the first hour or so of GTA 6, when you finally get control of Jason and head to your first property (the green stilted house) in the Leonida Keys, you will be able to explore a little. On my way to do a workout - you need to keep Jason a peak physical instrument for his work's sake, right? Right? - I happened upon a fitness magazine resting on a table on the decking out front.

According to recent reports, called 'Body Copy' - a joke I appreciate as a former magazine editor, naturally - this publication features a few different articles. There's Morning Glory, where Nolan Crockett jacks you with his early workout; Skindecent Exposure, a photo spread about why beaches are 'crazy' places to work out; Shrediquette, a piece dedicated to proper in-gym decorum; and Twilight Tantra, an interview feature and yoga masterclass with 'gerontophile' Fabien LaRouche.

Industry observers note that hang on, Fabien LaRouche… isn't that the French wellness guru and shyster that was a "close friend" to Amanda De Santa in Grand Theft Auto 5? Yes, yes it is. In fact, Fabien played quite a large role in that game, acting as the antagonistic force that eventually fractured Michael and Amanda's relationship. After conducting an affair under the guise of 'yoga lessons', Amanda left our protagonist, and moved in with Fabien, taking Michael's kids - Jimmy and Tracy - with her. In a totally healthy and normal way, you could follow their new life via updates posted to GTA's Facebook surrogate, LifeInvader. It's clear the kids never liked the pretentious, annoying man - but for Amanda, the penny is a little slower to drop. Eventually, Amanda and Michael reconcile their differences - with the latter whalloping Fabien in the process. The married couple pledge to resolve their differences, and Fabien is never seen again.

Until GTA 6, at least. Here, in what we assume is supposed to be 2025 or 2026, it's been about 13 years since we last saw the character (it is widely agreed that GTA 5 is set in 2013 - the game's original launch year). And it seems that the events surrounding Michael and his family really did a number on Fabien. Which - of course - he blamed on the universe, rather than taking any personal responsibility. Naturally.

According to recent reports, "Ever since the karmic decay of Los Santos was made perfectly clear to him in the early 2010s, yoga instructor Fabien LaRouche has been on an epic pilgrimage of enlightenment through the remotest ashrams and finest 5-star resorts and plastic surgery retreats of The East," reads the Body Copy article. "Having completed his chakric education and returned to the United States, we meet with him on the final week of his six-month residency at Dwindle Wood Retirement Community.".

Here, the article goes on to explore Fabien's penchant for older women - he seems to like their nature, their desire to learn about self-improvement and wellness, and (perhaps most importantly) their bank accounts. "Being over the hill doesn't necessarily mean you have nothing left to live for," he opines in the article. Thanks for that, Fabien.

It's interesting to see - very early in the game and quite plainly telegraphed - that Rockstar is willing to reintroduce us to people from previous games. This isn't a megaton addition by any means - Fabien was never a 'main character' - but I love that a nod to him, in a feature tucked away at the back of a magazine, sets the scene for more cameos, perhaps, later in the game. It reveals us, nice and early, that GTA 5 and GTA 6 are indeed connected. By inference, then, we know that GTA 4 is included in that same picture; there were recurring characters in GTA 5 that appeared in GTA 4, after all - Patrick McReary, Johnny Klebitz, Rocco Pelosi and more come to mind.

Recent findings suggest that it may seem like a small thing, but it's just another example of Rockstar using its expansive toolset, ridiculous budget, and well-established legacy to inject a sense of scale into the world of Grand Theft Auto 6. There are other nods to the universe out there, too. I won't spoil them here, but I've been a little bit dewy-eyed to hear rumours of some of the other nods to Rockstar titles hidden in the world of Leonida: it makes all the experiences I've had in past games, the emotional response I've had to previous Rockstar worlds, feel in some way valid and meaningful here, too. I'm looking forward to seeing what other little secrets await me across the keys and into the glowing neon promise of Vice City proper.
