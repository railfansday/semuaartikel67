Jude Bellingham underlined his class once more with a stunning goal as Real Madrid came from behind to thump PSV Eindhoven 5-1.

With Real trailing 1-0 at half-time, the England superstar - who started as a substitute to rest - was one of three changes made by Jose Mourinho and his impact was felt 15 minutes later in an emphatic style.

Collecting a loose ball from around 25 yards outside the box, Bellingham controlled it with his left foot, before flicking it up with his right foot and volleying it with the outside of that boot into the far corner of the PSV goal. The pace and precision of the strike was breathtaking as it flew past Matej Kovar and hit the inside of the post before going in.

It was a strike that was befitting of any match, and it proved to be the decisive one as that made it 2-1 to the hosts before they later ran riot.

Speaking post-match, Bellingham was clear where he ranked that finish, declaring: 'I think this was the best goal of my life.'.

He later elaborated to TNT Sports about his goal: 'They're [his goals] normally a lot closer, aren't they,' he joked.

Bellingham came off the bench to put Real ahead with this beautiful right-footed volley.

PSV goalkeeper Matej Kovar could only watch on as the ball flew past him and go into the net.

'That one was a nice one, it popped up for me nicely and I just thought I'd hit it with the outside of my boot.

'I'm not one for smashing the ball too hard and it was a nice moment and important for the win as well.'.

Bellingham's performance, he also set up Vinicius Jr for the fifth goal, was acknowledged by Mourinho and Real captain Fede Valverde, who scored their equaliser, post-match too.

In a recent development, 'Obviously Jude is a different type of player and he gave us what we didn't have in the first half,' Mourinho said. 'He changed the game, scored an incredible goal.'.

It has been revealed that valverde concurred declaring: 'He did well. We were struggling to keep the ball behind their midfielders and when Jude came in he gave us that tranquillity to control possession.'.

The strike comes a day after Daily Mail Sport reported that the 23-year-old is in advance talks over a upcoming Real Madrid contract that will make him one of the best-paid players in Europe.

Madrid have been eager to tie down the in-form England midfielder beyond his current contract, understood to be worth around £12.5million a year after tax, which runs until 2029, in recognition of his performances.

Talks were initiated in September over an extension until 2031 and have been progressing well since with sources at Madrid confident they are close to reaching a conclusion.

Bellingham is in advance contract talks with Real Madrid over signing an extension until 2031.

Bellingham is expected to be handed improved terms to put him on par with team-mate Vinicius Jr, who agreed a new deal last summer reputedly on around £500,000 a week after tax.

As for Tuesday's win over PSV, Real are now top of the Champions League league phase standings after maintaining their 100 per cent record in the competition this season with a fifth-straight win.

However, they didn't have it all their own way as PSV took a deserved lead in the 32nd minute from the penalty spot, with 37-year-old captain Ivan Perisic showing all his experience, ignoring the whistles ringing out around the Bernabeu before sending Thibaut Courtois the wrong way.

They almost doubled their lead before the break as the unmarked Kodai Sano glanced a header off target as the visitors continued to cause Real serious problems.

Real equalised five minutes after the break, thanks to Arda Guler's quick-thinking, with the Turkish midfielder flicking a backheeled pass to Valverde in the area, who steered his first-time shot into the bottom corner.

Real's fans were on their feet 10 minutes later, scarcely believing what they had seen, while Bellingham's team-mates looked similarly shell-shocked.

Marc Cucurella walked away with his head in his hands in wonder while the other Real players rushed to congratulate the scorer.

Real took command after Bellingham's goal and punished a loose pass in the PSV defence, Guler finding the unmarked Mbappe to extend the France forward's scoring run to seven successive Champions League appearances.

Mbappe added his second before Vinicius rounded off a resounding victory from Bellingham's pass, underlining the Spanish side's credentials as serious contenders to take Paris Saint-Germain's crown.
