📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that when Apple dropped the MacBook Neo earlier this year, it lit a fire under the companies that make Windows laptops to compete. One of the best of those is the Dell XPS 13, which is down to $549.99 directly from Dell, undercutting Apple on price for Black Friday.

The Dell XPS 13 with an Intel Core 5 320, 8GB of RAM, and 256 GB of storage.

Apple's base MacBook Neo has an A18 Pro chip, 256GB of storage, and 8GB of RAM. A solid alternative with a more powerful SoC.

This deal applies to the base model, with an Intel Core 5 320 "WIldcat Lake" CPU, 8GB of memory, and a 256GB SSD.Unlike the MacBook Neo, you get backlit keys and a higher-resolution 2560 x 1600 touchscreen.

Dell's system doesn't offer as much performance as the MacBook Neo (and graphics-intensive programs are out of the question), but for people who mostly use the web browser and office applications, it will do the job.Dell is undercutting the sale price for the MacBook Neo we've seen on Amazon and Best Buy by $100, making it a great price for this extremely solid competitor in the budget space.
