Whether you're into deadpan showdowns, niche hot takes, or simply wholesome interviews between creative minds, here's what we reckon is worth your time (and of course, we'd never leave out Mashable's own Say More, what are you, nuts?).

Druski followed up his hit SNL hosting gig with a round of Hot Ones and it requires much glasses-defogging. The internet-favourite comedian, Mashable 101 alumnus, and Collect & Praise star otherwise known as Drew Desbordes joined First We Feast's Sean Evans for the hot wing challenge. The pair chatted about Druski's career, from early content creation days to his upcoming Netflix series and working with Emma Stone and hosting SNL. And it gets...dramatic. *.

How to watch: YouTube, First We Feast, Instagram, TikTok.

In a recent development, for Mashable's Say More this week, entertainment reporter Belen Edwards sat down with Sunrise on the Reaping stars Mckenna Grace and Ben Wang for a game of Choose Your Squad. Who's in the ultimate Hunger Games team for these two stars? You'll have to watch.

How to watch/listen: Mashable, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok.

Happy Friendsgiving from Matt Rogers, Bowen Yang, and Ariana Grande on this phenomenal episode of Las Culturistas. It's a return visit, with the Wicked star joining the podcast two years ago for the legendary 2024 press tour. But this week, Grande's Focker-In-Law run brought her back to the couch with a tale about what Salina EsTitties brought for her family's white elephant gift exchange one year.

Owen Thiele interviewing Hunter Schafer on In Your Dreams? Cancel my looks.

It has been revealed that the Blade Runner 2099 star joined the content creator and Adults star for Thiele's slumber party-style podcast. It's a genuinely charming watch, with Schafer chatting about making impact as a trans lead on a major TV show, moving on from Euphoria, pivoting to action sets with the Blade Runner franchise, her start in fashion getting into London's Central Saint Martins, the joy of spending money on vintage clothes instead of property, toxic texting habits, and possibilities for her next project.

And if you haven't watched Schafer's attendance in Brittany Broski's Royal Court, gather ye round for squeals.*.

How to watch/listen: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram.

Sources indicate that geraldine Viswanathan joined Mashable 101 alumnus Drew Afualo-Tanuvasa on The Comment Section and they outfit-twinned. In this delightful chat, the pair unpack Viswanathan's career from Blockers to Mike Schur's newly introduced series Dig, dealing with misogynistic Australian men, and the joy of scrapbooking, junk journaling, and DIY magnets. A treat!
