Arsenal are eyeing Stuttgart central defender Finn Jeltsch ahead of the January transfer window, according to reports in Germany.

Jeltsch made his first-team debut for Stuttgart back in February 2025 having joined the club from German second-tier outfit Nurnberg earlier that winter.

The 20-year-old has gone on to establish himself as one of the brightest young talents in Bundesliga football, making 62 appearances in all competitions across his two-and-a-half year spell in Stuttgart.

Towards the end of last season, Jeltsch got his hands on his first piece of silverware at club level as Stuttgart got the better of Arminia Bielefel to win the German Cup for the first time in 28 years.

There was another career highlight for Jeltsch just last week as he made his senior international debut as a second-half substitute in Germany’s thumping 5-0 victory over the Netherlands in the Nations League.

According to Bild, Jeltsch’s outstanding form has not gone unnoticed overseas and a number of clubs have had Jeltsch on their radar for ‘quite some time’, including Arsenal.

It’s claimed the reigning Premier League champions are ‘closely monitoring’ Jeltsch’s progress from afar and will continue to check on the centre-half over the coming weeks and months.

Liverpool are also being credited with a strong interest in a hotly contested race which will only become more competitive if Jeltsch continues on the same meteoric trajectory.

The report adds that Stuttgart are yet to receive any concrete offers for Jeltsch, who has a market value of around €35million (£29m).

‘He wants to develop, be professional, and pursue his goals with all his might.’.
