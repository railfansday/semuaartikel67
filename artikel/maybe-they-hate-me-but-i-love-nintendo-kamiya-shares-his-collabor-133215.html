📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that it has been a long old time since we've heard anything from Hideki Kamiya's YouTube channel. The legendary designer kept us fed with gossip, grievances, and game dev stories a couple of years back, but things have been quiet for almost a year now. Until this week, that is!

It has been revealed that yes, Kamiya has uploaded a new video, and it's just as packed with interesting tidbits as you would hope. One part that particularly caught our attention, however, was when the dev started talking about studios he'd love to collaborate with in the future, including a certain Significant N.

"Maybe they hate me, but I love Nintendo," Kamiya jokes in his latest video (translated by Automaton), "so I think we may be able to do something together".

He doesn't go into any detail about what a Clovers X Nintendo collab might look like, but hey, there's nothing wrong with trying to manifest something by speaking it into existence.

It has been revealed that kamiya's collaboration bucket list doesn't end there, mind you. He goes on to say that he'd be game to work with Sony or Microsoft, Koei Tecmo on an Omega Force sequel, Square Enix on a King's Knight sequel, and Bandai Namco on a Tag Team Match: M.U.S.C.L.E. game. Dream substantial, eh!

Sources indicate that perhaps most eye-catching, he also says that he'd like to team up with Konami on a Castlevania game: “We’ll definitely make Castlevania. If you let us work on it, we can make something truly insane, so just contact us and we’ll be at your service”. There's a bit of classic Kamiya definitive language going on there, so we wouldn't take that as an announcement just yet — especially while he still has Okami 2 in the pipeline.

Industry observers note that they are some lofty goals, but we have to respect the ambition. Kamiya founded his new studio, Clovers, after leaving PlatinumGames back in 2023. He is currently working on the aforementioned Okami sequel with Capcom, where he will be back in the Director's seat.
