Industry observers note that while the urge to be an absolute menace in Grand Theft Auto 6 is strong, nothing stops you from playing as a law-abiding citizen who sticks to the speed limit between missions. This option to avoid attracting local law enforcement's attention will probably be best experienced on PS5, thanks to one key advantage: the DualSense controller and its adaptive triggers. On PS5, GTA 6 players can keep their vehicle from going over the speed limit, as the adaptive triggers provide resistance when you get close to the legal limit. That doesn't mean that you can't break past that barrier, but the adaptive triggers will help you match traffic speed most of the time and stick to legal restrictions. For example, you can summon your car via a smartphone app, which involves a Scooter Bros. employee appearing at your location, delivering your ride, and then disappearing into the sunset with the little two-wheeled ride they stashed in your trunk. Cars appear to be more vital than ever in GTA 6, and stealing a fresh set of wheels is also something that has a little more friction to it, as you could boost a junker with an empty tank of fuel or an expensive sports car that has GPS tracking inside of it.
