The holiday shopping season is here, and Apple Watch is always a popular gift. Apple’s lineup includes the affordable Apple Watch SE 3 and the more advanced Apple Watch Series 12.

Here’s how those two Apple Watch models stack up….

According to recent reports, the Apple Watch SE 3 is the most affordable way to enter the Apple Watch ecosystem. It got a massive upgrade last year, adding a variety of new higher-end Apple Watch features. It now offers an Always-On display, sleep apnea notifications, temperature sensing, interactive wrist flick and double tap gestures, and more.

In a recent development, the Apple Watch SE 3 offers 18 hours of battery life, though you can stretch that to 32 hours in Low Power Mode. It supports fast charging, going from 0-80% in 45 minutes.

Even though it hasn’t been updated in a year, the Apple Watch SE 3 is a very compelling option, especially for people fresh to the Apple Watch ecosystem. It’s pretty fantastic that you can get an Apple Watch with key display and health-monitoring features for under $200.

Recent findings suggest that the Apple Watch Series 12 is Apple’s newest smartwatch, and it’s a significant patch over its predecessor. This year, Apple doubled down on the health and fitness capabilities of Apple Watch with an all-newly introduced Health Sensing System.

Apple touts that the Apple Watch Series 12 delivers “the most accurate heart rate sensing in a wearable.” In fact, the company conducted a study of more than 1,000 participants that found the Series 12 “achieved statistically better overall accuracy than every competing wearable it tested across workouts and daily activities.”.

With the newly introduced system, Apple Watch Series 12 captures your heart rate every five seconds. Previous Apple Watch models, including the Apple Watch SE 3, take measurements every five minutes. The new Health Sensing System also enables a new readiness score feature, which factors in your recent activity, training load, vitals, and sleep score.

It has been revealed that as I wrote in my review, the Apple Watch Series 12 brings some of the biggest improvements to Apple Watch health tracking in years. The fresh sensing system unlocks a trove of new data along with better accuracy.

The Apple Watch lineup is incredibly strong right now. Whether you’re deciding between the SE 3 and Series 12, both offer great health tracking, battery life, and connectivity. The Apple Watch SE 3 is an excellent choice for people fresh to the Apple Watch ecosystem.

Sources indicate that the Apple Watch Series 12, however, is the best Apple Watch for most people. There are major improvements to the health tracking capabilities, offering meaningful steps forward in what the Apple Watch can tell you about yourself. In the two months I’ve had mine, I’ve come to appreciate the more frequent heart rate measurement and readiness score.
