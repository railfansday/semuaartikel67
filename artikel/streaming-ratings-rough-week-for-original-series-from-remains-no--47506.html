Streaming Ratings: Rough Week for Original Series, ‘From’ Remains No. 1 Overall

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that netflix’s Love Is Blind led the original series chart in Nielsen’s streaming ratings for the week of Nov. 2-8. But that’s not saying a whole lot — it was a very soft week for original streaming shows.

In a recent development, on the acquired series chart, however, From put up more significant numbers. The sci-fi/horror series led the overall rankings for a third straight week.

Love Is Blind’s 691 million minutes of viewing time were enough to lead the original series top 10, but that total is the lowest for the top original show in more than two years. The last time the No. 1 original was lower was in early August 2024, when The Boys had 662 million viewing minutes. The top 10 originals as a whole totaled 4.95 billion minutes of watch time, down 21 percent from the previous week. Nobody Wants This (559 million minutes) and The Diplomat (545 million) finished second and third among original series.

Industry observers note that the overall top 10 was down week to week as well, but not by as much (13 percent, from 9.03 billion to 7.84 billion minutes). From tallied 1.69 billion minutes of viewing on Netflix (the show originates on MGM+), nearly doubling the 857 million minutes for the second-place show, Grey’s Anatomy.

Some of the declines might be due to viewers following midterm election results. But not all: During the week of the 2024 presidential election, three original series drew more than a billion minutes of watch time.

The Major Bang Theory recorded its 53rd straight week on the charts with 817 million viewing minutes on HBO Max. The series has the second-longest current streak in the rankings, a little behind Grey’s (57 weeks) … and a lot behind Bluey (801 million minutes on Disney+), which is at 214 weeks or more than four consecutive years.

Sources indicate that disaster film The Day After Tomorrow led the movie rankings with 404 million minutes; it was added to Netflix on Nov. 1. Netflix’s original holiday movie A Dog’s Perfect Christmas (264 million minutes) finished third, and perennials Dr. Seuss’ The Grinch (184 million) and Home Alone (177 million) made their first appearances of the year. Both did so earlier than usual — they usually don’t show up until around Thanksgiving or early December.
