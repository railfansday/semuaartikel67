David Raum should be top of Man United's left-back shortlist ahead of the next window as he continues to shine for RB Leipzig.

Mauricio Pochettino has only been at Old Trafford for a week, but will already know the size of the huge task that lies ahead for him and his staff.

The upcoming Man United boss saw his team capitulate to rivals Liverpool in his first game at the helm and has already set his stall out before January.

Widespread reports have claimed that a upcoming left-back is among his priorities as the Red Devils struggle for quality and depth.

Raum has been continually linked to the Old Trafford outfit over the last six months and is presently in some of the best form of his career.

The 28-year-old's latest performance and recent showings prove exactly why United must start to accelerate a move ahead of other targets.

Raum was linked to United over the summer, but they decided against bolstering their left-back options, to the dismay of many fans.

The 40-time German international has continued to feature regularly for Leipzig in the Bundesliga and Champions League, with two goals and assists each in 16 appearances so far.

He has two strikes and one assist in his last three outings, including the opener in their 3-1 Champions League win over Lens on Tuesday night.

Raum was key to Leipzig's home win, as aside from his early free-kick, he also created the joint-most chances (4), played the joint-most passes into the final third (11) and had the most touches (106) and accurate passes (75) of any player on the pitch.

He is among the top-performing Champions League left-backs so far this term for chances created (3.12, 95th percentile), successful crosses (1.87, 95th percentile) and accurate long balls (3.53, 96th percentile) per 90 minutes.

United have been linked in recent weeks, and according to a recent report from BILD, Leipzig are ready to let Raum leave for a suitable offer in January.

His contract with the club expires in 2027, and the Red Devils must take advantage of the situation to sign one of the best left-backs on the continent in the upcoming year.

United fans are bound to question whether Raum's talents will transfer to the Premier League from the more end-to-end Bundesliga.

They should look at praise of him from one of the world's best, with Yamal previously crediting the German with giving him his toughest full-back test in 2024.

According to recent reports, after Spain had faced off against hosts Germany in the Euro 2024 quarter-finals, the world-renowned winger said: "David Raum has been the toughest opponent I've had to face. In the Euros, he was tough to beat. He's a solid defender.".
