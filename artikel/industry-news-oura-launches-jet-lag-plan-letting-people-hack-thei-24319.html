According to recent reports, jet Lag Plan builds a plan designed just for you. It uses your flight itinerary along with your sleep patterns and chronotype to craft a series of recommendations. It produces a personalized schedule around sleep, light exposure, activity, and caffeine for journeys that cross three or more time zones.

The feature uses Oura's AI-powered support, Oura Advisor, to adjust the blueprint as travel changes, in case things don't go as expected. Jet Lag Plan will be available globally to users with Gen 3 or newer hardware on both iOS and Android, as long as users have their language set to English.
