📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6 is a single player game, but Sony’s servers are taking a hammering as the game goes online around the world.

While you can’t play Rockstar’s latest with other people, you will need to verify your digital license once the title is available in your region – a timely reminder of the digital era we’re all now living in.

As the game has released in New Zealand, Australia, and parts of Asia, the PSN servers have held up sturdily – but there have been sporadic reports of outages over the past few hours, as Europe and the UK prepares to play.

According to Sony’s own Service Status Page, everything is working as it should. A quick reboot to your console is a quick way to remedy most challenges.

Remember, you won’t be able to play GTA 6 without the PS5’s latest firmware update v14.23.00 installed, so make sure you’ve got that downloaded and ready.

We’ll continue to monitor the situation as GTA 6’s all-crucial American launch nears, but fingers crossed there are no issues and the launch goes flawlessly.
