Root and mycelium pathways shape soil carbon dynamics in hyper-arid regions

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Researchers led by professor Zeng Fanjiang and associate professor Gao Yanju from the Xinjiang Institute of Ecology and Geography of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (XIEG) have revealed the contrasting yet complementary roles of plant roots and mycorrhizal mycelia in shaping soil organic carbon (SOC) dynamics during hyper-arid afforestation. Their study was published in New Phytologist on Nov. 4.
