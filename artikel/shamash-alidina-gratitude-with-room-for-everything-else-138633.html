Gratitude practices sometimes feel like one more thing to get right, as if we’re supposed to feel thankful on cue no matter what’s happening.

Industry observers note that our guest this week is Shamash Alidina. He offers a gentler approach. You make room for whatever you’re feeling, and then you see whether one small, ordinary thing you appreciate can sit alongside it.

Read and practice this meditation on your own, pausing after each step. Or listen to the guided audio.

According to recent reports, shamash Alidina has been practising mindfulness since 1998 and runs his own successful training organisation. He is the author of Mindfulness For Dummies and most recently, The Mindful Way Through Stress. He frequently pops up in newspapers, magazines and on radio highlights. Based in London, he runs online trainings and speaks at conferences all over the world. He’s been teaching mindfulness full-time since 2010.
