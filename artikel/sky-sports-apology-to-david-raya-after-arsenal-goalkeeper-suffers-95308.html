Sky Sports commentator Bill Leslie was forced to offer a swift apology in the wake of the injury David Raya sustained during Saturday’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

The Gunners dropped two points after letting a commanding position slip at St James’ Park, but the damage done to their No.1 goalkeeper may end up being more significant than their failure to extend an impressive winning run.

Mikel Arteta confirmed in his post-match press conference that he would not learn the extent of the injury until the Spain international undergoes a scan.

Raya was hurt shortly after Newcastle had overturned a first-half deficit and taken the lead through Malick Thiaw.

The former Brentford star collapsed under seemingly little pressure from William Osula as he collected a corner in the 70th minute, but Leslie’s initial reaction implied the 31-year-old was trying to initiate a tactical timeout.

Instead, it swiftly became apparent that Raya was in serious pain and would be unable to take any further part in a match that Arsenal ultimately earned a point from as a result of Noni Madueke’s excellent equaliser.

‘David Raya does indeed look in some discomfort,’ noted Leslie as Raya made his way off the pitch.

In a recent development, unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news, and he will get assessed now. He will probably get an MRI scan tomorrow, to see what exactly it is. At the moment, I don’t know.

In a recent development, it was a knock, I don’t know how it happened or what the injury is – we’ll have to wait and see. We know how important David is for us, but tomorrow we’ll have more news.

‘Apologies if I did him a disservice there because it really didn’t look like significant contact with Osula.’.

According to recent reports, co-commentator Chris Sutton added: ‘Raya is holding his arm. It looks like there’s some sort of sprain.
