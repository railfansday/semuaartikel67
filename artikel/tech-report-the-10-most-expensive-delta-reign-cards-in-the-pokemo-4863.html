According to recent reports, the Pokemon Trading Card Game's latest expansion, Delta Reign, has been available for around 10 days now. The Rayquaza-themed set is one of this year's best and features a handful of very cool, very expensive cards.

Now that the dust has begun to settle and the prices have started to stabilize, here are the ten most expensive cards that you can pull (or buy) from the Pokemon TCG's Delta Reign expansion.

I like this Groudon card a lot. 2026 has been a bit of a tepid year for Illustration Rares, but this is up there with the best. I'll stand by the fact that it's a shame that Groudon, Kyogre, and Rayquaza didn't all get Special Illustration Rares, but it's a nice card.

At $20, it won't break the bank, but having any Groudon card is going to be relatively pricey, and so it's made its way onto this list.

It has been revealed that shimaris Yukichi is one of the Pokemon Trading Card Game's most interesting upcoming artists. Their cutesy style has been a hit with fans, with the Team Rocket's Meowth and Dwebble Illustration Rares two of their best so far.

Recent findings suggest that they've now been entrusted with a Special Illustration Rare, and I love it. It has shades of Toshinao Aoki​​​​​​'s Lana's Aid card from Twilight Masquerade, and is well worth the $21.18 it's presently selling for.

According to recent reports, this Eevee card, along with Delta Reign's Zorua and Sprigatito Illustration Rares, was first released in Japan as one of three different decks. It's now made its way to the English-language Pokemon TCG, and it's a cutie.

I'm surprised this one is so cheap at just $21.84, because Eevee cards usually command a premium, and for a card this cute, that seems like a steal.

Mega Golisopod ex's Special Illustration Rare is one of Delta Reign's most disappointing cards, and typically the one SIR I've pulled from the set. It honestly just looks like a Full Art and features a Pokemon that isn't particularly popular.

Sources indicate that it does have the SIR tax, though, and it's at the moment selling for just shy of $24. It's not particularly playable, so it's a miracle it's as much as that.

Another underwhelming Special Illustration Rare is up next with Mega Golurk ex. It's certainly better than Mega Golisopod ex, with the towering golem standing in among ancient ruins, but I'd categorize it as okay.

It is the first card to break the $25 mark in the expansion, and that's in part because it's seeing some play in the competitive scene.

Sources indicate that when I was researching and putting together this list, the Kecleon Illustration Rare's price surprised me. Being illustrated by Tomokazu Komiya definitely helps, with their signature psychedelic style, but it's Kecleon.

I'd have expected the cutesy Growlithe, Sprigatito, and Zorua cards to be worth more, but this clearly shows how much people value an artist's work, with Kecleon sitting at $34.99.

It has been revealed that this card is very cool. I'm a big lover of Full Art trainer cards, and this epitomizes everything that's good about them. Zinnia is a bit of a fan favorite, and seeing her sitting on a rock, probably awaiting the return of Rayquaza, is iconic.

The price, $36.18, is fair for the card, especially as it's seeing action in competitive play. In decks that require a lot of energy, Zinnia's Trust's ability to move them around is very useful.

We're now into the substantial money, and this is my favorite card of the entire expansion. Raikou's Special Illustration Rare features the Legendary Beast bounding down a mountain with sparks flying. Whether by design or not, it looks like it's set in the same region as Zinnia's Trust.

At over $127, Raikou ex costs a pretty penny, but when all is said and done, it's likely to be one of the best cards to come out of the entire Mega Evolution era.

The Mega Hyper Rares are divisive. They're an interesting latest take on the Sword and Shield era's Gold cards, but it's hard to think they don't look a little tacky. This Mega Rayquaza ex, though, might be the best one released so far.

It's a Mega Hyper Rare, so it's always going to fetch a pretty penny, and when you throw in one of the TCG's most desirable Pokemon, you get the $221.55 price tag that this is presently selling for.

My price prediction for this card may have been off the mark, but the Mega Rayquaza ex Special Illustration Rare is unsurprisingly Delta Reign's premium card, reaching a few pennies over $300.

It has been revealed that it's illustrated by 2024 Illustration Contest winner Kazuki Minami, and it's one of the most uniquely styled cards the TCG has ever seen. I can't even begin to describe what is being depicted, but the giant green Mega Rayquaza, contrasted by the orange and yellow backdrop, is a sight to behold.
