While Saatva, Tempur-Pedic, and DreamCloud are offering the best Black Friday mattress deals we've seen this year, all three brands are also behind some of 2026's best mattress toppers. But which premium mattress topper should side sleepers buy in the Black Friday sales?

Industry observers note that we've tested the Saatva Graphite, Tempur-Adapt + Cooling and the DreamCloud mattress toppers and they each deliver the signature luxury of their respective brands' mattresses. This comparison guide will help you choose which topper is right for you if you're a side sleeper in need of extra comfort.

Its body-cradling foam supports all sleep positions, including side sleeping, while its anchor straps keep the topper in place. However, despite the infusion of graphite, this memory foam topper can trap body heat.

The addition of a cooling cover prevents heat build-up, but be aware that there's no official sleep trial.

It has been revealed that the DreamCloud Softer Mattress Topper is made from a 2" slab of gel memory foam that our testers found to be cushioning along the shoulders and hips, making it ideal for side sleepers. Its breathable cover complete with "cooling yarns" means the DreamCloud Topper doesn't overheat. However, there's no anchor straps to prevent shifting.

It has been revealed that all three mattress toppers are 3" thick and use memory foam to cushion firm beds. While each works well for side sleepers, their distinct features make them better suited for different types of side sleepers.

According to recent reports, for instance, the Saatva Graphite Memory Foam Mattress Topper caters to all sleep styles, making it ideal for side sleepers who sometimes switch to back or front sleeping — or side sleepers who share a bed with someone who sleeps in a different position. In contrast, the DreamCloud and Tempur-Adapt have a plush feel best suited to side sleepers only.

The Tempur-Adapt + Cooling Mattress Topper has a cooling cover to prevent side sleepers from overheating (the Saatva Graphite, on the other hand, is prone to heat build-up). The DreamCloud Softer Mattress Topper also sleeps cooler than the Saatva Graphite, but doesn't have the washable cover with anchor straps. Still, it's the cheapest of the three, starting at $179 (the Saatva starts at $225; the Tempur-Adapt at $259).

Industry observers note that when testing comfort and support, our testers sleep on each topper in the three main sleep styles (back, side, and stomach) to see how comfortable and supportive it is in each position. The Saatva Graphite performed the best in this area, scoring a near-perfect 4.5/5, because its medium-firm feel supports all sleep styles.

It has been revealed that in comparison, the Tempur-Adapt's 3.5/5 score may seem low, but that's because it's too soft to support back and front sleepers. For side sleepers only, the Tempur-Adapt + Cooling provides incredible pressure relief. The DreamCloud also lost a point for its softness, but it's still a great topper for side sleepers.

As for temperature regulation (how well a topper maintains a comfortable temperature), the Tempur-Adapt's cooling cover upgrade scored the highest points. Meanwhile, the DreamCloud's breathable cover, while not cool-to-the-touch, prevented overheating. The Saatva Graphite scored the lowest, as our lead tester found it was prone to trapping heat.

Here's a close-up look at each mattress topper, exploring their respective designs and prices, plus who we do and don't recommend them to...

The Saatva Graphite Memory Foam Mattress Topper is a 3" slab of graphite-infused memory foam that's enveloped in a breathable, antimicrobial organic cotton cover (the same material used for the covers of the best Saatva mattresses).

Sources indicate that despite a naturally cool cover and a graphite-infusion, our testers found the Saatva Graphite's memory foam to trap heat. However, both Saatva customers and our testers agree that the memory foam's medium-firm feel adds plenty of comfort and support to mattresses.

It has been revealed that the Saatva Graphite (or any other Saatva toppers, for that matter) are pretty much never included in the Saatva Black Friday sales, let alone the monthly Saatva sales. That means you'll always pick up a Saatva Graphite from $225 in a twin. Extras include a generous 180-night sleep trial and a so-so one-year warranty.

In a recent development, the 3" Temur-Adapt + Cooling Mattress Topper is actually an upgrade of the original Tempur-Adapt Mattress Topper. We found the original's use of pressure-relieving Tempur-Material was great for side sleeping, but was also prone to heat build-up.

That's why were so pleased when the + Cooling upgrade added a cooling cover, with out lead tester impressed with the difference it made. While not a great mattress topper for those who sleep on their back or front, it's a top pick for side sleepers who need to soften hard beds.

The Tempur-Adapt + Cooling Topper is the only one out of the three toppers to be included in the Black Friday mattress topper sales. This means you'll save up to 30% on all sizes in this month's Tempur-Pedic sales, with a twin now down to $259 (was $339). Disappointingly, there's no sleep trial, but you will get a generous 10-year warranty.

DreamCloud is known for its flagship DreamCloud Classic Hybrid, a luxury hybrid mattress at a medium-range price, so it's only fitting that it would offer a bed topper at a fraction of the cost of its mattresses, too.

The DreamCloud Softer Topper is one of two DreamCloud toppers; the other is called the DreamCloud Firmer Topper, which is designed to give a (you guessed it) a firmer feel. The DreamCloud Softer Topper is made from 2" of gel-infused memory foam, which our testers say provides a contouring feel for side sleepers.

The DreamCloud Softer Topper may not be on sale for Black Friday (no surprises there, it's never in the DreamCloud sales) but that doesn't stop it from being the cheapest option. A twin is priced at $179, and comes with a 100-night sleep trial, a one-year warranty, and free shipping and returns.

Industry observers note that for side sleepers who are looking to soften a hard bed, we'd reccomend looking at the Temour-Adapt + Cooling or the DreamCloud Softer Topper. Both have a plusher feel than the Graphite and are great at regulating temperature, though those who need a bigger comfort boost should go for the thicker Tempur-Adapt.

Those looking for the most cost-effective, on the other hand, should pick the under-$200-for-a-queen DreamCloud. Meanwhile, the Saatva Graphite is best for side sleepers who prefer a firmer feel, combination sleepers, and couples with different sleep styles.
