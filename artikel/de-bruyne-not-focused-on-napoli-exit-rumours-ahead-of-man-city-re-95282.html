Kevin De Bruyne insisted the "unpleasant" rumours surrounding his future at Napoli would be put to one side ahead of his return to Manchester City on Tuesday.

De Bruyne returns to the Etihad Stadium for the second straight season for another Champions League encounter, but the Belgian has been the subject of speculation.

It has been reported that the 35-year-old, who only joined the Serie A giants from Man City last summer, could be on his way out as early as the January transfer window.

PSV has been touted as a possible destination for De Bruyne, with the midfielder out of contract at the end of the 2026-27 campaign.

De Bruyne's wife revealed in a recent magazine interview that she would one day like to return to Belgium, though De Bruyne said her words were misinterpreted by the Italian media and was keen to put the record straight.

It has been revealed that "I'm quite sorry about it all, to be honest. If you go and read the article which you have not read, you will understand what she said," De Bruyne told reporters.

"I was away in Belgium and then a lot of things were being remarked and there were a lot of negative reactions towards my wife.

"I saw photos of my house in the press, which is not good. I know that journalists in Italy have created this situation and I don't know why.

In a recent development, "You do not know my private life, you have no right to comment on my private life. It was an unpleasant situation for me and my wife.".

In a recent development, when pressed further on his future in Naples, De Bruyne added: "I can't say very much at this point. I've played at 400% so far.

"I'm not 20 years old anymore, I'm almost 36, so there are lots of things to consider.

Industry observers note that "Right now, there's not a lot to say. I'm playing well, I'm trying to help the team to do well. There have been lots of challenges this year and a lot of things have happened.".

De Bruyne will be hoping to play a bigger part in Napoli's next trip to his former employers, having been substituted early on following Giovanni Di Lorenzo's red card.

City won 2-0 that day thanks to goals from Erling Haaland and Jeremy Doku, with City unbeaten at home against Napoli (W2 D1).

De Bruyne was directly involved in 41 goals in 72 Champions League appearances for City (16 goals, 25 assists), the joint second-most for the club along with Haaland (41 in 42 games), with the pair only trailing Sergio Aguero (43).

He registered 108 goals and provided 170 assists in 422 appearances in all competitions during a trophy-laden spell with City.

City were found guilty of more than 100 breaches of the Premier League's financial regulations between 2009-10 and 2017-18 in September, and they have since appealed the ruling. With the appeal still ongoing, the potential punishments should the verdict be upheld have not been revealed.

"This is my club, I love the owners. I've nothing but affection for this club and that will never change," De Bruyne said of City's situation.

"Of course, in life there are rules, everybody plays on the limit, I would say, and for me, I play between the white lines and I compete with other players and I have had a lot of experiences with my teams, with the players, so for me that is the thing that I value most.

"And even later on in my career, I think the most important is the relationships between everybody that I had and how you manage these things.

According to recent reports, "I've given my all and that is not going to change in my mind I don't think. Other people will change in their mind how they view me as a player or how we played but everybody has their own opinion I guess.".
