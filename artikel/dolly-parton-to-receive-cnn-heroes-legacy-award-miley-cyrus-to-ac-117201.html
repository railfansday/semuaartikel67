📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Dolly Parton will posthumously receive CNN’s Heroes Legacy Award this year, with Miley Cyrus onboard to accept the honor on her behalf.

In a recent development, billboard can exclusively announce Wednesday (Nov. 25) that the Queen of Country — who died at the age of 80 in August after briefly battling cancer — is set to become the fourth person to ever receive the award, which honors “not-so-everyday people dedicated to a lifetime of service,” according to a release. Wynonna Judd will present it during the 20th annual CNN Heroes All-Star Tribute special airing at 8 p.m. ET on Dec. 5 via the network.

Sources indicate that “Dolly Parton’s philanthropy spanned her tremendous career, including her efforts to increase literacy, boost graduation rates, support disaster relief and health initiatives, create jobs and reduce poverty where she grew up in East Tennessee,” CNN states of why Parton was chosen as this year’s recipient. “She inspired millions with her generous spirit.”.

In a recent development, as teased in a trailer for the special, Anderson Cooper and Laura Coates will return as hosts. Jon Bon Jovi, Edward Norton, Kelly Ripa and Mark Consuelos are also set to make appearances to help celebrate the honorees.

With the exception of the Legacy Award, the purpose of CNN Heroes All-Star Tribute is to recognize the selflessness and bravery of everyday people. The highest honor of the night is Hero of the Year, the contenders for which are nominated by viewers; voting is now open to the public to narrow down the top five.

According to recent reports, the past three months since Parton’s passing have seen a number of tributes in her honor, from Stagecoach announcing Tuesday (Nov. 24) that the first day of 2027’s festival will be “Dolly Day” to the CMAs opening with Lainey Wilson and Vince Gill performing “I Will Always Love You” at this year’s ceremony on Nov. 18. CNN will further celebrate Parton’s impact and songwriting mastery with a documentary titled Dolly Parton: The Songs That Shaped Us airing on Dec. 6.

PPlus, Parton recently received three fresh Grammy nominations — including one Cyrus was able to share in. The two women, along with Lainey Wilson, Reba McEntire and Queen Latifah, are up for best country duo/group performance for their joint re-recording of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” released in January, as announced earlier in November.

Recent findings suggest that “My Aunt Dolly is one of the greatest songwriters the world has ever known,” Cyrus wrote on Instagram at the time. “She never made music for recognition but to make the world a happier place through her songs … I miss her calling on big days like this but feel her in every moment. Love you Aunt Dolly.”.
