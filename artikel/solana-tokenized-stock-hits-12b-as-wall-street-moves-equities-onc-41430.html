Solana’s tokenized stock market has reached $1.22 billion in circulating value, nearly doubling in less than a month as demand for blockchain-based equities accelerates.

Recent findings suggest that growth has been driven by platforms including Ondo Finance, xStocks and Backpack Securities, which are bringing exposure to major US companies onto Solana. Apple, Nvidia, Tesla and other household names are increasingly appearing in crypto wallets and decentralized trading platforms.

Recent findings suggest that the expansion is also attracting traditional financial infrastructure. The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) plans to connect its tokenization service to Solana in 2027, potentially opening another route for established securities to enter blockchain markets.

According to recent reports, ondo Finance has become one of the biggest contributors to Solana’s tokenized stock market. The platform brought more than 200 tokenized US stocks and ETFs to the network in January, covering companies such as Nvidia, Apple, and Meta.

Industry observers note that its expansion has continued through additional trading and wallet integrations. According to the Solana Foundation’s October ecosystem report, Ondo’s tokenized assets on Solana surpassed $500 million.

Meanwhile, xStocks has accumulated $7.5 billion in trading volume on Solana, while Backpack Securities has processed more than $6 billion in tokenized asset trading volume.

It has been revealed that securitize is also expanding its presence. The tech giant added 13 US equities to its Solana-based offering on November 2, bringing its total catalogue to 25 stocks. Its products include shares in Exxon Mobil, Disney, Salesforce, and GameStop. Unlike purely synthetic products, Securitize notes its tokens are backed one-to-one by underlying shares and provide security entitlements with applicable economic and ownership rights.

It has been revealed that the biggest institutional development came on November 12, when DTCC unveiled looks to connect its Tokenization Service to Solana during the second half of 2027. The integration would allow eligible securities held through its Depository Trust Company subsidiary to become available in tokenized form on Solana.

DTCC is one of the most important pieces of infrastructure in US financial markets. Its subsidiaries processed $4.7 quadrillion in securities transactions during 2025, while its depository provided custody and asset servicing for $114 trillion in securities.

The initial tokenization service debuted on October 28, with support for Canton Network and DTCC’s private Besu network. Solana will become another destination as the company expands its blockchain integrations.

Twenty financial institutions signed agreements to access the service at launch, including JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi, Morgan Stanley and UBS. DTCC notes tokenized securities distributed through its service will retain the ownership rights, investor protections and regulatory safeguards associated with traditionally held securities.

In a recent development, this gives Solana a potential connection to established securities infrastructure rather than relying exclusively on crypto-native issuers and trading platforms. But the integration is still planned for 2027.

It has been revealed that as of now, the $1.22 billion milestone reflects a relatively small market compared with traditional equities. But the combination of expanding stock offerings, billions in cumulative trading activity, and growing institutional involvement suggests tokenization is becoming a more serious part of Solana’s financial ecosystem.

Solana is attracting billions in tokenized stock trading, but Bitcoin Hyper is betting that the next opportunity lies in bringing Bitcoin liquidity into a faster, more flexible blockchain environment.

Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER) is developing a Bitcoin Layer 2 powered by the Solana Virtual Machine (SVM). The architecture aims to combine Bitcoin’s enormous liquidity base with the speed and programmability associated with Solana.

People will be able to bridge BTC into the Layer 2, where they can access faster transactions, decentralized exchanges, staking, and other DeFi applications. Its design also uses zero-knowledge proofs and periodic state commitments to Bitcoin for settlement.

It has been revealed that that combination could open an interesting route for tokenized financial markets. Solana has already demonstrated demand for bringing traditional equities onchain. Bitcoin Hyper is targeting a different pool of capital: Bitcoin holders who want access to applications beyond simply holding BTC.

It has been revealed that the project has already attracted more than $33 million during its presale, while its testnet infrastructure is taking shape. Its wallet, explorer, faucet, and testnet bridge are among the components being rolled out ahead of the next development phase.

According to recent reports, the HYPER token currently costs just $0.0136, with presale staking offering an advertised 35% APY. That yield gives early participants another incentive to hold their tokens while the network develops. For comparison, conventional SOL staking yields are considerably lower than that offered by the presale rate. Bitcoin Hyper is using staking rewards to attract early participation before its wider ecosystem goes live.

The fundraising milestone also puts the project on a defined timeline. Bitcoin Hyper’s presale is scheduled to conclude on November 26, leaving buyers with a shrinking window before the next stage.
