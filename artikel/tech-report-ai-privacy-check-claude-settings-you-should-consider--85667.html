Recent findings suggest that if you use an AI chatbot and you’re concerned about your privacy and the personal information it ingests, you can do something about it. While Claude — our top pick for AI chatbots — is one of the better options when it comes to privacy, the default settings can still leave something to be desired if you value yours.

Luckily, you can mitigate or even eliminate the amount of data Claude knows about you, as long as you know where to look.

Industry observers note that anthropic will use your location when using Claude to “improve product experience.” It will use your device’s IP address to identify your city and region, but you can disable this setting — to some extent. Like other AI chatbots, Claude has built-in “memory” so it can more easily have context about your conversations. Memory is on by default: The chatbot will remember personal preferences and other information and will be able to take that into consideration in other conversations, even if you don’t explicitly mention it in your current session.

In a recent development, if you’d prefer a clean slate, you can ask in the chat what memories it has collected about you and then tell Claude to delete specific memories or all of them. However, there’s a dedicated section in the settings to help you view and manage memories directly.

In a recent development, if you have had conversations with Claude that were sensitive in nature and still had the “Help improve our AI models” option enabled, odds are that Anthropic has already trained Claude on that conversation. There’s no way to change that.

However, you should find those conversations in your chat history and delete them. After you’ve done that and disabled AI training on your conversations, you should opt for incognito mode for any further sensitive conversations you might want to have going forward.

Incognito chats aren’t saved in your history, nor are they used to train models.
