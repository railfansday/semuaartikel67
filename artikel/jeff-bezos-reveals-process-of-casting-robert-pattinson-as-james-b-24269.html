The choice of the next James Bond truly came down to the wire.

After Robert Pattinson was revealed as the successor slipping into the role as the titular secret agent, Jeff Bezos reflected on the Amazon MGM casting process, noting the choice was made just days before the big reveal.

Sources indicate that “We ran a really tight process and made the final decision on Friday [Nov. 13],” Bezos shared at the Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World event, per Variety. “There was a big group initially, and we down selected to five finalists. We did real, old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists.”.

Referring to the group of finalists—rumored to include Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, Jacob Elordi and Callum Turner—Bezos noted the process of elimination “was interesting,” adding that it involved “full costumes and real sets and other actors.”.

“We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists,” the Amazon founder explained, “and then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all the tests and reviewing all that.”.

And though all five finalists were “amazing,” it was the Twilight star who truly had the team shaken.

“There was something special about Rob and his performance,” the 62-year-old said. “It was immediately undebated. Denis [Villeneuve], the director…we all gravitated toward Rob. Initially, we were slightly biased against him just because he’s already so famous, like maybe we should have somebody more unknown, but in the screen tests, it was so obvious he is James Bond.”.

As for Pattinson himself, making the leap from spider monkey to special-ops enforcer is a move he deeply appreciates making.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson previous shared in a statement. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy [Pascal], David [Heyman], Tanya [Lapointe], and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”.
