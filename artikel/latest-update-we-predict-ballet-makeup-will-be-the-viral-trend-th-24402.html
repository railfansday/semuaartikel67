This holiday season, it's all about ballet-inspired makeup. Think Nutcracker and Swan Lake, but make it beauty. So many brands have been launching their limited-edition holiday collections recently with products inspired by ballet and all its iconic motifs.

In a recent development, we're talking everything from Ariana Grande's r.e.m. Beauty and its en pointe collection to C-Beauty brand Flower Knows and its newly introduced snow ballet line. These products don't just use a ballet aesthetic, though. The looks depicted are ones we associate with the performance. They're full of soft shimmers, flushed cheeks, delicate blue eyeshadows and glass-like skin.
