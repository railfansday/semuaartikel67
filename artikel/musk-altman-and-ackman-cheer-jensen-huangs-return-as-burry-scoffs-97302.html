It has been revealed that jensen Huang’s return as Nvidia’s chief executive on Monday drew cheers from the investors who campaigned for it. Its critics were scornful. Many of them reacted on X within minutes of the board’s decision.

Business Insider gathered the posts, along with comments from analysts and critics who wrote elsewhere.

Elon Musk had signed the letter calling for Huang’s reinstatement and compared his exit to dropping Michael Jordan. On Monday he replied to Nvidia’s announcement.

— Elon Musk (@elonmusk) November 23, 2026.

This month Brad Gerstner of Altimeter Capital co-wrote a letter asking the board to bring Huang back, with Atreides’ Gavin Baker. He posted a screenshot of the CNBC news with one word, “Unanimous”, and an American flag.

Unanimous. 🇺🇸@JensenHuang https://t.co/I4Uin8Glg4.

— Brad Gerstner (@altcap) November 23, 2026.

In a recent development, bill Ackman of Pershing Square had criticised the board for not acting in shareholders’ best interest. On Monday he credited the crowd.

The wisdom of the crowd prevails.

— Bill Ackman (@BillAckman) November 23, 2026.

Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick had promised to turn up at Nvidia’s headquarters with a “wafer-encrusted megaphone” to call for a walkout. On Monday he posted a picture of the megaphone. He wrote that he would save it for another day.

Will have to save this for another day 😅😎 https://t.co/dLqJosDpPZ.

— travis kalanick (@travisk) November 23, 2026.

All-In co-host Jason Calacanis posted an edited picture of Huang as Jesus, and wrote: “GPU Jesus has risen!”.

Industry observers note that going on @MSNOWNews with @SRuhle shortly to chop it up! https://t.co/yZKSpzFzxq.

— @jason (@Jason) November 23, 2026.

According to recent reports, openAI’s Sam Altman, whose own board fired and rehired him in 2023, had declined to say whether Huang should return. On Monday he wrote one word: “Jensen!”.

— Sam Altman (@sama) November 23, 2026.

Former Goldman Sachs chief executive Lloyd Blankfein took aim at the board.

According to recent reports, guess you can come back from the graveyard if you’re really, really, really indispensable. The NVDA board will not have that opportunity.

— Lloyd Blankfein (@lloydblankfein) November 23, 2026.

Katie Miller, the former DOGE lead, said Huang had done “more than an Altman” and the “same as a Scaramucci”. Anthony Scaramucci lasted 10 days as White House communications director.

Industry observers note that sequoia partner Shaun Maguire wrote that markets were “ripping” on the news. US stocks rose on Monday after the Dow, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq ended Friday in correction territory, CNBC reported. The S&P 500 fell more than 9% from Huang’s exit on 12 November to Friday.

Industry observers note that nvidia’s shares jumped 33%, putting it on course to add $900bn in market value, Reuters reported. CoreWeave and Nebius, cloud companies Nvidia has invested in, rose nearly 25%.

“It tells us to some extent that Jensen Huang was too substantial to be fired,” Steve Sosnick, chief strategist at Interactive Brokers, told Reuters.

“I certainly believe this establishes a bottom and a floor in the AI trade,” Mark Gibbens of Gibbens Capital Management told CNBC.

Analyst Dan Ives wrote that the board had “no choice”, as the pressure to bring Huang back was too much.

Sources indicate that jay Woods of Freedom Capital Markets was less sure. He told CNBC the rally would probably fizzle out, as investors still had the same questions about Nvidia.

It has been revealed that “Not sure yet whether good or bad that the fate of the market is almost equal to the fate of one person. But hey… We are not picky here,” traders at Goldman Sachs said, according to CNBC.

Recent findings suggest that michael Burry, the “Massive Short” investor, has long been bearish on Nvidia. He wrote that the reinstatement highlighted Nvidia was “weaker, more craven than even I thought”. He urged the Justice Department and the SEC to keep digging. “Godspeed,” he wrote.

Industry observers note that herb Greenberg, who writes the Herb on the Street newsletter, mentioned the return was about Wall Street and the power of Silicon Valley’s elite. He expects it to backfire.

“Any other CEO would’ve been fired and never hired back,” Greenberg wrote on Substack.

Sources indicate that emarketer analyst Jacob Bourne called the move “stunning” but not surprising. He remarked it raised questions about corporate governance like those after Altman’s ouster.

President Donald Trump called Huang a “Great Patriot and National Treasure” on Truth Social. He had given the board assurances before the vote.
