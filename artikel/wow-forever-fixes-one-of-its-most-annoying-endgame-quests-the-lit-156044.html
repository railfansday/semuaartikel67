The thing you must understand is that I'm not mad. No, really, I'm not upset at all—as a matter of fact, I'm happy for your level 60 character to complete Scarlet Subterfuge (and its follow-up, In Dreams) with less back-and-forth trekking than me. I'm chuffed for you. Really, I am.

Ahem—as you can tell by my current state of grumpiness, WoW: Forever issued a bunch of hotfixes, which I will do my due diligence in describing before I have a good moan: Alterac Valley has had some tweaks to stop people "frequently completing the battleground without capturing the objectives", balance druids have had some buffs, and some major bugs (such as hunter pets not being able to crit) have been addressed.

That last point's a bit of contention in the community, given hunters are out-performing other classes in damage, but given we're just three weeks into the game with a couple of weeks left to go before the raid tier, I'm not sweating the lack of an immediate nerf bat.

Recent findings suggest that right, there's my journalistic obligation taken care of. Here's why I'm (not) upset.

Scarlet Subterfuge—and In Dreams—is widely considered one of the crappiest quests in Classic, and given this is a game that's already rife with inconvenience—inconvenience I am usually all for—that's saying something. In it, you need to go and find Highlord Taelan Fordring, son of Tirion Fordring, and remove the scarlet-coloured wool from his eyes.

Sources indicate that story-wise, this quest kicks ass. You're finding the son of the guy who wielded the Ashbringer. Realising his life has been a lie, he hulks out, cleaving down his former scarlet allies, and marches out of the fortress side-by-side with you. Mechanically, it sucks so much, dude.

In a recent development, to reach Fordring the younger, you need to go halfway across the zone, talk to an illusionist hermit, and don a disguise. Then you need to jog all the way up to where Taelan is to give him the news.

Recent findings suggest that the only downside is that this disguise lasts for 30 minutes, and escorting Taelan, at least with the current strategy (more on this later) lasts for about 20 minutes. And if you don't have the disguise on, he's hostile to you, even if you're just restarting the escort quest. So that means that if anybody else is doing the quest by the time you get there, you need to either:.

Industry observers note that the actual escort is annoying, too: Since Taelan can technically handle the Scarlet Crusade on his lonesome, accepted practice for this quest has been to simply let him handle things. Which both makes it take ages and makes it difficult to find a group so that you can do it the intended way.

In a recent development, as a cherry on top, you can't really skip this quest outright as it gives some really powerful pre-BiS endgame items. Still, I persevered, I did it yesterday, and Blizzard, uh. Blizzard gave the disguise an hour-long duration literally today.

"Western Plaguelands - The Scarlet Illusion granted during the quest 'Scarlet Subterfuge' now lasts an hour (was 30 minutes).".

This doesn't make the quest any less of a headache to finish, but hey—at least if someone else is already doing it, you won't have to jog back to your hag friend or play the log-in, log-out Hokey Pokey until he respawns. Good for you.
