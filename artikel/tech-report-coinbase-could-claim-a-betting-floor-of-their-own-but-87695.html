Tech Report: Coinbase could claim a betting floor of their own, but what about Kalshi?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Coinbase wants a piece of the prediction markets action, with the exchange reportedly planning a launch next year.

In a recent development, right now, they don’t run their own; they borrow them. Users trade event contracts on the exchange through a Kalshi partnership. That could change next year though.

Citing sources close to the matter, a Bloomberg report claimed that Coinbase wants to roll out its own event contracts in the U.S in H1 2027. It would be open to both retail and institutional traders.

Sources indicate that but why bother? For starters, Coinbase gets to decide what launches and when. Next, it keeps a bigger share of the trading fees.

There’s real money at stake here. In Q3 2026, Coinbase’s prediction markets business was already on track to bring in over $200 million a year. If you think about it, this isn’t an overnight decision. In the run up to it all, Coinbase announced that it was buying prediction market startup The Clearing Tech giant in December 2025.

Then, in September this year, the CFTC gave the green light for its own clearing house.

Recent findings suggest that all that being stated, nothing’s set in stone. However, the pieces are clearly falling into place.

Crypto trading may have slowed down, but prediction markets have kept themselves out of the bleeding.

In a recent development, since launch, revenue from the business has grown by 82% QoQ on average. In Q3 alone, daily traders went up by over 60%, compared to the quarter before.

CFO Alesia Haas summed up the appeal pretty neatly. She said,.

Prediction markets are adding another revenue stream and are less correlated to crypto volatility….

A major deal for an exchange that lives and dies by market cycles.

On the earnings call, Haas also stated that the fresh clearing house would let Coinbase run its own contracts and earn more from them.

Worth pointing out, however, that Kalshi isn’t being cut out as partner contracts will stay on the platform.
