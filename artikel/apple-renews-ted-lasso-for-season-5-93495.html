One of Apple’s biggest reveals is sticking around for at least one more season. The streamer announced today that Ted Lasso has been renewed for a fifth season on Apple TV, after what the organization calls a “record-breaking season four debut.” There are currently no details about the new season nor when it might debut.

Ted Lasso has proven to be one of the tentpole series for Apple TV, and it returned in 2026 after a three-year hiatus. While the season 3 finale seemed to mark the end for the show, season 4 introduced a latest cast of characters with head coach Ted — played by Jason Sudeikis, who also just appeared as a voice actor in Grand Theft Auto VI — managing a new professional women’s team.

The news comes amidst a handful of other recent renewals from Apple — including a second season of Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed and multiple fresh seasons of Slow Horses — in what is turning out to be a strong year for the streaming service, as it attempts to reach a larger audience.
