📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

José Mourinho hasn’t budged on Endrick’s future, having long been in Roma’s sights as a way to strengthen their attack in the January transfer window.

Speaking at a press conference on the eve of the match against Celta Vigo, the former Giallorossi coach reiterated his desire to keep the Brazilian at Real Madrid, despite rumors of a possible departure: “I need him. I love Endrick, and Real Madrid is more crucial than him. This means we need him to stay here.”.

The message was also addressed to the 2006-born player’s entourage and to clubs interested in his signing, with Mourinho further clarifying his position: “My response to the agents, to the newspapers… is this: I need Endrick, and therefore Real Madrid.”.

It has been revealed that the coach’s position, however, doesn’t completely rule out a possible departure in January, considering that Mourinho himself has acknowledged the management’s decision-making power: “If the club’s higher-ups decide otherwise, I will respect it. But I want him to stay here 100%.”.

Roma continues to closely monitor the Brazilian’s situation, having been identified as one of Gasperini’s potential reinforcements for the January transfer window.
