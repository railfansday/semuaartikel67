A 50-inch 4K QLED TV for under $200.

The fix for thin TV speakers is $160 off.

Industry observers note that thin TVs have thin speakers, and a soundbar is the fix that doesn’t involve running wire around the room. Hisense rates it at 1,300 lumens, which means it will look its best after dark or with the shades down.
