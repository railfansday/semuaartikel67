I feel so blessed to have witnessed it and honestly, I owe that opportunity to the Unistellar eQuinox 2. After attaching the solar filter to the Equinox 2, I simply clicked "Go-to" on the sun from the Unistellar app and waited for it to find the blinding solar disk, which revealed up on my screen as an orange disk.
