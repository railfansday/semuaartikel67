This week, Soneil Spark rolls out its Mobile Battery Energy Storage System (Mobile BESS), the portable, modular battery storage built into a towable trailer.

The firm, offering bespoke, modular energy solutions, is introducing Mobile BESS at RE+ 2026. It delivers temporary backup and off-grid power, working with what’s available by taking power from the grid, a generator, or solar.

“Wherever your business goes, the power goes with it. Simply tow it where energy demand grows,” mentioned Sonya Jain, CEO of Soneil Spark, in a statement. “Start with one unit and add more as your needs change.”.

It has been revealed that it’s a simple set up, the company said. Mobile BESS works with no crane, no trenching, and no utility wait. That makes it a natural fit for a job site which may be months away from permanent service, a film set or event that can’t tolerate generator noise and fumes, a utility crew restoring power after a storm, or a remote mine or farm beyond the grid’s reach.

On sites that already run generators, the Mobile BESS syncs with them to cover peaks and start-up gaps, so the generators run fewer hours. Where diesel is restricted, it offers a quiet alternative.

According to recent reports, where containerized storage is built to be placed once, the Mobile BESS is built to be towed and to work in any weather. It includes:.

The Mobile BESS is built for generator and equipment rental, construction, emergency and backup power, utilities, electrical contractors, events, film and entertainment, and off-grid sites such as mining and agriculture. A hybrid option adds EV charging.
