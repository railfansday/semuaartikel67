📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bella Hadid may be the face of Saint Laurent and Prada, and she may own every high-profile runway she’s on, but her long-term life goal doesn’t involve spotlights or mega fame. In her new cover story for Harper’s Bazaar, the supermodel opens up like never before about what she sees for her future.

Industry observers note that “From an external space, success is about how known you are, how much you make, how many times you can be on TV, how many covers you’re on, how many fashion highlights you do. And that is really important in our industry, especially when you’re building,” she notes. “But for me, I am different.”.

While she feels endlessly lucky for her place in the fashion industry, and admits that having opened the Victoria’s Secret fashion show last month was a dream come true, she yearns for an easy life surrounded by family and nature.

“Success, for me, can be living in peace, in a small town, with a man that I love, with my child, having my animals,” Hadid says.

A cowgirl at heart, the Orebella founder has down-to-earth goals that do not necessarily involve modeling, but do involve having a small local business, leaning into hobbies that bring her peace, and becoming a wife and mother.

Sources indicate that “I’ve always wanted to start a farm-to-table bakery, with flowers, and everything comes from the community. I want to do things that are fulfilling for me in my soul: having a greenhouse that I can garden in, having my glassblowing and ceramic studio, and having lots of children and being a mother,” she states. “It wasn’t a dream that I’ve always had, but it’s something that now is so intense for me. My intuition is screaming so loudly at me about it.”.
