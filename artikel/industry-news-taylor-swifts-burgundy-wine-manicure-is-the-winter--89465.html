In a recent development, this week at the CMA Awards, Taylor Swift’s nails were so scarlet, they were practically “maroon.” The singer sported short nails painted a shade of deep red that evoked a tall, thin wine glass, and the manicure went with all three of her outfits of the night. It’s not the first time she’s given us nail inspiration—she recently had “gold fairy dust nails” that stole the show at the 2026 Academy Museum Gala. But this new color is the perfect shade for cooler weather.

According to recent reports, “I would describe this as a classic burgundy manicure,” says Vikki Swan, award-winning nail educator and founder of Vikki Swan Nail Academy in Los Angeles. “The deep wine-red shade creates a timeless, elegant look. It’s a great example of how a simple, solid color can make a statement without any additional nail art.”.

The color also isn’t completely opaque—but it also isn’t translucent. Jin Soon Choi, manicurist and founder of Jinsoon, describes the polish as a “crelly.” “The crelly finish combines the richness of a cream [nail polish] with the softness of a jelly,” she claims. “It gives this classic color a youthful, modern twist while keeping it soft and effortless.”.

It has been revealed that swift’s manicure fits right into winter nail trends. “We’re seeing richer, darker colors come forward: black cherry, garnet, burgundy, and deep wine,” notes Max Estrada, founder of eNailCouture. “They’re luxurious without relying on glitter or extra embellishment, and they work beautifully with winter fashion. It is the sort of color that gives a simple manicure presence.” Swift isn’t the only one—Jennifer Lopez and Anne Hathaway have both stepped out with similar manicures recently.

If you want to try it yourself, here’s how to re-create it. “Start with a clean manicure, shaping the nails short with softly rounded or soft-square edges and gently pushing back the cuticles,” Choi says. “Apply a base coat, followed by two thin layers of spiced red ‘crelly’ polish to build color without looking heavy. End with a top coat for an ultra-shiny finish that makes the color look lively and fresh.” And if you really want to nod to Swift, you can do it all while listening to “Red.”.
