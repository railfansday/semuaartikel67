📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that netflix has had many exciting TV highlights in 2026 so far, but its most-watched is a thriller series that seems like it will end the year at the top of the streamer's charts, and there are a few good reasons for the new show's immense success. Netflix's library of TV shows, like most other streamer, keeps renewing itself with fresh originals.

In a recent development, sometimes they are fully based on new ideas and concepts, which is how Stranger Things, the sci-fi series that has now led to two spin-offs being developed, was created. Other times, these include adaptations of novels, like the romantic Bridgerton, video games, such as Hayley Atwell's Tomb Raider: The Legend of Lara Croft, and more. Those highlights have become quite popular on the streamer.

Industry observers note that by looking at official Netflix data for the 10 most popular English-language TV reveals of all time on the streaming service, at the very top of that list is an adaptation of sorts, as Jenna Ortega's Wednesday taps into the horror comedy The Addams Family franchise. Wednesday season 1 leads the Netflix list with 252.1 million views, while season 2 ranks 6th with 119.3 million views.

Joining Wednesday on the chart are two Netflix series adapting hit novels, with two seasons of Bridgerton and Anya Taylor-Joy's The Queen's Gambit part of the list. Among those hit shows is a 2026 release, one of Netflix's many thriller shows, I Will Find You, which has risen above all other TV shows on the streaming service so far this year.

I Will Find You is part of that trend of Netflix hit shows based on books, as it is the latest Harlan Coben book adaptation on Netflix. The streaming service has found global success with other Coben novels over the years, including Run Away, Fool Me Once, and more. As such, I always expected I Will Find You to do well, but it surpassed all expectations. At the time of writing, I Will Find You stands as the 5th most-watched Netflix TV show of all time, having amassed a whopping 120.8 million views in its first 91 days of availability on the streamer.

Industry observers note that it is the only Netflix TV show released in 2026 to make it onto the list, having bumped Jon Bernthal's crime thriller series His & Hers off the top 10 after that project's January 8 release. Despite the success of other Coben adaptations on the streamer, there are no other TV demonstrates based on the author's work on the all-time Netflix chart, and that, alongside the fact that no other 2026 series is part of the list, shows why I Will Find You's streaming feat is so impressive, exceeding all expectations set before its release.

I Will Find You's global streaming success would not be possible if the quality was not high. The latest Netflix thriller series makes the most out of Coben's signature style, delivering a hard-hitting tale about a wronged father who does everything in his power to find out what happened to his son after receiving evidence that he might still be alive while having been wrongfully convicted for his own son's murder. It is an intense story with a tight script, morally complex characters, an immersive atmosphere that pulls the viewer in instantly, and makes for the perfect binge on Netflix.

As a limited series, this one is a must-binge, with viewers getting all the answers in one sitting, if so inclined, and I Will Find You makes sure that the viewer is. The Netflix thriller series is potentialized by strong dramatic turns from major actors, including Avatar franchise lead Sam Worthington as the father, David Burroughs, as well as names like Britt Lower (Severance), Milo Ventimiglia (This Is Us), Erin Richards (Gotham), Clancy Brown (The Penguin), and more. I Will Find You deserves its Netflix success.
