London, UK — GRIDSERVE, the UK’s most-used electric vehicle (EV) charging network, has secured a £420 million financing package, marking a significant milestone in the company’s financial development and providing a strengthened funding platform for its next phase of growth.

One of the largest financing packages of its kind in the EV charging sector, the deal comprises a £240 million loan to refinance existing debt, a £165 million capital expenditure facility to fund future investment in the network and a £15 million revolving credit facility to support working capital and liquidity.

Structured as green senior debt under GRIDSERVE’s Green Finance Framework, the financing was anchored by the National Wealth Fund, Crédit Agricole CIB, NatWest and Rabobank as cornerstone lenders, each acting as Mandated Lead Arranger alongside MUFG, with MUFG also acting as Sole Financial Advisor. The package strengthens the tech giant’s long-term funding position, providing greater financial flexibility and dedicated capital to support its ongoing investment in critical UK EV charging infrastructure.

The refinancing also reflects the significant financial progress GRIDSERVE has made under CEO Daniel Kunkel. In 2025, the organization reported positive EBITDA for the first time in its history, following a renewed focus on disciplined investment, utilisation-led growth and capital efficiency. The new financing structure builds on this progress, providing a robust foundation for sustainable growth and continued investment in the business.

Since opening its first EV charging station in Braintree in 2020, GRIDSERVE has established itself as a leading force in the transition to electric mobility. Its nationwide network now supports an average of 350,000 charging sessions every month across more than 200 locations. GRIDSERVE also remains the only UK charging operator to have invested in a dedicated interoperability testing facility, helping to underpin its 99% availability record and ensure that upcoming electric vehicle models, regardless of manufacturer, can charge seamlessly across its network.

Alongside continued investment in its existing network, the upcoming capital expenditure facility will support GRIDSERVE’s next phase of expansion, including the development of ultra-rapid EV charging provision beyond the motorway network.

Sources indicate that daniel Kunkel, CEO of GRIDSERVE, said: “Securing this refinancing package is a significant milestone for GRIDSERVE and reflects the progress we have made over the last year. We have demonstrated that our network can grow sustainably, supported by a disciplined approach to investment, utilisation and capital efficiency.

“The strength of this financing package, and the confidence shown in us by our lending partners, puts GRIDSERVE in a strong position for the future. It reinforces our long-term commitment to the UK and gives us the funding platform to continue investing in the infrastructure needed to decarbonise road transport. We look forward to building on this momentum as we enter the next phase of our growth.”.

Oliver Holbourn, National Wealth Fund CEO, remarked: “Drivers need confidence that reliable charging will be available where they need it before making the switch to electric. By lending alongside commercial banks, the National Wealth Fund is supporting GRIDSERVE’s continued investment in its network, including prepares to expand ultra-rapid charging beyond motorways. This is about backing the infrastructure that makes cleaner transport a practical choice for more people.”.

Priya Veerapen, Managing Director, Infracapital, said, “GRIDSERVE has made significant progress since our investment in 2022, and this £420 million refinancing marks an key next step in its growth. Alongside TPG, we have invested more than £300 million in the business, reflecting our conviction in the long-term need for high-quality EV charging infrastructure and the important role GRIDSERVE can play in delivering it across the UK.”.

Recent findings suggest that jamie Gilbert, Business Unit Partner at TPG Rise Climate, stated: “The strength of this refinancing reflects the quality of GRIDSERVE’s business and the progress the company has made in establishing itself as a leader in UK EV charging. It also underscores the importance of continued investment in the infrastructure needed to support the UK’s transition to electric mobility.”.
