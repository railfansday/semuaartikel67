Recent findings suggest that for the first time in 13 years, Taylor Swift is performing at the CMA Awards. It's kind of a major deal. Though she's strayed far from her country music roots over the last decade, she was once a two-time CMA Entertainer of the Year winner. In 2026, her song "I Knew It, I Knew You" is nominated for Single of the Year, which marks her 26th career CMA nomination.

Industry observers note that the awards ceremony will be hosted by Lainey Wilson for the third consecutive year and will feature additional performances by Ella Langley, Kacey Musgraves, Miranda Lambert, Tim McGraw, Zach Top, and more. There will also be a very special tribute to the one and only Dolly Parton.

If you want to catch Taylor Swift, Ella Langley, and other major performers live, here are all the details you need to know.

The 60th Annual CMA Awards will take place live on Nov. 18, 2026 at 8 p.m. ET and run through 11 p.m. ET.

The awards ceremony and performances will unfold live on your local ABC network for those with cable or an antenna. For those without cable access, you can stream the CMA Awards live on Disney+. If you don't mind missing out on the live show, you can also stream the performances on Hulu the following day.
