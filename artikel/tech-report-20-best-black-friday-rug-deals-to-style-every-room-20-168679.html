Rugs are one of those niche investment pieces that can cost a fortune but will have little to no resale value after your dog has done a number on it or dust and hair have built up a layer of filth on top. All to say: if you're going to invest in a rug, you might as well get a good deal. Now is that time.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday are when rug brands roll out their biggest markdowns of the year, from retail giants like Amazon and Wayfair to specialists like Ruggable that have made underfoot fabrics their entire business. This year's list also includes a once-a-year sample sale from Beni, where every rug on offer is the only one of its kind. Below, our favorite Black Friday rug deals (and their makers), with some designer pieces up to 50% off. We'll keep updating this guide through Cyber Monday as each Black Friday rug sale goes live.

In the current bargain bin, you’ll find rugs spanning the shape, color, and pattern spectrum.

The sale opens once a year, and this year it runs through November 30, with photoshoot and production samples marked 20 to 50% off. Quantities are limited, everything is sold as-is, and orders ship within two weeks.
