Industry observers note that most of the biggest streaming services are offering Black Friday deals this year, as they do every year. Nearly all of these deals are for latest and former customers only. Easy enough if you've canceled a subscription recently and want it back, but a little trickier if you aren't exactly a “new” customer. The ol' second (or third … or fourth) email address sign-up trick still works sometimes, but duplicated credit cards can sometimes flag an error.

According to recent reports, either way, here are the best Black Friday streaming deals I found. I'll update this list periodically through Cyber Monday as things change. And if you're looking for more deals, check out our guides to the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals and Best Black Friday TV Deals.

Deal Until December 1: $6/month for six months, billed monthly.

In a recent development, look, this list is alphabetical, but that works in my favor because I get to put Apple TV first. It's all killer, no filler. Widow's Bay, Pluribus, Ted Lasso, Slow Horses, Severance, Shrinking, Silo, and other series that begin with the letter S—Apple TV just has the most memorable demonstrates. And six months is just enough time to binge most (if not all) of them. (See WIRED's Guide to the Best Apple TV+ shows.).

Deal Until December 1: $4/month for six months, billed monthly.

Sources indicate that I may have fallen off the anime train, but I do find myself subscribing to Crunchyroll for at least a month every year, just to catch up on a series or two. Either way, this is a great discount if you've been meaning to try out or catch up on some anime.

Current offer: $12/month for six months, billed monthly.

Deal Until Dec. 1: $12/month for four months, billed monthly (locked in).

It has been revealed that watching all your local sports team’s games online in 2026 is an M.C. Escher-ian nightmare that somehow requires four different subscriptions and possibly a live-TV add-on package. Any deal that can soften that blow is a good thing.

Deal Until December 2: $3/month for two months.

According to recent reports, this is more of a trail subscription that a bonafide deal, but hey, let's all take whatever savings we can get. After all, two months might be all you need to binge the fresh Dexter: Resurrection or watch Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender.

Regular Price: $70/year billed annually.

Black Friday Deal: $12/year billed annually.

Sources indicate that this is a steep discount for a whole year of Starz streaming. Though, keep in mind, you're paying for the full year up front ($12), and you better set a calendar reminder 11 months from now if you don't want to end up paying $70 for Starz next year.

Deal Until December 2: $50/month for six months.

It has been revealed that if you're really trying to watch all the sports games (plus recreate that classic TV-package feel with local channels), YouTube TV's Sports+News package is a good option. You can get about $20 off each month's rate for six months with this deal.
