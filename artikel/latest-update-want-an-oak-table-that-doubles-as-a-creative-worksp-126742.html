Recent findings suggest that although we don't often talk about it, for many creatives – myself included – the dining table is never just for dinner. It's where you pop your laptop when your partner needs the home office. It’s where the kids' art projects sprawl across a Sunday afternoon. And sometimes, it’s where you spread out your sketches, prints or swatches, to give you room to think.

In a recent development, if your dining table is wobbly, scratched or simply too small for purpose, replacing it won't just improve your lifestyle, it'll help your work life as well. So this Black Friday, I've tracked down four beautiful oak dining tables, offering savings of up to £521.

They range from a compact four-seater to an extending table that seats a crowd. So whatever your space and numbers, there’ll be something for you below.

It has been revealed that size is the first thing to think about. A 150cm table like the Nordic gives you room for a laptop and a sketchbook at one end, while dinner happens at the other. Alternatively, an extending table like the Buxton lets you go bigger when a project demands it.
