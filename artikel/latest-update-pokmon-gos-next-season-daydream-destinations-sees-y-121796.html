With Twilight Trails soon drawing to a close, we've hit the point where Pokémon Go announces its next Season. From December 1st, we'll all be able to enjoy Daydream Destinations, which brings the usual array of wild encounters and events.

Recent findings suggest that part of that means a newly introduced rotation of Pokémon to battle against in Raids. On the Mega Raid Day front, that includes Mega Drampa (for those who want their dragons to look like an angry storm cloud) and Mega Froslass (for those who want some elegance in their Pokémon).

Industry observers note that meanwhile, in Dynamax battles, you can take on Alolan Sandshrew, Corphish and Slowpoke. None of those is particularly interesting to me, but I have always enjoyed that Alolan Sandshrew is trying to disguise itself as an igloo. As for the Season's Max Battle Day events, you and your pals can team up to bring down Gigantamax Inteleon and Toxtricity.

Sources indicate that we've also been given dates for the Season's Community Days, though we don't yet know which Pokémon will feature. Still, in case you wanted those in your diary, here they are:.

Now, while we don't know any of the Pokémon involved, we can assume that December 12th and 13th will be the annual format of featuring every Community Day Pokémon from the last year or two. So it's a good time to catch up on any Shinies you might have missed.

According to recent reports, finally, this Season's bonuses include boosted Friendship XP, x2 Raid damage and additional rewards for hatching Eggs. Of course, I'm sure more events will pop up along the way, but this is all we know for now!
