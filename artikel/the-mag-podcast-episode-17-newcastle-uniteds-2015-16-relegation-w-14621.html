The Mag Podcast Episode 17: Newcastle United’s 2015-16 relegation with lessons from the past.

Published: 16/11/2026 | Duration: 46 mins | Hosts: Jamie Smith, Simon Cahill & David Hutchinson.

In a recent development, in Episode 17 of The Mag Pod, we find Jamie, Simon, and Hutch discussing relegation, again! Special focus is on the 2015-16 season when the combined talents of Mike Ashley and Steve McClaren led to the club’s most recent drop to the Championship. So how does the current 2026-27 NUFC situation compare…?

Industry observers note that listen to the latest episode of The Mag Pod! Join Jamie, Simon, and Hutch for unfiltered football debate.

Jamie Smith, Simon Cahill and David Hutchinson revisit Newcastle United’s 2015-16 relegation season under the ownership of Mike Ashley, examining the failures under Steve McClaren, the arrival of Rafa Benítez and whether there are lessons to be learned amid the concerns surrounding the current 2026-27 campaign.

The lads also discuss Newcastle United Women’s derby draw with Sunderland and the controversial travel restrictions for the upcoming Tyne-Wear derby.

In the second of three episodes this week, Hutch takes us through another particularly forgettable chapter in Newcastle United’s history. From a disappointing start to the season and a recruitment policy that prioritised potential over experience, to a divided dressing room and a lack of leadership on the pitch, there was no shortage of contributing factors to United’s downfall.

There’s a look back at the performances of Georginio Wijnaldum, Aleksandar Mitrović, Fabricio Coloccini and Moussa Sissoko, as well as some personal memories of the campaign, including a frustrating afternoon at Old Trafford and a particularly miserable pre-Christmas encounter with Aston Villa.

Could an earlier appointment of Rafa Benítez have saved Newcastle from relegation?

The lads discuss the importance of knowing when to make a managerial change, whilst considering the differences between the Newcastle United of a decade ago and the club today.

There’s also an assessment of the increasingly congested Premier League table, the importance of experienced players in a transitional squad and the heroes and villains of the 2015-16 season.

Naturally, a controversial Sergio Agüero goal brings about another argument between Jamie and Simon over the merits of VAR, with all the maturity listeners have come to expect.

Another nostalgic look at a period Newcastle United fans would rather forget, but one which offers plenty of talking points given the challenges facing United today.
