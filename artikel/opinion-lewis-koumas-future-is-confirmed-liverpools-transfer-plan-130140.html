Lewis Koumas has been a bit of a revelation for Liverpool.

The Welshman has joined up with the Reds' first-team squad thanks to the appointment of Andoni Iraola and his trust in youngsters.

When it was first revealed that Koumas would be staying with the Reds, most people thought he'd be the last option on the Spaniard's list.

However, he's somehow managed to become what looks to be his fourth or fifth-choice attacker.

Granted, most of his playing time has come as a striker but, he can also be used on either wing if needed.

But, with Hugo Ekitike returning and many different attackers now being linked with Liverpool, questions are being asked about what the future has in store for the 21-year-old.

Liverpool have added Gustavo Varela to their shortlist as a long-term target.

As mentioned, Koumas has been Isak's back-up so far this season.

The attacker has also been used in other positions but, when Ekitike is back in action, it's hard to see where and how the youngster is going to get a regular role.

That is, unless Ekitike comes back as a fraction of his former self, of course.

Obviously, Liverpool won't want that as they spent around £70 million on the Frenchman last term and, before his injury, he was probably their best player.

So, for Koumas, this season could be his last as a regular first-team option.

That is, especially after the latest transfer enhancement from Football Insider on Liverpool's pursuit of Gustavo Varela.

"Liverpool will not prioritise a move to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela in January, sources have told Football Insider," they wrote.

"The 21-year-old has emerged as a target for the Reds, having struck 11 goals from his opening 11 games in Serie A so far this term.

"The Reds are "keeping tabs" on the Portuguese youngster, with several top Italian clubs also having their "eyes fixed on him" at the moment.

"However, sources say that Varela is viewed as more of a long-term option at Anfield, and the club are not likely to make a move for him this winter.

"Monza are relaxed over their frontman's future for now, but they know that further interest from top clubs is inevitable if he continues to perform.".

If Liverpool sign Gustavo Varela, there is going to be no room for Lewis Koumas.

Obviously, keeping Koumas a part of Liverpool's looks is a major thing for Iraola at the moment.

His endless running coupled with his keen eye for goal have made him an excellent option for Liverpool to have at their disposal.

However, with someone such as Varela, who is already the Serie A's top goal scorer at 21 years old, there's going to be a lot of competition ahead of him.

Indeed, his recent links with Liverpool are just rumours at the moment but, if he continues with this purple patch of form for the entire season, most clubs from across the world are going to be interested in acquiring his services.

Right now, it's unclear exactly what the Reds proposal to do with their squad but, the fact that they're already trying to sort their shortlist out for the future means that they must have a pretty accurate idea about what they want to do.

Here's hoping that the Reds sign enough players to make a difference to their team and actually improve it.
