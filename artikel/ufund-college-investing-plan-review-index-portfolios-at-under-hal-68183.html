📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Open a U.Fund College Investing Plan account if you invest with Fidelity or want low-cost index funds. Its index portfolios that follow your child’s age charge 0.12% a year, under half the average for such portfolios at plans sold without an advisor. Check your own state first, since a tax break limited to its own plan is lost here.

The U.Fund College Investing Initiative is Massachusetts’ 529 plan, managed by Fidelity and open to savers in any state, and its index portfolios that follow a child’s age cost 0.12% a year. At an assumed 6% return, saving $200 a month for 18 years costs about $680 in fees there by our math, against about $1,690 at 0.30%, Morningstar’s 2025 average for all such portfolios in plans sold without an advisor.

Fidelity customers, Massachusetts residents, and savers in states with no 529 tax break or one that covers any blueprint get a Gold-rated plan with index portfolios at 0.12% a year or less. Savers whose state gives its tax break only for its own plan should start with that plan.
