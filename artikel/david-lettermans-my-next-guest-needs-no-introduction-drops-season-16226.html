Netflix‘s My Next Guest Needs No Introduction With David Letterman will premiere its seventh season on Tuesday, December 15, and a newly introduced teaser (above) shares some insight into Letterman’s visits with Matt Damon (Season Premiere, 701), Julia Roberts (Episode 702), Jimmy Kimmel (Episode 703), and professional climber Alex Honnold (Episode 704).

The video also gives a sneak peek at Letterman and Damon’s recent foray as background actors on HBO’s The Gilded Age. For anyone who missed the news, the duo made a surprise appearance in the Season 4 premiere of the hit HBO period drama. In truly a blink-and-you-miss-it moment, the men dressed in era-appropriate attire, from their hats to their shoes, crossed the street. When Damon sits down for a chat on My Next Guest Needs No Introduction With David Letterman, all will be revealed about how the gig came to be and why they wanted to do it. Roberts also appears to join Letterman on some sort of adventure, dropping heavy items from tall buildings. Kimmell’s appearance brings together two titans of late-night, as he and Letterman talk about hosting duties. Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that netflix’s My Next Guest Needs No Introduction With David Letterman Combines humor, curiosity, and in-depth conversations with extraordinary people. The chat show has Letterman back in the host’s chair, following a 33-year reign in late-night.

Sources indicate that prior guests across 31 episodes in six seasons and four specials have included Barack Obama, Tina Fey, Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Jay-Z, Robert Downey Jr., Volodymyr Zelenskyy, Dave Chappelle, Michael B. Jordan, Miley Cyrus, Caitlin Clark, Billie Eilish, and Adam Sandler.
