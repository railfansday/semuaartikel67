Kepa handed Arsenal opportunity to prove he can fill Raya’s boots against Dortmund

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

With David Raya out, Kepa Arrizabalaga has a great chance to prove that he is more than just an Arsenal cup keeper. Mikel Arteta needs to have confidence in his number two. He needs to ensure that he upholds the same high bar set by the starting keeper as Borussia Dortmund come to the Emirates.

Raya has been ruled out of tonight’s Champions League game following a shoulder problem which he picked up in Saturday’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle United.

Recent findings suggest that whilst the initial diagnosis is good news, surgery is not required. Further tests will determine Raya’s recovery period.

As previously mentioned, Kepa will be stepping in for Raya at St James’ Park. Moreover, Arteta has stated his preferred choice of player in this situation. It seems that Illan Meslier will miss out on the opportunity to play this time.

It has been revealed that as well as playing for Arsenal, the Spaniard has formerly played in the Champions League for Chelsea and Real Madrid. He has already appeared 14 times for the Gunners. Including in the league stage match which saw them beat Kairat Almaty.

Whilst Kepa has typically been second choice to Raya, his game time has mostly been in the EFL Cup. He will now need to prove himself as a trustworthy option for Arsenal should Raya be injured or otherwise unable to play.

Sources indicate that as well as being a good goalkeeper, Raya is also good at passing and positioning. This in turn assists Arsenal in attacking from the back line.

Ex-Arsenal midfielder Paul Merson has acknowledged the quality that Spanish goalkeepers have at creating good defensive and central positioning. This can frequently be an under-recognised yet important component in Arsenal’s build-up play.

Like Arsenal, Kepa will also have to remain calm when playing out from the back. Although Kepa does not necessarily need to replicate all of Raya’s qualities, he will need to have good decision-making skills as well as good communication.

Meslier is still in contention, having played in the Carabao Cup game against Fleetwood Town. Kepa has been Arsenal’s number two keeper in the Premier League and was able to rush on in for Raya when he was substituted at Newcastle.

It does seem that Kepa is the obvious option, and he can further cement his position in Arteta’s team.

Arsenal’s schedule is demanding. They are playing Manchester City on a Sunday and there are also worries about Raya’s fitness. So far, the club has not yet received medical information on Raya’s status.

Sources indicate that then there is Dortmund. They have already secured victories in their first four games in the Champions League and are level on points with Arsenal in the league section.

Kepa does not have a great deal of flexibility. Arsenal require the latest goalkeeper to offer them security.

In case Raya needs more time to recover, it is necessary for Arteta to be able to guarantee that there is an effective replacement.

For Kepa, it is equally an opportunity to prove that he can assume this leadership position for the team during vital moments.
