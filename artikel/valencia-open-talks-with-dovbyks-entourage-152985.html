Artem Dovbyk also watched Bologna’s last match from the bench.

In a recent development, the Ukrainian attacker is still waiting for his first start for the Felsinei; first Tedesco and then Palladino aren’t impressed by the former Girona player. Quite the opposite.

The 1997-born player moved to the Rossoblu on loan this summer with an option to buy for €17 million, an option that will never be exercised and forces Roma to take direct interest, to avoid further devaluing a €41 million investment.

In a recent development, now Valencia are serious and considering signing Dovbyk. One of the challenges to be resolved is his €3 million net salary, of which €2.5 million is paid by the Rossoblu. The Spanish club is expected to pay half.

According to Il Resto del Carlino, recent news has revealed direct contact between the Spanish club and the Ukrainian player, who has reportedly expressed interest in a return to La Liga.

According to recent reports, the 1997-born player has left a lasting impression, having scored 24 goals for Girona. Valencia has initiated initial discussions with Roma to understand potential terms and costs: approval will be needed from Bologna, which will pay Dovbyk €2.5 million of his €3 million net salary.
