Famous financial educator and author of "Rich Dad Poor Dad" Robert Kiyosaki has disclosed when investors should begin accumulating Bitcoin (BTC) during the current cryptocurrency market bloodbath.

While he is consistently bullish on Bitcoin, Kiyosaki is asking investors to be patient and not buy the flagship cryptocurrency before the current crash ends.

Sources indicate that in his latest X post on Nov. 20, Kiyosaki shared an vital investment lesson and warned his followers against catching a falling knife.

"Never grab a falling knife," Kiyosaki warned.

The financial guru suggested that this means investors should wait until the cryptocurrency market crash ends before buying more Bitcoin.

"That means wait for the crash to end….before buying," Kiyosaki elaborated.

Notably, Kiyosaki shared that he began buying Bitcoin when its price was around $20,000 after another major market crash.

The best-selling author hinted that he is planning to do the same during the current market downturn.

"Will wait for Bitcoin bottom again," Kiyosaki noted.

The financial educator reminded his followers of one of his important investment tips.

"Your profit is made when you buy….not when you sell," Kiyosaki noted.

Basically, the financial educator suggested that investors should prioritize finding good entry prices rather than trying to buy Bitcoin while its price keeps dropping.

However, Kiyosaki did not specify a Bitcoin price target or how he is going to know that the market has bottomed out.

Kiyosaki's remarks follow another devastating Bitcoin crash that took the flagship cryptocurrency to its lowest level in more than four months.

Industry observers note that on Nov. 20, the world's largest cryptocurrency crashed below the key psychological price level of $60,000 for the first time since July.

In a recent development, according to Reuters, Bitcoin crashed to around $59,381. Bitcoin shed over 3% in a single trading session.

Interestingly, BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) saw a record $541.1 million in daily outflows.

Overall, the latest data shows that November has seen total net outflows from U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs worth around $3.47 billion.

Interestingly, Kiyosaki has shared his excitement about buying Bitcoin during major market corrections several times.

It has been revealed that earlier, on Nov. 20, Kiyosaki described the ongoing market crash as a possible buying opportunity. He suggested that experienced investors are sitting back and waiting to accumulate while bad investors are trying to sell everything.

"I am most excited about Bitcoin's crash. Waiting to buy more," Kiyosaki declared.

Kiyosaki stressed that Bitcoin has a limited supply of 21 million coins. He believes that this scarcity makes Bitcoin vastly different from traditional government-issued currencies.
