Everything We Know About Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Rumored Wedding Details

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that after five years together, Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are officially tying the knot. And according to several rumors, the couple is apparently walking down the aisle any day now.

On Friday, November 20, sources with “direct knowledge” told TMZ that Miley and Maxx are getting married in Southern California this weekend. Details about the ceremony are still under wraps, according to the rumored report.

While Miley and Maxx haven’t confirmed anything yet, Page Six alleges they may have already secretly tied the knot. During Miley’s Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony in May, she posed for photos with her family before her mom, Tish, blurted, “We’re gonna bring the husband.”.

For what it’s worth, this feels like a stretch, since she could have been alluding to Maxx eventually becoming Miley’s husband—but another Us Weekly source previously claimed that they were into the idea of eloping.

It has been revealed that “Miley is really happy right now and in such a good, calm place,” the insider shared in December 2025. “She’s not super into the idea of doing another wedding.”.

It’s unclear whether the rock star couple is actually tying the knot this weekend, but the timing is interesting. Not only does it fall close to Miley’s birthday on November 23, but it would also be nearly a year since the Liily drummer proposed to the Hannah Montana alum.

ICYMI, Miley sneakily debuted her engagement ring with a series of photos posted to Instagram from her birthday dinner last year. Maxx’s dad also appeared to be at the dinner, according to photos he posted that same night, where he congratulated Miley and Maxx on their engagement.

I’m taking this all with a grain of salt until they reveal deets themselves, but I feel like now’s as good a time as ever to listen to Miley’s song, “Let’s Get Married.”.
