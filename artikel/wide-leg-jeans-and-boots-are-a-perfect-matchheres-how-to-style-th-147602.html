Sure, it’s boot season, but our jeans aren’t going anywhere, which means these two were always going to end up together. Wide-leg jeans have become everyone’s favorite silhouette thanks to their comfortable, relaxed fit, yet they can easily be styled for a more elevated look. That’s where a polished boot comes in.

And whether you prefer a heel or a flat, the formula works. Wide-leg jeans and boots make a great pair, especially when that boot is an ankle boot. The shorter shaft lets the hem skim the top of your shoe without bunching, so your jeans keep their effortless drape and your boots still get their moment.

A pointed-toe or sleek block-heel bootie will sharpen the look, while a lug or flat sole style keeps things casual. Whatever look you’re going for, finishing your denim outfit with boots is a smart way to add polish, personality, and a little extra confidence to everything you wear this season.

Give your wide-leg jeans an effortless finish with an oversized leather jacket and the perfect everyday ankle boot, featuring a soft square toe and a short block heel.
