Man City will regret how they have 'treated' James Trafford - ex-goalkeeper

James Trafford was treated at Manchester City in a similar way to how Joe Hart was 10 years ago.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout and former professional goalkeeper, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 24-year-old has impressed this season after he was sold to Leeds United in a deal worth up to £45million in the summer transfer window, just a year after returning to the club.

Man City had re-signed Trafford from Burnley after utilising their matching rights clause, with the expectation being that he would be their newly introduced number one following Ederson's exit.

But just weeks after his return had been confirmed, transfer chiefs at the Etihad Stadium spied an opportunity to sign Gianluigi Donnarumma from PSG and did so.

The Italy international instantly became the number one in the Premier League and the Champions League, with Trafford resigned to appearances in the cup competitions.

In the 2015-16 season, Hart made 47 appearances for Man City and was England's number one goalkeeper.

But Pep Guardiola's arrival in the summer of 2016 changed everything. Hart was loaned out to Torino and initially replaced by Claudio Bravo, before Ederson arrived in 2017.

The Englishman would go on to play for West Ham on loan, before playing for Burnley, Tottenham and Celtic until his retirement in 2024.

"They followed the handling of him as they did with Joe Hart, it's the same treatment," King told Football Insider when asked about Man City's treatment of Trafford.

Sources indicate that "Joe Hart was England number one, and for some reason [they stated]: 'He isn't good enough. We're bringing in the Brazilian keeper, Ederson.'.

"Then Joe Hart leaves the football club and goes to Celtic and wins loads of cups and leagues and everything up there. He's now a pundit and a good one.

"He had his career pushed out, and it didn't help the standard of goalkeeping in England, did it? He was England's number one and all of a sudden, he wasn't good enough for Man City.

According to recent reports, "Because of the reason we've been talking about - his feet. That's why he got replaced, because they wanted to bring the Brazilian boy because he was good with his feet.".

Leaving Man City has been momentous for Trafford, who is now in contention to be in England's number one after an impressive start with Leeds.

And with Donnarumma attracting criticism this season, their treatment of their former academy player may well be something looked back on with regret.

"What a horrendous, horrendous feeling. Not playing," Trafford told BBC Sport. "You are ringing a lot of people and just venting.

"Like most people, you have a [bad] day at work and you ring someone up. I ring my dad, I ring my mum, I ring my missus, I ring my mates. I like ringing my mates and we can just have a good talk about it all. It's quite funny.

Sources indicate that "I had to play regularly this season for my own mental wellbeing. You just lose yourself not playing. Maybe I'm different, I don't know, but the feeling of not playing every week, it felt horrendous.".

"I just had to play every week this season for that mental feel-good and to keep progressing with my career and edging me closer to achieving the dreams I want to achieve.".
