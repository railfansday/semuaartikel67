Tech Report: Why is Crypto Crashing Today: BTC, ETH and XRP Fall Amid $1 Billion Liquidations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on Friday as heavy liquidations dragged the crypto market lower. Close to $1 trillion has now been erased since the market’s September top. BTC traded at $59,677, down 3.6% in 24 hours and 11.3% over seven days. Ethereum dropped 3.9% to $1,658.91 and is down 11.5% on the week. XRP lost 5.1% to $0.9072, a weekly drop of 8.7%.

It has been revealed that about $1.07 billion in leveraged positions was wiped out over 24 hours, and long positions took most of the hit. Total crypto market capitalisation slipped below $2.1 trillion for the first time in almost five months.

Large-cap tokens fell as hard as Bitcoin or harder:.

In a recent development, last week, Bitcoin held the $61,000 to $62,100 band near $62,000. That band has now broken, and the decline extends a run of six straight weekly losses, the longest since February. Ether has dropped through support at $1,730 to $1,800 and is now testing $1,650. XRP has fallen below weekly support at $0.93 to $0.94 and is trading just above $0.90.

Sellers Show No Sign of Fatigue.

On the daily chart, roughly $63,300 has flipped into resistance. That puts Bitcoin in a range between $59,443 and $63,300, with price drifting toward the lower edge. Sell-side volume has printed the second-largest bar of the current 31% correction and could surpass last week’s level. That points to capitulation, not seller exhaustion.

In past bear markets, Bitcoin has tended to weaken:.

According to recent reports, bitcoin’s realised price is below $54,000. Past cycle bottoms formed when on-chain risk readings fell under 0.1, against about 0.167 now.

Industry observers note that hotter-than-expected CPI data has revived fears of a Federal Reserve rate hike, and the 10-year Treasury yield is hovering near 5%. Equities are in a liquidation cycle after turmoil at Nvidia following Jensen Huang’s exit. Bitcoin’s February low near $58,000 is now the key level for the market.
