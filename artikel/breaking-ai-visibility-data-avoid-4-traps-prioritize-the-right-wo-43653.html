The first trap is treating AI visibility tracking like a keyword-ranking report.

A score is only useful if the questions being measured reflect the people you want to reach.

Instead, choose prompts that reflect your customers’ questions across buying stages, markets, and AI platforms before deciding what to change.

Imagine a tool reports 72% visibility. That number could look reassuring while describing a narrow set of branded, informational questions from one customer group, market, and AI platform.

If you cannot tell whether that score includes the questions your buyers ask when comparing options, it cannot tell you which content needs attention.

To assess our example 72% score, check whether it represents the customers and buying decisions that matter to your business, using a consistent set of prompts for comparison.
