In a recent development, GTA 6 is currently going live across the world, and in doing so, its PS5 Trophy list has now been made public. Exophase has the full list, which comprises 55 Trophies, including a Platinum Trophy.

The significant thing here is that — just like every other Rockstar title released from PS3 onwards — you'll need to achieve 100% completion to unlock every Trophy.

I've looked through the complete list, and there's nothing there I'd consider a spoiler; the Trophy names and descriptions are vague, so don't worry about having the ending spoiled or anything like that.

As stated, a Gold Trophy called A Long Road wants you to "attain 100% completion". Given how much content is packed into GTA 6, this could take you hundreds of hours to accomplish.

Alongside the requirements of the 100% completion, you've obviously got to complete the main story, win races and complete stunts, finish missions for specific characters (which I assume are side quests), and also succeed in an assortment of robberies.

I'm trying to keep things brief so you can experience the game as freshly as possible, but if you do wish to view the full GTA 6 Trophy list, click the link through to Exophase. Again, there are no spoilers.

Our GTA 6 PS5 review went live earlier today, calling it the "greatest open world game ever made" and giving it a 10/10 score. "GTA 6 is the deepest, densest, most accomplished open world since Red Dead Redemption 2," we concluded. "Rockstar has set a fresh sandbox standard here, with a world that feels alive and populated with real people; Leonida is a place where it appears almost anything can happen at any time.".
