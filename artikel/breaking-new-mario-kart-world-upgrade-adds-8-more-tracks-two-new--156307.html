Mario Kart World has a brand-newly introduced enhancement available to download right now on Nintendo Switch 2, bringing eight new courses to race on, two new Knockout Tour routes, and more. This new racing content arrives as part of update version 1.9.0 for Mario Kart World.

The major highlight of the update is the eight new courses, which are actually remixed spins on classic Mario Kart tracks from across the series' history. They are: Mario Bros. Circuit 2, Desert Hill 2, Shy Guy Bazaar 2, Wario Stadium 2, Airship Fortress 2, DK Pass 2, Koopa Troopa Beach 2, and Salty Salty Speedway 2. You can get a look at each in the image below:.

Sources indicate that this update also adds two fresh Knockout Tour routes: Feather Rally and Lucky Cat Rally. Both are available to race through starting today.

Elsewhere in the upgrade, Nintendo has revamped the Mario Kart World "Replay" feature, letting players watch replays of past races and battles.

You can check out everything in the newly introduced revision in the Mario Kart World Update 1.9.0 Trailer below:.

Mario Kart World launched last year alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 on June 5. Since then, Nintendo has continued to dole out updates for the kart racing game.
