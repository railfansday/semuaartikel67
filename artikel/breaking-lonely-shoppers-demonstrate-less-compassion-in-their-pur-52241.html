Breaking: Lonely shoppers demonstrate less compassion in their purchases than others, study shows

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (86 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Chronically lonely people are less likely to buy products that benefit others, such as fair-trade or child-labor-free goods, according to a new study by researchers at Concordia and the University of the Fraser Valley. The team ran four studies involving more than 1,400 participants across North America. Each person completed a standard loneliness questionnaire, then chose between ordinary products and versions that cost 15% to 20% more but carried an ethical or environmental label. Items included toothpaste, chocolate bars, jackets and backpacks.
