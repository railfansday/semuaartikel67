Minecraft is getting a newly introduced throwing disc weapon in its first drop of 2027, with full off-hand support also finally coming to Bedrock Edition.

Mojang revealed both features during Tubbo’s Mid-Offs show at TwitchCon San Diego on November 14, giving players a first look at what’s coming early next year.

The throwing disc is a new medium-range weapon that damages enemies as it passes through them, before returning to the player who threw it.

Coming soon to your Minecraft world: Throwing Discs & Off-hand! 🟢 The throwing disc is a newly introduced ranged weapon that returns after you throw it. It can ricochet off hard surfaces and can even go through multiple enemies! 🟢 Bedrock players will now be able to hold stuff with… pic.twitter.com/mROCOG3MPq.

“It doesn’t have the longest range, but you can kind of extend its flight time by ricocheting it off different surfaces,” one of the developers explained, adding that they hope it lets players “do some cool trick shots.”.

Players will be able to craft the disc from copper, iron, gold, diamond and netherite, and can choose to throw it from either their main hand or their off-hand.

Mojang stated it had been vital to balance the disc against the existing weapons in the game, describing it as “a newly introduced combat fantasy” that could shake up both combat and minigames.

The disc’s off-hand option led into the second announcement, with Mojang confirming that “full off-hand support is finally coming to Bedrock Edition.”.

Java Edition players have been able to use items in their off-hand for years, but on Bedrock the slot has been limited to a small number of items.

“I’m so happy we’re bringing this long-awaited feature to our Bedrock players,” a developer noted. “It will add so many new possibilities to play.”.

Bedrock players won’t have to wait long to try it, as Mojang confirmed off-hand support will arrive in the next preview build. More details on the first drop of 2027 are set to be shared soon.
