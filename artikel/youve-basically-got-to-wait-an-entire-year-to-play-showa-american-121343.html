Here is an incredibly far-off release date you can pencil in (and then probably rub out because it'll be delayed again): the wild and wacky Showa American Story is coming to PS5 on 28th October 2027.

It was originally planned to launch in 2026, so this is a pretty substantial delay to the game, but the "core game content" is now complete, according to the developer.

The next 11 months will be dedicated to "polishing and optimizing the experience so that when you step into Showa American Story, it feels cohesive, responsive, and complete".

According to recent reports, for those who don't know, Showa American Story is based on the scenario where "Japan has bought out most of the United States with its strong economic power. A surge of immigration has firmly planted Japanese culture within the American continent. Two cultures are rapidly amalgamated, although conflicts are inevitable, citizens gradually adapt to a life never imagined as time goes by.".

This leads to a bizarre adventure, which we got a small taste of at Gamescom earlier this year. You play Chigusa Choko, a female movie star brought back from the dead who sets out on a quest with vibes of Shenmue, Yakuza, and No More Heroes.

In a recent development, what we played was incredibly unfinished and janky, but there was still some promise on show. Let's hope almost a year of polish and further work on the game turns it into a strong experience.
