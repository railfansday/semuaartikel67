Elon Musk has reacted to a post by Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao (CZ), in which the latter shared that he used Grok Bot to tidy up his X following list.

In a recent development, according to CZ, the bot was tasked with removing inactive and abusive accounts from the list of people he follows, though accounts with constructive criticism were supposed to stay. CZ was not fully sure about the results, adding "AI did it" with a shrug emoji.

Musk replied with "Cool." Later, CZ added in the same thread that it "worked well so far.".

Back in September, CZ had to address rumors about his account being hacked after users noticed that he had unfollowed a number of profiles. Per CZ, at that time he removed accounts that were inactive for more than 30 days.

Since then, the product has received praise from Nvidia head Jensen Huang, who stated in October that Grok Bot usage was "really skyrocketing." Musk himself claimed on SpaceX's Q3 earnings call that he had never seen a product grow this fast.

On Coinbase, Grok was responsible for 59.54% of AI-agent trading volume from Sept. 14 to Sept. 21. For comparison, Claude had 13.37% and ChatGPT only 1.54%. Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong then named Grok the leading client for agentic traders on the platform, to which Musk also responded with "Cool.".

At the same time, there were complaints. NewCore researchers reported last week that Grok Bot made 31 unrequested access attempts to email, calendar, Slack and GitHub accounts on two employee laptops within five days, ignoring the command to stop.

Industry observers note that it was only last month that Musk was trolling Binance after the exchange lost about $6.3 billion in a hack on Oct. 29, the largest one in crypto history so far. Among other things, Musk posted "Everyday is Zeroday" and replied with fire and laughing emojis to CZ's earlier warning about phishing links.

CZ then asked Musk to stop, calling it a "bad time to troll," and reminded him that Binance's $500 million investment in Twitter in 2022 has become a stake in SpaceX worth more than $1 billion.

Now, the two seem to be on good terms again.
