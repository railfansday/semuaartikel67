Brock Purdy (ankle) missed a second straight practice on Thursday and is uncertain to play on Sunday against the Seattle Seahawks.

The San Francisco 49ers quarterback is dealing with an ankle injury suffered last week against the Minnesota Vikings. It is not a high ankle sprain, head coach Kyle Shanahan said this week, but there is a "possibility" Purdy sits this week.

It has been revealed that should Purdy be ruled out, Mac Jones would get the start. Jones won five of his eight starts last season as Purdy dealt with a toe injury.

Like Purdy, Justin Herbert (concussion) was a DNP on Thursday. The Los Angeles Chargers quarterback was placed in protocol after Sunday's loss to the Latest York Jets.

"This isn't my first rodeo with regard to starting quarterbacks having an injury you don't know at the beginning of the week when we start game planning," stated offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel, who added DJ Uiagalelei will start if Herbert gets ruled out for the 1-9 Chargers.

Other Week 12 injuries to monitor:.

Industry observers note that parker Washington has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Tennessee Titans. The Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver injured his shoulder in practice on Thursday and is considered week-to-week. Head coach Liam Coen added that he's "not sure yet" whether running back Bhayshul Tuten (foot/ankle) will play. Chris Rodriguez Jr. would get the RB1 responsibilities if Tuten cannot go.

Recent findings suggest that las Vegas Raiders listed tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) as limited on Thursday, but the team is optimistic he will suit up against the Cleveland Browns. Bowers missed last week's loss to the Denver Broncos after exiting Week 10 early.

Kirk Cousins likely won't get one of his weapons back in Week 12 as wide receiver Jordan Addison (thumb) remains sidelined. Minnesota Vikings head coach Kevin O'Connell said Addison is "progressing well," but it does not look like he will be back on the field in Week 12.
