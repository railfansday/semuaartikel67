Holland America Line is returning to Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Nov. 26 with an Alaska-themed float.

The cruise line will be back at the parade’s 100th edition for a second consecutive year, showcasing “The Land of Glaciers, Wildlife & Wonder” float featuring kinetic design elements inspired by the destination’s glaciers, wildlife and landscapes.

Performing atop the float will be Grammy-winning rock band Blues Traveler.

“We are thrilled to return to Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade for this milestone 100th march and once again share the wonder of Alaska with millions of people across the country,” noted Kacy Cole, chief marketing officer for Holland America Line.

Recent findings suggest that “As we look ahead to 80 years of exploring Alaska, we’re proud to bring a destination that has been part of our story for decades to one of America’s most beloved holiday traditions. Blues Traveler is an iconic band with a remarkable legacy in American music, and we’re honored to have them join us as we bring Alaska to the heart of New York City.”.

Recent findings suggest that the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will air Thursday, Nov. 26 in the U.S. on NBC and stream on Peacock from 8:30 a.m. to 12 p.m., in all time zones.

Holland America Line will mark 80 years of Alaska exploration in 2027.

Six ships will sail in the region from April through September on itineraries ranging from seven to 28 days, including the monthlong “Alaska Arctic Circle Solstice” and a newly introduced eight-day option roundtrip from Vancouver, Canada.

Guests can sail roundtrip from Seattle, Washington; roundtrip from Vancouver; or between Vancouver and Whittier (Anchorage), Alaska.

The company is also updating its Denali Lodge, which is getting 192 fresh rooms as part of a $70 million renovation.
