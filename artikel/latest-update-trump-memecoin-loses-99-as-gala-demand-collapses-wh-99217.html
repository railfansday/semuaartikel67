U.S. President Donald Trump wrapped up his third exclusive dinner for his biggest Official Trump [TRUMP] memecoin holders on the 22nd of November.

Hosted at Trump National Golf Club in Sterling, Virginia, the dinner offered access to the President and other prominent personalities to investors who held significant amounts of $TRUMP.

The top 185 holders qualified for the dinner, while the top 29 received invitations to a separate VIP reception. In fact, the top four were also promised an 18-karat gold Trump watch. This came after the November midterm elections, where Democrats swept the majority in both the House and Senate.

But this dinner is raising eyebrows like never before.

According to recent reports, this is because there was a sharp decline in participation. During the first $TRUMP memecoin gala in May 2025, eligible investors reportedly needed to hold at least $55,000 worth of tokens to qualify.

This time, one participant reportedly secured entry with just $518 worth of tokens.

Furthermore, more than 5,000 holders who could potentially have qualified did not even register, despite organizers extending the registration deadline by four days.

That stated, the combined holdings of participants reportedly fell from $148 million in May 2025 to just $8.6 million for the latest gala.

Remarking on the same, Donald Sherman, president of Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington ( CREW), mentioned,.

Donald Trump’s memecoin dinner is the clearest evidence that he cares more about lining his pockets and giving access to his personal investors than addressing the problems facing the American public.

It has been revealed that $TRUMP memecoin price action further reflected this momentum as it was trading below $1, compared with its peak of approximately $75. At press time, too, the memecoin was trading at $0.9005 after an 81.3% drop in the year so far.

According to recent reports, that remarked, the controversy goes beyond the declining price of a cryptocurrency. This is because the Democrats argue that Trump is using his position as president to financially benefit from businesses connected to his personal brand.

The concern is that investors may have purchased $TRUMP tokens not simply because of the memecoin itself, but because holding them could give them insider access.

It has been revealed that needless to say, eight Democratic senators, led by Adam Schiff, have already questioned Trump and his crypto ventures thanks to the insider activity and profit-making. As expected, they have also called for investigations into his crypto-related businesses.

All of this comes after the Trump family reportedly earned at least $635 million from the memecoin, according to previous reports from AMBCrypto.

Yet, despite these political differences, crypto continues to enjoy bipartisan support, with a good number of Democratic and Republican lawmakers backing digital assets.
