Southwest Airlines’ Companion Pass, which allows you to bring a friend nearly for free (you just have to pay the taxes and fees) on unlimited flights, is one of the most valuable assets in the world of points and miles.

It normally takes a whole lot of flying to unlock this pass — and one of the popular shortcuts that allows you to earn this pass with points from Southwest credit card bonuses is ending soon, which will only make it even harder to earn.

Recent findings suggest that but if you'd rather spend your Black Friday weekend booking flights than buying discounted sweatpants, there is some good news. There's a new limited-time opportunity to earn a promotional Southwest Companion Pass if you book one round-trip or two one-way eligible Southwest flights by Sunday, Nov. 29, 2026, and travel on those flights by Jan. 5, 2027.

Your promotional Companion Pass would then be valid for unlimited use between Jan. 8, 2027, and March 3, 2027.

First, register for the promotion with your Southwest Rapid Rewards number. Then, book one qualifying round-trip flight or two one-way flights using your Rapid Rewards number by 11:59 p.m. CST on Sunday, Nov. 29. Flights must be completed sometime from Nov. 27 to Jan. 5, 2027.

Successfully complete the above steps, and you'll earn a promotional Companion Pass valid for use between Jan. 8 and March 3, 2027.

All fare classes, including the carrier’s Basic option, are eligible for this promotion. And it doesn’t matter how you pay for your flight, as there's no specific spending requirement. Rapid Rewards point redemptions and Cash + Points bookings qualify too, as long as you book through Southwest channels, such as the carrier’s website or mobile app.

If you have existing flights on Southwest, they won't automatically count for this promotion. However, if you can cancel those reservations, register and rebook before the promotion ends on Sunday, that should work (provided you complete your travel within the qualifying travel window). Be sure to check for any fare differences before canceling.

Southwest’s Companion Pass is almost legendary in the world of points and miles. It lets you bring a companion along on unlimited flights, whether you book with cash or points, for almost free. As long as there’s an open seat on the plane, your companion can fly with you for just the cost of taxes and fees (which start at $5.60).

According to recent reports, you must designate a companion in your Rapid Rewards account. However, you’re allowed to change your designated companion up to three times per year, and this rule applies to promotional passes as well.

If you'd like to earn a Companion Pass for longer than just eight weeks or so this promotional pass is valid, there is still time to earn a Companion Pass for all of 2027 using the welcome bonuses from the Southwest credit cards as outlined here. You have to act rapidly, though, since those rules are changing soon.
