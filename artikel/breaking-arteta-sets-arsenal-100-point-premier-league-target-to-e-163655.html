‘It’s only been done once and that’s what we want’.

Arteta not being drawn on City’s financial charges.

Mikel Arteta has set Arsenal the ­target of emulating Manchester City by becoming only the second team in Premier League history to record 100 points in a season.

Arteta was an assistant for Pep Guardiola’s side when they achieved the landmark in the 2017-18 campaign, beating second-placed Manchester United by 19 points. City head to the Emirates Stadium on Sunday already trailing Arsenal by six points after just 11 games, with Enzo Maresca’s side having failed to win any of their four previous league matches.
