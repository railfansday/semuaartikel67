By TOM COLLOMOSSE, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 17:00 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 17:05 EST, 24 November 2026.

Alejandro Garnacho's future at Aston Villa was plunged into further doubt on Tuesday as he was overlooked for their defeat at Galatasaray.

The Argentine joined on loan from Chelsea last summer with a £43million obligation to buy, triggered when certain targets were hit.

Yet Garnacho has yet to start for Villa and was not even on the bench in Istanbul, even though two goalkeepers were named.

Asked whether Garnacho was fit for this match, Villa boss Unai Emery noted: 'Today I think the best players to play with us, to be with us, were here.'.

Daily Mail Sport has reported regularly this season that Garnacho may not see out his loan spell and now that looks increasingly likely to be the case.

There is not thought to be a break clause in the loan but if Chelsea and Villa agreed to cancel it, that would allow Garnacho to go elsewhere in January.

Alejandro Garnacho was not in the Aston Villa squad for their defeat at Galatasaray on Tuesday.

Villa boss Unai Emery chose to include two goalkeepers on his bench instead of picking of him.

Villa had a similar situation with Harvey Elliott. The midfielder joined on loan from Liverpool in summer 2025 but barely played.

Emery decided quickly that he did not want to sign Elliott permanently and could not risk triggering the appearance clause that would have seen him join Villa for £35m.

Recent findings suggest that villa are now without a win in six and Emery admitted: 'The next challenge against PSG at home will be huge. If we can recover the three points we lost today maybe we still have a chance to reach the top eight but we are not favourites.'.

Emery also bemoaned the home side's time-wasting tactics after Deniz Gul headed them into a 77th-minute lead.

He remarked: 'I am not blaming the referee but he was unfair with us by adding only five minutes. After they scored it was impossible to play.'.
