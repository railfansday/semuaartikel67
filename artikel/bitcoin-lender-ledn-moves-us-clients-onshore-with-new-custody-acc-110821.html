Bitcoin Lender Ledn Moves US Clients Onshore With New Custody Accounts

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that bitcoin lender Ledn is moving beyond loans. The tech giant launched Ledn Custody on Tuesday, a new account that lets eligible U.S. clients hold bitcoin, gold, stablecoins and U.S. dollars, and buy, sell and swap between them in one place, with no trading fees and a 0.5% spread.

Ledn notes that spread makes buying bitcoin through its app about half the cost of a standard purchase on the most popular U.S. bitcoin apps. The launch also moves Ledn’s U.S. clients, previously served from its Cayman Islands entity, to a U.S. manufacturer with a U.S. custodian.

Industry observers note that ledn has lent against bitcoin since 2018 and has serviced more than $11 billion in loans. Until now, most of its U.S. clients bought their bitcoin elsewhere and moved it to Ledn to borrow against it. The latest account brings that first purchase in-house, from buying through to borrowing.

Sources indicate that A standard bitcoin purchase on the most popular U.S. apps costs about 0.9% to 1.25% once trading fees and spread are included, according to Ledn’s own analysis of published fees from October. That puts Ledn in direct competition with the trading apps where many Americans buy their first bitcoin.

On a $1,000 purchase, that works out to about $5 at Ledn against $9 to $12.50 elsewhere. Clients can deposit dollars and hold them in the account, or use them to buy bitcoin, gold and stablecoins. BitGo describes the gold option as tokenized gold.

In a recent development, “What our clients want is to hold hard assets, whether that’s bitcoin, gold or stablecoins,” Ledn co-founder and Chief Strategy Officer Mauricio Di Bartolomeo stated. “Most people buy them in one place, keep them in another, and when they need cash, they sell. Now they can do all three in one account.”.

He added that clients can borrow against their bitcoin instead of selling, “with gold to follow.”.

Recent findings suggest that assets in a Ledn Custody account are held for the benefit of each client by an independent qualified custodian, segregated from Ledn’s own assets and never lent out for interest. BitGo Bank & Trust, a unit of BitGo (BTGO), provides the custody and trading services behind the account.

According to recent reports, the custody setup is a selling point for a lender that came through 2022. Ledn says it has returned every client’s collateral across two full market cycles, including the collapse that bankrupted Celsius, BlockFi and Voyager. The tech giant has also published monthly loan book and proof of reserves data.

Recent findings suggest that “We’ve spent eight years earning our clients’ trust as a lender,” Ledn co-founder and CEO Adam Reeds stated. “If you’re going to be a financial services company, boring is a good thing. We want to be the reliable, stable place where people build their wealth, not a platform with a million features built for trading.”.

The onshore move lines up with Washington’s message. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said in May that crypto’s problems stem from “the wild, wild west offshore,” adding, “we got to bring it onshore.” The Clarity Act, the market structure bill he urged Congress to pass, stalled in the Senate in September.

Recent findings suggest that ledn is chasing a massive market. More than 67 million Americans now own crypto, or about one in four U.S. adults, according to a May survey. Competition for bitcoin borrowers is also heating up, with Cross River Bank launching its first bitcoin-backed loans this week.

Ledn has leaned on the capital markets to fund its lending, selling $188 million in bitcoin-backed bonds in February. The custody account now gives the lender a way to win clients before they ever need a loan.

According to recent reports, ledn Custody accounts will roll out to eligible U.S. individual clients over the coming days. The organization intends to add lending against gold and open more U.S. states in the coming weeks.
