In a recent development, BYD is preparing to launch a new midsize SUV under its personalized, off-road-focused sub-brand. The new SUV was spotted testing, offering an early look at its boxy, rugged-looking design.

Fang Cheng Bao, or FCB as many call it, is another BYD sub-brand, alongside Denza and Yangwang.

The “personalization-focused” brand offers a lineup of hardcore electric and plug-in hybrid SUVs, including the Bao 5 and Bao 8, sold as the Denza B5 and B8 outside of China.

It also sells the Tai series, designed more as everyday family vehicles, such as the Tai 7. BYD sells the Tai 7 as the Ti 7 under its own brand in Europe and the UK, where Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) has taken legal action, alleging it’s a copycat of the Defender.

Meanwhile, BYD is gearing up to add another SUV to the lineup. FCB’s general manager, Xiong Tianbo, previously confirmed that the Tai 6, or Ti 6, will launch in 2027.

Recent findings suggest that ahead of its official debut, the new Ti 6 was captured in China. Although it’s still covered, the images sent to Autohome suggest it retains the boxy, upright design of the larger Ti 7 but appears slightly smaller.

It has been revealed that unlike the Ti 7’s split-headlight design, the smaller SUV features redesigned headlights. Like most of BYD’s new higher-end vehicles, the SUV is equipped with a LiDAR to support advanced safety and convenience features.

BYD has not revealed battery or powertrain specs, but the Ti 6 (Tai 6) is expected to be offered in pure electric (EV) and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) variants.

According to recent reports, it will likely offer a similar setup to the Ti 7. The Ti 7 PHEV uses a 1.5L engine and an electric motor that delivers a combined 360 kW (482 hp).

The pure electric version with a single motor delivers 300 kW (402 hp), while the dual-motor variant delivers 515 kW (690 hp).

Since the Tai 7 (Ti 7) starts at 179,800 yuan ($27,000), the smaller Tai 6 (Ti 6) will likely be priced in the 150,00 yuan ($22,500) range.
