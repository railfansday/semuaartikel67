Liverpool aren't in the best run of form at the moment.

The Reds are miles away from first-placed Arsenal and, right now, it doesn't feel like they have the quality among their ranks to get back to the top of the Premier League table.

Fans expected Liverpool to be challenging for the title this season, especially after their £150 million + spend in the summer.

That obviously hasn't worked out with fans now questioning whether the club even has enough strength among their ranks to get them where they need to be.

Right now, the January transfer window is just over a month away and, fans are expecting a lot of change to take place.

So, the fact that one of their reported targets are '100%' on their way out of Anfield is a very good sign.

Barcelona are '100%' going to sell Alejandro Balde in January.

Since the arrival of Ronald Araujo, Liverpool's defence has been fairly solid.

The Uruguayan has brought an extra layer of stability to Andoni Iraola's ranks and has helped take a lot of pressure off the shoulders of Jeremy Jacquet.

Thanks to how strong Araujo has been, Jacquet has been allowed to take his time to settle into Premier League life and, he's probably been one of Liverpool's best signings thanks to that.

Right now, it's unclear what the Reds' priorities are going to be in January but, thanks to an patch from journalist Juan Jesus, a bit of a better insight has now been given to fans.

In a recent development, "Despite Deco’s words, Alejandro Balde is 100% out. Several clubs know this and are closely monitoring his situation, including Manchester United, PSG, AC Milan and Liverpool," he shared via X.

"Barça expects the bidding to drive up the price and is confident of securing his departure for at least 45–50 million euros.".

Alejandro Balde's upcoming price tag is expensive.

When talk of Liverpool being linked with Balde first emerged, the deal was pitched as an unmissable offer for the Reds.

Talk of a deal worth around £30 million was being mooted with other clubs such as Manchester United also linked with an interest.

However, the fact that the deal could end up costing around €50 million (£41.9 million) makes things a little harder to understand.

Obviously, Balde is an incredible player and would help solve two of the Reds' main problems going into 2027.

But, £41.9 million is a lot of money for the club to spend on another left-back.

Everyone thought that Milos Kerkez was going to be the second coming of Andy Robertson after he was signed for £40 million but, in reality, he still needs to do a lot of work to improve his game.

Right now, it's unclear what plans Liverpool have for the upcoming transfer window but, if they don't make a couple of meaningful signings, by the end of the 2026-27 season, they'll be fighting for a Champions League place, rather than the title.
