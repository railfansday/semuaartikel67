Viktor Gyökeres has put to bed any doubts over his future at Arsenal by stating his desire to stay.

Industry observers note that the striker has been under question over his long-term future under Mikel Arteta after a difficult start to the 2026/27 season. His latest comments suggest he will not be seeking a move away from north London.

Gyökeres moved to Arsenal for £63.5m from Sporting Lisbon last summer. He’s played a key role in the club winning the Premier League last season with 14 league goals.

However, he has not featured as much this season. Kai Havertz often being Arteta’s go-to man centrally, resulting in him only starting once in the Premier League.

Recent findings suggest that this has led to some speculation that the Sweden international may look at his options in January. However, his latest interview with the Swedish outlet Fotbollskanalen suggests otherwise.

Gyökeres was asked if he wants to stay at Arsenal and has made it clear he wants to continue his career at the Emirates.

The striker also spoke about his relationship with Arteta.

He explained that they regularly discuss football, the team’s approach and the opposition. Those conversations also help him understand what Arteta expects when he is selected to start.

It is a key point to note that just because he hasn’t had much time on the pitch doesn’t mean there have been any challenges between the two.

Sources indicate that gyökeres has begun to show his worth again in recent weeks. He found the net against Fleetwood in the League Cup and then Slavia Prague in the Champions League.

This came after a long time to get his first goal of the season and gives him the chance to remind Arteta what he can bring to the team.

There is no doubt that Gyökeres has not lived up to the expectations he had when he came from Portugal.

But his overall effect cannot be ignored. He has contributed 23 goals and 6 assists in 69 games for Arsenal.

It has been revealed that the challenge will be finding a way to get the best out of him and Havertz. They add different attributes, and Arteta will have plenty of options to choose from depending on the opposition.

According to recent reports, they will also want to avoid losing someone they have invested heavily in so early on. Gyökeres has a contract until 2030, so they are in a great position.

His latest comments will help to reassure fans that he is not looking to leave the club after stating his ambition to play more games and make himself an indispensable part of Arteta’s squad.

In a recent development, the main thing for Arsenal will be to make sure that he can turn this attitude into consistent performances. Gyökeres has made his intentions clear and now needs to show why they brought him to North London in the first place.
