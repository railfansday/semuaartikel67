📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Besides removing the Samples tab, YouTube Music for Android and iOS is rolling out instant access to Ask Music with a new Gemini icon in the top-right corner.

The Gemini spark appears on almost every screen in the app, including the Home feed, Library tab, Now Playing, albums, and playlists. It matches Gemini in Gmail and other Workspace apps online, as well as Google Messages in a future enhancement.

Tapping launches Ask Music, which requires a YouTube Premium or Music Premium subscription, as a fullscreen sheet. This chatbot lets you create a mix, discover artists, find podcasts, build a playlist, and ask about your listening history. You can also “customize your queue or dive deeper into artist lore.” In addition to the text field, there’s also voice input.

Ask Music introduced in 2024 and was previously accessed from a card in the Home feed.

The newly introduced access point has been rolling out in recent days as a server-side revision, and we’re seeing it on many more devices as of today. YouTube previewed this addition back in September along with “your podcast lineup.” This “personalized AI-audio guide” appears in the Podcasts feed to provide a “quick, weekly spoken preview of recommended indicates and digs into why you’ll like them, before jumping into the suggested episodes.”.
