RN’s antisemitic past ‘coming back to haunt them’: Will Le Pen ‘distance herself’ from Bardella?

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (140 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that could new allegations of antisemitism damage Jordan Bardella’s standing and the far-right National Rally’s electoral prospects? Charli James is pleased to welcome Paul Smith, Associate Professor in French and Francophone Studies at the University of Nottingham. Smith examines the far-right leader’s continued denials and considers whether Marine Le Pen might eventually distance herself from him. In the meantime, Smith suggests that Bardella needs to “make his mind up” about how to respond to these latest troubling allegations published by Le Monde and Mediapart. “It’s very difficult to deny. Le Monde and Mediapart, certainly Le Monde, wouldn’t be publishing these details if they didn’t have some pretty substantial evidence.” Could this prove to be the Achilles’ heel of a far-right party with its sights set on the second round of the presidential election?
