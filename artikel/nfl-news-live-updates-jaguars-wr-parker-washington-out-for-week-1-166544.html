Week 12 of the NFL season kicked off with a rare Wednesday matchup between the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams, prepping us for a full Thanksgiving feast that delivered fans a full helping of excitement (and then some). Bears-Lions, Eagles-Cowboys and Chiefs-Bills all came down to the wire, allowing no time for a post-Thanksgiving nap.

The Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers will face off Friday in a matchup that will have plenty of eyes drawn — even if not for reasons that are entirely due to the actual football being played on the field. Ahead of that matchup, the Steelers made headlines by reportedly benching veteran quarterback and former MVP Aaron Rodgers in favor of backup Mason Rudolph for Week 12 after recent struggles on offense.

According to recent reports, this storyline then reached a fever pitch on Wednesday when Rodgers appeared on "The Pat McAfee Show" and went scorched-earth on a rant about head coach Mike McCarthy. Rodgers accused McCarthy of repeatedly falling asleep in meetings and ending a team breakdown with "1-2-3 Let's go Packers." Rodgers was released and officially cleared waivers, now free to sign with any team as a free agent.

We're watching plenty of injuries ahead of the action, including Las Vegas Raiders All-Pro tight end Brock Bowers, who missed Week 11 with an ankle injury, though he was reportedly pushing to play in that matchup. The Jacksonville Jaguars ruled out WR Parker Washington after he suffered a shoulder injury in practice this week, too, which will have fantasy managers looking for other options on the waiver wire.
