Items such as desks, large rugs, tables, and white goods like refrigerators or stoves exceed the size and weight limits of typical garbage carts. Forcing these objects into carts can damage collection vehicles and pose safety risks to sanitation workers.

Recent findings suggest that garbage trucks are designed for loose household waste, not heavy or oversized objects. According to the HelloNation article, placing bulky items into standard bins can jam the truck's compaction system or hopper, leading to costly breakdowns and delays.
