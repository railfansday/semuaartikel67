One Of GTA 6's Most Overlooked Mechanics Is Its Best

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The release of Grand Theft Auto 6 has proven to be revolutionary in nearly every sense of the word. The latest action-adventure title from Rockstar Games has excelled so much that GTA 6 has become the best-reviewed game of all time, finally surpassing 1998’s The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. A lot of this comes from how well-designed all of the features it presents are. The several gameplay mechanics, the story, the attention to detail, the technical quality, and more make for a complete package that will provide players with over 100 hours of delightful gameplay.

In Screen Rant’s review of GTA 6, we mention that it “delivers on pretty much everything it promises,” while also taking a lesson from Rockstar’s own Red Dead Redemption 2, released in 2018. The fresh release has tons of different features to use and even uncover, such as a top-down zombie survival mini-game. Even though some of the new features are the ones taking the spotlight for themselves, there are also a few classic features that make a comeback. Among the several returning mechanics, there is one that is seriously overlooked in Rockstar’s new game.

Amidst all the chaos of Vice City and the larger state of Leonida, you can potentially find some serenity by pausing the action with the game’s photo mode. Since the last entry in the series, photo modes have evolved drastically and have become foundational to most AAA games nowadays. The photo mode in Final Fantasy 16, for example, allows for some pretty spectacular images, be they of moments of tranquility or intense combat. In GTA 6, it’s not all that different, apart from the fact that it’s an entirely different setting.

According to recent reports, the upcoming photo mode allows you to take some stunning pictures in GTA 6, be they centered around characters, locations, moments, and more. As you’d expect from a feature like this, you can change the camera angle, move it around, and adjust tons of other settings, such as lighting, poses, expressions, and much more, all to create the perfect opportunity for a click. This, combined with the cutting-edge ultra-realistic graphics the game has to offer, can make for some seriously breathtaking pictures. It’s all about experimenting and playing around with the photo mode.

Not every game with a photo mode provides the right tools or the right art direction to make moments worthy of being captured, but with a game like GTA 6, photo mode is nearly a game of its own. Depending on what you’re trying to capture, you’ll spend hours moving around Leonida and changing outfits in GTA 6 to get the perfect picture.

Industry observers note that there’s no denying that Rockstar’s latest release is absolutely gorgeous. While the photo mode isn’t put in the spotlight as much as other fun features and additions to the franchise, such as the possibility of not being able to steal a vehicle or being mugged when using an ATM, the classic and ever-reliable photo mode is there, ready for you to use when you feel like capturing all the beauty Grand Theft Auto 6 has to offer.
