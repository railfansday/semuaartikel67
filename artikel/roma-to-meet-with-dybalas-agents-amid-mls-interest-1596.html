Paulo Dybala turned 33 yesterday, and is now preparing to return to the pitch.

The Roma striker has overcome the micro-tear to his calf that has sidelined him for the past month, a problem he sustained in the first half against Real Madrid.

The Argentine star is also aiming to return to scoring form, having not found the net since January 18th. Gasperini’s team is short of goalscoring prospects, and number 21 is among them. An anomaly. Dybala, however, has recharged his batteries and is ready to light up the pitch with his play.

Recent findings suggest that dybala’s contract expires on June 30th, at the end of this season. As Leggo reports, overseas destinations have been insistently being discussed in recent hours.

Two American MLS clubs and Boca Juniors have made their interest in the Argentine striker, both of which have rekindled speculation about a return to his homeland or a stint in the United States for the final phase of his career.

Roma and Gasperini, however, already have a strategy in mind: in the coming weeks, the management will meet with the number 21’s entourage and discuss a possible one-year renewal.
