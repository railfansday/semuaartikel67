📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Open the nbkc Business Account if your business deposits cash and wants checking with no monthly fee or minimum balance. nbkc charges nothing for cash deposits at its branches or deposit-enabled partner ATMs and refunds up to $12 a month in other ATMs’ fees. The account pays no interest, but nbkc’s Business Money Market does.

Sources indicate that nbkc bank is an FDIC-insured bank with four Kansas City-area branches, and businesses anywhere in the U.S., from sole proprietors to nonprofits, can open this account online. Money the business won’t need soon can earn 2.75% APY (annual percentage yield) in nbkc’s Business Money Market, which has no minimum balance.

Industry observers note that A freelancer or small business that deposits cash near a Kansas City branch or a deposit-enabled partner ATM pays nbkc nothing for those deposits or for the account itself. A business outside Kansas City that needs cash deposits available the next day is better off with a business checking account at a bank with branches nearby.
