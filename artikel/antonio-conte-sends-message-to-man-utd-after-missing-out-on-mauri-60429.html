Antonio Conte has played down suggestions he missed out on the Manchester United job because of his demanding reputation in management.

United made their third mid-season managerial change in three years after sacking Michael Carrick with Mauricio Pochettino coming in as his replacement earlier this month.

Sources indicate that conte was among the names initially explored along with Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann. The Italian, a free agent since parting ways with Napoli, was thought to be keen on the role at Old Trafford but it was widely reported United had reservations over some of his methods.

The Italian led Juventus, Chelsea, Inter Milan and Napoli to league titles but has not lasted longer than two seasons at any club since leaving the Bianconeri in 2014.

Conte has a reputation for being hugely demanding with players and the board but in an interview with Cronache di Spogliatoio he insisted anyone scared of his approach is scared of winning.

The coach recalled a meeting with Sir Alex Ferguson during his time in charge of Chelsea, praising the standards the Scot noted before insisting the idea United no longer want to meet those standards is ‘offensive’.

‘If someone is scared of my method, then they’re scared of winning, because that methodology leads to winning,’ Conte said.
