📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that zcash [ZEC] has been moving against the grain of the broader crypto market, gaining 2.86% over the past 24 hours while Bitcoin [BTC] slipped 0.07% and the overall market continued to trade flat. This suggested the recovery was specific to ZEC rather than a result of improving market-wide sentiment.

According to recent reports, however, the rebound came after a steep correction, as ZEC is down 19.85% over the past seven days. With no fresh catalyst to explain the move, the gains seem to be a short-term relief rally. The question now is whether buyers can sustain this recovery.

On the daily chart, bulls have started fighting back as short-term momentum picks up. At the time of writing, ZEC traded at $921.75, remaining within the lowest Fibonacci retracement band. This indicated that the recovery was still weak and left the broader bearish structure intact.

OBV demonstrated a modest uptick after its sharp decline, while the RSI climbed to 35.41, suggesting selling pressure had eased but not enough for buyers to gain the upper hand in the market.

The H4 chart offered a closer look at the token’s recent recovery, with ZEC trending towards the 23.6% Fibonacci level at $939.93. If the bulls can successfully break above this resistance, it would strengthen the bullish case and bring the daily $1,013 level into focus.

Industry observers note that the MACD turned positive, while OBV began to stabilize after its steep decline. However, the lack of a stronger OBV recovery suggests buying volume has yet to fully support the improving momentum.
