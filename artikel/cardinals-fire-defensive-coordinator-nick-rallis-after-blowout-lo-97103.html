📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Arizona Cardinals fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis on Monday, a day after a 34-7 loss to the Kansas City Chiefs.

Industry observers note that head coach Mike LaFleur confirmed Rallis' dismissal in his weekly news conference. He said the decision to fire Rallis was made out of a need for a "fresh perspective.".

"Nick's a guy that I have a lot of respect for as a person, as a coach," LaFleur said. "… I just felt in my heart this morning that we needed a change. We needed a fresh perspective, a fresh voice.".

Senior defensive assistant Teryl Austin will take over as interim defensive coordinator. Austin has previously worked as the defensive coordinator for the Lions, Bengals and Steelers, most recently with Pittsburgh from 2022-25.

Sunday's loss dropped the Cardinals to 3-8. They've allowed 30-plus points in eight of their 11 games and rank 31st in the NFL with 29.4 points allowed per game. They rank 26th with 355.2 yards allowed per game.

The Cardinals have plenty of challenges beyond their defense. They rank 25th in total offense (290 ypg) and 29th in scoring (17.4 ppg) as the owners of the worst record in the NFC.

In a recent development, former Broncos head coach Nathaniel Hackett is Arizona's offensive coordinator. Hackett has had four previous stops as an NFL offensive coordinator, including for LaFleur's brother, Matt LaFleur, with the Green Bay Packers. Mike LaFleur acknowledged Monday that the Cardinals need to improve across the board.

In a recent development, "It's not been good enough collectively, all phases as a team. I'm in the business, and it's my job to find solutions, and this is one that we're trying to find. ….

Recent findings suggest that "We're all in this thing together. If you're a real man in this league, you're gonna look at yourself in the mirror first.".
