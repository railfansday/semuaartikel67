It has been revealed that google is teaming up with Jeff Bezos’ Blue Origin to launch its AI server satellites. The companies revealed on Thursday that Blue Origin’s Latest Glenn rocket will shuttle satellites equipped with Google’s Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) into space in the first quarter of 2029.

The partnership is part of Google’s Project Suncatcher, a moonshot initiative that could eventually put AI data centers in space. Google deployed its first AI server satellite in October, successfully booting up its TPUs and using the onboard Gemma 4 model to transmit an AI-generated haiku to Earth.

The Blue Origin launch will be the “first dedicated heavy-lift launch” for Project Suncatcher, putting satellites with the “computing capacity at roughly the scale of a data center rack” into sun-synchronous orbit, the companies said. Google’s AI server satellite previously launched aboard SpaceX’s Falcon 9 rocket as part of a “rideshare” launch that included several other payloads.
