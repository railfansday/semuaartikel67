Travelers can buy tickets to this attraction and have their reservations instantly confirmed via Klook, an online travel agency that specializes in experiences. Madame Tussauds Singapore source Merlin Entertainments.

Industry observers note that klook’s K.ai doesn’t just advise small operators. It makes the changes for them, and an early user claims it is already saving time. The bigger payoff, for operators and for Klook, could come from the tools still in development, such as pricing advice that helps them sell more.

Every morning, staff at MyProGuide used to check inventory by hand, spending at least 30 minutes on each product. The tour-guide and day-tour supplier, which has sold on Klook for more than a decade, now hands much of that work to a recently released AI agent built into Klook’s merchant portal.

Founder Jo Wong said the agent, K.ai, already helps his 35 staff review bookings, spot products nearing capacity and pull together complex data more quickly. What he wants next is harder to deliver: pricing recommendations, market price comparisons and booking-trend analysis, the tools that could help him sell more. For now, he is using K.ai to run daily operations rather than to drive bookings.

According to recent reports, K.ai is aimed at the small operators that make up most of the experience sector. It takes instructions in plain language. Ask for last week’s bookings and it pulls a report. Tell it.
