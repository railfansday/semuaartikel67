Mauricio Pochettino will be under no illusions as to the task ahead of him after Manchester United were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool in his first match in charge.

The Argentine head coach knew that a point would be enough to take Man United out of the Premier League relegation zone following a disastrous start to the season, but those hopes almost ended after just eight minutes when Leny Yoro was given a straight red card after bringing Alexander Isak down just outside the penalty area as he bore down on goal.

Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the resulting free-kick and the hosts never looked back, with further goals from Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz wrapping up a comfortable win for Andoni Iraola's men.

Pochettino would have known the Red Devils' shortcomings coming into the clash against Liverpool, and there is plenty to fix as he looks to revolutionise United, but their defensive challenges in particular were highlighted again at Anfield.

There were massive question marks over the involvement of Matthijs de Ligt in the opening goal, as he failed to challenge for the ball that led to Isak being left one-on-one with Yoro.

The Dutchman had been handed his first Premier League minutes for almost one year following injury, and he was miles off the pace as Iraola's attackers took full advantage.

The 27-year-old still has two-and-a-half years remaining on his contract at Old Trafford but, based on his latest showing, it would be no surprise to see United cash in on the former Juventus star in 2027.

If truth be told, club chiefs need to carry out major surgery on the whole backline, with extra depth and upgrades needed in every one of the defensive positions.

Luke Shaw and Diogo Dalot can be effective full-backs when they are fit and available, but United need better competition for them, while Shaw could be on his way out next summer anyway when his contract expires.

The Red Devils have substantial problems at the heart of their defence though, and Lisandro Martinez needs to be sold in January.

It appears likely that Martinez will be on his way out of Old Trafford in the summer anyway when his contract expires, but United should bring his exit forward by six months.

The Red Devils have the option to extend the Argentina international's contract by a further 12 months, having signed him for a fee of £57m in 2022, but reports suggest that they may not even do that as they look to offload him in 2027.

There was hope that the centre-back, nicknamed 'the Butcher', would be a top signing for United and the early signs were promising.

Lisandro Martinez Injury Record at United.

However, the South American defender has ultimately been another failed signing for United, especially in more recent times, while he has also suffered from his fair share of injury problems.

The 28-year-old has become an increasing liability at the back, with United now having the worst defensive record of any team in the top five European leagues this term.

Following the Champions League defeat to Atletico Madrid last month, Martinez publicly called out Jason Wilcox for the club's recruitment, all while suggesting that some of his teammates aren't at the required level.

That surely won't have gone down well in the dressing room, and it's difficult to see how a player comes back from direct criticism of a director of football.

Wilcox is remarked to have given the Argentine a 'dressing down' as he was fined and made a public apology, but he isn't showing the quality to prove that he has a future at the Theatre of Dreams beyond this season.

An exit for Martinez next summer seems inevitable, but United should be getting rid of him even sooner than that as they look to begin a defensive rebuild.
