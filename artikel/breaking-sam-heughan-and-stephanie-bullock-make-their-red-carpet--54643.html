It has been revealed that sam Heughan is making things red carpet official with his girlfriend, model Stephanie Bullock. On Sunday, November 15, the Outlander actor brought his partner to the BAFTA Scotland Awards in Glasgow for their first joint appearance.

For the occasion, Heughan wore a Siobhan Mackenzie navy jacket and waistcoat over a white shirt, paired with a blue-and-green tartan kilt and black lace-up boots. Bullock complemented his look, wearing a matching tartan shawl with a floor-length black sequined gown.

Although they have kept their relationship largely out of the public eye, Bullock was seen with Heughan at Upcoming York City’s Tartan Day Parade in April.

The couple’s red carpet debut coincided with a significant moment in Heughan’s career. At Sunday’s ceremony, he accepted the Outstanding Contribution to Television award on behalf of Outlander.

In a recent development, heughan keeps a low profile outside of work. In a November 2024 interview with InStyle, the actor discussed how public recognition can affect his ability to fly under the radar.

“I think actors before social media were pretty elusive,” he said. “I love to people watch. I don’t like standing out in the crowd…I love sitting in a café or a bar and just watching people.”.

He prefers not to share much about his private life because “I just think it’s who I am.” And keeping a little mystery helps with work, too. “I think an actor should disappear behind the character,” he noted. “You shouldn’t be like, ‘Oh, there’s Sam Heughan onstage playing a character.’”.
