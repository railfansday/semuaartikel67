📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The most complete unit in Gasperini’s Roma is the defense: six players for three positions, ready to rotate without lowering their level.

The only one a bit behind is Koulierakis. The same cannot be said for other areas of the pitch: as for the wingers, especially in terms of numbers, the coach is looking for solutions on the market.

As Il Corriere della Sera reports, the Grugliasco-born coach expects a player capable of playing both as a striker and as a full-back, like Wesley.

Gian Piero Gasperini, in addition to a winger, expects a striker in the January transfer window.

In a recent development, these are the Grugliasco-born coach’s two priorities, considering the winter transfer window. It won’t be an easy transfer campaign, but the coach and management have clear ideas on how to proceed: two more starters, not just newly introduced players.

The goal is to bring in players at least at the level of those already in the squad to make Roma complete and ready to dream of a Scudetto.

There are many variables, starting with the ability to find someone who can make an immediate impact, like Malen did last season or Balerdi in his first few months with the Giallorossi. The most difficult player to find is a winger; they don’t just need a winger, but someone who can also play a bit of defense.
