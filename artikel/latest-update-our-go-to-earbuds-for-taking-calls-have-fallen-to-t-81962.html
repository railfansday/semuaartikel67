Sources indicate that if you’re looking for a new pair of earbuds, the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro are on sale for $129.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart right now. That’s the lowest price we’ve seen for Anker’s excellent wireless earbuds, which normally retail for $169.99 and come in four colors (black, white, blue, and pink) all of which are discounted through November 30th as part of Anker’s ongoing Black Friday promo.

While the Liberty 5 Pro have a number of great features — including support LDAC for high-res audio and an IP55 dust and water resistance rating — the call quality is what stands out. The microphone does an impressive job cutting out background noise, and my colleague John Higgins praised the Liberty 5 Pro for filtering out emergency vehicles, arborists cutting tree branches, and even loud children. The active noise cancellation is excellent, which helps when you’re trying to hear someone on the other end of the phone whose earbuds aren’t as clear as yours. The charging case also features a handy 0.96-inch screen, so you can adjust the ANC and sound profile on the fly.
