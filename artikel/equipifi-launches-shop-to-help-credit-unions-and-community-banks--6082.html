With a catalog of more than 10,000 participating merchants, banks and credit unions can now turn the banking app into the place account holders plan, shop, and pay.

Industry observers note that SCOTTSDALE, Ariz., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- equipifi, the bank-native Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) platform powering more than 100 in-house programs at banks and credit unions, declared today that consumers can now use BNPL to shop directly from their digital banking app. From the app, consumers can see their purchasing power from their own bank or credit union, find and discover merchants, and choose a fixed payment plan before they buy.

This launch completes the availability of BNPL along the shopping journey, powered by equipifi. The BNPL platform already enabled eligible account holders to proposal a purchase ahead of time with extra funds, and split completed debit card transactions into installments. Now with Shop, the purchase itself can originate within the banking app, allowing consumers to get funded and begin shopping through a BNPL program the institution fully owns and controls. Each bank or credit union underwrites its own prepares and sets purchasing power based on its relationship with the account holder, so spending stays manageable.

Industry observers note that the move builds on fast-growing demand for BNPL at banks and credit unions. October BNPL volume is running at double last year's pace, according to equipifi's Purchasing Power Index, which also found that returning users are spending more than last year and that more than 80 percent of BNPL sessions during Cyber Week 2025 happened on a mobile phone.

Recent findings suggest that pioneer Federal Credit Union was one of the first financial institutions to make Shop available on its in-house BNPL program. "Pioneer Federal Credit Union brought Buy Now, Pay Later to our members so they could pay over time with a partner that has their best interests at heart," mentioned Tracey Miller, COO/EVP at Pioneer Federal Credit Union. "Shop extends the program we already offer, so members can use their purchasing power from Pioneer Federal Credit Union right from their banking app, and it keeps us at their side throughout their everyday purchases.".

In a recent development, "Account holders already open their banking app to see what they have spent, and check to see if they can make a future purchase. Shop lets them act on it right there, with their own bank or credit union behind the purchase, instead of handing that moment, the data, and the value that comes with it to a competitor's app," mentioned Bryce Deeney, Founder and CEO of equipifi. "We give financial institutions the technology to turn every shopping moment into a reason to come back to their app. That means deeper engagement, stronger retention, and newly introduced revenue for the institutions we serve.".

Recent findings suggest that by connecting their budgeting and purchasing tools, account holders also gain more control over how they spend. "Consumers expect a seamless experience wherever they choose to shop," mentioned Alex Mersereau, Head of Product at equipifi. "Their purchasing power should go with them to the merchants they already love and reflect their finances in real time, straight from the institution that provides it. That gives them the visibility, control, and confidence to make every purchase decision on their own terms.".

equipifi looks to extend the experience to feature local and regional businesses in 2027, so account holders can shop close to home and make purchases that support the community their institution serves.

Sources indicate that for the more than 10,000 merchants account holders can reach today, the banking app becomes an additional source of high-intent traffic, alongside the channels they already rely on. "These shoppers have expanded their budget before they ever reach the merchant's site," Mersereau added. "They arrive already funded by their own bank or credit union, which gives merchants a stronger audience than almost any other traffic source.".

Industry observers note that the shopping experience is available today to existing equipifi financial institution partners at no additional cost to enable and comes standard for latest partners at go-live. Banks and credit unions can learn more at equipifi.com.

It has been revealed that about equipifi equipifi is a fintech company providing the credit infrastructure for modern banking. The result is durable customer engagement, sustainable growth, and a more competitive consumer lending experience delivered within the institution's own ecosystem. Learn more at www.equipifi.com.
