Gianluigi Donnarumma’s time at Manchester City could end after just two seasons, with the Italy captain reportedly struggling to win over Enzo Maresca.

It has been revealed that according to The Athletic, via CalcioMercato, City are considering a full overhaul of their goalkeeping department next summer, despite Donnarumma being under contract until 2030. Back-up Gerónimo Rulli could also be moved on.

According to recent reports, donnarumma joined City in 2025, straight after winning the Champions League with Paris Saint-Germain. Pep Guardiola’s departure and Maresca’s arrival have since changed the picture. The report claims the 27-year-old doesn’t fit Maresca’s style and slows down the build-up play the Italian coach wants.

In a recent development, he has also been criticised for errors against PSG, Leipzig and Aston Villa this season, even though he has made some key saves too. Rasmus Højlund’s opener for Napoli in Tuesday’s 2-2 draw squirmed under his glove.

Recent findings suggest that any move would not come cheap. Donnarumma is valued at around €45-50m and earns a reported €12m net per season at the Etihad, which narrows the list of possible suitors.

Both Juventus and Inter have been mentioned. The Bianconeri brought in Guglielmo Vicario from Tottenham this summer, but have long admired the Italy number one. The Nerazzurri, who presently rely on Josep Martínez and Ivan Provedel, have been keeping an eye on several goalkeeping options for the future.
