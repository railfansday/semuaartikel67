Breaking: Draconian Trump Executive Order Could Strip Art Orgs of Nonprofit Status

🌐 Source: Hyperallergic • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (150 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • After the Wall Street Journal reported earlier this month that the White House is considering an executive order that would strip nonprofits deemed to have alleged "terrorist ties" of their tax-exempt status, many arts and culture organizations are bracing for what could be a series of politically targeted attacks from the Trump administration.
  • According to the report, officials are reviewing Section 501(p)(2)(C) of the United States Internal Revenue Code, which authorizes the president to label nonprofits that are allegedly "supporting or engaging in terrorist activity." Organizations tagged with the label would have their tax-exempt status immediately suspended, spelling a potentially devastating blow to their operations.
  • The White House has not made an official confirmation or announcement of the measure, with a spokesperson telling Hyperallergic that "any policy announcements will come directly from the President," and "discussions about potential decisions or their impacts are otherwise baseless speculation.".
