The upcoming video game Star Trek: Voyager – Across the Unknown has let gamers and Star Trek fans explore the dangerous Delta Quadrant, and make life-or-death choices that would make Kathryn Janeway blanch. Now, a new expansion pack is taking the USS Voyager and her crew into the territory of one of the most dangerous alien races in the quadrant, one whose threat is more terrifying than even the Borg: those who run afoul of them need not worry about assimilation, but complete erasure from existence.

It has been revealed that released earlier this year, Star Trek: Voyager – Across the Unknown takes gamers into the scenario presented in the original Star Trek: Voyager TV series: a Starfleet ship on her first mission is catapulted across the galaxy to the distant and uncharted Delta Quadrant of the galaxy, along with a ship full of Maquis rebels. The two crews must unite to chart a course for home, encountering newly introduced friends and enemies along the way. Several DLC bundles, offering new scenarios and characters from some of the show's most popular episodes, have since been released.

It has been revealed that and now, one of the most acclaimed episodes of all, one that was almost an entire season, is coming to Across the Unknown. In a upcoming trailer for Year of Hell, we hear the voice of Voyager pilot Tom Paris (Robert Duncan McNeill, reprising his role from the original series) narrating the scenario: Voyager has been under assault for months by the forces of the alien Krenim and their obsessed commander, Annorax. Janeway thinks victory is possible, but Paris is dubious: he thinks they need more allies, and to push their Starfleet code of ethics like never before. Can you face down the Krenim without being wiped from the history books? You'll have to find out when Year of Hell is released for download on December 9.

It has been revealed that while Voyager remained largely unscathed in its scraps with Delta Quadrant foes like the Kazon, the Vidiians, and the Hirogen. But one two-part episode changed that: "Year of Hell." It pitted them against Annorax (Kurtwood Smith, RoboCop), a warlord from the Krenim race who wished to restore his species to its former glory by wiping its enemies from history with a time-altering weapon. In a months-long campaign against the Krenim, Voyager was battered and bruised, suffering heavy casualties as they assembled a ragtag coalition of the Krenim's foes. In the end, Captain Janeway sacrificed herself by crashing a near-derelict Voyager into Annorax's ship... and the resulting temporal shockwave restored everything back to where it had been before the events of the two-parter.

Sources indicate that the Year of Hell DLC will be available on December 9. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
