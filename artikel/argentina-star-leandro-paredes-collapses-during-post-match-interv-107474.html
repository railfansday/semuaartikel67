Argentina star Leandro Paredes collapsed during a live interview immediately after winning a trophy with his Boca Juniors side.

The 32-year-old was one of the focal points of a World Cup side that reached the final last summer, and went on to win the Copa Sudamericana on Saturday evening after beating Brazil's Atletico Mineiro 2-0 in Barranquilla.

But amid the thick humidity and Colombian heat, Paredes became overwhelmed moments after lifting the trophy.

After giving a brief interview, Paredes was captured closing his eyes, before falling towards the interviewer, who attempted to break his fall.

Instead, the former Paris Saint-Germain midfielder hit the ground, with attendants rushing to help him after his collapse.

Paredes had been taken off in the second-half after suffering from heat-related dizziness, with humidity at 80 per cent heading into the match, and 32-degree-temperatures.

Leandro Parades collapsed to the floor during a post-match interview after winning the Copa Sudamericana with Boca Juniors.

The interviewer attempted to break his fall as the former PSG crumpled to the turf.

'He felt dizzy towards the end of the first-half,' his manager Rodolfo Arruabarrena explained of the substitution. 'He improved a little during the break, but after 10 minutes in the second-half, you could see he was struggling.'.

Fortunately, Paredes was revived promptly and treated in the treatment room, before re-joining his team's celebrations after claiming the South American trophy.

The star shared a brief statement on social media confirming he was fine after the interview aired.

'We're fine,' Paredes confirmed with a picture taken during treatment, before adding in a separate caption: 'Thanks to everyone for the messages, it was just a dizziness from so many turns together'.

Paredes' triumph for his club side comes months after agonising defeat to Spain in New York at the World Cup final in July.

Argentina were felled by a solitary goal from Ferran Torres to end their reign as world champions, before the match descended into petty scraps - with Paredes at the heart of the bad behaviour.

Paredes furiously shoved Spain substitute Gavi to the floor after the final whistle blew, earning himself a punchy 10-match suspension from FIFA.

He faced three assault charges, while teammate Nahuel Molina faced two, and both players were also fined $90,000 (£66,000).

Paredes himself admitted that the length of the ban had put his international future under consideration.

Industry observers note that 'It's going to be difficult for me to stay with the national team,' Paredes told the media in September. 'Because of the suspension as well, because of (Lionel Messi) Leo's decision. It's going to be difficult for everything to carry on as before.

'I haven't discussed it with the coach (Lionel Scaloni) yet, but we'll talk about it and see what decision I make.'.

Paredes' announcement came just days after Messi decided to hang up his international boots, with the talismanic Argentina superstar treated to a magnificent send-off during the early Autumn international break.

'Leo's legacy will live on forever because of what he achieved, for never giving up and for showing that dreams are hard-won, pursued and, in the end, fulfilled,' Paredes added.

Paredes updated fans after the incident to confirm he was okay before rejoining the festivities with his team-mates (Source: Instagram/Leandro Parades).

'We'd spoken in the final days of the World Cup about how the final would surely be his last match, but even so, we weren't prepared for that because of what he means to us.'.

Although unable to compete alongside him in their country's colours for one last time, Paredes joined Messi on the pitch in Buenos Aires for his final farewell, and shared an emotional tribute post to his former captain.

'THANK YOU FOR ABSOLUTELY EVERYTHING CAPTAIN,' he captioned a carousel of photos of himself and Messi.

'It was a dream to be able to have achieved everything we achieved together and to have defended this shirt and our country with you, I'm leaving and we'll miss you always.

'It was a pleasure to be one of your soldiers for all these years and I will be forever! I love you buddy.'.
