The legacy-quel is alive and well, and it only makes sense that a sequel/reboot/reimagining would eventually come for “Home Alone,” Chris Columbus’ 1990 holiday classic starring Macaulay Culkin.

There was buzz that Culkin over the summer pitched an idea to Disney as to where a grown Kevin McAllister would be today after enduring two child abandonments and harassment and attempted murder from bumbling bandits (our interpretation, not necessarily his read on Kevin’s childhood shenanigans and trauma). Disney now seems to have warmed to the idea like a cup of hot cocoa, especially thanks to the two additional names who are working with Culkin on the project: Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg. Disney’s 20th Century Studios is in early development on a sequel to “Home Alone” that would star Culkin reprising his original role and in which Rogen and Goldberg on behalf of their Point Grey banner would write and produce the film, working with Culkin on his idea, IndieWire has learned.

Things are very, very early days, and any plot details are being kept under wraps for now, and no director is attached at the moment.

In a recent development, chris Columbus directed the original “Home Alone” and its nearly-as-loved sequel “Home Alone 2: Lost in Upcoming York,” and both written by the late great John Hughes. If you can believe it, there have been six “Home Alone” movies in all, with the third one at least released in theaters and doing respectable box office numbers. The others are all TV movies or in the case of the most recent 2021 film “Home Sweet Home Alone,” a Disney+ streaming movie.

The original two films though grossed $477 million and $358 million worldwide, respectively, and the original still gets tons of cable play and finds its way into the Nielsen streaming charts. It makes sense that a more serious sequel with Culkin back in the fold would be welcomed by audiences.

Sadly, “Home Alone” has been back on the minds of audiences of late after the recent passing of Catherine O’Hara, who played Kevin’s mom, and also Tim Curry, who had a small role as a hotel clerk in “Home Alone 2.” Joe Pesci and Daniel Stern as the Wet Bandits are still around, though Pesci has been mostly retired outside of his return in Scorsese’s “The Irishman” back in 2019 and his role in the Pete Davidson Peacock series “Bupkis” in 2023.

Culkin too has had a bit of a resurgence, making a cameo in the most recent season of “Fallout” and doing a voice in Disney’s “Zootopia 2.”.
