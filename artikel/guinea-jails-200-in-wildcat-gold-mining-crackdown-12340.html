Guinea jails 200 in wildcat gold mining crackdown

🌐 Source: Dw • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (40 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A Conakry court has jailed 22 former local officials from the gold-rich northeastern Siguiri region, as well as 200 people accused of illegal mining. It's part of a wider crackdown that sent thousands fleeing into Mali.
