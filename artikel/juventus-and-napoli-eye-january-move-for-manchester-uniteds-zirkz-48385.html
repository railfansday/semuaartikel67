Juventus and Napoli are both considering a January move for Manchester United striker Joshua Zirkzee.

Recent findings suggest that according to Fabrizio Romano, via IlNapolista, the Dutchman could be heading back to Serie A two and a half years after United signed him from Bologna for more than €40m. He hasn’t lived up to that price tag at Old Trafford, scoring only twice in 10 appearances this season.

Industry observers note that several Italian clubs have chased Zirkzee since he left Bologna. Roma tried to sign him last January, and Fiorentina and Juventus were interested in the summer.

It has been revealed that the Bianconeri have a key advantage in chief football officer Frederic Massara, who has wanted Zirkzee for a long time. He tried to sign him at Milan and at Roma, and again for Juventus in August. Juventus are looking for a latest striker, and Zirkzee could become a realistic option if United agree a favourable deal.

Napoli have a good relationship with Zirkzee’s representatives, and he could be on their list if Lorenzo Lucca leaves in January, with Bologna and Genoa considering a loan for the Italian.

United’s own situation has also changed after Mauricio Pochettino replaced Michael Carrick last week.
