According to recent reports, black Friday 2026 is here, and PlayStation is offering big discounts on games and gaming hardware for the big holiday shopping event. There are plenty of digital game deals at Sony's PlayStation Store as well as physical games at retailers like Amazon. PlayStation's official Black Friday hardware deals also include PlayStation 5 hardware, including the DualSense controller, PlayStation VR2, and Pulse audio accessories.

Recent findings suggest that newly introduced PlayStation 5 consoles aren't getting an official discount from Sony, which is disappointinggiven its recent price hikes. If you initiative on buying directly from PlayStation, this Black Friday seems like a good time to stock up on additional controllers if you right now only have one DualSense. If you're still intrigued by the PlayStation VR2 despite its lack of first-party support, that deal isn't too shabby either.

First-party games like Marvel's Wolverine, Saros, Ghost of Yotei Complete Edition, and Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls will also get discounted at the same time as those peripherals, which will be at 12 a.m. in your local time on Nov. 16. The deals are then expected to be live through Nov. 30 or as long as the items are in stock. It's also worth keeping an eye on other retailers like Walmart, as they may have some additional Black Friday deals on top of the ones Sony is setting for PlayStation.
