Mauricio Pochettino said he is not concerned by Manchester United’s running stats this season as he prepares to take charge of his first game at Liverpool on Sunday, as he described his team of coaches as “animals of the grass”.

Pochettino developed a reputation as a purveyor of high-intensity football during his previous spell in the Premier League at Tottenham, but United rank third-bottom when it comes to distance covered so far this season.

But Pochettino dismissed concerns that his players are not fit enough to adapt to his high-pressing style and argued that is “dangerous” to take United’s running stats under Michael Carrick without the proper context.

“It is a debate that I don't like to go in because I think we cannot talk about things without context, and data is about context,” stated Pochettino, who revealed that he will ask his players to be “brave” during his first press conference.

In a recent development, "If you don't put context on the data, I think it's very dangerous to talk, and I think that it's easy to manipulate the data. It’s true that, and I need to be honest, sometimes data shows something that you can then see on the field.

Recent findings suggest that “We are going to be very focused and try to improve our data, our numbers, in every single area of the field. And of course, we are here not to denounce things. We are here to build something with our players and with the club, and try to perform and win games.”.

Pochettino stated he and his team, including assistant Jesus Perez, are better coaches now than they were earlier in their careers and underlined the importance of their working on the training ground.

“I think we are animals of the grass,” he mentioned. “We like to feel the grass. We need to smell the grass and try to help the players improve every day. It’s true that it’s a different job when you are with the national team, but at the same time, you learn a lot. And I think we are much better coaches now, much better coaches.”.
