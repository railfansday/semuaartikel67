ArXiv’s cap of two submissions per researcher per month is a defensible shield for the preprint platform’s voluntary moderators (see Nature 658, 854; 2026). However, treating slop produced by artificial-intelligence tools as a moderation-workload concern understates the problem. As the article notes, papers turned away from one platform simply resurface on another website.
