Kyle Richards has shared adorable snaps from her daughter Farrah Aldjufrie's birth, during which she welcomed a baby boy named Brooks Steele Goldsmith.

The 37-year-old property mogul, who is the daughter of Richards and her ex-husband, Guraish Aldjufrie, shared the news in July that she was expecting her first child.

Then, one month later, she hard launched her relationship with Goldsmith while sharing a video of the two of them celebrating their gender reveal.

In a recent development, on Nov. 15, it was revealed that the TV alum welcomed her son.

Now, Richards is showing off snaps of her daughter in the hospital and opening up about why she was in full glam when she met up with her family.

In the photos, Richards could be seen donning an all-purple ensemble, which she also sported to the Maison D'OR Cocktails.

Goldsmith also opened up to People about the special day, revealing, “The birth was extremely easy, peaceful, seamless and happy. We had a party at the hospital and watched the sun come up after he was born. The transition has felt very natural. The house has been crowded with family giving so much love and support. We feel beyond lucky.”.

The upcoming dad added, "Brooks is an old English name, and I’m English, so it ties to my roots. My oldest son Adler wanted to name him River, so we took a variation of that which refers to a harmonious, running body of water which flows and connects life.”.

Sources indicate that page Six previously revealed that while Richards was attending the Maison D’Or cocktail launch in Los Angeles over the weekend, she reportedly told the outlet, “I’m on baby watch right now. I can’t believe [I’m gonna be a grandma]. I hope it’s not tonight, because am I gonna go to the hospital like this?”.

Then, according to the outlet, someone later asked Richards if Aldjufrie was in labor, to which she replied, "I think so.".

Meanwhile, Richards' sister, Kathy Hilton, told Page Six that she arrived to the event late because she was on the phone with the hospital.

“I’ve been calling to make sure this is not a false alarm because I’m gonna call the hospital to make sure that she gets right in and has a room because, you know, we’re very close to the hospital,” Hilton told the outlet.

Just days earlier, Richards' other daughter, Alexia, whom she shares with ex Mauricio Umansky, spoke with Page Six's Virtual Reali-Tea to reveal the family's holidays looks as they add a newly introduced addition.

"Well, typically, we always go to Aspen for the holidays. We spend Christmas and Upcoming Year's there. Even at our most times of difficulty, we always made sure that the family was together for the holidays. So the holidays are a special time for us," she said.

It has been revealed that "We will not be in Aspen this year because my sister is giving birth any day now. So we're not going to make her get on a plane and fly to Aspen," Alexia added.

Aldjufrie previously revealed that she was expecting a baby boy in an adorable video.

"Zach Goldsmith and I are having a..." she wrote in the caption of the clip, in which the happy couple were seen embracing each other as they discovered they are having a baby boy.

"Real Housewives of Beverly Hills" star Richards and her estranged spouse, The Agency founder Mauricio—who has helped to raise Farrah since she was a young girl—were also in attendance for the special occasion.

Aldjufrie's half-sisters Alexia Umansky, Portia Umansky, and Sophia Umansky could also be seen celebrating when their unborn nephew's gender was confirmed.

Sources indicate that it is unclear when Aldjufrie and Goldsmith began dating; however, the two appeared on the Netflix series, which followed Umansky's real estate business, together from November 2022 to March 2024. Aldjufrie ended an engagement to her former partner, Alex Manos, in March 2024.

Aldjufrie and Goldsmith still work together as real estate agents for Umansky's firm, The Agency, with the company's website listing both as active members of the team.

According to Goldsmith's bio, he focuses his efforts primarily on luxury real estate in Beverly Hills, where he is hailed as boasting "an array of nuanced market knowledge.".

"With a philosophy of building bridges (not burning them), he has become a nationally recognized figure and an industry trendsetter," the bio goes on.

The news of their baby's gender came after Farrah revealed she was expecting.

She shared the news on social media on July 3, posting a series of photos that showcased her growing baby bump alongside ultrasound images.

In the caption, Farrah described the baby news as her "most exciting launch yet," revealing that she is due to welcome her little one in late November.

Industry observers note that "My most exciting launch yet. Coming soon late November 2026," she captioned the image.

Her announcement was met with great excitement from her mother, who will become a grandmother for the first time when Aldjufrie gives birth.
