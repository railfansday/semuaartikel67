Sky and Peacock have offered the first look at The Day Of The Jackal Season 2.

Industry observers note that the teaser trailer features Eddie Redmayne as The Jackal, an internationally renowned assassin, standing at the top of the mountain and leaping into the unknown. Watch below.

“The man known as The Jackal has our attention,” a voice can be heard saying. The series premieres in March 2027.

The Day Of The Jackal Season 1 became Sky’s biggest-ever new series launch, drawing a UK audience of 4.6 million in its first 28 days, with the debut episode going on to reach 7M viewers. The thriller also became Peacock’s most-watched new original drama series ever after 75 days of viewership, according to NBCUniversal. It has been revealed that returning cast members include Úrsula Corberó (Money Heist), Charles Dance (Game of Thrones,), Chukwudi Iwuji (Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3), Eleanor Matsuura (The Walking Dead), Adam James (Mr Bates vs The Post Office), Christy Meyer (The Amateur), and Corey Johnson (The Bourne Ultimatum). Watch on Deadline.

Joining the cast are Pablo Schreiber (Halo), Weruche Opia (Genius MLK/X), Matt Bomer (White Collar), and Pia Tjelta (Jo Nesbø’s Detective Hole).

Based on Frederick Forsyth’s novel and the 1973 Universal Pictures film adaptation, The Day of the Jackal is produced by Carnival Films, part of Universal Global Television, a division of Universal Studio Group.

Recent findings suggest that the lead writer for Season 2 is David Harrower (Lockerbie: A Search for Truth) and the lead director is Brian Kirk (Furious). Executive producers are Gareth Neame, Nigel Marchant, Marianne Buckland (Carnival Films), Eddie Redmayne, David Harrower, Brian Kirk, Sam Hoyle (Sky), Sue Naegle and Ronan Bennett. Lis Steele serves as series producer.

Filming took place in Hungary, Croatia, Spain, Belgium, Austria and the UK.
