Russia targeted bridges in Kyiv and railway stations overnight in a major attack rolled out days after Ukraine badly damaged the strategically notable Crimean Bridge.

Ballistic missiles hit key crossings in the capital during a nationwide assault which Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said was "one of the most massive strikes" of the war so far and left at least nine dead.

Russian President Vladimir Putin had threatened to retaliate for the attack on the Crimean Bridge, which the Kremlin built to link Russia and the occupied peninsula.

The latest Russian strikes coincided with a flurry of talks around the war, and came as the Kremlin pushed back against a US claim that Russia could be open to Western-backed security guarantees for Ukraine.

The BBC's Sarah Rainsford is in Kyiv after the night of strikes.
