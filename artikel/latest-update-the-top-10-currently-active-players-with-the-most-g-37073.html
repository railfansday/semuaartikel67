With Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo plying their trade outside Europe these days, who are the players with the most goals to their names still active in Europe’s top five leagues?

It has been revealed that there’s some serious goalscoring talent in the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1. The current top 10 features a mix of world-class stars right now and some who peaked a decade ago but are still plugging away.

Here are the top 10 players at the moment active in Europe’s top five leagues with the most goals in those leagues.

Villarreal captain Moreno has spent his entire career in Spain, playing in La Liga since 2014 after scoring 46 goals in the third tier and 15 in the second tier.

He had a three-year spell with Espanyol but went back to Villarreal in 2018 and is now just three goals away from a century of La Liga goals in their colours alone.

Just ahead of Domenico Berardi and Paulo Dybala as the top active scorer in Serie A is Inter captain Martinez.

It has been revealed that inter brought him to Europe from Racing Club in his native Argentina in 2018 and neither party has looked back since. Martinez was Serie A’s top scorer in 2023-24 and 2025-26.

Kramaric’s first taste of playing in one of Europe’s top five leagues was when he came to the Premier League with Leicester, where he scored a grand total of two goals.

According to recent reports, it’s gone better for him in the Bundesliga with Hoffenheim ever since. He joined the German side in 2016 and has reached double figures of goals in nine seasons.

Another one from the category of: not much to write home about in the Premier League, but actually pretty good elsewhere.

It has been revealed that aspas arrived at Liverpool on the back of his debut season in La Liga, when he scored 12 goals for Celta de Vigo. He never scored in the Premier League, before scoring twice back in La Liga on loan at Sevilla and settling permanently with Celta again, going on to score more than 150 more times in the Spanish top flight.

Yep, not only is he still active in Europe’s top five leagues, he’s playing in the Champions League too at the ripe age of 40.

Recent findings suggest that giroud’s goals have been spread between spells in Ligue 1, the Premier League and Serie A. After a stint in MLS, he came back to Europe in 2025 with Lille, taking his chance to add to his 33 previous Ligue 1 goals (which he got with Montpellier before his Arsenal move).

Give him a couple of years and he’ll be in the top three, you’d imagine.

Haaland is quite simply a goalscoring machine and has been ever since he first featured in one of Europe’s top five leagues, when he went to Borussia Dortmund in 2020.

After his 62 Bundesliga goals, he’s gone on to become a member of the Premier League’s 100 Club while starring for Manchester City.

Back in March 2018, Dzeko became the first player ever to score 50 goals in three of Europe’s top five leagues.

Industry observers note that he’d already done it in the Bundesliga with Wolfsburg and the Premier League with Manchester City, before reaching the milestone in Serie A with Roma as well. He later surpassed 100 in the Italian top flight while with Inter.

Industry observers note that still playing at 40, he’s part of Schalke’s squad now, but after helping them win promotion earlier this year, he hasn’t scored for them this season yet. In fact, his last goal in one of Europe’s top five leagues was actually in May 2023.

Sources indicate that aubameyang has also since joined the exclusive group of half-centurions from three of the top five leagues, doing so in the Bundesliga, Premier League and Ligue 1. He was at Marseille in the 2023-24 season when he ticked off the last of the three.

He’ll have his work cut out to reach the milestone in La Liga as well, where he now plies his trade with Deportivo, but he’s having a fine season at the age of 37.

He’s scored five goals so far in the Spanish top flight this season, having previously scored 11 in it for Barcelona.

No surprises who’s in the top two – and it’s in the same order as for the 2026 Ballon d’Or.

Recent findings suggest that mbappe is one of the best players in the world right now and he’s been doing it for ages. Sure, he’s not won anything of note at Real Madrid yet, but you can’t argue with his goalscoring rates.

He claimed the Pichichi award as La Liga’s top scorer in both of his first two seasons in Spain. Before that, just the 191 goals in Ligue 1 with Monaco and then PSG, making him the sixth highest scorer in the competition’s history and the best this side of 2000.

But like with the Ballon d’Or, it’s Kane in top spot. He’s had a few more years to get the goals than Mbappe, to be fair, but his consistency has been incredible.

When he first emerged in the Tottenham first team, some feared he was a one season wonder. It’s been an awfully long season if that’s the case.

Recent findings suggest that he eventually left Spurs on 213 Premier League goals, more than anyone else except Alan Shearer. Rather than pursue that record, he chose to pursue trophies with Bayern Munich instead, casually reaching 100 Bundesliga goals within 98 games – 31 fewer than the next quickest player to reach the milestone.

Kane’s recent contract extension means there’ll be plenty more goals to come.
