It's been 10 years since Quantum Break made its debut as an Xbox console exclusive in 2016, and Xbox fans have often considered it somewhat of an underated cult classic from the developer behind the likes of Control and Alan Wake.

So, could we get a sequel?! Remedy Entertainment's X account has been talking about this over the last 24 hours, highlighting that the team love how much passion fans still have for the IP, but that the decision is in Xbox's hands.

In other words, it's down to Asha Sharma and her group at Xbox HQ.

Recent findings suggest that now, to be honest, we don't reckon it's a likely bet. The original Quantum Break was a really cool exclusive for Xbox to have 10 years ago, but it seems like the new Xbox CEO is more interested in focusing on substantial franchises like Gears, Halo, Fallout and The Elder Scrolls rather than dipping back into something from a difficult period in Xbox's history.

That's not to say it couldn't happen though, and in some ways you'd think it'd be an easy win for Xbox to cater to nostalgia and remind everyone how much they enjoyed the original Quantum Break. Remedy has made sequels to Alan Wake and Control in recent times as well, so they'd surely be up for revisiting QB if given the opportunity.
