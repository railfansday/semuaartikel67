Tech Report: Robert Pattinson cast as next James Bond

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that robert Pattinson has been cast as the next James Bond in the upcoming film from director Denis Villeneuve. It will be the 26th James Bond film and the first since Amazon MGM took over the iconic franchise.

Recent findings suggest that pattinson will be the seventh actor to play the role. Monday's announcement — which came in part via an Instagram post — ends a long period of speculation about who would take over as "007" after Daniel Craig.

"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson stated in a statement. "I couldn't be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.".

Industry observers note that pattinson has appeared in several significant-ticket films this year, including Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" and psychological thriller "Primetime," and he'll soon appear in "Dune: Part Three," also directed by Villeneuve. Pattinson, who rose to fame with the Twilight movie series, is also in production for Matt Reeves' "The Batman Part II," a follow-up to the 2022 film based on the DC comic.

The upcoming Bond film will have a script by Steven Knight and will be produced by Amy Pascal, known for her work on the Spider-Man franchise, and David Heyman, who produced all eight Harry Potter films.

"We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007," Villeneuve stated. "I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our fresh James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.".

Industry observers note that in 2022, Amazon bought MGM Studios, which had long produced the Bond movies, for roughly $8.45 billion. Amazon MGM then acquired creative control of the James Bond intellectual property rights in 2025, when it closed a deal with longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson.

The film is still untitled, and Amazon MGM hasn't announced a projected release date.
