Grand Theft Auto 6 is everywhere you look. Every news channel is talking about it. Celebrities and musicians are playing GTA 6 or posting about its release. It’s apparently the biggest game ever and has sold nearly 40 million copies already. But if you aren’t a big gamer or not a video game fan at all, you might have questions. I have answers.

Perhaps someone sent you this link because you kept asking them GTA 6 questions. Maybe you did a Google search, made it past the AI results and found this blog explaining what GTA 6 is to people who don’t buy or play games. Whatever the case, welcome. I’m going to walk you through what is almost certainly the biggest video game launch of the decade, why people are going so wild, and what the game actually is and isn’t.

Recent findings suggest that it is an open-world game that mixes together a lot of video game genres, like driving, shooting, and exploration. As with most open-world games, you can run around freely or do story missions. It’s up to the player. You control two characters, Jason and Lucia, who are criminals on the run in fictional versions of Florida and Miami named Leonida and Vice City, respectively.

In a recent development, more or less, yeah. The developer, Rockstar Games, has been working on this game since at least 2015 or so. Turns out creating a photorealistic recreation of a chunk of Florida that players can explore and do whatever they want in takes time!

Industry observers note that it took me about 90 hours to reach the end of the main story. I’ve since played over 150 hours, and I’m still finding newly introduced stuff, hearing new songs, and seeing new random events. Yeah, it’s big.

Well, a lot of people have been waiting for 13 years to play GTA 6. The last newly introduced GTA game, GTA V, was released back in 2013. So anticipation for this game has been building for a long time. And, surprisingly, GTA 6 lived up to the hype.

Sources indicate that there are a few reasons for the glowing critical reception. The game’s narrative is really good, featuring two extremely well-written characters that develop a meaningful relationship over the course of its five chapters. Another big reason for the high review scores is just how expansive and reactive the world is. Characters in the game will comment on your clothes, respond to your actions, and remember things you did hours and hours ago. It’s also a lot of fun to play, with challenging but rewarding driving, combat, and police encounters. And it looks incredible, with some of the best graphics ever seen in a console game. Some critics are calling it the best game of the year. And many players are calling it one of the best games ever.

GTA: Vice City on the PS2 was much, much smaller and less advanced (though in some ways it was pretty advanced for the time) and was set in the 1980s. While Vice City is a major location in GTA 6, this is an unconnected and different version of the city from that seen in the original 2002 PS2 game.

You can also just play through the game’s main story, which is all about Jason and Lucia trying to make some money as criminals to build a life for themselves and their son.

Industry observers note that yes, Jason and Lucia have a child, a fact which was omitted from any pre-release marketing but is revealed in the first 20 minutes of the game.
