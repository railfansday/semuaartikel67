By Daniel Holzman and Michael Chernow.

Upcoming York City chefs Daniel Holzman and Michael Chernow are gleefully willing to turn almost anything into a meatball (they were the owners of the now-closed Meatball Shop and are the authors of a cookbook by the same name), including everything they love about Thanksgiving. These inspired, playfully named meatballs bring together all the flavors of the holiday in one tidy little savory bundle: ground turkey, garlicky stuffing, tart dried cranberries, and a generous dose of autumnal spices. They’re particularly tasty topped with gravy.
