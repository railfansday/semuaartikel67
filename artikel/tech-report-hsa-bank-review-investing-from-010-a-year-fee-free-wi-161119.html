Recent findings suggest that open an HSA Bank health savings account if you’ll invest the money and want to choose how much help you pay for. There’s no monthly fee, and investing costs 0.10% a year on your own, 0.25% for a recommended fund list, or 0.35% for a managed portfolio, a fee waived any quarter your cash balance averages $7,500 or more.

According to recent reports, HSA Bank, a division of Santander Bank, lets you manage your HSA’s cash and investments in one online account and app. It suits anyone with an HSA-eligible health plan who’ll invest for years to make the most of an HSA’s triple tax break.

Sources indicate that savers who keep a cash balance averaging $7,500 or more pay no investing fee, even for a managed portfolio. Savers who’d rather invest every dollar on their own should open a Fidelity HSA, which charges no account fees and no commission on U.S. stock and ETF trades.
