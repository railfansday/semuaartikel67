A pregnant inmate has escaped from custody in the US state of New Mexico, after taking control of a police vehicle while returning to prison from a prenatal appointment, the state's corrections department has stated.

Rebecca Taylor, 26, took control of the Dodge Journey and drove away when the team transporting her and another inmate stopped to pick up food in Albuquerque, local media reported.

The Latest Mexico Corrections Department noted that the vehicle and her prison jumpsuit were later recovered, but Taylor remains on the run.

She had been imprisoned for multiple theft and burglary charges, the department remarked in a Facebook statement.

Local media reported that another inmate, also travelling back from a prenatal appointment, was able to get out of the car as Taylor took control on Wednesday morning.

In a recent development, the corrections department has said that a multi-agency search for Taylor is under way. The BBC has approached it for an enhancement.

In a recent development, the Albuquerque Journal reported that Taylor is currently in her second trimester of pregnancy. For this reason, she was not restrained during transportation, department spokesperson Brittany Roembach said.

"It is the understanding right now that one officer was with the vehicle but not inside of the vehicle when this occurred," she was quoted as saying.

All prisons in New Mexico went into lockdown following Taylor's escape, but it was lifted at 06:00 on Thursday.

However the prison where she was being held, the Western Newly introduced Mexico Corrections Facility, will remain on "modified movement", and no visitors are presently allowed.

The public has been told not to approach Rebecca Taylor if they see her, and call police straight away.
