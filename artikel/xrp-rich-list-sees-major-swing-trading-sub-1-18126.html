Individuals ranking at the creme-de-la-creme spot of the XRP rich-list have upped the ante for newcomers.

In a recent development, earning the top-tier wealth rankings on the XRP Ledger has become way easier since the native coin’s price dipped sub-$1. November’s XRP chain data registered 8.3 million funded accounts, highlighting a skewed distribution favoring smaller-type holdings, pushing the limits way lower.

Recent findings suggest that the present XRP rich-list thresholds are as follows: 2,151 XRP for top 10%, 7,503 XRP for top 5%, 44,896 XRP for top 1%, and 275,420 XRP for top 0.1%, according to the latest blockchain explorer statistics. The entrance level got considerably lower, while the top 3% topped up more.

Sources indicate that at the high-end of the XRP rich-list ranking, entering the top 0.01% requires an account balance of at least 3,836,116 XRP. The graphic estimates that approximately 804 accounts hold enough XRP to meet the threshold. Those figures represent accounts rather than unique individuals.

The snapshot of XRP’s account distribution matters due to the rotation of top whale holdings. While the broader crypto whales, otherwise known as large investors, have showcased multiple signs of distribution, the millionaire holders have vouched the possibility of buying the dip.

Recent findings suggest that as reported by DailyCoin earlier this month, XRP hit a density high XRP’s in crypto millionaire concentration, the highest count since last December, implying monumental long-term price  conviction among the largest XRP HODLers. But the smaller players have acted differently.

According to recent reports, the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) has hit a stupendously low degree of -0.47, the lowest point since February 5, 2026. Back then XRP’s price was still trading above $1.51, but the sell-off spree had taken the veteran altcoin towards a local bottom of $1.05 in a brief moment of time.

Right now, XRP’s price stands at the mid-point territory of the 24-hour range. The OG altcoin’s trading at $0.953, bouncing off the lower intra-day channel boundary at $0.9414. Whipping up a rally above $1.10 will be crucial as the long versus short ratio keeps slightly bullish above 1.
