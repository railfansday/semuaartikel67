Revision [Mon 23rd Nov 2026, 2:20pm]: Nvidia has posted an update confirming that Jensen Huang has now been reinstated as president, CEO, and board member following his earlier resignation.

Sources indicate that the post confirms that Nvidia's board "received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk".

Huang resigned following multiple instances of alleged infidelity, most notably with an alleged Chinese spy, leading to concerns over national security as the US and China butt heads in the race to become the dominant force in the world of AI.

Huang also contributed the following message.

According to recent reports, “I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a latest industrial revolution. I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as Chair, and to Colette Kress and our executive staff for leading NVIDIA during this period.

"I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can’t wait to get back to work.”.

We can only assume that the investigation into Huang's relationships turned up nothing unusual – surely they wouldn't just sweep it under the rug, right?

Meanwhile, US president Donald Trump celebrated Huang's reinstatement, calling him a "Great Patriot and National Treasure" while stating that the decision is vital to "ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Tech giant, but the American Economy".

Original Story: Nvidia, one of the largest tech firms in the world and the company responsible for the Switch 2's custom processor, has found itself embroiled in scandal over recent days.

As reported by The Guardian, Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang, who you might recognise from the 'Creator's Voice Special Edition' video for Switch 2, has now resigned after reported affairs with an alleged Chinese spy and a firm employee.

He will be replaced on an interim basis by Nvidia’s chief financial officer, Colette Kress, until a full-time successor is named. Nvidia's share price began to sharply decline following reports of the scandal, to the point where it is now no longer the most valuable organization in the world.

The BBC speculates that Huang's departure could set him up to be a "formidable opponent" to the very manufacturer he co-founded, with Elon Musk stating that it would be "an honour" to work with him at SpaceX.

In a recent development, with regards to Nintendo, it's likely that none of this will have any immediate impact on Nvidia's relationship with the Japanese company. Nvidia will no doubt be under contract to supply custom chips for the Switch 2, but what happens with the inevitable Switch 2 successor is under question.

Hopefully everything will go smoothly for Nintendo, but if it wants to utilise Nvidia's architecture for its next console – particularly for backwards compatibility – then its efforts may be impacted by Huang's departure along with Nvidia's sharp pivot towards AI.

Of course, it's really early to be speculating about the 'Switch 3', but you can bet that Nintendo's R&D department will be looking at all of this very closely and considering its potential impact.
