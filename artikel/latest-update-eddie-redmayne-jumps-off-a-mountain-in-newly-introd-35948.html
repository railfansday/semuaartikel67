Eddie Redmayne returns as the elusive assassin in a upcoming teaser for “The Day of the Jackal” Season 2.

“He’s meticulous. Careful. He’s a creature of instinct,” a disembodied voice (not Redmayne’s) intones over footage of the actor standing on the edge of a mountain. “He has been operating for a long time. Redmayne then rolls out himself off the side, with just a skydiving backpack to save him.

The second season of the globe-trotting thriller, which is based on Frederick Forsyth’s novel and the 1973 Universal Pictures film adaptation, is set to launch on Sky and Peacock in March 2027.

Redmayne earned Golden Globe, Critics’ Choice and SAG Award nominations for the first season, which premiered in 2024.

Also returning for the second season are Úrsula Corberó (“Money Heist”), Charles Dance (“Game of Thrones”), Chukwudi Iwuji (“Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3”), Eleanor Matsuura (“The Walking Dead”), Adam James (“Mr Bates vs The Post Office”), Christy Meyer (“The Amateur”) and Corey Johnson (“The Bourne Ultimatum”).

Not returning is Lashana Lynch as Bianca, after she comes to a sticky end in Season 1.

Redmayne will instead be joined by newcomers Pablo Schreiber (“Halo”), Weruche Opia (“The Jetty”), Matt Bomer (“White Collar”) and Pia Tjelta (“Jo Nesbø’s Detective Hole”.

“The Day of the Jackal” is produced by Carnival Films (“Downton Abbey”), part of Universal Global Television, a division of Universal Studio Group.

David Harrower (“Lockerbie: A Search for Truth”) is the lead writer for the upcoming season, with Brian Kirk (“Furious”) directing.

Sources indicate that exec producers are Gareth Neame, Nigel Marchant, Marianne Buckland (Carnival Films), Eddie Redmayne, David Harrower, Brian Kirk, Sam Hoyle (Sky), Sue Naegle and Ronan Bennett. Lis Steele is series producer.
