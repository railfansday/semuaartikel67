Now These Apple Services Are Down

If you're having trouble accessing certain Apple products and services today, you are not alone. As of early Tuesday afternoon, Downdetector shows user reports of issues across multiple Apple services. While Apple's System Status page initially showed far fewer issues than Downdetector people reported, the site has since updated to reflect general outages.

In a recent development, according to Apple, the disruptions aren't quite as widespread: Apple states that the App Store, Apple Music, AppleCare on Devices, and Schoolwork are all experiencing outages. Apple says that the App Store and Apple Music "may be slow or unavailable," while AppleCare purchase is right now unavailable. The manufacturer's System Status page shows an "issue" with iCloud Account & Sign In. Other services with user reports on Downdetector, including the Apple Card, Apple Online Store, and Apple Business, read as "Available." Apple also says there was an outage with the Volume Purchase Program, but that has since been resolved.

Recent findings suggest that it's not clear what's causing the issues with the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud Account & Sign In, or other Apple services at this time. However, the timing is odd. It comes after a number of banks experienced outages on Monday, as well as Microsoft's massive outage the previous week. In addition to all of these outages, the FAA unexpectedly grounded all U.S. flights on Friday, for only the third time in the agency's history.
