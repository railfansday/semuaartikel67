Mikel Arteta has confirmed he has taken “measures” that Arsenal players from selecting themselves or their team-mates on the Premier League’s Fantasy Premier League (FPL) game, in a bid to prevent his side’s line-ups and team news from being leaked.

It comes after eagle-eyed players of FPL, which has over 11 million total users worldwide, spotted that Arsenal winger Christos Tzolis had selected himself to play against Liverpool earlier this month when he had been previously reported as an injury doubt.

Tzolis selecting himself in his line-up revealed that the winger was fit to start, however, and could have alerted Liverpool that he was ready to return to action and revealed crucial team news before the match.

Chelsea’s Joao Pedro was previously revealed to be sidelined for their game against Tottenham after FPL players noticed that Chelsea staff had removed the club’s top scorer from their line-ups.

Arteta has now taken steps to ensure that his players cannot select anyone from Arsenal in their FPL teams, and even admitted that he is not sharing any team news with his children in case their teams were leaked.

"With Christos, with the squad, ‌with my kids as well, ‌they are constantly asking me about the line-ups as well because they want to know," Arteta said before his side travel to face Newcastle on Saturday.

"The confidentiality is something critical because it's about trust and what you're trying to do and that should be kept as a huge secret. And we will take measures to make sure that doesn't happen.”.

Industry observers note that according to FPL tracking websites, Greek international winger Tzolis’s team is called ‘Tzatziki FC'. Screenshots of the teams line-up showed Tzolis had transferred out himself, Ezri Konsa and Riccardo Calafiori from his side before the Gunners beat Hull 1-0 last weekend.
