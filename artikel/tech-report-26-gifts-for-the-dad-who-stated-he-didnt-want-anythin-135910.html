Recent findings suggest that picture this. You’re going through the grueling process that is gift shopping. You spend hours, days (but let’s be honest, not weeks because you left this for the last minute) mindlessly walking through stores, reading online gift guides, trying to find the perfect gift for every single person in your life. You check everyone off your list, one by one, until you get to the dreaded name: Dad.

Industry observers note that you ask your dad what he wants and you hear the famous words “I don’t want anything,” the least helpful words in the English language. You ask your mom for help and she tells you to get him socks. You rack your brain trying to remember what he likes, what his interests are and you’re drawing blanks. Your throat is closing up, and you start breaking out in hives as the last store is about to close the night before the holiday. You reach out and, in defeat, pick up tube socks before you black out.

If this scene feels too familiar, then you’re going to want to read this roundup. Whether your dad is up to date on all the latest tech, whether he’s a golf enthusiast or not, we’ve rounded up the 26 best gifts for the old man who taught us everything we know.

Whether you wanna pull an uno reverse card and be the one to get him a bike for a change, the comfiest loafers he’ll ever break in, or an espresso machine that will make you even more excited for your next visit, we’ve got you covered on all the things your dad actually wants, but won’t tell you.

The biblically accurate dad shoe (complimentary). He’ll look great running his errands, walking to the coffee shop, and doing any other dad-activity you can think of while also being comfortable.

Remember when you were a kid, and you’d go back to school after the holiday break sporting all your latest clothes, basking in all the glory after getting compliment after compliment? That’s how your dad will feel strolling into the office with this leather briefcase from Carl Friedrik.

Your dad has a lot of notable shit to sign, letters to write, etc. Make an otherwise boring task more fun with this fountain pen by Moleskine.

In a recent development, your dad was more than likely the person that gave you your first-ever bike and taught you how to ride it. Return the favor this time by getting him a bike of his own. It’s the perfect gift for him to get some exercise while he runs out to do errands, goes for a stroll in town, or just plays with the other dads in the cul-de-sac. Just don’t forget a helmet.

I’ll admit, in moments of desperation and not knowing what to get my own dad as a gift, I have gotten him a boring wallet more times than I can count. This wallet is not that. This bifold from Gucci features a luxe grainy leather and silver foil logo detailing that elevates what can otherwise be a boring gift. It’s a splurge that he wouldn’t make himself, and that’s what makes it special.

That’s how great they are.

Colognes are usually tricky to gift someone if you don’t know their preferred notes or fragrances. This option by Cartier is the perfect daytime scent—it has hints of citrus and cedarwood that make it a crowd-pleaser for just about everyone.

If your dad loves a good drink at home, get him this set to make the whole experience feel more luxe. The kit will look like a statement piece on his bar cart while he entertains and makes his world famous Scotch on the rocks.

He can manually pull his own espresso, or use the touchscreen to make his favorite drinks. It also has the added benefit of doubling as a present to yourself for the next time you stay over at dad’s for the holidays.

So he has a fancy fresh coffee machine, now he needs the best beans for the maiden coffee voyage. This subscription features coffee from all over the world and allows you to select either grounds, beans or pods - making it an easy gift for almost anyone.

Sources indicate that don’t get him just another boring polo. This sweater polo is a great level-up of the classic polo by the brand your dad knows and loves.
