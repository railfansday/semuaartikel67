Vinicius Junior has admitted that Arsenal’s push to sign him in the summer has impacted his form this season.

The Gunners held strong interest in him as they looked to kick on from their Premier League title win and make a marquee signing in attack.

Vinicius had entered the final 12 months of his contract at Real Madrid in the summer and there was strong speculation that he could leave the club.

With Real and Vinicius at a contract impasse and little progress made in negotiations, Arsenal made it clear they would meet the winger’s financial demands.

Mikel Arteta was desperate to sign the 26-year-old but the Brazilian ultimately ended up signing a latest six-contract at the Santiago Bernabeu.

Sources indicate that however, Vinicius has had an unhappy time at Real this season. In 12 appearances under Jose Mourinho, he has registered just one goal and three assists.

He was also handed a four-match suspension for pulling the hair of Villarreal midfielder Nathan Saliba.

Vinicius’ place in the team has come under threat from Yan Diomande and he has now addressed those struggles.

He pointed to the disappointment of Brazil’s last-16 exit at the World Cup and the uncertainty over his future as factors behind his poor form.

“I don’t think I prepared as well as I could have after the World Cup, because the impact of that tournament is so significant for all Brazilians,” he said.
