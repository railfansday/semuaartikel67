The AI revolution: Will I lose my job?

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Have you heard of FOBO? It stands for fear of being obsolete. So as AI spreads, is it going to impact women in the workforce more than men? And are we right to be fearful of becoming obsolete? The International Labour Organization has already found occupations dominated by women are nearly twice as likely to be exposed to job losses thanks to generative AI, compared to those dominated by men.
