Xabi Alonso has admitted he would like “more options” to pick from within his Chelsea squad, as he lamented their injury record and hinted at a possible need to strengthen in January.

The Blues’ boss has made 24 line-up changes in the Premier League this season, with only Tottenham (28) having made more.

That suggests Alonso’s team is unsettled, something backed up by their inconsistent result and which was put to the Spaniard on Friday.

“Once you have the whole menu, you can select what you like and I know what I like from the menu,” Alonso remarked.

“I would like to have more options with the whole squad available but that's something that's difficult to have during the season, but I am sure we will get close to that.”.

Alonso may well have been referring predominantly to players currently at the club while also alluding to the potential for his squad to be strengthened in the winter transfer window.

Chelsea are among the clubs keen on signing Bournemouth midfielder Alex Scott and are known to feel there is still progress they can make with their squad-building.

The Blues currently have Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix, Jorrel Hato and Reece James sidelined with injury, while Emi Martinez, Josh Acheampong and Jamie Gittens have just returned to fitness.

Alonso was asked whether he is concerned by the number of injuries his side of suffering, given that they are not involved in a European competition this season so fewer games ought to mean fewer injuries.

In a recent development, “It is a concern,” Alonso remarked. “Sometimes you can analyse them and find a reason but sometimes it is difficult to find a reason.”.
