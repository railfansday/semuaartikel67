Costco is coming to the rescue of anyone scrambling to put together a last-minute Thanksgiving dinner, bringing back its affordable meal kit with enough food to feed up to eight people.

Earlier in November, Costco made headlines for selling a massive 30-pound Thanksgiving dinner for $200, complete with a turkey, numerous sides, and desserts.

However, that extravagant feast required customers to place their orders by November 13, leaving anyone who missed the deadline out of luck.

Thankfully, with just days to go until Thanksgiving, Costco has brought back a considerably cheaper alternative that doesn’t require a pre-order.

A post shared by Laura Jayne Lamb (@costcohotfinds).

As reported by Delish, Costco’s Kirkland Signature Turkey Dinner Kit has returned to the retailer’s deli department for 2026.

Each kit comes with a seasoned, boneless half turkey breast, herbed stuffing, Yukon Gold mashed potatoes, green beans, cranberry sauce, and gravy.

The kits are priced at $3.99 per pound and weigh around 10 pounds, meaning shoppers can expect to pay roughly $40 for the entire meal.

According to Costco’s official product listing, each kit serves seven to eight people, bringing the cost down to around $5 per person.

Recent findings suggest that while the turkey still needs to be cooked, much of the preparation has already been taken care of. The meat even comes sitting on top of the stuffing, allowing the juices to soak into it while roasting.

Unlike Costco’s more expensive Thanksgiving feast, however, the kit doesn’t include dinner rolls or dessert, so you might want to grab one of the retailer’s famous pumpkin pies while you’re there.

Costco isn’t the only retailer trying to make Thanksgiving more affordable. Walmart is offering a $35 feast that feeds 10 people, while Amazon has a $25 Thanksgiving meal with same-day delivery on qualifying orders.

Both of those bundles primarily consist of groceries that customers will need to prepare themselves, giving Costco an advantage for anyone looking to spend less time in the kitchen.

Just don’t leave your shopping until Thanksgiving morning, as Costco’s warehouses will be closed on November 26.
