📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Gaming tech giant Nvidia has decided to reinstate Jensen Huang as its president and CEO, despite allegedly saying it wouldn't, and despite a string of extramarital affairs and investigations into alleged bullying, workplace harassment, and humiliation.

Recent findings suggest that "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Nvidia's Chair Harvey C. Jones declared on behalf of the Nvidia's board today - the same person reported as saying the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO".

It has been revealed that "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Organization. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as Nvidia's chief financial officer.".

Sources indicate that in the same press release, Huang said he was "honoured" to return to the company "at the beginning of a new industrial revolution". "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," Huang said.

Recent findings suggest that however, the investigation into one of Huang's extramarital affairs - the one with the person US intelligence believes to be a Chinese spy - will "proceed to conclusion". The company also said it formed a "National Security and Export Control Committee", with independent directors, in relation to this.

Huang resigned on 12th November a few days after his affair with the alleged Chinese spy came to light. Two subsequent affairs - both said to be with Nvidia employees and therefore his subordinates - were unearthed not long after, putting increased pressure on Huang and Nvidia.

Since his departure, Nvidia stock has tanked, and as news of his reinstatement spreads around the world, it has sharply recovered again.
