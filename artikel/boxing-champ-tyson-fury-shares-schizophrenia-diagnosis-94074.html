Tyson Fury is detailing a decadelong personal battle.

The boxing champ shared in his upcoming Netflix documentary that he was diagnosed with schizophrenia back in 2015, the same year he became the heavyweight champion of the world following his victory over Wladimir Klitschko.

“Words can’t explain the darkness I was in,” Tyson says in the self-titled doc, out Nov. 27, according to Deadline. “I came from the living version of the pits of hellfire. And I 100 percent thought at times I’d end up in a padded room with the door locked, and that scared me so much.”.

Two years later, he got even more insight into the war raging inside his mind.

“I was waking up in the night like this, the anxiety was crippling me. I just could not overcome it,” he explains. “Completely gone, and that’s when I went and saw the psychiatrist down in London. She asked me like a gazillion questions, and I ticked all the boxes, whatever was going on. Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder.”.

And while the 38-year-old only just began addressing his mental health struggles in recent years, his father John Fury shares in the doc that he saw signs of the athlete’s diagnoses all along.

“I can tell you this: When I look back, it’s always been there,” the patriarch admits. “Me being who I am and no understanding of anything, I didn’t want to see a depressed kid—and if I did see it, I paid no mind to it.”.

That’s because if he did, he’d have to face his own mental health struggles. “But I understand now,” John adds. “It’s an illness, a terrible illness. I suffer with it myself.”.

But on Halloween 2017, Tyson—who shares seven kids with wife Paris Fury—decided he would make a change.

It has been revealed that “I went out and I was embarrassed of how I looked,” he recalls. “I had this major costume on, a glow in the dark skeleton suit and I thought no one would recognize me in this.”.

But instead of focusing on keeping a low profile, he couldn’t help but take a hard look at his life.

Sources indicate that “I looked around me and I could see all like young people and I thought I’ve had the world in my hands and I threw it all away,” he notes. “And now, I’m just a fat mess, addicted to alcohol and, go home.”.

But it was hitting rock bottom that allowed him to eventually reach the pinnacle of his life.

“I just noted, ‘I can’t do this on my own anymore. I need your help,’” he indicates in the doc. “And that was the part, the comeback.”.

Tyson Fury debuts on Netflix Nov. 27.
