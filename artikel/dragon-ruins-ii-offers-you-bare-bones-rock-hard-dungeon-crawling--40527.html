📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that nowadays, the quintessential fun of a dungeon crawl is virtually unknown to the younger generation. What happened to the times when starving to death or getting lost forever was a very real occupational hazard for adventurers?! Well, that's what Dragon Ruins II seeks to recapture as it opens pre-registration on Android.

Sources indicate that as the sequel to the original Dragon Ruins, in Dragon Ruins II you'll return to the capital city of Isigwere, taking on quests from its inhabitants as you venture deeper into the dungeon. All with the ultimate aim of slaying the dragon that lurks therein and becoming the much-vaunted Legendary Hero.

It also expands on the original with new environments and monster records, allowing you to look back on those that you've slain. Having played the original Dragon Ruins myself, I can tell you it's not for the faint of heart, but if you're feeling brave, it can offer an adventure on the level of even the more visually flashy RPGs out there.

It has been revealed that finally, I find it interesting to see Kemco tackling publishing an indie title like this, as it's very different from their usual releases. Here's hoping Kemco will continue to expand its fantasy offerings with more like Graverobber Foundation's Dragon Ruins II!
