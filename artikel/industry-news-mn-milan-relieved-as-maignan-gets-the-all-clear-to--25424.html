Some positive news has arrived for Ruben Amorim and AC Milan a few days before they return to Serie A action against Frosinone.

Industry observers note that milanNews are reporting that the MRI scan that Mike Maignan underwent produced a negative result, which in this sense is positive as it means that no injuries showed up. As a result of this, the goalkeeper will be available for AC Milan vs. Frosinone on Sunday afternoon at San Siro.

In the UEFA Nations League match between France and Turkey on Sunday night, Maignan left the pitch at halftime with an adductor problem resulting from a collision with an opponent, which obviously sparked worries among fans and the Milan medical staff.

Yesterday, L’Equipe reported that initial tests had suggested the goalkeeper was in fine shape, and the tests conducted with the staff confirmed the initial findings. The goalkeeper has not suffered any muscular injuries and will be available for Sunday’s resumption of Serie A action.

Recent findings suggest that it is a relief for Amorim, who will have his captain between the posts for the game against a side that are sixth in the table and scoring plenty of goals. Lorenzo Torriani was ready to step in again had Maignan not been able to start, but the doubts seem to have been resolved.
