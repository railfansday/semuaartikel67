Mikel Arteta has insisted Arsenal are open to a transfer "opportunity" if it presents itself in the January window.

The Gunners sit six points clear at the top of the Premier League but questions remain over the forward options in the squad.

Gabriel Martinelli, Gabriel Jesus and Leandro Trossard were all sold in the summer, with only Christos Tzolis signed to bolster the attack.

Arsenal have scored 17 goals in the league this season, the worst record of the club's currently in the top six, and have been punished for wasteful displays in the last week against Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund.

RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa is among the players Arsenal are tracking, but January has historically been a quiet window for the Gunners.

Not since 2023, when Arsenal brought in Jorginho, Leandro Trossard and Jakub Kiwior mid-season, have the club made a January signing.

However, Arteta insisted Arsenal were working hard in all of those windows, even when no business was done, and he is confident the club will be alert to potential deals again this time.

“We've always been very active in the transfer window, especially when we have certain complications," Arteta said.

"Have we managed to get a deal over the line? Sometimes not. So we will see if there is an opportunity there and something that we believe is going to make a difference for the team.".

Arsenal co-chairman Josh Kroenke is expected in London next week and he is set to meet with Arteta and the club's hierarchy.

The January window will be one of the items on the agenda, though Arteta was keen to emphasise that the club have already been putting transfer plans in place.

“We don’t wait to talk about these things only when it is face to face," Arteta said of Kroenke's trip to London.
