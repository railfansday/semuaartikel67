American Airlines and Israir Airlines have just confirmed prepares to partner for flights between the United States and Israel. For now don’t expect huge implications, but over time, this has the potential to evolve.

Industry observers note that american and Israir are launching a commercial partnership focused on enhanced travel options between Israel and the United States. The partnership is expected to expand in phases, delivering some benefits to customers. American aims to resume flights to Tel Aviv (TLV) as of late March 2027.

How is this partnership expected to play out?

For context, as of March 27, 2027, American looks to resume flights between Upcoming York and Tel Aviv, using the Boeing 777-200ER. Israir operates five weekly flights between Tel Aviv and New York, using an Airbus A330-200 (funny enough, the airline flies ex-American A330s, which the airline retired shortly after the start of the pandemic).

For those curious about the interiors of Israir’s A330s, below is a video of them (sorry, I don’t know what’s going on in the video, but I haven’t otherwise been able to find a cabin video).

Here’s how American’s SVP of Alliances, Anmol Bhargava, describes this:.

“American looks forward to building a relationship with Israir that will give customers the convenient, well-connected travel options they desire. As we prepare to resume service between New York and Tel Aviv next spring, this partnership will help us deliver a stronger customer proposition in the market and create more opportunities for Israir’s travelers to connect beyond our JFK gateway.”.

When it comes to the new partnership between American and Israir, here’s what’s I find noteworthy:.

In a recent development, all-in-all, this is a pretty casual partnership. There’s no real downside here for American, since the carrier might just pick up a little bit of revenue on connecting Israir passengers beyond Upcoming York. Meanwhile Israir has a better framework for selling tickets beyond New York, which helps the carrier expand its potential customer base. But it is interesting how the codeshare is one-sided for now….

According to recent reports, american Airlines and Israir Airlines are launching a partnership for flights between the United States and Israel. Israir will be codesharing on American’s flight between New York and Tel Aviv (when that resumes in late March 2027, in theory), while American will have an interline agreement with Israir, allowing Israir passengers to connect on American flights beyond New York.

Sources indicate that we may see a closer level of cooperation between the two airlines in the future, including possible AAdvantage perks, but nothing has been finalized there yet. I wouldn’t read too much into this partnership for now, and think it falls more in the category of “better than nothing,” rather than anything beyond that.
