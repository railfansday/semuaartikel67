In a recent development, sticking with familiar brands and services is one of the best ways for the average investor to pick winning stocks. A stock's price tends to follow its earnings over time. A company growing earnings at least 15% a year can double in five years, and some of the most dominant consumer services can still achieve that level of growth.

Wall Street expects the following top growth stocks to deliver 19% or better annual earnings growth, putting a double by 2031 within reach.

Sources indicate that amazon (AMZN -4.10%)shares closed at $257.89 on Nov. 17, up 11% over the past year and 45% over the past five years, trailing the S&P 500's 56% five-year return. The reason it can double by 2031 is expanding margins, which the market hasn't fully priced in yet.

Over the past three years, Amazon's trailing-12-month operating income has risen 283% to $106 billion. Amazon's most profitable business is its cloud services segment. Still, it has many levers to increase profitability from growth in other non-retail services, which make up the majority of its $797 billion in annual revenue.

It has been revealed that advertising is a profitable business to watch. Amazon sells ads to brands and sellers that want to reach its shoppers. Ad revenue grew 12% year over year on a constant-currency basis to $19.6 billion in the third quarter, bringing it close to $80 billion annually.

However, that growth doesn't show the real momentum. Moving Prime Day into Q2 cut 8.5 percentage points from Q3 ad growth, according to CEO Andy Jassy. Excluding currency swings, ad sales grew 22% to 26% in each of the five prior quarters, and management expects ad growth to return to the high teens in Q4.

Sources indicate that amazon trades at about 27 times forward earnings, which is reasonable given analysts' expectations that earnings will grow about 24% per year over the coming years. If the valuation multiple holds, and Amazon delivers on those expectations, the stock could more than double by 2031.

Airbnb (ABNB -1.41%)shares closed at $180.94 on Nov. 17, up 54% over the past year but down 9% over the past five years. The business has continued to grow, creating a good buying opportunity.

Management says "Airbnb is back in growth mode." Q3 revenue rose 19% year over year, its fastest growth in more than three years, and management raised its full-year outlook.

Sources indicate that new products are driving the momentum. Airbnb shipped more than 50 products and features in the quarter, nearly 70% more than a year earlier, and says artificial intelligence (AI) is helping its teams move faster.

Recent findings suggest that its own AI travel agent starts rolling out in the first half of 2027. CEO Brian Chesky wants AI built into the way the firm works, and he has described AI tools as a superpower for running it. This spells faster updates for its platform, more efficient operations, and the potential for strong earnings growth.

Analysts expect Airbnb's earnings to grow about 19% a year over the long term. That is enough to double the share price, assuming the stock still trades at the same forward earnings multiple of 32. Chesky stated that as it becomes an AI-native organization, "the opportunity ahead of us is actually even bigger.".
