An eight-year ban has been handed to Ecuadorian footballer Jhon Perlaza after he threw a stone that hit an assistant referee during a third-tier match.

The game, between Perlaza's side Club Ciudad de Yantzaza and Club Rio Aguarico FC, took place on 17 October.

The rock was thrown in the 96th minute and struck assistant referee Lenin Giovanni Ojeda Nunez, after which the match was abandoned.

Ecuador's football federation (FEF) unveiled the sanction in a statement on Wednesday.

"The assistant referee suffered serious injuries that required surgery and a 60-day recovery period," the FEF noted in a statement on Wednesday.

"The disciplinary committee found the player responsible for physical aggression against a match official and suspended him for eight years from all football-related activity, until 19 October 2034.

"In addition, the FEF will ask Fifa to extend this sanction worldwide.".

The suspension runs from the federation's disciplinary process, which held the player responsible for physical aggression against a match official.

Yantzaza were also punished by the FEF, which said the club must pay a fine of $2,000 (£1520.19).

A further $4,369.73 (£3,321.41) is owed to cover the assistant referee's medical expenses.

The club issued its own statement on 20 October, apologising to the refereeing team and the fans for what happened.

"As an institution, we categorically reject all acts of aggression, whether verbal or ‌physical, both inside and outside sporting venues. ​We firmly believe that football should be ‌a space of respect, ⁠coexistence, and fair play," Yantzaza mentioned.

"Once the person ⁠responsible for this regrettable incident was identified, the institution made the ‌decision to ​remove him immediately, and he ‌is therefore no longer ​part of our club.".
