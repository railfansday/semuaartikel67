📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Kacey Musgraves may be a Nashville staple with deep Texas roots, but the country star is calling a latest locale home lately. The “Rainbow” singer, who has resided in the Music City for many years, revealed in a recent interview that she now lives in Mexico “most of the time.”.

According to recent reports, musgraves told People that the change of scene “ended up just completely changing my life and inspiring me so much. I’m so in love with so many aspects of it, and it’s really taught me a lot.” She explained that the transition took place over a year and a half ago when “this beautiful cosmic thing” drew her down to Mexico. Living there with her Australian cattle dog Pepper, the singer-songwriter found comfort in a slower pace of life, which has allowed her to be barefoot often and to spend plenty of time outdoors. The relocation even moved Musgraves to release a sister album to her most recent, Middle of Nowhere Vol. 2: Tex Mex Tapes, which will feature four songs in Spanish.

Sources indicate that musgaves previously opened up about her wanderlust and the possibility of leaving Nashville for the bright lights of Latest York City. “Girl, I’m always browsing Zillow late at night, acting like a fool—it’s my favorite,” she told People in 2024. “I wouldn’t rule [moving] out, but I do have a community in Nashville that I know that I couldn’t find anywhere else.”.

According to recent reports, back in 2022, the country queen and her 3,500-square-foot Nashville home graced AD’s May 2022 cover. She purchased the property in 2020 during a time of major changes in her life, which included her split from singer-songwriter Ruston Kelly. Still, she made it clear that the blank canvas was no “divorce house.”.

According to recent reports, “I wanted a place that felt like me,” she stated, “where I could express myself without having to think about another person and what they might want. This felt like a new beginning.” Musgraves went on to sell that space and ultimately bought a “cottage” in the Tennessee forest.
