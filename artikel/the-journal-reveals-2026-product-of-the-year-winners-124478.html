Sixteen companies were selected as winners for their product achievements.

Recent findings suggest that THE Journal has confirmed the winners of its 2026 Product of the Year Awards. Since 2020, these awards have recognized outstanding product development achievements of manufacturers and suppliers whose products or services are considered to be particularly noteworthy in the transformation of education technology.

This year, 16 companies were recognized across multiple categories for their impact in education. For the full list of winners, visit THEJournal.com.

"Congratulations to all of our 2026 Product of the Year Award winners," stated Rhea Kelly, editor-in-chief of THE Journal. "These innovative solutions reflect the continued evolution of education technology and the important role it plays in supporting educators, improving student experiences and helping institutions navigate an increasingly digital future. We're proud to recognize the companies driving meaningful progress across education institutions.".
