According to recent reports, progressive Ahmed Moor is running to beat Sen. John Fetterman, D-Pa., in 2028, and is running in the opposite direction of the lawmaker’s current positions, including bucking the war in Iran and abolishing ICE.

FIRST ON FOX: It’s a circumstance almost ripped from a screenplay: a Gaza-born political newcomer wants to topple one of the Senate’s staunchest supporters of Israel in the Democratic Party.

Recent findings suggest that but for Ahmed Moor, a member of the Democratic Socialists of America who is the first to toss his hat into the arena to challenge Sen. John Fetterman, D-Pa., the story doesn't end there.

It has been revealed that "Look, so this race is not about John Fetterman," Moor told Fox News Digital in an interview. "John Fetterman's story is over, but the story of the United States continues. I'm getting in now because I believe we're in the midst of a constitutional crisis.".

In a recent development, sen. John Fetterman, D-Pa., makes his way to votes in the U.S. Capitol on Sept. 30, 2026. (CQ-Roll Call, Inc. via Getty Images).

In a recent development, "I have some compassion for John Fetterman — I want to depersonalize," he continued. "Let's talk about policy. The policies that man has expressed and committed to are just a reflection of the special interests which have run his office for too long now. The problem of John Fetterman's policies is the problem of corruption in America.".

Fetterman is up for re-election in the 2028 cycle and has become a target for Democrats in Pennsylvania ready to replace him as he continuously moved more to the center of the political spectrum and aligned with Republicans on key complications like the war in Iran, government funding and immigration.

Sources indicate that whether he runs again for a second term remains in the air. When asked by Fox News Digital if he had made a decision, Fetterman mentioned, "The more, the merrier," to challengers entering the Democratic primary.

But Moor, so far, is the only candidate officially in the race.

FETTERMAN WARNS THERE IS NO LONGER 'COMMON GROUND' WITH FAR-LEFT DEMOCRATS OVER ISRAEL.

"I know, he seems to really love Hamas," Fetterman said. "And now, so, like, so if you tell him, say thank you for running.".

Moor was born in Gaza before immigrating to Philadelphia, where he went to public schools, attended the University of Pennsylvania and later went to Harvard to obtain a public policy degree.

Sources indicate that his policy positions are in line with the growing progressive flank in the Senate Democratic caucus and echo the positions that Fetterman first ran on in 2022 when he pledged to support the Senate’s progressive standard-bearer, Sen. Bernie Sanders, I-Vt., if he won.

According to recent reports, "I spent a lot of time on international policy, kind of a foreign policy specialist, one of the reasons the Senate job appeals to me," Moor said. "But equally, I'm committed to what I call the ‘Dignity Agenda,’ which is universal access to healthcare, childcare and affordable housing.".

Among the top concerns for Moor in the "constitutional crisis" he sees in the country is freedom of speech, which he contended came under assault during the Biden administration when protests against "the genocide in Palestine" exploded across college campuses.

FETTERMAN PLAYS POLITICAL MAVERICK, BUT HIS VOTING RECORD TELLS ANOTHER STORY.

Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, D-N.Y., during a news conference following Senate Democrat policy luncheons at the U.S. Capitol in Washington, D.C., March 3, 2026. (Graeme Sloan/Bloomberg via Getty Images).

Recent findings suggest that "Students were brutalized for assembly, for speech," Moor said. "That shouldn't happen in America. Donald Trump stepped through that breach and accelerated the assault. And ICE today is the worst expression, the violation of due process in America.".

Like other Democrats and progressives, Moor wants to abolish Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), but he would "like to see immigration law enforced.".

Industry observers note that "ICE is a lawless organization — ICE is a paramilitary militia. ICE is not law enforcement," he stated. "Let's abolish ICE. Let's hold the bad actors who've killed Americans and kidnapped others in our streets. Let's hold them to account for breaking our local ordinance and our local laws. That's a criminal matter today in America. I hope local prosecutors run with it.".

And like other progressives who scored stunning victories during the midterm elections to help flip the Senate, like Sens.-elect Abdul El-Sayed and Peggy Flanagan, Moor is against the old guard of Democratic leadership staying in charge.

Recent findings suggest that it’s a position that is gaining steam in the Senate, where Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., is mounting a rare challenge to Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, D-N.Y., to lead Democrats through the forthcoming majority in the upper chamber.

Moor said Schumer and House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., both have to go.

"Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries don't have what it takes to meet the moment. They need to be replaced," Moor said. "This campaign is also about sustaining pressure in that direction. If they survive the leadership contest, which is underway today, I'm going to continue to sustain the pressure on both of those men to do the right thing continuously.".
