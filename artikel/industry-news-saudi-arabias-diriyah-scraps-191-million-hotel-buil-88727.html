It has been revealed that diriyah has canceled a $191 million hotel contract and won’t say why. It’s the first Saudi hotel project known to be pulled since the Houthi strikes on Riyadh, and it comes from one of the few giga-projects still spending.

Diriyah Company, the Saudi sovereign fund-backed developer building a giga-project on the edge of Riyadh, has canceled a SAR 717 million ($191 million) contract to build a 325-room hotel, nine months after awarding it.

Industry observers note that the contract was awarded in February to a joint venture between BEC Arabia Contracting and the Saudi subsidiary of India’s Ashoka Buildcon for construction of the One Hotel. Diriyah told the joint venture that the project was “cancelled due to internal reasons attributable to the employer,” Ashoka remarked in a Nov. 19 filing to the Bombay Stock Exchange.
