Some Grand Theft Auto 6 players on PS5 say the game keeps crashing with error code CE-108255-1, and in some cases, it's stopping them from progressing through the story.

Recent findings suggest that "The game runs perfectly fine for a while. I can complete missions and chapters without any issues, but then it suddenly crashes after around 40 minutes and sends me back to the PS5 home screen", wrote one Reddit user. They added that other games, including GTA 5 and For Honor, run without crashing.

Another player told Dexerto that GTA 6 worked fine until Pipework, an early mission that introduces Mandy, but now crashes "every time I use the plunger or stand around the sink with it", which means they can’t progress.

Some PS5 Pro owners say the game crashes during the opening prologue, either at the same point every time or a few seconds in, and they haven't been able to play since launch.

Others say they've tried the usual fixes, such as clearing their console's cache, rebuilding the database, factory resetting their PS5, and even downloading the game three times, but nothing has worked thus far.

It's not just PS5 players that are struggling, either; some Xbox Series X players have reported crashes too, with one saying the game sometimes crashes after just 15 minutes.

Rockstar hasn't yet acknowledged the crashes or mentioned whether a patch is on the way, and its own support is sending players with console errors to PlayStation or Xbox directly for help.

Unfortunately, there's no firm fix just yet. PlayStation's support page describes CE-108255-1 simply as "an error occurred with the game/application”, and its only advice is to "try updating your game". Highlight GTA 6 on your home screen, press Options, and choose Check for Revision.
