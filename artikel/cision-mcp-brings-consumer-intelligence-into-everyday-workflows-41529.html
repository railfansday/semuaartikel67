In a recent development, CHICAGO, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cision, a global leader in consumer and media intelligence and communications technology, today unveiled the first release of Cision MCP, bringing its consumer intelligence closer to customers' everyday decisions. It follows the introduction of Cision Intelligence.

Recent findings suggest that cision MCP uses Model Context Protocol to connect supported tools and agents to Cision's specialist intelligence. The first release connects to Brandwatch Consumer Research agents, helping teams investigate consumer conversations, make sense of findings and stay informed about changes that matter to their business.

Recent findings suggest that "Customers should be able to explore an key question when and where it arises," noted Jeff Mullins, Chief Technology Officer at Cision. "Cision MCP brings our specialist intelligence into supported tools and workflows, starting with Brandwatch Consumer Research. Our shared AI foundation gives us a way to extend that access as we develop more capabilities across Cision.".

Customers can use Cision MCP alongside Brandwatch Consumer Research, giving them an additional way to work with its intelligence within supported environments.

One connection, expanding capabilities.

Cision Intelligence brings together Cision's specialist data, expertise and AI capabilities, with a shared foundation developed for reuse across its solutions and supported external tools and agents.

Cision intends to expand MCP access rapidly across its other solutions. Additional product agents will bring more capabilities into customers' workflows, while broader connections across products develop over time.

The first release connects customers to Brandwatch Consumer Research agents. Access to additional products and capabilities will be communicated as each release becomes available.

Explore the first Cision Intelligence capabilities available through Cision MCP at cision.com/cision-mcp/.

Existing products remain available, giving customers the choice to continue working in the solutions they use today.
