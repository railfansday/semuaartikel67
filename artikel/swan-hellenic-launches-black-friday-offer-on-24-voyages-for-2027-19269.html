📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Swan Hellenic has announced its Black Friday offer valid for 24 voyages in 2027.

The deal includes savings of up to $4,000 per person for double occupancy of a Balcony Stateroom, plus $250 onboard credit each and a double loyalty discount of 10 percent for returning guests.

The offer is available for upcoming bookings made by December 4, 2026, for selected voyages across the flleet ranging from six to 21 nights departing from March 9, 2027, through October 27, 2027.

“We’re delighted to launch Swan Hellenic’s Black Friday offering of 24 cultural expedition voyages that explore some of the world’s most extraordinary remote regions in real depth,” said Mario Bounas, chief commercial officer of Swan Hellenic.

“With no limit on choice, this is a unique opportunity for curious travelers to make 2027 a standout year of discovery, experiencing remarkable places and cultures in the comfort, style and adventurous spirit that distinguish Swan Hellenic. With savings of up to $4,000 per person plus $250 onboard credit and a substantial loyalty discount, our guests can create lifelong memories seeing what others don’t without having to wait a lifetime. Because the spirit of discovery should never have to wait!”.

The tech giant noted that back-to-back bookings attract an additional 5 percent discount, adding that the rates are also applicable to group bookings of six staterooms and more.
