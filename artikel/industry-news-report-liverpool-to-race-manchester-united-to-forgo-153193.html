The Reds have enjoyed a solid start to the season and find themselves just one point off second after 11 matches.

Planning has already begun within Anfield's recruitment team, but a surprise deal could reportedly now be on the cards.

Several positions look set to be addressed over the next 12 months, with multiple first-team stars' futures in doubt.

With Virgil van Dijk and Joe Gomez both out of contract at the end of the season, a new central defender could become a priority for Julian Ward sooner rather than later.

Some of the best defenders in Europe are being linked with Merseyside moves as potential replacements for Van Dijk, but a surprise candidate is now being mentioned.

According to a report from The Daily Briefing, Liverpool are monitoring Mohamed Simakan, the French central defender that swapped RB Leipzig for Al-Nassr in 2024.

The 26-year-old has been an important figure for the Saudi Pro League side and is now being pursued by the Reds as well as Manchester United.

Liverpool find themselves in a very different situation to their rivals, but both sides will likely consider January too soon to make a move for the Frenchman per the report.

Simakan can play either through the middle or as a right-back, stands at 6'2 and has generally been fairly injury free since his move to Saudi Arabia.

There haven't been too many examples of players moving back from the Saudi Pro League to top European sides, but Liverpool clearly still see a quality player and they could be tempted to outbid United for the defender's services.
