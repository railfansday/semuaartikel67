Working on one of the world's biggest football video games is the perfect combination of my two biggest passions, gaming and football.

It has been revealed that I'm CJ Ramson. I work on the creation of football video games. I work on Football Manager as an associate QA manager. QA stands for quality assurance.

In a recent development, we're the ones that test the game. We make sure everything works, and we work closely with the developers to try and make the most realistic simulation of football.

In a recent development, growing up, I loved football, whether that was playing football or playing football video games with my friends. I'd always be doing one or the other. With the video games, the thing that really drew me to them was I've always been into the tactical side of the game, either being able to control the players or create your own tactics.

So a typical day for us, we'll get a latest build in from the developers. We'll test it as a QA team, and we'll look for either bugsor ways that we can improve the game. That can be anything from the runs that players make, to decisions they make on the ball, what the team instructions and tactics do. So there's a lot of areas for us to cover. Our final goal is to make it as realistic to real football as possible.

So even though my background was in coaching, there was a lot of transferable skills that I learned and developed through my time coaching that I managed to transfer into this. So whether that's being really analytical and having a good eye for detail and knowingwhen to pick things out and what to look for, or it could be things like timekeeping and making sure things run perfectly on time, and working with other people as well. So social skills where you have to work within a team or even lead a team.
