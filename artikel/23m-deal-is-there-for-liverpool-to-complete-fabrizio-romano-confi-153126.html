Fabrizio Romano has confirmed it. Liverpool have a €23m deal if they want it - they just need to complete things.

Sources indicate that liverpool could use a latest left-back. Kostas Tsimikas, right now their no.2 option in the position, will almost certainly leave the club at the end of the season with the expiry of his contract.

It has been revealed that the jury remains out on Milos Kerkez, too, meaning it's unclear what kind of full-back Liverpool are after. Do they just want a backup option to replace Tsimikas?

Or maybe a development project who can potentially overtake Kerkez but start as an understudy? Or, maybe, Liverpool just want a newly introduced starting left-back - someone to replace the Hungarian from the get-go.

Recent findings suggest that there are certainly options to for any choice there and Liverpool will have some big decisions to make in 2027 as they lock-in their immediate future for the position. And one option there looks particularly tempting.

According to recent reports, it makes Brown incredibly tempting. The 24-year-old looks to be on the cusp of something special and is available for a really good price. The fact he also ticks that homegrown box just makes him near-impossible to pass up.

According to recent reports, and without a clear signing at left-back, we're not sure Liverpool will find a better choice. Brown is a very good player and ticks the right boxes. He may well be the Reds' next left-back.
