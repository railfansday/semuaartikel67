Solar Insure Launches 20-Year Commercial Battery Warranty, Expanding Protection for Solar & Storage Projects

Launching at RE+ Las Vegas, November 17–20 | Visit Solar Insure at Booth C4190.

In a recent development, commercial property owners invest in solar and battery storage to manage energy costs and strengthen resilience. One question can shape that investment from the start. Who pays when equipment needs repairs years after installation?

According to recent reports, solar Insure today expanded its SI-Commercial Suite with two latest warranties. SI-Commercial Battery is a 20-year limited warranty for commercial battery storage systems, and SI-Commercial Total covers solar and battery storage under one warranty. Together with the existing SI-Commercial Solar warranty, these additions allow Certified Providers to offer protection tailored to each project, whether it includes solar, battery storage, or both. Solar Insure protects more than 100,000 systems nationwide.

In a recent development, “Commercial projects are built for the long term, and their protection should reflect that,” said Ara Agopian, CEO of Solar Insure. “By expanding our commercial warranty suite, we’re helping solar companies offer protection that fits the systems their customers are investing in. It’s about giving property owners greater confidence not just in the installation, but in the years of ownership that follow.”.

Industry observers note that A failed inverter or declining battery capacity can lead to costs an owner did not budget for. Even when a manufacturer covers replacement equipment, the owner may still be responsible for labor and service costs. The expanded suite helps Certified Providers explain which repair expenses are covered so customers can evaluate their potential repair costs alongside system performance and installation price.

It has been revealed that “Commercial battery owners are making a long-term investment, but some of the most significant expenses can emerge years after installation,” remarked Dean Chiaravallotti, Chief Revenue Officer at Solar Insure. “A battery replacement or unexpected repair can change the financial picture of a project. When providers can explain how they address those costs before the customer signs, they help buyers make a more informed investment decision. That’s the value we’re bringing to commercial storage.”.

It has been revealed that solar companies must become Solar Insure Certified Providers to offer SI-Commercial warranties. Certification includes a review of the company’s qualifications, business practices, and commitment to customer service.

For qualifying claims, Solar Insure pays covered repair costs directly to the Certified Provider or service provider performing the work. If the original installer is no longer available, Solar Insure helps coordinate next steps, which may include identifying another qualified service provider. When a property changes hands, coverage transfers to the new owner at no cost.

It has been revealed that “A lot can change over the life of a commercial energy system. Properties change hands, service providers change, and equipment eventually needs attention,” remarked Chiaravallotti. “Property owners need to know what happens w          hen those changes occur. Our goal is to give them a clearer path forward, with protection and support that extend well beyond the original installation.”.

Solar companies can contact Solar Insure to learn about becoming a Certified Provider. Commercial property owners can speak with a Solar Insure Certified Provider about which warranty fits their project.

In a recent development, solar Insure is a leading provider of solar and storage warranties, focused on protecting long-term system performance and elevating accountability across the renewable energy industry. Through rigorous provider certification and governance, Solar Insure delivers durable protection that residential and commercial installers can stand behind.
