Milan Futuro fell victim to the old adage as they could not do it on a cold night in Stoke, losing 2-1 in the Premier League International Cup.

Industry observers note that the Futuro kept up their excellent recent form by sweeping aside Tritium 3-0 on Sunday, continuing their push towards the top. It put Sergio Navarro’s side now three points off top spot and with a game in hand on the leaders.

In a recent development, after recording an impressive 2-1 win over Everton in the opener, the hope was that the young Rossoneri could build on it with another positive trip to England. They faced Stoke City on Matchday 2 of Group B in the 2026-27 U21 Premier League International Cup.

It has been revealed that however, they fell to a 2-1 defeat in what was a tough game at the Bet365 Stadium. First half goals from Kyeremeh and Pinder put the home side in command and they proved decisive, as Navarro’s side fell short in their late comeback attempt after Balentien’s late strike.

It must be stated that several Futuro players were on the bench for the senior team in their Europa League win over Olympiacos, while the Potters had a starting XI that featured players born in 1994 and 2001.

In a recent development, the next PLIC fixture is away to Tottenham on December 16th. Before then, attention returns to Serie D, with Real Calepina visiting Solbiate Arno on Sunda at 14:30 CET.
