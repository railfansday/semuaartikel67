Use Claude on Mac or Windows? Anthropic just released a newly introduced way to make personalized desktop apps using AI tools.

“Starting today, you can use Claude artifacts to create, run, and share desktop apps. The “Install as app” feature is rolling out in beta in Claude for Mac and Windows,” Anthropic says.

According to recent reports, “When an artifact is installed as a Mac or Windows app, it gets its own icon and opens in its own window instead of inside Claude. You can share it with others, and it needs your permission before it can interact with your computer. You choose how much to allow.”.

Additionally, Anthropic indicates apps made with this method can benefit from Claude connectors. Claude usage is tied to desktop apps made with artifacts.

This form of Claude-created app is capable of sending notifications, adding items to the menu bar, and having its own permissions.
