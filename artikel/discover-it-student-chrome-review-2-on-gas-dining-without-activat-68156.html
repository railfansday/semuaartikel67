📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Get the Discover it® Student Chrome if you’re a college student who drives, eats out, and won’t track rotating categories. It pays 2% on up to $1,000 a quarter of gas and restaurant spending with nothing to activate, has no annual fee, and doubles all the cash back you earn in your first year.

Sources indicate that capital One bought Discover in 2025 and now issues the Discover it Student Chrome, so you apply for the card and manage it on Capital One’s website and app. It’s the student version of the Discover it Chrome, and college students don’t need a credit score to apply.

According to recent reports, this card fits students who drive, eat out, and won’t track rotating categories, since the 2% comes automatically and the first-year match doubles it to 4%. Students willing to activate those categories each quarter earn more with the Discover it Student Cash Back than with this card once more than $250 of a quarter’s spending lands in its 5% category.
