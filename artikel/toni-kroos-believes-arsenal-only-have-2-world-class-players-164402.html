Toni Kroos has revealed his belief that Arsenal only have two 'world-class' players in their midst, and Declan Rice isn't one of them.

In a recent development, mikel Arteta's men won the Premier League title last season and were only denied a historic double by a penalty shootout, losing on spot-kicks to Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League final in Budapest in May. The Gunners are on course to retain their crown as champions of England, as they right now sit six points clear, while many have tipped them to go all the way in Europe's elite club competition this term.

The North Londoners boast an embarrassment of riches across the pitch, with this deep and talented squad ultimately leading to all this success.

Despite this, six-time Champions League winner Kroos doesn't think there are more than two world-class players in Arteta's squad.

World-class has made its way into football's lexicon and is now considered an exclusive bracket of players that only the very elite on the planet fit into. However, a universal definition has never been established, despite many people arguing effusively whenever someone makes a claim about a player being world-class or not being world-class.

Ultimately, it can be assumed that the best teams in Europe all contain a multitude of world-class players, although Kroos doesn't believe so.

Speaking in an interview with Luppen TV, the former Real Madrid midfielder named David Raya and William Saliba as Arsenal's sole 'world-class' players, snubbing Rice in the process.

"I’m also in agreement that Arsenal are a world-class team for me, but not one studded with tons of world-class players,” he said.

Kroos added that he agreed with compatriots Mats Hummels and Kramer's take on Prime that Saliba is 'absolutely world-class', before disagreeing with the pair, who claimed that the Frenchman is the only Gunners player who falls into this category: “So, I’m not quite there, because I think, for example, Raya is also a world-class goalkeeper.".

Asked about Rice and Martin Odegaard, the German stated the pair are 'a bit behind that, but not as absolute world-class'.

"I believe that it is a world-class team in how they play together and how they function together. They’ve shown that.”.

Just last month, Rice was nominated for the Ballon d'Or, with the only other midfielders who were up for the award being Rodri, Fabian Ruiz, Jude Bellingham, Vitinha, Joao Neves and Bruno Fernandes.

Clearly, the general perception is that Rice is among the best few players in his position in the world, which would qualify him for a 'world-class' tag in many people's eyes.

Equally, Gabriel Magalhaes was also nominated for the Ballon d'Or, and was also snubbed by Kroos, while Bukayo Saka wasn't mentioned at all by the legendary deep-lying playmaker.
