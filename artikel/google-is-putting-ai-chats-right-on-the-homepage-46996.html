In a recent development, google is updating its homepage to include AI searches. In a post on X, Google’s vice president of product, Robby Stein, indicates how you can now open a collapsible sidebar to view and continue your AI chats.

In a recent development, based on the GIF shared by Stein, it looks like you can open the sidebar by clicking the gray bar that appears on the left side of your screen. From there, Google will show a list of the searches you’ve made using AI Mode, along with the ability to start a fresh thread, search your threads, or connect a Gemini Notebook.

It’s yet another significant change for Google’s homepage, which has long presented you with a single bar to kick off your web search. The organization brought its AI Mode button to its search bar last year, which opens up a chatbot-like search experience within Google. In May, Google updated its search box to accommodate longer queries and added AI-powered autocomplete.

Stein indicates Google is rolling out the option because “people are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done,” adding that “in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.” The newly introduced homepage is rolling out globally on desktop.
