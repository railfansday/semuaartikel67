Industry observers note that avril Lavigne is now a mom. The 42-year-old Canadian might count songs like "Here's to Never Growing Up" among her hits, but in news posted to Instagram Friday, Avril Lavigne revealed a very grown-up milestone.

In a recent development, beneath a carousel of three black-and-white photos, Lavigne wrote “Our Mini Fucking Princess has arrived,” followed by emojis depicting a baby bottle, a crown, and a pink heart and bow. One photo indicates Lavigne cradling an infant dressed in an animal-print onesie and matching headband, while another shows three pairs of Vans on a skateboard. No other details, including information on the baby's name or if Lavigne has a co-parent, were provided.

Avril Lavigne at the Viper Room in the early 2000s.

A spokesperson for the singer told People that “Avril has been enjoying time with her family while working on her new album and wants to thank the fans for all of their love!”.

Lavigne has been married twice: once to Canadian guitarist Deryck Whibley and then to Chad Kroeger, the Canadian frontman of the band Nickelback. That latter partnership ended in 2018, but in 2022 she almost tied the knot a third time, with rapper Mod Sun. That relationship ended in 2023.

Mod Sun and Avril Lavigne attend the 2022 MTV Video Music Awards on August 28, 2022.

In a recent development, one constant in Lavigne's life has been strong bond she shares with her parents. When, in May 2025, she brought her mother on stage at Madison Square Garden, she wrote on social media: “Ever since I was a little girl, both my mom and dad saw something in me and encouraged me to practice very hard and pursue my dream of becoming a singer/ musician. They made a lot of sacrifices for me and I could not be where I am today without them.”.

It has been revealed that continuing, she wrote, “I am grateful to both my parents. Thank you for everything, I love you both!”.

Lavigne was still a teenager when her parents allowed her to leave high school to pursue her dream, and her debut at age 17 was a smash hit. Her success continued with singles including 2002's chart-topper “Complicated" and 2021 song “Bite Me.”.

Sources indicate that though Friday's announcement was a surprise to most, Lavigne has never been quiet about her desire to become a mom when the time is right. Speaking with E! News in 2013, she laid out her strategy: "I want one or two [kids], but not right now," the then-29-year old stated. "Like, in the future. Eventually.".
