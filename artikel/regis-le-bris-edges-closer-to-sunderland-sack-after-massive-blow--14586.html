Regis Le Bris is at serious risk of losing his job at the Stadium of Light as Premier League relegation remains a danger.

The Black Cats have managed to move off the foot of the table, beating Chelsea 1-0 before the international break rolled around.

Enzo Le Fee's penalty made all the difference, and may have bought Le Bris a little more time to turn things around on Tyneside.

Stefan Borson told Football Insider that dropping out of the Europa League at an early stage would be beneficial for the club.

Having lost their last European game to Dinamo Zagreb, it is still very possible that Sunderland fail to secure qualification to the next stage.

That might be best if Le Bris wants to save his job, strangely, but now the manager has been dealt a new blow to those hopes.

The Black Cats have been without Dan Ballard, Habib Diarra and Romaine Mundle for quite some time already.

Granit Xhaka also joined that list, having managed just four minutes against Chelsea before having to be replaced due to injury.

Now, Brian Brobbey could be the latest casualty at the Stadium of Light after pulling out of international duty with the Netherlands.

Speaking to Football Insider, Mick Brown described the setback as a "massive blow" and one that could derail any Sunderland fightback.

It has been revealed that he argued that nobody else in the squad can bring what he does to the forward line, and he's absolutely right. Can Le Bris navigate this new blow?

Right now, there's no confirmed news about Brobbey's latest setback, but reports would suggest that he'll be sidelined for a few weeks again.

He already missed nearly a month of football, pulling up with an injury in the game right after his hat-trick against Manchester City in September.

This time, it's believed that the 24-year-old has suffered a tear in his upper leg, which could keep him out for several key matches.

Sunderland fans will be hoping that, once checked out by the club's doctors, the news won't be nearly as bad. But their season keeps going from bad to worse.
