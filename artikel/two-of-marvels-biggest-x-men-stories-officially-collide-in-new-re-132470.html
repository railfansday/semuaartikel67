Sources indicate that in the decades that Marvel has spent exploring alternate realities, few have presented such wildly different possibilities for mutantkind as House of M and Days of Future Past. The former saw Wanda Maximoff reshape reality, elevating mutants to positions of power and placing Magneto at the head of a royal family. The latter presented a horrifying future in which Sentinels had systematically decimated the mutant population. Naturally, Marvel has found a way to make life considerably worse for the inhabitants of both worlds.

With Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars on the horizon, Marvel Comics has been revisiting some of the major events that inspired the MCU’s upcoming crossover. Among them are the incursions that preceded Jonathan Hickman’s 2015 Secret Wars event, in which collisions between parallel universes threatened the existence of the entire multiverse. Marvel is now exploring what happened to some of its most recognizable alternate realities during those catastrophic events.

Marvel’s X-Men: Incursions #1, written by Alex Paknadel and illustrated by Jim Towe, is officially available following its November 18 release. The 32-page one-shot brings the worlds of House of M and Days of Future Past into direct conflict, giving Kate Pryde just eight hours to prevent both realities from being destroyed. The older version of Kitty Pryde finds herself exploring a world where mutants enjoy the freedoms she spent much of her life fighting for.

According to recent reports, X-Men: Incursions is the first of five one-shots revisiting the events leading up to Secret Wars. The next installment, Spider-Man: Incursions #1, pits Gwen Stacy against Mayday Parker, the daughter of Peter Parker and Mary Jane Watson from the MC2 universe. Fantastic Four: Incursions will also explore an alternate version of Marvel’s First Family with gamma-based powers. Perhaps the most exciting upcoming release for longtime X-Men fans is January’s X-Men ’92: Incursions, which brings together two generations of animated mutant heroes. The X-Men of the beloved 1990s animated series and its modern revival, X-Men '97, will collide with their younger counterparts from X-Men: Evolution, with writers Craig Kyle and Christopher Yost behind the crossover.

X-Men: Incursions #1 is available now for $4.99.
