Add 1½ lb. skinless, boneless chicken thighs (the miso and soy sauce are plenty salty, so no need to season the chicken) and toss to coat. Arrange on wire rack set inside a rimmed baking sheet lined with foil and spoon any marinade remaining in bowl over. Broil chicken, turning halfway through, until cooked through and deeply charred in spots, 10–15 minutes (keep a close eye on it). Thinly slice chicken. Serve over steamed white rice, topped with thinly sliced scallions, if desired.
