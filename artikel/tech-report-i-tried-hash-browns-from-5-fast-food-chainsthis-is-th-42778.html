Tech Report: I Tried Hash Browns From 5 Fast Food Chains—This Is The One I’d Wake Up Early For

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

People across the country take breakfast very seriously—after all, many believe it's the most important meal of the day. When you're rushing to work, grabbing something on the go is often the easiest option. And nothing screams breakfast quite like a side of hash browns. When a diner isn't in the cards, a fast food chain can be just the ticket.

According to recent reports, I tried hash browns from five different fast food chains to see which one serves up the best during the morning rush. Of the chains I tried, only Dunkin' serves hash browns all day. Everywhere else, you'll need to order during breakfast hours, which vary by location.

This proved to be one of the tougher taste tests I've conducted during my time at Delish. There were truly no terrible options, and some of the differences were pretty small. Here's how they stacked up.

Burger King's hash browns come in a handy carton filled with plenty of small, round crisps. They have a crunchy exterior and, honestly, are perfectly adequate. They're just up against some tough contenders. Of all the spuds I tried, these were the least memorable and had the least flavor, though nothing about them was off-putting.

Industry observers note that taco Bell served the most substantial hash brown on this list—it was quite hefty. The exterior had a nice crispiness, but compared to its competitors, the inside was much mushier, and the whole thing was noticeably greasier. It's not something I could eat a lot of.

In a recent development, dunkin's hash browns, which are served all day (!), were a bit more divisive in the Delish office. Some people stood ten toes down for the zesty onion flavor, while others (myself included) found it more reminiscent of leftover grease on a stove. That said, the texture was great, and whether you love them or hate them, these had the boldest flavor and stood out from the rest of the pack.

Industry observers note that like Taco Bell, McDonald's offers a larger hash brown patty than the others on this list. The chain totally nails the crispy outer edges and boasts great potato flavor. You can really tell this is McDonald's because the taste is so similar to the chain's iconic fries. If you like those, you'll love its hash browns. However, because of the patty's size, the middle lost some of the structure and crispiness I was looking for.

In a surprise win, Chick-fil-A's hash browns came out on top. The bite-sized pieces are easy to grab on the go, and their small size gives each one the perfect mix of a crispy exterior and soft interior. The flavor is spot-on, with savory onion and pepper notes. Best of all, they weren't overly greasy.
