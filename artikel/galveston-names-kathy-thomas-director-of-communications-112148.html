📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Galveston Wharves has announced the appointment of Kathy Thomas as its director of communications, formalizing a leadership role she has supported as a consultant to the port for the past seven years.

“Over the past seven years, Thomas has become an integral part of our team and has developed a deep understanding of our operations, our priorities and the important role the port plays in Galveston and the Texas economies,” said Rodger Rees, Galveston Wharves’ port director and CEO.

“As our port continues to grow and diversify, effective communications are more crucial than ever. Bringing Thomas onto our leadership team is a natural next step and will strengthen our ability to tell the port’s story, build relationships and keep our community and stakeholders informed.”.

Thomas leads the port’s communications and public relations efforts, overseeing strategic communications, media relations, crisis communications, community outreach, special events and digital content.

In a press release, the port mentioned that she plays a key role in communicating the port’s growing cruise, cargo and commercial operations; major capital development projects; and economic contributions to the region.

During her years as a consultant, Thomas crafted the port’s business-based strategic communications structure, directed the port’s 200th anniversary, released the port’s new website and organized several cruise, cargo and public engagement events.

It has been revealed that thomas brings communications experience to her role. As owner of Community Strategies, LLC, she provided a range of services to major energy, petrochemical and health care organizations.

It has been revealed that she began her career in journalism at the Galveston County Daily News, where she was managing editor. She holds a Bachelor of Journalism degree from The University of Texas at Austin.
