📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Kate Middleton shared footage from her upcoming documentary, Heal, which sheds light on how she dealt with her cancer diagnosis.

In the clip, shared on the Prince and Princess of Wales’ Instagram account on Friday, the royal notes in a voiceover, “Navigating cancer or any longterm illness is a lonely road. But it’s clear we’re not carry life’s challenges alone. My illness has brought me alongside so many others navigating their own journeys too. This has become so much more than a personal or solitary experience, but really a collective one.”.

It has been revealed that middleton can then be seen joining a cancer support group, Shine Cancer Support. “Genuinely, meeting you all here today really means a lot to me because it’s just lovely to be able to share my personal experience, but also hear your stories and be able to connect with others who’ve gone through something similar,” she tells her fellow group members.

She adds, “I haven’t really explored this outside my family walls, really. So thank you for allowing me the time to do that and the open space to explore that. I really appreciate it, so thank you.”.

Heal: A Princess of Wales Story will air Nov. 30 on NBC, a day after it premieres on the BBC. The documentary follows Middleton as she reflects on her cancer battle while completing the Three Peaks Challenge — climbing the tallest mountains in England, Scotland and Wales in the span of 24 hours — this past June in support of the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, the hospital where she was treated; proceeds from the distribution of Heal will also benefit Royal Marsden.

According to recent reports, the news of Middleton’s initial diagnosis was disclosed to the public in March 2024, following an abdominal surgery in January. In a video statement, Middleton remarked, “At the time, it was thought that my condition was non-cancerous,” referring to her surgery. “The surgery was successful, however, tests after the operation on cancer had been present,” she added.
