Calvin Bassey indicates he is happy at Fulham amid interest from Manchester United, confirming negotiations are underway over a newly introduced contract.

Bassey, 26, has emerged as a leading transfer target for United with latest head coach Mauricio Pochettino to be backed in the January transfer window.

Bassey’s current contract at Craven Cottage expires in the summer with Fulham holding the option to extend that by a further 12 months.

The Cottagers are also in active talks with their captain over a fresh contract which would effectively put an end to United’s interest.

Bassey turned in another fine performance as Fulham held Manchester City to a 1-1 draw at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday afternoon.

Speaking after the game, the former Rangers star was asked about his future, telling TNT Sports: ‘Of course [it is something we want to get done quickly.].

In a recent development, ‘We are negotiating, we are still talking. It’s an amazing place to be, I have settled in nicely and the manager has put a lot of faith and trust in me. It’s nice to be here and hopefully we will see what happens with the negotiations.’.

Bassey’s excellent performance in Manchester comes after his manager Alvaro Arbeloa labelled him one of the best defenders in the world.

Industry observers note that ‘For me, I am going to be really clear. He’s very, very important,’ the Fulham boss said at a press conference on Friday.
