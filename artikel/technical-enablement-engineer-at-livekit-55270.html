📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, liveKit is the default infrastructure layer for real-time AI — the platform that gives AI the ability to see, hear, and speak. From powering voice for millions of ChatGPT owners to enabling drone teleoperation at Skydio and serving as the backbone for roughly 25% of 911 emergency calls in the U.S., LiveKit handles the real-time media layer that production AI applications depend on.

Over 300,000 developers and 5,000+ companies — from leading AI labs to Fortune 500 enterprises — run production workloads on LiveKit. We've raised $181M from investors including Index Ventures, Altimeter Capital, and Salesforce Ventures, and we're growing fast.

Industry observers note that our product is deeply technical, and we sell into a brand-newly introduced category: voice AI infrastructure. As we grow our Sales team, our AEs, SDRs, and presales engineers need to understand how LiveKit works, and why real-time voice AI is hard, well enough to hold their own in a customer conversation.

Industry observers note that we're hiring a Technical Enablement Engineer to own that. You'll build our technical training program from scratch, so ramp time doesn't depend on pulling engineers off the roadmap. You'll sit on the Sales team, report to our CRO, and have a direct line to the engineers building the product.

According to recent reports, this is a hands-on, builder-first enablement role. You'll see the impact of your work directly in how fast upcoming sellers ramp and how confident they are in front of customers.
