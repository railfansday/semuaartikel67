Capcom has revealed Mega Man: Dual Override Boss Design Contest winner Cleanser Man, who will appear in the game as one of its Robot Master bosses.

Industry observers note that cleanser Man is a cleaning robot with an obsession for pristine beauty. Fueled by an extremely sensitive dirt sensor, Cleaner Man’s ideal world is one without a speck of dust. However, his devotion to cleanliness has a cost. His aggressive scrubbing causes great damage to global landmarks and historic works of art. This fixation has caused the public to view him as more of a threat than a helping hand. Furious and feeling misunderstood, Cleanser Man seizes control of trash processing facility and uses it to pollute the environment with hopes of forcing the world to appreciate the importance of cleanliness.

Boss Design Contests are a longtime Mega Man series tradition dating back to 1988 with Mega Man 2, and Cleanser Man carries that legacy forward as the latest Robot Master designed by a member of the Mega Man community.

Mega Man: Dual Override is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store in spring 2027.
