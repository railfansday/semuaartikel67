All About Lana Del Rey’s Husband, Jeremy Dufrene

  • 26, 2024, People reported that the couple obtained a marriage certificate.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

On Saturday, Sept. 7, 2024, Lana Del Rey officially debuted her relationship with Jeremy Dufrene, bringing him as her plus one to the wedding of model Karen Elson and Lee Foster in Fresh York City. The couple walked out of Electric Lady Studios hand-in-hand wearing substantial smiles.

Del Rey was also wearing a satin blue halter dress with white ballet flats while Dufrene wore a blue suit with a white button-up shirt and brown shoes.

Weeks later, they got married. On Sept. 26, 2024, People reported that the couple obtained a marriage certificate. They had their ceremony that same day. Here’s everything to know about Jeremy Dufrene, Del Rey’s husband.

Dufrene operates as a captain for a tour boat company in Des Allemands, Louisiana, close to New Orleans, called Airboat Tours by Arthur. The tours focus on local wildlife and apparently the company has a good reputation. In his bio, Dufrene indicates he once worked at a chemical plant and enjoys shrimping.

“After a little convincing by his family, he got his captain’s license and started running tours,” it continues.

According to People, Dufrene has three children, a son and two daughters.

Sources indicate that del Rey made an appearance in one of Dufrene’s social media posts in 2019 after taking a tour of his while visiting for the BUKU Music + Art Project festival. In a now private post on Instagram, Dufrene wrote, “I never know who I can run across on my tours but always a pleasure @lanadelrey.”.

She also shared shots of him in Facebook posts, captioning them, “Jeremy lemme be captain at Arthur’s Air Boat Tours.”.

Recent findings suggest that in May 2024, Del Rey reportedly took another tour. She tagged him in the caption of a photo with friends.

In August 2024, they were spotted at Reading & Leeds Festival and were seen in a video on TikTok holding hands. They then headed to London for a visit to Harrod’s and a local pub. After the images circulated, Del Rey denied she and Dufrene were dating in a comment on TikTok, simply writing “No,” on a video about their alleged relationship. It ended up later being a yes.

Recent findings suggest that people reported on Sept. 26, 2024, that the couple obtained a marriage license from the Lafourche Parish Clerk of Court on September 23. They had 30 days from that date to tie the knot.

Industry observers note that the same day as People broke the news, Del Rey and Dufrene got married in Des Allemandes, Louisiana. They had their ceremony at the bayou where Dufrene works. Photos surfaced of Del Rey walking with her father there, wearing a ruffle neckline gown with a full skirt. She had a blue ribbon tied in her hair, which was styled in a side ponytail.

Another source told People that the couple chose a place close to their hearts for the ceremony.

In a recent development, “The wedding ceremony and reception were both held in the same bayou where Jeremy operates his swamp boats tours,” stated the insider. “This is where they first met. It’s a special place for them. It was a beautiful, relaxed and family-focused wedding. The focus was just on their love story.”.

The source shared more about their love story, saying that Del Rey and Dufrene “initially met a few years ago the first time and then reconnected earlier this year.”.

They added, “It’s been kind of a whirlwind romance, but Lana’s close friends hoped she would get married. Jeremy is different from the men that Lana meets in the entertainment world. He’s a great guy. He’s charming and charismatic in a Southern way, very much a gentleman and he treats Lana really well. She’s an old soul.”.

Del Rey reportedly found her dress for the ceremony at a local boutique, Vintage Market by Trashy Diva, in Newly introduced Orleans, and purchased it that May—months before she went public with her and Dufrene’s relationship.

A source told TMZ that it’s “unclear whether Lana strolled into the store specifically hunting for a wedding dress, but after trying on a bunch of other stuff, she ended up falling for the dress she spotted in the window.”.

The insider didn’t know the price, but they estimated that $400 is the “typical price range for wedding dresses at the store, which features curated styles from the 1920s to Y2K.”.

On October 25, Del Rey attended the InStyle Imagemaker Awards and was asked about her marriage for the first time publicly.

It has been revealed that she simply replied, “It’s good. We’re happy.”.

According to recent reports, on Saturday, December 7, Del Rey attended Variety’s Hitmakers Brunch and spoke in honor of producer Jack Antonoff as he was named Producer of the Decade. During her speech, Del Rey opened up about her husband and how Antonoff’s relationship with his then-wife, Margaret Qualley, inspired her to connect with Dufrene.

Recent findings suggest that “Most of all, I’m grateful for him for his example that he gave me in his marriage to Margaret,” the singer noted, after thanking him for his work on her music. “I saw the way he looked at her when he met her, and he’s a big reason why I waited so long to get married and why I met my amazing husband.”.

She also admitted that having a high-profile career can make finding an authentic partner difficult.

“I really feel like there’s kind of a singer’s curse around meeting an honest partner who has no skin in the game, no dog in the race,” she remarked. “And it’s super nerve-racking to have to hold onto an innocent perception of how things could go when you’re in an industry where maybe your values or your morals don’t quite match up with what’s going on.”.

Del Rey added, “Especially when people think that you probably don’t have any morals or values.”.

She then thanked Antonoff for “maybe helping other people look at me in a way that is a little bit closer to how I perceive myself.”.

In a carousel of pictures posted on April 4, the singer revealed some intimate moments with Dufrene. She was also celebrating upcoming demonstrates at Stagecoach music festival. In the caption, she wrote, “This has been such a crazy nine months and I’m so blessed that come the 25th, It will have been a year since I got to headline Coachella. Thank you to my buddies over there at the festival for letting me play my new songs this year for you—and to my amazing band helping me kick things off with rehearsals this week.”.

It has been revealed that the carousel included a picture from a birthday party where she and Dufrene are wearing matching tiaras, as well as the crocodile-themed birthday cake referencing Dufrene’s business. There was also a sweet photo of Dufrene embracing Del Rey from behind.

The couple seemed to be enjoying Fresh York City in early spring and summer, based on a few posts from Del Rey. On May 7, she tagged Dufrene in a few clips and photos from a jazz club, writing they brought “a little taste of New Orleans” to the city.

On June 8, she shared a carousel of photos, many taken in Fresh York City, including a mirror selfie with a bunch of friends where it looks like Dufrene is standing behind her.

During her tour stop in Cardiff, Wales, on June 23, Del Rey made her affection for Dufrene very public. While performing “Stars Fell on Alabama,” she briefly stepped to the side of the stage to kiss her husband. After the moment of PDA, she returned to the mic and resumed singing.

Recent findings suggest that on July 31, 2025, Del Rey offered a rare glimpse of her and Dufrene’s private September 2024 wedding. She posted a photo from the ceremony on Instagram, featuring herself in a flowy white gown with a bow in her hair as she held hands with Dufrene, who wore a black suit.

Recent findings suggest that on September 26, Del Rey posted a carousel of images from her wedding to celebrate their anniversary. She included shots with Dufrene posing on an airboat and with friends and family, including Jack Antonoff and Margaret Qualley.

On October 5, Del Rey and Dufrene stepped out together for Valentino’s Paris Fashion Week runway show, marking one of their few public appearances since getting married. The couple arrived holding hands, with Del Rey wearing a lilac floor-length gown accented with a bow at the waist, while Dufrene opted for a navy jacket and matching trousers with tan boots.

Inside the event, the pair shared a kiss from their front-row seats.

Recent findings suggest that del Rey brought Dufrene as her date to the Ralph Lauren fashion show on Feb. 10, 2026. In a sweet photo from the event, she smiled happily while hanging onto her husband’s arm.

On February 17, Del Rey posted a series of Instagram Stories tied to the release of her newly introduced single, “White Feather Hawk Tail Deer Hunter.” One clip opened with footage from a cartoon, before cutting to a video of Del Rey and Dufrene dancing as the song played.

In the clip, she wore a brown sweater and an ivory skirt while Dufrene stood behind her in a khaki jacket. Del Rey released “White Feather Hawk Tail Deer Hunter” in full that day, tagging Dufrene in the announcement post as a co-writer on the track.

According to recent reports, on March 27, Del Rey posted upcoming pictures with Dufrene from their life together, including a trip to Home Depot, time on his boat, and flipping through wedding book memories. In the caption, she wrote, “Happy birthday week to the sweetest guy I know ❤️🦆”.

The couple was spotted in Los Angeles on April 17, enjoying a lunch date at The Ivy restaurant. Del Rey was wearing a striped shirt under an off-the-shoulder sweatshirt with denim shorts and ballet shoes. Dufrene was in a white T-shirt, khakis, and a black baseball hat. They appeared to have been out shopping. Del Rey carried her leather purse, and Dufrene had a shopping bag.

On August 27, Del Rey and Dufrene were photographed on a shopping trip in Los Angeles, where they stopped by a Ralph Lauren store.

Del Rey wore a white tank top with straight-leg jeans and an open light-wash denim shirt, finishing her look with white sneakers. Dufrene kept things casual in a gray T-shirt, black shorts, sneakers, a camouflage baseball cap, and sunglasses.

On September 27, Del Rey celebrated two years of marriage to Dufrene with an Instagram carousel featuring memories from their relationship, including footage of the couple kissing during their wedding ceremony.

It has been revealed that “Hi husband, happy second anniversary... Thank you for making everywhere I’ve ever lived feel like home again. And also to our lil fam...literally everything we do is the best. Xo,” she wrote.

Sources indicate that dufrene shared his own anniversary tribute, writing, “Happy Anniversary to the most Beautiful and Precious lil wife. I love you so much.”.

Industry observers note that on November 20, Del Rey shared a series of photos from a Grand Theft Auto VI launch party in Miami, where she was joined by her husband. “Cruisin,” she captioned the Instagram carousel, which included photos of the couple posing together at the event.

In a recent development, for the occasion, Del Rey carried a custom pink Hermès Birkin inspired by the video game and created by Privé Porter. The bag featured Swarovski-crystal designs referencing Vice City, the fictional Miami-inspired setting of the game. Del Rey also has a song, “Crazy for You,” on the Grand Theft Auto VI soundtrack, and she and Dufrene reportedly voice fictional radio hosts in the game.
