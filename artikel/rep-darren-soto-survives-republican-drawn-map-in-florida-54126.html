Florida Rep. Darren Soto, here at a hearing in July, has won a sixth term representing the 9th District. (Bill Clark/CQ Roll Call).

In a recent development, florida Rep. Darren Soto, the most vulnerable House Democrat running for reelection, narrowly won a sixth term Tuesday in a district that state Republicans redrew in their favor.

Sources indicate that soto was leading Republican Navy veteran Dan Green by less than a point when The Associated Press called the race for Central Florida’s 9th District a week after Election Day. His margin of victory was above the 0.5 percent threshold that would trigger a recount under state law.

Industry observers note that house Democrats have so far flipped more than 40 seats and appear on track to hold their biggest majority in the chamber in decades. Soto’s win means that no House Democratic incumbent lost to a Republican in the general election this year. And Florida Democrats defended all four seats the GOP had hoped to pick up under the state’s newly introduced Republican-drawn map.

Industry observers note that democratic Reps. Kathy Castor and Jared Moskowitz both won reelection from districts that shifted red, while Democrat Pia Dandiya, a former high school principal, defeated Republican Marine Corps veteran Casey Askar for a redrawn open seat in South Florida.

Soto, who declared victory last week, had been seen as the most vulnerable of the targeted Florida Democrats. The new map reduced the Hispanic population of his district as the seat shifted from one that Kamala Harris would have carried in 2024 to one that Donald Trump would have taken by 18 points.

It has been revealed that “Despite the Florida Republican legislature’s efforts to redraw me out of this district, my record of delivering for Florida showed voters that I am a reliable fighter for them in Congress,” Soto remarked in a Nov. 4 statement.

In a Tuesday memo detailing how House Democrats secured their historic majority, the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee took aim at Republicans’ mid-decade redistricting efforts, saying their “attempts to steal seats … supremely backfired.”.

In a recent development, while Florida Republicans were unsuccessful in flipping the four Democrat-held seats, the party held all of its House seats in the Sunshine State. Republican Rep. María Elvira Salazar narrowly won reelection in the Miami-area 27th District over Democratic challenger Eliott Rodriguez, the AP reported Tuesday. In the 7th District outside Orlando, Republican former TV news anchor Ryan Elijah defeated Democratic Navy veteran Bale Dalton, a former NASA chief of staff who conceded Tuesday.
