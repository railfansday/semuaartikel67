It has been revealed that there's a bull market... in fear.

Recent findings suggest that faced with a barrage of headlines from nationwide tech outages to Nvidia earnings and Anthropic's initial public offering, traders on Tuesday piled into protective put options on the S&P 500 Index at a rate that suggests fear beyond just an AI-specific correction.The Cboe Volatility Index (VIX) surged nearly 5 points to a high of 27.7 at the worst of Tuesday's selling and the ratio of put to call volumes on the SPDR S&P 500 ETF (SPY) hit 1.5, the highest since the first week of April, according to Barchart data. The 'VIX' was last still hovering above 26.With the VIX climbing four days in a row, the volatility gauge hasn't traded under 16 since Oct. 16, arguably trending higher as the S&P 500 slips below this month's lows, trading under 7,300 for the lowest price for the benchmark equity gauge since June. Cboe's three-month volatility gauge, VIX3M, briefly touched the highest since April.

According to recent reports, while Nvidia earnings are contributing to the uncertainty – the stock's options currently imply more than a 7% move after its report, the biggest in almost two years – volatility in the S&P 500 jumped more than in the Nasdaq 100 Tuesday, pointing to a more widespread fear among investors."SPX vol is expanding faster than NDX vol, as SPX vol is coming from a lower base," Eric Metz, CIO, SpiderRock Advisors for BlackRock, said via email. "Today, the market is pricing acute near-term stress in large-caps broadly, but tech-specific fear is still notable.Even within the technology cohort, Tuesday's laggards took on a different tone. Software stocks declined the most ahead of Anthropic's IPO, with options volume on the iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV) surging to almost four times the 30-day average, according to Cboe LiveVol data. Salesforce was hardest hit, with put contracts outpacing calls on twice the average options volume."There's a lot of reasons to be risk-off," said Noel Smith, co-CIO of Kai Asset Management. "This is a market-wide phenomenon today that's not just AI stocks."To be sure, bulls weren't totally absent from the session, with the Nasdaq 100 Index holding its November low and VIX still under its Nov. 5 high of 28.4."While vol is now at a relatively elevated level, we have not seen much fear in the options market," said VolSignals' Matthew Nadel, citing fixed-strike volatility in S&P 500 options, which measures volatility changes across specific options strikes. "We have seen a pretty decent move higher in SPX fixed strike vol, however the move up in vol has been mostly orderly.".
