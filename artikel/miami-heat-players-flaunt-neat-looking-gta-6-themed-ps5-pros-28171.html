The Miami Heat is presently fourth in the Eastern Conference but number one in terms of custom Grand Theft Auto 6 PlayStation 5 Pros received. The NBA team has been part of a high-profile and seemingly fairly expensive marketing campaign for Rockstar Games’ latest blockbuster, and tonight members of the team shared images and footage of special-edition consoles no one else can get.

I wouldn’t normally take the time to comment on a marketing stunt wrapped up inside of another marketing stunt, but these Vice City PS5 Pros look very cool. Plenty of fans think so too, and have been left wondering why the custom PS5 Pro plates aren’t available to buy online. This comes after the actual $400 Collector’s Box fans can buy was mostly ridiculed for being full of tchotchkes fans weren’t really into.

There’s a black and a grey model. Both come in a sort of Pelican-style briefcase complete with two matching GTA 6-edition DualSense controllers and a copy of the physical game box, i.e. a piece of plastic with a code inside. The PS5 Pro’s three black ribbons that cut through the middle of the plates are a backlit teal-to-magenta gradient that looks very retro and cool. Palm trees are etched into the plastic. I wouldn’t buy one, but I could totally understand people who would.

Recent findings suggest that except they aren’t for sale. These appear to be limited-edition PS5 Pro models made custom for the stars of the Miami Heat. There have been a number of fans pointing out that that seems like a waste. Not to mention PS5 Pro owners already rarely get new custom plates. The recently declared Prism Collection featuring translucent covers is for the base and slim PS5 models only.

In the meantime, Kaseya Center, where the Miami Heat play, has undergone a full Vice City makeover.
