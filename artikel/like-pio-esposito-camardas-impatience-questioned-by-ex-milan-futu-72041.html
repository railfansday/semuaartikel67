Massimo Oddo has admitted that Francesco Camarda has unquestionable talent, but suggested there has been some impatience regarding his development.

Industry observers note that going into the 2025-26 season, the decision was made to send Camarda out on loan to Lecce, where he had a mixed campaign to say the least. He did score his first ever Serie A goal, but played second fiddle for large spells until a shoulder injury saw him miss an extended period.

Ruben Amorim arrived and decided that he wished to keep the 18-year-old around, believing that he is ready to be the deputy to Goncalo Ramos, the new club-record signing. The Portuguese is struggling for goals, while Camarda is struggling for minutes, leading to suggestions of a new loan in January.

Oddo knows Camarda well having been the head coach of Milan Futuro during their first season in Serie C and then in Serie D following their relegation. Speaking to La Repubblica, he gave his take on the forward’s current situation.

“The talent hasn’t disappeared, but you have to be patient. I take Camarda as an example, one of the most promising centre-forwards I’ve ever seen,” he remarked (via Calciomercato.com).

Recent findings suggest that “He couldn’t wait and paid the price for his early leap to Serie A. He should have taken a more gradual path, like Pio Esposito. In general, I’m concerned about the excessive power of agents.”.
