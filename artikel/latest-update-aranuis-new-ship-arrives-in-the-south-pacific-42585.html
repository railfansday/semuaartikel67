The new Aranoa arrived in the South Pacific earlier this month following its delivery in China.

“After years of hard work and a long journey across the ocean, our brand-upcoming passenger-freighter has finally arrived in Tahiti,” the firm said in a social media enhancement.

“A new adventure begins for our organization! Soon, the Aranoa will set sail for the Austral Islands, carrying passengers and cargo while continuing the remarkable story already written by the Aranui 5,” Aranui added.

In a recent development, as reported by Cruise Industry News, the latest ship was delivered by Huanghai Shipbuilding in late September. The 7,000-ton ship is certified for 197 passengers and 103 crew, and has a cargo capacity for 48 TEUs.

Joining the Aranui 5 in the region, the Aranoa will offer cargo-carrying cruises to the Austral Islands starting in March 2027.

Sailing from Papeete, the vessel’s regular 13-night itinerary will include seven ports of call, including Raiatea, Bora Bora and Rurutu.

Aranui mentioned that the voyage is highlighted by hidden gems, which offer turquoise lagoons, fjord-like coastlines and more.

Also sailing to the Marquesas Islands, Picairns, Gambier and more, the Aranoa is set to offer 18 cruises during its inaugural season in 2027.

The ship was designed as a mixed passenger-freighter vessel, equipped with the latest technology to minimize its environmental impact.

In addition to a cargo area, the Aranoa offers over 90 guest staterooms, of which nearly 80 percent feature private balconies.

Guests can also take advantage of a series of public areas, including a lounge, a bar, two restaurants and jacuzzies.

According to Huanghai Shipbuilding, the ship was designed by SDARI, the Shanghai Merchant Ship Design and Research Institute, a unit of China State Shipbuilding Corporation.
