Sources indicate that rockstar Games will not be forced to formally recognize a union at the Grand Theft Auto 6 studio after its developers based in the UK voted overwhelmingly against the initiative. The weeks-long voting process played out as Rockstar launched the open-world hit to over 30 million sales in its first day.

In a recent development, voting began on November 12 and concluded today on November 26. Workers at Rockstar Games voted 75 percent against the recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union. The initiate would have formalized collective bargaining efforts around things like overtime, bonuses, layoffs, and AI. Developers in favor of unionizing can still work with the IGWB, but there will be no regular meetings with management to hash out new policies.

Industry observers note that “We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we’ve built together and the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6,” Rockstar head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe stated in a statement shared with Kotaku. “This result reflects our team’s strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong.
