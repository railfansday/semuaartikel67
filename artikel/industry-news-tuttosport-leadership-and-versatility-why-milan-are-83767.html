Bruno Fernandes seems to be emerging as more and more of a concrete target for AC Milan in the coming months.

Owner Gerry Cardinale has already laid out the guidelines for the next transfer window (and future ones): he is fully committed to investing in strong players, regardless of age, with the aim of returning to competing for trophies.

Milan needd to bring in both talented young players who can shape the future squad, as well as players who can provide the backbone in terms of leadership and experience.

Milan are not only looking for players like Endrick and Nicolo Tresoldi, therefore, but also those who can come into the dressing room and help younger players. The impact Zlatan Ibrahimovic and Simon Kjaer made on the last title-winning team is a good example of this.

Among the names on the shortlist, as reported by Tuttosport, is Bruno Fernandes. His contract with Manchester United is expiring at the end of the season, and the belief is that he could help fill the technical and experience void if Luka Modric departs.

Fernandes is an opportunity to be monitored closely, especially since the 32-year-old Portuguese would like to return to Italy, a country he has already frequented in his career given his experiences with Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria.

In a recent development, bruno is negotiating a possible renewal with the Red Devils but the Manchester club want to propose a new contract with a lower salary than his current one (around €10-11m per season). There is a €65m release clause in his current contract, the paper adds.

It has been revealed that this morning’s edition of the paper adds that the Portuguese star appeals because of his versatility, too. He can play as a deeper-lying midfield player – as he did under Ruben Amorim at Old Trafford – as a box-to-box player and as an attacking midfielder.

According to recent reports, the latter is perhaps his preferred role, especially now that he is into his 30s. Interestingly, Amorim has switched from being locked into the 3-4-2-1 seen at United to now using more of a 4-2-3-1 in possession, so in theory there could be an ideal role waiting for Fernandes.
