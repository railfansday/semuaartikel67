Instagram now lets you edit stories, but you have to act fast. Here’s how to do it

🌐 Source: Fastcompany • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (473 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that for anyone who has ever hit share, only to spot a typo moments later, Instagram now has a fix for that. The Meta-owned platform is rolling out a feature that lets you edit a story after you post it. Instagram head Adam Mosseri announced the newly introduced feature Wednesday in a post on his Instagram feed. “A lot of people have been asking for this, so I’m excited to get this one out there,” he shared in the post. You can edit text, music, stickers, and drawings, but there’s one catch: you have to act fast. You can only make edits to a story within 15 minutes of sharing. You can fix typos and other mistakes without losing your views, likes, or replies. Previously, the only option besides leaving the story up was to delete it and repost. A few other details worth knowing  When you edit a story, Instagram adds an edited label, and your audience stays the same. Any previous reshares of the story get removed. According to Instagram, editing is not available on stories that were also shared to Facebook or contain certain stickers. How to edit an Instagram story: Open your published story. Tap the three dots “More” button. Select Edit story. Fix your text, or update stickers, drawing, or music. Save the changes. Instagram announced the feature along with other stories upgrades, including new sticker designs, an easier way to select multiple photos, and backgrounds for photos of any size.
