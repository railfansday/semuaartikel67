📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

EXCLUSIVE: OneTwoThree Media has acquired worldwide sales rights to One Of Us, the mystery-horror film starring Emmy winner Kit Connor (Heartstopper).

The second feature of writer-director Stefan van de Graaff (Simmer), One Of Us “begins at a funeral, where every hour on the hour a family member will die. As the body count grows, it is a race against time to unmask the stranger in their midst.”.

Recent findings suggest that connor, whose star is fast-ascending after Heartstopper, Warfare and being cast in A24’s upcoming Elden Ring and Marvel’s X-Men, leads an ensemble that includes Sienna Guillory (Resident Evil), Charlotte Hope (The Spanish Princess), Siobhan Fallon Hogan (Dancer in the Dark), Ian Beattie (Game of Thrones), and Douglas Henshall (Shetland). According to recent reports, shot on location in Belfast, the film comes from the Jung School in association with Northern Ireland Screen. Raquel Baldwin produces, with Robert Machoian co-producing and van de Graaff executive producing. The movie has played at the UK’s Manchester Film Festival and at Newport Beach and SCAD Savannah in the U.S. Watch on Deadline.

The acquisition was negotiated at the AFM by Alan Green on behalf of UK outfit OneTwoThree Media and Stefan van de Graaff on behalf of the filmmakers.

“Acquiring One Of Us adds to a hugely successful AFM market,” mentioned Alan Green, founder of OneTwoThree Media. “With X-Men and Elden Ring ahead of him, Kit is about to become one of the most recognisable young actors in the world, and this film puts him front and centre in a commanding lead performance. Stefan has delivered a striking, unsettling debut with a terrific cast.”.

Industry observers note that stefan van de Graaff added: “Partnering with OneTwoThree is extremely exciting for us as Alan and his team bring a very fresh, strategy-first approach to a sea of sameness amongst sales agents. Our film is so unique and the indie sales industry is shifting so rapidly under our feet; a sales partner who is both bespoke and innovative is no longer a “nice-to-have”. The partnership is a meaningful step toward releasing the film to the tens of thousands of people who have messaged me asking when they can see it!”.
