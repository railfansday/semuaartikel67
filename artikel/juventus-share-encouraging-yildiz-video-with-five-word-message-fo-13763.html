Juventus have shared a video of Kenan Yildiz training on the pitch, with the caption: ‘One step at a time, Kenan.’.

The Turkish talent has played only one competitive game this season, suffering an ankle injury in the opening Serie A match against Frosinone that required surgery.

On Tuesday, Juventus showed their star training separately with the ball on the pitch and captioned their social media post with an encouraging message: “One step at a time, Kenan.”.

Back in August, reports said Yildiz would return to action between November and December, but multiple sources now say the Turkishman is slightly behind schedule and won’t return before early 2027.
