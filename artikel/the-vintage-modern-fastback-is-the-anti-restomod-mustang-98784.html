Recent findings suggest that taking a vintage muscle car and swapping in modern components to make it run better or add power is a story as old as, well, the LS2 V-8. Vintage Modern’s newly introduced Fastback Mustang turns that formula upside down—by swapping old-school body parts onto a modern S650-generation Mustang GT. But the Vintage Modern Fastback, officially revealed on Wednesday at Retromobile New York, is more than just a body kit.

According to recent reports, for starters, Ford’s standard 5.0-liter V-8 is mated here to a 3.0-liter Whipple supercharger for a total output of 840 horsepower and 615 lb-ft of torque. Prospective buyers can choose between a six-speed manual transmission with rev matching or a 10-speed automatic with steering-wheel-mounted paddle shifters. Other high-end Mustang features like active-valve performance exhaust with four sound modes, MagneRide adaptive suspension, six selectable drive modes, and Brembo performance brakes are also standard fare.

Industry observers note that “The Vintage Fastback is a car we’ve always wanted to build. We’ve blended classic-inspired design with modern safety, technology, and performance,” said Chau Nguyen, founder and CEO of Vintage Modern. “This is what Vintage Modern was created to do.”.

Even though the bones of the Fastback are from a current-generation Mustang, the heavily modified vehicle still underwent crash testing to meet applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards. This retro-inspired Mustang features eight airbags, crumple zones, side-impact beams, electronic stability control, traction control, and anti-lock brakes. That’s more than most restomod Mustang owners can say.

Sources indicate that most of the custom body panels, including the functional hood scoop, are carbon fiber; the only obviously metal pieces are the billet-aluminum grille, door handles, mirrors, and twin auxiliary driving lights. Similarly, the interior of the retro Fastback build features Alcantara and billet-aluminum trim over three different seat upholsteries: pleated, diamond-quilted, or woven. Each buyer can choose from more than 40 standard paint colors, as well as paint-to-sample finishes and stripe options, along with their own interior configurations; Vintage Modern is also offering an optional JL Audio premium sound system and leather-trimmed floor mats.

Vintage Modern’s carbon-fiber body panels are hand-made in Detroit before being assembled and finished at its Atlanta facility; all told, each unit requires around 900 hours of hands-on labor. The builder has big looks, too; it says it has plans to move into a new factory three times the size of its existing facility, hire more than 100 additional team members, and triple production capacity within two years.

No pricing details about the Vintage Modern Fastback were available at the time of publication, but we’ll upgrade this story when we learn more.
