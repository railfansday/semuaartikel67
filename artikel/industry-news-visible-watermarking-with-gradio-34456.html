In a recent development, last year, we shared a blogpost on watermarking, explaining what it means to watermark generative AI content, and why it's crucial. The need for watermarking has become even more critical as people all over the world have begun to generate and share AI-generated images, video, audio, and text. Images and video have become so realistic that they’re nearly impossible to distinguish from what you’d see captured by a real camera. Addressing this concern is multi-faceted, but there is one, clear, low-hanging fruit 🍇:.
