It has been revealed that noise-canceling headphones were once a niche category, dominated by high-priced over-ear cans. Throughout most of the 2010s, those seeking to drown out ambient noise distractions at work or during travel had to spend upward of $300 to get a pair up to the task. These days, that's no longer the case. The market is jam-packed with quality noise-canceling headphones, including reliable models that start below $100. Having so many options to choose from can rightfully be overwhelming even for tech-savvy shoppers. To help you find the best noise-canceling headphones worth investing in, I spent months testing highly rated options from leading brands and created this buying guide to share my recommendations.

Noise cancellation relies on built-in microphones that continuously monitor your surroundings. A dedicated chip processes the sound captured by the microphones before sending its exact aural opposite to your ears via the headphones' audio drivers. This sophisticated technology naturally contributes to the cost of noise-canceling headphones. Translation? The more you spend, the better you'll isolate yourself from your surroundings. Prepare to drop north of $300 if you are looking to achieve (almost) complete silence from your purchase.

Sources indicate that high-quality sound is also a must for a great pair of noise-canceling headphones. Support for immersive spatial audio is particularly crucial for top-shelf headphones. It makes live albums and video content more immersive and the listening experience more lifelike, especially if dynamic head tracking is available. Spatial audio is available on most top-shelf noise-canceling headphones. High-end sets without this functionality make up for it by producing pristine stereo sound, which makes them worth investing in. Speaking of stereo sound, today's best headphones in this space, including budget-friendly models, support lossless audio over wireless and wired connections. If you are using a streaming service that offers lossless audio, keep an eye out for this feature. It will serve you well.

Sources indicate that your next set of headphones should get you at least 20 to 30 hours of wireless playback between power-ups with noise cancellation. That’s the bare minimum for these products, with class-leading sets by Apple, Bose, and Sony delivering battery endurance in this ballpark. That’s enough to get you through a long flight or hectic workday in silence.Remarkably, cheaper cans now have much longer battery life. Some will give you double the endurance of the class-leading options with noise cancellation and a three-digit number of hours without it. Those numbers are remarkable, but trust me, stronger noise cancellation and higher-quality sound are worth the more frequent charging sessions.
