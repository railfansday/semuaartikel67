Nissan expects to sell some hybrid-only models in the U.S. around 2030, on its own timeline

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, nissan anticipates introducing hybrid-only versions of certain models in the U.S. around 2030, a more gradual pace than Toyota $TM and Honda $HMC have set, according to Bloomberg.

In a recent development, "We're getting ready to be full hybrid, maybe in 2030, but I don't think it's necessary to do it too quickly," Christian Meunier, chairman of Nissan Americas, told reporters in Detroit on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg. "We need to put the technology on at the right time, and at the right cost," he mentioned.

The timeline covers only those nameplates where Nissan would drop the gas-only option entirely, according to The Associated Press. The AP said the company’s near-term priority is to get its hybrid system established with buyers, and only afterward settle on a pace for retiring conventional versions.

In a recent development, meunier stated buyers on tight budgets can still turn to gas-only vehicles, since certain hybrids cost $2,000 to $2,500 more, according to Bloomberg. He said the premium can shrink as production volume rises, as it has for Toyota.

Nissan is overhauling its plants and expanding its network of suppliers to support hybrid output, according to Bloomberg. It intends to start production of the completely new Xterra, a midsize SUV that will offer a hybrid variant, at its Canton, Mississippi, plant in 2028, Bloomberg mentioned.

Recent findings suggest that toyota already sells a number of models only as hybrids, among them the RAV4, Camry and Sienna, according to Bloomberg. Honda said last week it will sell the 2028 CR-V with a hybrid powertrain only when the seventh-generation model arrives next year.

Nissan unveiled the 2027 Rogue with its e-Power hybrid system in September. In that system, the gasoline engine generates electricity for the drive motors, and the car needs no external charger. The Rogue is Nissan's top seller in the U.S. and competes with the Toyota RAV4 and Honda CR-V, which Meunier named as the Rogue's direct target.

Sources indicate that nissan unveiled on Oct. 30 that a limited number of Rogue Hybrid SR models would reach dealerships in late November, with SV, SR and Platinum versions due in early 2027, according to the AP. The AP mentioned Nissan looks to show the Rogue Hybrid at the Los Angeles Auto Show, which runs from Nov. 20 to Nov. 29.
