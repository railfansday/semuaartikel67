Jude Bellingham can now boast a century of La Liga appearances as a Real Madrid player and he’s following in the footsteps of some of the all-time greats.

Recent findings suggest that with the form he’s in right now, you can imagine Bellingham staying at Real Madrid for hundreds more games. He’s doing justice to the number five shirt on his back, previously worn by Zinedine Zidane.

Zidane, by all accounts, was an idol for a young Bellingham. Never mind the fact that Bellingham was three years old when Zidane retired. This is one of the best players of all time we’re talking about, so of course there’ll be elements of Zidane’s game that Bellingham has learnt about and modelled himself on.

Recent findings suggest that now he’s making a name for himself at the Bernabeu and recently brought up the milestone of 100 La Liga appearances when Madrid beat Celta de Vigo 2-1 last weekend. Bellingham marked the occasion with a goal, his 39th in the Spanish top flight.

Sources indicate that and that means he’s scoring at a better rate than Zidane was by the same milestone. In fact, he’s already got two more La Liga goals than Zidane ever had, and he’s also four ahead of him in all competitions for Madrid.

However, Zidane is ahead of him for assists, even though Bellingham’s output on that front isn’t too shabby either.

In a recent development, the exciting thing here from the Bellingham perspective is the stage of their career each was at when they reached 100 league games for Madrid. Zidane got there in October 2004, aged 32 and already a previous Ballon d’Or winner. Bellingham is still only 23 and has so much to aspire to ahead of him.

Sources indicate that zidane ended up on 155 La Liga appearances before he retired in 2006. In a best-case scenario, Bellingham could have 200 to his name by the 2029 expiry date of his current contract and we suspect it won’t be his last at the Bernabeu.

Here’s how Bellingham compares to Zidane after 100 La Liga games for Real Madrid – and it looks pretty good….
