Apple TV+’s Presumed Innocent will be back for a second season, but Season 2 will diverge from the first installment.

Recent findings suggest that the legal drama series was renewed in July 2024 with its final two episodes still to launch on Apple TV+. Starting out as a limited series, the series will take on an anthology style like The White Lotus after becoming Apple’s most-watched show.

Read on for all the plot details, character casting and more about Presumed Innocent Season 2.

In a recent development, when will Presumed Innocent Season 2 come out? Watch on Deadline.

In November, Apple unveiled that the second season of the crime drama will premiere February 19, 2027 with its first two episodes. The rest will follow weekly through April 2.

Who will be in the cast of Presumed Innocent Season 2?

Rachel Brosnahan (The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, Superman) will lead Season 2 of the legal series alongside Jack Reynor (Midsommar, The Perfect Couple, whose casting Deadline reported exclusively. Matthew Rhys (The Americans, Perry Mason)was also cast as a lead in early September 2025, and Fiona Shaw (Bad Sisters, Killing Eve) was cast as a series regular Oct. 1. Still more names include Courtney B. Vance, John Magaro, Michael Hsu Rosen and Lesley-Ann Brandt.

Ji-young Yoo (KPop Demonhunters, Expats) will recur in Season 2 as Deadline revealed in January 2026.

According to recent reports, what happened in Presumed Innocent Season 1? Who was in the cast of Presumed Innocent Season 1?

Season 1 of the series was inspired by Scott Turow’s 1987 courtroom thriller novel of the same name. It starred Jake Gyllenhaal in the main role of Rusty Sabich, the character Harrison Ford played in the 1990 film adaptation of Turow’s best-seller.

The cast of Season 1 also included Bill Camp as Raymond Horgan, Renate Reinsve as Carolyn Polhemus, Ruth Negga as Barbara Sabich, O-T Fagbenle as Nico Della Guardia, Peter Sarsgaard as Tommy Molto, Elizabeth Marvel as Lorraine Horgan, Lily Rabe as Dr. Liz Rush, Chase Infiniti as Jaden Sabich and more.

What will Presumed Innocent Season 2 be about?

It has been revealed that as Deadline first reported, Season 2 will be based on Jo Murray’s debut legal thriller Dissection of a Murder, which will publish in 2026. The book centers Leila Reynolds, who is way out of her death when offered her first murder case by a defendant that only wants her. Complicating matters is the fact that her husband is the prosecutor.
