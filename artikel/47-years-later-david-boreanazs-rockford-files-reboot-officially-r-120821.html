The Rockford Files, one of the most beloved detective series in TV history, is returning to NBC in the Upcoming Year. David Boreanaz will step into the (gum)shoes of James Garner in the rebooted series. Jim Rockford was the only character who was set to return for the new series, but now we know that one of the most memorable characters from the original show is making a comeback.

It has been revealed that boreanaz stars as Jim Rockford, a detective who was framed for a crime he didn't commit. After a spell in jail, he's back on the streets to solve the cases of the downtrodden and the underdogs... for a reasonable fee, plus expenses. The series will also star Michaela McManus (Redux Redux), Ilfenesh Hadera (Godfather of Harlem), and Jacki Weaver (Silver Linings Playbook); it is showrun by Mike Daniels (Sons of Anarchy), who executive produces with Carl Beverly, Sarah Timberman, and David Hudgins.

It has been revealed that deadline reports that Brandon T. Jackson will recur on the new series, and his character should excite any old-school Rockford fans out there. He'll play Angel, a street hustler with a smart mouth who finds himself on the wrong side of a law. He turns to Rockford to clear his name, and soon finds a place in the detective's orbit. Jackson is an actor and stand-up comedian from Detroit, Michigan, who's starred in films like Tropic Thunder, Percy Jackson and the Lightning Thief, and I'm Beginning to See the Light. The series is set to premiere with a special preview episode on January 2, 2027, at 8 p.m. after NBC's NFL broadcast; it will then debut in its normal timeslot on Thursday, January 7, 2027, at 8 PM.

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

Played by Stuart Margolin (Death Wish), Evelyn "Angel" Martin was an associate of Jim Rockford's during his stint at San Quentin Prison. A small-time con man and grifter, Angel's scams constantly got him into trouble with the law or other criminals: trouble that Rockford often had to get him out of, usually at great risk to himself. First appearing in the pilot movie for the series, later renamed "Backlash of the Hunter," Angel would appear in several episodes each season and in all eight of the Rockford Files reunion movies. Margolin won two Emmy Awards for his performance as Angel.

The Rockford Files will premiere on NBC on January 2, 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
