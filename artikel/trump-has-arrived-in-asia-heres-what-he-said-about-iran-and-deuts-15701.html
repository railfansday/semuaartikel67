Trump has arrived in Asia. Here’s what he said about Iran and Deutsche Bank

According to recent reports, U.S. President Donald Trump has arrived for his high-stakes trip to Asia, and took a range of questions on subjects from Iran to Deutsche Bank leaking details about his tax.

The president landed in Manila, Philippines, for the initial leg of his trip on Tuesday and answered rapid-fire questions from the press while meeting with other leaders.

Asked about a possible Iranian threat to Washington, D.C., he said that, if there were an attack, Iran would "pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before.".

In a recent development, the question was about CNN reporting that U.S. intelligence suggested the country was planning to target senior members of Trump's administration with a dirty bomb or chemical weapon in the U.S. capital.

Industry observers note that "We take it very seriously because they happen to be crazy," Trump mentioned. CNBC has not independently verified CNN's report.

But the president also remarked he believed his administration was "very close to getting it done" when it came to the Iran war.

While Trump was airborne, the Financial Times reported Deutsche Bank had mistakenly leaked details of his taxes in March 2017, during his first presidential term. The mistake was kept from the White House at the time, per the FT.

Asked about this in Manila, the president said: "I paid a lot of tax.".

It has been revealed that "They shouldn't have done it. It's illegal for them to do it," he added.

Sources indicate that trump was also asked about Nvidia's announcement on Tuesday of a fresh National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee. The company, which reports earnings later on Tuesday, is reeling from the sudden departure of CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned last week after having an affair with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes to be a Chinese spy.

"It's a great company. It's a great idea," Trump remarked.

Trump is due to fly to Shenzhen, China, where he is scheduled to meet Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin for a three-way meeting on Thursday, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

He is also due to hold a separate bilateral meeting with Xi. Asked by a reporter if he would have a separate meeting with only Putin, Trump mentioned: "I don't know, but I would, if it's available, I would do that. He's coming. I mean, it's really with the guests. I am the guest of President Xi.".

Trump met the leaders of Australia, India and Japan as part of the Quad grouping in the Philippines and the prime minister of Thailand. He is due to China on Wednesday.

Speaking at a dinner with Southeast Asian leaders as the event wrapped up for the day, Trump touted his record during his second term, citing investment into the U.S., trade deals made with countries represented by attendees at the dinner, energy infrastructure buildouts and America's AI industry.

Sources indicate that trump, who has led a campaign to rename artificial intelligence "super intelligence," or "SI," told leaders around the table that the U.S. is "leading the world" in the technology.

"We have the world's most powerful SI ecosystem," he said. "We're leading everybody by a lot, and we hope to keep it that way, and we will keep it that way.".

In a recent development, he added that the U.S. has "the strongest and most capable military on the planet," pointing to investments in the armed forces and the country's defense sector.

"We make the greatest equipment. You saw that in Venezuela. You see it in Iran. You see it everywhere," Trump remarked.
