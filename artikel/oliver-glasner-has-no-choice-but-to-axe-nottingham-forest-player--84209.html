Dan Ndoye was utterly dismal as Nottingham Forest were beaten 2-0 by Bournemouth on Saturday.

Evanilson and Justin Kluivert scored for the home side, who ultimately could have added one or two more on the day.

The visitors looked to come back into it in the second half but failed to find a way through, and have seen their momentum halted.

Nottingham Forest broken their duck under Oliver Glasner by winning just their second Premier League game of the season, against Manchester City just before the break.

But the Austrian will now have to return to the drawing board, and one of his first decisions will have to involve Ndoye.

The 26-year-old, who arrived last year for around £34million, is still yet to really find his feet in the Premier League.

Ndoye would only last 45 minutes before he was hooked by Glasner, such was his display appalling.

The winger failed to leave any impact, and was practically invisible as Nottingham Forest struggled to create.

He misplaced four of his 11 attempted passes, with not a single one of them resulting in a chance for his teammates.

Ndoye's only actual shot was blocked, and he made only two passes into the final third.

In a recent development, but it gets even worse. The 26-year-old was dispossessed twice and dribbled past once, giving away three fouls and not earning any himself.

Most shockingly of all, Ndoye would win just two of the 12 duels he contested against Bournemouth before he was hauled off.

In search of a better second half, Glasner brought on Igor Jesus for Ndoye, and the difference was immediate.

In his 45 minutes, the Brazilian created a chance, took five shots ad won four duels.

And as a team, Nottingham Forest took four more shots, created 1.27 more expected goals and doubled their touches in the opposition box.

It begs the question about why Jesus did not start the game, even when Ndoye has been for some time now.

Saturday's game should serve as a learning moment for Glasner, who can really not afford to have too many more.
