Inter eye Bundesliga talent Jeltsch as Stuttgart set €60m price tag

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Inter are interested in Stuttgart centre-back Finn Jeltsch, but the German club have set a €60m asking price for one of Europe’s most promising young defenders.

According to Il Giorno, the Nerazzurri’s management rate Jeltsch very highly. However, they know a deal will be difficult, with plenty of competition for the 20-year-old and Stuttgart in no hurry to sell, particularly in January.

Stuttgart’s valuation is a big problem. €60m is far more than Inter usually spend, especially as Oaktree are focused on cutting wages and spending carefully.

Jeltsch came through Nuremberg’s academy and has established himself in Stuttgart’s defence this season, making 12 appearances across the Bundesliga, the Champions League and the DFB-Pokal, for a total of 869 minutes.

According to recent reports, the Nerazzurri are thinking about their long-term options at the back. Cristian Chivu has relied heavily on experienced defenders like Manuel Akanji, Alessandro Bastoni and Yann Bisseck, and John Stones’ fitness is still being monitored. The club have also been considering whether to add another centre-back.

For now, Jeltsch looks more like a summer target than a January one.
