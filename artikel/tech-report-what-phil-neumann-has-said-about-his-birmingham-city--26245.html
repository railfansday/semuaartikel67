Birmingham City defender Phil Neumann has broken his silence after a fresh round of transfer speculation recently came to the fore involving the German centre-back.

Neumann has been one of Blues' standout performers so far this season as Chris Davies' side sit top of the Championship, with the division returning to action this weekend after the final international break of 2026.

Birmingham's recent form of six wins in seven games whilst boasting the second tier's meanest defensive record further highlights a vast improvement on last season's 10th-placed finish, with Neumann one of many players in the Second City who struggled for overall consistency in 2025/26.

That didn't stop the 6"3 figure from becoming the subject of transfer interest in January and, indeed, the summer window, although it is apparent Neumann isn't giving recent exit talk much thought at this moment in time, which will appease the Blues fanbase and Davies in equal measure.

Neumann, 29, officially signed for Birmingham on last season's July 1st start date, having penned a pre-contract agreement during his final months with 2.Bundesliga outfit, Hannover 96.

In his first Championship season, the towering centre-back featured 32 times in a defence which changed personnel on several occasions, but has been an ever-present this term alongside captain, Christoph Klarer.

Neumann attracted interest from Sassuolo in January and then from Premier League newcomers, Hull City and Bundesliga side, Borussia Mönchengladbach in the summer, but remained at St. Andrew's to aid Blues' flying start to the season.

After also netting twice against Wrexham in August, the defender recently became the subject of interest from Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina, before further links emerged over potential moves to Hull, Coventry City and Ipswich Town as early as January.

The Recklinghausen-born man has never featured in a top European division in his career so far, making 190 appearances in 2. Bundesliga for the likes of Hannover and Holstein Kiel, as well as his 47 Championship outings.

Speaking to Transfermarkt, Neumann admits that the Championship's physical nature has certainly brought the best out of him, whilst also reaffirming his commitment towards the cause of helping Blues realise their promotion ambitions despite the strong exit talk.

"Of course I hear about those kinds of reports, but I don't pay much attention to them. I have my advisors for that, people I trust who handle those matters," he mentioned.

According to recent reports, "My full focus is on Birmingham City and what we've set out to achieve as a team. There's nothing more crucial to me than doing everything I can to achieve our goals with the club.

It has been revealed that "We have a big task ahead of us, and that's where I'm concentrating all my energy. Everything else is secondary at the moment," Neumann stated.

"I'm in a good sporting position. Therefore, if I were to consider another option, the whole package would have to be right.

"The league is a factor, but by no means the only one. I want to know what role I'm expected to play, how the coach wants us to play, and what the club's future holds.".

The way in which Davies has altered Birmingham's style this season has clearly suited Neumann down to the ground, as well as the fact he has played all 1,350 Championship minutes.

It has been debated whether he would receive such numbers at the City Ground, despite the fact Forest could benefit from extra defensive depth due to Oliver Glasner's three-back system.

It is also clear that, at this stage of his career, Neumann is one of several players who are excited about what the future holds for Birmingham, including the planned move to the Powerhouse Stadium.

Whether he sees out the next five years in the West Midlands remains to be seen, but the chance of becoming a cult figure by helping return the club to the top-flight is unsurprisingly a major factor in his thought process.
