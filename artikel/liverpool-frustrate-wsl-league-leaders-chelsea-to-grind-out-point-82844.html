Chelsea missed the chance to open up a four-point gap at the top of the Women’s Super League having been held to a goalless draw by Liverpool.

Sonia Bompastor’s side remain unbeaten this season but their advantage over Manchester City, who play Sunday, is only two points after failing to find a way past Khiara Keating in St Helens.

The Liverpool goalkeeper tipped over Alyssa Thompson’s shot and also denied Sandy Baltimore at full stretch in the first half, before keeping out Wieke Kaptein, Keira Walsh and Sjoeke Nusken in the second.

Liverpool had ended a five-match run without a win with victory at Brighton last weekend and they carried that confidence into the game against the league leaders to move up to eighth.

In a recent development, "I am really pleased,” Liverpool manager Gareth Taylor said. “The girls stuck to the game blueprint phenomenally well against a tough opponent and I cannot give them enough credit for what they did today. A lot of top individual performances and as a collective we really stuck to the task.

In a recent development, "They had some chances, we had some chances - ours were probably better. And then in the second half, we didn't have the same amount because it's fatiguing to replicate that pressure and at times we didn't keep the ball well enough.

In the late kick-off, Danielle van de Donk and Alexia Putellas both scored twice as London City Lionesses closed the gap on Chelsea with a 6-2 victory against Crystal Palace.

Alanna Kennedy and Isobel Goodwin also found the net to draw Eder Maestre’s side to within four points of the leaders.
