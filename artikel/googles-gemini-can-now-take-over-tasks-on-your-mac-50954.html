📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Google’s Gemini assistant can now take over tasks on a Mac, clicking through apps, typing and filling in forms for you. Google unveiled on Thursday that computer use is rolling out in the Gemini app for Mac.

Sources indicate that you describe a task, and Gemini works through it across local files, desktop apps and websites. It runs in a separate background window with a live preview in the chat, so your screen and cursor stay free. You choose which apps it can use, can pause it, and must approve key actions.

According to recent reports, google’s examples include pulling weights and addresses from PDFs into a shipping quote form, and turning dashboard data into a Google Slides deck. It can also batch-edit photos or prepare resale listings from pictures in a folder.

Industry observers note that google warns that Gemini can make mistakes, including permanently deleting files or sharing them with others. It is designed to ask permission first, and it can only see folders you connect. Backups of changed files are deleted after 24 hours or when you start a new task.

Google is also building Gemini into the operating system of its Googlebook laptops, as rivals race to put agents on the desktop. OpenAI’s ChatGPT can log your keystrokes on a Mac to build its memory. Agents built on the OpenClaw framework have helped drive Mac mini demand.
