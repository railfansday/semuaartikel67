In a recent development, amazon's 12-day holiday shopping marathon has officially arrived. Starting Nov. 19, the retail giant is running its Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday sales through Nov. 30.

Recent findings suggest that in a firm blog post, Amazon mentioned its holiday savings events will feature "millions of deals across popular categories and latest daily themed deal drops," including "double drops" on Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper. Here's what you need to know before you start filling your cart.

According to recent reports, amazon's Black Friday Week sale went live at 3:01 a.m. ET (12:01 a.m. PT) on Nov. 19 — kicking off a full week before Thanksgiving Day.

This will be the fourth year in a row that Amazon starts its Black Friday sale at least a week early. (The actual holiday falls on Friday, Nov. 27 this year.).

Recent findings suggest that amazon is hardly the only major retailer that draws out its Black Friday programming. Best Buy, Target, and Walmart are also offering Black Friday deals throughout November — a win for early birds who worry about popular gifts selling out as the holiday shopping season revs up.

Amazon hasn't officially remarked when its 2026 Cyber Monday sale will start, but in the past, it's kicked off the day right after Black Friday. With that in mind, Mashable's Shopping team expects Amazon to make the shift into Cyber Monday mode on Saturday, Nov. 28 this year. They'll involve limited-time discounts of up to 60% on popular products from brands like Balsam Hill, HydroJug, and Theragun, and they'll go live once a day except on Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper. Shoppers will get two batches of Today's Big Deals on those days: once at 3 a.m. ET, then again at 11 a.m. ET.

According to recent reports, these will likely be some of Amazon's most competitive deals of the year. However, know that its prices may not be as low as they've been on Black Fridays past. That's because myriad products have seen price increases in 2026 due to factors like higher fuel costs, tariffs, and the ongoing memory shortage, aka "RAMageddon.".

The latter is the reason why Amazon raised the prices of its Kindle e-readers, Fire TV Sticks, and most Echo smart speakers in August.

Amazon's Black Friday sale won't be exclusive to Prime members. This is one of the main things that separates it from other Amazon events like Prime Day and Prime Major Deal Days.

Sources indicate that no, Amazon does not price-match competitors' deals — not on Black Friday, nor any other day of the year. If you find a better price on something you've recently purchased there, you'll have to return it to Amazon and rebuy it where it's cheaper.

Most items purchased on Amazon from Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 can be returned through Jan. 31, 2026, except for Apple products, which have a return deadline of Jan. 15, 2027.
