Mexican Hot Chocolate Mousse Pie

🌐 Source: Tasteofhome • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (57 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that we took the chocolate mousse pie recipe submitted by Keri Schofield Lawson of Brea, California, and added cinnamon and cayenne to make a no-bake, Mexican hot chocolate–inspired mousse pie. It's simple, rich and decadent with a spicy twist. Good luck stopping at just one slice! —Taste of Home Test Kitchen.
