Breaking: Ferrari F40 Driver in Hong Kong Arrested After Nearly Colliding With Double-Decker Bus

It’s almost unquestionable that the Ferrari F40 is one of the most desirable supercars ever made. A major part of its appeal is the absence of features that interfere with the connection between car and driver: there's no traction control, no ABS, not even carpeting. One thing the F40 does have, however, is windows. Apparently, however, a driver in Hong Kong forgot to look through them before pulling out in front of a double-decker bus. His F40 escaped an expensive crash, but he might not be as lucky when it comes to avoiding jail time.

Recent findings suggest that according to Dim Sum Daily, Hong Kong police arrested a 38-year-old man on November 9 on suspicion of dangerous driving. The incident occurred two days earlier on Repulse Bay Road; videos of the near crash began to circulate online, and prompted police to investigate. The video shows the red F40 emerging from a side road, apparently ignoring signs and pavement markings requiring traffic to yield. The driver of the double-decker bus reacted swiftly and slammed on the brakes to avoid hitting the multi-million-dollar Ferrari broadside.

It has been revealed that hong Kong Island Regional Traffic Investigation Unit reviewed the footage, and identified the vehicle and suspected driver. Officers then arrested the suspect in the Aberdeen area; police said they intend to pursue prosecution for dangerous driving.

In Hong Kong, dangerous driving is a serious offense. A conviction can result in a maximum fine of HK$25,000—approximately $3200 at current exchange rates—and up to three years in prison. Driving a 478-horsepower Ferrari with a 200-mph top speed and not an airbag in sight demands your complete concentration in any country, however. Thankfully, the bus driver in this situation was paying attention to the road; now the Ferrari driver may have to explain to a court exactly what he was doing instead of watching where his F40 was going.
