It has been revealed that grand Theft Auto 6 features the largest open world Rockstar has ever created. It’s a map filled with locations to explore. However, after playing over 130 hours of GTA 6, there were still plenty of locations, including a zoo and a small northern town, that I never visited once during the story. And according to Rockstar, that’s because the map in GTA 6 was built for the “future.”.

Ahead of Grand Theft Auto 6‘s launch, I asked Rockstar about the inclusion of at least two large casinos on the map. These locations can’t be entered and are never referenced or visited in the story or in any of the side missions I played. So I asked why Rockstar didn’t let Jason and Lucia bet it all on red.

That’s fair. GTA 6 is the first Grand Theft Auto game to really make money feel important, and at times it can be hard to scrounge together enough cash to buy a upcoming gun or fix up your car. So letting players save-scum their way to victory at the casino and have the characters come away with tens of thousands of dollars or more could have derailed the story Rockstar was trying to tell. But those casinos and other unused locations, like an extremely detailed zoo and a large graveyard, could end up being used for…something in the future.

Industry observers note that “The map has been built for the long term and with many factors in mind,” noted Donkin. “So there are a few areas on the map that give us options in the future should we have a gameplay or narrative need for them.â.

Rockstar didn’t share any more details with me about what those “needs” could be, but it does seem that the company has built GTA 6 as something it can expand upon over time, possibly with expansions, updates, and/or some kind of online mode. For now, there’s plenty of GTA 6 for players to get lost in, and even those out-of-the-way locations are fun to explore.
