Latest Update: Electric Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus deal knocks it down to just $232

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After a disappointing launch, Intel turned around the Arrow Lake generation with the 270K Plus and 250K Plus, which both now rank among the best CPUs for gaming. The former is down to is lowest price ever ahead of Black Friday, and by a massive margin. Amazon has the 270K Plus available for $232, 25% off its typical price of $310.

This deal isn't likely to last long. After showing up with a slight discount over the weekend, the 270K Plus jumped back up to its list price. Now, it's discounted again by a much larger margin. We've previously seen these Arrow Lake Refresh chips show up with substantial discounts, and they sell out quickly.

Get Intel's latest and greatest processor, the 270K Plus. The 8p/16e core processor has plenty of oomph to plow through any workflow and games well, too.

Although the 270K Plus carries the Core Ultra 7 branding, it comes with the same 24 cores as the much more expensive Core Ultra 9 285K. Additionally, it matches the 285K in single- and multithreaded applications, and it's faster in games thanks to Intel's new iBOT feature. You can see a full breakdown of performance in our Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus review.

Although AMD still holds the gaming crown with the Ryzen 7 9850X3D (which is also on a Black Friday sale), the 270K Plus offers a chart-topping application performance, even beating out chips that cost twice as much, such as the Ryzen 9 9950X, in some applications.

As you can see from the data in our CPU benchmark hierarchy, the 270K Plus is within 10% of the gaming performance of the Ryzen 7 7800X3D, and it's the fastest CPU we've tested in single-threaded performance. In multi-threaded performance, the 270K Plus and Ryzen 9 9950X are in lockstep.

Sources indicate that the 270K Plus slots into LGA 1851 motherboards. If you want to take advantage of the unlocked multiplier, you'll need to pair the chip with a Z-series chipset. It exclusively supports DDR5 memory. If you need a new motherboard check out our deals thread.

This CPU is an excellent deal, even at full price, so don't miss the Core Ultra 7 270K Plus for $232 at Amazon.
