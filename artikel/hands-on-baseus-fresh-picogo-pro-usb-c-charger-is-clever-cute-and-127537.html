USB-C chargers are pretty standardized at this point, but there’s a fun newly introduced trend of adding displays to your power brick that I’ve been very much enjoying, and the new PicoGo Pro from Baseus is a great example with three ports, optimized charging, and a cute little character to top it off – and for Black Friday, it’s pretty deeply discounted, too.

In a recent development, the Baseus PicoGo Pro (AH22) is a 100W, 3-port USB-C charger designed to top off your laptop, phone, and/or tablet, even all at the same time. For anyone who travels often or just has a lot of devices on hand, chargers like this are a must. Having something you don’t have to think twice about when it comes to charging rates is just so nice, and these have come a long way, too.

Recent findings suggest that using GaN tech, PicoGo Pro is very compact at about 2 inches tall and a little over an inch wide. I remember having single-port chargers that could barely fast-charge my phone that were twice this size just a few years ago. GaN just absolutely rules, and Baseus is using a special version that reduces heat and improves efficiency.

But the two reasons I really like this charger aren’t strictly to do with GaN.

Recent findings suggest that the first is the built-in display. Again, that’s a trend throughout the industry. Anker has several chargers out with built-in displays as the company helped to really make this popular – the Anker Prime 160W brick is really similar to PicoGo Pro (at a higher price), and it’s the only I’ve been using for months before trying out this Baseus model.

Baseus has a cute little animated character that accompanies your charging, with little eyes when the charger isn’t active and some fun animations to show your charging rate in real-time. Neat! You can “press” the button to move through the UI and even see the specific charging rate of each connected device. You’ll also see that the brick can recognize the device you have plugged in, though Android devices just show a generic “SAMSUNG” or whatever brand you plug in – Anker, again, had a similar set of features in a brick released earlier in 2026, but some of those features were iPhone-only, and that brick was only a 1-port, 45W charger anyway.

According to recent reports, but, like that Anker brick, Baseus supports “smart” charging that adjusts to your device. Plugging in a Galaxy Z Fold 8 that was around 65% full, I saw the brick adjust its charging speed to tailor to the phone, initially going up to full speed before settling down around 28W~. You can manually adjust this with “Turbo” or “Gentle” modes, the former maxing out the charging rate and the latter settling in around 15W for a slower charge. That flexibility is great because it means you can adjust the charging rate to what you need in that moment. Full-speed for a quick top-off on a busy day, slower charging overnight to preserve battery health.

Baseus isn’t doing much latest here, though the GaN tech mentioned earlier is enhanced by some architecture and algorithm adjustments the tech giant has made behind the scenes, but it’s a really good implementation.

Beyond that, it’s also priced pretty well.

Baseus PicoGo Pro’s full price is $59.99, but you’ll probably rarely see it there. Technically, today is its launch date, and it’s already down by over 30% at $39.70 for Black Friday. Value-wise, this one is hard to beat.
