The chairman and co-owner of the club has remained silent during this rough period for the club which has seen the sacking of Colin Kazim-Richards, an embarrassing FA Cup exit to seventh tier Wimborne Town, the club rooted to bottom of League Two and interim boss Kevin Blackwell give a damning verdict on the club’s recruitment.

It’s now been a month since Kazim-Richards went and fans are unclear where the club is with the appointment.

Khalili held a fans forum in February and gave an interview to the Crawley Observer/SussexWorld in January, but noone has heard from him since – in a period that has seen Scott Lindsey sacked and another squad overhaul.

This publication has requested interviews and so has the BBC’s Robin Grey – but nothing has transpired yet.

Fans have been calling for him to say something and now former Reds Scott Malone and Tony Vessey have both noted he should say something.

Speaking on the Crawley Town podcast on the BBC with Robin, the pair spoke about about the current situation what Khalili should be doing.

Former defender Malone, who is at the moment playing for Three Bridges in the Isthmian Premier, mentioned: “There's no one else attached to the club permanently to say anything apart from Raph.

“And he's not doing it. In my opinion, he should front up because at the end of the day, he's the substantial boss. He's the owner.

“If there's no one else to do it, he should come out and do it himself. Or is there nothing to say? I'd suggest that there isn't anything to say, otherwise he'd have come out and said it. But come out and say it if there's nothing to say. At least do that.”.

And Vessey, who has been captain and manager at Crawley, agreed. “It’s been four weeks of silence, pretty much. Just be honest. People just want you to be honest with them.

Say something like ‘we're trying to recruit because of the position we're in, we're finding it a bit difficult’. ‘We're relying on Kevin Blackwell to hopefully improve that position so that we can get a permanent appointment’. I think most people would accept that. You own the club.

“You've taken on that responsibility of guiding it. So guide it. And in any business, communication is key. So football's a business. You want your supporters to turn up week in, week out, then communicate with them. And I think that's an imperative for anybody.”.

Vessey added: “If he's a businessman,then communication is key in most businesses. So he should understand that keeping everybody on side is the way forward, not to be completely silent.”.

Khalili took over leadership of the club in August 2025 as KB Sports and Leisure but was previously a minority shareholder when WAGMI were in charge.
