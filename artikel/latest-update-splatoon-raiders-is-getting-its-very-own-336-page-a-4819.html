Nintendo has unveiled that it is teaming up with KADOKAWA on a latest art book all about Splatoon Raiders.

The 336-page book introduces in Japan on 9th February 2027 and will be available from both the region's online storefront and the official Nintendo Stores across Japan for 3,960 yen (around £19 / $25).

It's apparently rammed with over 5,000 pieces of concept art from the game, divided into five chapters focused on the artwork, creatures, style, and world, with a mysterious 'bonus' section at the end.
