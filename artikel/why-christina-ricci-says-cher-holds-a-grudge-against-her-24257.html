If Christina Ricci could turn back time, she would patch up her relationship with Cher.

The Addams Family star revealed that while she and the Grammy winner—who starred in the film Mermaids together—once shared a close bond, their relationship is nonexistent today.

Industry observers note that “Unfortunately, she's still mad at me,” Ricci told Andy Cohen on the Nov. 17 episode of Watch What Happens Live. “She holds a grudge.”.

As for what turned their connection to stone?

Sources indicate that “Well, when I was 19, I did some Rolling Stone thing, and they interviewed her,” Ricci explained. “Maybe it wasn't Rolling Stone. It might have been Première, but they interviewed her, and she remarked all these things that were not that amazing about me.”.

In an effort to explain the quotes, the “If I Could Turn Back Time” singer attempted to reach out to Ricci.

“She left me a voicemail about how journalists are always trying to get the worst things, and blah blah blah,” Ricci continued. “And she was so sorry, and I was 19, and so I didn't return that message.”.

And the Casper star believes their relationship is just as strained nearly 30 years later. As Ricci shared, “I've reached out to her since and gotten radio silence.”.

E! News has reached out to a rep for Cher for comment but has not yet heard back.

In a recent development, though the 46-year-old wishes she stood on firmer ground with the Burlesque star, she shared there’s no animosity on her end. In fact, she sees where the 80-year-old is coming from.

Sources indicate that “I adore her. She's the best,” Ricci said. “And I absolutely can 100 percent imagine being Cher and being like, ‘Oh, my God, they misquoted me, and I'm going to call this young person and explain to her that they misquoted me.’ And then the gall of that young person to not return the call and be like, ‘Oh, my God, Cher, it's okay. I love you so much.’”.

Recent findings suggest that “Like, I would be so pissed,” she added. “I get it.”.

That’s why her soft spot for Cher (real name Cheryl Sarkisian) persists.

“Love you, Cher,” she remarked. “I love you so much.”.

Cher and Ricci played mother-daughter duo Rachel Flax and Kate Flax, respectively, in the 1990 coming-of-age film. And years later, the Penelope star shared how their familial bond wasn’t just for the camera.

In a recent development, “Cher took me under her wing, and she recognized that sometimes you could be on a set, and you don’t know exactly what is going on behind the scenes—power struggles, or different problems,” Ricci told Demi Lovato in the 2024 documentary Child Star. “And if you don’t know any better, and have no experience, you can think it was your fault. And she never wanted me to feel insecure.".
