Reslimed was originally prototyped as a retro-style 2D platformer for Game Boy Color and was pitched by developer Doinksoft before the GTA 6 conversation really ramped up. "There is no doubt GTA 6 will have the spotlight before, during, and after November 19, but it’s not for everyone, or releasing on Steam the same day, so we figure there’s a bit of counter-programming that may work in Volvy’s favor," Devolver told Kotaku. GTA 6 has reportedly already sold nearly 15 million copies and generated about $1.5 billion in revenue.
