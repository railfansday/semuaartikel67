Cigna is making $31.5 million in grant funding available over the next three years to nonprofits in 10 states that are aiming to tackle several key community health priorities.

Industry observers note that the tech giant mentioned that funds will be made available through its philanthropic arm, the Cigna Group Foundation, with applications open now through Dec. 18. Cigna said in an announcement that it's looking for organizations tackling youth mental health, veteran wellness and the impact of complex health needs on patients and their caregivers.

Cigna mentioned the program seeks to deepen the manufacturer's community health strategy to address critical challenges.

The company first released a newly introduced approach to community health and engagement in 2024, and since then has deployed more than $30 million in funding to 190 nonprofit organizations. Projects backed by these grants have reached at least 321,000 people, Cigna remarked.

Sources indicate that "Over the past several years, we’ve seen the tangible impact we can make when we invest in strong nonprofit partners and come together to help communities achieve better health and vitality," said Melissa Skottegaard, board chair of The Cigna Group Foundation, in the announcement. "We’re building on what’s working, bringing together funding, collaboration and the skills of our employee volunteers to address some of the most pressing health challenges facing communities across the street and around the world.".

Cigna will allot $10.5 million to each of the three focus areas under the initiative, per the announcement. Youth mental health, for example, has been a leading topic at the company for some time, and the company remarked it will also continue to build on its partnership with the Boys and Girls Clubs of America in this area.

In that partnership, Cigna has helped build out trauma-informed care services to 90% of clubs, reaching 11,000 employees. The organization stated that these efforts have supported 1,600 youths, and that its workforce has contributed more than 2,000 hours to local clubs.

Eligible organizations can apply for two-year, $250,000 grants to support programs in the three categories. The funds will be available to groups in Arizona, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Missouri, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Tennessee and Texas, per the announcement.

Cigna said its foundation will also continue to support community health through its Health Equity Impact Fund.

Industry observers note that "Community organizations can be a powerful source of support for people navigating complex health conditions,” mentioned Matt Perlberg, president of Evernorth Pharmacy & Care Delivery and board member of the Cigna Group Foundation, in the announcement. “By helping patients and caregivers connect with resources and overcome barriers to care, these organizations can provide crucial support alongside essential medical treatment, helping people navigate their health and care journey.”.
