Travis Kelce is taking some bench time.

Industry observers note that the Kansas City Chiefs’ tight end exited the team’s Nov. 15 match up against the Atlanta Falcons after his very first catch of the game, suffering an apparent injury to an already tender ankle.

Recent findings suggest that while Kelce received quarterback Patrick Mahomes’ 23-yard pass, the 37-year-old was tackled by the Falcons’ DeMarcco Hellams. Kelce was slow to get up from the hit but was able to limp to the sideline without assistance where his ankle was taped by the medical team. Rather than retake the field, however, Kelce made his way to the locker room for additional evaluation.

The Chiefs noted his return to the field as questionable.

Thankfully, since he was able to complete a pass before his exit from the game, Kelce has now achieved a milestone 200 consecutive games with at least one reception.

It has been revealed that not present for the injury or latest achievement, however, was Kelce’s wife Taylor Swift. Though the Grammy winner has become a staple at the Chiefs’ home games at Arrowhead Stadium, Swift often skips Kelce’s away games, including the game at Atlanta’s Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

Recent findings suggest that but when she does take in the on-field action live in person, Swift is often accompanied by some pals in her VIP suite. Recently, she enjoyed an enchanting reunion with pal Karlie Kloss, who joined Swift at the Chiefs’ Nov. 8 home game against the Upcoming York Jets.

The pair—who were inseparable BFFs before a rumored growing apart in the 2010s—were all smiles and even shared a hug as they watched the game, with Kloss being joined by her and her husband Jared Kushner’s 5-year-old son Levi.

But even before they watched Swift’s guy on the Chiefs, Kloss and Swift had already proved they’d shaken off any rumored bad blood. After all, Kloss and Kushner were also on the 1,000-person guest list to witness Kelce and Swift’s nuptials in July.

And like any loyal BFF, Kloss refused to fill in any blanks spaces on what exactly went down inside Madison Square Garden.

In a recent development, “Listen, I try to protect the privacy of my friends’ personal lives,” the mom of three remarked when asked about the wedding on Today. “But what I will say is that they’re an amazing couple and I’m so happy for them.”.
