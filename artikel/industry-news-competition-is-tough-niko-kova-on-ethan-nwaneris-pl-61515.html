Ethan Nwaneri (19) registered two assists in England U21s recent 3-2 victory over France, but the Borussia Dortmund man has struggled for playing time in the Bundesliga recently.

Sources indicate that speaking at Thursday’s press conference, BVB head coach Niko Kovač weighed in on the player’s situation. “We have very good players in those positions up front, he explained. Nwaneri initially replaced Giannis Konstantelias, who suffered an ACL injury on his Bundesliga debut against Hamburg, while Fabio Silva is shining alongside Serhou Guirassy, while Konstantinos Karetsas has recently returned from a back injury.

“The competition is tough,” added Kovač, who did praise Nwaneri’s performances for England. “As far as Ethan is concerned, we’re delighted,” he remarked. ” It was a friendly match and he did really well.”.

With an assist to his name on his Bundesliga but against Hoffenheim on Matchday 2, Nwaneri has featured in eight of nine possible league games, playing 336 minutes for Die Schwarzgelben in Germany’s top flight.

On Saturday, Dortmund face Borussia Mönchengladbach at Borussia-Park as they look to maintain their unbeaten start to their Bundesliga campaign.
