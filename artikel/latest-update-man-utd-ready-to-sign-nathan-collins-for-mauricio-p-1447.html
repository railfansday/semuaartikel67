There may be more than six weeks until the January transfer window opens, but Manchester United are already considering how they can back upcoming manager Mauricio Pochettino in the market.

Pochettino was confirmed as Michael Carrick's successor at Man United on Friday, and he will officially leave his role as the United States manager after they play Haiti in the early hours of Wednesday morning, UK time.

The 54-year-old will then travel to Manchester, where he will only have a couple of days to prepare for his bow against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

It's a difficult fixture for a club who have only won one of their 10 Premier League matches this term, which has left them in the relegation zone as we approach December.

A big improvement is needed on the pitch and Pochettino must find that tune out of the players he has at his disposal, before the club can potentially make additions in January.

Fabrizio Romano has already confirmed that there will be at least one upcoming signing in the New Year, and potentially two or three, with centre-back and striker the two priority positions.

According to talkSPORT reporter Alex Crook, the Red Devils are interested in Fulham duo Calvin Bassey and Antonee Robinson, while Folarin Balogun is a potential target having narrowly missed out on joining Premier League rivals Everton on deadline day in the summer.

Robinson and Balogun have both worked under Pochettino for the USMNT, while Bassey could kill two birds with one stone for the Red Devils given he can play both left-sided centre-back and left-back, amid the possibility that both Lisandro Martinez and Luke Shaw could leave Old Trafford in 2027.

One other player that is being looked at, according to Crook, is Brentford captain Nathan Collins.

Pochettino's Tottenham team was built around a strong leadership team put in place for the Argentine, and it allowed them to thrive.

Goalkeeper Hugo Lloris was club captain for much of the 54-year-old manager's time at Spurs, but he was backed up by Harry Kane and Jan Vertonghen at the time.

Pochettino is inheriting some good leaders on paper at Old Trafford, including captain Bruno Fernandes and Harry Maguire, while the likes of Luke Shaw, Diogo Dalot, Youri Tielemans and Lisandro Martinez offer plenty of experience.

Nathan Collins is unlikely to walk into the Theatre of Dreams and become captain, but the fact that he is at the moment the skipper of both Brentford and the Republic of Ireland demonstrates what an vital addition to the dressing room he would be.

And that is just one of Collins' strengths, given he has established himself as one of the best in his position in the Premier League in recent seasons.

The Irishman, described as 'very talented' by Thomas Frank when he joined the Bees, stands at 6ft 5in tall and is known for his aerial prowess when it comes to winning headed duels, while he has strong tackling and clearance statistics for the Bees.

Brentford are also known to play out from the back, and that is something that Collins has proven he is more than capable of doing, which will suit Pochettino down to the ground.

That quality to make progressive passes from defence is increasingly important in the modern game, and the 25-year-old is capable of that and much more, even if he hasn't been able to show it too much this season because of injury.

Collins for Brentford (Premier League 2026-27).

One thing that could count against Collins is whether the Red Devils actually need a right-sided centre-back, given they already have Maguire, Leny Yoro and Matthijs de Ligt who prefer to play on the right.

There is a greater need for a left-sided player, given there are doubts over Martinez's future and Ayden Heaven is still yet to convince people that he should be a consistent starter.

In those terms, Jarrad Branthwaite of Everton might be a better addition, but Collins could cost a fraction of the price, especially given United and Brentford already have a working relationship after the Bryan Mbeumo deal in 2025.
