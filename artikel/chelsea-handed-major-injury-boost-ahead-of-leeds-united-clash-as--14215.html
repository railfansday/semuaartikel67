Chelsea goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez is set to return to action before Leeds United’s trip to Stamford Bridge.

The Whites are due to visit the capital on Saturday (November 21) and will be keen to avenge their 6-3 Carabao Cup loss to the Blues in September.

Chelsea have had to do without Martinez between the sticks in each of their last four games, as the 34-year-old suffered a broken jaw against Everton last month.

Mike Penders has deputised but Chelsea could have the opportunity to reinstate Martinez for the meeting with Leeds.

Argentina boss Lionel Scaloni has confirmed the goalkeeper will be in action tonight (November 17), when his side take on Costa Rica in a friendly.

According to recent reports, as reported by Football.London, he said: “Emiliano will play. He might have been okay for the previous game against Uruguay as well, but it was a risk. An extra three days was a prudent choice.”.

Chelsea will undoubtedly have an eye on the fixture, keen to see Martinez come through unscathed.

However, head coach Xabi Alonso will face a tough decision considering the understudy Penders has impressed in recent weeks.

Whites boss Daniel Farke, on the other hand, does not face much of a selection dilemma when it comes to goalkeepers.

Club-record signing James Trafford has been in stellar form and was impressive in England’s 3-2 Nations League win over Spain at the weekend. He has been showered in praise since the performance, which has led many to tip him to dislodge Jordan Pickford as the first-choice Three Lions stopper.

Michael Zetterer, who was between the sticks when Leeds visited Stamford Bridge earlier in the season, is likely to take the understudy spot on the bench.
