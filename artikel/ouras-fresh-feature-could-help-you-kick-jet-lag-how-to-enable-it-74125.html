Oura developed a tool for its frequent-flying members that could help them stave off the worst of jet lag.

Ahead of the busy holiday travel season, on Tuesday, Oura announced Jet Lag Proposal, a fresh feature designed to help travelers feel alert and energized after crossing several time zones.

Also: Oura Ring customers may get in-app access to real doctors soon – what we know.

Jet Lag Strategy takes into account your travel itinerary, sleep patterns, and body responses to provide guidance for traveling members entering new time zones.

Jet lag happens when circadian rhythms are at odds with local time zones, but there are a few things people can do to adjust.

Oura factors in the direction of travel, how many time zones are crossed, individual light windows, and sleep timing.

Ring users will enter their flight details, including information like multi-stop trips, and also include the reason for the trip. Oura adjusts the Jet Lag Plan accordingly, and a business trip’s plan may differ in intensity from that of a vacation.

“Two people on the same flight can get different plans, because guidance reflects your itinerary, your typical sleep schedule, and your chronotype,” Oura writes in a blog post. The chronotype refers to Oura’s sleep chronotype, which categorizes users into six types (early morning, morning, early evening, and so on) based on their historical sleep activity.

Also: I wore the Oura Ring 5 for a month.

After adding these details, Oura provides each user with timely guidance throughout their trip. This guidance could include recommendations on when to begin or end caffeine consumption or light exposure, and when to start preparing for sleep. The initiative is designed for those crossing three or more time zones. Members can follow along with these recommendations by checking them off as they complete each one. Customers can also talk to Oura’s Advisor in the event of flight or timing changes.

Jet Lag Plan is now available for Oura Members with Oura Ring Gen 3 or later.

In a recent development, go to your home screen, tap the + and select Jet Lag Initiative to get started. You can also tell Oura Advisor about a trip to initiate Jet Lag Plan. If the Oura app detects a time zone shift of 3 or more hours, Jet Lag Plan may appear as a push notification.
