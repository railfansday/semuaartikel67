In the final stretch of 2026, some XRP Ledger protocol features await mainnet activation, with validator support and amendment voting as the final steps to bring newly introduced functionality to the XRPL network.

In light of this, three amendments are racing against the 2026 calendar, with only a few votes left to reach a majority and enter the two-week activation period. These include SingleAssetVault, LendingProtocol and confidential transfers.

All three remain below the required validator approval threshold of over 80% support, making the final weeks of the year increasingly important for their activation prospects.

With about five weeks left in 2026, activation remains possible this year, but additional validator support for each amendment must arrive soon enough and be sustained to complete the required two-week activation window.

In a recent development, the lending protocol allows on-chain fixed-term, uncollateralized loans from pooled funds in a Single Asset Vault. There are two amendments: LendingProtocolV1_1 and the original Lending Protocol amendment.

The focus is on LendingProtocolV1_1, which extends the Lending Protocol and SingleAssetVault amendments with a newly introduced closed-ended vault and cash-basis accounting. With the amendment, new loan brokers can only be created against closed-ended vaults.

LendingProtocolV1_1 has reached 70.59% support with 24 out of 34 validators voting yes, according to XRPScan data. It needs 28 votes to reach a majority, or more than 80% of 34 votes, meaning it has just four votes left.

Industry observers note that A major privacy amendment for the XRP Ledger, confidential transfers, has 64.71% support and 22 of 34 validators voting yes. Confidential transfers, which rely on advanced cryptography (EC-ElGamal and ZKPs), offer institutional-grade privacy for Multi-Purpose Tokens (MPTs) and need six more yes votes to enter the activation window.

It has been revealed that singleAssetVault creates a structure for aggregating assets from multiple depositors and is intended to be used with the on-chain Lending Protocol. It has gained 73.53% support so far with 25 out of 34 validators voting in favor. Reaching more than 80% of 34 votes requires 28 votes; thus, three votes remain for it to enter the activation window.
