📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

File with FreeTaxUSA if you do your own taxes and have freelance, investment, or rental income, since its federal return is free at any level of complexity and each state return costs $15.99. Choose another program if you want a tax pro included, because FreeTaxUSA charges $44.99 for access to one.

Industry observers note that at list prices for 2025 returns, a return with freelance, investment, or rental income and one state cost $114 to $179 in H&R Block’s online tiers, unlimited expert help included, and $203 in TurboTax Premium. FreeTaxUSA’s maker, TaxHawk, Inc., also runs TaxHawk, a sister site this review covers too, since it has the same features and $15.99 state fee, though its Pro Support costs $64.99, against $44.99 at FreeTaxUSA.

Filers who can enter their own tax forms get a freelance, investment, or rental return filed for the price of the state return. Anyone who wants expert help in the price, or a pro to review the return, is better served by H&R Block’s paid tiers, which include the help and sell the review as an add-on.
