Exclusive: Measures aimed at tackling grey market sellers known to sell dangerous knives to children.

Ministers will introduce the world’s first licensing scheme for businesses and private individuals selling all types of knives including cutlery, the Home Office unveiled on Wednesday.

In a drive to fulfil the government’s pledge to halve knife crime within 10 years, shops and online vendors selling butter knives or machetes will be expected to apply for a licence or face fines.
