In a recent development, meghan Markle and Prince Harry blueprint to host Meghan’s mother for the holidays at their new U.K. home, where Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet will celebrate their first Christmas in Britain.

A trip to California had been a possibility, but the Duke and Duchess of Sussex intend to spend both Thanksgiving and Christmas in Britain after six years on the West Coast.

Doria Ragland is expected to join them, and Harry suspects his mother-in-law will have a hand in the holiday menu.

“My wife is very good in the kitchen and so is my mother-in-law, so I’ll leave that to them,” Harry, 42, noted during a Nov. 20 appearance on the British morning show "This Morning.".

He did have one dish on his mind.

“Maybe gumbo, yeah, maybe turkey, maybe gumbo,” he remarked.

In a recent development, for Meghan, 45, spending the holidays at home also means keeping things simple during a difficult stretch for her family. Her estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr., was arrested in Florida in early November on a federal charge involving child sexual abuse material.

“Everything going on with her dad is still weighing heavily on her,” a source told People. “She wants the holidays to be as quiet and enjoyable as possible for the family.

“Traveling back and forth would be too much for everyone,” the source added.

Since Archie, 7, and Lilibet, 5, returned to school, Meghan and Harry have been making more time for meals out together.

The source told People that one thing the couple appreciate about their new home is the privacy they enjoy nearby.

“They can go out for lunch or dinner, and people give them space,” the insider added.

According to recent reports, the children joined their parents for a night out in London on Nov. 18, when Hyde Park’s Winter Wonderland opened for the season. Harry and Archie took a spin on the bumper cars during what People described as the first public sighting of the children since the move.

In a recent development, christmas in Britain will be a first for both children, even though Archie was born there. He spent his first Christmas in Canada in 2019, and the couple later settled in California, where Lilibet was born in 2021.

Recent findings suggest that meghan spent Christmas at Sandringham with Harry and the royal family in 2017 and 2018. Neither child has taken part in the gathering, which traditionally includes a Christmas morning church service.

This year, the Sussexes are expected to celebrate at their own home, according to People.

As for what the children are hoping to wake up to on Christmas morning, Harry told "This Morning," “I think they’re looking forward to snow.”.

The news of their holiday aims comes after it was revealed that they reportedly moved into a fresh Cotswolds home on their friend's estate.

After making the move across the pond in August, the couple was living "in the main house" of their pal's sprawling property.

However, now, their living situation has reportedly changed, with the Daily Mail reporting that they have moved to another property "on the estate.".

According to the outlet, the friend is a wealthy businessman whom Harry befriended through their mutual support for various charities.

Harry and Meghan were rumored to have been house hunting, with several reports noting that the former actress was even looking to replicate the luxury they had in their Montecito, CA, mansion.

The duchess reportedly wants the U.K. residence to boast nine bedrooms—the same number as their California estate—as well as plenty of luxurious amenities.

The news of their move came mere weeks after Harry opened up about their decision to leave California behind and relocate their family to the U.K., revealing that their children's education and his desire to reconnect with his roots played a major role in the decision.

In an interview with GQ that was filmed in September but released only recently, Harry explained that the opportunity to enroll his children in British schools was particularly meaningful to him.

Recent findings suggest that “Opportunities for the family, being able to put the kids in a British school has meant a lot to me. I didn’t know how notable it was to me,” he noted.

“I always thought it was going to be something that I would try to do or that we would try to do, but now they’ve been in school for two weeks. They’re loving it, and it’s just giving them a whole different experience.”.

It has been revealed that harry emphasized that his decision was not a criticism of the U.S. education system, but rather an opportunity for Archie and Lilibet to experience life in two different countries.

“I’m not criticizing American schools, but I think for kids at 7 and 5 to be able to have that comparison and to have two completely different cultural experiences is so key today,” he explained.

Beyond their education, Harry stated returning to Britain has allowed his children to spend more time with relatives and develop closer relationships with family friends.

According to recent reports, another motivation for the move, he explained, was to “be back closer to my family and to be back on U.K. soil for longer than a few days or a week here or there.

“It’s been nice to be close to friends, family, friends and friends and their children as well, to be able to have endless playdates to really, like, help our kids settle in," he said.

Despite the significance of the relocation, Harry admitted that he and Meghan had not necessarily mapped out the move years in advance.
