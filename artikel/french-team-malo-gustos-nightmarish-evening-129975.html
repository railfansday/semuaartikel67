According to recent reports, for several gatherings, the corridors have been a real construction site for the French team. And it’s not this evening in Milan that’s going to fix things. At San Siro, the Blues started their meeting well, before gradually losing their footing and conceding their first defeat of the Zinedine Zidane era (3-1), on the occasion of this 5th day of the Nations League. In this debacle, Malo Gusto particularly suffered. Aligned in the right lane, the Chelsea full-back increased his attempts, struggling to contain the Italian offensives.

Industry observers note that the former Lyonnais, however, had a great opportunity to get back on his feet, after a complicated start to the club season. Far from being an indisputable starter this season under Xabi Alonso, he was still called up by ZZ to compensate for Jules Koundé’s injury. “ He was the next player on my list. He knows the selection. He certainly has less playing time at the moment, but he already has experience at this level. This is therefore the opportunity to give him the opportunity to return to Blue », justified the French coach when questioned about his choice to select Gusto. A missed opportunity. Taken behind his back, overwhelmed in duels and unable to secure his side, the 23-year-old was undoubtedly the worst French player on the pitch.

Faulty on the first Italian goal signed Pio Esposito after being overwhelmed on his side (26th) by Cambiaghi, Malo Gusto then caused a penalty, certainly missed by Sebastiano Esposito. Two situations which weighed heavily on his performance, while the full-back was greatly expected. Unable to reassure defensively, he only won his first duel… in the 47th minute of play (2 out of 6). Enough to accentuate his completely upside-down match, in addition to his eight lost balls and his three fouls committed. In the 77th minute, his ordeal ended with the entry of last-minute call-up Saël Kumbedi, two minutes after the second Italian goal, coming from his side with this cross from Dimarco for Frattesi (63rd).

According to recent reports, destroyed on all sides, he was however spared by his coach who preferred to give a general assessment of the meeting: “ there were some good things, especially in the first half. Afterwards, we did less good things. Particularly in duels and in 1v1s, we were a little worse… The start of the second half was good too. It’s true that when we were in difficulty, we didn’t know how to react and stay within the framework set up. We did it in the first, and afterwards when they took the advantage, we dispersed a little, also on the defensive level. » While no player stands out for the moment on the right side, Malo Gusto seems to have burned his cards during this first match. Conversely, he is even going through a very difficult period in the selection, since France conceded eight goals in their last 166 minutes of play with him on the pitch. Not sure that he will be renewed on Sunday against Turkey.
