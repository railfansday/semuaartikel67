It has been revealed that the Vice City portal Easter egg is a unique event in Grand Theft Auto 6 that lets you travel through time. It might not look like it, but the existence of a secret portal in Leonida is quite plausible, considering the UFOs appearing in the skies and the forgotten haunted hotels you can visit.

Industry observers note that to experience this event and open the portal, you will have to complete a long series of steps. But don't worry, below, we explain everything you need to know to complete the Vice City portal Easter egg in GTA 6.

If you want to start the Vice City portal event in GTA 6, you must travel to Key Lento, the initial area in the game, and head to the Badger radio tower, northeast of the Nifty Threads Island Wear shop. Enter the tower and climb to the top to reach what appears to be a control room.

Beside the door, there's a lever. Pull it up then press the button on the panel near the old television. If you've done everything correctly, the TV will turn on and display the word "Malibu." Now, leave the tower and prepare for the next step.

After you have finished the initial ratio tower step, you will need to visit 12 LPL substations around Leonidas following a specific order. There will be a letter or two on the outer wall of each substation, which will form a secret message when all 12 letters are put together. In each one, you will find either a button or a lever that represents a letter or symbol. To ensure you're interacting with the correct one, check the pink tape near the button/lever and look for the letter/symbol it features.

Recent findings suggest that before you start, keep in mind that you can't fast travel using the RydeMe app or swap between characters. Doing so resets the mission, and you will need to return to the radio tower to start it again. You'll know that you have completed this step when you receive a message from an unknown contact after you pull up the last lever. Below, we listed the substations in the order you must visit them, where you will find the button or lever you must activate, and which letter they represent.

Location: Vice City (Vice-Dale County) — Southwest corner of Downtown, just southwest of Zenith gym.

Location: Vice City (Vice-Dale County) — West side of Balsero, just northwest of Randy's shop.

Location: Key Lento (Mariana County) —Northwest of Kenton's shop.

Location: Vice Beach (Vice-Dale County) — North side of Penmarch Isle.

Location: Vice City (Vice-Dale County) — West side of Bayside, just northwest of the Vice City apartment.

Location: Port Gellhorn (Kelly County) — North of Moses' shop.

Location: Hamlet (Vice-Dale County) — East of Massive Shot Ammu-Nation shop​​​​​​​.

Location: Vice City (Vice-Dale County) — Northwest area of Gloriana Key.

Location: Hamlet (Vice-Dale County) - East side of Hamlet, just northeast of the Boone Speedway.

Location: Gellhorn (Kelly County) — Northeast of Raceway.

Location: Hamlet (Vice-Dale County) - South side of Hamlet, just southeast of Boone Speedway and the Pay 'N' Spray.

Location: North Mariana County (Mariana County) — South of Breakwater Quarry.

If you combine all the letters, you'll get the message: Life is passing me by, which is a PC cheat code that allows you to speed up time in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

The final step in the event requires you to follow the instructions in the message you receive after activating the 12th lever: head to the T. H. Power Station, east of Hamlet in Vice-Dale County, between 7 p.m. and 6 a.m. Once there, you will find the portal that will take you to the original Vice City.

According to recent reports, you can find the portal on the north side of the power station. You'll know you're at the right place if you can see traces of energy from the portal running through the cables in the area. Reaching the portal might be difficult, considering how heavily guarded the area is. Consider either using a helicopter or swimming around the area to find a good entry point.

Industry observers note that once you find the portal, pull the lever on the left side of the gate, and you'll be teleported. To leave the original Vice City, just head to the room behind the bar, and you will be back to the world of GTA 6. By completing this event, you will receive Tony Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt, the American Glory motorcycle (don't forget to register it!), and unlock a new livery for purchase at Vice Vinyl.
