📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Viking has added four more ocean ship options to its orderbook, according to its third quarter earnings presentation.

The newly introduced options include two ships for 2035 and two for 2036, according to the firm’s third quarter 2026 earnings presentation.

It has been revealed that as of Nov. 17, Viking had eight ocean ship options, up from four in its second quarter presentation (orderbook as of Aug. 19). The existing options still call for two ships in 2033 and two in 2034. With the newly introduced options, Viking’s ocean newbuild pipeline now runs through 2036 and totals 19 ships, up from 15.

Recent findings suggest that the firm ocean orderbook did not change. It shows 11 ships due between 2026 and 2032: one each in 2026 and 2027, two a year from 2028 through 2030, one in 2031 and two in 2032.

The expedition orderbook also stayed the same, with two committed ships due in 2030 and 2031.

It has been revealed that on the river side, Viking’s committed orderbook grew from 20 ships to 26, even though three ships came off the 2026 schedule. The remaining 2026 deliveries fell from five to two.

The 190-berth Longship series now has 14 firm ships, up from 12. Four ships due in 2029, which had been options in August, are now firm orders.

It has been revealed that viking also added fresh Longship options. It now holds 22 options, up from 16: six for 2030 and four a year from 2031 through 2034.

Committed orders for Viking’s smaller 80 and 82-berth river ships rose from seven to 11. This includes four newly introduced ships for 2029 and one for 2030. A single smaller Longship  is still scheduled for 2027.

Counting options, Viking’s river orderbook now totals 48 ships through 2034, up from 36 in August.
