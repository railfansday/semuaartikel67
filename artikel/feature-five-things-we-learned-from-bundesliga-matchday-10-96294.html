According to recent reports, the Bundesliga returned following the final international break of the year, and what a spectacle it was. From a swing of the title race pendulum, to changes at the bottom of the table, Get German Football News has you covered with the latest talking points after Matchday 10.

Here are five things we learned….

This would seem like an obvious thing to say after Harry Kane was ruled out for the next three months with an ankle injury,, while Luis Diaz, Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry are also out injured, but this has been a problem for most of the season too, and these injuries are likely to make the matter worse.

Even with Kane fit, Bayern have been wasteful in front of goal this season. Granted, some of the results the ball just wouldn’t go in, like against Schalke and Augsburg, but the difference between this season and last year was that Bayern would prove to be far more clinical in front of goal, but Germany’s record champions seemed to have lost that edge now, as shown in their 1-1 draw with Cologne on Friday night.

This has resulted in games where Bayern should win comfortably being a lot more difficult, mainly due to their own doing. With the injury to Kane, and with no fit back-up, this problem is likely to get worse and could make or break Bayern’s Bundesliga season. It’s now up to Vincent Kompany and his coaching team to find a solution, and quick.

According to recent reports, usually after Bayern drop points, Dortmund fail to capitalise, and ultimately, nothing would change. However, Matchday 10 was different.

According to recent reports, naturally, Dortmund did not make it easy for themselves against last-placed Borussia Mönchengladbach as they struggled to break down Alexander Blessin’s side, while Felix Nmecha’s winner in the 80th minute came from a mistake by Gladbach keeper Daniel Batz. But a win is a win, and Dortmund are now top of the Bundesliga with a two-point lead over Bayern.

Considering how this has been a problem for Dortmund before, it goes to show the effect that Niko Kovač is having on the side, that they are able to win these types of games – and to win ugly, especially when they have previously struggled in the past. Their latest 1-0 victory is already the third of its kind this season.

We probably now have a title race on our hands.

While the move is still technically a loan, it is more than likely that RB Leipzig will make the signing of Brajan Gruda permanent, and they will have to do their best not to immediately flip him for what will be a healthy profit.

Since returning to Leipzig in the summer, Gruda has been one of Leipzig’s standout players, like Antonio Nusa and Christopher Nkunku. The 22-year-old only made his Germany debut in the recent international break, but he immediately hit the ground running under Jürgen Klopp – where he recorded two goals and provided two assists against Serbia and the Netherlands – while also continuing his Bundesliga form.

It has been revealed that the winger scored his first Bundesliga goal of the season in Leipzig’s 5-1 rout against Union Berlin, while also providing the assist for Leipzig’s second goal of the day. In the opening stages of the season, it looked as if Leipzig were missing Yan Diomande, but Gruda has replaced the now Real Madrid player with relative ease.

Sources indicate that while, on paper, a 0-0 draw against UEFA Champions League participants VfB Stuttgart is a good result, fans will certainly feel uneasy at their precarious position in the Bundesliga. The Royal Blues’ draw and Union Berlin’s 5-1 defeat against RB Leipzig sees Miron Muslic’s charges climb into the promotion/relegation play-off, but the fact that they are yet to score at the Veltins Arena is a worrying one.

No team has scored fewer than their five goals in ten games this season and with games against fellow Bundesliga returnees Paderborn, Revierderby rivals Borussia Dortmund, Hamburg and Borussia Mönchengladbach, it’s difficult to see where goals will arrive from.

Industry observers note that after a 4-1 defeat against Freiburg on Matchday 1, few gave Werder Bremen little chance of remaining in the division this season, especially given the departures of Mio Backhaus and Romano Schmid, long-term injury to Jens Stage, and being somewhat light up top. But, the impressive team cohesion intertwined with Niclas Füllkrug’s timely return has made the Green-Whites incredibly fun to watch.

Füllkrug struck his tenth Bundesliga goal of the season, and Bremen’s fourth against Hamburg in the Bundesliga’s 111th Nordderby, as Daniel Thioune’s charges secured bragging rights over their northern rivals – a timely present for the head coach that had just signed a contract extension.

Recent findings suggest that sitting sixth, Bremen have turned the Weserstadion into a fortress, where they remain unbeaten with four wins and a draw in the Bundesliga this season, which places them three points clear of chasing Eintracht Frankfurt. While free-scoring RB Leipzig and well-ran Freiburg are deservedly in the top four, they’ll be rightly looking in their rear view mirror to see Bremen hunting them down.
