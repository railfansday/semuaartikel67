Royal Caribbean has announced the appointments of Greg Nicholas as senior director, Singapore, North & South APAC, and Megan Smith as head of marketing, Singapore, North & South APAC.

The appointments strengthen the company’s leadership team as it continues to expand across Asia Pacific, Royal Caribbean stated in a statement.

Based in Singapore, Nicholas will lead commercial operations across North and South APAC markets, while Smith will oversee regional marketing strategy and execution.

Together, they will play a key role in supporting Royal Caribbean’s continued growth, enhancing commercial capabilities and delivering the world’s best family vacations to guests across the region, Royal Caribbean added.

“These appointments come at an important time as we continue to grow our business and expand deployment across Asia. We are further strengthening our regional commercial capabilities and bringing our teams closer together to capture the opportunities ahead,” mentioned Ben Bouldin, president of Greater China and managing director Asia, Royal Caribbean.

According to the statement, Nicholas brings nearly a decade of experience with the company, having held a range of commercial leadership roles across Australia and New Zealand.

Most recently, he served as director, commercial planning for AUNZ, helping shape commercial and deployment strategies across the region.

Smith joins the Singapore team after leading marketing initiatives across China, EMEA, and the UK and Ireland.

The company said her experience spans brand strategy, customer marketing, partnerships, loyalty and trade marketing.

The appointments come as Royal Caribbean expands its presence in Singapore and Asia Pacific, with the Navigator of the Seas offering a year-long season in the market through October 2027, followed by the Quantum of the Seas.
