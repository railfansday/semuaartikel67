In a recent development, following the rollout of Siri AI to select Apple TV models with tvOS 27.2.1, Apple today expanded availability to users running the tvOS 27.3 developer beta. Here are the details.

According to recent reports, last Friday, Apple released tvOS 27.2.1, bringing the newly introduced Siri AI to Apple TV 4K 2nd-gen and 3rd-gen models. The feature requires a nearby, paired device with Siri AI enabled.

The enhancement came just hours after the official launch of the Apple TV 4K (4th generation), which ships with Siri AI support out of the box.

It has been revealed that however, users running tvOS 27.3 beta 1 still had no access to the latest Siri AI. This means that although the feature was available in the latest public release, users on the beta were left without it.

Earlier tonight, Apple released tvOS 27.3 beta 2, which, as leaker pdfu noted in a post on X, now includes a new setup screen inviting customers to enable Siri AI.

It has been revealed that new Siri AI upsell screen on tvOS 27.3 beta 2. pic.twitter.com/VIunVgCptG.

9to5Mac was able to confirm the change, with the upcoming splash screen introducing Siri AI and giving users the option to try the new assistant or continue using Siri Classic.

With Siri AI now available on both the public and beta versions of tvOS, the new HomePod mini 2 is still waiting for the feature. Apple previously confirmed that Siri AI would arrive in a future software update, but it was not included in last Friday’s HomePod 27.2.1 release, and the manufacturer has yet to announce a rollout date.
