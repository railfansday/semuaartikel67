It’s beginning to look a lot like Christmas, and if you’re settling on a holiday decorating vision, you’re likely looking for pieces that feel festive without dipping into tacky territory. So our editors sorted through Amazon’s vast selection of holiday decor to find 30 non-tacky finds you’ll be proud to display.

Whether you’re drawn to vintage-inspired charm, opulence and glamour, or a traditional Christmas look, these gems make decorating easier—and many are timeless enough to bring out year after year. Shop classic tree trimmings, wreaths and garlands, elegant mantel accents, and elevated pieces for your holiday tablescape. Whether you’re looking for a tabletop tree or plaid pillow covers for a Ralph Lauren-inspired holiday look, you’ll find it ahead starting at $12.

Recent findings suggest that nothing notes Ralph Lauren-inspired Christmas quite like tartan. These plaid pillow covers are available in a handful of coordinating color combinations, so at just $21 for a two-pack, we suggest snagging a few options to mix and match.

According to recent reports, even those who are picky about bedding can see the charm in swapping your linens for the holiday season. This red-and-white set is actually reversible, giving you the choice between a toile awash in a festive holiday print and classic stripes.

According to recent reports, this pre-lit garland brimming with berries and pinecones is a low-stress garland option. Wind it around a dramatic staircase, place it on top of your mantel, or even drape it across your dining table.

In a recent development, whether you want to add a secondary tree to a bedroom or live in a small space and lack room for a full-size tree, check out this adorable tabletop option. It’s equipped with over 50 lights, has flocked branches, and is easy to add tiny baubles to.

Industry observers note that dress up your kitchen or bathroom with these festive cotton towels, featuring a traditional English toile print and classic plaid. They’re a charming complement to your favorite holiday hand soap.

Recent findings suggest that your Christmas decorating isn’t complete until you add a wreath to your front door. This year, snag a pre-lit option adorned with pinecones, flocked branches, and berries. Reviewers are impressed by how lifelike it looks.

Sources indicate that for a vintage-inspired Christmas, these golden-hued jingle bells bring a warm, antique feel to your decor. Hang them on the tree or a front door wreath to welcome guests, or use them as a festive accent on your table.
