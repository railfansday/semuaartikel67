PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Spin.AI, a SaaS and browser security company, today announced that its browser extension risk assessment insights are now available in Microsoft Edge for Business, surfaced within the Edge management service. Organizations can use them to help evaluate browser extension risk, alongside the other insights and admin controls that Edge for Business already provides.

Recent findings suggest that browser extensions have become a significant source of supply chain risk inside the enterprise. Spin.AI's AI Compliance and Browser Extension Risks in 2026 research found that 50% of extensions in use are classified as high risk, and that organizations with 2,000+ employees use an average of 1,454 browser extensions.

Risk also changes after approval. In the 2025 RedDirection campaign, extensions that carried verified badges and high install counts turned malicious through routine updates, ultimately affecting more than 16 million users.

"The browser is where work happens now, and every extension running in it is part of the enterprise supply chain," remarked Dmitry Dontov, Founder and CEO of Spin.AI. "Security teams can't manually review thousands of extensions, and they can't assume an extension that was safe last month is safe today. Making our risk assessment insights available in Microsoft Edge for Business gives IT and security teams more context at the moment they decide which extensions belong in their environment.".
