A free browser game is going viral for letting players spend as much time as they want petting adorable cats, complete with different fur patterns, customizable sizes, and relaxing purring sounds.

Created by Japanese developer zubu, Cat Petting Simulator was released on November 22, 2026, and has swiftly become a hit among cat lovers.

Industry observers note that the concept couldn’t be simpler. Rather than completing challenges or earning points, players are presented with a cat lounging on a couch, and their only objective is to pet it.

Using a mouse or touchscreen, players can stroke the feline’s back, watching as its fur and body react to their movements.

According to Famitsu, the game offers 13 different cat appearances, including black cats, white cats, orange tabbies, and Siamese cats.

Players can also adjust their cat’s size and body width, allowing them to create everything from a small kitten to a particularly chunky feline.

It has been revealed that the attention to detail is surprisingly impressive. As players move their hands across the cat, its fur shifts realistically while its body squishes and stretches in response.

In a recent development, the game has already attracted considerable attention online. As Automaton reported, players have praised the realistic petting animations, with some joking that it’s their game of the year despite launching days after GTA 6.

The developer’s original announcement also surpassed 120,000 likes on X within a day of release.
