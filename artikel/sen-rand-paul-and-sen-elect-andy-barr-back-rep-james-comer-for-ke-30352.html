In a recent development, rep. James Comer, R-Ky., discusses Democrats’ calls to impeach and investigate their political opponents and President Donald Trump on ‘The Ingraham Angle.’.

It has been revealed that sen. Rand Paul, R-Ky., and Sen.-elect Andy Barr, R-Ky., are both backing Rep. James Comer, R-Ky., for Kentucky governor.

According to recent reports, "I’m proud to endorse my friend @JamesComer to be Kentucky’s next Governor! Please help him to get there!" Paul declared in a Monday night post on X.

Barr, a House Republican who just won a U.S. Senate contest during the midterms, also threw his support behind Comer's candidacy.

"No one will fight harder for Kentucky families, Kentucky farmers, and Kentucky's future than my friend James Comer. Kentucky conservatives have built a strong legislature, and now we need a Governor who will work with them, not against them," Barr said in a Tuesday statement.

It has been revealed that "With James Comer in the Governor's mansion, we'll finish the job on eliminating the state income tax and unleash Kentucky energy producers to lower costs and create jobs. I'm proud to stand with James Comer for Governor," he added.

Sources indicate that comer, who has served in the House of Representatives for a decade, just won re-election during the midterms. The lawmaker, who chairs the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform, declared a Bluegrass State gubernatorial bid on Monday.

He previously ran for Kentucky governor in 2015, but lost the GOP gubernatorial primary by only 83 votes.

The state's 2027 race for governor will take place in slightly less than a year, and the primary is set for May 18.
