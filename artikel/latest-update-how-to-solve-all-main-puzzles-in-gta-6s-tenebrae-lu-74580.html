Sources indicate that if you decide you’d rather sit at home all day and play a pretentious, cerebral puzzle game instead of go on heists, Tenebrae Lux is waiting for Jason and Lucia on their JoyStation in GTA VI. While on the surface it looks like just a funny gag poking at auteur culture (and one developer in particular), Tenebrae Lux is a surprisingly solid, short puzzle game that (if you’re not using this guide) may take you a few hours to get through.

But I’m assuming you’re here because you either want to beat it quickly, or you’ve gotten so stuck (as I did) on a few of the puzzles that you’re desperate for help. I get you. Here’s as simple a walkthrough as I could manage for Tenebrae Lux:.

You’ll start out on the shoreline. You can’t do much: just walk around and examine objects. To start with, you’re going to want to head to the left side of the shore (assuming you’re facing the shore from the beach you start on) where there are multiple run-down shacks lit by major bright lights.

Industry observers note that inside these shacks are three puzzles on gridded boards. On those grids, multiple pieces are placed with different 3D shapes atop: three-tiered tall ones, middling spiky ones, and flat ones with a tiny dot in the middle. The flat ones don’t move at all, the middle-sized spiky ones can slide across the board and clear the flat ones if they land next to them, and the three-tiered tall ones and middle-sized ones clear one another in the same fashion. Your goal is to clear all the pieces off the board.

You can do these puzzles in any order. One of them is easy and straightforward, but the other two require a little bit more thinking. You’ll have to use the fact that the flat pieces don’t move and cannot be cleared by the three-tiered pieces to set up the three-tiers so they and the mountain pieces can clear one another once all the flat ones are off the board.

The three main puzzles in this area should be fairly simple to do, though there are a couple of hidden ones you can optionally do that are more difficult. On at least one, you might need to set up your board so you have two three-tier pieces set up with a gap between them that a middle-sized piece can easily get into after clearing a flat one. When you enter the room upstairs in the bunker, you enter a loop that will keep perpetuating each time you go through the door and up the stairs. The room will be almost identical each time you enter it, but for two things. The first will be the calendar, which will show the date as being one of six different dates, and the second will be one of the other objects in the room.

Your goal is to find a six-digit code and enter it into the phone, followed by the # sign, to proceed.

In order to get the code, you need to pay attention to what day it is, and what is different in the room on that date. If you keep going up the stairs over and over, the dates will eventually cycle back through, so if you miss one don’t worry, you can keep going to see it again. The dates are as follows: October 11, 14, 20, 23, 27, and 30. You won’t see the dates in any particular order, but you’ll need to put them in that order when you input the numbers for each date into the phone.

Recent findings suggest that the numbers themselves, you get from finding what’s “different” in the room on a given date, and working out what number the difference corresponds to. The bridge has multiple rows of weird symbols on it. You have to walk over the correct symbol in order to proceed. If you walk over the wrong one, a lamp will go out. If you do it wrong three times, you fail and the puzzle resets.

The trick to figuring out the correct symbols is in the ruins and the poem on the plaque just outside.

The items will be scattered across the ruins. Also in the ruins you will find a bunch of weird, esoteric symbols glowing on the ruined archways above. These symbols correspond to the symbols on the bridge.

Recent findings suggest that to solve this, you need to visit each of the items in the order they appear in the poem, and match the item with the symbol on the ruins that corresponds to it, then follow the symbols across the bridge in order (the direction they’re facing doesn’t matter). It can be a bit tricky to figure out which symbol goes with which item. The best way I can describe it is the symbol for each item is the one that’s over the doorway “entrance” to each item, so the one you have to pass under to reach it.
