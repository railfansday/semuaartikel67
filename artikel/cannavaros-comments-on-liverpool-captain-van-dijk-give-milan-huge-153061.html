Fabio Cannavaro’s words have fuelled the rumours that Liverpool defender Virgil van Dijk would consider a move to AC Milan.

Cannavaro – who is now the coach of the Uzbekistan national team – offered a clue about the future of Van Dijk after a conversation that he had with the defender, revealed in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport.

It has been revealed that “I saw him at the World Cup and he told me he would like to have an experience in Italy, he is very fascinated. It would be nice to see Van Dijk at Milan , in recent years he has combined great physique and super speed,” he mentioned (via Calciomercato.com).

In recent weeks, a number of sources have claimed that Milan have had Van Dijk on their list of defensive reinforcements since the last summer window, when they decided to explore the possibility of signing the ex-Celtic man.

While it didn’t happen then, the fact that the 35-year-old’s deal is up at the end of the season might provide an opportunity next summer. It will be a complex negotiation to carry out, but at the moment Gerry Cardinale seems determined to try.

A different centre-back signing could be pursued in the meantime, but Ruben Amorim’s recent comments dismissing the idea of a move for his compatriot and former player Goncalo Inacio suggest a willingness to wait for the right man.
