Recent findings suggest that isn’t this a little surreal? After years of reading OMAAT, here I am writing my very first post.

In a recent development, first of all, a huge thank you to Ben for such a lovely introduction, and for everyone who gave me such a warm welcome in the comments already. I’m really looking forward to getting to know the OMAAT community Ben has built over the years.

In the first of what will undoubtedly be a lot of posts for One Mile at a Time, I thought I’d share a bit about who I am and what you can expect from me going forward.

I’m literally asking myself that as I write this.

Recent findings suggest that funnily enough, it was never my dream to be a writer, journalist, blogger, or anything similar. The only thing I can ever remember wanting to be “when I grew up” was a pilot. Despite several applications and reaching the final stage of cadet schemes with Aer Lingus and British Airways, it didn’t work out for me.

It has been revealed that determined to work in the industry, I quit my soul-destroying city job in London to do a Master’s in Air Transport Management at the University of Loughborough in the U.K. I was then offered 2 very different jobs: a junior marketing role with Air Transat, or to be part of a small team starting up TPG’s U.K. office. As a natural risk-taker, I took the latter. After almost 8 years of writing for various publications and dabbling in some video content, I’ve realized that this is what I was meant to be doing all along — the job just didn’t exist when I wanted to be a pilot as a kid.

So why OMAAT? Why now? I spent the last 2 years traveling the world, working remotely, in search of somewhere I felt connected enough to call home. In my heart of hearts, I always knew that was going to be Rio, so I moved here in September this year. I’ve also been considering a more permanent home for my writing and my online presence, so when happenstance did its thing and Ben offered me a full-time online home at OMAAT, it was the perfect opportunity.

I’m honored to have been entrusted to write alongside Ben, whose expertise I’ve learned from over the years. While I don’t promise to be an expert on everything, my almost 8 years of experience in this industry should hopefully mean my expertise meets the standard expected here.

It has been revealed that what started out as a passion for planes and aviation has turned into a love of all things travel, loyalty, and the miles and points that help make it happen. I’m really looking forward to sharing some of my learnings, experiences, and tips and tricks from the past couple of years that should complement Ben’s breadth of knowledge.

To start with, much of what you’ll see from me will be written. But as Ben said, the plan is to gradually bring in more video and social content, which I’m really looking forward to helping OMAAT with.

Industry observers note that I’m also a self-proclaimed expert on Rio and Brazil (I’ve been coming here since 2009), so if there’s any specific Rio, Brazil, or wider South American travel advice, reviews, or insights you’d like, I’d be more than happy to help, even if it means I have to hop a plane for the research. 😝.

According to recent reports, I’ll leave it at that for now, as I need to get packing to fly across the Pond for an exciting aviation event this week. All will be revealed soon. 🤓✈️.

According to recent reports, I’m very excited and honored to be Ben’s first full-time writer in a very long time. Today is the first day of a latest adventure for myself, Ben, and the OMAAT behind-the-scenes team, and I can’t wait to bring you, the readers, along for the ride.

It has been revealed that membership is free and takes no more information than commenting as a guest. Earn status points for good contributions, and see fewer ads as your status grows.
