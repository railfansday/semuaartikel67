Ruben Amorim had some interesting comments after the Frosinone win, including why he is not a fan of a term that is going viral.

Industry observers note that milan put on a five-star display at San Siro on Sunday night as they routed that Canarini. All five of their goals came via flowing counter-attacks, which has led to the tag ‘AmorimBall’ doing the rounds on social media, with many believing things are starting to truly click.

The head coach is keeping his feet firmly on the ground though, as he spoke after the game about the need to find consistency both within matches but also from game-to-game. In this sense, Thursday’s trip to face Olympiacos is another big test.

Amorim was present in the press conference room at San Siro following the win to answer questions from journalists, with his comments relayed by MilanNews.

According to recent reports, “I don’t like that expression. I think we play games to win, we want to dominate the opponent. I don’t think we succeeded today: every game has its own story, and today it was man-on-man.

“Today we didn’t have to prepare much: the idea was simply to give the ball to Pulisic, to Moreira, and then exploit the combinations of these players, the understandings they already have, and conquer space.

“Today you saw that we didn’t spend much time near the opponent’s penalty area and we scored five goals, because the space wasn’t there, it was forty meters away to attack on the run.

According to recent reports, “That’s why Cisse came on to play as a striker and not, for example, Camarda: because there was space behind him and Cisse is very good at that. So, I think we’ve played very good games in the past, but today it was just a question of the quality of the players, of one-on-ones, of reading the game.

In a recent development, “Today it wasn’t a question of the coach, I think, but of the players. And they, since it was man-on-man, just had to do what they can do. when they’re so good: beating the man and opening up space. So today the credit goes entirely to the players.”.

We’ve never seen a Rabiot like this….

“I watched the France game and I think he’s one of the best players on the team. So I think his importance is paramount. You can see there’s rotation, there’s a lot of talent. France has a lot of talent, but Rabiot is always there. There’s a reason for that.

Sources indicate that “And then, if you look at the way France play, with Mbappé, with the way all these players position themselves on the pitch, sometimes with four players with Olise as the No. 10, he has to stay there to play a different kind of game.

According to recent reports, “With us, he’s the player who has to push the team forward, he’s the decisive element. In France, however, he has a different job to do. So it depends more on his role in the national team and his role at Milan.

“This changes his way of interpreting the game, and it’s further proof of how Rabiot can play by adapting to the team he’s in. For me, therefore, he’s one of the best midfielders in Europe. I hope his contract renewal comes soon.”.

“Today’s match wasn’t just about tactics. We saw changes in position, one-on-one situations with plenty of space: it seemed like a crazy game. What I felt against Genoa and in the other matches was that we were too far away, we didn’t reach the final third of the pitch.

“We’re not like the major teams: if you look at the big teams, like Juventus, they might struggle to get results, but you see the volume they produce. For us, it’s a bit of the opposite, so I’m really worried that, if we don’t improve, we won’t be able to maintain this number of points.

Recent findings suggest that “Today, however, was a step forward: we saw many players attacking the depth, something we hadn’t seen against Ferencvaros. In the other matches, we’d managed the ball well, but today we needed pace and runs.

“We need to be able to play both games within the same game, but today was clearly the type of game we needed to win.”.
