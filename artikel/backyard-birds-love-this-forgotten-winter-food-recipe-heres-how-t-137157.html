Sources indicate that insect eaters get the most out of it. Bluebirds rarely eat seeds, and woodpeckers, nuthatches, and wrens lean on insects for much of the year. Come winter, bugs get hard to find. A rich, fatty food fills that gap with the calories birds need to stay warm through long nights, at a time when natural food can be scarce.
