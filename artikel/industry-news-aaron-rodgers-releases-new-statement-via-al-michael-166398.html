Sources indicate that aaron Rodgers made quite a few waves on his way out of the Pittsburgh Steelers organization earlier this week. He had more to say during their first game without him — a game they won 27-5 over the Denver Broncos.

Sources indicate that prime Video broadcaster Al Michaels relayed a text message he got from the former MVP during the Steelers' game against the Denver Broncos, which attempted to rebut some of his critics and explain why he handled things the way he did. Michaels read the text messages early in the fourth quarter of the Black Friday game.

First, Rodgers complained about the idea he has committed some sort of locker room faux pas by airing out dirty laundry involving Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, whom Rodgers accused of a number of shortcomings during an appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday:.

"A lot of talk about the sanctity of the locker room … The code or sanctity exists to protect the integrity of the locker room, not to provide immunity from accountability for its leadership.".

That rebuttal could be aimed at a number of notable figures:.

Rodgers' quarterbacking peer Tom Brady stated, "The sanctity of an NFL locker room, it's a very sacred place.".

Rodgers' former coach Mike Tomlin mentioned it was sad "to see laundry aired out that way in a public forum, those are things that kind of run counterculture to professional football.".

In a recent development, steelers owner Art Rooney II noted, "In general, I think, historically, what's in the locker room stays in the locker room. That's the way it's always been. That's the way it should be as far as I'm concerned.".

Former Steelers quarterbacks Terry Bradshaw and Russell Wilson both expressed their disapproval.

Aaron Rodgers still has something to say after the NFL's loudest divorce.

As for how things played out with the Steelers, it's known that Rodgers made some sort of ultimatum — reportedly that he wanted to call plays on offense or be released — and the Steelers opted to announce his benching late Tuesday night and then release him Wednesday after he aired his grievances against McCarthy.

Rodgers claimed he got no communication from McCarthy until his benching was announced, and that he only appeared on McAfee's show after the Steelers barred him from their facility:.

"They took this public, never heard from the head coach, then they leak at 11:00 p.m. They also barred me from the facility. That's why I went to McAfee.".

Odds are this won't be the last time we hear about Rodgers on this.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, his former teammates defeated the Broncos 27-5 on Friday behind a dominant defensive performance. Rodgers did appear to receive a shout-out from wide receiver DK Metcalf, though.
