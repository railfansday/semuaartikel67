Yahoo Sports is giving fans another way to play fantasy basketball with Perfect 5, a free-to-play game featuring each week's NBA on Prime doubleheader. The rules are simple: pick five players, stay within a $15 budget and rack up fantasy points through scoring, rebounds, assists, defensive stats and 3s.

Friday's slate features the Raptors at Pistons (7:30 p.m. ET) and Rockets at Nuggets (10 p.m. ET), both games NBA Cup Group Play matchups. The injury report is doing most of the talking this week. Cade Cunningham, Alperen Şengün and Scottie Barnes are all questionable, and Detroit is already without Jalen Duren and Duncan Robinson. The Raptors won't have RJ Barrett and Collin Murray-Boyles.

Sources indicate that using Yahoo projections, each player's last five games, the betting lines and the latest injury news, I ran 20,000 simulations of the slate against a simulated Yahoo field built from this week's pick rates. I'm paying up for the best player on the board and filling in around him with $2 and $3 players whose roles just got bigger. Here's my lineup:.

Recent findings suggest that this group finished in the top 1% of the simulated field 16.9% of the time — the best rate of the 20,000 lineups I tested — and beat every entry in the field more often than any other build (1.04%). Only about 0.03% of the field has these exact five.

Recent findings suggest that jokić leads the slate at 54.00 projected fantasy points, more than three points clear of anyone else, on a line of 24.1 points, 10.7 rebounds, 8.9 assists, 1.7 steals and a block. I like the situational spot: Denver is a 3.5-point home favorite, and the 227.5 total is the highest on the slate. Yes, three-quarters of the field has him, but the matchup sets up well, too.

Şengün took a shot to the face in Wednesday's win over Indiana and didn't return. Ime Udoka said he has swelling in his right eye and a scratched cornea in his left. If Şengün sits, Houston's answer for Jokić is Steven Adams, and a village. Houston won the first meeting 110-100 in Houston on Nov. 9; this one is in Denver. Fading Jokić is a real option in a top-heavy contest like this (see the contrarian build below), but my sims say he's worth going chalk.

According to recent reports, jenkins is the best value on the board and I like him whether Cunningham plays or not. He's projected for 33.02 fantasy points at $3, ahead of several $4 players. He's been hot this week, too: 25 points, 5 rebounds and 5 assists in Tuesday's win over San Antonio, then 17 points and 9 assists as the starting point guard in Wednesday's win at Newly introduced Orleans. Over his last five games, he's averaging about 36 fantasy points.

Cunningham sat Wednesday with right knee soreness on the second night of a back-to-back, and he's questionable again Friday. The expectation is he plays. But if he sits, Jenkins runs the offense and his outlook gets even better. That built-in insurance is why he showed up in the single best lineup in nearly 47% of my sims, more than any other player on the slate.

This is my leverage play. Walter is averaging 12.2 points, 2.5 3s and 1.3 steals, and he's projected for 27.15 fantasy points. What makes him interesting is everyone around him. Leonard and Barrett are out and Barnes is questionable after Toronto held him out of the back half of its back-to-back to manage his groin. Murray-Boyles is also in concussion protocol and won't play.

Recent findings suggest that walter dropped 25 points with seven 3s against the Knicks on Tuesday, then followed it with 18 points, 7 rebounds, 6 assists and 3 steals against Washington. Toronto has lost six straight. Someone has to take shots, and he's shown he will. At 4% picked, he's the piece that separates this lineup from the field.

Huerter was in the single best lineup in 45.3% of my simulations, second only to Jenkins. He's projected for 24.15 fantasy points at $2, and his role is growing with Detroit this banged-up. Robinson (left quad strain) will be re-evaluated in a week and Huerter started Wednesday, finishing with 11 points and 7 assists. He's hit double figures in five of his last six games, while averaging 33.1 minutes a night over that span. He's a cheap source of points, 3s, assists and the occasional block, and he doesn't need Cunningham to sit to pay off.

Johnson is the second-most popular pick in my lineup. He's projected for 23.13 fantasy points at $2, starting next to Jokić in the slate's highest-scoring game. He's averaging 11.4 points, 3.7 rebounds, 1.7 3s and 1.1 steals, a steady floor for the price. His recent body of work is meh (about 21 fantasy points a game over his last five), but he still landed in the optimal lineup about a third of the time. You don't get leverage from him, but you get it from Walter and Jenkins, and that's enough. I'm banking on a bounce-back from the 15.9 fantasy points he scored in 31 minutes versus Houston on Nov. 9.

It has been revealed that using Yahoo's projections, the highest-scoring possible lineup is Jokić, Cunningham, Huerter, Johnson and Alijah Martin at 161.63 fantasy points. Mine comes in just 0.18 points behind it.

On paper, it's the max. In the sims, it finished in the top 1% only 6.6% of the time, less than half the rate of my lineup. Two things hurt it. First, Cunningham's knee: a questionable tag means there's a real chance he sits. Second, Martin is a $1 punt projected under 10 points, so you're leaning on two players to carry the build. Swapping Cunningham and Martin for Jenkins and Walter gives up 0.18 projected points and removes most of that risk.
