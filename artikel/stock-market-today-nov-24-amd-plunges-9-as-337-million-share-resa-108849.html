By RJ Fulton – Nov 24, 2026 at 5:19PM EST.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD -8.86%) plunged 8.86% on Tuesday, November 24, closing at $413.27 as renewed selling pressure swept across the semiconductor industry.

Trading volume reached 45.7 million shares, approximately 64% above its three-month daily average of 27.8 million, reflecting heightened investor activity amid the latest sell-off.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/24/26.

It has been revealed that S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.50%) fell 0.50% to 7,201, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -1.48%) declined 1.48% to 23,734. Among semiconductor peers, Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%) closed at $155.38, down 5.86%, and Intel (INTC -8.15%) closed at $54.80, down 8.15%, underscoring the pressure across the chipmakers.

AMD's latest sell-off highlights growing uncertainty surrounding the financial implications of large-scale AI partnerships. Adding to these concerns, the firm recently filed a registration covering approximately 337 million shares for potential resale, raising questions about future shareholder dilution. Although the filing does not represent an immediate sale of shares, investors should consider how these arrangements could affect existing shareholders and prices.

Industry observers note that despite the recent volatility, AMD's underlying business continues to demonstrate strong growth. The company recently reported record quarterly revenue and raised its outlook, supported by demand for its data center processors and AI accelerators.

Recent findings suggest that looking ahead, AMD's ability to translate growing AI demand into sustained profitability will be critical. While the recent decline could present an attractive entry point, the combination of potential dilution, competitive pressures, and elevated semiconductor valuations warrants caution.
