Mauricio Pochettino is having to squeeze a lot into his first days as Manchester United, but there will be time for one tried-and-tested tradition.

The Argentine touched down in Manchester on Wednesday, just hours after overseeing USA’s 2-0 win over Haiti in Cincinnati. The newly introduced head coach has conducted his first interview with club media, posed for photos and met the players.

On Thursday, Pochettino will take charge of his first training session at the club’s Carrington base before conducting his first press conference on Friday ahead of his first game on Sunday, which is a daunting trip to Anfield to take on Liverpool.

But there will see be time for a team dinner.

The Independent report that Pochettino intends on taking the players and staff out for a team bonding evening, and he will be picking up the tab.

In a recent development, it is a tradition Pochettino is stated to have first introduced the custom during his time at Southampton, believing it was an notable way to build relationships and strengthen the bond within a squad. He then did it at Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea.

It has previously been reported elsewhere that Pochettino held team dinners a number of times during his five-and-a-half-year tenure at Spurs.

With Pochettino taking charge midway through the campaign, there is no opportunity for a traditional pre-season or an extended period to get to know his players. The dinner will therefore provide an early chance for the 54-year-old and his coaching staff to assess the group's dynamics away from the training pitch.

Pochettino believes creating a strong sense of togetherness is essential to success, and the gathering will allow him to begin establishing his approach at Old Trafford. It will also give players and staff an opportunity to get to know their upcoming manager and his backroom team in a more relaxed setting.

With a demanding schedule ahead, Pochettino will hope the occasion helps lay the foundations for a successful union between players and staff.
