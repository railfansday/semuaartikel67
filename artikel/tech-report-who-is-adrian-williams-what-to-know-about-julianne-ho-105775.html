Williams is a native New Yorker, with “bold Bronx energy,” according to his Peloton biography.

Hough has been extremely mum about her latest romance, but she has shared a bit about what she was looking for before she and Williams made their relationship Instagram official.

Industry observers note that “It's all energy-based,” Williams told E! News of what she’s looking for in a potential partner. “I don't really have a type. It's more connection, and that's it.”.

And while Hough was proudly touting her single era prior to her romance with Williams, she wasn’t closing off her heart.

“I'm always looking for love,” she remarked, “whether it's in a romantic sense or friends.”.

Of course, there was one parameter when it came to whether Williams could keep in step with her, as she previously teased to E!, her new partner needed to be “someone super hot.”.
