Ben Stiller And Skyler Gisondo Starred In Night At The Museum Together. Focker In-Law Was A Full-Circle Moment

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In 2014, Skyler Gisondo joined the Night at the Museum franchise to play Ben Stiller’s son, Nick Daley. While he’d worked a lot as a kid before that, this role marked a turning point in his career. Now, he’s reteaming with Stiller for Focker In-Law on the 2026 movie schedule, and he’s playing his son…again. So, considering that, I asked the Superman actor how his first time playing Stiller’s kid impacted his work in the Meet the Parents franchise, and he told me why it was a very full-circle experience.

Recent findings suggest that as a Meet the Parents and Night at the Museum fan, I couldn’t get enough of this reunion. It was also fun to see Stiller and Gisondo reunite now, because I’ve become such a substantial fan of the work the Booksmart actor did between Night at the Museum: Secret of the Tomb and Focker In-Law. So, I asked Gisondo how his history with Stiller played into this experience on Focker In-Law; he remarked:.

Recent findings suggest that it was great. I mean, Night at the Museum was such a formative experience for me. I was 17. It was my first time being a bigger part of a bigger movie like that. And Ben really took me under his wing then, and was so good to me. And I think I had almost forgotten how much I took from that time.

Again, while Skyler Gisondo was a successful child actor, Night at the Museum: Secret of the Tomb (which you can watch with a Disney+ subscription) was a turning point for him. It makes sense that he learned a lot from Stiller on it that has impacted his career since.

Recent findings suggest that speaking of his career, after Night at the Museum, Gisondo booked roles in projects like Vacation, Santa Clarita Diet, Booksmart and more. Also, his last couple of years leading up to his reunion with Stiller have been fantastic. He played the beloved Jimmy Olsen in James Gunn’s Superman, he finished the final season of The Righteous Gemstones, and he was a standout alongside Robert Pattinson in Primetime. Truly, he has become quite the actor, and he’s one of my favorite rising stars.

Recent findings suggest that so, to hear that Stiller impacted him is really sweet. What makes it even sweeter is that while working on Focker In-Law, Gisondo realized all this as he started to notice that some of the things he does as an actor are things Stiller does too:.

In a recent development, and then getting reunited on this, like 12 years later, there was all these things I would notice Ben doing before takes or something, and realize that I've done that my whole adult life. And I kind of had forgotten where I’d gotten it from or something. And I think it was really helpful for this movie to have that kind of built-in familial sort of relationship.

Sources indicate that knowing about Stiller and Gisondo’s history certainly played into my enjoyment of the movie, especially since the last time I saw them together, Gisondo was a kid. Now, he’s all grown up, and that fact certainly plays into Focker In-Law.

To that point, in Focker In-Law, we follow Greg Focker (Stiller) as he reunites with his family, including his son Henry (Gisondo). Things get chaotic because Henry brings home his girlfriend, Olivia (Ariana Grande), and he prepares to propose to her. After that, we dive into a lot of chaos as Greg butts heads with Henry’s partner, and obviously it leads to some very funny familial hijinks. It’s silly, and it was such a fun way to see the Night at the Museum actors work together again all these years later.

Your Daily Blend of Entertainment News.

Without the knowledge of their history, their dynamic is funny in Focker In-Law. However, knowing this is the second time they’ve played father and son, and also knowing that Gisondo really had a full-circle moment realizing how much Stiller has impacted him, makes it even better.

So, if you’d like to see this Night at the Museum reunion, and if you’d like to try and clock the ways in which Stiller and Gisondo are similar as actors, you can catch Focker In-Law in theaters on November 25.
