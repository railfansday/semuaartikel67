Draymond Green rarely, if ever, refuses an opportunity to jab back at his critics.

Following the Golden State Warriors' 88-87 win over the Boston Celtics on Sunday, the veteran forward had plenty to say to those who believe he's not carrying enough of the scoring burden with Stephen Curry sidelined due to an abdominal tear.

Green scored 9 points in the Warriors' win, his highest total of the season thus far. But scoring more has never been his role and he doesn't intend to change that, even with the team's top scorer out.

"I'm never going to, in Year 15, be like 'Oh, man, Steph's out — it's my chance,'" Green told reporters, via The Athletic's Jason Jones. "When I was in Year 6, it was my chance, and I would do what I do.

"But Year 15? That would never be my mindset," he added. "As someone who can do a multitude of things on the basketball court, I would never deduce myself down to you gotta go score.".

Sources indicate that all nine of Green's points on Sunday came in the first quarter when he hit three 3-pointers. Green said after the game that Warriors coach Steve Kerr told him to look to shoot from the corner. That helped Golden State run out to a 27-22 lead after the opening 12 minutes of play.

The Celtics went ahead with a third-quarter surge, leading by 11 at one point. But the Warriors tightened up on defense, allowing them to chip away at the second-half deficit. Golden State finally took an 88-86 lead with 2:38 remaining in regulation on a 3-pointer from Brandin Podziemski.

It has been revealed that on Boston's final possession, Green prevented Jayson Tatum from driving to the basket and forced him to take a fadeaway jumper. But the 16-footer missed and the Warriors got their ninth win of the season.

Industry observers note that tatum was held to 4 points in the second half with Green defending him. That's exactly what Golden State needs Green to do, according to Kerr. Green offered to come off the bench if it would help the team, but Kerr stated his defense is too valuable throughout the game.

According to recent reports, "I told him I had no desire to bring him on the bench because we've got to dig this thing out with defense," Kerr noted, via The Athletic. "And he remains one of the great defenders in the league. He indicated that tonight.".

In a recent development, green is averaging 3.6 points in 14 games this season. If that continues, it would be his lowest scoring average since his rookie campaign in 2012-13. He's also grabbing 4.8 rebounds and dishing out 6.3 assists while shooting 33% on 21 3-point attempts.
