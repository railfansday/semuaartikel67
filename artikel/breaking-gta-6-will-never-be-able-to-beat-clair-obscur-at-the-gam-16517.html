Breaking: GTA 6 Will Never Be Able to Beat Clair Obscur at The Game Awards

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that the triumph of Grand Theft Auto 6 at this year's Game Awards has seemed inevitable all year — especially in the face of what has turned out to be relatively weak competition. So you might assume that it's going to break records as it sweeps the board, just as it's going to break records commercially. But you'd be wrong.

Sources indicate that the records for most nominations and most wins were set last year by Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Sandfall Interactive's RPG was nominated for 13 awards and won nine. It is practically, if not theoretically, impossible for GTA 6 to beat these totals.

Although the two games are very different, Clair Obscur was similarly dominant in the awards narrative. Like GTA 6, nothing emerged all year that could realistically compete with it. And although it's a much smaller production, it excelled in the craft categories: performance, narrative, art direction, and music. It was nominated for, and won, most of the categories it was eligible for. The same looks likely to be true for GTA 6. So how come it can't beat it?

Recent findings suggest that crucially, Clair Obscur was eligible for two categories where GTA 6 can't compete: Best Independent Game and Best Debut Indie Game. Some have quibbled with Clair Obscur's definition as an indie, as it was a reasonably large-scale and well-funded production. But it was made by an independent studio for an indie-specialist publisher, so it's not an unreasonable definition. Either way, it happened.

Recent findings suggest that clair Obscur was nominated for Game of the Year, for all five craft categories (Game Direction, Narrative, Art Direction, Score and Music, Audio Design), and for the two indie categories. It had no less than three nominations in the Best Performance category (half the field). It was nominated for Best Role-Playing Game, its genre category. (Games can only be nominated in one genre category.) And it was nominated for the fan-voted Players' Voice award.

In a recent development, let's assume that GTA 6 matches Clair Obscur beat-for-beat across GOTY, the craft categories, its genre category (Best Action/Adventure Game, in this case), and in Players' Voice. That seems a pretty safe assumption. Even if it can match Clair Obscur's three performance nominations (a tall order, since jury members haven't spent as long with the characters), it would have to make up those two indie categories somewhere else to equal Clair Obscur's record.

According to recent reports, after ruling out the other genres, there are only a few other categories still on the table where that would be possible. GTA 6 is ineligible for most of them: Best Multiplayer, Ongoing Game, Mobile Game, VR/AR Game, and Community Support (as an unreleased game, it at the moment has no actual community). It hardly seems likely to get picked for Best Family Game or Games for Impact (however worthy it may or may not be). That only leaves Innovation in Accessibility. I have no idea about GTA 6's credentials in this field. It might be great, although, given its late release, it will be untested by the public.

So, in order to match Clair Obscur's record (never mind beat it), GTA 6 would have to be nominated for the accessibility award and get an unprecedented four Performance nominations. It's theoretically possible, but incredibly unlikely. I see GTA 6 netting out at 9 or 10 nominations, depending on whether both of the actors playing Jason and Lucia get nominated (or perhaps one of them plus a character part, such as Stephen Root's Brian Heder).

Sources indicate that it will also be very difficult for GTA 6 to beat Clair Obscur's nine wins. Clair Obscur ran an almost perfect game, winning in every category it was nominated for but two: Audio Design and Players' Voice. GTA 6 would have to win every single category I see it being nominated in to match that. It would need to be nominated in, and win, Innovation in Accessibility to beat it.

Neither scenario is impossible, but both are highly unlikely. These are two records GTA 6 is not going to break. In fact, given the unique circumstances surrounding Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — an indie game with AAA production values and a beloved cast of actors — I expect these records are going to stand for a long, long time.
