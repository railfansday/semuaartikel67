Industry News: West Brom can trust 23-year-old to bounce back from costly moment in Swansea draw, starting against Bolton Wanderers: Opinion

West Brom made it seven Championship games without defeat on Saturday as they drew 2-2 with Swansea City away from home. WBA went ahead on two occasions and were pegged back both times, but the most frustrating part of the tie was that they missed a gilt-edged chance to go 3-1 up.

After Rabby Nzingoula put the Baggies 2-1 up, Jayson Molumby was fouled from a set piece and the visitors were given a penalty. Isaac Price, who tapped home the opener, stepped up and saw his panenka attempt saved by Swans’ shot-stopper Lawrence Vigouroux.

The fluffed attempt was quickly followed by Elijah Just’s equaliser, and it prompted serious frustration towards Price. It proved to be a costly moment, but West Brom can rely on their star man to bounce back.

Industry observers note that price’s development has been fascinating to watch since joining West Brom. He played almost entirely in central positions in his youth career at Everton and he stayed there while at Standard Liege for the most part, occasionally filling in as a right wing-back.

According to recent reports, however, since joining West Brom, Price has become a true goalscoring attacking midfielder. He initially played in a central role as a No. 10, but in 2026 he has locked down a place on the left wing, and the 23-year-old has become a dependable goal threat.

Sources indicate that not only that, but the level of confidence Price is playing with for Albion has brought flair to his game. He isn’t opposed to taking on his man with quick footwork, improvising with a flick, or attempting a panenka penalty.

The WBA star produced a brilliantly executed chipped spot-kick against Blackburn Rovers. Some may argue that it was foolish of Price to attempt another just weeks later, and they’d be right in hindsight. However, the audacity to try back-to-back panenka penalties is part of being a flair player, and it emphasises the freedom and confidence he is playing with.

Sources indicate that was that confidence misplaced on Saturday? Perhaps. But if it had come off, there’s no doubt that Albion fans and pundits alike would be lauding the cheek of Price for pulling off such a finish once again.

Price will be determined to bounce back from Saturday’s penalty miss, and there’s no reason to doubt that he can. The West Brom star has been in fantastic form this season, and he has arguably become one of their most crucial players.

Despite the miss, Price now has three goals and an assist in his last three Championship games. He can be trusted to deliver goals from the left-hand side and plays a crucial role in build-up play, as his technical ability from his previous time as a central midfielder helps him play incisive passes and maintain control of the ball.

In a recent development, WBA face Bolton Wanderers on Wednesday night, and Price can produce an instant response to his Swansea City mishap. The Trotters play on the front foot and there will be gaps in behind for the Baggies’ attackers to exploit.

Price will likely match up against right-back Luca Stephenson, who is a dependable and energetic player. He has been booked four times in seven games though, so he could be worth targeting in one-on-ones.
