A racehorse sharing its name with IShowSpeed has claimed victory in Australia, adding another win to its growing record.

Streamer and internet personality IShowSpeed is well known for his athletic exploits, having challenged many, including fellow content creators, Olympic sprinters, and animals, to foot races.

Industry observers note that in April 2026, the streamer challenged a racehorse to a sprint during his livestreamed tour of Barbados. Despite demanding several rematches and insisting some rounds ended in draws, Speed was ultimately unable to outrun the animal.

On November 20, a horse named I Show Speed won the Jefferies Australia Class 2 Handicap at Bong Bong racecourse in Australia.

The four-year-old gelding, trained by Todd Smart and ridden by jockey Leandro Ribeiro, finished 1.3 lengths ahead of second-place Mystic Air in the 1,000-metre race.

According to Racenet’s racing records, this marked the horse’s third career victory, having previously won races at Canberra Acton in July 2025 and June 2026.

A post shared by SKY Racing (@skyracingau).

Industry observers note that the horse actually has a heartwarming backstory. According to a statement from Todd Smart Racing, I Show Speed was born prematurely, but overcame its difficult start to win its debut race in July 2025.

“We couldn’t be prouder of our little fighter I SHOW SPEED,” the stable wrote, when celebrating its debut victory.

Despite the racehorse sharing its name with the streamer, it remains unclear whether the horse was deliberately named after the streamer.
