By LEWIS STEELE, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 12:32 EST, 20 November 2026 | Updated: 18:17 EST, 20 November 2026.

Jack Grealish has been offered an olive branch by Everton boss David Moyes and said the sanctioned star is available for selection this weekend.

Sources indicate that the Manchester City loanee was punished by the club for failing to report to training sessions and missed several matches. He has not featured for four games: one because of illness, then a ‘breach of club discipline’ and a lack of fitness thereafter.

But Grealish will be considered for the squad when the in-form Toffees travel to Brentford on Monday night.

Moyes noted: ‘He has served his punishment and we will treat him as we were treating him before.

‘We will get on with things. The team is going really well at the moment and what I don’t want to do is turn it into a Jack story because it shouldn’t be.’.

Pushed if that suggests he will not play on Monday night, Moyes replied: ‘He could easily. He is back in the squad, he is a good player. Who knows?’.

Jack Grealish is available for Everton again after serving his punishment and regaining fitness.

Everton have won their last three games and go into Monday’s match sixth in the table.

In a recent development, asked what Grealish can offer, the boss added: ‘Jack can offer us a lot. I want more from him, I want him to show more than he has done. I want him to give me more assists, goals.

‘But there's lots of things about him which we need and he's good at retaining the ball, giving us good possession, carrying. So we hope that he can get back to those levels and give us what we need.

‘I think this time last year he was probably sitting on five or six assists so we're hoping to get that.’.

Grealish's social media apology came after he featured in a behind-closed-doors match during the international break.

'Nice to be back out there with the boys today after a few good days training!' Grealish wrote. 'I want to apologise to the fans (and) everyone at the club for not being able to help the team the last few weeks.

'I love playing for this club (and) can't wait to be back playing in front of you guys in an Everton shirt.

'See you all soon (blue heart)'.

At the start of his press conference Moyes was presented with a framed memento of his time in charge by defender James Tarkowski and former player Leon Osman after he set a new club record of 593 games in the hotseat.

Moyes remarked: 'It’s something that I’m really thrilled about but, if I’m being honest, it’s not something I really think about. I understand I’ve suddenly become the manager who’s had more games at Everton than anybody before and that’s a real honour and privilege to be put in that position.'.
