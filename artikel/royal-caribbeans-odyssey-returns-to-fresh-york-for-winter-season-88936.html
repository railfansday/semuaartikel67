The Odyssey of the Seas has returned to its homeport in the Newly introduced York region for a winter season offering cruises to the Bahamas and the Caribbean.

Having completed a seasonal deployment in the Eastern Mediterranean and the Greek Islands, the Royal Caribbean vessel arrived in Cape Liberty on November 8, 2026.

Before kicking off its winter season, the 2021-built vessel operated a trans-Atlantic crossing that started in Civitavecchia, Italy.

The 14-night repositioning voyage included visits to destinations in Spain and Portugal.

Odyssey’s New York season starts with a series of seven-night cruises to the Bahamas and Florida, which sail to Nassau and Port Canaveral.

The itineraries are also highlighted by visits to Royal Caribbean’s private island destination of Perfect Day at CocoCay.

Recent findings suggest that starting in January, the vessel will also offer longer cruises to the Eastern Caribbean, operating 11- and 12-night cruises to ports of call that include Bridgetown in Barbados, Charlotte Amalie in St. Thomas and St. John’s in Antigua.

The season runs through late April, when the Odyssey of the Seas is scheduled to embark on a repositioning voyage back to Europe.

Sailing from Cape Liberty to Civitavecchia, the 14-night cruise will feature visits to destinations in Portugal, Spain and Italy, including Ponta Delgada, Málaga, Cartagena and La Spezia.

The ship is scheduled to embark on another summer season in the Mediterranean in early November, offering weeklong cruises to the Greek Islands and Italy.

Completing five years of service in 2026, the Odyssey of the Seas underwent a routine drydock before its summer season.

The 4,200-passenger vessel underwent technical maintenance during the shipyard stay, in addition to overall upkeep work on public areas and staterooms.

Built at the Meyer Werft shipyard in Papenburg, Germany, the ship is part of Royal Caribbean’s Quantum-class series.
