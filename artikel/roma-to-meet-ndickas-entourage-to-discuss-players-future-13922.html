Evan Ndicka has become a pillar for Gian Piero Gasperini.

In Parma, the Ivorian defender will reach 140 appearances for Roma, confirming his status as one of the most used players in recent years.

Industry observers note that over the last three seasons, he has logged 6,830 minutes, just 36 fewer than Mancini, who leads the rankings for outfield players with 6,866. This consistency has also been accompanied by growth in performance.

The 1999-born defender’s contract expires on June 30, 2028, meaning the Roman club will soon have to make a decision: renew or leave, keeping in mind that Ndicka holds a 20% interest in the future transfer.

The former Eintracht Frankfurt player no longer has an agent and is personally managing his future.

Evan Ndicka has played 921 minutes this season, including a headed goal against Udinese and just one yellow card.

He was precise in his tackles, making very few mistakes or lapses in form.

As Il Messaggero reports, Roma has several contract renewals lined up: in addition to De Roon and Hermoso, the Giallorossi will also begin discussions with Ndicka regarding a contract extension.
