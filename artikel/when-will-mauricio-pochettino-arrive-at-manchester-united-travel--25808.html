The dawn of the Mauricio Pochettino era is upon us, with the Argentine having taken to the dugout with the United States for the final time on Tuesday evening.

Pochettino is returning to the Premier League for a fourth time, replacing Michael Carrick as head coach of Manchester United.

He is facing a sizeable rescue mission, with Man United languishing in 18th in the Premier League having won just one of their first ten matches. He faces a tricky start, too, with Liverpool, Sporting CP, and Brentford on the docket for his first matches in charge.

While Carrick was formally dismissed last week, after the Man Utd hierarchy held crisis talks regarding his future in Monaco, Pochettino has been delayed in actually starting his new role.

He first had to see out one last international break with the United States national team, which involved a two-legged tie against Haiti, before returning to England.

Pochettino’s term as Manchester United manager will officially begin today, November 18, 2026, as confirmed in a club statement last week.

That start date was chosen to allow him to lead the United States national team through their two Concacaf Nations League matches this international break, a two-legged tie against Haiti. The second of those two legs kicked off at midnight on Tuesday night for UK viewers.

Industry observers note that the Argentine was set to travel to Manchester immediately after the match, on a private jet laid on by United. That will see him touch down in the north-west on Wednesday morning.

Pochettino is expected to head straight from the airport to Carrington, where he will throw himself headlong into preparing for Sunday’s trip to Anfield.

Some of his backroom staff arrived on location in Manchester ahead of Pochettino, however, laying some groundwork before the head coach’s own arrival.
