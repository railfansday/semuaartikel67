📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

West Ham United have dismissed reports suggesting Konstantinos Mavropanos could be sold during the January transfer window, with a club source telling Claret & Hugh exclusively that the claims are untrue.

Speculation has gathered pace over the last 48 hours following reports that the Hammers would consider offers for the Greek international if a suitable bid arrived. The story has since been picked up by overseas outlets, including Greek broadcaster Novasports, which repeated suggestions that West Ham were open to a January departure.

However, Claret & Hugh has now sought clarification directly from the club, and our source has categorically rejected the suggestion that Mavropanos is available for transfer this winter calling the claims “nonsense”.

The latest speculation centres on Mavropanos reportedly failing to agree a proposed two-year contract extension during the summer, alongside his decision to change representatives and join the ROOF agency.

Those developments have prompted suggestions that West Ham could be prepared to cash in before the defender enters the final stages of his existing contract.

But that is not the position being communicated to Claret & Hugh.

Indeed, we exclusively reported in October that West Ham hold an option to extend Mavropanos’ current agreement, which is otherwise due to expire in 2028.

That provides vital context to the latest speculation about his contractual situation.

Dinos being sold in January has been described as nonsense by a West Ham insider.

The 28-year-old has been one of West Ham’s most productive players this season, scoring five goals despite operating in central defence.

His performances have attracted interest from elsewhere, with reports from Turkey last month suggesting Beşiktaş were considering a January approach.

However, those reports also indicated that West Ham were reluctant to let the defender leave.

The Hammers are hardly blessed with an abundance of experienced centre-backs, particularly following Axel Disasi’s departure and Jean-Clair Todibo’s loan move to Lens.

And while recent reports have raised questions about Mavropanos’ future, the information supplied to Claret & Hugh today offers a very different picture.

Whatever interest there may be from other clubs, West Ham’s current position is clear.

Mavropanos is not for sale in January.
