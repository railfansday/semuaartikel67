EVERTON WOMEN V LONDON CITY LIONESSES.

*Fixture date and time subject to change.

An exclusive early bird window will open for supporters to purchase tickets on Friday 20 November. During this period tickets are priced at £10 for Adults and £5 for Juniors (U18).

Tickets will go on general sale from 10am on Friday 11 December priced at £12 for Adults and £6 for Juniors (U18).

Supporters should note that in order to accrue an individual booking history, unique supporter numbers must be given for each attending supporter at the time of sale.

Sources indicate that tickets can be purchased online, over the phone on 0151 556 1878 or in person at the South Stand Ticket Office at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Opening times for all our ticketing outlets can be found here.
