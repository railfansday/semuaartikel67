Multiple outlets in Italy and Germany are reporting that Milan have reached an agreement with Eintracht Frankfurt over the compensation for Markus Krösche, who is set to join the Rossoneri in the role of Head of Football.

Recent findings suggest that according to Sky Sport DE and various other sources, Milan have triggered a release clause and will pay Eintracht Frankfurt a fee in the region of €5m for Krösche’s services. Reports back in October indicate that there was already an agreement in principle between Milan and Krösche in place.

Krösche left his position with the Bundesliga club earlier this season, but Frankfurt are reportedly owed compensation if he takes on another job before the summer of 2027.

Monday’s reports claim that Krösche will take over in the role of Head of Football at Milan on January 1, 2027.

Industry observers note that milan have been looking to appoint Krösche since the summer. The club has restructured its senior management team after parting tech giant with head coach Massimiliano Allegri, sporting director Igli Tare, technical director Geoffrey Moncada and CEO Giorgio Furlani in one fell swoop the day after the 2025-26 season ended.

Since then, Massimo Calvelli has served as CEO, Hendrik Almstadt has been serving as director of player trading and Bobby Gardiner is the director of football intelligence.

Krösche is now set to joining them, overseeing in the role of Head of Football.
