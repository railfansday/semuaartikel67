Moises Caicedo has retaken his place on Chelsea’s treatment table, compounding a miserable afternoon for the Blues.

Xabi Alonso’s side were handed a 4-1 hiding by Leeds on Saturday afternoon, leaving them with one win in their last five and under serious pressure to get back on track.

Their efforts to sink the Yorkshiremen were not helped when Caicedo was forced off with 20 minutes played, shortly after a crunching James Justin tackle.

With a Friday night trip to Nottingham on the cards, Alonso will hope to have the Ecuadorian at his disposal as he bids to get his struggling side back on track.

With that said, here is all the latest Chelsea injury news and potential return dates...
