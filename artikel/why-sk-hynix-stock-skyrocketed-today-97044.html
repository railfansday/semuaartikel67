Sources indicate that shares of SK Hynix (SKHY +20.00%) charged sharply higher on Wednesday, jumping as much as 23.7%. By the time the market closed, the stock was still up 20%.

The catalyst that sent the semiconductor specialist higher was news of a high-profile potential suitor.

Recent findings suggest that SK Hynix was reportedly the subject of takeover interest from Japanese investment holding organization and tech conglomerate SoftBank Group (SFTBY +19.27%). Chairman Masayoshi Son approached numerous Wall Street banks to discuss financing options to acquire the chipmaker for $1 trillion, according to a report that first appeared in The Information. The price under consideration would have been roughly 1.7 times SK Hynix's market cap of $576 billion as of Friday's market close.

The sheer magnitude of the offer and the potential national security implications were just a few of the obstacles that reportedly scuttled the deal, though it isn't clear whether a formal offer was ever tendered.

According to recent reports, softBank has made no secret of its ambition to achieve end-to-end control over all elements of the artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductor supply chain. The tech giant already owns data center chip designer Ampere Computing and power infrastructure specialist SB Energy, as well as an 86% controlling stake in chip designer Arm Holdings. SoftBank also holds a position in AI start-up OpenAI.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

According to recent reports, bringing SK Hynix into the fold would have given the firm a direct pipeline to DRAM, NAND flash, and high-bandwidth memory (HBM). However, in a statement, it stated, "SoftBank explores many opportunities, and this one is no longer under active consideration." The wording suggests the deal was, in fact, explored.

So is SK Hynix worth $1 trillion? It's doubtful. A premium of that magnitude would likely have drawn the ire of smaller SoftBank investors, even as Son holds a dominant 33% stake in the company. Moreover, as Korea's dominant maker of HBM, it's unlikely the deal would have passed regulatory muster.

At less than 7 times earnings, SK Hynix stock appears attractively priced. However, the historical boom-and-bust cycle has some investors concerned, and the jury is still out on whether the firm can maintain its juicy profit margins while the future of AI plays out.
