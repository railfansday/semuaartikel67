The Best Music Moments and Surprise Cameos From ‘GTA VI’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that the world has been waiting to play Grand Theft Auto VI, and the video game is finally here. Music, of course, is integral to all of it. There’s a soundtrack full of stars — the 34-track release, which is out today, features Travis Scott, Keith Richards, Dua Lipa, Arca, and more. In addition to that, there is a massive one-day festival inspired by the game, taking place in Miami on Nov. 21, with headliners Cardi B, Metro Boomin, and Justice, who also put together the game’s score.

Once you start playing, there’s even more music to discover through the game’s radio stations and celebrity DJ hosts. Here are some of the best music moments and surprise cameos from the biggest video game of the year.

The game is full of celebrity guest DJs, which Rockstar began sharing a few weeks back. There’s Cocoteo FM hosted by Bad Bunny and RaiNao, Back Country Radio hosted by Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey, and Radio La Ola with Gloria and Emilio Estefan, plus tons more. But one that may surprise people is Stockyard FM, featuring two DJs named Victoria and Harrison — better known as PinkPantheress and the Dare. Victoria and Harrison are their real names, and their radio station is dedicated to experimental, left-of-center pop, electronica, and indie sleaze.

One of the GTA VI radio stations, Radio La Ola, plays homage to Latin classics from over the decades. But one hugely surprising one is “Sopa de Caracol,” a massive but somewhat forgotten hit from the Nineties by Honduran band Banda Blanca. The inclusion made headlines in Central America, with the lead singer Pilo Tejada saying he was honored to have his voice reach new parts of the world.

Sources indicate that psych-rock band King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard is featured on the soundtrack: They’re one of several artists on the collaboration “Flower in a Hurricane,” which also earned them their first Billboard 100 entry. But that’s not the only place you can find them: Gizz fans noticed that throughout several parts of the game, you can hear snippets of what appear to be demos from the band, woven into the score. On Reddit, diehards have been pointing these out and playing guessing games as they wonder what projects these might be from.

We’ve known that the French electronic duo Justice composed the original score for the game, but the official album won’t be out until Dec. 11. We’re getting to hear some of what they came up with the release of the game but there’s still more to come, including an anticipated collaboration with the Weeknd. The duo have since mentioned this is the “first and last” video game they’ll ever score.

Industry observers note that the soundtrack, which dropped on Nov. 19, is packed, featuring 34 songs by some surprising artists and stars. There’s new music from Travis Scott, the Fuerza Regida track “Suzuki,” which is charting in 15 countries, and there are also songs from Keith Richards, Kali Uchis and Helado Negro, and more. A lot of the music was inspired directly by Miami, which you can hear in new tracks from artists like JT.

Artists are getting into the game, too. Tinashe delighted fans with a hilarious livestream playing for the first time. The stream captured her making an epic getaway by flying an airplane and hysterically crashing it into the ground, then running out of ammo in a shootout. “I have no bullets!” she screams. “Holy shit, I’m going to have to beat the fuck out of them myself.” After several days of sold-out highlights in the U.K., Noah Kahan stayed up late and wreaked havoc in Vice City while his wife was asleep.

The world lost D’Angelo back in 2025, but his legacy lives on in Vice City. GTA VI players discovered that an unreleased track called “Kamakazi,” as confirmed by D’Angelo collaborator DJ Dojah, is one of the songs you can hear on the radio station Worldwide FM. D’Angelo’s “Lady” also plays on the in-game Honey FM channel, which is hosted by Chilli and T-Boz of TLC.

While the creators of the game spent a ton of time adding interesting radio stations and track to the GTA VI universe, there is plenty of love for pop stars and major pop hits. Dua Lipa appears on the soundtrack, but the radio stations also feature plenty of pop girlies: There’s Chappell Roan’s “Hot To Go!,” Charli XCX’s “Good Ones,” and Addison Rae’s “Fame Is a Gun,” just to name a few, which players can blast while driving through Vice City.
