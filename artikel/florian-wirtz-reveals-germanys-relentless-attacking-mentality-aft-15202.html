Germany rounded off their 2026 international calendar in emphatic fashion on Monday evening, dismantling the Netherlands at Olympiastadion to secure the top spot in their Nations League group.

Derry Scherhant's superb first-half opener set the hosts on their way before a devastating second-half display saw Florian Wirtz score twice, with Nico Schlotterbeck and Brajan Gruda also getting on the scoresheet.

Speaking to ARD after his 50th international appearance, Wirtz touched on the attacking mentality in the second half, revealing that Germany had no intention of sitting back and protecting their advantage.

“I think we want to stand for playing attacking football,” stated the Liverpool midfielder. "We always want to score goals and dominate our opponents.

Sources indicate that “We wanted to keep pressing forward and score more goals. And I think we did that perfectly.”.

The 23-year-old also admitted that Germany had enjoyed themselves considerably more after the break, having initially struggled to find space against their Dutch opponents.

Industry observers note that "Of course, we hope that we can bring the fans a lot of enjoyment. And for us, it was also a lot of fun," Wirtz explained.

It has been revealed that "Especially in the second half. The first half still involved quite a lot of hard work and a lot of running.

Sources indicate that "In the second half, everything was a little bit easier because we had more space. And that's when it became really enjoyable.".

Reflecting on their progress after sealing their third win under Klopp, Wirtz was equally optimistic about what lies ahead.

"It's definitely a fitting way to finish, I'd say, and one that we're very happy with.

In a recent development, "We want to carry on like this next year. That's clear. We want to improve.

"I think we've definitely already taken some good steps forward in our development as a team.

“I'm very proud and happy to be part of this team, and it's really enjoyable.”.
