It has been revealed that the most affordable current-generation MacBook you can get under the tree this year just dropped to its best prices in several months with today’s extremely rare early Black Friday deals. Apple’s MacBook Neo lineup is nearly $50 off to deliver one of, if not the most surprising discounts of the 2026 Black Friday season yet.

Amazon has decided to get even more aggressive with its early Black Friday offerings this week with a solid discount on Apple’s 2026 MacBook Neo, right after delivering AirPods Pro 3 down at an impressive $70 off and the best prices ever across the Apple Watch Series 12 lineup.

Sources indicate that as part of the giant collection of fresh deals landing in its Black Friday sale section this week, it has now dropped the 256GB MacBook Neo down to $649.99 shipped, while the elevated 512GB model sporting the Touch ID keyboard down to $749.99 shipped. Both models are available in various color options at the discounted rate.

While we aren’t talking about something like the major $250 price drops live on the M5 MacBook Air lineup right now, this is, after all, the already extremely affordable current-generation MacBook, at least by Apple standards and despite this summer’s price increase.

While there was a time when you could score one of these for less in brand new condition at retail, those days are long gone now with no signs of return. MacBook Neo remains the most affordable way to land your special someone, or yourself, a new current MacBook this holiday, and it is now at the lowest price we have tracked since the price increases in late June.

Recent findings suggest that it is also worth noting that, while they were lower at launch, there were no overly significant deals back then either and it’s not likely they will drop much lower than they are right now. Not much is safe from Black Friday these days, but the MacBook Neo could have been one of only a very small handful of Apple releases that had and still has the potential to dodge the November price slashing.

Today’s surprisingly rare discounts land on the heels of Apple official raising the eduction pricing at the tail end of last month, and the release of “The Dreamer” – Apple’s new 2026 holiday film starring the new Neo.
