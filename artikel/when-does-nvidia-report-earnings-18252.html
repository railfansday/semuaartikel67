📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that nvidia will report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, after the US stock market closes. The results are expected around 4:20 PM ET, followed by an earnings call at 5:00 PM ET. For Indian investors, the results will be available around 2:50 AM IST on November 18. Wall Street expects Nvidia to report approximately $109 billion in revenue, up around 91% year-over-year, with adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.47. Data Center revenue is forecast to reach $101.8 billion, driven by continued AI chip demand. Investors will also watch fourth-quarter revenue guidance, Blackwell and Rubin chip demand, profit margins and management’s outlook for 2027.
