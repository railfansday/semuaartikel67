📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Roma is expanding their list of January targets. Among the names being followed by Tony D’Amico is Nicolò Cambiaghi.

Sources indicate that the Bologna winger is one of the players being considered to bolster Gasperini’s attack, who has been seeking a right-footed player capable of starting on the left for months. Endrick and Gittens remain the top choices.

AC Milan are also interested in the Brazilian striker, while the only concerns about the Chelsea winger are related to the 2004-born player’s physical issues. PSV has put up a wall for Van Bommel, valuing him at €50 million.

According to recent reports, in the meantime, Roma have set their sights on Cambiaghi. Il Resto del Carlino confirms that the Giallorossi have made inquiries about the 2000-born player, who Lazio also made a move for this summer.

The former Empoli player has played 799 minutes this season but has yet to find his feet. Cambiaghi, who played a decisive role in Italy’s victory against France, has provided four assists in Serie A without ever beating the opposition goalkeepers.
