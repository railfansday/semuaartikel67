Solana’s 2027 roadmap promises faster growth, but the market wants proof

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Solana’s core developer, Anza, has unveiled its 2027 roadmap.

Recent findings suggest that structurally, the roadmap focuses on making Solana faster, more efficient, and capable of handling more activity. The network aims to double its compute capacity and cut the compute units needed for token account transactions by 80%.

It also prepares to increase the number of accounts supported in each transaction from 64 to 96 and make it easier to run models fully on-chain.

Naturally, the question becomes whether Solana is preparing for its next growth phase. Interestingly, the previous major network upgrade was also performed by Anza.

This upgrade, called Alpenglow, brought down Solana’s finality time to 150ms from 12.8 seconds, making the network significantly faster.

The result? As the chart above reveals, Solana processed over 10 billion transactions in October, keeping monthly activity above that level. However, more than halfway through November, the network has recorded just over 2 billion transactions.

That’s 5x lower than October’s total, suggesting that the recent spike in activity may be losing steam. Given this, it might be too early to expect any bull run in 2027.

In fact, SOL is right now down 20% in October, which indicates that the market hasn’t seen increased demand from improving fundamentals just yet.

So, what does it say about the recent “Anthropic-driven” momentum on Solana [SOL]?

SPCX was first to demonstrate how major IPOs can bring fresh trading activity to Solana.

The launch of SpaceX’s tokenized stock, SPCX, on Solana on the 12th of June brought significant activity to the network.

The token recorded $108 million in 24-hour on-chain trading volume shortly after launch and crossed 10,000 holders within six days, with cumulative trading volume exceeding $350 million.

Recent findings suggest that notably, a similar trend has emerged following the ANT IPO launch. As the chart below shows, Anthropic’s ANT has generated over $29 million in trading volume on Solana since launch across three issuers. Backpack’s token alone accounted for $26.8 million, or nearly 92% of the total.

This shows how major IPO rolls out are bringing fresh trading activity to Solana.

Adding to this, DeFiLlama data reveals that Solana’s stablecoin market cap has risen nearly 3% in the past 24 hours, pointing to a fresh liquidity inflow after months of weakness.

However, the bigger picture remains mixed.

Industry observers note that despite the rise in on-chain activity and liquidity, SOL is still trading around $70. This suggests that the recent activity has yet to translate into stronger buying pressure.

For Solana’s 2027 roadmap to support a longer-term recovery, the network will therefore need to sustain this growth beyond short-term activity.

Anza’s 2027 roadmap aims to improve Solana’s speed, capacity, and overall network efficiency.

SPCX and ANT have boosted on-chain trading activity, but SOL’s price weakness suggests that stronger demand is still missing.
