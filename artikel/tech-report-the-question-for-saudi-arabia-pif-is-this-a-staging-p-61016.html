The Saudi Arabia PIF led takeover came with PSR headroom from the Mike Ashley years and looser rules on owner-linked sponsorship.

Around £450m went on transfers in the Amanda Staveley era, but it was spent steadily, not decisively, and commercial income never grew fast enough to fund the next step.

By June 2024 Yankuba Minteh and Elliot Anderson had to go for a combined £60m-plus just to stay inside PSR.

The structure never worked. Amanda Staveley was bought out in July 2024. Dan Ashworth left for Manchester United.

It has been revealed that paul Mitchell lasted under a year, branded the transfer operation “not fit for purpose” and ended up in a turf war with Eddie Howe which was largely irrelevant as every financial decision was made by Saudi Arabia PIF. Mitchell might have identified the problem but he had no answers.

Recent findings suggest that darren Eales and Dan Ashworth struggled to get past PIF’s processes. Good football people kept failing inside this framework. At some point you have to blame the framework.

Recent findings suggest that I agree Eddie Howe came back much too early and fatigued mentally and physically. But he still delivered the Champions League football.

It has been revealed that last seasons 12th with 17 league defeats was a bitter pill swallow and faced with the latest strategy and sale of his best players he walked away in July. He certainly was not at his best and was working with a divided squad where both Gordon and Tonali indicated to the club they were leaving before Christmas.

The central concern is our senior players simply had much higher earning potential at the six clubs who do not face the same SCR restrictions. Alexander Isak was reportedly assured of an improved deal by Amanda Staveley and Mehrdad Ghodoussi in March 2024. Paul Mitchell shelved it and Eddie Howe publicly cited PSR as the reason. Isak downed tools. Every dressing room and every agent drew the same conclusion: PSR then, SCR now, this club can’t pay you what you’re worth. So Gordon, Tonali and Bruno followed him out, with Trippier also gone.

Blaming Eddie Howe for transfers is a simplistic and nuanced view. None of the players signed in summer 2025 were his first choice, the embarrassing prolonged struggle to get transfers over the line are a clear manifestation of that. Nick Woltemade and Yoane Wissa were signed two weeks after the season started in desperation. That after the board took direct control of the Alexander Isak situation and prevented Eddie Howe from communicating with the player.

Industry observers note that beware the convenient narrative. It was Amanda Staveley’s naivety, then Paul Mitchell’s manner, then Alexander Isak’s greed, then Eddie Howe’s stubbornness. Now it is Matthias Jaissle’s tactics or Ross Wilson’s signings. A latest scapegoat every season is very useful to the people who never change. When the same problems outlast every individual blamed for them, the individuals were never the problem.

It has been revealed that under Ross Wilson the latest model has seen eight of the summer’s signings being 24 or younger, including 18-year-old Sean Steur from Ajax for about £20m. Dortmund is the stated template. Roughly £249m of players went out. The CEO’s line is that anyone under contract leaves “on our terms.”.

The problem is they still leave. Call it what it is: develop to sell. Under SCR, trading profit inflates the revenue side of the ratio, so I understand the logic.

According to recent reports, newcastle United are mid-table under Matthias Jaissle with the youngest squad in years. The question for Saudi Arabia PIF: is this a staging post at Newcastle United, or the destination?
