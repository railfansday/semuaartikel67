📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Gian Piero Gasperini wants to continue working with two players whose contracts expire on June 30: Mario Hermoso and Marten De Roon.

According to recent reports, both have delivered high-level performances this season, confirming their importance on the pitch. Experience and leadership are key.

Roma has already begun discussions regarding a contract extension with the Spanish defender: a two-year contract with an option for a further year is being discussed.

Industry observers note that for the Giallorossi, reaching a longer agreement also means being able to spread out his salary, thus lowering the player’s annual cost. Hermoso now earns €3.5 million plus bonuses, moving toward a salary reduction over several seasons. A deal is not in doubt.

A few days ago, Tony D’Amico took the first official steps toward renewing Marten De Roon’s contract.

Sources indicate that as La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, he has been in direct contact with the Dutch midfielder’s agent, a way to express his desire to continue working together for another season. If the former Atalanta player feels ready, an extension could be on the cards. De Roon is considering becoming a coach, and Gasperini has already raised the idea of ​​starting his career with him.
