Jamie Carragher was left stunned when Wayne Rooney named the English club with the most 'horrible' set of supporters.

It has been revealed that rooney retired back in January 2021, calling time on an illustrious career which spanned three different decades and four different clubs. After breaking through at Everton, the striker signed for Manchester United at the age of 18 in July 2004.

Making 559 appearances across 13 seasons for the north-west club, Rooney won five Premier League titles, an FA Cup, three League Cups, a Champions League and a Europa League during his time at Old Trafford.

In a recent development, rooney returned to Everton in the latter stages of his career, before enjoying late stints at D.C. United and Derby County. He retired as one of the best strikers to ever play the game, with a combination of his goalscoring record and his technical proficiency placing him into this pantheon of greats.

Having played at the top level for so long, Rooney would have entertained millions of fans in numerous different stadiums throughout his career.

Scoring 253 goals for United, he naturally established himself as the talisman of Sir Alex Ferguson's successful side of the late 2000s and early 2010s, and this label would've come with ample abuse from fans of the Red Devils' greatest rivals.

The supporters of Liverpool and Manchester City in particular wouldn't have been the biggest fans of the striker, and certainly would've made these feelings known to Rooney loud and clear throughout matches.

However, Rooney surprisingly cited a different club when asked which team he felt had the most annoying supporters, and he went as far as describing these fans as 'horrible'— a claim which left Carragher shocked.

"As a player going... [a club] that had horrible fans was Blackpool," Rooney stated on an episode of the Overlap back in 2025.

"Honestly, we played Blackpool away a few times and, like, god, they were horrible.".

Rooney's answer elicited a hysterical laugh from Carragher, who chimed in on the conversation to remind people that his Liverpool team lost twice to the Tangerines in the 2010/11 campaign: "We [Liverpool] got beat by them two times in the same season in the Roy Hodgson years.".

To this day, that was Blackpool's only season in the Premier League. The Lancashire club won promotion to the English top flight under Ian Holloway in the 2009/10 campaign, before going straight back down in their solitary season in the massive time.

Industry observers note that rooney played against the Seasiders twice that year, visiting the intimidating 16,000-seater Bloomfield Road Stadium in January 2011. Clearly, the hostility Rooney felt inside the quaint seaside venue got to him, as United found themselves 2-0 down at half-time.

Industry observers note that the legendary England centre-forward was hooked on 66 minutes, before the Red Devils staged a dramatic late comeback to emerge with a 3-2 victory. They'd go on to win the Premier League title.
