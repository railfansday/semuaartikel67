📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that millwall enter this weekend’s fixture against Burnley following a 2-1 defeat to Stoke City. Mark Sykes‘ wonder strike late in the second half wasn’t enough to stop the Lions from suffering their fourth consecutive loss.

Alex Neil’s side return to The Den with a poor record at home. The South London outfit have just won three times as hosts this season with a points-per-game average of 1.38.

Their opponents, Burnley, make the trip to the capital also after a loss. The Clarets were deservedly beaten 2-0 by Birmingham City to make it eight games without victory.

In a recent development, fabio Celestini’s side have an especially poor record away from home this term. Their only win this season has come on the road, but with 2.63 goals conceded per match as visitors on average, it’s been a nightmare campaign.

It has been revealed that “For their standards, it has been a poor season for both Burnley and Millwall. Either side would have had ambitions of a top eight finish at the least, but it looks highly unlikely they will be in the mix this term.

It has been revealed that “The Lions lacked cohesiveness against Stoke City on Wednesday night and, with an average of 1.76 goals conceded per game, they are a shell of last season’s side. This is Neil’s first real tough patch in charge of the South London outfit, and another loss would pile the pressure on his future.

Recent findings suggest that “Burnley are in real trouble, and Celestini has a difficult job to get the Clarets out of it. They do have a higher xG on the road though with 1.43, and coming up against another team out of form, it is a great chance to get their second win of the campaign.

“This is a tough one to call, and both managers need a win. I think if the Lions perform as they did in midweek, its Burnley’s for the taking. However, I will back Millwall to pick up a needed win.”.

“Millwall are a far cry from the team that came so close to promotion from the Championship last season. Alex Neil has been without some key players, and still is, but the summer recruitment drive has not helped the Lions move forward whatsoever.

“The South Londoners have regressed while their Championship rivals have made massive strides forward, and a tough season may await as a result – their defence is lacking solidity, and going forward they’ve been so uninspiring. On the plus side, they face a Burnley side with just one victory in 17 Championship matches this weekend.

“After losing to Birmingham City in the week, the Clarets now officially hold the record for the longest home winless run in the history of England’s top four leagues. The club are in a deep, deep rut on and off the pitch, with undermotivated, ageing and out-of-form players across the board.

“If the visitors are to get anything from this, it’ll likely be down to Jayden Banel or Largie Ramazani. With both teams struggling, I’ll say this ends level on Saturday.”.
