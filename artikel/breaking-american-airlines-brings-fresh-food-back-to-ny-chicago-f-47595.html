American Airlines said Wednesday it's bringing small, fresh "bites" to first class on flights between its hubs in New York and Chicago, the carrier's latest effort to win over higher-spending customers.

The changes start this month. Customers will be able to pick between two options, which include prosciutto and melon or apple cinnamon chia pudding for breakfast and a Thai noodle salad or mezze plate for lunch and dinner, American said Wednesday.

American last offered fresh food for first class on these routes in 2019, a spokeswoman remarked. Presently, customers in first class on these flights get a smaller, packaged snack. Airlines largely have limited food options for shorter flights, even in first class.

It has been revealed that american has fallen behind Delta Air Lines and United Airlines in profits and investments in premium options. The airline has been working to step up those premium offerings in recent years with fresh cabins, lounges and aims for seatback screens on its narrow-body fleets.
