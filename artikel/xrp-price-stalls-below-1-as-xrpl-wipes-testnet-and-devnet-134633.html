XRP price is still struggling to get back above $1, even after the Nvidia-led market bounce that happened to be just a temporary day. Now, the XRP Ledger has another headline, with developers preparing to wipe its Testnet and Devnet tomorrow following an infrastructure failure.

According to recent reports, the reset is scheduled for November 25, between 5 and 6 PM Pacific time. Everything sitting on the two networks goes. Accounts, balances, transactions, AMMs, escrows, offers, and other ledger data will be cleared. With this going on, developers are forced to rebuild their testing environments.

XRP, meanwhile, couldn’t make much of Monday’s rebound. Ripple briefly reclaimed $1 before being pushed back down in a dead cat bounce situation.

Industry observers note that the reset is not a routine wipe, so XRPL’s Testnet depended on a single full-history server that crashed and could not be recovered. Ripple claims it is increasing storage capacity to address the problem and reduce how frequently the network needs resetting.

Sources indicate that developers will lose their existing setups and need to collect fresh test XRP from the faucet. Those running their own servers will also need to clear their databases and restart them after the reset.

Testnet is used to mirror Mainnet’s amendment status, while Devnet gives developers a place to experiment with proposed features. Both serve as sandboxes, meaning the reset won’t touch real XRP or live ledger activity.

It has been revealed that A clean testing environment matters when teams are building fresh applications, experimenting with features, or checking integrations before they reach the live network. Losing existing test data is inconvenient, but keeping a broken full-history server running isn’t much of an option either.

Ripple notes the network URLs, IDs, and validator settings will remain unchanged. Developers can reuse some addresses, but recreated AMM pools will generally have different account addresses.

It has been revealed that XRP briefly moved above $1, but the level quickly turned into a ceiling again. Buyers have struggled to follow through, and another failed attempt leaves the token hovering around $0.94, and it lost momentum for a sustained reversal.
