Apple could see iPhone shipments rise 7% next year, giving it a 26% share of global smartphone shipments, according to a upcoming report. Here are the details.

As detailed by AppleInsider, a new Morgan Stanley research report indicates Apple is poised to ship approximately 270 million iPhones in 2027, a 7% bump from 2026.

The forecast comes amid a change of pace for Apple, which debuted only the iPhone 18 Pro models and iPhone Duo this fall, with the rest of its iPhone lineup reportedly set to arrive early next year.

Morgan Stanley notes that this could push some purchases from late 2026 into 2027, contributing to the projected shipment growth without necessarily reflecting an increase in underlying demand.

The report also comes as Apple and the rest of the smartphone industry continue to grapple with rising memory and storage prices, though previous reports have suggested that Apple is better positioned than many of its competitors to absorb those costs.

Apple raised iPhone prices in September, after keeping them stable for much of the year while competitors had already introduced price hikes. In some cases, they raised prices more than once within a matter of months.

In a recent development, back to the report, it notes that global smartphone shipments are expected to drop 5% next year, compared with a previous forecast of 3.6% growth. That stated, it’s still an improvement over the estimated 13.8% decline in 2026.

Sources indicate that when it comes to Apple and the iPhone specifically, Morgan Stanley notes that consumer interest in upgrading has remained largely unchanged year over year, with 37% of iPhone owners saying they are extremely likely to upgrade within the next 12 months. That’s compared with 41% of smartphone owners overall, a figure that dropped by 2 percentage points year over year.

As for the market’s reaction to the iPhone Duo, Morgan Stanley found that 22% of non-iPhone users surveyed expressed interest in switching to Apple’s foldable device, with that figure reaching 34% in China.

Importantly, Morgan Stanley’s survey was conducted after the iPhone Duo was confirmed, but before it released, so these figures reflect consumer interest rather than actual purchasing behavior.

Apple Intelligence was another factor examined in the survey, with Morgan Stanley concluding that AI features are becoming less key in driving iPhone upgrades, while factors such as battery life and upgrade eligibility are gaining importance.

Finally, Morgan Stanley found that Apple’s customer retention rate increased three percentage points to a record 93%, while its net switching rate improved to 12%.

Huawei was the only other major smartphone brand to show a positive and improving net switching rate, while Samsung and Google remained relatively neutral, and Xiaomi, Motorola, Honor, Oppo, and Vivo recorded negative rates.
