Grand Theft Auto 6 has become the biggest game launch in livestreaming history, racking up 420 million hours watched in its first week as TheBurntPeanut and IShowSpeed led the charge.

After years of anticipation, GTA 6 finally launched on November 19, with millions of players diving into Rockstar’s recreation of modern-day Florida.

The excitement extended to streaming platforms, with both Twitch and Kick giving away thousands of GTA 6 codes and Twitch even introducing fresh advertising technology to help creators monetize their broadcasts.

Now, the numbers are in, and Rockstar has obliterated the competition.

According to Streams Charts, GTA 6’s 420 million hours watched nearly tripled the previous launch week record set by Valorant’s closed beta in April 2020.

Industry observers note that incredibly, GTA 6 surpassed the combined opening week viewership of Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo IV, and Lost Ark. The game also set a launch record with 7.91 million concurrent viewers.

Twitch accounted for the most hours watched, followed by YouTube and Kick, with the latter two finishing remarkably close to one another.

TheBurntPeanut was the most-watched GTA 6 streamer during launch week, generating a staggering 11.43 million hours watched across his broadcasts, with YouTube accounting for the majority of that figure.

Meanwhile, IShowSpeed attracted the most concurrent viewers on a single channel as he approached the end of his 52-hour GTA 6 marathon, which saw the streamer break down in tears after completing the story without sleeping.

Spanish streaming star IlloJuan recorded the highest combined peak viewership across multiple platforms.

In a recent development, however, it wasn’t just streaming’s biggest names benefiting from GTA 6. Smaller creators also found themselves in the spotlight, including one streamer with zero viewers who received a surprise raid from Ibai.

In total, 1,056 channels broke their all-time peak viewership records while playing GTA 6, with 193 of them attracting at least 10 times their previous record audience.

With GTA 6’s multiplayer mode still to come and the potential for another generation of GTA RP content, Rockstar’s streaming dominance could be just getting started.
