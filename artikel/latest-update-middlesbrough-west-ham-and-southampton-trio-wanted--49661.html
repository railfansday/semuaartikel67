Latest Update: Middlesbrough, West Ham and Southampton trio wanted by Bundesliga side

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Bundesliga side are said to be keen on the Championship trio ahead of the January transfer window. They are positioned in 8th place in the table after their first nine fixtures and they have 12 points on the board.

In a recent development, morris, 25, joined Middlesbrough back in 2024, whilst Jander, 23, linked up with Southampton last year following their relegation from the Premier League. As for Engels, 23, he was snapped up by West Ham in the last transfer window after catching the eye up in Scotland at Celtic.

As per Bild, all three players are believed to be on Stuttgart’s radar as they look to bolster their options in the middle of the park. They risk losing key man Angelo Stiller in the near future so they will need to find a replacement for him in the midfield.

Middlesbrough, West Ham and Southampton are all eyeing promotion from the EFL this season if possible. Boro are joint-top of the table at the moment after 15 games along with Birmingham City and Stoke City after they lost in the play-off final at Wembley to Hull City back in May.

Industry observers note that middlesbrough won’t want to lose Morris, especially mid-way through this campaign. He is a key player for Boro and has stepped up since Hayden Hackney’s exit to Everton.

It has been revealed that morris, who is a USA international with 14 caps to his name, has made 98 appearances since his switch to the Riverside Stadium from Columbus Crew. Kim Hellberg’s side will face a battle to keep hold of him in the long run though, especially if they don’t go up this term.

Sources indicate that west Ham landed Engels to boost their midfield department after they slipped out of the top flight earlier this year. The Belgium international has played in the German top flight before with FC Augsburg before Celtic came calling.

In a recent development, southampton ace Jander was at Borussia Dortmund and Schalke as a teenager. He then made his name at MSV Duisburg before his previous club 1. FC Nürnberg swooped in for his signature.

According to recent reports, middlesbrough, West Ham or Southampton don’t need to sell anyone in January. They could all sabotage their promotion pushes if they cash in on any of their most prized assets.

Industry observers note that stuttgart will need to pay big money to get any of Morris, Engels of Jander anytime soon. They will probably need to wait until next summer as things stand.

Boro face Bristol City at home on Saturday, whilst the Hammers play struggling Preston North End at the London Stadium. Meanwhile, the Saints lock horns with Derby County at St Mary’s.
