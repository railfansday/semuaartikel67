Sources indicate that the best stocking stuffers are small gifts that feel like more than an afterthought. Not novelty socks for the back of a drawer, nor the sort of dreary practicalities anyone could pick up at a drugstore, but genuinely good things that happen to come in small packages. You know, like handsome accessories, clever gadgets, excellent things to eat, and the occasional charming oddity. We've rounded up a few ideas in that vein for the dads, boyfriends, brothers, and assorted other gentlemen on your list this year.

To the delight of anyone who wants their place to smell good, Le Labo brought its cult-favorite Santal 26 scent to incense this year.

18K gold-plated clovers for good fortune, silver-plated beads for good looks. Some people have all the luck. That luck being you shopping for them.

This collaboration between Caran d'Ache and Paul Smith brings a dose of color to the Swiss penmaker's iconic hexagonal ballpoint.
