Referral credits are applied only after your friend has passed their return window or made their second payment, whichever comes first.

Active and retired military members, as well as first responders, are eligible for a 10 percent Whoop discount on the Peak ($239/year) and Life ($359/year) memberships. You must verify your status via ID.me to receive the Whoop promo code.

It has been revealed that like the discount above, students can save 10% on Whoop. This 10% Whoop student discount applies to Peak, Life, and One memberships. To get the Whoop promo code for 10% off, simply verify your student status through StudentBeans.
