Like any specialized skill set, winter layering is one that must be continually practiced and perfected, lest your talents wane. It’s not as easy as throwing on a pair of cashmere leggings and a coordinating hoodie—that’s only the beginning. From there, it takes a careful curation of hero pieces (like puffer jackets and parkas), as well as a cast of supporting details (hats, gloves, and high-performance footwear), to layer effectively and aesthetically in the harshest conditions.

That said, even the most adept artist is nothing without an arsenal of trusted creative tools. And in these bitter temps, you’ll want to reach for the best, which essentially means any piece of gear with The North Face’s iconic rectangular logo attached to it. The storied brand has dominated the outdoor space for decades, having perfected the art of elevated style at elevated altitudes.

As we head into the chilliest season of the year, look to The North Face’s vast selection of dependable staples for all your layering needs. Designed both for performance and appearances, these pieces are in it for the long run.

Recent findings suggest that whether painting or dressing, it’s essential to start any creative endeavor with a blank canvas made of quality materials. A good base should be versatile and simple to pair with a host of helpful staples. Long-sleeve tees, cashmere turtlenecks, and crewneck sweatshirts, for example, are all good jumping-off points.

Sources indicate that if you’re looking for a high-performance piece with enough style power to stand on its own, we recommend the Women’s Chabot 2.0 Mock-Neck. This cozy layering top offers the same reliable comfort of a traditional insulated thermal with a few modern updates. Though understated by design, its delightfully oversized fit and a funky waffle knit texture make the basic feel intentional and interesting.

For a closer-fitting base, the Women’s Golden Ridge Ribbed Long-Sleeve Tee layers easily under other pieces. Its slim fit and ribbed texture give this practical layer a polished look, while thumbholes at the cuffs add a functional detail. Bonus points for the tee’s anti-odor technology that helps keep the fabric feeling fresh.

It has been revealed that after you’ve selected the first layer, the next stop should be your coat closet. This is where you’ll select the hero of your look. While each piece of the layering puzzle is uniquely important, the power jacket is a non-negotiable. The right iteration should supply a thick layer of warmth, while also doing its part to make the outfit, as a whole, feel cohesive.

Those who crave a more subdued winter aesthetic will love the classic appeal of the Women’s Arctic Bomber. The waterproof winter jacket boasts all the utility of a parka, with the sleek design details most commonly found in trend-focused outerwear. In four comforting colorways, this winter coat is an insulation powerhouse in a chic, wearable package.

Look to the Women’s Hydrenalite Down Jacket for a more classic puffer. This workhorse is a versatile down layer that can be slipped on at any time to keep you warm for any duration that you spend outdoors, whether you are on the mountain or running around town.

Recent findings suggest that for the more artistically inclined, try the Women’s Yumiori Off Peak Full Zip Jacket. This fleece outer layer offers unfailing warmth and a distinctly sporty flavor. Its two-tone color palette, meanwhile, adds a fun, whimsical feel to the utilitarian, zip-up-style silhouette.

According to recent reports, during the summer months, it’s all about the mani-pedi. But once that icy weather blows in, self-care starts to look a bit different. The North Face’s Etip Gloves are basically a hand treatment on the go. Crafted from soft, stretchy fleece made with 93 percent recycled polyester, these tech-friendly gloves will keep your hands comfortable on cold-weather outings. They’ll also keep you connected: Touchscreen compatibility lets you check your phone without taking them off, while silicone grip dots on the palms help you hold onto your device as well as smaller essentials like keys and credit cards.

For footwear, the Women’s THERMOBALL Traction Mule V will quickly become the standout of your seasonal lineup. These cloud-like slip-ons pair warm insulation with a quilted upper that gives them a casual, streetwear-cool vibe. The rubber outsole adds traction for quick trips outdoors.

Recent findings suggest that the minimalist philosophy requires you to take off one item before leaving the house. But during the cold weather season, it’s in your best interest to do the opposite. As you walk out the door, make sure you grab a beanie since most of your body heat can escape from your head.

Beanies are simultaneously the most underappreciated outfit addition and the most indispensable. The North Face’s Wool Blend Beanie, for instance, pairs the warmth of its 65 percent merino wool knit with a refined, metropolitan style. Its classic cuffed design and textured knit add visual interest, while its deep fit offers roomier coverage. If you prefer the rectangular logo with the same cuffed look, you can never go wrong with the rib-knit and soft lining of the Salty Lined Beanie. On the slopes or on your morning commute, either will add a polished finishing touch.

The importance of a functional winter hat cannot be overstated—think of it as the final brushstroke in the art of winter layering.
