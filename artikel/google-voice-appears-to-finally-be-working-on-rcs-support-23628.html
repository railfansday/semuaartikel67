In a recent development, it might finally be happening. A new Google Voice patch plants seeds of RCS support, though the exact details remain unclear.

It has been revealed that about APK Insight: In this “APK Insight” post, we’ve decompiled the latest version of an application that Google uploaded to the Play Store. When we decompile these files (called APKs, in the case of Android apps), we’re able to see various lines of code within that hint at possible future features. Keep in mind that Google may or may not ever ship these features, and our interpretation of what they are may be imperfect. We’ll try to enable those that are closer to being finished, however, to show you how they’ll look in case that they do ship. With that in mind, read on.

Google Voice is an invaluable tool for accessing one phone number across a host of different devices, but to say it’s becoming less impactful without support for RCS is a bit of an understatement. Now that virtually every Android phone and iPhone supports the latest messaging experience, Google Voice is less useful both for business and consumer users.

Google Voice for Android version 2026.11.01.1014278023 doesn’t enable RCS, but it does, for the first time, actually hint that the functionality might be on its way. In a teardown of the app, one simple string stands out:.

That points to a latest send button used for RCS messages rather than SMS. Google Messages similarly changes its send button to differentiate between the two standards.

This is great news. I can have both options now to text friends and businesses with RCS as a fallback option on Google Voice because I don't want to give out my Google messages number.

This upgrade contains a host of other fresh strings around images, videos, and more, but none of them are directly tied to RCS support.

The folks over at Android Authority dug up some other tidbits about RCS including an “RCS chat preference” toggle and various RCS actions and messages. But, again, nothing is live just yet, and there’s no word on when it might arrive, if ever.

Google has been championing RCS support for over a decade now, so it’s long past due for Voice, a long-running Google product, to get on board. While the tidbits in this update don’t serve as any confirmation RCS if coming to Voice, it’s the most exciting update on the matter in years.
