📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella predicts that the rise of AI agents is going to reshape the economics of the internet, impacting the business models of the companies that rely on advertising and what he referred to as “human web” traffic.

Speaking at The New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday, the tech executive argued that the shift to an agentic web could change which companies pay others for traffic and for transactions, similar to how the transition from web portals like Yahoo to search engines like Google did years ago.

In a recent development, “The current aggregators who have a business model which was for the human web will get, in some sense, reshuffled,” mentioned Nadella. “And if you were monetizing ads because of the eyeballs, you now have to probably get down to essentially maybe CPC [cost-per-click] wins; maybe it’s more transactional,” he continued. “And so if you don’t accept the agent, somebody else will.”.

Industry observers note that the discussion turned to Amazon and its decision to block AI agents from accessing its retail website, preventing them from browsing its catalog and making purchases on behalf of their users. Nadella suggested that companies trying to protect their existing business models could lose out to others that are more willing to embrace the AI agents. For instance, if Amazon continues blocking agents, another shopping platform such as Shopify, could allow them, and gain an advantage.

It has been revealed that “I think this is going to be interesting,” he mentioned, speaking to the changing models. “It’s the same thing that’s happening in other categories, right? Which is, if you’re in love with a particular business model, you have to kind of scramble,” he said.
