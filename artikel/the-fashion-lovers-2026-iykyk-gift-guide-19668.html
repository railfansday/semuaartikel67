It’s about that time again. For the fourth year running, we’re tackling gifts for one of the most challenging groups to shop for: fashion obsessives. We get it—and we speak from experience.

Sources indicate that here, we’ve compiled a list of “if you know, you know” fashion, beauty, and lifestyle gifts that won’t just satisfy, but impress the fashion lover in your life. Some are practical; others are simply beautiful; all are things Vogue editors can’t get enough of.
