The manner in which some players depart West Ham along with the bad feeling that develops as they press for a departure on their own terms can sometimes be the lasting memory that tarnishes their whole stay in east London.

Such was certainly the case of Marco Arnautovic, for example, a successful forward who demanded a new deal, got everything he wanted, and then quit anyway.

However, with some players, despite the bitterness, the rancorous ending of their career fades in the memory over time, and their stay is regarded more fondly for the highlights rather than the bad blood generated at the close.

Not quite sure yet which category the career and departure of Lucas Paqueta will fit into as his stay at West Ham thankfully fades into the dim and distant past.

Infuriating, gifted, inconsistent, yes, but forever remembered for ‘that’ pass to Jarrod Bowen in June 2023, Paqueta’s ‘yellow cards for bets‘ two year saga became, sadly, the other defining memory of his three years as a Hammer.

Winning the conference League title was his career pinnacle – until last weekend when he finally achieved ‘the ambition of a lifetime‘  (huh) with his boyhood club Flamengo.

Looking on the bright side, West Ham fans don’t have to endure the ‘baggage’ that came with Lucas Paquetá.

As reported by sportwitness.co.uk, West Ham’s former number ten referred merely to his time in England as ‘two very difficult years’ en route to winning the Brazilian league title:.

In a recent development, ““I went through two very difficult years in my life. I decided to come back because I wanted this feeling, I wanted to feel welcomed, to be at the club I love.”.

Sources indicate that “I have no words to describe what I’m feeling. I arrived at this club when I was eight years old. My biggest dream was always to win trophies wearing this shirt.”.

Well done, Lucas. We’re all very happy for you.

It feels a bit like learning an ‘ex’ has got remarried. Good for them. So what? I’m so over them now….
