AC Milan consider Virgil van Dijk to be their number one defensive target ahead of next summer, and believe a deal is possible.

According to a report from La Gazzetta dello Sport, Milan decided to explore the possibility of signing Van Dijk ahead of the current season as they looked at various options to bolster the centre-back department. However, the conditions were not present to go beyond simply being interested.

The Dutchman turned 35 in July but remains one of the most highly-regarded defenders of his generation. The difference ahead of summer 2027 is that things might finally line up with Milan’s desire to allow him to experience a spell in Italy.

In a recent development, virgil’s contract expires in June and he’s always been fascinated by the idea of a move to Milan and playing in Serie A. It’s still a complex negotiation to carry out, but at the moment Gerry Cardinale seems determined to try.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to La Gazzetta’s report, The Touchline are reporting the following: “Virgil van Dijk is AC Milan’s dream signing! Milan want to make him their highest-paid player, give him the captain’s armband and build their defence around him.

Industry observers note that “They see Van Dijk as the leader of a new era at San Siro. The idea is for him to set the standard in the dressing room, guide their younger defenders and lead a team built to challenge for the Serie A title and compete with Europe’s best.”.

Given the outlay planned to get Sporting CP captain Goncalo Inacio in January, it seems as though the Rossoneri are keen to build towards having the best defence in the country, which they are presently some way off.
