Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola insists the use of drones to record training sessions should be illegal after footage from a Manchester United training session leaked online.

The Reds face their biggest rivals at Anfield on Sunday afternoon in what is manager Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge of the Red Devils since his appointment last week.

Industry observers note that yet the build-up to the game has been overshadowed by a drone filming 12 minutes of Pochettino’s training session on Thursday. The owner has since apologised and the footage has been removed from YouTube.

While Iraola admitted he had seen the clip, the Spaniard has sympathy for his fellow manager and insisted “there are reasons why we train behind closed doors.”.

“They sent me the footage yesterday, but the crucial part of this is that it shouldn’t be legal,” Iraola said on Friday at his pre-match press conference.

“It happened to United but it could happen to us in training. Yesterday, if you had access to our training, the cameras recorded 15-20 minutes of our training; you know who did and didn’t train… it’s not a matter of advantage; it is the fact that someone can do this.

According to recent reports, “There’s a reason we train behind closed doors; there are some things that you have to respect. I don’t think it’s nice for any club, honestly.”.

Despite the leak, Iraola acknowledged that his team will have to “adapt” given Pochettino’s arrival as boss. The Argentine replaced Michael Carrick following a dreadful start to the season for United, who right now sit 18th in the Premier League table.

“They’ll be well prepared,” he added. “They have this advantage that we don’t exactly know or don’t have the references. The beginning of the game will be key; we’ll have to adapt to certain things and I hope we already have some time with the players and we can manage this situation as quick as possible.

“But we know the players very well, their strengths and weaknesses, and that’s not going to change from one day to the other.”.

Iraola confirmed that Virgil van Dijk, Dominik Szoboszlai, Jeremie Frimpong and Ryan Gravenberch are all fit for Sunday’s clash at Anfield despite recent injury issues.
