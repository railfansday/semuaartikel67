In a recent development, PS5 Pro consoles have been hard to find lately, but there is a trick to getting one: All you need is superb basketball skills and membership on an NBA team based in the state that inspired Grand Theft Auto 6's location. Easy! While it's a little too late to take advantage of this life hack now, things have worked out for the Miami Heat, as they were gifted a unique set of PS5 Pro consoles this week. Miami Heat player Myron Gardner posted a photo of the care package that arrived for him, which includes the aforementioned PS5 Pro console, two officially licensed GTA 6 DualSense controllers, and a physical copy of the game, all sealed in a padded case. Compared to the recent, flashy PS5 console covers, this variant of the beefy PS5 has a more subtle design, combining a matte black finish with the GTA 6 logo, palm tree silhouettes, and neon-inspired colors on the console's physical stripes. Image credit: Myron Gardner on Instagram The consoles will likely be a small incentive for the Miami Heat team, as they'll be representing their city and GTA 6 when they hit the court tonight for a match against the Milwaukee Bucks. Developer Rockstar Games and publisher Take-Two Interactive have taken over the Miami Heat stadium, decking it out in custom GTA 6 graphics and insignia for the basketball game, as well as custom uniforms for the team. The Miami Heat are the odds-on favorites to win, as they've enjoyed a five-game winning streak lately, while the Milwaukee Bucks are looking to break their current losing streak, which is now 10 games long. The A Night in Vice City basketball game is set to be a massive attraction, as it'll cap off the global rollout of GTA 6 tonight when the game unlocks in the US. Already active in several other time zones at the time of writing, GTA 6 has received overwhelmingly positive reviews and is the highest-rated game of the year. "Rockstar’s most ambitious game yet was worth the wait," GameSpot editor-at-large Tamoor Hussain wrote in GameSpot's GTA 6 review. With an absolutely massive sandbox and tons to do, it's a good idea to consult a few walkthroughs to help you keep track of everything. GameSpot’s GTA 6 guides hub lets you do just that, and it will be continually updated with newly introduced tips and tricks in the weeks and months to come.
