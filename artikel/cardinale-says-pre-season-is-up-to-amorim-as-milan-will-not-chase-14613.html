Gerry Cardinale has provided some refreshing comments on Milan’s pre-season prepares for the future, confirming that money will no longer dictate where they go.

In a recent development, over the last ten years or so, it has become far more common for big clubs to travel across the world for their pre-season campaigns. For example, Milan have been in Australia for two consecutive pre-seasons now, taking on Perth Glory and most recently Inter.

According to recent reports, while it’s great to connect with local fan bases, the travel is demanding for the players. This summer, Milan played games in Glasgow (Scotland), Perth (Australia), Jakarta (Indonesia), and Wroclaw (Poland). resulting in a lot of travel.

Speaking about the pre-season in his long interview with Gazzetta dello Sport, Cardinale remarked that the upcoming pre-season planning will be decided by Ruben Amorim. Money will no longer dictate where they go. Instead, the best plan for preparing the players will be chosen.

It has been revealed that in an effort to generate new revenue, are you also considering a tour for AC Milan next summer? Will that still be Australia or the United States?

Sources indicate that “We have already discussed it with Amorim, and I will just tell you that if we play friendlies abroad, they will be in the United States next summer. If we do… We haven’t decided yet, and the decision will be up to Ruben.

Industry observers note that “It will depend on what’s best for the team: playing friendlies abroad must add value to the group, not bring in a couple of million for the club. Those extra two million will no longer be our goal, as they were under the previous management. That won’t happen again under this ownership.

“Everything we organise in terms of friendlies will be carefully considered with our coach. Every move. And we won’t participate in tours organised by others. We put our players first. We want to help them prepare well to win. That’s our goal, and we aim to achieve it as soon as possible.”.
