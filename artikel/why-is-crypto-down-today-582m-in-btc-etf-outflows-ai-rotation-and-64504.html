📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that the early hours of the 20th of November saw Bitcoin [BTC] trading at $61,678 after a staggering 9.7% drop in the last 24 hours. Ethereum [ETH], following the crypto bloodbath, traded at $1,732 after experiencing a 3.7% slide in this time frame.

The crypto market’s current downfall is primarily due to persistent Spot ETF outflows and lingering fallout from last month’s Binance breach, which are making investors wary.

As the global cryptocurrency market cap fell 2.2% to $2.21 trillion in the last 24 hours at press time, the Crypto Fear & Greed Index slid to 12, indicating “extreme fear.”.

It has been revealed that after $306.5 million left Spot Bitcoin ETFs on the 18th of November, CoinGlass data showed that $582.3 million in outflows on the following day,  pushing this month’s outflows well past the $2.9 billion mark. Continued ETF bleeding signals that buyers continue to exit, leaving the market quite vulnerable to macro volatility.

Sources indicate that similarly, minutes from the October FOMC meeting, released on the 18th of November, showed that nearly all officials expect further rate hikes. Notably, Governor Christopher Waller even floated a half-point move for December.

With the Fed target at 3.75%–4.00%, digital assets will not have an easy battle.

Industry observers note that ever since investors have moved away from crypto and into AI, the former’s fall has been nearly constant. For the uninitiated, on the 18th of November, Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, thus valuing the AI company at roughly $1.5 trillion.

The news drew massive investor attention, pulling capital away from crypto markets as a result.

Regardless, the long-term sentiment of crypto investors has already been sullied due to the October 29 Binance hack that resulted in $6.3 billion in stolen funds.

Recent findings suggest that the crypto bloodbath will likely continue unless ETF flows stabilize or the Fed signals a pause. With the December 9 FOMC meeting as the only upcoming checkpoint, 60,000 is the next level for traders to watch. Losing it can tip Bitcoin deeper into the mid-50,000s.
