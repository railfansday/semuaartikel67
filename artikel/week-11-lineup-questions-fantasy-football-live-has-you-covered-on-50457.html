Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season is here — and there's no better place to get last-minute start-sit advice this Sunday than Fantasy Football Live!

Our top-notch fantasy analysts will cover last-minute pickups, inactives news and answer your lineup questions during the 2.5-hour AMA-style show, starting at 10:30 a.m. ET and going all the way up to kickoff.

Four of the most trusted names in fantasy will be answering your lineup questions:.

Justin Boone: A two-time winner of the FantasyPros Most Accurate Expert Award (2019, 2025), he has nine top-10 finishes in the competition.

Matt Harmon: Creator of Reception Perception, he's known for his expertise on wide receivers and his ability to translate Xs and Os for fantasy managers.

Josh Norris: One of fantasy's most trusted voices, backed by data-driven analysis and film study, he has produced the most accurate mock draft in NFL Draft history.

Hayden Winks: A three-time FSWA and Webby Award winner, he's built a loyal following for his advanced statistics work and film study.

QB | RB | WR | TE | FLEX | D/ST | Kickers.

Catch Fantasy Football Live on YahooSports.com, the Yahoo Sports app, the Yahoo Fantasy app, YouTube, X, Facebook and the Yahoo Sports Network, starting at 10:30 a.m. ET on Sunday.

Join the conversation on the YouTube chat or use #AskFFL on X.

Analyst Scott Pianowski will be answering start-sit questions on X (@YahooFantasy) beginning at noon ET — just tag your questions with #AskFFL.

According to recent reports, analyst Joel Smyth will also be live on TikTok at 12:15 p.m. ET, ready to help with any last-minute decisions.
