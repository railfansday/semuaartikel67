📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that philadelphia 76ers forward Jaylen Brown briefly went down with a potential head injury after knocking heads with Brooklyn Nets forward Julius Randle. Though Brown was bleeding, he eventually returned to the game with a bandage on his head, and played a few more minutes of the 76ers' 135-123 win over the Nets.

It has been revealed that the collision between Brown and Randle happened in the fourth quarter of Monday's matchup in Brooklyn, with Brown's forehead connecting with Randle's mouth. Both men were bleeding after the incident, though from different parts of the head.

Recent findings suggest that brown stayed down for a while, rolling onto his back and covering his face with his hands. The entire 76ers bench soon came out to surround him while he was tended to by trainers. Brown then received additional care on the bench, before returning to the game with a bandage on his head.

Brown eventually left again because of tightness in his left hamstring. He noted he will have additional testing on both his head and his hamstring on Tuesday.

Randle, meanwhile, got his bloody lip stitched up off the court and returned to the game as well. Randle was called for the foul on the play, but because it wasn't a shooting foul, Brown did not need to attempt free throws.

According to recent reports, at the time of the foul, Brown had scored 29 points and picked up 12 rebounds, thanks to a first-half double-double. He finished with 31 points, 13 rebounds and 4 assists in the 12-point victory.

Joel Embiid led both teams with 37 points, while Randle had 33 points for Brooklyn in the loss.
