📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the 2026 Brawl Stars World Finals, which took place this past week in Tokyo, Japan, already stood out for its exotic location. But it's not just the place that's catching people's eye, as Supercell have also teamed up with skateboard designer Bear Walker to commemorate the occasion with a brand-newly introduced deck!

It has been revealed that while those attending the Brawl Stars World Finals in Tokyo will have been in with a chance to buy one of a thousand limited-edition Melted Spike decks, now those of you who didn't get the chance can try to nab the Sakura Spike street deck instead! Designed around fan-favourite character Spike, and the iconic cherry blossom trees of Japan, this deck is only available to buy until November 29th!

It has been revealed that A lot of people who grew up playing Brawl Stars will now be in their mid-to-late 20s, and that means many will probably have their own interests such as, yes, skateboarding. And what better way to keep them interested than to play into those fans' hobbies?

The Brawl Stars World Finals themselves were a high-profile affair, with their own opening ceremony and even a brand-new trophy crafted in collaboration with silversmith Thomas Lyte. It all points to Supercell trying to really ramp up the cool factor for Brawl Stars.
