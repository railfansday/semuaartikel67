Industry News: Stuttgart coach Hoeness hails Inter ahead of Champions League trip: ‘One of the best in Europe’

Sebastian Hoeness believes Inter are one of the best teams in Europe, but insists Stuttgart can hold their own against the Nerazzurri at San Siro on Wednesday.

Sources indicate that with quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Stuttgart coach was speaking at his pre-match press conference ahead of the Champions League league-phase clash. Kick-off is at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT].

According to recent reports, the two sides are level in the table going into the game. Inter are 15th with six points, while Stuttgart are 14th with seven.

“I have to be very honest, it was impossible to expect that we’d be ahead of them,” Hoeness said.

Industry observers note that “We’re ahead and we’ll do everything to keep Inter behind us. We want to measure ourselves against them, they’re among the biggest teams in Europe, and we’re capable of standing up to such strong opponents.

According to recent reports, “Inter are very well organised, they have a great coach and great individuals. They find a mix between strong organisation, with very clear patterns, and the quality of the individuals.”.

Inter conceded four times at Atalanta on Sunday, and Hoeness was asked whether he could exploit their defensive problems.

According to recent reports, “We saw that it’s unusual for Inter to concede so many goals. I don’t know if there are structural flaws. I don’t think Inter will play with the same defence as in Bergamo,” he said.

In a recent development, “There are also absences, but they have a fantastic team, and we have to show what we’re capable of, perhaps by exploiting their weak points. It will be about seizing the moments that might be more favourable for us and making the most of them.”.

In a recent development, cristian Chivu will be without Piotr Zieliński and Hakan Çalhanoğlu. Tests on Çalhanoğlu revealed a muscle strain in the flexors of his right thigh, and he is expected to be out for around three to four weeks.

Hoeness confirmed that midfielder Angelo Stiller has recovered and is available.

Sources indicate that “We’re very happy to be here. We had a tour of the stadium and it was impressive. It’s huge, just as I remember it from seeing it as a spectator as a teenager,” he added. “This stadium has an incredible history, including the chapter of the 1990 World Cup. We’re happy to play here and be part of its history.”.
