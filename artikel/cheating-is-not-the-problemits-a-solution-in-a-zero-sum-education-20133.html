Cheating is not the problem–it’s a solution (in a zero-sum educational system)

🌐 Source: Eschoolnews • 📅 01 Oct 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that in a TED Talk viewed over 2 million times, Efosa Ojomo of the Christensen Institute made the provocative case that corruption isn’t the problem holding poor countries back. Corruption is the solution for people in poor countries to make progress when facing scarcity around basic needs like food, education, health care, economic opportunity, and jobs.
