One of the first things GTA 6 players searched for was strip club locations—genuinely, I have the data. So, as a responsible guide writer, I loaded up the game and proceeded to spend my initial hours beelining around Leonida's hotspots. There are a few different strip clubs in Leonida, each with their own price range and different activities to try (that sounds sordid, but I'm also talking about, like, pool tables).

There are three strip clubs in the game I've found, plus a load of other night spots, like the Rusty Anchor close to where Jason starts, that have drinking and other activities, too.

This fancy strip club in downtown Vice City is the classiest option you have available, but also the priciest. It's quite easy to get here, even from Leonida Keys where Jason starts—just drive along route 1 towards Hamlet, merge onto route 97, and head north into Downtown. The clubs are just to the right of the giant crossroads route interchange in west Vice City. NINE1NINE itself is next door to The Vault club. This is the best option if you have a lot of money to burn, but also it feels a bit too fancy.

Sources indicate that if you want a private dance, same as with previous GTA games, you just need to greet the dancer in question and then use the interaction icon to pay for it. Since this club is fancier, you can also get a private dance by interacting with the person by the entrance to the private rooms.

Recent findings suggest that I appreciated NINE1NINE's lively club atmosphere, but I also ran out of cash very fast. Maybe one to try later in the game when you're a lot richer.

This strip club in Vice City's Crosstown is directly north from NINE1NINE, but there's a catch: this place only unlocks via the main story. I assume it's Chapter 3, considering that's when you meet its owner, but either way, it'll be a while before you can visit this establishment. I assume in terms of price range, it's fancier than Delights (the cheapest option), but less fancy than NINE1NINE. This club gets one star because it wouldn't let me in, though maybe I'll change this review once I actually get inside.

Delights in Port Gellhorn is your cheapest option and it's a bit more rough and ready relative to NINE1NINE. It's also a long drive if you're just starting the game and want to visit the club in the northwest of the map. You'll want to drive to Hamlet via route 1 from the Keys, as with NINE1NINE, but then head northwest on route 82 towards Capri, then 103 merging onto the 404, past the airport towards Port Gellhorn. Bank a right when you reach the town, and the big Delights billboard to find it a little way further, on your left.
