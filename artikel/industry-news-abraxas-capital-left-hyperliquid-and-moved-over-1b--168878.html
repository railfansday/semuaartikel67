Industry News: Abraxas Capital left Hyperliquid and moved over $1B of Bitcoin — Why?

🌐 Source: Ambcrypto • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (316 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Wallets linked to Abraxas Capital received another 1,700 Bitcoin [BTC] from Kraken, worth roughly $103 million.
  • The latest Abraxas Bitcoin withdrawals brought the six-day tally across Kraken and Binance to about 21,500 BTC.
  • Lookonchain called the latest transfers a purchase.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Wallets linked to Abraxas Capital received another 1,700 Bitcoin [BTC] from Kraken, worth roughly $103 million. The transfers, reported on 27th November, arrived in batches of 700 BTC and 1,000 BTC.

The latest Abraxas Bitcoin withdrawals brought the six-day tally across Kraken and Binance to about 21,500 BTC. On-chain tracker EmberCN valued those transfers at $1.285 billion.

Lookonchain called the latest transfers a purchase.

By 27th November, Arkham had tracked about 28,982 BTC across addresses it attributed to Abraxas Capital’s Heka Funds.

Lookonchain first flagged Abraxas cutting shorts on 16th November, the day Lighter reported an API key leak. The tracker tentatively linked Abraxas to the affected Lighter account.

Abraxas Capital has now withdrawn all its funds from Hyperliquid.

According to recent reports, by 25th November, Abraxas had pulled its remaining funds from Hyperliquid. It also sold 219,081 HYPE and bought 4,230 Ethereum [ETH].

Sources indicate that additionally, on-chain researcher MLM described the Hyperliquid positions as part of Elysium Global Arbitrage Fund’s market-neutral strategy. The estimated profit was around $530 – $552.49 million.

According to one X user, Abraxas “pulled the funds to buy Bitcoin.” Another suggested,.

My guess is the shorts were moved to exchanges.

In fact, Hyperliquid records revealed that the shorts had closed. If those shorts hedged Bitcoin and Abraxas let them lapse, its net BTC exposure could rise without another purchase.

While those wallets received Bitcoin [BTC], recent holders have been sitting on heavy paper losses.

Industry observers note that on 26th November, Alphractal’s STH Unrealized Profit/Loss Ratio hit 0.01. With a reading of 0.01, the ratio indicated how far underwater recent holders were at press time.

In a recent development, in essence, it implied that short-term holders’ unrealized losses were roughly 100 times their unrealized profits, with Bitcoin’s price near $60,500. According to Alphractal Founder and CEO Joao Wedson, such extremes have “often accompanied market bottom formations.”.
