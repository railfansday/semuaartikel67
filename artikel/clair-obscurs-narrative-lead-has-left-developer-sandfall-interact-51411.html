📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Last night, it was revealed that Clair Obscur: Expedition 33's lead narrative designer, Jennifer Svedberg-Yen, won a BAFTA Breakthrough US award for her contribution to the hit 2025 game, which made history after winning 12 Game Award nominations last year. In the aftermath of her win, Svedberg-Yen dropped a pretty big bombshell: she's no longer with the game's developer, Sandfall Interactive.

In a recent development, "No, I left Sandfall in July, after wrapping up some final stuff for Expedition," she told Gamesbeat when asked if she's still with the organization. "We've parted ways." As to what Svedberg-Yen is doing next, she offered a cryptic answer, including talk of writing "my own projects, my own stories". She also discussed another project she's set her sights on: establishing a fund to help mentor and grow a new generation of video game narrative designers.

It has been revealed that "One of the initiatives I want to build is a narrative investment fund to focus on narrative-focused game creators, trying to bridge the gap between the seed stage and going to publishers," she explained, noting her background working in finance and how that dovetails nicely with her more recent experience as a narrative lead. "This is the chicken and egg question a lot of people face. To get money from publishers you need a demo, but to make a demo you need money. To get money you have to go to publishers. That’s an area where I would like to help a lot of these smaller creatives to get that seed funding so they can, in a way, de-risk themselves in the eyes of bigger investors.".

Her motivation for this initiative? Simply, she wants to change how the industry works. "I've discovered that quite a lot of studios don’t really know how to think about narrative, or how to integrate it into their pipelines and their processes. They tend to think it's a very narrow thing, when in fact it can influence all aspects. Part of this would also be an incubation program to help these studios develop those narrative foundations.".

In a recent development, "The strongest feeling in me right now for 2027 is I want to help," she added later. "That sounds so cheesy and idealistic. I always cringe at myself for saying it. But I don't know how else to say it and be truthful. I want to help in some way, help the industry.".

Sandfall Interactive previously said its guiding "North Star" will always be its "own personal taste", and what the team enjoys.She noted that she didn't think the studio would bow to pressure from fans to do more of the same, but instead be guided by what it thinks is best for itself, and for the series. "I've seen too many TV highlights and books be swayed trying to please a lot of people, and in the process you end up losing the heart of what's there," she noted.

In a recent development, we know that Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is "not the end" of the franchise, and it seems that whatever happens next, it'll have at least a little bit of Svedberg-Yen's creativity instilled in it. I wish her the best for whatever her next step is.
