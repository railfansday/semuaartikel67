For adventurers and outdoor enthusiasts who want more than the average smartwatch, Garmin sets the standard with its industry-leading features and durability. But while the brand is on many wishlists, its high price tags stop many shoppers from making the splurge. Fortunately, with Amazon’s early Black Friday sale, you can get one of its bestsellers, the 44mm Garmin Instinct 3 AMOLED, for 44% off at $249.99 (originally $449.99).

Recent findings suggest that the 1.2-inch color AMOLED display makes the screen more readable in bright daylight conditions, and battery life is best-in-class, lasting up to 18 days (even longer when you’re not using it regularly), which reduces the need for weekly charging, a must for those going off-grid. Durability is also a major perk, with features like a metal-reinforced bezel, chemically strengthened glass, and a 10 ATM water rating, which makes it suitable for underwater activities.

It comes with a built-in flashlight and multi-band GPS, supported by Garmin’s SatIQ technology to better balance location accuracy and battery use, while tracking features include heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, and other wellness metrics. There’s no shortage of training tools for workouts and outdoor activities, and like regular smartwatches, it displays alerts like texts and emails when paired with an iPhone or Android. The Garmin Pay feature is another bonus for those who don’t want to have their wallet on them at all times.

Recent findings suggest that if you want to log your activities, whether that’s skiing the slopes, heading out on a long trail run, or going on a camping trip in the woods, the 44mm Garmin Instinct 3 AMOLED is a rugged option with plenty of useful tools for those who love the outdoors. And you can’t forget its wildly impressive battery life. At $200 off, it’s a powerful entry into the Garmin world that can accomplish a lot and won’t set you back nearly as much as the brand’s more loaded watches.
