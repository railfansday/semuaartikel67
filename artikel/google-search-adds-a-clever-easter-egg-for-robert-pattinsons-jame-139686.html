Following this week’s casting of Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, Google Search has already added a clever little Easter egg to celebrate the announcement.

Amazon announced on November 16 that actor Robert Pattinson will take on the role of James Bond, though his first film does not yet have a firm release window. Denis Villeneuve, best known for the Dune trilogy, will direct the movie.

Commemorating the casting, Google Search has added a upcoming Easter egg when you search for “Robert Pattinson.”.

It has been revealed that the actor’s results page now shakes the highlighted photos before dropping a lemon peel garnish on top, a cheeky reference to Bond’s “shaken, not stirred” martini order. It’s a clever little Easter egg, and a timely one too as it appeared not long after the casting was announced.
