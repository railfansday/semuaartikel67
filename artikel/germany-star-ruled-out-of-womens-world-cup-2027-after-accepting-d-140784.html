Germany forward Laura Freigang will miss next summer’s Women’s World Cup after accepting a suspension over failure to comply with doping regulations.

The Eintracht Frankfurt captain was accused of failing to provide a drugs test or submit her whereabouts for testing on three occasions between March 2025 and February 2026, thereby breaching the World Anti-Doping Code.

She reached an settlement agreement with WADA, Germany’s National Anti-Doping Agency, and the German football federation over the case, which has resulted in a 16-month ban backdated to 8 June 2026, the day she was notified of the case against her.

According to recent reports, it means she will not be eligible to return to football until 8 October 2027. She will miss the Women’s World Cup, which runs from 24 June to 25 July, in Brazil.

The 28-year-old can return to training two months before the expiry of the ban.

Sources indicate that eintracht Frankfurt declared the news on Thursday, with the club to “soon begin discussions to establish the structuring of their future contract [with Freigang] in light of this suspension”. Freigang’s contract runs until 30 June next year.

Freigang released a statement on Instagram saying: “It is notable for me to emphasise once again that at no time was there any suspicion of doping against me.

Sources indicate that “The last few months have been extremely hard for me. Further proceedings would have meant high financial, mental and emotional burdens for me. That's why I chose this way, in the hope of finding some calm, clarity and perspective for myself.”.

She added: “It breaks my heart not to be able to be on the field, helping my team and being able to live the everyday life that I love so much.”.

None of Eintracht Frankfurt’s results since her ban began in June are affected.

Katharina Kiel, director of women’s football, said: “The agreement that has now been reached provides clarity and planning certainty in a process that is unique to Eintracht Frankfurt – for us as a club, but above all for Laura.

“The way she and the team have handled the situation over the past few months, and the way Laura personally led by example on the pitch during a very difficult time, deserves the utmost recognition.

“At Eintracht Frankfurt, we have witnessed the strong sense of unity that characterises this club. We will calmly discuss the future and the details of our working relationship with Laura.”.

Freigang has 45 caps for Germany and was a member of the squad which reached the Euro 2022 final. She has been at Eintracht Frankfurt since 2018.
