📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that nate Tice & Matt Harmon preview the biggest games of Week 11 of the NFL season. The duo start with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers taking on the Detroit Lions without Baker Mayfield. Can Tampa Bay's defense save the day against an explosive Detroit offense?

Industry observers note that next, the guys dive into the chess match of the season: Kyle Shanahan's unstoppable San Francisco 49ers offense taking on Brian Flores' immovable Minnesota Vikings defense. The guys guess at how Flores might try to slow down Brock Purdy. Wrapping up the deep dives, it's the New York Giants in a major prove-it match against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

Later, Nate & Matt dive into the Tennessee Titans taking on the Dallas Cowboys in a game that should result in plenty of points, as both offenses have been steadily improving (and both defenses are headed in the wrong direction). In a week full of double-digit spreads, the guys also discuss why they have a hunch that the Las Vegas Raiders have a chance to pull off a significant upset vs. the Denver Broncos. The show wraps up with their Hail Mary bold predictions for Week 11 involving Mike Gesicki, the Houston Texans and hammering unders in honor of GTA VI.
