Chelsea transfer news: Xabi Alonso has reportedly told Malo Gusto to find a fresh club, according to CaughtOffside, who are citing Simon Phillips’ Substack. The report claims the 23-year-old France international does not suit the wing-back system Alonso has built at Stamford Bridge. It also floats a surprise swap that would send Gusto to Liverpool and reunite Alonso with Jeremie Frimpong, his former Bayer Leverkusen right wing-back.

Liverpool’s interest has history. They tried to sign Gusto on loan on deadline day, but Chelsea refused anything short of a permanent deal. Their right-back problem has run since Trent Alexander-Arnold left. Frimpong cost around £29.5million and has battled injuries and form, while Ronald Araujo is a centre-back covering on a season-long loan from Barcelona.

Gusto’s standing has slipped swiftly. He started three of the first four league games, yet Josh Acheampong has since taken his place, and reports say he will be fourth choice behind Acheampong, Marco Palestra and Reece James once James returns from injury in early 2027.

Industry observers note that his strength is defending, with 73 tackles and 36 chances created in 45 appearances last season. The weakness is end product, because three goals and five assists are modest for a wing-back who must supply Alonso’s right-hand width.

Frimpong supplies exactly that. The 25-year-old Dutchman scored 28 of his 30 Leverkusen goals under Alonso and thrives on pace and early crosses. Defending is his flaw, at 5ft 8in and with a poor injury record at Anfield.

Industry observers note that no. Chelsea are fifth with 16 points from 10 games, but they have lost four, including 1-0 at Sunderland last Sunday and 3-0 at Brentford, and they concede too easily. Frimpong would add another attacker to a right side that already has James, Acheampong, Palestra and Pedro Neto, and he does nothing for a shaky back line. With no European football this season, Chelsea don’t need that many right-sided options anyway.

Sources indicate that the money matters too. Both players have contracts until 2030 and similar valuations, so a straight swap brings Chelsea no cash and leaves Liverpool with their problem solved.

According to recent reports, sell Gusto instead, preferably abroad, since Paris Saint-Germain have been linked and Chelsea are reportedly open to cutting their asking price. Then spend that fee on a defensive midfielder or centre-back, because that is where Alonso’s team needs help.
