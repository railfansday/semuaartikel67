Tony Mowbray has stepped down from his role as Blackburn Rovers manager due to health reasons.

Mowbray was diagnosed with bowel cancer in early 2024 and had to stand down from his role at Birmingham.

He was later given the all-clear after a spell at West Brom last year, he joined Blackburn for a second spell in June.

However, the Championship club have confirmed that he will once again be stepping away from work.

“Unfortunately, due to health issues, I have to step back from my role as Head Coach of Blackburn Rovers,” Mowbray stated in a statement.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m immensely proud of this group of players. Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I’ve loved working with them.

It has been revealed that “Thank you to my coaching staff for all your hard work and for the support you’ve given me. It’s been a pleasure working alongside you.

Industry observers note that “To the fans, thank you for the way you’ve supported me and the team this season. I’ve always tried to give you a team that you can be proud of. Your support has meant a great deal to me.

“I’m grateful to the Owners and Suhail [Shaikh, Blackburn COO] for giving me the opportunity to lead this great club once again.

“I’d ask that you give me and my family privacy at this time. Thank you for your understanding.”.

Mowbray previously managed Blackburn for five years between 2017 and 2022, and has previously held roles at the likes of Sunderland, Coventry, Middlesbrough and Celtic.

He has sparked something of a resurgence at Ewood Park, with Blackburn finding themselves just three points off the play-offs in 13th despite going into the season touted for relegation.

"Tony's passion for the club and his professionalism are clear to see, but it is his kindness and the care he highlights for players, staff and those around him that mean so much," Blackburn chief executive officer Steve Curwood added.

It has been revealed that "Our priority now is to make sure he feels that same care and support from all of us. We will support him and his family in every way we can.".
