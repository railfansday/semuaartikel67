It has been revealed that alphabet (GOOGL -0.88%)(GOOG -0.77%) has seen high volatility this year. The company's share price hit a lifetime high of over $400 in May, but it has since seen a valuation pullback. As of this writing, the tech giant's share price is down 27% from its valuation peak.

NASDAQ · As of 9:50 AM ET 11/17/26.

In a recent development, there are multiple catalysts behind Alphabet's valuation contraction. For starters, macroeconomic conditions have raised concerns about the health of the overall market -- with inflation, high bond yields, the rising national debt, and the likelihood of further interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve all adding to a general sense of unease among investors.

More specifically, the growth and valuation outlook for companies in the artificial intelligence (AI) industry has been called into question. With these dynamics pressuring Alphabet's share price, is the stock worth buying following recent pullbacks?

With recent downward pressure on valuations across the AI industry and uncertainty about the implications of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation, it's not unreasonable to expect that artificial intelligence stocks will continue to face near-term downside volatility. While this broader trend could continue to pressure Alphabet's share price through the remainder of the year, I also think the stock still has what it takes to be a winner for long-term investors at today's prices.

While growth in Alphabet's AI-centric Google Cloud business has been at the heart of the story for Alphabet stock recently, the company has strengths across numerous categories. It should continue to see strong growth in its cloud infrastructure business, even if the unit faces near-term headwinds that curb its expansion rate. Trading at roughly 23 times this year's expected earnings, Alphabet looks like a worthwhile long-term investment following recent volatility.
