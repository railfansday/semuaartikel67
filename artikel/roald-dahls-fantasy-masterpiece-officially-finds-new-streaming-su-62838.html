Before Harry Potter entered theaters in 2001, a highly praised children's novel was adapted into a film. The feature had a star-studded cast and earned praise from fans and critics alike. 30 years later, the feature has captured a latest audience and become a streaming hit.

The film in question is Matilda, a children's fantasy movie based on Roald Dahl's book of the same name, starring Mara Wilson as Matilda, a young girl who discovers she has powers. Unlike Stephen King's Carrie, a horror story that also follows a young girl with powers, Matilda uses her newfound abilities to help others. The book came out in 1988, and the film adaptation, which also starred Danny DeVito (It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia), Rhea Perlman (Barbie), and Pam Ferris (Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban), was released in 1996.

Recent findings suggest that recently, Matilda became a streaming hit on HBO Max, landing at #4 on the platform's Top 10 Movies Worldwide, sitting between The End of Oak Street and The Legend of Tarzan. Since its release, Matilda has grossed $47 million worldwide and earned a Certified Fresh critics score of 90% and an audience score of 73% on Rotten Tomatoes, plus a 3.8-star rating on Letterboxd.
