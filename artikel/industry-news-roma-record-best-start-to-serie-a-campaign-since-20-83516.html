Roma are back to winning ways after the end of the latest international break.

The Giallorossi beat Parma 1-5 thanks to four goals by Malen and one by Mora.

With the three points earned in Parma, Roma have reached 30 points.

This is their best start since the 2013-2014 season when Rudi Garcia was on the bench.

That team, after 12 matchdays, had 32 points. Last year, Gasperini had also 27 points at this stage.

Malen makes Roma history scoring 26 Serie A goals in less than a year.

D’Amico on Kone’s future: “It’s unlikely he’ll leave in January.”.

Lulli before Parma kick-off: “We’re ready to give our all.”.

Mora to start alongside Dybala vs Parma.

Roma’s new stadium enters crucial phase: construction could begin in April 2027.

Roma only behind PSG in defensive output this season.
