England will play either Belgium, Netherlands or Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals as Thomas Tuchel’s side look to build on their impressive victory over world champions Spain in the group stage.

The 3-2 win in Madrid, which featured a dominant performance before hanging on with 10 men, saw England top their group and they will enter Friday’s draw as one of the top seeds, along with France, Germany and Portugal.

England will play a two-legged quarter-final during the next international break in March, and will be at home for the second leg as they look to advance to the Nations League Finals, which will be staged in June and at the end of the season.

There have been reports of the Football Association expressing an interest in hosting the Nations League Finals, which feature the two semi-finals and the final. Portugal, Italy, the Netherlands and Germany have previously hosted the Finals.

The host country of the Finals is typically involved in the final four, although the Nations League could also allow the FA a test event before hosting Euro 2028 the following summer, with Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland also co-hosts.

Here’s everything you need to know ahead of Friday’s draw.

In a recent development, the draw will take place at 11am GMT on Friday 20 November. It will be conducted from Nyon, Uefa’s headquarters, in Switzerland.

England cannot draw Spain as they played them in Group A3.
