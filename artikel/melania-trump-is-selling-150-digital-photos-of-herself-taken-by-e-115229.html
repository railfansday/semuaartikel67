Sources indicate that U.S. first lady Melania Trump is selling digital photos of herself shot by influential fashion photographer Ellen von Unwerth for $150.

Sources indicate that melania made the announcement of the sale of “exclusive” photographs taken by von Unwerth in a post on X (the platform formerly known as Twitter) on Sunday. The launch coincides with the release of MELANIA: The Series, a upcoming two-part production focusing on the wife of President Donald Trump, which debuted on Amazon Prime Video on November 20.

— MELANIA TRUMP (@MELANIATRUMP) November 22, 2026.

According to the product listing on Melania’s official website for the “PhotoArchival Digital Edition”, she collaborated with von Unwerth over a two-day photo shoot “to develop the visual narrative” of the images. The photographs are being sold for $150 as a digital album delivered in the form of a movie file.

Industry observers note that according to a report by Newsweek, buyers are also required to have a cryptocurrency wallet on the Solana blockchain to store the digital collectible. While customers can currently pay by credit card, they must connect their cryptocurrency wallet to Melania’s website before completing the purchase.

Sources indicate that “Melania Trump is launching PhotoArchival Digital Edition, an exclusive collection of digital photographs by internationally acclaimed fashion photographer Ellen von Unwerth,” the announcement on X states. “The collection presents a visual portrait of Mrs. Trump, showcasing von Unwerth’s distinctive artistry. Its release coincides with the debut of MELANIA, The Series, a fresh two-part production streaming on Amazon Prime Video.”.

In a recent development, von Unwerth also shot the photography for Melania, the documentary about the first lady released in January, and some of the images featured in the $150 PhotoArchival Digital Edition appear to be the same. One of von Unwerth’s black-and-white photographs shows Melania wearing a fitted suit, sitting in a chair with her legs crossed and looking directly at the camera. Another image shows Melania standing beside a mirror in a strapless black dress as someone holds a clapperboard in the shot.

The release of Melania’s digital photo collection follows a recent interview in which von Unwerth, one of the most sought-after fashion photographers working today, claimed that “there’s no money left” in the industry.
