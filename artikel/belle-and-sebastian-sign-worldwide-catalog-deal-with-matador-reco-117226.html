📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Matador Records has secured worldwide rights to the complete catalog of Belle and Sebastian, bringing the Glasgow indie-pop group’s recorded output under the independent label’s global representation.

Recent findings suggest that the deal extends a relationship between the band and Matador that dates back to 1998, when the label released the group’s third album, The Boy With The Arab Strap, in North America before subsequently reissuing the band’s 1996 debut Tigermilk. Under the upcoming agreement, If You’re Feeling Sinister (1996) and The Boy With The Arab Strap, alongside the four EPs collected on 2005’s Push Barman to Open Old Wounds Volume 1, will be licensed from Bella Figura Music. These titles will carry both the Bella Figura and Matador Records imprints.

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, Dear Catastrophe Waitress (2003), The Life Pursuit (2006), Write About Love (2010) and The Third Eye Centre (2013), together with their associated EPs and singles, will be licensed directly from Belle and Sebastian. Originally released under Rough Trade, these titles will be branded as Matador/Rough Trade worldwide.

The band’s remaining seven albums — Tigermilk, Fold Your Hands Child, You Walk Like A Peasant (2000), Storytelling (2002), Girls In Peacetime Want to Dance (2015), Days Of The Bagnold Summer (2019), A Bit of Previous (2022) and Late Developers (2023) — will be licensed directly from the band and released under the Matador moniker.

The first release under the expanded arrangement will be a 30th anniversary edition of If You’re Feeling Sinister, featuring previously unreleased songs and unheard demos from the era.

Industry observers note that matador has recently expanded its catalog by adding the rights to recordings by a number of influential indie acts and artists, including Pavement, Spoon and Interpol. Further reissues and expanded editions are expected in 2027, according to a press release.

Belle and Sebastian have scored five top 10 albums on the Official U.K. Albums Chart, the most recent being A Bit of Previous, which landed at No. 8. Write About Love, meanwhile, stands as their highest-charting release on the Billboard 200, reaching No. 15.
