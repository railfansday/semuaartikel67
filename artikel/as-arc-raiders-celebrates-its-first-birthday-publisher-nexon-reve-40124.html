📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Arc Raiders, Embark Studios' popular extraction shooter, has exceeded 17m copies sold, according to publisher Nexon.

Sources indicate that arc Raiders was released at the end of October 2025, so reaching that milestone in a year is nothing to scoff at. According to Nexon's latest earnings report, however, only 1m copies of those 17 were sold in the third quarter - meaning sales have certainly slowed down.

Relatedly, Nexon said it expects Arc Raiders' revenue to decline in the fourth quarter compared to the same period last year - when the game enjoyed its hugely successful launch - and anticipates revenue will be "broadly flat sequentially". Despite this, the organization said Arc Raiders' revenue was "within the outlook", naming its Frozen Trail patch as a key moment that brought back some lapsed players while introducing upcoming ones.

Indeed, Frozen Trail boosted the game's Steam player count, and reports suggest it also helped Arc Raiders hit a six-month player high across various platforms.

Even with its slowing sales, Arc Raiders remains one of Nexon's most successful titles. The organization primarily attributed an overall year-on-year revenue increase, for the nine months ended 30th September, to the game.

Sources indicate that looking ahead, the biggest question hanging over Arc Raiders is whether or not it's going to get a dedicated PvE playlist. Following a successful test earlier this summer, Embark has been coy, and hasn't remarked whether PvE will return as a permanent feature. Arc Raiders' Chinese version does have a dedicated PvE mode, and executive producer Aleksander Grøndal said he could see both PvP and PvE modes co-existing in the future.
