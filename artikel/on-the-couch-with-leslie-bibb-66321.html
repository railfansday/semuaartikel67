📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Since appearing in season three of HBO’s The White Lotus in 2025, American actress Leslie Bibb has had no problem keeping busy. The North Dakota-born, Virginia-raised actress already had screen credits spanning from the Iron Man films to Apple TV+’s Palm Royale, but Bibb’s performance on Mike White’s hit series as one-third of a slightly toxic friendship trio spotlighted the actress’s knack for blending comedy and drama. Not to mention, her blunt bob served as hair inspiration for the masses. After The White Lotus, Bibb went on to guest star on HBO’s Hacks, landing her an Emmy nomination for Outstanding Guest Actress in a Comedy.

In a recent development, outside her day job, Bibb also happens to be a design enthusiast—she’s currently renovating the upstate New York home that she shares with longtime partner, fellow actor Sam Rockwell. For the interiors, Bibb has teamed up with ELLE Decor A-List designer Shawn Henderson, who often pops up on Bibb’s Instagram account, attending birthday parties and surveying the concrete shell that will one day be a pool. In the kitchen, Bibb is partnering with LG Signature on appliances that gel with the new renovation.

Next up for Bibb is a starring role alongside Brett Goldstein in Amazon Prime series Escorted. She is also executive producing a show with HBO called The Rush Whisperer, a dark comedy about sorority rush. Together with the home renovation, she has trouble choosing what to highlight. “I’m just grateful that I’m able to juggle work and life and my relationship with Sam,” claims the actress. For our On the Couch survey, she shares her greatest design inspirations, entertaining tips, and the artists that have her heart.

Favorite Eras of Design: All of them... well, most of them. I'm not into cold, modern pieces from the 1980s or that shabby chic stuff. But I do like a house that isn't just one thing, that is a mix of eras and has warmth—a house that is inviting and says, “Come in, kick off your shoes, grab a drink and let's hang out.” And I love color.

Favorite Sofa: I don't have a favorite sofa, but I do love an L-shaped sofa for watching TV so Sammy and I can be together but also have our own spaces to sprawl out.

Industry observers note that maverick design style trick: I feel incredibly lucky to have my friend and fabulous interior designer, Shawn Henderson, as my secret weapon. Home renovations can be a nightmare, but having Shawn as my partner in crime has really made the experience so much fun. And let's be honest, the kitchen is the heart of any home. When I think of LG Signature, I think about how they design to honor your lifestyle rather than compete with it.

In a recent development, most beloved buildings: I love the Chrysler Building. It always takes my breath away. Also, there is a church where my parents are buried in the country and I just love it. It is so small and quaint and the view from its windows and the grounds just make me smile.

In a recent development, destination Museum: Musée d'Orsay because I get to see my favorite painting by Gustave Caillebotte called The Floor Scrapers. Honestly, wandering through museums or art galleries is one of my happy places so in any city you will find me making a pit stop to get an art fix and steal some inspo. I just saw the Krasner and Pollock "Past Continuous" exhibit at the Met and it blew my socks off.

It has been revealed that go-to gallery or galleries: Any gallery, anywhere. Just go in and see what people are making. It will always inspire you in some way.

Favorite Artist/Artwork: I love art and wandering around a museum or gallery is one of my safe spaces. I am mesmerized by pieces from Noah Davis. I love Helen Frankenthaler and Hilma af Klint. Also, I love Nick Cave and Danny Fox and Ramon Enrich and Peter Doig. The photographs from Sally Mann I love, too. Honestly, there are so many. I am in awe.

Favorite getaway: Staycation...and hopefully at our home upstate once we are done!

Best Host/Hostess Gift: I love to wrap a bottle of olive oil in a great kitchen towel so it is two gifts in one. I like a gift that can be put to use.

In a recent development, entertaining Tips: Always have a well-stocked bar with loads of options and feed your guests. Also, make sure you have a killer dance mix going because it ain't a party unless people start dancing. And if you have more than 25 people, hire a bartender. Otherwise you won't get to socialize and you will be just hosting all night and you will be exhausted.

Cocktail or Mocktail of Choice: I am obsessed with this nonalcoholic Kolsch beer by Best Day Brewing Tech giant. And winter is upon us, so a great martini. I am in Italy right now and am besotted with Negronis.

Watch to Wear: There are two I really love: one I own that Sam gave me so long ago, my Rolex, and the other is one I want to get, which is the Cartier Baignoire.
