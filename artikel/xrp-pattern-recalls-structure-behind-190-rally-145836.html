A latest research paper identifies striking similarities between the current market and November 2022.

XRP’s price setup has printed higher highs in crypto charts on a monthly basis. With traders trying to locate the local price bottom, speculative positions are winding down on Perpetual markets. These two conflicting signals present a setup that has been witnessed once in 2022.

Industry observers note that recently, Turkish analyst Celal Kucuker dived into the monthly chart from Bitstamp, which shows XRP near $1 with an ascending green trend-line and price projections indicating potential moves to $3.19 and $5.94. The seasoned analyst framed $5 as achievable in the longer-term.

$XRP has one of the best-looking charts in the market.XRP has maintained a rising trendline since 2014 ts long-term chart structure is remarkable. $5 and beyond is certainly not a fantasy. pic.twitter.com/Wuvq7P3D0E.

Industry observers note that the X post attracted 522 likes and 31k views with mixed replies questioning near-term catalysts. He called XRP’ monthly setup “one of the best-looking charts”, adding that XRP has maintained a rising trendline formation since 2014, positioning $3.19 as the initial target in the next bull run.

The sentiment is bitter & the market mood resembles that of November 2022, latest research from Bitrue’s Research Institute states. The analysts at Bitrue analyzed a multi-cycle market data bundle from 2022 to 2026, showcasing similar levels of ‘extreme fear’ around 20.

Back in 2022, FTX fiasco has ambushed the global crypto markets with an uncalled for liquidation event erasing billions of dollars a day for consecutive weeks. The downswings in market value are also similar.

XRP’s cycle drawdown stands at 77% now in comparison to 81% then. Crypto chart analyst Cleo Schmidt marked a series of ‘bull-traps’ prior to XRP spiking by 1,000% and 800% during the bull runs back in 2021 & 2024.

$XRP keeps repeating the same pattern.A false bottom.A bull trap.A period of range-bound consolidation.And only then, the real bottom.Every previous rally has begun after this sequence, not during it.The chart is currently in that intermediate phase.The "launchpad" isn't… pic.twitter.com/OehoKC8hSX.

Schmidt’s chart examines XRP’s weekly price from 2017 through 2026, highlighting nearly-identical patterns across several market cycles.

Two months from the FTX-infused crypto beatdown, XRP’s holders roughly gained 190% from the recorded XRP price on November 21, 2022. It took them 8 months to gain 115%, but the OG altcoin backtracked to 60% of yearly gains one year after the FTX collapse.

Surely, the 190% pattern could play out quicker with proper demand. With Ripple scoring upcoming regulatory winnings, the compliance leadership doesn’t necessarily translate into XRP’s price appreciation. A part of that is attributed to the rise of stablecoins, along with Ripple’s RLUSD.

On the other hand, Bitrue Research Institute offers some encouragement in their Futures market data findings. Since 2017, XRP’s price has finished below $1 six times on a weekly basis, but restored that level each time.

The historical XRP price comparison heavily relies on holding the $0.859 support. Shaking below would basically invalidate the bullish thesis based on XRP’s price history.

Aside from technicals, institutions may pick it up: heading into 2027, ETFs will play a pivotal role in XRP’s bounce back attempt.
