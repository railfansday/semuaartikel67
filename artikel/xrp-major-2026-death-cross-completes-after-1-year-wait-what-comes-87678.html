The daily chart of XRP has yielded a death cross, the first time the signal has popped up in 2026. XRP experienced a death cross on its daily chart in November 2025 and then entered a downtrend for several months before a recovery in August 2026.

Recent findings suggest that buoyed by optimism around favorable crypto regulation, XRP had already seen a golden cross in September 2026 before this. But that didn’t last long. The Senate stalled on the Clarity Act, a bill on crypto market structure.

Since then, the wider crypto market has struggled. The declines deepened in late October, as concerns over artificial intelligence valuations and spending fueled a broad tech sell-off. They were compounded by the $6.3 billion Binance hack and weakening institutional ETF demand.

Recent findings suggest that U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded about $4.97 million in net outflows Friday, according to SoSoValue data, with the withdrawals concentrated in Bitwise's XRP fund.

Throughout November, the crypto market has struggled due to a combination of dollar strength and leveraged liquidations.

The 50 DMA fell below the 200 DMA on the daily chart, completing a death cross pattern, coinciding with a sharp decline that took XRP below a key support and left leveraged traders nursing heavy losses.

As seen on the monthly chart, XRP has declined below the monthly 50 MA, a key level that supported its 500% rise in November 2024.

In a recent development, however, all hope is not lost as the token’s daily relative strength index has dropped below 30, a level generally considered oversold. That can accompany a short-term rebound, but it does not fully establish that the decline has ended.

In a recent discussion on CNBC, Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse expressed hope for a crypto rebound.

Garlinghouse stated that the recent crypto sell-off reflects investors shifting funds into high-profile IPOs such as Anthropic. He expects to see the crypto markets bounce back after the listing, pointing to a similar rally after SpaceX’s IPO. If this scenario plays out, XRP will look to return to $1 and then $1.2. In contrast, support remains at $0.77 if the ongoing sell-off continues.
