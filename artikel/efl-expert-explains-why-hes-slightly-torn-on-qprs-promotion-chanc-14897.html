In a recent development, QPR occupy the final place in the Championship play-offs at the moment after their first 15 fixtures. They are in the top eight along with Stoke City, Wolves, West Brom, Swansea City and Southampton, with the likes of West Ham, Sheffield United and Portsmouth chasing them down.

Recent findings suggest that the Hoops won 2-1 at home against Lincoln City last time out before the international break after goals by Richard Kone and Glen Kamara. They are back in action this weekend against Millwall away.

Industry observers note that in the meantime, Maxwell has delivered his verdict on the London outfit on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “I’m slightly torn on whether they are contenders with caveats or play-off outsiders. They are presently 8th in the table so into that last play-off spot in the expanded play-offs this season. They’ve got great numbers QPR, great underlyings (numbers).”.

He added: “They’ve only lost to good teams which is a good sign I think, in a weird way. They’ve lost to Boro, Southampton, Birmingham, basically three of the best teams in the league. Outside of that they’ve had some really eye-catching wins. 5-0 against Swansea City, good away wins at Portsmouth and Blackburn. They thumped Derby at home as well. What I would say is they have played five of the bottom seven already at home.”.

QPR are winless in their last five matches on the road. They have lost their last two on their travels 3-0 and 4-1 to Birmingham City and Southampton respectively.

Recent findings suggest that therefore, the Hoops need to improve in their away games if they are to maintain their push for promotion. They have been too leaky recently and have to tighten things up at the back.

Sources indicate that julien Stephan‘s side have been solid at home though and are tough to play on their own patch. They have lost only once there this term and that was to 2nd place Middlesbrough 1-0.

Industry observers note that QPR will have the chance to freshen up their squad with some reinforcements in the January transfer window if needed. They will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door as well this winter to help free up space and funds for potential reinforcements.

It has been revealed that QPR finally have some stability under Stephan. They turned to the Frenchman in 2025 and he has since steadied the ship.

Prior to his switch to England, he had spells in his native France at Rennes and Strasbourg. He arrived as a bit of an unknown quantity but has since become a popular figure.

Industry observers note that stephan was handed a contract extension earlier this year which indicates he is highly rated by the club’s hierarchy. He will be eager for his team to pick up a positive result at The Den against Millwall on Saturday.

QPR have dangerous players at the top end of the pitch who will look to hurt the Lions. Kone will be in confident mood after he scored against Lincoln City, whilst the likes of Harvey Vale, Koki Saito and Kwame Poku, among others, are solid operators at second tier level.
