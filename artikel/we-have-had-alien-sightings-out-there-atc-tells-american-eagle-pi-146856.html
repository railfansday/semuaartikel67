An American Eagle pilot asked air traffic control about an unexplained traffic advisory near Baltimore and received an answer that probably was not what he was expecting: there had been “alien sightings” in the area. The exchange makes for entertaining listening, though the actual report is considerably less dramatic.

A recording circulating online captures an unusual conversation reportedly involving American Eagle flight 5432 from Washington National (DCA) to Philadelphia (PHL) on November 21, 2026.

According to International Business Times, PSA Airlines operated the flight with a Bombardier CRJ-900, using the callsign Blue Streak 5432. Near Baltimore, the pilot asked about a traffic advisory without an identification tag. The controller explained that the flight had already passed the only other known traffic nearby.

Then came the unexpected observation:.

“I mean, we have had alien sightings out there, so…”.

The controller went on to describe an earlier report from another pilot about something zigzagging ahead of an aircraft near Martin State Airport, without a corresponding radar target.

The American Eagle pilot took the comment in good humor, suggesting it would make an interesting story to tell his family.

Before you start booking a window seat from BWI specifically to look for visitors from another planet, the American Eagle crew was asking about a traffic advisory… these pilots did not actually see an unidentified object outside their aircraft.

The zigzagging object was part of the controller’s recollection of a different pilot’s report: there’s quite a leap from an unexplained alert and a controller’s remark to an extraterrestrial encounter.

Yet after so much grim aviation news lately, I enjoyed this one.

According to recent reports, pilots and controllers have serious responsibilities, but there is room for a little personality on the frequency when circumstances permit. It is often the testy verbal exchanges between pilots and air traffic controllers that make headlines, but a passing remark like this also gives us a glimpse of the human beings doing the work.

An American Eagle pilot’s question about a traffic advisory prompted an unexpected reference to earlier alien sightings near Baltimore. While there was no alien spacecraft sighting, it is an entertaining radio exchange and a rather good story.
