📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that what do you do when all hope seems lost, and you're the only one left? Well, clearly you go all out in your tracked war machine and hope to take the enemy down before they do the same to you! And that fast-paced, heavy metal experience is precisely what top-down tank sim Ironclad: Battle Tank promises to provide.

Industry observers note that ironclad: Battle Tank is as straightforward an experience as you can get, and about as subtle about it as, well, a tank. You play as the last lone machine in the fight against the Iron Fleet. Dropped behind enemy lines, each run pits you against bosses in your fight to reach the Citadel and the enormous Dominator that guards it to try and end the war once and for all.

Ironclad: Battle Tank is a refreshingly simple concept for a mobile game, and one that really feels like a throwback to the days of arcade vehicle sims like the Desert Strike series.

It has been revealed that right now in closed alpha, Ironclad: Battle Tank is expected to land on Android sometime in 2027, with no current plans in place for iOS. But watch this space, because if Ironclad turns out to be a rock-solid (metal-solid?) take on the genre, we'll be sure to inform you when it hits iPhone!
