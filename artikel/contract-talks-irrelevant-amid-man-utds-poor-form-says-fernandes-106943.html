Bruno Fernandes insisted his focus remained on turning Manchester United's fortunes around rather than putting pen to paper on a upcoming contract at Old Trafford.

Fernandes, who returns to Sporting CP with United in the Champions League on Wednesday, has been linked with a move away from the Red Devils over the last few years.

The 32-year-old was the subject of interest from the Saudi Pro League, while it has been reported he has turned down two offers to extend his stay with United.

Fernandes has a deal at Old Trafford until the end of the season, although the club hold an option to extend it through to June 2028, but the United captain's priorities lie on the pitch.

United were thumped 4-0 by rivals Liverpool in Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge, a result that leaves them 19th in the Premier League after 11 matches of the campaign.

Indeed, United's eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since 1972-73 (seven). That is also the previous season in which the Red Devils only managed one win in their first 11 league matches.

"For us to start winning games, and then we can start speaking about a contract," Fernandes told reporters when asked about his future. "That's not important now, it's irrelevant.

"Obviously, I know it's my future, and I probably shouldn't look at it that way because I haven't got much left in my contract, but I'm not really worried about that.

"I'm more worried about getting results, trying to bring the club back into a position that we can be proud of, and we're not there at the moment.

"We're going to have time to speak about that. As I remarked, we had conversations.

"The conversations stopped at the moment that we thought that was not as important at the moment because results and performances were not at the level we should be.".

Fernandes joined United from Sporting in January 2020 and has become a crucial part of the Red Devils' midfield, but only has an FA Cup and EFL Cup to show for his efforts.

In fact, since moving to England, Fernandes has only played in one Champions League knockout tie, coming against Atletico Madrid back in 2022.

"As a player, you want to play the biggest tournaments, the biggest games," Fernandes added. "Sometimes it's not possible.

"When I started my career, I never had the opportunity in the first few years to play in the Champions League, so for me that was a dream far away, and I'm very proud of what I have achieved, even if I have only played one time [in the knockouts].

"I still have got time, I still have got many years left in my career, I hope so, and I'm going to have many opportunities to do that, and why not this season?".

With his penalty against Roma last time out, Fernandes netted his 24th goal in major European competition for United, moving ahead of Cristiano Ronaldo into seventh spot on the club's all-time scoring list, and he will be hoping to have another impact at the Estadio Jose Alvalade.

Sources indicate that however, United are winless in their last six Champions League away games (D2 L4), losing both this season. They last had a longer winless run away from home in the competition between February 2004 and December 2005 (eight matches – D3 L5).
