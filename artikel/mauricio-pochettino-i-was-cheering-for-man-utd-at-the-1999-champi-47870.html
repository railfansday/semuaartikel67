Mauricio Pochettino has recalled supporting Manchester United at the 1999 Champions League final from the stands as the club’s new head coach challenged his players to rediscover their winning mentality.

The Argentinian arrives with United in the bottom three of the Premier League but he insists the focus must be on the club’s potential rather than their current predicament.

Pochettino, who quit as United States national team boss to succeed the sacked Michael Carrick, said the opportunity at Old Trafford was something “you cannot turn down”.

He said his admiration of United dated back to the club’s famous European triumph against Bayern Munich at the Camp Nou, where injury-time goals from Teddy Sheringham and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer saw the Red Devils produce one of the all-time comeback wins.

Sources indicate that “I was in Barcelona in that final, that was an iconic final,” he remarked in his first interview as manager. “We were shouting for Manchester United.

“From there, it was so exciting to see Manchester United, and more with the admiration of Sir Alex Ferguson – for me, the best coach in the history of the sport. I always admired him.”.

Pochettino will take charge of his first game as United travel to arch-rivals Liverpool on Sunday.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager told the club’s media channels: “I think it’s a mistake to see the situation. We need to see the potential of this club.

“Of course, circumstances are circumstances and we need to be responsible for the circumstances but, at the same time, we need to understand that it’s a game.

Industry observers note that “To connect with the game, we need to feel free, the players need to express their talent. We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform.

“The most key thing for now is to start to work hard to recover that feeling about being a winner – to win games, to perform.”.

United have won just once in 10 Premier League games this season with the team having been criticised in a number of areas.

The club have been linked with a number of potential signings ahead of the January transfer window but Pochettino insists the current squad will have his full backing.

He said: “We need to love the players that we have. We need to support the players, we need to provide all the tools for them to perform.

“It’s an act of responsibility now to start to work and provide them the possibility to translate all their talent on the field.”.

Pochettino recognises he faces a baptism of fire at Anfield this weekend.

He said: “I know very well what that game means and how important it is. The history talks for us and to what we need to expect.

“We need to make sure that we are brave and we are protagonists and go there with no fears and challenge a very good team like Liverpool.”.
