All those are valid ways to go, but our data demonstrates us there’s one particular item the Esquire reader is purchasing again and again whenever it’s on sale. It’s that time of year again—all hail the pocketknife sale.

You never know when you’ll need a blade to open a package (we do a lot) or when you’ll need tweezers. Again, we’re always wishing we had tweezers and a little pair of scissors around the house/office.
