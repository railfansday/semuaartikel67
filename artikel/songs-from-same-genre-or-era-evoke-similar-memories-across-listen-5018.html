Songs from same genre or era evoke similar memories across listeners

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The first few notes of an electric guitar can send memories from your teenage years rushing back. These memories often feel intensely personal. But a newly introduced study in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences finds that the memories evoked by songs are surprisingly similar from person to person and vary with a song's genre and decade.
