Kevin De Bruyne insisted his affection for Manchester City will never change, even after they were found guilty of breaking Premier League financial rules, and remarked he loves their owners.

The former City captain, who returns to the Etihad Stadium on Tuesday when Napoli visit in the Champions League, believes that even if his old club are stripped of some of their titles as punishment for their wrongdoing, it will not alter what he and his teammates achieved on the pitch.

De Bruyne, who made 422 appearances for City between 2015 and 2025, suggested others may be “disgraced” by what has happened off the field but the players are not, but stated he still has a close relationship with chairman Khaldoon Al Mubarak.

Industry observers note that he noted: "My feelings have never been different. Honestly, the way that I feel is just the way that I've been treated by this club, what I've done with this club for me personally, and how we've worked together as a group.

It has been revealed that “Of course, it's a lot of things that's been going on, but I cannot change anything about that. I’m proud of what I've done as a football player, and honestly, this is my club, I love the owners, I've talked about a lot of stuff outside football, I've even spoken to Khaldoon about how to buy a club and everything, so there's a lot of things that go around after this.

“So I have nothing but affection for this club, and that will never change.”.

De Bruyne won the Premier League six times and 19 trophies in all, but he joined during an era when City were deemed to have benefited from £900m in disguised funding in sponsorship deals.

But he stated: “It still doesn't change, my football has not changed. Of course, in life there are rules, but everybody plays on the limit, I would say, and for me, I played in between the white lines, and I competed with other players, and I had many experiences with my teams, with the players.

In a recent development, “So for me that's the thing that I value most, and even later, I think in my career, the most notable thing is the relationships between everybody that I had and how you manage these things. I've given my all, and that's not going to change in my mind.

“I don't think a lot of people will change in their mind how they view me as a player, or how we played, but everybody has his own opinion, I guess, and other people may be disgraced a bit, but I can't change anything about that.”.
