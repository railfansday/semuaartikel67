HX Expeditions and the University of Tasmania (UTAS) will launch a redesigned Antarctica Comprehensive Course for this Antarctic season, giving all travelers and polar enthusiasts a more flexible, engaging and personalized way to deepen their understanding of the White Continent.

Recent findings suggest that the program is open to all travelers worldwide, not just HX guests. The course allows learners to explore Antarctica through the subjects that interest them most.

Dr. Verena Meraldi, chief scientist for HX, noted: ” By redesigning our comprehensive Antarctica course into flexible, bite-sized modules, we’re creating a more personalized learning experience that allows people to explore the topics they’re most passionate about, while continuing to build knowledge and connection with one of the world’s most extraordinary destinations.”.

The company mentioned in a press release that the redesigned course responds to growing demand for flexible, self-directed learning, enabling guests to tailor their experience around wildlife, science, history, conservation and culture.

It builds on HX’s industry-leading approach to combining expedition travel with accredited educational and science opportunities and further strengthens its long-standing partnership with the university.

Prof Vanessa Lucieer, acting interim director and head of center of Ecology and Biodiversity, Institute for Marine and Antarctic Studies (IMAS), University of Tasmania, mentioned: “The redesigned course creates exciting fresh opportunities to bring UTAS polar research to a global audience.”.

“Building on a partnership that has already attracted more than 4,000 enrolments across three destinations, this upcoming format will extend our reach even further, making Antarctic science more accessible to a much wider community,” added Lucieer.

The fresh course transforms the previous 20-hour program into 11 bite-sized, stackable modules, allowing participants to choose topics that align with their individual interests and learning goals.

It also gives learners the freedom to “pick and mix” subjects rather than completing a single linear program, the firm added.

The HX-exclusive Antarctica Introductory Course, launched by HX in 2024 and updated for the 2026-27 season, still remains free to all guests on HX Antarctic voyages.

It provides the first stage of HX guests’ learning journey before they move on to the upcoming comprehensive course.

The updated course will be delivered in English at launch, with additional language options under consideration.

The course opens for enrolment this week from November 12, 2026.
