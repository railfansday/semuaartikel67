Brad Reese isn't sugarcoating a recent family revelation.

It has been revealed that nine months after the grandson of Reese's Peanut Butter Cups inventor H.B. Reese accused The Hershey Manufacturer of "quietly replacing" the candy's signature chocolate and peanut with different ingredients, he revealed how dad Charles Richard "Dick" Reese's biggest regret led to his newly introduced mission.

According to recent reports, "'I don't wanna die because I don't wanna go to heaven,'" Brad revealed Dick told him before his 2005 death in an interview with The Daily Mail published Nov. 22. His dad added that he was afraid to reunite with H.B. because he "double-crossed him" by selling the organization outside the family.

Though Brad—who noted that he still holds shares in Hershey—has no formal role in, or ownership of, Reese's since his dad and five uncles sold the rights to produce the candy to the company in 1963, no amount of sweet-talking would keep him from advocating for the peanut butter cups' legacy.

"He made me promise if he gave me my shares I would never sell them," he mentioned of their conversation. "There's no amount of money that would send me packing. I'm not doing this for money, and I'm not doing this for a position on the board.".

Brad's decision to unwrap his feud with Hershey—which went on to introduce other products like Reese's Pieces in 1978 and Reese's Puffs in 1994—turned a deeply personal situation public when he criticized the company online earlier this year.

Recent findings suggest that "I'm doing it because I believe in it, that's the most important thing," he clarified. "There's nothing that the Hershey company can do to financially hurt me.".

In a recent development, the U.S. Army veteran couldn't resist publicly criticizing the major chocolate manufacturer, alleging in a February LinkedIn post that Hershey replaced "milk chocolate with compound coatings and peanut butter with peanut-butter-style crèmes across multiple Reese's products" in recent years.

According to recent reports, "Right now, the Reese's story is diverging from what's inside Reese's products," he continued. "I'm not asking for nostalgia. I'm asking for alignment. For truth in Reese's brand stewardship. For a corporate narrative from The Hershey Firm that reflects the Reese's product consumers are actually receiving.".

Recent findings suggest that hershey disputed Brad's claims, telling E! News in a statement at the time that the peanut butter cups are still "made the same way they always have been, starting with roasting fresh peanuts to make our unique, one-of-a-kind peanut butter that is then combined with milk chocolate.".

However, the company noted that other Reese's products may have different ingredients.

"As we've grown and expanded the Reese's product line, we make product recipe adjustments that allow us to make newly introduced shapes, sizes and innovations that Reese's fans have come to love and ask for," Hershey continued, "while always protecting the essence of what makes Reese's unique and special: the perfect combination of chocolate and peanut butter.".
