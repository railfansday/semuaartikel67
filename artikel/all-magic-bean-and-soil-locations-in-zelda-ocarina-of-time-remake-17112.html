📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that magic Beans in the Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake are used to grow beanstalks in soft soil around the game, and in so doing result in beanstalks in the future once you've travelled through time and become an adult. Magic beans in OoT basically serve as a way to prepare access to locations that you wouldn't be able to get to otherwise, at least in the future. Below I'll lay out how to get Magic Beans, how many you'll actually need, and all the soft soil locations so you can plant them in a single run.

There are 10 soft soil spots in Zelda Ocarina of Time you can plant in, so you should buy 10 magic beans if you want to use them all.

It has been revealed that you can buy Magic Beans only from the vendor halfway along Zora River, once you've unlocked the Bombs after completing the Dodongo Cavern. He sells them to you for 10 Rupees, then increases the price by another 10 each time you buy them. He will automatically stop selling them once you reach 10.

Below is a list of all 10 soft soil locations in OoT where you can plant magic beans for beanstalks in the future (you cannot plant beans when Link is an adult). Keep in mind that you can also use bottled bugs to lure Gold Skulltulas out of the dirt too.

Once planted, beans eventually turn into plants in the future era that carry Link along predetermined routes, little hovering platforms. These allow you to reach previously inaccessible rewards and locations.

Recent findings suggest that at this point in the game, have you made sure you have the Zelda Ocarina of Time Golden Scale? Or if you want some even bigger upgrades, make sure you have all the Zelda Ocarina of Time Great Fairy Fountains you can get so far!
