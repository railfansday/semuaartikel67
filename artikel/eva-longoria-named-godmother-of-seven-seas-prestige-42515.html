Regent Seven Seas Cruises has declared Eva Longoria as the godmother of the Seven Seas Prestige, the first ship in the firm’s fresh Prestige class.

Longoria, who is an actress, producer, director, entrepreneur and activist, will officially christen the ship during a ceremony in Los Angeles, California, in February 2027, when its inaugural season will be underway.

Before that, the ship is set to depart on its maiden voyage from Miami, Florida on December 28, 2026, an 11-night roundtrip Caribbean cruise visiting destinations in Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras, Jamaica and the Cayman Islands.

The announcement follows a collaboration between Longoria tied to the upcoming second season of her travel and culinary series, Searching for France, as part of which she recently filmed an exclusive feature aboard the Seven Seas Grandeur.

“Eva Longoria embodies many of the qualities that define Seven Seas Prestige, from her sophisticated global outlook and passion for discovery to her ability to create meaningful connections through storytelling,” mentioned Wesley D’Silva, president of Regent Seven Seas Cruises.

“As we prepare to welcome Seven Seas Prestige and usher in a upcoming legacy for ultra-luxury, all-inclusive cruising, we are honored to have Eva serve as Godmother. Her influence, creativity and commitment to making a positive impact make her an extraordinary ambassador for this historic moment in Regent’s journey.”.

A video message from Longoria can be viewed here.

“It is an incredible honor to be named Godmother of Seven Seas Prestige. Travel has always been one of the most enriching ways to experience the world, bringing people, cultures and perspectives together in meaningful ways,” she noted.

In recognition of Longoria’s philanthropic work and longstanding commitment to empowering communities, Regent Seven Seas Cruises will be making a donation to Eva’s Heroes, an organization working to enrich the lives of individuals with intellectual needs.

Following the announcement that the ship would be led by Captain Luksa Kristovic in August, the cruise line recently shared additional details about the wellness options onboard, beverage concepts and entertainment offering.
