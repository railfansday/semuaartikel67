Fnatic have signed Czech duelist Martin 'marteen' Pátek from Gentle Mates as their first newly introduced player for VCT EMEA 2027. The London club are rebuilding after missing Champions for the first time in the team's history.

The London club’s newly introduced duelist is a player who hurt them badly in 2026. Colin ‘CoJo’ Johnson is listed as manager of the rebuilt roster.

It has been revealed that the move still has one step left. Pley.gg listed the signing as still awaiting approval from Riot Games. The link is not upcoming. Word of a verbal agreement between marteen and Fnatic emerged on 24 September. That was about a month before Gentle Mates released their whole roster.

It has been revealed that fnatic have already seen what the duelist can do. Gentle Mates came out on top when the two sides met at the 2026 EMEA Kickoff. That win sent Gentle Mates to Masters Santiago.

Sources indicate that he led several stat categories in Santiago. Gentle Mates still went out 1–2 to AG and finished joint seventh to eighth. That result earned the team $35,000.

Industry observers note that the rest of Gentle Mates’ year was quieter. The team failed to qualify for Masters London or Champions Shanghai. Marteen still finished VCT EMEA Stage 2 as the team’s top-rated player, with a rating of 1.23. Signing a player who stood out in a non-qualifying side suggests Fnatic are building around individual firepower.

Fnatic’s own 2026 campaign ended earlier than planned. Joblife knocked them out with a 2–0 win in the VCT EMEA Stage 2 Play-Ins.

Recent findings suggest that the signing follows a clear-out of long-serving players. Boaster had been with Fnatic since 3 February 2021. Alfajer arrived on 9 May 2022, and crashies on 11 December 2024.

In a recent development, marteen’s own spell at Gentle Mates ran from 20 November 2025 to 25 October 2026. The two rosters are now tied together by the same transfer window.

In a recent development, gentle Mates had already cleared their own decks. All five players on the active roster and both coaches were let go before the club’s first season as a VCT partner team.

For a British organisation, the swap leaves Fnatic’s identity in newly introduced hands. Boaster had been the longest-serving name in the departing group. Kaajak and Cloud are now the continuity, with marteen as the headline addition.

According to recent reports, fnatic do not need to qualify for the opening event. The club is one of eight EMEA partner teams for 2027, and each partner goes straight into Kickoff. Places at later events must be earned. Four teams from Open Qualifiers complete the field.

The first chance to see both rebuilt sides comes soon. The 2027 EMEA Kickoff, featuring 12 teams, runs from 14 January to 7 February 2027 at the Riot Games Arena in Berlin. Its top three teams will qualify for Masters Seoul, the first international event of the 2027 season.
