Final Fantasy Resonance producer Keisuke Nakashima has praised The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake as a great lesson in remakes.

Ignoring that it's from 1998, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is the highest-rated game of 2026, and will likely hold on to that title until at least this Thursday when GTA 6 releases (maybe even longer).

It has been revealed that but while a remake of one of the most beloved games ever is unsurprisingly a hit with players, other developers are coming out in force to praise the game too. Nier: Automata mastermind Yoko Taro has spoken about buying the remake because the original was such an influence on the Nier series. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick, meanwhile, calls it a "terrific" achievement Nintendo should be proud of, even if some do call it a rival to GTA 6. And now, another developer behind a popular 2026 release has chimed in.

In a recent development, final Fantasy Resonance producer Keisuke Nakashima has now finished the Zelda remake and has taken to Twitter to share his thoughts (via machine translation). Nakashima calls the remake amazing, adding that it's a game he has played a ton since its original release on the N64. He then calls it a fantastic learning experience for remakes.

Square Enix's Final Fantasy Resonance – which broke the Steam record for the biggest player peak for a JRPG – has proved a hit with series fans. And while it's not a remake, it technically does remake some of the mobile title Final Fantasy: Brave Exvius. But, that game's status as a remake aside, it does look like Zelda is going to be a benchmark in remakes the same way 2002's Resident Evil and 2019's Resident Evil 2 have become.
