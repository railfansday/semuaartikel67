Here’s your guide to following the Percy Jackson season 3 episode release schedule.

Season 3’s are drawn primarily from The Titan’s Curse, the third book in the series. You can basically figure out what’s going to happen in the season and in each episode.

Two new episodes will drop weekly on Disney+ every Friday.

In a recent development, typically, the episodes drop on the platform at midnight... if you live in California. Figure out what 12 a.m. PT is in your time zone, and you’re good to go!
