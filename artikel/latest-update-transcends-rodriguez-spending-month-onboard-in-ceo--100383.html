Transcend Cruises has launched its inaugural CEO-in-Residence Program, where its CEO Edie Rodriguez will spend a month sailing the Rhine onboard the Transcend Connect.

As a newly appointed CEO and board member, Rodriguez will run daily executive operations and leadership meetings from the ship between November 23 and December 22, 2026.

In what is noted to be the first time a cruise line CEO has spent an entire month onboard on either a river or cruise vessel, Rodriguez will meet with the onboard crew, charter clients and their guests and partners to help evolve Transcend’s brand strategy and product offering.

Corporate charters, MICE planners, travel advisors and affinity groups will also be able to connect and discuss customized itineraries and onboard experiences.

“Transcend Cruises was created to reimagine how groups come together on the rivers of Europe, turning every vessel into a customizable floating venue,” stated Rodriguez.

“Setting up my office onboard for a month allows me stay close to our crew and operations and collaborate directly with clients and partners, and to experience the journeys just as they do and to better understand areas where we can improve as we continue to grow.”.

As the first charter-only river cruise tech giant with ships built exclusively for the group, Transcend welcomed its first ship, the Connect this summer, embarking on its first voyage on August 3, 2026.

Two additional ships are right now under construction, with plans for the fleet to reach four in 2028 and ultimately grow to 12 ships.
