📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Book with Expedia when you’re putting a flight and hotel on one trip or staying where no hotel chain rewards you, such as an independent hotel or a vacation rental. At a chain hotel, check the chain’s own site first, since booking direct earns its points and Expedia’s rewards start at just 1% back.

Expedia sells flights, hotels, vacation rentals, rental cars, cruises, and activities, and it puts a flight, hotel, and car on one booking for travelers planning a whole trip.

Sources indicate that expedia suits travelers who book a flight and a stay together, or who stay at independent hotels and vacation rentals, which earn no chain points anyway. Anyone loyal to one hotel chain does better booking direct, where the stay earns points and elite night credit.
