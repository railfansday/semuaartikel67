Manchester United are very close to appointing their upcoming manager

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that the international break should be an opportunity for Manchester United to launch a latest cycle. Indeed, all the English media are unanimous in saying that Michael Carrick is condemned, and that his dismissal will be announced in the coming days. It must be stated that the Red Devils are in a sad eighteenth position in the Premier League standings, and that they have only won three of the fifteen matches played so far in all competitions.

According to recent reports, A meeting will take place in the coming hours between key Mancunian decision-makers to discuss Carrick’s future, but his fate appears sealed. We will therefore have to find a replacement for him, and many names have emerged in recent days across the Channel. Julian Nagelsmann, Antonio Conte and Eddie Howe were thus presented as serious candidates for the position. There was even talk of former French coach Didier Deschamps.

In a recent development, but everything indicates that it is Mauricio Pochettino who will sit on the Manchester United bench at Anfield, for the return match at Liverpool. A hell of a poster and a major baptism of fire for the North American coach in the process. The Argentinian’s name is clearly the one that has come up the most in recent hours, and The Athletic has even announced that the two parties are finalizing a deal for the former PSG to arrive at Old Trafford. The agreement was reached with the American coach, but it would also be necessary to compensate the American Federation, with whom he is under contract until 2030.

Industry observers note that manchester United will thus have to pay around 9 million euros to the Americans, and Pochettino should arrive in Manchester with his staff, again according to information from The Athletic. The former Argentinian international, who made an impression during his adventures at Southampton and Tottenham between 2013 and 2019 before trying his luck at Chelsea (2023/2024), has good odds across the Channel and really wanted to take charge of Manchester United for a while now. A mutual interest, since the Mancunians have often thought of him in recent years when it was necessary to find a coach. One thing is certain, he will face a hell of a job on the Carrington side….
