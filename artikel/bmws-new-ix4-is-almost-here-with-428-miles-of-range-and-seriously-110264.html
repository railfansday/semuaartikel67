It has been revealed that BMW’s upcoming iX4 is almost here, and it could make range anxiety a thing of the past. With 428 miles of EPA range, more than 500 miles on the WLTP cycle, and ultra-fast charging, the new EV could make road trips a whole lot easier.

Series production of the latest iX4 is officially underway at BMW Group’s manufacturing plant in Debrecen, Hungary.

The iX4 is the second fully electric BMW model built at the facility, following the record-breaking production ramp-up of the new iX3 SUV.

BMW noted the plant began series production of an entirely upcoming model in less than two months after the manufacturer introduced a 24-hour, three-shift schedule to ramp up output to meet higher-than-expected demand.

The fresh iX4 will go on sale in March 2027 as an all-electric replacement for the X4. It will be available in two variants: the BMW iX4 50 xDrive and BMW iX4 40 xDrive, with a range-topping M60 xDrive set to follow shortly after.

Industry observers note that both versions use a dual-motor all-wheel-drive (AWD) powertrain. The iX4 50 xDrive provides a combined 468 hp and 475 lb-ft of torque, enabling it to sprint from 0 to 60 mph in 4.7 seconds.

The performance M60 version delivers a combined 603 hp and 645 lb-ft of torque, which BMW says is good for a 0 to 60 mph sprint in just 3.9 seconds.

Industry observers note that the iX4 features a distinct coupe-SUV-like silhouette rather than the traditional boxy, upright SUV design of the iX3. With a rakish roofline and an aerodynamic design, the fresh iX4 has a drag coefficient of just 0.23.

Like the iX3, the iX4 is based on BMW’s Neue Klasse architecture with its sixth-generation (Gen6) eDrive system.

While EPA figures have yet to be finalized, BMW remarked the new iX4 has an estimated driving range of up to 428 miles on a single charge using a 112.2 kWh battery. Under WLTP conditions, it offers an all-electric range of up to 514 miles (828 km).

Thanks to its 800V architecture, BMW’s upcoming iX4 can add up to 184 miles of range (384 km on the WLTP cycle) in just 10 minutes with charging speeds of up to 400 kW. Charging the vehicle from 10% to 80% takes about 21 minutes.

Like BMW’s other Neue Klasse models, the new iX4 features its updated interior design. The setup includes a large 17.9″ infotainment screen and BMW’s new Panoramic Vision, a 42″ display spanning the base of the windshield.

According to recent reports, the iX4 is 188.3″ long, 74.6″ wide, and 63.3″ tall, with a 114.1″ wheelbase, which is about the same size as the iX3. However, it’s slightly lower than the iX3 thanks to the sloped roofline.

Like all Gen6 BMW models, the latest iX4 supports bidirectional charging, enabling it to be used as a mobile power source for e-bikes, camping equipment, or other electrical devices.

The newly introduced BMW iX4 50 xDrive will go on sale in March 2027, starting at $66,300, or $4,800 more than the iX3 50 xDrive. That doesn’t include the $1,495 destination fee.

In Germany, the new BMW iX4 40 xDrive starts at €70,900 ($77,500), and in the UK it’s priced from £53,250 ($69,500), OTR.
