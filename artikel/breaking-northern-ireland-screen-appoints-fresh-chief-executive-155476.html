In a recent development, northern Ireland Screen has appointed its new Chief Executive. Laura Livingstone will take on the role in January.

Livingstone will take over from current CEO Richard Williams, who unveiled in June that he would step down from the role in December after over 23 years in the position.

Sources indicate that the exec has two decades of experience across film, TV, VFX and creative technology. Formerly a Netflix VFX executive, she is currently a VFX producer on an Amazon MGM Studios series. The Armagh native previously co-founded Ntropic London and advised on the business case for Studio Ulster. Northern Ireland has attracted a number of leading film and TV series over the years, including Derry Girls, Blue Lights, The Fall, Line of Duty and Game of Thrones, and such as The Death Of Robin Hood, How To Train Your Dragon and Dungeons and Dragons and Star Wars. Watch on Deadline.

Speaking about her upcoming role at Northern Ireland’s lead screen agency, Livingstone remarked: “I am delighted and honoured to have been appointed CEO of Northern Ireland Screen. Northern Ireland has an extraordinary wealth of creative talent and has established an international reputation for excellence across the screen industries. That success is a testament to the work of Richard Williams, the Northern Ireland Screen team and the wider industry.

She continued: “Having worked internationally for many years, I am incredibly excited to bring that experience home. I look forward to working with the team and our partners to build on these strong foundations, champion our homegrown talent, embrace latest opportunities and ensure Northern Ireland continues to thrive as a place where great stories are created and told.”.

Sir David Sterling, Chair of Northern Ireland Screen, added: “On behalf of Northern Ireland Screen, I warmly welcome Laura as our latest Chief Executive. Laura brings with her a wealth of experience in the screen industry and her expertise will guide Northern Ireland Screen into its next chapter. I would also like to express our sincere gratitude to our outgoing CEO, Richard Williams, for his exceptional leadership of Northern Ireland Screen and more than 23 years of dedicated service to the organisation.”.
