Goodbye to Kyle Richards' life before grandchildren.

The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills star and husband Mauricio Umansky became grandparents after her daughter Farrah Aldjufrie gave birth to her and boyfriend Zach Goldsmith's baby boy Brooks Steele Goldsmith.

"The birth was extremely easy, peaceful, seamless and happy," Farrah told People in an article published Nov. 20. "We had a party at the hospital and watched the sun come up after he was born. The transition has felt very natural. The house has been crowded with family giving so much love and support. We feel beyond lucky.”.

Days before the reality TV family—also including Kyle's younger daughters Alexia, 30, Sophia, 26 and Portia, 18, with Mauricio—shared the news, the Bravo personality shared her excitement about the upcoming addition.

"I’m on baby watch right now. I can’t believe [I’m gonna be a grandma]," Kyle confessed to Page Six on a Nov. 14 red carpet, musing about her full glam, "I hope it’s not tonight, because am I gonna go to the hospital like this?".

In addition, the little one's great aunt Kathy Hilton joked at the same event that she hoped the 38-year-old heading to the hospital at the time wasn't a "false alarm" for the little one's arrival.

And for Kyle—who first became a mom when she had Farrah with ex-husband Guraish Aldjufrie before remarrying with the Agency founder in 1996—anticipation of the major milestone had been a long time coming.

"Our first BOY!!!!!" the 57-year-old replied to Farrah's pregnancy announcement on Instagram in August. "I can’t wait!!!".

That said, Zach (also dad to two boys with ex-wife Jennifer Hamilton-Goldsmith) dropped in some classic dad humor while sharing his excitement about the pregnancy announcement, which simultaneously hard-launched his whirlwind romance with Farrah.

In a recent development, "Comin in hot bringing mad boy energy to this girl heavy crew. It’s about to get hella messy up in here," he joked. "Pardon the interruption.".

As for how Zach, who sells homes alongside Farrah and her stepdad Mauricio at the Agency, fits into their family?

"Thumbs up for Zach, 100 percent," Mauricio told E! News. "We love him," adding that he'll be a "great father.".
