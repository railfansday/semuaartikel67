Jensen Huang reinstated as CEO of Nvidia, stock up 30%

Recent findings suggest that nvidia has revealed that disgraced CEO Jensen Huang has been reinstated to the role, effective immediately. The tech giant’s share price jumped more than 30% in response, with the rest of the market also making significant gains.

It mentioned that the board voted unanimously in favor of reinstatement despite its recent statement that it had no intention of doing so ….

It has been revealed that huang’s affair with a woman suspected to be a Chinese spy became the main headline news in the tech press after coming to light at the end of last month. The New York Times broke the story on October 29, citing it then as “a close relationship.” Nvidia initially denied that the relationship was anything out of the ordinary.

That changed on November 8 when Huang publicly admitted to the affair. Nvidia’s shares dropped 5%, and the company’s board of directors opened an independent investigation. Further details emerged over the following days. Things got even worse when the same paper reported that Huang had also had an affair with an Nvidia executive who reports to him.

That led to Huang being fired resigning on November 12.

He won’t make that mistake again!

Despite putting the company at enormous risk and initially lying to its board of directors, there were calls in some quarters for Huang to be reinstated. He responded by saying that he would be “honored” to return if asked to do so.

The board of directors, however, said that it had no intention of inviting him back.

Nvidia’s chair Harvey Jones said: “The board does not intend to reinstate him.”.

That changed today, with the organization making the announcement in a press release.

Nvidia today announced that effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA. In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.

Recent findings suggest that “The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers,” remarked Harvey C. Jones, Chair of the Board. “We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Manufacturer. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as NVIDIA’s Chief Financial Officer.” Harvey Jones will remain as Board chair.

It has been revealed that “I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” noted Jensen Huang. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as Chair, and to Colette Kress and our executive staff for leading NVIDIA during this period. I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can’t wait to get back to work.”.

As previously declared, in light of recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the Board formed a committee comprised solely of independent directors to investigate, and the committee retained independent counsel to assist it in that investigation. The Board reiterated that the committee’s investigation will proceed to conclusion. Mr. Huang fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it.

The company’s stock price jumped by more than 30% in response, with the rest of the market also up per Yahoo Finance.

Nvidia shares soared more than 30% Monday morning […] The S&P 500 (^GSPC) rose 4.5% following seven straight days of declines. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI) jumped more than 1,200 points to recover by 2.5% following its worst week since the COVID-related sell-off in 2020. The Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC) rallied 7%.
