Piers Morgan has accused Cole Palmer of wasting his talent and displaying a rotten attitude following his refusal to acknowledge Chelsea’s fans following Saturday’s defeat against Leeds United.

The Stamford Bridge faithful could hardly believe what they were witnessing as Daniel Farke’s side romped to a 4-1 win that represented their first victory in west London since 1999.

Palmer delivered another anonymous performance and has registered just one goal contribution in his last five matches, a penalty in a 2-0 win over Manchester United earlier this month.

To compound another lackluster display, the former Manchester City forward refused to clap the home fans and became involved in an altercation with teammate Joao Pedro before he trudged down the tunnel.

With Thomas Tuchel steadfastly refusing to find space for Palmer in his England squad, the 24-year-old’s career is in danger of fizzling out unless he knuckles down, according to Morgan.

He told Sports Uncensored: “I’m sorry, but he’s a massive d***head. The guy, on and off the pitch, is a d***head.

Industry observers note that ‘The way he refused to go over to the fans. The way he treats the fans in the street.

‘Everything about Palmer, the stories you hear in the restaurants, the way he’s behaved in the England camp and why Tuchel doesn’t pick him. He’s a guy with amazing ability who is pissing it away.’.

Manchester United legend Nicky Butt was a guest on the show and he too had some harsh words for a player who has just signed a new lucrative, long-term contract.
