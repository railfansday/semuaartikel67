Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto has never shied away from, well, anything really. Political corruption, gang violence, culture clashes, both the underworld and sleazy heights are well within Rockstar’s sights, both in their main campaigns and plethora of side content.

In a recent development, with Grand Theft Auto 6’s small-scale start, it can be easy to forget that, with various side missions here to remind us. One such mission without a name, one I’ll just be calling the Near Fatal Sex Scandal, sees a woman begging for help on the side of the road. Want to learn more, and even how to start it yourself? Let’s get straight to it then.

While driving down the US-70 post Chapter 2, you may hear a woman crying for help, clad in a bathrobe. If you stop to help her, she’ll explain that someone up by the house needs your help, and beg you to follow.

According to recent reports, follow her, and she’ll escort you through a very fancy southern style manor, before approaching a bedroom. She asks for your complete confidentiality before opening the door, revealing her husband, a right-wing politician, unresponsive in a sex swing.

Sources indicate that interact with the body to help her get him down, where she’ll realize he’s already dead. Quick on her feet, she demands you help dispose of the body, pointing you to the river just down the hill as the perfect spot. Pick up the corpse as she rushes you out to continue.

According to recent reports, as you carry the body down the hill, the politician himself will begin to stir, startling the player character, who drops him to the grass. Stunned and betrayed by his wife’s seeming attempt on his life, he’ll demand you back him up. Follow him back to the estate and allow him to interact with the bedroom door.

Recent findings suggest that his wife, again not missing a beat, immediately pivots back into their roleplay, belittling him and yanking her “bad little globalist” back into the bedroom. For your trouble (and trauma), you’re tossed a cool $10,000, an absolute game changer if found early enough in Chapter 2.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is back to its seams with all kinds of small encounters like this, perfect for a quick joke and a pocket full of cash as you pursue Jason and Lucia’s perfect life.
