It was less than four years ago that Harry Styles won the Grammy for album of the year, but he won’t get a chance to defend his title or win another Grammy this go around for his latest album Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally, as he was completely shut out of the 2027 Grammy nominations.

Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally was a bit more sonically complex than Styles’ past album of the year winner Harry’s House, and heading into this year, Styles was widely looked at as a Grammy contender for his latest release, which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 album chart while lead single “Aperture” hit No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100. Neither the album nor the single had the same staying power as Harry’s House or its lead single “As It Was,” though he gave the album more buzz with a 30-show Madison Square Garden residency as part of his Together, Together tour. Evidently, it wasn’t enough to sway the Grammy voters. It has been revealed that other surprise shutouts this morning include Gracie Abrams, who wasn’t nominated for her third album Daughter From Hell, which hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200, and Hilary Duff, whose comeback record Luck … Or Something, for new label Atlantic Records, was released in February and debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200. While Abrams scored a best new artist nomination in 2024, it’s not her first time getting the Grammys‘ cold shoulder, also failing to land expected song and record of the year nods last year for “That’s So True.”.

Sources indicate that others were lucky enough to score nominations but maybe not as many as pundits (or fans) had expected. Noah Kahan landed just one nomination, for best contemporary folk album, for his The Great Divide, despite a number of experts predicting the former best new artist nominee would score top nods for his latest effort.

“Life goes on,” Kahan tweeted on Monday.

And while Madonna‘s Confessions II was an eagerly awaited follow-up to 2005’s Confessions from the pop superstar and landed her four nominations, the release failed to net the seven-time Grammy winner any nominations in the major categories, including album, record and song of the year.

For anyone else, five Grammy nominations is an incredibly impressive feat, but even though that’s how many nods superstar singer-songwriter Taylor Swift received Monday — with noms in all three of the general categories — it’s perhaps an understated showing compared to what we’ve come to expect for the world’s biggest artist.

Speaking of superstar musicians, Paul McCartney was left out of the major categories for his latest release The Boys of Dungeon Lane, which only landed one nomination, for best traditional pop vocal album. But the former Beatle did land three nominations, two of which were for Morgan Neville’s Man on the Run documentary about McCartney’s departure from the iconic group.

Kanye “Ye” West continued his comeback with an unexpected Grammy nomination for best rap album for his latest release Bully, as the controversial rapper aims to move on from years of antisemitic comments.

Sources indicate that meanwhile, both Raye and Rosalía were looked at as contenders for some of the top prizes heading into announcement day, but few would’ve expected they’d be the most-nominated acts across the whole field. Raye’s 11 noms and Rosalía’s nine were some of the best surprises for two of the year’s most ambitious projects.

According to recent reports, other surprises include a handful of artists who landed nods despite not releasing an album during the most recent Grammys eligibility period. Beyoncé released one song, “Morning Dew (Donk),” on July 4, as part of the 20th anniversary edition of her 2006 album B’Day and netted her 100th Grammy nomination. Similarly, Sabrina Carpenter scored two nods, albeit both in the same category of best pop duo/group performance, for her collaborations with Swift and Madonna.
