Luciano Spalletti hailed Juventus for getting "back on track" after a tough start to their 4-1 Europa League win over Omonia Nicosia.

Omonia took a shock 15th-minute lead through Lubomir Satka, but amid growing frustrations among the crowd at Allianz Stadium, Juventus turned things around before the break.

Francisco Conceicao starred, setting up both Andrea Cambiaso's equaliser and Jeff Ekhator's goal in the first half before then netting Juve's third with 22 minutes left to play.

Jeremie Boga then rounded off the scoring five minutes later, with Juventus continuing their unbeaten start to the Europa League (W4 D1), which leaves them second in the table.

It was the second game in a row in all competitions that Juventus netted four goals, and Spalletti was thrilled with the response his side had to going behind against the run of play.

"Until the Cambiaso goal, it looked a lot like the Venezia game," Spalletti told Sky Sport Italia, via Football Italia.

"We were uncertain, and the opponents took advantage; we felt the pressure of a potential counter-attack, and everything started to feel complicated, but Cambiaso got us back on track with that wonderful goal, and from there the team played with greater calm.".

Ekhator (20 years and 15 days) is the youngest Italian player to score for Juventus in a major European competition since Niccolo Giannetti (19y 218d) against Manchester City on 16 December 2010, also in the Europa League.

"It's not easy to find form when called in, and some of these players hadn't started for a while, so lacking consistency is difficult," Spalletti said.

"However, we had some good moves, kept an acceptable level of pressing, and entertained the crowd with four fantastic goals.

"The Boga goal was my favourite because [Federico] Gatti won it back with that high press, and sometimes the players are a little hesitant to go that far forward, as it leaves gaps behind them. Gatti then ran to follow the move and was in a position to score himself.

"When you stop seeking something newly introduced, that is when you get pinned back. Ekhator didn't get enough service in the first half, because at times we consolidate possession and slow it down with too many touches, allowing the opponents to close up.
