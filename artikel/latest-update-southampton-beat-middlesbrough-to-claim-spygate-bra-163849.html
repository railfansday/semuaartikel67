Goals from Ryan Manning and Finn Azaz earned Southampton a 2-0 win against Middlesbrough, securing the bragging rights in the first match between the clubs since last season's Spygate controversy.

In a recent development, manning, making his 300th Championship appearance, broke the deadlock in the second half before former Boro forward Azaz punished a defensive error to double the lead. The result extended Saints’ unbeaten home run to 19 matches and moved them within four points of the league leaders.

The fixture carried significant context after Saints were removed from last term’s play-offs and hit with a four-point deduction this campaign for illegally observing training sessions carried out by Boro, Ipswich and Oxford.

Middlesbrough were restored to the play-offs as a consequence, though they ultimately missed out on Premier League promotion following a final defeat to Hull at Wembley.

While both managers sought to dismiss any lingering animosity beforehand, the contest carried clear competitive weight between two key promotion contenders.

Middlesbrough dominated the opening period, registering 11 attempts without finding a breakthrough. Leading scorer Will Lankshear squandered the best opportunities to add to his 15 league goals, firing straight at Daniel Peretz when put clean through in the 16th minute before directing a free header into the goalkeeper's midriff.

Callum Brittain also skimmed the crossbar with a cross, contributing to a frustrating run that saw Boro reach 49 away shots without scoring across their past three fixtures on the road.

Southampton had threatened earlier in the match, with Azaz striking the bar following a loose touch from Luke Ayling, while Radek Vitek produced impressive saves to deny Cyle Larin and Lewis Dobbin.

The home side eventually broke the deadlock after the break when James Bree lashed a low cross into the penalty area for Manning to divert into the net. The full-back celebrated by running directly to his manager.

Sources indicate that azaz, who moved to St Mary’s from Boro in the summer of 2025 following two seasons at the Riverside, sealed the victory in the 79th minute. Capitalising on a slip by Aidan Morris, the forward placed a cool finish into the bottom corner before running the length of the pitch to celebrate in front of his former fans while making a crying gesture.

The action sparked a late confrontation between the two sets of players, but Saints comfortably managed the remaining minutes to secure the points.
