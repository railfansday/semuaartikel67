As revealed by Memeio above, the Vice City portal Easter egg is a wildly complex multi-step time-travel secret with some very subtle clues, including "a sound effect from Vice City" when you power up the radio tower in Key Lento and individual letters that spell out a Vice City cheat code. The portal takes Jason back to a PS2-era version of the Malibu Club from the 2002 Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Completing it unlocks Tommy Vercetti's Hawaiian shirt and motorcycle.

It has been revealed that GTA 6 might not be coming to the Switch, but Rockstar at least demonstrates some love for Nintendo with its Mario Easter egg. Head north and seek out the ruined brick and stone building in the woods of southwest Moreau, Lummox County.

Using the bong on the blue-tarp crate in the middle of the ruins triggers a trip and spawns a translucent floating block that you can jump on or shoot to break, mimicking Mario breaking a Question Mark Block. The prize is a pair of Mario and Luigi-style hats marked "J" for Jason and "L" for Lucia.

According to recent reports, in the Gullet Marsh, Grassrivers and around Cottonmouth Lake, players have come across a piece of Florida folklore: Skunk Ape, the state's version of Bigfoot. A Weazel News report about sightings plays on the TV in Jason's house, and cryptid hunter Kirk offers a 'Search for the Skunk Ape' side mission available at night. Continuing a gag from Vice City, Rockstar has again included an actual Easter egg, but finding it isn't easy: you have to spend seven minutes just climbing the tallest radio tower in Vice-Dale Country for no other reason than to reach a bird's nest laden with eggs (and to enjoy a short cut scene).

According to recent reports, vice City is based on Miami, home to TV's favourite vigilante serial killer Dexter Morgan, and GTA 6 has several references to the show. You can find a dupe of his apartment in Trinity Apartments, Washington Island, near the Top Fun Drones shop at Vice Beach. Up on the roof, there's a toolbox, with a syringe, a knife, duct tape and rope.
