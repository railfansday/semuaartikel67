Enzo Maresca has dropped Enzo Fernandez as Manchester City host Fulham in Saturday’s lunchtime kick-off.

Since suffering their first loss of the season to Aston Villa, Man City have fallen to two further defeats at the hands of Brighton and Nottingham Forest.

With time to think over the international break, Maresca has decisions to make when it comes to his starting XI for the visit of Fulham to the Etihad Stadium this weekend.

Recent findings suggest that there were headaches in defence, where Matheus Nunes has missed out on the squad altogether. Abdukodir Khusanov will deputise as a full-back, with Marc Guehi and Ruben Dias starting in the middle.

Fernandez is definitely a favourite of Maresca, and while the Argentine travelled to South America to play two friendlies with his national side, he was rested for 35 minutes in the second game against Costa Rica in midweek.

Elliot Anderson received a similar amount of rest while away with England - coming off early during their 3-2 victory over Spain courtesy of a red card.

Fernandez drops to the bench here, though, with Bouaddi preferred as Anderson;s partner.

Phil Foden was dropped for the defeat by Forest, but is restored here as Rayan Cherki drops out.

Predicted Man City XI vs Fulham (4-2-3-1): Donnarumma; Khusanov, Dias, Guehi, O’Reilly; Bouaddi, Anderson; Ndiaye, Foden, Doku; Haaland.

Subs: Rulli, Kovacic, Cherki, Fernandez, Reis, Gvardiol, Allan, Samba, Lewis.

Time and date: 12.30pm GMT on Saturday November 21, 2026.
