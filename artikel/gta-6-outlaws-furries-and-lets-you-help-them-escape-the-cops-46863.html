GTA 6 outlaws furries and lets you help them escape the cops

GTA 6 makes being a furry illegal in the fictional state of Leonida, and one random encounter lets players help a fursuited character escape the police.

The ban comes up in an in-game TV ad paid for by the Anti-Furry Association. The ad warns tourists that hunting season is open and tells residents to report suspected furry activity, singling out expressions like “uwu,” “rawr xD,” and “hewwo.” The spot is part of a TV lineup that also includes a full cartoon series starring Macca the Gator.

Industry observers note that the ban also makes headlines inside the game. A screenshot shared by Cartoons On The Moon shows an article from the fictional Vice City Horn titled “Governor declares Leonida furry-free.” The account described the Furverts as a secret group formed in response to the ban.

GTA 6 has an Anti Furry Association??? pic.twitter.com/OEMgIP2Mud.

In a recent development, CNET reviewer David Lumb was driving around as Lucia at night when he spotted a police helicopter shining its light on a man in a fox suit. Lumb noted he broke through the police blockade to free him, then met up with the other furries and helped them escape to a compound.

GQ’s reviewer also came across a fursuited character under a helicopter spotlight and saw armed police take them down.

Industry observers note that korean gaming outlet Inven shared footage of the roadside standoff. Kinda Funny’s Andy Cortez had described a similar encounter back in August, when he heard a helicopter warn that a furry was on the loose during a private preview at Rockstar North.

The clips and screenshots have drawn mixed reactions. Some r/furry commenters read it as a joke about the panic around the fandom, while X user VenusMoonfang argued that it could encourage real-world hostility. Other fans on r/furry_irl wondered if they could buy a fursuit for themselves. A wearable fursuit for Jason or Lucia remains unconfirmed.
