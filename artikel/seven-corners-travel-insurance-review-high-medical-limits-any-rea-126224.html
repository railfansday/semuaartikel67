Sources indicate that get Seven Corners Trip Protection Choice for a trip abroad if you want high medical limits. It pays up to $500,000 for emergency care before your own insurance does, and up to $1 million for medical evacuation. Buy within 20 days of your first trip payment, or you’ll miss the cancel-for-any-reason upgrade and the pre-existing condition waiver.

Seven Corners’ Basic strategy covers $100,000 of medical care, paid only after your other insurance, and $250,000 of medical evacuation, against Choice’s $500,000 and $1 million. The gap matters most on trips abroad, where your own health plan or Medicare probably won’t pay.

Recent findings suggest that choice suits international travelers who want high medical limits and the option to cancel or cut a trip short for any reason. A domestic traveler protecting only a cheap flight should check the trip protection on their credit card before buying either strategy.
