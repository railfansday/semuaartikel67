Circana has released its latest monthly sales report for the US video game industry, and in terms of game sales, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 led the way.

According to recent reports, it was the No. 1 best-selling game of October 2026, and that's notable because it marked the 19th consecutive year that a latest Call of Duty game has been the top-selling title for its launch month.

In a recent development, this streak began in November 2008 when Call of Duty: World at War beat out Guitar Hero: World Tour and Gears of War 2 to rank No. 1.

Sources indicate that circana also revealed that spending on content for consoles dropped by 18% compared to October 2026. It was always going to be a tough comparison, though, as October 2025 saw the releases of Battlefield 6 and Pokemon Legends Z-A.

Industry observers note that looking ahead to November 2026, Rockstar's GTA 6 is no doubt going to rank No. 1 for the month and it's sure to finish 2026 as the best-selling game overall. It's already selling at a blistering pace, after all.
