Two Italians killed in Kuwait drone attack; Trump states Iran war ends ‘whenever I want it’

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Two Italians have been killed after an alleged Iranian attack on Kuwait, Kuwaiti forces remarked Thursday.

In a post on X, Kuwait's army stated the deaths followed Iranian attacks on the country, with the two Italians killed by falling missile debris. Two others were also injured.

Sources indicate that in response, a spokesperson for Iran's military told state media that no offensive or military operations had been carried out in the area and that "these accusations should be viewed as a cover for continued malevolence and an attempt to exacerbate insecurity in the region" by U.S. and Israeli forces.

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi stated on Telegram that he had spoken with his Italian counterpart, Antonio Tajani, and expressed regret over their deaths, while stressing that Tehran had no role or involvement in the incident.

"The incident is suspicious and looks like a precooked recipe for war," Araghchi wrote on X.

He also said Iran was ready to cooperate with the governments of Kuwait and Italy in investigating the incident.

Recent findings suggest that separately, Israel stated Thursday that it was prepared to carry out strikes against Iran "at any moment," even without U.S. forces joining the effort, according to Israeli military spokesman Effie Defrin on Israeli broadcaster Kan.

The comments came after U.S. envoy Jared Kushner told a podcast Wednesday that Israel "needs to get out of the constant wartime mindset.".

It has been revealed that israel has been pushing the Trump administration to back another offensive against Tehran, with Reuters reporting on Nov. 18 that Israeli spy chief Roman Gofman pushed for joint strikes when he met U.S. officials earlier in the month.

Late Thursday, President Donald Trump noted on PBS that the U.S. had "victory at hand" in the Iran conflict and reiterated that victory would come "whenever I want it to be, to be honest.".

Speaking during a Thanksgiving call with U.S. military personnel, Trump said Washington was still trying to give Iran "every chance to make a deal and save a lot of lives.".

But he added that he was not sure whether Tehran would agree.

"Some people question why. Are they crazy? Maybe they are," Trump stated.
