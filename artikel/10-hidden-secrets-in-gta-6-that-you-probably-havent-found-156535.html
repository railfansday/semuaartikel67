10 Hidden Secrets in GTA 6 That You Probably Haven’t Found

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Grand Theft Auto 6 map is massive - significant enough to fit multiple maps from previous games inside of it. With a map that big, there are bound to be a lot of hidden details. If you’ve been exploring Leonida, you may have found some of them.

In a recent development, however, you probably haven’t discovered many of the hidden secrets this newly introduced location has to offer. There are hidden missions and interactions that might blow your mind.

According to recent reports, for instance, have you discovered the alien hidden within the bunker? Maybe you haven’t been out to sea yet to find the kraken. There’s plenty to discover in this strange area.

In a recent development, deep in Pelican Valley, you can find an abandoned research center. This is the strange part; that comes when you are inside. The strange part is that when you step inside, you will get assaulted by a giant crab, which is something straight out of a horror movie.

In a recent development, this is a great easter egg in GTA 6, that connects the game to a movie from the 1980s called Island Claws. In this movie, a scientist breeds giant crabs, and then everything goes completely wrong.

Well, a scientist in GTA 6 tried doing the same thing, and it ended up breeding a giant crab that killed everyone, and now it is coming for you.

Once you have made your way to the Starlet Motel in Kelly County, head northwest. Locate the abandoned car with the flashing lights. From here, follow the path into the woods and you will discover a lady being abducted by a UFO. It is a wild sight.

Sources indicate that A few days later, go to sleep as the character you were playing as when you found the UFO. This will trigger a dream sequence where you need to sneak around alien eggs. It also gives you a look at the universe.

In a recent development, the whole dream sequence ends with you inserting a serum into a computer. I don’t want to give too much away, but it gets wild and there are cows involved.

It has been revealed that there is a strange case that takes you to an alternate dimension in GTA 6. In reality, it seems more like it makes you trip extremely hard, but either way it is out of this world. It all starts by going to a construction site in Millport.

Inside one of the shipping containers here, you will find a mysterious case that you can interact with, though the name of the item is listed as “???.”.

From here, get ready to be sent on a wild trip where you need to do a lot of parkour. You basically walk through various shipping containers and reach an area where you must parkour your way to victory. It isn’t just parkour, as you will need to swim at certain points, but it is a trip you won’t forget.

After you have completed the alien abduction event and had one of the weirdest dreams of your life, it is time to rescue an alien from an underground bunker. Once the dream sequence is done, head to Mariana County, just south of Hamlet.

Industry observers note that here, you can find an old secret base. You will need to break in and climb down the ladder to get inside. Type in the code, access the building, and follow the corridors like you did in your dream.

You will need to put in sequences and everything, but in the end, you will be rewarded by seeing a real alien, and you can even help it escape the base so that it can return home.

You may have spotted Skunkape during one of the earlier missions in GTA 6. During your time in Grassrivers, you can spot a creature running across the road. If you choose to follow it, it will disappear, and you may be wondering where it went.

Well, if you continue your search, you can find effigies that look similar to Bigfoot, but this is not Bigfoot. It is another creature known as Skunkape. When you turn away from the effigy, you will get hit from behind, and a cutscene will play.

Sources indicate that after you have this encounter, you can go to Skeeter’s Bar in Hamlet and begin a mission for someone who is looking for the Skunkape. It is something you definitely don’t want to miss.

Sources indicate that in Grand Theft Auto 5, you could get stuck out in the ocean, and it would require you to swim for a long time to get back to shore. It was very inconvenient, and so players probably aren’t very interested in getting stuck out at sea in GTA 6.

Well, if you decide to go far out into the ocean and wait for a while, you will get rescued by a dog that is driving a jet ski. It is quite odd, but an amazing interaction that you can certainly miss if you never go out of your way to be stranded in the ocean.

Industry observers note that the dog will also bring you to an island where there are other dogs, and it will also dig up some treasure for you. At least you don’t go home empty handed for all this work.

According to recent reports, you can go searching for treasure in many different locations around Leonida. One of the best places is deep in the ocean, as you can find gold doubloons and more. However, there are some treasures around the map that come straight from space.

If you search around Mount Kalaga, you can find what appears as purple mist. This is an impact crater, and it is one of the few sites where you can find a meteorite that has crashed into the ground.

While a space rock is cool, the best part about it is that you can sell it for a fair amount of money, so you will want to keep it on your person and take it to a pawn shop to get rid of it.

It has been revealed that something that the game doesn’t tell you is that there are cocaine flamingos, which is a term I never thought I would write, but welcome to GTA 6. There are 125 cocaine flamingos scattered around Leonida, and you need at least 100 of them to complete a task.

You can go the entire game without realizing these statues exist, but if you pay attention, you will notice that these lawn ornaments are different from the standard ones found in many lawns.

Instead of having the standard black and yellow beak, cocaine flamingos have a black and white beak. These flamingo ornaments can be destroyed to find bricks of cocaine that you can sell to a dealer.

Sources indicate that there are many different strange finds that you can discover under the surface of the ocean. Treasures galore wait for you in the deep, but so do monsters. Thankfully, they aren’t alive, unless you count a shark as a monster.

In a recent development, to the southeast of Gloriana Key, you can find a shipwreck. However, this is no ordinary shipwreck, as it was destroyed by none other than the mythical beast known as The Kraken.

Sources indicate that the Kraken’s tentacles are wrapped all around the ship, which reminds me of Pirates of the Caribbean. Keep in mind that you need to swim very deep to find this hidden secret, and it may take a while to spot thanks to how dark the ocean can be.

Sources indicate that there is a hotel that is run by The Kingfishers in GTA 6, but they unfortunately aren’t having a great day. This hotel is located in Pelican Bay, and you want to be careful when you go here, as there is a tiger.

When you approach the hotel room, you will hear gunshots. If you make your way over to the area, you will see a massacre, as a tiger is loose around the area and is eating the various gang members.

It is a crazy interaction that is never marked on your map until you are nearby, so make sure you search the entirety of Pelican Bay and don’t miss anything.
