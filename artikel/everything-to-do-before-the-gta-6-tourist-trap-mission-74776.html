Sources indicate that the GTA 6 Tourist Trap mission is a critical one as it's the beginning of the end of Chapter 1 and locks you into a sequence that introduces some radical changes. I won't go into details to avoid spoilers but just be aware that you have to accept Mattia's job and everything that ensures is completely unavoidable to significantly advance the main story (in fact, check out our GTA 6 Chapter 1 walkthrough if you’re stuck on what to do). The caveat is that there are a few things in Chapter 1 you can get locked out of by doing this mission, so I've explained below what you should be prioritizing before moving on to Chapter 2.I've kept this guide as spoiler-free as possible but consider yourself warned!

Before you begin the Tourist Trap mission in GTA 6, make sure you've done the following:.

In short, focus on wrapping up all the pink missions in the Key Lento area, and grabbing a few missable collectibles.

It has been revealed that thankfully, there's plenty that doesn't get locked off after reaching Chapter 2. Illegal Operations, such as the GTA 6 Exotic Animal Front and the two Kingfisher robbery opportunities, will remain accessible. You can also complete the entire GTA 6 Bigfoot mission chain as early as Chapter 1, but you there's no pressure as this mission won't expire. Finally, Kenton's shipwrecks and Cal's fishing challenge will be only temporarily disabled.
