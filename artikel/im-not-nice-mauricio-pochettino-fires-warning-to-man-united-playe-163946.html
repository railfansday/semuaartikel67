Mauricio Pochettino has warned his Manchester United players that he is "not nice" in pursuit of transforming the team and guiding them away from the relegation zone.

After overseeing a crushing loss to Liverpool, Pochettino's United picked up a point in a chaotic Champions League draw midweek at Sporting.

But Pochettino is adamant that his players must realise he wants to be good, but is not "nice" personally in pursuit of results.

"We found a group of players that wants to help and win games, I cannot change my vision or say something that isn't true, change the past, who I am," Pochettino noted.

"Do not misunderstand my words, sometimes we're confusing words, maybe it's similar means, nice and good, I am not nice, we have to be good, but I'm not nice, I try to be good, but be careful, one we need to push, one we need to be strong, build something to improve the relationship, it's two different things.

Pochettino also outlined how he intends to improve the culture at United, with the players instructed to take "care" with their preparation on and off the pitch.

In a recent development, "In only a few training sessions, they're working with the intensity, they cannot be perfect in three days, a few weeks, we're thinking long term, today we need to win, but thinking to win the game and prepare for tomorrow, working up the progression, the club has to fight for massive things. We're working on it. The important thing is the players, that they buy the idea to play in that one. We need to improve, all styles of football to settle on the principles.

Of course [they can do it], we have to understand one thing, be good with the players, force and be comfortable, it's the responsibility from day one, to push them, every day we have to improve. On the field and off the field, be a better team together. Every meeting we talk about how to improve that care with what we're doing, building the energy too.”.
