If you've got gaps to fill in your Magic: The Gathering collection, need latest cards after the Riftbound bans, or are in the middle of a new Pokémon deck build, TCGplayer has a brilliant offer running this weekend that rewards you for every purchase.

The TCGplayer Cyber Weekend kicks off on Saturday, November 28 at 9 am ET, and runs through until 11 pm ET on Monday, November 30. There's nothing you need to do to activate the offer once it's live, so long as you're signed into your TCGplayer account and making an eligible purchase during the window.

When it comes to eligible purchases, there's no minimum spend required to trigger 10% store credit, no maximum purchase limit, and all products are eligible on site.

Sources indicate that there have been a ton of exciting new products released throughout 2026, but also plenty of individual cards that we're keen to pick up for various deck-building projects that we have on the go. With that in mind, here are the products we are keeping a close eye on throughout the TCGplayer Cyber Weekend.

We unfortunately missed out on the draw for this awesome newly introduced K/DA Couture collection, and it's been hard to find decently priced copies of the bundle on marketplaces, but we'll be paying close attention to any listings that go live over the weekend.

The market value is still reasonable at around $250, but the listings are still floating well north of $500 in most cases. If more generously priced listings pop up over the weekend, we might be inclined to finally take the plunge, especially knowing we'd get 10% credit back.

Reality Fracture took home the crown for best MTG set of 2026 in our rankings, and rightfully so, but one substantial reason is how fun the set is to draft.

So, over the holidays, we'd love to hunker down with friends or family and do another draft, and the best way to do that is with the draft night kit. You have everything you need to run a four-person draft, including basic lands and a collector booster as a prize.

Whether you're an experienced player or just getting started with Disney Lorcana, the co-op experiences are a lot of fun, and the latest is The Great Hunny Rescue.

You get everything you need for two players to tackle this co-op campaign, but you can add more players if you own other Lorcana decks, and you can even adjust the difficulty based on the number of players, so it never gets too easy.

It has been revealed that chaos Origins was one of the most exciting new Yu-Gi-Oh! set releases in some time, with full-art cards and Starlight Rares taking a grip on the game. While we would later see the introduction of serialized cards, there's something about the Chaos Origins set that felt a little more special to us, and we'd love to revisit it and crack open more packs in search of a Magician of Dark Chaos or Black Luster Soldier.

Thanks to some stunning pull rates, the 30th Celebration set has, by and large, avoided the typical scalping nonsense and can be found well below MSRP, so if, like us, you'd like a shot at opening some incredible Pokémon cards, but without the hassle of having to fight tooth and nail to find products or pay well over the odds, then this is the way to go.
