Black Friday is here, and if you’ve been waiting to pick up a new Magic: The Gathering Commander deck, this is a good weekend to do it. Best Buy has the Fantastic Four deck for under $40, and Amazon has deals on precons from Final Fantasy and Star Trek.

We’ve rounded up 10 of the best Magic Black Friday deals below, including starter kits for new players and bundles for anyone who just wants to crack some packs.

Prices and availability may change throughout Black Friday weekend.

Sources indicate that if you want to teach a Final Fantasy fan how to play Magic, it helps to start with characters they already know. The Starter Kit comes with two ready-to-play 60-card decks built around Cloud and Sephiroth, so you can each pick a side and get straight to the game. This is a great starter product even at the $20 MSRP, but a dollar off at Amazon makes it that much more enticing.

In a recent development, Y’shtola, Night’s Blessed puts the Final Fantasy XIV cast in a white-blue-black Commander deck that rewards you for casting noncreature spells with mana value 3 or greater. Each of those spells can chip away at all three opponents while gaining you life. If you like passing with mana open and waiting to see whose aims are worth interrupting, this is the Final Fantasy precon I’d grab. This is another modest deal at only 13% off, but considering the fact that Y'shtola is the most popular Commander ever these days, it may be worth picking up this deck. As an added bonus, Final Fantasy Commander decks come with a two-card Collector Booster sampler pack — so you never know what great pulls you might get in there.

According to recent reports, the Aetherdrift precon is all about stockpiling energy counters to charge up your artifact creatures. Saheeli, Radiant Creator can spend energy to make a temporary copy of one of your permanents at the start of combat. Copy something with a good enters-the-battlefield ability, and you can get another use out of it before the token disappears. It’s the kind of deck that makes an unassuming card do a lot more work than anyone at the table expected. If you like playing with artifacts in these colors, then this one's a must-buy considering it's 19% off.

The Foundations Starter Collection feels like a must-buy for just about any kind of player, particularly newcomers who are just looking to build up their collection. Not only does it come with three Foundations play boosters, but you get a total of more than 350 cards, including many high-impact staples across all colors. This kind of curated pool makes it easy to build decks in any play format, from Standard to Commander. And at 25% off, there's never been a better time.

This Final Fantasy 10 precon takes its cue from the Sphere Grid, turning the video game’s progression system into a Commander deck that piles counters onto creatures. Tidus, Yuna’s Guardian can move a counter from one creature to another at the start of combat, so the creature your opponent let through last turn might be the one they regret ignoring this turn. It’s my pick of the two Final Fantasy precons if you like winning through combat.

The Reality Fracture Bundle gets you nine Play Boosters, a foil promo, basic lands, a spindown life counter, and a storage box. At $42.95, it works out to about $4.77 per pack before counting the extras. If you’ve been curious about the set and want a few packs to open over the weekend, this is the cheaper of the two bundles here. That's 26% off on a bunch of cards from the best Magic set in 2026.

Industry observers note that jean-Luc Picard captains a red-white-blue deck built around historic cards, a category that includes artifacts, legendary cards, and sagas. That gives you plenty of directions to take the deck if you want to upgrade it later. But if the whole reason you bought a Star Trek Commander deck was to get spacecraft onto the battlefield, there’s already a plan for you right out of the box. For a deck like this to be marked down by 26% so soon after release feels surprising — and enticing.

Best Buy has the Marvel Super Heroes Bundle for $25 off, which brings the nine included Play Boosters down to about $5 each before you count the extras. One of those extras is a foil Scarlet Witch promo, so if Wanda’s the character you’re shopping for, you can set her aside for a deck before you open a single pack. The bundle also comes with basic lands and a box to store the rest. If you're considering buying any Marvel Super Heroes packs in the near future, this is likely the best deal you can find.

According to recent reports, marvel’s First Family stars in this genuinely solid four-color Commander deck. Between a number of interesting upcoming cards and some valuable reprints, this $38.99 deal is probably the single best if you're looking for a precon this Black Friday. Building a four-color mana base from scratch can eat up an evening and a good chunk of your budget.

A lot of Magic experts might tell you that Commander is not a good place for beginners to get started playing the game, and yet, it's the most popular format for the game because it's a more social, casual experience. A lot of experts might also tell you that typal or kindred decks — which emphasize a single creature type — are also great for beginners. This precon's Commander is Captain America, Team Leader, and he wants you to fill the battlefield with heroes. Director Nick Fury comes in the box, too, as an alternate Commander. If you are someone you love like Marvel and are interested in trying out Magic, this might actually be one of the best deals to capitalize on this Black Friday.
