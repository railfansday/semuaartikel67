Recent findings suggest that TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box is back in stock for $164.99 at Amazon. You can also find cheaper listings live at Walmart.

Amazon's $59.99 Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box listing was our favorite trading card deal for some time. You needed to go through the "request invite" system, but $59.99 was way under TCGplayer's $115 market price.

We were desperate to see this deal return for Black Friday, but it's not looking good.

Industry observers note that the $59.99 deal might be dead, but stock has finally returned to Amazon. On Black Friday, you can pick up the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box for $164.99 on Amazon. That's over market price, but you don't need to request an invite to buy. Stock is readily available with third-party sellers.

Walmart also has stock, and for less than Amazon. You can find listings for $115.96 at the time of writing. That's right on TCGplayer’s $115 market price, so expect these to sell fast.

Buyers get nine 30th Celebration booster packs, an exclusive full-art foil Nidorina promo card, 16 foil Basic Energy cards, 65 custom card sleeves, damage-counter dice, a condition marker, card dividers, a collector’s player guide, and a code card for Pokémon TCG Live.
