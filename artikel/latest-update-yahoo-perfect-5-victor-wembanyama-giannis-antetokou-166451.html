Recent findings suggest that yahoo Sports is giving fans another way to play fantasy basketball with Perfect 5, a free-to-play game featuring each week's NBA on Prime doubleheader. The rules are simple: pick five players, stay within a $15 budget and rack up fantasy points through scoring, rebounds, assists, defensive stats and 3s.

According to recent reports, friday's slate is an NBA Cup Group Play doubleheader: Heat at Knicks (7 p.m. ET) and Lakers at Spurs (9:30 p.m. ET). The injury report is busy again. Karl-Anthony Towns was downgraded to questionable with an abdominal contusion, Andre Drummond is out in concussion protocol and OG Anunoby is out with a calf strain. Giannis Antetokounmpo is probable with groin tendinitis, Austin Reaves is back from a heel bruise and De'Aaron Fox is questionable with a shin injury.

According to recent reports, using Yahoo projections, each player's last five games, the betting lines and the latest injury news, I ran 20,000 simulations of the slate against a simulated Yahoo field built from this week's pick rates. I'm pairing the two best fantasy players on the board and spreading the rest of the lineup across all four teams, so I have a stake in every game.

This group finished in the top 1% of the simulated field 23.4% of the time and beat every entry in the field in 1.49% of sims, and nobody in the simulated field had these exact five. It holds up whether Towns plays or not: 24.3% of the time in the sims where he played and 21.6% when he sat.

The blocks are what separate him: he's averaging 3.8 a game, and every one is worth three points.
