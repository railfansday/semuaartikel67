Recent findings suggest that 1,000-Lb. Sisters star Amy Slaton’s ex-husband, Michael Halterman, has been arrested for alleged child abuse, Us can confirm.

According to recent reports, halterman, 43, was booked on a charge of assault in the fourth degree on Wednesday, November 25, according to Webster County jail records viewed by Us. He did not immediately enter a plea and was released on a $2,500 cash bond on Friday, November 27, ahead of a December 3 arraignment.

Details about the alleged incident that led to Halterman’s arrest were not immediately available.Us reached out to the Union County Sheriff’s Office for comment.

Halterman and Slaton, 39, were married from 2017 to 2023, divorcing months after welcoming son Glenn in 2022. The exes are also parents of son Gage, who was born in 2020.

Industry observers note that amy, who has not publicly addressed Halterman’s arrest, has documented her family’s life on TLC’s 1,000-Lb. Sisters since its 2020 premiere.

Sources indicate that “My husband, Michael, he means the world to me. Me and Michael are newlyweds, but we’ve been together four years now,” she stated in a confessional from the pilot. “I can tell him anything and everything. And Michael’s really supportive of me helping Tammy [Slaton, my sister].”.

Amy has since moved on with Brian Lovvorn, getting married in October 2025.

It has been revealed that “My wedding is a dream finally coming true. I’m getting my happily ever after,” Amy told People of her Halloween nuptials. “I deeply cherished the journey of wedding planning, embracing the art and location hunting, but selecting bridesmaids and groomsmen proved to be a profound challenge. I intuitively knew that my extraordinary friend Ollisha [Davis] would graciously accept the esteemed honor of being my maid of honor, even before I extended the heartfelt invitation.”.

According to recent reports, she continued at the time, “Getting married means that I’m no longer lonely because I’ve discovered my soulmate, Brian, is my happy place. Once you get to know him, you’ll find him to be the sweetest, most caring person you’ll ever have the pleasure of knowing.”.

Amy further praised Lovvorn as a stepfather to her two sons.

Sources indicate that “Not only did he capture my heart, but he also won over my boys and not many men out there will accept a single mom with two energetic boys,” she told the outlet. “I adore how he inspires me to be a better version of myself, how he prioritizes my well-being and that of my boys, selflessly putting our needs before his own.”.
