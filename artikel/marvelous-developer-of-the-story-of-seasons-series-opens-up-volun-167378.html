📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, marvelous, the studio known for its work on the Story of Seasons series as well as the upcoming Eternal Anima, has unveiled the introduction of a voluntary redundancy scheme. Through this program, it's aiming to lower its headcount by 65 employees.

Per Automaton, this "early retirement" program is part of an effort to restructure its console business, which refers to non-mobile, non-live-service games under its umbrella.

Sources indicate that the reason for this change is the shifting environment in game development, namely the growing scope of development and elongated development cycles. The organization cites a need to optimise its workforce and shift towards flexibility, per the introduction of the early retirement program.

Marvelous will begin gathering applications, with those choosing to take the manufacturer up on the offer set to leave at the end of the financial year in March 2027. These employees will receive a bonus as part of their redundancy package, as well as assistance finding upcoming employment elsewhere if they desire it.

Marvelous emphasised that despite this program, it still sees console game development as one of its core businesses. This comes in the same financial year Marvelous reported a 1,105 percent increase in profit between April 2026 - September 2026, credited to overseas sales of both upcoming indie releases and older console games.
