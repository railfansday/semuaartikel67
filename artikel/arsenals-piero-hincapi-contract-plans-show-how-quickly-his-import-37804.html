Arsenal are stated to be trying to reward Piero Hincapié with better contract terms after just months since making his move permanent.

It has been revealed that according to reports, negotiations are at an advanced stage between the Ecuadorian and international players. David Raya is also near to signing a fresh contract as Mikel Arteta looks to retain his key squad members.

However, Hincapié is in a slightly different position compared to his teammates. The 24-year-old only signed a five-year deal permanently in the summer.

Sources indicate that therefore, he could be discussing an increase in his salary rather than an extension. It has not been officially confirmed by either Arsenal or the player.

Arsenal have exercised their option to make Hincapié’s loan move from Bayer Leverkusen permanent.

It has been revealed that the defender made 39 appearances in all competitions during his first season in North London, scoring one goal and providing two assists. He has become an important part of Arteta’s defensive options.

His versatility has been an asset to Arsenal. His ability to play as a centre-back or left-back meaning he provides another option to utilise when problems occur with injuries or rotation in defence.

However, Hincapié has been overshadowed by Gabriel Magalhães and Riccardo Calafiori, making it a tough battle for playing time.

In the current campaign, he has played just five games in the league, with all of them coming off the bench. A total of 67 minutes has been played.

Hincapié will not be too concerned by this, as Arsenal have plenty of defensive options, and he provides cover in two positions.

The news that Hincapié is in talks is part of a bigger story around the club trying to keep their best players.

According to recent reports, declan Rice has signed a fresh five-year deal with Jurrien Timber, signing on improved terms. Raya is also noted to be close to signing a new deal.

Industry observers note that arteta has already signed his future to the club until 2030. It is clear that they want to keep the continuity going forward after recent success.

While Hincapié has a contract until next year, renegotiating his deal would show how much Arsenal values him.

They may want to reward their players who have swiftly become key members of their squad.

He has no reports of being unhappy and looking to leave the club. It seems he is being rewarded for his contributions to the club and to give him security in the long term.

This is a huge vote of confidence for a player who has only just completed his permanent move this summer.
