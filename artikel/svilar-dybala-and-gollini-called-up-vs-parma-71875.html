Roma are back with Mile Svilar and Paulo Dybala ahead of their match against Parma.

Both players are included in Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad for tomorrow’s away match at the Tardini stadium at 6:00 PM.

This is crucial news for the Giallorossi, who will once again be able to count on two key players.

The Giallorossi goalkeeper had missed nine Serie A and Champions League matches, while the Argentine was substituted at halftime of the match at the Olimpico against Mourinho’s Real Madrid.

Sources indicate that roma have called up 21 players for the match. The defense includes Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso, Balerdi, Koulierakis, Ghilardi, Lulli, Molina, and Wesley, while the midfield includes Cristante, De Roon, Kone, Pisilli, and Mora.

In addition to Dybala, Castro, Malen, and Soulé are also available in the attack, ready to provide alternatives for the Giallorossi’s attack.

Sources indicate that rensch is also expected to miss the Champions League away match against PSG. The Dutch full-back could return to Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad for next Sunday’s match at the Olimpico against Monza, led by former coach Juric.
