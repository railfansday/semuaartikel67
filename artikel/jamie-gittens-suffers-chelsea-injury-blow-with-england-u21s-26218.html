England Under-21 boss Lee Carsley addressed the injury directly after the game. He confirmed the nature of the knock and the decision to send Gittens home.

It has been revealed that “Jamie got a kick on his ankle,” Carsley noted after the match. “We never take any risks. With the age of these players, we don’t ever keep them in camp to see how they are. He will go back.”.
