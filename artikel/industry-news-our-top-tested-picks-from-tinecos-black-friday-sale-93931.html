There's a handy headlight that makes it easier to spot dirt and debris, and we like the large canister capacity. It performs better on hardwood than on carpet, though. This is the best price we've seen for it.

In a recent development, this carpet cleaner is on the heavy side, but it's one of our favorites, especially with its easy-to-rinse tanks for the dirty and clean water. There's a large LED screen that shows which cleaning mode you're in, and the drying mode gets everything dried off in a flash. It'll pull old stains from your carpet and even comes with an accessory for upholstery.

I haven't tested the newer version of this steam mop yet, but I've been testing models from other brands for an patch to that buying guide, and this older Floor One S7 still holds up. It looks great on its docking station, and it cleans my floors efficiently without leaving behind too much water. It does struggle along baseboards, but I truthfully have yet to meet a steam mop that doesn't. It is easy to maneuver with its self-propulsion and also has a handy light that'll show you cleaning progress as you pass over an area. I like the built-in suction too, which means I don't have to use two gadgets to clean one floor.
