📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This paper constructs an unconditional, zero-error Las Vegas algorithm on a single fixed uniform classical multitape Turing machine $\mathcal{M}$ that computes the exact complete prime factorization $N = \prod_{1 \le i \le k} p_i^{e_i}$ ($p_1 < \dots < p_k$ prime, $e_i \ge 1$) of every integer $N \ge 2$ given in standard binary representation, halting with probability $1$ over its read-once fair coin flips, in worst-case expected time $L_N[1/3, 105]$ and worst-case deterministic workspace $L_N[1/3, 40]$, where.

$$L_x(\alpha, c) := \exp!\bigl(c,(\ln x)^\alpha (\ln\ln x)^{1-\alpha}\bigr)$$.

and $T(N) \le L_N[\alpha, c]$ means.

$$ \limsup_{N \to \infty} \frac{\ln \max(1, T(N))}{(\ln N)^\alpha (\ln\ln N)^{1-\alpha}} \le c. $$.

Sources indicate that every prime factor in the output is certified deterministically via Agrawal–Kayal–Saxena primality testing and direct multiplication against $N$, with effectively computable constants and no unproven number-theoretic hypotheses. A complete, unconditional, Mathlib-only Lean 4 (v4.35.0) formalization of the entire proof has also been completed and will be released in a companion submission.

This paper and its Lean 4 formalization were entirely obtained by Cogentic (arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.40324), an agentic framework for mathematical discovery, using Gemini 4 as the base model. There was no human interaction other than the initial prompt for Cogentic to generate a full proof and formalization. The Cogentic harness was built by Yang Cai, Vineet Gupta, Yanchen Jiang, Christopher Liaw, Aranyak Mehta, Grigoris Velegkas, and Di Wang (Google Research and Google DeepMind).
