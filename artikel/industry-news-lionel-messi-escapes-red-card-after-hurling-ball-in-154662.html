Published: 22:41 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 03:57 EST, 23 November 2026.

Inter Miami star Lionel Messi was lucky not to have been sent off after hurling the ball in the face of his opponent during an embarrassing MLS Cup Playoff defeat.

In a recent development, in the Round of 16 contest against the Columbus Crew, Miami went down 1-0 early in the first half with a penalty converted by Gonzalo Tapia. The deficit was then compounded when Miami fullback Gonzalo Lujan was sent off with a second yellow card.

On the stroke of halftime, Messi nearly joined his teammate in the locker room with a moment of madness on a throw-in.

Tapia and Messi were both standing out of bounds as the Chilean striker prevented the World Cup winner from taking his throw right away.

Sources indicate that messi then used the ball to shove Tapia's face back. When the Columbus man tried jumping in the air to block the throw, Messi then threw the ball into Tapia's head.

Tapia crumbled to the ground as Messi raised his hands asking for a foul on the Crew star.

Lionel Messi takes a throw-in off Gonzalo Tapia's face pic.twitter.com/jX3tTuojHe.

Lionel Messi nearly got sent off during his team's MLS Cup Playoff match against Columbus.

A frustrated Messi launched the ball off the face of Crew striker Gonzalo Tapia as he prevented the Argentina star from taking a quick throw in. Both players received yellow cards.

Tapia got revenge by scoring a brace to lead the Crew to a 3-0 victory on Saturday evening.

Referee Allen Chapman was not happy with either player's antics, showing yellow cards to both Tapia and Messi.

But ultimately, Columbus and Tapia would get the last laugh as they won 3-0 in the best-of-three Round of 16.

Midfielder Taha Habroune scored the second goal of the evening in the 52nd minute as Tapia secured a brace just six minutes later.

The result has Inter Miami on the brink of elimination from the playoffs after winning the title last season. Columbus dominated every facet of the game, thanks in part to owning 62 percent of possession.

Messi attempted two shots on the evening – counting for half of the saves from Crew keeper Patrick Schulte.

Sources indicate that inter Miami must win the next match in Ohio to stay in the playoffs. If the game finishes tied, the rules dictate the match will proceed directly to penalty kicks.

Out of his three prior seasons in the United States, Messi's 2025 MLS Cup victory was an outlier. Miami missed the playoffs in 2023 and were eliminated in the first round in 2024.
