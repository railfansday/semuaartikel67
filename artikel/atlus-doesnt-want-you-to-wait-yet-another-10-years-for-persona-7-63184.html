Atlus Doesn't Want You to Wait Yet Another 10 Years for Persona 7

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that by the time Persona 6 comes out, it'll have been at least 10 years since the launch of Persona 5. That's a very long time between mainline instalments, and developer Atlus doesn't want you to have to repeat the process again.

Via a new manufacturer interview with creative producer Kazuhisa Wada, it's revealed the Persona team within Atlus is working on "various things" to make sure the wait between releases isn't so long. "Persona 6 is not the end," Wada stated.

"We are working on various things to ensure that you don't have to wait as long for the next instalment as we have in the past," Wada continued. "And in order to make Persona an even bigger IP, we are constantly thinking about creative ideas and developments that will not disappoint your expectations. Please look forward to what's to come from Persona.".

Wada does not comment on what those things it's been working on are, but clearly, the studio wants to bring its overall development times down.

It has been revealed that while there have been spin-off games that continued the story established by Persona 5, such as Persona 5 Strikers and Persona 5 Tactica, we're still waiting on the next main entry, Persona 6, almost 10 years later. It doesn't have a release date yet, but there's a lot of hope it'll be out on PS5 next year.

Industry observers note that on its release, Wada noted the sixth game is "even better than before, and we're feeling a real sense of accomplishment as we work on it". Basically nothing other than the logo is known about Persona 6, so fans will be hoping rumours tying more details to The Game Awards next month are true.
