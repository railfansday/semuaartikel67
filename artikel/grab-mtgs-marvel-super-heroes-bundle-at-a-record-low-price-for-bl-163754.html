TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Bundle is on sale for $44.95 at Amazon for Black Friday, down from its $69.99 list price.

Among all the ways you can start building a deck with your favorite Marvel characters in Magic: The Gathering, one of the expansion's smaller boxes has become a must-buy at its lowest price ever, courtesy of Amazon's Black Friday sale.

In a recent development, as of Nov. 27, you can pick up the Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Bundle for only $44.95 at Amazon. With 36% knocked off its $69.99 list price, that's another $7.93 below the best Amazon deal we found during September's Labor Day sale.

Sources indicate that with Kings Games handling the sale and Amazon taking care of shipping, Prime members can have theirs delivered for free as soon as Nov. 28, while non-members can expect theirs by Dec. 2. However, purchases are non-returnable.

At around $4.99 per Play Booster, this is the lowest price Amazon’s trading card deal section has seen yet, and is even only slightly over TCGplayer’s market price of $37.04 — $7.91 to be exact.

Alongside nine packs of 14 trading cards, you'll find a Bundle-exclusive foil promo card (mentioned in the reviews to be Scarlet Witch, but this hasn’t been 100% confirmed), 30 Basic Lands (including 10 with full-art designs), a supersized Spindown life counter, two reference cards, and a themed box to house your collection.
