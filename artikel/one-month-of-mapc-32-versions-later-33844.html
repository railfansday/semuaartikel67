One month after launch, MaPC has shipped 32 versions, added three stores, and run The Witcher 3 and Hogwarts Legacy on Mac.

It has been revealed that maPC released in mid-October as a free native Mac app that runs Windows games from Steam, Epic Games and GOG on Apple Silicon, with no Wine setup and no tinkering. Connect a store account, install a game, press Play. DirectX 9, 10 and 11 run through Wine, DXVK and MoltenVK, all set up automatically, with per-game graphics settings, Retina rendering and a performance HUD. The interface takes its colors from each game's cover art, and the library switches between a Magazine view and a Panels view.

In a recent development, the pace since launch has been fast. MaPC is now on version 1.1.32, with 32 releases in the 1.1 line alone. Four of them landed on a single day this week, mostly fixes for Red Dead Redemption 2 and the Rockstar Launcher. Three more stores joined during the month: Amazon Games, the EA app and Ubisoft Connect. Epic cloud saves arrived too, along with controller fixes and a help page for games that may run.

Industry observers note that the response has been the best part. The launch post passed 1,100 likes and 100 replies on Threads, and the game posts kept going: 007 First Light passed 1,200 likes, Hogwarts Legacy passed 700, The Witcher 3 passed 400. The games people played say it well: Guardians of the Galaxy, Red Dead Redemption 2, Batman Arkham Knight, Jedi Fallen Order, Kena, Winds of Viridia, Pragmata, and free demos of Onimusha and Resident Evil Requiem.

Two of the runs are on video: 44 seconds of Batman: Arkham Knight, and Red Dead Redemption 2 at 60 fps.

We will keep improving it, because we at Abduzeedo love to play games and we love our Mac. MaPC is free in public beta for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 15 and up. Find it at mapc.games, with the full changelog there.
