P&O Cruises has declared that it has placed regional choices at the heart of its Caribbean fly-cruise program, offering more than half of its charter flights for winter 2026-27 departing from airports outside London.

Carnival UK and P&O Cruises President Paul Ludlow mentioned: “A Caribbean holiday should feel effortless from the moment it begins and for many of our guests that means being able to start their journey closer to home.”.

“We have increased availability from Scotland, Belfast, Cardiff and Newcastle, as well as introducing Exeter, and regional flight availability will continue to be an vital area of growth for us in the years ahead,” added Ludlow.

The company stated in a press release that its program features 14-night sailings with flights included, allowing guests to start their Caribbean adventure from airports across the UK.

Manchester, Birmingham and London departures make up the majority of the program, with flights available for all 14-night Barbados roundtrip itineraries.

Manchester alone represents approximately half of all P&O Cruises non-London charter flights to the Caribbean.

The company stated that regional choice is also continuing to grow, with Scottish airports seeing the largest year-on-year increase in available departures for winter 2026-27, with charter availability growing by approximately a third, primarily from Edinburgh.

P&O Cruises has also increased availability from Belfast, Cardiff and Newcastle, while Exeter has been introduced as a new departure airport.

Guests also benefit from direct flights to the Caribbean.

P&O Cruises guests can also check in their luggage at their UK departure airport and have it delivered directly to their cabin onboard.

From November 2027, the Arvia and Iona will sail Caribbean itineraries starting and ending in St Kitts, with the island’s capital, Basseterre, becoming an additional turnaround port for Iona’s fly-cruise program.

P&O Cruises added that it has launched a Black Friday sale, offering savings of up to £500 per cabin on holidays alongside a Black Friday Price Drop Guarantee.

Available until midnight on December 1, 2026, the promotion applies to selected holidays booked on Select Price or Early Saver fares, departing between March 6, 2027, and October 28, 2028.

The sale covers holidays including the Caribbean, Norwegian fjords and the Mediterranean.
