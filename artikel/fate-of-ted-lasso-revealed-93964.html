The Lady Greyhounds are getting ready to take the pitch for another season.

Yes, Ted Lasso fans can believe that the beloved Apple TV series starring Jason Sudeikis is set to return for a fifth season, with the Saturday Night Live alum returning as the titular character and executive producer, according to multiple outlets.

Filming of the new season is expected to begin in April, per Deadline.

Industry observers note that since the season four finale, which aired Oct. 7, both viewers and the TV show’s stars have been curious whether the Emmy-winning series would be lacing up for another season. In fact, co-creator and writer Brendan Hunt—who also stars as Lasso’s right hand man Coach Beard—recently shared he didn’t know Apple TV’s game proposal after it was brought back for season four following a three-year hiatus.

“We’ve always basically hoped we were coming back for more than one,” he told Deadline last month, “but if it ends up just being one, then OK, that’s fine.”.

And it seems the show’s writers made sure to have a clean tackle while wrapping up the season four storylines, just in case.

In a recent development, after all, the season concluded with Lasso trading jerseys, so to speak, with assistant coach Alice Chilton (Tanya Reynolds) and joining Beard as an assistant coach. Meanwhile, Roy Kent (Brett Goldstein) accepted a fresh job as the Chelsea head coach while girlfriend Keeley Jones (Juno Temple) is expecting their first baby.

Meanwhile, Rebecca Welton (Hannah Waddingham) scored a hat trick by securing majority ownership in the Lady Greyhounds before her surprise proposal to boyfriend Matthijs (Matteo van der Grijn).

Plus, before Crown and Anchor pub owner Mae Green (Annette Badland) tragically died in episode nine, she passed the ownership of the bar to three of her thorny patrons.

In a recent development, “There’s not much in the way of cliffhangers,” Hunt continued. “But Ted and Beard as peers instead of hierarchically, I’m curious about that. Roy and Keeley becoming parents, curious about that. Rebecca married, curious about that. The fellas running the pub, and how’s the team going to take to Alice as their leader? These are all just more like questions than cliffhangers.”.
