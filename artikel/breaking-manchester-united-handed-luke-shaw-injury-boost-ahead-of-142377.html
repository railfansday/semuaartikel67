Luke Shaw has returned to the Manchester United squad ahead of tonight’s Champions League fixture against Sporting in Lisbon.

The England international has missed the last eight matches in all competitions through injury. Shaw has not played since the 1-1 draw with Tottenham Hotspur at Old Trafford on 10 October. The 31-year-old trained with his team-mates at Carrington on Tuesday before being included in Mauricio Pochettino’s 22-man travelling squad.

Pochettino worked with Shaw during the early stages of the defender’s career at Southampton. Speaking at Tuesday’s press conference, the United head coach was reluctant to say whether the left-back would feature against Sporting.

Sources indicate that “Obviously, it’s good to see him and he came from a small issue but now he needs to recover his best form,” Pochettino told the club’s official website. Lisandro Martinez started at left-back against Liverpool on Sunday before moving into central defence following Leny Yoro’s dismissal.

Recent findings suggest that goalkeeper Karl Darlow is also back in the squad, although Marcus Rashford, Carlos Baleba, Manuel Ugarte and Benjamin Sesko remain unavailable. Matheus Cunha is suspended following his red card against Roma.

It has been revealed that united have six points from their first four Champions League fixtures, having beaten Sabah and Roma at Old Trafford. Wednesday’s match will be Pochettino’s first European fixture in charge of the club.

With Brentford visiting Old Trafford at the weekend, Pochettino has two opportunities this week to assess Shaw’s match fitness, should the defender be ready to play.
