📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that fans are already counting down the days until we see Allie and Dean’s love story pan out on Off Campus season 2, and I don’t blame them one bit. It’ll be a minute until we reunite with the Briar U gang, but fans are keeping up with every revision on the TV adaptation of Elle Kennedy’s book series in the meantime. And the latest news on season 2 is that a certain cast member won’t be returning.

“I’m not gonna be in the next season,” Riley Davis, who plays Allie’s ex-boyfriend Sean on the Prime Video series, told People at the New York City premiere of his movie November 1963 on November 18.

According to recent reports, as for the reason behind his exit? Riley says there were some scheduling conflicts—which checks out, since he has a role in another YA series, Netflix’s Crew Girl. It’s unclear whether that particular project played a part in his departure from Off Campus.

It has been revealed that “There was a proposal for some Sean in season 2, and we just had a bit of a scheduling thing on my side and a bit on their side,” he explained. “So we’ll see what happens down the line.”.

What’s more: Riley’s exit could potentially signal that the second season of Off Campus might go a different direction from the book series it’s based on.

As viewers saw in the show’s first season, Riley’s character, Sean, plays a pivotal role in Allie and Dean’s romance. In the books, he and Allie have an on-again, off-again relationship—but we might not see that pan out now that Riley is out.

Sources indicate that this isn’t the first time the TV adaptation shook things up with some changes from the source material. In season 1, the show’s writers introduced John Logan’s nonbinary sibling Jules Logan, who noticeably wasn’t in the OG book series.

We’ll have to see how Riley/Sean’s absence is really handled when Off Campus season 2 hits Prime Video on April 28, 2027!
