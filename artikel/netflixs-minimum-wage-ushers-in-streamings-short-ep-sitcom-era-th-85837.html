Netflix’s ‘Minimum Wage’ Ushers In Streaming’s Short Ep Sitcom Era: “The Future Is 15-Minute ‘SpongeBob’-Length Episodes”

In a world of increasingly short attention spans, social media slop and doomscrolling brainrot, it’s a wonder why many television reveals got longer in the streaming era.

Case in point, the 22-minute broadcast sitcom was largely replaced by the 30-minute dramedy and the 43-minute procedural has been overshadowed by the hour-long premium drama series.

Netflix, however, has found success with a much shorter concept in Minimum Wage.

The comedy series, which comes from Jeremy Garelick and Will Phelps’ American High, consists of 14 episodes between 12 and 20 minutes in its first tranche, which debuted at the end of October.

Set in a pizza joint and starring new SNL darling Grace Reiter, Aidan Micho, Hyde Healy, Julia Dicesare, Luke Burke, Ryan Micho, Tommy Armstrong and Randall Park, its short episodes are a feature, not a bug. Watch on Deadline.

Phelps told Deadline, “The future is these 15-minute kind of SpongeBob-length episodes where it’s really fast. There’s not any fat.”.

In fact, Netflix demanded short episodes for the show, which began life as a series of YouTube sketches shot during the middle of the night with interns as camera operators and a building manager handling the boom mic.

Garelick said, “When we closed the deal, [Netflix] said that they wanted it to be a minimum of twelve and a half-minute episodes. It was an interesting challenge because most episodic TV indicates are 22 to 30 pages. It was almost like we were inventing a new structure as we were going and it was hard.”.

Recent findings suggest that episodes were also capped at 20 minutes with shooting scripts ending up being around 15 pages and the cast encouraged to improv. “You can captivate an audience for 15 minutes really well,” Phelps added. “Jeremy and I are coming at it from traditional screenplay writing and the cast is coming at it from making the most exciting one-minute thing for TikTok or Instagram.”.

This contrast is both the essence of Minimum Wage and also highlights one of the industry’s challenges as creators such as Druski and others are increasingly working on scripted projects for streamers.

Industry observers note that “Everyone’s clamoring for creators to make shows now [following] the success of Obsession and Minimum Wage and I’m nervous that without the guidance of traditional screenwriters, you might fall into a trap of creators that are used to taking a one-minute premise and being told expand that into 15 minutes. That’s just going to be full of fat. Whereas we’re taking stories that really should be a half-hour and squeezing the fat out from the other side. That’s the secret sauce that we have.”.

Garelick began his career working as an assistant for Joel Schumacher and working on movies such as The Break-Up and The Wedding Ringer before teaming up with Phelps to buy a high school in upstate Newly introduced York, where they made films such as Banana Split, Holly Slept Over and Pete Davidson-starrer Big Time Adolescence.

“We’ve become a traditional Hollywood studio, but we give a lot more trust to the creators and the artists, and we have an academy, our shorts and our sketch teams, and we’re kind of combining [it all],” remarked Garelick.

This also allowed American High to deliver more episodes of Minimum Wage than Netflix had originally ordered. The streamer initially greenlit 20 episodes but they ended up writing 28 scripts and persuaded Netflix that it would be cheaper to produce them all.

“We don’t get hung up on the actual business side of things. We’ll just do it for the love of the game,” added Phelps.

Minimum Wage debuted last month and reached Netflix’s global top ten charts with 2.4M views, impressive given the season’s run time is under four hours.

Sources indicate that it is one of a number of streaming highlights keeping episode lengths concise. Shane Gillis’ Netflix comedy series Tires is generally in the 20-23-minute range, while HBO Max’s The Big Bang Theory spinoff Stuart Fails To Save The Universe consists of episodes between 18 and 20 minutes with one episode clocking in at only 15 minutes.

Co-creator Chuck Lorre, who has spent the vast majority of his career working in broadcast comedy, said there was “no real reason” to pad the episodes. “The historic length of a show is entirely arbitrary, it comes from network television where a certain amount of advertising has to be sold to pay the way of making this television, but it’s not an element any longer in streaming,” Lorre said. “So, why not just make the best show we can and hope the audience is entertained and is not looking at their watch.”.

Minimum Wage works because it is a workplace comedy, one featuring an overwhelmed group of teenagers struggling to run The Slice Club, where the “stakes are low and the pressure is immense.”.

In a recent development, it’s not clear whether the remaining 14 episodes that American High shot will be labeled as the second part of season one or season two, but they are expected to air in spring 2027. There’s also plenty of road for more episodes; Garelick remarked that they’ve already got around 50 more draft scripts.

According to recent reports, “It takes a few months to get there, and then it takes a year or so of incubating that online to get a fan base, and then you’re ready to start a show with some solid footing behind it, and the audience feels like they were part of the inception of this,” said Phelps. “I think it’s the future of TV.”.
