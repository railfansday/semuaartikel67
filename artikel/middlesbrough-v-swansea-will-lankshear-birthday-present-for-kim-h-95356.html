Boro were second when Hellberg took charge on November 24, 2025 - succeeding Rob Edwards who left for Premier League struggles Wolves.

They ran out of steam in the automatic promotion race before going onto lose in the Championship play-off final to Hull City after earning a reprieve over the Southampton ‘Spygate’ scandal.

This season - propelled by the goals of Will Lankshear - they have rarely been out of the promotion conversation and moved to the top of the league with Saturday’s 2-0 win over Bristol City.

“It’s been a great year, a fun year,” said Hellberg, ahead of Tuesday night’s visit of Swansea City. “I’ve learned a lot, I’ve developed together with the players and I’m feeling that the club is in a better place and further ahead than we were a year ago.

It has been revealed that “It’s great (to be top), but it goes quick in this business and it’s a latest game Tuesday. We’re happy with the points tally we’ve gathered and we’ve played some great football at times, but there’s always ups and downs that you have to handle in this tough league.

“And as we learnt last year, it doesn’t matter how many days you are in the top two, all that matters is the last day of the season. It’s about performances and hopefully people can see we’ve made strides forward.”.

In a recent development, lankshear has scored 13 goals in 12 Championship starts but missed the defeat at Wrexham and the draw at Watford with a hamstring injury. He came off the bench for a 13-minute cameo at the weekend.

Hellberg confirmed on Monday he is “ready to go” against Swansea.
