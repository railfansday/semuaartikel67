Look, I can't call the Bose TV Speaker HiFi — but I can call it a great deal with $70 off.

It has been revealed that A whole surround sound package for under $320?! Wowee! This is actually a great deal — Hisense soundbars sound great, and you won't find a surround sound speaker, bar and subwoofer combo this cheap.

It'll feel inadequate, and that would be cruel. And you're not cruel. Are you?

If spaceships were small enough to fit on a TV stand. You never know; the aliens might be tiny. Either way, $200 off a brand new soundbar with excellent Dolby Atmos and great style is nothing to sniff at.
