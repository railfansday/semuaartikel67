Leeds United prospect Jayden Lienou has reportedly attracted interest from several Championship clubs.

The 18-year-old was recruited from Manchester City’s youth system in the summer of 2025 and has since been knocking on the door of Daniel Farke’s first team.

He has not yet made a first-team appearance, but he has been on Farke’s bench and is already being named in senior Wales squads.

According to the Daily Mail’s Tom Collomosse, the defender has caught the eye of several Championship clubs who could explore a loan deal in January.

Posting on X, he said: “Leeds very excited about Jayden Lienou, 18-year-old left-back in full Wales squad. Lienou has been training with first team since February and featured in summer.

“Several Championship clubs interested in possible loan in Jan. Decision soon.”.

According to recent reports, lienou is well thought of in LS11 but regular first-team action may prove hard to come by if he stays put. Not only does Farke have Sweden international Gabriel Gudmundsson in his left-back department, the Leeds boss can call upon former France youth international Melvin Bard.

Lienou penned a newly introduced contract at Elland Road in the summer and after committing to the Whites until 2029, he noted: “It feels amazing - another step in the right direction, and another step towards where me and my family want me to get to in the future.

“I'd say I'm probably further ahead than I thought I was going to be when I first signed for Leeds. When me and Scott sat down at the start of the season we set some goals to get to certain situations, certain milestones in my career and I think I've exceeded that.

“Getting the support from all the different people, the different knowledge, the different views on different points that they can give across just helps you develop as a player, especially coming through as a young player at the club.”.
