📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sony’s struggling to meet demand for the PS5 following last week’s release of GTA 6.

Many major retailers in the US, including PS Direct, are at the moment completely sold out of the system – although you may find isolated instances of stock in stores.

It has been revealed that the platform holder has no promotions on hardware this Black Friday, presumably because it can’t meet demand at full-price. Remember, the console is significantly more expensive than it was at launch right now, due to spiralling global memory costs.

Writing on Bluesky, Circana analyst Mat Piscatella noted that PS5 hardware purchases increased 2.4x year-over-year in America through the week ending 14th November, just prior to the release of Rockstar’s open world.

Sony indicates GTA 6 is the biggest launch in its history, and it observed “the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays” on 19th November.

It added that for 70% of those newly connected consoles, GTA 6 was the first game played.

I imagine Sony is trying to get as many systems onto store shelves as it possibly can to meet this unprecedented demand, and I suspect demand will remain high through the holidays now.

In response to the situation, GameStop recently raised the price of used PS5 Pro consoles way beyond their already-sizeable MSRP.
