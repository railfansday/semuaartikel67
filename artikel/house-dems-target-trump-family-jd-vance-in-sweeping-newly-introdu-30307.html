It has been revealed that white House counsel Will Scharf joins 'Fox News Sunday' to address incoming Democratic investigations targeting the Trump administration. Scharf warns that the Department of Justice will push back aggressively if lawmakers use their oversight powers to threaten or intimidate administration staffers.

The likely next chairman of the House Oversight Committee is officially taking the first steps to launch a wide array of investigations before Democrats take control in the new Congress.

Rep. Robert Garcia, D-Calif., is sending document preservation letters to 12 figures in President Donald Trump's orbit on Wednesday morning, including Vice President JD Vance, Donald Trump Jr., Eric Trump, Jared Kushner, former U.S. Ambassador to Greece Kimberly Guilfoyle and White House chief of staff Stephen Miller.

House Oversight Committee Democrats are looking to investigate the Trump family's business deals, alleged misconduct at the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and the federal government's handling of Jeffrey Epstein's case, they said in a release.

Garcia remarked the probes "will lay the groundwork for legislation which helps all Americans.".

THE TOP TRUMP OFFICIALS DEMOCRATS ADMIT ARE ON THEIR HIT LIST FOR INVESTIGATIONS IN THE NEW CONGRESS.

The California Democrat is all but guaranteed to hold the Oversight gavel in the next Congress and has already signaled that he intends to kick off the new year with an aggressive investigation schedule.

‘LAWYER UP’: DEMS IMMEDIATELY PUT TRUMP ON NOTICE AMID REPUBLICANS' MIDTERM BLOODBATH.

His committee will also be key to any potential impeachment efforts House Democrats take on in the next term.

In a recent development, other investigatory targets include former DHS Secretary Kristi Noem, former DHS senior advisor Corey Lewandowski, former White House chief of staff Susie Wiles, Attorney General Todd Blanche, Federal Bureau of Prisons Director William K. Marshall III and Trust for the National Mall CEO Catherine Townsend.

"Oversight Democrats have been investigating the potential corruption, self-dealing, and abuses of power that have funneled billions of dollars to the Trump family as Americans struggle with rising costs," letters to the president's two eldest sons read.

"While the Trump Administration’s failed policies have made life increasingly unaffordable for the public, you have engaged in business pursuits that appear to have allowed you to personally profit from your father’s presidency on a massive scale and at the expense of the American people.".

Rep. Robert Garcia, D-Calif., gives remarks to media outside the House Democratic Caucus meeting in the Longworth House Office Building on Nov. 10, 2026, in Washington, D.C. (Aaron Schwartz/Getty Images).

The letter to Miller reads in part, "Oversight Democrats have heard directly from the victims of the lawless conduct of President Trump’s mass deportation campaign and from whistleblowers appalled by agency behavior. There must be accountability for those responsible for orchestrating, carrying out, and covering up these abuses, at all levels of the agency.".

DEMS PIVOT FROM ‘AFFORDABILITY’ PITCH TO DHS PROBE THREATS AFTER MIDTERM WINS.

Democrats have positioned their investigative efforts as accountability measures long-sought by the American public. But Republicans have argued that it highlights Democrats are more interested in political retribution than working on solutions to lower concerns like the high cost of living that millions of Americans are dealing with.

Trump and his family have long denied any wrongdoing when it comes to their business interests.

And while Miller and Noem did face bipartisan criticism over the administration's crackdown on illegal immigration, the majority of Republicans have lined up behind the White House and maintained that it is what Americans voted for.

It's not immediately clear whether the investigatory targets will comply with House Democrats' demands to preserve documents and communications.

Republicans selected House Judiciary Committee Chairman Jim Jordan, R-Ohio, as their leader in the latest Congress and de facto top spokesman against Democrats' partisan activity.
