📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that historical simulation game Nobunaga’s Ambition: Hishou will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on March 12, 2027 in Japan and Asia for 11,880 yen, publisher Koei Tecmo and developer Kou Shibusawa announced. It will support Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

It has been revealed that pre-orders include the bonus scenario “Battle of Itsukushima,” which features the final battle between Sue Harukata’s forces and the great strategist Mori Motonari. Early purchases include the bonus scenario “Battle of Kawanakajima,” which features one of the major battles between the Tiger of Kai, Takeda Shingen, and the Dragon of Echigo, Uesugi Kenshin, as they aim to take control of Kawanakajima.

Here is an overview of the game, via Koei Tecmo:.

What is it that warriors fight for?

In a recent development, each warrior of the Warring States period had their own reasons for riding into battle—be it territory, social standing, the sake of humanity, or their own ambition. This title focuses on those reasons, with battles between evenly matched warriors and an exploration of the bonds between ally and enemy.

Enjoy a full revival of the city development portion of domestic affairs for the first time in nearly 20 years. Freely develop your own castle town, depicted in beautifully evolved graphics.

Experience story mode for the first time in series history. Both series fans and newcomers to the Warring States and historical simulation games can enjoy this story focused on the life of Oda Nobunaga.
