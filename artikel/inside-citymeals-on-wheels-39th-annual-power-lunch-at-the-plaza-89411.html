📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, in Newly introduced York City, a lunch reservation at The Plaza is always a good idea. But on Thursday afternoon, there was a particularly good reason to be there. Around 400 guests from the fashion, business, entertainment, and philanthropy worlds arrived at the storied hotel for Citymeals on Wheels’ 39th annual Power Lunch, raising more than $1.3 million to help feed older New Yorkers.

Recent findings suggest that this year’s honorees included Upcoming York City police commissioner Jessica Tisch, Imaginary Ventures co-founder Dame Natalie Massenet, and Today co-host Al Roker, each honored for their longtime support of Citymeals. Deborah Roberts, a Citymeals board member and ABC News correspondent, served as emcee.

A number of familiar faces from the city’s social scene turned out, including Karlie Kloss, Lauren Santo Domingo, Tamara Mellon, and Derek Blasberg. Event chairs Blasberg, Dr. Samantha Boardman, Margo M. Nederlander, and Lizzie Tisch helped bring the afternoon together, alongside Citymeals board co-presidents Anne Cohen and Julie Daum.

Sources indicate that of course, the most crucial number of the afternoon wasn’t the guest count. The funds raised will provide approximately 130,000 meals to older residents across the five boroughs, where Citymeals right now serves 22,000 people.

Recent findings suggest that for Citymeals CEO Beth Shapiro, the afternoon was a reminder of what a gathering like this can accomplish. “The generosity we saw in the room today is a powerful reminder of what’s possible when Upcoming Yorkers come together to care for one another,” she mentioned in a statement.
