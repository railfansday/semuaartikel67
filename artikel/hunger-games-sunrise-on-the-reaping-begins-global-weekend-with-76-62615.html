📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that EXCLUSIVE: Lionsgate‘s sixth Hunger Games move, The Hunger Games: The Sunrise on the Reaping, is off to a strong start with $24.6M through Thursday night. Add that to the expected $51M Friday, which includes $21M in previews, and the global running cume is around $76M. Already, that’s close to toppling the previous $100.1M worldwide start of 2023’s The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

The fifth Francis Lawrence directed movie in the Suzanne Collins franchise has opened to No. 1 in 66 out of 67 markets to date with another 17 territories set to bow this weekend. It would not be shocking if the movie files a global opening plus-or-minus $200M global. It has been revealed that on Wednesday, the Joseph Zada, McKenna Grace, Elle Fanning, Jesse Plemons, Glenn Close, Kieran Culkin ensemble pic bowed in France to No. 1 with 154,000 admissions or $1.4M, which was +157% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes. Watch on Deadline.

It has been revealed that in Scandinavia, Norway on Wednesday, Sunrise opened to No. 1 with $494,000, marking the largest opening day in the Hunger Games franchise locally. Sweden opened to No. 1 with $459,000, coming in +31% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes.

Industry observers note that belgium bowed to No. 1 as well with $343,000, the largest opening day locally for the feature take of the Collins’ novels.

On Thursday, Haymitch set down in Australia with a No. 1 $1.5M opening for the day, +137% ahead of Songbirds and Snakes. Germany was also No. 1 with $1M, +83% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes.

Lionsgate distributes their movies in Latin America with partner IDC. The series has always called at the B.O. there and combined all markets did close to $3M at No. 1. Of that, Mexico minted $1M, +107% ahead of Songbirds and Snakes, while Brazil grossed $679,000.

Italy’s first day was also molto bene, +79% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes, for a No. 1 of $680K.

It has been revealed that in Scandinavia, Denmark bowed to No. 1 with $461,000, +65% ahead of the previous 2023 chapter. Finland was also No. 1 with $205,000 +36% higher than the Tom Blyth and Rachel Zegler starring installment.

Netherlands charted No. 1 yesterday with $412,000, +66% over Songbirds & Snakes.

It has been revealed that in the Middle East, Sunrise on the Reaping bowed to first place in all markets with a combined $390,000. The UAE opened to $129,000, +135% ahead of Songbirds & Snakes. Saudi Arabia opened to $126,000 was also +155% ahead of the last Hunger Games movie.

According to recent reports, the net production cost of Sunrise on the Reaping is $150M, with a majority of that financed via foreign sales. The U.S. marketing spend is $60M, sources say. Lionsgate also directly distributes the movie in the United Kingdom.
