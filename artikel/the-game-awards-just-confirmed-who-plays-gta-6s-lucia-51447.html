Manni L. Perez has been confirmed as the actress behind Grand Theft Auto 6's Lucia Caminos, just days before the game launches.

As spotted by IGN, the confirmation came during The Game Awards' nominations livestream today, where Perez was nominated for Best Performance for her role as co-lead Lucia.

Perez had been widely rumoured to play Lucia for months, and fans have regularly filled her social media livestreams with questions about GTA 6, which she has been oh-so-carefully sidestepping.

Lucia is one half of GTA 6's lead pair alongside Jason Duval, making it the first Grand Theft Auto game with a playable couple.

Jason's actor wasn't among the nominees, so who plays him is still a mystery.

Perez has previously had guest roles in a string of US TV dramas, including Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Chicago P.D., The Blacklist, and Blindspot, and has also done voice work for GTA Online.

Funnily enough, Lucia isn't her first Lucia - she also played Lucia Santiago in a 2023 episode of CBS's East Latest York.

GTA 6 arrives 19th November on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. It is now available to pre-load for anyone who pre-ordered the digital version, and those with the code-in-box physical version.

It has been revealed that it's long been revealed that GTA 6 will have a regional release, meaning that some lucky, soon-to-be players will be able to access the game much earlier than others. Rockstar's regional release schedule sees the game launching at midnight on 19th November regardless of what region you're in, and if you wish to join those playing almost a day early, there's the good ol' Xbox Upcoming Zealand trick for eager players to try.
