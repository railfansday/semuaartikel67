It has been revealed that trying the latest and greatest running shoes is an expensive hobby, but sometimes you get lucky and find a bargain on top-tier kicks. Today is one of those days: You can score the Adidas Hyperboost Edge, the brand’s latest super trainer, on sale for up to 25 percent off.

The Hyperboost Edge is on sale at Adidas for $160—that’s 20 percent off its $200 MSRP. The shoe is on sale in both men’s and women’s in several colorways, and plenty of sizes are in stock (for now). Adidas has the best size and colorway selection, but you can also find this shoe for just $150 in men’s at Nordstrom.

Recent findings suggest that the Hyperboost Edge is a big shoe with bold engineering, and it’s designed for long training runs up to marathon length or even longer. It features a towering 45mm stack height, and the midsole is made with pelletized PEBA, which Adidas calls Hyperboost Pro. There’s no midsole plate or other stiffening tech, just a thick block of this soft, exceptionally bouncy foam. It’s topped with a durable Primeknit upper (more on that in a moment).

In a recent development, overall, RW testers and editors loved the Hyperboost Edge. Editor Amanda Furrer called it “near perfect” and noted it’s a “go-to long-run shoe.” Its pillowy, responsive ride helped her shave a full minute off her usual pace on a 10-mile run. And it’s not just for plodding through high-mileage workouts, either: Testers mentioned it feels so great, you can even wear it casually.

“It’s responsive enough for easy mileage, durable enough to handle regular training, and comfortable enough that you could leave it on all day without thinking twice,” mentioned tester Dee Koutsourais.

The upper, however, did cause some problems, namely blisters and hot spots across the top of the foot. It’s an unfortunate drawback to such a high-performance shoe, but even so, the Hyperboost Edge still earned a 2026 Shoe Award for its unique tech and sublime ride. It sets a upcoming standard for super trainers.

Now’s the perfect time to try it: Snag your pair for up to 25 percent off at Adidas or Nordstrom.
