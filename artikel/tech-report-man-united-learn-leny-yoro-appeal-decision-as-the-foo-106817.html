Manchester United will have Leny Yoro available for Saturday's Premier League clash against Brentford, with his one-game suspension being removed.

Yoro was sent off in the early stages of Sunday's Premier League clash with Liverpool following a last-man challenge on Reds forward Alexander Isak.

The France international was facing a one-game suspension, ruling him out of Man United's next Premier League fixture against Brentford.

However, the ban has now been removed following a successful appeal.

"Leny Yoro’s one-match suspension has been removed following a successful claim of wrongful dismissal," read a statement from the Football Association.

It has been revealed that "The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November. The independent Regulatory Commission’s written reasons for its decision will be published in due course.".

There had been a lot of debate surrounding whether the foul was a clear denial of a goalscoring opportunity, as Isak appeared to lose possession of the ball.

Man United head coach Mauricio Pochettino and midfielder Kobbie Mainoo both criticised the decision in their post-match comments.

In a recent development, "The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, [Yoro] never had the intention to connect with Isak," stated Pochettino.

"When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper.".

In a recent development, mainoo, meanwhile, noted: "The game's been taken out of our hands by a red card that I don't think can be a red card. No chance. The ball's already with the goalkeeper.".

Yoro is right now with the Man United squad in Portugal preparing for Wednesday's Champions League fixture with Sporting Lisbon.
