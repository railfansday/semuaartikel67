Recent findings suggest that amazon just gave its Kindle lineup a major makeover, and the smallest model might be the most exciting. Earlier this month, the company introduced a redesigned 6-inch Kindle that's more compact than any e-reader owners have seen before. It’s got a sleek upcoming look, faster performance, and four gorgeous colors that make the popular device feel like a cute accessory in its own right. After spending some time with the new Alpine Sky version, I can confidently say that its tiny size is one of my favorite things about it.

Industry observers note that the new Kindle is officially Amazon's most pocket-sized e-reader yet, featuring a redesigned flush-front display that eliminates the raised border around the screen. The 6-inch, 300-ppi glare-free display is still black and white, but the device itself now has color that stretches seamlessly from front to back. Amazon also says the new Kindle opens books 30 percent faster than its predecessor and offers up to six weeks of battery life on a single charge, which has been ideal for me, someone who forgets to charge any of his devices.

It has been revealed that one of the biggest updates is the introduction of two different versions of the 6-inch Kindle. The standard model starts at $149.99 and comes in Ube (a pretty purple shade) and classic Graphite, with 16 GB of storage. For $189.99, you can upgrade to the latest Kindle Aluminum, which comes in Alpine Sky and Seaglass Green, with a premium matte aluminum back, 32GB of storage, and no lock-screen ads.

Sources indicate that I have the Alpine Sky version, which has a beautiful, soft blue finish that always gets compliments from friends. But beyond the newly introduced colors and materials, I've appreciated most how easy this little thing is to bring everywhere. I live in New York City, so I'm constantly taking the subway, and carrying a physical book around isn't always convenient. It’s not impossible, but it can be a nuisance when I'm reading something particularly thick. The Kindle is smaller than most of the books I own, takes up little room in my bag, and feels comfortable to hold for longer reading sessions without making my hand ache.

I also love that I can throw it into pretty much any bag I'm carrying, whether it's a small everyday bag or something much larger. And, admittedly, there's something funny about watching other commuters on the subway notice it. I swear I can see people clocking the tiny blue Kindle and thinking how cute it is (but that could just be my main-character energy). The text is still plenty readable despite its smaller display, and sometimes I feel like the person sitting next to me is following along with whatever chapter I'm on.

It has been revealed that of course, the smaller Kindle doesn't have every feature you'll find on Amazon's pricier models. It lacks waterproofing and adjustable warm lighting like the Kindle Paperwhite, so readers who prioritize those features may prefer the larger option. But if you want a convenient, portable way to read, there's plenty to appreciate here.

In a recent development, the newly introduced 6-inch Kindle and Kindle Aluminum are available to shop now, with prices starting at $149.99. Whether you're new to e-reading or just want something smaller for your commute, Amazon's latest release makes a pretty convincing case for carrying an entire library in your bag.
