EFL pundits Callum Fowler and Jimmy Smith have issued a warning to the Plymouth Argyle hierarchy after Tom Cleverley's side were defeated 2-0 by Peterborough United at Home Park without the attacking quality of Canadian forward, Bim Pepple.

Argyle's positive start to the League One season has hit stumbling block, with the home defeat to Robbie Savage's side following a heavy 3-0 defeat to Sheffield Wednesday at Hillsborough.

Harry Leonard's goal put the seal on things for Posh after his namesake, Harry Ashfield, opened the scoring midway through the first period.

Other results meant the Green Army dropped to seventh in the table, having taken 23 points from their opening 16 matches.

However, the growing concern surrounding Cleverley's men is their transfer strategy and lack of potential strength-in-depth, with the former Manchester United and England midfielder opting for a front two of Will Evans and Caleb Watts, with Pepple absent for just a third time this campaign.

Pepple moved to Home Park from Luton Town last summer in a deal which could rise as high as £600k, with the former Chesterfield loanee netting 16 times in just 35 League One appearances for the Pilgrims last term as Cleverley's men narrowly missed out on the play-offs.

The 23-year-old has scored five times so far this term, but was sidelined before the defeat to Sheffield Wednesday with a muscular problem.

Regardless, Cleverley backed his fellow attackers to step up to the plate and provide goals, but with blanks fired in the past 180 minutes' worth of action, questions have arisen on this particular front.

Pepple was one half of Argyle's formidable attacking duo last season, with Lorent Tolaj finding the net 21 times in his 35 appearances for the club, before the Swiss youth international was sold to Bristol City in a £4m deal.

Plymouth then sought to replace the 25-year-old with Evans, who is yet to replicate his prior Mansfield Town exploits after a £400,000 agreement, having netted just three times whilst starting in all of his newly introduced employers' 16 league encounters.

Discussing a potential overreliance on Pepple, Fowler stated on the Pyramid podcast: "They've got some good players, but they're screaming out for a 'proper' forward.

"Pepple didn't play in this (game). He's not there.

It has been revealed that "So, from what were your starting strikers last season in Tolaj and Pepple, both brilliant for this level. They've got Evans and Watts," he added.

"That's where your problem is, it's a lack of quality," Fowler claimed.

"I do think Plymouth's owners are trying to be as sustainable as possible.

"If you continue to sell your best players and try and replace them with a cheap option, you will just go down and down and down.

Sources indicate that "Plymouth aren't completely out of this run-in. Obviously, it depends on how long Pepple's out for, but it is a bit concerning losing 2-0 at home to Peterborough.".

The concerns raised by Fowler and Smith are particularly valid, both in the short and long-term, as it remains to be seen when Pepple returns from injury, as well as where he is playing his football in the coming months.

Several Championship sides have been credited with interest in his services, as well as Scottish Premiership side, Celtic, within the past 12 months, and it would be no surprise if the forward wanted to test himself regularly at second tier level after just 13 prior appearances.

With Evans and Watts only scoring five between them so far this season, it is apparent that Argyle may need to reinvest further in January if they are to keep pace with the many promotion hopefuls currently in League One, as only nine points separate Huddersfield Town in second and 13th-placed Mansfield right now.
