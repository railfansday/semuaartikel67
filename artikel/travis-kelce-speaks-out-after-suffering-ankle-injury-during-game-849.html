Travis Kelce is shaking off his latest injury.

The Kansas City Chiefs tight end dismissed concern over the ankle injury that left him temporarily sidelined during his team's 35-13 victory over the Atlanta Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta Nov. 15.

According to recent reports, "I'm all good," Kelce told USA Today after the game. "I probably could have went more than I did when I came back, but it was the direction the team wanted to go, so I was right there with them.".

"We had a lot of young guys step up," he added, "so it was really nice.".

The 37-year-old—who had injured his right ankle during the Chiefs' Oct. 18 victory over the Los Angeles Chargers—was slow to get up after being tackled by DeMarcco Hellams following his reception of quarterback Patrick Mahomes' 23-year pass during the game's first quarter.

After limping to the sideline, Kelce's ankle was taped by the medical team before he made his way to the locker room for additional evaluation.

And though the Chiefs officially listed Kelce's return to play as questionable, he rejoined the game during the second quarter.

The Upcoming Heights podcast host—who married Taylor Swift in July—made sure to shout out Mahomes for his best performance in a game in four years, which saw him throwing for 348 yards for a total of five touchdowns.

"That’s our fearless warrior, man," Kelce said. "He’s always going to be in position to lead us to victory. That’s why we like having him.".

Ultimately, it was an eventful day on the field for the Chiefs, which Kelce viewed as a net positive for his team.

According to recent reports, “There were a lot of questions, wasn’t there?” he noted. “We got some answers.”.
