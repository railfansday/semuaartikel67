Melissa Gilbert expressed her gratitude for the support she and her family received while her husband, Timothy Busfield, faced allegations of child sex abuse.

Taking to Instagram on Thursday, November 26, Gilbert, 62, shared a message of thanks following a challenging year.

“This Thanksgiving I’m incredibly grateful for all those who came running toward us this year,” she wrote alongside an image of a turkey surrounded by fruits and vegetables. “To those who checked in, took care of our home and animals when we were away, came to hearings, fed us, housed us, spoke up, testified, wrote letters, listened, comforted us, held our hands and checked in regularly, we love you.”.

According to recent reports, she continued, “Our undying gratitude also, to our extraordinary team who bravely fought and continue to fight for so hard for what is right and true. On this day, as we all acknowledge the blessings in our lives, we send our love and eternal gratitude to each and every one of you. #thanksgiving #gratitude #blessed.”.

Last month, a Fresh Mexico judge granted Busfield’s motion to quash his indictment on four counts of criminal sexual contact of a minor.

It has been revealed that “The prosecuting attorney failed to act in a fair and impartial manner, including the failure to properly instruct the grand jury on the law for calling witnesses and in instructing the grand jurors on a procedure that effectively withheld requested witnesses,” the judge wrote in court documents, citing prosecutorial misconduct and errors that compromised the legal process. “In doing so, the grand jury proceedings were conducted in a manner that violated grand jury statutes designed to protect the structural integrity of our grand jury system and the presumption of prejudice applies.”.

However, the state remains free to institute latest criminal proceedings against Busfield, 69, per the filing.

It has been revealed that in response to the judge’s verdict, Busfield’s attorney told Us, “Tim said from the beginning that he was innocent. That he would fight to prove his innocence. And he did.”.

Gilbert has been supportive of Busfield, who was first indicted on child sex abuse charges by a grand jury in February.

The actor was accused of touching a child actor inappropriately in incidents that allegedly occurred on the set of The Cleaning Lady between late 2022 and early 2024, according to the Albuquerque Police Department affidavit obtained by Us. (The West Wing actor pleaded not guilty to the charges.).

Recent findings suggest that shortly after her husband’s arrest, a rep for Gilbert remarked in a statement, “During this period, her focus is on supporting and caring for their very large family as they navigate this moment. Melissa stands with and supports her husband and will address the public at an appropriate time.”.

Appearing on Good Morning America in April, Gilbert further defended Busfield against the claims.

Industry observers note that “[He’s] the last person in the world who would hurt a child. And believe me, if I thought for a second that Tim Busfield hurt a child, he’d have a lot more to worry about than prison,” Gilbert remarked at the time. “I am 100 percent confident he will be exonerated.”.
