Bitcoin , Ethereum and XRP Price Crash Ahead – Analyst Warns

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin, Ethereum, and XRP are at risk of another sharp fall as key support levels begin to break. With the crypto market cap near $2.08 trillion, analyst Ali Martinez has issued a blunt warning to traders: “Prepare for the worst.”.

Here are the major support levels to watch for BTC, ETH, and XRP.

Industry observers note that bitcoin is leading the overall weakness, falling about 7.58% from $63,652 to $58,701. Ali Martinez is focusing on the $59,324 cost-basis area, where roughly 400,000 BTC were traded.

That level is notable because a sustained close below $56,800 could leave Bitcoin with far less on-chain support. The next major cost-basis zones sit much lower at $46,700, involving about 103,750 BTC, and $37,867, where roughly 198,470 BTC changed hands.

The chart also shows that some buyers are preparing for that possibility. Large whale orders are stacked between $50,000 and $52,000, creating a potential demand zone if Bitcoin falls further.

In a recent development, the pressure is already visible in institutional flows. U.S.-listed Bitcoin ETFs recorded about $1.8 billion in weekly outflows for the fourth consecutive week, adding to the selling pressure around BTC.

Recent findings suggest that ethereum is showing a similar pattern. ETH is trading near $1,642, about 67% below its August 2025 record of roughly $4,946, and has already slipped below the $1,680 area where about 1.14 million ETH have a cost basis.

Martinez sees $1,584 as the next important level.

A break below it could expose $1,237, where roughly 1.37 million ETH were acquired, followed by $1,089, linked to about 967,750 ETH.

Sources indicate that that weakness is also appearing in corporate treasuries. On 24 November, Ethereum treasury BTCS sold 27,300 ETH for $43.8 million to cut Aave debt by about 81%, while CoinShares sold its entire ETH holdings for $16.6 million to fund a share buyback.

Meanwhile, Veteran trader Peter Brandt has separately warned that an Ethereum break below $1,383 could open a path toward $500.

If $ETH cannot remain above 1383 I see no reason why price cannot go back to sub-$500 pic.twitter.com/OT9hQ3lBFU.

Recent findings suggest that XRP is facing its own pressure point at $0.84. About 833 million XRP have a cost basis there, while the biggest cluster of net longs was opened around $0.982. With XRP near $0.923, most of those positions are already underwater.

If $0.84 fails, the next cost-basis zone is near $0.62, involving about 1.11 billion XRP.

Below that sits $0.36, the largest concentration on the chart, with about 6.57 billion XRP changing hands.
