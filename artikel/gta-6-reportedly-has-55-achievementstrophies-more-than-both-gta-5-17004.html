📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, GTA 6 is due to release on the 19th November on PS5, and Xbox Series X/S, and ahead of the highly-anticipated launch of Rockstar's latest sandbox adventure, people just can't get enough information about the game. To that end, overnight, we saw our latest leak: the game will have 55 Trophies/Achievements.

It has been revealed that in a now-deleted tweet from (in)famous Rockstar dataminer, TexFunz2 - re-posted by the Twitter account iGrandTheftAuto - it appears the game will have 55 individual rewards for completing certain tasks and missions. At the time of writing, we don't know specifically what these challenges are, but it seems none of them will be attached to the online version of the game; the base game launches without GTA Online, and so we can safely assume that all these rewards will be for the single-player only content. GTA Online will drop sometime in 2027, according to Twitch's CEO.

Sources indicate that if you've been paying attention to previous Rockstar titles, you'll know that Grand Theft Auto 5 debuted with 51 Achievements/Trophies - again, all single-player - and Red Dead Redemption 2 launched with 52. So the latest game has more than either game got at launch - and I can tell you from experience, getting Platinum in either title was a very time-consuming experience. Expect the same to be true here, too.

It has been revealed that it's worth highlighting that GTA 5 did, eventually, get more Achievements and Trophies: the GTA Online content upped the complete total to 78, adding 27 new challenges to complete. I would expect GTA 6 to do the same… whenever the PC version of GTA comes out, or GTA Online lands in the new game (it's possible both could happen at the same time).

According to recent reports, recently, we heard why a former Rockstar Games tech director thinks GTA 6 could be coming to PC sooner than you'd expect (mostly thanks to AI clones), when we can expect the pre-load of the game (10th November), and how big the final game install is expected to be (210GB). The final game is expected to be so big and dense with detail that no single Rockstar employee has seen the entire game, meaning there are plenty of easter eggs and ridiculous things to discover.
