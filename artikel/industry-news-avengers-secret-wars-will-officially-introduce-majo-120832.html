The Marvel Cinematic Universe is going deeper into the comic lore.

In a new interview with Entertainment Weekly, Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige revealed some new details about Avengers: Secret Wars, strongly hinting at the MCU's take on Battleworld. The executive shared, “Secret Wars is different than any other movie we’ve ever made. Look at the comics. The characters and the settings and the worlds within this singular planet where our movie Secret Wars takes place is the imagination gone wild, which is what’s exciting about it.".

Recent findings suggest that he elaborated on how audience members can turn to the comics if they want more hints at where the MCU film is going. Feige stated, "If you wanted spoilers for The Odyssey, you could go read the book. If you want spoilers for Doomsday and Secret Wars, you can go look at the comics. It is very much the same thing that we’re doing, which is the fun of what happens in the culmination of an incursion, multiversal-collapse event, and what Doctor Doom does next. And that very much is what Secret Wars is.”.

Avengers: Secret Wars opens in theaters on December 17, 2027.
