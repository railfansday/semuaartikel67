Industry News: The dos and don'ts of using AI for travel

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (161 words, 📖 Standard)
  • AI tools are increasingly used for travel planning, from researching destinations to managing loyalty programs and building itineraries.
  • Travelers can leverage AI for comparing hotel locations, understanding travel requirements and maximizing points or miles without sharing sensitive account details.
  • Verifying AI-generated advice is essential, as inaccuracies can lead to inconvenience or missed opportunities during trips.
  • Sharing passwords or sensitive data with AI tools is discouraged due to privacy and security risks.
  • Not all travel and financial account providers permit AI agent access; check for authorized integrations before connecting accounts.
  • AI tools may retain or use your data for purposes such as model training, so review privacy settings and understand how your information is handled.
  • Always require explicit approval before allowing AI to make purchases or change reservations on your behalf.
