📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Dancing With the Stars operates on a pretty simple scoring system that pretty much boils down to one part judges and one part audience. But pro Sharna Burgess thinks fans are using their online votes to manipulate the show and vote off talented dancers that shouldn’t be going home.

It has been revealed that speaking on Instagram Stories after Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson were eliminated, Sharna remarked (per E! News), “The online discourse is so wild that [it] was pretty clear who fans were ‘voting off’ and how they have felt about dance experience contestants.”.

“[It’s] so disappointing to see our voting system is now being used by viewers to vote people off instead of voting for your favorite,” she added. “I say we got [sic] back to yall only have 10 votes total and you have to figure out how to dish them out. This voting ppl off thing is so insanely negative and not what we are built on and it’s seemingly given fans the chance to manipulate the show. JMO.”.

There’s been a lot of discourse about the Dancing With the Stars voting system in light of Jenna Dewan and Harry Shum Jr. both going home despite delivering flawless routines and getting high judges’ scores. Jenna even alluded to the online discourse playing a role in her elimination, telling Extra, “I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it. I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”.

Dancing With the Stars producers haven’t addressed voting concerns or indicated that they’re making any changes, but stay tuned.
