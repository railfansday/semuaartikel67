Boxing, golf, and football reign supreme in this week’s Gospel Stats Weekly Brand Report, with sponsorships from DAZN, DraftKings, Lowe’s, and more.

Industry observers note that #1 The Boy Who Punched Back ?? Channel: Zack D. Films Brand: DAZN Views: 13,632,976.

Recent findings suggest that zack D. Films‘ DAZN promo starts like a Dhar Mann video: A kid, relentlessly bullied, finally has enough and stands up to his schoolyard oppressors.

Recent findings suggest that the Short keeps this inspirational tone, but it swings toward sports. Viewers learn the “kid” is actually world champion boxer Canelo Álvarez, and that after spending years at the top of his sport, he was defeated. Now, at 36, he wants to reclaim one of his old titles–World Boxing Council super middleweight champion–and to clinch it, he has to go up against undefeated young gun Christian Mbilli.

Recent findings suggest that and, of course, that match can only be watched on DAZN. The sports-specific streamer has been in our space since the Logan Paul vs KSI days, and continues to partner with creators for special events.

#2 64 Golfers Compete For 4 Million Dollars | Internet Invitational Episode 1 Channel: Fore Play Golf, Bob Does Sports, Barstool Sports, Internet Invitational Brand: Reese’s, DraftKings, Dunkin, ZYRTEC Views: 12,095,382.

And video #1 isn’t the only sports championship in the cards this week.

Fore Play Golf, Bob Does Sports, and Barstool Sports brought 64 creator golfers together for the Internet Invitational, which will pit them against each other on the green until just one puts their way to victory.

Episode 1 of this tournament is over five hours long–which is a lot of airtime for sponsors Reese’s, DraftKings, Dunkin, and ZYRTEC. We’re obviously intrigued by the tourney’s player lineup (which includes Dude Perfect‘s Tyler Toney, former Good Good-er Grant Horvat, TikToker Josh Richards, and Nelk Boys member Salim, with Barstool’s Dave Portnoy and pro golfer Kevin Kisner as rules officials), but we’re also intrigued by the brand choices. There’s some hefty companies here.

It has been revealed that draftKings is basically a ubiquitous presence these days no matter which sport you’re watching–or which YouTube video. It paid for more than 80 videos this week alone. Then there’s Zyrtec, which we supposes connects here because these golfers are getting snootfuls of grass out on the green. (This is the only video Zyrtec sponsored.) Dunkin and Reese’s round out the snacks-and-drinks set, and prove once again that major food n bev brands are firmly committed to spending their marketing budgets in our space.

#3 Making the worlds most extreme candy (it short-circuits my brain) Channel: NileBlue Brand: Brilliant Views: 12,475,822.

He’s one of our few non-sports entrants this week, but NileBlue is still getting into the ring–and putting his tongue on the line.

His thesis is simple: candies like Warheads are trying to give the eater a truly unpleasant experience. But none of them are quite overwhelming enough.

“I think this is mostly because they’re all trying to only assault one thing at a time,” Nile says. “Which personallly, I think is a mistake, because it leaves so many completely available tastebuds completely unaffected. And what I really want is a candy that targets everything.”.

That means a candy which is sour, sweet, spicy, icy, bitter, salty, and umami to the max, all at the same time.

Can he succeed? Viewers who lock in for his full 38-minute journey will be rewarded with the answer (and sponsor Brilliant will be rewarded with their watch time).

According to recent reports, #4 LIVE with Lowes playing Madden 27, featuring Justin Jefferson! #ad Channel: MMG Brand: Lowe’s Views: 11,333,221.

Our final sports video of the week is from OG Madden YouTuber MMG, who’s joined by Minnesota Vikings wide receiver Justin Jefferson for a chat and a few rounds in Madden 27.

The YouTube world and the sports world are becoming increasingly intertwined (just look at the above creator invitational, for example), so it’s not as unusual to see athletes popping up on creators’ channels, but it’s still cool every time. MMG introduced his channel in 2015, and after 11 years of work, he’s sitting pretty with NFL stars.

According to recent reports, this stream was part of the Lowe’s “Earn Your Sunday” campaign, which urges dudes to get their sh*t done around the house so they can kick back and watch the weekend games. As part of the deal, MMG gave away a signed Jefferson helmet and jersey; viewers who head over to the Lowe’s website will also see another, bigger giveaway for the “ultimate Super Bowl Sunday.” Lowe’s is definitely putting stock into making the end of this NFL season a touchdown.

BONUS #2,410 I Played Skyrim as a Lore Accurate Vampire Channel: OSK Gaming Brand: Idle Obelisk Miner Views: 124,561.

In honor of everyone who waited 15 years for a name reveal, we brought in OSK Gaming and his very dedicated Skyrim playthrough as our bonus video.

Bethesda Game Studios boss Todd Howard has a contentious reputation among gamers, and OSK has another gripe for the pile: He’s pretty sure the ol’ Todd hates vampires. So what’s a guy to do if he wants to play a real vampire within the sprawling fantasy world of Skyrim? You download a boatload of mods and do it yourself. Who cares if Skyrim won’t let you be a real vampire? OSK is gonna make it happen anyway.

Sources indicate that on his journey, he’ll actually be playing two games, thanks to his sponsor Idle Obelisk Miner. As you may expect from the name, IOM is an idle title, where you get set up and then all you have to do is check in on things every once in a while. What you might not expect is that one game title would sponsor a video about another completely unrelated game. But this is the perfect pairing. While OSK hikes across Skyrim, IOM runs in the background, so he can game while he games.
