City made just one change to the starting XI that had thumped Hassocks 6-0 in midweek.

Skipper Rob Hutchings began at left-back with Zac Jeaneson the other side of Ben Pashley and Will Sweet in the heart of the defence. Max Evans was between the posts and Joe Moore, Lucas Pattenden, Isaac Bello, Joe Clarke and Emmett Dunn started in midfield with Harry Bridle up front.

Ethan Prichard, Olly Munt, Finn Bello, Lloyd Rowlatt and Brandon Chebby warmed the bench.

Dunn brought down Dan Bayliss for a ‘Stoke free kick and Kristian Shevchenko, son of Dynamo Kyiv, AC Milan and Chelsea legend Andriy Shevchenko, was flagged offside.

Basingstoke’s Amardeep Singh blazed over the bar before Pashley blocked a shot and another one was diverted for the first corner of the afternoon. Joe O’Loughlin took it and Shevchenko headed wide.

Another shot went off target in the ninth minute as Chi struggled to get going, but the hosts won their first corner 60 seconds later and Jeanes fired one wide.

Hutchings earned City a set-piece up against Okine-Peters and Pattenden and Jeanes hooked up for a cross that Bello couldn’t profit from, and Pattenden had a shot blocked.

Clarke headed away a free kick after Hutchings fouled Shevchenko and O’Loughlin delivered it into the box.

Basingstoke keeper Tyla Dickinson made a mistake on 16 minutes but no one was there to capitalise.

Evans claimed Scott Armsworth’s header before a dangerous cross was headed out for a Lillywhites’ goal kick and Stoke captain Billy Upton missed an opportunity.

Thomas Blake was replaced by James Henry for the visitors, and a Bello bomb of a throw was launched after Pattenden battled with O’Loughlin, although Dickinson collected at the second time of asking.

Skipper Hutchings’ cross was claimed by Dickinson on the half hour and Singh was given the first yellow card for taking out Hutchings. A poor cross from Pattenden didn’t cause the visitors any trouble before referee Joshua Langley-Fineing sent Shevchenko off for raising an arm on Hutchings in the 37th minute.

Bridle couldn’t pick out Bello and Pattenden won a corner that Hutchings took, and Sweet turned the loose ball in to put City one up on 41. Bridle struck a shot off target as the half petered out.

The City striker had another chance soon after the break before the away side equalised on 51 through Okine-Peters at close range from a cross via the left wing.

Chi won a couple of corners and Bridle somehow had an effort saved following good work between Bello and Pattenden.

Sources indicate that the away side might have taken the lead after a Pashley slip but Evans blocked Okine-Peters’ shot. Then a super header from Dunn put Chi in front on the hour – his third goal of the week after two at Hassocks.

Jack Ball pulled an attempt wide in the 65th minute; and Dunn headed wide. Three minutes later ‘Stoke delivered a corner too deep.

Bayliss was booked for dissent and Hutchings got done on 76 and the visitors earned a corner that was headed away. A calamity led to the equaliser as Okine-Peters made it all square.

Chebby came on for Jeanes as Miles Rutherford and co rejigged, and Bello dropped back into defence.

The Chi sub was outmuscled by D’Andre Brown, and a penalty claim on Stoke sub Cheick Sylla was waved away by the referee.

Munt replaced Moore and a header from Clarke was deflected for a City corner.

Evans saved a speculative effort from long distance at the other end and somehow denied Henry with a brilliant save as we entered five extra minutes.

Chinedu Agu was booked for pulling back Huthchings and Bello did well to prevent Agu getting in on 93 minutes.

Ball entered the ref’s notepad just before full-time.
