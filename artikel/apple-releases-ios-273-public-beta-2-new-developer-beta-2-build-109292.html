Industry observers note that apple has released iOS 27.3 public beta 2 for iPhone. Today’s release also includes a fresh build for iOS 27.3 beta 2 for developers. The revision brings new Calendar controls, more Siri AI capabilities, and new features for iPhone Duo. It also includes the redesigned Health app and other changes introduced earlier this month.

The patch follows yesterday’s second developer beta and the first public beta on November 5.
