Can Instagram reduce prejudice? Study reveals the potential and limits of social media contact

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, A research team at the University of Potsdam has conducted a field experiment to investigate whether contact through social media can reduce prejudice. The findings suggest that following an unfamiliar Instagram profile for just three days can lead to more favorable attitudes—not only toward the group represented by the profile. The study was published in the Journal of the Association for Information Systems.
