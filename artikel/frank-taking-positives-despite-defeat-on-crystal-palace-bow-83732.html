Thomas Frank believed his Crystal Palace team were worthy of a point in their defeat to Coventry City, with the new Eagles boss pleased with what he saw on his debut.

Frank's reign got off to a far-from-ideal start as Coventry took the lead inside five minutes through Taiwo Awoniyi, but Palace responded impressively through Yeremy Pino.

Tatsuhiro Sakamoto's stunner restored Coventry's advantage, but Jorgen Strand Larsen, who teed up Pino's goal, got himself on the scoresheet in first-half stoppage time.

With the second half failing to replicate the drama of the first, the contest was decided by a cruel twist of fate when Dwight McNeil turned into his own net in the 85th minute.

Palace have now lost their last five Premier League games, leaving them at the bottom of the table, but Frank is confident he has the tools to turn things around.

Recent findings suggest that "I think we could have got a point. I also think we easily could have won it," Frank told Premier League Productions.

It has been revealed that "The big positive was that we created a lot of chances and scored two goals. I think the mentality and the effort, the passion of the players was very, very good.".

Frank's comments came after a whirlwind week, after only being appointed on Monday and having little time to work with a squad short on confidence.

While encouraged by his side's response to going behind twice, Frank admitted Palace were too open at times during the first half, as their defensive woes continued.

"In the first half I think we gave away two massive chances. The first goal and then the one where they had a one-on-one with Dean [Henderson]," Frank added.

"Then they had a wondershot from the edge of the box.".

According to recent reports, palace have now conceded 27 goals in the Premier League this season. Only in 1927-28 in the Third Division South have they conceded as many or more after 11 games in a league campaign (28).

Meanwhile, in the opposite dugout, Frank Lampard hailed an "crucial result" for his Coventry side after securing all three points.

Coventry have now won four of their last seven Premier League games (L3), with no side scoring more goals in the competition since the beginning of October (12, level with Bournemouth), moving them up to 13th in the table.

"The spirit, the performance, individuals, the way they're playing with a belief on the pitch and in the stadium was a massive deal for us, and it got us a really important result at this point," Lampard mentioned.

The Sky Blues boss also reserved special praise for Sakamoto after the winger's superb first-half strike.

According to recent reports, "Amazing. He's in great form. The quality of the strike, his overall performance, his work ethic, he's an absolute pleasure. I'm so delighted to see him doing it at this level.

"His professionalism is a real standard for everyone else in the group. In this way, the way he's playing, it's like, go, he's got to be on the pitch.".
