Publisher Feardemic and developer LEAP Game Studios will release simulation horror game Retail Hell for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and Switch, the companies announced. A release date was not announced.

Retail Hell first released for PC via Steam on May 21.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:.

Welcome to Retail Hell, the most “beloved” convenience store in Purgatory, where nothing stays stocked, no customer is human, and every shift lasts forever.

You play as an overworked clerk stuck in a literal dead-end job. Each shift forces you to juggle supernatural customer service, collapsing inventory, and the unsettling mystery of how you ended up here.

Industry observers note that in Retail Hell, customer service is eternal. Good luck, management does not believe in refunds.
