AirTag-like trackers for Android and iPhone are everywhere nowadays, but Kodak’s new SlideTag steps it up with a built-in E-Ink display that can show off your photos, widgets, and more along with being able to track down your items.

According to recent reports, compatible with both the Android Find Hub and Apple Find My networks, the Kodak SlideTag is a tracker inspired by the golden era of Kodak photography. The tracker is built by Reto, the same tech giant that built the Kodak Chamera keychain digital camera.

SlideTag’s premise is relatively simple. You get a tracker with an E-Ink display and a frame in one of eight styles inspired by classic 35mm slides. Those styles are sold in a blind box format, meaning you don’t know what you’re getting until you open the box (unless you buy the full set). There’s also a ninth style that only demonstrates up in one out of every 64 boxes.

It has been revealed that the E-Ink display can show six-color versions of your photos, or it can display things like lists, calendars, and more. There’s enough storage on the tracker for a 12-photo slideshow, synced through the Kodak Memory Link app on Android and iOS.

In a recent development, connect instantly via Bluetooth to the KODAK Memory Link App. From live slideshows and custom QR codes to countdowns and dual-network tracking with Apple Find My and Google’s Find Hub, the KODAK SlideTag seamlessly integrates retro aesthetics into your everyday carry.

Kodak SlideTag is available today from Amazon.
