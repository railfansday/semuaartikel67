Sources indicate that go figure that the two superstars most often on this list are also carrying the two hottest teams in the NBA right now. The San Antonio Spurs haven't lost a game since Oct. 23, approaching a month of perfect basketball on a 12-game winning streak. Much of that is thanks to the play of Victor Wembanyama.

Industry observers note that right behind the Spurs are the Los Angeles Lakers, on an eight-game heater having not dropped a contest since Oct. 30. You know exactly who's responsible for the Lakers' impressive play (Austin Reaves!). No. It's Luka Dončić, who is chasing his first NBA MVP, leading the league in scoring with a crisp 39 points per game. If Luka keeps this pace, it would be the highest PPG average in a season since Wilt Chamberlain back in 1962-63 (44.83).

In a recent development, what does all of this mean for High Score fantasy basketball? Well, that if you were able to draft one of those two stars, you're likely doing pretty well as we head into Week 5. Let's take a look at the perfect lineup from Week 4 for High Score.

Here's a look at the top performers for High Score fantasy basketball from Week 4.

Cade Cunningham: The Wizards and Pistons played arguably the best game of the regular season, a 144-140 2OT thriller that Detroit escaped victorious. Cade posted a 52-point double-double with 11 assists, 5 rebounds and 2 stocks in the win. Clearly Cunningham wasn't happy with his 17-point outing in the prior game, also against Washington. This type of ceiling is nothing new for Cade, something we saw plenty last season. His 85 points were the third-highest fantasy output for High Score so far this season.

Recent findings suggest that luka Dončić: With the 81 points in Week 4, Dončić has hit at least that mark in each week for High Score this season. We saw the 90-point ceiling a few weeks back and the floor doesn't get much higher for the Lakers' leading scorer. Not only is Dončić lighting the fantasy basketball world on fire, but he has L.A. competing for the top seed in the West. At this rate, Luka is looking like the early-season MVP in both real-life and fantasy.

Victor Wembanyama: Perhaps the player with the best shot of topping Dončić is Wemby, who is second in terms of NBA MVP odds as we go into Week 5. The pair of MVP favorites make up five of the top six scores in High Score fantasy basketball this season. Wembanyama started the week hot with a 40-point double-double in which he added eight blocks for good measure. On Tuesday, we get a heavyweight bout when the Spurs and Wemby take on Cade and the Pistons.

It has been revealed that cooper Flagg: "He's only 19." Flagg's play should bring us back to the days of Jayson Tatum when he was a teenager in the NBA (Tatum is still 19, right?). The Mavericks' second-year star is also doing things we hadn't seen since Kobe Bryant entered the league back in the '90s. This is Flagg's second appearance on the High Score perfect lineup after he posted 81 points a few weeks ago. You get more variance with Flagg because Dallas has some games in which it isn't very competitive. But on weeks in which the Mavs and Flagg play 3-4 games, he should be able to get you to 70-80 fantasy points.

Giannis Antetokounmpo: Giannis and the Heat capped their Week 4 with an impressive 20-point win over the Pistons. The Greek Freak now has a double-double in all but three games this season. In that victory over Detroit, Antetokounmpo dropped a 34-14-8 line with 4 stocks. It was also his third straight week featured in the High Score perfect lineup. After going slightly overlooked in drafts, Giannis is trending toward being a top-five fantasy asset this season.

Industry observers note that franz Wagner: The Magic massive joins the likes of Ty Jerome, Jeremiah Fears and Keyonte George as outliers among the High Score perfect lineup. There's usually at least one every week. Not to say that Wagner doesn't deserve superstar status given his play this season, particularly with Paolo Banchero sidelined for most of the campaign. Wagner is averaging 24-7-8 and comfortably occupies the UTIL spot in this lineup thanks to 35-9-12 last week against the Bucks.
