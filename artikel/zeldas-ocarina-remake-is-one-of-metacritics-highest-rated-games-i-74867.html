It's been a huge year for the games industry with all sorts of heavy-hitters releasing over the past few months, and as we look towards the end of 2026, we're now checking in on Metacritic's highest-rated games of the year based on the user score.

While Grand Theft Auto 6 may have officially surpassed Ocarina's Remake as the highest-rated game of 2026 on the review aggregate website (and also matched the original N64 hit as the highest-rated critic Metascore of all time), it seems the classic outing on Switch 2 has won players' hearts worldwide.

Recent findings suggest that according to the latest patch from the site, Ocarina's Remake is presently sitting in the top highest-rated games (above 90), based on user score (it's on 9.2/10 at the time of writing). It's in good organization with other games like Resident Evil Requiem and Splatoon Raiders.

Obviously, there are all sorts of different factors in play here when it comes to user scores (and the system in place isn't necessarily perfect). For example, Ocarina's Remake has over 25,000 user reviews, whereas other titles may have around 100 reviews. Keep in mind, user scores are also subject to change over time.

And if you're wondering how GTA 6 has been received by players, it's currently sitting on a user score of 5.5/10 after more than 30,000 user reviews. Even with supposed review bombings, it might take some time before it wins over some players. Despite top marks from critics, this new entry in the GTA universe appears to have divided the fanbase in its opening week.

As attention shifts towards the end-of-year game awards, both Ocarina and GTA 6 have already been nominated for multiple industry awards.
