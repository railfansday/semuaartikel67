Joshua Zirkzee refused to blame the officials for Manchester United's failure to beat Sporting CP in the Champions League, instead urging his team-mates to find a response.

Mauricio Pochettino's wait for a first win in charge of United continued on Wednesday following a 2-2 draw with Sporting, though the encounter did not come without controversy.

Luis Suarez gave Sporting a 33rd-minute lead with a penalty, just three minutes after seeing a spot-kick saved by Senne Lammens at the Estadio Jose Alvalade.

United thought they had equalised through Amad Diallo before the break, but the goal was ruled out for a foul by Youri Tielemans when he appeared to win the ball cleanly.

The Red Devils then netted twice in the space of two minutes early in the second half, with Bruno Fernandes scoring a penalty before seeing his long-distance strike hit a divot and fly over the stunned Kaique.

But more drama would come, with Lammens failing to deal with a cross and allowing Suarez to score, but United were denied a late winner when Patrick Dorgu was adjudged to have fouled Kaique from a corner, with the decision confirmed by a VAR review.

Speaking to MUTV, Zirkzee remarked: "In my opinion, there were definitely some key decisions there that didn't really favour us.

"We shouldn't look at the referee, we should look at ourselves instead, but it definitely has an impact on us, you can't deny that.

"But the team that we have, we should come here and not look at the referee and make sure that we get the win, regardless of the decisions that he makes.".

United have the chance to put things right against Brentford in Pochettino's first game at Old Trafford, and Zirkzee acknowledged the importance of getting all three points.

"It's a major test," Zirkzee noted of Brentford. "The fans deserve a result and all we want is a result. We've got to dig ourselves out of the trenches.".

And Lewis-Potter's fine display, along with his performances this season, earned him a fresh five-and-a-half year deal with Brentford, which was declared earlier this week.

In 11 games this season, Lewis-Potter (four) has already beaten his best-ever scoring season in the Premier League (three in 36 apps in 2025-26 and three in 30 in 2023-24).
