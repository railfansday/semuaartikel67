Industry News: Airports, public transport shut down amid national strike in Belgium

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (64 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Belgium's prime minister on Monday set a fresh year-end deadline for his coalition government to agree a cost-cutting budget or face collapse. The announcement came as a national strike against the planned cuts grounded all flights in and out of Belgium. Public transport, schools and other public services also faced disruption. France 24's Dave Keating has the latest from Brussels.
