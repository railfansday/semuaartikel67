It has been revealed that this is a weekly feature that runs down the week’s Top 10 announced funding rounds in the U.S. Check out last week’s biggest funding deal roundup here.

Industry observers note that it was a massive week for tech startup exits, headlined by Anthropic pulling off its massive IPO. On the down side, however, tech stocks took a huge dive in the past few days. Should that negative sentiment and downward pressure persist, we can expect it to spill over into private markets.

For this past week, however, U.S. startups continued to secure sizable rounds, led by brain-computer interface company Science landing a fresh $400 million. Another seven American companies announced venture or growth funding of $100 million or more, and across the Atlantic, UK-based AI biotech startup Isomorphic Labs landed $5 billion, the largest financing of the week.

Industry observers note that 1. Science, $400M, neurotech: Science, a startup focused on brain-computer interface technology, and neural engineering, secured $400 million in Series D funding. The round brings total funding to date for the Alameda, California-based organization to more than $900 million.

It has been revealed that 2. Model ML, $250M, AI and fintech: Model ML, an AI platform for financial and professional services, closed on $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. General Atlantic and QED Investors led the round for the three-year-old company, which is based in Fresh York and London.

3. Galvanize Therapeutics, $130M, biotech: Redwood City, California-based Galvanize Therapeutics, a developer of pulsed electric field ablation technologies for oncology and chronic disease, picked up $130 million in Series D funding led by HealthQuest Capital.

4. General Flash, $128M, critical metals: General Flash, a developer of a new method to produce critical metals, announced that it has raised $128 million across seed and Series A rounds from a long list of investors.

5. Ramon.Space, $124M, space tech: Denver-based Ramon.Space, a developer of space computing infrastructure, pulled in $124M in Series B funding led by PSG.

Recent findings suggest that 6. Marrow Therapeutics, $120M, biotech: Watertown, Massachusetts-based Marrow Therapeutics, a developer of bone marrow-targeted therapies to treat severe blood disorders, confirmed its launch with a combined $173 million in Series A and B funding. Orbimed led its $120 million Series B round while Scion Life Sciences and venBio led the $53 million Series A.

Sources indicate that 7. Noeri, $110M, enterprise AI: Noeri, a developer of self-improving AI for enterprises and researchers, debuted with $110 million in initial funding. Menlo Ventures led the round for the Palo Alto-based startup, which was founded by Stanford University computer science professors.

Sources indicate that 8. Seawolf Therapeutics, $100M, biotech: San Diego-based Seawolf Therapeutics, a biotech startup developing a novel DNA delivery platform, emerged from stealth and announced that it has raised $100 million in funding to date, with Versant Ventures, MPM BioImpact and Novartis Venture Fund as lead investors.

Industry observers note that 9. BrightAI, $97M, physical AI: BrightAI, a startup developing a sensor, drone and robotics-driven platform to bring continuous observability to infrastructure-heavy industries like power, water, and energy, secured $97 million in latest funding. Smith Point Capital led the financing for the Silicon Valley-based company.

Recent findings suggest that 10. Vincue, $93M, vehicle intelligence platform: Kansas City-based Vincue, developer of a vehicle lifecycle intelligence platform, closed on $93 million in growth capital led by Wavecrest Growth Partners.
