Michigan online casino revenue rises 16.7% in October as US iGaming growth slows

It has been revealed that michigan's online casinos generated $305.3 million in adjusted gross receipts in October, up 16.7% from a year earlier. The double-digit gain comes as national iGaming growth cools, though Michigan's own pace has slowed from a year ago.

Michigan’s online casino market kept growing at a double-digit rate in October. Gross receipts came to $318.1 million, according to figures the Michigan Gaming Control Board released Nov. 18. It was the state’s second-best iGaming month of 2026. Only March, with a record $309.1 million in AGR, was higher. March also holds the monthly gross receipts record of $322.1 million, which topped the $315.8 million from December 2025.

Industry observers note that the October result followed a soft patch. September AGR was $288.15 million, 0.9% lower than in August.

In a recent development, the year-over-year gain stands out against the national backdrop. Citizens JMP Securities estimated that US iGaming revenue grew 12% in the third quarter of 2026, the slowest pace it has recorded. Growth had been 19% in the first quarter and 16% in the second. The same data put Michigan’s third-quarter iGaming growth at 17%. Only Delaware, at 36%, and West Virginia, at 26%, grew faster.

Michigan’s own pace has still slowed sharply. In October 2025, online casino AGR grew 31.8% year over year, nearly double this October’s rate. That month produced $261.7 million in AGR and $278.5 million in gross receipts, a record at the time.

Industry observers note that the Michigan figures also invite comparison with Pennsylvania. Its online casinos generated $278,845,570 in October revenue, a state record for Pennsylvania. That figure came in a release issued Nov. 17, a day before Michigan published its October numbers.

The year-over-year numbers show where Michigan’s growth comes from. Online sports betting AGR fell 3.3% from October 2025. For all of 2025, sportsbooks produced $435.9 million in AGR, while iGaming generated $3.1 billion in gross receipts. Online casino play therefore accounts for most of the state’s online gambling revenue.

October sportsbook figures swung sharply from one month to the next. Sports betting AGR jumped 91.3% from September. Handle rose 11.1% to $605.4 million, and sportsbook gross receipts reached $70.7 million. Combined online casino and sports betting gross receipts totaled $388.8 million.

The broader online market had been growing faster earlier in the year. From January through July, combined online casino and sports betting AGR totaled $2.21 billion, up 20.9% from the same period in 2025. October’s casino growth is consistent with a market that is still expanding, but at a slower rate than a year ago.

Tribal online operators also made $8.9 million in payments to their governing bodies for October.
