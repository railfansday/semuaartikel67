YouTubers IShowSpeed, Michelle Khare, MrBeast, Dhar Mann, Spaceskits, Jordan Howlett, Kylie Kelce, and fern all took home prizes from the inaugural Clio Creator Awards.

An offshoot of the long-running Clio Awards, which launched in 1959 to honor excellence in advertising around the world, the Clio Creator Awards are presented by YouTube and backed by Influential, the content creator-led marketing firm Publicis Groupe bought for $500 million in 2024.

The first Clio Creator event brought host Terry Crews to the Dolby Theater in Los Angeles to hand out awards in categories like Creator & Brand Collaborations, Public Relations, Integrated Campaign, and Written Content.

“Creators aren’t just participating in culture, they’re shaping it,” Nicole Purcell, CEO of the Clios, stated in a statement. “The inaugural Clio Creator Awards recognize the incredible talent and originality driving this industry forward, from creators building their own communities to brands finding new ways to connect with audiences. These are storytellers whose influence extends far beyond their platforms, and we’re proud to give them the recognition they deserve.”.

“As the presenting sponsor of the awards and home to the creators shaping culture today, YouTube is proud to champion the craft and expertise that goes into creator partnerships and creator-led work,” YouTube noted in a company blog post.

According to recent reports, “A huge congratulations to every creator, brand, and agency honored last night. Spanning across Shorts, live streams, podcast and cinematic content for the biggest screens, these winners show that creators on YouTube are taking their content and brand partnerships to upcoming heights,” it added.

Creator x brand collabs have gotten nods in other ad industry events and award shows (including Tubefilter’s own Streamy Awards)–but the fact that a legacy show like the Clios are embracing the creator world, and that these awards are backed by a Publicis Groupe subsidiary, points to further enmeshing of our space with traditional entertainment.
