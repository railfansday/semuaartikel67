Jorge Jesus was appointed Portugal manager in July.

Jorge Jesus has responded to claims by Cristiano Ronaldo's mother, Dolores Aveiro, that her son had been "deceived" by the Portugal manager.

Ronaldo, 41, retired from international football on 5 November, ending a career with his country during which he won 234 caps and scored 146 goals.

But his retirement also came less than a month after he had been given a 30-day suspension and fined Â£215 for walking out of the Portugal camp in September after being told he would not start the Nations League match against Denmark.

Portugal played their first game since Ronaldo's announcement against the Danes on Saturday, losing 4-1 at home.

While attending a Sporting event on Monday, Aveiro was asked about Ronaldo's national team retirement, and said:, external "Of course he was sad.

It has been revealed that "They deceived him. And I'm sad, too. The defeat [against Denmark] was well-deserved. I was actually glad.".

Before Portugal's Nations League game against Wales in Cardiff on Tuesday (19:45 GMT), Jesus was asked for his thoughts on Aveiro's comments.

"Now I have to respond to Cristiano Ronaldo's mother?" he mentioned.

"She is a lady I greatly respect - she suffered a lot to raise her children.".

Asked if the debate about Ronaldo's international retirement would end, Jesus added: "I don't know when it's going to stop.

Sources indicate that "If you guys ask the questions, I have to answer. If you ask about Ronaldo next year, I will answer.".
