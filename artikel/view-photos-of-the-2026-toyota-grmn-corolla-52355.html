More carbon, more wing, more carbon wing—the 2026 Toyota GRMN Corolla is the most aggressive (and expensive) Corolla yet. It has slightly more torque than the normal Corolla, but great efforts have gone into refining its steering, suspension, and cooling.

In a recent development, only 730 GRMN Corollas will be built, and a few have already arrived to customers. The GRMN is available only in North America, Japan, and Australia.

The big carbon-fiber wing at the top of the hatch has five angles of adjustability. One thing you can’t change is which transmission it comes with. The GRMN is sold exclusively with a six-speed manual.

Industry observers note that two spray nozzles flank the GRMN’s front-mounted intercooler. Toyota says that activating the intercooler spray button can lower intake temperatures by up to 20 degrees.

Every GRMN comes with Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 tires.

According to recent reports, not getting enough daily fiber? Well, the GRMN is. The carbon-fiber hood has been carefully designed to direct air through the engine bay.

In the wild, it might be tough to distinguish the GRMN from a modified GR, but look for the MN badge. GRMN stands for “Gazoo Racing tuned by Meister of Nürburgring.” Yeah, that’s probably why the badge doesn’t spell it out—too many letters.
