Humanity is preparing for alien war.

Netflix has released a first trailer for 3 Body Problem season 2, which sees humanity on the brink of conflict with a hyper-intelligent alien race. Based on the Remembrance of Earth's Past trilogy by Liu Cixin, the Netflix series sees humanity dealing with the approaching arrival of an extraterrestrial civilization that wants to conquer the planet over the span of decades. 3 Body Problem season 2 was recently confirmed to be arriving soon,. The show was given a definitive release date of January 14, 2027.

Now, Netflix has released a full trailer for 3 Body Problem season 2, which sees the conflict with the core alien race about to grow into an ever bigger problem for humanity. The trailer sees the aliens deciding to send an emissary down to Earth for peace talks, a ploy that some vital figures, such as Thomas Wade (Liam Cunningham), don't believe is in good faith. The trailer teases more events in space, while on Earth, operations to protect the planet begin to unfold. This includes missions on the ground, alongside tense discussions of what humanity should prepare for.

Check out the full trailer below:.

3 Body Problem season 2 pulls from the second book in Remembrance of Earth's Past, titled The Dark Forest. As seen in the trailer, it appears some additional Earthly events will be unfolding as space takes on a bigger role. This includes newly introduced alien characters getting introduced, with some hints that the emissary is, in fact, not there to prevent a larger war from taking place. Based on the events of the trailer, it seems like whatever happens in season 2, humanity will never be the same.

The cast of 3 Body Problem will expand in season 2, with Claudia Doumit portraying Captain Van Rijin and Ellie de Lange joining as Alya. Both will have main roles in the show, making them vital additions to the larger cast. Recurring roles will also be fleshed out as Alfie Allen, David Yip, and Jordan Sunshine join as undisclosed characters. Netflix also recently confirmed season 2 will be shorter than season 1, clocking in at 6 episodes. This puts it two shy of the sci-fi show's debut batch.

Sources indicate that david Benioff and D.B. Weiss return as showrunners following the end of 3 Body Problem season 1. The first three episodes of season 2 will be directed by Jeremy Podeswa, while Miguel Sapochnik will direct the second half of the season. Writers include Benioff, Weiss, and Alexander Woo for episode 1, Rose Cartwright for episode 2, Woo for episode 3, Nichelle Tramble Spellman for episode 4, and Benioff and Weiss for episodes 5 and 6. Season 2 was filmed back-to-back with season 3, which will end the series.

The trailer for 3 Body Problem season 2 indicates the story will be pushed forward in a massive fresh way, as the conflict between humanity and its ever-present outside threat continues. It remains to be seen how the series will measure up to the books, but it appears certain this adaptation of the novel series will have plenty of memorable moments in its middle third.
