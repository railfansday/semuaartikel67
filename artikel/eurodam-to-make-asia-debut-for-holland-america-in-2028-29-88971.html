📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Holland America Line unveiled its 2028-29 Asia cruise season, marking the Eurodam’s debut in the region.

The season will run from September 2028 through May 2029, with itineraries ranging from 11 to 27 days.

Departing from Tokyo, Japan; Hong Kong, China; and Singapore, they will take guests to Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan, the Philippines and Southeast Asia.

“Asia is one of those regions where you can experience something completely different from one port to the next, from a major city like Hong Kong to our latest call at Coron in the Philippines, known for its turquoise waters and rich history,” remarked Paul Grigsby, vice president of deployment and revenue planning for Holland America Line.

“That contrast is what makes these itineraries so interesting to build. The new ports take guests beyond the places they may already know, introducing them to islands, coastlines and historic communities that reveal another side of the region, while natural phenomena like Japan’s cherry blossoms can make the experience completely different depending on when you visit.”.

Five new ports will be featured: Coron, Busuanga Island, Philippines, Hirara and Wakayama in Japan, Magong in Taiwan and Muara, a gateway to Brunei Darussalam.

Across the season, guests can enjoy overnight stays and late-night departures in 14 ports throughout the region, including two fresh ones Keelung (Taipei) and Phú Mỹ (Ho Chi Minh City).

In a recent development, as recently announced, there will also be a 27-day Coffee, Tea & Spice Route Legendary Voyage, departing Jan. 3 roundtrip from Singapore.

The Destination Dining program will bring the itinerary onboard, allowing guests to sample dishes including Japanese rockfish and fresh sushi, Korean bibimbap with beef bulgogi, Thai mango sticky rice, Filipino chicken adobo, and more.

For a limited time, when guests book 2028-2029 Asia cruises with the Have It All premium package, the standard package amenities of shore excursions, specialty dining, a Signature Beverage Package and Surf Wi-Fi are included.

There is also the added perk of free prepaid crew appreciation, along with free upgrades to the Elite Beverage Package and Premium Wi-Fi.

Guests can also take advantage of the Exclusive Mariner Society Early Booking Bonus and Mariner Society loyalty members can get up to $400 onboard credit per stateroom.
