Lost homes, stolen freedom: National Book Award winners on exile and imprisonment

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (90 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that two books about losing everything have just won America's most prestigious literary honours. Hannah Lillith Assadi took the National Book Award for fiction for "Paradiso 17", a novel inspired by her late father, who was driven from Palestine in 1948 during the Nakba. Abdelrahman ElGendy won the non-fiction prize for "Huna", his memoir of six years as a political prisoner in Egypt, arrested at 17 alongside his father. ElGendy couldn't be in New York to collect his award: his visa was denied.
