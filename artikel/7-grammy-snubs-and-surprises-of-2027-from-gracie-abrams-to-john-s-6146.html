7 Grammy snubs and surprises of 2027, from Gracie Abrams to John Summit

The 2027 Grammy snubs and surprises include shutouts for Gracie Abrams and Hilary Duff, a single folk nod for Noah Kahan and no major-category nods for Madonna.

In a recent development, A Grammy nominations list does two jobs at once. It indicates what voters chose to reward, and it shows whom they passed over. The list for the 2027 awards, unveiled on a Monday, delivered plenty of both. Several chart-topping releases and latest records from long-established stars came away with few nominations, or none at all, in the races that draw the most attention.

It has been revealed that the top of the field was crowded. Raye led every act with 11 nominations, and Rosalía followed with nine. Kanye "Ye" West earned a best rap album nomination for Bully. Beyoncé reached 100 career Grammy nominations with "Morning Dew (Donk)," a song released July 4 on the 20th anniversary edition of B'Day. Sabrina Carpenter picked up two nominations for best pop duo/group performance. Both came from her collaborations with Taylor Swift and Madonna.

The 2027 Grammy snubs sit on the other side of that ledger. Commercial success did not guarantee a place on the ballot. One album reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and still went without a single nomination. A comeback record debuted in the top three and received nothing. Veteran artists, including a seven-time Grammy winner, collected nods elsewhere while missing album, record and song of the year entirely. In one case, a documentary about a former Beatle earned two nominations, while his fresh album earned one.

Other results stood out for different reasons. A former best new artist nominee landed a single nod in a folk category. A pop star placed in all three general categories, and her total still counted among the year's surprises. A dance artist answered his result with public criticism of the awards.

According to recent reports, the seven slides that follow are a selection of notable snubs and surprises from the 2027 nominations, not a complete accounting. They cover Gracie Abrams, Hilary Duff, Noah Kahan, Madonna, Taylor Swift, Paul McCartney and John Summit. Each slide names the artist, the work in question and the nominations it did or did not earn, so any entry can be read on its own.

Gracie Abrams received no 2027 Grammy nominations for Daughter From Hell, her third album. The record had reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

The Hollywood Reporter counted the shutout among the snubs and surprises of the 2027 nominations.

Recent findings suggest that the Recording Academy has recognized Abrams before. She was a best new artist nominee in 2024. That category honors an artist's breakthrough rather than any single release.

According to recent reports, the Daughter From Hell result also follows an earlier miss. Last year, Abrams had been expected to earn song of the year and record of the year nominations for "That's So True." Neither nomination came.

In a recent development, those two categories reward different work. Song of the year goes to the songwriters behind a composition. Record of the year covers the finished recording, including the artist's performance and its production.

It has been revealed that the Hollywood Reporter described the 2027 shutout as not her first Grammys disappointment. Across those two cycles, Abrams went without nominations for a single that had been tipped for major categories and for an album that topped the Billboard 200.

Abrams opened for Taylor Swift on select dates of the Eras Tour.

Hilary Duff's comeback album, Luck … Or Something, received no nominations when the 2027 Grammy nominations were revealed, according to The Hollywood Reporter. The record went unrecognized in every category.

It has been revealed that the shutout followed a top-three chart debut. Luck … Or Something entered the Billboard 200 at No. 3. The Billboard 200 ranks the most popular albums in the U.S. each week.

In a recent development, duff released the album in February. It came out through Atlantic Records, her new label.

A newly introduced label, a comeback release and a No. 3 debut did not add up to a place on the 2027 Grammy ballot. Duff's album was absent from the general field and from the genre categories alike.

Noah Kahan's album The Great Divide earned one 2027 Grammy nomination, for best contemporary folk album. It received no nominations for album, record or song of the year.

According to recent reports, that outcome fell short of some forecasts. The Hollywood Reporter noted that several experts had predicted top-category nominations for The Great Divide.

In a recent development, the two kinds of category work differently. Best contemporary folk album is a genre award, so The Great Divide is up against other folk releases. Album, record and song of the year sit in the general field. There, entries from every genre compete against each other.

It has been revealed that kahan has reached the general field before. He was previously nominated for best latest artist, which is also a general-field category. This year, his only recognition came in the folk field.

Kahan responded on the Monday the nominations were confirmed. "Life goes on," he tweeted.

Recent findings suggest that madonna's album Confessions II earned four nominations for the 2027 Grammys. None of them came in the three major categories: album of the year, record of the year and song of the year.

According to recent reports, confessions II is the long-awaited follow-up to Confessions, the album Madonna released in 2005. All four of the sequel's nods came in other categories.

In a recent development, madonna is a seven-time Grammy winner. That record did not carry Confessions II into any of the three major races this cycle.

Her name also appears in another artist's results. One of Sabrina Carpenter's nominations for best pop duo/group performance came from a collaboration with Madonna.

The Hollywood Reporter included Confessions II among the notable snubs and surprises of the 2027 nominations.

Industry observers note that taylor Swift earned five nominations for the 2027 Grammys. One came in each of the three general categories: album of the year, record of the year and song of the year.

That result placed Swift in every one of the top races. It still put her on The Hollywood Reporter's list of snubs and surprises from the 2027 nominations, as a surprise rather than a shutout.

It has been revealed that the Hollywood Reporter described her five nominations as perhaps understated relative to what is expected of her. That judgment belongs to the outlet. Its point concerned the size of her total, not whether she was recognized in album, record or song of the year.

Sources indicate that swift's entry differs from the shutouts elsewhere in the 2027 Grammy snubs. Her case rests on a nomination count that the outlet measured against expectations, with no gap at the top of the field.

Paul McCartney's album The Boys of Dungeon Lane did not make it into any of the major categories in the 2027 Grammy nominations. Its only nomination came in best traditional pop vocal album.

According to recent reports, the major categories are album of the year, record of the year and song of the year. The newly introduced McCartney record appeared in none of them. The Hollywood Reporter included that result in its rundown of snubs and surprises from the nominations, which were unveiled on a Monday.

According to recent reports, mcCartney still finished with three nominations overall. Only one of them belongs to The Boys of Dungeon Lane.

According to recent reports, the other two came from Man on the Run, a documentary directed by Morgan Neville. The film covers McCartney's exit from the Beatles.

That split means a film about the end of his Beatles years drew more nominations than his newly introduced album did.

The best traditional pop vocal album nod keeps The Boys of Dungeon Lane in contention for a Grammy. That chance comes in a single genre category. It does not extend to album, record or song of the year, the three general categories.

Recent findings suggest that john Summit publicly criticized the Grammys after a dance snub in the 2027 nominations, according to a headline from The Hollywood Reporter. That headline quotes him directly: "It's All a F***ing Joke.".

Recent findings suggest that the outlet ran Summit's reaction as a separate story, apart from its main roundup of the year's snubs and surprises. The headline does not name the category, track or album involved.

Summit works in the dance music world that the snub concerns. The Chicago Sun-Times describes him as a DJ raised in Naperville. His music pairs high-energy tech house rhythms with catchy sung hooks, the paper reports.

Sources indicate that summit took an indirect path into music. Born John Schuster, he worked as an accountant and DJed on the side. He later left that corporate job to pursue music full time.

One track raised his profile. Defected Records released his song "Deep End," which went viral and helped lift him to global prominence as a DJ.

Industry observers note that summit has also built an audience online. He shares footage from major festival performances alongside glimpses of his ordinary daily routine.
