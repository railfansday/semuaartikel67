Whether your ideal après involves a packed bar, live music, a DJ spinning late into the night, or a mountain town with enough energy to keep things going until last call, some ski destinations know how to bring the fun long after your final run.

These 10 resorts earned the highest marks from readers in SKI’s Reader Resort Survey for après and nightlife. If a lively scene is just as important to your ski vacation as the skiing itself, consider this your shortlist.
