Mexican Edson Alvarez has received plaudits after his satisfactory return to the West Ham United starting line up following Scott Parker’s decision to re-integrate him into the side. Alvarez’ evident emotional release at being recalled to the team is testimony to the struggle he’s endured both on, and off the pitch since last playing a league game as a West Ham player.

Injury, surgery, kidnapping scandal, covid certificate allegations – all must have taken their toll on the Alvarez family as did Nuno’s decision to freeze the midfielder out of the West Ham team after, it must be remarked, a pretty decent World Cup.

Here is a player who had not started a competitive fixture since August yet he was ready to go when called upon and lasted a whole 90 minutes. As reported by futnsoccer.com, Nuno’s treatment of the Mexican national team captain evidently took a significant toll, and it was only through his own resilience that he was able to jump when Scott Parker called:.

In a recent development, “It has been a very difficult period for me after four months without playing, you know? And a lot of things happening in my life, a lot of changes,” Alvarez remarked.

“Imagine being away from the team, like training alone…..I’ve been working really hard by myself, and I’m just looking forward”.

Emotional and successful return for Edson Alvarez: Who’d have thought it.

Alvarez was so moved that he sought Parker out before the start of the game:.

Sources indicate that “I spoke with [Parker] at the beginning of the game and I said thank you. I said thank you to him because he is giving me again the opportunity, the chance to show myself, and that means a lot to me.”.

In a recent development, “I need to pay him back that confidence, and I think the only way that I can do it is on the pitch; like showing myself, fighting, and doing everything with my experience for the team. I really missed it.”.

In a team famously devoid of leadership, it sounds as if Scott Parker has just found himself a lieutenant who will go into battle for him – and for West Ham – any time he’s asked. All of which can only throw into question Nuno’s decision to ‘exile’ Alvarez and so many other players from the training ground.
