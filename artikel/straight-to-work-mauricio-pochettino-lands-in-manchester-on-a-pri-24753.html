Mauricio Pochettino made a promise to end Man City's dominance as Man United manager in his last interview as United States national team coach on Tuesday evening.

The newly introduced manager landed in Manchester at 11.32am on Wednesday as he prepares to start his new job later in the afternoon.

Pochettino had taken off from northern Kentucky in a private jet chartered by the club after his final match on American soil, which saw the US beat Haiti 2-0 in Cincinnati.

But before becoming airborne, Pochettino had time for a final statement Stateside, after being taunted that 'Manchester is blue, and will always be blue' by one of his interviewers.

'No way,' he shook his head. 'You will see.'.

In a recent development, 'Give me time. Give me time and then that - that's going to change.'.

Latest Man United manager Mauricio Pochettino made his way to Carrington straight after landing in Manchester.

The Argentine took off from northern Kentucky in a private jet last night and landed at 11.32am.

The manager shared a picture from his journey as he prepared to touch down at the airport (Source: Instagram/Mauricio Pochettino).

The plane believed to be carrying Pochettino and his staff was hired by Manchester United.

Pochettino also shared a poignant farewell message to his US set-up, days after breaking down in tears in a press conference ahead of his penultimate time in charge.

Man United fans may also have been pleased to read of Pochettino's new-found love of John Denver's song Country Roads in his goodbye message, a song which is played before kick-off at Old Trafford.

Sources indicate that 'It is never easy to say goodbye, especially after everything we experienced together.' Pochettino captioned a carousel of photos of his time in the role. 'I always wanted the opportunity to coach in the USA, and I was very happy to have this chance with the national team. Two incredible years, with so many great moments and experiences that will stay with me always.

'From the challenges we faced at the Gold Cup to the excitement of the USA hosting the World Cup, and now finishing our time by reaching the semi-finals of the Nations League, I am proud of what we achieved.

'When we arrived, I wanted us to believe that we could compete with anyone. I hope today that belief is stronger, not only with the players but with everyone who supports this team. Not everything in football can be measured by results, and I believe we have helped build something important for the future of soccer in this country.

'This experience has re-energised me and given me so much, both in football and personally. I have discovered a wonderful country, made upcoming friends and enjoyed so many of the traditions. Every time I hear Country Roads, I will forever think of all of you with a smile.

'Thank you to the players, staff, everyone at (the US national team) and all the supporters. Especially to those who believed in us and gave us this opportunity.

According to recent reports, 'Leaving is tough at this moment, but sometimes in football an opportunity comes that is impossible to say no to. I have always believed in following my heart, even when it means leaving a place where I have been very happy.

'I hope this team continues to grow and achieves all the success it deserves. Thank you USA.'.

Pochettino had previously explained his emotions as stemming from how difficult the decision had been to leave the national team and re-start at Old Trafford.

In a recent development, ‘I haven't prepared a speech, I'm talking with my heart,’ he stated on Friday. ‘It's difficult for me to talk now because I am a very emotional guy.

According to recent reports, ‘When these moments arrive, it is always tough. Normally it is the opposite – you have been sacked! You cry because you want to stay.

‘I think it was one of the most difficult decisions of our career, but you have to move forward.

Recent findings suggest that ‘Not too many opportunities can happen in your career with a side like Manchester United. That is difficult to turn down when it arrives.

Sources indicate that ‘In some part it’s a very sad decision, in another part we are professional and we are trying to make our dreams come true. We are excited for that step forward.'.

Man United first made contact with Pochettino following their breathless 4-4 draw against Aston Villa in their last match before the international break.

The Man United hierarchy had previously given former head coach Michael Carrick a stay of execution amid a worrying start to the season, but as they prepared to head into the two-week pause in the relegation zone, the board became keen to make a change.

An agonising wait for Carrick followed, with the club legend taking training for multiple sessions at the start of the week as his future was openly mulled by executives including CEO Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox.

After the duo returned from Monaco, where they had flown to speak to co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe about Carrick's future, the 45-year-old was finally despatched last Thursday morning.

Pochettino's United States farewell match went off in pitch-perfect style, which allowed thoughts to quickly move to the next task at hand after the final whistle.

Christian Pulisic helped make sure that Pochettino's reign in charge ended on a high note.

The manager will now focus his attentions on getting Man United out of the relegation zone.

In a recent development, 'When you finish the game, you start in your mind to think about different things,' Pochettino said after the win. 'Of course, it's tough because it's emotional. Because I really love these guys, and I love the players. I love the staff. I love the fans and everything.'.

Travelling with Pochettino were outgoing USMNT first-team coach Miguel D'Agostino, strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya and analyst Alec Scott, while his assistant Jesus Perez, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez as well as Pochettino's son and fitness coach Sebastiano have already begun preparing for Sunday's matchup with Liverpool at Anfield.

Man United paid a staggering £10million to cleave Pochettino and his staff away from the United States, after the head coach signed a fresh contract with the national team in the wake of this summer's World Cup.

It has been revealed that the quick turnaround means Pochettino will be able to work with the players as they begin reporting back from international duty. Furthermore, he is expected to speak to club media before conducting his pre-match press conference around lunchtime on Friday.

Before boarding his flight, Pochettino not only waved to his adoring fans, but took a photo with staff and players on the pitch in Cincinnati.

Sources indicate that 'It's impossible not to cry, but that is because we care about the people and the people care about us,' he told reporters. 'When that happens, it's normal to show your emotions, no? Because we are human. We are no robots.'.
