Africa’s Intra-Continental Trade Hit $213.8 Billion But Faces Payment Barriers

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (39 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The value of trade between African nations rose about 6% last year to $213.8 billion, but accounted for only 14.6% of the continent’s total trade due to regional economic integration challenges, according to a study.
