By JAKE FENNER, US SPORTS WRITER.

Published: 09:15 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 09:15 EST, 22 November 2026.

Kansas City Current co-owner Brittany Mahomes led the celebrations as the women's soccer club won its first ever NWSL Championship on Saturday.

Brittany, the wife of Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes, was at Audi Field in Washington, DC for the final match of the season between the Current and the Washington Spirit.

A thrilling finish to the contest resulted in a 2-1 win for the Current, who have only been in existence for five years.

Brittany, who is pregnant with her fourth child, was seen on the pitch celebrating after her team's inaugural title.

She was seen embracing her team and her co-owners as they jumped up and down in celebration of the achievement.

Brittany was a part of the inaugural ownership group of the club when it was unveiled as an expansion team in 2020.

KC Current co-owner Brittany Mahomes celebrated her team winning the NWSL title.

She was seen jumping up and down and celebrating with the players as the title was clinched.

It's the first championship for the expansion club, which began playing in 2021.

Brittany's husband, Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes, also celebrated the achievement on social media.

The three-time Super Bowl champion reshared the club's announcement on X, adding, 'AHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!' He later added, 'LETS GOOOO!!!!!!' and a string of light blue hearts.

Kansas City went ahead in the match in the 54th minute with an 'Olimpico' goal from Vanessa DiBernardo, who watched her corner kick curl to the opposite post to break a first-half deadlock.

With Washington pressing late in added time, the Current were able to score on a counter attack goal from the foot of Brazilian star Debinha to make it 2-0.

Mere seconds later, Washington's Trinity Rodman scored a goal of her own to briefly give Spirit fans hope of a miraculous comeback.

But after 11 elapsed minutes - well beyond the nine given, thanks in part to a scary collision between Rodman and KC keeper Laney Rouse - the final whistle blew as Kansas City clinched the title.

The win for the Current means yet another season of heartbreak for Washington, who have made the last three straight NWSL Championship games and have lost all three.
