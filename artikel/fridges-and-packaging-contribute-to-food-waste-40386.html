Fridges and packaging contribute to food waste

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (57 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Food waste depends not only on what people do but also on how refrigerators and packaging are designed. This is the conclusion of a study from Linköping University (LiU) in Sweden. Information campaigns are not enough. Refrigerators and packaging must also be designed based on how people actually behave, according to the researchers.
