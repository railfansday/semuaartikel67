The Florida Judicial Qualifications Commission states a judge who recently retired no longer faces an ethics case for questioning whether a Black defendant had ever “chopped cotton” and berating two public defenders in open court.

According to recent reports, judge John Jordan of the Ninth Judicial Circuit Court of Florida retired from the bench Nov. 16 and agreed to not hold future judicial office, according to a story by Law360.

The commission’s investigative panel and Jordan had previously agreed to a public reprimand as a disciplinary measure for his behavior.

However, the Florida Supreme Court determined that reprimand was not the appropriate consequence and sent the case back to the commission to be reconsidered.
