LangChain raises $300m at a $3bn valuation to help companies run AI agents

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that langChain, which makes tools for building and running AI agents, has raised $300m at a $3bn valuation, the firm revealed on Friday. The Series C brings its total funding to $460m.

It has been revealed that capitalG, Alphabet’s independent growth fund and an existing investor, led the round. Newly introduced investors GIC and DFJ Growth and existing investors IVP and Sapphire Ventures also took part. Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight and MongoDB Ventures joined as new investors. Sequoia, Amplify and Benchmark put in more.

The valuation is more than double the $1.25bn of about a year ago. LangChain then raised $125m in a round led by IVP, TechCrunch reported.

Industry observers note that langChain began in 2022 as an open-source project. The money will go into LangSmith, its platform for running, testing and governing AI agents. It works whatever framework or model a tech giant uses.

According to recent reports, the company indicates more than 7,000 organisations use LangSmith. They range from startups such as legal AI firm Harvey, Rippling and Listen Labs to carmaker Toyota, Nvidia and Salesforce. Workday uses it to run and govern agents. That cut Workday’s time to deploy a fresh agent from five months to under a day, LangChain remarked.

“Every organization should be free to choose the best models for its needs, control its data and infrastructure, and continuously improve its agents,” said Harrison Chase, LangChain’s co-founder and CEO.

Chase told the Journal that LangChain’s products aim to make agents’ behaviour easier to control.

“The agent’s behavior is the hard part to control,” Chase stated.

LangChain has 435 full-time staff and aims to grow to 850 by the end of next year, the Journal reported. It also has 47 forward deployed engineers and 13 deployed architects, who work directly with customers. It will hire engineers, researchers, marketers and salespeople with the fresh money.

“It’s an insanely competitive market,” Mo Jomaa, a partner at CapitalG, told the Journal.

LangChain states it will show newly introduced LangSmith features at a release event on 20 November.
