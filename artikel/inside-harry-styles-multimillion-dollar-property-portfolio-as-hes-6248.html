Inside Harry Styles’ Multimillion-Dollar Property Portfolio as He’s Snubbed in the 2027 Grammy Nominations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Harry Styles was 18 when he reportedly bought his first house in North London. More than a decade later, the One Direction alum’s real estate deals have taken him from a gated midcentury retreat in Los Angeles to one of Tribeca’s most star-studded condo buildings.

But some of his biggest purchases have brought him back to the same London neighborhood where he started.

The singer was shut out of the 2027 Grammy nominations on Nov. 16, despite a year in which his fourth studio album—"Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally.— reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and its lead single, “Aperture,” topped the Hot 100.

His previous album, "Harry’s House," won album of the year and best pop vocal album in 2023.

Styles’ first reported purchase was a white-stucco house near Hampstead Heath in 2012, when he was still in One Direction.

Industry observers note that according to Architectural Digest, he paid about $4.8 million for the four-bedroom home. High walls shield the property from the street, while French doors and skylights bring light into the roughly 2,300-square-foot interior.

The house also has a home gym and a landscaped back garden.

It has been revealed that several years later, Styles reportedly paid $11.6 million for another house in the area and $5.5 million for the adjoining property. It’s those two homes that he has reportedly moved to combine.

Sources indicate that according to The Standard, work had begun on the project in December 2025 after planning approval that spring. The aims call for a cinema and swimming pool on the lower level, as well as a sauna, gym and gallery.

The project also comes with a requirement for some considerably smaller homes. The Standard reported that the approved plans include boxes for bats and hedgehogs and two insect shelters.

Neighbors raised concerns about how the work could affect the light reaching their homes, and Styles’ team submitted revisions in response.

When reports linked Styles to an $8.71 million Tribeca condo in 2017, the purchase had been made through a trust.

The building was already drawing celebrity buyers, including Blake Lively and Ryan Reynolds.

While details of Styles’ own interiors have largely stayed private, the building’s shared amenities include an indoor lap pool, private fitness studios, and a landscaped rooftop terrace.

Styles’ first reported California purchase was a $4 million property in the Beverly Hills Post Office area in 2014.

Architect Alejandro Ortiz designed the midcentury compound, where a wood bridge runs through the trees and a pool sits in a courtyard below the surrounding decks.

The grounds also have a detached guesthouse. Styles sold the property off-market for $3,175,000 in 2016, about $825,000 below his purchase price.

His next Los Angeles home traded the wooded setting for city and ocean views above the Sunset Strip.

According to recent reports, the roughly 4,400-square-foot Hollywood Hills house had four bedrooms, a home theater, and terraces overlooking the city. Below the main living area, a covered lounge opened to a pool and spa bordered by bamboo.

Styles bought the Hollywood Hills property for about $7 million in 2016 and put it back on the market the following year for nearly $8.5 million.

It took roughly two years and several price cuts to find a buyer. The home was sold for $6 million in 2019, the same year Styles reportedly made the first of the two purchases behind his Hampstead renovation.
