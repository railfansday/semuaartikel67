Derby County will hope influential star can build on Bolton Wanderers display and hit form after a tough season: Opinion

According to recent reports, derby County remain rooted in the Championship relegation zone after a tough season to date, but they enjoyed a much-needed win over Bolton Wanderers before the break. Not only did the 3-1 triumph mark the end of a 10-game winless streak for DCFC, but it might just have kept John Eustace in his job.

Sources indicate that ryan Nyambe made headlines for scoring his first career goal in the win, while Jack Robinson and Rhian Brewster also got on the scoresheet. They were among the standout players against the Trotters, while Mo Fuseini and Jacob Widell Zetterstrom also impressed.

Carlton Morris was another man to play a central role in the win. The Rams striker has come under fire amid his poor form at the start of the 2026/27 season, but after a much-improved display against Bolton Wanderers, Morris will be eager to kick on and get back to his dangerous best in front of goal.

Although some have overstepped the mark, criticism of Morris has been justified this term. He hasn’t been helped by a Derby County side desperately lacking in creative spark and quality in possession, but one goal in 12 games across all competitions marks a big drop-off from last season.

His sole goal has come from only 2.38 xG in the Championship too, so not only has he struggled to convert his opportunities, he isn’t getting many high-quality chances either (FotMob).

Recent findings suggest that morris’ wait for a first Championship goal since August continued against Bolton Wanderers, but the all-round play of the 30-year-old striker was far better. It was his header across the box that Robinson scored the second goal from. And, after he held up the ball well, his cross was flicked onto Nyambe by Fuseini for the equaliser.

The DCFC man created three chances in total and won 11 aerial duels (FotMob). He was tireless in attack for Eustace as they fought to secure a key win, and it highlighted why the boss has retained faith in Morris.

It has been revealed that there have been criticisms about his mobility from supporters. But, in Morris, Eustace has a powerful outlet at the top of the pitch who will run and battle for his team. He leads from the front, and if he can rediscover his goalscoring touch, he can play a key role in turning around the Rams’ fortunes.

Morris’ display against Bolton Wanderers indicated how he brings other players into the game and offered a timely reminder of how his aerial ability and physical traits can be used with better service.

But, ultimately, Morris needs to start scoring to really silence doubters and keep his place. Lars-Jorgen Salvesen has found game time hard to come by but his two goals equate to 0.75 goals per 90. It has raised fair arguments that the Norwegian should be given a starting chance over Morris.

Sources indicate that morris scored 12 goals in 31 games last season and he was excellent before injury ended his campaign early. His Bolton Wanderers performance was a much-needed reminder of just how influential he can be, and it can be a catalyst for the striker as he looks to inspire DCFC’s rise away from the Championship relegation zone.
