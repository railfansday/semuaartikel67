The past weeks have seen the broad crypto ETF market face consistent withdrawals from investors, causing Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana, and others to record steady outflows since October.

While the negative trend has continued, Solana has just taken over the leadership in the broad ETF market after recording its first inflow in a while.

Latest data from SosoValue shows that while Bitcoin, Ethereum, and XRP ETFs have continued to struggle with consistent weekly outflows, Solana has just beaten the trend after it finally ended a six-week streak of consecutive weekly outflows.

Notably, the data shows that Solana spot ETFs have recorded a net inflow of $4.38 million over the week.

Although the net amount of capital received by the ETFs appears decent, it is significant considering the growing crypto market weakness.

While this marks an end to the Solana ETFs' six-week outflow streak, this is the first positive weekly performance they have recorded since October 9.

Further data shows that Bitcoin spot ETFs suffered the largest withdrawals as investors pulled out about $1.79 billion during the same period.

The negative trend also extended to the Ethereum ETF market as the funds posted a total of $314.17 million in net outflows following the consistent selling pressure.

In addition to this, the XRP spot ETFs were not spared, as the funds recorded $11.11 million in weekly outflows, leaving Solana as the only one among the four major crypto ETFs to finish the week on a positive note.
