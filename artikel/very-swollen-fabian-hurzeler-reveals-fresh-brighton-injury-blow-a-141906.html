Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler gave a worrying injury update for Matt O'Riley after the 4-0 win Conference League against Craiova.

O'Riley was clobbered in the 39th minute by visiting defender Vladimir Screciu, who was sent-off after VAR review.

It was a poor challenge from Screciu and one that saw O'Riley’s right ankle buckle into an unnatural position.

The Denmark international tried to play on but was replaced at halftime by debutant Femi Azeez.

Recent findings suggest that "It did not look good," mentioned head coach Hurzeler to Sussex World after the match. "It is very swollen. We will have to see.".

It's a blow for O'Riley who has had limited chances to impress this season.

The Denmark international has totalled just 370 minutes of action this season from nine appearances.

He's now highly unlikely to feature in the matchday squad to face Newcastle in the Premier League this Sunday.

Ibrahim Osman scored his first Brighton goals as his side returned to winning ways by cruising to a 4-0 success over 10-man Universitatea Craiova in the Conference League.

Talk of the high-flying Seagulls potentially being shock Premier League title contenders this season was dampened slightly by Sunday’s surprise 2-0 defeat at Hull.

Winger Osman finished a flowing move to set Albion on course to avoid a further unexpected defeat before Maxim De Cuyper superbly curled home his fifth goal of the campaign to double the lead.

Romanian champions Craiova were left with little hope of a comeback at the Amex Stadium when defender Vladimir Screciu was sent off in the 39th minute for stamping on the ankle of Matt O’Riley.

Osman set up Charalampos Kostoulas for the third goal before swiftly claiming his second with a fine individual effort as Brighton made it 10 points from four games in the competition on a bitterly cold evening in Sussex.

“I think that was a really good start, and also very important to make the game look easy, because especially the first goal helped us force [the opponent] to open up the space,” he mentioned.

“That meant we could have more space to combine and more opportunity to attack their box. So I think we were very professional with good standards in the first half. Then, of course, the red card definitely helped us to finish - or to change - the structure of the game.

In a recent development, “In the second half we created plenty of chances. I wasn't happy that we weren't clinical and clean enough finishing these chances, because I think we dropped our level a little bit there. Overall, though, winning 4–0 means that we did a lot of things well.”.
