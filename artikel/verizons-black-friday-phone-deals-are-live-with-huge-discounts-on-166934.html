📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Verizon’s Black Friday sale is officially underway, bringing steep discounts on flagship phones, free accessory bundles, and also lower monthly rates for family plans. There's additional ways to save, too, including getting more value out of your trade-in to lower your out-of-pocket expense at checkout.

Here’s a look at the best Verizon Black Friday phone deals you can snatch up right now.

There’s no direct discount on the iPhone 18 Pro at Verizon, but signing up for a three-year Simplicity Plus or Simplicity Pro strategy gets you a free Apple Watch Series 12 and iPad 11.

That’s a massive value, considering those two devices would set you back $898 if you bought them separately at full price. In order to qualify, you need to activate separate Verizon service plans for both the watch and tablet to claim the bundle for free.

According to recent reports, there's also another offer that consists of a bundle deal that gets you a free Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Buds 4. However, you need to keep in mind that you'll need to activate that watch on a Verizon service initiative if you want to claim the free buds.

I love the passport-shaped design of the standard Galaxy Z Fold 8, which Verizon discounts to a monthly payment $39.99, saving you $460 off the total price of the phone.

You can also score a free Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Buds 4 in the process, just as long as you pair the phone with an eligible Simplicity proposal and activate a separate Watch service plan.

In a recent development, additionally, you can snag a ton of free accessories in the process — with Verizon throwing in a Moto Watch Ultra, Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G, and a pair of Meta glasses. As usual, you'll need to activate separate service prepares for both the watch and tablet to claim the bundle.

Industry observers note that if the S26 Ultra has too much for you, then the Galaxy S26 Plus might be more up your street. It lacks premium features like the S Pen and fourth camera lens, but it still offers a bright 6.,7-inch display, good battery life, fast charging, great cameras and the best Galaxy AI has to offer.

If the Galaxy S26 Ultra feels like overkill for what you need, then the Galaxy S26 Plus might be right up your alley. While it skips Ultra-exclusive perks like the built-in S Pen and fourth rear camera lens, you still get a bright 6.7-inch display, solid battery life, fast charging, great cameras, and the best Galaxy AI features Samsung has to offer.

Recent findings suggest that the Pixel 11 Pro XL is also eligible for a free Pixel Watch 5, Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE 5G, and Meta glasses just like its other offers. Data plans are required on them to claim this bundle.

A similar discount is available on the standard Pixel 11 Pro, dropping your monthly payment from $30.55 down to just $13.33 on a three-year Simplicity proposal.

It has been revealed that you can also pick up a free Pixel Watch 5 and Google Pixel Buds 2a if you want to complete the purchase. In order to get the Buds 2a, you just need to commit to a data plan with the watch.

Samsung's flagship phones are pricier than ever before, so it's nice to see Verizon slashing $220 off the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE — dropping it to $479 (or $13.33 a month over 36 months). That's already a strong value on its own, but this deal gets you a solid mix of flagship features without forcing you to pay Samsung's inflated retail prices for its top-tier models.

Even though it’s last year’s model, the Motorola Razr 2025 is easily the cheapest foldable phone you can buy right now — dropping to just $239 (or $6.66 a month over 36 months) at Verizon.

You still get a vibrant 6.9-inch 120Hz main display, 3.6-inch cover screen capable of running full Android apps, solid cameras, strong battery life, and plenty of Moto AI features to play with.
