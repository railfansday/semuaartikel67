790 bricks in a box that doubles as storage.

The LEGO Classic Large Creative Brick Box is $33.49 at Walmart, $26.50 less than LEGO charges, and LEGO.com lists it as temporarily out of stock. Fortnite’s loot piñata in 691 pieces.

LEGO rates it for ages 12 and up, which makes it a better gift for a teen gamer than for a little kid.
