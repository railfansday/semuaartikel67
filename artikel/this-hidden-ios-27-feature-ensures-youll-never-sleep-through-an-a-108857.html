A lesser-known iOS 27 feature includes a new alarm volume setting that gives alarms, timers, and ringtones independent volume control. You can adjust the volume settings for alarms and timers independently of your phone’s alerts and system sounds, so you don’t miss an alarm after lowering your ringtone volume.

Also: Apple reveals daily limits on your Siri AI use – here’s how much you’ll have to pay.

There have been ways to adjust the iPhone alarm volume for a few years, but there’s an important distinction between what the new feature entails and what those methods involve.

According to recent reports, previously, you could adjust your Sleep Schedule alarms to always go off at a specific volume, regardless of the iPhone’s shared volume. Sleep Schedule alarms have a dedicated volume slider that you can set and adjust as needed, all configured within the Sleep focus. The new feature, however, lets you set an independent volume for all alarms and timers, not just those in the Sleep Schedule.

Alarms, Timers, Alerts, and System Sounds are set to match your ringtone volume by default, but you can turn them off in the Sounds and Haptics setting.

According to recent reports, once you disable the option to match your alarm volume to your ringtone’s volume, you can set your volume for upcoming alarms. With this feature, you can maximize your alarm volume without affecting the volume of incoming calls and notifications.
