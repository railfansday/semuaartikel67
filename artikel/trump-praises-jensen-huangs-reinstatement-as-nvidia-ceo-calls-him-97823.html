In a recent development, jensen Huang has been reinstated as Nvidia CEO, after his resignation earlier this month following his admission of an affair with a woman some suspected to be a Chinese spy. Huang's departure was criticised at the time by many, but news of his reinstatement has earned both Huang and Nvidia praise from very high places.

US president Donald Trump posted on Truth Social following the news, saying: "The Nvidia Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO.".

In a recent development, "The United States government has no reason to believe that Jensen's return to Nvidia poses any risk to national security. The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the US government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.".

Recent findings suggest that "On a personal note," Trump continues. "I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a great patriot and national treasure.".

In a recent development, the US president signs off his post with "Congratulations to Jensen, the Nvidia board, to the people of the United States—and indeed, the world! Thank you for your attention to this matter. President Donald J. Trump.".

The mistake has been correctedNovember 23, 2026.

Meanwhile, SpaceX CEO Elon Musk has posted on X in response, simply saying "The mistake has been corrected." Epic CEO Tim Sweeney's response is even shorter, simply reading "lol" alongside a link to AC/DC's "Back In Black.".

Huang's contributions to Nvidia were defended by many after his departure, and a petition to reinstate him gained hundreds of thousands of signatures. By the looks of things, many will continue to celebrate his return to the helm of Nvidia—especially with the stock rebounding by 33% at the time of writing as trading begins this morning.
