In a recent development, thousands of travellers were stranded over the weekend as the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)'s nationwide ground stop saw flights cancelled, diverted, or held. It was chaos. And the President of the United States steered well clear of the crisis.

According to recent reports, on Monday's Late Night, Seth Meyers responded to Donald Trump's lack of action during the ground stop. During the major disruption, the president posted about anything but on Truth Social and avoided public statements — aside from blaming the Biden administration for the FAA's systems, insulting reporters, and directly telling press, "I don’t take any responsibility.".

Sources indicate that "No, of course not! Why would you?" responded Meyers. "The Constitution claims the president's only responsibilities are tweets, memes, and ballrooms.".

Questioned by press about excessive Washington DC construction spending over the weekend, Trump defended his White House vanity projects including the controversial ballroom and newly introduced stone patio, with insistence on the stone.

Industry observers note that "They don't want to put their cameras on the stone because nobody gives a ****," mentioned Meyers. "They're the news, therefore they only film things that are newsworthy. The nation's air traffic control system broke down, one of the most valued companies in the world is tanking, there's a war in Iran and no end in sight, and you think people want to turn on the TV and here tonight on ABC News, we open with close-up of stone?".
