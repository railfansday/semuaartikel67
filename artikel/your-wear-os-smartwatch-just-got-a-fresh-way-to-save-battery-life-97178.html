Your Wear OS smartwatch just got a fresh way to save battery life

🌐 Source: Zdnet • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (303 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that some of the most popular Android smartwatches just got a battery-saving patch. Google released an updated version of Google Play Services on Tuesday that can help extend the battery life of a Wear OS smartwatch.

Industry observers note that the v26.45 update concerns location tracking during outdoor workouts. Wear OS smartwatch consumers can now use their phone’s Global Navigation Satellite System to track their location during an outdoor workout, which can conserve battery life by outsourcing the smartwatch’s energy to the smartphone when it isn’t in Battery Saver mode.

Also: Pixel Watch 4 vs. Watch 5: Is Google’s upcoming model worth it?

According to recent reports, constantly communicating with satellites during an outdoor workout can be a battery drain, and every minute of battery life counts when you use your smartwatch for communication, workout tracking, and health monitoring. GPS services, a bright, always-on display, and continuous health sensors like SpO2 can squeeze the most juice out of your smartwatch.

Sources indicate that wear OS software is used by many of the most popular smartwatch brands, including Google, Samsung, and OnePlus. Both the Google Pixel Watch and Samsung Galaxy Watch’s largest batteries last approximately 40 hours. That’s nearly two days of use from two top Android smartwatches, but you’d be surprised how rapidly it can drain.

The upgrade improves battery life without users having to take any action or change any settings — the Google Play Services version 26.45 works quietly under the hood.

That’s not the only Google Play Services upgrade that Wear OS smartwatch people can take advantage of. Another recent update lets users view on their phone a larger preview of any watch face they want to install on their smartwatch.
