According to recent reports, pokémon Pokopia has limited-time events that occur for a few days before disappearing, though you can time travel back to them, if you miss out on any. Some events feature Pokémon and habitats you can only unlock via the event, so you'll want to make sure you're paying attention to any guests who are the "talk of the town.".

You can swap through your regular and event Habitat Dex/Pokédex by using the right trigger on the respective menu.

If you're looking for the regular habitats and Pokédex, check out our Pokopia Habitat Dex list and Pokédex.

Upgrade (Nov. 16): We've updated this guide with the information from the "Apples to Applin" event.

In a recent development, below you can see a complete list of the event habitats that have appeared in the game so far. You can see a full list of events on our Pokopia event list.

According to recent reports, note that the habitat can be made whenever, as long as you have the required items. If you missed out on the items, but you don't want to time travel, you can always ask a friend with the items to open up their island. If you visit and take a picture of the item, you can use the 3D printer in the Pokémon Center to make things you've missed.

If you're viewing our list on mobile, make sure to swipe horizontally on the table to see all the information.

1 flower backpack, 1 Hoppip water bottle, 1 lunch box.

1 dandy flowers, 1 flower cushion, 1 lunch box, 1 flowery table setting.

1 adventure kit, 1 map and compass, and 1 treasure.

1 Jirachi lamp, 1 starry-sky bed, 1 star closet, 1 cloud table.

1 swim ring, 1 cooler, 1 beach volleyball set.

1 table (any), 1 ghost smoke projector, 1 candy bucket.

1 Trevenant streetlight, 1 pumpkin lantern tower, 1 pumpkin lantern.

1 Syrupy Apple tree, 1 harvest basket.

1 Apple pie, 1 table (any), 1 seat (any).

3 kitchen tables, 1 dipplin mixing bowl, 1 appley blender, 1 kitchen scale.

Table images: Koei Tecmo, Game Freak/Nintendo, The Pokémon Organization via Polygon.
