When is You Can See Everything streaming? What we know.

  • But what if you'd rather ignore the advice from fans saying this movie is a must-see in theaters with a crowd and experience it at home?
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nathan Fielder teamed with director Lance Oppenheim for the Elizabeth Holmes documentary You Can See Everything. And as bonkers as this configuration of facts sounds, the resulting three-hour movie is even wilder than you can predict.

Sources indicate that critics are raving. Audiences snapped up tickets to 2,000 showtimes over the opening weekend. And soon, more moviegoers had a chance to see You Can See Everything as distributor A24 expanded its U.S. theatrical release.

But what if you'd rather ignore the advice from fans saying this movie is a must-see in theaters with a crowd and experience it at home? Or perhaps you just want to know when you can see it again to replay, pause, and freak out on every tiny detail in your own time?

You Can See Everything is available on digital for rental or purchase as of Nov. 24.

While this falls into A24's usual window from theater to digital release, it's earlier than we expected! But perhaps A24 thought the perfect time to drop one of the most talked about movies of the year was right before the U.S. gathers together over Thanksgiving meals. As you pass the turkey, you can casually ask what your uncle thinks about Billy and that bowl of eggs.

For more on why this release date is surprising, check out Mashable's prior reporting below:.

In terms of speculation, Forbes notes that A24 tends to release titles to digital streaming via premium video-on-demand 31 to 38 days after their theatrical debut. Recent examples using such a streaming strategy include The Drama, The Death of Robin Hood, and Tony.

Sources indicate that however, when an A24 movie is crushing at the box office — as You Can See Everything currently is — that window tends to be longer. For instance, the horror hit Backrooms opened on May 29 and didn't come out until July 14, 46 days later.

Then there's the Oscar campaign question. If You Can See Everything is going to be a contender for the Academy Awards, A24 might keep it off streaming for longer. That strategy would encourage potential voters to see the film in theaters. This not only improves the audience's focus on the film (being less distracted by their phones, for one thing), but also offers viewers the chance to enjoy the community experience of seeing You Can See Everything with a crowd.

Sources indicate that last year, when A24 was campaigning Marty Supreme for award season, the studio withheld a digital release for 45 days. However, that movie opened on Christmas Day. So, between You Can See Everything's box office promise, its awards potential, and its fall release date, Fielder fans might have to wait even longer for this title to be available at home if Oscar gold is A24's goal.

But let's say, theoretically, that A24 follows Backrooms or Marty Supreme's digital release strategy. That would mean You Can See Everything would hit digital on Dec. 1.
