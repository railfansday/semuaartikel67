Breaking: God of War Laufey's Faye actor jumps from games to live-action, as Amazon casts Deborah Ann Woll in upcoming series

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After appearing in both God of War Ragnarok and next year's God of War Laufey, Deborah Ann Woll is about to reprise her role as Faye in Amazon Prime Video's upcoming live-action series.

Variety reports that the God of War cast list is now welcoming a second actor pulled straight from Sony Santa Monica's video game series. Deborah Ann Woll first revealed up in the games as Kratos' wife and Atreus' mother during God of War Ragnarok, but she's also about to enter the spotlight proper in God of War Laufey.

Amazon's God of War cast now includes Dave Bautista (Guardians of the Galaxy) as god killer Kratos, Callum Vinson (Chucky) as Atreus, Mandy Patinkin as Odin and Ólafur Darri Ólafsson as Thor. Alastair Duncan is also making the game-to-show jump by stepping back into the figurative shoes of chatty head Mimir.

In a recent development, earlier in the year, the God of War show halted production when former Kratos actor Ryan Hurst suffered an on-set injury. Bautista soon took over the role and production began again in October - all of Hurst's scenes had to be reshot, of course.

According to recent reports, there's no release date yet for the show, but it's noted to follow the Norse era of the games which began in 2018's more serious and stern revamp. Amazon ordered a two-season run for the show, which apparently means both seasons will be filmed back-to-back.

Meanwhile, God of War Laufey was recently delayed from February 2027 to May 2027, giving the PS5 exclusive slightly more breathing room away from Fable, Persona 4 Revival and Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis.

"We will make the most of each moment until then by putting in the finishing touches and pushing the game to its fullest potential," developer Sony Santa Monica stated at the time. "On behalf of everyone on the team, we appreciate your patience and we'll be back to share more updates soon.".

While the game puts Faye in the leading role as she battles with gods in a multi-mythological afterlife, the studio was quick to note that her journey isn't considered a side game. God of War Laufey will still connect to future games in the series and, yep, Kratos will eventually be back, too.

"Genuinely, I thought I was going to be much more offended by it, but one, I'm not on social media, so that helps, and two, I don't know, I feel totally unbothered," Woll said of the online trolls who were targeting Laufey after its reveal this summer. "Because I just know it's great. It's okay if it's not your cup of tea, but in no universe is it bad.".
