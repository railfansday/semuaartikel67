Calvert-Lewin joined Leeds United in 2025 after nine years at Everton.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin has signed a newly introduced contract to stay with Leeds United until 2029.

The former Everton striker, 29, has five goals and one assist so far in the Premier League this season, with Daniel Farke's side sitting seventh in the table.

Confirmation of Calvert-Lewin's deal follows speculation about his future at the club, with reports that he was interesting Manchester United.

Calvert-Lewin scored 15 goals across all competitions during his first season at Elland Road after joining in August 2025, and the form earned him an England recall in March.

He was also called up by Thomas Tuchel this autumn and came off the bench in England's wins against Czech Republic and Spain.

He scored a penalty and had an assist in Leeds' 4-1 thrashing of Chelsea on Saturday.
