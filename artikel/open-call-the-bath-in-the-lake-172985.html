THE BATH IN THE LAKE competition is a Madeby initiative created to recognise architectural proposals that explore the relationship between landscape, water and architecture by taking the bathroom out of its usual setting and placing it in a natural environment.

THE BATH IN THE LAKE competition is a Madeby initiative created to recognise architectural proposals that explore the relationship between landscape, water and architecture by taking the bathroom out of its usual setting and placing it in a natural environment.

The call invites participants to reflect on the idea of bathing, the space that contains it and the way it is inhabited, treating the bathroom as an object of research and examining its relationship with the surroundings.
