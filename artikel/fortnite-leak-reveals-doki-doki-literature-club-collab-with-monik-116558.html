Fortnite might have signed up four new club members, and a leaker spilled their names before Epic could even unlock the door.

Sources indicate that the roster was already crowded, since Treasure Hunters had spent November piling anime crossovers onto Crossbones Isle, from the One Piece crew to Hatsune Miku. This next arrival, however, hails from a game that spends its opening act masquerading as a cutesy dating sim before it starts shredding players’ nerves, which makes it a strange fit for a battle royale that’s apparently making room for it anyway.

According to recent reports, on November 24, HYPEX claimed that Doki Doki Literature Club would land in Fortnite within the week, bringing the club’s four leading ladies along: Sayori, Natsuki, Yuri, and Monika. According to the leaker, no other cosmetics will tag along, so don’t hold out for a poem-shaped pickaxe.

In a recent development, the post built on what HYPEX first dredged up on November 12, when an encrypted set of four female cel-shaded skins surfaced in the game’s files. Each came with a custom lobby pose, at least two sported cloth physics, and one flaunted special hair physics. At the time, the leaker also said the skins would sell as a bundle as well as individually.

It has been revealed that epic Games hasn’t announced the collab yet, and prices remain under wraps. Still, with Treasure Hunters barreling toward its December 5 finale, the club doesn’t have long to make its entrance.

It has been revealed that the choice of franchise raised a few eyebrows, too. Team Salvato’s 2017 visual novel lured players in with cheery classmates and a poetry club before swerving hard into psychological horror, and its Steam page still warns that it isn’t suitable for children.
