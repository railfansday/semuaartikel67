The latest physical sales chart for the UK is in, and in perhaps the most unsurprising bit of news in yonks, Grand Theft Auto 6 is quite the hit.

In a recent development, yes, the lack of a disc in the game's 'physical' release wasn't enough of a deterrent for many picking up Rockstar's latest, as it snatched up first place in its debut week. With EA Sports FC 27 standing ever strong in second, that means Ocarina of Time has been bumped down yet another place to third.

And for those wondering about the real biggest game of the last month, no, Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy hasn't made its way onto the chart yet. Maybe next time, eh?
