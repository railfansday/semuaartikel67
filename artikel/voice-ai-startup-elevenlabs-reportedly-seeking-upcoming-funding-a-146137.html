UPDATED 20:42 EST / NOVEMBER 25 2026.

It has been revealed that artificial intelligence developer ElevenLabs Inc. is reportedly in early talks to raise capital at a $33 billion valuation.

Bloomberg today cited sources as saying that the firm looks to close the round in the first quarter of 2027. According to the report, some investors are pushing for the raise to happen sooner. That hints the planned round may include a secondary sale, a transaction that enables existing investors to cash out their shares.

It’s unclear how much ElevenLabs hopes to raise or from whom. Late-stage startup investments almost always include contributions from existing backers. ElevenLabs’ most recent raise, which valued it at $22 billion, saw the participation of Nvidia Corp.’s NVentures fund, Blackrock, Wellington and several others.

ElevenLabs’ fundraising push reportedly comes amid a surge in customer demand. Its annualized revenue is noted to have grown by $100 million since September, to $700 million.

It has been revealed that founded in 2022, ElevenLabs develops a series of AI models optimized to process audio. Companies use them to field call center inquiries, transcribe meetings, generate music and perform related tasks. It also offers a service called Productions that delivers ready-to-use audio assets.

It has been revealed that the company debuted its newest and most capable model, Eleven v4, in late September. The algorithm’s flagship feature is its ability to tailor its speaking style to each text snippet. It does so by automatically tuning parameters such as tone and pacing.

Users can manually customize Eleven v4’s speaking style by adding instructions to scripts. They can also generate conversations with multiple synthetic speakers. A real-time version of the model, Eleven v4 Turbo, generates audio with only 150 milliseconds of latency for use cases such as customer support.

In a recent development, elevenLabs offers Eleven v4 alongside more narrowly focused models. In May, it introduced an algorithm specifically optimized to dub videos. Additionally, it provides developer tools that make it easier to integrate its AI models into enterprise applications.

It’s possible ElevenLabs is raising upcoming capital to finance more acquisitions. Earlier this month, it agreed to buy Kotoba Inc., a startup that develops audio processing models for companies in Japan. Nikkei Asia reported that the deal is worth $100 million. ElevenLabs will hire more salespeople in Japan over the next few months to grow its market presence.

Recent findings suggest that A few days before the acquisition, ElevenLabs chief executive Mati Staniszewski stated that the company plans to start preparing for an initial public offering in 2028. That time frame suggests the round the software maker is currently raising could be followed by additional venture investments.
