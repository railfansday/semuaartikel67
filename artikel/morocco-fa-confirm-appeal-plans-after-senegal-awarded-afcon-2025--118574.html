Morocco FA confirm appeal plans after Senegal awarded AFCON 2025 title

The Moroccan FA say they proposal to explore all legal avenues to appeal after expressing their “protest” against the CAS ruling to reinstate Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners.

While Senegal won 1-0 against Morocco in the final of AFCON 2025, the match was beset by controversy. Senegal players and staff left the pitch in the 98th minute in protest against the award of a penalty for the hosts, which left the game paused for 14 minutes. The match eventually resumed, Brahim Diaz missed the spot kick and the game went to extra time, which saw Senegal score and go on to lift the trophy.

Sources indicate that but African football governing body CAF retrospectively awarded the title to Morocco two months after the final, stating that Senegal’s conduct during the match had disqualified them as winners. Morocco were named automatic 3-0 winners.

The Senegalese FA took an appeal to the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) which, after a months-long saga, reinstated Senegal as champions on Tuesday.

But Morocco have not given up, citing reports of an alleged telephone conversation between CAS panelist Ulrich Haas and the president of Senegal’s football federation hours before Tuesday’s ruling as one reason they have grounds for an appeal.

Sources indicate that the CAS ruling said: “The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) upheld the appeal by the Senegalese Football Federation (FSF) against the Confederation of African Football (CAF) and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF, in French) concerning the final of the Africa Cup of Nations Morocco 2025 (AFCON 2025). The decision rendered by the CAF Appeal Board is set aside and the decision by the CAF Disciplinary Board is reinstated.

“Consequently, the result obtained at the end of the AFCON 2025 final is confirmed (1-0 for Senegal).

“By blowing his whistle to restart play, the referee considered that the conditions for resuming the match had been met – both teams were on the pitch again, and both expressed their willingness to resume.

“In the Panel’s view, there is no doubt that the effects of the Referee’s decision were limited to the pitch. As such, the Panel considers that the Referee’s decision was covered by the "field of play" doctrine.

“Since Articles 82 and 84 of the AFCON Regulations are intended solely to determine the outcome of a match when a team’s conduct leads the referee to call off the game permanently, they are therefore not applicable in this case.”.

But Morocco have vowed to appeal CAS’s ruling.

Sources indicate that A statement from the Moroccan FA stated: “We take note of the decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport regarding the appeal filed by the Senegalese Football Federation against the decision of the CAF Appeals Committee, relating to the final match of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations. In this context, the Royal Moroccan Football Federation expresses its surprise and protest at the failure to take into account its demand to arrange the necessary legal consequences for practices that would compromise the integrity and credibility of the arbitration procedure, especially what has been confirmed regarding communication between the German referee Ulrich Haas (who sat on the CAS panel) and the president of the Senegalese Football Federation, 48 hours before the decision was issued.

“The Royal Moroccan Football Federation also notes that the decision did not take into account the approach recently adopted by both the International Football Association Board (IFAB) and FIFA, regarding players leaving the field of play without the referee’s permission, and the resulting disciplinary responsibilities and penalties.

“Accordingly, the Royal Moroccan Football Federation confirms its intention to pursue all available legal avenues to appeal the decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport.”.
