📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov came in second during an absolutely incredible Dancing With the Stars finale, and their flawless freestyle is even more impressive knowing how little sleep Amber’s running on. The Olympian told Variety that she was drug tested the night before the finale, saying, “I actually had...you saw the drug testing show up last night. Yeah, so didn’t get much sleep. Third time they’ve tested me this season, by the way, guys, what’s happening? So, I’m excited to get some rest.”.

At this point you’re probably wondering if Dancing With the Stars is drug testing contestants, which, nope! As Variety notes in their Twitter caption, Amber is referring to being tested by USADA (United States Anti-Doping Agency), which is the “national anti-doping organization (NADO) in the United States for Olympic, Paralympic, Pan American, and Parapan American sport.”.

As their website puts it, they’re “charged with managing the anti-doping program, including in-competition and out-of-competition testing, results management processes, drug reference resources, and athlete education for all United States Olympic & Paralympic Committee (USOPC) recognized sport national governing bodies, their athletes, and events.”.

It has been revealed that since Amber is currently training for the U.S. Figure Skating Championships (taking place in Utah this January), she’s being tested by the USADA like all athletes. Which might come as a surprise to everyone who didn’t realize Amber’s been juggling training with DWTS!

Her coach, Damon Allen, explained to KRDO “She's been balancing dance on a rigorous schedule with that, as well as, being able to continue training, getting ready for nationals in January. It's been tiring. But she knows how to recover, and she's got a regimen, and she knows how to take care of her body, and figure out how to be at the top of her game at all times, whether it's in the dance arena or back on the ice.”.
