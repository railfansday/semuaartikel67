📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A lot goes down in Yellowjackets' Season 4 premiere! The team returns to civilization in the past timeline. Meanwhile, in the present, Misty (Christina Ricci) is plotting to kill Shauna (Melanie Lynskey), while Melissa's (Hilary Swank) looking to outwit the former Antler Queen with a major move.

And yet that's not the cliffhanger that the premiere episode goes out on.

Instead, the first episode of Season 4 takes us back to the wilderness, right after Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) has successfully called for help using the frog scientists' SAT phone.

Sources indicate that she races back to camp, shouting, "It worked! We're getting rescued." But as she reaches the team's shelters, she stops in her tracks, and says, "What the fuck?" as she looks at...something.

At the end of Season 3, Natalie conspired with Misty (Samantha Hanratty), Hannah (Ashley Sutton), and Van (Liv Hewson) to sneak away during the hunt, so she could call for help. This means Natalie wasn't present when Mari (Alexa Barajas) became Pit Girl, or for the feast that follows. The following morning is when Shauna (Sophie Nélisse) realizes Hannah has been wearing Natalie's garb to hide her absence from the ritual.

Sources indicate that so, option one is that Natalie, who ran back to the camp not with joy but determination, was hoping to stop the hunt with the news. But she arrived to find Mari like the frog scientists had found Coach Ben (Steven Krueger): ritualistically slaughtered and cut to pieces.

Option 2 relates to the episode's opening credits. Season after season, Yellowjackets has teased details of future episodes through imagery in the title sequence. In the premiere episode, those images included an image of Teen Shauna lying on her back in the snow, badly beaten.

At the end of Season 3, Shauna was furiously crying out to know where Natalie had gone. The answer was to the mountains to use the SAT phone. So, did something happen in the camp between that moment and Nat's return that results in her shocked expression at the end of episode 1? There's only way to find out.
