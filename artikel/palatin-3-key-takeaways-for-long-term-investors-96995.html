The tech giant is directing its research budget away from legacy eye-care programs to focus on high-value metabolic candidates. Three things management stated stand out.

According to recent reports, management is prioritizing the PL2000 lipidated peptide program, which targets the MC4R brain pathway to reduce appetite without activating MC1R skin receptors that cause skin darkening. In addition to injectable peptides, Palatin is advancing an oral small-molecule candidate that offers easier administration for patients managing lifelong conditions. Management targets candidate selection and Investigational Newly introduced Drug application studies in the first half of 2027 to support Phase I safety trials in human subjects later that year.
