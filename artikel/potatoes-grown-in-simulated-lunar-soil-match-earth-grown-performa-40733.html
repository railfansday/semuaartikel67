Potatoes grown in simulated lunar soil match Earth-grown performance

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (90 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that researchers have shown that potatoes grown in simulated lunar soil amended with organic matter are safe to eat and can achieve growth and yields comparable to those produced on Earth. "We found that potatoes can complete their entire life cycle in simulated lunar soil when supplemented with organic matter," said Aymeric Goyer of Oregon State University's Department of Botany and Plant Pathology. "That's an important step toward developing reliable food production systems that could support long-term human exploration of the moon.".
