Grand Theft Auto 6 contains a few fresh interesting pieces of evidence that seem to indicate Rockstar’s popular crime-sim franchise lives in the same universe as its also-popular cowboy series, Red Dead Redemption.

Let’s start with one of the most compelling and obvious connections between GTA 6 and Red Dead: a book you can find in the newly introduced game that directly references events from RDR 2. As spotted and shared online by GameRoll, there’s a book players can find early on in Brian’s Marina in GTA 6 that contains many references to both Red Dead Redemption and its prequel, Red Dead Redemption 2. For example, the book mentions a gunslinger players can meet in RDR 2, as well as mentions of locations from those games such as Blackwater and New Austin.

A more subtle connection, but one that has some interesting implications, can be randomly spotted on the license plates of some cars in GTA 6. Every so often, you might spot a car with a Upcoming Austin license plate. In the world of Red Dead, New Austin is intended to be the game’s version of Texas. Things get a bit weird if you think about all of this too much, as Texas has been mentioned in both franchises before. But then again, GTA V mentions Florida, which has been retconned into Leonida in GTA 6. So this stuff happens sometimes.
