Industry observers note that listen, we all know groceries are more expensive than ever right now. The average American family is spending $50 to $100 more on groceries per week this year than in 2025, which makes a food-focused holiday like Thanksgiving feel more daunting than ever, if your budget is already tight. So while the Instagram-worthy six-cheese macaronis and truffled mashed potatoes sure look tasty, how many of us can really spend that much to make one side? If you’re stressed about Thanksgiving costs this year, here are some of the best Thanksgiving meal deals of 2026 to help you stay on (or under!) budget and still get to enjoy feeding your family their Turkey Day favorites.
