Elizabeth Debicki Thanks the People Who Opened Doors for Her in Her ELLE Women in Hollywood Speech

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, elizabeth Debicki knows that no career is built alone. On Monday night, the actress accepted her honor at ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood celebration, taking a moment to recognize the people who helped make her career possible.

After being introduced by Alexander Skarsgård, Debicki thanked her fellow honorees before reflecting on the support she has received throughout her journey.

“I really love to work. It is my great love,” she said. “And standing here tonight, I feel a really deep sense of gratitude for all the people—the small army of people—who have fought for me to be able to do my work.”.

Recent findings suggest that debicki recalled how casting director Ronna Kress helped her secure an audition for Baz Luhrmann’s The Great Gatsby, which became her breakout film role. At the time, she was just 20 years old, had recently graduated from drama school in Australia, and had never done a screen test.

In a recent development, “Now, Baz, who is my fairy godfather, he fought very, very hard for me after I auditioned for him,” she explained. “But the only reason I was able to get that audition was because Ronna Kress watched the shoddy rookie tape I made back home and somehow managed to convince Baz to sit still long enough to watch it (which, if you know, you know is an actual miracle).”.

Industry observers note that she continued, “Ronna fought very, very hard for the studio to fly me out here to L.A.—I had never been here before—and to read for the part. And truly, that changed everything. That was really, really my door. And Ronna and Baz, they held that open for me.”.

Debicki also praised the other women being recognized that evening: Julianne Moore, Penélope Cruz, Florence Pugh, Inde Navarette, Rosalind Eleazar, and Carrie Coon.

In a recent development, “In your work, your audiences are so privileged to witness your talent and your intelligence,” she said. “And this generosity in the way that you work, it makes us feel sane, and it makes us feel seen. So thank you, so, so much.”.
