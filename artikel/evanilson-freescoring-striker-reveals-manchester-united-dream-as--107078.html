Bournemouth striker Evanilson has revealed that he is a substantial fan of Manchester United - sparking rumours that the Brazilian could make the switch to Old Trafford.

Evanilson has been Bournemouth’s talisman this campaign, scoring in six consecutive Premier League as Marco Rose’s side have shot up the table.

The Cherries have now won nine games in a row across all competitions - including a 3-0 dismantling of Manchester United at Old Trafford in October.

Evanilson scored the pick of the goals, firing home from an audacious angle after rounding Senne Lammens.

The former FC Porto man shared that his favourite club in England is Manchester United - sharing a soft spot for the Red Devils with his fellow countryman, exciting winger Rayan.

Industry observers note that evanilson told Globo Esporte: “So I joke around with Rayan. I think that, here in England, of the big clubs, United are the team I like the most. Rayan mentioned the same thing. When I was a kid, I also used to watch United.”.

Sources indicate that “When Cristiano Ronaldo played for United, I watched loads of his highlights. And, man, knowing that I’m here playing against them….

“This is my third season playing against them. I scored last season and I scored this season at Old Trafford.

“It’s a dream, and I tell everyone this in interviews: it was a dream to play at that stadium, and scoring goals is even more vital.

“So it’s a dream I’ve been fulfilling, scoring a goal against United because of how significant the club is.”.

Evanilson believed his strike at Old Trafford was the finest of his career to date.

He continued: “I think it was the most beautiful goal of my career. Because of the importance of the match and the team we were playing against, scoring a goal like that at Old Trafford.”.

“Without a doubt, that was the most beautiful goal of my career, and I was very happy.”.

“I had a difficult angle there. It was hard to believe, but when I saw the ball going in, it was a moment of enormous happiness.”.

Evanilson’s comments coincide with reports that Manchester United are after a latest striker in the January transfer window.

The Times have reported that Mauricio Pochettino is keen on bringing in another striker to help alleviate the pressure on injury-prone frontman Benjamin Sesko.

Bournemouth paid Porto roughly £40million to secure the signature of Evanilson in the summer 2024, with the Brazilian under contract until the end of the 2028/29 season.

So far this campaign, Evanilson has scored eight goals in 15 appearances across all competitions.
