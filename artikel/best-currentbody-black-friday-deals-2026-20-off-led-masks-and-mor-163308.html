In a recent development, beauty tech titan CurrentBody's Black Friday deals are live and run until December 8. Most of the brand's products are 20 percent off, including bundles. While we haven’t reviewed every bundle, we’ve tested the majority of CurrentBody's devices, and they’re consistently among our top recommendations. The Series 3, for example, is one of the best LED face masks on the market, and the LED Hair Growth Helmet has delivered some of the most noticeable results we've seen in testing.

CurrentBody gadgets aren’t cheap, so I almost always recommend waiting for a sale. And if there’s one time to shop, it’s Black Friday. For more bargains, check out our roundup of the Best Black Friday Deals.

According to recent reports, I already loved the Series 2, but the latest Series 3 is in a different league. The soft, flexible silicone mask now features three additional wavelengths: blue (415 nm), green (532 nm), and yellow (590). That means it can tackle a wider range of skin concerns, including acne, hyperpigmentation, and scarring. It also significantly ups the LED count, from 236 to 750 bulbs, and increases irradiance to 40 mW/cm².

You get four preset treatments that cycle through different wavelengths, plus MyDose, which lets you customize your session including the wavelengths used and treatment time. I have yet to see another LED mask offer this level of customization and variety at this price. Don't get me wrong; it’s still a pricey skincare gadget, even with a discount, but I’d take advantage of this deal, since we're unlikely to see a better one anytime soon.

CurrentBody’s LED Hair Growth Helmet is our favorite at-home treatment for hair loss. It’s cordless, Bluetooth-enabled, and available in two sizes for a more comfortable fit. You only need to wear it for 10 minutes a day, and CurrentBody has thought of the little things that make sticking to a routine easier: cushy earmuffs, a removable faux leather lining, and even built-in music playback, so you don't have to sit alone with your thoughts while you grow out your hair.

Sources indicate that best of all, WIRED reviewer Molly Higgins and her balding roommate both noticed visible results after 12 weeks of consistent use. Molly reported thicker hair, less breakage, and fewer strands caught in her hairbrush; her hairstylist noticed, too. Molly's roommate spotted newly introduced baby hairs sprouting across his scalp. This discount makes the splurge a little less extravagant.
