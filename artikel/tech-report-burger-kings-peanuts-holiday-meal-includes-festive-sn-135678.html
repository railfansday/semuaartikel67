Snoopy, Charlie Brown and Woodstock are taking over Burger King with a new holiday-themed menu and adorable collectibles.

In a recent development, whenever Burger King drops a upcoming limited-edition themed menu, I basically grab my keys and head out the door. Yes, it is that serious! I was lucky to grab a King Jr. Meal with a Hello Kitty toy the day before my local BK sold out of them completely. If you like collectibles, I’m sure you feel the same way.

According to recent reports, between last year’s Spongebob Meal and this year’s Mandolorian Meal, I have been really happy with all the new food drops from Burger King. I dream of eating a Whopper off of a square yellow bun again. So when I saw that Burger King will have a limited-edition Peanuts Holiday Meal this year, I cleared my schedule for the day the meal drops. Here’s everything you need to know about the festive new menu. Good grief!

The star of the collaboration is the Peanuts Pack, a holiday feast served in a collectible Snoopy Doghouse Box. But the packaging isn’t the only fun surprise: Each Peanuts Pack also comes with one of two exclusive Snoopy holiday beanies. Talk about a cute way to top off your meal!

For the main course, guests can choose between a flame-grilled Whopper with cheese or a 10-piece order of chicken nuggets. The nuggets come with a upcoming Woodstock Golden Ranch dipping sauce, which I can’t wait to try.

Here are the other upcoming treats that come in the meal:.

The Peanuts King Jr. Meal comes in its own Snoopy Doghouse Box and includes a choice of hamburger, cheeseburger or four-piece chicken nuggets. Kids can pick applesauce or french fries for a side, along with milk or apple juice. The best part? Each meal comes with one of eight collectible holiday Peanuts toys: Charlie Brown, Lucy, Linus, Sally, three Snoopy designs or a Snoopy doghouse.

The Burger King Peanuts Holiday Meal arrives Tuesday, Dec. 1 at participating restaurants nationwide. The limited-time offerings will be available while supplies last, so fans should initiative their visits accordingly. Like I noted, these cute collectibles can sell out quickly.

Sources indicate that between the Snoopy beanies and the adorable collectible toys (not to mention the cheesy potato tots!), this collaboration is a pretty sweet way to celebrate the holidays. And if you’re already planning to watch A Charlie Brown Christmas this season, a Snoopy-themed meal sounds like a perfect addition to the festivities.
