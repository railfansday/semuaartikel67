Get your cowboy boots ready, because The Hunting Wives is officially back for another wild season filled with secrets, scandal, and lots of drama! Of course, this has us all thinking about whether or not we’ll be back for another rodeo in Texas with a possible third season. And, we can tell you that you don’t have to put those chaps back in the closet, because the show is officially returning for another season. So what else can we expect?

Here’s everything we know about The Hunting Wives season 3.

We all know how obsessed we are with The Hunting Wives and apparently Netflix has noticed too as the show was officially renewed for a third season even before the second one was released.

“Buckle up, because Season 3 will be even more scandalous and twisted,” remarked Creator/Showrunner/Writer/EP, Rebecca Cutter. Oh, we’re so ready!

Industry observers note that while the first season was mostly based off of May Cobb’s iconic book, it’s actually a standalone novel. Which means that what is going to happen next is totally up to the show creatives and also that we have no idea what exactly will happen next. At least things in Maple Brook are never not brewing, so we can definitely expect tons of more drama and some super hot moments.

Industry observers note that the Hunting Wives season 2 came out relatively rapidly as it only took a bit over a year to come back. And with an early renewal, it’s totally possible that we get to see that pattern again next year. Hopefully, it’ll follow Netflix’s old format and come back in late 2027, nearly a year later after season 2.
