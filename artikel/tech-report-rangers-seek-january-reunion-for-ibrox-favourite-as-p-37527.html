Rangers are ready to reignite their interest in former fan favourite Vaclav Cerny, with Besiktas reportedly ready to let him leave in January.

According to reports, Rangers manager Derek McInnes highlighted interest in the winger during the summer transfer window, and the Glasgow giants reportedly held discussions with his club to bring the Czech international back to Ibrox permanently.

Those talks are understood to have broken down over Besiktas’ asking price. However, Rangers are ready to return to the negotiating table when the transfer window reopens, with reports claiming the club now believes that he could be made available in January after failing to impress in Istanbul this season.

Cerny, 29, spent a successful season on loan at Rangers during the 2024/25 campaign, notching 18 goals and assisting a further nine across all competitions after being signed by Philippe Clement from Wolfsburg that summer. However, a permanent deal didn’t materialise, as the club changed ownership and named Russell Martin as the club’s new head coach.

Recent findings suggest that after leaving Rangers, he moved to the Turkish Super League instead, and performed admirably in his debut campaign at the Tupras Stadium, registering 15 goal contributions as Besiktas finished fourth. However, he hasn’t replicated that form so far this term, last scoring in a 4-1 win over Marseille in the Europa League back on September 17.

Meanwhile, on-loan Rangers winger Oscar Cortes looks set to return to the club in the fresh year, with reports claiming Huracan have turned down their option to make the deal permanent at the end of the Argentinian season, which is scheduled to end in December.

It has been revealed that cortes, 22, originally signed for Rangers on loan from Ligue 1 outfit RC Lens in February 2024, scoring once and assisting twice in his first seven appearances, before injury ended his season. After joining permanently for £4.5million at the end of the campaign, the Colombian’s injury troubles followed him into the new season, and he missed several games after developing a knee injury in the 0-0 draw with Hearts on opening day.

Industry observers note that dogged by various challenges throughout his first full season at Ibrox, he made just 10 starts and was allowed to sign on loan for Spanish second tier side Sporting Gijon. He struggled in Spain, though, failing to score in eight appearances, and had his loan cut short before moving to Huracan on a similar deal.

According to recent reports, his stint at Buenos Aires has been more successful, though. A regular in the Huracan starting XI, he has registered nine goal contributions across the season. However, despite his strong form, reports in South America say he will be allowed to return to Govan when their season ends next month, with Huracan unable to pay the Ibrox side's asking price of around £4.2million.

The fee has also scared off Atletico Nacional, who were mulling over a deal to bring him back to Colombia recently, with the club understood to have cooled their interest over Rangers’ asking price for Cortes.
