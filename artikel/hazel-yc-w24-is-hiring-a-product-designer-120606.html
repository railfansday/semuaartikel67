AI-enabled procurement for government.

Our mission at Hazel is simple and powerful. We use AI to help U.S. government purchasing teams buy at the speed of need with unparalleled efficiency and cost-effectiveness. It is a $2.7T problem every single year, and Hazel is at the forefront with Federal and State, Local, and Education (SLED) agencies.

Hazel has identified a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to solve this by deploying cutting-edge AI to the public sector and unlocking the full potential of our nation. We empower our partners to move with expediency against the most pressing challenges of this century. Hazel builds AI for the public interest.

Hazel is transforming government procurement: how government agencies purchase goods and services from commercial vendors. Our federal partners use our AI-forward bid evaluations solution to assess vendors more accurately, reliably, and rapidly. With the federal government under increasing pressure to accelerate acquisitions, our tools give them a real edge.

In a recent development, our COTS products, led by Document Builder and Evaluations, serve state & local governments, schools, and special authorities. The users are procurement officers working through long, high-stakes documents and decisions, and they deserve software that is as clear and trustworthy as it is powerful.

It has been revealed that as Hazel's contract Product Designer, you will elevate the user experience and visual design of products government teams rely on daily. Hazel has built a substantial product based on deep understanding of our consumers' needs. In this role, you will refine our UI/UX and establish standards for our engineering team going forward.

There are three main deliverables:.

Federal. Upgrade the UI/UX of the bid evaluations solution we are deploying for our largest federal contract.

Commercial. Improve the UX of our COTS product flows and define consistent UI elements to unify the product design.

Playbook. Deliver a practical style guide and Figma component set so Hazel engineers can ship consistent, high-quality UI after the engagement ends.

Audit Hazel's existing workflows and identify the highest-impact UI/UX improvements.

Design high-fidelity screens, flows, and interaction states for complex, document-heavy workflows, including how people review and act on AI output.

Work directly with Hazel's founders and engineers, sharing work early and iterating quickly.

Hand off production-ready designs and review what ships to ensure quality.

Build a reusable set of Figma components and documented patterns.

Write a practical style guide our engineers can follow for newly introduced features, including accessibility basics (Section 508 / WCAG 2.1 AA).

Proven experience as a product designer or similar role, with a portfolio of shipped work.

Experience designing complex, workflow-heavy B2B applications (dense tables, long forms, multi-step reviews and approvals).

Experience working within and contributing to a design system.

Ability to improve an existing product without starting from scratch.

Fast turnaround and independence: you ship in days, not weeks.

Expert in Figma or similar tools.

Excitement about bringing innovation to the public sector.

Has worked at a startup or startup-like environment.

Building a design system from scratch and documenting it for others.

Front-end coding experience (React, TypeScript).

Designing AI features, including surfacing sources and letting users review and correct model output.

Document-centric interfaces: reading, annotating, or comparing long documents.

Working with or adjacent to the public sector.

Accessibility work to Section 508 or WCAG 2.1 AA.

Federal acquisition or source selection.
