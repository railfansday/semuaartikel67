Bilt Offers JetBlue Elite Status For December Rent Day—With A Fast Track To Keep It Through 2027

🌐 Source: Viewfromthewing • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 50 min read (10020 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Bilt’s December Rent Day offers a three month trial of JetBlue elite status, with a much easier path to keeping it through January 2028.
  • When Bilt added JetBlue as a transfer partner last month, I wrote that status matches could be coming.
  • There’s also up to a 100% bonus redeeming Bilt points for credit card statement credits, a targeted spending offer, and Brittany Snow playing Rent Free.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bilt’s December Rent Day offers a three month trial of JetBlue elite status, with a much easier path to keeping it through January 2028. Silver and Gold members get Mosaic 1 at no points cost. Platinum members start at Mosaic 2, which means EvenMore seats at booking instead of waiting until closer to departure. When Bilt added JetBlue as a transfer partner last month, I wrote that status matches could be coming. You qualify based on your Bilt status, so you don’t need to bring elite status from another airline. There’s also up to a 100% bonus redeeming Bilt points for credit card statement credits, a targeted spending offer, and Brittany Snow playing Rent Free. I have no idea who she is. I’m much more interested in the JetBlue offer than cashing out Bilt points at under 1.5 cents apiece. Three Months Of JetBlue Status, With A Path Through January 2028 Here’s the initial status offered: Bilt Status   JetBlue Trial Status   Points Requirement Blue Mosaic 1 10,000 Bilt points Silver Mosaic 1 None Gold Mosaic 1 None Platinum Mosaic 2 None Blue members have to transfer 10,000 points to trigger the match. Everyone has three months after receiving the matched status to earn and receive enough qualifying tiles to extend it through January 31, 2028: Status Earned   Challenge Requirement   Normal Annual Requirement Mosaic 1 10 tiles 50 tiles Mosaic 2 25 tiles 100 tiles Mosaic 3 40 tiles 150 tiles Under JetBlue’s normal earning rules, a tile takes $100 in qualifying spending on flights, JetBlue Vacations or TrueBlue Travel, or $1,000 in JetBlue credit card spending. So the travel equivalents are $1,000, $2,500 and $4,000. Taxes and fees don’t count. The corresponding spend amounts would be $10,000, $25,000 and $40,000, or you could combine tiles from travel and card spending. Spending on a Bilt card isn’t JetBlue card spending. You can earn JetBlue tiles via travel on JetBlue, United Airlines, JetBlue Vacations; cobrand card spend; purchased or earned bonus tiles (but gifted tiles don’t count); and the annual 25 Tile Bonus awarded to JetBlue Premier Wcardmembers. Folks who have received a past JetBlue challenge are eligible for this offer. Where The Status Is Most Useful The immediate value is better seating and fewer fees on trips you already intend to take. Mosaic 1 includes one checked bag, priority services, eligible same-day switches, and complimentary EvenMore selection starting 36 hours before departure for the member and up to two companions, subject to availability. Mosaic 2 adds a second checked bag and moves EvenMore selection to booking for the member and up to two companions. Mosaic 3 includes four Premium Upgrade Certificates, the upcoming name for Move to Mint certificates (and those meeting the challenge requirements will receive them). Main Base fares exclude important seating benefits. And Mosaic 1 through 3 don’t include Mosaic 4 lounge access. JetBlue has also announced complimentary upgrades to its new BlueFirst domestic first class for all Mosaic tiers as equipped aircraft enter service. These are space available upgrades, with higher Mosaic levels ahead of lower ones. Main Base and EvenMore Base fares don’t qualify. Premium Upgrade Certificates can confirm Mint or BlueFirst, including on eligible TrueBlue points tickets. Four certificates don’t necessarily mean four upgrades because the number required varies by flight. Starting February 1, JetBlue will also let Mosaic 2 members select a certificate through Perks You Pick. I’ve asked whether matched members get that selection. There’s A United Airlines Angle, Too Under JetBlue’s Blue Sky partnership, Mosaic members receive benefits on eligible United Airlines trips booked through JetBlue, including a checked bag, preferred seats after booking and Economy Plus at check-in. Mosaic 1 gets Group 2 boarding, while Mosaic 2 and above get Group 1. United flights booked through JetBlue also earn tiles under the ordinary program. So you can keep JetBlue status through travel on United. There’s No Transfer Bonus Bilt points transfer to JetBlue at 1:1. This December announcement doesn’t include a bonus on those transfers. I’m generally not moving Bilt points to JetBlue for ordinary redemptions worth around 1.3 cents apiece. One option worth comparing is a regular paid JetBlue ticket purchased through Bilt’s portal using points. Under ordinary revenue-ticket rules, that should earn tiles, whereas transferring points into TrueBlue doesn’t earn tiles. Portal bookings do give up JetBlue’s direct-booking points bonus. The more interesting transfer uses remain partner business class awards, such as the 54,000-point Condor business class awards from the East Coast to Frankfurt I’ve covered, when available. Mosaic doesn’t add benefits to a Condor flight. If you already have the JetBlue Premier card, its 15% award rebate includes partner flights. A 54,000-point award returns 8,100 TrueBlue points, for a net cost of 45,900. You need all 54,000 upfront, and the rebate comes back as JetBlue points, not Bilt points. A 100% Statement Credit Bonus Still Only Gets You About 1.1 Cents Eligible cardholders get increased statement credit redemption value from November 20 at 9 a.m. Eastern through December 2 at 2:59 a.m. Eastern. The minimum redemption is 1,000 points. This bonus depends on which Bilt credit card you have, not your Bilt elite status. At the usual 0.55-cent statement credit redemption rate, the math is: Bilt Card   Bonus   Value Per Point   Credit For 10,000 Points Blue 25% 0.6875 cents $68.75 Obsidian 50% 0.825 cents $82.50 Palladium 100% 1.1 cents $110 I wouldn’t do this. Even Palladium’s doubled rate is less than the $125 in travel those same 10,000 points buy through Bilt, before considering Hyatt or airline transfers. I passed on last month’s gift card bonus, too. Two Separate Opportunities To Earn 1,000 Extra Points Some cardholders will receive a targeted offer for 1,000 bonus points after $500 in everyday purchases from November 20 at 9 a.m. Eastern through November 30 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. Rent and mortgage payments don’t count. Check your account for eligibility. Then the usual Rent Day double points apply on December 1, with a cap of 1,000 additional points: Bilt Blue: 2 points per dollar on everyday spending. Bilt Obsidian: 6 points in your selected dining or grocery category, 4 on travel and 2 on other everyday spending. Bilt Palladium: 4 points per dollar on everyday spending. Rent and tax payments don’t qualify for the extra points (tax payments usually don’t earn at all). On Palladium, $500 in eligible December 1 spending earns the full 1,000-point Rent Day bonus on top of the usual 1,000 points. The holiday shopping offer ends the night before Rent Day. The same purchase can’t satisfy both offers. A targeted Palladium member with $500 of ordinary spending before the deadline and another $500 on December 1 could earn 2,000 promotional bonus points across the two. Brittany Snow, Free Rent And December Experiences Brittany Snow is this month’s Rent Free guest. The game opens November 20 at noon Eastern and closes December 1 at 3 p.m. Eastern, with results after 4 p.m. The top 10 members win a month’s rent up to $2,500, with additional rent winners depending on how well she does. Rent winners also get a signed copy of September Letters. The next 100 receive 1,000 points and the next 500 receive 100 points. There’s also a November 22 brunch with Snow and September Letters co-founder Jaspre Guest at The Twenty Two in New York, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. It’s $45 or 3,000 points, includes bites and cocktails, and opens for booking November 20 at 9 a.m. Eastern. Guests must be at least 21. The booking is final sale. The book itself is $20 or 2,000 points, with 100 copies available beginning November 20 at 9 a.m. Eastern. Members can apply up to $20 of their monthly Bilt Cash allowance. I’d use available Bilt Cash rather than redeem points at a penny each. Shipping is expected in two to three weeks, and sales are final. Members can write letters through the app, choosing whether to keep them private or share them. Select Bilt Alliance buildings will also have September Letters mailboxes, with letters mailed back to their writers in a year. Bookings for the following experiences open Saturday, November 21 at 11:50 a.m. Eastern for Gold and Platinum, and noon for everyone else: Dining: December 1 meals at 40 restaurants across nine cities. Most cost $35–$78 or 2,500–5,000 points. There are three-course menus at most venues, four courses at Hutong and omakase at seven Bondi locations. Drink add-ons cost $20–$46 and must be purchased when booking. Up to $50 Bilt Cash can be applied to one dining experience, with the rest paid in cash. Book by November 30 at 5 p.m. Eastern. There’s also first access to American Cut’s December 31 cocktail party in New York at 6 p.m., priced at $250, with 20 seats. Comedy: 20 highlights December 4–19. Club experiences cost $40 or 2,500 points, except Mic Drop MANIA in Chandler at $30 or 2,000 points, with two drinks included. Premium experiences cost $75 or 5,000 points and include two drinks and light bites. Austin’s The Creek & The Cave is among the venues. Members can use up to $50 Bilt Cash toward a comedy show for the month. Booking closes at 11 a.m. Eastern on the show date, or when sold out. Fitness: Free December 1 SoulCycle and Barry’s classes, with 1,175 and 580 spots respectively. Bookings close November 30 at 7 p.m. Eastern. Dining allows four seats per member per booking, and comedy allows four tickets per person. These experiences are final sale. I’d use Bilt Cash before points: a common $60 dinner costs 4,000 points, but applying $50 Bilt Cash leaves $10 to pay and keeps your points available for travel. And on December 1, Bilt’s cafe at 837 Washington Street in New York will offer members a complimentary Daniel Boulud tasting from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern: lobster vol-au-vent (with a vegetarian option), warm apple chausson, a hot chocolate shake and warm spiced cider.
