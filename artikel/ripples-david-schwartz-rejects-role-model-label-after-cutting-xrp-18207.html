Ripple CTO emeritus David Schwartz has replied to XRP holders who criticized him for selling most of his XRP with a Saturday Night Live GIF of Natalie Portman saying, "I never said I was a role model.".

Recent findings suggest that according to Schwartz, his XRP holdings are down 88%, from a peak of roughly 26 million tokens to 3 million. His Bitcoin went from about 1,500 coins to one, and his Ether went from about 40,000 to six.

According to recent reports, schwartz wrote that he "really doesn't like risk." According to Schwartz, he and his wife agreed years ago to sell some of their coins every time crypto reached a upcoming all-time high. In 2025, Schwartz sold around 2.5 million XRP and paid about $1.7 million in capital gains tax on the sale. He had received these tokens in 2020 and 2023.

Schwartz mentioned that all of his sales were made under written trading looks, and a lawyer approved each strategy at least 30 days before each sale. According to Schwartz, his largest risk asset is still his stake in Ripple.

It has been revealed that schwartz also said that he was a skeptic from the start and did not believe in the early price records of crypto. "When XRP hit 10 cents, that seemed ridiculous. When ETH hit $1, that seemed ridiculous," he wrote.

Recent findings suggest that in his view, the price of XRP today depends on overall sentiment in the crypto market, and fees on the network barely move it. Schwartz allows that the token could capture some of the value of a successful ecosystem in the future, but there are no guarantees. On Monday, the price of XRP stands at $0.956.

It has been revealed that on Nov. 16, Schwartz also posted a review of the XRPL codebase by the Qwen AI model. "I asked Qwen to rate my work on the XRPL software. It was mostly good. But this was harsh and it's not wrong," he wrote.

Sources indicate that according to the review, the overall architecture of XRPL is good. Still, Qwen said that some components of the code are too tightly coupled, and the migration to a upcoming testing framework stopped halfway. The AI model also called the code generation in XRPL opaque.

Several XRPL developers replied to the post and said that they are already rewriting some of these parts of the code. Other customers asked for formal verification of the XRPL core before the new features planned for 2027 go live.
