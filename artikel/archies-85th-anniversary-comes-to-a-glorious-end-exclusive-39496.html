In a recent development, america’s favorite teenager, Archie Andrews, is closing out his 85th anniversary in style, and ScreenRant is pleased to bring fans an exclusive first look. Archie, Jughead, Betty, Veronica and the rest of the Riverdale gang have become pop culture icons, known and beloved around the world, and in 2026 they celebrate 85 years of excellence.

According to recent reports, debuting in December 1941’s Pep Comics #22, Archie Andrews and his pals and gals would promptly become the face of the organization, eventually eclipsing its offerings in other genres. Over the past eight and a half decades, Archie Comics has continually reinvented itself for the times, always seeming to find humor and heart in teenage life, no matter the year. In recent times, Archie Comics has emerged as one of the most forward-thinking publishers in the industry. The publisher created the line’s first openly gay character in Kevin Keller, and branched out into acclaimed horror comics too.

It has been revealed that recently, Archie and Oni Press joined forces to launch a newly introduced line of titles designed to introduce the Riverdale crew to new audiences. The imprint’s flagship title, Archie, debuted to praise from fans and critics alike, while its second offering, a slightly more family-friendly take on Sabrina, the Teenage Witch, is off to a strong start as well. A third book, Archie in Hell, arrived in shops this month too. Now, this December, the anniversary celebration is coming to a spectacular end, but the party is just kicking off.

According to recent reports, today, Archie Comics and Oni Press have revealed details about December’s Archie #4. Released to coincide with the character’s debut that same month 85 years ago, the issue will be an incredible 56 pages long, and 100% returnable for retailers. Archie #4 will ship with several variant covers, and they are shared here, as are a handful of exclusive preview pages.

Archie and Oni have assembled some of today’s top talent to help close out Archie’s 85th anniversary. The current Archie creative team of Ben H. Winters and Fabio Moon provide the main story, while Kelly Sue DeConnick, Ryan North, Derek Charm, Leonardo Romero, Caroline Cash and Dan Parent all provide their talent for this joyous celebration.

In a recent development, archie and his friends have survived decades of changing markets and tastes, and have stayed relevant for 85 years. While many characters from the Golden Age are still in circulation today, few outside of Superman, Batman and Wonder Woman have achieved the same levels as Archie Andrews. The concept, and the characters, have proven to be quite versatile, as well as relatable, which is another factor contributing to Archie’s long-term success. The success of Oni’s new line of Archie titles is proof that the character still has many years of stories left in him.

In a recent development, archie’s 85th anniversary is coming to a close this December, but the two publishers are just getting started on a fresh era for the venerable character. Oni and Archie’s new collaboration is off to a strong start. Not only has it hooked in a new generation of Archie fans, but it has also appealed to long-time readers as well. The future is bright for America's favorite teenager.

Sources indicate that archie #4 is on sale December 30 from Archie and Oni Press! FOC is November 30!
