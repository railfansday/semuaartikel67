Honestly, we can’t think of anything better than a galette. They've got all the delicious elements of pie—flaky, buttery crust and tender baked fruit—but none of the fussy technique. With galettes, you don’t have to worry about any crimping, latticework, or blind baking. Sometimes, it's okay to take the easy road. This classic apple galette is one of our favorite examples. Warm, comforting, and bursting with sweet, spiced flavor, it’s everything you love about apple pie with a fraction of the effort.

What People Are Saying:“I've already made this twice. Really really nice recipe. So fast and simple, not too sweet and not at all goopy. This will be my go-to filling for apple galettes from now on.” - PurpleRing.

According to recent reports, first, combine the apples, brown sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Then, preheat the oven to 400° and prepare a baking sheet by lining it with parchment paper. Coat a clean surface with a dusting of flour, then roll the chilled pie dough into a 12” circle that is about ⅛” thick. Transfer the dough to the baking sheet.

It has been revealed that arrange the apple slices on top of the dough in alternating rows, leaving about a 1 ½” border around the edge. Then, fold the dough edge nearest to you toward the center and rotate the baking sheet, repeating this step until all 4 edges of the dough are folded in toward the fruit.

Then place the galette into the oven to bake for 40 to 45 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the fruit is fragrant and tender. A galette is essentially an open-faced pie. Thinly sliced apples are tossed in cinnamon and sugar and layered on top of a single sheet of pie crust. The crust is folded over the apples in a rough circle and baked until the crust is flaky. Apple pie has a top and bottom crust and is usually formed in a pie dish. The extra crust and overall shape are the only real differences, and for the apple obsessed, galettes provide the perfect ratio of crust to filling.

Anything you'd serve with an apple pie would work here as well. That means your favorite ice cream, frozen yogurt, caramel sauce, or candied nuts would be great galette sidekicks.

Any leftover galette can be stored for up to 4 days in the refrigerator.
