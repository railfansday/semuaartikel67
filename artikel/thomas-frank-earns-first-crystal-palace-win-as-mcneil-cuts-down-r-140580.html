Football seasons can pivot on certain moments and Thomas Frank will hope that Crystal Palace’s turning point came 17 minutes from full time here. Deservedly trailing to Mamadou Sarr’s goal having been second best throughout against Real Sociedad, this game – and perhaps the south London club’s entire trajectory – was turned on its head after Jon Martín was sent off for denying Jean-Philippe Mateta an equaliser.

In a recent development, the France striker made no mistake with his penalty before Dwight McNeil – who made up for scoring an own goal in Saturday’s defeat by Coventry capped an extraordinary comeback in Frank’s first home match since succeeding Pierre Sage. Despite Yéremy Pino’s saved penalty in stoppage time and a number of other chances that were squandered, Dean Henderson pulled off a stunning late save to seal the victory and revive Palace’s Europa League campaign. Suddenly Palace have hope where there appeared to be none and the Premier League’s bottom side will head into Sunday’s crucial showdown with Hull with a spring in their step.
