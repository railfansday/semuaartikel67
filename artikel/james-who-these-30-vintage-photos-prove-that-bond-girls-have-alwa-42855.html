I’m as excited as the next person about this week’s news that Robert Pattinson will be the next Bond—not so much because I actually want to see the next installment in the James Bond franchise (I mean…I will, eventually!) but because it gives me a culturally relevant excuse to revisit all the classic Bond movies and the iconic style they pioneered.

In a recent development, yes, James Bond has always looked great on film, whether he’s played by Roger Moore or Daniel Craig or Sean Connery, but the real fashionistas of the franchise were the Bond girls who gave me a very unrealistic expectation of just how glamorous and high-stakes life as an adult woman would be. Let’s take a moment to appreciate their onscreen fashion, from Grace Jones’s May Day to Michelle Yeoh’s Wai Lin.
