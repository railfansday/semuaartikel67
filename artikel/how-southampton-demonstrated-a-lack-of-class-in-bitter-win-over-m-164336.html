By BOBBY MANZI, FOOTBALL REPORTER.

Published: 16:57 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 18:50 EST, 27 November 2026.

Southampton embarrassed themselves in their first meeting with Middlesbrough since the Spygate scandal as tempers flared at St Mary’s.

The club conducted themselves disgracefully in a bitter reunion six months on from their ejection from last season’s Championship play-off final.

Ryan Manning tucked James Bree’s cutback past Radek Vitek on 59 minutes to open the scoring for Saints before attacker Finn Azaz brought the contest to boiling point.

The former Boro forward scored Southampton’s second goal late on and then ran the length of the pitch to goad the away supporters by pretending to cry.

It sparked a fracas as Azaz and Will Lankshear - who wasn’t even at Boro last season when tensions were at their highest between the clubs - squared up, while travelling fans tried to get over the barricade.

It has been revealed that speaking after the match, Azaz said: 'They sang about me home and away last season, so I was just waiting for my moment to get one back. I have no regrets whatsoever.'.

In a bitter reunion, Ryan Manning tucked James Bree’s neat cutback past Radek Vitek on 58 minutes before Azaz added a second as Southampton remained unbeaten at home in 10 months. Saints, though, were needlessly provocative throughout.

The scenes calmed post-match but Southampton’s off-field operation humiliated the club - taking their celebration too far.

Southampton's Finn Azaz mocked Middlesbrough's fans by pretending to cry after scoring.

His celebration - in their first meeting since last season's 'Spygate' - sparked a brawl, with Azaz being booked.

Hellberg cried in his press conference at Southampton last season when Boro lost in the play-offs semi-final before being reinstated. Saints reminded him of that incident by playing Oasis' Stop Crying Your Heart Out over the speakers. Throughout the match, the Saints fans sang: 'We saw you cry on the tele.'.

Shamelessly, they added a box of tissues to the podium in the press conference room, which mortified Eckert, who removed them before Hellberg gave his post-match remarks.

‘I hope everyone who came here from the Middlesbrough staff knows that these things don't come from me,' Eckert said after removing the box of tissues, having stared at them for 20 seconds.

‘Everything you do in defeat and if you win you do in class and I try to do that. I hope that there's an appreciation that this doesn't come from me.’.

According to recent reports, hellberg added: 'In general, those who show more emotions and stand up for what is right are brave people. Hiding behind people and being afraid of emotions, that's the problem we have in the world a little bit.

'We have to hide from everything, how people are feeling, mental health and everything. It’s crucial we stand up for that – I'm not afraid of showing feelings.'.

Saints beat Middlesbrough in last season’s play-off semi-final but were booted out by the EFL four days before the final after Daily Mail Sport revealed Southampton had sent their very own Johnny English to spy on Boro’s training sessions.

Both managers called for an armistice before the match but that memo clearly hadn't reached the Southampton marketing team, who posted an inflammatory video on social media.

Hellberg cried in his press conference at Southampton last season and a box of tissues were shamelessly added  to the podium in the press conference room after today's match.

The uncouth 79-second film mocked Boro, with the narrator declaring, ‘they got another go, their story continued’ over footage of Oli McBurnie’s winning goal for Hull City in the play-off final was also projected onto the big screen just eight minutes before kick-off.

'I didn't like the video this morning,’ Eckert noted. If we look back a couple of months, the reality is both clubs have lost.

‘One club has lost off the pitch, and the other club has lost on the pitch. It’s an episode that is good to close for both clubs to move forward.

It has been revealed that ‘It’s time to move on at one point. For me, that episode from last year is closed. I've apologised. I've apologised to Kim (Hellberg). I've invited him for a drink. I just hope that we can do everything we do with class.'.

Considering Southampton started this season on minus four points for the breaches, their supporters’ staggering endorsement of the cheating was as indiscreet as the espionage itself.

It has been revealed that ‘We’ll spy when we want,’ sang the Southampton fans – a reminder of when their spy kid tried to conceal himself behind a tree near Middlesbrough’s training ground to film their training session. The EFL discovered three further spying missions on investigation.

Southampton fully embraced their cheats tag as they claimed three points, responding to chants of ‘you cheating b*******, you know what you are’ with sarcastic applause.

Industry observers note that victory moves Saints within four points of Middlesbrough, who remain top of the table going into the weekend but could slip down as far as fifth. Sadly, that was overshadowed by their crass conduct.
