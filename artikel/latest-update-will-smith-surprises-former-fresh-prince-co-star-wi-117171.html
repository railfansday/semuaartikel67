Will Smith had nothing but superlatives for his former The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air co-star Tatyana Ali on Tuesday night’s (Nov. 24) season 35 finale of Dancing With the Stars. Ali and her professional dance partner, Jan Ravnik, performed three times, earning high marks for their salsa to Hugel & Solto’s “Jamaican (Bam Bam),” jive to Wilson Pickett’s “Land of 1000 Dances” and freestyle to Cab Calloway’s “Minnie the Moocher” and Judy Garland’s “Over the Rainbow.”.

According to recent reports, “I am amazed,” a smiling Smith told Ali in a surprise video that aired before the season’s winner was revealed. “I am in awe, I am inspired. Keep doing what you’re doing. I love you,” he added as he blew a kiss to the camera.

Industry observers note that ali and Ravnik ended up in fourth place at the end of the night, with Paralympic athlete Ezra Frech and his professional partner Daniella Karagach taking home the mirror ball trophy. After the crowning, Ali and Ravnik spoke to ABC 30 and the professional dancer said he was still blown away by the A-list support the pair got. “Elmo? I mean, Will Smith, yes, but Elmo?” Ravnik joked after the fuzzy star dropped in to say hello earlier in the episode to his old friend Ali and let the pair know all his friends on Sesame Street were pulling for them.

Ali said she was totally taken aback by the shout-outs from Smith, Elmo and the Black Eyed Peas’ will.i.am. “I was surprised by all of them. Did you know?” Ali asked Ravnik, who said he got emotional when he saw Ali’s family reacting to their performances.

“Tonight was magical,” Ali stated. “From the first performance all the way through to [the last one] … tonight was really miraculous.”.

Industry observers note that ali, 47, began her career as a child actress on Sesame Street in 1984 before landing the co-starring role as Ashley Banks on The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air, the 1990 sitcom that helped propel Smith to global superstardom. In addition to appearing on the shows Zoe Ever After, Abbott Elementary and the 2023 Fresh Prince reboot Bel-Air, Ali released her debut album, Kiss the Sky, in 1998, collaborated with Smith on her 1998 single “Boy You Knock Me Out” and on his 1999 Willennium track “Who Am I.”.
