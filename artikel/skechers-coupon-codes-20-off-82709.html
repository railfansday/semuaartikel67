According to recent reports, like many Newly introduced Yorkers, I’m always looking for new shoes. Living in a walking city means burning through shoes faster than you can believe and always needing new pairs that are comfortable for an active lifestyle. That’s why, instead of heels and dress shoes, you see everyone in NYC wearing some riff on comfortable sneakers. Brooks, Hoka, New Balance, and yes, Skechers.

Industry observers note that skechers isn’t the flashiest brand, but its shoes prioritize comfort and reasonable prices across a slew of categories: running, walking, hiking, home, and work, to name a few. In the 90s, it aligned with utility boots and grunge fashion, but it quickly grew to focus on casual, comfortable, and athletic shoes for basically everyone. Today, you’ll see some of Skechers' most prominent shoe lines, including “Cozy Fit”, “Slip-ins”, and “Wide Fit”.
