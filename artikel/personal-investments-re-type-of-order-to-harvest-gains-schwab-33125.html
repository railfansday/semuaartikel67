Note that if you are harvesting gains, you want to sell the shares with the smallest long-term gain.For example, suppose you can harvest a $20K gain, and you have 500 shares bought for $30, 1000 shares bought for $40, and the price is now $50. You can sell either lot and rebuy at $50. If you sell the $40 lot, then the next 1000 shares you sell in a later year will have a $50 basis. If you sell the $30 lot, then the next 500 shares you sell in a later year will have a $50 basis but the 500 shares after that will have a $40 basis, for a higher capital gain in a later year.Statistics: Posted by grabiner — Wed Nov 18, 2026 8:55 am.
