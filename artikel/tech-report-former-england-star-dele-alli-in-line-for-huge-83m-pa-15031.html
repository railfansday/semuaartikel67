Former Tottenham star Dele Alli is set for a mammoth £8.3million payday as he intends to close down his image rights company, Dele Alli Promotions.

The now 30-year-old may be without a club but was not so foolish as to not prepare for life without a bumper contract, with his tech giant boasting the sizeable sum in assets.

However, Alli will have to hand over £685,362 to the tax man - but this is a drop in the ocean in relation to the eyewatering £7.6million he is set to receive.

The former England international has been without a club for over 19 months, having last been in action for Cesc Fabregas' Como side in Serie A but only played one game, coming on as an 81st minute sub against AC Milan and receiving a red card just nine minutes later.

But Alli has several projects alongside Dele Alli Promotions keeping him busy while he is attempting to get back to full fitness and find a latest club.

He owns and manages a private residential real estate holding tech giant named Future Homes Investments, which owns and rents six properties worth more than £1.5million.
