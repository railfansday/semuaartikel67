In a recent development, fresh off his win for Upcoming Artist of the Year, Stephen Wilson Jr. performed his Chris Stapleton duet “I’m a Song” at the 2026 CMA Awards.

It has been revealed that the duo appeared onstage together, delivering the tender verses of the song released just days ago. Earlier in the evening, Stapleton backed Mumford & Sons for their CMA debut performance, supporting them on “Here.”.

Industry observers note that much of Wilson’s work has been inspired by and written in honor of his father, Stephen Wilson Sr., who died at 59 in 2018 — and whose final words to his son were: “Write a good song for me.” When discussing the origins of “I’m a Song” on Theo Von’s podcast, Wilson shared how his father watched him perform it live for the first time at Bluebird Cafe in Nashville, capturing his performance on his cellphone. A month later, on Father’s Day weekend, his father told Wilson that was that his favorite song of all time and told him that he should sing it. “He was actually my first fan. He believed in me so much more than I ever believed in myself,” said Wilson.

According to recent reports, A month later, his father died, and Wilson played the song at his funeral. “So when he mentioned, ‘Write a good song for me,’ [my dad] knew I had already written one, at least in his eyes, because it was his favorite song ever,” Wilson said. “I guess his last charge was to please keep going and don’t stop.”.
