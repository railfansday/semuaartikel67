Brittany Mahomes scored extra points with her style at the 2026 CMA Awards.

One week after the wife of Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes declared she is pregnant with the couple’s fourth baby, she attended the country music event in a dazzling dress that highlighted her growing baby bump.

Recent findings suggest that for the Nov. 18 outing with pal Taylor Swift, Brittany donned a silver halter top gown embellished with jewels that were cinched around her stomach. And in addition to wearing her long blonde hair down around her shoulders, the 31-year-old sported a pair of strappy black heels.

Once inside Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, she sat next to and laughed alongside Taylor—her fellow Chiefs WAG—who returned to the CMA Awards for the first time in 10 years to perform on stage.

Since Brittany and Patrick—parents to Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months—revealed they were expanding their family roster, the NFL star has shared his excitement, as well as his hopes that another baby boy would be joining him.

“Obviously it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way. I’m excited for it,” Patrick said during a Nov. 11 press conference. “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score.”.

And as it turns out, the 31-year-old was granted his wish, announcing Nov. 17 that Brittany is having a baby boy.

Beyond his excitement about growing his team, Patrick has been vocal about how grateful he is to have Taylor in his and Brittany’s life.

Recent findings suggest that "Taylor being at all the games has been awesome," he mentioned at a press conference ahead of Super Bowl 2025. "It's been awesome to showcase her bringing light to the sport that I love and getting little girls like my daughter interested in watching the game.".

Industry observers note that plus, Patrick added, "I feel like I've built a great friendship with her, and my wife and Taylor have built a friendship as well. So it's just cool to see how football can bring people together from every aspect of life.".
