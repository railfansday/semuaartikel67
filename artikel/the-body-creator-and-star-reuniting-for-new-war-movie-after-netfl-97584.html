Breakout star Sara Boustany is reuniting with The Body creator in an upcoming war drama movie, over a week after the Netflix show’s successful debut.

The Body is Netflix's newest psychological thriller drama following a Catholic high school dance team whose Halloween initiation ritual goes terribly wrong after one of their recruits falls into a coma. Boustany portrays Elise Zakaria, one of The Body's main characters who's introduced as the unapologetic co-captain of the Lions team. The show hails from creator and showrunner Quinn Shephard, who previously directed Zoey Deutch and Dylan O'Brien in Hulu's 2022 comedy film Not Okay. Following its release, the genre-bending series became an instant social media sensation, debuting at number three on Netflix's global top ten chart in 80 countries.

It has been revealed that over a week after winning the hearts of audiences with her breakout performance in The Body, Boustany has officially found her first movie project as lead. According to Deadline, the Lebanese-American actor has been tapped to star in Between Us and the River, an independent coming-of-age movie set in 2005 during the Iraq War. The character-driven feature reunites her with The Body creator Quinn Shephard, who has signed on as a producer. Boustany will also be joined by fellow TV standout Amélie Hoeferle, who also recently gained recognition for her fan-favorite role in the hit Prime Video show Sterling Point.

Recent findings suggest that reviews for The Body praised Boustany for her sharp comedic timing and multi-layered performance as Elise. Her magnetic chemistry with co-star Kristina Bogic has also been one of the main highlights of the show. Before landing The Body, Boustany originally had minor appearances in the 2019 shows Pen15 and Dolly Parton's Heartstrings. She also had a supporting role in 2025's Hello Beautiful movie, which featured Tricia Helfer as a famous model who gets diagnosed with breast cancer at the peak of her career.

It has been revealed that between Us and the River will center on a first-generation Iraqi-American college student and aspiring dancer, played by Boustany, who falls in love with her Mexican-American classmate. However, their relationship faces a difficult challenge when a shocking discovery leads them both to confront grief, identity, and the impact of war. Filmmaker Nika Fehmiu is directing the movie, which serves as her feature-length directorial debut. She also co-wrote the script with Baghdad Stories author Nadia Al Sultani. Before the upcoming movie, Fehmiu wrote, directed, and edited the short film Look at Me, which debuted at the Tribeca Festival.

Besides Boustany and Hoeferle, Between Us and the River will also star Alejandro Akara (Landman), Sarah Himadeh (The Last of Us), Laughton Royce (Clean Hands), and Sean Luc Rogers (Clarkston at Echo Theatre Company). It is produced by Shephard through her production banner, Ingénue, along with Fehmiu for North Wind Films. Nuer Taqa and Zoey Pressey of Radiance Pictures are also producing alongside Matt Cappetta of Planet 9 Films. Production on the upcoming coming-of-age war drama movie has already been completed.

At the time of writing, the release date for Boustany's Between Us and the River movie remains under wraps.

After a dance-team initiation mishap, a group of Catholic school girls experience prophetic visions that incite mass hysteria in their town.
