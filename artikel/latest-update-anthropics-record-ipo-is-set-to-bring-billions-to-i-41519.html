Latest Update: Anthropic's record IPO is set to bring billions to its top investors

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic is poised to generate multibillion-dollar gains for its largest financial backers when its stock starts trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday, according to Bloomberg.

According to recent reports, the organization set a price of $83 a share on Wednesday, which puts its fully diluted market value at about $1.5 trillion. The stock will trade under the ticker ANT. The offering raised $81.3 billion, including stock sold by existing stockholders, the company mentioned.

According to recent reports, that total exceeds the $75 billion SpaceX raised at the start of its June IPO, according to NPR. SpaceX's total climbed to $85.7 billion once its underwriters took up the option to sell additional shares, according to CNBC.

It has been revealed that lightspeed Venture Partners has invested more than $3.5 billion in Anthropic since early 2024. Its stake comes to roughly $28 billion at the offering price, according to Bloomberg. ICONIQ has put in more than $6 billion, starting last year when Anthropic was valued at $183 billion, and its stake is worth over $26 billion. Menlo Ventures led the organization's Series D round in 2024 with $500 million, has invested more than $1 billion in total and holds a stake worth nearly $22 billion.

The stake values are estimates, and the dilutive effect of the new shares may not be factored in, according to Bloomberg. ICONIQ, Menlo and Lightspeed have placed orders for more shares in the offering, but the amounts they will be given have not been made public.

In a recent development, coatue Management has committed $4.75 billion to Anthropic over several funding rounds, including $2.5 billion in the February round and $2 billion in May at a $965 billion valuation. Its first investment was $250 million in 2025, in a round that valued Anthropic at $183 billion. At the IPO price, its stake is worth about $16 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Recent findings suggest that singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC has invested more than $4.5 billion and holds a stake worth about $15 billion. General Catalyst has put in about $2 billion for a stake worth around $10 billion. Bessemer Venture Partners has invested more than $800 million for a stake of about 0.5%, or $7.5 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Spark Capital invested $75 million in 2023, and its stake is worth about $11 billion, according to Bloomberg. An investment team that includes Guy Oseary, Ashton Kutcher and Sound Ventures managing partner Effie Epstein owns shares valued at close to $11 billion.

Recent findings suggest that former Google $GOOGL Chief Executive Officer Eric Schmidt, 71, backed Anthropic in 2021 with a check in the tens of millions, at a time when the manufacturer was valued at $461 million. His stake is projected to come to roughly $15 billion at the IPO valuation, according to Bloomberg.

After a $26 billion private placement with seven tech companies, Amazon $AMZN holds close to 15% of Anthropic's shares outstanding, while Google has roughly 14%, according to Bloomberg.
