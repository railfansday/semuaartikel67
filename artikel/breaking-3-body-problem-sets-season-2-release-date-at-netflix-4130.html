More than two years after the first season of Emmy-nominated 3 Body Problem aired, Netflix has set Thursday, January 14 for its Season 2 return.

It has been revealed that at the time of the premiere, creators David Benioff, D.B. Weiss and Alexander Woo confirmed that the drama series would run for three seasons. The number of seasons matches the books in Cixin Liu’s trilogy, on which the series is based, and the trio noted that the format will allow them to tell the story as they had intended.

Weiss also discussed the challenges a Season 2 would pose.

“The story gets really wild in the best possible way,” he mentioned at the time. “With something that’s that wild, there are a lot of choices to be made and a lot of things to be figured out. We’ve been putting our heads together to figure them out recently especially the past couple of months.” Watch on Deadline.

According to recent reports, 3 Body Problem chronicles humanity’s first contact with an alien civilization. It follows a young woman, whose fateful decision in 1960s China reverberates across space and time into the present day. When the laws of nature inexplicably unravel before their eyes, a close-knit group of brilliant scientists join forces with an unflinching detective to confront the greatest threat in humanity’s history.

Recent findings suggest that in Season 2, the alien invasion nears and humanity prepares to meet them — on Earth and beyond. While a space fleet embarks on a mission that spans centuries, a secret locked inside of one man’s mind might be humanity’s only hope for survival.

Industry observers note that based on the international bestselling novels, following its 2024 debut, the series spent seven weeks in the Global Top 10, reaching the Top 10 in 93 countries and hitting #1 in 44, with 46.6 million views. Three songs from its soundtrack also reached Billboard’s Top TV Songs chart, including Lana Del Rey’s “Video Games” at #1.

Benioff, Weiss and Woo are creators, writers and executive producers on the series. Season 1 executive producers also included Bernadette Caulfield, Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman, Nena Rodrigue, Lin Qi, Zhao Jilong as well as Brad Pitt, and Dede Gardner’s Plan B Entertainment and Jeremy Kleiner’s tech giant along with Rosamund Pike and Robie Uniacke for Primitive Streak.
