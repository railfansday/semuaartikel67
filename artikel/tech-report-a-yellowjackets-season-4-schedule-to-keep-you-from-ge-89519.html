It has been revealed that the fourth and final season of Yellowjackets is here to deliver shocks and finally answer some questions about the cannibalistic soccer team we’ve grown to love. How’d they get home? Is there really a supernatural force controlling them, in the wilderness and in Latest Jersey? Will anyone survive? What happened to Melissa’s hat? Follow this Yellowjackets season 4 schedule so you don’t miss a single second.

We’re going back to the wilderness. We’re going back to the equally chaotic present-day timeline. And since we now get to see the immediate aftermath of the young Yellowjackets’ return to civilization and processed food... we’re also going back to high school. The fourth season has also added a ton of latest cast members, including Molly Ringwald, Devon Sawa, June Squibb, Ken Marino, Eric Stoltz, and Dulé Hill.

The first episode premieres November 20, 2026 on streaming, and then subsequently will air on cable November 22, 2026.

Yellowjackets is a Showtime show, which means you can stream it on Paramount+ and then later watch it on the Showtime channel.

There are ten episodes in the final season of Yellowackets. There have been ten episodes every season except season 2... unless you count that totally real, but still totally unseen, secret unreleased episode.
