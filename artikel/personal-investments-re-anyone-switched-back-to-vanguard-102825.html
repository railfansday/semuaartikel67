Sources indicate that I’m still curious about the “number of bad experiences” people have had. I’ve personally had very good experiences when moving accounts and I needed to contact service on 3 occasions.#1. No chat or off hours support, and phone support could take 20-40 minutes to get through. I also like to use chat so I can have a hard copy of the transcript.#2. My personal advisor would change every 3-6 months, with no handoff or notice. I would sometimes see the name change on my homepage. #3. Their trading desk does not understand their own funds. I wanted to move from VWIUX to VTEB. The trading desk wanted me to sell my VWIUX, and use 90% of the expected dollars to buy VTEB (because we would not know the final price until the end of trading that day. Then, the next day, once I knew the amount I would get, use the remaining to buy VTEB. I found out on this board that I could simply exchange VWIUX to VTEAX, and then do a mutual funds to ETF conversion to VTEB. Much simpler, no market skew, no buying/selling.#4. I spent a year trying out Schwab and Fidelity. I used ACATS to move a small amount of funds to both Fidelity and Schwab and spent the better part of a year seeing how they worked. Vanguard never notified me that I transferred funds, either through email, a message on the web sites, nor did my advisor call. I eventually decided to go with Fidelity. I moved my piece at Schwab to Fidelity. Within a couple of hours my advisor at Schwab called me to make ensure this is what I wanted to do, I also got email notification. When I moved the rest of my funds from Vanguard to Fidelity, again, nothing, nothing at all. I eventually closed my accounts out, never had anyone contact me until years later wanting to know if I wanted to come back to Vanguard. When I moved some funds from Fidelity to MerrillEdge to keep my accounts there at a certain level, I immediately got email notifications.#5. Fidelity has a pretty good banking setup, Vanguard, at that time, had nothing. I love Vanguard funds, and still use them for almost all my assets.
