Bitcoin (BTC) bull and SkyBridge Capital founder Anthony Scaramucci has argued that the world's biggest cryptocurrency could find a critical bottom as soon as this week.

Scaramucci's comments follow the leading cryptocurrency's struggle to recover from a catastrophic crypto market crash that took BTC to its lowest price level in over two years.

In a recent development, in the latest episode of All Things Markets on Nov. 20, the SkyBridge Capital founder joined Galaxy Digital CEO Mike Novogratz to discuss a potential bottom for Bitcoin.

Scaramucci shared his view that Bitcoin could see its bottom on Nov. 25, citing the cryptocurrency's four-year halving cycle as the driving force behind his comments.

"Before we came on, I asked ChatGPT when's the expected halving for Bitcoin," Scaramucci explained.

"And then when are we 500 days out from that halving? And the estimate was November 25th," he added.

This prediction stems from the idea that Bitcoin's price trends could follow a predictable four-year cycle that coincides with the cryptocurrency's halving events, when the new BTC supply is cut in half.

This theory suggests that Bitcoin could find its major bottom around the 500-day mark before its next halving, expected in 2028.

However, these theories are by no means a certainty when it comes to predicting future price action, especially with the current level of institutional participation and macroeconomic forces impacting the crypto market to an unprecedented extent.

During the conversation, Novogratz also took note of Bitcoin's history of forming double bottoms during its four-year cycles.

"And every four years, Bitcoin double bottomed," Novogratz remarked.

At the time, the Galaxy Digital CEO's comments suggested a potentially positive scenario, as Bitcoin had been able to hold its psychological $60,000 price level.

"If it doesn't break 60, that could be a good sign," he noted.

It has been revealed that unfortunately for Bitcoin, the cryptocurrency subsequently crashed below this critical price point. This has left some wondering whether the cryptocurrency has already found its ultimate bottom.

During the same conversation, Novogratz suggested that Bitcoin's short-term outlook could largely depend on the current situation in the stock market.

Specifically, the billionaire said that a rebound in tech stocks could lead to a strong rally in Bitcoin's price.

Recent findings suggest that "If we put in a bottom in the next couple days in the S&P here, down 3% from here, down 5% from here. And we have a rally back up. Man, Bitcoin is going to rally hard with it," Novogratz predicted.

However, he also warned that Bitcoin could see a lot of market-driven movements going forward, as opposed to crypto-specific movements.

"I think we're going to be stuck tethered to general sentiment and risk," he stated.

Novogratz also believes that Bitcoin could trade in a range between $60,000 and $75,000 before a breakout or breakdown becomes apparent.

Bitcoin plunged to around $55,100 on Nov. 21, its lowest price level since September 2024, following another severe wave of sell-offs in the crypto market.

According to recent reports, it then bounced back strongly to climb above $60,000 again, albeit briefly, before falling back to around $59,000 on Nov. 22.
