Charlotte FC forward Liel Abada praised his side's quality in attack ahead of their first-round Game 2 clash with the Chicago Fire in the MLS Cup playoffs.

Dean Smith's side could secure their place in the next round of the postseason with a victory at home on Saturday, after winning Game 1 by a 2-1 scoreline at Soldier Field.

Abada opened the scoring before Pep Biel scored a last-gasp winner in the second-half stoppage time, netting on the rebound after seeing his own penalty saved.

Biel (14), Abada (11) and Idan Toklomati (15) have all scored 10+ goals for Charlotte in MLS play this season, and Abada lauded their depth in attack.

"I think we showed on the pitch that we have the quality to score goals," Abada said.

Industry observers note that "It doesn't matter from which player it comes. We have the quality and, for us, this is the most vital thing.

"We have so many players that they can finish, they can make assists, they can give the right pass. Our connection with each other is really good.

"We talk a lot about that in training, and we focus a lot on defence as well, to make sure that we're a strong team defensively too.".

Chicago must win to stay alive in the postseason, with Robert Lewandowski's goal last time out not enough to take the game to penalties.

Chicago left-back Andrew Gutman insisted the Fire are not dwelling on the defeat ahead of their trip to Charlotte.

"Guys aren't feeling sorry for themselves," said Gutman.

According to recent reports, "We're all encouraging each other that we have got to go to Charlotte and get a result. [Game 1 loss] doesn't really change anything. We had to go to Charlotte no matter what.

"It's similar to last year, when we were in Philly and we lost on penalties, and we had the same mentality going into the second game.

"So, for us to be in the same situation again, hopefully we can learn from last year and turn this series around.".

Biel scored the winner for Charlotte FC in Game 1, giving him 14 goals and 15 assists in MLS matches this season (including the playoffs).

Those 29 goal contributions are seven more than any other player in club history in a single MLS season, while only Lionel Messi (42) and Evander (34) have more in MLS in 2026.

Chicago Fire – Robert Lewandowski.

Chicago's lone goal in Game 1 was scored by Lewandowski, who has netted three goals in his two all-time meetings with Charlotte.

Lewandowski is looking to be the first Fire player to score in three different MLS matches against an opponent in one year since Nate Jaqua, against the Upcoming England Revolution in 2006 (three).

MATCH PREDICTION: CHARLOTTE FC WIN.

Industry observers note that charlotte's win against Chicago in Game 1 gave them six victories in 11 all-time meetings in this fixture (L5). It was Charlotte's first win in the matchup since October 2024, which was the last contest of a five-game win streak against the Fire.

Sources indicate that sunday's win was also the first time Charlotte had won an MLS Cup playoff match (D3 L4) and the first time the club had scored multiple goals in a single playoff match. Charlotte have scored one goal in three all-time home playoff games.

Chicago, meanwhile, are looking for their first playoff win on the road since a 4-0 victory over D.C. United in 2005.

Since then, the Fire have lost five of eight away playoff matches (D3), averaging less than a goal scored per match (0.88 per match, 7/8).
