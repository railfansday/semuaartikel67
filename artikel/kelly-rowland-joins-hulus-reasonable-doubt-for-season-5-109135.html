SPOILER ALERT: The following reveals major plot points from Hulu‘s Reasonable Doubt‘s Season 4 finale titled “Moment of Clarity.”.

Hulu’s Reasonable Doubt has closed out its action-packed fourth season with a bloody good surprise: Kelly Rowland has joined the cast for Season 5. She portrays the character Demitra, a staffer on a mayoral campaign who stumbles into trouble that could threaten not only a politician’s future but her own life. It is as yet unknown if she will have a series regular or recurring role.

To set the scene, Jax (Emayatzy Corinealdi) and Krystal (Angela Grovey) are outside the law firm talking when Demitra walks up to them and asks, “Are you Jax Stewart?” Jax, looking worried, confirms her identity as Demitra walks closer, showing blood on her hands and all over her shirt, and asks for help now. Jax and Krystal are stunned, replying, “Fuck!” simultaneously as the episode ends. A photo of the moment can be found above. “After being such a fan of the show for such a long time, It’s surreal to be a part of it,” exclaimed Rowland. Watch on Deadline.

Series creator, showrunner and executive producer Raamla Mohamed Hunter remarked of the cast addition, “Kelly Rowland, THEE Kelendria Rowland is in my show. The one I created. I didn’t think that I could bump like this, but now I have the motivation to be a survivor in this TV game. I’ll be screaming until Season five!”.

In a recent development, season 4 of Reasonable Doubt is about survival, truth, and rebuilding under pressure. Every character was forced to confront consequences they can no longer outrun as Jax’s fate hangs in the balance, driving the entire story forward in the new season.

In a recent development, the official synopsis: After a brutal attack leaves Jax’s family and newly minted law firm shaken, nothing feels certain, especially their future. With Jax’s most loyal client at the center of a spiraling media disaster, The Stewart Firm is thrust into a high-stakes battle where every move could mean survival … or collapse of the firm. As grief lingers and pressure mounts, one question looms: Can they rebuild in time, or will the cost of moving forward too quickly be everything they stand to lose?

Industry observers note that in addition to Mohamed Hunter, executive producers also include Kerry Washington alongside Pilar Savone for Simpson Street, Larry Wilmore for Wilmore Films, Tamara Gregory, Anton Cropper, Lena Cordina and Erika L. Johnson. Shawn Holley, Jon Leshay and Zahir McGhee serve as co-executive producers. The series hails from Onyx Collective and is produced by 20th Television, a part of Disney Television Studios.
