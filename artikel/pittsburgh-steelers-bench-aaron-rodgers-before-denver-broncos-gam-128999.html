📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Aaron Rodgers will be riding the bench.

Industry observers note that the Pittsburgh Steelers confirmed that when they face down the Denver Broncos on Nov. 27, it won’t be Rodgers who will be taking to the field as quarterback.

“The Pittsburgh Steelers have named Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for the game this Friday against the Denver Broncos,” Steelers spokesman Burt Lauten confirmed on Nov. 24. “Aaron Rodgers will be inactive for the game on Friday.”.

For his part, Rodgers—who is not right now on the team’s injured reserve list—has not personally addressed the decision, and there have been no additional updates from the team, however he was not present at the start of the team's Nov. 25 practice.

The decision comes after the Steelers have lost their last two games, during which Rodgers only completed 45 of 73 passes, per ESPN, amounting to a completion rate of around 61 percent for this NFL season.

It has been revealed that yet, when asked about Rodgers’ future following the team’s loss against the Eagles on Nov. 22, head coach Mike McCarthy affirmed, “Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback.”.

For Rodgers, this season with Pittsburgh marks his last in the NFL, the 42-year-old having previously announced his prepares to retire after the 2026-2027 season, marking an end to more than two decades on the field.

As he told Pat McAfee in June, this final run with Pittsburgh “was really about finishing with a lot of love and fun and peace for the career that I've had.”.

In a recent development, “I played 20 fricking years,” he added at the time. “It's been a long run. I've enjoyed it, and no better place to finish than in one of the cornerstone franchises of the NFL.”.

Industry observers note that as for what inspired Rodgers to leave it all out on the field one last time, a conversation with his wife Brittani was the final push he needed. After all, he was already intrigued about hitching his wagon to the Pittsburgh team after McCarthy—under whom Rodgers played while on the Green Bay Packers—was named head coach.

Sources indicate that "I thought last year might be it, and then Mike got hired,” Rodgers, who spent two seasons with the Latest York Jets, told reporters during a July 28 press conference. "I talked to the wife. She remarked, 'You can do one more year and see if it works out.' And then it kind of all came together.".

Yet, no matter how this season plays out for the Steelers, whose record so far stands at 6-4, Rodgers will never again don an NFL helmet.

As he previously told reporters, there is “zero debate” about returning to the field after the 2026-2027 season: “This is it.”.
