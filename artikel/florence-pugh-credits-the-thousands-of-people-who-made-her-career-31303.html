Florence Pugh Credits the ‘Thousands of People’ Who Made Her Career Possible

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, florence Pugh is making sure every person who helped make her career possible gets their due. In her Women in Hollywood speech, the Dune: Part Three star reflected on what a “bonkers ride” it is to be a woman in the industry. “Being bold and powerful and opinionated—always opinionated—and then vulnerable whenever the cameras need, is a skill I’m still in awe of all of us being able to do,” she stated. “Thank you, ELLE, for honoring all of these powerhouse women.”.

She went on to acknowledge the “thousands of people” who helped make her career possible. Being an actress was a childhood dream. “When I was child, I was asked what I wanted to be when I grew up, and it was this,” she recalled. “But I didn’t know what ‘this’ was, though. I just saw the women and men on my TV and would feel supremely jittery and thrilled watching them. I would get close to the screen, so close I could feel the warmth and static and study all of their faces. I would mainly get jealous of men actually, and weirdly, he’s in my speech, Matt Damon, I was just always jealous of him. And I don’t know why! I don’t! He’s got it all!

In a recent development, “I knew I wanted to do that,” she continued. “All of it, the bells, the whistles. The ability to be free and perform and live in make-believe. The thing is, I wasn’t aware that the biggest and best thing about working in this industry was yet to come. Yes, the explosions and close-ups and locations and free breakfast, lunch, and dinner is cool, but when I truly sit and think about how I got here, it’s down to the thousands of people who have been a part of my career all the way through. They made the path smooth so I could climb to the top of the mountain and wave my flag, which is what I am doing right now. My career and existence in this job is down to thousands of people.

Recent findings suggest that “I think of the crews I’ve been lucky enough to work with, who create the space and the stage so I can do what I do comfortably. I think of the casting directors at the beginning of my career, who took a risk arguing for me, claiming that it didn’t matter that I had no qualifications and only one film credit. Or for the dressers, who would fib and say there was a costume malfunction when they could see I hadn’t had enough time to eat my lunch—this was pre-SAG membership. So don’t panic, SAG. Or to the glam teams all over the world who join because they believe in you and what you do. The makeup artists and hair stylists who wash and clean brushes well into the night just for you, tomorrow at 5 A.M.”.

“I think of all of these people who protected me on my journey,” she said. “And it would not have been possible without every single one of them. Just in case you’re listening now and you’re not aware how grateful we are and how important you are, I just received an ELLE Women of the Year honor, and I didn’t get here on my own. I only took stock in myself because I could see you doing it for me first.”.
