Baywatch alum Erika Eleniak is candidly opening up about how starting her own OnlyFans account helped her recover from severe financial strain.

Sources indicate that “I’m in this position where I’m not really acting anymore,” Eleniak, 57, acknowledged on the Tuesday, November 17, episode of the “Miss Understood” podcast. “You find yourself in that position where it’s like, ‘I need to make a certain amount of money in order to pay my overhead.’ What do I do? Where am I going to make that kind of money?”.

Eleniak is best known for playing rookie lifeguard Shauni McClain on the first three seasons of Baywatch in the 1990s. Due to acting work slowing down in recent years, she officially joined the subscription-based OnlyFans in August 2026 after being served an eviction notice on her home.

Recent findings suggest that “I do my content,” she noted during the podcast interview. “I shoot my own stuff and do my own everything. They do give you — like, in the beginning, you get certain questionnaires about yourself so people can get to know you and OnlyFans can get to know you. But pretty much I do my own stuff.”.

The model turned actress clarified that she’s “not nude” on OnlyFans, having instead focused on other types of content that she is more comfortable with.

“The idea was to start with feet, but it really just immediately kind of manifested into what it is now,” Eleniak stated.

After only a “couple months” on OnlyFans, Eleniak said she was able to work with the owners of her rental property to pay off money owed.

“The people that were in charge of my rental situation were really kind to me and really worked with me, so that was really a blessing,” she remarked.

Eleniak has followed several stars —including Denise Richards, Shannon Elizabeth and Morgan Fairchild — in joining OnlyFans as the platform expands beyond simply providing explicit content.

“I knew that OnlyFans has changed tremendously,” she stressed. “I know OnlyFans of yesteryear, and I would have no interest in being a part of that. But I know that today, it’s just this incredible social media platform for interacting with your fans. It’s just so many people are doing it. Celebrities are doing it. It’s just changed tremendously.”.

Sources indicate that “The OnlyFans that is today that I’m a part of, I’m proud of and I love,” she went on to say. “I think it’s so much fun.”.

Another bonus was that Eleniak promptly came to realize OnlyFans gave her fans an unexpected way to directly interact with her work.

Industry observers note that “Part of the appeal of jumping back in again was to see if I have a loyal fan base or not, because I’ve had a diverse career, and I’ve had a lot of people stay loyal and follow my career over the years,” she noted.“So it was interesting to me to see if they were gonna jump on board with OnlyFans as well. And they have, which has been really nice.”.

The Beverly Hillbillies actress told TMZ in October that she’d already made $300,000 in her first three months on the platform and was on track to earn $1 million by the end of her first year.
