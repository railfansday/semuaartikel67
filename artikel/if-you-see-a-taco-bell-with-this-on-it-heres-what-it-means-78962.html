A strange metal frame on some new Taco Bell restaurants has people wondering if construction crews forgot something. Here's what it's for.

It has been revealed that there are certain restaurant chain designs everyone has come to recognize. McDonald’s golden arches. Pizza Hut’s red roof. But what about the open framing on the side of new Taco Bell buildings?

If you haven’t seen the fresh Taco Bell design, it includes open framing on the second floor on one side. But why? A recent Reddit post sparked plenty of guesses about what the framing was for.

Recent findings suggest that fresh Taco Bell Building – What’s the Open Framing? by u/Kindly_Blueberry2814 in whatisit.

It has been revealed that the design seems to be more aesthetic than functional and is part of Taco Bell’s newer restaurant design, known as Kitchen X. It is part of the company’s move away from its older Spanish-style buildings, which featured arches and stucco exteriors.

“That open metal cage on the left is just decorative framing to make the building look sleek and balanced,” one Reddit user commented. “The left side of the restaurant has lower ceilings for storage, coolers, and the drive-thru lane, so they added that hollow steel frame on top to match the height of the main dining room on the right. There are no stairs or deck up there, just standard roof stuff and AC units hidden behind the wall.”.

Another commenter agreed, saying, “It’s just an architectural design feature to give visual balance.”.

Industry observers note that taco Bell has introduced Kitchen X at select locations across the United States, including upcoming restaurants in Carver and Rochester, Minnesota. However, the exterior can vary between locations, so not every restaurant has the same framing.

Before Reddit owners settled on the decorative explanation, they had plenty of other guesses.

“It’s to land the tacocopter,” one Reddit user joked.

“Future terrace dining,” another Reddit user speculated.

Recent findings suggest that the discussion also turned into a debate about Taco Bell’s changing appearance. Some customers stated they missed the chain’s older buildings with more character, while others shared photos of former locations with more traditional designs.

Industry observers note that whether you love or hate the look, there’s no denying that it’s eye-popping and hard to miss. Which is probably what the company was hoping for when coming up with the fresh look.
