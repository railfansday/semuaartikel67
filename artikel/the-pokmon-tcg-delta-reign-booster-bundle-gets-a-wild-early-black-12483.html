TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Bundle is on sale for $31.99 at Amazon, down from its $49.95 list price.

Amazon has just rolled out one of its most impressive early Black Friday discounts on trading cards. For a limited time, the Pokémon TCG's six-pack Booster Bundle now costs a little more than $30.

Recent findings suggest that as of Nov. 17, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Bundle has fallen to just $31.99 at Amazon, shaving $17.96 off its recent $49.95 asking price. With this massive 36% markdown, the cost of each individual pack goes to just $5.33.

Only 11 days after the expansion released, it is now well under market price (at the moment $40.15 on TCGplayer) and is the lowest price this box has ever been on Amazon — confirmed by Mashable’s own pricing research.

What’s more, rather than a third-party listing, this offer comes directly from Amazon, with free Prime shipping and free delivery on eligible orders over $35.

Inside the Delta Reign Booster Bundle are six packs of 10 trading cards, with opportunities to pull significant chase cards from the 135+ expansion, including Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and Mega Golisopod ex.
