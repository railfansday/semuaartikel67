Founded in 2016 in Ho Chi Minh City, MakeYourAsia is a Destination Management Firm that had split into four separate business lines: Travel, Incentive, Consulting, and Logistics. Each division carried its own visual direction, and the old multicolored striped globe mark with a heavy sans-serif wordmark couldn't hold them together anymore. Brand designer Hoai Nhan stepped in to replace those four competing looks with a single brand identity. Looking at the before-and-after comparison slide, the shift is immediate. The four-color swirl gives way to a crisp, symmetrical compass symbol built from four directional arrows, with the south-pointing arrow filled in to anchor the company's focus on Southeast Asia.

Sources indicate that hoai Nhan drew the emblem's internal geometry from traditional Indochine encaustic floor tiles, turning regional architectural craft into a clear navigational mark. On the construction grid, the circular symbol sits at 2.5x square, separated from the 9x Cormorant Garamond wordmark by a tight 0.5x gap. That pairing between the ornamental tile-inspired symbol and high-contrast serif lettering gives the wordmark real editorial poise without feeling fussy. For supporting copy and digital UI, the system pairs Cormorant Garamond with Inter 18pt.

Color stays disciplined across every physical touchpoint. Deep red and navy blue, pulled from Southeast Asian national flags and everyday regional life, carry the bulk of the brand identity. You can see how well that restraint works on the deep red crewneck sweatshirt, where the white embroidered compass mark reads clearly at chest scale without losing its four fine cardinal dots. On the stationery side, red, white, and navy pencils carry the lockup alongside a blind-debossed tagline: "DRAW YOUR ROUTE. MAKE YOUR ASIA." Even in architectural space, white vinyl lettering on blue-tinted glass beneath a solid red ceiling keeps the system unmistakable. It's a clean, grounded identity that doesn't need extra ornament to hold a room.
