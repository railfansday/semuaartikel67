On Friday, we asked Newcastle United fans for 50 words on Bruno Guimaraes.

Saturday of course sees the former United captain return to St James’ Park.

Bruno Guimaraes and Arsenal on Tyneside for a 5.30pm Premier League kick-off.

Much has been stated about this return some three and a half months after deciding to leave.

We thought we would ask a number of Newcastle United fans for their thoughts,.

Rather than giving any leading pointers as to what to write, we totally left it up to the regular/irregular contributors to The Mag as to what they wanted to come out with.

We simply asked these Newcastle United fans ‘Please give us 50 words on Bruno Guimaraes’….

According to recent reports, trophy-winning captain who nearly cost us at Wembley when he went offside as Isak netted, then dallied on the ball and let Liverpool score. He profited professionally by joining us and we profited financially when he left. Loves to be the centre of attention, great drive, one to have in the trenches but don’t let him take a pen.

Bruno was what he was: part of the trio that jumpstarted this past half-decade of rejuvenation and triumph, the club’s heart and soul, and the technical genius that helped make the United midfield so imposing. I bear him no grudges for leaving or how, though doing so makes for better theatre. So now Bruno is what he is – another rival. Howay the lads!

Sources indicate that I find it hard not to harbour love for our former captain. He was an absolute hero during his time here. Like the ‘one that got away’ it hurts to see him with someone else. I wish him no luck at Arsenal but he’s a Newcastle legend for me.

Bruno joined a relegation fight with no relegation clause. He dragged us up the league, almost singlehanded at times. He gave everything for the club and for us and won us a trophy. Disgraceful rules dictate we can’t match his or many players ambitions. Saturday aside I wish him well.

He was the face of the exciting first phase after the takeover, a true top class player who could drag us to wins himself. He led us to glory which will was amazing but he tarnished his legacy on the way out after saying he wanted to be a legend.

Sources indicate that before the Arsenal game, let’s remember Bruno Guimarães as a footballer: brave in possession, relentless in the press, imaginative with the ball and wholehearted in every challenge. The disappointment of his leaving should not diminish what he helped create: belief, connection and a Newcastle side that made supporters dream again.

He lifted the trophy into the night sky at Wembley Stadium in March 2025, and for that he will always be a Newcastle United legend in my eyes. Did he blot his copy book with the move to Arsenal? Maybe, maybe not. Personally, I think the jogging bottoms he wore whilst paraded in front of an expectant audience at the Emirates were more offensive than his desire to leave us for them.

I like Bruno. He put more into my club than 95% of others. He captained us to the only trophy most fans have seen us win, and got us to the Champions League. That’s a good CV. Loyalty? Footballers are like us – loyal to family first. PL rules say we couldn’t double his wages. Arsenal could, and did, so he went. For his family. I won’t boo him.
