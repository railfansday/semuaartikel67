📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As you've probably heard, GTA 6 is out on consoles this week and targeting 30 FPS on that hardware. Despite reports claiming one may not be far away, there's still no official word on a PC port, hence why we've not got a review that delves deep into analysis of just how central to the experience the smell reaction system is.

The wait for confirmation of a PC version continues, then, and it looks like that might well be the only chance people get to play the crime sandbox at 60fps, with Rockstar having confirmed that higher frame rate modes aren't en-route to even the most beefy of consoles.

Speaking to Kotaku, GTA 6's devs made clear optional 60 or 40fps performance modes aren't on the way, even for the more powerful Pro variant of the PS5.

It has been revealed that "Our base presentation makes use of ray-traced reflections and global illumination. On the PS5 Pro, this is enhanced with the addition of ray-traced shadows, as well as overall added clarity through the use of PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR)," Rockstar technology production director Marc Chambers put it. "The PS5 Pro features an upgraded graphics processor and upscaling technology that we leverage to increase the game’s visual fidelity. While the PS5 Pro’s central processor is slightly better than the PS5 base console, it is not powerful enough to run the game’s complex simulation at higher frame rates.".

So, there you go. The consoles just ain't got that dog in 'em. One would think, then, that being able to run GTA 6 in all its glory at higher frame rates will likely be a selling point for that PC port that's assumed to be on the way. Whether the average PC owner's graphics card and other bits will have the horses to deliver a smooth 60 is another question, and an especially pertinent one given the ongoing RAM crisis has made upgrading certain aspects of your setup ludicrously expensive.

If it requires a 5090 at a minimum, I guess those of us with less than absolute cutting edge cards will just have to keep playing Sleeping Dogs until the AI bubble bursts.
