The good folks over at Digital Foundry have been sharing their deep dive analysis on the Xbox Series X|S versions of GTA 6 today, presented in the form of a 23-minute video on YouTube, and a separate article over on their website.

Looking at both of these, there's a ton of interesting information that's shared about the two Xbox versions of the game, so we thought we'd provide a quick summary of what they think about GTA 6 on Xbox Series X and S.

This is just a summary though, and there's way more context and detail in the video at the bottom of this article.

Industry observers note that digital Foundry reckons that the Xbox Series X version of GTA 6 is a great-looking game for the most part, highlighting things like lighting, reflections and even the incredible hair system, while also pointing out minor downsides such as missing shadows and occasionally blurry appearances. They also reckon that the frame rate generally maintains its 30FPS target, but will slip in busy situations like chases and explosions.

"Generally speaking, the game acquits itself quite well on Microsoft's senior current-gen console. Visual settings seem pretty solid across the board.".

Recent findings suggest that the Xbox Series S version is also said to be pretty good in the graphical department, retaining the core features that make GTA 6 such a great game visually. It's the frame rate that bothers DF the most, as it's much more variable and prone to falling below its 30FPS target. That said, it appears to hover around 25-30FPS for the most part.

In a recent development, "Series S offers the loosest 30fps lock, with large portions of the game playing out at frame-rates from the mid to high 20s. Again, it's possible to depress the frame-rate to around 20fps with vehicular destruction. The other consoles do a credible job of hitting 30fps, but Series S often lies in a 25-30fps no-man's-land.".

Sources indicate that if you're wondering which console is the absolute best to play GTA 6 on, it's unsurprisingly the PS5 Pro. Nevertheless, Digital Foundry says that both the Xbox Series X and the PS5 versions "look and play well" despite their softer image (and the Series X is actually "slightly smoother" when setting the Xbox to 120Hz with a VRR display), while the Xbox Series S version is the "hardest to recommend" despite its surprisingly impressive visuals.

According to recent reports, "In terms of visual features and performance, the PS5 Pro version is the best option available, with solid frame-rates, 4K-like image quality and a noticeable visual upgrade thanks to ray-traced shadows. However, the game looks and plays well on base PS5 and Xbox Series X as well, despite occasional minor frame-rate drops during regular play and a softer 1440p image. The Series S version of the game is the hardest to recommend, with consistent frame-rate drops to the mid-20s, a 1080p output resolution and a long list of visual cutbacks, though the overall character of the game's visuals still shines through.".

Both the video and the website article are totally different from each other, so it's worth watching / reading the pair of them and getting a better idea of how GTA 6 fares across both Xbox consoles (and PS5 for that matter).

In my experience, I've found GTA 6 to be fantastic on Xbox Series X across the board for the most part, and while the Series S version is clearly a little more unstable, I'd wager that most players will still be perfectly happy with it.
