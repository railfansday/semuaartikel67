iPhone Duo 2 prototypes suggest Face ID, Action Button, other potential 2027 features

🌐 Source: Zdnet • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (656 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Although the iPhone Duo was released just this fall, Apple is finalizing the foldable’s second generation.
  • Apple reportedly wants to bring Face ID to the foldable, potentially integrating it into the outer display.
  • Also: iPhone Duo review: Hard to recommend, even harder to put down.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Although the iPhone Duo was released just this fall, Apple is finalizing the foldable’s second generation. According to a Bloomberg report, Apple has already created prototypes for the second iPhone Duo, codenamed V78, and some include a sought-after feature: Face ID.

Apple reportedly wants to bring Face ID to the foldable, potentially integrating it into the outer display. However, Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman notes that Apple appears to be running out of time to finalize its plans for the Duo ahead of a fall 2027 launch.

Also: iPhone Duo review: Hard to recommend, even harder to put down.

iPhone launch events typically take place in early September, when Apple releases its fresh smartphone lineups. To have the iPhones ready for sale on the heels of their announcement, Apple must manufacture the new models well before the launch event.

Because of this timeline concern, Face ID isn’t guaranteed for the iPhone Duo 2, though it could come to a third generation instead.

Other planned uogrades — including an Action Button, a telephoto camera, and a thinner, lighter construction — are also noted in the report.

Apple reportedly wants to add an Action Button, the customizable button that performs an action when pressed. The iPhone Duo 2 may combine the Action Button and the Camera Control buttons into a single button, providing a useful physical shortcut instead of adding a newly introduced button.

Sources indicate that the report indicates that many customers don’t use the camera control button as often as Apple expected. Combining the camera and action buttons gives Apple the real estate necessary to add both options in an already constrained space.

Apple also wants to incorporate a telephoto camera into the iPhone Duo, though this is likely to happen in one of the next couple of generations. A telephoto camera would give consumers greater flexibility to zoom in and capture clear photos of distant objects, which would be an upgrade to the Duo’s photography features.

Technically, equipping the Duo with a telephoto camera is feasible, but the biggest obstacle is space constraints. A telephoto camera needs more room than a wide-angle one, since it requires additional optical elements to magnify distant objects.

Apple already uses a tetraprism design in its Pro iPhones, which allows a longer optical path without too much physical depth.

Apple also wants to reduce the weight and thickness of the iPhone Duo, which is legitimately clunky and cumbersome. To do this, Apple needs to balance the large internal and outer displays, battery capacity, camera array, and the hinge mechanism.

For many users, reducing the weight and thickness of a passport-sized foldable could matter more in buying decisions than adding another button or camera.

Sources indicate that gurman’s latest report doesn’t mention any planned changes for the iPhone Duo’s hinge mechanism, even after concerns with the first generation’s durability. With manufacturing deadlines fast approaching, however, it’s unlikely that Apple will be able to make changes to the Duo 2 based on consumer feedback. More than likely, these changes would come with the third-generation model.

Recent findings suggest that CNET tested the iPhone Duo’s hinge mechanism and found that, after around 440,000 cycles, the hinge stopped opening flat on its own. Loosening and other signs of wear were evident after 390,000 folds.

Given the thinner, lighter construction of a future Duo model, one could assume that changes to the hinge and the foldable’s internal structure are imminent, but this has not been confirmed.

Sources indicate that because Apple will not have enough time to gather feedback on the iPhone Duo before the manufacturing deadlines for the iPhone Duo 2, I don’t expect the second-generation iPhone Duo to be significantly different from its predecessor. We do expect a thinner iPhone Duo 2 and a way to incorporate the Action and Camera buttons.
