Karl-Heinz Rummenigge defends Bayern Munich concerns over Jamal Musiala

It has been revealed that bayern Munich supervisory board member Karl-Heinz Rummenigge has defended recent comments from Uli Hoeneß and Jan-Christian Dreesen concerning Jamal Musiala’s long-term future, insisting that the club’s consideration of the German international’s health and lucrative contract is entirely normal. Speaking after Friday evening’s 1-1 Bundesliga draw against Cologne, the former Bayern CEO expressed support for his colleagues’ concerns and suggested that difficult financial decisions could eventually become necessary.

Sources indicate that musiala made his return as a substitute against Cologne, playing approximately 20 minutes after missing nearly two months with a thigh injury. Rummenigge nevertheless emphasized that it remained unclear whether the 23-year-old would recover his previous performance level.

It has been revealed that the comments represent another development in a discussion that has increasingly occupied Bayern’s hierarchy. Get German Football News previously reported on Uli Hoeneß’s concerns about Musiala’s ability to regain his previous form. Bayern CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen subsequently raised the possibility of discussions involving the midfielder’s family and representatives should his performances remain below expectations.

Musiala’s current contract runs through 2030 and could reportedly earn him as much as €30 million annually. Head coach Vincent Kompany addressed the situation at Thursday’s press conference, emphasizing that his responsibility was to maintain confidence in his players while respecting the different obligations of Bayern’s executives. Rummenigge, meanwhile, explicitly supported the club’s financial concerns.

Recent findings suggest that the 71-year-old suggested that Bayern might eventually need to reconsider the financial arrangements surrounding Musiala’s contract if the midfielder proved unable to recover his previous level. He also expressed doubts that another club would be prepared to purchase Musiala under the present circumstances.

Nevertheless, Rummenigge emphasized that no immediate decision was necessary. Bayern would first observe how Musiala developed over the coming weeks before considering further steps.

“I know what you’re getting at, and I don’t think there’s anything scandalous about this at all,” Rummenigge told Sky Germany. “What Uli and Jan Dreesen mentioned was completely fine, in my opinion. When a club has to consider how to deal with that situation, I consider it responsible and completely normal.”.

According to recent reports, “Unfortunately, what we know suggests that it will be difficult for him to return to the level he has so often demonstrated here in this stadium,” the former Bayern executive added. I can’t imagine Jamal leaving the club or another club buying him under these circumstances. That’s a problem.”.

It has been revealed that “We’re all very interested in seeing him come back, ideally in top form,” Rummenigge concluded. “But what happens if that isn’t the case? Then we naturally have to consider the finances as well. We want to wait for now.”.
