Hershey’s is bringing back its original five-cent chocolate bar price for one day only to celebrate the theatrical release of its upcoming movie, giving customers several ways to take advantage of the promotion.

The chocolate organization is celebrating the November 25 debut of Hershey, a biographical film exploring the lives of Milton Hershey and his wife, Catherine “Kitty” Hershey, with a limited-time deal inspired by the brand’s history.

The promotion will allow customers to purchase classic Hershey’s Milk Chocolate Bars for just a nickel, matching their original price from 1900. However, the process for claiming the discount varies depending on where customers shop.

The promotion will take place on Wednesday, November 25, with discounted chocolate bars available through Hershey’s Chocolate World, Walmart, and Amazon Grocery.

Customers visiting Hershey’s Chocolate World locations in Hershey, Pennsylvania, or Times Square in Fresh York City can purchase the bars for five cents each beginning at 9 AM local time.

There is a limit of 10 bars per person, and the offer cannot be combined with other discounts. Applicable taxes will still be charged.

Industry observers note that walmart customers can also take advantage of the promotion through two different methods. Those shopping online can purchase a classic Hershey’s Milk Chocolate Bar for five cents by placing an online pickup order through Walmart.com using their Walmart account.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, customers shopping at Walmart stores can receive the discounted price through the Ibotta app. After purchasing a qualifying chocolate bar, shoppers must upload a photo of their receipt to receive cash back.

Walmart’s promotion is limited to five bars per person and does not apply to orders shipped directly to customers’ homes.

Amazon Grocery will also participate in the promotion, offering five-cent chocolate bars through same-day delivery in select markets.

Customers with qualifying delivery addresses can use a promotional code shared through Hershey’s social media channels to receive free shipping at checkout. The offer is limited to two bars per customer while supplies last, with taxes calculated based on the total order.

“The five-cent bar gives fans a chance to experience a sweet piece of Hershey’s story for themselves,” the tech giant remarked in a statement shared with PEOPLE.

“Bringing it back on the day HERSHEY debuts in theaters gives fans a fun way to celebrate the brand’s roots and the story that inspired it.”.

Alongside the promotion, Hershey’s is introducing vintage-inspired packaging for several products, including its Milk Chocolate Bars and Kisses chocolates.

Recent findings suggest that the packaging features the tech giant’s historic wordmark, Milton Hershey’s signature, and typography inspired by the brand’s early years. Each package also includes a QR code directing customers to more information about Hershey’s history.

The upcoming movie stars Finn Wittrock as Milton Hershey and Alexandra Daddario as Catherine Hershey, following their relationship and the development of the chocolate manufacturer.

Directed by Mark Waters and filmed in Pennsylvania, the movie also features Alan Ruck, David Costabile, Richard Kind, and several other cast members.
