Industry News: NFL Injury Report, Week 11: Latest news, updates for Kenneth Walker III, Brock Bowers and more players for fantasy football

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It was a rough week for the New England Patriots over in Germany. As we look over the Week 11 NFL injury report, the Patriots are going to pop up quite a bit going into their bye. We also have five other teams on bye — the Packers, Seahawks, Browns, Falcons and Rams. Those teams will hopefully get healthier over that span.

Below, we'll go over the latest injury news, providing updates on key players for fantasy football.

Drake Maye, NE (ankle) — Out indefinitely.

Geno Smith, NYJ (concussion/ankle) — Intends to play.

Sam Darnold, SEA (quad) — bye week.

Maye: New England heads into its bye, though the season may be lost coming out of it. At this point, it's feared Maye has a fractured ankle and could need season-ending surgery. At the very least, he could miss multiple games or be placed on IR. It's almost helpful that New England is on bye in Week 11, as fantasy managers would already be looking for a Maye replacement or streaming option.

If Maye is done for the season, it's difficult to trust anyone on this offense. There are injuries and ailments everywhere, from A.J. Brown to Rhamondre Stevenson and TreVeyon Henderson. The Pats are well behind the 8-1 Bills in the AFC East, though they are still in the wild-card picture. But if Maye is out, that outlook could change fast. If you have any skill-position player on New England, be sure to have contingency looks. This team could turn into a fantasy wasteland soon.

According to recent reports, mayfield: The Bucs are coming out of the Week 10 bye but may be without Mayfield. He suffered a lower-leg injury in Week 9's loss to Chicago, and the extra rest didn't appear to help. He missed Wednesday's practice but was back out there in a limited fashion on Thursday and it looks like he has a shot to play. This feels like a situation we won't know until Saturday evening or Sunday morning.

Recent findings suggest that if Mayfield is out against Detroit in Week 11, Jalon Daniels, who started three games earlier in the season, would get another shot. With so many teams on bye and Maye managers searching for answers, Daniels is a potential option. Tampa Bay has a pretty soft schedule the rest of the season. Expect the Bucs to continue to ride the running game with Bucky Irving and Kenny Gainwell.

Kyle Monangai, CHI (thumb/toe) — Expected to return.

Jacory Croskey-Merritt, WAS (groin) — Could return from IR.

Jonathon Brooks, CAR (core) — Could return from IR.

Walker: The Chiefs added Walker to the injury report last Friday and he was swiftly ruled out for Week 10 against the Falcons. As a result, rookie Emmett Johnson thrived in lead-back duties, totaling 171 yards on 28 touches (six catches) with two TDs for 32.1 fantasy points. Reports suggest this will be a short absence for Walker, but he did miss practice Wednesday and Thursday.

If Walker is able to play this week, he should return to his role as the lead back and get the majority of the work. Johnson has played well enough to warrant touches, however, and could get extra work given the opponent. Kansas City is a heavy favorite in Week 11, and if the team pulls away, we could see Walker rest and Johnson get work in garbage time. If Walker is inactive, Johnson becomes a must-start.

Texans RBs: Marks was forced to leave Week 10 against the Browns due to a concussion. He's been ruled out for the Thursday matchup against the Colts. Montgomery had three straight full practices this week and is expected to play. This will be his first time suiting up since suffering a groin injury in Week 5. Montgomery is in the low-end RB2 range in a good matchup and with several teams on bye.

Jaylen Waddle, DEN (concussion).

Smith: The Eagles' top wideout has played just once over the past six weeks (not including the Week 10 bye). It's looking like he'll get back on the field after being a full participant at practice Thursday, but this Philly offense hasn't scored more than 21 points in any game during its seven-game losing streak. Getting Smith back will be helpful but it's tough to expect much with Joe Milton under center. Perhaps having a dynamic receiver will unlock something in Milton, but the Steelers present a tough draw as well. Smith is a risky WR3/4 option in PPR formats coming off injury and returning to a team in shambles.

It has been revealed that waddle: The Denver receiver was back at practice in a full capacity on Thursday and is trending toward being ready for Week 11 against the Raiders. It's been a down season for Waddle, his first with the Broncos, as the passing game looks to get things going. Waddle will have a good matchup against Las Vegas, so consider him a WR3/flex option for this week. Pat Bryant and Courtland Sutton are also OK flex options in PPR.

Burden: The Bears were on bye in Week 10 and Burden has had ample time to get right. Head coach Ben Johnson told reporters on Tuesday that Burden had cleared concussion protocol and is good to go for Week 11 against the Saints. With Burden out there, it's all systems go for the Bears coming off the bye.

It has been revealed that evans: The Niners may not be in a rush to get Evans back given the 46 points the offense scored on Sunday against the Cowboys. The veteran wideout missed his third straight game but will have a shot at returning in Week 11 against the Vikings, though he missed practice Wednesday. San Francisco also has a pretty crowded receiving room with Deebo Samuel Sr. returning and Keenan Allen coming over via trade, not to mention George Kittle and the emerging Jordan Watkins. If Evans can get back in Week 11, he's a low-upside WR2/3 given the matchup.

Sources indicate that W. Robinson: The Titans placed the wideout on IR and he is out for the season. Elic Ayomanor should get a nice boost with Robinson out, though Carnell Tate could return against the Cowboys on Sunday — the rookie had a limited practice Wednesday. Ayomanor had 5-79-0 on 10 targets against Jacksonville. He's a decent waiver add this week considering the matchup and lack of depth at WR for Tennessee.

Jake Ferguson, DAL (knee/ankle).

Bowers: Reports came out that Bowers avoided a major injury in Week 10's loss to the Seahawks, which is good news. He has a shot to play in Week 11 against the Broncos, but he did miss practice Wednesday. Head coach Klint Kubiak suggested Bowers could play this week without practicing. Plus, he was back on Thursday as a limited participant, which is encouraging.

In a recent development, the tight end didn't get much time to develop chemistry with new starting QB Fernando Mendoza on Sunday, catching just two passes for 18 yards before exiting. With Mendoza under center, it's a slight downgrade for Bowers, but he's still a locked-in TE1 if he plays. If Bowers ends up missing the game, Michael Mayer is a solid streaming option. The Broncos have been a soft matchup for TEs this season.
