Industry observers note that as the seasons shift from summer to fall, nails start shifting from brighter hues to moody colors like burgundy and brown. But maybe there’s a place for vibrant color in the colder months after all? At least, Hailey Bieber thinks so: The beauty mogul just debuted “blue slushy” nails for fall.

Recent findings suggest that this past week, Bieber attended back-to-back business events in Upcoming York. First, at the 2026 DealBook Summit at Lincoln Center—a conference that unites some of the biggest leaders in tech, business, and politics—Bieber came to the stage in a black-and-white ensemble to talk Rhode in a spring/summer 1998 outfit from Yohji Yamamoto. Famously worn by Carolyn Bessette Kennedy, the minimalist look served as the perfect neutral backdrop for her electric blue nails. Just one day earlier, Bieber was seen with the same blue manicure at the WWD 2026 Beauty Inc. Awards—this time paired with a black-and-white dress and contrasting brick-red lip.

Industry observers note that the Rhode founder has long been a substantial fan of chrome manicures, and her current mani appears to have a hint of shimmer. This isn’t the first time she’s rocked blue on her nails. While in Latest York this past summer, she was seen with a similar shade—but instead of the electric “blue slushy” color, this one leaned more powdery.

Bieber also loves to sport blue on her toes. In July, she posted a photo showcasing how the Rhode pimple patches can be used to heal blisters. Seen alongside the pastel-colored pimple patches was her sea-blue pedicure.

In a recent development, blue has been on Bieber’s mind lately (not to mention “Blues” is the middle name of her son Jack). Her latest collection for Rhode, which officially launches November 20, pegged to her 30th birthday, features navy colorways for the first time, both for the product packaging and on the brand’s first-ever wearable merch. The collection includes the brand’s first matte blush in a mini size, a reversible windbreaker, a phone case accessory, and a upcoming variation of its Peptide Eye Prep patches and Peptide Lip Boost—all in a rich shade of blue.
