It has been revealed that the busy Thanksgiving travel season is just about to kick off as millions take flight around the country. You can expect crowded terminals, long security lines and potentially surge-priced rides to and from the airport.

The good news is that major ride-hailing players Uber, Lyft and Blacklane all have launched Thanksgiving offers, including one that is exclusive to our TPG members.

Here's how to make the most of your holiday ride transport.

Recent findings suggest that through Nov. 30, Uber is running a promotion called "From Bad Seats to Better Rides." If you get squeezed into a middle seat with no armrest or end up in the last row by the lavatory, you could receive up to 30% off your Uber ride home from the airport.

To take part, register your flight and seat details on Uber's Thanksgiving promotion page. Uber provides several examples of undesirable seats but has not published a complete list of what qualifies as undesirable. Individual savings vary, and terms apply, so check the offer added to your Uber account before booking. In our testing, you will not be required to submit a boarding pass or an image of your undesirable seat to receive a discount.

It has been revealed that TPG tip: Link your Delta SkyMiles and Uber accounts and select Delta as your earning partner before you travel. You will earn 1 mile per eligible dollar on UberX airport rides, 2 miles per eligible dollar on Uber XL, Uber Comfort, Uber Black and Uber SUV rides and 3 miles per eligible dollar on Uber Reserve rides in the U.S. Note that taxes, fees, mandatory charges and tips do not earn miles.

No purchase is necessary to enter either sweepstakes.

Recent findings suggest that TPG tip: Link your Marriott Bonvoy and Uber accounts and select Marriott as your earning partner. You will earn 3 Marriott Bonvoy points per eligible dollar on Uber XL, Uber Comfort, Uber Black and Uber SUV rides in the U.S., including those booked with Reserve and 2 points per eligible dollar on UberX rides booked with Reserve. Members who have not linked the two accounts can also earn 1,000 bonus points for linking by Nov. 24, plus another 1,000 points after completing an eligible premium or Reserve ride by the same date.

You can link both Delta and Marriott to Uber, but only one can be selected as the earning partner for a ride at a time, so choose which currency is more useful to you.

United MileagePlus members can earn 8 United miles per eligible dollar on scheduled airport rides with Lyft through Nov. 30, twice the standard rate of 4 miles per dollar. Link your MileagePlus and Lyft accounts and select United as your active rewards partner before booking.

According to recent reports, the offer applies to eligible rides requested, completed and paid for in the U.S. or Puerto Rico.

Make sure you schedule your ride in advance, because unscheduled airport rides only earn 3 miles per eligible dollar, while most standard personal rides earn 1 mile per eligible dollar.

Note that taxes, fees, tolls and tips do not earn miles.

If you would rather avoid a crowded airport pickup zone, why not treat yourself to Blacklane? The global luxury chauffeur service operates in more than 500 cities across more than 60 countries, and its fixed prices include standard taxes, tolls, tips and fees.

According to recent reports, TPG members can right now receive an exclusive $80 credit toward two chauffeur journeys in the U.S. To claim it, sign in to or create a TPG account through the TPG Member Offers page, then click through to Blacklane from the offer.

TPG tip: Bilt members can redeem Bilt Cash toward Blacklane rides each calendar year: up to $50 for Blue and Silver members, $100 for Gold members and $150 for Platinum members. Blacklane rides booked through Bilt also earn 1 Bilt Point per dollar.

Thanksgiving airport rides can be expensive, but this holiday season you may be able to reduce the cost and/or increase your rewards.

Uber's bad-seat offer is the most straightforward discount if your aircraft seat qualifies and is a fun and lighthearted way to reward those whose seat allocation is unfortunate. Uber One members should also check their accounts for targeted airport and hotel offers, while the Lyft promotion is an easy way to earn double United miles on a ride many travelers will book anyway.

Link your loyalty accounts, select the right earning partner and check your card benefits before traveling to ensure a happy and rewarding holiday.
