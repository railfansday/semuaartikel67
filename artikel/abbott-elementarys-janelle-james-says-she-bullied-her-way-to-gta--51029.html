Janelle James is a Grand Theft Auto fan celebrating being part of GTA VI as consumers worldwide get their hands on the long-awaited game.

In a new social media post, the Abbott Elementary star explained how she got involved in the game.

“It’s very key to me that everybody knows that not only is that me, I’m not simply ‘voice acting,'” James shared on her Instagram Stories. “I pursued, conceptualized, pitched, scripted AND voice acted my radio show.”.

She continued, “Rockstar [Games] didn’t ‘choose’ me, I bullied my way to them. I’ve been playing this game since Vice City (was previously overcommitted to Metal Gear), and this is a literal dream come true!!”.

The stand-up comedian said that she’s been “told the response is already great,” adding, “Hope to catch yall in these online streets where I will promptly kick you square in your back then put 2 in your dome.” Watch on Deadline.

Industry observers note that as players explore the GTA VI world, some noticed a very familiar voice in the game. James appears in the game as the host of She Got Assets. Players have been clipping the moment, which you can preview in the video below.

Grand Theft Auto VI is an action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games and is the eighth main entry in the GTA video game series. The long-awaited game comes 16 years after 2013’s Grand Theft Auto V. Set in the fictional Vice City (based on Miami), the new game is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.
