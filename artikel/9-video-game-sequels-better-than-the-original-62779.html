In theory, sequels should always be better than the original since it is easier to see the flaws of the first and correct them. However, sometimes it’s too difficult to capture the certain magic that the first title had, and this is especially true for video games like The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild. However, that isn’t always true, with some sequels not only living up to the hype, but also becoming better video games.

According to recent reports, that is why this list will feature eight video game sequels that are better than the original based on aspects such as improvements, design, gameplay, narrative, originality, fan opinion, critical acclaim, and overall quality. These games took the base of something great and made it even better, becoming the definitive version and a sequel that improved on almost everything. Please note that Red Dead Redemption 2 will not be on this list because it is a prequel, not a sequel.

According to recent reports, games on this list don’t specifically need to be the second game in a franchise, as long as they are a sequel, which opens the door for Baldur’s Gate 3. When the player is infected by a Mind Flayer parasite, they will turn into one if they don’t find a cure for themselves. Teaming up with others in their position, gamers travel across the country in search of this concoction, but the brewing war between gods and mortals makes this much more complicated.

In a recent development, baldur’s Gate 3 is the most recent game on this list, but it didn’t take long to become one of the most influential titles of all time. This is the ultimate role-playing experience, improving on the narrative, scale, gameplay, graphics, and options. Baldur’s Gate 3 is as good a sequel as it can get, even if this entire franchise is home to masterpieces. There is so much love and passion put into this game, and fans can feel it in every ounce of the design, which is exactly what every sequel should have.

Batman is one of the biggest superheroes of all time, with his presence extending from comics to movies and even video games. Batman: Arkham City puts Bruce Wayne into the titular prison, which is a walled-off section of Gotham City. Donning the cap and cowl, players must learn of Protocol 10, the mysterious strategy Hugo Strange has set, but first, Batman needs to take care of the many villains trying to kill him.

In a recent development, batman: Arkham Asylum is a fantastic game, but most fans will agree the sequel is much better. The open-world allowed for more engaging traversal, the narrative was tenser and had fewer errors, and the gameplay was extended with more combos, making it one of the best martial arts video games. It is the perfect follow-up to an already incredible video game, arguably establishing itself as the definitive Batman experience not just in video games, but overall.

Resident Evil is celebrating its 30th anniversary in 2026, with massive projects such as Resident Evil Requiem and the film directed by Zach Cregger. However, nothing can compare to Resident Evil 4, the best sequel in the franchise. When the daughter of the president is kidnapped by a cult in Spain, Leon S. Kennedy goes to save her. But when he learns the cult has been infected by a parasite, survival and escape become even more difficult.

Industry observers note that the first Resident Evil is great, but most of the sequels are better than it, especially Resident Evil 4. While polarizing to some, the changes made in Resident Evil 4 revitalized the franchise, adding stylish action mechanics and an over-the-shoulder camera view. It was simple, but very effective, making this game way easier to play than other titles while maintaining the core difficulty and fun. Resident Evil 4 is a horror masterpiece and a sequel that is one of the best games ever.

It was recently announced that EA shut down BioWare, the game studio that brought fans so many classics, including the Mass Effect franchise. Each game is nearly perfect, but Mass Effect 2 in particular stands out as one of the best. Years after the first game, Commander Shepard is killed in the line of duty, only to be revived shortly after. Those who brought him back to life task him with investigating a mysterious alien race that has abducted entire human colonies, harvesting people to build a newly introduced threat to the entire galaxy.

Sources indicate that the first Mass Effect was a terrific video game that established the beloved formula, and this sequel took what was great in that and expanded on it. The first bright spot is the fact that progress from the first game is saved in the sequel, creating an interconnected feel that makes decisions long-term. Mass Effect 2 strips away the original inventory management, repetitive exploration, and stiff shooting, replacing them with improved mechanics and design for a much better experience. With the gameplay greatly improved, it could then focus on what the franchise is best known for: the worldbuilding, which is what makes Mass Effect 2 one of the best sci-fi video games for beginners.

Assassin’s Creed is one of the best historical video game franchises of all time, and while it has changed its formula to be more mythological and open-world RPG, the roots of historical stealth are unmatched, and that is highlighted by Assassin’s Creed II. Set in Renaissance Italy during the late 15th century, the story follows Ezio, a young Florentine nobleman whose father and brothers are framed for treason and publicly executed. He dons his father’s assassin robes and embarks on a quest for vengeance against those who wronged him, including the Templar Order.

Recent findings suggest that when it comes to superior sequels, Assassin’s Creed II is a textbook example, transforming the foundation into a player-driven experience. Adding mission variety, this mix of tasks built a much better experience in terms of gameplay, and set in the gorgeous Renaissance cities, it was a true joy to explore. Ezio is also regarded as the best protagonist in the franchise, and it isn’t hard to see why, providing a decades-spanning character arc that shows his entire evolution. Assassin’s Creed II is a complete overhaul that creates the quintessential game in the franchise.

The Witcher franchise is at an all-time high, with a TV show adaptation, upcoming DLC, and a sequel, which all started because of the popularity of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. Geralt of Rivia is a Witcher, but he must put aside his usual monster-hunting gig in order to save Ciri, his adoptive daughter, from the titular spectral group that wants to use her powers for evil.

According to recent reports, when talking about The Witcher, most fans just ignore the first two since they didn’t play them. And while they are good, they don’t hold a candle to the third game. The combat can be better, but the worldbuilding and narrative more than make up for that. Lore and details spawn in every corner of the map, with side quests starting as something simple and then evolving into a grand yet personal tale. Not to mention, monster hunting is a pure delight that forces players to be tactical, proving why it is one of the best video games of the 2010s and better than its predecessors.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

Sources indicate that you want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Grand Theft Auto is arguably the greatest video game franchise of all time, and if there was any doubt, it disappeared after Grand Theft Auto VI came out to glowing reviews. Bringing gamers back to Vice City decades later, they control dual protagonists, Lucia and Jason, a couple who work their way through the chaotic city from common thieves to a criminal power couple.

In a recent development, grand Theft Auto VI is officially the highest-rated video game of all time, meaning it is clearly better than its predecessors. While some fans will disagree, there is no denying that the hype paid off, delivering an incredibly rich world of crime, freedom, and chaos. The sheer detail in every corner of Vice City is astounding, and beyond looking gorgeous, GTA VI is the most mechanically deep in the franchise, proving why it is the definitive GTA sequel.

While the most recent Street Fighter movie was critically panned, the sequel has a good chance of being better since the original Street Fighter II is the best game in the franchise. M. Bison hosts an international fighting tournament that brings in the strongest brawlers from around the world, all competing to test their might and win the ultimate prize, but there might be some ulterior motives behind this competition.

In a recent development, the fighting genre, as gamers know it, was built on the back of Street Fighter II, which not only improved the franchise but gaming as a whole. The added combo system became a staple in virtually every game going forward, and the improved graphics, controls, and combat variants made gameplay infinitely more engaging. There aren't many fighting games better than Street Fighter II; it's just a pity the franchise peaked this early.

Valve is one of the greatest video game studios ever, and it seems they have a knack for sequels, with a couple of masterpieces, such as Half-Life 2 and Portal 2. The latter is set years after the first, with Chell waking up to the same facility, except this time it is falling apart. Working with a friendly robot, she attempts to escape the facility, but the reawakening of GLaDOS makes this more challenging.

Portal 2 is an amazing game from start to finish that takes the base of the first game and builds something entirely new on top of it. This game makes the first look like a vertical slice because of the sheer details, expanded gameplay, narrative, and scale. The co-op mode also offers a fun multiplayer experience and new ways to play. Portal 2 isn’t just better than the first game, but it may be the greatest game of all time.
