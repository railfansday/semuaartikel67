Hi there! We're hiring a Senior Customer Intelligence Manager to join our Customer Excellence (Customer Success) team.

metric definitions, data requirements and acceptance criteria that engineering and systems teams can build against.

Working knowledge of how CRM and CS platform objects, CTAs and scorecards fit together (Salesforce, Gainsight, Precursive, or similar)This role is Camunda's single business owner for the data that tells us what a customer is entitled to, what they actually use, and whether they are healthy, at risk or ready to grow. You will turn license, telemetry, usage, contract and engagement data into defined signals, business rules and playbooks that our Customer Success teams act on, and you will lead how Customer Success rolls out AI. This is a senior individual contributor role reporting to the Director of Customer Excellence, with no direct reports, working across EMEA and AMER time zones with Product, GTM Systems, RevOps, Support, Legal, Finance and CS leadership.

Be the business owner of Camunda's license management tool and lead its business-side implementation: represent Customer Success in license key management decisions and vendor selection, and own requirements, acceptance and rollout readiness through launch.

Own licensing and entitlement business rules, including grace periods, the non-payment process, over-usage caps, expiry warning cadence and treatment of existing customers at renewal.

Set telemetry requirements with Product and design the opt-in program that gives self-managed customers a reason to share data.

Own health, risk and opportunity signals: define the health score methodology, specify the CTAs and playbooks each signal triggers, and measure whether signals predict churn and expansion.

Govern customer data definitions: maintain one dictionary of customer metrics and their sources of truth so dashboards, Gainsight and CS AI agents use the same numbers.

Lead AI for CS: run intake and prioritization of use cases, manage rollout of Claude skills, agents and Camunda on Camunda automations, track consumption, cost, adoption and time saved, and own the communications and enablement behind each rollout.

Act as the single front door for CS Operations and GTM Systems requests in these domains, turning ad-hoc asks into problem statements with goals and success criteria.

What You Will Bring to the Role.

Experience owning customer health scoring, churn and expansion signals in B2B SaaS or enterprise software, ideally from CS Operations, CS Strategy or Revenue Operations.

Experience validating data and testing whether a signal predicts outcomes using SQL or BI tools.

A track record of driving cross-functional decisions with Product, Legal, Finance and CS leadership without direct authority.

Clear writing: decision memos, playbooks and enablement content.

Experience leading the business-side implementation of an enterprise system (requirements, UAT, rollout, change management) alongside a technical owner.

Ability and/or willingness to use our product.

Software licensing, entitlement management or usage-based pricing experience.

Exposure to product telemetry and data privacy constraints (GDPR, consent-based data collection).

Experience with self-managed or on-premises software where vendor visibility is limited.

Hands-on experience running AI or automation programs, including using tools like Claude to build analyses, prototypes or agent workflows.

Nice to have: familiarity with process orchestration or Camunda's customer base.
