Bradford led trwice through goals from Nick Powell and Stephen Humphrys in an eventful second-half.

But Rovers indicated their character to respond twice, with a brilliant overhead kick from Jordan Thomas drawing them level at 1-1 before a dramatic stoppage-tome header from Sean Grehan earned them a point.

After brutal away days at Kidderminster and MK Dons, it was a response of real character from Rovers, who host Huddersfield Town next Thursday.

Sources indicate that mcCann remarked: “I've got a real belief in the group. I just need them to believe in themselves a wee bit more.

"Because there's a lot of ability in the group.

"I thought it was a pretty even game. A really good derby game.

"The shape of our team and the organisation and structure was really good for large spells of the game. When you have that, it gives you an opportunity to try and get something out of the game. I was disappointed with the two goals we conceded.

Sources indicate that "I think we could certainly defend those better. But the response from the boys and the two goals that we scored ourselves are really good goals to get us a point.

“I thought Jordan was really good today all day and he's met against a tricky customer in Josh Neuville and Kayden Jackson when he went out there in the second half. But some of his some of his calmness and touch (was excellent); it was a really, really good performance from JT.

“But I thought it was a real team effort today. Not just the lads who started, the boys who came off the bench made a real impact. James Collins, Alfie May, Haks (Hakeeb Adelakun), Neill Byrne and Leon Akinde, they've all contributed to the result today.

“I thought it was always going to be that team effort to get us that today.

“It is a difficult place to come, because you get 25-thousand Bradford fans in the stadium and when you've got that sort of support, the boys will run that extra yard, they'll fight for that extra ball.

Sources indicate that "If you ask any football player, when you've got that type of support behind you, it just gives you that extra motivation to do all those things I just spoke about. But emotionally I thought we were good.

"We went 1-0 down, we didn't panic, we equalised. We went 2-1 down, we didn't panic. The two goals were great for us.”.

Earmarking special praise for James Collins following his debut from the bench, he added: “It was brilliant from James Collins for the first one to tee up JT and it was a brilliant overhead kick.

"And again, the delivery from Gottsy (Robbie Gotts) and the experience of Colo to take two of their defenders under the ball to free up Sean was excellent.”.
