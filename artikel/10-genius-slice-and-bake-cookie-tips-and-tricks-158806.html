It has been revealed that instead, Josh recommends using something like a fish fillet knife, which is ultra-thin and very flexible. If you don’t have one on hand, use a paring knife—just make sure it’s long enough to cut through to the other side in one motion. Maggie has also seen bakers successfully use unflavored dental floss or metal wire. Wrap it around the dough log, then pull the strings together to cut through—it works better than trying to use it in one direction like a knife.
