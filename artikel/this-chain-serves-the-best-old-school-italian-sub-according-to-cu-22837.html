📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

You might not expect it, but sandwich chains are holding it down when it comes to Italian subs. Of course, there is no denying the appeal of a small, locally owned shop, but there are many reasons why you might opt instead to head into a chain to get your Italian hoagie fix, the most obvious of which is proximity. But there is also the fact that some chains just make darn good food. Food Republic did its research and compiled a list of the best chain Italian subs based on customer reviews, and each entry brings its own unique flavor to the table. But only one, the No. 13 Original Italian from Jersey Mike's, can claim to be the best old-school version of this particular sub.

"They do it right," one Reddit user said, referencing the Original Italian. They continued by stating that if they had a classic Italian deli near them, they'd go there, but since they don't, "it's Jersey Mike's without question." Another user in that same thread agreed, writing, "I love this sub and am always a little amazed [it comes from] a chain" and not, presumably, from a traditional deli. And over on Google, a reviewer shared that the Original Italian reminded them of a sub they'd had during their childhood and expressed their gratitude that "there is a place that can make a real Italian hero like the old days.".

It's worth noting that an old-school-style Italian sub owes it all to the Italian immigrants who populated this country throughout the 19th and 20th centuries. There are a few stories about how the hoagie came to be, including one based in Philadelphia (where it's the official sandwich of the city) involving shipyard workers and their distinctive lunches. There is another version that describes how Giovanna Amato sold filling sandwiches to dockworkers in Maine. Whatever its origins, the classic Italian sub came to be characterized by Italian meats and cheeses, a variety of fresh, crisp vegetables, a savory dressing, and an oily, vinegary drizzle.

Recent findings suggest that jersey Mike's Original Italian proves a chain can still honor those old-school roots. It checks every box, from five different Italian meats to provolone cheese to the default setting, Mike's Way, which includes onions, lettuce sliced super-thin, and tomatoes, as well as red wine vinegar and an olive oil blend, plus two impactful seasonings: salt and oregano.

It has been revealed that but of course, Jersey Mike's serves 21st-century customers who like having options, so the sandwich, in addition to scratching an old-school itch, can also serve as a jumping-off point for some. You might, for example, upgrade your Original Italian sub by choosing the rosemary parmesan bread instead of plain white. There are a number of additional toppings available as well, such as jalapeño peppers, dill pickles, and hot chopped pepper relish.
