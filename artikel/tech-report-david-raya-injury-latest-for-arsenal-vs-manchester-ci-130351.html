Mikel Arteta states David Raya will undergo more tests on his shoulder injury before Arsenal’s Premier League game against Manchester City on Sunday.

Raya was forced off in the second half of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw away to Newcastle United on Saturday evening.

The Spanish goalkeeper appeared to be in significant discomfort and has been unable to train since Arsenal’s game at St James’ Park.

Kepa Arrizabalaga took Raya’s place in goal for Arsenal’s 2-2 draw at home to Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday night.

But Arteta admits Raya is still an injury doubt for Arsenal’s next Premier League fixture against City at the Emirates Stadium this weekend.

Speaking ahead of kick-off against Dortmund, Arteta told Prime Sport: ‘Again, we need some more tests.

According to recent reports, ‘He’s evolving well. Obviously, we were very relieved with the news because we feared the worst. But we’ll have to wait and see.’.

When asked after his side’s 2-2 draw with Dortmund, Arteta added: ‘We need to know first how bad it is and whether he has a chance or not in the next few weeks.’.

During his pre-match press conference on Monday, Arteta confirmed that Raya will not need to undergo surgery.
