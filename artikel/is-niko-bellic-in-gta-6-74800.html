Niko Bellic is one of my favorite GTA protagonists, a down-to-earth immigrant looking for a better life in Liberty City, though admittedly with a few anger challenges and skeletons in his closet.

Still, when people spotted that Niko's voice actor, Michael Hollick, was listed in the GTA 6 credits as "The Hitman", the rumour mill really kicked into overdrive around whether he was playing the same character.

According to recent reports, everyone knows how much GTA games love their callbacks and cameos, though I'm sure no one would want him to meet the same fate as Johnny from the Lost and Damned in GTA 5. Again, if you don't want to know who Hollick plays in GTA 6, then leave now.

Industry observers note that the straight answer is, yes, Niko does appear in GTA 6 during Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma, near the very end of the game, specifically, in the Taking Liberties mission. Obviously, he's a lot older and more grizzled considering GTA 4 was released *checks watch* 18 years ago (I immediately turned to dust).

It has been revealed that I won't go into the specifics of his appearance—you can definitely track it down if you want to, but you've most likely still got quite a few Chapters to play through before you'll reach this cameo. It's definitely a fun one for us fans of Niko and who still think the 4th game in the series is the best, though GTA 6 might convince me yet.
