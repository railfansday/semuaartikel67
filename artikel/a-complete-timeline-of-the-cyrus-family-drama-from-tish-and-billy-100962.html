And tbh, we’d tune in. But if you haven’t been Keeping Up With the Cyruses, gather round because it’s equal parts complicated and messy.

According to recent reports, the short version: Everyone picked sides when Tish Cyrus and Billy Ray Cyrus got divorced, but after years of feuding, it seems the family may have buried the hatchet to all come together for Miley’s wedding...for now, anyway. Baffled? Same, so let’s dive in.

Tish filed for divorce from Billy Ray in April 2022, and they released the following joint statement to People:.

I mean, sounds pretty chill! But Tish has since claimed she put up with “disrespect in every form” during the marriage, so...it seems like there was more going on behind the scenes than fans initially thought.

In fact, just a few months after Tish and Billy’s split announcement, a source told E! News that “the divorce between her parents has put a strain on [Miley’s] relationship with her dad and it’s been hard to connect with him in the last year. She hopes they can eventually work on their relationship.”.

Tish married Prison Break star Dominic Purcell in August 2023, and the internet was quick to clock that neither Noah Cyrus nor Braison Cyrus were in the bridal party (whereas Miley Cyrus, Brandi Cyrus, and Trace Cyrus were).

Instead, Noah and Braison seemingly spent the day chilling at Walmart and posted a selfie that featured Noah in a Billy Ray Cyrus t-shirt.

A few months after, a source confirmed to Us Weekly that Noah has “always been close with Billy Ray,” adding that this has also “caused a rift between her and Miley.”.

Oh, and another insider claimed, “The family dynamics haven’t been the same since the divorce, and now that Tish and Billy Ray are with other people, there’s even more division among them all.”.

While Miley and her sister have historically been close, fans spotted the first signs of drama in October 2023, when Noah wrote, “The disrespect in this video...” in the comment section of a clip of Miley on The Joe Rogan Experience.

Right around the same time, Billy Ray married Firerose—but it’s unclear whether any of Billy’s children were in attendance.

After winning an award at the 2024 Grammys, Miley left Billy out of her acceptance speech, and a source later told Us Weekly that he’d been attempting to reach out with congratulations.

Industry observers note that “He’s tried reaching out to Miley many times and congratulated her on her Grammys,” the insider said. “The kids have chosen sides, but friends are hoping the rift doesn’t last forever. Miley and Billy Ray are on the outs. Miley’s very close with her mom and is standing by her.”.

Another source put it more simply, saying, “Miley hasn’t gotten over the disrespect she feels Billy Ray showed Tish and the family.”.

It has been revealed that later on in the month, in a truly wild plot twist, an insider alleged to Us Weekly, “Noah was [seeing] Dominic when Tish started pursuing him,” adding Tish “was aware" of the situation and that “the turmoil between Noah and Tish goes far beyond [what people think]. Noah is very distraught over Tish stealing Dominic from her.”.

Key note: Tish later claimed that she and Dominic met via DMs and did not mention anything about Noah...

Us Weekly also spoke to a source who remarked, “Noah and Tish haven’t spoken since the whole thing happened with Dominic,” and that “Tish is spiraling out of control and trying to figure out how to defuse this.” According to this person, Noah and Dominic were dating for “about eight to nine months” but were “having concerns.”.

Recent findings suggest that however! The Daily Mail spoke to an anonymous insider who claimed, “Noah never dated Dominic and they were not together. She is delusional to think otherwise and is trying to create a narrative that will paint her as a victim.” This source emphasized that “Noah was not invited to Tish’s wedding and she would not have come even if she was. Tish wanted a peaceful wedding and did not want Noah to create any sort of scene.”.

The insider went on to add, “Noah has always craved the attention because she did not get it growing up. Tish was always so Miley-sided because that is where the money was. Miley and Tish are like sisters and Noah has always been envious of this because she’s never had a connection with Tish like Miley has.”.

Recent findings suggest that when it comes to whether or not Miley knew about Noah and Dominic, sources differ. One person told People, “Miley had no idea about the drama with Dominic” and “confronted her mom about it,” while another told Us Weekly that Miley “absolutely knew” and was “fully aware.” Yikes!!!!

Yep! The musician shared a birthday message on Instagram for her mom in May amid the duo’s alleged feud alongside the caption, “Happy Birthday Mom.”.

Aaaaaaand by July, the two were seen out and about together in Los Angeles.

It has been revealed that during her My Next Guest Needs No Introduction episode, which aired in June, Miley explained (per E!), “Honestly, my mom is my hero. My father—I’m grateful for being able to watch him ahead of me. He’s almost given me this map. There is a map of what to do and what not to do. And he’s guided me on both.”.

She continued, “My dad had a really rough childhood. And my childhood—I mean, we can go and talk about the hard times or the struggles—but I had food, I had love, I grew up in a beautiful substantial house and my dad didn’t have that.”.

Miley’s rare comments were aired amid Billy Ray filing for divorce from Firerose after seven months of marriage due to irreconcilable differences and “inappropriate marital conduct.”.

It has been revealed that following his dad’s performance at Trump’s inauguration Liberty Ball, Trace posted an open letter to Billy on Instagram, expressing concern for his health. Trace noted, “Me and the girls have been genuinely worried about you for years but you’ve pushed all of us away.”.

According to recent reports, he went on to add, “Noah desperately has wanted you to be a part of her life and you haven’t even been there for her. That’s your baby girl. She deserves better. Somehow just like me she still idolizes you though.”.

Apparently, Billy Ray later threatened him with “legal action” for his public plea, because three days later, Trace followed up with since-deleted IG note, explaining that his open letter “was beyond loving” and that he “could have been extremely honest about a lot more.”.

According to recent reports, billy ended up making a cryptic statement, saying, “Giving thanks for the California Rain . Praying for the brokenhearted and their pain. Praying for my family. For my children … sons and daughters…and their mother. Let this moment be the start of healing ❤️‍🩹 for us all. ‘The past does not equal the future.’ Amen.”.

After Trace’s open letter to Billy Ray went viral, Braison (who helped Billy produce his latest album) took to his IG Stories to respond to his older bro, noting that their dad was “happy, healthy, and ready to make this record.”.

A fan then slid into Trace’s DMs to question Braison’s post, and Trace replied with this cryptic message: “Birds of a feather flock together.” Well then!

Meanwhile, a “Cyrus family source” told People that family was attempting to work things out. “They got through the drama of 2024, and it’s a upcoming year,” the source said. “Miley and Noah and Braison and Tish, they don’t want to be this Cyrus family feud. This is not enjoyable for them.”.

Recent findings suggest that per a source for People, “Miley used to feel overwhelmed by the family drama. She worked hard to remove herself. She’s not about to get involved again. She’s in a great place now. She’s thriving and looking out for herself.”.

For Braison’s 31st birthday, Miley and her fiancé Maxx Morando joined Billy on his farm in Tennessee, and her dad snapped a cute selfie to prove it.

During a profile with The Latest York Times that same month, Miley revealed that she and Billy have healed their bond. “As I’ve gotten older, I’m respecting my parents as individuals instead of as parents—because my mom really loved my dad for her whole life, and I think being married to someone in the music industry and not being a part of it is obviously really hard,” Miley remarked at the time. “And so I think I took on some of my mom’s hurt as my own because it hurt her more than it hurt me as an adult, and so I owned a lot of her pain.”.

She added, “But now that my mom is so in love with my stepdad, who I completely adore, and now that my dad, I see him finding happiness, too—I can love them both as individuals instead of as a parental pairing.”.

The following month, during an interview on Monica Lewinsky’s podcast, Miley opened up about “Secrets” off the deluxe edition of her album Something Beautiful.

“I wrote this song about my dad, because I wanted him to tell me even though there were secrets, even though I didn't really want to know,” she explained. “I wanted to be the one he felt safe enough to tell me the things that were damning and damaging to the family. I wanted him to think that as a middle child, I’m old enough that I could take some of that.”.

In a recent development, she also talked about where her family stood in general, saying, “My family and I have had a really difficult…What did you call your decade? Dark decade. Yeah, we had one of those.”.

She continued, “Half of us weren't speaking to each other at one point, and we cleaned all that up. That was a really important part of my year this year, was all of my family putting those lines of communication back together. A lot because of, you know, I had a lot of loyalty to my mom, the way that families do when parents get divorced.”.

Miley sat down with her mom and sisters for an interview with The Cut, where it was revealed that the Cyrus ladies are all on good terms.

At one point, Miley said, “One thing I’ve always loved about our family is that we’re very honorable and respectful of each other’s differences; I think a lot of families get torn apart from their differences...but as we’ve gotten older, we’ve found our own opinions and ideas individually.”.

To celebrate 20 years of Hannah Montana, Miley starred in a TV special, during which both of her parents made appearances.

Miley tied the knot with Maxx in Palm Springs, where—according to a source for People—“the most special part was seeing Miley’s family all come together to celebrate her.”.

In attendance were Miley’s parents Tish and Billy, Billy’s girlfriend Elizabeth Hurley and her son Damian Hurley, Tish’s husband Dominic, Miley’s sister Noah and her fiancé Pinkus, and Miley’s sister Brandi and her fiancé Matt Southcombe.

It’s unclear, however, whether Miley’s brothers Trace and Braison (or her half-brother Christopher Cody) were there.
