📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that princess Catherine and Prince William are starting their tour of India off strong. Today, the royal couple arrived in Mumbai for their three-day state visit—their first joint one in four years. And as photos show, the pair is ecstatic to be there.

Kate brought a colorful wardrobe for the mini trip with her husband. She made her first public appearance this morning in a fresh pistachio-green blazer from Veronica Beard, and then changed into a bright cobalt-blue dress from Roksanda.

She paired the blazer with a breezy white top, slim white trousers from McQueen, and black-and-beige flats with pearl-embellished buckles from Camilla Elphick for a fun game of street cricket. Dangly crystal earrings and a matching teardrop necklace completed the daytime look.

Then, to take a ride on Mumbai’s Metro and to attend the Earthshot Prize Impact Assembly, Kate changed into her vibrant Roksanda midi dress—the same one she wore to the Wimbledon men’s final in July 2025.

The design has a pretty V neckline. short, structured sleeves with a bow-like detail on one shoulder, a belted waist, and a flowy skirt.

The Princess of Wales styled the dress with chocolate-brown accents: suede Gianvito Rossi heels and the small Hudson bag from DeMellier.

Finally, she upped the glamour with blue and gold jewelry, including a pair of lapis lazuli-and-diamond drop earrings by Indian jewelry brand Amrapali—pieces which she wore to a charity gala in Mumbai during her 2016 tour of India.

William and Kate are in Mumbai to attend the sixth annual Earthshot Prize Summit, but during their visit will also take the time to carry out engagements relating to sustainability, innovation, and the relationship between India and the United Kingdom.
