Sony and Rockstar Games are making damn sure you know GTA 6 has just launched, as the PS5's Welcome hub — the landing page for the console — has been taken over by the new game.

The page's icon at the moment has that telltale pink/orange gradient and palm trees, and before you see your usual assortment of widgets, a new panel promoting GTA 6 appears.

You can dismiss it if you don't want to see it, and there's also an option to view the game, which takes you to its PS Store page.

Scroll down and you'll find your usual Welcome dashboard, but there's also a new icon here, letting you know there are new, GTA 6-related backgrounds available.

Recent findings suggest that click through and you'll see a GTA 6 folder, which contains five new wallpapers with which you can decorate your Welcome hub. They're all in that signature illustrated style, with most featuring protagonists Jason and Lucia.

It's always nice to get more backgrounds for the Welcome page, and this game getting special treatment is no real surprise.
