📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Are you ready for a rude reminder about the fragility of time? December is nearly here. Impossibly, we’re already heading into the final stretch of 2026, though at least the best streaming services are helping to soothe that calendar shock with a fresh slate of upcoming movies and TV highlights. And HBO Max, in particular, has a stacked lineup with which to spend your final weeks of the year.

It has been revealed that things kick off on December 1 with a fist-pumping throwback as an iconic, multi-decade boxing franchise — spanning from Sylvester Stallone’s 1976 classic to its modern spinoffs — steps into the ring. Elsewhere on the film front, a provocative comedic thriller led by the winning duo of Olivia Wilde and Cooper Hoffman makes its streaming debut on December 4, before an acclaimed sci-fi horror sequel lands later in the month to keep things spooky.

Recent findings suggest that over on the TV side, the platform is capping things off on Christmas Day with a polarizing new series adaptation that kicks off a fresh era for a massive wizarding franchise. From nostalgic knockouts to broomstick-flying drama, here’s a guide to everything new on HBO Max in December 2026.

Sources indicate that one of the most talked-about new movies of the so-called "awards season" thus far has been "I Play Rocky," an underdog biopic starring Anthony Ippolito as Sylvester Stallone during the tumultuous production of his iconic boxing flick "Rocky," which famously went on to win the Oscar for Best Picture. But before you tune into that new bio-drama, why not go back to where it all started with a big "Rocky" movie marathon?

Helpfully, HBO Max isn't just bringing back the 1976 original — which is celebrating its major 50th anniversary this year and centers on a small-time Philadelphia boxer (Stallone) who gets a once-in-a-lifetime chance to face the world heavyweight champion in the ring — but all nine films in the "Rocky" franchise beginning on December 1. That includes the "Rocky" sequels “Rocky II” (1979), “Rocky III” (1982), “Rocky IV” (1985), “Rocky V” (1990) and “Rocky Balboa” (2006), as well as the Michael B. Jordan-led "Creed" spinoff trilogy comprising “Creed” (2015), “Creed II” (2018) and “Creed III” (2023).

That should keep you busy until Latest Year's, right?

Stream "Rocky" on HBO Max starting December 1.

Few Hollywood stars had a 2026 like Olivia Wilde, who directed arguably the year's best comedy, "The Invite," and also pops up opposite Pedro Pascal in the cellist dramedy "Behemoth!" and alongside Cooper Hoffman in this Gregg Araki-directed erotic comedy.

In "I Want Your Sex," Wilde plays renowned artist and provocateur Erika Tracy, who takes fresh-faced twentysomething Elliot Bell (Hoffman) as her assistant and sexual muse. As their arrangement begins to blur professional and personal lines, Elliot is pulled into a world of sexuality, obsession, power, betrayal and murder.

Along with Wilde and Hoffman, the star-studded cast includes Charli XCX, Mason Gooding, Chase Sui Wonders, Johnny Knoxville, Margaret Cho and Daveed Diggs.

Stream "I Want Your Sex" on HBO Max starting December 4.

It has been revealed that one of the most anticipated TV titles of the year has also been its most controversial, drawing backlash over J.K. Rowling's involvement, casting choices, and, most odd of all, children's teeth. Regardless, plenty of HBO viewers will undoubtedly be tuning into another year at the Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, with a new set of fresh faces playing some of modern-day literature's most iconic characters.

The eight-episode series will follow pretty much the same plotline as the first "Harry Potter" book and subsequent film, with Dominic McLaughlin as Harry Potter, Arabella Stanton as Hermione Granger, Alastair Stout as Ron Weasley, John Lithgow as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, and Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape.

Stream "Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" on HBO Max starting December 25.
