Sources indicate that EXCLUSIVE: Flying largely under the radar early on, Reasonable Doubt has become a major Hulu success story, leading to a Season 5 renewal ahead of the legal drama’s Season 4 finale on Nov. 24.

The early pickup comes as Season 4 has become the series’ most-watched installment to date, according to Hulu, posting double-digit percentage gains over every prior season when most returning streaming series are trending down.

According to recent reports, it also makes Reasonable Doubt, starring Emayatzy Corinealdi, the second longest Hulu Originals drama series behind The Handmaid’s Tale. It is also the longest-running scripted series out of Onyx Collective after helping launch the Disney brand as its first show. Starting off with a largely lesser known core cast, Reasonable Doubt, from creator/executive producer Raamla Mohamed Hunter and executive producer Kerry Washington, was well received, scoring 100% on Rotten Tomatoes for its first season. The series has since become a magnet for major-name talent, adding Morris Chestnut in Season 2, Joseph Sikora in Season 3 and Durrell “Tank” Babbs and Meagan Good in Season 4. Watch on Deadline.

Since Season 2, Reasonable Doubt also has run on an yearly cadence, helping with engagement, and has gotten awards recognition.

Building momentum in Season 4, the latest episode surged more than +40% over the season premiere, based on one day of views globally, while also trending on social media following its debut.

Per Disney, Reasonable Doubt has been streamed for over 150 million hours globally to date across Hulu and Disney+.

Season 4 follows Jax’s (Corinealdi) most loyal client at the center of a spiraling media disaster, which thrusts The Stewart Firm into a high-stakes battle where every move could mean survival … or collapse of the firm. As grief lingers and pressure mounts, one question looms: Can they rebuild in time, or will the cost of moving forward too swiftly be everything they stand to lose?

Reasonable Doubt Season 4 cast includes series regulars Corinealdi, McKinley Freeman, Tim Jo, Angela Grovey, Babbs and Good, alongside recurring guest stars Chestnut, Sikora, Brandee Evans, La La Anthony, Rutina Wesley, Taylor Polidore Williams, DeVaughn Nixon and Alexxis Lemire.

It has been revealed that the series is created, written and executive produced by Raamla Mohamed Hunter. Kerry Washington executive produces alongside Pilar Savone for Simpson Street, Larry Wilmore for Wilmore Films, Anton Cropper, Lena Cordina, Tamara Gregory and Erika L. Johnson. Shawn Holley, Jon Leshay and Zahir McGhee serve as co-executive producers. The series hails from Onyx Collective and is produced by 20th Television, a part of Disney Television Studios.
