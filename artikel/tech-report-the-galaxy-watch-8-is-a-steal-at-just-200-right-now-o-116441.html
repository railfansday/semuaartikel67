Amazon has lowered the price of the Samsung Galaxy Watch 8 into the “steal” category right now, as the Wear OS smartwatch starts at just $199 as part of the extended Black Friday deals.

According to recent reports, while these are technically last year’s wearables, you get a lot of smartwatch for your money. Samsung tends to keep upgrades to a minimum each year, so the differences between the Watch 8 and Watch 9 are very minimal.

There’s no doubt that the Galaxy Watch 8 in 40mm is a steal, but the 44mm “regular” size watch is down to just $229.99, which is a 30% discount off the MSRP. If you prefer the Galaxy Watch 8 Classic, it’s down to $349.99 right now over on Amazon, but it’s a much bulkier wearable.

Industry observers note that the entire lineup offers you all the trappings of a modern Wear OS smartwatch, Gemini integration, long-lasting batteries, and access to Samsung’s watch accessory ecosystem. If you’re a Pixel owner, you’ll still have a great time, but for Galaxy smartphone owners, you get access to a few exclusive functions like ECG, some Galaxy Kids functions, and enhancements to the Modes and Routines options.
