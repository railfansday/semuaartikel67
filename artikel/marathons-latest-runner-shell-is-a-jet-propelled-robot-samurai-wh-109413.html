We're just a couple weeks away from Marathon's Symbiosis patch, a hefty overhaul patch that's aiming to inject some juice into the shooter's live service appeal with the addition of PvE roguelite and Team Deathmatch modes, new weapons, and even a social hub for hanging out with fellow runners and testing guns at the firing range.

It has been revealed that and that's all great. But today, Bungie dropped a latest cinematic that presents its strongest argument for why Symbiosis should have our attention: You can play as a terrifying, sports-car scarlet robot samurai with a sword arm and jet legs.

While Bungie previously revealed Bishop, the newly introduced runner shell arriving with Symbiosis, in the update's first gameplay trailer earlier this month, the new cinematic provides a strong sense of the power fantasy it's trying to capture. By which I mean Bungie clearly wants you to feel like a nightmarish apex predator. Like a xenomorph if a xenomorph was bright red and had cool space guns.

As the trailer progresses, we watch as a team of runners is steadily cut down by a Bishop shell's unflinching onslaught: First, one of its members is culled after taking one of Bishop's throwable hacking knives to the neck, which seems to disrupt the target's functions. The next is blasted out of midair while trying to zipline away to safety. The third is hounded by autopistol fire into a position where they can be conveniently pounced on from above.

According to recent reports, but the trailer's peak moment happens as the Bishop prepares to loot one of its kills and is winged by a bullet from a nearby Rook, who evidently hoped to clean up after watching the whole fight pan out. This is proven to be a mistake, because the Bishop settles into a crouch, extending a plasma-edged sword blade from between its knuckles and engaging the jet thrusters in its calves.

You know that thing in samurai movies where a swordsman does a lunging slash, leaving the body of his opponent to realize that it's been cut down behind him? It's like that. But with robots and jet propulsion. Marathon's going to have iaijutsu, is what I'm saying. Something for us all to look forward to.

Marathon's Symbiosis update lands on December 8.
