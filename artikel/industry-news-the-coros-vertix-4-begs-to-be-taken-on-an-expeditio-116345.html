The Coros Vertix 4 sports sapphire glass and a titanium bezel, including a built-in flashlight for the first time ever.

According to recent reports, coros is best known for its impressively accurate, lightweight smartwatches for runners, but the company continues to expand with a new rugged Vertix model. The Coros Vertix 4 sports a 1.47-inch LTPO AMOLED touchscreen display with 3,500-nit peak brightness, sapphire glass for protection, a titanium bezel for added durability, and an operating range of -30 to 50 degrees Celsius (seriously).

The Coros Vertix 4’s built-in flashlight is a first for the company’s smartwatches and comes after years of fans begging for such a feature. Coros says it specifically positioned the light toward where your hands are working, making it more than just a fun, niche feature. As with all Coros watches, this one sports weeks-long battery life, rated at 30 days of normal use and even including 105 hours of GPS tracking per charge.

Industry observers note that the Coros Vertix 4’s newly introduced processor can render offline maps in vivid detail, including landform, road, contour line maps, and satellite maps. 64GB of onboard storage should be more than enough to hold all your tunes (and offline maps), and a 4th-generation optical heart rate sensorwith SpO2 capability will give you great health insights along the way.

The Coros Vertix 4 acts as a “consolidation” of previous Vertix lines, mashing together the disparate features of the Vertix 2 and 2S, all in a 14% slimmer body. All the fan-favorite Coros features are here, too, including a boatload of tracked sports modes and free workouts and training from Coros coaches and athletes. The Vertix 4 also ships with both silicone and nylon bands, so you don’t have to buy extra stuff. The watch ships in black and silver colorways for $799.
