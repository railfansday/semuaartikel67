Marli Salmon was sent off as England Under-19s fell to a heavy defeat against Austria.

The 17-year-old made his debut for that age group last week in a comfortable win over Slovenia, having been called up as an injury replacement.

However, it was a much more difficult game for Salmon on Tuesday as his afternoon came to a premature end.

The Arsenal defender got the nod against Austria and it was an eventful start to the match, with three goals inside the opening ten minutes.

Austria scored again before half-time to lead 3-1 and that was still the scoreline heading into the final 20 minutes of the match.

Salmon was booked for dissent, having shouted in the referee’s face and waved his arm angrily after conceding a free-kick.

Four minutes later, the teenager was then shown a straight red card to complete a miserable occasion for him.

Reigan Heskey played a loose pass back that left his team-mates scrambling and Salmon could not cut out the danger on the halfway line.

Austria’s Johannes Moser got to the ball first and was running clean through on goal, only for Salmon to slide in and bring him down.

He immediately punched the ground in frustration and did not even look back at the referee, knowing the red card was coming, and he began trudging off the pitch.

Austria went on to score another goal with the man advantage and secure a 4-1 win.

It is the second international break in a row that an Arsenal player has been sent off for the England U19s.

Max Dowman was shown a straight red card last month against the USA for a reckless challenge.

In a recent development, salmon was shown a straight red card for dissent while captaining Arsenal’s Under-21s in a 1-0 defeat by Ipswich Town back in Feburary. He signed his first professional deal with the Gunners in April.
