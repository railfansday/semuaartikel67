Latest Update: Roberto Fernández goal earns Eder Sarabia’s Espanyol win at San Mamés

  • A crowd of 46,107 was at San Mamés on Friday 20 November 2026.
  • The win was Eder Sarabia’s second in charge of Espanyol and his second in a row away from home.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Espanyol beat Athletic Club 1-0 in Bilbao through a Roberto Fernández goal. The result extends a strong run since Manolo González left and leaves Athletic seventh after a home defeat in which they dominated the statistics.

A crowd of 46,107 was at San Mamés on Friday 20 November 2026. The fixture opened Matchday 13 of LaLiga EA Sports.

The win was Eder Sarabia’s second in charge of Espanyol and his second in a row away from home. The club have now taken seven of the nine points available since González left, according to EFE. Espanyol named Sarabia head coach on 28 October 2026, on a contract that runs until 2028.

Recent findings suggest that his appointment followed a 4-3 Copa del Rey defeat at Sestao River. A 1-0 win at Villarreal, a 0-0 draw with Deportivo La Coruna and Friday’s victory in Bilbao make up Espanyol’s results since González’s exit. Their goal difference now stands at zero.

In a recent development, the first half ended goalless. Tyrhys Dolan replaced Pere Milla in the 57th minute. Sarabia also sent on Gabriel Moscardó and Javi Hernández in the 65th minute.

According to recent reports, A minute later, Dolan set up Fernández for the winner. It was the striker’s sixth league goal of the season, putting him eighth in the LaLiga scoring list. He finished with 3 shots and a rating of 7.92.

Sarabia then brought on Unai Núñez and Javi Puado in the 84th minute to protect the lead. Clemens Riedel was booked in the third minute of stoppage time, Espanyol’s only card of the night.

Goalkeeper Ángel Fortuño made six saves to keep a clean sheet.

Recent findings suggest that the result gave Espanyol a third successive league win over Athletic. They won 2-0 at home on 13 May 2026 and 2-1 at San Mamés on 22 December 2025.

It has been revealed that athletic controlled most of the statistics. They had 58% of possession and 9 corners to Espanyol’s 2. On target, the hosts had 6 efforts and the visitors 3.

It has been revealed that edin Terzić moved away from the 4-4-2 he had used in previous matches to fit Oihan Sancet into a 4-2-3-1. The change did not produce more attacking threat. Deia judged this to be Athletic’s worst performance at San Mamés since Terzić took charge.

Nico Williams had 5 shots and 3 key passes. Iñigo Ruiz de Galarreta also created 3 chances in 29 minutes on the pitch. Athletic collected three yellow cards, for Iñaki Williams, Ruiz de Galarreta and Yeray Álvarez.

Before kick-off, San Mamés paid tribute to Elene Gurtubay and Daniela Agote, who won the Under-20 World Cup.

Sources indicate that after 12 matches, Terzić’s side have 16 points. Their recent run reads one draw, two wins and one defeat in the league before Friday’s loss. Real Sociedad sit fifth on 22 points.

Athletic travel to bottom side Malaga on Saturday 28 November. Espanyol host Getafe on Monday 30 November.
