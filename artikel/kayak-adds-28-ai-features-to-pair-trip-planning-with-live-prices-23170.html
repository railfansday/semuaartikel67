KAYAK confirmed on November 17, 2026, that it is rolling out 28 latest and enhanced AI features across its travel-search experience, expanding Ask AI with flexible-date price searches, conversational itineraries, mobile access and more detailed flight and hotel searches.

In a recent development, the updates extend a hybrid workflow in which travelers discuss and refine intends in Ask AI while live flight, hotel and rental-car results and prices patch beside the conversation. KAYAK’s conventional filters and sorting tools remain available to compare those options, connecting open-ended conversation to the evaluation steps that follow.

It has been revealed that in KAYAK’s distributed release, CEO Peer Bueller explained the reason for connecting the chat to current prices: “AI is great at suggesting where to go, but it can sound confident even when it’s out of date. That’s why our AI is tied to live prices, so you can verify what it suggests before you book.”.

Sources indicate that KAYAK reports that travelers who engage with Ask AI are about 2.5 times as likely to book as those who do not. The figure associates the tool with booking behavior without establishing causation, but it makes the integration commercially relevant for KAYAK.

Flexible planning is a central target. KAYAK says 30% of sampled Ask AI conversations since the product’s launch included flexible or open-ended requests, such as finding the cheapest weekend to fly to Rome in January.

According to recent reports, for that kind of search, Ask AI uses the technology and data behind KAYAK’s Buy vs. Wait recommendations to identify promising dates, then checks those dates against live prices for available, bookable flights. KAYAK says this goes beyond some tools that suggest dates using average prices because the traveler can see what is currently available and what it costs.

It has been revealed that one prompt can also generate a personalized, day-by-day itinerary, with subsequent conversational changes updating the accompanying flight, hotel and rental-car results. Travelers can set Price Alerts inside Ask AI while continuing to weigh prepares. The updates also bring Ask AI to KAYAK’s iOS and Android apps, extending the chat-and-results workflow to mobile devices.

According to recent reports, for flights, clickable prompts can compare basic economy with main cabin and explain baggage rules, cancellation policies and aircraft details for the specific flight and fare being viewed. A fresh Starlink Wi-Fi filter, or a query to Ask AI, can identify flights offering Starlink connectivity.

Sources indicate that hotel searches can use descriptions such as a rooftop pool or reviews mentioning quiet rooms, even when no standard filter exists for the requested characteristic. AI-powered summaries beneath hotel listings highlight details such as kid-friendly swimming pools. Updated images and AI-powered neighborhood summaries add context for deciding where to stay.

It has been revealed that route on-time performance would let travelers weigh reliability alongside price and schedule, while preference memory would use shared details such as nonstop flights or morning departures to personalize future recommendations. KAYAK marks both capabilities as coming soon, distinguishing them from the features available in this rollout.

In a recent development, KAYAK introduced Ask AI on May 14, 2026. The original product already supported natural-language questions, real-time refinement and live flight, hotel and rental-car results, with bookable options from hundreds of travel partners. At that launch, Chief Product Officer Matthias Keller described it as combining conversational planning with the traditional search and filters travelers use to evaluate options.

The latest features deepen that existing product, with live, bookable inventory acting as the check on increasingly flexible AI planning. KAYAK’s reported booking engagement underlines the commercial stakes: turning open-ended travel ideas into decisions while preserving the fare and price checks of conventional search.
