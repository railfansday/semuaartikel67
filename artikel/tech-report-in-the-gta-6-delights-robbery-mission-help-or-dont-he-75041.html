📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

You'll face a choice in Chapter 2 of GTA 6, when you're ready to help Vicky rob her boss, Frankie, at the Delights strip club in Port Gellhorn. Frankie asks you an key question and you've only got a couple seconds to respond:.

"Can you dance? Yes or no, dude?" Frankie has caught you (as Jason) coming in the back door of the club as you and Lucia prepare to rob it. It's ladies night and one of the male strippers is out of commission, but Mario, the lead stripper, gets an idea when he meets Jason. Frankie has the same idea, and asks if Jason knows how to dance.

Yes, they are asking Jason if he can fill in as a male stripper for the show.

Personally, both versions are worth seeing, but Jason playing a stripper, and Lucia's utter delight at seeing him play a stripper, is way more fun.
