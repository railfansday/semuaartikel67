There’s nothing wrong with chicken tendies and fries, but sometimes you want something a little more memorable. Maybe it’s a mountain restaurant with a killer view and food to match, a slopeside spot that makes you want to linger a little longer, or a dinner in town that’s worth getting out of the hot tub for.

According to recent reports, whatever your appetite, some resorts do the food thing better than others. We asked SKI readers to weigh in on the dining scenes at their favorite Western resorts, both on and off the mountain. These 10 came out on top. Bon appétit—and save us a table.

Score: 7.87 Other Strengths: Après, Nightlife Weaknesses: Parking, Value.

In a recent development, “Activities for all. Great shopping, great dining. A real vacation.”.

“Vail is a great mountain, great restaurants, excellent hotels but ridiculously expensive.”.

Score: 7.91 Other Strengths: Lodging, Après Weaknesses: Snow Quality, Safety.

“Nice variety of terrain; cool downtown with lots of lodging and restaurants. Downtown is close to the slopes.”.

“This has an authentic town versus a manufactured resort village. Plenty of restaurants and shopping. The town is very walkable and has an excellent bus system with multiple pick-up/drop-off points at the bases of multiple lifts. Snow can be great in a strong snow season.”.

Score: 7.94 Other Strengths: Parking, Safety Weaknesses: Local Flavor, Challenge.

Recent findings suggest that “One of the best hidden gems of a ski resort in the United States. Few crowds, incredible runs, great staff, and delicious food. One of my favorite places in the United States.”.

“Tamarack did an amazing job with the limited snowfall we had this year. Groomed as much as they could. The staff are consistently friendly and helpful. Mid-Mountain Lodge has excellent food and friendly staff with great seating and views of the mountain.”.

Score: 8.00 Other Strengths: Guest Service, Lodging Weaknesses: Parking, Terrain Variety.

“Beaver Creek, as a whole, delivers the best experience if you are looking for a combination of skiing, dining, and lodging.”.

“There is Beaver Creek, and then there is everywhere else. Excellent mountain for trees and moguls, which many people don’t seem to realize. Rarely crowded. Beautiful setting. Fine dining and lodging options.”.

Score: 8.01 Other Strengths: Après, Local Flavor Weaknesses: Challenge, Grooming.

“Snow was great, lines were short, food was good, town and locals were nice. Varied terrain for the group.”.

“Ski-in/ski-out, with great shopping options and great food options. Nice open-air entertainment après ski.”.

Score: 8.20 Other Strengths: Access, Parking Weaknesses: Lodging, Nightlife.

It has been revealed that “Snowbasin is my home away from home. The people are great, the runs are top notch, the variety is unmatched, and the food is sooo good. Also, the views!”.

“Despite the lack of snow, I had a blast every day I was skiing up there this season. My kids and I had fun, their instructors were amazing, and the food was delicious. I can’t wait for snow to fall again.”.

Score: 8.51 Other Strengths: Après, Nightlife Weaknesses: Parking, Value.

“The food is superior to most all other resorts with fair pricing.”.

Industry observers note that “The Aspen skiing complex is one of the best in North America. On-mountain eating may be pricey, but the food is fantastic and carefully prepared.”.

Score: 8.69 Other Strengths: Parking, Lifts Weaknesses: Challenge, Terrain Variety.

In a recent development, “No lines, close and free parking, very authentic vibe, and friendly staff. Food and restaurants are the best in the world. Even in a low snow year, Sun Valley never held back or gave up—they made snow non-stop and groomed the heck out of it. Love the fact they know it’s all about being the best they can.”.

“Best service. Every staff member is courteous and helpful. Lots of dining options with vegan and vegetarian options that are a must.”.

Score: 8.90 Other Strengths: Lodging, Après Weaknesses: Access, Safety.

In a recent development, “By far the best place I’ve skied in North America. The skiing itself is fantastic, there are side country and backcountry options and plenty of inbound terrain. The village is very nice with lots of lodging and food options. Ski-in/ski-out options are available.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I have been to Whistler many times. You can always count on a reliable base of snow during a down year. They have fantastic lodging and dining. Awesome base village. Lots of runs and grooming.”.

Score: 8.92 Other Strengths: Grooming, Guest Service Weaknesses: Value, Challenge.

Snow making was taken to a new level this year. These guys worked miracles. All in all it could have been a disaster, but it was still a very fun year.”.
