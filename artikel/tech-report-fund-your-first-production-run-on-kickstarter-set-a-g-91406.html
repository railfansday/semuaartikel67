📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that selling preorders on Kickstarter can pay for your first production run without a loan or giving up a share of your business. Backers pay only if you reach your goal, and then you owe each one the reward they pledged for. So set the goal at the full cost of making and shipping every reward, plus fees.

According to recent reports, A campaign that misses its goal leaves you owing nothing, since no backer is charged and Kickstarter takes no fee. The risky campaign is the one that reaches a goal set too low.

Industry observers note that once a campaign is funded, Kickstarter’s terms require you to finish the project and deliver every reward as promised. If the rewards cost more to make and ship than the pledges bring in after fees, you pay the difference. Promise backers a delivery date with room for factory and shipping delays, too.

It has been revealed that add up what delivering every reward costs: your factory’s price for the smallest run it will make, packaging, and shipping to each backer, by country if you ship abroad. Then add Kickstarter’s 5% fee and payment processing, which on a U.S. project is 3% plus 30 cents for each pledge of $10 or more.

Say a board game designer’s printer quotes $14 a copy for 2,000 copies, and shipping averages $10 a backer. She sets a $30,000 goal and raises $41,000 from 900 backers. Fees take about $3,550 and shipping $9,000, together about 30% of what she raised. The $28,450 left pays for all 2,000 copies, with 1,100 to sell after the campaign.

Industry observers note that to reach a goal that covers every reward, line up backers before you launch. Collect email addresses, then send people to your project’s Kickstarter pre-launch page, which emails its followers when you go live. Kickstarter says that, on average, 32% of the people who follow a pre-launch page go on to pledge, against 17% of those who follow after launch.

Kickstarter locks the goal at launch, so get your factory’s and shipper’s quotes in writing before you set it.

Start your project’s pre-launch page on Kickstarter while you collect those quotes.
