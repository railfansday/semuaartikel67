Mauricio Pochettino could consider bringing Weston McKennie and Folarin Balogun to Manchester United with him.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 54-year-old's appointment was confirmed last week, with his first game in charge to come against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

Pochettino was in charge of the United States for two years and guided them to a positive World Cup campaign in the summer, earning himself a new contract that he signed in August.

But the Argentine could not turn down Man United's offer down, and will return to the Premier League with a third team after stints at Tottenham and Chelsea.

His first transfer window will be a key one, with the Red Devils in the relegation zone and in need of major intervention.

McKennie and Balogun have been key parts of Pochettino's US team in the past two years.

The former is a key player for Juventus and has been capped 72 times by his country, appearing on both wings and in the centre of the pitch.

According to recent reports, balogun, on the other hand, declared for the US in 2023 and has scored 13 goals in 32 caps since. The 25-year-old came very close to signing for Everton on transfer deadline day and is expected to leave Monaco.

"I bet he wishes he could take Harry Kane," King told Football Insider when asked what Pochettino could take from his experience with Tottenham.

"Pochettino was obviously involved when Harry broke into the team. Manchester United, like Tottenham, need a centre-forward. There's no chance of Harry Kane going there, that is for sure.

"He's got a couple of options with the American players. He's got the boy McKennie at Juventus. He's got the lad who's at Monaco, Balogun.

It has been revealed that "He didn't win anything at Tottenham. He played decent football, but decent football doesn't wash at Manchester United. They've got to win cups.".

Speaking last week, Pochettino was asked about potentially signing players from his squad with the US.

Sources indicate that "Now is not the time [to talk about this]. All the good players always have dreams to reach the highest level," he noted. "And I think Manchester United is one of the teams that is in the highest level and it's the dream for many of the players.

"But now, I think they need to be focused on today, on tomorrow's game, on their clubs. I know really well for more than two years every single player. And I think they are all players with unbelievable talent.

"Of course, in the future, when that happens, of course we are going to pay attention, you know, to make some decisions and to pay attention to players that we know really well.".
