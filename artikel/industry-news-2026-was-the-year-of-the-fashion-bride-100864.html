If—for a certain set of cool, young designers—doing bridal was once thought uncool, 2026 may have been the year that finally put that notion to rest.

Sources indicate that we’ve been seeing the shift for years now: For many brides, the wedding-day dream has gone beyond the beautiful, white, traditionally “bridal” wedding dress. Increasingly, brides want something, well, cool—an expression of their personal brand and sensibility, whether that means a painstakingly sourced archival look or a custom piece by an unexpected designer. This year, that trend was on full display, as wedding fashion increasingly became a stage and cultural touchpoint for brides and designers alike.

2026 was a banner year for high-profile weddings and celebrity brides wearing everything from romantic gowns to razor-sharp suits: Zendaya and Tom Holland, Dua Lipa and Callum Turner, Dove Cameron and Damiano David, Cristiano Ronaldo and Georgina Rodríguez, Emma Roberts and Cody John, Karl-Anthony Towns and Jordyn Woods, to name just a few.

Industry observers note that and that’s without even mentioning the “royal wedding” of Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce. While the photos are yet to be released (and if you ask Swift bestie Dakota Johnson, they never will be), we do know that Swift became Jonathan Anderson’s first celebrity bride, a move that had people feverishly searching for clues at his Dior Haute Couture show in July. Could it be that white chiffon and degradé lace final gown, which later graced the runway at Vogue World: Milan? Nothing was revealed, apart from Anderson telling Women’s Wear Daily, “It was a joy to work with her. We became very good friends,” and “It’s an emotional thing doing someone’s wedding.”.

Dua Lipa’s August wedding, meanwhile, was a fashion extravaganza, starting with a custom Schiaparelli white skirt suit, designed by creative director Daniel Roseberry. Who can forget the image of the pop star, beaming under a burst of confetti as she walked out of the town hall as a newlywed? Her suit was inspired by Bianca Jagger’s bridal look for her wedding to Mick Jagger in Saint-Tropez on May 12, 1971, perhaps the ultimate cool-girl bridal nod. Lipa rounded out her bridal looks with a white leather Bottega Veneta custom gown by Louise Trotter, and a Chanel haute couture gown for her main wedding dress.

Dua Lipa in Chanel haute couture.

According to recent reports, beyond the celeb wedding world, this year saw rising stars and in-demand designers put forward bridal looks, some for the first time. Dilara Findikoglu debuted her first bridal capsule, a rule-breaking, lingerie-forward offering adapted from her ready-to-wear line, with campaign photos of models smashing plates, smoking, and mounting their grooms. (“I really believe that the power of my clothes is to bring out the uniqueness in someone,” Findikoglu previously told Vogue. “That’s exactly why they became Dilara Brides: to reveal the most unique version of who they already are.”).

Dilara Findikoglu’s debut bridal collection.

Sources indicate that batsheva debuted her first bridal collection at Newly introduced York’s Bridal Fashion Week. Christopher John Rogers solidified his status as a sought-after designer for stylish brides, including Vogue fashion market director Naomi Elizée Blue, TikTok brand strategist Ali Lebow, and film director Pam Nasr. In an interview with The New York Times about his rising profile in the wedding world, Rogers even teased his very own bridal collection.

Naomi Elizée Blue in Christopher John Rogers.

CFDA/Vogue Fashion Funders Zoe Gustavia Anna Whalen and Miss Claire Sullivan created custom bridal looks for Architectural Digest senior design editor Hannah Martin and the artist Tourmaline, respectively. Martin’s gown was inspired by a look from Whalen’s spring 2025 collection, featuring a corseted bodice with a padded hip. Whalen previously told Vogue about why she decided to take on a bridal client, a rare move for her. “It aligns so much with my general ethos of garments being these weighted, emotional objects,” Whalen mentioned. “So when it’s the right fit, being able to create that with such intention for somebody else is so beautiful.”.

Tourmaline (right) in Miss Claire Sullivan.

According to recent reports, elsewhere, Raul Lopez dressed his friends Juice Rodriguez and Cristal Veras in coordinated Luar for their Fresh York wedding. Gabbriette became the first bride for Matières Fécales, the cult Paris label whose show Rihanna attended in September. And Colleen Allen, winner of the 2026 CFDA Emerging Designer of the Year Award, has quietly become the fashion girls’ dream wedding dress designer, after making a custom look for Charli xcx’s wedding reception last year.

According to recent reports, “Bridal” no longer means just one thing. For independent designers, it can be a playground to express something newly introduced, a platform for their brand, and, as Joe Bobowicz observed in a Vogue Business article, a dependable revenue stream. And as people expand their ideas of what a wedding can look like—donning colorful gowns in every color from butter yellow to pink to black, and playing with material and silhouette—as we head into 2027, the future of bridal style has never been more thrillingly uncertain.
