Sources indicate that according to a Foundation for Individual Rights and Expression survey, 9% of Americans have full confidence that the Democratic Party will protect their free speech rights, and a statistically indistinguishable 10% have full confidence that the Republican Party will do so. Assuming no overlap between the two groups, that means 80% of Americans do not have full confidence in either party on this, which of course is the right answer.

I've got to say that I'm skeptical about the people who said "quite a lot of confidence" to each (17% as to the Democratic Party, 12% as to the Republican Party), but at least they don't have full confidence, so there's that.
