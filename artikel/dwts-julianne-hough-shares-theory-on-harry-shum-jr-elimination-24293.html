In a recent development, julianne Hough has a theory about why Harry Shum Jr. took his final bow.

The Dancing With the Stars cohost reacted to the actor's shocking elimination from season 35 after he and pro partner Jenna Johnson earned perfect 10s from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli on both of their dances during the Nov. 17 semifinals.

"Sometimes when people are doing so well in the competition, people don't necessarily vote," Julianne told the duo, "because they think you're doing so well with scores.".

And the pair did do well, earning 30 out of 30 points on both their paso doble set to Rihanna's "We Found Love" as well as their quickstep to Amy Winehouse's "Valerie." The combined 60 points from the judges put them at the top of the leader board alongside Amber Glenn and partner Pasha Pashkov, who also earned a perfect 60.

But Harry seemed to have his own ideas about what may have messed up his mojo on the dancefloor, as the Glee star thanked Jenna for helping him navigate drama throughout the season.

"I want to say you have been dealing with everything with grace, with the noise outside," he told her, "and teaching me the things that I have only dreamed of to learn.".

The 44-year-old added, "This has been one of the most beautiful experiences. So, thank you so much.".

Amid that noise—like when Carrie Ann critiqued an illegal lift in their routine, which led to Jenna's husband Val Chmerkovskiy calling out Carrie Ann on social media and getting suspended for a week—Harry has certainly swept the judges off their feet.

And that was evident after their routine to "Valerie," which was reminiscent of one of his scenes on Glee.

It has been revealed that "I'm speechless," Carrie Ann told them after the performance. "I'm blown away because you really have learned how to lead the dance... The technique was absolutely brilliant.".

And for Derek, he fully believed Harry would be leaping all the way to the Nov. 24 finals.

"That is how you quick step into the finals, right there," he stated. "There was something about you tonight, too. There was like a focus. There was like an eye on the prize.".

Instead, the finalists who will get in the groove next week will be Amber and Pasha; Ezra Frech and his pro partner Daniella Karagach; Maura Higgins and her partner Mark Ballas; Tatyana Ali and her partner Jan Ravnik and Connor Wood and his partner Rylee Arnold.
