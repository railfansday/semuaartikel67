Tech Report: Sci-fi RPG Exodus gets even more Mass Effect-like with its female lead, who they are calling Femjun

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We've said previously that, for better or worse, the third-person sci-fi RPG Exodus looks a lot like Mass Effect. It's a characterization that developer Archetype Entertainment isn't shying away from—in fact, it seems to be actively embracing it with the reveal of the game's female protagonist, who they refer to as Femjun.

I kid you not, this is not a name I've cooked up or run across on social media. It's right there on the Exodus website: "Meet Femjun! You’ve been waiting to see FemJun, and today we’re happy to say she’s finally here.".

Femjun is a very direct reference to Femshep, the female version of Commander Shepard in BioWare's Mass Effect trilogy. While the male version was effectively the default playable character through the first two games, BioWare took steps to put them on equal footing for Mass Effect 3. That effort included a vote to let fans decide the character's official face, which was subsequently used on alternate or inside covers of Mass Effect 3 packaging.

Voiced by Jennifer Hale, Femshep became so popular that when BioWare dropped the Mass Effect Legendary Edition trailer, it was she who appeared most prominently.

So, back to Exodus: Fenjum will be portrayed by Carina Conti, who's appeared in television shows including Tracker, Good Trouble, All Rise, and Winning Time: The Rise of the Lakers Dynasty. Exodus will be only her second videogame credit, though, following Mafia: The Old Country, in which she portrayed Isabella Torrisi. The male version of Jun will be played by veteran voice actor Stephen Oyoung.

Narrative director Drew Karpyshyn also confirmed that romances in Exodus won't be locked out based on whether you play male or female Jun: "We want to make sure that players can engage in what romances they want if you pursue them.".

Exodus is set to launch on April 7, 2027, and will be available for PC on Steam and the Epic Games Store.
