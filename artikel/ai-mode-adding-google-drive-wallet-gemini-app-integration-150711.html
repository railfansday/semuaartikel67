AI Mode adding Google Drive, Wallet, & Gemini app integration

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Personal Intelligence in AI Mode is expanding today with Google Drive, Wallet, and even Gemini app integrations.

It has been revealed that before today’s announcement, Personal Intelligence in AI Mode supported Google Photos and Workspace (Gmail and Calendar). This is used to customize responses based on your data. The newly introduced Google Drive support lets AI Mode take into account your Docs, Sheets, and PDFs.

According to recent reports, following the Gemini app, Google Wallet integration is coming to AI Mode. On the safety front, AI Mode can only read information from your financial accounts. It cannot “move, manage, or invest your money.”.

It has been revealed that …you can map out your financial milestones and identify actionable ways to trim everyday spending. You can also maximize credit card benefits — like optimizing cash-back categories, travel perks, and reward points — based on your cards linked via Wallet.

The last integration today is the most interesting. You will be able to “securely connect your Gemini app to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode.”.

…if you explore an idea in the Gemini app, AI Mode will have that foundation to build on, and vice versa.

This builds on the existing ability to have the Gemini app factor your Search history into answers. With this enhancement, AI Mode can access “your Gemini chats, what you’ve shared with Gemini Apps (like photos, files, audio or video attachments, and screens you ask about), and associated insights and locations.” The option to make the connection is available today, but the feature will go live in the coming weeks.

You can control Personal Intelligence in AI Mode from this page.

Google will soon show a “Personalization” icon below tailored responses that lets you “view the context AI Mode uses.”.

The final patch today will make AI Overviews and AI Mode responses “more personally relevant.”.

So if you’ve been researching and planning for a trip, you can pick up right where you left off in a fresh search thread without starting over or repeating yourself. As always, you can adjust or turn off personalization at any time in your Search Settings.
