TL;DR: Pokémon has confirmed that the Mega Evolution Flowing Fists expansion will drop in February 2027, debuting Z Mega Evolution cards.

According to recent reports, can the trading card market just calm down for a few months? Can Pokémon stop pumping out collection after collection, and can collectors stop buying up stock like jackals? No? OK, then.

We're still very much in the midst of Pokémon’s 30th-anniversary and Delta Reign mania, but your next obsession is already on the way.

In a recent development, the next Pokémon TCG expansion — Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Flowing Fists — officially launches at participating retailers worldwide on Feb. 6, 2027. The Pokémon Manufacturer has also confirmed that all subsequent Pokémon TCG expansions will launch on Saturdays, rather than the traditional Friday drops.

Following their debut in Pokémon Legends: Z-A’s Mega Dimension DLC, Mega Lucario Z, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Absol Z, and Mega Glimmora headline the new expansion as heavy-hitting Mega Evolution Pokémon ex cards.

Noteworthy cards from this expansion include:.

17 ultra rare Pokémon, Trainer cards and Special Energy cards.

Six special illustration rare Pokémon and Supporter cards.

If you don't want to wait for retail shelves on Feb. 6, local independent game stores hosting Play! Pokémon Prerelease tournaments get early Build & Battle boxes starting Jan. 23, while Pokémon TCG Live unlocks digital packs and login bonuses two days early on Feb. 4. To be clear, we're talking about playing with the fresh cards, not shopping.

In a recent development, we don't need to tell you how fiercely scalped recent Pokémon TCG drops have been. With heavy hitters like Mega Lucario Z and Mega Garchomp Z anchoring the set, expect Elite Trainer Boxes to sell out within minutes once preorders quietly surface over the next few weeks. If you need Pokémon cards under the tree next month, stick to in-stock sets already discounted for Black Friday.
