📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Chapter 5 of Toby Fox’s newest indie game opus, Deltarune, debuted back in June of this year. It’s kind of interesting to think about; when that chapter launched, it completely dominated the gaming sphere for a good month, even if it didn’t quite hit with everyone. Now that GTA 6 is out and completely taken over the scene, it’s gone largely quiet outside of its specific fanbase. Still, perhaps that’s for the best. A little less attention means that Fox and his team will have some extra breathing room as they work on the next stretch of the game, which is set to encompass both Chapter 6 and Chapter 7.

Based on some little hints that Fox has previously dropped, Chapter 6 will be when the game starts reaching its narrative crux, bringing an end to “wacky silly adventures” in the face of all the mysteries that have been building up, and there are a lot of them. It’s still going to be a while before we actually get to see any of this, mind you, but according to Fox, development is going pretty swimmingly.

Industry observers note that in the newest edition of the Deltarune newsletter for Fall 2026, Fox confirmed that Chapter 6 is “structurally complete,” in that you can technically play it from start to finish. The next orders of business will be to hammer out the nitty-gritty details, as well as get things rolling in earnest on Chapter 7.

Industry observers note that as Fox explained in the newsletter, Chapter 6 is a “structurally complete draft that can be played from the beginning to the end. All the maps, events, and battles exist,” though he parenthetically noted that there’s a particular optional event that’s causing the overall development to drag out a bit.

“By the time you read this, we should have started the localization and some preliminary testing. Much of the game looks complete… but, some important scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation, so it's going to take a bit longer before we can really move to full-on testing,” Fox said.

Recent findings suggest that he admitted that, while Chapter 6 will be shorter in length than the previous chapters, it’s going to be a pivotal one, and he wants it to hit as hard as it possibly can. In short, this is not a process that can be rushed; it’ll only be ready once he’s satisfied with it.

By the time you read this, we should have started the localization and some preliminary testing.

Recent findings suggest that on the bright side, thanks to a fan vote, Fox opted to share a brief sneak peek of Chapter 6 in the form of a quote unquote “stupid scene” involving Queen meeting with Pink in Castle Town and being… her Queenly self, let’s say. Hey, it made me laugh.

The newsletter also shared an upgrade on Chapter 7, though as Fox rather succinctly puts it, “Well, we've only just started…”.

While development on Chapter 7 has only just begun, Fox feels as though he’s on the right track with his team and their work. Apparently, the way they’re handling development is by having Fox read out the events and dialogue of the chapter to the team, then stopping just as the story reaches a critical point. The only way for the team to find out what happens next is for more of the game to be completed. A bit of an unusual approach, but apparently, “Everyone agrees it's more fun this way.”.

Sources indicate that with so much of Deltarune already under the team’s belt, Fox is feeling optimistic as they all get started in earnest on Chapter 7. “Unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence...!”.

Fox concluded the newsletter with a reminder that, as development on the game goes on, the dev team keeps getting bigger, which is why more time is needed to ensure everything comes out right.

“I know it's probably annoying to not have any updates, but no news is good news.”.
