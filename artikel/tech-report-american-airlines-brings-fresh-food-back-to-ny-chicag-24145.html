Tech Report: American Airlines brings fresh food back to NY-Chicago first class for first time since 2019

American Airlines noted Wednesday it's bringing small, fresh "bites" to first class on flights between its hubs in Fresh York and Chicago, the carrier's latest effort to win over higher-spending customers.

Recent findings suggest that the changes start this month. Customers will be able to pick between two options, which include prosciutto and melon or apple cinnamon chia pudding for breakfast and a Thai noodle salad or mezze plate for lunch and dinner, American stated Wednesday.

American last offered fresh food for first class on these routes in 2019, a spokeswoman mentioned. Currently, customers in first class on these flights get a smaller, packaged snack. Airlines largely have limited food options for shorter flights, even in first class.

Industry observers note that american has fallen behind Delta Air Lines and United Airlines in profits and investments in premium options. The airline has been working to step up those premium offerings in recent years with latest cabins, lounges and plans for seatback screens on its narrow-body fleets.
