According to recent reports, I don't want to write about video games any more, I want to deliver milk in the countryside and hang out with a cow and quirky people! Sorry, what, there's a game all about that called Milki Delivery that came out today? Alright, I'll write about video games again, especially when they look as charming as this one does.

In a recent development, the stakes at play in Milki Delivery are very low (as are the steaks, no butchery to be found here, only one very sweet looking cow I couldn't possibly slaughter). You play as Neha, and she's made a promise to her pet cow, the titular Milki, that you'll find her a friend (I think this is a world where there aren't any cows left; yikes!). To do that, all you have to do is deliver her milk! Accept because this is an adventure game, you'll have to help out a few folks along the way.

In a recent development, it's a game very much about the journey, as when riding about this lush and bright countryside, you might find a group of cats that just need a little taste of that sweet creamy juice you've got. Or maybe you have some rope that a farmer needs to wrassle up some sheep. Sometimes you'll have to get a bit more involved with the various people you meet, but predominantly, you'll be delivering milk to folks who need it.

According to recent reports, getting around the place is quite simple too, you plot out your path ahead of time and let the game take you as it's all mouse driven. A relaxed experience to be sure, but the game's art is just so delightful to look at, I'm quite enticed by the idea of just slowing down and enjoying some pretty pictures and making friends along the way. You can make different kinds of milk, too, just be feeding Milki different ingredients. Not how that works in real life, but now I kind of wish it was.

In any case, Milki Delivery is out now, and you can pick it up on Steam.
