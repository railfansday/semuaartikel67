📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The 60th CMA Awards delivered their largest TV audience of the decade.

Recent findings suggest that the Nov. 18 awards averaged 7.62 million viewers on ABC and Disney+, the best same-day total for the awards since 2019 (when it brought in more than 11 million viewers). Wednesday’s show finished just ahead of the 7.57 million viewers for the 2022 awards — with the caveat that Nielsen has made several changes to the way it measures ratings in the years since then.

The CMA Awards drew a 1.01 rating among adults 18-49 (equivalent to about 1.37 million people in the demographic), the first time the show has cleared a 1.0 rating since 2022. The telecast grew by 26 percent in viewers and 55 percent in adults 18-49 from a year ago (6.04 million viewers, 0.65 rating in the 18-49 demo). Recent findings suggest that ella Langley set a record with six wins at the awards, including entertainer of the year. Her long-running No. 1 hit “Choosin’ Texas” won CMA Awards for song, single and music video of the year.

Along with Langley’s big night, the CMA Awards featured a performance by Taylor Swift — her first at the ceremony since 2013 — and a tribute to Dolly Parton that featured Vince Gill and host Lainey Wilson performing a duet of “I Will Always Love You.” Several other artists paid tribute to Parton during the show and on the red carpet beforehand.

Sources indicate that ABC’s annual Countdown to the CMAs special on Tuesday also earned multi-year ratings highs. Hosted by Robin Roberts, the show drew just under 3 million viewers, its best showing since 2023.
