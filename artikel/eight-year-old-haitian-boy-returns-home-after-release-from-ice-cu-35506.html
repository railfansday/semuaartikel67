Child reunited with family after he was detained by immigration agents in San Diego and transferred to Texas.

An eight-year-old Haitian boy who was put into restraints and detained by immigration agents in San Diego on 4 November and transferred to a facility in Texas has been reunited with his family in California.

Representative Juan Vargas shared a photograph of the boy and his family in a social media post on Monda, writing: “11 days ago, an 8-year-old in San Diego was taken into federal custody during an immigration check-in. He was separated from his loved ones and held in a facility in Texas. Today, he’s finally home.”.

The Haitian Bridge Alliance, an immigration advocacy group, confirmed that the third-grader had been released from Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) custody.

“An eight-year-old child belongs in a classroom, on a playground, and surrounded by love – not in immigration detention. We welcome his release, but his freedom should never have required a public outcry,” Guerline Jozef, executive director of the Haitian Bridge Alliance, said in a statement.

The third-grader at Fay elementary school was put in restraints and taken into custody by federal immigration officers at a check-in earlier in the month, according to video obtained by NBC News and an oversight letter Democratic lawmakers sent to the Departments of Homeland Security and Health and Human Services.

A spokesperson for the homeland security department said ICE took the child into custody “out of an abundance of caution and to ensure the child’s safety”.

The uncle, the spokesperson said in a statement to the Guardian, was unable to prove legal guardianship – and that officials were obliged by federal anti-trafficking laws to take the boy into care as an “unaccompanied alien child”.

The Haitian Bridge Alliance mentioned the boy was in the care of his uncle and cousins after his father was deported last year.

According to recent reports, “I’m so grateful that he’s home, but this should have never happened in the first place,” Vargas said in a second social media post, adding that he would be asking the Trump administration for answers. While he was detained, the child was “held in a facility in Texas, far away from his home in San Diego”, Vargas said.

Celebrating the boy’s return, Jozef of the Haitian Bridge Alliance told NBC News: “The aunt was literally crying, and she stated: ‘I have not eaten since the Wednesday they took my baby.’ They haven’t been able to sleep because of that.” She added: “The cousins were there when they took him, and wondering: will they ever see him again? But today [Monday] they are able to see him again. They are able to hug him.”.
