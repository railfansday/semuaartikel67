When a player enters the last year of their contract, it leaves their club with a massive question: cash in while you can?

The risk of losing a top player for free can strike fear into even the biggest clubs. Thus, once a player’s contract ticks over into its last year, they have to act fast to decide if they’ll be offering a renewal or selling before it’s too late.

There are some high-profile players in that kind of predicament this year and it could lead to some blockbuster moves in 2027.

We’ve picked out an XI of players in the final year of their contracts, arranged conveniently in a 4-2-3-1 formation.

The best goalkeeper in the world? Those of a Real Madrid persuasion would certainly argue so.

Courtois is now 34 and Madrid don’t really do long-term deals for players of his age. But he remains their regular starter and has been named in La Liga’s team of the season for the past two years running, despite his club winning nothing of note in either of them.

We’re pretty sure that as long as he’s at his peak, Courtois will be a Madrid player.

A newly crowned World Cup winner, even though he only played six minutes of football beyond the group stage, Llorente is into the last year of his contract with Atletico Madrid, who he joined from cross-city rivals Real in 2019.

Very much a part of the furniture at the Metropolitano now, the midfielder-turned-right-back emphasised in March: “I want to retire at Atletico. I want to stay longer at the club and hope to reach an agreement for a renewal.”.

Could it soon be time for another modern-day Liverpool legend to leave the club?

After Andy Robertson and Mohamed Salah this year, Van Dijk’s long-term future will surely come into question over the next few months.

According to recent reports, now 35, Van Dijk is past his prime but remains a key player for Liverpool. You can picture him retiring there, but some might have thought that about Robertson and Salah.

If it wasn’t for injury concerns, Martinez’s future at Manchester United might be secure by now.

But those physical problems will have to be factored in for any decision about the 28-year-old’s future.

And he hasn’t exactly helped himself with comments against the club and some poor performances of his own. Might be best for everyone if he moves on.

Captain of RB Leipzig, Raum has spent his whole career in Germany but it could be time to broaden his horizons.

Now 28, Raum has been linked with a few Premier League clubs over the past couple of transfer windows.

There was a time when Calhanoglu was a classy attacking midfielder with an eye for a spectacular free kick.

Industry observers note that in open play, he operates deeper these days. He is in his thirties now, after all. But he remains a key player for Inter and scored three goals in his first four league games this season.

This is now Ayari’s third season as a first-team regular with Brighton and he might be coming into form just at the right time – either for a contract extension or a significant move. Classic of the genre.

Ayari has three goals from his first nine games in the Premier League this season and looks well on course for the best campaign of his career.

Industry observers note that “I’m at Brighton and I’m happy where I am,” he’s insisted. “Things are going well for me right now. Then we’ll see what’s to come.”.

Real Madrid acted swiftly to renew another winger whose deal was due to be up in 2027, Vinicius Junior.

According to recent reports, brahim is still waiting for more clarity on his own future, though. He’s only started once in La Liga this season.

Fernandes committed his future to Manchester United last summer in the face of interest from Saudi Arabia and they were glad of it as he went on to break the record for the most assists in a Premier League season.

The 32-year-old still has huge ambitions for what he wants to achieve with United, so you’d suspect he still has unfinished business at Old Trafford.

But will their union last forever? It could be on shaky ground at the moment.

Mitoma used to dazzle in the Premier League with Brighton, before going off the boil a bit.

He’d still be a smart pickup for someone on a free, though, if the Seagulls can’t extend his stay. He’s been out all season with a hamstring injury, mind you, and any potential suitors will have to be wary.

Sources indicate that we probably should have put Jean-Philippe Mateta in here, but can’t look past Ronaldo. Come on, you can’t blame us.

Recent findings suggest that he’s retired from international duty with Portugal, but still has a few months of commitment left with Al-Nassr. Will there be any more after that?

According to recent reports, now in his forties, Ronaldo is in the endgame of his career. But someone would surely be tempted to sign him if he was to leave Al-Nassr and put retirement on hold.
