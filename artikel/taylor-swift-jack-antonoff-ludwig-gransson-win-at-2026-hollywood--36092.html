Taylor Swift & Jack Antonoff, Ludwig Göransson Win at 2026 Hollywood Music in Media Awards: Full Winners List

🌐 Source: Billboard • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 15 min read (3009 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Taylor Swift and Jack Antonoff and Ludwig Göransson began their long march to widely predicted Oscar glory on Wednesday, Nov.
  • “I Knew It, I Knew You,’’ which Swift and Antonoff wrote for Toy Story 5, won song – animated film.
  • It has been revealed that göransson’s score for The Odyssey won score – feature film.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Taylor Swift and Jack Antonoff and Ludwig Göransson began their long march to widely predicted Oscar glory on Wednesday, Nov. 18 when the 17th annual Hollywood Music in Media Awards (HMMA) were presented at the Avalon in Hollywood, Calif.

“I Knew It, I Knew You,’’ which Swift and Antonoff wrote for Toy Story 5, won song – animated film. Swift accepted her first HMMA in a prerecorded video shown during the ceremony. (Swift was in Nashville on the same night, set to perform on the CMA Awards, where “I Knew It, I Knew You” was nominated for single of the year.).

It has been revealed that göransson’s score for The Odyssey won score – feature film. Last year, Göransson won two HMMAs for his work on Sinners and went on to win both the Oscar and Golden Globe for best original score.This wasGöransson’s eighth HMMA; his second in this category.

It has been revealed that legendary songwriter Diane Warren won her third HMMA for “One Right Here” from Maserati: The Brothers. She performed the song during the show joined by Tia Simon and Joshua Wentz. “One Right Here” was recorded by Andrea Bocelli and Jennifer Hudson for the upcoming film.

Lana Del Rey and David Arnold won song – video game (console & PC) for “First Light” from 007 First Light, which they co-wrote and Del Rey performed.

The Rise of the Red Hot Chili Peppers: Our Brother, Hillel, directed by Ben Feldman, won music documentary – special program.

Linda Perry performed her song “What Lies With You” from Linda Perry: Let It Die Here and won for song – documentary film. The Decemberists frontman Colin Meloy performed his nominated song “The Better Part of Bravery” from Wildwood, and Brother Verses Brother stars Ethan Gold and Ari Gold performed their nominated song “Brothers Keep Goin’ Anyway” from the film.

In a recent development, the Isley Brothers, joined by David Kushner and Pink Beard, opened the ceremony with a performance of Warren’s song “By Any Means” from the film of the same name. The video for “By Any Means,” directed by Fran Strine, won the HMMA for music video.

Michael star Jaafar Jackson won his first HMMA for song – onscreen performance (film) for “Bad.” Daveed Diggs won song – onscreen performance – TV show/limited series for “Raise Him Up” from The Boys, which he co-wrote with composer Christopher Lennertz.

La Bola Negra composer Raül Refree won for both score – independent film and song – independent film for “La Nieve.” The song was co-written by Guitarricadelafuente, Raül Refree, and Lucas Duran, and performed by Guitarricadelafuente.

It has been revealed that behemoth! won music-themed film, biopic or musical, and its director Tony Gilroy was honored for his commitment to the art of film music in Los Angeles.

The award for score – short film [live action]) went to The Seventh Turn composer Michael Wandmacher, who transformed Evanescence frontwoman Amy Lee‘s voice into a full multi-instrumental film score.

Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced received two HMMAs. Composers Brian Tyler and Stephen Lukach won score – video game (console & PC), and Ubisoft Music won soundtrack album.
