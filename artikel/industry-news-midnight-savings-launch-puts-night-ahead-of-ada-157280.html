Monument Bank’s Midnight rollout links banking activity more directly to NIGHT than ADA, but its £250M bid reflects initial capacity.

The £250 million figure attached to Monument Bank’s Midnight rollout is an initial capacity limit, not confirmed customer deposits, according to a video from crypto commentator FireHustle.

That distinction sits at the heart of the host’s analysis: a bank adopting technology built by Cardano’s developers does not necessarily translate into immediate demand for ADA.

The video describes a savings product that keeps customers’ money in pounds while recording their accounts on Midnight, the privacy-focused blockchain developed by Input Output.

Its potential significance lies less in the headline amount than in which token benefits from the activity.

It has been revealed that according to Fire Hustle, Monument’s offering pays existing customers a fixed 5.2% for one year. Account details remain private, and customers cannot sell or transfer the blockchain records, even to another Monument customer.

Cardano’s Founder Charles Hoskinson celebrated the rollout with the phrase “250 million pounds on-chain.” But the host says Monument has not disclosed actual deposits, despite tokens representing the full initial allocation already having been created.

The YouTube video also cites Midnight Foundation president Fahmi Syed saying seven other banks are considering similar products: six in Canada and one in Japan.

Sources indicate that canada merits attention because Calgary-based digital-asset custodian Balance handles Monument’s tokens, according to the video. The host explicitly cautions against assuming the unnamed institutions are Canada’s six largest banks.

Midnight’s token structure is the investment question. The video claims holding NIGHT generates DUST, the resource used to pay network transaction fees. Banks or service providers can therefore cover customers’ activity without requiring savers to handle tokens themselves.

Recent findings suggest that “Somebody still has to hold the Night that makes the Dust,” she says. That creates a more direct connection between Midnight usage and NIGHT than between that usage and ADA, although additional activity does not guarantee token-price gains.

The ADA connection is more distant. Cardano stake-pool operators are expected to help secure Midnight in early 2027, with potential rewards extending to their ADA delegators. Those rewards are prospective, not existing income.

In a recent development, fire Hustle reports ADA trading more than 90% below its peak at recording. However, the more useful indicators are actual Monument deposits, transaction volumes and confirmed bank introduces.
