A Bojangles on the Las Vegas Strip has been accused of charging Formula 1 fans $100 to sit inside for just 10 minutes during the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend, with a manager allegedly threatening to call police on those who didn’t pay.

The fried chicken restaurant sits on the LINQ Promenade directly beside the Strip circuit, giving anyone inside a front-row view of the cars racing past.

The claims were shared on X by a user known as Jedi Rich, whose video of the restaurant has been viewed more than 427,000 times.

“So we’re down here at the Bojangles and they’re charging $100 for 10 minutes to sit here because they have probably one of the best places to watch the race on the track,” they said in the clip.

$100 to sit at BOJANGLES for TEN MINUTES—and the manager threatened to call Metro unless people paid?! 🤯That’s $10 a minute at a chicken joint, with a police threat thrown in.The F1 view is incredible, but this is ridiculous. Is that even legal? #LasVegasGP #Vegas pic.twitter.com/yIJYz7Utvd.

In a follow-up post sharing footage from their livestream, Jedi Rich said security told people to either buy something or pay for seating, and claimed a manager yelled at a customer, “After 10 minutes, I’ll go get Metro.”.

Here’s the moment from our livestream. 👀Security says buy something or pay for seating. As I open the door, the manager yells at a customer:“After 10 minutes, I’ll go get Metro.”The stream was choppy here because bandwidth was limited. pic.twitter.com/gY2K3OG6jf.

That works out at $10 a minute for a seat at a fast-food restaurant, leaving many racing fans and viral accounts questioning whether the charge was even legal.

It remains unclear whether the fee was approved by Bojangles’ corporate headquarters, and the chain has yet to comment on the video.
