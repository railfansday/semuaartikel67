Good evening and welcome to live coverage of tonight’s Carabao Cup third-round tie between Chelsea and Leeds United. This is a rematch of last season’s FA Cup semi-final which Chelsea edged by a single Enzo Fernández goal, but plenty has changed since then. Xabi Alonso has taken over the reins at Stamford Bridge and made a promising start, reeling off three wins before losing to Arsenal in an absorbing contest last Sunday. Chelsea are not involved in European competition this season, so the domestic cups represent an avenue to silverware, as well as an opportunity to tinker.

Industry observers note that in Alonso’s way, though, stands a Leeds team whose manager, Daniel Farke, told the media on Tuesday that his side are aiming to win the Carabao Cup. Farke has overseen an impressive run of form, having lost only two of their previous 16 competitive matches in a stretch that extends back to March. Two wins at Nottingham Forest preceded draws against Brentford and Brighton to keep Leeds undefeated this term. Farke has suggested he will rotate in west London to “integrate new players” having brought in seven new recruits during the summer.

In fact, Chelsea have lost at this stage of the competition just once in the past 15 years, and that was against Pep Guardiola’s Manchester City four seasons ago.
