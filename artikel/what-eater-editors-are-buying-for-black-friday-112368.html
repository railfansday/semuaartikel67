We’re a far cry from the Black Friday traditions of yore. Instead of storming the gates of department stores at dawn for Salad Shooters and Cabbage Patch dolls, we now sit on our own couches to peruse the deals online — and there are lots of deals. Too many deals! But, not enough that are worthy to qualify as truly worthwhile Black Friday promotions. (Ten or 15 percent? Please.) For the most part, however, the digital age of Black Friday deal-hunting is about machete-chopping your way through the roughage of “meh” promotions.

There are exceptions: Black Friday is usually when brands who rarely run promotions release limited-time deals, and it’s the smartest time of year to buy countertop appliances and electronics. Our online-shopping experts have done the hunting for you.

Below, we’ve rounded up the best Black Friday kitchen and food deals that have already gone live, and which are actually worth your time. There are appliances you’ll use daily, dinnerware beloved by the Eater staff’s favorite restaurants, and GOATed French knives. Find the caffeinated beverage of your choice, and let’s shop.

Sources indicate that this is the moment to invest anything you’ve ever pined over from heritage cookware brands, including the substantial kahuna: Le Creuset. Le Creuset’s Dutch ovens and baking dishes are going to be on escalating promotions across various retailers, so snag the colorways you love most while they’re still available.

Industry observers note that oft-imitated but never equaled, KitchenAid’s stand mixers last decades and look damn good on a counter, with their streamlined mid-century design and array of shades. Black Friday is the time to make the splurge, as they’re on sale across Crate & Barrel, Williams Sonoma, Amazon, and other retailers. This porcelain-white 5-quart option is a classic iteration that will look good in every kitchen, and it’s currently 30 percent off.

In a recent development, renowned for their intricate craftsmanship and ergonomic, lightweight design, Laguiole knives have been made in the same eponymous French village for hundreds of years. The discounts have already started rolling out; there’s a set of six wood-handled knives on sale at Wayfair, a carving set priced down at Overstock that’ll come in handy for serving meat at future holidays, and a cream-colored set on sale at Mark and Graham that comes with the option of custom engraving.

Los Angeles-based lifestyle brand Flamingo Estate is a major fave among Eater editors, ogled for luxe items like its beloved heirloom-tomato-scented candle and Hachiya persimmon vinegar. It’s having a rare sitewide sale this week, with 20 percent off everything, including the new Substantial Pasta Dinner set and Affogato set with vanilla olive oil and espresso salt. Truly a one-stop-shop for fancy food gifts.

I’m not alone: The New Jersey-based ceramic studio has an impressive roster of restaurants that use its dinnerware, including Gramercy Tavern, Lilia, and restaurants at cool hotels like the Ojai Valley Inn; that’s probably because they’re durable, come in unique colorways, and dishwasher- and microwave-safe. These gems don’t go on sale often, so add them to your cart while you can.
