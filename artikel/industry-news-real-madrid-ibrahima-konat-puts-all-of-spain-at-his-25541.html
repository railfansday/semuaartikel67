It has been revealed that A few months ago, Ibrahima Konaté was going through one of the most complicated periods of his career. Regularly singled out for his defensive errors and his lack of consistency at Liverpool, the French international ended his adventure with the Reds under fire from criticism. His arrival at Real Madrid had also raised some questions, as his latest performances in England had left doubts about his ability to regain his best level. And during his first matches in Spain, he was sometimes singled out. But for several weeks, the 27-year-old defender seems to have definitively left this period behind him. Installed in José Mourinho’s eleven, Konaté made a series of starts and gradually established himself as one of the strong men of Madrid’s defense. A rise in power which was further confirmed this weekend, during Real Madrid’s victory on the Valencia pitch (0-3).

Against the Valencians, the former Leipzig player delivered a particularly impressive performance, leaving almost no room for maneuver for the opposing attackers. Solid in duels, imperial in the air and clean in his restarts, Konaté perfectly embodied the newfound solidity of the Merengues. A performance praised by the Spanish press, starting with Marcawho gave it a score of 9. “ The Frenchman’s level of play has been constantly improving for several matches. He resists in duels and raises cleanly », Underlines the Madrid daily. Same enthusiasm on the side of Goal Españawho also gave it a 9: “ a physical presence that Valencia’s attackers simply could not overcome. Konaté dominated the air and demonstrated excellent reading of the game, contributing to his team’s five interceptions. He remained unfazed under pressure and prevented the home side from creating any clear chances in the game. ».

In a recent development, and this rebirth also bears the mark of José Mourinho. Since his arrival on the Madrid bench, the Portuguese technician has demanded total investment from his players, both in matches and in training. A philosophy to which Konaté immediately adhered. Very involved in the daily work at Valdebebas, the Frenchman has gradually gained the trust of his coach, who does not hesitate to entrust him with the keys to his defense. His association with Dean Huijsen is also starting to work wonders, the two defenders displaying a particularly interesting complementarity. “ Exceptional. Impassable on the surface and in depth. Playing alongside Huijsen allows him to perfect the art of progressive passing. They complement each other wonderfully and enhance each other. », enthuses A.S.seduced by the Frenchman’s performance.

According to recent reports, A real revenge for the man who seemed to have lost part of his confidence during his last months at Liverpool. From now on, Konaté finds this aggressiveness in duels and this serenity which were his strength. In a team that is performing well and with a teammate who is also performing well, Konaté seems to have found the recipe to seduce José Mourinho. While Real Madrid are showing great momentum, the French international appears today as one of the big winners of the Portuguese coach’s method. And after going through difficult times, the Frenchman can therefore enjoy his return to form. To the delight of Madrid supporters.
