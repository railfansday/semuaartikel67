AI May Be Scanning Your Private Signal Chats

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you use Signal, it's likely you care at least a little bit about keeping your communication private so only you and those you're chatting with can see your message threads. Signal's end-to-end encryption keeps prying eyes out, but if you use agentic AI on your device, your chats may not be as locked down as you think.

In a recent blog post, Signal president Meredith Whitaker outlined the potential threat AI assistants like Siri and Gemini pose to the platform's promise of privacy. Systems-level capabilities like Gemini Intelligence (on Android) and Siri AI's Onscreen Awareness (on iOS and macOS) scan your screen and may be able to capture data from Signal conversations in the process, which can then be used to perform tasks. Not only does this potentially expose your information, but that of other conversation participants as well. Whitaker notes that this leaves privacy protections outside of Signal's control because the platform cannot easily restrict AI access to the app.

According to recent reports, signal has already blocked screenshot capabilities for Microsoft's Recall AI, which by default was able to take and store screen grabs of Signal messages on Windows 11. While Signal cannot guarantee that AI won't have access to your data, there are a few protections now available for those on iOS and Android as well.

The main workaround in Signal for now is to restrict screenshots and screen recordings inside the app, though this does not prevent AI agents from potentially grabbing content from other parts of your device. According to Signal's support documents, these protections will now be enabled by default on iOS 27 and Android with the latest versions of the Signal app.

Sources indicate that people on iOS 27 can confirm or manually disable screenshot protection via Settings > Privacy > Block Siri AI. The compromise with this setting is that it also blocks you from taking screenshots of Signal conversations in the app and App Switcher, though you can disable it temporarily for 10 minutes if needed. On Android, Signal automatically engages the "Screen security" setting when it detects an AI agent attempting to access the screen. This, too, blocks screenshots inside the app and can be disabled via Settings > Privacy > App security > Screen security. Signal cannot block screenshots on macOS 27. However, users can add Signal to their Excluded Apps list via System Settings > Siri, which at least limits proactive access to conversations by Siri AI.
