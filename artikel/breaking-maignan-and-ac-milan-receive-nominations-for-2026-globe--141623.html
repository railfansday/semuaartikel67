The 2026 Globe Soccer Awards will be held on December 28 and AC Milan will be among the nominations.

Sources indicate that the Globe Soccer Awards – nicknamed the ‘Dubai d’Or’ – are an annual event held in the United Arab Emirates, recognising players, coaches, clubs and industry executives. The upcoming 17th Edition is scheduled for 28 December.

In a recent development, it was founded in 2010 by Tommaso Bendoni, Riccardo Silva (a Milan investor) and football agent Jorge Mendes. The ceremony is organised in partnership with the Dubai Sports Council and backed by bodies like UEFA, the European Club Association (ECA), and the UAE Pro League.

The AC Milan captain Mike Maignan is back to his best form with club and country, and he is one of ten candidates for the award of ‘Best Goalkeeper of the Year’.

The French goalkeeper will face Gianluigi Donnarumma, Thibaut Courtouis, David Raya, Diogo Costa, Joan Garcia, Gregor Kobel, Emiliano Martinez, Matvey Safonov and Unai Simon.

A post shared by Globe Soccer (@globesoccer).

Meanwhile, Milan as a club have been nominated for the Fans’ Historic Club Award. The other nominees are Real Madrid, Al Ahly, Barcelona, ​​Celtic, Inter, Juventus, Bayern Munich, Liverpool, Manchester United, Benfica, Boca Juniors, River Plate, Penarol and Flamengo.

Fans will be able to vote until November 30th for 12 of the 14 categories, while in the second phase the public votes will be combined with those of the jury. You can cast your votes for Milan and Magic Mike here.
