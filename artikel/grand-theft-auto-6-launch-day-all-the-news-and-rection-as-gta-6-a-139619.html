GTA 6 is Metacritic's highest rated game ever.

More ridiculous GTA 6 launch piggybacking and this time it's an email with a therapist talking about how the game might help teens with their dating outlooks.

Says Natalie Buchwald, therapist and founder of Manhattan Mental Health Counseling:.

"Psychologists sometimes talk about 'relationship scripts': the unspoken expectations we absorb about how relationships work, what partners owe each other and what success looks like. We pick these up long before we have much experience of our own, from family, friends, online spaces and the stories we see in entertainment.

It has been revealed that "No single film, game or influencer shapes someone's values on its own. What matters more is the overall mix of messages a person encounters, and whether they have the space to question them. That's particularly relevant now that dating advice is so widely available online, where some content frames relationships around status, control or getting something in return, rather than mutual respect.

According to recent reports, "That's why the most useful thing parents can do isn't to hand over a set of rules. It's to help young people think critically about the scripts they're picking up. Being curious rather than critical, and asking what they've seen, what they think of it and what they believe makes a good partner, tends to keep conversations open.

According to recent reports, "Entertainment can play a helpful role here. A story built around a partnership, like Jason and Lucia's, can give parents a natural, low pressure starting point for conversations that might otherwise feel awkward.".

Industry observers note that chris referenced some of this in his review, praising Jason's tender and caring side, and his not being a toxic asshole. GTA 6 is also the first game to not only feature a couple but a female playable lead, having very much revolved around male fantasies in the past.
