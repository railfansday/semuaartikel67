As some of the entertainment industry’s biggest names, these women know what it means to make a statement on and off the red carpet. Naturally, tonight was no exception—and they weren’t the only ones who set the tone for the occasion.

Recent findings suggest that this year’s honorees were joined on the red carpet by their equally stylish peers, from Karen Pittman in a metallic hooded dress to Louisa Jacobson, who donned a fitted white jacket with trousers from Ralph Lauren.
