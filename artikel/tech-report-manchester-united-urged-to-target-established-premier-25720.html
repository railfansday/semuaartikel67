Former Manchester United winger Ashley Young has revealed he would replace Bruno Fernandes with Nottingham Forest midfielder Morgan Gibbs-White.

Fernandes's contract expires at the end of this season, though United can extend their captain’s deal by an additional year and also prevent him from speaking to foreign clubs in January about a potential free transfer.

United want Fernandes to stay, but the 32-year-old has already turned down two fresh contract proposals as he weighs up his options following more managerial turnover at Old Trafford.

Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of his first training session on Wednesday after leaving the US men’s national team to replace Michael Carrick as United head coach.

Fernandes enjoyed a superb campaign last term, breaking the single-season Premier League assist record to help United qualify for the Champions League.

This season, however, he has struggled to replicate the same form, and he drew criticism after calling into question Carrick’s tactics following United’s 4-1 Champions League defeat to Como in October.

While Young admits there is no doubt about Fernandes’ contribution to United, with the Portuguese midfielder producing 75 goals and 74 assists in 239 Premier League appearances, he insists that in order for the Red Devils to move on, they should look to replace the former Sporting man with England international Gibbs-White.

Sources indicate that “Bruno has been fantastic for Manchester United. He has been, and no one can say that he hasn’t,” Young tells Standard Sport.

In a recent development, “There's obviously been a few things that have come up of late. If a club like Manchester United are going to build a team around him, he's going to be 33 next year.

Industry observers note that “If it was me, I would be looking at going to get another number 10 and build it around him. I'd go and get Morgan Gibbs-White.

Recent findings suggest that “I think he's got 18 months left on his contract. He's established in the Premier League, he’s a leader, he's showing his qualities at Nottingham Forest. And I'm sure Nottingham Forest won't thank me for saying that.”.

He's established in the Premier League, he’s a leader, he's showing his qualities at Nottingham Forest.

Ashley Young on Morgan Gibbs-White.

No midfielder scored more than Gibbs-White’s 15 Premier League goals last season, with the 26-year-old helping drag Forest clear of the relegation zone.

Despite his performances, Gibbs-White has continually been overlooked for England selection and missed out on a place in Thomas Tuchel’s World Cup squad this summer.

Young believes a move to United would therefore appeal to Gibbs-White and potentially put him on Tuchel’s radar ahead of the 2028 European Championships.

“I'm sure when he's been away with England as well, you go, and players talk. They'll be talking about the different titles they're going for, the different cups and trophies,” Young continued.

“As a player, you want to play for the biggest clubs in the world. For me, Manchester United is one of those clubs.

“It's going to be down to, obviously, Mauricio Pochettino what he wants to do with Bruno Fernandes. But if you want someone who's properly in their prime, I think Gibbs-White turns 27 next year. I would go after him.”.

Young was speaking ahead of the launch of the Panini Official EFL Sticker Collection and said his first memories of collecting the stickers came as a kid at school.

“I always remarked that, you know, when you used to open packs and get a shiny (sticker), it was the best feeling ever. It was like Christmas come all over again.

“First memories of swapping at school as well. Kids at break time were like, ‘have you got this, have you got that?’ Trying to get the best ones.”.
