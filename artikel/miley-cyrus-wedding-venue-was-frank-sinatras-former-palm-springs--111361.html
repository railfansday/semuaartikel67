After a stunning ceremony in Palm Springs, CA, Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are officially husband and wife—and part of the history of an iconic midcentury modern estate that formerly belonged to Frank Sinatra.

The musicians said “I do” on Nov. 21, surrounded by family, friends, and a bounty of stunning flowers. The picturesque celebration took place in the backyard of Twin Palms, right on the edge of the piano-shaped pool the “Fly Me to the Moon” singer once lounged by, according to Elle Decor.

Commissioned by Sinatra in 1947, the 4,500-square-foot home is located in the Movie Colony, a legendary Palm Springs neighborhood that Hollywood entertainers flocked to in the 1930s and 1940s.

The official Twin Palms website explains that Sinatra came to the desert town in hopes of finding a weekend retreat for his family after signing a movie contract with MGM Studios. He was initially looking for a Georgian-style estate with a lavish brick facade, but architect E. Stewart Williams persuaded him to go for something more “desert appropriate.”.

The website also claims that the $150,000 commission was a hassle, with Sinatra giving the team just a few months to design and build the property so it would be ready in time for a Christmas party. The abbreviated timeline required contractors to work around the clock and made the process much more expensive.

Sources indicate that the six years the famed crooner resided in the home were action-packed. He initially moved in with his first wife, Nancy Barbato, and when their relationship ended in 1948, it would become the backdrop for his highly publicized relationship with second wife, Ava Gardner.

According to recent reports, today, Twin Palms is available as a short-term rental for a variety of needs. It can be used as a vacation home or private event venue, and can also accommodate commercial uses such as photo shoots and filming. While exact pricing varies for vacation rentals, it’s typically about $4,400 a night.

According to recent reports, cyrus and Morando are far from the first couple to tie the knot at Twin Palms, with the website including a gallery of photos from previous weddings. The property can host up to 150 guests and includes a variety of spaces that can be used throughout the evening.

While the “Black Mirror” star opted to seat guests around the pool, the impressive great room, circular motor court, and additional lawn space opposite the pool are also available.

It has been revealed that with four bedrooms and seven bathrooms, the estate comfortably sleeps eight guests. Each of the bedrooms boasts an en suite bathroom.

The primary suite has its own wing—a feature that was particularly appealing to Sinatra, who valued privacy.

In true midcentury modern fashion, the home features massive sliding glass doors that integrate indoor and outdoor living, connecting the great room to the sprawling patio and uniquely shaped swimming pool.

A detached pool house includes a kitchenette, bar, and cabana-style bathrooms.

Recent findings suggest that in recent years, the home has been totally restored, using vintage designer furnishings to maintain the original retro character. Twin Palms still has the original sound and recording system, as well as Sinatra’s original recording studio—although, per the website, it is no longer functional.

In a recent development, the kitchen has been updated with modern Viking appliances and St. Charles modern cabinetry to call back to the original design.

In addition to memorabilia celebrating the singer around the home, there’s a chip in a bathroom sink which Sinatra had apparently damaged with a Champagne bottle.

Recent findings suggest that sinatra reportedly sold the home in 1953, after his split from Gardner. The home was designated a Palm Springs Class 1 Historic Site in 2011, by the Palm Springs Preservation Foundation.
