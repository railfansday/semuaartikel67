Stephen A. Smith and Kevin Frazier have long appeared on camera to reported on sports and entertainment. Now the duo gets to manage such programming from behind the scenes.

Recent findings suggest that smith and Fraizer, longtime friends who last year came together to expand Smith’s production outlet Straight Shooter Media, are teaming up with Skydance’s BET to telecast six HBCU basketball games in primetime, starting in December. Nick Cannon’s Wild N’ Out will provide comedy and entertainment during halftime. According to recent reports, the goal, says Frazier, is to “represent Black college basketball in a different light,” placing emphasis on many elements that don’t often get screentime. The duo wants to call attention to the culture around the events, the bands, dance teams, cheerleaders and school spirit. “You will see what it’s like to be inside the arena,” Frazier vows, when HBCUs play a game.

It has been revealed that both men have more at stake than just producing a game. Smith attended Winston-Salem State University, where he played basketball, while Frazier grew up watching his father coach at Morgan State University. “Everything that I have is partly because of Black college basketball,” indicates Frazier, who has long worked as a co-anchor at “Entertainment Tonight.”.

Smith remains best known for the opinionated sports oratory he delivers most days on ESPN, but under a deal he struck last year with ESPN’s corporate owner, Walt Disney Co., he is free to pursue outside projects, and to identify potential concepts and content ideas, with Disney getting a first-look window of around 30 days. He and Frazier have known each other since earlier stints at Fox Sports.

Recent findings suggest that smith believes HBCUs haven’t always had the resources of other universities to put their sports under a national spotlight. These telecasts, he claims, will change that. And he hopes to see Straight Shooter Media keep developing the telecasts over a longer period of time.

According to recent reports, the opening matchup, Grambling State vs. Howard University, kicks off Thursday, December 3 at 8:00 p.m. eastern. Subsequent games will air on BET on Thursday evenings and include teams from Moorehouse, Norfolk State and Loyola University Chicago.
