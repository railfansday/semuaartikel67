📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

West Ham United are not pursuing a transfer for River Plate goalkeeper Santiago Beltrán, according to a well-known football agent speaking to Claret & Hugh this afternoon. The Argentine has been linked with a move to the London Stadium for several weeks as the Hammers look to resolve their goalkeeping problems.

Industry observers note that earlier today, Claret & Hugh reported that River Plate could be reluctant to part with Beltrán until the summer. However, it would appear that West Ham’s January recruitment plans lie elsewhere.

When we put the latest transfer reports to a well-known football agent this afternoon, the response was emphatic: “You can forget about Beltrán going to West Ham. They’re looking for a keeper, I’ve seen four or five mentioned and he wasn’t on the list.”.

Beltran will not be heading to West Ham in January.

Perhaps the most encouraging aspect of our conversation this afternoon is that the Hammers are looking at several goalkeepers ahead of the January transfer window.

With Alphonse Areola struggling with fitness and Scott Parker alternating between Mads Hermansen and Fin Herrick, it’s a position that needs addressing sooner rather than later.

Whilst our source didn’t identify the other goalkeepers being considered, it would appear that Beltrán is not presently among them.
