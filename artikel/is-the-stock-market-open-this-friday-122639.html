Is The Stock Market Open This Friday?

🌐 Source: Coinpedia • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (324 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

US stock markets will be open on Friday, November 27, but only for a shortened session. The New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq will close at 1 pm Eastern time, or 11:30 pm IST, on Black Friday. Both exchanges will be fully closed on Thursday, November 26, for Thanksgiving. The US bond market follows a similar pattern, shutting on Thanksgiving and closing early at 2 pm Eastern on Friday, according to the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association. After this week, normal trading resumes until late December. Markets will close early at 1 pm Eastern on Thursday, December 24, for Christmas Eve, and stay shut on Friday, December 25. Stock and bond markets will also be closed on Friday, January 1, for New Year’s Day, with bond markets closing early at 2 pm Eastern on December 31. Crypto markets, including Bitcoin and XRP, keep trading around the clock throughout the holidays.
