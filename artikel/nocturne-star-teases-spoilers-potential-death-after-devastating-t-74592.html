Chrissy Metz is speaking out on the latest twist on Nocturne that is hinting at a major death on the show.

During the Friday, November 20, episode of the Apple TV show, Jonah (Liev Schreiber) came to the realization that Jurek’s (Stephen Graham) kidnappings weren’t about the people who were taken. Instead, it was about hurting the people closest to them, who previously died by suicide as a result of the grief.

At the same time, Ida (Metz) — who was reunited with her son after his abduction — tried to decode a message Michael (Landon Valentin) was trying to give her from the person who took him. A chilling game of hangman led to Ida realizing the mystery word was her name — as the ominous hangman hinted that Ida would need to take her own life in order to get her daughter back as well.

Recent findings suggest that “That’s what’s so petrifying about Jurek is that he doesn’t do the killing. The people do it to themselves,” Metz, 46, explained about the psychological warfare during an exclusive interview with Us. “It’s a lot.”.

Metz called the reveal “beyond chilling and scary and sad and just devastating.”.

“Nothing is really stronger than a mother’s love and I think that at that point she’s prepared to do whatever he asks of her and to keep her children safe,” she mentioned about Us‘ concern for Ida moving forward. “I think you got that right.”.

In a recent development, nocturne follows a former homicide detective who relocates to a small Pennsylvania town, which comes under attack by an imprisoned serial killer. At the center of the case is Ida’s family, who is reeling from the return of her son — but also her daughter’s ongoing disappearance.

While Metz told Us she would love another season, she remained more tight-lipped about whether Ida could actually return, adding, “It was wonderful to work with everybody and they’re obviously so talented and it was really wonderful to watch a master class of actors. Should you be worried? Yes, I think you should be a little worried.”.

She continued: “There’s some things that will transpire that I think will be shocking and I know for me it’s something that I’ve never done in any role before, which was concerning for Stephen [Graham] and Liev, who were like, ‘Are you sure?’ And I’m like, ‘This is not real.'”.

Metz hinted that the story lines evolved.

“They were sort of campaigning for us to do something different but it is shocking. It’s scary,” she told Us. “It is very intense. Something very different.”.

While reflecting on the role, Metz also discussed getting the chance to depict such a rich story.

“I always say that women aren’t depicted on screen enough. The stories are just not told enough. I’m so glad that they made her a woman [since she is a man in the book] and a mother. She is a regular woman just trying to survive as a single mom and I know that story very well because my mom was a single mom and raised children on her own,” she told Us. “I know what it’s like to sort of live paycheck to paycheck, and hope that you have enough for bills and to put food on the table. So I gravitate toward those those women and those characters and the stories because they don’t get told enough. I think people need to feel seen and heard in any way and that’s what’s brilliant about art and TV and film.”.

Nocturne airs upcoming episodes Fridays on Apple TV.
