According to recent reports, this property comes partially furnished with a generous selection of items left behind by the previous tenant. You’re welcome to make use of everything they’ve left behind.

*Basement access is not recommended.

If you hear any strange noises from the basement, it’s best to ignore them. There are many rooms down there, and most people never come back up.

Your personal storage box is located upstairs and is available throughout your tenancy.

Industry observers note that should you discover anything of value in the basement, you may return it to storage for future use. Please note that basement access points are fragile and may not remain available after use. Management is not responsible for any items abandoned in the basement.

Industry observers note that previous tenants thoughtfully left behind many objects in the basement, including knives, wands, amulets, rings, swords, shields, armors and other useful possessions. We encourage you to make good use of them if needed.

Please respect the residents’ personal space whenever possible.
