According to recent reports, west Ham’s Argentinian striker Taty Castellanos has been the subject of multiple transfer offers, Claret & Hugh can reveal today. Speaking exclusively to a club insider this lunchtime, we have learnt the details of one such offer from Everton, which has been rejected.

As Claret & Hugh revealed earlier this month, the Hammers have been actively looking to offload Castellanos, with the club even being described as “desperate to get rid of him”.

Speaking this lunchtime, our source informed us: “There’ve been a few offers for Taty but they’ve all been loans. The biggest was from Everton who wanted to take him on loan with no fee with an option for £15 million.”.

Taty wants to leave but who can realistically afford him?

David Moyes was known to have an interest in Taty and made a transfer deadline day offer in the summer, which was rejected by the club. At the time, West Ham were unable to source a suitable replacement for the Argentine.

Suffice to say, this season has not worked out as everyone would have hoped, and it’s now looking increasingly like a missed opportunity that West Ham didn’t accept the Toffees’ offer in the summer.

When Claret & Hugh asked our club source whether they expected Everton to come back with an improved offer, we received no reply.

However, speaking to a football agent within the last few minutes, we were told that it was unlikely Everton would return with another bid.
