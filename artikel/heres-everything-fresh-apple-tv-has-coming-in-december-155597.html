Recent findings suggest that apple TV (now $6.99/month for Black Friday) is about to bring back a beloved comedy from Ted Lasso and Shrinking’s creator, plus a newly introduced holiday special, documentary event series, and more. Here’s everything new coming to Apple TV in December.

It has been revealed that “Bad Monkey” hails from Ted Lasso and Shrinking creator Bill Lawrence. After Andrew Yancy (Vince Vaughn) witnesses the murder of Florida’s finest fixer, an ambitious state attorney pairs him up with a strait-laced forensic accountant to solve the crime. Eager to regain his detective’s badge, Yancy finds himself embroiled in a turf war between rival criminal organizations as he navigates a variety of Florida oddballs, including an erudite mob boss, his unhinged son and a hard-edged young woman who needs Yancy’s protection. Meanwhile, Yancy contends with his aging father; unresolved romantic tension with Dr. Rosa Campesino (Natalie Martinez), who debuts her own investigation into a mysterious rash; and a bombastic animal relocation specialist whose business threatens the local environment.

Recent findings suggest that in this Fraggle Rock holiday special, Wembley is named Spirit Captain and sets out to spread cheer throughout the Rock. But when the Fraggles unknowingly take a cherished snow globe from Doc’s workshop, the holiday spirit begins to fade. Wembley must find the courage to return it, learning that kindness, giving and helping someone in need are the true gifts of the holidays.

Recent findings suggest that “TIMBERLAKE” is an expansive portrait of the life and career of multi-hyphenate artist and icon Justin Timberlake. Raw, reflective and revealing, Timberlake sits down to discuss the triumphs that made him a global success, his personal perspective beyond the headlines and the challenges he’s faced behind the scenes. Directed by Academy Award winner Fisher Stevens (“The Cove,” “Beckham”), the series spans three decades of music and popular culture and features never-before-seen archival footage and exclusive interviews with family, friends and collaborators.
