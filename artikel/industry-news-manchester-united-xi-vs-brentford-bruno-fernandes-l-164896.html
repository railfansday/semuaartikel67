Bruno Fernandes is a doubt for Manchester United as Mauricio Pochettino prepares to welcome Brentford to Old Trafford on Saturday.

The club captain was unable to train on Friday morning due to a virus, which has thrown into doubt his involvement in Pochettino’s first home match in charge.

Pochettino is still figuring out how best to set up his fresh team, and this is a potentially huge spanner to throw into the works.

Fernandes’s absence could see Matheus Cunha move into the No10 role, supported by Amad Diallo and Patrick Dorgu, with Bryan Mbeumo leading the line.

Industry observers note that elsewhere, the new manager also has a decision to make in defence. Having opted for a back three in the 4-0 drubbing by Liverpool at Anfield last weekend, Pochettino switched to a four-man defence for the chaotic 2-2 Champions League draw with Sporting on Wednesday, in which Fernandes scored both goals.

Although Brentford will fancy their chances of victory based on the respective league positions of the two clubs - Man Utd 19th, the Bees in sixth - United will be urged by their fans to attack. As such, a more timid back three seems unlikely.

Industry observers note that it will be a case of balancing fitness levels with only two days of rest between games. Goalkeeper Senne Lammens has come under fire in recent weeks for his performances, but back-up Karl Darlow has struggled with injury since arriving in the summer.

Sources indicate that pochettino’s defensive options are fairly limited. Leny Yoro is available after his early red card against Liverpool was overturned, while Harry Maguire was restored to the starting XI for the Sporting game.

Brentford’s pace in behind may necessitate a quicker backline, meaning Maguire could make way again here, having also reportedly complained of an injury after the midweek match.

Lisandro Martinez has been at left-back, but Dorgu’s attacking qualities will likely be required on Saturday and Luke Shaw is also now back in training. By moving Martinez back to centre-half alongside Yoro, Pochettino can find the pace required. Diogo Dalot may get the nod ahead of Noussair Mazraoui at right-back.

Pochettino has quickly settled on a midfield partnership of Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans, who was controversially sent off late on against Sporting but is available in the Premier League.

Should Fernandes be passed fit, the attack continues to pick itself - if not their exact positions.

Fernandes was deployed on the left behind Cunha in the defeat by Liverpool, while Mbeumo was briefly used as the lone forward under Michael Carrick.

Here, we’re predicting Cunha to lead the line after his midweek rest due to suspension, with Fernandes in behind as the No10 and Mbeumo on the left wing.

If Amad is fit enough to start after being forced off in the draw with Sporting, expect the Ivorian on the right flank. If not, youngster Shea Lacey could be a candidate to start.

Recent findings suggest that marcus Rashford and Benjamin Sesko remain injured, as do midfielders Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte. Pochettino revealed that Baleba would be out for 10 to 12 weeks from when he sustained his hamstring problem against Roma in early November.

Predicted Man Utd XI vs Brentford (4-2-3-1): Lammens; Dalot, Yoro, Martinez, Dorgu; Mainoo, Tielemans; Amad, Fernandes, Mbeumo; Cunha.

Injured: Rashford, Baleba, Ugarte, Sesko.

Time and date: 3pm GMT on Saturday November 28, 2026.
