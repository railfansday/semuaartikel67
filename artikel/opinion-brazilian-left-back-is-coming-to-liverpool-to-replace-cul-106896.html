Liverpool could be about to sign an exciting left-back.

Andoni Iraola is getting the Reds back to exactly where they want to be. Liverpool's 4-0 demolition of a wilted Manchester United side was nothing to scoff at.

Sure, Man United have been ravaged by dark times, and yes, they went down to ten men within the opening ten minutes of the game.

But this was still an astonishing and overbearing performance from Liverpool that highlighted the positive tendencies of Iraola's team.

Recent findings suggest that there will be tougher challenges on the way. But right now the objective is to continue to claw up the league table and seize the opportunity that has been presented to them.

Ultimately, Liverpool will want to be competing for the Premier League title, even if that seems unfeasible right now. Nothing should be written off at this stage.

Liverpool's left-back looks certain to leaveIt's been a period of change at Anfield. Liverpool have a new coach, new sporting director and even new minority owners in 1892 Holdings. The player turnover has been huge as well. We've said goodbye to so many legends in the last 12 to 18 months, and more are expected to depart in the coming months ahead. One player who Liverpool are ready to allow to leave is Kostas Tsimikas. The Greek international has become a cult hero at Anfield. However, it's clear that his time is up. He will be turning 31-years-old by the end of the season and renewing his contract just doesn't make any sense for the Reds. They need to be moving on and giving Milos Kerkez a genuine competitor for the left-back spot.
