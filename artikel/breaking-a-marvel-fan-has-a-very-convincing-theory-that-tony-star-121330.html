A Marvel fan has a theory that it's Tony Stark who's the variant of Victor Von Doom in the MCU – and not the other way around.

Recent findings suggest that robert Downey Jr. is returning as Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday, and, so far, we don't know why the actor is playing both roles.

One fan, though, has an idea. They speculate that, when Tony goes back in time to meet Howard Stark in Avengers: Endgame, there's a clue that the timeline for Maria Stark's pregnancy doesn't add up. That suggests Howard and Maria might have adopted a baby instead – possibly a baby from Latveria?

According to recent reports, "This is why Doctor Doom has Tony's face," the theory concludes. "In the multiverse, some worlds never adopted Victor. He stayed in Latveria and became the God-King we'll see in Avengers: Doomsday. Robert Downey Jr. returning makes sense now. Everything we loved about Iron Man was just a mask for the monster inside." While this is a compelling idea, there's nothing in Endgame that explicitly stops the timeline from making sense, especially since Howard actually suggests Maria's pregnancy is very far along. Still, it is an interesting possibility – and considering Downey Jr. is playing both roles, there has to be some kind of connection between the characters.

"People wondered, 'Is Doom just a variant of another character? Is this just a shortcut to bringing back another character?' The answer is no. He's Victor Von Doom," Kevin Feige has previously teased.

Sources indicate that we'll find out more when Avengers: Doomsday arrives on December 18. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the upcoming Marvel movies and TV reveals, or see our breakdown of the final trailer.
