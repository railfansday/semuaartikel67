Apple's Black Friday deals are here, and I'm on the hunt for the best AirPods Black Friday discounts to help you save. I test headphones all year round, so I have a really good sense of what's worth your money.

Industry observers note that the discounts so far aren't huge, but there's some sales worth snagging now. For example, the AirPods Pro 3 are $179, down from $249. And the AirPods Max 2 are $419, down from $549. That's close to the all-time price low.

Here are the best AirPods Black Friday deals so far.

The EarPods are Apple's cheapest earbuds. Right now, the Lightning version is on sale, which is great for pre-iPhone 15 owners. However, newer iPhone owners will need to pay the full price of $19 for the USB-C version.

The AirPods Pro 3 are Apple's flagship earbuds and some of my favorite earbuds in general. The sound is exquisite, and the ANC is almost Bose-beating. If you want the best AirPods available, it's gotta be the AirPods Pro 3.

The AirPods Max 2 are the only over-ears made by Apple, so if you want headphones, it has to be the Max 2. I reviewed these headphones, and the near-perfect sound and impressive ANC completely blew me away.

I'd love to see some discounts on the fresh AirPods 5 (both with and without wireless charging case). I reviewed the new entry-level earbuds upon their September release, and they're a huge improvement over 2024's AirPods 4. Let's hope Apple treats us to some AirPods 5 discounts closer to the significant day.
