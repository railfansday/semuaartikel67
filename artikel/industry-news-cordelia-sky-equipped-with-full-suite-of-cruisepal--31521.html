cruisePAL has deployed its integrated suite of maritime technology solutions aboard the Cordelia Sky.

Formerly sailing as the Empress of the Seas, the 2,000-passenger ship debuted for Cordelia in late October, operating itineraries from Mumbai.

The deployment includes cruisePAL‘s Shipboard Management System (SMS), Point of Sale (POS), Safety, eMustering, IssuTrax Hotel Maintenance & Guest Engagement, as well as additional operational solutions that support shipboard operations, onboard revenue generation, safety compliance and an enhanced guest experience.

“Bringing together multiple shipboard systems through an integrated technology environment requires more than software. It requires deep maritime expertise, careful planning, rigorous execution, and close collaboration with the customer,” said Jimmy Lopez, CEO, cruisePAL.

“We are incredibly proud of our partnership with Cordelia Cruises. Successfully launching our full suite across a 2,000-guest vessel like Cordelia Sky proves that complex, fleet-wide digitalization can be executed smoothly.”.

The deployment involved the implementation, configuration and integration of multiple operational systems as part of the vessel’s preparation for service, noted cruisePAL.

For Jurgen Bailom, president & CEO, Cordelia Cruises, it was key to implement technology solutions that would support both operational teams and guests from day one as the manufacturer prepared the Cordelia Sky to join the fleet.

The ship joined the Cordelia Empress, which started its season from Kochi, marking the first time the firm is operating two ships from two Indian homeports simultaneously.

The Indian fleet is set to grow to three next year, as the Cordelia Sun is set to join the two ships in November 2027.
