Harlem Globetrotters Team With Sprite in Rivalry Tour Sponsorship

The Harlem Globetrotters and Sprite have expanded their 2026 partnership to make the beverage brand the global presenting partner for the Harlem Globetrotters: Rivalry Tour.

Recent findings suggest that the deal grows a relationship that began earlier this year with one of the Globetrotters’ original brand partners. The Coca-Cola Tech giant, which owns Sprite, has been involved with the team for nearly 75 years.

Clothing brand Starter also joins the sponsor lineup as official outfitting partner for the Harlem Globetrotters as well as their three rivalry teams for uniforms and apparel.

Sprite’s standing as global presenting partner will include on-site and in-game integrations, Sprite challenges for prizes, a new social series that will live on the Harlem Globetrotters’ owned platforms, local in-market activations with distributors, and on-court branding.

According to recent reports, starter’s involvement will include outfitting two new rival teams that will play the Globetrotters in 2027, adding The Sweet Lous and The Curlys alongside longtime foil The Washington Generals. Starter has created new uniforms for all four teams.

“Expanding our relationship with Sprite provides us an incredible platform to celebrate basketball culture, enrich a deeper connection with fans, and provide fresh energy to an experience that continues to to inspire audiences around the world,” remarked Keith Dawkins, president of Harlem Globetrotters and Herschend Entertainment Studios, in a statement.

“Expanding our relationship with the Harlem Globetrotters gives us an incredible platform to celebrate basketball culture, connect with fans, and bring fresh energy to an experience that continues to inspire audiences around the world, as it does with our other basketball partnerships,” remarked Terika Fasakin, senior director brand director for Sprite.
