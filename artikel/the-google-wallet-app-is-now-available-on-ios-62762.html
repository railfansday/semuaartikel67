Google Wallet is now available on iOS to “organize purchases, tickets, boarding passes, and more in one easy place” in the US.

The Google Wallet app on iPhone does not offer tap-to-pay functionality due to platform restrictions. Instead, Google touts three intended use cases:.

Sources indicate that app navigation is handled by a floating pill (in Liquid Glass) with two tabs. The homepage starts with a carousel of recent purchases slated for delivery, followed by boarding passes, and finally a grid of loyalty cards.

The next tab highlights a list of recent transactions associated with Google. The Wallet app will display purchase emails and summarize them. You also get a list of Passes, which you can pin/unpin from the homepage, and payment methods saved to your Google Account.

The ‘plus’ FAB (floating action button) lets you add loyalty, gift, and payment cards (for use in Chrome). There’s a convenient “Everything else” camera scanner that can be used to digitize anything else.

The other main reason Google Wallet now exists on iOS is that the company wants the app to be a “secure hub for managing agentic purchases across Google.”.
