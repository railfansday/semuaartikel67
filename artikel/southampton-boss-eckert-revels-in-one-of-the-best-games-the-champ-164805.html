Tonda Eckert described his Southampton side's victory over Middlesbrough as "one of the best games the Championship could produce".

It has been revealed that the Saints won 2-0 at St. Mary's Stadium after second-half goals from Ryan Manning and Finn Azaz, with the latter running the full length of the pitch to celebrate in front of the away end against his former side.

Much of the build-up to the match was focused on the 'Spygate' scandal from the end of last season, but Southampton managed to put that to one side on Friday to move within four points of Championship leaders Boro.

Boro had their chances, accumulating 1.4 expected goals (xG) from 19 shots to Southampton's 1.12 from 12 attempts, but Kim Hellberg's side went scoreless again on the road.

They have failed to score in three consecutive Championship away games for the first time since January 2015, having 49 shots without success.

The Saints, meanwhile, extended their unbeaten run at home to 19 games in the league (W15 D4), and Eckert was delighted with the result.

"I think [that was] one of the best games the Championship could produce," Eckert told Sky Sports.
