One of the most exciting names in the Premier League a couple of seasons ago was Archie Gray.

When the Englishman moved from Leeds to Tottenham, many thought he was nailed on to become a full England international, but the 20-year-old's career has stalled.

Tottenham's recent struggles may have contributed to Gray's slow progress, and a fresh challenge could reignite the wonderkid's career.

According to Sky Sports' Lyall Thomas, Brentford are monitoring the 20-year-old's situation ahead of the January window after showing interest in the summer.

Gray has played regularly for Spurs this season but only played one minute in Saturday's victory over Ipswich after being dropped to the bench.

Roberto De Zerbi's side are still some way off where they want to be, and cashing in on Gray this winter could be a tempting offer for them.

The Reds hold a historic interest in the midfielder, who has played mostly as a right-back this season, and have shown a clear pathway to first-team football for several other young talents who have made Merseyside moves in recent years.

If Gray does become available this winter Julian Ward should definitely take note, and the 20-year-old could be given the chance to relaunch his career in L4.
