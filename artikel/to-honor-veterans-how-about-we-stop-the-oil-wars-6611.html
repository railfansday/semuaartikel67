To Honor Veterans, How About We Stop The Oil Wars?

Sources indicate that today is Veterans Day in the United States. Naturally, across the country, people have spent at least part of the day thinking about veterans they know as well as veterans they don’t and thanking them for their service. Whether you agree with the various wars the United States has gotten into or not, you have to honor and appreciate the human beings who offered to put their lives on the line in order to support their country (or think that they’re doing so, at least). I know several people who are veterans, and they typically sacrificed a lot in their previous military roles.

It has been revealed that however, I do have to come back to the rationale for the wars we as a country get into. I’m 44 years old, and it seems like we’ve been involved in wars related to oil and gas for more than half of my life. It’s just a continual process. We get into a war in the Middle East connected to XYZ, but it’s really about oil. After years of over-involvement, destruction, and death, society realizes it’s been a super counterproductive, idiotic war and we pull out. The, after a few years, we begin again and the story repeats.
