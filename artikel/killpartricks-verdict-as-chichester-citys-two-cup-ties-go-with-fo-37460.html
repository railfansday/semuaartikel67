City were always going to be second favourites visiting Salisbury in the second round of the FA Trophy last Saturday.

The Wiltshire side are flying high in National League South and are reaping the rewards of Kuwaiti investment which saw them travel there for warm-weather training last month.

And there was no ‘cup-set’ on the day with Salisbury running out 4-0 winners.

On Tuesday nighr the boot was on the other foot as City were the team looking to avoid a shock when they went to SCFL Premier side Hassocks in round three of the Sussex Transport Senior Cup.

A professional and clinical display at the Beacon ended with Chi winning 6-0 to reach the last eight of a competition that ends with an Amex Stadium final.

Two goals apiece by Emmett Dunn, Isaac Bello and top scorer Harry Bridle secured the quarter-final spot.

It was the last of a relentless run of midweek matches that have stretched City’s small squad – and the coaching staff are looking forward to a couple of game-free Tuesdays when they can work on rest, recovery and other challenges.

Killpartrick noted: “We have no complaints about the outcome at Salisbury. They’re an extremely good side, very fit and played the conditions better than us.

"We kept battling and highlighted fighting spirit, which is all you can ask sometimes.

According to recent reports, "You have to take into account the difference between the two clubs. They’ve been training in Kuwait, we’ve been training at Oaklands Park – and I’m not knocking Oaklands Park, by the way!

Sources indicate that "At Hassocks we were very, very professional. We had to be and we were because that could have been a tricky tie.

"We brought in three from our B team and another youngster and were pleased with the performance and professionalism of the whole squad.".

Meanwhile after a run of seven away games in ten matches, seventh-placed City are back at Oaklands Park this Saturday to take on 14th-placed Basingstoke in a Southern League Premier South game.

After their last home match, versus Bath City, drew more than 600, they would love another major crowd in – on the day when the city centre stages its lights switch-on just after the end of the match.

Killpartrick said: “We’ve had some really entertaining home games and will aim for another.”.
