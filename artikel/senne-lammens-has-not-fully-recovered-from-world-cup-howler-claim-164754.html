Manchester United legend Peter Schmeichel fears Senne Lammens has yet to recover from the blunder he made during Belgium's World Cup campaign last summer.

Lammens made two costly mistakes in United’s 2-2 Champions League draw with Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday night, conceding a needless penalty and dropping a clanger in the dying minutes to allow Luis Suarez to score twice for the home side.

The 24-year-old goalkeeper, who was one of Manchester United's standout performers last season following his £18.1million transfer from Royal Antwerp, has looked unconvincing so far this term and played a part in the club conceding more goals (35) than any other side in Europe's top five leagues.

Former Red Devils goalkeeper Schmeichel believes Lammens has never fully recovered from his error that led to Belgium being dumped out by Spain at the World Cup quarter-final stage last summer.

In the 88th minute of the match, with the score locked at 1-1, Lammens spilled the ball into the path of Mikel Merino, who tapped home to send Spain through to the semi-finals.

Asked if the confidence-shot goalkeeper should now be dropped by Mauricio Pochettino for Karl Darlow, who was signed by United in the summer, Schmeichel told CBS Sports: ‘I don’t know where that point is.

‘Mauricio Pochettino has come in, he’s brought his own staff, that means he’s got a new goalkeeping coach and maybe that is something that can change it for him.

‘I don’t know if you remember in the World Cup, he came against Spain and made a bit of a mistake, Merino scored and they got knocked out, and I feel like he’s lost a little bit of confidence from that mistake, hasn’t really recovered.

‘As a goalkeeper when you’re not on your best form you need your defenders in front of you to help you out and the sad truth is that they haven’t been able to do that and of course, that makes it so much worse.'.

Joshua Zirkzee insists United’s players will rally around Lammens and won’t let their under-fire goalkeeper ‘suffer by himself’.

The two costly errors undid Lammens’ good work after he saved an earlier penalty from Suarez, but Zirkzee said the players are determined to support the Belgian.

According to recent reports, ‘This team is a family, so there's no one who will suffer by themselves,’ said Zirkzee. ‘Mistakes happen. Let's go on to the next one. Senne’s a great goalkeeper.

‘In this moment, we just give him space. I’m not sure if he wants to hear anything at all. But Senne’s a great kid. He's got a good head on him and we all know that next game he is going to be at the top of his game. We didn't draw because of him. I'm also someone who likes to look at myself and, you know, I didn't score.’.

United had two goals disallowed by Polish referee Szymon Marciniak as well as conceding the two penalties and seeing Youri Tielemans sent off – three days after Leny Yoro was shown a controversial red card at Liverpool – and Zirkzee admitted that it feels like the world is against Pochettino’s team at the moment.

The Belgian shot-stopper conceded a needless penalty just minutes after saving a spot-kick.

He then spilled the ball into the path of Luis Suarez late on, gifting Sporting a late equaliser.

It has been revealed that ‘Yeah, we're in a place where it can feel like that pretty quick,’ he added. ‘But as I said, we've got a locker-room that's built on supporting each other and we will get out of it eventually. If it's us against the world, so be it.

‘For the team that we are, we shouldn't really be looking at what the referee decides, but you cannot deny that it was some key decisions that weren't in our favour, to put it like that.

Sources indicate that ‘But, as I remarked, we shouldn't really be looking at the referee. All we have to do is look at ourselves. In the end, I think we drew because of small details, little mistakes. But we definitely have something to build on, mainly the way we started the second half. I think that's something we can build on and move on with. Our team should be capable to win even if we're not being favoured in those moments.’.
