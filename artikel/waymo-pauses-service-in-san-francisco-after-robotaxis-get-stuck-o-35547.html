📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

This isn’t the first time the manufacturer’s robotaxis have caused chaos in the city.

In a recent development, waymo has temporarily paused its robotaxi service in San Francisco due to complications dealing with flooding on some streets, according to an X post from District 5 Supervisor Bilal Mahmood and a message in the Waymo app. The interruption in service follows several incidents of Waymos blocking intersections because they're seemingly unable (or unwilling) to drive through water.

Riders who want to get picked up are now greeted with a message in the app that claims "we're paused for now due to weather" and that service will resume "when road conditions improve and the forecast looks clear." San Francisco is currently dealing with heavy rain and flooding as the region heads into what forecasters expect could be a record El Niño winter.

In a recent development, ahead of the pause, videos posted on X highlighted a Waymo stopped in the middle of a flooded crosswalk, forcing cars to drive around it. Another Waymo idled in the middle of a road and instructed its rider to stay inside.

In a recent development, engadget has asked Waymo to comment on the stalled robotaxis and the complications it's facing with San Francisco's current weather. We'll patch this article if we hear back.

Sources indicate that waymo previously paused service in San Francisco in December 2025, when a power outage led to similar instances of robotaxis blocking city streets. The company has also dealt with flooding in the past. In May, Waymo recalled nearly 4,000 vehicles and paused its service after a robotaxi drove into a flooded road in San Antonio. The company issued a software update to address the issue, but later had to pause service in Atlanta when a Waymo drove into a flooded street again.

According to recent reports, the fact that the organization is dealing with these problems in San Francisco suggests solving this problem might be more complicated than it appears. The pause only affects the city, but it comes just a day after the California DMV restricted Waymo from the state's freeways, prompting the company to pull its robotaxis off freeways in all of its markets.
