Roma will return to action on Saturday at 6:00 PM against Parma, a match on Matchday 12 of Serie A.

After their international matches, the Giallorossi aim to resume their excellent run, which concluded with a 3-0 victory at the Stadio Olimpico against Sassuolo.

Ahead of the match at the Tardini, Gian Piero Gasperini hopes to see Mile Svilar fit again. The Giallorossi goalkeeper has missed the last nine games due to a hairline fracture in his foot sustained during training.

Sources indicate that this unfortunate injury has prevented the number 99 from defending Roma’s goal. His presence in Parma, despite having partially returned to training with the team, is still uncertain.

The break has helped Roma recover almost all of their injured players. For the trip to Parma, Gasperini will be able to count on Dybala again, but the notable absentee in recent weeks has been Svilar.

In addition to the Argentine striker, the Giallorossi coach is also counting on having his number 99 back. According to Il Messaggero, however, the Roma goalkeeper is still experiencing some discomfort when shooting. This situation will need to be monitored on a daily basis.
