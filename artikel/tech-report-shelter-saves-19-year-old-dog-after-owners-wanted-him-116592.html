A dog shelter in Florida has attracted online attention after saving a “healthy” but elderly dog after the long-time owner dropped him off to be euthanized.

Pet shelters around the world take dogs in for a number of different reasons, but one of the biggest reasons centres on moving home and going somewhere that doesn’t allow pets.

Recent findings suggest that in the case of one pet owner in Florida, they wanted to give up their 19-year-old Dachshund because they were moving to an entirely different country. As per TikToker Kaitlyn Margo, the owner initially wanted to have the elderly dog euthanized, but employees were able to convince them that the dog should be put up for adoption.

“He was brought in to be euthanized, but his blood work is perfect, and he has no challenges, and he’s healthy,” Margo said. “He’s just old. He has no issues, not even diabetes. He’s a good boy.”.

The TikToker’s video quickly captured the attention of many, with around 1 million views on it, and a number of Floridian viewers offering to adopt Bear.

Sources indicate that “Excuse me, they had him for 19 years, and they’re not taking him wtf is wrong with them?” one asked. “If I had the choice, I wouldn’t even bother moving. Absolutely not,” another chimed in.

It has been revealed that margo noted that it didn’t take long for Bear to be adopted following the attention. “Bear is going home! Thank you to everyone who shared,” she commented.

She also responded to some suggestions that she had made up the story in order to get the dog adopted.

Recent findings suggest that “Truly insane that people try to accuse people trying to help animals find loving homes of lying or “rage baiting”. No girl, unfortunately this is the reality of working at a shelter,” the TikToker noted.
