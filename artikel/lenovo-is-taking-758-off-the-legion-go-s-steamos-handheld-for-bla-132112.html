It has been revealed that save $758: You can get Lenovo’s Legion Go S gaming handheld with SteamOS for $850, down from $1,608 in Lenovo’s Black Friday sale. For that price, you’ll get the configuration with an AMD Ryzen Z1 Extreme processor, 32GB of RAM and a 1TB SSD, which will give you room for a lot of games. Lenovo has labeled the deal a doorbuster and is limiting orders to five units, so you should act fast if you want it, as you may not be able to get this price through Black Friday on Nov. 27.

Industry observers note that CNET’s Lori Grunin prefers the Legion Go S with SteamOS over the Steam Deck OLED. You’ll get an 8-inch display on the Legion Go S, which is larger than the display on the Steam Deck OLED. The display can refresh at up to 120Hz, so fast-moving games will look smoother. This one runs SteamOS, which will give you a console-like interface and quick access to your Steam library.

You can also grab the Legion Go S carry case at checkout, where the case is down to $8, from $20. We don’t know how long Lenovo’s stock will last, so you should order soon if you want the Legion Go S at $850.

You would have paid $900 for this Legion Go S configuration at launch, and as much as $1,608 after that as gaming component costs rise. At $850, you’ll be paying $50 less than the launch price.

The Steam Deck OLED has a smaller screen than the Legion Go S, though, so you’ll have to decide if the $61 saving makes up for the difference in size.
