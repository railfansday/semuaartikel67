Tottenham fans will be keeping their fingers crossed that Roberto De Zerbi continues to increase Luca Williams-Barnett's first-team minutes over the coming weeks.

The 18-year-old wowed with an electric two-minute cameo against Crystal Palace last month, before then getting seven minutes off the bench in the 1-0 defeat at Leeds United last time out.

It was no surprise to see the boss turn to the starlet late on at Elland Road after what he produced a week earlier in the 2-0 win over the Eagles.

Williams-Barnett went so close to marking his Premier League debut with an assist but unfortunately Mohammed Kudus put the ball wide of the empty net from point-blank range after an audacious piece of skill from his teammate to tee him up.

The calls from Tottenham fans for Williams-Barnett to start are only getting louder and now all eyes are on De Zerbi's team selections.

Saturday's home encounter against Ipswich Town could represent the perfect opportunity for De Zerbi to give the talented star even more minutes under his belt.

There could even be an opening for him to start if De Zerbi sees fit as Mathys Tel, who Williams-Barnett replaced on the left flank against Crystal Palace, is set to face a spell on the sidelines through injury.

While Savio is fit enough to return to the squad, De Zerbi confirmed on Friday afternoon that Mykhailo Mudryk isn't fit enough to be involved, just days after saying joining Arsenal was his dream move.

The Tractor Boys have fared well since returning to the Premier League at the first time of asking, after registering three wins from their first ten games.

However, Gary O'Neil's side do concede a lot of goals having shipped 20 so far, thus giving Williams-Barnett and his Tottenham teammates plenty of opportunities to attack them and cause some real challenges.

More minutes will come the youngster's way over the coming months, with supporters desperate to get a real look at the academy ace from the off very soon as he can have a huge part to play in the club's future.

Luca Williams-Barnett's Statistics vs Crystal Palace.

Tottenham do have a decent set of fixtures coming up before they host Arsenal in the first North London Derby of the season on Sunday, December 6.

After hosting Ipswich Town this weekend in a 3pm kick-off on Saturday, Spurs play away at Sunderland the following Thursday before returning to home soil four days later with a Thursday evening game against Fulham.

The trio of sides are currently in the bottom half of the table and they all look good opportunities to give Williams-Barnett some more playing time.

Ipswich appear the obvious choice for the reasons outlined above, with Sunderland a bit more of a test for the youngster due to the atmosphere generated at the Stadium of Light.

You would certainly back the Luton-born player to handle anything thrown at him, though, but De Zerbi will undoubtedly be cautious and ease him into the first team to begin with.

Tottenham quite clearly have a star on their hands and it may not be too long before Premier League defences have to try and deal with him on a regular basis.
