A cat in England has been reunited with his family after he travelled on a 37-mile solo train trip before finally being sent home by station staff.

It’s been a big year for animals travelling vast distances, as we’ve seen everything from stolen dogs making cross-country escapes to their original homes to a French cat that travelled 155 miles home from Spain.

Now, an orange cat (it’s always orange) named Zeus has entered the competition after he boarded a train from Essex all the way to London.

Reported by the BBC, the four-legged fare dodger caught the 8:08pm train from Braintree, a town in Essex, England, on November 10 and travelled 37 miles to London Liverpool Street.

In a recent development, why four-year-old Zeus decided to take a trip to London remains to be seen, but he didn’t manage to do much sightseeing as staff later found him at the station. Zeus was then taken into an office before being escorted on another train all the way back to his hometown.

Owner Lily Burroughs stated she was shocked, but not exactly surprised by Zeus’ recent escapade as it turns out the crafty cat isn’t exactly a stranger to train travel.

Sources indicate that “I just thought, ‘oh my God, he’s done it again,’” Burroughs told the BBC. Before moving to Braintree, Zeus had made trips from his former home in Stansted Mountfitchet to London Stansted Airport.
