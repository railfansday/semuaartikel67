According to recent reports, video game consoles are more expensive than ever, but sales are growing regardless, at least in the US. Circana has released its report for US video game industry sales in October, confirming that PS5 unit sales increased by 5% compared to October 2025. That's despite the average selling price of a PS5 increasing by 13%. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4FatSqS3VYY Xbox Series X|S unit sales jumped by 10% in October 2026 compared to October 2025, and the average selling price rose by 29% year-over-year. The Switch 2 was October 2026's top console in unit and dollar sales, and it remains 2026's best-performing console by those measurements, followed by PS5. However, Circana did not break out the specifics for Switch 2 unit sales or average selling price changes. Total hardware spending in the US on game consoles was up by 2%, reaching $357 million. Higher pricing drove the dollar sales growth, Circana remarked, noting that PS5 dollar sales rose by 20% and Xbox Series X|S jumped by 42%. However, Switch 2 sales, measured by dollar sales, fell by 11%. Looking back to September 2026, PS5 hardware dollar sales grew by 43%, while spending on Xbox Series X|S dropped by 2% and Switch 2 fell by 12%. It will be interesting to see the November US hardware data when it comes in, as experts have said the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S could get a jolt in sales from people buying systems to play GTA 6. However, Circana has warned that sales might not be as strong as expected due to the increased prices. Microsoft, Sony, and Nintendo have raised prices on their consoles due in part to the ongoing memory and component shortages from the AI buildout. Tariffs have also played a role. On the subject of tariffs, all three received refunds from the government after the Supreme Court deemed the tariffs illegal, but those companies are keeping the money and not giving anything back to you. The companies argued that people who paid more knew what they were doing and don't deserve to get any money back.
