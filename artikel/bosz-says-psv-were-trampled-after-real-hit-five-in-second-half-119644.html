PSV and Feyenoord face an uphill task to reach the group stages of the Champions League after defeats on Tuesday night saw them drop out of the qualifying places.

Industry observers note that PSV briefly threatened to cause an upset in Madrid as they took a 1-0 lead into the half-time break. Ivan Perisic’s penalty after 32 minutes was their only reward for a courageous first-half display in which Ruben van Bommel and Sven Mijnans also missed clear chances to score.

Recent findings suggest that but the introduction of Jude Bellingham at half-time changed the picture as Real’s superstars regrouped to score five goals. The England midfielder’s dazzling volley was the pick of the bunch, while Kylian Mbappé bagged a double and Vinicius Junior also got on the scoresheet.

Head coach Peter Bosz admitted his team had been “trampled underfoot” in the second half. “I think you had to be there to see how massive the difference was between the first and second half,” he stated.

“In the first half we were brave, put our opponents under pressure and had three substantial chances in the first four minutes.

According to recent reports, “When you look at the second half, they scored swiftly and the tempo of their game was much higher. That’s the quality they have with the players they can bring off the bench.”.

PSV’s next match is a home tie against Real’s city rivals Atletico on December 9, before they travel to Stavanger to take on Viking in January and round off the group stage at home to Stuttgart.

Feyenoord suffered a narrower defeat at home to Porto as they were unable to recover from an own goal after two minutes by Oussama Targhalline.

Goalkeeper Florian Kastenmeier vindicated the decision of Giovanni van Bronckhorst to replace the out-of-form Tjark Ernst by making some vital saves to keep the Rotterdammers in the game.

Feyenoord staged a late rally, with Gjivai Zechiël going close and Targhalline striking the crossbar from long range, but they were unable to breach the Portuguese side’s defence.

The fans in De Kuip were briefly celebrating after a bizarre incident when referee Irfan Peltjo’s watch told him a header by Nacho Ferri had crossed the line, but video replays clearly showed it bounced off the post and into the hands of Diogo Costa.

Recent findings suggest that feyenoord have just three points from their five matches so far, one less than PSV. Their next two matches are away to Viking on December 8 and Galatasaray on January 19, before they close their campaign in Rotterdam against RB Leipzig.
