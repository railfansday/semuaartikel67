In a recent development, tina Turner wasn’t just a magnetic stage presence; she was a force of nature, the eighth wonder of the world. The vast archive of photographs from her concerts makes that plain enough: from the very start of her career, when she performed and toured with her first husband, Ike Turner, through to her hard-won breakthrough as a solo artist in the 1980s and beyond, few could match her electric energy.

Now, three years after her death, we’re looking back at some of the most delightful vintage snapshots of Tina Turner, who would be 87 today.
