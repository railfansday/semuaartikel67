For its last outing before the international break, Paris Saint-Germain hosts Troyes at the Parc des Princes, in Ligue 1. For the moment, Luis Enrique’s men are ahead after a goal from Illya Zabarnyi before the break. A special meeting for Parisian supporters, who are celebrating the 35th anniversary of Virage Auteuil this Saturday.

Sources indicate that before kick-off, the PSG ultras put on a magnificent show in the stands of the Parc des Princes. On the occasion of this anniversary, Virage Auteuil deployed a spectacular tifo, accompanied by songs and smoke bombs, to mark the 35th anniversary of one of the historical symbols of the reigning double European champion club.
