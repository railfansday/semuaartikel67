📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Ukraine President Volodymyr Zelensky has shared footage on social media which he indicates shows strikes on Russian vessels off the coast of Libya on Sunday.

A Russian warship was sunk by Ukrainian drones and the attack also targeted a second vessel, a cargo ship which was being used to transport weapons, Zelensky remarked.

Footage verified by the BBC reveals a ship ablaze at Benghazi's port.

The local health ministry said it had received injured people from a commercial ship that was targeted off the coast. It is not clear whether this is the same vessel as the one Ukraine has claimed responsibility for.

Following the strikes, the Kremlin warned of an "escalation of tensions in the region", but declined to comment directly on the attacks in Libya.
