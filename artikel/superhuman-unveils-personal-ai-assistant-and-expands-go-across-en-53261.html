UPDATED 09:00 EST / NOVEMBER 19 2026.

Superhuman Inc., formerly known as Grammarly, today declared the expansion of Go, its proactive artificial intelligence assistant and multi-agent platform, along with updated features across its entire suite.

It has been revealed that among the updates, the company added a latest personalized AI assistant in Go that consumers can name, give work to and ask to watch projects and to-do lists in the background. The new assistant will work across connected tools, suggest next steps, draft content in the user’s voice, and manage a team of other AI agents, with the user always in the loop.

Superhuman also brought agents into Docs and Mail and updated Fathom, the firm’s note-taking app, to act as a collaborative, bot-free app that connects natively to every application across the Superhuman suite and captures video, screen sharing, and audio.

Sources indicate that “Superhuman is solving for the last mile of AI: the gap between deployment and adoption,” said Chief Executive Shishir Mehrotra. “The best AI highlights up for your whole team, not just the power users who go looking for it. It’s embedded in the tools you already use, it works ahead of you, and it collaborates alongside you.”.

Sources indicate that superhuman Go is growing with a personal agent that works ahead of a user on their behalf, with their approval. The company stated the newly introduced Go assistant acts like an extra set of eyes and ears, watching out for users throughout their day, finding tasks they need to do, and keeping up with their “need to know.” It tries to stay ahead of their task list, offers to complete things it can, picks up slack, summarizes meeting notes, and answers key emails or preps for important meetings.

Recent findings suggest that it also writes like the user and gets better over time with direction. It learns from past responses to draft content that sounds like them, refines writing before they hit send, and keeps learning their style to improve over time.

Superhuman noted that it added more than 20 fresh partner agents, which can help pull in expert knowledge and capabilities from enterprise services. For example, a user can connect to Legora AB’s agent for legal intelligence, Databricks Inc.’s Live Metrics agent for metrics and relevant numbers, Amplitude Inc.’s Product check agent to verify product usage claims, and Blackboard Inc.’s Assignment Coach agent to help students improve coursework.

Recent findings suggest that with the rise of personal AI agents such as OpenClaw and Meta Platform Inc.’s Muse, the open-source community, startups and enterprises have begun offering personalized agents that autonomously assist users with day-to-day tasks and work. Anthropic PBC also recently released Claude Duet, a similar proactive AI agent for professionals and small businesses in private preview, that completes tasks when asked and keeps working in the background.

Superhuman Go agents are now native in Docs, the company’s AI-native collaborative workspace that combines documents, spreadsheets and automated workflows. Owners can share a doc with any agent, just mentioning it with @ to hand off work, and it will show up as another collaborator.

Consumers can add agents with triggers for when to run, and they will interact with the document whether it’s open or not, updating it whenever something changes. This will allow agents to collaborate by adding to the document and changing it by checking email, watching connected apps or checking connected tables, so that the information in the doc stays fresh.

Similarly, agents connect to Mail and every other tool. This means that when drafting an email, agents can suggest content as consumers type. Superhuman Mail can use agents to pull in information from Slack, meeting notes or internal documents. The company said auto drafts already saves users an average of nine minutes per draft. For a user who replies to four emails a day, that can save about three hours a week.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to these changes, Superhuman said the Go platform software development kit is generally available to partners and customers with new agent-building capabilities. Partners can now easily build and connect their own agents that show up anywhere within Go. The organization aims to add more specialized partner agents.
