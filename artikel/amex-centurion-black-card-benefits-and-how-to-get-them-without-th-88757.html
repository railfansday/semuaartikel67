Amex Centurion (black) card benefits — and how to get them without the card

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (175 words, 📖 Standard)
  • The Centurion Card is an invitation-only product known for its high fees and exclusive perks, serving as a status symbol for cardholders.
  • Many of its benefits, such as hotel elite status, airport lounge access and concierge services, can be accessed through other, less costly cards.
  • Some Centurion-specific perks, like enhanced hotel credits, expanded lounge guest policies and unique event access, are harder to replicate elsewhere.
  • The card's value depends largely on how much you use its exclusive services compared to what you could obtain with alternative cards.
  • There is no published spending threshold or guaranteed path to receive a Centurion invitation, making eligibility unpredictable.
  • Centurion cardholders face significant initiation and ongoing fees, which far exceed those of most other premium cards.
  • Certain benefits, such as expanded Clear+ coverage, PS Salon visits and Centurion Lounge guest access, are uniquely enhanced for Centurion members.
