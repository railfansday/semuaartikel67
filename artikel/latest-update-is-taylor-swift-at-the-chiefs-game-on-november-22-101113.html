Another Chiefs game, another round of “Is Taylor Swift in the stands?”.

Tay has shown up to most of Travis Kelce’s home games this season (including one with none other than Karlie Kloss), and it looks like she is officially at the Chiefs vs. Cardinals game at Arrowhead Stadium on November 22. After some speculation that she’d skipped the game, Taylor was photographed in the stands with her parents, as well as Travis’ family.

According to recent reports, taylor’s appearance comes after she made a public appearance in Nashville earlier this week, where she performed (for the first time in 13 years!) at the 2026 CMAs. In case you missed it, Tay dropped a fleet of Debut TV clues thanks to kicking things off in a green room, where she was surrounded by iconic dresses from her earliest era.

It has been revealed that considering she also incorporated “Our Song” into her performance *and* this is the 20th anniversary of her first album, fans are convinced Debut TV is incoming. And let’s not forget that we know for a fact that Taylor has the album re-recorded already—she confirmed as much in the letter she wrote about buying back her masters.

AHEM: “I’ve already completely re-recorded my entire debut album, and I really love how it sounds now. Those 2 albums [Taylor Swift and Reputation] can still have their moments to re-emerge when the time is right, if that would be something you guys would be excited about. But if it happens, it won’t be from a place of sadness and longing for what I wish I could have. It will just be a celebration now.”.

Okay, off to analyze Taylor’s recent appearances for more Debut TV clues (let me live).
