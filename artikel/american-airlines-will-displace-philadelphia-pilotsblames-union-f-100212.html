American Airlines states a “significant number” of Philadelphia pilots will be displaced from their current positions after their union’s board rejected a deal on staffing Airbus A321XLR flights to Europe. The airline is reopening a domestic Airbus pilot base and shrinking its international one for June 2027. That can change which trips pilots fly, the schedules they can hold, and potentially their commutes. American is blaming the union for rejecting what it calls an “objectively better path forward.” Aviation watchdog JonNYC shares the tech giant’s message to pilots: When we publish our June 2027 vacancy announcement tomorrow, you will see that we are reopening a PHL A320 Domestic base (Section 17.S.1) and reducing the PHL A320 International base. This will, unfortunately, result in the displacement of a significant number of pilots from the PHL A320 International base (Section 17.D). The airline says it had agreed to a term sheet with the Allied Pilots Association, completed the contractual language, and expected a signed side agreement. The union’s board rejected it November 6.
