Bitcoin Price Trades Near $64,000 as Fed’s Waller Floats Half-Point Hike

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitcoin price traded near $64,000 on Wednesday morning, according to Bitcoin Magazine data, as Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller remarked a half-point rate hike is probably needed in December. The bitcoin price had slipped below $64,000 on Tuesday afternoon. It climbed back to about $65,000 that evening after Nvidia beat earnings estimates, then faded through the U.S. morning.

The bitcoin price is down about 21% from a month ago, when it traded above $81,000.

Waller told CNBC in a live interview that the Fed probably needs a half-point increase at its Dec. 8-9 meeting. He said the central bank should reverse its three quarter-point cuts from last year and may need to do more. “You might need to, like, shock people,” Waller said.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m very, very concerned about inflation. It’s kind of headed in the wrong direction, and it’s spreading out,” he said. Waller said the Fed should deliver the larger move if it is not prepared to signal a faster pace of hikes in the coming months. He also said about 70% of items in the core services basket are running above 3%, which has nothing to do with oil.

The 2-year Treasury yield rose more than 4 basis points to 4.83% after his comments. The dollar hit a 19-month high, and gold dropped 2.5% to its lowest level in more than a year. Futures markets fully price a quarter-point hike in December and another increase in January, CNBC reported. St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem suggested Tuesday that as many as four hikes could come by spring.

Minutes from the Fed’s Oct. 27-28 meeting are due Wednesday afternoon. Policymakers held rates at 3.75% to 4% in a 10-2 vote, with Fed Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissenting in favor of a hike.

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.84 billion after the bell Tuesday, more than double a year earlier and above the $109.1 billion analysts expected. Data center revenue more than doubled to $107.6 billion, and the company guided to about $130 billion for the current quarter. Interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia will remain “disciplined in how we commit capital.” The board said it would not reinstate Jensen Huang, who resigned as CEO last week.

Nvidia shares were up about 2.6% in premarket trading. Nasdaq-100 futures gained 0.8% and S&P 500 futures rose 0.3%, but the bitcoin price did not follow tech higher. IREN (IREN), the bitcoin miner turned AI cloud provider, rose between 8% and 10% in extended trading along with CoreWeave and Nebius, and was recently up about 3% premarket. Strategy (MSTR) closed down 6.3% at $96.50 on Tuesday and was little changed before the open, while Coinbase (COIN) closed down 4% and was up about 0.5% premarket.

Recent findings suggest that U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs shed a net $239.9 million on Tuesday, a day after snapping a seven-session outflow streak. BlackRock’s IBIT accounted for $209.5 million of that. Anthropic is expected to price its IPO Wednesday evening, with shares set to start trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. The firm marketed 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, and sources told CNBC on Tuesday that the deal will likely price above that range.

President Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday for a summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. AI chip exports are a key issue.

A break of the weekend low near $62,000 would put last week’s flush back in view for the bitcoin price.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $63,800.
