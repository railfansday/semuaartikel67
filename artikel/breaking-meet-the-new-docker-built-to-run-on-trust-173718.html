📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Today, Docker is officially launching its new brand, and I’m excited to tell you what it’s all about and why we think now is the time for change.

Industry observers note that let’s start with the big picture. We’re living through a transformative moment: for Docker, for developers, for the industry, and quite possibly, for humanity itself. AI is creating possibilities that would have sounded like science fiction not long ago.

Industry observers note that but it’s also forcing the industry to confront an almost existential question: how do we harness all that power and intelligence without compromising security, governance, or control? Or, put more simply: how do we get to a point where we can actually trust agents?

That one seemingly simple yet infinitely complex question is why we’re here today, introducing you to a new Docker.

A new Docker, but a familiar story.

It has been revealed that ok, not exactly here, but once upon a time, a decade or so ago, the tech industry was at a similar inflection point. Digital transformation was the term du jour, promising a new way of engaging customers and powering the global economy. There was one significant constraint, though: teams needed a better way to build, ship, and run software consistently across any environment.

Enter a manufacturer you might have heard of: Docker.

It has been revealed that together with all of you, Docker built the standard trusted by millions of developers. For over a decade, developers have relied on us to make software portable, open, reproducible, and secure. Docker turned the frustration of “Well, it works on my machine!” into the assurance that “Yes, it works everywhere.”.

It has been revealed that today, of course, the challenge confronting us is upcoming and evolving, and we all need to evolve with it. We cannot simply continue to do what we’ve always done and expect to succeed in this new era. That applies to business leaders, developers, tech companies, everyone. It certainly applies to Docker. That’s what this rebrand is about: evolving to meet the moment for our customers.

It has been revealed that let me be unequivocal: this is not about leaving Docker’s legacy or heritage behind. We’ve spent more than a decade earning your trust. This is about building on that foundation, not replacing it.

The qualities you value us for aren’t changing. We’re still committed to openness and industry collaboration. We’re still, and always will be, developer-first. We’re still fanatical about simplicity, and about showing up everywhere you need us.

Recent findings suggest that on the product front, the containers you know and love aren’t going anywhere. Containers still play a huge role in the software development lifecycle. As long as that’s the case, Docker will continue leading the way.

Agents have introduced a newly introduced problem. Containers isolate workloads. But agents aren’t workloads. Agents are actors.

Agents make their own decisions about what to run, what to access, and what to change. Developers and enterprises need solutions built specifically to isolate and govern them, so they can give those agents real autonomy without giving up control.

Docker’s next chapter is about delivering exactly that: security, isolation, and governance, so we can all trust agents to do meaningful work without feeling like we’re always one step from catastrophe.

In case I haven’t made it blindingly obvious yet, the new Docker is all about trust.

Trust Docker because our track record speaks for itself. Trust Docker because we’re proven, neutral, and secure. Trust Docker because it’s pretty darn clear you can’t trust the agents to manage themselves.

Trust us because we have a software supply chain that is already the standard for building securely. Trust us because nobody isolates agents where they run better than we do. Trust us because we’re taking a comprehensive approach to improving governance and minimizing risk across the entire enterprise.

In a recent development, trust Docker because we’re not egotistical enough to think we can solve a challenge this significant alone. Trust us because everything about our history, our heritage, and our actions over the past year tells you we’ll partner with the smartest and build alongside the best, and not create vendor lock-in.

Recent findings suggest that most of all, trust Docker because we’re not just talking the talk. We’ve got the goods to back it up: trusted supply chain for your agents to build from, trusted runtime to isolate your agents, and a trusted ecosystem to ensure all the layers of trust are covered, whether made by Docker or not.

Industry observers note that as our product team innovated to meet the moment and our messaging in the market began to shift, one thing became clear: the speed and scale of evolution happening inside Docker called for an equally momentous evolution on the outside. Something that makes it unmistakably clear that it’s a new day for Docker. Something that says and demonstrates, loud and clear, for all the world to see, that Docker is not just about containers anymore.

Something, dare I say, whale sized.

I’m talking, of course, about our beloved Moby.

Sources indicate that if you know Docker, then you know Moby, and if Docker is evolving, Moby should too. The only question was to how and to what extent? We looked at a lot of whales, folks. (I may or may not have been seeing whales in my sleep.) Ultimately, with the help of our friends at the strategic brand transformation company, Motto, we landed on a newly introduced Moby for the new Docker.

In many ways, Moby is a microcosm for Docker and the changes we’re making: the Moby you love and trust, evolved for a new era. Just as the new Docker is no longer singularly defined by containers, neither is the new Moby.

It has been revealed that we’ve got lots to show you. Poke around our new website. Find and engage with the new Docker brand on social media. And if you want to see what trust in the agentic era looks like in practice, try Docker Sandboxes and Docker AI Governance. We’d love to hear what you think.

In a recent development, wherever this new era takes us, Docker will always honor and cherish the trust you continue to place in us. Now, let’s go build what’s next.
