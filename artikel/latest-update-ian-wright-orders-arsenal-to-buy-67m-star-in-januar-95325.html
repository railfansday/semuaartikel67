Ian Wright has urged Arsenal to go out and buy Antonio Nusa from RB Leipzig in the January transfer window.

Despite a 2-2 draw with Newcastle United on Saturday night, the Gunners remain six points clear at the summit of the Premier League and are still firm favourites to defend their crown.

But their limitations in attack were again exposed on Tyneside. Eberechi Eze opened the scoring on the night with Noni Madueke impressing on the right with Bukayo Saka out injured but the visitors were short on inspiration off the bench.

Christos Tzolis came on in the second-half for a forgettable cameo with Viktor Gyokeres labouring once again after his introduction.

Arsenal explored deals to sign Julian Alvarez and Vinicius Jr in the summer but Tzolis was their sole addition in attack once the window closed with Gabriel Martinelli and Leonardo Trossard also moving on.

Arsenal are interested in signing RB Leipzig star Nusa who is enjoying a superb season with the German club, with seven goals and eight assists in 16 appearances.

Leipzig have put a huge €80million (£67m) price tag on the Norway international who operates on the left for club and country.

While convinced Arsenal are well stocked in all other areas, club legend Wright believes Nusa could offer a much-improved option in attack and also raise the game of Kai Havertz.

In a recent development, ‘I’d want to go [and strengthen] on the left side and up front. At some stage we have got to say “we have got to go for it.”.
