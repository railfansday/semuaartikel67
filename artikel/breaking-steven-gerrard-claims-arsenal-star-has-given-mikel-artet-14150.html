Steven Gerrard believes Ezri Konsa has already given Mikel Arteta ‘a massive problem’ after joining Arsenal.

Konsa was signed by Arsenal in a £55 million deal from Aston Villa in August and has hit the ground running for the Premier League champions in the absence of William Saliba.

However, Saliba appears to be nearing a comeback from his back injury as he made the bench for Arsenal’s 2-1 win over Slavia Prague in the Champions League earlier this month.

Arteta now faces a tricky selection decision at centre-back when Saliba, Konsa and Gabriel Magalhaes are all available over the winter period.

‘I think Konsa has improved so much under Unai Emery,’ Gerrard stated on The Overlap Daily.

‘I think he’s given Mikel Arteta a substantial problem, Konsa, the way he’s played for Arsenal.

‘I think he’s become England’s number-one centre-back, which I thought was [Marc] Guehi last year.

‘If we can get Guehi and Konsa as a main two [for England], I think that potentially can be good enough.’.

Gerrard also believes Ben White has become a leading contender to secure his status as England’s first-choice right-back after an impressive performance in the 3-2 win over Spain on Sunday night.

‘Ben White is the interesting one because I think everyone naturally thinks of Reece James and Trent Alexander-Arnold, and in some games it can be because in some games you are going to need a little bit more than Ben White in terms of opening the door and that special pass or a little bit of magic in a game,’ Gerrard said.

‘But in the major games, when you sometimes need someone who is going to be a little bit more solid, a little bit more disciplined, in terms of positioning, who can swing to a back three at any point, Ben White will be a huge positive for the manager [Tuchel] off the back of last night [against Spain].

‘You would have had to have been inside the England camp to know the details around why he wasn’t happy, and why he didn’t want to come, that’s his business with whoever else, but from afar, I never once heard Ben White say he didn’t want to play for England.

Industry observers note that ‘When it comes down to football, I think he played against Spain like a proud Englishman, he looked, to me, like he wanted to be there. Hopefully it’s all water under the bridge and Ben White can be available because you’ve seen what he’s done from an Arsenal point of view and England need that.

According to recent reports, ‘We’ve got the attacking ones who are really good going forward, that might be needed when you’re playing against a low block but in certain games, when it’s a bit more 50-50, and you need someone to defend one-v-one, have positional discipline, suffer if you go to ten men, I think Ben White was terrific against Spain, scored a great goal, but I was more happy with his overall performance. Just having him there and him having the opportunity to win the fans back over, it was a significant night for him.’.
