A floor plan arrives as a PDF. It demonstrates the existing layout of a small store slated for renovation, and the client has sent it over the day before the first meeting. Before anyone can begin sketching a proposal, someone has to redraw the plan, line by line, in CAD. Once the ideas begin to take shape, the same space is modeled again in another program, this time in 3D, to produce images and renderings. The furniture list goes into a spreadsheet, the presentation comes together in a third program, and the client's comments arrive by email, text, and voice message, often with screenshots marked up in red, several steps removed from the drawing they refer to. If a wall moves after that, someone has to make the same change in each file.
