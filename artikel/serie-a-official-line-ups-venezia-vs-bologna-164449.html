Raffaele Palladino has left Riccardo Orsolini and Artem Dovbyk out of the Bologna XI for tonight’s trip to bottom-of-the-table Venezia, with Jens Odgaard leading the line between Federico Bernardeschi and Samuel Mbangula.

Kick-off at the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo is at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT].

Davide Nicola sticks with a 3-5-2 for Venezia. Gianluca Busio starts in midfield alongside Kike Pérez and Simon Sohm, while Akor Adams and John Yeboah lead the attack.

In a recent development, the two sides are heading in opposite directions. Venezia are bottom of Serie A with six points from 12 games and were beaten 4-0 at Juventus on Sunday. Bologna are ninth on 18 points, having drawn 1-1 with Udinese last time out after three straight league wins, including a 4-2 victory at Milan.

It has been revealed that venezia are without Bartol Franjić, Marin Šverko and Thierry Correia through injury. Bologna are missing Lorenzo De Silvestri, Emil Holm, Juan Miranda, Oussama El Azzouzi, Jay Enem and Ukko Happonen.

Venezia (3-5-2): Stanković; Halhal, Moreno, Juan Jesus; Hainaut, Kike Pérez, Sohm, Busio, Haps; Yeboah, Adams Coach: Davide Nicola.

Bologna (3-4-3): Skorupski; Vitík, Casale, Theate; Zortea, Pobega, Ferguson, Alhassane; Bernardeschi, Odgaard, Mbangula Coach: Raffaele Palladino.
