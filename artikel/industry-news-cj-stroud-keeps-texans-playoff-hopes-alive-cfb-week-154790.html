The NBA season started one month ago today. The first stanza of the campaign brought a great deal of excitement at the top of the league with superstars doing superstar things and most of the projected championship contenders getting off to hot starts. But we also saw injuries, a disappointing first handful of games from Mikal Bridges and an Eastern Conference that is not nearly as dominant as it was supposed to be.

It has been revealed that those are three of the biggest letdowns of the young season. Here's our John Gonzalez on why Bridges might be the greatest dud of any individual player:.

Another major disappointment: The Kings. How can a team underperform expectations when those expectations are already about as low as they come? Well, Sacramento sure has found a way. With a 1-13 record and after a puzzling trade, the Kings earned their spot in our ranking of the NBA's most hopeless teams.

Sources indicate that the College Football Selection Committee is in love with the SEC. Only one time in three different rankings have the bracket-makers ranked a team from that conference lower than AP Top 25 voters. Whether or not you think it's fair, the numbers largely back it up. In fact, two-loss No. 5 Texas even has a better resume score than undefeated No. 2 Utah! So maybe the committee is not all that out of line. You can be sure they will have their eyes on these five key matchups in Week 12.

Another important clash is the only top-25 contest of the week: No. 1 Georgia at No. 21 South Carolina. In his Six Pack of picks, our Tom Fornelli explained why the Bulldogs will roll on the road.

Industry observers note that moving away from the SEC, look out for a potential upset in the Big Ten. Nothing is going well for Michigan, but the Wolverines are only small underdogs against unbeaten No. 4 UCLA. These five factors will determine whether the Bruins move one step closer to the CFP or fall for the first time this season.

Here's how to watch the biggest games of Week 12 and our picks for each of them:.

It has been revealed that this is easily the most lopsided week of the NFL season. The Bills and Chiefs are three-score favorites. The Bears and Broncos are also favored by double digits. The Cowboys, Lions, Jaguars and Bengals are all favored by about a touchdown. You know how these things go, though. One of these favorites is bound to lose. We put a few of them on upset alert, and a handful of our experts are in agreement that the Lions are among the most vulnerable.

First of all, the Buccaneers have just as much to play for as the Lions. Both of them are locked into tightly contested division battles. The motivation will be there for a Tampa Bay team that could somehow sneak into the playoffs even with a losing record. And then there's the Tampa Bay defense, which has forced at least four turnovers in each of its last three games. Our Tyler Sullivan remarked it would not surprise him if Jared Goff's interception streak continues.

And even though our Jordan Dajani could not bring himself to pick the upset outright, he does see the Buccaneers keeping this one highly competitive.
