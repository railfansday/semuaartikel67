Square Enix has been one of the biggest third-party supporters of the Nintendo Switch 2, and it seems it's already paying off.

In the Japanese tech giant's latest financial report, Square Enix president Takashi Kiryu has credited year-over-year net sales (¥11.6 billion to ¥35.6 billion) and operating income (¥8.0 billion to ¥13.0 billion) profits partly to its sales on Switch 2. Here's what he had to say:.

Takashi Kiryu: "Efforts such as supporting new hardware, including the Nintendo Switch 2, and strengthening catalog title sales also contributed to our results. In the second half, in addition to FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE, which debuted on October 22, we blueprint to release DRAGON QUEST MONSTERS: The Withered World in December and Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United in February 2027. All are new titles, including remakes.".

As touched on here by the president, Square Enix most recently released Final Fantasy Resonance on the Switch 2 and this will be followed by Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World in December and Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United early next year.

This follows its release of games such as Dragon Quest VII: Reimagined, Kingdom Hearts Collection, Final Fantasy VII Rebirth and more this year. It's also released its MMORPG Final Fantasy XIV Online on Nintendo's hybrid platform in August, and next April sees the arrival of Final Fantasy VII Revelation.
