All Mariah Carey wants for Christmas, is to put this topic to rest.

Industry observers note that as the queen of the holiday season performed at Farmasi Arena in Rio de Janeiro Nov. 21, she appeared to address speculation surrounding her health following her latest concerts.

It has been revealed that “By the way, don’t believe everything you read online,” Carey noted in a video shared by fans on social media after the show. “Don’t be making me mad, now.”.

The “We Belong Together” singer’s comments shortly followed fan speculation that she was going through some sort of health battle due to the blocking in her recent demonstrates.

“Is Mariah Carey choosing to perform as stiff as possible, or is something going on with her health?” a fan wrote on X earlier this month. “She is 57 and needs assistance to even walk around the stage.”.

Another responded to a separate video of Carey—mom to 15-year-old twins Moroccan and Monroe with ex Nick Cannon—appearing to struggle to walk in high heels, “If her health really is bad then she needs to take time off and work on herself.”.

But some of Carey’s Lambily quickly hopped in to defend her, even before the Grammy winner herself stepped in. As one fan joked in response to the speculation, “She’s still defrosting!!”.

The comment, of course, references Carey’s reign as the queen of the festive season—but she has at least begun to thaw this Christmas by kicking off her latest tour.

“Thank you São Paulo!!” Carey wrote in a Nov. 18 post on social media, alongside picture that featured her decked out in Santa outfits. “What an incredible way to kick off my Christmastime Tour.”.

And while Carey has become synonymous for her holiday hit—which was only just this year dethroned as the longest charting Billboard hit by Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas”—she has previously emphasized that it isn’t a fluke and she “really, truly loves the holidays.”.

“It’s not fake,” she told Good Morning America of her seasonal spirit in 2023, noting that she loves touring during the winter to ensure her fans have “the merriest of Christmases.”.

Industry observers note that even 32 years after she put out the record, Carey isn’t surprised it remains her chart-topper, either. After all, fans being obsessed with it was the point. As she put of writing it back in the early 1990s, “My goal was to do something timeless.”.
