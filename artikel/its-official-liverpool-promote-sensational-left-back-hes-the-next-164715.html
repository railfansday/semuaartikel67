That solution is Scottish youth international Cameron Williams, who has been catching the eye in the academy recently.

Williams trained with Scotland's senior national team when he was just 16-years-old. He is a highly talented left-back, with excellent qualities going forward, who many regard as the new Andy Robertson.

Sources indicate that the youngster has struggled with a few injuries here and there. However, he is now back playing regularly for the club's U18 side and has subsequently earned himself an official promotion.

Looking at Liverpool's squad on the UEFA website, Williams has been added to the team through the B-list, as highlighted by Lewis Bower as well.
