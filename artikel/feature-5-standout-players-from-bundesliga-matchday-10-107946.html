Bundesliga Matchday 10 delivered an eventful return from the international break, with Werder Bremen dominating the Nordderby, RB Leipzig scoring five against Union Berlin and Bayer Leverkusen recording an emphatic victory over Paderborn.

Elsewhere, Borussia Dortmund went top after overcoming Borussia Mönchengladbach as Bayern Munich dropped points against Cologne, while Augsburg ended their seven-match winless run against Mainz.

Here are five players whose individual performances made the difference for their teams….

Justin Njinmah produced one of the outstanding performances of Matchday 10 as Werder Bremen dismantled Hamburg 4-0 at the Weserstadion.

Industry observers note that making his first start since suffering an injury in August, Njinmah scored twice in four minutes during the second half. His goals in the 66th and 70th minutes ended any realistic possibility of a Hamburg comeback after Marco Friedl had opened the scoring.

While Niclas Füllkrug completed the victory with his 10th Bundesliga goal of the season, Njinmah was the standout performer.

According to recent reports, his pace, directness and finishing provide Daniel Thioune with another dangerous attacking option alongside Füllkrug. His return to fitness could prove particularly vital as Bremen look to maintain their impressive start to the campaign.

Afonso Moreira delivered an impressive performance as Bayer Leverkusen defeated newly promoted Paderborn 4-0 at the BayArena.

In a recent development, the Portuguese winger opened the scoring in the 14th minute before doubling Leverkusen’s advantage shortly after half-time. Patrik Schick and Ezequiel Fernández subsequently completed the scoring.

Moreira’s brace demonstrated his attacking potential and ability to make decisive contributions in the final third.

According to recent reports, for Leverkusen, having additional goalscoring threats alongside Schick could prove essential in their pursuit of Champions League qualification. Moreira’s performance was an encouraging indication that he can provide precisely that.

Brajan Gruda continued his impressive development as RB Leipzig secured a commanding 5-1 victory over Union Berlin.

With Leipzig leading 2-1, Gruda scored in the 72nd minute to give Martín Demichelis’ side some breathing room. Rocco Reitz and Ridle Baku subsequently added further goals to complete an outstanding second-half performance.

Following his impressive display against Augsburg before the international break, Gruda is becoming an increasingly influential figure in Leipzig’s attack.

His technical ability, creativity and willingness to take on defenders give Leipzig an crucial attacking alternative. Demichelis’ praise following the match further underlined the growing importance of the young German international.

Michael Gregoritsch was instrumental in Augsburg’s much-needed 3-1 victory over Mainz, ending a seven-match Bundesliga winless run.

It has been revealed that the Austrian international opened the scoring in the 19th minute before completing his brace shortly after half-time. Juma Bah also scored for Augsburg, while Mainz reduced the deficit through Ransford-Yeboah Königsdörffer.

Gregoritsch’s two goals took his Bundesliga tally to seven this season, highlighting his importance to Augsburg despite their difficult start to the campaign.

For a team struggling to establish consistency, having an experienced centre-forward capable of delivering in decisive moments could prove fundamental to their survival ambitions.

Jonathan Burkardt once again demonstrated his importance to Eintracht Frankfurt by scoring the only goal in their 1-0 victory over Hoffenheim.

The German international broke the deadlock in the 25th minute, securing three valuable points for Adi Hütter’s side in a difficult encounter at Deutsche Bank Park.

Following his brace against Paderborn before the international break, Burkardt has now scored three goals across his last two Bundesliga appearances.

Industry observers note that frankfurt’s inconsistent start has raised questions about their European ambitions, but Burkardt’s goalscoring form remains one of the most encouraging aspects of Hütter’s rebuilding project. His ability to decide tight matches could prove essential as Frankfurt look to climb the Bundesliga table.

Matchday 10 highlighted the importance of players delivering in different circumstances. Njinmah enjoyed a memorable comeback in the Nordderby, Moreira showcased his attacking potential, Gruda continued his development, Gregoritsch helped Augsburg end their winless run and Burkardt secured an notable victory for Frankfurt. All five produced performances worthy of recognition following the international break.
