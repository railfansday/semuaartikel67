Liverpool transfer news: Liverpool have opened contract talks with Alexis Mac Allister, according to @tribuna_ftbl, with his current deal running until June 2028. The report arrives days after his father, Carlos, told ESPN that no discussions had started, although he hinted at news before the end of the year.

It also reverses the summer, when the club extended Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai but made no offer to the 27-year-old, something Mac Allister revealed in September while admitting he had stayed despite chances to leave.

Sources indicate that his form explains the change. After a poor 2025/26 under Arne Slot, he has become Andoni Iraola’s first-choice midfielder alongside Szoboszlai, pushing Gravenberch to the bench. He has scored once in the Premier League and three times in the Champions League, including the winner against Atlético Madrid, and holds a 7.29 FotMob average rating across 758 league minutes. Liverpool sit fourth.

Sources indicate that fotMob places him in the 88th percentile among midfielders for shot attempts, and he has said the man-to-man system suits him because it demands less communication than Slot’s approach. His weaknesses are just as clear.

Sources indicate that his defensive contributions (45th percentile) and aerial duels (46th) sit below the midfield average, and pundit David Lynch has questioned whether his legs have gone in Iraola’s 4-4-2, where two central midfielders cover huge areas. He has also collected four league yellow cards already.

Industry observers note that yes, but not on the terms his summer rebound might demand. Ten league games of good form do not cancel a season in which he looked off the pace, so Liverpool should offer around three years, with wages pitched below the bumper deals Gravenberch and Szoboszlai signed.

The timing still makes sense. With 18 months left, he enters the final year next summer, when Liverpool would face either a discounted sale or a free departure. His FotMob valuation of €66m has almost halved from the €130m peak of September 2025, so selling now would lock in the lowest price.

Iraola also needs a controller to sit beside Szoboszlai’s runs, and no one in the squad does that better. The caveat is that Mac Allister turns 28 next month, so Liverpool must pair him with a quicker partner next summer rather than treat his renewal as the end of the midfield rebuild.
