Sources indicate that “I was blessed to work with Andy Taylor – such a genius… he played the guitar solo in MAD ABOUT YOU, appears in the video and he made special appearances onstage for some of my first solo highlights. What a talent,” writes Belinda Carlisle on her social channels. Sources indicate that “Thank you Andy Taylor for the music and all of the joy you brought to the world which will live on forever. I hope you are feeling the love…Godspeed.”.

Recent findings suggest that legendary Led Zeppelin frontman Robert Plant joined Taylor on stage in Oxfordshire, England back in 2023, at a charity fundraiser gig for The Cancer Awareness Trust. “A tough fighter, Andy Taylor,” he recounts, “like so many…some of us get through…condolences to his tribe,” he writes.

Fellow axeman Steve Stevens, the longtime talisman of Billy Idol, was heartbroken on the news. “F#ck cancer,” he writes. “Have so many memories with Andy. He was the first guitarist to sing my praises in the English press, I was forever grateful. He, Tony, & Robert are definitely jamming now,” states Stevens, a nod to the late Power Station members Tony Thompson and Robert Palmer.

It has been revealed that duran Duran and Spandau Ballet were, for a time, the British second invasion’s pin-up boys. “Spandau Ballet are deeply saddened by the passing of Andy Taylor,” reads a message from the pop group. “Andy was a hugely talented musician and an crucial part of the British music scene that we were all proud to be part of. Our thoughts are with his family, friends and fans around the world.”.

Spandau Ballet’s co-founder and songwriter Gary Kemp had his own words. “So sad to hear the news of Andy’s passing,” he writes. “A great player who set the tone for the eighties and influenced many of us along the way with his sparse staccato style. He brought shameless rock to the mix. All my love to the Duran family. RIP Andy.”.

Like Duran Duran, UB40 for formed in Birmingham, England. “We have just heard the sad news that Andy Taylor, Duran Duran guitarist, has lost his long battle with Cancer,” a post reads. “Our thoughts are with his band mates and his family. Another one gone too soon! Big Love UB40.”.

It has been revealed that veteran British singer Michael Des Barres stepped up when Robert Palmer stepped out of The Power Station, the funk-rock band Andy and John Taylor formed in 1985 when Duran Duran was on hiatus. “He was a tough and rocking guitar player,” writes Des Barres. “He knew what he wanted, and he gave it to us. I was able to learn 22 songs in five days of rehearsal with the Power Station because of Andy‘s tremendous enthusiasm and professionalism. Simply put, he loved rock ‘n’ roll. RIP- My friend.”.

Sources indicate that terri Nunn’s Berlin paid tribute with a lyric to the band’s 1993 comeback classic “Ordinary World,” which featured Warren Cuccurullo on guitar. “Where is the life that I recognise? (Gone away) RIP #AndyTaylor.”.

The Dandy Warhols shared a picture of Courtney Taylor-Taylor in his happy place, with a glass of red in his hand, and Andy Taylor at his side.

Andy Taylor (no relation with John and Roger) had multiple stints in Duran Duran, scoring 13 hits on the Billboard Hot 100, including two No. 1s: “The Reflex” in 1984 and “A View to a Kill” in 1985. The group’s beloved second album Rio album (which housed “Hungry Like the Wolf,” “Save a Prayer,” and the title track) hit the Billboard 200 in 1982 and remained on the chart for a whopping 128 weeks.

As a member of The Power Station, he earned three top 40 hits on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1985, and as a soloist, he scored two entries on the chart: “Take It Easy” (No. 24 in 1986) and “When the Rain Comes Down” (No. 73 in 1986).

Taylor was inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2022 in absentia due to his illness, which his bandmates revealed from the platform of their induction speech for the very first time.

Sources indicate that it was the rocker’s former bandmates who shared the tragic news “Andy was there at the very beginning of our story,” the group wrote on their website and on Instagram. “From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music.”.

The message continued, “His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.”.

It has been revealed that also, Taylor’s wife, Tracey, shared a statement: “To the world, Andy was a brilliant guitarist, but to us, he was the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather,” she wrote. “He was the rock of our family. For all the incredible experiences music brought him, family always came first.”.

Andy is also survived by his children — Georgina, Isabella, Bethany and Andrew — and grandson Albie.
