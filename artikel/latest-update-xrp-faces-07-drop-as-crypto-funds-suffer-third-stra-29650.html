XRP might witness another notable decline before staging a major recovery, according to renowned market analyst Ali Martinez, who identifies $0.78 as a potential bottom.

Even though the short-term outlook remains bearish, the analyst’s long-term picture points to an $18 target if a multi-year chart pattern plays out.

Sources indicate that what’s cooking behind the scenes? Martinez notes that a large ascending triangle has been forming on XRP’s weekly chart since 2017, with resistance at $3.50 and an upward-sloping support trendline presently approaching $0.78.

It has been revealed that with XRP down more than 43% from its October high of $1.55, the analyst believes the ongoing correction could push prices toward this rising support. CoinGecko data reveals that XRP is presently trading at $0.9663.

XRP’s technical setup is unfolding against an increasingly difficult crypto market backdrop. On-chain metrics provider WealthManager pointed out that another $1.065 billion flowed out of crypto investment funds last week, marking three consecutive weeks of outflows.

More importantly, heightened withdrawals point to weakening institutional demand, which could potentially limit the capital inflows needed to fuel a broader cryptocurrency recovery.

According to recent reports, meanwhile, security concerns are mounting within the XRP community. Vet, an XRP Ledger contributor and dUNL validator, recently raised an alarm over increasingly aggressive scam attempts targeting XRP holders.
