Black Friday is entering the evening, which means one of the best times of the year to save some cash on a new pair of AirPods or the latest Apple Watch is coming to a close. After all, the holiday savings can help offset the unfortunate price hikes from earlier this year, when Apple raised the cost of some of its most popular gear. Some discounts still apply to recent releases like the Apple Watch Series 12 and touchscreen MacBook Pro, too, which are available at their lowest prices to date.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to a variety of discounts across retailers, Apple is offering up to $300 in gift cards with select purchases between November 27th and November 30th. How much you get back is determined by how much you spend, with up to $100 available for some iPhone and Apple Watch purchases, and up to $300 for the 16-inch MacBook Pro. That said, in some cases you may be able to save more by buying from other retailers.

Industry observers note that the latest AirPods Pro offer improved noise cancellation and richer bass, along with built-in heart sensors that work with Apple’s Fitness app to monitor calories burned across more than 50 workouts. Read our review.

Beats’ fitness-focused earbuds feature ear hooks designed to stay put during even the most intense workouts as well as built-in heart rate monitoring. They also deliver relatively bass-rich sound for their design, along with solid noise cancellation, wireless charging, and AirPods features like adaptive audio thanks to Apple’s H2 chip. Read our review.

In a recent development, the latest Beats Studio Pro feature improved noise cancellation, sound quality, and comfort. They also support lossless audio over USB-C and offer native support for a range of iOS and Android features like other Beats headphones. Read our review.

According to recent reports, the Beats 360 are ANC headphones for people who prefer headphones over earbuds at the gym. They’re Beats’ first IP-rated cans, with ear cushions that you can easily remove for cleaning (or style points). They also stay in place during most exercises, while still delivering solid bass. Read our review.

It has been revealed that apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review.

Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review.

The Apple Watch Series 12 introduces AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, including sound recognition alerts, Live Rewind, and Siri Recap. It can also track your heart rate every five seconds, even when you’re not exercising, and offers faster charging than prior models. Read our review.

Sources indicate that apple’s latest AirTags feature an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range. Read our review.

Industry observers note that the Baseus PicoGo AM52 is a 10,000mAh magnetic power bank that supports fast 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging and up to 45W wired charging via its built-in USB-C cable. It was one of the fastest wireless chargers we tested, bringing an iPhone 17 Pro Max to 65 percent in an hour. Read our guide to the best Qi2 batteries.

According to recent reports, apple’s 11th-gen iPad offers 128GB of storage — twice that of its predecessor — and runs on a faster A16 chip. It also comes in four colors: blue, pink, yellow, and silver.

Apple’s entry-level stylus attaches magnetically to supported iPads, though it doesn’t support magnetic charging. Instead, you pair and charge it by connecting it to your device’s USB-C port. It includes tilt sensitivity and Apple Pencil Hover support, but it doesn’t offer double-tap gestures or pressure sensitivity.

Industry observers note that the Icemag 3 can wirelessly deliver up to 25W of power to compatible smartphones and up to 35W wired charging via USB-C. The 10,000mAh Qi2 charger can also act as a foldable kickstand, comes with an integrated USB-C cable that serves as a lanyard, and sports a transparent design with colorful RGB lighting.

Designed to pair with a Mac, the latest Apple Studio Display features a sharp 5K Retina display, a great 12-megapixel Center Stage camera that keeps you in frame during video calls, and a good six-speaker sound system with spatial audio. Sadly, it only offers two Thunderbolt 5 ports in addition to a pair of USB-C ports. Read our review.
