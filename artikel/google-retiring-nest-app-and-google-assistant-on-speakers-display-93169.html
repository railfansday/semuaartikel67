Following the Android deprecation in September, Google Assistant is going away on speakers and smart displays next year. Additionally, Google Home is fully replacing the Nest app in January.

The Nest app on Android, iOS, and the web will stop working on January 25, 2027. The Google Home app, which requires a Google Account, will be needed to “continue controlling and receiving notifications from your Nest devices remotely.”.

With home.nest.com going away, you have to download any custom video clips since they will not transfer over to Google Home. A one-click “Download All” button is available.

According to recent reports, otherwise, Nest app consumers do not need to do anything else, with devices automatically transferring over to the Google Home app. No Nest thermostats, cameras, doorbells, smoke alarms, or locks are losing support with this change.

Additionally, Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions will automatically switch to Google Home Premium “with no additional cost and no credit card re-entry required at this time.”.

The other change unveiled today is that Google Assistant on speakers and smart displays is going away on August 2, 2027. It will be replaced by the Gemini for Home voice assistant.
