Youssouf Fofana continues to impress with Sevilla, and AC Milan are already thinking about what to do ahead of next summer.

According to recent reports, as Calciomercato.com write, Fofana put in a solid performance in Saturday’s Seville derby at the Estadio Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán, which finished 1-1. He was positioned alongside Lucien Agoume in the double pivot at first, but as the game went on he took up more of a box-to-box role.

Recent findings suggest that after Antony’s opening goal, Fofana continued to push forward in the second half, coming close to equalising in the 82nd minute. Sevilla did draw level in the 91st minute thanks to a powerful left-footed strike from Kike Salas that Valles failed to hold.
