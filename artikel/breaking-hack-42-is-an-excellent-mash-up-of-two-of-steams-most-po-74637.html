It has been revealed that this is a typing game in which typing words aids you in your efforts to hack into nations’ infrastructures and rob them of their money. The more money you steal, the more unlocks you can buy for your hacking abilities, until eventually youâquite literallyâtake over the world.

Industry observers note that in the hacks themselves you have a network of nodes, each unlocked by accurately typing in their assigned word or words, which opens up more nodes to access. Each time you unlock one you gain cash, but you’re also engaged in a complex game of stamina management. Just the act of typing in letters uses stamina, but unlocking nodes restores some. So of course fucking up is very costly, driving you to be as accurate as possible. Or, if you’re me, entering a shame spiral of discovering you can no longer type the word “SYNTAX” and have entirely forgotten where the X key is on your keyboard, and then becoming panicked that perhaps this is dementia kicking in.

In a recent development, with the money you earn (which is all lost if your stamina runs out before you exit a server), you can unlock and upgrade a ridiculous number of skills, the most important of which improve the sheer power of your typing, making nodes easier to crack. To boost this, you need to engage in a whole second page of typing shenanigans where you release fragments by typing in pairs of letters, which are decoded into cells (pink diamonds). This, too, is an area you can incrementally improve, with the task eventually becoming fully automated and optimized until you’re creating hundreds of cells per second without even trying.

There are a bunch of other bonuses to discover, and each completed nation’s hack unlocks the next, harder challenge. It is, like all incremental games, about the inherent satisfaction of watching numbers go up, and in this case leaping from making a handful of dollars to quintillions. It only lasts about three hours, as is the nature of this genre, but it’s such a satisfying time! The UI is splendid, and the way everything jiggles and reacts as you type is engagingly kinetic. Every word you have to type is nerdy in some respect, including an awful lot of acronyms and annoyingly spelled Linux-adjacent terms, which really catches your fingers out. (I’m fascinated by how when it’s a recognizable word, an entirely different part of my brain kicks in and just types the letters in instantly, but when it’s unfamiliar I suddenly can’t remember which row of the keyboard E lives on.).

It has been revealed that and yeah, it’s kinda dumb. But in a really smart way. And at only $4, it’s the ideal price for taking a chance on something that’s unlike anything else you’ve played.
