Bayern Munich could make a move for the AC Milan target and Club Brugge star Nicolo Tresoldi, following Harry Kane’s injury.

Tresoldi has been making waves at Club Brugge this season, scoring an impressive five goals in the Champions League, and Milan are one of several big clubs that have been linked with him. Of course, those links are mainly fueled by the fact that the striker is a big Milan fan.

As Milan fans know well after Ardon Jashari and Charles De Ketelaere, it’s notoriously difficult to negotiate with Club Brugge. That means a high offer is needed, which is something that could arrive at the Belgian side’s doorstep in January.

According to Gazzetta dello Sport, Bayern Munich have been keeping an eye on Tresoldi for some time and initially viewed him as a potential signing for the 2027-28 season. However, due to Harry Kane’s ankle injury, they could move much earlier.

In a recent development, the Englishman will be out for at least three months, having had surgery this morning, and that should keep him sidelined until February/March. That puts Bayern Munich in a difficult spot, perhaps forcing them to make a move in January.

In a recent development, club Brugge want at least €60m for their star and knowing Bayern Munich’s resources, they would be far more comfortable paying that amount than Milan. In short, Kane’s injury has not only resulted in a headache for the German side, but also for the Rossoneri.
