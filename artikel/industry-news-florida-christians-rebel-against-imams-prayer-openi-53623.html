An imam who was scheduled to deliver the invocation at a city commission meeting Monday evening in a southwest Florida town was interrupted by Christian protesters — who had a prayer of their own to share.

Footage from inside the meeting obtained by Fox News Digital highlights dozens of Christian activists crowded into the back of a Bartow, Florida city commission meeting room. As Imam Sajid Bhuiya of the town's Masjid Al-Nur Mosque stepped up to the lectern to give the invocation, they began reciting the Lord's Prayer in unison. Some chants of "USA!" broke out, as well.

In a recent development, according to Robert Goodman, the southeast regional director of the conservative activist group Citizens Defending Freedom, news of Bhuiya's scheduled appearance began making the rounds on social media last weekend. He was among those who shared the news on social media. Goodman mentioned about 100 people from different groups ended up attending the meeting.
