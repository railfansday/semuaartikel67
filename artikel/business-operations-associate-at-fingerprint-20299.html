Industry observers note that fingerprint empowers enterprises to detect and stop online fraud with the world’s most accurate device intelligence. We lead our industry with bleeding-edge identification capabilities and work on turning new ideas and discoveries in the fraud detection space into reality. Our customers range from innovative startups to leading enterprise companies, including Plaid, Dropbox, and Booking.com.

Sources indicate that fingerprint is a globally dispersed, 100% remote firm. We were named on on the 2026 Forbes Best Startup Employers list and ranked #803 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list of America’s fastest-growing private companies.

We have raised $77M and are backed by Craft Ventures (Tesla, Facebook, Airbnb ), Nexus Venture Partners ( Postman, Apollo.io, MinIO, Druva) and Uncorrelated Ventures ( Redis, Rollbar,  Gradle).

According to recent reports, as a Business Operations Associate, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the strategic direction and growth of our organization. This role reports to the VP of Business Operations and comes with the exciting responsibility of advancing critical strategic initiatives. Business Operations serves as the swiss-army knife of the organization - you’ll work on a diverse set of projects and no two weeks will be the same. You will collaborate extensively with colleagues from various departments, including Product, Engineering, GTM, People, and Finance. This role offers unique visibility into a hyper-growth startup, providing the opportunity to shape its trajectory and make a substantial impact.

High Quality Decision Making & Leadership Alignment.

Process Improvements & Efficient Operations.

At Fingerprint, we believe in pay transparency — it's part of how we operate as a distributed, remote-first team. Salaries are based on function, level, and geographic location, benchmarked against similar stage growth companies.

Because this role is open across the United States and can be based anywhere our team members work from, the salary range reflects that geographic breadth: $92,000 - $125,000.

Some example salaries for specific locations are below:.

LA, NY, Boston: $109,000 - $125,000.

Chicago, Dallas: $92,000 - $106,000.

Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors, including the successful candidate's location, experience, and expertise, and may vary from the amounts listed above.

Recent findings suggest that due to regulatory and security reasons, there’s a small number of countries where we cannot have Fingerprint teammates based. Additionally, because Fingerprint is an all-remote manufacturer and people can join our workforce from almost any country, we do not sponsor visas. Fingerprint teammates need to be authorized to work from their home location.

It has been revealed that we are dedicated to creating an inclusive work environment for everyone. We embrace and celebrate the unique experiences, perspectives and cultural backgrounds that each employee brings to our workplace. Fingerprint strives to foster an environment where our employees feel respected, valued and empowered, and our team members are at the forefront in helping us promote and sustain an inclusive workplace. We highly encourage people from underrepresented groups in tech to apply.
