‘It’s a real joy’ – Florian Wirtz on playing for Germany

Sources indicate that florian Wirtz continued his good form for the German national team with a brace against the Netherlands. After the game, the Liverpool man mentioned he and his teammates always want to make fans happy with their performance.

According to recent reports, “The first half still involved quite a bit of hard work and a lot of running. In the second half, things got easier because we had more space. That’s when we really started enjoying ourselves,” he said after the game. “We always want to score goals and dominate our opponents. We wanted to keep pushing forward and score more goals. And I think we did that perfectly.”.

Wirtz also stated that the team wants to build on the win and improve in the new year. “I would definitely say it’s a fitting end to the year, and we’re very happy with it. We want to build on this next year. We want to keep improving. I think we’ve definitely taken some good steps forward in our development as a team. I’m very proud and happy to be part of this team. It’s a real joy,” he added.

Jürgen Klopp applauded Wirtz’s performance and labelled him an extraordinary footballer. The coach also indicated that they have managed to find the right position for him. This is indicated by how Wirtz seems to be thriving under Klopp’s leadership.
