📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that dolly Parton’s final recording is making its mark across multiple Billboard charts, as her collaboration with U2, “Torn,” arrives on country and rock rankings (dated Nov. 28), while standing as one of the top-selling songs in the United States.

The song becomes the legend’s first posthumous entry on Hot Country Songs as it debuts at No. 39 with 1.6 million official streams, 1.2 million in radio airplay audience and 6,000 sold Nov. 13-19, according to Luminate. It marks U2’s first appearance on the ranking.

(Parton first made Billboard’s charts in 1967; U2, in 1981.).

Recent findings suggest that on the Nov. 21-dated Country Airplay chart, “Torn” opened at No. 28, securing Parton her highest-ever debut and introducing U2 to the survey.

Meanwhile, “Torn” enters Hot Rock & Alternative Songs at No. 24, becoming Parton’s second and top-charted hit on the tally, surpassing “World on Fire” (No. 26, 2023). The collaboration also debuts at No. 2 on Billboard’s all-genre Digital Song Sales chart.

Sources indicate that the track closes U2’s Carnaval De Luz, which debuts at No. 4 on the Billboard 200 with 49,000 equivalent album units, becoming the band’s 14th top 10. The set also opens at No. 1 on Top Rock Albums, Vinyl Albums and Indie Store Album Sales and No. 2 on Top Album Sales, Top Rock & Alternative Albums and Top Alternative Albums.

Produced by longtime U2 collaborator Jacknife Lee, the 12-track collection is the band’s first album of fresh material in nine years and its 16th studio LP.

Recent findings suggest that the team-up took shape when Parton offered U2 an unfinished song of her own. U2 frontman Bono and Snow Patrol’s Johnny McDaid subsequently reworked Parton’s original tale of a woman torn between two men into the story of a mother and her two estranged sons, divided by political differences.

In a recent development, “Torn” is the last song that Parton recorded before her death Aug. 25 at age 80. She contributed her vocals in Nashville in June while battling illness.
