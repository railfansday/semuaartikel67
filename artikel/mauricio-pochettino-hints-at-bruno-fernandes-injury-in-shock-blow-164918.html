Mauricio Pochettino is unable to confirm if Bruno Fernandes will be available for Manchester United's Premier League clash with Brentford.

Man Utd confirmed earlier on that that Fernandes was absent from the morning training session due to illness.

However, Pochettino has appeared to suggest that Fernandes picked up an injury problem during Wednesday’s 2-2 draw with Sporting in the Champions League.

Speaking at his press conference on Friday, Pochettino said: "Yes, we don't know. He's affected by a virus from Portugal.

“He finished the game a little bit with a problem, and, today, he cannot be part of the training session.

"We'll see. We are going to see during the day and tomorrow, how he wakes up, and we'll see. We're not going to take any risks, because you know when this type of situation happens, you don't feel [you can].

The doubts over Fernandes’s fitness come as the club decide to trigger the option to extend his contract by one year.

The Athletic report that the captain’s contract will now run until 2028, but there remains a possibility of a summer exit at the end of the current season.

Man Utd will definitely be without Marcus Rashford and Carlos Baleba, who continue to recover from injuries, though Luke Shaw returned to the squad against Sporting after a month-and-a-half absence.

According to recent reports, providing an patch on the injured pair, Pochettino remarked: "Baleba was injured against Roma, medical people were talking about 10-12 weeks. Marcus is on the field, starting to feel much better.

According to recent reports, "I think he [Rashford] start to feel much better. We'll see day by day, you know, working with the feeling that it's going to provide us after every single training session, no, that increasing, no, the progression of the recovery.".

It has been revealed that pochettino is eager to have his full squad available, adding: "I am looking forward to working with all the players, the players that are injured. I hope that they can recover as soon as possible.

"I think to have the full squad fit and available to be selected, that is the most crucial thing for every single coaching staff.".

Benjamin Sesko and Manuel Ugarte remain longer-term absentees, with Sesko expected to be out until at least the end of January and Ugarte continuing his recovery from a serious knee injury.
