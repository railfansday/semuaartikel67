Upside-Down Apple Cider Cheesecake

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

If you feel there’s never enough graham cracker crust with your cheesecake, then this version is for you. The entire no-bake cheesecake is surrounded by crust, so there are buttery graham crackers and creamy cheesecake in every single bite. The exterior isn’t just hiding a silky-smooth cheesecake filling, but also a surprise syrupy apple filling. If you want to win the holidays this year, set this unassuming dessert on the table and watch everyone ooh and ahh as they cut in and discover the sweet, spiced, and completely delicious treat hiding inside.

In a recent development, how to make it upside-down:To achieve a double layer of crust, you need to freeze one layer so that it’s easy to place on top of the cheesecake. Before serving, you’ll flip the whole thing onto a plate, which puts the seam on the bottom and gives you a beautiful display of a solid crust. It’s easier than you think to flip it, and the parchment will ensure nothing sticks to the springform pan.

Make ahead & storage:I love to make this dessert during the holidays because you can prep and make it all ahead of time. The apple filling can be made up to 3 days ahead, and the assembled cheesecake can be made up to 2 days ahead of when you want to serve.
