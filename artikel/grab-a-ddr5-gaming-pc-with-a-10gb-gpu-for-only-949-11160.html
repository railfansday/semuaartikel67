For 1080p gaming, 10GB of VRAM is a good amount to handle the textures and shaders on high and ultra settings. However, on higher settings it will start to struggle in 1440p gaming when that VRAM gets swallowed up and starts using the CPU to compensate.

Thanks to the skyrocketing price of memory and storage, buying a prebuilt gaming PC is now a much cheaper way to own your own gaming PC rather than trying to construct your own.
