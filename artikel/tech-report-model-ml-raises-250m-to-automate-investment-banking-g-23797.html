📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, model ML, a London-based startup that uses AI to automate work traditionally performed by junior bankers and consultants, including building financial models, preparing pitch decks, and writing due diligence reports, has raised $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. The new round, which comes a year after its $75 million Series A round was announced, was co-led by General Atlantic and earlier backer QED Investors.

Brothers Chaz and Arnie Englander co-founded the three-year-old startup after previously co-founding two other companies, including Fat Llama, a peer-to-peer rental marketplace acquired by Swedish rival Hygglo for $41.5 million in 2022.

Sources indicate that model ML develops AI agents that integrate with clients’ systems, data, and tools, including Excel and PowerPoint. The startup indicates its agents understand the full context of deals that bankers and consultants are working on and can automatically patch financial models and presentations when upcoming developments affect their analysis.

Other startups automating the routine, repetitive tasks for investment bankers and management consultants include Hebbia and Rogo, which raised $160 million at a $2 billion valuation in April.

Anthropic and OpenAI have also built tools for financial professionals, but Model ML claims it runs on open source models and that doing so allows it to operate at about 2% of the cost of frontier models while delivering faster results.
