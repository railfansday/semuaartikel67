In a recent development, no shade to those who love their subscription services, but viewing physical movies is a time-honored tradition that shouldn’t go away. Watching them doesn’t feel all that different, but deciding which movie to watch feels less annoying; sifting through a movie collection on your shelf or in a storage binder is so much more fun (and less overwhelming) than scrolling endlessly in apps.
