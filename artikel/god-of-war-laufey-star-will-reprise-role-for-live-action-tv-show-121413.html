God of War Laufey’s lead Deborah Ann Woll will reprise her role as Kratos’ wife Faye in Amazon Prime’s upcoming live-action TV adaptation.

The actress – who also played the character in God of War Ragnarok – will star opposite Dave Bautista’s Kratos in the glitzy show.

The re-telling should end up feeling quite familiar, as Alastair Duncan will also return as Mimir.

Fans of the franchise will know Woll dies at the start of the story, kickstarting Kratos’ quest as he and son Atreus embark on a journey to scatter her ashes.

But we now know, during that period, Faye is trapped in a kind of afterlife for the gods known as the Everywhen. That’s where God of War Laufey will take place.

It’ll be interesting to see whether the TV show pulls in elements from the upcoming PS5 game, or if Woll’s role here will be to show Kratos’ family life prior to the events of the main game.

There’s no release date for the show just yet, but filming restarted in October with Baustita in the lead role, after previous actor Ryan Hurst was recast due to sustaining an injury on set.

Woll is an accomplished actress and knows the character inside-out, so this casting makes perfect sense to me.
