📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The number of texts, DMs, WhatsApp messages, and Slack chats I receive asking for beauty recommendations only skyrockets around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. For better or worse, I’ve become the resident beauty expert among my friends and family, and it seems everywhere I turn, someone has an inquiry about what to buy (especially when it’s deals on the line).

In a recent development, gone are the days of lining up outside mega retailers right after your turkey dinner to rush in store and snag early-bird holiday gifts. As an introvert, I’d never endure a line or consumerism chaos in pursuit of any deal, ever. But for those who are sale-insatiable, online shopping requires no change out of your post-Thanksgiving sweats (or leaving the couch). Believe it or not, Thanksgiving is less than a week away—and yes, the early deals are already starting to appear. Of course, holiday shopping season is also good time to stock up the best deals for skincare products, coveted gift sets, and more from your favorite beauty brands. Below is a tight curation of the best early black Friday deals, so far.

Sources indicate that believe it or not, there’s another shopping platform to peruse—say hello to Shop, the consumer-facing sister of Shopify. Everything from those buzzy direct-to-consumer brands to K-beauty favorites is in one place, with no tab-hopping. What to buy? Take Beauty of Joseon’s brightening face mask, or Merit’s non-sticky lip oil—you can add both to cart right now.

In a recent development, the “everything store” seems to have deals on everything—and sometimes that means sorting through bizarre, random products to find what you’re actually looking for. Amazon is the place to go for almost any annual, semi-annual, or ad hoc sale, from Prime Day to Cyber Monday and more. For a skin-smoothing face mask, Biodance’s bio-collagen mask is a viral option. Meanwhile, Bioderma’s micellar water is a French pharmacy favorite.

Sources indicate that something about Nordstrom brings up nostalgic memories of wandering into a department store with my mom (and inevitably, being a child, wanting to go home). But there’s no need to peruse between departments IRL. Nordstrom’s virtual sale is notoriously good each year—especially for beauty. There’s prestige skin-care brand Joanna Czech (from the mastermind of the famed aesthetician), with their hydrating duo that Vogue’s Kiana Murden swears by on sale for a steal. Plus, tried-and-true makeup brushes from Sigma, for those looking to szoosh up the makeup tools.

Beauty is Bluemercury’s specialty—offering everything from wellness essentials and makeup deals to premium moisturizers, viral mascaras, and even the brand’s coveted annual Advent calendar. While the deals are just starting to roll in, there’s plenty more to come. Right now, the Holiday Shopping Party is in full swing. Cross $200 and the whole order drops 20%, cross $800 and the savings climbs to 25%, and if you’re a substantial spender, cross $1,500 and you’ll get 30% off.

One of the main beauty mega-retailers stateside is Ulta Beauty—a wonderland of beauty, everything from affordable to prestige prices in just about every sub-category.

Industry observers note that credo—with its meticulously vetted products that marry safety, sustainability, and performance—is the go-to destination for the clean beauty buff. Take Sakara’s probiotic and collagen supplements, designed to improve your skin health from the inside out. Then there’s Three Ships’ hydrating serum, which uses tremella mushroom as a botanically derived substitute for hyaluronic acid.

While you’re filling your cart with trending clothing and accessories at Revolve, it’s well worth taking a peek at the beauty deals, too. The selection might surprise you. Those aiming to make their living space cozier might like Vitruvi’s essential oils kit—add a few drops to your diffuser. Plus, there are plenty of hair accessories, like Lele Sadoughi’s big claw clip for those looking to jazz up their next updo.

For the minimalist beauty lover, Merit’s annual cyber sale is a can’t-miss moment—it’s quite literally the brand’s only sale of the year. The deals have already started (and will last through Cyber Monday), offering up to 44% off sitewide. Those who dread taking off their makeup after a long day will appreciate Merit’s Great Skin Double Cleanse—the foaming formula works double duty to remove makeup and cleanse skin all in one go. Meanwhile, the brand’s no-liner lip liner offers a soft, contour-like definition that enhances lips without harsh lines.

Right now, you can score up to 25% off everything—makeup, skincare, fragrance, even merch at Glossier. As for scents, the brand’s beloved You eau de parfum remains the ultimate skin fragrance: warm, intimate, and entirely addictive. Then there’s Plush Blush, the cushioned, powdery evolution of the beloved original liquid Cloud Paint; it blends like a dream, wears like a whisper, and looks almost good enough to eat.

It has been revealed that speaking of clean beauty powerhouses, Sasha Plavsic released Ilia 14 years ago with a mission to create skin-first formulas that perform. The brand has since become a staple for beauty insiders, offering everything from milky toners (a favorite of Vogue’s beauty editor Kiana Murden) to serum-like skin tints with SPF and natural-looking lip liners. In short, Ilia has something for everyone. Right now, the brand is offering 20% off sitewide, with surely more deals ahead in the coming days.
