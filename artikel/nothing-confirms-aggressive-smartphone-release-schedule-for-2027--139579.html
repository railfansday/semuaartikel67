Alongside a teaser for the new Ear (note) recorder, Nothing CEO Carl Pei has confirmed the firm’s aims for a major 2027 in terms of smartphone releases, as well as a focus on the US market.

Speaking to Bloomberg, Carl Pei says that Nothing currently has “tens of millions of devices in use,” and that there are major plans for 2027.

This includes “more attention” to the US market for Nothing, with the US being Nothing’s fastest-growing market for audio devices. In 2026, Nothing introduced its Ear (3a) earbuds, multiple CMF-branded devices, and two pairs of over-ear headphones including its high-end Headphone (1) Pro in September, all of which are sold in the US (and on sale during Black Friday).

Pei further noted that Nothing would have a more “aggressive” smartphone release schedule in 2027. Previously, it was said that Nothing would launch six latest smartphones next year, following just three new smartphones in 2026.

In a recent development, pei noted Nothing now has “tens of millions of devices in use” across its smartphones, earbuds and other products. The company is also planning to devote more attention to the US market, while pursuing a more aggressive smartphone release schedule next year than it did in 2026.

The tech giant noted it has generated more than $2 billion in revenue since its first product release in 2022 and that the US is its fastest growing market for audio gear.

According to recent reports, nothing doesn’t have a strict schedule for upcoming product rolls out, but Nothing Phone (4a) debuted in March 2026, with Phone (3) launching in the Summer of the year prior. With six new devices supposedly in the works, it’s anyone’s guess as to when they might release.
