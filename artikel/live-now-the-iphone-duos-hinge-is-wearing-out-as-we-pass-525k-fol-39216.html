Industry observers note that as we continue our fourth week of folding Apple’s iPhone Duo in our custom Fold-a-Phone machine, the $1,999 foldable phone has surpassed 525,000 folds and it’s showing. While the Duo is still capable of opening and closing, on our Tuesday livestream you can see that the phone is having difficulty staying closed. Our testing does continue, but we are closely monitoring the Duo’s hinge as the folding stress continues to add up.

Recent findings suggest that you can watch as we continue folding the iPhone Duo on our YouTube channel, embedded above. At our CNET Labs location in Louisville, Kentucky, this robust custom-built machine has been opening and closing the foldable phone around 20,000 times per day to simulate years of wear and tear on the hinge.

The livestream isn’t just for watching a phone open and close — you’ll also be able to chat with CNET editors who will be available to answer your iPhone Duo questions live in the chat.
