Thanksgiving weekend is the perfect time to catch up on your favorite shows — or discover some newly introduced quality TV.

Netflix has a few highly anticipated reveals that are worth rewatching before they return with latest episodes later this year. From The Hunting Wives and Emily in Paris to Selling Sunset, TV fans will be kept well-fed this holiday season.

Industry observers note that there are also some hidden gems — such as The Real Housewives of London — that are finally available to stream. And for music fans, Jennifer Lopez puts on a show in her concert special, which premieres on CBS Thursday, November 26.

Keep scrolling for Us’ top picks to watch this Thanksgiving weekend:.

Industry observers note that the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives is the gift that keeps on giving with the OC spinoff introducing Us to a latest group of saints and sinners. Season 1 focused on Jen Affleck and her influencer friends in California as they pulled back the curtain on their lives.

It has been revealed that guns, murder, sex and chaos — what more could you ask for? After an exceptional first season, The Hunting Wives is officially back, and since May Cobb only wrote one book, it remains to be seen which direction the new episodes will go in.

According to recent reports, meghann Fahy has done it again with her performance in the TV adaptation of Karin Slaughter‘s 2017 thriller novel, which follows sisters who must once again contend with a traumatic and violent event from their past when a new crime hits their small town. Rose Byrne and Brendan Gleeson join Fahy as part of the Quinn family.

Grey’s Anatomy fans in need of more Sarah Drew on their screens should turn to Mistletoe Murders, in which she owns a year-round Christmas store — and also finds time to help solve local crimes.

Emily (Lily Collins) brings Us on one last trip to Paris when the show returns December 24 — but for now, we will be reliving her adventures abroad while waiting to see whether season 6 will finally bring her and Gabriel (Lucas Bravo) back together.

Apple TV officially picked up the fan favorite for season 5, so there is no excuse not to check in with Ted Lasso, which returned earlier this year after a lengthy hiatus.

Phony is all about the journey instead of the destination as viewers tune in week after week to see whether Sonny’s (Sam Nivola) mother, Ellen (Connie Britton), is an imposter. Thanksgiving weekend is the perfect time to catch up before the December 1 finale.

According to recent reports, run, don’t walk to Peacock to check out the full second season of RHOLDN. All six original cast members — Juliet Angus, Amanda Cronin, Karen Loderick-Peace, Juliet Mayhew, Panthea Parker and Nessie Welschinger — returned amid various tensions within their friend group.

Whether you had tickets to Lopez’s Las Vegas residency — or if you just watched over social media — CBS is giving you the chance to relive the best moments in a concert special.

For those fans who aren’t yet ready to leave the Halloween season behind, The Body is the ideal fix with initiation rituals, religious hysteria and more.

Netflix’s hit real estate show will look a little different when it returns December 4 following several shocking cast exits. Luckily, fans have some time to catch up with the first nine seasons before meeting the next generation of stylish (and messy) realtors.

The Thanksgiving episode alone is enough to convince Us to rewatch Line of Fire — a.k.a Gossip Girl with more politics.

Stephen Graham will keep you up at night after his performance (opposite Liev Schreiber) as a dangerous serial killer still finding ways to terrorize a small town while behind bars.

CBS has a timely hit show on their hands with Cupertino, which hails from Robert King and Michelle King and follows a law firm representing people harmed by the technology industry.
