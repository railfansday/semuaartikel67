It has been revealed that traveling to newly introduced places is good for the soul, but then there’s jet lag, which can cause fatigue, headaches, insomnia and even nausea during your trip. Oura, maker of the Oura Ring, wants to help you combat these effects with its latest feature, Jet Lag Plan, which debuted on Tuesday.

A press release explained that Jet Lag Plan was developed using a March 2025 study conducted by the National University of Singapore. The study analyzed de-identified data covering 1.5 million nights of sleep from over 57,000 Oura members to determine how sleep shifted before and after about 65,000 long-distance trips.

Jet Lag Plan is specifically designed for customers who travel across at least three time zones and is based on typical sleep patterns, flight itinerary and chronotype. The latter describes whether you’re a morning person, a night person or somewhere in between.

Oura uses 40 to 90 days of sleep timing and activity and average body temperature trends to assign customers one of six chronotypes: early morning type, morning type, late morning type, early evening type, evening type and late evening type. This can change over time.

In a recent development, both your chronotype and sleep history also inform your circadian low point. Usually around 4 a.m., this is when your body temperature is at its lowest and you’re the most ready for sleep.

In the Oura app, you can start a Jet Lag Plan in one of four ways: by adding travel from the Today tab, selecting “Jet Lag” from the app menu, asking the AI-powered Oura Advisor to create a plan for your upcoming trip or responding to a prompt when Oura detects that your phone has changed time zones.

In addition to your flight details, you’ll also want to share the reason for your trip, as a work-related Jet Lag Plan will differ from a vacation one.

Your Jet Lag Proposal will then populate with a recommended schedule for sleep, caffeine, activity and light exposure before, during and after your trip, with reminders and the ability to mark actions as complete. You can request adjustments to this plan using Oura Advisor.

During your trip, you’ll be able to compare your readiness score to the typical score for similar trips. This is based on data from 10 million trips already taken by Oura members.

Once your trip ends, you’ll receive a report comparing your sleep and readiness scores before and during your trip.

Jet Lag Blueprint is now available to Oura members whose language is set to English. It works with an Oura Ring 3 or newer on both Android and iOS.

The latest Oura Ring 5 starts at $399, while a membership is $6 per month or $70 per year.
