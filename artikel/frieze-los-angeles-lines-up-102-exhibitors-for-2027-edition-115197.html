Frieze Los Angeles has named the 102 exhibitors that will participate in its upcoming 2027 edition, scheduled to run February 25–28 at the Santa Monica Airport.

In a recent development, the line-up includes several returning blue-chip galleries like Gagosian, Hauser & Wirth, Pace Gallery, David Zwirner, White Cube, Lisson, Gladstone, Sprüth Magers, and Thaddaeus Ropac. Galleries returning to the fair after an absence include Regen Projects, Luis De Jesus Los Angeles, Mariane Ibrahim, and Petzel.

Though several LA-based galleries and LA outposts for larger galleries have closed in the past year and a half, Frieze LA will feature 38 exhibitors with spaces in the City of Angels. They include Commonwealth and Council, Vielmetter, Marc Selwyn Fine Art, Charlie James, Night Gallery, Parker Gallery, Roberts Projects, and Make Room, which has been promoted to the main Galleries section. “Los Angeles has many centers, each with its own history and communities, and you feel that in the work artists make here: the personal histories they draw on and the questions they’re asking about belonging and who public space is for,” Frieze Americas director Christine Messineo said in a statement. “For the city’s galleries, this is their hometown fair, a moment for the whole LA art world to show the work being made now, by artists across generations. What I love is that visitors from around the world get to experience this work right where it comes from.”.

The fair will feature 22 first-time exhibitors, with 10 of them coming from the Focus section, for emerging galleries, which will be organized by Hammer curator Erin Christovale. They consist of four LA galleries, including Rajiv Menon Contemporary and House of Seiko, and six Latest York galleries, including Entrance, King’s Leap, and Gladwell Projects. Galleries making their debut in the main section include Gallery Wendi Norris, G Gallery, and Yehudi Hollander-Pappi.

Industry observers note that at the fair’s entrance, visitors will be met with LA-based artist rafa esparza’s Queercancha, which will take the form of two painted adobe panels supported by a steel armature showing a scene of queer Latinxs cruising in a lowrider in the 1990s. The commission comes shortly after Hauser & Wirth confirmed its representation of esparza earlier this month. Additionally, his painting WeDGAF (2026) will be the main marketing image for the 2027 fair, appearing on billboards and digital screens in partnership with Orange Barrel Media, the organization founded by ARTnews Top 200 Collector Pete Scantland.

“Every city brings something distinct to our network,” Frieze executive director of fairs Kristell Chadé said in a statement. “In Los Angeles, it’s how closely the visual arts sit alongside the city’s other creative industries. The fair is an opportunity for galleries and artists to connect with a collecting community that reaches into film, music, design and technology.”.
