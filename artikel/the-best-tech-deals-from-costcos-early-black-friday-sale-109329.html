Sam's Club, Walmart, Amazon, and other retailers have been churning out early Black Friday deals since last week, so if you haven't been paying attention, you've already missed out on some good deals. But all hope is not lost: Costco recently started its own early sale, so you're still in time to find something you want at a low price. Below, I've rounded up my favorite tech deals from their sale—but keep in mind you'll need a membership to take advantage of these deals.

A powerful monitor with high-end specs can make gaming more immersive and greatly reduce input lag, which is helpful for any serious gamer looking for an edge over their opponents. The Samsung Odyssey G9 is a 49-inch gaming monitor with some impressive specs, including a 240Hz refresh rate and a 1 ms response time. It's available for $699.99 (originally $899.99), $200 off and the lowest price it has reached this year.

Sources indicate that if you're looking for a more premium (and far more massive) gaming monitor, the 57-inch Samsung Odyssey Neo G9, one of the best ultra-wide gaming monitors on the market, is presently on sale for $1,449.99 ($2,499.99 at launch). This monitor came out in 2023, earning an "excellent" review from PCMag for its impressive 7,680 x 2,160 4K resolution, its great color range and accuracy, a 240Hz refresh rate, 1,000 nits of brightness, and its overall design. To make full use of its powerful 240Hz refresh rate, you'll need to use DisplayPort 2.1, or you'll be limited to 120Hz. You'll also need a powerful graphics card. But if you have the right setup, this thing really delivers.

According to recent reports, the 15.3-inch Lenovo Legion is a powerful gaming laptop with premium specs, and with the current $600 discount, it's down to $1999.99 (originally $2,599.99). It has a powerful AMD Ryzen AI 9 processor, 32 GB of RAM, a GeForce RTX 5070 graphics card, and 1TB of SSD. PCMag gave the more affordable 5i version an excellent review for its price and performance, so if you have the cash for it, this one is even better.

Recent findings suggest that A soundbar is a great way to improve a home entertainment system without spending too much money. This 5.1 TCL Q6CH setup is right now $199.99 (originally $299.99), including a soundbar and subwoofer. This soundbar came out in 2024 and supports Dolby Atmos and DTS:X for that 3D sound, as well as AI Sonic, a tool that tunes your sound settings based on the setup of your room.

In a recent development, the latest AirPods Pro are a big step up from their previous generation. The third iteration of Apple's iconic earbuds deliver a number of quality-of-life and software improvements that make them worth the typical premium over the AirPods Pro 2—and right now, the AirPods Pro 3 are even cheaper than the previous generation: They're $179 at Costco (originally $249) in an early Black Friday deal.

In a recent development, the 15-inch MacBook Air is down to $1,249 (originally $1,499), the lowest price since Apple hiked prices this past summer. The M5 MacBook Air is the best laptop for most people looking for a newly introduced machine in 2026. In comparison to the already excellent M4 Air, the M5 base model has double the storage, and the powerful M5 chip makes the performance even smoother, according to Mashable's review. The aluminum design is as good as you'd expect from Apple's most popular portable laptop, and it includes a Liquid Retina display, Touch ID, a 12MP Center Stage Camera, and the Magic Keyboard.

According to recent reports, the 15.6-inch Acer Aspire Go is a great value at its current $499.99 price (originally $699.99). The display is a touchscreen; it sports an AMD Ryzen 5 processor and it has 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage. These aren't specs you normally find at this price, making it a great laptop for anyone looking to stretch their money as far as possible while getting a workhorse of a laptop.

I'm a fan of TCL TVs, and currently own the "non-pro" version of this TV. The 65-inch QM6K Pro is $599.99 (originally $999.99) and has the same internal hardware and Mini-LED panel as the regular QM6K, but it adds a CrystGlow anti-reflective coating that makes it more viewable in well-lit rooms and helps maintain a high picture contrast. The 144 Hz native refresh rate makes gaming smooth, and support for Dolby Vision IQ, HDR10+, HDR10, and HLG means you're well positioned to get the best sound quality when streaming movies or indicates.

Sources indicate that nowadays, both large retailers and small businesses compete for Black Friday shoppers, so you can expect practically every store to run sales through Monday, November 30, 2026. The “best” sales depend on your needs, but in general, the biggest discounts tend to come from larger retailers who can afford lower prices: think places like Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and Home Depot. You can find all the best sales from major retailers on our Black Friday coverage.

Sources indicate that yes, Apple participates in Black Friday, though you may want to compare their sales with other retailers like Best Buy and Walmart. So far, the best Black Friday sale on an Apple product is the M5 MacBook Air on sale for $200 off, the lowest price since the price hike this summer.

Recent findings suggest that in short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.
