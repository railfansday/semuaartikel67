Macys Thanksgiving Day Parade postponed for the first time in history

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not be happening on Thanksgiving. Yes, you read that correctly.

The annual event, which is celebrating its 100th anniversary this year, has been rescheduled for Saturday, Nov. 28, due to severe weather.

Industry observers note that according to the Weather Channel, New York City is bracing for a nor'easter on Thanksgiving Day. Before the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was officially rescheduled, officials discussed how the parade could continue. After a 1997 incident where a balloon in the parade crashed into a lamppost and critically injured a bystander, New York City rules state that the parade balloons cannot fly in weather with "sustained winds of 23 mph or gusts of 34 mph.".

The parade has never been postponed before. It has been canceled only three times before, from 1942 to 1944, due to World War II. Balloons in the parade were last grounded 55 years ago, in 1971, due to severe rain and winds.

The annual tradition is televised nationwide on NBC and its affiliates every Thanksgiving.

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration," Macy’s said in a statement on Wednesday. “Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.".

The Macy's Thanksgiving Parade is now scheduled for 8:30 a.m. ET on Saturday.
