📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

At long last, GTA 6 is finally here, and wouldn't you believe it – not only is it rather good, but it's also pretty popular. That wasn't unexpected, of course, and if one market intelligence and analyst firm's launch-week prediction proves accurate, Rockstar could be on track to earn more than the biggest Star Wars, Lord of the Rings, and Harry Potter movies combined.

This is an observation highlighted by Gearbox boss Randy Pitchford, who writes on Twitter: "Newzoo's forecast is that GTA VI will generate more in its launch week than the entire original theatrical runs of the most successful Harry Potter, Lord of the Rings, Star Wars movies combined.".

Recent findings suggest that he appears to be citing Newzoo's July estimate, following the game's pre-orders going live. At the time, it was predicted that GTA 6 could earn between $3.3 billion and $5.2 billion in total sales by the end of its launch week.

It has been revealed that rockstar hasn't released any official data yet, but it's clear that GTA 6 is off to an exceptional start. Before launch, Sony and Microsoft confirmed that it was the most pre-ordered game of all time on their respective digital stores, and market intelligence firm Sensor Tower estimates that, as of yesterday, it had sold 17.07 million units and brought in $1.65 billion in revenue.

According to recent reports, so, how does that compare to the movies Pitchford highlights? Per Box Office Mojo, the single most successful movies (by lifetime box office revenue) from each franchise are Star Wars: The Force Awakens, The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King, and Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows Part 2. The worldwide lifetime gross revenue for each of those comes in at around $2.07 billion, $1.12 billion, and $1.34 billion, respectively, for a combined total of about $4.53 billion.

Sources indicate that if Sensor Tower's most recent estimate is correct, then GTA 6 hasn't reached this high just yet, but we're only just on launch day – Newzoo was allowing for the full week in its previous forecast. And, if the analytics firm ends up being correct, then it could be on track to earn over half a billion dollars more than those movies in its first days, which is wild.

With 10/10 GTA 6 reviews almost across the board, though, it's a deserved result for Rockstar. We'll just have to wait and see how high that number can go.
