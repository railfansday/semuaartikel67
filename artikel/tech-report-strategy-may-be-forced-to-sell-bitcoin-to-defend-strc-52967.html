Recent findings suggest that bitcoin critic Peter Schiff has once again predicted that Strategy will be forced to sell its Bitcoin stash to defend the unrealistic yields of its controversial STRC stock. Schiff, known for his crypto-skeptic tweets, has again said STRC is under pressure after total BTC holdings plummeted more than $10 billion in value following the latest market squeeze.

In a recent development, schiff highlighted that even with the latest buyback programs, STRC is likely to face further pressure and, eventually, Strategy will be forced to burn through its cash reserves, including USDT and then Bitcoin itself. The only alternative, he suggests, is to raise or suspend the dividend, which will tank the stock price and send the whole Saylor initiative further down the abyss.

Sources indicate that STRC is a variable-yield perpetual preferred stock issued by Strategy, which is the largest corporate Bitcoin holder. The stock was used to raise funds to fuel Saylor’s aggressive BTC buying strategy. The shares are designed to trade near $100, with monthly adjustments based on their dividend rate, which is presently as high as 12% and paid daily.

Industry observers note that while Bitcoin proponents initially welcomed Saylor’s flamboyant approach, they have generally become skeptical of the complex offering because of its risk and complexity. CryptoQuant CEO Ki Young Ju, among others, has criticized it over the years. He believes that Saylor has made a habit of buying the digital currency at relative highs, putting STRC under further pressure. It has become a running joke that whenever Saylor buys, it marks a relative top and a sign for short-term traders to sell.

Recent Strategy buys confirm this unfortunate Saylor trend of buying BTC at its relative highs. It is not Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) by any stretch of imagination, as STRC comes under pressure with each dip, and Saylor simply doesn’t have enough funds to purchase the crypto at that time.

Industry observers note that so, Schiff is by no means an isolated critic of the STRC stock. The move has invited criticism from the industry’s own players. However, Schiff is connecting STRC directly to BTC, even though it is a corporate decision and has no relation to the decentralized cryptocurrency’s ecosystem. He has predicted the fall of the top cryptocurrency by market capitalization for most of the last 13 years. However, this time he could be proven right, as BTC remains in relative free fall, and with each dip, STRC drops into deeper trouble.
