Chex Just Turned Everyone's Favorite Childhood Sandwich Into Breakfast Cereal

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Growing up, you may have packed a peanut butter and jelly sandwich for lunch, but now those classic flavors are making their way into your breakfast bowl. General Mills is launching a whole new lineup of cereals and granolas, and one in particular stands out: Chex Peanut Butter & Jelly Cereal.

Giving an old favorite a fresh twist, Chex's latest creation combines strawberry- and peanut butter-flavored pieces in one cereal box. It's everything you love about the classic sandwich, minus the bread. And Chex isn't alone in experimenting with the combination, as Reese's Puffs released its own PB&J cereal earlier this year.

The new Chex cereal is already rolling out exclusively at Walmart, with nationwide availability expected in January 2027. But fans aren't wasting any time, with many eager to stock up. "I will absolutely be on the look out for these," one person commented on a Facebook post.

"I don't eat cereal but. Yes," another admitted. Someone else was already thinking beyond breakfast: "Bet these will make a great batch of muddy buddies!".

In a recent development, and who says you even need milk? "Probably eat these straight from the box 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥," one person wrote. Another declared, "Cannot wait. I'm sold on anything PB&J.".

It has been revealed that even if PB&J isn't your jam (get it?), General Mills has other additions on the way, including Ghost Protein Cereal x Trix and Annie's Protein Cereal in Honey and Berry Vanilla flavors. Just judging by that lineup alone, 2027 is shaping up to be a pretty protein-packed year in the cereal aisle.
