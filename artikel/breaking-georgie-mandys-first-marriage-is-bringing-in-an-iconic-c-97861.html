According to recent reports, georgie & Mandy's First Marriage has been in more than a few seasoned actors during its run. Now, for Season 3, the producers have recruited a veteran from one of the best sitcoms of all time, and I couldn't be happier with this casting decision. Cheers actor John Ratzenberger will join the fun in an upcoming episode amid the 2026 TV schedule, and I'm hoping the door is open for future appearances.

According to recent reports, according to TV Insider, Ratzenberger will join the CBS series during its December 10th episode, which will air on the network and be available to those with a Paramount+ subscription on streaming. Known for his role as mailman Cliff Clavlin on Cheers, Ratzenberger plays a World War II veteran named Davey, who crosses paths with Jim McAllister (Will Sasso) when he attends a community bingo night. Here's what Ratzenberger had to say about it:.

[Jim] and his bride come in, and they’re uncomfortable at first because there’s a bunch of old people in there, but eventually they warm up to it, they understand the meaning of it.

Industry observers note that it sounds like a great episode for Jim and Audrey, who have struggled to come to grips with aging in the past couple of seasons. Jim selling his tire shop to Georgie allowed him to retire, but it has now left him with a lot of spare time on his hands that he doesn't know how to fill. Could this bingo night become a new tradition for him and Audrey?
