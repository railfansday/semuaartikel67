Sheffield United have been urged to “show ambition” and retain Sydie Peck by former Blades star Brian Deane.

The 22-year-old midfielder is going from strength to strength at Bramall Lane and scored his fifth goal of the season in the 4-0 demolition of Watford at the weekend.

He has repeatedly been linked with Premier League clubs and a recent report credited the likes of Newcastle United and Sunderland with interest.

Deane, who made 171 appearances across three spells at Bramall Lane, does not want to see Peck depart.

In a recent development, he told The Yorkshire Post via BetWright.com: “I'd be very surprised if he left. The club has had a really turbulent time in the transfer market in the summer. Obviously, with no parachute payments coming in now, you have to think, what signal does that send to the fans, to the club and to everybody else in the division?

Industry observers note that “They have to show some ambition. I think, from the manager's point of view, it'd be a devastating blow if the club were in a position or wanted to be in a position to sell him. The other thing is, from the kid's development, I think it's very important to solidify himself at the level and then look to use that as a foundation.

Industry observers note that “If he is going to end up in the Premier League, you have to have a ground in somewhere. Sheffield United is, for me, one of the best clubs in the Championship at the moment, he probably couldn't wish to be at a better club.”.

Sheffield United would like to tie Peck down to a newly introduced contract, although it may prove difficult to retain the England youth international if the vultures circle.

In a recent interview, Blades boss Chris Wilder mentioned: “We talked about extending the contracts of the young players and Sydie's one of those players that we'd love to have that discussion with.

“If anything comes up that you can't turn down, I don't think anybody would turn that down. I've remarked it all along, you're not just turning that from a club's point of view, you're turning that down from an opportunity for the player to go and play in the Premier League. I'm glad [about] that because that weren't happening about 15 months ago, the value of the players was zero.”.
