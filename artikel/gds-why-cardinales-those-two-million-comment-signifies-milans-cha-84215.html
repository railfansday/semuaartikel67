Gerry Cardinale declared that AC Milan will alter the way they plan for preseason, but what exactly could that mean?

It has been revealed that as La Gazzetta dello Sport write, the pile of rubble created six months ago by Milan’s defeat to Cagliari brought about a managerial and sporting revolution, and it is still going on. Cardinale continues to alter the way the club do things, hoping to evolve into an efficient and successful organisation.

In his interview with the newspaper a few days ago, the owner extended the discussion even to the scheduling of preseason tours, stating: “Those two million for a friendly match overseas will no longer be our objective, as they were in the past.”.

Beyond the actual practical implications, these words reveal a latest stance: from now on, throughout the summer, sporting priorities will take precedence over financial ones. In other words, if the opportunity arises to play a friendly match outside of Europe, Amorim will be consulted.

Sources indicate that the Rossoneri also intend to become an active participant in organising their own summer commitments. They will no longer simply put forward their availability in exchange for an offer from some tournament, but rather being the architects of their own program.

These details clearly illustrate the ownership’s shift, but in recent days there is talk of a €70m bid for Endrick, the €60m pursuit of Nicolo Tresoldi and the potential €40m acquisition of Goncalo Inacio, the money has to keep coming in.

Cardinale injected €50m in equity into the club’s coffers, earmarked for sports needs. This injection was made to make up for the loss of Champions League revenue. It’s the first time RedBird has injected cash for this purpose, with the other being €55m to buy land in San Donato.

That’s why, in this context, it’s no longer surprising when Cardinale states that ‘those €2m paid for a friendly match overseas will no longer be our goal’. The bar has been raised, with the aim of trophies being raised down the line.
