📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that five9 announced on November 24, 2026 that it had completed its acquisition of Level AI, under terms valuing the customer-experience AI firm at approximately $325 million. Level AI’s applications will be offered as standalone products and as part of Five9’s Genius CX Platform.

Sources indicate that level AI adds applications intended to improve agent performance, automate customer interactions across voice and digital channels, and turn customer conversations into insights for the wider organization. The acquisition gives Five9 an AI-native product set for the customer journey, with deployment options that extend beyond its own contact-center platform.

In a recent development, standalone availability covers enterprises using any contact-center platform, including organizations that are not ready to move on-premises systems to the cloud and those already using another cloud platform. They can add Level AI without first buying Five9’s contact-center platform, while Genius CX customers can obtain the applications through its AI application layer.

“Customers asked us for a standalone AI solution, and Level AI is that answer,” Five9 CEO Amit Mathradas mentioned. He described Level AI as a partner customers already know, with applications already integrated with Five9, and said he expected the acquisition to accelerate the company’s product roadmap. The existing integration gives that roadmap a technical foundation predating the closing.

It has been revealed that five9 looks to take the combined portfolio to its more than 4,000 customers through existing go-to-market channels and customer relationships. That customer base is central to the distribution rationale: Level AI gains access to Five9’s relationships, while Five9 can market a broader AI portfolio through channels it already operates.

Recent findings suggest that level AI’s suite includes AI agents, agent-assist, quality-management and coaching tools on a shared conversation-intelligence layer. The platform runs on Level AI Latitude, a proprietary family of models built for customer conversations. Five9 describes Latitude as delivering lower costs and latencies than frontier models and the platform as giving enterprises direct control over data privacy.

In its November 5, 2026 agreement announcement, Five9 reported that Level AI handles more than one billion customer interactions annually for enterprise customers including Gusto, Chime, Empyrean and VistaPrint. Five9’s board unanimously approved the cash transaction, with payment to come from the company’s balance sheet. The companies had worked together since 2022, with Level AI available through the Five9 CX Marketplace and integrated with Five9’s platform.

Recent findings suggest that the completed deal gives Five9 a way to expand AI adoption without requiring every customer to migrate its contact center, while adding Level AI’s models and applications to its own platform strategy. The central business question now is whether Five9’s customer base and distribution channels can turn that wider availability into adoption across both standalone and Genius CX accounts.
