Latest Update: 5 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade tips for first-time watchers

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (203 words, 📖 Standard)
  • The 2026 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City has been postponed to Saturday, Nov. 28, due to severe weather, with festivities starting at 8:30 a.m.
  • Attending the parade in person is a memorable experience, but requires early planning, including arriving before dawn for prime viewing spots or reserving special brunches or hotel rooms along the route.
  • The balloon inflation event on the Upper West Side the day before the parade offers a unique chance to see the giant balloons up close as they are prepared.
  • Hotels on or near the parade route offer various options for viewing, but parade-facing rooms often come at a premium and may require minimum stays.
  • Expect large crowds, with over 3.5 million attendees annually, and plan to arrive early to secure a good spot.
  • Weather can be unpredictable in late November, so bring appropriate clothing and supplies for comfort during long waits.
  • Some performances are exclusive to the area in front of Macy’s Herald Square and may not be visible from other parts of the route.
