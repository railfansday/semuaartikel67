Sources indicate that not all Black Friday deals are created equal—and not all early Black Friday deals warrant stepping away from the turkey to input your credit card info. About once a year, though, your pals at GQ encounter an early Black Friday deal so undeniably good we interrupt our own bird-basting to tell you about it. This is that deal. Because if you're looking to buy a watch—could be your first, could be your fiftieth, but it should be attractive, dependable, and fun, preferably at a price that won’t require a second mortgage—a rare sale on the Seiko 5 is a stop-the-presses development.

Recent findings suggest that for decades, the Japanese watchmaker’s entry-level sport line has attained a massive cult following in horology circles for its seemingly impossible quality-to-price ratio. Heck, we dig the Seiko 5 series so much GQ watch editor Cam Wolf called it the “ultimate entry point for any aspiring watch collector,” and its flagship model “the only first watch you'll ever need.” 'Nuff stated, right?

Sources indicate that well, not today. Because today, a grip of Seiko 5 watches is even more affordable and attractive than usual, thanks to a slew of discounts across the web. In case you're still not convinced, though, allow us to spell it out real clearly. These tickers come complete with just about everything you could possibly want in a modern watch: automatic movement, day and date display, protected crown at the 4 o’clock position, and water resistance, all packed into a near-indestructible case.

Whether you’re after a rugged field watch or a handsome diver, Seiko 5 is a serious cheat code for leveling up your wrist game. Which, again, is exactly why we've recommended the line's budget-friendly timepieces over and over again, in article after article, for literal years. Which also helps explain our delight when we spotted six of ‘em—in dueling shades of blaze orange and verdant green, among others—in the mix during the usual pre-Black Friday festivities.

If you're curious what all the Seiko 5 hype is about, we've collected a handful of those very watches for your purchasing pleasure right here. Unlike the last-generation air fryer you've been eyeing on Amazon or whatever, the six tickers here aren't on sale for, like, a gazillion dollars less, but—and we can't reiterate this enough—even at full price they're already a hell of a bargain.
