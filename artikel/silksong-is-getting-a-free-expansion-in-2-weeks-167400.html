📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that in a "Cherry Time!" presentation, Hollow Knight developer Team Cherry has just revealed a first look at its much-anticipated first content patch to Hollow Knight: Silksong. The nautical-themed Sea of Sorrow will come as a free update on December 11.

In a recent development, team Cherry said that Sea of Sorrow will contain multiple newly introduced areas, power-ups, and bosses, while I clocked at least three new friendly NPCs in the trailer: A townsperson, a new shopkeeper, and a ferryman. Area-wise, I made out three distinct tilesets in the trailer: A rain-blasted fishing town on the water, a harrowing climb with an electrical storm element, and a green space reminiscent of the early game Bell Bottom.

Industry observers note that at least the first of these areas appears to be built on cut content for Silksong: "Pharloom Bay" is well-documented on the Hollow Knight wiki, and would have been located on the east side of the map, near the thematically-similar Greymoor. Some elements in the trailer correspond to ones documented by the wiki, like an underwater submersible vehicle, or a scientific lab in the electrical storm area.

In a recent development, which all sounds rad to me, particularly the submersible. Among the all-timer videogame level concepts, "underwater techbase" is one of my very favorites, and Team Cherry seem to be approaching these concepts with a Lovecrafty, Innsmouth flair that reminds me of the Fishing Village zone from Bloodborne.

Industry observers note that silksong is already a massive game, and I'm curious as to how Sea of Sorrow will fit both the map, and the game's overall flow from act to act. We'll find out for sure when the free expansion releases on December 11.
