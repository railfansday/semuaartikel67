📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Finding, buying, and stealing your dream car or bike is a peak part of Grand Theft Auto, and mine is the Fast and Furious Mitsubishi Eclipse in GTA 6, based on the bright green car driven by Paul Walker as Brian O'Conner in the original movie. This gorgeous motor can be found locked in a garage in Vice City in GTA 6, but it can be stolen, and, if you play your cards right, you can even register it as your own for free. Here's how.

Industry observers note that to find Paul Walker's Fast and Furious Mitsubishi in GTA 6, you need to head towards Vice Beach, to the east of Vice City. The car is locked in a garage on Vespucci Island, one of the Rialto Islands to the west of Vice Beach. You can see the exact location marked on the map above.

You'll have to break into the garage and steal the car, which is actually a lot easier than it sounds, but I've got more details on that below.

As you'd expect, the Mitsubishi handles really well and hits high speeds, so it's one of the best GTA 6 cars you can get your hands on, especially in the early game, for free, and without picking up too much heat.

To steal Paul Walker's GTA 6 car, head to the garage and break in. This triggers an alarm, but you can switch that off pretty quickly on the wall to your left as you head in. Open the garage door here too, break into the car, hotwire it, and drive away - simple as that.

There are a few additional details to note here, though, that make this experience easier or better for you overall.
