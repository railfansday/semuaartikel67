Liverpool have a lot of players' contracts that are about to expire over the next couple of years.

This summer alone, a handful of players are looking likely to exit Anfield for nothing.

Virgil van Dijk and Alisson are among the biggest names to be linked with a departure but, as of now, nothing is set in stone.

This is obviously a tad worrying, especially because during the January transfer window, those players who's contracts expire in June 2027 will be allowed to speak to foreign clubs about a free transfer.

Obviously, the last couple of years have seen Liverpool lose Ibrahima Konate and Trent Alexander-Arnold for nothing so, in order for that not to become a regular occurrence, new agreements need to be agreed soon with one Liverpool star now "desperate" to extend his tenure.

Alexis Mac Allister wants to extend his Liverpool contract soon.

One of the surprise packages of Liverpool's 2026-27 season has been Alexis Mac Allister.

The Argentinian was frowned upon by fans last season for how poorly he'd performed.

In fact, some fans, pundits and journalists even stated that his performances were among the worst in Arne Slot's team.

However, after the 2026 World Cup, things just seem to have clicked with the 27-year-old and now, he's one of the first names on Iraola's team sheet.

This upturn in form has led to fans demanding that his contract is extended with his current deal set to expire in June 2028.

And, although Liverpool's tune has been that they don't want to negotiate a latest deal for him, that looks like it's about to change with Football Insider now reporting that Mac Allister is 'desperate' to be offered a fresh contract.

"Alexis Mac Allister is desperate to get his fresh Liverpool contract signed as soon as possible, sources have told Football Insider," they wrote.

"He notched his first Premier League goal of the season against Crystal Palace before the international break, already equalling his overall tally of five from the whole of 2025-26.

"He previously admitted his disappointment at not being offered fresh terms by the Anfield club, as his current deal is set to expire in June 2028.

"Liverpool have now opened preliminary contract talks, and as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Mac Allister is pushing to get the deal over the line in the coming weeks and months.

"He has found a latest lease of life under Iraola and wants to keep playing for the Reds well into his 30s.".

Liverpool must decide how important Alexis Mac Allister is to them.

Right now, Mac Allister is Liverpool's best-performing midfielder.

The Argentinian has been a rock in the middle of the park for the Reds and, his ability to win duels and create something out of nothing is top tier.

Obviously, these aren't the only things that players need to be successful but, the fact that Mac Allister is one of the only midfielders who does this at Liverpool certainly helps him out.

Mac Allister is also 27 years old so, it's unclear how long he has left at the top of his game.

But, if his weekly wage doesn't need to be bumped up all that much, there's no harm in locking him down so that he doesn't exit for free.

Here's hoping that a decision is made on the midfielder soon so that Liverpool's fans can figure out what the transfer aims of their team is.
