It has been revealed that it will be one of the most eagerly devoured documents in football circles, assuming its contents are ever fully revealed. In September, the increasingly powerful European Football Clubs body announced a “feasibility study” into how it can become established on a more global scale, morphing into an organisation that represents clubs all over the world.

Sources indicate that the study’s findings could, some leading figures in the sport’s governance believe, define football’s long-term future. It has the potential to transform a landscape destabilised by the scandals surrounding Fifa and Gianni Infantino.
