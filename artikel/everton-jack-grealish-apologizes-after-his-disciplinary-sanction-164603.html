Everton: Jack Grealish apologizes after his disciplinary sanction

Jack Grealish has decided to break the silence after several complicated weeks at Everton. Left out of the squad by David Moyes for missing training without permission, the 31-year-old England international publicly apologized to supporters and club members. Thursday evening, after returning to the field during a friendly match played behind closed doors against Derby County, the player on loan from Manchester City published a message on Instagram to acknowledge the situation and show his desire to return. “ Glad to be back on the field with the guys today after a good few days of training! I would like to apologize to the supporters and all members of the club for not being able to help the team in recent weeks “, he wrote, accompanying his publication with several photos of the meeting. Grealish also reaffirmed his attachment to the Toffees: “ I love playing for this club and I can’t wait to play in front of you again in an Everton shirt. See you soon! » A speech which comes as the former Aston Villa player has not played an official match since the defeat against Chelsea (0-2) on October 17.

This exclusion followed a violation of the club’s disciplinary regulations. After initially citing illness to justify his absence during the trip to Arsenal, Grealish was sanctioned by Everton, notably missing the matches against Newcastle and Coventry City. David Moyes, however, left the door open for his return, specifying that a decision would be made with the managers when the player could be available again. On Thursday, the Englishman took a first step by participating in Everton’s victory against Derby County (4-1) during a friendly meeting organized at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Alcaraz scored twice, while Jonathan Nsangou and Christian Norgaard also scored. Returning for a second consecutive loan after a first season disrupted by injury, Grealish is still looking to relaunch his career away from Manchester City, who recruited him for more than 100 million euros in 2021. With only six appearances this season before his sanction, the attacking midfielder now hopes to regain the confidence of David Moyes and rapidly reintegrate the professional group.
