📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Join Freelancer.com if you’re new to freelancing and will bid often on small projects to build reviews, since $4.99 a month buys 50 bids and the fee is 10% of each job, or $5 on fixed-price jobs under $50. Skip it if you’re a specialist who won’t compete on price, because projects draw dozens of rival bids.

It has been revealed that on a $100 fixed-price job, Freelancer.com takes $10 the moment you accept it, and a $1 withdrawal fee leaves you $89. Clients post fixed-price and hourly projects for freelancers around the world to bid on, and run prize contests you can enter for free.

In a recent development, newcomers who bid on small jobs every week can build reviews here, and one extra $15 job a month nets $10 after the fee, twice the Basic initiative’s $4.99. Specialists who won’t compete on price do better finding clients through referrals, or through a vetted network such as Toptal if they’re developers, designers, or finance experts.
