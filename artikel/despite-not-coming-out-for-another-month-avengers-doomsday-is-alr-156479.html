It might not be out for another month, but Avengers: Doomsday is already on an Oscars finalist list.

Per Variety, Doomsday has made the cut for the visual effects top 20. While the Academy doesn't confirm its top 20, Variety indicates it has verified the list with sources.

According to recent reports, judging by the trailers released so far, Doomsday is indeed a VFX heavy movie, so it's not a huge surprise that it might make it to the list. The full roundup is below:.

Avengers: Doomsday arrives this December, and it sees Earth's Mightiest Heroes go up against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. How and why Doom and Tony Stark shares the same face is still a mystery for now.

"People wondered, 'Is Doom just a variant of another character? Is this just a shortcut to bringing back another character?' The answer is no. He’s Victor Von Doom," Kevin Feige said recently, only confusing us further.

Industry observers note that the latest trailer for the movie, however, is focused on Steve Rogers, who is returning after retiring in the past in Avengers: Endgame. However, with some timeline confusion, it looks like his situation is far more complicated than it seems.

In a recent development, doomsday arrives on December 18, while Oscars nominations are revealed on January 21, 2027. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the upcoming Marvel movies and TV shows.
