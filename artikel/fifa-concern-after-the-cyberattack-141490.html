Recent findings suggest that “FIFA confirms that it has been the victim of a criminal cyberattack resulting in unauthorized access to a limited number of personal and professional information. » This is how the FIFA spokesperson commented on the cyberattack which targeted the computer system of the governing body of world football.

In comments relayed this Thursday by L’Équipe, the spokesperson also judged that it was a “serious and unacceptable violation of privacy”. But promptly, questions turned to the fate of files linked to Gianni Infantino’s aborted project to sell shares of the World Cup to private funds. A project kept secret for a long time by the significant boss of FIFA, before it was revealed without his knowledge and triggered a huge controversy.

Quoted by the sports media, the Reuters agency indicated that the files in question would not have been violated. In reality, only 0.1% of all FIFA data would have been affected. And these famous files would not be part of it.

It has been revealed that as a reminder, Gianni Infantino remains under threat of a summons and a hearing before the North American authorities concerning his strategy to sell shares in the World Cup. FIFA, for its part, completely rejected the accusations made against it.

In the meantime, the current Italian-Swiss-Lebanese boss presents himself as the major favorite for his own succession, during the FIFA presidential election scheduled in Morocco on March 18. Four candidates facing him, including Philippe Diallo, the boss of the French Football Federation (FFF).
