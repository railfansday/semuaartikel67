On Saturday 8 August 2026 it was confirmed that Newcastle United captain Bruno Guimaraes had signed for Arsenal.

It had become clear over the summer that the Gunners were doing everything they could to get the Brazil international on the cheap.

Arsenal attempting to take advantage of the fact that Bruno Guimaraes was willing to leave, Mikel Arteta and the North London club trying to sign United’s best and most key player for £50m or less, shamelessly using media stooges to try and force Newcastle’s hand.

The sales of Alexander Isak (£125m), Sandro Tonali (£100m) and Anthony Gordon (£70m) had proved that the Newcastle United owners were never going to allow any player to leave for less than the club’s valuation.

In the event, Arsenal eventually accepted that they belatedly had to act in a professional manner, made an official approach and Newcastle United reluctantly accepted £75m for a player who turned 29 earlier this month.

That transfer fee was very likely excessive for Bruno Guimaraes when you are just coldly looking at finances and overall profile of a player.

However, nobody at Newcastle United, whether the owners, players, manager, fans, would have chosen to sell at even that price.

It was only because Bruno Guimaraes wanted out and the club reluctantly allowed him to leave.

Exactly 15 weeks after leaving, Bruno Guimaraes returns to St James’ Park with Arsenal.

Bruno Guimaraes telling ESPN ahead of his return to Tyneside: “I think I was one of the most beloved players during my time there…so it was a difficult decision, but deep down I felt I needed a new challenge…after almost five years at Newcastle I’m driven by challenges and wanted a new one.”.

Bruno Guimaraes going on to say: “It wasn’t an easy decision. I had to think long and hard about it at the time. It was very difficult, right after the World Cup which was a very turbulent time, so it was difficult for me in many ways. But in the end I chose to make the move and I’m happy here [at Arsenal].”.

The former United captain now finds himself only a squad player at Arsenal, behind the likes of Rice, Odegaard and even defender turned midfielder Lewis-Skelly.

Sources indicate that bruno Guimaraes reflecting: “I think that I am increasingly understanding Mikel Arteta’s approach, which is completely different from what I was used to doing at Newcastle…it is not easy to switch clubs after nearly five years, when you were already completely used to the routine, to everything. This is a huge challenge and I am ready for it.”.
