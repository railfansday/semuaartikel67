Traditionally, Black Friday deals have been focused on spending money, but a special subcategory of deals actually lets you buy money. That's because over at Amazon, quite a few gift cards are reduced, letting you spend less money on a card than their financial value actually is.

There are plenty of gift card deals at Amazon, and that includes a few reductions which will be particularly interesting for gamers. The actual selection of gaming gift card deals is limited (to, uh, one), but there are also movie theater chains and fast food outlets which have their gift cards reduced too. So we'll hopefully be able to find you something useful to you, or that you'd happily give to someone.

The 15% reduced from the cost of this Domino's gift card only applies to the $50 option, but that's all you'll need for a family meal or indulgent movie night.

Sources indicate that this discount only applies to $50 gift cards and, strangely, only certain designs. Oh well; the look of the gift card doesn't matter if you're getting more for your money.

This Cinemark gift card doesn't just let you buy theater tickets for less, but it also applies to the chain's cafes and restaurants too.

A 20% discount in the price of a gift card to Regal theaters means you can visit the major theater group for a lower price, and you're going to get plenty of snacks for the reduced price.

Know a Roblox fan? This gift card is 15% reduced, saving a bit of money to spend on Robux. The card also lets them get some extra perks across the game when it's redeemed.

This Taco Bell eGift card deal is a bit different: you need to redeem it when ordering from the app, not in person. The 15% discount only applies to the $50 card, not any of the other ones offered.

Industry observers note that for an alternative fast-food chain which has discounted gift cards, Panera Bread's $50 physical gift card is 20% off. However the $25 or $75 alternatives aren't reduced at all.

Sources indicate that on the surface, a reduction in a gift card seems like a nice little way to save money on a gift, or a workaround to get a cheaper meal or theater ticket. But they're a little better than you'd first think.

Recent findings suggest that usually, when a retailer or outlet is offering promotions, it'll let you pick a certain one. You generally can't double promotions in most shops, so you have to pick whichever is best for your purchase.

Sources indicate that however if you're paying for said purchase with a reduced gift card, that often doesn't count as a promotion. In short, if Domino's is running several deals on pizzas, you'd usually only need to pick one; if you're using a gift card you bought for less, this effectively acts as a second deal.

This is generally the case, but not always, and you can be sure by checking the small print in the Amazon listing before you buy it.

Industry observers note that just check before you buy the card: some of these are physical ones, which can only be used in the brand's branches, and others are online ones that can only be redeemed in certain places. Make sure you're buying a gift card that you, or your recipient, can actually use.

And before you start planning an elaborate system: you can't use a gift card to buy a gift card. That'd be an amazing hack that'd save you literally infinite money if done enough times, but the shops are way ahead of you and prohibit that.
