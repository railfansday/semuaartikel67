Industry observers note that during Cyber Week, from Nov. 26 to 30, agentic traffic is expected to hit 150x last year’s Cyber Week figures, according to cybersecurity firm Human Security. Retail traffic from agents in particular is set to more than double from pre-holiday levels to its Black Friday weekend peak, mirroring last year’s 145% jump.
