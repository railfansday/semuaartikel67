Industry observers note that percy Jackson and the Olympians has given fans two exciting seasons, and the series isn't done yet. Adapting Rick Riordan's popular novels, the TV show follows the titular hero, played by Walker Scobell, making a name for himself in the first two seasons. Yet, Season 3's return is especially exciting for fans, as it is the first time Riordan's third book, Percy Jackson and the Titan's Curse, has been adapted. Both newly introduced fans and book readers have a lot to look forward to after the events of Season 2, with the stakes higher than ever.

After Season 1, the gods are anticipating war, but Percy and his friends face an entirely separate problem because Camp Half-Blood is in danger. Seeing the camp's destruction in a dream, Annabeth (Leah Jeffries) shows up in New York in the Grey Sisters' cab to collect Percy, who brings along Tyson (Daniel Diemer), a Cyclops and Percy's half-brother through Poseidon (Toby Stephens). They arrive just in time to see Luke (Charlie Bushnell) poison Thalia's tree, which maintains the camp's magical border. The friends realize the only way to save the camp is to travel through the Seas of Monsters and find the legendary Golden Fleece, giving them a new mission.

Meanwhile, Chiron (Glynn Turman), the demigods' beloved mentor, is no longer camp director after the gods fired him. Tantalus (Timothy Simons) has taken the role, but he dislikes all demigods, especially Percy. When Tantalus refuses to grant a quest, Annabeth contacts Chiron, who admits the quest could work, but insists that Percy cannot go because of the Great Prophecy. He reveals the prediction to Annabeth, on the condition that she won't tell Percy. Later, a disastrous chariot race convinces Tantalus to approve the quest, but he chooses Clarisse (Dior Goodjohn) to lead it. Though Clarisse is allowed companions, Annabeth talks her out of choosing Percy, creating a rift between the friends.

As a member of the original trio, Grover (Aryan Simhadri) naturally has a main role in Season 2, but this time, he's not questing with Percy and Annabeth. At the end of Season 1, Grover leaves to search for Pan, the missing god of the wild, but Season 2 reveals that his mission has gone wrong.

Recent findings suggest that facing angry demigods and monsters, Grover falls into the trap that the Cyclops Polyphemus (Aleks Paunovic) has set with the Golden Fleece. An empathy link allows Grover and Percy to appear in each other's dreams, and Grover uses it to call for help. Grover pretends to be a female Cyclops and promises to marry Polyphemus, but this is only a stalling tactic, giving Percy limited time to save him.

Before Percy can leave, Annabeth and Tyson join him, and they narrowly escape Tantalus. However, when they reach the cruise ship Hermes suggested, it's full of monsters and Kronos' (Nick Boraine) allies, including Luke, who hope to use the Golden Fleece to heal Kronos' body. Though the Titan's army tries to recruit them, Tyson creates an opportunity for them to escape on a lifeboat. The quest gives Percy and Annabeth time to discuss their issues, including Annabeth sharing more about the Great Prophecy — which predicts that a child of the eldest gods will either save or destroy Olympus when they reach their sixteenth birthday.

Recent findings suggest that meanwhile, Clarisse goes on her own quest, complete with an ominous prophecy from the Oracle. Choosing to go by herself, Clarisse gets a ship from Ares (Adam Copeland) staffed with soldiers who were lost in battle. Yet, they soon realize that Tantalus gave them the wrong directions, and Clarisse seeks out Percy and Annabeth. Despite their contentious dynamic, the two groups agree to work together. To enter the Sea of Monsters, they must face either Scylla or Charybdis, and Annabeth recommends Scylla, but Clarisse balks at sacrificing her crew and redirects them toward Charybdis, planning to blow the monster up. Percy uses his ocean powers to help, but the fight against Charybdis explodes the ship, sending the characters in different directions.

After the explosion, Percy and Annabeth wake up at CC's spa, but their friends are lost, and the Sirens make escape impossible. They meet Circe (Rosemarie DeWitt), a seemingly friendly sorceress who traps heroes on her island for decades. When Percy confronts her, Circe turns him into a guinea pig. Annabeth realizes what has happened and saves Percy with the vitamins Hermes gave them. They blueprint to sail past the Sirens, plugging their ears with the wax Circe keeps hidden, but Circe takes it back at the last second. Hearing the Sirens, Annabeth goes to them, but resists her vision until it shifts. Percy saves her from the final Siren, and they flee, reaching Polyphemus' island. Yet, the encounter reveals that Percy's fatal flaw is loyalty, adding to his worries about the prophecy.

Elsewhere, Clarisse washes up on Polyphemus' island, falls into a trap, and becomes his captive. Their escape attempt fails, and Polyphemus reveals that he's been tricking Grover the entire time. Meanwhile, Percy and Annabeth plan a rescue, and before Annabeth discovers Polyphemus' scheme, Percy becomes his prisoner as well. However, they do find the Golden Fleece in the cave. As Annabeth faces Polyphemus, she is seriously injured. Yet, Tyson arrives, having survived the ocean as a son of Poseidon, and fights Polyphemus.

On the cruise ship, Clarisse chooses to find the Golden Fleece while Percy prioritizes Annabeth. However, after they split up, each changes course.
