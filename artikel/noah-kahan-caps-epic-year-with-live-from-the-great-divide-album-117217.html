Sources indicate that what better way to celebrate an absolutely epic year than with a live keepsake? Noah Kahan confirmed that he will cap 2026 by releasing the Live From the Great Divide album on Dec. 11. The 16-track collection was recorded across North America during Kahan’s sold-out summer Great Divide Tour. Explore Noah Kahan See latest videos, charts and news.

The collection is fronted by a live version of the fan-favorite “Orbiter,” which appeared on the expanded edition of the singer’s fourth album, The Great Divide: The Last of the Bugs Digital Extended Album. The song was recorded at Kahan’s sold-out CitiField show in Newly introduced York this summer and it’s accompanied by an in-concert video (see below) of the singer strumming his acoustic guitar and playing the hushed ballad to a rapt crowd of nearly 45,000. The sequel to Kahan’s 2024 Live from Fenway Park album features songs recorded at more than a dozen stops on this summer’s tour, including Boston, Atlanta, Chicago, Toronto, San Francisco, Cincinnati, Minneapolis, Los Angeles, Vancouver and Seattle.

Kahan is not resting on his laurels, either. After headlining baseball stadiums in North America and arenas in Australia, New Zealand, Europe and the U.K. this year, he immediately sold out all 24 North American dates of his recently announced 2027 The Last of the Bugs World Tour, slated to kick off at Sanford Stadium in Athens, Ga. on April 24 and run through a July 25 show at Wembley Stadium in London. He will be joined on the dates by a rotating cast of support acts including Buffalo Traffic Jam, Gregory Alan Isakov, Remi Wolf, Ashe, Kingfishr, Alyssa Grace and Tiny Habit.
