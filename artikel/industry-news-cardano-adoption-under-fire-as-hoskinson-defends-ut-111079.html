Industry observers note that cardano is under fire as Hoskinson defended the network’s utility amid a notable price fall. This came after Newly introduced Zealand-based crypto trader and influencer Lark Davis revealed that Cardano’s real-world adoption remains despite being in existence for almost nine years.

Via his X account on Sunday, Nov. 22, Davis asked Cardano CEO Charles Hoskinson about aims to grow adoption on the network. With his huge crypto audience, the trader said many users often ask him what has been holding Cardano back. As a result, he inquired why the protocol has failed to attract customers and capture meaningful market share. Compared with newer crypto networks like Solana, the analyst pointed out that Cardano’s daily active users and DeFi activity appear significantly low.

Recent findings suggest that the Cardano founder suddenly emerged online on Sunday and responded to the celebrated trader. Hoskinson highlighted possible reasons, though with cautionary remarks. His answer hinted that a crypto network’s adoption is not determined by price, but by usage and transactions – indicators of real adoption.

It has been revealed that according to Hoskinson, Cardano powers immense real-world on-chain use cases, financial applications, and tokenization utilities whose values are not directly measured by ADA price. He said Cardano’s infrastructure and applications are stronger than before, regardless of the ADA price.

According to recent reports, he maintained that price doesn’t always show a network’s full capabilities. He added that different networks are on their own journeys and priorities, so comparing Solana with Cardano’s situation doesn’t make sense.

According to recent reports, despite Cardano launching four years earlier, its daily active users are roughly 1% of Solana’s. Today, ADA trades at $0.1547, 95.3% down from its $3.09 peak reached five years ago in 2021.

In a recent development, the question above about ADA and SOL highlights the battle for leadership in the crypto space. The two assets have emerged as two of the most popular chains, each with their own strengths and limitations.

Recent findings suggest that in terms of market value, Solana has vastly outpaced Cardano. As of today, SOL holds a market capitalization of $45.2 billion compared to ADA’s $5.7 billion. Besides that, Solana’s TVL presently reaches $$4.61 billion, significantly higher than Solana’s $43.14 million.
