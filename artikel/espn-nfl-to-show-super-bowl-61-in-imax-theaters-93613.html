📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Super Bowl 61 will be telecast live in hundreds of Imax theaters in February via a partnership with the NFL and Disney's ESPN, which has the broadcast rights to the 2027 game.

It marks the largest Imax live event in the manufacturer's history and the first time that the Super Bowl will be available in the format, according to a news release.

In a recent development, the four-hour event on Feb. 14 will include ESPN's main Super Bowl game telecast, as well as Jay-Z's halftime performance and pregame and postgame coverage.

Industry observers note that tickets will be available for theaters in the United States and select international markets, including Australia, Upcoming Zealand and Brazil. Tickets are expected to go on sale in January. Pricing was not disclosed.

"Watching the Super Bowl with your fellow fans is one of the great shared experiences in sports, and this first-of-its-kind collaboration with the NFL and IMAX takes it to a historic new scale," said Ken Potrock, president of major event integration for The Walt Disney Co.

In addition to ESPN channels, this season's championship game will also be available on streaming platform Disney+ as well as Disney's broadcast network ABC.
