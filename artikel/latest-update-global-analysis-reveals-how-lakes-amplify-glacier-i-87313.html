Latest Update: Global analysis reveals how lakes amplify glacier ice loss

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that glacial lakes can accelerate the retreat of glaciers. If glaciers border a lake, the water can melt the ice from underneath; furthermore, chunks of ice can break off and fall into the lake. The extent to which these processes influence the situation, compared with other factors, had not been sufficiently clarified at a global level until now.
