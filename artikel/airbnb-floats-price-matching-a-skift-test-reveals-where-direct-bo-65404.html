Are we one day going to see an Airbnb best price guarantee for short-term rentals? AI agents may force the glitch.

What happens to Airbnb when personal AI agents can look at a listing and instantly check the price on the property’s own website, where Airbnb doesn’t collect a commission?

Industry observers note that using the AI agent Instinct, Skift compared 16 Airbnb listings with rates on the properties’ own websites. The direct site was cheaper in 10 — by as much as 42% — and one was essentially a tie.

When an account on X questioned whether Airbnb’s profits would suffer if AI agents could compare prices right before guests book, CEO Brian Chesky replied: “You presume we could never offer a price-match guarantee.”.

The post wasn’t an announcement that Airbnb will match prices on short-term rentals like it recently started for hotels. But it signals that Airbnb could, and the test suggests it may eventually have to.
