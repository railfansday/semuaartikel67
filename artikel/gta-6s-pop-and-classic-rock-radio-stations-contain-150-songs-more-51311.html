If you haven’t heard already: Grand Theft Auto 6, finally out now on PS5 and Xbox, is a very big video game. It’s also one of the best games I’ve played in years. And one reason I love it so much is because of all the great music on the in-game radio stations. There are so many songs. In fact, just two of the game’s biggest radio stations contain more songs than all of GTA: Vice City’s radio stations combined did back on the PS2.

Recent findings suggest that two of the biggest radio stations inÂ GTA 6Â are FLASH FM, a pop station, and V-ROCK, a classic rock station. Funnily enough, both of these stations appeared in Vice City, too. But this time around, the stations have a lot more music. Let’s do some number crunching!

Recent findings suggest that in GTA: Vice City‘s version of V-Rock, there were (before some songs were cut in later ports for licensing reasons) 17 songs. Meanwhile, Vice City‘s Flash FM featured 15 songs. And in total, there were 106 songs in GTA 6 across its various stations. In GTA 6, Flash FM alone now boasts 80 songs, according to Rockstar’s recently released credits. And V-Rock now has 70 songs. Combined, there are more songs on just these two stations than in all of Vice City.

It has been revealed that after playing 150 hours of GTA 6, I’m still hearing latest songs. Last night, I booted up the game and heard “Hot To Go!” for the first time, which blew me away. And I listened to these two stations a lot!
