Sources indicate that I have to admit, I have a bit of a gripe with washable rugs. For years, the design industry has been trying to convince us that the answer to a beautiful, livable home is a washable rug. And while I understand the appeal, especially as a mom to two young kids, a dog, and a household that sees its fair share of spills, muddy shoes, and general chaos, I’ve just never been entirely sold on the idea.

While washable rug options have definitely improved over the years, there’s still something about the weight, texture, and character of a traditional rug that I find hard to forgo for the sake of washability. I want a rug that feels substantial underfoot, looks better with age, and doesn’t require me to wrestle an eight-by-ten-foot piece of fabric into my washing machine every time someone spills a juice box.

Sources indicate that when I learned about Ernesta’s protective rug treatment, I was intrigued. The custom rug brand offers an optional treatment designed to help repel spills and make everyday messes easier to clean, without requiring you to compromise on your rug’s style, material, or size. In theory, it offers the best of both worlds: the look and feel of a beautifully made rug, with a little extra insurance against the realities of family life. Plus, it’s a treatment that’s typically hard to access on your own without going custom through a designer.

According to recent reports, I actually have several Ernesta rugs in my home (I’m a huge fan of their quality and approachable customization options!), but after swapping in an Ernesta rug treated with the brand’s protective finish in our living room, I don’t think I’ll ever go back to ordering a rug without it. Here’s what you should know about the treatment, how it's held up in my own home, and why I think it may be a smarter investment than yet another washable rug.

Best for: This rug is an excellent option for families, pet owners, or anyone who just prefers not to have to be too precious with their things.

Not ideal for: Anyone looking for a quick-ship rug option.

Bottom Line: The customization of Ernesta’s rug assortment makes it easy to get the exact look and function that serves your home best, including different sizes, non-slip bases, and protections.

It has been revealed that one of the reasons I love Ernesta so much is how approachable they make the ordering process. There’s no visiting a showroom or making 400 phone calls to try to get the right custom-cut size. You simply log on to their site, browse the options, then choose up to five samples to try for just $10.

My samples came incredibly swiftly—within a few days, they were at my doorstep, ready for review. Because I already liked the look of the rug we had in our living room (more on that later), I knew I didn’t want to stray too far from the neutral, functional foundation it gave us.

When we started, we had Ernesta’s Emerson sisal rug in the space. The nubby texture and color looked beautiful, but it was a bit small (it was originally sized to an old home that we have since moved from), and the deeper grooves of the weave made it hard to clean up spills. I have two young kids and a dog, and truly not a day goes by where someone hasn’t spilled yogurt or cracker crumbs or juice on the rug.

Recent findings suggest that still, I don’t think you should have to sacrifice beauty for function in a family home, and Ernesta helped us find that balance. After debating between a few different styles, I ended up settling on the Cally wool flatweave rug in Camel. Wool flatweave rugs are notoriously durable on their own, and I knew the protective coating would only help it hold up better. Bonus: The tighter weave meant fewer nooks and crannies for gunk to get into.

Once we decided on our newly introduced style, we measured out the size we’d need, which ended up being around 13 x 15 feet. I loved how easy it was to order the exact size we needed for our space, especially given that it wasn’t a standard measurement. If you’re unsure about the right size for your rug (or how many legs of your furniture should be on the rug at one time), Ernesta has helpful guidance on their site.

Placing the order was easy; you can opt into the protective treatment before adding the order to your cart, as well as choose other details, like the type of edge binding and whether you’d like to include a rug pad (we chose the attached version).

Protective treatments for rugs are nothing latest; they’ve been available to design industry insiders for years. That stated, very few DTC companies offer the treatment, unless you’re buying through a designer or want to seek out a supplier on your own.

That’s what’s so great about Ernesta’s offerings—it’s literally as easy as checking a box before you add your rug to cart. The treatment starts at $62 and increases depending on the size of your rug. Their formula is non-toxic and works by acting as a protective layer between the surface of the rug and any spills or debris, preventing stains from setting in and keeping the fibers intact for longer.

According to recent reports, receiving and installing our Ernesta rug couldn’t have been easier, especially considering its large size. It was delivered and placed in our entryway, where it waited for a few days until my husband and I had a spare moment to lay it out.

The rug was packaged beautifully, with thick plastic wrapping protecting the roll, a firm cardboard tube at the center, and fabric wrapping the edges so the binding didn’t get roughed up in transit. I thought this was a particularly nice touch.

As with most rugs, the corners were a bit flared when we first unrolled it, so here’s my favorite tip: add several ice cubes to any corner you want to lay flat and let them melt. The slow dissipation of moisture will relax the rug's fibers, and by the time it has melted away completely, you should have a totally flat rug.

I was immediately impressed by the look and feel of this rug—the wool was so soft and plush, and the rubber mat integrated on the bottom meant it did not budge once we laid it down (though it did make it difficult to reposition when we laid it a little wonky initially, word to the wise). There was no “chemical” smell from the protective treatments, and the fibers still felt just as soft as they did on the sample I received before ordering.

Sources indicate that aesthetically, I love the vibe that this rug brings to the space. It’s soft and plush and cozy, and finally in better proportion with the rest of the room—which is great, because I have a pair of armchairs I won at auction waiting in the wings for their moment. Before, they were floating off the rug and looked odd, but now, they’ll be perfect.

It has been revealed that logically, the protective treatment was 100 percent the right choice for our family. Not even five minutes after laying it out, my three-year-old squished an applesauce pouch on the rug, and it came up with ease. In the month since having it, we’ve definitely put this rug through the paces of family life, and it still looks as good as the day we opened it. Cleaning it is easy, and Ernesta has a whole page on their site dedicated to care instructions, depending on the material your rug is made of.

Overall, this is the ideal solution to me: a rug that looks as good as it performs, and doesn’t require any design sacrifice (or yelling from mom) in the process.
