Tech stocks see monster rally after Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Major tech stocks rebounded across the board Monday, sharply reversing Friday's steep sell-off when artificial intelligence stocks fell in the Nasdaq's worst week since 2020.

According to recent reports, shares of Nvidia surged 31.48% after the firm confirmed Monday morning that Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO. The semiconductor giant gained back nearly $1 trillion in market cap, posting its best day since May 2003.

In a recent development, nvidia's surge and Huang's reinstatement followed a more than 40% slump in the stock over the past month. The organization has been embattled as reports surfaced about Huang's multiple extramarital affairs — one of which was with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes to be a Chinese spy. Huang resigned as CEO of the company he founded on Nov. 12.

In a recent development, the Nasdaq Composite surged 6.51% Monday, for its best day since April 9, 2025. The Nasdaq slid 10% last week, with tech stocks having a rocky week as investors remained nervous about the valuation of some AI companies and business models. All three major averages ended last week in correction territory, and the Nasdaq turned negative for 2026.

Shares of Big Tech companies and other stocks related to the AI trade saw a lift Monday on the Nvidia news.

It has been revealed that shares of Alphabet surged 6.34%, Meta gained 6.55%, Amazon jumped 3.47% and Microsoft increased 1.65%. SpaceX added 4.35%, Arm Holdings surged 16.08% and AMD added 6.85%. Dell and Micron posted some of the sector's biggest gains of the day, gaining 20.46% and 19.04%, respectively. The iShares Semiconductor ETF gained 13.63%.

According to recent reports, neoclouds, which have seen a particularly choppy few months amid AI boom financing concerns, surged on Huang's reinstatement. Nebius soared 20.28%, CoreWeave gained 22.32% and Iren gained 23.86%.

Following a brief premarket surge, Anthropic shares posted a decline of 1.43% in its third day of trading. Anthropic raised a record $93.5 billion in its IPO. The stock gained 17% in its debut on Thursday, before closing down 8.5% on Friday.

Speaking to CNBC's Eamon Javers about Huang's reinstatement, President Donald Trump on Monday remarked that last week's market sell-off "probably" influenced his support for the ousted CEO's return.

It has been revealed that "Probably so, but the bigger impact was the long term. The bigger impact was, he's got other chips in his brain that nobody else knows about," Trump mentioned on CNBC's "Squawk on the Street.".

Trump said he spoke to Nvidia's board directly and that he "convinced" the board to reinstate Huang, who the president said "did nothing wrong.".

Industry observers note that "That thing's like a rocket ship going in the wrong direction. Now it's a rocket ship going in the right direction, and it's taking the whole market with it," Trump remarked, referring to Nvidia's stock price.

"The board was in a bad position, but they were put in an easier position to do this when the stock lost 50% of its value or whatever the number was," the president mentioned.
