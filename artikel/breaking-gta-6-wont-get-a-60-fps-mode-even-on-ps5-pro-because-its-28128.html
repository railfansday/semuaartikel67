Breaking: GTA 6 Won’t Get A 60 FPS Mode, Even On PS5 Pro, Because It’s ‘Not Powerful Enough’

Earlier this month, Rockstar Games confirmed Grand Theft Auto 6 targets 30 FPS on all platforms, even PlayStation 5 Pro. Before that news was revealed, I asked Rockstar if GTA 6 could receive a 60 FPS or even a 40 FPS mode in the future. And it sounds like, at least on current gen, that won’t be happening.

Industry observers note that when Rockstar revealed that GTA 6 would target 30 FPS on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X, and Xbox Series S, some players didn’t take it well. Many had hoped that Rockstar would, like so many other developers this console generation, offer some kind of performance mode. So for some, it was very sad news. But others held out hope that, perhaps in a post-launch update, Rockstar would add in a 60 FPS option or even a 40 FPS option for players enjoying the game on a 120hz TV.

However, Rockstar has confirmed with Kotaku that this isn’t going to happen, even on PS5 Pro. GTA 6 is just too much for these current consoles.

“Our base presentation makes use of ray-traced reflections and global illumination,” explained Technology Production Director Marc Chambers. “On the PS5 Pro, this is enhanced with the addition of ray-traced shadows, as well as overall added clarity through the use of PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR). The PS5 Pro features an upgraded graphics processor and upscaling technology that we leverage to increase the game’s visual fidelity. While the PS5 Pro’s central processor is slightly better than the PS5 base console, it is not powerful enough to run the game’s complex simulation at higher frame rates.”.

I’ve played over 130 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6 on PS5 Pro, and I don’t think the game is worse off for being locked to a framerate of 30 FPS. I’m somewhat of a framerate snob, but the gameplay in GTA 6 works totally fine at 30 fps. And the visuals are truly stunning, and more than make up for the lower framerate.

I know some people might suggest Rockstar turn off ray tracing and other fancy features to offer a 60 FPS option, but it would really take away from what the studio has built. GTA 6 is one of the best-looking games I’ve ever seen and easily sports the best-looking open world I’ve explored in my life. And a lot of that is down to the incredible lighting and ray-tracing tech that can be found in so many different parts of the world. Campfires cast orange, flickering glows on faces. Fancy red sports cars reflect the sunlight, tinting my crappy white car as I drive by them. The lights of my car’s dash and fuel gauge reflect in the windshield of my pickup truck as I cruise around the woods in the dark. Stuff like that adds a lot to the world and the vibes. Removing it would take away so much.

According to recent reports, even if you turn those visual features off, the game is still massive and incredibly alive, with dozens of planes in the air at all times, huge amounts of traffic, and multiple NPCs filling every frame of the game. So I’m not convinced it would be able to hit 60 FPS even with ray-tracing turned off or scaled back.

Sources indicate that and hey, if history is any indicator of the future, this is the worst version of GTA 6 we’ll ever play. The game will almost certainly get ported to PC in the near future and run at 60 FPS or more over there, assuming your rig can run it. Later ports on next-gen machines in the future are also likely coming and will probably feature improved framerate options and more. So if you really want a 60 FPS option in GTA 6, you could just wait. But personally, I’m loving the game even at 30 FPS.
