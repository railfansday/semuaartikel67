On November 26, 2026, XEBO.ai revealed a $10.5 million Series A investment from Emirates Growth Fund (EGF), which becomes the AI-native experience-management company’s first institutional investor.

The capital will fund a broader voice-of-customer (VoC) portfolio, including call analytics and digital experience analytics, alongside further development of agentic AI across the platform. The company’s stated goal is to give enterprises an AI-native alternative to legacy experience-management systems.

Recent findings suggest that co-founder and CEO Yash Sultania connected the investment to that founding purpose. “We founded XEBO.ai to help enterprises act on what their customers tell them,” he mentioned, adding that the EGF backing sets up the firm’s next phase of growth as a global company.

Sources indicate that sultania founded the business with Anand Nigam. Its software applies AI to feedback from customers and employees, combining survey tools, social listening and journey mapping in a single system that covers customer experience, employee experience and market research. Those inputs are designed to produce decision-ready insights.

XEBO.ai reports having served more than 350 organisations in over 10 industries, among them banking, aviation, telecommunications, insurance, retail, healthcare and the public sector. Its clients span EMEA, Asia Pacific and North America, while more than 100 employees work across offices in the United States, the UAE, India and Saudi Arabia.

In a recent development, the commercial strategy extends beyond product engineering. XEBO.ai intends to broaden go-to-market activity across the GCC, establish a US presence, strengthen teams and appoint senior leaders across functions. It also intends to use its technology integration with Microsoft, together with other partner channels, to reach additional enterprises internationally.

EGF’s contribution also includes operational support. The fund will work with management on governance and senior hiring and provide access to its government and private-sector network, under an investment made through its Advanced Technology mandate.

Industry observers note that on EGF’s official website, the fund describes itself as an AED 1 billion growth investor backed by Emirates Development Bank, with the aim of helping UAE-based small and medium-sized enterprises build scale, resilience and global competitiveness. Advanced technology sits alongside manufacturing, food and agriculture, and healthcare among its listed investment sectors.

In a recent development, anshul Garg, an investment director at EGF, mentioned XEBO.ai had built a product addressing a real market need and won the trust of demanding enterprise customers. “That commercial foundation, combined with differentiated AI capabilities, makes it a compelling investment for EGF. We want to help the organization translate its regional success into international growth,” he said.

Sources indicate that the funding gives XEBO.ai room to pursue product development and commercial expansion together. Its next phase will turn on how effectively the new analytics and agentic-AI capabilities, combined with EGF’s hiring support and network, can translate an established enterprise footprint into wider international adoption.
