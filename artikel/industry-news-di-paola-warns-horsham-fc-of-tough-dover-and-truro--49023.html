The Hornets, fresh from their FA Trophy triumph over Torquay United, travel to Kent to take on the Whites this Saturday.

And Dover are ‘having the season of all seasons’, according to Di Paola.

The Whites will welcome former Premier League champions Leicester City to the Megger Community Stadium in the second round proper of the FA Cup on Saturday, December 5 after beating division-above Wealdstone in the previous round.

Dover, who boast former Hornet Jack Bates among their ranks, sit 13th in the National League South, level on 22 points with 12th-placed Horsham albeit with two games in hand.

In a recent development, di Paola told us: “Dover are having the seasons of all seasons with the FA Cup run. Dover are in the play-off mix, they’ve got their FA Cup tie against Leicester, they’ve clearly got a good thing going on.

In a recent development, “It’s always tough down there. They’ve got a significant, wide pitch and big crowds usually and they’ve got some really good players down there, including Batesy [Bates] who was with us last year very briefly.

“It’ll be a good test up there. As I said, they’re in great form and they’re an athletic, mobile side. They’re a big, powerful team who put it on you and make you defend.

“We’re going to have to be resolute and strong mentally and physically to cope with them. It’s going to be another tough challenge for our boys but hopefully we’ll be okay in terms of the squad.”.

The Hornets will then welcome Truro on Tuesday evening, with the visitors facing a round trip of around 505 miles for the midweek encounter at the Fusion Aviation Community Stadium.

The Tinners have impressed since dropping down into step two after relegation from the National League in 2025-26.

The Cornwall outfit are, at the time of writing, fourth in the table and unbeaten in seven in the league, winning four out of their last five.

And Di Paola has predicted another formidable challenge against ‘one of the favourites for the title’.

Industry observers note that he added: “I haven’t done anything on Truro yet. We’ll take a good look at that after Dover on Saturday.

Recent findings suggest that “I think they are one of the favourites for the title so that’ll be a tough game. I’ve watched them seven or eight times when watching some of the teams we’ve played, and they’re a good side.”.

Horsham have no fresh injury concerns, with midfielder Ronan Silva closing in on a return to action.

Recent findings suggest that di Paola noted: “Ronan is almost back. We could have put him on the bench on Saturday, so hopefully by the weekend he’ll be good to go back into the squad.”.
