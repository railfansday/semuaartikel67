It has been revealed that birmingham City's recent progress has led to a strong amount of player turnover at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, and now there is a strong focus on what could be in store come the opening of the January transfer window.

It has been made no secret that Chris Davies' side are looking to reach the Premier League in the near future, with the 41-year-old receiving plenty of backing from Knighthead Capital Management during a near two-and-a-half-year tenure.

Birmingham's strong start to the Championship season followed an intriguing summer transfer window, but they were swiftly knocked off the division's summit last weekend after a lacklustre defeat to fellow promotion hopefuls, Stoke City, on Saturday.

It was an encounter in which plenty of issues which came to a head last season for Blues were evident, including a lack of urgency in attacking areas. As such, the debate has continued as to which areas need strengthening within Davies' squad with the January window looming large.

On top of such decisions, it feels inevitable that some of those within the Blues ranks may move on to new pastures, including rather luckless full-back, Lee Buchanan.

Buchanan's senior career began seven years ago, earning a first-team berth with Derby County under Phillip Cocu for the 2019/20 campaign.

It wasn't until the 2020/21 campaign, which saw the Rams narrowly escape relegation under the management of Wayne Rooney, where the full-back earned his major breakthrough with 37 appearances, being named the club's Young Player of the Season in the process.

During the early weeks of that particular season, Buchanan was called up to the England Under-20s squad, where he revealed that Rooney, who became his Birmingham boss just under three years later, predicted a bright future in the game for the defender.

“He’s England’s all-time top scorer so speaking to him obviously helps a lot," stated Buchanan.

“He told me to go out there and enjoy myself, to keep doing what I do for the club and that he’s sure I’d be fine.".

The 2021/22 season was a difficult campaign for Derby, although Buchanan was one of several academy graduates to emerge with credit for his performance levels at Pride Park which would have resulted in Championship survival, were it not for a 21-point deduction.

The defender was then the subject of a controversial departure to Bundesliga side, Werder Bremen, where he would link up with current Birmingham teammate, Marvin Ducksch, for the first time, making 21 appearances in the German top-flight before swiftly returning to the Midlands under current Rams head coach, John Eustace.

At 22 years of age, Blues certainly invested in Buchanan's potential in their first transfer window of the Knighthead era, with the left-back penning a five-year deal.

Three of those have now passed, and the defender's time in B9 has become one to forget, not least due to an unfortunate ACL injury which kept him sidelined between December 2024 and August 2026.

Buchanan has only played 40 times for Birmingham since his arrival in July 2023, with the bulk of said appearances coming during the club's relegation campaign of 2023/24, prior to Alex Cochrane's arrival.

Davies opted to select Buchanan during pre-season and in the 1-0 win over Swansea City in the EFL Cup first round following his recovery with the Blues boss saying: “I looked at him before the game and thought if he’s called upon he's ready to go now and it says a lot for the man, as well as the player.”.

In a recent development, whilst Buchanan's character has never been doubted, his long-term position at St. Andrew's is far from secure, and that was certainly the case in the summer window.

In January, Davies was in no rush to loan out the defender despite Kai Wagner arriving as back-up to Cochrane, and even though the German swiftly returned to Philadelphia Union, Buchanan also attracted transfer interest.

League One promotion hopefuls, Luton Town and Reading, were credited with interest, but, ultimately, a move failed to materialise.

At 25 years of age, Buchanan is set to be entering the prime of his career, and the chances of reigniting his career seem very little at Birmingham right now.

Cochrane has continued as Davies' mainstay following the exit of Wagner, and whilst Kristoffer Lund has also suffered injury misfortune this season, the seven-time USMNT international does appear to be ahead of Buchanan in the pecking order.

In January, there will be 18 months left on the full-back's contract, making it a prime opportunity for Blues to recoup some form of cash on his services, whilst allowing the player to rediscover form and match sharpness after just one competitive appearance 23 months.
