The radio and podcasts can be listened to in a number of ways in GTA 6.

The most obvious way is while driving. In any vehicle, if the radio isn't on already, hold Left and turn it on with Cross/X. You can scroll through stations with L1 and R1:.

To access podcasts, move the right stick down:.

Outside of vehicles, you can find radios dotted around the environment; you can toggle them on with Left and choose a station (you cannot listen to podcasts on a radio):.

Another option is to use headphones or earbuds.

Electronics stores sell over-ear headphones and earbuds, which allow you to listen to radio stations and podcasts while you're on foot.

To find an electronics store, open the map with Touch Pad, then use the directional buttons to reach Style, then Electronics. Nearby shops will populate the list, and you can set a waypoint from there:.

Once you've bought a pair, equip them by holding L1, pressing R1 to access your items, then highlighting Headgear and pressing Cross/X as shown:.

Now, you will be able to select radio stations and podcasts just like when you're driving.
