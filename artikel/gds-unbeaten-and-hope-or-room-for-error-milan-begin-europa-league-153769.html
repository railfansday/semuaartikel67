📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

AC Milan have four games left to play in the Europa League and they’re in the do-or-die stage of the competition already.

Sources indicate that thoughts on the Europa League this season were mixed. On paper, Milan should have been competing at the top. Yet, they’ve struggled through the League Phase so far, leaving themselves with something of a mountain to climb, starting against Olympiacos later this week.

It has been revealed that as Gazzetta dello Sport writes, the Rossoneri are in a more-than-difficult position. Sitting 28th in the rankings, they are currently outside the qualification spots and calculations are difficult to make about how much they’ll need to improve.

From the past two seasons, they’ll need between 14 and 16 points to qualify for the top eight, which feels like a distant hope. If they’re to achieve that amount, they must win all four of their remaining games and hope that 15 is enough.

According to recent reports, the play-offs, on the other hand, are more than feasible. Sitting four places off currently, there’s a one-point gap to overturn and history states they’ll need either nine or 10 points. This would allow far more room for error, with only two wins and a draw needed to ‘guarantee’ a spot in the next round.

In Greece, they’ll face the first hurdle and getting three points would make life much easier for everyone involved.
