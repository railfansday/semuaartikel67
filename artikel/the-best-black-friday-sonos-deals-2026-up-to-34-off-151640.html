It's Black Friday, and I am seeing some serious Black Friday Sonos deals for 2026. While there is nothing earth-shattering (don't expect to see any 50 percent offs here), I'm always on the lookout for a good Sonos deal, as the brand makes some of our favorite audio products. Black Friday deals like these below make all of their products easier to recommend, as they tend to be more expensive in general.

In a recent development, I've been reviewing audio products and covering major sale events like Black Friday for over four years. Below are the best Black Friday Sonos deals I found. I'll be sure to keep this updated through the weekend and Cyber Monday, though I don't expect much to change—brands like Sonos historically keep their discounts pretty consistent over the massive shopping event.

Sources indicate that the Sonos Arc Ultra is our favorite soundbar, and this deal brings it down to its lowest price ever. The Arc Ultra is a fantastic all-in-one solution to any home entertainment setup, with convincing, surround sound audio in a single-speaker package.

Recent findings suggest that if the Arc Ultra is outside your budget (or simply too dang long to fit on your media console), the compact Beam (Gen 2) is also at its lowest price ever right now. This was my home soundbar for years—it's tiny but mighty.

Sources indicate that want to add some serious bass in a small package? The Sonos Sub Mini is your best bet. It's smaller than most subwoofers on the market and pairs seamlessly with other Sonos speakers.

Recent findings suggest that if you're truly looking to create a home theater even your local cinema will be jealous of, the Sub 4 is the sub to get. It packs serious punch and serious depth (though it's very major).

According to recent reports, the Sonos Era 100 is one of our favorite compact home audio speakers. It delivers on all fronts—sound quality, compact size, convenience, and smarts. This deal is only OK, though (in 2025, it was discounted even further to $169).

Recent findings suggest that the Sonos Era 100 SL is the exact same speaker as the regular Era 100, minus the microphones and voice control. Ditching those features puts it at a lower cost, especially on sale.

One of the only home speakers built with spatial audio in mind. The Sonos Era 300 is a great speaker, but transforms when you play Dolby Atmos tracks, activating its upfiring tweeters for more dimensionality.

This giant Sonos speaker is getting a little long in the tooth (it released in 2020), but it still delivers the strongest, most bass-heavy sound of any single Sonos speaker.

Sonos' smallest speaker, the Roam 2, is a handheld portable Bluetooth speaker you can basically take anywhere. What separates it from the pack (and Sonos' Bluetooth speakers generally) is that they also connect to Wi-Fi to sync up with your home speakers.

A step up from the Roam 2, the Sonos Play is one of the brand's newest portable speakers, and one of its best, according to former WIRED reviewer Ryan Waniata.

In a recent development, I would argue that the Sonos Move 2 is more of a portable home Bluetooth speaker than a lightweight, compact, throw-it-in-your-bag for a trip to the beach-type speaker like the Roam 2 and Play above. Think of it like a portable, dust and water resistant version of the Era 100.

Recent findings suggest that the latest Sonos Ace Ultra headphones are not on sale, but the capable originals are. The Sonos Ace don't connect or sync to other Sonos products like the brand's speakers do, but they are capable stand-alone headphones, with clear sound, a comfortable fit, and a surprisingly good transparency mode.
