Is China Misreading the Moment?

🌐 Source: Realclearpolitics • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (94 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that when U.S. President Donald Trump lands in Shenzhen later this week for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, he will sit down with Chinese leader Xi Jinping for the fourth time amid an unexpected detente between Washington and Beijing. The trade war of 2025 is in abeyance, the two governments have confirmed a relationship of constructive strategic stability, and, to the alarm of American allies, Trump and Xi are planning to meet with Russian President Vladimir Putin. Beneath the surface, however, the detente is beginning to crack.
