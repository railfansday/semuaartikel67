Newcastle United host Arsenal on Saturday.

A 5.30pm kick-off against the Gunners.

All of the ‘experts’ taking for granted that Arsenal will win against Newcastle United.

The reigning champions having won nine of their ten Premier League matches and clear at the top, plus they haven’t conceded a single goal in their last seven hours of EPL football.

They say timing is the secret of great comedy.

Well, the timing of this football match is absolutely perfect for Newcastle United and it is no great secret. Not if you look hard enough.

There is something about this time of year at St James’ Park, darkness setting in, under the lights at SJP.

Something magical happens, time after time.

Here are the St James’ Park matches of recent seasons at this time of year….

2022-23 Newcastle 4 Aston Villa 0 (29 October 2022) Newcastle 0 Palace 0 and NUFC win 3-2 on penalties (9 November 2022) Newcastle 1 Chelsea 0 (12 November 2022).

2023-24 Newcastle 1 Arsenal 0 (4 November 2023) Newcastle 4 Chelsea 1 (25 November 2023_.

2024-25 Newcastle 2 Chelsea 0 (30 October 2024) Newcastle 1 Arsenal 0 (2 November 2024).

2025-26 Newcastle 2 Manchester City 1 (22 November 2025).

As you can see, a stunning set of results around this time of the year.

Especially our cockney friends, Arsenal and Chelsea, the cold dark nights on Tyneside and even better when it is a 5.30pm kick-off and a turbo alcohol charged crowd.

As fans, we just need to do our bit and let the dark and the cold do the rest!
