Jennifer Garner is getting a head start on Christmas at her Los Angeles home, where thousands of lights are already twinkling and monogrammed stockings hang above the fireplace.

The 54-year-old actress shared a look at her holiday decorations on Instagram, revealing that even her greenhouse got a festive touch with a wreath of its own.

Industry observers note that thanksgiving preparations were underway in the kitchen, where Garner gave the turkey a trial run ahead of the big day. Her dogs and cat were dressed for the occasion, too, in turkey costumes.

“We’re ready over here,” Garner wrote in the caption.

Garner has been known to love the holiday season, having previously opened up about her favorite memories during a Nov. 4 episode of the "Happy Hour With Ina Garten" podcast.

“My favorite Thanksgivings were when my kids were really little, I think it was right at the end of 'Alias,' and it was at a big house,” remarked Garner, who shares Violet, 20, Seraphina, 17, and Samuel, 14, with her ex-husband Ben Affleck.

“My kids were little, and Ben and I were there, and we all cooked. Bradley made the stuffing and Victor made ... I think Victor’s sister came. It was just our little 'Alias' family cooking together. It was so special," she added, referring to her co-stars Victor Garber, Ron Rifkin, and Bradley Cooper.

Despite separating in 2015 and divorcing in 2018, Garner and Affleck have continued to celebrate the holidays together.

Last year, a source told People that the couple spent the holidays together in Los Angeles.

“Jen makes it very special. She’s such a great chef. She loves having her whole family together, too. Ben’s always welcome at her house,” the source told the outlet.

Garner is known for showing off touching memories at her dwelling.

Last year, Garner and her mom were brought to tears when they were surprised with a near-perfect re-creation of the kitchen that sat inside the actress' childhood home in West Virginia.

Garner and her mom, Patricia Ann, couldn't help but get emotional when they were brought to the space, which had been designed by appliance tech giant KitchenAid to perfectly mirror the kitchen of their Charleston, WV, home.

The mother-daughter duo initially thought they were being brought in to take part in a holiday photo shoot, but after stepping inside the kitchen set, which looked identical to the one in their Greystone Place abode, they were left gobsmacked.

Both Garner and her mom began welling up at seeing their vintage wooden table and green refrigerator, with both women promptly recalling the happy memories they had made in the original space.

“This is where we would hang out, all our crafts happened right here with somebody looking through from that side," Garner said, while looking through the cupboards. "This is the cabinet where you hid the chocolate chips.".

The kitchen boasted a large wooden table, matching wooden cabinets, yellow oven, granite countertops, drawings, and a yellow KitchenAid mixer.

Industry observers note that the "13 Going on 30" star then pointed at a nostalgic photo of her mom that was placed on the counter. It reminded her of the days that Patricia would get her perm done while "sitting at the table.".

“It’s a privilege to get to see where we used to be. I want that to be the den, and then I want to hear dad come home from work. That’s what I feel," Patricia noted.

The 87-year-old noted that the patterned wallpaper in the replica kitchen first caught her eye.

Garner noted that the experience gave her a "really deep feeling of homesickness and gratitude.".

Patricia previously joined her daughter on Garner's latest installment of her "Pretend Cooking Show.".
