French team: Christophe Dugarry reveals the reason for the absence of Jean Butez

For the two Nations League matches against Italy and Turkey, Zinédine Zidane found his three goalkeepers. During the September rally, the coach of the France team chose to call Jean Butez (31 years old, Como) in order to compensate for the loss of Robin Risser (21 years old, Lens). This time, the Lensois is indeed present alongside Mike Maignan (31 years old, AC Milan) and Guillaume Restes (21 years old, Toulouse). The decision to do without the services of the Como doorman may have been surprising given his recent performances. But according to Christophe Dugarry, his friend Zidane has a very clear strategy.

“Today, they started with a strategy of trying to relaunch Mike Maignan as best as possible and to have young people behind him. (…) Fabien Barthez and Zinédine Zidane think that these two players (Restes and Risser, editor’s note) are young players, but who have real potential, they believe in it. Zizou and Fabien are part of the long term. Maignan will be the starter for the next Euro. Will he be ready at 35 for the next World Cup? Today, they are looking for a goalkeeper for the next World Cup. So taking a 31-year-old guy who plays in Como, as good as he is, that’s not the point. There is already a number one today. There is no need to look for a holder. Today, we have to look for a future holder, a future number 2”he declared at the microphone of RMC.
