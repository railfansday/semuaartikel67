K-beauty stocks rally as Korean brands top Amazon bestseller lists globally ahead of Black Friday

South Korean beauty stocks jumped Thursday as the country's skincare brands dominated Amazon's bestseller rankings ahead of Black Friday.

Shares of K-beauty giant APR climbed 3.77%, while cosmetic contracts manufacturers Kolmar Korea and Cosmax gained 5.46% and 2.91%, respectively.

APR shares, in particular, have rallied sharply this week, up 28%, defying a broader market sell-off in Seoul markets, with the benchmark Kospi index down 2.65% during the same period.

A cosmetic sector index tracked by Meritz Securities had gained 12% this week until Wednesday, according to a Thursday note from the brokerage.

The rally reflects renewed optimism over earnings growth following strong performances by K-beauty brands across major e-commerce platforms in the U.S. and Europe with Black Friday sales already underway, as well as increasingly attractive valuations following months of share price declines, according to Meritz.

Sources indicate that K-beauty products topped Amazon's skincare bestseller rankings across six major markets — the U.S., the U.K., Germany, France, Spain and Italy — as of Nov. 25, according to data compiled by Meritz Securities. Other Korean beauty brands, including Biodance, Illiyoon and Anua, also ranked among the top 10 in the U.S., the data demonstrated.

As of Thursday morning, Korean beauty products occupied several of the top spots in Amazon's U.S. beauty and personal care category, the e-commerce website highlighted.

Meritz Securities expects APR shares to maintain their positive momentum through the year-end, with a more substantial rebound in early 2027.

The brokerage named APR and Cosmax as its top picks in the cosmetics sector. Korean beauty brands have established an "overwhelming presence" in the bestseller rankings of major e-commerce platforms during Black Friday sales, prompting the market to reassess the growth's potential, the brokerage said.
