📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Aster [ASTER] has been making headlines over the past day due to its sharp price fluctuations. After climbing to $0.7124, the token plunged to $0.4812 before making a notable recovery over the past 24 hours, seeing an increase of 4.29%.

In a recent development, the token’s volatile activity prompted Aster’s CEO, Leonard Leung, to announce that an investigation was released into the price’s abnormal movements, which he attributed to liquidations of highly leveraged positions, potentially involving malicious activity. He clarified that the incident was unrelated to market-making arrangements and that the platform was operating normally.

Further details are expected in subsequent reports.

The daily chart revealed bulls fighting to recover some of the ground lost during the recent selloff. At the time of writing, ASTER was trading at $0.5342, breaching the 23.6% Fibonacci level. This suggested that buyers had successfully reclaimed part of the preceding decline.

Indicators had yet to confirm a trend reversal, and in fact, hinted that the recovery was an early rebound. The OBV’s sharp rebound suggested that buyers had returned to the market. However, RSI at 31.99 highlighted that the bullish momentum was still extremely weak, despite just recovering from deeply oversold territory.

The H4 chart offered a clearer yet more cautious picture. Like the daily timeframe, ASTER had also reclaimed the 23.6% Fibonacci level at $0.5236, suggesting that the recovery was still relatively shallow.

Moreover, OBV had started ticking downward again after its initial rebound, indicating that buying interest was losing strength. The MACD suggested that bearish momentum was beginning to ease, although the overall trend remained weak.

Although ASTER’s recovery had some underlying buying support, the lower timeframe had yet to confirm a sustained shift in momentum. Therefore, unless buyers regain strength, the rebound could struggle to advance towards the next Fibonacci resistance levels.
