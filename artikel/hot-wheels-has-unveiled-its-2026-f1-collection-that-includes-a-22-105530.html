We're still reeling from Lego's F1 announcement, but now Mattel has given F1 fans another reason to clear shelf space.

Mattel and F1 have unveiled an expanded Hot Wheels Formula 1 collection that includes all 11 teams and 22 cars from the grid for the first time. The significant headline for collectors is the debut of F1’s two newest constructors: Cadillac and Audi Revolut.

In a recent development, we've all come to expect glaring holes in these F1 collections. Teams constantly shift or expand, so it's not easy for the likes of Mattel. Getting to see 11 teams in a single cohesive lineup is a major win for fans and collectors.

Mattel is splitting the 2026 grid across four distinct price points. Budget-minded collectors can grab Hot Wheels Silver Series Tooned Formula 1 cars for just $3.99 each, while serious collectors can step up to Hot Wheels Premium Formula 1 single cars ($9.99) and driver 2-Packs ($24.99), which offer full metal bodies and chassis, Real Riders tires, display plinths, and collector packaging.

If you want the entire paddock in one shot, the Hot Wheels Formula 1 Season Display Set ($220) packs all 11 teams alongside a double-sided display mat, with exclusive preorders opening on Mattel from Dec. 4.

It's vital to note that we won't see the first Cadillac and Audi $9.99 Premium vehicles until February 2027, with updated liveries for the remaining nine teams, the $3.99 Tooned cars, and fresh Racing Formula 1 Track Sets rolling out gradually throughout 2027.
