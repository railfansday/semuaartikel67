Recent findings suggest that the release of GTA 6 has been a swirling vortex which most companies have actively worked to avoid. Beyond avoiding this period for launching anything, lots of other studios have opted to avoid putting out any substantial updates on their games either. Now that the initial fervor is dying down a little, the team behind Palworld is putting feelers out on letting fans know what's coming on that front.

Sources indicate that over on Twitter, John 'Bucky' Buckley, the head of communications and publishing at Pocketpair, tweeted about whether the coast was clear. "Is it OK to start talking about Palworld 1.1 and Palfarm now?" he posted, hinting at the Grand Theft Auto 6-sized blanket that now lays over the collective medium.

Last week, he was quite clear that we wouldn't be hearing about the next major patch for Palworld, or any of the game's spin-offs, for a minute because it just wasn't the right time. Rockstar's gargantuan sandbox was sucking all the air out of the room.

Is it OK to start talking about Palworld v1.1 and Palfarm now? 😇November 24, 2026.

Not everyone was playing GTA 6 at launch, nor does every person into video games care for it, but enough do that it's crucial to be wary of the news cycle and the attention it's getting at the moment. Headline space is predominantly going toward Leonida for the time being, and I say that as someone who's writing about it in some form on the daily.

That'll loosen up as the weeks go on, because there'll be less to cover regarding Jason and Lucia, and people's attention will start to scatter again. Those Palworld players who've departed for sunnier skies will come back, ready to see what's going on in the realm of monster collecting.

Industry observers note that I'm not sure that's right now, but it'll be soon. The first week or two is really the peak, and then other games can start poking back into the limelight. World of Warcraft: Forever and Final Fantasy 14 are happening, as well. The industry is still turning.

Once the Palfarm and Palworld 1.1 updates arrive, we'll make sure you know. It's just extra helpful if there's less chance they get immediately buried under yet more Leonida-shaped shenanigans.
