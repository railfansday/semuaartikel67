Recent findings suggest that VANCOUVER, BC and LONDON, Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- WeFetch AI Corp. today reported 30 million in AI agent traffic over the prior 30 days across its products. Shoppers now use AI, and gtinfetch.ai delivers the barcode for the AI agents of consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands and retailers. gtinfetch.ai, the Company's lead product, holds 84,810,515 barcodes, known as Global Trade Item Numbers (GTINs), and eliminates the guesswork.

The market: McKinsey projects that AI agents will orchestrate $3 trillion to $5 trillion in global commerce by 2030, up to $1 trillion of it in US retail. Every AI agent purchase needs the right barcode.

An AI agent that shops needs the barcode, not a description of the product. gtinfetch.ai is the agentic software that delivers it: the right barcode, verified, to the AI agent that asks. gtinfetch.ai removes the guesswork and connects consumers shopping with AI to the brands. Access is now sold two ways.

In a recent development, the enterprise licence and PayGo on x402 run on the same software and the same 84 million barcodes. gtinfetch.ai holds the barcodes in full on Microsoft Azure in France, Singapore and the United States, with redundancies on Amazon Web Services (AWS). gtinfetch.ai is live on Model Context Protocol (MCP) at mcp.gtinfetch.ai/mcp.

The Fastest Shift in FMCG and Retail on Record.

Industry observers note that in 2024, shopping with AI was a curiosity: a few hundred million people used AI at all. In the fourth quarter of 2026, three billion people use AI, and more than half of consumers shop with AI. The change took 24 months and reached fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) and retail first.

In a recent development, FMCG and Retail Now Run AI Agents. Their Product Data Is Not Ready.

Shoppers moved to AI first. Brands and retailers are now building AI agents to meet AI shoppers, on product data that was never made for AI agents.

Recent findings suggest that the same product carries a different description in every system. An AI agent that matches on words can pick the wrong size, the wrong pack or the wrong maker. The barcode is the one identifier that the brand, the retailer and the AI agent already share.

"We built gtinfetch.ai as the AI agentic shopping barcode engine, the largest AI-agent-native source of verified barcodes, for every consumer AI agent that buys," remarked Matthew Keddy, Chief Executive Officer of WeFetch AI Corp. "A shopper's AI query starts as text, and it turns into a sale more reliably when the answer is anchored to a barcode. With 84 million barcodes and 30 million in monthly AI agent traffic, we connect our customers to the sale.".
