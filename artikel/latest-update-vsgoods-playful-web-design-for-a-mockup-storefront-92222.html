📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

VS/Goods pairs a warm-stone web design with a dark asset shelf, floating award cards, and interactive mockups built for Figma and Jitter.

According to recent reports, born out of a few templates the team built for Jitter that turned out to be too much fun to stop, VS/Goods skips the usual sterile filter grid. Instead, the storefront greets visitors with a fanned stack of editorial portraits and a clean top bar holding just Menu, All assets, and Account.

Industry observers note that scroll past the opening stack and the page flips from bright white to charcoal dark. Here, tilted pastel cards flash recognition from Awwwards, Motion Design Awards, The Webby Awards, FWA, and CSS Design Awards above a crisp headline. It's a quick credibility beat, and it sets up the dark asset shelf right below it.

Recent findings suggest that that shelf treats each template like a playable preview rather than a flat thumbnail. Cards for Typography, Animated Screens, Animated Mobile Screens, and Rotating Cards sit side by side with clear euro pricing and bright pink Try For Free buttons. You don't have to guess how a file moves before clicking in. Further down, the background warms into an oatmeal tone for the community wall, where softly masked project tiles orbit headlines like Real projects, real energy and All created with our mockups.

Sources indicate that even the footer refuses to sit still. A full-width VS/Goods wordmark with its wavy flag emblem spans the dark canvas above a rotating analog watch dial stamped with the line Move Fast Design Right. Together, the pacing between light editorial sections and dark product shelves gives this web design a rhythm that matches the motion assets it sells.
