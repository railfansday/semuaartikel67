There’s something particularly satisfying about finding a great gift at a reasonable price point. And when it comes to design, a smaller price tag doesn’t have to mean settling for something ordinary. Some of the most memorable objects are also the simplest: a cleverly designed kitchen tool, a colorful accessory, or an everyday essential with an unexpected twist.

For this year’s selection, we’re looking for gifts that bring a little more personality to the everyday. Think playful forms, interesting materials, useful details, and objects that reflect the creativity of the people who designed them. Whether you’re shopping for a friend, a coworker, or someone who’s notoriously difficult to buy for, these gifts under $50 offer plenty of reasons to think beyond the usual suspects.
