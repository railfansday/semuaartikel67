Remi Wolf Earns First Billboard No. 1 as ‘Bottle’ Tops Adult Alternative Airplay

Remi Wolf scores the first Billboard No. 1 of her career as her single “Bottle” rises 2-1 on the Adult Alternative Airplay chart (dated Nov. 28).

Industry observers note that released Aug. 20 via Island/Republic Records, the track reaches the summit with a 13% increase in spins at the format in the Nov. 13-19 tracking week, according to Luminate. (The Adult Alternative Airplay chart measures songs’ weekly plays, as tabulated by Mediabase and provided to Billboard by Luminate, on reporting U.S. adult alternative, or triple A, radio stations.) “Bottle” became Wolf’s first Adult Alternative Airplay entry. She first appeared on Billboard’s charts dated Dec. 12, 2020, when the aptly titled “Hello Hello Hello” debuted on Alternative Airplay, before reaching No. 28.

“Bottle” is the second single from the Palo Alto, Calif.-raised artist’s third studio album, Mud. The set reached No. 18 on the Billboard 200 and No. 4 on Top Alternative Albums in October, both career highs. Her previous best came with 2024’s Substantial Ideas, which reached No. 95 and No. 14, respectively. Six Mud have hit Hot Rock & Alternative Songs, led by “Bottle” (No. 32 peak). The others: “Champagne” (No. 35), “I Won’t Cry” (No. 37), “Twiggy” (No. 40), “Moon So High,” with John Mayer (No. 44), and “Burn Me Blue” (No. 50).

“Bottle” is also the first Adult Alternative Airplay No. 1 released on Island this year, and the fifth this decade, following Alabama Shakes’ “Another Life” (2025), Brittany Howard’s “What Now,” U2’s “Atomic City” (both in 2023) and the Killers’ “Boy” (2022).

The No. 1 caps a big week for Wolf. On Monday (Nov. 16), she earned her first Grammy nomination, for best latest artist. The week before, she was declared as a performer at the 2027 Coachella festival and as a supporting act for select dates on Noah Kahan’s The Last of the Bugs North American tour next June. She also unveiled tour dates for her own Mud headlining tour, which kicks off in February.
