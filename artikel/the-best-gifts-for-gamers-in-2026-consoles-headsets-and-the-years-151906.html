Everyone has a gamer in their life, and the holidays are the perfect time to get them something special, whether it’s a latest console, a buzzy title, a pair of headphones or an accessory that lets them play on the go.

Variety has curated this list of the best video game gifts in 2026, so you can surprise your gamer loved ones with the perfect presents this holiday season.

Nintendo’s latest upgrade to its flagship console is a crowd-pleaser for all ages. Its modular design means you can play on the TV or in handheld mode, with a bigger, sharper screen than the original Switch. The Switch 2 is perfect for the person in your life who is obsessed with “Super Mario,” “Pokémon” and “The Legend of Zelda.” Some highly anticipated fresh games are only available on the newest console, while most older Switch games still work on it too.

For the gamer who wants Xbox’s huge library without the higher price tag of the Series X, the Xbox Series S is a great option. It’s all-digital and compact enough to fit onto a crowded media shelf. Still, it runs the year’s biggest games, including “Grand Theft Auto VI” and “Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4.” Pair it with a Game Pass subscription and get access to hundreds of titles.

In a recent development, the PlayStation 5 is best for substantial, blockbuster games, and it’s the console to beat for anyone who loves an interactive cinematic adventure. “Grand Theft Auto VI” landed on the PS5 to near-perfect reviews, and it’s the only console where you can play “Marvel’s Wolverine.” The experience is top-notch down to the controllers, and the deep library spans everything from family-friendly hits to grown-up thrillers.

Experience one of the greatest video games ever made, built anew for the Switch 2. Hyrule has never looked this lush, and modern controls and smart updates smooth out the original Nintendo 64 game’s frustrations while keeping every dungeon, side quest and secret intact. You can even play Link’s songs by humming into the console’s microphone. This is the perfect gift for nostalgic “Zelda” fans and newcomers preparing for Nintendo’s upcoming live-action movie.

Recent findings suggest that take part in the cultural phenomenon. Thirteen years after “GTA V,” the most anticipated game of all time is finally here, and critics have deemed it worth the wait. “Grand Theft Auto VI” heads back to Vice City in Rockstar’s sprawling, sun-baked take on Florida. The story follows criminal duo Lucia and Jason, a modern-day Bonnie and Clyde, in a world so detailed it could take you a lifetime to run out of fun discoveries. For the adult gamer on your list — this is definitely no “Mario Party” — no other game comes close this year.

In a return to form, “Call of Duty” has produced the series’ best campaign in years with “Modern Warfare 4.” The new game features a globe-spanning story built around a North Korean invasion of South Korea. The multiplayer game mode lets you play online with friends, and it brings back DMZ, in which squads battle enemies and rival players to escape with their loot. For the first-person shooter fan in your life, this is a sure bet.

Industry observers note that every kid with a Wii’s dream vacation spot was Wuhu Island, the tropical resort where you can skydive above the clouds, duel above the ocean and play ping-pong by the beach. “Nintendo Switch Sports Resort” brings back the sunny getaway spot, updating Wuhu Island for the Switch. The game features 12 sports, including golf, bowling, archery and a surprisingly intense thumb-wrestling event, all played with simple motion controls the whole family can learn. This is the ultimate, easy holiday party game for grandparents, kids and everyone in between.

In a recent development, video games exist in everyone’s pocket. And with the 8BitDo FlipPad mobile gaming controller, you can turn your smartphone into a handheld console. The tiny controller has a built-in USB-C connection, which allows you to plug and play any number of mobile games immediately. This is the perfect affordable gift for a gamer who likes to play on the go.

It has been revealed that what’s old is latest again. Vinyl, VHS and now the Evercade Nexus, a retro gaming system built around curated cartridges of classic games, with no downloads, subscriptions or online accounts required. This bundle includes a cartridge with “Banjo-Kazooie” and “Banjo-Tooie,” two of the most beloved 3D platformers from the 1990s. Play the Nexus on the go or on the TV and grow your collection of cartridges from a library of more than 700 classic games, from “Metal Slug” to “Earthworm Jim.” It’s simple enough for kids and pure nostalgia for their parents, which makes it a gift the whole household can share.

Recent findings suggest that excited about the announcement that Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond? Go deeper into the spy franchise with “007 First Light,” a brand-fresh origin story from the makers of the “Hitman” games. The game follows a young Bond from his early days in the Navy to his first missions with MI6. It plays like a movie you can control, with sneaky spycraft, car chases and explosive set pieces across luxurious locations. Lana Del Rey even wrote the theme song!

One of the best-selling video game franchises in the world is back with another installment, and “Mario Kart World” is its biggest iteration yet. Instead of separate tracks, the game’s courses are stitched into one giant world you can drive across without stopping, almost like an open-world game. Plus, each race now holds 24 racers, which means twice the red shells and banana peels. The standout game mode is Knockout Tour, a marathon race where players are eliminated at each checkpoint until one champion remains. This game is perfect for the whole family, so fire up the engines and pray you don’t get blue shelled.

A PlayStation 5 exclusive, “Marvel’s Wolverine” hails from Insomniac, the studio behind the hit “Spider-Man” games. “Wolverine” is a full superhero blockbuster, and it’s much darker and bloodier than “Spider-Man.” The game’s story finds Logan clawing his way through the mystery of his own past, and the controller rumbles with every grisly slash. For PS5 owners, this is one of the year’s must-have games.

Imagine “Animal Crossing,” but with Pokémon. In “Pokémon Pokopia,” you can rebuild a ruined world into a paradise for Pokémon — where they can live, farm, craft and just vibe. There are no battles to stress over, just a relaxing routine of gardening, building and making new friends. This game is certainly not for your action junkie friend, but it is the cozy pick of the year, a surprise hit that was well-liked by critics.

For the “Grand Theft Auto” fan who can’t get enough of its soundtrack, this vinyl bundle has 34 original tracks inspired by the game’s neon-soaked Vice City. Artists include Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, Travis Scott, SZA, Justice, Tame Impala, Burna Boy, Jennie, Morgan Wallen and Keith Richards. Pressed on two transparent magenta discs with blue splatter, the records look as good on the shelf as they sounds on the turntable.

For gamers who like to talk to their friends while playing — or just like to soak up the sound with a good pair of headphones — the HyperX Cloud III is the solid, budget-friendly pick. They’re wired, which means no batteries are necessary, and it plugs into every console. And controls sit on the ear cup for easy volume and mic modification.
