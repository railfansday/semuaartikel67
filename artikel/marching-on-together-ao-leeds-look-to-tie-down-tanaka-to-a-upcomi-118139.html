Published: 14:08 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 03:18 EST, 25 November 2026.

Leeds United are in advanced talks with Japan international Ao Tanaka over a latest contract.

The 28-year-old midfielder is close to agreeing terms on a deal that will keep him at Elland Road until 2029 with his current contract due to expire in the summer of 2028.

Leeds have lost only 10 games out of the 66 Tanaka has started since joining the club from Fortuna Dusseldorf in 2024. He has had an impressive start to the current campaign, becoming a key figure in Daniel Farke's side, starting in nine of Leeds' 11 Premier League matches. They right now sit seventh in the table.

Leeds have already tied down Ethan Ampadu to a four-year deal while Joe Rodon, Jayden Bogle and Brenden Aaronson all signed fresh contracts during the summer transfer window.

Talks are also expected to progress over a new deal for Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who has been linked with a move to bitter rivals Manchester United as they look to bolster their striking department.

Discussing Tanaka's form ahead of their emphatic win over Chelsea at the weekend, Farke said: 'It was a major ask for him after spending many years on second-tier level in Germany or here in the Championship.
