📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Koto's Nubank brand refresh turns a 140-million-customer bank into one flexible system: purple, modular, and unmistakably Nu.

In a recent development, nubank started in Brazil with a simple idea, that banking did not need to be so complicated. It now serves more than 140 million people across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. Koto kept the logo and wordmark, refined the signature purple inside a wider palette, and built a system of color, modules and layers that stretches across new markets.

The type does the heavy lifting. Nu Sans Display and Nu Sans Text are Nubank's first proprietary typefaces, drawn to hold up as poster-scale headlines and app labels alike. Display runs huge and loose across the ads, white on purple, the small purple nu square sitting inside the copy like punctuation.

According to recent reports, the grid is a set of simple rules. Color, modules and layers combine and recombine, so the same ingredients build a billboard, a card or a phone screen. Photo strips stagger across the purple field, and motion extends the same logic, from bold brand moments to animated illustrations in the app.

Sources indicate that the physical pieces carry the same discipline. The purple Mastercard keeps its clean face, chip and wordmark, and the welcome card pairs it with illustrated green hills and blue sky under "Hello Alex!" The card is the product, and Koto photographs it that way, on a restaurant table among receipts.

Sources indicate that digital is where the system works hardest. Koto rebuilt nu.com to hold products, languages and regulations behind one effortless front, with a bright purple hero, a row of photography and a giant lavender "That's Nu." The app follows the same order, a piggy bank banner, deposit and send buttons, balances split between spending and savings.

Two libraries give the system range. The global photography library, more than 50 photos and films, shows real relationships and unguarded moments of joy, people out in the world instead of models selling a bank. Beside it sits a hand-painted illustration library, drawn to move as well as stand still, from the welcome art's green hills to the rider on a purple card.

The refresh took the stage in Miami, at Nu Stadium, on September 10, 2026, as Nubank started operations in the United States and introduced Nu Global, a multi-currency account for global lives. Koto shaped the moment from screen to stage, with launch films and the founder's keynote.

It has been revealed that the sub-brands stretch the system without breaking it. Croma turns more expressive and colorful, with a gold card and a Portuguese voice that talks about progress rewarded. Ultravioleta goes richer and more refined, deep purple packaging with gold lettering and debossed type, premium without old codes of exclusivity.
