Tech Report: France: Retirees march against 2027 budget cuts

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

More than a thousand people marched in Paris on Thursday, called by a union alliance joined by student and high-school groups, to protest social and tax measures targeting retirees in the 2027 budgets. The government wants retirees to contribute about €5.4 billion, by freezing the indexation of pensions above €1,260 and reforming their 10% income tax allowance. Union leaders say the money should not come from retirees’ pockets, as FRANCE 24's Luke Shrago explains.
