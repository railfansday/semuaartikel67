It appears Gabbriette and Matty Healy are expecting their first child together, and the former is ready to put her “succubus chic” stamp on maternity wear.

Over the weekend, the newly-married pair was seen shopping around Bristol Farms in West Hollywood, California, where Healy played the part of a supportive husband, carrying their bags as Gabbriette cradled her growing baby bump.

The I Know What You Did Last Summer star kicked off her maternity era in gothic fashion, stepping out in a body-hugging black midi dress for a wonderfully moody moment.

Over the years, Gabbriette has refined her singular aesthetic, curating a ’90s-inspired wardrobe of Victorian hues, leather accessories, and smudgy makeup. Even when she got married this past July, she did so in the edgy, alternative label of the moment: Matières Fécales.

For her Icons interview with Harper’s Bazaar this past July, she opened up about her style, saying: “I’m a different person than I was 10 years ago, obviously, but I’ve always loved a leather jacket and a leather pair of pants or some jeans.” Clearly, that’s not going to change with impending motherhood.

On this outing, Gabbriette kept it understated, pairing her simple black dress with a set of leather ballet flats, a chunky bracelet, and a quilted top-handle tote bag from Chanel. Healy, meanwhile, wore a gray tee and distressed jeans with gray boots and a navy zip-up jacket.

In a recent development, the couple tied the knot in Los Angeles this past summer, at the Castillo del Lago mansion in Hollywood Hills. On how she thinks her life may change as a married woman, Gabbriette told Bazaar: “I don’t feel like it’s gonna be very different. I feel like I’ve already met my best friend. … The wedding is for our moms and dads. I wanted to do, like, a 50-person thing in a field, so.”.

However, it seems there will be at least one major change in the next nine months.
