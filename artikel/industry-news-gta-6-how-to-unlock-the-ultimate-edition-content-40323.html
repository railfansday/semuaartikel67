In a recent development, for those who decided to dish out the extra cash to buy the Ultimate Edition of Grand Theft Auto 6, then you’ll get quite a bit for your dollar. The Ultimate Edition comes with a slew of in-game items, but most of them aren’t unlocked immediately.

It has been revealed that while some will appear on Jason’s property right away, most are tied to shops within the game or are found after certain events further into the story, some of which are only available basically at the end. With that said, this will hopefully give you a good idea where to find everything.

These are the first two vehicles you’ll have access to outside of Jason’s Ganado muscle car after you start the game. They will be conveniently placed near Jason’s house in Chapter 1 to take for a ride.

In a recent development, the Ganado Retro Build is one of the first Ultimate Edition item you can get in your campaign, and it’s the easiest. All you will need to do, starting in Chapter 1, is drive into a Pay ‘N’ Spray with Jason’s Ganado, which will be impounded past Chapter 2, and go into Complete Kits. The first option should be the Retro Build, which upgrades Jason’s wheels decently, at least for a starter vehicle.

Sources indicate that there are a couple of revolvers ready to be picked up at an Ammu-Nation locker whenever Lucia gains access to them starting in Chapter 2. These are powerful guns that should be put in your arsenal.

Recent findings suggest that on top of that, there are weapon variants for a handful of guns, such as the Girardi ES9 and Klose K17, which can be customized at a locker whenever you see fit. These will upgrade the two weapons nicely.

In a recent development, this is technically available at the beginning of the game, but you likely won’t come up to the shopping district that houses this store until Chapter 3. This is located within Vice City’s southern portion of the map and has some rather nice new clothes for you to pick up. The downside is that you have to bye them in-game.

According to recent reports, hair is an important feature, so why not hit up Sara’s Unisex Salon? The signature hair is found at every salon in the game, but the unique hairstyles are located in Hamlet starting in Chapter 1. This is located relatively close to the correctional facility.

Recent findings suggest that this is the first boat you’ll be able to own, but it’s only available in Chapter 4. As soon as you move into your upcoming apartment and kick off the chapter, there will be a new icon immediately to the northwest. You’ll find the boat here at the Washington Bach canal dock.

One of the most iconic cars in GTA Vice City, you’re able to pick up this vehicle much later in the game, specifically in Chapter 5 at the Pegassi Residences.
