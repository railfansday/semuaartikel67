The leaders of the United States, Russia and China gathered in Shenzhen for historic three-way talks, emphasizing the importance of continued diplomacy and maintaining open dialogue despite longstanding differences.

President Donald Trump joined the leaders of China and Russia Thursday for an unprecedented three-way summit, marking a historic moment in global diplomacy as the three powers discussed nuclear arms reductions, the war in Ukraine and global security without announcing a major breakthrough.

The unprecedented three-way meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin underscored the leaders' commitment to continued diplomacy, with Trump emphasizing the importance of maintaining dialogue among rival nuclear powers.

It has been revealed that trump left the trilateral meeting, which lasted more than an hour, around 5 a.m. ET and headed to the airport. He is not expected to deliver public remarks before flying back to Washington, Fox News reported.

"We are really making history," Trump declared during the meeting in Shenzhen, China, as he sought to cast the gathering itself as a diplomatic achievement.

The three leaders sat down at a roundtable just after 3 a.m. ET, roughly two hours behind schedule.

Trump argued that bringing the three leaders together offered an opportunity to address problems that had long divided their countries.

Sources indicate that "Too often, past leaders avoided talking to nations with whom they had disagreements," Trump stated. "But no problems were ever solved under the old school refusing to talk about them.".

"Together we are choosing a different course," he added.

Ahead of the summit, Trump told Fox News' Jacqui Heinrich that he wanted to meet with Xi and Putin together "because they work together, they have a relationship.".

In a recent development, trump argued that previous U.S. policies had helped bring Moscow and Beijing closer together, despite their historically strained relationship.

"They were natural enemies, but because of Obama, not Biden, it was Obama before he drove them together because of energy," Trump mentioned.

Russia and China had a fractured relationship for decades following their split in the 1960s, but energy ties deepened through a major 2014 gas deal signed during Obama's presidency after roughly a decade of negotiations.

It has been revealed that xi, who hosted the gathering, called the meeting a "groundbreaking move" and stressed the responsibility of the three powers to maintain global stability. However, no substantial breakthroughs were immediately announced.

"Our positive interaction is not optional, but a must," Xi stated through a translator, arguing that the countries must work together to confront global challenges and preserve international peace.

Putin similarly emphasized the countries' shared responsibility for international security, pointing to their status as permanent members of the United Nations Security Council before turning to one of the summit's most pressing issues: the war in Ukraine.

"Our countries have numerous shared interests in the settlement of regional conflicts, including, obviously, the conflict in Ukraine," Putin mentioned through a translator.

The Russian president confirmed that Ukraine had been a focus of his earlier bilateral meeting with Trump, saying the discussion took place at the American president's initiative.

Trump also addressed the conflict directly, saying he believed both sides wanted to bring the fighting to an end.

"I think things are happening," Trump remarked. "I think that he and I hope the other side would like to see an end to that war.".

Sources indicate that the historic summit followed separate meetings between Trump and the two leaders. Trump met with Putin for approximately 40 minutes before holding a meeting with Xi that lasted more than an hour.

The president also raised the possibility of broader nuclear arms reductions while acknowledging the difficulty of persuading nuclear-armed nations to surrender their weapons.

"It's a very hard thing to do, because once you have the nuclear weapons, it's very hard to not have them," Trump said.

Trump said ahead of the trilateral meeting that the leaders would discuss nuclear proliferation, including Iran, and indicated he would propose ways to protect national sovereignty from international organizations, singling out the International Criminal Court.

Trump held separate bilateral meetings with Putin and Xi earlier in the day, first meeting with the Russian leader at the APEC summit site before traveling to Kylin Villa, a government-owned lakeside resort, for talks with the Chinese president.

Trump stated his meeting with Putin helped "set the stage" for the trilateral talks and expressed hope that cooperation with China could help end the war between Russia and Ukraine.

"The dialogue between President Putin and President Trump, which the entire world is watching closely, is crucial both to resolving the conflict in Ukraine and to advancing relations between Russia and the United States, including economic cooperation," Special Envoy to the Russian President Kirill Dmitriev told Fox News Digital.

Sources indicate that "Both presidents are committed to finding a peaceful solution to the Ukraine conflict. There are, of course, forces in Europe and elsewhere seeking to derail this process, but the importance of this dialogue cannot be overstated," Dmitriev added.

Prior to his meeting with Xi, Trump identified Iran as an area of potential cooperation with Beijing, emphasizing the importance of keeping energy flowing through the Strait of Hormuz and preventing Tehran from obtaining nuclear weapons.

Recent findings suggest that "Both the United States and China have an interest in ensuring that energy flows through the Strait of Hormuz, and that Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon," Trump said. "We both feel that very strongly on both points.".

Speaking on China's own turf amid a fierce technological rivalry between Washington and Beijing, Trump asserted American dominance in artificial intelligence, declaring the U.S. the world leader in "superintelligence" and claiming Americans invented the technology.

"Americans invented. We just invented [superintelligence], and it is unleashing extraordinary prosperity and growth for people all around the globe," Trump said.

Trump described superintelligence as the greatest technological transformation since the Industrial Revolution, acknowledging that the technology has "some bad elements" that must be carefully managed while rejecting international efforts to regulate it.
