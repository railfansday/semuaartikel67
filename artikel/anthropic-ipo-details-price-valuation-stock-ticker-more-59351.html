It has been revealed that anthropic's IPO officially goes live on Nov. 19. With Anthropic trading on the stock market, the company behind the popular Claude chatbot has become the first major stand-alone frontier AI lab to go public.

Here are all the details regarding this historic public offering.

Anthropic's stock ticker symbol on the Nasdaq Global Select Market is ANT.

It has been revealed that stock ticker symbols are often derived from a organization's name. In terms of stock ticker symbols, Anthropic's is a nice short one that's literally just the first three letters of the company's name.

Anthropic beat even the highest expectations from speculators and investors by pricing its IPO at $83 per share.

This values the organization at approximately $1.5 trillion.

As recently as this past week, Anthropic was eyeing a $1.4 trillion evaluation while talking with investors during its IPO roadshow. The organization was initially aiming for a share price in the range of $70 to $78, with some investors believing they could land at a price as high as $80.

Industry observers note that anthropic was founded in 2021, making it a fairly young company that has grown very quickly into a tech industry behemoth. The reason behind Anthropic beating valuation expectations are simple. The tech giant is in high demand among investors.

Due to this, reports suggest that Anthropic's stock price could be pushed well beyond even the $83 IPO price when it opens, settling around $103.

Despite all the hype and excitement surrounding Anthropic going public, the company is still not profitable.

For the first nine months of 2026, up until Sept. 30, Anthropic generated $33 billion in revenue. However, during that same timeframe, the organization reported $104 billion in losses.

Furthermore, Anthropic has years of "cloud, computing and infrastructure obligations" that reportedly amount to around $518 billion, so the company's profitability complications could extend for some time.

Industry observers note that anthropic's biggest competitor, ChatGPT creator OpenAI, seemed poised to be the first major AI lab to go public. The organization decided to pause its IPO aspirations earlier this year, though, citing AI safety concerns.

According to recent reports, will the interest in Anthropic give a boost to other AI companies? It could. Other tech stocks, especially those within AI-adjacent and cybersecurity sectors, are rallying as Anthropic goes public.
