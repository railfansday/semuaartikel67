XRP Price Reclaims $1 Despite ETF Outflows: What’s Driving the Price?

🌐 Source: Coinpedia • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (441 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • XRP price rebounds 11% from $0.893 as whale activity and market demand strengthen further.
  • XRP Whales moved 140.35 million XRP worth roughly $136 million to private wallets recently.
  • spot XRP ETFs recorded $19.13 million in weekly outflows despite XRP’s rebound.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

XRP price rebounds 11% from $0.893 as whale activity and market demand strengthen further.

XRP Whales moved 140.35 million XRP worth roughly $136 million to private wallets recently.

U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded $19.13 million in weekly outflows despite XRP’s rebound.

Ripple’s XRP has climbed 4% in the past 24 hours, reclaiming the $1 level, while outperforming Bitcoin and the overall crypto market. This rally comes despite U.S.spot XRP ETFs recording $19 million in outflows last week.This surprising 11% jump from its recent low of $0.893 raises the question: What’s driving the XRP price up?

As XRP moves toward the key $1 level, its 24-hour trading volume has increased 15.90% to $1.815 billion, showing stronger trading activity. This follows a 10-day decline that pushed XRP down from $1.32 to a low of $0.893.

It has been revealed that one of the primary reasons behind this rise is large-wallet activity. Recent Whale Alert data reveals that whales moved around 140.35 million XRP, worth about $136 million, to a wallet address rLxFW.

Neither receiving wallet belongs to a known crypto exchange, which means the tokens were not sent directly to exchanges for selling.

According to recent reports, this matches data from Glassnode, which indicates that the number of wallets holding at least 10,000 XRP reached a record 344,832. This means more large holders are keeping their XRP instead of selling,.

The rally also put pressure on leveraged traders, as XRP saw around $6 million in liquidations, including $3.56 million from short traders. For short-term price moves, spot trading and whale activity can therefore have a bigger impact.

Sources indicate that while XRP is trying to move higher, ETF outflows continue to put pressure on its price. On November 17, U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded $6.16 million in outflows.

Sources indicate that although U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded $19.13 million in outflows over the past week, their largest weekly withdrawal in eight months, spot market buyers have continued to support XRP near key price levels. Grayscale and Bitwise led the outflows.

ETF holdings also represent only around 1.6% of XRP’s circulating supply.

Recent findings suggest that XRP is now near the $0.978 level, with $0.952 acting as nearby support and $1.02 as the next upside target. The 14-period RSI is at 68, showing strong buying activity but also leaving room for a short-term pullback.

For a stronger recovery, XRP needs to close above $1.00 on the daily chart. A break above this level could open the way toward $1.09–$1.15.

On the downside, if XRP falls below $0.97 and loses the key $0.90 support, the price could drop further toward $0.80.
