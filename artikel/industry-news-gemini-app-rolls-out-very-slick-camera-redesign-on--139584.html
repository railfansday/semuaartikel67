Google has thoroughly redesigned Gemini for Android’s in-app camera that lets you swiftly add an image to prompts.

According to recent reports, available from the ‘plus’ menu in the prompt box, the old interface rolled out a fullscreen viewfinder that was quite generic. This UI is still in use when you launch the camera from the Gemini overlay.

The fresh one appears inline on the Gemini homepage or inside a conversation. It debuts as a bottom sheet that you can drag up — with a really slick animation — to take up most of the screen.

This nearly edge-to-edge redesign, which extends behind the system navigation bar, is a stark contrast to the old approach.

To the right of the capture button is a three-dot overflow button that animates on open, revealing options to control the flash and switch to the front-facing camera. There’s a back button on the other side, but you can also just swipe down.

This camera redesign first launched in the Gemini for iOS app, but has now widely rolled out on Android in both the beta and stable (version 17.76+) channels. Be sure to Force stop the Google app if you’re not seeing it live yet.

This follows the launch of GIF creation in the Gemini app yesterday. Powered by Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, select GIF from the Format menu or specify “Create a GIF” in your prompt. Google frames this feature as an alternative to searching for GIFs.
