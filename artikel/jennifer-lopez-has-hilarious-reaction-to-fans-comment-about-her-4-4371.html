Jennifer Lopez’s fans have her back when it comes to her love life.

On Sunday, November 15, the Office Romance star, 57, received unexpected words of wisdom as she opened up about her four divorces during a special screening and Q&A for her new concert film, The JLo Show: Live in Las Vegas, at the 92nd Street Y in New York City.

“It’s a massive part of my story,” she said, noting that she’d been divorced “four times,” per footage from the event shared via Instagram.

The actress and singer then burst into laughter as an audience member shouted, “You have six more fingers.”.

A bemused Lopez told the fan, “Yes, I do!”.

Recent findings suggest that “Oh, my God, that is the funniest thing I’ve ever heard in my life,” she continued. “That’s a T-shirt — ‘I got six more fingers.’”.

Comedian Nikki Glaser commented on the Instagram post, “6 More Fingers needs to be her next single.”.

Other commenters praised the fan’s support of Lopez.

“Women supporting women 🙌😂❤️,” wrote one, while another said, “Way to turn this positive!! YES!’ 🔥👏.”.

Lopez was married to actor Ojani Noa from 1997 to 1998, dancer Cris Judd from 2001 to 2003, singer Marc Anthony — with whom she shares twins Max and Emme, 18 — from 2004 to 2014 and actor Ben Affleck from 2022 to 2025.

Earlier this year, the “On the Floor” singer talked about getting over her divorce from Affleck, 54, during an appearance on Good Morning America.

“I took a year off,” she said, explaining that she had to “stop everything” following the split.

It has been revealed that lopez remarked, “I canceled tours. I decided to just be home and sit in what had happened without running away from it through work, through another person, through anything. Just sit.”.

According to recent reports, “I was just at a point where I was like, ‘What is going on with you?’” she continued. “I couldn’t blame anybody else because I don’t think that’s where the lesson is. And so, I really needed to figure myself out.”.

Lopez added, “I feel like for the first time in my life, I’m free. I’m on my own. It feels really good. I didn’t really know what that felt like since I was in my early 20s, and even before that I always had a boyfriend. There was always someone in my life and so many other things that I felt were out of my control. I’ve gotten to the point where I really trust myself and appreciate myself a little bit more.”.
