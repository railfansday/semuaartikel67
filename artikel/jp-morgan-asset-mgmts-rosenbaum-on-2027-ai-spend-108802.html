J.P. Morgan Asset Mgmt’s Rosenbaum on 2027 AI Spend

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (67 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that nathaniel Rosenbaum, JPMorgan’s Head of U.S. High-Grade Credit Strategy, expects another busy year of AI-related borrowing but states issuance growth could crest during 2027. High-grade supply surged nearly three and a half times this year, he noted; next year, it could reach a record $2.1 trillion without repeating that jump. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg).
